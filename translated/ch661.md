# Chapter 661: The Sentient Lotus Platform, Instantly Killing Four Golden Cores!

The Thousand-Ren Mountains spread across tens of thousands of li.

There was more than one towering peak within them.

They seemed to stand scattered everywhere, but faintly, there was still a pattern.

The lower the mountain, the lower the grade of the mineral deposit within it, and the more numerous the low-rank strange beasts lurking there. They emphasized victory through quantity.

The taller the mountain, the higher the grade of its mineral deposit.

Not only that, the grade of the strange beasts guarding the mountain was also higher, almost perfectly corresponding.

The difference was that once the mineral deposits and strange beast grades rose, their numbers decreased.

First-tier beings numbered over ten thousand. Second-tier locations often had thousands. By the time one reached third-tier mineral mountains, their numbers were around the hundred level.

But once fourth-tier mineral mountains were involved, the number of strange beasts inside sharply decreased, only single digits, and perhaps only one or two strange beasts guarded them.

Under such circumstances, for Golden Core cultivators, aside from first-tier mineral mountains, mineral mountains of all other levels were not easy to conquer.

Second-tier mineral mountains were low grade, but the strange beasts were too numerous.

Third-tier mineral mountains had slightly fewer strange beasts, but a sudden charge of over one hundred third-tier strange beasts was not something ordinary Golden Core cultivators dared face directly.

Even a powerful trio like Luo Chen's group had to avoid the sharp edge of the third-tier Black Gold Viper swarm.

There was no need to mention fourth-tier mineral mountains.

At this moment, beneath a blue fourth-tier mineral mountain towering into the clouds, four figures were rapidly flying down.

After leaving the range of the blue mountain, the four hurriedly entered a formation they had arranged in advance.

Each held their breath and focused, looking outside.

Before long, they saw a giant dark-blue ape jump back. After reaching the mountain, it seemed to discover something missing and could not help roaring in furious frustration.

Hearing this roar, all four revealed smiles.

Inside the formation, a shadowy figure emerged.

The shadow had a slender build. Seeing the four laughing, she immediately asked, "Did you get the Blue Evolution Lead?"

The leader was a red-haired old man. He nodded.

"Naturally. We used a major method and directly dug out that Blue Evolution Lead vein. The most essential parts are all in my hands. This time, we must thank Fellow Daoist Youya for luring away that giant ape."

Youya's face was covered beneath a hood, and her expression could not be seen. She merely extended a hand toward the old man.

"My share."

The old man raised his brow. "This vein is complete and not easy to divide. Why not wait until..."

"Give it to me!" Youya's tone was extremely forceful.

The old man's face darkened.

Seeing this, the other three hurriedly spoke to advise them.

However, Youya remained unmoved.

She stared fixedly at the red-haired old man. "Adept Red Corpse, since I can lure away a fourth-tier strange beast, I can naturally lure it back. Are you certain you will not give it to me?"

In an instant, the atmosphere became tense.

The old man took a deep breath and removed a cluster of crystalline blue crystal from his storage bag.

When he took it out, the expressions of the other three all changed.

"This is the best quality among that batch of Blue Evolution Lead, even close to fifth tier. How can you..."

The old man raised a hand and interrupted the person behind him, forcefully squeezing out a smile.

"The Thousand-Ren Mountains are the most dangerous and richest place in Five Elements Heaven. We avoided the other four places with the most people and came here to search for rare mineral deposits. What we rely on is Fellow Daoist Youya's shadow escape art."

"She contributed the most and naturally should receive the best portion of the spoils."

"Fellow Daoist Youya, I hope we continue cooperating sincerely later."

Youya snorted softly and accepted the Blue Evolution Lead under Adept Red Corpse's reluctant gaze.

Once it was in her hands, she could not help letting out a breath.

'With this item, my shadow escape art will have no more flaws. Even Sages will not be able to find my true body. Perhaps I can go challenge that inheritance place.'

Having made her decision, there was no longer any need for later cooperation.

In front of the four, her figure gradually began to scatter.

Adept Red Corpse's expression changed greatly. "Fellow Daoist Youya, are you going back on your word?"

Youya shook her head. "I am not going back on my word. I am merely ending our cooperation."

"You!"

"Keep her here!"

The three were furious and were about to act to force her to stay.

However, Adept Red Corpse gave a sharp shout and interrupted their movements, visible dread on his face.

He still worried about the dark-blue giant ape nearby.

Seeing this, Youya could not help laughing softly.

"Adept Red Corpse has good bearing. Then this little woman cannot go too far. I have a piece of news for you. Perhaps it can make up somewhat for your later losses without me."

"Three hundred li from here, I saw the Nine Yang Golden Eagle fighting people."

"If you go quickly enough, perhaps you can pick up a bargain."

Her voice still lingered by the ear, but when they looked again, Youya had already left the formation and vanished without trace.

This was the shadow escape art of the Shadow Demoness famous throughout the Northern Sea.

Her concealment was unsolvable, and she treated formations and restrictions as if they were nothing.

Adept Red Corpse was not surprised by her ability, but truly seeing her freely enter and exit his formation still chilled his heart.

At this moment, he turned and looked at three pairs of dissatisfied eyes, then shook his head.

"We cannot keep her. And with the giant ape enemy right beside us, unless you want to die."

How could the three not understand this principle? They were merely unwilling.

Without the Shadow Demoness restraining the strange beasts of this realm, their later gains would probably drop by seventy or eighty percent.

If that was the case, they might as well search for treasures in the Sand Sea or Wood Heaven Plain.

Adept Red Corpse had the same thought, but he had not expected the Shadow Demoness to end their cooperation directly after obtaining the Blue Evolution Lead.

"Forget it. Let us first go to the Nine Yang Golden Eagle and see whether we can pick up anything. Afterward, we will leave the Thousand-Ren Mountains and go to Wood Heaven Plain to gather spirit medicines."

He was the one with the highest realm among the five, possessing the ninth level of Golden Core.

With the Shadow Demoness gone, everyone naturally followed his decision.

Immediately, the four packed up the formation, concealed their auras, circled around the mountains of varying heights, and headed toward the Nine Yang Golden Eagle's territory.

In a valley among the mountains, Cave Master Moyun's expression was extremely ugly, because a black jiao and an azure bird were surrounding him, watching like tigers stalking prey.

If it were only one demon beast, he would not take it seriously.

But two were very troublesome.

Especially the Black King, which was like both python and jiao. The aura it displayed at this moment was clearly late third-tier, and it was incomparably vigorous, not inferior to Great Demon Kings that had advanced for many years.

He raised his head to the sky and looked at the two figures standing in midair.

"Devil Lord Azure Yang, is what you said true?"

Before Luo Chen answered, Ding Yi spoke.

"Is truth or falsehood decided by his words alone? Cave Master, you and I have known each other for many years. At this moment, if you do not trust me, do you trust him?"

"If he kills me, the next to die will be you. Earlier, he secretly set an ambush without warning and lured strange beasts to attack the two of us. That was his fine doing!"

"Cave Master, do not hesitate!"

Cave Master Moyun seemed moved, but still did not immediately act.

Luo Chen glanced at him coldly. "Your hesitation means you believe my words. Fellow Daoist Moyun, think carefully. Does the Ding Yi you know only know how to activate a gourd magic treasure? What about his other methods? Does he not want to use them, or can he not?"

Cave Master Moyun's face grew increasingly gloomy.

It should be that he could not.

He knew what Ding Yi was capable of. At the ninth level of Golden Core, Ding Yi had two or three powerful magic treasures in hand, absolutely no weaker than that yellow-skinned gourd.

Previously, against the Nine Yang Golden Eagle, Cave Master Moyun had used all his methods, but Ding Yi had been stretched thin.

Given their friendship, how could Ding Yi sit and watch him be attacked by the Nine Yang Golden Eagle?

Thinking about it now, if Ding Yi had truly fallen and the so-called Tai Sui was now on his body, it could explain why the other party had regulated his breath for half a day in that stone hall, yet his mana had barely recovered.

Because the other party had clearly used that time to refine Ding Yi's magic treasures.

And because time was short, he had only managed to refine the yellow-skinned gourd magic treasure.

Seeing him remain silent, Luo Chen sneered at "Ding Yi." No, he should be called Tai Sui.

"At this moment, with my killing intent exposed, you do not think of fighting me to the death, but instead try to persuade Fellow Daoist Moyun. It seems you know you are not my opponent."

"Since so, go die!"

As soon as his words fell, Luo Chen instantly formed a spiritual seal.

The All-Encompassing Fire Prison immediately began contracting from outside to inside, enclosing him and Tai Sui together.

Tai Sui's expression changed greatly. He wanted to mobilize the mana in the Golden Core, but felt it was restless and hard to control freely.

The Luo Chen opposite him had already moved.

Seven swords suddenly became one.

They pierced through the air.

"Stop!"

Tai Sui hurriedly shouted.

But Luo Chen showed no sign of stopping. Facing the terrifying baleful qi assaulting his body, Tai Sui truly panicked.

"Do you not want to know the true location of the Five Elements Lotus Platform?"

As he spoke, he clapped both hands fiercely.

The fat on his body once again rolled in waves. Under intense activation, Ding Yi's body became thin as a bamboo pole.

Boom!

The sword tip formed by the seven baleful swords in one was blocked outside by an illusory great fan.

Tai Sui gritted his teeth, his expression ugly to the extreme.

He had completely failed to expect Luo Chen's strength to reach this level, mastering a method similar to a Sage domain and suppressing him until his mana could not be used.

And after using his innate art on the Nine Yang Golden Eagle, little of its remaining power was left.

If he truly fought the other party to the death, this remnant-thought incarnation might be lost.

"Devil Lord Azure Yang, I know what you are thinking."

"You saw this world's spiritual qi distribution and likely think the place where the Five Elements Lotus Platform is located must be a place dense with wood-attribute spiritual qi, and that even if you kill me, you can find it yourself."

"But I tell you, you are wrong!"

Tai Sui spoke urgently and quickly. During this process, his body became increasingly withered, no longer describable as a bamboo pole.

He was simply a skeleton wrapped in skin.

Luo Chen's expression did not change as he controlled the Seven Baleful Swords through the Soul Heaven Sword Case.

He said coldly, "If it is not in a place dense with wood-attribute spiritual qi, then it should be where five qi condense!"

"Wrong, still wrong!" Tai Sui hurriedly denied it and said anxiously, "The Five Elements Lotus Platform in the Demon-Fall Land has already reached the limit of fourth tier and is about to break through to fifth tier. It long ago broke free from the restriction of a five-qi condensation environment. It is now wandering somewhere in Five Elements Heaven. Ordinary people cannot find its true location at all. Only I know. If you kill me, even if you search Five Elements Heaven for one hundred years, you will not find that Five Elements Lotus Platform's true body!"

Clang!

The sword tip paused slightly.

Sensing that the baleful qi no longer assaulted his protective art, Tai Sui finally let out a breath of relief.

He hurriedly said, "I absolutely do not deceive you. That Five Elements Lotus Platform has already gained sentience. It is definitely not comparable to the forced pseudo-spirits of the Thousand-Ren Mountains. If you want this item, you must rely on me!"

Luo Chen frowned and rapidly communicated with Han Zhan inwardly.

"Is what he said correct?"

"Difficult to judge. After all, the Five Elements Lotus Platform has always been controlled by the Holy Lands. The one Qingdan Valley obtained back then had only just entered fourth tier and had not had time to gain sentience. However, this person's soul fluctuations are extremely intense. He should not be lying."

Was that so?

Luo Chen hesitated. Previously, Han Zhan had said the Five Elements Lotus Platform might be in a place where five qi condensed.

This was the main reason he had dared set an ambush to kill Tai Sui.

Without the other party, he could still find the Five Elements Lotus Platform.

But now, it seemed an accident had occurred.

In an instant, Luo Chen made a decision.

"I can spare you, but this time, you must sincerely help me find the Five Elements Lotus Platform!"

Tai Sui let out a breath of relief. "Naturally. I can even swear a Dao oath. However... you must also agree to one matter."

"Hm?" Luo Chen's tone grew heavy.

But this time, Tai Sui did not back down. "You must agree. Otherwise, even if you kill me, it is useless. Ding Yi's body only carries one of my remnant thoughts. To me, this body is merely an incarnation. At worst, I abandon it."

Luo Chen suppressed his impatience. "What matter? Tell me."

There was room to talk.

Tai Sui immediately said, "Very simple. After I help you find the Five Elements Lotus Platform, you and Cave Master Moyun must go to where my main body is and help me undo the restrictions imprisoning it."

"Impossible!" Luo Chen decisively refused.

He narrowed his eyes and stared at the other party. "A mere incarnation is already this troublesome. Luo does not dare imagine how powerful the main body is. What if you kill the donkey after the grinding is done..."

"I can swear a Great Dao oath!" Tai Sui said sharply, as if grabbing a life-saving straw. "If I act against you after the matter is done, let five thunders strike me and let me never recover through ten thousand calamities!"

"Devil Lord Azure Yang, you need not worry about my sincerity. My main body is Tai Sui, a spirit plant that gained sentience, and I fear lightning and fire most. This kind of oath has great restraint over me!"

Luo Chen hesitated.

Looking at the sincere Tai Sui, he slowly said, "An oath alone is not enough. I must place one or two restraining methods on you."

As he spoke, a faint, drifting cluster of azure flame rose in his palm.

With only one glance, Tai Sui's pupils shrank.

But now that life and death were controlled by another, he had no choice but to lower his head.

"I agree."

Luo Chen smiled faintly. After the other party swore the oath, he slapped that cluster of Withering Glory True Fire into the other's body, close to the qi sea.

In this way, there was double insurance.

Only now did the tense atmosphere gradually scatter.

Luo Chen dispersed the All-Encompassing Fire Prison, put Black King and Heavenjade into the spirit beast pouches, and no longer suppressed Cave Master Moyun.

Afterward, he put away the Myriad Souls Banner.

When the black mist filling the sky scattered, the sky immediately became clear and the air clean.

At this moment, a cry of surprise appeared.

"Supreme-grade Nine Yang Geng Metal! And such a large piece!"

Along with the voice, four figures appeared.

They occupied the high air, looking down at the three below from above.

One ninth-level Golden Core cultivator had a greatly weakened aura, as if he had just used some secret art that badly damaged his vital energy.

One seventh-level Golden Core cultivator had a pale face, torn robes, and eagle claw marks on his body.

The other person's aura was rather impressive, but he only revealed a sixth-level Golden Core realm.

"It seems you spent enormous effort to kill this Nine Yang Golden Eagle."

Adept Red Corpse laughed darkly.

With a wave of his hand, the other three slowly spread out and surrounded Luo Chen's group.

Not only that, two figures also appeared behind him, without the slightest aura of life, yet with bronze skin and iron bones. At one glance, they were not easy to provoke.

Facing this scene, Cave Master Moyun treated it like a great enemy and had already raised his lifebound magic treasure, that copper furnace.

Tai Sui smiled bitterly.

"Devil Lord Azure Yang, right now, I truly have no strength."

Luo Chen's expression was calm, and he shook his head.

"No matter. Only a group of fools seeking death."

As he spoke, the vanished All-Encompassing Fire Prison unfolded again, enclosing the entire valley.

A terrifying pressure immediately enveloped the four Golden Core cultivators.

When this pressure appeared, Adept Red Corpse's expression changed.

"A Sage?"

There was no answer. Only an enormous azure hand seal broke through the air.

And behind layer upon layer of Azure Yang Great Handprint were four incomparably sharp flying swords.

Before the other three, a flying sword also attacked each of them.

Those three middle Golden Core cultivators wanted to resist, yet for some reason, they could not mobilize mana in time.

With only one lapse, the flying swords pierced through their chests, terrifying baleful qi pulsing, shattering their cores and breaking their souls.

Adept Red Corpse's face was full of terror, and he hurriedly controlled two refined corpses comparable to late cultivators to block before him.

Boom! Boom!

Terrifying palm seals sent the two refined corpses flying backward. Although they were not killed, the protective formation was broken.

Behind the palm seals, four flying swords instantly arrived and continuously launched attacks with Adept Red Corpse as the center.

Clang! Clang! Clang! Clang!

Sharp metal collision sounds continuously rang out, showing that this person also had a fine protective magic treasure.

Amid his terror, Adept Red Corpse had only one thought.

Run.

Mana surged out wildly, temporarily pushing away that strange domain suppression and gaining a moment of peace.

At this time, he could finally control his mana calmly.

Under the attacks of four flying swords, he was about to flee.

But at this moment, an explosive sound rang out abruptly.

The next moment, the red-robed Daoist suddenly appeared, blocking his path.

Adept Red Corpse stared at those cold eyes and hurriedly spoke. "Fellow Daoist, this is all a misunderstanding. We were only passing by..."

Dark eyes, as if falling into illusion.

When Adept Red Corpse woke in sharp pain, four sharp swords had already pierced through his chest, and his Golden Core was being held in the red-robed Daoist's jade-like hand.

"How could..."

Thud.

The corpse fell to the earth.

The two refined corpses also made no sound, falling to the ground with two thuds.

On the ground, Cave Master Moyun looked at the three corpses, then raised his head and looked at the sky in awe.

One against four, instantly killed.

Devil Lord Azure Yang was truly this terrifying.

Tai Sui's expression was likewise uncertain. He knew the other party might be very strong, strong enough to erase this incarnation.

But he had not expected the other party's methods to be so abundant, and his battle awareness even more exquisite.

There was no prolonged bitter battle. There was only an instant kill as fast as thunder.

'Ding Yi, you truly found me a fine helper!' Tai Sui thought resentfully.

In the sky, Luo Chen was about to land when he suddenly turned his head.

He subconsciously looked in a certain direction, but discovered nothing.

He frowned, then descended to the earth. The seven sharp swords drilled into the sword case on his back like swallows returning to their nest.

He patted the spirit beast pouch, and an azure figure flew out. It was Heavenjade.

"Gather the spoils for me. Those two refined corpses should be intact. Bring them over."

"Yes, Master!"

Heavenjade answered crisply and first walked toward the millstone-sized Nine Yang Geng Metal on the ground.

Seeing Heavenjade fly out and change from an azure fighting gull into human form, Cave Master Moyun and Ding Yi were shocked again.

A transformed demon cultivator?

But why was she only third-tier?

Luo Chen ignored their shock and walked straight to Tai Sui.

"Tell me about this so-called Five Elements Heaven."

*(End of Chapter)*
