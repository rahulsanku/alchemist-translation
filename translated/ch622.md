# Chapter 622: Dread in the Heart, the True Meaning of a Wrong Path

Inside Star Dou Hall, a white-robed Daoist sat cross-legged.

Silent and still, he seemed as if he had entered extinction.

After a long while, he suddenly exhaled a mouthful of turbid air.

Such a long breathing rhythm would have suffocated anyone else long ago, yet on this Daoist's body it was filled with cadence and rhythm, as if an ancient black turtle were swallowing and exhaling the spiritual qi of heaven and earth.

Abruptly.

The Daoist slowly opened his eyes.

Sensing his own state, he revealed a smile of relief.

"As expected, focusing on artifact-forging for a long time affects cultivation enormously. Fortunately, my foundation is solid enough, and I stabilized it."

To refine the Demon Emperor shed husk, Luo Chen had spent a full year.

Before that, he had only just broken through to Golden Core Stage Six.

It should have been a time to strike while the iron was hot after his realm improved, but he had never expected that such prolonged artifact-forging would cause tremendous depletion to his spirit and physique.

It was even because he had swallowed supreme-grade True Flame Pills like qi-replenishing pills without proper refinement that a large amount of fire poison had settled in his qi sea.

Previously, while immersed in artifact-forging, he had not noticed it.

Only after placing the treasure embryo into nurturing and returning to his normal cultivation rhythm did Luo Chen realize something was wrong.

His Golden Core Stage Six realm, which had only just advanced, had nearly fallen back.

Because of this, Luo Chen first used the Dragon-Slaying Art repeatedly to remove the fire poison in his body, then ate large quantities of various supplements, and finally rested for about a month before restoring his body to normal condition.

After that, Luo Chen cultivated with restraint for another two months and finally stabilized his realm.

At this moment, on the attribute panel, the entry [Golden Core Stage Six 1/100] hung there quietly, bringing Luo Chen an enormous sense of security.

"The treasure embryo has already been cast. What remains is nourishing it and engraving formations. My time has suddenly become much more ample."

"Three months of rest and recovery restored my essence, qi, and spirit to their peak. After this, I cannot slack off again."

Looking at the attribute panel, Luo Chen planned his next matters.

The time spent waiting for the treasure embryo to emerge naturally could not be wasted. He had to find things to do.

The first matter, beyond any doubt, was cultivation.

For Luo Chen, his ordinary cultivation was divided into two parts.

Qi cultivation and body cultivation.

In terms of battle strength, body cultivation naturally took priority, but where the Great Dao of longevity was concerned, qi cultivation could not be neglected in the slightest.

Luo Chen roughly estimated according to his previous cultivation speed.

With the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture as his lifebound cultivation method, large quantities of upper-grade True Flame Pills as aid, and the most central support of Star Dou Hall's third-tier upper-grade spirit land, his cultivation speed was that of a genuine heavenly spirit root cultivator.

In the end, Luo Chen arrived at a rough time limit.

"Ten years!"

"If no outside affairs disturb me, then secluded inside Star Dou Hall on Peng Lake, I need only ten years to advance to Golden Core Stage Seven and become a genuine great cultivator!"

Previously, when advancing from Golden Core Stage Five to Stage Six, his original estimate had been six or seven years.

In the end, it took fully nine years.

The extra two or three years were firstly because he went out to Quyang Island's defensive line to carry out a garrison mission. The cave dwelling there only had a third-tier lower-grade spiritual qi environment, which greatly delayed his cultivation speed.

Secondly, in the middle of that time, he had once placed his attention on improving Myriad Path Convergence. While constantly adjusting the body-tempering art, he had caused quite a few effects to his body.

Ultimately, this resulted in three extra years being wasted before he advanced to Golden Core Stage Six.

Dual cultivation of law and body was like this.

Immortal cultivators did not value the physique, yet the physique truly and materially affected cultivation efficiency.

When the two were cultivated together, there would always be times of conflict. Even with the system panel's assistance, Luo Chen had no choice but to pay a price for it.

Fortunately, Myriad Path Convergence had already been adjusted successfully. Next, it should not affect his normal cultivation.

He already possessed battle strength comparable to a great cultivator now, and ten years later he would also possess a genuine great cultivator's realm. At that time, the increase in his battle strength would become even more terrifying.

If the treasure embryo's formation engraving succeeded and he gained a defensive magic treasure refined from a Demon Emperor shed husk.

At that time, Luo Chen might be able to declare with heroic spirit, "Below Sage, who can contend with me!"

Of course, such hopes still existed only in fantasy.

Right now, the road still had to be walked step by step.

After qi cultivation came body cultivation.

[Archaic Tier Three 81/100]

Looking at the attribute panel, Luo Chen counted the quantity of Black Emperor Ointment on his fingers.

"It should last until body cultivation reaches third-tier perfect completion, right?"

He was somewhat unsure.

This body-tempering path he had created himself had gone smoothly at first. Later, he used fire in place of lightning to accelerate body tempering, and not long ago, by borrowing the artifact-forging path, he refined inside and out together, which could be called perfect and flawless.

At the very least, it was perfect and flawless within Luo Chen's current vision and scope.

But this path, taken as a whole, still seemed rather rough and savage. Even in the system's grading, it was not divided into Stage One, Two, Three, Four, Five, and Six like the Golden Core realm, but merely used the broad realm division of Archaic Tier Three.

This also meant that Luo Chen's overall perception of each level's improvement was rather vague.

Especially since the cultivation of any Great Dao became harder the further one went.

This progress bar at eighty-one was still separated from perfect completion at one hundred. As for what degree of resource consumption lay between them, Luo Chen had no definite number in his heart.

"I can only take it one step at a time!"

This path had no method to rely on and no precedent to follow. Luo Chen could only feel his way across the river by the stones.

After qi cultivation and body cultivation, Luo Chen's attention fell on the formation column.

Fourth-tier formation: Two Modes Six Armors Array

This was a combined formation, using the third-tier Two Modes Array as its core and supplementing it with six third-tier defensive formations.

Among the six third-tier defensive formations, he had cultivated all of them to above the Grandmaster level, and with them as cores, he had refined six sets of mysterious armor.

But if he wanted to refine the treasure embryo cast from the Demon Emperor shed husk, then the most central Two Modes Array could not be neglected in the slightest.

This formation was only third-tier in grade, but its power had almost no upper limit.

What affected the formation's power lay in the materials used to arrange it.

It had been mentioned before: what were the two modes?

Yin and yang were the two modes.

Heaven and earth were also the two modes.

Odd and even, or hard and soft, mysterious yellow and heaven-earth could likewise be mutually taken as the two modes.

Even clear and turbid, water and fire, men and women, old and young all contained the meaning of the two modes.

In short, a formation arranged by taking opposing meanings possessed the true meaning of the Two Modes.

Its power also depended on the two.

From this point, this third-tier Two Modes Array somewhat resembled Ghost God Valley's third-tier Heaven's Craft Spirit-Seizing Array from back then. Both were said to possess no upper limit in power.

Back then, the Two Modes Five Elements Sword Array Han Zhan relied on as a killing method had also valued the Two Modes Array's upper limit.

After Luo Chen obtained that set of artifact-forging blueprints, he learned the Two Modes Array from Han Zhan and easily entered the threshold.

But in later practice, his progress became increasingly slow.

Even now, it was still stuck at the Perfect level and could not reach the Grandmaster level Luo Chen regarded as the minimum requirement.

As the core of the Two Modes Six Armors Array, how could it fall behind the Six Armors?

"Looks like I need to find time to visit Han Zhan and ask him for some guidance."

Luo Chen sighed, but there was a trace of reluctance in his eyes.

Perhaps if outsiders learned of their relationship, they would find Luo Chen's reluctance strange.

They had clearly helped each other in friendship before and lived together harmoniously. How could he now be unwilling to meet?

This had to mention the thought of dread in Luo Chen's heart.

Back then, on Langya Mountain's merchant vessel, the Langya, when Luo Chen removed the foul qi from Han Zhan's soul-nourishing wood, he had encountered a foul qi assault on his sea of consciousness.

That time had been rather dangerous.

Inside his sea of consciousness, illusions had been born one after another, and even the soul-defense magic treasure, the Rotten Axe-Handle Black Chess Pieces, could not resist.

It was Han Zhan who had personally acted to resolve Luo Chen's illusion crisis.

Because of that matter, Luo Chen had profited from disaster. He had cultivated the Dragon-Slaying Art to near Great Perfection and, taking that opportunity, upgraded the illusion technique Mirror Flower, Water Moon, giving it a second layer of illusion. After Mirror Flower, Water Moon, the Ghost Immortal Pavilion manifested again, and the latter name had come from Luo Chen's inspiration after the white-robed cultivator in the demon hunter assessment mission blurted out the three words "Ghost Immortal Tower."

But because of that matter, Luo Chen also developed a hidden worry.

That was: Han Zhan could ignore the Rotten Axe-Handle Black Chess Pieces' defense and invade his sea of consciousness!

He did not know whether that was a common method among Sage cultivators, or a special secret art Han Zhan possessed in soul-refinement. In short, in front of Han Zhan, Luo Chen felt for the first time the naked fear of being completely undefended in terms of his soul.

Along the way before, Luo Chen had never shown any similar dread, and he continued getting along well with Han Zhan.

But ever since arriving at the Myriad Immortals Assembly and entering this Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake, he had deliberately lived apart from Han Zhan.

He had used a very reasonable excuse.

That was that Han Zhan had obtained the soul-nourishing wood and needed to recover his cultivation and strength. Luo Chen himself had many affairs; if they stayed together all the time, he would not be able to resist disturbing him.

Han Zhan was naturally happy to accept it. Or perhaps he had seen through Luo Chen's thoughts and actively avoided suspicion.

In any case, the two had already "lived separately" for many years.

Even when he encountered confusion while improving Myriad Path Convergence, he had relied on his own exploration and had not sought Han Zhan's guidance.

But now, he still had to take the initiative once.

"I should meet him too, lest we become estranged."

Luo Chen muttered, then sent a voice-transmission talisman outside the hall.

...

Within Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake, on a solitary island.

Unlike other places, there were no flowers, grasses, water birds, insects, ants, or beasts here. The barren island's ecology even gave one a feeling of all vitality being extinguished.

Yet this was precisely Han Zhan's solitary dwelling place.

Several days earlier, Luo Chen and Han Zhan had agreed on a time to meet.

Today, Han Zhan set aside time to see Luo Chen.

"There is something I have always wanted to say. Your path in practicing formations is a little rigid, or perhaps one should say it has gone somewhat astray."

"In the past, I took you for someone with talent in formations. You could easily get started with all kinds of low-level formations as soon as you learned them, and once you began, you advanced by leaps and bounds. Some formations you arranged even had effects better than those of a dignified Sage Perfected Being like this old man. It was truly inconceivable."

"But later, I discovered that you seem to be fixated only on precise imitation and engraving, valuing only the form and not the meaning."

"This learning method may be feasible when used on low-level formations, but once it involves high-level formations, it becomes somewhat insufficient."

"You must know that truly formidable formation masters never arrange formations by sticking rigidly to established rules. They adapt to local conditions, but likewise transform the environment, arranging the formation most suitable in their own minds. Why can they do this? Because they understand the true essence of formations!"

"Because they understand, they are not restricted."

"Because you do not understand, you are restricted everywhere."

"Every high-level formation contains the creator's design concept within it. You can only arrange a perfect surface state of a formation, but cannot touch its true core. That is merely possessing the form without the spirit."

"Take the Sea-Sealing Eight Trigrams Formation as an example. You can only arrange it in a place thick with water vapor, but this old man can forcefully arrange it in a place where a volcano is erupting. Can you understand the gap between the two?"

"Or take this Two Modes Array. You got started with it extremely quickly, yet now you are stuck at the Small Accomplishment level. The power of what you arrange can at most make early Golden Core cultivators feel troubled. Do you know the essence of the Two Modes Array?"

Luo Chen never would have expected that what he had thought would be an ordinary meeting where he asked for guidance and Han Zhan instructed him.

The other party actually gave him a sharp warning in the study of formations.

The self-satisfaction he had held in the past seemed so laughable amid these words of guidance.

He had thought that by relying on the proficiency panel, he could learn all kinds of formations without upper limit. Now it seemed he had clearly taken the wrong fork in the road.

As he reflected inwardly, he also found a rough parallel in the alchemy path for the reasoning Han Zhan had spoken of.

His alchemy skill, even after reaching Great Perfection, still had an upper-grade pill success rate of around fifty percent, which meant there remained a fifty percent failure rate.

This probability was actually already very high.

It was already enough to be called an alchemy master.

But one thing was wrong. Ever since Luo Chen refined the fourth-tier Transformation Pill, he had already become a fourth-tier alchemist.

Such an existence, never mind a single individual pill, could be called an alchemy grandmaster even among the refinement of all kinds of pills. Compared with those sacred hands of alchemy, he was only one line short.

Likewise, when such an existence faced low-level pills, even if the pill success rate could not be said to be one hundred percent, it ought to be nine out of ten.

But he could not do that!

It was not that he entirely could not do it. Luo Chen had long since discovered that the longer he refined a certain pill, even after Great Perfection, the success rate could still slowly rise, and it was actually not far from the so-called nine-out-of-ten certainty.

At the time, he had only taken the principle within as "nothing special, only practiced hands."

Now it seemed it was clearly "read a book a hundred times, and its meaning appears of itself"!

He had clearly, on the alchemy path, been subconsciously comprehending a certain pill's essential meaning.

At this moment, Han Zhan's sudden awakening strike on the formation path instead allowed him to understand another layer of meaning outside the proficiency panel.

That was: above proficiency, there was true meaning!

Looking down from a high vantage, Luo Chen worked backward through everything in the past.

After Fireball reached Great Perfection, the additional "Blazing Sun Art" effect appeared. That was the true meaning inherent in Fireball itself.

After Spiritual Eye reached Great Perfection, it rooted itself in both eyes. When this art was originally created, it should have been meant to transform the cultivator's eyes, allowing them to possess excellent vision, see signs of life and death, and avoid dangerous places.

Mirror Flower, Water Moon was like this. The Dragon-Slaying Art was like this.

All sorts of things were like this.

Then for the other spells that had not shown special effects, was it because their own upper limits were not high, or because he had not understood their true meaning, so they stopped at Great Perfection?

For a time, inside Han Zhan's cave residence, Luo Chen sank into deep thought.

Han Zhan did not disturb him either.

Only after Luo Chen woke from it did he return to the topic and hand him the advanced cultivation method for the Two Modes Array.

When Luo Chen walked out from the barren, lifeless solitary island, he could not help glancing back in Han Zhan's direction.

"It seems I measured a gentleman's heart with a petty man's mind."

"Such instruction and guidance is no less than that of a selfless teacher or a close elder."

"That is right. With the Great Dao oath he swore back then, how could he harbor any crooked thoughts toward me?"

Luo Chen laughed at himself, and his heart finally relaxed enormously.

It was both because of Han Zhan's selfless guidance today, and because he now knew how to cultivate the Two Modes Array to a higher level.

...

Night had fallen low.

A flawless white jade disk slowly rippled in the water.

Deep beneath the dark lake, inside a water-curtain cave overgrown with algae and waterweed and bright with coral, a huge jiaolong was coiled there, eyes closed in deep sleep.

Beside him, however, was a delicate figure sitting cross-legged with her waist and back straight, her hands forming seals before her as she breathed in and out, swallowing the incomparably pure spiritual qi of this place.

Looking carefully, the figure was a young girl.

She wore water-green clothing, embroidered with white lotuses so lifelike they seemed real.

White silk socks appeared and vanished beneath her skirt, and her slender waist seemed as if one hand could encircle it.

As for her face, she had fine brows, snow-white skin, and a tall, straight nose.

Unfortunately, though brimming with spiritual qi, she was overly clear and beautiful, lacking some of the baby softness unique to young girls.

Abruptly, a deep voice sounded beside the girl's ear.

"You should go back."

The girl opened her eyes, and a crafty look flashed through them.

"Uncle Dragon, how did you know I woke up?"

Black King looked at the girl helplessly. "What you absorb is the pure spiritual qi I refine and exhale. How could I not know what level means you should stop?"

The girl pursed her little mouth. "Tch. Boring."

Black King sighed. "Go back. I heard Master say before that today is the Mid-Autumn Festival. Are you not going to reunite with your family?"

When the full moon came, it was the time for reunion.

Zhang Jingqing stared blankly outside, speechless for a moment.

Rustling sounds arose as Black King moved his enormous body.

The girl instantly panicked. "Uncle Dragon, just let me stay here for one night, all right? Please."

Black King was puzzled. "You are this unwilling to see your mother, and your father as well?"

"Father? His eyes only have that little brother in them." The girl lowered her head, her mood low. "As for Mother, ever since she went to Quyang Island two years ago to deliver spirit stones to Lord Lushan and took Lord Lushan as her master, she gave up her work at Azure Yang Hall and wholeheartedly cultivates at Lord Lushan's side. I can go see her after some time."

In her words, she still held a little attachment toward her mother, but toward her father she was full of resentment.

As Black King listened, his body began gradually sliding outward.

Seeing this, the girl's eyes almost filled with tears.

"You do not like Little Qing either?"

Black King helplessly turned his head. With a roll of mana, he carried her onto his back.

"Master has business with me. What does that have to do with whether I like you or not? Little girl, you have quite a lot on your mind. Come out with me. Taking advantage of this fine festival, perhaps Master will be in a good mood and accept you as a disciple."

The master Uncle Dragon spoke of?

Sitting on the jiaolong's back, complicated emotions appeared on the girl's face.

There was worshipful admiration, reverent fear, and as her gaze shifted, even more anxious unease.

Who knew where such a young child got so many emotions from?

The jiaolong swam extremely quickly. After leaving the water-curtain cave, he broke through the water waves and ascended all the way.

With a splash, water sprayed everywhere as the jiaolong appeared on the surface of the great lake.

On the dragon's back, the girl raised her head and looked up.

Beneath the bright jade disk of the moon, one man and one woman stood in the air.

The woman stood behind. She was someone the girl had known since childhood. The outside world called her Fairy Heavenjade, while the girl would sweetly call her Aunt Heavenjade.

As for the white-robed man standing at the very front, his skin was like blazing fire and his body like a towering mountain. Even with moonlight falling over him, he seemed like a volcano that might erupt at any moment.

The jiaolong lowered his head. "Master, what instructions do you have for Old Black?"
