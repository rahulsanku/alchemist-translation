# Chapter 926: Canglong Crosses Tribulation, Borrowing Lightning to Refine Treasures

The Upper Ruins City of today had long lost its former glory as the gateway to the sacred land.

That enormous palm print descending from the heavens had forcibly split the once-great immortal city into seven districts.

The massive pit in the center, filled with rolling magma day and night, was called the "Heavenly Fire Basin."

The remaining six relatively intact areas housed a large number of wandering cultivators who dwelled and cultivated there.

Some might lament the city's collapse, but the rogue cultivators living here clapped their hands in approval.

If the Nether Origin Sect hadn't fallen, where would they ever have obtained such a precious cultivation ground?

Led by the late-stage Adept old monster Ge, a group of Adept cultivators had banded together, dredging blocked spirit veins and gradually restoring a semblance of order to Upper Ruins City.

A host of powerful figures lived scattered across the area, each claiming a territory for themselves.

On this day.

A cultivator with a ruddy complexion quietly waited outside a mist-shrouded formation.

After a long while, the mist parted, and Chu Mo looked at the visitor in surprise.

"Fellow Daoist Feng, what brings you to my humble abode today?"

Feng Yinuo laughed heartily, shaking the chillingly cold gourd in his hand.

"I happened to find a bottle of Sorbet Wine. Since you and I are the only fellow wine connoisseurs nearby, I naturally had to share it with you. Besides, I've always remembered how you helped me drive off that mortal enemy last time, and I've been thinking of how to repay you. I figured a bottle of wine wouldn't be something you'd refuse."

Chu Mo's face lit up with surprise, and he rubbed his hands together. "Could it be the Sorbet Wine brewed by the Dieyun Sect back in the day?"

Feng Yinuo smiled smugly. "Exactly! With the Dieyun Sect's destruction, all kinds of popular spirit wines have become increasingly scarce on the market, especially this Sorbet Wine. I went to great lengths to acquire this gourd from the black market."

With that, he gestured with his chin toward the interior.

"Aren't you going to invite me in?"

Chu Mo chuckled. "Please, come in! Come in!"

If Feng Yinuo had brought anything else to repay him, he might not have accepted it—after all, the help he'd given back then was merely a small favor.

But for someone as addicted to wine as he was, the Sorbet Wine, known as a pseudo-fourth-grade vintage, was truly the finest of the finest.

Even back when his small family clan hadn't yet been destroyed, he could only enjoy a few cups a year at most.

Since that was the case, why refuse?

The two entered the cave dwelling and began drinking cup after cup with abandon.

Their conversation flowed freely, touching on all manner of topics.

"Your Chu family was once a great clan of the Tianji Zen Domain. How did it vanish just like that?"

"When the city gates catch fire, the fish in the moat suffer the consequences!"

"But I heard that when the Demon Emperor attacked the Tiangong Sect, they didn't lay a hand on the Tianji Zen Domain?"

"Alas, the calamity didn't come from those Demon Emperors. It was the evil karma left behind by that old bald donkey, Monk Huiping!"

"You mean... the Demon-Suppressing Tower?"

"Yes. Monk Huiping died in the Million Mountains, and the Demon-Suppressing Tower was left without anyone to oversee it. After many years, the seals loosened, and the evil demons and heretics imprisoned within finally broke free. Brother Feng, just imagine—after being suppressed for so many years, how much resentment must they have harbored? The moment they emerged, they went on a killing spree. If I hadn't been fighting at Lingtian Pass back then, I would have died at home too. It's just a pity for my clansmen!"

"And who isn't the same? I once established a small sect, called myself a patriarch, and was quite impressive. Now my mountain gate is gone, and I've become the kind of rogue cultivator I once most despised."

The great war between humans and demons, which had lasted nearly two hundred years from start to finish, had left an enormous impact.

It had completely overturned the order that had stabilized the Eastern Wilderness for three thousand years.

Even though a few well-known Sage sects still stood tall, countless small sects and families had been destroyed in an instant.

Were the many Adept-level rogue cultivators in Upper Ruins City truly rogue cultivators?

Probably not!

Take the most powerful one, Old Monster Ge—it was said he had once been a true disciple of some Sage sect, with hopes of forming a Nascent Soul!

As they spoke of the past, both men couldn't help but sigh with emotion.

Chu Mo tapped the table. "Why talk about such sad things? Drink! Drink!"

Feng Yinuo laughed heartily. "Right! Today we only talk about wind and moon, not sad matters. We're free and unfettered now. Though we live for today without knowing about tomorrow, at least we're no longer burdened by family or sect. This fine wine—we no longer have to save it!"

As they laughed heartily, they drank with even more gusto.

But gradually, Chu Mo felt something was off.

"Brother Feng, this Sorbet Wine of yours has quite a kick. Why do I feel a chill all over my body?"

Feng Yinuo also felt a creeping coldness, but he didn't mind. "Isn't it better to be cooler? This Upper Ruins City, affected by that Heavenly Fire Basin, is unbearably hot. Perhaps this Sorbet Wine has aged so much that it's gained this miraculous effect!"

Chu Mo grunted in acknowledgment, finding it reasonable.

But after another short while, he finally sensed something wrong.

"It's not in my stomach—it's from outside!"

He immediately released his divine sense to probe the situation.

After a moment of stunned silence, Feng Yinuo did the same.

Soon, they discovered the source of the bone-chilling cold.

On a broken mountain peak, a vast amount of spiritual energy was continuously gathering, radiating a terrifying chill wherever it went.

Wherever the spiritual energy passed, it condensed into frost, as if winter had arrived.

Even the scorching heat emanating from the Heavenly Fire Basin was temporarily suppressed.

Seeing this, the two exchanged glances and couldn't help swallowing.

"Drawing upon the spiritual energy of heaven and earth, with strange phenomena appearing—someone is breaking through to the Sage stage!"

"That place... could it be Old Monster Ge?"

"No. Although Old Monster Ge primarily cultivates a water-attributed technique, ordinary cultivators with five-element spirit roots don't trigger such peculiar phenomena when breaking through to Sage. Could it be someone else?"

They stopped guessing, left the cave dwelling, and found a spot to watch from afar.

Their reaction was relatively slow.

Before them, many Adept cultivators in various parts of Upper Ruins City had already noticed the anomaly.

One by one, they looked up at the sky above Old Monster Ge's cultivation grounds.

As a massive amount of spiritual energy gathered, ominous clouds converged, and strands of silvery-white electric arcs began to flicker continuously within the clouds.

Spiritual energy gathered, strange phenomena appeared.

Tribulation clouds manifested, heavenly lightning arrived.

This was unmistakably the great tribulation of an Adept cultivator breaking through to the Sage stage!

And the appearance of this scene in Upper Ruins City—everyone understood what it meant.

It signified that a fellow rogue cultivator had fused the three treasures of essence, energy, and spirit, and had finally succeeded in taking that last step.

Would they succeed?

They hoped the one crossing the tribulation would succeed, for it would prove that even rogue cultivators could achieve the Great Dao!

But as the thunderclouds grew heavier and the silvery-white arcs flashed more wildly, every heart grew heavier.

"This is no ordinary lightning tribulation!"

"Such a grand display—even more terrifying than the tribulation I witnessed that heavenly prodigy undergo last time. Just how profound is this person's foundation?"

"Is he really a rogue cultivator?"

Amidst the murmurs, Old Monster Ge, one of the parties involved, was at that moment carefully standing respectfully to one side.

His gaze kept shifting between the woman sleeping with a pill in her mouth inside the ice coffin and the young man sitting cross-legged nearby.

The terrifying thing wasn't the woman crossing the tribulation.

It was the young man helping her cross it!

What kind of great power could make a slumbering female cultivator trigger a lightning tribulation?

Especially the pill in her mouth—if his guess was correct, it was likely a mid-grade Nascent Soul Pill!

Nascent Soul Pills were already rare in the world. Even the Sage sect Old Monster Ge had once belonged to had only a few in reserve, carefully treasured as sect heritage.

But a mid-grade Nascent Soul Pill?

Old Monster Ge couldn't imagine who in the world could refine such a thing.

No!

Perhaps there was one.

Alchemy Sect's Luo Chen!

Thinking of the legendary figure and his past illustrious achievements, Old Monster Ge couldn't help holding his breath.

If the person before him was truly that man, how should he conduct himself afterward?

Suddenly, his heart leaped.

The woman in the ice coffin—her eyelashes trembled, and she slowly opened her eyes.

Her beautiful pupils held countless complex, vicissitudinous emotions.

She didn't even notice Old Monster Ge's presence; her gaze was fixed solely on the young man manipulating the treasure mirror.

With a gulp, the Nascent Soul Pill was completely refined.

The surrounding spiritual energy gathered even faster, and the tribulation clouds in the sky grew so heavy they seemed ready to crush Upper Ruins City entirely.

The woman opened her mouth stiffly, and finally uttered a single sentence.

"I've truly troubled you."

Luo Chen smiled faintly, retracting the Dustbound Immortal Mirror that had been concealing Canglong, and tucked it into his storage ring.

Then he pointed outside. "The bigger trouble is still out there. Senior Sister will need to be careful later."

Canglong slowly rose from the ice coffin, glanced outside, and nodded to Luo Chen.

"I will do my utmost!"

Then she floated upward, breaking through the restrictions of the cave dwelling's formation, and headed into the sky.

Not only that, but a pagoda and a flying sword followed alongside her.

The pagoda had nine stories, half-new and half-old—it was the Icecastle's top-grade sect-suppressing treasure, the Moonlight Ice Castle, which Luo Chen had helped repair. The name "Icecastle" itself came from this treasure.

The flying sword had an elegant design, with a Canglong pearl embedded at the junction of the hilt and blade. It was the natal treasure of the Icecastle's founding ancestor. Even Canglong Fairy's name was derived from it.

Now, as Canglong crossed her tribulation, she could only rely on these two accompanying treasures and her own strength.

Would she succeed?

Even Canglong herself had little confidence.

The reason was that this lightning tribulation far exceeded the one she had experienced last time.

The cause was partly due to the increased difficulty of a second tribulation, but also partly due to Luo Chen's involvement.

Especially the latter!

That was the main reason for such a terrifying lightning tribulation!

But Canglong pressed forward without hesitation!

Having experienced the centuries-long dream within the Dustbound Immortal Mirror, she had come to understand too much.

Without an indomitable spirit, without the determination to find life in death, how could heaven and earth allow you to become a "true person"?

Moreover, the gaze watching her from behind gave her absolute confidence.

She believed that when she could no longer hold on, he would act.

Because this lightning tribulation was not hers alone.

"I will do my utmost!"

The woman, her face still somewhat pale, rushed toward the lightning tribulation.

Luo Chen, however, did not completely relax.

With a flick of his hand, a massive stone suddenly appeared, suspended in midair.

Opening his mouth, he spat out a wisp of blue-white flame that shot forth like a fire serpent, beginning to scorch the large stone.

Though the flame was small, its temperature was extremely high.

In just a short moment, the golden stone began to melt continuously, dripping into streams of golden liquid.

Like mercury, it flowed slowly through the air.

Old Monster Ge watched this scene and couldn't help swallowing.

Geng Metal!

And not just any Geng Metal—it was Nine Yang Geng Metal, the highest grade!

And such a large piece at that!

If this object were to appear in the world, countless cultivators would fight over it, stirring up a bloody storm.

But at this moment, he dared not harbor the slightest greed.

Because that piece of Geng Metal was being slowly melted into liquid by a terrifying true flame.

According to legend, Alchemy Sect's Luo Chen had once fought a life-and-death battle with Huang Man, the son of the Ancient Demon Emperor, in the Medicine King Domain!

That battle had ended with Alchemy Sect's Luo Chen wielding a mysterious and powerful flame, burning the Ancient Demon's son to ashes.

What kind of powerful flame could incinerate a Great Demon Emperor?

Perhaps only this blue-white flame before him, capable of melting metal and forging iron!

Luo Chen glanced at him and said calmly, "You've guessed my identity?"

Old Monster Ge didn't hesitate. With a thud, he knelt on one knee.

"Former true disciple of Firework Ancient Isle, Ge Wangji, pays respects to Senior Alchemy Sect!"

"Firework Ancient Isle?" Luo Chen's lips curled slightly. "A descendant of an old acquaintance, then. What relation is Xiao Shiniang to you?"

"She was my master!"

"I remember Fellow Daoist Xiao fell in the battle at Lingtian Pass. Since you are her disciple, why didn't you rebuild the sect after your master's fall, instead of becoming an unknown rogue cultivator in this ruined city?"

"Even cultivating alone, I walk on eggshells. How could I speak of restoring the sect's glory? Moreover, the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world today is controlled and enslaved by the Tianyuan Dao Sect, which seems poised to stir up conflict with the demon race again. Under such circumstances, even if I rebuilt Firework Ancient Isle, I would only be cannon fodder. So it's better to be a rogue cultivator, free and unrestrained, not subject to manipulation."

Luo Chen sighed softly upon hearing this answer.

Rogue cultivators had the advantage of freedom, but only they knew the hardships.

Ge Wangji—a good name.

In the midst of blades and war, he chose to forget his scheming heart, seeking natural inaction and transcendence from the mundane.

"Borrowing your cave dwelling for this tribulation today is a matter of fate."

"But my identity is sensitive and not easily exposed. Moreover, after today, this cave dwelling will likely be difficult to preserve, and you will have no place to cultivate."

"Therefore, I give you two choices. One is to enter my Luo Tian Sect as a guest elder. The second..."

Ge Wangji didn't hesitate. He knelt fully on the ground and kowtowed to Luo Chen.

"I choose the first!"

He didn't even listen to what the second choice was.

Luo Chen laughed heartily. A mere Adept cultivator—how could he speak of transcending the mundane?

This Ge Wangji certainly knew how to read the situation.

Of course, his actions did somewhat smack of bullying the weak by virtue of his strength.

With a wave of his hand, a jade bottle landed before Ge Wangji.

"I saw you staring intently at that Nascent Soul Pill. It seems you're approaching a cultivation bottleneck. Consider this the first salary for my Luo Tian Sect's guest elder!"

Ge Wangji took the jade bottle, his heart pounding uncontrollably.

Although he said he didn't want to get entangled in worldly affairs or fight with others, hadn't he ever longed for the good old days when a great power sheltered him and he only needed to focus on cultivation?

The Alchemy Sect was indeed generous!

At that moment, Canglong's tribulation outside was gradually falling into danger.

In a cultivator's life, if they proceed smoothly, they will experience three nine-fold heavenly tribulations, three times in total, each with three rounds.

Canglong had already passed the first two rounds, but the third round had yet to descend, only building up power in an endless wait.

In the unbearable waiting, Canglong gasped for breath, her heart sinking lower and lower.

Just the first two rounds had completely surpassed the full three rounds she had experienced back then.

She couldn't imagine what power the final round would possess!

Bolts of lightning as thick as water buckets flickered continuously within the clouds, gradually revealing their forms.

A total of nine bolts!

Canglong drew a sharp breath, and the onlookers nearby erupted in an uproar.

How could there be such a terrifying lightning tribulation?

After a long buildup, the heavenly punishment no longer gave people time to react.

Nine bolts of lightning crashed down in unison.

At that moment.

A figure appeared abruptly, blocking Canglong's path.

Five fingers spread wide, then clenched into a fist.

In the next instant, a punch was thrown!

Boom!

The resounding blast left countless people's ears ringing, and the endlessly annihilating electric arcs stung their eyes.

Under Canglong's shocked gaze, Luo Chen bathed in the rain of thunder and lightning. Five sword pellets floated lightly from his body, wrapped in large amounts of molten Nine Yang Geng Metal liquid, and charged straight into the thunderclouds.
