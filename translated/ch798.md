# Chapter 798: Greed Deep in the Soul, Divine Origin Descends

**Second Nascent Soul!**

This term was not unfamiliar to Luo Chen.

In fact, he had once come close to possessing a second nascent soul of his own.

But within the Demon-Fall Land, that second origin pill, nurtured for nearly a hundred years and boasting a cultivation base even higher than his own lifebound golden core, had unfortunately been destroyed.

At the time, he hadn't regretted it much.

For one, that second origin pill had taken the brunt of the Nine-Refined Beacon Fire's attack in place of his lifebound golden core, saving his life.

For another, that second origin pill had also been a major trump card for Han Zhan to seize his body. If he had truly nurtured a second nascent soul during his breakthrough to the Sage stage, Han Zhan could have used a secret art to enter that second nascent soul with his soul, then seamlessly occupy Luo Chen's Purple Mansion, achieving a perfect and flawless usurpation of his nest!

After Luo Chen had counter-killed Han Zhan back then, he had actually felt quite fortunate that the second origin pill had been destroyed.

However.

After seeing Shi Ju's lightning clone within the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, Luo Chen had begun to feel a twinge of nostalgia for that second origin pill.

An external incarnation was naturally a great divine ability beyond his reach.

But a second nascent soul, a step lower, was also an extremely formidable technique within the cultivation world.

Nostalgia was, in the end, just nostalgia.

The Origin Pill Art could only be cultivated during the Adept stage.

Forcibly cultivating the Origin Pill Art at the Sage stage would not allow one to condense a second nascent soul, as it lacked the heavenly infusion that followed the tribulation lightning of breaking through a major realm.

He could only watch this art from afar, at most selecting suitable talents within the Luo Tian Sect to try cultivating the Origin Pill Art during their Adept stage.

To this end, he had even specifically used his divine sense to inspect all the Adepts of the Luo Tian Sect.

Currently, the only one who met the requirements for cultivating the Origin Pill Art was Li Yingzhang.

This was because one of the inherent prerequisites for cultivating the Origin Pill Art was having profound and extraordinary magical power.

Li Yingzhang possessed a Ninefold Winding Body. Even though his foundation wasn't as perfect as it had been during his Initiate stage, his magical power still surpassed that of his peers.

Luo Chen planned to let him learn this secret art only after his cultivation reached the mid-Adept stage.

Of course, Luo Chen would not do anything like seizing a body, so he naturally wouldn't leave any backdoors in the art.

That was a digression.

After Luo Chen had refined the Nine Separation Pill for Nine Spirit Primogenitor, the long-dead desire for a second nascent soul in Luo Chen's heart had flared back to life.

If he could separate a "spirit infant" and then combine it with the divided soul split from the *Soul-Splitting Art*, wouldn't the two together achieve the same effect as a true second nascent soul, just through different means?

Of course, this was merely a sudden conjecture on Luo Chen's part.

It had no foundation, no corresponding techniques or secret arts, and he didn't even have a Nine Separation Pill of his own.

Moreover, the number of difficulties involved was not something Luo Chen could solve.

For example, how to cultivate something similar to the nine fleshy tumors on Nine Spirit Primogenitor's nascent soul!

Or, for another example, when separating the fleshy tumor, could one rely solely on pills to avoid harming the original nascent soul?

The former was the threshold, the foundation, but the latter could potentially destroy the fruits of his two hundred years of bitter cultivation… no, adding the fifty years in the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, that was nearly three hundred years of hard work. If he didn't handle it properly, everything he had could be ruined in an instant!

"I'll study it bit by bit!"

"If possible, asking Nine Spirit Primogenitor for some related basics might also provide some inspiration."

In the end, Luo Chen couldn't suppress the greed in his heart.

The prospect of a second nascent soul, or even multiple nascent souls, was simply too wonderful.

If successful, it wouldn't just be a simple matter of increasing his magical power like Nine Spirit Primogenitor, or cultivating a single great technique.

For him, if he truly succeeded, combined with his proficiency panel, he might actually be able to achieve mastery in all professions and all schools!

What a terrifying future that would be!

How could he not be tempted by it?

…

"Husband, I'm back."

"Qin'er, did you find out about Xiaojiu's situation?"

"I did. He hasn't disappeared recently. He's been following the Supreme Elder, learning the art of talisman crafting."

"Master is also proficient in the talisman dao?"

In a secluded cave dwelling, Qu Lingjun revealed a surprised expression.

Meng Qin'er spoke with an admiring tone: "I didn't quite believe it at first either, but a batch of exceptionally high-quality second-grade talismans has indeed appeared at Sect Leader Li's place. In my opinion, the craftsmanship of those talismans is even superior to some of the high-quality talismans that the Divine Talisman Sect specifically supplies for auctions."

Qu Lingjun blinked, then let out a silent laugh.

As expected of his master, he truly knew everything and nothing could stump him.

Seeing Qu Lingjun smile, Meng Qin'er felt quite helpless.

"Husband, you can still laugh?"

Qu Lingjun smiled gently. "Why can't I laugh? Master treats my son and me so well; this is genuine happiness from the bottom of my heart!"

"Treats you well?" Meng Qin'er's delicate brows furrowed slightly as she glanced around. "You mean sending you to this place far from the sect's core, instead of letting you retake control of the Pill Hall?"

Qu Lingjun shook his head and said calmly, "After a year of solitude, I've gradually come to understand. Master seems to be neglecting me, but in truth, he's giving me an opportunity."

"An opportunity?"

"Mm. Think about it. If I were in charge of the Pill Hall, with power in my hands, what would I do during this period when the Luo Tian Sect is recovering from a hundred ruins?"

Meng Qin'er answered without thinking: "Naturally, you would refine pills as much as possible, guide the juniors, restore the reputation you lost before, establish authority among your fellow disciples, and earn everyone's love and respect."

Qu Lingjun smiled. "And a hundred years from now… no, forty years from now?"

Meng Qin'er fell silent.

She naturally understood his meaning.

If it were truly like that, forty years later, when Qu Lingjun's lifespan was exhausted, all glory and disgrace would turn into a handful of yellow earth.

But now, although he bore the misunderstanding of some people and was sent to a remote place far from the sect's core, it gave Qu Lingjun an environment to set aside all worldly affairs and cultivate in peace!

Her husband's master truly had good intentions!

But!

Meng Qin'er looked around very dissatisfied. "Master is indeed good to you, but the spiritual energy concentration in this cave dwelling is only at the mid-second-grade. Your spirit root, husband, has four attributes; it simply can't help you quickly improve your realm. I'm afraid that when your lifespan runs out, it will be difficult for you to even reach the Great Circle of the Initiate stage, let alone charge towards the Adept stage."

At this, Qu Lingjun could only sigh.

This was all the Luo Tian Sect could offer at the moment.

Not to mention him, even the Adept cultivators within the sect had to negotiate the use of third-grade cave dwellings.

He was only at the fifth layer of the Initiate stage. Being allocated a mid-second-grade cave dwelling was already a special arrangement by Li Yingzhang, considering his master's face.

Moreover, his master hadn't just thrown him aside and ignored him completely.

The storage pouch that Li Yingzhang had given him back then contained many second-grade high-grade pills, all of the cultivation type!

Not only that, it also included a copy of the *Pure Source Marvelous Pill Art* annotated with Luo Chen's personal cultivation insights.

The intention was clear!

Given the limited conditions, his master's support for him was already maximal.

"I don't mind for myself. I only feel wronged for you, Qin'er, having to endure this hardship with me."

He reached out, stroking the woman's soft cheek, his expression full of emotion.

She had refused to divorce him, even abandoning the comfortable environment of the Splendid Cloud Palace to accompany him through the last few decades of his life.

Added to that was her willingness to fight for the Luo Tian Sect before.

This genuine affection had completely erased the estrangement that had existed between him and Meng Qin'er.

Meng Qin'er rarely saw Qu Lingjun so gentle, and her cheeks flushed.

"You go cultivate. I need to do my evening cultivation practice too."

"Mm."

Qu Lingjun smiled and entered the cultivation chamber.

But in less than the time it takes to drink a cup of tea, he came out with a look of surprise.

"Qin'er, come in and sense this for me. See if I'm mistaken."

"What's wrong?"

Meng Qin'er was puzzled but followed him into the cultivation chamber.

"Oh?"

In an instant, Meng Qin'er noticed the difference from usual.

"Why is the spiritual energy concentration so much stronger?"

Qu Lingjun quickly nodded. "Exactly, I wasn't mistaken. The spiritual energy concentration here is indeed a small grade higher than before. I even feel it's still increasing at a faint rate."

Meng Qin'er grabbed his hand. "If you have a high-second-grade spiritual energy environment, husband, your hope of breaking through to the late Initiate stage is much greater!"

Qu Lingjun swallowed hard, his gaze instinctively turning towards the direction of Luo Chen's residence.

"Is this Master's doing?"

…

Time passed bit by bit.

The cultivators of the Luo Tian Sect also began to gradually notice the strange changes in the Hundred Mile Green River.

The spiritual energy concentration was becoming increasingly abundant, and the large number of planted medicinal herbs were growing much faster than before.

Some cultivators, in obscure corners, discovered that new second-grade spiritual energy springs had formed.

Several late Initiate cultivators in their high-second-grade cave dwellings suddenly found a massive influx of spiritual energy, pushing the concentration all the way to the third grade. This forced them to leave their caves to avoid affecting their physical bodies during cultivation.

All these anomalies were reported to Sect Leader Li Yingzhang.

Li Yingzhang was also astonished and once went to consult Luo Chen.

But the answer he received was to handle it as he saw fit and allocate the corresponding cave dwellings reasonably.

This calm answer undoubtedly declared that this strange change was Luo Chen's doing.

This made Li Yingzhang even more in awe and admiration of Luo Chen's methods!

He immediately made the corresponding arrangements, and even went a step further. He specially selected a few high-quality high-second-grade cave dwellings and asked Min Longyu to set up spirit-gathering formations, attempting to actively guide the cave dwellings to upgrade.

These changes were unknown to the outside world.

Even when Nine Spirit Primogenitor, summoned by Luo Chen, stepped into the Hundred Mile Green River again, he didn't notice anything amiss.

After all, it was just a third-grade spiritual vein!

Whether a bit stronger or a bit weaker, it was insignificant for the Sage stage.

At nightfall, the clear moonlight was bitingly cold.

In the height of summer, some prematurely ripened first-grade copper coin grass emitted a golden light under the moonlight.

When Nine Spirit Primogenitor saw Luo Chen, even someone as composed as him couldn't help but feel a little impatient.

"Is it done?"

Luo Chen first returned the bronze figure inscribed with the pill formula to him, then nodded confidently.

"If it weren't done, why would I have called you here?"

Nine Spirit Primogenitor finally breathed a sigh of relief.

This was the last batch of raw materials for the Nine Separation Pill that he had gathered with all the resources of the Nine Spirit Sect.

Before this, he had already sought out several fourth-grade alchemists, but all had failed.

If this attempt failed again, he wouldn't be able to gather the corresponding materials again anytime soon.

Seeing the other's relieved expression, Luo Chen suddenly asked:

"Before I hand over the pills, I must ask you one thing. Do you know the contraindications for taking this pill?"

Nine Spirit Primogenitor was startled, not understanding his meaning.

Luo Chen explained: "The Nine Separation Pill is different from any pill I have ever refined. Its medicinal power is extremely aggressive. In fact, when the pill was formed, it nearly harmed the assistant who was helping me refine it. For such a domineering pill to be applied to a nascent soul, the consequences…"

So he was worried about him!

Nine Spirit Primogenitor chuckled. "There are a few records of taking this pill in my sect's ancient texts, left by the founding ancestor. It is said that when taking the pill, it will cause the spirit embryo to grow wildly in a short period. Taking this opportunity, I can then separate it and form an independent spirit infant."

His tone was quite confident.

Luo Chen asked casually: "So those fleshy tumors are the spirit embryos? Such a miraculous creation, I wonder how you managed to condense them, Fellow Daoist?"

Nine Spirit Primogenitor's smile faded as he looked coldly at Luo Chen. "Fellow Daoist, you are overstepping your bounds."

Instantly, the atmosphere turned icy.

Luo Chen laughed it off. "Why be so sensitive? I was just thinking of you… I spoke out of turn."

Luo Chen sighed inwardly. This Nine Spirit Primogenitor was indeed not easy to fool!

He had wanted to probe for some basic experience.

In the past month, he had studied the Nine Separation Pill and that bronze figure, but had found nothing.

Now that Nine Spirit Primogenitor was so vigilant, perhaps he truly wasn't meant to have this opportunity!

Luo Chen didn't want to ruin the reputation he had painstakingly built. He took out nine jade bottles from his storage ring.

Each bottle contained a single Nine Separation Pill, to prevent them from affecting each other.

Seeing the nine jade bottles, Nine Spirit Primogenitor's eyes immediately lit up.

But just as he reached out to take them, Luo Chen suddenly withdrew his hand.

"Fellow Daoist, you!"

"You brought someone with you?"

Luo Chen's eyes narrowed as he took a step forward.

In the blink of an eye, he was at the summit of the Great Green Mountain.

His sharp eyes looked down.

By the Blackwater River, a figure shrouded in a black robe was standing silently, without anyone knowing when they had arrived.

The figure was extremely gaunt, their thin shoulders barely able to support the loose black robe. When the night wind blew by the riverbank, it occasionally revealed withered arms like chicken claws.

But it was this kind of gaunt existence, devoid of any aura, who, when quietly looking up at Luo Chen, seemed to be looking down on him.

Although Luo Chen was in a position of superiority, he suddenly felt a sense of insignificance.

Their eyes met in an instant.

At that moment, Luo Chen clearly detected a glint of greed.

It was a greed no less than his own desire for a second nascent soul, perhaps even deeper, as if it originated from the very depths of the soul.

It was like looking at prey.

A hoarse voice, as if a throat had been scorched by red-hot charcoal, sounded.

"Are you Dan Chenzi?"

Suppressing the discomfort in his heart, Luo Chen quickly searched for the identity of this person.

In the vast Eastern Wilderness, someone who could give him such pressure, possessed such a cultivation level, and would come looking for him at this time…

Without any pause, a name leaped into his mind.

"Five Elements Divine Origin!"

"You recognize me?"

The black shadow remained shrouded under the robe, but a voice of surprise came from within.

Luo Chen's expression was calm. "The name of Sage Divine Origin is known to all in the Eastern Wilderness. But to come uninvited at such a late hour is not the act of a guest!"

Sage Divine Origin lowered his head, seemingly lost in thought.

Suddenly, he lifted his head slightly.

"If you invite me up, wouldn't I become a guest?"

Luo Chen's heart tightened.

Under the current circumstances, would this old villain Divine Origin be so bold as to try and forcefully make a move against him?

The originally stable spiritual energy within the Great Green Mountain began to stir restlessly.

An irrepressible aura of fierce balefulness stirred from within the earth vein, ready to spring forth.

Not only that!

Another, even more majestic aura soared into the sky, startling the hundreds of Luo Tian Sect cultivators.

With these two auras present, Luo Chen's heart settled slightly.

He slowly unfolded his Sage domain, his gaze cold and sharp as he stared at Sage Divine Origin, and spoke softly.

"Please!"
