# Chapter 1059: Teleport to Eastern Wilderness, Azure Frost Ascends

The north wind howled, snow and ice lay vast and white.

A figure stood at the edge of a blood lake as thick and viscous as a muddy swamp, silently watching the enormous blood cocoon at its center.

The Three Wanderers had once been the cream of the Northern Sea's rogue cultivators, looking down on their peers. But with the Blood Wanderer missing and the Moon Wanderer enslaved, the glory of the Three Great Wanderers had long since vanished.

As someone who had once been ranked alongside them as the "Wilderness Wanderer," Luo Chen had never expected to encounter the last one, the Mad Wanderer, under such circumstances.

Yet they hadn't even exchanged the most basic words. There was only a single glance between them—everything conveyed without speech.

Now, gazing at that thick blood cocoon, Luo Chen knew the Mad Wanderer had reached his most critical moment. If he could pass this trial, new opportunities would surely await him. If he failed, only one outcome awaited the Mad Wanderer—death.

"Good luck, Fellow Daoist!"

After staring for a long time, Luo Chen uttered these words, then turned and left.

After he departed, the blood lake began to stir with unusual agitation. Great quantities of blood water and black qi surged continuously toward the blood cocoon. A vigorous heartbeat thumped out—*thump, thump, thump*—as if heralding the birth of a new life.

...

Within the Buddhist hall of the ruined city of Taohai.

Kongchan sat cross-legged on a meditation cushion, chanting Buddhist scriptures. The atmosphere around him was peaceful and calm, with no trace of the stench of demonic aura.

Suddenly!

The prayer beads in Kongchan's hand came to an abrupt halt. He subconsciously opened his eyes and saw a black-robed figure standing silently before the statue of the Bright King Buddha.

For some reason, Kongchan felt that at this moment, the black-robed Daoist bore a striking resemblance to that Bright King Buddha statue—even though the man was clearly not a Buddhist.

"Master, you've emerged from seclusion?"

Kongchan tried to stand, but the other man waved his hand, indicating he needn't bother.

Then he heard the man's unhurried voice.

"I am about to leave. Will you come with me, or remain in the Northern Sea?"

Leave?

Kongchan's heart stirred, and he immediately understood that the "leave" the other spoke of meant not just leaving the Arctic Yemo Heaven, but departing the Northern Sea entirely.

He hesitated.

Luo Chen didn't let him ponder for long. He offered a condition.

"Where you are doesn't really matter. What matters is that, before long, you will need to make a move for me. Just once. After you succeed, I will release your soul restriction, and you will be free. The outside world will never know you once submitted to a human cultivator."

Kongchan's eyes lit up at once. He asked directly, "May I ask who Master wishes to deal with?"

"The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!"

Those four words, spoken so lightly, now weighed like a mountain on Kongchan's heart, nearly suffocating him.

"This..."

"Think it over carefully. I hope to see your figure on the battlefield when the time comes."

Luo Chen chuckled softly, then turned and walked out.

He wasn't forcing the issue.

Unlike Black King and Little White, the demon monk Kongchan's "master-servant" relationship with him wasn't deep. Even if he compelled the man to act, there was a good chance he would go through the motions without putting in real effort.

But if the man chose to act of his own accord, he would surely give his all.

Luo Chen believed Kongchan would make the right choice. After all, if Luo Chen failed, the soul backlash Kongchan would suffer would be no small matter either.

A hesitant voice came from behind.

"Time? Place?"

"Ten years from now, at the sect's ancestral court on the Eastern Origin Continent!"

After these words, the Buddhist hall was empty of Luo Chen's figure. Kongchan sat on his meditation cushion, his expression shifting between light and dark.

For a long time, the sound of chanting scriptures and praising the Buddha rose again in the hall.

Only now, the chanting was unusually heavy.

...

"Master, how are we going back? Do we follow the same route?"

After leaving the ruined city of Taohai, Little White asked this question with concern.

Black King also popped out and said in his simple voice, "That would be too slow. And we'd have to avoid the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's eyes and ears."

Little White snorted. "Do you have a better idea?"

Black King's words caught in his throat.

Unexpectedly, Luo Chen said, "There is indeed a faster way back."

With that, Luo Chen released the Frost Eagle Shuttle and stepped onto it in one stride.

The shuttle turned, heading east.

That was a course to cross the entire Northern Sea! The distance was no shorter than the original route back, after all—the Arctic Yemo Heaven was already at the northernmost point of the Northern Sea.

But Black King seemed to guess something when he saw this.

"Master, do you intend to..."

"Mm."

Luo Chen nodded lightly, acknowledging the other's guess.

If they took that route, they could save at least half the time.

Only Little White remained confused.

Black King chuckled but didn't explain to her. Instead, he emerged and said to Luo Chen, "Master, while you were in seclusion, we thoroughly searched the Primordial Demon Sect ruins and found quite a few good things!"

As he spoke, he spat out a pile of miscellaneous items from his mouth, like a child showing off treasures.

Luo Chen wasn't particularly interested. With his current strength and realm, ordinary treasures didn't catch his eye. Moreover, back when the demon race was trapped in Yemo Heaven for two hundred years, even if the Primordial Demon Sect had left behind any rare treasures, they would have long been plundered clean by the demon strongmen.

So the "good things" Black King spoke of were bound to be ordinary goods.

Yet as Luo Chen's gaze swept over the pile of miscellaneous items, his eyes suddenly moved. He reached out and summoned a statue.

"Oh!"

Black King said proudly, "Those young demons did search thoroughly, but there were always places they missed. I dug this statue out from deep underground. Little White and I studied it—there might be a body-tempering technique recorded on it. I know Master likes collecting various body-tempering techniques, so I specially kept it."

"You've done well."

Luo Chen hummed in acknowledgment, carefully stroking the statue in his hands.

The thing carved was quite eerie. It was clearly human, yet had two horns on its head. And on its pitch-black body, blood vessels were crystalline and translucent, covered by a layer of bloody light.

It didn't match any of the demon races recorded in Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's scriptures, but it was definitely neither human nor demon.

"What the hell is this thing carved as?"

At first glance, the statue gave off a feeling of inexhaustible blood qi and immense physical power!

Black King had reason to say it recorded a body-tempering technique.

But this body-tempering technique wasn't fully displayed—it seemed to require some kind of secret method.

Luo Chen calmed his mind and began to study it carefully.

...

Half a year later.

On the Frost Eagle Shuttle, Luo Chen held the "demon statue," his gaze focused.

He infused it with mana, but the statue didn't move.

Luo Chen applied source power, and the bloody light suddenly began to flow, surging along those blood vessels.

But when it reached a critical node, the bloody light abruptly stopped.

Clearly, mana couldn't activate this artifact. Even source power, condensed from blood qi, could only barely activate it, unable to manifest the true technique.

Luo Chen had roughly guessed what was needed to activate it.

One would need pure demonic qi to activate this demon statue and manifest the complete technique. Otherwise, even wild beast source power could only barely complete a small portion of the circulation route.

But in the present age, who possessed such pure demonic qi?

For a moment, two figures appeared in Luo Chen's mind.

One was the Mad Wanderer, who had been lying in the blood lake of Yemo Heaven not long ago. If he successfully transformed his demonic constitution, producing demonic qi would be only natural.

The other was Ning Yuzhuo, whom he had met in the Southern Frontier back then. That fellow should be a true disciple of the Primordial Demon lineage.

"I'll look for those two when I have time!"

Luo Chen put away the demon statue, and then Black King's voice came from outside.

"Master, we've reached the Swallow Islands."

Luo Chen walked out slowly, standing on the shuttle suspended nine thousand li in the sky, his gaze fixed far below.

In his divine sense, a woman in a blue dress was quietly and serenely tending to medicinal herbs in a garden. Beside her, a small lake was filled with spirit fish swimming freely to and fro.

"Mid-stage Adept. In these hundred years, Haixin hasn't slacked off in her cultivation!"

Black King tested the waters. "Master, do you want to go see that mistress?"

Luo Chen pondered for a moment, then shook his head.

"Forget it!"

"My own future is still uncertain. Why drag her into this? Besides, with Haixin's current strength, she wouldn't be of much help to me."

"Let her live here in peace!"

With that, Luo Chen turned the shuttle and headed east.

Little White finally realized something. She looked toward that archipelago shaped like a spreading swallow, and her divine sense vaguely observed a woman with beautiful features and a curvaceous figure.

That should be the Dao companion Black King had mentioned earlier—the master's partner in the Northern Sea.

Was her name Cheng Haixin?

As Little White speculated, the Frost Eagle Shuttle moved at great speed, already leaving the Swallow Islands behind. Moments later, it descended on a nameless islet where seabirds circled and water vapor was abundant.

Little White didn't understand. Why stop here?

"Master, aren't we returning to the Eastern Wilderness?"

Luo Chen put away the Frost Eagle Shuttle, smiled slightly, and walked into the small hill at the center of the islet.

"The road home is inside!"

Pushing aside the vines, he entered a cave. An ancient yet ornate teleportation array sat quietly there.

Luo Chen bent down and carefully inspected it, then stood up satisfied.

"Just as I expected. Back when Rosycloud returned from the Northern Sea to the Eastern Wilderness, she repaired this teleportation array."

Luo Chen turned to look at the one person and one ghost.

This kind of cross-continent ultra-long-distance teleportation array was convenient, but it also had high requirements for its "passengers."

Back then, he and Han Zhan had only barely succeeded in teleporting here thanks to the Azure Luan feather.

Now he had to bring these two with him. Without the Azure Luan feather's help, he would have to rely on his own strength.

Facing Luo Chen's contemplative gaze, Black King reacted first. He coiled around Luo Chen's arm and looked up.

"Master, don't worry. I won't let the tragedy of that teleportation to the Central Continent happen again."

Luo Chen was speechless.

Teleportation accidents had indeed happened once before.

That time, without informing Fu Chaosheng and the others, he had quietly brought Black King from the Northern Sea to the Central Continent, and it had led to a disaster.

The large number of dragon earthworms Black King carried had been crushed to death by the teleportation array's pressure. When they arrived, the massive amount of dragon earthworm blood had directly contaminated a teleportation array in the Central Continent.

For that, the Fu family had paid no small compensation.

That tragedy of blood staining the Central Continent had seemed ominous to some, as if it were a blood disaster.

Now it seemed that tragedy had already foreshadowed the future. This time, when he returned, wasn't he targeting the Central Continent, bringing a storm of blood to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect?

Luo Chen exhaled a turbid breath and beckoned to Little White.

"Let's go!"

His whole body shook, and vast mana surged out, completely enveloping Little White. He also attached a thread of mana to Black King.

Casually, he tossed out a few top-grade spirit stones, striking them into the array's grooves.

With a thought, the array rumbled to life.

*Boom!*

A massive pillar of light shot up into the sky, tearing through the void, then vanished in an instant.

On Moon Inviting Island, in the Ten-Mile Calm Lake within the Swallow Islands, several hundred li away from this nameless islet, the blue-clad woman was diligently tending to the medicinal herbs in her garden.

She wasn't skilled in alchemy. Growing these herbs was merely a way to pass the time.

If possible, when she saw Luo Chen again, she could give him a little surprise—after all, he was an alchemist.

Some of the herbs in this garden were even extinct varieties, which she had cultivated with great effort.

But when she would see him again was something Cheng Haixin couldn't know.

Suddenly!

She subconsciously looked up, gazing into the distance.

A pillar of light shot up into the sky, tearing through the void toward the east. Though it vanished in an instant, she still caught a glimpse of it.

For some reason, the moment she saw that pillar of light, her heart clenched for a moment.

"How strange."

Cheng Haixin muttered to herself, set down her work, and flew on her light toward where the pillar of light had appeared.

Soon, she landed on the nameless islet and walked nervously into the cave.

A familiar scent rushed toward her.

The woman's lips pressed tightly together.

...

A teleportation array could be seen as an upgraded version of a large transposition formation.

It didn't simply throw a person from one end to the other without reason, but transported them through the void.

However, due to the enormous spatial pressure, many people couldn't stay conscious during the process, giving them the illusion of instantaneous movement.

Luo Chen had grown accustomed to that feeling, expecting to wake up only when he arrived at Green Parasol Mountain.

But this time was completely different.

He remained conscious!

He clearly saw how he was shuttling through the void. One spatial node after another appeared before his eyes. Layer upon layer of spatial fluctuations rippled outward like water rings.

This wondrous experience wasn't just because he had condensed his primordial spirit before teleporting, allowing him to observe with greater detail, but also because of a unique talent.

"Is this the spatial talent I gained from Azure Frost?"

"But it was never this obvious before!"

As his thoughts turned, Luo Chen's gaze was profound, as if he could pierce through the void itself.

Little White beside him had already fainted. Black King was slightly better, but his physical body had begun to tremble. Luo Chen specially supplemented him with some mana.

And inside his sleeve, the Soul-Refining Banner gave a faint *crack*, but the sound vanished in an instant.

With the power of a fifth-rank Kui Niu ghost spirit, it was enough to endure this kind of ultra-long-distance teleportation.

"Fairly smooth!"

Amidst the upheaval of heaven and earth, Luo Chen held Little White and Black King in his two hands, and his figure materialized on the underground teleportation platform.

Luo Chen looked around and breathed a slight sigh of relief.

Green Parasol Mountain had arrived!

Light footsteps sounded. Luo Chen swept his divine sense and already knew who was coming.

Nether Spring, the deputy hall master of Truth Crossing Hall.

At this point, Luo Chen should have withdrawn his divine sense.

But he inexplicably paused his movement, subconsciously looking upward.

A clear, cold, and familiar divine sense met his exactly.

Luo Chen's lips parted slightly. In surprise, he said, "You've ascended?"
