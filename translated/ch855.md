# Chapter 855: Blades and Halberds Will Cease, Drifting Away (Thanks to Big Shot Guo Jiuhang for the Silver Alliance Reward!)

Great Green Mountain.

On the plaza before the Luo Tian Grand Hall.

Luo Chen stood with his hands behind his back, gazing into the distance toward Lingtian Pass.

Lingfengzi and Emperor Slaughter stood to his left and right.

Li Yingzhang, Wang Yuan, and a host of Adept cultivators stood behind them.

All of them followed Luo Chen's gaze toward Lingtian Pass, but confusion was written plainly on their faces.

What exactly was happening?

They didn't know, so they could only wait for Luo Chen to speak.

After staring for a long time, Luo Chen finally spoke slowly.

"Black King, did you sense it?"

As a desolate beast, Black King possessed the talent of absorbing spirit energy, allowing him to draw in the world's spiritual qi even while sleeping.

Over time, this had made him sensitive to a higher level of energy—origin qi.

Back when that old thief Divine Origin had come to the Hundred Li Green River and spied on Great Green Mountain, Black King had been the first to detect him!

Under Luo Chen's questioning, Black King said with some fear, "Yes, I did sense it. Just now, the origin qi of the entire Fenghua Domain was disturbed. Even the spiritual qi of Hundred Li Green River was drained by quite a bit."

To be able to manipulate a domain's heaven-earth origin qi from such a great distance—such means could only be achieved by an Ascendant-level powerhouse!

And for an Ascendant to use such methods, their opponent must surely be of the same rank!

Had Ascendants started fighting over at Lingtian Pass?

Black King was somewhat curious. His master had been strong before, but he hadn't had such a keen perception of heaven-earth origin qi!

Especially since this place had just experienced a great battle, leaving the spiritual qi agitated and chaotic. Others were blinded by the environment and couldn't detect anything at all.

But his master had sensed the disturbance in heaven-earth origin qi almost simultaneously with him.

As for this point, Luo Chen offered no explanation.

Because even he had only vaguely grasped this ability to perceive origin qi three days ago.

But he didn't need to explain to Black King. Instead, he asked without turning his head, "Emperor Slaughter, your expression changed just now. It seems you noticed something. Why don't you share it with everyone?"

Emperor Slaughter was startled and instinctively didn't want to speak.

But facing Luo Chen's unguarded yet mountain-like back, he hesitated for a moment before speaking in a low voice.

"It should be... my father making his move."

Luo Chen nodded. As someone close to the ancient demon Di Tian, Emperor Slaughter's words shouldn't be wrong.

Then his own sensing and speculation were indeed correct.

The others were still in the dark. Who was this father of the spirit pet that the Supreme Elder had recently subdued?

"Ancient Demon Di Tian!" Lingfengzi gasped, giving the answer.

Emperor Slaughter's expression turned ugly.

Those of lower status should maintain respect toward their superiors—how dare they speak his name directly?

The title Lord Pingshan was what Lingfengzi and the others should use.

How could they speak the full name of a father right in front of his son?

The others didn't care about that. After learning this answer and seeing that Emperor Slaughter didn't refute it, their faces all changed dramatically, filled with terror.

They might not know the titles and honorifics of the three founders of the Nether Origin Sect, but the four words "Ancient Demon Di Tian" were known to everyone in the entire Eastern Wilderness!

That was an existence comparable to an Ascendant!

That was the evil culprit who had plunged the entire Eastern Wilderness cultivation world into war!

The once-thriving Luo Tian Sect had been forced to leave their homeland and fall to this state because of this demon!

Now that such an existence had personally made a move, Lingtian Pass...

Lingfengzi's face showed a look of relief. Good thing he had joined Luo Tian Sect and hadn't stayed on that great battlefield.

With existences like that making a move, even just the aftershocks would be more than an Infant Soul Sage could bear!

"He's stopped!"

"Huh?"

Luo Chen repeated without turning his head, "Lord Pingshan has stopped!"

Black King also nodded on Luo Chen's shoulder, "Indeed, he's already stopped. The heaven-earth origin qi in the Fenghua Domain is no longer disturbed. It seems they didn't really fight over there."

Lingfengzi hesitated and asked, "Senior Brother, what should we do now?"

Luo Chen waved his sleeve, "Do nothing. Don't send anyone to meddle at Lingtian Pass either. Just guard the mountain gate. Emperor Slaughter, come with me!"

After Luo Chen left with the gloomy-faced Emperor Slaughter, the others seemed to finally breathe a sigh of relief.

But some were still puzzled by Luo Chen's decision.

Sima Huiniang stepped forward, "Just follow the Supreme Elder's decision. Don't you trust him?"

Li Yingzhang said in a low voice, "It's not that I don't trust the Supreme Elder, but the Luo Tian Sect is too small. If we don't obtain frontline intelligence in advance, then when the time comes, we won't know whether to fight or flee. Is it really good to do nothing?"

Sima Huiniang looked at this third-generation sect master and asked back, "Then what do you want to do?"

Li Yingzhang hesitated for a moment, then said with a firm expression, "Send one or two disciples to risk going to Lingtian Pass to investigate. Even if there's danger to their lives, it would be a contribution to the sect."

"Send who?" Sima Huiniang said bluntly. "On that battlefield, even Infant Soul Sages might not be able to protect themselves. Who in Luo Tian Sect can go? Do you want the Supreme Elder to personally take the risk?"

Li Yingzhang opened his mouth and instinctively looked at Lingfengzi.

This one had only just announced his joining of Luo Tian Sect earlier.

And the Supreme Elder had given him great leeway, allowing him to establish a separate hall to continue the inheritance of the Misty Void Sect.

The others also instinctively looked over.

Facing all those gazes, even from those of lower cultivation levels, Lingfengzi's expression became unnatural.

"Ahem!"

"Don't look at me. I exhausted all my magical power just to survive the hands of the Seven-Rhino Great Demon Emperor, and I haven't recovered yet."

"But don't worry. I still have familiar friends over at Lingtian Pass. I'll ask them later!"

Sima Huiniang and Li Yingzhang exchanged a glance and nodded in tacit understanding.

"Then we await the Second Elder's good news!"

The corner of Lingfengzi's mouth twitched, and he felt both amused and exasperated inside.

Earlier, he had praised Luo Tian Sect's cultivators to Luo Chen as being dragons and phoenixes among men, and now these dragons and phoenixes were using their coordinated skills on him.

Oh well!

Since he had joined Luo Tian Sect, it was time to do something.

...

Inside the grand hall.

Luo Chen looked down at Emperor Slaughter standing below.

"Did you know that Lord Pingshan would make a move?"

"I did."

"Was it for that devoured beast turned zombie?"

"Yes."

"Is Lord Pingshan really that fixated on that one zombie?"

"Gao Chang is different from ordinary zombies. His origin is a desolate beast, and an ancient exotic species at that. Born seemingly specifically to target qi cultivators, he is extremely fond of devouring cultivators' dao fruits. My father once said that this world dislikes the excessive demands of our demon cultivators and human cultivators alike, so it spawned such an existence, intending to destroy everything. If he can elevate that zombie to the Ascendant realm, he might be able to roam the five continents and unify the Mountains and Seas Realm!"

Luo Chen was momentarily stunned.

He had heard similar claims from Lei Daozi before, but the other party had ultimately concealed things.

Only now, under Emperor Slaughter's narration, did he learn what terrifying origins the zombie named Gao Chang truly had.

His eyes blazed as he stared straight at Emperor Slaughter, and the vast divine soul in his sea of consciousness pressed down on a strand of soul blood.

Under his gaze, Emperor Slaughter's body trembled slightly, as if life and death were only a thought away from the other party.

"Master, I am not lying in the slightest!"

Luo Chen slowly released his suppression and fell into deep thought.

Emperor Slaughter breathed a sigh of relief as if pardoned. Only then did he realize that his back was soaked with cold sweat.

That feeling of having life and death in another's hands was truly terrifying.

Suddenly!

A voice rang out in the hall.

"If Lord Pingshan knew that I had enslaved you, do you think he would come to kill me?"

"He would. Father is very—"

"You're lying!"

Luo Chen shouted in a low voice.

With a thought, Emperor Slaughter felt his mind shake, and an extreme pain came over him.

He couldn't control himself, clutching his head and falling to the ground.

Golden fur even began to emerge on his body, as if he would lose control and manifest his true form at any moment.

Luo Chen watched this scene coldly. Although he had outwardly released his divine soul suppression just now, he had actually been paying close attention to the other party's reaction.

When he suddenly posed that question, Emperor Slaughter hesitated for a moment, then gave an answer only after his divine soul had fluctuated violently.

That was not a person's instinctive reaction!

Therefore, the other party was lying!

After a moment, Emperor Slaughter, drenched in sweat and pale-faced, knelt on the ground submissively.

"Master, he probably won't come to save me."

"How so?" Luo Chen asked, then added, "I hope you won't deceive me again. You've seen that soul banner erected over Hundred Li Green River. Our sect has some skill in torturing souls."

Emperor Slaughter swallowed his saliva. He had already felt it.

"I dare not! Never again!"

"Then explain!"

Emperor Slaughter smiled bitterly and slowly recounted the past.

"My father's true form is a white tiger of extreme bloodlust. He grew up slaughtering in the vast mountains..."

Ancient Demon Di Tian, whose true form was a white tiger, was extremely bloodthirsty.

With no one to help him, he carved out a world for himself from the vast mountains of the Eastern Wilderness, bit by bit through slaughter, eventually achieving the status of ancient demon.

In this process, his bloodlust gradually reined in due to threats from the human side, and he moved toward grand strategy, eventually unifying the Eastern Wilderness demon race.

During this time, he fathered three sons.

The eldest was Huang Man, one of the nine great commanders.

This demon had mixed bloodline and didn't inherit Di Tian's talent, but he did inherit Di Tian's early bloodlust, which made him highly valued by Di Tian.

He had even said in front of a group of demon strongmen—"This son is like me!"

The third was born after Di Tian achieved the Ascendant realm, with his favored consort Jinxiu Xian.

That one was a "female"!

Emperor Slaughter called her "little sister."

This demon was extraordinary from birth. Not only was her white tiger bloodline pure, but she also had wings on her back, born exceptional!

Ancient Demon Di Tian loved her dearly, protecting her with utmost care, even keeping her hidden in a grotto-heaven, not letting outsiders see her.

In comparison, Emperor Slaughter was not so fortunate.

As the second son, he neither received his father's attention nor his father's favor. Moreover, because he was born later, he was completely suppressed by his elder brother, unable to build his own power base.

Of course, relying on "my ancient demon father," Emperor Slaughter still lived a good life among the demons, with few daring to provoke him.

Unfortunately, that was among the demons.

In this human-demon war, he had wanted to make a name for himself and volunteered to capture Luo Chen.

But who would have thought he would encounter such a baleful star!

After hearing this story, Luo Chen finally understood why the son of an ancient demon was so pathetic in battle.

Not valued like the eldest son, nor doted on like the youngest daughter, yet having a powerful father to shelter him from the wind and rain. Perhaps his cultivation level could be raised with the massive resources provided by his ancient demon father, but his mental fortitude had fallen far too short.

As for strength?

With such poor mental fortitude, no matter how high his cultivation level, how much strength could he actually display?

And Luo Chen was already at the same level as him. When fighting at the same rank, Luo Chen had never feared anyone!

"Father stopped, probably because he encountered a great power from the Nether Origin Sect. With the war stalled, he must be at his wit's end. How could he care about a failure like me?"

Emperor Slaughter knelt on the ground, dejected, with a look of grievance.

"He probably won't come to save me."

Luo Chen showed a thoughtful expression.

After a long while, Luo Chen said slowly, "You guard this Great Green Mountain for me!"

Emperor Slaughter was stunned, not understanding the other party's meaning.

...

The next day.

Outside the mountain gate of Fenghua Palace, stained with blood, a large group of guests arrived.

Before the guarding disciple could react, she saw a familiar face.

"Senior Sister Meng, you're back!"

Meng Qiner saw the guarding disciple and couldn't help but show a brilliant smile, grabbing the other's hand.

"Xiao Ke, you're safe and sound. Senior Sister is so glad."

Junior Sister Xiao Ke nodded excitedly, "You're still alive too. I'm very happy."

After a brief reunion, Xiao Ke looked doubtfully at the group outside the mountain.

"They're cultivators from Luo Tian Sect, right? Why have they all come over?"

Meng Qiner's expression turned serious, "The earth veins at Hundred Li Green River were destroyed, and the spiritual qi is in turmoil. It's not suitable for living there for a while. So, they want to borrow Fenghua Palace to stay for some time."

"This..." Xiao Ke's face showed a troubled expression.

Meng Qiner patted the back of her hand, which had a claw mark on it, "Don't worry. This isn't something you can decide. I'll go report to the Grandmaster. She'll make the decision."

Xiao Ke finally breathed a sigh of relief.

"Then Senior Sister, go up the mountain first. I'll entertain the Luo Tian Sect brothers."

Meng Qiner hummed in acknowledgment, then rode a beam of light into the mountain gate.

Not long after, the figure of Fenghua Fairy appeared at the mountain gate.

Welcoming them in person!

From a distance, Luo Chen sent out a gift.

Thump!

A huge headless deer corpse fell to the ground.

"Luo Tian Sect cultivators wish to borrow your precious land to rest for a few days. A small gift, not worthy of your acceptance."

Fenghua Fairy raised an eyebrow, "Brother Luo, you're being too polite!"

Luo Chen smiled, "It's ultimately an imposition. Besides, our sect borrowed Hundred Li Green River to live for so many years. One demon emperor corpse is really nothing."

"This gift, I truly cannot accept!" Fenghua Fairy's expression turned serious. "Brother Luo's kindness in lending two spirit pets to help me quell the Fenghua disaster far outweighs my small gestures of goodwill."

Seeing that Luo Chen still insisted on giving the gift, Fenghua Fairy waved her hand.

"Xiao Ke, Qiner, what are you standing there for? Hurry and welcome the Luo Tian Sect brothers in!"

This meant she agreed!

Then, she looked at Luo Chen and gracefully extended her hand in an inviting gesture.

"Brother Luo, please come in!"

Luo Chen was speechless. He didn't persist any longer and followed Fenghua Fairy into the precious land of Fenghua Palace's mountain gate.

Everywhere they passed, though equally scarred, female cultivators could be seen everywhere tidying up. It seemed it wouldn't take long for life to return.

This was the benefit of a large sect!

Vast spirit lands, numerous disciples.

Even after several calamities, the foundation remained.

The reason he had sought out Fenghua Palace was something Luo Chen had thought through.

After many battles, Fenghua Palace's spirit veins might have collapsed, but the main vein still existed, and the number of disciples had sharply decreased.

Making room for Luo Tian Sect's mere two hundred or so people to rest was no problem!

As long as Li Yingzhang kept the disciples in line and didn't excessively disturb the female cultivators of Fenghua Palace.

A man and a woman walked along the rugged mountainside toward a well-preserved peak.

"This great war is finally coming to an end!"

Accompanied by the mountain breeze, the woman's voice was both heavy and somewhat relieved.

"Oh, it seems you know something about the situation at Lingtian Pass?" the man asked curiously.

"I don't know the specifics. I only know that after nine days and nine nights of battle, the beast tides have gradually retreated. I sent a few disciples to investigate the specific situation over there. We'll have to wait for them to return for detailed intelligence. Speaking of which, where is Fellow Daoist Lingfengzi?"

"He went to Lingtian Pass as well, to contact friends and gather information."

"I heard Lingfengzi has joined your sect? That man's escape technique is exquisite, even surpassing mine. Luo Tian Sect has gained another great general!"

"Haha, we're just huddling together for warmth."

Fenghua Fairy looked seriously at Luo Chen, her footsteps slowly stopping at the peak.

"This is Green Duckweed Mountain. It was once my junior sister's cultivation place. There's a fourth-rank lower-grade spirit vein inside. It can be used for your cultivation, and Luo Tian Sect's disciples can also be settled on this mountain."

The cultivation place of the Second Palace Master?

Luo Chen vaguely remembered that the Second Palace Master's title was Green Duckweed Fairy.

The origin of this title was that she had refined a large amount of green duckweed secluded wind, making her drift like duckweed in battle, elusive and untouchable.

Unfortunately, when Jinxiu Xian led a group of demon emperors to attack back then, the Second Palace Master was held back by the sect, unable to fully display her strength, and ultimately met with the end of dao dissipation and body destruction.

Of course, even if the Second Palace Master had been unencumbered at that time, she surely wouldn't have been a match for Jinxiu Xian and the other demon emperors.

"Brother Luo, are you satisfied with this place?"

"Satisfied, naturally. As Lingfengzi's cultivation place, he should be very satisfied too." Luo Chen nodded.

A smile appeared on Fenghua Fairy's face, but she quickly reacted.

"You're not staying here?"

"I have my own place to go. During the days I'm away, I hope you'll look after my disciples a little. If there's any specific intelligence from Lingtian Pass, please also send word to me."

Luo Chen offered a communication jade talisman he had personally refined.

This was a product he had created by combining the talisman arts of Lord Lushan's ten-thousand-li voice transmission from the Northern Sea with his own formation concepts, and then refining it with artifact-refining methods.

Attached to it was a strand of divine sense he had cut off. As long as the other party recorded information inside, Luo Chen could receive it instantly on his end.

However, this kind of thing was one-time use.

And Luo Chen didn't dare give it to just anyone. If the other party used that strand of divine sense to secretly harm Luo Chen with some secret art, the loss would outweigh the gain.

Fenghua Fairy, who had fought alongside him, was someone Luo Chen could relatively trust.

Fenghua Fairy was somewhat puzzled, but she still accepted the jade talisman.

After the Luo Tian Sect cultivators ascended Green Duckweed Mountain, Luo Chen smiled slightly, made a bowing salute to Fenghua Fairy, and then drifted away.

Fenghua Fairy watched that free and easy back, her brows slightly furrowed. She felt as if the other party was on guard against something.

But what could a pill sect capable of defeating a Grand Cultivator possibly be afraid of?

"Safe journey!"

(End of chapter)
