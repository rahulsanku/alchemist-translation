# Chapter 846: Collision of Thunder and Fire

Shouyang Mountain.

One of the few fourth-rank spirit mountains within the Lingtian Pass defensive line.

It had once been the cultivation abode of Elder Lu of the Eastern Sun Sect. Under his leadership, some of Lingtian Pass's harsher regulations had been relaxed, and transactions between cultivators had become more open and aboveboard.

Shouyang Mountain had once flourished like never before.

Elder Lu had even earned the revered title of "Shouyang Mountain Man" because of this.

Unfortunately, after a certain battle, the Shouyang Mountain Man had suffered severe injuries and was forced to return to the Eastern Sun Sect to recuperate.

Even though the Eastern Sun Sect later sent another Sage elder to replace him, Shouyang Mountain had inevitably declined.

Recently, however, Shouyang Mountain had begun to bustle with activity once more.

All because the Pill Sect had issued a broad invitation to the Eastern Wilderness Sages, seeking to restart the Shouyang Mountain Dao Discussions!

On this day.

Elder Jiao of the Eastern Sun Sect stood atop a mountain peak, watching the low-ranking cultivators scurrying about everywhere within the mountain.

An Eastern Sun Sect disciple said discontentedly, "What right does the Pill Sect have to borrow Senior Uncle Lu's reputation to invite all the various Sages? Isn't Danluo Mountain big enough for them?"

Elder Jiao twitched the corner of his mouth. "Danluo Mountain is certainly spacious enough, but where does it have the prestige of Shouyang Mountain, or of our Eastern Sun Sect?"

The disciple was puzzled. "Are we really going to hand over the Shouyang Mountain Dao Discussion Society signboard that Senior Uncle Lu worked so hard to establish?"

Elder Jiao's expression darkened. Then, as if thinking of something, his face relaxed slightly.

"Let them make a fuss for now. In the end, they're just jumping clowns!"

With that, he waved his hand and vanished into the mountain.

...

Different people had different attitudes toward Luo Chen.

After all, he couldn't make everyone like him.

But at least with this invitation, the Pill Sect's reputation had received an enormous response!

On the ninth day of the ninth month, in the 3627th year of the Mountains and Seas Calendar.

Chrysanthemums filled the garden, and dogwood branches were inserted everywhere.

On Shouyang Mountain, crowds surged like a tide, and streaks of escape light wove together like threads.

One by one, figures appeared at the Dao Discussion Society according to the agreed-upon time.

They hadn't come alone; they had brought their most outstanding disciples with them.

"Wait outside for now, and take the opportunity to exchange with others. Fengyue, you will be managing the Splendid Radiance Palace in the future, so building a broad network is your first priority." The Splendid Radiance Fairy instructed.

Fengyue nodded heavily, her mature face now bearing only a sense of responsibility.

After her master entered the central hall, she looked around and finally took a step, walking toward a somewhat unfamiliar cultivator.

"Are you Fellow Daoist Yu?"

...

Leaving aside the exchanges between the younger generation, the Splendid Radiance Fairy immediately sensed the scale of this Shouyang Mountain Dao Discussion upon entering the main hall.

Compared to before, the number of participants had decreased somewhat, but the forces involved were by no means inferior!

Beside her, Lin Qingxuan of the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect and Ye Lan of the Divine Talisman Sect were engaged in conversation.

Her gaze crossed paths with Lingfengzi of the Ethereal Sect, who was alone, his expression desolate.

The Leisurely Crane Sage of the White Crane Sect and the Crane Emperor were walking together, having just entered the hall.

Additionally, the Withered Phase Daoist of the White Deer Grotto sat alone in a corner. It was said that the Withered Phase Daoist was Senior Brother of the Iron View Daoist and extremely powerful. He had actually accepted Luo Chen's invitation.

The Grand Mountain Sage of Mount Tai Sect and the Nine Spirits Primordial Lord of the Nine Spirits Sect were whispering something to each other, their lips moving but no sound audible—clearly having set up a sound-isolation spell.

Even the Medicine Master of Seven Valleys, who was rumored to have a poor relationship with Luo Chen, was conspicuously present.

The only ones missing were probably the Nether Origin Sect cultivators!

Just as the Splendid Radiance Fairy was surveying the entire scene, she suddenly sensed two gazes falling upon her.

She looked up and saw Luo Chen and Tian Duzi at the upper seat, smiling at her as if inviting her over.

Without hesitation, the Splendid Radiance Fairy moved her steps lightly and walked up.

"Fairy, you've arrived."

"Tian Duzi, Pill Sect!"

After exchanging simple greetings, the Splendid Radiance Fairy asked curiously, "What were you two discussing?"

Luo Chen smiled faintly, but his words made her expression change slightly.

"We were discussing that Gao Chang!"

The Splendid Radiance Fairy was startled. "Do you have news of that demon?"

Luo Chen's expression was calm as he countered, "Now that Lingtian Pass is being rebuilt, it should be opening its doors wide. Yet it's heavily guarded instead. Such an anomaly only appeared after the arrival of Thunder Daozi. Do you think it's possible that he captured Gao Chang and is keeping him trapped within the city?"

The Splendid Radiance Fairy opened her mouth, clearly realizing this possibility as well.

Luo Chen said to Tian Duzi, "The Splendid Radiance Fairy knows more about that demon Gao Chang than I do. If you have any questions, feel free to ask her."

Tian Duzi was not one to stand on ceremony and actively began inquiring about the situation from the Splendid Radiance Fairy.

The Splendid Radiance Fairy was generous with her information, recounting everything she had told Luo Chen before.

But Tian Duzi had clearly come prepared, and his questions were quite targeted.

"No true body?"

"Correct. At first, he was parasitizing on another demon emperor named Bai Lan, which caused us to misjudge the situation and cost three fellow daoists from your sect their lives. Not only that, but when he fled, he was hit by Thunder Daozi's killing move, yet he parasitized onto Huang Fengke's body to escape certain death."

"Are his attack methods really that simple?"

"Though bizarre and abnormal, they are straightforward—he simply pounces. In particular, he can ignore protective qi shields, and ordinary defensive magic treasures cannot stop him. Additionally, he seems able to penetrate our cultivators' Purple Mansion barriers, treating them as if they don't exist, and directly attack our Nascent Souls!"

"Does he have any illusionary abilities?"

"This... ah, it seems he does. One Nether Origin Sect fellow daoist seized an opportunity and nearly killed him, but stopped at the critical moment. Not only that, he even turned and wounded his own sect brother, allowing Gao Chang to escape once more."

...

Question after question, Tian Duzi's eyes grew brighter, but his expression became increasingly grave.

Luo Chen watched this and couldn't help asking, "You are well-traveled and knowledgeable. Do you know the origins of this demon?"

Tian Duzi pondered for a long time before finally nodding.

"Based on your descriptions, this demon is no different from the legendary chang ghosts."

"Chang ghosts?"

Luo Chen was taken aback. "The chang ghosts from storybooks?"

"Correct!" Tian Duzi nodded. "In secular folklore, it is said that all tiger demons have the ability to collect the souls of the dead as chang ghosts, hence the term 'acting as a tiger's accomplice.' But in reality, this is a misconception. Just as mortals believe that all cultivators have the power to move mountains and overturn seas, everyone knows that only the top experts possess the strength to hold up the sea. And the ability to collect chang ghosts is definitely not something an ordinary tiger demon can do."

Luo Chen frowned. He believed Tian Duzi's comparison.

Moving mountains and overturning seas sounded simple, but in reality, only Sages could do it easily, while ordinary Adepts often needed to join forces.

And as for the so-called strength to hold up the sea, even Luo Chen himself had not yet mastered it.

Therefore, the ability to collect chang ghosts was like Black King's spirit-devouring talent—far from ordinary.

"The ancient demon emperor, Heavenly Lord, whose Dao name is Pingshan Lord, has a tiger demon as his true form. Moreover, he is undoubtedly the strongest tiger demon in the entire Eastern Wilderness, and even the entire Mountains and Seas Realm!"

Tian Duzi spoke heavily, his face solemn.

"And for such an existence, ordinary beings are not even worth him using such a method. That Gao Chang's original form must have been extremely extraordinary, which is why he was collected as a chang ghost and entrusted to a commanding-level great demon emperor for protection."

The Splendid Radiance Fairy looked shocked.

The fact that Gao Chang might be a chang ghost of the ancient demon emperor Heavenly Lord was already shocking enough.

But Tian Duzi's claim that his original form might be even more extraordinary was even more worrying.

"According to what you say, if Gao Chang has been captured by Thunder Daozi, could it provoke the ancient demon emperor Heavenly Lord to personally take action?"

Tian Duzi took a deep breath. "Why do you think the entire city is under martial law right now?"

"What can we do?" At the thought that an ancient demon might make a move, even the Splendid Radiance Fairy, a Sage herself, couldn't help but pale.

Luo Chen let out a long, quiet sigh, his gaze sweeping across the bustling hall.

"When the nest is overturned, no egg remains intact. Since the Nether Origin Sect dares to take such a risky action, they must have their reasons. And today, we have gathered here precisely to see what their confidence is based on."

"Later, I will inform all fellow daoists of this matter!"

Tian Duzi hesitated. "Pill Sect, won't this offend the Nether Origin Sect?"

Luo Chen bared his teeth. "Who knows?"

...

As more and more Sages entered the hall, and even Divine Fire Sage of the Five Elements Divine Sect and the barely surviving Motian Old Ghost arrived, Luo Chen announced the official start of the Dao Discussion.

Unlike usual, Luo Chen skipped the earlier segments of bartering and cultivation experience exchange, and took the initiative to stand up.

Under everyone's gaze, he spoke in a heavy tone:

"On this Double Ninth Festival, friends should be gathering joyfully."

"Yet after over a hundred years of war, we are mired in the quagmire of battle, and our fellow daoists continue to fall one after another. I recall our last gathering, with the Shouyang Mountain Man presiding over the Dao Discussion, Pavilion Master Li, Heavenly Master Hua, Sage Mu still vivid in my memory..."

"In another hundred years, I wonder how many of the fellow daoists present here will still be with us?"

These words cast a pall over what had been a lively atmosphere.

Pavilion Master Li, Heavenly Master Hua, the three Sages of the Diedie Cloud Sect... which of them were not renowned figures of long standing?

The Shouyang Mountain Man and the Iron View Daoist were also no mere names without substance.

Yet under the torrent of war, they too had suffered damage to their Dao and had been forced to retreat to the rear.

If this war continued to spread, how many of those present could guarantee their own safety?

Someone spoke up. "Pill Sect, your words are too pessimistic, too alarmist. Now that the Nether Origin Sect has entered the fray, the end of this war is within sight!"

Luo Chen glanced at the speaker. It was the Wondrous Sound Palace Master of the Divine Sound Palace.

And the Divine Sound Palace had an excellent relationship with the Nether Origin Sect, second only to the closeness between the Eastern Sun Sect and the Nether Origin Sect.

Luo Chen was not angered. Instead, he asked, "May I ask the Wondrous Sound Palace Master, how many Sages and how many disciples has the Divine Sound Palace sent to support Lingtian Pass this time?"

The Wondrous Sound Palace Master frowned. "Only one Sage, myself, and three thousand disciples."

Luo Chen gave a derisive laugh. "I have heard that the Divine Sound Palace has three Sages and eighty thousand disciples. Since you are so optimistic about this battle, why not send your full strength? Could it be that you do not trust the Nether Origin Sect?"

"You!"

Luo Chen shook his head, not arguing with her.

"We are different from you. We have no mountain gates to rely on, no homes to return to. We can only depend on Lingtian Pass for a last-ditch stand, and so we give our all."

"For this battle, how many disciples, descendants, and bloodlines have sacrificed themselves, pressing forward wave after wave!"

"Yes, the Nether Origin Sect has now joined the battle. But they still hold themselves high above, and when they have plans, they are too proud to share them with us."

"It is difficult for this sect to imagine that when the great war begins, they might treat us as cannon fodder."

At these words, everyone's expression changed.

The Wondrous Sound Palace Master's face turned ashen. "Pill Sect, these are words that cut to the heart!"

"Cut to the heart?" Luo Chen gave a faint, ambiguous smile, his gaze sweeping across the entire hall.

The Medicine Master of Seven Valleys pressed his lips tightly together. The Splendid Radiance Fairy's face was filled with grief. Qin Baixiang of the Hundred Beasts Sect clenched his fists so hard that his knuckles cracked audibly.

Even Tian Duzi beside him couldn't help but sigh.

Their sects had all suffered greatly from this.

And these were merely the surviving sects.

Looking further back, the destroyed Overlord Blade, Azure Sky, Fire Ridge, Taihu Palace, and more recently the Void Emperor Sect, Diedie Cloud Sect, and so on—there was no one left to speak for them.

"Ha!"

Luo Chen waved his hand. "Let's set that aside for now. This sect has something to say at present, and I hope someone can give me an answer."

Amid everyone's curiosity, he recounted the matter of Gao Chang in detail.

Rumors about Gao Chang had actually surfaced as early as the century-long human-demon war.

This time, there were simply eyewitness survivors.

Combining the two, the truth was beyond doubt.

Even Divine Fire Sage, who had always been at odds with Luo Chen, showed a look of hatred on his face upon hearing that his senior brother Jin Ling had been killed by Gao Chang.

However, upon hearing that Gao Chang might be a chang ghost of the ancient demon emperor Heavenly Lord, all that anger and hatred turned into fear.

"According to the information I have received, the Nether Origin Sect has already captured Gao Chang."

"The Dao of Thunder is the most restrictive against yin ghosts and evil demons, so this is not unexpected."

"But what is unexpected is, why doesn't the Nether Origin Sect kill this chang ghost, or send him back to the Nether Origin Sacred Land as a prisoner? Why keep him trapped in Lingtian Pass?"

"What are they trying to do?"

Faced with Luo Chen's questions, everyone's hearts began to sink.

Especially the recent martial law in Lingtian Pass, which further corroborated Luo Chen's claims.

What were the Nether Origin Sect trying to do?

Provoke a full-scale attack from the demon army?

Or perhaps, provoke the ancient demon emperor Heavenly Lord into making a furious personal move?

If it came to that point, they didn't know what would happen to the Nether Origin Sect cultivators.

But their own forces would certainly suffer heavy casualties!

Just as everyone was feeling uneasy, a domineering voice suddenly rang out in the hall.

"What my Nether Origin Sect does requires no explanation from the likes of you!"

Boom!

The tightly shut doors burst open.

A towering figure suddenly appeared before everyone.

Luo Chen sat on the high platform, not rising, merely staring at the man.

His face was somewhat familiar.

With just one glance, he recognized this person—it was the powerful cultivator he had seen cultivating with heavenly thunder in the blue void when he returned to the Eastern Wilderness all those years ago.

If he wasn't mistaken, this was Thunder Daozi, the most prominent figure of the Nether Origin Sect at present.

Luo Chen took a deep breath, suppressing the agitation in his heart, and slowly spoke: "I need an explanation!"

These words seemed to strike a chord with everyone.

For a moment, the momentum that had been suppressed by Thunder Daozi's appearance began to rebound, even taking on a hint of united defiance.

Thunder Daozi frowned, his gaze directly falling on Luo Chen.

"You want an explanation?"

"Yes!"

"Fine. Let me see if you're worthy!"

Amid everyone's confusion, Thunder Daozi suddenly raised his hand. Between his palm, a bolt of thunder burst forth, shooting straight toward Luo Chen.

This move was too abrupt; no one had time to react.

No one expected this man to be so arrogant and overbearing, attacking the renowned Pill Sect without warning.

For a moment, no one came to his aid.

Only Tian Duzi, who was very close to Luo Chen, had been on guard.

With a soft sigh, his mana surged forth.

But just as Tian Duzi was about to make his move, he let out a soft exclamation of surprise.

In his line of sight, Luo Chen slowly pushed out a palm, terrifying mana contained within.

It was his most iconic Azure Yang Great Handprint!

But unlike usual, this palm strike was also entwined with strands of strange blue-white flames.

Thunder and fire collided in an instant.

Then they vanished into nothingness, fading into silence.

Only Luo Chen's calm voice rang out once more.

"Now, can your sect give us an explanation?"
