# Chapter 853: One Finger of the Alchemy Saint, Seven Rhinos Retreats (Asking for Monthly Tickets)

The four great demon emperors—three dead, one surrendered!

Luo Chen sat quietly cross-legged in the void, calming his surging mana.

Golden Tiger Emperor Ditu lay prostrate nearby, reattaching the arms Luo Chen had returned to him onto his body.

Perhaps he was still somewhat unaccustomed to it, but it was ultimately more convenient than slowly regrowing them.

Ditu gazed at the white-robed man so close at hand, his eyes flickering uncertainly, hot breath continuously streaming from his nostrils.

With his experience, he could naturally see that Luo Chen at this moment had expended a great deal of mana, far from the calm and composed manner he had displayed earlier.

Breaking the Nine-Chapter Bone-Dissolving Grand Formation with his sword, while simultaneously driving a precious cauldron and five sword pellets—just thinking about it made one realize how terrifying the mana consumption of such actions would be.

Especially during the battle, Luo Chen had unsparingly unleashed various high-power spells.

Thus, at this moment, Luo Chen might well have exhausted his mana, appearing strong but being hollow within—precisely the best opportunity for him to counterattack!

Yet this thought had barely arisen for an instant before Ditu forcibly suppressed it.

Just now!

He had already handed over his soul blood, and Luo Chen had placed a divine soul restriction on him!

As long as the other party wished, a single thought could annihilate his soul, making it difficult to enter reincarnation.

Faintly, Ditu felt some regret.

If only he had taken the chance to escape when Luo Chen had released his flying sword to kill Lu Changqing with all his might.

But the opportunity had slipped away in an instant; his indecisiveness had ultimately made him the other party's mount.

A faint voice came.

"In your name, order the nearby demon beasts to surrender without a fight!"

"This..." Ditu showed hesitation.

Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes, staring coldly at him.

Ditu gritted his teeth and flew down reluctantly.

He was indeed a fourth-tier demon emperor, and moreover possessed the noble identity of an ancient demon's son.

But this battle had been too disgraceful. Now he had to go and persuade the demon race he had brought to surrender. Even though he had already accepted the reality of becoming Luo Chen's mount, he still found it somewhat shameful to speak of.

Watching Ditu's retreating figure, Luo Chen snorted coldly.

This demon, empty in realm, was in fact utterly vulnerable!

A mid-fourth-tier demon emperor could fully rival a mid-stage human Sage.

But which mid-stage Sage Luo Chen had seen was not an earth-shaking powerhouse in their own right?

Blood Wanderer, Zhou Yunshen, Jin Ling, Fenghua, Pavilion Master Li, Deng Taiyue, Nine Spirits Primogenitor...

How could any of them be like Ditu—unskilled in combat, poor in judgment? Even if he had some techniques, they were crudely employed, full of flaws.

Above all, he lacked a strong heart for battle.

At least the three Lu brothers who had come together still had the courage to fight to the death!

"I wonder if he's been spoiled by that ancient demon father of his?"

Luo Chen muttered inwardly for a moment, then heard cheers rising from below.

Though there were only a little over two hundred people, the sound soared into the clouds!

"The beast tide is retreating!"

"We've won!"

"Look, up in the sky!"

The golden tiger walked on four legs, stepping on golden clouds, and came respectfully before Luo Chen.

What greeted him was a reprimand.

"Why did you dismiss the remaining demon beasts?"

The order Luo Chen had given earlier was for Ditu to command those demon beasts to surrender, not to make them retreat!

Ditu answered fearfully: "It's not that I was unwilling, but the low-level demon beasts are mindless, and those third-tier demon kings were subordinates of the three Lu brothers and did not obey my commands. Seeing that the situation was lost and I had surrendered... they each fled for their lives."

Luo Chen stared at him fixedly, saying not a word.

Only when Ditu lowered his head did he snort coldly and withdraw his gaze.

Never mind!

So many demon beasts were not something the current Luo Tian Sect could digest. If he truly accepted them, it would easily create a situation where the tail wagged the dog.

Moreover, there was another matter to attend to at present!

Luo Chen slowly rose, his pale golden eyes gazing into the distance toward Fenghua Palace.

A battle was still raging there in full swing.

Since she was a neighbor, and Fenghua Fairy called him "Brother Luo," he naturally ought to draw his sword and help.

Just as Luo Chen was about to move, his pupils suddenly contracted!

In the high sky, an ethereal wind came drifting, formless and intangible, impossible to grasp.

Under ordinary circumstances, Luo Chen would have praised: "What an excellent wind concealment art!"

But at this moment, he had no such interest whatsoever.

Because behind that ethereal wind was a vast, unparalleled streak of light, sweeping wind and thunder, in hot pursuit!

A great demon emperor!

"Damn it, how did Lingfengzi lead such an enemy here?"

Luo Chen cursed inwardly, his face already showing gravity.

He had recognized the owner of the wind concealment art who was fleeing in panic—it was Lingfengzi, and the other party was also heading in his direction.

A thousand li, reached in an instant!

Boom...

The ethereal wind arrived lightly, but when it stopped, it shook the void.

Lingfengzi had barely revealed his form before he uncontrollably spat out a mouthful of stagnant blood.

"Pfft... Fellow Daoist, save me!"

Luo Chen looked closely. The other's robe was tattered, his complexion deathly pale, and his usually well-combed hair bun had long since fallen in disarray.

There was a fist-sized hole in his chest and abdomen, blood flowing freely.

His mana fluctuations were meager to the point of near absence.

Was he already in a state of utter exhaustion?

He took a deep breath and asked, "What happened? Why are you so disheveled, Fellow Daoist? And who is the one pursuing you?"

"It's the Seven Rhinos Demon Emperor, one of the nine great commanders!"

Lingfengzi gasped for air, a morbid flush appearing on his face.

"Over at Lingtian Pass, everything has descended into chaos. The battle lines of the Sages are spread very wide. Somehow, that Seven Rhinos commander learned that I was the one who investigated the intelligence on the Overlord Blade Domain back then. He actually abandoned the Mingyuan Sect grand cultivator he was locked in stalemate with and frantically chased after me!"

"If it weren't for the Wind Aperture Pill you refined that allowed me to perfect my life-preserving escape art, I would already be dead."

"Alchemy Saint, you must save me!"

Luo Chen had never seen Lingfengzi display such a "frail" posture, not even back when the Lingtian City Lord had forcibly sent him out to investigate intelligence. He had never been this fragile before.

Between life and death, there truly is great terror!

Seeing Luo Chen silent, Lingfengzi said urgently: "I know you have that ability. On Shouyang Mountain, you weren't even afraid of Lei Daozi. The Seven Rhinos commander has already been heavily wounded by the Lingtian City Lord—he's no match for you!"

"You overestimate me far too much!"

Luo Chen gave a bitter laugh, but he hadn't expected that the other party would run straight to Boli Qingchuan precisely because of his glorious deeds of standing up on Shouyang Mountain.

But he did not refuse.

Only because Boli Qingchuan lay right beneath his feet.

The cheers of the Luo Tian Sect cultivators still echoed in his ears.

Moreover, the abruptly halting Seven Rhinos Demon Emperor had already locked his divine sense firmly onto him.

"Retreat behind me!"

Luo Chen said slowly, violent mana fluctuations beginning to emanate from his body.

His Sage domain burst forth with a roar, and countless free-floating spiritual energy in heaven and earth began to surge toward him.

Lingfengzi breathed a sigh of relief and obediently retreated behind Luo Chen.

Only then did he suddenly notice that a golden tiger, whose realm was even higher than his own, was staring at him gloomily.

Not only that, as Lingfengzi looked around, his eyes nearly popped out.

"This..."

All the shock in his heart ultimately transformed into absolute confidence in Luo Chen.

Those grand cultivators couldn't care less about his life or death; only Luo Chen, who had always acted like a gentleman, might be able to save him.

This trip, he had come to the right place!

Lingfengzi did not ask about the situation here, and Luo Chen had no time to explain.

At this moment, his mana was fully raised, his three souls united.

His realm was completely unshielded!

The vast blood energy in his body was continuously converted into source power, spreading throughout his entire body.

The enemy had arrived!

Boom!

Demonic qi surged to the heavens, demonic wind filled the air.

A figure stepped through the void, arriving.

Eyes full of hatred instantly locked onto the white-robed man.

"You are the Alchemy Saint, Luo Chen?!"

An interrogative sentence, but a tone of certainty.

The Seven Rhinos commander stared fixedly at Luo Chen. "I heard you also participated in the battle of the Overlord Blade Domain!"

"Wuguang died by your sword. Countless of my sons and grandsons suffered your cruel hands!"

"When you are alone, do you ever hear their wailing in your ears?"

Faced with these accusations, Luo Chen remained silent.

The Seven Rhinos commander's gaze swept across the surroundings. Beneath his feet was a panicked fleeing beast tide; on the plain lay a deer corpse pierced by ten thousand swords; by the Blackwater River was another headless deer corpse.

And that ancient demon's son was craning his neck to look this way. Though he said nothing, his eyes were full of pleas for rescue.

This battle record made his heart tremble.

The three Lu brothers were no weaklings!

Even under the ancient demon Di Tian, they were heavily relied upon, usually specially assigned to serve his favored consort, Jinxiu Fairy.

He never expected them to die so miserably.

His face turned solemn as he looked at Luo Chen again.

"Hand over Ditu. I will not pursue past grievances and will turn and leave immediately!"

Luo Chen's lips moved, and finally he spoke.

"What if I say no?"

Just this one sentence made the Seven Rhinos commander fly into a rage.

"Since you are unwilling, then let's settle old and new grievances together!"

As the words fell, vast mana burst forth, and a blue, glistening precious pearl was spat from his mouth.

"Ditu, don't blame me!"

"As the son of an ancient demon, you can die but not surrender. Dying here today is a good thing, whether for you or your father!"

"When this battle is over, I will take your remains back to Pingshan Sacred Land."

Faced with the Seven Rhinos commander's overwhelming momentum, Luo Chen's eyelids twitched violently.

So this was a great demon emperor?

No wonder Lingfengzi had fled for his life without restraint.

His mana was definitely superior to Luo Chen's own.

Especially that precious pearl—it gave him a tremendous sense of threat.

Lingfengzi had indeed brought him a "fine opponent"!

Though shocked inwardly, Luo Chen showed no intention of retreating.

At a time like this, during a human-demon war, how could he negotiate terms with a demon race powerhouse?

If word got out, he, Luo Chen, would have no place to stand.

Only battle!

Luo Chen stood quietly in the void, slowly raising his right hand, fingers together like a sword, gently pressing down.

At this moment, heaven and earth seemed to resonate with him!

Wind and clouds changed instantly, the sky's light faded.

Vast spiritual energy from all directions surged in, mixed with some indescribable, indefinable things.

On the ground, green grass withered, trees decayed.

The demon beast corpses that had fallen in battle visibly turned into piles of bleached bones.

Even the two demon emperor corpses of Lu Changqing and Lu Chang'an had their spiritual light dimmed to the extreme.

It was as if the residual vitality on their physical bodies had been drawn out by Luo Chen.

Everything was restlessly stirring.

Including the Seven Rhinos great demon emperor opposite, whose mana was faintly agitated.

Seven Rhinos showed an expression of disbelief, as if he had seen a ghost.

But in reality, in his line of sight, there was no ghost—only Luo Chen, whose momentum was continuously rising, like a blazing sun.

"This is... the true intent of the laws!"

When this thought surfaced in his mind, that blue, glistening precious pearl had already been eagerly shot out.

Luo Chen paid it no heed, his right hand still slowly pressing down.

Such an opponent could no longer be dealt with by ordinary means.

Fourth-tier master-level spells were insufficient in power.

The Greater Five Elements Sword Formation was incomplete.

The newly created Triple Prison might suppress ordinary experts, but it absolutely could not suppress a great demon emperor.

The Yuantu Sword, which had once slain a Ascendant-level great power, might be able to fight, but the sword spirit had ultimately not fully acknowledged him as its master.

Thus, only the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger!

One finger falls, the myriad things wither!

The fiercely incoming blue pearl visibly slowed down.

The finger, condensed with vast mana, pressed against the pearl, as if engaged in a contest of strength.

Stagnant, unable to move an inch, gradually... retreating!

Behind Luo Chen, Ditu watched this scene in terror.

He had never imagined that Luo Chen, whom he believed to have exhausted his mana and was strong only in appearance, still had such a killing move in reserve!

Amidst the fear, there was also a trace of relief.

Fortunately, he had not wavered and defected back and forth at that time.

Lingfengzi trembled all over, revealing an expression of disbelief.

He had thought Luo Chen would be strong, but he never imagined he would be this strong!

No wonder Luo Chen had dared to connect all sides back then, representing the major powers of the Eastern Wilderness to stand up and force the Mingyuan Sect experts to give them an explanation.

Possessing such a killing move, even a grand cultivator would not dare to easily withstand it, right?

"No, this is not an ordinary killing move—this is a divine ability!"

Just as this thought occurred to Lingfengzi, that blue pearl was forcibly pressed back by Luo Chen.

As if nothing had happened at all.

The Seven Rhinos commander's face was ugly as he grasped the pearl.

"So young, at such a realm, and yet you have comprehended the true intent of the laws, even mastered the corresponding divine ability!"

"Alchemy Saint Luo Chen, your reputation is well-deserved!"

"But unfortunately, you are still no match for me!"

"Today, you... hmm?"

Just as Seven Rhinos was adjusting his mana, attempting to strike again, he suddenly let out a muffled groan.

His ugly face became filled with terror.

He instinctively touched the top of his head.

Blood gushed forth like a fountain!

There, once, was the place of his single horn, which he had deliberately preserved when he transformed.

Unfortunately, during the great battle of the Overlord Blade Domain, that horn had been forcibly pulled out by the Lingtian City Lord of the Mingyuan Sect, leaving him severely wounded.

After years of recuperation, it had already been suppressed.

But now!

Blood spurted like a spring, impossible to stop.

The wound on his head even began to crack and expand.

Not far away, a light laugh came.

"You didn't think that my divine ability containing the true intent of the laws was that simple, did you?"

Indeed, it was not simple. Unbeknownst to him, it had even affected Seven Rhinos' true body, making it difficult to suppress his injuries, and even his physical body felt a sense of withering.

"You didn't think I could only use that move once, did you?"

Amidst the rhetorical question, Luo Chen put away his smile, his expression turning icy cold.

He once again slowly raised his right hand, fingers together like a sword!

Threads of spiritual energy from heaven and earth gathered toward his fingertip, forming tiny vortices.

Seeing this scene, Seven Rhinos felt his soul nearly flee in terror. He no longer dared to linger, turned around, and fled.

He had come as fast as he had, but he left even faster!

Only Fenghua Fairy, who had hurried over, caught a glimpse of that back with a fountain of blood spraying from the top of his head, and couldn't help but open her mouth wide.

"What exactly happened?"

Luo Chen did not answer her.

Spiritual energy vortices still spun continuously at his fingertip, his cold eyes gazing into the distance.

A figure slowly emerged.

He cast a glance full of wariness at Luo Chen, then pursued Seven Rhinos.
