# Chapter 604: What It Means to Have a True Heavenly Spirit Root

```
【Lifespan: 146/500】
【Spirit Roots: Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, Earth】
【Realm: Golden Core Stage Five (30/100) | Archaic Body Tier Three (75/100)】
【Cultivation Methods: Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture (Skilled 201/300) | Myriad Path Convergence (Perfected Mastery) | Myriad Beast Sutra (Perfect 375/500)】
【Spells:
  Fourth-Tier: Dragon-Slaying Art (Perfected Mastery) | Yuan Dan Art (Perfect 375/500)
  Third-Tier: Sevenfold Blazing Heaven Rings (Grandmaster 700/1000) | Life-Severing Finger (Initiate 15/100)】
【Martial Arts: Voidburst Step (Grandmaster 575/1000)】
Perfected Mastery Spells (tap for details)
```

This condensed attribute panel, unseen for years, now appeared before his eyes once more.

Even for Luo Chen — who had maintained a long-cultivated stillness of heart — ripples stirred at the surface of that composure.

After settling into Peng Lake, it had taken only two years to advance from a Golden Core Stage Four cultivation that was already halfway through its progress to Golden Core Stage Five — in a state of near-ideal conditions across every variable. More remarkably, the following three years had seen his cultivation pace not slow in the slightest, barreling forward at ten progress points per year until he stood at thirty percent through Stage Five.

The distance to Golden Core Stage Six was only six or seven years away.

Such a rate, measured against the entire world of Mountain and Sea, would be considered extraordinary.

But Luo Chen knew — this was simply what normal cultivation speed looked like for him.

*Comparable to a Heavenly Spirit Root* was not an empty phrase.

According to legend, a cultivator with a Heavenly Spirit Root could reach Foundation Establishment in ten years, Golden Core in a hundred, Nascent Soul in two hundred — and the further along the path, the faster the pace, with virtually no bottlenecks.

Behind such an astonishing legend lay a series of hidden conditions that outsiders never knew.

Cultivation methods. Spirit land. Guidance from a master. Supporting resources — all of these.

Previously, Luo Chen had borne the name of *comparable to a Heavenly Spirit Root* but his actual cultivation speed had been little better than an ordinary dual-spirit-root cultivator.

The greatest limiting factor was spirit land.

Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake, with its aggregated and upgraded spirit veins, had resolved that limitation.

And so his cultivation had surged forward at last.

Of course, in that process, the massive supplementary role of countless pills — and the rising proficiency of his natal cultivation method — had also been critical.

Especially the latter.

"The Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture is truly miraculous. Not only has it improved the quality of my spirit roots, the mana it cultivates comes out remarkably pure — almost entirely free of impurities — sparing me an enormous amount of futile refining work."

In the midst of that reflection, Luo Chen found himself wondering about the origin of this cultivation method.

Could it truly be a human-form cultivation art left behind by the Phoenix clan?

Without that, it was hard to explain effects this extraordinary.

And this was only the Golden Core volume. What divine arts and dao-principles did the Nascent Soul volume hold? The Soul Division volume? The complete edition?

He shook his head and stopped dwelling on it.

His attention shifted to his physical cultivation progress.

**Archaic Body Tier Three (75/100)**

As his self-created Myriad Path Convergence reached Perfected Mastery, the efficiency of his body tempering had reached its peak — aided further by the Black Emperor Ointment's relentless refining. During these years of travel and movement, his immortal path cultivation had fallen behind slightly, but his body tempering progress had scarcely halted.

In one continuous climb, the progress bar had reached seventy-five, seemingly only one step from Tier Four.

But that single step was clearly not so easily taken.

Luo Chen had already begun to sense it — ordinary Tier-Three Black Emperor Ointment no longer had any effect on him. Even taken in large quantities, progress crept like a tortoise.

This was not much of a problem, however.

His storage ring still held a sizable batch of Tier-Four Black Emperor Ointment — forged from the shells of over a dozen Tier-Three Demon Crab Kings hunted down over the years. With that medicine's support and continued persevering cultivation, the day he refined his Archaic body to Tier Four was only a matter of time.

His gaze dropped to the "sparse" spells section, and he fell into thought.

"Dragon-Slaying Art at Perfected Mastery..."

Murmuring to himself, he raised two fingers pressed together like a sword — on the verge of unleashing a strike.

In the end, he restrained the impulse.

Some spells, upon reaching Perfected Mastery, gave rise to an additional special effect.

Either it was the product of pushing human capability to its absolute limit, or the spell's potential was not yet exhausted — a higher-tier version still existed.

His very first spell, the Fireball Art, had reached a state where no further progress was possible and naturally gained a "fusion" effect, becoming what was now called the Blazing Sun Art.

Over the years, Luo Chen had learned countless spells of every grade.

But those that gained a special effect upon reaching Perfected Mastery were exceedingly few.

The most recent was the illusion technique Mirror Flower Water Moon.

And then — unexpectedly — this Dragon-Slaying Art, sought out specifically to purge the foreign mana from Chu Kui, had also produced a surprise upon reaching Perfected Mastery.

Looking carefully at how it had happened: the Dragon-Slaying Art's Perfected Mastery had been set in motion during the voyage from the Langya to Juingkong Harbor.

The catalyst that completed it was the work done to clear the contamination from the soul-nourishing wood for Han Zhan.

That contamination was no simple presence.

It was a residue left behind by one of this world's peak-tier powerhouses.

To clear such a thing with a Grandmaster-level Dragon-Slaying Art, as Luo Chen possessed at the time, had been like moving mountains — arduous in the extreme.

But after succeeding, Luo Chen had quietly gained two benefits — without Han Zhan knowing.

The first: advancement in illusion technique.

With a thought, depths of darkness stirred behind Luo Chen's eyes — strange visions flickered and surfaced, resolving at last into a scene of drifting spectral shapes and forest-towers of dread.

He had worked that terrifying illusion — the one the contamination had unleashed inside his divine sea — into the Mirror Flower Water Moon.

It was precisely this refinement that had allowed him to ensnare a true direct disciple of the soul-cultivation lineage of the famous Primordial Demon Sect.

At the time, that upgrade had still been experimental.

After years of quiet training since, the Mirror Flower Water Moon's potency had deepened into something difficult to measure. Luo Chen was confident: once this technique was deployed, even a late-stage Golden Core cultivator would find it hard to resist.

The second benefit was also unknown to Han Zhan.

It was the transformation that came with the Dragon-Slaying Art's Perfected Mastery.

*Slays dragons. Slays qi. And now — slays the divine.*

"That contamination was not merely a presence — it was also the residual power of a great being's divine soul. Through repeated severing of it, the Dragon-Slaying Art, upon reaching Perfected Mastery, gained this additional effect: severing the influence of a divine soul."

Though it was a gain, it also came with a notable limitation.

The range of the Dragon-Slaying Art's soul-severing effect was extremely narrow. It could not be projected outward from the body — it could only operate within himself, inside his own divine sea.

Most likely the cause was his insufficient realm, or the fact that the original contamination's illusions had only ever manifested inside his divine sea.

In any case, it was still an unexpected windfall.

Who could say when it might prove its worth?

Luo Chen drew a slow breath and turned to look at the Yuan Dan Art's progress bar, a faint smile touching his face.

He had, in the end, succeeded in cultivating this Falling Cloud Sect fourth-tier secret art without relying on the system.

That Hidden Dust Sand had by now transformed into a Yuan Core, settled within his qi sea.

Not only would it breathe in ambient spiritual qi automatically as he ran his natal cultivation method — it also stored up large reserves of pure mana within itself, available whenever needed.

In short, Luo Chen's mana reserves were now more than double what they had been before.

His mana had always surpassed cultivators at the same stage — both in quality and in quantity. Now, doubled outright, his reserve was no less than that of a veteran late-stage Golden Core cultivator.

Greater mana brought obvious benefits.

Luo Chen could feel it: whether he was controlling magic instruments or casting large-scale offensive spells, he now had room to maneuver. He would no longer find himself after a hard battle with mana nearly depleted, reduced to a craftsman with no materials.

And clearly, the gains from these years extended further still.

He spread open his palm. A strand of deep cyan flame — carrying a faint blue tinge — rose quietly upward.

*The Withering-Bloom True Flame.*

Its quality was visibly finer than before.

The change had not come through cultivation.

It had come through absorption.

In the battle at the Quyang defensive line, he had reversed the cyan fire beads released by the white-robed cultivator and absorbed a large quantity of high-quality Azure Yang Demon Flame.

That prize had sat unused — even Angler, upon their return to Dragon Abyss Immortal City, had never mentioned it once.

After settling into Peng Lake, Luo Chen had spent idle hours studying this so-called demon flame — said to be artificially produced from the vital blood of living beings.

His conclusion was that it was inferior to his Withering-Bloom True Flame.

Having it in his hands was rather like having something of little use.

Yet one day, while forging a treasure, he acted on a sudden impulse and tried using the Withering-Bloom True Flame to refine the Azure Yang Demon Flame — and succeeded.

His reasoning at the time had been simple: if the demon flame was supposedly forged from the vital blood of living beings, and his Withering-Bloom True Flame was capable of burning down living things and extracting life essence from them, then perhaps fire could consume fire.

The result had surprised him greatly.

The cyan fire bead was entirely devoured by the Withering-Bloom True Flame.

And that had only made his origin true flame more formidable than before — in both the casting of fire-affinity spells and the refining of pills and treasures.

With a thought.

The attribute panel expanded again.

```
【Vocations】
【Tier-Two Artifact-Forger】
【Forging Arts: Iron-Smelting Art (Perfected Mastery) | Quenching Art (Perfected Mastery) | Hundred-Tempering Method (Perfected Mastery) | Flowing Fire Hammer Method (Grandmaster 678/1000) | Iron-Tempering Hand (Perfect 400/500) | Gold-Shaving Art (Perfect 366/500) | Snow-Blowing Method (Initiate 79/100)】
【Magic Instruments: Lower-Grade: Cyan Fire Sword | Mid-Grade: Peerless Ring, Jasper Twin Blades | Upper-Grade: Nine Dragon True Fire Canopy, Shooting Star Fire Ring, Flame Dragon Whip】
【Law Devices: Not yet entered】
```

Looking at this vocations panel, Luo Chen felt a momentary daze — as if he had been transported back to the day he first saw his alchemy attribute panel.

Only this time it was the Forging section.

Though alchemy and artifact-forging were not directly related, beneath their differences lay a shared root — the two were broadly similar in form.

Just as an alchemist needed to master foundational pill arts, and a healer needed to master medicine, an artifact-forger also had a set of specialized small techniques unique to the craft.

The origins of Luo Chen's many forging arts were somewhat motley.

Some came from the three-hundred-year evolved techniques of the Duan family in the Eastern Wasteland. Some were generic foundational methods circulating freely in Heavenly Sail City. There were even foundational forging arts from Qingdan Valley, and low-grade puppet-refining methods from the Falling Cloud Sect.

Beyond those, the more advanced arts among his collection came from Polished Blade Hall and the Hundred Craft Mountain on Cold Light Island in the Northern Sea cultivation world.

He had acquired the storage pouches of the younger Wu brother from Polished Blade Hall and the burly man and old woman from Hundred Craft Mountain.

Their inheritance had yielded almost no cultivation manuals or secret arts — but the texts related to artifact-forging had been collected in large quantities.

This was easy to understand.

High-level cultivators had long since memorized their natal cultivation methods by heart — unless they were yet to begin practice, or had specifically recorded them for posterity, they rarely carried the texts on their person.

But books on technical crafts were different. Cultivators of high standing frequently kept them at hand — a craft was something to revisit constantly, and a high-level cultivator with time to spare often cultivated secondary skills, requiring ongoing study.

Thus it was that Luo Chen had profited so greatly.

In a span of mere years, his artifact-forging skills had advanced in leaps and bounds — earning him, among the Qi Refinement and Foundation Establishment wandering cultivators, the informal title of artifact-forging master Azure Yang. And all of it was owed to these inherited spoils.

Even Han Zhan had contributed little to this particular progress.

By thoroughly integrating the foundational forging arts of both the Eastern Wasteland and the Northern Sea cultivation worlds, combining them with various magic instrument and law device crafting schematics, and further reinforced by years of unbroken formation study — Luo Chen had maintained both high productivity and high quality in his forging.

But Luo Chen was clear-eyed about his limits.

With his current level of mastery, attempting to forge the relic shed by a demon sovereign was a fool's dream.

Failure he was not afraid of.

What he feared was wasting the "Xuanyan Armor" itself.

*I still need to deepen my foundation.*

He let out a sigh and dismissed the attribute panel.

As for the Alchemy and Healer sections, he barely glanced at them.

The former two had seen little active investment over these years — only becoming more solid, without meaningful advancement.

The Formation section, on the other hand, he checked regularly, its contents already committed to memory long ago.

In fact, the several high-tier formations required to forge the Xuanyan Armor — he had already begun studying them in earnest.

As the attribute panel closed, Luo Chen caught a brief glance at his Achievement Point count.

**Achievement Points: 207**

An unprecedented figure.

At this sight, Luo Chen did nothing more than smile quietly.

Given his current depth of insight, intelligence, and realm, he could enter most cultivation methods and secret arts without achievement points at all.

Unless they were cultivation methods or secret arts entirely beyond his current stage — or high-tier pill formulas — there was hardly anything that required spending from this enormous reserve.

*Save them. More is better.*

With a quiet laugh, Luo Chen turned and walked into the main hall of his cultivation residence.

Unlike the austere cave dwelling one might imagine, the hall Luo Chen occupied was extraordinarily opulent.

Overhead, a canopy of stars — examined closely, each one was a luminous pearl, representing all five elemental phases.

Underfoot, pale black jade, with what seemed like wisps of jade smoke rising from its surface.

Enormous pillars carved from ancient timber spanning a thousand years held up the sky and earth, running through the entire hall from floor to ceiling.

Threads of golden tracery ran from the jade floors along the pillars, across the ceiling and through each pearl, spreading into the intricate patterns of a formation array.

Beyond that, the hall was furnished with screens, meditation cushions, potted plants, miniature rockeries with flowing water, and more.

This splendor combined the grandeur of mortal imperial halls with the wondrous beauty of an immortal dwelling — anyone who stepped inside might find themselves momentarily lost.

But this was no indulgence in comfort.

In truth, the arrangement carried a deeper purpose.

Luo Chen had invested over a million spirit stones in this hall.

Those formation veins did not stop at the walls of the Star-Dipper Hall — they radiated outward to cover all eight hundred li of Peng Lake.

If someone's divine sense were to sweep the entire lake at this moment, they would find formation flags of every size planted in over three thousand six hundred spots throughout.

This was the spirit-aggregating, grade-elevating great formation Han Zhan had laid for him.

A Falling Cloud Sect technique — and a secret art of the Mingyuan Sect.

This was what had elevated Peng Island's Star-Dipper Hall to a Tier-Three upper-grade spirit land.

And what gave Luo Chen the foundation for full-speed cultivation.

The moment he stepped inside, Luo Chen removed his tall hat, pulled free his hair ornament, and shrugged off his white outer robe.

He let his black hair fall loose behind him.

Lighting a stick of calming and mind-clearing sandalwood incense, he settled cross-legged in easy meditation on the golden cushion.

He applied a coat of black ointment to his body, took an upper-grade True Flame Pill in his mouth, and set his natal cultivation method running.

Enormous quantities of spiritual qi surged with visible speed from the full eight hundred li of Peng Lake toward the Star-Dipper Hall, condensing at last into a white qi-dragon that Luo Chen swallowed in one great breath.

If not for the formation's concealment, the spectacle would have drawn an uproar.

Even the condensation of a Core in Foundation Establishment was not so dramatic as this.

Luo Chen's heart was still as a windless pool — the pain of the Black Emperor Ointment's refining as if nonexistent.

A thousand rounds of alchemy, a hundred turns of artifact-forging — all of it was secondary. Cultivation was the essence.

A spirit land of this quality was one of only two he had ever possessed in a hundred years of cultivation.

He was unwilling to waste a single day.

...

Meanwhile, deep in the ravines and valleys of the Fulong Mountain Range, atop a soaring peak that pierced the clouds.

An ornate cloud-carriage parted the mist and descended slowly onto the summit.

The Moya Cave Master stepped out before his disciples could even form a proper welcoming line — and frowned immediately.

"Still here?"

In the next moment, a figure in purple dao robes with gold brocade stepped out from the cloud and mist.

His eyes were bright and vivid, his gaze commanding and penetrating.

Seeing the Moya Cave Master, he clasped his hands in greeting.

"Fellow Daoist Moya — on your trip to Peng Lake, did you manage to meet with its master?"

*(End of Chapter)*
