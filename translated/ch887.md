# Chapter 887: The Shortcut Is a Dead End, The Complete Nirvana True Scripture

"A shortcut is a dead end."

Such a simple sentence sent waves crashing through Luo Chen's heart, leaving him unable to compose himself.

In truth, he had long harbored certain doubts about his own situation.

Especially after stepping into the mid-Sage stage, those doubts had grown increasingly loud!

The subject of his doubt was that his cultivation speed seemed to have slowed down.

Yes, while others thought he cultivated extremely fast, the truth was that it had actually slowed.

When he first became a Sage, he had advanced like a splitting bamboo, climbing from initial Sagehood all the way to Sage Second Layer in what amounted to not many years.

But now, over a hundred years had passed since his Sage formation, and he was still stuck at Sage Fourth Layer.

Part of it was certainly due to the constant battles that prevented him from settling down to cultivate in peace, but that factor alone shouldn't have had such a great impact.

After all, his supply of auxiliary cultivation pills had never been cut off. Moreover, he possessed a Fire Spirit Body, which was perfectly suited for absorbing fire-attributed spiritual energy.

Most importantly, he had the Purple Mansion barrier—forged during his Sage formation on the foundation of his five-element spirit roots—which allowed him to recklessly absorb external spiritual energy and refine it into his own dharma power.

So where exactly did the problem lie?

This was a question that no one had been able to answer for Luo Chen before, and he had no one to ask.

Until this moment, when this woman standing at the peak of the Mountains and Seas Realm casually awakened him with a single sentence.

"The path is wrong?"

Rosycloud seemed extremely pleased to have obtained the Dao Union Pill formula, so she responded with a smile to Luo Chen's involuntary plea for guidance.

"When you studied the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture*, what was your purpose?"

Luo Chen subconsciously looked at the black crow perched on Rosycloud's shoulder.

"Fire control."

At that time, his alchemy had already reached a respectable level—he could be called a grandmaster alchemist.

But the problem was that the grade of the Withering-Bloom True Flame he possessed was too high, causing him to frequently fall short in the most critical aspect of fire control.

Given this situation, he naturally couldn't rashly attempt to refine a fourth-tier Transformation Pill.

Thus, Nether Spring had bestowed upon him the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture*, allowing him to completely shift from dual cultivation of fire and wood to walking the solitary path of fire.

Rosycloud said matter-of-factly, "Since it was for fire control, why have you instead been controlled by fire?"

"How could I possibly be controlled by fire..."

Luo Chen instinctively wanted to retort, but in an instant, his entire face froze.

At that moment, it was as if Luo Chen had received an epiphany!

Truly, success came from the Withering-Bloom True Flame, and failure also came from the Withering-Bloom True Flame!

He had always been very careful about controlling this powerful source-less fire seized from the Flame Alliance. Especially when it was on the verge of giving birth to spiritual intelligence, he had deliberately suppressed it for a period of time.

When it finally advanced to a fifth-tier divine flame, Luo Chen dared not use it lightly.

At most, he had only fully unleashed it during his battle with Huang Man and Azure Frost.

He had thought that by controlling it this way, he could avoid the situation where the guest overpowered the host.

But in reality, he had already been influenced by the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame without even realizing it.

His main cultivation technique, the *Withering-Bloom Dao Scripture*, appeared to be a self-created compilation drawn from the essence of a hundred schools, but its core lay in the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame.

When cultivating and refining spiritual energy, it always had to pass through the step of being tempered by the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame. His dharma power was intimately connected to the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame.

Even the law true intent that Luo Chen had comprehended was the Withering-Bloom True Intent!

Just as Luo Chen's heart was in turmoil, Rosycloud's laughter still rang in his ears.

"The *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture* is the core technique of the Phoenix clan, capable of directly reaching the Great Vehicle stage."

"For humans, it is no less than a first-rate fire-element technique."

"Cultivating with it grants a cultivation speed comparable to those with heavenly spirit roots. Especially after completing the Second Nirvana and obtaining a Fire Spirit Body, it becomes even faster. After all, a Fire Spirit Body is almost indistinguishable from naturally born strange fires in heaven and earth, so it naturally isn't affected by the slowdown in cultivation speed that heavenly spirit root cultivators experience after advancing to Sage."

"Furthermore, if you had been even slightly clever, you would have naturally comprehended the True Intent of Fire during the Sage stage, smoothly advancing to Ascendant."

"But look at what you've become now."

"Withering-Bloom True Intent?"

"Isn't that a true intent within the wood-element laws?"

"A person cultivating a fire-element technique has comprehended a wood-element law true intent. Don't you find that laughable?"

Luo Chen didn't know whether it was laughable or not. He only knew that he had indeed been influenced far too deeply by the Withering-Bloom True Flame—to the point where he hadn't even noticed it himself.

No wonder his cultivation speed had slowed down after Sage Fourth Layer.

A Fire Spirit Body, yet grasping a wood-element law true intent—with the conflict between the two, it was only natural that his cultivation would stagnate.

And this was a situation he should have realized long ago!

After all, on his attribute panel, the Withering-Bloom True Intent was indeed marked as "Wood"!

But because the grade of the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame was so high, and the divine ability of the Withering-Bloom Dao Art was so powerful, he had deliberately overlooked this hidden danger.

He had been walking on a shortcut, thinking only of overtaking on the curve, leaping over the accumulated centuries of others' efforts.

But in reality, this shortcut was a dead end!

If he continued down this path, not only would he fail to advance, but breaking through to Ascendant in the future would also be incredibly difficult.

Swallowing his saliva, Luo Chen couldn't help but look at that supremely noble woman.

"Senior, is there a way to resolve this?"

Rosycloud looked surprised. "Haven't I already given it to you?"

Luo Chen was stunned for a moment, then suddenly realized—the ray of firelight that had struck into his body earlier was the solution!

But as for the specifics, he would need to carefully comprehend it.

"You may leave!"

Rosycloud covered her red lips with her hand, seeming to be overcome with weariness as she waved at Luo Chen.

Luo Chen looked up blankly. Was this it?

"Where should I go?"

Rosycloud's face showed impatience, not bothering to disguise it at all.

"Go wherever you like. If not for the Dao Union Pill, I wouldn't have wasted so much breath on you."

Luo Chen understood that his visit to Green Parasol Mountain had come to an end.

This was somewhat unexpected.

After all, he had thought he would be staying here for quite a while.

But thinking carefully, from the very beginning, Azure Frost had only said that Rosycloud wanted to see him.

Now that she had obtained the Dao Union Pill formula, he—merely a Sage cultivator—naturally became insignificant to the mighty Rosycloud.

As Luo Chen felt relieved, he also felt a sense of loss.

Walking toward the exit of Tongxuan Hall, he even found himself lingering with reluctance.

If only he could receive a few more pointers from Rosycloud...

But he knew that was impossible. She had repaid one favor with another, correcting his cultivation errors as compensation for the Dao Union Pill formula—that was already quite generous.

How could he dare to ask for more?

As for the true value of the Dao Union Pill formula, Luo Chen couldn't fully grasp it yet. But even if he did, compared to today's guidance, he would still unhesitatingly choose to make the exchange.

If the very path beneath his feet was unstable, how could he speak of the future?

When Luo Chen stepped out of the Tongxuan Hall's main gate, no one stopped him, proving that Rosycloud's words "go wherever you like" were no lie.

But this left Luo Chen feeling lost.

Should he go back?

...

*Duo!*

The crow's sharp beak pecked at the table, swallowing a black bead into its mouth.

Instantly, a surging aura burst forth. But the moment that aura touched Rosycloud beside it, it shrank back, forced to be swallowed by the black crow and refined bit by bit.

"Slow down. This is the soul of the Primordial Demon Sect's Soul-Refining lineage ancestor. Even if it's just scattered soul essence, you can't refine it all at once."

Nether Spring nodded repeatedly. The Primordial Sovereign was indeed very good to her—every time they met, she always gave her some delicious snacks.

Footsteps sounded.

Moon Wanderer entered and bowed respectfully.

"Primordial Sovereign, he has left."

Rosycloud hummed in acknowledgment, not asking where he had gone.

Nether Spring, as if stuffed full, asked lazily, "Has he left Green Parasol Mountain?"

Moon Wanderer replied with some surprise, "No. He went back to the cave dwelling he stayed in before."

Nether Spring didn't seem surprised. "That kid is especially passionate about cultivation. No wonder he reached this level in just over two hundred years. And this is without a master's guidance—otherwise, he might have almost caught up to Azure Frost. Now that he knows where his problem lies, he naturally won't want to give up the excellent cultivation environment of our Green Parasol Mountain."

Hearing this, Rosycloud blinked, a thoughtful expression appearing on her face.

Nether Spring suddenly asked, "Primordial Sovereign, the position of Truth Crossing Hall Master has been vacant all this time. Do you really have no plans to appoint someone?"

Hearing the words "Truth Crossing Hall Master," Moon Wanderer's expression shifted slightly.

Rosycloud said indifferently, "I did have someone in mind before, but she never accepted, and now she's trapped in a net. As for the present..."

As she spoke, her gaze fell upon Moon Wanderer.

The other party remained motionless, but her heart had already begun to race.

But soon, the woman's gaze passed over her, drifting vaguely toward the outside of the hall.

"We'll talk about it later!"

...

Contrary to everyone's expectations, Luo Chen, now free of restrictions, did not leave Green Parasol Mountain.

He calmly returned to the cave dwelling he had occupied before.

The formation activated, mist swirling as the great gate rumbled shut.

Sitting in the cultivation chamber, facing the endless supply of rich spiritual energy, Luo Chen did not immediately begin cultivating.

Instead, he held his breath and concentrated, directing his attention to his Purple Mansion.

There, a bright golden spark was hovering over a cyan-white small tree.

The once-mighty Withering-Bloom Divine Flame now seemed to have encountered its natural enemy, trembling and afraid to act rashly.

This was something that Luo Chen, as its master, had been unable to achieve.

After all, the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame's power had already surpassed his own.

Previously, he hadn't worried about this, even relying on it as a great killing weapon in his hands.

But now, he understood what happened when the subject was weak and the minister was strong.

"You have truly misled me!"

Luo Chen sighed. His Sage infant's gaze fell upon that golden spark.

Rosycloud was indeed extremely powerful. With just a casual flick, she could breach a cultivator's final line of defense—the Purple Mansion barrier.

Logically, Luo Chen should have been alarmed, even afraid.

But her utterly indifferent attitude toward whether he stayed or left made him understand that even alarm and fear were useless.

She simply didn't care about him, so why borrow trouble?

"She said the solution lies within."

"Let me take a look!"

The Sage infant moved, summoning that golden spark.

Instantly, countless bird illusions appeared, accompanied by a clear phoenix cry that resounded through his heart.

Finally, those birds formed into unique characters.

Luo Chen didn't recognize these characters, but as those bird-shaped characters actively flew toward him, he understood their meaning without a teacher.

After a long while, Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes, a barely suppressed joy shining within them.

The subsequent techniques of the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture*!

And not just the Sage stage—even the subsequent Ascendant, Void Refinement, and Body Integration stages were all present.

As for the so-called direct path to the Great Vehicle stage, that was impossible for Luo Chen.

After all, he was ultimately not a phoenix.

But this was already enough!

According to what the technique said, as long as he successfully converted back, he could completely control all the fires of the world.

If he cultivated to great completion, he might even be able to cultivate a Nirvana Sacred Flame of his very own!

Luo Chen didn't hope for some so-called Nirvana Sacred Flame. It would be enough to fully control the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame. If he could wield this flame without any burden during the Sage stage, that would be even better.

(End of this chapter)
