# Chapter 880: A Certain-Death Situation, Heavenly Tribulation Descends (Happy New Year!)

Three attacks, without regard for front or back, all aimed at a single target.

Terrifying demonic aura instantly crushed everything.

*Boom!*

With a single thunderous sound, the earth sank flat, and the void exploded with ripples.

In the twisted space, that mountain of white bones, like a hellish landscape, collapsed in an instant.

The powerful aftershocks of battle swept in all directions like a storm. Those with weaker cultivation were jolted back repeatedly.

Three figures stood tall in the high sky, silently gazing at the center shrouded by endless smoke.

One had a skeletal body and a fierce bull's head, like a demon or a ghost.

One had a face full of ferocity, his sharpness fully exposed.

One had a sallow complexion and a listless demeanor, yet his eyes held extreme brutality.

Because of these three's intervention, the other battle groups had already been scattered.

On the demon clan's side, upon seeing the two newly arrived figures, exclamations of surprise arose.

"Commander Huangman!"

"Commander Bizheng!"

On the human side, the faces of the five Nascent Soul cultivators changed instantly.

"Wasn't Bizheng over in the Five Elements Domain? Why is he here?"

"After the Biyou peace talks broke down, Huangman retreated into the Million Great Mountains. Why did he return?"

"Why are they targeting Luo Chen?"

This last question was asked by Lingfengzi, his voice even trembling slightly.

No one answered them.

The auras of three great demon emperors filled the sky, their respective Sage domains partitioning the heavens, making it difficult for all living beings to breathe.

Qin Baixiang watched this scene with trembling eyes, his steps involuntarily moving backward.

Suddenly!

"This battle has exceeded my capabilities. Forgive me, but I am powerless. I take my leave!"

"You..."

Lingfengzi looked at Qin Baixiang in shock, but the other man had already turned and fled without hesitation, not wanting to stay on this battlefield for a moment.

Although he had retreated before the battle, the strength of the opponents had indeed exceeded their imagination.

Xianhe Zhenren exchanged a glance with Crane Emperor. With their tacit understanding, they already shared a common thought.

Although they hadn't fled in panic yet, they were indeed in a posture of retreating immediately if things turned unfavorable.

As Luo Chen's nominal junior brother, Lingfengzi was now in the most difficult position of being torn between advance and retreat.

He could sense that Luo Chen's aura still existed, but he dared not imagine the situation that would follow.

Should he fight or flee?

His conflicted gaze fell upon Nine Spirits Lord.

The other man stared unblinkingly at the center of the smoke and dust. A sealed medicinal pill was taken out and placed in his mouth, on the verge of dissolving.

"Luo Chen isn't dead yet!"

"This battle, he came for me. Then I, this lord, cannot abandon him!"

These words were spoken with resounding force, causing even Bizheng, high in the distant sky, to look over fiercely.

Nine Spirits Lord showed no weakness. His aura began to rise from slow to fast, and the Great Nine Heavens Xiao River once again appeared beside him, flowing slowly.

Bizheng snorted coldly, ignoring Nine Spirits Lord, and cast his gaze back to the very center of the three's combined attack.

Thick smoke and dust fell from the sky, forming a smoky curtain, making it difficult to see the specific situation inside.

But within, there was indeed a vigorous and flourishing life force, spreading outward without restraint.

A hoarse voice came from within.

"In the past, three human great cultivators besieged Cangwu Qingshuang. Today, three great demon emperors join forces to deal with me."

"What virtue and ability do I, this sect leader, possess!"

"*Huuu...*"

The final sound was like the breath of an ancient desolate beast that once roamed the vast earth. With a single exhale, countless smokes were blown away.

The three great demon emperors looked down in unison.

In a terrifyingly deep pit, a nine-zhang-tall giant knelt on one knee, a blood sword in hand, with a gray small cauldron with dim spiritual light covering his head.

He was slowly standing up.

As he rose, a shocking sight was revealed.

A large number of broken armor fragments were embedded in his flesh and blood. The exposed fragments clinked and clattered under the impact of the fierce wind.

Those fragments were from the Mystic Dust Armor, which had not yet fully recovered.

The nine-zhang giant opened his mouth and exhaled. The Primordial Unity Cauldron, which had borne the brunt of the three demon emperors' attacks, shrank in size and, swaying unsteadily, was swallowed into his Purple Mansion.

He reached out and pulled out a dark yellow armor fragment from his flesh and blood.

The giant glanced at the fragment, a look of regret on his face.

Damaged to this extent, it would probably take centuries of nurturing before the Mystic Dust Armor could be restored.

Centuries?

His cultivation time hadn't even been that long. If he had to wait for the Mystic Dust Armor to recover, it would be better to just refine a new protective magic treasure.

After pulling out the fragment, the rolled-up flesh and blood on the giant's body quickly healed under the writhing of his muscles.

As if the terrifying wounds from before had never existed.

In the sky, the three demons watched this scene, their pupils contracting.

As if they had thought of something.

Ox Ghost said in a deep voice, "I have no mortal enmity with the Alchemy Sect. I only want the Accumulated Sky Flower!"

Bizheng snorted coldly, "Luo Chen is someone Lord Aokuang has personally named for death. He must die today!"

Huangman's face was full of hatred, his eyes fixed on the giant slowly straightening his body.

"The Nether Origin Sect harmed my father king, and this Luo Chen is the most valued existence of the Nether Origin Sect. On top of that, he once enslaved my younger brother."

"Today, old and new grudges will be settled together!"

"I want the Nether Origin Sect to feel the pain!"

The three spoke to themselves, as if answering Luo Chen's earlier words.

But none of them made a direct move, because at this moment, Luo Chen's body was emitting an aura that made even them feel a sense of dread.

*Shua! Shua!*

Luo Chen straightened his body and suddenly looked up, his pale golden eyes like those of a mad beast, fixed on the three.

"Do you really think you have me cornered?"

Before the words faded, Luo Chen stomped his foot.

*Boom!*

The earth surged, as if a ground dragon was turning over.

Luo Chen shot up like an arrow from a bow. Amidst the void's explosive roar, he swung his sword at the three demons.

At this moment, he had abandoned all external objects, relying only on the blood sword in his hand.

Boundless mana was unsparingly poured into the Yuantu Sword. A brilliant sword light slashed diagonally upward.

Faced with this scene of the outnumbered taking the initiative to attack, the three demon emperors remained unmoved. Their divine senses had been locked onto Luo Chen all along.

Seeing him take the initiative, they immediately struck together.

*Boom!*

With a single blow, the sword light vanished without a trace.

And Luo Chen came as fast as he went.

Like a meteor, he crashed back down to the earth.

Huangman watched the other man putting up a desperate fight, a fierce look flashing across his face. "This man won't surrender. To avoid any more complications, let's finish this quickly!"

Bizheng nodded. "After this battle, I will return to the Million Great Mountains."

As he spoke, a black curved blade flew out from his sleeve with a *whir*, whistling at his side.

With a chop of his hand, the curved blade shot towards Luo Chen with lightning speed.

Ox Ghost didn't want to delay any longer either. He raised his white bone staff with both hands, ready to strike.

Just then, a nine-colored radiance rushed towards him from afar.

"Ox Ghost, our battle is not yet over!"

It was Nine Spirits Lord!

Seeing him make a move, before Ox Ghost could react, the other three surviving ordinary demon emperors moved.

However, they did not block Nine Spirits Lord. Instead, they were intercepted by Lingfengzi and the other two.

"Your opponents are us!"

Lingfengzi gritted his teeth and spoke harshly, actually using his early Nascent Soul cultivation to forcibly take on the mid-fourth-rank demon emperor.

Xianhe Zhenren and Crane Emperor each took on one demon as well.

However, their battles were consciously spreading outward, moving away from this great cultivator-level battlefield.

Ox Ghost frowned, pointing his white bone staff at Nine Spirits Lord, who was charging in fearlessly.

"After I deal with this man, I'll come help you kill Luo Chen!"

Huangman glanced at Nine Spirits Lord, a rough stone rod appearing in his hand.

"No need for you. Bizheng and I are enough!"

As the words fell, the three demons split up.

Ox Ghost's staff grew with the wind, reaching a thousand zhang in height. He gripped it with both hands and slammed it into the earth.

Instantly, white bones rose from the ground, creating a new mountain!

This white bone mountain had once trapped Luo Chen inside, forcing him to endure the combined attack of three demon emperors. How would Nine Spirits Lord block it?

Nine Spirits Lord did not flee. Instead, he charged straight into it, the nine-colored light river beginning to wash away the endless white bones.

He could not escape. If he left, Luo Chen would have to face the combined attack of three great demon emperors.

Therefore, even if he had to enter the trap himself, he had to hold back Ox Ghost!

As for Luo Chen's side...

*Clang!*

A sword blocked the black curved blade, brilliant sparks constantly bursting forth.

Suddenly, the black curved blade split into many, turning into endless blade lights that drowned Luo Chen.

"*Hmph...*"

With a muffled grunt, Luo Chen's source power erupted, forcibly forcing back the countless blades.

When he reappeared, his body was covered in dense, new wounds.

Bizheng flew over, watching this scene. Between his solemn expression, it seemed a realization dawned on him.

"As expected, severed limbs regrow, flesh and blood regenerate. I never thought that a mere human like you could possess such body refinement skill, comparable to a demon beast."

Luo Chen gasped for breath. The other party's methods were too formidable.

That black curved blade was incomparably sharp and unpredictable.

He no longer had the Mystic Dust Armor to protect him and could only rely on his powerful physical body to endure.

Although this physical body, under his constant tempering, had become comparable to a true artifact in strength, no matter how tough it was, it couldn't withstand such a relentless barrage of attacks.

*Whoosh!*

A sound of air being torn suddenly exploded.

It was the moment when Luo Chen's old strength was exhausted and new strength had yet to be born.

Above his head, a thick stone rod appeared, smashing down with the force of a thunderbolt.

Luo Chen gritted his teeth and stretched out his left hand, striking out with a Mountain-Breaking Strike.

But it only slightly delayed the stone rod, which still smashed down towards him irresistibly.

At this helpless moment.

"*Roar!*"

On Luo Chen's left arm, black qi surged. A black dragon, with prominent horns and a majestic head, roared out.

With one strike of the rod!

Black King was hit as if by a heavy blow.

But he did not retreat in the slightest. His massive body, like a vine, coiled upwards, wrapping around the stone rod, gradually wearing down its thunderous momentum into nothing.

*Swoosh!*

The stone rod suddenly shrank and flew back into Huangman's hand.

He looked down in surprise. On the nine-zhang giant's body, a small black dragon was coiled around Luo Chen's waist and arm, with only its dragon head exposed, glaring fiercely at him.

"Demon beast? No, it's a desolate beast!"

Having blocked these two consecutive attacks, Luo Chen finally caught his breath and transmitted his voice with his divine sense.

"Black King, I'll entrust you with protecting my physical body."

Black King replied in a gruff voice, "Little Black will do his duty without hesitation! However..."

Black King swept his gaze over the two great demon emperors, his tone quite heavy.

"Master, how did you provoke such powerful enemies this time?"

Luo Chen shook his head and did not answer.

It wasn't him provoking them.

They had come prepared!

This battle would be the most dangerous one of his life!

It had only just begun, and his life-bound magic treasure was severely damaged, while his protective armor was virtually destroyed.

If it weren't for his powerful soul, the backlash from the severe damage to two magic treasures alone would have left him powerless to continue fighting.

In fact, it was precisely because the Primordial Unity Cauldron was severely damaged that his mind was distracted, making him look so miserable when he received these two attacks just now.

But for now, he couldn't afford to feel distressed.

Luo Chen lowered his head and looked at the Yuantu Sword, which was trembling and humming incessantly.

"Today, I must rely on you!"

A fierce look flashed across Luo Chen's face. He bit the tip of his tongue.

*Splash!*

A mouthful of essence blood splashed onto the Yuantu Sword.

Since you like my blood, then drink your fill!

At this moment, Luo Chen could no longer care about the preciousness of his essence blood. He was about to awaken the Yuantu Sword Spirit.

Replenished with essence blood, the Yuantu Sword let out a *zheng* sound.

A flash of red light suddenly appeared, and an unparalleled bloody aura surged in all directions.

Vaguely, within it, the wails of a dying Ascendant great power before his miserable death echoed.

In the midst of a great battle, to perform such a self-harming act?

Huangman and Bizheng exchanged a glance, already realizing that the other party was about to fight to the death.

"Attack together!"

"We can't give him any more breathing room!"

The two figures suddenly burst forward.

The black curved blade and the rough stone rod struck out simultaneously.

Luo Chen frantically retreated while feeling the bloody aura rushing towards him from his hand.

The third seal had been temporarily broken by his essence blood.

The Yuantu Sword Spirit was awakening!

Facing the incoming attacks, Luo Chen formed a hand seal with one hand, his mana surging.

A fire phoenix shrieked and flew out.

A flame dragon descended, roaring as if to destroy the world.

But these were ultimately just ordinary techniques. Even at their peak, they couldn't withstand a single blow from a great cultivator.

Let alone being cast in haste?

They only delayed for a moment. The fire phoenix wailed and dissipated, and the flame dragon was smashed to pieces by the thick stone rod with a single blow.

Just as the curved blade transformed into a myriad again and the thick stone rod was about to hit his body.

Luo Chen gripped the thick sword hilt with both hands and swung it in the air.

*Boom!*

The terrifying mana fluctuation twisted the void and split the earth with a shocking crack.

The Yuantu Sword, with its seal released, actually possessed such power?

The two great demon emperors' pupils contracted, realizing that the other party seemed to have released something extraordinary.

Luo Chen staggered backward like a kite with a broken string.

The meridians in his body were instantly drained.

He hadn't expected that after the Yuantu Sword Spirit awakened, it would absorb mana so terrifyingly.

His Nascent Soul output mana again, but it was already too late.

Luo Chen took out a jar of wine, directly smashed the jar, and opened his mouth to inhale like a whale.

A vast amount of Golden Wind and Jade Dew flowed into his body through his gaping mouth.

With the replenishment of spiritual energy, the feeling of emptiness in Luo Chen instantly dissipated.

Looking at the two who were relentlessly pursuing him, Luo Chen knew that if he continued like this, even with the sharpness of the Yuantu Sword, he wouldn't be able to outlast them.

They wanted a quick victory, and didn't he want the same?

With a thought, Luo Chen stepped with Moon Step, dodging and weaving through the void, then with an explosive roar, he charged straight at Bizheng.

He needed to find a breakthrough!

Facing Luo Chen's charge, Bizheng laughed instead of being angry.

"Seeking death!"

The myriad blade lights suddenly merged into one.

Holding the black curved blade, a crescent moon appeared behind him.

The crescent moon merged into the blade, and then he slashed out!

Luo Chen swung his sword as well.

Blade and sword clashed once more.

*Clang!*

To Bizheng's shock, his life-bound magic treasure, which he had painstakingly refined for centuries, now had a notch.

The backlash from the magic treasure caused him to pause for a moment.

At that very moment, Luo Chen patted his lower abdomen.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron, which had been put away earlier, reappeared, floating unsteadily in the air above.

*Bang!*

The huge stone rod suddenly smashed over.

It was clear that Huangman would not give Luo Chen a chance to break through Bizheng alone. His attacks never ceased.

And this time was different from before.

Black King roared and went to receive the stone rod again, but a thick yellow light empowered the rod, causing him to be hit as if by a heavy blow.

"Master, be careful..."

Before Black King could finish his words, the terrifying force was transmitted to Luo Chen's body.

It sent him crashing down into the dust once more.

This time, Luo Chen fell into a surging river. Blood flowed profusely, and the river water constantly washed over him, making him utterly miserable.

However, without any pause, he pointed a finger at the Primordial Unity Cauldron in the sky.

*Crackle!*

A wisp of electric arc flashed out with a *crackle*.

Huangman and Bizheng, who were about to press the attack, stopped in their tracks.

They looked up at the sky. More black electric arcs began to spread from the small cauldron.

Dark clouds began to gather frantically, and the world became gloomy.

Threads of lightning crisscrossed, constantly interweaving.

Vaguely, it seemed as if some living being was about to walk out from within the dark clouds.

And this familiar scene was by no means unfamiliar to any cultivator who had advanced to the Nascent Soul level.

Two voices, as if by prior agreement, blurted out at the same time.

"Heavenly Tribulation!"
