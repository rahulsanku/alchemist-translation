# Chapter 852: Slaying Three Demons, Capturing Di Tu, Seven Rhinos Descends

"Come! Come! All of you shall be buried here today!"

In his grim smile was endless arrogance and defiance, as if he were venting something.

And in truth, Luo Chen *was* venting!

Ever since returning to the Eastern Wilderness, he had repeatedly displayed his power before others, amassing great renown.

But only he knew that every action, every decision, had to be made with extreme caution.

He had to consider the nuances of interacting with people, while also being wary of potential enemies lurking in the shadows. Above all, the Nether Origin Sacred Land weighed down on him, making it hard to breathe.

For Luo Chen, who had roamed freely in the Northern Sea for a hundred years, this was undoubtedly a suffocating experience.

The pursuit of immortality was nothing more than freedom and ease.

With such timidity and caution, where was the freedom? Where was the ease?

Under the overwhelming tide of events, even the once-lofty Sages had become cannon fodder that could be discarded at will.

And now, even a few demon beasts dared to boast arrogantly about capturing him!

Even Luo Chen's long-tempered state of mind could not help but burn with blazing fury at this moment!

Thus, his subsequent attacks became increasingly violent.

The two Nine-Zhang Deer attacked from left and right. Luo Chen neither dodged nor evaded. Spreading his hands wide, his surging blood and qi transformed into source power as he threw a punch with each hand.

It was the Mountain-Breaking Style from the Devil Lord's Seven Scattered Hands!

"Break!"

"Break!"

Under the immense force, the two Nine-Zhang Deer were no match for Luo Chen. Their massive bodies flew backward uncontrollably.

"Trying to run?"

Luo Chen did not pursue the two demons. Instead, he fixed his gaze on Di Tu, whose arms he had already broken.

He stomped heavily, and the void boomed.

When he reappeared, he had crossed several dozen zhang in an instant, kicking toward the panicked Di Tu.

Thunder Kick!

*Boom!*

A golden light that Di Tu had just spat out, not yet fully unfolded, was kicked apart with a single blow.

Di Tu's expression was one of utter shock. "How is that possible?!"

That golden light was actually a defensive treasure at the true artifact level, a gift from his father when he had advanced to the fourth rank.

Even if he hadn't spent much time refining it over the years, it shouldn't have been kicked apart with a single blow!

Was this guy human or a desolate beast?!

A fist suddenly appeared before his eyes.

Luo Chen's attacks came like an unending tide.

Forced into a corner, Di Tu summoned a demonic wind and revealed his true form.

A massive golden tiger appeared in midair, spitting a golden orb of light toward the fist.

*Rumble!*

After the explosive sounds, Di Tu finally caught a breath.

His terrified tiger eyes stared at the tiny human figure, his mouth dry and his soul fleeing in fear.

Even with the three great demon emperors surrounding Luo Chen, he still felt no sense of safety.

In terms of mana, they were no match for Luo Chen.

In terms of spells, he wielded them with effortless ease.

In terms of treasures, anything he casually brought out was a treasure of the age.

Even their demon race's trump card—their massive true bodies—seemed merely ornamental and fragile before his seemingly slender frame.

This battle was already lost!

Luo Chen stood in the void, looking down at the three enormous demonic true bodies. A smile hung on his lips, but his eyes were ice-cold.

These fellows had exhausted all their tricks.

Whether it was formations, treasures, or various demon arts, they had all been broken or destroyed, posing no threat.

Now that they had revealed their true bodies, they were nothing but lambs waiting to be slaughtered before him.

"One by one, then!"

Luo Chen took a deep breath, ignoring the two red-eyed Nine-Zhang Deer. He moved directly toward the golden tiger.

That demon's arms had been destroyed earlier. Even though he had grown two new claws after revealing his true form, they were ultimately not as good as the originals.

Strike while the iron is hot!

The void exploded as Luo Chen flickered with Voidburst Step, appearing above the golden tiger once more. He struck down with a palm.

The golden ring-blade slashed through the air toward him. Luo Chen didn't even glance at it, sending the Yuantu Sword to meet it while focusing entirely on the golden giant tiger.

The golden tiger lashed its tail like a thunderclap.

But as palm met tail, the golden tiger was struck as if by lightning, while Luo Chen lightly withdrew.

The two Nine-Zhang Deer seized the opportunity and attacked from both sides.

Suddenly, Luo Chen's body, which had been as light as a willow catkin, stepped through the air and turned like a spring swallow returning. In an instant, he broke free of the two deer's encirclement and appeared on the other side of the golden tiger.

Sinking his waist and leaning his shoulder, he drove an elbow forward.

Mountain-Crushing Style!

The golden tiger's massive body became a burden at this moment, unable to react in time.

With a single blow, it tumbled backward toward the two Nine-Zhang Deer.

Creating distance, Luo Chen rapidly formed hand seals and spat out.

"Go!"

A massive fire phoenix let out a piercing cry as it pounced toward the three demons.

The three demons did not sit idly by, either. They unleashed their demon arts to resist.

Golden orbs of light and black mist surged forward.

Even with their combined strength, they seemed fragile before this fire phoenix.

*Rumble!*

The three demons were forced back wildly by the immense heatwave generated by the collision of spells.

The terrifying heat nearly warped the void itself. In the distorted space, Luo Chen's figure appeared again, stepping down toward the golden tiger at the forefront.

"Kneel!"

With a single stomp, it was like Mount Tai pressing down from above.

The golden giant tiger plummeted uncontrollably.

Di Tu was filled with rage. He, the son of an ancient demon, was someone even the Nine Great Commanders had to show some face to. Who would have thought that today, he would be stepped on by a human?

And yet, he couldn't resist.

The boundless fury turned into endless fear when the corner of his eye caught Luo Chen recalling the blood-colored flying sword.

The blood sword descended, ready to strike!

"I surrender!"

The blood sword seemed to pause for a moment.

Di Tu instinctively added, "I am willing to surrender! I am willing to be your mount!"

Behind him came an incredulous voice.

"Di Tu, what are you saying?!"

Only then did Di Tu realize what he had just said. Shame flooded his face.

But feeling the blood sword at his neck, he opened his mouth but said nothing.

Luo Chen stood atop the giant tiger's head, his blood sword poised to strike. But in the end, he did not bring it down.

His expression was somewhat subtle. He had never expected this to actually happen.

Raising an eyebrow, Luo Chen looked at the other two demon beasts with a half-smile.

"Even the son of an ancient demon has surrendered. Are you two still going to put up a futile resistance?"

Lu Changsheng and Lu Changqing exchanged glances.

Finally, the elder brother slowly lowered his proud head.

"Big Brother!" Lu Changqing couldn't believe it. "Third Brother died at his hands!"

Lu Changsheng slowly bent his front knees, making a gesture of kneeling and submission toward Luo Chen, while speaking slowly.

"Second Brother, the situation is stronger than we are. Luo Chen is this powerful and this young. There's a chance he might reach the Ascendant realm in the future. Being his mount is not shameful."

"But—"

"Excellent! Excellent!" Luo Chen laughed. "We humans have an old saying: 'A wise man submits to circumstances.' Your decision is the right one."

Lu Changsheng lowered his head. A drop of golden blood slowly floated out from his body.

"Lord Luo Chen, this is my soul blood."

Luo Chen smiled and reached out to beckon it. The blood essence, containing a portion of his soul, drifted slowly toward him.

But halfway there, the soul blood suddenly exploded!

A compass platform descended toward Luo Chen's head.

And Lu Changsheng's bent knees suddenly straightened. He began to run.

His four hooves pounded the air in an alternating rhythm.

His steps were graceful and exquisite, containing a certain unique cadence.

Within the space sealed by the Triple Prison, he ran ceaselessly.

Luo Chen watched this scene, the smile on his face gradually fading.

"I knew you weren't truly surrendering!"

Without any visible movement from him, the outer sword formation began to spin rapidly, closing in to kill the Nine-Zhang Deer.

Lu Changsheng seemed to ignore the countless sword energies, maintaining his unique pace.

Miraculously, as he leaped and dodged, he passed through one wave of sword energy after another.

Suddenly!

Lu Changsheng paused in his steps, turning back to look at the bewildered Lu Changqing.

"I have found the flaw in the sword formation. Luo Chen is also trapped by my lifebound treasure. Second Brother, escape on your own!"

As if realizing something, the confusion on Lu Changqing's face gradually faded with his elder brother's words, replaced by panic.

"No—"

*Boom!*

No one had time to react.

Within the sword formation, Lu Changsheng's massive body suddenly self-destructed, turning into an endless storm of spiritual energy that swept in all directions.

Waves of sword energy were battered and scattered.

Even the five sword pellets were forced to reveal their true forms.

An opportunity to escape had appeared.

"GO!!!"

In the void, only that roar remained, echoing endlessly.

Lu Changqing's eyes were filled with tears. He shot a hateful glance at Luo Chen, then summoned a demonic wind and charged out of the formation.

Luo Chen stood atop the golden tiger Di Tu's head, coldly watching it all.

The construction of the Triple Prison had been a sudden inspiration he had while perfecting the Wind Leg with Wang Yuan.

The new Wind Leg was a fusion of the *Void-Stepping Art* and the *Three Styles of the Wind God*, emphasizing the unity of form and intent, the complement of emptiness and substance.

By analogy, he had thought of the incomplete Grand Five Elements Sword Formation.

This sword formation was powerful, but its threshold was too high. It required five sword pellets at the true artifact level, each belonging to one of the five elements.

This requirement alone had stopped countless people.

Even though Luo Chen had managed to set it up through clever means, it still had many flaws.

Thus, he used the solid Primordial Unity Cauldron as a supplement, linking it with the All-Encompassing Fire Prison to construct the Triple Prison!

Under this Triple Prison, the suppression was terrifying—the fact that even the four great demon emperors couldn't resist was proof enough.

But in the end, time had been too short. There was still one unresolved problem.

In Luo Chen's conception, the Triple Prison ideally required three divided souls, each presiding over one part.

Luo Chen currently only had two divided souls, so he had to use his main soul as a substitute.

But during battle, the main soul also had to execute other techniques, so the Triple Prison occasionally showed signs of instability.

To cover up this flaw, Luo Chen had pushed the attack rhythm extremely fast, giving the enemy no breathing room.

Especially after learning Di Tu's identity, he had attacked him even more frantically to divert the attention of the other three demons.

He never expected that Lu Changsheng had actually found the flaw in such a short time!

So calm!

Such a strong understanding of formations!

Not only that, but he had even sacrificed himself by self-destructing to forcibly create an escape route.

Letting his only remaining younger brother flee.

"What a good big brother!"

Luo Chen sighed with genuine feeling.

Hearing this, Di Tu's mouth twitched. He almost felt like he was watching a crocodile shed tears.

But he was also filled with resentment!

Since Lu Changsheng had found the flaw, why hadn't he acted earlier? Why did he have to wait until he, Di Tu, had humiliated himself by becoming a mount before self-destructing to break the formation?

Wait!

Di Tu thought of a possibility. That bastard had been holding back from the very start of the battle, watching coldly as Luo Chen relentlessly pursued *him*.

He had clearly been using him as bait to buy time to break the formation!

From the moment the Triple Prison had fallen, Lu Changsheng had already judged that they were no match for Luo Chen!

Just as Di Tu was burning with rage, a mocking laugh came from above his head.

"Last time, I let you three brothers escape. This time, you think you can run?"

A terrifying wave of flame surged from Luo Chen's body, instantly breaking through the compass platform that had trapped him.

Luo Chen looked at the massive Nine-Zhang Deer that had already fled a hundred li away. He pointed a finger!

The Yuantu Sword transformed into a beam of blood light and shot out.

Not only that!

Luo Chen took a deep breath, rapidly formed hand seals, and cast a spell with lightning speed.

High in the nine heavens, the clouds churned.

The roar of a flame dragon could be faintly heard.

Di Tu sensed this scene and was puzzled.

It was already so far away. Perhaps he could severely wound Lu Changqing, but killing him would still be quite difficult!

Why was Luo Chen wasting his mana?

As he wondered, Luo Chen reached out and beckoned.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron rapidly shrank and landed before him. A spiritual light appeared.

It was a small deer Nascent Soul, its eyes tightly shut, lifelike.

"Lu Changqing, don't you want your third brother anymore?"

The clear shout rang through the clear sky.

Lu Changqing, who had already fled very far, instinctively looked back. He saw his third brother's Nascent Soul held in Luo Chen's hand, the latter smiling.

His third brother's Nascent Soul was still alive?

He froze for a moment, instinctively meeting Luo Chen's eyes.

One look, and it felt like ten thousand years, as if falling into a dream!

*Roar!*

The Flame Dragon descended, sweeping across the eight directions.

Blazing flames burned, and the demon deer howled in pain.

Then came a flash of blood light as a sword descended.

*Crack!*

The massive deer's head separated cleanly from its body.

Then both fell to the earth, perishing at the foot of Daqing Mountain, by the banks of the Blackwater River.

Di Tu stared blankly at this scene.

"Despicable!"

"What did you say?"

Luo Chen flew before him with a smile, meeting those tiger eyes directly.

Di Tu fell silent in fear, lowering his once-arrogant head.

---

Bai Li Qingchuan was very close to Fenghua Palace!

No more than a thousand li.

The earlier battle, due to the sealing of the Triple Prison, had not been very visible.

But when Luo Chen killed Lu Changqing in an instant with a triple-pronged attack, the massive commotion could no longer be contained.

Demon Emperor Can Lou, who was suppressing Fenghua Fairy, instinctively looked toward Bai Li Qingchuan.

"Such a massive leakage of demonic aura... who has fallen?"

Because of his momentary distraction, Fenghua Fairy finally caught a breath. She executed a Wind Concealment Art and escaped the suppression of that incomplete divine tower.

After escaping, Fenghua Fairy was still shaken.

This demon emperor was truly formidable. Not only was his mana deep, but that incomplete divine tower was especially powerful, far beyond what ordinary true artifacts could compare to.

Didn't everyone say that the demon race was not good at refining artifacts?

Why did he have such a terrifying treasure?

However, the commotion from Bai Li Qingchuan was clearly good news.

"As expected of the Alchemy Sect. They never disappoint!"

Demon Emperor Can Lou frowned and recalled his divine tower treasure.

This thing was powerful, but it was too slow to activate. If the enemy was on guard, it was hard to make it work.

Using the same trick again would probably not suppress this woman.

He sneered, "Over there, four great demon emperors are working together, and the son of an ancient demon has personally taken action. The Alchemy Sect's Luo Chen can barely save himself. You don't think he can still spare the strength to come and save you, do you?"

Hearing this, Fenghua Fairy was momentarily stunned.

Four great demon emperors working together!

The son of an ancient demon!

Under these circumstances, Luo Chen had still managed to kill one?

She instinctively extended her divine sense toward Bai Li Qingchuan.

Can Lou did not take the opportunity to attack, clearly doing the same.

In an instant, both showed different expressions.

One was overjoyed, the other shocked.

"Can Lou, you miscalculated!"

Demon Emperor Can Lou couldn't believe it. "How can this be?"

"Today, you will stay in Fenghua Domain!"

Fenghua Fairy manipulated her flying sword and attacked Can Lou again, even transforming into several phantom figures that spread out around him.

This posture clearly meant to entangle Can Lou, waiting for Luo Chen to come and reinforce!

Can Lou was naturally not afraid of Fenghua Fairy, but the brief glimpse he had caught of the situation at Bai Li Qingchuan was hard for him to accept.

Four great demon emperors, three had fallen.

The sole survivor—Di Tu—was instead kneeling humbly before that tiny human.

Had he surrendered?

The second son of the ancient demon Di Tian—how could he bend his knee and surrender to a human?

If so, not only would this battle fail to take Fenghua Domain, but he also couldn't go back to explain things to Ancient Demon Di Tian.

No!

If he kept pestering like this, he might not even be able to go back himself!

Should he continue fighting, kill Fenghua, and then go to Bai Li Qingchuan to rescue Di Tu?

Or should he flee, to avoid falling into the encirclement of two powerful enemies later?

As he hesitated, Demon Emperor Can Lou suddenly looked up.

High in the nine heavens, a faint breeze passed by in an instant, its speed even surpassing that of Fenghua Fairy before him.

Hot on its heels was a massive blue-white streak of light, carrying the roar of wind and thunder, chasing after it.

Can Lou was overjoyed and shouted loudly.

"Commander Seven Rhinos, please save the son of Pingshan Lord!"

The massive streak of light stopped!
