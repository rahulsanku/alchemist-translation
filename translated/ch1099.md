# Chapter 1099: Sitting and Discussing the Dao, the Devil Lord's Legacy

Amidst the vast ocean, great waves surged.

Between the mountain ranges, earth dragons writhed.

A pure contest of divine sense had actually stirred up such formidable momentum.

Suddenly, a human-fish figure leaped out of the sea, its mouth uttering a melodious song that calmed the waves.

Within the mountain ranges, the phantom of a towering, heaven-supporting strongman also appeared, suppressing the balance of this place.

Luo Chen glanced at those two figures, a hint of surprise in his heart.

Across from him, Li Canghai was even more astonished.

In a contest of divine sense, even if it was merely a sparring match and not a life-or-death struggle, it was often extremely dangerous, allowing not the slightest negligence.

This Luo Chen actually still had spare energy to pay attention to other matters.

"Fellow Daoist, your divine sense is powerful. How about we stop here?"

"Mm."

The two tacitly withdrew their divine senses at the same time, causing no harm.

Li Canghai drank a mouthful of spirit wine, and only then did his expression relax.

He looked at Luo Chen and couldn't help but praise, "No wonder Fellow Daoist harbored killing intent; it turns out you possess a sharp weapon! This old man has never seen nor heard of anyone who has just entered the Ascendant stage being able to have their primordial spirit as stable as a mountain, with not the slightest hint of emptiness in their realm. I wonder how Fellow Daoist managed to achieve this."

Luo Chen smiled but did not speak.

He was indeed very different from ordinary Ascendant cultivators.

At its core, it was due to his terrifyingly profound soul foundation, and the fact that he had already mastered a full three types of Law True Intent during the Sage stage.

Ascension was merely a natural progression for him.

Especially since, when he broke through the realm, he had also taken a fifth-rank Soul-Scouring Pill, which thoroughly cleansed all distracting thoughts from his primordial spirit.

Therefore, he dared to venture out and explore the world as soon as he became an Ascendant.

One must know that with Luo Chen's personality, every time he broke through a realm, he would need to stabilize for a long time before going out, let alone fighting with others.

After witnessing the strength of Luo Chen's primordial spirit, Li Canghai no longer doubted his power.

After all, this was a monstrous talent who, while still in the Sage stage, had reversed the flow and slain an Ascendant great power.

He said solemnly, "Those two ancient demons are only slightly higher in realm than us, still at the early Ascendant stage. I do not know their combat abilities, but I imagine they are not weak. After all, great demons of this level often already possess innate divine abilities, which are not necessarily inferior to the divine abilities and techniques developed by us human cultivators."

Luo Chen asked, "Do you have any sharp weapons at hand?"

Li Canghai replied, "The demon race is not skilled in alchemy or artifact forging. They often refine their unique physical characteristics into magical treasures. They may have the power of a spirit treasure, but they lack the substance of one. If Fellow Daoist possesses a spirit treasure, you need not fear them."

Luo Chen recalled the greedy looks those two ancient demons had given his Yuantu Sword and Burial Bone Umbrella at the end of the Heaven-Earth battle, and he could guess that they probably did not have true spirit treasures in hand.

At least, Li Canghai had not shown such a greedy look.

Luo Chen also swept his gaze across this heaven and earth, feeling a peculiar sensation.

Li Canghai continued, "The Great Sage Beili lives on Hanging Blade Peak, while the Sea Emperor Whale's lair is in the Sea Emperor Palace. If you wish to find them, you can go to these two places..."

Li Canghai hesitated for a moment, then sighed, "There's really no need for fighting and killing. At our level, it's easy to determine victory or defeat, but when it comes to life and death, the price is too great."

Luo Chen snorted lightly, "They still have to give me an explanation for offending me that day."

Li Canghai was speechless.

If he had been in that situation that day, he too would have felt angry.

If he had handled it even slightly poorly, Luo Chen might have already fallen outside Heaven-Earth Island.

"Forget it." Li Canghai let out a long sigh. "You, having just become an Ascendant, will find it much more convenient to make a move than us old-timers anyway."

Luo Chen's eyes flickered. "What do you mean by that?"

Li Canghai asked in return, "After Fellow Daoist became an Ascendant, did you have any special feelings?"

Luo Chen frowned and answered truthfully, "Unfreedom! It feels as if there is a shackle on my body, and this heaven and earth also feels too narrow to me."

"That's right." Li Canghai nodded, then explained, "The pinnacle of cultivation in this world, aside from the heaven-and-earth-born desolate beasts, is the Ascendant stage. Once one enters the Ascendant stage, a heavenly shackle binds the body, restricting us from fully displaying our abilities. This is the origin of the rumor that Ascendant cultivators cannot easily make a move."

Luo Chen was puzzled. "Then how do you explain the frequent battles between Ascendant great powers over these past five hundred years?"

Li Canghai sneered. "It's nothing more than breaking the shackles and putting oneself in danger."

In his subsequent explanation, Luo Chen finally understood what the so-called "shackles" meant.

After a cultivator achieves the Ascendant stage, their own mana, empowered by the primordial spirit, can use a small force to leverage a large one, controlling the origin qi of heaven and earth.

Especially for those who have comprehended Law True Intent, they can control even more of the origin qi of heaven and earth.

From this perspective, Ascendant cultivators can almost be considered the executors of the authority of the world's Great Dao!

But controlling the origin qi of heaven and earth can, conversely, be seen as being controlled by the origin qi of heaven and earth.

The shackles are formed at this time.

The earlier one achieves Ascension and the longer one stays in the Mountains and Seas Realm, the heavier these shackles become.

Once one engages in unrestrained, full-power combat, the shackles will break, and this world's assimilation of the Ascendant cultivator will intensify.

At that point, forget about advancing further; even ascending to the Upper Realm would be a vain hope.

It's like the relationship between a child and their parents. The more the child takes from the parents, the less they can grow themselves. When they later want to leave home and explore, the parents won't support them.

This analogy might be somewhat biased, but it roughly conveys the meaning.

"But I observe that the Ascendant great powers of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect don't seem to have this problem?"

"Sect cultivators are different from us. They do not pursue ascension to the Immortal Realm. They merely regard the Mountains and Seas Realm as a cradle for extracting resources and absorbing spiritual energy. Even if they cannot ascend in the end, they can still use the sect's passageway to ascend to... the Mystic Realm."

A sharp light flashed in Luo Chen's eyes. "So Fellow Daoist also knows about the Mystic Realm!"

Li Canghai laughed heartily. "That place is no secret to us. The Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's formation inheritance contains many descriptions of the customs and practices of the Mystic Realm."

After mentioning the words "Devil Lord Heaven-Refining," the atmosphere between the two took on a strange change.

The two of them could actually both be considered disciples of the Devil Lord's legacy.

One had obtained the alchemy inheritance, the other the formation inheritance.

Otherwise, how could a rogue cultivator and a Northern Sea cultivator with no great background have broken through to the Ascendant stage?

From this perspective, Luo Chen would even have to call Li Canghai "Senior Brother."

Since the topic had come to this, Luo Chen did not hesitate and went straight to one of the purposes of his visit today.

"May I ask Fellow Daoist Li, how much do you know about the Demon-Fall Land in the Fallen Sea?"

Li Canghai looked at him with a half-smile. "You've been inside too, so why ask me?"

Luo Chen said calmly, "When I entered back then, I was merely at the Adept stage. I did not understand the Great Dao, nor did I know the depth of heaven and earth. How could I have glimpsed the full picture of the place where the Devil Lord fell?"

Li Canghai was stunned.

He only then remembered that Luo Chen, whom he had been treating as an equal, had actually only been a small Adept-stage cultivator three hundred years ago.

Thinking of this, he became even more amazed at Luo Chen's outstanding talent.

Forming a core in a hundred years, condensing a nascent soul in two hundred, and now, at less than five hundred years of age, he had already become an Ascendant.

The length of his remaining lifespan was truly enviable.

Shaking his head, Li Canghai squeezed out a bitter smile. "When I entered back then, I was only at the mid-Sage stage. What I saw of the Devil Lord's former grounds was merely the tip of the iceberg."

Then, like a successful man recalling the arduous years of his past, he spoke of events from long ago.

"The Fallen Sea is said to be born every thousand years, but that's just a general figure. To be precise, it should be about one thousand five hundred years."

"In those one thousand five hundred years, the Demon-Fall Land has opened four times. By my calculations, the fifth time should be soon. However, the spirit-gathering formation of the Fallen Sea was damaged by demon clan experts, so I don't know if the fifth opening will happen on schedule."

"The first time it opened, I hadn't even been born yet. I only heard some information through hearsay. It was too strange and bizarre back then, and the situation in the Fallen Sea was unclear, so very few people went in to explore."

"The second time it opened, the Northern Sea cultivators already had a good understanding of the place. A batch of cultivators with considerable Dao cultivation went in. Not many came back, but each one had their own gains, which made others envious."

"Therefore, by the third opening, the number of people entering was the largest in history! I also entered the Demon-Fall Land that time."

"That time, the Primordial Demon Sect organized a huge force, led by the Blood Sea Ancestor, supplemented by twelve Sage elders of the demon sect, and the number of Adept-stage cultivators exceeded ten thousand. The scale of the lineup was astonishing. Besides that, among the human cultivators of the Northern Sea, aside from myself, there were also some famous and powerful figures. The demonic giant Hatred Heaven Old Monster, the Grand Cultivator Wei Changkong, the Wanderer Layman Fengling. On the demon clan's side, they were equally impressive: White Faith Weng, Coral Master, the Mighty Barbaric Demon Jiao, and so on."

"But the results of that exploration shook the entire Northern Sea!"

With a lingering fear in his heart, Li Canghai listed the fates of most of the people.

The very first trial of the Leaving Forgetting Heaven caused a large number of accompanying low-level cultivators to become lost within it.

Luo Chen remembered this vividly.

If it weren't for his firm Dao heart and his frequent encounters with heart tribulations and obsessions during the Qi Refinement and Initiate stages, giving him rich experience, he probably would have found it difficult to pass the illusion trial of Leaving Forgetting Heaven.

Then came the resource-rich Five Elements Heaven. For the sake of the abundant resources, a group of experts engaged in fierce battles within the Five Elements Heaven.

The demon clan expert Coral Master was defeated by Layman Fengling and then devoured by a strange beast. Wei Changkong and White Faith Weng fought, both suffering heavy losses. That demonic giant Hatred Heaven Old Monster even single-handedly killed three Primordial Demon Sect elders before being slain in anger by the Blood Sea Ancestor...

In the final inheritance grounds of Bright Radiance Heaven, the injured Wei Changkong and White Faith Weng both fell. That Wanderer Layman Fengling was even more unlucky. Before entering Bright Radiance Heaven, he was ambushed out of hatred by the Mighty Barbaric Demon Jiao, who had liked Coral Master, and fell into the Demon Fall Abyss. Although it was unknown how Layman Fengling later escaped the Demon Fall Abyss, he went mad after coming out, becoming the Mad Wanderer among the Three Great Wanderers, sometimes lucid and sometimes insane.

Among these people, the biggest event was the fall of the Blood Sea Ancestor within Bright Radiance Heaven!

Because of his fall, the demon clan, which had been restless, conceived the idea of launching a war to destroy the Primordial Demon Sect.

Also because of this incident, coupled with the heavy losses of that expedition, the enthusiasm of the Northern Sea's high-level cultivators for the fourth opening of the Demon-Fall Land was not so high.

Everyone knew how dangerous it was inside. Even Grand Cultivators and Ascendant great powers could easily fall within. Who would dare to take the risk?

If it weren't for those whose lifespans were running out, or those with ulterior motives, or low-level cultivators desperate to break through their realms, the number of cultivators entering the Demon-Fall Land for the fourth time would have been even smaller.

After listening to Li Canghai's reminiscences, Luo Chen couldn't help but be secretly astonished.

He had thought that the lineup for his entry into the Demon-Fall Land three hundred years ago was already luxurious enough, gathering the Sage experts of the four major overlord forces of the Northern Sea at the time, plus a group of demon clan experts.

He never expected that six hundred years ago, it was even more terrifying.

There were many Sages, Grand Cultivators and Great Demon Emperors were heard of endlessly, and even an Ascendant great power had entered one.

"In the end, it was still Fellow Daoist Li who had the superior skill, obtaining the benefits amidst a pack of wolves!" Luo Chen sighed with emotion, a hint of respect in his tone.

Li Canghai shook his head. "It wasn't that I had superior skill. It was just that I was cautious, avoided conflict with others as much as possible, and maintained a good enough state to enter Bright Radiance Heaven. And in the end, I luckily passed the Formation Realm trial and obtained the Devil Lord's formation inheritance."

Luo Chen understood and did not refute.

Who among those who could cultivate to a high realm was not cautious?

But in the end, it was Li Canghai who obtained the inheritance, which showed that he must have had outstanding qualities.

However, Luo Chen thought about it and realized that Li Canghai's caution truly surpassed the ordinary.

He remembered that Li Canghai had achieved the Ascendant stage four hundred years ago.

That meant he had entered the Demon-Fall Land six hundred years ago and obtained the inheritance. After coming out, he kept a low profile, cultivated in seclusion for a full two hundred years, and only began to make a name for himself after successfully breaking through to Ascension.

This was far more cautious than Luo Chen himself!

Moreover, although Li Canghai later established the Cang Hai Alliance, he basically didn't manage its affairs. He simply used this force to collect cultivation resources for him.

When the demon clan army broke free from Yemo Heaven, he did not stick his neck out.

After the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect took over the Northern Sea, he opportunistically switched sides.

Even eighty years ago, when he and Luo Chen joined forces to storm Heaven-Earth Island, upon seeing signs of a Mystic Realm True Lord descending, he was the first to unhesitatingly flee.

With such caution and a heart devoted to the Dao, if he didn't become an Ascendant, who should?

By his ear came Li Canghai's regretful voice.

"Although I luckily obtained the Devil Lord's formation inheritance, there was no inherited spirit treasure in the formation hall. After I came out, I established a force, gathered resources, and expended immense effort before finally, based on the methods recorded in the Devil Lord's inheritance, I refined a spirit treasure that suited my wishes."

"Is it this Mountain-Seal Seal?" Luo Chen looked around, a hint of admiration in his eyes.

Li Canghai smiled proudly. "Exactly! Let Fellow Daoist know, this seal contains famous mountains and great seas, forming its own cave heaven paradise. Not only can it be carried around for my cultivation, but it also serves both offense and defense, not inferior to those famous spirit treasures."

Luo Chen flattered him. "Indeed, I have never seen a spirit treasure with two artifact spirits."

The mermaid was a sea spirit, and the giant who suppressed the mountains was a mountain spirit. Luo Chen had long discovered these two non-human entities.

Faced with Luo Chen's flattery, Li Canghai was a bit embarrassed.

"There is a bit of a shortcut involved, which I hope Fellow Daoist Luo will not laugh at."

Seeing Luo Chen's confusion, Li Canghai briefly explained.

For a magical treasure to give birth to spiritual intelligence is an extremely difficult matter. It often requires a long period of nurturing, and the material itself must be of excellent quality.

Based on formation principles, Li Canghai deduced that heaven and earth have spirits, and mountains and seas also have spirits. Therefore, he took a shortcut to condense the two artifact spirits, the mountain spirit and the sea spirit.

Speaking of this, he was quite envious and asked with a hint of probing, "You passed the Pill Realm trial, so you should have obtained the inheritance and spirit treasure from the pill hall, right?"

Luo Chen said calmly, "I obtained the alchemy inheritance, but as for the spirit treasure... it was not meant for me."

Luo Chen briefly mentioned the self-destruction of the Heaven-Refining Cauldron, which made Li Canghai quite regretful.

He and Luo Chen had become kindred spirits—they had obtained the inheritance but not the spirit treasure.

Amidst their sighs, Luo Chen suddenly changed the subject.

"Since you have been into the Demon-Fall Land, you naturally know that the opportunities hidden within are far richer than the outside world. Have you never thought of going in again?"
