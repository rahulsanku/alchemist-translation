# Chapter 945: Heaven and Earth Overturned, Alarming Exaggeration

Good news stays within doors, but bad news travels a thousand miles.

Even the cultivation world could not escape this principle.

Luo Chen was well aware that the commotion he had caused at the Illumination Sect could not be hidden from others.

Moreover, unlike the blocked information in the Northern Sea, where the various sects of the Eastern Wilderness each swept their own doorstep, the Southern Frontier had unique messenger gu insects that allowed news to spread extremely quickly and widely.

Therefore, when the master of Nine Haze Immortal City, Daoist Zhenguan, called out his title, he knew that the matter from a year ago at the Illumination Sect had come to light.

For anyone else, this might have caused great panic.

After all, a major power like the Illumination Sect, deeply rooted in the Southern Frontier for many years, had numerous connections.

Whether it was friendly sects with ties and dealings, or the personal network of that Lamp Master himself, together they formed a formidable force.

Under the pressure of such a force, who could not feel terrified?

But Luo Chen paid it no mind!

At his level, possessing such great power, he was not afraid of beating the young and having the old come, or wiping out one nest only to have another nest appear.

If truly pushed to the brink, he would not mind once again committing an act of razing mountains and exterminating sects, wiping them all out in one fell swoop.

Luo Chen walked in front, his stride like a dragon's, his bearing imposing.

Zhenguan followed behind, a strange light flickering in his eyes.

This cultivator surnamed Qi truly lived up to his reputation as a tyrant cultivator!

Such bearing was extremely rare in the Southern Frontier cultivation world, which advocated hiding one's light and acting with low profile. The Illumination Sect had truly courted disaster by provoking this man.

Curious, he lowered his voice and asked, "Thunderclap Cave Master, Fellow Daoist Qi is from Wind Thunder Gorge?"

Thunderclap Cave Master nodded matter-of-factly. "That's right. He is one of the thirty-six cave masters of Wind Thunder Gorge. Although he attained the Dao somewhat later, his strength surpasses mine."

Daoist Zhenguan gave him a knowing smile. "Is that so?"

A trace of unnaturalness flickered across Thunderclap Cave Master's face, but he quickly affirmed, "How could it be false? If Fellow Daoist Zhenguan does not believe me, you may send someone to my Wind Thunder Gorge to investigate."

Daoist Zhenguan waved his hand. That would be too impolite.

Besides, there was no need!

As they entered the main hall, Daoist Zhenguan subconsciously began, "Fellow Daoist Bei, allow me to introduce..."

He stopped mid-sentence.

He looked suspiciously at the two men standing opposite each other inside, especially Qi Tian, who wore a puzzled expression.

"You know each other?"

Bei Puti shook his head. "I have only heard of Fellow Daoist Qi's name before, but we have never met. However, Fellow Daoist Qi's demeanor seems as if he recognizes me?"

Luo Chen had only just arrived in the Southern Frontier; how could he possibly know a local cultivator?

He merely sensed a faintly familiar aura from the other party.

"Perhaps I mistook you for someone else," Luo Chen explained.

This time, it was Bei Puti's turn to show a look of surprise and uncertainty.

Daoist Zhenguan quickly stepped forward and laughed, "The cultivation world is vast; it's not unusual for two people to resemble each other. Allow me to make the introductions!"

"Bei Puti, Left Envoy of the Luo Sect."

"Qi Tian, Second Cave Master of Wind Thunder Gorge."

After they exchanged greetings, Bei Puti could not help but ask, "What kind of blessed land is Wind Thunder Gorge, that it has produced two powerful cultivators like Fellow Daoist Qi and Fellow Daoist Thunderclap?"

Thunderclap Cave Master said dryly, "It is merely a place for wandering cultivators with nowhere else to go to hide from the world. There is nothing blessed about it; on the contrary, it is extremely harsh. My achievement of the Nascent Soul stage was merely a stroke of luck."

Bei Puti shook his head. "Then Fellow Daoist Qi—surely he was not just lucky?"

Luo Chen said calmly, "After I achieved some success in my cultivation, I left the Southern Frontier and traveled the Mountains and Seas Realm. My footprints have covered all four continents of the world, and I have even set foot in the endless Demon Sea, where few humans tread. Having such strength is only natural; it is not merely due to the geographical advantages of Wind Thunder Gorge!"

Upon hearing this, everyone was somewhat surprised.

It was normal for cultivators to travel and gain experience.

However, due to the burdens of family and sect, they often limited themselves to one or two continents. Especially when their cultivation levels were relatively low, the Southern Frontier cultivation world alone was enough for low-level cultivators to explore for a lifetime.

This Qi Tian's footprints actually covered all four continents of the world?

Thunderclap Cave Master subconsciously breathed a sigh of relief. With these words as a foundation, it would be easier to explain where Luo Chen had come from in the future.

After they exchanged a few pleasantries and talked about their backgrounds, the conversation turned to the main topic.

Daoist Zhenguan's expression turned serious. "I assume you have all come here to join the Fierce Mountain Corpse Alliance?"

Bei Puti nodded without hesitation. "Exactly! I have heard that the Fierce Mountain Corpse Alliance's leader, Sage Songyang, has joined forces with Li Tianshi to develop a method to resolve the problem of qi deviation. I still want to advance further in this life, so I urgently need this method!"

"Then are you prepared to become an enemy of the Life and Death Gate?" Daoist Zhenguan countered.

Bei Puti hesitated.

The Life and Death Gate was the only Ascendant sacred ground in the Southern Frontier. Not to mention becoming its enemy, even provoking it on ordinary days was unthinkable.

And now, the cultivators of the Fierce Mountain Corpse Alliance were speaking so openly about it?

Daoist Zhenguan shook his head. "You want the secret method but are unwilling to bear the risk. Left Envoy Bei, how could there be such a good thing in this world?"

Bei Puti fell silent. After a long while, he said in a low voice, "My Luo Sect spent a long time recovering its strength after the internal turmoil back then. We do not wish to participate in any more conflicts. Moreover, I am merely one of the two envoys, left and right. I cannot represent the entire Luo Sect."

"Then have your sect leader agree first, and then go seek the formula from Alliance Leader Songyang!"

Daoist Zhenguan seemed accustomed to this situation, his response so practiced.

Thunderclap Cave Master, on the other hand, looked bewildered.

What qi deviation?

What becoming an enemy of the sacred ground?

Luo Chen, however, was gradually putting the pieces together.

If the problems he had encountered while cultivating at the Illumination Sect were not an isolated case, it meant that similar situations were occurring elsewhere in the Southern Frontier cultivation world.

When Nascent Soul cultivators cultivated, they became too close to heaven and earth. Under the erosion of massive amounts of origin qi, they were extremely prone to qi deviation, or even being assimilated by heaven and earth!

Moreover, this had likely been going on for a long time.

The Fierce Mountain Corpse Alliance was probably using the means to resolve this calamity as leverage, causing many Nascent Soul cultivators who had "nowhere else to turn" to voluntarily join them.

This timeline should have started in the last two or three hundred years.

But neither the Eastern Wilderness nor the Central Continent or Northern Sea had similar situations?

"Could it be that Sage Heavenly Mountain's departure from his homeland to join the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect in the Central Continent back then was also for this reason?"

The Heavenly Mountain Cult of the Southern Frontier had long since declined.

As its last leader, Sage Heavenly Mountain had no real attachments and could simply leave.

But these originally prosperous native Southern Frontier sects were not so simple.

"Then why hasn't Thunderclap Cave Master shown any signs of this problem?"

"Is it because he has just formed his Nascent Soul, or because of Wind Thunder Gorge..."

Luo Chen suddenly realized that although the environment of Wind Thunder Gorge was extremely harsh, precisely because of the conflicting earth qi and the raging wind and thunder, it had actually turned misfortune into a blessing, resisting the erosion of heaven and earth's origin qi.

A clever man like Luo Chen, through just a few words exchanged between them, combined with his own experience, had deduced many things.

Whether his deductions were correct was something to be verified later.

On the other side, seeing that Luo Sect's Left Envoy Bei Puti was hesitant, Daoist Zhenguan did not press him. He turned his attention to Luo Chen and Thunderclap Cave Master.

"The last time I saw you, Cave Master, was at Taimao Immortal City. Now that you have taken the initiative to come to Nine Haze Immortal City, have you already sensed the changes in your cultivation?"

Thunderclap Cave Master opened his mouth, then could not help but ask curiously, "What exactly are these changes? Please speak frankly, Fellow Daoist!"

"Eh?"

"Eh?"

Two exclamations of surprise came from Daoist Zhenguan and Bei Puti respectively.

They exchanged a glance, both focusing their attention on Thunderclap Cave Master.

He still hadn't been affected?

For a moment, the atmosphere in the hall became strange.

It was not until a light cough broke the silence that their stares were interrupted.

"Cough, cough!"

Luo Chen said calmly, "If there is something to say, say it plainly. Thunderclap and I live in a remote place and are not very familiar with much of the cultivation world's intelligence. There is no need to beat around the bush."

Daoist Zhenguan frowned. After a moment of hesitation, he spoke frankly:

"This sky, this earth."

As he spoke, he pointed to the sky, then to the ground.

"The heaven and earth of the Southern Frontier have been undergoing subtle changes."

Luo Chen pretended to be unimpressed. "Things change, seas become mulberry fields. Nothing in this world remains unchanged."

"It is different." Daoist Zhenguan shook his head, his expression grave. "That change is from top to bottom, an unalterable change. Some top-tier experts have discovered that the spiritual qi between heaven and earth is continuously increasing."

Thunderclap Cave Master was puzzled. "Isn't an increase in spiritual qi a good thing?"

Daoist Zhenguan gave a wry smile. "For wandering cultivators, it is certainly a good thing. That is how you, Fellow Daoist, had the opportunity to attain the Nascent Soul Dao. However, the spiritual qi of the world, because of our cultivation, is always being used up. Especially after great experts ascend, they take a large amount of spiritual qi with them. So where does this increased spiritual qi come from?"

Cultivators—their power resides in themselves!

But where does this power come from?

From cultivation?

No, it comes from plundering and seizing the spiritual qi of heaven and earth!

It can be imagined that under this constant plundering and seizing, the spiritual qi of heaven and earth will always become less and less, gradually drying up, eventually leading to an age of Dharma's End.

Thus, there is the distinction between the ancient times, the primeval times, and the present age.

In ancient times, wild beasts roamed, swallowing the origin qi of heaven and earth, chasing the stars and the moon.

In primeval times, demon beasts emerged in large numbers, wreaking havoc on the land.

Now, humanity is the protagonist, exclusively occupying the five refined continents.

Those who do not understand see only the changes of the times, each taking their turn on the stage.

Those who truly have insight see the most essential thing.

That is, from wild beasts, to demon beasts, and even to humanity, the size of their bodies has continuously shrunk, and their consumption of spiritual qi has gradually decreased. Especially humanity, with the help of various exquisite techniques, has reduced consumption while greatly increasing utilization efficiency.

But no matter how exquisite the utilization, it cannot change the fact that the vast number of cultivators have seized incalculable amounts of spiritual qi and trapped it within their bodies.

Especially as their realms improve, their lifespans become longer, they live longer, and the spiritual qi trapped within their bodies increases.

Conversely, this means that the spiritual qi of heaven and earth gradually decreases.

But now, the spiritual qi of the Southern Frontier is actually becoming more and more abundant!

Such an anomaly must have a reason!

Thunderclap Cave Master did not understand the meaning and frowned deeply.

Luo Chen, with his extensive experience, vaguely understood the other party's meaning but still could not grasp the key point.

Daoist Zhenguan did not beat around the bush and said directly, "The imbalance of heaven and earth, the intensification of spiritual qi—this is a sign of the arrival of a spiritual qi tide! If it continues to develop like this, then the ocean of origin qi, which only Ascendant cultivators can explore, will overturn the Southern Frontier, causing a violent spiritual qi tide that will destroy everything!"

Thunderclap was shocked.

Luo Chen gave a wry smile.

"Fellow Daoist, isn't that a bit too alarming?"

Daoist Zhenguan shrugged. "Whether it is true or not, you can judge for yourselves. But I only want to say two things. First, the frequent qi deviation during the daily cultivation of high-level cultivators in the Southern Frontier is one manifestation of the ocean of origin qi sinking and becoming extremely close to the Southern Frontier. And second... the root of this calamity should lie within the Life and Death Gate!"

Was this the reason the Fierce Mountain Corpse Alliance wanted to attack the Life and Death Gate?

Luo Chen fell into thought.

There was nothing more to discuss after that.

Luo Chen and the others stated their original intention for coming—to participate in the Nascent Soul exchange meeting at Mijie Mountain and purchase some resources.

Daoist Zhenguan did not refuse, only saying that they would have to wait a few more days. He then arranged accommodations for them.

...

"The spiritual qi in this cave dwelling is so abundant; I will leave it for Fellow Daoist Qi!"

Bei Puti declined a fourth-grade mid-grade cave dwelling that had been specially arranged for him because he had arrived first, and instead chose another ordinary cave dwelling in Nine Haze Immortal City.

Thunderclap Cave Master stood to the side and laughed, "It seems they all know your reputation. There was no need for me to introduce you at all!"

Luo Chen waved his hand dismissively.

Reputation was a stepping stone, but where that stone came from was also an art.

Without Thunderclap Cave Master, he would not have been able to proceed so smoothly.

Thunderclap Cave Master watched Luo Chen inspect the cave dwelling and could not hold back.

"Brother Qi, what Zhenguan said earlier—in your opinion, is it true or false?"

Luo Chen stood at the entrance of the cultivation chamber without turning his head. "Whether it is true or false is not important. But we have only just met them, and they are already speaking so frankly, even spouting such grand words about heaven and earth overturning. At the very least, the phrase 'alarming exaggeration' cannot be avoided."

Thunderclap Cave Master nodded in agreement. He had never sensed any changes in heaven and earth while in Wind Thunder Gorge.

After a thorough inspection, Luo Chen confirmed that there was nothing wrong with the place.

He was quite looking forward to Mijie Mountain.

The spiritual vein grade of Nine Haze Immortal City was already so high that it surpassed the mountain gates of many ordinary Nascent Soul upper sects.

And this was merely the gateway to Mijie Mountain.

It could be imagined that the spiritual veins within Mijie Mountain would be even better!

Ordinary people might be wary of the danger of assimilation by heaven and earth and dare not cultivate recklessly, but Luo Chen had no such concerns.

His deep Purple Mansion barrier, coupled with the Dustbound Immortal Mirror, which was supported by the power of his three souls, was enough for him to cultivate with abandon.

He was just curious about what method that Alliance Leader Songyang used to solve this problem for others.

"By the way, let me ask you something," Luo Chen said, as if remembering something, turning to Thunderclap Cave Master. "That Bei Puti—do you know anything about him?"

Thunderclap Cave Master was taken aback for a moment, then said frankly, "I have not had much contact with him, so I don't know him well. However, I do know a little about his background."

Under his narration, Luo Chen's impression of Bei Puti became more three-dimensional.

The Luo Sect was a major sect in the Southern Frontier!

It had millions of followers, and the number of inner disciples was as high as hundreds of thousands!

On the surface, it had six Nascent Soul cultivators.

The sect leader was at the late Nascent Soul stage, the left and right envoys were at the mid-Nascent Soul stage, and there were three Dharma Kings protecting the sect.

However, a mishap had occurred within the Luo Sect's inner sect back then.

The right envoy of the Luo Sect, Nan Yiqi, who was on par with Bei Puti, had secretly killed outstanding disciples within the sect, cultivated evil arts, and even set his sights on the three Dharma Kings.

At the time, people thought he had refined a gu insect like the Devil Heart Gu and had thus undergone a drastic change in temperament.

Later, when the sect leader personally suppressed him, it was discovered that he had actually cultivated the Northern Sea demonic path and intended to use the entire Luo Sect to refine a Million-Soul Baleful Banner that could threaten an Ascendant great power!

A great battle broke out within the inner sect. The right envoy Nan Yiqi disappeared without a trace, and the Luo Sect was severely weakened.

After finishing, Thunderclap Cave Master could not help but sigh with emotion.

"I have also heard of the Northern Sea soul banners. They are classified by the number of souls. An ordinary Ten-Thousand-Soul Banner is comparable to a magical artifact, a Hundred-Thousand-Fierce-Soul Banner is comparable to a true artifact, and a Million-Soul Baleful Banner is comparable to a spirit treasure. But Nan Yiqi thought too simply. Even if he had truly exhausted the Luo Sect's millions of followers, the Baleful Soul Banner he refined would probably be a mess. How could it threaten an Ascendant great power?"

Luo Chen nodded in agreement.

Refining a soul banner was not simply a matter of the number of souls inside; quality also mattered.

A Million-Soul Baleful Banner comparable to a spirit treasure would require at least a hundred Nascent Soul-level souls, and it would also need an Ascendant-level main soul.

Nan Yiqi had taken the wrong path.

Suddenly.

Luo Chen finally understood why Bei Puti gave him a faint sense of familiarity.

"Nan Yiqi, Bei Puti... heh, that Old Ghost Nanqi, whom I met briefly in the Northern Sea's Moluo Stream back then, had the same aura as Bei Puti!"

After figuring this out, Luo Chen saw Thunderclap Cave Master off and closed the cave dwelling.

The changes in the Southern Frontier were dangerous for others.

But for him, they were no different from a stroke of good fortune.

As long as he tightly sealed his Purple Mansion and had the help of the Dustbound Immortal Mirror, he could cultivate with abandon!
