# Chapter 1074: Meeting Rotten Axe-Handle Again, Retrieving the Life Soul

Within the Three-Tiered Waterfall Cave, Zhou Fuyou cultivated in stillness.

Unlike previous breakthroughs where he had quickly stabilized his realm, this time, although he felt his foundation remained solid, he did not rashly leave. Instead, he patiently ground and refined his dharma power, bit by bit.

Such an unusual reaction stemmed from the unknown and from fear.

He did not know where the spring in his body had come from, and he feared the terrifying power contained within that spring.

Only by refining his dharma power to the utmost, by mastering his own strength as completely as possible, could he have the confidence to face that spring.

But as he polished his realm year after year, he suddenly discovered that his dharma power was rising like a rising tide, growing wildly at a speed visible to the naked eye.

This already exceeded the total amount of spiritual energy he absorbed from the outside world!

Energy in the world could not arise from nothing; there must be a source.

And for Zhou Fuyou at this moment, that source was all too easy to explore.

It was precisely that spring!

Threads and strands of origin power flowed out from the spring, eventually forming a surging river that roared and tumbled through his meridians and Purple Mansion.

His realm advanced by a thousand li a day!

Sage Layer One, Sage Layer Two, Sage Layer Three…

In this almost insane climb of realms, Zhou Fuyou suddenly understood why his dharma power had been so abundant since childhood.

Clearly, he cultivated the *Dew Cicada Art*, a technique that pursued shortcuts but produced thin dharma power, yet his power had always crushed those of the same rank. The source of it all was also this spring.

It truly seized the creation of heaven and earth, capable of nurturing a river or sea on its own.

Thus, even if only a trickle occasionally leaked out, to him, who had once been at a low realm, it was like a monstrous wave.

The *Dew Cicada Art* was merely a key, a key to continuously open the door to higher realms. Once it opened a little more, the spring would provide him with even more power.

Until he entered the Sage stage!

Until he felt the true meaning of the laws of the Water Origin!

"Although I don't know what kind of existence this spring is, I absolutely cannot let it devour me!"

Zhou Fuyou took a deep breath, calmed his mind, and focused on comprehending the Water Origin.

He had a feeling that as long as he further grasped the Water Origin, he would have a chance to perfectly master this spring.

…

Fifty years later!

The location of the Three-Tiered Waterfall had become a forbidden land, devoid of any living creature.

Even a hundred li away, one could faintly hear the rumbling sounds coming from within the mountain waterfall.

Like a heartbeat, like monstrous waves rolling.

This day!

*Whoosh…*

Vast dharma power surged out from the Three-Tiered Waterfall, transforming into boundless black giant waves that swept in all directions.

Wherever they passed, grass and trees vanished in an instant, mountain rocks decayed into sand, and a thousand li of land became a vast swamp.

A figure floated in the midst of the swampy ocean, lips tightly pressed together.

"It's Dark Water!"

"This spring not only contains the Water Origin, but is also condensed from the purest Dark Water True Essence."

"No wonder I was born with a Profound Dark Dao Body. This body of mine was nurtured bit by bit by the Dark Water Spring!"

Amidst his sudden realization, Zhou Fuyou also felt quite fortunate.

At least, he could now slightly control this spring.

With a thought, the boundless dharma power immediately flowed back, surging and returning to his body.

The outside world was a barren wasteland, but within his Purple Mansion…

Apart from his natal Sage holding the Sea-Calming Pearl in its mouth, a spring was rooted there, slowly oozing black liquid outward.

These black liquids intimately surrounded the Sage infant, allowing it to play and drink like a child.

His mind returned, and Zhou Fuyou let out a long breath.

Counting on his fingers, from the time he entered the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, about eighty years had passed.

Such a long time was a mortal's lifetime, but for a high-level cultivator, it was merely a single closed-door session.

At this point, Zhou Fuyou actually wanted to leave the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven.

Even if only ten months might have passed in the outside world, for him, he was already somewhat impatient.

He had cultivated to the Sage stage, living up to the expectations of his ancestor and not disappointing his master's teachings. He also wanted to share this joy with his senior sister, second senior brother… and his little sister, Zhou Qingyu.

But before that, there was one more matter to handle.

Zhou Fuyou took out a transmission talisman. Within it, Uncle-Master Li's voice still echoed.

An elder had a request of him, hoping he would contribute to the sect. Naturally, he could not refuse.

Zhou Fuyou slightly identified the direction, then flew towards the very center of the grotto-heaven.

When passing by a valley, Zhou Fuyou paused his steps, his gaze fixed.

Although this place seemed ordinary, in his eyes, now that he had comprehended the true meaning of the laws, this valley was too strange.

Not only did it not spew out spiritual energy, but it was also frantically absorbing it.

Countless strands of free heaven and earth spiritual energy were sucked in, vanishing like clay oxen entering the sea.

"Interesting!"

Zhou Fuyou was quite curious. His form flickered, and he appeared at the valley entrance.

Just as he was about to enter, a familiar graceful figure walked out from inside.

"Gu Muyu?"

Gu Muyu was also stunned to see Zhou Fuyou, especially when she sensed his undisguised dharma power fluctuations. She showed a look of shock.

"Senior Brother Zhou, you've broken through to the Sage stage?"

Zhou Fuyou slightly nodded, then looked somewhat curiously at the valley behind her.

Gu Muyu was a bit nervous, but thinking of the Ancient Gu Great House she had backing her, she finally mustered some confidence.

"This is a medicinal garden specially opened by my Gu family within the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven for cultivating spiritual herbs. I hope Senior Brother Zhou will keep it a secret for me."

Using a grotto-heaven secret realm to cultivate spiritual herbs?

Such a thing was not strange, but the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven was different from ordinary secret realms. It was the cradle for cultivating high-level talents of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, and the power within needed to be continuously accumulated.

Normally, they would not waste it on spiritual herbs and plants. It was usually opened every five hundred years for newly advanced Sages to comprehend the true meaning of the laws.

The Gu family actually dared to do such a thing secretly?

Zhou Fuyou said calmly, "If it needs to be kept secret, why tell me the truth?"

Gu Muyu gave a bitter smile. "Actually, people have tried this before. But each opening requires five hundred years. Placed within the grotto-heaven, that's fifty thousand years. Such a long time—the spiritual herbs planted by the previous generation either withered or were destroyed by illusion beasts. There were few harvests. Therefore, the sect's high-level turns a blind eye to such things and doesn't pursue them. I told Senior Brother only hoping you wouldn't spread it around, to avoid the Gu family bearing criticism."

"I see." Zhou Fuyou nodded, then looked inside curiously. "Can I go in and take a look?"

Gu Muyu hesitated for a moment, then moved aside.

"Please!"

She thought it through clearly. Given Zhou Fuyou's age, his talent, and especially now that he had broken through to the Sage stage, he would surely become a high-level figure of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect in the future.

Such an existence could not be easily offended.

Besides, he only wanted to take a look.

Under Gu Muyu's lead, Zhou Fuyou entered the valley.

As soon as he stepped inside, he felt a great difference.

An extremely rich, clear and pure spiritual energy filled the air. Just smelling it was refreshing and uplifting.

Looking around, Zhou Fuyou couldn't help but marvel inwardly.

There were Thunder-Struck Trees growing extremely well, Flame Flame Flowers like clusters of fire, and a large patch of green grass like sharp swords…

"Did Junior Sister plant all these?" Zhou Fuyou was a bit incredulous. With his knowledge, he could naturally tell that most of these rare treasures had already matured.

Gu Muyu shook her head. "Senior Brother overestimates me. I've only been in the grotto-heaven for eighty years. These things were planted by an elder of my Gu family named Gu Yuan when he participated in the grotto-heaven grand competition two hundred years ago."

Zhou Fuyou suddenly understood, thinking to himself: "Two hundred years ago, that was the grotto-heaven grand competition my master participated in."

Calculating it this way, the medicinal herbs here were probably already ten-thousand-year spiritual herbs!

Their medicinal power, their grade…

"Eh, why haven't these flowers, trees, and grasses become sentient?"

"That, I wouldn't know. I was just instructed by the elders in my clan to come in and take care of them. After all, no one expected the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven to open early, before the five hundred years were up."

Zhou Fuyou nodded. This was indeed something many hadn't anticipated.

He walked around the valley but didn't stay long. After chatting with Gu Muyu for a bit, he hurriedly left.

After he left, Gu Muyu finally breathed a sigh of relief.

Everyone said this contemporary prodigy was aloof and hard to get along with, but after actually interacting with him, he seemed okay.

Was it because his realm had broken through?

The woman didn't dwell on it. As long as she endured the last twenty years, her task would be complete.

When she went out then and reported the situation of this spiritual herb valley to the elders in her clan, she would surely be rewarded, and she would also receive the clan's full support when she broke through to the Sage stage.

On the other side.

Leaving the Spiritual Herb Valley, Zhou Fuyou was still thinking about that medicinal valley.

"These great houses and noble clans, their thoughts and plans are all far-reaching. While ordinary families are still chasing petty gains, they have already made plans to cultivate ten-thousand-year spiritual herbs. Once successful, the future benefits will be immense."

"Thinking about it this way, my Zhou clan's development might be a bit short-sighted."

"When I go out, I need to return to the Zhou clan and make some changes based on the actual situation. That way, I won't let down my ancestor's parting instructions."

Perhaps after breaking through in realm, his mood had become much more open.

Zhou Fuyou, who had previously only considered himself and his little sister, now gradually extended his thoughts to his clan.

This might be what it meant: when poor, one cultivates oneself alone; when successful, one benefits the world. Even if 'benefiting the world' only extended to one's own clan members.

Carrying this thought, Zhou Fuyou journeyed on and finally reached his destination before sunset.

Looking down from a high altitude, thirty-seven figures were scattered like stars across a perfectly flat plain.

A feeling of grandeur and vastness arose spontaneously.

It was as if they weren't cultivating here, but suppressing something?

Zhou Fuyou's mind stirred, and he called out to the crowd: "Disciple Zhou Fuyou pays respects to all uncles and masters!"

His arrival had long been noticed by everyone.

Someone laughed. "Since you've achieved Sagehood, you are of the same generation. Calling us 'senior brother' is enough."

Another disagreed. "No, no, no. Although Nephew Zhou also comes from a noble clan, within the Dao Sect, he follows the master-disciple lineage. How can he casually change his address and disrupt the seniority?"

"What do we cultivators care about such things?"

A familiar voice sounded. Uncle-Master Li waved at Zhou Fuyou from a distance.

"Come, I'll take you to meet someone!"

Zhou Fuyou cupped his hands in all directions, then followed behind Uncle-Master Li, walking towards the very center.

When he actually set foot on the ground, he could feel a vast power hidden beneath.

It was an extremely immense power, even more terrifying than the Dark Water Spring within his body. Truly stepping on it gave one a feeling of terror.

"You felt it?" Uncle-Master Li asked with a smile.

Zhou Fuyou nodded, extremely curious. "What is this? And what are you all doing?"

Uncle-Master Li stroked his beard and smiled. "When you see Senior Brother Ke, you will naturally understand everything."

Senior Brother Ke?

Zhou Fuyou's eyes lit up. "Could it be the legendary Chess Saint, Uncle-Master Ke Lianshan?"

Uncle-Master Li hummed in affirmation, then stopped explaining and led Zhou Fuyou forward.

Soon, the two arrived at the very center of the wasteland.

There, an ancient-looking high platform had been erected. An old man sat cross-legged upon it, letting the evening breeze brush his face.

Seeing this old man, who was as famous as the Alchemy Saint, Zhou Fuyou bowed deeply. "Disciple Zhou Fuyou pays respects to Uncle-Master Ke."

Ke Lianshan carefully examined him, and after a long while, said leisurely, "Not bad. You are very good. Having just entered the Sage stage, your dharma power is already this solid and abundant. For someone your age, this is truly rare. It seems Junior Sister Qinglan did not force growth but taught you well."

"Thank you for the praise, Uncle-Master. My master has indeed been very kind to me." After exchanging a few pleasantries, Zhou Fuyou couldn't help but ask, "May I ask why Uncle-Master summoned your disciple here?"

Ke Lianshan's smile faded, and he spoke slowly. "Beneath our feet lies one of the Dao Sect's three treasures—the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard!"

Zhou Fuyou suddenly understood. No wonder he had felt that way earlier.

But could a single spirit treasure be this powerful?

The old man continued to explain in detail, allowing Zhou Fuyou to not only have his questions answered but also to be deeply shocked.

"This spirit treasure once suppressed the Central Continent for nearly four thousand years. Even though it was once damaged and broken, it never changed its nature of suppressing earthly qi and governing the spiritual opportunities of an entire continent."

"We are here, using the spirit treasure and formations, to absorb spiritual opportunities and sense the Central Continent."

"When the right time comes, we will exhaust the creative power of heaven and earth, draw the four great continents back together, and recreate the Central Continent!"

"This act is too heaven-defying, thus requiring an extremely vast amount of power. Everyone present has gathered for this matter. To avoid failing when success is within reach, you may stand guard. At a critical moment, act as a substitute and lend us your strength."

After speaking, Ke Lianshan extended his right hand, withered and bark-like, towards Zhou Fuyou.

"Before that, I will take a wisp of your life soul, allowing you to form a connection with this spirit treasure. Then, your power can be integrated. Are you willing to contribute a share of strength to the sect?"

Take a wisp of life soul!

This matter was quite terrifying to ordinary people.

But for the true disciples of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, it was the most common thing.

Any Sage who achieved the stage would offer a portion of their life soul to reside within the spirit treasure.

This was not just control, as outsiders thought, but a form of protection!

If a Dao Sect Sage went missing or fell in the outside world, the sect would receive corresponding information. And if they were willing to pay a great price, they could even resurrect the fallen one!

When the time came to break through to the Ascendant stage, the Dao Sect Sage could apply to the Sect Master to retrieve their life soul and go all out for the Ascendant breakthrough.

Perhaps afraid that the young man would fear this matter, Ke Lianshan said somewhat kindly, "This matter also has benefits for you. When the Central Continent is reunited, it will trigger a vast tide of spiritual opportunities. I will take this opportunity to break through to the Ascendant stage in one go. Through the connection of the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard, you can also personally experience and comprehend it. When you break through to Ascendant in the future, you will have corresponding experience to refer to."

Zhou Fuyou was stunned. "It can be like that?"

Ke Lianshan laughed. "Others cannot, but I am the one who has bonded with the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard. Achieving this is not difficult."

Zhou Fuyou took a deep breath. "Your disciple dares not refuse. I am willing to contribute a share of strength to the sect!"

"Good!"

Next, Ke Lianshan curled his right hand, extended a finger, and gently tapped it on Zhou Fuyou's brow.

In an instant, a tearing pain arose, and Zhou Fuyou almost fainted.

But at the same time, a feeling of fullness suddenly appeared.

Zhou Fuyou staggered and fell onto the high platform with a thud.

Ke Lianshan didn't notice anything unusual. Although he was the master of the Rotten Axe-Handle, because he hadn't advanced to Ascendant, his control over this spirit treasure wasn't perfect. In the past, when Sect Leader Mo Shouzhuo used the spirit treasure to collect life souls, others had occasionally shown such reactions. He simply thought Zhou Fuyou had cultivated for too few years and his soul wasn't tough enough.

"Junior Brother Li, take Zhou Fuyou to rest. There are still some days before we activate the spirit treasure."

Junior Brother Li acknowledged, led Zhou Fuyou down from the high platform, and settled him at the periphery of the wasteland.

After all, he was only a substitute. Unless an accident occurred, he wouldn't be needed.

But within the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, with no outside interference, what accident could happen?

"Fuyou, rest well for a while."

Zhou Fuyou sat cross-legged on the ground, head lowered, his face unseeable. He only hummed softly.

Uncle-Master Li left calmly, not seeing the young man's lowered face, nor the eyes that were as deep and cold as a frozen abyss.

Within the Purple Mansion, the quiet Dark Water Spring began to boil violently.

A restless atmosphere filled the Purple Mansion.

Furthermore, a wisp of golden light emanated from the spring, enveloping the tiny Sage infant.

The Sage infant began to struggle, but a will suddenly descended. The Sage infant's body stiffened, and it closed its eyes.

When it opened them again, the infant's gaze was exactly the same as the physical body's eyes.

Low, dark, cold, as if it could devour everything!
