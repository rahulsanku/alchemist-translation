# Chapter 1170: Entering the Seventh Ring, Luo Chen Intervenes

The Ninth Ring could no longer accommodate him.

This was the judgment Luo Chen had reached after a full year of cultivation.

Over the course of that year, by constantly adjusting the opening and closing of his apertures and guiding varying degrees of chaotic energy into his body, he had gradually explored his own limits, perfectly mastering the balance between drawing in energy and absorbing and refining it.

But unfortunately, the concentration of chaotic energy in the Ninth Ring could not reach the limit Luo Chen required!

Indeed, the Ninth Ring—which ordinary martial artists considered extremely dangerous—was no longer sufficient to maintain his peak cultivation speed once he had grown accustomed to the environment.

Often, a single session of cultivation with all seven hundred and twenty apertures open could last at most three days.

After three days, his body could still hold up, but the nearby energy would become extremely thin, nearly nonexistent.

Luo Chen would either have to stop and wait for the surrounding chaotic energy to gather again, or he could only open a third of his apertures, allowing him to cultivate continuously.

But the latter option yielded at most one-third of the cultivation speed.

This was unacceptable to Luo Chen.

Therefore, he had to change his cultivation environment.

According to the book Gu Xun had given him and what Xiangfei had said, the closer one got to the tower's center, the denser the chaotic energy became.

Luo Chen pressed forward, heading toward the central area.

Along the way, he occasionally encountered some martial artists immersed in cultivation.

There were quite a few of them, each sitting on cultivation stones of varying sizes.

Luo Chen did not disturb them, actively keeping a safe distance as he passed by.

Just as he was about to reach the center of the Ninth Ring, his steps paused slightly.

Because the owner of the nearest cultivation stone was someone he knew.

When Fang Zhibai saw Luo Chen, a flicker of surprise crossed his face.

"Brother Luo, I heard you were cultivating on the eastern periphery. Why have you come here?"

Luo Chen spoke frankly, "I'm planning to change locations."

Fang Zhibai asked instinctively, "Are you heading to the central platform?"

After saying this, afraid that Luo Chen might not know the danger, he specifically reminded him.

"The central platform is not just a passage to the upper ring; it's also a channel where chaotic energy from above pours down to the next ring. The chaotic energy there is even more violent and is not suitable for martial artists like us to cultivate in. Even Martial Dao Human Immortals are the same!"

"Thank you for the reminder, I know that." Luo Chen smiled, then said, "I'll take a look first. If it doesn't work, I'll go up to the Eighth Ring."

Fang Zhibai was stunned.

He asked incredulously, "It's only been a year, and you're already unsatisfied with the cultivation environment of the Ninth Ring?"

Luo Chen shrugged helplessly. "I'm a bit different from you all."

Well, Fang Zhibai had to admit that people were different.

Take himself, for example. While others were still gradually adapting to the outer areas of the Ninth Ring, he, thanks to the guidance of his clan elders, had long since come to cultivate near the central platform area.

Ordinarily, he took pride in this.

But compared to Luo Chen's progress, that pride was shattered into nothing.

In silence, he recalled the judgment that had led him to give Luo Chen the Carefree Gold Token in the first place.

Back then, the reason he had thought highly of Luo Chen was because the other party possessed a formidable physical body, which allowed him to easily defeat Iron Vajra, who was at the late Furnace Realm.

A physical body of that caliber was indeed uniquely suited to withstand the impact of chaotic energy in these Nine Rings of Ruin.

He had found a reason for the difference between himself and Luo Chen.

Seeing that Luo Chen was about to leave, Fang Zhibai suddenly reminded him, "There's one thing you need to be aware of. The cultivation stones that are everywhere in the lower three rings are quite limited in number once you reach the middle three rings. If you're not absolutely certain, it's best not to force your way into the middle three rings."

Luo Chen looked at him with surprise.

This detail, he truly didn't know.

Neither Gu Xun nor Xiangfei had mentioned it.

The former might not have cared, and the latter simply didn't know. But Fang Zhibai had brought it up, and it was indeed helpful to him.

And most importantly, from these words, it was clear that Fang Zhibai thought highly of him, already considering the time when he would reach the middle three rings.

"Thank you for the advice, Brother Fang!"

Luo Chen cupped his hands, then drifted away.

Watching Luo Chen's retreating figure, Fang Zhibai let out a wistful sigh.

For someone like Luo Chen, once he adapted to the environment, the Nine Rings of Ruin would be like a fish in water, a dragon entering the sea!

...

At the central platform.

Luo Chen observed silently.

Like a standing tornado, violent chaotic energy poured from above, cascading down into the Ninth Ring.

In such an environment, it was impossible for a martial artist to cultivate normally.

Closed apertures would be forcibly pried open by the external pressure.

Luo Chen stood still for a long time, but ultimately decided to set foot on a higher level.

Because of the Primordial Unity Cauldron, even though he could endure it briefly, it was still too dangerous.

He needed to have all dangers under his control.

With a single step, Luo Chen entered the central platform.

Instantly, a tremendous force pressed down on him.

Luo Chen's apertures were tightly shut, his body tense, as he moved upward against the cascading energy.

This upstream process was not long, about half an incense stick's worth of time.

For an ordinary martial artist, it might take longer, but Luo Chen felt no particular difficulty.

As soon as he entered the Eighth Ring, without even looking for a cultivation stone, Luo Chen immediately opened a portion of his apertures and silently observed the concentration of chaotic energy flowing in.

"Compared to the Ninth Ring, the concentration has increased."

"But still not enough!"

"Rather than spending a long time adapting here, only to change cultivation environments again soon, it's better to go directly to the Seventh Ring."

Having made his decision, Luo Chen did not linger and continued forward.

Along the way, compared to the Ninth Ring where martial artists were everywhere, the number in the Eighth Ring was somewhat fewer.

Because most had their apertures tightly closed, he couldn't sense their cultivation levels through qi, but based on Luo Chen's inference, the Eighth Ring was mainly occupied by early Leakless Realm martial artists.

If so, then the Seventh Ring should be mainly occupied by early and some mid-stage martial artists.

Soon, a familiar scene appeared again.

Passing through the central platform of the Eighth Ring, Luo Chen successfully entered the Seventh Ring.

Once inside, he did not stop but headed straight for the central area.

Along the way, any martial artists who encountered him cast surprised glances.

Luo Chen was simply too unfamiliar.

If he were a renowned martial artist, he should be in the middle three rings.

If he were a martial artist who had experienced one or two hundred-year cycles and had long resided in the lower three rings, they should also have some impression of him.

And if he were a newly advanced Leakless Realm martial artist, he should now be in the Ninth Ring, at most the Eighth Ring. How could he possibly set foot in the Seventh Ring?

In the end, although Luo Chen's reputation was known, he had only participated in the trading meeting.

Many people still didn't recognize his face.

Finally, Luo Chen's steps stopped at the central area of the Seventh Ring.

But upon arriving here and looking for a suitable cultivation stone, he discovered that things were quite lively around here lately!

...

Purple Cloud City Lord sat cross-legged on a cultivation stone a full zhang in diameter. Her pale face carried a hint of gloom.

Especially when she looked at the shadowy figures gathered around, her heart sank to the bottom.

She had succeeded!

But she had also failed!

Not long ago, she had taken the Blood Dragon Ginseng she had purchased at the trading meeting, nourished the hidden injuries on her meridians, and broken through to the mid-stage of the Leakless Realm in one go!

After breaking through, her boldness surged. She wanted to take advantage of the remaining medicinal power of the Blood Dragon Ginseng to enter the Sixth Ring within the middle three rings for cultivation.

And she had indeed successfully broken into the Sixth Ring.

However, the number of cultivation stones in the Sixth Ring was extremely limited, only thirty-six in total.

To secure a foothold, she carefully selected a martial artist who had entered the Ninefold Immortal Tower at the same time as her and issued a challenge.

If she won, she could take his cultivation stone.

But unfortunately, she lost.

Without a stable cultivation stone, a martial artist simply could not cultivate in an environment constantly scoured by chaotic energy.

Purple Cloud City Lord was forced to retreat to the Seventh Layer.

If that were all, it would be one thing.

But precisely because of that challenge, she had suffered considerable injuries.

As a result, her cultivation stone, which still retained some warmth, had attracted the covetous gaze of another strong practitioner.

Today was the day that strong practitioner would come to challenge her.

And the people gathered around were here to watch the excitement.

Yes, even within the Nine Rings of Ruin, there was no shortage of spectators eager for a show.

The cultivation environment here was too lonely and cold. One would often stay for a hundred years, with only an occasional one-year break to go outside.

Even though high-level martial artists had strong wills, it was still hard to bear.

Therefore, whenever a battle over a cultivation stone occurred, the martial artists would agree on a time to go and watch together.

Over time, an unwritten rule had gradually formed here.

Regarding disputes over cultivation stones, a formal challenge had to be issued, with more than three martial artists notified and invited as witnesses.

If the challenge succeeded, no one else could challenge for the same cultivation stone within ten years.

If the challenge failed, no one else could challenge the original owner for ten years either. This was to avoid the attrition of a war of attrition.

It was called an unwritten rule.

Because there were always those extremely powerful martial artists who would ignore any opponent and directly issue a challenge.

Although it broke the rules, as long as they could also bear the consequences of facing endless challenges from latercomers, it was fine.

Purple Cloud City Lord was somewhat regretful.

She regretted not arranging for a friend to challenge her in advance.

That way, they could put on an act, she would win, and she could keep this decent cultivation stone.

Although it might be a bit embarrassing in the eyes of the discerning.

But this matter couldn't be entirely blamed on her.

She had entered a hundred years ago.

Among that batch, those with exceptional talent had entered the Sixth Ring. Those with poor aptitude couldn't even enter the Ninefold Immortal Tower and were forced to return.

As for the rest, they either remained in the Eighth or Ninth Ring, or had already silently fallen.

She herself was immersed in cultivation and had almost no friends within this Seventh Ring.

"If only I had joined the Vulture Alliance and used their connections to keep this place."

The thought flashed by, but regret was useless.

Purple Cloud City Lord had to face the approaching enemy.

As the surrounding crowd let out shouts, Purple Cloud City Lord knew her enemy had arrived.

"Old Freak Qu!"

From afar, a strange, cackling laugh came.

Then, an odd-looking old man with pale eyes and a face where all features were scrunched together broke through the air and stopped in the center of the crowd.

"Purple Cloud City Lord, hand over this place. Don't force me to make a move!"

Purple Cloud City Lord's face was gloomy, and she said coldly, "Do you really think you're a match for me?"

Old Freak Qu cackled, "Our strength is evenly matched, and now you're injured on top of that."

"So what if I'm injured?" Purple Cloud City Lord snorted coldly. "In a life-and-death struggle, it's still unknown who will win."

At these words, the crowd couldn't help but murmur in admiration.

This Purple Cloud City Lord was indeed fierce and unyielding, truly a remarkable woman who dared to force her way into the Sixth Ring.

It was worth noting that many of those present had entered the Nine Rings of Ruin a hundred, or even two or three hundred, years ago.

None of them dared to force their way into the Sixth Ring, but Purple Cloud City Lord did.

What she relied on was that courageous and fierce will in martial arts cultivation.

However, she had ultimately failed, which was why she had ended up in her current predicament.

Old Freak Qu spread his hands, his scrunched-up face revealing a creepy smile.

"Do you really want a life-and-death struggle? Win or lose, you will inevitably be severely injured. And now that the Ninefold Immortal Tower has just closed, do you have the confidence to hold on in the Nine Rings of Ruin until the next opening a hundred years from now, to go out and seek healing? Or do you intend to let your martial arts stop here?"

A series of rhetorical questions gradually silenced Purple Cloud City Lord.

This was the truth.

Moreover, even if she held on until the next opening a hundred years later, could she really be as lucky as this time and manage to bid for a Blood Dragon Ginseng?

Most importantly, after wasting a hundred years, given her lifespan, even if she had the chance to enter the middle three rings in the future, it would be absolutely impossible for her to take another step forward and achieve the Martial Dao Human Immortal realm.

So, the answer before her was actually only one.

Endure, admit defeat!

Relying on the effect of the Blood Dragon Ginseng in nourishing her meridians, she would make a comeback in ten years!

"Ten years?"

Just as Purple Cloud City Lord was hesitating, a voice suddenly rang out from the crowd.

"This place has a quiet environment and is spacious. How about letting it go to me, Luo?"

At these words, the scene instantly fell silent.

An interloper?

Everyone instinctively turned their gazes toward the person who had spoken.

White robes, black hair, handsome brows and bright eyes.

As he stepped out from the crowd, he carried an air of detachment and otherworldliness that was completely different from that of a martial arts strong practitioner.

Purple Cloud City Lord was stunned for a moment, then instinctively called out his name.

"Luo Chen!"

Luo Chen smiled and nodded.

The crowd was bewildered. The name was somewhat unfamiliar, yet somewhat familiar, as if they had heard it somewhere before.

But soon, through word of mouth, everyone learned of his origins.

A remnant immortal from the Remnant Immortal City, a former cultivator, now a Leakless Realm martial artist.

Most importantly, he was suspected to be a high-level alchemist, holding the Meridian-Mending Pill, which was extremely precious to martial artists!

Purple Cloud City Lord hesitated. "But..."

"I, Luo, won't take your cultivation stone for nothing. One Meridian-Mending Pill as compensation, how about it?" Luo Chen tossed out a jade bottle.

Amidst a crowd of envious eyes, Purple Cloud City Lord caught the jade bottle.

Her expression shifting, she had already made her decision and said heavily, "This place is yours!"

The sudden turn of events caught everyone off guard.

Especially Old Freak Qu, who had come to challenge her. He felt a sense of absurdity.

I was here first!

Luo Chen leaped lightly and landed on the cultivation stone, which was about a zhang in size. As he looked it over, he nodded repeatedly.

Not bad, not bad!

Among the lower three rings, this cultivation stone was indeed excellent. Moreover, because it had been scoured by chaotic energy for years, a thin layer of defensive stone film had formed on it, providing a brief shielding effect when the martial artist was resting.

Most importantly, based on Luo Chen's observation, the concentration of chaotic energy around it far exceeded that of other places.

This was probably also the reason Purple Cloud City Lord had been able to quickly charge into the Sixth Ring.

And of course, it was also the reason Old Freak Qu had set his sights on this place.

Luo Chen's smile did not fade as he looked around, finally settling his gaze on Old Freak Qu's face.

"This friend, do you have any objections?"

Old Freak Qu ground his teeth and said, "Yes, and a big one!"

Luo Chen flicked his hands and beckoned to Old Freak Qu.

"Very well, then come!"
