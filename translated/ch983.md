# Chapter 983: Dharma Sea Suppresses Demons, Demon and Monster Join Forces

Amidst the sky-filling sand and dust, two figures slowly advanced.

They did not seem to raise a magical barrier, yet all the sandstorms around them appeared to have no effect whatsoever.

"I wonder when this sandstorm we caused will finally dissipate?"

Luo Chen walked through the all-encompassing sandstorm, quite emotional.

Sages were already the most supreme existence below Ascendants in this realm. If they did not act, all was well, but once they fought without restraint, they often caused immeasurable consequences.

Whether it was the former Absolute Spirit Domain or the Dieyun Domain, both were destroyed under such circumstances.

Although only two people participated in his battle with Azure Frost, the final techniques each employed were difficult to match even if seven or eight more Sages joined in.

The post-battle destructive effects also exceeded imagination.

That sandstorm, from its birth to now, had lasted nearly ten days.

Not only had it not dissipated, but it continued to expand, sweeping from south to north unceasingly, filling heaven and earth with flying sand and rolling stones, a vast expanse of murky yellow.

Azure Frost beside him remained unmoved, merely holding a compass-like magic treasure in her hand, frowning as she fiddled with it.

Luo Chen glanced at it and asked, "What is that?"

Azure Frost replied without raising her head, "A Star-Fixing Compass."

"Star-Fixing Compass?" Luo Chen was startled. He had never heard of such a magic treasure. What he saw on it were only hazy and indistinct specks of light.

Azure Frost hummed in acknowledgment, "This treasure was refined by my sister in her spare time, recording the teleportation nodes covered by that super teleportation formation at Green Parasol Mountain. I recall there are one or two places here in the Western Desert as well, but now, affected by the sandstorm and my own..."

Before she could finish speaking, Azure Frost's willow-leaf brows flew upward, and her form began to gradually dissipate, about to merge into the void.

Yet for some unknown reason, the mana fluctuations on her body subtly clashed with this void.

She stumbled and fell out.

On the other side, Luo Chen reacted even faster. Between the annihilation of sparks, he had already shifted dozens of zhang away.

Seeing this, Luo Chen frowned and raised his hand toward the void above.

"Hmph!"

With a muffled grunt, Luo Chen felt a weight like Mount Tai pressing down and had to relax.

Taking this opportunity, Azure Frost finally flew away from her original spot.

And just as they left the next moment...

*Dong!*

A huge purple bowl inverted down from the sky, covering a hundred zhang range, nearly trapping the two inside.

"Eh?"

A light exclamation came from outside the sandstorm, seemingly quite surprised that the two had managed to dodge this move.

Then, a Buddhist chant sounded from outside, transforming into a surging wave of sound that dispersed the surrounding sandstorm completely.

Under the boundless desert, hundreds of figures watched the separated man and woman from front and back.

Luo Chen looked up and saw that the hundreds of people were clearly divided.

One side wore simple monk robes, holding staffs and monastic swords, completely surrounding Azure Frost.

The other side had varied clothing styles, dressed like ordinary cultivators.

Seeing this, Luo Chen frowned slightly. His divine sense spread and quickly locked onto the true leaders.

He looked across the distance, "May I ask which two fellow Daoists have set up this formation to await us?"

A hearty voice rang out.

"Fellow Daoist, last time I delayed your great demon-slaying matter. This time, Zhou has specially invited Great Master Cihai from Mount Wutai, only to make up for my mistake."

Two figures descended from the sky.

One was the human Sage who had briefly appeared earlier.

The other was a dignified, righteous-looking bald great monk, presumably the Great Master Cihai invited by this Zhou-named Sage.

He was currently reaching out to retrieve that purple bowl.

As it continuously shrank, it transformed into a purple-gold alms bowl held in his hand.

A pair of Dharma eyes fell doubtfully on Luo Chen and Azure Frost, seemingly very surprised that the two were walking together.

"I am Zhou Mingxuan, clan leader of the Zhou clan at the foot of Mount Wutai. This is Great Master Cihai, abbot of Zhenhai Temple on Mount Wutai. May I ask for the fellow Daoist's name?" Zhou Mingxuan spoke quite amiably.

Luo Chen pursed his lips, originally intending to use a pseudonym to muddle through.

But considering he was currently showing his true face, he saved himself the trouble.

"Luo Chen."

Zhou Mingxuan was stunned. He racked his brains but could not find any Western Desert powerhouse matching that name in his memories.

It was Great Master Cihai who spoke with surprise and uncertainty, "The eminent one from the Eastern Wilderness, Alchemist Luo Chen?"

"Haha, I didn't expect that even here in the Western Desert, someone knows my name." Luo Chen smiled freely and nodded in acknowledgment.

As he admitted it, Great Master Cihai's face showed respect.

Zhou Mingxuan beside him was puzzled. Cihai's lips moved slightly as he briefly explained via voice transmission.

Luo Chen did not care about their conversation, nor was he surprised that they knew his origins and name.

The reputations of top Sages in this realm often were not confined to a single continent.

Especially among those at the same cultivation level, some famous figures would be closely watched by those intelligence-sensitive forces.

Like the Three Great Wanderers of the Northern Sea, or the Chess Saint of Central Continent, the former Alchemy Saint of the Eastern Wilderness, Tian Duzi, and so on.

Now, Alchemist Luo Chen had taken the position of the Alchemy Saint.

What he was curious about now was their grand, showy display.

His gaze fell on the hundreds of monks surrounding Azure Frost and the formations they had each formed.

"What are you all doing?"

Zhou Mingxuan had already learned of Luo Chen's origins and was even more respectful.

He said with slight reverence, "Earlier, I ruined the fellow Daoist's great demon-slaying matter, feeling deeply guilty. Therefore, I specially invited Great Master Cihai from Mount Wutai to assist you. Now, with the three of us joining forces, and the Five Directions Sea of Suffering Formation set up on the side, we can surely slay this demoness!"

Luo Chen frowned and looked toward Azure Frost.

Coincidentally, she was also looking coldly back at him.

Luo Chen pursed his lips, hesitated for a moment, and slowly said, "There's no need..."

But before he could finish, the dignified Great Master Cihai had already spoken out loudly.

"Disciples, form the formation! Subdue the demon and vanquish the fiend!"

"Drink!"

The hundreds of monks responded in unison.

In an instant, figures flickered, completely surrounding Azure Frost at the center.

These monks were no ordinary opponents; each had considerable cultivation.

If divided by Qi Refinement realm, they should be at the Adept stage.

A hundred Adepts, combined with a formation, would be difficult for even an ordinary Sage to handle.

Was Azure Frost an ordinary Sage?

Clearly not!

She stood proudly within the formation, facing the overwhelming staff shadows that came at her, neither dodging nor avoiding. She merely waved her sleeve.

A vast suction force erupted without cause.

Light flickered, and the hundreds of monks strangely disappeared.

It was the divine ability that had once overwhelmed the Luo Tian Sect's pioneering army — Sleeve Universe!

This move might lack power against those of the same cultivation level, but against those below the Sage stage, it was a sure catch, taking in as many as came.

In an instant, the surroundings were cleared.

Seeing this, Cihai's expression changed slightly.

"What a great demon! This old monk will surely subdue you today! Watch my Sumeru Bowl!"

As he spoke, that purple-gold alms bowl appeared again, enlarging in the air and covering Azure Frost.

A power that bound space and suppressed everything spread in all directions.

Azure Frost, within it, frowned slightly and retreated repeatedly.

This magic treasure was somewhat strange!

It seemed to greatly restrict her.

Especially now that she appeared unharmed, she was actually severely injured, with only ten or twenty percent of her mana remaining, and the Accumulated Void Flower limiting her.

With internal and external troubles, facing Cihai's move was indeed somewhat difficult.

"Great Master, I'll assist you!"

Zhou Mingxuan shouted loudly, manipulating his flying sword to join the battle.

Facing the pincer attack of the two, Azure Frost seemed somewhat angered.

At some point, she had already put on those gloves.

The sword came, and she suddenly grabbed it.

In Zhou Mingxuan's shock, she directly crushed it into a pile of fragments.

Then she threw it back!

*Clang! Clang! Clang!*

The flying sword fragments struck the Sumeru Bowl, producing a series of metallic clashes.

Then Azure Frost stepped lightly, as if no longer resisting the suppression and absorption power of the purple-gold alms bowl, charging headlong into it and raising her fist.

*Dong!*

A dull reverberation spread across the wilderness.

Then, under everyone's shocked gaze, the Sumeru Bowl flew backward, covered in shocking crack lines.

"Pfft!"

"Hmph!"

Zhou Mingxuan's lifebound magic treasure was destroyed, and with the connection of heart and mind, he spat blood.

Great Master Cihai's heavy treasure was damaged, and he was not much better off.

In one go, Azure Frost's capabilities were fully displayed.

Breaking the formation, destroying the treasure.

Then, pressing the advantage without mercy, she closed in.

A fair white wrist reached out from the void, smashing toward the big bald head.

Seeming to know that the opponent was not an easy target, Great Master Cihai had already prepared, a golden light covering his body.

*Boom!*

Under one punch, the golden light swayed unceasingly.

Azure Frost's figure flashed again, shifting dozens of zhang, and kicked Zhou Mingxuan, who was caught off guard.

Even though Zhou Mingxuan had also prearranged defensive measures, under this punch, they shattered inch by inch, and his entire body fell into the desert like a torn cloth sack.

"Clan leader!"

"Brother Zhou!"

The remaining group of onlookers, facing all that had happened in the blink of an eye, were both shocked and terrified.

Azure Frost snorted coldly, about to strike again.

But a figure had already blocked her path.

Azure Frost narrowed her eyes, staring at the familiar profile.

"You want to stop me?"

Luo Chen sighed, "The Western Desert is ultimately the home ground of the Buddhist sect. Mass slaughter will not benefit our subsequent actions. Let it end here!"

Azure Frost was not someone unreasonable. Her anger gradually dissipated.

Looking at the two with fear in their eyes, she snorted lightly and flicked her large sleeve.

*Whoosh!*

The hundred or so monks immediately fell to the ground like rolling gourds, dazed and confused, as if unaware of what had happened.

"If you dare chase again, die!"

Leaving these words, Azure Frost turned and left.

Luo Chen smiled helplessly, cupped his hands in apology to the crowd behind him, and then chased after her.

Faint sounds of their conversation could still be heard in the wind.

"Your temper is getting more irritable by the day!"

"Wouldn't you be angry if someone surrounded you for no reason?"

"Haha, true enough."

And at the original battlefield, the crowd looked at each other in bewilderment.

Zhou Mingxuan, pale-faced, was helped up and walked to Cihai, who was regulating his mana.

"Great Master, I... cough, cough."

Large chunks of blood came out of his mouth. His physical injuries were more severe than they appeared to outsiders.

And this was merely from one kick by Azure Frost.

He said with difficulty, "It was my oversight. I didn't expect that even severely injured, this demon still had such capability."

Great Master Cihai slowly opened his eyes, a trace of faint anger in his gaze.

"Don't blame yourself. Originally, I imagined that Luo Chen would also stand with us. I never expected that demon and monster would join forces!"

Demon and monster joining forces!

These four words were spoken with a chilling tone.

Zhou Mingxuan opened his mouth but didn't know what to say.

Clearly, what they had seen before was a life-and-death situation between that one person and one demon. How had they come together in just a few days?

Although Luo Chen had not made a move and had even helped them block the killing blow, still...

He hesitated, "Alchemist Luo Chen, as you said, is also known as the Demon-Slaying Sage. He shouldn't be of the demonic path, right?"

Cihai slowly stood up, "That aura of malice, balefulness, and even blood energy on him is denser than any demonic villain or demon clan expert I have ever seen. If it were just the path of killing to protect life, that would be one thing. But he walks together with a great demon like the Azure Luan. You say he has not fallen into the demonic path?"

Zhou Mingxuan was speechless.

Cihai reached out to retrieve the purple-gold alms bowl, looking at the sparse cracks on it, with a hint of resentment in his eyes.

"They won't get away!"

"That demoness's mana is unstable, and I did not see the legendary style of the strongest under Ascendants. She is undoubtedly severely injured."

"Such a great demon dares to enter our territory. Mount Wutai will not allow her to run rampant on the pure land of Buddhism!"

"And that Luo Chen..."

Zhou Mingxuan hesitated, "If we gather experts to surround and kill Azure Frost, it might still be possible. But Luo Chen once fought her to a standstill. If he interferes?"

"Hmph!"

Cihai snorted coldly, "This is the Western Desert, not the Eastern Wilderness! It's not his turn to act fierce here. If he truly wants to collude with the demoness, don't blame this old monk for inviting the eminent monks of the Hanging Temple down the mountain."

Zhou Mingxuan was stunned, "Hasn't the Hanging Temple closed its gates?"

Cihai glanced at him and said calmly, "You have been guarding the rear border for a long time and don't know the frontline situation. Do you think the demon army led by the Ancient Red Sand Scorpion withdrew from the Buddhist lands because of those cultivators sent by the Tianyuan Dao Sect?"

The Hanging Temple's mountain gates had opened wide!

...

Yellow sand rolled, figures flickered.

Since breaking away from Zhou Mingxuan, Cihai, and the others, Luo Chen and Azure Frost's pace grew faster and faster.

The starlight on the Star-Fixing Compass had also converged on one point.

"Buddha Mother Temple is our target. There is a teleportation formation that my sister and I arranged together, which can directly reach Green Parasol Mountain."

Luo Chen was extremely curious.

"What exactly does Rosycloud seek by spreading the super teleportation formations of Green Parasol Mountain across the four continents of the Mountains and Seas Realm? It can't just be for convenience in traveling, can it?"

Azure Frost put away the Star-Fixing Compass and said indifferently, "Don't ask what you shouldn't ask."

"Heh."

Luo Chen smiled. Although she didn't say it, he could naturally guess a thing or two.

No matter how unruly and powerful Rosycloud was, she wouldn't go against the world, wanting to destroy all sacred lands.

The reason the teleportation formations were spread across the four continents should be to seek certain assistance.

Assistance for attacking Central Continent!

That Central Continent was suppressed by the spirit treasure Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard, which gathered the spiritual energy of the five continents, making it the strongest cultivation blessed land in this realm.

To break this place, even someone as strong as Rosycloud would find it extremely difficult.

From some intelligence he had obtained, the four Archaic beasts that attacked Central Continent in that battle happened to come from the four continents of east, south, west, and north, subtly matching the geography and fate.

The Tianyuan Dao Sect forcibly seized the spiritual essence of the Mountains and Seas Realm to nourish Central Continent was itself an act against the Heavenly Dao.

Archaic beasts were the darlings of heaven and earth in this realm.

Rosycloud leading the four continents' Archaic beasts to attack Central Continent to reclaim the spiritual mechanisms and fate was also an act in accordance with heaven's will.

Therefore, the teleportation formations spread across the four continents should be one of her arrangements for gathering helpers over the years.

Of course, there might be some other considerations, but those were not something Luo Chen could guess now.

His gaze looked into the distance, and he murmured, "Buddha Mother Temple? Legend has it that the Peacock Great Bright King gave birth to the Buddha and is revered as the Buddha Mother. And the great demon Peacock was born from the Phoenix. Did you establish this stronghold based on that?"

Azure Frost said nothing, merely hurrying on.

But both were severely injured, and their pace was far slower than before.

Even as Luo Chen's mana gradually recovered and he took out a traveling flying boat, their speed was not much faster.

In fact, three days later, Luo Chen's flying boat was forced to stop.

A grand Buddhist chant came from afar.

"Heaven has the virtue of loving life. Benefactor Azure Frost, if you can put down your butcher's knife and become the guardian spirit venerable of Mount Wutai, your life may be spared."

Azure Frost's lips curled slightly. She glanced at Luo Chen beside her, then her killing intent surged forth!

Luo Chen pursed his lips and shook his head helplessly.

(Chapter End)
