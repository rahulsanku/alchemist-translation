# Chapter 641: A Self-Created Domain, the All-Encompassing Fire Prison

Luo Chen said he still needed to think about the Sunken Sea, but the moment he gave Mountain Lord Hundred Craft's set of Seven Baleful Swords to Han Zhan, he had already shown his resolve to make the trip.

Such hesitation and repeated changes of mind rarely appeared in Luo Chen.

But the changes in this matter had to be analyzed according to the circumstances.

At first, after years of seclusion and the successful completion of his treasure, he had been brimming with confidence. With spirit medicine tempting him as well, he had easily made his decision.

After the journey to Withered Wood Ridge, however, where he had directly faced Sage-level battle power, his heart had inevitably wavered, and thoughts of self-preservation had arisen.

In the end, though, Ding Yi's intelligence left him no room to be cautious about his own skin.

Once the Sunken Sea was thoroughly controlled by the demon race, what would it matter even if he reached the late Golden Core stage in the future, or even Great Perfection?

Across the vast Northern Sea, fourth-tier Demon Emperors stronger than Golden Core cultivators were beyond counting. A key place like the Sunken Sea would certainly have Demon Emperors guarding it. At that point, he would no longer have any chance of obtaining a Five Elements Lotus Platform.

Luo Chen had searched for that spirit medicine for many years and had found nothing.

Both Ding Yi and Han Zhan had told him that the thing basically did not circulate on the market. Only Sages occasionally traded it among themselves.

In reality, even the stock in Sages' hands was pitifully small.

The true situation was that the Five Elements Lotus Platform was controlled by the Holy Lands of the Five Continents.

He did not know where Qingdan Valley's portion of Five Elements Lotus Platform had come from, but he knew that the Sunken Sea was his only chance.

What truly made him settle his resolve was also Han Zhan's promise.

The other party had agreed to lend him aid at the critical moment, just as he had when they encountered He Yuan, who had been possessed by Blood Nightmare Moluo, on Cold Light Island.

With a Sage protecting him, and with Luo Chen himself acting more carefully, harboring no greed, leaving as soon as he obtained the Five Elements Lotus Platform, the safety of this trip would be greatly guaranteed.

Of course, relying on others in all things was not Luo Chen's nature.

Once he truly made up his mind, he began preparing with all his strength for this exploration of the ruins.

The direction of his preparations was precisely the several problems he had identified beside the cold abyss on Purple Spirit Island.

Once those three problems were solved, his strength would inevitably surge. With greater strength, he would naturally have a few more life-preserving methods.

For this reason, he even had Heavenjade postpone the second-day meeting with the Zhou clan's Golden Core cultivator.

Tailoring a defensive magic treasure for someone was time-consuming and laborious.

Especially when his artifact forging still depended on the Primordial Unity Cauldron, the matter became even more troublesome.

Because the Primordial Unity Cauldron was involved in the secret art he had been researching during this period.

"You want to imitate that Primordial Demon Sect remnant and research a pseudo-domain art?"

"Yes. Senior once said this art was rather useless to me, but if I truly am going to the Sunken Sea, I feel that mastering it may give me a few more means of preserving my life."

"Times differ. Your consideration is not wrong. But since you came looking for me, do you already have an idea?"

On the solitary island in Peng Lake, Han Zhan looked curiously at Luo Chen.

Luo Chen abandoned the distance he had maintained from Han Zhan over the years and recently began visiting him often, all to use the other party's help to perfect his conception of a pseudo-domain.

He immediately told Han Zhan everything he had researched over the years.

Back then, the white-robed cultivator from the Primordial Demon Sect had used a split-soul Ghost King, the Myriad Souls Banner, and corresponding secret arts to arrange a pseudo-domain capable of suppressing Golden Core cultivators of the same rank.

Luo Chen's plan was to use his own Golden Core, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, and a formation to achieve a similar effect.

This idea undeniably carried the shadow of Mountain Lord Hundred Craft's Northern Dipper Seven Peaks Array.

After Han Zhan listened, he sank into thought, clicking his tongue from time to time.

"Using your lifebound magic treasure to amplify the might of your mana, pouring your own mana outward to form a small-scale mana domain?"

"Such a method is far too wasteful. Ordinary Golden Core cultivators would never dare even imagine it."

"You are different. First, your five-spirit-root physique has forged an extremely deep mana foundation. Second, you cultivate the top-grade Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture, refining pure and domineering fire-attribute mana with strong suppressive effects. Finally, your Primordial Unity Cauldron truly has sufficient foundation to amplify externalized mana severalfold."

Luo Chen nodded repeatedly. Only after deducing it again and again had he confirmed that he possessed the necessary foundation, daring to attempt such an audacious plan.

In particular, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, his lifebound magic treasure, possessed a foundation that was far too profound.

It itself had miraculous effects in suppression, confinement, and capture.

What Luo Chen intended was merely to use the power of a formation to bring those traits to their utmost.

At the end, Luo Chen added, "Senior, don't forget that I have also refined a second Origin Pill. In terms of accumulated mana, among the Golden Core cultivators of this age, there is likely no one above me!"

Han Zhan's eyes lit up. He had nearly forgotten this point.

He himself had taught Luo Chen the Origin Pill Art, which Luo Chen had cultivated using the third-tier Great Perfection Hidden Dust Sand.

Over these years, he also had the assistance of Peng Lake's Star Dou Hall, comparable to an upper-grade third-tier cultivation site. Luo Chen's accumulated mana had likely reached a terrifying extreme.

According to Luo Chen's idea, his lifebound Golden Core would serve as the core of the pseudo-domain, while the second Origin Pill would act as the mana core for ordinary battle. Using both together would not affect his normal combat.

"I have to say, your idea is very interesting, and it does indeed possess a certain feasibility. What do you need me to do now?"

Luo Chen's expression became solemn as he looked at the Nascent Soul miniature.

"I need Senior to unfold your domain and let me experience its might up close. Only by feeling it personally can I imitate it, and perhaps even borrow its prestige!"

Han Zhan was startled. Then his Nascent Soul trembled, as if he had thought of something.

He gave Luo Chen a deep look.

"Very well. Since you have a request, how could this old man refuse?"

As he spoke, a thought moved in his mind. Nascent Soul mana began to release outward. At the same time, heaven and earth seemed to answer it, and large amounts of wandering spiritual qi surged over from the eight hundred li of Peng Lake.

In an instant.

Boom!

On the solitary island, it was as if heavenly might had descended.

Luo Chen's entire body shook. He was pressed flat to the ground by an enormous, indescribable pressure.

It had caught him a little off guard.

He gritted his teeth. His bones trembled, blood rushed through him, and he actually tried to stand bit by bit.

Watching Luo Chen go from lying prostrate with all five limbs on the ground to half-kneeling, Han Zhan could not help inhaling slightly.

With his mana suppressed, this boy could still move.

The strength of his body was simply inhuman.

It was hard to imagine. If the other party's physique rose another level, would he be able to resist the suppression of a Sage domain without breaking through to the Golden Core stage?

The thought flashed through Han Zhan's mind. His expression did not change, and the three-inch Nascent Soul spoke calmly.

"Do not resist. Calm your mind and feel what a domain is."

"Only by understanding its essence can you imitate it, or even resist it."

Luo Chen took a deep breath and painstakingly adjusted his body until he sat cross-legged on the ground.

Even though his flesh and bones continuously sent signals of unbearable weight, he still mobilized his divine sense to perceive the surrounding situation.

Gradually, he detected a few things.

Before he knew it, the pressure around him scattered.

Luo Chen looked over in confusion. Han Zhan smiled helplessly.

"In the end, this old man has no fleshly body. I do not dare manipulate the domain too much, or my Nascent Soul will be assimilated by heaven and earth's spiritual qi. Go back for now. Come to me once every month and feel the power of a true Sage domain."

Luo Chen nodded, stood drenched in sweat, bowed to Han Zhan in thanks, and then flew toward Peng Island.

On the way back, he muttered one word.

"Assimilated..."

Inside Star Dou Hall, Luo Chen sat cross-legged. The mana in his body was like a tide, surging outward as he attempted to merge it with the dense spiritual qi outside.

Thoughts rose and fell ceaselessly in his mind.

'Since ancient times, fighting across realms has always been incomparably difficult because high-realm cultivators possess natural suppression over low-realm cultivators.'

'This suppression is not merely a difference in the quantity of mana. It lies even more in some kind of essential transformation.'

'Foundation Establishment True Cultivators are stronger than Qi Refinement cultivators because their divine sense can externalize. They can control artifacts and magic treasures more easily, and their attack range also greatly increases with divine sense supporting it.'

'Golden Core High Men can extinguish Foundation Establishment True Cultivators with a flick of a finger because of the enormous difference between mana and spiritual power, forming so-called spiritual pressure. The two share the same origin, yet one surpasses the other and crushes it.'

'As for the domain power of a Sage...'

'When a cultivator forms a Nascent Soul, essence, qi, and spirit, the three treasures, unite to forge the body of a Nascent Soul, allowing it to face the might of heaven and earth directly. In essence, it is pure mana creating a second body for the soul outside the fleshly body's sea of consciousness. Mana originates from heaven and earth's spiritual qi, so the Nascent Soul body can control heaven and earth spiritual qi of the corresponding attribute. Thus the so-called domain is formed.'

'What I sensed recently was correct. Within that range, almost all of it was pure wood-attribute spiritual qi. Han Zhan's mana within it was like a general leading ten thousand soldiers, producing thorough suppression against me as an individual.'

Amid his rolling thoughts, Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes.

He increasingly felt that his judgment had taken the correct direction.

Han Zhan had lost his fleshly body, which meant that the Nascent Soul formed from the union of the three treasures lacked the part called "essence."

Without the separation and concealment of blood essence and physique, if the Nascent Soul body frequently manipulated heaven and earth's spiritual qi, it would be extremely easy for it to be assimilated by heaven and earth.

After all, how could the influence of an individual's soul compare to the vastness of natural heaven and earth?

This was the reason Han Zhan had said the word "assimilated."

Under these circumstances, if Luo Chen wanted to walk the path of the pseudo-domain, he could not learn from a true Sage domain.

Because he could not control the wandering spiritual qi outside. Before refining, purifying, and attaching his own will to it, he could not influence external spiritual qi with his own will.

'Since it cannot be used by me, then reject it. Let the space I occupy contain only my own mana. In this way, it is equivalent to forming a special Sage domain.'

After thinking this through, Luo Chen's spirit shook.

Without hesitation, the Golden Core in his body spun rapidly. Great waves of mana poured out, were amplified by the Primordial Unity Cauldron, and surged outward again.

Only this time, he no longer tried to merge with the heaven and earth spiritual qi in Star Dou Hall. Instead, he forcefully repelled it.

It was like a giant wave pushing aside the surrounding seawater.

In an instant, fire waves rolled through Star Dou Hall.

If Luo Chen was compared to a giant wave, and heaven and earth to an endless sea, then the wandering spiritual qi was the seawater.

A single giant wave naturally could not compare to the endless sea.

But when it surged and struck, it could still push away a certain amount of seawater, ensuring that its own crest continued to rise.

The Sages merely controlled a greater quantity of seawater. They were still far from forming an ocean of their own.

Standing within the rolling fire waves, Luo Chen's eyes were bright.

"This time, perhaps I have truly found the right path!"

Without hesitation, he withdrew all his mana, left Star Dou Hall, and rushed straight to Han Zhan's cave residence.

The familiar heaven and earth spiritual qi pressed down on him again, like a prison confining Luo Chen.

Luo Chen glanced at Han Zhan's location, then released surging mana according to his conception.

In an instant, green and crimson light opposed each other in the void.

Within that dreamlike radiance, Luo Chen slowly stood.

Han Zhan was incomparably astonished. "Your physique... no, your pseudo-domain succeeded?"

The domain withdrew, and Luo Chen also withdrew his mana.

He frowned and shook his head. "Not yet. It only has an embryonic form. Besides, Senior probably used only a fraction of your strength just now, right?"

Han Zhan gave a hum. "Around thirty percent."

Thirty percent was enough.

At least for the current Luo Chen, it was enough.

Thoughtfully, he walked outside.

His conception still had flaws. It was only an embryonic form. If he wanted to resist the might of a Sage domain, there were still many areas to perfect.

The first point was to replace the Northern Dipper Seven Peaks Array.

That formation truly did not suit him.

Watching that departing back filled with contemplation, Han Zhan sighed inwardly in amazement.

'This boy's talent in spells is truly frightening. He can actually create a secret art comparable to a Sage domain while still in the Golden Core realm.'

After his amazement, a startling thought arose.

'If he truly advances to the Sage stage, will his comprehension of the principles of heaven and earth also seem divinely aided?'

The so-called principles of heaven and earth were the laws of nature.

And such mysterious things were generally controlled only by Ascendant great powers. Those who could comprehend them at the Sage stage were few and far between in the present age.

While Luo Chen immersed himself in bitter cultivation, the outside world was not peaceful.

The demon race's movements on the border of the Sunken Sea gradually appeared before the powerful figures of the Myriad Immortals Assembly as time passed.

Ordinary wandering cultivators did not care at all.

But the top Perfected Beings could not ignore it.

After Blood Wanderer issued an order, the cultivators stationed at the Three Oceans defensive line began leaving the line to actively attack low-level demon beasts near the Sunken Sea.

Not only that, more and more high-rank missions related to the Sunken Sea appeared. One Golden Core wandering cultivator after another went to the Sunken Sea under the lure of high mission rewards.

Ding Yi came to find Luo Chen once and explained the situation.

Their shared guess was that Blood Wanderer had likely also set his eyes on the treasure land of the Sunken Sea.

Ding Yi was somewhat anxious, because the date of the previous celestial phenomenon's birth was drawing closer and closer.

Luo Chen, on the other hand, remained unhurried. He merely told Ding Yi to keep watching more closely.

As for Luo Chen himself, he remained immersed in researching his own domain.

"The path at the beginning was wrong. Fortunately, I corrected it later."

On an uninhabited island in Peng Lake, Luo Chen stood alone.

With a thought, blazing, scorching mana erupted from him.

In an instant, the surrounding heaven and earth spiritual qi was pushed aside.

Not only that, the mana was extremely aggressive and began constantly expanding. One li, two li, three li...

Luo Chen moved within it as if he were a fish in water. When his hands formed incantation seals and cast spells, their might was even greater than before.

"A formation is not better simply because it is higher grade. It is better when it suits oneself."

"The Nether Prison Fire-Control Array [TERM?] from Mountain Lord Hundred Craft's storage ring is only third-tier, but it suits me far more."

The third-tier grand formation, the Nether Prison Fire-Control Array [TERM?], had no offensive or defensive ability, nor any power of illusion or suppression. It was merely an ordinary formation that Hundred Craft Mountain, as an artifact-forging sect, usually used to control earthfire.

Under this formation's control, earthfire could be made to burn slowly at low power, or gathered together to burn a city at full force.

After obtaining this formation, Luo Chen engraved it onto his body.

Using his body as the formation base, the cauldron as the formation eye, and his lifebound Golden Core as the energy source, he could thus form a pseudo-domain belonging solely to him.

He named it the All-Encompassing Fire Prison [TERM?].

However...

"The range is too broad. The might is not concentrated enough."

With a thought, Luo Chen began slowly retracting the mana he had released.

From ten li outward, it continuously shrank until it finally formed a fire prison covering an area of one hundred zhang.

Within it, it was as if he stood in Avici purgatory.

Luo Chen's mana became even more lively. When he cast spells, they were faster, and their might was greater.

If an enemy were inside it...

The urge to test his sword arose in Luo Chen's heart.

And the target of this test?

Without hesitation, Luo Chen returned to Peng Island, gave Heavenjade a few instructions, then hurriedly left Peng Lake and headed for the Demon Hunter Office.

*(End of Chapter)*
