# Chapter 1115: Starfire Burns the World, Descendant of an Old Friend

Yellow sand churned, and the red earth stretched for a thousand miles.

A bottomless pit gaped in the vast desert like a bloody maw that could swallow a man whole.

The scorching heat warped the surrounding space.

Within the twisted void, several beams of escaping light shot over.

They all landed before the bottomless pit.

"It should be inside!"

"There are some fragments of the falling star scattered around by the master; the main body should be within."

"Such a massive celestial meteorite, and it could withstand one of the husband's killing techniques. If any remnant remains, its defensive power must be astonishing!"

After exchanging glances, Kongchan volunteered.

"Let this humble monk go down and take a look. If the situation is unfavorable, I can extricate myself easily."

Among the five, Kongchan was the strongest.

He was a Great Demon Emperor who had been famous for many years, and having followed Luo Chen all these years, he had gained many benefits, even learning Luo Chen's Ascendant secret art, the *Moonlight Pure Heart Mantra*.

His current abilities made him a standout among late-stage Sages.

This was evident from the fact that he had been the main force in several previous battles against fifth-rank desolate beasts.

Since he volunteered, no one would contend with him.

Gu Caiyi said, "Go ahead, Daoist. We'll guard you."

Kongchan smiled slightly. "I'll trouble you all to wait a moment."

With that, he leaped into the bottomless pit created by the celestial meteorite.

Watching him, Gu Caiyi couldn't help but marvel that the rumored "Monk Kongchan" wasn't as ill-tempered as the rumors suggested!

He was so polite, just like an enlightened eminent monk.

But then she thought of the terrifying, ugly sight of Kongchan's true form and the skull prayer beads around his neck—that was probably the ferocious style the rumors spoke of.

His current good temper was all because of her husband.

Her gaze swept over the three guardian spirit beasts beside her, and Gu Caiyi's heart stirred. They were probably the same!

Tianxuan was fond of fighting, Black King was cunning, and Xuanming was aloof. None of them had normal temperaments. The only reason they could gather peacefully was entirely due to her husband's control.

She wondered what would happen to these four guardian spirit beasts if her husband ascended in the future.

Everyone waited outside, their hearts at ease.

Time passed, and after half a day with no sign of him, their worries began to grow.

Suddenly.

There was movement from within the pit.

Whoosh!

A tentacle shot up and hooked onto the edge of the pit.

Then, a figure used it for leverage and flew out of the pit.

It was Kongchan.

He held a fist-sized ore in his hand, emitting a deep, dark light.

As everyone stared at the black light, they felt as if they were about to be sucked into it.

"Fortunately, I didn't disgrace my mission. It was a bit troublesome, but I managed to bring this thing out. I don't know what material this celestial meteorite is made of, but it can absorb spiritual power, making it hard to move for a short time. I had to use pure physical strength to bring it out."

Kongchan held up the black stone, quite emotional.

Just then, a surprised voice came from the air.

"It's actually Transformation Spirit Crystal!"

Then, the surprise turned into ecstasy.

"With this, I can forge a suitable magic treasure that will perfectly counter the spells and techniques of human cultivators!"

On the ground.

Gu Caiyi and the others looked up sharply. When they saw the magnificent, divine golden roc, their expressions changed drastically.

"Desolate beast!"

"It approached silently without any of us noticing. Could it be a fifth-rank desolate beast?"

"It speaks human language and knows about ores. Its intelligence must be extremely high. This is trouble."

The terror of desolate beasts lay in their powerful physical bodies.

In terms of intelligence, they weren't considered very clever, as most wild beasts acted on instinct.

The Gui Li was considered relatively smart.

But compared to the golden bird before them, it was still far inferior.

A fifth-rank desolate beast with extremely high intelligence would be no weaker than a normal human Ascendant!

With just the five of them, including Kongchan, they might not even survive.

The Golden-Winged Great Peng looked down at the trembling group below, a disdainful expression on his face.

"Little ones, hand over the Transformation Spirit Crystal willingly, and I might be in a good mood and keep you as slaves."

Gu Caiyi took a deep breath and forced herself to step forward.

"Senior, we are traveling through the wilderness with the great power Luo Chen. This object was obtained by my husband breaking apart a falling star while resisting the might of heaven. For you to forcefully take it now is a bit..."

"A great human power?"

The Golden-Winged Great Peng blurted out, his eyes showing suspicion.

He instinctively looked around but found no trace of any human Ascendant.

"Are you trying to deceive me?"

The Golden-Winged Great Peng said angrily, then snorted heavily.

"So what if there is a great power? I survived the terrifying battlefield in the Central Continent! Am I afraid of some nobody I've never heard of? I suppose the Ascendant you speak of is a newly advanced one!"

With that, ignoring their reply, he brazenly extended a claw, reaching straight for them.

In his mind, even if a great power was nearby, so what?

As long as he took the celestial iron and the Transformation Spirit Crystal, then turned and fled.

With his flying speed, how could any mere human Ascendant possibly catch up?

But the moment he reached out, the Golden-Winged Great Peng suddenly felt a sharp edge.

He instinctively withdrew his claw, but was still a moment too slow.

Chi!

On the Golden-Winged Great Peng's claw, which seemed forged from gold, a deep, bone-deep sword mark appeared.

He instinctively looked to where it had come from.

At some point, a blood-colored long sword was hanging in the void.

It was this object that had wounded his powerful body with a single strike.

Such a sharp sword was rare in the world. In his life, the Golden-Winged Great Peng had only seen such a technique from the incarnation of a sword dao powerhouse who had crossed over during the battle in the Central Continent.

"A spirit treasure!"

His heart sinking, the Golden-Winged Great Peng already felt a hint of regret.

But it was too late for regret now.

Or rather, it had been too late the moment he opened his mouth to demand the celestial iron and spoke recklessly to enslave Gu Caiyi and the others.

The dim sky suddenly solidified like a swamp.

Vast spiritual power swept across the heavens, stirring up boundless origin qi.

The residual heat left by the falling meteor was once again gathered by this spiritual power.

In his sight, the Golden-Winged Great Peng only saw, in the twilight sky, countless millstone-sized stars taking shape, then bursting with light and heat, all grinding towards him.

Under such terrifying power, containing the might of heaven and earth, the Golden-Winged Great Peng felt his soul flee in terror. He frantically tried to escape, shouting,

"Daoist, it's a misunderstanding!"

The only reply he got was,

"Remember, this technique is called Starfire Burns the World!"

The next moment, meteors fell from the sky, and starfire bloomed.

The entire twilight sky was filled with boundless flames.

The Golden-Winged Great Peng's body, like golden glass, fled in panic within it, his entire form seeming to melt, dripping streams of golden liquid.

He fled for his life, reckless of everything.

His speed was even faster than when he had attacked the Central Continent back then!

Because he knew that the Ascendant who had cast this divine ability was even stronger than the incarnations of those upper-realm powerhouses who had descended during the Central Continent war.

If he didn't escape quickly, and the other party arrived in person, he would be in deep trouble.

"Luo Chen, I'll remember you!"

Only this angry, unwilling voice came from the distant sky.

Gu Caiyi and the others stared blankly at the scene. Brilliant starfire fell around them, but protected by the sword shield formed by the Yuantu Sword, they were completely unharmed.

Just one move!

Her husband, a thousand li away, had routed the Golden-Winged Great Peng, who they couldn't even resist, with a single move!

Both were fifth-rank. Why was Luo Chen, who had just become an Ascendant, so powerful?

It was one thing to easily deal with those fifth-rank wild beasts that acted on instinct, but this Golden-Winged Great Peng was an intelligent being!

Beneath the vast starfire, a figure stepped through the void. Wherever he passed, the flames vanished.

Everyone stared blankly, feeling as if an entire world was pressing down on them.

"Husband..."

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow, instantly withdrawing his mind.

The connection between his primordial spirit and heaven and earth was severed, but he still seemed to have some lingering ties to this world.

"By observing the celestial phenomenon and the residual power of the fallen meteor, I forcibly executed a divine ability I had temporarily comprehended. But the price..."

Luo Chen sighed softly.

He really couldn't stay in this Mountains and Seas Realm any longer.

If this continued, he would eventually be assimilated and lose the power to ascend.

"Let's go!"

"Where to?"

"Back to the Eastern Wilderness."

"By the same route?"

"No need for that. We've traversed the wilderness and reached the easternmost point. This boundless desert must be the Vast Sea Desert. Passing through it leads to the Western Desert cultivation world."

A look of melancholy appeared in Luo Chen's eyes.

"Let's go to the Western Desert first. I promised a friend back then that after becoming an Ascendant, I would return to the Western Desert to burn a stick of incense."

No one had any further doubts. They all boarded the Frost Eagle Shuttle.

As for the Golden-Winged Great Peng, Luo Chen did not pursue him.

This beast's flying speed was unmatched in the world. Even if Luo Chen could catch up, killing him would take considerable effort.

And in his current state, because he had comprehended a divine ability and was too closely connected to the Mountains and Seas Realm, frequent combat would only deepen the heavenly shackles.

This trip to the wilderness had already fulfilled its purpose, whether it was hunting powerful wild beasts or honing his own skills.

There was no need to stay any longer.

...

In the Vast Sea Desert, a figure advanced rapidly.

His whole body was suffused with red light, emitting a bloody glow.

If anyone saw him, they would surely exclaim that this was the famous demonic art of blood escape.

But this blood escape art greatly depleted one's origin and was not to be used except in life-or-death situations.

Even though no one was chasing him, this Sage cultivator was going to such great lengths. Why?

Only Qi Ying himself knew the reason for his desperate flight.

If he ran any slower, he would probably be caught by the Golden-Winged Great Peng again and forced to refine that so-called Relic Pill.

But even though he was running fast, there was still unease in his heart.

"This drop of blood in my body is too domineering, too sharp. It's almost piercing my Purple Mansion barrier. I can't suppress it with secret arts alone."

"No good. If this continues, before my origin is drained, this blood will break through my Purple Mansion and destroy my Sage embryo."

"I have to stop first and suppress this blood!"

Thump!

The figure suddenly halted. Qi Ying stumbled to a stop, unable to control himself, and rolled a few times in the sand.

The moment he landed, he frantically mobilized his spiritual power to restrict that drop of golden blood.

Completely focused on suppression, Qi Ying was unaware of the outside world.

When a surprised exclamation sounded in his ear, he opened his eyes and realized it was already dawn.

In the faint light of dawn, an extremely young white-robed Daoist landed before him.

"Young friend, the aura on your body feels somewhat familiar to me. May I ask where you studied and who your master is?"

It would be laughable for a Sage to be called "young friend."

But Qi Ying didn't dare to be careless, because in his eyes, the other party seemed like a piece of heaven and earth, bringing him immense pressure.

An Ascendant!

These four words naturally appeared in his mind.

Qi Ying swallowed his saliva, a bitter smile on his face.

He had just escaped the demon lair of a fifth-rank wild beast, and now he had run into a rare Ascendant. What kind of ill luck had he been having lately!

"This junior is Qi Ying, of the Nether Origin Sect..."

As soon as he said this, Qi Ying felt something was wrong.

Because the young man opposite him frowned instinctively.

"The Nether Origin Sect?" The young man hadn't expected that, while casually sweeping with his divine sense through the Vast Sea Desert, he would encounter a remnant of the Nether Origin Sect.

Qi Ying was terrified. Did this Ascendant have a grudge against the Nether Origin Sect?

Suppressing the fear in his heart, he forced himself to finish the sentence with difficulty.

"My master is Xuan Yan."

Unexpectedly, after this sentence, the young man suddenly smiled.

"So you are the only personal disciple of Master Xuan Yan. No wonder you give me a familiar feeling."

Qi Ying was stunned and asked cautiously, "Does Senior know my master?"

The young man smiled slightly. "I am Luo Chen. I have some friendship with Master Xuan Yan. You should recognize this book!"

As he spoke, a pill formula appeared before Qi Ying.

It was titled *Titillating Pill*.

But it was not the complete formula for the Titillating Pill. It was the incomplete formula that Master Xuan Yan and Alchemy Saint Chu Yan had derived together back then.

The Titillating Pill that Luo Chen later refined was based on this, with his own deductions and completions.

But the familiar handwriting was immediately recognized by Qi Ying as his master's.

And the name Luo Chen was like thunder in his ears.

His feat of attacking the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect and defying the great power Lian Cheng was known to all under heaven.

Moreover, he did have some connection with the Nether Origin Sect and his master, Xuan Yan.

Back then, Luo Chen had joined the Nether Origin Sect and, it was said, entered the Alchemy Saint Hall with his master to undergo the inheritance assessment. Unfortunately, the specific outcome was unknown, only that Rosycloud had destroyed the Nether Origin Sect.

Qi Ying had been traveling abroad at the time. Later, when the Nether Origin Sect was destroyed, he didn't return and wandered alone. In recent years, he had entered the wilderness to pick a medicinal herb that had gone extinct in the cultivation world, only to be captured by the Golden-Winged Great Peng.

"Since you are the descendant of an old friend, I will help you out!"

Luo Chen saw at a glance that the other party's situation was dire. Without waiting for Qi Ying to speak, he formed a sword finger with his right hand and pointed it at the other's lower abdomen from a distance.

A wisp of extremely hot power entered his body, surged upwards, and found that drop of golden blood.

"Wild beast origin blood, containing the origin of metal, which happens to be countered by me."

With a single point of his finger, Luo Chen spoke casually, and the means left by the Golden-Winged Great Peng on Qi Ying's body vanished like smoke, as if nothing had ever happened.

Qi Ying stared blankly at Luo Chen, instinctively touching his lower abdomen. There was no longer any discomfort.

"Little friend Qi Ying, we are going to pass through the Western Desert and return to the Eastern Wilderness. What are your plans?"

Qi Ying opened his mouth, hesitating. "This junior... this junior would like to travel with Senior, if I may?"

"Haha, then let's travel together!"

Luo Chen laughed heartily, turned, and boarded the shuttle hovering high in the sky.

Qi Ying looked up at the sky, then back at the path he had come from. He felt as if he were in a dream, utterly dazed.

(End of Chapter)
