# Chapter 953: Heavenly Master’s Treachery, Quenching Thirst with Poison

“Stiffness Poison Gu—a parasitic gu on Flying Yakshas, containing ultimate corpse poison.”

“Immortal-Falling Feather—the ninth head’s shed feather of the ancient ferocious bird Ghost Chariot. When burned, it produces a deadly toxin that can cause a cultivator’s Nascent Soul to collapse. Also known as Nine-Headed Poison.”

“Mother-Child Linked Gu—refined from the heartblood of nine hundred and ninety-nine mortal mothers and their children. It contains a terrifying blood-relation resentment poison.”

Inside the cave dwelling, the returning Purple-Robed Marquis spoke with ease, giving a general introduction to the three fourth-rank poison gu he was handing over to Luo Chen.

After finishing, he stared at Luo Chen, waiting to see his reaction.

But instead of the expected ecstasy, all he got was a furrowed brow and silence.

Purple-Robed Marquis asked in surprise, “Fellow Daoist Qi, these fourth-rank poison gu are rare even in this world—personal treasures of Heavenly Master Li himself. Why do you seem so unmoved?”

Luo Chen spoke slowly, “It’s not that I’m unmoved, but rather that they are far too precious.”

Purple-Robed Marquis paused, then laughed. “No matter. If you have any resources of equivalent value, you can take them out for exchange. Even if the quality isn’t up to par, Heavenly Master Li said quantity can make up the difference.”

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow. “Is Heavenly Master Li treating me so generously?”

There was more than a hint of suspicion and speculation in these words.

After all, there was no such thing as a free lunch in this world.

Yet the more directly he questioned, the more relaxed Purple-Robed Marquis became.

“Of course Heavenly Master Li is reluctant to part with these three poison gu. He also said frankly that with their empowerment, you will surely be able to advance the Hundred Poison King Gu to the fourth rank, and your body refinement will undergo a qualitative leap.”

“But times change. No matter how precious these poison gu are, they are ultimately only fourth-rank. What Heavenly Master Li truly wants is the fifth-rank gu recipe within the Yin-Yang Remnant Realm. Compared to that, these are nothing.”

“Therefore, improving your strength is more important to him!”

The Yin-Yang Remnant Realm!

This was precisely the ancient secret realm that Heavenly Master Li had mentioned before.

Legend had it that it only appeared at the boundary of dusk, when yin and yang merged. Over the millennia, only a handful of people had managed to break in.

According to those who came out, it was the place where a great power had sat down in meditation, and there was a high chance a fifth-rank gu recipe was hidden within.

But the place was filled with traps and dangers.

To obtain such a treasure, one needed astonishing abilities just to get far.

To this end, Heavenly Master Li had openly and secretly assembled a lineup that could only be called luxurious, all for the sake of exploring the Yin-Yang Remnant Realm.

Within this lineup, Luo Chen’s role was extremely important.

Apparently, there was a certain barrier that could only be crossed by a pure body refiner.

If that was the requirement, then his attentiveness toward Luo Chen made sense.

Moreover, the two had never known each other before, so there was no reason for the other party to plot against Luo Chen.

In silence, Luo Chen accepted the three poison gu, then took out a pile of odds and ends from his own storage pouch.

There were high-grade medicinal herbs, ores, magical artifacts, and some spirit stones.

After a brief inspection, Purple-Robed Marquis showed a troubled expression.

“What Fellow Daoist has offered is not bad, but compared to the value of these three poison gu, there is still a gap!”

Luo Chen waved his hand. “This is all I have. If I really had anything good, I would have used it long ago as a lone cultivator. Why would I keep it until now? Purple-Robed Marquis, you’re just acting as a middleman. Gains and losses here have little to do with you, so why be so meticulous?”

Purple-Robed Marquis was taken aback.

“Just take it back. Heavenly Master Li won’t mind,” Luo Chen added.

Purple-Robed Marquis gave a wry smile, waved his sleeve, and swept the pile of treasures—things that low-level cultivators would cherish—into a storage pouch.

“It seems I overthought things. I’ll take my leave now. Farewell!”

“Farewell.”

Luo Chen spread his hands and saw him out of the cave dwelling.

Only after Purple-Robed Marquis’s figure had disappeared did Luo Chen turn his attention back to the three poison gu.

He narrowed his eyes, calculating inwardly.

“Heavenly Master Li truly lives up to being the creator of the Hundred Poison Body Refinement Art and the Hundred Poison King Gu. He even anticipated the problems I would encounter.”

“With these three poison gu helping me, I am indeed confident of advancing the Hundred Poison King Gu to the fourth rank in a short time and completely breaking through the bottleneck.”

“And since my research on the natural spatial restriction is almost done, I can devote all my time to body refinement from now on.”

…

Time passed slowly.

It had been nearly half a year since Luo Chen arrived at Mijie Mountain.

And his cultivation progress had reached the most critical juncture.

The Stiffness Poison Gu had been completely refined.

After the Hundred Poison King Gu devoured it, it released several times the original amount of corpse poison, spreading throughout Luo Chen’s entire body.

Under the erosion of the terrifying corpse poison, large amounts of flesh and blood rapidly transformed into indestructible zombie flesh.

If this continued, Luo Chen might very well walk the path of ghostly body refinement like the Flying Yaksha.

But with his profound physical foundation, an endless stream of vitality destroyed the stiffened flesh, reorganizing it into his own lively, vigorous body.

Then came the Immortal-Falling Feather, also known as Nine-Headed Poison!

Strands of white, feather-like spiritual light enveloped Luo Chen’s entire body.

If the host who took this poison had a Nascent Soul, it would surely collapse, and their mana would dissipate.

Heavenly Master Li had never considered that Luo Chen might have a Nascent Soul, thinking he was merely a pure body refiner—after all, Purple-Robed Marquis had said earlier that Luo Chen only planned to turn to the path of refining qi later.

As for this hidden danger, Luo Chen had his own solution.

The formidable Purple Mansion barrier, along with the two divine fires contained within, was enough to protect his Nascent Soul from this poison.

He simply let the Hundred Poison King Gu devour it, then transform it into Nine-Headed Poison to temper his flesh.

During this process, aside from his body, the only thing affected was probably the source power he had cultivated!

While the Nine-Headed Poison tempered his flesh, the flow of source power became quite sluggish, uncomfortable in every way.

But Luo Chen persevered.

Because, from his proficiency panel, he could clearly see his body refinement progress rising at a visible rate.

When he first arrived in the Southern Frontier, the progress bar was around sixty.

Before reaching Mijie Mountain, it had only increased slightly.

Yet in the short half-year at Mijie Mountain, this progress had climbed with unimaginable efficiency, bit by bit.

Now it had reached as high as sixty-five!

Although it seemed like only four points had increased, when viewed across the entire major realm, this efficiency was terrifying.

Compared to the path of qi refinement, it was almost equivalent to going from the middle of Sage Fifth Layer to Sage Sixth Layer!

This was also why he was willing to endure that boundless pain, day and night without rest.

Seeing feedback at any time during cultivation was truly addictive.

According to Luo Chen’s calculations, when his body refinement progress crossed the threshold of [Archaic Fourth Rank 66/100], it should be considered entering the late stage.

This also made him look forward to the final Mother-Child Linked Gu while digesting the Immortal-Falling Feather.

One day.

During a break in cultivation, Luo Chen took time to leave his cave dwelling and went to Heavenly Master Li’s residence.

There, he met the relevant personnel involved in the Yin-Yang Remnant Realm expedition.

Heavenly Master Li was the initiator, joined by Purple Blood Sect Leader Purple-Robed Marquis, Hidden Sword Pavilion Master Fan Wujiu, and even Red Leaf Granny, whom he had met a few times before.

“There is one more person, not currently at Mijie Mountain. He will come to meet us when we set out.”

After the introductions, Heavenly Master Li asked about everyone’s preparations.

Red Leaf Granny’s formation preparations, Purple-Robed Marquis’s secret treasure refinement progress, and finally Luo Chen’s body refinement progress.

“Don’t worry. I won’t let you down!”

After throwing out this one sentence, Luo Chen had no interest in chatting with the others and left on his own.

This self-willed style of conduct surprised everyone.

But what was most astonishing was Luo Chen’s external appearance.

His skin was livid, his expression ferocious, his nails long and narrow, and when he opened his mouth, fangs were faintly visible.

Yaksha Aspect!

Purple-Robed Marquis had seen the complete Hundred Poison Body Refinement Art, so he naturally knew what this meant.

After Luo Chen left, he couldn’t help but sigh, “This Fellow Daoist Qi is truly a body refinement genius rarely seen in a thousand years! That technique, while not complicated, is extremely difficult to practice. Even if you gave me a hundred years, I might not achieve much. Yet he has accomplished so much in such a short time. It’s truly enviable.”

Red Leaf Granny and Hidden Sword Pavilion Master Fan Wujiu didn’t understand the difficulty.

But Heavenly Master Li smiled in acknowledgment.

It wasn’t just his body refinement talent that was outstanding; what was even more admirable was his ability to endure the terrible pain brought by the poisons.

That was good!

The stronger Luo Chen was, the more confident he would be in the Yin-Yang Remnant Realm!

“He should be at the stage of refining the Mother-Child Linked Gu by now!”

Heavenly Master Li murmured to himself.

The Yaksha Aspect had appeared externally, and the feather poison covered his body. Clearly, the other party had reached a bottleneck.

To cross that bottleneck, he would need the Mother-Child Linked Gu to give him that final push!

…

Just as Heavenly Master Li had thought, Luo Chen had indeed reached that stage.

Moreover, he had made all the preparations for refining the Mother-Child Linked Gu.

In the secret chamber, Luo Chen stared at the gu in his hand, his eyes eager.

He didn’t care about external ugliness or beauty. Even if the Yaksha Aspect was unpleasant, it was tolerable given the rapid improvement in his strength.

And once he crossed this stage, after the corpse poison and Nine-Headed Poison were completely refined, the so-called Yaksha Aspect would also come under control.

So what was there to worry about?

“Everything depends on this final step!”

Murmuring, Luo Chen activated the Hundred Poison Body Refinement Art and instantly refined the restless Mother-Child Linked Gu in his hand.

In an instant, a fierce resentment poison entered Luo Chen’s body.

The Hundred Poison King Gu instinctively devoured it, then regurgitated an even more terrifying poison gas.

Following the technique’s circulation, this poison gas gradually flowed throughout Luo Chen’s body, even spreading toward his sea of consciousness…

Three days later!

“Get out!”

With a low shout like a thunderclap, the seated black-armored man suddenly erupted with dazzling golden flames.

Large amounts of resentment poison gas, under the pressure of the brilliant golden flames, were expelled thread by thread.

Finally, they condensed into a gu insect with open eyes.

It was the Mother-Child Linked Gu!

Luo Chen’s face was dark as he pinched the gu, muttering under his breath, “Heavenly Master Li!”

He had been on guard a thousand times, fearing someone might tamper with the gu insects. Every time he refined them, he inspected them meticulously.

Yet he had still fallen into the trap.

The three gu insects Heavenly Master Li had sent were all fine.

The problem was that after the three gu were refined, with the residual poison not yet cleared, the three poisons acted simultaneously, triggering an indescribable chain reaction.

Flesh stiffening, source power stagnating, resentment poison attacking the sea of consciousness!

At that moment, Luo Chen nearly lost his self-awareness and control over his own body.

If not for the Illuminating Spirit Gu constantly monitoring his whole body, if not for his divided soul being ever vigilant and the Prison Suppression Soul Art constantly reinforcing him, if not for the two divine fires hidden in his Purple Mansion…

At this moment, Luo Chen used the Nirvana Sacred Flame to force out the Mother-Child Linked Gu, and the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire to suppress the Hundred Poison King Gu. The feeling of losing himself had disappeared.

He had interrupted the cultivation process.

Thus, he naturally wouldn’t let Heavenly Master Li’s goal be achieved.

But the price of forcibly interrupting the cultivation process was also real.

At this moment, the Hundred Poison King Gu’s advancement had been interrupted, its momentum broken, and it was rapidly declining.

Even with the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire suppressing it, it only slowed the process slightly.

Once the Hundred Poison King Gu collapsed, the terrifying toxicity contained within would erupt instantly, and Luo Chen’s flesh…

“Everyone says it’s hard to deal with Southern Frontier cultivators. Now it seems that’s no lie!”

Luo Chen sneered, but there was no panic on his face.

At this point, his Hundred Poison Body Refinement Art had been cultivated to the master level. His understanding of its essentials far surpassed that of the technique’s creator, Heavenly Master Li.

He could see that the Hundred Poison King Gu was collapsing because its momentum had been broken and its advancement forcibly interrupted.

If he threw the Mother-Child Linked Gu back in, he could replenish that power.

But it didn’t have to be this gu!

What the Hundred Poison King Gu needed was poisonous substances—the more toxic, the better!

And such things, Luo Chen was not lacking!

The reason he hadn’t used them before was that the Hundred Poison King Gu was still too weak, and he feared it would be destroyed.

But now, it was just right…

“Heavenly Master Li, whatever your purpose, I will remember this grudge for now!”

Murmuring, Luo Chen suddenly took out a small jar.

With a flick of his finger, a drop of liquid flew out.

Wrapped in mana, it carefully entered his body and was eventually delivered to the Hundred Poison King Gu in his stomach.

The mana dispersed, and a dangerous aura immediately spread.

However, to the Hundred Poison King Gu, this liquid—a deadly poison to ordinary people—was a great tonic.

The Withering-Bloom Divine Fire suppressing the king gu was immediately withdrawn, and the king gu unhesitatingly swallowed the liquid.

In an instant, the gu insect began to roll violently, and its color gradually shifted from pure white to a jade-white hue, as if turning to jade.

At the same time, large amounts of poisonous mist were released in this process.

Under the erosion of this mist, Luo Chen’s incredibly tough flesh began to disintegrate inch by inch, turning into pools of blood.

Such a terrifying scene directly forced Black King out, hiding in a corner with a terrified gaze.

“Has the master taken that Poison Grain Millet?”

Poison Grain Millet—a secret poison wine from the Eastern Wilderness Nascent Soul Sect, Dieyun Sect.

When he first obtained it, even the poison-immune Black King dared not drink it. Even Luo Chen, who had only tasted a drop, had his flesh corroded.

Now, amplified by the Hundred Poison King Gu, a single drop had produced ten or even a hundred times the effect.

Under extreme corrosion, Luo Chen’s flesh began to collapse, disintegrate, turning into a pool of blood.

Only a small Nascent Soul figure, clad in black armor and holding a cauldron and a sword, floated in the void, its expression neither sad nor happy as it quietly watched this scene.

(End of this chapter)
