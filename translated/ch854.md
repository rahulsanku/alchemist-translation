# Chapter 854: Lingfeng Joins the Sect, an Ascendant Makes a Move

"That's Sage Chongzhen of the Nether Origin Sect!"

Gazing at the retreating figure, Lingfengzi spoke in a low voice.

Luo Chen squinted. "Was Seven Rhino fighting him earlier?"

Lingfengzi nodded.

Luo Chen understood. So Seven Rhino's retreat wasn't just because he was intimidated by his Withering-Bloom Dao Finger—Sage Chongzhen had also been pursuing him!

He had been so immersed earlier that he'd overlooked Sage Chongzhen's arrival, only sensing someone secretly watching from the shadows later on.

Still, this place was temporarily safe now.

He flicked his fingers, releasing the tightly clenched hand seal.

The spiritual energy that had been drawn in, as if losing its support, immediately dispersed.

Fenghua Fairy stared at Luo Chen's hand, her lips dry.

"Brother Luo truly possesses extraordinary ability!"

Lingfengzi chimed in, "Slaying three demons, capturing the Emperor, and defeating a Great Demon Emperor with a single finger—Brother Luo's skill is unmatched even among our fellow Sages."

Luo Chen glanced at Lingfengzi, a faint, ambiguous smile on his face.

Lingfengzi felt a bit awkward under that gaze.

Luo Chen didn't press the matter for now. Instead, he looked at Fenghua Fairy.

"How are things on your end?"

Fenghua Fairy's expression shifted slightly. "The Remnant Tower has retreated, but a massive horde of demon beasts is still besieging Fenghua Palace. I'll take my leave for now. We'll talk later!"

"Wait!"

Luo Chen stopped her, then shook his left arm.

A wisp of black qi emerged, rapidly expanding into a hundred-zhang-long dragon.

"I'm somewhat exhausted and can't help in person. Let this dragon and tiger assist you, Fellow Daoist!"

The desolate beast Black King, and the Golden Tiger Emperor, slaughter!

Fenghua Fairy gave Luo Chen a deep look.

"Thank you!"

Before leaving, Emperor Tu stole a glance at Luo Chen.

Was he truly exhausted this time?

...

After Fenghua Fairy departed with Luo Chen's two spirit pets, the area grew quiet.

Lingfengzi stood beside Luo Chen, feeling uneasy.

Suddenly, Luo Chen spoke.

"Lingfengzi, do you plan to return to the Lingtian Pass battlefield, or stay?"

Without hesitation, Lingfengzi replied, "Lingtian Pass is in chaos. Since I did hold back Commander Seven Rhino for quite some time, naturally I should stay..."

But his words gradually slowed.

Luo Chen seemed indifferent to his response, drifting down from the high sky.

As he landed, a group of Luo Tian Sect cultivators rushed over.

Every single one of them bore expressions of respect and adoration.

The number of demon beasts this time far exceeded any in Luo Tian Sect's history, and there had even been several Demon Emperors involved.

Yet, under the Grand Supreme Elder's leadership, the Luo Tian Sect had survived with the fewest casualties!

How could they not respect him? How could they not adore Luo Chen?

Especially Sect Master Li Yingzhang, whose eyes shone with even more excitement than Luo Chen's two Dao companions.

"I'm fine. Yingzhang, take the people and clean up the battlefield!" Luo Chen said with a slight smile.

"Yes!"

Without hesitation, Li Yingzhang led the group to disperse across the area.

The battlefield was now littered with countless demon beast corpses—for cultivators, these were all resources for cultivation!

Huiniang and Caiyi also came forward to check on Luo Chen. Seeing him unharmed and looking normal, they led their people away.

Luo Chen then called Heavenjade over.

"How many souls did that Myriad Souls Banner collect?"

"Tens of thousands!" Heavenjade replied, his face full of excitement as he added, "This banner, after Master's early years of nurturing, already had the foundation of a Hundred Thousand Fierce Soul Banner. With the addition of these new souls, it only lacks a single Sage-level main soul to truly advance to a Hundred Thousand Fierce Soul Banner!"

An ordinary soul banner was at most a magic treasure.

But once the banner contained a hundred thousand souls and had a Sage main soul, it could advance to a Hundred Thousand Fierce Soul Banner, rivaling a true artifact.

Luo Chen smiled slightly. "Perfect timing!"

He reached out, took the Myriad Souls Banner, and then reached into the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

A small, tightly shut-eyed Sage infant, wrapped in countless black arcs of lightning, fell into his hand.

It was the Sage infant of Lu Chang'an, the third of the Lu brothers!

Under Lingfengzi's shocked gaze, Luo Chen struck down layer after layer of restrictions, then withdrew the black lightning arcs.

Lu Chang'an's eyes snapped open, staring venomously at Luo Chen.

Luo Chen smiled faintly. "The Primordial Unity Cauldron is my lifebound magic treasure, not a suitable dwelling place for you, Fellow Daoist. It's time to change your residence!"

With a flick of his hand, Lu Chang'an's Sage infant was thrown into the Myriad Souls Banner.

Instantly, black qi roiled, and countless resentful souls howled as they pounced on the small infant.

Eerie, piercing wails echoed out.

"Take it and refine it well! With this banner, combined with your Nine Winds Plantain Fan, few below the Sage level will be your match."

Heavenjade's eyes lit up, and he excitedly accepted the Myriad Souls Banner.

After Heavenjade happily withdrew, Luo Chen's gaze fell on Wang Yuan.

Their eyes met. Luo Chen was full of concern, but Wang Yuan's expression was somewhat dazed.

"What's wrong?"

"Nothing." Wang Yuan shook his head.

Luo Chen frowned, sweeping his divine sense over the other. Though there were some wounds, nothing was serious.

Seeing that Wang Yuan didn't want to talk, Luo Chen didn't press. He turned to Lingfengzi and issued an invitation.

"With the great battle over, Fellow Daoist, would you like to take a stroll with me through this Hundred Li Green River?"

Lingfengzi nodded instinctively.

After the two left, Wang Yuan remained standing there, his weathered, resolute face full of bewilderment.

He knew Luo Chen was now very strong, very powerful!

Whether in cultivation level, battle achievements, or the deliberate restraint he showed when sparring with him, it all spoke volumes.

But he never imagined Luo Chen's strength would surpass his understanding!

When the Great Demon Emperor arrived, its terrifying pressure had made the entire Hundred Li Green River unable to breathe.

At that moment, even Wang Yuan, who had experienced countless battles, felt a sense of helplessness, unable to resist.

He even felt that even if he advanced to the fourth level of body refinement, it wouldn't change the situation at all.

Yet, Luo Chen had defeated it with a single finger!

"The little brother I once knew has grown to a level I can no longer fathom?"

Wang Yuan murmured to himself, finally recognizing the gap between him and Luo Chen.

...

The stench of blood filled the air; devastation was everywhere.

This was the current state of Hundred Li Green River.

Gone was its former scenic beauty and vibrant life.

Yet Luo Chen walked through it with a smile on his face, as if in excellent spirits.

Lingfengzi followed behind him, distracted. The wound on his chest and abdomen, though still hideous, had stopped bleeding and was no longer a threat for now.

When Luo Chen reached the bank of the Blackwater River and looked up at the severed head of the Nine-Zhang Deer, cut off by the Yuantu Sword, he stopped.

"Lingfengzi, does your Misty Sect still have any surviving disciples?"

"A few are still alive. I used my connections to send them to the rear, so they should be able to avoid this life-and-death calamity."

"Only a few? I've heard that at its peak, the Misty Sect had tens of thousands of disciples and thriving incense offerings."

"Alas, it's ultimately my incompetence. I couldn't protect the sect's old and young."

"Have you thought about rebuilding the Misty Sect in the future, reviving its legacy?"

"Naturally, I have. But given the current situation, I'm powerless to do so!"

Lingfengzi sighed, his expression bleak.

Rebuilding a Sage-level sect from nothing wasn't something that could be done with just cultivation level alone.

First, one needed the Sage realm.

Second, one needed talented disciples who were loyal to the sect.

Finally, one needed a suitable spiritual land as a site to establish the mountain and found the sect.

Of these three conditions, the first was the most important, but the latter two were also indispensable.

Especially the spiritual land!

For the sake of such a thing, most Sage sects had to launch pioneering wars to wrest territory from countless demon beasts.

The current Misty Sect was utterly incapable of launching a pioneering war.

Even if the human-demon war ended completely, Lingfengzi didn't know how much time and energy it would take to restore even a tenth of the Misty Sect's former glory.

And plans could change. If something unexpected happened, the Misty Sect's legacy might be cut off in his hands.

Luo Chen looked at the dejected Lingfengzi and suddenly asked, "Then what do you think of my Luo Tian Sect? Can it succeed?"

Lingfengzi thought for a moment, then nodded. "It definitely can! Although the Luo Tian Sect currently has few disciples, from what I observed earlier, each one is outstanding and highly loyal to the sect. Coupled with your strength and prestige, Fellow Daoist, you only need to raise your arm, and outsiders will be willing to help you slay demons and carve out a spiritual land."

Luo Chen smiled. With his current influence, doing so would indeed not be difficult.

However!

"Other people's help can never match one's own disciples working together with one heart!"

"It's a pity that aside from me, the Luo Tian Sect has no other Sage cultivators. I have to handle many things myself, which is truly troublesome. If someone could assist me, it would be much easier."

"Lingfengzi, would you be willing to join my Luo Tian Sect?"

Lingfengzi's head snapped up, meeting Luo Chen's smiling eyes.

"Fellow Daoist Luo, isn't this a bit..."

"Pushing someone against their will? Repaying a favor with a demand?" Luo Chen laughed heartily, his expression open. "If you're unwilling, Fellow Daoist, I won't force you. You may go!"

Lingfengzi opened his mouth, his expression turning extremely ugly.

He lifted his foot to leave, but as he raised it, he saw the deer head before him, still oozing blood.

His raised foot dared not fall.

In his hesitation, he recalled Luo Chen's single finger defeating the Great Demon Emperor.

If word of this spread, the Alchemist Sect's reputation would surely reach new heights, blazing like the sun!

And if he left now, would he be severing the Alchemist Sect's friendship forever?

Would he even have no place to stand in the entire Eastern Wilderness?

His heavy foot slowly withdrew.

Lingfengzi looked at Luo Chen, who now showed no trace of magical power fluctuation. He couldn't tell if Luo Chen's mana was exhausted or if he still had strength in reserve.

But he knew that Luo Chen, who had comprehended the true meaning of the laws, was on the path to becoming an Ascendant!

Such an existence had now extended an olive branch to him. Would he take it?

If he didn't, it would be ingratitude and self-destruction of his future.

If he did, then he and Luo Chen would be family from now on.

But once he accepted, the Misty Sect would be completely scattered. The heavy hopes placed on him by predecessors and descendants would vanish in an instant.

Could he commit such an act of betraying his ancestors and teachers?

Countless thoughts erupted in Lingfengzi's mind, various conflicting ideas constantly clashing.

His originally stabilized wound, due to the turmoil in his spirit, began to loosen, sending waves of pain.

Luo Chen didn't speak to urge him. He stood quietly by the Blackwater River.

Time passed slowly.

The two stood there for half a day.

Until the setting sun was like blood, spilling across the surging black river.

Finally, a voice, heavy and even somewhat hoarse, came.

"Lingfengzi is willing to join the Luo Tian Sect."

Luo Chen clasped his hands behind his back and said calmly, "Have you thought it through?"

Lingfengzi replied in a low voice, "I have. Fellow Daoist has shown me kindness many times, and this time, you saved my life while under the pressure of a Great Demon Emperor. Such great kindness is beyond repayment. The only way is to pledge myself to you."

What a "pledge myself to you"!

Luo Chen reached out and heavily patted his shoulder.

"From now on, you are the Second Elder of the Luo Tian Sect. You may establish a Misty Hall and continue its legacy!"

That pat seemed to lift the burden from his shoulders. Lingfengzi felt as if a weight had been lifted.

By the Blackwater River, under the afterglow of the evening sun, he bowed deeply to Luo Chen.

"Lingfengzi pays respects to the Grand Supreme Elder!"

Luo Chen smiled and helped him up.

"Though we never studied under the same master, we now share the title of fellow sect members. I am slightly higher in cultivation than you, so you may call me 'Senior Brother.'"

Now that the words were spoken, Lingfengzi felt his shackles fall away.

He smiled bitterly. "Senior Brother Luo Chen."

Luo Chen laughed heartily. "Since you call me Senior Brother, I must give you a meeting gift. Let it be this deer head!"

Lingfengzi instinctively looked up.

The deer head hanging by the riverbank stared with unseeing eyes, its antlers fierce and imposing.

Lingfengzi breathed a sigh of relief, a hint of relief in his heart.

This was the fate of Luo Chen's enemies.

And as his family, benefits were immediately delivered to his doorstep.

He had probably made a good decision, hadn't he?

...

After the great battle, the Luo Tian Sect's strength had not diminished but instead gained the greatest enhancement in its history!

Not only had they harvested numerous demon beast corpses, but they had also gained the allegiance of Sage Lingfengzi.

Perhaps his combat ability wasn't outstanding, but his escape techniques were unparalleled, especially in intelligence gathering.

From now on, the Luo Tian Sect's intelligence capabilities should be able to catch up.

But that matter could wait for now.

With the final decisive battle still ongoing, any further planning was just a castle in the air.

Three days later.

Outside the Great Green Mountains, clouds rolled in, and wind howled.

Clouds follow the dragon, wind follows the tiger.

Disciples busy in the mountains looked up and saw a giant dragon and a golden tiger breaking through the air.

If it had been before, they would have panicked.

But now, there was only a full sense of security.

Because they knew those two demon beasts were the Grand Supreme Elder's spirit pets!

Boom!

The golden tiger landed, its immense weight pressing a deep pit into the white jade square in front of the Luo Tian Grand Hall.

"Little Emperor, hold back! Every brick and tile here is the master's property!"

Emperor Tu snorted heavily, glaring fiercely back.

Black King, who had shrunk his body, glared back.

"What do you want? Don't look at me like that just because your cultivation is higher. You joined later than me. When I call you Little Emperor, you have to answer!"

"Sometimes the master even calls me Little Black."

Black King muttered, then flew into the Luo Tian Grand Hall.

Emperor Tu hesitated for a moment, then also took human form and walked inside.

The hall was quite lively at the moment.

Led by Li Yingzhang, the Luo Tian Sect's Adept cultivators were reporting various matters to Luo Chen.

Casualties, gains—the chatter was somewhat tedious.

But Luo Chen still listened attentively.

"The biggest problem is that the spiritual vein here has been disrupted. Spiritual energy is now running wild, and various spiritual apertures have collapsed. Min Longyu is leading a group of disciples to sort out the spiritual vein, but the effect is minimal."

"Hundred Li Green River is no longer suitable for our cultivation for the time being."

Li Yingzhang said with a worried expression.

Luo Chen nodded. He had naturally noticed this situation.

When he had tried to meditate earlier to recover his mana, he found that the efficiency of absorbing spiritual energy had greatly decreased.

The cause of this wasn't just the impact of a hundred thousand demon beasts. The main reason was his battle with Emperor Tu and the other Demon Emperors!

Even though he had controlled the battlefield and not affected the lower-level cultivators below, Lu Changsheng's self-destruction and Lu Changqing's fall had both triggered massive spiritual energy storms.

Hundred Li Green River was ultimately just a third-grade spiritual land. It couldn't withstand such an impact.

Now, as Li Yingzhang said, the spiritual vein here was in turmoil, spiritual energy was running wild, and it was completely unsuitable for lower-level cultivators to train.

"Master, this is simple. Leave it to Little Black. In two or three years, I can guide the spiritual energy back into place!"

As soon as he entered the hall, Black King volunteered.

Two or three years?

Luo Chen shook his head. That was time they couldn't afford to waste.

He looked at the two demons and asked, "How are things at Fenghua Palace?"

Facing the gazes of the crowd, Emperor Tu felt very uncomfortable.

He cleared his throat and said, "After we arrived, the crisis was quickly resolved. The reason we delayed so long was that beast tides kept surging from Lingtian Pass, so we stayed for three days."

Luo Chen understood.

So it seemed Fenghua Palace was safe for now.

Hundred Li Green River was no longer livable. Perhaps he could talk to Fenghua Fairy about temporarily staying at their Fenghua Palace?

Just as Luo Chen was pondering, his expression suddenly changed.

Not only him, but even Black King instinctively coiled his long body, looking up at the hall's dome in alarm.

"What's wrong?" Lingfengzi, who was sitting nearby listening, was confused.

Emperor Tu was also bewildered, but he felt a vague sense of familiarity.

Luo Chen and Black King exchanged a glance, their expressions turning grave.

The origin qi of heaven and earth was in turmoil!

Had an Ascendant made a move?
