# Chapter 834: Sword Formation Upgrade, Surprise Attack on the Rear

Demon Wuji, body without spine.

Only when the longsword was drawn did one understand the origin of his name.

Luo Chen's brow furrowed as he sensed a trace of danger.

Without a second thought, he struck fiercely!

As always, the Azure Yang Great Handprint served as the opening move, but unlike usual, Luo Chen followed it up with the Cloud-Seizing Divine Claw.

*Shua!*

After the majestic palm imprint, a claw suddenly reached out from the sky, heading straight for the Wuji Demon Emperor.

Seeing this, the Wuji Demon Emperor was not alarmed but delighted instead.

"Excellent!"

His figure shot forward, neither dodging nor evading, charging straight at the Azure Yang Great Handprint.

One sword strike, and the handprint was broken.

Another sword strike, and the Cloud-Seizing Divine Claw was severed in two.

Watching this, Luo Chen's pupils contracted slightly, his gaze involuntarily falling upon that sword.

This was a genuine true artifact-grade treasured sword!

Even if refined with crude demon-refining methods, after years of nurturing, its power was in no way inferior to the inherited true artifacts of those great sects.

Two spells were somewhat insufficient.

Seeing the Wuji Demon Emperor drawing closer, Luo Chen gave a light shout and opened his mouth to spit out.

*Xiu! Xiu! Xiu! Xiu! Xiu!*

Only the sound of breaking air could be heard as five streaks of light flew unceasingly toward the Wuji Demon Emperor.

They were precisely five sword pellets!

The Wuji Demon Emperor's divine sense was extremely sharp. Wielding his longsword, he scattered sword qi, deflecting each sword pellet one by one, a look of disdain in his eyes.

Such crude methods of controlling sword pellets, and he dared to show off before him?

But in the next moment, he saw the five sword pellets array themselves in five directions, evolving into thousands of sword qis, surrounding him completely.

"Not right!"

The Wuji Demon Emperor's expression changed slightly. Merging man and sword into one, he transformed into a beam of light, about to break out of the formation.

Yet from the corner of his eye, he saw a flash of golden light in the Daoist's eyes opposite him.

In that instant, illusions seemed to arise in his mind.

There were demon wolves roaming the mountains and forests, howling back and forth, crying at the moon.

There was a massive, snow-white tiger descending from the heavens, overpowering the demon wolves.

Sensing something amiss, the Wuji Demon Emperor instantly realized he had fallen under a soul-bewitching technique. He bit the tip of his tongue, and his entire being snapped back to clarity.

What met his eyes was a pair of golden pupils staring coldly at him.

"Daoist friend, since you've entered the formation, why rush to leave?"

The Wuji Demon Emperor's expression turned ugly. He looked around and saw strands of sword qi like silk threads crisscrossing, sealing off every direction he could escape.

A sword formation!

Suddenly, it was as if he remembered something.

"This sword formation!"

"What relationship do you have with Han Zhan?"

Luo Chen's brow furrowed slightly. The Grand Five Elements Sword Formation was derived from the Yin-Yang Five Elements Sword Formation, and in terms of external appearance, it indeed looked very similar.

But how could this Wuji Demon Emperor, whose name he had never heard before, know about these sword formations?

Since the other party was already trapped in the formation and couldn't escape for a short while, Luo Chen deigned to converse with him.

"Who are you?"

"I am Wuji, as I said before, am I not?"

"No, I'm asking which commander you serve under?"

The Wuji Demon Emperor spoke with a hint of pride. "I serve under Commander Aokuang!"

As soon as this name was spoken, Luo Chen understood immediately.

Aokuang was the lord of all wolf clans in the Eastern Wilderness's Million Great Mountains, with absolute peak strength and exalted status, second only to the Ancient Demon Emperor Di Tian.

And every single demon wolf in the Million Great Mountains could be considered his descendant.

Back then, the Aoxiao Demon Emperor of Xiaoyue Mountain Range was a wolf clan prodigy he greatly favored.

Unfortunately, that demon died before achieving success.

Having barely advanced to the fourth rank, he encountered the Falling Cloud Sect's expansion war and even died under the siege of Han Zhan and a group of Sage-level true men.

In that battle, Han Zhan's most proud technique was none other than the Yin-Yang Five Elements Sword Formation.

At the time, many demon beasts had escaped and passed this information back. It was only natural that the wolf clan would know some of it.

But even the Wuji Demon Emperor and others remembered it so clearly. It was evident that his superior, Commander Aokuang, placed great importance on this matter. He might even have thoughts of avenging the Aoxiao Demon Emperor.

If Aokuang found out that he had mastered the sword formation left behind by Han Zhan, he might very well link Luo Chen with Han Zhan. With the hatred transferred, he would come seeking revenge.

At this thought, Luo Chen's killing intent grew even heavier!

Seeing Luo Chen silent, the Wuji Demon Emperor could no longer contain his impatience. A fierce look crossed his face.

"Do you really think this formation can trap me?"

As his words fell, he held his sword with both hands and charged in all directions.

A storm of sword qi began to erupt wildly, centered on his body.

In just a few breaths, another sword qi storm arose within the formation, annihilating all the sword threads that approached.

Ironically enough.

Earlier, the Taobao Demon Emperor, who didn't understand the sword dao, had seen through the formation's weakness and escaped.

This Wuji Demon Emperor, who was proficient in the sword dao, was now breaking it with brute force!

Was it disdain, or...

"Trying to exhaust my magic power this way?"

Maintaining the sword formation required not only immense divine sense pressure but also deep magic power as support.

In such a situation, even if Luo Chen could multitask, it would be difficult for him to employ other methods.

This Wuji Demon Emperor had clearly understood Luo Chen's intelligence and knew that his methods were varied.

So rather than countering each move as it came, it was better to engage Luo Chen in a battle of attrition on his own strongest ground.

However, Luo Chen only sneered at this.

In the eyes of others, he was merely at the third layer of the Sage stage. No matter how deep his magic power, it couldn't be that high.

But only he himself knew that his true cultivation was at the fourth layer of the Sage stage!

And because of his terrifying foundation, his magic power was more than ten times that of his peers!

Among those at the mid-Sage stage, aside from Nine Spirit Primordial Lord, Luo Chen considered himself unmatched by anyone.

This Wuji Demon Emperor had miscalculated!

"Perfect timing. I'll use this opportunity to become more familiar with this Grand Five Elements Sword Formation."

Luo Chen pursed his lips. Before the formation was broken, he intended to practice well.

With both hands forming sword fingers, the method of controlling sword pellets derived from the *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon* became more fluid as he formed one sword seal after another. The five sword pellets moved with greater ease, far more refined than the crude sword control from before.

The flaws of the Grand Five Elements Sword Formation also appeared before Luo Chen's Illusion-Breaking Eye for the first time, so intuitively.

"The reason the Grand Five Elements Sword Formation cannot display its full power is because the five sword pellets are of uneven quality. This is the greatest flaw."

"But before this flaw is discovered, it won't be easily broken by others."

"Logically speaking, with two true artifact-grade sword pellets, the killing power shouldn't be unable to handle even a fourth-rank initial Demon Emperor..."

The more Luo Chen observed, the clearer his understanding became.

Gradually, he found the main reason for the insufficient power of his Grand Five Elements Sword Formation.

The two most powerful true artifact sword pellets belonged to the metal and wood attributes respectively. Metal overcomes wood—this was a natural restraining relationship!

The conflict between the two actually affected the formation's power.

So, if he couldn't unleash the full power of the five elements' mutual generation and restraint, what would happen if he abandoned one of them and instead used the power of mutual generation among the five elements to fully unleash the killing power of a single sword pellet?

At this thought, Luo Chen immediately had the urge to experiment.

He was already proficient in formations, and his ability to adapt in battle was also first-rate.

As his sword seals suddenly changed, the five colors within the sword formation became blurred. The cyan-green color abruptly weakened, becoming similar to the azure blue, crimson red, and murky yellow.

And that white-gold sword pellet, amidst the circulation of the four colors, blazed with intense light!

*Xiu! Xiu! Xiu!*

Countless white-gold sword threads came faster and went denser, crisscrossing to the point where they even faintly seemed to cut through the void.

"Yes!"

"That's it!"

Luo Chen's eyes showed surprise. Under this operation of his, the formation's power didn't decrease but increased instead, becoming a full times more powerful than before!

Not only that, he discovered that by concentrating the power on a single sword pellet, the burden on his divine sense was also greatly reduced. This meant he could free up his hands to use other methods.

What a continuous stream of pleasant surprises!

And the Wuji Demon Emperor trapped inside naturally felt this even more deeply.

The sword qi storm formed by his man-sword unity had been moving freely before, but now it seemed to encounter a sieve.

Whenever it collided with the white-gold sword threads, part of his sword qi storm would be sifted away.

He understood that it wasn't being sifted away, but rather, in the fierce confrontation, it was being ground down by the sword qi emitted by the formation.

"He's using me to test his sword! And improving extremely quickly. If this continues, I'll be strangled to death by these impenetrable sword threads!"

At this thought, the Wuji Demon Emperor could no longer sit still.

He hadn't seen any sign of Luo Chen's magic power running out, but his own side was growing weaker and weaker.

He had to get out!

The previously weakened sword qi storm suddenly expanded several times, charging with unstoppable momentum toward a weak point in the sword threads.

The Wuji Demon Emperor was proficient in the sword dao, so he had naturally long seen through the formation's weakness.

He could come and go as he pleased, which was why he had been so arrogant earlier.

Now that he didn't want to sink deeper, he naturally should escape from the weak point.

However!

A figure blocked his path out of the formation. A white-robed figure, one hand forming a vertical palm, struck out with a thunderous palm strike.

It was a purely physical palm!

After being thrown, thick blood qi formed a massive palm imprint, as if even mountains would be fragile before it.

Mountain-Breaking Style!

*Hong!*

With a single palm strike, he forcefully pierced through the sword qi storm, sending the Wuji Demon Emperor's true body back into the Grand Five Elements Sword Formation.

Outside the formation, Luo Chen's hearty laughter rang out.

"Daoist friend, since you're being so helpful, why not help me find more flaws in this sword formation? This sect has nothing to offer in return, but I can promise you a complete corpse!"

"Bastard!"

The Wuji Demon Emperor was furious. He swung his sword, sending out arcs of sword qi, already burning with true anger.

But Luo Chen could hear the bluster in his words.

Did he really think this Grand Five Elements Sword Formation was to be taken lightly?

Even with flaws, it was more than enough to kill a Demon Emperor.

Luo Chen stood guard outside the formation, one hand forming seals to control the sword formation while the other appeared at any moment on the Wuji Demon Emperor's escape route, using pure physical techniques to force him back.

As time passed, the Wuji Demon Emperor charged left and right but could never break free of the formation.

His formidable sword dao couldn't be applied to Luo Chen either, only allowing him to match wits with the ever-changing sword formation.

It was easy to imagine that before long, relying on the concept of mutual generation among the five elements, the inexhaustible white-gold sword qi would grind this Demon Emperor to dust.

Luo Chen wasn't in a hurry either. Killing the enemy while checking and filling gaps in the sword formation—where could he find such a good opportunity?

But in the next moment, his expression showed surprise.

A voice transmission entered his ears.

Luo Chen's brow furrowed slightly, muttering inwardly, "Why would Tian Duzi summon me back to the city at this time?"

Inside the sword formation, the Wuji Demon Emperor was already a cornered beast, about to jump the wall in desperation.

Ignoring the cutting of the sword threads, his true body suddenly transformed.

A cyan-red long worm stretched across the sword formation. The longsword was inserted into its body, turning into a dragon-like great spine.

As its body arched, boundless magic power erupted violently.

*Zheng!*

Only a sword cry in the void could be heard as the bow-shaped long worm split the sky.

The formation was broken!

But the Wuji Demon Emperor's eyes only showed confusion.

It wasn't him who broke the sword formation, but the opponent who had voluntarily withdrawn it.

His sword strike landed on empty air, giving him instead a nauseating feeling of punching cotton.

"Did he know I had this move? So at this critical moment, he withdrew the formation?"

"What terrifying grasp of battle opportunity!"

"What sharp eyesight!"

A chill rose uncontrollably from the Wuji Demon Emperor's heart.

Across from him, Luo Chen stared coldly, the five sword pellets circling around him, dancing like fish.

The Wuji Demon Emperor, still shaken, dared not linger.

"Luo Chen, I'll remember you!"

Leaving behind a harsh word, he fled without delay.

Luo Chen didn't pursue. He withdrew the five sword pellets and drifted back to the city.

The grand formation of Lingtian Pass was still being activated, like sharp sickles, mercilessly reaping the low-rank demon beasts charging the city walls.

Mu Zhenren greeted Luo Chen with a hint of reverence.

"Congratulations, Daoist friend, on defeating the enemy again!"

"Just some minor techniques, nothing worth mentioning. Tian Duzi has summoned me, so I must go over. I'll trouble you with matters here."

Luo Chen waved his hand and went straight into Lingtian Pass.

After he left, Mu Zhenren breathed a sigh of relief.

This Dan Sect Luo Chen had such a fierce killing intent! Just standing next to him made it hard to breathe. He couldn't imagine the pressure those Demon Emperors who faced him directly had to endure.

No wonder he was called the Demon-Slaying Sage!

...

The city lord's mansion was in the western district. With Luo Chen's speed, he arrived in an instant at full speed.

However, when he reached the city lord's mansion, he found that he was probably the last to arrive.

Inside the great hall, several old acquaintances were already present.

Nine Spirit Primordial Lord, Iron View Daoist, Jin Ling, Shouyang Mountain Man, Hua Tianshi, Deng Taiyue, Fenghua Fairy, and Pavilion Master Li.

A total of eight people!

Most importantly, all eight were experts at the mid-Sage stage.

In the current Lingtian Pass, they were absolutely the top experts below the three great cultivators.

Even placed across the entire Eastern Wilderness cultivation world, they were existences capable of guarding a region.

Seeing this lineup, Luo Chen's pupils contracted slightly, knowing that something major must have happened.

Otherwise, with the four commanders driving billions of demon beasts to attack the city, the Lingtian City Lord would never have pulled them out individually.

The eight true men clearly understood this principle as well, each with an undeniable look of worry on their faces.

Of course, Luo Chen's arrival still drew greetings from a few friends.

"As expected of the Demon-Slaying Sage! One moment he's in fierce battle with a Demon Emperor, the next he can withdraw unscathed. It shows his strength is abundant and he has plenty of room to maneuver!"

Shouyang Mountain Man, also known as Elder Lu of the Dongyang Sect, said this with a chuckle.

Luo Chen smiled naturally. "That Demon Emperor had no intention of fighting to the death. When he saw me stop, he didn't linger either, and we each went our separate ways."

Is that so?

The others half-believed, half-doubted this.

Luo Chen couldn't be bothered to explain. He couldn't very well say that battles with those fourth-rank initial Demon Emperors were generally under his control!

Pulling Nine Spirit Primordial Lord aside, Luo Chen asked in a low voice, "Do you know what major event has happened, that they're calling us so urgently, not even caring about the battle at Lingtian Pass?"

"I don't know either, but it's definitely no small matter. Perhaps if you ask Fenghua Fairy and Pavilion Master Li, they might know some inside information." Nine Spirit Primordial Lord said.

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow, his gaze sweeping over Fenghua Fairy and Pavilion Master Li.

Sure enough!

Their faces were now completely unable to hide their worry.

Not only that!

Luo Chen's gaze swept over the others. Aside from Jin Ling and Nine Spirit Primordial Lord, almost everyone had worry on their faces.

Even Shouyang Mountain Man, who had taken the initiative to greet Luo Chen earlier, had an undeniable doubt deep in his eyes.

"Could it be about matters in the rear?"

Just as Luo Chen was pondering this, a figure walked out from behind the hall.

Not only that, but a phantom image also materialized out of thin air within the hall.

The former was the Lingtian City Lord, while the phantom was Tian Duzi.

Seeing that everyone had arrived, the Lingtian City Lord's expression was solemn.

"I'll make it brief. I've gathered you all to inform you of something. Currently, the three great commanders Bi Zheng, Juan Kuang, and Jinxiu Xian are leading a group of Demon Emperors to bypass Lingtian Pass and launch a surprise attack on the rear."

"Their intention is not occupation, but destruction and slaughter!"

"In this situation, ordinary experts are insufficient. So we need you to mobilize, cooperate with the various forces in the rear, and drive them out!"
