# Chapter 725: The Five-Hundred-Year Grand Competition, Dao Protector Luo Chen

"Fellow Daoist Luo Chen, you will not blame this old man for using a favor to seek repayment, will you?"

Fu Chaosheng spoke with a beaming smile, his small eyes flashing with sharp light as they kept sizing Luo Chen up.

Luo Chen smiled awkwardly. This was somewhat unexpected.

Back then, Han Zhan had indeed analyzed for him that Fu Chaosheng must have some aim in showing him such goodwill.

Yet he had never expected the other party to act so directly, climbing straight up the pole without the roundabout manner of a merchant bargaining back and forth.

But since the matter had come to this point, Luo Chen could not push it away.

"How could I? Fellow Daoist, what are you saying? You may speak frankly about what the matter is. If it lies within Luo's ability, I will certainly assist with all my strength!"

What a fine phrase, "within Luo's ability"!

Fu Chaosheng cursed inwardly. He had known that this Devil Lord Azure Yang, whom he had briefly met back then, was absolutely no simple ascetic who knew only bitter cultivation.

He dared not say Luo Chen understood every affair of the world, but in worldly dealings, the man was not one bit inferior to old merchant hands like them.

His face turned solemn. Fu Chaosheng put away his smile and said seriously:

"This matter is not related to me, but to a granddaughter of mine. I wish to ask you to protect her for a period of time."

Act as a bodyguard?

Luo Chen frowned. "What cultivation does your granddaughter possess?"

"Comparable to yours."

"This..." Luo Chen's expression grew even more solemn. "If her realm is comparable to mine, and she is also a disciple of a sacred land, she surely has her own accomplishments. Moreover, you, Elder Fu, are honored as a Tianyuan Dao Sect disciple, with countless powerful experts assisting within, and as an elder of the merchant alliance who has done business in the Northern Sea for years, your network is incomparably broad. Let alone me, I fear even a mid-stage Sage cultivator could be invited. What kind of matter actually requires a little newly advanced Sage cultivator like me to protect her?"

As he said this, Luo Chen had already begun to retreat in his heart.

Fu Chaosheng sighed and said faintly, "I wonder whether you have heard of the Tianyuan Grand Competition?"

"A sect grand competition?" Luo Chen nodded. "I have naturally heard of it. It is said to be held once every hundred years, and each time, experts from great powers across the five continents come to observe the ceremony and celebrate the birth of a new generation of heavenly talents in the Tianyuan Dao Sect. A competition of such scale is top-tier even among the countless internal sect competitions beneath heaven. But I am an outsider..."

"Then have you heard of Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven?"

Luo Chen was at a loss.

Fu Chaosheng looked as though he had expected this and slowly explained the origins of Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven.

In remote antiquity, desolate beasts covered the Mountains and Seas Realm. They were the true masters of this world.

At that time, the human race was weak, scattered across the land, and slaughtered at will by beasts.

Only when the five great sacred lands appeared did an ancient war lasting ten thousand years begin. One powerful desolate beast after another was either slain, suppressed, or forced to retreat into the wilderness.

Contesting every inch of land in this way, bit by bit, the five great sacred lands opened up the five continents, the most essential lands in the vast Mountains and Seas Realm and those most suitable for human cultivation.

The five great sacred lands had cut through thorns and brambles, and their merit was immense!

That war was called the ancient human-demon war [TERM?]. The reason it was not called the human-desolate war was that in the middle and later stages, many weaker desolate beasts changed over to the demon cultivation path to speed up their realm advancement, and only later changed banners and identified themselves as demons.

Even now, many demon beasts in the Mountains and Seas Realm could decide at the fourth tier whether to transform, either walking the demon cultivator path or shattering their demon cores and reverting to their desolate ancestry.

Black King was the product of such a choice. Having lost his demon core, he accordingly could also be said to have been forced onto the path of a desolate beast.

Leaving aside the internal divisions between the demon race and desolate beasts.

Simply speaking of that war, the Tianyuan Dao Sect paid the greatest price and achieved the most glorious results. Therefore, after the war, it obtained the essence within the essence of the Mountains and Seas Realm: Central Continent!

There, there was neither the threat of endless demon beasts like in the Eastern Wilderness and Northern Sea, nor the corrosion of gu insects and poisonous mists like in the Southern Frontier. Its richness in resources far surpassed the barren Western Desert.

And Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven was one of the fruits of that ancient war.

"The Mirage Dragon was one of the top great desolate beasts. It swallowed clouds and exhaled mist, transformed the sun, moon, and stars, and formed a grotto-heaven within itself."

"Even after death, its cultivation did not disperse, but remained in Central Continent, causing endless disaster."

"Our sect's experts could have removed it, but under the first-generation sect master's decision against all objections, that Mirage Dragon corpse was transformed into a secret realm. This is the origin of Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven."

Fu Chaosheng paused for breath, then continued, "The transformed secret realm generates fortune within itself and can borrow the power of the Mirage Dragon to allow Sage cultivators to merge spirit with heaven and earth and comprehend the true meaning of laws."

Surprise appeared on Luo Chen's face. "When Sage cultivators merge spirit with heaven and earth, are they not afraid of being assimilated by heaven and earth?"

"Were you not also merging spirit with heaven and earth just now?" Fu Chaosheng asked in return.

Luo Chen explained, "I happened upon an opportunity and resonated with the true flame inside my body. Borrowing the response between true flame and heaven and earth, I touched the truth of heaven and earth indirectly."

Fu Chaosheng smiled faintly. "Is that not a coincidence? The power of the Mirage Dragon is different in method but similar in result to your source true flame. Moreover, the Mirage Dragon itself is proficient in illusion arts, enough to make falsehood indistinguishable from truth. It can allow cultivators inside to comprehend the true meaning of laws, and because the Mirage Dragon's body forms its own grotto-heaven, it has a natural barrier from the outside world. In this way, it can avoid to the greatest extent the assimilation of the cultivator's divine soul by heaven and earth."

Luo Chen opened his mouth, but for a time did not know how to answer.

After absorbing the netherfire inside Old Monster Netherfire's body, his Withering-Bloom True Flame had transformed between life and death, and by a fortunate accident entered that state of sudden enlightenment.

Unexpectedly, within the Tianyuan Dao Sect, this method had already been "standardized."

One could even say that the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven faced not merely one person, which meant it had been expanded.

Seeing Luo Chen's shock, Fu Chaosheng shook his head and continued:

"Even within the Tianyuan Dao Sect, this kind of opportunity is a top-tier fortune that countless people dream of."

"But not everyone is qualified to enjoy it."

"Because the power of the Mirage Dragon is limited. Each time it is consumed, it needs several hundred years to recover a little."

"Therefore, our Dao Sect fixed the interval at once every five hundred years. This time also happens to coincide with our sect's grand competition, so every five hundred years, that internal disciple competition is inevitably the fiercest and has the most generous rewards."

What kind of reward counted as the most generous?

Magic tools and magic treasures? Pills and spirit medicines?

No!

Compared to these, merging spirit with heaven and earth and comprehending the Great Dao was undoubtedly the reward cultivators longed for most.

Ordinarily, the Tianyuan Dao Sect's grand competition was also divided into three realms: Sage, Adept, and Initiate, with one competition set for each realm.

But in the five-hundred-year competition, the participants were almost all early-stage Sage cultivators!

As for why only early-stage Sage cultivators participated, and not Adepts, or mid-stage and even late-stage Sage cultivators?

Fu Chaosheng also explained this point briefly.

For Adept cultivators to use that opportunity would be far too wasteful. Moreover, Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven also generated illusion beasts, which Adept cultivators found extremely difficult to resist.

As for mid-stage cultivators, because they cultivated over long years, their Purple Mansion barriers gradually thickened and were not like early-stage Sage cultivators, who still maintained a certain degree of resonance with heaven and earth.

As for late-stage Sage great cultivators?

The Tianyuan Dao Sect's late-stage Sage great cultivators had more or less all participated in a grand competition after advancing to the Sage realm.

Even if some had not encountered the five-hundred-year grand competition, at that age they could not very well compete with juniors for opportunities.

"In the more than three thousand years since the ancient war, our sect has successively held six five-hundred-year grand competitions. The detailed rules have also gradually been perfected. By now, it has been clearly established that only those who advanced to the Sage realm within the past hundred years, or those below that, may step into Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven."

"It is incomparably dangerous inside. Especially when merging spirit with heaven and earth to comprehend the Great Dao, there are illusion beasts attacking from outside and opponents interfering. Therefore, every participant may bring one Dao protector who meets the rule restrictions."

"Luo Chen, you are the most suitable Dao protector I have chosen for my granddaughter!"

With this speech, he slowly laid out the entire reason for the matter clearly.

Whether anything was still being held back could be set aside for now, but Luo Chen had indeed formed an initial concept of this five-hundred-year grand competition.

Under the other man's gaze, he raised the first question.

"Why me?"

"Why can it not be you?" Fu Chaosheng asked in return. Then he gathered his focus and said, "Over these years, I have widely built connections and successively found several suitable Dao protectors, but compared to you, they are ultimately lacking."

Then, as if counting treasures in his own household, he listed Luo Chen's battle achievements.

In the battle of Coldlight Island, Luo Chen had fought many with few, and crossed realms to battle, finally leaving calmly.

In the Myriad Immortals Assembly, he had ranged across the Three Oceans defensive line, killed countless powerful demon beasts, and established Devil Lord Azure Yang's illustrious name.

Within Demon-Fall Land, he had counter-killed Feiyunzi!

"And all of these were glorious achievements you obtained while still at the Golden Core Stage. I cannot say no one will ever come after you, but they can be counted as unprecedented."

Luo Chen tugged at the corner of his mouth. "Fellow Daoist has paid quite a bit of attention to me!"

"Haha!"

Fu Chaosheng laughed loudly. "Do not be angry. My Tianyuan Merchant Alliance's influence spreads across the Northern Sea, so we do have some advantage in intelligence."

The next moment, his tone abruptly shifted.

"You were already that formidable at the Golden Core Stage. Now that you have achieved the Sage realm, you will only have climbed another floor!"

"Nine days ago, when you overwhelmed third-level Sage Split Heaven Stream Lord with a pure Sage domain, that already explained everything."

"There is no one but you to be my granddaughter's Dao protector!"

Luo Chen fell silent.

Indeed, any one of the things he had done could be taken out and made into a legend.

If an ordinary Adept cultivator had even one of them, they could boast about it for a lifetime, but he had done many.

There were even some things Fu Chaosheng had not found out.

After a long silence, Luo Chen asked the second question.

"Is there danger in the five-hundred-year grand competition?"

"If I said there was none, this old man would be far too insincere, but it absolutely, absolutely will not lead to death. After all, even within our sect, Sage cultivators are very important talents. How could we be willing to let them be easily lost in an internal competition?"

"Certain?"

"Certain!"

Luo Chen fell silent again, lowering his head in thought.

Fu Chaosheng did not disturb him. This kind of matter always required someone to consider clearly.

He had indeed shown Luo Chen favor, but that had been a Sage cultivator toward an Adept cultivator.

Now that their realms were equal, those favors could only count as minor kindnesses.

If the other party refused to acknowledge them, there was nothing he could do.

Surely he could not truly demand a spring of repayment for a drop of water?

The relationship between the two of them was not that close.

Only after a long time did Luo Chen raise his head.

"I have considered for too long and made you wait, Elder Fu. But this matter is indeed difficult for me to decide. After all, if I decide to become your granddaughter's Dao protector, I will have to face the other heavenly talents of the Tianyuan Dao Sect no matter what. With one careless step, perhaps enmity will be formed."

Fu Chaosheng's expression changed slightly. "Though my Fu family cannot be counted among the great houses within the Dao Sect, we are still an aristocratic clan that has been passed down for several thousand years. Those grudges, I imagine..."

"If that is truly so, why does Fellow Daoist not choose a Dao protector from within the family?" Luo Chen asked in return.

Fu Chaosheng's face stiffened. These words were somewhat painful.

If the Fu family were truly that formidable, the clan would absolutely not lack suitable experts.

And the position of Tianyuan Merchant Alliance elder seemed incomparably honored in the eyes of outsiders.

But if converted to a small sect or faction, in truth it was merely a deacon handling worldly affairs outside.

What kind of people and families would be sent to such positions?

Clearly, Luo Chen was no fool.

This time, it was Fu Chaosheng's turn to fall silent.

Under Luo Chen's long gaze, Fu Chaosheng could only smile bitterly.

"It seems I have made you laugh. My Fu family is indeed somewhat lacking in successors. Before my granddaughter, I was the only Sage cultivator, barely able to keep us from falling out of the aristocratic-clan ranks. Now that my granddaughter has advanced to the Sage realm, I ask for more. I hope she can break through to Ascendant in the future and raise the Fu family to the rank of great house. For this, I can only stake everything."

At this point, his spirit lifted.

"You seem to need this thing very much?"

The glazed treasure bottle appeared in his hand.

Luo Chen received it. It was precisely the soul essence Old Monster Netherfire had transferred earlier.

"If you agree to help, I can gift you two more bottles."

Luo Chen raised his brow. "Also souls of ten million people?"

"Do not underestimate me. The Northern Sea cultivation world has seen heaven-shaking slaughter over these years, and who knows how many demonic path scoundrels have secretly refined soul essence. With my position as a merchant alliance elder, a mere few bottles of soul essence from ten million people can still be collected."

Luo Chen was somewhat moved.

"Not only that. If you agree, I can also recommend you to become a guest elder of my Dao Sect. At that time, even if you offend other heavenly talents inside Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, with this guest elder identity, the other party will not dare deal with you openly."

A Dao Sect guest elder!

Luo Chen took a deep breath, and his heart had already made its decision.

But at this moment, since the other party was asking him rather than him asking the other party, he had to strive as much as possible while bargaining.

"To tell the truth, Luo is grateful to you and indeed has the intention to assist. But now, I have just achieved the Sage realm, and on the path of cultivation there are countless tangled threads, to the point that I cannot sleep by day or night. If Fellow Daoist can help me resolve some trivial matters and save that time, I will certainly be able to focus on cultivation, increase my ability, and do my utmost in the five-hundred-year grand competition!"

Trivial matters?

Fu Chaosheng smiled faintly. "Feel free to speak!"

Luo Chen also smiled, and immediately stated these so-called trivial matters one by one.

Fu Chaosheng's head ached as he listened, but he discovered they indeed were not major matters. With his authority in the merchant alliance, accomplishing them would not be difficult.

Moreover, Luo Chen was not blindly demanding things, but was willing to pay remuneration and was merely asking him for help.

Rather than calling the third matter a condition, it was more like normal business.

...

Inside a secret chamber in Molan Court.

Old Ghost Nanqi rushed in hurriedly.

"Split Heaven, you did not accomplish the matter you promised me before. This time, you must help me!"

The moment he entered, he went straight to the point.

Split Heaven Stream Lord was displeased. "That matter was not because I failed to accomplish it. Old Monster Netherfire simply gave no opportunity."

"I do not care. That Commanding Wind Gu of mine helped you greatly in gaining your current ability. You ought to return peaches for plums, yes?"

Split Heaven Stream Lord took a deep breath. "Fine. Speak for now and let me hear it."

"This matter is simple. You and I find an opportunity and do that Wilderness Wanderer in! Everything on him belongs to you, except that bottle of soul essence from ten million people, which belongs to me!"

Old Ghost Nanqi's expression was sinister. "He is a mere rogue cultivator who has just formed his Nascent Soul, yet he has that level of strength and wealth. One can see how abundant his net worth is, so do not tell me you are not tempted. Moreover, on the plaza of Return Origin Palace, you failed to establish your might and instead were stepped on as a stepping stone by someone else. Do not tell me there is no resentment or hatred in your heart?"

Split Heaven Stream Lord did not refute.

After weighing it for a while, he slowly nodded.

"When do we act?"

"When the War-Ceasing Assembly ends!" Old Ghost Nanqi was full of confidence. "At that time, everyone will return to their own homes, and no one will meddle. With you and me joining hands, Wilderness Wanderer and Sage Langya will absolutely not be our opponents."

"That is indeed a decent opportunity." Split Heaven Stream Lord smiled. "I heard that after that kid healed Netherfire, his own vitality was greatly damaged, and he will probably have to stay in Molan Court for some time. This period will be enough for us to plan the details."

Old Ghost Nanqi also revealed a smile, as if everything was already certain.

Yet they did not know that Luo Chen's figure had already long since left Molan Court.

In the deep sea, a candle flame slowly rose within dark, foul-smelling walls of flesh.

"So this is Black King's stomach pouch [TERM?]?"

Looking at the terrifying acid secreted from the walls of flesh, goosebumps actually rose over Luo Chen's powerful fleshly body.

If he did not make the slightest defense, this acid could probably corrode and digest even his fleshly body!

"Sage, where are we going now?" Sang Jinghe asked curiously.

Luo Chen smiled faintly. "First we will make the planned trip to Coldlight Island, then go near Divine Origin City and wait for Sage Fu."

After saying this, he took out a chair and lay down comfortably.

A thin beast-hide booklet appeared in his hand, and he soon became absorbed in it.

Sang Jinghe felt nothing toward Coldlight Island, but he understood Divine Origin City very well. That place was extremely close to the Myriad Immortals Assembly!

It was also very close to his Earth Mulberry Island!

For a time, he felt an unexpected shyness at nearing his homeland.

He wondered how those junior brothers of Earth Mulberry Sect were doing now?

In the deep sea, a black jiaolong wrapped in currents surged forward, bypassing spirit-vein islands one after another as it swiftly advanced toward Coldlight Island.
