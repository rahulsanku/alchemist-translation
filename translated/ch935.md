# Chapter 935: Courting Disaster, Inviting Tribulation, the Tyrant Cultivator Qi Tian

Black armor, dark and heavy.

Jade belt, bright and gleaming.

Tall crown, like a mountain peak.

Golden plumes, like sharp blades.

With each step, his movement was ethereal and unfathomable, yet between motion and stillness, he seemed like a dragon strolling or a tiger leaping, his presence suffocating.

When Luo Chen emerged from the nameless desolate mountain where he had been refining his natal treasure, Cheng Bai felt as though a demon king had walked out from a mountain of corpses and a sea of blood, bringing with him a thick, bloody aura as he arrived before him.

"Gulp..."

An involuntary swallow went down his throat, and Cheng Bai knelt without hesitation.

"This junior, Cheng Bai, pays respects to Senior!"

Luo Chen looked down at the man at his feet and said gruffly, "Your name is not important. I wish to know who this Thunderclap Cave Master is, and which force he hails from."

Cheng Bai kept his head low and said in a trembling voice, "Thunderclap Cave Master has no sect or faction. He attained the Dao as a rogue cultivator, and he is the only Sage among the thirty-six cave masters of Wind-Thunder Gorge."

"A rogue cultivator?"

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow. Since when had rogue cultivators found it so easy to achieve the Sage realm?

In the vast Northern Sea, the only named Sage-level rogue cultivators were the Three Great Wanderers.

Now that he had just arrived in the Southern Frontier, there was suddenly another such figure rising from the grassroots?

Cheng Bai nodded repeatedly and began to speak of Thunderclap Cave Master's growth experience.

Wind-Thunder Gorge had originally been a treacherous and harsh place, unworthy of any major power's attention.

It was only because of the presence of a large number of scattered spirit veins that it attracted many unattached rogue cultivators willing to risk coming here to cultivate.

Over time, the saying of the thirty-six cave masters came into being.

At that time, most of the thirty-six cave masters were at the Initiate realm, and the occasional one or two Adepts were considered anomalies.

They gathered together, each minding their own business, and when faced with oppression from outside major powers, they would also band together for warmth and jointly resist external enemies.

However, in the last two or three hundred years, things had gradually changed.

Many cave masters had inexplicably achieved success in cultivation, greatly increasing their realms.

Especially that Thunderclap Cave Master, who had endured countless hardships and dangers, finally became a Sage, dominating the thirty-six caves alone.

As Luo Chen listened to this account, his eyes narrowed slightly.

"Two or three hundred years..."

As he muttered to himself, Cheng Bai let out a sigh of relief, carefully raising his head.

Looking at that incredibly youthful face, he asked the doubt in his heart.

"This junior presumes to ask, is Senior also a Sage?"

"Oh?" Luo Chen looked at him with a half-smile. "What if I am, and what if I'm not?"

Cheng Bai forced a smile. "Our cave master most enjoys making friends with—"

"Tell the truth!"

Cheng Bai's heart trembled, and he quickly said, "In recent years, Wind-Thunder Gorge has seen many experts emerge, and rogue cultivators have greatly increased in realm. The nearby largest Sage family, the Feng clan, believes that Wind-Thunder Gorge is a hidden, peerless treasure land and has designs on it. Although our cave master is strong, he has been a Sage for too short a time and lacks connections. Faced with the Feng clan's covetousness, he is at his wit's end. Especially since the Feng clan has been making frequent moves lately, widely inviting allies, attempting to invade and occupy Wind-Thunder Gorge—this has become a major worry for our cave master. His bitter cultivation of the *Flash Thunder Art* is also because the Feng clan's supreme elder has an outstanding movement technique and incredible speed; he was thinking of making up for this shortcoming..."

"Long-winded!" Luo Chen snorted lightly. "Just say you want to invite me to lend a hand."

Cheng Bai nodded repeatedly. "Exactly so. If Senior could help a little, the thirty-six caves of Wind-Thunder Gorge, with their tens of thousands of cultivators, would be endlessly grateful. A minor like me, by recommending a strong expert, would count as a great merit, and might also receive some small reward."

The last part was at least frank.

Without hesitation, Luo Chen asked directly, "What are the benefits?"

Cheng Bai opened his mouth, seemingly unable to react to such a straightforward way of asking for benefits.

Seeing this, Luo Chen waved his hand. "Never mind. I will ask Thunderclap Cave Master for them myself when the time comes."

With that, he turned and walked back into the cave dwelling.

"Wait outside!"

Cheng Bai breathed a sigh of relief. He had probably made it through this ordeal.

He had been afraid that these old monsters would kill without asking any questions.

As for whether introducing a strong expert to his cave master would bring fortune or disaster, that was a matter for later and had nothing to do with him. He just wanted to survive the present.

The only question was whether this old monster was strong enough.

He still hadn't said whether he was a Sage or not.

Just as his heart was filled with anxiety, a voice exploded like thunder into his ears.

"I am not at the Sage realm, but any ordinary Sage cannot withstand a pair of my bare fists!"

Cheng Bai's mouth hung slightly open, and two words echoed in his mind.

"Body cultivator!"

...

Inside the rough cave dwelling, Luo Chen looked at the mess around him. The cave, as if carved by knives and axes, struck by lightning and burned by fire, made his heart sink.

With a thought, the Primordial Unity Cauldron spun and appeared before him.

Looking at it, the spiritual energy was contained within, the radiance was restrained.

Although it was not outstanding, anyone who saw it would know that this was an extremely rare treasure in the world.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron was naturally fine, and in excellent condition, far surpassing its peak state.

Especially after it had melted the Xirang and most of the Five Elements materials from the Five-Colored Pagoda, its heaviness was beyond words. Even the main heavy-suppression treasures and true artifacts of the peak period of Cloud-Scraping Cave or Hundred Crafts Mountain could hardly compare.

The problem lay in the refining process.

At that time, it had inexplicably attracted a "heavenly tribulation"!

The dark clouds gathering and thunder and lightning that Cheng Bai had seen earlier were the phenomena produced by Luo Chen's refining of his natal treasure.

Although it was not a true heavenly tribulation, merely drawing upon some scattered thunder and lightning power from heaven and earth, the Primordial Unity Cauldron was only a true artifact, far from reaching the level of a spirit treasure!

"Why did it attract a faint lightning tribulation?"

Luo Chen naturally did not think it was the Xirang's problem. No matter how powerful Xirang was, the quantity was too small, and it was merely a material.

If it had such a side effect, how would the Divine Origin City have dared to use it against enemies all these years?

Immediately, he realized the two anomalies within his natal treasure.

One was the gray light from the Demon-Fall Land. Even now, he had not figured out what it was. He only knew that after it merged, his success rate in refining treasures and pills had become incredibly high, and it could even help refine thunder and lightning power.

If the gray light had attracted it, that was possible.

The other was the fifth-order divine gu—the Tribulation Gu—that he had taken from his son Luo Lingxi's body.

Thinking of the latter, Luo Chen's mood became somewhat heavy.

The hidden danger of the Tribulation Gu was far greater than the world imagined.

Even Gu Xianfengjiang could not suppress it and could only lock himself in Sealing Immortal Valley.

Even so, that Sealing Immortal Valley was plagued by constant earthly disasters, making it hard to find peace.

"Could it be that after my natal treasure absorbed the Tribulation Gu, it also gained the effect of courting disaster and inviting tribulation?"

"This is not a good thing. If I sacrifice the Primordial Unity Cauldron during battle and it instead attracts a heavenly tribulation to strike me, that would be harming myself."

His expression shifting uncertainly, Luo Chen began a series of actions.

He struck a series of seals and restrictions onto the Primordial Unity Cauldron, temporarily sealing it.

This was the best method he could think of at the moment.

Sealing his natal treasure would prevent it from causing any trouble during daily cultivation or sudden battles.

Fortunately, Luo Chen currently had no shortage of various weapons.

Perhaps in terms of comprehensiveness, those treasures were not as good as the Primordial Unity Cauldron, but in terms of individual power, they were roughly comparable.

Especially the Yuantu Sword, a killing true artifact that was already infinitely close to a spirit treasure. If it was not used, it was fine, but once used, it would surely draw blood, surpassing even the Primordial Unity Cauldron's killing ability.

"However, I also need to consider the possibility that my reputation might spread to the Southern Frontier. If my commonly used methods are recognized, it will not be good for my travels in the Southern Frontier."

As he thought, Luo Chen's hand shook, and several treasures appeared.

There was a fine needle shining with golden light, five dark and dull awls, a slowly spinning sword pellet, and a thick, gray-white stone rod.

Especially the latter. On that stone rod was a conspicuous sword scar, left by the Yuantu Sword back then.

Luo Chen thought for a moment and swallowed the sword pellet into his Purple Mansion.

Although the Greater Five Elements Sword Formation was good, it was clearly an immortal technique and not very suitable for his current "body cultivator" identity.

Moreover, he had used the immature Greater Five Elements Sword Formation many times during the battle at Lingtian Pass, and some people knew of this technique.

So, only the other three remained.

The Soul-Weaving Needle and the Five-Pronged Suppression Awls came from the Great Demon Emperor Hundred Legs. One could attack the soul, and the other could seal magical power.

That stone rod, named the Chaos Heaven Staff, came from the Great Demon Emperor Huang Man. It had not undergone much refinement, but purely by virtue of its special material, it had become a powerful weapon comparable to a true artifact.

"Using these three as my main attack methods, combined with the Mystic Dust Armor, the Nine-Jade Belt, the Mountain-Leveling Crown, the two Life-Seizing Golden Plumes, and the Cloud-Stepping Boots, should be enough."

As Luo Chen muttered, he moved.

The golden needle was hidden under the root of his tongue.

The black awls were embedded within his belt.

As for that thick stone rod, it kept shrinking in his hand. Finally, as if thinking of something, a playful mood arose, and he actually stuffed it into his ear.

Patting his ear, Luo Chen grinned.

"Perfect!"

Only then did he confidently leave this temporary cave dwelling.

If outsiders knew that Luo Chen had prepared eight true artifacts for his journey to the Southern Frontier, they would probably be stunned.

With such a lavish wealth, where in the world could he not go?

He was simply too cautious!

...

"What is this?"

The sudden question almost made Cheng Bai's hand tremble, crushing the illusory little insect.

Turning back, it was the same senior as before.

He took a deep breath and respectfully handed over the insect in his hand.

"This is a Formless Gu larva. It likes to parasitize reptiles and demon beasts. Once it matures, it will devour the demon beast's essence blood."

Luo Chen frowned, took the larva, and looked at it carefully. It looked like an earthworm.

"What use is it?"

Cheng Bai was somewhat surprised. Was this senior not very familiar with gu insects?

The great reputation of the Formless Gu was known to everyone in the Southern Frontier!

Perhaps seeing his confusion, Luo Chen said calmly, "The path I walk is different from ordinary gu cultivators. I am a body cultivator, and I have been wandering in the wilderness all these years, so I am not very familiar with these little things."

So that was it.

Cheng Bai nodded, then couldn't help but say, "This is no little thing! Once it matures, after a cultivator refines it, they can transform without form, and their movement technique becomes incredibly agile."

"That Feng clan's supreme elder's wind escape technique is exquisite and unparalleled. Ordinary people can hardly even touch the corner of his robe. Our cave master has been troubled by this for a long time. He finally managed to obtain a *Flash Thunder Art*, but because he lacks a Formless Gu, he has been unable to even begin."

"Senior, the Formless Gu in your hand, although still a larva, if catalyzed with a secret method, might have a chance to mature early and help our cave master learn the true technique."

Luo Chen was inwardly surprised. Using gu insects to assist in cultivating spells?

He knew that there was such a cultivation habit in the Southern Frontier.

Using various gu insects to assist in cultivation, from Qi Refinement entry to Initiate and Adept, the shadow of gu insects ran through the entire life of a Southern Frontier cultivator.

But he had not expected that they could also be used to assist in cultivating spells.

Somehow, he thought of his brief exchange with Sage Heavenly Mountain back then.

The other party had once used a sword technique. Now that he thought about it carefully, that sword technique also seemed to be performed using gu insects.

Sword Gu?

And this movement technique gu?

Were there any suitable gu insects to assist in body cultivation?

Thinking of this, Luo Chen's heart could not help but pound.

His body cultivation realm had progressed extremely slowly since reaching the mid-fourth stage.

The self-created *Myriad Path Convergence* had yielded little effect.

Later, it was only by relying on the batch of thousand-year Wumiao Immortal Brew that Black King had obtained for him that his body cultivation progress began to improve bit by bit, starting from his internal organs. Now, he had barely reached [Archaic Fourth Stage 61/100].

Measured by the immortal cultivation realm, it was roughly equivalent to the sixth level of the Sage realm.

On the surface, it seemed comparable to his current Qi Refining realm, but one had to know that Luo Chen's body cultivation progress had once far surpassed his Qi Refining. When he achieved the fourth stage, he was still in the middle Adept realm.

Years had passed, and it had actually been caught up.

This showed how slow the progress was.

Moreover, the stock of thousand-year Wumiao Immortal Brew was limited. One jar drunk meant one jar less. After so many years, only five jars remained.

Luo Chen was reluctant to continue drinking it and deliberately kept it as a backup.

If he could find a method in the Southern Frontier to once again skyrocket his body cultivation progress, then this trip would not be in vain, whether or not he could find the Devil Heart Gu King.

Of course, Luo Chen suppressed this impulse and did not ask Cheng Bai further.

A senior expert should naturally have the bearing and demeanor of a senior expert.

An old monster who had been out of the world for a long time could not act like a clueless newcomer.

Especially now that his self-set persona was completely different from the former Alchemy Sect's Luo Chen.

Under Cheng Bai's eager gaze, Luo Chen threw the Formless Gu larva back.

Then!

He turned around!

Pressed down on the void!

*Boom!*

Under Cheng Bai's wide-eyed stare, that nameless desolate mountain, which had earlier attracted the worship of countless demon beasts, was crushed into powder by a tremendous force.

The earth raised dust, and the void exploded with waves.

A surging air current rolled in.

Luo Chen flicked his hand and said calmly, "Let's go!"

Cheng Bai swallowed his saliva and was swept up by a gust of wind, following Luo Chen into the clouds.

Without any flying treasure, without any great magical power, he simply flew swiftly through the air.

Cheng Bai pointed a direction, and Luo Chen flew rapidly towards it.

After a long silence, Cheng Bai finally asked with reverence and caution, "This junior presumes to ask, how should I address Senior?"

In the howling wind, Luo Chen's voice entered his ears like steel.

"I am Qi Tian. Some enemies, before they died, said that my actions were quite overbearing, so they added a Dao name for me: the Tyrant Cultivator."

The Tyrant Cultivator Qi Tian!

Cheng Bai firmly etched this arrogant name, which dared to rival heaven, into his heart.
