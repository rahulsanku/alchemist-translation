# Chapter 979: Great Desolate Awakening, Riding the Sea to Sink the Land

“Huff… huff…”

Within the vast sea region, a figure stood amidst the waves, rising and falling with the swells.

His mouth was slightly open, breathing heavily in great gasps.

His originally handsome face now appeared quite ferocious.

And even more ferocious were the dense wounds covering his body.

His shoulders, neck, arms, waist, abdomen, thighs, knees, joints, and finger bones—all bore scars of varying sizes, with flesh turned outward and blood flowing without cease.

This was an extremely astonishing thing for Luo Chen.

With source power in his body, even when the Nether Dragon had destroyed half his body with a single strike, he had been able to consume most of his source power to quickly regenerate.

For his injuries to remain unhealed for so long, there were only two possibilities.

One was that his source power had been too depleted to close the wounds in time.

The other was that his opponent's attacks carried some unique attribute that made the wounds extremely difficult to recover from.

And both of these situations were now simultaneously present on Luo Chen's body.

"She is even stronger than I imagined!"

Luo Chen took a deep breath, then pressed his lips tightly together. His crimson-golden pupils had already fully opened, staring intently at his surroundings.

Azure Frost's method of body refinement had gradually been seen through by Luo Chen during their continuous battle.

Like himself, she was not a traditional body cultivator, nor had she walked the path of qi and blood transformation.

But unlike him, who used vast external resources to transform into boundless physical power, Azure Frost's strength in body refinement lay in her original form!

Her original form was the divine bird Azure Luan from immortal and god legends!

Unlike most demon beasts who, after transforming, focused solely on qi refinement, Azure Frost had not abandoned the advantage of her own physical body. Not only did she cultivate qi and comprehend the laws of space, but she also persisted in tempering her body, forging her already immensely powerful physique into something like a peerless divine weapon.

Thus, she could achieve the state where her physical body commanded mana. An ordinary punch or kick, when executed, was like a spell or divine ability, sweeping all before it.

No fancy tricks, yet one force overcame ten techniques!

If Luo Chen had not known ten or twenty techniques, if his methods were not endless and varied, he likely would not have been able to withstand her simple and unadorned attacks time and again.

"No wonder she is hailed as the number one under the Ascendant realm!"

As he praised her in his heart, a golden light flashed in Luo Chen's eyes.

The Yuantu sword, held reversed behind him, instantly transformed its sword light, splitting into five, each stabbing toward the directions above, below, left, right, and straight ahead.

Puff!

Puff!

Puff!

Puff!

Four sounds of illusory shadows shattering rang out in succession.

And the final solid sword tip met a slender palm.

Clang!

Even though Luo Chen had seen through her Void Concealment Divine Art many times, and even broken her Void Shadow Residual Method—which she had optimized during battle—Azure Frost still could not hide her surprise.

With one hand gripping the Yuantu sword and the other sidestepping to close in on Luo Chen's body, she struck toward his head.

Her autumn-water eyes met his crimson-golden pupils, and Azure Frost seemed to ponder.

"Is it those eyes?"

Her left palm strike transformed into two fingers, forming a gouging motion.

Luo Chen flattened his waist and abdomen, executing a move similar to an iron-plank bridge from the mortal world, while the Yuantu sword in his hand, under the added force of source power, began to spin wildly.

Buzz!!!

The extreme rotation made it impossible to hold on.

Azure Frost felt pain and was forced to release the sharp Yuantu sword. Her left fingers only managed to tear two strips of muscle from Luo Chen's retreating body.

"Huff..."

Luo Chen exhaled a mouthful of turbid qi. Looking at the bone-deep wound on his forearm, a flash of insight struck him. He suddenly understood why his opponent, despite having a weaker soul foundation, could ignore his Ghosts and Gods Give Way soul art and accurately hit his true body.

"It's qi sense induction!"

Luo Chen was not unfamiliar with this technique.

Because Wang Yuan had once used it.

So-called qi sense induction was the movement of spirit following intent, and intent issuing from qi.

Every living being had qi sense that manifested externally!

Unless one's control over the body reached the stage of perfect completeness, with no leaks and no discharges, one could not conceal their qi sense.

Martial arts masters who had achieved success in cultivation, even without divine sense, only needed to spread their own qi sense. Within a certain range, they could sense the qi sense of others that differed from their own.

This method did not require a particularly strong soul foundation. One only needed to sense the qi and respond accordingly.

Luo Chen had not expected that Azure Frost, a non-orthodox body cultivator, would also know this technique.

And it just happened to counter his Ghosts and Gods Give Way.

After all, Luo Chen did not have a Leakless Golden Body that could seal all qi sense from his entire body.

"In that case, this soul art has become a burden instead."

With a thought, Luo Chen dispersed the layers of illusions swirling around him. His attacks no longer sought cleverness and agility but became wide and forceful instead.

Seeing this, Azure Frost roughly understood his intention.

Her Void Concealment Divine Art had been seen through, and being at the shore of the Yellow Spring Sea prevented her from using many techniques. So she tacitly engaged in a head-on collision.

Crackling, clattering.

Fists, palms, legs, kicks, finger and claw transformations, the sweeping of the longsword, the silver hand crossing the air.

In the midst of their wide and forceful exchanges, they traded injuries in the purest sense.

The loss of their respective powers also became faster and faster.

After another headbutt collision, the two staggered apart, blood pouring from their scalps, looking utterly disheveled.

This time, neither was in a hurry to attack again. They stood still, recovering their breath.

For someone with source power as vast as Luo Chen's and mana as sharp as Azure Frost's to need a moment to catch their breath showed how great the consumption of this battle had been.

And just as they were panting, the battle on Soul-Breaking Cliff had clearly reached an extreme.

The grand formation enveloping the eight-hundred-li soul island suddenly began to tremble violently.

Infinite spiritual light expanded and spread in all directions at an unimaginable speed.

The next moment!

"Caw!"

Only a heaven-shaking phoenix cry resounded in all directions.

A fiery red figure leaped out of the grand formation and slapped backward with one hand.

Boom!

The formation, already strained to its limit, collapsed and shattered.

Not only that, but the residual force of the formation's collapse also affected the six people in battle.

Rosycloud was the most severely injured. Her previously invincible might now made her stagger in the air, looking utterly disheveled.

But although she was the most injured, her foundation was deep, and she endured it.

The Five Elders of Heavenly Origin were not so fortunate.

The great power Chunyuan, who had been restraining her, spontaneously combusted without any wind, letting out a miserable scream.

"Junior Sister!"

"Senior Sister!"

Anxious voices came from Mo Shouzhuo and the others, but to no avail.

In an instant, Chunyuan's physical body turned to ashes.

A primordial spirit, identical in size to her original body, fled in panic.

But the golden flames followed like a shadow, giving her no room to escape.

The dignified and noble figure stood in the void, looking down at this scene. Wiping the blood from the corner of her mouth, she revealed a fierce smile.

"Forcing me to self-destruct my spirit treasure, the Myriad Beast Diagram, you can be proud of yourselves."

"Now, just obediently die!"

As she spoke, she raised both hands.

Whoosh!

A golden-red phoenix phantom emerged behind her, towering thousands of feet high, its pressure overwhelming Soul-Breaking Cliff.

In the distance.

Luo Chen's peripheral vision caught this, and his heart shook violently.

He had seen this phantom before, during the battle at Nether Origin.

At that time, he had not understood the significance of the phoenix phantom, thinking it was just some spell or divine ability.

It was not until he personally witnessed Youli Divine Lord's ascension that he understood what it truly meant.

A phantom form!

Also known as a Law Manifestation!

And Rosycloud's Law Manifestation was even more solid than Youli Divine Lord's, almost indistinguishable from a real object.

As soon as the phoenix Law Manifestation appeared!

The colors of heaven and earth changed instantly, and the mountains and seas shook.

The Yellow Spring Sea, already turbulent with monstrous waves, became even more violent.

As if stimulated by something, endless waves surged from afar, rolling back thousands of feet, rushing toward Rosycloud's phoenix Law Manifestation.

Luo Chen and Azure Frost, who were within the Yellow Spring Sea, immediately lost control of their bodies and were swept away by the great waves.

During this time, divine fire erupted, and cyan light swirled, but under wave after wave of giant surges, the two figures appeared so insignificant that they could not struggle at all.

Faced with the counterattack of the Yellow Spring Sea, Rosycloud was surprised and even more enraged.

"Beast, you dare to come against me too!"

The phoenix Law Manifestation screeched as it flew out. As its wings flapped, Nirvana Sacred Flame scattered boundlessly, and its long tail feathers left an empty trail across the sky.

Wherever it passed, the waves of the Yellow Spring Sea were completely evaporated.

The surviving Five Elders of Heavenly Origin below saw that this was an opportunity not to be missed and unleashed their divine abilities and techniques, striking at Rosycloud.

Rosycloud's eyes burst with divine light, and thunderous sounds came from her mouth.

"Ant-like things, you dare to fish in troubled waters!"

She swooped down, facing four opponents without fear.

But the enemies were not only the Five Elders of Heavenly Origin and the waves of the Yellow Spring Sea.

The Soul-Breaking Cliff, which had stood towering and unmoving for millions of years, also began to tremble.

A fleshy doll was thrown out.

Dark light swirled, and the corpse of the Ghost Immortal Tower, which had been washed to the foot of the cliff, was immediately covered.

The fleshy doll, with its dazed eyes, returned to the corpse once more.

In an instant!

Two balls of ghost fire emerged from the hollow eye sockets.

The massive Ghost Immortal Tower swept through the mountains and seas, turning against its former master and charging toward Rosycloud.

Rosycloud, who was in the midst of fierce battle, suddenly turned her head and slapped the Ghost Immortal Tower from across the air, directly blasting a horrifyingly large hole in its abdomen.

"Worthless thing, you also... not you!"

Rosycloud was stunned. Looking at the Ghost Immortal Tower, which showed no sign of stopping its charge, her gaze fell upon the thousand-foot solitary cliff beneath her feet.

In an instant, as intelligent as she was, she understood the plan of the Five Elders of Heavenly Origin.

The true killing move was not the combined attack of the Five Elders of Heavenly Origin, but this legendary, innately ignorant, top-tier great desolate beast of the Mountains and Seas Realm.

Diaohun Desolate!

The so-called Yellow Spring Sea was not a sea at all.

It was the Law Manifestation of this desolate beast, which was why it could temporarily entangle her own Law Manifestation.

Now, it had even separated its consciousness, borrowing the Ghost Immortal Tower's physical body to fight her.

Thus, Rosycloud, already covered in injuries, not only had to face the siege of the Five Elders of Heavenly Origin but also the restraint of the top desolate beast of the Mountains and Seas Realm. Her situation became extremely dangerous.

But who was Rosycloud!

The phoenix was the lord of the feathered race, the emperor of all demons!

On her delicate, peony-like face, a rare hint of fanaticism appeared.

"Come on!"

"Let me see what you can do to me!"

"Even if I exhaust the mountains and seas, I can still be reborn through nirvana. Today, you will all go to hell together!"

Boom!

Boundless golden flames erupted wildly with her at the center.

Wherever they passed, everything became a sea of fire!

Determined to burn the world to ashes, to consume all things.

...

On a continent spewing endless magma, streaks of light flew back and forth.

Every figure, as they looked down at the shattered scene below, had sorrow and anger in their eyes.

But within that sorrow and anger, there was also unconcealable fear.

This landmass was the Central Continent, yet it was not the Central Continent.

Strictly speaking, it was only a southern part of what had once been the Central Continent.

After that world-destroying great battle, the once behemoth-like Central Continent had split into five, dividing into five landmasses of varying sizes.

During that great collapse of the Central Continent, countless aristocratic families and great clans had turned to ashes.

Some famous places, such as the Muting Grasslands, Shangjun, and Lanyan Mountain Range, had all sunk into the endless deep sea.

And all of this was thanks to that woman.

The demon phoenix, Rosycloud!

The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect would never rest until she was dead!

Of course, every Dao Sect cultivator thought this, but those who truly dared to do so were few and far between.

After all, the demon phoenix's strength was not something they could shake now.

Only by cleaning up the mess, cultivating diligently, and reaching the Ascendant realm as soon as possible could they fight her.

"But now that the Central Continent has collapsed and countless spirit veins have been severed, do we still have a chance to cultivate to the Ascendant realm?"

An older cultivator muttered in despair.

A much younger junior beside him had not yet given up hope and encouraged him, "Before the Sect Master and the others left, they issued a recall order. The Dao Sect cultivators scattered across the four continents are gradually returning. By then, we will still have the opportunity to regroup and rise again."

The older man shook his head, his face ashen.

"You don't understand. In the face of absolute individual power, numbers mean nothing."

The younger junior still wanted to argue, but when he looked at the sea in the distance, his expression instantly became terrified.

He pointed a trembling hand into the distance.

"What is that?"

The senior sect member suddenly turned around, and a yellow color was reflected in his pupils.

That boundless Yellow Spring came from the south, heading west, scraping past a corner of what had once been the Central Continent.

The already shattered landmass, under the scouring of this seemingly world-ending tsunami, was as fragile as sand.

The solitary cliff stood tall, like a sharp sword, cutting through the continental plate without any pause.

When this unnamed fragment of the Central Continent sank into the ocean, the black solitary cliff became even darker and purer, with dark light swirling without cease.

Suddenly!

"Caw!"

A phoenix cry resounded through heaven and earth. A golden-red figure was about to rush out of the Yellow Spring Sea.

But the replenished Soul-Breaking Cliff did not want to let her go. Wave after wave, under the empowerment of dark light, turned into chains, dragging her back again.

The Yellow Spring Sea traveled all the way west. Wherever it passed, whether continents or mountains, they all turned to powder.

No living being could escape the sweep of the waves, becoming a part of them.

In the sky, dark clouds gathered, thunder and lightning flashed, and then lasted for months without retreating.

Torrential rain poured down, as if the ancient immortals and gods had sent a great flood, wanting to destroy this world.

After a long time, the golden light within the Yellow Spring Sea became extremely dim, until it completely disappeared.

Only then did this movable yellow ocean stop its rampage, gradually disappearing from the sight of the world.

Even the solitary cliff sank into the depths of the sea.

...

A blazing red-gold sun hung high above, like an inverted furnace. The clouds had long been evaporated by the high temperature into wisps of pale residual floss. The sky's cracks extended to the horizon like a spiderweb.

The rolling hot air distorted everything in the world beyond recognition.

Only the occasional shadow of a vulture passing over the sand gave this world a bit of life.

On a sand dune shaped like a giant Buddha's joined palms, a rumbling sound suddenly came.

The vulture's gaze shifted, and it swooped down.

But before it could get close, a strand of sword qi, emanating from within, shredded it into a mist of blood.

Suddenly, the sand sank, and two figures shot into the sky. In the midst of their mutual strikes, they suddenly separated.

"Stop!" A hoarse male voice sounded, no longer having its usual mellow tone.

Not far away, another disheveled woman in green had a rare fierce expression on her face.

"It can't stop!"

She formed a hand seal with one hand, placing it before her pale, thin lips, and spoke a clear word.

"Appear!"

The next moment, a fierce wind came from within the rolling heat.

A massive green bird fluttered its wings, slowly rising from the horizon. Even the blazing sun could not block the dazzling cyan light.

The man sighed, but there was no trace of retreat in his eyes.

"The Yellow Spring Sea restricted you, but it also restricted me, didn't it?"

He slapped the ground with one hand, and the dense fire-attribute spiritual qi around him converged toward him.

As he formed hand seals and chanted, a fire phoenix soared out.

Not only that, but the man opened his mouth and spat out a wisp of golden flame, which wrapped around the fire phoenix.

With the addition of the golden flame, the fire phoenix became even more majestic, as if it were no different from a real phoenix.

Seeing this, the massive green bird became even more furious.

"Using the Nirvana Sacred Flame that Sister taught you to deal with me, Luo Chen, you truly deserve to die!"

(End of this chapter)
