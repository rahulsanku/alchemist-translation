# Chapter 1003: Shut the Door and Beat the Ghosts, Break Through the Nether Abyss

Someone had made the first move!

The fierce battle came to an abrupt halt.

"The Heavenly Nether Palace has opened!"

"Ghost King Wangliang wants to get there first!"

"Go in!"

What had everyone gathered here for?

Was it truly to fight Luo Chen to the death?

The answer was clearly no!

In an instant, everyone made their decision.

Seizing the moment the Heavenly Nether Palace opened, figures whistled and rushed inside, with the Mother-Child Ghost Fiend and the other great ghost kings taking the lead.

Luo Chen, who had fallen behind, was instead left completely ignored.

This startling turn of events was almost too fast to follow.

Luo Chen was stunned for a moment, then let out a light laugh.

"Arriving early is not as good as arriving at the right time!"

He glanced at the Luo Tian Sect members who wanted to follow him, then waved his hand.

"You don't need to go in. Just wait outside."

Having said that, he strode freely into the palace.

The Luo Tian Sect members were worried. Gu Caiyi, who had been silent all along, blurted out, "But..."

Deng Taiyue stepped in front of everyone, cutting off Gu Caiyi's words.

"Brother Luo's abilities have already surpassed our imagination. Going in with him would only make us a burden."

As he said this, his eyes clearly held a deep look of fear.

Everyone recalled the brief battle earlier. Luo Chen, surrounded by a group of ghost kings, had still remained completely composed. This showed how powerful he had become.

Considering that Luo Chen's methods often had a wide area of effect, if they really went in, they would indeed become a burden.

Deng Taiyue was right.

But this battle concerned the entire sect. Was it truly good to let the Supreme Elder handle it alone?

Wang Yuan said in a deep voice, "All we can do is watch for any gaps. If anyone snatches the Netherwater Spring Eye and tries to escape, we kill them!"

Deng Taiyue looked at him, then slowly nodded.

...

Inside the Heavenly Nether Palace.

A dark pool, no more than a zhang or so in circumference, sat in the very center of the great hall.

At this moment, the dark pool was bubbling violently, as if boiling.

But the chilling cold that spread outward dyed the surroundings in a layer of white frost.

A figure arrived in an instant!

After entering the palace, without any hesitation, he plunged directly into the dark pool.

Then, one whistling figure after another followed closely behind.

The ghost kings transformed their forms, all staring fixedly at the boiling dark pool.

"The Netherwater Spring Eye is inside!"

"Ghost King Wangliang actually dared to rush straight in. Does he not want to live?"

"No, that's not it. Ghost King Wangliang's body is condensed from Netherwater True Essence, of the same origin as the Netherwater Spring Eye. He wants to forcibly refine it!"

Amidst the exclamations, someone couldn't hold back and also rushed straight into the dark pool.

But in less than a breath, a miserable scream came from within.

"Ah!"

A wisp of green smoke rose slowly.

This signified that a powerful ghost king, rarely seen in the outside world, had fallen right there.

Everyone was greatly shocked. They all retreated, instinctively moving away from the dark pool.

At the same time!

A piercing sound came from behind.

The sharp-eyed Mother-Child Ghost Fiend suddenly turned her head, just in time to see a Daoist in feathered robes and a high crown slowly closing the great hall's doors.

This sight made her pupils contract sharply.

The restrictions on the Heavenly Nether Palace's main gate had taken them several years of combined effort to break open a crack just wide enough for cultivators to pass through.

Opening it was difficult; closing it was even harder.

Yet Luo Chen had actually used his own strength to forcibly shut the great doors.

And the signal this action sent was enough to make anyone's heart tremble with fear.

"Luo Chen, what are you trying to do?!"

As she shouted, everyone else finally realized the dangerous signal that Luo Chen's action carried.

"Hoo..."

Luo Chen exhaled a mouthful of turbid air and subconsciously shook his hands.

This Heavenly Nether Palace gate might not look like much, but closing it was quite exhausting.

Facing the stares of the ghost kings, Luo Chen smiled slightly and inserted the Soul-Refining Banner beside him.

"Shut the door and beat the ghosts. Isn't that obvious enough?"

Everyone was stunned, and then they saw that soul banner expand at an incredible speed, in an instant reaching the palace ceiling. The banner fluttered without wind, and countless faces, some ferocious, some resentful, emerged upon it.

Not only that, Luo Chen opened his mouth and spat out a small grey cauldron that spun and flew out.

*Clang!*

The small cauldron crashed to the ground, and the extremely hard floor actually sank three inches.

One had to know, this was the palace of an Ascendant-level great power!

The fact that this small cauldron could create such a dent showed its extraordinary nature.

Most importantly, it was Luo Chen's words of total annihilation.

Earlier, outside, they had briefly exchanged blows, and everyone already understood that Luo Chen was no easy opponent.

Now, locking the doors made his intent of fighting to the death even clearer.

Huan Youji asked in confusion, "The Netherwater Spring Eye is the most important thing. Sage Luo, why must you do this? Do you truly want to let that Ghost King Wangliang profit from the conflict?"

Hearing this, Luo Chen smiled slightly.

"Simple. Kill all of you, then take the spring eye."

"Arrogant!"

"Courting death!"

"The treasure is right in front of us. There's no need to talk about righteousness with him. Everyone, attack together!"

Amidst shock and anger, someone could no longer hold back and made a move.

Facing a rusted iron sword that flew towards him at great speed, Luo Chen's eyes lit up.

"Good timing!"

He casually pinched it, and the extremely fast iron sword landed between his fingers. With a slight exertion of force, it shattered into pieces of iron.

Then, he tossed them back, eliciting a series of miserable screams.

Luo Chen said no more. Taking advantage of the momentum, he pressed the attack, using all kinds of spells at his fingertips.

A blazing sun rose into the air!

Sparks rained down like fire!

A fire phoenix soared through the hall, and a flame dragon roared within it.

In a short time, he unleashed numerous fire-type spells as if his mana were limitless.

And every single spell contained the power of the Fire Origin, carrying the aura of the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame.

A slight touch meant either an immediate explosion of the ghostly body, or wisps of green smoke rising.

What should have been a scene of a group of ghost kings besieging Luo Chen had, after the battle truly began, become Luo Chen besieging them single-handedly.

This sight truly stunned the four great ghost kings.

The Mother-Child Ghost Fiend, shocked and furious, yet with an irrepressible fear, let out a voice that was both old and young at the same time.

"Kill him first!"

Before the words had even faded, she had already whistled forward and charged.

Huan Youji followed without hesitation, pointing a finger at Luo Chen from afar.

The Illusory Nether Finger—anyone struck by it would sink into an endless illusionary realm.

The other two great ghost kings no longer hid themselves, each unleashing their own techniques to begin encircling Luo Chen.

Facing all of this, Luo Chen remained completely unafraid.

He and Azure Frost were now on par in terms of strength, but their combat styles were very different.

Azure Frost excelled in elusive movement techniques and had terrifying single-target, point-to-point killing power that was hard for those of the same rank to withstand.

His own combat style had been taking shape since the Initiate stage.

Vast mana, profound soul foundation, and large-scale lethal spells.

Against a powerhouse of the same level like Azure Frost, he might have to fight a tough battle.

But against these opponents—whether in realm, strength, or even their most praiseworthy soul foundation, all of which were inferior to his—it was almost a complete crushing victory.

Especially fire techniques, which, aside from lightning techniques, were the most effective at restraining yin-attributed ghostly beings.

Numbers were simply not the key!

No matter how many came, he would kill them all!

Now, without having to worry about the Luo Tian Sect members, Luo Chen fought with full force, and the results he achieved were truly shocking.

*Boom!*

*Boom!*

*Boom!*

The violent booming sounds passed through the thick palace walls and echoed far from Mount Yanfu.

The terrifying tremors could be felt even by the Luo Tian Sect members on the square outside, and their expressions all changed.

Time passed, second by second.

Every second, with the commotion coming from inside the Heavenly Nether Palace, felt like a year.

Every moment made people's hearts pound with anxiety.

What they didn't know was what kind of one-sided massacre was truly happening on the battlefield inside.

"Even if an Ascendant personally made a move, it probably wouldn't be more than this, right?"

After another failed attack, Huan Youji thought this with a trembling heart.

At this moment, inside the hall, very few fourth-rank ghost kings were still alive.

The ghostly qi released from the bodies of those who had been blown apart filled the entire hall.

Countless unattached ghost souls launched suicidal attacks at Luo Chen, but they couldn't even get close.

She took a deep look at Luo Chen, then reluctantly glanced at the increasingly boiling dark pool, before flying without hesitation towards the palace gate.

Neither the Netherwater Spring Eye nor Luo Chen were things she could covet anymore.

But she wanted to leave, and Luo Chen wouldn't allow it.

"Where do you think you're going!"

*Whoosh!*

A chain suddenly shot out from the void, directly piercing through Huan Youji's layers of defense.

Huan Youji's expression changed drastically. She subconsciously tried to use the formless dispersion technique that all powerful ghost cultivators knew.

But for some reason, a great binding force spread through her entire body, making it impossible for her to scatter and escape.

"It's that great cauldron!" Huan Youji's soul nearly fled in terror, staring at that grey treasure cauldron.

At the same time, Luo Chen let out a low shout, and four extra arms suddenly sprouted from his body.

Six arms emerged together, each forming a different hand seal.

*Clatter!*

More and more crimson chains flew out from the Primordial Unity Cauldron, stabbing towards the three other figures emitting powerful auras in the hall.

The Mother-Child Ghost Fiend tried to resist, but there were too many chains, and a suppressing force vaguely limited her range of movement.

*Chi!*

A chain pierced through her body, pinning her firmly to a pillar.

On the other side.

A giant, heaven-shaking palm descended from the clouds of ghostly qi, slapping down a great ghost king.

"Roar!"

That great ghost king rolled on the ground and transformed into a demon beast that looked like an ox but also like a deer, roaring in fury.

This was an ancient demon beast that had turned into a ghost cultivator after death.

Revealing its true form was its strongest state.

But what awaited it was a ghost claw that seized its body.

*Chi! Chi! Chi! Chi!*

Four crimson chains pierced through its body, nailing its four limbs firmly.

On the other side, the last hidden great ghost king, seeing this scene, was already scared out of its wits.

It transformed into a black three-legged crow, trying to fly out of the Heavenly Nether Palace.

But Luo Chen had a clear view of the entire situation and wouldn't give it a chance. He simply cast a profound glance at it.

As their eyes met, Mirror Flower Water Moon unfolded.

Though it was only for an instant, the black crow fell from the sky.

*Chi! Chi! Chi!*

Three chains shot through the air, piercing its body while also binding its three legs.

In the blink of an eye, the four great ghost kings had all been defeated by Luo Chen!

They struggled fiercely, trying to break free from the binding and suppressing power of the chains, but no matter how they struggled, they couldn't escape.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron was once a vessel that had nearly suppressed the Azure Luan's true body!

Just them?

"Weaklings like you should have died a hundred or a thousand years ago. Surviving until now, you might as well become the enslaved ghosts in my sect's banner!"

Above the hall, beneath the dome, Luo Chen stood firm in the void.

His middle pair of hands formed hand seals, and the Soul-Refining Banner fluttered with a *clatter*, transforming into a vast absorbing force that pulled in the massive amounts of ghostly qi released after the ghost kings' deaths.

Even some remnant souls, after being swept by Luo Chen's gaze, unknowingly entered the Soul-Refining Banner.

His other four hands each formed different seals, controlling the Primordial Unity Cauldron to restrain the four great ghost kings.

"You all go in too!"

Luo Chen shouted sternly, his mana surging. The nine chains immediately trembled in unison, pulling the four great ghost kings towards the Soul-Refining Banner.

"Don't even think about it!"

"Impossible!"

"Never!"

"Break free for me!"

In the hall, the four great ghost kings spoke at the same time, simultaneously gathering their power to break free from the Primordial Unity Cauldron's suppression.

Huan Youji controlled her magic treasure, the red paper umbrella, and self-detonated it on the spot, turning it into a storm of spiritual qi that swept towards Luo Chen.

The Mother-Child Ghost Fiend let out two voices in succession—one an old woman's hoarse cry, the other a child's shrill scream—launching a fierce attack on Luo Chen's soul.

That ox-deer ghost king even forcibly stood up, dragging the four chains as it moved back step by step, away from the Soul-Refining Banner.

The three-legged black crow emitted wisps of black flame from its body, burning the crimson chains until they swayed precariously.

Facing all of this, Luo Chen's expression still didn't change.

The spiritual qi storm struck him, but it was just a gentle breeze against his face.

The dual demonic sounds drilled into his ears, but he stood as firm as a mountain.

The two demon ghosts were merely putting up a desperate struggle.

Luo Chen's one hand suddenly stretched out, grabbing the four chains, and engaged in a tug-of-war with the ox-deer, pulling it back step by step.

At the same time, he opened his mouth and spat out a wisp of golden flame. It spread rapidly along the crimson chains, extinguishing the black flames on the three-legged black crow in an instant.

With this continuous display of power, the four great ghost kings could do nothing against Luo Chen.

The situation of being pulled into the Soul-Refining Banner seemed irreversible.

Just then!

Luo Chen's eyes focused, his gaze landing on the dark pool.

The boiling pool water suddenly stopped.

A sinister ghost claw reached out from within, pressing down on the hall's floor.

"It's not your turn to come out yet!" A fierce light flashed in Luo Chen's eyes, and he struck a palm from afar towards the center of the dark pool.

The pitch-black ghost claw seemed to sense this and also pushed out a palm horizontally.

The two clashed. The opponent seemed not to have expected Luo Chen's strength to be so great, and was forcibly slapped back.

But soon, hands reached out from inside again.

And this time, it was two hands!

"Could Ghost King Wangliang have already refined the Netherwater Spring Eye so quickly?" Luo Chen frowned, finding it hard to believe.

How could such a heavy treasure be refined so quickly!

The reason he had shut the door to beat the ghosts, aside from absolute confidence, was for several other purposes.

One was to resolve the situation of powerful ghost cultivators being everywhere in the Nether Abyss, making it easier for Luo Tian Sect disciples to come and go more freely in the future.

The second was to imitate Rosycloud!

If that woman could command the nine great ghost emperors to serve her, then why couldn't he enslave these fourth-rank ghost kings?

He had the Primordial Unity Cauldron to suppress everything, a suitable soul-path magic treasure in the Soul-Refining Banner, and a bottomless soul foundation—enough to accomplish all of this.

But all these purposes were insignificant compared to the Netherwater Spring Eye.

Controlling the Nether Abyss and recreating the Nether Origin Sect's blessed land in the future could serve as both a foundation for the Luo Tian Sect's prosperity and a preparation for his own breakthrough to Ascendant.

This was the most important thing!

Suddenly, a tremendous force came, shaking the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

It was the four great ghost kings, taking the opportunity to try and break free.

Luo Chen took a deep breath and shifted his gaze.

"You truly are stubborn and unrepentant!"

The next moment, the Nirvana Sacred Flame that had appeared earlier suddenly blazed up, rolling forward along the chains and enveloping the four great ghost kings.

The current Nirvana Sacred Flame could not yet burn souls, but it could burn away mana and physical bodies.

Even if he couldn't have the complete four great ghost kings, he had to clear the battlefield to deal with the Ghost King Wangliang who would inevitably appear.

And indeed!

After the Nirvana Sacred Flame appeared, the ghostly bodies of the four great ghost kings began to visibly melt.

Their resistance instantly weakened.

Luo Chen didn't hesitate. His mana surged wildly, and his four arms shot out simultaneously, like pulling in a tug-of-war, directly dragging the four great ghost kings into the Soul-Refining Banner.

*Swoosh!*

Luo Chen teleported in front of the Soul-Refining Banner, formed a hand seal, and the soul banner shrank in an instant.

Roars came from within, and countless ghost faces emerged on its surface, trying to break free.

Since they were already inside, how could Luo Chen let them escape?

His ten fingers tapped rapidly, unleashing one restriction after another. The Myriad Formation Gu he had obtained earlier was also playing its role now.

In just a few breaths, the commotion from the Soul-Refining Banner grew smaller and smaller.

Until it was completely sealed!

Later, he only needed to cooperate with the Soul-Nourishing Banner, refining them bit by bit, to enslave half of the fourth-rank ghost kings of the Nether Abyss.

But Luo Chen had no time to rejoice, because the true enemy had appeared.

*Boom!*

A jet-black water column shot into the sky, breaking through the dome of the Heavenly Nether Palace with unstoppable force.

Its residual power was unceasing, charging straight up.

It pierced through the Nether Abyss, reaching the sky.

In an instant, the vast origin qi of heaven and earth poured down like a torrent.

(End of this chapter)
