# Chapter 961: Nether Mountain, Yin-Yang Grave

Liancheng Ancestor!

One of the five Ascendants still alive in the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

He came from the Lian family of the Twelve Great Houses, having attained the Dao most recently and being the youngest among them.

Compared to this Youli Ancestor of the Southern Frontier, who was said to have already lived past the Ascendant lifespan limit, he could be considered a junior.

Thus, his words were quite courteous, addressing him as Divine Lord Youli.

But though his words were polite, his hands were not at all lenient.

With a single divine ability unleashed, the Gate of Life opened wide.

That gate which had protected the Life and Death Gate for thousands of years was suddenly thrown open.

Across the mountains and wilds, one formation after another reacted in a chain, lights flickering one after another as they tried to close the Gate of Life.

Yet for some unknown reason, the meaning of endless flowing and unceasing circulation always failed to reach completion at the crucial nodes, keeping the gate in a constant state of openness.

Seeing this, Liancheng Ancestor increased his divine power.

*Boom!*

Thousands of formations shattered one after another with crackling sounds.

Liancheng Ancestor was overjoyed, but what greeted him was a voice full of vicissitude.

"Finally returned!"

Within this voice, there was excitement, relief, and also a hint of liberation.

Liancheng Ancestor did not understand, but he had no time to ponder its meaning. He immediately issued an order.

"Kill!"

The next moment, the million-strong army of cultivators gathered at Unicorn Mountain surged like a flood toward the Southern Frontier's sacred ground, the Life and Death Gate.

And the cultivators of the Life and Death Gate, who had been recuperating and building their strength within for many years, launched their counterattack without hesitation.

...

Within the Yin-Yang Realm.

The nearly thousand-zhang giant still stood tall, his head touching the sky and his feet planted on the ground.

Roars like those of ancient desolate beasts continuously issued from his mouth, shaking everyone's minds and hearts.

Boundless, incomparable force centered on his hands, pressing against the hard wall before him.

That bronze gate, sealed for many years and seemingly indestructible, let out a piercing sound under his push, a crack slowly appearing.

"Open!"

It was as if the giant was splitting heaven and earth from chaos.

With a furious roar, Luo Chen concentrated all his strength into a single point and suddenly erupted.

*Boom!*

Li Tianshi and the others felt their heads buzzing, their minds unable to control themselves.

When they came back to their senses, they were shocked to find that the gate before them had been completely opened.

A red moon hung high within.

A sense of gloom and terror arose spontaneously, chilling them to the bone.

Looking at Luo Chen, that thousand-zhang giant form rapidly shrank.

When it returned to a normal human state, he was completely exhausted of qi and strength, unable to even stand steadily.

One could imagine how much consumption pushing open that gate with pure physical strength had cost him.

Luo Chen stumbled and stood before the gate.

"Everyone, I have not failed your trust!"

Li Tianshi let out a relieved smile. "I knew I hadn't misjudged Fellow Daoist Qi. Such body refinement skill—I'm afraid no one in the current world can match it."

Luo Chen, his face pale, shook his head. "This matter is concluded. Qi should also take his leave. Everyone, please do as you wish!"

Li Tianshi was surprised. "Do you truly not wish to glimpse the scenery of a great power's final resting place?"

Luo Chen waved his hand weakly. "In Qi's current state, I'm afraid I cannot get past the countless dangers within. Enough, enough. Leaving this place, Qi should continue his travels through the Mountains and Seas Realm, to see the chaos of the Northern Sea and hear the Buddhist hymns of the Western Desert."

This tyrant cultivator Qi Tian was not only leaving the Yin-Yang Realm but even planning to leave the Southern Frontier?

Li Tianshi's smile gradually faded. He suddenly asked, "Fellow Daoist Qi, why have you been painstakingly seeking the Devil Heart Gu Emperor?"

Luo Chen's gaze sharpened.

At that moment, an attack without form or substance struck from behind.

Sensing it, Luo Chen turned and slapped back.

*Bang!*

Under the exchange, Luo Chen was actually at a disadvantage, a palm print with all five fingers clearly visible appearing on his back.

His expression changed drastically. Borrowing the force, he withdrew and leaped into the Yin realm behind the Death Gate.

Li Tianshi raised his right hand, looking at the strange cyan color spreading on it with some surprise.

"After exhausting his strength to open the Death Gate, he still had the power to counter an attack?"

The others did not understand. What had just happened was simply too fast.

Hearing Li Tianshi's muttered words, they only then grasped roughly what had occurred.

Meng Shentong instinctively distanced himself and demanded, "Li Tianshi, what are you doing!"

Bai Xuanjun stood shoulder to shoulder with him, his gaze cold. "There's no need to kill the donkey so quickly after grinding it!"

Even Redleaf Matron, Purple Robed Marquis, and Hidden Sword Pavilion Master were taken aback.

In response, Li Tianshi waved his hand.

As he waved, that strange cyan color on his right hand was stripped away to the ground.

It hit the ground, making a sizzling sound.

A plume of green smoke rose, showing how potent the poison was!

He smiled slightly and explained, "You may not know this, but this tyrant cultivator Qi Tian has another origin. He is not a native cultivator of our Southern Frontier, and his intentions are suspect."

"Hmm?" Meng Shentong looked unconvinced.

Li Tianshi explained carefully, "Strong ones in this world always have roots. Though the Mountains and Seas Realm is vast, to us Sages, it is not infinite. Yet someone as top-tier as Qi Tian has no related growth traces. Are you not curious about his origins?"

The crowd was stunned.

While they were puzzled, Li Tianshi spoke a name.

"Eastern Wilderness Pill Sect, Luo Chen!"

Everyone was shocked, especially Purple Robed Marquis, who looked utterly incredulous.

"According to rumors, that Pill Sect of the Eastern Wilderness is the most supreme of qi refiners, with unparalleled Dao techniques. He shares not a single similarity with this Qi Tian. How could he possibly be him?"

"Ha, Purple Robed Marquis, are you doubting my intelligence gathering abilities?" Li Tianshi countered.

Purple Robed Marquis was momentarily speechless. If there was one Sage in the Eastern Wilderness with the widest intelligence network, Li Tianshi was undoubtedly the first choice.

Not only did he rely on the intelligence channels of the Fierce Corpse Stiff Alliance, but as a gu cultivator, he had also built up a vast network of connections. It was even said that among the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect cultivators who had entered the Southern Frontier, there were those who were on good terms with him.

If he wanted to investigate the origins of a strange cultivator, there would be no secrets left.

So, was the tyrant cultivator Qi Tian truly the Pill Sect Luo Chen?

Everyone's minds were still reeling.

The two had nothing in common—neither in style of conduct, nor cultivation system, nor combat methods. How could they be linked?

Meng Shentong had no time to dwell on it and pressed, "Even if he is the Pill Sect, so what? He spent great effort to open the Death Gate for you. Why turn against him and kill the donkey after grinding it?"

"I did not want to act. If he had been willing to accompany us through this final stretch, we could have parted on good terms. But he insisted on leaving early. If he goes outside and leaks information about the Yin-Yang Realm, what then?" Li Tianshi countered.

Without even waiting for an answer, he gave his own.

"Once the news leaks, countless cultivators will flock to this great power's fallen land, stirring up waves again. Even the sacred ground would not sit idly by, after all, this is their own ancestor's tomb."

"To prevent any mishap, I was forced to take this measure."

"Come, time waits for no one. We should enter."

Having said that, he offered no further explanation and took the lead into the Death Gate.

The Yin realm!

The red moon hung high, desolate and lonely.

A beast's roar seemed to come from afar.

But the scene before them showed no trace of Luo Chen.

Li Tianshi pursed his lips and murmured to himself, "Such good nourishment. You won't get away."

...

Within the Yin realm, outside a mountain hollow.

Numerous rotting beasts gathered, and skeletons and zombies converged from all directions.

But these beings, who knew no danger, seemed to lose their way once they reached the hollow, circling aimlessly outside.

Inside the hollow, Luo Chen sat within his formation.

The palm print on his back gradually smoothed under the flow of his source power.

After a day and a night, Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes, a relaxed expression on his face.

"Who would have thought Li Tianshi's timing was so precise!"

He touched his chest, as if he could still feel the terrifying force of that palm piercing through his protective qi armor and shaking his five viscera.

Gu art? Spell art? Or perhaps a body art similar to Luo Chen's?

Luo Chen could not distinguish and felt there was a mix of each.

This also let him know that Li Tianshi's hidden strength was only the tip of the iceberg, far from that of an ordinary gu cultivator.

But in this way, Luo Chen's list of suspects instantly narrowed down to only him.

"Li Tianshi is the Devil Heart Gu Emperor!"

The reason didn't matter; evidence didn't exist. It was a gut instinct.

From the moment the other party had given various intelligence, trying to mislead him into suspecting others, yet secretly setting up the chain poison of the Mother-Child Trigger Gu, Luo Chen had harbored a measure of vigilance against him.

But he still hadn't expected the other party's timing to be so perfect.

It wasn't that Luo Chen was unprepared; it was simply that pushing open the Death Gate had cost him too much.

After a day and a night of rest, relying on his powerful recovery ability, Luo Chen's condition had recovered to about seventy or eighty percent.

Time to move!

He withdrew the formation, letting the skeletons, zombies, and even rotting beasts charge into the hollow.

Watching all this, Luo Chen fell into thought.

"Li Tianshi was able to shake off the control of the previous Devil Heart Gu Emperor back then, even overcoming his superior. Perhaps it was related to his gains in this Yin-Yang Remnant Realm!"

As he pondered, sword qi burst from his fingertip.

Everything before him was reduced to fragments.

A deafening beast roar came from afar. A powerful ghostly demon was charging this way.

Luo Chen's mind moved. He restrained his aura completely and silently vanished under the blood moon.

Moments later, a ghostly demon three to four hundred zhang tall, its flesh and blood rotted away, leaving only a skeleton frame, burst into the hollow but found nothing.

...

On the vast Yin realm earth, several figures moved forward swiftly.

Li Tianshi was in the middle, explaining the situation here to the others as he flew.

"The great power Yin-Yangzi was unwilling to die of old age together with the desolate beast he had planted the Life-Death Symbiosis Gu on. Thus, before his death, he became extremely mad. He exhausted the sacred ground's power, capturing a large number of demon beasts to experiment with various gu insects."

"Later, he even went so far as to use the sect's disciples as experimental subjects."

"Divine Lord Youli could not bear to see the sect harmed by him and forced him away. He came to this Golden Lion Empire and established the Yin realm."

"The countless fierce ghosts and demon beasts we now see are all products of Yin-Yangzi's research."

"We must be extremely careful. Among these beings, there are not a few top-tier ones comparable to us. Even if they lack intelligence, they are not easy to deal with."

These words drew no rebuttal.

Because during this day and night of travel, they had already encountered some troublesome enemies.

Apart from Li Tianshi and Redleaf Matron, the others had taken turns attacking and eliminating enemies.

But the numbers were truly too great. After a series of battles, each had consumed some of their magical power.

From this point, one could see how mad Yin-Yangzi had been before his death, to make even such a luxurious exploration team feel the strain.

The mess he left behind also caused the Life and Death Gate endless trouble.

For this reason, the Life and Death Gate had to establish the Yang realm above the Yin realm and even create a vast empire to suppress the Yin-Yang Realm.

Even those dragon-lion statues outside were specially arranged to prevent the Yin realm's ghostly demons from escaping.

"Another troublesome one is coming!" Li Tianshi raised an eyebrow.

The others behind him remained silent, all looking at Meng Shentong.

Without a word, he exchanged a glance with Bai Xuanjun.

The latter voluntarily left the group to meet the approaching enemy.

It was a ghostly demon like a mountain-sized giant ape, its body huge and sturdy, but with little flesh left, only white bones.

Bai Xuanjun soared into the sky, and bone spikes shot out from his body.

Each bone spike struck the mountain giant ape with a deafening boom.

Li Tianshi exclaimed in surprise, "The Whitebone Sect's bone spike gu has such power?"

Meng Shentong laughed. "That's because Bai Xuanjun is skilled. Otherwise, ordinary bone spike gu wouldn't have such killing power."

The killing power of the bone spikes was indeed astonishing.

From the moment Bai Xuanjun acted, in just a short while, the mountain giant ape was dismantled into pieces.

Soon, Bai Xuanjun caught up.

"Solved."

He said it calmly, but the magical power fluctuations still lingering on his body showed how much effort he had put into the seemingly simple battle.

Li Tianshi praised him, then said with a serious expression, "What we encountered on the road are still just appetizers. The truly troublesome ones are at the Yin-Yang Grave!"

The crowd was puzzled.

But three days later, looking at the mountain piled high with bones and the endless sea of ghostly demons, everyone drew in a cold breath.

What exactly had Yin-Yangzi done before his death?

Giving them no time to digest their emotions, Li Tianshi immediately issued an order.

"Everyone, attack together! Protect me as I enter Nether Mountain. The Yin-Yang Grave is inside the mountain!"

Everyone agreed in unison and displayed their various means.

Sword leaves flew everywhere, purple light surged, and a hundred-zhang-tall bone giant crawled out of the earth, clearing obstacles.

Redleaf Matron charged at the front, holding a compass, unraveling one decaying formation after another.

Meng Shentong followed behind Li Tianshi, dealing with the ghostly demons that approached.

Their magical power was consumed at an unimaginable speed.

And the figures of the six gradually disappeared into the depths of Nether Mountain.

Amidst the endless wailing of ghosts, a figure slowly emerged outside the mountain.

"Is this the great power's final resting place?"

Luo Chen narrowed his eyes. He captured a wisp of ghostly demon aura and attached it to himself.

Disguising himself with these ghostly demon auras was the reason he had been able to travel unimpeded all the way.

But whether to enter Nether Mountain next made him hesitate.

Only because this mountain emitted a palpitation-inducing aura.

It was as if an ancient giant beast was sleeping inside, ready to wake at any moment and devour everything.

Yet this hesitation lasted only a moment before Luo Chen charged in without looking back.

"Li Tianshi, don't you die. You must wait for me!"

(End of chapter)
