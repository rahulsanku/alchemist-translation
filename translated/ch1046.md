# Chapter 1046: Nether Water True Essence, Black Dragon Appears

"Senior Sister, I'll trouble you with this."

Within the Dawn-Dusk Palace, Zhou Fuyou solemnly handed a letter to Shi Miaoyin.

Shi Miaoyin accepted it. "No trouble at all. A small matter."

For her, this truly was not a troublesome affair.

Zhou Fuyou's request was merely that the senior sister deliver a letter to the Zhou clan, bring his younger sister Zhou Qingyu to the Tiannan Dao Palace to audit the lectures, and provide her with some daily care.

Yes, the master had already approved Zhou Fuyou's request, allowing his sister to join the Houtu Hall.

From now on, the siblings could live together again.

Shi Miaoyin paid little mind to such trivial matters. She sighed and said, "When the master asked for your opinion earlier, you could have used the excuse of unstable cultivation after forming your core to decline going to the East Sea coast. Why did you agree so readily?"

Zhou Fuyou replied calmly, "Since the master asked me, it meant he had that intention. How could I go against it? Besides, is my cultivation truly unstable?"

Shi Miaoyin fell silent.

An extraordinary fact had been presented before everyone in Houtu Hall.

That was—Zhou Fuyou, who had formed his core at forty, showed not the slightest sign of unstable cultivation. Instead, his dharma power was exceptionally pure and profound, vast as an ocean, as if he had spent over a decade in secluded meditation polishing it.

This was simply unbelievable.

"Actually, I roughly understand the master's thoughts."

"My cultivation speed is too fast, yet my realm is abnormally stable. On the surface, everything seems fine, but there is one weakness."

"I am too young, with too little experience, leaving my heart-mind lacking."

"The master fears that if I continue charging forward like this, I might suddenly fall into qi deviation one day. If that happens, it would be difficult to reverse. To prevent such a possibility, the master wants me to gain more experience. Whether it's broadening my horizons, tempering my heart-mind, or accumulating combat experience, it will all benefit me greatly in the future."

Zhou Fuyou spoke freely, his face showing no dissatisfaction toward his master Qinglan.

Seeing this, Shi Miaoyin no longer tried to dissuade him.

"I'll ask your second senior brother and the other senior brothers and sisters to look after you. You must also be careful yourself."

"Mm, thank you, senior sister."

After Shi Miaoyin left, Zhou Fuyou stood at the entrance of the Dawn-Dusk Palace, his expression somewhat dazed.

The next time he returned, he might be cultivating in a different place.

That place would still be called Dawn-Dusk Palace, following his preference, but whether in terms of spirit vein grade or attending servants, it would far surpass this one. What Zhou Fuyou thought about was that he hoped to see his little sister when he returned.

"I wonder how long this journey will take. I should make some preparations."

Zhou Fuyou muttered to himself and returned to the cultivation chamber in his bedchamber.

He first performed an internal examination, checking his condition.

Just as before!

His qi sea was deep and vast, his golden core floating and sinking.

Especially that pure black golden core, containing not a trace of impurity, crystal clear and profound like an ox's eye.

The pure dharma power contained within it made even Zhou Fuyou click his tongue in amazement.

Back in the mid-Initiate stage, his spiritual power was already comparable to early Adept cultivators. By the time he reached the great perfection Initiate stage, he felt that even ordinary mid-Adept cultivators were nothing special.

Now that he had formed his core, the dharma power derived from it gave Zhou Fuyou an extreme sense of expansion.

Under this extremely inflated powerful force, he felt as if he could do anything!

His divine sense circled around the golden core, and Zhou Fuyou's mind became somewhat entranced. He even wanted to sink into the abyss-like qi sea along with this golden core.

"Ha!"

With a low shout, Zhou Fuyou suddenly woke up.

A look of lingering fear appeared on his face.

His power had increased too quickly. If he didn't restrain himself, he would likely lose himself in it, becoming a beast that only pursued strength.

"We cultivators seek immortality, not power. Power is merely a means to protect the Dao on the path to immortality. The master sent me down the mountain to gain experience, wanting me to control this surging power. Truly, his intentions were profound."

Sighing with emotion, Zhou Fuyou paused his cultivation.

Instead, he turned his attention to external things.

Because his realm had advanced extremely quickly and his dharma power was abundant, his combat style had always been one of overwhelming suppression and direct crushing.

And because of his astonishing talent in spell arts, he had mastered a large number of water-type spells.

But what Zhou Fuyou truly regarded as his trump card was the ancestral technique, *Qi Sea Floating Heaven*.

This spell art was not only scalable but also possessed strong inclusiveness, just like water—embracing all things without contention!

Initially, Zhou Fuyou had refined a large amount of miasma from the White Egret Marsh, giving *Qi Sea Floating Heaven* a strong toxicity that could threaten early Adept cultivators.

Later, through continuous experimentation, he had integrated some low-level spells into it, such as Water Arrow Art, Mist Concealment Art, Water Concealment Art, Wave-Treading Step, Glazed Heavenly Canopy, and so on.

During the half-year journey spent with the ancestor, who was the creator of this spell art, the ancestor had given Zhou Fuyou many suggestions, helping him perfect it further.

By now, Zhou Fuyou could even integrate some third-order water-type spells into it.

For example, *Mist-Shrouded Jade Pavilion* and *Spirit-Communicating Form-Retaining*.

As long as he unfolded *Qi Sea Floating Heaven*, Zhou Fuyou could rapidly cast these water-type spells without restraint. Not only would they not conflict with each other, but their power would also multiply.

This also conformed to the concept of "embracing all things without contention."

Thus, with just this one spell art, he had gathered many advantages: concealment and escape, defense and protection, suppression and sealing, combat and attack.

Of course, there were also drawbacks.

That was—the required dharma power was truly vast!

However, this drawback was almost nonexistent for Zhou Fuyou. In his few battles in life, he had never experienced exhaustion of power, and he couldn't imagine a situation where his dharma power would be depleted.

Now, after some sorting, Zhou Fuyou took out a jade pot flowing with treasure light.

It was the most common type of Wave-Swallowing Sea-Containing Pot found in the cultivation world, specifically used to store liquid materials.

But compared to the common goods outside, the material of this Wave-Swallowing Sea-Containing Pot bestowed by his master was far superior. And what was stored inside was something many people dreamed of possessing.

Pinching a spell seal, Zhou Fuyou casually guided it, and immediately a black stream as fine as mercury flowed out.

"Nether Water!"

"And not just any Nether Water—this is the highest grade, Nether Water True Essence!"

"Legend has it that for the Eastern Wilderness sacred land, the Nether Origin Sect, to refine a single drop of Nether Water True Essence, a Sage must use their Sage true fire to smelt Nether Water day and night for one hundred and eighty days! This pot my master gave me—its value is no less than tens of millions of spirit stones!"

Zhou Fuyou, who was usually indifferent and aloof toward outsiders, felt warmth in his heart when he thought of his master's care for him.

He took a deep breath, steadying his mind.

Next, he planned to refine the Nether Water True Essence bit by bit into *Qi Sea Floating Heaven*, adding power to this spell art!

But this process required complete concentration throughout, without the slightest negligence.

Nether Water could corrupt a cultivator's dharma power, and even their golden core or nascent soul.

If one wasn't careful, it could damage their Dao foundation.

Zhou Fuyou drew out a drop of Nether Water True Essence, then unfolded *Qi Sea Floating Heaven*. Instantly, streams of black qi surged from his body, wrapping around the Nether Water True Essence.

He was extremely careful throughout this process, afraid of something going wrong.

But after just a moment, an expression of disbelief appeared on his face.

"It's already been successfully refined?"

...

Three days later.

Within the Tiannan Dao Palace, four flying boats at the magic treasure level slowly rose, circling above the palace.

Then, figures of people rode beams of light, soaring up from the four great halls into the flying boats.

Zhou Fuyou was among them.

Boarding the flying boat, he greeted the leading second senior brother.

"Greetings, Second Senior Brother!"

The second senior brother was named Fu Bin, said to be a member of Master Qinglan's own clan.

Seeing Zhou Fuyou, he immediately smiled and nodded.

"Your room is the middle one, adjacent to mine. If anything comes up, just call for me."

Zhou Fuyou acknowledged with an "mm," then greeted the other senior brothers and sisters. Without hesitation, he passed by everyone and entered the room.

A moment later, a azure light screen completely enveloped the room.

Seeing this, Fu Bin was momentarily stunned.

The nearby Fifth Senior Sister, Eighth Senior Sister, and Seventh Senior Brother also didn't react in time.

Why did he hide in there so quickly?

Seventh Senior Brother frowned. "Little Junior Brother is too diligent. We still need to arrange squad assignments and meet with the Adepts from the other three halls. How can he just ignore all that?"

Fu Bin waved his hand. "Perhaps this is the main reason why Little Junior Brother successfully formed his core at just forty, far surpassing us mediocre types."

Seventh Senior Brother curled his lips, not agreeing with this view.

Their core formation ages were actually not old, mostly around a hundred years old, which would be considered top-tier geniuses in any sect.

It was just that Little Junior Brother was too monstrous—it wasn't that they were mediocre.

"Don't worry, I'll handle the rest. You all first form squads according to the roster. I'll personally arrange Little Junior Brother's placement!"

Fu Bin gave the orders, and the junior brothers and sisters all acknowledged.

For this expedition, Houtu Hall dispatched five Adepts: Second Senior Brother, Fifth Senior Sister, Seventh Senior Brother, Eighth Senior Sister, and Zhou Fuyou.

Additionally, there were one hundred Initiate true cultivators accompanying the expedition.

The other three halls had roughly the same configuration.

Thus, Tiannan Dao Palace quickly formed an elite force of twenty Adepts and four hundred Initiate true cultivators. Barring accidents, this force would soon be deployed to the East Sea coast battlefield to slay demons and protect the Dao Sect!

Fu Bin stood at the bow of the boat, facing the howling wind outside, his expression somewhat melancholy.

If only this were the former Central Continent, where teleportation arrays were spread across the land. Going to the East Sea coast would only require two or three teleportations—not this much trouble.

It was all because of that demon phoenix, which had shattered Central Continent, causing most teleportation arrays to nearly collapse and become paralyzed.

Now demon troubles had arisen again, truly giving one a headache.

"Never mind. We're just following the example of our predecessors, doing it all over again. What our predecessors could achieve, we can achieve too."

"Set out!"

...

Ever since boarding the Houtu flying boat, Zhou Fuyou had been immersed in refining the Nether Water True Essence.

He discovered that refining this substance was actually faster than the White Egret Marsh miasma!

Not only faster, but after refinement, controlling it was also exceptionally smooth and effortless—it was like an extension of his arm.

So he didn't waste any time, day and night continuously refining the Nether Water True Essence into *Qi Sea Floating Heaven*.

And the power of this spell art improved day by day.

Thus, half a month passed.

Zhou Fuyou slowly opened his eyes.

Above his head was a pitch-black canopy formed by a large amount of White Egret Marsh miasma.

Beneath him was a flowing expanse of black liquid.

Qi Sea Floating Heaven, black clouds pressing down on the city.

Still waters run deep, ready to devour!

Zhou Fuyou moved his mind, reaching out to summon a transmission talisman that flew in.

The transmission talisman ignited without wind. Zhou Fuyou's brows twitched slightly, and instantly *Qi Sea Floating Heaven* returned to his body. He stood up and walked to the flying boat's deck.

On the deck, cultivators had already gathered, waiting quietly.

Zhou Fuyou swept his gaze across and saw a few familiar figures.

He didn't greet them, walking over to the side of Second Senior Brother and the other three.

"Second Senior Brother, have we arrived?"

Second Senior Brother smiled warmly. "Not quite yet, but the city lord of Zhenhai City has sent word that he will come to welcome us. So we're preparing in advance to avoid any breach of etiquette."

"City lord of Zhenhai City?" Zhou Fuyou was startled.

Seventh Senior Brother beside him explained, "It's Sage Wen. Their clan has lived on the East Sea coast for generations and fortunately survived the Central Continent catastrophe. So the Dao Sect has continued to entrust them with coastal defense duties."

Zhou Fuyou was somewhat surprised. A dignified Sage was willing to humble himself to welcome them?

Second Senior Brother straightened his back and explained calmly, "Little Junior Brother, don't underestimate the status of us Dao Palace disciples!"

Hearing this, Zhou Fuyou suddenly understood.

Usually within the Dao Palace, everyone competed with peers of the same age. Except for a few like himself, most people didn't deeply feel their own position.

But in reality, the young people who could join Tiannan Dao Palace were, when placed outside, all outstanding geniuses with excellent aptitudes, all future pillars of various great clans.

And if they developed well in the future, they could even become major figures of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!

This time, the more than four hundred cultivators dispatched by the Four Imperial Halls were the very embodiment of genius.

None of the Initiate cultivators were over a hundred years old.

The Adepts were mostly young and vigorous, geniuses with hope of reaching the Sage realm in the future.

Sage Wen, by condescending to welcome them in advance, was both showing his importance and seeking to form a good connection.

After understanding this, Zhou Fuyou became completely at ease.

They were the newcomers, but also honored guests!

Soon, a magnificent and majestic flying boat flew toward them from afar.

Figures rode beams of light from it, carrying large amounts of wine, fruits, and refreshments to comfort the Dao Palace disciples.

Meanwhile, the twenty Adepts from the Four Imperial Halls boarded that flying boat to accept the banquet hosted by Sage Wen, the city lord of Zhenhai City.

...

Several thousand li away, a wisp of green smoke coiled faintly within the clouds.

Gazing at the flying boat fleet in the distance, the green smoke halted its advance.

"That kid entered Tiannan Dao Palace for twenty years. I never thought that when he came out again, he would already be at the Adept realm!"

"Tsk tsk, this cultivation speed—even my master in his prime couldn't match it!"

"With a Sage appearing, although I'm not afraid of being discovered, it's better to keep my distance."

The green smoke transformed into a dark cloud, embedding itself into the cloud layer, drifting slowly.

It circled around the halted flying boat fleet and, by midnight, drifted over the vast sea.

Suddenly!

"Such heavy demonic aura!"

The dark cloud dispersed, moonlight fell, revealing a graceful, slender figure.

It was Xiao Bai, who was responsible for secretly observing Zhou Fuyou!

At this moment, she frowned, spreading her divine sense to examine the situation on the East Sea.

The demonic aura was so heavy that it almost made her feel she had returned to the battlefield of the ancient Eastern Wilderness human-demon war.

Especially the vigorous soul power—she detected several powerful presences.

"One, two, three... There are actually three demonic cultivators at the Sage realm!"

"Such a large formation... Could it be... No, this aura—"

Xiao Bai's expression changed slightly. She concentrated her divine sense and charged toward a certain sea area.

There, sea demons were numerous, birds circled, and demonic aura was thick.

And at the center of the gathered demon beasts, a magnificent palace sat quietly on the seabed.

Xiao Bai's divine sense broke through like a sharp spear, barging straight into the palace.

Inside, a transformed demonic cultivator was appreciating the beautiful dance of clam-women and mermaids below.

And at the seat below him, a thick black python had its head buried in a vat of wine.

"Brother Black, how is the taste of my Dry Blue Ice Wine?"

Hearing this, the black python raised its head abruptly, splashing wine everywhere.

Looking closely, what python was this?

Its head bore horns, its eyes were like an ox's—it was clearly a black dragon!

Just then, the transformed demonic cultivator seemed to sense something. Rage erupted on his face, and he raised his hand to strike an attack outside the palace.

"Vile creature, how dare you spy on this Emperor!"
