# Chapter 776: A Century of Friendship Ends Here

“Grandmaster, can Senior Sister Qi really defeat Baleful Dragonzi?”

“What, you don’t think highly of Emei?”

On a tall building not far from the dueling platform, an old man and a young man gazed into the distance, discussing the battle.

Ran Feng shook his head. “The disciples of our sect are top-tier experts wherever they go. How could I doubt Senior Sister’s strength?”

In his words, there was still a stubborn pride for the Five Elements Divine Sect, which had become a homeless dog.

Indeed, at its peak, the Five Elements Divine Sect was worthy of such pride!

Back then, each of the Five Peaks had a Sage.

Moreover, the late-stage Sage, Patriarch Divine Origin, sat in command, suppressing all directions.

Such strength surpassed even the renowned Dongyang Sect.

It was precisely because of this peak glory that the Five Elements Divine Sect caught the eye of the Eastern Wilderness holy land, the Nether Origin Sect, which once promised to let Patriarch Divine Origin cultivate in their ancestral land.

The reason it was only “once” was because of what happened later.

While working for the Nether Origin Sect, Patriarch Divine Origin suffered severe injuries. When the demon army later pressed against their borders, he could not act. Under the leadership of the Five Peak Sages, the sect retreated step by step.

By the time they withdrew to Fenghua Domain, two of the Five Peak Sages had died. Even Evergreen Sage, renowned for his survival abilities, had his physical body destroyed, with only his Nascent Soul surviving.

From then on, the Five Elements Divine Sect declined.

But a starved camel is still bigger than a horse. Even as homeless dogs fleeing their homeland, the sect’s disciples still maintained their former pride.

Of course, having experienced many things—watching towers rise and towers fall—these disciples had also become much more realistic.

At least they were no longer arrogant enough to look down on everyone.

Ran Feng lowered his voice. “Baleful Dragonzi is no ordinary person. He’s the young leader cultivated with full resource support from the Luo Tian Sect. His body refinement has long reached the Furnace Realm. His released astral qi can melt metal and break stone. Especially on this dueling platform, the fighting range is too small, which favors his style. Senior Sister will likely be constrained everywhere.”

The third realm of body refinement—the Furnace Realm!

Legend had it that one who understood the four stages of blood and qi changes could condense astral qi, possessing the courage of ten thousand men!

For a same-realm Adept to fight him, a direct frontal assault was absolutely impossible. The only way was to keep distance and use the advantage of divine sense to attack the enemy’s weakness.

Divine Fire Sage pursed his lips, his expression somewhat serious.

But the words he spoke were still reassuring.

“Since I sent Emei to fight, I have my reasons.”

“She has already learned the Red Thread Dragon-Binding Art.”

Ran Feng was startled. “The Red Thread Dragon-Binding Art specifically used against body refiners?”

Divine Fire Sage smiled slightly. “Exactly. You should know that our sect once imitated ancient times and cultivated Five Elements Warriors. In that cultivation process, the warriors endured the most cruel and painful treatment. To prevent them from turning on their masters, we developed specialized binding and killing methods. And…”

He paused here, his gaze falling on the tall man standing opposite Qi Emei.

“This person has an iron will, but his heart cultivation is fragile. If he still cherishes old feelings and hesitates even slightly, he will surely lose!”

Ran Feng opened his mouth. Perhaps this last point was the main reason Grandmaster was confident in Senior Sister Qi’s victory!

…

Under the gaze of the crowd, the battle had already begun.

Unexpectedly, the first to attack was not the body-refining cultivator Baleful Dragonzi, who needed to seize the initiative, but the early-stage Adept Qi Emei!

Red threads coiled around her body, and her flying sword shot from her sleeve.

Baleful Dragonzi held a broadsword, barely blocking the bizarre attacks of the flying sword in the void.

When he couldn’t block, he flapped his wings and teleported away.

Clang, clang, clang—the sounds rang out continuously on the dueling platform, quite lively.

But for the watching audience, it still wasn’t intense enough!

“Why isn’t he going for the kill?”

“With all this flirting, are they having a romance here?”

“Show us your real skills!”

Baleful Dragonzi continued to focus on evasion, but Qi Emei was losing patience.

She controlled the flying sword with one hand while forming hand seals with the other.

Suddenly!

Flames surged violently on the flying sword thrusting at Baleful Dragonzi.

Even though he narrowly dodged, a large portion of his thick black hair was burned away.

The cultivators of the Divine Sect were best at spell techniques!

This was no empty reputation!

Their Five Elements spells were not only powerful but often integrated into various combat details.

Using artifacts to channel spells was their specialty!

A single flying sword sometimes rolled with waves of flame, sometimes scattered fiery rain, making Baleful Dragonzi look quite haggard and beaten.

“Be careful!”

When Baleful Dragonzi blocked another flying sword attack, an anxious reminder came from outside the arena.

The voice was clearly sharp, yet it sounded so gentle to the ears.

Baleful Dragonzi knew who the voice belonged to, but he had no time to pay attention.

Because in his pupils, a majestic palm print was rumbling toward him.

Great Handprint of Primordial Qi!

The most iconic external spell of the Five Elements Divine Sect. Disciples of all peaks and cultivators of all attributes could practice this technique.

It had even spread to many places in the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world.

But unlike the castrated versions learned by outsiders, the Divine Sect’s Great Handprint of Primordial Qi was the most orthodox!

When the palm pressed down, even though it was fire-attributed, it contained vigorous vitality.

This was the true essence of the Great Handprint of Primordial Qi—using the mutual generation of the Five Elements to multiply spell power, then using the mutual conquest to destroy the enemy!

Unfortunately, Baleful Dragonzi had seen this move many times during the years he fought alongside Qi Emei.

He knew the countermeasures by heart.

Instead of retreating, he advanced, thrusting his great sword straight at the handprint.

Overbearing astral qi poured out like an ocean, stabbing directly at the center of the palm print.

The mutual generation of the Five Elements had its own order.

Where the power was strongest was also where it was weakest.

The flaw lay in the flow of mana in the palm’s center.

Boom!

With an explosion, Baleful Dragonzi broke through the handprint.

He curled one finger, and a bloody energy bolt shot out abruptly.

Blood God Tribulation Finger!

This finger was perfectly timed, exquisitely precise!

It struck exactly during the vacuum period after Qi Emei’s spell, when her flying sword couldn’t attack to force a defense.

Using astral qi to execute it, as long as it hit a vital point, it could…

Chi!

A deep hole appeared in the dueling platform’s floor tiles, which could withstand a full-force attack from a late-stage Adept.

It was behind the woman.

If one judged by its trajectory, it had barely grazed past her head.

Baleful Dragonzi had missed!

The watching crowd immediately erupted in uproar.

What a pity—they almost saw the beautiful, heroic female cultivator’s head burst open like a melon!

Qi Emei stood there in a daze, a blood mark slowly appearing on her cheek.

She couldn’t help touching her face. It felt wet.

“As expected, your combat talent is rare in the world, no less than your master’s.”

“But!”

“Your heart is soft!”

As she spoke, her ten fingers spread wide, and her murmurs grew louder.

Threads of blood, woven into a large net at some unknown time, had already enveloped Baleful Dragonzi when he broke through the Great Handprint of Primordial Qi.

Now, her ten fingers suddenly clenched!

Shua!

Countless red threads instantly bound Baleful Dragonzi’s entire body.

At that moment, regret was clearly visible on Baleful Dragonzi’s face.

He had seen through the flaw in his opponent’s spell and seized the chance for victory.

But she seemed to have known he would show mercy, planting a trap within the flaw.

Now, a mass of red threads enveloped him like a fishing net from all directions.

His wings, which could have flown at high speed, were forced down.

His thick armor emitted sounds of unbearable compression.

The red threads tightened, and cracks began to appear on his protective armor.

In particular, he found that his fierce, unmatched astral qi was completely suppressed under these seemingly soft red threads.

“Yilong, admit defeat!”

The woman’s voice, full of certainty, reached his ears.

Baleful Dragonzi ignored her, forcibly spreading his wings to break free from the red threads.

His eyes swept across the high platform below.

The watching crowd was still frenzied, almost morbidly so.

They seemed to wish he would be strangled to death.

Behind Qi Emei, Sima Wenjie’s disgusting face wore a bright smile as he chatted happily with the cultivator beside him.

In the distance, another group of people watched him quietly, not joining the frenzied cheers.

The leader was tall and sturdy, his expression cold and indifferent.

Even though he had fallen to this state, he seemed unmoved.

Instead, a woman in colorful clothes standing beside him frowned deeply.

Behind these two were faces he knew very well—fellow disciples he had lived with day and night.

But intimidated by the cold man’s authority, even though they were worried, they couldn’t cheer for him.

Only a woman who had previously warned him was now moving her lips, murmuring words he couldn’t hear.

“Master must be very disappointed in me, right?”

Baleful Dragonzi thought this.

The surrounding shouts surged like a tide, waking him.

“Kill him! Kill him!”

“Hurry up and lose! I bet on Sima Wenjie’s lineage to win. As long as this match is won, I have a chance to make a fortune!”

“Except for the first match, where Li Yingzhang unexpectedly won through a grueling battle, the subsequent Fu Jiusheng, Chu Kui, and Yang Ping all lost consecutively. If this match is lost again, that’s four straight losses. Sect Master Yao’s lineage will have no chance to turn things around!”

“Kill, kill, kill!”

With a light sigh, Baleful Dragonzi understood why his master was so cold.

By entrusting him with the crucial fifth match, he was placing the Luo Tian Sect’s future on his shoulders.

And he indeed had the strength!

If he let the sect fall into an irredeemable situation just because of personal feelings, he would be letting everyone down.

To be worthy of everyone, he could only let down the person before him.

Unlike the outside world’s stereotype that body refiners only focused on physical training and neglected mental cultivation, Wang Yuan’s lineage had emphasized willpower training from the very first realm of body refinement. With the *Fine Dust Primordial Art* left by Luo Chen before he left, the Luo Tian Sect’s body refiners had very few flaws in their mental state, and their recovery was extremely fast!

A hundred thoughts flashed by in an instant.

Baleful Dragonzi had already made his decision!

With a low shout, his wings stretched and contracted, then suddenly exploded!

Qi Emei was caught off guard, her face showing disbelief.

“Those wings—weren’t they a gift from an elder he respected most? He’s self-destructing them just like that?”

Boom!

With a loud noise, the wings, infused with all his spiritual power, suddenly burst open, creating a massive shockwave that pushed open a small space around the red threads binding him.

Baleful Dragonzi, at the center of the explosion, took a greater hit.

But with his physique, harder than diamond, he spat out a mouthful of blood and endured it.

In his sight, the scattered red threads seemed to be tightening again.

Once they touched his body, the red threads would use softness to overcome hardness, greatly suppressing his astral qi.

Baleful Dragonzi forcibly moved his right hand, pressing his thumb against his chest.

In an instant, the essence of his five organs gathered on his heart.

Five qi merged into one, and the Giant Spirit Transformation began!

Visibly, Baleful Dragonzi’s body started to swell.

In a moment, a muscular, ten-foot-tall giant appeared on the high platform.

He violently grabbed the red threads on his body, tearing them off along with his broken armor.

Then, taking large strides, he dragged his broadsword and charged furiously at Qi Emei.

As he leaped into the air and swung his sword down, Qi Emei could only retreat while activating her defensive shield and recalling her flying sword.

Boom!

“Emei, this is where we end!”

The words in her ear were a resolute roar, accompanied by the sound of astral qi tearing through her shield.

In her sight, his hair and beard stood on end, his eyes wide with fury.

This scene was exactly like when they fought side by side in the past—so heroic and brave!

After the sword strike, he flipped midair and smashed down with his elbow.

Not only that, but as he shifted his force, blood light burst forth frequently.

Even when she barely dodged, the blood light that had already passed would turn and stab again.

The storm of attacks left everyone stunned.

Only Qi Emei, exhausted from fleeing, had a complex look in her eyes.

“So this is what it’s like to be his enemy—such overwhelming attacks.”

Although the high platform was large, for Adepts, it was still too small, with no room to maneuver.

After a few dizzying rounds of pursuit and defense…

Boom!

The platform’s stones exploded. The giant grabbed the woman’s struggling ankle, descended from the sky, and his other large hand already gripped her slender neck.

Chi!

The flying sword, a magic treasure, without anyone controlling it, stuck diagonally into the floor tiles.

Everything was settled!

“Huff, huff, huff…”

Baleful Dragonzi panted heavily, his ferocious face staring intently at the woman before him.

“You forced me.”

“Kill me, then!”

His thick hand immediately tightened.

But just as he was about to crush that fragile neck, Baleful Dragonzi suddenly threw her off the dueling platform.

This scene immediately drew sneers and disdain from the crowd.

Baleful Dragonzi’s body gradually shrank. Ignoring the public criticism, he lowered his head and slowly walked down the high platform.

“Senior Brother, are you alright?”

A female cultivator ran over first, asking with concern.

Baleful Dragonzi glanced at her, shook his head, and said nothing.

Instead, he walked step by step to the tall man’s side.

“Master, I…”

Wang Yuan gently patted his shoulder, said nothing, and walked past him, leaving the dueling platform.

As Wang Yuan strode forward, the Luo Tian Sect cultivators followed behind him.

Wherever they passed, cultivators made way.

The name of War Demon Wang Yuan was known to all in Lingtian Pass!

Even Sages would treat him with respect.

That face had to be given.

Baleful Dragonzi, like a child who had done wrong, walked among his fellow disciples.

It wasn’t until Gu Caiyi spoke that he looked up.

“No need to be ashamed. Winning this match is already good enough.”

“Uncle-Master Gu, I didn’t kill her.”

“I know. A century of friendship made you soft-hearted. But only this once. The next time you face each other, it will be life or death.”

As she said this, Gu Caiyi made no attempt to hide her meaning. Her gaze fixed coldly on another group of cultivators walking out of the crowd.

Sima Wenjie happened to look over as well.

“This time, you got lucky. Next match won’t be so simple. I know exactly how much that old man Min Longyu is worth!”

Gu Caiyi’s brows shot up. “Beast, you’d better think about how to face your sister!”

Mentioning his sister, Sima Wenjie’s face immediately darkened.

“Don’t worry, you won’t last until then!”

As he spoke, a female cultivator stepped out beside him. She had sword-like brows and starry eyes, her aura intimidating.

A Jade Cauldron Sword Cultivator—Hong Ling!
