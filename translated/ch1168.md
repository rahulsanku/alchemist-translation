# Chapter 1168: Entering the Ninth Ring, Primordial Unity Shows Its Might

"Luo Chen, I wish you success!"

"Although the Ninefold Immortal Tower protects you, the chaotic energy within each of the Nine Rings of Annihilation is different. Brother Luo must pay close attention."

"With Brother Luo's aptitude, entering the middle three rings should be no problem. If you have free time, you can come find me at these coordinates, and we can discuss cultivation together."

……

One figure after another took the initiative to greet Luo Chen as they passed by him.

The warmth in their words was beyond imagination.

These people were all from the older batch of martial artists who had previously cultivated within the Nine Rings of Annihilation, so they were more familiar with the situation.

Naturally, they had a clearer understanding of the importance of the Meridian-Connecting Pills that Luo Chen possessed.

For those who sought to build goodwill, there were naturally also those with ill intentions.

Apart from these people, the malicious gazes of others also fell upon Luo Chen's line of sight, one by one.

"It seems it won't be peaceful inside either!"

Luo Chen smiled faintly, appearing unconcerned.

As long as the Martial Dao Human Immortals of the upper three rings didn't trouble him, he didn't put these Leakless Realm martial artists in his eyes.

"Senior, I'm ready." Xiangfei's voice came from behind him.

Luo Chen felt somewhat helpless. "Actually, with the book Gu Xun gave me, there's no need for you to personally guide me inside."

Xiangfei shook her head. "What is learned from books is ultimately shallow. If I accompany you on this journey, you can become familiar with the cultivation environment of the Nine Rings of Annihilation more quickly. Senior need not worry about me. If you feel it's inappropriate, I will take the initiative to withdraw."

Luo Chen thought for a moment, then nodded slightly.

That would be fine, then!

Behind them, from the stone house, batch after batch of people were being teleported over.

These people were far fewer in number than before, totaling no more than a few dozen.

Their identities were somewhat similar to Luo Chen's.

They all belonged to this session's newly promoted Leakless Realm martial artists invited by Wuyou City, entering the Ninefold Immortal Tower for the first time.

Standing behind Luo Chen, gazes fixed on his back, those eyes were filled with scrutiny, curiosity, and defiance.

We're all Leakless Realm martial artists—why should you be designated early as a "Human Immortal Seed," placed alongside those Martial Dao Human Immortals?

The future would still depend on the actual cultivation situation later.

Luo Chen couldn't care less. After adjusting his state, he led Xiangfei and flew toward the Ninefold Immortal Tower.

The closer they got, the more they could detect the energy fluctuations surging like tidal waves around the white giant tower.

"It's already leaked this far?"

Xiangfei murmured to herself, then reminded him, "Senior, first open your acupoints, draw a portion of chaotic energy into your body, attempt to refine it, and become familiar with this process."

Luo Chen's expression turned slightly serious, and he followed her instructions.

The Ninefold Immortal Tower allowed entry but not exit for a hundred years.

The first year was an exception.

At this time, when the Immortal Tower was open, a small amount of chaotic energy would spill out.

This portion of chaotic energy, having been filtered through the Ninefold Immortal Tower, was no longer so violent and would not actively erode a martial artist's flesh.

This precisely gave new martial artists a process of familiarization.

If one could truly absorb it into the body and transform it into blood qi, then the next step would be to truly enter the Immortal Tower.

That was also how the Nine-Ring martial artist from Mocheng City Lord had come about.

He had once successfully drawn qi into his body, refined it, and after assessing himself as capable, briefly entered the Ninefold Immortal Tower to cultivate for a period.

Xiangfei had also tried, but she had only forcibly entered for a few days before she couldn't bear it and was forced to withdraw.

Of course!

This process would inevitably screen out a large number of unsuitable people.

Those who were screened out, unwilling to accept that they couldn't enter the tower, would linger in the peripheral areas, persisting in cultivation for decades, only to end up with a body full of hidden injuries and return in dejection.

Those returnees who had once besieged Luo Chen were exactly that kind of person.

No matter how great Luo Chen's ambitions were, the first hurdle he had to cross was this one!

With a thought, Luo Chen slowly opened his acupoints.

In the gentle breeze, he attempted to guide the surrounding energy into his body.

The blood qi furnace also began to rotate.

The moment the chaotic energy entered, that long-unfamiliar feeling of external energy entering finally appeared.

Luo Chen carefully wrapped this energy with his Blood-Refining Divine Astral, then guided it into the blood qi furnace for refinement and absorption.

During the process, he felt somewhat uncomfortable.

Because this energy was too mixed!

Of it, only one or two percent could actually be transformed and absorbed.

The efficiency was far too low.

But there was still some effect!

Luo Chen could feel a slight increase in the blood qi within his body.

Just as he was about to expel the remaining chaotic energy that couldn't be transformed, the gray small cauldron suspended above the blood qi furnace trembled faintly for an instant.

Luo Chen was stunned!

……

With the experience from last time, coupled with her repaired body, Xiangfei's process of drawing qi into her body and then refining it was relatively smooth this time.

While the dozens of other martial artists were still attempting, she had already completed this process.

After opening her eyes, she instinctively looked toward where Luo Chen was.

Unexpectedly, Luo Chen had already finished and was now showing a frowning, contemplative expression.

Xiangfei's heart tightened, and she asked with concern, "Senior, is there a problem?"

Luo Chen waved his hand. "It's nothing. We can enter the tower."

Xiangfei breathed a sigh of relief. "That's good, that's good."

Before leaving, the two glanced at the others around them.

Half a day had passed, and still no one had successfully completed this process.

Some with unstable foundations even spat blood and collapsed to the ground.

This situation was not unexpected.

Martial artists of the Great Annihilation Realm were accustomed to transforming blood qi through dietary supplements, usually focusing on circulating and tempering their own blood qi. Faced with an influx of external energy, they were often at a loss.

Moreover, chaotic energy was such a mixed and impure existence.

In a year's time, very few could adapt.

"Actually, it's a good thing. If they really can't adapt, leaving early would allow them to enjoy a few hundred years of power and pleasure in the Great Annihilation Realm," Luo Chen said.

Xiangfei sighed. That was true, but when it came down to it, who would truly be willing to give up?

With a flicker of their figures, the two had already passed through that thin energy layer and truly stepped into the Ninefold Immortal Tower.

As his eyes took in the scene, Luo Chen's gaze immediately froze.

Even though Xiangfei had described the situation inside, and he had learned about the tower's environment from the book Gu Xun had given him, seeing it in person was still shocking.

Desolate, boundless!

In the pitch-black space, there was little light.

Only pieces of meteorites, large and small, drifted about, flickering with faint light.

It was less like a space inside a tower and more like a silent, dead universe.

And unlike what the eyes saw, what the physical body could feel was even more!

It was layers of air waves that truly crashed against the body like waves!

And this was only the very bottom layer of the Ninefold Immortal Tower.

"Is this the Ninth Ring of the Nine Rings of Annihilation?"

Xiangfei took a deep breath and nodded emphatically. "Yes!"

"If the Ninth Ring is like this, one can imagine how turbulent the chaotic energy above must be." Luo Chen looked up, his tone filled with curiosity.

Xiangfei reminded him, "Senior must not be careless. Chaotic energy itself is composed of mixed elements, and we can utilize less than one percent of it. The higher you go, the more dangerous it becomes. I've heard that the middle three layers already have storm-like force, and the upper three layers are called the Chaotic Ocean. No matter where one cultivates, our martial bodies are constantly subjected to the impact and corrosion of chaotic energy. Countless martial artists die within it every hundred years!"

Luo Chen hummed in acknowledgment. He roughly knew this situation.

Not to mention anything else, just this dim, lightless environment—staying in it for a hundred years could drive even the strong-willed mad.

And every hundred years, the number of martial artists who could leave the Ninefold Immortal Tower was far less than the cumulative number who had entered.

This alone showed the danger of the Nine Rings of Annihilation.

"Let's go. First, find a safe cultivation stone," Xiangfei suggested proactively.

Luo Chen had no objection. Following beside her, he roamed the peripheral area of the Ninth Ring.

The so-called cultivation stones were those meteorites floating in the air that remained stationary.

These meteorites had been scoured by chaotic energy for years without being destroyed, and they themselves possessed strong resistance to chaotic energy.

However, they were relatively few in number, and the larger ones were harder to find.

Often, large cultivation stones where one could stretch out and move freely were already occupied.

To obtain one, you either had to wait for someone to leave voluntarily and move to the next ring, wait for them to fall, or fight for it. The winner would naturally enjoy a better cultivation environment.

But according to Xiangfei, seizing cultivation stones through battle wasn't that easy.

Martial artists who cultivated in the Nine Rings of Annihilation year-round, in order to firmly hold onto good cultivation stones, would often form cliques and alliances.

If you provoked one person, you might incur a greater backlash.

And once injured within the Nine Rings of Annihilation, recovery was extremely difficult.

As Xiangfei searched for a cultivation stone, she explained the situation to Luo Chen.

"Senior has just arrived and is not yet familiar with the environment here. Even if you possess great power, it's best not to fight with others for now."

"I understand."

Luo Chen said with a smile, then suddenly changed the subject.

"What about that cultivation stone over there?"

Xiangfei was startled. Following Luo Chen's gaze, she saw no target in the dim space.

After flying for a while longer, she finally saw an irregular cultivation stone.

It was about two feet wide, barely enough for one person to sit on.

"Senior has sharp eyes!"

Xiangfei praised Luo Chen first, then said happily, "This cultivation stone is not bad. It's enough for Senior's personal cultivation needs."

"And you?" Luo Chen asked back.

Xiangfei smiled. "I came in this time only to guide Senior. Now that the goal is achieved, I should naturally leave."

But as she spoke, the smile on her face gradually faded.

Her closed red lips seemed to want to speak but held back.

Finally, she gave Luo Chen a graceful bow.

"Senior, I wish you a flourishing martial path and a breakthrough to the Human Immortal realm. I will be waiting for you outside. If you have time in a hundred years, you can come find me in Sanxiang City. I am willing to light another stick of incense for you and brew a pot of tea."

Luo Chen did not try to keep her. He simply nodded gently.

"Farewell!"

Watching Xiangfei's figure gradually fade into the darkness, Luo Chen's mood rippled slightly.

Saying farewell, it was likely hard to meet again.

In one's life, one always meets all kinds of people.

Some are stunningly brilliant, some are utterly ordinary.

Xiangfei was somewhere in between, but to Luo Chen, she was ultimately just a passerby.

Their fate began with a fight that led to acquaintance, and ended in just a few words.

Taking a deep breath, Luo Chen adjusted his mindset, as if nothing had happened. He immediately sat down cross-legged.

Then, forming hand seals and circulating his qi, his aura instantly became solemn and dignified.

Shua! Shua!

Shua! Shua!

Four more arms extended from his waist and ribs.

Some held the primordial unity, some pointed to heaven and earth.

This was completely different from the three-headed, six-armed state of Luo Chen's thousand-zhang golden body. In that state, the six arms were all on his shoulders.

This, however, was the standard posture Luo Chen assumed when cultivating the *God-Devil Formlessness*.

From the very beginning, he had directly entered the most serious state.

Then, all his attention sank into the natal magic treasure within his body!

The Primordial Unity Cauldron!

Just now!

While he was still outside, adapting to the process of refining chaotic energy, his natal magic treasure had trembled strangely.

Although it was only for an instant, Luo Chen had precisely captured it.

When he tried to observe it again later, there was no further reaction.

At that time, Luo Chen speculated that perhaps the chaotic energy outside was too thin. He planned to continue observing once he entered.

Now that he had a foothold, he couldn't afford to be negligent.

All the acupoints on his body opened simultaneously.

As he breathed, the surging air waves nearby seemed to find an outlet and surged toward him en masse.

This was an amount of energy that would be enormous even for an early-stage Sage of the Qi Refining path. Absorbing it all could easily burst the Purple Mansion.

Moreover, high-level cultivators, in order to manipulate the spiritual qi of heaven and earth, kept their acupoints open year-round and couldn't actively close them. After absorbing beyond their limit, they would continue to absorb chaotic energy, ultimately ending up being burst apart alive.

But for martial artists, it was relatively less dangerous.

After fiercely absorbing a surge of chaotic energy, Luo Chen forcibly closed all his acupoints.

Immediately, the surrounding energy, finding no breakthrough point, lingered for a moment before slowly dissipating.

From this, one could see why the Wuyou City Lord only allowed Leakless Realm martial artists to enter the Nine Rings of Annihilation for cultivation.

If one couldn't achieve a leakless body and control the opening and closing of all acupoints, they would likely be burst apart alive the moment they entered.

And even for Leakless Realm martial artists, precisely controlling their absorption limit was a very demanding task.

Luo Chen didn't care about his limit.

At this moment, he was only focused on one thing!

When that surge of chaotic energy entered, the blood qi furnace immediately began to rotate, attempting to absorb it.

But this time, Luo Chen controlled the blood qi furnace's movement. Instead, he used his vast Blood-Refining Divine Astral to wrap the chaotic energy and guide it all into the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

The gray small cauldron immediately shook!

The originally dim cauldron body was covered with patches of light.

The patterns on it also became clear.

There were towering mountains and surging great rivers.

Flowers, birds, insects, and fish were lifelike.

Vein-like channels, as if alive, began to writhe.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron began to attempt to refine this vast chaotic energy, but it wasn't very fast.

If a Leakless martial artist operating a blood qi furnace could refine one or two percent of a surge of chaotic energy, with the rest being impurities to be expelled, then the Primordial Unity Cauldron could at most refine one or two times more on that basis.

This change, if spoken of, would shock ordinary martial artists.

Luo Chen was also secretly pleased. Indeed, his natal magic treasure was effective in refining chaotic energy.

However, the surprises didn't end there.

Suddenly!

A gray light, uncontrollably, spread from deep within the cauldron.

Wherever the gray light passed, the chaotic energy boiling inside the cauldron suddenly became calm, like a pool of autumn water.

The moment he saw that gray light, Luo Chen was stunned.

Drip!

Drip!

Drop by drop, liquid dripped from within the Primordial Unity Cauldron, falling into the blood qi furnace.

Without any effort, the blood qi furnace, as it rotated, refined it, transforming it into the purest blood qi.

Its quantity was far greater than before, by a hundred or a thousand times!

The process was so effortless it was beyond compare! (Chapter End)
