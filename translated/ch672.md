# Chapter 672: Four Great Fire Spirits, an Archaic Beast Body

Blazing Refinement Prison.

A holy land for those who cultivated fire arts!

Within this place, fire-path cultivators could display strength beyond their usual level. Their mana was inexhaustible, and their courage multiplied. Even Moya Cave Master had once been so eager that he nearly challenged Luo Chen. From this, one could see it clearly.

Cultivators who practiced other methods would be greatly suppressed within Blazing Refinement Prison, especially those of metal, water, and ice.

Apart from this, Blazing Refinement Prison had another major characteristic: many sourceless flames were hidden here.

These sourceless flames might not be high in rank, but their kinds were varied and numerous.

Moreover, the vast majority had begun gradually gaining intelligence because of Blazing Refinement Prison's special environment.

For Luo Chen's Withering-Bloom True Fire, back in the Yan Alliance's Incense Burning Valley, it had barely developed a tiny bit of intelligence only when it advanced to the fourth tier.

Yet inside Blazing Refinement Prison, some third-tier sourceless flames already possessed awareness.

Especially those fourth-tier sourceless flames. Their spirituality was abundant, and they could already derive illusory bodies and become fire spirits!

This kind of fire spirit was extremely dangerous to cultivators.

And unlike ordinary wood and stone spirits, whose methods were lacking and combat strength low, fire-type spirits were born with destructive power.

Especially!

Blazing Refinement Prison was like their home. Fighting on home ground greatly increased their battle strength!

Luo Chen had already discovered this when devouring that fourth-tier Burning Heaven Fire [TERM?].

In that battle, he had even briefly transformed into his celestial peng true body. This showed how powerful fourth-tier fire spirits were. Even if they were inferior to orthodox Sage Sages, the difference was not large.

And in Blazing Refinement Prison, such existences were not limited to the Burning Heaven Fire.

As Luo Chen traveled, he had constantly explored the habitats of those powerful fire spirits.

Originally, he had still needed to spend much effort searching. But as the spirituality of the Withering-Bloom True Fire increased greatly, perhaps because like attracted like, he sensed several places suspected to contain fourth-tier fire spirits in a short time.

If Han Zhan had not reminded him at the time, Luo Chen might already have lost himself in the joy of seeking, capturing, devouring, and refining fire spirits.

Now...

Those unfinished matters had instead become an additional trump card in Luo Chen's hand.

Bang!

In the void, an explosive sound rang out.

A figure abruptly appeared above an erupting volcano.

Beneath his feet, black smoke rolled and firelight filled the sky.

In the distance, a generation's Demon Emperor was being attacked from both sides by two great fire spirits, hard-pressed and barely holding on.

Luo Chen took a deep breath. With one hand, he formed several seals in succession, then pressed down with a palm.

The Mountain Collapse Art, which he had once relied on to roam the Initiate stage, had slowly been shelved after he formed his Golden Core. Yet in this battle, it shone brilliantly.

In this world, there had never been any useless spell!

At the right time and the right place, even the most useless Cleansing Art could bloom with a different radiance.

At this moment, Mountain Collapse appeared again!

"Come out!"

Luo Chen gave a low roar. Seeing the mountain collapse and the earth split, a black fire spirit like a tiger slowly opened its eyes.

Its sharp eyes first fell on Luo Chen, the culprit.

But immediately afterward, it moved its gaze away and looked toward Shark Emperor Liujun, who was fiercely battling the two great fire spirits in the distance.

In silence, the tiger fire spirit stepped with vigorous strides. Unknown black flame coiled around it as it flew toward that battlefield.

Seeing this, Luo Chen could not help letting out a breath.

"Han Zhan did not deceive me!"

"Devouring large amounts of sourceless flames here strengthened the Withering-Bloom True Fire, and at the same time, it also made me gradually merge into Blazing Refinement Prison, almost showing signs of assimilation."

"This was originally a bad thing, but now it has instead become a good thing!"

His body carried a dense aura of the Withering-Bloom True Fire. In the eyes of these powerful fire spirits, which had gained intelligence but not much wisdom, he seemed like a "brother or sister."

Ordinarily, brothers and sisters might fight and quarrel.

But now that their homeland had been invaded, it was brothers quarreling within the walls while resisting insult from outside!

Another fourth-tier fire spirit joined the battle!

Shark Emperor Liujun's heart and courage turned cold, especially toward that black tiger treading through the air, whose body was covered in strange and powerful flames.

Even the silver giant python and azure fire tree seemed slightly inferior.

One fire spirit, he did not fear.

Two fire spirits, he could still fight.

But three fire spirits comparable to Sage combat strength, occupying the home-field advantage and countering his water arts, made him have to weigh his own strength.

Four figures moved rapidly through Blazing Refinement Prison like flowing light.

Each collision between them erupted in powerful battle aftermath, sweeping across hundreds and thousands of li.

Large mountain ranges were destroyed, and some low-tier sourceless flames emerged innocently.

The blue light that spread from time to time also became increasingly weak.

Suddenly...

Shark Emperor Liujun looked toward the ground with eyes nearly splitting.

A black-armored man quietly stood before a rolling magma river.

Looking at the bubbles rising and falling, the man seemed to be thinking, and also hesitating.

As if sensing Shark Emperor Liujun's sideways gaze, he turned and raised his head, smiling slightly toward Liujun.

That smile was ferocious and mad!

In the next instant, the black-armored man raised both hands high. Behind him, countless streams of magma shot into the sky and became a crimson fire curtain.

Within that fire curtain, a bright red swimming fish swam out in confusion.

As the fish swam, the magma river also began following its path.

"Madman!"

Shark Emperor Liujun gritted his teeth and cursed. Then the oil lamp appeared in his hand again.

Liquid like blood and oil fell from his fingertip, and blue flame instantly ignited.

This time, Shark Emperor Liujun's face turned somewhat paler.

It seemed that activating this true artifact was also a great burden to him.

When the flame lit, a trace of hope finally appeared in his eyes.

With a flick of his fingertip, the blue flame split into four and flew toward the four great fire spirits.

Finally, he looked hatefully at Luo Chen, transformed into a blue escape light, and flew away.

Beside the great river, Luo Chen watched him flee without moving.

Han Zhan's voice rose beside his ear again.

"He previously took several of your punches head-on, and his body is damaged. He activated the sharkfolk inherited true artifact twice in succession, greatly consuming his mana. Add his original injuries, and now he is already a candle in the wind. Will you not press the advantage and chase him down?"

"No."

"Oh? You have changed nature. Did you not keep saying earlier that you wanted him dead?"

"They were only harsh words in battle. Hearing them is enough. Besides, how can a Sage Sage be so easy to kill to the last?"

Luo Chen stood in place, recovering the restless mana within his body. The madness from earlier had already vanished from his eyes.

He had underestimated Sage Sages!

Originally, he thought that after his body reached Archaic Tier Four, he had the capital to contend with Sage Sages. At worst, it should be a fifty-fifty situation.

Yet he had not expected this battle to end only at four-six!

He was four. Shark Emperor was six.

And this was under the circumstances that he occupied the home ground and had Han Zhan assisting him.

If not within Blazing Refinement Prison, the odds of victory would likely be even lower.

Three-seven?

Of course, neither side had reached the point of fighting with their lives, and neither side's true foundation had fully appeared.

Luo Chen's physical body had a deep foundation. If it truly became a long battle, it was still hard to say who would die.

Demon-Fall Land was not a good place for a final life-and-death battle.

Within the soul-nourishing wood, Han Zhan nodded inwardly.

It seemed Luo Chen had truly listened to those words of his.

While appearing mad on the surface, his state of mind was extremely calm. Even when fighting a Sage expert, he still kept his head clear and used the environmental advantage to the greatest extent.

The flames of battle gradually subsided.

There were still loose ends.

Before Shark Emperor Liujun fled, he split four blue flames to attack the four great fire spirits.

The four fire spirits responded in different ways.

The swimming fish was contaminated and directly leapt into the magma river. After several rises and falls, the blue light vanished.

The black tiger's black flame blazed high. In only a few moments, it ground away the blue flame.

This scene made Luo Chen's eyelids jump wildly.

What sourceless flame had birthed that fire spirit?

It was actually so powerful and tyrannical!

If he had not known life from death earlier and had tried to devour this flame, he might not only fail to kill it but injure himself instead.

As for the remaining two great fire spirits, the Withering-Bloom True Fire this time displayed a different brilliance. As its branches swayed, it was slowly devouring the blue flame!

"It can devour even this?"

Luo Chen was stunned.

"Then why before... Ah, I see. The four of them are facing a divided blue flame, while the left hand I used before faced an entire mass."

"In that case, my Withering-Bloom True Fire is not necessarily weaker than the blue flame. It is just that I used it improperly?"

As he murmured, Luo Chen's gaze fell on the silver-white fire python.

Among the four spirits, it was the most wretched.

It kept rolling on the earth, silver fire surging, yet it still could not shake off the blue flame's corrosion. Its aura was even gradually weakening.

"Go help it."

After the Withering-Bloom True Fire devoured its blue flame, Luo Chen's mind moved, and the azure fire tree covered the sky above the silver-white fire python.

Azure flame scattered down, thread by thread, like spring wind and rain. It gradually drew away that quarter of the blue flame as if peeling cocoon silk.

Then Luo Chen opened his mouth and inhaled.

The Withering-Bloom Fire Tree immediately flew back and entered his qi sea, happily stretching its branches.

Hiss, hiss, hiss...

The silver fire python swam before Luo Chen, circled him three times, and finally escaped into the depths of the earth.

Luo Chen looked at this scene in surprise and gradually understood.

"Was it showing goodwill?"

This made Luo Chen feel somewhat absurd. He had clearly destroyed the other party's cultivation place, yet now it had come to thank him.

In the distance, the black tiger glanced at Luo Chen, then directly leapt into a still-erupting small volcano.

That glance seemed to carry a hint of warning.

"This fire spirit is quite extraordinary. I have never heard of it or seen it. Its aura far surpasses the demonic qi on He Yuan of the Yuan Demon Sect. It should be some kind of demon flame."

Luo Chen took a deep breath and no longer considered targeting these fourth-tier fire spirits.

At his current stage, he could not provoke them and did not want to provoke them.

Whoosh!

Whoosh!

Whoosh!

Three escape lights landed beside Luo Chen.

"Master!"

"Master!"

Black King and Heavenjade spoke first.

"Are you two all right?"

His first sentence asked about their condition, causing warmth to flow through the hearts of the two demons.

"We are fine. We made Master worry."

"What about that Sea-Swallowing Golden Toad?"

"Heavenjade handled matters poorly and let it escape. Master, please punish me!" Heavenjade said guiltily.

Black King coiled beside her and also looked puffed up with anger.

"That fellow was too strange. He was clearly a water-attribute demon beast like us, yet he could hide himself with magma."

That was not a demon beast, but an orthodox archaic beast!

Luo Chen shook his head and looked at Tai Sui.

With one glance, he fell slightly silent.

Tai Sui's body had changed from the earlier skin-wrapped-bone bamboo pole shape back to the form of a big-bellied fatty.

What did this represent?

Tai Sui had no desire to explain. He threw the object in his hand onto the ground.

"The Sea King Squid was killed by me."

"Cave Master took Dao Lan's strongest strike head-on and fell unconscious."

The thing thrown on the ground was Moya Cave Master, unconscious. His robes were tattered and torn, and across his chest and abdomen was a huge saber wound so deep bone could be seen.

Only a little more, and he would have been diagonally cut in two!

"As for Dao Lan, he was buried in the volcano crater. I fear no corpse or bones remain."

Luo Chen nodded.

"For him, that can also count as release."

Being enslaved by another, body not controlled by oneself, even forced to draw a blade against an old friend... This was something no powerful cultivator who had bitterly cultivated for hundreds of years could accept.

Dao Lan's death likely also contained the thought of freeing himself.

Tai Sui gave a hum. His gaze suddenly fell on Luo Chen's left arm.

"Your hand?"

"To force back a generation's Demon Emperor, how could I not pay some price?"

Luo Chen smiled slightly. Then he beckoned toward a certain direction.

A stream of light broke through the air and came.

It was precisely his left hand, severed at the wrist. Azure and blue flames were still clashing, while the flesh on the hand had already vanished completely, leaving only white bone.

Tai Sui's eyes carried some gravity.

Not because of the azure and blue flames.

But because of the powerful battle strength Luo Chen had erupted with in a short time earlier!

He could contend with a Sage!

"As expected, the powerful aura Ding Yi sensed back by Peng Lake was absolutely not accidental, nor was it caused by Peng Lake's grand formation."

Thinking this in his heart, Tai Sui comforted him verbally.

"This severed hand is already ruined. However, our kind has many methods to reconnect limbs. After we return, I can..."

His words stopped abruptly.

In his sight, Luo Chen's right hand formed seals and waved repeatedly.

The Dragon-Slaying Art was used again and again.

The blue light gradually weakened. Later, with only a brush, it completely vanished.

Afterward, the man before him attached the skeletal left hand back to the severed wrist.

Blood light surged around his body. A layer of bloody radiance covered the white bones and gradually seeped in.

A red membrane appeared, connecting the palm and arm.

Rustling sounds rang out.

Strips of flesh spread over the white bones, and tendons, meridians, and blood vessels faintly appeared. In only the blink of an eye, Luo Chen's left hand was as good as new.

Seeing this, Tai Sui involuntarily swallowed.

His gaze slowly rose and landed on the handsome face of the man before him, seeming somewhat unable to believe it.

"You..."

Luo Chen tugged at the corner of his mouth.

"I still have something to do. Fellow Daoist Tai Sui, please look after Cave Master here. I will go and return shortly."

Then he leapt up and again entered Vermilion Bird Mountain.

In a daze, his back vanished.

After he left, Tai Sui stood quietly in place.

Suddenly, his body began trembling uncontrollably.

"That was source blood!"

"An archaic beast body. He has actually refined an archaic beast body with a human body!"

"If such a body can be taken by my main body, it will surely break True Lord's restrictions and help me force my way into the inheritance land!"

...

Vermilion Bird Mountain.

Above the magma lake.

The familiar figure appeared again.

Feeling the strange numbness of his newly grown left hand, Luo Chen kept twisting his wrist.

"A method specifically targeting powerful archaic beasts. Legend says that in remote antiquity, the sharkfolk race was weak and was once enslaved by the archaic beast Kun [TERM?]. Later, the human-demon war erupted, and the archaic beast Kun vanished. From then on, the sharkfolk became independent and became a great demon race of the Northern Sea."

"I did not expect they would use their own blood oil to develop such a powerful method against archaic beasts."

"If I had not had the strengthened Withering-Bloom True Fire helping me at the time and decisively cut off one hand, I fear he truly would have succeeded."

Looking back now, that blue flame had not been absorbing his vitality at all.

It had been using the residual source power on his arm to burn, thereby suppressing the Withering-Bloom True Fire.

Regarding this, Luo Chen could only sigh that the world was vast and that no person or race could be underestimated.

Especially Shark Emperor Liujun, who was a powerful figure that had survived the demon-devil war!

Shaking his head, Luo Chen cast aside these thoughts. His gaze fell on the magma lake below, which was bubbling and gurgling.

"Is the Five Elements Lotus Platform still inside?"

"It is still there. My avatar has been following it from afar the whole time."

"Then that is excellent!"

Luo Chen's suspended heart finally settled.

During the Vermilion Bird Mountain battle earlier, Han Zhan seemed not to reveal mountains or water, but in truth, he contributed a great deal.

First, he resolved Shark Emperor's charming song for Luo Chen. Then he used the Seven Baleful Sword Formation to break open the unknown round pearl treasure the other party took out. Finally, when Luo Chen left, he released a refined-corpse avatar to follow the Five Elements Lotus Platform.

All of this sounded complex, but in reality, it had all proceeded smoothly.

The coordination between the two of them had grown increasingly tacit after many years together.

Often, no words were needed for them to understand each other's thoughts.

Suddenly, Han Zhan's tone became grave.

"That Five Elements Lotus Platform was captured by Shark Emperor Liujun earlier and is now like a startled bird. Its current hiding place is extremely dangerous. You must act carefully!"

"Hm?"

"If you are careless, you and I will both be buried here! Because the place it is now is extremely close to the legendary Vermilion Bird Fire Spirit!"

Luo Chen's whole body tensed, and his heart rose to his throat again.

*(End of Chapter)*
