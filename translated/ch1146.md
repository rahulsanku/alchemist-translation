# Chapter 1146: One Move

The third battle!

Iron Hero versus Helian Shanque.

With Xiao Feng's side having lost two consecutive matches, the importance of this third battle was beyond doubt. The attention of the entire audience converged as one.

Everyone knew that if Iron Hero lost, the position of Yixian City Lord would fall to Xiao Yun.

Of course, that situation would not arise so quickly, at least.

Whether it was Iron Hero or Helian Shanque, both had long-established reputations. The audience also had a general understanding of their relative strength.

Therefore, for this battle, with the stronger Iron Hero holding a greater chance of victory, what the audience wanted to see was merely a passionate and intense fight.

But unfortunately, Helian Shanque did not fulfill their wishes.

From the very beginning, Helian Shanque knew he was the one meant to be sacrificed. Thus, from the start to the end of the battle, there was never a life-and-death struggle where neither side would yield an inch.

The two exchanged only a dozen or so moves. After Helian Shanque tested the waters and discovered a vast gap in strength between himself and his opponent, he decisively conceded on the spot.

Such a scene naturally drew boos from the entire audience.

Too perfunctory!

Where was a martial artist's dignity?

Where was the bearing of an expert?

To this, Helian Shanque seemed oblivious, merely declaring his surrender with heartfelt sincerity.

For him, losing this match was not shameful. On the contrary, it gave him something to boast about in the future.

Because under his probing, Iron Hero, who wanted a quick victory, had unsurprisingly revealed methods of the Leakless Realm. From then on, he could tell others that he had once fought across realms and survived.

The third battle ended hastily. Before the audience could fully vent their anger at Helian Shanque's voluntary surrender, the fourth battle arrived.

It was also a battle many wanted to see!

Xiao Sanniang versus Hua Guzi.

The grudges and romantic entanglements between these two women had long been a well-known tale of romance in Yixian City.

It was because both had once vied for the affections of the same man, engaging in countless open and covert struggles.

That man was the previous city lord!

Although he had already left this place, the romantic affairs of the past were still remembered by some.

Now, the two parties in that love triangle had met again, and in a place like the martial arena. The sheer entertainment value could be seen from the cheers and shouts that soared into the sky.

"Women fighting! This is going to be good!"

Even Luo Chen, faced with a battle between two evenly matched female martial artists, couldn't help but show some interest.

This surprised Iron Hero, who had just returned dejectedly from the arena. He remembered that Luo Chen hadn't shown such interest in the previous battles, whether it was Red Flame or Granny Yu.

"Fellow Daoist Luo, what do you think of this match?"

"What do I think?" Luo Chen's lips curled slightly as he watched the two women already squaring off in the arena. He chuckled lightly, "I don't really care who wins or loses. But from the sense of qi, Hua Guzi is slightly stronger than Xiao Sanniang. Although qi doesn't represent true strength, if our side wants to win, Xiao Sanniang will have to show some special qualities."

Iron Hero raised an eyebrow and nodded almost imperceptibly.

This Luo Chen truly had exceptional insight. Without being able to use the divine sense that cultivators relied on most, he had analyzed this most critical point relying solely on the Blood River Realm of martial arts.

Moreover, he had indeed mastered the subtle method of qi sensing in advance!

If that were the case, it meant Luo Chen also possessed considerable talent in the path of martial arts.

Given enough time, this Yixian City might...

Thinking of a certain possibility in the future, Iron Hero's raised brow furrowed slightly.

Luo Chen, watching Iron Hero's expression from the corner of his eye, noticed that he wasn't overly focused on the arena. His heart stirred slightly.

"Such an attitude... it seems Xiao Sanniang really has mastered some special techniques. That's interesting."

As thoughts shifted among those offstage, the battle on the arena had already begun.

And the intensity of the fight escalated from the very start, reaching a white-hot stage.

Fists, palms, feet, and legs—they exchanged blows back and forth.

Astral qi shot in all directions, sand and stones flying.

Amidst the fierce battle, shouts and curses were occasionally mixed in.

Words like "bitch" and "slut" rang out incessantly, only exciting the audience further.

Occasionally, the sound of slapping flesh could be heard, making one wince in sympathy.

From an ordinary person's perspective, the two women seemed evenly matched. But in Luo Chen's eyes, Xiao Sanniang had actually fallen into a disadvantage. Several of her acupoints had been sealed by Hua Guzi's peculiar finger techniques, and the astral qi she could release was growing less and less.

"Those are the Flower-Plucking Fingers, a finger technique the old city lord was quite proud of. Each strike lands precisely when the enemy's astral qi is at its peak, repeatedly suppressing the enemy's strongest attacks, then tracing back to temporarily seal the acupoints." Iron Hero explained, but there was no worry in his tone.

Hearing this explanation, Luo Chen became somewhat interested in this finger technique.

In normal combat, one's own side would typically play to their strengths and avoid weaknesses, while the enemy would evade their sharp edge and strike where they were unprepared. He hadn't expected there to be a martial technique that worked in the opposite way.

But thinking about it further, he felt it did have some merit.

The stronger an enemy's attack, the greater the flaw it exposed.

If one could truly seize the opportunity and counter each time, not only would the enemy grow weaker, but their momentum and confidence in battle would also be continuously worn down.

"Finger techniques, huh? I'm quite skilled in those too!" Luo Chen murmured to himself.

One of the divine abilities he had created was a finger technique. After falling into this Great Destruction Realm and switching to martial arts, he had also learned the three finger techniques Wang Yuan had created. If he could obtain the manual for this *Flower-Plucking Fingers*, he would surely master it quickly.

It seemed that after he raised his realm to the Furnace Stage, he might visit the higher floors of the city lord's mansion's scripture pavilion to learn about the exquisite martial techniques of this world.

He wondered how they compared to the self-created martial techniques Wang Yuan had recorded in his *Divine Astral Fighting Qi*.

As he pondered, Luo Chen's gaze suddenly sharpened. The turning point in the battle had arrived!

"Ha!"

A crisp shout came from the palace-dressed beauty whom anyone could see had fallen into a disadvantage.

The next moment, Xiao Sanniang did not dodge or evade the finger Hua Guzi pointed at her. Instead, the astral qi that had been dispersing outward suddenly retracted wildly, as if she had become a massive vortex.

The surrounding astral qi began to rotate counterclockwise, and the acupoints previously sealed by the Flower-Plucking Fingers were unsealed inch by inch.

In just an instant, a red ball of light appeared in the martial arena.

Hua Guzi, caught within it, her face drained of color. She retreated frantically in panic, even losing her composure as she cried out, "Heaven-Earth Reverse Rotation, Furnace Overturned! Have you lost your mind?"

But Xiao Sanniang paid her no heed. Her tightly shut eyes snapped open, and she formed a palm, striking out from a distance.

*Boom!*

Under the gaze of the entire audience, with just one palm strike, Hua Guzi seemed bound by some strange force, unable to dodge or escape, meeting that palm head-on.

In an instant, her features twisted, and her entire body crumpled into a ball.

Then, a sound like air leaking quietly emerged.

*Chi...*

Seeing this scene, the high-level martial artists present all changed color.

Hua Guzi had been crippled!

The qi-blood furnace she had cultivated by condensing her core into astral qi had been forcibly shattered by Xiao Sanniang. Now, the astral qi throughout her body, no longer constrained, was uncontrollably dispersing and escaping from her.

On the martial arena, only Xiao Sanniang's heavy panting and Hua Guzi's sharp, piercing screams as she curled up on the ground could be heard.

Listening to those sounds, watching that scene, every onlooker felt their scalps go numb and a chill rise in their hearts.

Such ruthless methods! Ruthless to herself, and even more ruthless to her enemy!

Even on the main viewing platform, Xiao Yun stood up abruptly, his face dark as he stared at Xiao Feng.

"That martial technique is a forbidden incomplete art. Even Father couldn't complete it, and you dared let her cultivate it?"

Xiao Feng sat as steady as a mountain, replying calmly, "We both have incomplete forbidden arts. What's so surprising about that? I wonder, are you distressed for Hua Guzi, or for Xiao Sanniang? Or perhaps you're afraid the city lord position within your grasp is about to slip away?"

"You!"

Xiao Yun glared fiercely at Xiao Feng, but gradually his expression calmed, and a sneer appeared on his face instead.

"There's still one more match!"

Xiao Feng smiled slightly. "Yes, one more match. Why not sit down and enjoy it?"

"Hmph!"

Xiao Yun snorted coldly and sat down directly.

For this final match, he had absolute confidence!

Iron Tu was no mere name. He was Iron Hero's junior apprentice brother, and he also practiced the art of horizontal tempering. With the battle armor Xiao Yun had crafted for him and the several secret techniques he had taught, Iron Tu could even contend with Iron Hero himself, let alone that new guest elder!

Meanwhile, in a corner of the martial arena.

Xiao Zirui watched the young man stepping onto the arena with a mocking expression.

"Luo Chen, is it? I don't know why Xiao Feng values you so much, even giving you full access to the scripture pavilion. But if you suffer a crushing defeat at Iron Tu's hands today, I wonder what face you'll have left to stay in Yixian City."

In his ears were the roaring cheers and shouts of the crowd, filled with bloody words.

"Kill him!"

"Tear that bastard apart!"

"Iron Tu..."

...

On the martial arena, Luo Chen wore a helpless expression.

In his centuries of cultivation, he had seen many arena battles.

There were gang competitions, sect grand tournaments, and even his own Luo Tian Sect had held many inner sect trials.

But this was the first time he had stepped onto such a stage. The experience was truly peculiar.

No, actually, it wasn't the first time. Back when he was at the Initiate Stage, he had also been on an arena, but that time it was for an alchemy competition.

"Ah, whether it's martial arts or alchemy, this feeling of being watched like a monkey in a show is ultimately not very comfortable!"

Luo Chen sighed, his gaze falling on the man walking toward him step by step, clad in silver heavy armor.

The heavy armor covered his entire body, leaving only a sliver of light between his nose and eyes.

In his hands, he held a massive long blade.

The blade had no edge, but was immensely thick.

This man was Iron Tu.

Meeting Luo Chen's gaze and seeing the helplessness on his face, Iron Tu spoke in a muffled voice from beneath the heavy armor.

"No need to be helpless. I didn't expect it to drag on to a fifth match either. Don't worry, I'll end your suffering quickly."

"*Ben Zuo*?" Luo Chen laughed softly. "It's been a long time since I've heard that title. Very well, let's finish this quickly then!"

The moment the words fell, Iron Tu moved.

The heavy blade in his hand swung down, a massive blade astral qi striking from afar.

But Luo Chen stood rooted to the spot, neither dodging nor evading. He merely raised his hand and flicked a finger.

*Dong!!!*

A dull sound, like striking a bell, spread out melodiously.

That flick, as light as a feather, landed on Iron Tu but felt as heavy as the heavens.

With just that single contact, he was struck as if by a heavy blow. His entire body of horizontal tempering skills couldn't even let him hold onto his heavy blade, which flew from his hands.

As terror filled his eyes, the young man opposite him took a step forward.

Just one step!

Luo Chen seemed to shrink the distance, crossing three *zhang* to appear before him.

Then, his fingers moved like threading flowers, his hands like fluttering butterflies.

Those knobby hands struck out streams of force at him.

Iron Tu had no time to react, and his own qi hadn't even sensed anything. He merely entertained a fleeting, hopeful thought.

"Just Dan Force, not astral qi. It can't break through my Tu Battle Armor. Although he's fast, as long as I catch him once, I can tear him apart alive."

This thought came quickly and left just as quickly.

Because Iron Tu was shocked to discover that those streams of force had eerily followed the gaps in the joints of his Tu Battle Armor, drilled inside, and then seeped into his body.

Thread by thread, strand by strand, stream by stream.

In an instant, countless streams of force coiled around Iron Tu's entire body like clinging bone parasites.

When everything was ready, Luo Chen raised a palm and gently pressed down.

"Remove!"

The next moment, amidst the stunned gazes of the entire audience, the Overlord Removed His Armor!

The indestructible Tu Battle Armor clattered to the ground.

The immense force Iron Tu had previously ignored also arrived.

Just one palm!

This was no Dan Force, nor was it astral qi. It was merely the purest physical strength.

It was as if a mortal had been slapped by a black bear, stomped by an elephant, or swiped by a whale's tail.

Iron Tu staggered backward, each step leaving a footprint, retreating seven steps in total.

When he stopped, all the immense force had dissipated. But he had no thoughts left. His entire body felt limp and weak. His eyes closed, and he collapsed onto the obsidian-paved martial arena like a pile of mud.

Luo Chen didn't even glance at his outcome. He turned around, hands behind his back, and calmly walked off the stage.

The audience, which had been roaring like a tsunami just a second ago, now seemed as if someone had grabbed them by the throat, unable to make a sound.

The entire martial arena was dead silent!
