# Chapter 785: Rewards According to Merit, Third Generation Sect Master

"Zeng Yilong, you have protected the sect with merit and possess a firm will. In the time of upheaval, you stepped forward, living up to the hopes your father held before his passing. I bestow upon you a pair of superior-grade flying treasures, the Broken Moon Wings, and one attacking treasure, the Ten-Thousand-Jun Halberd!"

Under the gaze of countless eyes, Zeng Yilong suddenly lifted his head, staring in disbelief at the figure above him.

A gentle laugh came.

"What are you standing there for?"

Zeng Yilong's body trembled. He quickly stepped forward, half-bowing.

"Thank you for the bestowal, Supreme Elder!"

Two streaks of light drifted lightly before him, accompanied by Luo Chen's words of instruction in his ear.

"The Broken Moon Wings have been personally refined by me over many years. Paired with the Ninety Thousand Li I taught you in your early days, your escape speed will be extremely fast. Moreover, this treasure is not only skilled in flight but also possesses considerable offensive capability. I have already removed all personal imprints from it. Take it and perform a blood-drop recognition, then refine it carefully."

Zeng Yilong's eyes gleamed with brilliance as he carefully accepted the Broken Moon Wings.

He had watched back then as Luo Chen commissioned experts from Tianfan City to forge the Broken Moon Wings, and had even casually given him a pair of defective wings.

Unfortunately, those wings had accompanied him through a hundred years of battle, finally collapsing on the dueling platform.

He never expected that, in the blink of an eye, an even better pair would arrive!

And this great halberd…

Zeng Yilong subconsciously reached for it, and then…

*Thump!*

He fumbled, and the halberd crashed straight to the ground, smashing a hole in the hard blue bricks.

If he hadn't forcibly gripped the halberd's shaft, it would have fallen completely through.

Under the gaze of countless eyes, his face flushed red.

Luo Chen smiled slightly. "Infuse it with your astral qi, then activate it."

Zeng Yilong suddenly understood and quickly followed the instructions.

It wasn't that he was stupid; it was simply that in the current cultivation world, weapons specifically crafted for body refiners were far too rare. Almost all treasures were powered by magical power, so he had subconsciously not infused it with blood and qi.

As he channeled his astral qi into it, the immensely heavy Ten-Thousand-Jun Halberd was pulled out inch by inch.

The more he pulled, the more excited he became.

"This halberd is so heavy, so large, so powerful!"

"If I master this halberd, combined with the high speed of the Broken Moon Wings, even a mid-Adept cultivator might not be my match!"

Luo Chen glanced at Zeng Yilong, saw that he had grasped the technique, and stopped paying attention.

He wasn't too pained to give away these two treasures.

The Broken Moon Wings' limit did not stop at superior-grade treasure, but its growth speed could no longer keep up with him.

The Ten-Thousand-Jun Halberd was excellent, but he was not a pure body refiner. He had previously wielded it with the overwhelming brute force of his fourth-stage physique. It was better to give it to someone more suitable, letting it unleash sharper edge.

As one of the two leading figures of the younger generation of the Luo Tian Sect, especially since Zeng Yilong had grown up before his eyes since childhood, he deserved these treasures.

As for the other leading figure of the younger generation?

Luo Chen's gaze, complex, fell upon another man of distinguished appearance, whose expression was slightly melancholy.

"Qin Yuanjiang."

Qin Yuanjiang was startled. He hadn't expected that today's reward distribution would include him.

He stepped forward nervously.

Looking at this face, which had three parts of Qin Liangchen's masculinity and several parts of Murong Qinglian's elegance, Luo Chen sighed softly.

"It is easy to add flowers to brocade, but difficult to offer charcoal in snow. You came to our sect during its lowest point and have remained loyal for many years without leaving. This shows a chivalrous heart. Today, I bestow upon you three volumes of puppet secret manuals."

"Thank you, Supreme Elder."

Qin Yuanjiang bowed and accepted the three manuals, which looked like they had been recently transcribed. Just as he was about to put them away, he glanced at Luo Chen.

"Little Tiger, cultivate diligently. Do not be lazy!"

Qin Yuanjiang was taken aback, but Luo Chen had already begun rewarding others.

Qin Yuanjiang retreated to the back and, in confusion, glanced at the three manuals.

Two were indeed puppet manuals, with a rather archaic style, presumably from ancient times.

But the remaining one…

His pupils contracted, and his heart shook.

"How is this soul-splitting technique so similar to the legendary secret arts of the Falling Cloud Sect?"

He looked up in shock at the young man.

The other paid no attention to his gaze, continuing to announce the rewards for those who had rendered meritorious service over the past century. The treasures he brought out one by one dazzled the crowd.

"Min Longyu, you have worked tirelessly, devoted your heart and soul, and protected the sect with merit. I specially bestow upon you a Sealing Divinity Compass, containing three great formations and eighteen small formations. Also bestow a volume of formation manuals, *Stone Chamber Immortal Secrets*!"

"Li Yingzhang, you have endured hardship with perseverance, neither rushing nor lagging. When danger loomed, you turned the tide. Bestow one volume of fourth-stage water arts, various third-stage pills, and five treasures: the Canglan Dharma Robe, the Sea-Suppressing Pearl, the Flame-Avoiding Canopy, the Treasure-Dropping Copper Coin, and the Dragon-Nurturing Basin."

"Yang Pingdu, you have been loyal and protected the sect with merit. Bestow one volume of fourth-stage earth arts, various pills, and a set of Wuji Apricot-Yellow Banners!"

Treasure after treasure, dazzling to the eye.

Name after name, some expected, some surprising.

Like Qin Yuanjiang, who had joined later, or Yang Pingdu, who had also received generous rewards.

Yet there were still a few whose names had not been called.

Fu Jiusheng sat calmly, Chu Kui fidgeted uneasily, and Qu Lingjun kept his head low, his emotions unknown.

Suddenly.

"Fu Jiusheng, Chu Kui!"

The two subconsciously looked up at Luo Chen. Six eyes met, and the hidden guile in their hearts dissipated like smoke.

"Two old friends, I have not misjudged you. After I left, you were still willing to protect our sect, unlike that ambitious and treacherous Fifth Qi."

"Once, we walked side by side. Now, I have taken the step to become a Sage before you. I hope you will catch up soon."

"I specially bestow upon you a set of Nascent Soul methods!"

Fu Jiusheng and Chu Kui were stunned, their faces full of disbelief.

Nascent Soul methods!

Their aptitudes were not good, their realms were not high, and they had no exquisite techniques in hand. Adept was almost the limit they could strive for.

But if they had Nascent Soul methods, who would be willing to end up as a handful of yellow earth after five hundred years?

Two streaks of light shot from Luo Chen's fingertips, entering their brows.

"Take them and study them well. Make up for your shortcomings. Though your aptitudes are poor and your years are many, when your lifespan draws near, you will still have the strength to fight for Sagehood. Fellow Daoists, let us encourage each other!"

As the divine sense entered their minds, Fu and Chu were dazed.

Finally, they both bowed deeply to Luo Chen.

"Thank you for the bestowal, Supreme Elder!"

"Thank you for the bestowal, Supreme Elder!"

Luo Chen smiled slightly, then finally looked at the one who had lowered his head, like a child who had done wrong.

He was no longer a child, in fact. From the wrinkles on his face, he looked even older and more weathered than Luo Chen.

But no matter when, in front of Luo Chen, he was still that child who, in the slanting moon valley amidst the falling snow, had loudly recited the *Hundred Herbs Canon* to him.

"Qu Lingjun!"

Qu Lingjun slowly raised his head to look at Luo Chen, a plea in his eyes?

What was he pleading for?

Was it to receive a reward like the others, so as not to fall behind?

Or was it to not be rewarded, but rather punished, so that he could find peace of mind?

Luo Chen did not see that plea. He only said calmly, "You are my disciple, so you should naturally be under the Luo Tian Sect. From today onward, you will resign from your position as guest elder of Fenghua Palace and return to the Luo Tian Sect's fold."

This was his punishment?

Qu Lingjun's whole body trembled. He stammered, "Mas… Master, what about Fenghua Palace…"

"I will speak with the Sage there myself. But the things that connect you to Fenghua Palace must be severed by your own hand, including your second wife."

"Of course, if you do not wish to return, then let this matter rest!"

Luo Chen said calmly.

But in his words, there was a sternness that had never been there before.

Qu Lingjun's expression was dazed as he retreated.

In his life, during the Qi Refinement stage, he had been fixated on trivial matters. After reaching Initiate, he had wanted to emulate his master Luo Chen, but then chaos struck. To save the sect, he had thrown himself into Fenghua and become entangled with another woman, Meng Qiner.

Trapped by love, unable to control himself.

Unable to balance love and duty, how was he to conduct himself?

…

The distribution of rewards seemed to be over?

But unexpectedly, Luo Chen suddenly announced another piece of news.

"Yao Mingyue, the second-generation sect master of the Luo Tian Sect, took command in a time of crisis, managed the sect's affairs, and maintained the sect's lineage, ensuring that the Luo Tian inheritance was not severed. Her merit is beyond measure!"

"Now, she will step down from the position of sect master and cultivate by my side. She will form her core on a chosen day!"

"Li Yingzhang!"

Amidst a wave of murmurs, Li Yingzhang, who had just stepped back, came forward again.

"Disciple is here!"

As if he knew what was coming, he lifted his robe and knelt directly on the ground.

"From now on, you will be the third-generation sect master of the Luo Tian Sect. You must devote yourself wholeheartedly, never let the sect's honor be disgraced, and let the name of Luo Tian shine brightly!"

After saying this, Luo Chen took a deep breath.

Then, he slowly pointed a finger at Li Yingzhang.

A terrifying power filled the hall!

Li Yingzhang looked straight at Luo Chen, facing that finger directly.

He only felt a slight heat on his brow, and then it was over.

Everyone looked and saw that on his forehead, there was now a lifelike green flame mark, like a third eye suspended between his brows.

After delivering this finger, Luo Chen seemed somewhat tired.

He slowly rose, with Yao Mingyue and Wang Yuan following beside him.

"Yingzhang, you may now begin the tasks I have assigned to you."

Li Yingzhang acknowledged, and after Luo Chen had completely left, he slowly stood up.

As he rose, countless eyes followed him.

No wonder Li Yingzhang had received the most among all the rewards.

A fourth-stage technique that could be cultivated to the Sage stage, various pills, and a full five treasures!

Five treasures! Ordinary people could hardly drive even one. And Li Yingzhang already had his long-refined Chiwen Sword. Why give him so many?

At the time, people were cowed by Luo Chen's authority and only thought he was being somewhat partial.

Now it seemed, it was all to pave the way for Li Yingzhang's succession as sect master!

Everyone looked at Li Yingzhang, but when they saw the green flame mark on his face, they subconsciously averted their eyes, no longer daring to look directly at him.

Commanding respect without anger, drawing sidelong glances—such was the case.

Li Yingzhang's heart was filled with complex emotions.

Grandfather had probably never imagined that he would reach this point!

Back then, he had worried that the Luo Tian Sect would swallow the Li family, and had laid out various contingency plans, negotiating terms with Luo Chen.

He never expected that the Li family would indeed be completely integrated into the Luo Tian Sect, but that he, Li Yingzhang, would become the sect master of the Luo Tian Sect!

How strange the world was!

Li Yingzhang smiled slightly. "The Supreme Elder has given me a task, and I will need the help of my fellow disciples."

Everyone agreed in unison. This was a task handed down by the Supreme Elder; it wasn't just help, though the new sect master was being polite.

"It's very simple. Go to the various regions and invite the Sages to Hundred Li Green River to attend the Sage Ascension Ceremony of our sect's Supreme Elder, Luo Chen!"

"The Fenghua Domain has already been taken care of by Xu Xiaoliu and his team. The Supreme Elder has also already informed the White Crane Domain. Elder Wang Yuan will make a trip there when the time comes. So, that leaves three domains. Send six Adepts, two traveling together, to issue the invitations."

"This is the greatest event of our sect in a hundred years. Do not be negligent!"

As he finished speaking, Li Yingzhang's pace slowed, but his tone grew heavier.

The gathered cultivators all solemnly acknowledged!

…

"Luo Chen, are you alright?"

"It's nothing, just a bit taxing on the spirit."

Facing Wang Yuan's inquiry, Luo Chen waved his hand.

The mark he had left on Li Yingzhang's brow was not as simple as it appeared.

It contained three things!

First, the divine ability Withering-Bloom Dao Finger. Once unleashed, even an early-stage Sage would be severely injured if not killed.

Second, a Withering-Bloom Fire Seed. It had no offensive effect, but when activated, it could transform into Luo Chen's Sevenfold Blazing Heaven Rings defensive spell, enough to withstand a single blow from a Sage.

Finally, it was a strand of Luo Chen's divine sense that he had split off.

If the first two could not protect Li Yingzhang, then Luo Chen would have to show himself and negotiate with the enemy.

Combining all three into a single mark was itself a delicate task.

If Luo Chen had not now attained the level of a fourth-stage formation master, fully integrating the seven basic restrictions, it would have been difficult to achieve this level.

In particular, he had to guard against this mark affecting Li Yingzhang's cultivation, so he had put in extra effort.

Fatigue was only natural!

Luo Chen looked at the woman following behind him and smiled. "Mingyue, are you a little reluctant to give up the position of sect master?"

Yao Mingyue looked up and smiled. "A little reluctant, but as you say, my lifespan is running out. I must prioritize the Great Dao! Even if I clung to the position of sect master, I would have at most forty or fifty years of glory. Why force it?"

Having said this, she was completely relieved.

However, Yao Mingyue was also curious.

"Uncle-Master, can you really help me form my core? I'm only at the eighth layer of Initiate right now."

Her master was Sima Huiniang, so she could naturally call her Dao companion Luo Chen "Uncle-Master," which sounded even more intimate than "Supreme Elder."

Luo Chen said frankly, "Nothing in this world is ever certain. But if you have fifty years to devote to cultivation, combined with my pills and techniques, and my personal guidance, reaching the Great Perfection of Initiate is within reach. As for forming your core, that will depend on your fate."

Yao Mingyue was a little disappointed at first.

But then she immediately understood!

The chances of Luo Tian Sect cultivators forming their cores were extremely high. As long as one had cultivated the *Fine Dust Primordial Art*, there was almost no obstacle to forming a core.

This technique was extremely difficult to cultivate, and there had been precedents like Xing Zonghan who had gone mad from it.

But now she had the personal guidance of Uncle-Master Luo Chen. As the creator of this technique, he was certainly experienced. Even if she couldn't achieve great mastery, minor mastery should not be difficult.

In other words, as long as she, Yao Mingyue, reached the Great Perfection of Initiate, forming her core was a foregone conclusion!

After understanding all this, Yao Mingyue completely relaxed.

She could not forcibly hold onto the position of sect master. Under the current circumstances, the sect needed a capable and powerful leader to live up to the name "Luo Tian Supreme Sect"!

Li Yingzhang, with his tenacious character, steady handling of affairs, and extraordinary calmness in the face of major events, was undoubtedly the first choice!

She was at ease entrusting the sect to him.

She was even more at ease entrusting herself to Luo Chen.

With that, what was there to worry about?

In just a few words, Yao Mingyue's heart knot was untied.

When they reached Little Stone Flat, she entered her cave dwelling to visit her master, Sima Huiniang.

Meanwhile, Luo Chen and Wang Yuan remained outside.

"Brother Wang, I've rewarded everyone else but not you. You won't blame me, will you?" Luo Chen asked with a grin.

Wang Yuan rolled his eyes. "Between you and me, there's never been any formality. And from the look of you, you've definitely prepared something. Just give it to me!"

"Hehe."

Luo Chen waved his hand, and a large pile of items appeared on the stone table.

Books, bamboo slips, jade slips, iron tablets—all inscribed with textual information.

"What are these?"

"All the classics related to body refinement that I've collected over the past century. They cover the Northern Sea, Central Continent, and span from ancient times to the present. Some of the body arts within are extraordinary in power. I've even integrated a set of my own, the Seven Devil Lord Dispelling Hands."

"But in the end, I cannot devote myself wholeheartedly to body refinement, and I cannot fully excavate the profound mysteries within these classics. Entrusting them to you is probably the most suitable."

Wang Yuan nodded as he opened one classic after another, browsing through them.

"*Wind, Moon, Thunder, Cloud: Stepping on the Immortal Path*—Wind Leg, Moon Step, Thunder Kick, Cloud Slippers. Exquisite body art footwork, exquisite conception. If fully mastered, one could completely erase the distance gap in attacks between oneself and a cultivator using footwork and speed. Missing three techniques?"

"*Flowing Cloud Immortal-Seizing Finger*—it has a similar effect to my self-created three fingers of Immortal, Demon, and God, the Blood God Tribulation Finger, but it excels in having a domineering and fierce power beneath a complete lack of ostentation…"

"*Extreme Mountain Six Forms*? Mediocre. But the attached Extreme Mountain Body Refinement Art is somewhat interesting."

Watching Wang Yuan mutter to himself, his eyes gleaming, Luo Chen looked helpless.

He had guessed this might happen.

He had originally had another gift to give him.

Seeing him so engrossed, he didn't mention it, so as not to distract him.

He would refine it first, then give him a surprise later!

Luo Chen did not disturb Wang Yuan. He strolled leisurely on Little Stone Flat, thinking about matters related to the Luo Tian Sect.

The sect master—he had already reappointed.

Li Yingzhang had the strength, the potential, and the ability to manage a sect!

Next was his Sage Ascension Ceremony.

But the main purpose of this ceremony was not to show off power and prestige, but to build a wide network of connections.

His connections, and the Luo Tian Sect's connections.

Originally, he had wanted to return to the Eastern Wilderness and bring the Luo Tian Sect disciples directly to Central Continent.

But news of the Tianyuan Dao Sect's war against the Northern Sea had already arrived, and he knew the Dao Sect's intentions.

Central Continent was "crowded," with resources insufficient for everyone, hence the expedition to other continents.

Under such circumstances, bringing the Luo Tian Sect there would only make them cannon fodder for the conquest of foreign lands.

Since that was the case, he, Luo Chen, planned to stay for a while and properly manage the Luo Tian Sect. That way, when the time came to take people away, they would be an elite force with sufficient self-preservation ability.

As for the third matter…

Looking at Hundred Li Green River, Luo Chen shook his head.

The current Luo Tian Sect could not withstand much upheaval. Even if it didn't expand, it needed a period of rest and recuperation.

Low-level disciples needed to have their loyalty secured.

High-level cultivators, like Gu Caiyi and the other Adepts, also needed time to stabilize their realms.

At a glance, Luo Chen could see that, aside from Li Yingzhang and the older generation Fu Jiusheng, all the other Adepts had somewhat unstable magical power and unsteady realms.

"Just as well. I've been stabilizing my own realm recently anyway. I'll accompany them!"
