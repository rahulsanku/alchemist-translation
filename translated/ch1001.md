# Chapter 1001: A Sage’s Cave Dwelling, the Great Ghost King Appears

On Yanfu Mountain.

A massive tiger-shaped puppet, its blade-like wings spread wide, bounded through the mountains at tremendous speed.

Over a dozen figures stood atop it, the fierce wind whipping their robes and making them snap loudly.

Even now, Deng Taiyue was still filled with astonishment.

“This White Tiger Puppet of your sect… I daresay it rivals a late-stage Sage!”

Luo Chen glanced around as he replied, “Brother Deng’s eye is not mistaken, but the spiritual power required to activate this puppet each time is beyond what ordinary people can imagine.”

Deng Taiyue’s gaze fell upon the puppet’s torso and, most crucially, its core head. He could not help but nod to himself.

Every single one was inlaid with top-grade spirit stones!

Even in the hands of a Sage, such stones were hard currency.

In peacetime, even a first-rate Sage sect with a slightly better spiritual vein could only extract one top-grade spirit stone every ten years, without damaging the vein’s grade.

With a top-grade spirit stone as its core energy source, the puppet’s killing power was naturally astonishing.

“Did Brother Luo refine this puppet himself?”

“You flatter me. I merely obtained this item by chance from an ancient ruin.”

Luo Chen brushed it off with a casual remark.

The White Tiger Puppet beneath his feet was, naturally, the one he had once obtained from Han Zhan. If one traced its true origin, it would go all the way back to the Demon-Fall Land.

He had spent over a hundred years painstakingly unlocking its seals, bit by bit. Later, to guard against any unforeseen incidents while he was away from the Luo Tian Sect for too long, he had specially left it for Li Yingzhang. He had never expected it would be used at this very moment.

“Still, it’s good that it came!”

Luo Chen’s gaze swept across a seemingly unremarkable waterfall ahead, and his right hand rose high.

Seeing this, Cang Long, who was controlling the puppet, immediately understood. He formed a hand seal and struck it onto the White Tiger Puppet beneath them.

The next moment, as Luo Chen’s right hand fell heavily, the White Tiger Puppet, which had halted its advance, opened its massive maw.

*Whoosh!*

A white beam of light, filled with an aura of destruction, shot from its mouth, hurtling straight toward the waterfall.

*Chi!*

As if cloth were being torn, the waterfall suddenly trembled.

Then, as the white beam swept from right to left, the waterfall was severed in the middle.

The waterfall ceased to flow, and the scene within was instantly revealed.

Three figures, seated cross-legged, opened their eyes at the same moment.

“Who is it?”

“Who dares to disturb the Three Fallen Blossom Ghosts?”

“Seeking death!”

The three Ghost Kings simultaneously dropped what they were doing and shot into the air.

However, upon seeing the Luo Tian Sect party, their expressions changed drastically.

“Master, what should we do now?” Bai Meiling looked at the three Ghost Kings who had been blocked, a restless eagerness appearing on her face.

Luo Chen’s lips curled slightly. “Kill them!”

The moment the words fell, the Luo Tian Sect members all sprang into action.

Wang Yuan took the lead, his blood and qi bursting forth in full force, forming a startling pillar of blood and qi that rose like smoke.

“This one is mine. I want to test how Divine Astral Fighting Qi restrains yin-soul ghostly beings!”

The others naturally did not contend with him for it, and instead turned their attention to the remaining two Ghost Kings.

“Junior Sister Cang Long, please hold the line for me!” Ling Fengzi laughed heartily, took out his magic treasure, and charged at one of the Ghost Kings.

He was eager to display his skills and show Luo Chen his current capabilities.

This was the confidence that came after breaking through to a new realm.

Cang Long nodded slightly, holding a treasured sword in hand, and fell back to the rear.

As for the last Ghost King, it was directly entangled by Bai Meiling, who had transformed into the Nine Nether Great Python.

But comparatively, Bai Meiling was slightly weaker, for this last Ghost King was also the strongest of the Three Fallen Blossom Ghosts. If not for the Soul-Refining Banner unfolding its domain, the outcome would have been decided quickly.

On the puppet, Luo Chen said calmly, “Caiyi, go help Little Bai.”

Gu Caiyi hummed in acknowledgment, flicked her long whip, and descended straight from the puppet.

The other four Adept cultivators followed suit, forming a formation with the Soul-Refining Banner as its core, firmly suppressing that Ghost King.

The deep pool beneath the waterfall instantly became an orderly battlefield.

The Three Fallen Blossom Ghosts cried out in shock and anger from time to time.

“You people are utterly unreasonable!”

“We yield this cave dwelling to you! Spare us!”

“Who exactly are you?”

The Luo Tian Sect members paid no heed to these shouts and pleas for mercy. At least, until the Supreme Elder gave the order, they would not stop their actions.

Deng Taiyue, standing shoulder to shoulder with Luo Chen at the rear, clicked his tongue in admiration. “Brother Luo, you’re using these ghost cultivators as practice for your Luo Tian Sect disciples!”

Luo Chen hummed in affirmation. “They do need the practice. Otherwise, if a great war erupts again in the future, how will they have the strength to protect themselves?”

Hearing this, Deng Taiyue was puzzled.

The great war in the Eastern Wilderness was nearly over. Although some demon clans still lingered in the thirty-six domains of the Eastern Wilderness, the main force had ultimately retreated.

Where would another war come from?

But Luo Chen did not explain. Instead, he began to offer pointers from afar, directing the ongoing battle.

“Ghost cultivators have no physical form, hence no qi mechanism. Concentrated attacks are effective but hard to land. Use area-of-effect strikes to force them to condense into a single point.”

Wang Yuan, who was locked in a difficult struggle, suddenly saw the light upon hearing this.

The surging pillar of blood and qi, centered on him, began to churn wildly, transforming into an endless ocean of boiling blood and qi.

The boundless ghostly qi surrounding him, under this supremely yang and vigorous blood and qi, sizzled and melted away like snow under the spring sun.

The Ghost King’s true body was beginning to reveal itself.

As for the rest of the battle, Luo Chen didn’t even need to watch to know the outcome.

Body cultivators focused on concentrating their attacks into a single point, wasting nothing, creating extreme bursts of power.

Once Wang Yuan locked onto the true body, defeat was only a matter of time.

“Ling Fengzi, stop hiding your strength. If you keep testing the waters, the enemy will run away.” Luo Chen said helplessly.

This fellow, in the earlier years of the human-demon war, had been known for his life-preserving and escape techniques. Even his fighting style always left a huge margin for error, embodying the principle of retreating at the first sign of trouble.

But now, with the Luo Tian Sect holding a massive advantage, and with himself, Deng Taiyue, and even a White Tiger Puppet backing them up, continuing like this was just a waste of mana.

Ling Fengzi, who was still trading blows with the enemy in a measured fashion, heard this and burst into laughter.

“So the elder brother has noticed.”

“Very well!”

Ling Fengzi suddenly withdrew.

The enemy was stunned, and then saw Ling Fengzi, not far away, extend his left hand toward him from afar.

A black hole appeared in the center of his palm.

“Wind Cave, open!”

The next moment, an endless gale erupted from within, bursting outward.

It swept everything around, surging toward the black hole in his palm.

Even the Ghost King, who was constantly shifting forms, could not resist this immense suction force. It let out a series of miserable screams as it was sucked into the pitch-black wind cave.

In the distance.

Luo Chen saw this and raised an eyebrow.

Wind aperture!

He had once refined a special fourth-grade pill for Ling Fengzi, called the [Wind Aperture Pill].

This pill had no effect on improving a cultivator’s cultivation base. Its sole purpose was to open a new aperture in the human body.

Its name was the Hidden Wind Aperture.

This aperture could accommodate Immaculate Wind and, by drawing upon the cultivator’s entire mana reserve to wash over it again and again, enhance its power.

Back then, Luo Chen had judged that after opening this aperture, Ling Fengzi’s strength might rise by a whole tier.

But judging by what happened later, his combat power hadn’t improved much. The only thing worth mentioning was probably that his life-preserving escape techniques had become even more masterful.

Even a Great Demon King, the Seven Rhinos, had chased Ling Fengzi all the way to Baili Qingchuan before he escaped.

Now it seemed that the Hidden Wind Aperture wasn’t ineffective at boosting combat power.

It was just that Ling Fengzi, back then, had chosen between improving combat power and life-preservation, opting for the latter in order to survive the human-demon war.

And now, with his breakthrough to the mid-Sage stage, he had finally developed the combat techniques it deserved.

“Sweeping everything away, extracting the soul and refining the spirit—with just this one move, he can threaten ordinary fifth-layer, even sixth-layer Sages.”

Luo Chen nodded inwardly, but he wasn’t particularly wary.

This move was clever, but it had a fatal flaw.

If used against him, all he needed to do was release a wisp of Withering-Bloom Divine Fire. The fire would ride the wind’s momentum and burn his Hidden Wind Aperture to ashes.

He turned and looked at the other side, speaking directly.

“Little Bai, the Soul-Fusion Secret Art I taught you—if not now, when?”

Even with the help of the five-person formation led by Gu Caiyi, Bai Meiling was still at a disadvantage.

Hearing this, she immediately changed her tactics.

With a single cry of “Fuse,” the Soul-Refining Banner’s fabric rolled high, and an endless stream of ghostly qi surged toward Bai Meiling.

This was different from the Soul-Fusion Secret Art that Luo Chen had taught.

When Luo Chen performed the Soul-Fusion Secret Art, he used the ghostly qi and a large number of souls within the Soul-Refining Banner to condense a powerful Ghost King.

Bai Meiling, however, used herself as the center to bear the boundless power.

In an instant, a massive Ghost King crouched down, its feet planted in the deep pool.

Its aura began to rise continuously, from the early fourth-grade to the mid-grade, even pushing infinitely toward the late stage.

“Enough!”

Luo Chen shouted in a low voice, waking Bai Meiling, who was currently the main soul.

The power of countless ghost souls was indeed formidable, but it was also impure.

Bai Meiling, in her current state, simply could not continue to improve. Otherwise, she would lose herself and turn into a mindless vengeful spirit or fierce ghost.

Although she could not continue to improve, it was already more than enough.

Bai Meiling let out a sharp screech. Her massive ghostly body slowly stood up. With a single motion, she broke through the formation formed by Gu Caiyi and the other four. She raised her great hand and grabbed at the enemy.

With Luo Chen’s verbal guidance, in just a short moment, the three battlefields had undergone earth-shaking changes.

Deng Taiyue, watching from the side, felt his eyelids twitch wildly.

He was both amazed by Luo Chen’s vast knowledge and experience, able to effortlessly command any combat technique, and impressed by the strength displayed by this group of Luo Tian Sect Sage-level powerhouses.

Especially!

The immense soul foundation that Luo Chen had revealed while overseeing the three battlefields was likely only the tip of the iceberg.

Looking at Luo Chen’s focused profile, Deng Taiyue was deeply shaken.

“Those rumors… there was no exaggeration at all!”

And the battle situation, under Luo Chen’s guidance and Deng Taiyue’s shock, gradually drew to a close.

Divine Astral swept the four fields.

The wind stopped. The ghost clouds dissipated.

The massive Ghost King stood with both feet planted in the deep pool, emitting a series of sharp screeches that forced the previously severed waterfall to reverse its flow, surging back up a thousand feet.

“Stop screeching, stop screeching! Do you want to shake Caiyi and the others to death?”

Luo Chen spoke helplessly.

Bai Meiling giggled. Her massive ghostly body instantly dissipated, transforming back into a pitch-black Soul-Refining Banner.

This banner had absorbed another fourth-grade Ghost King, and its color had become even deeper.

“Supreme Elder! There’s a cave dwelling here, its protective formation mostly broken!” Min Longyu exclaimed joyfully, calling out from afar.

The group rode their flying lights to the middle of the waterfall.

There, a simple and archaic gate was indeed set into the rock face.

Luo Chen looked at the cave dwelling’s gate and slowly nodded.

“Yanfu Mountain is the sacred mountain of the Nether Origin Sect. Ordinarily, besides the Three Great Ancestors residing in the Yanfu Heavenly Palace, there were also many Sages living and cultivating here. This place should be the cultivation cave dwelling of a Sage.”

“Back then, the Three Great Ancestors used the Netherwater Spring Eye to suppress and refine Qinghe Immortal. They dismissed most of the Sages from the mountain, and those Sages left in an orderly fashion.”

“So, there probably isn’t much good stuff left inside.”

Hearing this, the crowd was enlightened, but also filled with regret.

If they could obtain the legacy of a Sage-level powerhouse, it would be a considerable help to the current Luo Tian Sect.

Min Longyu looked at the broken guardian formation and said regretfully, “So, are we just going to leave this place alone?”

Luo Chen smiled. “Our goal this time is only the Netherwater Spring Eye. Before the Netherwater Spring Eye, all resources, treasures, and even the Nether Origin Sect’s inheritance cannot compare to it. Of course, leaving it for others is not our style either.”

As he spoke casually, he raised his hand and sent out a series of seals, landing them on the broken guardian formation.

In an instant, the seals merged into it, beginning to fuse and supplement at an extremely fast pace.

*Boom!*

The severed waterfall came crashing down once more.

This signified that the formation was once again complete.

As the group flew back, they stared at Luo Chen in astonishment.

“Such a fast seal technique!”

“In that instant just now, the Supreme Elder must have unleashed over a hundred seals!”

“I never thought that the Pill Sect would have such profound attainments in the Dao of Formations!”

Looking back at the waterfall, Luo Chen nodded.

“Let’s go. If we obtain the Netherwater Spring Eye, this Yanfu Mountain will also become property of the Luo Tian Sect. There will be plenty of time later to explore these cave dwellings one by one.”

With a wave of his hand, the group once again boarded the White Tiger Puppet.

As for the Mystic Turtle?

Before setting out, Luo Chen had already sent it back to the Dan Saint Blessed Land to report their safety to Li Yingzhang and the others.

As they set off again, a cheerful atmosphere prevailed on the White Tiger’s back.

To prevent the group from becoming arrogant and complacent, Luo Chen offered a slight reminder.

“These ghost cultivators, though they have lived for ages, have been suppressed for a thousand or ten thousand years. Their techniques are not very refined. Moreover, lacking suitable protective magic treasures and true artifacts, their true strength cannot be compared to orthodox Sages of the outside world.”

“With Taiyue Sage and myself backing you up from the rear, they were also extremely wary, which is why you fought so easily.”

“But if you fight orthodox cultivators in the future, it won’t be so simple.”

These words were spoken with considerable weight. The group’s expressions sobered, and the earlier cheerful atmosphere subsided somewhat.

Along the mountain path, the winged White Tiger leaped and bounded, climbing upward at great speed.

Thanks to the ghost cultivators who had cleared the way, most of the seals and formations had been broken over the past ten years. Thus, the Luo Tian Sect party encountered little obstruction.

Occasionally, they came across scattered ghost cultivators, and Luo Chen had his subordinates frequently engage them, either eliminating them or driving them away.

In this process, the combat techniques of these nine Luo Tian Sect cultivators became increasingly proficient.

Deng Taiyue, however, was somewhat puzzled.

If Luo Chen was so intent on exhausting his subordinates’ mana, how would they deal with a real battle later?

Would it just be the two of them, himself and Luo Chen?

The White Tiger leaped, crossing the clouds, and a vast white jade square could faintly be seen floating above.

Suddenly!

A ghostly claw, every detail vividly clear, descended from the sky, grabbing at the group.

It came without a trace, fell without a sound.

The Xuanhuang Great Seal suddenly materialized, blocking above.

*Chi!*

Deng Taiyue frowned and withdrew his magic treasure. On its surface were five deep claw marks.

“It’s a Great Ghost King, comparable to a late-stage Sage!”

Luo Chen looked up calmly, locking eyes for a single instant with a figure that had burst into the white jade square.

“So, this is the one who scattered my Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain technique earlier!”
