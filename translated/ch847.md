# Chapter 847: Nether Origin’s Indulgence, the Heaven-Earth Peak That Can Never Be Returned To

Alchemy Sect Leader Luo Chen had received a blow from Nether Origin’s Thunder Daozi?

This lightning-fast exchange had caught everyone off guard.

And the result was even more unbelievable!

Who was Thunder Daozi?

Under the Nether Origin Sacred Land’s Ascendants, he had the greatest reputation, the strongest strength, and was likewise the cultivator in the Eastern Wilderness with the highest hope of advancing to the Ascendant realm.

Since emerging on the scene, he had almost no record of defeat.

Rumor had it that he was that one-in-ten-thousand peerless genius who could comprehend the true meaning of laws at the Sage realm.

And his recent battle record proved his capability without exception.

Pursuing and killing Brocade Immortal, defeating Bi Zheng and Kuang Kuang when they joined forces, capturing Gao Chang alive from amidst ten thousand demons!

Such battle achievements almost had the style of the Azure Frost Demon Emperor as rumored.

And faintly, some had already crowned him with the reputation of being invincible under the Ascendants in the Eastern Wilderness.

Such an existence could not be opposed except by cultivators of the same realm. Even if it was just a casual strike, it was absolutely not something that weak cultivators could receive.

Yet right before their eyes, Alchemy Sect Leader Luo Chen, famous throughout the world for his alchemy, had silently and without revealing his hand, blocked this blow.

Who wouldn’t be utterly shocked?

Heavenly Capital Master’s face carried an expression of admiration, and he murmured inwardly: “Although Thunder Daozi didn’t use his full strength, Luo Chen’s response truly surprised this old man.”

Nine Spirit Primordial Lord’s expression was grave as he deeply scrutinized Luo Chen, as if meeting this friend for the first time.

Lin Qingxuan pursed his lips, grateful that he hadn’t haggled with Luo Chen before.

Fenghua Fairy was quite astonished; she was certain that the strength Luo Chen had displayed in that instant surpassed even the great battle at Heavenly Capital Domain. Was it hidden strength, or progress made in a short time?

Divine Fire Sage and Motian Old Ghost exchanged glances and nodded to each other.

That strange flame again!

One after another, gazes carried different meanings.

But after all were cast upon Luo Chen, it was like stars surrounding the moon, the hopes of the multitude resting upon him, making his seated figure, calm as a mountain, seem much more imposing.

And under this boost of momentum, the weight of the question Luo Chen asked again became decisive.

“Now, can your sect give us an explanation!”

Thunder Daozi slowly withdrew his hand, sizing up Luo Chen with great interest, as if somewhat surprised.

As for this question, he instead became less concerned than before.

“This war has already dragged on too long. If my Nether Origin does not move, that is one thing, but if we move, we must pacify the demon chaos. Rather than continuing to drag things out, it’s better to concentrate our forces for a single decisive battle. That captive ghost is the best bait.”

Luo Chen frowned. “Are you certain the demon race will attack with all their strength?”

Thunder Daozi smiled confidently. “That captive ghost has that weight! Its original body was a desolate beast variant, a Devouring Origin Beast. Born to devour the origin qi of heaven and earth, it especially favors the dao fruits of cultivators, even feeding on them. When the Ancient Demon Emperor Heaven Transformation underwent his tribulation, it tried to ambush him, but instead had its physical body destroyed and was taken as a captive ghost.

For many years, the Ancient Demon Emperor Heaven Transformation had been seeking a method to cultivate this ghost. Later, he finally found one—having it devour the Nascent Souls of human cultivators could allow it to advance. Once this demon broke through to the Ascendant realm, Heaven Transformation would effectively have an Ascendant-level captive ghost.

He couldn’t resist this temptation, so even though there was an unspoken rule during the human-demon war that Ascendant-level powerhouses could not act, he still secretly sent out his captive ghost to ambush us Sage-level cultivators.”

This secret history revealed left everyone exchanging glances.

Desolate beasts in the current era had already declined; even if they existed, they were mostly in the desolate wilderness.

Now, a captive ghost whose original body was a desolate beast variant had actually appeared?

And it was born to devour the origin qi of heaven and earth, even feeding on the dao fruits of cultivators?

What was a cultivator’s dao fruit?

The foundation of cultivation, the golden core of the Dao, the Three Treasures Nascent Soul, and even the primordial spirit—all could be said to be the condensation of a cultivator’s lifelong dao fruit.

In other words, that so-called Devouring Origin Beast was born with immense malice toward cultivators!

“According to my research, after this beast turned into a captive ghost, having lost its physical body, it suffered a great loss of vitality. But up to now, it has already re-cultivated to the late Sage realm, only one step away from Ascendant.”

Thunder Daozi spoke freely, truly giving everyone a detailed explanation.

Then, he waved his hand grandly.

“Therefore, the Ancient Demon Emperor Heaven Transformation will absolutely not give up the captive ghost he has cultivated for a thousand years. Even if he has to mobilize the full strength of the Eastern Wilderness demon race, he will definitely try to take it back!”

“And this is the opportunity for the final decisive battle to arrive!”

“Everyone, I have said my piece. Do you understand now?”

No one spoke. Each person was digesting the astonishing news they had received in such a short time.

No wonder Lingtian Pass had been on high alert recently!

No wonder the Nether Origin Sect had sent a large number of cultivators into the city, with even the number of Sage-level cultivators exploding from the initial ten-plus to thirty. Even the Grand Cultivators, including the Lingtian City Lord, now numbered six!

So they were preparing for the final decisive battle!

*Thump!*

The sound of a table being struck rang out.

Everyone looked toward the source—it was Alchemy Sect Leader Luo Chen.

They suddenly realized that it was fortunate that Alchemy Sect Leader had taken the lead in initiating this gathering, forcing the Nether Origin Sect to give an explanation.

Otherwise, when they suddenly faced the decisive battle between the two races, they might not even be mentally prepared, and might suffer heavy losses.

Luo Chen looked directly at Thunder Daozi and spoke slowly:

“That captive ghost is so important that it might even force the Eastern Wilderness demon race to fight with all their strength. If they cannot take Lingtian Pass, will the leading Ancient Demon Emperor Heaven Transformation disregard the unspoken rule that Ascendant powerhouses cannot act, and personally take action against us? In that case, how will your sect handle the situation?”

He asked the question that concerned everyone the most.

Facing a room full of tense faces, Thunder Daozi laughed heartily.

Suddenly, the laughter stopped.

“Simple. My Nether Origin’s great power will naturally take action against him!”

“Everyone, I have said all there is to say. Prepare well for the upcoming battle!”

After speaking, Thunder Daozi strode away, paying no more attention to the cultivators in the hall who were alternating between surprise and joy.

After he left, a heated discussion broke out in the hall regarding this matter.

Gradually, everyone’s attention focused on Luo Chen, who had stood up for them earlier.

“Alchemy Sect Leader, what do you think we should do?”

Facing a room full of anxious faces, Luo Chen sighed.

“As Thunder Daozi said, this war, like a quagmire, has dragged us down for far too long. It would naturally be best to resolve it quickly.”

“Before that final decisive battle, we should make as many preparations as possible.”

“This Dao Discussion Meeting today cannot be held in vain. If any fellow daoists have resources they don’t need, feel free to trade them to strengthen each other’s strength.”

Everyone nodded, saying that Alchemy Sect Leader was absolutely right.

Although they feared that final decisive battle, in truth, everyone also looked forward to it in their hearts.

Because it had truly dragged on for far, far too long!

Even if only a little over a hundred years had passed in reality, that pressure of never knowing if they would survive, hanging over their heads at every moment, still made it hard to breathe.

Only by truly ending this war could each person and each faction find release.

Divine Fire Sage sat in the corner, watching Luo Chen speak freely, a hint of fear in his eyes.

When had this fellow been able to sit at the head and issue orders, with everyone acknowledging his words?

Had his prestige really become this high?

...

City Lord’s Mansion!

The Lingtian City Lord looked at Thunder Daozi, who had just returned, and asked directly: “Did you tell them everything?”

“I did. What should have been announced by you, Senior Brother, this junior brother has overstepped this time.” Thunder Daozi replied with a smile.

The Lingtian City Lord shook his head. “There’s no such thing as overstepping. It was only a matter of sooner or later. But I heard that you had a conflict with Luo Chen?”

“It wasn’t a conflict, just a test of skill.” Thunder Daozi waved his hand, then revealed an interested expression. “If I hadn’t tested him, I wouldn’t have known. After testing, I discovered that this man is deeply hidden and truly worthy of my Nether Origin Sect’s regard!”

The Lingtian City Lord smiled. “After all, he is the one who disrupted the Tianyuan Dao Sect’s five-hundred-year grand competition. After returning to the Eastern Wilderness, he successively earned the two titles of Alchemy Sect Leader and Demon-Slaying Sage. How could he be a man of undeserved reputation?”

However!

“No!” Thunder Daozi shook his head, his expression solemn as he said: “What I mean by deeply hidden is that he may have already comprehended the true meaning of a law!”

The Lingtian City Lord was stunned.

Thunder Daozi paced in the courtyard, muttering to himself.

“Junior Brother Chongming received an imperial decree and went to the Central Continent to observe Chu Yan’s ascension ceremony.”

“Chu Yan did not forget old ties and bestowed a Heavenly Secret Pill upon Junior Brother Chongming. According to him, there were five Heavenly Secret Pills in total at the time. Three were left at the Tianyuan Dao Sect, and the last one was given to Luo Chen, whose Dao name was still Wilderness Wanderer at the time.”

“The Ancestor specifically ordered that after the war, Luo Chen be brought back to the Nether Origin Sect. Initially, it was also for that Heavenly Secret Pill. Later, he was attracted by Luo Chen’s alchemy skills, hoping that he would replace Chu Yan and become the new generation Alchemy Saint of my Nether Origin Sect.”

“But now it seems that Luo Chen has long since taken the Heavenly Secret Pill and comprehended the true meaning of a law. Otherwise, with just his mid-Sage realm cultivation, he could not have blocked my blow!”

The Lingtian City Lord swallowed his saliva, a look of disbelief and envy in his eyes.

“Do you know what true meaning he has comprehended?”

Thunder Daozi frowned. “I can’t tell. Logically, it should be the true meaning of fire. But from his attack, his dharma power did not have a corresponding boost.”

The Lingtian City Lord hesitated. “Could it be that you misjudged?”

Thunder Daozi waved his sleeve. “I absolutely did not misjudge. That palm strike of his must have contained something higher than ordinary dharma power. It looked like origin true fire, but it was actually higher than any true fire a Sage-level cultivator could master. Only the true meaning of a law can explain it.”

The Lingtian City Lord fell silent.

But in his mind, he couldn’t help but recall the scene when Luo Chen had first appeared back then.

One move had refined Motian Old Ghost’s true artifact, Motian Cliff.

“Junior Brother, you might really be mistaken. Luo Chen does indeed possess an extremely powerful origin true fire.”

Thunder Daozi was stunned for a moment, then said incredulously: “The true meaning of thunder that I have comprehended is the most domineering true meaning of law in the world. What kind of true fire could resist it? A fifth-grade divine fire? Haha, how could Luo Chen, merely a Sage-level cultivator, possibly master a fifth-grade divine fire?”

Thunder Daozi still insisted on his own view, believing that Luo Chen had comprehended some extraordinary true meaning of law.

Seeing that he could not convince the Lingtian City Lord, he no longer pressed the issue, only saying “You wouldn’t understand” to end the topic.

The three words “You wouldn’t understand” caused the Lingtian City Lord’s expression to darken.

Thunder Daozi paid no attention to this senior brother’s mood, instead bringing up another topic.

“Luo Chen’s prestige in the Eastern Wilderness seems to be a bit too high!”

The Lingtian City Lord agreed indifferently: “He has always had a good reputation and has built extensive connections. It’s only natural that his prestige is a bit higher.”

“No!” Thunder Daozi frowned. “It’s excessively high. Today, the crowd looked to him as their leader, including some who didn’t have a good relationship with him. If we let this continue, it will be difficult for us to forcefully bring him back to the Nether Origin Sect.”

The Lingtian City Lord revealed a surprised expression.

He had not been present at the Shouyang Dao Discussion today, and the news he had received was relayed by others, so it was somewhat incomplete.

For his junior brother to be so grave, it seemed that some unforeseen incident had indeed occurred during the meeting!

...

The Shouyang Dao Discussion did not last for several days as before.

It ended hastily after only one day and one night.

After all, the current background was that the entire city was under martial law, preparing against a full-scale demon race attack.

But all those who had attended left satisfied.

Whether it was inside information or trading resources, everyone had gained something.

And in this regard, Luo Chen was undoubtedly the most dazzling person.

This was evident from the fact that almost everyone took the initiative to bid him farewell when the meeting ended.

Those who accompanied Luo Chen to the Dao Discussion Meeting were Gu Caiyi and Wang Yuan.

They had been outside earlier, communicating with others, and only noticed this anomaly when the meeting ended.

“Husband, why do I feel that those Sage-level cultivators respect you greatly?”

Luo Chen controlled the dharma cloud, carrying the two of them as they flew toward Danluo Mountain.

He asked calmly in return: “Hasn’t it always been this way?”

Gu Caiyi shook her head. “There’s still some difference from usual. Brother Wang, don’t you think so?”

Wang Yuan hummed in agreement. He had also noticed the difference.

Previously, those Sage-level cultivators might have respected Luo Chen, but it was often only superficial.

Not like today, which carried a somewhat heartfelt meaning.

At this, the corners of Luo Chen’s mouth lifted slightly, but he did not explain too much.

He had finally reached this step after all!

Since returning to the Eastern Wilderness, he had used his alchemy skills to build extensive connections in all directions.

In successive great battles, he had killed countless demons, and his strength had gained the recognition of others.

If he continued like this, at most others would curry favor with him, but it wouldn’t count as respect, let alone looking to him as their leader.

But coincidentally, after several great battles, the power structure among the Eastern Wilderness powerhouses had changed.

A large number of those at the same level as him had either fallen or been injured.

And those above his level—Divine Origin Old Thief paid no attention to worldly affairs, only appearing during wartime.

Heavenly Capital Master had originally been highly respected, repeatedly speaking out for various sects and factions, vaguely bearing the responsibility of representing the major powers in negotiations with the Nether Origin Sect.

But after the Heavenly Capital Sect suffered a heavy blow and the Lingtian Pass grand formation was destroyed, his personal spirit and personal prestige had both inevitably declined.

Apart from those Grand Cultivators of the Nether Origin Sect, there was another Grand Cultivator in the Eastern Wilderness—the Supreme Elder of the Eastern Sun Sect.

But this person not only refused to leave the Eastern Sun Sect, even if he did come out, he would definitely stand on the Nether Origin Sect’s side.

Under these circumstances, no one dared to step forward!

Even though Deng Taiyue was at the sixth layer of the Sage realm, even though Nine Spirit Primordial Lord was said to be comparable to a Grand Cultivator, their personal prestige was still insufficient.

Luo Chen seized this gap, using the name of Alchemy Sect Leader to stand up for the crowd, leveraging the public’s expectations to force the Nether Origin Sect to step forward.

Especially, he had used the power of the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire to forcefully receive a blow from Thunder Daozi.

In this way, fame and strength combined, along with his style of conduct, had faintly replaced Heavenly Capital Master, making him the person with the highest prestige within Lingtian Pass, aside from the Nether Origin Sect cultivators!

This was what Heavenly Capital Master had once possessed, and what Shouyang Mountain Hermit had once yearned for, now resting on Luo Chen’s shoulders.

Most importantly, through yesterday’s Dao Discussion Meeting, he had probed the Nether Origin Sect’s attitude toward him.

Tolerance, even indulgence.

Faintly, the intention to recruit him had never ceased!

When they arrived at Danluo Mountain, after Gu Caiyi and Wang Yuan left at his command, Luo Chen stood alone at the mountain peak, his smile gradually fading.

“With such overtures of goodwill, that Heaven-Earth Peak… I’m afraid I can never return to it.”
