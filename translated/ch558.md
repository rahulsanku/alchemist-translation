# Chapter 558: Gone Without a Trace, Leaving Xuanyan

In the black night sky, a streak of escape-light cut across like a falling star.

*Whoosh.*

Abruptly, the light seemed to exhaust itself and dropped into a forested island below.

Wild beasts roared among the trees — but the moment they sensed the enormous spiritual pressure radiating from that descending light, they fell to the ground, tails between their legs, trembling.

By moonlight, a figure in rich, distinguished robes appeared within the forest.

Upright posture, fine features — the senior brother of Feiyun Ravine, Gu Shaoshang.

His mana was utterly depleted. His face was pale with shock.

He looked back toward the distant horizon. Nothing.

Only then did he let out a long, involuntary breath.

"Phew..."

The drawn-out exhale spoke to the turmoil within.

His face wore a lingering aftershock of real fear.

"A Sage Sage."

"If my senses weren't wrong, that was unmistakably the aura only a Sage Sage could project. Faint as it was, it was nearly identical in quality to Father's domain pressure."

"No wonder that person dared fish in troubled waters right at the dragon's mouth, walking away with everything. I had no idea there was a hidden Sage-level elder involved."

"I set a trap expecting a rabbit, and nearly caught a tiger. Truly insane of me."

His thoughts churned. Beyond the fear, he felt considerable relief.

Seeing no pursuers, Gu Shaoshang relaxed a fraction and checked his condition.

Pure all-out flight had consumed most of his mana. In this state, traveling alone over open sea was inadvisable.

He took a pill immediately, settled cross-legged on the spot, and began recovering.

As his mana gradually returned and his mind grew calmer, Gu Shaoshang found something nagging at him.

"If that truly were a Sage Sage, there'd be no need to hide so carefully. One person of that level could sweep the entire Xuanyan Sea Region clean by themselves."

"All that concealment — skulking beneath the formation's eyes — clearly speaks to insufficient personal strength."

"Could my senses have been wrong?"

"No, they weren't. Compared to the pressure Father occasionally lets slip, it was genuinely at the same level. But that being the case..."

Mid-puzzlement, a possibility suddenly struck him.

*A soul-refining banner.*

Could it be that the wisp of Sage aura had merely been a Sage-level soul *inside* a soul banner?

And the one actually controlling the banner was himself only an Adept?

Only that explanation could account for someone who possessed that level of power choosing not to engage him directly — and instead releasing just enough aura to frighten him away.

Just like himself: he carried trump cards from his father at the Sage level, yet he still acted with care and restraint, not deploying that lifesaving technique unless absolutely necessary.

"A soul banner containing a Sage-level soul is no longer an ordinary Myriad Souls Banner. That would have to be called a Hundred-Thousand Fierce Souls Banner."

"A vicious soul like that would be extremely difficult for any Adept to properly command. Releasing a single wisp of its aura may well have been the controller's absolute limit."

"By that reckoning, I was far too cautious."

Thinking about how that excessive caution had cost him the Xuanyan Demon Emperor's relic, Gu Shaoshang couldn't help but let regret show on his face.

He could have used his father's trump card and staked everything on a direct contest.

If he had won, the Demon Emperor's relic was worth ten, a hundred times any ordinary Ring-Headed Turtle material. His father's chances of crossing the Fallen Sea would have multiplied severalfold. And in his father's joy at the news, rewards would surely have poured down on him.

But at the last moment, he had flinched.

He had returned alone to the Xuanyan Sea Region — that alone took genuine courage. Yet to flinch at the very last step — anyone would find that regrettable.

"Forget it. In the vast Northern Sea, a cultivator possessing a Hundred-Thousand Fierce Souls Banner of that caliber would never be someone to trifle with. Nine chances in ten it's a remnant of the Primordial Demon Sect."

"If I'd faced them head-on — winning wasn't certain. And if things escalated and drew in the five royal crab clans, the situation would only get worse."

"With my aptitude, reaching the Nascent Soul stage holds genuine promise. There's no reason to risk my life in a place like this."

Having talked himself back into equilibrium, Gu Shaoshang set aside the regret and turned his full attention to recovering.

...

Back on Xuanyan Island — or rather, where it had been.

After the great battle, devastation lay in every direction.

Though "devastation" was a loose word for it now. Xuanyan Island had sunk beneath the sea. The ocean's currents had smoothed out the most grotesque battlefield scars, making them somehow less horrible to look at.

But the wounds on the survivors of the demon crab side remained enough to turn the stomach.

Jin Hun lay within the fractured remnants of his five-color shell, listless.

He had weathered the tribulation lightning that had concentrated mainly on the five patriarchs — by relying on the shell's protection — but the lightning energy coursing through his body still left him miserable.

Beside him, one demon crab reported the battle's losses in a steady voice.

Listening to number after number of staggering casualties, Jin Hun's heart sank lower and lower.

Abruptly, he cut the calm-voiced demon off.

"Golden Armor. Did I make a mistake?"

Golden Armor was briefly startled, then responded with clear conviction: "The patriarch made no mistake. This battle was costly — but a bloody victory is still a victory."

Some of the weight in Jin Hun's chest lifted.

But still...

"The Xuanyan Demon Emperor's relic, taken by another..."

Golden Armor said with firm deliberateness: "Patriarch, there's no need to keep dwelling on it. A Black Tortoise shell holds limited relevance to our clan. What truly matters is this fourth-tier spirit vein land — and the resources of this entire sea region."

Jin Hun's eyes sharpened. He looked at Golden Armor.

The great demon king lifted his head and spoke at an unhurried pace. "Xuanyan Island may have sunk, but the spirit vein's foundation remains. It can still support a third-tier Perfected Completion demon beast through to the transformation stage. Furthermore, the Azure Emperor Crab and Nine-Clawed Poison King Crab clans have suffered losses even heavier than ours. If we seize this moment to absorb their forces — our Overlord Crab clan not only recovers, but advances further."

Jin Hun fell into thought.

If things went that way — even without achieving the original five-clans-united plan and sole dominance over the Xuanyan Sea Region — they could still emerge as the undisputed hegemon. The Overlord Crab clan's supremacy would be secured.

He'd been letting himself get caught in a dead end.

"In your assessment — when should we move on the two clans?"

"Sooner rather than later. We must strike while their patriarchs are freshly fallen and no new leader has emerged."

"Who leads the action?"

When it came to this, Jin Hun's gaze conveyed a clear meaning as he looked at Golden Armor.

In ordinary times, the Overlord Crab clan's external campaigns were always led by the formidable Golden Claw.

And Golden Claw had long been Jin Hun's chosen candidate for the next patriarch — he had even intended to provide the clan's deep resources to support Golden Claw's breakthrough to the transformation stage.

An ideal situation: before breaking through, Golden Claw conquers two clans and expands the territory, and then receives cultivation resources as a justified reward.

But this time, an unexpected complication had arisen.

Golden Armor stepped forward. Two intact claws came together.

"Golden Armor is willing to take this command."

Jin Hun raised an eyebrow. "You excel at internal management, not external warfare."

Golden Armor showed no fear. "There is no Overlord Crab who cannot fight. And furthermore —"

Here he paused, then said without any attempt to hide his meaning: "Furthermore — Golden Claw's right claw is severed. His strength is greatly diminished. And after that human cultivator's ambush, he took serious wounds on top of it and cannot move freely. Under these circumstances, I am the natural choice to step forward in an emergency — it is entirely proper."

Jin Hun was silent for a while.

Then he nodded slowly.

"Go then. Move quickly — don't let Red Flame and Zhu Xiang catch on."

"Understood."

Golden Armor left the five-color shell, assembled its force, and moved out directly.

Old patriarch Jin Hun remained inside the shell and quietly sighed.

A war planned over many years, and at the final moment, so many things had gone wrong.

First Hailo refining the Xuanyan Armor into himself. Then Qihuan's absolute, unwavering determination — willing to fail the tribulation just to drag the crabs down with him. And finally, a bolt from nowhere: a human cultivator sitting in the shadows, collecting everything.

That human cultivator especially.

The earlier surprises, though unexpected, had a certain logic to them. In a war of extinction, some defiance was natural.

Had that human cultivator not appeared, the Overlord Crab clan would still have come out as the biggest winner by far.

But that person had appeared, snatched the Demon Emperor's relic, and with one palm killed three and wounded four.

Thinking of that palm strike — like an azure sun blazing through the rainstorm — Jin Hun's heart clenched.

That strike had been gathered for a long time. Released at the perfect moment.

Even in full health, taking a palm like that meant death or grave injury. As for Golden Claw's group — already struggling under the tribulation — the result was obvious.

Three dead in one stroke. Three severely wounded. Only Golden Armor, with its extraordinary defensive capabilities, had escaped with light wounds.

"Don't let me find out who that person is. When I do — I'll pay it back tenfold."

A cold gleam passed through those aged eyes.

Then, as if remembering something, he sent out a transmission.

Shortly after, a demon crab crawled inside.

"Patriarch, what are your instructions?"

"What are the other four clans doing right now?"

The demon crab seemed to have the answer prepared. "The Azure Emperor Crab clan is in mourning over their patriarch's death — the whole clan in grief. The Nine-Clawed Poison King Crab clan is in chaos, fighting over succession. As for the Red Rock Crabs, there's something unusual — it appears they've been summoning forces from their home territory, with plans to set up camp at Xuanyan Island's old site."

None of that surprised Jin Hun. All of it was predictable.

The first two were ideal timing for Golden Armor to strike. The third — he could negotiate with that old Red Flame.

But the Demon Spider Crabs?

His subordinate was equally baffled.

"Oddly enough, they left only a small number of demon crabs at the old Xuanyan Island site. The main force followed patriarch Zhu Xiang and departed the area in a hurry."

"Oh?" Jin Hun frowned with genuine puzzlement.

At a time like this — instead of staying to divide the spoils — running away?

What was Zhu Xiang scheming?

...

In the coral trench, red light filled the water.

Time passed. Three days went by in a blink.

Over those three days, Luo Chen recovered while processing the demon king materials he had taken.

The supplementary ingredients he carried for refining Black Emperor Paste had long since been used up.

So this batch could only be treated in advance for preservation, to be properly refined once he returned.

During the processing, he also took stock of the gains and losses from this campaign.

His trusted magic treasure, the Mystic Flame Sword, was destroyed. Of the three Soul-Breaking Nails, two were gone — only the Soul-Breaking Nail itself remained. And he had taken some minor wounds.

Those were the losses.

The gains were considerable.

Demon crab king materials sufficient to support Archaic Tier Three late-period tempering. The Xuanyan Armor. The Cliff Serpent's body. And tens of thousands of demon beast souls now filling the banner.

By any measure — the gains far outweighed the losses.

"As expected: great risk, great reward. This time, I genuinely came out ahead."

"And the real combat has revealed a number of gaps in my fighting methods. Once things settle, I'll address them one by one — my combat toolkit will be significantly stronger for it. That alone counts as another enormous gain."

After taking full stock, Luo Chen began clearing out the cavern.

Everything went into the large-capacity storage ring.

It was time to leave.

The coral trench was well-hidden, but it remained within demon crab territory. It would eventually be found.

*Hummm.*

Before he could even leave the cavern, a sound like thunder reached his ears.

He knew perfectly well it wasn't thunder — it was a belch.

He retrieved the Sealing Divinity Compass. The red radiance faded at once, leaving only the natural scattered glow of blood coral in the trench.

Curled within the winding sea canyon was an enormously bloated Black-Scale Jiaolong.

Its eyelids drooped. Its belly bulged. Its mouth kept opening and closing, drool flowing, punctuated by that low, resonant hum.

That was Black King.

After devouring the entire Cliff Serpent, it had been in this state ever since — as if suffering from indigestion.

"Black King. We're leaving."

Black King forced one eye open. "Are we going home, Master?"

Luo Chen's brow furrowed. "Can you shrink yourself down?"

"Might be a bit difficult. Let me try..."

Black King rolled its body, demon qi wafting, attempting to compress its size.

After considerable struggle, it had managed to shrink from thirty zhang to... barely over ten.

Still far too large.

A body like this was completely unsuitable for a quiet departure.

Luo Chen shook his head. "You greedy thing. Get in here."

A small cauldron came spinning out of his sleeve. Under his mana control it expanded rapidly, enormous in an instant, pushing the surrounding seawater away.

Black King looked at it with some reluctance.

"Master, I can't digest the Cliff Serpent properly in there. And it's very cramped."

The interior of the Primordial Unity Cauldron did contain its own internal space — born of the late-stage Sage Azure Frost's spatial law technique, combined with Heaven Smelter's craftsmanship.

But Luo Chen's current mana and restriction capability wasn't yet sufficient to fully expand that internal space.

Hence Black King's complaint about it being cramped.

"Stop talking back. Get in. I'll find you a proper place to sleep when we're back."

Luo Chen's tone sharpened with mild displeasure.

This creature had accumulated quite a bit of accumulated merit lately — and was getting a little spoiled on the strength of it.

At Luo Chen's reproach, Black King gave a shudder and immediately flew into the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

He formed a seal. The cauldron shrank back to its small size, then slipped into Luo Chen's sleeve.

"Let's go."

A low command, and he shot upward.

After breaking the sea's surface, he sent out a divine sense transmission.

Shortly after, a seagull came cutting through the air and hovered obediently before him.

"You haven't recovered your mana yet — you can carry me for now."

He gave the instruction, stepped up onto Heavenjade's back, pointed in a direction, and the Fighting Gull immediately beat her wings and flew.

Not long after they had gone —

The coral trench received a group of uninvited visitors.

*Cough, cough...*

Zhu Xiang, making that strange coughing sound, stood outside the trench, frowning in puzzlement.

"That person was actually in here?"

A round, glowing form came flying out from among the demon crabs, speaking with respectful urgency.

"Reporting, my lord — that person should have been in here."

Zhu Xiang shook his head. "Other than some residual traces, I detect no sign of anyone present."

His perception capability, within a certain range, was not inferior to a transformation-stage Demon Emperor.

He barely even needed to send his clanspeople inside to investigate.

But as a precaution, he sent them in to sweep the area anyway.

The result was exactly what his senses had told him.

"So this is the answer you Hidden Dust Sand give me?" Zhu Xiang said icily, his aura erupting even through the exhaustion.

Though much of it was bluster over an empty shell, it still made that round glow freeze in terrified silence.

The form shivered. "But — according to what Yuan Yin said — that person should be here! We even confirmed the Fighting Gull's roost."

"Is the Fighting Gull there now?" Zhu Xiang asked suddenly.

The early third-tier Hidden Dust Sand creature blinked, answering reflexively: "It occasionally goes out to feed. Right now it seems to have left that small island."

"Damn it all."

Annoyance swelled in Zhu Xiang's chest.

This place clearly showed all the signs of someone who had packed up and left.

The Fighting Gull's absence only confirmed the guess.

And that meant the plan that had driven him here in a rush — ignoring his own wounds — had yielded nothing.

"I want Yuan Yin to give me an explanation."

"Otherwise — the Xuanyan Sea Region may be large, but going forward there will be no room in it for the Hidden Dust Sand clan."

With that, he led the surviving elite of the Demon Spider Crabs away in barely contained fury.

The round glow was left behind, at something of a loss.

*Get an explanation from Yuan Yin?*

It didn't even know how to contact Yuan Yin.

Looking at the coral trench — abandoned and empty as a bird-deserted nest — the Hidden Dust Sand creature could only smile bitterly.

"Yuan Yin, where did you disappear to? If you don't come back soon, we're going to be moving again."

*(End of Chapter)*
