# Chapter 860: Eight-Word Counsel, Biyou Negotiations

Riding a tiger through the pass, summoned by a great power, hosted by the city lord—these three events proclaimed the return of a generation's Alchemy Saint!

Accounts of Luo Chen's deeds, spread by those with intent, sank deep into people's hearts time and again.

Refining a hundred pills without a single failure—worthy of a grandmaster.

Slaying a demon emperor, repelling a commander, traversing the human-demon battlefield without a single defeat—the title Demon-Slaying Sage was well earned.

Thus, after he took up residence on Danluo Mountain, those seeking to visit him came in an unending stream.

In ordinary times, Luo Chen would have received them.

Even now, it would be more proper to maintain social connections.

Yet, faced with repeated requests for visits, Luo Chen refused them all.

He quietly stayed on Danluo Mountain, seeing no one, offering only the reason of devoted cultivation.

Those with ulterior motives returned empty-handed.

Among them were even friends Luo Chen had befriended in earlier years.

"Cough, cough..."

On the mountain path, Elder Fu's intermittent coughing echoed.

"He doesn't want to see us!"

Nine Spirit Lord walked beside him, hands clasped behind his back, an unreadable look in his eyes.

"Yes, summoned by a great power, Lord Lingtian personally called him junior brother at the banquet—doesn't that say it all?"

Elder Fu hunched over, his face aged to a degree that even acquaintances would find hard to accept.

He stopped, leaning on his cane, and asked, "Do you think he's already looked down on us?"

"I never said that!" Nine Spirit Lord denied firmly, but quickly lowered his voice to explain: "Times have changed."

"What do you mean?"

"When he first returned to the Eastern Wilderness, he had no foundation and his cultivation wasn't particularly high, so he needed our support. Now, he bears the dual titles of Alchemy Saint and Demon Slayer, and even the Nether Origin Sect dispatched a Nascent Soul great power to personally invite him. Under such circumstances, the value of us old friends has naturally diminished greatly. Refusing to see us is only to be expected."

Elder Fu's gaze flickered as he savored the words of the man closest to being a grand cultivator beside him.

Yes, times had changed!

Did Luo Chen still need them now?

The answer was—most likely not!

Backed by the sacred land, relying on a great power, wasn't the entire Eastern Wilderness at his beck and call?

But recalling his past interactions with Luo Chen, Elder Fu still shook his head, unwilling to believe it.

"He shouldn't be that kind of person."

Nine Spirit Lord waved his hand. "I don't know if he's that kind of person, but I do know that if we want to rebuild our mountain gates, we must form an alliance next and fight for suitable spiritual lands."

Faced with these words, Elder Fu nodded in deep agreement.

If one counted from the day the Falling Cloud Sect launched its war of expansion, the great war between humans and demons had lasted a full one hundred and fifty years!

In those hundred and fifty years, the seas had changed, and countless sects had scattered like smoke in the wind.

Leaving aside the small sects and minor schools, even among the notable Nascent Soul superior sects, more than half had been destroyed.

Medicine King Sect, Joyous Union Sect, Tianfan City, Firework Ancient Isle, Overlord Blade Gate, Taihu Palace, Fire Ridge, Azure Sky Sect, Mu Manor, Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion, Diedie Cloud Sect, Void Emperor Sect...

Among these destroyed sects, some had long lost their substantive sect structures and corresponding spiritual lands.

But others, like Mu Manor, Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion, Diedie Cloud Sect, and Void Emperor Sect, still had a large number of low-level disciples surviving and occupying their original spiritual lands.

Regarding the future direction of the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world, every Nascent Soul true person had analyzed it to some extent.

With the two races ceasing hostilities, they would inevitably negotiate the relevant details.

What mattered most to their Nascent Soul superior sects was the ownership of spiritual lands!

Now, humanity had lost fifteen domains, but the demons only occupied the former extreme eastern six domains, leaving nine domains as a buffer zone.

Those nine domains were very likely the space for bargaining between the two sides.

For Nine Spirit Sect, Divine Talisman Sect, and even Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion and Five Elements Divine Sect, they did not want to choose future mountain gate spiritual lands from among those nine domains.

The reasoning was simple—the demons had now fully come into their own!

When would the next great war between the two races occur? Who could say?

Given the lessons of this time, the further back one was, the safer.

Even though there had been the incident of three great commanders leading troops to raid the rear, it was still a low-probability event, and they had all been repelled.

Therefore, the places Nine Spirit Lord, Elder Fu, and others had their hearts set on were naturally the existing twenty-one domain spiritual lands.

This meant selecting from among those sects that had been destroyed but still nominally existed.

Mu Manor, Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion, Diedie Cloud Sect, Void Emperor Sect, Heavenly Ultimate Zen Sect, and even the extremely declined Seven Peaks Mountain and Myriad Beasts Sect!

As long as their sects settled in these places, they could avoid bordering demon territory!

In the future, after recuperating, they could achieve maximum development!

But there was one problem!

That was the long period during which a Nascent Soul superior sect controlled a domain.

Even if the Nascent Soul generation was broken and no true person sat in charge, the sect would still receive corresponding short-term protection from the Nether Origin Sacred Land.

Unless there was internal sect strife within the great domains—power struggles, even name changes.

For them, these foreign sects that had been established for hundreds or even thousands of years, seizing places that did not belong to them would be a great transgression, incurring severe punishment.

It was under these circumstances that Nine Spirit Lord and others had tried to visit Luo Chen.

They wanted to use Luo Chen to bind the various sects together, then negotiate with the Nether Origin Sacred Land to win concessions.

Luo Chen indeed had such influence now!

Whether in strength or reputation.

Especially since he had once shown this inclination and acted on it.

That was at the Shouyang discussion, where he united everyone to pressure the Nether Origin Sect.

It was precisely because of that early explanation that the various sects could prepare in advance, thus preserving their strength as much as possible in the tragic battle at Lingtian Pass.

This was evident from the number of Nascent Soul true persons who died in battle.

Seven died at Lingtian Pass, only two of whom were not from the Nether Origin Sect.

In the Five Domains War, only that sword cultivator from Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion, who sacrificed himself to protect his sect, unfortunately fell.

For all these reasons, they had placed their hopes on Luo Chen.

Yet the reality was that Luo Chen had already joined the Nether Origin Sacred Land!

"He refuses to see us—his stance is clear. Perhaps we need to choose another representative," Nine Spirit Lord said.

Elder Fu pondered, then offered his own suggestion.

"Such a representative must be someone with both strength and reputation. In the vast Eastern Wilderness, aside from the Nether Origin Sect, in my view, only a few can satisfy this. Dongyang Supreme Elder, Heavenly Capital Master, Five Elements Divine Origin!"

Nine Spirit Lord shook his head. "Dongyang Sect is the Nether Origin Sect's lackey. How could their supreme elder stick his neck out for us?"

"As for Heavenly Capital Master, his sect suffered considerable damage in the battle a few years ago. Moreover, he has accumulated hidden injuries from controlling formations in multiple great battles. I'm afraid he has no such interest."

"Divine Origin True Person..."

Mentioning this one, Nine Spirit Lord fell into thought.

Elder Fu showed a bitter smile.

The current Divine Origin True Person was reclusive, acted strangely, and always moved alone.

Asking him to step forward would be extremely difficult.

"How about you, Lord?"

Nine Spirit Lord was startled, then repeatedly waved his hand. "I need not. Without the substance of a grand cultivator, and without much of a reputation, the Nether Origin Sacred Land doesn't even look at me squarely."

"Lord is too modest," Elder Fu said, somewhat unwilling to give up.

Nine Spirit Lord said seriously, "It's not modesty. That's just how it is."

Elder Fu sighed. Indeed, that was how it was!

The esteemed Nine Spirit Lord, reputed to be comparable to a grand cultivator, was still an ant before the vast and towering Nether Origin Sacred Land.

Unlike Luo Chen...

Thinking of Luo Chen, Elder Fu felt even more emotional.

In just twenty years, the other party had built a great reputation from nothing, attracting a Nascent Soul great power to personally invite him. Such methods were truly unmatched in the world today.

It was just a pity that the other party had now been "bought off."

Just as the two were tangled in a hundred knots and lost in confusion, a rustling sound came from beneath their feet.

Nine Spirit Lord noticed it first and looked down. A black snake with dragon horns slithered down from the mountain.

"Demon Emperor!"

"Wait!"

Elder Fu stopped Nine Spirit Lord and said hesitantly, "I learned from Fairy Fenghua that Luo Chen has a spirit pet, extremely powerful. It seems to be of the dragon lineage."

Nine Spirit Lord slowly lowered his hand.

Black King crawled before them, as if he hadn't seen the palm that was ready to strike.

Opening his mouth, he spat out a slip of paper.

As the two looked on in confusion, the slip suddenly unfurled, then was annihilated into powder in a sudden burst of flame.

Black King swallowed the powder in one gulp. "This is my master's message. Farewell."

With that, Black King twisted his body and disappeared into the depths of Danluo Mountain.

After he left, the two standing there looked at each other.

"'Hold still, await the heavenly mechanism.' What does this mean?"

"Unclear. Could there be changes in this situation that's about to stabilize?"

"Heavenly mechanism—what is the heavenly mechanism?"

"I don't know, but I know that Fellow Daoist Luo has not stood idly by. He understands the situation we face now. According to the meaning of these eight words, he is advising us not to privately collate first, but to wait until the situation is completely settled before making plans."

Nine Spirit Lord opened his mouth, somewhat disapproving.

If they truly waited until the situation was settled, would they still have a chance to covet those high-quality spiritual lands?

But...

Nine Spirit Lord turned back to look at Danluo Mountain, shrouded in mist and clouds, as if he could see Luo Chen watching them.

After a long time, he let out a faint sigh.

"The Luo Chen of today, I find increasingly hard to fathom. Never mind. Spare myself these mundane affairs. I will focus on cultivation and strive to solidify my name as a grand cultivator as soon as possible!"

The two gradually walked away.

Because of Luo Chen's eight words, the undercurrents surging in Lingtian Pass gradually subsided.

Everyone seemed still immersed in the joy of once again repelling the demon invasion.

Everyone's attention was fixed on the upcoming Biyou Mountain negotiations.

...

Mountains and Seas Calendar, year 3,632!

Five full years had passed since the last great battle at Lingtian Pass!

Under everyone's anxious and fervent anticipation, the date for the ceasefire negotiations was finally set.

July fifteenth!

This date carried a certain significance.

Because the war of expansion launched by the Falling Cloud Sect had also officially begun on this very day.

What started when it started, what ended when it ended—everything seemed fated.

On this day, a streak of light flew into Danluo Mountain.

Luo Chen, who had been cultivating, suddenly opened his eyes and reached out with a beckoning hand.

The transmission talisman, wandering like a headless fly in the vast mist, fell into his hand.

The talisman ignited spontaneously in the windless air, and its contents entered Luo Chen's ears.

Blinking, Luo Chen rose to his feet.

"It's time!"

With a flicker of his form, he was already out of Danluo Mountain, shooting straight into the nine heavens.

Not long after, Luo Chen halted his figure.

Facing the black-robed elder at the head of the five before him, Luo Chen bowed respectfully.

"Greetings, Ancestor!"

Black Marsh glanced at him, and with a flick of his hand, a ball of golden light rolled out, landing at Luo Chen's feet.

As soon as the thing came out, it let out an angry roar.

"Roar!"

Luo Chen frowned and scolded, "Whelp!"

The golden light paused, and from the soul-level oppression, it immediately reacted.

"Mas... master."

It was Golden Tiger Emperor Slaughter.

But compared to before, Emperor Slaughter now bore a subtle, indescribable change.

He seemed... much more violent?

Luo Chen couldn't pinpoint the origin of that change, but he knew that Emperor Slaughter had definitely undergone some transformation in the two years under Black Marsh Ancestor.

Only because the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit deep in his Purple Mansion had trembled for an instant.

And the Yuan Tu blood sword he had refined for years on his right arm had just emitted a scorching heat.

"Was it a Nascent Soul method?"

Luo Chen thought to himself, growing more wary of the upcoming Biyou Mountain ceasefire negotiations.

Seeing Luo Chen subdue the golden tiger, Black Marsh Ancestor nodded with satisfaction.

"Luo Chen, Lord Pingshan has specifically demanded that we hand over his son. Later, when you see him, dissolve the enslavement seal and return the soul blood. That is your task for this trip."

Luo Chen's expression was calm, as if he had already guessed all this.

He merely nodded slightly.

"Fine. Just us few. Let's go!"

Black Marsh Ancestor waved his hand grandly, and a surge of mana wrapped around the other five, flying toward Biyou Mountain in the Azure Sky Domain.

Only then did Luo Chen have a chance to examine the other four.

All were late-stage Nascent Soul grand cultivators!

And all were familiar faces!

Thunder Path Master and Lingtian City Lord of the Nether Origin Sect.

Heavenly Capital Master of Heavenly Capital Sect, Divine Origin True Person of Five Elements Divine Sect!

Luo Chen didn't know what significance this personnel arrangement held, but as far as he could see, three from the Nether Origin Sect and three from outside it—together, they were enough to represent the entire Eastern Wilderness cultivation world.

With such a lineup for negotiations, the related interests and demands would be more easily accepted by everyone.

As he examined them, the others also sensed his gaze and looked back.

Thunder Path Master grinned, his eyes mocking.

Ling Tian City Lord looked grave, seeming to want to speak to Luo Chen but hesitating.

Heavenly Capital Master, however, had joy on his face, and looked quite comforted when he saw Luo Chen, as if very pleased that Luo Chen could participate in such a major event.

That Divine Origin True Person was still shrouded in a black robe, his true face unseen, his expression unknown.

But before Black Marsh Ancestor, none of them spoke. The journey was silent, save for the rumbling wind of their extreme speed.

With a Nascent Soul great power's flying speed, crossing three domains was but a moment's work.

While everyone's hearts were still stirred, the mana enveloping them suddenly dissipated.

"We're here."

Then, the six slowly descended upon Biyou Mountain.

As they descended, Luo Chen caught a glimpse of a giant handprint on a cliff face in the mountains.

That was from the battle of Absolute Spirit back then—his own work!

So this was Biyou Mountain?

It seemed fate had brought him here.

Just as Luo Chen was lost in thought, demonic winds swept up from below, and a voice like clashing blades and spears rolled forth.

"Black Marsh, you're late!"

The sound waves rolled, sweeping away the clouds that had shrouded the mountain peak for countless years.

Six figures came into view.

At the head was one wearing a Leveling Heaven Crown, clad in a white-gold ceremonial robe, his countenance radiating authority without anger.

Was that the Lord Pingshan who had pacified the million mountains and united countless demons under his rule?

Luo Chen felt his entire body tremble, his soul quaking uneasily.

Before this man, it seemed that in the next moment, he would become meat in his mouth.

He was not the only one to react this way.

The five Nascent Soul cultivators who followed all had reactions.

Especially Divine Origin True Person, whose black robe billowed, quite losing his composure.

"Hmph, you're the ones who came early!"

Black Marsh Ancestor snorted coldly, retorting.

With that one sentence, the pressure on everyone vanished, and they felt as if a great weight had been lifted.

At this moment, Luo Chen finally had a chance to examine the other five standing behind Lord Pingshan.

With just one glance, Luo Chen no longer harbored any illusions.

"All are commander-level great demon emperors!"
