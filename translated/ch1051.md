# Chapter 1051: Canghai Luo Chen, an Ascendant-Level Strike

The weathered scholar, though aged in countenance, possessed an elegant and refined temperament that immediately conveyed a sense of profound erudition.

Yet the moment his eyes narrowed, the entire world seemed to transform.

An inexplicable pressure pressed down upon Luo Chen from all directions.

This pressure was not in the air, nor on the ground, yet it was omnipresent—from the breath entering his mouth and nose to the opening of his pores, as if the entire world was oppressing Luo Chen.

Was it an illusion?

No, it was an intuitive sensation upon the soul!

Li Canghai, the sole human great power of the present-day Northern Sea!

He spoke softly, "How do you intend to prove it?"

Prove that Black King was his spirit pet?

Luo Chen's expression turned cold. He first formed a hand seal before him and uttered the three words, "Suo Po He."

Then his aura underwent a dramatic transformation.

A sharp, unmatched momentum erupted from the depths of his soul, like a sharp sword, directly piercing through that omnipresent pressure.

Li Canghai raised an eyebrow, surprise flickering in his eyes.

Primordial spirit!

He had not expected that the man before him had already achieved the unity of Sage and primordial spirit, combining all his power with his soul, enough to resist his divine sense suppression.

So young, yet possessing such cultivation?

The next moment.

Luo Chen took a step forward, planting his foot firmly.

"Senior wishes for proof, then I shall provide it!"

A strange light gleamed in his eyes as he fixed his gaze upon the black dragon prostrate on the blue ice surface.

A playful smile spread across Li Canghai's face.

This young man could condense his primordial spirit at such an age, suggesting he had a unique understanding of the soul path.

But if he thought he could use soul arts to undo the restrictions on the black dragon, he was gravely mistaken.

Everyone knew that the Northern Sea great power Li Canghai had achieved Ascension through the Dao of formations. When it came to restrictions and formations, there was hardly anyone in the world who could match him.

This Luo Chen might have broken through the Mist-Shrouded Spirit-Confusing Grand Formation with those eyes of his and his considerable understanding of formations, but trying to undo the black dragon's restrictions with that was nothing but overestimating himself!

Just as expected!

Under Luo Chen's gaze, although the black dragon's face showed confusion and struggle, it did not awaken.

It only felt that this man was somewhat familiar, much like the Mirror Flower Water Moon illusion Zhou Fuyou had cast earlier—merely giving it a vague, ambiguous feeling.

"Young friend, it seems you've made a mistake!" Li Canghai laughed.

Luo Chen remained unmoved, his eyes fixed intently on Black King.

At the same time, a layer of bright red light suddenly burst forth from his body. That red light spread out in an instant, enveloping Black King.

In that moment, Black King's massive heart began to beat violently.

Thump!

Thump!

A power of the same origin as Luo Chen's naturally rose up.

In mutual resonance, Black King let out a pained roar, its enormous body thrashing about on the ice.

Crack! Crack! Crack!

Large sheets of ice shattered, and Black King plunged directly into the Blue Ice Sea.

Seeing this, Li Canghai vaguely sensed what was happening, but he did not stop it. Instead, he curiously observed Luo Chen.

A low shout suddenly erupted in the air.

"Fool, wake up!"

The next moment!

"Roar!"

From the icy sea, a black dragon shot into the sky, brandishing its claws and wagging its tail, its body radiating a red light of the same origin as Luo Chen's.

That light was powerful and brimming with life, as if it could never be extinguished.

It opened its mouth to roar, but cowered before Li Canghai, quickly slithering behind Luo Chen.

"Master, that old... old grandpa is very, very powerful!"

Luo Chen twitched the corner of his mouth, meeting Li Canghai's surprised expression.

"Senior, is this proof enough now?"

Li Canghai clicked his tongue in admiration. "You certainly spare no expense, raising a spirit pet with your own essence blood!"

"Senior is wise, seeing through my little trick at a glance."

Luo Chen was not proud. Awakening Black King was as easy for him as he had told Little Bai earlier.

This had nothing to do with slave seals or soul restrictions.

A fundamental fact had always existed between Luo Chen and Black King.

Back when Black King was in the dangerous situation of having its physical body shattered, Luo Chen had used the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's secret method to build a blood pool, even pouring his own tongue-tip essence blood into it, thus reconstructing Black King's body.

That drop of tongue-tip essence blood, containing Luo Chen's powerful strength, had long since merged with Black King, existing forever.

In a sense, Black King possessed Luo Chen's bloodline and could be considered his blood descendant.

The simplest example was when Black King advanced to the fourth rank. Without a slave seal, Luo Chen had relied solely on bloodline resonance to subdue it again.

What he had just done was use his own source blood to awaken the power within Black King's bloodline that was of the same origin as his own.

Desolate beasts did not focus on qi refinement or soul refinement; flesh and blood were their foundation!

The memories rooted deep in the bloodline could not be completely suppressed by any slave seal or restriction.

This was the purest instinct of a desolate beast!

However, although Black King's memories had been awakened, the soul restrictions placed on its soul had not been undone. Thus, even after regaining clarity, Black King still instinctively feared Li Canghai.

Luo Chen addressed Li Canghai, "Senior, please be merciful and undo the restrictions on Black King."

But Li Canghai did not answer directly. Instead, his wise gaze continuously appraised Luo Chen.

"At such an age, with such cultivation, and even your physical body is incredibly strong!"

"Excellent!"

"If you can withstand one strike from me, I will undo the restrictions on it."

As he spoke, without waiting for Luo Chen to react, he casually thrust forward.

The fishing rod in his hand appeared before Luo Chen in an instant.

The speed was so fast that no one could react.

Neither Black King nor Little Bai, who was attached to Luo Chen, had moved past Li Canghai's praise of their master.

And that fishing rod thrust seemed to contain the grand momentum of heaven and earth, the ultimate truth of the world.

Under that single thrust, even the residual force forcibly expelled Little Bai, who was attached to Luo Chen.

On the endless ice field, the young man's expression shifted slightly, as if the next moment he would be pierced through by that fishing rod.

Within the sleeve of his left hand, a powerful force stirred restlessly, eager to burst forth.

But Luo Chen suppressed that impulse.

At this moment, he had already condensed his primordial spirit, allowing his divine sense to turn with extreme speed.

With a thought, Luo Chen seemed to move slowly yet swiftly, extending his right hand, fingers together like a sword, as sword qi burst forth.

Chi!

A strand of pure Scarlet Firmament Sword Qi met the fishing rod, then shattered in an instant.

But Luo Chen was not flustered. His sword fingers continued forward, his dharma power surging like a tide, as sword qi from all around gathered at one point.

Boom!

A terrifying sword qi, formed from who knew how many hundreds of strands, erupted violently, clashing head-on with that unremarkable fishing rod.

Amidst Li Canghai's renewed surprise, Luo Chen lightly shouted, "Break!"

The next moment, the fishing rod shattered into powder, returning to heaven and earth as origin qi.

But Luo Chen's sword qi did not cease. From one strand, it transformed into five hundred, shooting toward Li Canghai like flowing light.

Unfortunately, after dispersing, the power of the sword qi was no longer as formidable. When it reached Li Canghai, it was blocked by something and dissipated in an instant.

Li Canghai looked at the dissipating sword qi before him and praised, "The might of a single sword strike, comparable to an Ascendant-level blow. In this present age, you can be considered the number one Sage!"

Luo Chen was not proud. He slowly released his sword fingers. "Senior's abilities are as vast as an ocean. I have merely made a poor showing."

Mutual praise elevated each other.

Then silence fell.

Li Canghai carefully examined Luo Chen, then casually waved his hand, and a six-pointed star flew out.

Facing this six-pointed star, Luo Chen's gaze sharpened, then relaxed, allowing the six-pointed star to land on Black King's head.

Then, a sizzling sound like snow melting under the sun continuously emitted. Black King seemed to be in great pain, rolling back and forth on the ground.

Li Canghai glanced at Luo Chen's left sleeve, then stood up from the ice layer and pointed behind him.

"Young friend, the Blue Ice Sea is quite cold. Are you interested in going up the mountain for a chat?"

Luo Chen looked up. A towering mountain, entirely formed from mysterious ice, stood majestically.

This mountain should not have been here.

A hundred years ago, Li Canghai had collected it into the Mountain-Sea Seal.

But it should also be here, because Li Canghai himself was here.

"Master..."

A timid voice came from behind, carrying a hint of worry.

"It's fine. Take care of Black King. I'll be back shortly."

Luo Chen flicked his sleeve and flew to Li Canghai's side.

Li Canghai seemed to greatly appreciate Luo Chen's courage, smiling as he extended his hand. "Please!"

Luo Chen nodded slightly and walked side by side with the old man, flying up to that Ice Soul Spirit Mountain.

At the foot of the mountain, Little Bai watched Black King while occasionally glancing at the ice mountain, her eyes full of worry.

...

Unlike the situation Little Bai worried about, the palace on the mountain was warm as spring, and the atmosphere of conversation between the old and the young was quite friendly.

"Luo Chen, I've heard your name quite a bit over the years!"

"I wonder if it's a good reputation or a bad one."

"Haha, how can we cultivators distinguish between good and evil? Even if one's name spreads across the five continents as a heinous, utterly depraved demon, so what?"

"From that perspective, my reputation is indeed not very good!"

"Young friend is too modest. Though you bear the fearsome name of Devil Lord and Demon Luo, who wouldn't want to curry favor with the Alchemy Sect?"

"Senior flatters me."

The friendly atmosphere and polite conversation had been like this from the moment they entered.

Luo Chen knew that this was the equal treatment he had earned by withstanding the other's strike earlier.

Before that, let alone chatting and laughing, even sitting at the same table would have been a fantasy.

Luo Chen did not want to engage in excessive hypocrisy with the other party. After a few simple exchanges, he directly cut to the chase.

"Senior, do you have a grudge against the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect?"

Li Canghai revealed a playful smile. "What makes you say that? Don't you know that I have a good relationship with Zhan Bushou, one of the Heavenly Origin Five Elders? We once fought side by side, intimidating the two great ancient demons of the Northern Sea?"

"The past is the past, and the present is the present." Luo Chen shook his head, glanced outside the hall, and then said, "Black King colluded with the demon race to attack the Tiannan Continent. Was that its own decision?"

Li Canghai withdrew his smile, staring at Luo Chen with an inscrutable expression.

The Black King he had casually subdued was a minor piece on his chessboard. Outsiders did not know Black King was his spirit pet, so sending it to harass the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect would not arouse suspicion.

He had never expected that Luo Chen, the true master of Black King, would track it down and discover this.

Thus, his intention to target the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect had actually been exposed.

Even if only a little was exposed, to those in the know, it could already be everything.

"I've heard that you were once a guest elder of the Star Gate under the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect and even helped the Heavenly Origin Five Elders deal with the Phoenix Sisters? Are you asking now on behalf of the Dao Sect to make trouble?"

Luo Chen twitched the corner of his mouth. "I was merely an external affairs guest elder, and that was long in the past. Has Senior not heard that I once joined the Nether Origin Sect as well?"

Li Canghai was taken aback.

This matter, he truly was not very clear about.

After all, although Luo Chen had some reputation before, he was ultimately a minor Sage. The fact that he knew some things about Luo Chen was only because Luo Chen's fame had grown in recent years, almost earning him the title of the number one under Ascension, which drew his attention.

The matter of Luo Chen joining the Nether Origin Sect and becoming the master of the Alchemy Saint Hall had already been buried history the moment the Nether Origin Sect was destroyed.

While he was stunned, Luo Chen added another sentence.

"Senior previously called me Demon Luo, so you must know that this title was earned on the Western Desert Buddhist Lands. Naturally, you should also know about the matter of the demoness Azure Frost and Formless Demon Luo jointly battling the eminent monks of the Hanging Temple. Therefore, the matter of me and the Heavenly Origin Five Elders dealing with the Phoenix Sisters is also long past."

Li Canghai was speechless. He had overlooked that point and said with a hint of mockery:

"Indeed, there are no eternal enemies in this world, nor eternal friends."

He and the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect were like this, and Luo Chen and the Phoenix Sisters seemed to be the same.

Luo Chen nodded in agreement, then said seriously, "Then I wonder if I, Luo, have the honor of becoming Senior's friend?"

Li Canghai said naturally, "The fact that you can sit here already makes you my friend."

But Luo Chen slowly shook his head. "No, I mean a friend who joins forces to deal with the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!"

Li Canghai's expression suddenly changed.

His originally elegant temperament became unsettling, his blazing eyes making one feel as if needles were pricking their back.

"What is your purpose?"

"And what is Senior's purpose in attacking the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect?"

The two locked eyes, but neither continued speaking.

The hall fell into silence.

After a long time, Li Canghai spoke softly, "If you want to deal with the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, you'd better speed up."

Was this already a tacit understanding?

Luo Chen immediately asked, "Why?"

Li Canghai, like Luo Chen earlier, looked outside the hall, but his gaze was distant, as if crossing thousands of miles of clouds and roads, landing on the three continents of the Central Continent.

"The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect has deployed large-scale formations on several continents, attempting to reunite the Central Continent and even rebuild the Heaven-Earth Peak. Once they accomplish such a feat, you should know what you'll be facing, right?"

Luo Chen's face changed.

Pulling continents together and reuniting the Central Continent—such a matter was an earth-shattering, heaven-defying undertaking for low-level cultivators, but it was not difficult for Ascendant-level great powers.

And once the Central Continent was relatively complete and the Heaven-Earth Peak was reconnected, it meant that the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect would have passed through its weakest period.

Back when Rosycloud attacked the Central Continent, her greatest enemy was not the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect of the Mountains and Seas Realm, but the many great power incarnations descending from the Heaven-Earth Peak into this world.

If the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect truly accomplished this, Luo Chen would never be able to deal with them in his lifetime.

He could never possess Rosycloud's level of strength within his lifespan.

Even Rosycloud had only severely wounded the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, not completely destroyed it like the Nether Origin Sect.

Was it because that woman didn't want to?

No, it was because even someone as strong as Rosycloud couldn't do it at the time!

Luo Chen swallowed his saliva. "Senior, are you telling the truth?"

Li Canghai nodded heavily. "I achieved Ascension through the Dao of formations. I understand the changes in the grand momentum of heaven and earth better than anyone. It's not just the three continents now; they have even set up a god-summoning formation on the Heaven-Earth Island. If you don't believe me, you can go verify it yourself!"

Luo Chen's mouth went dry. "How much time?"

"At least sixty years, at most a hundred." Li Canghai sighed. "You must believe in the foundation of the strongest holy land in the Mountains and Seas Realm. Even without the Star Gate, one of the three treasures of the Dao Sect, they still have the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard, a supreme spiritual treasure capable of suppressing the fate of an entire continent!"

Sixty years!

This timeframe was so familiar.

A graceful figure flashed through Luo Chen's mind. Not long ago, that woman had also personally spoken such a timeframe.

Why did she say she would retrieve his life soul only after sixty years?

Could it also be related to this matter?

Luo Chen unconsciously clenched his fist.

(Chapter End)
