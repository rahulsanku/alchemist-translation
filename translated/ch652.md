# Chapter 652: A Thunderclap in a Clear Sky, a Desolate Beast in Peng Lake

Beside the Sunken Sea, Ding Yi, shrouded beneath a wide black robe, quietly appeared.

He watched the golden color filling the sky and remained silent.

Suddenly, his expression focused, and he glanced toward a distant corner.

A giant turtle surfaced amid the rippling waves, with three people standing on its back.

The leader raised his head to the sky, as if calculating something. After a long while, he shook his head.

Behind him stood a man and a woman, both with young appearances.

While Ding Yi watched them, the young man also looked over coldly.

Ding Yi tugged at the corner of his mouth and, without hesitation, dove under the water and disappeared.

The young man frowned and said softly to the middle-aged man with slightly white temples in front of him, "Father, many people have ambitions toward the Sunken Sea secret realm."

The middle-aged man did not turn his head and said faintly, "This secret realm has appeared for over a thousand years. There is no wall in the world that lets no wind through. Others naturally know. In the past, the Primordial Demon Sect locked it away, and others did not dare interfere. Now that the Primordial Demon Sect is gone, naturally all kinds of trash want to take a share. Do not think too much. They are only Golden Core cultivators. Even if they enter, they are courting death."

"What about Xiaolian and me?"

"You two are different. I will protect you. If we can find the Spirit-Molding Sacred Spring [TERM?] that Great Power Li soaked in back then, not only can you reshape your spiritual roots and easily advance to the Sage stage, in the future, you may even hope to comprehend law power early at the Sage stage and step into the Ascendant realm!"

"Is the Spirit-Molding Sacred Spring [TERM?] real?" Confusion appeared on the young man's face, as if he did not believe such a treasure truly existed in the world.

His father did not explain much. After observing the Sunken Sea for a while, he formed a plan.

"The death qi accumulated in this sea is constantly venting toward the Arctic Nightmara Heaven [TERM?], artificially causing the secret realm to open early. This is good news and bad news. It has opened early, so its momentum is insufficient and the process is incomplete. We still need to wait several months before entering."

"Let us first find a place to stay. When the time comes, we will choose an entrance with greater activity."

As he spoke, the giant turtle beneath them slowly sank, finally disappearing to an unknown place.

On another side, after leaving the Sunken Sea shore, Ding Yi let out a slight breath of relief.

"That connection is still faint and untraceable. It can be seen that the secret realm has not completely opened, which gives us some time."

"However, we cannot delay. After returning, I must urge Devil Lord Azure Yang."

As he murmured inwardly, a ruthless glint appeared in Ding Yi's eyes.

Devil Lord Azure Yang was the suitable candidate he had personally selected. He had strength, yet was still within control and would not overshadow the host.

But if the other party advanced to late Golden Core, the variables would become too great.

One month later, on Peng Island, Ding Yi and Cave Master Moyun arrived together, forcefully requesting to meet Luo Chen.

This time, Luo Chen did not refuse to see them.

The meeting place was set in the Pill and Artifact Hall, which was not suitable for receiving guests.

Watching Luo Chen forge a sword case while repairing a flower basket magic treasure, Ding Yi and Cave Master Moyun looked at each other, seemingly unable to have expected this.

"Because of this, you shut your door to visitors and would not even see us?"

Without raising his head, Luo Chen said, "According to what you said, the Sunken Sea trip is quite dangerous, and none of us has absolute confidence in retreating safely. Then before leaving, some matters naturally should have a beginning and an end. This flower basket magic treasure is something I promised a friend years ago that I would help repair. Before departure, only by resolving this matter can Luo's thoughts flow smoothly."

Ding Yi had nothing to say.

Cave Master Moyun did not care much. It was rare to have an opportunity to personally observe Daoist Azure Yang forging artifacts, so he was currently focusing on the other's technique.

After watching for a while, however, he did not see anything profound or exquisite, and inwardly felt it a pity.

Luo Chen slightly slowed the movements of his hands and raised his head to look at Ding Yi.

"You came in such a hurry to see me. I assume it is not because you are concerned about Luo's body."

Ding Yi shook his head and immediately explained everything he had seen and heard at the Sunken Sea.

At the end, he said seriously, "The ruins are opening right now. We must make plans early."

Luo Chen frowned. "That is not what you said back then. The time when the ruins open and close occurs together with the heavenly phenomenon. At the shortest, one or two years. At the longest, forty or fifty years. Do we not still have plenty of time?"

"Times differ. Naturally, they cannot be discussed as the same thing!"

Ding Yi took a deep breath and combined the many circumstances he had observed to give his inference.

"This opening of the ruins is not natural, but artificial. Rumor says Jiaoren Emperor Flow Lord used a secret method to intercept the Sunken Sea's death qi to undo the Nightmara Heaven seal, which caused the ruins to open early. No one knows whether they will close early too. If we are trapped inside, I fear we will not be able to come out."

"Moreover, going one step earlier means gaining more benefits."

"Daoist Azure Yang, we cannot delay!"

Hearing this, Luo Chen's brows furrowed even more tightly.

After a long while, he said faintly, "Give me a little more time."

After speaking, he continued his previous artifact-forging work.

This already showed his intention to send guests away.

Anger flashed across Ding Yi's face, but under Cave Master Moyun's signal, he ultimately did not act and returned unwillingly.

Luo Chen did not see them out. He continued busily working in the Pill and Artifact Hall.

Half a day later, he stopped.

Repairing Duan Li's flower basket magic treasure was actually very simple. It only needed some materials, and after it was repaired, the owner could nurture it for a period of time.

What Luo Chen cared about was the unusual artifact Han Zhan had entrusted to him.

Soul Heaven Sword Case [TERM?].

By using better jiaoxiao silk [TERM?] to replace Sick Willow Silkworm Silk [TERM?], the effect of isolating baleful qi had reached its best.

The other supplementary materials were also fine choices.

After one month, it was completed in one go.

How great its might was, he could not say, but at least it could satisfy Han Zhan's requirements.

Stroking the sword case's soft surface, Luo Chen narrowed his eyes slightly.

Ding Yi was very anxious.

And increasingly anxious.

This was good news for Luo Chen.

That impatience might not let him turn the guest into the host, but it would allow him to stand on equal footing in later actions.

As for himself, he was not anxious.

He was only one final step away from Ancient Wilderness Fourth Tier.

This trump card might be his only means of resisting Sages. No matter how urgent things were, he could not act rashly.

"Slowly, then."

"Perhaps those who go early will reincarnate early."

Luo Chen laughed softly, stored the refined Soul Heaven Sword Case [TERM?], turned, and entered Star Dou Hall.

Recently, he had gained new insights into Ten Thousand Daos Converge [TERM?]. The closer he came to Ancient Wilderness Fourth Tier, the more he discovered many omissions in this body-refinement method.

If he could resolve them one by one, perhaps he could add a few degrees of might after transforming into his true body.

The Myriad Immortals Assembly was a gathering place for wandering cultivators.

In the hearts of low-rank wandering cultivators, it was even a holy land for wandering cultivators no weaker than the Ascendant Holy Lands.

As the righteous-demonic war continued over these years, more and more wandering cultivators gathered at the Myriad Immortals Assembly, making each day seem like a new atmosphere.

But this year, those low-rank wandering cultivators increasingly felt oppressed.

It seemed that more and more high-rank experts were appearing within the Myriad Immortals Assembly's territory.

Especially after the strange golden sky and black sea phenomenon appeared, those experts who had sneaked in gradually could no longer restrain themselves.

Slowly, a rumor began spreading through countless mouths.

Within the Sunken Sea, there was an ancient secret realm.

It contained countless treasures left behind after ancient cultivators died. If ordinary people obtained them, they might extend their lifespans, greatly raise their realms, or, with good luck, perhaps even advance to Sage or Ascendant and dominate a region.

Logically speaking, such baseless rumors would not make everyone believe them.

Yet the strange heavenly phenomenon continued.

Added to this was someone spreading the claim that Li Canghai, the current number one person in the Northern Sea, had obtained benefits from those ruins and only then advanced to the Ascendant realm.

In this way, the news seemed increasingly reliable.

Many wandering cultivators wanted to explore the Sunken Sea.

Originally, they worried that the Myriad Immortals Assembly, closest to the Sunken Sea, would obstruct them.

Unexpectedly, after the three great eight-star demon hunters led teams to sweep away the demon beasts occupying the outskirts of the Sunken Sea, they all withdrew, seemingly not caring at all.

The cultivators with designs on the place immediately grew restless.

Countless cultivators began gathering toward the Sunken Sea.

Until three months later.

Hundreds of sky-reaching waterspouts rose from the Sunken Sea, charging straight into the heavens.

After the waterspouts disappeared, vortexes remained on the Sunken Sea one after another.

Massive heaven and earth spiritual qi was drawn into the vortexes and disappeared without trace.

Some said the locations of those vortexes were the secret realm entrances, and that the treasures within the secret realm were countless, first come, first served.

Immediately, all those who had been watching could no longer hold back.

Beside the Sunken Sea, Ding Yi and Cave Master Moyun rode clouds and mist, watching from afar as streaks of light fled into the black ocean and swam toward the vortex locations.

At this moment, it was as if countless mayflies were joining one sea.

"These people are not stupid. They know not to fly there from the sky." Cave Master Moyun said with a smile.

"Who does not know the great name of the Void Burning Wind [TERM?]? However, these people blinded by treasures and opportunities have made not the slightest preparation against it. Rushing into the Sunken Sea like this is purely seeking death."

Ding Yi snorted, extremely disdainful of their actions.

Cave Master Moyun fully agreed. To enter the sea, which of them had not planned for years and gathered powerful companions before daring to go inside?

These people truly did not know life from death.

Within their sight, quite a few wandering cultivators panicked and fled out as soon as they entered.

The black turbid seawater was crazily corroding their mana barriers and protective treasures.

Qi Refinement cultivators could not swim ten li.

Foundation Establishment cultivators could at most travel one hundred li.

This was for those who knew their limits.

Those who overestimated themselves would quietly die in the endless black sea as they swam.

Some Golden Core cultivators had made thorough preparations and cautiously entered the Sunken Sea.

But before finding any vortex, they were swept by the invisible Void Burning Wind [TERM?] within the seawater, becoming muddled and senseless before being corroded and submerged.

In only a few days, the scene of countless escape lights swimming into one sea vanished.

Beside the Sunken Sea, there were more terrified survivors and onlookers.

During this process, Cave Master Moyun, who was proficient in formations, noticed something.

"The deaths of these people brought large amounts of death qi to this sea. The death qi flows into the vortexes and strengthens them. This should be caused by some formation specifically designed to draw death qi."

Ding Yi was thoughtful. "Perhaps this is why the Myriad Immortals Assembly's higher-ups do not prohibit low-rank cultivators from approaching the Sunken Sea."

Using human lives to fill it and increase the Sunken Sea's death qi.

After observing for a moment, Cave Master Moyun regretfully shook his head.

"Unfortunately, Three Pillars Gorge [TERM?] was destroyed and the terrain changed. A portion of death qi is gathering toward Nightmara Heaven. This causes these vortexes to form and grow very slowly."

Ding Yi said decisively, "That has nothing to do with us. The Sunken Sea has accumulated death qi for who knows how many tens of thousands of years. How could it be drained dry in a short time? It is enough for us to enter and seek treasure."

Cave Master Moyun hesitated. "Then when do we go in?"

During the days they had been observing, they had even discovered Sages who disdained concealing their tracks entering.

If they delayed further, there would be nothing left.

When this question was mentioned, Ding Yi's face instantly darkened.

"Devil Lord Azure Yang!"

"He has come to urge me again?"

Inside Star Dou Hall, Luo Chen flicked away the ash left by the burned transmission talisman, his expression neither sorrowful nor joyful.

It was as though others' urgency had nothing to do with him.

His body was bare and smeared with black mud.

Within him, true fire blazed, scorching every corner of his physique.

Extraordinary pain blessed his body. If it were an ordinary person, they would have long since fainted, unable to endure it. Luo Chen seemed not to notice.

He even followed the Ten Thousand Daos Converge [TERM?] he had revised over these days, urging the true fire to focus on tempering certain areas.

One should not underestimate these details.

Only by relying on quantitative change was there an opportunity to reach qualitative change.

Time slowly passed.

While Luo Chen focused on body refinement, the outside world's shock over the Sunken Sea phenomenon did not fade because of the deaths of large numbers of low-rank cultivators. Instead, it gradually grew greater.

One powerful expert after another from unknown origins entered the Sunken Sea from every direction.

There were even peerless Demon Emperors who stirred wind and waves from the depths of the demon sea and forcefully stepped into the Sunken Sea.

Not only that, chaos also appeared within the Myriad Immortals Assembly.

The three great eight-star demon hunters disappeared together.

Some said they had also gone to that secret realm.

Not only Angler and the others, but even Blood Wanderer, who had moved frequently before, had not shown himself for a long time.

The righteous-demonic war that had lasted for decades strangely ceased after the Sunken Sea phenomenon appeared.

Rumor said that Mad Wanderer, one of the Myriad Immortals Assembly's three great wanderers, had appeared in the Canghai Alliance, and he was the one who caused the righteous-demonic war to stop.

All of this had nothing to do with Luo Chen.

One year later, Ding Yi stepped to the shore of Peng Lake again.

What entered his eyes was still vast smoke-like waters, cloud and mist filling the area, with no trace visible in the boundless expanse.

Ding Yi's face was full of anger. Without waiting for the lake-guarding cultivator's warning, he rose into the sky. A yellow-skinned gourd appeared in his hand.

He was going to force his way into Peng Lake.

"Devil Lord Azure Yang, to break faith to this extent. Today, you must give me an explanation!"

With an angry shout, he slapped the green-skinned gourd.

Immediately, brilliant golden sand surged out from the gourd and charged toward the Peng Lake grand formation.

At this moment, a violent wind swept over, stirring the golden sand.

Ding Yi snorted, and the rolling golden sand suddenly took shape, gathering into a hundred-zhang monster that was like a snake yet not a snake, like an earthworm yet not an earthworm, almost exactly like the rumored Sand Worm [TERM?].

Amid the mist, an enormous jiao dragon slowly surfaced from beneath the water, revealing the powerful aura of a Great Demon King.

The jiao dragon rose into the sky and slapped with its tail.

The Sand Worm [TERM?] did not fear at all. After being shattered into sand filling the sky, it gathered and formed again, instead trying to wrap around the black jiao dragon.

Another strange wind swept over, trying to help the jiao dragon blow apart the golden sand.

Ding Yi's expression did not change. He slapped the yellow-skinned gourd again, and another Sand Worm [TERM?] flew out.

Within the mist, Heavenjade's expression changed slightly. While withdrawing, she continuously waved the Nine Winds Banana Fan [TERM?] in her hand.

Ding Yi's expression was ruthless. His figure moved, forcefully entering the Peng Lake grand formation.

"The two of you cannot stop me. Tell Daoist Azure Yang to roll out and see me!"

At this moment, a sword cry seemed to ring out in the void.

Clang!

Piercing killing intent covered him from above, and Ding Yi's expression changed slightly.

"Is this the killing move of the Peng Lake grand formation?"

Ding Yi had a premonition that if that sword swung, he absolutely could not resist it.

This was not an attack a Golden Core cultivator could release at all. Only a killing formation arranged with the eight hundred li of Peng Lake could explain it.

"I was too arrogant today. I should have brought Cave Master Moyun with me."

Ding Yi regretted his carelessness today and flew out of Peng Lake's range.

Landing on the shore, he resentfully looked toward Peng Island.

"Daoist Azure Yang, if you will not go, then do not blame us for not waiting for you."

After shouting loudly, he no longer lingered. He turned and left, already planning in his heart to go directly to the Sunken Sea with Cave Master Moyun.

Just as he turned...

Suddenly!

A thunderclap seemed to explode in the clear sky.

Ding Yi abruptly turned his head. The sight he saw made his pupils shrink sharply.

Large sheets of cloud and mist formed an inward-collapsing momentum, rolling toward the center of the eight hundred li of Peng Lake.

The lake surface, originally peaceful and without waves, now boiled, raising rolling waves.

Not only that, terrifying amounts of spiritual qi rose from each island and even from the lakebed, gathering in that direction like ten thousand rivers returning to the sea.

"This..."

While Ding Yi was shocked, in the next moment, the inward-collapsing cloud and mist flew back outward, once again covering the vast Peng Lake.

It seemed as though everything before had been an illusion, as if nothing had happened.

But what kind of person was Ding Yi? Even with the grand formation blocking him, he could still sense the boiling agitation of the spiritual qi here.

Especially in the misty, unknowable depths of Peng Lake, a terrifying atmosphere was currently brewing, as if an ancient desolate beast were slowly waking from deep sleep.

*(End of Chapter)*
