# Chapter 1126: Autumn Harvest, Reaping Rice

The next day.

Jing Lan opened her eyes from the bed, dazed for a moment, then as if remembering something, she abruptly looked to the other side.

When she saw the empty corner, her heart tightened in panic, and she quickly got up.

After pushing open the door, she finally breathed a sigh of relief upon seeing the figure standing in the yard.

"You're up so early!"

Luo Chen hummed in acknowledgment without turning his head, then asked, "Going to work this early?"

Jing Lan smiled and said, "Not today. The tobacco workshop gave us time off to tend to the crops in the fields. I was planning to cut some rice while it's still cool before the sun comes out."

Luo Chen understood, then said naturally, "I'll come with you!"

Jing Lan hesitated, looking at Luo Chen's thin, frail back. "But your body..."

"It's fine." Luo Chen turned around and smiled faintly. "I'd feel bad eating and drinking for free without doing anything."

"Well..." Jing Lan wavered for a moment, then said, "Wait for me a bit. I'll prepare the things."

With that, she went back inside.

Luo Chen remained standing outside, his expression neither sad nor happy.

His body was still far from recovering its peak condition, but simple activities wouldn't affect him much.

After all, he had the foundation of a desolate beast's physique—how could he be so delicate?

Moreover, he planned to use integration.
