# Chapter 650: The Benefits of Seven Stars, the Strengths of Many Schools

This concerned the trip to the Sunken Sea, so not the slightest carelessness was permitted.

Luo Chen intended to seize the chance to push his body refinement to the next realm. He did not know exactly how much time it would take, but his foundation was absolutely sufficient.

He still had plenty of fourth-tier Black Emperor Paste left, plus the enormous vitality he had absorbed on the Sunken Sea battlefield over the past year. He had complete confidence in crossing the final step.

He had even begun further developing offensive methods for his physique.

The Heavenly Peng true body was powerful, but it was straightforward and lacked variation.

This problem had already been exposed during the battle with Mountain Lord Hundred Craft on Seven Stars Island.

Previously, he had been delayed by developing the All-Encompassing Fire Prison. Now that time had opened up, he had to solve it.

Luo Chen's solution was very simple.

To widely gather the strengths of many schools.

And now, he indeed possessed the conditions to do so.

Seven-star demon hunter status greatly increased his authority compared to before.

It was enough for him to browse more intelligence and secrets collected by the Demon Hunter Office, and even exchange for all kinds of cultivation methods and secret arts.

At the same time, he constantly paid attention to the situation at the Sunken Sea.

On the second day after Luo Chen reported that a fourth-tier Demon Emperor had descended on the Sunken Sea, Blood Wanderer personally went to the Sunken Sea. With great divine ability, he forcefully defeated Jiaoren Emperor Flow Lord, causing him to flee from the Sunken Sea toward the depths of the Northern Sea.

Afterward, several days later, the three great eight-star demon hunters led fifty Golden Core cultivators of the Myriad Immortals Assembly, over ten thousand Foundation Establishment True Cultivators, and countless Qi Refinement cultivators to sweep across the Sunken Sea, clearing the battlefield.

Not only that.

The three families of Wuzhong Valley were also ordered by Moon Wanderer to send out the elites of their clans and sects to attack the ruined city of Taohai outside Nightmara Heaven.

They blocked and killed the low-rank demon beasts trying to escape from Nightmara Heaven at the exit.

During this process, Wuzhong Valley's Grand Elder, Imperial Spirit True Being, personally acted, breaking through the ruined city of Taohai and attempting to reinforce once again the outer sealing formation covering Nightmara Heaven.

This intelligence sounded simple when spoken, but by the time all of it reached Luo Chen's ears, more than half a year had already passed.

In a heavily guarded hall of the Demon Hunter Office, Luo Chen moved among bookshelves one after another, his eyes bright as he looked through ancient records.

At this moment, he held a green stone slab in his hand, frowning slightly as he looked at the introduction on it.

Extreme Mountain Six Forms: the purest physical killing moves of ancient body refiners. Grade unknown. The most important Mountain-Splitting Form and Extreme Mountain Form are lost, leaving only four killing moves. Body refiners enter the Dao through the body, comprehend changes in blood qi, and achieve the Immaculate True Body. Apart from blood qi, their physiques also far surpass qi refiners. This set of physical arts does not emphasize blood qi, but instead emphasizes use of the body. To learn it, one must possess a sufficiently powerful physique.

When he saw that the six killing moves lacked the strongest two forms, Luo Chen was somewhat disappointed.

But when he saw the description saying that this set of physical arts relied more on a powerful physique rather than changes in blood qi, Luo Chen's eyes lit up.

"Not bad. I will take it!"

Luo Chen took down the stone slab, then spent most of the day wandering through the Myriad Immortals Assembly's scripture hall before arriving on the first floor.

"Senior Daoist Azure Yang, have you finished choosing?"

"Mm. These are the ones. Please help me copy them."

"No trouble, no trouble. Let this junior first look them over and calculate the approximate price."

Luo Chen handed over a pile of jade slips, beast skins, and even that green stone slab. The sheer number made the eyelids of the Foundation Establishment cultivator stationed at the scripture hall twitch wildly.

However, after seeing that most were body-refinement arts and that many were incomplete, he was no longer surprised.

In the Myriad Immortals Assembly, quite a few Golden Core cultivators exchanged for one or two such records in their spare time, especially late Golden Core cultivators.

That Foundation Establishment cultivator looked them over while comparing them against the catalogue established by the Myriad Immortals Assembly and calculating the price.

Finally, he reached the corresponding result.

"Wind, Moon, Thunder, and Cloud Tread the Immortal Path lacks Wind Leg, Moon Step, and Cloud Shoe, leaving only the Thunder Kick form. Exchange price: seventy thousand spirit stones."

"Five Monument Hands, grade unknown. Only Golden Monument Hand and Jade Monument Hand remain. Exchange price: twenty thousand spirit stones."

"Extreme Mountain Six Forms is fairly complete, with four forms in total. Exchange price: one hundred twenty thousand low-grade spirit stones."

"Flowing Cloud Immortal-Seizing Finger has complete techniques, but the cultivation method is somewhat incomplete. Exchange price..."

"Senior, twelve secret manuals in total require seven hundred eighty thousand low-grade spirit stones." The Foundation Establishment cultivator raised his head and stated the total price.

Luo Chen was somewhat surprised. "So expensive?"

The Foundation Establishment cultivator smiled. "It is normal for them to be expensive. Now the qi-refinement path flourishes, and body refinement has long since declined. These body-refinement techniques and records were scattered everywhere, making them quite difficult to collect. Moreover, our qi refinement often injures our own bodies, so body-refinement methods also have the effect of using another mountain's stones to polish jade and are often used for reference. As time passed, the price rose. I hope Senior understands."

Luo Chen sighed emotionally, indicating that he understood.

Body-refinement methods were indeed hard to find in the present cultivation world.

Back then, when he and Wang Yuan refined their bodies, they could only explore by themselves. After great difficulty, they bought a set of Qi and Body Share One Origin at the Tianlan Immortal City auction, and even that could only be used as reference.

Later, he and Wang Yuan had both been forced onto the path of creating their own body-refinement methods.

For the Myriad Immortals Assembly to collect this many high-level body-refinement methods and physical killing moves, even if most were incomplete, was already extremely difficult.

"Fine. Seven hundred eighty thousand it is. This is my waist token. Use it for the discount."

"Uh..."

The Foundation Establishment cultivator was startled and accepted the waist token.

Seven-star demon hunter, thirty-percent price. Converted, it would only cost a bit over two hundred thousand.

Although he marveled at the other party's high star grade, the cultivator had no objection. In any case, he only collected a small copying fee. The major loss belonged to the Myriad Immortals Assembly.

He only said that it would take some time and asked Luo Chen to wait.

Luo Chen was not in a hurry. After leaving the other party time, he went out of the scripture hall and headed to another Treasure Pavilion.

Before long, he returned fully loaded under the enthusiastic sendoff of a false-core cultivator.

Three magic treasure-grade spirit beast pouches were in hand.

They came from the hands of a Sage artifact-forging master of the Canghai Alliance, so their quality was guaranteed.

Even top Great Demon Kings could be stored inside them. They were truly fine treasures.

Not only that, Luo Chen also bought a pile of artifact-forging materials.

By the time he returned, the scripture hall cultivator had already completed copying the texts he needed, placing them on jade slips one after another.

Luo Chen inspected them. Nothing was missing, and there were not even any related restrictions.

"There is no need to swear an oath to prevent secondary trading?"

"Senior worries too much. They are merely incomplete methods, and related to body refinement at that. The rules are not so strict. Of course, it would naturally be better if Senior paid some attention."

"Hehe, this seat understands."

Bringing a pile of jade slips and materials, Luo Chen returned fully loaded.

At the same time, Heavenjade also returned from her errands.

"Master, I have already ordered all the medicinal materials you wanted for True Flame Pills. According to your meaning, this batch of materials is enough for five thousand refinements."

"What about the price?"

"For those that could use Demon Hunter Office channels, I reported Master's name as a seven-star demon hunter, so there will naturally be a discount. For those that could not use Demon Hunter Office channels, after hearing Master's fearsome reputation and the terrifying quantity of materials needed, those forces also gave the highest discount. Overall, this batch of materials' cost was controlled under ten million spirit stones."

Ten million?

Luo Chen nodded and revealed a satisfied smile.

The cost of refining one furnace of True Flame Pills was very high, far higher than Starry Pills.

Even though he had long refined this pill, had not only raised its proficiency to Great Perfection but also increased the pill yield rate of supreme-grade True Flame Pills from a fixed ten percent to twenty percent, the cost remained high.

Ordinarily, if he wanted to purchase five thousand sets of raw materials in one breath, the cost would be at least thirty or forty million.

Now, with the convenience of seven-star demon hunter status, he could fleece the Myriad Immortals Assembly.

Added to the external devilish prestige of "Devil Lord Azure Yang," he had actually saved the cost down to around ten million.

Reputation was truly quite useful sometimes.

"For these five thousand sets of raw materials, I will not refine upper-grade True Flame Pills this time. I will use all of them to refine supreme-grade True Flame Pills. They are not only for cultivation, but also to keep as backup for replenishing mana during the Sunken Sea trip."

"With my current alchemy attainments, I should be able to obtain ten thousand supreme-grade True Flame Pills. No matter what, that will be enough."

Luo Chen mentally calculated, then asked aloud, "How is Black King?"

Heavenjade answered without thinking. "Ever since Master gave him that Sea-Overturning Demon Jiao corpse, he has been hastening to digest it. According to my last observation, his breakthrough to late third-tier is already certain."

"This fellow eats and drinks, sleeps a great sleep, and can still advance. What luck."

Luo Chen laughed and scolded, but he was not surprised at the other's cultivation speed.

Over so many years, relying on large amounts of supreme-grade Emperor Flow Nectar, Black King had long since shed the miserable aptitude of a Black-Scaled Giant Python and gained a terrifying foundation.

Added to the large number of demon beasts he had devoured in earlier years, especially the cliff serpent corpse that had failed to break through to fourth tier, it was only normal that he would now progress further by relying on the Sea-Overturning Demon Jiao corpse.

However...

"Heavenjade, your cultivation cannot fall behind either."

Heavenjade's expression darkened. She had clearly received a cultivation method tailored by her master and had a transformation body, yet after all these years, she was still stuck at the third level of Golden Core and had been unable to break through to the middle stage. It was truly somewhat embarrassing.

She wanted to explain that she had been busy handling trivial affairs for Luo Chen, but Master's occasional guidance in cultivation completely made up for that deficiency.

'In the end, my aptitude is insufficient.'

She sighed inwardly.

Just as she grew gloomy, a storage bag flew before her, carried by mana.

"Inside are all the third-tier Starry Pills I refined when I first left the Flying Swallow Islands, as well as the Emperor Flow Nectar I occasionally refined over the years. Their grades are all quite good. Take them. Do not be stingy. Consume them in large amounts within a controllable range and strive to break through to the fourth level of Golden Core within these few years."

Heavenjade was startled. Immediately, wild joy surged onto her face.

"Master, I..."

"Cultivate well. Do not manage those miscellaneous affairs recently either. Let the Zhang clan consider them themselves."

Luo Chen patted Heavenjade's shoulder and stepped into Star Dou Hall.

Many cultivation methods had all been obtained.

With dense vitality supporting him, his physique could completely rise another level.

Now that everything was ready, he should finish it in one breath.
