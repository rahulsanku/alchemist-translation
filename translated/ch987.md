# Chapter 987: Buddhist Divine Ability, Three Heads and Six Arms

"Fellow Luo, you harbor many hidden troubles, but most are neither great nor small—each can be remedied through cultivation. Only two of them are like fishbones stuck in the throat, impossible to ignore!"

Monk Xuan Du spoke unhurriedly, as if he had seen through Luo Chen's current situation at a single glance.

Luo Chen immediately became interested.

He was well aware that he had hidden troubles, but he thought there was only one.

Yet the other party spoke of two—could there be one he himself didn't know about?

"Please speak frankly, Great Master!"

Monk Xuan Du smiled faintly and pointed through the air at Luo Chen's brow.

"A deficient destiny soul—that is the first."

Luo Chen was not surprised that the other could see this. This Glazed Pure Land was quite miraculous; within it, one's entire body was as transparent as glazed crystal. Since the monk could perceive the flaws in Azure Frost's soul, he could naturally detect this hidden trouble in Luo Chen as well.

But being so directly called out still weighed on Luo Chen's heart.

With three parts expectation, he pressed further: "Is there a solution?"

Monk Xuan Du shook his head.

Luo Chen was startled. "If there's no solution, how can the Great Master claim to have conditions that would move me?"

Monk Xuan Du laughed. "The destiny soul is the foundation of the soul. Ghost cultivators struggle to achieve the Great Dao precisely because they naturally lack three parts of their destiny soul. How could I possibly make up for yours? But Fellow Luo is ultimately not a ghost cultivator, and the depth of your soul foundation is rarely seen in this world—thus, there is a thread of hope."

Luo Chen's expression grew serious as he listened attentively.

"Given Fellow Luo's foundation, you must have already reached the stage of condensing your primordial spirit, yet you have been unable to succeed because of your deficient destiny soul. I have a dharma mantra that can help you bypass this flaw and forcibly condense your primordial spirit."

Luo Chen's eyes instantly lit up.

"Truly?"

"A monk does not speak falsely!" Monk Xuan Du sincerely bowed to the Buddha, then said bluntly: "But this method only treats the symptoms, not the root cause. If you wish to cultivate without hindrance, Fellow Luo must still find a way to complete your destiny soul."

Luo Chen fell into deep thought.

Clearly, that dharma mantra could only resolve the immediate crisis. The primordial spirit condensed through it would likely have defects, making it utterly unsuitable for rushing toward the Ascendant realm.

But it was still a path!

Moreover, if he could first condense his primordial spirit and then go to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect to retrieve his destiny soul, it would be much safer.

After some deliberation, he said reluctantly: "I am somewhat tempted, but a single dharma mantra is not enough to make me risk my life for you."

This meant he wanted to hear the second condition.

Monk Xuan Du was not surprised and directly stated Luo Chen's second hidden trouble—one that truly caught Luo Chen off guard.

"Fellow Luo's magnificent physique is too mixed and chaotic, and it is now suppressing you instead."

"Eh..." Luo Chen was baffled.

Monk Xuan Du explained in detail: "Has Fellow Luo not noticed? Your physique is so formidable that it is no less than Azure Frost's divine beast true body, no weaker than the Buddhist Great Vajra physique, and even comparable to the orthodox body cultivator's Leakless Golden Body. Yet when you fight, you lack the seamless harmony of those three, and there are even many places where things feel awkward, are there not?"

Luo Chen's brow furrowed deeply.

He thought back to an unexpected discovery during his battle with Azure Frost.

That was when source power had washed over him but could not wash away the strange dharma power she had left on his body, rendering his flesh-regeneration ability nearly useless.

At the time, he had thought it was due to Azure Frost's bizarre techniques. But now that he thought about it, how could source power be so weak?

Where had things gone wrong?

He chewed on the word "mixed" that Monk Xuan Du had used, suspecting that the problem lay in the many body-tempering methods he had explored on his own without guidance.

First, he had walked the path of baleful qi body-tempering, then taken the path of demonic transformation, supplemented by thunder-fire refinement, inner alchemy and external medicine, gradually stepping onto the archaic cultivation path.

But the archaic body-tempering method had its upper limit, forcing him to draw from the Southern Frontier's gu arts and undergo the cruel hundred-poison body-tempering.

Although all methods ultimately converged into source power, were they truly without problems?

Why could that unnamed mixed poison not merge into the source power, remaining independent instead?

As Luo Chen pondered painfully, Monk Xuan Du's gentle voice continued in his ear.

"For Fellow Luo's situation, there are actually many solutions, but all require giving up a part."

"I imagine Fellow Luo is very unwilling to discard all the cultivation you have gained, which is why you need a method that achieves both ends."

"Our Buddhist school has a body-tempering divine ability that is famous throughout the world. If Fellow Luo is willing, I would like to gift you this technique to resolve your hidden trouble!"

Luo Chen blinked. "May I ask what divine ability it is?"

Monk Xuan Du smiled faintly. "Merely Three Heads and Six Arms!"

Luo Chen was stunned.

This truly was a divine ability!

The Buddhist term "three heads and six arms" had already become an adjective for "divine ability."

But he knew that Buddhism truly possessed such a body-tempering method.

The Wrathful Vajra he had fought was rumored to be the abbot of a small Western Desert temple called Golden Mountain Temple. He had obtained a fragmentary Three Heads and Six Arms technique, which allowed him to advance rapidly in body-tempering.

Unfortunately, the method was ultimately incomplete. The Wrathful Vajra had only cultivated two heads and four arms, leaving his physical body flawed—so much so that Luo Chen had personally shattered his golden body.

Now, Monk Xuan Du was willing to pass on such a supreme method to him?

"With the Three Heads and Six Arms method, you can sort through yourself, differentiate the various techniques, preserve them all, and keep them from interfering with each other. In battle, its power will double, leaving few able to oppose you."

"Fellow Luo, what do you think of this reward?"

Monk Xuan Du asked gently.

This time, Luo Chen was truly tempted.

He instinctively looked at Azure Frost. Though she said nothing, the tacit understanding between them told Luo Chen that she too was tempted.

Of course, even now Luo Chen had not yet made up his mind.

"The two of us are heavily injured. Even if we obtain these methods, we won't be able to form combat strength in a short time. I'm afraid we won't be of much help to you, Great Master!"

Monk Xuan Du waved his hand. "That is easy to resolve!"

Luo Chen was somewhat surprised. The injuries he and Azure Frost bore would take at least several years to fully recover, even with the aid of a spirit land and miraculous pills.

Yet in the other's mouth, it seemed like the simplest thing.

With that, was there any reason to refuse?

Hesitating, Luo Chen suddenly asked: "Earlier, the Great Master mentioned that Master Huiping belongs to the Chan sect, Mahakala belongs to the Pure sect, and the Hanging Temple claims to be the world's Buddhist ancestral court. Which lineage does the Great Master come from?"

Monk Xuan Du pressed his palms together in a Buddhist greeting and said: "I primarily cultivate Mahayana Buddhism and serve the Moonlight Glazed Bodhisattva."

...

The Glazed Pure Land was vast.

Three places were most conspicuous: the Buddhist temple, the bamboo sea, and the pagoda forest.

And at this moment, Luo Chen was right in the midst of the layered, boundless white pagoda forest.

"All beings dwell in the sea of suffering, and the sea of suffering is hell. The Glazed Pure Land does not have the eighteen hells, but it has the eight cold and eight hot hells. If Fellow Luo wishes to quickly master the Three Heads and Six Arms method, the Eight Cold Hells are the place for rapid achievement."

A calm, balanced voice came from afar. As light flowed, the surrounding white pagodas began to tremble.

Luo Chen was not surprised.

He and Azure Frost had already reached an agreement with Monk Xuan Du.

On the condition of generous rewards, they would help Monk Xuan Du resist external enemies and assist him in breaking through to the Ascendant realm.

But the external enemies had not yet arrived, so they still had ample time.

Taking advantage of this time, Luo Chen and Azure Frost naturally began to recover their condition and test whether the methods Monk Xuan Du had given them could truly resolve their hidden troubles.

It had to be said that the white-robed monk was a generous host.

Not only did he provide the methods, but he also gave them matching cultivation grounds.

These Eight Cold Hells were the perfect environment to help Luo Chen rapidly master the Three Heads and Six Arms method!

As the white pagodas trembled, vast amounts of cold air surged up from underground. In an instant, the place became a land of raging blizzards, frozen glaciers, and blocked passes.

The power of extreme cold began to accumulate continuously.

Luo Chen had not yet gathered his mind. Looking outward, he pondered Monk Xuan Du's actions.

"From Azure Frost's and my probing, he has already condensed his primordial spirit and possesses the Great Vajra physique. His foundation is naturally solid."

"But without a fifth-order spirit vein, how will he break through to the Ascendant realm?"

"Earlier, when I observed with my Crimson-Eyed Golden Pupils, this Glazed Pure Land was absorbing external spiritual mechanism. Could it be that the key to breaking through to Ascendant lies in spiritual mechanism, not ordinary spiritual qi? And this Glazed Pure Land, a spatial spirit treasure, is replacing a fifth-order spirit vein, frantically absorbing spiritual mechanism to fuel his breakthrough?"

It had to be said that Luo Chen's reasoning was very sound, even close to the truth.

But that made the problem obvious.

For the Glazed Pure Land to absorb external spiritual mechanism, it could not close its entrance.

Being so conspicuous, the day the Hanging Temple monks arrived was already numbered.

"I wonder how much time Azure Frost and I can buy?"

Shaking his head, Luo Chen cut off his distracting thoughts, sat cross-legged on the ground, and took out two items.

A jade slip and a string of prayer beads.

The jade slip recorded the Three Heads and Six Arms method, while the prayer beads were for cultivating the Buddhist dharma mantra used to condense the primordial spirit.

Looking at the two items, what tempted him most was naturally the Moonlight Pure Heart Mantra, which could ignore the deficient destiny soul and forcibly condense the primordial spirit.

But after some deliberation, he suppressed this impulse.

This mantra treated the symptoms, not the root cause. Learning it in the short term would be useless.

Better to focus on the great divine ability of Three Heads and Six Arms for forging the golden body!

"I also want to know whether this divine ability is as miraculous as Xuan Du claims—whether it can help me sort through my mixed techniques and unify all my powers!"

Ignoring the increasingly cold temperature outside, Luo Chen carefully studied the jade slip.

In just half a day, he had completely memorized it.

But that was all—just memorized.

The jade slip was filled with Buddhist secret language, not only obscure and difficult to pronounce but also extremely hard to understand.

Monk Xuan Du had probably never expected Luo Chen to master this method quickly. He had only wanted Luo Chen to recover his condition as soon as possible in these Eight Cold Hells.

But who was Luo Chen?

He had a cheat!

Divine abilities were at the fifth-order level.

To get started, he only needed a hundred achievement points!

Luo Chen glanced at the attribute panel, which had been gathering dust for years. With a thought, he smoothly invested achievement points into the Three Heads and Six Arms divine ability.

In an instant, light flickered.

And on the physical arts column, a new entry had already appeared.

[Three Heads and Six Arms: Beginner 1/100 (Divine Ability)]

As expected, this physical art was indeed at the divine ability level.

But unlike the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger, which was understood once and for all, Three Heads and Six Arms had a proficiency system.

Clearly, this was a divine ability that could be gradually mastered through hard cultivation and physical tempering.

Such a divine ability had a relatively low threshold, and its upper limit was probably not high either.

But Luo Chen didn't care. As long as it could truly solve his problems and improve his strength, it didn't matter.

He had even cultivated the demonic transformation method in his early days—why not a Buddhist divine ability?

When the system forcibly initiated him, Luo Chen immediately sensed some subtle changes in his body.

Source power still flowed like a great river within him.

But vigorous blood qi, the omnipresent mixed poison, and strands of dharma power were all entangled with each other, influencing and balancing one another.

In the past, Luo Chen had pursued a single fusion, concentrating all power into one point.

But now it seemed that without sufficient realm, fusion only tied his hands, while sorting and differentiation was better.

Just then, a surging cold current struck from outside.

His physical body reacted instinctively, and vast dharma power immediately began to resist the cold current.

The Eight Cold Hells were divided into eight minor hells: Blisters, Chattering Teeth, Splitting Like Blue Lotuses, and so on.

Within them, the physical body was greatly suppressed, requiring different powers to deal with each.

This passive response was just right for sorting through the powers in Luo Chen's body, then using the Three Heads and Six Arms method to guide them into different heads by category.

The first to be activated was dharma power—also one of Luo Chen's strongest forces.

Then came source power, the unconverted surging blood qi, mixed poison, baleful qi, pill qi...

When he saw the extremely refined baleful qi and pill qi, Luo Chen was quite shocked.

Since his cultivation had achieved some success, he had been using the perfected Cleansing Art and Dragon-Slaying Art to remove the foreign auras and powers from his body, to avoid revealing his characteristics.

Yet he had never expected that such pure baleful qi and pill qi were still hidden deep within his flesh.

"Finding them is a good thing."

Luo Chen muttered and continued to push his physical limits, trying to force out more residual power from his body.

As time passed, when a wisp of pure black qi emerged from his arm, Luo Chen was even stunned for a moment.

He actually had demonic qi on him?

But in an instant, he realized that this demonic qi most likely originated from the Yuantu Sword!

He had never imagined that the Yuantu Sword, which he nurtured day and night, while seemingly influenced by him, was also continuously affecting him in return.

No wonder Xuan Du saw at a glance that my studies were mixed. With so many powers mixed in my body, I'm like a powder keg. It's only because my dharma power and source power are strong enough that I've suppressed all the mixed forces.

After muttering to himself, Luo Chen continued to push his physical potential.

When no more extra power emerged, he finally began to laboriously circulate the Three Heads and Six Arms method, starting to differentiate and control the many powers.

Inside the Glazed Buddhist Temple.

When Luo Chen began to properly circulate the Three Heads and Six Arms method, Monk Xuan Du opened his eyes in considerable surprise.

"So fast?"

He had thought that Luo Chen's mixed studies meant he must be exceptionally talented, able to incorporate the strengths of many schools.

But he had never expected the other's talent to be so formidable.

In just a few days, he had directly taken up a body-tempering divine ability.

It was said to have a low threshold, but that was only on the basis of a powerful physical body. Few could meet that condition.

Monk Xuan Du couldn't help but pay more attention to Luo Chen.

As feedback from the Eight Cold Hells arrived, he grew increasingly shocked.

The profound power like an ancient desolate beast, the extremely refined baleful qi, and that faint, elusive demonic qi...

"How much slaughter has this Luo Chen committed to have such terrifying baleful qi!"

"And he even wields a demonic treasure!"

"I wonder if letting him cultivate the Three Heads and Six Arms method is good or bad. I hope I haven't sealed a fallen Buddha only to raise a great demon."

"I can only hope he values the Moonlight Pure Heart Mantra, upholds his Dao heart, and does not fall into evil."

Murmuring to himself, Monk Xuan Du no longer focused on Luo Chen's cultivation, nor did he pay attention to Azure Frost in the Glazed Jade Pure Vase.

Because as the days passed, winds and clouds had finally begun to gather outside the Glazed Pure Land.

They had arrived.

(Chapter end)
