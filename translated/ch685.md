# Chapter 685: The System Opens a Cheat, Another Path Is Found, Three Pills Become One, the Assessment Is Passed

For the Spirit-Root Pill formula, Luo Chen prepared Heaven-Earth Root as the main material, with Nightwing Flower, Purple Lord Leaf, and thirteen other medicinal materials as auxiliaries.

Beyond that, he had also selected several dozen other medicinal materials as backup auxiliary ingredients, ready to be swapped in at any time.

Did that sound like too few?

In truth, that number was already quite large.

In Luo Chen's view, although the conjecture Devil Lord Heaven-Refining had left behind was somewhat outrageous, it was ultimately meant for mortals who had not yet stepped onto the path of cultivation.

Therefore, the grade of the Spirit-Root Pill could not possibly be too high.

If it were too high, mortals would not be able to withstand the relatively terrifying medicinal force.

And for ordinary first-tier or second-tier pills, the number of main materials was not like third-tier or fourth-tier pills, where several kinds often existed at once.

As for auxiliary materials, Luo Chen had already chosen more than enough.

To bring out the advantages of the Heaven-Earth Root while avoiding as many of its flaws as possible, the auxiliary material combinations involved were an extremely difficult matter even for a fourth-tier alchemist, and required careful weighing.

Fortunately, now that the main direction had been established, he only needed to feel his way forward bit by bit.

Within the Pill Realm, Luo Chen's figure shuttled among three mountains and five lakes. The herb basket on his back filled, was changed out, and filled again.

One medicinal material after another that he required was personally harvested by his own hands.

It had to be said that this was work that looked tedious, yet brought an immense sense of harvest.

Luo Chen also applied some of the knowledge he had learned from the bamboo slips in the study to the practical act of gathering herbs. Another portion of his herb-gathering experience came from the accumulation of the first half of his life.

It was also at this moment that he suddenly realized why most of this world's medicinal materials were five hundred years old or below.

In a marshy valley, Luo Chen carefully dug out a section of Withered Chen White, a medicinal material similar to fleeceflower root.

Straightening his back, the Daoist looked all around.

"If this world was a medicinal garden established specifically for the Spirit-Root Pill assessment, then it is only natural that the age of these medicinal materials would be low."

"Mortals cannot bear the powerful medicinal force brought by materials of too great an age."

"Only, how did Devil Lord Heaven-Refining keep the age of these medicinal materials neither too high nor too low? Among all the methods I know, not one can accomplish this."

After sighing over it for a while, Luo Chen set out in search of the next medicinal material.

Herb gathering was only a trivial bit of minor work.

After spending roughly half a month, Luo Chen successfully gathered most of the medicinal materials he needed within the Pill Realm.

Then he returned to the stone house's Herb Room and began processing them in different ways.

Every kind of medicinal material required a different handling method. Some could be used directly as medicine, some had to be sun-dried in advance, and others had to be ground into powder and set aside.

It was precisely during this process that Luo Chen's understanding of these medicinal materials deepened, and with it, he gained more ideas about the Spirit-Root Pill formula he had written before.

While keeping the Heaven-Earth Root unchanged as the main material, he tried replacing the auxiliary ingredients.

Time flowed away, bit by bit.

Another half a year passed. Once everything was prepared,

Luo Chen did not immediately begin attempting to refine the pill.

Instead, he stilled his heart, concentrated his spirit, and opened his attribute panel.

There was no look of apprehension on his face, because he had already tried earlier to see whether he could open the attribute panel here.

Only, unlike usual, when he mainly opened the basic attribute section, this time he only opened the alchemist portion of the Profession section.

Profession section:

[Fourth-Tier Alchemist]

[Alchemy Arts: Scale-Turning Hand at Great Completion, Burning Crane Hand at Great Completion, Great Completion Alchemy (open for details)]

[Fourth-tier pills: Transformation Pill, Beginner 5/100; Purple Qi Poison-Resisting Pill, Beginner 30/100]

Third-tier pills: Starry Pill at Great Completion; True Flame Pill at Great Completion; Nether Origin Pill, Beginner 6/100

Second-tier pills: Blood-Stasis Transforming Powder, Perfect 376/500

First-tier pills: Condensing Water Pill at Great Completion

More Great Completion pills (open for details)]

After most of the contents were collapsed, the alchemist attribute panel appeared before him in a simple, clean form.

Compared with years ago, a few unfamiliar pill entries had been added, though they were not truly unfamiliar.

The Purple Qi Poison-Resisting Pill had been improved by Luo Chen from a fourth-tier poison-avoidance formula specifically for the trip to Purple Spirit Island. After that journey succeeded, Luo Chen stopped there, leaving only a few pills in reserve and no longer refining it.

Among the third-tier pills, the one that had not yet reached Great Completion was the Nether Origin Pill. This was a pill formula he had learned back then in order to break through to Golden Core.

And among the second-tier pills, the newly added entry was Blood-Stasis Transforming Powder. This was a pill he had newly learned in order to recover sooner from hidden injuries left on his body by body tempering.

But at this moment, none of those mattered.

Luo Chen's gaze swept to the achievement point count at the end of the panel: [300].

This was his greatest reliance!

Originally, he had 334 achievement points, but earlier, in order to quickly enter the fourth-tier Aperture-Opening Art, he had spent 34 points. To Luo Chen, that expenditure was worthwhile, and somewhat beyond his expectations.

He had originally thought a fourth-tier spell would cost even more achievement points. Yet after grasping the prerequisites for the Aperture-Opening Art, namely Origin Pill Art and Locked Pearl Curtain, he had spent only thirty-four points at the time and successfully entered it.

Now he had a full three hundred points remaining. For this derivation of a pill formula, that gave Luo Chen immense confidence.

"Even if someone is walking ahead of me, if the examination question is the same, then no matter how formidable that person's alchemy is, as long as they have not reached the level of the Pill Saint of Central Continent, they will have to test and err bit by bit, consuming countless time and energy."

"I am different. With the panel in hand, I have no need to waste all that time!"

"Even the actual refining process can save effort."

Luo Chen smiled slightly, his confidence growing stronger in his heart.

In his view, the one who had gone ahead might have a high attainment in alchemy. Otherwise, they would not have crossed the first trial in less than three years after Demon-Fall Land opened.

But to say they could rival that Pill Saint of Central Continent from legend?

That was impossible.

How could such an existence come here and place themselves in danger?

His smile drew in slightly. Luo Chen took a deep breath, and a Spirit-Root Pill formula he had adjusted earlier surfaced in his mind.

In an instant, corresponding information silently flowed out on the system.

[Detected unknown pill formula, incomplete version. Complete it?]

Luo Chen frowned. This was the formula he had the most confidence in after careful selection, and he felt it was extremely mature.

It was still an incomplete version?

With a thought, he sent another pill formula into the system.

[Detected unknown pill formula, incomplete version. Complete it?]

[Detected unknown pill formula, incomplete version. Complete it?]

Incomplete again and again, and each time the system asked whether to complete it. This meant that the formulas Luo Chen had derived could not be considered mature.

Luo Chen was somewhat displeased, but his mood was not too heavy.

In his view, this Spirit-Root Pill formula was only second tier. Completing it would cost no more than ten achievement points.

But he restrained the impulse to complete it.

"This is only the first trial. No one knows how many trials there are afterward, nor how difficult they will be. These three hundred achievement points are the greatest confidence I have accumulated over many years. Best not to waste them lightly."

After murmuring this, Luo Chen returned to the desk and continued writing swiftly.

He tried making new arrangements and combinations, letting the system check them. Yet under the system's examination, all medicinal material combinations were incomplete.

Several days later, Luo Chen sat blankly on the chair, his eyes bloodshot, when his expression suddenly stirred.

"Perhaps by wanting to take a shortcut, I instead walked down the wrong path?"

Looking at the densely packed drafts on the desk, Luo Chen clearly felt traces of success growing farther and farther away.

From the pile of drafts, he found the first few pill formulas.

After reading more than ten thousand scrolls, his earliest intuition should have been the correct one.

The reason they were incomplete should not lie in the medicinal material combination, but in the dosage ratio...

Once he had this thought, he began modifying the pill formula from a different angle.

...

Another month later, Luo Chen obtained a satisfying answer from the system.

[Detected complete Spirit-Root Pill formula, second tier.]

[Enter?]

In an instant, surprise and delight appeared on his face.

As expected, this line of thought was feasible.

By reading a vast number of medicinal materials, relying on his own understanding, selecting the optimal solution, and narrowing the scope,

Then correcting the medicinal material ratios and even adjusting the refining method, before using the system to check whether it was complete,

He could resolve problems that most alchemists had no way to improve.

Perhaps doing so carried the faint suspicion of taking a shortcut.

But Luo Chen was no pedantic person. A slight sense of moral guilt would not affect him.

Because he had essentially researched the concrete formula himself. Success was close at hand. He had merely borrowed the system to accelerate that process and avoid meaningless trial and error in the middle.

"Then next, I begin attempting the refinement!"

With a murmur, Luo Chen walked toward the Pill Room and took out the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

This time, however, he did not choose to directly use achievement points to enter the formula.

For a formula he had derived himself, everything was under his control.

In addition, it was only second tier. With his current alchemy, he had more than enough ability.

The large quantity of medicinal materials prepared earlier came into use at this moment.

Several months later.

Inside the Pill Room, a strange pill fragrance drifted out.

Luo Chen held several mottled pills in his hands, his face full of relief.

"Does this count as success?"

He looked around. No unusual phenomenon had appeared!

Yet the Spirit-Root Pill had indeed been refined by him.

It was at this moment that a somewhat weak voice exploded from the unseen, echoing inside the Pill Room.

"Pill formed: second-tier lower-grade. It can temporarily substitute for a spirit root and absorb the spiritual qi of heaven and earth. After consumption, its medicinal power can remain undiminished for ten years."

"Assessment result: inferior-low. Failed."

The moment those words emerged, Luo Chen's expression changed greatly.

He was shocked by the voice's appearance, and also baffled by the assessment result.

"Where did this voice come from?"

"And who made it?"

"Why does it carry an unconcealable sense of weakness?"

Naturally, no one could answer these questions for him.

Luo Chen took a deep breath and set all his doubts upon the assessment result.

"That should not be!"

"The pill was clearly refined. Even my own system evaluated it as a complete pill formula. How could..."

As he murmured to himself, Luo Chen suddenly recalled the contents of that voice's statement.

"It can temporarily substitute for a spirit root and absorb the spiritual qi of heaven and earth. After consumption, its medicinal power can remain undiminished for ten years."

This line was a description of the medicinal effect of the Spirit-Root Pill he had refined. The other party had never consumed this pill, so how could such an evaluation be made?

Unfortunately, there was no medicine tester in front of him.

Luo Chen hardened his heart and directly swallowed a Spirit-Root Pill. Then he immediately sat cross-legged and sensed the changes in the medicinal force.

A mere second-tier pill was nothing at his current realm. Even if there were backlash, he could withstand it by force!

One day later, Luo Chen faintly sensed where the problem lay.

"This Spirit-Root Pill does indeed have the effect of temporarily substituting for a spirit root and absorbing the spiritual qi of heaven and earth, but the medicinal force is too powerful. Only Qi Refinement cultivators can bear it."

"But the group this pill faces is mortals. They want to use this pill to cultivate to the Qi Refinement stage."

"In that case, it contradicts itself."

For a time, Luo Chen's expression shifted between dark and bright.

In the end, he had overestimated mortals' ability to withstand pills.

Second tier had already exceeded the syllabus!

But how could he resolve it?

Refine a first-tier Spirit-Root Pill?

That was impossible. A first-tier pill could not have such a miraculous effect. Moreover, a pill refined with a five-hundred-year Heaven-Earth Root as the main material could not be too low in grade.

This doubt persisted for several days.

Until Luo Chen recalled the process by which he had consumed pills when his realm was low, and how it differed from others.

A powerful body, detoxifying alchemy, and so on.

All of those had allowed him, at the same realm, to withstand greater and stronger medicinal force.

Since he could not lower the Spirit-Root Pill's grade, could improving mortal physiques become another path, helping people cultivate by a different route?

At this thought, countless sparks of inspiration instantly surfaced in Luo Chen's mind.

Immediately, he stepped into the study and continued writing swiftly.

Three months later, Luo Chen returned to the Pill Room with two pill formulas in hand.

This time, he did not stay inside for very long.

After only two months, two more kinds of pills appeared in his hands.

At the same time, that somewhat strange and weak-toned voice appeared once more.

"Five Viscera Origin-Nurturing Pill. Pill formed: first-tier lower-grade. It can help mortals nourish the five viscera."

"Snow Spirit Meridian-Opening Powder. Pill formed: first-tier lower-grade. Long-term consumption can help mortals open the meridians and clear the mysterious pass."

"Spirit-Root Pill. Pill formed: second-tier lower-grade. Used together with the Five Viscera Origin-Nurturing Pill and Snow Spirit Meridian-Opening Powder, it can help a person absorb the spiritual qi of heaven and earth, and its medicinal force can remain undiminished for thirty years."

"Assessment result: good-middle... passed!"

The moment those words emerged, Luo Chen's face instantly filled with wild joy.

As expected!

His conjecture had been correct.

When his own foundation could not further optimize the Spirit-Root Pill, opening another path and researching auxiliary pills to strengthen a mortal's physique was the better choice!

Thirty years!

That amount of time was already enough.

If within thirty years a mortal could not use the Spirit-Root Pill to cultivate to the Initiate stage, then everything would end.

But if they succeeded by chance, they would be able to establish a Great Dao foundation!

At that time, it would count as initially accomplishing the conjecture of breaking the divide between immortal and mortal.

As for how one should continue cultivating after relying on a pill-spirit-root to reach the Initiate stage, that was not something Luo Chen could resolve now.

Or rather, one step at a time. When that time came, perhaps a better method could be found.

At the very least, with the dantian transformed into a sea, the qi sea opened, and the Great Dao foundation established, it would be far better than when they had been mortal.

If compared to those false-core cultivators in the Northern Sea, who relied on demon beast inner cores to cultivate to the Golden Core stage, it was easier to understand.

False-core cultivators were also unable to advance to the middle stage of Golden Core for life.

But those who chose that path had naturally prepared themselves long ago.

Thinking to this point, another spark of inspiration seemed to surface in Luo Chen's mind.

From a pill-spirit-root, what if one changed paths and walked the route of a demon-spirit-root?

The details within were like cloud and fog, and he could not grasp the method, but at least there was now a road.

Just as Luo Chen was excited, the surroundings suddenly became illusory.

"This is?"

In his shock, Luo Chen seemed to realize something.

With a thought, he hurriedly flew toward the study, raised his large hand, and swept it toward the bamboo slips.

However, something unexpected happened.

Those bamboo slips were also becoming unreal at the same time.

"I cannot take them away?"

Luo Chen felt somewhat regretful.

In the next moment, the stars shifted and the world turned. He appeared in a strange land.

*(End of Chapter)*
