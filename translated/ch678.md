# Chapter 678: The Primordial Unity Cauldron and the Octagonal Pavilion, Forced Suppression

The moment Luo Chen appeared, Angler and Purple Dowager recognized him.

"Luo Hai!"

"Daoist Azure Yang!"

Their voices overlapped, each carrying disbelief.

In Myriad Immortals Assembly, Luo Chen's public identity was Luo Hai.

In the Northern Sea's broader cultivation world, many people knew him by another title.

Devil Lord Azure Yang.

That title had not come from courtesy.

It had been carved out through pill skill, fire arts, and repeated killings.

Among ordinary Golden Core cultivators, the name already carried enough weight to make people cautious.

But Angler and Purple Dowager were not ordinary Golden Cores.

They were eight-star demon hunters, people who had fought fourth-tier demons and lived.

He had risen too quickly in these years. From an outside Golden Core alchemist, he had become a figure who could stand among the top demon hunters, slay fourth-tier beasts, and contend with Sage shadows. Even so, Angler and Purple Dowager had never imagined that they would meet him here, at this exact moment, with Ghost Spirit Child lying half-dead under his foot.

No.

Not half-dead.

Purple Dowager's spiritual sense swept across Ghost Spirit Child and her expression changed.

His aura had already scattered.

The ghost qi that had always surrounded him was vanishing into the mud.

Ghost Spirit Child was dead.

One kick.

A single kick had killed an eight-star demon hunter.

Angler's hand tightened around the black-gold fishing rod.

He had fought alongside Ghost Spirit Child more than once. That man's methods were strange, his body elusive, and his life-saving arts extremely difficult to deal with. Even if Angler personally attacked, he would not dare say he could kill him in a single exchange.

Yet Luo Chen had done it casually, as though crushing a worm on the road.

Purple Dowager drew in a quiet breath. "Fellow Daoist Luo's body cultivation has become more and more unimaginable."

Luo Chen smiled faintly. "Without Sage protection, if I want to enter the Fallen Sea, I naturally need a body that can withstand some trouble."

The words were casual.

But both Angler and Purple Dowager heard the hidden meaning.

He had never treated the Fallen Sea as a place where he could rely on others forever.

If a cultivator wanted to survive there, the most reliable thing was still his own strength.

Angler's face was dark. "Fellow Daoist Luo, this Octagonal Pavilion was something we seized first. Is taking it like this not a little too excessive?"

Luo Chen looked at him. "You seized it from Penglai. I seize it from you. Where is the excess?"

Angler was momentarily speechless.

Purple Dowager's eyes flickered. "We are all from Myriad Immortals Assembly."

"Were."

Luo Chen's answer was short.

Angler understood the meaning behind that word, and his heart sank.

But the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion was too valuable.

It was a true artifact.

It was also a movable spirit herb garden, and the Divine Iris garden had already been moved inside. If he simply let Luo Chen take it, how could he explain himself to Moon Wanderer?

"Then I can only offend you."

Angler flicked his wrist.

The black-gold fishing rod bent like a full moon.

The Heaven-Remnant Line shot out soundlessly, thinner than a hair and sharper than a blade, winding toward Luo Chen's wrist. At the same time, a small bronze bell flew from Angler's sleeve and rang without wind.

Sound-Capturing Bell [TERM?].

The bell tone did not spread outward.

It collapsed inward, trying to seize the rhythm of Luo Chen's breath and heartbeat.

Purple Dowager also moved.

She did not attack openly. Her silver needles scattered into the air and vanished, hiding in blind spots around Luo Chen's body.

The two had cooperated almost instinctively.

Angler restrained head-on.

Purple Dowager waited for openings.

If Luo Chen used a magic treasure to block the Heaven-Remnant Line, the needles would search for his exposed meridians.

If he attacked Purple Dowager first, Angler's line would bind his limbs.

Facing two eight-star demon hunters, Luo Chen only raised one hand.

He grabbed the Heaven-Remnant Line directly.

Angler's pupils shrank.

The Heaven-Remnant Line was not an ordinary treasure. It was famous for being difficult to detect and difficult to break. When driven by his secret art, it could cut protective light, bind magic treasures, and even injure the meridians of body cultivators.

Luo Chen had actually caught it with his bare hand.

Angler immediately shook the fishing rod.

The line vibrated at high speed.

Fine blood marks appeared on Luo Chen's palm, but they were only shallow cuts.

Luo Chen looked at the red marks and smiled. "Not bad."

Then he clenched his fingers.

The Heaven-Remnant Line groaned.

Angler's face changed sharply. He spat a mouthful of tongue blood onto the rod and used the blood to activate the line's hidden power.

The thin line suddenly became slippery and vicious, trying to twist free while cutting deeper into Luo Chen's palm.

Azure flame rose from Luo Chen's hand.

It burned along the line.

The flame was not especially large.

It was only a thin layer of azure light clinging to his palm.

Yet the moment it touched the Heaven-Remnant Line, Angler felt as if the spiritual imprint he had refined for many years was being scorched.

Angler's expression twisted with pain. He hurriedly drew back the rod, using the mouthful of blood to extinguish the flame and sever the burned portion.

Only half of the Heaven-Remnant Line returned to him.

The other half had already turned to ash.

Before Angler could feel heartache, Luo Chen disappeared from where he stood.

The next moment, he arrived before Angler.

There was no elaborate spell.

No sword light.

Only a palm pressing down.

At their level, the simpler the attack, the more terrifying it could be.

That palm did not carry a brilliant aura, but it compressed Luo Chen's qi, blood, and body strength into one direct blow.

Angler raised the black-gold fishing rod across his chest. The rod bent violently, and the bones in both his arms cracked at once.

He flew backward, blood spraying from his mouth.

A crescent moon suddenly appeared on his chest.

Moonlight wrapped around him and dissolved most of the remaining force, allowing him to land without having his body shattered.

Luo Chen's eyes moved slightly. "Moon Wanderer's protection?"

Angler coughed blood and forced himself upright. "Moon Wanderer personally wants the Octagonal Pavilion."

Purple Dowager spoke quickly from the side. "She has entered Demon-Fall Land as well. She already went to Bright Radiance Heaven. Before leaving, she ordered us to seize this treasure."

Luo Chen paused.

Moon Wanderer.

He and that woman had never been close, but she was one of Myriad Immortals Assembly's true pillars. She had also helped him indirectly more than once after he joined the Assembly.

Taking this treasure meant offending her.

And not in a small way.

The Penglai Octagonal Pavilion was not a disposable treasure.

If Moon Wanderer had personally ordered Angler and Purple Dowager to seize it, then she must have made plans around it.

Luo Chen taking it meant cutting into those plans with his own hands.

Angler saw his hesitation and immediately said, "Fellow Daoist Luo, even if you take the Octagonal Pavilion, you will not be able to use it. This is a Penglai inherited true artifact. Without Penglai's secret method or a Sage's means, you cannot shrink it, refine it, or store it. Carrying it around will only expose you."

Luo Chen looked at him. "You have a Sage method?"

Angler's expression froze.

He had spoken too quickly.

Since Moon Wanderer had ordered them to seize the pavilion, she had naturally given them a way to collect it afterward.

Luo Chen smiled.

Angler's heart sank further.

After a long silence, Luo Chen waved his hand. "Leave."

He did not return the burned half of the Heaven-Remnant Line.

He also did not move Ghost Spirit Child's corpse aside.

This was mercy, but it was also a warning.

Purple Dowager was stunned. "Fellow Daoist Luo?"

"Because we are acquainted, I will not kill you."

His tone did not leave room for negotiation.

Angler stared at him for several breaths, then cupped his hands with difficulty. "Then we will remember Fellow Daoist Luo's mercy."

He grabbed Purple Dowager and retreated quickly.

Only after the two disappeared beyond the mountains did Moya Cave Master and Tai Sui descend from the clouds.

Moya Cave Master looked at the direction they had fled and said regretfully, "You should have cut the roots. Leaving them alive will bring trouble."

Luo Chen glanced at him. "That is not your place to say."

Moya Cave Master immediately shut his mouth.

He had forgotten for a moment that the people who had fled were Luo Chen's acquaintances from Myriad Immortals Assembly, not enemies who had offended him personally.

More importantly, he had no right to instruct Luo Chen how to handle such a matter.

Tai Sui chuckled. "Fellow Daoist Luo was not being soft. That Angler still had a Sage attack hidden on him. If pushed to the end, he might have dragged someone down with him. Fellow Daoist Luo feared Moon Wanderer, and they feared Fellow Daoist Luo. Letting them go was the best choice."

Luo Chen did not deny it.

The crescent moon protection on Angler had already shown Moon Wanderer's hand.

If he truly forced Angler to death, there might be an even more dangerous method waiting.

Tai Sui looked at the Octagonal Pavilion with interest. "But how does Fellow Daoist Luo plan to collect it? A true artifact draws heaven and earth qi on its own. A Golden Core cultivator can release it, but storing it is another matter. Unless the hidden Sage beside Fellow Daoist Luo is willing to act?"

His words sounded casual.

Luo Chen understood the probe at once.

He smiled. "No need. This is easy."

With a wave of his sleeve, Black King and Heavenjade appeared, each guarding one side.

Then Luo Chen opened his mouth and spat out a small cauldron.

The cauldron rose into the wind.

In the blink of an eye, it became a hundred zhang tall.

Primordial Unity Cauldron.

A deep red light fell from the cauldron mouth and covered the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion.

All-Encompassing Fire Prison unfolded, isolating the flow of heaven and earth qi around the pavilion.

The pavilion immediately resisted.

Five-colored light surged first, corresponding to metal, wood, water, fire, and earth.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron rumbled.

Five chains of light emerged from within the cauldron, each carrying a different elemental aura. They wrapped around the pavilion's eaves and pressed down fiercely.

Moya Cave Master's eyes shone. "A good treasure!"

Tai Sui's face, however, remained skeptical. "The Octagonal Pavilion does not have only five attributes."

As if responding to his words, three more lights burst from the pavilion.

White frost.

Azure wind.

Silver thunder.

The three forces gathered with the five elements, forming eight streams of qi that tried to break the cauldron's suppression.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron trembled.

For a moment, it seemed that the true artifact would break free.

Han Zhan's voice sounded in Luo Chen's sea of consciousness. "Do not forget what materials were used to refine your cauldron."

Luo Chen's eyes brightened.

Thousand-Year Thunder Essence.

Ice Soul Cold Marrow [TERM?].

Qingping Nether Wind [TERM?].

These were not decorations.

With a thought, three more auras erupted from the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Thunder, ice, and wind joined the five elements.

Eight against eight.

The Penglai Octagonal Pavilion was indeed a true artifact, higher in rank than the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

But it had no master controlling it now.

It had also been isolated from heaven and earth qi by the All-Encompassing Fire Prison.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron, on the other hand, was personally driven by Luo Chen's magic power and spiritual sense.

The balance tilted.

The Octagonal Pavilion slowly sank beneath the cauldron light.

Tai Sui watched this scene, his face turning iron blue.

Once again, he had failed to draw out the hidden third person.

And once again, Luo Chen had solved the problem by relying on his own strange accumulation.

*(End of Chapter)*
