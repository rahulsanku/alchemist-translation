# Chapter 927: Thunder and Fire, Twin Dragons Meet, Azure Firmament Shared Journey, Reunion After Long Separation

"Sect Elder, go ahead and take the final step of shattering your core to form your Nascent Soul. Leave the rest to me!"

The man with his back to Canglong spoke thus, his calm words filled with unquestionable confidence.

Canglong stared at that back with a complex expression, her autumn-water eyes holding both shock and a measure of reverence.

Under the third round of heavenly thunder, against which she had been utterly powerless to resist, the other party had shattered it with nothing but a pair of bare fists.

The ability revealed in this had far exceeded her imagination.

The young man who had once needed her guidance and care had, without her noticing, grown into a towering tree capable of sheltering her.

She did not doubt Luo Chen's words. She merely said with concern, "Be careful," then drifted down from the air.

The Moonlight Ice Castle stood within the cave dwelling, and Canglong was inside it.

In her qi sea, the golden core emitted cracking sounds as strands of fissures appeared upon its surface.

A vast amount of dense spiritual qi from the outside world rushed into her body, and gradually, a tender pink infant stretched its arms out from the golden core.

The three treasures united; the golden core shattered to form the Nascent Soul.

Once this step was crossed, another Sage would be added to this world.

Outside.

The moment Luo Chen appeared, exclamations rang out in all directions.

It wasn't that they recognized Luo Chen's identity—after all, he had altered his appearance somewhat.

Rather, it was that Luo Chen's actions were simply too subversive of common sense.

When cultivators undergo tribulation, they most fear others "interfering."

The word "interfering" here does not merely refer to attacking.

Helping someone undergo tribulation is also interference, and it has an even greater impact than hostile attacks.

The former distracts the tribulation-crosser's attention and methods.

The latter causes the heavenly tribulation to believe that the tribulation-crosser lacks sufficient strength and is trying to slip through, thereby increasing the intensity of the thunder punishment.

Back when Luo Chen had undergone his tribulation, his foundation had been so deep as to be terrifying, drawing a rarely seen heavenly tribulation. Black King had once wanted to help but was stopped by Han Zhan—this was the reason.

Yet now, Luo Chen's actions clearly showed that he had participated from beginning to end.

And yet, he had succeeded!

Each of those nine bolts of thunder possessed the power of a peak strike from an early-stage Sage.

Yet under his fist, they shattered one after another.

This meant he possessed strength far exceeding that of an early-stage Sage.

Was it merely mid-stage Sage?

Staring at the back of the man standing in the void, contemptuously facing the oppressive black tribulation clouds, countless people swallowed dryly, involuntarily gulping to calm their excitement.

Such ability was not something less than a Grand Cultivator could possess!

Could this old monster be one of the famous late-stage Grand Cultivators of the Eastern Wilderness?

Everyone analyzed his methods, his bearing and aura, trying to match him with famous Grand Cultivators.

But no matter how they analyzed, they couldn't make a match.

At the peak of the Eastern Wilderness, there had been many Grand Cultivators. The Nether Origin Sect alone had at least six or seven on the surface, and the other Sage-level sects had five or six combined.

But after two hundred years of the human-demon war and the destruction of the Nether Origin Sect,

now only a few old monsters who had been famous for many years remained.

The Great Elder of Dongyang Sect, Tian Duzi of Tiandu Sect, and Divine Fire Sage of the Five Elements Divine Sect.

Yet none of them matched this one.

"Then who is he?"

Amid everyone's guesses, the man moved.

Five rays of light flew up from his body and shot into the thunder clouds.

Accompanying them was a large amount of golden liquid.

The man's fingers flew up and down, continuously forming hand seals, his posture elegant and skilled, his bearing unhurried.

Someone said hesitantly, "Is that... refining artifacts?"

As soon as these words were spoken, everyone was in an uproar!

Ordinary people would avoid heavenly tribulation thunder punishment if they could.

Not only did this old monster help someone undergo tribulation, but he was also trying to use the heavenly tribulation to refine treasures?

With such heaven-defying actions, was he not afraid of heaven and earth's wrath?

As everyone thought, Luo Chen's actions thoroughly angered the heavenly tribulation.

The already extremely thick thunder clouds began to churn wildly.

Silver-white electric arcs, dense as spiderwebs, burst forth, illuminating the dark world in a sheet of white.

Its anger was accumulating, its power increasing!

The electric light reflected off Luo Chen's somewhat pale face. Even he couldn't help but become solemn now.

"Helping Canglong undergo tribulation was already an act of defiance."

"And now I'm borrowing the power of this thunder tribulation to refine five sword pellets, trying to elevate three of them to the true artifact level, thereby completing the Great Five Elements Sword Formation."

"As it stands now, I'm afraid it won't end well."

Luo Chen pursed his lips. Although his expression was solemn, there was no fear in it.

The greater the boldness, the greater the harvest.

Without taking some risks, how could he reap three true artifact sword pellets in one go?

He had even invested the Nine Yang Geng Metal he had treasured for many years. He would not allow failure this time!

At this moment, the five sword pellets were like fish, constantly shuttling through the thunder clouds.

The Nine Yang Geng Metal merged into them, making them incomparably sharp.

As the sword pellets extended, they sometimes turned into silk, sometimes suspended as steel wire, incredibly agile.

The heavenly tribulation ignored these inanimate objects, focusing its attention solely on Luo Chen.

After continuously building up power, the thunder clouds finally showed something unusual.

A claw slowly extended from the thunder clouds.

It had five toes, both ferocious and elegant.

The moment he saw the claw, Luo Chen's heart suddenly tightened.

"Transformed thunder beast!"

Before he could think further, with a roar, the thunder clouds trembled in unison.

A golden light shot out from the pitch-black thunder clouds, rushing toward Luo Chen at an unimaginable speed.

It was a dragon!

Without wings, but whether it was the rabbit eyes, deer antlers, or eagle claws and fish scales, any cultivator with a modicum of common knowledge would know it was a dragon from myth and legend!

Moreover, it was a five-clawed golden dragon.

A five-clawed thunder dragon condensed from pure golden thunder!

Facing this threat, Luo Chen was not afraid. The spell he had been building up for a long time was suddenly released.

"Roar!"

An identical roar came from his mouth.

A crimson red dragon roared out from his body, charging straight at the golden thunder dragon.

The initial momentum of the crimson flame dragon was inferior to that of the golden thunder dragon, but as it flew out, a vast amount of origin qi from heaven and earth poured into it, causing its body to grow massive.

Especially in this Upper Nether Ruined City, due to Rosycloud's furious strike, a large amount of fire-attributed spiritual qi remained.

Under the flame dragon's guidance, it all converged.

With the advantage of the terrain, the flame dragon's power grew rapidly, in the blink of an eye becoming no less than the golden thunder dragon, and even showing signs of surpassing it.

All of this, described in detail,

happened extremely quickly.

In the space of a single thought, a dazzling halo exploded in the void.

Heavenly thunder met earthly fire. Destructive power spread in all directions at an unimaginable speed.

Especially the hundred li of land beneath Luo Chen's feet, which suffered a devastating blow.

Boom!

The already fragile ruined city, under the violent explosion, was ravaged once again.

Ge Wangji's cave dwelling was instantly reduced to powder.

Only a Moonlight Ice Castle stood within, emitting a hazy white light.

Luo Chen glanced at that ice castle, his heart relaxing.

Then he looked up again at the thick thunder clouds in the sky.

He reached out his hand!

Swish! Swish! Swish! Swish! Swish!

Five rays of light, like fledgling birds returning to their nest, were swallowed into his mouth in one gulp.

Then, Luo Chen coldly stared at the thunder clouds again.

This heavenly tribulation, as he had thought, was ultimately judged based on the tribulation-crosser's strength.

Moreover, Luo Chen's true cultivation level, after digesting the comprehension of fire origin, had only just advanced to the sixth layer of the Sage stage.

Combined, the intensity of the thunder tribulation just now was at a level that only Grand Cultivator-level experts could handle.

In theory, it should have been enough to obliterate both Canglong and Luo Chen.

But even at the sixth layer of the Sage stage, Luo Chen's explosive strength was in no way inferior to an ordinary Grand Cultivator.

Therefore, that golden transformed thunder tribulation had not harmed him in the slightest.

"Is there more?"

Murmuring to himself, Luo Chen focused intently, his divine sense constantly capturing the changes within the thunder clouds.

Faced with yet another failure, the heavenly tribulation seemed even angrier.

Within the thunder clouds, changes came one after another.

Apart from the golden electric light, purple and black electric light flickered intermittently.

But in the end!

Whoosh...

As a wind blew past, the massive thunder clouds slowly dispersed.

The sky was clear, as if nothing had happened.

Those low-level cultivators who had fled far away felt as if it had all been a dream.

Some bold ones even rushed toward the cave dwelling where Old Monster Ge had been.

"I have a decent relationship with Old Monster Ge; maybe I can curry some favor."

"Such a powerful figure, yet he chose to undergo tribulation in unclaimed land—he must be a loner. If I can become his disciple, then when I attack the Sage stage in the future, I'll have someone to protect me."

...

Luo Chen cared nothing for what they were thinking.

At this moment, Luo Chen, along with Canglong and Ge Wangji, were sitting in the Frost Eagle Shuttle, roaming above the azure firmament.

"Two seniors, feel free to chat. Call me if you need anything."

Ge Wangji bowed and withdrew.

Canglong glanced at that person and clicked her tongue in admiration. "He's a good seedling!"

"Anyone who survived the human-demon war has some exceptional qualities. Even if it's just luck, it shows they have fortune on their side." Luo Chen smiled.

Then, his expression turned solemn.

"Forgive me, Sect Elder. You've just broken through and haven't had time to stabilize your realm, yet I have to drag you on a journey."

"It's fine. There's no rush."

Canglong smoothed the strands of hair on her cheek, revealing a gentle smile.

Her body emitted intense mana fluctuations—a sign that her realm had not yet stabilized, the Purple Mansion barrier had not fully formed, and her Nascent Soul was still faintly resonating with heaven and earth.

It was like a person breathing, producing a faint airflow.

When the Nascent Soul resonated with the outside world, it created mana fluctuations.

Whenever one had just broken through a realm, the most important thing was to stabilize the realm and prevent such violent mana fluctuations.

Canglong knew that the Upper Nether Ruined City was not a good place to stabilize her realm, but she was curious why Luo Chen wasn't returning to the Alchemy Saint Blessed Land, instead heading east.

Faced with this doubt, Luo Chen shook his hand.

A jade slip was faintly flickering with light.

"An old friend sent a message. I have to go see them."

"An old friend?"

"Yes, Talisman Elder Fu of the Divine Talisman Sect. You should have heard of him."

"So it's that old gentleman. I've not only heard of him, but I was fortunate enough to meet him once when I was young. I didn't expect you to have such a good relationship with him."

"We've interacted often, so naturally the relationship grew. However, this reunion will probably be our last meeting."

Luo Chen sighed, his hand holding the jade slip feeling somewhat heavy.

Back then, after the Luo Tian Sect's expansion war ended and before the Nine Spirits Sect and Divine Talisman Sect's expansion, Luo Chen had given Nine Spirits Primogenitor and Talisman Elder Fu each a jade slip that could directly contact him.

That represented that when they encountered danger, the two could ask him to help once.

Nine Spirits Primogenitor's had been used in the Medicine King Domain war.

As for Talisman Elder Fu's, it was because his lifespan was nearly exhausted that he had specifically contacted Luo Chen, wanting to see him one last time.

This message had been sent two years ago.

At that time, Luo Chen had only recently comprehended the true meaning of the law and was at a critical juncture for digesting origin qi and refining his mana, so it wasn't convenient to meet immediately.

Moreover, he was vaguely worried that people from the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect might use this to lure him out.

Going two years later, he didn't know if it would still be in time, but at least it could avoid some risks.

Canglong blinked, roughly guessing what was going on.

Given Luo Chen's character of valuing relationships and loyalty, a so-called "last meeting" was certainly not about severing ties.

It meant that Talisman Elder Fu was probably nearing the end of his life.

That was the true meaning of "last meeting."

She comforted him, "This is how our path of cultivation is. No matter how peerless and stunning one is, if they cannot attain immortality, they will eventually become a handful of dirt. There's no need for you to be sad."

Luo Chen smiled self-deprecatingly. "Sect Elder is right. I'm being overly sentimental."

Hearing Luo Chen call her "Sect Elder" again, Canglong couldn't help but feel helpless.

"Even the heavenly tribulation couldn't do anything to you. What realm are you at now?"

Luo Chen told her truthfully, "The sixth layer of the Sage stage. I've only recently broken through."

Although she had imagined Luo Chen's realm would be high, she hadn't expected it to be this high!

Canglong felt somewhat dazed.

"When you left the Ice Castle back then, you seemed to be only at the Initiate stage?"

"How much has happened in the years I was asleep? How much have you experienced? It feels like everything has changed when I woke up."

Luo Chen was also quite emotional. "Sect Elder..."

"Stop!" Canglong suddenly raised her hand, her finger pressing against Luo Chen's lips. "Either call me by my name, or call me Junior Sister. I absolutely cannot bear the title 'Sect Elder' anymore now."

The warmth of the man's breath came through her fingertip. Canglong suddenly realized the impropriety and subconsciously withdrew her slender jade finger.

She looked sideways out the shuttle window at the azure firmament scenery, where the stars were dazzlingly bright.

Luo Chen smiled slightly and said naturally, "Junior Sister, you came out of that dreamlike bubble with my divided soul. Don't you know what I experienced?"

Canglong shook her head. "It's too vague."

"Never mind. The road is long, and we have nothing to do. Let me tell you about my insignificant yet magnificent half-life!"

Luo Chen spoke with a hint of playfulness and a touch of emotion, recounting the past.

There were many things he couldn't tell those close to him, for fear of worrying them.

But Canglong was different.

Her past relationship with Luo Chen had been both teacher and friend.

Now, their relationship had reversed, but it was still both teacher and friend.

Telling her about some long-buried matters was fine.

It would also give her a general understanding of the current state of the cultivation world.

"It all started with the Falling Cloud Sect's expansion war!"

...

In the azure firmament, the shuttle was like a firefly, passing by faintly.

Under the starlight, the man and woman spoke in low voices, the atmosphere harmonious.

It wasn't until the moon set and the stars sank that the murmuring stopped.

In its place came a long-unheard, aged voice.

"You've finally come."

(End of chapter)
