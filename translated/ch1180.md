# Chapter 1180: Commanding the Ninefold, Seven Hundred Divine Slaughter

For the endless universe, the Guixu, whose location is unknown, has no concept of time or space.

But for the living beings who truly exist within the Guixu, another hundred years had flowed by.

Worry-Free City!

Once again, it was time for the bustling festivities.

Top martial artists from the seventy-two cities gathered here, many of them bringing along family members, attendants, or merchant caravans.

Xiangfei had witnessed this lively scene twice before. This was the third time.

The beauty-preserving effects of martial arts techniques were ultimately inferior to the nourishment of immortal qi. Compared to before, she had aged considerably. Her attire was no longer flashy and seductive, but simple and dignified.

In the courtyard of Yuelong Lane, Xiangfei sat in a chair, quietly listening to the intelligence her subordinates had gathered.

"The Ninefold Immortal Tower has opened. From the upper third ring down to the lower third ring, all the martial artists have come out."

"All of them?"

Xiangfei was somewhat shocked.

Although every hundred years, a large number of martial artists would take the opportunity to come out for rest and recuperation—gathering medicinal herbs for healing, or meeting friends and family to relax—there were always some martial artists obsessed with cultivation who continued to train inside the Ninefold Immortal Tower.

Why was there such a commotion this time?

Her subordinate nodded, equally astonished.

"I heard that everyone came out. The news came from another human immortal ancestor of the Fang clan, Fang Qingxue. Moreover, I saw City Lord Xiao Lin on the street."

"Xiao Lin!"

Xiangfei uttered this name, and she had no choice but to believe the intelligence.

That one was younger than her, but they had entered the Annihilation Nine Rings to cultivate at the same time, barely considered peers.

Everyone knew he was an out-and-out martial arts fanatic.

Unless there was a major change, no one could drive him away. He only wanted to cultivate within the Annihilation Nine Rings for his entire life.

If even he had come out, one could imagine what changes were currently taking place within the Annihilation Nine Rings.

What changes, she didn't care about.

What she cared about was that, up until now, she still hadn't seen Luo Chen.

Could it be that during the hundred years of bitter cultivation, he had fallen inside?

"Have you heard any news of Senior Luo Chen coming out?"

"There's no news of him coming out, and no one has seen him." The subordinate shook his head.

Just as Xiangfei was feeling disappointed, the subordinate suddenly brought up a piece of news.

"I heard some martial arts grandmasters from the fourth ring talking about something in a tavern. It seems Senior Luo Chen has likely broken through."

The fourth ring? A breakthrough?

Could it be the realm of a human immortal martial artist?

Xiangfei's heart trembled.

Luo Chen had only been inside for a hundred years, and he had only switched to martial arts for less than two hundred years. How could his cultivation advance so rapidly?

Even though she had witnessed too many of Luo Chen's incredible abilities, Xiangfei still found it hard to believe.

Her expression shifted constantly, and finally, she said, "Continue investigating related intelligence. As soon as you have news of Senior Luo Chen, tell me immediately."

"As you command!"

After her subordinate left, Xiangfei let out a faint sigh.

She was old, truly old.

She had broken through to the Leakless Realm around the age of three hundred. She had waited a hundred years before barely managing to challenge the Annihilation Nine Rings, then returned regretfully and waited another hundred years for Luo Chen. Now, another hundred years had passed.

Her actual lifespan had already reached six hundred years!

There wasn't much time left before the normal eight-hundred-year lifespan limit for a Leakless Realm martial artist in the Great Annihilation Stage.

If she still couldn't see Luo Chen this time, it might become an eternal regret.

So, she had taken the initiative to come to Worry-Free City, hoping to meet Luo Chen.

Even if it was just for a single year, even if it was just for a single moment.

But the changes in the world never shifted according to the will of insignificant people like her.

"Will I ever see him again in this life?"

...

Three months later.

Another message came from her subordinates. In fact, it was said that this news had already spread throughout the entire Immortal Relic City.

The Ninefold Immortal Tower had closed!

Closed ahead of schedule!

All the chaotic energy that had been dissipating outside had vanished. All the older generation martial artists who had come out, as well as the newly promoted martial artists who hadn't yet entered, were all blocked outside.

This news shocked everyone.

Could it be that the Worry-Free Realm Lord didn't want anyone to go in and cultivate anymore?

Amidst a wave of panic, Fang Qingxue stepped forward and gave a brief explanation.

"The Ninefold Immortal Tower will still open, but the time is uncertain. I ask everyone to wait patiently."

This explanation had no specific time, which left much room for speculation. But at least it was an official announcement, barely enough to calm people's hearts.

Xiangfei knew that this time, she might truly not see Luo Chen.

Everyone had come out, but he hadn't. And the Ninefold Immortal Tower had suddenly changed.

It was very likely that this change was related to Luo Chen.

She shouldn't wait any longer.

With regret, Xiangfei returned to Sanxiang City, feeling desolate.

...

Xiangfei's guess was not wrong. The change in the Ninefold Immortal Tower was indeed related to Luo Chen!

Three years later.

Inside the Ninefold Immortal Tower, in an extremely hidden location.

Countless lines crisscrossed and overlapped. Array upon array stood in close order.

If one looked carefully, they would notice that many of the array patterns were broken, preventing them from connecting completely.

This was the array hub of the Ninefold Immortal Tower!

Two figures stood at the periphery. One held a gourd, taking small sips. The other held a dim golden page book, standing with eyes closed, saying nothing.

Suddenly!

The young man in white robes abruptly opened his eyes and struck a hand seal at the dim golden page in his hand.

"Activate!"

The next moment, the dim golden page erupted with brilliant light.

As if responding to a call, the originally dead array patterns suddenly flickered with light.

Like highly intelligent snakes, they began to flow and connect. Occasionally, when they encountered a broken section, they would divert to another path.

In a short while, most of the arrays within the Ninefold Immortal Tower had been activated.

The Worry-Free Realm Lord, dressed in moon-white brocade robes, watched this scene, took another small sip of the Golden Wind and Jade Dew wine from his gourd, and a look of appreciation appeared in his eyes.

"You really can do it!"

Luo Chen replied, "With the complete layout diagram of the Ninefold Immortal Tower, plus your guidance, it still took me a full three years to barely drive the normal operation of the Ninefold Immortal Tower. In terms of array mastery, I am far inferior to you, Realm Lord!"

The Worry-Free Realm Lord laughed heartily. He didn't deny Luo Chen's flattery but accepted it gladly.

Three years ago, when he first met Luo Chen, he had noticed the modified storage ring on Luo Chen's finger.

It looked unremarkable, but he knew it required extremely strong artifact-refining skills and array mastery to modify a storage ring, which primarily used a cultivator's mana and divine sense as its driving energy, into a portable storage space powered by a martial artist's blood qi.

With this inference, it was easy to conclude that Luo Chen might have once been a "versatile genius"!

Not only did he cultivate both dharma and body, but his divine soul was also exceptionally powerful.

He was also proficient in the hundred arts of immortal cultivation, such as alchemy, artifact refinement, and array formations.

And this was perhaps just the tip of the iceberg that Luo Chen had revealed, limited by the environment of the Guixu.

He was very curious about how Luo Chen had mastered so many things at the mere age of six hundred.

But it was just curiosity.

Because any cultivator who could achieve a high level of cultivation had their own opportunities and encounters. It was like how he wouldn't explain to others where he had learned the Immortal Realm's Golden Erosion Script.

However, Luo Chen's versatility suddenly sparked an idea in him.

The Worry-Free Realm Lord had originally planned to close the Ninefold Immortal Tower after he left. But with Luo Chen's appearance, perhaps it was not impossible to consider entrusting the task of controlling the Ninefold Immortal Tower to Luo Chen.

This was a very demanding task.

It required the master of the Immortal Tower to simultaneously satisfy three conditions: a powerful divine soul, profound array mastery, and deep artifact-refining skill.

Fang Qingxue, who was staying behind, couldn't do it. Gu Xun couldn't either. Fortunately, there was Luo Chen.

"In the future, the martial artists who can still cultivate here should all be grateful to you, Luo Chen."

Luo Chen breathed a sigh of relief, and his weary mind also relaxed.

He looked at the thin but heavy-as-a-mountain golden page in his hand. The words on it, as if corroded, were flowing mysteriously.

"They should be grateful to you." Luo Chen said from the bottom of his heart, a hint of admiration in his eyes. "Such a heavy immortal treasure, yet you, Worry-Free Realm Lord, didn't take it away with you. Instead, you left it behind to maintain the cultivation environment of the Annihilation Nine Rings for everyone to train in. I am merely managing it on your behalf. How can I take the credit?"

The Worry-Free Realm Lord shook his head. "If I could truly take it away, why would I leave it behind? You don't think I'm the kind of morally pure sage who would give up a treasure, do you?"

Luo Chen was speechless.

The Worry-Free Realm Lord smiled and said bluntly, "Now that you've mastered the Golden Erosion Array Diagram, you should have sensed it too."

Luo Chen nodded slightly.

He had indeed sensed it.

In fact, this immortal artifact, the Ninefold Immortal Tower, was nearly destroyed!

Its internal arrays were severely broken, and its power was less than one-tenth of its original. Only the basic function of channeling energy was still operational.

Even if he took it away, it would just be a dead object.

On the contrary, by leaving it here, with the endless chaotic energy as a supplement, it could still be barely usable.

"Regardless, I, as well as all the martial artists outside, should thank you."

The Worry-Free Realm Lord laughed heartily and shook the wine gourd in his hand.

"No need to thank me, no need to thank me. In these three years, I've drunk up seven or eight-tenths of the Golden Wind and Jade Dew wine you brought when you ascended. I haven't lost out."

"Realm Lord, you are quite fond of wine. I still have some surplus here. Please take it all!"

"Forget it, I've had enough. Keep it for yourself. If, in the end, you still can't leave the Guixu, perhaps this wine will be your last comfort from your homeland."

Within the Guixu, although resources were abundant, they were bizarre and unsystematic.

Especially in terms of medicinal herbs and plants, it was difficult to find anything with age.

Spiritual wines, which were common in the outside world, were precious commodities here, hard to come by even for a single cup.

The Thousand Mountains Twilight Snow that Fang Wuyou had brought in back then had long been drunk dry. The Thousand Mountains Twilight Snow brewed by his descendants and juniors to please him ultimately lacked its true essence.

Even the Amber Flowing Light that Mu Bugui carried with him only had a small pot left.

If Luo Chen were ultimately trapped in the Guixu, facing the great destruction during the cycle of epochs, a mouthful of Golden Wind and Jade Dew might allow him to die in relative peace.

The Worry-Free Realm Lord sighed with emotion, picked up his wine gourd, and turned to leave.

Luo Chen stood within the array hub, not following him out. He simply bowed deeply, sending him off from afar.

Both were fellow sufferers in this fallen world!

As for their final fates, and whether they could find a way out, that could only depend on their respective fortunes.

...

When Luo Chen restarted the array hub, the Ninefold Immortal Tower opened once again.

Brilliant light fell from the top of the tower like ribbons.

Chaotic energy swept across the surroundings like a storm, then gradually calmed down.

Within the storm, the Worry-Free Realm Lord departed gracefully, with Mu Bugui following behind.

Then Fang Qingxue and Gu Xun re-entered the Ninefold Immortal Tower and ascended to the third ring.

The energy fluctuations outside gradually stabilized. Most of the older generation martial artists returned, while a small number remained outside.

If it were still the time when the Worry-Free Realm Lord ruled the seventy-two cities of the Great Annihilation Stage, these lingering martial artists would surely be punished.

But Luo Chen paid them no mind and was too lazy to manage them.

There were only three to five hundred years left. Let them be!

When the newly promoted martial artists adapted to absorbing chaotic energy into their bodies and entered the Annihilation Nine Rings one by one, Luo Chen activated the Golden Erosion Array Diagram and closed the Ninefold Immortal Tower.

Another hundred years of bitter cultivation officially began.

Third Ring, a small island.

Fragrant grass surrounded it, and fallen petals drifted in the wind.

A small river flowed from south to north.

Luo Chen descended onto the island, flying unhurriedly, his gaze sweeping over the island's scenery, a look of emotion on his face.

This island was once Mu Bugui's cultivation stone!

Unlike Fang Qingxue, who used martial arts divine intent to influence the external environment, the flowers, grass, and trees on the island were actually planted by Mu Bugui's own hands. They were real!

The scenery was unfamiliar, but the aura felt somewhat familiar to Luo Chen, reminiscent of the immortal scenes he had seen while cultivating in the Mountains and Seas Realm.

From this, one could see how much Mu Bugui missed his homeland!

Finally, Luo Chen's footsteps stopped before a thatched cottage.

The roof was covered with grass, and the walls were a simple mixture of bamboo and mud. It was plain and simple.

Entering the cottage.

The bookshelves were completely empty, but the desk was piled high with books like a small mountain.

Mu Bugui's original belongings had long been taken away.

These books were copies brought from the clan by Fang Qingxue on this trip home, for Luo Chen to use in deducing the cultivation technique for the fifth realm of body refinement.

This matter was Luo Chen's own request. After the Worry-Free Realm Lord agreed, he had Fang Qingxue do it.

Luo Chen remembered this favor.

If one day he found a way out, he would definitely bring the Worry-Free Realm Lord, or Fang Qingxue, along.

His fingers brushed over the books, which still exuded the fragrance of ink and paper, and Luo Chen's mind was at peace.

Although he had taken over the Ninefold Immortal Tower on behalf of the Worry-Free Realm Lord, he did not go to the second ring to cultivate as the Realm Lord had done.

Nor did he give in to curiosity and go to the first ring to see that Gate of Annihilation.

The realm not being high enough was not the main issue. The pressure of the cultivation environment on him was not that critical because of the Primordial Unity Cauldron. He was simply in a period where he needed a relatively quiet environment.

The third ring was enough!

When he had cultivated to a certain level, he would naturally ascend to the second ring, and even face the Gate of Annihilation in the first ring!

And for now...

Luo Chen's hand left those books, and he didn't go to the only thing Mu Bugui had left behind, the cold jade bed.

He left the cottage and came to a cliff near the edge of the cultivation stone.

Outside was the surging chaotic energy; inside was the peaceful environment resembling Mu Bugui's homeland.

Luo Chen sat down cross-legged, his mind highly focused.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron within his body was no longer turning.

The blood qi furnace, due to his breakthrough in realm, had become more solidified, almost like a physical entity.

And streams of Blood-Refined Divine Astral flowed through his limbs and bones, exuding a lively aura.

But none of that mattered now.

Within his heavenly court acupoint, a martial god was sitting quietly in meditation.

After sitting quietly for a long time, Luo Chen slowly threw a punch outward.

Within the Celestial Court acupoint, the martial god, condensed from Blood-Refined Divine Astral, also threw a punch.

When the two were superimposed, the power doubled!

Boom!

A terrifying fist shadow crashed into the sea of chaotic energy, stirring up ripples.

At the same time, a term on the attribute panel flickered violently, as if about to take shape.

Luo Chen opened his eyes and silently gazed at his own fist.

This technique was the martial divine ability he had created himself, after two hundred years of switching to martial arts, racking his brains, exchanging martial arts concepts with others, and challenging many martial arts grandmasters.

Although it was still just a prototype now, it had indeed been created.

This was just one martial god in a single acupoint cooperating with him to throw a punch.

If, as his realm improved, he could condense martial gods in all seven hundred and twenty acupoints, then when he threw a punch, it would be like over seven hundred Luo Chens simultaneously throwing a full-power punch.

Such a killing move would be unstoppable against martial artists of the same realm!

Even martial artists one or two major realms higher than Luo Chen might not be able to withstand it.

Luo Chen was very much looking forward to the power of this technique once it reached perfection.

Looking at the summoned attribute panel light screen, at the rapidly flickering phantom of the term, Luo Chen suddenly said:

"Let it be called 'Seven Hundred Divine Slaughter'!"

As his murmured words fell, the light screen before him immediately stopped trembling.

And on it, a new term had appeared.

[Martial Divine Ability: Seven Hundred Divine Slaughter]
