# Chapter 925: Carrying the Coffin, Suppressing Ghosts and Gods

"Why have you returned so early?"

In the quiet cave dwelling, the elderly woman asked.

Canglong stood there gracefully, answering respectfully, "After some travel, my goal is set. Continuing to wander outside would only be a waste of time."

"Oh?"

The previous Icecastle Sect Master raised an eyebrow and asked about the journey.

Canglong recounted in detail, from leaving Jade Cauldron, traveling through the six domains, to setting foot across the entire Eastern Wilderness.

"That unclaimed great domain, half a year after I left, had already been renamed the Extreme Zen Domain. The eminent monk Huiping, who came from the Western Desert, cleared away the filth, established the Extreme Zen Sect, built a Demon-Suppressing Pagoda, and suppressed countless demons and monsters from all directions."

"As for the Bei Hai Ning Yuzhuo, who was extremely murderous and came from a great background, he was also suppressed within the Extreme Zen Sect."

"It was only then that I understood that the so-called Adept stage is merely the starting point of the Great Dao."

The old sect master nodded, deeply feeling the same. "I was afraid that you would become arrogant and conceited after reaching the Adept stage at such a young age. Now, after this tempering, you have indeed gained something. Yes, the Adept stage may allow you to dominate a small territory, but it is utterly impossible to command wind and rain in the cultivation world. Under the slightest pressure from a Sage, you are as fragile as a grain of sand."

After her lament, she asked again, "What do you think of Han Zhan?"

Canglong spoke frankly, "Fellow Daoist Han is indeed a rare cultivation prodigy in this world. Not only that, but he is also persistent in pursuing the Great Dao. During our journey together, I never saw him slacken in his daily cultivation. Even in places lacking spiritual qi, he would often manipulate puppets to hone his combat skills."

"Is that all?" The old sect master was satisfied with her evaluation, but could hear that there was more unsaid.

Canglong hesitated for a moment, then finally spoke her truest opinion.

"This man can be a fellow Daoist, but only a fellow Daoist. It is difficult for him to become a Dao companion."

"His thoughts are too heavy, his calculations too deep. Even when traveling together, one must be wary."

"Disciple agrees to an alliance with the Falling Cloud Sect, but is unwilling to enter into a marriage alliance!"

After hearing these words, the old sect master fell silent.

She stared at her last disciple, scrutinizing her carefully.

Finally, she let out a relieved sigh.

"Let it be as you wish!"

In truth, she was also reluctant to marry off such an outstanding disciple to another sect.

Canglong was delighted. "Thank you, Master!"

"But!" The old sect master suddenly changed her tone, catching Canglong off guard, and made a request. "You must immediately succeed me and become the master of Icecastle!"

Canglong had thought this day would come, but not so soon.

She hesitated. "Master, you are in your prime, just when you should be spreading your wings. Moreover, I have devoted myself to cultivation all these years and am unfamiliar with sect affairs. Isn't this too hasty?"

The old sect master sighed and waved her hand.

"Not hasty, not hasty. It's not hasty at all."

"Since my trip to the Void Secret Realm, my injuries have been severe and difficult to heal. I am merely struggling to hold on. Do I look like I am in my prime with this head of white hair?"

"Besides, the mundane affairs of the sect can be handled by your fellow senior and junior sisters. You need not worry."

"Finally!"

She paused and smiled. "I also need a reason to deal with the Falling Cloud Sect!"

Indeed, how could the master of a major Adept sect marry out?

This was the best reason.

Even an ally could hardly criticize.

After hearing this, Canglong no longer refused and immediately knelt on the ground.

"Disciple obeys!"

...

A year later, in the presence of thousands of Icecastle disciples, Canglong received the position of sect master from the old sect master.

Some Icecastle disciples were puzzled, but few questioned it.

Whether it was the old sect master's prestige, Canglong's outstanding performance during the beast tide, or her exceptional cultivation talent, all proved that she was the most suitable ruler for the now-precarious Icecastle.

After taking over, although Canglong still insisted on cultivation, she inevitably became busy due to the shortage of Icecastle personnel.

First, she submitted a request to the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect, stating that Icecastle would no longer participate in the Void Secret Realm.

This represented Icecastle completely ceding this portion of benefits.

Then, she dispersed a large number of low-level Icecastle disciples to various immortal cities in the Jade Cauldron Domain, both to handle mundane affairs and to gain experience.

During this process, she also gave the Icecastle disciples an additional task.

To pay attention to mortals with spiritual roots and recruit them into Icecastle.

When encountering wandering cultivators with clean backgrounds and certain skills, they could also be invited as guest elders of Icecastle.

On the Falling Cloud Sect side, after negotiations, a certain cooperative relationship was also established.

Including but not limited to regular exchange competitions between disciples, resource exchange, mutual advancement and retreat, and so on.

This was for external affairs. Internally, she distributed the resources vacated by several Adept cultivators who had been injured in the Void Secret Realm to talented low-level disciples to aid their cultivation.

Not only that, but through meticulous sorting, she also uncovered the backgrounds of some personnel within the sect and carried out a purge.

The biggest of these matters involved Icecastle's chief alchemist!

"Junior Sister Xiaorou, do you need my help with your Dao companion matter?"

Under Canglong's vigorous governance, in just a short one or two hundred years, Icecastle showed signs of revival.

The number of disciples exceeded ten thousand, and outstanding disciples emerged in succession.

Even Adept cultivators, after the passing of the old sect master and other elders, were gradually replaced by several.

Gu Xiaorou was one of them.

At this moment, Gu Xiaorou, at the early Adept stage, heard Canglong's words and couldn't help biting her lip.

After a long while, she shook her head with difficulty.

"Since it is a family matter, let me resolve it myself!"

Canglong looked at her seriously and finally nodded slightly. "Then do it yourself!"

Soon after.

On the outskirts of Icecastle, known as the Death Ice Sea, a chase, a battle between husband and wife turned enemies, began.

Under the bombardment of numerous talismans, Shang Hu, who was skilled in alchemy but weak in combat, eventually lost.

He looked at Gu Xiaorun, who was approaching with a sword, his eyes full of disbelief.

"Why?"

Gu Xiaorun's face was cold. "Humans and demons walk different paths. That is all."

Shang Hu was unwilling. "But I have never done anything harmful to the human race, nor have I harmed a single Icecastle disciple."

Gu Xiaorun pursed her lips and retorted, "Then why were you secretly contacting the demon beast clans in the Great Snowy Mountains? And after you joined Icecastle, beast tides have been frequent. How do you explain that?"

Shang Hu struggled to say, "If I say those beast tides have nothing to do with me, will you believe me?"

"I believe you!" Gu Xiaorun nodded, but before Shang Hu could breathe a sigh of relief, she raised her sword. "But the sect will not."

Shang Hu opened his mouth, and finally his expression turned bitter.

"Xiaorun, must you be so heartless?"

"Forgive me!"

Gu Xiaorun thrust her sword forward. Blood blossomed on the ice lake, and a huge white fox lay prostrate on the ground, lifeless.

After doing this, Gu Xiaorun staggered a few steps, as if drained of strength.

Finally, she supported herself with the sword tip, leaning on the ground.

In the wind and snow, Canglong walked slowly.

She glanced at the white fox corpse and looked at Gu Xiaorun with a smile.

"Xiaorun..."

But Gu Xiaorun didn't even look at her. She picked up her sword and brushed past her.

Only a cold, frosty sentence came through the wind.

"Don't call me that name anymore."

Canglong's lips moved slightly, but in the end, she could only smile bitterly.

For the sake of the sect, she had to be the good person and the bad person. All the good and bad fruits were hers to swallow.

But having a junior sister who was once as close as a sibling become so distant—this feeling was indeed hard to bear!

She personally disposed of Shang Hu's corpse, took his belongings back, and entrusted someone to deliver them to Gu Xiaorun.

Among them was a damaged alchemy vessel, the Shangshu Cauldron.

From then on, the sect became a solid block, both internally and externally.

Icecastle grew increasingly prosperous, with strong cultivators emerging endlessly. Within the entire Jade Cauldron Domain, it became the third-largest sect, second only to the Sword Sect and the Falling Cloud Sect.

Even the Qingdan Valley, which had a considerable number of Adept cultivators, was far inferior to Icecastle in momentum.

But as Icecastle grew stronger, internal conflicts began to erupt.

There were hundreds or even thousands of Initiate disciples, and the number of Adept cultivators approached double digits, yet their territory had not expanded, and resources were becoming increasingly insufficient.

The disciples who had once loved and respected her, now under the command of various Adept elders, had split into several factions, constantly fighting openly and covertly.

Canglong gradually grew weary of all this.

Especially when news came that Han Zhan had persisted in his travels and eventually reached the Sage stage, she suddenly woke up.

"I understood earlier than him that without becoming a Sage, one is ultimately an ant. But how did I become so immersed in sect affairs?"

She knew it was because of her master, but also because of her own chaotic thoughts.

If this continued, the once-stunning Fairy Canglong would merely become one of the most ordinary Adept cultivators among the masses.

At this point, Canglong completely set aside mundane affairs, allowing those below to fight for power and profit, and devoted herself to cultivation.

As long as she could reach the Sage stage, any injustice within the sect could be suppressed by her alone!

From then on, the Heavenly Mountain was sealed, and no one saw the snow lotus bloom again.

And Canglong's cultivation progressed faster and faster, from mid-Adept to late Adept, breaking through smoothly.

The fusion of essence, qi, and spirit accelerated.

Especially her vast soul, which unknowingly had surpassed the limits of the Adept stage and touched the edge of the Sage stage.

In this process, Canglong gradually became aware of something.

Had those previous events been experienced many times before?

Walking the same path again, the state of mind was no longer the same.

Sinking into it time and again, struggling with all her might time and again—when would it end?

How could this worldly entanglement be severed?

In a daze, she saw a red dog walking alone on the white ice.

"It's the big dog!"

The red dog, which had disappeared for many years, appeared again, staring at her coldly across the ice lake.

Then, the red dog turned and left.

This time, Canglong did not let it escape. She followed without hesitation.

...

"It's time!"

Three years after Luo Chen had comprehended the origin of fire, he rose to his feet on this day.

Turning into a streak of light, he rushed toward the Heavenly Mountain.

Amidst the astonishment of the Heavenly Mountain disciples and the Heartless Fairy, an ice coffin, wrapped in red light, slowly rose into the sky.

"Supreme Elder?" the Heartless Fairy called out.

Luo Chen smiled slightly, strapped the ice coffin to his back, looked up at the sky, then down at the crowd below.

"Soon, you will see your senior sister return safely."

Then, without hesitation, he carried the ice coffin and left the Dan Saint Blessed Land.

On the way, a black light burst from the ground and merged into his shoulder.

Li Yingzhang stood before a towering, majestic palace, watching Luo Chen's departing back, his expression complex.

This departure of the Supreme Elder—who knew when he would return?

There was nothing he could do to help. The only thing he could do was to take good care of the Luo Tian Sect for him.

"Rest assured, Yingzhang will not let you down."

"When you return, the Luo Tian Sect will be a qualified Sage-level sect!"

He bowed deeply from afar toward that retreating figure.

...

Upon leaving the Dan Saint Blessed Land, Luo Chen immediately sensed something unusual.

An invisible pressure descended from above.

Its target was not him, but the ice coffin on his back.

"Just as I thought!"

"The Dan Saint Blessed Land isolates the outside world, possessing the miraculous effect of avoiding heavenly tribulation and earthly calamities."

"If Canglong is to awaken and survive the Sage tribulation, she must go to the outside world!"

Luo Chen took a deep breath, forcibly concealing the other wave of magical power constantly emanating from the ice coffin with his great magical power, and swam toward the outside.

The Mystic Turtle had been waiting for a long time and picked him up.

Halfway there, Bai Meiling, who had been cultivating outside all these years, also caught up.

She looked curiously at the ice coffin on Luo Chen's back. "Is she the one undergoing the tribulation?"

Luo Chen nodded. "Did you find a suitable place for the tribulation? Is it arranged?"

Bai Meiling answered without thinking. "There's a ready-made place right now. It's the Upper Wasteland Ruined City. The fourth-order spirit vein there was repaired by a group of Adept cultivators. It should be usable for the tribulation."

The Upper Wasteland Ruined City?

Luo Chen's eyes flickered. That was indeed a good place.

Although there were many treasure hunters, most were of low cultivation.

Even if there were a few Adept cultivators, they wouldn't hinder Canglong's tribulation.

When passing by Yanfu Mountain, Luo Chen inquired about its current situation.

Bai Meiling also reported in detail, speaking very carefully.

With the help of Luo Tian Sect's experts, she had established the White Spirit Dao Field in the Nether Abyss as her cultivation site.

Over the years, many low-level ghosts had attached themselves to her.

With these low-level ghosts, Bai Meiling could know the general situation inside Yanfu Mountain even without exploring it personally.

Currently, in Yanfu Mountain, there were at least a dozen fourth-order ghosts lingering, trying their best to break the formations inside.

But with little success!

Not to mention entering the cultivation palaces of the three ancestors, even some of the remaining cave dwellings of Sage-level experts halfway up the mountain had not been opened.

It wasn't that their skills were insufficient, but that the formations on Yanfu Mountain were too powerful.

Even though Rosycloud had destroyed the most important part, the rest was not something these ghosts, who had been dormant in the Nether Abyss for thousands of years, could quickly break through.

"In my opinion, without a hundred or eighty years of effort, no one can reach the Yanfu Heavenly Palace!"

The Yanfu Heavenly Palace consisted of the Heavenly Nether Palace, the Heavenly Abyss Palace, and the Heavenly Marsh Palace, corresponding to the last three Ascendant ancestors of the Nether Origin Sect.

And within them, there must be the most important inheritance of the Nether Origin Sect!

At the very least, Luo Chen knew that the Nether Water Spring Eye was definitely in the main hall of the Heavenly Nether Palace.

"Very good. Continue to monitor that place for me. Once someone reaches the Heavenly Palace, crush the jade pendant and notify me." Luo Chen instructed.

Bai Meiling hummed in acknowledgment. This was all she could do for Luo Chen.

"By the way," Luo Chen said, as if suddenly remembering something, or rather, a question that had been weighing on his mind for a long time. "Do you know where those ghosts and gods went?"

By ghosts and gods, he meant Ascendant-level ghosts!

As far as Luo Chen knew, the number of such ghosts in the Nether Abyss was not small.

Wouldn't they covet the things on Yanfu Mountain?

This was also the most important reason why Luo Chen dared not make a move on Yanfu Mountain for the time being.

Bai Meiling hesitated. "There are still a few ghosts and gods alive, but I heard they dare not appear. The Nirvana Sacred Flame left above the Nether Abyss was specifically left for them. If anyone dares to show their head, they will suffer a devastating blow."

Luo Chen frowned, but then understood the reasoning.

When Rosycloud was besieged by many ghosts and gods, if she wanted to escape, she would have to set up some countermeasures. Otherwise, it wouldn't have been so easy.

As he became more familiar with the Nirvana Sacred Flame, Luo Chen also discovered the special nature of this powerful flame.

Just like the nine-tribulation, three-nirvana process of cultivation, each time one passed a nirvana, a miraculous effect would be gained.

After the nirvana of magical power, the sacred flame could burn the enemy's magical power shield, melting spiritual qi and even origin qi.

After the nirvana of the physical body, once the sacred flame touched the enemy, it could turn their flesh to ash.

After the nirvana of the soul, the sacred flame could even directly attack the enemy's soul.

The countless Nirvana Sacred Flames that Rosycloud had left above the Nether Abyss were specifically targeted at those powerful ghosts and gods.

Luo Chen had not yet passed the third nirvana, so he could not grasp the secret. He could only barely traverse it by relying on the Nirvana Sacred Flame of the same origin.

"That's good news!"

Luo Chen nodded and then no longer lingered on these trivial matters.

Everything that needed to be arranged had been arranged in the past three years. Everything that needed to be said had been said.

Now that he was going out, those long-standing matters that had been weighing on his mind should also be resolved!

Soon after.

As the Mystic Turtle surfaced, emerging from the water.

Through the thick mist, the Upper Wasteland Ruined City was right before his eyes!
