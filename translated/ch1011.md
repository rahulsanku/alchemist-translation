# Chapter 1011: Imperishable Legacy, Comprehending the Origin

A gray, three-legged cauldron stood silently before the robed Daoist.

The small cauldron was ancient, perfectly round and seamless, lacking the elaborate patterns typical of normal cauldron-type treasures.

Even the butterfly patterns once engraved upon it had become dim and faded, ever since the tribulation gu had been extracted by Lord Youli.

But at this moment, a faint glimmer of light flickered upon the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Seeing this, Luo Chen smiled faintly.

"You sense your impending demise, and that is why you are so unsettled?"

His finger touched the spot where the faint light flowed, and he could vaguely feel a hint of moisture.

The Netherwater Spring Eye was restless, uneasy, terrified!

Once, it had existed for ten thousand years, and even the successive ancestors of the Nether Origin Sect could do nothing against it.

Even after its collapse, the weak will that was reborn could still assimilate the Ghost King and contend with Luo Chen.

But now, facing Luo Chen's gaze, its ignorant will also sensed the danger.

*Thump! Thump! Thump!*

A vibrating sound rang out—the Netherwater Spring Eye struggling.

But it was futile!

This time, Luo Chen did not even release his fifth-rank divine flame. He merely closed his eyes and silently recited a Buddhist forbidden mantra.

After several days of rest, his condition had stabilized.

Having cultivated this Buddhist forbidden mantra to the master-level proficiency, he could recite it fluently, knowing it by heart.

When he reached the final line, Luo Chen pressed his palms together with one hand and softly intoned:

"Svāhā!"

Instantly, his vast soul merged with his Nascent Soul, coalescing into a mighty primordial spirit power!

Luo Chen did not rush to release his divine sense to grind away the spring eye's will. Instead, he took this opportunity to first thoroughly experience the state of his primordial spirit.

Powerful, vast—as if he could control everything.

Clear, penetrating—his thoughts flashed with extreme speed, as if in an instant he could perceive all things in heaven and earth.

And because his divine sense was tainted with the true meaning of laws, he could truly, with a single thought, draw upon the omnipresent origin qi of heaven and earth!

At this moment, Luo Chen finally understood the difference between an Ascendant grand cultivator and a Sage.

The primordial spirit was no longer merely a Golden Core or Nascent Soul that could contain spiritual power and mana; it had transformed into a real existence.

The method of using divine sense to draw upon the origin qi of heaven and earth, when slightly employed, was a killing technique far surpassing any Sage-level means.

If a cultivator had also comprehended the true meaning of laws, the combination of the two would make this killing technique even more formidable.

This was what was meant by great power, great divine ability!

However, Luo Chen's situation was also very different from that of normal great cultivators.

Someone like Xuan Du could also be called a prodigy of his generation. In the late Sage stage, he had condensed his primordial spirit, could control the origin qi of heaven and earth, and even ascended to the Ascendant stage in one step.

But the amount of origin qi Xuan Du could control back then was not very great.

The reason was that he had not comprehended the true meaning of laws.

But Luo Chen was completely different. He had comprehended them—and two at that!

The Withering-Bloom of Wood, the Origin of Fire!

With the stacking of these two, the amount of origin qi Luo Chen could leverage was not simply two or three times greater, but at least four times or more!

"I wonder how I compare to an Ascendant grand cultivator?"

Luo Chen lacked a clear reference object.

Even though he had used the Arhat Grand Formation of the Hanging Temple to measure himself, even though he had personally witnessed Ascendant grand cultivators in action.

But the Ascendant stage—the difference between the early, middle, and late phases was simply too great.

The weak ones, like Qinghe Immortal, could be trapped by a group of Sages from the Nether Origin Sect.

The strong ones, like Lord Youli and Rosycloud, could—one with a single move quell a calamity of war, the other sink continents and burn seas, leaving no one in the world to oppose them.

How could one compare?

The only thing Luo Chen knew was that his potential hypothetical enemy, the only surviving Ascendant grand cultivator of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, one of the Five Ancestors of Heavenly Origin, Ancestor Liancheng, was at the early Ascendant stage.

How long ago the other party had advanced to Ascendant, Luo Chen did not know.

But with the profound foundation of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, Ancestor Liancheng would certainly not lack offensive methods, nor would he be as weak as Qinghe Immortal.

If he were to face Ancestor Liancheng now, he would be ten times dead with no chance of survival!

He was thinking too far ahead.

Luo Chen gathered his thoughts with a single intention and continued to assess his current state.

Strong as it was, the weakness was also obvious.

It was not sustainable!

His primordial spirit state, as things stood, could only last for the time it takes to drink a cup of tea, not even a full quarter of an hour.

This was an innate deficiency caused by the lack of a life soul, forcibly condensing the primordial spirit using the *Moonlight Heart-Cleansing Mantra*.

Such a short time was far from sufficient, whether for battle or for breaking through to a higher realm.

"So, even if I cannot quickly cultivate the Heart-Cleansing Mantra to the Great Perfection realm, I must constantly condense my primordial spirit, get used to this state, and extend the duration as much as possible."

"Right, I wonder if completing the third Nirvana—the Soul Nirvana—in the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture* would allow me to go a step further?"

With some thoughts, Luo Chen opened his eyes.

Closing his eyes and opening them—a complex train of thought had, in reality, taken only the blink of an eye.

Such was the wonder of divine sense!

Rapid thinking, clear perception—it could be said that anyone who could condense a primordial spirit and evolve divine sense was no fool.

Luo Chen's gaze fell upon the Primordial Unity Cauldron. He smiled faintly, and his divine sense entered the cauldron leisurely.

In an instant, he captured the location of that dark spring eye.

That strand of consciousness seemed to understand that, under the suppression of the Primordial Unity Cauldron, escape was impossible. Facing Luo Chen's divine sense, it finally stirred up a desperate will to resist.

The two collided instantly.

Then began the most extreme and perilous mutual grinding!

A boundless sea stirred up monstrous waves, a terrifying abyss devoured everything, turbid black water defiled all...

Yet, amidst these infinite visions, the colossal primordial spirit, bearing a face identical to Luo Chen's, merely pointed one finger at the earth and one at the sky, unmoving as a mountain!

With an imposing and righteous momentum, it calmed the monstrous waves, suppressed the abyss, and purified the filth!

...

"Huff..."

Luo Chen exhaled a mouthful of turbid air, slowly calming his mind.

At this moment, he had withdrawn from the primordial spirit state.

And the will of the Netherwater Spring Eye within the Primordial Unity Cauldron had been completely ground away. What remained inside was nothing but the purest spring eye.

All that was left was to imprint his own mark upon it.

This was not difficult. After a short rest, Luo Chen began this task.

He channeled his mana to drive the Primordial Unity Cauldron, starting to burn and refine the spring eye, while his vast divine sense surged into it, beginning to leave his own imprint.

Compared to grinding away the spring eye's will, refining the spring eye itself was not as difficult, but it was still quite time-consuming.

Because the spring eye itself contained a vast amount of power. Without completely controlling it, one could not use it to master the Nether Abyss.

But as the imprints grew more numerous, and his divine sense's contact with the spring eye deepened, Luo Chen gradually felt a strange sensation.

"This is..."

Luo Chen abruptly withdrew his divine sense and stopped the action of refining with his true fire.

He looked at the location of the Netherwater Spring Eye with shock and uncertainty, recalling the feeling from before.

Finally, he uttered four words.

"The Origin of Water!"

This was not unexpected. Even before subduing the Netherwater Spring Eye, Luo Chen knew that this heavenly-earthly wonder contained a true meaning of laws, and it was very likely the most fundamental Water Origin among the water-type laws.

But only upon truly making contact did Luo Chen understand just how bright, clear, and naked that Water Origin was.

It was as if, as long as he was willing, he could comprehend it and make it his own.

This was far too easy!

Suddenly, Luo Chen thought of something.

Was it possible that the successive ancestors of the Nether Origin Sect had not been unable to subdue the Netherwater Spring Eye, but had deliberately left it behind, fixed within the Heavenly Nether Palace, to give their descendants an opportunity to comprehend the true meaning of laws in advance?

This was a foundation for ten thousand generations, an imperishable legacy.

As long as the Nether Abyss still existed, the Netherwater Spring Eye would always be there, and that true meaning of laws would be inexhaustible.

"That Sage named Chongming back then also comprehended the Water Origin. Although he was granted a Heavenly Secret Pill by Chu Yan, his comprehension was far too fast, almost no slower than mine. But I spent fifty accelerated years in the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven and only comprehended the Withering-Bloom True Meaning after witnessing the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire ascend to the fifth rank. What right did he have?"

"Moreover, during the Yanfu Mountain lockdown, no other Sage could go up the mountain, yet Chongming could come and go freely. What right did he have?"

"Now it seems, it was all because of this Netherwater Spring Eye!"

After figuring this out, Luo Chen's gaze at the spring eye within the Primordial Unity Cauldron grew fervent.

If he could comprehend another law's true meaning, even if it was just the most basic origin, and even if it didn't perfectly match his own attributes, the boost to his strength would be immeasurable!

His primordial spirit's existence time was too short.

To unleash enough power to threaten an Ascendant grand cultivator within that short time, he had to leverage as much origin qi as possible.

And as things stood, for every additional law's true meaning he comprehended, the amount of origin qi he could leverage would multiply.

Thus, if he could comprehend the Water Origin as well, with his vast mana and primordial spirit, he might be able to leverage eight times, or even over ten times, the origin qi.

Could an Ascendant grand cultivator achieve this level?

Luo Chen did not know.

But he knew that this might be one of the few shortcuts he had to directly confront an Ascendant grand cultivator.

Should he give it a try?

Luo Chen licked his dry lips, his face full of eager anticipation.

Suddenly!

"Perhaps I am too impatient?"

The thought popped into Luo Chen's mind. He then silently recited the Heart-Cleansing Mantra, quickly calming himself down.

This *Moonlight Heart-Cleansing Mantra* was not only useful for condensing the primordial spirit; its own effect of clearing the mind and calming the spirit was also very good. In fact, this effect might have been the very purpose for which this Buddhist forbidden mantra was created.

After calming down, Luo Chen thought seriously.

He had not originally planned for this. Refining the spring eye was only meant to ensure that the Luo Tian Sect's foothold could not be threatened by others. Moreover, once he controlled the spring eye, it would be much more convenient for sect disciples to enter and exit the Nether Abyss in the future.

Now that the opportunity was here, the potential dangers could not be ignored.

The Water Origin did not match his own attributes!

Furthermore, he currently had no related cultivation techniques to practice, so comprehending it might not be so easy.

Most importantly, the Fire Origin he had comprehended on his own and the Water Origin were mutually restraining. If something went wrong, with water and fire clashing, could there be even greater unforeseen consequences?

Due to these many concerns, Luo Chen's eager thoughts seemed to be suppressed.

But only *seemed*!

His large, body-refined hand stroked the smooth belly of the Primordial Unity Cauldron as he considered the possibility of success.

First was his own lifebound treasure!

It could suppress the Netherwater Spring Eye, which meant the progress of comprehension was within his control.

Once he encountered a dangerous sign, he could stop at any time.

Second was the concern about difficulty, and in this regard, Luo Chen happened to have something in hand that could overcome this problem.

The Initiation Pill!

Its wondrous effects needed no elaboration. He didn't even need to painstakingly search for a new law's true meaning; there was one right in front of him.

Finally...

"When heaven offers a gift and you do not take it, you will instead suffer the consequences!"

Luo Chen sighed with emotion.

To refuse a chance that was delivered to one's doorstep—where was the courageous, advancing spirit of a Dao cultivator?

"Let me try it. If it really doesn't work, I'll stop immediately!"

Luo Chen finally convinced himself.

The Netherwater Spring Eye before him, now devoid of will, was truly harmless. How could he remain unmoved?

Of course, the necessary preparations still had to be made.

The cautious Luo Chen first rested for two days to dispel the fatigue from grinding away the spring eye's will.

Then he bathed and burned incense, adjusting his state.

He even set up several alerting formations within the Dan Chen Hall, so that if he became too engrossed, he could be quickly awakened.

He also arranged some small measures on the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

After everything was ready, Luo Chen took out the large gourd containing the Initiation Pills.

He hadn't noticed before, but upon observing now, he discovered that among this batch of Initiation Pills, five had medicinal power far fuller than the remaining ten.

"Fourth-rank, mid-grade!"

"Was it that last furnace?"

"According to the panel's calculations, the last furnace was at the proficient level, but it shouldn't have reached the mid-grade level, should it?"

"Could it be the effect of the Primordial Unity Cauldron's rank enhancement?"

After some thought, Luo Chen took out two pills.

He swallowed one and held the other under his tongue.

It was said that a person could only use one Initiation Pill in their lifetime, but that was an ancient saying.

Whether this newly refined Initiation Pill still had this taboo had not yet been tested.

Just in case, Luo Chen simply ate one and kept one in reserve.

When the Initiation Pill was refined within his body and its medicinal power spread out, Luo Chen clearly felt the difference.

His mana circulation became faster, his little Nascent Soul became lively, and faintly, the outside world seemed to resonate with his Nascent Soul.

One had to know that Luo Chen's Purple Mansion barrier was far thicker than that of his peers!

Being able to sense heaven and earth through such a thick Purple Mansion barrier showed the wonder of this pill.

Without hesitation, Luo Chen submerged his soul into his Nascent Soul, and that medicinal power followed, slowly being inhaled by the little Nascent Soul through its nose.

Instantly, a shimmering light enveloped the Nascent Soul.

"It's a change brought about by combining with spiritual opportunity. This way, during enlightenment, there is protection, and I won't be afraid of being assimilated by heaven and earth."

A flash of understanding arose spontaneously.

Luo Chen felt even more at ease. He then manipulated the Primordial Unity Cauldron, releasing its restraint on the Netherwater Spring Eye.

Instantly, a dense water vapor filled the air.

The little Nascent Soul was also attracted, emerging from the Purple Mansion and floating within the dense water vapor.

After a lively swim, it quieted down, sinking into it, as if returning to the mother's womb, utterly comfortable.

That Water Origin became even more apparent, within easy reach.

Luo Chen no longer hesitated. He rapidly absorbed this power, as if drawing nourishment from the mother's body.

The legacy of the Nether Origin Sect had now become Luo Chen's opportunity.

He wondered if Ancestor Heize and the others, if they knew in the underworld, would be jumping with rage?

Probably not, right? After all, Luo Chen was, at the very least, the master of the Dan Saint Hall and also a member of the Nether Origin Sect.

A smile curled at the corner of Luo Chen's mouth, and he fell silent.

(Chapter End)
