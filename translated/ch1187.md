# Chapter 1187: Four Great Powerhouses, Corpse Remnant Immortal Domain

Weeping Abyss.

On a cliff face, Yuan Qingzang and Green-Robed One were having a pleasant conversation.

At least, that was how it appeared on the surface.

Yuan Qingzang was warm, generous, and talkative, while Green-Robed One wore a cold expression, looking impatient yet forced to listen.

The disparity in their strength created this strange scene.

"Actually, there's no need for our two clans to fight to the death all these years. You need our Lightning Absorption Stones, and don't you also need the Green Parasol Wood from the Canopy Tower Forest?"

"Old Man Sorrowful Wind is too stubborn. You're different—you could choose to cooperate with the Canopy Tower Forest!"

"Yes, I've already left. But now Yuan Shiqi is stationed at the Canopy Tower Forest. He's considered a friend of yours, so you could sit down and talk, just like we are now."

Green-Robed One twitched the corner of his mouth, utterly speechless.

What did he mean by *friend*?

His battles with that fellow Yuan Shiqi had nearly cost each other their lives several times.

And *sit down and talk*?

If not for Luo Chen's help, Yuan Qingzang wouldn't even have had the chance to enter the Weeping Abyss.

He took a deep breath, unwilling to continue this conversation, and cast his gaze into the abyss.

"Why isn't that one coming out yet?"

A full month had passed since Luo Chen entered the Weeping Abyss.

Throughout that month, there had been no news at all.

If not for the loud weeping and wailing sounds rising from the abyss, indicating activity below, they would have thought Luo Chen had perished inside.

Yuan Qingzang rubbed his chin and muttered, "He should still be alive. Staying down there this long without coming up... could it be..."

He recalled what Luo Chen had done at the Canopy Tower Forest.

That fellow couldn't possibly take something away from every place he visited, could he?

Just as the two were conversing, the wind in the abyss suddenly howled.

Both stood up at the same time.

"He's coming out!"

Whoosh!

A streak of blood-red light burst forth from the ground, shooting upward.

In an instant, Luo Chen appeared before them, elegant and otherworldly.

"Sorry to keep you two waiting so long!" Luo Chen said with a smile.

"Luo..."

"I wonder if Fellow Daoist Luo has gained anything?"

Green-Robed One spoke first, cutting off Yuan Qingzang's words, though a hint of worry flickered in the depths of his eyes.

"Unfortunately, nothing much. The bottom of the abyss is a dead end." Luo Chen shrugged, then cupped his hands. "Fellow Daoist Green-Robed, I've troubled you this past month."

With that, he gave Yuan Qingzang a meaningful glance and prepared to leave.

Green-Robed One did not stop him, merely watching coldly as he departed.

After all, he couldn't defeat them, nor could he keep them here. He had no choice but to let them go.

Yuan Qingzang, however, seemed somewhat reluctant. "Green-Robed One, look—whether it's Brother Luo or your own Ancestor Sorrowful Wind, both say this place is a dead end. Why hold on stubbornly? Now that you've cultivated to the Human Immortal realm, why not come out and explore the world with us?"

Green-Robed One shook his head without saying a word.

Yuan Qingzang had no choice but to follow Luo Chen out of the Weeping Abyss.

Once they had left the abyss behind,

Yuan Qingzang looked at Luo Chen curiously.

"You stayed down there for a whole month. There's no way you found nothing. What exactly did you do?"

Luo Chen stretched his body, and the Blood Refined Divine Astral Force surged out, condensing into a pair of astral wings just like Yuan Qingzang's.

This technique of transforming astral force into wings was not complicated for Luo Chen.

Back in the Initiate stage, he had possessed a pair of magic artifacts called Blazing Cloud Wings and had mastered the method of transforming spiritual power into wings.

Now, it was just a matter of using a different type of energy.

Using this method to travel through the Ruins was more economical with astral force and also safer.

Earlier, Luo Chen had used this same method to travel with Yuan Qingzang to the Weeping Abyss.

At this moment, his expression was calm.

"Let's talk while we move."

The suspicion in Yuan Qingzang's heart was almost confirmed. "You didn't actually steal something from someone else's home, did you?"

"The word 'steal' sounds too harsh. I'll explain later when we have time."

Luo Chen flapped his blood-red wings, and his figure shot into the distance.

Yuan Qingzang looked back at the Weeping Abyss, showing a sympathetic expression, then activated his lightning wings and followed closely.

---

After they left,

Inside the abyss, Green-Robed One sat quietly for a long time before suddenly realizing something was wrong.

He immediately plunged straight down into the depths, releasing his Magnetic Divine Astral Force as he descended.

Under the blessing of his martial will, the changes in the vicinity were reflected one by one in his mind.

"How has the Magnetic Divine Power weakened to such an extent?"

"Could it have been absorbed by Luo Chen?"

"Impossible!"

"Not good..."

*Thump!*

At the bottom, where Yuan Qingzang could not enter, Green-Robed One, with the blessing of his Magnetic Divine Astral Force, was able to go deep.

The moment his feet touched the ground, he quickly observed his surroundings.

Sure enough!

The originally dense Magnetic Divine Power had become so thin it was hard to imagine, clearly having been absorbed by some great divine ability.

Green-Robed One's face was full of disbelief.

The Magnetic Divine Power was extraordinarily strange; ordinary martial artists could not utilize it at all.

If not for their clan inheriting the bloodline power of Ancestor Sorrowful Wind, allowing them to barely adapt, it would have been difficult for them to survive, let alone cultivate, in this environment.

That Luo Chen didn't seem to have such a bloodline. What on earth had he used to absorb such a vast amount of Magnetic Divine Power?

If he had taken away an entire vein of magnetic ore, Green-Robed One wouldn't have been so shocked. But the magnetic ore vein underground was still intact.

In other words, Luo Chen had something on him that could carry the Magnetic Divine Power.

But none of that mattered now!

What mattered was...

Green-Robed One was anxious. He continued deeper into the darkness, using strands of Magnetic Divine Astral Force as guidance, and finally opened a stone door that emitted no aura at all.

A miniature space, barely a foot in size, appeared before him.

The space was extremely small, barely enough for a curled-up infant to hide inside.

The surroundings were entirely covered with the purest and most transparent magnetic ore.

Seeing this miniature space, Green-Robed One breathed a sigh of relief.

"Thank goodness, the intact egg is still here!"

---

On a desolate, boundless desert, two streaks of light shot past at low altitude.

In the blink of an eye, they disappeared, leaving only two trails in the air.

Amidst the howling wind came Yuan Qingzang's muffled voice transmission.

"Luo Chen, we're far enough away now. You can tell me!"

"Why are you so curious!"

Luo Chen was helpless.

But considering his guilt over absorbing the Chaotic Lightning Pool earlier, he still slowed down.

With a flick of his finger, a wisp of ethereal, thread-like mist appeared before him.

Yuan Qingzang was startled and blurted out, "You stole the lifeblood of their Weeping Abyss?"

"'Stole' sounds too harsh. I just took a portion for use. And this isn't their lifeblood—it has a name. It's called Magnetic Divine Power." Luo Chen explained.

Yuan Qingzang stared at the mass of gas in Luo Chen's hand. "I don't know about Magnetic Divine Power, but I know that Old Man Sorrowful Wind relied on this stuff to withstand my Thunder Astral Force without losing ground. Against other attributes of astral force, he was even more invincible. I never expected you to take it all away."

Luo Chen shook his head. "Not quite. I didn't go too far—I left the vein behind. In time, Magnetic Divine Power will be born there again."

Yuan Qingzang was speechless.

Wasn't this exactly the same as what he had done at the Canopy Tower Forest?

Luo Chen relied on that special "Selfless Divine Intent" to both accommodate and imitate other martial intents, change the attributes of his astral force, and thereby learn from others' strengths to refine the essence of a hundred schools.

Yuan Qingzang said sourly, "So it seems this journey will be one long plunder for you."

Luo Chen smiled but did not deny it this time.

The word "plunder" sounded slightly better than "steal."

And that was indeed what he was doing.

In the Ruins, although the environment was harsh and resources scarce,

the resources that had survived were all uniquely special.

Like the Soul-Shaking Divine Thunder, like the Magnetic Divine Power.

Even if some things couldn't increase his strength right now, they could be saved for future use.

Moreover, Luo Chen tried not to go too far.

Just as he had discovered that "safe room" within the earth's magnetic vein, he had neither destroyed it nor taken it away.

One must leave some leeway!

Thinking of that safe room, Luo Chen's previously good mood became three parts heavier.

Clearly, that sealed space, built entirely from the most perfect magnetic ore, was specially constructed by someone to avoid tribulation.

The principle was similar to the Empty Sprout Crystal Cabin that Gu Xun had mentioned before.

Places where magnetic ore was found often had extremely stable world barriers.

A safe room built from it should be no different.

Such a grand undertaking was likely the work of Old Man Sorrowful Wind.

But in the end, he had abandoned this path and chosen to follow the Carefree Realm Master.

It was conceivable that he had no confidence in the safe room he had built himself, so he left it for his junior who had just broken through, while he went out to seek opportunities.

In the Ruins, everyone wanted to escape.

For now, the most promising opportunity was the Ancient God Dao Field, which had been the most dangerous over the years.

But was the Ancient God Dao Field truly an opportunity?

Luo Chen didn't know. He simply calculated the time silently. There was only about a hundred years left before the Dao Field opened.

He had plenty of time to get there, but he didn't know if there would still be space.

---

For ten thousand li, there was no sign of human habitation—only bones underfoot.

White peaks rose from the plains, and vultures and crows flew in flocks.

Between the sky and the earth, layers of dark clouds gathered, with thunder and lightning flashing.

Occasionally, white light streaked across, revealing hideous spatial cracks.

On this land that looked like an apocalyptic scene, two figures slowly landed.

"Is this the Corpse Remnant Immortal Domain?"

"Yes. Here, you must be careful. You can't act recklessly and arrogantly like before."

Yuan Qingzang's face was solemn, his whole body covered in Thunder Astral Force, protecting him tightly.

Luo Chen's gaze began to spread from the layers of bones at his feet into the distance.

An endless sea of bones, like an ocean.

The jagged white mountains were actually piles of bones.

A scene of a sea of bones and a thousand mountains.

Activating his Crimson-Golden Pupils, Luo Chen looked thousands of miles away.

The bolts of lightning, the spatial cracks like centipedes—all made one's heart tremble with fear.

"How could anyone survive in a place like this?"

Yuan Qingzang sighed. "So, the Corpse Remnant Immortal Domain is the only major force in the Ruins that is not a large human settlement."

Luo Chen was puzzled. "If it's not a large human settlement, how can it be a major force?"

Yuan Qingzang spread his hands. "Simple. As long as the person stationed here is strong enough."

Luo Chen hesitated. "You mean... that existence called 'Corpse'?"

"Yes, Corpse. No one knows Corpse's origins, but its power is beyond doubt. Its status is enough to rival the three others: the Carefree Realm Master of the Great Destruction, the Heavenly Abyss Constellation of the Star Sea, and the Old Monster Sky-Weep of the Heavenly Wind Tribe."

After saying this, he looked seriously at Luo Chen and warned again:

"So this time, don't go too far... No! Don't do those things anymore."

Luo Chen nodded solemnly.

He had never tested the Carefree Realm Master's power, but the fact that he could guard the Destruction Gate for three thousand years showed how terrifying his strength was.

To be ranked alongside him, that existence called "Corpse" must not be trifled with.

He just wondered if Corpse had already left like the others.

Luo Chen pulled himself together and continued along the planned route.

As he flew over the sea of bones beneath his feet, he couldn't help but ask curiously, "So many bones, some of which still retain the remnants of powerful auras—where did they come from?"

Yuan Qingzang said in a low voice, "According to our speculation, these bones should be the remains of living beings who died tragically in the fog during the last wave of the Destruction Fog. For some unknown reason, they gathered here, giving rise to the name 'Corpse Remnant Immortal Domain.'"

"Ten thousand years ago?" Luo Chen asked.

Yuan Qingzang hummed in affirmation. "The Destruction Fog comes once every ten thousand years. The twelfth time is the cycle of the epoch, known as the Great Destruction Era, which is the era we are in now. The Destruction Fog of the Great Destruction Era is inescapable for all living beings. But if it's just the ten-thousand-year Destruction Fog, its destructiveness is not that strong. Some traces will always remain, and top experts might even have ways to survive."

Luo Chen understood.

His gaze then fell on those spatial cracks.

"Can these cracks be used to leave the Ruins?"

Yuan Qingzang followed his gaze and shook his head regretfully.

"No!"

"Why not?"

"I don't know why! Some have tried to escape through the spatial cracks that appear in the Ruins, but the result was often their remains appearing elsewhere in the Ruins. According to the inference of the Heavenly Abyss Constellation of the Star Sea, the spatial cracks in the Ruins do not lead to the outside world but are caused by the continuous collapse of the internal space. The closer we get to the Great Destruction Era, the more spatial cracks will appear. They are definitely not a path to life."

The Star Sea—Heavenly Abyss Constellation.

Yuan Qingzang mentioned this name again.

Luo Chen committed it to memory, as one of the large human settlements on the necessary route to the Ancient God Dao Field was that very place.

As for what Yuan Qingzang had said, combined with what the Carefree Realm Master had told him before, Luo Chen had some guesses.

If the entire Ruins was the digestive organ of the endless universe, then these spatial cracks were neither the entrance nor the exit, but merely internal peristalsis.

Trying to escape through them was just accelerating the process of being digested.

So what was so special about the Ancient God Dao Field that made countless martial artists flock to it?

Just because it had existed long enough?

---

The two traveled forward, neither fast nor slow.

A month later.

Luo Chen's figure suddenly stopped.

Yuan Qingzang didn't understand why.

Luo Chen squinted into the distance and asked in a low voice, "Are you sure that the Corpse Remnant Immortal Domain only had Corpse before?"

Yuan Qingzang answered matter-of-factly, "Yes. This place is too dangerous. Apart from bones everywhere, there are no resources at all. Naturally, no normal humans would come to settle here. Besides, Corpse doesn't allow others to invade its territory. There are some small tribes around, but they strictly keep to their boundaries and don't easily set foot in the Corpse Remnant Immortal Domain."

Luo Chen shook his head.

"That's not what I see!"

Yuan Qingzang was puzzled but followed Luo Chen forward.

After several thousand li, faint chanting sounds drifted over.

"All living things decay, only bones endure forever
Corpse wax sacrifices, qi and blood burn as fuel
..." (End of chapter)
