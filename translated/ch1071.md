# Chapter 1071: Grand Formation of the Mystic Realm, Grotto-Heaven Opens Anew

On the Great Catalpa Flat, a young man in white robes stood proudly, holding a azure jade ruler in his hand.

Beneath his feet lay his arena opponent, his face filled with terror.

"Is this your true strength?"

In that instant, the power his opponent had unleashed had completely surpassed the Adept stage, leaving him utterly unable to react—defeated in a single blow.

Zhou Fuyou did not look at him. Instead, his gaze swept the surroundings, his brow slightly furrowed, as if lost in thought.

In the distance, the Dao Sect elders watching the battle were full of praise.

This junior of the Shi family had always borne the name of a genius. In this Grand Competition, everyone had placed high hopes on him, expecting him to contend for the top five and aim for the top three. And yet, he had been so easily defeated by Zhou Fuyou's hand.

That instant of explosive power had already surpassed the Adept stage, nearly rivaling an early-stage Sage.

"As expected of Fairy Qinglan's beloved disciple!"

"As I see it, he probably hasn't even used his full strength yet."

"That wasn't his full strength? At that same realm, I never had such formidable magical power."

"I've heard that Zhou Fuyou's signature technique rivals a Sage's domain, earning the praise 'Qi Sea Floating Heaven, Myriad Manifestations Dense and Majestic.' Have any of you seen him use that move?"

"Tsk tsk, someone with a special Dao body is truly monstrous. No wonder this child is called another Shi Ju..."

"Watch your words!"

Amidst the clamor of discussion, a handsome man flicked his sleeves and departed, his brows and eyes concealing displeasure.

It was none other than the current head of the Shi family—Shi Ju!

His cultivation level was not particularly high, merely the sixth layer of the Sage stage.

But his overall strength was extremely extraordinary. It was rumored that back when he was at the fourth layer of the Sage stage, he had briefly crossed hands with a Great Demon King in the Eastern Wilderness without losing the upper hand. Over a hundred years had passed since then; he must be even more formidable now.

After the Shi family's Supreme Elder had fallen at Heaven-Earth Peak, he had taken up the position of family head, becoming the current ruler of the great Shi House.

The disciple who had just lost to Zhou Fuyou was a junior he had favored, yet he had lost so completely.

He was displeased with his own clansman's defeat, but even more furious that he had lost to the disciple of that wretched woman!

Faced with Shi Ju's graceless departure in displeasure, no one offered any commentary.

Many knew that Shi Ju had been arrogant and overbearing since childhood, difficult to get along with. Especially after the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven Grand Competition, his temperament had become even more unpredictable, often striking out and injuring others at the slightest provocation. It was said that within the grotto-heaven, he had been defeated by a certain Dao protector and had also lost a painstakingly refined clone. Such a massive blow had made his personality increasingly extreme.

A Dao Sect elder stepped forward, announced Zhou Fuyou's victory, and informed him to rest for three days before participating in the subsequent matches.

Zhou Fuyou nodded calmly, then walked down from the Great Catalpa Flat.

Shi Miaoyin, along with several junior fellow disciples, happily came forward to greet him, their voices filled with congratulations and praise.

Yet Shi Miaoyin detected, beneath Zhou Fuyou's seemingly calm expression, a hint of bewilderment in his eyes.

"Junior Brother, what's wrong?"

Zhou Fuyou shook his head, his gaze still aimlessly searching the surroundings, but finding nothing.

"What was that? Why did it give me such a heart-pounding sensation just now?"

"Who was watching me?"

"If it was an elder of the sect, they could have openly observed my match. If it was some petty villain, with my divine sense—which my master says rivals that of a Sage—they certainly wouldn't have escaped my perception. But why did that feeling come and go in an instant?"

Zhou Fuyou's heart was unsettled.

The "strong enemy" he had faced on the Great Catalpa Flat's arena earlier was not actually strong.

He could have taken him down with ordinary techniques and the Slaughter Yin Ruler gifted by his ancestor. But because of a prickling sensation, like needles at his back, his mind had wavered, and he had subconsciously unleashed his full power.

The impression it gave to onlookers was naturally earth-shattering, but no one knew the turmoil in his heart.

Letting out a breath of pent-up frustration, Zhou Fuyou pushed through the crowd surrounding him.

"I'm a bit tired. I want to go back and rest. My apologies."

Everyone was taken aback.

Although they were not all disciples of Fairy Qinglan, they were all disciples of the four halls of the Tiannan Dao Palace.

They had competed before, but only within the Tiannan Dao Palace, and had already established their rankings.

Zhou Fuyou was the number one of Tiannan!

Coming this time to compete at the ancestral seat of the Dao Sect in the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range, they all represented the Tiannan Dao Palace. Any rankings they achieved would ultimately be fed back as rewards to the Tiannan Dao Palace that had taught them.

Yet unexpectedly, Zhou Fuyou was still so aloof.

Senior Sister Shi Miaoyin sensed that Zhou Fuyou's mood was off. After he left, she offered a brief explanation to the others.

"Shi Rui's strength is considerable. Defeating him took a lot out of Fuyou. In three days, he'll be facing Gu Yu in battle. Give him some time to recover!"

The others understood, and then began discussing Zhou Fuyou's next opponent, Gu Yu.

She was a woman, from the Gu family, one of the Twelve Great Houses. She was also renowned as a genius across the Libei Continent, and was a top contender for first place in this Sexagenary Grand Competition, even above Shi Rui.

Such an opponent was indeed worth proper rest and recovery, to be taken seriously.

Three days later, there would surely be an exceptionally brilliant battle!

...

Leaving the Great Catalpa Flat, Zhou Fuyou did not return to his lodgings to rest as he had said. Instead, he slowly walked along the scenic paths within the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range.

He kept examining himself, searching for the source of that heart-pounding sensation from earlier.

But still, he found nothing.

He could only think that perhaps he was overthinking things, or that some high-level senior from the sect had unintentionally put too much pressure on him while watching the match.

Passing by a mist-shrouded mountain, Zhou Fuyou paused.

"Is this the Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak, famous alongside the Stone Chamber Mountain? When my master was studying under Ancestor Chunyuan, she cultivated here for a long time. If I can break through to the Sage stage in the future, my master wants me to enter this mountain to cultivate, continuing the lineage of Chunyuan."

Zhou Fuyou gazed at Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak for a long time, his face revealing no particular expression—whether satisfied or not was unclear.

He resumed walking, his pace alternating between fast and slow within the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range.

When the moon reached its zenith, he had unknowingly walked out of the vast Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range.

The sound of rolling waves reached his ears; the Passing River flowed eastward at his feet.

Suddenly!

Zhou Fuyou's gaze sharpened. He saw two figures standing side by side in the distance.

As if sensing something, the two figures glanced at him, then departed.

What kind of looks were those?

One was calm as a lake, its gaze like the moon, as if utterly uninterested in him.

The other carried a hint of appraisal, curiosity, and even a trace of surprise.

"Are they senior brothers from the Dao Sect?"

Zhou Fuyou frowned, thinking. He should have approached them, but for some reason, his feet remained rooted to the spot as he instinctively watched them walk away.

"There are still two matches left. I can't afford any complications!"

"My master and the others have placed great hopes on me. The Observatory Master and the other three Hall Masters even joined forces to refine my lifebound magic treasure. I must win first place this time."

"I'd better go back and rest properly!"

Under the moonlight, the white figure walked upstream, returning to the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range.

...

"That junior just now—do you know him?"

"I don't."

"How could you not know him? He performed exceptionally well on the Great Catalpa Flat. His magical power is unparalleled among those of the same realm. In all my life, I've never seen such an outstanding Adept-stage cultivator. Didn't you notice him?"

"What are you trying to say, Senior?"

"Heh, I sensed something interesting from him. He seems to have some inexplicable connection with you."

Luo Chen stopped walking and looked at Li Canghai without any emotion.

In response to this reaction, Li Canghai became even more certain of the strangeness he had detected earlier.

He was a Formation Dao Ascendant. He could see the grand trends of heaven and earth, the flow of origin qi, more clearly than ordinary Ascendants. Around Zhou Fuyou, a vast amount of origin qi lingered, as if he could absorb it freely at will. No wonder the young man had reached the ninth layer of the Adept stage at such a young age.

And most importantly, the other's soul aura might be able to fool a Sage, but not him.

Especially when Luo Chen was standing right beside him!

The two were definitely connected!

Thinking of Luo Chen's audacity in targeting the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, and his boldness that suggested some hidden preparations...

"That junior is your..."

"Senior Li!"

Luo Chen interrupted him. This was the first time Luo Chen had cut him off since they had known each other.

Faced with this rude gesture, Li Canghai's eyes narrowed slightly.

Luo Chen, acting as if nothing had happened, said calmly, "You and I are merely cooperating. Just as I haven't pressed you about your motives for targeting the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, I hope you won't meddle too much in my affairs."

Li Canghai gave a wry laugh, then waved his hand.

"It was this old man being too nosy."

Luo Chen did not pursue the matter further. His gaze looked into the distance.

"Let's go. Let's take a look at that God-Descending Grand Formation you mentioned!"

The two figures flickered, ascending straight into the nine heavens. They summoned clouds to conceal themselves, then flew out beyond the Dongyuan Continent.

Leaving the continent, crossing the vast ocean, they finally stopped within a layer of clouds.

Their gazes fell together upon an island below.

Heaven-Earth Island!

It was less an island and more like a small continent.

The broken Heaven-Earth Peak lay there quietly, as if it had always been that way.

Yet everyone knew that Heaven-Earth Peak had once connected heaven and earth, and its current appearance was only a little over a hundred years old.

"As you said, Senior, a hidden grand formation is indeed laid out on Heaven-Earth Island, constantly absorbing earth qi from the seabed. Is that the God-Descending Formation?"

"Yes, that is the famous ancient formation from the Mystic Realm—the God-Descending Formation." Li Canghai nodded, a look of longing in his eyes. "Legend has it that the God-Descending Formation was set up by people of the Mystic Realm to communicate with the divine thoughts of immortals who had ascended to the Immortal Realm. Its mysteries are profound. If this old man hadn't learned of this formation from the inheritance of Devil Lord Heaven-Refining, I probably wouldn't have recognized it either."

Saying this, Li Canghai gave Luo Chen a meaningful smile.

"Young friend seems to have entered the Fallen Sea as well. In that Demon-Fall Land, did you gain anything?"

Luo Chen replied frankly, "A little. I obtained the Devil Lord's alchemy inheritance."

Li Canghai thought to himself, *Just as I suspected!*

Ever since they had parted ways at the Ice Pole Sect in the Blue Ice Sea Region, he had made a point of investigating Luo Chen thoroughly.

He had discovered that Luo Chen, like himself, had once entered the Fallen Sea.

After coming out, he had been pursued by the Moon Wanderer.

That meant he certainly hadn't come back empty-handed. He must have obtained some major gain, which was why the Moon Wanderer had pursued him so relentlessly.

And in Li Canghai's speculation, Luo Chen had most likely obtained the alchemy inheritance. Otherwise, there was no explaining how he possessed such heaven-defying alchemy skills.

But Li Canghai himself didn't particularly covet it.

Inheritance was inheritance. Being able to absorb, digest, and advance to Ascendant still depended on one's own fate.

He didn't think that by snatching it, he could achieve anything in the alchemy path.

Rather than snatch it, it was better to befriend him. That way, he could later get to know an alchemy-path Ascendant.

Pulling his thoughts back, Li Canghai cleared his throat and pointed below as he explained, "The conditions for activating this God-Descending Formation are extremely harsh. First and foremost, it requires a 'waypoint'!"

"What is a 'waypoint'?"

"Literally, it can be understood as a road marker, a spatial direction, and so on. But in the understanding of us cultivators, you can think of it as something capable of cross-realm communication—such as divine thoughts, bloodlines, or even magic treasures."

"The Star Gate?"

"The Dao Sect's spirit treasure, the Star Gate, is indeed such an existence, and its effect is excellent. But if Liancheng went to the trouble of setting up the God-Descending Formation, it shows he either doesn't have the Star Gate, or he has it but can't use it."

"Please continue, Senior."

"Waypoints are relative. Not only is there the object of supplication, but the supplicating side also needs a corresponding existence. The intact Heaven-Earth Peak could serve as a landmark for positioning. But to summon a Mystic Realm powerhouse down to this world through the God-Descending Formation, the amount of energy required is also rarely seen in this world. At the very least, relying solely on this Heaven-Earth Island is not enough!"

Luo Chen's eyes showed a contemplative expression as he speculated, "According to what you're saying, Senior, relying on Heaven-Earth Island alone isn't enough. That's why Liancheng plans to reassemble the Central Continent, attempting to use the power of an entire continent to bear the descent of a powerhouse from the upper realm?"

Li Canghai nodded, a tone of admiration in his voice: "If it were merely summoning an existence below the Ascendant stage across realms, Heaven-Earth Island itself would suffice. But if they need to mobilize the earth qi of the Central Continent, then the existence descending would be terrifyingly powerful—most likely a Deity Transformation stage being!"

The universe was boundless, space infinite.

According to the records in the *Mystic Realm Secret Record*, the Mystic Realm and the Mountains and Seas Realm were not adjacent; the distance between them was extremely vast.

To descend across such a great distance, and with a cultivation level of Deity Transformation—a realm not permitted in this world—mobilizing the power of an entire continent might even be considered minimal.

Luo Chen did not want to see that happen.

"It must be stopped! Once, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect used the spirit treasure Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard to suppress the spiritual mechanism of an entire continent. With the collapse of the Central Continent, the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard was also severely damaged. A hundred years is not enough for it to recover. That's why the Dao Sect chose to reassemble the Central Continent with formations. If we destroy those formations and prevent the Central Continent from being reassembled, can we stop the God-Descending Formation from being activated?"

"It is indeed possible, but this only treats the symptoms, not the root cause. After all, formations can be rebuilt at any time. But if we destroy them frequently, we will inevitably invite the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's counterattack. Can you bear that?" Li Canghai asked in return.

Luo Chen shook his head. Not even he, let alone Li Canghai, could withstand the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's counterattack.

In all the world, only Rosycloud could make the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect cautious. Even Patriarch Liancheng dared not set foot in the Eastern Wilderness.

Li Canghai smiled. "Therefore, the best way to stop the God-Descending Formation's activation is to find the perfect timing. At the very beginning of its activation, destroy the pivotal formation that reassembles the Central Continent. That way, not only will the God-Descending Formation fail to successfully summon a powerhouse from the upper realm, but Liancheng himself, who is presiding over the formation, will suffer backlash. At that moment, you and I will make our move and kill him. Then we will no longer have to fear the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's counterattack."

Luo Chen's eyes flickered. Strike at the moment the formation is activated, causing the opponent's efforts to fall short and even triggering backlash?

This was indeed the best method!

"Let's wait. It should be about a year from now. In the meantime, this old man will also take the opportunity to study this profound God-Descending Formation."

"Didn't you say Liancheng is on Heaven-Earth Island?"

"Only observe from afar, don't approach. With this old man's methods, I can still glimpse the clues. Liancheng won't discover us."

"Is that so? Then I'll take a look too!"

"You?" Li Canghai was startled for a moment, then reacted and laughed heartily. "I forgot that your formation skills are also quite impressive."

Luo Chen smiled faintly. He was indeed quite curious about this special kind of formation.

It could allow an immortal's divine thought to descend, or allow weaker Ascendant-stage greats or even Deity Transformation lords to descend into the mortal realm. If used well, after he ascended and left the Mountains and Seas Realm, he might still be able to maintain contact with old friends in this world.

To avoid being discovered by Patriarch Liancheng, the two kept their distance from Heaven-Earth Island. But each had their own methods to spy on the situation there.

Formations emphasize form but are not bound by rigid rules. In the eyes of a formation master, formations are more about the evolution of the grand trends of heaven and earth.

Thus, by observing the changes in this part of heaven and earth, one could conversely deduce the principles of the formation.

That was Li Canghai's method.

Luo Chen's was simpler. He merely used his naked eyes to observe.

In the midst of this observation, time passed quickly. In the blink of an eye, a month had gone by.

Suddenly!

"Someone has landed on the island."

Luo Chen was startled awake by Li Canghai's divine sense transmission. He instinctively looked toward the shore of Heaven-Earth Island.

A group of forty to fifty people were disembarking from a flying boat and setting foot on the land of Heaven-Earth Island.

Most of them were Sages, with only ten younger ones being Adepts.

Luo Chen's gaze sharpened. He saw Zhou Fuyou among them.

At that moment, Li Canghai also moved closer.

"That junior is among them too."

Luo Chen said nothing, merely curious about what this group was doing here.

Li Canghai also looked curious as he observed the situation from afar. But soon, his expression changed slightly.

"Pay attention to concealment!"

After saying this, he flicked his hand, and a stream of light enveloped the two of them.

The next moment, from within the broken Heaven-Earth Peak on Heaven-Earth Island, a grand voice resounded.

"Have you come?"

"Reporting to the Ancestor, everyone has arrived. Please, Ancestor, open the grotto-heaven gate!"

"Hmm."

After a faint response, two large hands formed from magical power extended from within the broken Heaven-Earth Peak, reaching toward a certain void space at the foot of the mountain.

The two hands seemed to embed themselves into the spatial barrier, then slowly pried it open from the outside.

*Rumble!*

A thunderous roar erupted. The earth trembled, and the seawater nearby churned violently.

Only a sound like *ripping* was heard, and then a vast amount of white mist surged out, completely enveloping the entire Heaven-Earth Island.

After doing all this, the two large hands slowly disappeared. The voice from before spoke again.

"Go on in. Ke Lianshan is waiting for you inside."

Clearly, forcibly opening that space seemed to be very taxing for this person, as even his voice had become weary.

And in the distance, Luo Chen stared at this familiar scene, his face filled with shock and uncertainty.

"The Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven has been opened again?"
