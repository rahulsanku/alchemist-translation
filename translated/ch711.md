# Chapter 711: Black King Transforms into a Dragon, Luo Chen Feels the Quickening

Strictly speaking, the first pill book Luo Chen ever encountered came from Mi Shuhua's reward.

It was called Detailed Explanations of Qing Yuanzi's Alchemy Dao [TERM?].

This pill explanation recorded in detail the refinement experiences for low-tier pills, and also included several basic alchemy arts such as Stream-Washing Hand [TERM?] and Jade-Breaking Hand [TERM?].

When Luo Chen's realm was low, these alchemy arts had provided him enormous convenience.

Aside from these, there was another art named the Qingyuan Pill-Nurturing Art [TERM?]!

Back when Qing Yuanzi [TERM?] wrote this pill book, his realm had only been the Foundation Establishment stage, yet he had forcibly refined third-tier pills.

He did not have a cheat like Luo Chen's attribute panel. The reason he could refine pills above his own realm was this pill-nurturing art.

Qingyuan Pill-Nurturing Art [TERM?]:

It required an alchemy tool, at the very least an upper-grade law device, with supreme-grade better, and magic-treasure level best!

The pill to be nurtured also had to be supreme-grade!

Not only that, during the nurturing process, at least ten portions or so of raw materials also needed to be provided.

In addition, it had to borrow geographical advantage. A spirit vein location was best, and at the very least it needed to be a special environment beneficial to the pill.

Finally, the alchemist had to constantly temper the pill's quality with Foundation Establishment true fire.

And the time for this nurturing could be as short as half a year, or as long as a hundred years.

It could be called a complicated process that consumed an extremely long time!

Luo Chen remembered this method deeply mainly because the conditions for using this alchemy art were incomparably harsh for Qi Refinement and Foundation Establishment cultivators!

But looking at it from his current realm, these so-called harsh conditions were actually not that difficult to satisfy.

Moreover, with Luo Chen's attainments as a fourth-tier alchemist, when he reverse-deduced the Qingyuan Pill-Nurturing Art [TERM?], he discovered many errors and omissions within.

Even so, Luo Chen still felt this alchemy art possessed certain merits.

Especially since he already naturally satisfied some of the conditions.

For a spirit vein location, he had a fourth-tier one.

For the required true fire, he had the already sentient Withering-Bloom True Flame.

As for time, he lacked that even less.

As for the other conditions...

"The pill grade needs to be supreme-grade, but that restriction only exists because low-tier pills lack medicinal power. My fourth-tier Nascent Soul Pill, if placed below fourth tier, how is it not the finest of the finest?"

"Around ten portions of raw materials, that is even less of a problem. Although there are not many scraps left, I have ten complete finished pills, and the effect will only be better!"

"The only thing restricting me is instead the alchemy tool."

Luo Chen already had the idea of nurturing the Nascent Soul Pill to raise its grade.

Just as he had said to Han Zhan, if he could ensure a hundred percent success, why risk it on a seventy or eighty percent chance?

When it came to alchemy tools, Luo Chen had something of a headache.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron could not be used.

Of the three backup alchemy tools, two had been scrapped after refining the pills because they could not withstand the Withering-Bloom True Flame's firepower.

The remaining alchemy furnace was now also on the verge of collapse.

Logically speaking, Luo Chen had no fallback.

Yet, as it happened, he actually still had one object in hand!

Whoosh!

Spiritual light flashed, and a yellowed copper basin landed before him.

It was precisely the Dragon-Nurturing Basin [TERM?]!

This treasure had the effect of nurturing treasures and raising their grade.

In the past, Luo Chen had used it to nurture the Primordial Unity Cauldron, nurturing that lifebound magic treasure extremely well. Originally, it had been estimated to require two or three hundred years of nurturing to completely remove impurities, yet after using the Dragon-Nurturing Basin [TERM?], in only a few decades, the Primordial Unity Cauldron had reached the point of revealing its sharpness.

At that time, he had thought this magic treasure could only be used to nurture weapons.

But later, he slowly realized that he had fallen into a rut, influenced by the operation of the Dragon-Nurturing Basin's [TERM?] previous owner, Qian Ting [TERM?].

This magic treasure could in fact be used not only to nurture weapons, but also to nurture pills, and even ores and medicinal grasses to some extent.

Its effects were outstanding, absolutely not something contemporary cultivators could refine.

Luo Chen had no intention of digging into the Dragon-Nurturing Basin's [TERM?] origins, but right now, it had become the best tool for nurturing Nascent Soul Pills.

After seriously thinking for two days and ensuring there were no omissions, Luo Chen began the preparatory steps before nurturing the pills.

He placed the Dragon-Nurturing Basin [TERM?] in the location within the earth abyss where the spirit qi was most abundant.

He arranged formations nearby, then placed nine Nascent Soul Pills within.

Yes, only nine.

To guard against mishaps, Luo Chen left one behind.

This way, even if the pill-nurturing failed, Luo Chen would not lack a backup.

Lastly came the Withering-Bloom True Flame!

Luo Chen split off a wisp of Withering-Bloom True Flame seed. Under slow scorching, it could last one year.

A year later, he could simply replenish it.

Speaking of which, this small method of splitting fire seeds was also something he had comprehended during his years of seclusion.

The Withering-Bloom True Flame of the past was only one blossom and absolutely could not be split.

But after the Withering-Bloom Fire [TERM?] absorbed many fire spirits and its foundation became increasingly deep, Luo Chen had comprehended this small method without a teacher.

Previously, he had specially split off three fire seeds and given them to Heavenjade, which was a flexible use of this method.

Looking at the copper basin, where under the formation's guidance the medicinal power of eight pills slowly gathered toward the central pill, Luo Chen nodded in satisfaction.

Before he had more raw materials to refine pills in great quantity, improve his alchemy proficiency, and refine middle-grade or even upper-grade Nascent Soul Pills, the wondrous use of this pill-nurturing art to raise the grade of finished pills, even by only one small level, was absolutely an unexpected joy.

"I do not ask for it to rise to fifth tier, but as long as it can rise from lower-grade fourth tier to middle-grade, or even upper-grade, it will be enough for my Nascent Soul formation."

After murmuring, Luo Chen returned to the thatched hut.

Glancing at the attribute panel, the realm entry above showed that Adept Stage Nine had already reached halfway through its progress.

Only a little under ten years remained before Adept Perfected Completion.

Perhaps he needed to hurry a little.

...

To call it urgent, in truth, it was not that urgent.

In cultivating the Adept Stage Nine realm, after stripping away the process of attempting to unite the three treasures, Luo Chen only needed to increase mana and polish mana. What he did was very simple.

Therefore, he still had free time to often go out, chat idly with Han Zhan, and ask him about the many matters requiring attention during the Nascent Soul formation process.

The place for chatting and discussing Dao was generally Mount Rosy Hill [TERM?], which was also the highest and most scenic place on all of Dragon Abyss Island [TERM?].

On this day.

Luo Chen and Han Zhan still sat opposite one another, drinking wine and chatting.

"Since coming to Dragon Abyss Island [TERM?], it has already been seventeen years. I see that your realm is only one step away from Adept Perfected Completion, yet I find that your three treasures of essence, qi, and spirit remain clearly separated. Luo Chen, have you taken a wrong path?"

Han Zhan voiced a doubt he had suppressed for a long time.

Over these years, Luo Chen had re-engraved the Concealment Veil Formation, and origin power constantly spread throughout his body, so Han Zhan was unable to observe Luo Chen's specific situation.

He could only glimpse the tip of the other man's iceberg through occasional exchanges.

But he could still see that essence, qi, and spirit were not subordinated to one another and showed no sign of fusion.

At this, Luo Chen laughed despite himself.

"Since Senior knows this, how could you not know the principle that the three treasures must be balanced and ordered? Before I have absolute confidence, how would I dare force the fusion?"

Han Zhan was thoughtful. "You have always developed in all directions and have almost no weakness. How could your three treasures be imbalanced? Could it be that the gap is caused by your physique being too strong?"

Luo Chen smiled and nodded.

The other man had not suspected a lack in his divine soul foundation.

This was only natural.

Although Luo Chen had never deliberately cultivated any profound spirit-refining method, his divine soul foundation had never been poor.

In the past, when he was at Adept Stage Six, it could already compare to Adept Stage Eight and was infinitely close to a Stage Nine Great Cultivator.

Now, he was one step away from Adept Perfected Completion, and his divine soul foundation had also, without him realizing, approached infinitely close to that of a Sage cultivator.

In the end, the reason Luo Chen was trapped and unable to advance further was still that his physique was too strong and his mana too weak.

However!

This problem was also about to be resolved.

His fingertip circled, and a wisp of spiritual light flickered faintly.

Although its quantity was small, the power contained within drew the eye.

Han Zhan looked at this scene in shock, somewhat unable to believe it.

Was this the quality of mana an Adept cultivator could possess?

Its purity could completely compare to the mana that condensed a cultivator's Nascent Soul body!

He shook his head and sighed. "I truly do not know how you polished it to this degree. Looking at it now, losing that second Origin Core [TERM?] was a blessing born from misfortune for you instead."

Luo Chen deeply agreed.

In the past, cultivating two Golden Cores at the same time had made him pursue quantity too much, and without realizing it, he had neglected polishing his mana.

Although it was still purer than that of his peers, it had ultimately been lacking, far from reaching the limit Luo Chen himself could achieve.

During these dozen years on Dragon Abyss Island [TERM?], Luo Chen, having calmed his heart, had returned to the proper path.

Every wisp of mana condensed into his Golden Core had been constantly purified by him, then constantly tempered with the Withering-Bloom True Flame, finally producing a qualitative change.

Now, even though his mana level was still inferior to origin power, with the cooperation of divine soul power, it could barely achieve the fusion of the three treasures.

Luo Chen had already made his plan. Once he reached Adept Perfected Completion, he would carry out this process.

Since Han Zhan knew Luo Chen had reached this point, he explained the details within to him.

When cultivators reached Adept Stage Nine, they had to coordinate the three treasures of essence, qi, and spirit, allowing them to achieve a certain balance.

The sign of this balance being achieved was condensing the Origin Embryo [TERM?]!

Essence blood and divine soul merged into the Golden Core, using the Golden Core as a vessel to form the so-called Origin Embryo [TERM?].

This was completely different from the divine soul being tinged with mana during the early Adept stage.

That kind of tinging was no more than a hurried passerby, withdrawing at the slightest touch.

But this kind of integration required long-term residence.

When a certain opportunity arrived, the Origin Embryo [TERM?] would resonate with heaven and earth, producing a "quickening" only the cultivator himself could perceive.

At that time, it would be the day of Nascent Soul formation!

To summarize:

The three treasures unite, and the Golden Core transforms into an embryo.

The quickening brings tribulation, the core shatters and forms the Nascent Soul!

Only then could one be considered a Sage Perfected Being, able to sense the truly vast heaven and earth!

Luo Chen listened as if intoxicated, while constantly reflecting it against the classics he had read in the past.

It was said that in ancient times, Qi cultivator realms were not divided as strictly as now, and had many titles.

For example, the Qi Refinement stage was divided into Drawing Qi [TERM?] and Spiritual Light [TERM?], while Foundation Establishment also had sayings such as Opening Apertures [TERM?], Sensing [TERM?], Dividing Meridians [TERM?], and Liquefaction [TERM?].

In the Adept stage in particular, it was even more complicated.

Entering Spirit [TERM?], Expelling Balefulness [TERM?], Seeking the Root [TERM?], Quickening [TERM?]... several titles stood between the Adept and Sage stages.

But myriad arts returned to one source, and the qi refinement path ultimately reached the same destination by different roads. When these mysterious titles were connected to his own cultivation, Luo Chen felt enlightened, as if a blockage had suddenly cleared.

Han Zhan's words had pointed out a clear road for him amid much chaos.

A clear road verified countless times by predecessors in the current cultivation world!

At dusk, this discussion of Dao came to its end.

After Luo Chen rose, he bowed to Han Zhan, his bearing respectful, sincere, and earnest.

"You..."

"Along this path, although you and I have supported one another, in the end Senior has given more guidance and I have benefited more. This bow comes from Luo Chen's heart."

Han Zhan was caught somewhat off guard. In the past, Luo Chen had never respected him this much!

The former him had been a remnant Sage body, barely surviving under Luo Chen's roof.

Although there had been a difference in realm, circumstances had not been up to him.

Especially to seek this possessed flesh body, he had painstakingly helped Luo Chen improve his strength and research combat methods.

Now that the other man spoke from the heart, he was instead somewhat stunned.

Only, looking at Luo Chen's sincere expression, his gaze gradually became complicated, and for a moment he had no words.

Just then, Luo Chen suddenly turned!

Han Zhan did not understand, but in the next instant his expression also shifted slightly, and he raised his eyes to gaze toward the endless blue sea in the distance.

A powerful aura was continuously brewing in the deep sea.

In the sky, dark clouds gathered suddenly.

They came without cause, and their momentum was great!

Crack!

White light flashed, and thunder and lightning split through the air.

"This is a sign of undergoing tribulation!" Han Zhan blurted out.

Luo Chen, however, looked startled and uncertain, and finally slowly shook his head.

"It is not undergoing tribulation, but the natural completion of the process, with heaven and earth sounding thunder in congratulation."

Sure enough.

In the next moment, the sky-full dark clouds leisurely dispersed.

Just as they had gathered without cause, they dispersed silently.

There was no startling thunder or lightning, no bitter wind or cold rain.

On the sea not far from Dragon Abyss Island [TERM?], countless strands of spirit qi gathered, forming a funnel shape and pouring backward into the sea.

Han Zhan stared at this scene in astonishment, faintly feeling somewhat familiar.

Suddenly!

He turned his gaze onto Luo Chen.

Yes, the last time this strange celestial phenomenon appeared, it had appeared on Luo Chen.

At that time, at Peng Lake, his flesh body had undergone a transformation, as if becoming an ancient archaic beast!

"Has Black King stepped onto the path of archaic beast evolution?" Han Zhan said.

Luo Chen slowly nodded. "It should be so. His demon core has already shattered, and the blood pill I condensed for him was only an expedient measure. Moreover, in earlier years, Black King mentioned more than once that he did not want to walk the demon beast transformation path, and instinctively wanted to walk the archaic beast path. Now, it can be considered his final decision."

When likewise advancing to fourth tier, demon beasts would undergo a qualitative change.

Not only could they transform into human form, they could also condense a Nascent Soul like humans, being called both Demon Emperors and demon cultivators.

At the same time, their perception of heaven and earth was faster than when they were in their original demon bodies.

But archaic beasts, aside from giving birth to origin power, did not undergo too great a change.

This was also the main reason why, after the ancient war, the vast majority of demon beasts changed their path and walked the transformation route.

Whether Black King's choice was right or wrong was unknown.

At this moment, Han Zhan had another feeling.

"In the past, people all said archaic beasts were the true favored children of the Mountains and Seas Realm, and that even when advancing to fourth tier, this world would not send down lightning tribulation."

"At that time, I only took it as rumor."

"But with you and Black King as examples, now I have no choice but to believe it."

Mentioning lightning tribulation, Han Zhan asked curiously, "Have you thought about how, when advancing to Sage, you will resist heavenly might and pass that Nascent Soul lightning tribulation?"

Luo Chen nodded but did not say much. He leapt up, stepped on auspicious clouds, and flew to the shore.

Standing there quietly, Luo Chen sensed Black King's transformation.

This stand lasted an entire night.

Early the next morning.

The waves parted, and a huge deep-black dragon head slowly emerged from the water.

Its face was like a camel's, and its single horn was like an inverted cone, with spiral patterns spreading around it ring after ring.

Two strands of yellow whiskers hung leisurely down.

Its slightly open mouth revealed one snow-white tooth after another, as if ready at any moment to tear apart anything that dared challenge it.

Whoosh...

When it exhaled, two streams of smoke sprayed from its enormous nostrils, splashing up countless waves.

And beneath that high, protruding brow, round golden eyes stood vertically, containing a cold and chilling intent.

Meeting those eyes with only one glance, one knew the violence contained within.

Luo Chen's expression was calm as he slowly stretched out his right hand.

At this moment, hesitation and struggle appeared in the dragon head's vertical eyes.

But in the end, he still slowly brought his dragon head close in intimacy.

His palm pressed against the slick, ink-black dragon face, and Luo Chen revealed a satisfied smile.

He could sense that there was still a faint connection between himself and Black King.

It was not a slave seal.

That slave seal had already collapsed during his physical nirvana. Not only Black King, even Heavenjade no longer had his slave seal on her.

This connection came from bloodline!

Thinking about it now, the reason Black King finally chose the archaic beast evolution path was, aside from instinct, also partly due to that drop of tongue-tip essence blood Luo Chen had given back then.

Luo Chen himself also cultivated an archaic beast body cultivation method!

"Master..."

A thick and heavy voice slowly sounded.

Thump!

Luo Chen patted his face and said with a smile, "Go back! You finally advanced, so consolidate your realm properly and don't wander around everywhere. Soon, I will attempt to break through to the Sage realm. I will still need you to guard Dragon Abyss Island [TERM?] to prevent petty people from disturbing me."

Black King snorted loudly and slowly retreated into the sea, then soared up and plunged into the ocean like a sharp arrow.

Amid splashing water, Luo Chen vaguely saw the four feet beneath Black King's belly.

Although he still had a single horn, feet had finally grown from his belly, and he had become a true jiaolong!

No longer half one thing and half another like before.

Luo Chen turned around, his back to the morning glow, his face full of smiles, clearly in excellent spirits.

Behind him, Heavenjade had already been waiting quietly for a long time.

Seeing this scene, she could not help asking, "Master, has Black King advanced?"

"Yes." As Luo Chen passed her, he patted her shoulder and encouraged her with a smile. "You should not fall too far behind either."

He was in a very good mood.

Not at all embarrassed that his own spirit pet had advanced one step ahead of him.

If one truly spoke of it, his achievement of a fourth-tier archaic beast body had come before the other party's, so what embarrassment was there?

He only felt gratified, gratified that everything he had invested had finally blossomed and borne fruit.

He just did not know what Black King's abilities were now.

...

After returning to the earth abyss, Luo Chen next remained indoors, and did not even visit Han Zhan at all.

Clearly, he had now truly begun death seclusion.

Time slowly passed.

His mana began increasing at an unquestionable speed.

When the entire vast qi sea could no longer absorb even one drop of spirit qi, it meant he had set foot at the peak of the Adept stage: Perfected Completion!

At this point, his mana could no longer advance an inch, nor could it continue to be polished and purified.

Only a crimson Golden Core, compressed to the utmost and incomparably solid, hung high in the qi sea like a great sun.

Luo Chen tried to unite the three treasures, mobilizing the divine soul in his sea of consciousness and merging it into the Golden Core. Not only that, drop after drop of essence blood emerged from every corner of his body and also gathered toward the Golden Core.

His originally powerful body seemed to become shriveled and withered.

But within his qi sea.

Liquid essence blood, the solid Golden Core, and the illusory divine soul began tangling together, merging into one.

An indescribably wondrous change continuously arose within him, continuously transforming, continuously spreading toward the peak.

Until three years later.

Thump!

A soft sound, almost inaudible.

On the white-robed Daoist sitting dry and still within the thatched hut, a ring of invisible ripples surged outward, causing the earth abyss to rumble.

The Daoist slowly opened his eyes.

The quickening had come!

*(End of Chapter)*
