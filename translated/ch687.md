# Chapter 687: City of Puppets, the Simplest Trial

"This old body, Cape Heaven Matron, greets Fellow Daoist!"

The moment her words fell, the old woman abruptly attacked, flicking a bent finger toward Luo Chen.

A ghostly light arrived in an instant.

As decisive as she was, she had directly formed the thought of eliminating her competitor.

But at that very moment, when the ghostly light approached within a radius of one zhang around Luo Chen, it vanished without cause.

Just like that, soundlessly, without the slightest movement, it disappeared.

"This!"

The old woman cried out in alarm, seemingly having never expected such a turn.

On Luo Chen's side, he also let out a startled sound.

"Hm..."

Within the Pill Realm, that weak voice appeared once more.

"Within the Pill Realm, only the Dao of Pills endures. Do not recklessly create conflict or give rise to killing intent. Violate this again, and be executed!"

The rumbling voice echoed among mountains, rivers, and white clouds. Though weak and low, it did not change the stark killing intent within.

Under that voice, Cape Heaven Matron's expression turned ugly.

Luo Chen, however, began to smile.

When he had seen the other party just now, he had been fully on guard, already prepared for battle.

Especially that strange attack she had sent out. Its speed was so swift, and the power contained within so great, that one look was enough to know it was not something a Golden Core cultivator could unleash.

If they truly fought, he would inevitably fall into a bitter battle.

He had not expected this realm to possess such a restriction.

That made sense. The Pill Realm was an alchemy inheritance. All competition lay in alchemy assessments. How could it allow crude fighting and killing?

He smiled faintly. "Cape Heaven Matron? Before meeting me, you left bamboo slips to mislead others. At our first meeting, you immediately attacked. Tsk, tsk. Is this the bearing of a demon emperor?"

Cape Heaven Matron looked at Luo Chen without sorrow or joy, and no thoughts could be read from her face.

In the next moment,

An explosive cry rang out. Luo Chen's figure suddenly appeared beside her, wild wind surging as his words mocked her.

"A beast is a beast. Even after taking human form, you still cannot change the style of eating raw flesh and drinking blood, of savagery and recklessness."

"You!"

Luo Chen shook his head and strode past her, heading straight for the center of this realm.

Behind him, Cape Heaven Matron clutched her herb basket. Her expression, which had shown neither sorrow nor joy, was now livid.

She, Cape Heaven Matron, was one of the foremost existences among the demons of the Northern Sea. Even if her realm did not count as top tier, any fourth-tier demon emperor had to show her a measure of respect.

One could even say that in private, some human Sage Sages maintained good relations with her.

All of this was because she excelled at alchemy.

She was an undisputed grandmaster-level figure in the Dao of Pills in the Northern Sea.

She did not know how many years it had been since anyone dared to show her such disrespect or speak so wildly.

If they were in the outside world, she would not even need to act personally. With one command, there would be no shortage of people willing to deal with this junior for her.

Unfortunately, inside this Pill Realm, an even stronger power suppressed them...

An even stronger power...

The old woman suddenly let out a cold laugh.

"Human child, so discourteous. Do you truly think that relying on this place's rules can protect you for a lifetime?"

Luo Chen's steps paused.

He did not turn around. His tone was simply incomparably confident.

"I used only three years to catch up to your several years of progress. I imagine that even if there are other trials later, my speed in breaking them should be above yours. I, Luo, will have this inheritance!"

"As for after we leave this place, who kills whom remains unknown."

"Cape Heaven Matron, is it? You should first think about whether you can pass the assessment and leave the inheritance land!"

After speaking, he no longer paused at all and leisurely departed.

That powerful confidence seemed to linger here even after he left.

The old woman's cold laugh had already vanished. She watched Luo Chen's back, uncertain and suspicious, her expression constantly changing.

...

Not long after, in a black-soil dense forest, Luo Chen walked neither fast nor slow, his face full of thought.

"Cape Heaven Matron. So it was this demon!"

He knew this name because he had once seen it by chance in the intelligence library of the Myriad Immortals Assembly.

This demon had an extremely resounding reputation in the Northern Sea cultivation world!

She was a grandmaster alchemist. Though only at early Sage realm, she held lofty status among the demon race.

Not only that,

She was also one of the few demon race existences with indirect contact with major human forces.

It was said that at one human alchemy gathering, she had even participated personally. Even after her identity was later discovered, she still left calmly.

Her influence was so great that it crossed the difference between human and demon.

If Luo Chen encountered such an existence normally, he would certainly handle her carefully.

Even if he did not deliberately curry favor, he would not offend her excessively. How could he have mocked her to the utmost as he had just done?

The reason he acted this way was actually very simple.

With the inheritance before them, they were enemies.

Since the rules of this realm restricted them and prevented them from resorting to force, he would use the sharpness of his tongue to disturb her Dao heart.

As for the effect?

"It should have some effect, right?"

Luo Chen murmured, not entirely certain in his heart.

At the level of a transformation-stage demon emperor, she was no longer one of those birds and beasts with shallow thoughts and little intelligence. She was almost no different from a human Sage Sage.

One could say that even if her schemes were not deep as the sea, she would not be someone whose mind could be stirred by a few words.

But in Luo Chen's view, no matter how good one's temper or how deep one's schemes, being struck in the craft one excelled at most could never be accepted with perfect calm.

Among the people Luo Chen had met before, whether Huang Hezi of Azure Pill Valley or Meng Changshou of the Medicine King Sect, if one said their realms were not high, they would only smile and not care at all. But if one said their alchemy was poor, they would truly become anxious.

Another example was the recently familiar Moya Cave Master. At other times he was fine, but whenever artifact refining and treasure forging were involved, he would show entirely different emotions.

That Cape Heaven Matron was extremely old, yet her realm was not especially profound. From this, one could see that she had placed most of her energy into alchemy.

Luo Chen attacked her through this, so it was impossible for her to be unaffected.

Especially because part of what he said was the truth!

He, Luo Chen, had indeed used only two years to catch up to her footsteps and reach the third trial!

If things continued like this, surpassing Cape Heaven Matron would only be a matter of time.

Luo Chen did not believe that the other party could remain as steady as Mount Tai before such a direct truth.

"If I can disturb her heart, naturally that would be best. Even if only for a moment, it can win me a little time."

Luo Chen murmured. His gaze fell upon the black forest filled with devil qi.

The fruits grown by the spirit trees here were things he had never heard of or seen.

And that sinister, eerie devil qi undoubtedly confirmed his earlier guess.

The assessment of this third trial was very likely related to the devil race.

This time, it would probably be harder than the first two trials.

...

In a world of cold steel and towering giant trees,

The sound of mechanical gears, clacking and turning, echoed everywhere.

If one looked down from high in the sky, one would see countless dead-silent beings flickering indistinctly within that steel city.

This place was the City of Puppets!

Suddenly,

A giant bird soared from the great city, stirring countless winds and clouds.

When it reached an extreme height, its wings spread flat and its body gradually slowed.

Within its spirited eyes, delight filled every inch.

"Ha ha, this inheritance is actually puppet art! It suits this old man perfectly!"

In just two years, Han Zhan had broken through nine trials in succession and conquered the third City of Puppets.

At this moment, the puppet controlled by his Nascent Soul had astonishingly reached the early Sage realm!

This was already the strongest puppet he had been capable of refining when his fleshly body still existed.

"I wonder how Luo Chen is doing over there?"

The strange giant bird, full of mechanical feeling, continuously circled above the puppet capital. Below, one wooden bird after another, all of the same model but different sizes, flew up and began circling around him.

After muttering about Luo Chen for a few lines, Han Zhan drove the giant bird and controlled the flock of birds, flying toward a farther place.

Several days later,

An even more towering steel city came into view.

Han Zhan drove the giant bird, his gaze looking down over the great city.

Unlike the previous capital city filled with countless puppets, this time there were no shadowy forms in the steel city. There was only one towering figure crouched at the very center.

Its entire body was silver-white, and its fur was like blades.

On its forehead, a huge character meaning "king" struck straight into the heart.

The instant Han Zhan saw this thing, his heart could not help trembling.

"Could it be that this time, this old man must refine a puppet capable of defeating this white tiger puppet comparable to a late-stage Sage?"

"How could that possibly be done?"

...

Sabers, spears, swords, halberds; axes, battle-axes, hooks, forks; staves, cudgels, lances, whips, maces, hammers, claws...

This was a world of weapons.

Or rather, it was more like a graveyard for all kinds of weapons.

When a clear wind brushed past, the sound of killing rang out cold and sharp.

If high-level immortal cultivators were here, their hearts and spirits would certainly surge.

Because the weapons inserted in the earth and scattered everywhere were all rare treasures in the outside world.

From low-tier law devices to high-tier magic treasures, and even true artifacts, they could be seen everywhere.

How could that not dazzle the eyes?

Beneath a towering iron mountain,

An elegant middle-aged man was now wearing a sweat-soaked shirt, vigorously swinging the iron hammer before him.

In front of him, an iron sword had already taken on a rough shape.

"If I use an ancient method to refine this sword, perhaps I can complete the assessment and climb another mountain!"

As he hammered the sword body, the middle-aged man's thoughts stirred slightly.

"I wonder how Shaoshang and Xiaolian are doing?"

"Li Canghai did not speak fully or honestly. He only said there was a Spirit-Molding Holy Spring within the inheritance land, but did not say there were more than a dozen different inheritances."

"The two paths Shaoshang and Xiaolian chose separately... do they contain danger?"

Just as his mind was full of concerns, his heart suddenly moved.

He stopped moving and looked far in the direction he had come from.

Someone was climbing Weapon Mountain!

"It is that Moya brat!"

Relying on the method he had left earlier, the middle-aged man instantly knew who the pursuer behind him was.

Thinking of that person, the man's expression darkened.

"That man's name contains Yun, cloud, and clashes with my own name, Gu Feiyun. Now that we contest the same inheritance, we are even more directly opposed."

"Judging by the Weapon Mountain he is climbing, he has probably refined the specified lower-grade magic treasure required by the question. I cannot delay any longer."

"Only..."

He looked toward the iron mountain behind him, as though seeing through the mountains to the even taller Weapon Mountains farther back.

"This is only the third trial, and it already involves an upper-grade magic treasure. If I want to obtain the true inheritance, will I have to refine a true artifact, or even a spirit treasure?"

Thinking of the difficult road ahead, Gu Feiyun felt increasingly uneasy.

...

"The devil race has three thousand two hundred clans. Seven or eight tenths of them refine the body. What they seek is the body of the ancient devil."

"Ancient devils uphold heaven and earth, their feet treading mountains and rivers... The foundation of all things begins from the smallest..."

Inside the study, Luo Chen closed his eyes and skillfully researched the examination question.

On the first reading alone, he discovered with surprise and delight that this trial might be the simplest of the three for him.

The pills he needed to refine were astonishingly body-tempering auxiliary pills!

Moreover, their grade was not high. They were merely rather complicated.

They extended separately from the three directions of skin and flesh, bones, and blood.

Although, as with the first two trials, there were still no concrete formulas, Luo Chen had tempered his body for more than a hundred years without cease and was incomparably familiar with this Dao.

His current Archaic Tier Four body was enough to let him look down from a higher vantage and reverse-derive low-tier body-tempering pills.

The only thing he needed to consider was probably that these pills had to be adapted to devil bodies.

Luo Chen did not understand the devil race, and had even less seen what devil bodies were like.

But he believed the answers must be hidden in the bamboo slips in this study!

Next, he only needed to read all the bamboo slips here once, gather medicinal materials, deduce the formulas, and refine the pills.

"This time, I have finally run into something simple!"

Luo Chen murmured.

But immediately, he put away his smile again.

He remembered very clearly that when he first arrived here, Cape Heaven Matron had been gathering medicinal herbs.

That meant the other party was very likely already ahead of him.

"I cannot delay."

Without hesitation, Luo Chen stepped among the bookshelves and immersed himself in an ocean of knowledge.

Not long after,

The old woman in a bright floral dress returned to the study. Looking at the young man holding a bamboo slip and greedily drawing in alchemy knowledge as if hungry and thirsty, resentment appeared on her face.

"This junior is arrogant. Does he truly think the examination question laid out by an ancient cultivator is so simple it can be passed at will?"

"Though I do not know how you luckily passed the first two trials, this third trial will surely leave you unable to advance or retreat, unable to find the thread."

With complicated thoughts, she snorted coldly and turned to leave.

She had already gathered the medicinal materials for the new formula she had derived. This time, she would definitely succeed!

At that time, she would certainly pass this trial one step ahead of him!

Not only would she have the inheritance, after leaving this place, she would also capture this fellow called Devil Lord Azure Yang and make him unable to live or die, repaying his earlier offense against her!

*(End of Chapter)*
