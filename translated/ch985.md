# Chapter 985: White-Robed Vajra, Mahakala

The little novice monk smiled faintly. "Amitabha. It is this humble monk."

When the other party frankly declared his origins, Luo Chen couldn't help but exchange a glance with Azure Frost. In each other's eyes lay a hint of gravity.

If the two of them were at their peak, even if Xuan Du himself stood before them, they would not fear him in the slightest.

But in their current state, if some strong practitioners below the Great Cultivator level came, they might still be able to handle them. If it were a Great Cultivator, especially someone like Xuan Du, a renowned top-tier Great Cultivator, if a real fight broke out, casualties would be unavoidable.

Azure Frost remained silent, ceding the initiative in communication to Luo Chen.

She was the most famous top-tier Demon Emperor in the Mountains and Seas Realm. Having especially revealed her true form before human cultivators, the more she spoke, the more mistakes she might make.

For Luo Chen, there was no such concern.

The reputation of an Alchemy Sect could earn him a seat as an honored guest wherever he went.

Seeing Xuan Du's smiling, amiable demeanor at this moment, Luo Chen's heart stirred slightly.

"Master, do you also intend to stop us?"

The little novice monk shook his head. "Stopping is not the word. I merely wish to invite the two of you to my Pure Glass Pure Land as guests."

"Guests?" Luo Chen's mouth twitched. "In name, guests, but in reality, house arrest!"

The little novice monk quickly waved his hand. "Fellow Daoist Luo, you worry too much. This humble monk sincerely invites you, wishing to share tea and discuss the Dao with you two. If you are unwilling, I will not force the matter. However, this Vairocana Golden Body will not be lent to you two friends for use."

Luo Chen frowned and instinctively looked at Azure Frost.

Azure Frost transmitted her voice: "The Western Desert teleportation formation was built in secrecy and was not established on a spirit vein. To activate it, one must borrow the Buddhist power accumulated in this Peacock Great Bright King Golden Body. Logically speaking, such a method is very secretive. Xuan Du should not have discovered it."

Luo Chen's heart sank. He had previously discussed the route back to the Eastern Wilderness with Azure Frost.

The most convenient was naturally that super teleportation formation connecting to Green Parasol Mountain, which could reach it in a single day!

If they had to rely on their own legs to traverse east and west, the time consumed would not be a matter of one or two years.

Especially since, during this process, they would have to pass through the Central Continent, which was most tense after the great war. That would be even more prone to unexpected complications.

He and Azure Frost were in very poor condition and urgently needed to find a spirit land to recuperate.

Moreover, the most important thing was that nearly ten years had passed since he left the Eastern Wilderness. Luo Lingxi was getting older. If they delayed any further, it might truly affect her future cultivation.

No more delays!

The best solution at the moment was undoubtedly to capture the little novice monk and seize control of the teleportation formation here.

But since the other party was waiting for them at leisure, it meant the teleportation formation was also under his control.

If, during the teleportation process, Xuan Du's true body personally intervened to affect the formation, the consequences would be unimaginable.

He squinted at the little novice monk. "Merely being guests?"

The little novice monk's realm was not high; he was clearly not Xuan Du Dharma Master's true body, merely a body possessed by a strand of his divine sense.

Yet under Luo Chen's soul pressure, he remained neither humble nor arrogant, not greatly affected.

His expression turned solemn. "A monk does not speak falsely. This humble monk actually wishes to discuss a cooperation with the two benefactors."

"Cooperation?"

"Yes. The specific content of the cooperation cannot be detailed here. But the conditions I offer will absolutely be beneficial and harmless to you." Having said this, the little novice monk clasped his hands in a bow. "After the matter is concluded, you two may go freely. This humble monk will not disturb you further."

Luo Chen pondered for a moment, then finally nodded.

"Lead the way! I also wish to see what that legendary Pure Glass Pure Land truly looks like."

The little novice monk revealed a sincere smile and walked out of the Buddha Mother Temple.

Behind him, Luo Chen and Azure Frost exchanged a glance. They did not speak, but a certain tacit understanding had already formed.

...

The Western Desert was a name distinct from the five continents of the Mountains and Seas Realm.

On this land, the true appellation was Buddha Land, or Oases.

Luo Chen and Azure Frost had emerged from the extreme eastern Hanhai Desert, adjacent to the Monan Royal Court.

Legend had it that the Monan Royal Court was a vast country where Buddhist cultivators and mortals coexisted. It was dotted with large and small kingdoms, enshrining countless golden bodies of Buddhas and Bodhisattvas, with countless thousand-year-old ancient temples and great monasteries.

The demon beast army that harassed the oasis Buddha Land flowed out from the extreme western lands.

As for the Pure Glass Pure Land that Luo Chen wished to see, in this Western Desert region, it was more akin to a legend.

It had no fixed mountain gate, no disciples traveling outside. There was only the legend of one monk and one temple.

Some even said that the Pure Glass Pure Land was a hidden grotto-heaven, or a wandering Buddhist temple.

Regarding these various rumors, Luo Chen soon obtained the answer.

Staring at the spatial fluctuations faintly visible in the void, he exclaimed in surprise, "A spatial spirit treasure?"

The little novice monk was preparing something. Hearing Luo Chen identify the essence of the Pure Glass Pure Land in one breath, he couldn't help but regard Luo Chen with greater respect.

"A mustard seed contains Sumeru; billions merge into an inch. The naked eye cannot discern it, but the Dharma realm shines with boundless light. Benefactor, your pair of eyes are truly extraordinary!"

Luo Chen smiled, his gaze involuntarily falling on Azure Frost.

Azure Frost had painstakingly cultivated the true meaning of spatial laws. If she had such a spirit treasure by her side, she could permanently reside in the chaotic void currents. In the world today, no one would truly be able to threaten her.

Even a Divine Transformation Great Power!

Unexpectedly, Azure Frost was not particularly moved. She was merely a little curious.

Seeing her reaction, Luo Chen fell into thought.

Ah, yes!

Azure Frost was in the process of refining the Accumulated Void Flower. If she truly succeeded and opened up an internal space within her body, she herself could traverse the chaotic void currents. Why would she need to rely on so-called spirit treasures?

The little novice monk's preparations had reached the final stage, about to open the Pure Glass Pure Land.

With some leisure, he spoke unhurriedly, "The two benefactors may not know this, but the Pure Glass Pure Land is the first and only spirit treasure of the Western Desert Buddhist sect. Compared to the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard of the Central Continent, it is not inferior in the slightest."

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow. "Can this treasure also suppress earth qi and gather spiritual energy?"

"More than that!" The little novice monk smiled, his words full of confidence.

Luo Chen, however, was somewhat skeptical. "If it were truly that powerful, how could it be solely in your hands, Fellow Daoist? Could the Hanging Temple, the true master of the Western Desert, watch such a heavy treasure fall into outside hands?"

The little novice monk did not answer. He merely cast the final hand seal towards the sky.

The night sky hung high, originally without stars or moon.

But after this hand seal was cast, the horizon trembled, and dark clouds retreated.

A gentle moonlight poured down, illuminating the depths of the void.

Gradually, a glass Buddhist temple nestled in a bamboo forest with a pagoda grove revealed the tip of the iceberg.

"Two benefactors, please!"

Luo Chen's steps paused, and he halted.

Now that the moment had arrived, he was the one hesitating instead.

When it came to the Dao of space, it was profoundly mysterious. Countless cultivators in the world grasped at most a superficial understanding.

But within the Buddhist sect, this was not the case.

The saying "a mustard seed contains Sumeru, Sumeru contains a mustard seed" had been passed down for a long time. There were not a few strong practitioners within the Buddhist sect who were skilled in spatial restrictions. For example, the purple-gold alms bowl in the hands of Cihai of Mount Wutai was a fairly good spatial dharma treasure.

He had not expected the Pure Glass Pure Land to actually be a spatial spirit treasure.

Once they entered it, getting out again would probably not be so simple.

"Let's go in!"

Unexpectedly, Azure Frost showed no fear at all.

She transmitted her voice: "The restriction that Xuan Du's possessed little novice monk just cast was a stabilization technique. With this technique in place, the Pure Glass Pure Land is in a state of being open to the outside. If I wish to leave, I can break free in an instant."

With this assurance, Luo Chen finally felt at ease.

He lifted his foot and stepped across the shimmering void. In a daze, heaven and earth had turned upside down.

Looking up, he saw a bright moon hanging high in the night sky.

Truly, the void contained a small realm; a mustard seed contained Sumeru!

And unlike unstable small worlds like the Yin-Yang Realm, the space of this Pure Glass Pure Land was exceptionally stable, no different from the outside world.

With Luo Chen's means, it would probably be difficult to tear open a void rift.

Azure Frost paused at the entrance for a moment, confirming that her perception was correct, then nodded to Luo Chen.

That little novice monk did not come in.

After seeing the two enter, his whole body trembled. Then, a look of confusion appeared in his eyes. He bowed once towards the Buddhist temple shrouded in moonlight, then left on his own.

Under the moonlight, the green bamboo sea swayed with the wind, and the white pagoda grove stood solemn and dignified.

Such a strange phenomenon was clearly visible even a hundred li away.

Many people had even seen Luo Chen and Azure Frost walk in.

In an instant, news spread rapidly: the Pure Glass Pure Land had appeared in the world, and Alchemy Sect Luo Chen and Demon Emperor Azure Frost had entered it.

...

From within the Buddhist temple, which seemed to be cast from glass, came the sound of a wooden fish, calming the heart and mind.

Two figures crossed the threshold and walked in side by side.

A white-robed monk had his back to them. Two meditation cushions were placed in the great hall, as if waiting for a long time.

"Please!"

An exceptionally youthful voice came.

Luo Chen and Azure Frost exchanged a glance. Without fear, they generously sat down on the meditation cushions.

That white-robed monk continued to knock on his wooden fish, chanting sutras in a low voice.

After a long while, he finally stopped his daily practice and slowly turned around.

Just one look!

Their eyes met. Within Luo Chen's pupils, the phenomenon of Mirror Flower Water Moon abruptly bloomed.

The young monk did not avoid it. He still sat cross-legged within, allowing the blooming flowers and circling heavenly maidens.

The boundless illusions sought to make him sink, but he held fast to his mind, unmoved.

Outside.

Azure Frost threw a punch that shattered the void. Her pure white wrist seemed capable of tearing everything apart.

The young monk smiled faintly. One finger formed a flower-picking gesture, gently flicking out.

*Dong!*

A dull sound rang out. The young monk's body trembled, as if a great bell had been struck.

Azure Frost slowly withdrew her right hand, frowning slightly.

"A Great Vajra physique, even surpassing that bald donkey Ci'en of Mount Wutai!"

On the other side, Luo Chen closed his eyes with some fatigue, then slowly opened them, looking at the white-robed monk before him with shock and uncertainty.

"You!"

The young monk rubbed his brow, praising, "The Alchemy Sect's abilities far exceed the world's imagination. If this humble monk had not condensed his primordial spirit in advance, under that soul-suppression as vast as an ocean and as deep as a hell, I would have sunk into boundless illusions in an instant."

Luo Chen twitched the corner of his mouth. "Dharma Master Xuan Du flatters me!"

Azure Frost was somewhat surprised by this result.

She had experienced the depth of Luo Chen's soul foundation herself.

If not for the protection of the Nether Soul Pearl, even she would not be Luo Chen's match.

It could be said that in the current world, no Sage's soul foundation could rival Luo Chen's.

In fact, in Azure Frost's view, Luo Chen's soul was so vast that it was infinitely close to that of a Divine Transformation Great Power.

Yet, under these circumstances, Dharma Master Xuan Du could actually withstand his soul art!

Luo Chen, however, exhaled a turbid breath, knowing that their tacit plan had come to nothing.

They had never intended to discuss any cooperation or exchange any conditions.

From the very beginning, their plan was to enter the Pure Glass Pure Land, launch a sudden attack, join forces to subdue Dharma Master Xuan Du, then seize control of the teleportation formation at the Buddha Mother Temple and leave the Western Desert directly.

They had not expected that this one was also a master of both dharma and body cultivation!

He had withstood their combined strike in the very first moment.

What's more, the other party had already condensed his primordial spirit, only one step away from Divine Transformation!

If it came to soul foundation, Luo Chen was far stronger than him.

But Dharma Master Xuan Du had already condensed his primordial spirit. His soul was so condensed that it was enough to remain unaffected by Mirror Flower Water Moon.

And that Great Vajra physique...

Luo Chen shook his head at Azure Frost, abandoning their initial plan.

If the two of them were each at their peak, they would both have the strength to fight Xuan Du. The outcome would depend on their respective combat abilities.

But at this moment, it was not suitable for a life-and-death struggle.

"Two benefactors, can we talk properly now?" The white monk's robe slowly settled. Dharma Master Xuan Du did not seem to mind their earlier sudden attack.

Luo Chen rubbed his brow. "Dharma Master is quite persistent. I wonder what matter is so important that you would go so far as to threaten us two with the teleportation formation."

Dharma Master Xuan Du seemed not to hear the sarcasm in Luo Chen's words. Instead, he said solemnly, "It is not persistence. The truth is, that matter concerns all living beings under heaven. I need your assistance."

"All living beings under heaven?" Luo Chen was startled. After so many years, this was the first time he had heard this term. He couldn't help but let out a light laugh. "Dharma Master is compassionate. But what do all living beings under heaven have to do with me?"

Xuan Du said calmly, "Benefactor, you are also a part of all living beings. When a great calamity befalls, you may not be able to remain untouched."

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow. "Alarmist talk!"

Xuan Du pointed at the ground beneath his feet. "Earlier, Benefactor asked why such a heavy treasure as the Pure Glass Pure Land is under my control, yet the Hanging Temple is not tempted. The answer is actually very simple. This treasure was stolen by this humble monk from the Hanging Temple, to prevent them from wielding a sharp blade and giving rise to murderous intent!"

Luo Chen frowned, putting away his earlier dismissive attitude.

Ever since he had killed Huang Man and Shen Yuan, he had been very confident that, with Divine Transformation not appearing, he could claim invincibility under heaven.

His only hypothetical opponent was Azure Frost, who possessed spatial abilities.

But when it came to holy lands, he never dared to be careless.

The Hanging Temple was the sole overlord of the Western Desert, a Divine Transformation holy land!

"For a great monk like you to resort to theft, it seems the matter is indeed of great importance!" Luo Chen said.

Xuan Du nodded and asked, "Benefactor, do you know of that Arhat of the Hanging Temple?"

Arhat?

Luo Chen pressed his lips together.

The cultivation system of the Western Desert was slightly different from the other four continents.

Apart from the common Qi Refinement and Initiate stages, there was another mature system of the Buddhist sect.

Novice monks and bhikshus, Dharma Masters and Vajras, and even Arhats!

The reason he had wanted to join forces with Azure Frost to deal with Xuan Du earlier was also because the other party could attach his divine sense to a little novice monk without harming or affecting that novice.

From the very beginning, Luo Chen had never underestimated Xuan Du.

It was just that the other party's subsequent performance was too astonishing, somewhat exceeding his expectations.

As for the Arhat the other party now mentioned, he also knew a little about him.

The current master of the Hanging Temple, the legendary Mahakala!

Eight hundred years ago, the head of the Prajna Hall, he once subdued the desolate beast Golden-Winged Great Peng, adding a guardian spirit venerable to the Hanging Temple.

After advancing to the Divine Transformation realm, he naturally took over the position of abbot, presiding over the Hanging Temple.

It was said that later, the Hanging Temple closed its mountain gates and ignored worldly affairs, also under his influence.

Mahakala was not famous in the outside world. Apart from strong practitioners who were more interested in the Buddhist Dao, many people did not know that such a true Buddha existed in the Western Desert.

If Luo Chen had not been interested in the monk Huiping of the Extreme Heaven Domain and had briefly looked up relevant information back then, he probably would not have known much about this person either.

Now, hearing this great power mentioned by Dharma Master Xuan Du, Luo Chen couldn't help but become curious.

"What happened to him?"

Xuan Du's expression was mournful. He said with deep pain, "He... has fallen into corruption!"
