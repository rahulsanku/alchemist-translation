# Chapter 1130: Blood Qi Transformation, Instantaneous Ming Jin

Before the dam, Luo Chen stood with his hands behind his back, silently watching Steward Yan and his group descend the mountain.

Behind him came soft footsteps, along with a woman's hesitant voice.

"Are you leaving?"

"Mm."

Luo Chen acknowledged.

He had already accepted Steward Yan's invitation, but there was no rush to depart for now. He had to wait until the Green Mountain Tobacco Workshop's affairs were finished, and then travel together with Steward Yan when he returned at year's end to that Lost Immortal City.

Roughly calculating, it would be at least another half year.

Behind him, Jing Lan opened her mouth, but in the end could only let out a soft sigh.

She knew this was inevitable, destined from the moment he had inquired about the outside world and even about Green Mountain Town.

But what she hadn't expected was that this day would come so quickly.

After that, she would have to...

"You'll come with me then."

The calm voice carried an unquestionable tone.

Jing Lan was startled, then a look of pleasant surprise bloomed on her face.

She did not refuse, but replied firmly, "Alright!"

"Take inventory of what Steward Yan sent over. Pick out what's useful, and if there's extra, you can share some with the villagers."

"Alright!"

Luo Chen no longer watched Steward Yan and the others depart. He turned back, smiled at Jing Lan, and then entered the house.

...

"Qi-Dissolving Art?"

Luo Chen opened the booklet and studied it carefully.

After a long while, a thoughtful expression appeared on his face.

Rather than calling it a cultivation technique, it was more accurate to call it a secret art.

Its function was not for combat, nor even for cultivation—instead, it was a secret art that consumed magical power.

By performing this art, one could reverse the process of refining essence into qi, achieving the refinement of qi into essence.

In layman's terms, it meant slowly dissolving one's magical power, transforming it into the purest blood qi. During this process, a significant portion would be lost—a result that any cultivator would find absolutely unacceptable.

After all, who would be willing to sacrifice centuries or even millennia of hard-earned cultivation, converting it to the physical body?

But what about in a place like the Great Destruction Realm?

When cultivation can no longer be sustained, magical power cannot be released externally, and even lifespan continuously decreases as magical power gradually fades and one's realm plummets—what meaning does that vast magical power hold?

Transforming it into surging blood qi, even if a portion is lost, would ultimately benefit one's remaining years.

If one had a resolute will, they might even use this to embark anew on the path of body refinement!

"No wonder Steward Yan said this art might be useful to me. In truth, it would be immensely useful to any cultivator stranded in the Great Destruction Realm. Perhaps this art was created by some senior forced into desperation under such circumstances."

Luo Chen let out a faint sigh. In his mind's eye, he could almost see the senior who created this art, in the helpless despair of being stranded in the Great Destruction Realm, completely dispersing their magical power and abandoning the fruits of a lifetime of cultivation.

However, this art was indeed of no use to him.

Because he had barely any residual magical power left on him.

All he could rely on was this terrifyingly formidable physical body, and the source blood he had accumulated drop by drop.

Even if those Ascendants and even Void Refining Lords reversed their magical power and dissolved their cultivation, they would still be far inferior to him on this point alone.

But Luo Chen felt no pride. Instead, there was a wordless sense of crisis in his heart.

The reason he had once been able to roam the Mountains and Seas Realm, looking down on his peers, was his comprehensive strength that had almost no weaknesses.

Magical power, divine sense, source power—these formed a triple-core drive, complemented by an indestructible physical body, numerous divine abilities, techniques, spirit treasures, and artifacts.

But now, he had lost too much. His capabilities were less than one percent of his peak.

For Luo Chen, who had always been accustomed to possessing great power, how could this not breed a sense of crisis?

Especially in this bizarre Great Destruction Realm, he urgently needed powerful strength to protect himself.

Recovering from his physical injuries was not something that could be accomplished overnight. It all depended on the source power derived from source blood to heal him bit by bit—but that was too slow!

Moreover, while consuming source power, Luo Chen was also depleting his foundational origin.

"I can't go on like this any longer!"

Luo Chen's expression was resolute, and a different idea gradually formed in his heart.

His use of source power had always been crude. Perhaps this was because there was no precedent for this power to follow, or perhaps because he had previously focused his efforts on refining magical power.

To avoid excessive depletion of source blood, Luo Chen had to use more refined methods to utilize it, and later restore and strengthen it, even to the point of re-condensing true spirit blood!

In Luo Chen's view, more refined methods were none other than the orthodox path of body refinement walked by predecessors.

Being able to transform pure blood qi into jin force and astral qi—how could that not be called refined?

"Before I leave, I still have some time. I can try to switch to orthodox body refinement."

Luo Chen thought this way.

As for how to make the switch, it was actually not difficult for him.

His mind held records of many wondrous techniques and marvelous methods. For getting started, the best choice would undoubtedly be the *Divine Astral Combat Qi* that Wang Yuan had systematically summarized and written.

This book was the culmination of Wang Yuan's lifelong martial arts.

Originally, the orthodox path of body refinement in the Mountains and Seas Realm was nearly severed, with only the Western Desert Buddhist lineage preserving some inheritance.

Relying on his own talent, the full support of the Luo Tian Sect, and Luo Chen's occasional assistance, Wang Yuan had drawn from and absorbed various sources bit by bit, eventually creating the *Divine Astral* book, and had himself cultivated to the fourth stage of body refinement—the Leakless Golden Body realm.

Within it, the foundational methods of body refinement, along with various taboos and personal insights, were described in great detail.

Using this as a starting point would be the best choice for Luo Chen!

As for how to continue after reaching the fourth stage, Luo Chen also had a rough plan in mind.

But for now, he would first take the first step.

Luo Chen closed his eyes and pondered for a moment. His decision was clear!

Within his formidable physical body, a single drop of supremely pure source blood suddenly shattered, then transformed into surging blood qi, washing through his entire body.

In terms of orthodox body refinement, Luo Chen was actually only at the first stage—Vajra Realm perfected—and had just barely entered the second stage—Blood River Realm.

What he was doing now was using blood qi to wash through his entire body, allowing every part of this body to be infused with blood qi.

This was called Blood Pervades the Long River!

In the quiet house, only a muffled grunt was heard.

When Luo Chen opened his eyes, his originally thin frame had bulged somewhat, giving him a robust appearance.

The first step was complete!

Next came the gradual transformation of the surging blood qi into refined and subtle jin force.

This step required enlightenment.

Enlightenment of the changes in blood qi, mastery of the four types of jin force: Ming, An, Hua, and Dan.

In the past, Luo Chen had found it troublesome and had given up at this step, shifting his focus to qi refinement cultivation. But now, no matter how troublesome it was, he would take it step by step.

"Ming Jin..."

Murmuring to himself, Luo Chen tried to compress and fuse a surge of vigorous blood qi. Gradually, a more refined yet more powerful force was born.

Was this what they called Ming Jin?

Luo Chen looked at the jin force swirling around his fingertip, a look of realization dawning on his face.

So, it wasn't that difficult after all!

At the same time, the long-dormant attribute panel flickered with a faint light.

(End of this chapter)
