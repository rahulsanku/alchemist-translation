# Chapter 693: The True Lord's Inheritance

For mortals, those with spirit roots counted as spirit bodies.

Even if they did not cultivate immortal Dao methods and only practiced martial arts and tempered the body in daily life, so long as they did not suffer serious injuries or grave illness, they often lived longer than others. Living eighty or ninety years, or even past a hundred, was normal.

But in the cultivation world, spirit roots were only the most basic element of cultivation.

They could not be counted as any kind of spirit body.

Luo Chen had summarized it years ago. Cultivator aptitude was divided into three broad categories.

They were spirit-root type, divine-soul type, and physique type.

There was no need to say much about spirit-root type. The number of roots decided cultivation speed, at most further distinguishing heavenly spirit roots, variant spirit roots, and the like.

Divine-soul type referred to comprehension, temperament, innate divine soul foundation, and even the saying of awakened wisdom from reincarnation.

Physique type was reflected in bodily functions: meridians, apertures, physical divine strength, unusual blood, and so on. For instance, Li Yingzhang's Nine-Bend Returning-Circle constitution meant that his meridians were innately different from ordinary people. Another example would be those born with divine strength, whose muscle structure differed from others.

However, strictly speaking, these three divisions were not actually so clearly separated.

The three often affected and changed one another during infancy and youth.

Especially the interaction between spirit roots and physique was extremely common.

The so-called Innate Geng Metal Sword Body was because a metal spirit root possessed too high an affinity with heaven and earth, indirectly affecting the person's body through interaction with heaven and earth. It manifested as extremely high affinity and control toward metal-attribute objects. Such people possessed extraordinary talent whether practicing saber or sword. It was merely because Innate Geng Metal Saber Body sounded less elegant than Sword Body that this name was used.

Other examples included the Nine Yang Divine Body, Ginseng-Wood Spirit Body, and so on. These could all be counted as a certain attribute spirit root being overly prominent in a cultivator, thereby changing the body and gradually affecting external objects.

Among these many spirit-body Dao physiques, the Five-Spirit Dao Body was absolutely a famous one.

Those with this physique, no matter which one of the five elements' cultivation methods they practiced, were comparable to heavenly spirit root cultivators with a single root.

This point alone meant the Five-Spirit Dao Body was not restricted by cultivation method.

Not only that, when such people advanced in realm and switched to any primary cultivation method, they would not be affected. It was extraordinarily convenient.

One could even say that even if they cultivated all five attributes at once, their cultivation speed would still far exceed ordinary five-spirit-root people.

Luo Chen had seen information about this physique in an ancient book from the Myriad Immortals Assembly.

It not only mentioned various descriptions of this physique, but also some methods predecessors had used to develop and apply it.

There was one point Luo Chen remembered very clearly.

That was [simultaneous cultivation of all methods]!

The Five-Spirit Dao Body could ignore the conflict among the five elements' spiritual qi and cultivate several methods at the same time.

As long as one was not afraid of cultivation speed being slowed, one could multiply one's own mana.

Moreover, when facing enemies in battle, one did not need to consider the mutual restraint of the five elements. Instead, one could adapt to the situation and counter enemies with countering methods.

Generally speaking, those who possessed the Five-Spirit Dao Body were all good seedlings that great sects carefully protected, sparing no resources in cultivating them.

Under massive resources, the so-called slowing of cultivation speed became nonsense.

Such existences not only cultivated extremely quickly, but often possessed combat ability higher than same-tier cultivators.

Luo Chen sat quietly in the spirit spring. With a thought, the mana transformation in his body became even smoother than before.

Even between water and fire, there was almost no conflict.

Clearly, this was also one of the miraculous uses of the Five-Spirit Dao Body.

And if he meditated to cultivate, its speed was even above the false heavenly spirit root speed transformed by the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture.

This point alone made Luo Chen's heart surge.

"The appearance of my Five-Spirit Dao Body should be the result of the mutual influence between my five-element spirit roots and Archaic Tier Four body, using that special energy in this spirit spring, ultimately fusing into one."

"The five-element spirit roots and archaic-beast body are the basic conditions. The most important thing is that energy!"

Looking at the thin, pale-white spring water in his hand, Luo Chen seemed thoughtful.

Only, right now was not the time to study his own physique, nor the time to study this spring water.

Splash...

Luo Chen rose to his full height and walked step by step out of the pond, which was only two zhang across.

When he reached the bank, he paused.

He looked back at the pale-white spring water, thoughtful.

"If I were at Sage realm, I should be able to absorb it completely."

"A pity..."

"But wasting it like this would be even more of a pity."

Mana patted the spirit beast bags at his waist, and two spirit beast bags emitted light.

Then Black King and Heavenjade appeared before him.

Their enormous bodies made this dim room rather crowded.

"Shrink a bit!"

Black King lazily shrank his body, but Luo Chen still felt it was not enough and made him shrink again and again.

When he was around one zhang, Black King began complaining without stop.

"Master, that should be enough, right? Little Black is truly cramped."

Luo Chen acknowledged him, then looked at Heavenjade, who had already transformed into human form. "The two of you, go in and soak."

Black King subconsciously looked at the misty pool and asked, "In there?"

Heavenjade frowned. "Together with him?"

Such a great benefit lay before them, yet they were still so coy.

With a wave of his sleeve, Luo Chen directly swept the two demons, entirely unprepared, into the pool.

"How much you can absorb depends on your ability. Be as quick as possible. Do not delay my business."

After speaking, he strode out of the dim side hall.

The two demons remained soaking in the spring water, looking at each other.

But very quickly,

They sensed something was wrong.

A powerful energy was pouring into their bodies. That force seemed to contain immense fortune, remolding their demon bodies.

Intense pain spread from both of them.

Black King was somewhat better, but Heavenjade found it difficult to endure.

Even so, they bitterly gritted their teeth and bore it.

Because they could sense that this energy was beneficial and harmless to them, and perhaps it was the greatest opportunity of their lives.

And this opportunity came from their master's grace!

...

Tap! Tap! Tap!

Crisp footsteps echoed across the palace's white jade floor.

Luo Chen, dressed in white, walked neither fast nor slow. Half his mind remained in his qi sea, and half rested on the surrounding architecture.

Within the qi sea, Luo Chen moved his lifebound Golden Core and placed it once more inside the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

This action made it convenient to cast the All-Encompassing Fire Prison at any time if he encountered enemies.

His movements were somewhat cautious, because that fifth-tier Law-Derivation Pill he had personally refined had taken up residence inside the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

It was best that the two not collide.

After everything was adjusted, Luo Chen's gaze fell on the buildings.

The overall style followed the same lineage as the stone houses outside.

But in the corresponding details, the difference was like heaven and earth.

Even with Luo Chen's knowledge, he could not fully identify the materials used for the palace.

He only knew they were excellent! Precious! Rare!

And these materials were all fused together, as if born naturally.

Suddenly,

Three paths appeared before Luo Chen.

"Three paths again?"

Luo Chen murmured and followed his heart toward the rightmost path.

After only several breaths, he reached the end.

A faint pill fragrance drifted out from within.

"Hm?"

Smelling that complex pill fragrance, Luo Chen could not help pushing open the side hall door.

Within sight stood one empty wooden shelf after another. Looking across, there were layers upon layers, like a forest of trees.

His figure moved, and Luo Chen arrived beside a shelf, taking down a jade bottle.

He pulled out the stopper!

There was nothing!

Nothing, all empty.

The immense surprise in his heart turned into immense disappointment.

This side hall was clearly a place for storing finished pills.

But for some reason, it was empty. All the pills had been taken away, and even the corresponding pill-protecting formations had lost their energy source and no longer activated.

"Was it the cultivators of Yuan Demon Sect?"

Luo Chen regretfully held a high-grade spirit stone and casually squeezed it.

Fine powder fell, showing that the spiritual energy inside had long been exhausted, leaving only an empty shell.

He could imagine that when this place was at its peak, it could store tens of thousands, or even millions, of pills!

It could completely satisfy the needs of a top great force.

If he obtained such a legacy, he would not need to struggle for the rest of his life.

Sitting and eating, even mountains would not become empty!

Especially if there were high-tier pills similar to the Law-Derivation Pill inside, then...

"Perhaps it would not be limited to the Law-Derivation Pill!"

"With Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's talent, even a corner of the iceberg is already astonishing enough. A fifth-tier divine pill might not be his limit. Sixth tier, seventh tier..."

As Luo Chen murmured, his sense of loss could not be concealed.

He withdrew from this side hall and pulled the doors closed.

Returning to the original starting point, he hesitated for a moment, then chose the leftmost path.

In his thinking, the middle path should lead to the main hall, or perhaps the way out.

Before leaving, he could still explore a little.

Step by step, he walked forward. Finally, a side hall with carved beams, painted rafters, and reliefs of strange beasts appeared before him.

Luo Chen took a deep breath and pushed open that ancient door.

Like before, there were wooden shelves here as well.

But the difference was that these shelves were clearly for storing books.

And this time, Luo Chen was not disappointed after all.

Reflected in his pupils, though most places were empty, there were more than a hundred protective light shields shining brightly.

More than a hundred!

He walked before these protective shields, somewhat hesitant.

Because he could sense that these light shields contained extremely terrifying power. With his ability, he probably could not open them.

Especially the rearmost and highest bookshelf, where a blurry black page slowly floated.

That place was the most dangerous!

Just as Luo Chen hesitated, this place seemed to sense something, and all the light shields slowly withdrew.

"Did it sense that I am someone who passed the inheritance?"

The aura of the spirit spring on Luo Chen's body had not yet disappeared.

As he thought, he walked to the nearest silver beast-hide book.

He took it down, and light flashed once.

Then Luo Chen slowly spread open the silver beast hide.

What entered his eyes was a pill-refining method Luo Chen had never seen before.

Its name was Withered Bone Pill Method!

Using beast bone as the guide, fusing it with the alchemist's essence blood, and then using living beings' lives as firewood; if there was fresh resentment, that was naturally best...

"It always feels a little sinister?"

Luo Chen muttered and put this book into his storage ring.

Afterward, he looked toward the next one, a book carved onto an evil ghost statue.

Golden Pole Yaksha Seventh Pill Scroll.

"I know what a yaksha is. A ghostly thing, belonging to yin souls, dwelling in the blue skies and yellow springs, the netherworld mansions. But what is a Golden Pole Yaksha? A kind of devil race?"

Luo Chen recalled the classics from the third trial. It seemed there had indeed been a brief mention of a devil race.

It had some relation to ghost yakshas and was named the Golden Pole Yaksha.

In that case, what was recorded on this statue should be alchemy knowledge unique to that race.

Take it!

With no time to examine it carefully, Luo Chen stored them one by one.

Later, he was like a wind sweeping away clouds, taking everything into his bag.

Only before a thick black-covered classic did he pause for a long while.

Its name was Heaven-Refining Alchemy Canon.

If nothing unexpected happened, this thick classic was probably the Dao of Pills classic personally authored by Devil Lord Heaven-Refining.

In silence, Luo Chen raised his head and looked at the bookshelves in the hall.

"Perhaps this is the true inheritance content!"

"Seventy-two alchemy books covering the three great races of human, demon, and devil, and countless strange minor races. If one could learn them all, becoming an alchemy grandmaster would be easy, and becoming a sacred hand of the Dao of Pills would be no problem. One might even go further and comprehend the law of the Dao of Pills..."

He stepped forward again, his hands moving rapidly. Though the later books were also bizarre, their recorded contents were few.

Because what they recorded were pill formulas, complete or incomplete.

Counting them overall, there were thirty-six pill formulas in total.

Most of these formulas could be directly viewed, with grades from first to fifth, everything included.

The Law-Derivation Pill formula was astonishingly among them!

Only, while he could view the Law-Derivation Pill formula, the other fifth-tier formulas seemed to still have their withdrawn protective shields functioning, blocking his spiritual sense.

What excited Luo Chen most were the final three pill formulas, whose complete appearances likewise could not be opened and viewed.

One stabilized spirit, one filled the void, and their grade descriptions were both sixth tier!

In other words, these were pills only Void Refining True Lords could consume.

As for the final one, Luo Chen's hand trembled simply holding it.

It was a page shrouded entirely in black mist, blocking Luo Chen's spiritual sense, mana contamination, and even focused eyesight.

Only one piece of information abruptly entered his mind.

[Eighth tier: Dao Union]

At this moment, no words could describe Luo Chen's feelings.

He could only carefully store this pill formula into the Ink Ring, hiding it in the deepest place and mixing it with the large number of "garbage" books he had collected.

At this point, Luo Chen softly and carefully withdrew from the side hall.

Before leaving, he thought for a moment, turned back, and bowed.

Having received this inheritance, he should perform the rites of a disciple.

It was a pity the True Lord was not before him. Otherwise, even three kneelings and nine kowtows would be something Luo Chen willingly performed!

"I only do not know the True Lord's specific name. Devil Lord Heaven-Refining is only a Dao title. Otherwise, I would set up a tablet for my benefactor and offer incense to console his spirit in heaven."

Suppressing the excitement in his heart, Luo Chen slowly returned by the original path.

Finally, he stood once more before the three-way fork.

By this moment, his mood had already been forcibly calmed.

It was time to go outside and look.

He strode forward, moving farther and farther... until finally, Luo Chen's steps stopped at the entrance of the main hall.

With one glance, Luo Chen's hard-won calm surged again.

A great black cauldron stood quietly in the center of the hall.

With Luo Chen's knowledge of artifact forging, he could not see the grade of this cauldron at all.

He only knew that the invisible pressure spreading from it was far greater than any person he had ever seen!

Whether Han Zhan and Aoxiao, the two great wanderers Blood and Moon, or even Nether Spring and Azure Frost, under this pressure they would be like ants.

With such might, it was absolutely no ordinary magic treasure.

It had probably long surpassed the rank of true artifact. Only the legendary spirit treasure could be compared to it.

"Could this be the true inheritance Devil Lord Heaven-Refining left in the Dao of Pills lineage?"

Wild joy showed on his face.

His body moved of its own accord.

Luo Chen stepped forward, involuntarily walking toward that black cauldron.

His right hand could not help reaching out, about to touch the great black cauldron.

At this moment, a weak voice faintly emerged from within the cauldron.

"You have come."

*(End of Chapter)*
