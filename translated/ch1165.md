# Chapter 1165: Nine-Tiered Immortal Tower, the Century Mark Approaches

“This is… a teleportation formation?”

Standing on an earthen platform that exuded an ancient, sanguine aura within the Fang clan’s ancestral grounds, Luo Chen spoke with some uncertainty.

“No, it’s more like a crude variant of a translocation formation. What truly enables the teleportation is the large number of Spatial Prism Apertures.”

Fang Zhibai cast a surprised glance at Luo Chen. “I didn’t expect Brother Luo to be well-versed in formations as well?”

Luo Chen replied noncommittally, “I’ve dabbled a little.”

Fang Zhibai chuckled. “Crude is better! Overly intricate formations are easily damaged in the Ruins. This short-range teleportation formation relies mainly on materials for transport, though the passengers will endure considerable pressure during the process.”

Luo Chen nodded, very much agreeing with this assessment.

When he had set up those three grand formations in Red Leaf Valley, he had come to a similar realization. As long as it works, that’s enough; being too exquisite is actually a drawback.

As the two spoke, the preparations for the teleportation formation were complete. A red light blazed up from the earthen platform.

Fang Zhibai extended his hand in a gesture of invitation, and Luo Chen stepped onto the platform.

The next moment, the red light abruptly contracted, and the two figures on the platform had vanished.

When they reappeared, the two found themselves in a narrow room.

“Careful.”

Luo Chen instinctively reached out to steady Fang Zhibai, whose figure was staggering.

Fang Zhibai steadied himself, circulating his astral qi for a moment before the stifling sensation in his chest finally eased.

“Apologies, Brother Luo, for making a fool of myself.”

“It’s nothing!”

Luo Chen brushed it off, already walking out of the narrow stone room with his hands clasped behind his back.

Behind him, a flicker of surprise passed through Fang Zhibai’s eyes.

The pressure this crude, modified teleportation formation exerted on its passengers was unimaginable to ordinary people. Even a Furnace Realm martial artist would be crushed alive by it; only those who had cultivated a Leakless Golden Body could endure it somewhat. Yet even then, the teleportation process was still agonizing.

But Luo Chen seemed completely unaffected. He had clearly broken through later than Fang Zhibai himself!

After a moment’s hesitation, Fang Zhibai followed him out.

Outside the room, no one was on guard.

A profound silence reigned.

Luo Chen stood there quietly, gazing into the distance at a towering pagoda that soared ten thousand zhang into the sky, its spire completely hidden within the clouds!

The pagoda was pale white, its flying eaves and layered ridges densely packed, adorned with carvings of countless rare birds and exotic beasts. From afar, they gave a lifelike impression.

Around the pagoda, rays of light in five colors and ten hues constantly shifted, creating an illusory sense of spatial collapse.

It was as if the pagoda did not stand upon this desolate land, but existed within another, untouchable world.

The whole sight was indescribably awe-inspiring!

“Breathtaking, isn’t it?” Fang Zhibai walked up beside Luo Chen, standing shoulder to shoulder with him, his voice carrying an inexplicable tone of reverence. Even though he had seen it many times before, every encounter with the Nine-Tiered Tower still left him unable to contain himself.

What kind of existence, wielding what kind of divine power, could have refined such an inconceivable treasure!

Perhaps it truly was the legendary Immortals!

Luo Chen took a deep breath, the look of wonder in his eyes lingering for a long time.

This white pagoda, merely standing there, gave him an absolute sense of being unshakable.

No spirit treasure had ever given him such a feeling.

Even the Heaven-Refining Cauldron from the legacy left by the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining, which was hailed as a heaven-reaching spirit treasure, could not compare to even a thousandth or a hundredth of this white pagoda.

What grade of artifact was this?

Was it an immortal artifact used by Immortals, or a Dao artifact that condensed the Dao resonance into one?

Fang Zhibai gave him an answer.

“According to my clan’s ancestor’s speculation, this should be an immortal artifact refined by a powerful Immortal. For ten thousand years, it has stood here. No one can subdue it, no one can shake it, standing adjacent to that most dangerous Gate of Annihilation.”

“Even though my clan’s ancestor has probed it many times, he can only grasp its most superficial functions.”

“But even just those superficial functions have been enough to delineate the nine rings of the Annihilation cultivation zones, giving our generation of martial artists a chance to ascend to a higher level.”

Luo Chen nodded silently.

Perhaps only a treasure of the immortal artifact level could withstand ten thousand years of erosion by spatial vortices without being damaged.

He suddenly asked, “Where is that spatial vortex called the Gate of Annihilation?”

Fang Zhibai pointed to the highest heavens, obscured by thick clouds.

“It should be up there!”

“Should be?”

“Yes, I’ve never seen it myself, only heard about it. Only those who have cultivated to the Human Immortal realm and stepped within the third ring—that is, the upper three tiers of the Nine-Tiered Immortal Tower—can glimpse a corner of the Gate of Annihilation’s true visage.”

“How many such people are there?”

“Very few, just a handful.”

“I recall Brother Fang mentioning that Carefree Realm Master hopes I will break through quickly and come to Carefree City to study the Dao together with them?”

“That is indeed the case, mainly because he was worried Brother Luo would break through too late and miss the next centennial opening of the Nine-Tiered Immortal Tower. However, Brother Luo’s cultivation speed is faster than imagined, so it seems you won’t miss it now. As for my clan’s ancestor?”

Fang Zhibai smiled. “Because the Nine-Tiered Immortal Tower cannot be mastered, even he can only enter and exit within the prescribed time. Now that the century mark has not yet arrived, he is still cultivating inside the tower. If you want to see the ancestor, you’ll have to wait at least another ten-plus years.”

“Ten-plus years, huh?”

Luo Chen murmured, then nodded slightly.

He could afford to wait that long.

As long as that epoch-turning Annihilation Mist did not surge forth immediately.

Thinking of this, Luo Chen made a point to inquire further.

But even Fang Zhibai could not give a specific time.

He merely briefly recounted the Fang clan’s history of existence.

To date, it had been three thousand years!

In those three thousand years, they had not yet been destroyed, meaning they had not encountered that terrifying Annihilation Mist.

Finally.

Fang Zhibai spoke with a hint of worry. “The specific details are only known to the clan ancestor. Some have asked, but he is unwilling to say. Our private estimate is that the next wave of Annihilation Mist will come in as little as a hundred years, or as long as a thousand years. By then, whether it is the ten-thousand-year Annihilation Mist or the once-per-epoch Great Annihilation Mist, none of us will be able to survive it.”

Hearing this intelligence, Luo Chen’s mood could not help but grow heavy.

He vaguely understood why Carefree Realm Master did not reveal these things to the world, not even to his own descendants.

In the face of such world-destroying power, it was better for mortals to live out their lives carefree and without worry than to know the truth and live in constant fear.

But for high-level martial artists like himself, not knowing the exact time of their doom was the greatest pressure and torment.

“Let’s go back!” Luo Chen said.

Fang Zhibai was taken aback. “Just a distant look like this?”

“That’s enough.”

Luo Chen turned and entered the stone room.

…

When he left the Fang clan’s ancestral grounds, Luo Chen also inquired about one more thing.

That was the stone box that had previously held the immortal essence stones—the Void Sprout Crystal.

Regarding this item, Fang Zhibai was also very generous in revealing its origin.

It was produced from the Gate of Annihilation, occasionally appearing within the Nine-Tiered Tower.

In fact, immortal essence stones also came from there!

And it wasn’t just Void Sprout Crystals and immortal essence stones. According to Fang Zhibai, every so often, the Gate of Annihilation would sweep in special treasures from other worlds.

If Luo Chen wanted to obtain more Void Sprout Crystals, he would either have to enter the Nine-Tiered Immortal Tower himself to search, or trade with those martial artists who had long been cultivating within the nine rings of Annihilation.

After returning.

Luo Chen asked Xiangfei about the specifics.

“I’ve heard there is such a thing. Some martial artists occasionally harvest all sorts of strange items within the nine rings of Annihilation. Unfortunately, this humble woman spent too short a time there and never had such a fortuitous encounter.”

“Tell me about the cultivation conditions inside the tower.”

“The Nine-Tiered Immortal Tower has stood since time immemorial, its internal space vast and boundless. Once inside, there are almost no stable footholds.”

“It sounds like the Nine-Tiered Immortal Tower is an immortal artifact imbued with the power of spatial laws.” Luo Chen mused, then asked, “Since there are no stable footholds, how do you all cultivate inside?”

Xiangfei answered without hesitation. “Although there are no stable footholds, the place is filled with scattered, drifting meteorites. We martial artists rely on these meteorites to stay. The larger and better the meteorite, the safer it is. If one wishes to remain there long-term, it’s best to have the most solid meteorite as a foothold. In fact, to compete for such cultivation stones, conflicts occasionally break out within the tower.”

Luo Chen pursed his lips, understanding the situation well.

Back in the Mountains and Seas Realm, whether it was residences in market towns or spirit mountain cave dwellings in immortal cities, the better ones were always the most sought after.

Back then, in Tianlan Immortal City, he had expended great effort to secure one of the only three third-tier spirit vein cave dwellings, using it to break through to the Adept stage.

After a moment’s thought, Luo Chen asked again.

“The tower is divided into nine tiers, and the areas filled with chaotic energy are divided into nine rings. How do you all ascend to higher tower tiers and enter zones with richer chaotic energy?”

Xiangfei recalled her brief cultivation career within the nine rings of Annihilation and answered with some uncertainty:

“The source of the chaotic energy is at the very top of the Nine-Tiered Immortal Tower, seemingly drawn in from that Gate of Annihilation. It then descends layer by layer, diminishing layer by layer, with the central passage of the immortal tower as its core, spreading outward in a ring-like pattern. That is how the name ‘nine rings of Annihilation’ came about. The lower the tier, the thinner the chaotic energy, suitable for the weak to cultivate. The higher the tier, the more turbulent the chaotic energy. Unless one is sufficiently confident, it is difficult for ordinary experts to climb the tower and break through to higher ring-shaped cultivation zones.”

Having said this, Xiangfei hesitated.

Facing Luo Chen’s inquisitive gaze, she still spoke up.

“Even so, even the ninth ring, where the chaotic energy is thinnest, is not an environment that ordinary Leakless Realm martial artists can endure or cultivate in for long periods. Senior, although you possess great power, you have only recently broken through to the Leakless Realm. Do you truly intend to enter the tower to cultivate thirteen years from now?”

Luo Chen nodded.

Xiangfei bit her lip lightly. “Then this concubine will also…”

“There’s no need to force it. Follow your own heart!” Luo Chen interrupted her, speaking unhurriedly. “You have done enough for me over the years, enough to atone for the sin of attempting to assassinate me back then. Before I enter the tower, I will remove the astral qi restriction on your body and grant you your freedom.”

Xiangfei opened her mouth, wanting to speak but stopping.

She very much wanted to say that the mere grace of Luo Chen not killing her back then was already ample compensation for all her years of running around for him.

Not to mention that Luo Chen had also used a precious pill to heal her injuries.

But clearly, Luo Chen was not someone who cared about such things.

To Luo Chen, kindness was repaid with kindness, and enmity with enmity. As for the specific measure of kindness or enmity, he only judged by his own heart.

Next, Luo Chen carefully learned some more points to note about cultivating in the nine rings of Annihilation, and then announced that he would go into seclusion.

Before entering the Nine-Tiered Immortal Tower, there were still some things he needed to prepare!

Chief among them was the matter of splitting his soul!

His main body’s soul was simply too vast!

At the early Ascendant stage, his soul foundation was comparable to that of a great power at the late Ascendant stage.

Even if he split it into nine divided souls, each one would still be no weaker than a normal Ascendant great power.

In this bizarre Ruins world, a soul at the Ascendant level was far too “conspicuous.”

Inside the Nine-Tiered Immortal Tower, it might be even more conspicuous, attracting the Soul-Shaking Thunderous Sound.

Therefore, whether for self-preservation or to temper his “martial dao divine spirit,” he had to split off even more divided souls.

With seven hundred and twenty apertures, he would need to split at least seven hundred and twenty divided souls, hiding them within the apertures, wrapped in Blood-Refined Divine Astral Qi.

Only then could he avoid drawing attention.

This project was far too massive.

The pain it would entail was unimaginable to ordinary people.

Even for Luo Chen, with his great tolerance for pain, it would be an excruciating ordeal.

There was even a chance he would go mad during the process.

The only thing that gave Luo Chen sufficient confidence was probably his cultivation of the *Soul-Splitting Art*, which he had perfected to the ultimate level!

…

Thirteen years passed in the blink of an eye.

Thirteen years later, Luo Chen emerged from seclusion.

The moment she saw Luo Chen, Xiangfei instinctively covered her mouth.

She had never seen Luo Chen so “haggard.”

Although his physique was still robust, his eyes were bloodshot, and he seemed utterly soulless.

A walking corpse—that was probably the best way to describe Luo Chen’s current state.

Could he really enter the Nine-Tiered Immortal Tower to cultivate in such a condition?

“Has anyone come out?”

That was Luo Chen’s first question after emerging from seclusion.

Xiangfei replied with a worried look. “People have been coming out one after another. I’ve even heard that the three Martial Dao Human Immortals of the upper three rings have temporarily left the Nine-Tiered Immortal Tower. But the one Senior is most concerned about, the Fang clan ancestor Carefree Realm Master, has not come out this time and remains inside.”

Hearing this answer, Luo Chen frowned involuntarily.

Since that one had previously expressed goodwill toward him through his descendant Fang Zhibai, why had he not come out this time?

Had he encountered some matter, or was he at a critical point in his cultivation?

Luo Chen had actually been looking forward to his first meeting with Carefree Realm Master.

After learning about the Fang clan’s history, he could roughly estimate Carefree Realm Master’s lifespan.

The Fang clan had existed for over three thousand years, so Carefree Realm Master’s age must be even greater, as he was the clan’s founding ancestor!

And within the Ruins, a martial artist’s lifespan limit was far shorter than in the outside world.

Even a powerful existence at the sixth stage of Annihilation had a maximum lifespan of only three thousand years.

If that were the case, then Carefree Realm Master was very likely, like Luo Chen, a cultivator who had drifted into the Ruins from the outside world.

And there was a high probability that he was a Void Refining True Lord, an existence enjoying a lifespan of over five thousand years!

If he could meet him, he would surely learn much more about the Ruins.

But unfortunately…

Luo Chen sighed. “Never mind. As long as I can enter the upper three rings, I will eventually be able to see him.”

Hearing this, Xiangfei suddenly spoke up. “Since Senior intends to charge into the upper three rings, why not take this opportunity to meet with those martial artists who have come out to rest?”

“Oh?” Luo Chen’s interest was piqued.

Xiangfei smiled slightly. “They have been cooped up inside for a long time and will rest for a while after coming out. During this process, they also meet and exchange ideas privately. Moreover, Senior mentioned earlier the special materials like Void Sprout Crystals and immortal essence stones. It’s possible that some of these people have them. If Senior wants them, you could easily trade using the Meridian-Repairing Pills you used to treat me, or pills of a similar level.”

A glint of light flashed in Luo Chen’s weary eyes. “An excellent suggestion!” (Chapter End)
