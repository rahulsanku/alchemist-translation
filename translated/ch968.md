# Chapter 968: Great Desolate Pursuit, Scooping the Moon from the Realm

"What is this now?"

Elder Hongye's face was filled with shock as the exclamation slipped from her lips.

First, a fifth-rank desolate beast, the Netherworld Earth Dragon, had appeared. Now, strange flames were erupting from within Luo Chen's body.

Either of these two things alone was enough to bring her a sense of utter hopelessness, a feeling that she was completely incapable of fighting back.

Within her line of sight.

Branches swayed, light and flames flickered.

The cyan-white flames, ever since Luo Chen had cast his spell, began to erupt and surge, expanding rapidly outward with Luo Chen's body as the center.

In the blink of an eye, they had already reached Elder Hongye.

She cried out in despair, "Fellow Daoist Luo..."

Yet this voice did not travel far at all.

Or perhaps, Luo Chen heard it but simply ignored it.

The Withering-Bloom Divine Fire swept past. Elder Hongye, already severely injured, deployed various defensive measures, but she could not buy even a moment of time or space.

In an instant, nothing remained on the spot but a puff of ash.

And this ash, under the scorching flames, also turned into nothingness.

A storage treasure exploded, sending a cascade of light flying in all directions, but under the burning of the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire, most of it turned to nothing.

It wasn't just Elder Hongye.

Everything around was instantly plunged into a sea of fire. Even the previously indomitable walls of flesh began to ooze blood and emit the burnt, scorched smell of charred meat.

It was like a scene of world-destroying cataclysm!

In the hazy light, Luo Chen's figure flickered in and out of view.

He glanced at the spot where Elder Hongye had been, then suddenly lifted his head and looked upward.

"Time to go!"

With a single thought decided, he shot upward like a rocket.

Wherever he passed, the layers upon layers of flesh walls, rocks, and earth all turned into rolling magma.

*Swish!*

A sound of something breaking through the air came abruptly.

Its speed was so fast that even Luo Chen's powerful divine sense could not catch it. He merely reacted instinctively, slapping downward.

The immeasurable divine fire gathered together with this slap, condensing into a flaming divine palm.

The flaming divine palm met the unknown attack in an instant, and a piercing sound rang out.

*Crack...*

The giant palm print was pierced through in an instant, crossing several zhang and directly blasting a large hole in Luo Chen's body, nearly shattering half of his form.

Looking closely, it was clearly a tongue covered in barbs!

"Hmph!"

Luo Chen let out a muffled grunt. Source power flowed rapidly, and the shattered half of his body began to quickly reorganize and recover at a speed unimaginable in his lifetime.

He stared fixedly at the tongue that was retracting, a hint of ruthlessness in his eyes.

In Luo Chen's usual battles, he had a myriad of tricks up his sleeve. He had even occasionally used the Nirvana Sacred Flame. So why was the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire used so rarely?

It was precisely because this fire was of too high a grade. In his previous realm, every time he used it, it would almost drain all his magical power.

Even after receiving guidance from Rosycloud and switching to cultivate the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture*, the situation had improved, but he still dared not be too reckless.

Now, having comprehended the origin of fire, with the blessing of the true intent of the law, the magical power consumed in controlling the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire had been reduced to a certain extent.

But the improvement was still a bit lacking. According to Luo Chen's estimation, this situation might only be completely resolved after he broke through to the late Sage stage.

Only then would he be able to wield this fifth-rank divine fire with relative ease.

Before that, unless absolutely necessary, he would try to avoid using this fifth-rank divine fire.

And now!

Facing a fifth-rank desolate beast head-on was an absolute necessity!

"Go!"

With only a sharp shout!

The branches of the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire swayed, like vines following in pursuit. In the blink of an eye, they broke through the yellow light enveloping the tongue and began to burn the tongue's main body.

The intense pain was inflicted upon the Netherworld Earth Dragon.

Both being of the fifth rank, how could the divine fire be weaker than the desolate beast?

The speed at which it came was even faster when it returned.

The tongue was swallowed back, and a large amount of saliva was secreted, drowning the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire.

But the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire was highly spiritual. From the inside out, it began to incinerate the Netherworld Earth Dragon's entire body.

Its incredibly tough physical body, in front of the most supreme divine fire of the Mountains and Seas Realm, seemed to become fragile.

A strange burnt meat fragrance wafted out, almost making one's mouth water.

But the one involved was not having such a good time.

"Roar... roar..."

Continuous roars of pain sounded. Earth-yellow halos continuously erupted from within its body, forcing the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire back.

*Boom...*

The earth-yellow halo suddenly expanded, completely forcing back the flames. The body of the Netherworld Earth Dragon, over ten zhang long, was revealed again.

Only this time, it was in a rather sorry state.

Its entire body was lacerated, flesh and blood dripping, with hardly a patch of good skin left.

It was furious!

It looked up above. The enemy's figure was gone.

But he couldn't escape!

The Netherworld Earth Dragon opened its massive mouth, and a terrifying suction force was generated.

The surrounding indestructible walls immediately began to writhe, turning into streams of blood that flowed towards it like a hundred rivers returning to the sea.

With the collapse of the walls, the Netherworld Mountain, which covered the Yin-Yang Tomb, also began to cave in from the inside out.

...

*Whoosh!*

Under the blood moon, a figure hung high in the air.

A high crown with golden feathers, half-armor clad on his body.

Luo Chen let out a long breath of turbid qi, his mind slightly relaxed.

He had finally escaped!

He looked down below.

If he hadn't looked, it would have been fine, but once he did, he immediately went on high alert.

The Netherworld Mountain, thousands of zhang tall, was rapidly collapsing, caving in from the inside out.

As rocks tumbled down, large patches of writhing flesh were exposed.

And all this flesh was contracting inward, as if something inside was devouring it.

Luo Chen swallowed his saliva, his eyes filled with shock.

"Could it be that this Netherworld Mountain was actually formed from the flesh of that old Netherworld Earth Dragon that died of old age back then?"

The more he looked, the more real this guess became!

No wonder he had that strange feeling of entering the mouth of a giant beast when he first entered Netherworld Mountain.

"This place cannot be lingered in!"

With this thought, Luo Chen turned and fled.

In fact, he didn't even use flying or sword escape techniques. Instead, he directly consumed his remaining magical power to execute his fastest method: the fire escape technique.

Brilliant sparks bloomed.

When he reappeared, Luo Chen's figure had already vanished from the spot and appeared several li away.

A gourd appeared in his hand. He took a sip, and the magical power fluctuations on Luo Chen's body stirred. Then, sparks appeared again.

This speed was unimaginable.

Yet the sense of crisis in Luo Chen's heart had still not been dispelled.

When he was a hundred li away, he subconsciously looked back.

He saw a giant beast, a full thousand zhang in size, crouching on the ground, raising its head to look at the moon, emitting a low roar.

It was a monster covered in earth-yellow scales, its four legs like heavenly pillars, its tail like a giant hammer.

When it slowly stood up, it blotted out the sky and covered the sun, the earth trembling!

*Shua!*

A pair of green, ghost-fire-like eyes locked directly onto Luo Chen, who was a hundred li away.

It bared its teeth, juices flowing.

Then, it took a step!

*Dong!*

It began to run. The scenery behind it seemed to be retreating wildly as if shifting sideways. The earth seemed to be carrying it forward.

Every step seemed to shrink the earth into inches.

Every leap seemed to cross space.

A hundred li distance, in just a few breaths.

A true fifth-rank desolate beast!

This thought arose in Luo Chen's mind, and without thinking, he headed towards the Death Gate.

Sparks continuously appeared, magical power frantically consumed.

Suddenly, a gale arose behind him!

Without thinking, Luo Chen turned and pointed a finger.

The long-accumulated Withering-Bloom Dao Finger appeared in the world once more!

This time, it was no longer a reversed divine ability, but the purest transformation of life force into death intent. It was a killing move.

Vast heaven and earth origin qi gathered, forming a vortex.

The Withering-Bloom Dao Finger condensed from within the vortex, pointing towards the head of the giant monster behind him.

*Puff!*

A bloody hole suddenly appeared.

The divine ability technique was effective after all.

However, for a thousand-zhang body, this bit of injury was utterly insignificant.

But Luo Chen, with his rich combat experience, seemed to have anticipated this.

Along that bloody hole, the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire appeared again.

The Netherworld Earth Dragon also seemed extremely wary of this cyan-white flame. Blood plasma gushed from within its body, turning into a water column to force the cyan-white flame back.

Not only that, it even slapped the ground with its feet.

The earth trembled, and the ground rolled like waves, pressing towards Luo Chen.

Furthermore, endless yellow halos came from all directions.

"Escape!"

Luo Chen subconsciously wanted to use the fire escape technique again, but for some reason, he could no longer merge with heaven and earth.

"Is it that yellow halo?"

A fierce light flashed in Luo Chen's eyes. He raised his hand, and the Yuantu Sword appeared in his grip.

*Slash!*

A vast sword light, invincible wherever it went.

With one slash, the earth wave was severed.

Luo Chen used the force to retreat backward, like a kite, flying into the distance.

After escaping the range covered by the yellow halo, the fire escape technique was used again.

During the pause, Luo Chen didn't care about the emptiness of his magical power, nor the slow replenishment of the Golden Wind and Jade Dew Wine. He directly mobilized the pure magical power from his Sage's main body.

His hands flew, his fingertips flowing.

His hands stopped, the seal was formed!

He slapped backward with all his might.

It was the Azure Yang Great Handprint!

Unlike the ordinary Azure Yang Great Handprint, this handprint was dazzling with golden light, filled with sacred flames.

It was clearly imbued with the Nirvana Sacred Flame!

With one palm strike, it pushed across heaven and earth.

But Luo Chen's face was pale. He didn't bother to look at the result, continuing to flee.

This sorry state was rarely seen on Luo Chen.

It was laughable that he had earlier felt that the battle with Li Tianshi hadn't been satisfying enough.

A violent explosion sounded behind him, but it only lasted a moment.

The fishy smell from the Netherworld Earth Dragon, carried by the gale, drew closer and closer.

"Could I be destined to die here today?"

With this thought, Luo Chen made a decision. He flicked his hand, tossing something into the distance.

A flash of light appeared, and the Netherworld Earth Dragon's movements paused for an instant.

A flicker of joy arose in Luo Chen's heart, but it sank again the next moment.

What he had thrown out was the Life-and-Death Gu he had obtained earlier.

But it was clear that even if he voluntarily handed over this item, the Netherworld Earth Dragon had already completely locked onto him.

"At worst, I'll fight to the death!"

Luo Chen's face twisted ferociously. Magical power flowed within his body, and the little Sage figure gradually began to show signs of becoming illusory.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron appeared beneath his feet, spinning madly.

The Yuantu Sword in his hand hummed incessantly. The sword spirit within seemed to sense Luo Chen's thoughts, its spiritual light blazing, causing the blood-colored halo on the sword's body to flow and turn. As sword qi pulsed, faint ripples even appeared in the void, as if a casual swing of the sword could tear the void apart.

"I never thought the Yuantu Sword Spirit would be willing to completely submit to me under these circumstances?"

Amidst his astonishment, the ferocity on Luo Chen's face gradually gave way to a hint of composure.

This Netherworld Earth Dragon had been lying dormant for over a thousand years. Although it was of the fifth rank, it wasn't necessarily that powerful.

This could be seen from the fact that it had been injured by the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire in their initial exchange.

Its current might was only because it had devoured the flesh of the old Netherworld Earth Dragon.

If he went all out, using all his treasures, it might not necessarily be a certain death situation.

Especially!

"The Death Gate is not far from here. As long as..."

Just as Luo Chen was about to make a desperate fight, it wasn't just the Yuantu Sword Spirit that sensed his intent.

*Whoosh!*

A black light flew out along Luo Chen's arm, circled his body once, then leaped into the sky and flew towards the Netherworld Earth Dragon without looking back.

Luo Chen was stunned, blurting out, "What are you doing?"

Black King's voice rumbled over, "Master, you go first. Little Black will hold it off!"

As he spoke, Black King's body began to expand and grow rapidly.

Ten zhang, fifty zhang, a hundred zhang, two hundred zhang...

By the end, it was a full five hundred zhang in size!

Such a physique, even among the ranks of demon beasts and desolate beasts, could be ranked as mid-fourth rank.

This showed that Black King's claim that he hadn't been slacking off all these years was not a lie.

But no matter how hard he tried, at this moment, he could not bridge the chasm of a major realm.

In front of the Netherworld Earth Dragon, Black King looked as small as a child.

Luo Chen's face changed rapidly. No one knew what he was thinking.

In the distance, Black King had already engaged the Netherworld Earth Dragon.

The sound of miserable screams rang out at the very first contact.

Black King tried to entangle the Netherworld Earth Dragon, but the earth-yellow halo spread, tearing his body into two sections.

His hand was trembling.

It wasn't Luo Chen's hand, but the Yuantu Sword's violent trembling.

"Do you want to fight too?"

Luo Chen murmured, as if he wasn't asking the sword, but himself.

In truth, when he asked this question, the answer was already obvious.

A small, illusory figure soared into the air.

The long sword in his hand was like a rainbow, its sharp sword qi bursting into the nine heavens.

Following the *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon*, Luo Chen formed a sword seal, pouring his vast magical power into the Yuantu Sword.

"Just this one move!"

With his mind decided, Luo Chen's eyes blazed with golden light, sweeping over the Netherworld Earth Dragon's entire body, seeking a weakness.

His gaze froze.

"Slash!"

Sword qi shot into the sky, the sword's hum shook the wilderness.

The vast sword light turned into a blood-colored ribbon, like a Milky Way from the nine heavens descending upon the world.

Wherever it passed, thin black lines appeared in the void, a sign of spatial cracks forming.

The distant Netherworld Earth Dragon immediately sensed danger. It shook off Black King, who was trying to entangle it again, and its form flickered. With an agility that belied its massive size, it tried to escape.

But above its head, a giant cauldron emitted a hazy light. Chains flew out in unison, plunging into the earth, slightly slowing its massive body.

The sword qi ribbon was already before its eyes!

Luo Chen panted heavily, looking at his target.

At this moment, the magical power within his body was nearly exhausted, and even his Sage was listless.

Not to mention the consumption from his battles with Li Tianshi, the Purple-clad Marquis, and others, just the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire and the successive activations of the fire escape technique had drained his magical power immensely.

This sword was already his strongest move, short of collapsing his Sage!

If it still couldn't achieve results, he would have no choice but to abandon Black King and flee for his life with all his might.

With the giant cauldron suppressing from above and the blood sword breaking through the air from afar, the Netherworld Earth Dragon no longer tried to dodge or escape. Its entire body blazed with an earth-yellow halo, forming an indestructible light barrier to protect its entire body.

The next moment!

The sword qi struck its body, capable of shattering mountains and severing peaks.

The earth-yellow halo was cut open like cloth, breaking inch by inch.

It plunged into the massive body.

"Roar!!!"

A cry of pain came.

Joy arose in Luo Chen's heart. "Did it work?"

But what his divine sense perceived made his heart sink continuously.

The Crimson-Eyed Golden Gaze had indeed found a weakness, and the Yuantu Sword had indeed inflicted considerable damage on the opponent.

But its vitality far exceeded his imagination.

The earth-yellow halo flickered, once again completely enveloping the Netherworld Earth Dragon's body.

The massive wound caused by the Yuantu Sword was also rapidly closing.

Like Black King, this Netherworld Earth Dragon clearly possessed a similar talent for an undying body.

Once it recovered and was on guard, even if Luo Chen could still unleash that heaven-shaking sword strike next time, it would likely be difficult to break through.

"The chasm of realms is ultimately unbridgeable!"

Luo Chen smiled bitterly, turned without hesitation, and fled.

As for Black King, he could only leave it to fate.

But if he wanted to escape, he had to see if the enemy would allow it.

*Swish! Swish! Swish!*

On the ground, one earth-yellow pillar of light after another burst forth from the earth, shooting straight into the sky.

The Netherworld Earth Dragon was still roaring. The pillars of light were seamless, boundless.

Centered on its massive body, they wildly spread.

The earth's crust shook, great mountains split apart, and the entire Yin-Yang Realm seemed to tremble.

It was truly enraged!

Just then!

The high-hanging bright moon suddenly descended with a hazy moonlight.

Wherever it passed, the pillars of light dissipated, and the earth was pacified.

The turbulent Yin-Yang Realm also instantly calmed down.

Luo Chen, who had been on the verge of despair in the distance, couldn't help but freeze, looking at that bright moon.

A figure appeared upon it, as if within this realm, yet as if separated by millions of li.

An aged voice slowly emanated from within.

"Senior Brother, since you have already passed away, why are you still lingering?"

The figure reached towards the Yin-Yang Realm and scooped.

The bright moon instantly fell into his hand!
