# Chapter 1022: Should I Call You Wilderness Wanderer or Pill Sovereign?

Upon the boundless sea, a streak of black light sped across the waters, stirring not a single wave.

Its speed was extreme, yet its movement was remarkably subtle.

This demonstrated both the sharpness of this treasure in flight and the exquisite control of the person manipulating the flying shuttle.

Nevertheless, from time to time, towering waves still crashed around it, echoing with roars like those of wild beasts.

*Whoosh!*

A beam of white light drilled its way to the outermost room of the flying shuttle's core chambers, waiting silently for the summons of its master within.

Yet during this wait, the white light instinctively kept its distance from the room. For within that room resided a sword intent so sharp it seemed capable of piercing through anything. Even though the owner was suppressing that sword intent with all his might, Bai Meiling still dared not rashly charge inside.

After waiting for some time, that sword intent finally dissipated slowly.

From within the room, a low voice emerged.

"What is it?"

Bai Meiling's spirits lifted, and she flew inside.

Reporting to Luo Chen, who sat cross-legged, she said: "Master, a large number of sea demons are appearing on the Eastern Sea, stirring up waves and causing quite a disturbance."

Luo Chen frowned slightly, seemingly caught off guard by this situation.

Once upon a time, a vast sea region had lain between Central Continent and the Eastern Wilderness. Although there were few exposed landmasses within this sea, the world still called it the Eastern Sea.

As far as Luo Chen knew, while there were demon beasts within the Eastern Sea, their numbers were not particularly large.

Especially when averaged over such a vast area, they were even fewer and farther between.

This was closely related to the former dominance of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!

In any sea region close to Central Continent, most of the demon beasts had been cleansed through year after year of slaughter.

Now, only a little over thirty years had passed since the collapse of Central Continent and the heavy blow dealt to the Dao Sect. Given the growth cycle of demon beasts, how could so many sea demons have appeared in such a short time?

Luo Chen questioned Bai Meiling in detail about what she had observed, finally reaching a conclusion.

"Such a change is certainly not a natural occurrence. Someone must be deliberately orchestrating it. I just don't know if this is happening only in this one sea region, or if similar situations exist near all three landmasses of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect."

Luo Chen could not guess the mastermind behind the scenes, but he vaguely sensed that the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect would face troubled times ahead.

"Did they attack the Frost Eagle Shuttle?"

"No, they did not. The shuttle's speed is too fast. By the time they notice it, it has already passed beyond their attack range."

"Mm, let it be then. For now, we don't need to concern ourselves with them."

Bai Meiling nodded respectfully and withdrew.

After she left, Luo Chen pondered the matter a little longer, then shook his head and set it aside. Instead, he focused his attention on the blood-colored longsword resting across his knees.

The Yuantu Sword!

It had originated from the Demon-Fall Land, a treasure casually refined by Devil Lord Heaven-Refining in his spare time.

Upon its first emergence, it had slain an Ascendant-level great power.

Very early on, it had already earned the title of pseudo-spirit treasure.

Later, after a century of Luo Chen's nurturing, it had absorbed countless amounts of blood, including the entire cultivation base of the Nether Origin Sect's great power, Ancestor Black Marsh.

By all logic, it should have long since crossed that final step and achieved true spirit treasure status.

Yet now, the Yuantu Sword remained merely a true artifact.

Luo Chen vaguely sensed what the final missing element was.

But he had not yet made up his mind.

A sword is a tool for killing; it slays the enemy but also wounds the user!

If one were not careful, it could harm one's very foundation.

To ensure success without fail, Luo Chen had to possess sufficient certainty to take that final step.

And regarding how to increase this so-called "certainty," Luo Chen already had a plan.

"Proficient level!"

Gazing into the void, Luo Chen saw a faintly shimmering entry on the attribute panel that others could not see.

**[Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon: Proficient 201/300]**

This sword canon had originated from Fu Chaosheng, who had specially gifted it to compensate for the fifty years of lifespan Luo Chen had lost back then.

Its grade was extremely high, reaching the fifth rank, but the canon itself was not particularly exquisite.

The reason it could reach the fifth rank despite relying on an unremarkable technique was that once this sword canon was fully mastered, it possessed a terrifying might comparable to a divine ability.

*Thousand Skies Converge, Qi Surges to the Starry River!*

Once one reached this level, even an Ascendant great power would find it difficult to withstand the ultimate sword strike.

But cultivating that one sword strike was far too difficult for ordinary cultivators.

An ordinary person would need a year, or even many years, to refine a single wisp of firmament qi. Reaching the thousand-skies realm would require a full thousand years.

Even with Luo Chen's astonishing capabilities, he had previously only managed to refine one wisp every half year.

Moreover, the greatest flaw of this technique was that it was a one-time offensive method. Once the sword qi was released in battle, it was very difficult to recover.

In other words, even if one painstakingly spent several hundred years cultivating several hundred wisps of terrifying sword qi, a single battle could reduce everything to nothing.

Luo Chen was no exception to this rule.

During his journey to the Southern Frontier, in order to survive the pursuit of the Nether Earth Dragon, he had exhausted years of accumulation, unleashing a single sword strike that consumed all the crimson firmament qi he had amassed.

And now, Luo Chen was picking up this sword canon again, his purpose naturally clear.

Even if it was merely a one-time killing move, if it could play a role at a critical moment in the future, all the effort would be worth it.

Furthermore, Luo Chen had discovered early on that there was a significant difference between his cultivation of the *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon* and that of ordinary people.

That was, as his proficiency increased, the efficiency of refining firmament qi greatly improved.

From one wisp every half year at the initial entry level, to one wisp every three months at the skilled level... until recently, during the journey, Luo Chen had continuously refined firmament qi, finally breaking through to the proficient level.

Sure enough!

Luo Chen found that the efficiency had once again doubled!

From one wisp every three months, it had been reduced by half again, reaching one wisp every forty-five days—roughly a month and a half!

As a result, Luo Chen saw hope of achieving great mastery of this sword canon within his lifetime.

"Following this pattern, if I reach the perfect level next, will it be one wisp every twenty days? And at the master level, can I refine one wisp every ten days?"

"If that's truly the case, I might not even need a hundred years to have a chance at cultivating this sword dao divine ability!"

"The only constraint, instead, is the quantity of firmament qi."

Luo Chen's gaze fell upon his storage ring.

Previously, Deng Taiyue had used his Nascent Soul Pill as a gimmick to convene a high-spec large-scale auction, collecting a considerable amount of nine-skies qi for him.

A total of two hundred wisps!

On average, each Nascent Soul Pill had brought in twenty wisps of nine-skies qi that met Luo Chen's requirements.

This price was somewhat inflated.

Collecting nine-skies qi was not difficult, but it was very time-consuming. It required a Sage to spend all day above the nine heavens, extending their divine sense to search and capture it—a laborious and time-consuming task.

Most Sages would not deliberately engage in such thankless work. They usually collected it as a side task when ascending to the nine heavens to search for suitable materials.

The reason so much had been harvested this time was largely because the Eastern Wilderness Sages were doing Luo Chen a favor.

Giving the Pill Sovereign face!

Over two hundred years of the human-demon war, the Eastern Wilderness had suffered heavy losses. Nascent Soul Pills were the highest quality resource, capable of rapidly increasing the high-end combat power of various sects. Since Luo Chen could refine Nascent Soul Pills and was willing to trade them, even at a premium, everyone was willing to engage in the transaction tacitly.

All to maintain good relations with Pill Sovereign Luo Chen.

"Two hundred wisps—not enough to break through to the master level, but sufficient to raise proficiency to the perfect level."

"Once they are consumed, I will need to do it myself, personally collecting nine-skies qi."

Regarding future matters, although Luo Chen knew they would be troublesome, he had not lost confidence.

Because he had already begun planning for this long ago.

The true artifact he had borrowed from the Nine Spirits Sect was specifically for this purpose!

Of course!

Before that, he still had to arrange the most important matter first.

The flying shuttle continued forward, departing from the direction of the Eastern Origin Continent and heading toward the Tiannan Continent.

...

One year later.

Luo Chen awoke faintly from his seclusion.

"Master, we have arrived!"

Bai Meiling's voice carried a hint of exhaustion. For an entire year, she had been the one operating the Frost Eagle Shuttle, which had taken a considerable toll on her soul and mana.

It was precisely because of this that they had saved time, crossing the Eastern Sea in just one year, compared to the three years it had taken back then.

"You have worked hard. Enter the Soul-Nourishing Banner and rest for a while!"

Luo Chen took out a white banner, collected Bai Meiling inside, then shrank the banner and hid it in his sleeve.

Putting away the Frost Eagle Shuttle, Luo Chen's feet finally stepped firmly onto solid ground.

Looking around, seabirds circled overhead, and in the distance, verdant green dotted his vision.

"Is this the former southeastern region of Central Continent, now the Tiannan Continent?"

Murmuring to himself, Luo Chen closed his eyes.

His soul power, vast as an ocean, transformed into divine sense, spreading out gently from him like ripples.

His divine might was like the sea, but as it fell upon this continent, it was as gentle as the wind, with no one able to detect Luo Chen's arrival.

Even when it passed over the occasional Sage or two, there was still no reaction.

The gap between them was already as vast as a chasm.

Luo Chen's divine sense spread extremely quickly and reached an extremely far distance.

In but an instant, it had already surpassed the limits of a Sage, covering a distance of ten thousand li!

It must be known that the Great Elder of the Dongyang Sect, who was reputed to be a Great Circle Sage, had a divine sense coverage of only eight thousand li.

Yet Luo Chen, with ease, had crossed over ten thousand li.

And the trend of his divine sense continuing to spread showed no sign of stopping, showing every sign of going even further.

Just how far could the divine sense of a normal Ascendant great power extend?

This thought surfaced for a moment, then was quickly suppressed.

Because Luo Chen had found his target!

"Old friend, so you are here!"

Luo Chen smiled slightly, took a single step, and when he reappeared, he was already an extremely distant place away.

This was still the physical application of the Voidburst Step.

But compared to the great commotion of void explosions that had accompanied this technique in the past, Luo Chen's current execution of it carried not a trace of ostentation, nearly reaching the point of merging into the void.

As for why there was such an improvement, Luo Chen could not say for certain, but it might be related to Azure Frost, with whom he had formed a life-and-death contract.

...

White Egret Marsh.

Once a water-rich area in Central Continent, it contained a third-rank spirit vein and was held by a humble Lu family.

With the collapse of Central Continent thirty years ago, the Lu family had been completely wiped out.

White Egret Marsh had also suffered a great calamity during the collapse, with rivers changing course and miasmas springing up everywhere.

But disaster and fortune relied on each other. After the Tiannan Continent broke away, the earth's energy shifted, eventually forming a spirit acupoint beneath White Egret Marsh. The originally ordinary third-rank spirit vein, due to the appearance of this spirit acupoint, leaped to the fourth rank.

Later, when the various families of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect divided up the territories, this place attracted the covetous eyes of many humble families. But the result was quite unexpected—White Egret Marsh was actually taken by a family whose member was a guest elder of the Star Gate.

Even though some aristocratic families were dissatisfied, under the suppression of surface-level rules, they ultimately gritted their teeth and acknowledged the ownership of this place.

Thus, White Egret Marsh became the territory of the Zhou clan!

But this was not permanent. If, after a hundred years, the Zhou clan had no Sage to guard it, this place would still fall into the hands of other major powers.

In a simple and unadorned hall.

Zhou Yunshen, ancient to the point of being on the verge of death, looked at the three Core Formation cultivators kneeling below him. His deeply wrinkled face held only deep worry.

"Perhaps I was wrong to seize this place for you. Instead, I have invited a deadly calamity."

The three did not look convinced in their hearts. Especially a robust man who spoke boldly: "Why must the ancestor worry so much? As long as Brother Zhou Cang borrows the fourth-rank spirit vein to break through to the Sage realm, he can protect the family for another five hundred years!"

Zhou Cang was the eldest among the three, his hair and beard already half-gray.

And his realm was Core Formation ninth layer!

However, Zhou Yunshen shook his head. "Xiao Cang's accumulation is still insufficient. Moreover, thirty years ago, he injured his foundation protecting the family. The chances of him breaking through to the Sage realm are far too low."

Zhou Cang lowered his head, a faint sigh escaping him.

Among the three, the other was a young woman, her eyes filled with concern.

"Ancestor, with you here, isn't our Coral Sea Zhou clan still as stable as Mount Tai?"

Zhou Yunshen's lips trembled as he said in a low voice: "Zhenniang, do you still remember when my eight-hundred-year birthday celebration was held?"

Zhou Shuzhen was stunned.

The memory of that grand and joyous birthday celebration had already become very blurry in her mind.

At that time, she had only just barely reached Initiation.

And now, she was already at mid Core Formation, a full one hundred and seventy years having passed!

Legend had it that the lifespan of a normal Sage was only about a thousand years. Even with methods to extend life, it was only around eleven or twelve hundred years.

And that was assuming no major injuries had been sustained.

As for their ancestor, who had led the family from the Northern Sea, breaking through the siege of demon beasts all the way to Central Continent, experiencing the great battle of the Northern Sea along the way, and recently engaging in a simple exchange with three Sages—it was absolutely impossible that he had not been injured.

Zhou Shuzhen choked up and said, "Ancestor..."

Clearly, the other two Core Formation cultivators of the Zhou clan were not fools either. They too had sensed the key point.

Their ancestor's lifespan was running out.

Once he passed away, how could the Zhou clan, whose strength was already so weak, continue to occupy this fourth-rank spirit land of White Egret Marsh?

A somber atmosphere gradually spread.

Just then!

Zhou Yunshen's aged eyes suddenly burst forth with a meaning of both surprise and joy.

"You may all withdraw!"

"Eh?"

The three had not yet reacted.

Zhou Yunshen added, "Cultivate diligently. Do not slack off."

Although puzzled, the three still bowed respectfully and withdrew.

After they left, Zhou Yunshen took a slight breath and looked toward the entrance.

Amidst the shifting light and shadow, a familiar yet unfamiliar figure was reflected in his pupils.

Zhou Yunshen said with a complex expression: "Daoist Luo, should I call you Wilderness Wanderer or Pill Sovereign?"
