# Chapter 947: Grimpeak Corpse Alliance, A Gathering of Experts

**Grimpeak Corpse Alliance!**

One of the largest powers in the Southern Frontier today.

In earlier years, it had always remained hidden in the shadows, quite mysterious.

It was only in recent years, with frequent movements, that it gradually surfaced.

And the origins of those relatively active experts let people know just what kind of existence this behemoth of a force truly was.

The Alliance Leader, Sage Songyang, Supreme Elder of Songshan Sect, at the late-stage Sage realm.

The Deputy Alliance Leader, Matron Redleaf, Supreme Elder of Redleaf Sect, also a long-established Grand Cultivator, rumored to be extremely skilled in formations.

These two individuals alone, along with the forces behind them, were already top-tier powers in the Southern Frontier.

United together, if one also added the Purple Blood Sect, White Cloud Temple… these Sage-level forces, the world could hardly imagine what they intended to achieve by combining.

And that unimaginable thing, by now, was already semi-public.

Dealing with the Life and Death Gate!

The Life and Death Gate, ah—that was the only Ascendant sacred ground in the Southern Frontier.

Even if it was somewhat more declined compared to the sacred grounds of the other four continents, with only a single Ascendant Great Power sitting in charge, it was still not a force that any Sage-level power dared to provoke.

The Grimpeak Corpse Alliance's move was no different from an egg striking a stone.

But they had to do it, *must* do it!

Moreover, there were not a few people attempting to join this grand undertaking.

"They are all driven to desperation too!"

Atop Mijie Mountain, amidst layers of flickering shadows, an old woman in red robes with white hair, a look of pity on her face, murmured softly.

Beside her stood a man in a wide robe and cloak, with a fierce look in his brow.

Hearing this, he couldn't help but snort coldly.

"Driven to desperation, perhaps. But more of them covet the sacred ground's wealth!"

Matron Redleaf was startled for a moment, looking at the man with confusion. "Songyang, what do you mean by that?"

Sage Songyang clasped his hands behind his back, his gaze looking far down below, sweeping over every person climbing the mountain.

"You have long resided in the Redleaf Sect, unaware of the changing winds beyond this continent, ignorant of the great currents flowing through this realm."

Matron Redleaf frowned. "Speak plainly, no need to be cryptic."

Sage Songyang twitched the corner of his mouth. "First, the Hanging Temple in the Western Desert closed its gates. Then, the Primordial Demon Sect in the Northern Sea was destroyed. In this process, the Life and Death Gate gradually reduced its influence over the Southern Frontier, until it sealed its mountain gates. Clearly, there is an undercurrent in the entire Mountains and Seas Realm that even the sacred grounds are desperate to avoid. Once encountered, it brings the danger of annihilation."

"Too alarmist!" Matron Redleaf shook her head, not accepting this view.

In the Mountains and Seas Realm, Ascendants were the pinnacle of the world. What kind of undercurrent could make them afraid?

"Then how do you explain the destruction of the Nether Origin Sect?" Sage Songyang countered.

Matron Redleaf frowned slightly. "I heard they provoked a peerless great demon, and thus had their mountain gates stormed."

Sage Songyang nodded, then shook his head. "A sacred ground—how could there be talk of 'provocation'? Who do they not boss around? Just like the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect now, striking out on all sides. Eighty percent of the Northern Sea's cultivation world has already fallen into their hands. The Eastern Wilderness is nominally under their control as well, and they are leading troops in fierce battle against the demon race. Following that, they have begun to set their sights on our Southern Frontier. Under these circumstances, who dares to speak out?"

"The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect?" Matron Redleaf's face showed a look of bewilderment.

That was a true behemoth!

Ruling over an entire continent, no one dared to provoke it.

Lying dormant for three thousand years, when it did not move, all was still; when it moved, it was with the momentum of dominating the world.

If this Southern Frontier endeavor succeeded, it was likely that this very sky would change its surname to "Heavenly Origin."

Thinking of this, the old woman suddenly reacted. "You mean… these people eager to join our Grimpeak Corpse Alliance want to take advantage of our alliance with the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect to charge into the Life and Death Gate together and seize resources?"

Sage Songyang let out a low laugh, not denying it.

He himself had founded the Grimpeak Corpse Alliance and knew this force best.

To outsiders, it seemed powerful, but in reality, it was merely an alliance of experts from various families, with no cohesion to speak of.

At the top-tier expert level, there were only a few late-stage Sage Grand Cultivators.

It was a crushing existence for ordinary forces, but against the Life and Death Gate, it was completely inadequate.

Yet, under these circumstances, he had issued the bold declaration of attacking a sacred ground.

As long as the people of the world were not fools, they could probably guess where his reliance lay.

The simplest was to ally with the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, using the hands of the Dao Sect's Great Powers to contend with the Life and Death Gate's Great Powers!

Risk was present, but opportunity was also present.

Whether it was to resolve the shackles on their cultivation or to seize the resources of the sacred ground, no one would not be tempted.

"It's just a pity that the time given to us is still too short. The power we have accumulated is far from enough." After his light laugh, Sage Songyang became somewhat melancholy. "If we could also pull in those two old fellows from the Luo Sect and the Xuanji Sect, we would have a bit more certainty."

Matron Redleaf hesitated. "Now, within the Corpse Alliance, we have you, me, Heavenly Teacher Li, and that old man from the Burial Life Valley. Is that not enough?"

"Not enough!"

Sage Songyang took a deep breath. "Not only is it not enough to deal with the Life and Death Gate, but our bargaining chips are also insufficient in the face of cooperation with the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect."

Matron Redleaf couldn't help but say, "Speaking of which, you have been strongly advocating cooperation with the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. Are you not afraid of inviting a wolf into the house, only to be purged by them afterwards?"

At this, Sage Songyang's expression shifted constantly, and in the end, he only murmured, "There is no choice."

Then he stopped discussing this topic and turned his attention to the climbers below.

"Most of these people I invited this time are ordinary. However, there are indeed two or three who are worth noticing."

Matron Redleaf followed his gaze and couldn't help but nod in agreement.

Indeed!

Whether it was the famous Luo Sect Left Envoy Bei Puti, or that white-robed monk with a completely different temperament, wavering between righteous and evil, they were both outstanding.

As for the black-armored man walking at the very front, she didn't pay much attention to him.

Just a reckless brute.

However, considering the man's reputation, Matron Redleaf thought for a moment and said, "I hear that Qi Tian, though not an orthodox cultivator, has extremely high combat power. Perhaps we could invite him to join the Corpse Alliance?"

Sage Songyang nodded. "I have the same intention. When the time comes to attack the Life and Death Gate, using this man as the vanguard, we will be unstoppable!"

...

At this moment, within Mijie Mountain.

Luo Chen walked alone, unaware that anyone was paying attention to him, and even less aware that his reputation as a high-level body cultivator was worth nothing more than a sneer in the eyes of these Grand Cultivators.

But even if he knew, he wouldn't care.

As he walked, his eyes scanned his surroundings, and the look of confusion on his face grew stronger.

"A strange place."

"A strange spatial structure!"

When he saw the spatial passageway, he thought that Mijie Mountain would be like the Nether Abyss, full of spatial cracks everywhere, extremely dangerous.

Otherwise, why would the outside world widely say that no martial force was allowed within Mijie Mountain?

Now, it seemed not to be the case.

Although the spaces within Mijie Mountain were layered upon each other, they were actually extremely stable!

Even if one accidentally took a wrong step, they did not encounter any danger. They merely incurred the displeasure of the master of that particular space, who would then invite them out.

"Is it because the spaces here have gradually stabilized over the long passage of time?"

Luo Chen walked forward, thinking all the way.

When he reached the end, he was surprised to find that Zheng Guan and the others had already arrived.

He had taken the lead, yet he was behind everyone else?

Facing a few slightly questioning gazes, Luo Chen paid no attention, still immersed in what he had seen and heard along the way.

Layered spaces would gradually stabilize according to certain laws.

In this process, some natural spatial restrictions also played a corresponding role.

Could the Nether Abyss, where the Luo Tian Sect now resided, also be stabilized in the future using this method?

If he learned these natural spatial restrictions, could he grope for the rhythm and pattern of living beings shuttling through them, thereby countering Azure Frost's elusive spatial laws?

Seeing Luo Chen silent, Zheng Guan thought he had lost face in front of the crowd and was thus keeping quiet.

He smiled. "This is the first time for all of you to come to Mijie Mountain. Whether you want to join the Grimpeak Corpse Alliance or are here for the exchange meeting, there is no rush. Please rest for now. Tomorrow, a ten-day exchange meeting will be held."

Everyone responded in agreement, and then, under Zheng Guan's arrangements, each chose a residence to rest in.

Inside his room, Luo Chen was still thinking, until Ming Lei Cave Master came to visit, snapping him out of that state.

"Now that we are here, my task is complete," Ming Lei Cave Master said.

Luo Chen nodded slightly, acknowledging this.

Originally, their transaction was for Luo Chen to make a move and resolve the crisis at Wind Thunder Gorge, and then Ming Lei would introduce him to the Sage circle of the Southern Frontier.

Now that he had successfully arrived at this Mijie Mountain and had become acquainted with Zheng Guan, the master of Nine Haze Immortal City, he no longer needed Ming Lei Cave Master's help for the subsequent matters.

But before that, Ming Lei Cave Master still carefully reminded him of some things.

"Just in case you don't know, that high-level body cultivation technique is in the hands of the Purple Blood Sect's Supreme Elder, Purple Robed Marquis. Yes, the man we saw in the Sumeru Pavilion just now."

"Him?" Luo Chen recalled the purple-robed man he had seen earlier. His aura was indeed extraordinary.

"Yes! It was him who brought it out for trade last time." Ming Lei Cave Master nodded, then said uncertainly, "But according to my inquiries in recent days, he might not be the true owner of that body cultivation technique. He was just trading on someone else's behalf."

"Oh?"

"The true owner should be the leader of the Worship Moon Sect, Heavenly Teacher Li."

"Worship Moon Sect?" Luo Chen's face showed a look of bewilderment. He had never heard of such a great sect in the Southern Frontier.

Ming Lei Cave Master explained, "The Worship Moon Sect is not a large sect. Its inner disciples are extremely few, and it is famous for cultivating gu insects. Heavenly Teacher Li is even a top-tier gu cultivator in the Southern Frontier. Many experts consider it an honor to ask him to refine gu insects. But you must not underestimate this man. He is a genuine Grand Cultivator, and even within this Grimpeak Corpse Alliance, he is a highly respected figure. It is rumored that his status is no less than that of Sage Songyang!"

"An existence like that has also joined the Grimpeak Corpse Alliance?" Luo Chen murmured.

Perhaps because Luo Chen had made a move today to withstand Ning Yuzhuo's oppressive anger on his behalf, Ming Lei Cave Master was quite grateful and gave very detailed reminders on some points.

"I know your combat power is formidable and your methods are extraordinary. But do not underestimate the experts of the Southern Frontier."

"Not to mention the sacred ground, the Life and Death Gate. Just within the Corpse Alliance itself, from what I have learned through my inquiries, there are quite a few top-tier experts sitting in it."

"Sage Songyang of Songshan Sect, Matron Redleaf of Redleaf Sect, Heavenly Teacher Li of Worship Moon Sect, and the mysterious Burial Life Valley Master, Burial Life Old Man. None of these are easy to deal with. You may be arrogant and conceited on ordinary days, but within Mijie Mountain, do not provoke them."

Faced with this advice, Luo Chen smiled ambiguously and asked back, "Do you think I am arrogant and conceited on ordinary days?"

Ming Lei Cave Master was taken aback.

The next moment, Luo Chen shook his head and chuckled.

"Regardless, I still thank you for your guidance."

Ming Lei Cave Master looked relieved, then continued, "Besides these people, there are some other experts to watch out for, each with astonishing skills. Such as Zheng Guan Daoist, Purple Robed Marquis of the Purple Blood Sect, Luo Sect Left Envoy Bei Puti, the Sword Collection Tower Master, the Huanhua Sect..."

Listening to these names, some familiar, some unfamiliar, along with their respective origins, Luo Chen's dismissive attitude gradually receded.

Since his fierce battle with the three Great Demon Emperors and his personal slaying of Sage Shen Yuan, he had indeed developed a bit of a mindset that looked down on the world's experts.

Even Great Demon Emperors and Grand Cultivators were enough for him to kill. As long as no Ascendants appeared, what did he have to fear?

Even Ascendant Great Powers had died miserably by his sword.

But now, having witnessed the wonder of Sumeru Mountain and considering the Southern Frontier's miraculous use of gu insects, he knew that in areas he did not understand, he should indeed be a bit more careful.

Luo Chen put away his smile and teased, "This 'know-it-all' look of yours doesn't match what you said earlier about having little intelligence and not knowing the winds of the Southern Frontier!"

Ming Lei Cave Master said helplessly, "Back then, I was only focused on my own little patch of Wind Thunder Gorge! And... this is all I can help you with."

After this exchange, Luo Chen fell somewhat silent instead.

Ming Lei Cave Master was definitely not a good person.

This was evident from how he could unhesitatingly sacrifice his loyal subordinates to cultivate his techniques.

But in his dealings with Luo Chen, he had indeed done his utmost.

Clearly, this intelligence had been gathered with great effort by the other party in recent days.

"By the way, are you going to join the Grimpeak Corpse Alliance?"

At the end, Ming Lei Cave Master abruptly asked this question.

Luo Chen asked back, "And you?"

Ming Lei Cave Master waved his hand. "I know my own measure very well. The Grimpeak Corpse Alliance is full of experts. Afterwards, they are going to attack a sacred ground. That's not something I can participate in. As for those sacred ground resources that make Sage experts envious, they are even less something I can covet. So, after attending the exchange meeting for a few days, I plan to quietly slip away."

The way of a wandering cultivator to wisely protect himself was now fully displayed in Ming Lei Cave Master.

Luo Chen pursed his lips slightly and said calmly, "I will join!"

Ming Lei Cave Master frowned, wanting to persuade him, but the words reached his lips and he swallowed them back.

In the end, he only said, "Good luck to you!"

Ming Lei Cave Master left.

Only Luo Chen remained in the room, silently reciting the series of expert names the other party had just uttered.

Would the Devil Heart Gu Emperor be among them?

Whether it was among them or not didn't really matter.

By using this motley great force, the Grimpeak Corpse Alliance, he could also inquire about the other party's whereabouts through its intelligence.

He did not believe that a generation of Sage experts could truly vanish from the world without a trace.

The Grimpeak Corpse Alliance contained most of the forces of the entire Southern Frontier. He would always be able to find some clues about the other party.

Moreover!

Only by joining the Grimpeak Corpse Alliance would he have the chance to stay in this Mijie Mountain and study those stable spatial restrictions.

...

The next day.

Mijie Mountain was still unremarkable.

But one by one, powerful beams of light began to shoot out from various independent spaces, flying towards the summit of Mijie Mountain.

This grand Sage gathering of the Southern Frontier, convened by the Grimpeak Corpse Alliance, Luo Chen happened to be present for it!

(End of this chapter)
