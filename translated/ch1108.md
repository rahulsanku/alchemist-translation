# Chapter 1108: Military Dissolution Reincarnation, Vacuum Gu

Regarding matters of the Luo Sect, Luo Chen's understanding was limited, most of it gathered from hearsay.

However, he was still quite familiar with some of the major events.

Especially the internal strife within the Luo Sect back then, when the Right Envoy of the two envoys, Nan Yiqi, cultivated the Northern Sea demon arts, intending to refine the entire sect alive into a million-strong Baleful Soul Banner capable of contending with Ascendant-level powerhouses.

From Luo Chen's current perspective, Nan Yiqi was simply delusional.

A million-strong Baleful Soul Banner was indeed at the spirit treasure level, but it also depended on who was controlling it and what grade its main soul was. Otherwise, how could it contend with an Ascendant powerhouse?

Hadn't he, Luo Chen, previously refined a million-strong Baleful Soul Banner, with its main soul being a fifth-grade ghost god Kui Ox? Yet it had still been lost to the profound foundations of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

Nan Yiqi, in his delusion, ultimately failed and fled to the Northern Sea.

That internal strife had severely wounded the Southern Frontier's top-tier Luo Sect, forcing their Supreme Elder Meng Shentong to go into seclusion for years to recover from his injuries.

Most importantly, a hereditary Gu insect had been stolen by Nan Yiqi in the process.

Now, using this as a pretext, Old Ghost Nanqi had indeed piqued Luo Chen's interest.

The path of Gu insects was mysterious and wondrous!

Whether it was the evil of the Devil Heart, the pain of a hundred poisons, or the heavenly-earthly fortune connected to the two divine Gu of calamity and blessing, all contained immense opportunities.

Luo Chen had suffered greatly from them, but had also gained considerable benefits.

A portion of his formidable physique was due to the Hundred Poison King Gu!

"Hand over that Gu insect!" Luo Chen stated directly, without any circumlocution.

Old Ghost Nanqi was startled. "You know it's a Gu insect?"

Luo Chen smiled faintly. "For Southern Frontier cultivators, techniques, treasures, talismans, and pills are not that important. Gu insects are the foundation. Since you said it was an important item, it must be a Gu insect."

Old Ghost Nanqi's expression shifted, the fluctuations of his emotions lively and obvious without the cover of a physical body.

He hesitated. "That treasure is indeed a Gu insect, and I can gift it to you, Senior. But Senior must spare my life!"

"Must?" Luo Chen countered, a hint of mockery in his expression.

Old Ghost Nanqi's spirit trembled, and he hurriedly said, "This Gu insect has already been blood-refined by me. If I fall, it will self-destruct along with me. Senior, please do not force the issue."

Luo Chen's lips curled slightly. "I naturally won't force it. It's merely an external object. However..."

Even as he spoke, an object appeared in his hand.

It was a message Gu, given to him by Meng Shentong during their brief exchange back then.

Its purpose was to notify Meng Shentong when he encountered Old Ghost Nanqi.

The moment he saw the message Gu, Old Ghost Nanqi's expression changed drastically.

"What do you think? If I hand you over to Meng Shentong, with the strictness of the Luo Sect's rules, how would he deal with you, a traitor?"

Old Ghost Nanqi couldn't help but tremble.

He was a disciple of the Luo Sect and understood this top Southern Frontier sect better than anyone.

Its secret arts targeting the soul were countless, and there would always be one suitable for dealing with his current state.

Moreover, from Luo Chen's earlier brutal soul-search of Split Heaven Stream Lord, which had revealed his profound soul art mastery, it seemed even Luo Chen alone would be an insurmountable obstacle.

In the silence, Luo Chen's faint voice rang out.

"Behave yourself. Today, you are destined to fall. But even in falling, there are two kinds: utter annihilation of soul and returning to reincarnation. If I am in a good mood, I might allow you to dissolve through military dissolution yourself, giving you hope for reincarnation and future cultivation."

The word "if" was clearly not a promise. How Luo Chen would act depended entirely on Old Ghost Nanqi's own performance.

Amidst the oppression, Old Ghost Nanqi suddenly let out a sigh of relief.

"Forget it. Since I've fallen into your hands today, Senior, I won't put up a stubborn resistance."

"Senior, please look at this object."

"Its name—Vacuum Gu!"

With that, Old Ghost Nanqi's Nascent Soul figurine opened its mouth and spat out a black light.

Luo Chen focused his gaze.

It lay quietly dormant in the air, about the size of a child's thumb, with a mouth but no teeth. Its overall appearance was like pure dark glass that had been stripped of all reflective ability.

But when his divine sense swept over it, he discovered that its surface was not smooth like glass, but rather covered with countless extremely fine folds and grooves. Those folds and grooves, seemingly tiny, gave the impression of a stacked state formed by layers upon layers of ancient ice being crushed.

Sensing the foreign divine sense, the Vacuum Gu suddenly became restless.

Ripples, generated by the writhing of its folds, slowly spread outward.

The entire hall seemed to sink into illusion.

"Quiet!"

Luo Chen let out a low shout, his eyes suddenly changing, becoming red-pupiled with golden irises.

Looking again now, those ripples were clearly manifestations of distorted space.

Luo Chen's heart stirred slightly, and he murmured, "This object actually contains a trace of the true meaning of spatial laws!"

Old Ghost Nanqi looked at Luo Chen in surprise. "Senior's insight is extraordinary. You recognized the essence of this Gu at a single glance. The Vacuum Gu indeed contains a trace of the true meaning of spatial laws. Legend has it that it was born in the boundless void, and when grown to its limit, it can even transform into a world of its own. Our Luo Sect has researched various secret arts and divine abilities based on it, and there is even the theory of the Vacuum Homeland. But unfortunately, over the past few thousand years, the Luo Sect has not produced a single Ascendant powerhouse, and could not nurture this Gu to the fifth grade. Otherwise, the Vacuum Gu's reputation would not be inferior to the three great divine Gu of the Life and Death Gate."

Luo Chen's gaze flickered, landing on Old Ghost Nanqi.

"So, earlier, when you forcibly broke through my blockade and escaped the battlefield, you used a secret art relying on this Gu?"

Old Ghost Nanqi nodded, then smiled bitterly. "Unfortunately, my skills are not refined enough to fully unleash this Gu's power. If our sect's Supreme Elder Meng Shentong had used this Gu, combined with the Void-Breaking Blade, he would have been unmatched among Nascent Soul cultivators, and might even have had a sliver of a chance to escape when facing an Ascendant."

"The Void-Breaking Blade?" Luo Chen silently recited the name. That object was currently sitting safely in his storage ring.

Back during the Netherworld Realm expedition, Meng Shentong had died at the hands of Li Tian Master, and his belongings had fallen to Luo Chen.

At the time, seeing that the Void-Breaking Blade was extraordinary, Luo Chen had carefully stored it away.

He hadn't expected that magic treasure to be used in conjunction with the Vacuum Gu.

No!

Perhaps the Void-Breaking Blade relied on the Vacuum Gu, but the Vacuum Gu didn't necessarily have to be used with the Void-Breaking Blade.

Hadn't Old Ghost Nanqi relied on this Gu to barely escape from Luo Chen's grasp once? Even if he was quickly caught again.

If he himself mastered this Gu, in what direction should he utilize it?

Inexplicably, Luo Chen thought of a divine ability left by Azure Frost—Sleeve Within a Universe!

Seeing Luo Chen lost in thought, Old Ghost Nanqi could only wait quietly.

After a long while, Luo Chen's voice slowly rang out.

"Release the imprint!"

Old Ghost Nanqi took a deep breath, reluctantly striking out streaks of hand seals at the Vacuum Gu.

Soon, a wisp of blood light flew out from the Vacuum Gu's head.

At the same time, Luo Chen flicked his finger, sending out layers of restrictions that landed on it, completely sealing it.

Old Ghost Nanqi only glanced at it, and his spirit tightened.

The restrictions Luo Chen had cast were all the most exquisite spatial restrictions. No wonder he had been caught back after escaping earlier.

In the dao of space, even if both were at the entry stage, there was still a difference in level.

"Senior, the Vacuum Gu has been given to you. Now, about..."

"Not yet!"

Old Ghost Nanqi's expression changed slightly. "Could it be that Senior intends to go back on his word?"

Luo Chen smiled faintly, flicking his finger to send out a gray light.

*Thump!*

The sound of a heavy object hitting the floor echoed through the hall. A small cauldron stood on the ground.

The cauldron was so heavy that even the hard floor was dented.

Most terrifying of all was the hazy light emanating from the gray cauldron. When it leaked slightly, Old Ghost Nanqi felt as if he were being clamped down, his entire Nascent Soul prostrating on the ground.

He stared in shock at the hazy light. "Yuan Magnetic Divine Light?"

Luo Chen shook his head. "I'm not asking you to look at that. Pay attention to what's inside."

As he spoke, he channeled a stream of mana to support Old Ghost Nanqi's Nascent Soul, temporarily granting it freedom of movement.

Suppressing his shock, Old Ghost Nanqi's divine sense entered the Primordial Unity Cauldron and soon detected a strange yet familiar aura.

"A Gu insect?"

"No, just the residual aura of a Gu insect."

"Sigh... what kind of Gu insect is this? Why does even a mere residual aura make my mind chaotic, giving me a feeling of impending great disaster?"

It was a fluttering butterfly.

But it had no physical form, only a shadow, a wisp of aura.

Luo Chen said calmly, "The name of this object should be thunder in your ears. Its name is Tribulation Gu, also known as Calamity Gu. It is one of the three great divine Gu of the Southern Frontier's sacred land, the Life and Death Gate. Before Ascending, Divine Lord Youli took away the Calamity Gu, but left me a wisp of its aura. This object has been stored in my lifebound magic treasure for many years, but I have never been able to use it. Since you are well-versed in the dao of Gu, do you have any insights?"

Upon hearing this, Old Ghost Nanqi was inexplicably shaken. He hadn't expected that the Vacuum Gu he had been so proud of was nothing important in the other's eyes. After all, Luo Chen had once possessed a fifth-grade divine Gu, the famous Calamity Gu. He also hadn't expected Luo Chen to have a connection with the legendary Divine Lord Youli.

Unable to hide his astonishment, Old Ghost Nanqi smiled bitterly. "I'm sorry. With my level, how could I understand the capabilities of such a divine Gu? But according to rumors, the residual aura of this Gu should attract disaster and calamity. That's not a good thing."

Luo Chen frowned. "Attract disaster and calamity? But back then, Divine Lord Youli said that this residual aura would allow me to seek a chance of survival amidst great tribulation?"

Old Ghost Nanqi was stunned, then helpless. "I wouldn't know about that. The abilities of a Divine Lord are far beyond my speculation."

Luo Chen was silent.

The residual aura of the Calamity Gu had been stored in his lifebound magic treasure for a long time.

He clearly remembered the words Divine Lord Youli had spoken before Ascending.

In the future, he might face an unexpected great tribulation, so the other had deliberately intercepted a wisp of the Calamity Gu's aura and placed it in the Primordial Unity Cauldron, so that at a critical moment, it could withstand the great tribulation, even allowing him to escape death.

After leaving the Southern Frontier, he had indeed faced one great tribulation after another.

Whether it was the battle at Broken Soul Cliff, the fierce fight against the crowd in the Western Desert, or even the battle at Heaven-Earth Island, none were ordinary tribulations that ordinary people could escape.

But Luo Chen had also relied on his own abilities to endure them all.

The only problem was that during these great tribulations, the residual aura of the Calamity Gu had never played any role!

It even felt as if these calamities had been attracted by that aura.

Luo Chen didn't believe Divine Lord Youli would harm him, nor did he have any reason to. But why had the residual aura of the Calamity Gu not played any role in these great tribulations?

Could it be that these didn't even count as life-and-death great tribulations?

And now, he, Luo Chen, was at the Ascendant level. In the vast Mountains and Seas Realm, he could walk sideways. What danger could still threaten his life?

"Forget it!"

Unable to find an answer through bitter contemplation, Luo Chen put away the Primordial Unity Cauldron, then turned his gaze to Old Ghost Nanqi.

At that moment, Old Ghost Nanqi also looked over.

Their eyes met, and their intentions were clear.

"Young friend, please!"

Old Ghost Nanqi opened his mouth, then helplessly said, "I take my leave."

He didn't want to die, but he even less wanted to end up like Split Heaven Stream Lord.

Mind unclear, soul scattered, without even the chance to re-enter reincarnation and cultivate anew.

In comparison, Luo Chen giving him a chance to dissolve through military dissolution himself at least preserved a sliver of hope for future cultivation, provided he could awaken his past-life wisdom.

"Without life, without mother, return to the Vacuum Homeland. Return to ultimate bliss, return to dust..."

In a hoarse chant, the Nascent Soul figurine disintegrated inch by inch, and the soul within it gradually dissipated.

The pure mana that constituted the Nascent Soul also returned to pure spiritual energy in the process, giving back to this heaven and earth.

This was different from Split Heaven Stream Lord's Nascent Soul collapse. Old Ghost Nanqi's Nascent Soul disintegration was a collapse caused by the inability to maintain form after extracting all the soul's power. It was not very destructive.

Like snow, one was an avalanche, the other was melting. The effects were also completely different.

"Amitabha."

Kongchan, who had been watching silently from the side, softly intoned a Buddhist chant, his expression quite complex.

He had seen Nascent Soul cultivators fall, and had personally killed some Nascent Soul Sages. But watching a former formidable Nascent Soul colleague dissolve through military dissolution himself still moved him.

If he himself could not break through to the Ascendant level in the future, would he also end up like this?

Or even, he might not be able to do it so gracefully, only able to watch his lifespan run out, his soul weaken until he passed away in meditation?

"Grieving for one's own kind?" Luo Chen smiled. "Then cultivate well. Your soul foundation needs about another hundred years of nurturing, and it should be enough. Then, cultivate the Buddhist scripture I passed on to you, the *Moonlight Pure Heart Mantra*. You might even have a chance to condense your primordial spirit early and sprint for the Ascendant level. If you succeed, you can become a Dharma protector elder of the Luo Tian Sect, enjoying all honors."

Kongchan bowed deeply. "This humble monk will remember Master's teachings!"

"Go now. If you have nothing to do, take care of the surrounding demon beasts. Don't let them disturb the peace here."

"Yes."

After Kongchan left, Luo Chen reached out and summoned two storage pouches.

They were left behind by Split Heaven Stream Lord and Old Ghost Nanqi before their deaths.

With their abilities, they couldn't manage to destroy their own storage magic treasures before dying like Liancheng had, so they could only benefit Luo Chen.

But it was only a small gain.

Luo Chen's divine sense swept over, easily dissolving the spiritual imprints on them, and everything inside came into view.

Split Heaven Stream Lord's storage pouch had the most items, the most miscellaneous, and the styles were all inconsistent.

Clearly, most of these things weren't his own, but obtained by killing others.

For Adept cultivators, they were priceless. Even Nascent Soul Sages would covet them. But for Luo Chen now, they weren't valuable.

Only a few books caught Luo Chen's special attention.

One was a fourth-grade wind-attribute technique, the *Feather Splitting Art*, Split Heaven Stream Lord's lifebound technique, focusing on escape speed. A high-level spell recorded within, the *Heaven-Splitting Divine Claw*, had some similarities with his early specialty, the *Cloud-Seizing Divine Claw*.

One was a fragment of the *Blood God Scripture*, likely from the Blood Sea lineage of the Primordial Demon Sect, recording some methods for refining Blood God Children.

Finally, there was a set of secret arts, the evil method Split Heaven Stream Lord had used to ravage the Northern Sea, massively hunting Adept and Nascent Soul cultivators to enhance his own cultivation.

Luo Chen had already browsed through it in his memories, so he didn't take it, only using it for reference.

Then came Old Ghost Nanqi's storage pouch.

This was quite shabby, completely unworthy of Old Ghost Nanqi's mid-Nascent Soul status.

But considering he had suffered a great calamity, even losing his physical body, how many good things could he have left?

Luo Chen swept his gaze over it and took out a book.

He studied it carefully, and finally, a look of realization appeared on his face.

This book recorded some secret arts matching the Vacuum Gu, as well as techniques and methods for cultivating it.

Gu insects weren't just for use; they also needed to be raised.

After all, they were living creatures. Without food, how could they survive and grow?

Just like the Hundred Poison King Gu, which needed toxins as food.

The Vacuum Gu was very special. Its food was something called [Void Sprout Crystal], which needed to be supplemented long-term for it to continuously grow and advance.

For thousands of years, the Luo Sect had been unable to cultivate the Vacuum Gu to the fifth grade. Apart from having no one in the sect who had achieved the Ascendant level, it was also due to a lack of Void Sprout Crystals.

In the Mountains and Seas Realm, Void Sprout Crystals had long been extinct.

"But it doesn't affect me much. I'm not praying to nurture this Gu to the fifth grade. The fourth grade should already be enough for me to get started on Azure Frost's divine ability."

Luo Chen smiled faintly, his gaze turning to the adjacent room.

Next, while waiting for Cheng Haixin to emerge from seclusion, he wouldn't be bored.

Refining his lifebound magic treasure, cultivating Sleeve Within a Universe—there was always something to do.

"Let me first make the Vacuum Gu recognize me as its master!"

(End of this chapter)
