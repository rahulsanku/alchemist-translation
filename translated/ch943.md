# Chapter 943: Illuminating the Spirit, Entering the Mansion; The Calamity of the Demon Heart

Black King was indeed gifted.

In the earliest days, when Luo Chen had used a large quantity of top-grade Imperial Nectar to nurture him, he had catalyzed a Devouring demon art, allowing him to consume all kinds of flesh and blood, extracting energy beneficial to himself.

Later, after advancing to a desolate beast, he had successively acquired two desolate beast talents: the Indestructible Body and Spirit-Into-Earth.

The former manifested in battle. Even if his limbs were severed or part of his flesh was destroyed, as long as he wasn't instantly obliterated, he could slowly recover. Back in the battle with Azure Frost, Black King had been beaten to a miserable state, yet now he was still bouncing around, full of life.

As for the latter, Luo Chen had always regarded it as merely a tool for nurturing spirit lands and improving the grade of spirit veins.

But now, it seemed he had somewhat overlooked the Spirit-Into-Earth talent.

Whether it was the Falling Cloud Sect's search for spirit wine, or the vast collection of cultivation technique scriptures found within the Five Elements Divine Sect, Black King had displayed an extreme sensitivity to places rich in spiritual qi.

And now, he had brought back another good thing!

"Perhaps I should pay more attention to related resources in the future and dig deeper into Black King's talents in this area."

After all, a man can never have too many skills!

The same went for Black King. It wasn't as if he had to fight every day. He couldn't just eat and sleep, then eat and sleep again.

Having another skill wouldn't disgrace his status as Luo Chen's number one spirit pet.

Luo Chen muttered to himself, then began to study the "good thing."

The Illuminating Spirit Gu!

An unspoken secret of the Illuminating Shadow Sect.

And clearly, the grade of this Illuminating Spirit Gu that Black King had found was not low.

Looking at the dormant gu insect, which resembled a caterpillar, Luo Chen used his limited knowledge of the gu path to carefully distinguish its features.

Those black lines and rings were not patterns, but wrinkles.

The more there were, the older it was.

"Even if it's not a mother gu, it's at least an adult, enough for a Sage-level powerhouse to use."

"But as for how to actually utilize it, I'll need to study it further."

Luo Chen pursed his lips and began flipping through the other scriptures in his storage pouch.

Faced with the densely packed texts, Luo Chen couldn't help but cast another appreciative glance at Black King.

This fellow knew he loved to read widely and collect resources, so he would always deliberately sweep through everything when gathering.

What a clever mind!

With a smile, Luo Chen sank his mind into the texts.

...

A flying boat sped from east to south.

Minglei Cave Master stood on the bow deck, facing the wind, his expression complex.

At this moment, he was the one controlling the flying boat with his mana and divine sense.

A dignified Sage-level powerhouse, reduced to piloting a boat for someone else—who wouldn't feel complicated about that?

But this was the Overlord Cultivator Qi Tian!

Not to mention the formidable strength he had displayed, the mere fact that he had saved his life earlier meant he had to show some gratitude.

A few words of thanks wouldn't demonstrate sincerity.

And the other party didn't care about various resources.

So, he could only express himself through actions.

But thinking back to the terrifying power Qi Tian had shown in those two battles, Minglei Cave Master couldn't help but wonder.

"Is body cultivation really that powerful?"

In the world of immortal cultivation, body cultivators, or physical refiners, were a long-declined path.

Even though the Southern Frontier, compared to the other four continents, had preserved the dao of body refinement relatively intact, not many people took it seriously.

At most, before reaching the Sage stage, it was used as an auxiliary method to strengthen the physical body.

After all, when forming a Sage, one needed to balance the three treasures of essence, qi, and spirit. If the physical body was too weak, it couldn't withstand the process of shattering the core and forming the Sage.

But the power Luo Chen had repeatedly displayed had completely exceeded Minglei Cave Master's imagination.

In truth, this was a case of a leaf blocking his view.

Luo Chen's strength was not merely a matter of his body refinement realm.

His Crimson-Gold Pupils allowed him to ignore illusions and concealment techniques, and to find flaws in his opponent's combat methods.

His Nether-Purging Soul Art granted him powerful soul defense capabilities, making him utterly unafraid of the soul attacks that body cultivators feared most.

With these two points, combined with his terrifying physique, Luo Chen was an indestructible weapon of attack.

Moreover, when it came to the richness of combat experience, how many Sages in the world could compare to Luo Chen?

Leaving aside Minglei Cave Master's burgeoning thoughts on body cultivation, Luo Chen had already begun to make some headway in his research on the Illuminating Spirit Gu.

Inside the cabin.

Luo Chen held a book, reading it carefully.

After a long while, he put down the book titled *A Hundred Solutions to Gu Insects*, lost in thought.

"To put it simply, the function of the Illuminating Spirit Gu is clearly that of a monitoring instrument!"

According to the introduction in *A Hundred Solutions to Gu Insects*, which the Illuminating Shadow Sect had collected, the Illuminating Spirit Gu was different from ordinary gu insects that assisted cultivation or increased mana and soul power. It did not directly enhance a cultivator's strength.

However, it was indeed helpful for finding and filling gaps in one's cultivation.

Once refined into the body, the Illuminating Spirit Gu could clearly perceive changes in the body.

And this refinement was not like ordinary magical artifacts or pills, which were refined with mana. Instead, it was purely refined with the soul.

In this way, the Illuminating Spirit Gu's memories and perceptions would be sensed by the host afterward, due to the soul connection.

Cultivation could be said to be a slow, grinding task, but also a meticulous one.

Most people were muddled. As long as their realm could improve, their cultivation was generally acceptable.

But to walk steadily through each realm and lay a perfect foundation, one needed to be meticulous to the extreme.

The Illuminating Spirit Gu was specifically used to make up for the lack of detail.

After each cultivation session, the host could observe the changes throughout their body during the process through the Illuminating Spirit Gu, understanding not only the "what" but also the "why," rather than glossing over things in a sloppy manner.

"Interesting..."

Luo Chen murmured to himself.

Then, he made up his mind.

He would refine this Illuminating Spirit Gu!

All along, Luo Chen's foundations in each realm had been solid.

But in truth, many times, he himself didn't understand the peculiar changes during the cultivation process. He merely followed the paths of his predecessors, muddling through.

With this gu insect's assistance, he could comprehend the essence of cultivation. In the future, he could constantly adjust the details of his cultivation and also clarify the direction of his future practice.

The only thing he needed to worry about was how to refine it.

Because he didn't have the specific method for refining the Illuminating Spirit Gu from the Illuminating Shadow Sect.

But for this, Luo Chen had his own solution!

He glanced outside, estimating the time in his mind.

The journey to Mijie Mountain was long.

Originally, he had planned to fly at full speed with Minglei Cave Master to get there quickly.

But because he needed to study the gu insect, they had switched to the flying boat.

This naturally slowed them down.

But it also gave him plenty of time.

"Before we reach Mijie Mountain, I should have enough time to refine this Illuminating Spirit Gu."

After making this calculation, Luo Chen calmed his mind, squeezed a drop of essence blood from his fingertip, and let it fall onto the caterpillar-like fleshy insect.

As the blood entered its body, the dormant Illuminating Spirit Gu immediately awakened.

The rings of black wrinkles began to writhe, and its eyes, resembling insect compound eyes, opened in confusion, its first glance falling on Luo Chen.

"Come in!"

Luo Chen opened his mouth and inhaled, swallowing the gu insect in one gulp.

The gu insect entered his body and headed straight for his Purple Mansion.

During this process, the Illuminating Spirit Gu also became active, frequently sending out its consciousness to scan Luo Chen's entire body.

But the moment it entered the Purple Mansion, it froze.

A small, blue-white tree stretched out its branches, emitting a slow flame.

Not only that, but a bird resembling a phoenix curiously flew towards it.

Its posture seemed as if it wanted to eat it.

The Illuminating Spirit Gu was greatly alarmed and hurriedly crawled deeper inside.

Suddenly, it saw a small gray cauldron standing quietly there, as if it could suppress everything in the world.

This would be a good refuge.

The Illuminating Spirit Gu instinctively crawled towards the small gray cauldron.

However, before it could even climb onto the cauldron's leg, it was struck as if by lightning and scrambled back in panic.

Inside the small cauldron, there was something even more terrifying!

Moreover, the natural hierarchical suppression from its own kind was far greater than the fear the fire tree and fire bird had given it.

For a moment, the Illuminating Spirit Gu was filled with panic, fear, and confusion.

Such a vast Purple Mansion, yet there was no place for it to stand!

Just then!

A chubby, oversized hand descended from the sky, grabbed it in one go, and instantly refined it into the body.

The Sage—the fusion product of the three treasures of essence, qi, and spirit—was also the second dwelling place of the soul, besides the physical body.

Luo Chen's main soul was currently inside it. After grabbing the cowering and fearful Illuminating Spirit Gu, he began to use the most basic method to slowly refine the gu insect's own consciousness with his soul.

This process could not be slow.

If it was too slow, the Illuminating Spirit Gu would react and resist him.

But it also could not be fast.

If it was too fast, Luo Chen's powerful soul would instantly destroy this fragile gu insect.

If that happened, all his efforts would be wasted.

Time slowly passed during this process of refining the gu insect.

...

Half a year later.

The wind howled outside the boat.

Inside the boat, it was silent.

*Gulp.*

A mouthful of wine entered his stomach, and the conversation between the two inside the boat immediately opened up.

"The calamity of the Demon Heart Gu, strictly speaking, erupted five hundred years ago."

"But the actual time when the Demon Heart Gu appeared was even earlier. Legend has it that it was the product of a powerful gu master researching a fifth-grade gu insect, the Life-and-Death Gu, based on ancient texts."

"At the time, people discovered that this gu was extremely perfect. Once refined, it could greatly increase the efficiency of refining essence into qi."

"Brother Qi should understand that our cultivation essentially follows two paths. One is to absorb and refine spiritual qi from the outside world, and the other is to convert mana from the qi and blood of the physical body."

"The former is very cumbersome. It requires a corresponding grade of spirit land, a decent soul, and a good cultivation technique. There are too many restrictions."

"But refining essence into qi is relatively simpler. Whether it's training the physique, dietary supplements, or medicinal supplements, they can all have immediate effects."

"Moreover, the dao of body refinement has always been passed down in the Southern Frontier. Therefore, after the Demon Heart Gu appeared, it immediately became the fastest method to improve one's immortal cultivation realm!"

After more than half a year of refining the gu insect, Luo Chen could finally spare some time.

The conversation topic that had been interrupted earlier was brought up again by him.

Minglei Cave Master had long been prepared for this matter, so he narrated it unhurriedly, with almost no interruptions.

It was under his narration that Luo Chen learned just how lively the Southern Frontier had been back then when the Demon Heart Gu appeared.

It was like some kind of top-grade pill being released. The appearance of the Demon Heart Gu had also caused a huge uproar.

In fact, because of the lack of restrictions on its conditions, the impact it brought was even more frenzied than the restrictions of pill grades!

Perhaps the only threshold that prevented the Demon Heart Gu from spreading to every cultivator was its exorbitant price!

"I was still young back then. As a rogue cultivator, I had no spare assets, so naturally, I couldn't afford the spirit stones to buy a Demon Heart Gu."

"But that also happened to save me from a great catastrophe!"

Speaking of this, Minglei Cave Master's face was full of lingering fear and emotion.

Luo Chen was curious: "What catastrophe?"

Minglei Cave Master asked in return: "Brother Qi, do you know about hierarchical suppression?"

Luo Chen nodded. "This common knowledge is natural. High-grade demon beasts suppress low-grade ones. A powerful cultivator, even without making a move, can naturally instill fear in the weak. Is it the same for gu insects?"

"Naturally, it is the same for gu insects, but the suppression of the Demon Heart Gu is even more severe!"

Minglei Cave Master said this, then changed the subject.

"Let's not talk about hierarchical suppression for now. Let's just talk about the attributes of mana! Although the mana refined using the Demon Heart Gu would have different attributes due to the cultivator's spirit root and constitution, people later discovered that there was still a commonality within these differences!"

"Commonality?" Luo Chen seemed to think of something.

Minglei Cave Master nodded. "Yes, someone discovered that between two different cultivators who had both refined the Demon Heart Gu, their mana could actually be absorbed and converted through each other's Demon Heart Gu. If their realms were similar, they could even achieve near-lossless absorption! The catastrophe also arose from this!"

Luo Chen thought of himself.

Although he had never refined a Demon Heart Gu, he had relied on his powerful physical qi and blood to forcibly nurture a low-grade Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu to the level of a third-grade Demon Heart Gu.

Later, when his physical body underwent nirvana, the Demon Heart Gu died along with it.

But half of its mana had been mysteriously transmitted to Huiniang, who was billions of miles away.

Luo Chen didn't know the mystery behind this transmission. He only attributed it to the laws of heaven and earth.

But the fact that Sima Huiniang could receive his mana was evidence of this.

The only flaw was probably that Huiniang's own physique was insufficient, so she hadn't been able to nurture the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu on her body to the level of a third-grade Demon Heart Gu. This was why she couldn't fully convert the overbearing fire-attribute mana he had transmitted.

It was only through the slow evolution afterward that the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu had advanced to the third grade and become too deeply bound to Huiniang.

Beside him, Minglei Cave Master's voice continued to come slowly.

"After someone discovered this, endless bloodshed erupted in the Southern Frontier's immortal cultivation world."

"To seek rapid improvement in their cultivation, many people chose a dangerous path that was even faster than a shortcut. They began to hunt down other cultivators who had also refined the Demon Heart Gu on a large scale, converting their mana into their own. Later, they didn't even need to distinguish; they would just kill first and ask questions later."

"At that time, I was still in the Qi Refinement stage, living in constant fear every day. I wished I could carve the words 'I haven't refined the Demon Heart Gu' on my forehead, afraid that someone would make a move on me."

Luo Chen couldn't help but ask: "With such chaos, didn't the Holy Land intervene?"

Minglei Cave Master nodded, then quickly shook his head.

"They did intervene, but only nominally. After purging the Demon Heart cultivators within their own ranks, they only issued a single decree to the outside world, forbidding the further spread of the Demon Heart Gu in the Southern Frontier."

"What truly calmed this catastrophe was the appearance of an old Sage."

"Wherever he passed, any cultivator who had refined the Demon Heart Gu, regardless of their realm, had no power to resist. All their mana was harvested by him."

"It was only then that people realized that besides being able to convert mana of different attributes, the Demon Heart Gu also possessed a natural hierarchical suppression of the same gu. In front of a fourth-grade Demon Heart Gu, no matter what realm you had cultivated to, even if you were also a Sage, as long as your own Demon Heart Gu's grade was insufficient, you could only become fish on the chopping block, at the mercy of the slaughterer."

"And that old monster was called the Demon Heart Gu Emperor!"

Luo Chen's expression changed slightly.

Demon Heart Gu Emperor. This name had finally appeared!

But he wasn't in a hurry, because it was very likely not the one he was looking for.

Sure enough, as Minglei Cave Master continued his story, Luo Chen finally got the information he wanted.

"Such a terrifying existence naturally angered many Sage-level powerhouses in the Southern Frontier. After all, anyone who could achieve the Sage stage back then wouldn't be a loner."

"They also had disciples and descendants, bloodline heirs. Among these juniors, there would always be those who sought a quick and convenient path, refining the Demon Heart Gu to accelerate their cultivation."

"The mutual hunting was still a matter of resource competition. But the appearance of the Demon Heart Gu Emperor was like a harvest. No one could sit idly by and watch their own juniors become the nourishment for someone else's advancement."

"A war to besiege and kill the Demon Heart Gu Emperor thus began!"

Speaking of this, Minglei Cave Master visibly breathed a sigh of relief.

He smiled. "Under that level of pursuit, the Demon Heart Gu Emperor no longer dared to be arrogant and could only act in secret. Then, one day, a message came from somewhere, saying that the Demon Heart Gu Emperor was dead."

"Dead?" Luo Chen raised an eyebrow.

Minglei Cave Master nodded slightly. "Yes, dead. And he died in the most unexpected way. He was backlashed. A low-level cultivator who had also refined the Demon Heart Gu overcame him from below and backlashed him."

"Who was that person, and where are they?"

Minglei Cave Master spread his hands. "I don't know. No one knows. That person was very mysterious, and their way of doing things was different from the old Gu Emperor. They never launched a harvest against others and quietly disappeared from the Southern Frontier's immortal cultivation world."

Luo Chen opened his mouth, then finally said slowly: "A cautious one."

Minglei Cave Master smiled. "Maybe it wasn't caution, but fear. After all, the scale of the pursuit against the Demon Heart Gu Emperor back then was too great. Dozens of top-tier faction experts had joined forces. I'm afraid even the Holy Land would find it hard to bear such wrath."

Luo Chen also smiled, but he was smiling at Minglei Cave Master's overly simplistic thinking. The Holy Land's foundation was enough to suppress an entire continent.

Without the intervention of external forces, even if all the powerhouses of the Southern Frontier gathered together, they wouldn't be enough for the Life and Death Gate's Ancestor to kill alone.

He was just curious. Where exactly had that new Demon Heart Gu Emperor gone?

"Later, with the disappearance of the Demon Heart Gu Emperor, the various forces in the Southern Frontier turned their spears against the remaining Demon Heart Gu cultivators. Some were hunted, some were imprisoned, and various secret methods were used to try to remove or suppress the gu."

"Anyway, under the calls and decrees of the various families, the Demon Heart Gu, a cultivation tool that was like cheating, gradually disappeared from the history of the Southern Frontier."

A catastrophe, a piece of history, was narrated from the perspective of a bystander.

This was also the first time Luo Chen had a complete understanding of this history.

The information useful to him wasn't much, but he had still managed to capture some.

Such as the habits and effects of the Demon Heart Gu, and... the personality of that new Gu Emperor!

"Where would he hide?"

"He couldn't have already died of old age, could he?"

Amidst these speculations, Luo Chen's heart suddenly stirred.

"Brother Minglei, I'll take my leave for now. I have some cultivation matters to attend to."

"Go ahead!"

Minglei Cave Master said this, watching Luo Chen enter the cabin again.

There was a hint of envy in his eyes. The other party was already so strong, yet he was still constantly improving himself, not even relaxing during the journey.

In comparison, after he himself had achieved the Sage stage, he had instead become somewhat lax.
