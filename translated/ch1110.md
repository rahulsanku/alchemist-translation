# Chapter 1110: Life and Death Opens Again, Realm Mountain Enters the Cauldron

Southern Frontier, the Life and Death Gate had opened once more!

Nearly two hundred years had passed since the Fierce Mountain Corpse Alliance joined forces with the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect to attack the Life and Death Gate.

In those two centuries, tales of Lord Youli repelling the great army and ascending in broad daylight still circulated throughout the Southern Frontier cultivation world. Countless cultivators held this—the Southern Frontier's only Ascendant sacred land—in great awe.

It was an existence that, even after being sealed for centuries, still possessed terrifying foundations.

Some even said that in the current Mountains and Seas Realm, where most sacred lands had either been destroyed or suffered severe losses, the perpetually sealed Life and Death Gate might be the entity with the most intact strength.

Now that the sacred land's gates were wide open, naturally, many cultivators paid close attention.

Yet unexpectedly, after opening its gates, the Life and Death Gate made no major moves. Not even attempts to reclaim former territories and industries were seen.

A piece of gossip spread like wildfire from a faction that had good relations with the Life and Death Gate.

According to rumor, this emergence of the Southern Frontier sacred land was to welcome a single person.

Opening the gates, with cultivators lined up to greet—all to show respect!

Just what kind of existence would that person be?

Amidst countless speculations, within the Scripture Pavilion of the Life and Death Gate, Grand Elder Zi Yan Sage respectfully followed beside a white-robed Daoist, speaking in a low voice.

"The calamity gu is indeed the tribulation gu, but the world has many misunderstandings about this gu."

"This gu appears to invite disaster and calamity, but in essence, it tempers its host through misfortune. Each time one passes a tribulation's test, the host receives tremendous improvement. Especially those natural and earthly disasters that contain the creation of heaven and earth—during the tribulation process, the host can most directly and clearly comprehend the ultimate principles of the Dao within."

"If one retreats in the face of difficulty, only then does the calamity gu become a true great tribulation!"

"The opposite is the fortune gu. On the surface, it brings many blessings, but everything has already been marked with a price. Take our sect's already-ascended Supreme Elder, Lord Youli. Because of a moment's greed, he caused the origin qi ocean to sink, nearly destroying the entire Southern Frontier cultivation world with a spiritual qi tidal surge. Fortunately, the Supreme Elder was a rare genius who used this to condense his Law Manifestation and ascend in broad daylight."

"Senior need not worry about that trace of residual aura from the calamity gu. Such a faint aura will not bring you much disaster. On the contrary, in desperate situations, it might instead bring you a thread of life. After all, fortune relies on disaster, and disaster relies on fortune."

"I imagine this is also the reason behind the Supreme Elder's arrangements before his ascension!"

The white-robed Daoist stopped in his tracks, the book in his hands spread open.

He wore a thoughtful expression, murmuring the phrase "fortune relies on disaster, and disaster relies on fortune" to himself. Finally, the corners of his mouth lifted slightly as he closed the book titled *Strange Gu Discussions*.

In this realm, as long as he didn't encounter the top-tier Great Desolate Mourning Wasteland, who could bring him calamity?

Lord Youli's good intentions had been wasted.

The white-robed Daoist handed *Strange Gu Discussions* back, but unexpectedly, Zi Yan Sage hurriedly declined.

"Since Senior Luo is interested in the Dao of gu insects, please accept this book as a gift."

The one speaking face-to-face was none other than Luo Chen.

He smiled faintly. "Since that's the case, I shall not refuse."

Then, Luo Chen's tone shifted abruptly as he asked about another matter.

"Regarding Realm Mountain, how much does your sect know?"

Zi Yan Sage asked, "Is Senior referring to the former headquarters of the Fierce Mountain Corpse Alliance?"

"Exactly." Luo Chen nodded.

Zi Yan Sage pondered for a moment before speaking slowly. "Realm Mountain has been known in the Southern Frontier for a long time. It is a great wonder. Once inside, the spaces are layered upon layer, as if three thousand worlds converge within a single mountain. If one is not careful, it is very easy to get lost. Moreover, when a cultivator's Sage escapes, it should be able to shrink the earth to an inch and flee a thousand li in a single step. But within Realm Mountain, it is greatly suppressed and cannot flee far. Some speculate that this mountain was transformed from a great cultivator's spirit treasure, or formed from the remains of an ancient desolate beast. But more believe it is the result of heaven and earth's creation—a half-formed grotto-heaven."

Luo Chen chuckled lightly. "This information is known to all the world. I am also aware of it. Tell me something I don't know!"

Zi Yan Sage looked at Luo Chen in surprise, then recalled a certain legend.

Once upon a time, a body-cultivation rogue cultivator named Ba Xiu Qi Tian had joined the Fierce Peak Corpse Alliance. This person's origins were mysterious and unfathomable, and he had disappeared without a trace.

Combined with the circumstances of the Supreme Elder's ascension, could it be that this Luo Chen was that Ba Xiu Qi Tian?

He hesitated for a moment, then still spoke. "The true origin of Realm Mountain is exactly what most people speculate. It is a grotto-heaven that existed when heaven and earth first opened and the Mountains and Seas Realm was born. According to ancient records, heaven and earth were like a chicken egg, a single chaos. After creation, the clear qi rose, the turbid qi sank, and finally formed the bright universe. But in this process, there were some peculiar places that, under the pressure of chaos's opening, did not separate into clear and turbid, but instead overlapped. Realm Mountain is such an existence."

Luo Chen seemed to understand. "Clear and turbid never separated, chaos still remains—so that's why Realm Mountain is so peculiar?"

He recalled that year he spent in Realm Mountain.

Aside from cultivating the *Hundred Refinements Body Art*, most of his time was spent observing the ubiquitous spatial restrictions within Realm Mountain—and those were the externally manifested spatial restrictions.

It was clearly a massive overlapping of spaces that caused the spatial restrictions to manifest naturally.

It was precisely through that year of observation, combined with his Crimson Eye Golden Pupil, that he was able to gain an advantage in his subsequent battle with Azure Frost.

Listening to the Life and Death Gate Grand Elder's explanation now, it indeed matched that description.

"But such an existence should be extremely unstable, shouldn't it?"

Zi Yan Sage nodded. "Senior is wise. Realm Mountain is indeed unstable. That's why it appeared and disappeared unpredictably in the Southern Frontier cultivation world, elusive for thousands of years without anyone finding its roots."

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow. "Then why was it able to stabilize later?"

Zi Yan Sage explained, "This is related to the mutation brought about by our sect's Supreme Elder's cultivation. At that time, the origin qi ocean sank, and the spiritual qi tidal surge was about to erupt. The immense external pressure forced a half-formed grotto-heaven like Realm Mountain to accelerate its formation. The Supreme Elder detected this and secretly acted, using great divine abilities to stabilize Realm Mountain's position in the real world. He originally planned to subdue this mountain after emerging from seclusion to enrich the sect's foundation. But the Supreme Elder's ascension was too hasty, leaving behind this loose end."

Speaking thus, he couldn't help but feel regret.

"That was a good place, but now it's been given cheaply to a bunch of rogue cultivators."

After Lord Youli defeated the allied forces of the Fierce Mountain Corpse Alliance and Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect withdrew from the Southern Frontier, and the Fierce Mountain Corpse Alliance subsequently collapsed, no longer daring to exist openly.

Naturally, Realm Mountain, which had served as the Fierce Corpse Alliance's headquarters, was left vacant.

Some rogue cultivators seized the opportunity to occupy the mountain peaks and use them as cave dwellings.

Luo Chen probed, "Now that the Fierce Mountain Corpse Alliance has disbanded and the Southern Frontier is still dominated by your sect alone, why not take the opportunity to claim Realm Mountain?"

Zi Yan Sage smiled bitterly. "Realm Mountain is not actually important to us. What matters is that the Life and Death Gate has been sealed for too long, and the Southern Frontier's reverence for us has long since faded. Although the Fierce Corpse Stiff Alliance has disbanded, various factions have fortified their foundations and secretly colluded. Forget Realm Mountain—even our sect wanting to reclaim some territories and industries has been somewhat constrained, for fear of provoking another great war. Of course, the most fundamental reason is that our sect lacks an Ascendant great power capable of subduing this mountain."

"Is that so?" Luo Chen roughly understood the Life and Death Gate's current predicament.

In terms of strength, the Life and Death Gate was undoubtedly the strongest force in the Southern Frontier, and even across the five continents of the Mountains and Seas Realm, its strength was among the most intact of the sacred lands.

But with the precedent of the Southern Frontier's various factions jointly forming the Fierce Mountain Corpse Alliance, and now without an Ascendant great power to sit in charge, the Life and Death Gate could not easily pursue a hegemonic path.

Luo Chen paused in his steps, leaning against the window, his gaze cast upon the scene outside the Scripture Pavilion.

Within the Life and Death Gate's mountain gate, which had rested and recuperated for centuries, vitality flourished, with many disciples—a sign of rising momentum.

Zi Yan Sage, standing behind, vaguely sensed something. He tentatively asked, "Senior, could it be that you are interested in Realm Mountain?"

Luo Chen did not conceal it and spoke bluntly, "I am refining a precious treasure. It has ample heaviness but insufficient agility. Realm Mountain naturally contains countless spaces and abundant spatial restrictions; it is itself a moving grotto-heaven. If I can refine this mountain into it, I can make up for the defect. However, the Southern Frontier is ultimately your sect's territory. Because of Lord Youli's sake, I do not wish to take without asking."

Hearing this, Zi Yan Sage instead felt relieved.

He sighed with a smile and said, "Senior need not mind. As I just said, Realm Mountain is not important to us now. If you wish to take it, simply take it yourself."

Luo Chen, his hand resting on the window sill, said solemnly, "Since that's the case, I shall not refuse. However..."

Speaking thus, he turned around and looked seriously at Zi Yan Sage.

"Today, your sect has opened wide the door of convenience for me, granting me whatever I ask. I am not one to accept favors for nothing. I will help you with a small favor!"

Zi Yan Sage's eyes lit up. Could it be that...

"As you think, my name can be lent to you for use. But how you handle the aftermath in the future will depend on your sect's methods."

"That is enough. Zi Yan thanks Senior!"

...

The Southern Frontier had changed!

Or rather, the sky over the Southern Frontier had never truly changed—it had always been overshadowed by the Life and Death Gate.

Only this time, in the absence of Lord Youli, the Southern Frontier had gained another great backing.

A great power from the Eastern Wilderness, crossing continents, had put out word that he would assist the Life and Death Gate in reclaiming lost territories. Anyone who dared to resist would be killed without mercy!

Under this declaration, those stubborn factions that had been clinging together for warmth collapsed one after another.

And the Life and Death Gate, having rested and recuperated for centuries, emerged in full force, displaying its complete strength to the world.

The territories, industries, and even vassals that had once been lost were reclaimed one by one.

Thus, the sacred land's momentum gradually recovered.

The only thing that surprised people was probably the current rogue cultivator sacred land of the Southern Frontier—Realm Mountain!

The Life and Death Gate's cultivators surrounded it but did not attack, merely ordering the rogue cultivators inside to withdraw.

Such gentle methods gave rise to improper thoughts in some, who only thought that the Life and Death Gate was wary and afraid of arousing public anger.

Therefore, many high-level cultivators inside instead lingered, unwilling to leave, trying to bargain with the Life and Death Gate.

The leader among them was a demonic giant.

His name was Ning Yuzhuo, known to the world as the Demon Buddha!

This person cultivated both demonic and Buddhist arts, rising to fame in the Southern Frontier two hundred years ago and performing excellently during the attack on the Life and Death Gate.

He was now at the fifth layer of the Sage realm, with formidable strength that allowed him to protect himself even against late-stage Great Sages.

Such formidable strength was his confidence.

As long as the Life and Death Gate did not agree to his conditions, he would not leave Realm Mountain.

If they had the ability, then come in and fight!

Everyone knew that Realm Mountain contained countless spatial restrictions, and cultivators simply did not dare to fight freely inside.

Ning Yuzhuo was confident that even if a Great Sage entered, he could make them return in defeat!

But this confidence did not last long.

On this day.

Over Realm Mountain, the wind and clouds suddenly changed, and spiritual qi fell into chaos.

The entire space trembled, as if some existence was trying to forcibly squeeze in.

Tens of thousands of cultivators walked out of their cave dwellings, looking at the sky in shock.

Ning Yuzhuo was among them, his face solemn as he looked at the sky.

Two streaks of light slowly flew over from nearby, landing before him with worried expressions.

"Brother Ning, has the Life and Death Gate made a move?"

"It should be." Ning Yuzhuo's expression was uncertain.

Another Sage asked in confusion, "How dare they? Realm Mountain exists within spatial turbulence, with unstable origin qi. There is only one external passage. If they don't enter through the passage, how can they enter this realm?"

Ning Yuzhuo said in a low voice, "No matter how turbulent the origin qi, what if someone can control the heaven and earth's origin qi?"

The two were stunned.

Ning Yuzhuo said despondently, "It should be that great power from the Eastern Wilderness personally taking action."

"This..."

"What do we do?"

The three Sages looked at each other, not knowing how to respond.

Just then.

In the azure sky, a crack split open.

It was cut open by a sword!

Then, two large hands grabbed the edges of the crack and pulled them apart.

*Rip...*

An ear-piercing sound spread through the ears of all cultivators within the mountain.

Those with low cultivation were immediately knocked unconscious.

Even those with profound cultivation like the Demon Buddha Ning Yuzhuo felt unsettled.

Then, they saw a hand reach in from the crack.

The palm was flat, facing upward, and a gray light flew out with a spin.

The gray light was extremely small at first, but it grew with the wind, instantly becoming thousands of feet in size.

Three legs, two ears, a round belly.

It was actually a gray great cauldron!

The great cauldron hung in the air, emitting a hazy light. Streams of magnetic divine light hung down like bead curtains, thousands of strands, enveloping the entire Realm Mountain.

"If you do not wish to die, do not resist. Enter my sleeve!"

A voice like a great bell rang out, drilling into everyone's ears. Even those who were unconscious could hear it.

Ning Yuzhuo sighed. "Such a precious land, after all, is not something someone like me can possess!"

He gave up resistance, letting the great sleeve's suction pull him in.

The suction was so great that even his full magical power could not resist it.

Before being taken in, Ning Yuzhuo couldn't help but sigh, "Such heaven-reaching methods—he could have just taken everyone away directly. Why bother with kind words? This Ascendant great power has a good temper."

Once inside the sleeve, he felt heaven and earth darken, and his thoughts slowed.

In a chaotic state, Ning Yuzhuo felt the world turn upside down and suddenly woke up.

*Plop!*

*Plop!*

*Plop!*

Tens of thousands of rogue cultivators fell like dumplings into the Nine Haze City.

In their daze, everyone looked at the sky.

A white-robed Daoist was slowly withdrawing his right hand. In his palm, a small cauldron emitting a strange light was clearly appearing.

The small cauldron seemed to be in this realm, yet also as if it would break through the air and fly away at any moment.

Seeing this, the world knew that Realm Mountain had fallen into the hands of the Eastern Wilderness great power!

"Just who is this senior?"

"Such great means of breaking through the void and moving mountains is truly beyond imagination!"

"Seeing his appearance and his gentle, tolerant attitude toward us rogue cultivators, could he be the rumored Eastern Wilderness Alchemy Sect?"

Discussions rang out throughout the Nine Haze Immortal City.

Ning Yuzhuo was also among them, but for some reason, as he looked up at the white-robed Daoist's figure, he felt a hint of familiarity.

"Have I seen this person before?"

...

"Congratulations, Senior, on acquiring a precious mountain and enhancing your magic treasure's power!"

On a mountain peak, Zi Yan Sage offered his sincere congratulations.

Luo Chen shook his head with a smile. "Not enough. Just as my cauldron has three legs, to achieve the stability of a tripod, I still need to supplement it with another top-grade spiritual mountain that can match the Magnetic Divine Mountain and Realm Mountain."

Zi Yan Sage was stunned.

The Magnetic Divine Mountain was the gateway to the Northern Sea sacred land, the Primordial Demon Sect. Realm Mountain was formed at the beginning of heaven and earth's creation. What could compare to these two? In the world, there were very few.

As far as he knew, there might be a handful left in the world.

Such as the perilous and unpredictable Soul-Breaking Cliff, the Feather Clan's sacred land Green Parasol Mountain, the Demon Clan great power Great Sage Beili's dwelling at Suspended Blade Peak, or the Jambu Mountain that once hung high in the nine heavens but eventually fell into the Nether Abyss.

He just didn't know which famous mountain Luo Chen had his eye on next.

But with Luo Chen's strength, most of them were within easy reach.

Following Luo Chen's gaze toward the north, Zi Yan Sage's heart stirred.

"Could it be Suspended Blade Peak?"

"No!"

"He went to the Northern Sea first. There's no reason to turn back. This direction..."

Zi Yan Sage's expression froze, and he already had a guess.
