# Chapter 746: The Rotten Axe-Handle Spirit Treasure; Illusion Arts Reveal Their Power

After arriving in Central Continent, many things overturned some of Luo Chen's views.

For example, experts were as numerous as clouds on this continent, and clans stood everywhere.

Sages, once rarely seen, were everywhere.

He convinced himself with the reason that Central Continent gathered the essence of the Mountains and Seas Realm.

But some of the high-level cultivators here also often made him inwardly criticize them.

They clearly had profound Adept and Sage realms, yet whether in dealing with people, handling affairs, or bearing and demeanor, they were far from the level of high-level cultivators outside Central Continent.

Immature!

These two words were Luo Chen's evaluation of some of Central Continent's "young" high-level cultivators.

Later, he gradually understood why this situation existed.

Central Continent's cultivators had enjoyed long peace. Their temperaments were comparatively free and detached. Even if they competed, it was often placed upon a go board of limited space. They were unlike a "mud-legged peasant" like Luo Chen, born with no relation to clear wind and bright moon, whose life had always been accompanied by flashing blades, sword shadows, blood rain, and foul wind.

The cultivators of Central Continent were like immortals from storybooks, free of calamity, struggle, and hardship, cultivating at ease.

But because they lacked tempering, these people had realms without corresponding bearing.

Luo Chen did not know whether this cultivation mode was ultimately good or bad for cultivators, but in the end, he could no longer experience it. If he wanted to climb to this realm's peak, he had to take each step solidly.

Of course, Luo Chen also knew that these noble geniuses of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect were not without merits.

They possessed outstanding aptitude, and from childhood, they were in an atmosphere of internal competition among sect and clans, so their temperaments were not poor.

Add to that the foundation of profound realms!

In the future, with only a little tempering, unpolished jade could shine and reveal the bearing a Sage ought to have!

This was what Luo Chen thought.

Therefore, even though he looked down in his heart on Fu Qinglan and other Sages being ranked equal with him in the honor of Sage, he had never shown it on his face.

But!

That was based on the other party truly being a piece of unpolished jade.

Now, when Luo Chen glimpsed those fingers unconsciously twisting together, his brows could not help furrowing.

To be so unbearable merely because of a single gaze, could this temperament truly count as unpolished jade?

Did she truly have the ability to bear Fu Chaosheng's expectations and revive the declining Fu family?

Or even, did she have the qualification to peer into the Ascendant realm?

"Forget it. In the end, she counts as my employer. How can I let her be humiliated like this?"

Luo Chen sighed inwardly and shifted his steps lightly, blocking Fu Qinglan.

His tall body covered the woman's figure and blocked the mocking gaze from the opposite direction.

Fu Qinglan froze and subconsciously raised her head.

Broad shoulders and a solid back were like a great mountain shielding her from wind and rain.

She opened her mouth, gratitude arising in her heart.

But in the next instant.

Whoosh!

Visible to the naked eye, a flush spread from her long neck to her cheeks.

It was a blush of shame, a red flush of humiliation and anger.

It came from the shame and anger of having her cowardice discovered by a friend who had recently interacted with her as an equal, and even being protected by him.

However, this shame and anger did not continue for long. Luo Chen, who was good at social interaction, quickly guided her attention away with words.

"Shi Ju? Is he one of the thirteen participants in this Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven competition?"

"Yes. We spoke of this before. Among the thirteen, Great House disciples occupy three seats: Gu Yuan of the Ancient Gu Great House, Shi Ju of the Shi Great House, and Gu Daosu of the Gu Great House. Shi Ju is one of the three, and also the strongest among them. It is said that he did not even bring a Dao protector for this trip into Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, which shows his confidence."

"How much do you know about him?"

"He is the person with the best aptitude and strongest talent in our generation. Strictly speaking, he should not count as our generation, but rather a younger one, yet because his cultivation speed was too fast, he caught up with us. Since childhood, the Shi Great House devoted all its strength to cultivating him. After entering the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, he cultivated one of the Seven Great True Scriptures, the Superior Profound Cave-Bright Blazing Flame True Scripture[TERM?]. He took first place in three consecutive minor competitions. By the time he formed his Nascent Soul, he was barely two hundred years old!"

"Oh?"

Hearing that the other party had formed his Nascent Soul in only two hundred years, Luo Chen's heart stirred.

By full calculation, his own Nascent Soul formation time was also around two hundred years.

The reason he had been two hundred sixteen when he left the Demon Sea was because he spent twenty years stabilizing his realm.

Calculating like this, the two of them had similar cultivation times!

"Could he have heavenly spirit root aptitude?"

Perhaps because emotions stirred in her heart, Fu Qinglan, who was usually cold, spoke rather much at this moment, continuously transmitting this person's circumstances to him one by one through divine sense.

"Heavenly spirit roots may be rare elsewhere, but in my sect, they are not uncommon. Among the thirteen participating in the competition this time, seven possess heavenly spirit roots."

"This aptitude seems to make cultivation extremely fast. But if one does not want the Purple Mansion barrier after Nascent Soul to be too thin and delay the Ascendant path, then one cannot take too many pills to assist cultivation before the Nascent Soul. Therefore, their cultivation time is actually not short. To normally cultivate to the Sage realm requires around three hundred years no matter what."

"The true reason Shi Ju is so outstanding, besides his fire-type heavenly spirit root, is that he possesses an innate Nine Yang Dao Body[TERM?]. He can rely on refined source-less fire to temper his own magic power, and thus avoid delaying cultivation."

Luo Chen raised a brow. While listening to the woman's words, his eyes involuntarily fixed upon that young man.

White robes like snow, just like him. Similar cultivation time, and even a physique so alike!

The only difference was that his Fire Spirit Body had been cultivated by acquired human effort, while the other party's Nine Yang Dao Body was innate.

Perhaps because Luo Chen had blocked Fu Qinglan's body, the man in the opposite peach grove could only place his gaze on Luo Chen.

It was originally unintentional, but as Shi Ju watched, disgust inexplicably arose in him.

Without turning his head, he gave an order.

"Go find Old Ghost Qi."

The servant behind him froze, but quickly acknowledged and hurried away from the Peach Garden.

On Luo Chen's side, Fu Qinglan's words continued.

Only toward the end, her tone became lower and lower.

"Rumor says Shi Ju has a pure child's heart. Even after cultivating for so long, he remains the same through a hundred years."

"But that pure child's heart is not kind. It is merely straightforward and willful, not following rules. He only does what he wants and never cares how others see it."

"Among our peers, many people have suffered much because of his so-called pure child's heart. The reason Senior Brother Liu Yuan fled far to the Northern Sea was mainly to temper himself, but a secondary reason may not have been without Shi Ju's targeting and suppression."

"It is not only Senior Brother Liu. Even other senior and junior brothers usually dislike Shi Ju's conduct. But because the sect ancestors value him, and with the Shi family's Great House status supporting him, everyone dares be angry but dares not speak."

Luo Chen secretly shook his head as he listened.

How was this a pure child's heart? It was clearly a spoiled brat's bad nature!

If this were the mortal world, he would long ago have been taught how to behave.

But because this was the cultivation world, the foundation of sect and clan and the blessing of individual power magnified the malice brought by that bad nature a thousandfold.

Sage Dragon Abyss was not born with a constitution that enjoyed abuse. Why would he abandon the excellent sect cultivation environment and run to the Northern Sea to live and die?

Thinking of this, his heart suddenly stirred.

"How has this person treated you?"

This time, Fu Qinglan fell silent.

Luo Chen did not press her and did not look directly at Shi Ju again.

It was better not to provoke this kind of spoiled child. He did not want to get filth all over himself.

Therefore, Luo Chen placed his attention on the other people present.

Those who visited today, apart from servants, attendants, and low-level disciples of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, were mostly Sages.

Luo Chen was unfamiliar with most of these Sages' origins.

But with Winter Mountain Hermit and Lu Qiuzhen, two Sages who had arrived in Central Continent earlier, making introductions, he barely recognized the general situation.

This one was the patriarch of such-and-such clan, that one was the current family head of such-and-such clan, and there were outside-continent cultivators mixed among them as well.

Once outside-continent cultivators were involved, their identities became even more complicated.

Lu Qiuzhen and the others knew only some older-generation Sages who had come from Heaven-Earth Peak.

As for the rest?

"They should be people sent by great outside-continent forces to attend the ceremony!"

Attend the ceremony? What ceremony?

The Alchemy Saint's ascension ceremony!

At the same time, they would conveniently watch what kind of outstanding figures emerged from this Heavenly Origin Dao Sect Heavenly Origin Grand Competition.

According to the customs and clothing styles recorded in classics, Luo Chen carefully distinguished them and saw clues on some people.

The West Desert had sent people, not eminent monks from the sacred land Hanging Temple, but several fairly famous Buddhist forces. Their bright bald heads were quite conspicuous in the crowd, and they stood rather near the front, seeming very interested in this high-level go match.

The Southern Frontier had also sent one person, but this person was quite mysterious, entirely shrouded in a black robe. Their true face could not be seen, and their sex could not be distinguished. They stood behind the crowd, and if no one around had dared approach them, Luo Chen would have found it hard to associate them with a Southern Frontier cultivator.

No one from the Northern Sea!

He did not know whether no one had come today to watch the excitement, or whether the Northern Sea was in such perilous war that after the Primordial Demon Sect's destruction, no one cared about Central Continent's affairs and no one had been sent at all. In any case, Luo Chen saw no Northern Sea cultivators apart from the three of them.

As for the Eastern Wilderness, even amid the raging human-demon war, the Nether Origin Sect had still sent a representative.

It was a very young cultivator wearing a Daoist robe with the Nether Origin Sect emblem. He stood beside Shi Ju and the others. Others were chatting and laughing, and though he also smiled, it was extremely forced.

It was understandable. The Alchemy Saint who was about to ascend had once been a person of their Nether Origin Sect!

Now, they had to attend her ascension ceremony as outside-continent cultivators. Who would feel good about that?

Along with this, Luo Chen felt that even the smile on Shi Ju's face was so provocative, so gloating.

At this moment.

Inside the field, Eminent Daoyin spoke in a clear voice.

"Today, Senior Brother Ke has time to open this game. Gu is truly fortunate. Leaving victory and defeat aside for now, Gu wishes to borrow a Rotten Axe-Handle chess piece[TERM?] for my clan younger brother. I am willing to exchange the Gu family's treasured No Pardon Game Record[TERM?] for it. I hope Senior Brother Ke will grant this."

As soon as these words emerged, the disorder in the field immediately became solemn.

The white-haired Ke Lianshan smiled, clearly moved by the so-called No Pardon Game Record.

"Black stone, or white stone?"

Gu Daoyin answered without hesitation. "Black stone!"

"Fine!" Ke Lianshan smiled slightly, but his tone shifted. "However, I must first see whether your go path has improved. Otherwise, if I return disappointed, one copy of No Pardon Game Record alone will not move me."

Gu Daoyin let out a breath of relief, held a black stone, and gently placed it.

"Senior Brother, please!"

A simple conversation involved an exchange of items.

But the impact it brought to everyone present was rather great.

Shi Ju's expression instantly changed.

Luo Chen was the same.

"What is a Rotten Axe-Handle chess piece?"

Fu Qinglan's expression had already changed. Facing Luo Chen's question, she immediately replied, "That is my sect's sect-guarding spirit treasure!"

The two words "spirit treasure" were enough to explain why the expressions of all cultivators present changed.

But the true reason Shi Ju, Fu Qinglan, and the others were emotionally stirred lay in the function of the Rotten Axe-Handle black stone.

Through Fu Qinglan's explanation, Luo Chen gained more understanding of the spirit treasure called Rotten Axe-Handle.

This was not a single spirit treasure, but a set of spirit treasures.

It consisted of a go board, go stones, and a rumored unfinished game.

These three together formed the Rotten Axe-Handle spirit treasure, and each had different functions.

Rumor said that in the hands of an Ascendant great power, the Rotten Axe-Handle go board could suppress an entire continent's foundation and prevent spiritual impulse from leaking out.

As for the stones, they were divided into black and white. There were one hundred eighty-one black stones, and every one had the function of protecting the divine soul. There were one hundred eighty white stones, and each was a supreme treasure of attack.

That unfinished game was even more wondrous and unfathomable.

Apart from the one who refined this treasure, anyone else who entered this game simply could not escape and would be trapped inside for life.

Moreover, to fully refine this treasure, one had to solve the unfinished game.

The Rotten Axe-Handle spirit treasure had hung empty in the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect for several thousand years, with no one able to refine it as master. At most, they could borrow one or two parts of the go board's power.

Only seven hundred years ago, when Ke Lianshan rose like a comet and revealed a shocking talent in the go path, did the spirit treasure tremble.

And he did not disappoint. When he reached the Sage realm, he made a brand-new move in the unfinished game, thereby obtaining the Rotten Axe-Handle artifact spirit's recognition and becoming the master of Rotten Axe-Handle.

The name Chess Saint began from there!

The reason Luo Chen was shaken was not this treasure's wonders, but that so-called black stone.

"The Rotten Axe-Handle black stone I obtained back then, was it also part of this spirit treasure?"

"Probably not. That was only a low-grade magic treasure, with a world of difference between it and a spirit treasure."

"But judging from the described effect, and even the division of black and white stones, they are so similar. If there is no relationship at all, I absolutely do not believe it."

"In that case, what relationship does the Eastern Wilderness Adept great sect Ghost God Valley have with this Heavenly Origin Dao Sect?"

For a time, Luo Chen's thoughts fluctuated, and all his attention was no longer on the two players.

...

"Almost time."

When the afternoon arrived, Luo Chen felt the intention to leave.

His go skill was not refined, and he did not want to spend his mind deducing the game. Added to his lack of attention, there was no meaning in continuing to stay here.

Moreover, the purpose of this trip had already been achieved.

Through Fu Qinglan's introductions, he had roughly seen several participants' Dao protectors and formed initial impressions.

As he continuously estimated matters in his heart, he already had a few parts of confidence.

Since that was so, he would leave.

Seeing Luo Chen about to go, Fu Qinglan hesitated, then followed.

Luo Chen calmly glanced at her and said nothing.

He could see clearly that her thoughts today were not on the go game either.

To make her lose interest even in the go she so loved showed how much pressure Shi Ju of the Shi family had brought her.

The two of them walked out of the Peach Garden neither quickly nor slowly. The surroundings were incomparably quiet, no longer lively with people coming and going as before.

Fu Qinglan seemed to dislike this quiet and took the initiative to find topics.

"The Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven competition concerns the future Ascendant opportunity of us Dao Sect true disciples, so those serving as Dao protectors are often carefully chosen by the major clans. Not only must they be outstandingly strong, their background must be known to the root, and ideally they must be absolutely loyal."

"Therefore, servants cultivated by clans from childhood became the best choice. My good friend Little Linzi, Lin Bufan, is like this."

"Senior Brother Liu Yuan, whom you were curious about before, lacks that clan background and corresponding connections. But he received the help of the master-disciple lineage!"

"The master-disciple lineage has declined in my Heavenly Origin Dao Sect for a long time. Even their Sage cultivators' qualifications to enter Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven were stripped away. This time, under the name of helping Senior Brother Liu Yuan, they sent their most outstanding disciple of the current generation, Senior Sister Mingyan[TERM?]. This is also something all our major clans have discussed endlessly. At that time, whether Senior Sister Mingyan protects Senior Brother Liu Yuan, or Senior Brother Liu Yuan instead protects Senior Sister Mingyan, is hard to say."

"The Rotten Axe-Handle spirit treasure has a great reputation, but no one could refine it as master before. However, study of this treasure has never stopped, so many imitations emerged."

"I saw that Dao Brother paid quite a bit of attention to this treasure just now. After we return, I can take the initiative and give you a magic-tool-level imitation."

...

Luo Chen stopped walking.

At a bend where the mountain turned and the Passing River roared.

Fu Qinglan looked at him in confusion.

Luo Chen's expression was calm, and his gaze gentle. "If you have words, say them."

Fu Qinglan opened her mouth. Her small red lips trembled slightly.

Then came a soft sigh.

"Dao Brother's eyes are bright as torches. Little Sister truly cannot hide anything from you."

"Bright as torches? Like that Shi Ju?"

The calm sentence stabbed somewhat at the heart.

Fu Qinglan lowered her head, hair drifting in the wind.

"This person has always harbored improper intentions toward me. He once said he would have me enter the Shi family and become his concubine. Not only that, he said he would gift me a family servant to protect me in the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven competition. In the future, he would even help me revive the Fu family. Even the name of Great House would not be impossible."

Luo Chen raised a brow. "Since the conditions are so generous, why call them improper intentions? As far as I know, marriage alliances among your Aristocratic Clans are quite frequent."

Fu Qinglan shook her head. "It is different. In marriage alliances, one becomes a wife, but if I went over, I would be a concubine. Moreover, in my view, Shi Ju's words are more like a jest, words meant to toy with me!"

"Oh?"

"He has prior conduct. During the Initiate stage, he used this to deceive an Aristocratic Clan young lady. That fellow disciple was at the Adept realm then and was rather outstanding, once holding hope of Sage. But after entering the Shi family, not only did her Dao path stagnate, even the clan behind her was swallowed by the Shi family in passing."

"Truly?"

"Absolutely true!"

Luo Chen frowned. "With such conduct, no one in the Dao Sect questioned it?"

Fu Qinglan pressed her lips and brushed aside the hair hanging near her mouth, her expression bitter. "Everything followed logically, and even that fellow disciple's clan willingly allowed itself to be swallowed. Who would question it?"

Luo Chen understood.

From this, that Shi Ju truly was a genius spoiled rotten by sect and clan!

How could a cultivator's promise be so lightly broken?

Even if not speaking of contracts or oaths, ordinary promises ought to be treated solemnly.

The word "toyed" used by the woman before him was quite vivid.

Shi Ju was not coveting beauty, and perhaps not even plotting against the small clans behind those women. He simply wanted to tease and play with them.

Nestled in this peaceful region with only internal strife, apart from cultivation, he was probably bored stiff and so sought such amusements.

So Luo Chen thought.

Fu Qinglan explained a few more sentences, such as how she was the Fu family's hope and could not possibly marry far into another clan.

Luo Chen had no mind to continue listening to these familiar old lines. His expression grew solemn.

"So this is why someone is specifically waiting for us."

Fu Qinglan froze.

In the next moment, from the bend in the mountain hollow, an old farmer walked out with both hands tucked into his sleeves.

His clothes were plain, with no silk or brocade in sight, only hemp clothing and cloth shoes. White leggings were tucked into his socks and shoes, and a white cloth commonly used by spirit farmers to wipe sweat was tied around his head.

His body was hunched, his expression bitter.

Facing this person, Fu Qinglan blurted out.

"Elder Qi!"

"Miss Qinglan is still as kind-hearted as ever, unlike others who call me Old Ghost Qi with every breath."

Seeing Fu Qinglan, the old farmer nodded in acknowledgment.

Then he looked at Luo Chen. As he spoke, he straightened his waist.

"You are Miss Qinglan's Dao protector? This old man would like to test your ability and see whether you are qualified to protect her."

When his back straightened, gusts of astral wind roared. His tall body instantly stood before them like a mighty city.

Without giving Luo Chen any chance to speak, he stepped out and smashed a fist down.

Luo Chen narrowed his eyes slightly. A Sage? Or a body cultivator?

Or perhaps both?

If he had not seen wrong, that fierce and powerful energy looked very much like the astral qi only orthodox body cultivators could cultivate.

If such an expert were outside, Luo Chen would naturally fight him to his heart's content. In truth, he had always wanted to test his desolate-beast body-refining method against orthodox body cultivation to see which was stronger.

But this was the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range!

The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's mountain gate!

He was an outsider and could not lightly act.

If he acted, the Fu family might not be able to protect him, and Heaven-Earth Peak would probably not exert itself to protect a mere external affairs guest elder to the death.

The other party, however, was so decisive, so he clearly had no concerns in this regard.

Since that was so...

Facing the punch smashing down from above, Luo Chen neither dodged nor avoided.

Boom!

A shallow pit appeared on the ground.

Its shallowness showed both the other party's precise control of force and that he had not used full strength.

But Qi Xiongcheng froze.

Passing by?

How could this be!

He raised his head and saw the white-robed Daoist before him with a calm expression, illusions coiling around him as magic power surged.

Those warm eyes, in this moment, became like a bottomless abyss, drawing people to fall.

"This..."

As Qi Xiongcheng stood dazed, his tenacious will instantly broke through the illusion.

When he opened his eyes again, where was the Daoist before him?

Only a phantom remained, lingering with traces of magic power.

He turned back and saw Luo Chen already drifting away, while Fu Qinglan followed closely behind, though she kept looking back at him.

Qi Xiongcheng's right hand hung in the air as he stood there blankly.

"Illusion art?"

"Then when did I fall for it?"

Though he clearly stood there with his back straight, for some reason, his waist seemed to hunch down again.

*(End of Chapter)*
