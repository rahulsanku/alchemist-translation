# Chapter 729: Dead, Mad, Missing, the Ominous Sea

Little Qiong Mountain [TERM?].

One of Divine Origin City's fourth-tier spirit mountains.

Even though it was only a small fourth-tier spirit vein, under the skilled craftsmen of the Tianyuan Merchant Alliance, more than a hundred cave abodes had been opened, enough to accommodate many cultivators cultivating at the same time.

By comparison, although the Myriad Immortals Assembly's Fulong Mountain Range was vast, its degree of spirit-vein utilization was far too crude.

Inside the spacious cave abode, Luo Chen walked out from the quiet chamber, his face rather satisfied.

The spiritual qi concentration here was quite high, very suitable for cultivation at his current realm.

He did not ask about the rent. Without thinking, he knew that rent for a cave abode of this level would inevitably be astronomical, perhaps even impossible to rent with spirit stones at all.

It required connections!

He himself could use it free of charge for a while only because he had reached a cooperative relationship with Fu Chaosheng.

After all, every bit Luo Chen's cultivation increased meant a few more assurances in protecting Fu Chaosheng's granddaughter later.

Fu Hongzhang [TERM?] waited quietly in the guest hall. Seeing Luo Chen's satisfied smile, he could not help but release a breath.

"What does Sage think?"

Luo Chen smiled faintly and nodded.

"I have troubled you. Not only are you collecting resources for me, you have even arranged such a comfortable cultivation environment."

Fu Hongzhang [TERM?] hurriedly waved his hands. "We will certainly do our utmost with matters the family head has entrusted to us. If Sage has any other needs, feel free to speak. This junior will certainly do everything possible to satisfy them."

At this point, he said with quite some confidence, "Although this junior lacks ability, I have handled the merchant alliance's worldly affairs for many years and have touched on matters large and small. The family head said in his letter that whatever Sage requests, I must satisfy it as far as lies within my ability."

Luo Chen raised his brow. Fu Chaosheng had truly paid a heavy price!

Not only had he ordered people to prepare a batch of resources Luo Chen needed long before Luo Chen arrived, he had even handed over clan cultivators for Luo Chen to command.

Thinking of this, Luo Chen pondered for a moment.

Then he suddenly asked, "I wonder, do you understand the current movements of the Myriad Immortals Assembly's Three Great Wanderers?"

Fu Hongzhang [TERM?] froze. He had thought the other party would be curious about Divine Origin City's high-level cultivators or the situation in Central Continent, and had already prepared general answers.

Unexpectedly, the other party was more interested in the Northern Sea's Three Great Wanderers.

But then, his thoughts turned. This person called himself "Wilderness Wanderer," so it was only natural for him to be interested in predecessors who were likewise called Wanderers and had become famous earlier.

Fu Hongzhang [TERM?] thought for a moment and slowly stated the intelligence he knew.

"Mad Wanderer was originally called Layman Fengling [TERM?] and had always cultivated quietly in Fengling Temple [TERM?]. But later, Fengling Temple [TERM?] was occupied by the Primordial Demon Sect, and he became a homeless rogue cultivator. Wind Wanderer [TERM?] became Mad Wanderer. Over these years, he has wandered all across the Northern Sea, showing the head but never the tail. Intelligence regarding him is too scattered. If Sage wishes to understand it in detail, I can arrange for someone to send you a summarized dossier later."

Luo Chen made a sound of acknowledgment, neither confirming nor denying.

He was not particularly interested in this late-stage Sage great cultivator whose name he had only heard and whose true appearance he had never seen.

His true targets were the other two Great Wanderers!

"As for Moon Wanderer..." When Fu Hongzhang [TERM?] mentioned that woman, he hesitated somewhat.

Luo Chen blinked. "What happened to her? I hear that the Myriad Immortals Assembly collapsed under the demon-beast coalition's assault. What is Moon Wanderer's current situation?"

Fu Hongzhang [TERM?] hesitated. "That senior's situation is somewhat difficult to judge."

"Oh?"

"Before the Demon Alliance [TERM?] attack, she once came to Divine Origin City and borrowed a cross-sea teleportation formation [TERM?]. Luo Chen listened seriously while his mind kept turning.

Moon Wanderer borrowing the teleportation formation should have been when she went to hunt him.

After that star-chasing demonic beast [TERM?] and his divided-soul puppet both died at Soulbreak Cliff [TERM?], Moon Wanderer lost his trail and could only return without success.

Her cultivation went without saying. She had already revealed it in Bright Radiance Heaven.

She, who had once been the weakest of the Three Great Wanderers, had instead leaped to the same realm as Mad Wanderer, and her mind was clearer, her strength steadier.

With her strength and the manpower and resources of the Myriad Immortals Assembly, so long as there was no late-stage Sage Demon Emperor among the attacking enemies, the Myriad Immortals Assembly should always have been able to preserve itself.

According to the intelligence, she had indeed nearly turned the tide and propped up a collapsing mansion.

But the problem lay precisely in the word "nearly."

"Why did she retreat?" Luo Chen asked in a low voice.

Fu Hongzhang [TERM?] shook his head. "Unclear. Everyone knew that the Demon Alliance [TERM?] had only mobilized one Ascendant ancient demon [TERM?]: Sea Emperor Whale [TERM?].

No other reason!

It was simply that in the current Northern Sea cultivation world, Ghost Immortal Tower's ferocious name could be called heaven-filling!

Wherever it passed, almost no one was its match.

A large part of the reason the righteous and demonic paths had stopped fighting and joined hands was that they wanted to free late-stage Sage great cultivators to target this beast.

But it was also good that she had disappeared. At the very least, before he left the Northern Sea, he would not need to guard too much against this woman.

However, Fu Hongzhang's [TERM?] next sentence made Luo Chen's relaxed heart and expression change slightly.

"Speaking of which, the once-glorious Northern Sea Three Great Wanderers: one is mad, one is dead, and one is missing, and all three went to the Fallen Sea. As expected, there is a reason that sea region is called the Ominous Sea [TERM?]."

"Who died?"

Luo Chen blurted this out, his eyes full of shock.

The mad one and the missing one, he could both match.

Then the dead one, could it be Blood Wanderer?

But...

Luo Chen found it difficult to digest this news.

He said softly, "No matter how strong Sage cultivators are, how could they affect an area of ten thousand li?"

The destructive power of Sage cultivators was indeed terrifying, something Luo Chen himself had experienced.

But that was under the condition of a "mobile battle," where constant movement and the continuous release of battle aftermath affected celestial phenomena and terrain.

Yet according to Fu Hongzhang [TERM?], after the battle between the two Bloods [TERM?], a blood sea evolved, and this blood sea seemed to have life force, invading outward.

Fu Hongzhang [TERM?] thought for a moment and said, "According to the family head's guess, it may be that Blood Nightmare Moluo bore the power of the Blood Sea Ancestor of the Primordial Demon Sect. Ascendant-level power could only be resolved by an Ascendant great power!

Luo Chen sank into silence.

The generation's famous Blood Wanderer had fallen just like that?

And Blood Nightmare Moluo, who had a grudge with him and from whom he had only collected a little interest, had truly died as well?

They had the power of the Blood Sea Ancestor on them? Then when he retreated at the time and saw that enormous shadow beneath the sea surface, was that the manifestation of that power?

No one could give him accurate answers to these questions.

The Tianyuan Merchant Alliance's intelligence was limited to this: Coldlight Island, once limitless in glory, had now become a true "spiritless land."

No spirit veins, no spiritual qi. Even the wandering spiritual qi between heaven and earth would avoid that place.

The guest hall fell into an indescribable stillness.

"Master, everything has been counted. Basically, they are all items named on your list. According to Fellow Daoist Fu's markings, the specific price is thirty million lower-grade spirit stones in total."

Thirty million?

Luo Chen even thought that later, he would hand part of the materials for refining Condensing Liquid Pills [TERM?] and the new Nascent Soul Pill over to the Tianyuan Merchant Alliance to help collect as well.

Otherwise, once his cooperation with Fu Chaosheng ended, he would no longer have this channel.

Battles between human-shaped computers?
