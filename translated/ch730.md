# Chapter 730: Heaven-Earth Peak's Star Gate, Tribulation Lightning Tempers the Divine Soul

The moment he stepped inside, Shen Wandou felt profoundly uncomfortable.

Divine sense like a vast ocean spread out without restraint, as if prying into him at every instant.

He pursed his lips, and the left hand tucked inside his sleeve formed a seal.

"Oh?"

A soft sound of surprise drifted over, and Luo Chen, dressed in loose white robes with his hair hanging unbound, walked out from the quiet chamber.

He smiled apologetically. "Forgive me. I am cultivating a certain art, which has made my divine soul too active. For the moment, I cannot draw it back in."

Though he said this, his gaze never stopped sizing Shen Wandou up.

The other man's original realm was plain to see, roughly around the third level of the Sage stage, and his divine sense withdrew after the barest touch against Luo Chen's.

Yet in that instant, it had revealed a strength in no way inferior to Luo Chen's own divine sense.

Afterward, he had used some unknown secret art to hide every trace of his aura perfectly, so completely that even face to face, Luo Chen could not detect the slightest bit of it.

Shen Wandou likewise showed surprise, startled by Luo Chen's excessively youthful appearance.

In the cultivation world, there were countless secret arts and pills that could preserve youth forever, so a youthful face had never been the standard for judging age.

There were even old monsters who looked like children or youths, yet had in truth lived for centuries.

But no matter what, such people always carried some inescapable "old air" about them. Their speech and conduct were often heavy with age as well, with not the slightest trace of youth to be seen.

Yet from Luo Chen's fair skin and beardless cheeks, what Shen Wandou sensed was a rich "vitality."

This meant the other party truly was very young, and his actual years of cultivation definitely did not exceed four or five centuries.

He exclaimed in admiration, "Fellow Daoist is so young, yet possesses such powerful divine sense. This is truly rare in the world!"

Luo Chen smiled faintly. "Fellow Daoist Shen overpraises me. With your ability, your divine sense is not the least bit inferior to mine. As expected of the grand elder of the Northern Sea Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance!"

Unexpectedly, when Shen Wandou heard this praise, a bitter smile appeared on his face.

"In your eyes, the position of merchant alliance elder may seem incomparably honored, but to people like us, it is nothing more than a hard assignment."

Luo Chen was astonished.

An elder of the Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance, whether in the Eastern Wilderness or the Northern Sea, was an incomparably esteemed great figure wherever he went.

Under him were countless cultivators to command, outside he had broad connections, and through his hands flowed innumerable rare treasures and scarce resources.

This could be seen clearly from how easily Fu Chaosheng had helped him collect a large quantity of cultivation resources.

If placed in a mortal kingdom, would such a person not at least count as a high official governing a whole region?

This was also a hard assignment?

Seeing Luo Chen's surprised expression, Shen Wandou shook his head and sighed. "Fellow Daoist does not know. My Heavenly Origin Dao Sect is vast and rich, with geniuses emerging in every generation. The truly formidable figures all cultivate quietly inside the sect. Only those of us with mediocre aptitude, ordinary family background, and no further possibility of advancing on the Dao path after growing old are exiled to the Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance to handle such lowly merchant affairs. It is one thing for those who do not know the truth to respect us, but for someone like Fellow Daoist, who will sooner or later become a guest elder of my Star Gate, there is no need."

Luo Chen grew even more surprised.

He knew the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect was powerful and occupied the essence land of an entire realm, overlooking the four continents of Mountains and Seas.

But surely a Sage cultivator should not be reduced to the word "exiled," no matter what?

Moreover...

"How does Fellow Daoist Shen know I am to become a guest elder of your sect? And what is this Star Gate guest elder?"

Shen Wandou smiled. "This is quite normal. We merchant alliance elders have this duty to begin with: to recommend some powerful figures from the four continents into the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. Within the Dao Sect, Star Gate is where people like you go. I imagine Elder Fu invited you here because he also intends to ask you to join our sect!"

"Star Gate?"

Luo Chen repeated the name, his expression solemn. "Please enlighten me, Fellow Daoist. What sort of place is Star Gate?"

Previously, Fu Chaosheng had only said that after the matter was accomplished, he would recommend Luo Chen to become a guest elder of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, but had not mentioned this special place called Star Gate.

The shortage of time then was one reason; another was likely that the other party had held something back.

From the present situation, it was hard to say whether Shen Wandou and Fu Chaosheng were enemies or friends, but if he could obtain some intelligence from Shen Wandou, he would have an easier time acting later.

Shen Wandou carefully examined Luo Chen. His bearing was calm and open, neither servile nor overbearing, and although he was only at the first level of the Sage stage, his divine sense was no weaker than Shen Wandou's. He truly was a rare figure.

After a brief thought, Shen Wandou smiled broadly and said:

"Star Gate is a special institution of our sect, and its residence is located on Heaven-Earth Peak."

"Within it, there are altogether nineteen elders. Of these nineteen elders, twelve come from the Twelve Great Houses, two are appointed in rotation by the Thirty-Six Aristocratic Clans, and the remaining five are recommended outside guest experts."

"But Star Gate's overall composition is in fact made up of outside experts like you. The lowest standard for recruitment is Sage cultivator. Once one enters, one is divided into three ranks: ordinary guest elder, Heavenly Origin offering elder, and Heaven-Earth Peak elder."

"Does Fellow Daoist know of Alchemy Saint Chu Yan?"

Luo Chen froze, then subconsciously nodded.

This person had a name like thunder in the ears; Luo Chen had heard of him as early as the Qi Refinement stage.

Rumor said he was originally from the Eastern Wilderness holy land, the Nether Origin Sect, yet for some unknown reason, he had gone far away to Central Continent and joined the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

According to rumor, he was also the only alchemy master in the world capable of refining fifth-tier pills while still in the Sage stage!

Why had Shen Wandou mentioned him now? Could it be...

"He is one of Heaven-Earth Peak Star Gate's nineteen elders, and he has served as a Heaven-Earth Peak elder for five hundred years!"

Luo Chen's heart shook. He asked in puzzlement, "What benefits does Heaven-Earth Peak's Star Gate have, to attract so many experts of the Mountains and Seas Realm to join?"

"Benefits?" Shen Wandou laughed freely. "There are far too many."

After that, he explained at length.

Outside Sage cultivators could not directly join the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. In this respect, the taboo was much the same as other sects, all to guard against those who brought their arts with them and threw the ancestral court into chaos.

For this reason, Star Gate had been specially established as an institution to accommodate outside experts.

Once they joined it, ordinary guest elders could directly enjoy a fourth-tier spirit vein free of charge without spending a single spirit stone.

If, after becoming a guest elder, one made some special contribution to the Dao Sect, or raised one's realm to late-stage Sage, one could even become a Heavenly Origin offering elder.

At that time, some of the resources within the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect would open to the offering elder.

As for the five Heaven-Earth Peak elders, they were even qualified to participate in some of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's major decisions.

Apart from this, there were two other benefits difficult for outsiders to know.

One was that after joining Star Gate for a full five hundred years, one could recommend one's own descendants or disciples to join the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect and formally become part of the Dao Sect.

The other involved the secret of ascension!

Shen Wandou did not elaborate on this final point, but even what he had already mentioned was enough for Luo Chen to digest.

There were so many benefits to becoming a Star Gate elder; no wonder all the experts beneath the heavens flocked to it like ducks to water.

"Now the Northern Sea is in great turmoil, and demon beasts are in the ascendant. Many Northern Sea experts have lost their footholds. This time, Shen has also recommended several Northern Sea Sages into Star Gate. Wilderness Wanderer, if you have time, I can organize a gathering and introduce you to them, and you can exchange cultivation insights along the way. Shutting oneself away and building a cart behind closed doors is never beneficial to people like us." Shen Wandou stroked his beard and smiled.

Luo Chen took a sip of tea, but still declined politely.

"Many thanks for Fellow Daoist Shen's good intentions. You have seen my present condition. My secret art is not complete, and my divine soul is active and scattered. It is not very suitable for me to converse with others. My apologies."

"That is true!"

Shen Wandou looked at Luo Chen and sensed that omnipresent divine sense. Vaguely, it gave him the feeling of needles pricking his skin.

He knew the other party was not doing it deliberately, but it truly did make cultivators of the same level uncomfortable, and was not suitable for social exchange.

"In that case, let it be."

Then, he changed the subject. "No wonder you are so interested in puppet arts. Divine sense as powerful as yours is indeed suited to cultivating puppet arts. This old man happens to have an ancient puppet art text in hand. Perhaps it can lend you some assistance."

Luo Chen hesitated. "I wonder what its value is?"

Shen Wandou laughed loudly. "Why speak of yellow and white things? Today, you and I have conversed happily. Consider it a house-call gift. I shall give it to Fellow Daoist."

Seeing Luo Chen still hesitate somewhat, he said grandly, "It is only a copied text, and of no use to this old man besides. Fellow Daoist need not refuse."

Only then did Luo Chen accept the jade slip that had a puppet art copied into it.

They chatted a little more about Central Continent's customs and local conditions, and then Luo Chen personally escorted Shen Wandou out of the cave abode.

On Little Qiong Mountain, Luo Chen held the jade slip. The smile remained on his face, but his heart had grown somewhat heavy.

He had only treated the trip to Central Continent as an interlude, something completed once he fulfilled his promise to Fu Chaosheng.

Now it seemed the place he was going to was a colossal existence, and even just a glimpse of the iceberg weighed down on him until it was hard to breathe.

Star Gate, Heaven-Earth Peak...

Below Little Qiong Mountain, Shen Wandou likewise still wore a smile, the corners of his mouth unable to stop rising slightly.

"This trip was the right one after all."

"This Wilderness Wanderer has excellent potential. At such a young age, he has already reached this realm. After he joins Heaven-Earth Peak in the future, he is bound to grow even faster."

"But that is the future. As things stand now, his Sage realm is newly formed, and he cannot even withdraw and release his divine sense at will. Clearly, his battle strength is ordinary. In Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven's five-hundred-year grand competition, he poses no threat to Liu Yuan."

"I merely paid out a copied puppet art text that is of no use to me, yet I can make him divide his attention toward puppet arts and prevent his strength from increasing in the short term. I can also make him grateful to me, and when he attains some small success in the future, he may become an outside arm of assistance for my Shen family."

"Speaking of which, that fellow Fu Chaosheng has decent eyes. He actually managed to dig out such a genius!"

...

The matter of Shen Wandou visiting Luo Chen could not be hidden from others.

Very quickly, the high-level cultivators in Divine Origin City all learned that a Sage-stage rogue cultivator had come to the city.

They intended to pay a visit, but basically all of them were turned away at the door.

No one held any resentment, because the explanation given from Little Qiong Mountain was that "Wilderness Wanderer formed his Nascent Soul not long ago and needs time in seclusion to consolidate his realm."

The high-level cultivators all understood this very well. After all, the other party was a rogue cultivator.

Being able to break through to the Sage stage was already no easy matter. Now that he had temporarily obtained a fourth-tier spirit vein site, it was only natural that he seize the time to consolidate his realm.

Yet the true situation was completely different!

Inside the cave abode, Luo Chen consumed one Great Soul Pill every month.

After only three months, his divine sense, which had been impossible to draw back, became completely free to release and withdraw.

To Luo Chen himself, this represented enormous progress in his cultivation of the Soul-Splitting Art.

[Soul-Splitting Art proficiency 1/100]

Luo Chen looked at the newly born entry on the attribute panel and felt as if a heavy burden had been lifted.

At this moment, within his sea of consciousness, two soul phantoms, one primary and one secondary, each sat in silent stillness.

In terms of size, the divided soul was far inferior to the main soul, but its foundation was already no weaker than the main soul of an ordinary first-level Sage cultivator.

"In only three months, I have grown to this point. This Great Soul Pill is truly wondrous beyond measure!"

Even Luo Chen, calm as he was, could not help the surge of emotion in his heart. He was rather excited.

Even without the complete Soul-Splitting Art, and without a method to accelerate the divided soul's cultivation speed, he had ultimately used the Dao of alchemy to make up for this flaw.

As for the Great Soul Pill, one furnace produced twelve pills, and one successful refinement was enough for a year's needs.

One bottle of soul essence sufficient for tens of millions of people was enough for a thousand pill refinements.

Everything was developing in a good direction.

The only thing that left Luo Chen doubtful was probably that the newborn divided soul, though strong, was somewhat weak.

This sounded contradictory, but in truth it had an inner logic.

It was strong in "quantity," weak in "quality."

This divided soul was unlike his main soul, which had long been washed by the years, tempered by realm advancement, and had even undergone the stage after the Sage tribulation when the yin soul transformed into a yang spirit.

Therefore, although the divided soul seemed to possess Sage-level power, if he truly used it to attack an enemy, it would inevitably fall short.

"From this perspective, it was understandable that Han Zhan lost to me back then."

Luo Chen spoke to himself.

Clearly, even Han Zhan, who had cultivated the complete Soul-Splitting Art, had not solved this problem.

Thus, although he appeared to possess nine great divided souls, in truth they were swollen and unwieldy, their quality uneven, which led to Luo Chen breaking them one by one again and again with the Dragon-Slaying Art.

And now, this same problem had appeared on Luo Chen as well.

If he only pursued quantity, that was absolutely not what Luo Chen wanted.

However, Luo Chen already had an initial idea for solving this problem.

That was his life-bound treasure!

A small gray cauldron appeared above Luo Chen's head, spinning smoothly in place.

Strands of silver-white lightning arcs flashed across it from time to time.

There was a trace of fear in Luo Chen's eyes, but also a measure of anticipation.

"If I use the power of tribulation lightning to temper the divine soul, can I remove the dross, preserve the essence, and strengthen the divine soul's fundamental nature?"

"Or even, not only the divided soul, but if this is feasible, perhaps even the main soul can be tempered this way?"

Beings of yin soul nature feared yang and hard forces most.

And tribulation lightning was undoubtedly the most hard and yang thing beneath the heavens!

If the divided soul could endure the power of tribulation lightning, its strength would become unimaginable.

Luo Chen carefully released the divided soul while splitting his attention to make the Primordial Unity Cauldron release a single strand of silver-white lightning.

Crack!

The moment the two touched, Luo Chen felt as though he had been struck by lightning. Because of the connection between the divided soul and the main soul, even his eyes instinctively rolled back, and he fell straight into unconsciousness.

Only after most of a day did Luo Chen slowly wake, his pale face filled with lingering fear.

Those beneath the heavens who dared use thunder to temper the body were already exceedingly rare.

Someone like him, who used tribulation lightning to refine the soul, was truly unprecedented and would have no successor.

It was enough to call him willful, reckless, and audacious beyond measure!

Yet on Luo Chen's face, still filled with lingering fear, there was now also several traces of wild joy.

"It works!"

"It absolutely works!"

"It is only that the amount of tribulation lightning just now was too great. If it can be reduced by more than half, my divided soul will be able to endure it."

The Primordial Unity Cauldron was his life-bound treasure, and he had already gained some understanding of how to control the amount of tribulation lightning it released.

Next, he only needed to experiment bit by bit and control the proportions properly.

Even if, because of improper operation, he destroyed this divided soul, Luo Chen would not pity it.

He had learned the Soul-Splitting Art and mastered the method for refining the Great Soul Pill. As long as his main soul remained, at worst he could simply start over.

And if he managed to explore and develop such a soul-tempering method, he might surpass Han Zhan's attainments in the dao of soul refinement and from then on turn his divine-soul weakness completely into an advantage.

When his divine soul became powerful, he could also study and learn corresponding divine-soul methods for attack and defense!

"The future is bright!"

Luo Chen smiled and took the Primordial Unity Cauldron back into his Purple Mansion.

Rice had to be eaten one mouthful at a time, and matters had to be handled step by step. Since the divided soul was wounded, he needed to take another Great Soul Pill to replenish it.

...

On this day, Luo Chen sat alone in the cave abode.

Before him was still the go board made from a Demon King's leg bone, but clearly his attention was not on the shallow endgame displayed across the board. Instead, he was wholly focused on the jade slip in his hand.

He had already studied the puppet art Shen Wandou gave him for some time.

Ancient puppet arts were not very different from those of the present day. After all, they were a minor path to begin with, and there was only so much they could change.

With Han Zhan's occasional pointers over the past hundred years, Luo Chen had long since acquired the corresponding foundation in puppet arts.

Therefore, he picked it up quite quickly.

As for making puppets, he was not very interested. In his view, making puppets shared the same origin as artifact forging; the only difference was that weapons and magic treasures such as sabers, spears, swords, and halberds were replaced by more complicated puppets.

What interested him was the aspect of "controlling puppets."

After a long while, Luo Chen put down the jade slip, a thoughtful expression appearing on his face.

If one wanted to show achievement in puppet arts, the most basic condition was that the divine soul be powerful, and preferably able to split attention to several tasks at once.

This was an old topic.

But there was indeed reason for saying so, because controlling puppets required powerful divine sense.

This was especially true of some powerful puppets that contained spirit souls of their own.

The path of puppet control was divided into four levels.

Thread-suspension puppet control [TERM?] was beneath the threshold, spiritual-power guidance barely entered the door, divine sense forming threads allowed free control, and deep-planted soul seal made transformations unfathomable!

"This Cangli Puppet Art actually records a complete puppet-control method, and it has not even omitted the method for creating soul seals."

"Shen Wandou truly gave me a great gift!"

After sighing with emotion, Luo Chen sank into thought again.

Could the method of planting soul seals control that White Tiger Puppet, which was comparable to a late-stage Sage?

Could this set of methods truly touch the sword spirit inside that blood sword and refine it into recognizing him as master?

He had far too little experience in this area.

Especially when great killing weapons like the White Tiger Puppet and the blood sword were unlike his life-bound treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, which he could command as easily as moving his own arm. Therefore, he had to be extremely careful and could not tolerate the slightest mistake.

Otherwise, if those two things went out of control, he would be the one who could neither swallow the loss nor spit it out.

Just as Luo Chen was pondering bitterly, his expression stirred slightly.

"Heavenjade, come in!"

Outside the cave abode, Heavenjade answered and entered.

The moment she came in, she flicked her sleeve.

"Master, Black King has returned."

A coiled jiaolong fell onto the ground. Its flesh was torn open, and clusters of faint blue flame burned across its body.

Luo Chen raised his brow. "Black King, how did you end up in such a sorry state?"

Black King said hatefully, "I ran into an enemy. These wounds are nothing serious, but this fire will have to trouble Master to handle."

Looking at the blue flames, Luo Chen vaguely sensed something familiar.

Together with Black King's word "enemy," Luo Chen instantly recalled a person.

And in the next moment, his gaze shone like lightning, piercing through the cave abode.

"Black King, you brought someone back with you."

Black King froze. "Master, how did you know I brought... no, that old bastard followed me too!"

He suddenly turned his head, his dragon tongue hissing, his eyes filled with ferocity.

Luo Chen waved a hand and rose to his full height.

"An old friend has come to visit. Why be so vicious? Stay here and behave yourself. I will go meet an old friend."

*(End of Chapter)*
