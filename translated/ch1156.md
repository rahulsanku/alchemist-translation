# Chapter 1156: The Immortal Bequeathed Changes Hands, Blood-Refined Divine Astral

"Looks like West Long City Lord and the others truly failed."

Under the moonlight.

The Moya Wind City Lord spoke with a sigh.

Across from him, Luo Chen stepped through the moonlight, his gaze piercing like a torch.

Xiangfei, who had once been quite beautiful and moving, now seemed as dim as an ant, silently following behind Luo Chen.

Landing on the tall building, Luo Chen swept a glance at the Moya Wind City Lord and could not help but let out a soft "Eh."

In his qi-sense perception, the Moya Wind City Lord was like a water tank full of holes, constantly venting astral qi outward.

This situation should not occur on a Leakless realm martial artist whose body was supposed to be without leaks.

A Returner, not far from death.

For some reason, Luo Chen recalled that saying.

Xiangfei looked at the Moya Wind City Lord, a hint of sympathy for a dying fellow appearing in her eyes.

The other party's current condition was perhaps the fate that awaited her not long from now.

If they had truly been able to defeat Luo Chen before and divide his primordial spirit, perhaps...

No sooner had this thought arisen than Xiangfei's heart trembled, and she dared not gaze at Luo Chen's back.

"Indeed?" Luo Chen smiled. "So Moya Wind City Lord never had high hopes for their actions from the very beginning?"

The Moya Wind City Lord gave a bitter laugh. "From the moment Senior cast that glance at me from afar in Moya Wind City, I knew that Senior's strength surpassed even the Seven Ring martial artists among the Nine Rings of Destruction. For a powerhouse of the Middle Three Rings, facing multiple opponents alone was nothing difficult. Especially for people like us... sigh."

Speaking to the end, the Moya Wind City Lord let out a self-pitying sigh.

If there had been any possibility, who would have wanted to Return!

The location of Carefree City was a true martial arts holy land that held the hope of breaking through realms!

The Moya Wind City Lord sighed and bowed to Luo Chen with cupped hands. "Senior has come here, I presume not to call me to account. If you have any instructions, please speak frankly."

A man who knew the times.

Luo Chen nodded slightly and asked directly, "According to Xiangfei, among the seven of you, only you managed to break into the Ninth Ring. Is this true?"

"It is not false."

"Within the Nine Rings, it is said to be filled with chaotic energy. What kind of power is that?"

"Chaos is also known as primordial chaos. The energy within is mixed and disorderly, extremely violent and wild."

"It sounds somewhat similar to the origin qi of heaven and earth in the immortal cultivation world?"

"Senior may not know this. At first, some immortal cultivators who had drifted into the Returned Ruins mistakenly believed that chaotic energy was the same as origin qi of heaven and earth and entered it to attempt cultivation. But the consequence was invariably the shattering of their golden cores and nascent souls. Even the primordial spirits of great cultivators could not refine a single strand of chaotic energy. Only we martial artists can activate our qi-blood furnaces, refining a tiny bit to strengthen our own qi and blood."

Luo Chen frowned slightly. "Why is that? Could it be that even those Ascendant great cultivators who have cultivated primordial spirits cannot absorb chaotic energy?"

The Moya Wind City Lord shook his head. "I do not know either. In fact, because the chaotic energy was too violent, it shattered my qi-blood furnace, which is why I was severely injured and had to leave Carefree City."

Speaking up to here, he hesitated for a moment, then speculated with an uncertain tone, "It is said that chaotic energy contains trace amounts of law fragments. For high-level immortal cultivators who specialize in the power of laws, this is a highly toxic substance."

"Law fragments?"

Luo Chen was startled, then a look of uncertainty flickered across his face.

The Great Destruction Realm—no, it should be called the Returned Ruins!

This world was said to have the Great Dao severed and laws shattered. If law fragments were truly mixed within the chaotic energy, then immortal cultivators would indeed find it difficult to endure.

But this hypothesis required a premise!

That the Great Dao laws had condensed into a tangible form, and only then could they shatter into fragments.

This was a scene that even the legends of immortals dared not imagine!

Wasn't the Door of Destruction just a spatial vortex? How could it generate law fragments?

If it was not an ordinary spatial vortex, then what kind of place was the Door of Destruction?

"It seems that hearsay is ultimately not the truth. If I want to gain something, I will have to make a personal trip in the future."

This thought flashed through Luo Chen's mind. He exhaled a turbid breath and turned his gaze to the Moya Wind City Lord.

Facing his stare, the Moya Wind City Lord instead appeared completely at ease.

A man about to die, what was there to fear?

"Heh."

With a light chuckle, Luo Chen turned and left.

As he had said earlier, he had not come to call anyone to account. He merely wanted to understand the situation of the Nine Rings of Destruction.

As for this Moya Wind City Lord, letting him die a natural death could be considered Luo Chen's magnanimity for his non-participation in the siege.

"Three Lakes City Lord?" The Moya Wind City Lord called out to Xiangfei, hesitating as if he wanted to speak.

But Xiangfei could only force a bitter smile.

Although she was not dead, her life and death were now in Luo Chen's hands. She was no longer free.

"I wonder what fate awaits me in the future?"

Sighing mournfully in her heart, Xiangfei followed Luo Chen away.

...

One month later.

The Immortal Bequeathed City.

At dusk, a group of people walked out of the government office.

City Lord Xiao Feng led the way, with Tie Xiong and the others behind him.

If one looked closely, besides Iron Vajra and Silver Hand Yama, the group even included Red Flame Old Monster, Granny Yu, and others who had long been sent out on assignment.

This was merely a routine outing, yet they made such a big production.

To outsiders, it seemed utterly inexplicable.

But only Xiao Feng himself knew what he was guarding against.

"Ten years, and still no news from the people stationed at Moya Wind City?"

Tie Xiong shook his head with a grave expression.

Xiao Feng's face grew even uglier, and he even began to regret his earlier actions.

At first, he could not tolerate Luo Chen's status being equal to his own, so he made his first move.

But after truly witnessing Luo Chen's capabilities, even if the other party rose above him, he would gladly accept it, as long as he could keep his own life.

However, over the past ten years, Luo Chen's demands for resources had been utterly insatiable, which had completely shaken Xiao Feng's rule over the Immortal Bequeathed City.

In his view, Luo Chen was merely treating him as a tool. If one day he could no longer supply the resources Luo Chen demanded, he would be discarded.

Xiao Feng did not want to face that day, so he was forced to make another move!

And this time, he had made ample preparations.

Through the notes of Xiao Feng's ancestor, Xiao Lan of the Immortal Bequeathed, he used the excuse that an immortal cultivator's primordial spirit was a great supplement for body refiners, persuading the seven Returners including West Long City Lord.

The price in interests that the Immortal Bequeathed City had to pay went without saying.

Seven Leakless realm powerhouses, and during Luo Chen's weakened period after collecting the Unforgiving Heavenly Wind—how could they possibly fail?

Yet day after day, year after year passed, and he still had not received news of the six returning.

He sent people to contact the Moya Wind City Lord, but the other party always avoided meeting him.

As time went on, Xiao Feng became increasingly terrified, even recalling all the powerhouses he could summon to protect him at all times.

He was afraid that Luo Chen had not died and would return for revenge.

"Is there any movement from the Luo Estate?" Xiao Feng asked again, unwilling to give up.

Tie Xiong sighed. "Third Lady has been keeping an eye on the Luo Estate. Aside from Steward Zhong dying of old age and Jing Lan promoting a new steward from among the Yan family's disciples, there has been no activity."

"And what about that woman, Jing Lan?"

"She has added some guards around her and spent a large sum to hire a Furnace realm martial artist. But overall, there has been no outrageous behavior. She still goes between Renji Hall and the Luo Estate every day."

Speaking up to here, Tie Xiong said with emotion, "Young Master need not concern yourself with this woman. It seems Luo Chen does not care much for Jing Lan. Spending effort on her would only be in vain."

Xiao Feng was somewhat unwilling. "I just wanted an extra layer of security."

If he could take Jing Lan hostage, perhaps Luo Chen would be hesitant to act, and there might be a chance for negotiation.

As they conversed, the group had already stepped onto the final ten-li-long street leading to the Xiao Estate.

At the end of the long street was the Xiao Estate, and about three li away was the Luo Estate residence that Xiao Feng had once bestowed.

But this time, Xiao Feng could not cross this ten-li-long street.

Because one person was leisurely watching him.

White robes, black hair, sword-like brows, starry eyes.

Just standing there, he drew everyone's attention.

Even Xiao Sanniang, who lay limp and breathless beside Xiangfei, went unnoticed.

Xiao Feng trembled all over, opening his mouth to speak.

"Luo..."

"Watch out!"

Tie Xiong's expression changed drastically. His left arm shot out in a single punch.

But it was already too late.

Luo Feng pointed from afar. A strand of golden astral qi broke through the air, piercing through Tie Xiong's entire left arm like a hot knife through butter, then, with its remaining momentum unabated, lightly tapped Xiao Feng between the eyebrows.

Xiao Feng's eyes bulged wide, and his body slowly fell.

Dead with his eyes open!

In an instant, the entire crowd erupted in uproar.

Tie Xiong let one arm hang limp, unable to accept it.

He was an orphan picked up from the battlefield by the old city lord Xiao Lin, who raised him and found him a famous teacher, allowing him to achieve his current accomplishments.

Since Xiao Lin left for Carefree City, he had made up his mind to protect Xiao Feng.

Even though his cultivation had reached the Leakless realm, his loyalty remained unchanged, and he was willing to humble himself under Xiao Feng.

How could he now accept seeing his young master die before his eyes?

In an instant, his eyes turned bloodshot. He raised his head and glared viciously at Luo Chen.

But Luo Chen did not even look at him.

"Xiangfei, the rest of these people are yours to handle."

Xiangfei said softly, "Kill them all?"

"Kill them. Let the Immortal Bequeathed City change its blood. I don't need disobedient people. When you're done, have Chu Zhaonan lead the Mysterious Armor Army to clean up."

After giving his orders, Luo Chen pushed open the gate of the Luo Estate and walked straight in.

Behind him, Xiangfei's eyes swept over the crowd, her gaze gradually turning icy cold.

If not for these people from the Immortal Bequeathed City stirring up trouble, how would they have gone to provoke that murderous star, Luo Chen?

How would she, the master of Three Lakes City, have fallen into such a state of being controlled by others?

Although Xiao Feng was dead, her anger had not yet dissipated.

"I'll take it out on you all!"

Xiangfei raised her flute and softly played a melody of the souls of the departed.

...

Inside the estate, the crisp sound of the flute could not be heard, only words of concern.

Jing Lan, who had already shown signs of age, personally served Luo Chen hot tea and asked with concern whether his journey had gone smoothly.

At the end, she said with some regret, "No wonder Xiao Sanniang has been getting closer to me these past few years. She was afraid that Brother Luo would retaliate against them and wanted to use me as leverage!"

Luo Chen blew on the steam and savored the ordinary mortal tea.

Xiao Feng's betrayal was unexpected, yet also understandable.

This power of the Immortal Bequeathed City was, after all, not something he had personally fostered, nor had he invested any effort into it.

All he had done was take without giving.

In the eyes of outsiders, it could even be said that he was insatiably greedy, drinking blood and eating flesh.

No matter how obedient, no matter how fearful, when faced with true conflicts of interest, a foolish impulse could ultimately override everything.

Setting down his teacup, Luo Chen looked calmly at Jing Lan.

"If they had truly used you to threaten me, what do you think I should have done?"

Jing Lan was stunned for a moment, then said with a smile, "Brother Luo should do whatever he wants. There's no need to worry about me. Actually..."

The woman looked at the silk and satin on her body, then glanced around, and continued with a satisfied expression, "To have had twenty years of good days like this is already my fortune."

"In that case, then you shall be the master of this Immortal Bequeathed City for me!"

Luo Chen said casually.

As if in his mouth, the master of a grand city was as ordinary as a home-cooked meal, a single sip of tea.

Jing Lan was dumbfounded, her lips trembling. "But I have no martial arts, no prestige, no experience in managing a city."

"It doesn't matter. Xiangfei will assist you. If you encounter any difficulties, just have her take action." Luo Chen said this.

This was also the last reason he had spared Xiangfei's life and brought her back.

From the moment the six Leakless realm powerhouses had surrounded him, he knew that Xiao Feng was the one pulling the strings behind the scenes.

At that time, he had already planned to change his proxy.

Jing Lan, who knew the situation and was relatively trustworthy, was the only candidate.

But this candidate had many shortcomings.

Xiangfei's assistance from the side could perfectly resolve all the shortcomings.

And with her backing and endorsement, Luo Chen's strength could be spread far and wide, and the business the Immortal Bequeathed City wanted to do in the future could grow larger and larger.

Relying on a single city's resources to support his own cultivation was ultimately somewhat harsh.

But as long as the business grew large enough, he could then draw resources from other major cities to nourish himself, especially places like West Long and Purple Gold that had once offended him.

"Oh, right. Move the residence!"

...

The change of master in the Immortal Bequeathed City happened in the blink of an eye.

But the impact it caused shook the entire region for months.

Yet no one dared to question it!

Whether it was Luo Chen's supreme strength in killing Xiao Feng in the street, or the Carefree Token representing Carefree City, both were enough for Jing Lan to secure her position as city lord.

After Jing Lan took power, aside from expanding external business, all other systems remained unchanged from before.

The only change was probably that the Luo Estate had replaced the former Xiao Estate!

One year later.

At the Luo Estate.

The collapse of buildings caused by the great battle within the estate twenty years ago had long been restored to its original state.

Halls stood in rows, with flying eaves and hooked corners.

In early spring, rain fell like pearls, continuously dripping down the eaves.

Before the vast Ten-Thousand-Carp Lake, Luo Chen sat cross-legged in the Tide-Watching Pavilion, with gold and black colors flowing over his body.

His eyes were half-open, half-closed, as if he were observing something.

And in the void invisible to others, there was indeed a light screen hanging.

"*Divine Astral Combat Qi* has unknowingly been cultivated to the Grandmaster level. The proficiency is sufficient. Although no special ability has been born, it is just right for my next step of fusing the two astral qi."

Luo Chen moved his mind and put away the attribute panel.

Then he calmed his heart and released the two distinct astral qi within his body.

As soon as they appeared, the astral qi took form.

The Unforgiving Heavenly Astral transformed into a Heavenly Phoenix spreading its wings and soaring. The Nine Tribulations True Astral evolved into a True Dragon roaring and growling lowly.

Gazing at the two astral qi of completely different natures, a look of firm determination shone in Luo Chen's eyes.

He already had a general idea of how to fuse these two astral qi.

He saw a slight movement at his brow, and a drop of blood as red as fresh dew floated out.

It was precisely the source blood that had not been used up when he advanced to the Furnace realm.

Now only thirty-six drops remained.

This number exactly corresponded to the requirements for cultivating *Divine Astral Combat Qi*.

As if guided, that drop of source blood floated before the Heavenly Phoenix and True Dragon, like a bright pearl held jointly by the dragon and phoenix.

Gradually, the astral qi that had originally appeared in gold and black colors began to stain with a trace of blood light.

Refining True Astral with Blood!

If successful, Luo Chen would be able to possess his very own Divine Astral Combat Qi—the Blood-Refined Divine Astral!

(End of this chapter)
