# Chapter 812: Beast Tide Attacks the City, Reinforcements Arrive, Heavenly Stars Array, Azure Sky All Darkened

Sunset, dusk.

Golden afterglow spilled across the broad streets of Lingtian Pass, dyeing the ground a brilliant gold.

Cultivators bustled to and fro, busy at their tasks.

Only one leisurely figure walked unhurriedly through the city, seeming out of place.

Luo Chen let out a sudden sigh.

"In the end, I'm too idle!"

At such a critical juncture, Huiniang's desire for a child was utterly at odds with her usual character, which valued the big picture above all.

But Luo Chen could understand.

Nowadays, Sima Huiniang had long since stepped down from her position as Luo Tian Sect's Sect Master, with no need to worry about any sect affairs, large or small.

Though she had resigned before, the life-or-death survival of the sect had still hung over her head.

But ever since he returned, even that concern was no longer hers to bear.

Because of the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu matter, Luo Chen had forbidden her from continuing to cultivate.

One thing led to another, and Sima Huiniang had become completely idle.

For a cultivator, not cultivating, not working, not enjoying pleasures, not even holding onto former power—watching their lifespan slip away day by day—that was no different from the most agonizing torture!

To alleviate this torment, Huiniang had volunteered to manage the newly established Luo Tian Treasure Pavilion.

But with Luo Chen's reputation abroad, Luo Tian Sect cultivators assisting wholeheartedly, and behind the scenes, experts from the Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance offering guidance, there was little need for her to exert herself.

In the end, she had conceived an additional idea.

Just like cultivators with insufficient talent who, upon growing older, subconsciously wished to leave behind a legacy or bloodline.

She wanted a child. She wanted to pour all her attention and hope into a descendant of her own blood.

This thought was not wrong. What was wrong was the timing—in this chaotic era.

Even Luo Chen was not fully confident in preserving himself, so how could he easily allow his offspring to face the storms of the cultivation world?

"We'll see in the future!"

Luo Chen sighed faintly and returned quietly to his cave dwelling.

...

Multiple beams of spiritual light floated in midair.

Luo Chen gazed at them for a long moment, then pushed them one by one in order before the Red Dog.

"These illusion core weapons range from magic tools to low-grade true artifacts."

"Though they lack specific refining methods, with my skill in artifact refinement, I can always reverse-engineer rough blueprints based on the formations, materials, and refining techniques. If all else fails, I can use the system to verify whether the blueprints are acceptable."

"Additionally, this is the artifact-refining manual left behind by Feiyunzi back in the day. Study it carefully for reference."

The Red Dog puppet silently accepted these items. With their linked minds, there was no need for Luo Chen's true body to say anything.

Suddenly, it opened its mouth.

Across from it, Luo Chen's fingertip trembled slightly. A clump of blue-white flame slowly flew out and sank into the Red Dog puppet's mouth.

This was a spark of Withering-Bloom True Flame, containing a portion of Luo Chen's mana.

It might not be sufficient for battle.

But it was more than enough to assist the Red Dog puppet in dismantling magic tools and true artifacts.

After giving these instructions, Luo Chen entered another secret chamber within the cave dwelling.

Inside, the chill was biting, with blocks of ice piled high.

An ice coffin lay in the center, Canglong resting quietly within, still frowning.

Luo Chen's hand flickered, and a book of ice made from mystic ice appeared.

*The Ten Directions Extreme Freezing Dao*

This was precisely the core cultivation technique of the former Icecastle Sect, only cultivatable by inner disciples with water spirit roots or ice-type aberrant spirit roots.

When Heartless Fairy had handed over the ice coffin to Luo Chen, she had also given him this book.

This was an act of great rebellion—transmitting the sect's core technique to an outsider!

But Icecastle was already a shadow of its former self. To save Canglong, Heartless Fairy had no choice.

Luo Chen's gaze swept over the ice book. He was not particularly concerned with the cultivation methods within.

Even though this was a fourth-grade technique that could lead straight to the Sage stage.

What he cared about were the several sealing arts and formations recorded within.

"Ice Extreme Seal—I've already mastered it. I can lift the seal on Canglong and awaken her at any time."

"But the time is not yet right!"

"In the coming days, I may not have much time, so I must prepare for the worst. I need to master these ice-type formations as soon as possible, to channel the profound cold power of mystic ice and prevent the ice coffin from collapsing."

Luo Chen's thoughts flickered as he read through the formations in *The Ten Directions Extreme Freezing Dao* word by word.

...

Three days later.

In the western tower of Lingtian Pass, the Black Ape puppet slowly opened its eyes.

In its field of vision, Luo Chen walked in, looking somewhat weary.

"I've taken care of things on my end. I'll personally guard this place from now on. Don't slack off—I have another important task for you."

"This is the puppet art submitted to the sect by Qin Yuanjiang, originating from the Falling Cloud Sect lineage."

"Study it well. You might find the specific methods Han Zhan used back then to control that White Tiger puppet."

The Black Ape puppet silently accepted the thick volumes and went to a corner to read quietly.

At first, Qin Yuanjiang had not submitted these puppet arts.

It was only after Luo Chen returned and taught him the *Soul-Splitting Art* that he handed them over after careful consideration.

To be honest, these puppet arts might be somewhat exquisite, but their level was truly not high—not even comparable to the ancient puppet arts Luo Chen had collected in the Northern Sea years ago.

But what mattered was that these puppet arts shared the same lineage as Han Zhan!

Back then, Han Zhan had subdued that White Tiger puppet at the early Sage stage, relying entirely on Falling Cloud Sect puppet arts.

Perhaps Luo Chen's opportunity to master that puppet lay within these texts.

If he could acquire that puppet as a companion, he would gain another trump card for the coming great battle.

Once the Black Ape had settled down, Luo Chen glanced at the crescent moon outside the western tower, like a curved hook. He waved his hand casually at the ground.

A Five Elements Lotus Platform materialized.

He drifted up to sit cross-legged, placed a high-grade Hongyuan Pill in his mouth, and entered a state of meditation.

Around dawn, Luo Chen's weariness had completely faded. He felt his spirit and qi at their peak!

Without any hesitation, he laid the Yuantu Sword across his knees, drew a wisp of Scarlet Firmament Qi into his mouth, and began circulating it between himself and the sword.

...

That day.

In the dead of night, Luo Chen suddenly opened his eyes.

A wisp of Scarlet Firmament Qi turned to smoke and sank into his chest.

Without a moment's hesitation, he flew out, landing on the pitch-black city wall. He raised his head and gazed into the distance.

The distance was too great; his divine sense could not perceive clearly.

But he had another pair of eyes that could see through thousands of miles!

In his vision, dots of torchlight lit up all at once.

Vaguely, he could hear commotion rising within the city.

"Has the battle begun?"

Just as everyone had discussed before, after the arrival of the Yellow Barbarian, the demon race's strength had doubled, and they could no longer hold back.

They hadn't even lasted half a month before launching an attack.

As the eastern gate directly facing the demon army, it bore the brunt of the assault from the very first moment.

At this very moment, millions of cultivators were rushing toward the eastern city.

Perhaps having learned from the lesson of the Seven Rhinoceros Commander, who had failed to breach Lingtian Pass by force and suffered severe injury instead, the demon race had not sent top-tier experts to directly storm the pass this time.

Instead, they dispatched numerous low-grade demon beasts to assault the four city gates.

Luo Chen soared into the air, reaching a high altitude.

His sharp eyes surveyed all directions.

Under the night sky, demon beasts surged like a flood from the east.

Upon reaching the plains outside Lingtian Pass, the massive beast tide split into two streams, encircling the northern and southern flanks.

It was as if Lingtian Pass were an elephant, and the demon beasts were ants even smaller, splitting into three streams to surround and devour this elephant.

"I hope the fellow daoists can hold on!"

Luo Chen sighed, then instructed Zeng Yilong and the others, who were on high alert, to prepare to eliminate any demon beasts that came near.

He had no intention of going to support.

The four Sages each guarded one direction; none could easily abandon their posts.

Relatively speaking, the western district, located in the rear, was the safest spot.

If not for Luo Chen having established his reputation as an Alchemy Saint earlier and receiving preferential treatment, this position would not have fallen to him.

For now, he had gotten off easy, not having to bear the brunt of the demon army's assault.

But Luo Chen knew well that as the number of demon beasts grew and Demon Emperor-level commanders arrived one after another, the western city would eventually be surrounded as well.

"When will reinforcements arrive?"

...

The sounds of battle from the east, south, and north never ceased.

From deep night until dawn.

One formation after another was deployed under the direction of cultivators within the city.

Devices resembling spirit energy cannons consumed spirit stones without restraint, unleashing ferocious attacks on the demon beasts outside the city.

Occasionally, Sage-level demon emperors launched probing attacks against Lingtian Pass.

In response, the Lingtian City Lord activated the city-protecting grand formation.

A mysterious sword traversed all directions. Though it could not strike out, neither could anyone enter.

At this point, the demon race's experts understood that taking Lingtian Pass by individual might alone was impossible.

Only one option remained—attrition!

Once the large and small formations of Lingtian Pass were worn down, and the resources within the city were exhausted, the Black Tortoise Four Directions Formation would become nothing more than a formation.

The battle raged on without end!

Time passed, day by day.

By the fifth day, scattered remnants of demon beasts began appearing at the western city that Luo Chen guarded.

These were broken remnants routed from the main battlefield.

Without hesitation, Luo Chen ordered their extermination.

Under the leadership of Adept-level cultivators, the western city's cultivators charged out of the city, hunting down these demon beast remnants one by one.

Luo Chen had deliberately chosen not to use the western city's formations.

These scattered remnants were not worth deploying killing formations.

It was better to use this opportunity to temper the western city's cultivators.

A bloody battle soon unfolded before Luo Chen's eyes.

He extended his divine sense to its fullest, covering a thousand miles around the battlefield, on guard against any Sage-level demon emperor launching a surprise attack on the western city.

But clearly, for now, the demon emperors' attention was focused on the main battlefield, and they had not yet come to his side.

Thus, the battle among his subordinates remained within a controllable range.

Luo Chen deliberately focused part of his attention on the Luo Tian Sect cultivators, especially Zeng Yilong.

Having received the Broken Moon Wings as a reward, his speed weakness had been completely filled.

When Zeng Yilong was young, Luo Chen had taught him the Ninety Thousand Li technique and gifted him a pair of top-grade flying wings.

Those wings had been destroyed on the dueling platform.

Now, the Broken Moon Wings were of high-grade true artifact level—a tiger gaining wings for Zeng Yilong!

In his line of sight, Zeng Yilong spread his wings, holding the Ten-Thousand-Jun Halberd. When still, he was motionless; when he moved, he covered hundreds of feet in an instant, delivering a thunderous strike.

Low-grade demon beasts died in a single blow, reduced to pulp.

Even ordinary third-grade demon kings could not withstand more than a few exchanges.

Seeing this, Luo Chen breathed a slight sigh of relief.

"Wang Yuan's training of him over the years has been truly excellent. This boy has inherited his father's innate battle instincts, and now with the treasures I've bestowed upon him. As long as no late third-grade great demon king appears, or he doesn't fall into an encirclement of demon beasts, no one can harm him."

Luo Chen was very satisfied. Zeng Yilong's performance fully justified the Luo Tian Sect's investment in him.

As long as he did not fall prematurely, he would become a banner figure for the Luo Tian Sect in the future!

As for the other Luo Tian Sect cultivators, they also performed well in the melee.

Occasionally, when one was exhausted, comrades would cover their retreat.

The large number of talismans Luo Chen had bestowed in the past also began to shine, killing many demon beasts in succession.

"It's a pity I haven't mastered the art of external incarnations. If I could split off an incarnation to mass-produce talismans, I would shine brilliantly in this battle!"

Luo Chen still harbored lingering thoughts about techniques like the second Nascent Soul and external incarnations.

Suddenly!

Luo Chen's divine sense stirred, landing on something hundreds of miles away.

A massive fluctuation of spiritual energy rushed toward him without any concealment.

He raised his eyes and looked into the distance. In the sky, a large flying boat was breaking through the air.

On the flying boat flew a red flag with mysterious patterns.

It was the Divine Talisman Sect!

Elder Fu stood on the flying boat, gazing from afar at Lingtian Pass.

When he saw Luo Chen, he smiled and nodded in greeting.

Luo Chen also breathed a sigh of relief.

Reinforcements had finally arrived!

*Boom!*

The flying boat docked ten miles outside the city gate.

Luo Chen transmitted his voice from afar: "No need to divert people to deal with the battle here. Submit to inspection and enter the city directly!"

Elder Fu understood instantly.

Clearly, compared to the scattered skirmishes outside the western city, the pressure on the main battlefield was far greater.

During the interval when the Divine Talisman Sect cultivators were entering the city one after another, Luo Chen exchanged a few simple words with Elder Fu.

"Has young friend Yelan succeeded?"

"With the aid of your pill, he has finally crossed that threshold."

"Congratulations, how delightful!"

"Haha, if he hadn't succeeded, I couldn't have come to Lingtian Pass with peace of mind!"

"When you have free time, how about a few cups of wine?"

"An invitation from the Alchemy Saint—I dare not refuse. This old man will certainly come!"

After a brief exchange, Elder Fu led several thousand cultivators into Lingtian Pass one after another.

The Divine Talisman Sect was one of the relatively intact Sage-level sects in terms of organizational structure.

Today, they had brought several thousand people, and this was only a portion.

The "young friend Yelan" Luo Chen mentioned was the Golden Core Great Perfection cultivator from the Divine Talisman Sect who urgently needed a Nascent Soul Pill.

To be honest, though Luo Chen had never met this man, there was some connection between them.

His elder brother was none other than Ye Lao, who had participated in the war to open up the Xiaoyue Mountain Range back in the day.

The arrival of the Divine Talisman Sect's reinforcements seemed to be a signal.

From then on, more and more cultivator troops set out from the five domains of Lingtian, arriving at Lingtian Pass one after another.

The three sects of Fenghua Domain—Fenghua Palace, Motian Cliff, and even the Five Elements Divine Sect—arrived first.

Then came the Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion, the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect...

The number of Sage-level cultivators within the city increased sharply, exerting immense pressure on the outside.

At some point, the beast tide besieging Lingtian Pass, leaving behind a dense layer of corpses, gradually dispersed.

Clearly, the demon race had also noticed the changes in Lingtian Pass and ceased their futile attrition.

Luo Chen no longer had to stand guard on the city wall day and night.

But just as he was about to leave, the City Lord's Mansion sent someone with a strangely shaped compass.

"What is this?"

Luo Chen fiddled with the artifact—square on the outside, round on the inside, with an uneven surface—showing a puzzled expression.

Ye Hong, who had delivered the item, explained: "The City Lord instructed that this be buried at the base of the city wall, and that all true persons should activate it with their mana."

Though puzzled, Luo Chen followed the instructions.

He drifted down from the wall, placed the strange compass at the base, and then poured mana into it.

The moment the mana entered, a tremendous suction force arose, as if it would drain Luo Chen dry.

Luo Chen frowned but did not stop.

After all, it was just an artifact. How much of his mana could it hold?

Sure enough, after consuming one-tenth of his mana, the suction gradually weakened.

The round ball inside the square compass seemed to have had its fill, spinning rapidly.

Suddenly!

*Whoosh!*

The ball transformed into a beam of light, shooting straight into the sky, disappearing beyond the horizon in the blink of an eye.

Even when Luo Chen tried to sense it with his divine sense, he found nothing.

At the same time, beams of light erupted from various points within the city, piercing into the azure sky above.

As Luo Chen watched in astonishment, a blurry figure hovered in the air, suspended at the center of Lingtian Pass.

He pointed into the void, and the beams of light gradually faded.

The originally clear sky began to darken.

"The sun and moon wax and wane; the stars and constellations spread across the heavens."

"Heavenly stars arrayed; the azure sky all darkened!"

Beneath the dark canopy, stars flickered into being, arrayed in all directions, perfectly enveloping Lingtian Pass.

As the blurry figure suddenly opened his hand, the dark canopy abruptly dispersed, and the azure sky and stars were no more.

It was as if nothing had happened.

But Luo Chen knew very well that many things had changed.

It was as if something in the sky was watching him.

He felt somewhat uncomfortable and cast several concealment spells on himself, which somewhat reduced that sensation.

Luo Chen looked up, lost in thought.

"That person just now... did he tamper with the azure sky, or did he borrow the hands of us true persons?"

He lowered his head slightly. The token at his waist trembled gently, and the voice of the Lingtian City Lord came through.

"Everyone, please come to the City Lord's Mansion for a meeting!"
