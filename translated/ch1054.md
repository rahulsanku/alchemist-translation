# Chapter 1054: Primordial Magnetic Aurora, An Enemy Appears

The Arctic Yemo Heaven!

Once the mountain gate of the Nether Origin Sect, the holy land of the Northern Sea!

It was itself an excellent cultivation site, especially with its natural defenses, capable of serving as the eternal foundation for a large sect.

With the Fallen Sea at its back, where demon beasts descended for a thousand years, it was difficult for them to approach. In front, the Primordial Magnetic Aurora suppressed the Five Elements, making it impossible for ordinary cultivators to break in.

How exactly had the Northern Sea Demon Alliance and that Rosycloud managed to charge their way in?

But Luo Chen couldn't yet spare the thought to consider how to reach his destination. He first needed to pass through the Arctic Demon-Sealing Formation.

In a world of shimmering starlight, chaotic and hazy, Luo Chen walked slowly, his expression somewhat solemn.

Holding his token, his speed was not as fast as he had imagined.

"Master, did Li Canghai deceive us?" Xiao Bai asked worriedly.

Luo Chen gazed ahead, searching for clues within the formation as he answered Xiao Bai.

"He didn't deceive us. Otherwise, we wouldn't be so safe and sound."

"But we're still trapped inside this great formation!"

Within the Arctic Demon-Sealing Formation, there was an extremely powerful sealing and suppressing force.

That force locked a cultivator's mana tightly, even seeming to slow down the circulation of thoughts.

Not only that, but the surrounding starlight pressed down like mountains, suppressing the physical bodies of all living beings.

Even someone as strong as Luo Chen, walking inside now, felt as if he were trudging through a swamp or quagmire.

But in Luo Chen's view, this was not Li Canghai playing tricks on him. Rather, he marveled at Li Canghai's mastery of formations.

"As expected of a formation-path Ascendant!"

Luo Chen's eyes blazed with golden light as he surveyed his surroundings, even lifting his head to gaze at the sky.

"Using the earth's spirit veins as a foundation, drawing upon the power of the stars, and even absorbing a portion of the Primordial Magnetic Aurora's strength, he formed this heaven-and-earth-spanning sealing formation!"

"Even though the impact of the Fallen Sea's power broke the formation, decades later, it is actually repairing itself."

"Operating normally, ceaseless and unending. As long as this land remains, and these stars endure, the formation's power will persist. I wonder if this formation itself seizes the wonders of heaven and earth, or if it is the so-called laws of formation dao that maintain all this?"

Amidst his musings, Luo Chen stopped.

He even sat down cross-legged on the spot, entering a resting state, as if waiting for something.

Xiao Bai did not understand, but she did not ask.

When it came to knowledge of formations, her master surpassed her by a thousandfold.

---

Outside the formation, three figures followed in.

The moment they entered, they were immediately caught in the mountain-like, immense suppression.

Even the demon monk Kongchan, under this tremendous pressure, could barely move, frozen in place.

"Stream Lord, quickly use the Magnetic Sea Longitudinal Shuttle!"

But Liujun only shook his head solemnly. "The Magnetic Sea Longitudinal Shuttle is specifically for passing through the Primordial Magnetic Mountain Path. This formation was laid by the great power Li Canghai; that shuttle is useless."

The demon monk Kongchan was stunned. "Then how did you get out of Yemo Heaven back then?"

Shark Emperor Liujun smiled bitterly. "The two ancestors joined forces to open a crack in the Billion-Soul Demon-Locking Formation. Then, using a Magnetic Sea Longitudinal Shuttle specially refined by the Nether Origin Sect, I passed through the Primordial Magnetic Mountain. As for this Arctic Demon-Sealing Formation, the method of passing through requires a different trick."

"What trick?"

"Wait!" Liujun took a deep breath and looked up at the sky. "Wait for the stars to move, for the starlight to weaken. Then, restrain your mana and walk slowly. Only then can we break out of this formation."

The demon monk Kongchan was stunned. "That simple?"

"Simple?" Liujun laughed in anger. "You'll know whether it's simple or difficult when the time comes."

Time passed slowly. After about three sunrises and three moonsets, the starlight within the Arctic Demon-Sealing Formation dimmed slightly.

Liujun's eyes lit up. "Lucky, we didn't have to wait long. Let's go!"

With that, he carefully took a step forward.

Taisui and Kongchan beside him also perked up and hurriedly took a step.

But the moment they lifted their feet, the suppressing force struck again. Though not as terrifying as before, it still made their movements extremely difficult. Taisui even stumbled, nearly falling flat on his backside.

Liujun glanced at them and walked forward at a steady, unhurried pace.

"Still simple now?"

The two behind him looked at each other, and could only restrain their impatience, following behind him.

Walking ahead, Shark Emperor Liujun spoke softly, imparting his experience to the two behind him.

"This formation is too ingenious. It draws upon the power of stars from beyond the heavens, so it can automatically recover and operate endlessly. Even if we seize the cover of celestial timing, taking advantage of the starlight's weakening to traverse it, we still cannot underestimate that power."

"After all, this is a fifth-rank formation laid by a great power!"

"Follow my rhythm step by step. Do not mobilize the mana within your body. Like this, after about twelve cycles, we should be able to pass through this formation."

---

"Master, the starlight is weakening!"

"Mm."

Luo Chen slowly rose, silently watching the sky for a long moment, then nodded slightly.

"Let's go!"

Token in hand, he took a single step, and the next moment, he appeared ten zhang away.

When he landed, his steps faltered slightly.

Luo Chen frowned, silently calculating, then took another step to the left.

This time, the distance he crossed exceeded fifty zhang!

The corner of Luo Chen's mouth lifted slightly. "Just as I thought!"

After that, he no longer hesitated. Treading with a strange footwork, maintaining a unique rhythm, he wove left and right within the formation, yet always moving forward.

Within the Arctic Demon-Sealing Formation, the black-clad figure fluttered like a butterfly, dancing gracefully without any pause or stagnation.

It was as if the omnipresent, terrifying suppressing force had vanished.

After about a day and a night, Luo Chen's vision suddenly brightened.

Before him, a violent blizzard howled fiercely. Behind him, starlight flowed like an ocean.

Xiao Bai's cheerful voice rang in his ears.

"We're finally out! Master, you're amazing!"

Luo Chen shook his head with a light laugh. "It's not that I'm amazing. It's just that this token Li Canghai gave me allowed me to easily see through the operating principles of this formation."

He didn't explain further, instead focusing his attention on the blizzard ahead, which surged forward endlessly before constantly retracting.

His expression grew somewhat solemn.

Though solemn, he stepped into it without hesitation.

Because this thousand-year, unceasing blizzard was his destination!

The Nine Heavens Firmament Qi he sought was within the Arctic Yemo Heaven, yet not within Yemo Heaven itself. The true location was inside the Primordial Magnetic Aurora, which the Nether Origin Sect had relied upon as their protective barrier!

The moment he entered, wind and snow struck him.

The mana shield he had deployed was violently battered.

Bang! Bang! Bang!

Luo Chen advanced without hesitation. The ground beneath his feet was uneven; upon closer inspection, it was covered in layers of ferocious beast bones.

Who knew how many demon beasts were buried on this mountain path?

Perhaps most of them were the dead from the battle to attack the Nether Origin Sect back then?

Suddenly!

Luo Chen felt gaps appear in his deployed mana shield.

And they weren't torn open by impact, but gnawed away by something.

The Daoist abruptly opened his eyes. His crimson-golden pupils expanded, clearly revealing palm-sized insects.

These insects were carried by the blizzard, quickly gnawing holes in Luo Chen's protective light shield, which was so tough that even ordinary Sage cultivators couldn't break it.

Such a sight would make anyone pale.

But Luo Chen, instead, breathed a sigh of relief.

"Indeed, there are still large numbers of Firmament Insects here. That means there will be large amounts of Nine Heavens Firmament Qi nearby!"

In the Northern Sea, there was a type of demon beast called the Rosy Cloud Beast!

Born in the sea, dwelling in misty places, feeding on all kinds of auroras.

Rumor had it that in the Primordial Magnetic Aurora land of the Arctic Yemo Heaven, large numbers of Rosy Cloud Beasts gathered.

Luo Chen had known this rumor two hundred years ago, and had even encountered someone disguised as a Rosy Cloud Beast.

It wasn't until later, when he ascended to the Nine Heavens to capture Firmament Qi, that he cross-referenced the two and realized that the so-called Rosy Cloud Beasts were actually Firmament Insects!

Though their names differed, they were essentially the same existence.

Back then, Luo Chen hadn't made the connection. After Li Canghai's guidance, he figured it out: since Firmament Insects fed on Firmament Qi, places with large numbers of Firmament Insects would inevitably have traces of large amounts of Firmament Qi.

Reasoning backward, the Arctic Yemo Heaven was clearly the place where Firmament Qi was most concentrated in the world!

Now, with Li Canghai's help, having forcibly crossed the Arctic Demon-Sealing Formation, Luo Chen had finally reached his destination.

He pressed forward, charging through the blizzard.

Gradually, the mana shield on his body kept dissolving, unable to withstand the gnawing attacks of the Firmament Insects.

But Luo Chen wasn't particularly alarmed. With a thought, he spoke a true incantation.

"Armor!"

The next moment, a suit of pitch-black armor materialized on Luo Chen's body.

The armor was ancient, covered in patterns as if carved by knives and axes, full of a sense of vicissitude.

Spiritual light flickered, forming a thick qi shield outside the armor itself.

This was Luo Chen's newly refined Mystic Dust Armor!

Supplemented with the Xuanming Turtle's origin blood and four top-grade lotus seeds, after decades of Luo Chen's nurturing, the defensive power of the newly emerged Mystic Dust Armor had skyrocketed wildly.

The violent wind and snow beat against it, unable to shake even the qi shield.

Even though large numbers of Firmament Insects gnawed at it, they were like mayflies trying to topple a tree, utterly overestimating themselves!

Luo Chen walked step by step. Midway, he suddenly looked up.

"It's on the mountain!"

Without a second thought, Luo Chen shot into the sky.

In this process, the Mystic Dust qi shield on his body inexplicably dissipated. The nearby freely flying Firmament Insects, seeing this, screeched and swarmed forward again.

Luo Chen paid them no mind. Even without the qi shield, the defensive power of the Mystic Dust Armor's main body was even more formidable.

Ignoring the siege of the Firmament Insects, his gaze swept over two large mountains beside him.

On the mountains, wisps of peculiar gray-white light emanated. This light pervaded the entire void, extending all the way up to the Nine Heavens.

"These mountains are Primordial Magnetic Mountains!"

"This light is the Primordial Magnetic Aurora, said to overcome all Five Elements!"

"So that's why the qi shield automatically released by the Mystic Dust Armor disappeared for no reason."

"And that's why large amounts of Nine Heavens Firmament Qi come here on their own—they are swept by the Primordial Magnetic Aurora and forced down."

Having understood this, Luo Chen landed on a mountain peak.

Looking at the large number of Firmament Insects swarming around him, Luo Chen snorted coldly.

The next moment, terrifying Scarlet Firmament Sword Qi erupted from his body, cutting down a large patch of Firmament Insect corpses.

Then, with a slap of his hand, the Nine Firmament Urn from the Nine Spirits Sect landed beside him.

Luo Chen formed a hand seal, and the Nine Firmament Urn began to tremble. The Nine Heavens Firmament Qi that had accumulated for centuries surged toward the urn in a torrent.

Within the boundless wind and snow, rosy light pervaded, all returning to a single urn.

This scene was breathtakingly beautiful!

Luo Chen's heart was also full of joy.

Although this Nine Heavens Firmament Qi was mixed and not entirely the three types he needed, it was still far better than his aimless searching through the azure void.

The time saved was no less than three to five years!

"I'll remember this favor from Li Canghai."

Luo Chen chuckled lightly, slowly closed his eyes, and spread his divine sense to assist the Nine Firmament Urn in collecting more Firmament Qi.

---

A year later.

Luo Chen suddenly opened his eyes. His large hand slapped the Nine Firmament Urn, stopping the collection of Firmament Qi.

Xiao Bai noticed this and asked curiously, "Master, what's wrong?"

Luo Chen said nothing, his gaze falling on the mountain path below, shrouded in the year-round blizzard.

This was the only path into the Arctic Yemo Heaven.

Within, there was the assault of the extreme-cold blizzard, the attacks of Firmament Insects, and the suppression of the Five Elements by the Primordial Magnetic Aurora. Just the natural geographical dangers alone were not something ordinary cultivators could pass.

During the Nether Origin Sect's peak, with a few strong cultivators stationed there, it was the most impregnable path in the world.

This was evident from the countless layers of beast bones and corpses littering the mountain path.

Even now, without strong cultivators guarding it, the omnipresent dangers should not be something that just anyone dared to force their way through.

But in his earlier divine sense perception, he had indeed sensed someone breaking in.

"Is it that chanting monk stationed in Taohai City?"

Under Luo Chen's gaze, a streak of light broke through the air, traveling upstream against the fierce blizzard.

The surrounding Firmament Insects paid it no heed, and even the Primordial Magnetic Aurora enveloping the mountain path seemed to have no effect.

Looking closely, the streak of light was clearly a strangely shaped flying shuttle.

"A flying shuttle forged from a single piece of Primordial Magnetic ore?"

Luo Chen was somewhat surprised inwardly, but soon a hint of astonishment appeared on his face.

With his composure, even when alone, he shouldn't be so unsettled. But this time, there was a reason.

Inside the shuttle were three people.

One of them, Luo Chen had recognized when he previously infiltrated the ruined city of Taohai—it was clearly that late fourth-stage chanting monk, known as the demon monk Kongchan, famous throughout the Northern Sea.

As for the other two...

"Shark Emperor Liujun!"

"And that other fellow... why does he also give me a feeling of familiarity... no, even a feeling of closeness?"

Luo Chen frowned slightly, coldly watching the shuttle disappear into the blizzard, gradually vanishing without a trace.

Their target should be the true Nether Origin Sect mountain gate, the Arctic Yemo Heaven.

Li Canghai's Arctic Demon-Sealing Formation was on the outside, while the Billion-Soul Demon-Locking Formation laid by the Nether Origin Sect's soul-refining lineage ancestor was on the inside.

The former was laid by a formation-path great power, so after being damaged, it could repair itself, gradually returning to its peak. But the soul-refining ancestor's formation had been largely ineffective since the year when large numbers of demon beasts used the Fallen Sea's power to break out.

These three had spent a year breaking through the Arctic Demon-Sealing Formation, and had passed through the Primordial Magnetic Mountain Path without injury. Ahead, the path should be smooth.

Luo Chen had a grudge with Shark Emperor Liujun, but for now, collecting Firmament Qi to cultivate his sword canon took priority. There was no need to create complications.

But that fellow who gave him a feeling of familiarity and closeness made Luo Chen unable to sit still.

"Besides Cheng Haixin in the Northern Sea, why is there someone else who can give me this feeling?"

Amidst deep confusion, Luo Chen softly instructed:

"Xiao Bai, follow them in and check on the movements of those three for me."

Xiao Bai materialized from Luo Chen's body, standing gracefully beside him.

"Alright!"

Luo Chen thought for a moment, reached into his body, and slapped Xiao Bai. A black light sank into her body.

"I've infused the Mystic Dust Armor into you. It contains a strand of my mana, enough to protect you in a critical moment."

"As for those three, just observe. Don't make a move!"

"If anything changes, or if you encounter danger, notify me."

Xiao Bai hummed in acknowledgment, a smile on her face. Then she jumped off the Primordial Magnetic Mountain and flew toward Yemo Heaven.

Her speed was neither fast nor slow, and the surrounding wind, snow, and Firmament Insects posed no obstacle.

Seeing this, Luo Chen immediately understood that soul bodies were not affected by this place.

"This is a loophole for safely entering and leaving the Nether Origin Sect's mountain gate... Hah, at its peak, the Nether Origin Sect, renowned throughout the Mountains and Seas Realm for its soul-refining lineage, would hardly fear mere ghost cultivators or soul bodies sneaking into the mountain gate."

He laughed at himself mockingly, then sat down cross-legged again.

The Firmament Qi collected was still not enough. Continue!
