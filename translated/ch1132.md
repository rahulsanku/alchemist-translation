# Chapter 1132: Ghost Nightmare Enters the Dream

"Ghostly presence?"

Luo Chen frowned slightly, staring quietly outside the door.

At some unknown moment, strands of bloodshot veins had already spread across the whites of his eyes.

Divine sense was unusable, spiritual power was completely gone—but that didn't mean his eyes had gone "blind."

After centuries of nurturing, his Crimson Eye Golden Pupil had long reached great completion, and by drawing on source power, he could still activate it to some extent.

A pair of blood-red eyes peered through the door and window toward the outside. Under the night sky, the torrential rain obscured all external objects, but that uncomfortable sensation still lingered.

"What exactly is lurking in the shadows, spying on me?"

With a thought, Luo Chen mobilized even more source power, gathering it in his eyes.

Gradually, faint outlines began to take shape amidst the wind and rain.

These outlines were not fixed. Sometimes they resembled humans, sometimes wild beasts, and more often than not, they scattered into smoke, merging indistinguishably with the rain vapor, making them extremely difficult to detect.

It lingered outside the door, seeming eager to barge in, yet blocked by something, appearing anxious and restless.

Luo Chen subconsciously looked toward the fire pit inside the room, where wisps of blue smoke from the dreamstone were spreading.

"Strange beings that come with the ghost rain, yet blocked by the blue smoke from burning dreamstone—what exactly are they?"

Curious, Luo Chen rose to his feet.

At the doorway, he hesitated for a moment, then picked up the tarnished green lamp.

He had nothing else on him. Bold as he was with his skills, this was ultimately the unfamiliar Great Destruction Realm, and caution was paramount.

This tarnished green lamp was at least a top-grade magic artifact, and he could activate it to some extent using his blood and qi power.

A stream of blood and qi was injected into it, and a green light suddenly flared.

Carrying the green lamp, Luo Chen opened the door and stepped outside.

The moment he emerged, the ghostly thing in the rain curtain rushed forward impatiently, but upon reaching him, it was blocked by the vigorous yang force that had already spread across his entire body.

Using the light of the green lamp artifact, Luo Chen carefully observed the blue smoke swirling around him, a contemplative look in his eyes, as if searching for a corresponding record in his memory.

Gradually, a hint of doubt appeared in his eyes.

"Could it really be that legendary thing?"

The next moment!

Luo Chen deliberately opened a gap in his vigorous yang force, allowing the ghostly thing to pounce onto his body.

Instantly, his body stiffened, as if something heavy had pressed down on him, though his eyes remained bright.

After a long moment, Luo Chen's blood and qi suddenly swelled. The thing that had rushed in earlier was directly washed away by the formidable blood and qi, cleansed without a trace.

Vaguely, Luo Chen seemed to hear a scream.

Under the rain curtain, Luo Chen stood beneath the eaves, lamp in hand, a look of sudden realization dawning on his face.

"So that's what it really is!"

Through that personal experience just now, Luo Chen had verified a certain record in his memory. That strange thing was a creature called a "ghost nightmare."

This being was extremely rare, but legends about ghost nightmares had long circulated in the world.

Ghost nightmares, also known as dream demons!

They were a type of ghostly entity with no actual physical form, yet truly existing.

They were as famous as the well-known specters, phantoms, and other ghostly beings.

But for the Mountains and Seas Realm, this creature existed only in legends, rarely encountered.

The reason was that ghost nightmares fed by invading the dreams of living beings, plunging them into anxiety, unease, anger, fear, and other negative emotions within their dreams, until they finally sank into nightmares from which they could hardly awaken. The ghost nightmare could then take the opportunity to absorb the pure soul power of the living. However, the Mountains and Seas Realm was a world dominated by cultivators. Let alone powerful Ascendants and Sages, even the weakest Qi Refinement cultivators had already begun to condense their divine souls. By the Initiate stage, they could even release their spiritual sense, illuminate their surroundings, and repel all evil.

In such an environment, ghost nightmares simply couldn't survive!

Even if ordinary mortals occasionally encountered ghost nightmares, they were only the weakest ones. Those with slightly stronger minds, or even just clearer-headed individuals, could awaken from their nightmares. After waking up, at most they would fearfully mutter about "ghost pressing the bed," without losing too much spiritual energy.

Over time, ghost nightmares, lacking food, existed only in legends.

To put it mockingly, this peculiar existence had no soil for survival and simply starved to death.

The reason Luo Chen knew about this creature was that when he had refined the Fate Mirror artifact back then, he had studied a large number of formations related to illusions and dreams, thus building up this knowledge base.

He never expected to encounter a real ghost nightmare in the Great Destruction Realm.

Though there was only one, and it was equally weak.

The slight interest he had just felt vanished in an instant.

Shaking his head, Luo Chen picked up the green lamp and prepared to return indoors.

But his footsteps stopped mid-air the moment he pushed open the door, as if a flash of inspiration had struck his mind.

"According to what Jing Lan said, whenever the ghost rain comes, the villagers light their stored dreamstone. It seems now that this is clearly a precaution against ghost nightmares."

"Such a traditional custom didn't form overnight. It must be because someone fell victim long ago, had their dreams invaded by ghost nightmares, and had their soul power devoured."

"In other words, this Great Destruction Realm must contain a large number of ghost nightmares that appear along with the ghost rain."

"And in this world, cultivators cannot survive long, so naturally there is no situation where powerful divine souls can grind and crush ghost nightmares. Even high-level body refiners mostly rely on their vigorous blood and qi and strong will for passive defense."

"Thus, over time, wouldn't powerful, ancient ghost nightmares be born?"

At this thought, a gleam of light flashed through Luo Chen's eyes, which had already dimmed.

A certain hypothesis leaped into his mind!

Back then, when he refined the true artifact Fate Mirror, his purpose was to allow Canglong to strengthen his soul foundation through sleep, letting him break free on his own.

Therefore, Luo Chen had consulted a large number of formation texts related to illusions and dreams.

Among the knowledge he had acquired, it was mentioned that ghost nightmares were the most suitable for arranging dreams and also the most suitable for breaking soul prohibitions!

After all, this creature itself fed on the soul power of living beings.

At the time, Luo Chen hadn't paid much attention to the latter point, because his own soul was extremely powerful, and he was also skilled at breaking soul prohibitions.

But now...

Luo Chen's divine sense could not leave his body, and his formidable soul foundation had no place to be used—all because his main soul had been sealed by Rosycloud.

With his own strength, it was very difficult to break Rosycloud's soul seal, after all, at her peak, she was an unmatched existence at the Void Refining stage.

The sealing of his main soul had triggered a chain reaction.

The most direct consequence was that even though Luo Chen could convert blood and qi power into a small amount of spiritual power, without divine sense to assist, he still couldn't open his storage ring.

But if he could use a large number of ghost nightmares, or powerful ones, to forcibly break through Rosycloud's soul seal, then his soul could escape its cage!

Even though the Great Destruction Realm had the soul-shaking thunder sound that intimidated cultivators, with divine sense, Luo Chen could still do many more things.

For example, opening his storage ring and taking out some materials inside.

Or using divine sense to illuminate his body and assist in body refinement.

Even deducing techniques, gaining insight into the essence of this world, and so on.

After all, divine sense simply couldn't leave the body; it could still be used internally.

Luo Chen's thoughts churned, ideas surging endlessly.

"Hmm..."

A murmur of sleep came from inside the room. It seemed that the crack in the door had let in some cold wind, making the sleeping woman feel a chill.

Luo Chen looked at the pitch-black night, a gleam of light flickering in his eyes before finally settling into calm.

"No rush. One step at a time!"

He returned to the room, closed the doors and windows tightly, and resumed his cultivation, as if nothing had happened.
