# Chapter 897: Fifth-Order Immortal Gu, Crimson Eyes and Golden Pupils

Solve the Devil Heart Gu simply?

If it were truly that simple, it wouldn’t have plagued Luo Chen for decades.

It was precisely after deeply engaging with this Gu insect that Luo Chen understood why the Southern Frontier had once so severely cracked down on its circulation.

Once a cultivator became too deeply bound to it, it was like a bone-gnawing maggot, impossible to remove.

Even Sage Heavenly Mountain, who hailed from the Southern Frontier, dared not speak of it lightly. How could this Gu Immortal before him be so arrogant as to claim otherwise?

Under Luo Chen’s skeptical gaze, Feng Jiang’s eyelids lifted slightly.

“Your previous line of thinking was wrong.”

“You only thought of removing this Gu, but never considered turning it to your own use.”

At these words, Luo Chen’s expression shook violently.

In a daze, it felt as though a layer of window paper was being slowly pierced.

But what lay behind that window paper, he still could not see clearly.

He said hoarsely, “Indeed, I previously felt this Gu harmed people greatly, so I only thought of stripping it away. I never considered the direction you mentioned.”

Feng Jiang said in a deep voice, “Fortune lies in misfortune, and misfortune hides within fortune. The reason the Devil Heart Gu was once so popular and widespread must be because of its extraordinary qualities. Handled well, it might not be a great opportunity for your son.”

“Fellow Daoist, how do you instruct me?” Luo Chen asked sincerely.

“Ordinary people probably couldn’t use that method, but your son’s situation is special. It wasn’t implanted later; he was born with a Devil Heart Gu as a congenital companion. Perhaps it can truly succeed.” Feng Jiang glanced at the youth dozing in Luo Chen’s arms and lightly uttered, “It’s nothing more than dividing and transforming it, then turning it to your own use!”

Luo Chen’s spirits lifted. He vaguely saw the scenery behind the window paper.

…

A quiet, remote side hall on Welcoming Immortal Peak was hidden among clusters of green pines.

Outside the hall, the youth sat cross-legged beneath a large pine tree, breathing in a rhythmic pattern.

Inside the hall, the young man watched this scene through the window, his expression conflicted.

After a long while, Luo Chen withdrew his gaze and once again focused his attention on the thin sheet of rice paper before him.

Written on it was the specific method Feng Jiang had taught for solving the Devil Heart Gu problem.

The Devil Heart Gu was a Gu insect that had once been popular and widespread in the Southern Frontier.

Rumor had it that once it was planted, cultivated to a sufficient degree, and the host was finally killed, the one who planted the Gu could instantly obtain most of the other party’s cultivation results.

Especially when breaking through a realm and needing sufficient explosive power.

Such convenience was truly a model of the demonic path!

Hence it was named “Devil Heart.”

Later, after news spread that a Devil Heart Gu Emperor existed and that all Devil Heart Gu below the fourth order were merely nourishment for it, its popularity waned somewhat.

Yet there were still those who tried to take the risk.

The main reason was that the Devil Heart Gu itself not only possessed the ability to plunder another’s cultivation but could also accelerate the cultivator’s own training speed.

As long as one controlled that “degree,” it could be stripped away at any time!

But that “degree” was also something ordinary people could hardly master.

After all, those who couldn’t resist the temptation of such cultivation convenience would naturally not be able to stand on the edge of the abyss without falling while accompanied by it day and night.

They would only sink, then fall into eternal damnation, becoming nourishment that the Gu Emperor could take at will.

Luo Lingxi was different!

His binding with the Devil Heart Gu was too deep, far beyond the level of ordinary people.

Thus, although it could not be completely stripped away, from another perspective, he himself could be seen as a “low-level Gu Emperor.”

Although still subject to the only Devil Heart Gu Emperor in the world, Luo Lingxi himself also possessed the ability to plunder other people’s Devil Heart Gu.

If!

Here, “if” — if Luo Lingxi could elevate the Devil Heart Gu that was born with him to the fourth order, he might not be unable to achieve the “inferior overcoming superior” step that Sage Heavenly Mountain had mentioned.

Even if he couldn’t reach that level, as long as he stayed far away from the Devil Heart Gu Emperor, he wouldn’t have his cultivation plundered at any time.

“Divide and transform, turn to one’s own use… Truly simple, yet too vicious and overbearing.”

On the rice paper was written the method for mass-producing low-level Devil Heart Gu.

As long as one followed this method, used the natal Gu to create low-level Devil Heart Gu, spread them out, and implant them into other people’s bodies, the step of “dividing and transforming” would be complete.

In this process, the natal Gu was not weakened, so it wouldn’t provoke resistance from the Devil Heart Gu.

On the contrary, as the scattered low-level Devil Heart Gu continuously grew, as long as they were eventually retrieved, the natal Gu would also grow.

The host himself could then take this opportunity to cultivate quickly, raising his weak cultivation level, even extending his lifespan, and so on.

As for whether to later retrieve the Gu insects and harvest the nourishment, that was the subsequent step of “turning to one’s own use.”

“Theoretically, it’s feasible.”

“In my view, the specific method of operation also has no flaws.”

“But should I really do this?”

Luo Chen hesitated for a moment.

The divided Devil Heart Gu would inevitably seek hosts.

Even if Luo Lingxi was completely unaware, it would still be demonic conduct.

Luo Chen himself had earned the name “Azure Yang Devil Lord” in his early years, but his style of action had always been measured, never fully sinking into the demonic path.

If Luo Lingxi did this, from the very beginning, half his foot would be stepping into the demonic path.

And most demonic cultivators didn’t end well.

What father in the world would be willing to have his son do this?

“Let’s wait and see. He’s still young.”

With a soft sigh, Luo Chen solemnly put away the slip of paper.

…

“Luo Chen, you’ve finally agreed to go out. I thought you had no interest at all in the Alchemy Saint’s inheritance and was planning to have the relevant texts transcribed and delivered to your door.”

Ye Lingtian said, half complaining and half delighted.

Luo Chen said helplessly, “Who wouldn’t want the Alchemy Saint’s inheritance? But there’s still that little one who needs looking after.”

Ye Lingtian laughed heartily. “No matter. Ruoli is one of the most meticulous juniors in my Ye family. With her helping to look after him, you needn’t worry.”

Luo Chen nodded and didn’t object to his words.

He had already examined that Ye Ruoli earlier. She was indeed a gentle and kind person. Entrusting Lingxi to her care for a short time wouldn’t cause any major problems.

But thinking of the future, Luo Chen still asked a couple more questions.

“Gu Immortal Feng Jiang — what kind of person is he, really?”

Ye Lingtian hesitated for a moment, then said slowly, “He acts without scruples, a man who walks the line between good and evil.”

Luo Chen frowned. Subconsciously, this didn’t match the straightforward, helpful Feng Jiang he had met.

But recalling the solution the other had offered, he had to admit Ye Lingtian’s summary was very accurate.

Casually using low-level cultivators as nourishment and a method to divide the Devil Heart Gu — that wasn’t something a late-stage Nascent Soul grand cultivator would say to a peer he had just met for the first time.

“When we cultivators seek spiritual lands, we all pursue immortal clarity, purity, and freedom from defilement. Why did Fellow Daoist Feng Jiang choose Sealing Immortal Valley as his cultivation site?”

Luo Chen asked again, adding another question.

“Moreover, observing his mana, it’s constantly surging, with a danger of being uncontrollably released. Doesn’t he want to achieve the Ascendant realm in the future?”

Faced with these two questions, Ye Lingtian showed a bitter smile.

He said with emotion, “Junior Brother Feng probably won’t be able to achieve the Ascendant realm in this lifetime.”

“Oh? How so?” Luo Chen was genuinely interested.

From his observation, Feng Jiang’s mana was extremely vigorous, surpassing even Luo Chen’s current level, and even Lord Lingtian City’s.

His realm had definitely exceeded Nascent Soul seventh layer, probably around the ninth layer.

He was only one step away from the Ascendant realm.

Could it be an age restriction?

But Ye Lingtian called him junior brother, so his age probably wasn’t much older.

Ye Lingtian fell into hesitation.

Seeing this, Luo Chen put away his curiosity.

“If it’s inconvenient to say, then don’t. Don’t put yourself in a difficult position, Brother Ye.”

“Sigh…”

Ye Lingtian let out a long sigh. “Anyway, Luo Chen, you’re almost considered a member of my Nether Origin Sect now. Telling you won’t matter.”

It was indeed “almost considered,” not “already.”

Because Luo Chen had yet to take possession of the Alchemy Saint Hall, let alone register his name on the sect roster, change his affiliation, and become a true disciple of the Nether Origin Sect.

But Ye Lingtian had confidence in Luo Chen, far more than Chu Yu, Chen Mozi, and others.

After all, that line from years ago — “refining a hundred pills, proficient in all” — still left a memorable impression.

He said in a low voice, “Whether it’s his inability to break through to Ascendant or why he chose Sealing Immortal Valley as his cultivation site, it’s actually the same problem. And the source of this problem goes back to when Junior Brother Feng went to the Southern Frontier…”

Gu Immortal Feng Jiang was one of the most outstanding cultivators of Ye Lingtian’s generation in terms of talent, surpassing even Lei Daozi Zhang Che, Ye Lingtian, and others.

Perhaps only the Alchemy Saint, who had carved his own path through alchemy, could compare.

Very early on, he had already condensed his Nascent Soul, showing signs of catching up to and surpassing Ye Lingtian.

Even strong cultivators like Chong Xuan and Han Yang were overshadowed by Feng Jiang in their same period.

At that time, Feng Jiang felt there were no peers worth fighting within the Nether Origin Sect, and Lord Lingtian City only barely suppressed him by virtue of seniority — not someone he could catch up to in a short time. So he simply decided to travel abroad.

The first stop of his travels was the nearby Southern Frontier.

He had planned only a brief visit before heading to the expert-filled Central Continent and the Northern Sea, where strong cultivators emerged endlessly. But unexpectedly, the first stop kept him there.

He entered the Life and Death Gate!

That place which made the Southern Frontier change color, the famous Ascendant sacred ground of the other four continents!

Once you entered the Life and Death Gate, from then on, life and death were no longer your own.

Although he later escaped and greatly advanced his cultivation, becoming a late-stage Nascent Soul grand cultivator, many things were beyond his control.

“Junior Brother once said that the Life and Death Gate is extremely chaotic.”

“Many powerful Gu Emperors either disappeared into hiding or fled far away. Those who remained were all exceptionally strong. These remaining ones ceaselessly fought among themselves, only to enter the true Life and Death Gate and seek the opportunity for Ascension.”

“Junior Brother Feng was among them and even took the lead, seizing a fifth-order Gu insect that could be called an immortal seed.”

“Later, pursued and hunted, he fled back to the Nether Origin Sect.”

“But that pursuit forced him to prematurely unseal the fifth-order Gu insect’s seal, resulting in him now suffering from its backlash.”

“Sealing Immortal Valley wasn’t originally like that. It was one of the spiritual lands closest to a fifth-order spiritual vein in our Nether Origin Sect, not far from the immortal clarity, purity, and freedom from defilement you mentioned. It was because Junior Brother spent centuries suppressing the immortal Gu there that the earth veins collapsed, bringing endless natural disasters and earthly tribulations.”

“It was precisely because he needed to continuously suppress the immortal Gu that he couldn’t rush toward Ascension. Perhaps once he achieved the Ascendant realm, that immortal Gu would bring even greater calamity.”

Listening to this past story, Luo Chen’s eyes sparkled with unusual light.

The chaos within the Southern Frontier was not something he was hearing for the first time.

He had heard about it years ago from Sage Heavenly Mountain.

Even Sage Heavenly Mountain, the last sect master of the Heavenly Mountain Cult, had left his homeland to join the Tianyuan Dao Sect for this very reason.

The mysterious Southern Frontier was truly too chaotic.

But that Feng Jiang could infiltrate it was somewhat frightening.

What kind of Ascendant sacred ground would allow talented disciples from other sacred grounds to just sneak in?

And what kind of existence could force Feng Jiang, a Nether Origin Sect disciple with formidable combat skills who had already advanced to late-stage Nascent Soul, to prematurely unseal the immortal Gu?

Regarding this, Ye Lingtian wasn’t very clear either, only briefly mentioning a few names.

Sword Gu Emperor, Ghost Judge, Divination Daoist…

“According to what Master said, among those who pursued Junior Brother, the weakest were at mid-stage Nascent Soul. As for the three I just mentioned, they were even more famous late-stage Nascent Soul grand cultivators. That Junior Brother Feng could survive back then truly shows his extraordinary skill.”

Among these names, Luo Chen only felt a slight familiarity with Divination Daoist.

Probably Sage Heavenly Mountain had mentioned him in passing during one of their exchanges.

As for the others, Luo Chen knew nothing.

But he was quite curious.

“I imagine that the fifth-order immortal Gu he unsealed during that successful escape played a huge role!”

Luo Chen said this, because otherwise he couldn’t imagine how powerful Feng Jiang would have to be to escape from a siege of grand cultivators and even mid-stage Nascent Soul cultivators.

Even he himself wouldn’t dare claim he could do such a thing.

“We’ve arrived!”

Ye Lingtian pointed a finger. A palace eaves hidden deep within white clouds came into Luo Chen’s view.

It was the Alchemy Saint Hall!

Luo Chen looked at him. Ye Lingtian’s expression was completely frank.

“Sorry. Other things can be discussed, but the immortal Gu Junior Brother Feng seized is somewhat special and cannot be disclosed.”

“No matter. You’ve already cleared up many of my doubts.” Luo Chen cupped his hands, then alone, drifted up the mountain.

…

If the Nether Abyss and Sealing Immortal Valley were forbidden grounds of the Nether Origin Sect, then this Alchemy Saint Hall could also be considered a forbidden ground of the Nether Origin Sect over these past few centuries.

After entering the mountain, no trace of living beings was seen. It was as desolate as a dead land.

Yet the surrounding spiritual energy was so rich that it made cultivators feel as if bathed in spring rain. How could it be compared to a dead land?

Especially after approaching the Alchemy Saint Hall, Luo Chen felt a certain aura that was particularly familiar to him.

It was like a woman’s faint, secluded fragrance, or perhaps the smoke and fire of a kitchen. But upon careful examination, Luo Chen could keenly discern it as an incredibly thick pill qi!

“Just how many miraculous pills did Alchemy Saint Chu Yan refine in this Alchemy Saint Hall?”

Luo Chen was secretly astonished.

With such a terrifying accumulation of pill qi, any later cultivator refining any pill would surely have their success rate greatly increased!

No wonder it was the former Alchemy Saint’s dojo!

Luo Chen took a deep breath and was about to step into the Alchemy Saint Hall to prepare in advance for the Alchemy Saint Hall trial.

But just as he was about to enter, his heart stirred.

He formed a hand seal with his spiritual sense, and his eyes instantly blazed with golden light.

Not only that, but he carefully infused his source power into his eyes.

Strands of red threads extended and spread from the whites of his eyes, ultimately connecting to that golden pupil.

In an instant, outside the golden pupil, his eyes became blood-red.

This was Luo Chen simultaneously using his mana and source power to activate that spiritual eye, forming the appearance of crimson eyes and golden pupils!

He had done this once before, on Heaven-Earth Peak during the Ascension Ceremony.

Only that time, Luo Chen’s mana was not as strong as now, and his eyes had not yet been enhanced by Imperial Golden Pupil Liquid.

Thus, the crimson eyes and golden pupils appeared for the first time.

Under these eyes, which seemed capable of seeing through everything, Luo Chen looked toward the Alchemy Saint Hall and finally discovered the source of the sudden strange feeling.

“It’s spiritual opportunity!”

The entire Alchemy Saint Hall was shrouded in clouds and mist.

And within that mist, wisps of spiritual opportunity rippled.

Although very little and thin, far less dense than when the Alchemy Saint had ascended years ago, it was indeed present.

Whether it was left by the Alchemy Saint or drawn here by a great power of the Nether Origin Sect was unknown.

But this one point made Luo Chen’s heart tremble with awe.

“The Alchemy Saint’s inheritance may indeed have something worth taking!”

In an instant, Luo Chen, who had originally thought little of the Alchemy Saint’s inheritance after obtaining the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining’s alchemy inheritance, cast aside his disdain and gained a bit of determination to obtain it.

A realm’s spiritual opportunity was exclusive to Ascendant great powers and ascenders. Ordinary people who obtained even a little would treasure it like a precious gem.

How could the Alchemy Saint Hall, guarded by spiritual opportunity, be an ordinary inheritance?

(Chapter End)
