# Chapter 692: Spirit-Molding Holy Spring, Five-Spirit Dao Body

Within the Pill Realm, there was flower fragrance but no birdsong. It was truly a place of silence.

Suddenly,

A delighted laugh rang out, echoing for several breaths without stopping.

When the laughter gradually ceased, an old woman with silver hair walked out of the Pill Room, body and mind relaxed.

In her hand, she clearly held several pills.

Most were incomplete. Only one was full of spiritual qi.

Looking at the pill in her hand, Cape Heaven Matron could not help grinning.

"Five years. After the comparatively familiar Demon-Transformation Pill, I finally refined the Human-Transformation Pill."

"Tsk, tsk. This Human-Transformation Pill is truly not bad. Once I leave Demon-Fall Land, I will refine a few more and give them to the third-tier juniors of my clan, letting them take human form ahead of time."

"At that time, either they will learn my Dao of Pills early, or cultivate arts of attack and killing. From then on, the weak Cape Heaven clan will thoroughly rise in my hands!"

Holding the Human-Transformation Pill, Cape Heaven Matron was filled with joy.

But after that brief joy, the things she had previously refused to think about while immersed in pill refinement had to be faced after all.

Five years!

That human boy had already advanced to the next trial long ago.

She did not know how his progress was now.

If he had already cleared all trials and accepted the inheritance, then what use were all the efforts she had made now?

Thinking to this point, a bitter expression involuntarily appeared on Cape Heaven Matron's face.

To be honest, her alchemy and comprehension of the Dao of Pills already counted as top tier!

Fourth-tier pills that ordinary alchemists found difficult even to glimpse had been refined by her from nothing into two kinds in only five years.

Such ability absolutely did not disgrace the reputation of a grandmaster.

But compared with that Devil Lord Azure Yang...

"He is simply monstrous!"

A demon emperor calling someone else monstrous showed the awe and fear Cape Heaven Matron held toward Luo Chen.

This attitude was actually quite normal.

Luo Chen had overtaken her from behind. From initially lagging two trials behind, to walking shoulder to shoulder, then surpassing her, and finally crossing the fourth trial at an unquestionable speed.

Such speed had completely surpassed Cape Heaven Matron's understanding.

In her heart, she had unknowingly given Luo Chen many titles: ancestor of an alchemy Dao sect, direct powerful heir of an Ascendant Holy Land, and so on, to comfort herself.

It was not that she was incapable. Her opponent was simply too strong!

"Sigh..."

With a sigh, the old woman carefully stored away the Human-Transformation Pill and planned to rest properly for two or three days.

Next, as long as she refined the most difficult and unfamiliar Devil-Transformation Pill, she would pass the fourth trial.

At that time, perhaps she could still catch sight of Devil Lord Azure Yang's tail...

Just as this thought emerged in her heart, her body suddenly staggered.

"Hm? Am I too tired?"

Subconsciously, this thought surfaced in the old woman's mind.

But immediately, she sensed something was wrong.

No matter what, she was a dignified Sage-level demon cultivator!

How could she be unable to stand steady?

What made her stagger was this world!

In her naked eye, the distant green mountains were vanishing!

The earth trembled, black clouds pressed down on the city.

Boundless darkness, from far to near, was madly devouring all light in the Pill Realm.

When the darkness covered the stone house courtyard, the teleportation formation lit up.

Seeing this scene, Cape Heaven Matron seemed to understand.

The Pill Realm was closing.

Just like the Formation Realm, after Li Canghai cleared it and obtained the inheritance, the Formation Realm also closed completely, preventing those who came later from entering to obtain the inheritance.

By this reasoning, there was only one reason that could make the Pill Realm close.

Someone had cleared it.

"In the end, I was still one step slower than him?"

"Devil Lord Azure Yang..."

Murmuring, Cape Heaven Matron vanished into the twisted space, unwilling yet also carrying a trace of release.

After an unknown amount of time,

Cape Heaven Matron slowly opened her eyes.

Before she could observe her surroundings, a sense of loss suddenly surged into her heart.

She held her head and shook it hard several times.

Then disbelief appeared on her face.

"Where are those pill formulas?"

"Where are my pill formulas!"

"How could I have forgotten them all? Even the corresponding materials have been erased from my memory."

At this moment, she only remembered that she had refined many pills in the Pill Realm, but no matter how she thought, she could not recall those formulas at all.

The Spirit-Root Pill, the demon-soul pills, the devil-tempering pills... and especially the transformation pill formula she had regarded as a treasure that could raise the Cape Heaven clan!

Everything seemed to have been wiped away by a great hand.

She madly tried to recall them, unwilling to accept it, but in the end could only unwillingly accept reality.

Cape Heaven Matron realized that this method was probably set by the master of this realm to prevent those precious pill formulas from leaking.

It might even be that not only the core formulas within the Pill Realm, but the other dozen or so worlds also had similar measures to some degree.

After all, that knowledge looked unremarkable at first glance, but every piece, when carefully examined, was incomparably precious.

Especially things like the Spirit-Root Pill, demon-soul pills, and transformation pills. All of them could become the key to the rise of a great force, or even an entire race.

Before becoming the true inheritor of the Devil Lord, how could these precious things be easily taken away?

Thinking to this point, Cape Heaven Matron's lost expression became dejected.

She had not only lost the most important inheritance, but also lost the pill formulas she once possessed!

The only fortunate thing was that Demon-Fall Land's master did not have strong killing intent. Even though she had failed the inheritance assessment, her life was still spared.

She lifted her head, her dejected gaze looking around.

This was an empty, boundless plaza. Cloud qi drifted like an immortal realm.

Above the empty plaza, fourteen palaces hovered in the void. They were like fourteen huge stars, illuminating this sea of clouds.

Looking at those fourteen star-like palaces, Cape Heaven Matron seemed to realize something.

"So this is the true Bright Radiance Heaven!"

Among the fourteen palaces, only two were currently truly emitting light.

Through the suspended plaques, Cape Heaven Matron faintly saw their names.

[Pill Palace]

[Servant Palace]

Based on her own experience, Cape Heaven Matron already understood.

When the corresponding palace lit up, it meant someone had entered and was obtaining the true inheritance.

Inside the Pill Palace should be the person calling himself Devil Lord Azure Yang.

Then who was inside the Servant Palace?

As she pondered, a pillar of light descended not far from her.

Soon, the light dispersed, revealing a black-robed figure.

The figure was filled with loss, speaking words of regret.

"A pity. In the end, I was one move short and failed to refine an Ascendant puppet."

As he murmured, he discovered the other person here.

Their eyes met, and an odd atmosphere spread.

...

Deep within Bright Radiance Heaven's Pill Palace,

There was a pool of milky-white spirit spring, bubbling steadily.

A figure soaked within it, rising and falling as if consciousness had been lost.

Large amounts of milky spring water continuously gathered toward him.

It nourished his dry eyes, filled his emptied qi sea, and even restored vitality to his weakened body.

Not only that!

Under the washing of large quantities of milky spring water, inexplicable impurities were continuously expelled from the Daoist's body.

As those impurities were expelled, his entire person became increasingly clear and numinous, and his sense of harmony with heaven and earth grew stronger.

Several days later,

The white-robed Daoist slowly awoke from sleep. He rubbed his eyes, somewhat confused.

"I am..."

Before this thought fully surfaced, Luo Chen immediately grew alert to the surrounding environment.

There were no outsiders!

There was only warm spring water that made his whole body feel comfortably warm.

"What kind of spirit liquid is this?"

Luo Chen was both shocked and delighted!

He could sense that the spring water contained a special energy, which his body was continuously absorbing.

As that energy was absorbed, his body was gradually undergoing a transformation.

The main areas transforming were astonishingly his tongue root, qi sea, and essence chamber.

And these three places were where the true spirit root resided!

It was as if that energy was reshaping and remolding his spirit roots, evolving them toward the limit!

Between shock and joy, Luo Chen released his resistance and began fully absorbing the power within the milky-white spring water.

"Although I do not know what this spring water is, it must contain the power of fortune. I should soak in it as much as possible."

Luo Chen settled peacefully within the spring water.

In addition, he split off a portion of his mind to inspect himself.

Before he fell unconscious, his Primordial Unity Cauldron had been left outside.

Now, under his perception, the Primordial Unity Cauldron was still within his qi sea.

Not only that, inside the Primordial Unity Cauldron there was a pill that was gold with a trace of red!

It was not his lifebound Golden Core. Both his Golden Core and Origin Pill were above the qi sea, gulping down pure spiritual qi from the outside world, while this pill...

"It is the Law-Derivation Divine Pill!"

Gulp!

He swallowed, wild joy surging through his chest.

Luo Chen recalled the situation before he had fallen unconscious.

Before he exhausted all his source power and mana, and even ate one-tenth of Tai Sui's main body, he had ultimately endured through the final stage.

He had successfully refined the fifth-tier divine pill, the Law-Derivation Pill!

Thus he obtained the approval of that weak voice from the unseen and successfully passed the fifth trial.

Recalling it now, Luo Chen felt a trace of lingering fortune.

A fifth-tier divine pill truly was not something his current realm could touch.

Perhaps his alchemy attainments were barely enough to refine it, but his own realm was still far too forced.

Relying on an Archaic Tier Four body, as well as the system's assistance... no, and also the aid of the entire Pill Realm!

With Luo Chen's formation knowledge, when he fully refined the Law-Derivation Pill, he had faintly understood the essence of that illusory world.

That was clearly a heaven-reaching and earth-penetrating grand pill-refining formation!

It existed specifically to assist someone in refining pills.

Only, it had been artificially remodeled into an inheritance place.

In essence, however, it remained a grand formation for assisting pill refinement.

That was why all kinds of heavenly phenomena had appeared during refinement.

"If I tried again in the outside world, even with a complete formula and medicinal materials, I am afraid I could not reproduce that feat."

Luo Chen muttered to himself.

It was both comfort for himself and words of release.

He had never imagined that refining pills could refine a person into that state.

It was as if everything had been squeezed dry.

Even this pair of eyes had nearly failed to be preserved.

Splash!

Luo Chen touched his moist eyes and only felt his gaze clear and lubricated.

Five years of unceasing pill refinement, especially the final three years when Spirit Eye Art had never stopped, had caused tremendous damage to his eyes.

Fortunately, everything had been endured.

After relief, heavy sleepiness surged into his mind again, and Luo Chen slowly fell asleep once more.

Only this time, he was more actively choosing sleep, choosing to absorb that special energy in the spirit spring.

...

One year later,

Within Bright Radiance Heaven, more and more figures appeared.

On the cloud-sea plaza, those who had luckily survived the inheritance lands were actually all failures.

There were four people in total.

They were Cape Heaven Matron with a head of silver hair, a middle-aged man whose whole body was pitch-black, a cool and beautiful peerless woman, and an old Daoist transcendent of the mundane.

And then...

Another pillar of light descended.

Two figures fell to the ground.

One was a middle-aged scholar, the other a stooped elder.

But age and appearance were not criteria for judging an immortal cultivator's realm and ability.

In an instant, the four Sage Perfected Beings already standing there all fixed their gazes on the middle-aged scholar.

"Feiyunzi!"

"Perfected Being Feiyun!"

Several voices rang out at the same time.

Feiyunzi looked at the four and called out their names one by one.

"Fellow Daoist Jiyuan, Fellow Daoist Feileng, Cape Heaven Matron... and this one is?"

The man shrouded in black robes chuckled lightly. "A nameless wandering cultivator, not worth mentioning."

A nameless wandering cultivator at the Sage stage?

Feiyunzi frowned, feeling that this person's speech was stiff, as if the voice was not coming from a normal throat.

Afterward, without caring what they wanted to ask, he directly acted, reaching toward the elder who had appeared alongside him.

Moya Cave Master saw this scene, his expression filled with terror.

"Senior, spare my life!"

"How could I spare you? When climbing Weapon Mountain, you ruined my great affair. If I do not kill you, it will not vent my resentment!"

Boom!

Two streams of mana collided. Feiyunzi frowned and looked toward the black-robed cultivator.

"Fellow Daoist, what is the meaning of this?"

The black-robed cultivator beckoned. Not understanding why, Moya Cave Master subconsciously walked over. At least just now, this person had saved him.

Then the black-robed cultivator smiled and said, "Everyone here is a Sage Perfected Being. Why bother quarreling with a Golden Core junior?"

Feiyunzi snorted coldly, fear faintly appearing in his eyes.

From that brief exchange just now, he discovered this person was not inferior to himself.

If they truly fought, there were others here, especially Old Daoist Jiyuan, who belonged to Penglai Immortal Sect. That sect was hostile to the Cang Hai Alliance he belonged to, and someone else might reap the fisherman's profit.

"A head-hiding, tail-shrinking sort!"

With a cold snort, he no longer attacked.

For a time, the cloud-sea plaza became somewhat silent.

Everyone's attention turned to the palaces above.

Still, only the Pill Palace and Servant Palace were shining with light.

Privately,

Old Daoist Jiyuan was transmitting his voice to Fairy Feileng.

"Junior Sister, did you obtain the painting you wanted?"

"Mm."

"What treasure is it that makes you care so much, to the point that you did not hesitate to enter Demon-Fall Land again?"

"My matters are not yours to manage."

Being rebuffed, Old Daoist Jiyuan did not mind.

These people had long stopped taking their sect seriously.

He changed the topic. "I wonder how Blood Nightmare Moluo is doing over there?"

Fairy Feileng said crisply, "He came this time for the place where the Blood Sea Ancestor sat in death. No one is competing with him, and he does not care about the inheritance assessment. I imagine he has nine chances in ten."

When these words emerged, Old Daoist Jiyuan shook his head.

If Blood Nightmare Moluo truly obtained the inheritance of Yuan Demon Sect's ancestor, then the Northern Sea would probably gain another blood-path powerhouse in the future.

If the other party luckily advanced to Ascendant, perhaps Yuan Demon Sect might even see the day of reappearing.

This was not good news.

Unfortunately, he himself had not passed the Talisman Realm inheritance...

Suddenly, as if remembering something, he said with a gloomy expression, "Your elder sister also came in."

Elder sister?

In an instant, Perfected Being Feileng's face turned snow-white.

She raised her eyes and looked all around, finally fixing them upon the Servant Palace.

If her guess was not wrong, that woman was probably inside!

Old Daoist Jiyuan said with lingering fear, "She still harbors resentment toward Penglai Immortal Sect and even me. Perhaps when we meet again, she will immediately shout for battle and killing. I hope that when the time comes, Junior Sister can help me..."

"I cannot control her. She is mad." Perfected Being Feileng pursed her lips, fear flashing through her eyes.

Old Daoist Jiyuan's heart rose.

That woman, known as one of the Three Great Wanderers of the Northern Sea, had ability directly approaching the master of Penglai Immortal Sect. If she truly bit onto him and refused to let go, he would probably have ten deaths and no life.

He should never have taken this trip!

Right now, the only option was to find Blood Nightmare Moluo and gather the strength of three people. Perhaps they could contend with her.

No!

There was actually another way.

As long as he left Bright Radiance Heaven ahead of time and broke away from Demon-Fall Land, that would suffice.

And this cloud-sea plaza beneath his feet was clearly the teleportation formation for leaving Demon-Fall Land.

Thinking of this, Old Daoist Jiyuan began wandering around the surroundings.

The others saw his anxious manner and were somewhat puzzled.

After watching for a while, they no longer looked and instead placed their attention on those star-palaces in the sky.

Those were all places that hid inheritances!

Even if they had not passed the inheritance assessments, could they try to open them from the outside?

Everyone thought this way, and from time to time used methods to try opening the palaces.

Yet with their abilities, when their attacks struck the palace doors, it was like ants shaking a tree, unable to stir the slightest reaction.

Several days later,

Another palace lit up.

This meant someone else had passed an inheritance assessment.

In a single instant, everyone's attention projected over.

[Light Palace]

...

Leaving aside the changing situation outside and the many experts' unpredictable thoughts,

Inside the Pill Palace, someone had completely awakened.

As milky spring water slid down, Luo Chen slowly sat upright.

Looking at the clear spring water that had thinned greatly, Luo Chen only felt regret.

With his powerful body, he still could not completely absorb this spirit spring's energy. One or two tenths still remained.

Even so, his benefits were extraordinary!

Looking inward at himself, he felt his whole body comfortable, his mana surging.

Without realizing it, his realm had risen by two small levels!

[Golden Core Stage Eight 51/100]

Such a gain was enough to save several decades of bitter cultivation.

But clearly, this was only one of the most insignificant gains.

The most important gain was that faint feeling of harmony with heaven and earth.

Every pore of his body seemed to have opened. As long as he wished, external spiritual qi of heaven and earth could be absorbed into his body.

This feeling was like... "a heavenly spirit root!"

"It is a true heavenly spirit root. And not only of one attribute, but all five!"

"A five-attribute complete heavenly spirit root!"

Luo Chen swallowed, feeling somewhat shocked.

He could sense that his spirit-root aptitude, together with his entire body, had undergone some kind of transformation during this year and more.

Five-element heavenly spirit root?

No!

The changes in his body were clearly almost the same as a certain constitution recorded in the classics.

That was a special constitution called the Five-Spirit Dao Body.

It originated from five spirit roots, yet was superior to five spirit roots!

The concrete changes were not entirely obvious at this moment, but Luo Chen estimated that perhaps he had obtained the greatest opportunity of his life!

He could not help scooping up a handful of spring water and examining it carefully.

"What exactly is this spirit spring?"

*(End of Chapter)*
