# Chapter 1029: Qi-Refining Technique, Both Parents Dead

In the early morning, the clinic door was knocked on urgently.

Not long after, a young boy rushed in hurriedly.

"Young Master, Young Master Yuqi has come to see you." Granny Lin's expression was not good.

Zhou Yuqi couldn't see Granny Lin's disgusted face as he hurried into the inner room.

"Mayfly, how is Little Mao?"

The gaunt young boy pulled the bed curtain shut, then turned around, revealing a face that hadn't slept all night.

He stared fixedly at the tiger-headed, tiger-brained Zhou Yuqi, as if trying to discern something from him.

Zhou Yuqi said anxiously, "I heard this morning that someone rushed into Clan Elder Haibo's clinic last night. When I heard it was you, I came right away to see."

Zhou Fuyou asked abruptly, "Who are 'they'?"

Zhou Yuqi was startled and answered subconsciously, "Fourth Aunt Yin and a few servants. Why?"

Zhou Fuyou said nothing.

Fourth Aunt Yin was the middle-aged woman who had previously delivered the tonic on behalf of Seventh Uncle's family.

Her status was the same as their own Granny Lin—both were maids who had come with their mistresses as dowry.

Thirty years ago, the Zhou clan had taken up residence in Egret Marsh.

Seven years ago, the Zhou clan of Egret Marsh, the Lin clan of Green Parasol, and the Yin clan of Cold River Valley had formed an alliance and begun preliminary cooperation.

There were many specific terms of cooperation, one of which was marriage alliances.

A large number of low-ranking clan members intermarried among the three families.

In this regard, their Zhou clan had benefited the most, taking in many female cultivators from the Lin and Yin clans.

Zhou Fuyou's mother came from the Lin clan of Green Parasol, while Zhou Yuqi's mother came from the Yin clan of Cold River Valley.

It was precisely because they had similar family backgrounds and were born in the same year that Zhou Fuyou and Zhou Yuqi often played together and had the best relationship.

Occasionally, when they played too long, they would eat and sleep at each other's homes.

Ordinarily, the adults of both families also interacted closely, occasionally sending small gifts.

Things like Seventh Uncle's family sending Fourth Aunt Yin to deliver tonic a few days ago were not unusual; it had happened before, so there wasn't much inspection.

But because of that one oversight, something went wrong.

Zhou Fuyou was willing to believe what Clan Elder Haibo said—that his sister had merely mistakenly drunk his tonic, causing her weak body to be unable to handle the nourishment. But after he took the Fire-Dispelling Pill and vomited out the strange poison, he no longer believed those were tonics!

Especially since they had only arrived at the clinic last night—how did Seventh Uncle's family know about it this morning?

Had Fourth Aunt Yin been watching their house all along?

Was this her own doing, or was it at the instruction of Seventh Uncle and Seventh Aunt?

"Mayfly, what's wrong?"

An unfamiliar voice came from beside him, and Zhou Fuyou snapped back to reality.

"Nothing. Mao Mao is just a little unwell. We'll be going home soon."

Zhou Yuqi breathed a sigh of relief, a smile spreading across his face. "Good, good. As long as it's nothing serious. Little Mao's health is too poor, and you've been negligent as her older brother. You need to take good care of her from now on!"

"Mm."

Zhou Fuyou nodded lightly, his gaze fixed on Zhou Yuqi.

That smile was genuine, carrying the innocent, childlike purity unique to children.

"He probably doesn't know anything, right?" Zhou Fuyou thought to himself.

Outside the clinic, the rain continued to fall, as if it would never stop.

The cultivators of the Zhou clan seemed already accustomed to it.

If it weren't for the clan's current lack of resources, they would have long since set up a grand formation to block out this cold, bone-piercing spring rain.

By noon, when the rain had lessened.

Granny Lin held an umbrella, and Zhou Fuyou carried the still-sleeping Mao Mao on his back as they walked out of the clinic.

The tiger-headed, tiger-brained Zhou Yuqi followed behind, wanting to see them home.

But as soon as they stepped out of the clinic gate, Zhou Fuyou suddenly spoke.

"Yuqi, you don't need to see us off. Go home!"

Zhou Yuqi was stunned.

When he wanted to say something, his friend had already stepped into the curtain of rain with his sister on his back, attended by the servant, his figure gradually blurring.

...

The Zhou clan had an eight-hundred-year heritage in the Northern Sea.

At the peak of their clan numbers, they had over ten thousand cultivators and hundreds of millions of clan members, far surpassing ordinary Nascent Soul sects, almost indistinguishable from a secular dynasty.

Unfortunately, when they fled from the Northern Sea in disaster, only a few dozen people remained.

Truly tragic!

It was only after arriving in the Central Continent and resting and recuperating for over a hundred years that they had barely recovered some vitality.

But the number of core clan members was actually not that many.

The "core clan members" mentioned here refer to those who could cultivate and possessed formal generation names, not those collateral branches unworthy of even having generation names.

Counting down from Ancestor Zhou Yunshen, the second generation had only five people, who were also the five Golden Core Superiors of the current Zhou clan.

The third generation numbered about a hundred.

Zhou Fuyou's father, Zhou Bohan, was one of them, belonging to the "Hai" generation.

And because he had successfully established his foundation, he held a certain status in the current Zhou clan.

Thus, Zhou Fuyou's family not only had their own independent courtyard but also a decent second-rank mid-grade cave dwelling, sufficient to supply the daily cultivation of the couple Zhou Bohan and Lin Jingshu.

Apart from Granny Lin, the household also had several mortal servants who couldn't cultivate, attending to their daily needs.

After Zhou Bohan and his wife went out on missions, Zhou Fuyou became the de facto and nominal head of the family.

Returning home, he first settled his sister, instructing Granny Lin to take close care of her, then went back to his favorite Drink-Green Waterside Pavilion.

He needed to be alone now.

The sudden upheaval overnight had left the young boy's mind exhausted, his thoughts far more chaotic than in the four years he could remember.

Standing by the pavilion's edge, his gaze scattered among the green algae and duckweed in the water, the young boy quietly stared into space.

Slowly, his attention began to focus, his expression turning resolute.

He turned and left the Drink-Green Waterside Pavilion, heading toward his father's study.

...

"Brother... brother... brother!"

Suddenly, the little girl woke up, her face covered in fine beads of sweat.

Granny Lin gently wiped the sweat away with a handkerchief.

Zhou Qingyu clutched the old woman's hand in fear. "Granny, where's my brother?"

"Don't be afraid. He's here."

"I want to see him!"

"But..."

"I want to see him, boohoo..."

As she spoke, Zhou Qingyu began to cry.

Granny Lin had no choice. She dressed the little girl in thick clothes, then carried her out of the room.

After asking the servants, Granny Lin's face showed confusion, but urged on by the little girl, she still headed toward where Zhou Fuyou was.

Soon, Granny Lin arrived at a study.

Unlike the poetic charm of the Drink-Green Waterside Pavilion, this study was dominated by red and black colors, quite solemn.

At the desk, a young boy was perusing a scroll of bamboo slips.

"Young Master, Miss Qingyu is awake."

Zhou Fuyou snapped back to reality, then took the squirming little girl from Granny Lin's arms.

"Mao Mao..."

Once in his embrace, the little girl smiled, as if nothing had happened yesterday.

Granny Lin looked at the siblings with affection, her peripheral vision catching the bamboo scroll on the desk.

*The Guihai Sutra*

Granny Lin's expression changed drastically. "Young Master Fuyou! How could you disregard the Master and Mistress's warnings and touch a Qi-Refining technique before the proper age?"

Zhou Fuyou held his sister and replied calmly, "I'm not cultivating. I'm just looking."

Granny Lin breathed a sigh of relief, but her expression remained stern.

"Young Master, our path of cultivation depends on spirit roots, talent, and most importantly, age. Without spirit roots, one cannot cultivate. If the body's constitution is insufficient, cultivation will harm the body. And all of this must be built upon reaching the appropriate age."

"You are not yet twelve. Your body has not fully developed. Your meridians and acupoints have not taken shape. Even whether you have spirit roots has not yet manifested. You absolutely must not cultivate ahead of time."

"Otherwise, at best, it will affect your future Dao path. At worst, it could cost you your life on the spot."

Zhou Fuyou raised an eyebrow, realizing the severity in the old woman's words.

Even the mildest consequence was affecting his future Dao path—this showed how harmful premature cultivation could be.

He reassured her, "Granny, rest assured. I've just been reading too many martial classics, Daoist scriptures, and local chronicles. I'm only passing the time out of boredom."

Granny Lin looked seriously at the young boy, feeling uneasy in her heart.

Finally, she admonished, "Cultivating ahead of time brings endless troubles. Remember this well!"

Zhou Fuyou smiled and comforted her a few more times before Granny Lin finally relaxed.

Then Zhou Fuyou played with his sister for a while. The little girl, still recovering from her illness, soon grew tired, and Zhou Fuyou had Granny Lin take her back.

Before leaving, Granny Lin instructed, "Young Master, you've been tired for a day and a night. I'll have the servants prepare hot food and water. Eat something and rest early."

"Alright. Sorry for worrying you, Granny."

Zhou Fuyou didn't defy Granny Lin's arrangements. She was his mother's close attendant and had watched both siblings grow up.

In name, she was a maidservant, but in reality, she was no different from an elder.

After eating the steaming hot food and washing up briefly, Zhou Fuyou went to sleep soundly.

But after waking up, he once again stepped into his father's study.

From the bookshelf, he took down the *Guihai Sutra* he hadn't finished reading yesterday.

This was the Zhou clan's foundational Qi-Refining technique, passed down through the ages, widely sold even in the Northern Sea cultivation world.

Above it should be the Foundation Establishment stage's *Ten Thousand Rivers Sutra*, then the Golden Core stage's *Canglang Sutra*, and finally the Nascent Soul stage's *Infinite Sutra*.

In reality, these were all one complete set of techniques. But the ancestors, fearing the leakage of high-level techniques, had artificially divided them into four chapters, corresponding to four realms, and only allowed clan members of the corresponding realms to cultivate them.

Linked together, they meant "Ten Thousand Rivers Return to the Sea, Surging Waves Without End."

The ancestor himself had cultivated this set of techniques. Its advantage was that at the same realm, one's magical power was abundant, vast and boundless like the sea. Its disadvantage was slow advancement and difficulty breaking through realms.

For Zhou Fuyou, even just the Qi-Refining stage's *Guihai Sutra* was somewhat difficult to read.

But only somewhat difficult!

At two, he had learned to read, write, and speak eloquently. At three, he had already mastered poetry and song.

At four, no longer satisfied with ordinary literary classics, he had begun to dabble in martial classics and Daoist scriptures related to cultivation.

Now, half a year shy of six, he could still be said to have "thoroughly read the Daoist canon"!

With such literary foundation, coupled with the constant exposure to his two Foundation Establishment parents, he could indeed, without anyone's guidance, laboriously read through the introductory Qi-Refining technique bit by bit.

But recalling Granny Lin's warning from yesterday, he forcibly suppressed the restless urge within him.

As Zhou Fuyou rested, his gaze fell on the bookshelf.

On it were not only ordinary texts issued by the Zhou clan but also those collected by his father, as well as some his mother had brought from the Lin clan. His mother, Lin Jingshu, came from a humble family within the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. The techniques she practiced were orthodox Dao Sect methods, and the texts she brought were even more extraordinary.

If the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect weren't currently absorbing former Star Gate guest elders, they probably wouldn't have relaxed such restrictions.

"So many books. I'll just read and learn more."

The young boy murmured to himself, his mood neither anxious nor impatient.

Outside the study, rain beat against the forest leaves, crackling and pattering.

...

After the incident of Zhou Qingyu "fainting from a weak body unable to handle nourishment," time seemed to slow down.

From then on, Zhou Fuyou hardly went out. Several times when Zhou Yuqi came to find him, he was turned away at the door.

Nothing major happened within the clan, except that Second Grandfather Zhou Junxiao was planning to develop Egret Marsh.

Egret Marsh was vast, spanning over three thousand li. Within it, not only had a new fourth-rank spirit vein been born, but there were also numerous scattered small spirit veins.

However, due to the miasma of the evil marsh, many areas of Egret Marsh remained undeveloped.

Moreover, the Zhou clan had few members, and a group of their strong had been sent out on the sea-patrol mission assigned by the Southern Heavenly Dao Palace. Thus, the plan to develop Egret Marsh was continually delayed.

But the sea-patrol mission wasn't continuous; it was rotated among the many families of the Southern Heavenly Continent. And this time, the Zhou clan had been hastily conscripted as an addition, so it would probably end in about half a year.

Half a year passed in the blink of an eye for cultivators.

But for a child, it seemed so long.

"Brother, when will Father and Mother come back?"

"Soon. Probably within these few days."

"Great, great! Mao Mao hasn't heard Father tell stories from outside in a long time, and she hasn't eaten Mother's white jade soup in a long time either."

"Mao Mao, go play outside. Brother needs to read."

"I won't go out. Brother, you read. I won't make any noise."

Zhou Fuyou smiled helplessly, then continued reading the book in his hands.

This was a Qi-Refining technique called the *Dew Cicada Art*, taken from the meaning "morning dew is fleeting, the winter cicada drinks little."

Its cultivation philosophy was the same.

Most cultivators in the world had ordinary talent, unable to touch high realms in their lifetime, their lifespans as brief as morning dew and winter cicadas.

Thus, some chose to take shortcuts, cultivating techniques that advanced quickly.

The *Dew Cicada Art* was such a technique. Moreover, it was a rare all-attribute technique, unconcerned with spirit root attributes—anyone could begin.

But its drawbacks were obvious. The realms cultivated this way might come quickly, but one's magical power was far less profound than that of cultivators at the same level. Furthermore, the yin-cold magical power it produced easily damaged the meridians and dantian.

It was said this technique came from the Northern Sea, likely from the former Northern Sea overlord, the Primordial Demon Sect.

In other words, a demonic technique!

If Zhou Fuyou had to choose, he would prefer steady, solid techniques like the *Guihai Sutra*, the *Evergreen Art*, or the *Thick Earth Art*.

"No rush."

"After six, I should start nurturing my body, practicing basic body-tempering techniques, combined with medicinal baths, to build up my strength. Then at twelve, I'll test my spirit root attributes. By then, Father and Mother will naturally choose a suitable technique for me."

Zhou Fuyou closed the book and stuffed the *Dew Cicada Art* into the most remote corner of the bookshelf.

Half a month later.

The entire Zhou clan was in an uproar!

Because the clan's strong who had participated in the sea-patrol mission were about to return.

Zhou Fuyou's family was the same. Early that morning, he carried his sister and, accompanied by Granny Lin, arrived early at Coral Town to wait.

Coral Town was a small settlement near the Zhou clan.

Most of its residents were mortals without spirit roots.

It could be imagined that as the Zhou clan developed and its members multiplied, many, many mortals without spirit roots would be born.

Those who were clever, agile, and loyal would remain within the clan to serve the cultivators, but more mortals would be sent to Coral Town.

And this Coral Town would gradually grow in population, from a small town to a city, and might even merge with the mortals of other cultivation families to form something akin to a secular small country.

Zhou Fuyou paid no attention to these things. He simply stood in an open space, gazing into the distance.

A delighted voice came from beside him.

"Mayfly! You're here too!"

It was Zhou Yuqi.

Zhou Fuyou greeted him simply, but when he saw the middle-aged man beside Zhou Yuqi, he couldn't help showing a puzzled expression.

"And this is?"

Zhou Yuqi introduced him simply, "A mortal Xiantian grandmaster found in Coral Town. He's temporarily responsible for accompanying me on my daily outings."

Zhou Fuyou looked around, then asked, "What about Fourth Aunt Yin?"

Zhou Yuqi's face showed grief. "Fourth Aunt... half a month ago, she accidentally caught the miasma of Egret Marsh. The treatment was too slow, and she unfortunately passed away."

Zhou Fuyou was stunned.

Half a month ago?

Wasn't that around the time when news spread that the clan's strong were about to finish their mission and return to Egret Marsh?

Fourth Aunt Yin didn't die early or late—why did she die precisely at that time?

Moreover, although the miasma of Egret Marsh was terrifying, as long as one didn't go deep, and with the clan's detoxification pills, it wasn't that easy to contract.

Just as he was puzzling over this, cheers erupted around him.

The cheers grew from small to large, gradually becoming dense and numerous.

Because in the distant sky, a small black dot was rapidly approaching.

Soon, that small black dot filled everyone's eyes.

It was the Zhou clan's Coral Flying Boat!

Boom!

The massive flying boat landed in the open space of Coral Town, stirring up currents of air.

Figures stepped out from within, and the nearby Zhou clan members enthusiastically welcomed them.

Only Zhou Fuyou, his sister, and their servant stood frozen in place.

They didn't see familiar figures.

Until a gentle, elderly woman walked up to them.

"Elder Shuzhen!" Granny Lin bowed respectfully, then was about to continue speaking.

But the Golden Core stage Zhou Shuzhen sighed, her gaze falling on Zhou Fuyou and the little girl he held, meeting their expectant eyes as she spoke slowly.

"Your parents unfortunately fell in the mission. Accept my condolences."

(Chapter End)
