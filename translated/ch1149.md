# Chapter 1149: Body Transformed into a Furnace, Panel Opened Again

The previously quiet cultivation chamber was now filled with rumbling sounds.

Like a great river surging, like muffled thunder rolling, like a giant beast snoring.

A sturdy, naked figure sat cross-legged in the center of the cultivation chamber, his body radiating blazing red light.

Luo Chen's eyes were tightly shut as he used his formidable willpower to suppress the formation of the final strand of Dan Force, refined to its utmost limit.

Under his control, this strand of Dan Force ultimately condensed into a seed the size of a dragon's eye.

A Dan Seed!

Though this seed appeared small, the qi and blood contained within it was immense.

An ordinary Blood River realm martial artist would need only one such seed to attempt a breakthrough to the Furnace realm.

And if someone could look past Luo Chen's fleshly body, they would discover that within him, there were now a terrifying one hundred and eight Dan Seeds!

Each Dan Seed rested quietly in its place.

With thirty-six Dan Seeds forming one cycle of the Great Circulation, Luo Chen had constructed three complete Great Circulations!

The surging power of qi and blood, at the moment the final Dan Seed took shape, could no longer be suppressed.

Perfect!

Luo Chen no longer intended to suppress himself either.

With his physical foundation, converting to martial arts and cultivating to the Furnace realm was actually a trivial matter.

The reason he had spent so much time was simply because Luo Chen wanted to achieve a perfect breakthrough.

Now that one hundred and eight Dan Seeds had formed, the breakthrough was a natural progression.

The next moment, Luo Chen moved his mind.

All the Dan Seeds within his body began to rotate in an extremely violent manner, yet within this crude simplicity, there was a hint of inexplicable profound charm.

The Great Dao is simple—nothing more than this.

As the Dan Seeds howled, blood clouds spread, forming what seemed like a nebula within Luo Chen's body.

The nebula surged mightily, the many Dan Seeds like scattered stars, operating spontaneously, ultimately slowly gathering together.

This process felt extremely slow to Luo Chen, almost like a year passing in a single day.

He did not know how long passed.

Suddenly!

From within the nebula came a muffled sound.

*Dong!*

Like the first opening of heaven and earth, like a star exploding, yet ultimately giving one the feeling of a giant's heart pounding vigorously as he split heaven and earth apart.

The Dan Force, condensed to its extreme, exploded with a bang. The vast qi and blood refined to its utmost formed strands of unparalleled Astral Qi.

As the Astral Qi circulated, it coalesced into a nearly substantial furnace cauldron.

Heaven and earth are the great furnace, creation the craftsman.

Yin and yang serve as fuel and carbon, all things as copper.

The human body is an immortal body, thus the human body can also serve as a great furnace, refining all things in heaven and earth, achieving the realm of Human Immortal!

This slowly rotating Qi and Blood Furnace was the foundation of the Great Dao that could refine everything.

A powerful suction force, centered on Luo Chen's body, extended wildly toward the outside world.

Unfortunately, in the end, it grasped nothing, and the Qi and Blood Furnace gradually stopped rotating.

"It's because this realm lacks spiritual energy!"

Inexplicably, Luo Chen gained some understanding.

In the orthodox path of body refinement, after transforming the body into a furnace, one could automatically absorb the spiritual energy of heaven and earth, carrying out the reverse process of refining essence into qi in immortal cultivation.

That is, refining qi into essence!

Refining the most fundamental and omnipresent spiritual energy of a world into one's own essence.

The richer the world's spiritual energy, the higher the efficiency of this refinement.

For example, in the Mountains and Seas Realm, the spiritual energy was quite abundant. That was why Wang Yuan had only used a little over two hundred years of cultivation to reach the fourth realm of body refinement and cultivate the Leakless Golden Body.

Looking at this cultivation speed, Wang Yuan's achievements were actually not inferior to Luo Chen forming his Nascent Soul at two hundred years old.

But now, Luo Chen was in the Great Destruction Realm, and the situation was vastly different.

This place was a land without spiritual energy!

The spiritual energy contained in the void was nearly nonexistent. How could he refine the origin of heaven and earth with his physical body?

"No wonder the lifespan of Furnace realm cultivators in this realm is only two to three hundred years, far inferior to Wang Yuan's five-hundred-year limit comparable to Adept cultivators. I suppose it's also due to the lack of spiritual energy in this realm."

Luo Chen thought this way.

Suddenly!

At the moment Luo Chen's body transformed into a furnace, a faint vibration came from within his originally sealed Purple Mansion.

As if in response!

Luo Chen abruptly opened his eyes, a look of astonishment on his face.

"The Primordial Unity Cauldron?!"

If he hadn't sensed it incorrectly, that vibration, like a responding call, was precisely from his lifebound treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, which had accompanied him for centuries!

This response had even pierced through the seal of his Purple Mansion.

As if by instinct, no suppression could isolate this response.

Luo Chen suppressed the shock and confusion in his heart, carefully pondering the reason.

After a long while.

Luo Chen made a hypothesis, though not very certain.

His lifebound treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, had always had a profound foundation. Later, he had added many precious materials for refinement. Especially when leaving the Mountains and Seas Realm, he had expended great effort to refine three fifth-rank spirit mountains into it.

Logically speaking, with such a foundation of supreme treasures, it should have long since advanced to the level of a spirit treasure.

But even when Luo Chen ascended, the Primordial Unity Cauldron was still a true artifact, having not yet given birth to its own spiritual intelligence.

Now, the Primordial Unity Cauldron was responding from afar to the Qi and Blood Furnace through the sealed Purple Mansion. Did this mean that the Qi and Blood Furnace could promote the advancement of the Primordial Unity Cauldron?

Hence, the active response of the lifebound treasure?

After a period of deep thought, Luo Chen ultimately could not find the true answer.

At present, his divine sense was sealed, his Purple Mansion petrified, and his entire body had not a trace of spiritual power. Especially his formerly painstakingly refined primordial spirit, which had been shattered by the demon phoenix. He simply could not open the sealed Purple Mansion, let alone retrieve the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

To truly verify this hypothesis, he would at least need to recover his divine sense and refine a bit of mana before he could retrieve the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

But Luo Chen was not discouraged.

Because in his view, that day was not far off!

The Furnace realm could already be considered a stage of entering the hall in martial arts cultivation. Paired with this body comparable to the Great Desolate, subsequent cultivation would only get faster and faster. Once he cultivated the Leakless Golden Body, Luo Chen could attempt to use the method he had previously thought of to break the demon phoenix's seal on his divine sense.

Yes, Luo Chen's Great Desolate physical body was still preserved.

This was the only comfort he had after falling into the Great Destruction Realm.

It had been somewhat fragile before, but after gradually recovering, it could now briefly exert a bit of power.

That was why, in the martial arena, he could defeat Iron Vanguard in a single move.

That move appeared to be the Overlord Removes Armor from the *Devil Lord's Seven Scattering Techniques*, but in reality, it was the power and speed of this physical body's essence.

With the purest force and speed, he had crushed Iron Vanguard.

Next, he only needed to follow the orthodox path of body refinement and quickly climb to higher realms.

In silence, Luo Chen opened the long-dormant panel.

Like a water curtain, the panel slowly unfolded before Luo Chen's eyes. Looking at the large number of dimmed, gray-white entries, Luo Chen couldn't help but feel a bit of melancholy.

**[Lifespan: 600/2000]**
**[Spirit Root: Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, Earth]**
**[Physique: Fire Spirit Body (Five-Spirit Dao Body hidden), True Spirit Body (Incomplete)]**
**[Realm: Archaic Fifth Rank 1/100, Furnace Realm 1/100]**
**[Laws: Wood's Withering-Bloom 35/100, Water, Fire, Earth Origin 1/100]**
**[Cultivation Art: Divine Astral Qi Perfect 460/500]**
**[Spells: Unusable]**
**[Soul Arts: Unusable]**
**[Body Arts: Three Heads Six Arms (Divine Ability), Devil Lord's Seven Scattering Techniques, Wind Moon Thunder Cloud Stepping on the Immortal Path, Three Fingers Extinguishing Immortals (Blood God Tribulation Finger, Immortal Returning to Penglai, Blood Transformation Demon Finger)]**
**[Achievement Points: 170]**

His first glance, as always, fell upon his lifespan.

At present, he was a full six hundred years old!

Calculating carefully, he had ascended at five hundred and seventy years old, and about thirty years had passed since then.

These thirty years, except for the most recent one, were almost completely without memory.

In other words, that was the thirty years he had drifted unconscious in the cosmos.

All of this was thanks to that demon phoenix!

Thinking of this, even with a Dao heart as clear as Luo Chen's, his thoughts became blocked, his heart filled with hatred!

He hated not only the humiliation the demon phoenix had brought him but also the loss of a full three thousand years of lifespan!

Yes, three thousand years!

At this moment, Luo Chen's upper lifespan limit was two thousand years.

But Luo Chen still remembered that when he ascended, his upper lifespan limit had surpassed any previous Ascendant great power, reaching an unprecedented five thousand years.

This was only because, at that time, he not only had the Ascendant realm but his physical body had also broken through to the Archaic Fifth Rank by consuming the Demon-Devil Heart-Linking Fruit, achieving the True Spirit Body, which added an extra two thousand five hundred years of lifespan.

The two combined, achieving a lifespan of five thousand, was not inferior even when compared to the legendary Void Refinement True Lords.

However, because of Rosycloud, Luo Chen's primordial spirit was shattered, his immortal cultivation realm fell to the bottom, and he lost two thousand five hundred years of lifespan out of nowhere. Moreover, due to the injuries to his physical body, he had permanently lost another five hundred years of lifespan.

Now, in terms of lifespan, he was barely comparable to those who had just entered the Ascendant realm.

This grudge, as long as he could leave the Great Destruction Realm, he would repay tenfold, a hundredfold!

Otherwise, his thoughts would not be clear!

Luo Chen let out a long breath of turbid qi, temporarily suppressing the resentment in his heart.

His gaze moved downward, pausing for a moment on the Physique entry.

"True Spirit Body... the end point of the Archaic Body Refinement Art is indeed the True Spirit!"

This was not the first time Luo Chen had made a similar exclamation.

Earlier, when he had first taken the Demon-Devil Heart-Linking Fruit and used it to advance to the Archaic Fifth Rank, he had already exclaimed this.

Because that was the first time the entry **[True Spirit Body]** had appeared in the Physique column.

According to what was said in the *Mystic Realm Secret Record* gifted by Nether Departure Divine Lord, in the Mystic Realm, there existed some powerful beings comparable to Great Vehicle stage cultivators.

They were neither human, nor demon, nor monster, yet they grasped the laws of the Great Dao that only humans, demons, and monsters could comprehend through cultivation!

Such beings were called True Spirits!

Born of heaven and earth, from the moment of their birth, they were the darlings favored by heaven and earth.

However, the Mystic Realm was ancient beyond compare, and such powerful beings had become fewer and fewer. Few people had heard news of them.

Only some powerful cultivators who enjoyed research, based on records in ancient texts, speculated that these True Spirits were very likely ancient desolate beasts that had grown to their extreme.

Luo Chen had not originally cultivated the path of qi and blood; he had purely refined his body, stumbling onto the path of Archaic Body Refinement. By the time he cultivated to the Archaic Fifth Rank, he was already no different from the Great Desolate beasts of legend in the Mountains and Seas Realm.

And on the panel, the marked Physique entry was not **[Desolate Beast]**, but **[True Spirit]**.

From this, it could be seen that the research in the Mystic Realm was not false; the end point of desolate beast evolution was indeed the True Spirit.

Unfortunately, Luo Chen's True Spirit Body was no longer complete; it had become incomplete.

Luo Chen knew where the incompleteness lay.

"The only True Spirit Source Blood was actively burned away by me."

In silence, Luo Chen shifted his gaze.

And next, the speed at which his eyes moved far exceeded any previous time he had browsed the attribute panel.

All because, on the panel, all entries related to qi-refining immortal cultivation were submerged in dim gray-white.

Whether it was the Ascendant realm he had once reached, the qi-refining cultivation arts he had practiced, or spells, soul arts, even alchemy, artifact forging, talisman drawing, puppetry, and so on.

Even the two divine abilities he had created himself, *Withering-Bloom Dao Finger* and *Stellar Fire Burning the Realm*, as well as the divine ability *Thousand Rivers Guide* learned from the Nether Origin Sect's Heavenly Water Palace, were still unusable.

It could be said that anything related that required mana and divine sense to activate was completely sealed.

Only medical arts and formation arts, which did not strongly require mana and divine sense, were still emitting faint light, but they were still largely dimmed.

The only thing that surprised Luo Chen a little was that on the Laws column, the four laws of Wood, Fire, Water, and Earth that he had comprehended still existed.

It seemed these things had not disappeared because his realm had fallen.

"Once proven, forever proven?"

Murmuring, Luo Chen could not find the answer.

He only knew now that on the panel, the entries that still emitted bright light were all related to martial arts cultivation.

Martial realm — Furnace realm early stage.

Cultivation art — *Divine Astral Qi*.

And the body arts and martial techniques he had either once mastered or learned in the past year.

Such as the *Devil Lord's Seven Scattering Techniques* he had collected, the supplemented *Wind Moon Thunder Cloud Stepping on the Immortal Path*, and even the three finger techniques Wang Yuan had created himself.

Even the martial divine ability *Three Heads Six Arms* learned from the Pure Land of Lapis Lazuli was displayed in an available state.

Of course, Luo Chen knew in his heart that, with his current state, forcibly using Three Heads Six Arms could at most be maintained for half an incense stick's time; he could not fight for long.

Otherwise, this physical body would likely suffer severe damage again.

After briefly browsing the attribute panel, Luo Chen had a complete concept of his current situation.

Regarding qi-refining immortal cultivation, he could completely abandon any thoughts, at least within the Great Destruction Realm, he should not harbor any illusions.

Regarding Archaic body refinement, although it was displayed as cultivable, Luo Chen would certainly not do so.

What the Archaic body refinement method required was never talent or perseverance, nor cultivation art manuals; what it required was endless resources!

Think about how much demon beast flesh and blood, immortal pills and wondrous medicines, spirit rice and spirit wine he had consumed in the Mountains and Seas Realm, even using poison gu to refine his body in extreme ways. One could see that it was a bottomless pit of a cultivation path.

In the Great Destruction Realm, at least as far as it seemed now, there were not so many external resources for Luo Chen to take.

Therefore, if Luo Chen wanted to climb upward, there was only one path.

To follow the orthodox body refinement method of the qi and blood path.

Although this cultivation system still required some external resources, it focused more on inward excavation, developing the limits of the physical body!

The human body itself was an existence containing infinite treasures!

Luo Chen spread out his palm.

A wisp of pure white qi force slowly rose.

Compared to the four types of qi force—Bright, Dark, Transformation, and Dan—from the Blood River realm, this wisp of pure white qi force was countless times stronger.

This was Astral Qi!

An energy that could be compared to the mana of Adept cultivators.

If it were only this, it would not be worth his high hopes.

What truly made Luo Chen care was the possibility of this Astral Qi evolving.

"In Wang Yuan's *Divine Astral Qi* book, it records how to evolve one's own Astral Qi to a very high level, cultivating the Divine Astral Qi, which can contend with magical treasures and true artifacts."

"Thirty-six wisps of divine wind, thirty-six wisps of earthly balefulness!"

"I never paid attention before, but now it seems, that has already taken on the embryonic form of a martial divine ability. It shares the same subtlety as my cultivation of the *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon*, nurturing a mouthful of Scarlet Firmament Sword Qi in my chest."

"I wonder if there are so many divine winds and earthly balefulness in this Great Destruction Realm?"

Luo Chen's gaze flickered, and he calmly rose from the cultivation chamber.

It seemed.

He would have to make a trip to the City Lord's Mansion.

He wondered if Xiao Feng would still welcome him.
