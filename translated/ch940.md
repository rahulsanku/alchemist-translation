# Chapter 940: Reflection Sect, Lantern Lord

High in the sky, clouds drifted in clusters.

Suddenly, two figures shot through them, sweeping away large swaths of clouds and leaving the sky looking incomplete.

After the two figures had passed, echoes of their conversation still lingered.

"Mount Mijie is one of the great wonders of the Southern Frontier!"

"It appears half-hidden, half-revealed in the mortal world. Only a few hundred years ago was its true entrance discovered and stabilized through great means."

"Once inside, the spaces layer upon layer, as if three thousand worlds converge within a single mountain. If one isn't careful, it's very easy to get lost. Moreover, when we Sages escape, we should be able to shrink the earth to an inch and flee a thousand li in a single step. But within Mount Mijie, one is greatly suppressed and simply cannot flee far."

"Therefore, Mount Mijie forbids the use of force!"

"Some say Mount Mijie was transformed from a great power's spirit treasure. Others speculate it might be the remains of an ancient desolate beast. But most believe this place was formed by the natural creation of heaven and earth—a semi-formed cave paradise."

A wondrous land containing countless small spaces within?

Luo Chen found this introduction highly intriguing.

Could it be something similar to the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven?

But according to what Cave Lord Minglei had said, the entrance to Mount Mijie itself existed in the mortal world, clearly not formed from the remains of the Great Desolate.

By that reasoning, it obviously wasn't transformed from a spirit treasure either.

Could it truly be a cave paradise naturally formed by heaven and earth's creation, like the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land?

"With such a wondrous place, wouldn't the holy land Life and Death Gate be tempted?" Luo Chen asked curiously.

Cave Lord Minglei replied, "It's not that they weren't tempted, but before Mount Mijie appeared, Life and Death Gate had already closed its mountain gates and rarely interacted with the outside world. Then this place fell into the hands of a great cultivator named Sage Songyang. After occupying Mount Mijie, he used it as his base to establish the Fierce Peak Stiffness Alliance."

Sage Songyang, Fierce Peak Stiffness Alliance.

Luo Chen savored these names, but what intrigued him more was the matter of Life and Death Gate closing its mountain gates and cutting off contact with the outside world.

However, Cave Lord Minglei couldn't provide an answer to that either.

He couldn't even pinpoint the exact time when the mountain gates had been sealed.

"Back then, I was merely a mere Adept cultivator—how could I be qualified to know such secrets!" Cave Lord Minglei said with a bitter smile.

Luo Chen then realized that Cave Lord Minglei wasn't like Lingfengzi, a seasoned Sage who knew everything about the local cultivation world's intelligence, big or small.

After all, he was a wandering cultivator who had only recently achieved the Dao.

Not to mention his strength, just in terms of information channels, he couldn't compare with Sages who had been famous for many years.

Thinking back, when Luo Chen himself was at the Adept stage, it was much the same.

He had relied on the intelligence channels of the Luo Tian Sect in the Eastern Wilderness and the Myriad Immortals Assembly in the Northern Sea to avoid being completely in the dark about the outside world.

"At the last exchange meeting, someone recommended me to attend. First time's strange, second time's familiar—this time I can recommend you to join as well."

"Is it that troublesome?"

"It's quite troublesome. Southern Frontier cultivators are skilled in using gu insects that harm people invisibly. Coupled with the earlier calamity of the Devil Heart Gu wreaking havoc in the Southern Frontier, many people fundamentally distrust outsiders. Various circles are quite closed off. Without an acquaintance's recommendation, even if one's cultivation is heaven-reaching, they'll only be a lone operator."

"You jest. If one's cultivation truly reached heaven, why not establish their own sect?"

"Haha, Brother Qi speaks truly. A true strong person should be like Sage Songyang—occupy a precious land, establish a foundation, and have a hundred respond to their call, with countless answering."

"Sage Songyang, ah..." Luo Chen sighed, then abruptly changed the subject. "Cave Lord, did you experience the calamity of the Devil Heart Gu?"

Cave Lord Minglei's expression changed, and just as he was about to speak, a voice suddenly came from below.

"Is it not a joy to have friends come from afar? Since Brother Minglei is passing by my mountain gate, why not come down for a chat?"

The voice was ethereal, clearly coming from below, yet its source was difficult to trace.

This showed that the existence who had sent out this voice possessed remarkably powerful divine sense.

However, Luo Chen merely glanced once and then withdrew his gaze.

He focused his attention on Cave Lord Minglei. "Who is that down there?"

Cave Lord Minglei's expression was very unnatural. He said in a low voice, "You could call him a friend of mine. He was also the one who recommended me to join the Sage circle last time. His real name is unknown; outsiders call him Lantern Lord, and his identity is the leader of the Reflection Sect."

"What do you mean by 'you could call him'?" Luo Chen stared at Cave Lord Minglei with a half-smile.

The other party said helplessly, "Ever since I formed my Sage, I've been keen on expanding my network. Lantern Lord knew me from early on, and I once considered him a friend. But last time when the Feng family attacked Wind Thunder Gorge, I widely invited helpers, yet received no response. So naturally, the word 'friend' has to be heavily discounted."

Luo Chen understood, then asked, "So what do we do now?"

Cave Lord Minglei thought for a moment and made up his mind.

"We've been traveling for many days, and my mana has been greatly depleted. Why not go down and borrow some ground to recover? And... also ask about why he didn't respond before!"

"That might be somewhat impolite."

Luo Chen laughed heartily and had already descended from the clouds.

And in a great sect where lanterns hung high and figures flickered in abundance, a gloomy-looking man in a large red robe had just emerged from a heart-pounding sensation.

"Again!"

"That feeling again!"

"No, it seems somewhat different?"

While he was still in doubt, his externally released divine sense had already discovered the two figures descending from the clouds.

His heart paused. He struck several hand seals onto himself, then went out to greet them.

...

"Cave Lord, won't you introduce this new friend to me?"

From a distance, Lantern Lord called out with a laugh.

Cave Lord Minglei said somewhat awkwardly, "The Tyrant Cultivator Qi Tian, one of the thirty-six cave lords of Wind Thunder Gorge."

Lantern Lord's footsteps paused, and a look of surprise appeared on his face.

At that moment, Luo Chen, who had been observing the surrounding scenery with his hands behind his back, slowly turned around. A powerful surge of blood and qi shot up into the sky, turning into rolling wolf smoke.

This wolf smoke of blood and qi was extremely alarming, but it appeared and vanished in an instant, like a fleeting flower.

Lantern Lord exclaimed in surprise, "A body cultivator?"

Cave Lord Minglei's expression returned to normal. He smiled and said, "Yes, Brother Qi is a body-refining cultivator. Although the path he walks is different from ours, in terms of capability, he is in no way inferior to me."

Lantern Lord couldn't help but regard Luo Chen with even greater respect.

In his heart, he was even more full of praise.

Wasn't Wind Thunder Gorge a bitterly cold and harsh place?

How had it produced two formidable figures in succession?

No wonder that old fellow from the Feng family was so covetous of Wind Thunder Gorge and had even given advance notice for him not to get involved.

Though he was grumbling inwardly, on the surface, Lantern Lord still wore a harmonious smile.

"Come, come, come! Since you've arrived at my Reflection Sect, let me offer you proper hospitality. Please, come inside!"

Cave Lord Minglei stood motionless.

Lantern Lord raised an eyebrow. "Surely the Cave Lord isn't blaming me for not helping you earlier?"

"How would I dare!"

"Alas, there's nothing to be done. I had a deviation in my cultivation at the time and simply didn't dare to act rashly. Now that I see the Cave Lord is safe and sound, and even able to travel far, it's clear those troublesome matters have been resolved. This sect leader's heart is greatly at ease!"

Cave Lord Minglei was surprised. "You had a deviation in your cultivation?"

Lantern Lord waved his hand. "Some troubles. Let's sit down and discuss them in detail!"

Cave Lord Minglei hesitated for a moment, then looked at Luo Chen.

The other party didn't speak but had already taken a step, heading toward the mountain gate of the Reflection Sect.

Cave Lord Minglei immediately followed.

Watching this scene, Lantern Lord fell into thought.

Between the two of them, it seemed the newly risen Tyrant Cultivator Qi Tian was the one in charge?

(Chapter End)
