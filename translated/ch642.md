# Chapter 642: Sixfold Imperial Flying Cloud, Visitors from Central Continent

There were no sun or moon in the mountains, and a thousand years had passed in the world.

The saying was somewhat exaggerated, but for Luo Chen, two years had unknowingly passed while he devoted himself to studying the All-Encompassing Fire Prison [TERM?] at Peng Lake.

During these two years, he improved at every moment.

He had not only developed the killing move known as the All-Encompassing Fire Prison [TERM?], but had also gained in other areas.

His attainments in formations had improved, and so had his refinement of his lifebound magic treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Perhaps the only thing dragged down was his cultivation speed.

Using too much mana to deploy a domain had, in the end, delayed his cultivation progress. His original estimate of advancing to the seventh level of the Golden Core stage in around ten years would likely be greatly postponed. He did not know whether he could break through before heading to the Sunken Sea.

Luo Chen did not force the issue.

In his view, unless one broke through an entire major realm and formed an essential transformation, an improvement in a minor realm would not create an earthshaking difference.

In battles at the same rank, magic treasures, secret arts, and all kinds of external objects were the decisive killing moves.

The All-Encompassing Fire Prison [TERM?] he was currently researching was clearly a killing move capable of deciding life and death.

To thoroughly master this move, Luo Chen had to use real combat to understand its strengths and weaknesses, lest unexpected situations arise in a true life-and-death battle.

When Luo Chen arrived at the Demon Hunter Office and saw its bustling, flourishing scene, he realized that he was not the only thing that had changed. This cultivation world, which seemed unchanged for ten million years, had changed as well.

An enormous Demon King's corpse lay in the open ground of the Demon Hunter Office. When Luo Chen passed by, the corner of his eye twitched.

A golden single horn. A silver-white body. It resembled a water buffalo.

"Golden-Horned Silver Rhinoceros [TERM?]. This thing was actually hunted?"

The Foundation Establishment cultivator leading the way likewise sighed in amazement. "To tell the truth, we were just as shocked at the time. When this Demon King's corpse was brought back, the entire Demon Hunter Office boiled over. Even among third-tier Demon Kings, the Golden-Horned Silver Rhinoceros [TERM?] is among the finest, a powerful demon beast race ranked alongside the Great King Squid [TERM?] and the Sword-Halberd Whale [TERM?]. For Lord Dao Lan to slay it is worthy of the reputation of the Myriad Immortals Assembly's fourth eight-star demon hunter!"

Was it Dao Lan who killed it?

Luo Chen narrowed his eyes slightly, recalling the silent man who had ranged freely through Withered Wood Ridge with only a Golden Suanni Saber.

With the Withered Wood Ridge mission's contribution added to his many years of accumulation, he had finally become the Myriad Immortals Assembly's fourth eight-star demon hunter after Angler, Purple Dowager, and Ghost Spirit Child.

Luo Chen paused outside for only a short while before entering the inner hall under the guidance of the Demon Hunter Office cultivator.

This time, Purple Dowager came to receive him.

"Where is Angler?" Luo Chen asked in puzzlement.

Purple Dowager smiled faintly. "He received a reward from the Perfected Being and has been cultivating in seclusion these past two years. This enormous Demon Hunter Office can only be managed by this weak woman alone."

Luo Chen came to a realization.

After a brief exchange of old pleasantries, he explained his purpose to Purple Dowager.

"You want to go to the Sunken Sea battlefield?"

"Mm. Is there any problem?"

"None at all. I could not ask for more." Purple Dowager revealed a delighted expression and immediately explained the current situation at the Demon Hunter Office to Luo Chen in detail.

Ever since Blood Wanderer issued the mission to clear out demon beasts around the Sunken Sea's outer perimeter, many wandering cultivators in hidden cultivation had taken the initiative to go to the battlefield for the high merit rewards.

But most of them were low-rank wandering cultivators.

Along the shore of the Sunken Sea, the number of demon beasts that had gathered over thirty years had unknowingly accumulated to a terrifying amount.

According to estimates by attentive people, there were several hundred thousand of them.

Even if they were scattered, they were still not something ordinary Myriad Immortals Assembly cultivators could easily contend with.

Especially when this was an active attack and they could not borrow the advantage of the formations on the three great defensive lines.

Luo Chen did not understand. "Isn't it said that under such circumstances, the other great forces of the Northern Sea usually provide assistance as well?"

Purple Dowager smiled bitterly. "This time, the demon beasts are not attacking the human race's hinterland. Why would they help? Moreover, among the Northern Sea's top forces, apart from our Myriad Immortals Assembly and the Tianyuan Merchant Alliance, all have been dragged into the righteous-demonic war. How could they have spare strength to support us?"

"So that is how it is." Luo Chen was thoughtful.

At the same time, he understood why Purple Dowager was so delighted when he volunteered.

A powerful Golden Core cultivator could rival hundreds or thousands of low-rank cultivators alone. If he attacked without reservation, he could completely annihilate over ten thousand low-rank demon beasts.

Clearly, Luo Chen was this kind of landmark powerful Golden Core.

"Are there any suitable missions? I can do one along the way and avoid wasting the trip."

Purple Dowager's eyes lit up. "Naturally. I currently have a small mission on hand that is troublesome, yet also rather loose. Daoist Azure Yang, if you are willing to do it, you can earn quite a few merits."

What did she mean by troublesome yet loose?

Puzzled, Luo Chen followed Purple Dowager to a side hall at the rear of the Demon Hunter Office.

Three unfamiliar Golden Core cultivators were sitting idly inside.

When Luo Chen entered, the three looked over curiously, but after one glance, they withdrew their gazes with little interest.

Perhaps because Luo Chen had concealed his cultivation and appeared perfectly ordinary, it was difficult for him to draw interest.

"These are?" Luo Chen frowned.

Purple Dowager pulled Luo Chen aside and transmitted softly, "Only three Golden Core wandering cultivators who recently joined the Myriad Immortals Assembly. Your mission is to bring them to the front line. As for what they do afterward, you need not worry."

"That easy?" Luo Chen's doubts deepened.

Purple Dowager thought for a moment. "Of course, if they encounter life-threatening danger and you have spare strength, it would be best if you look after them a little."

Why did this feel so familiar?

For no apparent reason, Luo Chen thought of what Sang Jiugong had done back then.

Only the people being sheltered had changed from a group of Foundation Establishment cultivators to three unfamiliar Golden Core cultivators.

If he followed that line of comparison...

Luo Chen's divine sense quietly swept over the three.

"Hmph! Fellow Daoist outside, that is a little impolite." A delicate-looking female cultivator glared out in displeasure.

Did she have some treasure that could detect another person's divine sense perception?

Luo Chen could see clearly that the cultivation the three displayed was around the third level of Golden Core. They should not have been able to detect his divine sense probing.

On the surface, Luo Chen still cupped his hands from afar and made an apologetic gesture.

Inwardly, however, Luo Chen had already reached a conclusion.

These three should be from some sect or clan force. They were definitely not normal wandering cultivators.

"Daoist Azure Yang, will you accept this mission or not?" Purple Dowager asked anxiously. She had recently had piles of troublesome matters and wanted to throw out whatever she could.

Seeing Luo Chen hesitate, Purple Dowager gritted her teeth and said, "These three came under Longyuan True Being's personal care, which is why the merit reward is so generous. Treat it as doing a good deed along the way and accept the mission! I do not feel assured handing them to the other Golden Core cultivators at the front line. Only you have this ability."

So they came through Longyuan True Being's route.

Luo Chen's heart chilled slightly, but his face showed no hesitation.

"Since a Perfected Being has shown concern, I naturally cannot shirk responsibility."

Purple Dowager clearly let out a long breath. "Good."

Outside the Demon Hunter Office, the four Golden Core cultivators looked at one another.

In the end, Luo Chen helplessly took out a magic treasure-grade flying vessel from his storage bag.

"Wow, a wandering cultivator like you actually has such a fine magic treasure flying vessel!" The delicate female cultivator cried out in surprise at a single glance.

On both sides of the road, many wandering cultivators of Longyuan City also gave startled sighs, their gazes falling on the carriage.

How was it merely fine?

It was simply luxurious.

Three plain and three splendid tones, cloud patterns spread across it.

Long blue, black, and white sails hung on one side, while purple, gold, and red sails mirrored them on the other.

On the roof of the carriage, ruyi pearl-dripping boards were spread throughout. Flowing tassels and flying sleeves hung down, and a noble five-colored canopy stood high above.

Around the broad carriage, white jade was laid everywhere, with golden radiance gleaming.

Even the six wheels beneath it were extraordinary. The spokes crisscrossed to a full count of eighteen, each dusted with gold powder.

The corner of Luo Chen's mouth twitched slightly. He had known that once he took out this Sixfold Imperial Flying Cloud Carriage [TERM?], he would not be able to keep a low profile.

But there was no helping it. He did not have a suitable flying magic treasure for travel.

He did have two lower-grade artifact flying boats, but they were not suited for four Golden Core cultivators traveling together, much less for long-distance, high-speed travel.

Only this Sixfold Imperial Flying Cloud Carriage [TERM?] from Mountain Lord Hundred Craft's inheritance was relatively suitable.

Thinking about it, it was obvious. A travel vessel carried by the sect master of a great artifact-forging sect was bound to be extremely luxurious.

Its grade alone was as high as middle-grade.

If its specifications were just a bit higher, calling it a Sage's carriage would not be excessive.

However...

Luo Chen looked strangely at the delicate female cultivator. Although she had cried out in surprise, it was not because of the cloud carriage's luxury. She was merely surprised that he, a wandering cultivator, possessed a carriage of this level.

In other words, what she ordinarily saw must include flying carriages comparable to the Sixfold Imperial Flying Cloud Carriage [TERM?], or even more luxurious ones. Only then would she be so accustomed to them.

This made Luo Chen even more curious about the origins of the three.

"Three Fellow Daoists, please board the carriage."

Among them, the middle-aged man with a long beard at his chin had his ears tremble. His expression grew somewhat solemn. He cupped his hands to Luo Chen, then called the other male cultivator onto the cloud carriage.

Luo Chen did not hesitate either. He strode onto the cloud carriage and sat in the central carriage chamber covered by the canopy, releasing mana to activate the flying vessel.

There was a buzzing sound.

The next moment, brilliant light suddenly bloomed before the cloud carriage, and three jiaolong phantoms with claws and fangs appeared.

As the jiaolong roared, clouds and mist spread beneath the carriage, slowly lifting it upward.

After the cloud and wind patterns around the carriage flashed with light, it whooshed away, pulled by the three jiaolong into the horizon.

After they left, outside the Demon Hunter Office in Longyuan City, many wandering cultivators were still looking up, exclaiming in wonder.

The Sixfold Imperial Flying Cloud Carriage [TERM?] was the sect master's carriage of Hundred Craft Mountain.

It had been passed down generation after generation, completed by countless skilled craftsmen.

In terms of functions, it integrated flight, defense, and attack.

The so-called Sixfold Imperial referred to the overall restriction structure of the treasured carriage, which fused large numbers of mountain, water, wind, cloud, brightness, and emptiness restrictions into one. Only obscuring restrictions were relatively few.

It was not strange for there to be few obscuring restrictions. A sect master's carriage was meant to be open and glorious. Why hide its head and tail?

However, this cloud carriage did have one flaw that was not quite a flaw.

Aside from the warm chamber of the main seat, which had the canopy as cover, all other areas were open and spacious. No ship cabins, palace halls, or other structures had been installed.

This was probably to make it convenient during travel for disciples to stand on the deck and enhance its prestige.

But at this moment, an awkward point appeared.

Only Luo Chen occupied the broad space of the main seat.

The other three were left outside the shielding.

After activating the formation, Luo Chen walked out of the main seat and saw the three standing on one side of the deck, looking at the scenery flashing past below.

Hearing his undisguised footsteps, the three turned around at the same time.

Luo Chen smiled faintly, cupped his hands, and greeted them. "My humble surname is Luo, single name Hai. Fellow Daoists here have shown me favor and granted me the Dao title Daoist Azure Yang. Greetings to you three Fellow Daoists."

Among the three, the older middle-aged man stroked his beard and returned the greeting with a smile. "This humble one is Changfengzi [TERM?]. Greetings, Fellow Daoist Luo."

The other younger man carried a trace of arrogance. He glanced at Luo Chen and said casually, "I am Wu Tao [TERM?]!"

Luo Chen seemed not to see his arrogance and looked toward the last person.

The delicate female cultivator frowned, as if she did not want to pay attention to Luo Chen.

But under Changfengzi's look, she still performed a reluctant salute and introduced herself.

Her name was Guan Xiaoping [TERM?].

After the four exchanged names, Luo Chen naturally extended an invitation.

"The journey to the Sunken Sea is long. It will take at least ten or twenty days. The wind outside is strong. The three of you may cultivate inside to avoid the wind and frost."

Changfengzi, with his drifting long beard, seemed somewhat moved.

However, before he could speak, two voices came from beside his ear.

"It is only a little wind and frost."

"I don't want to go in. I will just watch the scenery outside."

Changfengzi opened his mouth, but in the end, he helplessly cupped his hands to Luo Chen.

"Thank you for your kind intentions, Fellow Daoist. With mana protecting our bodies, a little wind and rain is nothing."

Luo Chen's expression remained natural. "No matter. If anything comes up, simply call me."

After speaking, he entered the warm chamber of the main seat and openly activated the shielding formation.

After he went in, Changfengzi sighed.

"Junior Brother Wu, Junior Sister Guan, when traveling outside, one should still be a little more polite to others."

Wu Tao curled his lip. "He is only an ordinary Northern Sea wandering cultivator. How is he worth our politeness? On this trip, we came for Sage formation. If we are to befriend anyone, it should be powerful figures like Purple Dowager and Dao Lan."

The dissatisfaction on Guan Xiaoping's face deepened. "That person is impolite. He used divine sense to spy on us just now. I don't want to deal with him!"

Facing their pride, Changfengzi grew even more helpless.

He spoke earnestly. "This person is not as simple as he looks. Although he was probing us earlier, the three of us almost failed to notice. If Junior Sister Guan's Spirit-Waking Plaque [TERM?] had not been passively triggered, we might have been seen through completely without sensing anything. This shows that his realm is still above ours."

As soon as these words came out, Wu Tao looked at the carriage chamber covered by the canopy in disbelief.

"Him?"

Changfengzi nodded. "Do not truly treat him as a nameless wandering cultivator. Did you not notice earlier? When this cloud carriage appeared, many wandering cultivators in Longyuan City looked over. Yet they showed no greed. Instead, they revealed awe. I deliberately listened for a while and learned that this person is the lord of Peng Lake and is also famous within the Myriad Immortals Assembly."

Guan Xiaoping was half-believing and half-doubtful. "Is he really that formidable? Then why did the intelligence we received from Liu Perfected Being not include such a person in the Myriad Immortals Assembly?"

Changfengzi hesitated. "After our clan uncle successfully formed his Sage body in the Myriad Immortals Assembly, he secluded himself for fifty years and only recently emerged. The intelligence may be delayed. This person likely rose in the past fifty years, so it is normal that the intelligence contains no information on him."

The man and woman looked at each other.

Changfengzi finally sighed. "In short, even if you do not befriend Daoist Azure Yang, do not offend him too deeply. The Northern Sea is vast, and formidable figures emerge one after another. Even though we come from Central Continent, we should not look down on Northern Sea heroes."

Inside the warm chamber of the main seat, Luo Chen looked at the water mirror before him and listened to the voices transmitted from the jade qing beside him. A thoughtful expression appeared on his face.

Senior and junior siblings. From Central Continent. Their purpose was Sage formation.

Liu Perfected Being. Clan uncle.

Finally, he murmured a sentence.

"Could it be that Longyuan True Being?"

*(End of Chapter)*
