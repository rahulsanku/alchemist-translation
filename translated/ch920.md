# Chapter 920: Dream Bubbles, Entering the Dao Through Talismans

"Kill!"

Within the vast snowy mountains, the sound of battle cries was deafening.

Countless figures held their ground atop a series of pristine ice fortresses, continuously striking down the demon beasts that charged out from the Great Snowy Mountains.

Yet this beast tide was unexpectedly ferocious.

Under the repeated, relentless assaults of several demon kings, the ice fortresses—despite being laden with numerous defensive formations—collapsed one after another.

*Boom!*

When another ice fortress crumbled, two nearby fortresses collapsed in succession as well.

This was the chain reaction caused by interconnected formations.

Relying solely on a group of Initiate and Qi Refinement cultivators, their ability to withstand this beast tide was entirely due to the power of the formations.

But once a formation eye at a certain point failed, the backlash would be all the greater.

Gu Xiaorou and a group of junior sisters were forced to retreat. Staring at the massive Frost Wolf King that had smashed through the ice fortress, despair crept into their eyes.

A junior sister beside her asked, bewildered, "Where are the senior masters and uncles? Why haven't they made a move yet?"

Hearing this, Gu Xiaorou gritted her teeth and forced out the words, "They were severely injured exploring the Lingkong Secret Realm organized by the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect. They can't spare any strength right now. We have to rely on ourselves!"

As she spoke, she pressed a talisman against her body. Then, instead of retreating, she charged straight at the Frost Wolf King, drawing gasps of alarm from her junior sisters.

A vast amount of heaven and earth spiritual energy was attracted by the Ice Talisman, converging upon her and causing her aura to surge continuously.

The scene bore a striking resemblance to a Sage cultivator commanding the spiritual energy of heaven and earth.

However, the Frost Wolf King merely cast a contemptuous glance at her.

One swipe of its paw!

*Thump!*

As fast as Gu Xiaorou had gone, just as fast did she return.

She tumbled like a rolling gourd across the bitterly cold ground.

When she finally managed to stop, she spat out a mouthful of blood.

*Pfft!*

At this moment, despair filled the young woman's eyes.

The gap was simply too vast. Without being an Adept cultivator, there was no way to resist a third-rank demon king.

And this was just one Frost Wolf King.

On the nearby battlefield, there was also a Seven-Ring Snow Python and a Barred Snow Owl.

Those two were also third-rank demon kings, comparable to Adept cultivators. Without the aid of the formations, she and the other low-ranking female cultivators of the ice fortress were no match for them.

"Is this really the end?"

Unbidden, the image of the most reliable figure surfaced in Gu Xiaorou's mind.

"Sister, where are you?"

Just then!

A sword aura shot up from the Heavenly Mountain, piercing the sky.

A dazzlingly brilliant sword light crossed a thousand li in an instant. Before the terrified Frost Wolf King could react, the sword pierced it straight through the heart.

Gu Xiaorou was stunned.

Then she saw a familiar figure gradually materialize before her.

"Sister Canglong?"

The woman did not turn around. Her gaze was fixed on the distance.

Beside her, the massive corpse of the Frost Wolf King crashed to the ground. In the distance, the Seven-Ring Snow Python came surging in on a blizzard. In the sky, the Barred Snow Owl scattered its feathers, which shot down like sharp swords.

Her expression was calm. She merely raised her flying sword once more, aiming it at the Barred Snow Owl King.

As for the rampaging Seven-Ring Snow Python, she seemed to pay it no heed at all.

Gu Xiaorou struggled to her feet, crying out in alarm, "Sister, watch out!"

The next moment, Canglong shook her hand, and something resembling a castle or a pagoda appeared in her palm.

With a forceful toss!

The castle expanded continuously, eventually reaching nine stories in height before crashing down with immense force.

*Boom!*

The terrifying Seven-Ring Snow Python was instantly crushed and pinned firmly beneath the nine-story ice fortress.

Meanwhile, the Barred Snow Owl King let out a miserable shriek, flapped its wings, and fled without a second thought.

In an instant, of the three Great Snowy Mountain demon kings that had led this beast tide, one was dead, one was wounded, and one had escaped.

The crisis was resolved.

Without their leaders, the horde of low-rank demon beasts tucked their tails and fled in panic.

Only then did Canglong turn around, smiling at the young woman.

"Junior Sister Xiaorou, how did you end up in such a sorry state!"

Gu Xiaorou's mouth fell open in shock. She asked, incredulous, "Sister, have you broken through to the Adept stage?"

Canglong smiled faintly. With a wave of her hand, she recalled the bloodstained flying sword. With a single wipe, it revealed its original treasure light.

"If I hadn't reached the Adept stage, how could I have slain a demon king?"

Gu Xiaorou swallowed hard. Her sister's Adept stage was clearly not an ordinary one.

Having just broken through, she already possessed such terrifying combat power. She could even control two magical treasures simultaneously without any strain. The depth of her divine soul foundation was so formidable that even the senior masters and uncles she had seen in the past couldn't match this display.

And if she wasn't mistaken, that nine-story ice fortress should be the sect's ultimate treasure—the Moonlight Ice Fortress!

When had the master entrusted this item to her sister?

Canglong didn't chat with her. She instructed her to go and tend to her injuries, then walked across the battlefield, mopping up the remaining pockets of conflict.

Gu Xiaorou struggled to find a wall to lean against, checking her wounds.

A gentle, magnetic voice sounded beside her.

"Fellow Daoist Gu, seeing you charge at the Frost Wolf King just now was truly a sight to behold. It commands a whole new level of respect!"

Gu Xiaorou looked up and saw an extremely handsome face before her.

If she remembered correctly, this person should be a cultivator from a family attached to the ice fortress. His surname was Shang?

He had been invited this time to help resist the beast tide, and he appeared completely unharmed.

Gu Xiaorou shook her head, dejected. "It was nothing but overestimating my own abilities."

Cultivator Shang smiled. "To dare draw your sword against a third-rank demon king while only at the mid-Initiate stage—that courage alone is worthy of praise."

Hearing this, Gu Xiaorou felt a warmth in her heart.

Actually, she hadn't thought much at the time. She had just instinctively felt she couldn't let her other junior sisters face the demon king, so she had charged out.

She struggled to stand up. "I am Gu Xiaorou. May I ask your esteemed name, fellow Daoist?"

Unexpectedly, the man pressed a hand on her shoulder.

Amidst Gu Xiaorou's confusion and embarrassment, the man laughed. "Don't move recklessly. I am an alchemist, and I also have some knowledge of medicine. You've likely broken bones and torn meridians. If you accidentally damage your qi sea, it would be disastrous."

"Let me give you some simple treatment first!"

Gu Xiaorou opened her mouth, her face turning red, but in the end, she didn't refuse his kindness.

The handsome man moved swiftly, resetting her bones and reconnecting her meridians, casting spells to remove the bruises on her body.

He also revealed his background.

"My name is Shang Hu. Just call me Little Shang. As for age... I suppose I'm about the same as you."

"Mm."

Just as the two were chatting, cries of alarm suddenly rang out in the distance.

A blizzard swept in once more from the Great Snowy Mountains!

Within it, a red light flickered faintly, exuding a powerful aura that made it impossible to look directly at it.

Could another demon king be coming?

Canglong once again stood before everyone, sword in hand, her expression a mix of shock and doubt.

Behind her, a junior sister was about to strike, raising her magical artifact, but Canglong stopped her.

"Sister?" the junior sister asked, confused.

Canglong shook her head and, amidst everyone's worry, took a step forward.

"Is that you?"

In the wind and snow, a massive red dog paced out.

Seeing who it was, Canglong's face broke into a smile of reunion after a long separation.

"Big dog, you're finally back!"

...

On the Heavenly Mountain, the red dog sat on its haunches, gazing down at the teeming masses below.

Behind it, within a cave dwelling, Canglong sat by a bedside, respectfully chatting with a sickly but beautiful woman.

"Master, thanks to the sect's ultimate treasure you bestowed upon me, your disciple has resolved the beast tide calamity."

The woman looked at Canglong with great affection and said softly, "Even without the Moonlight Ice Fortress, slaying those three demon kings would not have been difficult for you, given your strength."

"Master overestimates me."

The woman sighed with emotion. "It's no overestimation. Your divine soul foundation is so profound it's rarely seen in this world. Even Han Zhan, that once-in-a-century genius from the Falling Cloud Sect, falls short of you. When Adepts duel, it's a matter of mana first, then divine sense. You have already mastered the *Ten Directions Extreme Freezing Dao* to perfection, your mana is abundant enough to look down on your peers, and with this vast divine sense you've somehow cultivated—our ice fortress finally has a worthy successor."

Canglong was initially a bit proud, but upon hearing this, she grew flustered.

"Master, you and the other senior masters are still alive and well. Why are you speaking of such matters?"

The woman took the young woman's slender, tender hand and said, coughing, "The Jade Cauldron Sword Sect harbors ill intentions. Using the exploration of the Lingkong Secret Realm as bait, they caused heavy losses to the experts of our six sects. Even your Uncle Wen fell inside. In the coming century, the Sword Sect will likely make further moves."

Canglong was shocked. "But they are a lofty Sage-level superior sect! How can they act so despicably?"

The woman smiled. "There are differences even among Sage-level superior sects. The Jade Cauldron Sword Sect may throw their weight around in the Jade Cauldron Domain, claiming unmatched combat power. But compared to other superior sects with deeper foundations, they are utterly fragile. They can't even muster enough manpower to scheme for the domain's interests. So they resort to underhanded tricks to weaken us Adept-level great sects."

Canglong opened her mouth, absorbing this information that felt both foreign and strangely familiar.

Those tricks—whether sowing discord or applying pressure—were all meant to make them fall into chaos on their own. Then the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect could gradually take control of the Jade Cauldron Domain without lifting a finger.

"Master, what should we do?" she asked, her heart heavy with worry.

The woman sighed. "What else can we do? Nothing more than uniting internally and seeking allies externally."

Then, she analyzed the potential allies for Canglong one by one.

"Especially the Falling Cloud Sect!"

"In this exploration of the Lingkong Secret Realm, they relied on the advantage of their puppets, suffering the fewest losses and preserving the most strength. Among their younger generation, there are also talents like Han Zhan and Linghu Jie. It can be said that the rise of the Falling Cloud Sect in the future is inevitable."

"If we can form an alliance with them, our ice fortress won't be left isolated and without support."

"Coincidentally, I recently heard that Han Zhan has successfully formed his core and plans to travel and explore the various domains of the Eastern Wilderness. You can accompany him and strengthen our ties."

Canglong was taken aback. She had met Han Zhan before.

He had a simple, honest face, but his thoughts were quite deep.

When he was at the Initiate stage, he had once wanted to borrow the ice fortress's ice prison to cultivate a divine soul secret art, but she had refused because she needed the ice prison to cultivate the *Soul-Splitting Art* herself.

"Travel with him?"

The woman smiled. "You should get out and see the world too. Even if you possess heaven-defying skills, if your mind and experience are lacking, it's easy to fall into a ditch. Once you've grown in all aspects, I can rest assured handing over the ice fortress to you."

Hearing the tone of her master entrusting her with a legacy, the woman's heart ached.

"Ran Ran understands."

"Ran Ran?"

The woman was startled. "You've remembered your past?"

Canglong nodded. "When I broke through to the Adept stage, I remembered everything. My surname is Xie, my given name Ran. I am from Guangling Commandery. I embarked on the path of immortality to seek medicine for my mother."

Upon hearing this, the woman's expression was complex.

She reached out and stroked the young woman's cheek.

"Good child!"

"In that case, during this journey, go back and visit your hometown!"

Canglong nodded. After chatting a while longer, she no longer disturbed her master's rest and respectfully took her leave.

Once outside the cave dwelling, the woman's heart was heavy.

Only when she saw the crouching red dog did her mood lighten a little.

"Big dog, now that you're back, you won't leave again, will you?"

The red dog did not answer. It merely cast a glance at Canglong before descending the Heavenly Mountain.

"She remembers yesterday's affairs, yet still hasn't realized she is in a dream."

...

"It's not time yet!"

In the cold, quiet new cave dwelling, Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes.

The Heartseverance Fairy asked anxiously, "My senior sister still can't wake up?"

Luo Chen cast several hand seals toward the ice coffin and the Dustbound Immortal Mirror suspended above it, then rose unhurriedly.

"The timing isn't right. Waking her now would do more harm than good."

"We need to wait a while longer!"

The Heartseverance Fairy's face fell with disappointment, but she thought that since she had endured so many years already, waiting a few more days wouldn't hurt.

As long as there was still hope for her senior sister!

Luo Chen looked at the cold woman, so prone to anxiety and hope, and recalled a joking remark once made inadvertently by a cultivator surnamed Fei from the Ailao Mountains, as well as what he had seen in the dream constructed by Canglong. He couldn't help but chuckle.

The Heartseverance Fairy was puzzled. "What is it?"

"Nothing. I just didn't expect you to be quite brave back then!"

"Huh!?" The Heartseverance Fairy was utterly confused.

"Gu Xiaorou, charging at a third-rank demon king while at the mid-Initiate stage. Truly, a woman of valor who yields not to men!"

With a laugh, Luo Chen strode out of the cave dwelling.

Only the Heartseverance Fairy remained, her icy face flushing crimson, breathtakingly beautiful.

...

Dan Mountain!

This was Luo Chen's current cultivation abode.

He had named it himself, partly in memory of the Alchemy Saint, and partly because the mountain had been scorched by the Nirvana Sacred Flame, leaving it a blazing red landscape.

The spirit vein hidden within the mountain had not been destroyed by the Nirvana Sacred Flame. On the contrary, it had attracted a vast amount of fire-attributed spiritual energy, making it an ideal cultivation site for a Fire Spirit Body like Luo Chen's.

However, other cultivators would find it unsuitable for cultivation here.

After all, the place was far too hot and dry.

Returning to his cave dwelling on Dan Mountain, Luo Chen breathed a sigh of relief.

Now that the Luo Tian Sect was settled, and although the time wasn't right for his old acquaintance Canglong, step by step, she would eventually awaken.

He could finally focus on his own cultivation.

But if he followed the normal cultivation process, at best he would only increase his mana and slightly improve his realm.

It wouldn't bring about an absolute, qualitative change in his current strength.

That was not what Luo Chen was pursuing.

"Han Zhan once said that with my talent for spells, after advancing to the Sage stage, there was a high probability I could comprehend the corresponding law intent ahead of time."

"But the misleading influence of the Withering-Bloom True Flame made me take a shortcut, and instead, I went in the opposite direction."

"If not for the misleading influence of the Withering-Bloom True Flame..."

"Rosycloud also said that with my Fire Spirit Body after nirvana, I could naturally comprehend the true intent of fire, and even advancing to the Ascendant stage wouldn't face major obstacles."

"Now that I have corrected my path, how am I supposed to comprehend the true intent of fire?"

For a moment, Luo Chen was trapped in frustration, unable to find a starting point.

Quiet cultivation?

Too slow.

Epiphany?

A rare, elusive opportunity.

Borrow the majestic, righteous Nirvana Sacred Flame to glimpse the mysteries?

Wouldn't that be repeating the same mistake as with the Withering-Bloom path!

Unable to find a way forward, Luo Chen had become the most troubled person among the five hundred disciples of the Luo Tian Sect.

Suddenly!

In the midst of his frantic pondering, he recalled the image of the Heartseverance Fairy, Gu Xiaorou, using an Ice Talisman in the dream he had just witnessed.

"The so-called true intent of law is nothing more than the essence of nature, manifested externally as the ability to control the spiritual energy of heaven and earth, and even the origin power of the world."

"The path of talismans is exactly that!"

"Can I enter the Dao through talismans, comprehending the true intent of law that is uniquely mine?"

At this thought, Luo Chen's heart began to pound violently.
