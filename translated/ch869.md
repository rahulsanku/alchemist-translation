# Chapter 869: Pill Sovereign, You Have Gone Too Far!

**2025-12-11**
**Author: Yu Qu Yu Xu**

## Chapter 869: Pill Sovereign, You Have Gone Too Far!

Peacock Isle!

A rather famous third-order spirit land within Fenghua Domain.

Not large, but quite exquisite and beautiful.

A Core Formation sect had established its sect here, also named Peacock Isle.

However, in recent decades, this sect had been forced to relocate to an extremely barren, ordinary spirit land.

The reason was simple: Peacock Isle had once been occupied by the Five Elements Divine Sect.

Only recently had the seven or eight hundred disciples of Peacock Isle managed to return.

"Finally, we are back!"

The Sect Master of Peacock Isle stood upon the mountain that seemed poised to take flight, his tone filled with bitterness.

First, their mountain gate had been seized by the Five Elements Divine Sect, then they had suffered the beast tide onslaught in Fenghua Domain. Peacock Isle, once a relatively strong sect, could now not even muster a thousand people.

The sole remaining Core Formation elder beside him offered comfort: "Sect Master, do not grieve. As long as we still exist, and the spirit land remains, there will always be a time for recovery."

Hearing this, the Peacock Isle Sect Master nodded heavily and forced himself to rally.

"Yes, we must not let our sect's prestige decline. We must revive it and make it flourish once more."

"I have three strategies to discuss with you, Elder. Please offer your advice."

"First, open the mountain gates wide and recruit a large number of mortals with spirit roots and rogue cultivators with clean backgrounds to replenish our sect's living strength."

"Second, make certain resource allocations within the sect. First, reduce the monthly stipends of some early Foundation Establishment cultivators, and concentrate the cultivation resources on seven late Foundation Establishment disciples like Xue Xiao, to help them form their Golden Cores as soon as possible. In this way, Peacock Isle can once again gather the strength of three Core Formation cultivators and return to the ranks of major sects."

"Third, at this time of rebuilding from ruins, several Nascent Soul Supremacies are attempting to wage pioneering wars. We can also participate and gain some long-term benefits."

Hearing these three strategies, the prudent and steady elder could not help but stroke his beard in approval.

The Sect Master's three strategies were indeed solid.

They neither wallowed in the past nor ignored the present, and even factored in the future distribution of benefits after the pioneering war.

With such a Sect Master, how could Peacock Isle's future not be bright?

However, there were still some details to consider.

"Most of the mortals in the frontlines have died. To recruit disciples, we must go to other, relatively safer domains further back. Such cross-domain recruitment of mortals can easily offend other sects; we must act carefully."

"As for training Core Formation cultivators, it is not advisable to cast a wide net. In my opinion, we should concentrate our efforts on three people: Xue Xiao, Lu Jun, and Shu Miaomiao."

"As for participating in the pioneering war..."

The elder fell into deep thought, voicing his concerns.

Currently, the sects clearly preparing to wage pioneering wars were the Five Elements Divine Sect, Motian Cliff, Luo Tian Sect, Nine Spirits Sect, and Divine Talisman Sect. Peacock Isle was too weak to diversify its investments; they needed to carefully choose one and place all their bets.

If successful, even if they couldn't obtain a spirit land, securing one or two shops would be a stable source of income.

But who to choose?

Just as the two were hesitating, sudden cries of alarm came from the distance.

They both instinctively looked up.

They saw a massive dark cloud slowly moving across the sky, heading towards Lingtian Pass.

It appeared slow, but in reality, it was incredibly fast!

In just a dazed moment, the dark cloud had already reached the skies above Peacock Isle.

"Is that a flying ship?" The Peacock Isle Sect Master's mouth fell open, his mind captivated.

The Core Formation elder gazed into the distance and happened to see hundreds of cultivators standing on one corner of the ship.

Among them, the men were handsome and valiant, the women slender and beautiful. They neither possessed the arrogance of a Supremacy nor displayed any signs of inferiority or timidity.

Each one had a resolute expression and a steady aura, as if they would not change their expressions even if Mount Tai collapsed before them.

Such bearing was completely different from that of various large and small sects.

Especially after the hundred-and-fifty-year human-demon war, cultivators were mostly somewhat fearful and uneasy, rarely showing such composure.

"Which sect's cultivators are these?"

The Peacock Isle Sect Master came to his senses and unconsciously uttered three words.

"Luo Tian Sect!"

The Core Formation elder suddenly understood. Although the Luo Tian Sect had few cultivators, their reputation had always been excellent. Now, seeing them in person, the rumors were indeed true.

With this kind of demeanor, even if their cultivation levels were not high, they were bound to achieve great things in the future.

As long as the Luo Tian Sect settled down and recuperated properly, they were sure to flourish!

"Are they starting their pioneering war now?"

"It should be. Look at the banners: the Rock Solid Gang, the Heavenly Wolf Society... even the Lie Feng Valley has joined in."

"Wasn't the Lie Feng Valley destroyed by the beast tide?"

"Even our Peacock Isle managed to preserve a shred of strength, let alone the Lie Feng Valley, which had a mountain gate to rely on."

"With so many Core Formation sects going, how can Peacock Isle be left behind? For this pioneering war, let's bet on the Luo Tian Sect!"

The Core Formation elder no longer hesitated. After discussing with the Sect Master, he personally selected elite cultivators and followed.

Peacock Isle's cultivator count was not large, only about a hundred people, trailing far behind the Heavenly River Void-Sailing Ship.

They were too weak; a Nascent Soul Supremacy would not look twice at them, so naturally, they could not board the ship.

But pioneering wars also had some unwritten rules.

As long as one participated and helped the Nascent Soul Supremacy, then after a domain was pacified, as long as the requests were not excessive, the other party would more or less grant them.

Therefore, in a pioneering war, although only a few sects formally participated, the number of cultivators often reached hundreds of thousands or even millions!

Among them, only a few were qualified to eat the meat; the others could more or less drink some soup.

The Luo Tian Sect's formation this time was massive!

It wasn't just the full support of Tiandu Sect and Fenghua Palace, but more so the personal appeal of Pill Sovereign.

After all, back then, the Medicine King Sect, with the Medicine Emperor at its helm, was already extremely prosperous. Now, Pill Sovereign surpassed the Medicine Emperor, and his future would only be brighter.

Wherever the Heavenly River Void-Sailing Ship passed, forces large and small, as well as powerful rogue cultivators, would occasionally take the initiative to follow.

Combined with the various forces the Luo Tian Sect had organized themselves, the numbers grew larger and larger.

By the time they reached Lingtian Pass, with no no-fly formations in effect, the Heavenly River Void-Sailing Ship sailed straight through, drawing gasps of astonishment from the city.

At the City Lord's Mansion.

The Lingtian City Lord paced to the eaves, gazing into the distance at the sky-covering treasure ship.

In his line of sight, thousands of sword lights soared upwards.

That was Lin Qingxuan leading the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect and the Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion cultivators.

Then, a fortress shrouded in yellow light slowly rose, flanking the Heavenly River Void-Sailing Ship alongside the forest of sword lights.

These should be the cultivators of Taishan Sect.

Under the leadership of their Supreme Elder, Deng Taiyue, they had not left Lingtian Pass but were quietly waiting for the Luo Tian Sect's arrival.

The Lingtian City Lord watched all this in silence.

Tiandu Sect, Fenghua Palace, Sword Sect, Taishan Sect... In such a short time, Luo Chen had managed to gather such a massive force.

And this was only a part of it; surely more major forces would join later.

"Truly a generation's hegemon!"

"If he is given enough time to cultivate connections, there will be few in the entire Eastern Wilderness who can stand against him."

"Fortunately, such a talent has already joined the ranks of our Nether Origin Sect!"

The Lingtian City Lord smiled slightly, both marveling at the Ancestor's decisive personal invitation and rejoicing that the Nether Origin Sect had acquired such a great talent.

With a thought, he sent a voice transmission.

Soon, his junior, Ye Hong, entered respectfully.

"Take my seal and inform Real Person Lin and Real Person Gu that they should not conflict with their neighbor, the Luo Tian Sect, in the future. Remember to maintain friendly relations."

...

After picking up the Sword Sect and Taishan Sect forces, the Heavenly River Void-Sailing Ship did not linger and slowly departed from Lingtian Pass.

After leaving the city, two more forces quickly caught up.

One had a large number of cultivators, around fifty to sixty thousand, riding on various colored lights, flying swords, and spirit boats. The main body was a magnificent seven-story treasure pavilion covered in various talismans.

This Seven Treasure Pavilion, though perhaps not as large as the Heavenly River Void-Sailing Ship, was three times more splendid.

This was the Divine Talisman Sect. Even facing the predicament of having no successor for the Nascent Soul generation, they were still wealthy and powerful, not falling behind in appearance.

The other force had fewer cultivators, but still over ten thousand.

Their main flying treasure was a large boat that seemed to be a modified relic of some demon beast.

The cultivators standing on it all had imposing auras, sharp and unmatched.

The Nine Spirits Sect had also arrived!

After the merger, two beams of light flew out and entered the Heavenly River Void-Sailing Ship.

Luo Chen, who was conversing with Fenghua Fairy, Deng Taiyue, and Lin Qingxuan, smiled slightly.

"Elder Fu and Nine Spirits Lord have also arrived!"

The doors opened wide, and two figures walked in.

"Both of you, please, take a seat!"

The two did not decline and each chose a chair to sit in.

As soon as they sat down, Nine Spirits Lord asked:

"Who else is missing?"

Luo Chen chuckled lightly: "Only Real Person Xianhe is missing. He should be almost done on his end!"

Speak of the devil, and he appears.

The six Nascent Soul Real Persons moved their divine senses and swept outwards.

In the distance, a large amount of spiritual energy fluctuations came.

Soon, they saw tens of thousands of cultivators, riding flying cranes, arriving gracefully.

Among them, an old Daoist, after instructing his disciples, drifted onto the treasure ship.

"Haha, I'm not late, am I!"

"Not late at all, Fellow Daoist, you've arrived right on time!" Luo Chen said with a smile, his heart completely settled.

Seven Nascent Soul Real Persons, representing seven Nascent Soul Supremacies.

Behind them was the righteous support of the Nether Origin Sect and the assistance of the Tiandu Sect.

The cultivators under their command were gathered, their numbers constantly increasing, already exceeding one hundred thousand.

This number, compared to the million cultivators from the Falling Cloud Sect's pioneering war in Xiaoyue Mountain Range back then, seemed somewhat meager.

But in reality, it wasn't calculated that way.

When the Falling Cloud Sect waged its pioneering war, the so-called million cultivators were ninety percent unremarkable rogue cultivators, mostly Qi Refinement cultivators.

But the current one hundred thousand cultivators of the Luo Tian Sect were all absolute elites preserved after the baptism of the human-demon war!

Each one had numerous skills and rich combat experience.

Let alone fighting one against many, under cross-realm suppression, one against a hundred was not an exaggeration.

If facing demon beasts...

Luo Chen chuckled to himself. In this battle, his enemy was not demon beasts!

Real Person Xianhe had just arrived. Seeing everyone chatting and laughing cheerfully, he asked curiously: "Everyone seems so confident. What's the situation on the Motian Domain side?"

Luo Chen replied: "Ling Fengzi has gone to investigate. General intelligence is being sent back. This time, those helping Motian Cliff are three sects: Seven Hollows Mountain, White Deer Cave, and the Five Elements Divine Sect."

Real Person Xianhe raised an eyebrow: "I didn't know Old Ghost Motian had such good connections!"

"Haha!" Deng Taiyue laughed heartily, "Fellow Daoist, you guessed right. Although nominally three sects are helping, Seven Hollows Mountain and White Deer Cave are actually there for the Five Elements Divine Sect."

Real Person Xianhe suddenly understood: "So, our real opponent in this battle is the Five Elements Divine Sect?"

Nine Spirits Lord snorted coldly: "With our seven sects joining forces, and Pill Sovereign backed by the Nether Origin Sect, I doubt they would dare to resist."

Fenghua Fairy shook her head: "That's not how it's said. Motian Domain is originally Motian Cliff's territory, and that Divine Sect's Supreme Elder is a late Nascent Soul Grand Cultivator. This trip will likely not go smoothly."

Upon hearing this, everyone's expressions varied.

Some only wanted to go through the motions, both currying favor with Luo Chen and avoiding losses to their own cultivators.

Like Lin Qingxuan, Fenghua Fairy, and Real Person Xianhe.

Some wanted a big battle to make Luo Chen remember their contributions more.

Like Deng Taiyue.

The remaining Nine Spirits Lord and Elder Fu also had to face the pressure of pioneering wars later, so they had conflicting mindsets in this battle.

They wanted to fight to deepen their relationship with the Luo Tian Sect, so the other party would help them more in their pioneering wars. But they also didn't want the battle to be too brutal, lest it affect their own subsequent pioneering efforts.

Luo Chen saw through everyone's thoughts.

His expression turned serious, and he said solemnly: "Everyone, rest assured. This trip will not involve a large-scale battle. After all, I have the support of the Nether Origin Holy Land. They must show some respect."

Everyone breathed a sigh of relief.

But Luo Chen's next sentence made their hearts rise again.

"However, to make a Grand Cultivator back down for no reason is certainly not an easy matter. So..."

Luo Chen's gaze swept over everyone, and he tapped the table heavily with his finger.

"There will be no large-scale battle, but a confrontation is inevitable. It will be limited to us Real Persons!"

"I already have a plan. When the time comes, just follow my orders."

Everyone exchanged glances, then nodded one after another, not refusing Luo Chen's face.

...

The Luo Tian Sect's grand army marched eastward.

Leaving Fenghua, passing Lingtian, crossing the utterly spiritless Badao Domain, and following the two regions of Jiuling and Taihu, they gradually approached Motian Cliff.

Wherever they passed, many cultivators were shocked by the changes in the natural environment.

Badao had no spirit, the Fire Ridge was scorching hot, and the Golden Wind of Bikong Domain was sweeping in all directions, showing signs of spreading.

The former sect homeland of Jiuling Domain was now a vast ocean, causing Nine Spirits Lord to look on in sorrow.

Taihu Domain was a barren land for ten thousand li, a shocking sight.

And all of this was due to the Ascendant-level battle.

Looking back at the road they had traveled, Deng Taiyue's heart was stirred.

"I regret not being able to enter the Ascendant stage!"

As he said this, his gaze frequently swept over Luo Chen.

The opportunity for him to enter the Ascendant stage was all pinned on Luo Chen.

He wondered when the Tihu Pill would be researched.

Luo Chen saw the hint but did not make any promises.

He had assigned the pill formula to a divided soul for constant research, but completing it was not a matter of a month or a year.

And at present, there were more important things waiting for him.

One hundred thousand troops, arriving at Motian!

Having already received the news, the Motian Cliff side gathered all their cultivators, blocking the way at the boundary marker between the two domains.

Old Ghost Motian, looking incredibly aged, stepped forward from the crowd.

Under the gaze of the masses, with the support of his magical power, he shouted loudly.

"Pill Sovereign, you have gone too far!"

(End of Chapter)
