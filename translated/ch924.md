# Chapter 924: Luo Chen Breaks Through, Fire Origin

The tribulation gu had vanished within his own lifebound treasure—that was no small matter. How could Luo Chen not change color?

Fortunately, when he had sealed this gu earlier, he had laid down many restrictions upon it.

Now, guided by his divine sense, Luo Chen eventually found its trace somewhere within the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

It was precisely the location where cracks had split open during the great battle in the Medicine King Domain all those years ago.

Those strands of cracks, after years of nurturing, had begun to gradually heal, but they always fell just a little short.

It was also because of this shortcoming that Luo Chen had been reluctant to fight with others in recent days.

After all, his lifebound treasure was flawed. If someone found its weakness and destroyed it, the backlash on his heart and mind—regardless of how many divided souls Luo Chen possessed—would be extremely severe.

At this moment, those shocking cracks had actually outlined the form of a fluttering butterfly!

Luo Chen was startled and uncertain. He touched the butterfly pattern with his finger.

He was certain—this was that fifth-rank tribulation gu!

Even, if he wished, he could still peel it out of his lifebound treasure.

But Luo Chen did not.

"Did my lifebound treasure sense that this gu could help repair its defect, and so actively absorb it?"

Luo Chen murmured.

He could feel that the current Primordial Unity Cauldron indeed no longer had any flaws.

But then another question arose!

The Primordial Unity Cauldron was not yet a spirit treasure that had birthed an artifact spirit. How could it have its own consciousness and actively absorb external objects?

Suddenly!

A scene from the Demon-Fall Land surfaced in Luo Chen's memory.

In the Pill Hall's Heaven-Refining Cauldron, a sealed gray light had escaped, once causing Moon Wanderer to fight over it.

In the end, that gray light was absorbed by the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Later, because of that gray light, the Primordial Unity Cauldron was able to absorb the most terrifying final round of lightning tribulation when Luo Chen crossed his Sage heavenly tribulation.

Now, actively absorbing the fifth-rank tribulation gu was likely also its doing.

"So, what exactly are you?"

Luo Chen spread his divine sense, searching the Primordial Unity Cauldron up and down, continuously probing.

In the end, nothing!

"Are you too powerful for me to find, or have you completely merged with my lifebound treasure, becoming omnipresent without any particular distinctiveness?"

Luo Chen could not find the answer.

The only thing he could be certain of was that he could still control the current Primordial Unity Cauldron as if it were an extension of his arm, and that gray light had no harmful effect on him.

On the contrary, in two consecutive autonomous actions, it had done things beneficial to him.

"The vast world holds too many mysteries and wonders. With my current realm, I am far from being able to perceive the essence of things."

"Only by comprehending the true meaning of laws, understanding the laws of nature, and the essence of the world can I have the foundation to explore these mysterious things."

"Let it be for now!"

Luo Chen sighed faintly, no longer troubling himself with unnecessary worries, and shifted his focus to comprehending the true meaning of the fire-attribute law.

...

The matter of Luo Lingxi and her son causing chaos among the sect's low-rank disciples did not stir up any waves.

Everything was carried out in secret, and ended in secret.

Sima Huiniang was punished with confinement. Luo Lingxi's cultivation was halted at the fourth layer of Qi Refinement. Zang Yuzhi and the other three did not understand what great event they had experienced.

They only knew that the Supreme Elder appreciated their diligent cultivation, so he specially called them to Dan Mountain for personal instruction and bestowed upon them a large amount of cultivation resources suitable for Qi Refinement cultivators.

After returning, under their promotion, the low-rank disciples of Luo Tian Sect actually became even more diligent in their cultivation.

In just a few short years since entering this Alchemy Saint Blessed Land, several people had successively broken through to the Initiate realm.

Even one Adept had been added.

Everything was developing in a positive direction. Luo Chen was the same.

After drawing the fourth-rank Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain talisman and pushing that technique to the Great Perfection level, creating similar fourth-rank fire-attribute talismans was no longer very difficult for Luo Chen.

What he needed was merely to push the Flame Dragon Descends spell to the Great Perfection level through repeated production processes.

Of course, there were differences.

Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain was a large-area attack similar to the Azure Yang Great Handprint, covering enemies indiscriminately, so its momentum was enormous.

But Flame Dragon Descends was not like that.

It emphasized locking onto the enemy with divine sense, able to track them even from a thousand li away.

The target was precise, with no escape!

Therefore, when drawing the talisman, the required soul power was also greater.

Every time he drew one, Luo Chen had to be extremely careful, allocating the proportions of mana and divine sense properly to avoid an unbalanced situation.

Time flowed slowly as Luo Chen focused entirely on talisman-making.

Just one year later, wind and clouds rose again above Dan Mountain!

Vast, hazy spiritual energy poured into Dan Mountain like a great river.

Luo Chen slowly closed his eyes, silently reciting in his heart: "It is time!"

His physical eyes closed, but his Sage's eyes had already opened. His vast divine sense spread out as before, extending into the depths of the void.

This time, no one disturbed him, and Luo Chen did not look back.

He finally arrived at that ocean of origin qi, like a mother's embrace!

Lost within it, yet guided by spiritual insight, Luo Chen gradually, from the ever-changing origin qi containing countless attributes, extracted what he sought like pulling silk from a cocoon.

It was an ordinary flame.

Far less complex than the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire, not as full of spirit as the Vermilion Bird Divine Fire, let alone comparable to the noble and radiant Nirvana Sacred Flame.

But it burned silently there, providing light and heat to the world.

It could be the scorching heat erupting when a star was destroyed, or the soul fire of living beings burning vigorously in the Nine Nether, or even the oil lamp lit by a mortal.

Master this origin, and one could command any flame in the world.

"So this is the Fire Origin."

Murmuring, a vast, overwhelming force swept in, wanting to annihilate Luo Chen's soul within it, to assimilate it.

Fortunately, with the spiritual insight on his person, Luo Chen retreated calmly.

Even before leaving, he rolled up a mass of extremely pure origin qi.

Boom!

His divine sense returned. Luo Chen opened his eyes.

Mana surged out from his body, transforming into streams of blazing flames in the spacious cave dwelling.

These flames were purely formed by activating his mana!

The Withering-Bloom Divine Fire and the Nirvana Sacred Flame, stimulated, fled from Luo Chen's Purple Mansion and flanked him on left and right, showing submission.

Luo Chen was not surprised. After all, these two divine fires were things he had refined himself.

With a thought, his divine sense spread out.

Above Dan Mountain, the volcano that had been dormant for many years suddenly trembled again.

Great torrents of magma flowed down.

Towering flames erupted from the crater.

Luo Chen slowly flew out of his cave dwelling, placing himself amidst them. Instead of feeling scorching heat, he found it exceptionally comfortable.

As if he were born a spirit of fire!

With a thought, he was like an emperor of fire, able to freely command the fire-attribute spiritual energy drifting in the world.

Not just command!

He could even convert spiritual energy of other attributes into the fire attribute.

Luo Chen gradually understood why Ascendant great powers could change the weather and fight freely anywhere.

Comprehending the true meaning of a law meant possessing the authority to control the corresponding law.

Origin qi was the aggregate of all spiritual energy. Using the authority of the law, one could convert it.

Like when the Black Marsh great power set foot on Green Parasol Mountain, with torrential rain.

Like when Li Canghai appeared on the battlefield of Ice Pole Sect, with wind and clouds transforming into countless formation patterns.

Or like when Rosycloud descended upon the Nether Origin Sacred Land, with everything in the world turning into a golden sea of fire, even the Nether Abyss boiling for her.

Luo Chen, limited by his own strength and realm, could not yet achieve that level.

But changing a corner of the world was no longer difficult.

Most importantly, immersed in it, Luo Chen gradually realized why Sage cultivators who had comprehended the true meaning of laws could cultivate at speeds far exceeding ordinary people!

"Converted attribute spiritual energy can also be absorbed and refined. Thus, one is not constrained by spiritual veins. As long as the quantity is sufficient, one can cultivate anywhere in the world."

"The efficiency of this, even if not comparable to directly refining world origin qi, greatly saves the troublesome process of filtering out corresponding attribute spiritual energy."

"Not only that..."

Thoughtfully, Luo Chen formed hand seals.

A fire phoenix soared to his left and right; a flame dragon descended from the sky, lowering its dragon head.

Dragon and phoenix circled each other, faintly possessing a terrifying power that could destroy everything.

Could the true meaning of the law also be integrated into spells?

Would that count as a divine ability?

...

The strange changes on Dan Mountain were extremely loud.

They immediately attracted the attention of many people throughout Luo Tian Sect.

Figures rushed toward Dan Mountain, worry in their eyes.

Sima Huiniang, under confinement, also received news from her disciple Yao Mingyue. Anxious, she wanted to break through Luo Chen's formation and go out to see if he was safe.

Wang Yuan, Lingfengzi, Bai Meiling, and even Black King—the four of them were the first to arrive at the scene.

But before they could approach, a voice came ethereally.

"It's nothing. I have merely gained some insights from cultivation."

"Withdraw!"

Everyone was astonished.

What kind of cultivation could cause such a huge commotion?

Earthfire erupting, magma spewing, wind and clouds gathering, dragon and phoenix circling—even standing more than ten li away from Dan Mountain, they all felt somewhat uncomfortable all over.

Lingfengzi felt it most deeply.

He tentatively released his Sage domain, only to find that he could not control the world's spiritual energy at all. Instead, he felt scorching hot all over.

This discovery shocked him greatly.

"Could it be that Senior Brother Luo has comprehended another true meaning of a law?"

Wang Yuan did not primarily practice the qi-refining path, so he did not understand the terror of it. He asked curiously from the side, "Does that mean Luo Chen has now comprehended two true meanings of laws?"

Lingfengzi's expression was dazed as he murmured, "For an ordinary person to comprehend one true meaning of a law at the Sage realm is already a peerless genius. Senior Brother Luo now possesses two true meanings—I'm afraid he is the only one in the world. Once he steps into the late Sage realm, the title of 'Number One Under Ascendant' will likely change hands again."

Number One Under Ascendant?

Hearing this title, Black King was unusually silent.

He had faced Azure Frost Demon Emperor directly and deeply knew how powerful she was.

Could his master, who had comprehended two true meanings, compare to her?

He did not know.

At this moment, what he was curious about was that the voice just now did not seem to come from his master on Dan Mountain.

"Which divided soul was that?"

At the same time.

On a beautiful snowy mountain named "Tianshan," Luo Chen's divided soul emerged from the Dustbound Immortal Mirror, gazing into the distance at Dan Mountain.

"The main body has finally broken through!"

"Once he digests this gain, his Dao conduct will surely advance greatly. The day we leave the Alchemy Saint Blessed Land is within reach."

"Next, it's just my side."

Murmuring, the divided soul slowly dissipated, once again entering the Dustbound Immortal Mirror.

...

"Daoist Canglong, shall we rest here tonight?"

The middle-aged man in blue robes suggested, but his eyes were fixed on that bright-eyed woman, like a snow lotus from the Heavenly Mountains.

Canglong nodded, not refusing the proposal.

But after entering the guest room, she was about to turn and close the door.

The blue-robed man blocked the door, hesitating, "Han has some cultivation doubts and would like to discuss them with you. Would that be convenient?"

Canglong smiled gently, but shook her head firmly: "It is late, Daoist. Please rest early! If you have any questions, we can talk tomorrow."

Then, she slowly closed the door.

Han Zhan was rebuffed, his face showing displeasure.

This was not what his master had told him before leaving the sect!

Hadn't she said that Icecastle had suffered heavy losses in the Lingkong Secret Realm and urgently needed to seek external allies?

Sending Fairy Canglong out with him to travel together through the Eastern Wilderness was also a great idea to promote their relationship, with the thought of forming a Dao companion.

In common terms, a marriage alliance!

Yet, along the way, passing through more than a dozen great domains, although Fairy Canglong was gentle, beneath that gentleness was a politeness that kept people at a distance.

"Can we really only be friends, not Dao companions?"

After his anger, Han Zhan was quite unwilling.

Among the women he had seen, there were many powerful ones, but few as mysterious as this one, inspiring infinite longing to explore.

Like a deep-sea treasure, like a Heavenly Mountain snow lotus—mysterious, pure, aloof, and self-contained.

Dejected, Han Zhan returned to his own room.

He did not let that disappointment affect him. Soon, he cast aside distracting thoughts and persisted in cultivation, relying on the not-very-rich spiritual energy of the inn.

In the middle of the night, there was a sudden knock on the door.

Han Zhan woke up, swept out his divine sense, and found Canglong.

His heart leaped with joy, thinking she had changed her mind. He immediately opened the door.

Canglong stood outside, looking lovely. Seeing Han Zhan, she said softly, "There's a good show to watch. Want to come?"

Han Zhan was startled, realizing he had misunderstood her intentions, but his expression did not show it. He nodded and followed.

The two figures leaped up and disappeared into the night.

Soon, they appeared in a patch of random graves.

Several figures surrounded a young man in a black robe in the middle.

They were saying things like "hand over the treasure."

Such a scene went without saying.

It was the most common robbery-for-treasure drama in the cultivation world.

So this was the good show Canglong had mentioned?

Han Zhan found it somewhat dull, but then he noticed something.

"Why did she discover it before me?"

"My Falling Cloud Sect specializes in soul refinement. I have already entered the gate of the Soul-Splitting Art. My soul foundation is deep enough to look down on those of the same rank, yet I cannot compare to her?"

While he was puzzled, the battle in the random graves had already erupted amidst failed negotiations.

The black-robed man fought several opponents alone, yet did not fall behind at all.

A long saber was wielded with perfect ease, each slash bringing a storm of blood.

Seeing that they could not take down the black-robed man, the encircling group quickly shouted:

"This one is tough! Form the grand formation!"

In the blink of an eye, the several people took positions, occupying various directions, their mana linking to form a grand formation.

The black-robed man grinned fiercely. "Ning thought Eastern Wilderness cultivators were different from elsewhere, but it turns out you are just as rude! Since that's the case, all of you can die together!"

As he spoke, layers of black qi surged from the Ning-named cultivator's body.

That black qi was overbearing and unmatched. With just a few impacts, it shattered the grand formation the group had formed.

He himself merged with his treasure saber, charging left and right, increasing the number of dead beneath his blade.

This battle was already over.

The one or two survivors fled in terror to the rear.

The Ning-named cultivator's killing intent was aroused, and he chased directly after them.

Just as he passed a treetop, his figure suddenly stopped. He turned and slashed down with his saber.

The overwhelming saber qi directly split the tree, which would take three or five people to encircle, in two.

A brilliant, snow-white sword light sprayed out, colliding fiercely with the saber qi.

Boom!

The Ning-named cultivator squinted, looking at the two figures who had revealed themselves.

"Are you two also after the treasure on my person?"

Canglong flicked her sleeve, dispersing the surrounding dust. "We are not mantises stalking the cicada, Daoist Ning. No need to make a big fuss."

The Ning-named cultivator raised an eyebrow. "Early Adept?"

"To be able to take one of my slashes at this level, state your name!"

Canglong cupped her hands. "Jade Cauldron Icecastle, Tianshan Canglong. May I ask your esteemed name, Daoist?"

The Ning-named cultivator frowned. "Jade Cauldron Icecastle? Never heard of it! I am Ning Yuzhuo of the Northern Sea Primordial Demon Sect. I have remembered your name. Until we meet again!"

With that, he vanished.

He was going to pursue and kill the two who had just escaped.

Han Zhan breathed a sigh of relief. "This man has quite the killing intent!"

Canglong shook her head. "Not just killing intent. The power on his body is quite formidable. I'm afraid it's the demon qi cultivated by the orthodox Primordial Demon lineage of the Primordial Demon Sect."

Han Zhan was surprised. "An orthodox disciple of the Primordial Demon Sect, how did he come to our Eastern Wilderness?"

Canglong did not know either. She only thought she had seen a good show tonight.

Just as the two were about to return, a vast voice suddenly rang out from the city.

"Demon scoundrel, how dare you be so arrogant!"

A golden alms bowl hovered in the sky.

Amidst the chanting of Buddha, the previously invincible Ning Yuzhuo of the Primordial Demon Sect was directly sucked inside.

Canglong stared blankly at this scene, feeling that this journey through the Eastern Wilderness could now end here.

"With mere Adept cultivation, roaming one continent may be fine, but thinking you can come and go as you please is far too arrogant."

"Daoist Han, let us part ways here!"
