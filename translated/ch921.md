# Chapter 921: The Way of Talisman-Making, Jambu Sacred Mountain

Having decided to enter the Dao through talismans, Luo Chen immediately threw himself into a flurry of activity.

He had some experience with talisman-making techniques.

During his time in Baili Qingchuan, bored and looking to enhance his divine sense while also crafting some life-saving items for his disciples, he had begun producing talismans in large quantities.

From first-rank, to second-rank, and finally to third-rank talismans—he could refine them all.

However, compared to the first and second-rank talismans he could produce with ease, drawing third-rank talismans was somewhat more difficult.

It wasn't that Luo Chen lacked the capability, but rather that the many battles that followed had prevented him from fully immersing himself in this path.

Now that he had free time, Luo Chen devoted himself entirely to it.

He was not short of raw materials for talisman-making.

Not to mention the stable exchange relationship between the Divine Talisman Sect and the Luo Tian Sect, the sheer volume of beast hides accumulated by the Luo Tian Sect over the century-long human-demon war was a vast quantity, more than enough for Luo Chen to squander.

Soon, Luo Chen had picked up his talisman-making skills again.

Unlike before, when he had refined all kinds of talismans, this time he focused solely on fire-attribute talismans.

He started with first-rank Fireball Art and second-rank Blazing Sun Art to warm up, then began mass-producing third-rank fire-type spell talismans.

His most proficient technique—the Azure Yang Great Handprint!

Then came techniques he had learned before but never paid much attention to: Explosive Flame Wave, Frenzied Serpent Dance, Sevenfold Blazing Heaven Rings...

Time passed bit by bit. With no one to disturb him, Luo Chen's progress was swift.

Within a year, he had achieved complete mastery over drawing third-rank talismans, encountering no difficulty at all.

This smooth progress was also related to Luo Chen's earlier understanding.

The so-called way of talisman-making tested one's raw materials, control over spells, and most importantly, the cultivator's mana and soul.

Luo Chen had no shortcomings in these areas; all that remained was improving his proficiency.

But in the second year, when he tried to inscribe fourth-rank fire-type spells into talismans, he hit a bottleneck.

No matter how careful he was, he would always fail just as success was within reach.

After repeated attempts, Luo Chen stopped his futile efforts.

"Where is the problem?"

"Whether it's my spell proficiency, the depth of my mana, or the strength of my soul, by all logic I meet every requirement."

"Could it be a problem with the raw materials?"

Luo Chen looked at the array of beast-hide talisman papers before him. The highest grade among them was made from third-rank demon king hides.

If there truly was a problem, it could only lie in this aspect.

Rather than working in isolation, Luo Chen immediately sent a divine sense transmission, summoning the Heartseverance Fairy.

...

"Greetings, Supreme Elder!"

Unlike her previous departure, when she had been both embarrassed and annoyed, the Heartseverance Fairy had now resumed her cold, indifferent demeanor.

Even her address for Luo Chen had changed from the earlier, more distant "Adept" to "Supreme Elder," the title commonly used by Luo Tian Sect disciples.

Luo Chen understood the core of this change. From the memories of Canglong, he knew that she was not inherently cold-natured. It was only after certain events that she had frozen her once-passionate heart.

He had no time to dwell on the Heartseverance Fairy's psychological state, and directly asked about the problem he was facing.

Within the entire Luo Tian Sect, aside from Luo Chen himself, no one had a higher talisman-making attainment than the Heartseverance Fairy.

Whether from what he had seen in his dreams or from the talisman techniques she had displayed in earlier years, she far surpassed ordinary talisman cultivators and could even rival some experts of the Divine Talisman Sect.

As expected!

After Luo Chen posed his question, the Heartseverance Fairy pondered for a moment before giving an answer.

"It should be as you suspect. The raw material grade of the talisman paper is too low, which is why you cannot successfully produce fourth-rank talismans."

After speaking, the Heartseverance Fairy looked at Luo Chen with great surprise. When had this Grandmaster of the Alchemical Dao become so skilled in the way of talismans?

She knew that Luo Chen could make talismans.

After all, in all her years in the Luo Tian Sect, even if she had never used them, she had seen low-ranking Luo Tian Sect disciples using talismans personally drawn by Luo Chen.

Especially Qu Lingjun's son, Qu Jiu, who was said to have received Luo Chen's guidance in talisman-making and had made rapid progress in this path.

But in the Heartseverance Fairy's eyes, while Luo Chen's talismans were certainly excellent, their rank was too low to be considered refined.

Now, however, he was casually bringing up the refinement of third and fourth-rank talismans.

Could there truly be geniuses in this world who were proficient in all the hundred arts of cultivation?

After receiving the answer and confirming it against his own speculation, Luo Chen determined that the root of the problem lay here.

"Does that mean fourth-rank talismans absolutely require fourth-rank talisman skin to be drawn?"

The Heartseverance Fairy nodded slightly. "The principle behind this is easy to understand. Supreme Elder, if you were to unleash a powerful fourth-rank spell, could a third-rank demon king withstand it?"

Luo Chen shook his head. That was naturally impossible.

Therefore, a powerful spell required equally powerful raw materials to bear and preserve it as a talisman that could be activated at any time.

Then, Luo Chen frowned.

"But our Luo Tian Sect currently has barely any fourth-rank talisman skins!"

Due to the century-long human-demon war, most talisman skins circulating on the market were made from beast hides.

No matter how clever one was, fourth-rank talisman skins had to be tanned from the hides of fourth-rank demon emperors.

But where could one find so many fourth-rank demon emperors falling, let alone being refined into talisman skins?

Every fourth-rank demon emperor was a formidable being.

When their raw materials fell into human cultivators' hands, they were either used as medicine or refined into powerful magical treasures.

Just like Luo Chen's Mystic Dust Armor and Frost Eagle Flying Boat.

Using them as talisman skins would be a terrible waste.

The Heartseverance Fairy reminded him, "Supreme Elder, the talisman skin used to make talismans is not limited in type by its nature. Do not forget this."

Luo Chen was startled. Had he been unable to see the forest for the trees?

The Heartseverance Fairy gave an example. "For instance, the Divine Talisman Sect's favorite talisman skins in earlier years were the bark of various precious spirit trees. As for me, my favorite raw material for making talismans is the inexhaustible hundred-year, thousand-year, or even ten-thousand-year ancient profound ice from my Icecastle. As far as I know, there are many materials that can be used as talisman skins—paper and silk, metal and iron, and of course, jade and stone. Legend has it that great talisman cultivators who entered the Dao through talismans could even use the sky and the earth as talisman skins, drawing terrifying talismans capable of destroying heaven and earth."

After speaking in one breath, the Heartseverance Fairy paused and added, "Demon beast talisman skins are merely the most convenient raw material to obtain in recent years, not the only option."

After listening quietly, Luo Chen said sincerely, "Fellow Daoist Gu, I have been taught."

The Heartseverance Fairy's expression turned somewhat unnatural, both because Luo Chen had abruptly called her by her surname and because he had addressed her as an equal "Fellow Daoist."

She coughed dryly and took out a pile of items from her storage pouch.

"These are my accumulated savings over the years, all fourth-rank talisman skins. I had originally planned to use them to draw new talismans after achieving the Sage stage. For now, I present them to the Supreme Elder!"

Luo Chen glanced over. There was ten-thousand-year profound ice exuding a chilling aura, as well as green wood blocks radiating rich vitality.

All excellent materials!

He did not refuse and accepted them generously.

"Thank you!"

The Heartseverance Fairy waved her hand. "It is my duty."

Luo Chen smiled faintly, noted this kindness in his heart, and then casually asked about the current situation of the Luo Tian Sect.

The Heartseverance Fairy told him everything she knew.

In truth, there was not much to say—just minor matters.

In the past year, the Luo Tian Sect had fully settled into the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land.

With its vast terrain and numerous spirit veins, there were many choices.

For long-term planning, Sect Leader Li Yingzhang had not occupied every fourth-rank spirit vein.

Aside from the Alchemy Mountain where Luo Chen resided, he had only selected one fourth-rank spirit vein with excellent feng shui as the Luo Tian Sect's mountain gate.

There, Lingfengzi had chosen a spirit mountain, opened a cave dwelling, and reestablished Misty Peak.

Although Wang Yuan did not care much about spiritual qi, he had still chosen a fourth-rank cave dwelling and established War God Peak.

Bai Meiling, on the other hand, was somewhat restless. After settling in, she had felt a strong urge to explore the Nether Abyss, but Li Yingzhang had vetoed the idea.

"I had forgotten. Little Bai is now a Ghost Emperor, more than capable of roaming the Nether Abyss."

Luo Chen chuckled, then asked curiously why Li Yingzhang had refused.

The Heartseverance Fairy replied, "First, the Sect Leader feared that Senior Bai might attract some powerful ghostly beings from the Nether Abyss to the Luo Tian Sect. Second, some restless low-ranking disciples could not contain themselves and wanted to follow along, attempting to explore the former Nether Origin Sect site in search of treasures."

Luo Chen was speechless. So that was the reason!

Then Li Yingzhang had not been wrong.

Although the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land was hidden and difficult for outsiders to enter, its entrance and exit were ultimately within the Nether Abyss.

If the door were blocked by others, there would be trouble.

After some casual conversation, the Heartseverance Fairy took her leave.

After a brief moment of thought, Luo Chen began attempting to refine fourth-rank talismans once more.

Using the batch of fourth-rank talisman skins left by the Heartseverance Fairy as raw materials, he tried to incorporate the fourth-rank fire-type spells he was most proficient in.

...

Half a year later.

A figure suddenly appeared outside the ruins of the Alchemy Saint Hall.

Dressed in black robes, with sword-like brows and bright eyes—it was Luo Chen!

In half a year, Luo Chen had exhausted the batch of fourth-rank talisman skins the Heartseverance Fairy had given him, and he had indeed succeeded in producing a few fourth-rank talismans.

But it was ultimately a drop in the bucket.

"My talisman-making skill is not a matter of talent, but purely driven by my own profound foundation and the layer-by-layer advancement of my proficiency panel. Therefore, I need a large quantity of raw materials."

"The Luo Tian Sect's reserves in this area are insufficient, but nearby lies a natural treasure ground!"

The Heartseverance Fairy's earlier words had reminded him.

The Nether Origin Sect, as the Eastern Wilderness's first and only Ascendant sacred ground, possessed a depth of foundation that ordinary people could not imagine.

Even though its mountain gate had been destroyed and its disciples had all fallen, many things had still been left behind.

The magical treasures and true artifacts Luo Chen had picked up while crossing the Nether Abyss back then were examples.

And besides these things, could there not be other items preserved?

High-grade talisman skins were the goal of this trip!

At the entrance, Luo Chen waited for a short while before seeing a giant turtle part the water and swim slowly over.

Luo Chen leaped onto its back and said softly, "Let's go. To the former cultivation site of Sage Hanyang!"

Generally speaking, top-tier forces did not focus solely on a single path. They cultivated in various aspects to avoid being constrained at critical moments.

For example, the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect cultivated only the sword path, the Divine Talisman Sect focused solely on talisman-making, and the Medicine King Sect specialized only in alchemy and medicine. Thus, none of these could be considered top-tier forces.

In contrast, the Five Elements Divine Sect had developed in all directions in earlier years, which was why it had become the strongest force in the Extreme East Six Domains and could even be considered a major power throughout the Eastern Wilderness.

The Nether Origin Sacred Ground naturally surpassed even the Five Elements Divine Sect!

In alchemy, there was the Chu family represented by the Alchemy Saint. In beast taming, there was the Lu family. In personnel and mundane affairs, the Ye family was outstanding. And in formations, every family had some involvement.

Among these, the great cultivator Sage Hanyang, whom Luo Chen had met once, was the Nether Origin Sect's most famous talisman expert.

His lineage—both Sage Hanyang himself and his disciples and grand-disciples—were all very skilled in talisman-making.

In earlier years, when Talisman Elder of the Divine Talisman Sect chatted idly, he had once praised this man.

This man had fallen at the hands of Rosycloud, falling like a star.

But his cultivation site might still hold some legacies.

He wondered if this Profound Turtle knew where Sage Hanyang had cultivated.

...

Traveling through the Nether Abyss was a very dangerous affair.

Fortunately, with the Profound Turtle's help, Luo Chen could avoid many unnecessary troubles.

As he had thought, the Profound Turtle did not clearly remember Sage Hanyang's cultivation site, so the initial journey was not smooth.

Fortunately, Luo Chen was clever. After searching a few places, he finally found a map that roughly recorded the topography of the Nether Origin Sacred Ground, and thus determined the direction.

Three months later, Luo Chen stopped at a mountain range where spiritual light was dim and filthy Nether water flowed everywhere.

"Is this Hanyang Mountain?"

If it were in the outside world, this would have been a cultivation site with rich spiritual qi.

But now, submerged by Nether water, it had nearly become a ghost realm.

Luo Chen sighed, then ordered the Profound Turtle to wait outside while he charged in alone.

This trip took a full two months.

Just as the Profound Turtle was drooping its eyelids, on the verge of falling asleep, Luo Chen emerged from within.

The Profound Turtle perked up and looked at Luo Chen curiously.

At this moment, Luo Chen's mana was greatly depleted, but the excitement on his face was unmistakable.

This exploration of the great cultivator's former residence had not been entirely smooth.

A large number of low-ranking ghostly beings had been dwelling inside, including several third-rank Ghost Kings!

Luo Chen could tell that most of them were ghostly beings transformed from fallen Nether Origin Sect disciples, and some of those who had been at higher realms in life had begun to gradually recover their intelligence, just like Pang Renxiong.

Naturally, they had resisted the intrusion of an outsider like Luo Chen.

Of course, in the face of Luo Chen's formidable strength, this resistance had not raised any waves.

He had spent some time clearing out the ghostly beings, then slowly searched, eventually finding several well-preserved secret vaults, from which he harvested a large quantity of mid and high-grade talisman skins.

"With this batch of talisman skins, it should be enough to support me in drawing a large number of fourth-rank talismans!"

After returning fully loaded, Luo Chen did not linger. He was not tempted by the broken magical treasures and materials on Hanyang Mountain, and directly urged the Profound Turtle to leave.

The return journey was much smoother.

With the map in hand, Luo Chen directed the way, and in just over a month, they were about to reach the entrance to the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land.

However, Luo Chen suddenly spoke up midway.

"Stop!"

The Profound Turtle was puzzled, then raised its head in confusion to look into the distance.

There, a great mountain stood quietly within the Nether Abyss.

The omnipresent Nether water was actually being pushed aside, unable to intrude even a single inch.

This mountain was so familiar that any Nether Origin Sect disciple would recognize it at a single glance.

It was none other than Jambu Mountain, which had hung high above the sacred ground for three thousand years!

And there was only one reason Luo Chen had taken the initiative to stop.

Inside, a familiar aura was engaged in fierce battle with an enemy.

Luo Chen frowned. "How did Little Bai still manage to come out?"
