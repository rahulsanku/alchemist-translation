# Chapter 695: Wanderer Moluo, the Battle of Two Bloods

One, two, three, four!

In the twentieth year after the Demon-Fall Land opened, a fourth palace among Bright Radiance Heaven's fourteen palaces quietly lit with radiance.

Why "quietly"?

Because the light of this palace, compared with the other three, was somewhat dim.

It was precisely the Dark Palace, corresponding from afar to the Light Palace.

If not for two people suddenly appearing upon the cloud-sea plaza, almost no one would have noticed that the Dark Palace had opened.

The two figures had only just steadied themselves when one of them attacked without the slightest hesitation, as though everyone around him simply did not exist.

A blood-colored ribbon condensed to the limit descended with a crashing, majestic saber aura.

Boom!

A violent explosion rang out, forcibly driving the other man several hundred zhang away.

Seeing the tall, bearded man who held the blood blade about to press his advantage, his opponent immediately shouted, "Blood Wanderer, are you truly determined that one of us must die? The Dark Realm inheritance has already been taken. Why must you and I fight to the death here?"

Beneath the cold saber light, the tall man's true appearance was revealed.

His expression was firm and unyielding, his beard tangled thickly across his jaw.

His blood robe streamed, domineering and wildly arrogant.

And the long saber in his hand, shaped like a miao saber, instantly let everyone nearby recognize his identity.

The Divine Astral Blood Blade!

Blood Wanderer!

One of the Northern Sea cultivation world's Three Great Wanderers!

Legends of this man spread through every corner of the Northern Sea. He was the sort of existence whose name alone could stop children from crying.

Only after learning the newcomer's identity did everyone understand why he could be so overpowering.

Blood Wanderer had that qualification!

As for the other man, although he likewise wore a blood-colored Daoist robe, his wan and withered momentum was far inferior by comparison.

At this moment, Blood Wanderer lifted his head toward the sky and looked at the palace pavilion slowly emerging in light.

Seeing his movement, Blood Nightmare Moluo finally released a breath.

"You and I have fought for more than ten years without deciding a victor. Now that the Dark Realm inheritance has already been taken, if we keep fighting like this, it will only be the snipe and clam struggling while the fisherman profits."

As he spoke, his gaze swept over the group behind Blood Wanderer.

At that.

"One of us must die?"

Blood Wanderer's gaze slowly withdrew, and mocking words spilled from his mouth.

"Without deciding a victor?"

With every word, Blood Nightmare Moluo's expression grew uglier.

Blood Wanderer gave a cold snort. "Borrowing the power left behind by your Primordial Demon Sect's Blood Sea Ancestor, you bitterly held on for a dozen years, and now you truly think yourself a figure equal to this one. How much longer can you support yourself from here?"

"You!"

Blood Nightmare Moluo's face flushed red as he said hatefully, "Are you not coveting that power all the same?"

Blood Wanderer nodded without concealing it in the least.

"Correct. This one wants that power, to bring my devil art to full completion and step into the realm of a Great Cultivator. Unlike you, who clearly possess heaven-flooding power yet cultivated yourself into something neither fish nor fowl, incomprehensible and absurd."

Without waiting for Blood Nightmare Moluo to refute him, his gaze swept behind him with chilling force.

"Besides, after I kill you, how could these petty wretches become the fisherman who profits?"

Under that aggressive, domineering stare, every person swept by it subconsciously retreated a step.

Those as weak as the Moya Cave Master were even more frightened, their minds filling with layered illusions of corpse mountains and blood seas.

"It is killing-blood baleful aura!"

A low shout startled the Moya Cave Master awake.

When he looked again, the Nascent Soul Sage shrouded in a black robe had already moved to shield him behind himself.

"Many thanks, Senior!"

Though he did not know why the other party would protect him, the Moya Cave Master still thanked him sincerely.

The black-robed Sage did not answer. At this moment, he looked as if he were facing a great enemy.

In midair, Blood Wanderer glanced at him and sneered, "Neither human nor ghost, a puppet body. You do have a few tricks."

The black-robed Sage nodded and led the Moya Cave Master several steps back.

"Blood Wanderer, please proceed!"

The implication was that he would not participate in their dispute.

At the same time, the rest also expressed their stances one after another.

Cape Heaven Matron was the most guarded of all.

In this place, all were human experts; she alone was of the demon race.

If everyone suddenly formed the thought to kill her first...

Under that concern, forget being the oriole behind the mantis stalking the cicada; the first thing she considered was preserving herself, so naturally she could not join the battle of the two "Bloods."

"If only Longzong and Liujun were here. How could I have fallen into such danger?"

Feiyunzi's eyes flickered, faintly showing a restless impulse.

His gaze drifted for a time between Blood Wanderer and Blood Nightmare Moluo, then landed for a time on the black-robed Sage, but in the end it still settled on the jeweled palaces high in the nine heavens.

He had not the slightest interest in these meaningless battles.

For a while, only two people in the field had not avoided Blood Wanderer's gaze.

"Looking at you two, it seems you do not know life from death?"

Fairy Jadecold's expression was cool. "My apologies. Before entering, the three of us made an agreement to advance and retreat together."

Old Daoist Jiyuan smiled bitterly. "Wanderer, why fight over a moment's temper? Why not stop and make peace? We can wait together for Bright Radiance Heaven's fourteen palaces to open, then seize benefits from those who obtained inheritances."

Facing these words, Blood Wanderer laughed savagely.

"Those things have nothing to do with me. I only want the power left behind by the Blood Sea Ancestor!"

"The three of you may come at once!"

"What has this Wanderer to fear?"

As he spoke, rich blood light surged from his body into the sky, and a human figure walked out from him.

That person's face was sinister, his eyes long and narrow at the corners.

His appearance was plainly entirely different from the tall, imposing Blood Wanderer, yet when the two stood one before the other, their overall aura was almost identical.

When this person appeared, Fairy Jadecold cried out softly, "Elder Yan!"

"No, that is not Elder Yan. That is a Blood God Child refined by Blood Wanderer!" Blood Nightmare Moluo shouted, revealing the newcomer's identity and quickly explaining, "Blood Wanderer refined four Sage-stage Blood God Children in total. One was destroyed by the devils of the Dark Realm; one Blood God Child perished together with my one hundred and eight Blood God Children of the Heavenly Net Earth Baleful Array; another died beneath my sword. Now only this one remains."

After learning this news, everyone on the cloud-sea plaza was shaken.

They were shocked by Blood Wanderer's strength: at mid-stage Sage, he had forcibly refined four Blood God Children of the same tier, no wonder he was said to rival a late-stage Great Cultivator.

They were also curious what methods Blood Nightmare Moluo had used to cut down two of them and still survive by luck until now.

No matter how one looked, Blood Nightmare Moluo only possessed an early-stage Sage realm.

Could what he relied upon be the "power left behind by the Blood Sea Ancestor" of which Blood Wanderer spoke?

Facing everyone's gazes, Blood Wanderer was utterly unafraid.

This time, he wasted no words and attacked directly.

Holding the Divine Astral Blood Blade, he charged straight for Blood Nightmare Moluo.

Behind him, the Blood God Child that seemed to have been transformed from the Primordial Demon Sect's Elder Yan alone blocked Fairy Jadecold and Old Daoist Jiyuan.

"Junior Sister, be careful. The Blood God Children refined by Blood Wanderer use the Primordial Demon Sect's Blood Sea lineage methods. They are the most mad and cruel." A horsetail whisk appeared in Old Daoist Jiyuan's hand, flying swords circling around him.

Fairy Jadecold's expression was calm.

"I know better than you. I do not need your reminder. You had best think about how to help Blood Nightmare. If he dies, when my elder sister comes out later, no one will help you."

The moment these words emerged, Old Daoist Jiyuan's expression changed.

From the current situation, Blood Nightmare Moluo's abilities did indeed exceed what they had expected.

He truly had obtained the power left behind by the sect ancestor and could directly face a mid-stage Sage expert. At his peak, perhaps he might even be able to rely on that power to challenge a Great Cultivator.

If Jiyuan wanted to survive, then he truly had to...

Taking a deep breath, Old Daoist Jiyuan urged, "Attack together first. Destroy this Blood God Child!"

"Good!"

Without hesitation, the two each summoned their magic treasures and killing moves.

On the plaza, the cloud sea churned, showing that the battle of two Bloods had erupted again.

But overall, Blood Wanderer held the overwhelming advantage!

The majestic saber light moved vertically and horizontally, unmatched, pressing Blood Nightmare Moluo down from beginning to end.

As he had said, the other party was merely borrowing the ancestor's power; in the end, it was not his own strength.

During their dozen years of fighting in the Dark Realm, Blood Wanderer had relied on his three Blood God Children to continuously wear down the other party's power, and now it had reached the point where he could take him down in a single battle.

Seeing himself about to fail, and with the cloud-sea plaza looking broad yet offering no place to hide from sight, Blood Nightmare Moluo began calling for help.

"Why are you still tangling over there? Come assist me at once!"

Old Daoist Jiyuan gritted his teeth. "Junior Sister, stop the Blood God Child. I will go help him!"

After speaking, he ignored Fairy Jadecold's attitude, swept his horsetail whisk, temporarily trapping the Blood God Child in dancing white threads across the sky, then rushed like wind and lightning to support Blood Nightmare Moluo.

Seeing this scene, Fairy Jadecold bit her lip, pain flickering in her eyes.

Again, this way!

The people of Penglai Immortal Sect were always like this.

It had been so back then, and it was still so now.

Boom!

After a brief clash, Blood Nightmare Moluo spat fresh blood and flew backward.

Blood Wanderer was just about to pursue when Old Daoist Jiyuan intercepted him halfway.

Kiss!

Blood Nightmare Moluo landed on the ground, his expression ferocious.

"Jiyuan, buy me a little time while I communicate with the sword spirit!"

As the words fell, a blood sword appeared in his hand.

It was precisely the God-Slaying Blood Sword!

But the "communication" Blood Nightmare Moluo spoke of was not so simple.

He pressed a finger to his brow.

A drop of blood, alternating gold and red, slowly flew out.

Essence blood?

No!

This was soul-blood stained with a cultivator's divine soul!

As the soul-blood drifted out, Blood Nightmare Moluo's face paled by several shades, yet he could not spare any concern for it. Holding the soul-blood in one hand, he wiped it across the God-Slaying Blood Sword.

Afterward, in an instant, he formed several hundred seals in succession.

At last, he came to a sudden halt.

"Obey!"

With that one word, the trembling God-Slaying Blood Sword immediately became docile.

It was also at this time that Old Daoist Jiyuan, who had been blocking Blood Wanderer, was blasted directly to a spot not far from Blood Nightmare Moluo.

High in the sky, the tall man stood with loose hair, looking down at them from above.

"A dying struggle, stubborn resistance in a corner!"

"Is that so?" Blood Nightmare Moluo's expression was savage. He crooked a finger. "Go!"

The God-Slaying Blood Sword became a streak of blood light and raced away.

Facing this sword, Blood Wanderer's expression grew a little solemn, but his eyes still held confidence.

The long saber in his hand chopped down vertically.

The saber light swept out like a ribbon, contending constantly with the God-Slaying Blood Sword.

Clang!

Clang!

Clang!

In a little over ten breaths, saber and sword had crossed more than a hundred times.

When the two each withdrew for the second time, Blood Wanderer lowered his head to look at the long saber before him. Dense chips had appeared along its edge.

The true artifact Divine Astral Blood Blade, famed throughout the Northern Sea, had suffered a complete defeat in this clash!

"That sword again?"

He murmured, and his right hand grasped empty air.

Snap!

Suddenly, a long whip cracked through the void.

As the long whip danced, it was like a snake and a python, like a vine and a dragon.

Faintly, there were even soul-seizing crow cries resounding through the sky.

"The Blood-Kiss Whip was originally a true artifact I refined to seize the Blood Sea Ancestor's power. This time, let me see whether it can restrain that sword."

Thoughts flickered through his mind, and Blood Wanderer lashed down.

Pa!

The void trembled. One whip became two shadows, striking toward the two men.

Facing this lash, Old Daoist Jiyuan's expression changed drastically.

The white threads of his horsetail whisk transformed, enclosing him in a great cocoon.

Pa!

One lash!

With only one lash, the great white cocoon was whipped several hundred zhang away, then burst apart in midair. As threads drifted everywhere, Old Daoist Jiyuan spat blood, his eyes losing focus.

On the other side, Blood Nightmare Moluo exhausted all his strength to control the God-Slaying Blood Sword, cutting toward the long whip's true body.

Boom!

Whip and blood sword met, mana surging and rolling up boundless wind and cloud.

"Haha, your true artifact has been sacrificially refined for too short a time. In the end, it can do nothing to me."

Blood Nightmare Moluo's loud laughter rang out.

Blood Wanderer's face sank slightly. Gripping the whip handle, he snorted coldly, "It is merely a matter of spending a little more time."

Time.

Yes, to everyone, time was the most important thing.

Just as it was for Luo Chen.

...

Inside the Pill Palace.

At this moment, Luo Chen had fallen into the greatest life-and-death crisis he had ever faced!

Two extremely high-grade source flames were using his body as a battlefield, erupting into an extreme battle.

After a struggle of more than a thousand years, both had long since exhausted nearly all of their strength, leaving only a tiny bit of source power.

But their grades were simply too high!

Even if only a tiny bit of power remained, it was absolutely not something Luo Chen could resist.

After the Heaven-Refining Cauldron's artifact spirit passed away, there was no longer any existence that could suppress them.

And because the mana cultivated by the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture shared the same lineage as the Nirvana Sacred Fire, the Nirvana Sacred Fire that had lain dormant for many years once more began the war.

Against all of this, Luo Chen was helpless.

He could only watch with open eyes as the two great flames fought wildly inside his body, frantically ravaging his flesh.

"No!"

"I will never allow it!"

A hoarse, exhausted roar surged through Luo Chen's divine soul.

Even though his mana had already been mobilized by the Nirvana Sacred Fire, he still had another power.

Source power!

As Myriad Path Convergence began to revolve, streams of source power rose from every part of his body and wrapped his body completely.

This "battlefield" absolutely could not collapse!

In the brief battle, the Nine-Refined Beacon Fire gradually began to fall into disadvantage.

In the end, it was a fire without a source.

The Nirvana Sacred Fire, however, had Luo Chen's mana to assist it.

At this time, the situation had already reversed. It was no longer the Nine-Refined Beacon Fire that held the upper hand.

Sensing this, the Nine-Refined Beacon Fire could no longer restrain itself.

When it again drilled into the qi sea, it actually charged with absolute resolve toward the lone white pill drifting there.

The second Origin Pill!

"No!!!"

Perceiving all of this, Luo Chen's expression filled with incomparable terror.

In the next moment, the white pill was enveloped by nine-colored brilliance, beginning to swell and grow. Its mana contracted and expanded...

Then.

Boom!

The second Origin Pill exploded!

Terrifying mana, like a river bursting its banks, poured out in an instant.

At that moment, Luo Chen's resilient qi sea, of which he had once been so proud, instantly shattered!

Boiling mana like surging tides, like wild horses loosed from their reins, swept from the collapsed qi sea as its center and washed toward every part of Luo Chen's body.

His flesh began to disintegrate!

*(End of Chapter)*
