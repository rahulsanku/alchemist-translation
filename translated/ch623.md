# Chapter 623: Water and Fire Merge, the Two Modes Generate Each Other, Peng Lake Trembles

Luo Chen treated the two demons, Black King and Heavenjade, differently.

One was left to range freely, while the other was kept at his side.

This difference in treatment was not merely because their forms were different.

It was also because their cultivation paths had already become completely different back when Heavenjade transformed years ago.

Over these years, Heavenjade's cultivation progress was slower than Black King's. Even though she could receive Luo Chen's guidance from time to time, when it came down to her realm, she had only just barely reached Golden Core Stage Four.

But Heavenjade had not the slightest dissatisfaction.

She knew very clearly that Black King's advancement was so fast firstly because he had awakened a special bloodline talent, and secondly because he had devoured too many powerful demon beasts in the earlier years. In particular, after digesting that Cliff Serpent corpse, Black King had rushed in one breath to the peak of the third-tier middle period. As for her own cultivation speed, after obtaining the Huan Yu Secret Canon tailor-made for her by Master, it was already considered very fast.

It was easy to imagine that through long-term cultivation of this secret canon, her cultivation speed would become faster and faster. She would no longer be like demon beasts, who often needed hundreds or thousands of years to improve by one major realm, but would instead be comparable to human immortal cultivators.

Black King was the same; he did not envy Heavenjade for being able to remain before Master and receive guidance at any time.

He had his own cultivation method.

Even if it was comparatively rough and primitive, he subconsciously felt that this path was the most suitable one for him.

In ordinary times, Master left him to roam freely.

But once Master sought him out, there was bound to be some matter to instruct him on.

At this moment, Luo Chen said indifferently, "It is nothing major. Later, just cooperate with me."

After saying this, his gaze fell on the girl on Black King's back.

Bright eyes and white teeth, brimming with spiritual qi.

Because of that water-green clothing, her skin appeared as white as frost and snow.

"She is... Zhang Jingqing?"

After a little thought, Luo Chen found a vague impression in his memories.

"Little Qing, quickly salute Master!" Black King urged.

Zhang Jingqing stood up and bowed respectfully. "Little Qing greets my lord!"

The Zhang family of Cailian Island was Luo Chen's vassal. When those within it saw Luo Chen, even if they did not call him Master like Black King and Heavenjade did, they had to call him "my lord."

After she finished saluting, Black King said in his deep, muffled voice, "Master, what do you think of Little Qing?"

Luo Chen quietly sized up Zhang Jingqing, this girl whose very name had been chosen to curry favor with him.

She stood gracefully, delicate and pitiful.

From her, Luo Chen sensed the passage of time.

Fourteen years old!

This also meant that he had already been in the Myriad Immortals Assembly for a full fourteen years.

These fourteen years had been the fastest stretch of his life, so fast that almost nothing major seemed to have happened in his memory, so fast that an infant who had once wailed at birth had, in the blink of an eye, inadvertently become a Qi Refinement cultivator.

In his divine sense perception, the young girl's mid Qi Refinement spiritual power fluctuations were very well concealed, yet they remained as conspicuous as fireflies in the dark night.

This concealment method seemed to be one he had taught Black King and Heavenjade.

It was the final essence Luo Chen had extracted after removing the dross from the many qi-concealing methods he had learned in his life, and its effect was extremely good.

Even if Little Qing had learned only the surface, she could already hide from Foundation Establishment cultivators.

For Black King to teach Little Qing such a method, it seemed he truly doted on this child from the bottom of his heart.

And now, he was asking Luo Chen for his opinion of Zhang Jingqing?

Luo Chen smiled faintly. "Quite good."

The girl's originally nervous face revealed a delighted smile. Azure Yang, whom even her father and the others treated with extreme caution, had praised her as quite good.

Yet when Black King heard it, he sighed regretfully.

"Heavenjade, take Little Qing to the side. There will be some commotion later; protect her a little."

After Luo Chen gave the instruction, he walked toward the depths of Peng Lake.

Black King followed closely behind him. Along the way, he could not help saying, "That child has quite good aptitude. Dual water and wood spirit roots, and she is especially clever from birth. She is a good seedling for cultivation."

Luo Chen said indifferently, "I have no intention of taking disciples."

"But Xu Muxian of the Swallow Islands?"

"Only a nominal disciple. She cannot truly be considered a disciple. Besides, clever?"

Luo Chen shook his head slightly. To possess such a delicate, clever mind at such a young age was not a good thing.

The saying that extreme cleverness could wound oneself did have a certain logic.

Of course, it was not that being too clever was bad. When one grew older and one's mind matured, cleverness was definitely a good thing. But to be overly precocious while young, to see through worldly human affairs too early and have too many distracting thoughts in the heart, was an enormous hidden danger for laying the foundation during Qi Refinement.

Zhang Jingqing's change was probably related to the Zhang family.

From when Luo Chen had first met him, Zhang Jiadi had been an outwardly fierce but inwardly weak man with a soft character. Back then, it had been Wen Xiu, that fallen clan cultivator, who had spoken freely before Luo Chen and stabilized their relationship with him.

Later, Zhang Jiadi gradually exposed certain habits of an opportunist.

He gave his daughter a name meant to please Luo Chen, never offended anyone when dealing with outsiders, and when he learned there was a chance to attach himself to the Coral Sea Zhou clan, he immediately ingratiated himself without hesitation. Because of this, he married Zhou Shanshan, neglected Wen Xiu, and even gradually distanced himself from the daughter he had once doted on most.

Luo Chen usually said nothing about all this, but in his heart he understood it perfectly.

Because he had once been such a person too.

Only, beneath his opportunistic methods, he still had things he would do and things he would not do.

Back then, when Mi Shuhua had used Sikong Shoujia to secretly convey an intention of marriage alliance to him, Luo Chen had refused without hesitation.

Because he knew very clearly that the so-called marriage alliance, the so-called Dao companion relationship, could not completely bind a person to a force, but it was a statement of allegiance!

Once that statement was made, every future action would be constrained everywhere.

Now Zhang Jiadi was openly Luo Chen's vassal, yet he also had the additional identity of son-in-law to the Coral Sea Zhou clan.

If Luo Chen and the Zhou clan had aligned interests for now, all was well. But if conflict arose later, how should the Zhang family conduct itself?

Luo Chen naturally did not care about the small movements of those under him. In any case, there were still places where Zhang Jiadi was useful now, and through Zhang Jiadi's hand, he could fleece some benefits from the Coral Sea Zhou clan.

If Zhang Jiadi truly developed divided loyalties in the future, Luo Chen could simply use thunderous methods and carry out replacement and enthronement.

From this point of view, Wen Xiu and her daughter gradually moving away from the Zhang family's upper core was instead a good thing.

"Heh, I am thinking somewhat far ahead."

Luo Chen chuckled softly, and his steps stopped in a stretch of water blocked off by islands.

The place was not large, altogether only a little over a hundred li.

"Master, what should I do?"

"Very simple. Use your innate demon art to gather the surrounding water vapor and become my formation eye!"

Black King was somewhat muddled.

But under Luo Chen's direction, he still meticulously began using that so-called "innate demon art."

As he opened his bloody maw, demon qi surged madly out from within his body.

The water vapor within several dozen li began frantically converging toward him.

Luo Chen looked at this scene, sensing that increasingly powerful momentum, and a flash of amazement appeared in his eyes.

This jiaolong's devouring demon art was truly somewhat formidable.

If he were allowed to stack it without restraint, its area of effect would likely spread beyond a hundred li.

This was probably also why mid Golden Core cultivators seemed somewhat unable to withstand a single blow before Black King!

"Master, I am almost at my limit."

As Luo Chen quietly waited, Black King gradually began to strain.

And this stretch of water had long since become a storm of raging waves and roaring winds, as if the apocalypse had arrived.

Luo Chen nodded faintly, then drew in a deep breath. His Daoist robe billowed without wind.

With one hand, he raised his palm and pushed horizontally. A tyrannical, unmatched wave of fire-attribute mana surged out wildly, its intensity roughly equal to what Black King was currently releasing.

Water and fire converged together in an instant, and a terrifying collision immediately erupted.

Unmatched force exploded again and again in the void.

Black King's demon power was inferior to the Heavenly Phoenix mana Luo Chen had painstakingly cultivated for many years.

But through the previous stacking, it had already reached its most powerful moment, and only then could it barely match Luo Chen. This was still while Luo Chen intentionally restrained himself.

Now, the two energies of water and fire began colliding frantically, and destructive power constantly scattered in all directions.

Some islands were annihilated silently.

Some sprays of water bloomed to their extreme before evaporating.

Even Black King's eyes revealed fear. He faintly felt that he could no longer control his own power.

"Master, what did Old Black do wrong? Are you going to kill me?"

Luo Chen said nothing. He merely watched the place where water and fire collided.

Suddenly.

With his empty right hand, he mobilized another stream of mana and drew repeatedly in the void.

Wisps of formation patterns began appearing between heaven and earth.

Not only that, in that critical instant, he followed the mind-link between master and spirit pet to regulate Black King's uncontrolled demon power.

As he regulated it, the collision between water and fire slowly began to ease, replaced by a sense of fusion.

Luo Chen seized that sudden sense of fusion, and his formation-pattern drawing became increasingly urgent.

Just as Black King's strength was failing, Luo Chen's eyes suddenly brightened.

"Yes, exactly like this!"

In the next moment, he pushed horizontally with both palms and completely fused water and fire together.

A yin-yang fish, half black and half red, appeared in the void.

The instant the yin-yang fish appeared, an invisible energy fluctuation spread from Black King and Luo Chen, covering a hundred li in the blink of an eye and even continuing to expand outward.

One hundred li, two hundred li, three hundred li...

Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake trembled all at once at this moment.

The spiritual qi of heaven and earth became incredibly violent. Boundless lake water boiled and churned, and terrifying water vapor surged wildly toward Black King.

There was also fire-attribute spiritual qi drifting between heaven and earth gathering toward Luo Chen, but its quantity was too small.

But Luo Chen did not care in the short term. His mana was still above Black King's. Even though Black King had gained the support of geographical advantage, Luo Chen could still continue releasing mana to maintain this formation.

"So this is the Two Modes Array!"

Luo Chen's eyes became brighter and brighter, and his understanding of the Two Modes Array grew deeper and deeper.

Inside the attribute panel, the Two Modes Array stuck at the Perfect level also broke through silently at this moment, reaching the Grandmaster level.

As one of the two great formation eyes of this formation, Black King naturally also sensed the change in heaven and earth.

When that formation was born, he suddenly felt that he could do it again!

His originally exhausted state, after gaining the support of external spiritual qi, seemed endless.

Only, that power was too terrifying, and for a time his heart became uneasy.

He both hoped Master would quickly stop the formation, and hoped it would continue being maintained.

At this moment, Luo Chen's eyes had already widened to the extreme, frantically capturing the changes between heaven and earth.

Because water and fire had fused, an even more extreme power had been born, and this power caused the gathering of the two kinds of spiritual qi, water and fire, between heaven and earth.

It was because of the formation!

It was also because of that higher-level power!

And this gathering of specific spiritual qi formed the formation, and also formed a special field.

That feeling was like...

"A Sage domain!"

The instant this inspired thought flashed through his mind, the Two Modes Array could no longer be maintained.

With a boom.

Black King flew backward several li and smashed into an uninhabited small island, snapping one of its mountain peaks in half.

Luo Chen ignored everything else. He merely stood in the sky, eyes closed, savoring that special experience from before.

Originally, tonight, he had only sought out Black King to practice the water-fire fusion of the Two Modes Array. He had not expected to gain additional insight.

He had captured that thread of inspiration.

From this, he began imagining continuously.

On the Quyang Sea Region, the black domain the white-robed cultivator had arranged with the Myriad Souls Banner.

On Tiangu Plain, the collision between Han Zhan's and Wolf Emperor Aoxiao's respective Sage domains, and how the former, when inferior, had forcefully resisted by relying on the Two Modes Five Elements Sword Array.

Gradually, a conception appeared in his mind.

"Can I use formations, treasures, and even other powers to forcefully arrange a domain similar to a Sage cultivator's while still at Golden Core?"

...

On the way back.

Looking at Black King's body, covered in wounds, Luo Chen felt somewhat apologetic.

"Sorry. When the formation went out of control at the end, it injured you."

Black King bared his mouth. "It's nothing. Old Black has rough skin and thick flesh. Just some external wounds."

Luo Chen smiled. "After we return, I still have some remaining third-tier Black Emperor Ointment. Take it and try it!"

Black King's eyes instantly brightened.

Black Emperor Ointment was Master's secret body-tempering medicine.

And for their demon race, even if they had taken the transformation path, tempering the physique was still of utmost importance.

If he obtained Black Emperor Ointment...

After wild joy, he suddenly cooled down again.

The Black Emperor Ointment Master had left should not be much, only one or two bottles. For his enormous body, that was nothing but a drop in the bucket.

Thinking of this, Black King hesitated a little.

"Master, can I not take the Black Emperor Ointment?"

"Oh? Then what do you want?"

"Can Master personally act and help me refine a magic treasure?"

"A magic treasure? Did I not grant you the two flying knives, Yin Slaughter and Yang Severance? Those are a matched pair of middle-grade magic treasures with extremely strong killing power."

"Uh, it is not for me to use myself. I want to ask Master to use this object as the raw material to refine a magic treasure and give it to that child, Little Qing."

Luo Chen looked at the skin roll Black King spat out from his mouth.

It seemed like a flesh membrane, and also like a sheet of leather silk.

From it, he sensed a dense Cliff Serpent aura.

"This is the skin membrane from the Cliff Serpent corpse that I did not digest. It is extremely tough. If it is used as the raw material, perhaps a decent defensive item can be obtained." Black King explained the object's origin, and rare softness appeared in his eyes. "That child, Little Qing, is somewhat pitiful."

"You, a snake-python who rolled around in muddy pools, pity a family young lady raised from childhood in silk and jade?"

Luo Chen gave a derisive laugh.

Black King was momentarily confused.

"Give it to me. She is still young, and her realm is not enough. We will speak of it after Foundation Establishment."

Luo Chen accepted the Cliff Serpent skin membrane, but no longer mentioned the matter of rewarding Black Emperor Ointment.

After the two left that stretch of water, Heavenjade hurriedly came forward with Zhang Jingqing.

"Congratulations, Master, on great accomplishment in formations!"

Luo Chen hummed in acknowledgment, his gaze sweeping across the low-hanging night.

The commotion just now seemed somewhat large.

He truly had not expected that with two figures comparable to late Golden Core cultivators serving as formation eyes, the Two Modes Array could erupt with such power.

But in this way, he was even more certain that if he used the Two Modes Array as the core, combined it with six third-tier defensive formations, and arranged it on the Demon Emperor shed husk, its defensive might would surely be terrifying beyond measure!

For a time, he even somewhat could not resist wanting to engrave the formations onto the treasure embryo on the spot.

But after the excitement passed, he still suppressed his restlessness.

"It has only been nourished for one year. If it were an ordinary Six Armors item, that would be enough, but for my body-protecting treasure, it is still far from enough."

"Nourish it more. Nourish it more!"

...

Cailian Island.

Where the Zhang family's clan grounds were located.

After more than ten years had passed, the Zhang family population had indeed flourished considerably.

Apart from dependent wandering cultivators, Zhang Jiadi had taken quite a few more concubines, adding no small number of people to his household.

However, the one he loved most in his heart was still the son Zhou Shanshan had borne him.

On this moonlit night of reunion, Zhang Jiadi was surrounded by charming women and encircled by sons and daughters, the scene incomparably harmonious.

When that terrifying spiritual qi fluctuation swept across Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake, he woke abruptly.

"What is this?"

A young woman holding a little boy also rose in alarm.

"This feeling is extremely like the Sage domain my Zhou clan ancestor unfolded when suppressing a third-tier perfect completion Great King Squid!"

As soon as these words emerged, husband and wife could not help looking at each other.

In today's Peng Lake, there was only one person capable of causing such a commotion!

A look of reverence appeared in Zhang Jiadi's eyes. After dismissing the concubines, he left only Zhou Shanshan behind.

"You have seen Azure Yang's power as well. This is merely the tip of the iceberg. As for your plan to help that clan elder brother of yours scheme for Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake, you should give it up as soon as possible!"

Zhou Shanshan was somewhat unwilling. "He is only a wandering cultivator..."

"So what if he is a wandering cultivator? Your Zhou clan ancestor still has to rely on him to refine defensive magic treasures for you. Do you still dare scheme against my lord?"

Zhou Shanshan was rendered speechless.

Zhang Jiadi shook his head. "Since you have entered my Zhang family, from now on you are a member of my Zhang family. My Zhang family is Azure Yang's vassal. In the future, you should be more respectful toward him!"

The woman acknowledged it in her heart, but her mouth remained unforgiving.

"If you are so capable, do not use my Zhou clan's spirit pills."

Zhang Jiadi chuckled and once more embraced the woman's plump body.

"For matters within one family, why distinguish between Zhang family and Zhou family? Madam, you are being too distant!"

...

And on the solitary island in Peng Lake, Han Zhan walked out of his cave residence.

Looking into the distance toward the direction where the spiritual qi disturbance had erupted earlier, approval appeared in the Sage little figure's eyes.

"The Two Modes Array can greatly raise the upper limit of power. My Two Modes Five Elements Sword Array is like this, and the Two Modes Six Armors Array Luo Chen will arrange in the future will also be like this."

"If I recover to my peak state, attacking with the sword array and defending with the armor array, perhaps I can climb to greater heights again!"

"For now, let Luo Chen explore the path for me first!"

*(End of Chapter)*
