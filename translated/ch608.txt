Chapter 608 – A Fine Show? A Farce!

A life-and-death struggle.

The four words sounded simple, but in reality, how many people would truly choose that path before reaching the point of absolute desperation?

Whether they were elite disciples of sects or cultivators from great clans, elders would always bestow all manner of defensive treasures and techniques before sending juniors out to travel the world. The purpose was simple: to prevent promising seedlings from ever being forced into such a situation.

Compared to them, rogue cultivators cherished their lives even more.

After all, sect cultivators and clan cultivators had mighty trees sheltering them. Rogue cultivators, on the other hand, had earned everything they possessed through painstaking effort and countless times the hardship.

Once a battle to the death began, the loser would lose everything. All their years of labor would vanish into smoke, perhaps even becoming another man’s spoils.

Huangfu Song was descended from the Great Zhou imperial clan and had enjoyed luxury since childhood.

Even after the upheaval that forced him into exile in the North Sea and reduced him to a rogue cultivator, he still had supporters like Elder Gui and Elder Ye. He even enjoyed the protection of a late-stage Golden Core powerhouse like the Purple Queen.

For him to risk his life over the Eight Hundred Li Penghu? He simply could not bear it.

Luo Chen was the exact opposite.

In his youth, he had pinched every spirit stone for the sake of a little cultivation resource.

Every battle he fought, unless it erupted suddenly, was meticulously planned in advance.

Even so, he rarely held anything back once he moved. He struck with everything he had.

Just like the battle at Little Huan Mountain years ago, when he destroyed the Duan Clan from the shadows with a fully perfected Great Fireball Technique.

Or the recent battle at Coldlight Island—facing multiple enemies at once, he had activated the Heavenly Roc Transformation without hesitation and displayed his full strength immediately.

A trump card only counted once it was used.

If you died before using it, then those trump cards simply became someone else’s.

Listening to Luo Chen’s threatening words and feeling his spiritual pressure rise ever higher, Huangfu Song released the vast pressure of a seventh-layer Golden Core cultivator.

Suppressing his anger with difficulty, he tried to persuade him.

“Qingyangzi, why must things escalate this far between us? I sent a formal invitation because I wished to resolve this dispute peacefully.”

Luo Chen sneered.

“Then why force your way into my dao field after being refused entry?”

Huangfu Song faltered before replying,

“The Eight Hundred Li Penghu is extremely important to me. But you are different. You only have a handful of attendants and spirit pets. Wouldn’t exchanging it for another third-rank low-grade spirit land suffice?”

“Heh. Your cultivation is clearly at the seventh layer of the Golden Core Realm. A mere low-grade spirit land should not help you much. Why insist on keeping it?”

Questions without answers.

Every word was a challenge.

Luo Chen’s attitude was excessively domineering.

At the same time, Huangfu Song was inwardly astonished.

He had already unleashed his spiritual pressure to the limit. Ordinarily, it should have completely suppressed anyone below the seventh Golden Core layer.

Yet Luo Chen’s fifth-layer Golden Core pressure met his head-on without yielding an inch.

In fact, it seemed to be pushing back.

Was the man truly only at the fifth layer?

Huangfu Song steadied himself. Though he tried to remain calm, irritation still seeped into his voice.

“What exactly will it take for you to surrender Penghu?”

Negotiate terms?

Mockery appeared on Luo Chen’s face.

Then he slowly shook his head.

“Not happening.”

The instant the words fell, his spiritual pressure surged to its peak.

The two waves of power collided in midair.

Huangfu Song’s body shook violently, and he stumbled for a moment.

Luo Chen, however, remained motionless as a mountain.

The six Golden Core cultivators behind Huangfu Song all turned pale.

They knew exactly what this meant.

Luo Chen’s magical power was purer and more profound than even Huangfu Song’s.

Even if only slightly stronger, stronger was stronger.

Huangfu Song’s expression darkened.

From the moment Luo Chen appeared, he had forced him to activate defensive measures with a single palm strike, refused to yield in verbal exchanges, and now even defeated him in a contest of spiritual pressure.

Everything had gone beyond his expectations.

Though Huangfu Song still believed he could defeat Luo Chen with his hidden cards in direct combat, the legendary Qingyang Devil Lord was likewise known for ruthless methods.

If both sides were forced to reveal their true trump cards, the price would be unbearable.

But now, beneath countless watching eyes, Huangfu Song was riding a tiger and unable to dismount.

Perhaps that technique would be suitable here.

He lowered his head slightly and formed a subtle hand seal behind his back.

Then he suddenly shouted,

“Qingyang Devil Lord! Under heaven, all land belongs to the king! Why cling so stubbornly to a mere Eight Hundred Li Penghu?”

Luo Chen frowned.

“Penghu may not be large, but I have managed it for years. If I were to hand it over, how could it possibly be done so easi—hm?”

His expression abruptly changed.

A layer of black radiance flickered across his body.

Inside him, the Rotten Ke Black Chess Piece trembled faintly, as though it had suffered an attack.

“A soul-shaking technique? Or perhaps one of the verbal curse arts rumored to belong to the Yuan Demon Sect?”

As Luo Chen considered this inwardly, Huangfu Song stared fixedly at him, unwilling to miss even the slightest change in expression.

When Luo Chen had spoken those words earlier, Huangfu Song had almost relaxed.

‘Not so easily’ meant there were terms to negotiate.

His imperial secret art had worked after all.

Without realizing it, Luo Chen’s will had begun to waver.

But the next instant, Luo Chen’s soft murmur made Huangfu Song realize something was wrong.

“So his magical power surpassing mine wasn’t enough—his soul foundation is also this terrifying? He broke free from my Imperial Sound so quickly.”

“No good. I need another few lines to break his resolve completely.”

Just as Huangfu Song opened his mouth to continue, his body suddenly froze.

He met Luo Chen’s gaze.

Deep as an abyss.

Cold as eternal ice.

In a daze, illusions emerged within his sea of consciousness.

“Illusions!”

A streak of purple light rose from his chest, shielding him from Luo Chen’s eyes.

Huangfu Song relaxed slightly.

Fortunately, the treasure left behind by his ancestor could resist all illusion arts beneath the Nascent Soul Realm.

Such shallow soul attacks were nothing more than tricks before that treasure.

Yet the illusion within his sea of consciousness abruptly transformed.

Towering celestial palaces appeared high in the heavens, immortal qi drifting through clouds.

Graceful heavenly maidens danced through the skies in flowing gauze.

Immortals in feathered robes laughed and conversed as they came and went.

But after descending from those lofty halls, every celestial maiden and immortal transformed into wailing ghosts and vengeful spirits that lunged toward him.

Looking carefully, those immortal palaces were not sacred realms at all.

They were ghostly towers and spirit mansions.

Buzz!

Purple light exploded brilliantly and flooded his sea of consciousness, forcibly dispersing the illusions.

Huangfu Song shuddered violently and snapped awake.

Within the illusion, it had seemed as though a very long time had passed.

In reality, only an instant had gone by.

Cold sweat drenched his back.

Subconsciously, he avoided Luo Chen’s eyes.

Luo Chen’s calm voice rang out beside his ear.

“Daoist Huangfu, please leave.”

Huangfu Song opened his mouth, yet no words emerged.

His Imperial Soul-Shaking Voice had failed to intimidate Luo Chen.

Instead, Luo Chen’s illusion arts had nearly shattered his mind.

If not for the treasure left behind by his Nascent Soul ancestor, Luo Chen could have killed him countless times in that single instant.

Fear arose uncontrollably.

Fear of the lingering ghostly visions.

And fear of the endless methods possessed by the Qingyang Devil Lord.

Almost instinctively, he turned around.

“Let’s go.”

“My lord, what are you saying?”

Huangfu Song froze.

Facing Elder Ye’s shocked question, he subconsciously shook his head.

“No… I…”

“Daoist Huangfu. The weather is poor today. I shall not see you off. Please leave.”

This time, Luo Chen’s voice turned even colder.

The killing intent within it had reached its limit.

Like a volcano on the verge of eruption.

An ugly smile appeared on Huangfu Song’s face.

“My apologies for the disturbance.”

Ignoring the dissatisfaction of Elder Gui and Elder Ye, he led his four Golden Core subordinates away.

As they departed, he continuously tried explaining himself.

Their side lacked unity.

The Qingyang Devil Lord was too troublesome.

Penghu was not that valuable.

There were better spirit lands within the Hidden Dragon Mountain Range.

The outsiders, ignorant of the brief exchange between him and Luo Chen, half-believed his excuses.

But the two old retainers who understood their young lord’s temperament inwardly sighed.

Sometimes, taking a step back did not lead to open skies.

It led to eternal damnation.

Had their lord truly understood the severity of the matter?

Once they reached the lakeshore, Zhang Jiadi returned the cloudship and the hundred or so Foundation Establishment cultivators trapped by the formation intact.

Then Huangfu Song’s group departed Penghu in disgrace.

The spectators felt indescribably disappointed.

Where was the great battle?

Where was the life-and-death clash?

The show had barely begun before it ended.

Meanwhile, Luo Chen coldly watched Huangfu Song’s group leave.

After they had gone far away, Tianxuan could not help asking,

“Master, those enemies were so disrespectful. Are we really letting them go?”

What she truly meant was that Luo Chen had always been decisive and ruthless.

How could he tolerate such provocation?

This did not fit her image of him.

Luo Chen merely raised his eyes.

“While cultivating within the Myriad Immortal Assembly, some people’s face still needs to be respected.”

He looked toward the clouds drifting across the sky, as though gazing at someone hidden beyond them.

Tianxuan quickly understood who he was wary of.

She also realized that although Luo Chen had acted domineering throughout the day, he had in fact been restraining himself the entire time.

Otherwise, with his usual methods, how could he have tolerated such provocation?

Still, she remained worried.

Though Luo Chen had forced Huangfu Song away in front of countless spectators and increased his prestige, offending a late-stage Golden Core cultivator and then letting him leave alive...

“What if they seek revenge later? Huangfu Song is still a major cultivator at the seventh Golden Core layer.”

“Heh.”

Luo Chen laughed mockingly.

Even Duanli, who had remained silent nearby, found it unbelievable.

Since when were major cultivators looked down upon like this?

Luo Chen waved a hand dismissively.

“No matter. Huangfu Song bears the title of a major cultivator, but he is merely an embroidered pillow.”

An embroidered pillow?

Duanli looked puzzled.

The man’s cultivation was unquestionably real.

That brief display of purple sword qi had nearly taken his life.

How could someone like that be called useless?

Facing their confusion, Luo Chen sighed softly.

“I only met him once, yet the man is far too shallow. One glance is enough to see through him.”

“He appears fierce yet lacks courage. He schemes much yet cannot decide. He cherishes his own life when undertaking great matters, yet risks everything for petty gains. How could such a person ever become a true threat to me?”

Regardless of whether the two understood his words, Luo Chen did not think this matter would become much trouble.

After all, his own actions in the Flying Swallow Archipelago served as the perfect comparison.

For the sake of the third-rank low-grade spirit vein on Moon Invitation Island, he had struck decisively before Yan Nantian could react.

At the time, his foundation had been unstable and his cultivation not necessarily stronger than Yan Nantian’s.

But Huangfu Song?

Despite possessing greater personal strength and more subordinates than Luo Chen, he still insisted on polite negotiations and schemes.

And after failing to outtalk or overpower him, he resorted to underhanded tricks.

If Luo Chen had not been wary of the Eight-Star Demon Hunter behind him, he would have killed Huangfu Song right there in Penghu.

As for Nascent Soul trump cards?

There truly was a Nascent Soul expert hidden within Penghu.

Han Zhan’s soul body had become increasingly stable over the years thanks to the Soul Nourishing Wood.

Surely his strength had recovered by at least thirty percent.

If pushed far enough, Han Zhan would definitely act on Luo Chen’s behalf.

“Enough.”

Luo Chen turned toward Duanli.

“Thank you for stepping in today, Daoist. I will remember this favor.”

Duanli hurriedly waved his hands.

“It’s hardly a favor. If anything, I only caused you trouble.”

He finally understood.

With Qingyangzi’s abilities, those enemies had never truly threatened him.

His own attempt to help had merely forced Luo Chen to reveal himself early, perhaps even disrupting other arrangements.

Luo Chen did not mind.

“I appreciate the sentiment. But now is not the time for idle conversation. I still have another guest to receive. Let us talk again in a few days.”

Duanli froze.

Another guest?

His gaze swept across the surrounding area.

Did he mean those rogue cultivators hoping to visit after hearing his reputation?

“The Old Fisherman.”

The divine sense transmission entered his ears, and Duanli immediately understood.

“Tianxuan, see Daoist Duanli out.”

“Yes, Master.”

After Tianxuan escorted the guest away, Luo Chen drifted back toward Penghu Island.

Moments later, a streak of light descended beside him.

“Today truly was a fine show,” the Old Fisherman laughed.

Luo Chen shook his head.

“Nothing more than a farce.”

The Old Fisherman twitched his lips.

A farce?

Not necessarily.

“I never expected the famous Qingyang Devil Lord to also be skilled in artifact forging.”

Luo Chen was unsurprised that the Old Fisherman had recognized the alias he used in the North Sea.

He had never hidden it.

In fact, he had deliberately publicized it.

After today’s events, those coveting Penghu would likely think twice.

At the very least, they would investigate the battle at Coldlight Island.

That battle record alone was terrifying enough.

Killing several cultivators of the same rank while fighting two late-stage Golden Core cultivators at once.

Even within the Myriad Immortal Assembly, very few could compare.

Perhaps only the three Eight-Star Demon Hunters and a handful of Seven-Star Demon Hunters.

“Artifact forging is merely a little hobby I practice in my spare time,” Luo Chen said modestly.

Then he asked meaningfully,

“And what was that person’s reaction?”

The Old Fisherman knew exactly whom he meant.

“She was dissatisfied, yet also satisfied.”

Luo Chen frowned.

The Old Fisherman laughed.

“Relax. The Purple Queen was dissatisfied with Huangfu Song’s temperament, but satisfied with how you handled the matter. Don’t overthink it. Though she has ties to Huangfu Song, decades of rogue cultivation have long diluted that relationship. Right now she only wishes to reach Golden Core perfection and break through to the Nascent Soul Realm. Why would she start a major conflict over the junior of an old acquaintance?”

The brief exchange contained a great deal of information.

Especially the last part, which finally eased Luo Chen’s concerns.

Cultivators at the late stages of each realm generally disliked conflict.

Unless absolutely necessary, they preferred secluded cultivation.

Luo Chen himself had done the same back in the late Foundation Establishment stage.

Aside from gathering resources and seeking revenge, he had spent most of his time cultivating quietly atop Tianlan Peak.

Compared to advancing from Foundation Establishment to Golden Core, breaking through from Golden Core to Nascent Soul required even greater caution.

Such people would not act lightly.

“So it seems my handling of today’s matter struck the proper balance,” Luo Chen said with a light laugh.

Then he cupped his hands toward the Old Fisherman.

“Of course, I must also thank you for your assistance.”

The Old Fisherman waved him off.

“Calling me ‘Senior’ feels too distant. We may address each other as fellow daoists.”

What was a fellow daoist?

Someone walking the same path.

The Old Fisherman’s meaning was obvious.

He was referring to Luo Chen’s guidance regarding body cultivation.

Luo Chen understood immediately and gestured invitingly.

“We already spoke of arranging a time to exchange pointers. Since chance has brought us together today, let’s do it now.”

The Old Fisherman smiled broadly.

“I couldn’t ask for more.”

The two exchanged smiles and stepped together into the palace atop Penghu Island.

End of Chapter 608
