# Chapter 1181: Divine Power Perfected, Unexpected Joy (Asking for Monthly Votes)

The Third Ring.

What was once Never-Return Island had now changed its name, called Departing Dust Island.

The meaning behind it was self-evident.

On the island, the scenery was beautiful.

Two men and one woman sat together discussing the Dao.

The content of their conversation was entirely about martial arts cultivation and martial arts philosophy.

"For us martial artists, upon cultivating to the Human Immortal realm, we give birth to martial divine intent. This will allows our originally ordinary natal astral qi to become stronger and gain dominance. Thus, we can refine and add some special external materials into it, and without being affected, make it more in line with the characteristics of our divine intent. From my observation, Luo Chen, your martial divine intent seems to have already passed this stage?"

"Indeed, what I, Luo, have learned was passed down by an old friend before my ascension. Because the cultivation environment was different back then, with abundant resources. Moreover, that old friend cultivated both Dharma and Body, so he very early conceived the idea of incorporating external objects to enhance natal astral qi, and even put it into practice."

"Tsk tsk tsk, that old friend of yours is a rare martial arts genius, to be able to think of the Human Immortal realm's cultivation method while at a low realm."

His tone paused slightly, and Gu Xun sighed. "If that's the case, then your Human Immortal realm cultivation can save a lot of trouble. You won't have to be like us, searching for suitable materials and then figuring out how to refine and fuse them."

Luo Chen smiled. He hadn't expected such a fortunate coincidence.

He had often marveled before that the *Divine Astral Fighting Qi* created by Wang Yuan was quite miraculous. The astral qi cultivated from it could even directly clash head-on with the dharma treasures and true artifacts painstakingly refined by cultivators.

Now it seemed that Wang Yuan had actually crossed several realms and, at the Furnace stage, had already begun part of the Human Immortal realm's cultivation.

Fang Qingxue, who was beside him, gazed at the martial deity Luo Chen had actively released, also clicking her tongue in wonder.

"Little Sister never expected that Fellow Daoist Luo, though only having just reached the Human Immortal realm, has a foundation that is not inferior to mine, who is at the mid-Human Immortal stage!"

"Oh, how so?"

As Luo Chen watched curiously, Fang Qingxue closed her eyes.

Then, a faint light bloomed from the Celestial Court acupoint between her brows, and a small figure, quite similar in appearance to her but with features reduced many times over, flew out.

That was her martial deity!

"Fellow Daoist, take a look. A martial deity like yours or mine is the hallmark of the mid-Human Immortal stage."

Luo Chen suddenly understood.

His cultivation progress was indeed different from ordinary martial artists!

Not only had he completed the fusion of external objects into his qi ahead of time, but he had also finished forming his martial deity when he broke through to the Human Immortal realm.

Fang Qingxue withdrew her own martial deity, then thoroughly laid out the complete cultivation process of orthodox martial arts for Luo Chen.

"The first stage of Body Tempering is to polish the physical body until the Vajra realm. Only then can the body bear the subsequent internal changes of blood and qi within the martial artist. Of course, while tempering blood and qi, the martial artist's physical body will also correspondingly strengthen. Some exceptionally talented individuals can even condense a Great Vajra realm physique in addition to blood and qi. My ancestor, who has already departed, was such a stunning figure."

"The second stage of Body Tempering goes without saying. Complete the transformation of blood and qi, then condense the core into astral qi, turning the body into a furnace. At this point, one can absorb and refine external energy, gathering all power into oneself. To prevent the leakage of power and also to prevent the external world from interfering with the martial artist, one must master the opening and closing of acupoints. When one perfectly controls the opening and closing of the physical body, that is the Leakless realm."

"The cultivation of the Leakless realm, on one hand, is about accumulating energy and strengthening one's own astral qi; on the other hand, it is about comprehending martial divine intent. This kind of thing is difficult to accomplish with the help of others. Consider my Fang family, with many descendants and numerous geniuses, and with the ancestor sitting in charge, yet over so many years, only I, a junior, have managed to comprehend martial divine intent."

Speaking of this, Fang Qingxue thought of one person and couldn't help but sigh with emotion.

"That fellow Zhuoshi was actually the one we were most optimistic about before. But what he sought was too great. He intended to directly cultivate the terrifying chaotic energy into chaotic astral qi. He even successfully took the first step, allowing his body to simply adapt to chaotic energy. But this kind of astral qi, while formidable, trying to comprehend a martial divine intent unique to him from such a complex energy combination—that was simply a pipe dream!"

Luo Chen also thought of that short, old man.

The first move he made, which was also his last, had actually broken through Luo Chen's Blood Cloth Robe and even penetrated his fifth-rank physical body with his own astral qi!

From this, one could see the ferocity of his chaotic astral qi.

The power of that move had certainly reached the level of a Human Immortal's strike, which was why it had stirred up the resistance of all the Blood-Refining Divine Astral Qi in Luo Chen's body, ultimately helping him successfully comprehend the "Selfless Divine Intent."

Luo Chen didn't know if the other party had gotten his wish before dying; he only felt regret for such a good opponent.

He nodded in agreement. "It is indeed a pity."

Fang Qingxue returned to the previous topic and continued, "The comprehension of martial divine intent is divided into three stages: the embryonic form, the seed, and the blossoming and bearing fruit. But as our trump card for martial artists to resist the soul attacks of cultivators, the upper limit of martial divine intent is not just this!"

"Upon reaching the Human Immortal realm, astral qi fuses with external objects, and divine intent further strengthens. The two combine to form the martial deity!"

Her gaze fell upon Luo Chen's martial deity, which he had not yet withdrawn.

"Fellow Daoist Luo, your technique and your previous cultivation process have already covered these two parts. That's why, upon first entering the Human Immortal realm, you can compare to the two of us. As for how to cultivate subsequently, there's no need to rush. You can simply continue following this model."

Gu Xun, sitting opposite, nodded in strong agreement.

Discussions like today's Dao discourse had already taken place several times, all initiated by Luo Chen.

The purpose was to help Luo Chen perfect his main cultivation technique.

He and Fang Qingxue were willing to help Luo Chen, so they came happily each time.

The process of discussion was also quite selfless. Even Luo Chen had let down his guard, allowing them to observe his martial deity to facilitate the advice they gave.

But Luo Chen's cultivation achievements truly left them with no faults to find.

Faced with the two's recognition, Luo Chen was not proud. Instead, he fell into deep thought.

His self-created *God-Devil Formless* was originally only meant as a transition for the fourth stage of Body Tempering, but he hadn't expected it to already encompass the cultivation method of the Human Immortal realm.

This left him both satisfied and somewhat dissatisfied.

After pondering for a long while, Luo Chen suddenly spoke. "Fellow Daoist Fang!"

"Luo Chen, you are a bit older than me. Why not, like Brother Gu Xun, call me Little Sister Fang!" Fang Qingxue smoothed the hair by her cheek, a rare look of girlish coyness appearing on her youthful face. "I like this form of address. It makes me feel like I'm still very young, able to live for a very, very long time."

Luo Chen was speechless.

Martial artists of the Great Destruction realm were truly different from those cultivators of the outside world.

They rarely put on airs, acting entirely according to their own hearts, truly following their nature!

He did not stand on ceremony either, asking with a hint of familiarity in his tone, "Little Sister Fang, your family's knowledge is profound and deep, having received the true teachings of the Carefree Realm Lord. You must have some understanding of the cultivation situation after the Human Immortal realm. Could you share some related knowledge with me?"

Fang Qingxue hesitated for a moment, then generously spoke up.

"After the Human Immortal realm is the Destruction realm. My family's ancestor is at this realm. In his words, it is when the martial deity is perfected, becoming no different from the original self. Even if the physical body is destroyed, the martial artist's will can survive with the help of the martial deity, truly achieving immortality and indestructibility..."

...

Fang Qingxue and Gu Xun had already left.

But Luo Chen was still immersed in the martial arts knowledge Fang Qingxue had spoken before she left.

After the Human Immortal realm is the Destruction realm. The cultivation of the Destruction realm varies from person to person.

At this stage, the martial deity is perfected. The physical body can be destroyed, but the will does not perish. This sounds the same as cultivators focusing on cultivating their primordial spirit.

But in reality, it is completely the opposite!

At that realm, the martial deity must actively adhere to the martial artist's physical body, making the two even closer, and must never abandon the physical body.

Otherwise, even if the will remains, over time it will scatter and go mad, turning into a persistent obsession in the world.

In fact, one of the reasons there are so many ghostmares in the Great Destruction realm is also related to the large number of martial artists here.

Martial artists have strong wills. After death, they still have lingering thoughts, and they subconsciously want to possess another physical body.

That's why they turn into ghostmares, invading human dreams, and then trying to seize and occupy them.

This is the instinct of ghostmares!

Even if they can't truly occupy a human body, they will still go forward one after another to do so.

Getting off track.

The martial deity cannot abandon the physical body. Instead, it must become even closer to the physical body, forming a unified whole!

Only then can one break and then establish.

When cultivated to the limit, one performs the work of shattering the blood and qi furnace within the body, using a lifetime's accumulation of astral qi as foundation to open up an internal space.

At this point, one can reach the seventh realm of martial arts that all martial artists dream of—the Nirvana realm!

"Nirvana realm!"

Muttering this extremely familiar term, Luo Chen was somewhat dazed, as if he thought of Rosycloud, thought of the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture* he had cultivated before.

Shaking his head, Luo Chen withdrew his thoughts.

Gathering all power into oneself, giving birth to a martial deity that even heaven and earth cannot control—this is called Human Immortal.

Martial Human Immortal, seeing divinity, is imperishable.

After destruction, nirvana rebirth!

This is clearly a cultivation path of constantly shattering and constantly reorganizing.

In the midst of a thousand tempers and a hundred refinements, one achieves shocking divine power!

"If so, then I have a direction for creating a cultivation technique suitable for my subsequent training."

Luo Chen vaguely grasped some clues.

As for the cultivation of his current realm, after several discussions, Luo Chen had also made up his mind.

Generally speaking, he could follow what Gu Xun and Fang Qingxue said and keep things unchanged.

But in the details, there would be changes.

Normal Human Immortal realm martial artists can only condense one martial deity, and all cultivation revolves around this single martial deity.

Luo Chen, however, was slightly different.

His physical body was too strong, like a desolate beast true spirit, like a demon beast or devil race. Even the acupoints he had tempered were different from ordinary people's in their strength.

Moreover, with the Primordial Unity Cauldron assisting in converting chaotic energy, the efficiency was over a hundred times higher.

Thus, he could completely condense a greater number of martial deities!

If this cultivation method were spoken of, it would surely make Gu and Fang exclaim that Luo Chen had gone mad.

Because the appearance of other martial deities within a martial artist's body would affect the one most aligned with the original will.

But Luo Chen was not afraid.

The martial divine intent he had comprehended was the Selfless Divine Intent, which could also be seen as the Egoistic Divine Intent!

No matter how many martial deities appeared in his body, they would not affect each other, and they were all products under the will of Luo Chen.

Most importantly!

The *Seven Hundred Divine Slaughter* that Luo Chen had created himself required enough martial deities to activate.

After understanding all this, Luo Chen was no longer confused.

In the following days, aside from resting and occasionally visiting Gu and Fang for small gatherings, he was basically in seclusion, cultivating.

...

In the mountains, there are no sun or moons; in the world, a thousand years have passed.

Under the state of devoted cultivation, time always passes faster than imagined.

Seventy-two years later, in the Chaotic Sea.

A white-robed Daoist sat cross-legged in the void, letting layers of energy waves crash against him, unmoving as a mountain.

Suddenly!

The white-robed Daoist's eyes snapped open, as if lightning had flashed in the pitch-black chaos.

The Daoist slowly threw a punch towards the Chaotic Sea, and a soft shout came from his mouth.

"Slaughter!"

Infinite killing intent condensed, boundless astral qi erupted, and the acupoints within his body flickered like stars, finally converging into that single fist.

Boom!

A massive, substantial fist of astral qi shot out from the Daoist's hand, piercing through all the Chaotic Sea within sight.

Fully ten thousand zhang away!

Even where the fist passed, no end could be seen.

Only a dark, oppressive passage stood shockingly in the air, as if the sea had been parted by someone.

Luo Chen took a rough breath, satisfied as he watched this scene.

To conserve astral qi, he had only used a tenth of his strength in this move just now.

But its power was already beyond imagination.

If he were to use his full strength, back in the Mountains and Seas Realm, he could probably already tear space itself, even sink the entire Eastern Wilderness with one punch!

Such power had long surpassed ordinary Human Immortal martial artists, even Ascendant great powers.

Presumably, a Void Refinement True Lord would be no more than this, right?

Luo Chen mused for a moment.

The reason he had such confidence came from the seven hundred and twenty radiant martial deities within his body!

In seventy-two years, with the terrifying efficiency of ten per year, he had finally condensed seven hundred and twenty martial deities.

Thus, his self-created martial divine power, *Seven Hundred Divine Slaughter*, was nearing perfection.

And his martial arts realm had unknowingly reached the late Human Immortal stage.

This cultivation progress was equally shocking.

No matter where it was placed, it was enough to astonish the world.

Luo Chen knew that the contributors to such astonishing cultivation speed were the Primordial Unity Cauldron and this Chaotic Sea with its inexhaustible energy!

"If I can get out, restore my Ascendant cultivation, and combine it with my current martial arts realm, even against a Void Refinement True Lord, I would have a three-tenths chance of self-preservation."

"Normal cultivation for two or three hundred years would never achieve this level."

"Thinking about it this way, this Guixu can be considered a blessed land for me, Luo Chen."

Luo Chen thought of the trace of Calamity Gu origin energy that Youli Divine Lord had left in his Primordial Unity Cauldron back then.

At that time, before Youli Divine Lord ascended, he had said that Luo Chen would face a great calamity in the future, and that trace of Calamity Gu origin energy might help him withstand it a little.

Luo Chen had indeed encountered many dangers afterwards, but he had always turned danger into safety.

Until his ascension, he had never encountered a great calamity that could provoke a reaction from the Calamity Gu.

He had thought it was just Youli Divine Lord's unnecessary worry.

But he never expected that the great calamity would originate from Rosycloud!

Now, thinking carefully, a figure like Rosycloud must have had dealings with Youli Divine Lord. Coupled with Luo Chen's main cultivation technique, the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture*, perhaps Youli Divine Lord had already seen some clues back then, which was why he left behind a means to deal with it.

That Luo Chen was able to come to Guixu was probably also guided by that last trace of Calamity Gu origin energy.

It truly fulfilled the saying: blessing lies within misfortune, and misfortune lies within blessing!

Of course, all of this was predicated on Luo Chen being able to leave Guixu in the future.

Otherwise, having great power would only lead to a regrettable fall.

Luo Chen sighed and planned to return to Departing Dust Island.

On the way back, he casually glanced at the attribute panel.

As he expected, his martial arts realm was indeed stable at the late Human Immortal stage. Not only that, but because of the realm's improvement and the growth of his martial deities, the move *Heaven-Earth Inverse Reincarnation* had also appeared in the martial divine power column.

This was an unexpected joy.

Luo Chen smiled and was about to close the panel, but the corner of his eye froze on one entry.

That was the real unexpected joy!
