# Chapter 683: The Pill Realm Stone House, a Treasure Ground, the True Lord's Question, the Divide Between Immortal and Mortal

"Pill Realm?"

Luo Chen murmured the two words carved on the gray-white stone stele, a thoughtful look appearing on his face.

"Could this starry passage point to an inheritance land related to alchemy?"

The moment this thought emerged, Luo Chen became certain it was correct.

Earlier, while outside, he had faintly sensed that among the thirteen paths, two emitted a strong attraction toward him.

Looking at it from the reverse, if Devil Lord Heaven-Refining wanted to choose the person most suited to inherit his Daoist tradition, it would naturally be someone extremely compatible with a certain inheritance.

As for Luo Chen, among all his skills, his attainment in alchemy was the highest.

He had even reached the level of a fourth-tier alchemist when he was still at early Golden Core.

Even across the Mountains and Seas Realm, he was one of the most talented existences, without equal.

He could be called a master of his generation.

After thinking this through, Luo Chen's mood became somewhat complicated.

In terms of alchemy, his attainments were already high enough.

Whether for the Golden Core realm or even the future Sage realm, he felt he already had more than enough.

If he improved further, it might not bring much direct benefit to his own cultivation.

This was why, after coming to the Northern Sea, he had spent effort on body cultivation, artifact-forging, learning techniques, and studying arts, but had not focused on alchemy as intently as he had in the Eastern Wilderness. He had merely continued repeatedly refining pills he had already mastered.

Enough was enough. Why suffer to go further?

Now, an opportunity to obtain an inheritance lay before him, yet what he wanted more was something in another direction...

"Forget it!"

"Since I have come, I might as well settle here!"

"With my alchemy attainment, passing this Pill Realm test should be more than easy enough."

Luo Chen put away his regret and readjusted his mindset.

He had heard that inheritance lands were extremely dangerous. Even if this Pill Realm looked peaceful and auspicious, who knew whether danger hid within?

Luo Chen first examined his own condition.

His magic power circulated smoothly. His magic treasures could be used at any time. The magic power of his second Origin Pill was somewhat depleted and needed time to recover.

His storage ring could also be opened. As for the spirit beast bag...

Just as Luo Chen wanted to open the spirit beast bag and summon Black King and Heavenjade to scout the path for him, he suddenly discovered that the bag seemed to be sealed by some invisible force and could not be opened in this world at all.

Luo Chen frowned. "It does not want outsiders helping during the inheritance?"

Shaking his head, Luo Chen immediately sat cross-legged before the stone stele.

Before exploring the Pill Realm, there was still one matter he needed to handle.

With a thought, his magic power surged.

As the magic power moved, an unusual force within Luo Chen's body began stirring restlessly.

Very soon,

An anomaly appeared.

Looking at the mushroom-like thing growing from his shoulder, Luo Chen's face became somewhat gloomy.

Tai Sui's power!

This strange force would brazenly absorb the magic power from his body, thereby producing strange objects such as mushrooms and lingzhi.

Luo Chen even had a feeling that after his magic power was exhausted, his fleshly vitality and even his soul might be drawn out and become nourishment for the mushrooms.

"A vicious method, and a strange power."

After muttering this, Luo Chen slowly closed his eyes. His spiritual sense spread out, filling every corner of his body.

Then the Withering-Bloom True Fire inside him slowly diffused outward, wrapping around his entire body.

Under this slow burning refinement, the threads of strange power left by Tai Sui's avatars were continuously being refined away.

If this were an ordinary person, they would never dare burn their own body with true fire like this.

But Luo Chen was different. His powerful body and soul, comparable to an archaic beast, had been tempered day and night by fire to begin with.

"As expected, my strengthened Withering-Bloom True Fire does have some restraining effect against Tai Sui's power."

"If I had ignored everything and continued devouring fire spirits back then, reaching the level of that Vermilion Bird fire spirit, perhaps even Tai Sui would not dare provoke me lightly."

"Only, if that had happened..."

He curled his lip and did not dwell on events that had never occurred.

Luo Chen sank his heart and persistently refined the strange power on his body.

...

Several days later.

A red-robed Daoist strolled between green mountains and clear waters, light and relaxed, leisurely and free.

He did not fly. He simply stepped on the earth with both feet, walking neither quickly nor slowly.

This person was Luo Chen, who had removed Tai Sui's power.

What made him advance so leisurely was that this world was filled everywhere with rare flowers, strange grasses, and many spirit herbs of the immortal path.

Although from his perspective, these herbs seen everywhere were not especially high rank, mostly first to third tier, they existed in countless varieties, and many were rare treasures seldom seen in the outside world.

For example, right now.

Luo Chen crouched on the ground and took a medicinal hoe from his storage ring, digging carefully.

After a while, a section of root without tendrils appeared before his eyes, shaped like a human tongue.

"It really is Heaven-Earth Root!"

"I thought so. The spirit vein here is clearly not high-ranked, yet it attracts all sorts of mixed spiritual qi to gather here."

"Only Heaven-Earth Root could have such an effect."

Luo Chen dug out the tongue-like root, his face filled with surprise.

He was not unfamiliar with Heaven-Earth Root.

When he was still in the Qi Refinement realm, he had once seen someone bring it out for auction at the Zhenlong black market in Dahe Market.

It was said to have come from a Golden Core ruin in the Guyuan Mountain Range.

This spirit herb had extremely special effects. It could ignore attribute restrictions and absorb all kinds of spiritual qi into itself.

The older its age, the better the effect.

It had an unimaginable effect on cultivators breaking through realms.

Back then, Duan Feng had relied on a section of eight-hundred-year Heaven-Earth Root to successfully break into Foundation Establishment.

According to records, Heaven-Earth Root's wonders were not limited to Foundation Establishment. As long as its age was long enough, it could assist even Core Formation and Nascent Soul condensation.

After the initial surprise came regret.

The section of Heaven-Earth Root in his hand had an ordinary spiritual qi density, about five hundred years old, barely enough for Qi Refinement cultivators to use when breaking through to Foundation Establishment.

Its medicinal power was even somewhat weak.

"Yes, in this world, immortal cultivators are active everywhere. Thousand-year spirit herbs are hard to find, and ten-thousand-year spirit herbs are even rarer. How could there be so many..."

Just as he was sighing, Luo Chen subconsciously looked toward this stretch of green mountains.

A feeling of incongruity surfaced in his heart.

"Leaving aside how Devil Lord Heaven-Refining arranged this Pill Realm while he was alive, just from the time after his fall, Demon-Fall Land has existed for a thousand years until now. Why are the spirit herbs here basically all five hundred years old or younger?"

"That should not be."

Puzzled, Luo Chen quickened his steps. His spiritual sense and spiritual eyes continuously swept across this region.

Snow-Clear Grass, three hundred and fifty years old.

Red Pearl Flower, one hundred years old.

Unknown spirit herb, roughly two hundred...

Luo Chen's figure traveled farther and farther, and the doubts in his heart grew stronger.

After he crossed three mountains and five lakes and saw more than a thousand kinds of spirit herbs, that sense of incongruity became intense to the extreme.

The spirit herbs in this place all seemed stuck around the five-hundred-year mark.

At this moment, before he could search for the answer, a corner of a flying eave appeared between the green mountains.

Luo Chen's spirits lifted, and he hurriedly flew in that direction.

Before long, a stone hall occupying about five mu appeared before his eyes.

Its familiar architectural style, in the same line as the stone house where the spirit-gathering formation had been arranged when he came here, made Luo Chen feel at ease.

"Is this the place to receive the inheritance?"

He approached the stone hall carefully and stretched out both hands to push open the main door.

There was no imagined dust stirred up by long years without maintenance. Only a faint scent of books and the fragrance of herbs filled his nose.

When the stone hall's main door opened, three small paths paved with pebbles appeared before Luo Chen.

Each path had a sign.

Study.

Herb Room.

Pill Room.

It was indeed the standard arrangement of an alchemist. When Luo Chen had been at Luo Tian Sect, he had also arranged things this way.

He stopped before the three stone paths, hesitated for a while, then walked toward the Pill Room.

"Perhaps there are wondrous spirit pills left behind by Devil Lord Heaven-Refining?"

With this thought, Luo Chen pushed open the Pill Room's main door.

Everything he saw was plain and ordinary. A rather unremarkable law-device pill cauldron sat above the earth-fire outlet.

A law-device pill cauldron?

Amid surprise, Luo Chen swept his spiritual sense into the cauldron. There were no pills left behind.

After circling once, Luo Chen had no choice but to give up that fantasy.

Only at the earth-fire outlet did he pause for a moment.

The blackened, scorched traces showed that someone had used the earth fire here not long ago.

"Someone came here before me?"

Luo Chen was suddenly startled.

Before entering the inheritance land, he had considered the possibility of meeting people inside and had prepared to fight.

But after arriving at the fourteen starry entrances of Bright Radiance Heaven, that thought had faded greatly.

There was no other reason. There were fourteen in total... no, thirteen paths in total, each corresponding to one kind of Devil Lord inheritance.

Those who could reach Bright Radiance Heaven were either Sage Sages or Golden Core cultivators protected by Sages.

There were not few people who met such conditions.

But under the elimination of Leaving Forgetting Heaven and Five Elements Heaven, very few could reach Bright Radiance Heaven.

Under such circumstances, they would still be split among thirteen paths. Luo Chen did not think he would encounter someone who chose the same path as him.

Yet unexpectedly, the traces here clearly showed that someone had entered the Pill Realm before him and accepted the Devil Lord's alchemy inheritance.

Luo Chen narrowed his eyes slightly.

"Who could it be?"

Looking around, most traces had been deliberately erased. Only that little blackened scorch mark at the earth-fire outlet had been left behind.

After thinking for a while, Luo Chen could not identify anyone among those who had entered Demon-Fall Land this time who was especially skilled in alchemy.

Or rather, any high-level cultivator dabbled in alchemy to some extent. The difference was only whether they had merely tasted it or achieved something.

Unable to think of who it was, Luo Chen temporarily left the Pill Room.

There were no pills in the Pill Room. Surely the Herb Room had herbs!

Whether finished spirit herbs or collected medicinal materials, he would not mind...

Fine. It was still empty.

There were no medicinal materials or spirit pills at all.

There were only all kinds of tools for processing medicinal materials, messy yet extremely complete.

Silver knives, pestles and mortars, golden jars, grinding boats... there was even a juice press!

These tools were both familiar and unfamiliar to Luo Chen.

Familiar because he recognized them all.

Unfamiliar because he rarely used them. Most of the time, the medicinal materials he needed for alchemy did not require him to personally process them. Instead, he had subordinates prepare them in advance, or bought finished materials from shops.

Even after arriving in the Northern Sea cultivation world, he had Cheng Haixin, Heavenjade, and even cultivation families like Zhang Jiadi's to process medicinal materials for him.

Therefore, when it came to these tools for processing medicinal materials, he was indeed the most familiar stranger.

After staying in the Herb Room for a while, Luo Chen finally stepped into the Study.

When the main door opened, the fragrance of books rushed toward him.

As far as the eye could see, ten rows of tall bookshelves stood like a forest, and stacks of bamboo slips were piled like small mountains.

A pale-red desk made from some unknown material stood by the window.

Luo Chen slowly walked past the bookshelves and reached out to take down a bamboo slip.

"Snow-Clear Grass: born from the soil when rain and snow have just stopped and the sky clears. It likes damp cold and dislikes dry heat. Form: the main root is plump and extends straight downward, with many fibrous roots. The stem stands upright, about two inches long, and the leaves are six-cornered like snowflakes... Cultivation: wild Snow-Clear Grass should be accompanied by Snowglow Moths, which gather and scatter grass seeds across the wild. For artificial planting, take the root and stem... Note: best paired with intermittent activation of a Minor Snow Formation and Minor Cloud-Rain Formation. Medicinal effect: sweet in taste, neutral in nature, non-toxic. It clears heat, detoxifies, stops coughing, and calms wheezing. It is most suitable for detoxification pills. Because its medicinal nature is balanced and mild, it is also suitable as the main ingredient for pills that dredge the meridians. Processing method: grind the leaves into powder and dissolve them with Clear Spirit Spring Water during refinement. Similar types: resembles White Snow Grass. Similar nature to Yinmoon Flower..."

Luo Chen read ten lines at a glance, quickly sweeping through the contents of the bamboo slip.

At first, he did not think much of it.

He recognized this herb. In the past, Icecastle had produced large quantities of it.

But as he read more carefully, his expression gradually changed.

The descriptions on it were so detailed that they were rarely seen in the world.

Even cultivators in Icecastle who specialized in cultivating this spirit herb probably could not explain so many aspects of it.

From growth habits to physical characteristics, to cultivation, planting, medicinal use and processing, and even similar related plants, everything was included.

At the end, it even specifically mentioned several natural enemies harmful to Snow-Clear Grass.

"It is actually this detailed?"

Luo Chen put down the bamboo slip in surprise and took down another.

It clearly recorded another medicinal material Luo Chen did not recognize, named Heaven Scorpion Leaf. The contents were likewise densely packed, even to the point of being tedious.

Although he did not recognize Heaven Scorpion Leaf, after reading through it once, Luo Chen had already built an extremely clear image of this medicinal material in his mind.

From its growth habits to how it was best processed and used as medicine, everything was clear.

Luo Chen put down the bamboo slip and quietly looked at this study, where ten wooden bookshelves stood in rows. Waves rose in his heart.

If all the bamboo slips here recorded medicinal materials, and each was this detailed, then this study was simply a treasure ground that alchemists, spirit planters, physicians, and even herb gatherers dreamed of.

The value of this place far surpassed any Hundred Herbs Detailed Explanation or Spirit Herb Illustrated Record.

In any case, none of the herb books Luo Chen had seen before were this detailed.

If one could read through all the bamboo slips here, even without being able to refine pills or make medicine, one could easily become a senior appraiser in some shop or auction house.

As his heart surged, Luo Chen circulated the Fine Dust Primordial Art to suppress his stray thoughts and walked toward the desk by the window.

On it lay a jade slip.

If these bamboo slips recording detailed medicinal-material knowledge were one part of the inheritance left by Devil Lord Heaven-Refining,

Then perhaps they could be considered precious, but they absolutely did not match the grandeur of a generation's Void Refining True Lord.

In that case, this jade slip...

Filled with anticipation, Luo Chen sent his spiritual sense into it.

"Why can immortals roam freely through heaven and earth, while mortals can only be frogs at the bottom of a well?

At the root, it is nothing more than the two words spirit root.

We alchemists understand medicinal principles and the human body, and can refine all kinds of spirit pills and wondrous medicines according to each person's differences.

Some can flesh white bones or revive the dead; some can avoid poison and resolve confusion, clear the mind and stabilize the soul; increasing cultivation and realm is even less worth mentioning.

Since that is so, why can we not refine a corresponding spirit-root pill to break open the divide between immortal and mortal?"

Clatter!

The jade slip fell onto the desk. Only Luo Chen's astonished face flickered in the light of the setting sun passing through the window.

*(End of Chapter)*
