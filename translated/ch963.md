# Chapter 963: This Sect Permits You, Your Full Strength

Hum…

A strange sound suddenly rang out, spreading throughout the entire Netherworld Mountain.

Redleaf Matron's face was pale as she tremblingly withdrew her hand.

"Broken!"

In her words, aside from a tone of relief, there was also a hint of joy.

It was because in her hand, there was now a gu insect that resembled a turtle shell.

It was precisely the fourth-rank top-grade gu insect, the Myriad Formation Gu!

This type of gu insect was not naturally born, but artificially refined after birth.

Using gu insects related to the dao of formations as a base, supplemented by a great demon's Law-Derivation Turtle, and engraving the seven basic prohibitions of formation dao, after many years of nurturing, it could finally take shape.

With this gu in hand, eight or nine out of ten formations in the world could be quickly set up.

Not only that, it was even rumored that formation dao experts could use this gu to comprehend the true meaning of the laws related to formation dao, thereby stepping into the Ascendant realm.

Redleaf Matron's purpose in this trip was specifically for this Myriad Formation Gu.

Li Tianshi stared at the turtle shell in her hand, a flash of greed passing through his eyes, but he still praised her verbally.

"Even the formation left by a great power was broken by you, Redleaf. Your attainment in formation dao must have reached its peak."

Redleaf shook her head. "It's not that I am formidable, it's just that Yin-Yangzi didn't set up much in his lifetime. After all, who would set up formations everywhere in their own cave dwelling?"

The logic was indeed so.

The Yin-Yang Realm was different from the ancient secret realms of ordinary significance.

Those secret realms were often places where cultivators unfortunately fell into trouble outside, and to prevent enemies from breaking in, they had to set up various dangerous obstacles. Some even set up special tests to pass on their own techniques.

But the Yin-Yang Realm was merely the crazy act of the great power Yin-Yangzi before he sat down and died. He was focused entirely on researching various gu insects that could turn death into life. Where would he waste his mind on arranging exquisite formations?

Moreover, as time passed, over two thousand years, the formations here had long since decayed beyond recognition, which gave Redleaf Matron the opportunity to break them quickly.

Even so, it was enough to show Redleaf's attainment in formation dao.

Redleaf Matron looked ahead with some curiosity and some wariness. "The outer formation of the Yin-Yang Grave has been partially broken. The rest will naturally collapse after a while. My task ends here. Next…"

Li Tianshi stared at the Myriad Formation Gu, wanting to say something more, when his expression suddenly changed.

His single palm flipped over and struck out toward the right.

Boom!

The palm strike was astonishing, but it landed on empty air.

"Li Tianshi, what a great temper!"

A figure slowly emerged, speaking sarcastic words.

Looking at the newcomer, Li Tianshi coldly spat out three words.

"Meng Shentong!"

Meng Shentong cupped his hands. "It is I, this old man!"

Then, he looked ahead at the formation that was continuously disintegrating and said in surprise, "The Yin-Yang Grave actually exists, and you really found it."

Li Tianshi took a deep breath and asked, "Aren't you supposed to be guarding outside for me? Why did you sneak in?"

Meng Shentong glanced contemptuously at Li Tianshi. "Don't treat everyone like fools. Do you really think I don't know what's inside the Yin-Yang Grave?"

Redleaf Matron was stunned for a moment. "Isn't it said that inside the grave there are only fifth-rank gu insect refining formulas?"

Li Tianshi's face had already turned ashen.

"Gu formulas?" Meng Shentong laughed heartily, ignoring Li Tianshi's ashen face, and said on his own, "With mere gu formulas, without the corresponding materials, without the right time, place, and people, can you actually refine a fifth-rank divine gu?"

Redleaf Matron seemed to think of a possibility, her lips trembling as she said, "Could it be that what is left inside is a fifth…"

"That's right!"

Meng Shentong wanted to say more, but was directly interrupted by Li Tianshi.

His expression was solemn, with a hint of reverence as he said, "According to various clues I have found from the ancient texts of the Life and Death Gate, the madness of the great power Yin-Yangzi before his death was not meaningless. He researched the method of feigning death and reincarnation. When his companion desolate beast, the Netherworld Earth Dragon, reached the end of its lifespan, he died together with it. But by relying on the Life-and-Death Gu, he could seize the body of another cultivator and be reborn, thus avoiding the great limit of life and death. Unfortunately, time was not on his side. When both of them sat down and died, the Life-and-Death Gu would also collapse. And the newly cultivated Life-and-Death Gu was not of high enough rank and needed time to grow. It was simply too late for him to feign death and reincarnate."

"And what is left inside this Yin-Yang Grave is precisely that one Life-and-Death Gu that had not yet had time to grow to the fifth rank!"

As soon as these words were spoken, a flush suddenly appeared on Redleaf Matron's pale face.

There was also a hint of fanaticism in Meng Shentong's eyes. "It hasn't reached the fifth rank, but it ultimately has the foundation of the fifth rank. If one can obtain it, nurture it to the fifth rank, and then go to the wild lands to find a great desolate beast with compatible attributes, we will have the opportunity to easily step into the Ascendant realm."

Li Tianshi coldly looked at the two. "So, you want to fight me for it?"

Meng Shentong retorted, "Faced with the opportunity for Ascension, would you let go?"

Li Tianshi looked at Redleaf Matron. "Redleaf, what about you?"

Redleaf Matron's lips moved, but she didn't know how to answer.

Just then!

Swish!

A black light shot out from the ground, directly charging into the Yin-Yang Grave, where the great formation had not yet completely dissipated.

Vaguely, one could see that within the black light was a figure with wings on its back.

"Damn it!"

"Someone wants to get there first!"

"Chase!"

Three sentences in a row came from the three people's mouths.

Especially the last one, which came from Redleaf Matron's mouth. It was as if she had found a way to step down, and she charged into the Yin-Yang Grave without hesitation. Clearly, just a moment ago, she had been thinking that her task was over and she would leave this place.

Meng Shentong followed closely behind.

Instead, Li Tianshi, who cared the most, fell to the back.

Looking at the two figures ahead, he squinted his eyes. Despite his ashen face, the corners of his mouth slightly lifted, revealing an extremely dangerous meaning.

Faced with opportunity, indeed, no one could be trusted!

…

The black-armored man, wearing a Flat Mountain Crown with golden feathers that shook the void, stood high in the air.

His eyes, like a dark abyss, looked down at the three people below, and he spoke words of slaughter.

"You want to stop me?"

Bai Xuanjun said nothing, but bone spurs began to sprout all over his body. From a distance, he looked like a hedgehog.

Fan Wujiu, the master of the Hidden Sword Pavilion, slapped the sword case on his back, and streams of sword-leaf light flew out.

"Apologies. I was entrusted by someone, so I must be loyal to that duty."

The Purple-Robed Marquis's expression was somewhat hesitant. He had known Luo Chen for not a long time, just over a year.

But they got along quite well and usually had a good relationship.

Now, facing each other with weapons drawn was not his wish.

But just as Fan Wujiu said, he was entrusted by someone, so he must be loyal to that duty.

He sighed, pulled out a purple-blood soft sword from his waist, and it hung before him like a pool of autumn water.

"Qi… Brother Luo, this marquis also wishes to witness the exquisite dao techniques of the Eastern Wilderness."

Brother Luo?

A hint of surprise appeared on Luo Chen's calm face.

"It seems you all already know my origins?"

The Purple-Robed Marquis said with emotion, "The alchemist Luo Chen did a great deed in the Eastern Wilderness, single-handedly destroying a mountain and annihilating a sect, then disappeared. And then, a top-tier expert of unknown origin and background suddenly appeared in the Southern Frontier. Anyone would connect the two."

"Is that so?" Luo Chen raised an eyebrow but did not argue.

Making the connection was one thing; confirming they were the same person was another.

Luo Chen asked himself that since coming to the Southern Frontier, he had changed his usual style. His natal treasure was sealed, his Sage mana was not revealed, and even his set of attire was something he had never used before.

The Mystic Dust Armor might be somewhat conspicuous, but this treasure could freely change its shape.

Under such circumstances, to recognize him…

The effort involved was definitely not just simple speculation.

"It was Li Tianshi, wasn't it?"

The only person Luo Chen could think of who had this kind of energy was Li Tianshi.

Just like in the Eastern Wilderness, relying on his identity as an alchemist, he had a wide network of connections. If he wanted to inquire about something, he could basically find out a thing or two.

Li Tianshi's status in the Southern Frontier was by no means inferior to his.

If he was determined, his background would ultimately not be hidden for long.

Luo Chen did not wait for the few to answer. He reached up to his ear and pulled.

Swish!

The Hundun Staff fell into his right hand.

"Never mind. Only by dealing with the three of you first can I enter."

Before the words had even fallen, he was already like an arrow from a bowstring, arriving above the Purple-Robed Marquis, and struck down with his staff like a thunderbolt!

Since they were friends, he should fight with all his strength.

Boom!

A staff smashed down, and purple light surged.

The purple-blood soft sword coiled around the Hundun Staff like a snake, trying to use softness to overcome hardness.

But with a flick of Luo Chen's hand, the purple-blood soft sword seemed to lose its bones and scattered with a clatter.

The Purple-Robed Marquis's face flushed red, and he staggered backward.

Just as Luo Chen was about to press the attack, a river of sword light surged from afar.

Without any visible movement, Luo Chen merely took a few ethereal steps in the air, and his figure began to gracefully walk through the river of swords, advancing continuously.

Seeing this, the master of the Hidden Sword Pavilion's eyes widened.

His sword-leaf gu numbered over a thousand, hence the name Thousand-Leaf Sword Case.

Under such dense attacks, Luo Chen could actually dodge them skillfully.

What exquisite footwork!

"No good! I need to activate the sword formation!"

The master of the Hidden Sword Pavilion's heart sank. His expression turned stern, and the sword finger in his hand suddenly changed.

Swish! Swish! Swish!

Countless sword leaves instantly scattered. Instead of attacking Luo Chen, they transformed into a massive sword formation covering three li in radius, roaring and howling.

In the distance, Bai Xuanjun, with a cold face and white eyes, flicked his fingers repeatedly.

Bone spurs broke through the air, charging into the sword formation, heading straight for Luo Chen.

However, as the bone spurs approached his body, Luo Chen showed no sign of active defense or even evasion, letting the bone spurs strike him.

An unexpected scene occurred again.

Thud! Thud! Thud!

All the bone spurs hit Luo Chen's body but seemed to enter a quagmire, unable to advance.

Bai Xuanjun raised an eyebrow. "Protective Qi Armor?"

With a thought, all the bone spurs no longer went straight but began to spin rapidly.

In a spiral posture, they continuously advanced.

Puff! Puff! Puff!

The protective Qi Armor that could hold for a moment was instantly shattered.

But when they reached his body, their momentum was exhausted, and they still could not advance an inch.

A round of earthy yellow light fell from the Flat Mountain Crown on Luo Chen's head, and together with the Mystic Dust Armor's main body, it blocked all the bone spurs outside.

Not only that, the sword leaves shooting from all directions were also blocked outside.

No one expected that the defensive power of the Mystic Dust Armor combined with the Flat Mountain Crown, once belonging to the ancient demon emperor Di Tian, could be so astonishing!

Just then!

"Go!"

The Purple-Robed Marquis's sharp cry rang out, and a purple light broke through the air, heading straight for Luo Chen's neck.

Was he trying to replicate the decapitation that killed the Four Ancestors of Golden Light earlier?

The purple light came extremely fast. Cooperating with the Thousand-Leaf Sword Formation and the spiral bone spurs, it finally broke through Luo Chen's defense.

However, Luo Chen suddenly reached out and grabbed it, catching the purple-blood soft sword in his hand.

Unwilling to be caught, the sword coiled up Luo Chen's arm.

In the distance, the Purple-Robed Marquis pulled downward, intending to scrape off Luo Chen's arm.

But with that pull, he felt a tremendous force transmitted through the air.

No matter how he urged his mana, the purple-blood soft sword could not achieve any success.

Within the sword formation, Luo Chen looked at his current situation and couldn't help but sigh inwardly.

Body refinement was indeed powerful, excelling in one-on-one combat and overall suppression.

But when facing multiple opponents, especially those skilled in various long-range attacks like Sage cultivators, it inevitably lacked flexibility.

Even with the exquisite Moon Step, he could dodge many long-range attacks, but in the end, two fists could not defeat four hands, or six hands.

No wonder the path of body refinement had declined in the current cultivation world.

Suddenly!

A strange buzzing sound rang out, spreading from the inside out throughout the entire Netherworld Mountain.

Luo Chen was startled. This was a sign of the great formation collapsing!

Had Li Tianshi and the others already broken the final defensive formation of the Yin-Yang Grave?

"Distracted during battle?"

The master of the Hidden Sword Pavilion was closest to him. Sensing his distraction, he was overjoyed.

His sword finger turned, and thousands of sword lights converged into a single thread, shooting straight at Luo Chen's brow.

Not only that, Bai Xuanjun and the Purple-Robed Marquis also sensed this opportunity and each launched a second attack.

Bone spurs bloomed like a lotus.

Purple light sprayed like rain.

As the three attacks struck his body, Luo Chen's expression remained unchanged. He only let out a cold snort.

"Since you want to see my true strength, then this sect will grant your wish!"

His left and right hands grabbed the purple-blood soft sword. With a pull and a twist, he directly twisted this true-artifact-level treasured sword into pieces.

Then, his figure flickered!

Swish!

Boom!

Ten thousand swords converged into one leaf, landing on empty air, splashing countless white bones.

The lotus bone spurs spun around but hit nothing.

Even the impenetrable purple light covering his body failed to harm Luo Chen in the slightest.

In the blink of an eye, the three experts were stunned.

They had clearly locked onto Luo Chen with their divine sense. How could their attacks miss, deviating by several inches?

They did not know that Luo Chen had long ago created a soul art called *Ghosts and Gods Give Way*, specifically used to confuse the enemy's divine sense, causing their attacks to deviate.

This soul art, Luo Chen rarely used, because it required his own soul foundation to completely surpass the enemy's.

Most of the enemies he had encountered along the way had soul foundations comparable to his, or even surpassing him. Using it would not have much effect.

But now, his *Soul-Splitting Art* was complete, with three souls standing firm within him. In terms of soul foundation, apart from great cultivators, few could compare to him.

Once *Ghosts and Gods Give Way* was activated, combined with the Mystic Dust Armor and the Flat Mountain Crown, plus his powerful physical body, even if he didn't make a move, he could be said to be in an invincible position among his peers.

"One does not return a courtesy without giving in return!"

Amidst the smoke and dust, only Luo Chen's light cry was heard.

The fragments of the purple-blood soft sword were infused with source blood by him and, using a special hidden weapon technique, shot toward Bai Xuanjun.

Then, he formed a sword finger with both hands, pointed at the ground, and shouted.

"Open!"

The sword formation rose!

The sword formation opened!

Five swords linked, severing the thread of life!

Boundless sword threads burst forth from the five sword pellets that broke through the ground, transforming into thousands of sword qi. With lightning speed, they spread out for ten li, a hundred li, encompassing most of Netherworld Mountain.

Once the Grand Five Elements Sword Formation was activated, sword threads crisscrossed, sword qi surged endlessly. Some were violent, some were wild, some were illusory, some were ethereal. Each move was different, each style was distinct.

But precisely, under the circulation of the five elements, every move complemented each other, leaving no flaw whatsoever!

Now, the Grand Five Elements Sword Formation was composed of five true-artifact-level sword pellets!

Moreover, these five sword pellets had been infused by Luo Chen with the most top-grade Nine Yang Geng Metal. Their sharpness was unparalleled in the world!

The master of the Hidden Sword Pavilion bore the brunt of it. The Thousand-Leaf Sword Case emitted a brilliant light, transforming into a curved sword moon that revolved around his body.

The sword moon was fiercely sharp, madly shredding the sword threads attacking from all directions.

However! There was also a gap between sword formations.

Only Luo Chen's low shout was heard from afar.

"Die!"

Chi!

A stream of sword qi, carrying thousands of sword threads, with an unstoppable momentum, shattered the Thousand-Leaf Sword Moon.

"Ah…"

A miserable cry suddenly rang out.

The master of the Hidden Sword Pavilion was washed over by countless sword qi, becoming like a sieve.

A Sage fled in panic.

But before the Sage could merge into the void and escape a thousand li away, an inexplicable suppressing force instantly enveloped it, rendering it immobile.

Looking at the sword qi surging from all directions, the master of the Hidden Sword Pavilion roared in despair.

"No!"

Puff!

The Sage shattered, transforming into vast spiritual power that swept in all directions.

The Grand Five Elements Sword Formation was completely unaffected. Under the circulation of the five elements, the sword formation rose again, and the sword moves transformed again!

The Purple-Robed Marquis kept retreating, but amidst the storm-like sword qi, he had no place to stand.

Puff, puff, puff…

A large amount of sword qi broke through his defensive treasure, pierced through his physical body, and shredded him into a pool of blood.

Luo Chen glanced at the pool of blood, changed his sword finger, and attacked Bai Xuanjun, who was farthest away.

"Your turn!"

There was a hint of fear in Bai Xuanjun's eyes, but facing the thousands of sword qi coming at him, he was not particularly flustered.

He formed a hand seal and muttered softly.

"Escape!"

The next moment, the thousands of sword qi hit nothing, leaving only traces of flesh and blood emitting a bloody smell.

A hundred li away, on a skeleton, spiritual light bloomed.

With a flicker, Bai Xuanjun staggered out.

Most of the bone spurs on his body had been broken.

And this was just a single exchange, and he had already prepared the bone escape technique.

"The might of the Alchemist Sect is truly terrifying!"

Bai Xuanjun looked back with lingering fear at the place shrouded in sword qi, then left the underworld without hesitation.

Hoo…

Luo Chen exhaled a mouthful of turbid qi, reached out, and the five sword pellets flew back with a spin.

The sword qi dissipated, and the wind was calm.

It was as if nothing had happened before.

However, the record of three mid-Sage experts—one dead, one injured, and one fled—was no lie.

Luo Chen glanced at the pool of blood on the ground, then his figure flashed, transforming into a burst of firelight and disappearing.

After he left.

The pool of blood on the ground gradually gathered and took shape.

The bloodless Purple-Robed Marquis, his body trembling, looked at the place where Luo Chen had disappeared.

"Is this his full strength?"

(End of this chapter)
