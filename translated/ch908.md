# Chapter 908: Nether Origin Catastrophe, Slay Enemies and Demons

"Why is she here?"

"How did she get in?"

"Why did she have to come precisely when I had set foot on Jambu Mountain?"

Three questions surfaced one after another in Luo Chen's mind.

The answers no longer mattered. What mattered was getting far away from the center of the battlefield!

Luo Chen fled madly. The surging origin qi of heaven and earth behind him rolled forward like tidal waves. Ignoring the danger of being assimilated by heaven and earth, he unfolded the Withering-Bloom True Intent and forcibly commanded this vast ocean of qi.

Though difficult, he could barely borrow some of its power.

His entire body fled from Jambu Mountain at an even faster speed, heading straight for the Dan Saint Hall.

At this moment, in his heart, the only relatively safe place was the Dan Saint Hall—specifically, the blessed land space inside that could isolate the outside world.

The distance between the two locations was not actually that far.

After all, Jambu Mountain had originally been excavated from the Dan Saint Blessed Land, merely relocated to the Nine Heavens above.

At Luo Chen's desperate escape speed, the majestic building of the Dan Saint Hall had already come into view.

Also falling into his line of sight were over a hundred beams of flying light heading toward him.

Led by Lingtian City Lord, this group of cultivators had naturally also sensed the anomaly in the Nine Heavens above and the Jambu Mountain falling from the sky.

In shock, they flew upward, just in time to meet Luo Chen, who was wobbling in the air like a kite.

"Luo Chen, what happened?"

In his urgency, Lingtian City Lord could no longer be bothered with honorifics like "Dan Saint Hall Master."

Luo Chen's current state was not only staggering, but blood was even seeping from his eyes, ears, nose, and mouth. His appearance was utterly miserable, a far cry from the celestial being he had seemed when he left.

He had clearly suffered extremely severe injuries!

Luo Chen had no time to explain. He only said one sentence: "A great enemy is coming!"

With that, he leaped past the crowd.

Lingtian City Lord was stunned for a moment, but gritting his teeth, he still issued the order.

"Everyone, follow me to the Nine Heavens to face the enemy!"

Over a hundred people all took out their magical treasures and flew toward the Nine Heavens as if ready to die.

At this moment, one went up, one went down.

The fleeing Luo Chen and the Nether Origin Sect cultivators advancing to meet the enemy formed a stark contrast.

No one blamed Luo Chen, because the state he had shown earlier was truly terrible.

But if one thought about it more carefully, the fact that he could still maintain such a terrifying escape speed showed that he still had the strength to fight.

The real reason the two sides went in opposite directions was actually only one thing.

He, Luo Chen, was ultimately not a Nether Origin Sect cultivator. He simply did not have that instinctive protection of the sect from the bottom of his heart.

Just as he was about to reach the Dan Saint Hall, Luo Chen couldn't help but look back at Ye Lingtian and the others.

Thinking of how ferocious Rosycloud was—able to destroy Jambu Mountain, which had stood for three thousand years, with a single blow—what was the difference between their going and moths flying into a flame?

He could not dissuade them.

Because this was their native Nether Origin Sect, where they had grown up!

Once inside the Dan Saint Hall, Luo Chen did not hesitate. He went straight to where Luo Lingxi was.

The first to discover him was Ye Ruoli.

"Hall Master, what happened?" Ye Ruoli walked out anxiously. As an Adept cultivator, she could already sense the power of heaven and earth shaking outside.

"Father!" Luo Lingxi looked bewildered, having no idea what was happening.

Luo Chen did not explain. With a sweep of his sleeve, his dharma power wrapped around the two.

With a thought, the hall's formation suddenly activated. A wisp of mist drifted over, rolling the three of them into the Dan Saint Blessed Land.

"Take care of Lingxi!"

After releasing the two, Luo Chen gave a simple instruction, then sat down cross-legged.

Source power circulated once within his body, and instantly, his shattered flesh rapidly coalesced.

The injuries that had looked so wretched earlier were actually not that severe, given his terrifying recovery ability.

In truth, this was also related to the fact that Rosycloud's attack had not been primarily aimed at him.

If the Seven Lamps Elder and the others had not been blocking the way, they would not have ended up utterly destroyed, their bodies and souls obliterated.

"What a pity for Fellow Daoist Fu and Fellow Daoist Gu."

Luo Chen sighed inwardly, then opened his eyes.

Meeting Luo Lingxi's worried gaze, he forced a faint smile.

"Don't worry, I'm fine."

He wiped his face, and all traces of blood vanished.

Then, Luo Chen fell into hesitation.

Luo Lingxi and Ye Ruoli did not know what he was hesitating about. They only saw his expression constantly shifting, as if he were thinking, or perhaps afraid.

And Luo Chen, now having a moment to catch his breath, could finally come back to his senses and ponder those three questions.

Why had Rosycloud come?

Her actions gave the answer: to rescue Qinghe Immortal, who was about to be refined by the three Nether Origin Sect ancestors.

But that last sentence she had spoken seemed more like the real answer!

To avenge the Nether Origin Sect's repeated provocations against Green Parasol Mountain!

"This woman truly cannot tolerate a grain of sand in her eye!"

Luo Chen swallowed his saliva.

He did not know to what extent her revenge would go—would it be a mere touch, or would she go on a killing spree?

But the fall of Jambu Mountain—such a great humiliation—was something the three lofty ancestors of the Nether Origin Sect could absolutely not tolerate.

Even if Rosycloud wanted to leave, she would likely have to fight bitterly.

And how had Rosycloud managed to enter the Nether Origin Sect silently?

Luo Chen had witnessed the Nether Abyss that guarded the Nether Origin Sect.

Sage cultivators simply could not force their way across. They had to rely on demon beasts like the Mysterious Turtle, which had grown up in that abyss, as ferry boats.

Even a mighty Ascendant cultivator, if they tried to cross by force, would inevitably cause a huge commotion. It would be impossible for them to reach the heart of the sect without anyone noticing.

Luo Chen could not figure out the answer to this question.

And he did not really care. A powerhouse like Rosycloud naturally had her own divine abilities and miraculous methods. What he cared about more was why she had come precisely when he had set foot on Jambu Mountain.

It was too much of a coincidence!

Suddenly!

Luo Chen recalled the anomaly he had experienced in the Heavenly Nether Palace.

When he had released the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire, the black water pool suppressing Qinghe Immortal had undergone a brief, intense change.

At first, he had thought it was a reaction caused by the powerful external forces.

But now, it seemed there was a deeper reason.

"Could it be that Qinghe Immortal sensed my dharma power?"

Luo Chen narrowed his eyes, beginning to consider this possibility.

If Nether Spring had not lied to him back then, then the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture* he cultivated should be from the same lineage as Rosycloud's.

Though the genders differed, and the races were completely different.

Still, dharma power cultivated from the same technique would always have similarities.

Given that Rosycloud was willing to break into the Nether Origin Sect to rescue Qinghe Immortal, the relationship between the two was clearly not shallow. Qinghe Immortal must also be familiar with the aura of her dharma power.

"So, at that time, Qinghe Immortal was not resisting the suppression, but sending a signal?"

Thinking of this possibility, Luo Chen's mind began to connect more dots.

He asked himself whether he had the qualifications to make a fair trade with an Ascendant powerhouse.

Even the most virtuous and respected senior would not easily trade a peerless technique that led straight to the Great Vehicle stage with a junior.

At least, if he himself were in Rosycloud's position, he could not do such a thing.

For example.

Luo Chen had obtained a large amount of spirit wine from the Dieyun Sect, whose value was certainly no less than half of the Dieyun Sect's peak-era foundation.

But all he had left behind were three Nascent Soul Pills.

No other resources at all.

Would Rosycloud be so generous?

And why would she let him come and go freely on Green Parasol Mountain? Was she really that favorably disposed toward him?

"Now it seems that trade was ultimately unfair after all."

"She forcefully took my Dao Union Pill formula. Knowing that I would definitely go to the Nether Origin Sect, she used the technique as a lure and a positioning signal."

"As for why she was certain I could ascend Jambu Mountain?"

On this point, even before he had undergone the Dan Saint trial, Luo Chen himself had been full of confidence.

After all, he had passed the alchemy inheritance trial of Devil Lord Heaven-Refining. How could a mere Dan Saint trial from the Mountains and Seas Realm be any harder?

Having figured all this out, Luo Chen suddenly saw the light.

But he did not complain about Rosycloud.

So-called unfairness sometimes depends not only on the value of the traded items.

But also on the importance of those items to the buyer and seller.

At least, Luo Chen, having switched to the new technique, felt he had not lost out.

It was only the danger today that left him with lingering fear.

"How is the situation outside?"

Luo Chen hesitated, then made up his mind.

Who would win, who would lose, and how the Nether Origin Sect would develop—he had to keep an eye on it.

But monitoring the battlefield of an Ascendant-level battle was still too dangerous. He would have to find another way.

Fortunately, he now had the conditions to do so.

Luo Chen looked up at this boundless blessed land space.

Although the token that served as the key had been taken back by Black Marsh Ancestor, this place had already acknowledged him as its master the moment he had unlocked the token's seal.

All the corresponding formations, even all the formations of the Dan Saint Hall, were under his control.

With his formation dao abilities, making some simple manipulations was not difficult.

His hands slowly moved, his ten fingers constantly forming hand seals.

Streams of light flew out from his storage ring and inserted themselves into the ground.

Ye Ruoli, watching from the side, saw that they were formation flags.

She was somewhat surprised. Could this Dan Saint Hall Master also be proficient in formations?

The next moment.

Luo Lingxi pointed at the sky in amazement. "Sister Ruoli, look over there!"

Ye Ruoli looked up and saw that water mirrors were beginning to appear in the sky.

At the moment, the water mirrors were rippling, showing no clear images.

"Is it the Water Mirror Art?"

"But with the fierce battle raging outside and the spiritual energy in turmoil, such a Water Mirror Art probably won't hold!"

At the same time.

Above the Dan Saint Hall, the mist that had just dissipated suddenly surged forth again.

It transformed into wisps of green smoke, drifting toward various parts of the Nether Origin Sect.

Inside the blessed land.

Luo Chen focused his mind and shouted, "Stabilize!"

Instantly, the rippling water mirrors, as if infused with some powerful force, stabilized immediately.

Images of the outside world began to appear on them one after another.

It was Luo Chen's self-created Cloud-Chess Water Mirror Art!

But this time, combined with the spirit vein and the Dan Saint Hall's mist formation, it had a wider monitoring range and a more stable state of technique maintenance.

After doing all this, Luo Chen finally breathed a sigh of relief and looked at the water mirrors.

He did not dare to deploy his monitoring range near Jambu Mountain. He could only scatter it over other areas within ten thousand li.

But by observing the small, he could perhaps glimpse a corner of the iceberg.

In his field of vision, there were already beacons of war and smoke everywhere!

Beams of escaping light, radiating powerful dharma power fluctuations, shot up from all over the Nether Origin Sect.

Each one represented a Sage!

Unlike Luo Chen, who had fled without looking back in the face of an Ascendant-level battle, these Nether Origin Sect Sages showed no fear at all, actively joining the fight.

"Is it because they had participated in the siege of Qinghe Immortal earlier that they are so bold and reckless?"

Luo Chen murmured. At least, he still did not dare.

One beam of fleeing light paused for a moment as it passed by the mist Luo Chen had scattered.

But it did not pay much attention.

At this moment, all over the Nether Origin Sect, formation lights were trembling chaotically.

Although this wisp of mist was somewhat strange, it at least carried the aura of the Nether Origin Sect.

After only a brief pause, the escaping light charged into the Nine Heavens.

"Not enough!"

"Still not enough!"

Luo Chen had wanted to observe the whole from the parts, to see the entirety from the scattered.

But the qi in the Nine Heavens above was too turbulent. He could not even glimpse a corner of the iceberg.

Gritting his teeth, Luo Chen moved his mind and formed a hand seal.

The next moment, above the Dan Saint Hall, the mist suddenly condensed into a giant red eye.

Within it, a golden pupil coldly gazed into the sky.

The first thing that entered his sight made Luo Chen's expression change drastically.

That figure in red, right in front of the three ancestors, casually crushed a light sphere with her bare hands.

Among the three ancestors, Heaven Abyss Ancestor immediately reeled as if struck by a heavy blow.

Rosycloud laughed lightly. "You asked how I crossed the Nether Abyss? With a clone of this one as a guide, it was the simplest thing."

As she laughed, she struck out with a palm, golden flames following.

Heaven Abyss Ancestor slashed out with his sword, conjuring a thousand-zhang sword light in the void.

But against that seemingly light palm, the sword light shattered like tofu. The palm struck Heaven Abyss Ancestor's chest from across the air.

"Scatter!"

Heaven Abyss Ancestor shouted in a low voice, and his entire body transformed into thousands of clones, flying in all directions.

Rosycloud was about to give chase when a precious diagram unfurled like a canopy of heaven. Within it, black smoke billowed, the Yellow Spring flowed, and it came crashing down from above.

Nether Abyss Yellow Spring Diagram!

One of the Nether Origin Sect's sect-suppressing spirit treasures!

Back then, the ancient demon emperor Di Tian had been trapped by this treasure and could not escape his doom of obliteration.

However, Rosycloud seemed utterly unconcerned. She neither dodged nor avoided, letting the heavenly canopy diagram cover her.

Seeing this, Black Marsh Ancestor was overjoyed.

"Courting death!"

This spirit treasure's killing power was somewhat inferior, but its ability to trap enemies was rarely seen in the world.

Think of how ferocious the ancient demon emperor Di Tian had been. Once trapped, he could only be slaughtered by them.

This Rosycloud was so careless—she was truly seeking death.

But the next moment, his expression changed drastically!

Within the diagram, spiritual light trembled violently. Wisps of golden flames continuously emerged from the painting.

Under his mental perception, Black Marsh Ancestor's heart was flooded with alarm.

This spirit treasure was about to be destroyed!

Black Marsh Ancestor shouted sharply, "Two senior brothers, lend me a hand!"

Heaven Abyss Ancestor, who had transformed into thousands of clones, immediately turned back, holding his sword as he stepped into the spirit treasure.

Nether Control Ancestor also did not hesitate. He waved his hand toward the azure void.

"Summon the wind!"

A wisp of azure firmament wind tore through the air, wrapping around him as he charged straight into the Yellow Spring Diagram.

They intended to repeat the old strategy of besieging the ancient demon emperor Di Tian, killing Rosycloud within the diagram.

Not only that, but before entering, Nether Control Ancestor also spread his divine sense throughout the entire Nether Origin Sect and issued a command.

"Nether Origin Thirty-Six Sons, form the Zhou Tian Slaughter God Grand Formation!"

"Thunder Daozi, I grant you control of the Nether Origin Sect's sect-protecting grand formation. Guide the Nine Heavens Divine Thunder, slay the enemy and kill the demon!"

The divine sense transmission rang out like thunder across the clear sky.

And that precious diagram, spanning ten thousand li, trembled with treasure light within. Golden flames surged violently, showing the intensity of the battle.

A vast ocean of heaven and earth origin qi was drawn in, continuously injected into the diagram, making the Yellow Spring surge even more fiercely.

In the sky, stars lit up one by one, forming the number of the Zhou Tian cycle.

Furthermore, the grand formation that had protected the Nether Origin Sect for thousands of years roared into activation. Under the control of Thunder Daozi, who floated up like a god or demon, purple lightning slowly rippled.

A pool of thunder gradually took shape.

The thunder pool was only a hundred zhang in radius, but the terrifying power contained within it made even all the Sage cultivators dare not touch it in the slightest.

Not only that, but on the earth, one ancient and powerful aura after another began to gradually awaken.

This was the Nether Origin Catastrophe!

The entire sect, hundreds of millions of disciples, must unite with one heart, slay the enemy, and kill the demon!
