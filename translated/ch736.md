# Chapter 736: Eastern Wilderness Secrets, Ghost Immortal Ring Body

Neither Shark Emperor Liujun's spying nor the tragedy of the Sage battle had made Luo Chen lose composure like this.

Even the sudden appearance of Ascendant great power Li Canghai was within reason, because everyone had mentioned the private friendship between Daoist Extreme Cold and Li Canghai in their early years.

But Li Canghai's action of lightly taking away Ice Soul Spirit Mountain with that small seal painted with indistinct mountain-and-sea patterns left Luo Chen's heart unable to calm even now.

Among Sage cultivators, when it came to formations, Luo Chen ranked far from the top.

Even though he could now arrange some fourth-tier formations, he still needed complete formation diagrams, enough preparation time, and suitable heavenly timing and earthly advantage to calmly set them up.

However, Luo Chen's foundation in the Dao of formations was extremely solid!

He believed that among those of the same realm, his formation foundation was also among the best.

He had begun studying formation theory in the Initiate stage and formally put his hands to it in the early Adept stage.

Whether it was the seven basic restrictions of the Dao of formations or the principles of the five phases generating and restraining one another, he knew them all by heart.

He had also received personal instruction from Min Longyu, Han Zhan, and others.

Most importantly, foundations were often composed of proficiency. With the system's support, the solidity of his formation foundation naturally became only reasonable.

Because of this foundation, when facing some formations that were not too complicated, he could often proceed from easy to difficult, strip away complexity, and quickly understand and grasp them.

He had once obtained a set of the island-linking formation method.

After studying it, he felt this formation was rather novel. Its protective power was secondary; the essence lay in linking heaven and earth and allowing the earth-vein qi of different places to interact.

Because of this, he had once even developed the thought that after returning to the Eastern Wilderness, he would search for Green Pill Valley's Five Peaks Valley-Canopy Art, add the Medicine King Sect Adept elder's Mountain-Moving Art, and combine them with this formation to construct a cultivation blessed land for his own sect.

But in that moment just now, all his ideas were overturned.

Li Canghai's method of taking away Ice Soul Spirit Mountain was absolutely not some Ascendant method or great-power divine ability!

Other Sages might be misled, but the most basic principles of formations would not deceive him.

That was the most basic formation!

A formation hidden beneath a great power's methods!

He had seen it with extreme clarity. In that moment, a large number of restrictions appeared from the Mountain-Sea Seal and resonated with Ice Soul Spirit Mountain. Among them, mountain restrictions, water restrictions, and spatial restrictions were the richest.

It was precisely because of this formation resonance that the complete Ice Soul Spirit Mountain had been pulled out without any damage and perfectly merged into the Mountain-Sea Seal.

When Luo Chen understood this moment, his entire body trembled somewhat.

There was fear and awe, but even more, there was inexplicable excitement and anticipation.

"The island-linking formation method became popular throughout the Northern Sea cultivation world within two hundred years, and any human territory of slightly larger scale has this formation arranged."

"There should be no few people like me, who pride themselves on cleverness and believe that beneath the first layer's defensive effect, the truly formidable effect is nourishing spirit lands."

"After such a long period of evolution, this formation has almost already embedded and fused itself into the Northern Sea's large and small spirit lands."

"If Li Canghai harbors evil intentions and incorporates every spirit land into the small Mountain-Sea Seal as he just did..."

As he murmured to himself, Luo Chen gradually stopped trembling.

Clearly, there was no if. Li Canghai had plainly already developed evil intentions!

One could even say that the moment this formation spread from his hands, the hidden disaster of today's matter had already been planted.

Otherwise, how could the formation of a dignified Ascendant great power be so easily learned by the hostile force Moluo Stream?

At Molan Court, the restrained island-linking formation method could not deceive anyone.

One had to know that was Blood Nightmare Moluo's lair, at least in name the banner of Moluo Stream!

Even there, a similar formation had been arranged!

"He spread one formation across the Northern Sea, taking lands of myriad spirits into his treasure seal. Li Canghai wants to merge all the spirit mountains and seas of the Northern Sea cultivation world into his magic treasure and refine it into a spirit treasure."

In the end, Luo Chen reached this astonishing conclusion.

Do not look at how spirit treasures seemed worthless inside Demon-Fall Land.

That was because Demon-Fall Land was the palace of Devil Lord Heaven-Refining, an artifact-forging expert at the Void Refinement realm.

In truth, before Luo Chen stepped into Demon-Fall Land, he had simply never heard the name of any powerful spirit treasure.

The closest thing to a spirit treasure was the Yuantu blood sword now in his hand.

What was a spirit treasure?

Only a magic treasure that had given birth to self-awareness, breathed in and out heaven-and-earth origin qi, and even contained the true meaning of laws could be called a spirit treasure.

If one looked only at the latter part of this description, did it not closely resemble a certain realm?

Correct, an Ascendant great power!

A powerful spirit treasure was itself an existence corresponding to the Ascendant realm.

For a cultivator to achieve Ascendant was already difficult beyond difficulty. Where would one have so much energy to nurture another "Ascendant existence"?

Therefore, in the vast Mountains and Seas Realm, spirit treasures famous enough to be named were few and far between.

Even if they existed, they would only exist within the Five Great Holy Lands.

Now, Li Canghai intended to do this great rebellious thing: refine all the named grotto-heavens and blessed lands of the Northern Sea cultivation world into a single seal and achieve the might of a spirit treasure.

If this matter truly succeeded, Luo Chen did not know how powerful the Mountain-Sea Seal would become, but the Northern Sea cultivation world would likely plummet from then on and no longer have spirit lands suitable for Sage cultivators.

"Alas..."

A sigh sounded. Luo Chen's full heart of excitement finally turned into a sigh of feeling.

Even if he could infer the whole from one leaf and vaguely glimpse Li Canghai's purpose, what use was that?

He could neither expose it nor profit from it.

He could only bury this secret in his heart.

Only, he had also inadvertently developed a similar thought.

"If one day I succeed in becoming an Ascendant, and if the timing is suitable, would I be unable to resist imitating Li Canghai's action?"

The answer was unknown.

...

The Sword-Halberd continued forward.

This time, with no demon-beast force trailing them, the journey was very smooth, and they drew closer and closer to Sun-Moon Island.

The low-realm cultivators on the ship had no way to personally spy on the Ice Pole Sect battle. They could only learn what had happened through the demon beasts arrayed outside the Sword-Halberd and the oral accounts of their elders.

A top Sage upper sect of a generation had scattered like clouds before the wind.

The high-level cultivators, however, gathered even more frequently.

The next day, inside Fu Chaosheng's Blue Tide Palace, the Ice Pole Sect battle was being intensely discussed.

There were seven Sages in total, and only Luo Chen was absent.

But as the main doors opened, everyone looked over, and the final absent Sage personally entered the hall.

"Wilderness Wanderer, Luo Chen!"

Fu Chaosheng gave a simple introduction.

Luo Chen nodded to everyone and calmly took his seat.

Afterward, he acted as a listener while everyone intensely discussed yesterday's battle.

"Even an ancient old monster like the demon monk Kongchan has appeared. It seems the Northern Sea demon beasts are determined to counterattack our human territory!"

"Now that there is no Primordial Demon Sect suppressing the Northern Sea, and that Great Power Li is not one to manage affairs much, the Northern Sea is in danger!"

"Does your esteemed sect truly have no thoughts about this matter?"

This esteemed sect naturally meant the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

To this, Fu Chaosheng only smiled without speaking.

From the smile that had hung on his face year-round, everyone could not see the slightest bit of information. Helpless, they could only continue.

"This old man has never in his life seen a Sage battle of such scale. Five famous human Sages, seven Sage-level demon cultivators..."

Luo Chen glanced at Fu Chaosheng's smile, and his heart gradually formed an understanding.

It was not that the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect had no thoughts. Rather, they had likely made hidden arrangements long ago, and corresponding backup plans had all been arranged one by one.

The Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance was the arrangement in the open, while the Penglai Immortal Sect was the hidden backup concealed for thousands of years, only surfacing during the righteous-demonic war.

Only, when the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect would formally begin interfering in the Northern Sea cultivation world was unclear.

"This seat knew Ghost Immortal Tower was very strong, but in the end, he is only within the ranks of fourth-tier desolate beasts, equivalent to the same realm as we Sages. Why was the dignified great cultivator Daoist Extreme Cold so unable to withstand him?"

"One roar. Merely the force of one roar, and Daoist Extreme Cold was captured without resistance!"

"Indeed. That scene nearly shook the conviction in this old man's heart. A late-stage Sage great cultivator absolutely counts as peerless among people like us. How could he be defeated so easily?"

What everyone discussed most was not the demon monk Kongchan arrayed before them, nor the Ascendant great power Li Canghai, who had appeared like a fleeting glimpse.

It was that extreme battle that had occurred in a very short time!

The desolate beast Ghost Immortal Tower against Ice Pole Sect grand elder Daoist Extreme Cold.

The latter's defeat had been far too tragic!

In fact, it had been so complete that they did not dare imagine it, for fear it would shake their faith in the Sage Dao.

This time, Fu Chaosheng gave a quiet sigh.

"Daoist Extreme Cold was already quite impressive. He did everything he could."

Immediately, he shook his head and said, "Do all of you know what kind of existence is strongest in the Sage realm?"

Everyone subconsciously wanted to answer late-stage Sage great cultivators, but when they thought of the battle result from before, everyone's mouth seemed to be blocked.

Fu Chaosheng stroked his beard. "Yes, it is not the great cultivator we usually think of, but an existence that has comprehended the true meaning of laws!"

Zhou Yunshen could not help asking, "Can one truly comprehend the true meaning of laws in the Sage realm without losing oneself in heaven and earth?"

Fu Chaosheng asked in reply, "If one does not comprehend it, how can one become Ascendant?"

Zhou Yunshen's expression stiffened.

Such essentials of cultivation had always been debated with various opinions.

Some said one had to first form the primordial spirit, using the powerful primordial spirit to hold fast to the original self, and only then could one comprehend heaven-and-earth laws without hindrance. This was to become Ascendant first, then comprehend the Dao.

Others said that comprehending heaven-and-earth laws came first, and only by stepping across this threshold could one become a true Ascendant great power. This was to comprehend the Dao first, then break through.

The difference between the two faintly divided into two cultivation paths.

From the mouths of the six Northern Sea Sages, Luo Chen obtained the former answer.

From Fu Chaosheng, a disciple of a holy land, he obtained the latter answer.

The former was the broad, level road for those of ordinary comprehension and excellent aptitude, with relatively fewer risks.

The latter tested comprehension to the extreme, yet it was the path most revered by holy land disciples.

"The reason I say Daoist Extreme Cold had already done everything he could is that there is precedent."

"All of you may not know this, but in the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world not long ago, a similar battle once occurred."

"There was a peerless Demon Emperor whose true body was a divine bird, an Azure Luan. She was only at the seventh level of the Sage stage, yet she instantly defeated five great Sages and killed her way into the human heartland as if entering an uninhabited realm. Even Zhang Che, a Nether Origin Sect genius at the eighth level of the Sage stage, did not dare act rashly before her and could only watch helplessly as she killed."

"And what that Demon Emperor relied on was precisely the true meaning of spatial laws she had comprehended in advance!"

"By comparison, Daoist Extreme Cold pointed from afar and blasted open Ghost Immortal Tower's head, then finally forcibly sent his Nascent Soul out and preserved a thread of life. How can one call that pathetic?"

Everyone listened with expressions full of wonder.

As people standing among the very top of the Mountains and Seas Realm, their intelligence channels were not poor.

But compared to the Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance, which conducted business across all four continents, the difference was heaven and earth.

They more or less knew some things about the Eastern Wilderness war, but only now did they truly learn a bit of the inside story.

Therefore, each of them began questioning him about the true situation at the time.

Luo Chen was also extremely curious.

At that time, he had only been early-stage Adept, and had long since become a prisoner. He simply had not known the outside situation.

Now, from Fu Chaosheng's mouth, he could truly view that battle from a higher perspective.

"That divine bird Azure Luan was named Azure Frost."

"Azure Frost's strength exceeded all of your imagination!"

"Instantly defeating five great Sages and intimidating Zhang Che was only one small line among her many battle achievements."

"To deal with her, the Eastern Wilderness holy land Nether Origin Sect assembled three great cultivators and killed into the Million Mountains. But beneath Green Parasol Mountain, the three of them still were not Azure Frost Demon Emperor's match."

"Those three great cultivators were absolutely not weak. Their true ability was only about equal to Daoist Extreme Cold's, and what they lacked was only geographical advantage."

"Even they were defeated. From this, you know what kind of monster a Sage cultivator who comprehends the true meaning of laws in advance is."

"Later, the Nether Origin Sect was even forced to personally dispatch an Ascendant great power."

At this point, he stopped abruptly.

Everyone present felt as if their hearts were being scratched, and they could not help asking again and again.

What happened after that?

Fu Chaosheng shook his head. "The more detailed inside story is not something this old man's authority can know. I only know that in that battle, Demon Emperor Azure Frost did not die, but the feather-race holy land Green Parasol Mountain strangely vanished."

Everyone grew somewhat dispirited.

Luo Chen likewise showed such an expression, but his heart was as clear as ice.

In that final battle at Green Parasol Mountain, Demon Emperor Azure Frost had indeed fully displayed her ability. Even in the hands of the Nether Origin great power, she had held on for several rounds.

Later, she had even activated Rosycloud's method, the Nirvana Sacred Flame, and forced the Nether Origin great power back.

Thinking to this point, he could not help comparing Demon Emperor Azure Frost and the desolate beast Ghost Immortal Tower.

Which was stronger?

Demon Emperor Azure Frost had mastered spatial laws and came and went like a ghost. Her body-refining attainments, viewed by the current Luo Chen at fourth-tier Archaic, were also world-shaking and terrifying.

The desolate beast Ghost Immortal Tower appeared to have a powerful fleshly body, but judging from Daoist Extreme Cold piercing his head with one finger, he did not seem to excel in this area.

The place where he was truly formidable was somewhat unexpected.

It was the dao of soul refinement!

Whether the ghostly phantasms beside him that could throw cultivators' divine sense into chaos, or the soul attack that shook Sage divine souls with a single roar, all of it explained this point.

If the two truly faced one another, perhaps the latter would be stronger than... no, Azure Frost had battle achievements to trace. She could hold on for several rounds even under a Nether Origin great power, while Ghost Immortal Tower's roar against Li Canghai was no more than a clear breeze brushing the face.

Subconsciously, Luo Chen placed Azure Frost, whose realm was relatively lower, on the stronger side.

Perhaps it was because of her battle record. Perhaps because he had once been captured alive by her. Perhaps because of his reverence toward Green Parasol Mountain. Luo Chen very much did not want to face those women of Green Parasol Mountain.

But if he truly returned to the Eastern Wilderness, could he avoid those people?

If he truly faced them, what could he rely on to prevent the shame of becoming a prisoner from repeating?

Luo Chen's fair, handsome face tightened, and he bid everyone farewell.

This was somewhat unexpected. He had come without saying a single word, and now he was leaving?

But considering his consistently solitary style of conduct, no one spoke to persuade him.

Fu Chaosheng only comforted him with one sentence. "We will soon arrive at Sun-Moon Island. At that time, we will take the teleportation formation back to Central Continent, and the Northern Sea's winds and clouds will no longer have anything to do with you. Do not think too much."

Luo Chen smiled and nodded. His tense expression seemed to ease.

...

The moment he returned to Kunxiao Palace's sealed chamber, Luo Chen threw himself impatiently into cultivation.

Through frequent pill refinement, he had already raised the Hongyuan Pill's proficiency to the Proficient level and could now refine middle-grade Hongyuan Pills.

Under assisted cultivation, this directly increased his original cultivation speed by a large margin.

According to his inference, if he used high-grade Hongyuan Pills to assist cultivation, he would certainly be able to shorten the time originally needed to advance to the second level of the Sage stage, seven or eight years, to four or five years.

If such speed were spoken aloud, probably no one would believe it.

But this was the true cultivation speed under the combination of terrifying aptitude and terrifying alchemy, so fast it seemed like a dream.

Yet when Luo Chen ended cultivation deep in the night, his face did not show any joy or ease.

"Not enough. Still not enough!"

"Relying on these alone is far from enough for me to catch up with those women!"

Quantified cultivation speed made one feel grounded, but it also let Luo Chen recognize with incomparable clarity how enormous the gap was between himself and those very top experts.

That was not something that could be smoothed away by a mere few years or decades.

"I need stronger methods!"

"Sword, cauldron, armor. With these three treasures at my side, I have no further need of external objects."

"The Dao of formations is powerful, but complex and slow. In a battle, it is also too late to respond."

"The White Tiger Puppet can serve as one hidden card, but true late-stage Sage great cultivators were no match for Demon Emperor Azure Frost. I fear the white tiger will not do either."

"It cannot all be external objects. I need to master a true killing move."

"Arts!"

Luo Chen's restless heart seemed to find something to rely on, and instantly calmed.

Only spells and arts, which had accompanied him through battle all the way and could be used at any time, gave him enough confidence when facing anyone.

Even Han Zhan, who had killed his way into Luo Chen's sea of consciousness, had been defeated by his Dragon-Slaying Art.

Yes, divine-soul arts!

Luo Chen seemed to have found a direction, and also seemed to have grasped a life-saving straw.

Emotionally, he felt Azure Frost was stronger, but rationally, he also felt Ghost Immortal Tower could fight Azure Frost.

His confidence in Ghost Immortal Tower lay in those divine-soul attacks.

"If I can master a similarly powerful spell, can I catch others unprepared, fight, and defeat them?"

Luo Chen's eyes narrowed slightly, and his mind continuously recalled every movement Ghost Immortal Tower had made during the Ice Pole Sect battle.

Beneath his long, narrow lashes, Mirror Flower Water Moon moved of its own accord, and layer upon layer of ghostly phantasms whirled around, like illusions of immortals dancing.

Later, as his magic power surged, baleful qi coiled around him.

Even the source power hidden deep within his flesh began circulating on its own.

The illusions in his eyes slowly spread from inside to outside.

In a daze, Luo Chen murmured to himself.

"Is it like this?"

*(End of Chapter)*
