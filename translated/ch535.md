# Chapter 535: Advancing by Leaps and Bounds, Han Zhan Awakens

Luo Chen felt a lingering chill in his heart.

He had not expected the medicinal power of the True Flame Pill to be so tyrannical!

It was not merely a matter of quantity.

Its tyranny lay even more in the impact of its quality.

If the medicinal nature of the Starfield Pill was like a sky full of stars, letting the pill-taker pluck them at will, then the True Flame Pill was a blazing sun. There was only one of it, yet its radiance shone so fiercely that there was nowhere to hide.

If ordinary cultivation pills were like thin streams, slowly absorbed without harming a Core Formation cultivator's foundation, then the True Flame Pill was like a surging rapid, rampaging through the meridians and qi sea without the slightest care for the pill-taker's condition.

Taken together, this True Flame Pill actually carried three parts of a "breakthrough-type pill's" nature.

Its defining feature was not gentle, enduring medicinal power suited for absorption.

Instead, it was famed for violent medicinal power that pried open the body's realm.

As everyone knew, in the cultivation world, although breakthrough-type pills contained enormous medicinal power, they were also the least suited for long-term auxiliary cultivation.

After all, prying open the body's realm was usually aimed at a higher realm, but if taken over the long term, it could also make the original realm unstable, causing incidents of realm regression from time to time.

Because of this, Luo Chen finally understood a doubt that had long weighed on his mind.

"No wonder few Yan Alliance cultivators use True Flame Pills for cultivation, yet they have always maintained a small amount of True Flame Pill refinement. They use this pill as a breakthrough pill!"

As he pondered this, Luo Chen could not help feeling a wave of relief.

Fortunately, his foundation was solid enough!

Otherwise, if he were an ordinary Core Formation cultivator, he probably would not dare use True Flame Pills in large quantities over the long term to aid cultivation.

There was no other reason. Luo Chen's meridians, acupoints, and even his qi sea had all been laid with an incomparably vigorous foundation back during the Foundation Establishment stage.

Once he condensed his Golden Core, that solid foundation had increased severalfold.

Over the long years of cultivation, its benefits had become more and more apparent.

In addition, his physique, comparable to that of a demon beast, could fully withstand the True Flame Pill's impact.

"Now let me search for where that medicinal balefulness is."

With a thought, Luo Chen spread his divine sense throughout his entire body, carefully searching bit by bit for the rumored fire balefulness of the True Flame Pill.

A long time passed before Luo Chen's expression stirred.

"Found it. To think it was hidden so deeply, attached to the Golden Core!"

A cultivator's Golden Core was the most important place, yet also the place most easily overlooked.

If Luo Chen had not faintly sensed some sluggishness in the circulation of his mana, he would nearly have failed to discover this abnormal trace.

It was precisely because of this that Luo Chen now understood why Yan Alliance cultivators, despite clearly being skilled in fire arts, did not dare take True Flame Pills for cultivation over the long term.

This fire-attribute medicinal balefulness was actually attached to the Golden Core.

Who would dare joke around with something like that?

But now that he had found it, things were easy!

Through Luo Chen's experiments, he gradually obtained three methods to remove the medicinal balefulness.

One was to slowly wear it away with mana. This was comparatively slow, and was probably also the common method Yan Alliance cultivators used after borrowing the True Flame Pill to break through, then removing the medicinal balefulness afterward.

Another was the Fine Dust Primordial Art, which had incorporated the Clear Origin Wondrous Pill Method. When circulating this art, it would passively scrape a little medicinal balefulness off the Golden Core, but the effect was minimal.

"As expected, medicinal balefulness is not medicinal poison. Poison-clearing methods have very little effect."

Luo Chen had anticipated this.

After all, the fire-attribute medicinal balefulness left behind by the True Flame Pill was itself part of its medicinal nature. Without this medicinal balefulness, that three-part "breakthrough" ability would not exist.

Fortunately, Luo Chen still had one final method.

In the quiet cultivation chamber, Luo Chen's hands swiftly formed seals. Amid the dazzling motions, a spell took shape according to a special route of mana circulation.

Then, slowly and with great care, he tapped it onto his own lower abdomen.

The next moment, in his qi sea, a small knife blazing with flames appeared.

The small knife chopped down at the Golden Core!

A faint wisp of aura separated from it.

The Golden Core spun around and around, as if it had no idea what fear was.

Afterward, the small knife chopped again.

More abnormal aura was peeled away, cut by cut.

Then Luo Chen held in a breath until his face swelled crimson, nearly purple, before he suddenly spat it out.

Hiss...

A scorched black hole immediately appeared in the ground.

Seeing this scene, Luo Chen was both delighted and startled.

He was delighted because the fourth-tier Dragon-Slaying Art was indeed effective at clearing medicinal balefulness, and its effect was excellent!

What startled him was that the True Flame Pill's medicinal balefulness was actually so vicious.

When it was attached to the Golden Core, it had not shown much, but once spat out, its savage toxicity fully manifested.

It was hard to imagine what miserable fate the pill-taker's Golden Core would suffer if the pill were taken over the long term without clearing the medicinal balefulness.

Looking at the scorched black hole on the floor, Luo Chen fell silent.

Only after a long while did he lightly nod.

"This medicinal balefulness is incomparably vicious. If only one or two strands are tainted, perhaps they can be worn away by patient grinding."

"But if I collect a thousand strands, even ten thousand strands, and use them against enemies..."

...

After receiving the assistance of eighteen "medicine apprentices," the time Luo Chen spent processing medicinal materials was completely freed up.

As a result, his cultivation in other areas made substantial progress.

Whether defensive spells or tempering his physique.

His lifebound magic treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, also began to gradually radiate brilliance under day after day of nurturing and sacrificial refinement. Its power became increasingly apparent, and Luo Chen wielded it with greater ease.

Not only that, but the two martial arts that had been stalled for many years also drew closer to perfection day by day as he occasionally found time to practice them in nearby sea regions.

His own cultivation advanced even more swiftly!

With the aid of a third-tier spirit land, he could cultivate without holding anything back.

A cultivation speed comparable to a heavenly spirit root!

The increasingly proficient refinement method for True Flame Pills produced higher-grade True Flame Pills.

And his understanding of the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture grew deeper and deeper.

With all these factors together, in merely two or three years, Luo Chen felt himself drawing closer and closer to mid Core Formation.

Of course.

Since his progress was so obvious, his influence over the Swallow Islands also became increasingly profound.

First was the Cheng clan.

In order to exchange for the Azure Yang Alchemy Canon on Invitation Moon Island, Cheng Dou had gone nearly mad, sparing no cost to collect all kinds of medicinal materials for Luo Chen.

These did not merely include the raw materials required for True Flame Pills.

They also included a portion of the raw materials required to refine Nascent Soul Pills!

Just as when Luo Chen had first entered Foundation Establishment, he had already been considering Core Formation, now that he was almost at mid Core Formation, he naturally had to consider future Nascent Soul formation.

In his view, Nascent Soul formation required only three conditions.

The first was sufficient personal accumulation. The second was a suitable fourth-tier spirit vein land. The last was the icing on the cake, the Nascent Soul Pill.

Especially since he still could not return to the Eastern Wasteland anytime soon, he might as well borrow the hands of the Swallow Islands cultivators to help him gather Nascent Soul Pill materials.

When the time came, he could slowly feel his way forward. Sooner or later, he would be able to refine the Nascent Soul Pill.

Only, with things like this, the various great clans of the Swallow Islands suffered.

The True Flame Pills alone cost no small sum, let alone the enormous quantity of medicinal materials required for Nascent Soul Pills.

Those small clans might have misgivings, but Cheng Dou did not care at all.

Without Yan Nantian's suppression, even though he spent years traveling outside, his realm still pushed bit by bit to Foundation Establishment Perfected Completion.

Core Formation was close at hand!

Looking across the entire Swallow Islands, they could not produce even one respectable Core Formation secret art. In the past, Swallow Islands cultivators had formed cores purely by relying on their own accumulation and heaven's favor.

Now that they had the Azure Yang Alchemy Canon, how could Cheng Dou not risk everything?

And it was not only him.

Among the thirty-six island groups, every clan with a late Foundation Establishment great cultivator of suitable age was sparing no cost to collect medicinal materials for Luo Chen, trying to exchange for the Azure Yang Alchemy Canon as soon as possible.

Under these circumstances, this small cultivation world in a remote sea region fell into a kind of sickly frenzy.

Every clan's reserves, wealth, and manpower were being continuously consumed.

Everything they consumed was ultimately transformed into countless medicinal materials, flowing toward the little island in the ten-li calm lake.

The strength of every great clan did not rise. Instead, it stagnated.

If there were not temporarily no external enemies, if Devil Lord Azure Yang were not suppressing things from above, and if they were not avoiding mutual conflict and internal strife, then forget stagnation, decline would even be possible.

Under such strange circumstances, those small clans with only one or two Foundation Establishment cultivators naturally trembled in fear.

But there were also discerning people who understood the logic within.

Since Devil Lord Azure Yang dared take out a Core Formation method to drive the masses of cultivators, it showed that he had the breadth to tolerate others and did not mind a Core Formation cultivator appearing under his command.

And the various great clans were all Foundation Establishment clans.

Once any clan produced a Core Formation cultivator first, it could immediately stand out from the rest and suppress the other clans.

Even the Cheng clan, which had the best relationship with Devil Lord Azure Yang, would lose the initiative if it were one step slower than others.

They were all in a race!

The various clans raced, and in the end, the only one who benefited most was Devil Lord Azure Yang.

With nothing more than a single Azure Yang Alchemy Canon, he had stirred the originally peaceful thirty-six Swallow Islands until the people were exhausted and the wealth was depleted. He truly deserved to be called a Devil Lord of his generation!

...

As for the madness in the outside world, and the slander against him, Luo Chen knew nothing. Even if he did know, he would ignore it all.

He immersed himself in his own little world, feeling his growth at every moment. How could he not be pleased!

If there were no limit on his lifespan, and if he had no worries for Hui Niang, Caiyi, Wang Yuan, Duan Feng, and the others, Luo Chen might even want to sink into this forever.

That feeling of constantly being able to sense his own growth was truly intoxicating.

Before he knew it, in a daze, three years slipped by in the blink of an eye.

On this day, a streak of escaping light flew back from beyond the sea. After a few flashes over the Swallow Islands, it descended onto the central island.

Cloud and mist parted. A tall woman stood at the palace entrance, watching the newcomer approach step by step with a smiling expression.

"Senior, you seem to be in a good mood today!"

Luo Chen laughed heartily. "Naturally. I have cultivated another major method to perfection, so of course my mood is excellent!"

As he spoke, he strode into the main hall with the bearing of a dragon and a tiger.

Cheng Haixin followed behind him, the respect in her eyes even thicker.

For these three years, she had essentially eaten and lived on Invitation Moon Island, devoting herself wholeheartedly to handling all kinds of miscellaneous affairs for Luo Chen.

Whether receiving medicinal materials sent by each clan, providing blood food to Luo Chen at fixed times and places, or carrying out the most important medicinal material processing work, she was basically the one responsible for all of it.

After three years, the "Fairy Haixin" who had only known how to cultivate carefree under the clan's protection had vanished. In her place was the capable, perceptive, tall, and dignified "Invitation Moon Fairy".

Only, this "dignity" of hers amounted to nothing before Luo Chen.

These three years, she had personally watched Luo Chen's dao attainment advance day by day.

From time to time, he would gain some small insight. Occasionally, when he took her out to sea to test spells and magic treasures, the scenes were often heaven-shaking and earth-shattering.

This speed of progress simply exceeded her fixed impression of Core Formation Seniors.

What happened to Core Formation Seniors needing decades, or even a hundred years, whenever they cultivated a prized divine ability or spell?

Her own master would gain a little insight in three to five days, advance by leaps and bounds in seven or eight months, and reach major accomplishment in a year or two.

And yet what he cultivated was not the sort of method that looked obviously quick to learn at a glance, but major methods that advanced steadily and had a solid foundation!

Perhaps this was what a Devil Lord of his generation was like!

Under Cheng Haixin's reverent gaze, Luo Chen stopped in his tracks. His eyes narrowed slightly, as if sensing something.

A moment later, he nodded in satisfaction.

"Muxian is not bad. He has already refined first-tier middle-grade Condensed Water Pills."

Just now, he had smelled an extremely faint pill fragrance.

When his divine sense spread out, he saw a young man in a small pill chamber joyfully collecting pills.

That was none other than Xu Muxian.

Cheng Haixin smiled gently. "All of this depends on Senior's cultivation."

"Muxian's alchemy talent is outstanding. That is why he has such progress."

Luo Chen modestly said one sentence, though the smugness on his face was not particularly concealed.

Without his cultivation, where would Xu Muxian get so many low-tier medicinal materials to practice with?

Without his guidance, how could the other party refine middle-grade pills in a mere three years?

With Luo Chen's present knowledge and ability, nurturing a talented seedling into an alchemist was simply too easy.

Moreover, he did not even need to spend his own resources!

"Here are ten bottles of supreme-grade Condensed Water Pills. Give them to Xu Muxian!"

Cheng Haixin was startled. "Senior, he has advanced to Qi Refinement Stage Six in three years. That is already fast enough. Why would he need supreme-grade Condensed Water Pills? Ordinary high-grade ones are already luxurious enough."

Luo Chen shook his head. "In any case, he can be counted as my nominal disciple. He should receive some benefits. Besides, I refined these Condensed Water Pills in my leisure time. Keeping them has no great use. Oh, right."

As he spoke, Luo Chen took several more jade bottles from his storage ring and stuffed them into Cheng Haixin's arms.

"Jade Dew Pills. You used them before. These six bottles of high-grade ones are enough for you to use for half a year."

Hearing this, Cheng Haixin's breathing could not help turning hurried.

High-grade Jade Dew Pills!

Previously, when Luo Chen had nothing to do, he had refined some pills, and Jade Dew Pills were among them.

At that time, Luo Chen had casually rewarded some to Cheng Haixin, who had been working like an ox and horse for him.

After the woman took them, she advanced to Foundation Establishment Stage One that very day.

Later, relying on the remaining Jade Dew Pills, and pairing them with a second-tier high-grade cave abode assigned to her on Invitation Moon Island, her cultivation speed advanced by leaps and bounds in three years, once leaping all the way to mid Foundation Establishment!

Such progress had truly stunned every cultivator in the thirty-six Swallow Islands!

It was precisely because of this that she received the alias Invitation Moon Fairy.

Taking the name of Luo Chen's Invitation Moon Island as her dao title displayed just how much favor she had received from Luo Chen.

Cheng Haixin was not displeased by this. She accepted it gladly.

And this time, what Luo Chen gave her was high-grade Jade Dew Pills!

If used well, perhaps this year, she, Cheng Haixin, could advance to Foundation Establishment Stage Five!

She had dual spirit root aptitude. At six, she had read Daoist scriptures, nourishing qi and refining her body to lay a foundation. At twelve, she had officially begun cultivation. Under the clan's cultivation, she had spent twenty-four years barely succeeding in Foundation Establishment.

That had already been a flying speed.

If she advanced to Foundation Establishment Stage Five five years after Foundation Establishment, it would simply be too inconceivable!

For a time, the woman's breathing was hurried, and her heavy chest could not help trembling violently.

The more she spent time with him, the more the name Devil Lord Azure Yang seemed undeserved!

How was he acting like one of the demonic path?

He was clearly a benevolent elder, a senior of the immortal path.

He rarely blamed low-level cultivators and fully displayed the bearing of an expert who supported juniors.

The world truly misunderstood him far too deeply.

Luo Chen glanced at her, then stepped forward. The woman followed behind him in something of a daze.

"Also, your Cheng clan's contribution value is about enough. You can notify your elder brother to come beneath the Invitation Moon Stele and receive the Azure Yang Alchemy Canon initiation."

After saying this, Luo Chen disappeared into the depths of the palace complex.

The woman stood frozen in place as if struck heavily.

Only after a long while did she bow deeply.

"Many thanks, my lord!"

With that, she hurriedly left the island and flew toward Black Swan Island.

...

On Invitation Moon Island, Luo Chen watched that departing streak of escaping light and could not help shaking his head with a light laugh.

Over three or four years, for the sake of the Azure Yang Alchemy Canon, the Cheng clan could be said to have suffered a great injury to its vitality.

That share that had seemed to be only five hundred thousand per clan, when the many small clans had the will but not the strength, had all been distributed among the few great clans intent on Core Formation.

Among them, the Cheng clan had been the most enthusiastic.

Therefore, they had also paid the most.

Not only had they spent a million, they had also completely stopped cultivating the clan's juniors.

These years, all the Cheng clan's clan production had fallen into collecting and purchasing medicinal materials.

That demon-hunting ship had also been activated frequently, traveling far into the deep ocean regions to capture large second-tier sea demons.

Even so, their contribution value was actually still a little short.

Luo Chen had merely opened a back door because Cheng Haixin had truly worked with wholehearted diligence these past few years and helped him a great deal.

"However, my Azure Yang Alchemy Canon is not easy to cultivate!"

Thinking of Cheng Dou's somewhat impetuous, even occasionally crazed temperament, Luo Chen curled his lip.

The Azure Yang Alchemy Canon was derived from the Fine Dust Primordial Art.

Even though most of it had been simplified, its cultivation difficulty remained extremely high. He was not very optimistic that this current batch of Swallow Islands cultivators could rely on it to successfully form cores.

On the contrary, Cheng Haixin, who stayed by his side, absorbing what she saw and heard and occasionally receiving his guidance, had a somewhat greater chance of forming a core.

"I wonder, with my ability, can I nurture a dual-spirit-root cultivator to the Golden Core stage within a hundred years?"

The corners of Luo Chen's mouth rose slightly, as if he were conducting some experiment.

As always, the resources being spent were not his anyway!

He turned and entered the cultivation chamber.

The formation activated, cutting off spying from the outside while gathering the nearby spirit qi to the greatest possible extent inside.

On the attribute panel, his first-tier spirit-gathering formation had already reached Perfected Completion!

With the support of the various great clans of the Swallow Islands, he had also established a large second-tier spirit-gathering formation around Invitation Moon Island, drawing in even more heaven and earth spirit qi.

As a result, although the spirit vein rank on this island was still third-tier low-grade,

the density of its spirit qi was already infinitely close to third-tier middle-grade.

And this was one of the reasons his dao attainment improved day by day, drawing closer and closer to Core Formation Stage Four.

Sitting cross-legged in meditation, Luo Chen opened his mouth and spat. A small cauldron appeared before him. It grew in the wind, and in the blink of an eye, it spun into a size of one zhang.

Looking at the Primordial Unity Cauldron, whose mental connection with him had grown increasingly close, Luo Chen revealed a satisfied expression.

If evaluated by the standards of the Foundation Establishment stage, his current sacrificial refinement of this cauldron had already reached ten-tenths!

It was enough to bring out all of this treasure's power to the utmost.

However, this very clearly was not the true "utmost."

A good magic treasure could amplify a cultivator's strength.

Especially a lifebound magic treasure that matched the cultivator's attributes!

That kind of amplification could be onefold, twofold, even severalfold!

Very clearly, the Primordial Unity Cauldron still could not achieve such amplification at present.

The problem lay in the dozen-plus portions of unprocessed raw materials it had absorbed during refinement.

Too many impurities!

"Continue the sacrificial refinement!"

Luo Chen sighed. Mana surged out, and divine sense coiled around it.

Not only that, he opened his mouth and spat again. Wisps of azure flame drifted out, transforming into nine fire dragons that wrapped around the small cauldron and exhaled azure flames.

This was him imitating Min Longyu's Nine-Turn Flame Dragon Grand Formation from back then, using it to sacrificially refine his lifebound magic treasure.

The only pity was that he did not have the formation diagram for the Nine-Turn Flame Dragon Grand Formation. This method had only its form and none of its spirit.

Even so, it was much faster than an ordinary sacrificial refinement method.

"I need to find time to search for the formation diagram of the Nine-Turn Flame Dragon Grand Formation."

A thought flashed past, and Luo Chen continued refining the magic treasure.

This was what he did daily. Only when his mana was almost depleted would he stop, then cultivate his lifebound cultivation art to replenish his mana while also improving his cultivation.

With such outflow and inflow, his rapidly advancing realm would also become more and more solid.

After many years of cultivation, Luo Chen had already grasped the true knack of consolidating his foundation.

Time slowly flowed by.

Just as Luo Chen's mana was running short and he was about to withdraw his azure flame, his mind suddenly stirred.

The next moment, the azure flames abruptly blazed higher, wrapping the Primordial Unity Cauldron layer upon layer.

Nine fire dragon heads stretched forward, looking down at the space inside the cauldron with predatory vigilance.

Luo Chen sighed softly.

"Senior, since you have already awakened, why not meet me openly and honorably? Why spy on Luo in secret instead?"

From within the cauldron came a faint, bitter laugh.

"Young friend, with you setting out such a formation, how would this old man dare rashly show his head?"

(End of chapter)
