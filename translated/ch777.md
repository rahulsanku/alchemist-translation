# Chapter 777: When the Sect Is in Trouble, How Can One Sit Idly By

The Ten Duels were scheduled over five days.

Two matches per day, one in the morning and one in the afternoon.

As the afternoon arrived, the dueling platform at Lingtian Pass, personally established by a Sage, welcomed the second internal struggle of the Luo Tian Sect.

On one side was Min Longyu, bearing the Dao name Thunder Arrayzi, from the lineage of the current Sect Leader Yao Mingyue. Legend had it that when he first entered the Initiate stage, he had used a single thunderous formation to blast five cultivators of the same rank to death! Over a century later, Thunder Arrayzi's cultivation had only grown stronger, his realm now at the Adept stage. He was the Luo Tian Sect's foremost formation master, renowned even within Lingtian Pass.

His opponent, however, was Hong Ling, personally invited by Si Wenjun!

Many titles hung upon this woman's head, but when all was said and done, a single word could describe everything about her: "Sword Cultivator!"

The crowd awaited this battle with great anticipation.

But the outcome of the battle was sealed from the very beginning.

*Ssshhh! Ssshhh! Ssshhh!*

On the dueling platform, sword qi surged in the thousands. The attacks Min Longyu unleashed crumbled into disarray under the onslaught of that rushing sword qi. The formations he had meticulously prepared were torn apart, scattered and broken.

He could only hunker down within a defensive formation, struggling to hold on.

"You are no match for me!"

"Back when I crossed blows with your Grand Supreme Elder, you had not even condensed your Adept core. Why struggle so stubbornly?"

"If you persist in this delusion, do not blame me for showing no mercy with my sword!"

A sharp cry came from all directions.

The overwhelming sword momentum showed no sign of decline; instead, it grew even fiercer, battering Min Longyu's protective barrier until it swayed precariously.

Min Longyu, his hair now stark white, clenched his teeth, continuously channeling his mana to keep the defensive formation he had prepared in advance from being broken.

But that alone had already consumed all his mental energy.

Seeing that his opponent's momentum was overwhelming, he steeled his heart and pulled a formation plate from his bosom.

Just then.

"We surrender!"

A voice came from below the stage.

The sword momentum vanished instantly. The thousands of sword qi strands converged in an instant, forming a single pellet that disappeared into Hong Ling's mouth.

Before leaving the high platform, she cast a deep glance at the formation plate in Min Longyu's hand, which had yet to be activated. A hint of wariness flickered in her eyes.

Min Longyu descended from the dueling platform, his face twisted with fury as he looked at Gu Caiyi.

"Give me an explanation!"

"You cannot die up there," Gu Caiyi said calmly.

"Why not? Li Yingzhang was willing to sacrifice his cultivation base and suffer a severe realm drop just to bite into the enemy. Why can't I? Do you have that little faith in me?" Min Longyu's face was contorted with rage. His hand clenched so tightly that blood dripped from his palm.

Clearly, he had been prepared to throw everything away for this battle.

To that end, he had been willing to use all his essence blood to activate his lifebound formation plate and take that sword cultivator down with him!

"That Hong Ling was once a peer of my husband, a strong contender from the same generation. Your specialty lies in formations, which require advance preparation. This dueling platform is simply not suited for your style of combat. Losing to her is no disgrace."

"You know I don't care about disgrace or not!"

Gu Caiyi did not want to argue with him in public any longer. With a curt "We'll talk back at the residence," she led the others away.

As the Luo Tian Sect contingent departed, the post-match discussions did not cease but grew even more heated.

"That's the fourth match, right?"

"Yes, the fourth loss. Thunder Arrayzi's defeat has all but extinguished the confidence that Baleful Dragonzi fought so hard to restore. If they lose two more matches, the power of the Luo Tian Sect will fall into Si Wenjun's hands. By then, this Adept-level sect may cease to exist, swallowed up by the Five Elements Divine Sect. Even if it ends in a five-to-five tie, with the combined pressure from the Five Elements Divine Sect and Motian Cliff, Fenghua Palace will not forcefully protect the Luo Tian Sect."

"A pity. Superior Li Yingzhang unexpectedly got off to a good start, but the subsequent direction has been like this."

"Nothing to pity. Behind Si Wenjun stand two major Sage-level sects, and those two sects can also recruit strong cultivators from other fallen sects to aid him. How could the Luo Tian Sect possibly win based on its own strength alone?"

"Indeed. From the moment the Ten Duels were established, the Luo Tian Sect had already lost. They're just going through the motions, giving Fenghua Palace some face."

"Win or lose, the War Devil Wang Yuan will definitely make his move tomorrow. That's the match I'm most looking forward to!"

"Same here. Everyone has placed heavy bets on the overall score; there's hardly any profit to be made. I just want that War Devil match to be an upset tomorrow, so I can clean up!"

"Heh, the War Devil having an upset? Dream on!"

"Yeah, dream on!"

The name of the War Devil, Wang Yuan, had grown increasingly renowned over the past century.

It was a reputation forged through real blood and fire, battle after battle.

He had the experience of single-handedly protecting the Luo Tian Sect as they fought their way out of the Xiaoyue Mountain Range. He had also charged alone into a sect's mountain gate, surrounded by a crowd of cultivators, and slain a traitorous ally in a stunning feat.

Not long ago, during the complete collapse of the front lines, he had revealed all his capabilities and actually managed to hold off a fourth-rank Demon Emperor.

Even if it was only for a brief moment, it had drawn the world's attention.

A Sage from within Lingtian Pass had once humbled himself to befriend Wang Yuan, even inviting him for a drink.

It could be said that as long as no Sages made a move, they could not imagine how Wang Yuan could possibly lose.

...

Gu Caiyi and Wang Yuan led their people swiftly through Lingtian Pass. They did not leave the city to return to the Luo Tian Sect's temporary encampment in the Fenghua Domain but instead headed toward a rented inn.

The white-haired Min Longyu walked among the crowd, able to feel gazes falling upon him from time to time.

This made his face flush red, a feeling of humiliation washing over him.

But gradually, he began to sense that those gazes were not mocking, nor were they questioning. They were merely expressions of concern from his fellow sect members.

He also slowly came to understand why Gu Caiyi had not allowed him to fight to the death.

Leaving aside whether he could actually take the enemy down with him, his mere death on that platform would be the Luo Tian Sect's greatest loss!

Over the years, the Luo Tian Sect had managed to preserve most of its strength through one escape after another, and his formations had played a huge role in that.

His fellow disciples had been able to maintain their realm advancement even while in exile, thanks in part to the assistance of his formations.

It could be said that if the Luo Tian Sect was destined to continue wandering in the future, anyone could die except for the banner figure Wang Yuan and himself.

Once he understood this, he stopped wallowing in self-pity.

When they reached their destination, he walked over to Gu Caiyi's side.

"My apologies. I was a bit harsh earlier."

Gu Caiyi forced a smile. "It's best that you've come to understand, Junior Brother."

Within the Luo Tian Sect, the various Adept cultivators did not all come from the same master, and most had been rogue cultivators.

But after two hundred years together, they had come to address each other as fellow disciples, a testament to their close bonds.

And because Gu Caiyi had formed her core first, she addressed Min Longyu as Junior Brother.

Min Longyu sighed. "Although I was preserved, our sect has already lost four matches. What are we to do next?"

At these words, everyone's spirits sank even lower.

"We'll discuss it inside!"

Wang Yuan said flatly, walking straight in.

He left behind a silhouette as tall as a tree, as broad as a mountain.

The crowd felt a sense of calm and followed him in.

Before long, the high-ranking members of the Luo Tian Sect who were qualified to participate in major sect affairs gathered in a single room.

There were five of them. Gu Caiyi had not yet arrived.

Min Longyu was carefully inspecting the formation that shielded them from external detection.

Wang Yuan sat with his eyes closed, resting. Baleful Dragonzi stood behind him.

Li Yingzhang, after his first match, had won a narrow victory through a grueling battle but had severely damaged his vitality. He had already been sent back to the mountain gate to recuperate.

The Adept cultivators present now numbered three.

They were Chu Kui, Fu Jiusheng, and Yang Pingdu.

At this moment, Chu Kui wore a look of shame.

"It's my fault. I should have won the second match."

Fu Jiusheng shook his head. "Don't blame yourself. The helpers Si Wenjun brought are all formidable, and he knows our strengths and weaknesses very well. If Junior Brother Li hadn't held something back, he wouldn't have been able to win the first match so unexpectedly."

Li Yingzhang had once been a cultivator from the Li family of Qinhua River. Since joining the Luo Tian Sect, he had kept a low profile.

Few cultivators in the Luo Tian Sect knew that he possessed a special constitution—the Ninefold Winding Body.

His mana had been stronger than his peers even during the Qi Refinement stage.

This ability should have manifested after he reached the Initiate stage.

But because of the battle at Mount Accumulated Thunder, he had been severely wounded. Even though the Grand Supreme Elder Luo Chen had later saved his life, his foundation had been damaged.

Since then, he had grown even more withdrawn.

But few knew that he had devoted a great deal of effort to studying the *Fine Dust Primordial Art*, advancing further than anyone else. He had mastered it and even used it to advance to the Adept stage.

With his Ninefold Winding constitution and the *Fine Dust Primordial Art*, the depth of his mana was beyond ordinary imagination.

Thus, even in his first battle against an enemy of a higher realm, he had used a war of attrition to wear the man down.

But the next three duels in a row had all been losses for the Luo Tian Sect.

Chu Kui had underestimated his opponent.

Fu Jiusheng had been countered.

As for Yang Pingdu, who had joined the Luo Tian Sect later, he was simply outmatched.

If not for Baleful Dragonzi resolutely severing his emotional ties in the fifth match, the Luo Tian Sect might have changed its name by today.

Chu Kui was deeply guilty, not daring to lift his head to look at Wang Yuan and his disciple.

Footsteps came from outside the door.

Everyone looked up as Gu Caiyi pushed the door open and entered.

Chu Kui quickly asked, "How is the old Sect Leader?"

The old Sect Leader, of course, referred to Sima Huiniang.

Gu Caiyi hesitated, then sighed.

"She can fight, but she wants to be saved for last."

Yang Pingdu frowned. "Given the current win-loss record, I'm afraid we won't be able to drag this out to the tenth match with our available manpower!"

"If we can't drag it out, we still have to!" Gu Caiyi's brows shot up, her tone resolute. Especially when she thought of Si Wenjun's smug expression during the day, her disgust deepened. "I'll take the field tomorrow. I want to know how that beast plans to counter me!"

Just then, Wang Yuan, who had been resting with his eyes closed for a long time, slowly opened them.

"It's not suitable for you to fight tomorrow."

"Hmm?" Gu Caiyi was puzzled.

Wang Yuan said calmly, "Unless absolutely necessary, neither you nor Junior Sister Sima should take the field."

Gu Caiyi was confused. "I can understand why Huiniang shouldn't fight. She's the old Sect Leader, and she's Si Wenjun's biological sister. The internal strife of the sect has already become a great joke; sibling killing each other would only be uglier. But what concerns are there about me?"

"You are his Dao partner!"

That one sentence said it all.

Gu Caiyi's face flushed, then paled. She had just sat down, only to stand up again. Her jade finger pointed at Wang Yuan, and she was so angry she couldn't speak for a long time.

Baleful Dragonzi said quietly, "Uncle-Master, leave these matters to us! If it really comes to it, we can ask Fairy Jueqing for help."

"She is not a member of our Luo Tian Sect. Who knows if she might switch sides at a critical moment?"

Gu Caiyi retorted.

Baleful Dragonzi was momentarily speechless.

Wang Yuan tapped the table. When everyone's eyes were on him, he said in a deep voice:

"Tomorrow's battle is of utmost importance. In the morning, I will personally take the field and win one match first to stabilize the situation."

"As for the second match, I have already sent word to the sect. Qin Yuanjiang will come."

"Whether he wins or loses, he will drag the Ten Duels to the final day. At that time..."

Wang Yuan looked at Gu Caiyi. "By then, there will be no one left in the sect, and it will be your turn."

Qin Yuanjiang was trustworthy.

He was the son of Luo Chen's good friends, Qin Liangchen and his wife. When the Falling Cloud Sect was destroyed, he had brought his severely injured mother, Murong Qinglian, to seek refuge with the Luo Tian Sect. Although Murong Qinglian later succumbed to her injuries and passed away, he had stayed.

"I do not doubt Qin Yuanjiang's loyalty, but I doubt his strength." Gu Caiyi was hesitant. The truth was that Qin Yuanjiang had formed his core too recently. She was more inclined to fight tomorrow herself.

If both she and Wang Yuan won tomorrow, they could drag it to the final day.

In her meeting with Sima Huiniang earlier, the latter had made up her mind to personally clean house, even if the sect's disgrace was her own biological brother.

Wang Yuan still disagreed.

Just as the two sides were at an impasse, a knock came at the wooden door.

Had Qin Yuanjiang arrived?

So soon?

As everyone wondered, Min Longyu went to open the door.

A man and a woman smiled at the assembled group.

The man had a weathered face, a look of melancholy, but his smile was as bright as it had been in his childhood.

"All you Uncle-Masters, Uncle-Master Wang Yuan. Your nephew has come late."

Everyone rose to their feet, staring at the newcomer in surprise.

Gu Caiyi was momentarily stunned, then quickly stepped forward and grabbed the man's arm.

"Lingjun, shouldn't you be staying put in Fenghua Palace? Why have you come here?"

Qu Lingjun shook his head. "When the sect is in trouble, how can I sit idly by? Besides, I owe the Luo Tian Sect a great deal over the years. It is time for me to act."

Owed a great deal?

Decades ago, those present might have believed that.

But now, no one doubted Qu Lingjun anymore.

In recent years, the Luo Tian Sect had seen a constant stream of Adept cultivators emerge. While this was certainly due to their own diligent cultivation and the core-formation secret techniques Luo Chen had left behind, it was also inseparable from Qu Lingjun's quiet support.

Especially when the Luo Tian Sect fled to the Fenghua Domain. Unlike other forces that struggled to find a foothold and had to fight tooth and nail with local powers, they had been immediately assigned a garrison ground with a third-rank spirit vein by Fenghua Palace.

And all of this clearly bore Qu Lingjun's imprint.

He was definitely not the kind of person who climbed a high branch and forgot his roots!

But still...

Gu Caiyi said, half amused, half exasperated, "You're only at the sixth layer of Initiate stage now. How can it be your turn to fight?"

Qu Lingjun smiled faintly, pointing to the woman beside him. "I naturally cannot, but my wife still has some skill."

The stunningly beautiful woman nodded in acknowledgment.

"Fenghua Palace, Meng Qiner!"

As she spoke, an Adept-level spiritual pressure slowly unfolded.
