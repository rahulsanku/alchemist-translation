# Chapter 1052: A Great Power Shows Favor, Arctic Yemo Heaven

In what had once been the grand hall of the Ice Pole Sect, the young Daoist sat with a heavy expression, silently pondering.

Li Canghai did not disturb him, contentedly sipping his tea.

After a long moment, Luo Chen finally came out of his reverie.

"Thank you, Senior, for informing me of this matter. I will personally go and verify it!"

Li Canghai set down his teacup and smiled faintly. "A trivial matter. In truth, this is not much of a secret. Anyone with a basic understanding of formations who pays close attention to the changes in mountain ranges and spirit vein movements can detect a few clues."

Luo Chen gave a wry smile. This man was a grandmaster of formations. What he called a "basic understanding of formations" likely required a level of expertise that ordinary people could hardly achieve.

Although Luo Chen himself had barely stepped into the realm of formation grandmaster, it was purely through accumulated proficiency. His talent in that area was relatively average. Unless there was a need, he rarely used a formation-related perspective to observe the changes in the grand momentum of heaven and earth.

"I had forgotten—you are an alchemy grandmaster. To have reached such a pinnacle in the Dao of alchemy at your age is already extraordinary. I cannot be too demanding of you in formations as well." Seeing Luo Chen's helpless smile, Li Canghai realized his mistake. He had been somewhat misled by Luo Chen's performance in forcefully breaking through the Mist-Shrouded Spirit-Maze Formation. As he saw it now, breaking through that formation had relied more on those eyes of his.

At that thought, a sudden idea stirred in Li Canghai's heart.

With a casual flick of his hand, a piece of black parchment flew before Luo Chen.

"What is this?"

Luo Chen grabbed the parchment. It was cold to the touch, rough at the edges, and half of it was missing.

The moment his eyes fell upon it, his vision brightened.

"*Spirit-Nourishing Pill*?"

Li Canghai smiled. "Indeed. I obtained this pill formula by chance. According to the description, it should be at the fourth tier. Unfortunately, the latter half was destroyed, and it has been sitting idle in my hands for many years. The world says that the Eastern Wilderness Alchemy Grandmaster is the foremost alchemist in the world since the Central Continent Alchemy Saint. I wonder if you can complete this pill formula?"

"The world exaggerates somewhat, but I do have some understanding of the Dao of alchemy." Luo Chen replied modestly, his gaze already fixed on the parchment.

Even with just a cursory glance, a surge of delight welled up in Luo Chen's heart.

Half of this *Spirit-Nourishing Pill* formula had indeed been destroyed, but the front portion was completely intact.

In particular, the main ingredients and the crucial auxiliary ingredients were all recorded, along with the corresponding proportions.

As for the destroyed part, it should have contained some ordinary auxiliary ingredients, refining conditions, taboos, and the like.

With Luo Chen's alchemy attainments, given enough time, completing a fourth-tier pill formula was no problem at all.

In fact, after a brief look at this incomplete formula, he had already thought of at least three ways to complete it.

No other reason!

This pill formula shared some similarities with the *Great Soul Pill* from the thirty-six pill formulas of Devil Lord Heaven-Refining.

One strengthened the soul, the other nourished the spirit—both focused on cultivating and strengthening the primordial spirit.

Even the main ingredients contained the same material—soul essence!

"Soul essence?"

Luo Chen's expression shifted slightly as he looked at Li Canghai, who seemed somewhat expectant. A thought surfaced in his mind.

Holding the parchment, he pondered for a moment, then spoke bluntly: "Senior Li, this pill formula of yours should have come from the Primordial Demon Sect, correct?"

Li Canghai laughed. "Little friend is quite sharp-eyed to recognize its origin at a glance. Indeed, this *Spirit-Nourishing Pill* formula comes from the holy land of the Northern Sea, the Primordial Demon Sect. More specifically, it is from one of their three main lineages—the Soul-Refining lineage. That lineage pursues the strengthening of the primordial spirit. They cultivate with soul-nourishing banners and fight with soul-refining banners. In this realm, they are the foremost in soul-path techniques. And this Spirit-Nourishing Pill is the favorite pill of the Soul-Refining lineage's Sages."

Just as I thought!

Luo Chen's speculation was confirmed.

Soul essence—a material both heinous and exceedingly rare—was produced only in the Northern Sea throughout the entire Mountains and Seas Realm.

And the method for refining soul essence in the Northern Sea originated from the Ascendant holy land, the Primordial Demon Sect!

If there was any method in the world that perfectly utilized soul essence, it would inevitably come from the Primordial Demon Sect.

Unfortunately, after the Primordial Demon Sect was destroyed, large quantities of soul essence had no place to be offered. The cultivators of the Northern Sea also lacked the corresponding methods, so most soul essence became the private collections of local powerhouses.

It wasn't that no one had tried to study methods for utilizing soul essence, but anything involving a cultivator's primordial spirit was far too dangerous.

One careless mistake could easily harm oneself.

Take, for example, Old Monster Netherfire, one of the twelve stream lords of the Moluo Stream. He had attempted such a thing.

And he had been very cautious, not even using it directly on his own primordial spirit. He had only used soul essence to nurture his own true flame. Yet even so, the flame's intelligence had surged and rebelled against him, nearly causing a severe loss in his cultivation.

He was thinking too far.

Basically, the moment Luo Chen noticed the main ingredient of soul essence, he had guessed the origin of the Spirit-Nourishing Pill formula.

"Can it be completed?" Li Canghai looked at the parchment. "If it can, that would be good. If not, it doesn't matter. I simply didn't want this rare soul-path pill formula to be lost to the world."

A fourth-tier soul-path pill formula was indeed useless to an Ascendant great power.

And the reason he had brought it out was simple.

First, to prevent the formula from being lost. Second, to test whether Luo Chen's reputation as an alchemy grandmaster was well-deserved.

So far, Luo Chen had performed decently, quickly guessing the formula's origin. But that was all.

Unexpectedly.

Luo Chen smiled faintly and spread open the parchment.

"It's quite simple. I have at least two methods to complete this pill formula. It depends on which one Senior prefers."

Li Canghai's face showed curiosity. "Oh?"

Luo Chen said confidently, "One method pursues an extreme approach, using auxiliary ingredients to stimulate the main ingredients like soul essence, allowing cultivators who take the Spirit-Nourishing Pill to greatly strengthen their primordial spirit. The other method pursues a gradual approach, slowly activating the effects of the main ingredients, gently nourishing the cultivator's primordial spirit. This latter method is also more in line with the original concept of the Spirit-Nourishing Pill."

This time, Li Canghai was genuinely a little shocked.

In such a short time, with just a glance or two at the incomplete formula, he could come up with methods to complete it.

And two methods at that?

Was this what it meant to be an alchemy grandmaster?

But he had known several alchemy grandmasters from the Northern Sea before. They could only refine a few types of fourth-tier pills. They had never shown such ability!

What he didn't know was that Luo Chen was not an ordinary alchemy grandmaster. He had absorbed the strengths of many schools and had long since formed a complete alchemy style of his own, making him a great alchemy grandmaster.

He was only a step away from the realm of Alchemy Saint.

Especially since this *Spirit-Nourishing Pill* and the *Great Soul Pill* both used soul essence as the main refining ingredient, and their auxiliary ingredient combinations were quite similar.

Li Canghai still had some doubt in his heart, so he said directly, "If Little Friend is not in a hurry, why not write down both completed formulas?"

"As you wish, I dare not refuse!"

Luo Chen chuckled lightly, took out two extra sheets of rice paper, and casually wielded his brush. In the short time it took to drink a cup of tea, two methods for completing the Spirit-Nourishing Pill formula were laid out on the paper.

"Senior, please take a look!"

Li Canghai took the black parchment as the main reference and looked at the two sheets of rice paper alongside it.

He had also dabbled in the Dao of alchemy. By the standards of an ordinary cultivator, he was at least at the level of an alchemy master. So he had a basic understanding of alchemy.

As he saw it now, either method of completion could perfectly connect with the original incomplete formula.

"Time was short, so I simply listed the subsequent auxiliary ingredients. The corresponding proportions and refining techniques haven't been noted yet. But those are not problems. With a bit more time to experiment with a few refining attempts, the perfect refining standard can always be found. I wonder if Senior is satisfied?"

Li Canghai raised his head, looking at the young man's face full of confident brilliance. It was very much like his own expression when discussing formations.

He set down the paper and said with heartfelt emotion, "Alchemy Grandmaster Luo Chen, your reputation is well-deserved!"

Luo Chen smiled faintly and did not modestly decline again.

This praise, he deserved!

Li Canghai put away the pill formula, thought for a moment, and then said, "Since you have completed the formula for me, I will return the favor and give you an opportunity!"

"Eh?"

Luo Chen was startled. He hadn't been thinking about any reward.

Li Canghai smiled faintly. "That sword technique you used earlier should be a one-time consumable sword technique, correct?"

Luo Chen's expression shifted slightly. "Senior has sharp eyes. Indeed, it is. That sword technique is extremely difficult to cultivate. It requires refining the Nine Heavens Firmament Qi with one's own mana to form unparalleled sword qi. Its power is rarely seen in the world, but its drawback is also obvious—once consumed, it is very difficult to replenish."

Li Canghai understood this very clearly.

It seemed that the hundreds of strands of sword qi Luo Chen had used to receive his attack were all he had.

"I know of a place that contains an incalculable amount of Nine Heavens Firmament Qi. If you wish to restore that sword technique to its peak, you might go there to search for some."

Luo Chen immediately became interested.

"Please enlighten me, Senior!"

"That place is actually quite famous. I'm sure you'll recognize it once I say it. Its name is—Arctic Yemo Heaven!"

...

On the Blue Ice Sea.

Black King had already fully recovered, coiled on the ice surface, looking up expectantly.

Little White stood on his head, leaning against that dragon horn, gazing toward Ice Soul Spirit Mountain.

The two chatted idly, but both were somewhat distracted. Their attention was always on that mountain.

At nightfall.

A dark shadow, under the faint blue moonlight reflected off the ice, drifted down from the mountain.

"Master, are you alright?"

"Master, Little Black missed you so much!"

"I'm fine." Luo Chen responded to Little White, then looked at Black King. "Really, or just saying that?"

Black King wept bitterly. "It's definitely real! Ever since I got separated from Master at the Yellow Spring Sea, Little Black couldn't eat or sleep. I've lost weight."

"Oh?" Luo Chen looked at his bloated dragon body with a half-smile. "Then why did Senior Li Canghai tell me that when he found you, you were back in your old home, surrounded by beauties, eating and drinking to your heart's content, living quite the carefree life?"

Black King was dumbfounded.

That old man, how could he go around telling people things like that!

He had been his master for a while too—didn't he know that family scandals shouldn't be aired out?

Luo Chen couldn't be bothered with this fellow. With a wave of his hand, a flying shuttle appeared before him.

"Get on!"

"Master, where are we going?" Little White, seeing that Luo Chen was fine, relaxed considerably. Seeing the Frost Eagle Flying Shuttle, she knew a long journey might be ahead, so she asked curiously.

Luo Chen looked around, and finally fixed his gaze firmly in a southerly direction.

"First, to the Central Continent!"

The Frost Eagle Flying Shuttle rose into the air. Little White was in charge of piloting it, while Luo Chen took out a jade slip and used his divine sense to transcribe the incomplete Spirit-Nourishing Pill formula. Black King shrank his body, lying on Luo Chen's shoulder, muttering about everything that had happened since they parted, trying to salvage his image of loyalty in his master's heart.

Luo Chen listened casually, not paying much attention to Black King's carefree days. His focus was on the incomplete formula.

When he had first seen this incomplete formula, he had instinctively thought of at least three methods to complete it.

He had already told Li Canghai about the first two, but he hadn't mentioned the last one.

Because that last method wasn't about completion—it was about optimization. It would even require changing the main ingredient configuration.

But those were matters for later, when he had free time. The urgent task now was to verify what Li Canghai had said.

Was the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect really preparing to reunite the Central Continent and rebuild Heaven-Earth Peak?

...

After the Central Continent Catastrophe, this continent—the largest landmass in the Mountains and Seas Realm—had split into five parts, forming four landmasses in the east, south, west, and north, along with a massive island called Heaven-Earth Island, which bore the broken Heaven-Earth Peak.

The western continent had already sunk, and many cultivators on it, including three of the Twelve Great Houses, had been swept away by the Yellow Spring Sea.

The remaining three continents became the resting and recuperation grounds for the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's cultivators.

The Northern Continent was the closest to the Northern Sea.

It was home to three of the former Twelve Great Houses—the Ancient Gu Great House, the Tan Great House, and the Gu Great House—the top-tier cultivation families. Its strength was second only to the Eastern Continent, where the sect's ancestral court was located.

But in recent years, the Northern Continent had not fared well.

A large number of sea demons from the Northern Sea frequently attacked the Northern Continent, draining the energy of many powerhouses.

Therefore, when a man, a beast, and a ghost quietly infiltrated the Northern Continent, no one noticed them.

In an inland lake, a lake-bottom palace had been constructed. Many figures were bustling about inside, extremely busy.

With the palace as the core, a vast number of formation patterns spread outward, extending to extremely distant places, almost without end.

Luo Chen walked leisurely along the formation patterns. Cultivators brushed past him, yet they couldn't detect him at all.

Luo Chen moved forward, his gaze always fixed on those formation patterns, and finally stopped outside a stone hall at the core of the underground palace.

He didn't enter, because he already knew what he needed to know.

"Li Canghai didn't deceive me!"

Luo Chen turned and left.

After leaving the underground palace, he went straight up into the nine heavens.

His Crimson-Gold Pupils opened wide, combined with the divine sense released after his primordial spirit had condensed, he observed the entire Northern Continent from a macroscopic perspective, even gazing toward the other two continents.

He watched silently, his mana circulating, his primordial spirit seemingly connected to heaven and earth.

Vaguely, he could detect the flow of origin qi of heaven and earth. Occasionally, his eyes could even catch a trace of spiritual opportunity leaping and shifting.

"Reuniting the Central Continent is not a difficult task for Ascendant great powers. Even we Sages have the ability to move mountains and fill seas."

"But the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect is deploying a large number of formations. Their purpose is not merely to reunite the several continents."

"They are also channeling spiritual opportunity!"

"If Heaven-Earth Peak is truly the core of the God-Descending Formation, then this spiritual opportunity must be flowing toward Heaven-Earth Peak."

Little White appeared beside Luo Chen, a puzzled expression on her face. "Master, if that's the case, why don't you destroy these formations?"

Luo Chen countered, "Li Canghai knew about this before I did, and he also has designs on the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. So why hasn't he destroyed them?"

Little White was speechless.

Luo Chen shook his head. These formations were only minor details. Destroy them, and they could be rebuilt.

The true core was Heaven-Earth Peak, and the spirit treasure that could suppress the spiritual opportunity of an entire continent—the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard!

Acting rashly now would only invite a devastating strike from the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, with no other effect.

Therefore, the only option was to wait, to bide his time!

But waiting and doing nothing was never Luo Chen's style.

"Let's go. To the Arctic Yemo Heaven!"

(End of Chapter)
