# Chapter 690: Controlling Qi Like Dragons, a Heaven-Earth Domain

[Detected complete Law-Derivation Pill formula, fifth tier.]

[Enter?]

The bright entries quietly hovered on the panel.

After confirming that the pill formula was real, Luo Chen's wildly joyous mood gradually calmed.

He began calculating.

Before coming to Demon-Fall Land, he had accumulated a total of 334 achievement points over many years.

Without the Rotten Axe-Handle Black Chess Pieces, and with the foundation of his divine soul slightly insufficient, he had spent 34 achievement points in order to guard against powerful existences like Shark Emperor Liujun attacking him with bewitching voices or even illusory-spirit arts, forcefully entering the fourth-tier spell Han Zhan had taught him, the Aperture-Opening Art [TERM?].

In the third trial of the Pill Realm, he had wanted to use extremely fast pill-refining speed to disturb the mind of his competitor, Cape Heaven Matron, so he used another thirty achievement points to enter three devil-race body-tempering pills. In truth, this was somewhat wasteful. With his ability, if he spent a little time, he could have normally refined third-tier pills.

In the fourth trial, among the three transformation pills, aside from the Human-Transformation Pill, he used another twenty achievement points to enter the Devil-Transformation Pill and Demon-Transformation Pill. This did not count as waste, because with his own level, a fourth-tier pill normally required several years to enter a single formula. For example, after obtaining Han Zhan's original Nascent Soul Pill formula, Luo Chen had pondered it for decades and still could not fully grasp it.

At this point, exactly 250 achievement points remained on the panel.

What a perfect two-fifty!

Originally, Luo Chen had been somewhat uneasy about whether his accumulated achievement points would be enough.

But after entering the fifth trial, he had already sensed from the unseen that this would be the final trial of the inheritance assessment.

And the assessment topic was to refine a fifth-tier Law-Derivation Pill.

Refining pills across major realms was not rare for Luo Chen. He had often done so.

During Qi Refinement, he had refined second-tier Imperial Moonlight Nectar. During Foundation Establishment, he had refined the third-tier Nether Origin Pill. During Golden Core, he had refined the fourth-tier Black Emperor Ointment, Purple Qi Poison-Resisting Pill, and even Transformation Pill.

Only this time, the matter stood out even more.

Because his alchemist rank was fourth tier, while his own realm was Golden Core, yet the Law-Derivation Pill he had to refine was fifth tier.

Under these circumstances, should it be counted as crossing one major realm?

Or crossing two major realms?

This was what left Luo Chen uncertain.

If it was only one major realm, that was fine. According to the cheat's rules he had explored in earlier years, the achievement point cost was generally between ten and one hundred.

But if it was crossing two major realms, that would be troublesome.

One hundred to one thousand?

In that case, would two hundred and fifty achievement points be enough?

Looking at the entry on the attribute panel, Luo Chen calculated for a long while and still did not make a decision.

"I cannot act rashly!"

In the end, Luo Chen suppressed the restlessness in his heart.

No matter how good the pill formula was, it only mattered if he could truly refine it.

The attribute panel was his greatest cheat. When his alchemy level itself was inferior to Cape Heaven Matron's, the reason he had pulled so far ahead and left her behind was that he relied on achievement points to forcibly enter formulas and save huge amounts of meaningless trial-and-error time.

If he invested achievement points and they were not enough to enter the formula, then it would be a loss not worth the gain.

Therefore, Luo Chen did not plan to enter it directly. He would first thoroughly research this pill formula!

The rules of panel use he had figured out were not only those on the surface.

For some spells, if the prerequisites were satisfied, forcefully entering them across tiers could save a large number of achievement points. The recent Aperture-Opening Art was an example; as a fourth-tier spell, it had not cost him one hundred achievement points.

Similarly, for high-tier pills, the deeper his understanding of the formula and the higher his own alchemy level, the more achievement points he could save when forcefully entering across tiers. For a concrete example, one could refer to entering Imperial Moonlight Nectar during Qi Refinement.

"One step at a time, then!"

Luo Chen rose and left that dim little room.

...

"The so-called Qi-Control Art is a method of distinguishing, controlling, and guiding external spiritual qi."

"Rather than calling this an alchemy art, it is more accurate to say it leans toward a spell. It is merely gentler when used in the Dao of Pills."

Within the Pill Realm, atop a sheer peak,

Luo Chen sat cross-legged, allowing the astral wind to strike his face while his Daoist robe snapped in the wind.

His eyes were clear, and the seals at his fingertips shifted without pause.

Overall, the movement was somewhat halting.

By now, more than half a year had passed since he arrived at the fifth trial.

During this half year, he devoted himself to studying the Law-Derivation Pill formula. He even specially copied out two versions, one carved into a jade slip with spiritual sense, and one written in brush and ink on bamboo slips.

The former was for preservation, while the latter made it convenient to consult at any time.

But his progress in researching this pill formula was extremely slow.

Under normal circumstances, fifth-tier pills were refined by fifth-tier alchemists. Some formidable fourth-tier alchemists could also refine them across tiers.

If matched to cultivation realm, that was the difference between Ascendant and Sage.

Because of this, refining this pill formula added many innate conditions that looked simple, yet were insurmountable to Golden Core cultivators!

How should one put it?

It was like third-tier pills often needing cultivator true fire to provide help at critical moments. But true fire was something that required Foundation Establishment or above to stimulate. Could a Qi Refinement cultivator do it? They could not!

Fifth-tier pills had many similar restrictions.

Without understanding the full reason, Luo Chen could only split everything apart and study it.

From the medicinal principles of each material to the concrete pill-refining method, the most complicated alchemy arts, and so on, he dissected them bit by bit.

In the end, he astonishingly discovered that the first three trials had given him tremendous help now.

The main and auxiliary materials required for the Law-Derivation Pill had all been recorded in the study's bamboo slips during the first three trials. Now it was not difficult for him to understand them.

As for alchemy arts, he also discovered that he had already learned what he needed during the first three trials.

Aside from the most basic Pure Heart Hand and Star-Plucking Hand [TERM?], the one he needed most right now was Qi-Control Art!

"If I had Sage realm, this art would be nothing more than something I could pick up at will."

"But unfortunately, I only have Golden Core cultivation."

After failing to control qi yet again, Luo Chen's face revealed a bitter smile.

Qi-Control Art was not difficult, but only for Sage Perfected Beings.

Because Sage Perfected Beings could control true Nascent Soul domains and distinguish the attributes of the spiritual qi wandering in natural space.

Even if they could only control the attribute they specialized in most, under noncombat circumstances they could still calmly stimulate spiritual qi of other attributes.

Han Zhan had explained the concrete operation to him before. It was no more than a little trick of borrowing force to strike with force.

But Golden Core cultivators had too shallow an understanding of heaven and earth.

In a field of chaos, how could they distinguish differences in attributes?

"Steady. As long as I enter it, everything will be worry-free!"

Beneath the bitter smile, Luo Chen still had some confidence.

This was the function of the proficiency panel. It was not afraid that cultivation would fail to reach a profound level, only that one would be blocked outside the threshold.

And now, Luo Chen had faintly touched the key to entry.

Mana circulated through his body, his eyes were clear, and his spiritual sense spread outward.

Not only that, the All-Encompassing Fire Prison directly expanded.

Faintly, scorching spiritual qi in heaven and earth seemed to respond.

Several days later,

The spirit seal in Luo Chen's hand paused. A wisp of spiritual qi was drawn by him from the void!

Luo Chen's gaze focused on the wisp of spiritual qi coiling at his fingertip, and satisfaction appeared in his eyes.

The attribute panel unfolded.

In the alchemy art column, [Qi-Control Art, Beginner 1/100] flashed with faint light.

"Done!"

Luo Chen felt as though a heavy burden had been lifted, and exhaled a breath of turbid air.

Next, he only needed to practice madly every day and raise its proficiency.

By his estimate, it would at least have to reach the Perfect level before he could barely handle the Law-Derivation Pill refinement. If it could reach the Grandmaster level, that would be even better.

The Grandmaster level on the panel was equivalent to the outside world's Great Completion standard, already the lifetime limit of ordinary alchemists.

Luo Chen rose, descended the sheer peak, and headed toward the stone house of the fifth trial.

Soon, he pushed open the Herb Room door.

Within sight, medicinal materials emitting powerful spiritual qi were arranged by category on wooden shelves.

Some were covered by mana shields, while others were stored in jade boxes or golden cauldrons.

This was the most straightforward trial among all five Pill Realm assessments.

There was no need for Luo Chen to spend effort gathering materials. Everything had already been prepared here in advance!

Luo Chen estimated that the earlier trials had already tested the alchemist's ability to recognize and process medicinal materials, so the fifth trial directly omitted this step.

Even so, Luo Chen did not treat it lightly.

He waved a hand, and a cauldron lid flew up. A fist-sized red fruit slowly floated out.

Looking at this red fruit, Luo Chen's eyes revealed infatuation.

A fifth-tier spirit medicine, the Jade Palace Vermilion Fruit [TERM?]!

Before entering Demon-Fall Land, he had never heard of this spirit medicine.

He only learned its name from the Pill Realm bamboo slips.

It grew at the edge of the Purple Palace of the Jade Pool [TERM?], formless yet possessing appearance. Only after being harvested would it condense a solid body, and when harvesting it, one had to pair it with a corresponding alchemy art. Otherwise, what one obtained would be only a cloud of mist.

It could be eaten raw to strengthen the divine soul!

And if used in medicine... its power was even more mysterious and unfathomable.

The concrete example was the Law-Derivation Pill!

This was also why Luo Chen was so entranced by this fruit.

Because it was the only main material of the Law-Derivation Pill!

According to the bamboo slip records, the Jade Palace Vermilion Fruit was born at fourth tier. Every three thousand years counted as one maturation period, each period raising it by one tier, for a total of three periods.

At its peak, it could reach as high as seventh tier!

The Jade Palace Vermilion Fruit in this golden cauldron was exactly fifth tier, at its first maturation period.

Before Devil Lord Heaven-Refining, no one knew how to use Jade Palace Vermilion Fruit. They all consumed it raw to strengthen the divine soul.

After Devil Lord Heaven-Refining, there came a formula to refine it into medicine and produce the Law-Derivation Divine Pill.

The talent of a True Lord could be glimpsed merely through one pill formula!

"If I do not refine a pill and simply eat it, I wonder whether it can strengthen my divine soul?"

For some reason, this thought rose in Luo Chen.

But in the end, he dismissed it.

Everything in the Pill Realm gave Luo Chen a dreamlike, illusory feeling.

The medicinal herbs across the mountains and fields could not be taken away.

The vast sea of bamboo slips could not be taken away.

Only finished pills he refined could be placed into the storage ring.

He did not know whether, if he consumed the Jade Palace Vermilion Fruit raw and then passed to the next stage, its effect would be preserved.

Moreover, the number of Jade Palace Vermilion Fruits inside the golden cauldron was limited. He had to consider the material cost of the Law-Derivation Pill.

"I should study it carefully!"

...

As Luo Chen researched the formula, practiced alchemy arts, and familiarized himself with medicinal materials, time slowly flowed.

One year passed in an instant!

On this day, Luo Chen stood atop the sheer peak, forming seals with both hands. Vast currents like flood dragons roared toward him from every direction.

Suddenly,

His seal changed, and the many air currents began to disperse, gradually revealing spiritual lights of different colors.

Seeing this scene, surprise and delight appeared in Luo Chen's eyes.

Controlling qi like dragons, each returning to its nature!

In one year, Qi-Control Art reached Great Completion and entered the Grandmaster level!

This was something he had not expected at the beginning.

It had taken him half a year just to enter the art.

But after entering it, when one method was understood, ten thousand methods became clear!

He used the All-Encompassing Fire Prison as a guide to control fire-attribute spiritual qi between heaven and earth. This was the first stage.

In the second stage, he tried converting spiritual qi, using his fundamentally five-element Golden Core to draw in five-element spiritual qi.

Finally, he thought of his signature spell, the Azure Yang Great Handprint at the peak of mastery!

This spell, at the very beginning, was not a fire-attribute spell, but the Inborn One Qi Great Handprint, complete with the five elements!

Therefore, Luo Chen returned to his original heart, reversed the flow of the Azure Yang Great Handprint, and cast the other four attributes of the Inborn One Qi Great Handprint, using them with Qi-Control Art to guide the other four attributes of spiritual qi between heaven and earth.

After the three stages passed, Luo Chen's proficiency in Qi-Control Art reached Perfect.

Then, with unceasing practice, he accumulated proficiency step by step and finally crossed the threshold into the Grandmaster level.

At this stage, Luo Chen could even sense that when he activated this alchemy art, he could guide spiritual qi of attributes beyond the five elements.

Wind, thunder, ice, light, darkness...

At this moment, Luo Chen sat cross-legged on the green rock of the mountain peak and slowly closed his eyes, as though gaining insight.

Three days later, Luo Chen's eyes abruptly opened.

He first tapped a finger to his chest. His lifebound Golden Core trembled, and the All-Encompassing Fire Prison instantly unfolded.

Then his left hand formed seals, and Qi-Control Art was cast.

In the void, large amounts of fire-attribute spiritual qi surged over and poured into the All-Encompassing Fire Prison.

In an instant, Luo Chen almost felt as if he controlled heaven and earth, as if only he were supreme!

But in only a breath, the All-Encompassing Fire Prison collapsed with a crash.

"Hmph..."

Luo Chen let out a muffled grunt, his face somewhat pale.

Unrefined spiritual qi of heaven and earth forcefully merged into the All-Encompassing Fire Prison was ultimately still somewhat strained.

But!

He had truly grasped that feeling.

That was a genuine Nascent Soul domain!

Beneath his pale face, sharp light flashed in his eyes.

"I did not expect that practicing an alchemy art could produce the effect of using another mountain's stone to polish jade, letting my pseudo-domain advance another step!"

"This gain alone means my trip through Demon-Fall Land has not been in vain!"

Luo Chen knew very clearly that if he grasped that feeling and cultivated it steadily, he had a very high chance of mastering a true heaven-earth domain.

In other words, he would master the so-called Nascent Soul domain at the Golden Core stage.

Such an accomplishment could be called world-shocking even in this vast cultivation world.

But right now, it was not the time to study this art deeply.

He had more important things to do.

The attribute panel unfolded again.

In Luo Chen's heart, the complete formula of the Law-Derivation Pill surfaced.

He had already done everything he could to the extreme.

Even if some parts could still be improved, the improvement would no longer be great.

He could not be sure when Demon-Fall Land would close.

And behind him, Cape Heaven Matron was bitterly chasing.

Therefore, whether he succeeded or failed, he had to forcefully take that step.

[Detected complete Law-Derivation Pill formula, fifth tier.]

[Enter?]

Luo Chen took a deep breath and silently recited the word, "Enter."

In the next moment, the achievement points on the panel rapidly flowed away.

250, 240... 180, 170...

Never before had achievement points been consumed so quickly.

Never before had Luo Chen's heart been so uneasy.

His mind stared fixedly at the panel. When the achievement point count fell to [160], his breathing almost stopped.

The next instant,

[150]

His heart nearly stopped!

After one breath, his heart began beating again.

Luo Chen opened his mouth wide, and finally let out a soundless laugh.

It did not fall any further. The achievement point count still remained at [150].

In other words, entering this pill formula had consumed exactly one hundred achievement points.

Logically, he should feel regret.

One hundred achievement points meant that the fifth-tier pill corresponded to his fourth-tier alchemist rank, crossing exactly one tier, still within the range of one hundred achievement points. In other words, even if he had not done all that research on the formula and practice of alchemy arts, he could have entered it directly.

All those things had been done for nothing.

But!

"As long as it entered, that is enough."

Luo Chen smiled faintly and freely descended the mountain.

After returning to the stone house, he sat silently for three days, bathed, changed robes, burned incense, and settled his spirit.

Three days later, Luo Chen left the stone house carrying a large pile of medicinal materials.

He found a mountain peak with abundant spiritual qi, reaching fourth tier. Opening his mouth, he spat out the Primordial Unity Cauldron, which spun as it flew out.

Everything was prepared. Whether he succeeded or not depended on the pill-refining result that followed.

Luo Chen, dressed in a moon-white Daoist robe, flicked a finger. The Withering-Bloom True Fire fell beneath the Primordial Unity Cauldron, and firelight surged violently in an instant!

*(End of Chapter)*
