# Chapter 709: Final Radiance Before Death, Jiaolong-Python Devours Dragon

Inside Bright Radiance Heaven's [TERM?] Pill Palace, thirty-nine pill formulas were preserved in total.

Aside from the three great sealed pill formulas, the remaining thirty-six corresponded respectively to the three races of human, demon, and devil, with twelve for each.

The method Luo Chen used to treat Black King was a fourth-tier blood pill method among the demon race's pill formulas.

It took the blood of many spirits to nourish a demon body, and could even condense a brand-new "demon core" from nothing.

This method suited the illness exactly!

Black King's current state was precisely a collapsing flesh body and a shattered demon core.

Over these years, Luo Chen had also checked Black King's condition from time to time, and everything had been developing in a good direction.

But Luo Chen understood very clearly that all of this was merely a tower in the air.

Everything was for the sake of nurturing Black King's divine soul.

If this step failed, all previous effort would be wasted!

Leaving the abyss and stepping out of the emerald pool, Luo Chen advanced all the way forward, with Heavenjade following closely behind him step by step.

Suddenly.

Luo Chen's steps paused slightly, and his gaze turned toward one of the rare tall peaks on Dragon Abyss Island [TERM?].

There, an old man in azure robes sat alone on a cliff, idly watching clouds roll and unfold, tides rise and fall.

It was Han Zhan!

"Has he been there all this time recently?" Luo Chen asked.

Heavenjade answered softly, "Perfected Being Han has gone in and out frequently over these years, saying that he is adapting to his new body and needs to move around often. That Mount Rosy Hill [TERM?] is where he often stays alone. Speaking of which, Perfected Being Han has an excellent temperament and has even given me guidance in cultivation several times."

"Is that so..."

Luo Chen muttered, then changed the topic.

"Speaking of which, these years I have been immersed in cultivation and have not paid much attention to your own cultivation situation."

Heavenjade shook her head. "Master helped me transform ahead of time, created a cultivation method for me, and also bestowed the opportunity to soak in the sacred spring. You have already done a great deal. How could a little daily cultivation disturb Master?"

Mentioning soaking in the Spirit-Molding Sacred Spring [TERM?], Luo Chen became interested.

"After soaking in that spring water, did your body undergo any changes?"

Heavenjade's spirit lifted, and she said with some excitement, "There were great changes. My daily cultivation speed has nearly doubled compared to before! Moreover, my talent in controlling wind has fully manifested. In Perfected Being Han's words, I seem to have excavated all the potential of the spirit root in my body, and combined with my demon body, I faintly possess the traits of the so-called Xun Wind Body [TERM?]."

"Xun Wind Body [TERM?]?"

"Yes. Although it is incomplete, the power I wield over the wind attribute has indeed been greatly amplified. I have now cultivated the Huan Yu Secret Canon to a level that can be considered entering the hall. I am currently at Adept Stage Six!"

Luo Chen nodded slightly. "Then it can be counted as a good thing. That opportunity was not wasted."

Back then, ninety percent of the pool of Spirit-Molding Sacred Spring [TERM?] in the Pill Palace had been absorbed by Luo Chen.

The remaining ten percent looked small, but for Heavenjade and Black King, it could absolutely be considered an enormous fortune.

Heavenjade's realm growth was within expectations.

It was only Black King's side...

As the two conversed, they had already arrived at their destination.

Heavenjade's previous joy had long since vanished.

Luo Chen's face also showed some solemnity.

The blood pool... calling it a blood pool was actually no longer very appropriate.

The originally crimson blood had now become clear and bright.

The blood essence power of countless demon beasts had all been absorbed into Black King's body!

If calculated carefully, on Dragon Abyss Island [TERM?], several million low-tier Dragon Earthworms [TERM?] had been melted into one pool, and there were also some third-tier Demon Kings Heavenjade had hunted and captured over these years, one after another. Finally, there was the drop of tongue-tip essence blood Luo Chen had bestowed with pain back then.

Such a vast power had been exchanged for Black King's flesh body recovering to its peak state.

It was even much, much stronger than at his peak.

"That one drop of essence blood of mine was actually this powerful!"

Luo Chen murmured.

He could sense that the main reason Black King had undergone such an enormous change was that one drop of tongue-tip essence blood from him.

Within it was not only his origin power, but also quite a lot of Tai Sui's terrifying vitality.

"Indeed. This old man also never expected that you could truly restore Black King's body to its original state!"

At some unknown time, Han Zhan had already descended from Mount Rosy Hill [TERM?] and arrived beside the Dragon Abyss Blood Pool [TERM?].

Luo Chen was not surprised. If he could discover Han Zhan's tracks, the other party would naturally also see him.

Looking at the figure coiled in the blood pool like a small mountain, like a jiaolong and like a python, with a single horn flashing ghostly light, Luo Chen sighed.

"Unfortunately, this is all I can do. Whether Black King can hold on still depends on his own fortune."

In his perception, Black King's divine soul was indeed recovering.

That was the sign that the sleeping method of the archaic beast Cliff Serpent had reached its limit!

What Black King had devoured back then was ultimately only the corpse of a Cliff Serpent that had failed to advance to fourth tier. To comprehend a sleeping method from that corpse to preserve himself was already enormous fortune.

But fortune, after sustaining him for ten years, eventually had a time when it was exhausted.

A weak divine soul fluctuation slowly spread from Black King's body.

"Mas... ter..."

Luo Chen restrained all of his own power, lest he harm Black King's frail divine soul, which was like an infant.

He said in a deep voice, "I have already restored your flesh body for you. Even the shattered demon core has been temporarily replaced by a blood pill. You only need to endure this period now and let your divine soul gradually strengthen and be nourished. Can you do that?"

Essence nourishes the spirit root, qi nourishes the spirit!

Everything Luo Chen had done was to provide Black King with a perfect environment for his divine soul to recover.

However...

"I am sorry. Little Black is afraid he cannot make it through... this trial." Black King's voice came intermittently, like a candle in the wind, swaying on the verge of collapse.

His divine soul aura was continuously vanishing.

At the side, Han Zhan sighed. "How could the might of the Heaven-Refining Cauldron's [TERM?] self-detonation be something an Adept Demon King could withstand? Moya Cave Master's body turned to powder on the spot, and his divine soul scattered completely. For Black King to have lasted until now is already truly fortunate."

Luo Chen fell silent.

Although he had previously spoken with conviction and said he had a method to treat Black King, clearly, this was all he could do.

He had already done everything within human power.

But to truly watch Black King sit in meditation and die before his eyes was still emotionally difficult to accept.

Black King himself had only been the most ordinary black-scale giant python among countless demon beasts.

He had been cultivated by an Initiate cultivator of the Swallow Islands using a secret method, becoming the weakest of jiaolongs.

One could say that when Black King reached third tier, his life had already come to an end.

But fortunately, Black King had encountered Luo Chen.

With large amounts of Imperial Moonlight Nectar poured into him, he had opened his bloodline inheritance and obtained a devouring demon art. Over several decades, Luo Chen had bestowed many great portions of flesh-and-blood food, spirit pills, and wondrous medicines, and there had also been the Cliff Serpent corpse for him to digest.

Although the path he walked was unlike Heavenjade's, which leaned toward the orthodox path of the human race, it was still a solid foundation, and he had step by step reached the late third-tier Great Demon King level.

In particular, he had also soaked in the Spirit-Molding Sacred Spring [TERM?]!

If not for that event in the Pill Palace, Black King should have had a fine future. One day, riding clouds and driving mist, transforming into a dragon in a single morning, might not have been impossible.

Luo Chen regretted the future Black King could no longer touch.

He also grieved over the loyalty the other had displayed in forcibly protecting him without the suppression of a slave seal.

Now, waiting bit by bit for Black King's divine soul to disperse, Luo Chen only felt his heart grow heavy and unbearably hard to endure.

Heavenjade could no longer suppress herself and cried out.

"Old Black, you must hold on! Master, did you not once have a pill that could strengthen the divine soul? Can you let Old Black try it?"

Luo Chen shook his head.

The Netherpassage Pill [TERM?] was too low in grade. For the current Black King, it was truly a cup of water against a cartload of burning firewood.

If he could refine the pill among the three fourth-tier human formulas left by Devil Lord Heaven-Refining that could strengthen the divine soul, perhaps there would be a little chance.

But it would only be a little.

The medicinal materials required for that pill were seriously lacking on Luo Chen's side, unless he went to the outside world and spent great effort collecting them.

But with a powerful enemy like Moon Wanderer outside, and with his Nascent Soul formation imminent, how could he flaunt himself everywhere?

Moreover, fourth-tier pills had medicinal natures far too violent. The current Black King might not be able to withstand one.

"Heavenjade, do not grieve for me. After I am gone... you must cultivate well and serve Master."

Heavenjade's lips trembled, and her voice choked.

"I will."

Within the blood pool, that jiaolong-python's eyes remained tightly closed.

But Black King's voice still slowly came out.

"Master, do you still remember a small matter I once begged of you?"

Luo Chen froze and continuously searched his thoughts. What matter was it?

Because of this recollection, he could not help feeling a trace of shame. For a moment, he actually had not remembered.

However, Black King had not seriously begged him many times, and in the end, Luo Chen still remembered.

"Refining a treasure?"

He quickly replied, "You mean refining a protective magic treasure for that little girl of the Zhang family?"

"Mm." Black King gave a weak acknowledgment.

There was such a matter!

Black King had pitied the Zhang family's little girl for her loneliness, so he had given the undigested Cliff Serpent hide membrane to Luo Chen and begged him to refine a protective magic treasure for the little girl.

Luo Chen still remembered that the little girl's name was Zhang Jingqing.

It was a name Zhang Jiadi had specially chosen to please Luo Chen.

Luo Chen said solemnly, "If there is an opportunity, I will certainly fulfill this promise and give that gift to little Qing."

"Haha..."

Black King's divine soul fluctuated with rare joy.

"Lastly, Little Black wishes Master a flourishing immortal path and success in forming your Nascent Soul!"

After giving these words, his divine soul fluctuations began rapidly fading away.

It seemed that the moment he awoke was the moment he would depart.

Luo Chen's face tightened, and he turned his gaze toward the old man in azure robes.

"Senior!"

Han Zhan waved a hand. "Do not look at me. Although I claim to be proficient in spirit-refining methods, I do not have such heaven-defying ability..."

Hearing this, Luo Chen was filled with regret as well.

However, he discovered that Han Zhan's words had become hesitant.

In an instant, Luo Chen seemed to have grasped a life-saving straw, and his burning gaze fixed on the other man.

Han Zhan frowned tightly, sometimes looking at Black King, sometimes at Luo Chen.

In the end, he transmitted in a low voice, "If you are willing to part with it, I really do have a method of treating a dead horse as if it were alive."

"Speak it for me to hear!" Luo Chen's spirit lifted!

Han Zhan rubbed his chin, as if recalling the past.

"Back then, my good friend Canglong [TERM?] failed her tribulation, and her yin soul was struck by the utterly firm, utterly yang power of thunder, suffering extremely severe injury."

"Later, her body was sealed in a mystic ice coffin by the disciples of Icecastle, waiting for a rescue method."

"In my idle time, I studied this situation and came up with one method to treat it, but I have never experimented with it."

"Speaking of which, Black King's current state is somewhat similar to Canglong's [TERM?]. That method may be usable on him."

Luo Chen suppressed the impatience in his heart and listened carefully.

Han Zhan explained in detail how that method was to be performed.

If the divine soul was injured, then the divine soul should be treated!

And if the divine soul was deficient, then the divine soul origin needed to be supplemented!

But the injured person no longer had the ability to autonomously strengthen and recover their divine soul origin, so a supplement from external power was needed.

"External power... pills?" Luo Chen frowned.

Han Zhan shook his head. "Pills of this kind are rare in the world. But you possess an object that surpasses all divine pills and wondrous medicines!"

"What is it?"

"The Stone Dragon remnant soul!"

In an instant, Luo Chen's expression changed.

Han Zhan chuckled. "You and I have both seen that Stone Dragon remnant soul. It possesses no intelligence at all, and its quality is clear and spiritual, as if naturally formed by heaven and earth. Such a remnant soul is absolutely the purest soul-origin power. I thought just now: if I use a stimulating method to make Black King flash with final radiance and reach his peak state, then have him use that innate demon art to devour this remnant soul, do you think there is a chance to bring him back from death?"

Devouring another's soul was an extremely dangerous thing.

But Black King was different. His innate demon art refused nothing that came. Perhaps it would have miraculous effect.

Only, with this, Luo Chen would lose a soul that had once reached the fifth-tier level.

That was the foundation he had intended to use after forming his Nascent Soul to cultivate a spirit vein, preparing for future cultivation at higher realms!

No wonder Han Zhan said it depended on whether he was willing to part with it.

Seeing Luo Chen's expression shift between clear and dark, Han Zhan's face turned solemn again. "I must remind you that I have never tried this method. It may not necessarily succeed. If it fails, not only will Black King still die, the Stone Dragon remnant soul you snatched from the jaws of danger in the Quicksand Sea [TERM?] will also disperse."

"You had best consider carefully!"

What was there to consider?

Even if Luo Chen considered himself selfish, that selfishness existed under circumstances where his strength was insufficient.

Now, he did possess this power.

Moreover, Black King had been loyal to him, and he himself had invested many sunk costs into Black King.

As for the Stone Dragon remnant soul, whether it could cultivate a fifth-tier spirit vein in the future was still unknown.

At this moment, there was no need to hesitate too much.

Luo Chen took a deep breath and took out that white Soul-Summoning Banner [TERM?].

"Senior, please!"

Han Zhan looked at Luo Chen with approval, then accepted the soul banner.

With a sweep of his hand, the restrictions Luo Chen had placed on it shattered one after another.

"Out!"

In an instant, a huge dragon head, half illusory and half solid, probed out from within.

Han Zhan's expression was solemn. His hands continuously formed seals, and wisps of ghostly light flew from between his brows, finally condensing into an ancient-patterned seal.

He tapped lightly with his fingertip, and that seal fell through the void into Black King's brow.

The ghostly light gradually sank, and Black King's divine soul fluctuation, weak to the extreme, began swelling madly at this moment.

Dead ash rekindling?

No, this was final radiance before death!

"You come out too!"

Han Zhan gave a low shout and pulled with his hand.

An illusory black shadow flew out from Black King's head.

It was the soul of a jiaolong-python only half a zhang long.

At this time, Black King still seemed extremely confused.

Han Zhan's lips trembled lightly, sending out the sound of divine soul.

In the next moment, Black King seemed to receive an order. Spiritual light blazed, and he roared toward that huge dragon head.

One jiaolong-python, one Stone Dragon.

As the jiaolong-python opened its mouth, the two illusory divine souls whistled wildly, and terrifying devouring power spread out.

Above Dragon Abyss Island [TERM?], wind and clouds gathered, and all spirits panicked.

Even though the Stone Dragon remnant soul had no intelligence, under instinct's drive, it still tried to break free of that devouring power.

But Han Zhan grasped the Soul-Summoning Banner [TERM?] and from time to time cast a spirit art to suppress it, helping Black King devour it.

Visible to the naked eye, wisps of fine power from its body were devoured by Black King.

Heavenjade stared at this scene in shock.

"Master, what is this doing?"

Luo Chen narrowed his eyes, memorizing bit by bit the seal Han Zhan had displayed, while answering Heavenjade.

"The final chance. Whether he can survive depends on Black King's own ability."

...

This jiaolong-python devouring dragon lasted a full half month.

In the sky above Dragon Abyss Island [TERM?], the positions of host and guest had already reversed between the two phantoms. Black King's soul had become a full hundred zhang long.

The Stone Dragon remnant soul had instead become delicate and small.

Half a month later, Han Zhan's face was pale, but his eyes shone with brilliant spiritual light.

"Great work completed, today is the day!"

As if hearing Han Zhan's shout, Black King roared, opened his mouth wide, and swallowed the Stone Dragon's final wisp of remnant soul in one bite.

Afterward, he shook in the air and burrowed into his body.

"Black King!" Heavenjade called out.

Han Zhan smiled faintly. "There is still a little finishing work. Wait a moment."

His hands formed seals rapidly, and ghostly lights flew out from Black King's body, gathering toward Han Zhan.

One, two, three... seven in total.

After the seven ghostly lights flew back, two vertical pupils like lanterns slowly opened.

Those eyes were cold, filled with calm and even unfamiliarity.

Luo Chen took a step forward, his brow slightly furrowed.

Before he could speak, Black King growled deeply. His body suddenly enlarged, breaking through the formation's suppression, continuously swelling from his previous ten-zhang body.

In the blink of an eye, he had become an enormous hundred-zhang black one-horned jiaolong, coiling in the void.

He looked deeply at Luo Chen, then nodded his dragon head slightly toward Han Zhan, as if in thanks, before swaying his head and tail and flying into the sea.

"This..." Luo Chen was a little unable to react.

Han Zhan let out a long breath, stood up, and smiled. "It should count as a success. However, the Stone Dragon remnant soul's power was too vast. Black King's intelligence has suffered an enormous impact and will need a very long time to gather itself. Right now, he merely remembers us. More memories are still being recovered."

So it was like that?

Heavenjade revealed a wildly delighted smile. "Black King was saved!"

Luo Chen nodded. He also smiled. "That earlier scene of parting by life and death turned out to be for nothing. But being alive is good. Being alive is good."

As he murmured, Luo Chen felt as if a heavy burden had been lifted.

Next, he could prepare everything needed for Nascent Soul formation without any worries.

And the first matter was refining Nascent Soul Pills!
