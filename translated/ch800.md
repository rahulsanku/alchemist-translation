# Chapter 800: Completing the Moon Step, the Three Domains in Crisis

Within the Luo Tian Sect, things had begun to get busy due to the impending patrol mission.

Under Li Yingzhang's command, one cultivator after another was selected and added to the teams.

In the past, when faced with such a situation, most people would have been apprehensive, because no one knew if they would be able to return from this trip. The Luo Tian Sect had never had a Sage-level expert before, and thus had no say among the upper echelons of humanity. Whenever war broke out, they were invariably sent to the most dangerous places.

The Luo Tian Sect, which had once boasted nearly ten thousand disciples, had dwindled to only a few hundred precisely because of mission after mission.

But this time, everyone was much more at ease, or at least their faces didn't look as panicked.

As for the reason...

"This time, heading to Lingtian Pass will be personally led by the Supreme Elder. All of you, perform well and don't hold us back!"

"Those whose names are called, go to Elder Tianxuan to receive the corresponding supplies."

"Spirit beast bags, Spirit-Feeding Pills, Fasting Pills... and don't misuse these talismans. They were personally refined for you by the Supreme Elder, and their power is extremely strong! Qi Refinement disciples get one each, and Initiate cultivators are standardly equipped with one Blazing Sun Talisman. Additionally, you may choose one second-grade talisman of another attribute that suits you."

"We depart in three days. Before that, check your magical artifacts and dharma robes. If there are any damages, report them as soon as possible!"

With a Supreme Elder at the Sage realm accompanying them to Lingtian Pass, everyone was naturally full of confidence.

Li Yingzhang was also aware of this situation, but as he instructed the various cultivators, he remained gentle yet firm.

He was afraid that the sect disciples, emboldened by the Supreme Elder's backing, might suddenly develop an arrogant and complacent heart.

Facing the new Sect Master's repeated exhortations, the selected disciples all listened attentively.

Counting from the Luo Tian Society period, the Luo Tian Sect had been established for a full two hundred years.

In two hundred years, it had seen three generations of Sect Masters.

The first generation, Sima Huiniang, was selfless and impartial, recognizing no kinship, only acting for the sect's interests. For the sake of the sect, her eldest brother Sima Xian died in battle, and her third brother Sima Wenjie didn't even receive resources for Initiation, eventually even betraying them out of resentment. It could be said that the Luo Tian Sect cultivators were extremely convinced by the first-generation Sect Master Sima Huiniang; her authority was something no one dared to violate.

The second generation, Yao Mingyue, was not particularly outstanding. Under her stewardship, the Luo Tian Sect gradually declined. But no one blamed her; everyone understood that under the circumstances at the time, Yao Mingyue, who was only at the Initiate stage, had already done well enough.

As for the third-generation Sect Master, Li Yingzhang, since taking office, he had kept a low profile. But gradually, without anyone noticing, he had completely sorted out the internal affairs of the sect. Moreover, from top to bottom, he maintained just the right relationship with everyone, handling social niceties with precision.

From an outsider's perspective, Li Yingzhang seemed to have a very easy time as Sect Master.

Above him, the Supreme Elder suppressed everything.

Below him, the cultivators, having endured a hundred years of war, had fully tempered their sect cohesion.

What he did now was to fill in the gaps, sort out the major and minor matters within the sect, and then proceed steadily, trying his best to stimulate everyone's potential.

It looked easy, but actually doing it involved a level of trouble that outsiders couldn't understand.

After selecting twenty Initiate cultivators, Tianxuan walked over.

"As for the Adept-level cultivators, have you decided which three to send?"

Li Yingzhang showed a rare moment of hesitation.

According to his thinking, even though the Supreme Elder possessed heaven-defying abilities, there was no room for carelessness. Naturally, he should send the sect's strongest Adept-level combatants to accompany him to Lingtian Pass.

Looking across the Luo Tian Sect, the strongest Adept-level combatants were naturally Wang Yuan, Sima Huiniang, and the newly arrived Elder Tianxuan standing before him.

But the Supreme Elder seemed to have a different idea...

---

"I don't need to go?"

"No."

"What about that big fellow in the earth vein?"

"Black King doesn't need to go either. He'll stay at Daqing Mountain and guard the sect with you. Hah, it's precisely because he came back early that I have the confidence to go out for a stroll."

"Who are you planning to take?"

"Chu Kui counts as one. He's fought alongside me and knows my style. Besides, he formed his core early, so he doesn't need to stabilize his realm. Yang Pingdu will also come along. As the sect's Law Enforcement Elder, he's good at disciplining the disciples and followers, and I'll also take the opportunity to examine his cultivation progress. Finally, I'll bring your disciple along."

"You mean Little Dragon?"

"Yes. As the current leading figure of the younger generation in the Luo Tian Sect, the more experiences he has and the greater the reputation he builds, the more it will help boost the confidence of the lower-level disciples within the sect. What, you're reluctant to part with your precious disciple?"

Wang Yuan put down the bamboo slip in his hand, his expression perfectly calm.

"It's always good to let him experience more of the storms of the cultivation world. Otherwise, he'll always think too well of people's hearts. But if you only take these people, if you really run into trouble, there won't be anyone to help you."

Upon hearing this, Luo Chen laughed heartily.

"You're overthinking it. With my current reputation, if I really encounter trouble, there will be plenty of people willing to help me."

Suddenly, his smile faded.

"On the contrary, with such a great reputation, if I were to also bring strong experts like you and Black King by my side, wouldn't everyone see the Luo Tian Sect as a major future threat?"

"A threat?"

Wang Yuan unconsciously furrowed his brow.

But then, he realized the special nature of Luo Chen's current identity.

A generation's Grandmaster of the Alchemy Dao was a top-tier figure capable of propping up an entire major sect.

If the younger disciples were also all exceptionally talented, anyone would feel threatened!

Back then, Sage Shenyuan could personally nurture five Sage-level cultivators. With Luo Chen's alchemy skills, given time, he would only surpass that number!

Could the Eastern Wilderness accommodate such a formidable existence?

"A person who shows his edge too sharply—one is enough. The rest of you still need to bide your time and hide your capabilities, waiting for the day you can make a stunning debut."

Luo Chen said this.

Then, with a sweep of his hand across the table, he laid out a row of jade bottles and a jade slip.

"This is the Black Emperor Ointment. I recently refined it in my spare time. Although it's extremely painful to use, it has a miraculous effect on strengthening the physique. You're just one step away from the fourth realm of body refinement. Perhaps these external things can help you. Unfortunately, the sect doesn't have many raw materials, so I could only refine these ten bottles. The corresponding usage methods, refining experiences, and some taboos are all recorded on the jade slip."

Wang Yuan glanced at them and naturally put them away.

He had never doubted anything Luo Chen gave him.

Then, he naturally handed Luo Chen a scroll.

"What's this?" Luo Chen asked curiously.

He casually unrolled the scroll, which was covered in some inexplicable footprints, showing no discernible pattern at all.

Wang Yuan said solemnly, "Do you still remember that set of *Wind, Moon, Thunder, Cloud: The Immortal Path* you gave me before?"

"This?" Luo Chen was surprised, realizing something.

Wang Yuan nodded. "This is exactly the missing technique I deduced by combining it with the changes in qi and blood. It doesn't focus on attack, but on maneuvering and dodging during battle. The steps are strange and unpredictable. It might differ from the original Moon Step, but in my opinion, there's absolutely no gap."

"However, if you want to practice it, you lack astral qi. Relying purely on a strong physique and dharma power, your agility might be slightly inferior to us body refiners."

Saying this, Wang Yuan let out a slightly regretful sigh.

"The complete *Wind, Moon, Thunder, Cloud: The Immortal Path* is definitely a top-tier fourth-grade body refinement technique. It combines rapid attacks, combat mobility, long-distance travel, and instantaneous burst power, allowing body refiners to completely erase the gap with qi cultivators. Unfortunately, besides the Thunder Kick, a full three techniques are missing. With my current ability, I can only make up for the Moon Step. As for the remaining Wind Leg and Cloud Slippers, I'm helpless."

Luo Chen listened to Wang Yuan's lament while studying the scroll with great interest.

Those inexplicable footprints, when his qi and blood covered them, seemed to come alive, becoming agile and spirited.

Clearly, this was a template Wang Yuan had inscribed in his own way. Without a strong qi and blood cultivator, even if one obtained this scroll, they couldn't learn it.

Under his perception, those footprints, which resembled crescent moons, were not only incredibly agile but also filled with powerful explosive force.

Luo Chen instantly understood that Wang Yuan had also incorporated the Voidburst Step he himself had created.

In that case, learning it would be much easier for him.

He put away the scroll in one swift motion, quite excited.

"Having two techniques is already very good. If I get the chance in the future, I'll collect more leg-related techniques for you to study."

"The Luo Tian Sect is in your hands to guard. If you encounter any trouble, feel free to order Black King around. He'll listen to you."

Having said that, he took the scroll and hurriedly left the Scripture Library.

As he came out, he brushed past a gray-robed cultivator who was sweeping the floor with his head down.

Luo Chen's steps paused, but he said nothing.

After he left, the gray-robed cultivator raised his head, his expression complex as he watched Luo Chen's retreating figure.

A cold wind blew, and his throat felt strange, causing him to cough uncontrollably.

Perhaps the coughing was too loud, startling the person inside the Scripture Library.

Wang Yuan's deep voice came out: "Zui, clean the third floor today. Min Longyu recently rearranged the mountain-protecting grand formation, and the earth vein tremors caused quite a bit of dust to fall on the shelves."

The gray-robed cultivator named "Zui" acknowledged, then slowly climbed the stairs, his figure disappearing into the depths of the sea of books.

---

The news that Alchemy Sect Master Luo Chen would personally come to Lingtian Pass quickly spread throughout the entire majestic city.

Countless cultivators were paying attention to the movements of this powerhouse who had risen like a meteor.

Rumors said he had risen from humble beginnings, possessing great personal charisma, and had many followers even back when he was at the Qi Refinement stage.

Rumors said his combat ability was quite formidable, once being undefeated among those below the Adept stage in an entire domain, with a brilliant record of being the best among Initiate cultivators.

Rumors said he had entered the sacred land of the demon race and emerged alive, his body shrouded in mystery.

Especially since returning from the hundred years in the Northern Sea, he had already reached the Sage realm, adding even more legendary color to his story.

Upon his return to the Eastern Wilderness, he suppressed Motian, widely networked using his alchemy skills. At his Nascent Soul Ceremony, the Lingtian City Lord personally congratulated him. Sage Xianhe publicly declared Luo Chen his friend. The Fenghua Palace had a marriage alliance with him. And the old and powerful Nine Spirit Primogenitor straightforwardly praised Luo Chen for having the demeanor of a gentleman.

Regardless of how much truth and falsehood there was in these rumors.

At least everyone knew one thing: Luo Chen had killed Yin Sixiang, the most promising Adept Great Circle cultivator of the younger generation of the Five Elements Divine Sect, in front of countless people, and yet he remained perfectly safe.

The founder of the so-called number one sect of the Extreme East, Sage Shenyuan, had still not come to demand an explanation from him.

From this, it could be seen that Luo Chen had already become a force to be reckoned with!

Therefore, upon learning that Luo Chen would personally arrive at Lingtian Pass, many cultivators prepared in advance to catch a glimpse of his true appearance.

That time came quickly.

A large cloud carriage flew at a moderate pace over the territory of Fenghua Domain.

Unlike the previous Sixfold Imperial Flying Cloud Carriage, which emphasized luxurious ostentation, this one had been greatly modified by Min Longyu and was now completely different.

Its banners flew high, emblazoned with the Luo Tian Sect's pattern of a cauldron rising with the sun.

One by one, spirited Qi Refinement cultivators stood on both sides, holding magical artifacts. Spirit cranes circled and danced on the periphery, occasionally landing back on the cloud carriage's deck to rest.

Initiate True Cultivators with extraordinary auras walked among them.

Occasionally, one or two Adept Superior figures could be glimpsed.

Houses and halls of various sizes rose and fell, the light of formations flickering faintly.

It could be imagined that once thrown into battle, this cloud carriage would transform into a mobile fortress, unleashing powerful firepower outward.

In the central hall, Luo Chen felt both satisfied and somewhat regretful.

Min Longyu's modification of the Sixfold Imperial Flying Cloud Carriage could be considered as perfect as possible, with targeted arrangements made specifically for the current situation. But one couldn't make bricks without straw. The cloud carriage itself was only a magical-artifact-grade flying boat used by the Hundred Crafts Mountain to showcase the Mountain Master's prestige; its offensive and defensive foundations were too poor.

If he could find the skeleton of a fourth-grade Demon Emperor and integrate it in, he could greatly enhance its upper limit.

Just like the Sword-Halberd of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, which could cut through thorns and brambles on the vast sea and roam the skies without anyone daring to block it.

"Chu Kui, we're going to the battlefield together again. How do you feel?"

Chu Kui, sitting beside him, said casually, "No particular feeling. Now that you're so powerful, just protect me, won't you?"

"Haha, you, you... where's the spirit you had back then when you took the initiative to invite me to roam the ten-thousand-li yellow sand?" Luo Chen said with a smile.

Chu Kui said lazily, "Back then, I thought I was someone important, so naturally I had high spirits, even calling myself Heavenly Champion. But after forming my golden core, I realized that beyond the sky there is another sky, beyond the people there are other people. The strong in this world are as numerous as the fish in the river. With my ability, it's already not easy to survive without dying, let alone stand out from the crowd."

Luo Chen smiled and didn't provoke him further.

People were different. Some would rise against the current, while others, upon seeing the blue sky and bright moon, would realize their own insignificance and thus retreat in their hearts.

To put it nicely, that was self-awareness.

And Chu Kui's current state of mind was actually quite suitable for the Luo Tian Sect.

The younger generation was too restless!

Having Chu Kui keep an eye on things would at least prevent any major chaos.

"I hope I can bring this batch of disciples back safe and sound."

Chu Kui was slightly surprised. "With you in charge, could something still go wrong?"

Luo Chen shook his head. "In the face of an overwhelming grand trend, even a Divine Transformation sacred ground could turn to ashes, let alone a mere Sage like me."

Chu Kui was stunned for a moment, but recalling the despair he felt for his own dim future path after forming his golden core, he now empathized with Luo Chen's feelings.

He was at the Sage realm, and the reality of the world he saw could only be deeper than his own.

Maintaining a sense of reverence was never wrong.

Luo Chen didn't continue chatting with him. He took a book from his storage ring and began to read it carefully.

**[Overlord Blade Domain: Occupied by the Overlord Blade Sect. This sect has flourished for only eighteen hundred years, but its internal cohesion is extremely strong! Ever since they conquered a domain during the Expansion War, the Overlord Blade Sect has dominated it alone, forbidding any force from setting foot in their territory. When the great tide turned and ten thousand cultivators retreated, only the Overlord Blade Sect stood firm like a mountain, defending their domain to the death. Now, the Overlord Blade Domain, along with the Azure Sky and Fire Ridge domains, are tightly surrounded by the demon army. The fate of the cultivators in these three domains is unknown.]**

"Did these Overlord Blade Sect cultivators get their brains addled from refining their blades? Why didn't they retreat when the demon army came?"

Luo Chen muttered a few words and continued reading the records below.

**[Azure Sky Domain: Occupied by Azure Sky Mountain. The founder of Azure Sky Mountain, the Azure Sky Elder, cultivated an ancient divine art. When his domain unfolded, it was renowned as the Azure Sea and Blue Sky. His successors, however, were not skilled enough in their arts, and it is rumored they can no longer manifest the Azure Sea and Blue Sky...]**

Overlord Blade, Azure Sky, and Fire Ridge—these were precisely the three frontline domains before Lingtian Pass.

Under the siege of the demon race, they had held out for nearly ten years.

This achievement had drawn the attention of the world.

But as time passed and the demon army gradually gathered, no one was optimistic about them anymore.

Once the three domains were breached, the demon army's spearhead would point directly at Lingtian Pass!

When the cloud carriage arrived beneath Lingtian Pass, Luo Chen closed the book with a solemn expression.

Looking up at the towering city walls with their fluttering banners, he murmured in his heart:

"I only hope that during my mission period, I don't encounter the demons attacking the pass."
