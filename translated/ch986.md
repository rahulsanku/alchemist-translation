# Chapter 986: The Fallen Buddha, the Buddha Land on Earth

"Fallen? What does that mean?" Luo Chen asked.

"Literal meaning."

"Hmm?"

Luo Chen frowned slightly, looking at the white-robed monk with confusion.

Monk Xuan Du sighed and said softly, "This matter... we must start from five hundred years ago..."

As he narrated in detail, Luo Chen and Azure Frost finally gained some understanding of the Buddhist sect's secrets.

Mahakala was a peerless genius!

He was hailed as the monk with the highest Buddha nature and the strongest cultivation talent in the Hanging Temple in a thousand years.

His depth of understanding of Buddhist scriptures and his speed of cultivation were unimaginable to ordinary people.

Before reaching three hundred years of age, he had already entered the Vajra Realm.

Early on, the previous abbot had appointed him as the head of the Prajna Hall.

Not only did his realm advance rapidly, but eight hundred years ago, he had single-handedly subdued the Golden-Winged Great Peng, which was then a fourth-rank desolate beast, adding a guardian spirit venerable to the Hanging Temple.

Because of this achievement, Mahakala's prestige within the temple grew higher and higher.

In the end, he did not disappoint the expectations of the many eminent monks in the temple, successfully stepping into the Arhat realm five hundred years ago.

"That is what you call the Ascendant stage." Monk Xuan Du specifically mentioned this for the convenience of Luo Chen and Azure Frost's understanding.

Luo Chen nodded. "I do know a little about this information. But five hundred years ago, after he achieved the Arhat realm, what exactly happened that led to what you call the 'fall'?"

"The fall did not appear suddenly at the beginning, but progressed gradually until now."

Monk Xuan Du softly chanted a Buddha's name, then explained: "Brother Kasyapa advanced too quickly. Even though his Buddha nature was extremely high and his comprehension and insight into Buddhist scriptures far surpassed ours, the time was too short. He could only master the Dharma, but found it difficult to penetrate the principles of Buddhism. Thus, hidden dangers were planted."

"Five hundred years ago, after achieving Arhatship, he felt that the Hanging Temple had very few who had attained the Arhat fruit, far fewer than holy lands like the Dao Sect or Nether Origin. The remaining fellow disciples also had their shortcomings. So he had a sudden idea, attempting to enlighten the guardian spirit venerable, the Golden-Winged Great Peng."

"Unexpectedly, his Buddhist principles were still shallow, and his divine powers could not overcome karma, causing the Golden-Winged Great Peng to sweep away the relics of thirty-six eminent monks and rebel against the Hanging Temple."

"If it had only been that, it would have been bearable. But it was his first failure in life, and under it, karmic obstacles and inner demons suddenly arose. He gradually became extreme."

"At that time, Brother Huiping once said that Kasyapa had been influenced by heaven and earth!"

Influenced by heaven and earth?

Luo Chen had a deep understanding of this term, which could be replaced by the word "assimilation."

No matter which system of cultivation one practiced, when reaching the fifth rank, interactions with heaven and earth would become frequent.

In this process, cultivators would heavily intercept heaven and earth's nature, comprehending the true meaning of laws. But the more they seized and comprehended, the easier it was to be assimilated by heaven and earth.

If one was not careful, their Dao heart would become unstable, sinking into the bitter sea.

Perhaps this description was not vivid enough.

But just look at the fate of Sage Divine Origin, and you could see a glimpse.

Under the madness of qi deviation, forcibly demanding power that did not match one's own, the only outcome was death!

Luo Chen keenly caught a familiar name in the other's words.

"Monk Huiping?"

He suddenly remembered that Monk Huiping was also from the Hanging Temple, and it was only a few hundred years ago that he left the Hanging Temple, going to the Eastern Wilderness to establish his own sect and spread the Buddhist path.

"Could it be that he also left the Hanging Temple because of this matter back then?"

Monk Xuan Du nodded slightly. "Exactly so!"

Luo Chen was speechless. "I hadn't noticed before that you were actually fellow disciples. Your Dharma names don't seem related at all."

"Buddhism emphasizes equality. So-called seniority is merely for convenience of address. Moreover, Brother Huiping comes from the Chan sect lineage, while Brother Kasyapa comes from the Pure Land sect lineage. Our Dharma names and seniority would naturally differ, but cultivation has no taboos on age or seniority. Thus, we adopted the most common practice in the cultivation world—whoever achieves mastery first is addressed as senior, for convenience." Xuan Du said this, then continued the previous topic. "Brother Huiping's realm was above ours, and he was also one of the eight great seat masters among the one hundred and eight eminent monks back then. He was the first to discover that something was wrong with Brother Kasyapa. When his persuasion proved ineffective, he used the propagation of Buddhism as an excuse and left the Hanging Temple alone."

Azure Frost, who had been silent all along, suddenly spoke.

"Monk Huiping is indeed not bad. Back then, when the three great cultivators of the Eastern Wilderness joined forces against me, only he could withstand my attacks head-on. In fact, if I hadn't unfolded my Azure Luan True Body, I might have been suppressed by him instead."

Luo Chen had the deepest experience of how strong the Azure Luan True Body was.

The Great Five Elements Sword Formation, combined with the Law-Suppressing Five Elemental Cones, plus the Soul-Weaving Needle, could not subdue her, forcing Luo Chen to release his lifebound treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, just to barely suppress her.

Even so, refining her was extremely difficult.

The Monk Huiping of the past, who could face the Azure Luan True Body head-on, was likely already not inferior to Luo Chen's current late Archaic stage in terms of body refinement.

Monk Xuan Du glanced at Azure Frost but did not show any surprise.

Clearly, he had collected intelligence on this matter and knew Azure Frost's general situation.

He continued: "After Brother Huiping left, I also gradually noticed that Brother Kasyapa's temperament was changing. To be safe, I also left the Hanging Temple. Not only that, but while Brother Kasyapa was cultivating, I secretly took away the Buddhist heavy treasure, the Pure Land, to prevent him from relying on this sharp tool to bring disaster to the world."

After hearing this, Luo Chen fell into thought.

The space he was currently in was exactly what the Pure Land had transformed into. Being inside it was almost no different from the outside world.

This wonder surpassed even the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven and the Yin-Yang Remnant Realm.

With such a treasure to assist him, Mahakala, who had already cultivated to the Arhat realm, would be able to act without restraint and without hindrance.

If he truly brought disaster to the world, who could stop him, aside from top experts like Rosycloud, who did not originally belong to the Mountains and Seas Realm?

What about Divine Lord Youli?

He had the strength, but he was restricted by the fifth-rank Fortune Gu and suppressed by that ocean of origin qi, unable to leave that small patch of land at the cloud sea's peak.

"Not long after I left the Hanging Temple, the monks inside, influenced by Brother Kasyapa, also gradually changed in temperament, becoming more extreme. This extremeness even affected many fellow Buddhists on the Western Desert Buddhist soil."

Luo Chen suddenly understood. "No wonder the several abbots of Mount Wutai I encountered earlier, despite their considerable realms, were quick to fight and kill, showing no trace of a monk's compassion. Were they also affected by this?"

"Mount Wutai is not far from the Hanging Temple, and once bore the duty of guarding the Green Gauze Screen. They have likely already sunk into it." Monk Xuan Du spoke with compassion and pity, his words full of regret. "The so-called claim of capturing Fellow Daoist Azure Frost as a guardian spirit beast is merely an imitation act born of extreme worship of Mahakala."

Eight hundred years ago, Mahakala captured the Golden-Winged Great Peng, adding a guardian spirit venerable to the Hanging Temple.

Eight hundred years later, the monks of Mount Wutai wanted to imitate him, but what they encountered was not the Golden-Winged Great Peng, but Azure Frost—and Azure Frost accompanied by Luo Chen!

Their defeat was only to be expected.

Luo Chen suddenly said, "Master, you don't mean to have us deal with Mahakala, do you?"

Monk Xuan Du smiled slightly. "The Alchemy Sect seems eager to try!"

Luo Chen was helpless. "Where did you get the idea that I was eager to try?"

Monk Xuan Du laughed. "To have such a thought, is that not eagerness to try?"

Luo Chen fell silent. Azure Frost, however, looked at him curiously.

Indeed, ordinary people would not think in that direction at all.

Even Azure Frost, who had twice faced off against Ascendant-level great powers, never thought about how to deal with such an existence.

Monk Xuan Du shook his head. "Both of you are outstanding among the world's cultivators, with few rivals under heaven. But an existence comparable to an Ascendant, like an Arhat, is still beyond your ability to oppose. Especially since you both have many hidden dangers and too many weaknesses. Even your allies could become burdens instead. This time, I invited you here to ask for your help in guarding the Pure Land, and nothing more."

"Guard the Pure Land?" Luo Chen was puzzled.

Azure Frost suddenly said, "Since we entered, the entrance to the Pure Land has never been closed."

Luo Chen was startled. What was so special about that?

The entrance being wide open was a sign of sincerity, making it convenient for them to leave at any time.

But then, he realized something was wrong.

He poured mana into his eyes, channeling source power into his eye sockets.

Amidst a brilliant golden light, his Crimson-Eyed Golden Pupils suddenly reappeared!

In his line of sight, faint spiritual energy could be seen entering the Pure Land from the outside, converging toward the white-robed monk before him.

"You are absorbing spiritual energy!" Luo Chen exclaimed.

"Benefactor, these eyes of yours are truly well-cultivated!" Monk Xuan Du praised Luo Chen's spiritual eyes once again, then did not avoid admitting what he was doing. "Yes, I have already begun preparing to break through to the Arhat realm."

Luo Chen shook his head. "Master, why are you in such a hurry? Such a breakthrough cannot be achieved in a short time. What if Mahakala interferes? Besides, the two of us haven't agreed to help you yet."

"There is no time." Monk Xuan Du said, a look of fear in his eyes. "The last time Mahakala struck from afar and repelled the Ancient Red Sand Scorpion, I discovered that he had already fallen into extremes. Now that the Hanging Temple's mountain gate is wide open, it also means he is prepared to turn the entire Mountains and Seas Realm into a Buddha Land on Earth. I must stop him!"

Luo Chen was puzzled. "Wouldn't it be a good thing for you Buddhist cultivators if all the world were in a Buddha's kingdom?"

Monk Xuan Du said calmly, "If everyone is good and understands the Dharma, what difference does a kingdom make or not? Conversely, if one uses the propagation of the Dharma as an excuse to commit acts of slaughter and conquest, that is merely indulging in greed, anger, and ignorance, creating needless killing."

"Master sees things clearly."

Luo Chen praised him, but it was only a praise.

Although the other spoke terrifyingly, invoking the common people and the Buddha Land on Earth, Luo Chen did not feel deeply moved.

From the beginning, Luo Chen was a selfish person.

Even though he had changed a lot along his cultivation journey, taking on many responsibilities and burdens, what he cared about most were still his friends and family, and his own cultivation.

His life soul was still in the hands of the Dao Sect. How could he have the leisure to pity the common people?

Azure Frost had the same reaction!

What she cared about was her sister, Rosycloud, and Green Parasol Mountain.

As long as Mahakala did not come to provoke Green Parasol Mountain, let the heavens fall and the earth flood!

Monk Xuan Du did not seem surprised by Luo Chen and Azure Frost's reactions. He did not force them either, because from the beginning, he had said this was a cooperation.

Facing their indifference, Monk Xuan Du said calmly, "If the two benefactors lend me a hand, not only will the teleportation array be returned to you, but I can also help you resolve the hidden dangers on your bodies."

At these words, Luo Chen and Azure Frost's attitudes immediately became serious.

Because earlier, Monk Xuan Du had said they had hidden dangers.

Luo Chen was fine with it. He knew that his cultivation drew from a hundred schools, and it was inevitable that he had absorbed some dregs, so hidden dangers were normal. But Azure Frost was somewhat puzzled.

"Xuan Du, what hidden dangers do I have?" Azure Frost asked directly, getting straight to the point without any beating around the bush.

Monk Xuan Du said calmly, "Your hidden danger is not much different from that of my Brother Kasyapa back then. It is that your cultivation time is too short, causing a disparity in your state of mind, making you prone to extremes."

Azure Frost frowned. "Among demonic beings, extreme temperament, great joy, and great anger are common occurrences. How can that be called a hidden danger?"

Monk Xuan Du countered, "Then why does the benefactor use a spirit treasure to protect your soul?"

Azure Frost was speechless.

She hadn't expected the other to see through this. She didn't know if it was due to his own strength or the wonder of the Pure Land.

Monk Xuan Du said softly, "I do not know how Benefactor Azure Frost cultivated such great power at this age. But by the lifespan of a divine beast like the Azure Luan, you have not even reached adulthood. Yet you blindly seek the true meaning of laws and the insights of the Dao. Can you truly withstand the backlash of the Dao in the future? Or have you been influenced by someone else, already given up on the future, and only seek the present?"

"Silence!"

A sharp shout suddenly exploded.

Azure Frost's face was as cold as ice as she rebuked the white-robed monk.

"I won't allow you to slander my sister!"

Faced with this sudden fallout, Luo Chen was caught off guard.

But soon, he realized the subtext in Monk Xuan Du's words.

Azure Frost's strength was indeed strange.

It was said that demonic beasts had lifespans far exceeding humans, and the price of a long life was slow cultivation speed.

Azure Frost's original form was the divine beast Azure Luan, which was said to have a natural lifespan of several thousand years.

Calculated by normal time, Azure Frost was indeed still a bit short of adulthood.

But she had already comprehended the true meaning of spatial laws, looking down on all others.

Beneath such proud abilities lay the fact that her soul was extremely weak.

In other words, the seemingly all-powerful Azure Frost had a huge weakness!

However, that weakness was temporarily covered by the soul-defense spirit treasure, the Nether Soul Pearl, given by Devil Lord Heaven-Refining.

"So, was this forced growth intentional on Rosycloud's part?" Luo Chen thought silently.

Monk Xuan Du was not angered by Azure Frost's rebuke. He only said calmly, "Whether it is slander or not lies in the heart. I only want to say that I have a method that can help you improve this weakness, at least to match your current realm."

This time, Azure Frost fell silent.

Silence was hesitation, and hesitation was temptation.

Monk Xuan Du did not press further. His gaze fell on Luo Chen.

Luo Chen smiled. "Master, do I also have hidden dangers?"

Monk Xuan Du's look at Luo Chen was somewhat complicated.

He first nodded, then shook his head.

"There are hidden dangers, but none are major. I don't know how you achieved such strength at this age."

Luo Chen understood. "Since the hidden dangers are not major, I imagine it will be difficult for you to propose a condition that moves me!"

Monk Xuan Du smiled slightly. "I will try my best!"
