# Chapter 757: Tearing Apart the Golden Body, Slaying an Enemy a Thousand Li Away

Within Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, it was suitable to comprehend the Dao, not suitable to cultivate.

Luo Chen had long since perceived this point.

Therefore, he had placed a great deal of time and energy into refining his combat methods.

If carefully calculated, the time since he formed his Nascent Soul could not be called very long.

Thus, after forming his Nascent Soul, he was not especially practiced in the various methods of this new realm. Before, he had also been busy dealing with the divine souls in his sea of consciousness, researching cultivation arts, gathering resources, and other miscellaneous matters, which led to him possessing many methods but lacking focus.

It was unlike when he was in the Adept stage, when his two combat systems had been extremely clear.

Often, before making a move, he would use Azure Yang Great Handprint to seize the opening advantage, then display all sorts of methods afterward, pressing the enemy down from beginning to end.

But the difference in time flow between Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven and the outside world had instead given him an opportunity to carefully polish all sorts of combat skills.

For example, this fourth-tier fire spell, Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain, had been cultivated by him to the grandmaster level in the short span of two years!

Do not look down upon this grandmaster level!

Although on the attribute panel, it had not yet reached the transformation realm, in the cultivation world, it was already the peak state of a spell.

At this moment!

The instant he used it at full strength, heaven and earth immediately changed color.

The enormous fire phoenix was extraordinarily divine and spirited, every fine strand of its fire feathers visible.

Its lifelike form was as if a true phoenix from the immortal realm had descended.

The moment the fire phoenix appeared, everyone changed expression.

Especially Fu Qinglan!

She had once personally observed Luo Chen use this spell, slaughtering fourth-tier illusion beasts with ease.

At that time, she had thought this was the limit of Luo Chen's spell.

But she had never expected that its power could actually climb once more!

Everything within a radius of a hundred li was enveloped beneath that violent might.

And on the enemy side, Yu Xu, Xiang Qi, and the others were even more horrified and shaken!

Before they had time to react, Yu Xu heard a low, fierce shout by her ear.

"Retreat!"

In Yu Xu's eyes, she saw Sage Heavenly Mountain strike backward with a palm, sending a powerful force onto her body.

She was just about to be shocked and angry when she instantly realized that this force was actually very gentle.

In an instant, she understood the other party's meaning.

Borrow the force, use force against force, and withdraw.

In Sage Heavenly Mountain's eyes, the piercing gale had already lifted the hood of his black robe, revealing a pale face.

"It is actually coming for me!"

From the very beginning, he had sensed Luo Chen's formidable points.

Thus, even under the two-person siege, he had always held back, avoiding the other party's full-strength attack while preparing to glimpse the other party's flaw and defeat him in one stroke.

Unexpectedly, Luo Chen had simply not used his full strength earlier. The moment he did, he directly locked his target onto Sage Heavenly Mountain.

"But if he thinks he can kill me with only this, he is thinking too lightly."

A snow-white lotus flower slowly flew out from his hand and covered his body.

When the fire phoenix arrived, the snow lotus abruptly swelled and grew.

A trace of pride appeared on Sage Heavenly Mountain's pale face, but in the next moment, only panic remained.

"This fire!"

Hiss...

Visible to the naked eye, the snow-white lotus showed a scorched yellow color.

This true artifact, once the sect-guarding true artifact of the Heavenly Mountain Cult, actually showed signs of shattering under the fire phoenix's impact.

Sage Heavenly Mountain no longer dared forcefully resist Luo Chen. He fled in panic, transforming into a streak of light and rushing toward the direction Yu Xu had gone, while behind him the fire phoenix's remaining force had not yet waned.

Luo Chen watched this scene, satisfaction appearing in his eyes.

With only one spell, he forced the other party to use a true artifact for defense, and even then, the other party could not block it.

As expected, his talent in spells, combined with years of effort, had finally revealed its true brilliance.

Next!

His gaze shifted, and his body suddenly moved.

Bang!

The void exploded, and Luo Chen abruptly appeared beside Wrathful Vajra.

The other party seemed to have been prepared long ago. His nine-ringed monk's staff abruptly smashed over.

Facing this monk's staff, Luo Chen previously would have needed to use all sorts of body techniques to deal with it, but at this moment he did not dodge at all, punching out.

With only a clang, peerless great force actually knocked that monk's staff from Wrathful Vajra's hand and sent it falling to the ground.

This scene left Wrathful Vajra's expression greatly horrified.

Through their earlier exchanges, he had naturally also discovered that the other party was a formidable body cultivator.

But in his judgment, Luo Chen was at most similar to his ordinary state and absolutely would not dare receive the monk's staff head-on.

Yet now, the bodily strength the other party displayed seemed to still be above his own, hammering an upper-grade magic treasure barehanded without the slightest effort.

Could it be that the brave display from before was still not the other party's full strength?

Then what had he been doing before?

Using me to test his hand?

"I cannot hold back!"

With a thought, Wrathful Vajra drew an illusory swastika barehanded and stamped it in reverse onto his own body.

The next moment.

Crack, crack, crack!

The sound of bones trembling rang out, and surging qi and blood rushed like a great river.

Golden astral qi circulated outward from his body.

His body also began rising section by section.

In the blink of an eye, an eighteen-foot golden figure appeared upon the earth.

Two heads strange and uncanny, four arms bold and majestic.

His four large hands held prayer beads, a golden alms bowl, and a precepts blade.

With a beckoning hand, the monk's staff entered his grip, leaving no hand empty.

He looked at the man opposite him, whose brows were slightly furrowed, and roared angrily, "Let me show you what the Great Vajra physique is!"

However, Luo Chen merely shook his head.

"In the end, it is not the Leakless Golden Body. It has only the appearance and is full of flaws. To break this kind of golden body, I need only one move."

Hearing this, the eyes on Wrathful Vajra's two heads widened hugely, and he appeared even more furious.

Luo Chen no longer wished to say anything more to him, and his figure abruptly moved.

When he appeared, the two were extremely close. Luo Chen punched out, and beside him was a blood-colored power like steam.

Wrathful Vajra was utterly unafraid. The golden alms bowl struck out, while the precepts blade chopped down at the same time.

Luo Chen lightly tapped with one finger, and the precepts blade was forced to fly back as if struck by lightning.

In the corner of his vision, the monk's staff came smashing down over his head.

Luo Chen struck backward with an elbow, knocking away the monk's staff, then borrowed the force to fly high into the sky. Afterward, he descended like Mount Tai pressing down, his kick like rushing thunder.

...

Move after move, form after form.

The scene seemed like before, but because several additional magic treasures had entered the battle, countless changes suddenly arose.

After several dozen exchanges, Wrathful Vajra stood upon the earth, looking at Luo Chen, who was breathing slightly, and laughed wildly.

"Defeat me in one move?"

"How arrogant, how shamelessly boastful!"

"Now a hundred moves have passed, and you have no way to deal with me at all. But I will no longer give you the chance to condense a spell. Die!"

Even though he had not withstood Luo Chen's fire phoenix spell head-on, lingering fear still remained in his heart.

Thus, he wanted to borrow close combat to deny the other party time to calmly cast spells.

The exchanges of body cultivators were too fast.

More than a hundred moves took place in only a blur of movement.

Outsiders only saw Luo Chen float backward, panting, while Wrathful Vajra fought with increasing courage, his Vajra physique rich with qi and blood, smoke rising like beacon fire.

Could Wilderness Wanderer really be only someone who spoke big?

Luo Chen lightly exhaled a breath of turbid air. His right hand lay flat before him, five fingers slightly spread, and each finger was connected to countless extremely tiny red threads.

"If I said one move, then it is one move!"

The instant his words fell.

His five fingers abruptly pulled, and the red threads all trembled at once.

Crack!

A sound of shattering rang with incomparable clarity through heaven and earth, and even more clearly beside Wrathful Vajra's ear.

He lowered his head to look.

One Buddhist hand fell to the ground for no reason.

Afterward, like a chain reaction, his thighs cracked, his four arms broke apart, and red fissures appeared all over his golden body.

Even on his thick neck, bowl-sized red lines rapidly expanded.

Countless fierce and tyrannical streams of astral qi poured uncontrollably from within his body, like a dam breaking and floodwaters rushing forth.

"This..."

Wrathful Vajra opened his mouth with difficulty, but his face was filled with cracked patterns like broken porcelain, leaving him unable to speak a complete sentence.

Countless thoughts filled his mind, replaying the earlier battle again and again.

Every time Luo Chen had attacked, every passive defense, every active strike...

Palm, fist, finger, claw; flicking, slapping, hooking, smashing; elbow strikes, knee strikes, kicks, empty steps...

Every time, a faint special power had remained. After each move, that faint power had entered his golden body.

A drop at a time seemed unworthy of notice.

But drops and streams gathered into a sea.

All of these things combined together.

"In the end, my physique has flaws, and that gave him an opportunity!"

As this thought flashed through his mind, the tall eighteen-foot golden body erupted with crackling explosions and began to break into pieces.

A white chess piece flew out from his body and enveloped his flesh.

The appearance of this scene already explained everything.

He had lost!

If not for the Heavenly Origin Sect Master, that Ascendant great power's method, he would have died here on the spot.

But one could imagine that after suffering such heavy injuries, even if he was teleported outside, he would suffer greatly.

Without several decades or a hundred years, he might not recover.

At this moment!

A voice transmission entered the white chess piece.

Before Wrathful Vajra dispersed, he bent a finger and pointed toward the string of prayer beads he wore around his neck year-round.

"Wilderness Wanderer, I will wait for you outside!"

Luo Chen sneered.

A defeated opponent, still making threats? Still making a dying strike?

The prayer beads flew out and abruptly trembled.

The golden thread snapped, and eighteen beads transformed into a ring that flew toward Luo Chen.

Luo Chen was calm and unafraid. Since he could dismantle that eighteen-foot golden body barehanded, he could not possibly fear a mere upper-grade magic treasure.

He did not even need any special method.

One Sunburst spell exploded with him at the center, transforming into a storm that pushed apart the encirclement formed by the eighteen prayer beads.

Suddenly!

In Luo Chen's eyes, a swimming fish entered his sight.

"Is it Sage Heavenly Mountain's flying sword?"

This flying sword had previously entered the void, and he had assumed it had also fled back after Sage Heavenly Mountain retreated. Yet now, at some unknown time, it had silently arrived before Luo Chen.

Just as Luo Chen was astonished.

The tiny flying sword revealed its original form. It was a creature that resembled a fish.

Gu?

The next moment, the swimming-fish flying sword erupted with endless sword light, completely enveloping Luo Chen within!

Hiss! Hiss! Hiss!

Between heaven and earth, dazzling sword radiance filled everything, making people dare not look directly, and chilling the flesh.

Seeing this scene from the side, Fu Qinglan's eyes nearly split, and her body trembled.

Luo Chen, who had originally held the great advantage, had actually encountered such a thing when victory was already decided.

If...

There was no if!

Within the endless sword qi storm, Luo Chen stepped out, his robes snapping loudly, without the slightest injury visible.

He reached back and clenched his hand!

That sword qi storm abruptly shrank, and a swimming fish was in the eye of the storm.

The fish glanced at Luo Chen with great spirituality, then entered the void and was about to flee.

"Want to run?"

Luo Chen smiled ferociously, and his divine sense expanded wildly, finally reaching a thousand li away.

When he saw that pale man, he began forming seals and drawing qi!

A thousand li away.

Yu Xu looked nervously at Sage Heavenly Mountain, who was sitting cross-legged on the ground.

When the other party opened his eyes, she hurriedly asked, "Did you succeed?"

Sage Heavenly Mountain shook his head regretfully. "This person's strength far exceeds our imagination. One spell made me flee in sorry state, and that terrifying attainment in body cultivation even let him tear apart Wrathful Vajra's eighteen-foot golden body by force. My fourth-tier Swordfish Gu could not threaten him even when it erupted suddenly."

Yu Xu's face was full of disappointment. "Even that did not injure him?"

Sage Heavenly Mountain gave a sound of acknowledgment and rose calmly. A weak swimming fish emerged from the void and was swallowed into his mouth.

"Let us go. Although I did not injure Wilderness Wanderer, I did anger him somewhat. At this moment, he is using some kind of spell, wanting to seek revenge against me. But do not worry. At such a distance, he has no way to do anything to us."

A Sage's divine sense could cover a radius of a thousand li or even several thousand li. When fighting at full strength, the area affected by their destructive power was also roughly within this range.

But this usually referred to the destructive power caused when both sides moved and fought.

If the distance was too great, then at the same realm, launching a spell that could threaten the other party was not especially possible.

This was precisely Sage Heavenly Mountain's reason for being so fearless.

It was also his confidence in daring to suddenly ambush Luo Chen only after fleeing a thousand li away.

If it succeeded, it succeeded. If it failed, it would not matter.

At this moment, what he was instead sighing over in his heart was Wrathful Vajra's "fall."

Inside this grotto-heaven, the ones most suited to battle were actually high-level body cultivators like Wrathful Vajra.

Qi refiners like them, however, were restrained in many ways.

The spiritual qi of heaven and earth was scattered, Sage domain power was weakened, and using all sorts of magic treasures consumed a great deal of magic power, far from as simple and direct as body cultivators.

Amid these sighs, Sage Heavenly Mountain subconsciously raised his head.

In the void, a giant dragon head suddenly emerged from the cloud layer.

Its horns and features were majestic, with long azure whiskers and beard.

It spat raging flames, and its two eyes were furious.

His face changed drastically, and he pushed away the dazed Yu Xu.

"Go to the old place and wait for me!"

The next moment, Flame Dragon Descends, destroying everything!

Yu Xu flew back several hundred zhang before she could even steady herself...

"Pfft!"

She spat out a mouthful of blood and looked in terror at the giant pit where vast magic power lingered.

Within it, Sage Heavenly Mountain's figure was already gone.

Yu Xu swallowed and could not help looking toward the sky, then back toward the path they had come from.

A divine sense swept over her body, making her cold from head to toe.

"From a thousand li away, he blasted a same-realm cultivator dead!"

"Could this spell be the Void Nether One-Qi Slash, one of our Dao Sect's Seven Great Inherited Divine Abilities?"

"No, absolutely not. That was clearly some kind of fire spell, and Wilderness Wanderer is only an external affairs guest elder of Star Gate. He is not even a proper guest elder, so he has no chance at all to learn my Dao Sect's divine abilities. Fu Qinglan herself does not yet have the qualifications to learn the Seven Great Divine Abilities, and it would be even more impossible for her to pass one to Wilderness Wanderer."

Amid the shock in her heart, thoughts rose and fell one after another.

Just as she was at a complete loss, a faint glimmer of light rose shakily, carrying a storage ring into her hand.

Looking at the firefly-like glow in her hand, Yu Xu let out a long breath.

"Sage Heavenly Mountain has a way to die in another's place. At most, he will recuperate for a while. I still have a chance."

After this thought flashed through her mind, she quickly left the area.

Only, before leaving, she still could not help glancing in Luo Chen's direction, her eyes full of terror.

...

"Even that did not kill him?"

Luo Chen released the seal, his expression somewhat tired.

The spell Flame Dragon Descends was more difficult than Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain.

Even now, he had only barely raised its proficiency to the proficient level.

He had thought that by catching Sage Heavenly Mountain unprepared, he could kill him, but he had not expected the other party to survive by some kind of substitute-death method.

"So this is a Southern Frontier gu cultivator?"

"Their battle style is so strange and sinister. If it were another Sage, they would probably have suffered a huge loss long ago."

"If I encounter Southern Frontier gu cultivators again in the future, I must be doubly cautious."

Thinking this, Luo Chen looked around and felt bored again.

He had fought for nothing. He had gained no spoils at all, only a dozen or so prayer beads.

He had wasted a portion of magic power for nothing.

It would be better if battles like this happened less often.

Fu Qinglan carefully approached.

"Xiang Qi ran."

"Mm. I know."

Luo Chen nodded.

As the Xiang family genius of the first of the Thirty-Six Aristocratic Clans, Xiang Qi naturally had no shortage of treasures.

When Luo Chen was battling the two great experts, the other party had used a treasure to withdraw far away. After seeing Wrathful Vajra defeated, he was even the first to vanish.

Let him run!

Without a Dao protector's support, whether hunting illusion beasts or comprehending laws, everything would be countless times more troublesome for him.

It could be said that even if Xiang Qi remained in Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, he could no longer pose any threat to Luo Chen and Fu Qinglan.

"Let us go!"

Luo Chen rose into the air.

Countless words filled Fu Qinglan's heart, but she had no way to begin.

After following Luo Chen for a while, she discovered something was not right.

"Are we not returning to the original place?"

"We are changing locations."

"Are you afraid the others will come looking for us?"

"That is one concern. After all, the commotion earlier was too great. But the main reason is that there are already very few illusion beasts in this area, and hunting them requires more and more time. Changing locations will make capturing illusion beasts more convenient."

"Mm. I will listen to you."

The figures of the man and woman gradually receded into the distance, and the sound of their conversation also scattered into the dry-hot wind.

Only that devastated earth and those broken mountains remained, telling of the one-sided battle that had once happened here.

*(End of Chapter)*
