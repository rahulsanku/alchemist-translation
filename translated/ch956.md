# Chapter 956: Opening the Door to Enter the Realm, an Army Presses on the Border

On the flying boat, inside a quiet room.

Luo Chen was reading a book while taking small sips of the Five Temple Immortal Brew.

This was one of his methods for suppressing the pain within his body.

Although it appeared that he had broken through to the late stage of Archaic Fourth Order in just one short year at Mi Realm Mountain, in reality, the *Hundred Poisons Body-Tempering Art* was not a quick-success method, but a water-grinding technique that required daily accumulation.

The toxins released by the Hundred Poisons King Gu slowly tempered every part of his body, bit by bit.

Through a process of constant destruction and constant reconstruction, areas deep within his flesh that were normally difficult to hone became tougher.

And this tempering was ongoing, without a moment's pause.

Therefore, pain was his constant companion!

Why did Purple Robed Marquis say this art was difficult to practice? He also cultivated body refinement, so why didn't he also practice this art?

The reason lay precisely here. This kind of pain was no longer simple agony; it also affected normal qi refinement cultivation.

With Luo Chen having one body and four souls, he could naturally block it out somewhat when breathing and refining qi.

But in daily life, it was ultimately unavoidable.

Fortunately, he still had some stock of the spiritual wine obtained from the Dieyun Sect. Taking an occasional sip, the pleasure of fine wine could numb many things.

Moreover, the Five Temple Immortal Brew had an extremely remarkable nourishing effect on the five organs of the human body. Otherwise, he wouldn't have been able to withstand that terrifying recoil force during his close-quarters battle with Azure Frost Demon Emperor back then.

"I wonder now that I've reached the late stage of body refinement, can I match Azure Frost in a head-on collision?"

The thought flashed through his mind as Luo Chen put down the book in his hand.

At that moment, the door was knocked.

*Dong!*

Luo Chen raised his eyelids. "Enter!"

The door opened, and a slightly short, wrinkled old man walked in.

Luo Chen's mouth twitched slightly. "Meng Shentong, what brings you to my place?"

Meng Shentong chuckled. "We're about to arrive at the Golden Lion Empire. I came to inform you along the way."

"Is that all?" Luo Chen didn't believe it. A single voice transmission was enough; it wasn't worth a First Teaching Grand Elder making a personal trip.

Meng Shentong's smile faded, and he cupped his hands. "That day's probing was somewhat rude. Please don't hold it against me, Fellow Daoist."

Luo Chen picked up his wine glass and took a light sip. "Speak frankly!"

The faint fragrance of the wine was refreshing and heartening.

Meng Shentong glanced at the wine glass, then his expression turned solemn.

"I heard from Bei Puti that you have met my Luo Sect's Right Envoy, Nan Yiqi?"

So that was it!

Luo Chen nodded calmly. "We did meet once, but I didn't know his name was Nan Yiqi at the time. The fellow daoists of the Northern Sea called him Old Ghost Nanqi."

"Old Ghost Nanqi?" Meng Shentong's mouth twitched, and then he asked curiously, "Where is he now? Has he joined some major force?"

"That, I wouldn't know. I only know that he was very close to Split Heaven Stream Lord of the Moluo Stream at the time."

"Split Heaven Stream Lord..."

Meng Shentong muttered the name, his expression turning uncertain.

He hadn't set foot in the Northern Sea for a long time, so the name was somewhat unfamiliar.

It was probably a newly advanced Sage from the last few hundred years.

However, the name Moluo Stream was somewhat familiar. It was said to be a united force similar to the Fierce Corpse Stiff Alliance, formed by remnants of the Demon Sect trying to revive it.

According to intelligence gathered from the Southern Frontier, it contained quite a few famous experts who had been renowned for many years.

He thought of one person, from whom he might be able to inquire about Nan Yiqi's whereabouts.

"Fellow Daoist Qi, do you know of the Old Monster Netherfire?"

Luo Chen's heart stirred. He definitely knew that one!

Not only did he know him, but the two had even had a transaction and cooperation before.

He didn't expect Meng Shentong to suddenly bring up this person.

"You mean Netherfire Stream Lord?"

"You could call him that. In the early years, I had a decent relationship with this man. I wonder if he's still alive."

Luo Chen shook his head. "This Qi wouldn't know. The situation in the Northern Sea is even more chaotic than in the Southern Frontier. Under such chaotic battles, even Sage-level experts can't guarantee they won't meet with an accident."

"Is that so?" Meng Shentong was somewhat disappointed.

The Luo Sect had no connections in the Northern Sea, and his personal network was limited to a few people, most of whom had already passed away due to lifespan.

If the Old Monster Netherfire was also gone, finding Nan Yiqi's whereabouts would be even more difficult.

Perhaps, after this matter was over, he should go to the Northern Sea, capture that Split Heaven Stream Lord who was close to Nan Yiqi, and interrogate him?

Luo Chen watched the pensive Meng Shentong and suddenly asked, "Old Ghost Nanqi is just a traitor to your sect. Why are you so concerned about him?"

"Precisely because he is a traitor, he must be punished. Even if he flees to the ends of the earth, he must pay the price!" A flash of ferocity crossed Meng Shentong's face, his words filled with determination.

The losses Old Ghost Nanqi had inflicted on the Luo Sect during the internal strife back then were unimaginable to outsiders.

The reason Meng Shentong had been in seclusion for so many years was not unrelated to the severe injuries he had sustained in that battle.

Most importantly, Old Ghost Nanqi had also taken away the Luo Sect's most important inherited gu insect!

Taking a deep breath, Meng Shentong regained his composure and looked at Luo Chen.

"If Fellow Daoist encounters this person again, please inform me. I will surely reward you handsomely!"

As he spoke, he took out a sleeping messenger gu.

Luo Chen looked at the messenger gu but did not reach out to take it.

Meng Shentong's hand froze in mid-air.

He frowned, then suddenly changed the subject.

"What reason did Fellow Daoist have for participating in this operation?"

"Invited by Li Tian Master."

"It seems it's the same for me. We've all more or less received favors from Li Tian Master, or made transactions with him. But!" Meng Shentong's tone shifted, and he transmitted his voice, "Be careful of Li Tian Master. This man's mind is cunning and deceitful. Beneath his kind brows and gentle eyes, there is nothing but calculation."

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow. "Oh? How so?"

Meng Shentong waved his hand and said earnestly, "It's hard to say specifically, but you can get a glimpse from the fact that he has been planning one thing for several hundred years."

Luo Chen blinked, pondering in his heart.

Was this why Meng Shentong, disregarding mutual face, had brought Bai Xuanjun along?

That Bai Xuanjun of the Whitebone Sect was no pushover!

Although his cultivation was only at the sixth layer of the Sage stage, rumors had it that his combat power was extremely formidable, with a dazzling array of attack methods.

Coupled with Meng Shentong's own Grand Cultivator strength, the two working together would be enough to handle the vast majority of situations.

Now, Meng Shentong was specifically reminding him, which could be considered doing him a favor.

After thinking it over, Luo Chen took the messenger gu and pushed a glass of wine over.

"Fellow Daoist, please!"

Meng Shentong was startled, then grinned and downed the glass of wine in one gulp.

After drinking, his face suddenly flushed red, then the color faded, leaving only one sentence escaping his lips.

"Good wine!"

How could the Dieyun wine, deemed worthy of tribute by the Nether Origin Sacred Land, not be good wine?

Exchanging a smile, the two left the room and arrived on the deck.

A crowd was already waiting in position. Li Tian Master stood at the bow of the boat, his hair and beard flying as he gazed at the golden kingdom in the distance.

"The Golden Lion Empire, we have arrived!"

...

The Golden Lion Empire was the most unique force in the Southern Frontier.

As everyone knew, the major forces in the cultivation world were mostly based on families, sects, religious orders, and gangs.

Even if there were national dynasties, they were mostly mortal organizations for unearthing spirit root seedlings, and were generally vassals of major sects.

But the strength and prosperity of the Golden Lion Empire far exceeded the world's imagination.

At its peak, it once had nine Sage-level True Men sitting in charge!

Even now, there were still five Sages coexisting in the world.

Such an anomaly, with the Southern Frontier Sacred Land, Life and Death Gate, turning a blind eye, had long aroused the suspicions of many.

But they didn't know that the Golden Lion Empire itself was just a branch of the Life and Death Gate.

Just like an ordinary Adept-level force might also support a few mortal kingdoms.

It was just that the Life and Death Gate's methods were a bit grander.

Inside a resplendent, majestic, and grand palace hall, Luo Chen and the others were chatting and laughing with a man dressed in imperial robes.

"National Master, you have traveled a long way and must be tired. Why not rest for a while? I will arrange a welcoming banquet, and tonight we will drink to our hearts' content."

Li Tian Master smiled. "Your Majesty is too kind."

The Emperor of the Golden Lion Empire said respectfully, "It's no trouble. You are all esteemed seniors; this is only right. Please wait a moment."

Having said that, he took the initiative to take his leave and depart.

After he left, Meng Shentong frowned and asked, "Aren't you afraid of alerting the enemy by making such a grand entrance into the palace?"

Li Tian Master smiled slightly, confident. "The point is to lure the snake out of its hole!"

As he spoke, he nodded to Old Woman Redleaf.

The old crone didn't waste words. She took out an array plate and extended her divine sense to follow the Golden Lion Emperor.

...

The Golden Lion Emperor left the hall and moved quickly.

Alone, he passed through layers of palaces and finally arrived before a bronze gate.

Taking out a token and making several hand seals, the bronze gate slowly opened.

He strode inside.

After a long while, he arrived outside a hall.

Hesitating for a moment, he activated a messenger gu towards a somewhat gloomy room.

Inside that gloomy room, an old man was intently fiddling with a gu jar. Inside, a small insect, wielding pincers like sharp swords, was crawling about happily.

However, the old man was not satisfied with this lively appearance.

"This shouldn't be!"

"The Bean Soldier Gu's power is restrained; it should be dull and heavy. Why is it so active?"

"Could there be a problem in my refining process?"

Just as he was feeling frustrated, a messenger gu suddenly stirred.

He waved his hand, and the door to the gu-refining room slowly opened.

The Golden Lion Emperor walked in and respectfully bowed to him.

"Grandson greets the Ancestor!"

The old man frowned. "What happened? Didn't I say not to disturb me during my gu refining?"

The Golden Lion Emperor said solemnly, "The National Master has returned!"

A look of joy appeared on the old man's face. "Old Li has returned? Perfect timing. I've run into some trouble with my gu refining and was just about to discuss it with him."

However, the Golden Lion Emperor's next words made his expression change instantly.

"The National Master also brought back six Sage-level experts. Their cultivation is profound and unfathomable. Grandson used the Imperial Heaven Treasure Mirror to secretly observe them but couldn't discern their specific realms. Li Tian Master claims he invited them as guests, but in Grandson's opinion, their intentions are far from friendly!"

"Even the Imperial Heaven Treasure Mirror can't detect their specific realms?" The old man's face changed drastically.

That treasure mirror was at the true artifact level.

Under its scrutiny, ordinary Sages couldn't hide their realms at all.

For the Imperial Heaven Treasure Mirror to be ineffective meant either the other party had an extremely powerful concealment technique, or their cultivation was at the mid-Sage or even late-Sage level!

Regardless of which, with a total of six such individuals, it was enough to be terrifying.

"What exactly does Li Tian Master want to do!"

The old man took a deep breath, his gaze falling on the gu jar. He couldn't leave just yet, but he couldn't ignore the situation outside either.

"You go and invite the Fifth Ancestor and the Third Ancestor. Have them stall for time first. I will be there shortly!"

The Golden Lion Emperor breathed a sigh of relief.

As a mere Adept-level cultivator, dealing with seven Sages was truly too much pressure.

With the Third and Fifth Ancestors stepping in, it would be much better.

After receiving the order, the Golden Lion Emperor respectfully withdrew, then went to two adjacent rooms, sending out messenger gus in turn.

Soon, two figures appeared one after the other.

A man and a woman, one old and one young.

The old man was the Third Ancestor, and the young girl was the Fifth Ancestor.

The two exchanged a glance, then led the Golden Lion Emperor out of this secret realm.

After the bronze gate opened and closed again, the Fifth Ancestor asked playfully, "Where are those people now?"

"The Hall of Nourishing the Heart!"

"Alright, let's go over now!"

The three didn't delay and headed straight for the Hall of Nourishing the Heart.

Soon.

They arrived outside the hall.

Looking at the shadowy figures inside, the Fifth Ancestor smiled slightly. Ever since the heaven and earth changes in the Southern Frontier, they had retreated into the Yin-Yang Realm to cultivate in seclusion, and she hadn't seen any strangers for a while.

Now, with seven fellow daoists arriving all at once, although she didn't know their purpose, exchanging a few words would be a good way to pass the time...

"Not good!"

The Third Ancestor swept his divine sense and immediately sensed something was wrong.

The Fifth Ancestor didn't understand, but after her divine sense swept over, she also understood why.

A palm strike sent a gale howling through the hall.

The seven figures silently turned to dust.

"It's the Puppet Human Gu!"

At that moment, a dull rumbling sound came from deep underground.

It was as if a giant was tearing through the earth and splitting rocks!

"They want to enter the Yin-Yang Realm!" the Fifth Ancestor blurted out, her expression furious.

The Third Ancestor was already moving, flying towards the entrance of the Yin-Yang Realm.

As he flew, he said, "The Yin-Yang Realm has an imitation Life and Death Gate. Without the token, it can't be opened. We still have time. Quickly activate the Imperial Palace Grand Formation and don't let them break in!"

At this moment.

The bronze gate, which had just been closed, was emitting a dense spiritual light.

Old Woman Redleaf held an array plate, making hand seals one after another. The needle on the array plate spun continuously.

Suddenly!

The needle stopped. Old Woman Redleaf's gaze focused on a certain spot.

"Weak point, right here! Everyone attack together and break it open."

But before the words were even out, Li Tian Master vetoed them.

"Our goal is to enter, not to break the door."

Old Woman Redleaf frowned. "If we don't break the door, how can we enter? And time is too tight. Once those two ancestors of the Golden Lion Empire activate the Imperial Palace Grand Formation and trap us, with them attacking from inside and outside, we'll be birds in a cage."

Li Tian Master smiled slightly, his gaze falling on Luo Chen.

"Don't break the door. Why not just open it?"

In the silence, Luo Chen stepped forward.

Under everyone's gaze, he slowly extended a single palm, placing it on the point Old Woman Redleaf had indicated.

Black armor trembled, great sinews moved like swimming dragons.

A surge of immense, majestic force erupted.

"Open!"

With a low shout, Luo Chen pushed hard.

To the shock of the crowd, the bronze gate, which seemed capable of isolating everything, slowly revealed a sliver of a gap.

Golden light flashed out from the gap.

At the same time, at the border of the Golden Lion Empire, countless streaks of escape light filled the sky, blotting out the sun.

The Fierce Corpse Stiff Alliance's army was pressing on the border!

(End of Chapter)
