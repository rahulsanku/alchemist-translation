# Chapter 668: Devouring Many Flames, a Land of Opportunity

There were many wondrous fires under heaven.

Even Ascendant Holy Lands might not necessarily record them one by one.

Yet there were always some legendary strange flames that even low-level wandering cultivators could casually mention.

For example, the Nirvana Sacred Fire that reversed life and death; or the Solar Divine Fire [TERM?] that shone across heaven and earth and made all evils retreat; or the Vermilion Bird Sacred Fire [TERM?], which was said to burn anything.

For tens of thousands of years, discussions about these three had been countless.

Because each one could be called the extreme of fire, a sovereign of ten thousand flames.

But as for which was truly first, and which was more powerful, that question had always been argued endlessly.

For high-level cultivators like Luo Chen, they naturally would not sink into such a boring debate.

In their eyes, so-called strange flames were like spells and magic treasures. There was no need to decide absolute superiority. The key lay in the ability of the controller.

It was just like the Withering-Bloom True Fire Luo Chen had refined.

After many years of nurturing, and after continuously refining demon beast vitality to strengthen itself, its current might was far beyond what it had been back in the Yan Alliance's Incense Burning Valley.

If judged by fixed rank, when it had been in Incense Burning Valley, the Withering-Bloom True Fire had just advanced to the fourth tier.

Now, no matter how one looked at it, it had reached the middle fourth tier, and it contained terrifying vitality. Its foundation was frightening beyond compare.

The vitality inside the Withering-Bloom True Fire was beyond doubt!

Every time it burned and refined living demon beasts, Luo Chen could share only one or two tenths of the vitality. Most of it was devoured by the Withering-Bloom True Fire itself.

Even so, it had created Luo Chen's powerful Archaic Tier Four body.

From this, one could see how immeasurable the foundation of the Withering-Bloom True Fire had become!

Yet even so, when he heard the words fifth-tier Vermilion Bird Sacred Fire, Luo Chen's pupils still shrank, and an incredulous expression appeared.

"Truly?"

"How could it be false?"

Tai Sui spoke in a low voice, and even his eyes held some fear.

"Within Five Elements Heaven, what I feared most was the sleeping Iron-Eating Beast [TERM?] in the Thousand-Ren Mountains. Next was the fire spirit Vermilion Bird in Vermilion Bird Mountain [TERM?]. Speaking of it, it is strange. A thousand years ago, Blazing Refinement Prison did not have Vermilion Bird Sacred Fire at all. Instead, after True Lord passed away, such a fire spirit appeared here, occupied the original Beacon Platform [TERM?], and derived fire spirits."

When he reached the end, Tai Sui also seemed to sink into confusion.

Luo Chen did not know the hidden secrets inside, and he intended to ask.

But Tai Sui himself did not understand the inside story, so naturally he had nothing to tell.

However, Luo Chen changed the topic and asked about other matters.

...

"Ah! It is the legendary Flame Demon Core [TERM?]. This place actually has such a thing!"

A cry of surprise came from Moya Cave Master. He impatiently lowered his magic cloud and reached out to pull something over.

It was entirely fiery red, and its heat was hard to endure.

Luo Chen watched from the side and did not seize it domineeringly, even though that Flame Demon Core had reached the fourth tier!

Moya Cave Master carefully glanced at Luo Chen. Afterward, he solemnly put away the Flame Demon Core.

The reason he behaved this way was entirely because Luo Chen's domineering style had become increasingly unrestrained earlier.

Forcefully taking the supreme-grade Nine Yang Geng Metal, demanding the earth-yellow crystals... every time, he had not discussed it with Moya Cave Master.

Under pressure from his "demonic might," Moya Cave Master could only cup his hands and yield.

Unexpectedly, after reaching Blazing Refinement Prison, the other party had actually changed his temperament. On the way to Vermilion Bird Mountain, whenever they encountered fire-attribute treasures, Luo Chen watched from the side and wanted none of them.

"Could he have changed his nature?"

Moya Cave Master muttered.

After a while, he landed from the sky again and collected a large batch of top-grade Fire Spirit Drills [TERM?].

They were precisely the Fire Spirit Drills Luo Chen had once searched for bitterly and failed to obtain when refining his lifebound magic treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron. Yet in Blazing Refinement Prison, they could be seen everywhere.

Luo Chen pursed his lips and said nothing.

Tai Sui secretly sized him up, also somewhat puzzled.

'That should not be. Devil Lord Azure Yang is not a generous man. There is no reason for him to turn a blind eye to resources beneficial to his cultivation.'

'Could it be that he has another scheme?'

Luo Chen did not care what they thought at all.

Ever since he displayed his abilities and forced Tai Sui to swear an oath, he had confirmed his leading position in their group of three.

He did not need to care about their thoughts.

This was originally the scene Ding Yi had once fantasized about. Only now, Luo Chen had reversed guest and host.

His gaze and divine sense extended as far as possible, exploring the surrounding environment.

In his mind, he recalled the information Tai Sui had told him earlier.

"The Thousand-Ren Mountains are rich in mineral deposits, the Flowing Sand Sea has a master, and the main resources in Blazing Refinement Prison are not those fire-attribute ores and demon cores at all, but sourceless flames!"

Correct!

According to Tai Sui, Blazing Refinement Prison was a base Devil Lord Heaven-Refining had specifically used to store and even cultivate sourceless flames back then.

No one knew why Devil Lord Heaven-Refining wanted to collect so many sourceless flames of different types and different uses.

But this place indeed hid many sourceless flames!

Those fire-attribute ores and demon cores were merely accompanying objects of various strange fires.

And Luo Chen's target was precisely those sourceless flames!

"Are they hidden this deeply?"

Luo Chen murmured, searching as much nearby information as possible while paying attention to the restlessness of the origin true fire in his body.

Beside him, mana surged once again.

It was Moya Cave Master, who had seen another unusual treasure and gone down to gather it.

Luo Chen glanced over and was slightly moved.

It was a treasured tree growing on the mouth of a volcano. It was crystalline and translucent, like colored glass. One could tell at a glance that it was valuable.

Suddenly, Luo Chen's gaze stopped.

Whoosh!

Moya Cave Master, who had just taken out a small shovel and was digging with effort, froze, because Luo Chen had landed beside him.

"Devil Lord, do you want this..."

"Continue."

Moya Cave Master was stunned. His hands continued moving, only slower than before.

Luo Chen frowned.

"Faster."

Forced by demonic might, Moya Cave Master had no choice but to swing the shovel faster. As the surface soil was dug open, the roots below the colored-glass treasured tree gradually appeared.

"Hm?"

Moya Cave Master was surprised as he dug away the surface soil and looked at the layer below.

One piece of glass after another, multicolored, was spread deep in the ground.

The roots of the treasured tree were like blood vessels, extending downward from those mixed-color glass pieces.

Seeing this scene, Luo Chen could no longer suppress the excitement in his heart.

"As expected. This is the legendary Glassfire Cedar [TERM?]. Wherever Glassfire Cedar survives, there must be Glassfire [TERM?]!"

These years, Luo Chen had never stopped reading classics.

Those classics were not limited to cultivation methods. They also included medicinal scriptures, alchemy books, artifact-forging manuals, and even more miscellaneous books recording local customs and all kinds of resources.

Perhaps the information in those varied classics was not always correct, but they had truly broadened Luo Chen's horizons and knowledge.

With his current age of one hundred and sixty, purely in terms of "breadth of knowledge," he was absolutely no less than those old Golden Core cultivators.

He might even surpass them slightly because of the breadth and completeness of his reading.

Therefore, at first sight of the Glassfire Cedar, Moya Cave Master merely treated it as a fire-attribute plant, while Luo Chen looked from surface to inside and judged the reason for its birth, as well as the thing necessary in its growing environment.

Glassfire!

A sourceless flame from ancient legends.

Legend said that in remote antiquity, there was a famous mountain, crystalline from top to bottom, so clear that one could see its inside from the outside. Its name was Guanyu Mountain [TERM?].

Within it lived a sourceless flame. Any living being that touched even a little of it would be burned into glass.

No one dared approach Guanyu Mountain. Even powerful archaic beasts did not dare test its edge.

Unfortunately, as time passed and seas changed into mulberry fields, Guanyu Mountain disappeared.

But the name of that sourceless flame that could burn people into glass was preserved.

It was precisely Glassfire!

"There must be one below!"

Luo Chen took a deep breath. The instant Moya Cave Master joyfully pulled out the Glassfire Cedar, Luo Chen slapped the ground with his palm.

"Devil Lord, what are you doing?" Moya Cave Master froze.

Luo Chen rebounded and looked at the fragments in his hand, his brows knitting slightly.

Then he shot into the sky, raising one palm high.

Abruptly, he dove straight down.

Surging qi and blood empowered boundless great strength. It was the Mountain-Breaking Form [TERM?] of the Seven Free Hands [TERM?]!

Buzz!

A muffled sound came from the void.

In the next moment, under Moya Cave Master and Tai Sui's alarmed gazes, the great mountain that had originally grown the Glassfire Cedar began rumbling and shaking.

Large chunks of soil fell away from the mountain's surface.

Brilliant glass reflecting red firelight was revealed beneath.

Those glass layers, violently struck by the Mountain-Breaking Form, were now cracking continuously.

Crack!

Crack!

Crack!

...

In only a few breaths, the originally crystalline mountain was split apart, covered in countless cracks.

Luo Chen snorted and stomped down!

In the next moment, the mountain beneath his feet collapsed like a castle built of sand, falling apart with a crash.

"It is here, as expected!"

With one glance, Luo Chen saw that at the center of the scattered glass, a beautiful and dreamlike flame was floating.

"A pity. Its rank is not high, only around the second tier."

Regret appeared on Luo Chen's face, but he soon became excited again.

"Second tier is fine. It is more convenient for me to subdue. If it were higher, it would be troublesome."

As one of the most dangerous kinds of sourceless flame in the Mountains and Seas Realm during remote antiquity, even third- or fourth-tier Glassfire would be extremely troublesome. If it was second tier, it would be relatively easier to subdue.

Having lost the protection of its treasured mountain, the flame seemed to sense danger and was about to rush out.

At that moment, a pressure like the might of heaven and earth descended from above.

The All-Encompassing Fire Prison spread across a hundred zhang!

Within it, Luo Chen opened his mouth and spat out the Withering-Bloom True Fire, which happily flew out.

The azure flame descended like a great net.

The Glassfire flame sensed danger, and its firelight suddenly blazed.

Wherever it passed, the azure flame began condensing inch by inch, burned into pieces of glass.

But absolute rank suppression made this resistance look like a mantis raising its arms against a chariot.

With an irresistible momentum, the Withering-Bloom True Fire completely surrounded the Glassfire flame.

Luo Chen sat cross-legged atop the shattered mountain and circulated the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture to accelerate the devouring of this flame.

All this sounded lengthy, but from Luo Chen's strike, to breaking the mountain, to devouring the flame, only a few breaths had passed.

Moya Cave Master and Tai Sui watched the scene, their eyelids twitching.

"What is he doing?" Moya Cave Master did not understand.

Tai Sui said dryly, "He seems to want to use his origin true fire to refine that sourceless flame."

Moya Cave Master's pupils widened, his face full of disbelief.

"How is that possible? Our true fires are singular. How can they be mixed with other foreign things? Does he not want his life?"

Tai Sui's tone slowed, and his expression was incomparably shocked.

"It is not impossible. Someone once did this."

"Who?"

Tai Sui stared fixedly at Luo Chen.

"What do you think Five Elements Heaven was made for? And why was this place named a prison?"

Moya Cave Master's heart pounded wildly.

"You mean Devil Lord Heaven-Refining also did something similar? He even arranged this place and kept many sourceless flames in captivity for the convenience of devouring and refining them?"

Perhaps seeing a familiar scene drew out more memories from Tai Sui's remnant thought.

At this moment, he looked somewhat dazed. He murmured words Moya Cave Master did not understand.

"Nine Refinements Method [TERM?]... beacon smoke..."

Later, he looked around in confusion.

"All of these are merely leftovers used by True Lord."

The leftovers of a Void Refinement True Lord [TERM?] were, to a Golden Core cultivator, a heaven-shaking opportunity.

When Luo Chen spent half a day completely devouring the Glassfire, his Withering-Bloom True Fire became even more vigorous.

In the void, the faint shadow of a small lush green tree even appeared.

On the little tree, branches stretched outward.

Looking carefully, how were they branches? They were clearly strands of incomparably pure tongues of flame.

The red-robed Daoist sat cross-legged in the void, looking at the green fire tree above his head, a thoughtful expression appearing.

Seeing this again, Moya Cave Master recalled the scene he had seen earlier.

"So at that time, Devil Lord Azure Yang had already devoured one sourceless flame?"

Tai Sui's body trembled uncontrollably, as though he had seen a natural enemy.

Within the soul-nourishing wood tablet at Luo Chen's chest, Han Zhan also became agitated at this moment.

Looking at the small tree shadow outside, his soul stirred violently.

"Five Elements Heaven! Five Elements Heaven!"

"What resource-gathering place? This is clearly where the five basic law powers of metal, wood, water, fire, and earth are spread everywhere."

"If one had enough patience to collect and comprehend them one by one, there would even be a chance to comprehend law power within Five Elements Heaven!"

"Hateful. Why do I have no fleshly body and have missed such a monstrous opportunity?"

...

Exactly when Luo Chen returned was no longer important.

Tai Sui and the others only knew that when the journey resumed, Luo Chen's previous strange behavior became completely reasonable.

He ignored all kinds of rare resources.

All his attention was placed on searching for the sourceless flames hidden in Blazing Refinement Prison.

Those things were hidden extremely deep.

Yet somehow, Luo Chen seemed to have guidance and could always find the approximate locations.

When Luo Chen once again stepped into a magma valley and caught a flock of fluttering fire butterflies [TERM?] with the All-Encompassing Fire Prison, the others stood outside the valley entrance and fell silent.

...

"Third-tier Red Lotus Earthfire!"

"Second-tier Glassfire!"

"First-tier Spotted Demon Butterfly Flame [TERM?]."

"Third-tier Amber Flame [TERM?]!"

"Third-tier unknown demon flame!"

"Second-tier unknown demon flame!"

Two months later, Luo Chen counted the sourceless flames collected along the way.

Most were ones he recognized, while a smaller portion were unfamiliar.

And those unfamiliar flames all carried domineering demonic qi. Some were similar to the mana aura of He Yuan from the Yuan Demon Sect, yet stronger by more than a level.

Luo Chen's mood became increasingly cheerful.

He could sense the strengthening of his origin true fire. Even the fire-attribute spells he occasionally used, such as the Azure Yang Great Handprint and Blazing Sun Art, had become stronger and stronger.

When Luo Chen once again stopped before a cloud layer stretching a thousand li, Tai Sui, who had been silent all this time, finally spoke.

"How long will it take this time?"

Luo Chen raised his head toward the sky and said softly, "This time will probably take a very long while, because I can sense that the Burning Heaven Fire [TERM?] within that cloud layer is extremely high rank, rare in this world."

At the least, it was fourth tier!

Refining such a sourceless flame would not only consume a very long time, but might even be accompanied by great danger.

Tai Sui said in a low voice, "Three hundred li ahead is Vermilion Bird Mountain. Will you continue delaying? The Five Elements Lotus Platform concerns your Sage-formation path!"

Luo Chen smiled slightly.

"Sage formation is certainly precious, but how can I refuse an opportunity that has fallen before me? I can feel that this trip through Blazing Refinement Prison will greatly benefit my future cultivation. I absolutely cannot miss it!"

Tai Sui opened his mouth, but in the end, he did not say any words of opposition.

"Then pay attention. Cave Master and I will go ahead and wait for you beneath Vermilion Bird Mountain."

"Mm."

Luo Chen nodded lightly. Then, in front of the two, his mana erupted outward with a roar.

On his red Daoist robe, a layer of qi armor naturally appeared.

Not only that.

Broken Moon Wings unfolded!

The All-Encompassing Fire Prison unfolded!

His whole body was also wrapped in a layer of azure flame.

He directly entered the second form of the All-Encompassing Fire Prison, the true-fire state!

Afterward, he charged straight into the sky and stepped into the thousand-li red burning cloud.

Almost the instant he entered, the red clouds filling the sky suddenly boiled.

Strange phantoms rose and fell within the red clouds.

Some were fine horses, some were pale hounds. There were also great pengs spreading their wings and owls crying.

Clearly, the fourth-tier Burning Heaven Fire dwelling within that fire cloud had already gained initial intelligence and possessed boundless power!

*(End of Chapter)*
