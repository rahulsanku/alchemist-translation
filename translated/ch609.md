# Chapter 609: Nascent Soul Formation Theory, Soul-Fusion Secret Art

"Transforming the meridians and cleansing the marrow, refining the organs and opening the channels, opening every aperture throughout the body. In this way, one can reach a transparent body [TERM?]... so that is how it is! Tsk, tsk. Truly, one conversation with you surpasses five years of reading."

"Angler, you are too modest. These principles of cultivation are already carried out among us qi refiners without our noticing. Before, you simply knew that it was so without knowing why. Now that the details are clear, your future body tempering will surely advance by leaps and bounds."

In the outer hall of Star-Dipper Hall, an old man and a young man conversed happily.

The Angler seemed as if he had received enlightenment poured over his head, thoroughly understanding the direction of his next cultivation.

Just as Luo Chen had said, body refiners and qi refiners did in fact have points in common.

When qi refiners cultivated, even if they did not deliberately temper the body, they unconsciously refined it, making the cultivator's body less frail and able to withstand the tremendous impact of outside spiritual qi.

However, though the matter sounded simple, the specific practice was clearly not that easy.

For example, arts for refining the organs, methods for opening the channels, and even means related to opening apertures were all obviously secret transmissions of various body refiners. They might even need corresponding secret medicines before they could be cultivated to great completion.

If one tried to carry out those practices through an immortal cultivator's qi-refining methods, one would likely fail to paint a tiger and end up with a dog.

Seeing the Angler sink into thought, Luo Chen pondered for a moment and advised, "In truth, these low-level body-tempering methods circulate widely through the cultivation world. Angler, as long as you are willing to spend some spirit stones, you can naturally collect many of them. When the time comes, classify and test them, then find the ones suitable for yourself."

The Angler first nodded. "Indeed, that is not difficult. The Demon Hunter Office has quite a few related methods."

Then his face showed difficulty.

"But if I want to reach the Furnace Realm [TERM?], the third realm of body tempering you mentioned, I fear it will not be so simple."

In the second realm, the Blood River Realm [TERM?], the first five minor realms all belonged to the stage of laying foundations.

To achieve something, one had to understand the transformations of qi and blood and refine the four forces of bright, hidden, transformed, and core [TERM?].

Those four realms were not easy to cultivate. They required comprehension, talent, and likewise resources and a great deal of time.

The Angler suddenly lifted his head and looked at Luo Chen.

"Fellow Daoist Luo, the path you walk seems somewhat different from that of the ancient body refiners."

Luo Chen raised a brow. "What makes you say that?"

He had not expected the other man's observation to be so sharp, actually detecting this point.

The Angler hesitated, then said, "In truth, this old man once saw an orthodox body refiner, and one who had reached the Furnace Realm, a high-level body refiner. Even with that person merely standing beside me, I could feel that tyrannical, matchless astral qi, fiercely invasive. His whole person was like a furnace, almost as if he were going to melt all things into himself."

"But you are different."

"The feeling you give me is vigorous vitality, surging blood qi, without that so-called tyrannical astral qi. In fact, it is more like an ancient primordial demon beast standing before me."

Luo Chen still wore a smile, but it had become somewhat strained.

He had not expected the Angler's perception to be so keen.

The path he walked was indeed not the orthodox path of body refiners.

Through the system, through his self-created cultivation method, he had skipped those four stages of understanding qi and blood transformations and instead taken a different body-tempering road that relied only on resources.

It was no longer bright, hidden, transformed, and core, but Blood Exchange and Bone Shaping.

Over these years, relying on eating large quantities of blood food and persistently taking Black Emperor Ointment, he had successively exchanged his blood several times. From the great completion of nine blood exchanges, only the final three remained.

Now, the blood inside him was like lead and mercury, surging like a torrential current.

As for Bone Shaping, all the bones in his body had also become harder and harder. Even when occasionally hacked with a magic instrument, they could not be harmed in the slightest.

He even had the confidence to take a direct strike from the body of a lower-grade magic treasure.

The reason outside cultivators could not see it was because the concealment array engraved on his body forcefully hid this terrifying body, which was like that of a savage beast.

'What secret art does this Angler cultivate that he can see through my true state... no!'

A thought suddenly emerged in Luo Chen's mind.

The other man's earlier expression had been hesitant, not as if he were completely certain.

In other words, perhaps the other man had not sensed Luo Chen's condition, but had learned of it through another channel.

The battle of Cold Light Island.

That was it.

At the Mo family fishery, Luo Chen had used a grand formation to temporarily block outside perception, but there had been survivors from that battle.

The elder of the Wu family's twin brothers had escaped.

With the Demon Hunter Office's intelligence capabilities, it was very possible they had collected related information, and that it had finally landed in the Angler's hands.

Facing the Angler's desire for knowledge, Luo Chen put away his smile and became slightly solemn.

"My apologies. This is my exclusive secret art, and I absolutely will not transmit it. I fear I must disappoint you."

The Angler opened his mouth, obvious disappointment appearing on his face.

To keep him from developing any greed, Luo Chen added, "In truth, my body-tempering art takes an extremely long time, and the resources it consumes are no small sum. Your realm is now Golden Core Stage Nine, only one step from Perfected Completion. If you cultivate my method now, it will certainly be too late. As far as I know, when a cultivator breaks through to the Nascent Soul stage, what matters is the union of the three treasures: essence, qi, and spirit. Though it is called union, it is not true fusion, merely adjusting the body's condition to the most harmonious state in order to withstand heavenly might. Under these circumstances, a cultivator's physique does not have to reach the third realm of body tempering. It only needs a solid foundation, with meridians and apertures able to withstand the scouring of outside spiritual qi."

The Angler listened to Luo Chen's attempt to smooth things over with slight disappointment.

The principle was indeed so. He was already old, a full four hundred years or so, with only about a hundred years before his great limit arrived.

In a mere hundred years, to reach Golden Core Perfected Completion and also walk another path from nothing to the third realm was delusional no matter how one put it.

But as the words continued entering his ears, the light in the Angler's eyes grew brighter and brighter.

Abruptly.

He suddenly stood and looked at Luo Chen in uncertainty.

Faced with such a loss of composure, Luo Chen was first puzzled, then his heart gave a thump.

It seemed he had spoken too deeply for such a shallow acquaintance.

The Angler's voice was dry and hoarse. "Fellow Daoist Luo Hai, are those theories about Nascent Soul formation you just mentioned true?"

Sure enough.

Luo Chen knew the problem was here.

Those theories of his concerning Nascent Soul formation came from the sudden enlightenment he had experienced years ago, combined with Han Zhan's occasional guidance, and were finally summarized by Luo Chen himself.

Such principles were not profound.

But to wandering cultivators, they were the Great Dao reduced to simplicity, something they could seek for ages without obtaining even a word or phrase.

Even as an eight-star demon hunter and one of the people in charge of the Demon Hunter Office, the Angler seemed to lack such reserves of knowledge.

Luo Chen feigned calm. "They should be true. These words were casually mentioned to me by True Being Fu, an elder of Tianyuan Tower, when I was on Cold Light Island. I imagine that, with the dignity of a Nascent Soul true being, he would not speak falsehoods to deceive me."

The old man paced through the hall in small steps.

True Being Fu?

That should be one of the four elders the Tianyuan Dao Sect had stationed in the Northern Sea's Tianyuan Merchant Alliance, whose true name was Fu Chaosheng [TERM?].

He did know these bits of common knowledge.

Rumor said this person was very kind to juniors and often gave support and guidance.

With Devil Lord Azure Yang Luo Hai's excellence, perhaps it was truly possible he had gained the other man's appreciation and received some pointers.

His steps stopped, and he looked at Luo Chen with burning eyes.

"Is there more?"

Luo Chen smiled bitterly. "What status do I have? I am merely a wandering cultivator. To receive a true being's favor and a sentence or two of guidance was already heaven-sent fortune. How would I dare ask for more?"

"True. Even though my Myriad Immortals Assembly has seven Nascent Soul true beings, I rarely receive their guidance in ordinary times," the Angler said.

Only, the gaze he gave Luo Chen was still half-believing and half-doubting.

In his heart, however, he had already made up his mind.

In the days to come, he had to seek out this Devil Lord Azure Yang more often and exchange cultivation insights with him.

Not only matters related to body tempering, but more importantly theories related to Nascent Soul formation.

Perhaps he could dig out even more.

Seeing weariness appear on Luo Chen's face, the Angler composed himself.

"Let today's exchange end here for now."

"This old man came over for other matters as well."

Luo Chen nodded. "Please speak."

"First..."

The Angler took out a scroll booklet, flipped to a page in the middle, and sent it before Luo Chen.

"What is this?" Luo Chen did not understand. He lowered his head to read the text upon it.

The Angler explained, "These are the relics of that white-robed cultivator from the Primordial Demon Sect. Apart from some things taken by the Nascent Soul true beings, everything remaining is listed here. I said before that these items would enter the Demon Hunter Office treasury for wandering cultivators to exchange for. You and Duan Li were involved in the matter, so you may choose and exchange first. Your priority is even ahead of Duan Li."

Soul-Decreeing Sword [TERM?]: mid-grade magic treasure, crafted primarily from thousand-year peach wood. Its attack trajectory is extraordinarily strange, and its power is considerable. It also has the special effect of decreeing souls and intimidating resentful spirits. Recommended for cultivators with wood spirit roots.

Myriad Souls Banner: originally contained tens of thousands of souls, but most were consumed in battle. However, the banner's foundation is quite good. If a cultivator supplements it with large quantities of souls, it may recover its former power.

Core Formation Insights: the Core Formation insights of a genius Golden Core cultivator. Comparatively shallow. Clearly, this person formed his core very easily and encountered no difficulties.

Moon Gold Stone [TERM?]: fourth-tier mineral material, suited for refining yin-attribute magic instruments and magic treasures.

...

Names of resources and related introductions were listed one by one on this page.

At the head of the page, a name was specially marked.

Yan Zhixian [TERM?].

Luo Chen fell into thought. It seemed that white-robed cultivator indeed had not been called Qin Tianming. His true name was Yan Zhixian.

"Have you chosen?" the Angler asked.

Luo Chen raised his head and tentatively asked, "Why is the secret art that person used to lay down the Black Domain not listed?"

During the battle at the Quyang defensive line, among Yan Zhixian's many methods, the one that had left the deepest impression on Luo Chen was undoubtedly the Black Domain he had laid down with the help of the Myriad Souls Banner.

It had resembled a ghost domain, and also a trapping grand formation.

But the closest comparison was the Nascent Soul domains that Wolf Emperor Aoxiao and Han Zhan had each unleashed during their duel on the Tiangu Plain battlefield years ago.

Within that Black Domain, enemies' mana was greatly suppressed, and even divine soul thought became somewhat sluggish.

Otherwise, how could Yan Zhixian, a mere early Golden Core cultivator, have faced two Golden Core experts, Luo Chen and Duan Li, at the same time?

If Luo Chen could master such a method, the advantage it would give him in battles against peers would be enormous.

Regarding this, the Angler said with no lack of regret, "Such a secret art must belong to a true inheritance disciple of the Primordial Demon Sect. How would he carry a copied text on his person?"

Luo Chen did not quite believe him.

But the Angler's expression was open, not seeming false.

After thinking for a moment, Luo Chen pointed at one line.

"Then I will choose this Soul-Fusion Secret Art [TERM?]."

The Angler smiled faintly. "About what I expected. I guessed you would want this secret art. After all, you also have a Myriad Souls Banner of no poor quality."

Luo Chen smiled knowingly.

His Myriad Souls Banner had come from Yan Nantian and had not originally been very powerful.

But in the Xuanyan Sea Region, Han Zhan had secretly collected many Demon King souls, making its power quite decent.

Then in the battle on Cold Light Island, Han Zhan used the Sea-Sealing Eight Trigrams Formation to swallow the soul of Qian Ting, the late Golden Core great cultivator who led the Qian family.

It was also by relying on that soul banner that Luo Chen had avoided the Black Domain's field control during his battle with Yan Zhixian.

Especially after the battle ended, the Angler had allowed Luo Chen to devour the many Ghost Kings left within Yan Zhixian's Myriad Souls Banner.

Over five years, the banner had already undergone earth-shaking changes.

One could even say that the Ghost King formed from the resentful soul of Qian Ting had devoured more than half of the strange Ghost King in Yan Zhixian's soul banner, the one that had seemed neither human nor beast. Now, it had grown increasingly powerful.

If he obtained this Soul-Fusion Secret Art.

Then perhaps Luo Chen could also do as Yan Zhixian had, fusing the many scattered Ghost Kings in the Myriad Souls Banner into one body and erupting with tremendous power.

In that way, Luo Chen would gain another trump card.

"This art is a spell that disciples of the soul-refining lineage of the Primordial Demon Sect all learn. Though it is not transmitted outward, Northern Sea cultivators have also figured out some similar spells over the years."

"Therefore, the exchange price for this Soul-Fusion Secret Art is not very high. Taking the Primordial Demon Sect's name into account, it was set at two hundred thousand spirit stones."

"You are now a four-star demon hunter. After the discount, you need only pay one hundred and twenty thousand spirit stones to exchange for it."

The Angler spoke with a beaming smile.

Two hundred thousand spirit stones truly was not expensive, especially after the discount.

Luo Chen also sighed. "The star-rank discount for demon hunters truly is good. If I had a nine-star rank, no, even seven or eight stars would do. This Peng Lake spirit land's yearly rent is also a huge burden to me."

The Northern Sea had many spirit veins, so they seemed less valuable.

But those with a proper grade were still very expensive.

Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake cost three million a year. Even after a forty-percent discount, it was still one million eight hundred thousand per year. Five years was nine million.

If Luo Chen's demon hunter star rank were higher, perhaps that rent would become seven million, six million, or even lower.

For an eight-star demon hunter like the Angler, renting a third-tier upper-grade cave dwelling cost only three million every five years. It was practically cabbage price. Perhaps the Demon Hunter Office even had other favorable treatment and did not collect that fee at all.

Hearing Luo Chen's sigh, the Angler shrugged.

"Is that not simple? With your ability, accept more missions, and it will not take long to raise your star rank. Even if you cannot reach our eight-star level, becoming a seven-star demon hunter is still possible."

In the Myriad Immortals Assembly's Demon Hunter Office, one to three stars were mostly Foundation Establishment true cultivators. Four to six stars were basically Golden Core masters, with the occasional Foundation Establishment four-star demon hunter only because they had rendered great merit.

But seven-star demon hunters were very few in number.

Those with that star rank had personal realms no lower than late Golden Core.

No, it should be put this way: in the Myriad Immortals Assembly, all demon hunters above seven stars were late Golden Core cultivators, but not all late Golden Core cultivators could become seven-star demon hunters.

For instance, Huangfu Song had a Golden Core Stage Seven realm, yet his demon hunter star rank was only six-star.

The Angler's words were without doubt the greatest acknowledgement of Luo Chen's strength.

He was directly comparing Luo Chen to those formidable late Golden Core great cultivators.

Regarding this, Luo Chen was very modest.

He waved a hand. "Most of those missions are dangerous. Even the most ordinary demon-hunting mission requires going into battle and killing. That does not benefit our quiet cultivation. I do not wish to accept them."

The Angler was speechless.

Devil Lord Azure Yang, whose demonic might had spread far and wide, disliked killing and only wanted quiet cultivation?

It was a little absurd.

But thinking again, Luo Chen had handled that farce before with a measured hand, indeed appearing unwilling to invite extra complications.

Perhaps he had thought wrongly.

However...

The Angler sighed. "Unfortunately, the second matter this old man must mention will spoil your refined pleasure in quiet cultivation."

Without waiting for Luo Chen to ask, he directly explained the situation.

It turned out that Golden Core demon hunters had corresponding compulsory missions every five years. This was something everyone had to understand before joining the Demon Hunter Office.

Luo Chen had cultivated at Peng Lake for five years, yet had not accepted even one compulsory mission.

Even with the Angler mediating on his behalf, it could no longer be delayed.

"But you need not worry too much. This sort of mission is generally not very dangerous. It merely takes a rather long time."

"Takes time?" Luo Chen frowned.

The Angler waved a broad hand. "I know what you are worried about. You merely fear that taking too long will affect your cultivation."

Luo Chen gave a sound of agreement. He was indeed worried about this.

The longer he cultivated at Peng Lake, the more his Dao progressed day by day. He truly did not wish to waste time on trivial missions.

If he did not absolutely have to go out, he would have loved to stay home every day and cultivate his realm to late Golden Core, then all the way to Perfected Completion.

"I have already selected a few missions for you. All of them take the least time. Why not let me tell you about them?" the Angler said with a smile. He was an old hand in the Demon Hunter Office. Which missions were dangerous, which were easy, and how long each mission took, he knew better than anyone.

Clearly, in order to befriend Luo Chen and learn more cultivation-related matters from his mouth, he had already begun using the authority he controlled.

Luo Chen's eyes brightened. "I would be glad to hear the details."

...

The next day.

In the Fulong Mountain Range, at Hidden Wind Cave.

A male cultivator with a pale complexion locked his cave dwelling, activated the formation, and walked out.

He had just emerged when he saw a streak of escaping light fly over from the distance, heading toward a mountain peak not far away.

Halfway there, it suddenly turned back and descended beside him.

"Are you Fellow Daoist Duan Li?"

Duan Li looked at him and replied with some hesitation, "Fellow Daoist Xiao?"

The newcomer was precisely Xiao Buhuan.

The five-star demon hunter who had been stationed on Quyang Island.

Xiao Buhuan laughed. "Exactly. I did not expect Fellow Daoist to still remember me after only meeting a few times back then."

Duan Li forced out a smile. "Does Fellow Daoist Xiao not remember me as well?"

Xiao Buhuan smiled, then looked somewhat doubtfully at the cave dwelling behind him.

"This Hidden Wind Cave?"

Duan Li nodded. "I rent it now. We are neighbors."

Xiao Buhuan was stunned.

Back then, he had introduced the place several times to Daoist Azure Yang, hoping to make the man his neighbor, but unexpectedly Duan Li had benefited instead.

Looking at Duan Li's pale face, it seemed the aftereffects of that battle had not yet been resolved.

Duan Li was instead somewhat curious. "Fellow Daoist has not shown himself these years. I thought you were cultivating in seclusion. Looking at it now, however, you have returned from a long journey. Could it be you only just came back from Quyang Island?"

Xiao Buhuan replied, "That is indeed so."

Duan Li was surprised. "A garrison mission is supposed to rotate once a year. How did Fellow Daoist stay stationed there for so long?"

"I am different." Xiao Buhuan shook his head. "I accepted a long-term mission, ten years in duration. During these ten years, the cave dwelling here at Xiushan [TERM?] was still reserved for me. At the same time, this sort of long-term garrison mission can slowly raise one's demon hunter star rank."

Duan Li suddenly understood.

Indeed, long-term missions that lasted ten years at a time consumed a great deal of effort even for Golden Core cultivators.

Especially when stationed at the front line resisting demon beasts, where battle might break out at any time.

This kind of mission was entirely different from ordinary five-year compulsory missions.

Xiao Buhuan asked curiously, "What about Fellow Daoist Azure Yang? Are you still in contact?"

"You do not know about him?" Duan Li asked subconsciously, then laughed at himself. "I forgot. You only just returned."

"What happened to him?" Xiao Buhuan grew even more curious.

Duan Li broke into a smile. "I was just about to visit Daoist Azure Yang at Peng Lake. Fellow Daoist Xiao, would you like to go and recognize his door?"

"Peng Lake!"

Xiao Buhuan's eyes lit up.

That Daoist Azure Yang was actually cultivating at Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake.

Since when could the spirit vein cave dwellings there be rented separately?

Immediately afterward, he nodded without hesitation.

"Together, then. Together!"
