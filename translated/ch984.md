# Chapter 984: Have You Seen My Full Strength?

Hoo...

A great wind arose, the sky cloudless, rolling yellow sand stretching for ten thousand miles.

Seven figures appeared in Luo Chen's sight.

Five were bald monks draped in kasayas, one of whom was the Master Cihai who had blocked them earlier. The other two wore ornate Daoist robes—one was Zhou Mingxuan, and the other, though his name was unknown, the faint fluctuations of his mana proclaimed he was no weakling.

They stood like this, blocking the path ahead of Luo Chen and Azure Frost.

Their mouths spoke of "Heaven's virtue in loving life" and "Amitabha," yet the deeds they came to perform were those of killing and slaughter.

Luo Chen felt helpless. "Fellow Daoists, we are merely passing through the Western Desert by chance. Is this relentless pursuit not a bit excessive?"

Cihai glared at him and barked, "That demoness Azure Frost—who doesn't know of her past deeds? If such a fierce bird had stayed put in her corner of the Million Mountains, so be it. But now she has come to the Western Desert. Who knows what sinister intentions she harbors?"

Luo Chen frowned. "But she has done nothing. Even during our last conflict, she didn't kill any of you."

"Not doing it now doesn't mean she won't in the future. We must nip this in the bud, lest the Buddhist lands be stained with blood again."

"Master, is this not a bit unreasonable? To impose such a false accusation..."

Before Luo Chen could finish, another monk with white brows and a white beard interrupted him.

"Fellow Daoist Luo Chen, the world says you have the style of a benevolent gentleman. Why do you walk with such a great demon? I hear you once slaughtered hundreds of Zhou clan members at the border with your sword formation. Now you protect the Demon Emperor Azure Frost so fiercely—have you fallen into the demonic path?"

Luo Chen's eyes narrowed, radiating a dangerous aura.

"Dare I ask the master's Dharma name?"

The white-browed, white-bearded monk pressed his palms together. "Amitabha. This old monk is from Puji Temple on Mount Wutai. My Dharma name is Ci'en."

"Ci'en?"

Luo Chen repeated the name, his gaze sweeping over the others.

"Then the other three masters are also from Mount Wutai?"

The three monks seemed somewhat uncomfortable under Luo Chen's stare, but emboldened by their numerical advantage, they did not flinch.

"Lingying Temple of Mount Wutai, Ciping, greets Fellow Daoist Luo!"

"Foguang Temple of Mount Wutai, Ciguang, greets Fellow Daoist Luo!"

"Shengshu Temple of Mount Wutai, Cishu, greets Fellow Daoist Luo!"

Five mountains, five temples, five monks—none were easy opponents.

Luo Chen pursed his lips and looked at the other two, especially the middle-aged man in ornate robes.

"And this fellow Daoist?"

The middle-aged man snapped his feather fan shut and said with an air of elegance, "Wu Changge of the Wu clan in Wen Province!"

As for Zhou Mingxuan, there was no need for introductions.

Seeing this lineup, Luo Chen's heart sank.

One might think he was afraid.

But what Luo Chen was actually thinking was: first two Sages, now seven. If things escalated further, what would come next?

What kind of existences would be dragged into this?

"Fellow Daoist Luo Chen, I urge you not to bring misfortune upon yourself by getting too entangled with this demoness. The sea of suffering is boundless; turn your head and see the shore!" the white-bearded monk Ci'en advised.

"I..."

Just as Luo Chen was about to speak, Azure Frost beside him grew impatient.

"So much useless chatter! In the end, we still have to fight. Get out of the way!"

A surge of wind blasted from beside him. Luo Chen instinctively stepped aside as Azure Frost shot forward like an arrow from a bow.

The five monks did not underestimate her. They immediately revealed their Buddhist treasures and met her head-on.

Luo Chen frowned and stepped forward, but Wu Changge blocked his path.

"Fellow Daoist Luo, we respect your reputation as the East Wasteland's Alchemy Sect. Even with some suspicion, we are willing to give you a chance. So in this battle, it's best you don't interfere."

The Western Desert was dominated by Buddhist forces, but that didn't mean there were only monks and nuns here.

There were still sect traditions and family lineages.

But to establish a foothold in the Western Desert, these major sects and great families were more or less aligned with Buddhism.

The Zhou clan was like this, and so was the Wu clan.

Luo Chen had some understanding of the Western Desert's situation, so he wasn't surprised to see Buddhists and Daoists acting together.

But now...

Luo Chen twitched the corner of his mouth. "Wu Changge, do you really think you alone can stop me?"

Wu Changge's expression turned serious. He said cautiously, "If you were at your peak, Fellow Daoist, I might have less than a thirty percent chance of victory. But the battle at Trapped Sand Valley was so fierce that I imagine you were severely injured even if you didn't die. Of course, even wounded, I don't have full confidence in taking you down. But stalling you for a while—I think that shouldn't be a problem."

He spoke neither humbly nor arrogantly, but with full confidence.

Because Luo Chen's external mana fluctuations were at the sixth level of the Sage stage.

And Wu Changge himself was also at the mid-Sage stage.

No matter how glorious Luo Chen's legendary battle achievements were, in the end, everything was built on the foundation of cultivation realm itself.

He had that confidence!

Luo Chen said nothing, just squinting as he watched the increasingly fierce battle ahead.

The five monks of Mount Wutai were indeed no pushovers.

The three who had announced themselves later had solid foundations, comparable to early-stage Sages.

Master Cihai, who had fought them before, was equivalent to a mid-stage Sage. His Purple-Gold Sumeru Bowl in particular suppressed space, subtly restraining Azure Frost.

And finally, there was the leader, Master Ci'en.

White beard and brows, appearing completely benevolent.

But when he moved, his monk's robe fluttered, muscles bulged, and the sound of wind and thunder was unceasing.

This monk was walking the Vajra Body-Tempering path!

He was even more formidable than the Wrathful Vajra Luo Chen had seen in the Central Continent. Astral energy surged, enough to meet Azure Frost's frontal assault head-on.

With the five of them forming a formation, Azure Frost couldn't take them down in a short time. Instead, she was in constant danger, growing increasingly strained.

Unable to secure a victory for so long, Azure Frost was showing signs of impatience.

If not for her severe injuries, if not for the restrictions of the Accumulated Void Flower, in her normal state, how could they possibly be her match?

Clang!

After raising her hand to block an attack from a crescent-shaped monk's staff, Azure Frost's face turned cold.

Sleeve Universe was useless. The Void Escape Divine Art was restricted. Even her most proud and powerful physical body was being suppressed by Ci'en and the Five Directions Sea of Suffering Grand Formation.

But did they really think this was enough to take her down?

Ignoring the Sumeru Bowl descending upon her head, Azure Frost began forming hand seals.

When those familiar seal gestures appeared, Luo Chen's eyelids twitched.

He took a step forward.

Wu Changge's full attention was on him. Seeing this step, he couldn't help but shout, "Fellow Daoist Luo, don't bring misfortune upon yourself!"

Luo Chen glanced at him and said calmly, "You said that when I'm at my peak, you have a thirty percent chance of victory. Have you seen my full strength?"

Wu Changge met those eyes and couldn't help but freeze.

It was like seeing flowers in a mirror, like falling into a moon in water.

In that dazed moment, Luo Chen had already passed him.

Within the grand formation.

A pitch-black fissure suddenly appeared, flying upward.

The Purple-Gold Sumeru Bowl that had been suppressing all directions split in two the moment the black fissure touched it.

Seeing an opportunity, Azure Frost shot straight up into the air.

But at that moment, a fist gleaming like gold smashed down from above, faintly revealing a swastika character.

Suddenly!

"Slap!"

A hand caught that enormous fist. Despite the "swastika" golden light surging along that arm, continuously assaulting its owner, the man did not retreat a single step.

Ci'en's beard and hair flew wildly as he mobilized all his astral energy, constantly washing over the one blocking his path.

His eyes bulged as he roared, "Luo Chen! Are you truly so stubbornly deluded?"

Rustle!

His robes flapped violently as Luo Chen's body erupted with red light, continuously forcing back the swastika golden light.

He said calmly, "It is you masters who are attached to appearances!"

Then, he raised one hand high and brought it down heavily.

"Roar!"

Above the nine heavens, a flame dragon charged down with a roar.

Ci'en bore the brunt of it and hastily retreated.

Seizing this opportunity, Luo Chen grabbed Azure Frost. With a flash of light in his eyes, he spotted the flaw in the grand formation and immediately drifted out.

The moment they broke free, a tremendous boom suddenly erupted.

Boom!

The Five Directions Sea of Suffering Grand Formation, which had caused Azure Frost so much trouble and slowed her movements, shattered instantly.

Five figures charged out furiously, chasing after Luo Chen and Azure Frost.

Faced with their relentless pursuit, Luo Chen sighed.

He formed hand seals, and a spell took shape in an instant.

Azure Frost beside him had also recovered and was forming seals as well.

The next moment!

Screech!

A unique bird cry resounded in all directions.

Red light blazed across the plains. Green light filled the sky.

Luo Chen looked slightly stunned at the two spells released together.

A fire phoenix—his most skilled technique, Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain.

A green luan—released by Azure Frost.

At this moment, the two seemed to achieve the same frequency and rhythm, intertwining seamlessly, crossing space to charge at the five... no, counting Wu Changge, six people.

Amid the harmonious cry of the luan and phoenix, Master Cihai's physical body exploded directly.

Ci'en was not much better off. His Leakless Golden Body at the Great Vajra realm actually shriveled for an instant.

The other three didn't take the first wave of the attack head-on, but they were still blasted back hundreds of feet.

Wu Changge was luckier. Waving his feather fan, he wiped away the last of the residual power.

When the smoke and dust cleared, looking at the empty space ahead, his face was filled with terror.

"Alchemy Sect Luo Chen... terrifying beyond belief!"

...

"I didn't expect you could use spells."

"Nonsense. I walk the pure Qi Refinement path. How else could I comprehend the true meaning of spatial laws?"

"Then why didn't I see you using spells to fight before?"

"Spells are too slow. You have to form seals. More troublesome than fists and feet. And that Green Luan Void-Annihilation Art consumes a lot of mana. Normally, there's no need to use it."

Through this exchange of questions and answers, Luo Chen learned the name of the spell Azure Frost had just used.

Green Luan Void-Annihilation!

Though not a divine ability, its power was vast and its range wide, comparable to his perfected Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain.

And strangely, at that moment, the two seemed to have a tacit understanding, releasing their spells together.

Under the coordination of the fire phoenix and green luan, there was actually a harmonious cry effect. When the combined move was unleashed, its power was no less than a divine ability!

In a single move, they severely wounded Ci'en with his Great Vajra physique, destroyed Cihai's physical body, and inflicted considerable injuries on the others.

And this was with both of them in a severely wounded state, with insufficient mana.

If each were at their peak, unleashing this move with the fullest power of their spells, the power would be...

"Was that your innate demon art?" Luo Chen asked.

Azure Frost did not answer. Her already fair skin was now even paler after several consecutive battles.

She just stared at Luo Chen and asked in a low voice, "Why did you save me?"

Luo Chen answered as if it were the most natural thing in the world. "I have your restriction on me. What if you drag me down with you before you die?"

Azure Frost shook her head. "With your restriction-breaking attainments that can see through my Void Escape Divine Art, those restrictions can't take your life."

Luo Chen smiled freely. "But the Same Life and Death Gu won't care about that!"

Hearing the name "Same Life and Death Gu," Azure Frost fell silent.

In the underground cavern of Trapped Sand Valley, the two had not only placed restrictions on each other and sworn Great Dao oaths, but also planted fourth-order Same Life and Death Gu.

In this way, the two were truly bound together, grasshoppers on the same string.

So, was he forced to act only out of necessity?

Seeing her silent, Luo Chen didn't try to find another topic. Instead, he thought of something else.

How had Ci'en, Cihai, and the others tracked them so precisely?

The answer became clear the moment they truly set foot on the Buddhist lands of the oasis.

Azure Frost's demonic aura was truly overwhelming.

Luo Chen's baleful aura, killing intent, and even the bloody qi remaining from the Southern Frontier battle, the Broken Soul Cliff battle, and the slaughter of a hundred thousand demon beasts with a single sword strike—all were too intense.

And on the Buddhist lands of the Western Desert, everything was filled with the fragrance of meditation and Buddhist qi.

The two walking through it were like the stars and moon in the night sky—so incongruous and dazzling.

The solution to this problem was very simple.

Luo Chen repeatedly used the Dragon-Slaying Art to cleanse himself of all bloody qi.

Then, using the Myriad Formation Gu, he set up a formation to conceal aura, thoroughly hiding Azure Frost's demonic aura.

If that were all, it might have been enough.

But Luo Chen still felt it wasn't safe. He took Azure Frost into a Buddhist temple and stole two strands of Buddhist qi from young novices, planting them on himself and Azure Frost.

"This should be foolproof!"

"Overkill!" Azure Frost said, but she still allowed Luo Chen to do as he pleased.

Luo Chen shrugged. "You might not be afraid, but I really don't want to fight anymore. If this continues, damaging my foundation and inner reserves, how will I break through to the Ascendant realm in the future?"

Azure Frost countered, "Missing a portion of your fate soul, no matter how deep your foundation or how abundant your reserves, how can you condense your primordial spirit and step into the Ascendant realm?"

This time, it was Luo Chen's turn to fall silent.

Inside the Buddhist temple.

The golden bodies of the enshrined Buddhas and Bodhisattvas shone brightly. The ancient temple's chanting was solemn.

Surrounded by a gathering of devotees, with incense burning vigorously, Luo Chen stood silently among them, troubled by worries.

Seeing him like this, Azure Frost knew that her casual question had touched upon a problem Luo Chen had never dared to delve into.

She sighed and said softly, "From the situation at Broken Soul Cliff that day, Heavenly Origin Lord Mo Shouzhuo didn't have your fate soul on him. So your portion of the fate soul is most likely hidden within the Dao Sect in the Central Continent. The fact that you haven't sensed anything now is good news. There will always be a chance to take it back in the future."

Luo Chen's lips were tightly pressed, his cheeks sunken.

"Let's go!"

Luo Chen turned around, carrying the gaze of the golden Buddha on his back, and left the temple.

Azure Frost said nothing more and immediately followed.

...

The journey that followed had fewer twists and turns.

Perhaps it was because they were clearing out the demon beast forces still lingering on the Buddhist lands, or perhaps because Luo Chen's concealment methods were too effective. In any case, the powerful Buddhist cultivators of the Western Desert never found Luo Chen and Azure Frost. They traveled peacefully and quietly, arriving at their destination half a year later.

The Buddha Mother Temple!

Despite being called "Buddha Mother," this ancient temple dedicated to the Great Bright Peacock King had little incense.

Only a few monks performed the tasks of sweeping and striking the bell.

A man and a woman entered quietly.

By now, Luo Chen had recovered from his low state half a year ago. He even had the interest to discuss with Azure Frost the application of spatial restrictions in teleportation formations.

"It's not that difficult. The hard part is actually the materials. Your lifebound treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, has excellent material quality, which is why it could bear my spatial prohibition back then."

"If that's the case, then the teleportation formation outposts that Rosycloud established throughout the Mountains and Seas Realm must have consumed a lot of precious materials."

"And you know how precious they are. The one in the Northern Sea—you cut off a corner of it, didn't you? It took me a lot of effort to repair that teleportation formation."

"Haha."

Luo Chen laughed it off, ignoring Azure Frost's glare, and released his divine sense.

Azure Frost wiped her Star-Fixing Compass, covering the ground.

"No need to check. It's right under the golden body of the Bright King in this Buddha Mother Temple."

Having said that, she was about to step inside.

But her raised foot hung in the air, hesitating and not falling.

Azure Frost looked in confusion at Luo Chen, who had reached out to stop her.

Luo Chen shook his head warily and slowly backed away.

From behind the mottled golden statue of the Peacock Bright King, an ordinary-looking young novice monk walked out.

Seeing the two, he was not alarmed. Instead, he bowed.

"Amitabha. This humble monk is Xuan Du. Greetings to the two benefactors."

The name Xuan Du made Luo Chen's expression change slightly.

As mentioned before, in the five continents of the Mountains and Seas Realm, the names of the top-tier great Sage cultivators often spread far and wide.

Apart from the relatively mysterious Southern Frontier, which had no famous experts with widespread reputations, the other continents all had such figures.

The Mad Wanderer among the Three Great Wanderers of the Northern Sea, the Chess Saint Ke Lianshan of the Central Continent, the Alchemy Sect's Luo Chen and Tiangouzi of the East Wasteland, and so on.

And in the Western Desert, the most famous great cultivator was only one!

"Xuan Du?"

Luo Chen pursed his lips and spoke the other's origin.

"Could it be the master of the 'One Man, One Temple'—the Lord of the Glazed Pure Land, the Great Dharma Master Xuan Du?"
