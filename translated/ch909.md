# Chapter 909: Stars Fall, Thunder Collapses, Reunited in the Sanctuary

*Clang! Clang! Clang!*

The once melodious bell tolls now rang like death knells throughout the Nether Origin Sacred Land.

All Nether Origin cultivators, upon receiving the notice, flew desperately toward Yama Mountain.

Even the Upper Nether Immortal City outside the sect had all its formations fully activated, ready for battle at any moment.

Within the Alchemy Saint Blessed Land.

The water mirrors flickered, dimly displaying scenes from various locations.

Escape light gathered like clouds, cultivators fell like rain.

All manner of magical treasures were displayed without restraint, exuding an aura of slaughter.

Upon the earth, lifeless puppets advanced coldly, and giant beasts nearly a thousand zhang tall roared and stampeded.

Luo Lingxi stared blankly at this scene, his young mind deeply shaken.

Was this the bizarre and dazzling world of immortal cultivation?

Although he had grown up in the Luo Tian Sect, Sima Huiniang knew her son would likely be unable to cultivate, so she deliberately shielded him from all this information.

Occasionally, when outsiders visited, they would mostly land their escape light at the foot of Motian Cliff.

The places he usually went were mostly bustling markets where mortals gathered.

Later, as Luo Chen left Motian Cliff, he had witnessed quite a few cultivators riding clouds and flying across mountains and seas, but those sights were still sparse.

Only at this moment!

The vast and spectacular scene of countless cultivators mobilizing together was deeply etched into his heart.

If one cannot cultivate, one walks this mortal life in vain!

Suddenly!

He felt a sharp pain in his hand and instinctively looked up at the woman beside him.

"Sister Ruoli?"

Ye Ruoli abruptly let go, panting heavily. "Sorry, Lingxi. Big sister hurt you."

Luo Lingxi shook his head. "It's fine. I know you're worried about the sect's safety."

Ye Ruoli bit her red lips, looking at the white-robed man sitting cross-legged ahead.

He wore the Snowfall Frost Robe bestowed by the Ancestor, bore the title of Alchemy Saint Hall Master, and was rumored to possess heaven-reaching abilities. At this moment, would he not go out to lend a hand to the Nether Origin Sect?

Luo Chen seemed oblivious, but his breathing grew heavier.

Through his vertical golden pupils, enhanced by the formation to see outside, he could clearly observe the violent flow of origin qi.

The battle within the Netherworld Yellow Spring Diagram had clearly reached its most critical moment.

The Ancient Demon Emperor had entered that diagram and ultimately perished, his Dao extinguished.

What about Rosycloud?

The answer was...

...

*Rip!*

A piercing sound overwhelmed the myriad battle cries and drowned out the bell tolls that shook heaven and earth.

Before the stunned eyes of all, that precious diagram, stretching ten thousand li and flowing with rolling yellow spring water, was torn apart.

A wisp of golden flame nimbly drilled out.

Then, a single spark ignited the prairie!

One wisp became ten thousand, and in the blink of an eye, it became a prairie fire, surging upstream in a violent and overwhelming momentum, rolling back the endless yellow spring.

At the end of the yellow spring, the three Ancestors looked terrified, each unleashing their ultimate techniques.

*Boom!*

In the violent collision, the Netherworld Yellow Spring Diagram was completely shattered.

This peerless spirit treasure, which had rendered illustrious service in ancient times and even the present era, was burned to ashes by the Ancient Demon Phoenix.

Its owner, Black Marsh Ancestor, was most severely wounded due to his spiritual connection with the treasure. He could no longer suppress his injuries and spat out bright red blood.

The three Ancestors did not hesitate, scattering to flee.

Rosycloud watched this scene, merely sneered coldly, and pursued Black Marsh Ancestor, who was the most severely injured.

Just then, starlight sprinkled down from above.

Rosycloud was oblivious, bathing in the starlight. Those starlight attacks, which contained immense killing power, posed no threat to her whatsoever, unable even to breach her protective qi shield.

"Qinghe, is this the All-Heaven Star-Slaying Grand Formation that captured you?"

Rosycloud sneered, spreading her five fingers toward the sky and clenching them slightly.

"How dare you all stand above me?!"

The next moment, starlight fell like rain.

Everywhere it landed, blazing flames erupted, as if meteorites were falling from beyond the heavens.

The thirty-six Sage cultivators forming the array felt an unbearable heat, their flesh and blood continuously withering away.

Those who were decisive immediately abandoned their physical bodies and fled with their nascent souls.

However, the strange golden flames clung to their nascent souls like stubborn maggots.

"Ah!"

"I don't want to die!"

"Ancestor, save me!"

Within the Alchemy Saint Blessed Land.

A mournful cry escaped involuntarily.

"Clan Leader!"

It was Ye Ruoli.

She watched helplessly as her clan leader, Ye Lingtian—the Lingtian City Lord most familiar to Luo Chen—was engulfed by the Nirvana Sacred Flame.

Luo Chen shouted coldly, "Quiet!"

At this moment, he was panting heavily, his eyes fixed on the outside world.

Unlike the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame, which only targeted the physical body, the Nirvana Sacred Flame clearly possessed a powerful attack against both the cultivator's nascent soul and divine soul.

This discovery excited him inexplicably.

It also confirmed what he had learned and understood before.

The Withering-Bloom Divine Flame was essentially a wood-attribute law, containing the dao of life through the cycle of reincarnation.

Only if the divine soul does not perish can life endure forever.

But the Nirvana Sacred Flame was different. From the very beginning, it was aimed at seeking life through death, resurrection through demise.

Especially the physical nirvana and divine soul nirvana within the Nine Tribulations Three Nirvanas—both wandered between the extremes of life and death.

In other words, if Luo Chen grasped its essence, he might also be able to use the Nirvana Sacred Flame to attack an enemy's divine soul!

As for the plight of Lingtian City Lord, who had a decent relationship with him, he could only express that he was powerless to help.

The Ascendant-level battle was never something they, as Sages, should meddle in.

The successful siege of Qinghe Immortal last time was merely an isolated case.

The opponent had only recently become an Ascendant, and his mastered Wood True Intent was completely suppressed, which led to their overwhelming victory.

But an isolated case was just that—how could it be fully replicated?

Having entered this game, such an outcome was something everyone should have anticipated.

But!

Anticipation was one thing; accepting the result was another.

Yu Ming Ancestor, who had already fled, suddenly turned around, his eyes burning with heartache.

Those were the backbone of his Nether Origin Sect, the future of the sect!

How could he watch them die before his eyes?

He pulled his hands apart.

"Wind, come!"

A blast of astral wind descended from the blue sky.

A wisp of nether wind rose from the depths of the earth.

He opened his mouth and spat out a thread of blood wind.

Three winds of different attributes merged in an instant, forming a strange blood-colored totem.

From afar, it looked like a demon beast, or perhaps a strange bird.

This strange bird swallowed Yu Ming Ancestor whole and then charged toward Rosycloud.

Facing this being, Rosycloud raised an eyebrow.

"The ancient desolate beast Biaofeng that once wielded the power of wind?"

"A pity!"

"Even if the Biaofeng beast were resurrected, it would only be worthy of kneeling at my feet! Let alone a mere divine ability!"

Rosycloud chuckled lightly and flew to meet it.

Amidst the boundless golden light, vast magical power surged forth, vaguely revealing a divine bird spreading its wings.

Torrential magical power swept through the surging origin qi.

Two majestic figures, staking everything on a single move, embodied the principle that on a narrow path, the brave prevails.

*Bang!*

The phoenix and the strange bird engaged in the simplest, purest collision.

Then, they separated.

The fire grew increasingly fierce, while the wind force weakened by three parts.

In terms of foundation, Yu Ming Ancestor was ultimately no match for Rosycloud.

Just then, thunder rumbled from the nine heavens above.

"Ancestor, lend me your strength!"

Black Marsh Ancestor, who had been hiding in the shadows, slowly revealed himself, blood trickling from the corner of his mouth. He pointed a finger at the sky above Rosycloud.

"Rain, fall!"

A torrential downpour crashed down.

Rosycloud, caught within it, found her magical power shield corroded, hissing with white steam.

Not only that, but even her speed seemed slowed by the rain curtain.

At that moment, the thunder pool rumbled.

Thunder Daozi, wielding a black greatsword, channeled the boundless thunderlight and transformed into a bolt of lightning, charging down.

This technique was the pinnacle of his achievements after inheriting the Thunder Law True Intent of the great power Bai Ye.

Even if he were to reach the Ascendant realm in the future, it would be difficult to execute.

For this technique also drew upon the power of the Nether Origin Sect's defensive grand formation, which had been operating for three thousand years.

Especially a thousand years ago, when the great power Bai Ye had added his most proud divine ability, the *Nine Heavens Universal Great Thunder Art*, to continuously draw thunder power into it day and night.

Three thousand years of power, a millennium's accumulation of thunder, finally formed that thunder pool and enabled Thunder Daozi's stunning sword strike.

In just a flash.

When Thunder Daozi reappeared, he was already within three feet of the red-clad woman.

The black greatsword, with an unstoppable momentum, pierced into the magical power shield that only an Ascendant-level power could break.

Hidden within the thunderlight, Thunder Daozi's expression was utterly resolute as he channeled unmatched magical power, determined to pierce the shield and kill this demon phoenix.

However, only at this moment did he realize why the All-Heaven Star-Slaying Grand Formation was ineffective against her.

"How can such an existence exist in this world?"

Rosycloud looked at the sword tip pointing at her brow, a displeased expression on her face.

Blazing flames surged upward, continuously clashing with the vast thunder.

"Junior, you are seeking death!"

"Not enough!" Thunder Daozi's face twisted. "Still not enough!"

Three thousand years of power!

A millennium's accumulation of thunder!

Still not enough!

Plus his own six hundred years of cultivation!

With a thought, within his Purple Mansion, the nascent soul of a Grand Cultivator began to tremble violently, then instantly collapsed.

It transformed into the purest magical power, merging into the thunder giant sword.

*Pfft!*

A crisp sound rang out. The sword tip finally pierced Rosycloud's magical power shield.

But that was as far as it went.

The woman flicked her finger at the gleaming sword tip.

*Ding!*

A clear sound. The sword tip shattered.

The shattering spread relentlessly. The black greatsword also began to crack inch by inch, eventually reaching Thunder Daozi.

*Crack! Crack! Crack!*

His physical body split apart. His nascent soul had long since collapsed. Only a wisp of divine soul drifted between heaven and earth.

"Ancestor, this disciple has done his best."

That wisp of divine soul was instantly wrapped in a raindrop, about to flee.

But Rosycloud's brows shot up.

"Black Marsh, you skulking rat, you think you can escape?!"

She raised her hands, and a torrent of golden flame surged forth.

Following the rain curtain in reverse, it chased after Black Marsh Ancestor.

In the process, the raindrop containing Thunder Daozi's divine soul turned into a wisp of steam, dissipating into the void.

Nascent soul collapsed, physical body shattered, divine soul scattered.

Zhang Che, the Thunder Daozi of Nether Origin who had once been invincible in the Eastern Wilderness, had completely perished, his Dao extinguished.

Yu Ming Ancestor, transformed into the Biaofeng beast, watched this scene, his heart like dead ashes.

"Rosycloud, do you truly intend to show no mercy?"

Rosycloud sneered. "Blame yourselves for provoking me. Today, I will leave this place with not a single chicken or dog remaining!"

"Fine words—not a single chicken or dog remaining!"

Yu Ming Ancestor took a deep breath, his face showing the same resolute expression as Thunder Daozi.

"Then let's fight!"

Even if he died, he would not let her have an easy time.

The Biaofeng beast, wrapped in endless wind, charged at Rosycloud.

The two sides engaged in another great battle.

Incaculable amounts of surging heaven-earth origin qi were drawn in. Both were representatives of ultimate speed. In their frenzied battle, the battlefield began to expand uncontrollably.

The vast Nether Origin Sacred Land, eight or nine times the size of the Jade Cauldron Domain, was, under their battle, traversed in an instant, like a tiny arena.

Wherever they passed, it was like doomsday.

No matter how sturdy the defensive formations built by various major powers within the Nether Origin Sect were, they were now like tofu blocks, shattering on contact.

Under the scorching flames and violent astral wind, countless living beings turned to ash, scattering with the wind.

Throughout this process, powerful combatants from the Nether Origin Sect's profound foundation would occasionally join the fray, trying to help Sect Leader Yu Ming Ancestor.

But Rosycloud's power and brutality were utterly unreasonable.

No matter what existence approached her, it would turn to ash in an instant.

The golden Nirvana Sacred Flame had become a terrifying flame that devoured everything.

However, Luo Chen could no longer see any of this.

For long before the Nether Origin Sect Leader Yu Ming Ancestor had engaged in direct combat with Rosycloud, the massive amounts of heaven-earth origin qi had already destroyed the spiritual eyes Luo Chen had set up outside.

At this moment, he could only anxiously and futilely remain within the Alchemy Saint Blessed Land.

He could sense the omnipresence of that golden flame.

Not just within ten thousand li!

As long as Rosycloud had enough magical power, even a hundred thousand, a million, or even ten million li would be engulfed in a sea of fire.

Only at this moment did he finally understand why the three Ancestors of the Nether Origin Sect valued this Alchemy Saint Blessed Land so much.

It was not only the source of Yama Mountain's birth, with the potential to advance to a fifth-rank spirit vein in the future.

It was also a sanctuary!

Able to withstand heavenly tribulation, evade earthly calamities, and preserve a spark of the Nether Origin Sect under powerful foreign invasion.

"So this is the meaning of a blessed land and grotto-heaven."

Luo Chen murmured, feeling incredibly fortunate.

If he had not mastered this place, his fate would likely be the same as Lingtian City Lord and the others!

Dying silently, without a trace.

Although Thunder Daozi died miserably, at least his death was heroic and stirring.

But soon, he realized something was wrong.

The blessed land was connected to the Alchemy Saint Hall, and the entrance door was open!

Given Rosycloud's power, it was absolutely impossible for her not to notice this place.

Sure enough!

In less than half a day, Luo Chen sensed a scorching force approaching.

*Chi!*

Within the blessed land.

A blade of grass ignited spontaneously.

A wisp of golden flame slowly rose.

Luo Chen's expression tightened. He immediately shielded Luo Lingxi and the other behind him.

That golden flame seemed contagious. Once it invaded the Alchemy Saint Blessed Land, it began to spread like wildfire.

"This cannot continue, or we will have no place to stay."

With a thought, Luo Chen summoned his own Nirvana Sacred Flame.

His Nirvana Sacred Flame was still relatively weak, but under Luo Chen's magical power, it formed a ring of fire, surrounding the three of them.

The invading Nirvana Sacred Flame, encountering this ring of fire as if meeting its own kind, indeed stopped its erosion.

Seeing this, Luo Chen couldn't help but breathe a sigh of relief.

"Just as I thought!"

"Since Rosycloud used me as a guide to find Qinghe Immortal's location, she wouldn't harbor much malice toward me."

"At the very least, in this battle, she won't harm me."

Just as Luo Chen was thinking this, a heavy *thump* suddenly echoed through the blessed land.

A torrential curtain of rain crashed down, forcing back an area where the Nirvana Sacred Flame was burning.

An aged figure lay on the ground, barely alive or dead.

Luo Chen looked into the distance and saw the old man holding a token, with a trace of breath still remaining.

"It's Black Marsh Ancestor!" Ye Ruoli exclaimed in pleasant surprise, about to rush out to save the Ancestor.

But her steps could never move forward.

Because Luo Chen's powerful spiritual pressure was holding her down.

"Alchemy Saint Hall Master?"

Ye Ruoli was confused, looking at Luo Chen with doubt and anxiety.

Luo Chen shook his head, his gaze like a torch, fixed on that figure.

Black Marsh Ancestor struggled to get up, his turbid eyes meeting Luo Chen's, and also fixing on that golden ring of fire.
