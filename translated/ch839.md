# Chapter 839: Nine Chapters of Bone Dissolution, Heavenly Pillar Mountain Breaks

A demon emperor comparable to a mid-Sage level cultivator had fallen at the foot of Heavenly Pillar Mountain without any chance to resist.

The three Lu brothers, who had been about to step forward, were greatly startled and immediately halted their advance.

Within the Heavenly Capital Sect, large numbers of disciples lay sprawled across the ground, their lives unknown, after the self-destruction of the flame giant.

Those who remained conscious could not help but cheer.

"Reinforcements have arrived!"

On Heavenly Pillar Mountain, the four Sages also sensed all of this, their faces brimming with ecstatic joy.

But the next moment, their expressions changed drastically.

"Don't let that demoness escape!"

Within the Eight Trigrams Diagram, Jinxiu Xian, who had originally been obscured by grayish mist, seemed to have detected the changes outside and began destroying the diagram formation with reckless abandon.

Her graceful figure flickered in and out of sight, as if she could break free from her cage at any moment.

This scene naturally fell into the eyes of Luo Chen and the other two.

"White-robed, skilled in transformation and escape, possessing yin-soul attributes, plus that faint trace of aura—this demon must be the one that gave me that palpitation in Overlord Blade Domain."

"With our combined strength, plus the Heavenly Capital Sect's grand formation, we managed to destroy it in a surprise attack."

"Now, we can go all out."

This was the preliminary plan Luo Chen had formulated earlier.

By combining the strength of the three of them, they would first eliminate the most threatening opponent in a surprise attack.

Now that the goal had been achieved, they could proceed more calmly.

Jin Ling shook the golden natal feather in his hand and charged directly at the nearest Thousand Ropes Demon Emperor. The moment they clashed, the sky and earth turned dark and chaotic.

Fenghua Fairy did not hesitate. A fierce glint flashed in her eyes as she attacked Yellow Wind Guest.

Two figures transformed into a cyan and a yellow gale, chasing and entwining with each other, pulling the battlefield away from the Heavenly Capital Sect's mountain gate and beginning to sweep across ten thousand li of land.

Finally, only the three Lu brothers remained!

They were also the strongest among the remaining enemies!

Although each of them appeared to be below the mid-fourth stage, they were all proficient in formations.

Especially since the three brothers looked alike, they must know the art of coordinated combat. United, they would likely be even more troublesome than an ordinary mid-Sage cultivator.

And this enemy, Luo Chen would personally handle!

As long as each of them dealt with their respective opponents, even if Jinxiu Xian later broke free, with the combined strength of Luo Chen's trio and the four from Heavenly Capital Sect, they would still have a fighting chance.

A golden light flashed in his eyes. Luo Chen's wide sleeves fluttered as he blocked the three demons.

"Luo Chen of the Pill Sect, please enlighten me!"

Hearing Luo Chen announce his name, the three brothers' expressions changed almost identically, showing slight surprise.

But clearly, there was no fear.

"Lu Changsheng!"

"Lu Changqing!"

"Lu Chang'an!"

"Please enlighten us!"

Before the words faded, Lu Chang'an shook the black banner in his hand. A gale swept forth, and a hazy demonic aura spread outward.

Seeing this, Luo Chen retreated without a second thought.

With a *bang* of voidburst, Luo Chen had already appeared a hundred zhang away.

Then, stepping with Moon Step, he evaded the surging black demonic aura and charged straight toward the three demons.

His movement technique was so bizarre it was difficult to fathom.

However, Lu Changqing slapped the bronze mirror in his hand and softly chanted: "Slow! Slow! Slow!"

With three consecutive cries of "slow," the bronze mirror shot out three clear, crystalline beams.

*Whoosh!* The first one, Luo Chen easily dodged.

*Whoosh!* The second one, Luo Chen narrowly brushed past.

*Whoosh!* The third one, Luo Chen turned his head to look back at the moon, abruptly stopping, barely avoiding it, with only a corner of his robe stained by a wisp of cyan light.

And it was precisely this wisp that caused Luo Chen's figure to suddenly slow down.

*Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh...*

A large amount of black qi surged from all directions, surrounding Luo Chen completely.

The eldest brother, Lu Changsheng, sat cross-legged on the ground, placed his hand heavily on the compass, and pressed down firmly.

*Click, click, click...*

The compass rotated, emitting harsh mechanical sounds.

"As expected of a renowned demon-slayer, so difficult to suppress."

Lu Changsheng steeled himself, bit his lip, and spat a mouthful of blood containing pure magical power onto the compass.

As if lubricated, the compass's rotation speed suddenly increased, and the sounds it made were no longer so harsh.

With the compass spinning rapidly, Lu Changsheng, who had been sitting cross-legged on the ground, floated up into the air.

Looking down from above, Lu Changsheng let out a low shout.

"Fellow Daoist, please enter the formation!"

He pressed down with both hands in the air, and within a radius of a hundred li, a terrifying pressure suddenly arose.

Even the ground was forcibly pressed down by several zhang.

And under this terrifying pressure, Luo Chen was forced into the formation.

"To activate a formation during battle, even with the combined strength of three, it still shows considerable skill in the dao of formations."

Luo Chen stood firm amidst the vast black mist, a look of wonder on his face.

He looked up at the three faint figures in the sky, appearing and disappearing, and could not help but ask loudly:

"What is the name of this formation?"

The voices of the three Lu brothers came drifting down.

"Let Fellow Daoist die with full understanding!"

"This formation was created by us three brothers, named Nine Chapters of Bone Dissolution."

"Those who enter the formation will have their flesh and blood destroyed, their souls dissolved, and their bones consumed!"

The three voices came from different directions, indicating that their positions were constantly changing.

And this formation, with the three of them as the core formation eyes, was constantly shifting, making it difficult to capture—clearly a "living formation."

As soon as the voice fell, a wave of black mist surged forward.

Without a second thought, Luo Chen struck out with a palm.

The Azure Yang Great Handprint collided with the black mist, annihilating each other.

A fire phoenix screeched as it flew out, stubbornly entangled with the newly formed black mist before being annihilated into nothingness.

*Roar!*

A shocking dragon roar erupted, and outside the formation, a Flame Dragon descended.

As if in response, a black dragon formed from black mist drilled out from within the formation, meeting it without hesitation.

Under the gaze of many low-level disciples of the Heavenly Capital Sect, the two giant dragons roared and collided, the powerful fluctuations of magical power grinding an entire mountain into dust.

Lu Changsheng, presiding over the formation, trembled inwardly, his eyelids twitching wildly.

"Nine Chapters of Bone Dissolution, with three as the limit. In the blink of an eye, three chapters have passed. This Pill Sect cultivator truly lives up to his reputation, forcing our formation to be passively activated to this extent in such a short time."

Lu Changqing transmitted his divine sense: "Big brother, we need to increase our magical power, otherwise the formation's operation will have problems."

Lu Changsheng shouted in a low voice: "Don't act rashly. Leave it to me and Third Brother!"

"Yes!"

Inside the formation.

Luo Chen withdrew his hand seal, a look of solemnity on his face.

Suppressed from within, attacks ineffective from without.

This formation was not as easy to break as he had imagined!

Especially since the core of this formation consisted of three living demon emperors, constantly moving. Even with his Illusion-Breaking Eye pushed to its limit, he could not find the formation's foundation for the time being.

Perhaps he should not have entered the formation?

At that time, with Luo Chen's abilities, as long as he was willing to pay some price, he could always forcibly create distance.

But if he did not enter the formation, these three demon emperors would probably ignore him and instead attack the Heavenly Capital Sect, reducing the pressure on Jinxiu Xian.

And once Jinxiu Xian broke free, the consequences would only be more severe.

"Very well. Since you use a formation to suppress me, I shall respond with a formation of my own!"

Luo Chen sneered coldly and stomped heavily on the ground with one foot.

"Sword formation, rise!"

The next moment, countless sword qi burst forth from the ground!

With a terrifying cutting intent, they swept across all directions.

Countless black mists surged forward, but often before the mists could take shape, they were completely annihilated by the sword qi.

Such a scene caused the faces of the three Lu brothers to change drastically.

"When did he set up the sword formation?"

"It must have been before he attacked Bai Lan. Since he could kill in a surprise attack, he must have laid down hidden measures."

"Let's release another three chapters. Second Brother, you break this sword formation!"

The three were of one mind, and with a brief exchange, they understood what each needed to do.

The black banner shook heaven and earth, and violent black qi surged out, transforming into small deer with prominent antlers that charged into the sword formation.

Black deer and sword qi fought each other, annihilating each other.

Looking at those familiar little deer forms, and then recalling the name of this formation, a sense of understanding dawned on Luo Chen.

"So the true forms of these three demons are Nine-Zhang Deer!"

He was not unfamiliar with this demon beast.

Years ago, a group of low-level Nine-Zhang Deer had been entrenched in the Nine-Fold Mountains between Dahe Market and Tianlan Immortal City, forcing them to take a detour.

This type of demon beast preferred to live in herds, relying on the strength of the group to dominate a region.

Ordinary cultivators simply dared not provoke them.

But this type of demon beast also had a weakness. What was overshadowed by their fierce group behavior was their weak individual strength and extremely low upper limit.

It was rare to hear of a Nine-Zhang Deer breaking through its bloodline limit, advancing to the fourth stage, and transforming to cultivate the Dao.

Yet unexpectedly, they had encountered Nine-Zhang Deer demon emperors this time, and three of them at that!

"Three thousand years of accumulation, the millions of great mountains in the Eastern Wilderness have truly nurtured some extraordinary existences!"

Luo Chen murmured, his pale golden eyes sweeping across the surroundings.

His Grand Five Elements Sword Formation was on the verge of collapse under the assault of the black mist.

Suddenly, a hazy cyan light shot in from outside the formation.

*Thud!*

*Thud!*

*Thud!*

*Thud!*

*Thud!*

Five beams of light in succession, each faster than the last.

The five sword pellets were forced to reveal themselves within the formation.

The sword qi that had been cutting in all directions also slowly dissipated at this moment.

Looking up, a multicolored-robed cultivator with a gourd-shaped topknot was holding a bronze mirror, shining it from afar at the five elemental sword pellets.

Seeing this, Luo Chen felt a pang of regret and immediately withdrew the five sword pellets.

"If the grades of the five sword pellets were not uneven, they would not have been so easily seen through in the collision."

"But!"

"It's enough!"

A fierce glint flashed across Luo Chen's face. His magical power erupted violently, and the Withering-Bloom Divine Seal within his body was quietly released.

A surging, terrifyingly powerful wave of magical power filled the surroundings of the formation, even causing the heaven and earth spiritual energy outside to resonate, howling toward him.

Encompassing Fire Prison!

With the power of his domain, he transformed into a red light and charged toward the mirror-wielding cultivator in the sky.

"How can this be? He is not affected by the formation's suppression of cutting off heaven and earth resonance?"

"It was the sword formation just now that temporarily offset the isolation power of the Nine Chapters of Bone Dissolution Formation, allowing him to gather his domain and no longer be suppressed by our formation."

"Second Brother, you've been exposed!"

Between questions and answers, the eldest brother Lu Changsheng cursed in anxiety.

Lu Changqing naturally understood his current predicament.

To break the Grand Five Elements Sword Formation, he had personally activated the bronze mirror, breaking its five-element circulation momentum, but his own position had also been exposed.

Now that Luo Chen was charging directly at him, it was clearly to break the three-legged stand.

As soon as his side was broken, the Nine Chapters of Bone Dissolution Formation would also collapse without a fight.

"Please help me!"

Lu Changqing cried for help while gritting his teeth, transforming into a cyan light and beginning to frantically dart around within the formation.

The other two dared not be negligent, especially the third brother, whose magical power was somewhat insufficient. He had already begun spitting out blood essence, forcibly activating the formation.

"Chapter Seven, Bone-Dissolving Qi!"

A mass of deep, unfathomable black mist spewed out from the banner, surging toward Luo Chen.

A stench, pungent to the nose.

Luo Chen held a poison-resisting pill in his mouth, neither dodging nor avoiding, and plunged straight into it.

The Mystic Dust Armor moved on its own, forming inner and outer layers.

The qi armor spread, constantly wearing away.

With a *hissing* sound, the Mystic Dust Armor's body also became pitted and scarred.

But Luo Chen forcibly endured this chapter, his pale golden eyes fixed on a certain location.

"Since you've already been exposed, don't think you can hide from my sight again!"

He grinned fiercely and reached out with a claw toward that location.

A cyan light shot out. The Cloud-Seizing Divine Claw was delayed for an instant and missed.

But Lu Changqing's figure was exposed once more.

Luo Chen charged again.

A faint, dark wind drifted over, eroding the soul and captivating the mind, causing one to sink into depravity.

Was this Chapter Eight?

A realization dawned on Luo Chen, but he had no good way to deal with it. Holding his breath, he advanced against the wind.

His powerful soul foundation was pushed to its limit, and the Aperture-Thatching Art continuously unfolded within his sea of consciousness, keeping him awake.

His speed had clearly slowed down.

But his forward momentum showed no sign of stopping!

The three presiding cultivators were horrified. "How can this be? The soul-eroding wind retrieved by Lord Ditian from Severed Soul Cliff cannot erode his soul? Just how deep is his soul foundation?"

"No, we cannot let him free his hands!"

"Let's release Chapter Nine!"

The three conferred and no longer hesitated.

A sound of the flagpole breaking came from the air. A large amount of black mist flew out from the shattered banner, separately entering the compass and the bronze mirror.

With the supplement of the black mist, the two treasures began to rumble and grow larger, transforming into giant objects like millstones.

Two huge millstones, one on the left and one on the right, rotated in different directions, grinding toward Luo Chen.

This was the final chapter of the Nine Chapters of Bone Dissolution Formation, named Yin-Yang Mill by the three brothers. It could grind away a cultivator's powerful magical power, as well as grind away a cultivator's fragile physical body.

Even within the millions of great mountains, this move had a notable record, having once ground down a powerful mid-fourth-stage demon emperor.

*Whoosh...*

Having endured the soul-eroding wind, Luo Chen suddenly woke up.

Then he saw two huge millstones slowly flying toward him.

Although the distance seemed vast, and the space seemed large, leaving enough room for maneuvering, when he tried to use Voidburst Step, Moon Step, Ninety Thousand Li, and other techniques, he felt the space was cramped and he could not move an inch.

"It's spatial restriction!"

"This formation contains a large amount of the basic restriction of spatial restriction, to prevent the enemy from escaping."

With Luo Chen's knowledge of formations, he understood everything in an instant.

Not only that, but with his combat awareness, he quickly realized that if he only wanted to escape, he would absolutely not be able to get out before the Yin-Yang millstones closed in.

"Since I can't escape, I'll dismantle it!"

A ferocious look appeared in Luo Chen's eyes. He pointed a finger at his chest.

The vast source power that had been nurtured for over a hundred years immediately flowed throughout his body.

His physique, like a demon beast revealing its true form, began to continuously expand and grow larger.

A nine-zhang-tall giant appeared in everyone's sight in the blink of an eye.

He neither dodged nor avoided, raising a palm like a hill and slapping it hard against one of the huge millstones.

Such a scene was truly horrifying.

But what was most horrifying was another earth-shattering sound.

*Boom!*

Under circumstances no one had anticipated, an endless, dazzling light burst forth from the Eight Trigrams Diagram.

A powerful force was unleashed upon the nearby Heavenly Pillar Mountain.

With a single blow, the most core Heavenly Pillar Mountain of the Heavenly Capital Sect was severed at its waist.

The towering mountain, as if broken by someone, crashed toward the earth.

Jinxiu Xian had reappeared in the world!

Not only that, but a white light shot out from the collapsed mountain peaks, flying toward the battle group where Jin Ling was.

"Be careful! There is a sinister, evil creature. It has devoured the nascent souls of my three junior brothers!"

Heavenly Authority's mournful, grief-stricken cry resounded between heaven and earth.

(Chapter End)
