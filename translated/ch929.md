# Chapter 929: Debt Collection!

In the vast Five Elements Domain, after many years, the Five Finger Mountain had reappeared.

Those five peaks that resembled fingers had been destroyed by a powerful demon emperor years ago. Their reappearance now signified that the Five Elements Divine Sect had returned to its former position.

However, whether there were Sage seat-holders presiding over those five peaks was another matter entirely.

Beyond the Five Finger Mountain, another towering peak, perpetually shrouded in mist, stood high above, overlooking everything within the Five Elements Divine Sect.

That was the cultivation abode of the sect's Grand Elder, Sage Divine Origin!

It was also, at present, a forbidden ground within the sect that made people pale at the mere mention of it.

Many disciples who accidentally entered would vanish without a trace. Even if the Sect Master, Divine Fire, personally went to search, it was very difficult to find them.

The world did not know what had happened to the Grand Elder, but Sect Master Divine Fire had repeatedly ordered that no one was to approach Divine Origin Peak!

On this day.

Sect Master Divine Fire stood on another cliff, gazing into the distance at Divine Origin Peak, his expression hesitant and conflicted.

Under his decades of vigorous governance, the Five Elements Divine Sect had gradually begun to show signs of prosperity.

Especially in the number of Sages, which, after years of silence, had finally begun to erupt.

By all appearances, the sect's future seemed bright and promising.

But this world, after all, would not allow people peace!

The demon race had attacked again, and this time, behind them was the personal support of that terrifying Lord of Green Parasol Mountain.

The overlord of the Central Continent had descended upon the Eastern Wilderness with force, intending to conquer its cultivation world.

To be honest, whether it was submitting to the Nether Origin Sect or bowing to the Tianyuan Dao Sect, it didn't really matter to these major Sage-level powers.

It was merely changing the sky above them.

But when two poles oppose each other, war is inevitable!

The current Five Elements Divine Sect simply could not withstand any more turmoil.

Sect Master Divine Fire did not want to see the excellent situation he had painstakingly built up destroyed once again by that damned human-demon war.

He wanted to go and seek an audience with his senior brother.

As one of the only three remaining late-stage Sage Grand Cultivators in the Eastern Wilderness, his senior brother was renowned far and wide.

If he were willing to personally approach the Tianyuan Dao Sect to plead for the Five Elements Divine Sect, seeking some easy and safe tasks in the coming war, that would be for the best.

Just like that lucky Divine Talisman Sect!

He had heard that they had obtained the protection of some high-ranking figure in the Tianyuan Dao Sect and did not have to go to the battlefield themselves, only needing to provide a large number of talismans steadily.

This kind of thing, his Five Elements Divine Sect could also do!

In fact, due to the comprehensiveness of the Five Elements Divine Sect, they could do it even better!

But his senior brother had not shown his face for a long time, not even when the Tianyuan Dao Sect's strong experts came to recruit him.

It was as if he was indifferent to everything happening in the outside world.

"Senior Brother, what exactly are you thinking?"

Sect Master Divine Fire muttered to himself, his expression gradually firming.

He had heard that there was a mountain in the Central Continent called Heaven-Earth Peak!

It connected the heavens and the earth, majestic and spectacular!

The Tianyuan Dao Sect had established a special organization on it—the Star Gate—specifically to accept strong experts from other continents.

Especially those who were older and of a higher realm, the Star Gate was most fond of accepting.

With his senior brother's realm and strength, even within the Star Gate, he would likely be an outstanding figure.

If his senior brother joined the Star Gate, then the Five Elements Divine Sect would have a backer in the future war, allowing them to avoid some dangerous battles.

Of course!

Sect Master Divine Fire also had a little selfish motive.

With his senior brother staying in the sect now, it was truly too dangerous.

If he left the sect, perhaps it would be a good thing for the sect itself?

Having thought through this point, Sect Master Divine Fire made up his mind, preparing to personally visit Divine Origin Peak to see his senior brother.

"I have inquired about this matter with the Tianyuan Dao Sect's strong experts. They swore it was feasible. Moreover, the Dao Sect's great expert currently stationed in the Eastern Wilderness is also somewhat interested in Senior Brother and is willing to accept him into the Star Gate."

"As long as Senior Brother agrees, for the future war, for the sect's development, and even for his personal desire to find a fifth-rank spiritual land to cultivate and advance to the Ascendant realm—once he enters Heaven-Earth Peak, it would be a good thing for him personally."

"Killing three birds with one stone, he should agree, right?"

Sect Master Divine Fire reaffirmed his thoughts, his mood no longer wavering. He took a step forward, intending to head towards Divine Origin Peak.

However, in the next moment, his pupils constricted, and his expression changed drastically.

He saw the vast mist that perpetually shrouded Divine Origin Peak in the distance begin to tremble violently.

The surging sea of clouds showed signs of collapsing.

"How is this possible?"

"That is Senior Brother's most prized Nine Heavens Cloud Chi Locking Star Formation, even superior to the sect's protective formation. How could it suddenly collapse?"

While he was still in doubt.

The vast mist suddenly became animated.

As the cloud and mist churned continuously, a pure white giant python, adorned with specks of starlight, raised its head proudly, its fierce gaze instantly locking onto a certain spot.

"Roar!"

The Star Chi Dragon roared, charging recklessly towards that spot regardless of everything.

Hoo...

A mountain wind blew, and a figure slowly materialized.

Facing the charging Chi Dragon, he was not flustered. He pointed a finger, which landed on the Chi Dragon's brow.

In an instant, the Star Chi Dragon, as if struck by a heavy blow, collapsed instantly.

But the white-robed man was not pleased; instead, he frowned slightly.

"That old thief's formation attainments are somewhat beyond my expectations."

Sure enough, the collapsed Chi Dragon regenerated within the vast sea of clouds, its ferocious gaze once again locking onto Luo Chen.

Boom!

A large amount of cloud and mist surged as the Chi Dragon shook its head and tail, charging towards the white-robed man.

Forcing him to retreat continuously.

Seeing this, Sect Master Divine Fire finally breathed a sigh of relief.

Although this man had used some method to temporarily break his senior brother's protective peak formation, it was clear he hadn't done a clean job, leaving some loose ends.

This, coincidentally, gave him enough time to react.

But!

In that fleeting glimpse, when he saw the white-robed man's face, Divine Fire's expression completely changed.

It was Luo Chen!

A sharp voice erupted from his mouth, spreading throughout the entire Five Elements Divine Sect.

"Enemy attack!"

Instantly, the entire Five Elements Divine Sect sprang into action.

The sound of bells rang out nine times in succession.

Vast quantities of mana surged forth violently.

And streaks of escape light shot up from the various peaks and valleys, rushing towards Divine Origin Peak.

Faced with all these disturbances, Luo Chen smiled helplessly.

At his current level, he naturally could not do as Rosycloud had done—forcefully charging into the heart of another's sect and slapping down Yanfu Mountain with one palm.

In fact, even Rosycloud had relied on the incarnation of Ancestor Tianyuan and using Luo Chen himself as an anchor point to achieve that single strike.

Therefore, Luo Chen could only sneak in quietly, planning to first deal with the protective formation of Divine Origin Peak, and then settle the old, unclear, accumulated debt of two hundred years with the old thief Divine Origin!

His actions were somewhat stealthy, but when it came to revenge, only the result mattered.

However, an accident occurred during the process.

The old thief Divine Origin's formation attainments were beyond the world's expectations.

He had actually reached the level of a Formation Grandmaster!

"But this formation ultimately has its flaws."

Luo Chen sneered, ignoring the Star Chi Dragon that cycled through life and death, endlessly reincarnating.

Mana flowed at his fingertips as he rapidly formed hand seals.

Then, he suddenly slammed his palm onto the ground!

"Mountain Collapse!"

Terrifying mana fluctuations swept through the world's spiritual energy, spreading from Luo Chen towards the towering Divine Origin Peak.

Not only that, a black light flew out from Luo Chen's arm, roared, and transformed into a several-hundred-zhang-long giant dragon.

Black King had already received Luo Chen's orders and naturally knew what to do next.

It fearlessly charged towards Divine Origin Peak, its massive body beginning to coil and spiral around the mountain, ascending upwards.

Terrifying force erupted from both the outside and the inside simultaneously.

Under the horrified gazes of the hastily arriving strong experts, that spiritual mountain, which had not collapsed even during the human-demon war, began to crack inch by inch.

The white-robed man slowly stood up, his gaze fixed on Divine Origin Peak.

Boom!

With only a single loud sound, the towering, majestic mountain collapsed into rubble!

The vast sea of clouds also dissipated into smoke.

The Star Chi Dragon within the sea of clouds let out a mournful cry before disappearing without a trace.

At this moment, the mountains crumbled and the earth split, as if doomsday had arrived.

Luo Chen watched the massive mountain collapse and shouted into the distance, "Old thief Divine Origin, how long will you hide?"

The questioning voice turned into a majestic wave of sound, spreading throughout the entire Five Elements Divine Sect.

However!

There was no response!

What responded were the angry shouts and curses behind Luo Chen.

"How dare you!"

"What reckless scoundrel dares to invade our sect?"

"Seize this arrogant madman!"

Facing these accusations, Luo Chen frowned and turned around.

Only now did his true face appear before the four hastily arrived Sages.

Since this was revenge, why hide one's head and show one's tail? His current face was his original appearance.

With just one look, Qi Emei exclaimed in shock, "Senior Dan Zong?"

"The Demon-Slaying Sage, Luo Chen!" another blurted out.

Yao Hong's expression was filled with doubt and uncertainty. "Senior, why are you acting like this?"

In the eyes of many, Luo Chen was a legend.

A legend who had risen rapidly and resounded throughout the Eastern Wilderness.

Especially his generous and charitable behavior towards the younger generation, which had earned him the goodwill of many.

Even Qi Emei and the others, having recently advanced to the Sage realm, had not yet changed their habits and still addressed him as "Senior."

To their questions, Luo Chen did not answer. Instead, he nodded slightly.

"In just a few decades, the Five Elements Divine Sect has nurtured three new Sages. Truly profound foundations!"

Then, his gaze shifted and landed on Divine Fire, the current Sect Master of the sect.

"Divine Fire, you should understand why I am here today, shouldn't you?"

Divine Fire's lips moved, but for a long time, no words came out.

Luo Chen shook his head. "Let's not even mention your Grand Elder's shady thoughts towards me. Just the matter of the three great demon emperors besieging me in the Medicine King Domain back then cannot escape the machinations of your Grand Elder. Today, Luo has come simply to collect a debt!"

Upon hearing this, Qi Emei and the other two looked at each other in bewilderment.

The details of the great battle in the Medicine King Domain had gradually spread over the years.

The number of top-tier experts involved was almost comparable to the most dangerous moments of the human-demon war.

Especially in that battle, Luo Chen's strength had been exaggerated to the extreme.

Facing the siege of three great demon emperors alone, not only did he survive, but he also personally killed Huang Man, the great demon emperor and son of the Ancient Demon Emperor, Di Tian!

Such an achievement was almost comparable to the legendary battle of Azure Frost, the number one under the Ascendant realm, who single-handedly fought three human grand cultivators.

But the result of that battle was not only Luo Chen's great victory but also his severe injuries, forcing him to flee and recuperate in a hidden place for ten years before returning.

Now, the person involved personally stated that he had fallen into that dangerous situation because of the underhanded methods of the Five Elements Divine Sect's Grand Elder?

If this got out, the reputation of the Five Elements Divine Sect's Grand Elder, who had harmed a fellow human strong expert during the human-demon war, would be completely ruined.

Even if it didn't get out, just at this moment, it was enough to unsettle the hearts of the sect's cultivators.

Seeing the atmosphere take a sharp turn for the worse, Sect Master Divine Fire could no longer sit idly by.

He said sternly, "Luo Chen, this is nothing but a trumped-up charge. You have no proof, just words. Do you have any evidence?"

"Evidence?" Luo Chen laughed.

His loud laughter spread across the land, causing the blood qi of the continuously arriving low-level disciples of the Five Elements Divine Sect to stir, their mana to become chaotic, and even their minds to become dazed.

Under the awed gazes of the four Sages, including Sect Master Divine Fire, Luo Chen slowly floated upwards, with the scene of the constantly collapsing mountain behind him.

"What need is there for evidence?"

"In our line of work, we act for the satisfaction of our own hearts!"

"The resentment your Five Elements Divine Sect has harbored against me, Luo Chen, has been building up for who knows how many years. Today, let's settle it all at once!"

As he spoke, Luo Chen's divine sense spread, and his powerful Sage domain suddenly expanded.

In an instant, it firmly suppressed the four restless Sages.

The terrifying suppressive force caused the expressions of the four to change drastically.

Especially Qi Emei and the other two newly advanced Sages. They had thought that after achieving the Sage realm, they could go anywhere in the world.

But only now did they realize that there were heavens beyond heavens, and people beyond people.

Even within the same Sage realm, there was a gap as wide as heaven and earth.

Luo Chen looked at the four people subdued under his domain, and at the vast sea of low-level cultivators who dared not even look directly at him, and found it utterly boring.

"Old thief Divine Origin, will you not show yourself yet? Must I force you by starting a massacre?"

In the midst of the deathly silence, a hoarse, sinister voice sounded.

"An external enemy has invaded, why aren't you attacking?"

An equally powerful Sage domain was released from the shattered Divine Origin Peak.

It instantly collided with Luo Chen's domain.

Boom!

Ripples bloomed in the void.

Due to this collision, Luo Chen naturally could no longer suppress the others.

Sect Master Divine Fire suddenly shouted, "Kill him! He has invaded the heart of our sect, inciting internal strife while the demon race is amassing its troops. Even if the Tianyuan Dao Sect personally investigates, we have a reason to argue!"

...

High above the nine heavens.

A flying shuttle flickered in and out of sight.

Elder Fu's expression was slightly worried. "Four Sages, and that top-tier expert, Sage Divine Origin, lurking in the shadows. Does Luo Chen really not need our help?"

Beside him, Canglong smiled faintly, his eyes full of trust in Luo Chen.

"No need. With Senior Brother Luo Chen's methods, as long as it's not a Grand Cultivator-level expert, no matter how many come, it's like a drop in the bucket."

Elder Fu glanced at Canglong, then looked down at the battlefield below, his expression quite complex.

He was both shocked by Luo Chen's formidable strength and saddened by the sudden appearance of three new Sages in the Five Elements Divine Sect, while the Luo Tian Sect had only gained one new Sage.

Why couldn't his Divine Talisman Sect have such a newly advanced strong expert!

Of course, this thought flashed by in an instant. His attention was more focused on the battle below.

Did Luo Chen really have the power to overwhelm all these people now?

...

Amidst the shouts, Sect Master Divine Fire was the first to act.

Opening his mouth, he spat out a crimson flame that shot directly towards Luo Chen.

Luo Chen frowned slightly, his divine sense still spreading, tracing upstream in an attempt to find the traces of the old thief Divine Origin.

He was completely unmoved by Sect Master Divine Fire's attack.

Playing with fire?

Before him, Luo Chen, this was asking for death!

Without any major movement, he simply grabbed the void, and the crimson flame obediently fell into his hand, swirling around his fingertips.

Not only that, as Luo Chen's finger pulled back.

Suddenly, something seemed to be drawn out of Sect Master Divine Fire's body.

"Is this your origin true flame?"

Pfft!

Spitting out a mouthful of blood, Sect Master Divine Fire's expression was filled with terror.

His origin true flame, which he had refined for centuries, how could it be broken so easily, and even directly drawn out?

It was as if the other party was the true master of his origin true flame!

How could he know that Luo Chen was a grandmaster of fire manipulation, with fire control attainments that were among the best in this world?

Moreover, having cultivated the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture*, the most top-tier fire-attributed cultivation method, he was immune to most fire-based attacks, and could even suppress them.

Most importantly, he had already comprehended the origin of fire!

Showing off fire in front of him was like wielding an axe at the door of the master carpenter Lu Ban.

As Sect Master Divine Fire made his move, the other three Sages also acted.

In response, Luo Chen only gave a contemptuous smile.

"You are all seeking death!"
