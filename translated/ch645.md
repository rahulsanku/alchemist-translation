# Chapter 645: Tears of the Jiaoren, Meeting Dao Lan Again, the Second Form of the All-Encompassing Fire Prison

In the dark seabed, violent rumbling sounds rang out from time to time.

The black turbid seawater was affected, forming waves that spread outward layer by layer from near to far.

Following the current back to the center of the great noise, two humanoid creatures were clashing at an extremely rapid frequency.

One had a human body and fish tail, held a long trident, and though his face carried baleful qi, he was incomparably handsome, like an exiled immortal from the heavens.

The other had wings on his back and wore black profound armor covered in ancient, simple patterns. As he moved freely back and forth, he continuously struck out enormous palm seals.

Outside them was a red sea of fire covering an area of one hundred zhang.

Within it, the profound-armored man frequently erupted with extreme speed, shifting through the void. All kinds of spells came readily to hand, and after they were released, their might rose by several degrees out of nowhere, suppressing the merman opposite him until he could almost not breathe.

Suddenly, the merman released the trident and let it float beside him, then blinked at the profound-armored man.

A drop of azure tear fell from his left eye.

As soon as the blue tear fell, he struck it toward the profound-armored man.

After releasing the tear, his aura abruptly weakened to the extreme, and blood even flowed from his eyes.

Facing that blue tear, the profound-armored man treated it like a great enemy. As he retreated frantically, he continuously struck out azure great hand seals.

Boom! Boom! Boom!

Three palms in a row forcefully shattered the blue tear into starry fragments filling the sky.

Only then did the profound-armored man finally let out a breath of relief.

However, in the next instant, the blue star fragments filling the sky erupted with blue light inside the fire sea, forming an enormous bubble that completely wrapped around the profound-armored man.

Seeing this, joy appeared on the merman's face.

Without another word, he reached out and summoned the blue trident, sweeping it repeatedly through the empty seabed. Blue chains extended from inside the bubble and shot toward the profound-armored man.

One could imagine that once those blue chains struck, the man's movements would certainly be obstructed.

At this critical moment, the profound-armored man remained calm. He gave a cold snort and pointed at his own qi sea.

The next moment.

Azure flames spread from his body and completely wrapped him.

At this time, within that grim fire prison, the man seemed to transform into a demon god wreathed in flames. Facing the blue chains flying toward him from all directions, he merely extended his hand and fiercely grabbed them.

He pulled!

Clatter!

As if drawn by force, the merman outside the bubble was forced to fly toward the man.

He tried to resist, mobilizing his demonic power and struggling constantly.

However, the surging domineering mana around him made his demonic power leap actively, yet it was completely outside his control.

Under his terrified gaze, the profound-armored man's azure flames blazed. In an instant, they burned away the blue bubble. Then he stamped the void, and with an explosive sound, appeared before the merman as if teleporting.

A broad hand gripped his slender throat.

"You are not a mermaid. The intelligence was wrong."

The man spoke coldly.

The demon beast whose throat was gripped flushed red. His face became incomparably ferocious, no longer showing his former handsome appearance. He seemed to roar, "How could I be a human plaything? I am a jiaoren!"

A jiaoren?

Then that was right.

Luo Chen nodded faintly. No wonder the opponent's battle strength was outstanding and his demonic arts were strange and unpredictable. Luo Chen had spent enormous effort, even releasing the second form of the All-Encompassing Fire Prison developed over these days, before taking him down.

However, it ended here.

He exerted slight force, choking the jiaoren until he nearly lost breath.

At the same time, the azure flames around Luo Chen seemed alive as they flowed from his fingertips over the jiaoren's entire body.

In an instant, a strange fragrance began to spread through the turbid, dark bottom of the Sunken Sea.

Luo Chen slowly closed his eyes, feeling majestic vitality flow into his body, so comfortable that he almost groaned.

How long had it been since he used his origin true fire to burn-refine demon beasts?

Especially this time, it was a late third-tier Great Demon King.

Under the burning refinement of his Withering Glory True Fire, unending vitality fused into his body, making Luo Chen almost tremble from comfort.

Opposite him, the jiaoren, who had only been six or seven chi tall, had violently trembled and now was nothing but skin and bones, no longer possessing his earlier handsome state.

A demon core, like a pearl, continuously struggled inside his body, but could not escape the azure flames.

An invisible roar of pain came from the demon core.

"When my race's Flow Lord escapes from Arctic Yemo Heaven, you will all sink forever!"

Luo Chen seemed not to hear. He merely continued burning the opponent's body with origin true fire, leaving only one piece of flesh untouched.

Around the time it took to drink a cup of tea later, Luo Chen loosened his hand.

The human-shaped charcoal transformed into ash filling the sky and fell to the seabed.

Luo Chen beckoned, and that piece of fresh flesh, along with the blue trident, fell into his hand. He directly placed them inside the Ink Ring.

Only then did he finally turn around.

Looking toward the place where the angry tide did not cease, he spoke faintly, "Fellow Daoist, you have watched for a long time. Have you seen enough?"

With an enormous saber light opening the way, a tall, stalwart man stepped out from within.

Luo Chen's eyes focused. "So it is Fellow Daoist Dao Lan. Luo pays his respects."

Pays respects?

What respect?

Dao Lan snorted softly. Did Luo Chen truly think Dao Lan could not see his guarded posture? Even the azure flames around him had not dispersed. He was completely acting as though facing a great enemy.

Dao Lan did not expose his thoughts. His gaze fell on the seabed mountains already submerged by rolling black turbid seawater.

Broken peaks and ruined cliffs, water and fire clashing, even a basin forcefully blasted into the seabed.

Dao Lan's pupils shrank slightly. "Devil Lord, fine methods!"

"Oh?" Luo Chen raised a brow.

Dao Lan, who had always been taciturn, seemed to become talkative at this moment.

"Among the Northern Sea's many demon races, the jiaoren count as an extremely strong race. Especially after advancing to third tier, they awaken their lifebound demonic art, Blue Sea Tear. This art cannot be used lightly unless they are at a moment of life or death. But once it is deployed, its force is like a thousand jun. One struck by it is as if struck by an entire ocean. Even human Sages who cultivate water-attribute techniques often collect Blue Sea Tears condensed by jiaoren demonic arts to craft great killing moves. Devil Lord, for you to easily block this move shows how extraordinary you are."

Was it the tear the jiaoren had dripped from the corner of his eye at the brink of life and death?

Luo Chen was thoughtful.

Suddenly, Dao Lan changed the topic.

"However, Devil Lord Azure Yang, you have gone a little too far. Along the way, you have already snatched five whetstones from me, causing me much distress!"

Luo Chen narrowed his eyes. "Yours? I have not seen that."

An invisible force clashed fiercely between the two.

Dao Lan looked eager to try, and the golden saber in his hand emitted tremors.

Luo Chen's face was cold, while azure flames rose and fell endlessly around him.

Suddenly, Luo Chen seemed to remember something and withdrew all his origin true fire.

Seeing this, Dao Lan was startled.

"Devil Lord, you..."

"Dao Lan, do you know a wandering cultivator named Ding Yi?"

Dao Lan's expression changed slightly, and the battle intent around him faded by a few degrees.

Seeing this, Luo Chen could not help smiling.

As expected, he had known Ding Yi's inside contact at the Three Oceans defensive line was Dao Lan.

Ding Yi knew the intelligence on the Sunken Sea like the back of his hand, and every time said it had been tortured out of Primordial Demon Sect disciples.

But some later intelligence was clearly recent information from these years.

Moreover, ordinary low-rank cultivators of the Three Oceans defensive line could not come into contact with that intelligence at all. Only garrison cultivators could learn a little.

But only a little.

To reach Ding Yi's level of comprehensiveness, only a powerful expert stationed at the Three Oceans defensive line long-term could know it thoroughly.

Looking across the garrison cultivators of the three defensive lines, only Dao Lan, this battle fanatic, would stay at the three defensive lines for long periods and actively search for corresponding movements of demon beasts everywhere.

A friend's friend could barely count as a friend.

In this dangerous place, why fight a great battle?

Shaking his head, Luo Chen walked straight toward Dao Lan.

The other party tightened his grip on the saber hilt. Yet until Luo Chen brushed past him, he ultimately did not make a move and did not use Luo Chen to sharpen his saber.

As they passed, Luo Chen said softly, "Do not worry. I will not touch the other Great Demon Kings for now. These fellows are more troublesome than the last. If you are interested, go yourself."

His voice still lingered by the ear, but when Dao Lan thought further on it, the man had already completely gone far away.

Dao Lan's expression shifted uncertainly. With his speed, if he caught up now, he might be able to fight Luo Chen. But thinking of the other party's methods, especially that strange force field covering one hundred zhang, he had no absolute confidence of victory.

"Forget it. This whetstone is too hard. It would be bad if it chipped my blade. Besides, he knows Ding Yi."

Perhaps Luo Chen's final words had moved him, or perhaps Dao Lan truly feared the other party.

But in the next instant, Dao Lan suddenly reacted.

"Scorchflame Sawtooth!"

Inside Taiping Fortress, in an ordinary second-tier cave residence, Luo Chen reflected on the five battles he had fought over the last few months.

The battle against the Sea-Overturning Demon Jiao, the battle against the Scorchflame Sawtooth Shark, the battle against the Heavenstar Ox, the battle against the Blood Lantern, and finally the battle against the jiaoren.

He had been somewhat arrogant in the battle with the demon jiao and had been forced to reveal the true body of the Heavenly Peng before taking it down.

The later battles were dangerous, but all within expectations.

The battle against the Heavenstar Ox had been somewhat unexpected.

That demon's mana was so profound it simply exceeded common sense. In Luo Chen's view, even a ninth-level Golden Core cultivator would be hard-pressed to compare.

Although the All-Encompassing Fire Prison had a certain suppressive effect, it could not completely finish the job.

It was also in that battle that Luo Chen comprehended a move on the spot, activating the Withering Glory True Fire and empowering his body with it. Added to the amplifying effect of the All-Encompassing Fire Prison, his attack power multiplied.

Not only that, within the All-Encompassing Fire Prison, he gained the prestige of a ruler.

Luo Chen called this move the second form of the All-Encompassing Fire Prison: the True Fire Form.

The Blood Lantern had been relatively simple. That demon was skilled at corroding enemy flesh, but under the Black Dust Armor's all-around protection, it posed no threat to Luo Chen at all.

Especially because the Blood Lantern's soul was extremely weak. After Luo Chen discovered that this demon was of no use for testing his sword, he used one move of Mirror Flower Water Moon to disturb its mind and one move of the All-Encompassing Fire Prison to suppress its mana, easily killing it.

As for this final jiaoren...

With a thought, Luo Chen took out that piece of flesh.

He injected mana into it, and with a clatter, a pile of miscellaneous items appeared on the ground.

There were coral sea horns, shell gauze, several artifacts and scripture books, and even a fist-sized, incomparably azure spirit stone.

At the first moment, Luo Chen's gaze was drawn by that spirit stone.

"Such terrifying spiritual qi concentration. Is this a supreme-grade spirit stone?"

Luo Chen was incomparably surprised. He grasped the blue crystal, and in his perception, it contained surging water-attribute spiritual qi.

Clearly, this was a water-element supreme-grade spirit stone.

Ordinary spirit stones, whether high-grade or low-grade, were not really divided by attribute and were relatively balanced.

Only supreme-grade spirit stones were divided by five elements, yin, and yang.

No, that was not quite right. It should be said that when a certain attribute was too extreme and filled the crystal ore, it created the so-called supreme-grade spirit stone.

"A pity it is not fire-attribute."

Luo Chen sighed, but still solemnly put it into the Ink Ring.

This one stone alone was worth no less than one million low-grade spirit stones.

Looking at the other miscellaneous items now, he suddenly felt them dull.

Even so, Luo Chen patiently inspected them one by one.

"Hm? These gauzes... are they jiaoren-woven jiaoxiao gauze?"

This was a rather fine material.

Jiaoxiao gauze was rarely seen in the world and had extremely strong restraining effects against baleful qi and filthy objects. Whether used to make robes or evil-warding items, it had marvelous uses.

His gaze fell on the artifacts and magic treasures. Luo Chen originally did not care much, but when he saw the characters carved on them, he stopped moving.

"Weapons of Primordial Demon Sect disciples?"

He pursed his lips, and a faint sorrow arose in his heart.

A former Ascendant Holy Land had now collapsed, its mountain gate ruined and its glory scattered. Weapons once used to slaughter in every direction had become trophies of demon beasts.

Thinking about it now, that blue trident possessed enormous might and exquisite workmanship. It clearly was not something the demon race could refine.

That upper-grade magic treasure was obviously also a weapon of a Primordial Demon Sect disciple.

With a sigh, Luo Chen sorted these things by category and stored them in the Ink Ring.

For a time, he felt somewhat listless.

"After several months of testing my sword, I have thoroughly grasped the advantages and disadvantages of the All-Encompassing Fire Prison. With this art's support, I can already fight and defeat Great Demon Kings that survived the Primordial Demon Sect battle. If replaced with late Golden Core human cultivators, the difference should not be large."

"In fact, because immortal cultivators rely even more on mana, the effect of this killing move will be even better!"

"Then next..."

Luo Chen intended to return, but when he sensed the majestic vitality his body was currently digesting, he hesitated.

This was a fine opportunity to openly hunt demon beasts, and at critical moments he could retreat back to Taiping Fortress.

If he could use origin true fire to burn-refine and devour the vitality of large numbers of demon beasts, perhaps he could push his physique up another step.

With a thought, the attribute panel appeared.

His gaze stopped on the realm column.

Ancient Wilderness Third Tier: 91/100.

"If nothing unexpected happens, at my current speed, I will need at least another ten-plus years to push it to Great Perfection, or even advance to Ancient Wilderness Fourth Tier."

"But if there is massive vitality to devour, that speed may increase greatly."

"And once I advance to Ancient Wilderness Fourth Tier, a qualitative change may occur. In that way, when I later join Ding Yi and the others to explore the ancient cultivator ruins of the Sunken Sea, I will have one more reliance."

After once again tasting the sweetness of the vitality fed back by origin true fire, Luo Chen was restless.

Suddenly, Luo Chen's divine sense moved. He reached outside the cave residence, and a transmission talisman flew in.

"Dao Lan?"

"Why has he come looking again?"

Luo Chen frowned, but he did not fear the other party. He strode out of the cave residence.

"You want the teeth of the Scorchflame Sawtooth Shark?"

"Correct. This item is of great use to me. I hope Fellow Daoist will part with it."

Now he called him Fellow Daoist, not Devil Lord?

Luo Chen laughed softly, then his expression became serious.

"I can trade this item to you, but..."

In the restaurant, Dao Lan's expression was expectant. "If you have any conditions, say them."

"Simple. Regarding my methods, you may not reveal even half a word to Ding Yi."

Dao Lan hesitated, but since this concerned his own cultivation, he still agreed.

Luo Chen looked around. "Let us change locations. This place is too narrow. There is no open ground for me to take out the demon shark corpse."

Dao Lan's spirit rose.

"Follow me!"

Not long after, under Dao Lan's lead, Luo Chen arrived at an open square in the fortress.

Several Foundation Establishment cultivators followed nearby.

Looking at the old black bloodstains on the square ground, Luo Chen realized something.

Dao Lan also introduced it from the side. "This is one of the places where wandering cultivators trade hunted demon beasts to the Demon Hunter Office. It is specially used to lay out the corpses of large demon beasts. These people beside me are appraisers responsible for determining demon beast rank and related materials."

Under Luo Chen's gaze, those Foundation Establishment cultivators puffed up their chests.

"Adept, rest assured. We are experienced and have seen the corpses of all kinds of powerful demon beasts. We absolutely will not misjudge the price and cause you to suffer losses."

Was that so?

Luo Chen smiled faintly, and the black iron ring on his left hand bloomed with light.

Thud! Thud! Thud! Thud!

Four enormous sounds rang out in succession. The next moment, four huge demon beast corpses appeared on the square.

A demon jiao, an azure ox, a blood-colored lantern, and a ferocious giant shark.

Looking at the four mountain-like demon beast corpses, those appraisers were dumbfounded.

Dao Lan, even though he had expected something in his heart, could not help inhaling slightly when he truly saw this scene.

These were not nameless nobodies.

Especially the Sea-Overturning Demon Jiao. Its fierce reputation was entirely no lower than the Golden-Horned Silver Rhinoceros he had previously exhausted his mind to kill.

The other three were only slightly weaker.

This Devil Lord Azure Yang was truly formidable.

At the time in the Sunken Sea, Dao Lan had intended to fight him. Thinking back now, that had been somewhat reckless.

Dao Lan was battle-loving, but he was not an idiot.

For a time, gazes of dread, awe, and even fear fell on Luo Chen from the eyes of the few people.

Luo Chen seemed not to notice. He merely waved his hand.

"Begin. Finish this earlier. I still need to go to the front line."

Dao Lan's eyes widened.

Had they not agreed he would not go? Why was he going back on his word?

*(End of Chapter)*
