# Chapter 829: An Old Friend Discusses the Sword, Overbearing Conduct

“Greetings, Elder!”

“Greetings, Elder!”

Amidst a flurry of greetings, Heartseverance Fairy walked alone to the cliffside cold pine tree.

Standing with her hands behind her back on the pure white snow, the woman revealed a complex expression rarely seen on her face in ordinary times.

Crisp footsteps sounded behind her. Without turning her head, she asked, “Xiaolan, when did you arrive?”

A young woman dressed in plain white robes, untouched by any powder or makeup, walked up behind her and replied respectfully, “I only arrived today. My three-year mourning period for my husband is complete, so it’s time I came out to do something for the Luo Tian Sect.”

“They… have they treated you well?”

Heartseverance Fairy hesitated slightly. The “they” she referred to were the cultivators on the mountain gate side of the Luo Tian Sect.

Ever since deciding to join the Luo Tian Sect, the surviving cultivators of the Icecastle lineage had all visited Bai Li Qingchuan at least once. She alone had never set foot on Daqing Mountain.

What subtle thoughts lay behind this was hard for ordinary people to fathom.

Shi Lan answered without hesitation, “They’ve treated us well enough. I’d say they treat us equally. Perhaps it’s because of my husband’s former connections, or maybe the Supreme Elder gave secret instructions. In any case, my sisters don’t feel much like we’re living under someone else’s roof over there.”

“Is that so… good, very good.”

Heartseverance Fairy murmured. Her feelings were extremely complicated regarding how smoothly the Icecastle sisters had integrated into the Luo Tian Sect, but in the end, relief prevailed.

She had taken it upon herself to violate the ancestral teachings and lead her disciples to switch allegiances. If she had entrusted them to the wrong person, she would truly be unable to atone for that sin even in death.

Shi Lan looked at the woman’s slender back and hesitated. “And you, Master? What about you?”

Heartseverance Fairy said calmly, “Don’t worry about me. You’ve dealt with him before too; you know his ways. As long as I don’t cross any lines, there won’t be any major problems. In fact…”

As she spoke, a flicker of magical power rippled across Heartseverance Fairy’s body. The snow beneath her feet crackled as ice crystals formed.

Seeing this, Shi Lan couldn’t help covering her mouth in surprise, her face showing astonishment.

She too cultivated the Icecastle’s core technique, the *Ten Directions Freezing Extreme Dao*, so she naturally understood what this phenomenon meant.

Her master’s long-stagnant cultivation realm had finally broken through!

Now, her master was a late-stage Adept Grand Cultivator!

This alone was enough to show how much Luo Chen valued her master.

And indeed, that was the case!

To outsiders, it seemed degrading that Heartseverance Fairy had placed herself under Luo Chen’s command, playing the lowly role of receiving and seeing off guests.

But the truth was that Heartseverance Fairy was one of the few people who could frequently see Luo Chen in person. Even her cultivation cave was a top-grade third-rank cave dwelling within this Danluo Mountain.

In her spare time, she could often receive pills bestowed by Luo Chen.

Her natural talent wasn’t bad to begin with, so stepping into late-stage Adept was only natural.

Shi Lan slowly lowered her hand and sighed emotionally. “Now we’ve all settled down. With the Supreme Elder’s protection, we no longer have to live in constant fear like before. If only Senior Sister Canglong could wake up, that would be even better.”

Senior Sister Canglong?

Hearing that name, Heartseverance Fairy’s lips curled up slightly, a rare sight.

She knew that the magic treasure Luo Chen had been refining recently was specifically meant to resolve Senior Sister Canglong’s injuries.

If it succeeded, there would come a day when her senior sister would finally awaken.

Perhaps she herself would walk ahead of her senior sister and form her Nascent Soul first. She wondered what expression her senior sister would have then?

Just as Heartseverance Fairy was in a good mood, she suddenly felt a chill of terror. The solid ice beneath her feet exploded violently.

She whirled around, staring at the great hall where Luo Chen had entered earlier.

A powerful wave of magical force rippled out from the hall like ripples on water. Wherever it passed, green pines snapped and white snow exploded into powder.

But soon, an even stronger force forcibly suppressed the aftershocks of the magical collision, preventing them from harming any of the Luo Tian Sect cultivators on duty.

What truly made Heartseverance Fairy’s blood run cold, however, were two beams of light—one cyan, one red.

“Those are… sword qi!”

A cyan and a red sword qi pierced through the hall’s dome, shooting straight into the high sky.

The sound of sword humming lingered for a long time.

Without a second thought, Heartseverance Fairy flew toward the great hall. Other Luo Tian Sect cultivators made the same move.

But just as they reached the hall’s entrance, Luo Chen’s gentle voice came from inside.

“It’s nothing. Just a small sparring session. Withdraw!”

“As you command!”

Heartseverance Fairy took a deep breath, casting a reverent glance at the deep, shadowy hall before leading the Luo Tian Sect disciples to retreat slowly.

…

Inside the hall.

Luo Chen slowly withdrew his joined index and middle fingers, a look of admiration on his face.

“Truly a startling sight in the sword world. I am far inferior!”

Lin Qingxuan withdrew his right hand, tucking it into his sleeve, his expression calm.

“The Pill Sovereign flatters me. You don’t primarily cultivate the sword path. To have achieved such pure and refined sword qi after only a few years of immersion is rare in this world. And that technique you used to suppress the aftershocks of the magical collision—that was true skill!”

One word, another word, and the originally tense atmosphere immediately softened.

Hall Master Li, sitting in the middle, laughed. “One excels in swordsmanship, the other in profound magical power. For little Jade Cauldron Domain to produce two peerless geniuses like you is truly a blessed land.”

“I dare not be ranked alongside the Pill Sovereign,” Lin Qingxuan said, lowering his posture.

It seemed to remind Luo Chen of the old days when they first met after forming their cores and rushed together to open up the battlefield. His mood improved greatly.

He laughed heartily, tapping the table with a bent finger.

“Since you’ve satisfied my wish, let’s discuss the cooperation on the Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid!”

At these words, Hall Master Li’s spirits lifted, and Lin Qingxuan blinked, also showing a hint of anticipation.

Luo Chen cleared his throat and smiled. “We discussed the cooperation details before. The Ten Thousand Sword Pavilion and the Luo Tian Sect will be responsible for collecting the corresponding raw materials, with costs split evenly. If the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect wants to participate, you’ll need to contribute some spirit stones.”

Lin Qingxuan nodded. “That’s only right!”

“I want fifty percent of the Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid produced! You can split the remaining half among yourselves.”

“This…” Lin Qingxuan hesitated.

“Pill Sovereign, that’s not what we agreed on.” Hall Master Li’s expression changed drastically, showing considerable dissatisfaction.

“We hadn’t finalized anything before. After all, I didn’t know how difficult the refinement would be back then.” Luo Chen narrowed his eyes, looking at the two with a half-smile. “Of course, if you don’t want to cooperate, I won’t force it.”

Didn’t know how difficult the refinement would be?

More like he didn’t know how good the Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid’s effects were back then!

Hall Master Li stared at Luo Chen’s amber right eye, anger simmering within. “We provided the pill formula. We provided most of the materials. And you want to take half with just one sentence? Isn’t that a bit excessive?”

Luo Chen rubbed his smooth chin. “Hearing you say that, it does seem a bit excessive.”

Lin Qingxuan and Hall Master Li both relaxed slightly when they saw him relent.

Business was like this—bargaining was the essence of business. One-shot deals didn’t last long.

Luo Chen thought for a moment and then said, “How about this? I’ll make a concession. Sixty percent goes to you, and I’ll take forty percent.”

Sixty-forty split?

Hall Master Li considered this, then looked at Lin Qingxuan.

Lin Qingxuan sighed. “I have no objections.”

Hall Master Li broke into a smile. “Alright, sixty-forty it is!”

*Thump!*

The table was tapped again, and Luo Chen showed a sincere, cheerful expression.

“Then let’s discuss my compensation for refining the pills!”

Pill trading was a long-term affair, but having Pill Sovereign Luo Chen personally refine the pills was not free, nor could it be settled by just giving up an extra ten percent share of the pills.

Especially since part of the raw material purchasing channels relied on Luo Chen’s connections to buy from the Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance.

Since he had dared to ask for fifty percent earlier, he must have had the confidence.

Hall Master Li also knew this. “We’ve prepared a considerable amount of Xiao Qi for you, which is what you need. Additionally, we have some decent pill formulas and body-tempering techniques…”

Clearly, Hall Master Li had made a point of inquiring about Luo Chen’s preferences to cater to them.

However, Luo Chen waved his hand.

“Sorry, I currently don’t lack Xiao Qi anymore. As for those low-grade pill formulas and body-tempering methods, heh.”

Hall Master Li frowned, then immediately thought of the situation at the Shouyang Dao Discussion.

It must have been then that Luo Chen collected enough Xiao Qi, so he no longer valued the small amount in Hall Master Li’s hands.

“Then what do you want?”

“Don’t worry, I won’t make exorbitant demands. I’m asking for what you have in abundance.” Luo Chen’s gaze swept over the two, and he spoke slowly. “Sword pellets! Wood, water, fire, and earth—four types of sword pellets at the true artifact level!”

Lin Qingxuan refused flatly. “Impossible!”

Hall Master Li shook his head. “We don’t have any!”

Luo Chen’s smile faded. “Truly impossible? Truly none?”

His gaze slowly swept over the two, as heavy as a mountain, bringing immense pressure.

“I already made concessions earlier. Now you respond like this—it’s really quite disrespectful, isn’t it?”

Lin Qingxuan opened his mouth, then lowered his head. “Fellow Daoist, you’re forcing the issue. Sword pellets are the condensation of a sword cultivator’s entire Dao attainment. Often, the sword is where the person is, and when the sword dies, the person dies. Our Jade Cauldron Sword Sect has only produced two Sage in total. Where would we have spare sword pellets? Unless you want me to give you my lifebound true artifact?”

Luo Chen nodded in understanding. “So that’s how it is. Then I suppose I am forcing the issue? Hall Master Li, is it the same for your Ten Thousand Sword Pavilion?”

Hall Master Li instinctively wanted to nod, but under that gaze, the words wouldn’t come out as a lie.

He could only explain, “My Ten Thousand Sword Pavilion does indeed have spare true-artifact-level sword pellets, but they were left by our ancestors and contain their will. Only those who cultivate our sect’s techniques can gain their recognition and use them as inherited treasures. The Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid is something I sought for personal reasons. It’s already excessive to use the sect’s manpower and resources. I cannot further deplete the sect’s interests.”

“Both have reasons, both have difficulties!” Luo Chen clicked his tongue, seeming to understand them well. But the words he spoke next plunged the hall’s atmosphere to freezing point. “Then I see no need to proceed with this cooperation.”

Silence. Stillness.

Lin and Li looked at each other, not knowing how to respond.

Luo Chen closed his eyes, resting in silence, giving them enough time to think.

Snowflakes drifted down through the previously pierced dome, adding another layer of chill to the hall.

After a long time, the heavy silence in the hall was finally broken.

“Fellow Daoist Luo, our Jade Cauldron Sword Sect has always had shallow foundations. After suffering great upheaval, we truly lack the strength. I cannot produce a true-artifact-level sword pellet. What I can offer is a single flawless sword pellet at the superior-grade magic treasure level, of the fire element. If you take it and nurture it well, elevating it to the true artifact level would only be a matter of enduring one thunder tribulation.”

As Lin Qingxuan spoke, his gaze swept over Hong Ling with an apologetic look.

Hong Ling bit her lip and nodded calmly.

Luo Chen opened his eyes. “I see your sincerity. Hall Master Li, what about you?”

Hall Master Li’s double pupils showed reluctance. “I can offer one wood-element true-artifact sword pellet with only my soul imprint inside, which can be removed instantly. Additionally, I can give two flawless sword pellets of water and earth elements, also at the superior-grade magic treasure level. Pill Sovereign, this is the limit of my Ten Thousand Sword Pavilion.”

One true-artifact sword pellet, three magic-treasure sword pellets.

This answer left Luo Chen somewhat dissatisfied.

But clearly, this was their limit.

After all, flawless sword pellets weren’t nurtured by individual cultivators. They were forcibly refined from excellent materials, specifically used to reward talented juniors or as personal backups in critical moments.

Just like the platinum sword pellet Luo Chen had seized from Feiyunzi—it was a flawless sword pellet, but Feiyunzi’s superb refining skills had forcibly pushed it to the true artifact level.

Under Lin and Li’s watchful eyes, Luo Chen pondered for a long time before finally tapping the table slowly.

“Acceptable.”

With that, the cooperation was finalized.

To ease the stiff atmosphere from the bargaining, Luo Chen proactively changed the subject, asking about the current situation of the human-demon war.

The two catered to him, giving a general overview.

Without holding a banquet, Luo Chen handed over three bottles of Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid, and they took their leave.

…

Three beams of escaping light flew away from Danluo Mountain, moving at a leisurely pace.

Lin Qingxuan said guiltily, “Sorry, Hong Ling. I gave away the flawless sword pellet I had prepared for you.”

Hong Ling forced a smile. “It’s fine. The sword pellet I’ve been nurturing myself is already sufficient. Whether I can successfully advance to Sage in the future is still uncertain. Improving your strength is more important, Senior Brother. With the Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid, you can make up for your deficiencies.”

Lin Qingxuan reached out and gently rubbed the woman’s head, letting out a soft sigh.

“The Pill Sovereign’s conduct grows more overbearing by the day!”

“Senior Brother, your hand?” Hong Ling caught a glimpse of a bloodstain when he withdrew his hand.

Faint, thin, yet startling.

The woman couldn’t believe it. “Were you injured during the sparring earlier? But clearly, you won in the sword discussion!”

Lin Qingxuan’s right hand trembled slightly as he tucked it back into his sleeve.

“I won in the discussion of the Dao, but in the sword…”

Hall Master Li said quietly from the side, “During the battle at Jueling, the Pill Sovereign’s demon sword was no ordinary item. Now he’s personally asking for sword pellets—it must be for that demon sword!”

As he spoke, Hall Master Li showed some dissatisfaction.

In his view, the reason Luo Chen had dared to make such exorbitant demands was partly because Lin Qingxuan had been at a disadvantage in the sword contest.

On the surface, Luo Chen was slightly inferior, but that was because he had only recently begun practicing the sword.

In reality, Lin Qingxuan was injured—this didn’t escape Hall Master Li’s notice.

Lin Qingxuan shook his head. “It’s not just that. In that instant of magical collision, I sensed a huge gap between myself and Luo Chen. He…”

The young man in the green robe showed a grave expression, as if he couldn’t believe the glimpse of Luo Chen’s vast, hidden magical power beneath the surface.

Hall Master Li hadn’t personally crossed hands with him, so he didn’t feel it as deeply. He simply assumed Lin Qingxuan was exaggerating Luo Chen’s strength to cover up his earlier defeat.

“As agreed, sixty percent of the Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid. I’ll take forty percent, you take twenty percent. Here!”

A jade bottle was tossed into Lin Qingxuan’s hands.

Then the two parties went their separate ways.

Lin Qingxuan looked at the solitary jade bottle in his hand. His other hand clenched involuntarily, veins bulging.

He had given up the pill formula, given up spirit stones, even given away the sword pellet prepared for his junior sister—and in the end, this was all he got?

“Senior Brother…” Hong Ling looked at the man with concern.

The young man, whose temples were gradually growing white hairs, pressed his lips together. “Let’s go back.”

(End of chapter)
