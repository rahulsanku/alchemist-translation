# Chapter 873: One Talisman Suppresses the Sea, Carrying Mountains and Chasing the Moon

"Who does he look like?"

On the *Heavenly River Void-Crossing Ship*, in a quiet room, Luo Chen casually sketched on a sheet of white rice paper.

Sima Huiniang stood beside him, her delicate brows slightly furrowed as she gazed at the gradually emerging outline.

Wrinkles crisscrossed like spreading roots.

Dry and withered, like a decaying piece of rotten wood.

His originally kind and gentle face had been rendered gloomy and sinister by the dark, fierce eyes Luo Chen had added as the finishing touch.

Faced with Luo Chen's question, Sima Huiniang hesitated for a moment, then parted her red lips.

"He does feel somewhat familiar. He looks a bit like a senior from the Five Elements Divine Sect who used to take the initiative to look after us."

Luo Chen put down his brush and looked at Sima Huiniang's profile as she pondered deeply.

Suddenly, her eyes lit up.

"That's right! This face is indeed sixty or seventy percent similar to Pure Wood Sage!"

"So it really is him?"

Luo Chen murmured to himself.

After he had privately exchanged words with Elder Fu, that was exactly what the other party had said.

Before Sima Huiniang had stepped down as the Luo Tian Sect Leader, she had had considerable dealings with the Five Elements Divine Sect, and naturally, she had also met that kind-natured Pure Wood Sage, who was known as a good-natured man.

Now, even she thought so!

Hearing Luo Chen's words, Sima Huiniang was somewhat puzzled.

"Husband, logically speaking, when you returned, Pure Wood Sage's physical body had been destroyed by the Demon Emperor, and his Sage infant was in seclusion healing. You shouldn't have seen him, right? Why are you suddenly drawing his portrait for no reason?"

Why?

Luo Chen wanted to ask that too.

For a cultivator who had not yet achieved the Ascendant realm and condensed their primordial spirit, having their physical body destroyed was a severe injury second only to damage to the Sage infant!

But such injuries had ways to be remedied.

One could seek suitable materials to reshape the physical body, or resort to the most despicable method of seizing a body.

In any case, no matter what, a Sage cultivator would handle this matter as quickly as possible.

Yet Pure Wood Sage had been healing his injuries for decades, never showing his face in public.

Even when the battle at Lingtian Pass was at its most dangerous, even when Golden Feather Sage of the Five Elements Divine Sect died in battle, he never appeared.

Was he really that patient?

As it seemed now, it probably wasn't a matter of Pure Wood Sage being patient, but rather that something unexpected had happened to him.

And this accident was most likely at the hands of his senior brother, Divine Origin Sage!

Otherwise, there was no explanation for why Divine Origin Sage would reveal such a true appearance.

"Fusion and assimilation? Absorption and devouring?"

Luo Chen didn't know exactly what method Divine Origin Sage had used, but he knew that this man was far from the kind person he had appeared to be before leaving.

After all, could someone who would harm even his own fellow disciple's Sage infant be considered kind?

Add to that the malice he had shown towards Luo Chen earlier...

Thinking about it, Luo Chen suddenly shook his head and smiled.

Forget it!

Once he had finished dealing with the loose ends here, he would be far away at the Nether Origin Sect. That old man Divine Origin Sage, who probably didn't have many years left to live, would likely have no further dealings with him.

Why think so much about it?

From his suddenly changed attitude, it was clear that he was also afraid of the Nether Origin Sect, which was why he had voluntarily wiped the slate clean of past grievances.

With a flick of his finger, a small fireball shot out, and the portrait was instantly burned to ashes.

Luo Chen turned and walked out. Huiniang followed beside him, casually chatting about the recent expansion matters.

Mostly Huiniang spoke, Luo Chen listened, occasionally offering a bit of advice.

"The Motian Domain has a large fourth-order spirit vein, stretching over three thousand li. This area has already been cleared by Wang Yuan and his people, with no more demon beasts lingering. Currently, disciples of the Tiandu Sect are clearing blockages in the spirit vein and setting up formations to activate the spirit points."

"In the future, the three-thousand-li area centered on Motian Cliff will be our sect's main mountain gate."

"According to the suggestions of Elder Fu and Splendid Cloud Fairy, the territory of a Sage Upper Sect must be at least ten thousand li in circumference. Even if we can't use it all now, the standards cannot be lowered."

Luo Chen nodded. A three-thousand-li sect core and a ten-thousand-li territory—this was indeed the standard specification for a Sage Upper Sect.

Only with such foundation could they support the cultivation of tens of thousands, or even hundreds of thousands, of disciples that a future generation Sage Upper Sect would inevitably have.

As for the grand feat of dominating an entire domain like the Medicine King Sect, it required not only formidable strength but also immense financial resources.

Otherwise, one simply couldn't control such a vast territory. It would be better to leave it to other smaller forces to manage.

As for whether the Sage Upper Sect would lose too much profit in this process?

There was another solution for that.

That was to jointly build immortal cities with the various small forces. The Sage Upper Sect would hold the largest share in each immortal city, and the subsequent profits from the cities would naturally be mostly taken by the Sage Upper Sect.

Accumulated in this way, the resources a Sage Upper Sect could obtain were terrifying.

As long as subsequent development wasn't too foolish, they would never be weaker than those Golden Core Great Sects.

Thus, over the past three thousand years, most Sage Upper Sects that managed to open up a domain grew stronger and stronger, with very few falling from the ranks of Upper Sects.

Even if, within the territories under their command, those Golden Core Great Sects produced geniuses like Divine Origin Sage, Jade Cauldron Sage, or Han Zhan, they couldn't challenge the local Sage Upper Sect in the short term. They could only seek external development under the constraints of the rules, venturing out for new expansion wars.

"Additionally, some resource-rich areas within the Motian Domain are being surveyed one by one by Elder Lingfengzi and his team."

"Other small forces are pushing forward frantically, sweeping away the remaining demon beasts in the Luo Tian Domain."

"However, there is no peace among them. To occupy low-level spirit veins with abundant spiritual energy, they often fight fiercely."

"Now, it's different from the time of the great human-demon war. No longer bound by the rules of Lingtian Pass, in just a few days, several factions have fought with real fire, suffering considerable casualties."

Huiniang said this, a hint of emotion in her eyes.

She too had once experienced the expansion war in the Xiaoyue Mountain Range back then.

Back then, the opponents of the Golden Core forces were demon beasts.

Even if several factions set their sights on the same good spirit land, they each relied on their own abilities to take that mountain.

Not like now. With few powerful demon beasts remaining in the Luo Tian Domain, whenever an ownerless spirit land was discovered, it turned into internal human strife and sect wars.

"Although they seem like small skirmishes to us, this is still a great era for our Luo Tian Upper Sect to open up a domain. It looks a bit bad."

"Husband, should we intervene?"

Luo Chen was about to say no, but stopped himself.

After a moment of thought, he nodded.

"Once Wang Yuan has some free time, let him take some people to mediate!"

"Also, bring Min Longyu along. When they encounter slightly larger third-order spirit veins, let him assess them and divide them among two, or even three, families to share."

Sima Huiniang initially didn't grasp the meaning behind Luo Chen's words as she listened.

But when she heard the phrases "two, or even three, families to share," she instantly understood her husband's intentions.

"Husband, your plan is quite venomous!"

"Hmm?" Luo Chen looked at her with a gentle smile.

Sima Huiniang pursed her lips and smiled, "But it's very good for the future development of our Luo Tian Sect!"

The two exchanged a knowing smile, both fully aware of the poison hidden within this seemingly peaceful and friendly mediation.

The Golden Core forces currently participating in the war had, after over a hundred years of the great human-demon war, seen their strength reduced to the extreme.

Two or three families combined were only equivalent to the peak strength of a single Golden Core Great Sect in the past.

But everything changes with time.

Sects need to develop, disciples will increase, but the spirit veins are only so big, and the territory only so much.

When their strength recovers, how could one or two third-order spirit veins possibly accommodate everyone's needs?

Just like the original Luo Tian Society, when they first occupied Danxia, they were quite satisfied.

But later, as their strength swelled and their influence spread, they successively took over several nearby first- and second-order spirit lands.

This process was accompanied by killing and conquest.

Would those small forces under the Luo Tian Domain remain peaceful forever?

The answer was definitely no!

Even if they restrained themselves, the Luo Tian Sect would secretly fan the flames and make them fight.

Luo Chen walked outside the room, looked up at the towering Motian Cliff, and let out a long sigh.

"In the end, it's because the Luo Tian Sect has too few disciples and insufficient strength that we have to resort to such underhanded tricks!"

Sima Huiniang took his arm, brushed her hair back with her other hand, and looked ahead with hopeful eyes.

"It will get better!"

"In the future, when you have leisure time and return from the Nether Origin Sect, the Luo Tian Sect will surely have flourishing incense, a gathering of disciples, and become the immortal paradise spoken of by mortals."

...

"Let it be this mountain!"

Before the Calming Sea Ridge, Nine Spirits Lord spoke with a hint of melancholy in his eyes, offering his suggestion.

Luo Chen nodded. "The terrain is majestic, storing wind and gathering qi. It is indeed a fine third-order spirit mountain. Moving it back to the Luo Tian Domain can well fill the gap in the spirit vein left by the Demon Emperor shattering the mountain range. Moreover, moving this mountain will open a gap, channeling some of the water resources from the Calming Sea Ridge towards the Tai Lake Domain, which will be good for mortals to thrive in the future."

Nine Spirits Lord hummed in agreement. How could he not know the value of this mountain?

Back then, the Nine Spirits Sect couldn't bear to part with this mountain, yet it was too far from the sect's heartland to occupy, so they had entrusted it to a vassal force to manage.

But now, this mountain was going to be moved to the Luo Tian Domain, becoming part of the Luo Tian Sect's foundation.

Luo Chen patted his shoulder in comfort, then looked at the other two.

"Elder Fu, Brother Deng, I'll trouble you."

Elder Fu forced a smile. "It's no trouble, just a matter of one talisman. You all, step back a bit."

After Luo Chen and the other two stepped back a few paces, he put away his smile and took out a talisman of a dark yellow hue.

Before the three of them, he formed hand seals, chanted incantations, and muttered words.

Suddenly!

He opened his mouth and spat out a stream of mana.

The dark yellow talisman drifted lightly towards the Calming Sea Ridge before them.

A vast power erupted from the talisman's body. As it pressed down, the seawater below seemed to boil like water.

Giant waves surged thousands of feet high, rolling back without cease.

Just as the waves were about to crash down again...

"Suppress!"

The dark yellow talisman stood firm before the terrifying waves, unmoving, a magnificent sight.

Elder Fu lowered his hand, tucking it back into his sleeve. "Done."

"One talisman suppresses the sea. Excellent skill!"

Deng Taiyue laughed heartily as he strode forward.

"Now, watch my move, Brother Deng!"

He leaped directly off the high mountain, landing on the exposed seabed.

Then, facing the previously selected peak, he placed both palms flat on the ground.

With a deep breath and a low shout...

"Rise!"

In the next moment, a deafening rumbling sound continuously arose.

The sound was slow at first, but then grew faster and faster.

Luo Chen and the other two felt their bodies rising higher and higher, until some of the clouds and mist in the high sky were within reach.

Nine Spirits Lord showed a look of surprise. "Collapsing mountains and destroying peaks is easy for us, and uprooting mountains and suppressing seas is not a difficult task. But to do it as effortlessly as Fellow Daoist Deng, that is truly impressive."

Luo Chen smiled beside him. "You may not know this, but Brother Deng primarily cultivates an earth-attribute technique, so he is naturally skilled at such matters. Moreover, back then, he was just a hair's breadth away from comprehending the true meaning of the earth laws. Although he missed that final step, the insights he gained along the way have benefited him greatly. This technique is indeed nothing special for him now."

Nine Spirits Lord raised an eyebrow, then fell into deep thought.

The true meaning of the laws?

Deng Taiyue of Mount Tai Sect—he had only been a casual acquaintance before.

It was only because of Luo Chen that the two had grown closer, to the point of inviting him to help with the upcoming expansion war of the Nine Spirits Sect.

His evaluation of Deng Taiyue had been neither high nor low.

Just an ordinary sixth-layer Sage cultivator, with a realm similar to his own, average strength, no killing power, and a rather honest personality.

He never expected that this man had once come so close to stepping into the realm that all Sage cultivators dream of.

Comprehending the true meaning of the laws!

Nine Spirits Lord considered that entering the late Sage stage was not a difficult task for him. Once he settled down and cultivated properly for a few decades, it would be a natural progression.

But comprehending the true meaning of the laws—he had no clue at all.

Even if he wanted to make a desperate gamble for Ascendant in the future, he would probably have to follow the conventional path of raising his realm and condensing his primordial spirit.

But that path was also the most arduous one. How could it compare to comprehending the true meaning of the laws?

Thinking of this, he couldn't help but look at Luo Chen beside him.

The divine ability Luo Chen had used to repel the Seven Rhinoceros Great Demon Emperor and fight Divine Origin Sage to a standstill was likely also a derivation of some law's true meaning.

"Ever since I cultivated the Great Nine Heavens River, I have had eyes for no one but the Great Cultivators."

"Now it seems, I was somewhat arrogant."

"Peng Xiang's magical techniques are exquisite and surpass mine. Deng Taiyue was one step away from soaring to the clouds. Although Luo Chen's realm is low, he has already walked ahead of me."

"One must not underestimate the heroes of the world!"

Just as he was lost in thought, a loud shout came from below.

"Let's go!"

Deng Taiyue rose into the air, his mana spanning the sky, one hand supporting the towering mountain as he headed east.

What had required five Golden Cores, many Initiate cultivators, and even formation assistance for Luo Chen to uproot during his Golden Core days was now nothing more than a casual lift for Deng Taiyue.

As the spirit mountain moved east and the talisman was recalled, the boundless seawater that had been suppressed immediately collapsed.

Turning into giant waves, it surged towards the terrifying pit on the ground.

After gradually filling up, a vast amount of lake water flowed along the gap, rolling back towards the dried-up Tai Lake Domain.

...

In the Luo Tian Domain, which had not yet been renamed but was destined to change its name.

On a small third-order spirit vein.

The aged Tao Yisheng looked displeasedly at the beautiful woman before him.

"Sister, is this the only foothold Luo Chen is willing to give my Azure Pill Valley?"

At some point, Tao Wan had removed the light veil that covered her face.

She walked through the uneven valley, her gaze falling on the low-level cultivators of Azure Pill Valley who were busily constructing their dwellings.

Under the moonlight, her expression was serene, no longer bearing the haughty air of years past.

"Is it not good?"

Tao Yisheng was filled with anger. "In the old days, my Azure Pill Valley possessed five peaks of spirit land, enough to accommodate several Golden Core superiors, and even a dozen or so if we squeezed in. But what is this place? A territory less than fifty li in circumference?"

"It is our home, the mountain gate of Azure Pill Valley." Tao Wan said calmly.

"You are too narrow-minded. My Azure Pill Valley will surely develop in the future. How far can such a small territory take us? That Luo Chen is truly ungratef—"

"Hmph!"

Seeing that her brother was about to say something even more excessive, Tao Wan let out a cold snort.

With just that one sound, her younger brother, who had reached the Golden Core stage but whose temperament had greatly changed, fell silent in fear.

She said coldly, "For the current state of my Azure Pill Valley, this piece of land is more than enough. Moreover, we have our own separate household, unlike the Heavenly Wolf Society and the Rock-Solid Gang, who are squeezed together. What more could you be dissatisfied with?"

"But..."

"No buts! Also, the name Luo Chen is not for you to address directly. You must either respectfully call him Senior Dan Zong or respectfully address him as a Sage!"

Tao Yisheng opened his mouth, but under his sister's stern gaze, he could only remain silent.

He didn't understand why his once-proud sister was now so willing to humble herself.

Clearly, their Azure Pill Valley had a good relationship with Luo Chen. They had some history, and he himself had even gone to the Luo Tian Society to teach low-level cultivators how to refine pills.

How could Luo Chen be so heartless now?

What happened to the gentlemanly conduct, the sincerity in dealing with others?

Seeing him like this, Tao Wan sighed. Her brother's temperament had changed too much over the years.

She felt she should explain the intricacies to him in detail, along with her speculation about the future development of the Luo Tian Sect, lest her brother speak carelessly and cause trouble later.

Just as she was about to speak, the originally bright moonlight suddenly dimmed.

She looked up.

Under the moonlight, a towering mountain slowly flew over.

Someone was carrying a mountain and chasing the moon, heading straight for Motian Cliff.

Tao Wan's delicate body trembled slightly. Pointing at the ethereal figure beneath the mountain, she said in a trembling voice, "Do you still want to reason with a Sage cultivator?"

(End of Chapter)
