# Chapter 677: This Treasure Is Fated with Me. You Have No Objections, Right?

The silent roar slammed into Luo Chen's sea of consciousness.

His soul shook violently.

For an instant, the cave before his eyes vanished. All he could see was blood-red demonic light and pitch-black devil qi surging together like two oceans overturning. The ancient demon and ancient devil phantoms roared with unwillingness, hatred, and the savagery of beings that had once stood at the peak of their races.

There was no sound in the outside world.

The roar existed only in the soul.

It did not travel through the ears, nor did it shake the air. It erupted directly from the deepest part of perception, like a memory that did not belong to him suddenly tearing open a wound.

The demon shadow seemed to be laughing.

The devil shadow seemed to be cursing.

Their hatred had been sealed in the Demon-Devil Heart Tree for countless years, yet it had not faded.

Luo Chen's body lost balance and fell backward.

But before he struck the ground, he stopped in midair.

A faint red aura rose from within his flesh. It was thin, almost like mist, but it carried an ancient and heavy pressure. At the same time, a second layer of source power wrapped around his body and steadied him.

Inside his second Origin Pill, a tiny point of light trembled.

The Aperture-Opening Art [TERM?] that Han Zhan had just taught him burst open like a lamp in darkness.

It did not erase the demon-devil echoes.

It only told Luo Chen one thing.

What he was seeing was not real.

That single awareness was enough.

His eyes opened.

The killing intent in them was cold enough to make Tai Sui's scalp tighten.

Tai Sui immediately raised both hands. "Misunderstanding! Fellow Daoist Luo, this was an accident. The demon-devil echoes were left behind when the tree was cultivated. They are not truly harmful. I swear by the oath we made, I had no intention of harming you."

Moya Cave Master looked between the two of them, somewhat confused. "What misunderstanding?"

Luo Chen landed slowly.

He stared at Tai Sui for several breaths.

Only then did the killing intent in his eyes gradually fade.

"No next time."

Tai Sui nodded quickly. "Of course."

Luo Chen turned back toward the cave. "Collect the tree."

His voice was calm.

The calmer it was, the less Tai Sui dared to continue probing.

They had made an oath, but an oath was not a shackle that could restrain every edge of conflict.

If Luo Chen truly believed Tai Sui had plotted against him, he might not kill Tai Sui immediately, but he would certainly make every following step far more difficult.

And Tai Sui needed him.

Tai Sui secretly let out a breath.

He stepped into the cave and began digging carefully around the tree roots.

The Demon-Devil Heart Tree was not tall. It was only about one zhang high, with dark red bark and black vein-like patterns. Its leaves were thick and fleshy, shaped like hearts, and every pulse of light from the trunk made them tremble softly.

Several fist-sized fruits hung beneath the leaves.

Half of each fruit was red and half was black, as though two kinds of blood had been forced to coexist.

Tai Sui dug while explaining, "The fruit of this tree strengthens the five viscera and six bowels. If it is cultivated further, it can even condense demon-devil heart blood. Normally, it must be fed with the flesh and hearts of demon beasts and devil beasts. If one had archaic beast flesh, perhaps the effect would be even better."

He said the last sentence casually, but his mind was not on the tree.

He was still thinking about Luo Chen's reaction just now.

The old ghost vine would not have mentioned "three" without reason.

Woodman King had sensed the same number.

Tai Sui did not believe that two ancient beings who had guarded Hundred Crow Ghost Forest for thousands of years would both make the same mistake.

There had been no aura of a third person.

Old Ghost could not have lied to him.

Then how had Luo Chen recovered from the demon-devil echoes so quickly?

Was it truly because of his archaic beast body?

If so, then this human body cultivator was even stranger than Tai Sui had judged.

If not, then the hidden person was far more careful than he expected.

Either answer made Tai Sui's heart feel heavy.

Luo Chen watched Tai Sui from behind, his expression calm.

Inwardly, however, he already understood what had happened.

Tai Sui had tested him.

The words spoken by that fifth-tier Duskvine Crow in Hundred Crow Ghost Forest must have made Tai Sui suspect the existence of Han Zhan. That was why he had deliberately allowed Luo Chen to enter first and face the demon-devil echoes alone.

Fortunately, Luo Chen had prepared.

Han Zhan had taught him the Aperture-Opening Art [TERM?] inside the Dusk Curtain. Luo Chen had immediately spent achievement points to learn it thoroughly, then engraved it onto his second Origin Pill.

When the demon-devil echoes struck, the Aperture-Opening Art had opened a narrow path for his consciousness and allowed him to awaken.

His archaic body had also helped.

Without those two, even if Han Zhan intervened, Tai Sui might have sensed something.

After the Demon-Devil Heart Tree was dug out, Luo Chen put it away directly.

Tai Sui looked toward deeper parts of Wood Heaven Plain. "There are still several good places. Some sixth-tier and even seventh-tier spirit herbs were once cultivated here. Though the formations are stronger, with time..."

"No need," Luo Chen said.

Tai Sui was startled. "Fellow Daoist Luo?"

"We will go directly to your main body."

Tai Sui's expression changed slightly.

Moya Cave Master also looked over in surprise. "There are sixth-tier spirit herbs and you do not want them?"

Luo Chen's tone was calm. "The stronger the spirit herb, the stronger the formation. The longer we delay, the more variables there will be. First finish the matter we came for."

Moya Cave Master opened his mouth, then closed it again.

He was greedy by nature, but he was not stupid.

The Demon-Devil Heart Tree alone had nearly caused a hidden conflict between Tai Sui and Luo Chen.

If they kept wandering through Wood Heaven Plain, who knew what other ancient restrictions, spirit guardians, or outside cultivators they would encounter?

For him, the inheritance in Bright Radiance Heaven was still the greatest prize.

Tai Sui stared at him for a moment, then smiled. "Fellow Daoist Luo keeps promises."

"I only dislike complications."

Tai Sui did not argue.

Two days later, the group was passing over a low mountain when violent spiritual pressure suddenly erupted from a hollow ahead.

Moya Cave Master's eyes brightened. "Someone is fighting."

Tai Sui frowned. "The aura of the Divine Iris garden."

Luo Chen looked down from the mountain ridge.

In the hollow below, ten cultivators were locked in fierce battle.

On one side were seven white-robed cultivators, their techniques bright and orthodox. On the other side were three familiar figures.

Angler.

Purple Dowager.

Ghost Spirit Child.

All three were eight-star demon hunters of Myriad Immortals Assembly.

Angler's black-gold fishing rod swept through the air with a heavy whistling sound.

His fishing line was almost invisible, yet wherever it passed, defensive light split apart.

Purple Dowager moved like a drifting shadow.

Silver needles appeared and vanished around her sleeves, each strike aimed at joints, meridians, or the space between protective treasures.

Ghost Spirit Child was the most sinister.

His body blurred in and out of ghost qi, and every time he appeared, a Penglai disciple would be forced back in panic.

The three had clearly cooperated many times before.

Although they were outnumbered, they had suppressed the Penglai side from the start.

Luo Chen's eyes moved slightly.

Myriad Immortals Assembly had always maintained a neutral stance in the Northern Sea. Why were its people fighting Penglai Immortal Sect disciples here?

Tai Sui said, "They are fighting over the Divine Iris garden."

Moya Cave Master's throat moved.

Divine Iris.

A Sage-formation body-stabilizing resource.

For a late Golden Core like him, it was almost impossible not to be tempted.

Luo Chen did not act immediately.

He watched quietly.

The battle below had already reached its end.

Several corpses already lay among broken formation flags.

The earth around the hollow had been dug open in many places, revealing rich spirit soil and the faint remains of a transplanted garden.

It was obvious that someone had already moved the true prize before the fight fully broke out.

The Penglai cultivators fought while retreating, but the retreat path had been cut off by Angler's fishing line.

Purple Dowager's silver needles flashed like thin lines of moonlight. A Penglai disciple screamed and fell, his protective light pierced through in an instant.

Only two people remained on the Penglai side.

One was Yu Gao, the leader. The other was a young disciple whose face was pale with fear.

Yu Gao's expression was extremely ugly. "Go!"

He opened his mouth and spat out a cloud of white qi.

The cloud surged forward, forcing Angler back. Then Yu Gao turned and rushed into the hollow.

The white qi was not ordinary magic power.

It carried a clean, lofty aura, like cloud vapor from an immortal mountain.

The moment it spread, Angler's line sank as though it had fallen into cotton, and even Purple Dowager's needles slowed for an instant.

Yu Gao seized that tiny opening.

The next moment, a radiant eight-corner pavilion rose from the valley.

It was neither large nor small, about several zhang across. Eight eaves spread out in different directions, each glowing with a different attribute of light. Spiritual qi gathered toward it like streams returning to the sea.

Moya Cave Master cried out, "Penglai Octagonal Pavilion [TERM?]!"

Luo Chen's eyes sharpened.

He had heard of this treasure.

It was a true artifact inherited by Penglai Immortal Sect, and a rare mobile herb garden with eight attributes. Each eave could draw in a different kind of spiritual qi, allowing spirit herbs of many natures to grow together within it.

If the Divine Iris garden had been moved inside, then the pavilion's value was even higher.

For ordinary Golden Core cultivators, a single Sage-formation spirit herb was enough to risk life and death.

The Penglai Octagonal Pavilion, however, was not a single harvest.

It represented the ability to carry a spirit garden, maintain it, and continue producing harvests for generations.

No wonder Myriad Immortals Assembly's people had disregarded Penglai's face to seize it.

Yu Gao stood on the pavilion and shouted angrily, "Myriad Immortals Assembly, you dare rob Penglai!"

Angler flicked his black-gold fishing rod.

A nearly invisible line flew out and wrapped around the Octagonal Pavilion.

Heaven-Remnant Line.

Angler laughed coldly. "People can leave. The treasure stays."

Yu Gao's face twisted.

Purple Dowager's needles shot out again, piercing his shoulder and thigh. Blood splashed across his white robe.

At the same time, Ghost Spirit Child pounced on the last Penglai disciple. A ghostly claw tore through the young man's chest, ending his life on the spot.

The young disciple's eyes were still wide open when he fell.

Yu Gao saw it, and hatred almost burst from his gaze.

But he was the leader of this party.

In such a situation, dying together would only mean losing both people and treasure.

He chose to live.

Yu Gao knew he could no longer stay.

His body suddenly became light and translucent, like a feather caught by the wind.

"Today's hatred, Penglai will remember! One day, I will flatten Myriad Immortals Assembly!"

With that furious roar, his figure scattered into white feathers and vanished into the distance.

Feather Transformation Flying Escape [TERM?].

Purple Dowager wanted to chase, but Angler stopped her. "No need. We cannot catch him."

Purple Dowager frowned. "Letting him go will cause trouble."

"The trouble already exists," Angler said. "This was Moon Wanderer's order. If Penglai wants an explanation, let them ask her."

Purple Dowager's expression shifted slightly. "Moon Wanderer may have come from Penglai."

Angler did not respond.

The implication was clear.

If even Moon Wanderer, who had a complicated relationship with Penglai, dared issue this order, then they did not need to hesitate.

In Demon-Fall Land, treasures belonged to whoever could seize them.

He exerted strength through the fishing rod. The Heaven-Remnant Line tightened, slowly pulling the Octagonal Pavilion down.

The pavilion resisted instinctively.

Eight kinds of light rose from its eaves, but without Yu Gao controlling it, the resistance was scattered.

The Heaven-Remnant Line wrapped around it like a thin but unbreakable shackle, dragging it inch by inch toward Angler.

Angler's face showed a trace of satisfaction.

But just as the treasure was about to fall into his hands, a giant claw suddenly stretched out from the clouds.

The claw grabbed one eave of the pavilion and shook.

The Heaven-Remnant Line trembled violently and loosened.

"Who!"

Ghost Spirit Child was the first to react. His body turned into a streak of ghost light and shot upward.

In the next instant, a foot descended from the clouds.

Boom!

Ghost Spirit Child smashed into the ground like a broken sack. Blood and mud splattered outward.

The ghost qi protecting him collapsed at once.

His chest caved in, and his limbs twisted at unnatural angles.

He had not even had time to use a life-saving art.

The difference was too great.

Angler and Purple Dowager's expressions changed drastically.

A tall figure stepped out of the clouds and placed one hand on the Octagonal Pavilion.

Behind him, the massive claw that had seized the pavilion slowly withdrew into the clouds.

It was not a real demon claw, but the manifestation of Luo Chen's qi and blood condensed through body cultivation.

Even so, the pressure it gave off made both Angler and Purple Dowager feel as though a fourth-tier beast king were staring down at them.

Luo Chen looked down at the two of them and smiled faintly.

"This treasure is fated with me. You have no objections, right?"

*(End of Chapter)*
