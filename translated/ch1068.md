# Chapter 1068: Number One in the Eastern Wilderness, Sword Pointed at the Dao Sect

As Luo Chen's expression darkened, the temperature in the great hall seemed to drop several degrees.

A suffocating, heavy aura spread slowly like a mountain, and everyone fell silent, trembling like cicadas in winter.

Feeling this aura that seemed like heavenly might, people seemed to remember only now that the Luo Chen who had just been warmly chatting and laughing with everyone was not merely the Alchemy Lord renowned throughout the Eastern Wilderness for his benevolent and gentlemanly conduct. He was also a baleful star who had weathered countless great and small battles, once hailed as the strongest under the Ascendant stage—the Formless Demon Lord!

Faced with this aura, Dongyang's Grand Elder's expression changed dramatically.

Even at his late-stage Sage cultivation, he felt an extreme danger.

And beside him, Lian Buwei and Shi Chuanting were even more unbearable, their faces deathly pale.

Especially Lian Buwei. He was the one who had just questioned Luo Chen.

But as a Sage of the Dao Sect, he naturally possessed the pride of a disciple from the number one sacred land under heaven!

"Luo Chen, do you intend to kill us to silence us?" Lian Buwei gritted his teeth and forced himself to speak. "Our deaths are nothing, but have you considered how you will face the wrath of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect?"

Luo Chen stared at him, his words like ice: "You say I collude with the demon race?"

Lian Buwei rallied his spirits: "Yes! Who in the world doesn't know that Rosycloud and our Dao Sect share an irreconcilable blood feud? Yet now you want to sign a ceasefire treaty with her. If that isn't collusion, what is?"

"Heh."

Luo Chen let out a cold laugh.

"On my path to rise, my sword has been stained with the blood and bones of countless demon race experts. How can you utter such laughable words accusing me of colluding with the demon race? Moreover, the one who holds a grudge against Rosycloud is your Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, not the various sects of the Eastern Wilderness!"

Lian Buwei's eyes widened. "Then how do you explain the destruction of the Nether Origin Sect?"

Luo Chen said calmly, "Nether Origin is Nether Origin. It has little to do with us. Could it be that anyone present here still wishes to avenge the Nether Origin Sect?"

As he spoke these words, the black-robed Daoist's gaze swept across everyone present, especially those sect masters who had once had good relations with the Nether Origin Sect.

"Azure Cloud Sage, does your Dieyun Sect wish to?"

The newly promoted Sage Azure Cloud subconsciously shook his head. In the eyes of outsiders, their Dieyun Sect was a vassal of the holy land, but in reality, they were no more than servants. Most of the finest spiritual wines they brewed each year had to be tributed to the Nether Origin Sect. There was no great kindness or virtue there.

Luo Chen's gaze shifted again, landing on the hale and hearty old man: "Or is it you, Dongyang Grand Elder?"

Dongyang's Grand Elder remained silent, then heavily shook his head.

For the Dongyang Sect, the Nether Origin Sect had been fine, but without the Nether Origin Sect, things were even better!

Lian Buwei shot a fierce glare at Dongyang's Grand Elder, then forced himself to say, "Yet one hundred and forty years ago, the demon race forces led by Green Parasol Mountain amassed troops on the border of the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world. That battle may not have been large, but the number of disciples who fell from various sects and clans in the Eastern Wilderness was not small!"

Luo Chen smiled.

"What was the true cause of that battle? You and I both know it well. If the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect had not entered the Eastern Wilderness and ignited the flames of war, how would Green Parasol Mountain have stirred up conflict again?"

Lian Buwei wanted to say more, but Luo Chen's smile vanished in an instant as he cut him off.

"In the end, you simply want us Eastern Wilderness sects to be the vanguard for the king, to block Green Parasol Mountain for you!"

"How many die, how many sects and families are destroyed—you don't care at all. What you care about is the strategic significance of the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world as a buffer zone, and that our lives can buy the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect time to recover its strength."

"But why should we bear all this?"

Lian Buwei's lips trembled, his face pale. He pointed at Luo Chen but couldn't utter a single word.

Everyone present seemed lost in thought. Clearly, Luo Chen had hit upon the intentions of the other party, and even of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

Fighting and killing for others—that was truly too foolish.

Once they calmed down and thought it through, they could naturally recall the many benefits they would enjoy after the ceasefire.

As for the drawbacks, there were naturally some—namely, the inability to open up new territories and obtain more resources. But this drawback did not exist at present, or even within a thousand years.

After the great human-demon war, the Eastern Wilderness, which had once been relatively "crowded," had long since become spacious.

Even now, there were nearly ten cultivation domains lying vacant, without any Sage-level sect taking up residence and governing them. The resources within were merely benefiting rogue cultivators.

As for a thousand years later?

The ceasefire period written on that scroll was only a thousand years!

A thousand years later, whether war or peace, whether the demon race would invade or humanity would expand—that was a matter for the future!

Suddenly.

Tianduzi's weathered voice slowly rang out.

"Alchemy Lord, is this treaty effective?"

When Tianduzi spoke, Luo Chen felt a slight relief in his heart.

Back when he became the master of Truth Crossing Hall, Rosycloud had sent him several great gifts, among which was this drafted ceasefire treaty.

At that time, Luo Chen hadn't quite understood her intentions, but after traveling through the Eastern Wilderness again, he had come to comprehend.

After the great war in Central Continent, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect had suffered heavy losses, and Rosycloud had also been severely wounded.

Under these circumstances, Green Parasol Mountain could not afford too much turmoil.

The first thing to do was to appease the neighboring Eastern Wilderness cultivation world.

Hence, this ceasefire treaty had come about.

At this moment, when Luo Chen brought up the matter of ceasefire, personal motives accounted for the majority—to stabilize the internal situation before dealing with external threats, he had to resolve the rift between the human and demon races to deal with the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. But there was also a third of public-mindedness: the current Eastern Wilderness cultivation world could not withstand a war covering the entire continent.

"Of course it's effective!"

Luo Chen patiently explained: "It bears Rosycloud's signature, representing the Green Parasol Mountain forces. And the current Green Parasol Mountain is the sacred land of the demon race in the Million Great Mountains. No one dares to defy Rosycloud's orders."

Tianduzi nodded, then asked again: "And what about our side?"

Luo Chen frowned. "The representatives of the major Eastern Wilderness sects are basically all here. Can't we just jointly sign it?"

Tianduzi slowly shook his head. "We can only represent ourselves. But the current Eastern Wilderness is full of various opportunities. In the future, it's inevitable that there will be great sect cultivators, clan experts, or even rogue cultivators who break through to the Sage stage."

As he said this, his gaze shifted to the back.

Many followed his line of sight to a man and a woman.

Tao Wan of the Azure Pill Valley, and the rogue cultivator Bo Hanjun!

They were such existences.

"In the future, if these newly advanced experts recklessly stir up conflict and initiate expansion wars, what then? This treaty has no binding force on them. Alchemy Lord, do you wish to see the matter of Falling Cloud and Joyous Union happen again?"

The matter of Falling Cloud and Joyous Union?

Everyone's spirits were shaken!

Luo Chen stroked the thumb ring on his hand, pondering Tianduzi's words.

Clearly, what Tianduzi said made some sense.

Since ancient times, although expansion wars had never ceased, with both successes and failures, they had basically been confined to a single sect or domain.

Only when it came to the Falling Cloud Sect had a change occurred.

The Falling Cloud Sect had launched an expansion war, not only using their own sect's strength but also inviting many other sects to assist.

Of course, this was within the rules.

But two things had crossed the line.

First, when Han Zhan fought against Wolf Emperor Aoxiao, other sect Sages had participated—those people were not cultivators of the Falling Cloud Sect!

Second, Cloud Crane Sage of the Joyous Union Sect had attempted to expand into the Great Snowy Mountains. This was equivalent to humanity expanding into two domains simultaneously, violating the rules!

Therefore, the demon race's counterattack later had been so massive, and they had ample justification, standing on the moral high ground because humanity had first torn up the ceasefire treaty.

Human hearts are always greedy.

Temporary restraint could only govern for a time, not the future.

To solve this kind of problem...

Luo Chen had an answer in his heart, but he did not say it directly. Instead, he looked at Tianduzi.

"Since you have raised this question, Fellow Daoist, you must already have a plan in mind, right?"

Tianduzi nodded slightly and said calmly: "We need a representative, a powerful representative like the Nether Origin Sect. Ordinarily, they would restrain the newly established sects, preventing them from acting rashly out of greed. And if there were an unexpected event like a demon race invasion, this representative would organize resistance immediately, so that we wouldn't have a repeat of that year when the demon race swept through fifteen domains, causing countless human casualties."

At these words, everyone nodded in agreement.

But the question arose again: who would be this representative?

Everyone understood that once they sat in that position, even if they weren't quite at the level of the Nether Origin Sect's former status in the Eastern Wilderness, they wouldn't be far off.

The benefits contained within were unimaginable!

While everyone was hesitating and restless, Tianduzi spoke again.

"I recommend the Alchemy Sect. I recommend the Luo Tian Sect to be this representative."

Luo Chen pressed his lips together. This proposal was the answer in his heart. But he couldn't bring it up himself; the experienced and steady Tianduzi was very suitable.

At this moment, as Tianduzi's proposal was made, the Shouyang Great Hall fell into agitation.

Clearly, there were many who wanted to be this representative.

Amidst the commotion, a clear and pleasant voice stood out.

"I agree with Fellow Daoist Tianduzi's proposal!"

Everyone followed the sound and looked at the woman they had been paying attention to earlier—Tao Wan of the Azure Pill Valley!

Faced with everyone's gaze, this newly promoted Sage seemed to feel immense pressure.

But she still spoke gracefully: "Everyone should recognize the name of the Alchemy Sect, right? In terms of personal prestige, no one in the current Eastern Wilderness surpasses him. In terms of personal strength, Fellow Daoist Luo Chen possesses an invincible aura that outshines all in the Sage stage."

Everyone was silent. Luo Chen indeed had extremely high prestige, not only among high-level cultivators but also widely rumored among low-level cultivators.

Compared to the Alchemy Saint of yesteryear, the current Alchemy Lord had even greater influence!

And his strength was already legendary.

He had earned the name Demon-Slaying Sage in the Eastern Wilderness, and in the Western Desert, he had made a name for himself as the Formless Demon Lord. Ordinary Sages didn't even have the courage to fight him.

Tao Wan spoke again, pointing outside: "And the Luo Tian Sect, although established far later than our superior sects, everyone should have seen roughly how strong the Luo Tian Sect is today. I ask, which sect can surpass the Luo Tian Sect?"

Everyone's hearts trembled as they recalled this matter they had overlooked, or even didn't want to think about.

But the facts were right before their eyes.

The Luo Tian Sect, which had been established for the shortest time, was now the strongest in the Eastern Wilderness, surpassing even the Dongyang Sect and the White Deer Grotto!

Outside the hall, four Demon Emperors were stationed!

Inside the hall, there were six Sages!

There were also Ghost Emperor Xiaobai and War Demon Wang Yuan, existences comparable to Sages!

Finally, Tao Wan exhaled a breath of turbid air, stood up, and bowed deeply to Luo Chen.

"I believe that Luo Chen and the Luo Tian Sect are the most suitable existences to represent the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world today!"

Others were still hesitant.

But right after Tao Wan, another white-browed man stood up.

"I am merely a rogue cultivator, unknown and insignificant, not worth mentioning. I received the Alchemy Lord's grace and managed to form my Nascent Soul by luck. Today, by chance, I also hope for a stable cultivation environment in the future. If the Alchemy Lord becomes the representative of the Eastern Wilderness, Bo Hanjun has no objections."

If Tianduzi represented the voice of the ancient sects, then Tao Wan and Bo Hanjun could be seen as representatives of the emerging forces in the future Eastern Wilderness.

With both sides expressing themselves this way, even if others had other thoughts, it was hard to voice them.

"I, Fenghua Palace, agree!"

"Nine Spirits Sect agrees!"

"White Crane Sect agrees!"

"Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion agrees!"

...

Those who spoke up next, from front to back, were basically sects with close ties to Luo Chen.

A rough count showed they already accounted for more than half of the forces present.

The remaining sects, as if influenced, finally relented and expressed their agreement one after another.

Even Medicine Master of Seven Mound Mountain, disregarding past grudges, acknowledged Luo Chen and the Luo Tian Sect's status.

In the end, only the two strongest—the White Deer Grotto and the Dongyang Sect—remained.

Under everyone's gaze, Sage Pengxiang of the White Deer Grotto laughed heartily: "In the past, my junior brother Tieguan fought alongside the Alchemy Lord in the Overlord Blade Domain, and I also helped the Five Elements Divine Sect oppose the Luo Tian Sect. Junior Brother Kongtongzi, on the other hand, received the Alchemy Lord's Nascent Soul Pill and thus advanced to the Sage stage. The grudges and favors are hard to weigh, but I trust the Alchemy Lord's character. Since everyone says so, the White Deer Grotto also agrees with this matter."

Thus, only the Dongyang Sect remained.

Once a vassal of the Nether Origin Sect, later the first to surrender to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, and even once giving up their mountain gate for Dao Sect experts to stay, Dongyang's Grand Elder now wore a bitter smile under Lian Buwei's fierce glare.

He did not voice his agreement, but neither did he oppose, as if silently acquiescing.

No one pressed him for a statement, instead turning their attention back to Luo Chen.

A sigh, faint and long, drifted out.

Luo Chen stood up and bowed to everyone with cupped hands.

"Since everyone thinks so highly of me, I will not decline. Since I retrieved the ceasefire treaty, then as the human representative of the Eastern Wilderness, I and the Luo Tian Sect will take on this responsibility without hesitation!"

"In the future, the Luo Tian Sect will restrain newly established sects and mediate relations with the demon race, but we will not interfere in the specific affairs of each sect. If there are major matters concerning the common interests of the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world, the Luo Tian Sect will take the lead and live up to everyone's expectations today!"

"Everyone, please!"

As his words fell, Luo Chen casually drew with his hand.

On the floating ceasefire treaty, five large characters appeared—**Luo Tian Sect: Luo Chen!**

The others no longer hesitated, each sending out their mana and imprinting their sect and personal names.

Thus, this ceasefire treaty gained its binding force.

The Eastern Wilderness cultivation world would welcome at least a thousand years of peace.

Luo Chen cast a seal on the scroll and spoke a few details, such as announcing it to the world in the future, and that when new Sage-level forces emerged, they would be asked to sign the treaty as well.

When he put away the scroll, the once noisy Shouyang Great Hall fell silent again.

One after another, gazes turned to him, filled with anticipation.

Luo Chen did not keep them in suspense.

With a flick of his palm, a golden pill appeared before everyone's eyes.

"It's the Initiation Pill!" Medicine Master of Seven Mound Mountain blurted out, his face full of surprise. "You really refined it!"

Luo Chen smiled slightly. "Yes, this is the ancient divine pill, the Initiation Pill, known as a pseudo-fifth-grade divine pill. It can help one enter a state of enlightenment, commune with heaven and earth, and comprehend the true meaning of laws."

Gulp!

The sound of someone swallowing saliva was clearly audible in the hall.

"Truly?" Tianduzi suddenly spoke.

The one who had just recommended Luo Chen as the Eastern Wilderness representative had appeared very calm and steady, but now he too lost his composure.

After him, Nine Spirits Primogenitor, Sage Pengxiang, and even Dongyang's Grand Elder all stared fixedly at that pill.

Luo Chen did not bother to prove the effects of the Initiation Pill. Instead, he declared loudly: "I have already completed the formula for the Initiation Pill, and there is no problem with the refining process. As long as you help me with one task, regardless of who it is, I can help refine an Initiation Pill!"

Everyone lost their composure.

Whether it was the aged Tianduzi, or the newly promoted Sages Bo Hanjun and Tao Wan, all were breathing rapidly.

As for the veteran Sages, they wished they could have an Initiation Pill in their hands right then and there.

They had even forgotten what Luo Chen's request was.

Whatever it was, big or small, nothing was unacceptable!

After all, this was a matter concerning the great path to the Ascendant stage!

Tianduzi let out a long breath, his eyes shining with spirit as he stared at Luo Chen. "Alchemy Lord, please speak plainly. What matter requires our help?"

Luo Chen smiled slightly. "You only need to make a move for me once."

Sage Pengxiang was surprised: "Just once?"

Luo Chen nodded. "Yes, just once. Life or death does not matter. After the deed is done, I will definitely present an Initiation Pill, along with relevant insights on taking it."

Nine Spirits Primogenitor asked in a deep voice: "Who is the enemy?"

Luo Chen's gaze shifted, landing on the two people at the back.

"The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!"

Everyone was stunned.

Boom! Boom!

In the silence, thunderclaps suddenly arose!

Lian Buwei and Shi Chuanting did not hesitate for a moment. Their mana exploded as they charged toward the hall's exit!

Luo Chen watched all this, seemingly unconcerned.

And the next moment!

A massive Xuanhuang hand descended from outside the hall, pressing down with overwhelming force.

Boom!

With a single strike, the two Dao Sect Sages were sent flying back.

A figure strode boldly into the hall.

It was none other than Taiyue Sage!
