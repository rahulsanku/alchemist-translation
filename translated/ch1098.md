# Chapter 1098: A Finger to Kill, A Clash of Divine Sense

A thousand-year descent into the Fallen Sea!

In ordinary times, the pitch-black seawater contained an extremely powerful corrosive force, especially against cultivator protective barriers. Relying on this, this sea region had once served as one of the Primordial Demon Sect's mountain-protecting barriers.

But once the Demon-Fall Land opened, the Fallen Sea would also spew forth a Void Burning Wind that harmed a cultivator's divine soul. That was the truly terrifying thing. Even Sage cultivators dared not touch it.

Calculating the time, more than three hundred years had passed since the last opening of the Demon-Fall Land, far exceeding the opening patterns previously detected by Northern Sea cultivators. But this time, the Demon-Fall Land had not opened normally as before.

According to some knowledgeable people's estimates, this might be related to how, back when the demon race had burst out of Yemo Heaven, they had diverted a large amount of the Fallen Sea's earth qi.

The demon monk Kongchan was one of those knowledgeable people.

Otherwise, he wouldn't have joined Tai Sui, the Shark Emperor, and others to infiltrate Yemo Heaven, just to find a way to retrieve the earth qi and reopen the Fallen Sea.

"You said they went in?" Luo Chen's eyes narrowed slightly as he asked softly.

Kongchan nodded solemnly. "That's right, this old monk personally watched them enter."

"Why didn't you stop them?"

Kongchan revealed a bitter smile. "It's not that this old monk didn't want to, but they moved too fast. This Gu Shaoshang of the Black Devil, having roamed the chaotic Northern Sea for these past few centuries, naturally has his own backing and an extremely vigilant nature. His younger sister's divine sense is even more astonishing; approaching would have been discovered immediately. Most importantly... the magical treasures in their hands were suspected to be spirit treasures. This old monk dared not act rashly."

Suspected spirit treasures?

Luo Chen took a deep breath. That Black Devil Jar and True Yang Crown were not merely suspected spirit treasures.

If the Heaven-Refining Cauldron's artifact spirit hadn't lied to him back then, among the many treasures the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining had refined in his life, three were existences that surpassed ordinary spirit treasures, known as heaven-reaching spirit treasures!

Based on Luo Chen's current understanding, spirit treasures were artifacts that had birthed their own artifact spirits, possessing strong autonomy. Whether for tracking attacks or automatic body protection, they were far more convenient to control than magical treasures or true artifacts.

As for heaven-reaching spirit treasures, their particularity lay precisely in the word "heaven-reaching."

Such treasures were not merely spiritual. They had the effect of amplifying the power of laws and increasing might!

Using the same divine ability, the power between casting it oneself and casting it through a heaven-reaching spirit treasure was completely different!

And the Black Devil Jar and True Yang Crown were heaven-reaching spirit treasures on par with the Heaven-Refining Cauldron.

Luo Chen had sent people to search for the Gu siblings, partly to eliminate the root of trouble, and partly... to conveniently seize those two treasures.

Back then, because of the dispute over the Nine-Refined Beacon Fire and the Nirvana Sacred Flame, he had missed out on the Heaven-Refining Cauldron.

Even though he had later nurtured the Yuantu Sword, a supreme killing treasure, through his own cultivation, it was ultimately not a heaven-reaching spirit treasure.

With his ascension imminent, how could Luo Chen feel he had too many methods?

"Let's go. Enter!"

Luo Chen took the lead, flying directly into the Fallen Sea.

Kongchan was momentarily stunned but still gritted his teeth and followed.

...

Deep within the Fallen Sea, a place of standing bones.

A figure was busily arranging something. Beside him was a petite and delicate woman.

Watching her brother's urgency, Gu Xiaolian finally couldn't help but ask, "Brother, do we really have to go back?"

Gu Shaoshang's voice was hoarse, and he replied without looking up, "We must go back!"

Gu Xiaolian's face showed worry. "But I feel like we shouldn't return to the Demon-Fall Land. Isn't there a rumor in the Northern Sea that a cultivator should only enter the Demon-Fall Land once in a lifetime at most?"

Gu Shaoshang snorted coldly. "Rumors are just rumors. Have you forgotten that Fairy Jadecold entered the Demon-Fall Land twice?"

Seeing that his sister was about to say more, and that his arrangements were almost complete, he straightened up and looked at her.

"Xiaolian, you need to understand. The Northern Sea no longer has a place for us. If we stay outside any longer, we'll eventually be targeted by Ascendant powerhouses."

Gu Xiaolian pouted, her chubby baby face puffing up as she said, "It's because you went too far, crazily killing people and demons, using their flesh, blood, bones, and souls to refine the Black Devil Jar."

"It has nothing to do with that." Gu Shaoshang shook his head. "The Black Devil Jar and the True Yang Crown you wear have been too conspicuous in recent years. Many have already guessed they might be spirit treasures. That alone is enough to move Ascendant powerhouses. If they learn that these two are heaven-reaching spirit treasures, the entire Northern Sea, both human and demon races, will go mad for them."

Gu Xiaolian blinked. Was that so?

Thinking about it carefully, it seemed about right.

Although she could only use a fraction of the True Yang Crown's power, that fraction alone was enough to keep her invincible within the Sage realm.

Her brother, Gu Shaoshang, was even more of a prodigy, constantly excavating the Black Devil Jar's potential. In just three hundred years, his cultivation had climbed to the sixth layer of the Sage realm, only one step away from the Great Sage realm.

"Xiaolian!"

"What is it?" Faced with her brother's sudden seriousness, Gu Xiaolian was a bit slow to react.

Gu Shaoshang's originally handsome face had now turned somewhat sinister.

"Do you still remember our father's death back in Bright Radiance Heaven?"

Gu Xiaolian was stunned, then her face became sorrowful.

"I remember. It was the Wilderness Wanderer, Luo Hai..."

"No, his name is Luo Chen! He is now one of the few top powerhouses in the world, even having killed an Ascendant from the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect across realms!"

Gu Shaoshang spoke word by word, his words filled with immense hatred, yet he recounted Luo Chen's experiences as if reciting them from memory.

Clearly, he had never let go of that deep-seated hatred from beginning to end.

"Facing such a monstrous existence, no matter how hard I try, no matter how crazy I become, I can only chase behind him for a lifetime, never able to surpass him."

"Only by returning to the Demon-Fall Land, taking all the Five Elements Heaven resources, and using them to aid our cultivation, can we possibly advance to Ascendant, and then take our revenge."

"That is the true purpose of our return!"

Gu Xiaolian nodded. "Then I'll listen to you, brother!"

Gu Shaoshang breathed a sigh of relief, a rare smile appearing on his face.

Now that the Black Devil Jar was set up, even though the earth qi was lacking and the timing wasn't right, relying on this heaven-reaching spirit treasure, he could still open a passage to enter.

Moreover, the commotion wouldn't be too great, making it less likely to attract attention.

The current Northern Sea cultivation world was not suitable for peaceful cultivation. Only by returning to the Demon-Fall Land and taking all the Five Elements Heaven resources could he break through to the late Sage realm, or even the Ascendant realm!

A vortex rose from beneath his feet—the entrance to the Demon-Fall Land.

Gu Shaoshang stepped into it, then turned back for one last look at the Fallen Sea, his gaze carrying a hint of reluctance.

He wondered how long it would be before he could come out this time...

One look!

Gu Shaoshang suddenly froze.

The seawater rippled and spread, and two figures abruptly came into view.

Among them, the bald monk's realm was clear at a glance—a genuine late-stage Sage.

Yet that bald monk was now respectfully, even humbly, following behind another young man.

So just what was the cultivation level of this somewhat familiar young man?

Familiar?

Gu Shaoshang blurted out, "It's you!"

What answered him was a cold glance that brought boundless illusions, and before the sinking illusion, a single finger of crimson sword qi.

*Xiu!*

The sword qi broke through the air, forcibly squeezing into the vortex, and struck heavily against Gu Shaoshang's body.

*Bang!*

Within the vortex, blood mist exploded.

Following that came the woman's heart-wrenching cry.

"Brother!"

Boundless light erupted from the woman's body, obscuring everything, illuminating the Fallen Sea.

It scattered the more sword qi that followed in all directions.

When all the clamor subsided, the vortex had already dissipated, leaving only two figures—one old, one young.

"It's an entrance." Kongchan made the correct judgment after a brief observation.

Luo Chen naturally knew that. What he cared about wasn't that, but the life and death of those two siblings.

Gu Shaoshang should be dead, dead beyond dead.

With his current primordial spirit strength, using Mirror Flower Water Moon had taken on different changes.

When the mirror shattered and the water was disturbed, that was when the flower withered and the moon faded. That represented the destruction of the soul sunk within.

That finger of Scarlet Firmament Sword Qi was also enough to destroy Gu Shaoshang's physical body.

As for that woman, Gu Xiaolian...

"She should still be alive. That True Yang Crown protected her at the critical moment. She should have escaped into the Demon-Fall Land."

One could only say it was worthy of being a heaven-reaching spirit treasure!

Facing an Ascendant's attack, even the person involved hadn't reacted, but that True Yang Crown could automatically protect its master.

If not for that, Luo Chen would have already completely eliminated the root of trouble.

Kongchan looked at the dissipated vortex and asked cautiously, "Should we enter?"

Luo Chen didn't answer, a thoughtful expression on his face.

Enter?

He didn't have the method to enter the Demon-Fall Land on hand.

Although he also had two spirit treasures personally refined by the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining, one was the Burial Bone Umbrella, which had been in the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect before the Devil Lord's fall, and the other was the Yuantu Sword, which had only fully awakened its spirit later.

Neither of them knew how to return to the Demon-Fall Land under abnormal opening conditions.

Moreover!

Although Ascendant powerhouses were the most supreme existences in the Mountains and Seas Realm, they were not invincible. There were still many dangerous places that could threaten an Ascendant's safety.

Like the Soul-Breaking Cliff that had sent Rosycloud back severely injured, or the Demon-Fall Land before him, which had once buried the Blood Sea Ancestor.

Forcing his way in would be extremely unwise!

"Let's go. First, go see an old friend."

"Who?"

"He also once entered the Demon-Fall Land, obtained great fortune, and proved the Dao to become an Ascendant. It's simpler to ask him about some things." Luo Chen's eyes narrowed slightly as he flicked his sleeves and left.

Kongchan was first confused, then suddenly understood.

The person his master spoke of had a great reputation, known to everyone in the entire Northern Sea.

It should be that formation dao grandmaster—Li Canghai!

...

Northern Sea, Heaven's End!

This place was once the headquarters of the Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance. At its peak, cultivators were like clouds, and flying lights were like threads.

Even though the Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance now existed in name only, and dominant powers like the Moluo Stream and Myriad Immortals Assembly had been scattered by wind and rain, Heaven's End was still bustling with activity.

This was because this was the only cultivation dojo of a human powerhouse in the Northern Sea!

The current Northern Sea cultivation world was extremely chaotic, with scenes of demon beast invasions and murder for treasure playing out at all times. Relatively speaking, Heaven's End, where a powerhouse resided, was much safer.

Regardless of whether Li Canghai cared about matters or paid attention to the life and death of the ants below, those demon cultivators and devil cultivators still had to give him some face and dared not cause trouble on his turf.

This day.

Above Heaven's End, two figures abruptly appeared.

The moment those two figures appeared, the master of this place seemed to sense something. The clouds and mist in the sky converged, actually opening a gate in the void.

"Daoist, if you don't mind, why not come in for a chat?"

Luo Chen looked at that void gate, a flicker of light in his eyes.

After instructing Kongchan a few words, he calmly and confidently walked in.

Once inside, an extremely rich spiritual qi immediately rushed toward him, mixed with many spiritual opportunities.

Looking around, the vast ocean was waveless, and mountains stood tall—a truly peaceful, otherworldly scene.

Among them, a few blurry sights made Luo Chen's heart feel a sense of familiarity.

The Ice Pole Sect's spirit mountain!

The Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range!

Two names popped up, and then Luo Chen's gaze moved downward, where he saw an old man boating on the calm sea, showing him a kind smile.

"Daring to enter this old man's Mountain-Sea Seal, it seems you have indeed proven the Dao and become an Ascendant. Congratulations, congratulations!"

As expected!

Luo Chen's guess was confirmed. This world was a self-contained realm, seemingly at Heaven's End, but actually in another space.

Inside the other party's Mountain-Sea Seal.

The Yuantu Sword in his body trembled slightly, appearing somewhat restless, clearly not liking this environment.

Luo Chen suppressed the spirit treasure's agitation and gracefully landed on that small boat.

"Please!" Li Canghai extended his hand in invitation.

Luo Chen sat down generously.

Beside them, the waves parted, and a graceful figure emerged from the sea, holding a silver flask as she poured wine for the two.

As the wine blossoms bloomed, smiles also blossomed on their faces.

Luo Chen withdrew his gaze from the woman who looked like a mermaid, looking at Li Canghai. "You old man is truly at ease. Outside is all chaos, yet you hide here in the mountains and seas, leisurely and carefree."

Li Canghai raised his wine glass, took a small sip, then exhaled a breath of wine qi.

"Ha... what does the outside chaos have to do with me? Our generation cultivates, isn't it to seek immortality and attain freedom?"

Luo Chen nodded in agreement, but also somewhat disagreed. "At least making your doorstep a bit quieter would be better, wouldn't it?"

For an Ascendant powerhouse, the so-called doorstep naturally wasn't just this small patch of Heaven's End, but the vast Northern Sea cultivation world in general.

The current Northern Sea cultivation world was indeed too chaotic.

Because of the demon race's rampage, because of evil devils running rampant.

Li Canghai's brow furrowed slightly as he began to carefully examine Luo Chen.

Across from him, the man's appearance was still excessively young, and his cultivation time was equally excessively young.

Sitting there now, he was like a pine, like a great mountain, towering and majestic.

Yet beneath his calm, water-like face, it seemed to contain a vigorous heat like a volcano.

This kind of contrasting temperament, this completely unrevealed realm, made it impossible to see his true level.

And this was within his carefully refined spirit treasure, the Mountain-Sea Seal!

Even Li Canghai couldn't see through Luo Chen's strength.

Was this still the performance of someone who had just broken through to Ascendant?

Li Canghai spoke slowly. "Daoist Luo, is this 'when poor, one cultivates oneself alone; when successful, one benefits the world'? Having just become an Ascendant, do you want to make some big moves?"

"Not big moves, but I can't let those two demon race powerhouses run rampant unchecked!" Luo Chen raised his wine glass, sniffed it, then drank it down in one gulp. "Good wine!"

Li Canghai was somewhat silent, then finally smiled bitterly. "It seems you still hold a grudge about the Sea Emperor Whale and Great Sage Beili almost making a move against you back then!"

Luo Chen neither confirmed nor denied.

At that time, if Azure Frost hadn't stood by his side, and if Li Canghai hadn't stepped in to mediate, he would likely have been taken advantage of by others.

Li Canghai shook his head. "Daoist, you've just become an Ascendant. Instead of urgently stabilizing your realm, you want to make a big move. Aren't you afraid of harming your Dao cultivation?"

"Stabilizing my realm?"

Luo Chen murmured, a slight smile curling at the corner of his mouth.

His divine sense slowly spread out.

Li Canghai realized what was happening and released his own divine sense as well.

The moment their divine senses clashed, primordial spirit phantoms appeared behind each of them. And within the world of the Mountain-Sea Seal, monstrous waves were stirred up, and the mountains shook.
