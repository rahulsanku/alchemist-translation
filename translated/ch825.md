# Chapter 825: A Feast of Division, Pseudo Fifth-Order Divine Pill

In the cultivation world, there is actually a very intuitive way to judge a demon beast's grade.

That is to look at its size!

First-order beasts are about one *zhang* in size.

Second-order beasts range from two or three *zhang* to seven or eight *zhang*.

But once they reach over ten *zhang*, they are basically third-order Demon Kings.

As for fourth-order Demon Emperors, which can rival human Sages, their bodies are often over a hundred *zhang*, sitting or lying down like mountains, and moving with the wind.

Hence the terrifying sight of a great demon on the move, demonic qi spreading and demonic wind sweeping.

The reason demon beast realms are linked to size is something predecessors have specifically studied.

Three conclusions were drawn:

First, large bodies intimidate peers and dominate territory—this is the subconscious pursuit of all beasts to be tall and large.

Second, demon beasts derive from ancient desolate beasts that transformed into the human cultivation path. In essence, demons and desolate beasts are not separate, and desolate beasts excel at body refinement, attempting to use their own bodies to manifest mountains, rivers, sun, and moon. The Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven that Luo Chen saw, and even the vast Gobi Desert in the Demon-Fall Land, were all transformed by powerful fifth-order desolate beasts. Even though demon beasts changed to the human cultivation system, they inherited this instinct.

The third point is somewhat discriminatory.

According to that predecessor, demon beasts are naturally stupid and cannot master human methods well, so their qi refinement path is extremely crude.

They cannot finely polish and refine their mana, nor can they use a small dantian qi sea like human experts to contain high-quality spiritual qi.

So they have to take another path, constantly expanding their bodies to contain enough spiritual qi, thereby pushing their realm forward.

This is roughly the analysis and research on demon beast body size.

Over time, this has also become a basis for cultivators to judge demon beast realms.

But there are exceptions!

Those conclusions mostly apply only to land beasts and sea monsters.

They are not universally applicable to flying birds, or even some exotic species.

Take the No-Light Beast, for example. It is both a flying bird and an exotic species, so its revealed true body after death was less than a hundred *zhang*.

Yet even so, no one present doubted the truth of Luo Chen's words.

That method of attracting surrounding free-floating heaven and earth spiritual qi, unique to beings at the Sage level, could absolutely not be faked.

At this moment!

Seeing Luo Chen take out the No-Light Beast corpse, a relatively complete one at that, everyone's hearts surged and their gazes flickered.

This Alchemy Sect Master did not use his most proud pills to lead the discussion, but instead showed off a demon emperor's remains. What was his intention?

To display military force?

One must say, this demon emperor's remains gave many people a great shock.

Among human Sages, many have defeated demon emperors.

Not a few have killed demon emperors.

But someone like Luo Chen, who could kill a demon emperor and still harvest a complete corpse, was extremely rare.

In the hundred-year human-demon war, the Absolute Spirit Battle was definitely the biggest commotion.

But in that battle, besides Luo Chen, no one else harvested a complete demon emperor corpse.

Only Iron View Daoist, with that Mystic Iron Dao Temple true artifact, obtained half a corpse.

The gap revealed here was Luo Chen's combat strength!

He was not a weak high-level alchemist, but a Sage with formidable strength!

Luo Chen calmly swept his gaze over everyone's expressions.

The batch of Sages from Lingguan Pass were relatively calm about this, after all, he had previously achieved the feat of harvesting the complete corpse of the Frost Eagle Demon Emperor.

But the new batch that had arrived in the past five years, these Sages from ancient Eastern Wilderness sects, were somewhat unsettled.

No matter how good their cultivation of composure, facing a demon emperor's corpse directly, they more or less lost their calm.

Luo Chen was very satisfied with this reaction!

This way, if others wanted to ask him for help in the future, they would have to weigh whether the compensation they offered was sufficient.

Asking an alchemist to take action already required personal favors.

And asking a powerful expert for help required even higher remuneration.

He had quietly raised the threshold for future pill refining requests, so that random people wouldn't come bothering him endlessly.

And there was another point in his consideration.

"These cultivators from ancient sects all look down on the newly promoted experts from the Eastern Wilderness's rising forces. I possess astonishing alchemy skills, so it's hard to guarantee that no one will harbor ill intentions, whether coercion or force, trying to get benefits from me."

"A demon emperor's corpse is enough to dispel those improper thoughts!"

As he thought, after the bloody No-Light Beast corpse appeared, the face of the Celestial Master from the Void Emperor Sect, who had previously taken the initiative to befriend him, changed several times.

The Elder Lu from Dongyang Sect sitting beside him also gripped his chair tightly, looking warily at Luo Chen.

And that Medicine Master's face was now pale, instantly realizing the huge gap between himself and Luo Chen in another aspect.

The atmosphere in the hall seemed to fall silent.

Luo Chen suddenly chuckled lightly, "What, do you all look down on this demon emperor corpse? Or do you not recognize the value of this beast?"

"Fine, I'll sing its praises myself!"

"The eyes of this No-Light Beast can distinguish the dark and turbid, and can discern life and death."

"These pitch-black wings may not have top-tier flying speed, but they possess the effect of concealment and stealth, flying silently without revealing spiritual light. If a skilled craftsman uses them as the main material to refine a treasure, it should be a fine esoteric magic treasure."

"Especially this scorpion tail stinger! It has already been tempered by the Transformation Tribulation lightning and has true artifact quality. If combined with the deadly poison from the No-Light Beast's venom sac, its killing power can be considered top-grade among true artifacts. Don't laugh, everyone, but I actually suffered a loss under this scorpion tail stinger back then, piercing through my protective treasure."

"As for this flesh and blood, if a spiritual chef uses a secret method to neutralize its attached toxicity, body refiners who consume it long-term might gain considerable poison resistance."

This self-praise fell on the ears of the crowd like an inventory of treasures.

Qin Baixiang of the Myriad Beasts Sect was the first to speak up, "I never thought the Alchemy Sect Master would be so knowledgeable about the characteristics of demon beast corpses. Truly broad-minded!"

Luo Chen smiled without speaking. He did have a "Seven Star Demon Hunter" license in the Northern Sea, after all, and had once crammed this kind of knowledge.

Nine Spirit Primus also chimed in, but the point of praise took a different angle.

"Brother Luo understanding these materials is one thing, but he is also so clear about what kind of magic treasure each part can be refined into. Could it be that Brother Luo is also skilled in the path of artifact forging?"

Luo Chen nodded modestly, "A little, a little!"

He had praised himself, and others praising him now undoubtedly certified the truth of his words.

Since that was the case, there was no more delay.

Soon, a group of Sages participated in the feast of dividing this demon emperor's remains.

Nine Spirit Primus made the first move, buying the most precious scorpion tail stinger and venom sac of the No-Light Beast!

For this, he paid a full thirty strands of the *xiao* qi that Luo Chen needed.

Luo Chen was somewhat surprised by his generosity. Hadn't this guy said before that he didn't have much *xiao* qi left?

As if guessing Luo Chen's thoughts, Nine Spirit Primus secretly transmitted an explanation:

"Last time I deceived Brother Luo, it was truly unavoidable. My Great Nine Heavens *Xiao* River itself needed a large amount of *xiao* qi, and now that it's refined, there is this surplus."

"After the battle with the Thunder Prison Divine Peng, I felt I was lacking in external objects. But now, it's too late to refine a suitable true artifact. This scorpion tail stinger already has true artifact quality, and with a little sacrifice and refinement, it might supplement me somewhat."

Luo Chen nodded slightly, accepting this explanation, and did not blame the other party for his previous concealment.

Instead, he realized that experts who could make a name for themselves had wealth far beyond ordinary imagination.

It seemed his previous prices had been a bit low!

Next, Xiao Shiniang, the Lord of Fireworks Ancient Island, took out five strands of Crimson *Xiao* Qi and bought the pair of bat-like wings.

Her combat style was somewhat similar to the No-Light Beast's.

If she could supplement it with these wings, her strength might be greatly enhanced.

However, how to use these wings would require finding a master refiner.

Qin Baixiang was the third to act, using ten strands of Red *Xiao* Qi to buy all the No-Light Beast's flesh and blood.

Thus, the main parts were gone, leaving only the "scraps."

But these scraps, being from a demon emperor, were still of considerable value.

"These No-Light Beast eyes, let Fellow Daoist Lu have them!"

"Alchemy Sect Master, five strands of Purple *Xiao* Qi, is that acceptable?"

Elder Lu said with a smile.

Luo Chen did not haggle and nodded with a smile as well.

In his heart, however, he was extremely excited!

Counting the eleven strands of *xiao* qi he had already refined, combined with today's gains, it had reached a hundred strands!

As long as he refined them all, he could reach the intermediate stage of the *Thousand Skies Sword Canon*!

And the surprises were not over yet.

"That large tendon, this Celestial Master wants it!"

"It's a pity this beast's hide is gone, otherwise I would have wanted to make a dharma robe from it. I'll take this pair of claws, go back and refine a magic treasure for my grandson; he should be pleased."

"Such a well-preserved heart? If refined with my sect's secret method, steeped in a thousand gold brew for a hundred years, it would definitely be top-tier spiritual wine. Fellow Daoists, let this old man have this item!"

"Old Deng wants the skull. I've heard the No-Light Beast has soul-confusing power; perhaps this skull can help resolve some confusion in Old Deng's cultivation."

One by one, the Sages quickly acted, each spending different prices to buy the materials of the No-Light Beast.

In less than half an incense stick's time, the corpse of the No-Light Demon Emperor, once famous in the wilderness, was divided cleanly among the crowd.

If this matter reached the demon clan's headquarters, it would surely cause great fury.

But such was the cultivation world!

The strong prey on the weak, survival of the fittest—where was the cruelty?

The flesh and blood of Initiate cultivators, the golden cores of Adepts—these were also great supplements for demon beasts!

Back when Luo Chen was at Green Parasol Mountain, he had seen Nether Spring using Heaven Smelter's Sage infant as the main material for a Transformation Pill.

Even setting aside the racial difference between humans and demons, within the human race itself, weren't there practices of taking souls to refine evil demonic techniques?

For the sake of cultivation, everything can be nourishment!

After Luo Chen threw out that huge "brick," it was time for others to bring out resources for trade.

Before encountering anything he wanted, he did not participate, only observing from the side.

In his heart, he was overjoyed at the dozens of strands of *xiao* qi that had successively come into his hands.

These old sects truly had deep foundations.

The *xiao* qi that had been hard to find before was now as common as cabbage, available everywhere!

"Right, once one achieves Sage status, who doesn't want to go up to the Nine Heavens?"

"Who doesn't want to know what lies beyond the world? Isn't the Immortal Realm just a step away from us?"

"Once in the Nine Heavens, in the blue void, materials like *xiao* qi can be gathered with some time and effort. Over time, a large stock of various *xiao* qi accumulates in the world."

Luo Chen thought this, his gaze casually glancing at Elder Lu beside him.

It was said that the Dongyang Sect had a method for controlling divine thunder called Purple *Xiao* Divine Thunder.

Divine thunder was hard to control, and using *xiao* qi to control it, judging by the name, the *xiao* qi used was Purple *Xiao* Qi.

Perhaps the Dongyang Sect had a large amount of Purple *Xiao* Qi stored internally!

The *Thousand Skies Sword Canon* he practiced, based on his physique and mana, was suited for Red *Xiao* Sword Qi. Purple *Xiao* Qi and Crimson *Xiao* Qi could also be converted into Red *Xiao* Sword Qi. If he could obtain a large amount of Purple *Xiao* Qi from the Dongyang Sect, he might not have to worry about these resources in the future.

While Luo Chen was thinking, Deng Taiyue of Mount Tai Sect below took out an item.

"Seven Souls Jade, a fourth-order ore, has the effect of fusing spirits and attracting souls. Suitable for making sect soul plaques, sensing the life and death of disciples and clan members."

"Old Deng wants to exchange it for some resources beneficial to the soul. Is any fellow Daoist interested?"

As soon as these words were spoken, they immediately attracted the interest of many.

Most top forces put great effort into cultivating their disciples and followers.

Especially those with outstanding talent, who often have a lot of resources poured into them.

If such excellent juniors fell, even Sage cultivators would feel heartache.

Therefore, top forces often make soul plaques, soul lamps, or use various secret techniques to sense them.

Seven Souls Jade is one of the best materials for making soul plaques.

Not only does it have excellent sensing effects, but it also requires very little soul blood, not damaging the soul foundation of talented disciples.

Unfortunately, over the years, the existing Seven Souls Jade in the thirty-six domains of the Eastern Wilderness had long been mined clean. Few major forces could make soul plaques anymore, having to settle for ordinary soul lamp methods.

And while the soul lamp method was common, the damage to the disciples' souls was obvious.

To keep a soul lamp burning, it required relatively more soul blood, and the cultivator had to replenish it from time to time—very troublesome in short.

Now that Seven Souls Jade had appeared, these Sages immediately became interested.

Elder Lu was the first to speak, "Taiyue Sage, was this Seven Souls Jade also mined from the Misty Marsh of the Million Great Mountains?"

Deng Taiyue did not mince words and said frankly, "Indeed. Ten years ago, while the demon beasts were pouring out and the Million Great Mountains were empty, I entered the mountains to gather herbs. Besides harvesting that demon emperor cub, this Seven Souls Jade was my biggest gain. Unfortunately, the quantity is limited, only enough to make about a hundred soul plaques."

As he said this, he patted his storage bag, indicating that besides the piece in his hand, there was more left.

When the others heard the number "a hundred," they were somewhat disappointed.

These ancient sects had thousands upon thousands of core direct disciples under them; a mere hundred was far from enough.

And what Deng Taiyue sought was resources beneficial to the soul!

If they had such good things, why would they exchange them for Seven Souls Jade, a resource that only worked on low-level cultivators?

For a moment, the Sages who had been interested earlier all fell silent.

But the long-silent Medicine Master seemed somewhat tempted.

The Seven Ridges Mountain had long declined, with not many disciples and even fewer outstanding ones.

This batch of Seven Souls Jade, if taken back, would be enough for this generation.

"Cough, cough!"

After coughing to draw everyone's attention, the Medicine Master slowly said, "Deng Taiyue, how about selling this batch of Seven Souls Jade to this old man?"

Hearing him addressed directly by name, Deng Taiyue, though used to it, still frowned in public.

"Medicine Master, what do you have to exchange?"

Without hesitation, the Medicine Master said, "I have been studying that Enlightenment Pill remnant formula for you for a long time. If it is really refined, it will be worth more than thousands of soul-beneficial pills."

Hearing this, Deng Taiyue's brow furrowed even tighter.

"If it is really refined"—that meant it hadn't been refined yet. Wasn't this the same as before, trying to get something for nothing?

Luo Chen blinked, and seeing Elder Lu beside him smiling as if watching a good show, he transmitted a message to ask, "What is an Enlightenment Pill?"

Elder Lu was stunned for a moment and subconsciously asked back, "The Alchemy Sect Master does not know the Enlightenment Pill?"

Then, he immediately remembered that Luo Chen had been cultivating for less than three hundred years, so it was normal for him not to know some things.

He quickly explained, "The Enlightenment Pill is a pill formula accidentally researched by an ancient qi cultivator. Although it is fourth-order, it is known as a pseudo fifth-order divine pill. Its effect is to help Sage cultivators receive sudden enlightenment, allowing them to comprehend the true meaning of laws in advance."

"Unfortunately, such a divine pill was bound to be envied by heaven. After that ancient qi cultivator used this pill to advance to Ascendant and ascend, the remaining pill formula was fought over by major forces and torn to pieces."

"Over the past few thousand years, even though Eastern Wilderness cultivators pieced it together, they could at most reconstruct fifty to sixty percent of the formula, still lacking the name of the most important main ingredient."

"After spending a huge price to research and refine it without success, over time, no one bothered with this pill formula anymore."

"I have heard that Alchemy Saint Chu Yan was once interested in this remnant formula and studied it for many years. But before any results were seen, she went far away to the Central Continent, and I don't know how her research turned out."

"I didn't expect Taiyue Sage to still be so persistent about this pill, constantly seeking people to refine it."

Having said this, Elder Lu suddenly remembered something.

"With the Alchemy Sect Master's ability, perhaps you could restore this formula?"

Luo Chen laughed dryly.

He waved his hand, "Even the Alchemy Saint has not produced results, so how could I have such means? However…"

Luo Chen's gaze narrowed, falling on the Seven Souls Jade.

This thing was one of the most suitable materials for refining the Heart-Inquiring Mirror!

He spoke up.

"Taiyue Sage, how about selling this item to me?"
