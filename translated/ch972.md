# Chapter 972: Soulsever Cliff, Netherspring Sea, Primeval Wasteland

*Whoosh!*

Like a blade-split black ridge, the mountain slanted across the land. No wind, no clouds. Night hung low.

Only the thick, wall-like giant waves rolled in from afar, crashing against the cliff base with a deafening roar.

Again and again they struck, but the black cliff stood towering and unmoving.

The murky yellow waves seemed helpless against it. After countless futile assaults, they gradually receded.

As the tide's roar weakened like a trapped beast's panting, the scenery at the cliff's base gradually revealed itself.

Smooth, polished sand and gravel lay scattered along the shore like pearls. Bones of unknown demon beasts were strewn everywhere.

A wind rose, blowing a skull across the soft coastline. It rolled *clatter-clatter* until it crashed into a charred black corpse.

*Bam!*

The corpse, once entirely blackened, suddenly bloomed with a wisp of cyan-white flame.

The skull, already covered in cracks, instantly turned to powder.

And the cyan-white flame, as if exhausted of all strength, sputtered out and dissipated into the corpse.

"Hmph..."

A muffled groan suddenly sounded. The corpse moved eerily!

Its wrist turned, groping on the ground for a moment. Then, with difficulty, it exerted force to flip itself over.

That single motion seemed to have used up all its strength.

*Shua!*

A pair of eyes snapped open. What met them was pitch black—no moon, no stars.

"Where have I ended up?"

Luo Chen's lips moved with difficulty, but no sound came out. Only his heart murmured.

He tried to spread his divine sense, but for some reason, it could only extend about a hundred zhang before encountering immense suppression, unable to probe further.

This suppression was even stronger than when the Yin-Yang Remnant Realm had deployed the Yellow Wind Formation!

Unable to seek outward, Luo Chen had no choice but to look inward.

The moment he did, his heart sank.

Terrible!

Very terrible!

Even worse than when he had left the Southern Frontier!

His Nascent Soul was withered to the extreme. The little figure couldn't even open its eyes, as if it would collapse at the slightest movement.

Throughout his body—meridians, acupoints—not a trace of dharma power could be found.

If that were all, it might be bearable. But even his most relied-upon physical body was now nearly depleted.

Source power was exhausted. Large stretches of meridians had cracked. His rock-hard bones were covered in crack-like patterns. Even the Hundred Poison King Gu sleeping in his stomach had fallen into deep slumber.

And all of this was thanks to that turbid yellow wave!

Luo Chen gradually recalled what had happened.

After he had used all his strength to throw the slow-moving Black King out, the wave had come too fast, sweeping him up in an instant.

He had thought he could erupt with full power and break free.

But unexpectedly, beneath that turbid yellow wave, his dharma power had inexplicably dissipated, as if absorbed by the seawater.

Forced into a corner, Luo Chen had repeatedly unleashed other means.

The Nirvana Sacred Flame burned everything, but in the boundless turbid yellow waves, it was like a drop in the bucket.

Sword qi surged. The Yuantu Sword tried to cut a bloody path, but each slash felt like striking empty air.

He tried to cross the yellow sea with his physical body, but inside it, he had no leverage and couldn't distinguish direction.

Worse, the turbid yellow wave seemed capable of dissolving everything, constantly eroding Luo Chen's flesh.

Forced to, Luo Chen continuously activated source power to protect his body.

Even that wasn't enough!

Luo Chen directly used his greatest trump card—the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire!

He had to admit, compared to the still-weak and incompletely transformed Nirvana Sacred Flame, this fifth-rank divine fire, which he had spent nearly three hundred years painstakingly cultivating, did not disappoint his expectations.

By recklessly consuming dharma power to drive the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire, he managed to withstand the erosion of the turbid yellow wave time and again.

He held on until the end.

But the price was complete depletion of dharma power.

And his physical body, due to overexertion of the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire, had suffered severe backlash.

Not only that...

Luo Chen looked inward at his Purple Mansion. The once verdant fire tree now hung limp and lifeless, devoid of any vitality.

He communicated with the fire spirit, but got almost no response.

Luo Chen understood. At the very end, without collapsing his Nascent Soul, his dharma power had been insufficient to sustain the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire. The fire spirit itself had exhausted its reserves to protect a sliver of his life force.

This was the fire spirit's automatic master-protection act.

"Here I am, a dignified Pill Sage, looking down on my peers, slaying powerful enemies time and again, with even great cultivators and demon emperors unable to do anything to me, and even a severely wounded Ascendant dying by my sword. Yet I never imagined that a single wave would nearly kill me."

"Haha..."

A bitter laugh rasped from Luo Chen's throat.

The sound scraped out like sandpaper rubbing against his throat, whimpering endlessly in the night.

It sounded miserable, but actually, it was good news—it meant Luo Chen was regaining control of his body.

With the immediate danger past, Luo Chen briefly assessed his condition and began to ponder his current situation.

"That turbid yellow wave... it seems familiar?"

He gathered his scattered thoughts, searching through his complex memories, ignoring the pain in his head, determined to find where that familiarity came from.

But no matter how he searched, there was no trace.

In the end, Luo Chen had to give up.

"I wonder if Black King managed to escape. I hope he wasn't swept in."

Luo Chen sighed inwardly, stopped wasting energy on futile thoughts, and calmed his heart. He began carefully mobilizing the dharma power of his Nascent Soul's main body.

His Nascent Soul was now like a porcelain doll.

If he wasn't careful, it might shatter and collapse.

If that happened, the vast dharma power constituting the Nascent Soul would surge out. With his current fragile body, he would probably be blown apart.

Fortunately, Luo Chen didn't need much dharma power.

Just a tiny bit!

Just enough to open his storage ring.

A flash of light at his fingertip, and a pill was taken out, gently pushed into his mouth.

*Gulp.*

After swallowing the pill, Luo Chen closed his eyes.

He didn't actively refine it, letting the pill's strands of medicinal power slowly nourish his body.

Time passed bit by bit.

The fading sound of the tide seemed to gradually return to his ears.

Luo Chen dared not imagine what would happen if the turbid yellow wave swept back in this state.

As dawn approached.

*Crack-crack-crack* sounds came from Luo Chen's body.

Large patches of charred black skin peeled off.

Luo Chen sat up with a start.

"Hoo..."

A turbid breath exhaled. Naked, Luo Chen didn't even have time to be glad that the Mystic Dust Armor wasn't on him. He instinctively scrambled backward a few steps.

*Splash!*

The turbid yellow wave surged, hitting the soft sand where he had just been lying.

Tide in, tide out. Luo Chen's heart calmed slightly.

After a night of recovery, his powerful physical body, relying on astonishing vitality and self-healing, had finally squeezed out some source power, giving him mobility now.

He stared fearfully at the turbid yellow sea surface not far away, which seemed capable of devouring everything. The sense of familiarity rose again in his heart.

"Where have I seen this before? Or which ancient text described such a scene?"

"Never mind that. First, leave this place!"

He tapped his ear. The Hundun Staff materialized. Leaning on it like a crutch, Luo Chen stood up with difficulty.

He didn't look at the turbid yellow sea again. Turning around—

In one glance, the blade-split black cliff slammed into his eyes.

In that instant, Luo Chen remembered everything.

He seemed unable to believe it. He turned back to look at the turbid yellow sea, his lips moving as he painfully uttered two names.

"Netherspring Sea!"

"Soulsever Cliff!"

*Whoosh...*

Sea breeze blew. The man, simply draped in a hemp robe, stood alone leaning on his stone staff, appearing so tiny between heaven and earth.

---

On a rugged mountain path, Luo Chen trudged alone.

Information about this Northern Sea forbidden land flowed like a trickling stream through his mind.

The Northern Sea was vast, with countless dangerous places.

In humanity's repeated expansions, many dangerous places had been conquered, becoming protective barriers for major sects.

Such as the Arctic Yemo Heaven, or the Endless Valley.

Even the life-repelling Purple Spirit Island had been conquered by Luo Chen, becoming a medicine garden for cultivating Purple Monkey Flowers.

But there were still many places that cultivators could not easily reach, or could only explore at great cost.

For example, the Fallen Sea that Luo Chen had visited, or this Soulsever Cliff before him!

Legend had it that Soulsever Cliff was a forbidden land deep in the Demon Sea. It was discovered by a wandering cultivator in the early years. Later, the Northern Sea holy land Primordial Demon Sect organized a group of Sages and Adepts to go there, but most fell within it, earning it the name "Soulsever."

After that, the Primordial Demon Sect's Soul-Refining Ancestor personally went, exploring Soulsever Cliff and reaping a great harvest.

But he only explored it once. He never set foot there again, until the Primordial Demon Sect was destroyed.

Such a forbidden land naturally left a deep impression on Luo Chen.

He had even seen it once before.

The reason he couldn't recall it last night was that the witnessing wasn't done by his main body. To escape the Moon Wanderer's pursuit, he had used the divided soul method, seeing it only through a puppet's eyes.

Later, after forming his Nascent Soul on Longyuan Island, Luo Chen had once tried to go against the current to see the scenery of Netherspring Sea and Soulsever Cliff.

Unfortunately, on the return journey, he found nothing.

That was also when Luo Chen understood that the Netherspring Sea was not fixed in one place, and Soulsever Cliff moved with it.

This was a walking forbidden land!

"Could it be that I crashed into the moving Soulsever Cliff and was swept into the Netherspring Sea?"

Luo Chen, halfway up the black cliff, caught his breath. Looking down, he revealed a bitter smile.

Was he that unlucky!

But the only explanation was probably this.

A still Netherspring Sea could at most be ignored. If prepared, one could avoid it.

But if the Netherspring Sea was in motion, its speed was unpredictable.

Especially since Black King, following Luo Chen's orders, had been swimming extremely fast and had directly crashed into it.

There was another doubt. According to Luo Chen's understanding, Soulsever Cliff was extremely eerie. Once living beings entered, their souls would become muddled and unable to resist, letting the Netherspring Sea devour them.

But although he couldn't break free at the time, he had indeed been able to resist somewhat.

"Is it because my soul foundation is strong? Or because the Prison-Suppressing Soul Art was constantly active, preserving this thread of life?"

Luo Chen didn't think he was that formidable.

Such an eerie place—even Ascendant greats dared not personally enter. His soul foundation alone could never have lasted so long.

He couldn't figure it out!

Luo Chen looked again at the boundless murky yellow sea surface in the distance and sighed deeply.

Before ascending, Divine Lord Youli had warned him that a great calamity would come unexpectedly in the future.

Could this be it—crashing into the Netherspring Sea, soul returning to Soulsever Cliff?

This was too unexpected!

He hadn't even had the chance to activate the calamity gu aura lingering on the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

"First, find a safe place and recover my condition!"

Stepping on the hard mountain rock, Luo Chen continued advancing away from the Netherspring Sea.

---

When night fell again, the tide sounds gradually faded from his ears.

Luo Chen stopped in a cave formed by a depression in a giant rock.

This place was good. Sheltered from wind and rain, with excellent visibility to observe the distant sea surface.

The only drawback was probably the lack of spiritual qi!

But that wasn't a major flaw, because in a day of walking, Luo Chen hadn't found any place with a spirit vein on this island.

He would have to make do.

With a thought, bottles and jars appeared before Luo Chen—all his accumulated pills over the years.

Most were of the highest grade!

Ordinary pills had long been consumed in daily cultivation.

Only these most precious pills were worthy of being kept by his side.

"I wonder if they're enough to restore my dharma power?"

Luo Chen muttered, a hint of worry in his eyes.

When crossing the Southern Ocean earlier, he had consumed many reserves to restore his dharma power.

Back then, there was at least some free-floating heaven-earth spiritual qi on the Southern Ocean. But here, it was like a spirit-void land—nothing at all. He could only rely on pills.

And it wasn't just about restoring dharma power. He also had to heal his body to generate source power for emergencies.

Although he hadn't seen any spirit veins or living creatures along the way, who knew if something unexpected might happen?

Only by returning to his peak state could Luo Chen's heart be at ease, and could he have the strength to find a way to leave this place.

But...

"Can I leave?"

Staring at the pitch-black sea surface, Luo Chen's heart sank without end.

---

Without spirit veins, restoring dharma power was extremely difficult.

Especially since Luo Chen also needed to allocate some dharma power to repair his physical body, making it even slower.

Time passed quietly. Unknowingly, several months went by.

One day, Luo Chen rose and left the cave dwelling.

Standing atop Soulsever Cliff, he gazed at the distant murky yellow sea surface.

Faint dharma power fluctuations lingered around him. His overall spirit and energy were quite good, indicating that months of painstaking effort had not been wasted.

But Luo Chen still dared not be careless in the slightest.

Looking into the distance, he pondered deeply.

These past months, he hadn't just been cultivating behind closed doors. He had also been conducting experiments from time to time.

Soulsever Cliff wasn't tall—about three thousand zhang. Where he was located was only halfway up the mountain, on the side facing away from the sun.

This island wasn't large either—about eight hundred li in length and width.

Thus, within this small range, Soulsever Cliff stood as a solitary peak, quite conspicuous.

But the Netherspring Sea that spread out was extremely vast!

Even with Luo Chen's Crimson Eye Golden Gaze, which could see thousands of li, he couldn't find its edge.

Moreover, through his experiments, he had indeed discovered that the island beneath his feet, and even the sea area, had been moving.

Although the speed was sometimes fast, sometimes slow, occasionally pausing, it was indeed moving.

Based on Luo Chen's judgment, it was currently stationary near the Southern Frontier, having not moved for a long time.

Additionally, there were several other exploration results.

Namely, not only was there no spiritual qi here, but even when activating the true meaning of laws, he couldn't draw in heaven-earth origin qi.

This discovery made Luo Chen horrified!

Heaven-earth origin qi was omnipresent, unless one left this world.

And without leaving, being unable to draw in heaven-earth origin qi meant what?

Luo Chen only needed to think briefly to arrive at the answer.

There was someone far stronger than him, blocking his connection with heaven and earth. That was why the true meaning of laws was ineffective.

What kind of strength could far surpass his?

Ascendant?

Even ordinary Ascendant greats probably couldn't achieve such a feat.

Only existences like Divine Lord Youli, who was infinitely close to the Void Refining stage, could do so.

Combined with Soulsever Cliff's constant movement, Luo Chen formed a guess in his heart.

"Divine Lord Youli once said that this realm cannot break through to Void Refining. Only the Mountains and Seas Realm's native primeval wastelands are unrestricted."

"If this island, this Soulsever Cliff, and even this Netherspring Sea are all manifestations of a primeval wasteland, then everything can be explained."

"Just like the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven in the Central Continent!"

"But is this primeval wasteland alive or dead?"

Luo Chen took a deep breath, slowly withdrew his gaze, and planned to return to continue his remaining cultivation.

However, as his gaze passed over the cliff base, it paused for a moment.

After the tide receded, the soft black sand faintly shimmered with strange light.

(Chapter End)
