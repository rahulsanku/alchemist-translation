# Chapter 974: The Dao Sect's Ambition, Unifying Mountains and Seas

Time flowed slowly.

On this Soul-Severing Cliff, devoid of any life or trace of spiritual energy, Luo Chen silently counted the passage of time.

In the fifth year, he had split off his seventh soul.

In the sixth year, the *Soul-Splitting Art*, which he had painstakingly cultivated for over a century, finally reached great completion. Yet faintly, he sensed that the fully mastered *Soul-Splitting Art* had some flaw. Where exactly, he could not tell. He suspected it was related to the gaps in Han Zhan's memories.

Regarding this, Luo Chen did not delve deeper.

He was well aware that from the moment he obtained this technique, the system had marked it as an incomplete version.

Moreover, because its level was too high, it could not be naturally completed merely by increasing proficiency.

To complete it, he would either need to invest a massive amount of achievement points or find some auxiliary treasure that even Han Zhan had not known about.

"My goal is nine split souls, breaking through the Sage realm's soul limit. For now, the *Soul-Splitting Art* is sufficient!"

When the seventh year arrived, Luo Chen immediately plunged into splitting the eighth soul.

The soul-tempering principle of the *Soul-Splitting Art* stemmed from quantitative change leading to qualitative change. When the main soul could no longer be improved, a large number of split souls would elevate it, thereby driving overall enhancement.

Thus, the more split souls, the better.

This was evident from the fact that Han Zhan had split off nine souls early on.

If Luo Chen had not been burdened by mundane affairs in his early years, he too would have reached that step long ago.

But now? It was not too late!

Especially with the aid of soul stones everywhere, it was twice the result with half the effort.

If Han Zhan were still alive and saw him cultivating the *Soul-Splitting Art* like this, his eyes would probably be red with envy.

Tonight.

The main soul was round and full, the split souls were vibrant and lively. Even the seventh soul, split off only last year, had grown to the mid-Sage level after a year of continuous soul stone nurturing.

It was time to split the eighth soul.

Luo Chen guarded his mind, transformed his divine sense into a blade, and began cutting the main soul.

However, the process he had grown accustomed to seemed somewhat difficult tonight.

The suppressed sound of pain lasted for a very, very long time before finally stopping.

In a daze, three days had passed.

Luo Chen's face was pale, his mind exhausted, as he looked at the newly born eighth split soul in his sea of consciousness.

"How can this be?"

"The further I go, the harder it gets. This differs from what the technique records."

"Could it be that this technique must be practiced according to Han Zhan's method—splitting the nine souls as early as possible?"

Luo Chen was utterly perplexed.

He did not think the difference between the two approaches was significant, especially since he had pushed the proficiency of the *Soul-Splitting Art* to great completion. Logically, it should have been easier.

Even if there were bottlenecks, they should not appear at the eighth or ninth soul.

He could not figure it out!

Luo Chen could only absorb wisps of green smoke to recover from his current weakened state.

Suddenly!

*Hum...*

A muffled sound arose, then continued without end.

The sound was very faint, yet it reached deep into his soul. Luo Chen, not understanding why, walked out of his cave dwelling.

As far as the eye could see, the sky was high and the earth distant, clouds and seas churning.

That stretch of Yellow Spring Sea surged violently, rolling outward.

Seeing this scene, a thought instinctively popped into Luo Chen's mind.

"It's moving again!"

In recent years, the Yellow Spring Sea had been relatively calm.

According to Luo Chen's inference, it had been staying near the Southern Frontier, hidden within the Southern Ocean.

But this current commotion was somewhat similar to when he had first encountered the Yellow Spring Sea.

"And this time, where will it drift to?"

Luo Chen murmured to himself.

But this murmuring gradually became grave.

His already pale face, from having just split the eighth soul, grew even more uncertain.

His gaze moved from the Soul-Severing Cliff beneath his feet, to the sandy beach covered in black stones that hardly seemed to diminish, and finally through the vast Yellow Spring Sea into the distance. His lips pressed tightly together.

"Could it be..."

A guess suddenly leaped into his mind!

If the Soul-Severing Cliff and even this Yellow Spring Sea were all transformed from a top-tier archaic beast, then where did the countless soul stones come from?

Earlier, he thought they had been washed in by the Yellow Spring Sea. Later, he discovered they were actually washed down from the Soul-Severing Cliff.

Yet, in this day-after-day washing, the Soul-Severing Cliff had not collapsed, and the soul stone beach had not diminished.

Then what about the massive amount of soul stones he had consumed over these seven years?

"Created from nothing? Impossible!"

"There must be an additional source of replenishment!"

Luo Chen connected all the intelligence he had about this forbidden land, the Soul-Severing Cliff, and reached an astonishing conclusion.

If this thing truly was an archaic beast, then its favorite food must be the souls of living beings!

And not just one or two souls, but the ubiquitous souls of living beings permeating heaven and earth.

Wherever there were more, it would automatically drift there, stay for a long time, slowly absorb them, and transform them into part of the Soul-Severing Cliff.

Among the five continents of the Mountains and Seas Realm, the Northern Sea had the vastest territory, and the endless Demon Sea was always filled with battles and slaughter. Thus, the Soul-Severing Cliff had always remained deep within the Demon Sea.

With the destruction of the Primordial Demon Sect, the three great powers of the Northern Sea fought among themselves, and even the human-demon war and the Dao Sect's invasion of the Northern Sea, the souls of the war dead grew ever more numerous. Therefore, the Soul-Severing Cliff gradually left the depths of the Demon Sea and came to the edge of human territory.

That was how he had accidentally encountered it back then.

And the reason the Soul-Severing Cliff had come near the Southern Ocean, close to the Southern Frontier, was clearly attracted by the great battle of the Life and Death Gate.

When Nether Departure Divine Lord ascended, he killed a million cultivators in a single strike. An unimaginable number of fresh souls filled the Southern Frontier's cultivation world, unable to dissipate for a time.

So, the Soul-Severing Cliff came.

Now, six years had passed, and the seventh year had arrived.

If there were no more major battles in the Southern Frontier, then the Soul-Severing Cliff should have finished its meal.

Its departure now must be because there was a place more attractive to it.

"Where has another war erupted comparable to the Northern Sea demon chaos or the Southern Frontier great battle?"

"Or is one about to happen, or has already happened?"

Several names surfaced in Luo Chen's mind, finally settling on the most familiar one: "Eastern Wilderness."

In recent years, battles in the Eastern Wilderness had been frequent. How many millions had died unjustly?

Moreover, with the destruction of the Nether Origin Sect, that Nether Abyss could no longer protect the Eastern Wilderness's ocean territories. The Soul-Severing Cliff might be heading toward the Eastern Wilderness.

If that were the case...

Hope gradually rose in Luo Chen's eyes.

He had long discovered the pattern of the Yellow Spring Sea's tides. They changed not only with the cycle of day and night but also with the passage of overall time.

The further it went, the calmer it became.

It was like eating: at the beginning, one always gobbles ravenously, so the wind and waves are strong. Later, when full, it is no longer so intense.

If he wanted to leave, he would have to take advantage of the time when the wind and waves were strong, swallowing everything!

With this thought, Luo Chen made a secret decision.

"Before that day arrives, I must split off the ninth soul and prepare everything!"

...

White light flickered in the sky.

A moment later, the sound of muffled thunder exploded in his ears.

On Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak of Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain, a figure leaned against the railing, gazing calmly at the dark clouds gathering in the nine heavens above.

Hurried footsteps sounded, and soon a figure walked behind the old man, bowing respectfully.

"Sect Master, everything is ready!"

Mo Shouzhuo, the Sect Master of the Tianyuan Dao Sect, nodded slightly and turned to enter one of the great halls on Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak.

In the empty hall, as he entered, four beams of light gradually lit up.

One of the beams, not yet stable, could not wait to speak.

"Elder Brother, why won't you let me re-enter the Southern Frontier?"

Mo Shouzhuo glanced at the hazy figure and said calmly, "The Life and Death Gate has gathered its forces. Can you break through?"

The one who had just spoken was choked.

The other three beams gradually stabilized, and a gentle woman's voice came from one of them.

"Junior Brother Liancheng, why be so impatient? Since Nether Departure has ascended, the Southern Frontier is merely masterless land. Let it struggle on for a few more days."

Liancheng said indignantly, "Without an Ascendant to preside over it, the Life and Death Gate can surely be broken. It's just a matter of spending more time."

Mo Shouzhuo retorted, "And you're not afraid of the means Nether Departure Divine Lord left behind before his ascension?"

Liancheng said hatefully, "He only used the vast spiritual opportunity drawn by his radiant ascension to defeat me in an instant. Now that he's gone, what fear is there of his leftover means?"

A hearty, aged laugh came from the side, but it was neither Sect Master Mo Shouzhuo nor the woman who had spoken earlier.

"Little Liancheng, don't underestimate the heroes of the world. Nether Departure Divine Lord's power is far beyond what you can imagine."

"Senior Brother Buxiu, why mock me?" Liancheng said angrily. "In the battle at the Life and Death Gate, your Zhan family also suffered heavy losses! Even Zhan Bingtao, whom you most favored to succeed you, has not yet recovered from his injuries. And Elder Brother Tan, that little girl of your Tan family—her corpse hasn't even been retrieved yet."

Zhan Buxiu's tone faltered, and he rebuked, "The Southern Frontier matter was your responsibility. Having made such a blunder, how dare you speak like this?"

Tan Fengyue, one of the Tianyuan Five Ancestors, could not help but sigh faintly.

"This campaign to conquer the four continents of Mountains and Seas was originally intended to train the younger generation. That little girl's death can only be said to be her misfortune, that she was fated to have this great calamity. But Nether Departure Divine Lord's ability truly exceeded imagination."

The discussion in the hall grew lively. Mo Shouzhuo seemed somewhat annoyed.

*Dong!*

He tapped the table with the back of his finger and said solemnly, "That Nether Departure Divine Lord would strike without regard for consequences was indeed unexpected. But it is also an indisputable fact that he has ascended and left this realm. In the short term, the Life and Death Gate cannot produce another Ascendant great power. The Southern Frontier falling into our Dao Sect's hands is merely a matter of time. Liancheng, be a little more patient. There will be a day when you return to the Southern Frontier."

"Hmph!" Liancheng snorted coldly. Though dissatisfied, he did not press further.

After all, the Tianyuan Dao Sect's campaign against the four continents had gone smoothly everywhere. Only his side had suffered a head-on blow.

But he was also very wronged!

Who would have thought that Nether Departure Divine Lord, whose lifespan was nearly exhausted and whose great limit was imminent, would suddenly ascend, and even forcibly strike to wipe out the Dao Sect's army despite the backlash?

Mo Shouzhuo suppressed this matter and began inquiring about the situation in other places.

"Junior Sister Chunyuan, how goes it in the Eastern Wilderness?"

The gentle woman's voice from before appeared again, clear and organized, as she recounted the situation in the Eastern Wilderness.

"The major and minor sects, under the threat of the demon army, have already submitted to us."

"Taking advantage of the great battle, I once spied from afar on Green Parasol Mountain. The heavenly tribulation has indeed been hanging there, undispersed for over a decade, terrifying to the extreme."

"Those three Dao friends of the Nether Origin were truly fierce. To still have such means before their deaths."

Mo Shouzhuo's expression relaxed slightly. "That is to say, that demon phoenix truly cannot make a move?"

Ancestor Chunyuan said softly, "Yes."

Mo Shouzhuo breathed a sigh of relief. "In that case, hasten the pace and unify the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world. But remember, our Dao Sect's reach is limited to the original human territory of the Eastern Wilderness. Neither the Million Mountains nor Green Parasol Mountain may be touched in the slightest."

In the hall, someone was puzzled.

"That demon phoenix was already heavily injured, and now suppressed by heavenly tribulation. Why such fear?"

The one who did not understand was Ancestor Liancheng.

Mo Shouzhuo waved his hand. "Junior Brother, you don't understand. The demon phoenix's original strength was still above Nether Departure Divine Lord's. Even with this realm's suppression and various constraints, it is not something we can casually provoke. If the Nether Origin Sect had not suddenly disappeared, our Tianyuan Dao Sect would never have set foot on Eastern Wilderness territory."

Ancestor Liancheng was uncertain. "If it is truly that powerful, would it not become a great enemy of our Dao Sect?"

"It is already an enemy." Mo Shouzhuo said calmly. "But she is an enemy of the main sect. In this Mountains and Seas Realm, if we do not provoke her, she had best not set foot on the Central Continent. Otherwise..."

A faint killing intent emerged in his words.

But this killing intent came and went quickly.

Mo Shouzhuo asked about the situation in the Northern Sea.

But Ancestor Chunyuan had not yet finished speaking.

"Elder Brother Sect Master, there are two people in the Eastern Wilderness that might require attention."

"Who?"

"One is the Demon Emperor Azure Frost, who possesses the true meaning of spatial laws!"

"I know this demon. Leave it be for now. Who is the other?"

"Luo Chen!"

"Oh?" Mo Shouzhuo's face showed surprise. "Is it that Luo Chen who made a splash in the five-hundred-year grand competition and later returned to the Eastern Wilderness to display his alchemy grandmaster skill?"

Ancestor Chunyuan hummed in acknowledgment. "It is that child. Originally, I only took notice of him because of my disciple Qinglan. But I discovered that, unlike your early judgment that he was already crippled, he has instead comprehended a fire origin that suits him."

At these words, the hall fell silent.

Everyone present was an Ascendant great power, and they also remembered the matter of the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven grand competition.

Luo Chen, who had made a splash, naturally was not forgotten.

But at that time, although Luo Chen had unexpectedly comprehended the true meaning of laws, any discerning person could see that his path had gone astray, and he had no hope of becoming an Ascendant in the future.

If not for the subsequent reports of his alchemy grandmaster reputation, especially the matter of him betraying the Tianyuan Dao Sect to join the Star Gate, which damaged the Dao Sect's reputation, they would not have remembered him so deeply.

But it was merely remembering.

Now, however, Ancestor Chunyuan said he had comprehended another true meaning of laws, and one that was very compatible—a fire origin. This was somewhat unexpected.

Mo Shouzhuo frowned for the first time. "Truly?"

Ancestor Chunyuan affirmed, "It cannot be false. I personally went to the ruins of the Five Elements Divine Sect to investigate. There are indeed traces of fire-attribute law true meaning remaining there. Moreover, Shi Ju arrived earlier and felt it more deeply."

That left no room for error.

Shi Ju, this junior, had also comprehended fire origin early. Under the mutual repulsion of the same nature, it was hard to mistake.

Mo Shouzhuo pondered for a moment, then slowly asked, "Where is he now?"

"I do not know!" Ancestor Chunyuan said, but quickly added, "I have heard that a new powerful body cultivator has emerged in the Southern Frontier. I suspect it is Luo Chen in disguise, because he once displayed an extremely high level of body refinement, contending with and defeating a great demon emperor!"

Ancestor Liancheng said in surprise, "The Overlord Cultivator Qitian?"

"Yes! Have you seen him, Junior Brother Liancheng?"

"I have not seen him, but according to Zhan Bingtao, the Death Gate was very likely pushed open by this person."

Everyone was surprised. To be able to push open the Death Gate, this body refinement skill must have already reached an extremely high and profound level.

Mo Shouzhuo asked, "Where has he gone now?"

Ancestor Liancheng had nothing to say.

Mo Shouzhuo frowned. "Enough. If he hides his name for life, so be it. But as long as he appears, he will eventually return to our Dao Sect's fold. Let us discuss other matters first! Senior Brother Zhan, how goes the situation in the Northern Sea?"

Zhan Buxiu laughed heartily. "Everything is progressing smoothly. Li Canghai is cooperating with us very well. I have also exchanged blows with the Sea Emperor Whale and the Great Sage Beili, though only from afar. But those two demons are no match for me. The Northern Sea is settled. What remains is merely a matter for the younger generation."

Mo Shouzhuo's brow relaxed, and he asked about the situation in the Western Desert.

Tan Fengyue also gave good news.

"The Hanging Temple still has its mountain gate tightly closed. This gives us a good excuse to send troops. The only concern is that I only found traces of the Ancient Red Sand Scorpion. The Golden-Winged Great Peng who cooperated with him has mysteriously disappeared."

The leaders of the Western Desert demon chaos were mainly these two.

One was the ancient demon, the Ancient Red Sand Scorpion. The other was an archaic beast, the Golden-Winged Great Peng, who had once received Buddhist enlightenment.

Now, although one was mysteriously missing, for the Tianyuan Dao Sect, it was actually good news.

After understanding the situation of the four continents, Mo Shouzhuo gradually had a clear picture.

The Northern Sea was settled. The Southern Frontier was within reach. The Eastern Wilderness only sought the original human cultivation world. The Western Desert was about to send troops.

Fifty years had passed since the initial deployment.

If they wanted to completely conquer, it might take another fifty years.

Three thousand years of conserving strength, a hundred years of conquering the four continents.

After a hundred years of conquest, there would be a thousand or ten thousand years of governance.

Before his own ascension, he might be able to see the grand scene of the Dao Sect unifying the Mountains and Seas Realm.

He could not help but smile. "Gentlemen, counting the variables, before we hand over our burdens and ascend, we may truly achieve the unification of Mountains and Seas. Let us encourage each other!"

The hall immediately echoed with responses.

"Excellent!"

"Let us encourage each other!"

This divine sense meeting spanning continents ended with laughter and joy.

Mo Shouzhuo rubbed his brow, left the great hall, and paced outside again.

Breathing in the spiritual energy of Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak, which was richer than before, the smile on his lips never ceased.

Although there had been some surprises in the battles across the continents, with him commanding from the center and the running about of his four fellow disciples, everything was developing in a good direction.

Even the variables were under control.

The undertaking that predecessors had failed to accomplish would finally be completed in his generation.

When the time came, even ascending to the Mystic Realm and returning to the main sect would count as a great merit.

Gazing into the distance, Mo Shouzhuo calculated the approximate time in his heart, but gradually his expression darkened.

Why had that patch of dark clouds not dispersed for so long?

"Wrong!"

"Variables that can be controlled have never been variables!"

"The Dao Sect's great calamity has arrived!"
