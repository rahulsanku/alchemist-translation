# Chapter 783: Pleasing Others with Beauty, Pitying Oneself for Appearance; the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu is Poisonous, Connecting Hearts and Connecting Spirits!

"Decades ago, the Five Elements Divine Sect took us in and gave us a place to stay."

"But it wasn't as good as it seemed on the surface back then. There were demon beasts causing chaos outside, and disciples were divided and disloyal inside... As for Lingjun's matter, although time later revealed people's true hearts, it did bring quite a bit of negative influence at the time. Quite a few disciples who weren't firmly committed to the sect were lured away to other sects."

"With internal strife and external threats, us older generation experts were doing our best to raise our cultivation levels, so we could shield the younger generation from the wind and rain."

"At that time, besides Fu Jiusheng, Chu Kui, and Wang Yuan who had Adept-level combat power, the only newly promoted Adepts were Xu Huanzhen and a guest elder who had joined our Luo Tian Sect midway."

"Huiniang and I were both anxious to break through to the Adept stage. At that time, the Divine Sect cultivators also ordered us to go to the Three Roads River battlefield and retake the strategic fortress."

"Then, one day!"

"Huiniang underwent a strange transformation. Her mana surged, her realm skyrocketed, and she stepped into Adept in one go. Her realm even climbed madly, completely unstoppable!"

"Back then, I didn't know what had happened to her. I only knew that when the Three Roads River battlefield was in crisis, even Wang Yuan was trapped in a bitter fight. She suddenly appeared on the battlefield in person and brought us all back alive."

"In that battle, Xu Huanzhen, who had barely managed to condense his Adept core, fell. The temporarily joined guest elder defected. Wang Yuan went into seclusion again after returning. The Luo Tian Sect had suffered a severe blow to its vitality. But Huiniang shockingly displayed the overwhelming power of the late Adept stage in that battle, astonishing the world!"

"It was precisely with this strength as a guarantee that we were later able to quietly escape to Fenghua Domain when the demon race charged the Five Elements Domain and the Five Elements Divine Sect was too busy to look after itself."

---

Hundred Li Green River had mountains and water.

The mountains were the Great Green Mountains, and the water was the Black Water River.

Along the banks of the Black Water River, Luo Chen stepped onto the Great Green Mountains step by step. Gu Caiyi walked beside him, meticulously recounting the past events of those years.

He had already learned about matters concerning Qu Lingjun before.

But the disciples becoming disloyal and even defecting wasn't entirely his fault.

At its peak, although the Luo Tian Sect boasted cultivators like clouds and nearly ten thousand disciples, it was ultimately just a small force established for less than a hundred years.

It had no foundation, no long-standing heritage, no deep cohesion.

Disciples flocked in for profit, and disciples bustled about for gain.

When disaster struck, each flew their own way—that was only natural.

On the contrary, in Luo Chen's view, the fact that three hundred people remained in the end was already quite good!

Of those three hundred, the vast majority were related to him by blood or friendship. This meant that his century of management hadn't been a complete failure.

Even though he himself had left, there were still people willing to stay because of him.

Of course, this couldn't have happened without the utmost efforts of Wang Yuan, Huiniang, Caiyi, and others.

What made him somewhat regretful was that Xu Huanzhen had died.

He was a beast-tamer specially recommended by Dan Tai Jin back then.

When Luo Chen first met him, he was already at the sixth layer of Initiate. Later, he gradually came to recognize the Luo Tian Sect, and even when the cooperation period ended, he stayed.

Unexpectedly, after finally cultivating to the Adept stage he had dreamed of, he died on the demon beast battlefield.

According to Gu Caiyi, Xu Huanzhen had used beast-taming methods to attack demon beasts on the battlefield, attracting the attention of a powerful Demon King, and was killed by force.

It was precisely: success due to beast-taming methods, death also due to beast-taming methods.

When he heard that Huiniang's mana had surged and her realm had skyrocketed, Luo Chen's expression tightened.

After asking Gu Caiyi for the specific time, it matched exactly with when he was in the Demon-Fall Land.

"It really was the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu!"

He sighed softly in his heart.

Then, he asked gently, "Even so, why is Huiniang unwilling to see me? Is it because of her brother's matter?"

Gu Caiyi nodded, but then shook her head.

"That's part of the reason, but the main factor lies in her appearance."

"Mm?" Luo Chen frowned slightly.

Gu Caiyi sighed and spoke of the later situation.

Nothing comes without a price.

Sima Huiniang's skyrocketing realm had also brought disaster.

That vast mana didn't match her own constitution or spirit root attributes at all. From the very beginning, there was a strong rejection reaction.

Sima Huiniang forcibly suppressed it!

But as time passed, the reaction grew more intense, even to the point of constant pain and endless howling day and night.

At that time, the Luo Tian Sect's physician examined her but was powerless.

It was Wang Yuan who took action, passing the *Bright Spirit Shattering Balefulness* method to Sima Huiniang, allowing her to use the pain to temper her will, while also enabling her to endure pain at a higher threshold.

Sima Huiniang endured it.

But the spontaneous changes in her body were not something she could suppress with willpower alone.

"Back then, she was already accustomed to covering her face with a black veil and not seeing outsiders."

"The last time I saw her was before the Ten Decisions Competition began. At that time, she no longer wore elegant palace robes. She was dressed entirely in black clothes and a black skirt, even her forearms were tightly covered."

"I'm afraid..."

When she reached this point, even Gu Caiyi showed an expression of unbearable pity.

Even though she had never gotten along with Sima Huiniang, after years of companionship, there was always a bond of fellow discipleship.

Especially since they were both women, the importance placed on appearance was instinctive!

Otherwise, why would the cultivation world have 'useless' pills like the Appearance-Fixing Pill?

For Sima Huiniang to shut herself away from others showed the extent to which that mana had ravaged her body.

Unknowingly, the two figures had already appeared outside a cave dwelling that had been opened up not many years ago on the Great Green Mountains.

Gu Caiyi chose to leave the time to Sima Huiniang and departed first.

Luo Chen looked around. There were no flowers, no grass, only withered trees.

This was an extremely rare sight in the scenic Hundred Li Green River, which was covered in medicinal herbs everywhere.

He did not conceal his aura, nor did he even lighten his footsteps. He simply walked toward that stone gate.

With his divine sense, he could naturally penetrate the formation restrictions and see the situation inside.

A woman stood anxiously behind the door.

"Open the door!"

Luo Chen said thus.

The woman instinctively raised her hand, but hesitated.

This was the first time in so many years that she had hesitated at Luo Chen's command.

"Your brother, I brought him back."

"The Luo Tian Sect will no longer be bullied by anyone from now on."

"I, Luo Chen, am now at the Sage realm, enough to stand tall in the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world!"

Upon hearing these words, the woman's hand lowered again instead.

It was as if a great burden had been lifted.

Luo Chen frowned slightly, then suddenly said, "Madam has never used beauty to please others. Why torment yourself now over your appearance? Do you think your husband is a shallow person who judges by looks and only cares about the outer shell?"

The woman in the cave was struck as if by a heavy blow, instinctively retreating a few steps.

A look of bewilderment appeared in her eyes.

Yes, from the very beginning, Sima Huiniang had never been known for her beauty. The reason she could step by step from a loose cultivator to her current high position, and even win the affection of the man outside, was never because of her face.

Now, lamenting over her ruined appearance was truly laughable.

It not only negated her own efforts but also belittled her husband's breadth of mind.

Taking a deep breath, Sima Huiniang formed a hand seal. The stone gate that separated inside from outside slowly rose with a rumbling sound.

The person she hadn't seen in a hundred years appeared, from feet to body, then to mouth, nose, eyes...

She slightly adjusted her sleeves, bent at the waist in a graceful bow, her autumn-water eyes filled with joy.

"Your wife welcomes my husband home!"

...

"Back then, when the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu mutated, I thought you had..."

"It was just a small mishap. Your husband is blessed with great fortune; how could he fall so easily? But you, knowing the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu was destroyed, still had such faith in me, telling them I was still alive."

"I wanted to properly guard what you left behind, so I had to stabilize morale. But sometimes, after telling too many lies, I had no face to face those who risked their lives for the sect. That's why I gradually shut myself in."

"Madam, you have quite the scheme!"

"I learned it from you, yet you still mock me like this."

After the reunion came tender affection and leisurely reminiscing of the past.

However, Sima Huiniang's smile was still somewhat forced.

She looked at Luo Chen, who had his eyes closed across from her, and said with trembling lips, "Can it be fixed?"

Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes. What met his gaze was a face that was completely disfigured.

In his hand, he held the woman's wrist, which was covered in uneven blisters like burn scars.

And beneath the clothes, it was even more riddled with holes, a horrifying sight.

Luo Chen fell into thought.

It really was caused by his own mana!

Back in the Demon-Fall Land, when his physical body underwent Nirvana, his mana had suddenly disappeared due to the collapse of his flesh. In reality, it had been transmitted to Sima Huiniang through the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu, which had been fed with his blood and flesh.

There was nothing wrong with this method.

The problem was that Luo Chen's mana was too overbearing!

At that time, he was at the sixth layer of Adept. His fire-attribute mana was supremely yang and supremely strong, rarely matched in the world.

Even Luo Chen himself could barely withstand the day-and-night erosion of his own mana, to the point where his face was perpetually flushed red, with external signs manifesting.

How could Sima Huiniang, at merely the ninth layer of Initiate, possibly endure it?

Although the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu helped her barely accept this mana, she simply couldn't suppress it!

She herself had water-wood dual spirit roots, and the technique she cultivated was *Near the Water Tower*. It was very difficult for her to control this mana.

The Adept core formed by the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu was not an orthodox one either.

Her control was extremely low!

There was another point: not only was Luo Chen's mana attribute overbearing, but the quantity was also large.

For a long time, Luo Chen's mana had been several times that of his peers. By the time he reached the Adept stage, his mana at the sixth layer could rival those at the ninth layer!

This resulted in Sima Huiniang reaching the heavens in one step, directly charging to the seventh layer of Adept.

The large quantity, the overbearing nature, pierced through her qi sea, constantly scouring her body day and night, ultimately causing irreparable damage.

But there was one point that was quite strange.

If she had vented this vast mana, at least she wouldn't have ended up in a state where her qi sea was riddled with holes, her meridians scorched and withered, and her body beyond recovery.

This question was instantly resolved when Luo Chen thought of the Luo Tian Sect's century of hardship.

Wang Yuan, to protect the sect, took a shortcut, cultivating a demon-like transformation, seeking only to obtain powerful strength.

Min Longyu painstakingly studied formation dao, to the point of overexerting his mental energy, his hair turning white, all in pursuit of greater power.

Qu Lingjun's talent was too poor, so he could only take a roundabout way to save the sect, joining the Fenghua Palace, also to obtain powerful strength, even if it was external.

Under such circumstances, how could Huiniang, who cared most about the Luo Tian Sect, possibly abandon that powerful mana within her body?

Luo Chen couldn't help but reach out and touch that face.

"You've suffered."

Sima Huiniang was somewhat shy and instinctively turned her head away.

Then, she heard words that delighted her.

"The problems with your body, your husband can solve them. Don't worry."

"Really?"

Sima Huiniang turned her head sharply, staring fixedly at Luo Chen.

Luo Chen nodded gently, stroking her hair.

"It might hurt a little. Bear with it."

"Mm, I'm not afraid."

Luo Chen smiled softly, telling Sima Huiniang to remove all her clothes. He looked at the scarred body as if it were nothing, and his divine sense sank into it.

Instantly!

The vast mana within the woman's body seemed to sense something and became restless.

"Come back!"

Luo Chen formed a hand seal and began to guide that mana to flow toward him.

After all, this mana was all painstakingly cultivated by him. Even though it had been converted by the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu for Sima Huiniang's use, it still recognized its master.

Luo Chen's treatment plan was not complicated.

Extract all the mana, destroy the Adept core, causing Sima Huiniang's realm to fall back to the Initiate stage.

This way, the source of the calamity would be gone.

Then, use medical arts to repair her body, restoring it to a normal state. At that time, she could break through to the Adept stage again!

The only problem was probably the matter of lifespan.

Sima Huiniang's age had long passed the 250-year limit of the Initiate stage. The reason she had survived until now was entirely due to the Adept core formed by the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu.

Even if it wasn't an orthodox Adept core, it was still an Adept core!

If this core was destroyed, combined with the losses her body had suffered over the years, she might not have many years left to live.

But Luo Chen had a solution for this problem too!

In the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, he had refined the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion and obtained a lifespan-extending spiritual medicine from the most secret spaces within it!

Its name was Hundred-Headed Asparagus.

This spiritual medicine was of the fourth rank. Consuming it would grant a hundred years of lifespan.

If refined into a corresponding lifespan-extending pill, the lifespan gained would be even greater.

Its origin was definitely not from the Penglai Immortal Sect's own cultivation, but rather picked by Penglai disciples from the Wood Heaven Plain in the Demon-Fall Land.

When Luo Chen had refined the Octagonal Pavilion in the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven and discovered this item, he had been overjoyed.

He even felt that the decades of lifespan he had lost in the grotto-heaven were not a big deal.

That was also one of the reasons why his attitude toward Fu Chaosheng had softened after coming out.

Now, this Hundred-Headed Asparagus would be used on Huiniang.

Even if he couldn't refine a lifespan-extending pill, relying on its own medicinal power alone would be enough.

A hundred years!

With Luo Chen's full support, Sima Huiniang forming her Adept core would be a simple matter!

Time passed slowly.

Just as Luo Chen had absorbed all the mana that Huiniang couldn't control, as well as the mana in her qi sea, leaving only that blood-colored Adept core, his movements stopped.

"Eh?"

"What's wrong?"

"Nothing."

Luo Chen replied softly and began to convert his mana, casting various medical arts that had long reached great perfection.

Five Organs Return to Origin, Bone Restoration and Meridian Unblocking, Spring Breeze Turns to Rain...

Later, he even took out a commonly stocked Cang Hai Pill from his storage ring, the kind specifically used to repair the dantian qi sea.

After the pill entered her body, Luo Chen showed no negligence.

He opened his mouth and spat out a wisp of blue-white intertwined flame, which appeared faintly.

With a thought, the flame completely turned from white to blue!

Unlike the deathly white flame on the Lingtian Pass dueling platform, the Withering-Bloom True Flame at this moment was no longer deathly still, but filled with vigorous vitality.

Luo Chen carefully spread out the true flame, like a veil of flame covering the woman's entire body, and began to gently burn.

The dead skin, the cells that had lost their vitality, the black-red blood—under this burning, they gradually began to radiate new life.

Even her sparse hair began to visibly grow, cascading down to her shoulders, neck, and waist.

And the Cang Hai Pill that Luo Chen had placed inside earlier also began to rapidly refine, turning into a viscous liquid that wrapped around the edges of the woman's qi sea, repairing it.

Too much is as bad as too little!

With a thought, Luo Chen withdrew the Withering-Bloom True Flame.

"Rest well. I'll come back in a few days to treat you again. Once your body is fully healed, you can take the Hundred-Headed Asparagus to restore your origin and increase your lifespan."

As he said this, he covered her with a thin blanket, concealing her fair, jade-like body.

Sima Huiniang stared blankly at Luo Chen.

"Are you leaving?"

"I'm not leaving. If I do, I'll take you all with me."

"Mm..."

The woman blinked. The exhaustion accumulated over a hundred years surged up instantly.

Surrounded by the long-lost comfort and sense of security, her long eyelashes opened and closed, and she gradually fell asleep.

Luo Chen tucked the corner of the blanket in for her. His originally gentle expression gradually turned gloomy.

At this moment, the woman still had a mid-Adept realm!

In her gradually repairing qi sea, an Adept core rose and fell, wrapped tightly by hundreds of restrictions.

"The Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu is poisonous, not only connecting hearts but also connecting spirits!"

(End of this chapter)
