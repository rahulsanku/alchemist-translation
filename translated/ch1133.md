# Chapter 1133: White Horse Avalanche, Meteor Bandits

Heavy snow filled the sky.

On a road that was not particularly flat, a bloated convoy was slowly advancing.

The convoy consisted mainly of horse-drawn carriages, supplemented by oxen and mules.

Ahead, figures moved in a blur, sturdy men continuously clearing the thick snow, opening a passable path for the carriages behind.

In the middle section, tall horses and ornate carriages dominated.

At the rear, a large number of mules, horses, and yellow oxen carried a vast quantity of goods. Moreover, the rear of the convoy was constantly growing as they passed through one town after another.

Inside an ornate carriage at the center of the convoy.

Outside, the cold was biting like ice, but inside the carriage, it was warm as spring.

Jing Lan somewhat clumsily poured tea and added water, while an old man and a young one chatted amiably.

It was now the end of the year, and several days had passed since they had set out from Qingshan Town.

Luo Chen, accompanied by Jing Lan, had traveled light and joined Steward Yan's convoy, heading for the legendary Yixian City.

Along the way, Steward Yan was very enthusiastic toward Luo Chen!

He not only arranged the best carriage but also spared no effort in food and drink, even considering sending a few pretty maidservants to attend to him.

Luo Chen cared little for such external things, so he declined the servants, only keeping Jing Lan by his side.

As for Steward Yan, he reciprocated the man's enthusiasm by occasionally inviting him over for casual conversation.

Steward Yan's given name was Yan Song, and he was fifty-six years old.

For body refiners, if one did not enter the third realm, the Furnace Realm, lifespan was hard to increase, and they would at most look a bit younger and more spirited than their peers.

However, fifty-six was not actually that old, at least not enough to look so aged.

The reason Steward Yan appeared so decrepit was that he had suffered a severe injury in his youth. Coupled with the tempering of his flesh and blood qi for body refinement, his appearance had aged far faster than a normal person's. Now, he looked almost like an octogenarian elder.

"If not for that injury, I wouldn't have stopped at the late Blood River realm, nor would I have thought of taking a shortcut to imitate the Qi-Shaping Transformation method of the Furnace Realm. Instead, I ended up with this nondescript appearance. Please forgive my embarrassment, Senior."

Steward Yan smiled bitterly, showing no shame in addressing himself as a junior before Luo Chen.

After learning that the other party might be an old monster who had cultivated for hundreds or even thousands of years, even if he currently had no magical power, his age alone was enough to warrant the title of "Senior." Especially after Luo Chen had displayed a level of body refinement far surpassing his own, this address came from the heart.

"Previously, I offended a guest elder in the city and was nearly beaten to death. If not for the young city lord taking pity on my years of loyal service, I would have lost my life. But to avoid implicating my family, I volunteered to come to this remote town and help the young city lord manage some of his assets."

Luo Chen listened quietly, not appearing particularly moved by Steward Yan's full confession.

It was obvious that the other party was speaking so frankly despite their shallow acquaintance only to draw closer to him and bind the two together.

In truth, it was unnecessary. As long as the other party performed well enough, Luo Chen would not mind giving him a hand.

Luo Chen asked casually, "Those merchant caravans that joined midway, are they also assets of that young city lord?"

Steward Yan hesitated for a moment, then nodded. "You could say that."

"Oh?" Luo Chen made a questioning sound.

Steward Yan explained, "Strictly speaking, the industries in the nearby towns all belong to the Yixian City Lord's Mansion. Lord Xiao has been away for over ten years, and the young city lord has been managing all affairs in his stead. Once the appointment from the Domain Lord comes down, the young city lord will officially become the true master of Yixian City. At that time, these assets will naturally belong to him."

Luo Chen frowned slightly. The real Yixian City Lord had been gone for ten years?

Steward Yan suddenly laughed. "Assets like these are spread across a thousand li around Yixian City, too numerous to count. The young city lord cannot manage them all alone, so he only oversees the important ones. The scattered and remote ones are mostly entrusted to guest elders or loyal old servants like me. If Senior gains the young city lord's favor, you could either reside in the city and enjoy wealth and honor, or be sent out to govern a region with sole authority. Wouldn't that be wonderful?"

Jing Lan, who had been quietly listening nearby, couldn't help but perk up her ears, a look of interest appearing on her face.

Life for humans in this region was quite difficult. The harsh natural environment and the frequent appearance of the three great natural disasters restricted their normal activities. Being able to find a stable place, whether in the city or elsewhere, was a dream come true.

However, Luo Chen's expression remained unmoved. Instead, he was thinking of other things.

He suddenly asked, "How many children did the departed city lord have?"

Steward Yan's expression changed slightly, a forced look appearing on his face.

His lips trembled as he hesitated whether to speak, but Luo Chen raised his hand to stop him.

"Never mind. It's not an important matter anyway."

Steward Yan was startled, then realized that this was due to the other party's strong self-confidence. He simply didn't care how many formidable enemies the young city lord had.

Luo Chen suddenly changed the subject and asked, "I wonder how much Steward Yan knows about the Ghost Rain among the three great natural disasters?"

Relieved by the change of topic, Steward Yan answered subconsciously, "Ghost Rain is the most frequent of the three great natural disasters, especially common in spring and summer. It's rare to see it in winter like this..."

From Steward Yan's mouth, the specific details of Ghost Rain were far more detailed than what Luo Chen had learned from the villagers.

From the patterns of its appearance, to its effects on nature and humans, and how to defend against it, he explained everything clearly.

Naturally, he inevitably mentioned the accompanying phenomenon of "Ghost Nightmares."

When speaking of Ghost Nightmares, Steward Yan deliberately observed Luo Chen's expression. Seeing that the other party seemed interested, he went into more detail.

"This thing has no physical form. It is born with the rain and gathers where people congregate. The more people there are, the more Ghost Nightmares appear. The stronger the expert, the more powerful the Ghost Nightmares they attract. According to records left by immortals who once drifted here, this kind of ghostly thing can only be destroyed by the purest soul power. For us body refiners, we need a strong martial will to resist and withstand it... However, as later generations studied it, they gradually figured out some additional methods of resistance. Among them, Dreamstone is the simplest and best way to block Ghost Nightmares. The reason our Yixian City ranks among the top of the seventy-two cities of the Great Cataclysm Domain is not only due to specialties like Mi Immortal Smoke and Heavenly Star Baleful Qi but also because we control the largest Dreamstone vein."

"Steward Yan, have some tea." Jing Lan refilled his cup.

Having spoken so much in one go, Steward Yan was indeed a bit thirsty. He thanked Jing Lan and downed the tea in one gulp.

"Senior Luo, that's all I know about Ghost Rain. There might be more detailed records, but those are in the city lord's collection, and I cannot access them."

Luo Chen nodded lightly, his thoughts already clear.

Sure enough, cultivators had recognized Ghost Nightmares long before him, and later generations had even figured out clear methods of defense.

Seeing that he couldn't learn more from Steward Yan, Luo Chen changed the subject again, preparing to learn about other matters.

"What about the Mi Immortal Smoke... Hmm?"

Luo Chen let out a soft exclamation and looked up outside.

Steward Yan was startled. "What's wrong?"

"Let's go out and take a look. Trouble has arrived."

Steward Yan frowned. Just as he was about to get up, a tremor shook the carriage, nearly toppling him. Fortunately, he circulated his leg strength and steadied himself.

He had just lifted the curtain and stepped out when he heard exclamations from all around.

"Avalanche!"

Steward Yan followed the sound. A few li away, on a not-so-tall mountain peak, rolling snow was surging down.

Could such a mountain peak trigger an avalanche?

Steward Yan was puzzled. And even if it was an avalanche, what kind of trouble was that?

But the next moment, his murky eyes widened in shock.

What was a few li away was no avalanche at all. It was clearly a herd of white horses galloping through the snow, churning up thousands of piles of snow, their momentum as vast as an avalanche.

And on the white horses were sturdy figures, many carrying blades and swords on their backs. Their fierce aura was chilling even from several li away.

Steward Yan took a deep breath, a name surfacing in his mind.

"Meteor Bandits!"
