# Chapter 741: My Name Is Chu Yan, and the Cultivators of the Mountains and Seas Realm Honor Me as the Alchemy Saint

The ascender was none other than Chu Yan, the Alchemy Saint!

After Luo Chen reached Central Continent, Fu Chaosheng no longer hid this inside story from him, but solemnly told it to Luo Chen and the others.

To be honest, Luo Chen had been deeply shocked when he first heard the news.

He had heard of that person's deeds since childhood, and among alchemists, the Alchemy Saint's name rang like thunder.

The world marveled at his profound mastery of alchemy, yet also kept up a quiet stream of criticism over his departure from the Nether Origin Sect to join the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

But looking at it now, it seemed everything had been worth it!

He had crossed the threshold of Ascendant, and would soon rise on rosy clouds and ascend. This was the ultimate goal of all who strove for long life.

Countless efforts were all for this ending.

Yet the Alchemy Saint's ending made one thing very clear: sometimes, choice outweighed effort.

If he had remained in the Nether Origin Sect, could he still have ascended on rosy clouds?

Most likely, it would have been very difficult. The Eastern Wilderness had not heard news of a cultivator ascending for over a thousand years.

When Fu Chaosheng had told them this inside story so solemnly, he had clearly meant to encourage them to join Star Gate.

Though it was not directly joining the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, entering Star Gate still meant a vast future.

First there had been the legendary Southern Frontier great power; then there was the Eastern Wilderness Alchemy Saint. Both had cultivated to the Ascendant realm within Star Gate, which showed that the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect did not discriminate against outsiders.

This was why, as soon as they entered Central Continent territory, Winter Mountain Hermit and Lu Qiuzhen had hurried straight to Heaven-Earth Peak. They were already somewhat unable to wait.

At this moment, Luo Chen stood inside Zhigu City[TERM?], gazing at the faint outline of Heaven-Earth Peak, and he too felt a little unable to restrain himself.

Fu Chaosheng saw the yearning on his face and smiled softly.

"Come. Let us go straight to Heaven-Earth Peak!"

...

They said they would go straight there, but within the immortal city there was an air-ban formation, so the two of them could only use walking in place of a carriage and leave the city step by step.

As he walked down the broad street, the conversations of cultivators around him drifted into Luo Chen's ears, faint yet distinct.

It was not deliberate. Once divine sense grew powerful to a certain degree, unless one intentionally blocked outside information, all things between heaven and earth would involuntarily gather toward the cultivator.

From this point of view, there was some reason why a Sage's Nascent Soul could easily be assimilated by heaven and earth.

The stronger one's divine sense, the less boundary there was between oneself and the world. Over time, one might even lose the ability to distinguish, 'Who am I?'

The phrase "spirit merging with heaven and earth" was no joke.

"Go, go, hurry to the Passing River[TERM?]! A grand event like this must not be missed!"

"What happened?"

"Fellow Daoist Li, do you not know? The Five Champions of Shangjun[TERM?] have all arrived at Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain to challenge Heavenly Origin Linxian[TERM?], right beside the Passing River! By now, the stones have probably already begun to fall."

"Hiss. First the Seven Disciples of Muyuan challenged Fellow Daoist Eminent; now the Five Champions of Shangjun have come to challenge Heavenly Origin Linxian. I truly cannot understand the go world anymore."

"What is there not to understand? Before every centennial competition, talented figures from every side gather at Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain. It is both to attend the ceremony and to raise their own names, hoping to gain the sect's regard. Martial contests are hard to control in measure, but literary contests have no such concerns. On the few inches of a go board, blades flash and swords gleam; it is you die or I perish, and it is the best way to decide superiority! And the three people now standing at the peak of the go world are the three brightest targets. Defeat them, and one can inherit the glory that has shrouded them for years. Nothing could be faster."

"Tsk. That may be true, but several people going up together rather lacks grace."

"Why worry about that for them? If those three wish to be spared endless challenges at their door, they naturally must do something extraordinary to stop later challengers from itching to try."

"Eminent Daoyin spent a month defeating the Seven Disciples of Muyuan. I wonder whether Heavenly Origin Linxian can defeat the Five Champions of Shangjun this time."

...

As Luo Chen walked, he listened with great relish.

Beside him, Fu Chaosheng casually explained the general situation.

Naturally, Luo Chen understood the three peak figures of the go world.

But he knew little of the so-called Seven Disciples and Five Champions.

Only after Fu Chaosheng's explanation did Luo Chen learn that these people came from other clans, large and small.

Because of regional ties and superb go skill, over time, meddlesome people had divided them by region and given them approximate titles.

The Seven Disciples of Muyuan came from several cultivator clans near the Muyuan Grassland. Their styles differed, but all had entered the ninth dan, and some had even reached rank.

The Five Champions of Shangjun referred to five go players who had made their names together in a tournament held in Shangjun City years ago. Though they came from different places, they had still been given this title.

Even so, they counted as young juniors, famous for only one or two hundred years.

Naturally, they could not be compared with those three.

Luo Chen, however, was somewhat interested in another piece of information in Fu Chaosheng's words.

"I understand the nine dans of the go path, but what does the ninth rank mean?"

Fu Chaosheng stroked his beard and smiled. "In Central Continent's go world, a go player's strength is divided into nine dan. Above the ninth dan, there is a separate nine-rank system corresponding to their go-path realm. For the likes of the Seven Disciples of Muyuan and the Five Champions of Shangjun, spending one or two hundred years to enter the ninth dan is not strange, but entering rank still lacks some tempering."

Luo Chen blinked. "What about those three? Have they entered rank?"

"Naturally. They are even outstanding among the nine ranks. For example, Gu Daoyin has at least reached the Netherpassage realm!"

Luo Chen listened with secret wonder.

In the Mountains and Seas Realm, the hundred arts of cultivation could be called a hundred flowers in bloom, but it seemed no path had developed with such fine detail as Central Continent's go.

Whether the go academies everywhere that taught the art, the Dao halls where players competed, or the large and small shops selling go equipment, game records, and classics, even the dan and rank realms were labeled with perfect clarity.

The go path's nine-dan, nine-rank system?

If the pill path were divided according to this, how should it be done?

"Fellow Daoist Luo, would you like to go beside the Passing River and witness this grand go event?" Fu Chaosheng suggested.

Luo Chen shook his head. "Luo has no interest in these matters. Better to go to Heaven-Earth Peak as soon as possible and settle my Star Gate guest elder position!"

"True. Proper business matters."

The old man and the young man moved against the tide of people and quickly left the city.

Wherever they passed, even the densest crowds unconsciously opened a path for them.

Not long after, the two of them stood beside a great river rolling eastward.

The axe-handle rots away; the departed flow on like this.

Above the Passing River lay the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range, the true mountain gate of the Dao Sect.

And on the bank of the Passing River stood Heaven-Earth Peak, solitary and independent, linking heaven and earth!

Luo Chen gazed across the river. Beneath that perilous peak, he felt himself so small, like a mayfly between heaven and earth, or a single grain of sand in the Ganges.

He had not felt this sensation for a very long time, not since advancing to Sage.

Yet in his memory, there clearly had been a similar illusion.

Where had it been?

Fu Chaosheng's voice sounded by his ear.

"My sect treats outsiders like you with exceptional generosity. Heaven-Earth Peak is, in fact, one of the few fifth-tier spirit lands beneath heaven. Star Gate is established upon it, which means you guest elders and offering elders who join Star Gate can enjoy an abundant spiritual qi environment at any time. Even if you cultivate to the Ascendant realm in the future, Heaven-Earth Peak can still serve as a cultivation cave abode!"

Yes, a fifth-tier spirit land.

Green Parasol Mountain, the sacred land of the Eastern Wilderness Feather Race!

That place, too, was a spirit land containing a fifth-tier spirit vein.

This Heaven-Earth Peak was not inferior to Green Parasol Mountain in the slightest.

As awe rose in Luo Chen's heart, his favorable impression of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect grew by several degrees. In this world, few forces would be so generous as to offer even a fifth-tier spirit land to outside cultivators.

"Let us go. We will ascend the mountain and settle your Star Gate guest elder matter. Do not worry. With my recommendation, there generally will not be a problem. When the Seven Elders of Star Gate[TERM?] ask about your situation, simply answer according to the wording I taught you."

The Seven Elders of Star Gate referred to the seven representatives sent from among the nineteen elders to review newcomers joining Star Gate.

Under normal circumstances, there would be five Star Gate guest elder representatives, one person from the Aristocratic Clans, and one final person from the Twelve Great Houses.

The final decision-making power usually lay with the elder representative sent by the Twelve Great Houses.

Without further thought, Luo Chen's Nascent Soul trembled and stirred the spiritual qi of heaven and earth, turning it into a misty spiritual light that wrapped around him. Like an arrow released from the string, he flew toward Heaven-Earth Peak.

Fu Chaosheng followed beside him.

Though the two were swift, in the eyes of others, they looked like mayflies rushing toward a high mountain, so very small.

But everyone knew that only Sages could fly freely upon Heaven-Earth Peak!

This was, in truth, a kind of invisible assessment.

Without a Sage domain, one could not resist the immense spiritual pressure naturally emitted by this fifth-tier spirit mountain.

Whoosh! Whoosh!

Two streaks of escaping light landed on a vast plaza paved with white jade and wreathed in cloud and mist.

The moment they landed, an overbearing divine sense swept over them, falling especially upon Luo Chen, this unfamiliar cultivator.

Fu Chaosheng's expression did not change. He bowed.

"Fu Chaosheng, current head of the Fu family, recommends Wilderness Wanderer of the Northern Sea to join Star Gate. The relevant circumstances have already been reported in advance."

As soon as he said this, the divine sense's domineering posture softened slightly, though it still enveloped Luo Chen.

A heavy, aged voice echoed around them.

"Go to Welcoming Immortals Hall and wait. Three days from now, the Seven Elders will jointly decide the matter of Wilderness Wanderer entering Star Gate."

Hearing this, Fu Chaosheng could not help letting out a breath of relief. He gave Luo Chen a look and left the plaza first.

Before leaving, Luo Chen looked curiously into the depths of the cloud and mist.

There was a great door there.

Starlight blazed upon it, as though it led into the unknown.

Was this Star Gate?

...

The Passing River rolled with waves.

On the riverbank, people were packed like woven threads.

Five go boards were set out, with five men seated at them.

A middle-aged man with a proud and unrestrained bearing walked among the five while drinking wine. Each time he drank deeply, he generously placed a stone.

His expression was carefree, but the five men were restless in their seats.

Outside the field, countless onlookers wore a feverish flush.

No one had expected this battle by the Passing River to be even faster than the one at the Hall of Eminents.

In only three days, Heavenly Origin Linxian had forced the Five Champions of Shangjun to their limit. Now it had reached the endgame, and Linxian's victorious momentum had been fully revealed.

In the crowd, Fu Qinglan's little face was flushed red. Her former coldness was gone, and she looked even more excited.

Yu Xu did not understand. Heavenly Origin Linxian and Eminent Daoyin were clearly almost equal in reputation, yet the latter had taken a month to defeat the Seven Disciples of Muyuan, while the former had established victory in only three days.

Could there also be an enormous gap between the two of them?

Fu Qinglan explained softly, "This is a difference in go style. Senior Brother Daoyin is ninth dan, fourth rank, and has entered the Netherpassage realm. He sees shape and blocks, excels at adapting to change, and specializes in countering moves as they come, so he took a long time. Senior Brother Linxian is also ninth dan, fourth rank, but his style is more domineering. He most likes the small seventh-rank method of fighting force. Once he moves, he must battle, clashing directly with the enemy, so if there is even a tiny difference in go strength, he amplifies it to the limit and creates the momentum of an avalanche flood..."

Yu Xu listened in a daze, her face blank.

When Fu Qinglan finished, she only spread her hands.

"As expected of a prodigy from the Tan Great House!"

Eminent Daoyin came from the Gu Great House of the Twelve Great Houses.

Heavenly Origin Linxian came from the Tan Great House, likewise one of the Twelve Great Houses.

Fu Qinglan felt helpless. The thing she loved most could not be discussed with others.

"Let us go!"

Yu Xu did not understand. "You are not watching the endgame?"

Fu Qinglan shook her head. "Under that kind of magnificent go style, all broad openings and sweeping closures, we cannot see the fine mysteries at all. This part cannot keep up either."

As she spoke, she pointed at her head.

Indeed, high-dan, high-rank go players considered hundreds and thousands of later hands with every move. In such a short time, their own go attainments could not make sense of it.

At this point, Fu Qinglan also felt sorry for the Five Champions of Shangjun.

They had truly chosen the wrong person to challenge in Senior Brother Tan Linxian. Being killed until they threw away helmets and armor was one thing, but because the time was so short, they would not learn much.

They would have been better off like the Seven Disciples of Muyuan, who could slowly learn some rank-entering techniques during their month-long battle with Senior Brother Gu Daoyin in the Hall of Eminents.

It was said that among the seven disciples, three had entered seclusion after that battle at the Hall of Eminents. Very possibly, after emerging, they would enter the three realms of the small seventh rank.

The two of them squeezed out of the crowd. Yu Xu stretched lazily, somewhat bored.

"Before we enter Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, we cannot cultivate. It is truly hard to endure."

The fanaticism faded from Fu Qinglan's face. She smiled. "Visiting mountains and friends, tasting tea and playing go. Can those not pass the time?"

Yu Xu sighed. She could not stay idle.

"The clan elders said that we newly advanced Sages still have thin Purple Mansion barriers, and our Nascent Souls also retain a closeness with heaven and earth. In this state, we are most suited to sensing the laws of heaven and earth within Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven. But as long as the grotto-heaven does not open, we cannot cultivate for even one day. It wastes so much time."

Fu Qinglan nodded.

Once they cultivated, they would unconsciously deepen the Purple Mansion barrier and sever assimilation with heaven and earth.

That was why there was the restriction of newly advanced Sages within a hundred years.

But in her view, compared with the chance to comprehend the true intent of laws and reach directly toward future Ascendant, this little bit of time could be wasted.

Sharpening the blade did not delay the chopping of firewood!

While the two were talking, someone poked his head through the crowd and trotted toward them.

"Eh, Seventh Uncle." Fu Qinglan recognized the newcomer.

Though his realm was only mid-stage Adept, he was an elder of her clan.

When the middle-aged man saw Fu Qinglan, he could not help letting out a relieved breath.

"The family head has reached Heaven-Earth Peak, and that person has arrived as well. Qinglan, come with me for a trip."

Fu Qinglan's eyes brightened.

Yu Xu beside her wanted to go with them, but with Fu Chaosheng's intention involved, she could not accompany them.

"Sorry, Little Xu. After I meet that person, I will introduce him to you," Fu Qinglan said apologetically.

Yu Xu nodded and did not insist.

But after the two left, her eyes rolled, and she quickly flew in another direction.

Fu Qinglan was somewhat unable to wait.

Even though her feelings were complicated, she still wanted to meet that Wilderness Wanderer soon.

But when she arrived at Welcoming Immortals Hall, she did not see anyone.

The cultivators in the hall told her that the other party had just left and was undergoing the Seven Elders' assessment.

Fu Qinglan breathed out a gloomy sigh. "I have waited this long. No need to hurry over this moment."

...

Luo Chen finally crossed that gate of stars.

Alone, he arrived in a space filled with countless starlights.

The instant he stepped in, seven illusory figures slowly appeared.

When seven pairs of eyes fell upon him, Luo Chen felt as if thorns pricked his back, cold spreading over his whole body.

Every one of them had a higher realm than him!

Every one of them gave him the same oppression Moon Wanderer had once brought him!

And among the seven, the slender figure whom he least dared look at directly made him subconsciously feel a sense of closeness.

"Oh? An alchemist, actually?"

A melodious, gentle voice sounded softly. While it drew surprise from the others, it also swept away the pressure the other six had brought Luo Chen.

Only then could Luo Chen raise his head and look directly at the seven illusory figures.

He took a deep breath and announced himself.

"Luo Chen, rogue cultivator of the Northern Sea, greets the seven elders."

A rogue cultivator?

Though they had long known his background through the intelligence submitted by the Fu family, when the seven Star Gate elders saw his appearance and that surging, vibrant life force, they still could not help marveling in their hearts.

To have formed a Nascent Soul so quickly as a rogue cultivator was truly a rare cultivation talent!

At this moment, the woman who had spoken earlier spoke again as if no one else were present.

"Can you refine fifth-tier pills?"

As soon as this was said, everyone was startled, and Luo Chen's heart trembled even more.

"Elder jests. Luo has only a slight understanding of alchemy. How could I refine fifth-tier pills at the Sage realm?"

The woman's tone was filled with regret.

"A pity. Before I leave, there is no fellow traveler to see me off."

Leave?

Luo Chen's heart stirred. He asked cautiously, "May I know the elder's honored name?"

"Impudent..."

Someone else began to rebuke him.

But the woman laughed softly. "No matter. Telling you my name has no consequence. My name is Chu Yan. The cultivators of the Mountains and Seas Realm honor me as the Alchemy Saint."

*(End of Chapter)*
