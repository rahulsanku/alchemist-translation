# Chapter 1075: Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Buries a Ghost, Heaven’s Vault Gathers the Lands

On a small island.

Clear waters, white sands, circling birds.

A solitary figure stood alone, gazing coldly into the distance.

In his line of sight, a massive white bird came flying across the sea from afar, its wings spread wide, almost blotting out the sky.

With each beat of its wings, it crossed tens of li in an instant, arriving at the edge of the island.

It landed, prostrating itself, then transformed into a tall, graceful figure who knelt on one knee.

“Heavenjade greets Master!”

Luo Chen’s gaze remained distant, still fixed on the horizon, as he asked softly, “Where are they?”

Heavenjade kept her head lowered, her voice trembling with barely concealed excitement: “They’ve all come. As you instructed, they are ten thousand li from the Eastern Origin Continent, ready to launch an attack at any moment. For now, they are holding still, only waiting for your command, Master!”

Luo Chen hummed in acknowledgment, his expression unchanged.

Heavenjade raised her head slightly, puzzled. “Master, when will we strike at the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect?”

Luo Chen’s lips twitched. “We still need to wait for the right moment—a moment when we can stake everything we have.”

Yes, Luo Chen was waiting for a moment.

Waiting for the moment when the formation Li Canghai had spoken of would activate, when all of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect’s power would be poured into it. That would also be the moment when the sect’s strength was at its weakest.

At that time, he would stake everything, go all in.

In his past life, there was a saying: “Going all in is a kind of wisdom.”

This wisdom was mainly reflected in strategic choice, information asymmetry, decisive action, and resource integration.

Luo Chen asked himself whether he had done his best to integrate every resource he could access. In all his history—past, and likely future—he might never again assemble such a formidable lineup.

And in terms of information and intelligence, he currently held a certain advantage over the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

Back then, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect had tried to swallow the Mountains and Seas Realm whole, but failed, making many enemies instead. Both the Northern Sea and the Southern Frontier bore grudges against the sect. In comparison, Luo Chen’s personal entanglements with the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect were nothing they would care about. Thus, with his deliberate control over information leaks, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect would not notice Luo Chen’s “little moves.”

After all, he was ultimately just a Sage-level cultivator.

Under these circumstances, Luo Chen pouring all his strength into a single breakthrough against the Eastern Origin Continent—one of the sect’s three continents—was undoubtedly the most correct strategic choice, the most decisive decision.

But no matter how correct or decisive, the enemy’s accumulated foundation was still beyond his ability to contend with.

Therefore, if he didn’t play this card, fine. But once he played it, he had to win everything!

Otherwise, he would perish, his four hundred years of bitter cultivation turning into a mirror flower, a water moon—an empty dream.

Luo Chen looked down at Heavenjade. This demon beast he had once forcibly subdued had finally cultivated to the fourth rank and taken human form over the past three hundred years.

The mana flowing through her body was so familiar, originating from the avian clan technique he had personally created for her back then—the *Huan Yu Secret Canon*.

Long ago, Luo Chen had already undone the enslavement seal on her.

She was free, yet still called him “Master” at every turn, showing how deeply she trusted him.

“Among the four great demon beasts under my command, you are the weakest in strength, but the most intelligent. I have a task for you.”

Heavenjade’s spirit lifted. “Please instruct me, Master.”

Luo Chen nodded slightly. "After the battle begins, you are not to participate in the front lines. Instead, go to the only route between the Eastern Origin Continent and the Libei Continent. Once any strong cultivators from Libei begin to reinforce the Eastern Origin Continent, immediately notify everyone to prepare for retreat. This task is not difficult, but it is important. It requires intelligence, sharp eyes, and sufficient speed—these are your strengths. Can you do it?"

Heavenjade answered with a heavy, excited voice: “Heavenjade will surely complete the task perfectly!”

Luo Chen smiled, feeling slightly relieved inside.

Although Li Canghai had said he had reached an agreement with a powerful being from the Northern Sea demon clan, and that a large number of demon beasts would attack the Libei Continent at the right time to hold back the top experts there…

Luo Chen still lacked confidence.

It wasn’t that he didn’t trust Li Canghai—though he didn’t trust him much either—but what he truly didn’t trust were the Northern Sea demon experts.

What if they just went through the motions and failed to hold back the people from Libei? Then the battlefield on the Eastern Origin Continent would suddenly face unforeseen changes.

At that point, regardless of whether he succeeded or failed, the group of experts from the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world would inevitably be caught in a bitter struggle.

Since he had brought them here, he still hoped to take as many of them back as possible.

“Master, I’ll take my leave now.”

Having received her task, Heavenjade hurried away.

Luo Chen watched her fleeing figure, his calm exterior hiding a turbulent heart.

It was close!

After a hundred years of scheming, that day was finally approaching.

He too should head to his own battlefield.

But before that, he needed to go to the Eastern Origin Continent and deliver a great gift to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

…

With the end of the Sexagenary Grand Competition, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect should have settled down from its lively state.

But that was not the case.

Although it was indeed quieter than before, the atmosphere was even more oppressive.

All over the Eastern Origin Continent, patrolling sect cultivators could be seen everywhere. The cultivators of the various clans were also ordered not to move about freely, but to remain within their clan territories. The commercial and trade activities that had gradually revived with the Central Continent’s recovery had all been halted.

All these signs indicated that something major was about to happen in the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

And among the Sage-level cultivators, this major event was no longer a secret.

The Ancestor was going to reunite the Central Continent, rebuild Heaven-Earth Peak, and restore the eternal foundation of that sacred land that once suppressed an entire realm!

For this matter, the sect had already been planning for over a hundred years. At the final stage, the manpower and resources poured into it were staggering.

Corresponding arrangements had been made on all three continents.

In the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range, all thirty-six registered Sages had been dispatched into the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven.

Moreover, on the Eastern Origin Continent, the four great houses—Ke, Shi, Li, and Lian—along with nine Sage-level aristocratic clans, had all sent their experts to guard various formation nodes.

The other two continents also had their respective Dao Palaces overseeing the overall situation and carrying out corresponding work.

In this oppressive yet restless, quiet yet murderous atmosphere, Luo Chen traveled upstream along the Passing River and once again arrived at the sect’s ancestral court—the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range!

This should have been the loneliest time for the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range, which was usually brimming with spiritual energy. There were not even a few Sages inside.

At least in Luo Chen’s divine sense sweep, he only found five.

All of them were at the late Sage stage!

“They must be the clan elders of the four great houses, sent to the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range to guard the most core formation nodes.”

Luo Chen smiled inwardly, not surprised at all.

According to his and Li Canghai’s deductions, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect’s continent-gathering formation this time, besides many minor nodes, had four main nodes.

They were the three Dao Palaces on the three continents, and Heaven-Earth Island.

These four places all had fifth-rank spirit veins.

Using fifth-rank spirit veins as nodes, along with pre-arranged formations, reuniting the Central Continent was a matter of course.

In this process, the pulling wasn’t actually difficult. The real difficulty was how to stabilize each continent and prevent major collisions during the reunification and fusion process, which could lead to even more terrifying earth disasters.

At that time, as long as these four nodes were destroyed and their repair prevented, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect’s plan would be completely thwarted.

On the Eastern Origin Continent, Luo Chen would leave the other minor nodes to the experts from the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world. But this place—he would handle it personally!

Luo Chen’s steps did not stop. Entering the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range, his gaze suddenly fixed on a towering, cloud-piercing peak brimming with spiritual energy.

Its name was Sitting-in-Forgetfulness!

This was the place!

Luo Chen moved silently into the mountain, entering the depths of Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak without alerting anyone.

A flick of his hand, and a banner appeared in his grasp.

The banner was pitch black all over, with faint cracks running along its pole, as if it might break at any moment.

Because of these cracks, furious howls and roars occasionally emanated from within, extremely terrifying.

“Stop howling. Soon, you will be free.”

Luo Chen’s tone carried a hint of a smile, but his expression was murderously cold.

He thrust it down with force. The Million-Soul Baleful Banner suddenly sank into the hard rock and soil, and black lines, like a spiderweb, spread outward in all directions from the banner as its center.

Then, Luo Chen’s ten fingers flew, striking one seal after another onto the baleful banner.

The aura within the banner began to expand violently. A ferocious, ox-like giant beast’s phantom roared out from the banner’s surface, as if wanting to devour anyone in sight.

But under the suppression of those seals, no matter how the Kui Niu ghost spirit roared, it could not break free.

When Luo Chen finished all the seals, the terrifying fifth-rank ghost spirit’s aura was once again suppressed.

From now on, as long as Luo Chen detonated this baleful banner at the right moment, he could release the Kui Niu ghost spirit.

Having done all this, Luo Chen left without a trace.

Without an Ascendant-level great power personally sitting in guard, no one in the entire Heavenly Origin Dao Sect discovered what a terrifying “nuclear bomb” Luo Chen had buried in their mountain gate.

In truth, it shouldn’t have come to this.

The mistake lay back then on the Yellow Spring Sea, when Mo Shouzhuo had called out to Luo Chen, who was about to leave.

Since he wanted Luo Chen to stay, today Luo Chen would return the favor and give the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect a great gift!

…

*Hmm!*

In the year 3780 of the Mountains and Seas Calendar.

On the first day of autumn, a strange sound began to spread outward from Heaven-Earth Island as its center.

This sound was like a heartbeat, like the howling of wind and thunder, full of rhythm and cadence.

Though no one was directing or mobilizing it, the moment the sound spread, the three continents, tens of thousands of li apart, all resonated.

The same sound echoed through heaven and earth together.

Beneath this grand sound, the three continents—Eastern Origin, Southern Heaven, and Libei—began to tremble violently.

An unprecedented great earthquake covered every region.

Houses collapsed, trees uprooted, mountains toppled. For a moment, it seemed like the end of the world had arrived.

Even at the location of the sunken Western Continent, monstrous waves surged, sweeping everything around.

But then, beams of starlight projected down from the sky, and all the earthquake phenomena vanished in an instant.

Not only that!

With these thick starlight pillars as centers, formation lines began to spread rapidly, like the crisscrossing lines on a chessboard, gradually covering every inch of land on the three continents.

Within this dense web of formation lines, the three continents suddenly became as solid as gold!

Then, all the starlight pillars converged in the void, gathering toward Heaven-Earth Island at the center, forming a dome that covered a range of millions of li.

Stars scattered like chess pieces, heaven and earth united as one!

At the center of the dome, a tremendous pulling force radiated outward, beginning to drag the scattered three continents together.

If one had good enough eyesight to look down from the nine heavens above, they would see the three continents slowly closing in toward Heaven-Earth Island at a visible speed.

In the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range, the five remaining late-stage Sages saw this scene and breathed a sigh of relief.

“The spirit vein restrictions have been opened!”

“Earth qi flows without obstruction, the formation operates smoothly.”

“Liancheng Ancestor’s plan is flawless. With this Heaven’s Vault Continent-Gathering Formation, we can indeed reunite the Central Continent!”

“May everything go smoothly.”

“Our side is fine. Now we just have to wait and see how the other two continents fare.”

At the same time.

On the Libei Continent, inside the Dao Palace, seven late-stage Sages sat around the center of the hall, looking at a vivid sandbox beneath them, their expressions full of anticipation.

On the sandbox, though the scenery was miniaturized to the extreme, anyone could tell at a glance that it was a complete map of the entire Libei Continent.

“Aside from the initial tremors caused by the formation’s activation, there are no problems overall.”

“No wonder this is the only sixth-rank formation brought down by our sect when it descended into this realm. This Heaven’s Vault Continent-Gathering Formation is truly miraculous. Once activated, it can move continents without damaging the earth veins in the slightest.”

“Brother Tan, do you not know? During the ancient great war, when our Heavenly Origin Dao Sect established the Central Continent, this formation was activated once. Otherwise, how could the Central Continent’s area far exceed that of the other continents in the Mountains and Seas Realm?”

Amid this cheerful conversation, a cultivator suddenly burst in.

Someone shouted to stop him: “Gu Yuan, who let you in without reporting?”

Gu Yuan’s expression was tense. “Reporting to the Observatory Master, a large number of demon beasts are attacking from the Northern Sea. The numbers are unclear, and the reason is unclear. But according to the observations of the guarding Sage, at least three Great Demon Emperors are among them.”

Hearing this, everyone’s expressions changed drastically.

The one addressed as Observatory Master, Gu Zhongtian, his face cold, pressed down with one hand to signal everyone to calm down.

“Daily skirmishes are one thing, but today is a great day for our sect. These beasts have grown tired of living.”

“Gu Daoyin, Tan Linxian, Gu Muye… Tan Wushuang, you go too. The four of you join forces, annihilate the invading demon beasts completely, leave no survivors!”

“The rest of you, stay with me to guard the Libei Dao Palace. Do not lose your composure.”

Everyone acknowledged with a roar, then took their orders and left.

After they left, the remaining people looked at the still-serious Observatory Master Gu Zhongtian and kindly advised him: “Observatory Master need not worry. Our three great houses preserved our strength relatively intact during the Central Continent catastrophe. Dealing with these beasts should not be a problem.”

Gu Zhongtian shook his head. “I am not worried about Libei. The Eastern Origin Continent has the four great houses and nine Sage-level aristocratic clans guarding it, so it is even less of a problem. What I am worried about is the Southern Heaven Continent!”

At these words, everyone suddenly understood.

Compared to the other two, the Southern Heaven Continent was the weakest in strength.

Originally, the two strongest among the twelve great houses—the Mo family and the Zhan family—had suffered the most severe damage during the Central Continent catastrophe, so much so that after the war, they only received the smallest piece of land, the Southern Heaven Continent, as their resting place.

At this critical moment, if someone attacked the Southern Heaven Continent, it would definitely affect the plan to reunite the Central Continent.

However, contrary to their worries, the Southern Heaven Dao Palace was peaceful and harmonious.

Zhan Bingtao and the rarely seen Mo family patriarch sat facing each other. Beneath their feet, the sandbox displayed the entire topography of the Southern Heaven Continent at a glance.

“Have the four hall masters been sent out?”

“Mm. With our two great houses sitting in the Southern Heaven Continent, that is enough. The four hall masters have been dispatched to the four directions to inspect the various formation nodes and ensure nothing goes wrong.”

“That’s good. Endure these few days, and the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect will be united once more.”

“Liheng, have you ever thought about truly entering the Southern Heaven Dao Palace? You know, once the Central Continent is reunited, the sect’s power structure will not return to its former state. The three Dao Palaces will become platforms for us to contend.”

“Let’s talk about it later. The Mo family can no longer withstand any more turmoil.”

…

Southern Heaven Continent, southeast direction.

Fu Qinglan rode a cloud carriage, continuously patrolling this region.

From time to time, Core Formation disciples from the Rear Earth Hall would return from outside, reporting the corresponding observations.

Everything was ordinary. No one disobeyed orders, and no enemies appeared.

Suddenly!

A figure rushed back in a hurry.

From within the cloud carriage, a woman’s beautiful voice came out.

“Fu Bin, why are you so anxious?”

Fu Bin bowed and said, “Disciple has discovered that a Sage-level aristocratic clan, disregarding orders, has begun moving toward the outer sea after the formation was activated.”

“Oh? Which clan is so bold?”

Fu Bin took a deep breath and said seriously, “The Zhou clan!”

On the Southern Heaven Continent, there were not many Sage-level aristocratic clans—at least not as many as the nine on the Eastern Origin Continent.

And among them, the one surnamed Zhou was unique.

“The clan where the mayfly is?” The voice in the cloud carriage changed subtly.

Fu Bin nodded. “Yes, the Zhou clan of White Egret Marsh!”

There was a moment of silence in the cloud carriage.

The next moment, a ripple of mana spread gently, and a graceful figure stepped out from within.

“Your master will go take a look. You continue with your previous patrol tasks.”

With those words, the woman transformed into a beam of cyan light and disappeared into the sky.

(End of chapter)
