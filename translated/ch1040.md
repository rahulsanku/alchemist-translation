# Chapter 1040: The Great Urn Seeks Qi, an Ancestor Visits the Mountain

With the complete subjugation of the Kui Ox, Luo Chen's hundred-thousand-soul banner finally lived up to its name.

At its peak sat a fifth-rank ghost spirit as the main soul; at its foundation lay a million low-ranking resentful souls solidifying the base; and in between were no lack of third-rank and fourth-rank Ghost Kings and Ghost Emperors.

Even if it could only be used once or twice, as long as he could condense the Kui Ox divine body at a critical moment, it meant Luo Chen had a helper comparable to an Ascendant.

Only at this moment did Luo Chen breathe a slight sigh of relief.

Whether it was the Blood Jade Three-Flame Fan, his powerful primordial spirit, or his various methods and divine abilities, none of them gave Luo Chen a truly solid feeling.

After all, the great enemies he envisioned were not only Ascendant-level powerhouses but also the terrifying millennia-deep foundation of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

In particular, during the great calamity of Central Continent back then, the large number of Ascendant-level projections that descended from the Star Gate passageway were something no other sect in the Mountains and Seas Realm could match.

Including the other four Sacred Lands!

Although he had heard that the Star Gate had mysteriously disappeared, and even the traditional Star Gate guest elder system had collapsed, it was hard to guarantee that the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect didn't still have similar means to seek aid from the Mystic Realm.

This Kui Ox ghost spirit was one of Luo Chen's major trump cards.

When things became untenable, he could also imitate Rosycloud, driving the ghost spirit to self-destruct, leaving himself a path of retreat.

"Next, it's time to cultivate the sword canon!"

Murmuring to himself, a wisp of rosy light appeared at Luo Chen's fingertip.

It was the Red Firmament Qi among the Nine Great Firmament Qi!

According to records in ancient texts, these special gases were natural products formed when turbid qi sank, clear qi rose, and various attribute spiritual qi gathered in the azure sky. They were the most essential substances between heaven and earth!

But as Luo Chen came into contact with more and more of them, refining large amounts of Firmament Qi, his understanding of their essence grew deeper.

At this moment, in his view, Firmament Qi was not merely that simple.

Firmament Qi was born in the extreme azure sky, backed by one side of heaven and earth, facing the boundless cosmos.

The reason it was so overbearing was very likely because it absorbed certain special substances from the universe, hence its formidable power.

Nine Spirit Primogenitor had not comprehended the true meaning of laws, but relying solely on refining large amounts of Firmament Qi, her strength was no less than that of her peers who had comprehended law true meaning.

Especially the Great Nine Firmament Heavenly River, whose power was comparable to divine abilities!

It was hard to compare the strengths of the *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon* and the *Great Nine Firmament Heavenly River*, but in Luo Chen's view, purely in terms of lethality, the Thousand Skies Sword Qi was at least one or two grades higher.

"Currently, I still have a hundred wisps of Firmament Qi left in my hands. Even if I refine them all, it won't be enough to raise my proficiency in this sword art to the Grandmaster level, let alone Great Perfection."

"It's time to capture wild Firmament Qi."

Luo Chen cast his gaze into this void, his eyes full of ambition.

Relying on luck to capture Firmament Qi was mostly a matter of chance.

Often, it would take several years to encounter one or two wisps.

That wouldn't help Luo Chen much.

Fortunately, he had long prepared for this!

With a flick of his wrist, accompanied by a dull *dong*, a large black urn landed before him.

It was the inheritance true artifact of the Nine Spirit Sect—the Nine Firmament Urn!

Relying on this treasure, the Nine Spirit Sect had been able to continuously capture large amounts of Firmament Qi, supporting Nine Spirit Primogenitor in cultivating the formidable technique of the *Great Nine Firmament Heavenly River*.

If not for the two-hundred-year Eastern Wilderness War, which prevented Nine Spirit Primogenitor from peacefully collecting Firmament Qi, she would have been even more powerful.

With his attainments in the art of artifact refinement, Luo Chen could vaguely discern some clues about this true artifact.

The main material was some special mineral with an extremely strong sensitivity to Firmament Qi.

The formations engraved on it were primarily for collection and suppression, with additional formations specifically arranged to assist the cultivator in expanding their divine sense search range.

As for assistance in combat offense and defense, it was not so obvious.

Clearly, this was a treasure specially refined for exploring, capturing, and even refining Firmament Qi.

Its method of use was not very complicated either: driven by dharma power, guided by divine sense, centered on the cultivator themselves, exploring the surroundings.

The stronger the divine sense, the wider the search range.

This was a great advantage for Luo Chen!

His current divine sense intensity could be said to be unmatched under heaven, making his efficiency in searching for Firmament Qi far greater than that of ordinary Sages.

"Then I'll multitask—refining the Red Firmament Sword Qi while searching for more Nine Firmament Qi!"

Luo Chen smiled slightly, sending a wisp of dharma power into the Nine Firmament Urn, his divine sense slowly spreading outward through the great urn.

To be honest, after being amplified by this treasure, Luo Chen's divine sense had a different kind of feeling.

Normally, whether it was divine sense, spiritual sense, or even the divine thoughts of an Ascendant powerhouse, they mostly sensed living beings, the "spirit" contained within them.

But after amplification by the Nine Firmament Urn, Luo Chen suddenly discovered that his divine sense also had a subtle sensitivity to objects without "spirit."

Especially Firmament Qi!

"Oh, what a coincidence! Eight hundred li away, there's a wisp of Firmament Qi?"

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow, a smile curling at the corner of his mouth.

With his robes rustling, he took a step, transforming into a streak of light that shot toward the distant azure sky.

...

In the Zhou clan's Drinking Green Waterside Pavilion in the Tiannan Continent.

Two young figures sat silently facing each other, eating small mouthfuls of steaming hot meals.

These meals were all imbued with abundant spiritual qi, the kind of spiritual feasts that low-level cultivators would drool over.

But for the two of them, no matter how delicious the dishes, they tasted bitter and tasteless in their mouths.

Time passed bit by bit, eventually reaching the moment of parting and dispersal.

*Pat!*

Chopsticks fell, and Zhou Fuyou raised his head, looking at the quiet woman across from him.

"Mao Mao, I'm leaving."

The woman lowered her head, chewing the distinct grains of rice, and said softly, "Mm."

"Before I leave, I've arranged everything. My share of cultivation resources will be collected by you from now on. Third Elder will personally teach you. Also, I specially applied to the Ancestor for a defensive magic treasure. When you build your foundation in the future, remember to refine it."

"I understand."

"Granny Lin is getting old. She might not have many years left before she passes away. When that happens, you must remember to take care of yourself in daily life. Don't be sparing with those servants; you can drive them as much as you like."

"I will take care of myself."

"Finally, I've considered your cultivation matters. As long as you work hard enough and have sufficient resources, you have a good chance of building your foundation. But after foundation building, your Dan spirit root will disappear. Relying solely on spiritual sense and qi sea, your cultivation will ultimately be slower than others. So, I think you can try switching to martial arts, relying on body refinement to reach a higher level. The large amount of lifespan provided by foundation building should support you in reaching the third stage of body refinement."

As Zhou Fuyou spoke, he took out a thick tome from his storage pouch.

"I don't have much experience with martial arts body refinement, but I've read martial classics since childhood and obtained a large amount of martial body refinement knowledge from the clan's scripture pavilion and the Ancestor. I've recorded it all in here. When you have free time from cultivation, be sure to read it and prepare for the future."

Every word and sentence was filled with deep care and affection.

The woman finally raised her head, tears welling up in her eyes.

"Brother, are you not coming back in the future?"

Zhou Fuyou shook his head, revealing a doting smile.

"How could that be? I'm just going to the Tiannan Dao Palace for advanced studies. Once I establish myself there, I'll come to take you. Then we siblings will cultivate together and become disciples of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect."

Zhou Qingyu asked tearfully, "Then why are you arranging so many things, like you're settling your final affairs?"

Zhou Fuyou was momentarily stunned.

He actually didn't know either.

With the Ancestor's promise, the clan would certainly not mistreat his sister, but he still couldn't rest easy, arranging everything, even considering the path his sister would take after foundation building.

Why was that?

The unease in his heart, or the confusion about the future?

Zhou Fuyou instinctively wanted to comfort her, "I just want you to live..."

"Brother, rest assured and go to the Dao Palace. I will definitely cultivate hard!" Zhou Qingyu interrupted him, wiped the tears from her face, and revealed a determined expression.

Zhou Fuyou opened his mouth but ultimately said nothing.

He had the servants come in to clear the table, then took Zhou Qingyu for a stroll through the Drinking Green Waterside Pavilion, gradually walking into the clan grounds.

Every place they went brought back many childhood memories.

The two siblings walked quietly, and every Zhou clan member they met greeted them respectfully.

It wasn't until late at night that they returned home and quietly fell asleep.

A night passed, and at the break of dawn.

At the entrance of the Drinking Green Waterside Pavilion, several figures whispered softly.

"Let's go!"

Zhou Yunshen waved his hand, releasing an unadorned flying boat, beckoning Zhou Fuyou to board.

Zhou Fuyou looked back. His sister stood beside the Third Elder, biting her lip, waving her hand.

"Brother, goodbye!"

Third Elder Zhou Shuzhen bowed respectfully, "Wishing the Ancestor a smooth journey!"

Zhou Yunshen smiled kindly. After Zhou Fuyou came aboard, he flicked his sleeve, and the flying boat lightly rose into the clouds.

In a flash, it disappeared into the vast layers of clouds.

...

Not long after leaving the Zhou clan, the flying boat carrying the old and the young hovered beside a great river.

Near the river was a deep valley, where many figures were coming and going, and occasionally streaks of light would rise.

This was Cold River Valley!

Zhou Fuyou looked at the Ancestor in confusion, not understanding why he had brought him here.

Zhou Yunshen did not avoid the topic. Spreading his divine sense, he said calmly, "Since joining the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, our Zhou clan has always been cautious, never offending others, but also never fearing anyone! A mere Cold River Valley Yin clan dares to covet the Zhou clan and use underhanded means. They deserve their punishment."

As he spoke, he snorted coldly and raised one hand lightly.

The next moment, the surging great river roared as it broke free from its channel, rushing directly toward Cold River Valley.

Not only that, but some of the demon beasts within it instantly turned red-eyed and began slaughtering the Yin clan members.

Blood spread wildly.

Occasionally, powerful Adept cultivators flew out, but they inexplicably couldn't use their strength and were pounced upon and devoured by powerful demon beasts.

The river changed course, enslaving living beings, tens of thousands of lives vanishing in an instant.

Watching this scene, Zhou Fuyou couldn't help but swallow his saliva.

Was this the might of a Sage?

But was doing this really appropriate?

Seeming to sense the young man's inner worry, Zhou Yunshen laughed, "Earthquake, river diversion, demon beasts running amok, slaughtering the Yin clan! Such a reason can be accepted by many."

"But how will the Tiannan Dao Palace respond?"

Zhou Fuyou was still puzzled.

He had thoroughly read the classics related to Central Continent.

The vast Central Continent was led by the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, supplemented by the Twelve Great Houses, Thirty-Six Aristocratic Clans, and three thousand humble families.

The Dao Palace encouraged competition but absolutely forbade meaningless slaughter.

Even though Central Continent had now collapsed into three landmasses and Heaven-Earth Island, the Dao Sect's control remained.

The Dao Palace on the Tiannan Continent was a banner!

As long as this banner stood, the competition among the major forces below could only be limited to a certain extent.

The Cold River Valley Yin clan's overnight annihilation would inevitably invite the Dao Palace's inquiry.

To this, Zhou Yunshen still showed an unconcerned expression.

"As long as you are still here, even if the Dao Palace suspects it was me, suspects the Zhou clan is the murderer, they won't hold the Zhou clan accountable. Instead, after this incident, it will deter the major forces within ten thousand li, bringing another hundred years of peace to the Zhou clan."

"As for a hundred years later..."

Zhou Yunshen looked at Zhou Fuyou.

Zhou Fuyou suddenly understood, "A hundred years later, as long as I am still here, I can certainly continue to protect the Zhou clan!"

"Perhaps!"

Zhou Yunshen smiled, not even looking back at the hellish Cold River Valley. He maneuvered the flying boat once more into the high sky.

Behind them, the rolling river washed away the blood, returning to its channel, while ahead lay a vast and unobstructed road.

Having casually destroyed an Adept-level clan, Zhou Yunshen didn't take it to heart at all. Instead, he asked about Zhou Fuyou's cultivation progress.

"What is your current realm?"

"Reporting to the Ancestor, I am now at the sixth layer of Initiation."

"Oh, so fast?"

The young man did not hide it, revealing his spiritual power fluctuations.

Zhou Yunshen examined him carefully for a long time, clicking his tongue in admiration.

"Such dharma power fluctuations, such spiritual sense—they can no longer be judged by common standards of realm."

In the cultivation world, judging a cultivator's realm often used the strength of dharma power fluctuations as the standard.

And this standard was mostly based on ordinary breakthroughs and cultivators of average talent at the same rank.

But if one encountered someone with a special constitution, terrifyingly abundant dharma power, beyond common sense, it became difficult to judge.

At least in Zhou Yunshen's view, the spiritual power fluctuations rippling around Zhou Fuyou had already surpassed many who had just entered the Adept realm.

The only difference was probably the gap between spiritual power and dharma power!

"Put it away!"

After Zhou Fuyou withdrew his spiritual power fluctuations, showing no trace of anything unusual, Zhou Yunshen stroked his graying beard and began to offer guidance.

"Entering the late Initiation stage is not difficult for you. Next, you need to consider how to form your core."

"And forming a core has countless methods, each with its own nuances."

"Finding the method most suitable for you can double your results with half the effort."

Zhou Fuyou immediately realized the Ancestor's intention.

This trip to send him to the Tiannan Dao Palace was a long journey, but the flying boat the Ancestor piloted was not particularly fast.

Clearly, the old man wanted to take the opportunity to guide him.

Zhou Fuyou did not hesitate and bowed respectfully: "I ask for the Ancestor's guidance!"

Zhou Yunshen smiled slightly, "First, I will teach you a secret technique, the most core core-forming secret technique of our Zhou clan. As long as you complete the prerequisite steps, it can increase your chances of forming a core by thirty percent!"

Hearing this, Zhou Fuyou's eyes immediately lit up.

...

The flying boat traveled westward, neither fast nor slow.

On the boat, the old and the young spoke softly, teaching earnestly.

This teaching was not even limited to cultivation knowledge; it also touched on combat.

Occasionally, the flying boat would stop, and the young man would go down to find opponents for battle.

The enemies he fought ranged from late Initiation grandmasters at the beginning to, later on, frequent appearances of early Adept and even mid-Adept cultivators.

Such abnormal behavior naturally aroused the curiosity of the major forces along the way, but under the unfathomable pressure Zhou Yunshen revealed, no one dared to resist.

Fortunately, Zhou Fuyou did not kill. He merely followed Zhou Yunshen's guidance, using battles with higher-ranked experts to test his cultivation progress.

Time passed day by day.

Thus, half a year later.

This year, Zhou Fuyou was twenty-one years old.

The flying boat hovered outside a mountain range shrouded in mist and dense spiritual qi.

"The Tiannan Dao Palace has arrived!"

Zhou Yunshen stood up, looking down at the mountain range below, a look of regret and desolation in his eyes.

No wonder it was once the strongest sacred land in the Mountains and Seas Realm, no wonder it was the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect that once possessed an entire continent.

Even though Central Continent had collapsed, on this Tiannan Continent, there was still a fifth-rank spiritual vein!

If he were in his prime, if he had comprehended the origin of water earlier, with this spiritual vein, he could have completely charged toward the Ascendant realm.

Unfortunately, there was no "if."

Taking a deep breath, Zhou Yunshen slowly spoke.

"The Zhou clan's ancestor and descendant come to visit the mountain!"

In an instant, the vast sound spread across eight thousand li, layers of mist rolling back, revealing lofty mountains, flowing water, and jade towers.

Was this the full view of the Tiannan Dao Palace?

Zhou Fuyou looked at the rows upon rows of palaces and temples, and the powerful auras erupting from various places, his heart filled with longing.

This was where he would cultivate in the future!

Reveal his talent, join the Dao Palace, stand out, become a true disciple, and ultimately become a pivotal figure in the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

(Chapter End)
