# Chapter 691: Source Power Appears Again, the Pill Realm Shakes

Pills were divided into nine tiers. The first three tiers were spirit pills and wondrous medicines in the common sense.

At fourth tier and beyond, because their medicinal nature was grand and strange, they were called golden pills.

Even if a pill's grade reached the ninth tier, it could still be called a Nine-Turn Golden Pill!

Among these many grades, only fifth-tier pills were quite different, carrying a symbol of the "divine and sacred."

Whether divine pills or sacred medicines, they contained the fortune of heaven and earth.

Therefore, there was an existence in Central Continent of the Mountains and Seas Realm called the Pill Saint, and the reason was that this person could refine fifth-tier sacred medicines.

When Luo Chen first entered the Dao of Pills, he had let his imagination soar and had once spoken wild words, saying that one day he would stand shoulder to shoulder with the Pill Saint.

But those were, in the end, only wild words.

He had not expected that one day, he would truly attempt such a grand undertaking.

Only, the reason a grand undertaking was called a grand undertaking was precisely because such a thing could not be done by ordinary people.

One month later,

Atop the sheer peak, an explosive sound came from within the great Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Powerful medicinal force broke through the restrictions of the pill seals, forming a circular ripple that swept in every direction.

Boom!

Luo Chen's sleeves whipped wildly, dispersing most of the impact, but a small amount of aftershock still struck his body and sent him flying several hundred zhang away.

On the ground, qi armor rose in roaring flames. It was the Mystic Dust Armor spontaneously generating qi armor to block those remaining shockwaves.

Luo Chen slowly flew back, his face gloomy.

Failure had come faster than he imagined.

And the degree of failure was even more difficult to accept.

The problem had appeared at the very first stage, the decomposition and fusion of medicinal materials.

The main material, the Jade Palace Vermilion Fruit, had not gained intelligence, yet it contained an incomparably pure force. It was precisely that force that blocked his refinement.

Arriving before the Primordial Unity Cauldron, Luo Chen waved his sleeve to disperse the pill stench that made one want to vomit, then personally entered the cauldron.

Soon, he carried out a large pile of scorched medicinal dregs.

Spreading them over the jade disc he had prepared on the ground, Luo Chen studied them carefully.

Several days later, Luo Chen's expression eased slightly.

"The methods and steps were all correct. The issue was in the details."

"As expected, since the system entered it for me, it acknowledges that I have the ability to refine it successfully. Then perhaps I must carefully ponder these details."

Luo Chen sank into contemplation.

...

Half a month later, the pill-refining work began again.

This time, Luo Chen slowed his movements greatly when decomposing and fusing the medicinal materials.

Not only that, he opened his spirit eyes wide, striving not to miss the slightest detail.

It had to be said that the spirit-eye art he had cultivated to Great Completion in his youth had played a great role throughout Luo Chen's cultivation life. Whether formations, pill refinement, cultivation, or battle, it had repeatedly produced miraculous effects.

Now, when refining a fifth-tier divine pill, it made Luo Chen even more like a tiger with wings.

In this way, the first step alone took Luo Chen a full three months before barely succeeding.

The price it brought was swollen eyeballs covered in blood vessels.

Once the Law-Derivation Pill began refinement, the process could not be interrupted.

Even though Luo Chen's eyes were uncomfortable, he still persisted through the subsequent steps.

The refinement method for this pill was not a single method in the ordinary sense. It required three methods refined together. Under the mutual aid of water and fire, qi-method refinement then completed the ending.

Fire-method pill refinement was Luo Chen's specialty.

As for water-method pill refinement, after refining Imperial Moonlight Nectar and Black Emperor Ointment over the years, he could be said to do it with ease.

Therefore, everything seemed to arrive naturally.

Another three months later, Luo Chen was full of expectation.

"The medicinal force fusion lacks only one final step. Next, I should use Qi-Control Art to perform qi-method pill refinement."

At that moment, because his mind relaxed for a moment, a low sound came from within the pill cauldron.

"This..."

In his astonishment, Luo Chen had already realized something was wrong.

As expected, in his perception, the pill within the cauldron had not formed and could not receive qi and gather spirit.

"One step from the door. I was only missing that final step!"

Luo Chen gnashed his teeth, filled with unwillingness.

But no matter how unwilling he was, he could only break it apart and swallow it into his stomach.

Cleaning the pill cauldron, gathering the medicinal dregs, studying the failed pill, reflecting on himself: this process, familiar from more than a hundred years of practice, was something Luo Chen performed step by step without tiring.

Starting over from the beginning was something he was more familiar with than anyone.

...

It was still that sheer peak.

Fifteen years had passed since Luo Chen entered Demon-Fall Land. Fourteen years had passed since he entered the Pill Realm. Two years had passed since he entered the fifth trial.

And more than a year had passed since he began refining the Law-Derivation Pill.

During this year and more, he had opened the cauldron to refine pills seven times in succession.

Seven times, all failures!

The reason for each failure was different.

Sometimes the main material had been processed improperly. Sometimes the fusion of medicinal force was flawed. Sometimes, under long-term tension, his mind lost its guard and one misstep caused eternal regret. There was even failure from insufficient mana, when the transition between the Golden Core and Origin Pill was improper, causing an abrupt halt.

The most serious time, the formed Law-Derivation Pill directly exploded. Its terrifying, surging medicinal force shook the Primordial Unity Cauldron violently without cease.

If not for the Primordial Unity Cauldron's excellent material, that explosion alone would have shattered any magic-treasure-grade pill cauldron to pieces.

Although the fifth trial had backup pill-refining vessels left by Devil Lord Heaven-Refining, Luo Chen was still accustomed to his own lifebound magic treasure.

And this most serious incident had happened not long ago.

Luo Chen rested for more than half a month, not only recovering his mana, but also sacrificially refining and nurturing the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

He sat cross-legged atop the mountain peak, still reflecting.

He was not only reflecting on past causes of failure, but also taking stock of what he had gained from refining pills this past year.

Refining pills across tiers, to him, brought a full harvest even in failure.

First was the growing activity of the Withering-Bloom fire spirit!

With a thought, a flame emerged from above his head and finally formed an azure fire tree.

As the fire tree swayed, it spat out flames. Its movements formed strands of branches.

Firelight spread like an azure canopy, strand after strand drooping down to cover Luo Chen's head.

Feeling the vigorous vitality of the fire tree, Luo Chen himself felt extremely delighted.

After devouring large numbers of sourceless fire spirits in Blazing Refinement Prison, the Withering-Bloom True Fire's spirituality had surged. Yet Luo Chen had found that Withering-Bloom fire somewhat difficult to control, so much so that its power was not condensed enough.

Now, after more than ten years of pill refinement in the Pill Realm, and especially with the Law-Derivation Pill refinement, Luo Chen's control over the Withering-Bloom True Fire had gradually recovered to the point where it responded as he wished.

His fingers formed a seal, and the branches of the fire tree gently brushed down.

Layer upon layer of ripples spread through the void.

Seeing this scene, Luo Chen was incomparably satisfied.

"This is the true power of my Withering-Bloom True Fire!"

"If I faced Shark Emperor Liujun now, even without the geographic advantage of Blazing Refinement Prison, relying on the Withering-Bloom True Fire alone would give me a forty-forty or sixty-forty chance. Against other more normal Sage Perfected Beings, the Withering-Bloom True Fire could be a great killing move!"

The seals dispersed, and the azure fire tree sank into Luo Chen's body.

And this was only the first gain.

The second gain lay within Luo Chen's qi sea.

One crimson and one pure-white Golden Core responded to each other like the Supreme Ultimate's two poles, rotating without cease.

The Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture circulated, and the two streams of mana advanced and retreated together, or surged from each core separately.

This kind of mana output that did not distinguish between one and the other and connected as he wished was precisely what Luo Chen had gained from one failure caused by a mana transition problem.

In the past, he had never cared about this flaw.

If the second Origin Pill's mana ran out, he could simply switch to the lifebound Golden Core. Why consider the matter of connection?

It was precisely after experiencing that failure that he slightly converted the mana between the two Golden Cores, and only then achieved this state of no distinction between the two.

At this point, although the lifebound Golden Core and second Origin Pill had a primary and secondary distinction, in essence they no longer had any difference.

If Luo Chen could advance to the Sage stage, he had great confidence that he could condense a second Nascent Soul according to the Origin Pill Art Han Zhan had taught him!

At that time, when Luo Chen first entered the Sage stage, perhaps he would already possess extremely strong combat power.

At the very least, he would not be weaker than Han Zhan's Sage-stage puppet from back then!

The third gain was the lifebound magic treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron!

Seven attempts at refining the Law-Derivation Pill, seven failures. Between each failure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron had suffered more or less minor damage.

After Luo Chen nurtured and repaired it, he unknowingly discovered traces that the Primordial Unity Cauldron had advanced another step.

He even discovered that the cauldron's power in suppression and capture had multiplied!

After carefully analyzing it, Luo Chen faintly perceived that some of the Law-Derivation Pill's materials had been absorbed by the Primordial Unity Cauldron, producing these changes.

At this point, even Luo Chen did not know whether the cauldron was refining the pill, or the pill was refining the cauldron.

After reflecting for a while, Luo Chen came back to himself.

He sighed faintly.

"So what if I have gained much?"

"Until the Law-Derivation Pill succeeds, all of it is false!"

"Can I succeed next time?"

For a time, he was not even certain whether the system law he had summarized in earlier years, that one success was guaranteed within ten attempts, could still trigger.

That was because a fifth-tier divine pill had completely exceeded his range of alchemy understanding.

With this uneasy and humble mindset, Luo Chen began the eighth refinement atop the sheer peak.

And this time, it was as if divine aid had arrived!

...

The moment the pill took shape, Luo Chen felt relieved, yet did not dare to relax in the slightest.

Although the pill had formed, this was like walking a hundred li and stopping at ninety.

Only the step of guiding qi into the pill remained.

According to the established plan, he cast Qi-Control Art.

In an instant, currents of air surged from every direction like furious dragons!

Under Luo Chen's repeatedly changing hand seals, these furious dragons of spiritual qi sank one after another into the Primordial Unity Cauldron, then were absorbed by the Law-Derivation Pill in different sequences.

At this moment, Luo Chen's emotions rose and fell.

"According to the pill formula, this finishing work only requires three months. After three months, the pill will be complete!"

He held his breath and concentrated. The two Golden Cores in his body continuously released mana, helping him guide external spiritual qi.

Gradually, Luo Chen discovered the signs.

In the void, spiritual qi returned like swallows to the nest.

On the earth, spiritual qi erupted.

Countless spirit veins planted with strange flowers and unusual grasses were trembling and releasing.

It was as if the entire Pill Realm were providing assistance for the birth of the Law-Derivation Pill.

Under this vast power, Luo Chen's act of using two Golden Cores to move the majestic spiritual qi of the outside world seemed like a child lifting a hammer, so strained and beyond his heart's ability.

He clenched his teeth bitterly and held on. One day, two days... one month, two months...

When three months passed, he looked with a pale face toward the Primordial Unity Cauldron. The pill still rotated endlessly within, shining like a star.

A feeling called hunger spontaneously arose.

"It is still not enough!"

Luo Chen laughed bitterly and released the seals of Qi-Control Art.

At this moment, the spiritual qi tide all retreated.

He stamped through the air. An explosive sound rang out, and he appeared a hundred zhang away.

He looked nervously toward the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Calm, just like the dead silence in his heart.

There was no cauldron explosion.

Nor was there success.

With his mana almost exhausted, Luo Chen slowly returned beside the Primordial Unity Cauldron and reached out to summon the Law-Derivation Pill.

What entered his eyes was yellow-gold and brilliant, like the Nine-Turn Golden Pill of immortal legends.

But Luo Chen knew this was an incomplete waste pill.

It had not absorbed enough heaven-earth spiritual qi, and could not possibly reach the effect of allowing Sage Perfected Beings or even Ascendant Perfected Beings to merge spirit with heaven and earth.

With a long sigh, Luo Chen carefully stored this "waste pill."

He patted the Primordial Unity Cauldron and sat down atop the sheer peak.

Beneath him, spiritual qi weakened by the formation slowly surged upward.

As Luo Chen absorbed spiritual qi to recover mana, he searched for the reason for that failure.

"Three months!"

"I clearly persisted for three months. Why did it still fail?"

Luo Chen was full of confusion.

...

Only when Luo Chen's mana had completely recovered to fullness did he find the answer.

"Those three months corresponded to at least three months for a Sage Perfected Being, not three months for a Golden Core cultivator!"

Under the night curtain, looking at the dreamlike stars in the clear night sky, Luo Chen murmured in a daze.

The same spell, in the hands of cultivators of different realms, had utterly different power.

The simplest Fireball spell was an example!

Although first tier, could a Fireball spell cast by an ordinary Qi Refinement cultivator be the same as one cast by Luo Chen at the Golden Core stage?

The answer was obvious.

If Luo Chen cast Fireball, let alone Qi Refinement cultivators, even Initiates would be killed with a flick of his finger.

By the same principle, the heaven-earth spiritual qi that Qi-Control Art could control and guide would also be affected by the cultivator's realm.

Even though Luo Chen had cultivated this art to Grandmaster proficiency, his own hardware could not reach the level of a Sage Perfected Being.

"Could it be that I stop here?"

Murmuring, Luo Chen was full of frustration.

...

In the days that followed, Luo Chen did not open the furnace to refine pills again.

If he could not resolve the final finishing step, then no matter how many times he refined, all would fail. Why waste effort?

He simply practiced Qi-Control Art day after day.

He tried placing hope in cultivating this art to Great Completion. Perhaps things would change then.

But on the panel, the increase in Qi-Control Art proficiency was like picking sand with a needle. It barely rose at all.

Luo Chen knew this was the limitation of realm.

Grandmaster level was already the limit of his current realm.

"Could it be that even this path is blocked?"

When another day ended, Luo Chen lay on the green rock, his expression dazed.

"But since the system entered it for me, it confirms that I have a chance to succeed. Where is that chance?"

"Qi-Control Art... three months..."

Gradually, Luo Chen seemed to realize something.

...

On this day,

Luo Chen formed familiar seals and flew between heaven and earth, strands of spiritual qi circling beside him.

Suddenly,

With a thought, he cut off the mana supply from his qi sea.

Those strands of spiritual qi were about to scatter like birds and beasts.

At that moment, Luo Chen gave a low shout, and another force erupted from his body.

It was a force bright red as blood!

Its name was source power!

It was a kind of power that had appeared after Luo Chen advanced his body refinement to Archaic Tier Four before leaving Peng Lake.

It hid within the body, waiting for the proper time to move.

The last time he used it had been after his left hand was severed, when he used it to perform the method of reconnecting a severed limb.

Aside from that, it had only been added to those body techniques when he transformed into the Heavenly Roc True Body.

Subconsciously, he had not treated this power as something similar to mana that could drive techniques.

But after searching bitterly without result, he remembered this power.

"Wang Yuan cultivated the body, possessed vigorous qi and blood, evolved pill force, and after reaching third tier, even generated astral force. That so-called astral force is said to stand shoulder to shoulder with the mana of Golden Core cultivators."

"Calculated like this, my source power gained through body refinement should also count as a special kind of energy!"

"It may even be that because its grade is so high, it belongs to the same level as the mana of Sage cultivators."

"If I use it to cast Qi-Control Art, can it rival a Sage Perfected Being controlling heaven-earth spiritual qi with majestic mana?"

Ever since this thought had inexplicably been born, Luo Chen had been testing its feasibility.

After spending a full month, he understood!

This time, a thin strand of source power cast Qi-Control Art.

In an instant!

Boom! Boom! Boom!

Around Luo Chen, spiritual qi surged in wild waves, angry dragons rolled, and one heaven-reaching air column after another rose from earth to sky with him at the center!

"So that is how it is!"

Luo Chen scattered his hands, and clear light appeared in his eyes again.

...

Atop the sheer peak with fourth-tier spiritual qi concentration, the familiar figure once more began the usual pill-refining steps.

Setting up the cauldron, lighting the fire, and pouring medicinal materials like rain.

Many alchemy arts came to hand with ease.

Mana flowed, water and fire aiding each other.

Every movement had already been simulated countless times in Luo Chen's mind over the years.

Eight failures, each one deeper than the last.

But that also meant each one was closer to success.

This was the ninth attempt.

It would certainly be the last attempt as well!

Several months later, the embryonic pill appeared.

Luo Chen's finger seals fluttered like butterflies, and Qi-Control Art appeared once more.

Under the guidance of mana, one stream of spiritual qi after another surged from every direction.

Three months later, just as he felt his mana becoming insufficient, Luo Chen's spirit seals abruptly changed!

Bright-red source power surfaced from his body, continuing to maintain Qi-Control Art.

Only, he did not make grand, sweeping movements and fully unleash it. Instead, he maintained roughly the same strength as before to guide the spiritual qi of heaven and earth.

In this way, although there was no majestic scene of air columns soaring into the sky as when he practiced Qi-Control Art with source power before, the advantage lay in its continuous, long-lasting flow.

His bright eyes stared fixedly at the Primordial Unity Cauldron, striving not to miss even the slightest change within.

Time flowed slowly according to a rule that allowed no one's will to interfere.

Three months, five months, one year... unknowingly, two and a half years had already passed.

At this point, Luo Chen could not believe why it still had not ended.

He had used nearly ten times the time to guide the spiritual qi of heaven and earth into the Law-Derivation Pill, yet the pill still had not completed.

At this moment, the mana in his qi sea was empty and insufficient, and his two Golden Cores were shriveled and contracted.

Within his body, the bright-red source power he once had was also nearly gone.

Even his eye sockets were dry, faintly leaking bloody tears.

His divine soul was exhausted to the extreme, as if a single thought would make him faint.

"Still not enough?"

His fingertip seals persisted bitterly. His fingers looked like deadwood, the result of a massive extraction of qi and blood.

Luo Chen even felt that the vast sea of vitality in his body had thinned into a stream, nearly severed.

Source power originated from the great power of his own body.

Once vitality was severed, there would be no more source power.

"I refuse to accept this!"

"This is the closest I have come to success. How can I fail!"

Luo Chen's expression turned fierce. From his storage ring, a handful of pills was stuffed into his mouth.

Among them were pills to replenish mana and pills to strengthen the body.

Even the Stone-Skin, Thunderbone, and Mercury-Blood Pills he had refined in the Pill Realm not long ago were included.

Relying on this batch of pills, Luo Chen persisted for another two months.

Two months later, it still was not done!

"I still have a spirit medicine that can replenish vitality."

"Again!"

With a thought, a huge white lump of flesh appeared before him. It was plump and soft like jelly, yet also crystal clear.

It was astonishingly Tai Sui's main body!

He opened his mouth and bit off a large chunk of Tai Sui.

As if feeling that was not enough, he bit down four more times in succession.

His sharp teeth, as hard as magic treasures, chewed mightily. This Tai Sui spirit medicine, known as clear as solid ice, long-lived and ageless, was continuously refined by him, finally transforming into source power.

Qi-Control Art, unfold again!

This time, Luo Chen no longer acted cautiously. He directly urged it with all his strength.

He would exhaust everything he had and complete the work in one battle!

In an instant, the Pill Realm shook.

That familiar scene appeared once more.

In the sky, wind and thunder roared. The earth trembled, and spirit veins split open.

Vigorous spiritual qi came rolling like ocean waves.

One day, two days... When another three months passed, Luo Chen's body suddenly stiffened.

Three years ended here.

His body toppled down unwillingly.

At the moment he lost consciousness, he saw a golden pill suffused with brilliant light, carrying a trace of blood color, slowly fly out from the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

By his ear, the weak voice seemed to appear again.

Splash!

Water surged, as if he had fallen into a cold spring.

*(End of Chapter)*
