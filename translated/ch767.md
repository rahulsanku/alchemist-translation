# Chapter 767: Laughing to the Sky, I Go Out the Gate. How Could We Be Mere Weeds?

"Withering-Bloom!"

As Luo Chen's finger fell, his thoughts seemed to return to that earlier muddled, chaotic experience.

Inside the sea of origin qi that made him feel incomparably close to it, he followed the guidance of the Withering-Bloom fire spirit and found the so-called true meaning of the laws: Withering-Bloom!

This thing was not spiritual qi, nor origin qi. It was not even a kind of substantial energy. It was more like a realization.

The moment one comprehended it, one already knew in the unseen many changes in the myriad things of the world related to the true meaning of Withering-Bloom!

Flowers bloom and flowers fall, clouds roll and clouds unfurl.

The sun rises and the moon floats up, birth, aging, sickness, and death.

Everything was merely the two faces of withering and blooming.

While inside the sea of origin qi, the speed of that realization was thousands or hundreds of times faster than comprehending by oneself. It was as though the entire world had placed before Luo Chen all the myriad changes related to the true meaning of Withering-Bloom, letting him pick them up at will.

Unfortunately, Luo Chen had borrowed external force after all.

The power of the Withering-Bloom fire spirit's advancement and transformation.

The spiritual resonance he had drawn in himself was not that much, so he could not continue staying inside this world's sea of origin qi.

He was pushed out by a consciousness that was cold yet also intimate.

With Luo Chen's understanding, that consciousness could be called the world's will, or the soul of a star. If described by the profound discussions in ancient records, there were two more common words for it.

The Heavenly Dao!

Because the time had been too short, Luo Chen's comprehension of the true meaning of Withering-Bloom was not deep. Very soon, he was pushed out of the sea of origin qi by the Heavenly Dao and could no longer continue comprehending more from within it.

But it was already enough.

If described according to the cultivation habits of more than two hundred years, it could be called "entry-level."

Since he had already entered the door, whatever comprehension followed would naturally be much easier than before.

The reason he had not awakened for so long afterward was that he had gained another insight after withdrawing from the sea of origin qi!

Only, what he comprehended this time was not some mysterious law of the Great Dao, but a spell related to battle.

Along his path, Luo Chen had created few spells himself.

And most of them were formed by blending other people's spells, ancient records left by predecessors, and some of his own understanding.

For example, Voidburst Step borrowed from Azure Frost Demon Emperor's method, Sevenfold Blazing Heaven Rings came from the fire-attribute defensive spell Fire Ring Art, and Ghosts and Gods Give Way drew inspiration from the Archaic beast Ghost Immortal Tower.

That set of the Devil Lord's Seven Scattered Hands was even more an excerpted summary and could not truly count as self-created at all.

But this time, it was purely his own creation!

Luo Chen took the true meaning of Withering-Bloom as the main body, supplemented it with changes of magic power, the obscuring and illuminating restrictions among the seven great fundamentals of formations, and even fused into it the source power in his body that was full of vigorous vitality.

All of this, in the end, let him comprehend the embryonic form of a new art today!

He named it: Withering-Bloom Dao Finger!

Thoughts flashed through his mind in an instant.

The battle in the outside world had already fallen into his eyes.

As Luo Chen's finger fell.

The black saber light chopping down from above shrank inch by inch. The magic power attached to it strangely vanished. In the end, it turned into the true body of a small black saber and fled with a mournful cry, but that strange power still followed it and even spread to its master.

After Qi Xiongcheng grasped the small saber, his whole body trembled, and disbelief appeared in his eyes.

He discovered that his magic power was diminishing, and his Sage was withering.

Especially his fleshly body, which had originally been at the third realm of body refinement, began to lose great amounts of vitality in a strange way. His entire person became aged.

"No!"

This time, he did not hesitate at all and activated the illusion chess piece.

A vortex appeared and enveloped him.

As his figure gradually vanished, a head of white hair flickered faintly in and out of sight.

On the other side, the green bamboo forest covering several li visibly withered.

Bamboo leaves scattered, green bamboo turned yellow, and even section after section of bamboo transformed from magic power began bursting open with popping sounds like firecrackers.

Amid Wang Yifu's shock, a cracking sound came from within his soul.

The bamboo staff, already heavily damaged by that blood sword in the battle at Three-Rainbow Falls, revealed cracks again, which rapidly expanded.

Until it snapped into two pieces!

This was his lifebound magic treasure!

With the magic treasure destroyed, the connection established by several hundred years of mental refinement instantly backlashed against his body.

And that strange power, with the bamboo forest no longer concealing it, finally landed on him.

Even though he continuously drove his magic power to resist it, in only a few breaths, he changed from an aged state into one decrepit with age, like a withered tree approaching death.

But none of this was the key.

Luo Chen's eyes stared fixedly at that strange attack of intertwined lightning and fire. It looked like a flame current, and also like lightning.

When his spell landed on it, the other party temporarily blocked it.

The enormous finger transformed by the spell was actually dispersing in return.

"What power is that? It is filled with an aura of destruction and can actually block my new art?"

But only temporarily!

After holding out for only one breath, the flame current and lightning suddenly collapsed.

"Pfft!"

Shi Ju uncontrollably spat out a mouthful of blood. Looking at the enormous finger pressing down from the air, madness filled his eyes.

"Damn it, failed again!"

He glared hatefully at Luo Chen, then suddenly pressed his hands together before forcefully spreading them apart.

As his left and right hands opened, his body was pulled wider and wider.

Until...

Swish!

A figure walked out from within him, with two hands, two feet, and head held high.

It had the same face and the same aura as the original Shi Ju, but was even more violent.

As soon as the two figures appeared, each flew to the left and right.

At this moment, Luo Chen had a faint hesitation.

Then he saw firelight appear and lightning flash.

"Fire escape?"

"Lightning escape?"

Amid Luo Chen's surprise, he controlled the final power of that finger and pointed toward the figure flickering with lightning.

Thunk!

A faint sound rang out in the void.

That lightning-flickering figure was truly struck by the finger and fell powerlessly toward the earth.

But in midair, it suddenly steadied itself, lightning reappearing, and fled dozens of li away.

As for the other figure shrouded in firelight, it had already vanished.

Luo Chen slowly withdrew his hand, a thoughtful expression on his face.

"So this is what he relies on? A lightning clone, and one condensed from a second Sage of the lightning dao!"

If that was the case, then everything made sense.

Those who possessed a divided soul had varied methods.

Those who possessed a second Sage had magic power even deeper, far surpassing their peers.

But if, on top of these two, one possessed the great method from immortal legends known as an external incarnation, then the strength of that person's combat power was immeasurable.

Especially!

Shi Ju's two bodies, one fire and one lightning, were both attributes extremely suited to battle.

With such a trump card, he had no need for a Dao protector at all. By himself, he could range unhindered through Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, and even when cultivating, he did not need anyone else to guard him.

That Fu Qinglan, who had previously comprehended origin power, was not Shi Ju's opponent also made sense.

But unfortunately, he had encountered Luo Chen.

Luo Chen's battle experience was richer, and his realm was higher than Shi Ju's. Especially since Luo Chen had also comprehended the true meaning of the laws, and had created a spell from it.

Shi Ju could not possibly be his opponent.

Now that Shi Ju had fled, Luo Chen had no intention of chasing.

Defeating him was enough.

Inside this grotto-heaven, if he truly drove Shi Ju into desperation, Shi Ju could at worst activate the illusion chess piece and leave. He could not be killed at all.

Chasing a cornered foe would not only be futile, it would instead draw the enmity between them even deeper. If it provoked the Shi Great House into personally entering the field, that would be rather bad.

Abruptly.

He raised his head and looked. The white eagle that had originally been diving down was rapidly fleeing.

Luo Chen smiled faintly. Since it had come, how could he let it leave?

No extra movement could be seen from him. Just as before, he pointed out with one finger.

The commotion was not as great as before, but the remaining momentum in the surroundings was still present. In an instant, it struck the white eagle.

Only a mournful cry could be heard.

The white eagle exploded in the air!

It turned into countless clouds and mist, then tried to rapidly condense again.

But how could the power of Luo Chen's finger be resolved merely by gathering form and scattering qi?

In the sky, great masses of white mist kept trying to condense, then kept collapsing. In the end, they turned into an enormous illusion core wreathed in clouds and mist, flying toward Luo Chen.

Grasping the illusion core in one hand, Luo Chen swept his gaze over Wang Yifu nearby, who no longer had any aura, then looked toward the shocked Gu Yuan and Gu Chen in the distance and smiled faintly.

"The matters here are finished. Let us go!"

Fu Qinglan looked at Luo Chen with worship in her eyes and nodded lightly.

"Mm."

Two illusion chess pieces activated at the same time, and two vortexes suddenly appeared, sucking them inside.

In barely one breath, the two figures vanished.

At this moment, the battlefield was in complete ruin. The piercing wild wind still howled, and the vast ocean of ice and snow slapped against broken walls and ruins, boiling without cease.

Gu Yuan stood there blankly.

Only after Luo Chen and Fu Qinglan had vanished without a trace did he say hoarsely, "Was that move a divine ability?"

What was a divine ability?

Ascendant cultivators, and even stronger experts of higher realms, fused the true meaning of the laws they comprehended into spells, giving them great power that could penetrate heaven and earth.

This was a divine ability!

No one answered Gu Yuan. Gu Chen's experience was still inferior to his, and the other man was immersed in the strangeness of Luo Chen's earlier finger.

It possessed the might of magic power, and also fused in the momentum of heaven and earth, and could even target a cultivator's fleshly body.

Such a terrifying method seemed to match only the descriptions of the Tianyuan Dao Sect's legendary seven great divine abilities.

But Luo Chen was merely an early-stage Sage cultivator!

And he was a rogue cultivator with no background!

Had he created a divine ability himself?

Gu Chen drew in a deep breath, his gaze sweeping over the dazed Gu Yuan, and he could not help sighing inwardly.

Perhaps among the cultivators who had entered Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven this time, there were many geniuses seen once every ten years or once every hundred years, but the true genius should be that Wilderness Wanderer, who had supposedly come only as a supporting leaf.

How could such a genius remain nameless forever? In the future, he would surely resound throughout the Mountains and Seas Realm!

...

Hum!

Hum!

Two vortexes appeared abruptly, quieting the originally restless great hall.

Everyone's gaze fell in unison upon that man and woman.

Fu Chaosheng was the first to rush up, blocking in front of everyone and protecting the bloodstained Fu Qinglan behind him.

The cultivators around them, however, mostly fixed their eyes on Luo Chen!

All kinds of different gazes: curiosity, shock, admiration, and even more, anger!

Especially the latter, which came from several Sage experts who made no attempt to hide their vast auras.

So many gazes that wished to choose someone and devour them pressed down on Luo Chen like a great mountain.

But Luo Chen treated them like a clear breeze on his face. A faint smile hung on his restored youthful features as he cupped his hands toward Fu Chaosheng.

"Fellow Daoist Fu, Luo has fortunately fulfilled his mission!"

Fu Chaosheng hurriedly lifted his hands.

Only, before he could say anything, a sinister voice came over.

"Wilderness Wanderer Luo Chen, you must give my Xiang family an explanation for this matter!"

Luo Chen looked over. It was an old man with a gloomy face, presumably the Xiang clan head, leader of the Thirty-Six Aristocratic Clans.

Indeed, from the other party, he could even feel a trace of familiarity.

Luo Chen remembered. Back then, when the Seven Elders had deliberated whether he could join Heaven-Earth Peak, the representative of the aristocratic clans had been this man.

His appearance was not actually gloomy. On the contrary, he had a full forehead, harmonious features, and an air of grand poise.

But the anger in his eyes allowed everyone to feel his mood at this moment. It seemed as though he wished to tear Luo Chen into pieces.

Fu Chaosheng gritted his teeth and stood out.

"In the competition, victory and defeat are common matters of war. Moreover, Xiang Qi was not eliminated by Luo Chen. He merely eliminated Wrathful Vajra in legitimate counterattack."

These words could not be faulted, because the one who eliminated Xiang Qi was Shi Ju.

But someone else had something to say!

It was the Lin clan head!

"How victory and defeat are decided is not something we can interfere with. But was Luo Chen not far too ruthless?"

He was a handsome middle-aged man, holding a corpse with an empty chest and abdomen, his face filled with grief.

It was Lin Jingtang's corpse, the child he had raised as a son.

Fu Chaosheng wanted to say something, but the Lin clan head glared at him directly.

"My Lin family and your Fu family have been friends for generations!"

Fu Chaosheng was left speechless.

Luo Chen's expression was calm, and his gaze slowly swept across the surroundings.

Most of the true disciples and Dao protectors who had been eliminated earlier were not present, but Qi Xiongcheng, who had come out almost immediately before them, was collapsed on a chair and had not yet had time to go out for treatment.

Presumably, he had told these people about what happened inside the grotto-heaven!

That made sense. In the fifty years inside Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, Luo Chen had made many enemies in order to protect Fu Qinglan.

Especially in the final battle. Under one finger, among four great Sages, one was injured, one fled, and two died!

In particular, the two who died made this, aside from the first competition, the greatest number of deaths in several grotto-heaven competitions over the past three thousand years.

No wonder they were so agitated.

The Xiang family, Lin family, Wang family, and also the Shi clan head and Gu clan head, who had not spoken but were likewise staring at Luo Chen.

Luo Chen smiled.

"Everyone, could it be that you wish to hold Luo accountable for doing his utmost to protect a Tianyuan true disciple? If so, then I fear that in the next grotto-heaven competition five hundred years from now, the new round of Dao protectors will not dare act freely."

As soon as these words came out, everyone's expressions changed strangely.

Footsteps came from the outer hall.

The person had not yet entered, but the voice had already arrived.

"Guest Elder Luo speaks correctly. The Dao protectors are people all of you invited, and what they protect is the Great Dao of each true disciple. You do not praise Guest Elder Luo's loyal and righteous deed, but instead all apply pressure together. Is this not beneath the bearing of great sects and great clans?"

Sage Seven Lamps walked in.

He represented the five outer-continent elders of Heaven-Earth Peak's Star Gate, and the form of address he used for Luo Chen was the two words "Guest Elder."

The meaning went without saying!

The Xiang clan head stepped out from the crowd and faced Sage Seven Lamps tit for tat.

"This matter is not Star Gate's to manage!"

Sage Seven Lamps stroked his beard, his gaze crossing past him to look at the two people behind.

"Fellow Daoist Shi, Fellow Daoist Gu, what do you think?"

Those two were also among the nineteen elders of Heaven-Earth Peak's Star Gate.

Under Sage Seven Lamps' gaze, the two seemed slightly silent.

In the end, although Luo Chen had offended two Great Houses, Shi Ju and Gu Daosu had still not been eliminated.

Making trouble at this time would not only lose their bearing, but also lacked standing.

Luo Chen had not expected Elder Seven Lamps to speak for him in the name of Heaven-Earth Peak's Star Gate at this time.

Just then.

A current of divine thought enveloped the entire great hall.

In an instant, everyone knew who it was.

"Greetings, Sect Master!"

"Greetings, Sect Master!"

Voices of salutation rang out at the same time, but Mo Shouzhuo's divine thought did not answer anyone. Instead, it fell upon Fu Qinglan.

After observing for only a moment, a soft laugh came.

"Excellent!"

"Fu Qinglan may enter Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak of the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range!"

When the words fell, the divine thought abruptly vanished.

Everyone in the hall looked at one another, each with different expressions.

Only Fu Chaosheng was beside himself with joy. His palms trembled, and the corners of his mouth quivered, a loss of composure caused by the thing he had dreamed of for many years finally being achieved.

He held Fu Qinglan's hand.

"Child, you may go to Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak!"

Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak was the true core of the Tianyuan Dao Sect!

It was the cultivation place of Ascendant great powers.

Below the Ascendant realm, if one wished to enter, one either had to establish heaven-great merit, or possess the potential to achieve the Ascendant realm.

Even a late-stage Sage great cultivator could not enter Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak without permission.

They either grew old alone within their clan or entered Heaven-Earth Peak.

It could be said that within the Dao Sect, the name of Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak was even greater than Heaven-Earth Peak!

Fu Qinglan, who had remained silent ever since coming out, now revealed a smile. While lightly patting her grandfather's hand, she looked toward Luo Chen.

"Big Brother Luo, thank you!"

"Ha ha, why thank me? It was only my duty!"

Luo Chen laughed loudly and left the great hall together with Elder Seven Lamps.

Gaze after gaze followed his back until he vanished, but the sound of his great laugh as he left still echoed by their ears.

Everyone knew that Luo Chen could not be touched.

The outer-continent forces of Star Gate were vouching for him!

Fu Qinglan, who was about to enter Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak to cultivate and had hopes of becoming an Ascendant in the future, also displayed without any coyness her intimate relationship with Luo Chen!

There was even Tianyuan Sect Master's divine thought, which had swept past. Although it had said nothing about Luo Chen's matter, saying nothing had already explained everything.

Some rules could not be thrown into disorder.

Some anger could only be suppressed.

As for hatred, that had to be swallowed with broken teeth.

Only, when each person looked toward the Fu grandfather and granddaughter, there was a trace of envy in their eyes.

How was their luck so good?

*(End of Chapter)*
