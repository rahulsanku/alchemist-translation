# Chapter 635: Shattering Islands, Breaking the Formation, Mountain Lord Destroyed!

Luo Chen dared to go out without bringing Han Zhan and roam the depths of the Northern Sea swarming with demons because he naturally had things to rely on!

His formidable combat ability was one. His extraordinary escape speed was another.

Even without using the Heavenly Peng transformation, the Moonbreaking Wings combined with Nine Myriad Li made his escape speed far surpass ordinary Golden Core cultivators.

If he were given even a little time to unfold the Heavenly Peng true body, perhaps only Sage Perfected Beings could chase him.

But now, this reliance had been broken!

Luo Chen could sense a powerful sealing and suppressive force crashing down from the giant mountain in the sky.

Under that enormous pressure, his escape speed greatly decreased.

If it were only this, he could still transform and leave.

However, beneath his feet, another terrifying gravity constantly pulled at him.

The two combined into one, and only then caused Luo Chen's steps to crawl like a turtle.

Sure enough, sometimes once a method was exposed before outsiders, it was extremely easy to be targeted.

Luo Chen's expression was gloomy, and his gaze again swept over that giant mountain slowly floating in the sky.

He recognized this mountain.

It was precisely the treasure mountain the Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft had held while forcefully attacking the Sea-Sealing Eight Trigrams Formation back then.

In the past, Luo Chen had used a formation to resist it. Now, the other party used a formation to suppress him.

This truly was karmic retribution, accurate time and again!

And very clearly, this grand formation using the Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft's lifebound magic treasure as its core was not merely as simple as sealing the air and suppressing. Once fully unfolded, it might have even more formidable methods.

"Since you do not want me to leave, then I will slaughter you!"

A flash of ferocity crossed Luo Chen's face, and he raced toward Liang Baizao.

While in the air, both hands constantly formed seals.

Round after round of blazing suns appeared behind him, fierce sunlight shining in the night sky.

When he reached one li away, he reached out with one palm.

Surging mana gathered into an azure palm print and pressed down with a crash.

Nine blazing suns accompanied this palm pressing down, forming a terrifying and horrifying scene.

Looking at this scene, Liang Baizao's expression also became solemn. He pushed one palm horizontally, and a brown-yellow shield immediately grew larger, blocking all threats from the sky outside.

This shield was extremely hard. Even though Luo Chen layered powerful spells, it seemed unable to completely shake its protective ability.

After the violent collision, the shield's spiritual light merely dimmed slightly.

Clearly, this was at least an upper-grade defensive magic treasure!

With only the first move, both sides went all out.

Madness seemed to flash in Luo Chen's eyes. The spirit seal in his hand suddenly changed, and he shouted fiercely.

"Mountain Collapse!"

Boom!

In the next moment, the tall mountain where Liang Baizao stood exploded in an instant.

The vast power of vibration, carrying lava and shattered stone, sprayed in all directions like a rainstorm. Within that storm, a human figure staggered as if unable to control himself.

If it were an ordinary Golden Core cultivator, under Luo Chen's three spells released together in a linked attack like this, few could preserve their lives.

But very clearly, the Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft was no incompetent person. Leaving aside how many magic treasures the sect master of a great artifact-forging sect would have protecting his body, his Golden Core Stage Eight realm alone was enough to make Luo Chen not dare look down on him.

After three spells fired together.

Luo Chen did not pursue closely to kill, but instead landed calmly.

With one hand, he curled his fingers and formed a spirit seal.

"Transform!"

With a fierce shout, his beard and hair immediately moved without wind.

And the muscles beneath his Daoist robe also began writhing and swelling.

One zhang, two zhang, three zhang... until he became a giant nine zhang tall!

Splash!

The Moonbreaking Wings grew larger along with him, accompanying him on either side like the wings of gods and devils.

His originally white Daoist robe also turned from white to ink, transforming into ancient-style black battle armor covering his whole body, leaving almost nothing exposed from the soles of his feet to his face.

After taking the opportunity to complete his transformation, Luo Chen reached out with one claw.

In the void, there suddenly came a crisp sound of metal striking metal.

Ding!

Looking carefully, between the giant's palms, he was firmly gripping a cyan flying sword.

The flying sword was like a swimming fish, struggling without cease in his hand like a fish caught in a net.

Luo Chen raised his head and looked toward the sky.

"A mere middle-grade magic treasure flying sword cannot take Luo's life. Mountain Lord, you look down on me too much!"

Whoosh!

In the void, a figure abruptly stopped. Letting falling stones rain around him, his aged eyes stared at Luo Chen.

"Receiving my Seven Baleful Soul-Seizing Sword [TERM?] with your physical body. Devil Lord Azure Yang, you have grown stronger!"

"That is right. I have grown stronger, while you have gone backward instead of advancing over these years. In this battle, the one who loses will definitely be you!"

As Luo Chen spoke, he drove his enormous strength, trying to forcibly crush this flying sword in his hand.

Piercing creaking sounds constantly came from his palm.

Through the mind-link between magic treasure and owner, the Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft sensed Luo Chen's intention, and his face turned sharp.

"Dream on!"

His hands formed seals, and he gave a soft shout.

"Break!"

Bang!

With only an explosive sound, Luo Chen frowned and involuntarily opened his fist, looking as that cyan flying sword staggered back to the old man's side.

"Still not possible?"

His current physique was already countless times stronger than back then, already reaching the level where he could take middle-grade magic treasure attacks head-on.

But being able to receive them did not mean he could destroy them.

Especially since the Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft had seemed to trigger the baleful qi hidden inside the flying sword just now, forcibly breaking free from the binding of his enormous strength.

After the flying sword returned to his side, the Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft could not help letting out a breath of relief.

Only, when he looked at Luo Chen again, the solemnity in his eyes grew increasingly dense.

After failing to chase Luo Chen down back then, he had returned to Cold Light Island and found the eldest of the Wu family, as well as some bystanders who had personally witnessed Luo Chen's battle, asking them in detail about Luo Chen's battle methods.

He knew deeply that Luo Chen had terrifying attainments in speed, strength, and spells.

Apart from defense, he had almost no shortcomings.

Especially since Liang Baizao had personally experienced one of the other party's illusion arts.

Such an existence, even merely at the mid Golden Core realm, was not easy to deal with.

Therefore, he had painstakingly schemed for years, using his lifebound magic treasure Hundred Craft Mountain [TERM?] together with Seven Stars Island's geographical advantage to arrange the Northern Dipper Seven Peaks Array [TERM?], suppressing Luo Chen's speed so he could not display his greatest battle advantage.

He had originally thought that in this way, the other party would become a turtle in his jar.

He had not expected that what one heard was always only what one heard. The other party's battle experience and awareness still surpassed his expectations.

The previous three spells released together had caught him unprepared and caused him minor injury.

He had wanted to use the flying sword to injure the other party amid the chaos, but unexpectedly the other party had nearly destroyed his sharp weapon instead.

"This child cannot be dealt with as a normal Golden Core cultivator. I must display my full strength!"

Thoughts flashed through his mind, and the Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft no longer probed.

His sleeves billowed, and his hands formed seals again and again.

The seven flowing, colorful flying swords behind him circulated nimbly like flying swallows.

"Go!"

He placed fingers together like a sword and pointed downward.

The target was the nine-zhang giant!

Luo Chen was unafraid. He strode forward, running across the earth. Facing the seven flying swords rushing toward him, his two hands struck out repeatedly.

Sometimes fist wind surged, sometimes giant palms slapped horizontally. His ten fingers flicked rapidly as if plucking strings in an urgent performance. Cloud-Probing Divine Claws stirred wind and clouds from time to time, knocking the flying sword attacks off-angle and even trying again to capture the treasures.

Under his actions, the seven flying swords with chilling baleful qi were actually unable to get close.

Seeing this scene, the Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft's eyelids twitched wildly.

These seven flying swords had been refined together, nourished together, and advanced together to middle-grade magic treasures as a matched set. Their power surpassed even upper-grade offensive magic treasures.

Under his control, they could not even get close.

Just how strong had Devil Lord Azure Yang's body cultivation art become?

"No!"

"The suppression is still not enough!"

"He is still too agile!"

Looking at the giant running over mountains and plains, the Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft steeled his heart, grabbed toward the sky, and pulled down fiercely.

"Suppress!"

At the extreme height of the sky, Hundred Craft Mountain rumbled and pressed downward.

The seven small islands arranged in a line like linked stars also gave rumbling echoes.

The nearby great sea rolled up heaven-reaching waves, surging toward the islands.

Luo Chen's running footsteps became slower and slower.

A powerful pressure came from above his head, and a terrifying gravity came from beneath his feet.

Under the combination of the two, it seemed to form a gravity field, forcibly making his charging momentum gradually stop.

Luo Chen's eyes were bright, and he faintly sensed the true meaning of this grand formation arranged by combining the seven islands.

Using Hundred Craft Mountain as the core, it drew the earth-vein qi of the seven islands. Within it, one was like being pressed by Mount Tai from above while earth qi pulled from below.

All living things were like this.

The reason the Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft was unaffected was because the formation core was his lifebound magic treasure.

But in this way, the other party's mana consumption must also be considerable.

To break this formation, the only way was to overturn that Hundred Craft Mountain!

But this mountain was connected to the seven islands. If one wanted to overturn the mountain, one had to overturn all seven islands at the same time.

While deep within it, this was as difficult as ascending to heaven!

Luo Chen's footsteps stopped.

"No heavenly timing, no geographical advantage. In that case, let us compete in foundations!"

With a soft shout, nine crimson chains suddenly extended from his body and lashed clattering toward the seven flying swords shooting at him.

Luo Chen himself stared fixedly at the Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft, sending out palm after palm with both hands.

Azure great handprints erupted repeatedly in the void.

Even the extremely fast Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft found it difficult to dodge under such covering attacks, and could only summon that shield to forcibly resist.

Yet the intense rebound force and the scorching flame streams still charred his beard and hair, as if they might ignite at any moment.

"You want to compete with me in mana reserves?"

After receiving several palms head-on, he saw through Luo Chen's intention.

Thinking of this, he could not help forming a cold smile.

Although his realm had regressed somewhat over these years, he was still a genuine Golden Core Stage Eight great cultivator!

No matter how quickly Devil Lord Azure Yang had advanced, he was ultimately only Golden Core Stage Six.

In attrition, he did not fear!

Moreover, he controlled a defensive magic treasure, which did not consume much mana to begin with.

By contrast, the other party was repeatedly casting wide-range killing spells. How many times could he release them?

Just as he made up his mind to exhaust Luo Chen like this, the corner of his eye suddenly froze.

A yellow copper coin, round outside and square within, landed on his shield.

"When?"

Clang!

A soft ring fell in the Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft's ears, but it was no less than a heaven-reaching roar.

The copper coin was knocked back by giant force, falling somewhere on the island.

But his body-protecting treasure seemed to suffer a heavy blow and slowed for an instant.

At that moment, an explosive sound came from the void.

The nine-zhang giant originally trapped in place stepped out, crossing one hundred zhang, and leapt above the old man.

Holding the giant hammer, he smashed down from the sky.

Boom!

A heaven-shaking sound rolled outward, who knew how many li away.

Luo Chen landed fiercely. The mysterious armor remained intact, but his physique inside the armor had cracks and bloodthreads appearing.

Even his physique, forged and tempered a thousand times over these years, had such injuries. From this, one could see how dangerous it was to forcefully use Voidburst Step while suppressed by the Northern Dipper Seven Peaks Array.

But in the end, he had gained merit in one move!

Looking at the figure within the smoke and dust, staggering to stand while holding a shield with dim spiritual light, he strode out without thinking, Flowing Fire Hammer in hand.

At the same time, spiritual light shot explosively from both his eyes.

Mirror Flower, Water Moon, open!

He wanted to leave no opportunity and forcibly kill the other party!

On the earth, the giant charged, the great hammer like fire, spiritual light shooting from both eyes, like a god, like a devil.

Within the smoke and dust, the old man's body was weak, as if a candle flickering in the wind.

But just as the two were about to approach, the giant's figure suddenly stopped.

The Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft spat out a mouthful of blood. Without thinking, he grabbed toward the sky again.

The towering giant mountain pressed down continuously like a falling meteor. During this process, its size became smaller and smaller, but its power became greater and greater.

He wanted to smash the other party to death alive!

An unexpected scene occurred.

A small gray cauldron floated out from above Luo Chen's head and, in the critical instant, forcibly withstood that small mountain.

"How is that possible? He was countered by the soul-refining method Blood Nightmare Moluo left behind, and now his illusion has backfired. Elder Xing's [TERM?] remnant soul is currently assaulting his divine soul, so how does he still have spare strength to control a magic treasure?"

Amid inward shock, the Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft's expression became increasingly hideous.

"In that case, I will flay you alive!"

He threw down the shield with dim spiritual light and beckoned with his fingers.

The seven flying swords flew back from afar and shot toward Luo Chen.

Just as they were about to reach his body, they seemed to collide with an invisible wall of qi and could not advance an inch!

The Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft froze. His gaze touched the black armor, and realization flashed through his eyes.

"I thought his reputation as an artifact-forging master was merely flattery from ignorant wandering cultivators, but unexpectedly, he truly has some ability, actually refining a body-protecting qi armor!"

"However, this armor cannot block my Seven Baleful Swords!"

The Seven Baleful Swords specialized in breaking defenses.

With a thought, the seven swords circulated, using Luo Chen as the center, and began spinning wildly.

A sword-blade storm abruptly took shape!

Terrifying cutting power began constantly slicing the protective qi armor guarding Luo Chen.

From the look of it, it seemed that before long, it would touch his main body.

Inside Xuanchen Armor.

Luo Chen's eyes were tightly shut, and frightening blood flowed down from the corners of his eyes.

Inside his sea of consciousness.

A remnant soul of a burly man roared and charged toward his soul body.

"It is the original owner of the Flowing Fire Hammer magic treasure. He died under Han Zhan's control of the Sea-Sealing Eight Trigrams Formation, yet still had a remnant soul preserved and refined into a method deliberately targeting my illusion arts."

His consciousness woke instantly from the severe pain. Luo Chen coldly watched the charging remnant soul, without any panic.

Mirror Flower, Water Moon had backfired, and he had not even had time to display the second-layer illusion. The pain within was not something ordinary people could endure.

But how could Luo Chen be an ordinary person?

During Qi Refinement, he cultivated Bright Spirit, Breaking Balefulness. During Foundation Establishment, he endured heavenly lightning pouring through his body. After Core Formation, he tempered his body day and night without rest. Whether true fire burning his body or Black Emperor Ointment's torment, all of it forged his exceptionally tough tolerance for pain.

Moreover, over these years, he had used artifact-forging methods to temper his physique. His will had long since been struck a thousand times, hammered a hundred times, unbending through every setback!

A mere instant of severe pain vanished in the blink of an eye.

He first released his lifebound magic treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, to withstand Hundred Craft Mountain's bombardment, then activated Xuanchen Armor's body-protecting qi armor.

Now, he would resolve the hidden danger inside his sea of consciousness.

Using soul as the body and driving the spirit as blade, Great Perfection Dragon-Slaying Art slashed out.

Severing qi, severing dragons, and severing spirits!

With only one slash, that burly man's remnant soul vanished into smoke.

"Eh?"

As it disappeared, there seemed to be a soft exclamation.

Luo Chen's mind returned, once again controlling his physique. He had no time to think back to that familiar soft exclamation and observed the current situation.

He was trapped!

Within his field of vision, a sword qi storm cut all things. Seven sharp swords, baleful qi crisscrossing, constantly assaulted his body-protecting qi armor.

Even though the qi armor repeatedly regenerated, it was still in imminent peril.

Countless tiny scars had appeared on Xuanchen Armor's main body.

"Fortunately, Xuanchen Armor has two defenses, inner armor and qi armor. It has not yet injured my main body."

Luo Chen felt fortunate inwardly, but the anger in his eyes became even thicker.

In the past, he had always been the one setting ambushes for others, using all sorts of methods to defeat the strong with the weak.

Today, however, he had fallen into another person's trap, passive everywhere, taking blows everywhere.

If his foundation were not deep enough and his methods not numerous enough, he would likely already have perished.

"Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft!!!"

A low roar came heavily from his mouth.

Outside the sword array, the old man sat cross-legged, mobilizing the mana accumulated over many years to maintain the Seven Baleful Sword Array [TERM?].

Although surprised that Luo Chen had awakened this quickly, he did not show much panic now.

"Devil Lord Azure Yang, since you have already been trapped inside the sword array, death and Dao extinction are already fixed. Why this useless rage?"

Luo Chen grinned.

"Is that so?"

Both feet stomped on the pitted earth.

Mana poured out, and the body-protecting qi armor, receiving support, became increasingly mighty.

Not only that, surging earth qi seemed to be drawn by something and involuntarily poured out, adding itself to the qi armor.

For a time, Xuanchen qi armor and the Seven Baleful Sword Array formed a stalemate.

Luo Chen could not get out, and the Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft could not attack in.

On this ruined second island of Seven Stars, a strange scene formed.

A giant stood with both feet on the earth, currents of qi spiraling around him.

A tiny old man sat cross-legged, the sword qi storm becoming increasingly fierce.

No one dared slacken. No one wanted to give up.

Time constantly passed in this stalemate.

It had been a pitch-black night curtain, but unknowingly, the moon sank into the sea, and the morning sun began to rise.

At some unknown time.

Boom!

Only a huge sound was heard.

The Seven Baleful Sword Array, unable to continue, collapsed and shattered.

Luo Chen, restored to his original body size, stepped out. His aura did not seem dispirited, and his eyes remained like lightning.

He raised his eyes and looked into the distance. An old man was fleeing in utter distress.

Even the seven treasure swords had been abandoned to buy time for his escape.

"A pity. You cannot escape!"

Luo Chen murmured, about to spread his wings and pursue, when he saw three attacks break through the air from the sky toward the old man.

The Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft looked terrified. He raised the defensive shield and used all his strength to block these three attacks.

However, after this, from the great sea, the jiaolong swung his tail and smashed down fiercely.

Boom!

The already ruined shield transformed into fragments beneath this jiaolong tail strike.

The old man's weak body exploded into a cloud of blood mist, leaving only a dim Golden Core trying to escape.

But it was also swallowed by the jiaolong in one mouthful.

Three streaks of escape light broke through the air and arrived. They were impressively Lord Lushan, Sang Jiugong, and Duan Li.

"Apologies, we came late."

"That strange grand formation stopped us from entering. This person's formation attainments were truly formidable."

"Daoist Azure Yang, are you all right?"

Under the three people's gazes, Luo Chen smiled freely.

Before he could speak, the sound of shattering came from beneath his feet.

Amid rolling tremors, the small island collapsed and broke apart!

Endless seawater surged in, erasing every trace of this great battle.

It was not only the small island beneath his feet. The other six nearby small islands also showed strange phenomena.

Some had earthquakes, some mountains collapsed, and there were even scenes of volcanic eruptions and birds and beasts fleeing in chaos.

Amid rumbling.

Those six small islands began constantly shattering, struck by seawater and split into pieces.

Everyone looked at this scene and could not help swallowing.

Their gazes toward Luo Chen became even more reverent.

Luo Chen sensed the surrounding changes and felt thoughtful.

"The earth qi of Seven Stars Island was linked together by the Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft, sharing glory and loss together. Under the absorption of my Xuanchen Armor and his grand formation, the earth qi was exhausted, the spirit veins shattered, and even the continental plates suffered a destructive blow."

After understanding all this, Luo Chen no longer lingered.

With a wave of his hand, several streaks of flowing light flew from the seawater toward him. There were swords, a mountain, a cauldron, and an unremarkable copper coin.

"Master, that old man is not dead yet. His divine soul is lingering in the Golden Core."

Amid surging waves, Black King's body rose and fell, sounding as if seeking praise and credit.

Luo Chen glanced at him and waved his sleeve.

"Ignore him for now. We go to Purple Spirit Island first. That is the proper matter!"

After saying this, he took the lead and flew toward the unfamiliar sea region no longer obstructed by formation force.

Behind him, Lord Lushan and the other two looked at one another, then silently followed.
