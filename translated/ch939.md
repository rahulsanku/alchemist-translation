# Chapter 939: From Friends to Foes, Storm Clouds over the Southern Frontier

What does it mean that "he who sees too clearly meets with misfortune, and he who knows too much invites calamity"?

It means that those who are too clever often come to no good end.

Curiosity can kill!

Thunderclap Cave Master's expression stiffened as he caught the veiled threat in Luo Chen's words.

He could only force a smile. "Brother Qi, you worry too much."

Luo Chen waved a hand. "Actually, you don't need to speculate too much. Whether to Wind Thunder Gorge or the Feng clan, I am merely a passerby. Why dig to the roots? Besides, since Wind Thunder Gorge can produce a Sage like you, it's perfectly normal for another one to emerge—me!"

With that, he winked.

Thunderclap Cave Master understood and said solemnly, "Indeed. Brother Qi was always meant to be a rogue cultivator from Wind Thunder Gorge, cultivating for many years before emerging from seclusion with heaven-reaching methods!"

Luo Chen smiled.

With Thunderclap Cave Master covering for him, his background now had a plausible origin.

When traveling through the Southern Frontier in the future, he could conveniently use the identity of Wind Thunder Gorge's Second Cave Master.

Even if someone wanted to investigate deeply, this layer of concealment would make it unlikely for anyone to connect him with the Eastern Wilderness's "Alchemist Luo Chen."

Of course!

There would certainly be many holes in the story.

But to Luo Chen, it didn't matter.

As he had said, he was merely a passerby. As long as he finished his business, nothing else mattered.

Right now, Luo Chen was more concerned about another matter.

"When do we set out for Mijie Mountain?"

Mijie Mountain was not where Thunderclap Cave Master had attended the previous Southern Frontier Sage exchange gathering.

But it was the venue for the next such gathering.

Rumor had it that this was also the location of the Fierce Peak Stiffness Alliance's headquarters!

Thunderclap Cave Master immediately replied, "Once I finish handling the trivial matters here, we can depart at once!"

"Excellent. I'll be waiting, Brother Qi."

Luo Chen did not press further. He believed that after demonstrating his formidable strength, the other party would not dare to deceive him.

Thunderclap Cave Master took his leave.

Luo Chen did not see him out. Instead, he began browsing through the large collection of body-tempering techniques that the Wind Thunder Gorge cultivators had submitted.

These body-tempering techniques were not of particularly high grade.

But they all bore a very strong Southern Frontier flavor.

What exactly was this Southern Frontier flavor?

The answer was obvious—they all used various kinds of gu insects to assist in body tempering.

The Strength Gu that Thunderclap Cave Master had given him earlier was the most basic yet orthodox method.

The techniques submitted by the rogue cultivators below were far more bizarre.

There were methods that used extreme weight to oppress the physical body, tempering great endurance, requiring Heavy Terrain Gu as an aid.

There were methods that opened the Gates of Life and Death, stimulating the body's potential to burst forth with several times the power at critical moments, requiring eight different attribute Eight Gate Gu to open the eight gates. The "eight gates" here did not refer to those in the esoteric arts of Qimen Dunjia, but rather eight weak points on the human body: Qi, Blood, Void, Fullness, Cold, Heat, Wind, and Dampness. Opening each gate brought different cultivation methods and unleashed different powers in battle.

There were also methods for cultivating extreme speed, for tempering qi force...

The list went on, each more peculiar than the last.

Many of these were completely at odds with orthodox body-tempering paths.

But Luo Chen found them very interesting, because he himself had not followed the orthodox path. He had started with a monster-imitating method and gradually moved toward the path of desolate beast body tempering. Thus, encountering these similarly strange Southern Frontier body-tempering techniques piqued his interest.

"Oppressing the physical body? With how powerful my body is now, I'm afraid there are few gu insects in the Southern Frontier strong enough to put pressure on me."

"Opening the Gates of Life and Death—interesting, very interesting. It has some similarities to the Withering-Bloom Divine Seal I've been pondering. I can borrow some ideas from it."

"Qi force? No need. I don't even know what that thing is yet."

"This method of cultivating extreme speed is quite intriguing. It tries to use gu insects to stimulate the major acupoints on the soles of the feet, allowing body cultivators to achieve ultra-high speed, thus closing the gap with cultivators' escape techniques. The level is somewhat low, but the advantage is that the corresponding gu insects are readily available and easy to acquire. Moreover, my own foundation is solid enough—even a slight increase would be an improvement for me!"

...

Thunderclap Cave Master went off to handle his affairs, while Luo Chen immersed himself in studying the body-tempering techniques.

Time passed gradually, and before he knew it, a month had gone by since Luo Chen arrived at Wind Thunder Gorge.

One day.

A figure stood still on the vast plain before Wind Thunder Gorge, as unmoving as an ancient pine.

Suddenly!

The black-armored cultivator lifted his foot and took a step.

His entire body moved.

It wasn't an explosive burst, but his speed was extremely fast, leaving afterimages in the void that dazzled the eye.

Luo Chen felt this speed and nodded with satisfaction.

"Excellent! A third-tier Swift Travel Gu can actually enhance even my current physical body."

Over these days, after studying the speed-enhancing technique, Luo Chen had gained some insights.

After some experimentation, he found that his normal movement speed had indeed improved considerably.

It was slower than the burst speed of his self-created Fire Escape Art.

But that Fire Escape Art placed a considerable burden on Luo Chen's mana consumption.

Now that he had cultivated this "Swift Travel," the burden was much lighter. The only cost was the third-tier Swift Travel Gu.

This gu insect was easy to buy, whether at Wind Thunder Gorge or in the outside world. He could stock up on a large supply, using one up and then replacing it.

"This trip to the Southern Frontier hasn't been wasted!"

"Of the four moves in *Wind, Moon, Thunder, Cloud—Stepping onto the Immortal Path*, I've fully completed three over the years. Only the Cloud Tread move has seen no progress. Now that I have this Swift Travel method, I might be able to integrate it."

Luo Chen was quite excited.

He placed great importance on movement speed.

For escape, he had the extreme Fire Escape Art.

In battle, whether it was the explosive Thunder Kick, the dense and continuous Wind Leg, or the unpredictable Moon Step, he had no weaknesses.

Only the Cloud Tread, used for long-distance travel, was somewhat lacking.

This Swift Travel method could indeed make up for that deficiency.

Just as he was pondering this, a beam of light came from afar, heading straight for Wind Thunder Gorge.

As it passed over the vast plain, it turned and flew toward Luo Chen.

Without even needing divine sense, Luo Chen recognized the newcomer by sight alone—it was Thunderclap Cave Master.

"Are your affairs settled?"

Thunderclap Cave Master's expression was somewhat unnatural, but he nodded.

"All settled. In a few days, I can accompany you to Mijie Mountain without any worries."

Having said this, he proactively changed the subject and looked at Luo Chen with curiosity.

"Brother Qi, are you practicing a movement technique?"

Luo Chen smiled. "Exactly! As you know, the biggest gap between body cultivators and Sages lies in escape techniques. Although high-level body cultivators can close that gap with burst speed, that's only burst speed—there's still some deficiency in endurance."

Thunderclap Cave Master nodded and praised Luo Chen, "Brother Qi didn't show this weakness in our previous battle. Have you made new progress?"

"Yes!" Luo Chen did not hide it and admitted frankly. Then he suddenly made a suggestion: "Earlier, when I saw you dealing with Feng Buyan, your movement technique was also dignified and proper. How about a little competition?"

Thunderclap Cave Master was startled and wanted to refuse.

But under Luo Chen's eager gaze, he eventually agreed.

"Let's have a simple contest on the way back to Wind Thunder Gorge!"

After that, the two stood still.

A leaf drifted down, and in silent understanding, they both moved.

Luo Chen took a step forward. Beside him, thunder flashed, and when it reappeared, Thunderclap Cave Master was already ahead.

He laughed heartily, activated the Swift Travel Gu attached to his body, combined it with his own escape speed, and hurried to catch up.

With his ultra-fast speed, even though Thunderclap Cave Master's figure flickered in and out, Luo Chen caught up from behind.

Luo Chen even had the leisure to observe the other's movement technique.

It should be the *Flash Thunder Method* that Cheng Bai had mentioned. It was indeed a superior escape technique.

But it was clear that the other party had only recently begun practicing it and was far from mastering it.

Thus, even Luo Chen's normal speed was enough to overwhelm him.

Moreover...

A moment later, Luo Chen stopped at the entrance of Wind Thunder Gorge and turned to wait for Thunderclap Cave Master's arrival.

Two breaths later, Thunderclap Cave Master halted.

He smiled helplessly. "I'm still no match for Brother Qi!"

Luo Chen waved his hand and asked curiously, "Cave Master, I've noticed a major flaw in your *Flash Thunder Method*!"

Thunderclap Cave Master's expression stiffened, then he said solemnly, "Please enlighten me!"

Luo Chen spoke bluntly, "I haven't practiced this movement technique myself, but I imagine that in battle, it emphasizes the transition between motion and stillness. Moving like lightning, silent and soundless, with thunder echoing only after you reappear, catching the enemy off guard. But whether at startup or when reappearing, you're too fast and too loud. If an enemy gets used to this movement technique and captures it with divine sense, they can easily seize the gap in your mana conversion and restrict you."

Hearing this, Thunderclap Cave Master's mouth fell slightly open, a look of disbelief on his face.

He had never imagined that Luo Chen could see through the flaw just from a simple speed contest.

The essence of the *Flash Thunder Method* was exactly as described.

It imitated the appearance of lightning and thunder in nature.

Light flickered, silent and soundless.

By the time the enemy heard the thunder caused by mana activation, he would already be at the enemy's weak point, striking in one blow.

But now, whether at startup or reappearance, there was a huge commotion.

With a cultivator's keen divine sense, after getting familiar with it, it was easy to spot the flaw and escape his attack range.

If Luo Chen had only said this, he might have dismissed it as mere theory.

But just yesterday, this flaw had been exploited by someone!

Thunderclap Cave Master said hoarsely, "Brother Qi's insight is keen. That Demon Monk Juening also escaped our encirclement because of this flaw."

Ning Yuzhuo had not been in the Southern Frontier long, but he had already made a name for himself.

As the Demon Monk Juening.

Luo Chen was startled. "You fought Ning Yuzhuo?"

"Not me—*we* fought him!" Thunderclap Cave Master sighed. "Remember the condition Feng Buyan offered me to get me to help?"

Luo Chen nodded.

"Just yesterday, that condition was fulfilled. At his invitation, I joined forces to besiege Demon Monk Juening. The other party had already been ambushed by Feng Buyan's gu insects, but even so, under our encirclement, he broke through from my side and escaped. What he relied on was catching the flaw in my movement technique."

Luo Chen clicked his tongue in wonder.

He hadn't expected Ning Yuzhuo to have such sharp eyesight, also quickly detecting the flaw in Thunderclap Cave Master's movement technique.

Nor had he expected that Feng Buyan, who had fought alongside them before, would turn against Ning Yuzhuo.

These Southern Frontier cultivators truly lived up to their reputation—unpredictable and untrustworthy!

He asked about the details, and Thunderclap Cave Master told him truthfully.

Feng Buyan had previously invited Ning Yuzhuo and Goat Daoist to attack Wind Thunder Gorge, promising generous rewards.

Wind Thunder Gorge had not fallen, and Goat Daoist had retreated in dejection, too embarrassed to ask for payment.

But Ning Yuzhuo had held on to the demand.

Moreover, because the other party's cultivation had gone awry, it had affected many Feng clan members in the Pine Wave Mountain Range.

That was why Feng Buyan could no longer tolerate it and had moved against Ning Yuzhuo.

Plausible!

But not logical!

Luo Chen remembered that when Feng Buyan had negotiated with Thunderclap Cave Master over the territorial rights of Wind Thunder Gorge, he had proposed the condition of asking the other party to take action.

At that time, he and Ning Yuzhuo had not yet fallen out.

So clearly, Feng Buyan had harbored the intention to deal with Ning Yuzhuo first, and only later fabricated these trumped-up charges.

"Interesting!"

Luo Chen smiled, but only smiled.

He didn't care about this dog-eat-dog business.

Now that matters were settled, it was time to head to Mijie Mountain.

As the headquarters of the Southern Frontier's largest force besides the Life and Death Gate, information there would be the most circulated. He might be able to find clues about the Devil Heart Gu King there.

They agreed to set out in three days, after Thunderclap Cave Master had recovered some of his mana. Luo Chen was about to return to his cave dwelling.

Behind him, Thunderclap Cave Master hesitated but couldn't help asking, "Brother Qi, do you have a way to fix the flaw in my *Flash Thunder Method*?"

Luo Chen stopped and turned around.

"I don't have the original text of the *Flash Thunder Method*, so how can I help you fix it? If you let me examine the *Flash Thunder Method*, I might be able to find a solution. But that's only a 'might'—I make no guarantees."

Thunderclap Cave Master hesitated again.

Seeing this, Luo Chen shook his head and returned to his cave dwelling on his own.

On the mountain path, Thunderclap Cave Master finally clenched his fist in frustration.

Under the pressure of a powerful enemy, to save time, he had chosen to use a sacrificial method, sacrificing his subordinate Cheng Bai to forcibly catalyze the Formless Gu larva.

This had allowed him to quickly master the *Flash Thunder Method*.

But he had never expected that this hastily learned technique would have such a glaring flaw.

Yet to let Luo Chen peruse the *Flash Thunder Method*, which he had spent a fortune to acquire, was out of the question.

Not just because of the cost, but because their relationship had not reached that level.

If the other party truly grasped his weakness, then life and death would truly be in another's hands.

Compared to Luo Chen, he preferred to trust himself.

"I'll slowly refine it further. Perhaps I can find a solution on my own!"

With these thoughts, Thunderclap Cave Master returned to his cave dwelling, sat in meditation, and recovered his mana.

Three days later.

Two beams of light left Wind Thunder Gorge, circled briefly in the air, and then headed south.

At the same time.

A large number of cultivators set out from all over the Southern Frontier, surging toward Mijie Mountain.

After centuries of undercurrents, storm clouds were gathering over the Southern Frontier.

(Chapter End)
