# Chapter 745: Three from the Great Houses, Nine from the Aristocratic Clans; Only One Humble Family Stands Alone

As dawn was about to break, Luo Chen satisfiedly put away a furnace of pills.

At his current realm, even with the Withering-Bloom True Flame inconvenient to use during its transformation, he could still activate Nascent Soul true fire to refine pills and forge artifacts.

In fact, refining pills with true fire was the normal state for most alchemists.

After all, not every alchemist could smoothly subdue a suitable source-less fire at a low realm. Once their realm rose, source-less fires of slightly inferior grade were not as stable and reliable as their own Nascent Soul true fire.

Luo Chen examined the Great Soul Pill in his hand. Its color was pitch-black, with golden light faintly hidden within.

"Held back and not released, medicinal power restrained within; plain and ordinary on the surface, with wonders concealed inside."

"It is undoubtedly high grade!"

Luo Chen took the opportunity to glance at the attribute panel. As expected, the Great Soul Pill proficiency on it had quietly advanced to high grade.

In this way, he would have somewhat greater confidence when taking Great Soul Pills to refine the soul.

Speaking of refining the soul, a strange look appeared in Luo Chen's eyes.

He had not noticed before, but in recent days while cultivating the Soul-Splitting Art, he had gradually sensed changes in his divine soul.

It was not the divided soul!

Because of one mid-grade Great Soul Pill each month, the divided soul had long recovered to early-stage Sage level. There was nothing much to say about that.

The important change was in the main soul!

At some point, Luo Chen's main soul had made great progress, and even faintly had a full and rounded feeling.

"Was this improvement brought by that sudden comprehension?"

Luo Chen attributed the reason to that half-year state of sudden comprehension. Other than this, he could find no suitable answer.

After all, the Soul-Splitting Art used splitting the main soul as its method. When one's own divine soul could advance no farther, it repeatedly split the main soul and strengthened divided souls, thereby indirectly increasing the divine soul foundation. In cultivating the main soul, it did not actually offer much help, at most making it a little tougher.

"Unless I break through in realm, my current main soul has already reached a bottleneck and can advance no farther."

"In the past, I could only cultivate slowly and wait quietly for a breakthrough. Now, with the Soul-Splitting Art, I can continue increasing my divine soul foundation."

Luo Chen understood very clearly that when the main soul reached its limit, it was a signal that a divided soul could be split.

Though the second divided soul was currently far from comparable to the main soul, he did not mind splitting out a third divided soul.

At that time, they would advance side by side, grow together, and improve together. Would that not be beautiful?

But that was only for later. With the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven competition imminent, this was not the best time to divide the main soul and fall into a weakened period. No matter what, it had to wait until after this matter.

Luo Chen put away the Great Soul Pill, cleaned the Primordial Unity Cauldron, and prepared to go out.

While cleaning the Primordial Unity Cauldron, he suddenly thought of something.

"Back then, how did Chu Yan, the Alchemy Saint, detect that I was an alchemist, and even an alchemist who might have refined a fifth-tier pill?"

Knowing that he might be an alchemist was easier to explain.

After all, Fu Chaosheng would surely provide Star Gate with corresponding intelligence. Luo Chen had accepted so many medicinal materials, yet had never been seen asking anyone else to refine pills, so they could only deduce that he digested them himself.

But this baseless notion that he had refined a fifth-tier pill was rather shocking.

"They might know I went to the Fallen Sea, but they absolutely do not know that I seized Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's alchemy inheritance in the Demon-Fall Land."

"In Bright Radiance Heaven back then, there were not many survivors. Han Zhan, Blood Wanderer, and Blood Nightmare Moluo are dead. Moon Wanderer vanished. Cape Heaven Matron is of the demon race. The Gu siblings hide their heads and tails and would not appear lightly."

"The only ones who truly knew I came out of the Pill Hall were Old Daoist Jiyuan of the Penglai Immortal Sect and Moon Wanderer's younger sister, Fairy Jadecold. The former has already fallen. As for the latter..."

Luo Chen drew a clue diagram in his heart.

Fairy Jadecold and Moon Wanderer, these sisters, both seemed to come from the Penglai Immortal Sect. And the Penglai Immortal Sect had extremely deep origins with Central Continent's Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. If Fairy Jadecold had taken refuge with the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect to avoid her elder sister's pursuit and incidentally leaked his intelligence...

"No!"

Very quickly, Luo Chen overturned this clue diagram himself.

The reason was simple. If that were true, Star Gate could not possibly have given him only a perfunctory status like external affairs guest elder.

An existence capable of refining fifth-tier pills at early-stage Sage would be the next Alchemy Saint without doubt.

Therefore, Star Gate did not know this intelligence at all.

So Chu Yan's judgment of him had only come from some kind of intuition belonging to herself.

"Is it a natural attraction and resonance between alchemists?"

"Or is it the wonder of the alchemy laws she cultivated to reach Ascendant?"

After forming his Nascent Soul, Luo Chen had made up for his lack of knowledge by reading many classics about higher realms.

Though most were vague, there were still many records concerning the path ahead.

The Great Dao had three thousand paths, and laws were countless.

The Dao could not be grasped or measured. Thus weak cultivators could only start by comprehending laws step by step, thereby continually drawing closer to the true Great Dao.

Among countless laws, there was no division of superior or inferior, but there was a division between Great Dao laws and left-path laws.

Great Dao laws needed no explanation: yin-yang and the five elements, wind, thunder, space, and so on, all of which constructed the entire world.

Such laws were the most complex and the most intricate. Often, beneath one law, many kinds of true intent were divided. However, once cultivated to great accomplishment, they also came closest to the source of the Great Dao.

As for left-path laws, most often they came from the hundred arts of cultivation.

Such as zither, go, calligraphy, and painting; such as pills, formations, artifacts, and talismans; and even more unorthodox ones, where people comprehended extreme laws from monstrous slaughter.

If Chu Yan, the Alchemy Saint, had truly reached Ascendant through a left-path law like the pill path, then sensing that he had once refined a fifth-tier pill seemed understandable.

Only, in that case, how much could his categorical denial back then have convinced her?

For a time, Luo Chen felt somewhat uneasy.

...

Inside the cave abode, it was warm as spring, but outside the cave abode, the cold pierced the bones.

Logically, high-tier spirit lands long possessed the ability to improve their own weather and environment, influencing the local land and water.

But whether Green Parasol Mountain, which Luo Chen had visited before, or Heaven-Earth Peak where he now lived, the changes of the four seasons followed the natural order.

Heaven-Earth Peak especially appeared even more natural.

When Luo Chen left the cave abode, he looked at the snowflakes falling from the sky and did not use magic power to block them. He allowed them to drift onto his head and shoulders.

Because of the Withering-Bloom Divine Seal, his hair was gray-white and wrinkles marked his face. He no longer looked so young, leaning more toward the state of a middle-aged man.

White snow fell upon his hair, making his temperament even more weathered.

For Luo Chen, changing this state would actually be extremely simple.

Either wasting a little magic power or activating source power could instantly restore his normal youthful appearance.

But he had no such thought.

Even in the cultivation world, where realm could not be judged by appearance, those excessively young often drew too much attention.

Especially since Luo Chen truly was not very old, yet his realm was so high!

The amazement of others certainly gave rise to pride in his heart, but it often brought additional trouble as well.

This was fine.

As Luo Chen smiled slightly, he looked toward the person coming through the wind and snow.

Wearing a sky-blue long dress, Fu Qinglan invited him with a charming smile.

"Dao Brother, let us go!"

...

Standing beside a murmuring brook, Luo Chen watched Fu Qinglan present an invitation to an Adept cultivator.

His gaze shifted as he looked around.

The stream flowed quietly, with no urgency. Ahead was soundless, but behind them, spiritual qi surged; clearly many people were hurrying here.

Chess Saint Ke Lianshan playing against Eminent Daoyin had attracted too many eyes.

It could be said that this was absolutely the foremost grand event before the Alchemy Saint's ascension and the Heavenly Origin Grand Competition, far surpassing the previous minor disturbances.

"Once I was lowly and humble, yet now I can enter and take a seat. It truly makes one sigh."

Emotion rose in Luo Chen's heart.

"Dao Brother Luo, we can go in."

"Mm."

This was Luo Chen's first true entry, in the real sense, into the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's mountain gate.

The Peach Garden, cultivation ground of Chess Saint Ke Lianshan.

Within it was a fourth-tier superior-grade spirit vein.

After Luo Chen passed through the formation at the end of the brook, the Peach Garden's grand scenery instantly entered his eyes.

It was not, as imagined, peach blossoms blooming everywhere and mountains flushed crimson. What entered his eyes were only crisscrossing field paths and spirit fields everywhere.

This scene made Luo Chen pause.

Perhaps seeing his puzzlement, Fu Qinglan reminded him from beside him, "Dao Brother, you might as well release your divine sense and view the land within a thousand li."

Luo Chen hesitated. "Would that be impolite?"

"Avoid other people and only observe the terrain."

Luo Chen understood and released his divine sense.

In an instant, the Peach Garden's general terrain entered his heart.

With this look, Luo Chen immediately felt its wonder.

Those crisscrossing field paths looked like piece after piece of good farmland, but from above, they outlined square and straight lines.

At every intersection of those lines, spiritual qi spring eyes had been arranged.

And within the spirit fields, the large numbers of flowers, grasses, and trees planted there seemed like go stones, contending with one another.

There were sword orchids with leaves like swords, sharp and matchless; ghost arbor trees whose shade covered them, defending against outside enemies...

For a time, the Peach Garden, which had first seemed peaceful and harmonious, gave off a feeling of flashing arms, armored horses, and radiant myriad phenomena.

Luo Chen withdrew his divine sense and could not help taking a deep breath.

"The Chess Saint uses the Peach Garden as a go board, field paths as lines, and spirit plants as stones, constructing a grand go game!"

Fu Qinglan smiled slightly. "Dao Brother has good eyes. I did not notice it at first either. Only after a sect elder reminded me did I learn of Senior Brother Ke's exquisite method. Senior Brother Ke himself is not only a transcendent saint of the go path, but also possesses impressive spirit plant cultivation arts. He combined the two and arranged this natural go game. By means of the struggle between flowers, grasses, and trees, he naturally derives the direction of the go game... It is said that when the game reaches its end, Senior Brother Ke has a great chance of comprehending laws and advancing to Ascendant!"

Luo Chen blinked, half believing and half doubting.

He admitted that this go game was exquisitely profound, but could a man-made object be called "natural"?

Luo Chen did not know, and had no intention of digging deeper. He followed beside Fu Qinglan and walked toward the depths of the Peach Garden.

Wherever they passed, cultivator figures became increasingly frequent.

Fu Qinglan seized this chance to begin introducing some people to him from one side.

"That one is Xiang Qi[TERM?], the most outstanding genius of the Xiang family in this generation, and also one of the most dazzling prodigies of our generation. At twelve, he made vows. At sixteen, he refined qi. At twenty-six, he became an Initiate. At one hundred, he successfully formed his core. Ten years ago, he advanced to Sage. Even now, he is not yet two hundred fifty. His talent is still above mine."

"And the Xiang family itself is the first among the Thirty-Six Aristocratic Clans!"

"This time, he will also enter Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven. His Dao protector is said to be a newly advanced Sage expert from the West Desert Buddhist schools invited by his clan, known as Wrathful Vajra[TERM?]. However, that Wrathful Vajra has not appeared today, so he is probably not interested in the grand events of the go world."

Luo Chen looked from afar at a young man flying across the sky. Many attendants surrounded him like stars encircling the moon.

The Xiang family?

He had heard of this clan's name, and had even dealt with its subordinate force, Myriad Manifestations Tower.

It did indeed have the air of the first among the Thirty-Six Aristocratic Clans, able to establish its own banner outside the Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance and conduct trade in the Northern Sea.

Though he could not probe the other party with divine sense, judging from the faint magic power fluctuations that echoed with heaven and earth, it was indeed the level of someone who had just entered Sage.

Roughly the same as Fu Qinglan.

"That one is Liu..."

"Sage Dragon Abyss!" Luo Chen's gaze focused as he spoke.

Fu Qinglan was somewhat surprised, but understood in an instant.

"Senior Brother Liu Yuan indeed used Dragon Abyss as his Daoist title while traveling in the Northern Sea. Dao Brother Luo, did you have contact with him in the Northern Sea?"

Luo Chen tugged the corner of his mouth and neither admitted nor denied it.

Sage Dragon Abyss had once been one of the Seven Great Sages of the Myriad Immortals Assembly, and also the only rogue cultivator who had used the status of a nine-star demon hunter to obtain resource support from the Myriad Immortals Assembly and advance to Sage.

Now it seemed that he was no rogue cultivator at all, but clearly a Dao Sect disciple entering the world for tempering.

Fu Qinglan transmitted with divine sense, her tone grave. "Among the thirteen Dao Sect Sages entering Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven this time, Senior Brother Liu is the only humble-family disciple who is neither from a Great House nor an Aristocratic Clan."

"He could have led the Liu family into the ranks of Aristocratic Clans long ago, but he kept delaying. He must want to gain something in Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven and thereby obtain more resources for the clan's later promotion."

"Originally, none of us paid attention to humble-family disciples participating in the competition."

"But Senior Brother Liu is different. He formed his Nascent Soul the longest time ago, nearly a hundred years. After forming his Nascent Soul, he never acted, suppressing his realm with secret arts so he could barely obtain the qualification to enter Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven. It is rumored that Senior Brother Liu fought through life and death in the Northern Sea, and he himself has extremely strong combat experience. At that time, not only will his Dao protector be a major threat, he himself can also be treated as immediate combat strength!"

Luo Chen nodded solemnly.

He had heard Sage Dragon Abyss's name countless times.

To obtain the identity of a nine-star demon hunter as a rogue cultivator was proof of past battle achievements.

Back then, inside the Myriad Immortals Assembly, not one of those high-star demon hunters had been easy to deal with.

In same-realm battle, Angler, Purple Dowager, and the others were not inferior to upper-sect disciples, and they had merely been eight-star demon hunters.

Sage Dragon Abyss's demon hunter star level surpassed them by one, showing his ability was even above theirs.

Speaking of this, under Fu Qinglan's introductions and comparison with previous intelligence, Luo Chen had already grasped the rough situation of this competition.

There was no need to say much about the lower-realm contests.

At the Sage realm, there were thirteen people in total.

Judging by background, three came from Great Houses, nine from Aristocratic Clans, and one from a humble family.

Sage Dragon Abyss was precisely that lone standout from a humble family!

"I wonder who his Dao protector is. Could it be the frail woman beside him?"

After Luo Chen estimated his chances of victory if he fought Sage Dragon Abyss, he casually asked.

Fu Qinglan was just about to answer when hearty laughter sounded behind the two of them.

"Is that Wilderness Wanderer? Long time no see!"

"Fellow Daoist Luo!"

Luo Chen looked back. As expected, it was the two familiar faces, Winter Mountain Hermit and Lu Qiuzhen.

"So it is you two fellow Daoists. Long time no see. Have you been well lately?"

He smiled and returned the greeting.

Though their friendship was not deep, at least on the surface, they were all from the Northern Sea cultivation world.

Now they had also joined Heaven-Earth Peak's Star Gate. Occasionally walking together was only human nature.

Fu Qinglan gave them a cold greeting, unlike her ease with Luo Chen.

In the end, she was a sacred-land disciple and had her own pride.

At ordinary times, apart from matters related to go, she had always shown a cold and aloof face. Luo Chen counted as an exception because he was her Dao protector, and the competition was a major matter. She had to build a good relationship, so she took the initiative to lower her posture and draw closer.

The two did not mind her cold attitude, smiling as they chatted with Luo Chen about past matters in the Northern Sea and some recent intelligence.

They were formal Star Gate guest elders and had the right to consult relatively recent intelligence, unlike Luo Chen, who still had to rely on the Fu family's connections.

From Lu Qiuzhen's mouth, Luo Chen learned that the war in the Northern Sea had grown increasingly fierce. Ghost Immortal Tower was becoming more and more arrogant. Even when the two late-stage Sage cultivators of the Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance personally acted, they could not stop Ghost Immortal Tower head-on.

And Li Canghai, the Ascendant great power who had always been like a dragon whose head was seen but not its tail, had also begun frequently appearing in the Northern Sea cultivation world these past two years.

The strange part was that his appearances were not to support the battlefront, but to collect spirit mountains and great seas.

This action undoubtedly confirmed Luo Chen's earlier speculation.

The great power Li Canghai was truly using the Northern Sea's spirit lands to refine a treasure!

He simply did not know whether the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect had discovered this clue.

Because the two interrupted them, Luo Chen and Fu Qinglan's conversation had to pause for a while. Before they knew it, they had arrived at the game site.

It was within a peach grove.

Amid falling blossoms, an old man and a young man sat within.

Around them, shadows of people were everywhere. The atmosphere was lively, but because none spoke, it seemed oppressive.

Before Luo Chen focused his attention on the two players, his mind stirred. He sensed a gaze falling upon him... and on Fu Qinglan beside him.

In that moment, Luo Chen clearly felt the woman's body tremble slightly beside him.

Luo Chen looked over openly. In the best viewing area of the peach grove stood a richly dressed, outstandingly poised man holding a white fan, watching them with interest.

Facing Luo Chen's gaze, that young man's lips lifted slightly.

"Who is that?"

Fu Qinglan lowered her head, black hair covering her eyes, while the fingers of both hands unconsciously tangled together.

"Shi family, Shi Ju[TERM?]!"

*(End of Chapter)*
