# Chapter 942: One Staff Breaks Ten Thousand Techniques, the Rabbit Dies and the Fox Grieves

Amidst his murmuring, Luo Chen's mana fluctuations gradually converged and grew calm.

His entire restless mood slowly settled as well.

Only at this moment did he have the leisure to savor that peculiar situation from before.

In this deep-level cultivation session where he had been prepared, he finally found the answer to why his Nascent Soul was so lively.

This cultivation chamber, this spirit vein, even this nearby heaven and earth—the concentration of origin qi of heaven and earth was simply too high.

It was fine under normal circumstances, not affecting cultivators much.

But once one entered a state of cultivation as calm as an ancient well, one would unconsciously sink into it.

Cultivation was originally a difficult and arduous task, consuming both spirit and energy. That was why many cultivators could not persevere on the path of the Great Dao and fell into the mortal world.

But if the external origin qi required little effort to easily refine and absorb into the body, who wouldn't want to effortlessly raise their realm?

However, there was never a free lunch under heaven.

Beneath this ease lay a relaxation of the mind, an unconscious sinking, a assimilation of man by heaven and earth!

It was only because Luo Chen's Purple Mansion barrier was far thicker than that of ordinary Nascent Soul Sages that he could, during deep-level cultivation, not so easily leave the protection of his physical body.

Especially since he had the assistance of the Dustbound Immortal Mirror!

If it were anyone else, the consequences would be unimaginable!

"So, do they encounter similar situations?"

As he muttered to himself, Luo Chen suddenly recalled the scenes of his battles with Sheep Daoist, Feng Buyan, and the others.

Their strength did not match their expected realm.

Luo Chen had previously thought it was due to the inadequate level of local cultivators in the Southern Frontier.

But now, could it be related to the changes in this heaven and earth?

Just as Luo Chen was pondering this, his outward-spreading divine sense finally noticed the situation outside the palace.

"Heh."

With a cold laugh, the corners of Luo Chen's mouth slowly lifted.

...

Deep within the territory of the Shadow Reflection Sect.

A massive lantern, a full hundred zhang in size, was emitting an eerie red light.

Within it, a figure was charging and flying like lightning and thunder.

Its speed was extremely fast, and its movements were quite violent.

A voice of approval came from the thick night.

"You only obtained that *Flash Thunder Art* not long ago, yet you've already mastered it. Quite the comprehension!"

"No, you used the human-refining gu method to catalyze the Formless Gu larvae, didn't you!"

"I wonder which unlucky soul fell victim to your poisonous hand."

The Thunderclap Cave Master did not reply, merely focusing on finding an opportunity to break through.

However, no matter how he charged left and right, the red light emitted by the lantern followed him like a shadow, tightly trapping him.

The enormous lantern began to slowly contract, compressing the space for the figure within to maneuver.

A sense of death surged within the Thunderclap Cave Master's heart.

He roared angrily, "Lantern Master, why are you ambushing me?"

Beneath the night sky, the red light illuminated the Lantern Master's face brightly. His narrowed eyes revealed a hint of sinister ruthlessness.

"Ambush?"

"You simply walked right into my trap!"

"Did you really think that recommending you last time was out of my good intentions? It was merely to lower your guard, to make things easier for me."

"If I hadn't made a mistake in my cultivation halfway through last time, would you have had the chance to return alive to Wind Thunder Gorge!"

As he murmured, he extended a tongue like a snake's and licked his lips.

"Your hide should make an excellent Thunder Lantern!"

The Thunderclap Cave Master was both shocked and enraged. "We have no past grievances or recent conflicts. You're going to deal with me just to refine a magic treasure?"

The Lantern Master sighed emotionally. "Though we have no grievances, I have other goals! In that barren Wind Thunder Gorge, you probably can't feel the changes in heaven and earth. In recent years, too many fellow cultivators have inexplicably gone berserk during cultivation, yet no one can find the reason. I heard that the Fierce Mountain Corpse Alliance has a solution, and I want to join them, but they won't accept the weak. If I can acquire one more lantern, my strength will greatly increase, and the Fierce Mountain Corpse Alliance won't be able to turn me away."

Having said this, he chuckled.

"Since I didn't help you last time, you probably already hold a grudge. Since we can't be friends, I might as well make use of the waste."

The Thunderclap Cave Master was furious!

He was no waste!

Seeing that he couldn't escape the blockade of the red lantern, the Thunderclap Cave Master immediately abandoned the idea of using the Flash Thunder Art to flee. He stood firm on the earth.

Watching the rolling red light coming from all directions, he took a deep breath, formed several hand seals, and his lower abdomen rapidly swelled.

Suddenly!

He expelled a breath and struck out with both palms.

Thunder radiance blazed!

Endless thunder light erupted from within his body.

Under the force of this thunderous power, the eerie red light retreated on all sides.

Seizing this opportunity, the Thunderclap Cave Master flashed out, escaping the suppression of the red lantern.

But before he could even breathe a sigh of relief...

A green lantern, emitting a hazy, shimmering light, illuminated his pale face.

The Lantern Master, carrying a green lantern, strode forward, his face filled with pity.

"Why struggle?"

"You're merely a rogue cultivator who attained the Dao. Your future is destined to be without hope for the Great Dao. Why not become a ghost beneath my lantern and fulfill this sect master?"

At this moment, the Thunderclap Cave Master's mana was depleted.

Panting heavily, he laughed bitterly. "I have cultivated bitterly for hundreds of years to reach this realm. How could I let you benefit so easily?"

The Lantern Master raised an eyebrow. "Fight, you can't defeat me. Flee, this is my Shadow Reflection Sect's mountain gate. Where can you run to? Don't even think about a mutual destruction. With this Green Soul Lantern, which specializes in suppressing mana, even if you try to self-destruct your Nascent Soul, you won't be able to harm me in the slightest."

Having said this, he shook his head disdainfully.

A rogue cultivator with no background, even if they reached the same Nascent Soul realm, was ultimately useless!

Seeing that the Thunderclap Cave Master remained silent, merely quietly accumulating mana, the Lantern Master seemed to recall something and couldn't help but laugh. "Still not surrendering? Are you waiting for that Second Cave Master of Wind Thunder Gorge?"

A flicker of panic flashed in the Thunderclap Cave Master's eyes. "What did you do to him?"

The Lantern Master laughed heartily. "I did nothing to him. After all, his aura is so terrifying. I assume he was the one who resolved the crisis at Wind Thunder Gorge for you. With such a strong cultivator, what could I possibly do?"

Then, his tone shifted!

"But I gave him the best cultivation cave in the Shadow Reflection Sect and secretly channeled a large amount of spirit qi from the vein over to him. I imagine he's already sunk deep into cultivation, unable to extricate himself."

"Even if he doesn't go berserk, my junior brother is leading three hundred disciples, having set up a grand formation and lying in wait for him."

"He can't even save himself!"

Hearing this, the Thunderclap Cave Master's face grew even paler.

No wonder he had felt that the spirit qi in Qitian's palace was so abundant earlier. It was the Lantern Master's deliberate doing.

Although he didn't know why Qitian would sink into cultivation and be unable to extricate himself, the Lantern Master's junior brother, the Shadow Nether Sage, was also a renowned veteran powerhouse.

With him leading people to set up an ambush in advance, Qitian's situation was even more dangerous than his own.

Just then!

"Roar!"

A low roar, like a ferocious beast awakening, emitted a deafening sound.

A vast wave of sound swept in all directions.

The Lantern Master and the Thunderclap Cave Master both turned their gazes towards it.

They saw a massive stone staff, shooting up into the sky like a heavenly pillar, smashing down with a boom.

Boom!

Figures flew up into the air.

But under this single staff strike, they all turned into clouds of blood mist.

Among them, a shadow, moving at extreme speed, split into thousands, fleeing in different directions.

It shouted loudly, "Senior Brother, save me!"

But the stone staff paid no attention to the thousands of shadow clones. It merely aimed straight in one direction and thrust forward.

Pfft!

Like a bubble being popped.

The Shadow Nether Sage, who was also considered a figure of note, was stabbed, his physical body exploding, along with his Nascent Soul, which he had cultivated for many years.

The vast mana, at this moment, turned into a storm of spirit qi, affecting the various mountains and palaces of the Shadow Reflection Sect.

The Lantern Master stared blankly at this scene, as if he hadn't reacted at all.

The Thunderclap Cave Master swallowed his saliva. Qitian's strength had once again exceeded his expectations.

And that staff, was it his magic treasure?

Amidst the storm, a figure, holding the Heaven-Mixed Staff, walked out, ignoring the impact of the spirit qi.

It was Luo Chen!

His cold gaze swept over and landed on the Lantern Master.

"You all are being rather impolite!"

"Damn it!"

"The Lantern Master roared, completely ignoring the Thunderclap Cave Master. With a wave of his hand, the large red lantern from before appeared again, covering towards Luo Chen."

Not only that, but he also threw out the green lantern in his hand.

Furthermore, a lantern emitting a faint purple glow rose from behind him, like a vortex, drawing everyone's attention.

In an instant, space seemed to become sluggish, and the circulation of mana became extremely difficult.

Even the Thunderclap Cave Master, who was standing nearby, felt dizzy and his divine soul wavered.

However, Luo Chen remained completely indifferent to all of this.

He merely gripped the Heaven-Mixed Staff, stepped through the air, and struck out with his staff.

The red lantern shattered, the green lantern extinguished, and even the purple lantern stumbled and dimmed.

No matter how many exquisite techniques, how many myriad divine abilities, before this absolute brute force, they were all like paper lanterns.

"How is this possible!"

The Lantern Master was horrified, never expecting such an outcome.

In an instant, all his courage vanished without a trace. He couldn't even muster the slightest desire to fight Luo Chen.

Without hesitation, he turned and fled.

Luo Chen sneered. He thrust the Heaven-Mixed Staff directly into the ground, then reached up to his head and removed the Flat Mountain Crown.

He plucked two Life-Seizing Golden Plumes from it and shot them towards the Lantern Master.

Chi! Chi!

The golden plumes were sharp, breaking the air without a sound.

The only two sounds came from touching solid objects.

One was the Lantern Master's physical body, which was pinned to a cliff face on a mountain.

The other was that dim purple lantern.

And the latter, after a flicker, enveloped a small Nascent Soul figure and disappeared into the night.

From Luo Chen's appearance, to his attack, to the end of the battle.

All of this happened in the blink of an eye.

Even now, the Thunderclap Cave Master was still dazed, as if he hadn't reacted at all.

Luo Chen reached out with his hand, and the two Life-Seizing Golden Plumes flew back, bringing back the Lantern Master's corpse.

The golden plumes shook off the blood and were once again embedded in the Flat Mountain Crown on his head.

As the plumes swayed, Luo Chen looked down at the corpse on the ground.

"Letting the Lantern Master's Nascent Soul escape."

Unless it was like the sudden, explosive strike that killed the Shadow Nether Sage earlier, even a Grand Cultivator would find it difficult to truly kill a Nascent Soul Sage in an open area.

The teleportation speed of that Nascent Soul, unless Luo Chen went all out, would also be very difficult to catch up to.

Of course, this was because his lifebound magic treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, was sealed. Otherwise, with that cauldron capable of sealing a space, the Lantern Master wouldn't have been able to escape even if he wanted to.

The Thunderclap Cave Master, his mouth dry, came closer and echoed, "It is a pity. If you don't kill the tiger completely, it will become a future calamity."

"Tiger?"

Luo Chen smiled contemptuously. To Thunderclap, the Lantern Master was a fierce tiger.

But in his eyes, he wasn't even worth a single exchange!

And!

"Let him go. Under this heaven and earth, it will be difficult for him to find a place to stay."

Luo Chen looked towards the night sky lit up by the flames of war, muttering softly.

The Thunderclap Cave Master did not understand his meaning.

But Luo Chen didn't want to explain either. His expression turned somewhat cold.

He shook his arm.

A black light shot out, then dove into the ground.

Since this local sect was so impolite, he would naturally collect some compensation.

"What is that?" The Thunderclap Cave Master glanced at the black light diving into the ground and asked curiously.

Luo Chen did not answer. His eyes narrowed slightly as he asked a question in return.

"What is the human-refining gu method?"

The Thunderclap Cave Master froze instantly.

...

The path of gu had an extremely wide range of applications in the Southern Frontier.

It was not merely as simple as assisting in cultivation and combat.

Revolving around the word "gu," many related industries, schools, and techniques had branched out.

Among them, the Gu Refiner was a very noble profession, almost on par with Luo Chen's most glorious identity as an alchemist.

The specific origin of the Southern Frontier's gu insects could not be verified, but now most of them came from the Southern Ocean.

The Southern Ocean was dangerous, mysterious, and unpredictable.

Within it, there were many powerful gu insects, but also many weak, young gu.

Young gu were weak, and to gradually cultivate them to a point where cultivators could use them required the involvement of Gu Refiners.

According to their habits, they would feed them various heavenly materials and earthly treasures to accelerate their growth.

There were even combined refinement methods, using several types of gu insects to refine a new, powerful gu insect.

And these were still considered relatively orthodox methods of Southern Frontier gu refinement.

However, there was one method that was not considered orthodox, and would even be looked down upon if spoken of.

That was the "human-refining gu method," using spiritually endowed humans to raise young gu, prematurely catalyzing their maturity.

This refinement method had the advantage of saving time and being highly efficient. Its disadvantage was that the gu insects refined often had great hidden dangers.

Generally speaking, the "human-refining gu" method was only used by heretical paths or demonic cultivators.

And now, Luo Chen had such a heretical, demonic cultivator by his side!

"So that's how Cheng Bai disappeared!"

Sighing, Luo Chen felt a trace of regret.

Although Cheng Bai's realm was not high, he was perceptive and had a lively mind.

Even if he wasn't taken as a disciple, he would have been a handy subordinate.

Unfortunately, he had been silently refined by the Thunderclap Cave Master.

He could understand the Thunderclap Cave Master's unavoidable actions under the immense external pressure at the time.

But he was even more sorrowful that the fate of insignificant people could not be controlled by themselves.

Once upon a time, hadn't Divine Origin Sage wanted to use his physical body to reverse-refine a Primal Embryo Dao Body?

He had succeeded in resisting.

But Cheng Bai had failed.

Now, feeling the same empathy, how could Luo Chen not sigh at Cheng Bai's fate.

"Last night, the Lantern Master tried to refine Thunderclap into a Thunder Lantern. Did he ever recall, even for a moment, what he had done to Cheng Bai?"

This thought flashed by, and Luo Chen's attention quickly turned outward.

Whoosh!

A black light rushed in.

Accompanied by Black King's depressed voice.

"A poor sect, can't squeeze a drop of oil out of them!"

As he spoke, Black King handed over a storage pouch.

Hearing those words, Luo Chen had already given up any expectations. But after taking the storage pouch and sweeping it with his divine sense, he couldn't help but be stunned.

He looked at Black King, his tone somewhat complex as he said:

"Is your talent focused on combat, or on treasure hunting?"

Black King was stunned. "I just swept through everything in a muddle. Did I miss a treasure that I overlooked?"

Luo Chen did not reply. He simply took out a jade box from the storage pouch.

On the box were carved ancient characters.

**[Illumination Spirit]**
