# Chapter 1101: This Seat Demands an Explanation Today!

The Endless Demon Sea, Giant Whale Sea Region.

Countless whale-type great demons dwelled within.

From the weak, like the Dwarf-Nose Whale, to the strong, like the Red Tiger Whale, they could be seen everywhere here.

Even the scarce, nearly extinct Dragon Whale and the primordial variant Flying-Winged Whale occasionally appeared.

This was the holy land of the Sea Clans, without equal!

All because the Sea Clan great power, Sea Emperor Whale, was stationed here!

Back when the Primordial Demon Sect had dominated the Northern Sea, the Giant Whale Sea Region was one of the few places that remained unaffected.

By now, with the demon race turning the world upside down and entering and exiting the Inner Sea unimpeded, a full two-thirds of the vast Northern Sea had fallen into demon hands.

The Giant Whale Sea Region had reached its peak!

In the past, only whale-type demon beasts had gathered here, but now, a great number of Sea Clans came daily to pay homage and praise the name of the Sea Emperor.

Recently, the number of experts traveling to and from the Giant Whale Sea Region had multiplied several times over.

It was said that eighty years ago, the Sea Emperor Whale Ancestor had obtained immense benefits from the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, greatly advancing his Dao!

Overjoyed, the Sea Emperor had set up a feast to entertain the myriad demons!

That was a feast hosted by a Transformation God Ancestor! Even casually taking out a few things would be enough to benefit Sage-level demon cultivators endlessly.

Therefore, many came, their numbers almost a stampede.

Deep beneath the sea.

A vast palace complex stood there.

Water rippled as numerous third-order great demons led guards on patrol.

Streaks of powerful flying light arrived from all directions, but no matter who it was, before the Sea Emperor Palace, they all restrained their auras and showed respectful expressions.

Inside the Sea Emperor Palace.

Clam masters played music, mermaids danced.

Figures sat within, a scene of great liveliness.

In a daze, one might think it was some human gathering.

However, there was indeed one human present!

At the front of the great hall, a man with features so handsome they were almost demonic, and a temperament as free and easy as flowing clouds, sat alone, cross-legged, drinking wine.

Surrounded by a crowd of demons, he showed no sign of restraint.

If this was not his nature, then he must have formidable strength as his backing.

"Who is that person, worthy of sitting alongside us demon cultivators?"

"With such a brazen attitude, does he still think this is human territory?"

"Hmph, even if it were human territory, can't we come and go as we please now?"

"Oh? Old Dame, do you seem to know that person?"

Among the demons, a white-haired, withered old woman sat in their midst.

Her realm was not high, nor her strength particularly great.

But from the respect everyone showed her, her status was evident.

This demon was none other than Cape Heaven Matron!

A rare Alchemy Grandmaster among the demon race!

She often refined pills for her fellow Daoists. In recent years, she had also frequently guided the younger generation of the Sea Clans.

Therefore, even though she was old and frail, few dared to offend her.

If one were to speak of the breadth of her connections, Cape Heaven Matron was second to none here.

It could even be said that Cape Heaven Matron's connections extended to human cultivators. Even before the Demon Sect's fall, she would often transform and travel to the human heartlands to exchange ideas and discuss the Dao with famous Northern Sea alchemists.

If anyone here could recognize that demonically handsome man, it would be none other than Cape Heaven Matron.

At this moment, Cape Heaven Matron gazed at the man in the green robe with his free and easy temperament, a thoughtful expression on her face.

"If this old woman is not mistaken, this person should be Layman Fengling."

"Layman Fengling?"

"Who is that?"

The demons transmitted their voices, whispering among themselves. They had never heard of this figure.

Cape Heaven Matron shook her head and explained, "He has another title, which you may have heard... the head of the Three Great Wanderers of the Northern Sea—Mad Wanderer!"

As soon as this name was spoken, all the demons were shocked.

The name of the Three Great Wanderers not only shook the human race but was also well-known among the demons.

Especially the head of the Three Wanderers, Mad Wanderer, whose name was like thunder.

It was not only his legendary experience of reaching the Great Cultivator realm as a rogue cultivator, but also his proud past achievements.

"Could it be that our Sea Clan's great demon king, Coral Lord, was defeated by this man?"

"That's right, it was him."

"Then what gives him the confidence to come to the Sea Emperor Palace?"

"Fellow Daoist, you go too far. That trip to the secret realm was merely a normal competition for resources between Coral Lord and him. Losing meant his skill was inferior. In the end, he only fell to the mouth of a strange beast. Besides, later on, the Great Strength Brutal Demon Jiao avenged Coral Lord. The old grudges have long since dissipated. For him to be here today, he must have been invited by the Sea Emperor Ancestor. Do not act rashly."

Cape Heaven Matron calmed the crowd, looked at Layman Fengling, and her expression was somewhat complicated.

He probably didn't recognize her anymore.

But she remembered it very clearly!

Back when she had gone to the human heartlands to secretly learn alchemy, she had encountered this man.

At that time, Layman Fengling had already become Mad Wanderer, and being mad and crazy was his normal state.

But even though he had immediately recognized her as a demon, he hadn't shouted for her death. Instead, during his occasional moments of clarity, he had given her much guidance.

It was because of this guidance that she had later been able to interact with humans multiple times without revealing her true nature, learning many alchemy techniques.

As she was observing him, Layman Fengling seemed to sense it and turned his head to look.

Their eyes met, and Layman Fengling gave a slight nod.

Cape Heaven Matron also nodded in acknowledgment.

No words were needed; there was already an understanding.

Just then, the water vapor in the hall trembled. In a daze, everyone saw a burly man sitting in the seat of honor.

Everyone immediately stood up, speaking words of reverence.

"Greetings, Ancestor!"

The sound was like a mountain roar and a tsunami.

Sea Emperor Whale was quite pleased. He stroked his whale whiskers and said generously, "Good, good, good! My children, please be seated! Today, this old ancestor is hosting a feast. You may all drink to your hearts' content."

It was clear he was in an excellent mood.

The rumor that he had obtained great benefits from the Dao Sect and greatly advanced his Dao seemed to be true.

During the feast, maidservants entered the hall one by one, presenting rare fruits and fine wines to each table. Consuming even a little could save demon cultivators years or even decades of cultivation effort.

Sea Emperor Whale first joked and laughed with everyone, then got down to business.

"This old ancestor has killed countless in his life and has personally experienced the battles that led to the fall of the two holy lands, the Primordial Demon Sect and the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. I have also gained many benefits, but unfortunately, I am only keen on cultivation and am powerless against some things. Today, I ask you all to identify them. If anyone can, this ancestor will not be stingy and will allow you to make a copy for yourselves."

As he spoke, he casually tossed something out.

Streams of light flew out.

Two of them landed especially in front of Layman Fengling and Cape Heaven Matron.

Layman Fengling seemed to have expected this. He picked up a copper piece, stared at the writing on it, and his gaze flickered.

After a long while, he transmitted his voice, "This is demon race script. It records some customs and practices of the demon realm. Nothing special. If the Sea Emperor wishes to hear, I can translate the corresponding content."

Hearing this, Sea Emperor Whale stared intently at Layman Fengling, then reached out and summoned the copper piece back.

Rubbing the copper piece, he felt a faint disbelief in his heart.

If it were really just that, why would this copper piece have been so solemnly placed in the Primordial Demon Sect's treasury back then?

Layman Fengling seemed to sense his distrust but didn't care. He continued to drink by himself.

Meanwhile, Cape Heaven Matron soon let out a surprised sound.

"Ancestor, this is a fifth-order pill formula!"

"Oh?" Sea Emperor Whale showed a curious expression. "What pill formula is it? Tell me."

Cape Heaven Matron said excitedly, "Its name is the Origin-Returning Pill. It records the method of refining the origin qi of heaven and earth into a pill, which can greatly increase the magical power of a Transformation God cultivator! This is a cultivation-type pill formula!"

There were countless pills in the world, naturally divided into categories.

If one were to speak of value, various realm-breaking pills were the most precious. If one were to speak of commonality, it was cultivation-type pills.

But common did not mean cheap.

On the contrary, cultivation-type pills needed to be taken regularly. They seemed unremarkable on a daily basis, but accumulated over time, their effects could be even greater than realm-breaking pills.

When she said this, Sea Emperor Whale was also moved.

At his level, increasing his cultivation by even a little was extremely difficult.

The origin qi of heaven and earth was indeed everywhere, but before condensing a Law Manifestation, who would dare to recklessly charge into the ocean of origin qi and seize the power of heaven and earth's origin?

This time, he had scraped together a large pile of benefits from the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, including many fifth-order pills, which had allowed him to greatly advance his Dao, only a step away from the mid-Transformation God stage.

Having truly experienced the benefits of fifth-order pills for cultivation, he valued them even more.

Unable to contain his impatience, Sea Emperor Whale blurted out.

"Can you refine it?"

However, at this question, after her initial surprise, Cape Heaven Matron showed a disappointed expression.

"I cannot."

Sea Emperor Whale was greatly disappointed. "You are the most skilled alchemist among my Sea Clan. If you cannot, then isn't this pill formula just waste?"

Cape Heaven Matron said helplessly, "I have disappointed the Ancestor. This kind of fifth-order pill formula, let alone me, there are not many in the world who can handle it. Perhaps the Alchemy Saint of the Central Continent could have in the past, but she has already ascended."

"Is that so..." Sea Emperor Whale showed a disappointed expression, then waved his hand. "Forget it. This seat has cultivated to this point relying entirely on myself. If these external things come, they come; if not, so be it."

Cape Heaven Matron clutched the pill formula tightly, a figure flashing through her mind.

She hesitated for a moment. "Perhaps, in this world, there is still one person who can refine this pill?"

Sea Emperor Whale's spirits rose again. "Who?"

"Azure Yang Demon Lord Luo Hai!"

"Who is that?"

"Oh, perhaps now he should be called... the Alchemy Patriarch."

Sea Emperor Whale was stunned. "You mean, Luo Chen?"

Cape Heaven Matron's expression was complicated. "Indeed, it is him. Back then, this person and I took the Pill Realm assessment together. In the end, he overpowered me and obtained the alchemy inheritance of the Demon-Fall Land. In those years, he was suspected of having refined a fifth-order divine pill. Now, three hundred years have passed. He has likely fully digested the alchemy inheritance. A fifth-order pill should not be difficult for him."

"Is it him?" Sea Emperor Whale muttered, his expression shifting uncertainly. Suddenly!

"Who is it!"

His vast divine sense surged out like a tidal wave.

Within the palace, all the demons were terrified.

At the same time, another divine sense, without dodging or avoiding, came crashing in.

The two divine senses collided, and in that instant, they produced a deafening sound in the void.

Boom!

Ripples spread, undulations cascaded.

The entire Sea Emperor Palace shook as one, and an immeasurable amount of water vapor spread out, almost forming a sea of clouds.

Within the sea of clouds, two figures walked out, one in front and one behind.

The one in the lead was imposing, his gaze unrestrained.

"Fellow Daoist, weren't you just discussing this seat? Why do you not recognize me now that we meet?"

"It's you, Luo Chen!" Sea Emperor Whale was stunned, then his expression changed drastically. "You have broken through to the Transformation God stage?!"

As soon as these words were spoken, all the demons in the hall were shocked.

The name Luo Chen had been like thunder in the Northern Sea over the past few years.

Not to mention his old deeds of walking the Northern Sea under the names Azure Yang Demon Lord and Wilderness Wanderer, just the feat of him, at the Sage realm, defying the heavens and slaying a Dao Sect great power was enough to make everyone in the world know him.

A moment ago, everyone was listening to others praise his alchemy skills.

The next moment, the real person was right before them, and in such a majestic and overbearing manner.

He was already a Transformation God great power!

Cape Heaven Matron's spirit shook. She secretly glanced up at Luo Chen, then quickly lowered her head.

Still so young, still the same appearance.

But his overall temperament was no longer the youthful greenness of the past. Instead, it had gained a bit of domineering arrogance and willfulness.

This kind of almost arrogant temperament could only be seen on great powers like Sea Emperor Whale.

It was something only those who stood at the peak of a realm and had immense confidence in their own strength could possess!

Luo Chen naturally sensed the prying eyes of others.

He swept his gaze over them. Cape Heaven Matron looked familiar, but the demonically handsome green-robed cultivator who met his gaze without flinching stirred something in his heart.

Why was Mad Wanderer here?

Suppressing his doubts, Luo Chen faced Sea Emperor Whale directly.

"Fellow Daoist, it has been eighty years. Your demeanor is even more impressive than before!"

Sea Emperor Whale snorted coldly. "A slight, insignificant improvement, not worth mentioning. But you, to have advanced to the Transformation God stage in such a short time, truly congratulations! And felicitations!"

Each word was heavily stressed, and it didn't sound like any congratulation.

Luo Chen laughed heartily. He reached out and waved his hand.

The dense water vapor instantly condensed into a chair. He sat down calmly, level with Sea Emperor Whale, facing him.

Seeing this, Sea Emperor Whale's pupils contracted.

He had attained the Dao as a Sea Clan member, comprehending the origin of water, and was most skilled at controlling water vapor.

Especially in this Sea Emperor Palace, with the advantage of location, everything related to the water element was under his control.

But just now, Luo Chen had flipped his hand and controlled the water vapor, seizing partial dominance of this place.

Could it be that the other party had also comprehended the origin of water?

Sea Emperor Whale said in a deep voice, "Coming uninvited and sitting without asking—this is not the way of a guest!"

Luo Chen curled his lips and sneered. "Eighty years ago, in the Dao Sect war, it was our Eastern Wilderness experts who took the lead. Yet you also forcefully plundered a large amount of spoils. Tell me, at that time, who was the guest and who was the host?"

Sea Emperor Whale took a deep breath, his expression growing more manic. "It seems you still hold a grudge about what happened back then and have come to my door today to seek justice."

"It is not about justice." Luo Chen's demeanor was calm, but his gaze was as cold as an abyssal pool, icy and bone-chilling. "This seat, today, merely wants an explanation!"

Hearing this, Sea Emperor Whale burst into loud laughter.

The rumbling laughter stirred the entire sea region into a boil.

"Good!"

"You want an explanation, and this emperor will give you one!"

"This emperor also very much wants to know, now that you, who could defy the heavens and slay a Transformation God at the Sage realm, have become a Transformation God yourself, what abilities do you possess!"

(Chapter End)
