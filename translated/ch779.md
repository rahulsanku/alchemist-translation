# Chapter 779: Leave the Rest to Me!

The next day.

As dawn was just breaking, teams of people filed out from various parts of Lingtian Pass one after another, heading toward the Battle Platform.

Gu Caiyi and Sima Wenjie arrived early at the City Lord's Mansion to submit the day's combatant roster.

As the leading sect of the Nether Origin Sect, the first frontline fortress city jointly built by the twenty-one sects, the position of Lingtian Pass's city lord had fallen upon a Sage from the Nether Origin Sect.

His name was Ye Lingtian, hence the city was called Lingtian Pass, and he was respectfully addressed as City Lord Lingtian.

At this moment, City Lord Lingtian held a list of names and revealed an intrigued smile.

"Wang Yuan is going to battle this early?"

"With nothing else to do, this city lord shall go watch today!"

Hearing this, Gu Caiyi was momentarily stunned, then inwardly delighted.

The Sage who had once humbled himself to befriend Wang Yuan and invited him for a drink was none other than City Lord Lingtian of the Nether Origin Sect!

He greatly admired Wang Yuan. If he watched the battle today, it would surely boost the morale of the Luo Tian Sect's army.

Sima Wenjie, on the other hand, was caught off guard, a look of unease crossing his face.

He also submitted his side's combatant roster.

City Lord Lingtian glanced at it, the corners of his mouth lifting slightly. "Yin Sixiang? Tsk, this battle is indeed worth my trip."

Hearing that name, Gu Caiyi's expression shifted slightly as she stared fixedly at Sima Wenjie.

"You're willing to part with him!"

Sima Wenjie said nothing, hastily leaving the City Lord's Mansion. He needed to inform Old Ghost Motian and Divine Fire Sage that City Lord Lingtian would be watching the battle.

...

As City Lord Lingtian had the day's combatant roster posted outside the Battle Platform, the atmosphere that had been gradually brewing around this Ten Duel competition reached its climax.

War Demon Wang Yuan would personally take the field!

And on Sima Wenjun's side, they had dispatched Yin Sixiang, the renowned late-stage Adept Grandmaster!

The latter was no ordinary figure—he was an extremely formidable cultivator of the Five Elements Divine Sect, having reached the Great Perfection of the Adept realm. Rumors had it that he was merely a step away from the Sage realm.

Many already regarded him as the next Five Peaks Elder of the Five Elements Divine Sect.

Oh, that wasn't quite accurate either. The Five Elements Divine Sect was already a dog that had lost its home; its mountain gate was gone. What Five Peaks Elder was there to speak of?

And on the combatant roster, Meng Qin'er's name also drew considerable attention.

Meng Qin'er was a female cultivator of the Splendid Wind Palace who was currently riding a wave of popularity.

With peerless beauty and exceptional talent, no one understood why the Splendid Wind Palace had betrothed her to an unremarkable Initiate cultivator as his Dao partner.

Especially since it was said that Meng Qin'er was married to that cultivator as a second wife.

At the time, people were utterly baffled.

But later, as that unremarkable Qu Lingjun gradually displayed his superb alchemy skills, people understood why the Splendid Wind Palace had betrothed such an outstanding disciple to him.

Perhaps it was a case of "a waterside pavilion gets the moonlight first." Meng Qin'er's subsequent development exceeded everyone's expectations.

In just a hundred years, she had advanced all the way from the ninth layer of Initiate to the Adept realm, and later her cultivation level soared, even breaking into the mid-Adept stage, keeping pace with the older generation of Splendid Wind Palace cultivators.

Now that Meng Qin'er was entering the battle, did it mean the Splendid Wind Palace had begun to fully support the Luo Tian Sect?

Everyone speculated inwardly, but their main attention still fell on War Demon Wang Yuan and Yin Sixiang.

One was a formidable body cultivator who had once clashed head-on with a fourth-tier Demon Emperor; the other was a Great Perfection Adept, an Adept Superior with hopes of reaching the Sage realm.

Their battle was undoubtedly one of the top-tier fights below the Sage realm.

Various betting pools on the outcome of their battle had opened in the gambling dens.

Some major figures who had received the news also took an interest, arriving one after another at the Battle Platform to watch this fight.

...

As the sky gradually brightened and sunlight slowly spilled down,

Wang Yuan, under the gaze of tens of thousands, stepped onto the high platform step by step.

With each step he took, terrifying blood qi surged forth, forming a shocking, dense pillar of smoke above his head.

His eyes coldly fixed on the middle-aged man who had already ascended the platform.

Plain attire, ordinary features.

Nothing remarkable about him.

Yet under the pressure of Wang Yuan's aura, he still stood against the wind, showing not a trace of weakness.

"Yin Sixiang—cultivating from a Four Spirit Root body all the way to Adept Great Perfection, with profound dharma power, unmatched among his peers!"

"Reminds me of an old acquaintance!"

Wang Yuan took a deep breath, exchanged not a single word with his opponent, and the moment he heard the referee's "Begin," he launched a fierce attack.

Just one step!

As if shrinking the earth into an inch, he crossed a hundred zhang in an instant.

*Boom!*

A single punch was thrown, but his opponent showed no panic. A wall of earthy yellow light rose between him and Wang Yuan.

A third-tier earth-type defensive spell: Endless Fortress!

The punch missed. Wang Yuan rapidly shifted his footing, throwing punches at his opponent from various angles.

But as its name suggested, once the Endless Fortress was deployed, it stretched boundlessly, like an impregnable fortress wall, repelling the enemy's relentless attacks.

*Boom! Boom! Boom!*

Amid the violent thunder, Wang Yuan began to intensify his astral qi, concentrating his attacks on a single point.

Faced with this, Yin Sixiang remained unhurried, continuing to cast other spells.

One hand pinched a talisman, the other formed hand seals.

"Descend!"

On the Battle Platform, a patch of black spots suddenly appeared.

Those black spots began to spread outward from Yin Sixiang, forming a muddy swamp a hundred zhang in radius.

Wang Yuan, caught in the middle, found his lightning-fast movement forced to slow down. He had no choice but to first leave this area.

Seeing this, Yin Sixiang's hand seals shifted again.

*Zheng! Zheng! Zheng!*

Behind him, beams of golden light erupted wildly, radiating a terrifying sharp metal qi.

Looking closely, they were countless golden swords condensed from dharma power.

When the myriad sword rain had formed, Yin Sixiang lightly shouted,

"Go!"

In an instant, the sword rain surged like a dragon, relentlessly bombarding Wang Yuan.

All of this happened within just a few breaths, dazzlingly fast, too swift to take in.

Only then did the outside world suddenly react.

"I thought this man had grown dull from too much bitter cultivation. Facing a high-level body cultivator, instead of keeping his distance, he chose to engage head-on—utterly foolish. Now I see, I was too narrow-minded!"

"Using Endless Fortress to block the first wave, deploying the combined water-earth spell Azure Nether Black Swamp to restrict the War Demon's speed and establish terrain advantage, turning the original disadvantage into an advantage. After forcing the War Demon back, he bombards him relentlessly with long-range metal-type spells."

"Tsk!"

"Exquisite tactics, rich combat experience. Superior Sixiang truly lives up to his reputation!"

But from Wang Yuan's perspective, he felt something else entirely.

Incredible casting speed, an endless array of Five Elements spells, and that deep, bottomless torrent of dharma power...

All of it reminded him of an old acquaintance.

That brother who often sparred with him.

"You truly delight me, cultivator!"

Wang Yuan laughed heartily, no longer fixated on ground combat, and shot straight into the sky.

As the sword stream relentlessly pursued him, he channeled his palm, astral qi erupting.

Pure Yang One Qi!

*Boom!*

A single palm strike, and the sword river scattered.

Seizing the moment of respite, the ferocity on Wang Yuan's face grew even heavier.

His ten fingers flicked rapidly, sending blood cones flying toward his opponent.

*Chi chi chi...*

Countless blood cones, but every one of them was blocked by that earthy yellow light a zhang before reaching Yin Sixiang.

Clearly, his mastery of this spell had reached perfection.

Yin Sixiang paid Wang Yuan no mind, continuing to form hand seals.

But Wang Yuan was equally rich in combat experience—would he use the Blood God Tribulation Finger for nothing?

"The flaw is here!"

He shouted low, his body shooting out like an arrow. Torrential astral qi flowed around him, converging onto his right palm.

His charge was fierce, like a meteor falling from the sky.

Yin Sixiang frowned slightly, abruptly changing the hand seal he had been halfway through forming.

"Rise!"

*Rumble! Rumble!*

Under the astonished gaze of countless onlookers, the Azure Nether Black Swamp covering the Battle Platform suddenly sprouted thick vines.

Like enormous pythons, they stretched and twisted through the air, madly strangling that figure.

Wang Yuan's forward momentum seemed to plunge into a wild forest.

His expression did not change. Such tactics, such sudden changes—others might be caught off guard, but he had grown accustomed to them a hundred years ago.

The astral qi that had accumulated in his right hand suddenly transformed into a red greatsword.

Facing the vines lunging at him with claws bared, he slashed again and again.

*Crack! Crack!*

The thick vines shattered continuously, but upon hitting the ground, they would suddenly revive and continue chasing Wang Yuan.

But their speed was far inferior.

Wang Yuan simply charged straight ahead, ignoring the threats behind him.

When he once again reached the Endless Fortress, he brought his blade down directly.

Not only that, but he also pointed a finger at the earthy yellow light barrier.

The blade descended, and the fortress trembled violently.

One finger turned blood, and in an instant, it collapsed.

Through the blurry vision, Yin Sixiang snorted coldly, sweeping his hand toward Wang Yuan.

Countless fine needles, like ox hairs, flew out.

On their tips gleamed a deep, dark light.

A poisoned treasure?

Wang Yuan frowned, not daring to press deeper, and was forced to retreat again.

A blood-red qi barrier formed in front of him, blocking the flying needles' assault.

When he landed a hundred zhang away, he suddenly sensed something amiss.

Looking down, the muddy black swamp was devouring him like a living thing.

This shouldn't be!

His control over distance was exquisite—how could he have stepped back into the opponent's spell range?

Unbeknownst to him, Yin Sixiang had been secretly expanding the spell's area of effect.

But it was only a minor spell; Wang Yuan was not afraid.

He opened his mouth and spat toward the ground.

Powerful astral qi, like the gale winds of the nine heavens, burst forth.

Like an autumn wind sweeping away fallen leaves, this mouthful of astral qi instantly scattered the muddy black swamp beneath his feet.

It was at this moment that Yin Sixiang, who had not spoken until now, finally opened his mouth.

"Since you've entered my domain, you might as well surrender!"

After the muddy black swamp was blown away, four small flags emerged from the ground.

Yellow, green, blue, and gold—they blazed with light.

The Battle Platform, already trapped by a formation, was now covered by another layer of light barrier.

This was Yin Sixiang's most formidable technique: using his lifebound treasure, the Four Spirit Flags, combined with the ancient Four Symbols Formation, to construct a dharma barrier.

The Four Symbols Dharma Realm!

Once this realm appeared, the receding muddy black swamp rapidly regenerated.

Golden swords and silver blades formed in the sky.

And massive, thick green trees, like battering rams, rumbled into existence within the dharma realm.

When this technique appeared, the outside world erupted in an uproar!

Old Ghost Motian mused thoughtfully: "A pseudo-realm similar to a Sage domain? I didn't expect this child to have such a trick up his sleeve. The contingency I prepared for him probably won't be needed. But that's fine—it saves me from drawing Lingtian's criticism."

As he thought this, he couldn't help but glance at Divine Fire Sage, who sat nearby with an air of calm composure.

The Five Elements Divine Sect was indeed not to be underestimated!

Wind Moon Fairy's expression shifted dramatically. If she were caught in such a situation, she would surely face certain death. A deep wariness arose in her heart, and she couldn't help but look at the woman standing before her.

"Master, is this kind of technique something an Adept cultivator can even cast?"

Wind Moon Fairy's master was none other than the Grand Palace Master of the Splendid Wind Palace!

She first frowned at the barrier, then shook her head.

"This realm is impure, seemingly a fusion of formations, spells, and treasures, supplemented by various pre-prepared materials. With sufficient resources and patience, coupled with profound dharma power, it could indeed be set up at the Adept stage."

"You must all be wary of Yin Sixiang in the future. This man is quite extraordinary!"

Wind Moon Fairy opened her mouth, her voice hoarse: "Does that mean Wang Yuan is in danger?"

The Grand Palace Master hummed in acknowledgment, but added uncertainly, "I've heard that Wang Yuan's body refinement technique is unique. He should still have some means of fighting back."

Among the crowd watching below, Baleful Dragonzi, unlike the others, looked at his master's back with full confidence.

"Trust in Master. This realm cannot trap him!"

On another high building, City Lord Lingtian, upon seeing the Four Symbols Dharma Realm appear, set down his cup. But after just one glance, he smiled and shook his head.

"Such a trick cannot trap the man this city lord has his eye on!"

The next moment.

Within the dharma realm, the wind and clouds shifted.

"Five Qi Return to Origin, Giant Spirit Transformation!"

A towering figure, like a small mountain, began to rise higher and higher.

Yin Sixiang, manipulating the four small flags, saw this and twitched his mouth.

"This trick again?"

He was not flustered. After the black swamp, giant trees, and golden swords and silver blades, he waved his hand and sent out a strand of red silk.

It was the same Red Thread Binding Dragon technique that his junior sister Qi Emei had used against Baleful Dragonzi.

And compared to Qi Emei, his mastery of this technique was even greater.

But then!

Outside that massive figure, strands of powerful astral wind and baleful qi suddenly appeared.

The astral wind swept through, and the black swamp, swords, and blades all swayed and scattered.

The baleful qi corroded, and the red threads dissipated inch by inch, unable to even approach Wang Yuan.

Below the platform, Baleful Dragonzi clenched his fist fiercely.

"So this is the power of refining thirty-six strands of divine wind and thirty-six earthly baleful energies into the fleshly body?"

"Nothing can invade, no treasure can harm his body!"

Within the Four Symbols Dharma Realm, the nine-zhang-tall giant stood towering, looking down with unparalleled arrogance.

Strands of divine wind and streams of astral balefulness swirled around him, chaotic and murky, like a nebula in orbit.

He exhaled, and with a single punch,

All the green giant trees charging at him shattered in an instant.

Then, his gaze grew fixed, he stared intently at the middle-aged man outside the dharma realm.

Ignoring the constraints of the Four Symbols Dharma Realm, his massive body assumed a running posture. With a single stomp,

He charged forward like a colossal beast, dragging the binding force of the barrier, pulling the four small flags that had been ripped from the ground, rushing toward Yin Sixiang.

His lifebound treasure trembled, and a trace of blood seeped from the corner of Yin Sixiang's mouth.

Seeing this, he wiped his lips.

"Forget it. This is ultimately not a suitable battlefield for me. To capture this man, I'll have to use that thing."

His left hand opened flat.

A miniature mountain materialized.

On the mountain, jagged rocks rose steeply, and perilous cliffs towered.

With a toss of his left hand,

The mountain instantly transformed into a sky-obscuring colossus, completely covering the giant that Wang Yuan had become, pressing down heavily.

"ROAR!!!"

The giant let out an inhuman roar, surging with unmatched strength, trying to overturn the mountain.

But at this moment, it was as if heaven and earth were pressing down on him. No matter his divine strength, his fierce astral wind, or his rampant baleful qi, he could not budge the mountain an inch.

The crowd fell silent.

Only City Lord Lingtian slammed the table and stood up. "Damned scoundrels!"

At some point, Divine Fire Sage had already appeared before him.

"City Lord, it's merely a junior. Why such anger?"

City Lord Lingtian stared at him coldly. "You've broken the rules!"

Divine Fire Sage shook his head. "The rules don't say one cannot borrow another's treasure, do they?"

Indeed, the battle rules of Lingtian Pass had no such clause.

But what City Lord Lingtian found contemptible was that he could clearly sense a powerful Sage-level dharma power lingering within that true artifact.

"Is it Old Ghost Motian's Motian Cliff?"

He muttered to himself, then snorted coldly.

"Do you really think that a mere strand of residual dharma power can forcibly suppress Wang Yuan?"

"Oh?" Divine Fire Sage revealed a surprised expression.

He turned to look at the high platform. Under the crushing weight of Motian Cliff, the giant's skin was splitting inch by inch, and great torrents of blood flowed down.

But at some point, the skin on Wang Yuan's body, which had been like armor, had turned into scales.

"Using his lifebound essence blood as a guide to undergo a demonic transformation?"

Divine Fire Sage's brow furrowed tightly. He recalled the feat of War Demon Wang Yuan clashing head-on with a fourth-tier Demon Emperor back in the day—could this be the technique he used?

But once this technique was unleashed, it would surely severely damage his vitality.

"Forget it. Old Ghost Motian's methods are not so simple."

On the Battle Platform, Wang Yuan's expression was wild and frenzied. Scales sprouted all over his body, and behind him, a tail like a hammer slowly formed.

This battle—he had to win!

Even if it meant severe damage to his cultivation, even regression in his realm!

Just as he was about to spare no expense to overturn this mountain,

A sudden explosive sound rang in his ears.

The next moment, he felt the mountain's crushing pressure suddenly lighten. His massive body staggered slightly.

Looking up, a blurry figure stood on his shoulder, robes flapping in the wind. One hand was raised, holding the towering cliff aloft.

"Brother Wang, leave the rest to me!"

An unfamiliar tone, a familiar address. Vaguely familiar yet not quite, that face—young as it had been back then—struck straight into his heart.
