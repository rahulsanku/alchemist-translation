# Chapter 632: Each Fighting Alone, the Vine King Emerges

Amid towering mountains and steep ridges, ancient trees stood unevenly, and no sunlight could be seen.

In this expanse of lush green, where was the rumored withered-wood mountain ridge?

Five human figures walked through the mountain ridge without hurry.

The leader was the seven-star demon hunter Dao Lan. He seemed like a sharp blade cutting through fog, walking the most direct route with unwavering firmness through the winding, trackless forest.

Angler stood in the middle, holding his unique magic treasure, the Sound-Capturing Bell, by his fingertips.

On the left and right sides were Star Hook Envoy and Huangfu Song, each with solemn expressions.

At the very back was Luo Chen, whose realm was the lowest among the five.

Dao Lan being in front as the vanguard was guaranteed by his powerful strength.

Luo Chen, with the lowest realm, holding the rear was likewise a display of strength!

The name Devil Lord Azure Yang was not usually spoken aloud, but everyone had a count in their hearts. This could be seen from Huangfu Song's wary glances toward Luo Chen from time to time.

Toward Huangfu Song's gaze, Luo Chen seemed not to notice. As his divine sense spread out to investigate all directions, his heart also recalled the situation of Withered Wood Ridge that Angler had described on the way.

This was a territory occupied by wood-attribute spirit plants.

It looked like all kinds of strange and varied flowers and grasses were growing, but in truth all the spirit plants were only companion things of the Withered Blood Vine.

In other words, from the first step they set on Withered Wood Ridge, they had already been discovered by that fifth-tier Withered Blood Vine.

Fighting in such an environment, there was absolutely no geographical advantage.

Especially since according to intelligence, apart from one fifth-tier Withered Blood Vine, Withered Wood Ridge also had countless lower-tier Withered Blood Vines from second-tier to fourth-tier!

These Withered Blood Vines, under the Vine King's control, could also erupt with astonishing battle strength.

But this was only an illusion!

"The world says Withered Blood Vines are communal things, but this is actually greatly mistaken."

"Withered Blood Vines belong to spirit plants with an extremely strong sense of territory. Often, within a thousand li, no spirit plant of the same kind exists."

"Those low-tier Withered Blood Vines are essentially the Vine King's branch roots and offshoot tendrils. They can be regarded as child vines."

"In ordinary times, the Vine King remains in a sleeping state, absorbing spiritual qi. Even when it encounters outside invaders, it will only have the child vines carrying its own consciousness deal with them."

"What we need to do is cut off as many child vines as possible, weakening the Vine King's main body strength."

"As for the child vines' battle strength, you all need not worry. Even a fourth-tier Withered Blood Vine is at most equivalent to someone newly entered Golden Core. They might be troublesome for other Golden Core cultivators, but for us, it is merely a matter of spending some time."

Recalling Angler's words in his mind, Luo Chen did not completely relax.

Although spirits born from wood, stone, and spirit plants had very ordinary battle strength, such as those Hidden Dust Sand, which despite being dignified third-tier Great Perfection beings could only erupt with battle strength at the early Golden Core level.

However, one could not withstand great numbers!

When there were enough ants, they could bite an elephant to death.

If it were truly so easy, Angler would not have formed a lineup of five great late Golden Core battle forces for this task.

Very clearly, this Withered Wood Ridge, hiding countless Withered Blood Vines, was absolutely not that easy.

Just as Luo Chen was thinking, his steps suddenly paused.

"What is it?" Star Hook Envoy ahead asked.

At the same time, Angler's Sound-Capturing Bell also gave a clear ring.

Angler looked toward Luo Chen. As the two met eyes, they nodded.

"They are here!"

But not from the front. They came from behind.

Within the gloomy forest, white vines like spiritual snakes and coiling pythons swam over from all directions. Rustling sounds gradually gathered into the roar of a vast river.

Angler remained unperturbed in the face of danger, his aged voice sounding from his mouth.

"Continue forward!"

"It does not want us to leave, so we will go find it."

At the very front, Dao Lan said nothing, but a huge broad saber appeared in his hand.

He cleaved out with one saber, and in an instant, saber light dozens of zhang long erupted.

Where the saber light passed, trees snapped and the earth cracked. The low-tier Withered Blood Vines that had not yet formed an encirclement were immediately severed by this slash.

Seeing this slash, Luo Chen could not help raising a brow.

An utterly ordinary slash, yet its power was almost no weaker than his use of Blazing Sun Art, and there was no spellcasting time required.

"So this is Dao Lan, the seven-star demon hunter famed for battle strength among countless wandering cultivators of the Myriad Immortals Assembly?"

Luo Chen muttered inwardly, but his hands did not move slowly.

His great sleeves waved wildly, and countless fist-sized fireballs flew from his sleeves, striking at the Withered Blood Vines swimming over from every direction.

First-tier spell, Fireball.

Why use an ox-cleaver to kill a chicken?

What appeared at this time were only low-tier child vines; it was not worth wasting mana.

And this kind of Fireball, Luo Chen could release almost without limit, and there was no spellcasting time at all.

Under Dao Lan's and Luo Chen's attacks from front and back, the five men's footsteps did not stop in the slightest. With swift strides, they charged toward the depths of Withered Wood Ridge.

And within the mountain ridge, countless white vines were drilling out from underground, gathering toward them.

If one looked down from high above, this scene was simply like a heaven-reaching flood charging at a few ants.

...

Boom!

A dignified and upright sword qi slashed out horizontally, and surging sword light swept out like a tide.

When the sword light spread to several li away and gradually subsided, Huangfu Song appeared with an arrogant face, his gaze falling on a white vine struggling beneath his feet.

It was thick as a water barrel, its patterns like tree rings.

"Fourth-tier Withered Blood Vine has appeared!"

His arrogance gradually faded, becoming slightly solemn.

He turned his head to look, and his pupils abruptly shrank.

In his field of vision, Daoist Azure Yang in a red-cloud Daoist robe had transformed out a giant mana claw and was tightly grasping a fourth-tier Withered Blood Vine.

That vine was like a python, constantly struggling in his hand, but no matter what, it could not break free.

Just then, Luo Chen turned around, his gaze falling on him.

With a faint nod, Luo Chen chopped off the Withered Blood Vine in his hand and placed it into his storage ring.

In any case, it was a fourth-tier spirit plant. Even if only a branch tendril child vine, presumably it was worth quite a bit.

At the center of the team, only Angler had not yet participated in battle.

He stared at the bell in his hand, which constantly gave crisp rings, and the joy on his face grew stronger and stronger.

"Our direction is correct!"

"The appearance of fourth-tier child vines means we are getting closer and closer to the Vine King's nest."

"Everyone, prepare for true battle!"

Huangfu Song's heart tightened.

The initial battle with fourth-tier Withered Blood Vines just now had already made him start using magic treasures.

And the intensity of the coming battle would surely rise another level. He had to raise his spirits.

"If not for needing to rely on the Myriad Immortals Assembly as a great backer, I would not want to do this sort of meaningless thing."

"But in the Myriad Immortals Assembly, there are several other seven-star demon hunters, all late Golden Core figures. Why did Angler insist on conscripting me, a five-star demon hunter?"

This had been the doubt in his heart since setting out.

It was also one of the reasons he was unwilling to interact much with the others.

He kept feeling that the way Angler looked at him was not quite right.

...

Deep in Withered Wood Ridge.

A valley stood there like a low volcanic crater.

Five streaks of escape light, swift as meteors, shot over.

The moment they landed, the five men's expressions all changed.

Outside the valley, huge white vines thick as water barrels raised their heads and stood, eyeing them like tigers watching prey.

Angler moved past Dao Lan and arrived at the very front.

"Their number is not small, fully over a hundred. Gentlemen, this will be a bitter battle!"

Over a hundred fourth-tier Withered Blood Vines meant over a hundred early Golden Core enemies.

Even if their attack methods were scarce, limited to instinctive attacks like entangling and piercing, one could not withstand such enormous numbers.

For a time, the hearts of the other four sank somewhat.

A bitter battle. It was indeed a bitter battle.

But with the five men's abilities, if they used guerilla tactics and defeated them one by one, this battle would not count as difficult.

Yet very clearly, Angler was planning to forcefully attack the valley and kill into the Vine King's nest.

Angler suddenly turned his head. "You will not flee in fear of battle, will you?"

A stinking mountain wind blew through the jagged, rising rocks, brushing across the four men's faces.

The broad saber in Dao Lan's hand pointed diagonally at the earth, and his hoarse voice sounded. "Merely grass and wood spirits. Cut them down."

Huangfu Song said loudly, "Angler, you promised me that as long as this task is completed, you will negotiate with the Tianqu Chen clan in the Myriad Immortals Assembly's name!"

Star Hook Envoy glanced around and shook his head. "A pity. These Withered Blood Vines have no demon cores and cannot be used to make star radiance."

Luo Chen did not speak, but a giant hammer was already gripped in his hand.

Seeing this scene, Angler nodded in satisfaction. With a flick of his hand, a black-gold long rod stood before him, with withered, charred threads drifting in the wind.

"Your battle intent is passable. Then... fight separately!"

At his command, battle abruptly began!

As if sensing something, those fourth-tier Withered Blood Vines, long ready to strike, shot out like arrows and came tearing through the air.

Without thinking, the five figures flew in different directions.

Immediately, the over one hundred Withered Blood Vines divided into five groups and pursued them separately.

Those pursuing Angler were the most numerous!

Those pursuing Luo Chen were the fewest!

Seeing this, Luo Chen's mouth rose slightly.

However, when he saw Angler's back heading straight into the valley, he felt a little puzzled.

"He alone dares go stroke the Vine King's whiskers?"

But before the army of Withered Blood Vines in hot pursuit, he no longer had time to think about this doubt.

Whoosh!

A pair of wings spread out from behind Luo Chen.

Holding the giant hammer, he shot into the sky.

Below, white vines thick as water barrels chased and attacked with claws and fangs.

In the air, Luo Chen's extreme speed erupted. His figure turned as he wished, transforming into a crimson meteor, charging left and breaking right.

Those slow-moving Withered Blood Vines could not catch up with him at all.

And the giant hammer in Luo Chen's hand, under his control, was like a blacksmith's hammer, constantly swinging out violent smashing blows.

The Great Perfection Flowing Fire Hammer Method was used by him in battle for the first time.

Its effect... was not very good.

Those struck might become dispirited, but after falling to the earth and pausing for a moment, they would make a comeback.

Luo Chen frowned, and his divine sense swept across the other battle groups.

Angler using the tactic of each fighting separately as the tone for this battle was something he could understand.

After all, the five people were not companions who knew one another deeply. Their battle styles would inevitably clash.

If each person's methods affected others during battle, that would instead be undesirable.

Only by widening the distance and fighting separately could each person's strength be displayed as much as possible.

At this moment, under his observation, everyone's battle style immediately entered his eyes.

Dao Lan remained open and forceful. Every time the long saber in his hand slashed out, it stirred up surging saber qi.

He stood in place, unmoving as a mountain, yet the Withered Blood Vines surrounding him found it difficult to approach within three zhang of him.

It was truly domineering beyond compare!

Star Hook Envoy's expression was calm. His feet moved in strange steps, traveling through the rugged mountain paths as if walking on flat ground.

Whenever a fourth-tier Withered Blood Vine attacked, he would launch star radiance of different colors.

That was the Heavenly Demon Star Sect's secret-transmitted star radiance art, praised by countless people.

Yet in Luo Chen's spiritual eyes, he discovered that those were clearly third-tier Demon King inner cores!

Using Demon King inner cores as stars to cast star radiance arts?

Worthy of a former upper sect powerhouse, with deep reserves. Even in the midst of this fierce battle, he remained as leisurely and composed as if strolling through a courtyard.

Huangfu Song's side looked somewhat more unsightly.

At first, he tried to cut vines with a longsword, but fourth-tier Withered Blood Vines were incomparably tough. His sword qi was inferior to Dao Lan's saber qi and could not sever them in one strike.

When one plan failed, he wanted to imitate Luo Chen and first pull open distance.

But by then, the Withered Blood Vines had already completed their encirclement.

Helplessly, layers of purple light erupted from his body, covering several li around him.

Within the purple light, his sword qi seemed to gain support and became increasingly smooth and relaxed.

That purple light was very familiar to Luo Chen.

Back then above Peng Lake, when Luo Chen had used Mirror Flower, Water Moon to bewilder the other party, it had erupted once.

Luo Chen guessed that this was probably the trump card the other party relied on most.

After hastily sweeping across everyone's battle scenes, Luo Chen immediately understood.

"Hammer strikes and bombardment are not suitable for dealing with these fourth-tier Withered Blood Vines. I need to change methods."

"Slashing and piercing are more suitable."

"Unfortunately, my proficient Mystic Flame Sword is already destroyed."

"However, apart from that, other methods are not necessarily unusable."

With a thought, the Flowing Fire Hammer entered his storage.

Without the Flowing Fire Hammer's suppression, the white vines pursuing from below became increasingly rampant.

Luo Chen ignored them and merely opened his mouth to spit!

A wisp of pale smoke surged out from his mouth.

Looking at this wisp of smoke, Luo Chen's eyes carried a trace of regret.

After the battle on Cold Light Island, his Blazing Fire Miasma, refined from Withering-Bloom Balefulness, was reduced to only this clump.

He had originally wanted to use it as a base and slowly restore the complete Blazing Fire Miasma.

He had not expected to be busy with artifact-forging all these years and have no time to replenish it.

"But this art does not help me much in battle now. Let its final remaining might stay here on Withered Wood Ridge!"

With a murmur, Luo Chen's mana surged wildly out.

Along with the mana came wisps of azure flame.

Withering-Bloom True Flame!

Withering-Bloom Balefulness was originally baleful qi generated in places where Withering-Bloom Fire burned for many years.

At this moment, with the support of its source, plus Luo Chen's mana amplification.

Immediately, the smoke qi transformed into endless smoke and fire.

Luo Chen pressed down horizontally with one palm, surging downward.

In an instant, the white vines came into contact with the pale smoke and fire.

Sss! Sss! Sss!

A charred stench spread in all directions.

Seeing those fourth-tier Withered Blood Vines still struggling frantically inside the Blazing Fire Miasma, Luo Chen gritted his teeth and increased his mana output.

"Burn for me!"

Sss! Sss! Sss!

Increasingly loud burning sounds rang out. In the sky, more than ten huge vines were fully covered in azure flames, like azure dragons.

The extremely corrosive Withering-Bloom Balefulness constantly invaded their bodies, destroying the internal root tendrils of the Withered Blood Vines.

This process continued for fully the time it took to drink a cup of tea.

Only when Luo Chen almost felt his strength failing did those "azure dragons" slump weakly and fall to the earth.

Bang!

Luo Chen landed with both feet and looked at the fourth-tier Withered Blood Vines on the ground that were still struggling, unable to help frowning.

"Although they are said to possess Golden Core battle strength, their methods are ultimately single. Once the targeted method is found, they cannot withstand a single blow."

"However, this vitality is truly too tenacious!"

He drew in a deep breath, and mana inside his body again circulated as he wished.

Luo Chen's hands repeatedly formed spirit seals, then he placed his fingers together like a sword.

Sword qi after sword qi slashed from his hands.

Grass-Cutting Geng Metal Sword Qi!

This art was not high-grade, only second-tier. It was not even originally a battle spell, but merely a spirit plant art used by spirit planters to harvest mature spirit plants.

Yet precisely this art had rather good power against wood-attribute spirits.

Amid the shooting sword qi, the white vines on the ground that were still struggling were immediately severed.

As the vines broke, their flourishing vitality also vanished without trace in the blink of an eye.

Seeing this, Luo Chen subconsciously looked toward the valley.

"The life force of these child vines should originate from the mother body?"

Just as he raised his head and looked, he saw an aged figure stepping on countless fourth-tier Withered Blood Vine corpses shredded by threads, climbing to the top of the valley cliff.

That person turned back and looked down over the earth.

In his field of vision, the red-robed man stood empty-handed, no enemies remaining at his side.

The large man dragged his long saber backward and retreated, yet no white vines pursued him.

Star Hook Envoy in his resplendent star robe beckoned toward the void, and star radiance surged toward him one after another.

In the only place where battle had not ended, within the haze of purple light, following an angry roar, a square seal smashed out, forcibly crushing the fourth-tier Withered Blood Vines spread across the earth into powder.

Angler looked at that purple seal, and satisfaction appeared on his face.

"The wings have been removed. Now you should come out!"

As if sensing something, the earth responded with tremors.

Within the valley, a surging, unmatched pillar of spiritual qi shot into the sky.

The five great Golden Core cultivators raised their heads and looked up, their pupils trembling.

Inside the pillar of spiritual qi, a white long vine seemed like a giant dragon, stretching between heaven and earth.

*(End of Chapter)*
