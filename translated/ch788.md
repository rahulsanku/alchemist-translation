# Chapter 788: Convincing with Reason, Suppressing with Power

The feast began, and dishes were served.

There were also female cultivators invited from Lingtian Pass, performing light songs and graceful dances, playing zithers and blowing flutes.

Amidst the endless stream of people, two figures swam against the current and sat down beside Luo Chen.

Facing the curious gazes of the crowd, Luo Chen simply introduced them. "These are my two Dao companions, Sima Huiniang and Gu Caiyi!"

The assembled Sages swept their gazes over the two women, nodding in acknowledgment as they committed their faces and auras to memory.

If they encountered them in the future, they would more or less sell Luo Chen some face—if not treating them with courtesy, at least not excessively making things difficult for them.

Because of these goodwill-filled glances, the taut bodies beneath the two women's robes relaxed.

They sat behind Luo Chen, watching this man drink and chat cheerfully before the crowd, speaking with ease. Occasionally they exchanged glances, finding joy and happiness in each other's eyes.

Once, they had sought merely to achieve Qi Refinement, yet could not.

Now, they had built their Dao foundations, formed their golden cores, and could even sit calmly amidst a gathering of Sages.

This was a dreamlike scene they had never imagined!

And all of this was bestowed upon them by the man before them.

As their hearts surged with emotion, the dancing in the hall had long since stopped. Only the soft sounds of zithers and flutes accompanied them, along with the casual conversations between the Sages.

"The Northern Sea?"

"Yes, precisely the Northern Sea. The customs and people there are utterly different from our Eastern Wilderness. Especially the resources—they are incomparably abundant. Even among the rogue cultivators, there are those who have achieved the Great Dao of Sagehood!"

"I have read in ancient texts about the Northern Sea's abundant resources, but I never imagined they were so plentiful that even rogue cultivators could reach the Sage stage."

"Not just Sages! Take the Three Great Wanderers, for example—they are all capable of contending with late-stage Sage grand cultivators. The experts beyond the seas are truly as numerous as the stars, countless and endless!"

"The Three Great Wanderers? Does one of them happen to be called the Blood Wanderer?"

"Ninth Spirit Lord, do you know this person?"

"In my youth, I traveled through the Western Desert and indeed met this man. At that time, he was being hunted by the Buddhist sects of the Western Desert, quite miserable. But no matter how miserable, he still managed to survive. I took the opportunity to test my skills against him, and we were evenly matched. This man is indeed a worthy overlord, in no way inferior to us sect cultivators."

"True Sage Luo, you broke through to the Sage stage in just a hundred years. You must be quite famous in the Northern Sea as well, yes?"

"I have a bit of a reputation, but compared to the great wars currently raging in the Northern Sea, it's hardly worth mentioning."

"The great wars of the Northern Sea…"

Luo Chen started a topic and began telling the crowd about the past events of the Northern Sea.

One had to admit, tales of foreign lands were truly captivating.

Under his vivid narration, the atmosphere in the hall grew lively.

Especially when they heard the details of the Demon Sect's destruction and the rise of the four new overlords of the Northern Sea, everyone became somewhat restless.

But after the Lingtian City Lord swept his gaze over them with a smiling expression, no one had any superfluous thoughts.

The destruction of the Primordial Demon Sect was due to their lack of strength—they deserved that fate.

Their Nether Origin Sect was not so weak that they would be destroyed by mere demon beasts attacking their gates. It would be shameful to even speak of such a thing.

Suddenly.

"Fellow Daoist Luo, you say that after escaping from Green Parasol Mountain, you went to the Northern Sea?"

It was Lin Qingxuan, a former friend, now one pressing him aggressively.

Luo Chen smiled faintly. Since he had brought up the Northern Sea as a topic, he was naturally prepared.

Taking this opportunity, he would tell some of the past events from those years—half-truths, half-falsehoods—so that people would stop speculating about him.

He did not deny it, nodding his head.

"Yes!"

Lin Qingxuan shook his head violently. "Impossible! So many Sages, so many Adepts didn't escape. How could you have escaped alive?"

Luo Chen's expression was calm. "I don't know whether they escaped or not. But how can you assume that I couldn't? You weren't there, so how do you know the details?"

Lin Qingxuan was taken aback, then looked toward the two representatives of the Five Elements Divine Sect who had come today.

Divine Fire immediately picked up the thread.

"Back then, to rescue the trapped cultivators, the Nether Origin Sect organized three grand cultivators to charge into Green Parasom Mountain. In the final battle, the Nether Origin great power personally intervened, forcing Green Parasol Mountain to flee into the blue void."

"In that battle, Tianji Huiping and the Joyous Union Ancestor died tragically, and our Divine Sect's ancestor was severely wounded."

"In such a perilous situation, you were merely at the Adept stage. How can you speak of surviving?"

His words came fast and urgent, and the inside information he revealed shocked everyone present.

Everyone knew of the Green Parasol Mountain battle, but few knew the details and inner workings.

Tianji Huiping and the Joyous Union Ancestor were both world-shaking, legendary experts who had been renowned for centuries.

What kind of attack had they encountered that even their nascent souls couldn't escape, leading to direct fall?

And what means had a fifth-order sacred land used to flee into the blue void?

As Divine Fire Sage casually spoke these words, everyone's gazes involuntarily turned to Luo Chen.

At that time, Luo Chen was only at the Adept stage—yet he had survived?

What did that imply?

The golden-robed man beside Divine Fire Sage spoke sinisterly. "Could it be that you are a demon race…"

"Do you not understand the method of void translocation?"

A light laugh, laced with mockery.

Luo Chen glanced at the golden-robed man. Amidst everyone's confusion, an object appeared in his hand.

He casually tossed it—a cracked, compass-like object—which flew first to the Lingtian City Lord.

"Within Green Parasol Mountain, there was a peerless translocation grand formation. Using this formation, one could travel from the Northern Sea to Green Parasol Mountain in the morning, and cross the nine heavens into the blue void."

"But this formation was too important, and the demon cultivators of Green Parasol Mountain would not activate it unless absolutely necessary. They were forced to do so because the senior from the Nether Origin Sect had pushed them into a corner."

"And this gave me, Luo, the perfect opportunity to escape."

"Taking advantage of the translocation through the void, I activated my own self-made translocation array plate… but unfortunately, my cultivation was too low at the time, and I couldn't control the direction or distance. In the end, I was thrown into the Northern Sea cultivation world."

He couldn't possibly reveal that he had used the Azure Luan feather and ridden a teleportation formation. Otherwise, people would only ask why he possessed an Azure Luan feather—whether he had already defected to the demon race and gained their trust.

Thus, the translocation method he had mastered after advancing in formations became a new excuse.

And this excuse, when woven into the events of that time, was plausible.

He had indeed been teleported away, and the direction of the teleportation had indeed changed because Green Parasol Mountain had fled into the blue void. According to Han Zhan's original plan, they should have been teleported directly back to the Great Snowy Mountains.

This was precisely the kind of half-truth, half-falsehood that was easiest to use to fool people.

As Luo Chen spoke, he observed the Lingtian City Lord's reaction.

The other party examined the array plate with great interest for a while, but showed no major reaction.

After looking at it for a moment, he tossed it to someone else.

Elder Fu took it, somewhat puzzled. "This array plate… is a bit new?"

Luo Chen admitted it. "Indeed, it is somewhat new. The old array plate was destroyed long ago. This object was made by me, Luo, within the Tianyuan Dao Sect in the Central Continent, and I have used it several times."

That array plate was precisely the Nine Heavens Ten Earths Grand Transposition Array Plate that Luo Chen had made in the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven.

As for "several times," that was an exaggeration.

But to the people who successively examined it, an array plate that could be used for translocation several times was already of excellent quality!

If Luo Chen had possessed a similar treasure back then, and used the opportunity of Green Parasol Mountain's peerless grand formation to activate it simultaneously, there was indeed some hope of escape.

But only some!

Jin Ling did not take the array plate handed to him, staring coldly at Luo Chen.

"If we're talking about formations, Heaven Smelter, who was imprisoned there at the time, was far superior to you, was he not!"

Luo Chen smiled without warmth, staring back at him. "The attention that a Sage cultivator receives is not something someone like me could compare to. Heaven Smelter's physical body was destroyed, his nascent soul imprisoned—he was even worse off than a minor Qi Refinement cultivator."

"Then what about Haoranzi and Wei Wuya, who were also trapped in Green Parasol Mountain at the time? They were also proficient in formations. Were they inferior to you?"

"Yes, they were inferior to me!"

"You!"

Luo Chen's face was full of confidence. "Otherwise, how could I have achieved Sagehood? How could I stand shoulder to shoulder with all of you?"

Half-truths and half-falsehoods were indeed easy to make people believe, but whether others believed them depended heavily on their personal subjective feelings.

Clearly, these two from the Five Elements Divine Sect had never trusted Luo Chen from the bottom of their hearts!

Since that was the case…

If he couldn't convince them with reason, then he would suppress them with power!

A surge of aura slowly spread from Luo Chen's body.

Like an abyss, like a prison—domineering and unparalleled!

Behind him, Sima Huiniang and Gu Caiyi's faces were filled with shock, their hair flying wildly. They felt as if an ancient volcano was about to erupt before them.

The immense pressure nearly suffocated them.

But with a thought from Luo Chen, the oppressive feeling vanished without a trace.

Catching their breath, the two women exchanged glances, their expressions shaken.

If just a trace of residual power made it hard for them to breathe, then what kind of pressure was the main target of this aura enduring?

Sage domain!

Almost the moment that aura surged forth, everyone present recognized Luo Chen's method.

In an instant, all eyes turned sharply to the two from the Divine Sect.

Luo Chen's provocation demanded a response.

But he was merely a newly ascended Sage. Divine Fire Sage responding would be appropriate enough.

"Hmph!"

A cold snort rang out, and golden light seemed to flash through the hall.

"Perfect. Let this old man test your mettle!"

Before the words faded, another aura, in no way inferior to Luo Chen's, soared into the sky.

The surrounding spiritual energy of heaven and earth began to converge madly, condensing into a sky full of golden light above the golden-robed man's head.

It was Jin Ling!

The Grand Palace Lord of Fenghua Palace's expression changed slightly. Domain collision was the purest and most direct contest of Dao cultivation, not involving divine abilities, spells, magical treasures, or true artifacts—second only to a contest of divine sense between cultivators.

This method of purely displaying one's own magical power, she had originally not worried about Luo Chen.

Since he had been able to suppress the old ghost of Motian back then, Divine Fire Sage probably wouldn't pose any threat to him.

But no one had expected that it would be Jin Ling who made the move!

As the head of the five peak elders of the Five Elements Divine Sect, Jin Ling had the highest cultivation, at the fifth layer of the Sage stage!

His strength was second only to the Supreme Elder of the Five Elements Divine Sect.

Placed here, aside from the Lingtian City Lord, almost no one dared to claim they could defeat Jin Ling with certainty.

Even she and the Ninth Spirit Lord were only on par with Jin Ling in cultivation.

The Lord of the Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion might be very strong in combat, but his cultivation was slightly inferior.

It could be said that, in the absence of grand cultivators, Jin Ling was already considered a top-tier expert.

How could such an existence be compared to Luo Chen, a newly ascended Sage?

"Fellow Daoist, you are going too far," she said coldly.

Beside her, Xianhe Sage also spoke with a displeased tone. "Is the Five Elements Divine Sect always this overbearing? One might not know and think you were a sacred land expert."

As he said this, Xianhe Sage secretly glanced at the Lingtian City Lord above.

But the other party sat there calmly, watching the two with great interest, his expression unmoved.

The Lingtian City Lord did not intervene—that was tacit approval for this "Dao discussion" to continue.

Receiving the hint, Jin Ling revealed a ferocious smile.

"Let me see what confidence you have to boast about standing shoulder to shoulder with us!"

His Sage domain, already compressed to the extreme, began to slowly fly toward Luo Chen at his mental command.

This domain collision was different from any Luo Chen had ever seen.

In the past, they were broad-domain collisions, where neither party deliberately compressed the power of their domain. Instead, they expanded their range as quickly as possible, encroaching on each other and vying for the area covered by heaven and earth's spiritual energy.

But this time, due to the venue's limitations, they couldn't do that. So they pursued extreme collision in a small area!

It was a test of control over one's own magical power!

It was a test of the depth of one's magical power and the corresponding amount of heaven and earth's spiritual energy one could draw!

What was being compared was the fine, meticulous control each could exert within a square inch!

Looking at Jin Ling's ferocious face, a line of text from the *Luo Tian Compendium* surfaced in Luo Chen's mind.

**[Duan Feng and his wife, due to their expertise in identifying ores, were ordered by Sage Jin Ling of the Five Elements Divine Sect to go to the Two-Modes Tin Forest support battle in the Wuji Domain. They fell there, leaving no descendants.]**

"I haven't even come to demand an explanation from you, yet you come to me on your own. Perfect!"

A cold glint flashed in Luo Chen's eyes as his Sage domain pressed toward Jin Ling.

The next moment, red clouds and golden light collided, forming a storm of spiritual energy in the hall that swept in all directions.

The zithers and flutes had long since stopped.

Only the sound of tables and chairs shaking, and beams and pillars trembling, remained.

This commotion was a bit too much.

Seeing this, the Lingtian City Lord prepared to intervene and preserve the venue.

After all, this was Luo Chen's Sagehood celebration. Although he wanted to see what abilities the other party had used to overpower his peers in the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, he didn't want to let outsiders see the other party possibly embarrass himself later.

But just as he was about to make a move, his gaze suddenly sharpened, looking at the hall's dome. Then he revealed a thoughtful expression.

"Is it some treasure or formation covering this hall? This Luo Chen, though he looks young, has deep foundations and endless methods."

The extreme domain collision erupted with indescribable magical power bombardment.

Jin Ling's cultivation was higher than Luo Chen's, but he found that the other party's magical power was incredibly deep, drawing in no less heaven and earth spiritual energy than he could.

For several whole breaths, he couldn't overpower the other.

Jin Ling gritted his teeth, surging more magical power from his nascent soul, and his entire body inexplicably floated up.

Sitting cross-legged in the void, facing Luo Chen from afar.

"In the end, your cultivation is inferior to mine!"

With that, the golden light domain, aided by the nascent soul's magical power, suddenly expanded, dazzling and blinding, impossible to look at directly!

Luo Chen's white-gold Dao robe rustled loudly, his long black hair tied behind his back flying wildly. As the red light blazed, his entire body seemed about to ignite.

In his vision, his domain was retreating step by step.

Faced with this scene, Luo Chen grinned.

The inferior cultivation was not the main reason—his magical power was even deeper than the other's.

The reason his domain couldn't overpower the other was actually due to his unstable cultivation and poor fine control over his magical power.

But all of this was within his expectations.

Without any visible movement, he softly uttered a single word.

"Melt!"

The next moment, the red clouds that had been retreating step by step suddenly surged back, completely enveloping the golden light.

A wave of domineering spiritual energy began to frantically expel the metal-attribute spiritual energy.

And a wisp of pale white flame suddenly appeared in the domain, as if it had been hidden for a long time. The pale white flame chased after the original magical power that Jin Ling had released, constantly eroding and annihilating it.

Without the magical power to guide it, the masterless heaven and earth spiritual energy collapsed even faster.

Fire inherently countered metal!

And the move "Melting the Void" possessed a powerful exclusivity.

As Luo Chen's hidden move was revealed, the situation in the hall suddenly changed.

The golden light shattered inch by inch, and the red clouds continuously converged, pressing toward Jin Ling.

Only a sound… *Boom!*

A dull, heavy sound rang out, and Jin Ling fell to the ground, uncontrollably stumbling backward.

*Stomp! Stomp! Stomp!*

When he finally stopped, he turned his head sharply, his face full of fury.

"You have no martial virtue! You used external force!"

Defeated!

Jin Ling, who had been in the upper hand just moments ago, was defeated in the blink of an eye!

Lin Qingxuan, only at the first layer of the Sage stage, was bewildered. Divine Fire Sage was stunned.

Fenghua Fairy was incredulous, yet also somewhat thoughtful, as if she had glimpsed the essence of Luo Chen's technique.

She looked up at the Ninth Spirit Lord opposite her. He was the same, stroking his chin, as if savoring Luo Chen's performance in the domain battle.

And above, the Lingtian City Lord's finger traced the rim of his wine glass, and he couldn't help but nod slightly.

Such ability was indeed enough to overpower the newly ascended Sages of the sacred lands. Even compared to the seasoned experts of ordinary sects, Luo Chen was in no way inferior.

As for the others, they showed different expressions and attitudes as they watched the scene above.

*Whoosh…*

The sky-filling red clouds dispersed, and Luo Chen sat calmly above.

His black hair lay flat against his back, the corners of his robe smooth.

The golden-red dragon-patterned crown remained steady on his head.

He coldly looked at the furious Jin Ling. "What external force did I use?"

"That fire!" Jin Ling pointed at Luo Chen, his whole body trembling. Being defeated before the crowd drove him nearly mad. "You hid a strange fire within your domain!"

Luo Chen's mouth twitched, and a wisp of pale white flame appeared at his fingertip.

"You mean this?"

"Yes!"

"Haha, this is my original true flame. Does this count as external force?"

At these words, Jin Ling's expression froze.

The others in the hall were all curious.

Integrating one's original true flame into one's own Sage domain—that was indeed a good idea!

But clearly, what allowed Luo Chen to defeat Jin Ling was not just this small trick. His domineering Sage domain, which forcibly expelled other attribute heaven and earth spiritual energy, was the main reason.

Add to that the mutual counter of the five elements, and Luo Chen's unexpectedly deep magical power… Jin Ling's defeat was not unjust!

Facing the pitying and sympathetic gazes, Jin Ling almost vomited blood. In the end, he suppressed the anger in his heart.

"This is but a minor trick. Another day, we shall truly test our skills!"

Throwing down a harsh word, Jin Ling left directly.

After he left, everyone's gaze fell upon the other person from the Five Elements Divine Sect.

Divine Fire Sage!

It should be said that at today's Sagehood celebration, Luo Chen had only invited Divine Fire. Jin Ling had come uninvited.

Now, under everyone's gaze, Divine Fire sat uneasily.

Luo Chen smiled, raising his wine glass to the crowd.

"Continue the music! Continue the drinking!"

The zithers and flutes started again, cups and goblets clinked without end, as if nothing had happened. But many things had indeed happened.
