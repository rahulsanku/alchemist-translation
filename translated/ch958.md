# Chapter 958: Yellow Wind Grand Formation, Dragon Lion Awakens

"You deserve death!"

Amidst the furious roar, a vast expanse of golden light scattered.

The golden light was biting cold, containing endless killing intent within.

Looking closely, it turned out to be countless fine grains of sand, blotting out the sky and covering the sun, inspiring despair in one's heart.

The Hidden Sword Pavilion Master's expression shifted slightly upon seeing this. Countless sword-leaf gu insects flew out from the sword case on his back, forming a barrier of sword qi to block the attack.

Yet the moment the sword leaves touched the sand, tiny holes appeared on many of them, as if they had been eaten by insects.

The Purple Robed Marquis was also having a hard time. His body transformed into a purple light, darting left and right.

But under the golden light's envelopment, the purple light grew increasingly dim, even beginning to waver unsteadily.

Just then.

A cerulean light screen rose from the earth.

Like a trickling stream, it leaped past the two men, flew into the sky, and suddenly became incredibly tough, blocking the vast golden light outside.

Before a palace gate, the old man the Golden Lion Emperor had seen earlier blurted out upon seeing this cerulean light screen: "Glazed Gu!"

Then he became furious:

"Li Tianshi, you have overstepped your bounds!"

Before the bronze gate, Li Tianshi laughed heartily: "Lion Minister, why must you be so angry?"

Upon hearing this, the old man called Lion Minister laughed in anger: "Forcibly breaking into the Yin-Yang Realm, killing my fellow disciples, and you ask me why I am angry?"

Li Tianshi said calmly: "If you voluntarily tell me where the Death Gate is located, I can spare your lives."

Death Gate!

As soon as these two words were spoken, there was no response from Lion Minister's side.

Li Tianshi frowned. Just then, the Hidden Sword Pavilion Master and the Purple Robed Marquis returned together.

"Are you alright?" Li Tianshi asked casually.

The Purple Robed Marquis breathed a sigh of relief. "Nothing serious. I just didn't expect this legendary Wrathful Manifestation Sage to have such strength."

Wrathful Manifestation Sage was Lion Minister's Dao name. Compared to his real name, more people in the Southern Frontier cultivation world knew him by this Dao name.

Hidden Sword Pavilion Master Fan Wujiu felt deeply moved, especially when he looked at the tiny holes on his sword leaves. His face couldn't help but show heartache.

These sword-leaf gu insects were all painstakingly refined by him with great effort and cost.

He hadn't expected that in a single encounter, ten to twenty percent of them would be destroyed.

Li Tianshi said matter-of-factly: "Lion Minister is not only a Grand Cultivator who has been famous for many years, but also a former true disciple of the Holy Land. It's not surprising that he can easily handle you two."

The Purple Robed Marquis and Fan Wujiu looked at each other, momentarily speechless.

Were all Holy Land disciples this formidable?

They asked themselves, in the outside world they were already regional powers, outstanding among their peers.

But whether in realm or technique, they were completely suppressed by Lion Minister.

This gap...

"Alright!"

Suddenly, a relieved voice came from behind.

Everyone looked and saw that Old Lady Redleaf had just walked out from the formation's light.

She exhaled a mouthful of turbid qi. Though her expression showed fatigue, there was more joy.

"The formations of the Life and Death Gate are indeed different from the outside world. Even this brief study has benefited me greatly."

Li Tianshi didn't want to hear this. He asked directly: "Is the barrier complete?"

Barrier!

Upon hearing this term, Luo Chen's heart stirred.

Restrictions, formations, barriers—these were progressive relationships.

Restrictions were the foundation of formations, and barriers were the high-level derivation of formations.

A large number of restrictions gathered to form a formation, and a reasonable arrangement of formations formed a barrier.

But the latter was not common, because arranging such things was too cumbersome, impossible to use during battle, and was often only set up within the interiors of large sects.

Oh, there was another situation.

Many ancient cultivator ruins were formed when the formations arranged during their lifetime naturally combined with the surrounding heaven-earth textures and mountain-river trends over many years, spontaneously forming barriers.

Therefore, many secret realms were often given the appellation "realm."

He had only thought Old Lady Redleaf was setting up a formation earlier, but now looking up, it was clearly a small barrier that completely enveloped the area within three li of the bronze gate.

Old Lady Redleaf smiled: "I have studied the Empty Bright Realm you gave me for decades. Though time is short now, I have not failed my mission. With this Empty Bright Realm, outsiders cannot enter, and those inside cannot leave!"

Upon hearing this, Li Tianshi's face lit up with joy.

"In that case, this Yin-Yang Remnant Realm is like a turtle in a jar. As long as you all help me open the Death Gate and enter the underworld where the Great Power Yin-Yangzi passed away, this journey will be considered a complete success!"

Hearing these words, Meng Shentong beside him said displeasedly: "Why is it so troublesome?"

Luo Chen also found it quite troublesome.

Not to mention the realm within a realm, but within this Yin-Yang Realm, there was also an underworld?

Then conversely, the place they were in now was presumably the mortal world?

And Li Tianshi's answer indeed confirmed Luo Chen's thoughts.

"When a generation's Great Power is implicated and passes away, how deep must their resentment be? If they hadn't opened up two realms to separate inside from outside, do you think these Life and Death Gate cultivators could truly suppress the Great Power's lingering thoughts?" Li Tianshi asked in return.

Meng Shentong had nothing to say.

"Let's go! Once I enter the underworld and find the gu recipe, this journey will be considered a complete success. The favors you all owe me will also be wiped clean!"

Li Tianshi waved his hand and strode forward.

Meng Shentong asked curiously: "Do you know the direction?"

Li Tianshi said confidently: "I naturally don't know. After all, when I accidentally entered the Yin-Yang Realm back then, I also went directly into the underworld. But the Yin-Seeking Flying Ants I painstakingly modified have already made some progress. I've roughly identified three locations that might be the Death Gate."

Meng Shentong suddenly understood.

No wonder those flying ants earlier were so similar to Soul-Chasing Flying Ants.

So it was this old fellow's modification.

Yin-Seeking?

Quite fitting!

Just as everyone was about to set off, the earth suddenly began to tremble.

Even more, waves of yellow wind rolled in from all directions.

Seeing this, Li Tianshi was furious: "How dare you! I haven't come to find trouble with you, yet you dare to strike first?"

This voice, to Luo Chen's ears, was very close, yet ethereal and elusive.

As if right before his eyes, yet far beyond the horizon.

His expression changed slightly. He took a step forward, wanting to approach Li Tianshi.

But after one step, the yellow wind was still rolling, and no one was in sight.

"Is it that Lion Minister's technique?"

Luo Chen frowned and drew his fingers across his brows.

Open!

His Crimson-Gold Pupils manifested, illuminating the heavens and earth.

Yet what he saw was empty, with no one there.

Luo Chen opened his mouth slightly and immediately realized something.

"A thousand li in an inch, a translocation technique!"

Clearly, the cultivators of the Golden Lion Empire had not been idle during their many years entrenched in the Yin-Yang Realm.

They had even set up a grand translocation formation on this land, capable of forcibly moving people without any warning.

But this way, wouldn't everyone be separated?

And recalling Lion Minister's attitude earlier, Luo Chen's heart suddenly chilled.

"Separate and scatter, then pick them off one by one!"

For a moment, he looked around at his surroundings, feeling a sinister atmosphere, as if within the rolling yellow wind, countless gazes were coldly watching him.

...

Inside a majestic palace.

Lion Minister and another old man in splendid brocade robes stood before a bronze mirror, both with gloomy expressions.

"Old Four is dead. Old Three and Old Five are trapped outside. Now within the Yin-Yang Realm, it's just you and me. I've used the translocation technique to forcibly separate them, but this method can only last at most seven days. After seven days, the Yellow Wind Formation will collapse on its own, and they will surely have a way to contact each other and quickly regroup. So, seven days is our last chance. Second Brother, what do you plan to do?"

"Either open the Death Gate and hide in the underworld, or pick them off one by one." The Golden Lion Second Ancestor gave the expected answer.

But Lion Minister directly rejected the first suggestion.

"The Death Gate cannot be opened. Times have changed. The Life Gate and Death Gate are closely related. Now that our sect's mountain gate is tightly shut, if the Death Gate opens, the Life Gate will also reveal its weakness. Moreover, it's obvious that old thing Li Tianshi is after the underworld behind the Death Gate. Once the Death Gate is opened, the commotion will be too great, and he will surely follow the sound."

"Then kill them?" The Golden Lion Second Ancestor hesitated.

Lion Minister asked in return: "Isn't that our duty? To both suppress the Yin-Yang Realm and prevent outsiders from stealing Ancestor Yin-Yangzi's research results."

Second Ancestor was somewhat worried. "But those seven are no easy opponents!"

Indeed, they were not easy to deal with.

Li Tianshi's prestige in the Southern Frontier was too high, his appeal extremely strong.

Ordinary secret realm explorations generally only involved a few Sages entering. Even Ascendant secret realms, Sages didn't dare to enter.

But Li Tianshi, through favors, transactions, inducements, and various other means, had forcibly assembled a luxurious lineup of three Grand Cultivators, three mid-stage Sages, and one body-refining expert.

Among them, none were simple.

Lion Minister asked: "Don't you want to avenge Old Four?"

The Golden Lion Second Ancestor was somewhat silent.

They addressed each other as brothers and sisters, but they were not blood-related.

All were from the Life and Death Gate, with relationships that were naturally good and bad.

He and Old Four really didn't have much friendship.

"Enough. Li Tianshi, Meng Shentong, and Redleaf—these three, you don't need to deal with. I'll handle them myself! Among the remaining four, you take care of them one by one. Be quick, don't delay too long."

"That's doable." The Golden Lion Second Ancestor showed a look of interest. He was, after all, at the mid-stage Sage realm and came from the Holy Land. He had no fear of dealing with those of the same realm. But he still couldn't help asking: "Divide and weaken our strength, why not join forces to attack?"

"Absurd! Don't you know that the Yellow Wind Formation arranged by Ancestor Youli only allows one person to occupy a single space? We can use the formation's power to forcibly enter, but it only allows one person." Lion Minister scolded.

The Golden Lion Second Ancestor suddenly understood, then immediately remembered something. "Then I request to awaken the Dragon Lion as an aid!"

Lion Minister looked at Second Brother, feeling helpless inside.

This Second Brother was truly cautious and afraid of death!

His strength was clearly not weak, yet he dared not take risks.

He nodded. "That's fine too. With the Dragon Lion's help, you can finish the battle quickly!"

The Golden Lion Second Ancestor was overjoyed!

He himself was not weaker than those four, and with the Dragon Lion's assistance, he could even fight a Grand Cultivator.

"Act as soon as possible, before they adapt to the Yellow Wind Formation." Lion Minister urged.

"Mm." Second Ancestor nodded. Before setting off, he asked casually, "Who do you plan to deal with first?"

Lion Minister's face was full of anger. "Naturally, that ingrate Li Tianshi!"

...

Within the rolling yellow wind, a black figure walked alone.

The Crimson-Gold Pupils were no longer opened.

Because Luo Chen had discovered that within this Yellow Wind Grand Formation, using spiritual eyes for too long would make his eyes burn unbearably, tears falling uncontrollably.

The wind and sand had blinded his eyes, so to speak.

Not only his eye techniques, but even his divine sense, when Luo Chen cautiously tested it, could not extend beyond ten li.

This abnormal situation made him extremely vigilant.

Holding a sachet in his left hand, Luo Chen marveled inwardly.

That Li Tianshi's gu-refining methods were truly wondrous.

In a situation where his eyesight couldn't reach and his divine sense couldn't spread, this Hundred Thoughts Sachet, besides refreshing his mind, actually had a faint, indistinct connection.

He was now following this faint connection's guidance, moving closer to reunite with Li Tianshi and the others.

The Yin-Yang Realm was divided into the mortal world and the underworld.

What the underworld was like specifically, Luo Chen didn't yet know, but from the current situation, the mortal world was quite large.

If he wanted to reunite with the others while ensuring safety, it would take at least three to five days.

As for how to ensure safety?

Luo Chen's ears suddenly twitched!

The sound of sand falling rustled in the wind.

This was not unusual, but another low, threatening roar came faintly.

"What is that?"

Luo Chen looked up and saw a hundred-zhang-tall golden lion statue crouching on the ground undergoing a strange change.

Sand fell, golden light flickered.

Suddenly!

The golden lion opened its eyes, staring at Luo Chen.

This statue had actually come to life!

Luo Chen's expression changed slightly, and he directly struck out with a palm.

Boom!

The statue had only just awakened, yet it displayed agility completely inconsistent with its size.

With a single roll, it dodged this palm strike.

Then, it took steps and charged at Luo Chen with an unstoppable momentum.

Thump!

Thump!

Thump!

As the Golden Dragon Lion ran faster and faster, the rolling yellow wind seemed to become its aid, forming clouds beneath its feet, as if in a dream.

Luo Chen still did not retreat, allowing the Golden Dragon Lion to charge before him.

Facing the claw that came smashing down, he soared into the sky like thunder and kicked out.

Boom!

With just one kick, the previously majestic Golden Dragon Lion was heavily struck.

Its massive body began to shatter inch by inch like sand.

Eventually, it turned into a pile of rubble.

"All show, no substance!"

Luo Chen shook his head, not taking it seriously.

But the next moment, his face sank.

Because from the edge of his ears came the rumbling sound of running.

How large the mortal world within the Yin-Yang Realm was, he didn't know, but from that brief glimpse before the gate earlier, there were no less than a dozen Golden Dragon Lion statues within his line of sight.

Now, more and more Golden Dragon Lions were awakening, all charging toward him.

If such a scene was not being controlled by someone, Luo Chen would never believe it.

"So, where is the real enemy?"

Luo Chen slowly floated into the sky, his hands brushing past his tall crown, removing two Life-Seizing Golden Plumes and holding them in his hands.

Looking at the dozens of Golden Dragon Lions surrounding him from all directions in the rolling dust on the earth, his face was fierce.

With a flick of both hands!

Crack!

Crack!

The void exploded, and the Life-Seizing Golden Plumes stretched out without reason, turning into two long whips.

"Once I kill all these stupid creatures, you will surely appear!"
