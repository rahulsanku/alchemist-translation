# Chapter 544: Devouring Demon Art, Advancing to Mid Stage

The Black-Scale Jiaolong originated from a kind of demon beast living in the Boundless Strait[TERM?], the Black-Scale Giant Python[TERM?].

This kind of demon beast had an enormous body but was not particularly outstanding in strength.

If it were strong, it would not have been captured and subdued by Kun Jiao of the Jiaolong Gang.

The reason it later evolved in the direction of the jiaolong race was because Kun Jiao used a secret method to cultivate it, thereby giving birth to that dragon horn.

Even so, its combat strength still did not achieve a qualitative improvement. When meeting demon beasts of the same tier, its chances of victory were only fifty-fifty.

Now that it had encountered an Overlord Crab famed for combat power, it was utterly routed.

Black clouds covered the sky, and wild winds howled.

Luo Chen hid on the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull's back, carefully watching the battle below.

His originally delighted gaze gradually became solemn as his divine sense spread to other places.

"This formation..."

Under the perception of his vast divine sense, within the surrounding several hundred li of sea, who knew how many demon crabs were hidden in the depths.

Even in farther places, some black shadows could faintly be seen rising and falling among the waves.

At this point, how could he not understand? The Black-Scale Jiaolong had been ambushed!

"I overlooked it."

"Letting the Black-Scale Jiaolong hunt in one area for a long time, how could it not attract attention?"

"Especially from this kind of large demon beast race that lives in groups!"

"In that case, it is actually inconvenient for me to easily interfere."

Struggles between demon beasts were still within their bottom line.

But once an outside race interfered, the nature of the matter became completely different.

Especially now that the Primordial Demon Sect had fallen in the Northern Sea. Even though the demon race experts occupying Arctic Yemo Heaven had not launched large-scale attacks, it still marked that demon beasts were currently powerful.

"For now, I will let this third-tier Overlord Crab go!"

As he muttered, Luo Chen rationally abandoned the idea of acting.

Down below, the heavily injured Black-Scale Jiaolong was already filled with terror.

Watching its own scales shatter inch by inch beneath that pair of giant claws that could destroy anything, fear gradually spread through its entire body.

Especially since,

from all directions, who knew how many malicious gazes were staring at it.

At this moment, it truly had no path to heaven and no gate into the sea... wait, heaven?

Its gaze flicked over. Beneath the black clouds, there was indeed a graceful figure continuously circling.

It could no longer care about dignity and cried out hoarsely.

"Heavenly Pivot, save me!"

At the same time,

the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull transformed into a mass of hurricane and dove from the sky.

Its two enormous wings flapped continuously.

A breath of wind grew from small to large, transforming in an instant into a tornado.

Where the tornado passed, a terrifying whirlpool formed with a boom in the great sea.

The place this whirlpool covered was precisely the battlefield where the Black-Scale Jiaolong and that Overlord Crab were fighting.

Under the support of outside force, both were forced to stop their fight.

An opportunity!

The Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull dove, its two talons grabbing the Black-Scale Jiaolong's enormous body. From its mouth came a charming yet domineering voice.

"Do not resist!"

Afterward, it leaped up, following the trend of the tornado whirlpool and flying into the sky.

After several flaps of its wings, its figure gradually vanished into the horizon.

After they left,

boom!

With a rumble, the colossal creature suffused with golden color raised its two cold golden giant claws and violently shattered the tornado whirlpool.

That Overlord Crab walked out from within, both eyes vicious as it looked toward the direction the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull had flown away.

Its mouth continuously issued angry roars.

After the tornado whirlpool was shattered, the powerful members of the demon crab race hidden in all directions gradually surrounded it.

"Lord Golden Claw[TERM?], why let that mixed jiaolong run?"

"In human words, releasing a tiger back to the mountain leaves endless disaster!"

"There were rumors before that this Black-Scale Jiaolong had helpers. It escaped with its life after being surrounded and attacked several times by the Azure Emperor Crab race. Looking now, it was indeed true."

"Letting him run this time will make it difficult to arrange an encirclement next time."

Facing the voices of doubt, Golden Claw's violent gaze swept across them.

Wherever it passed, all fell silent.

He snorted coldly. "After eating this round of attacks from me, that mixed jiaolong is gravely wounded even if not dead, and cannot move in the short term. Moreover, he should already have been frightened out of his wits."

Afterward, his gaze shifted toward a distant place in the great sea. There stood an island with the largest land area in the Xuanyan Sea Region.

"The urgent matter is to attack Xuanyan Island[TERM?]!"

"The Xuanyan Turtle Emperor[TERM?]'s lifespan ended, and three hundred years ago, he went to the Fallen Sea ancestral land to return to the void[TERM?]. Before leaving, he left behind a shed husk for the Ring-Headed Turtle race."

"So long as I obtain that shed husk, not only can I craft the strongest battle armor, I may even have hope of advancing to fourth tier. At that time, my Overlord race will be the true and worthy overlord of the Xuanyan Sea Region!"

"Golden Armor[TERM?] and the others have already besieged Xuanyan Island for several years. Now that I have come out of seclusion, I will break through that turtle-bastard island in one stroke!"

As he spoke, the two claws waving before him became increasingly cold and sharp.

The world only knew that demon crabs ran rampant in the Xuanyan Sea Region.

But it did not know that the true master of this sea was instead a Ring-Headed Turtle with Black Tortoise bloodline.

Even the name of the sea region came countless years ago from that Ring-Headed Turtle's true name.

...

Heavenly Pivot Island.

Inside the cave residence.

Looking at the Black-Scale Jiaolong curled into a ball and covered in wounds, Luo Chen's mouth twitched.

Before, because they were far apart, he had not discovered that his spirit beast was actually injured so severely.

Those hideous and terrifying wounds, if placed on a human cultivator, would have killed them thousands of times over.

Only the Black-Scale Jiaolong, whose original body was a Black-Scale Giant Python, could hold on. Although it had no great combat strength, it won in its enormous body and vigorous vitality.

However, from this, one could also indirectly see that the true realm of that Overlord Crab might be above the apparent mid third tier.

"Could it be a late third-tier great demon king?"

While muttering, Luo Chen circulated medical arts and began treating the pitiful Black-Scale Jiaolong's injuries.

For such terrifying external injuries, ordinary cultivators truly had no methods and could only rely on nurturing.

Fortunately, when Luo Chen's realm had been low, he had concurrently cultivated many medical arts, and had cultivated all of them to Perfected Completion.

At this moment, under his unrestrained use, medical art after medical art fell like rain.

Organs returned, bones restored, rot removed, bleeding stopped.

Spring wind transformed into rain, healing and growing flesh.

Paired with some wound medicines he had brought before leaving, the Black-Scale Jiaolong slowly escaped danger.

However, because its body was truly too enormous, this treatment took Luo Chen a full ten days. After all, Luo Chen could no longer freely use five-element medical arts as he had back then, and could only cast a little before converting his mana.

Ten days later,

looking at the pitiful Black-Scale Jiaolong, Luo Chen's heart softened for once.

"In consideration of how hard you have worked this past year, here, these things are your reward."

As he spoke, crystalline pills dropped before the Black-Scale Jiaolong.

Seeing those things, the Black-Scale Jiaolong's eyes lit up.

Demon cores!

And all of them were second-tier demon cores.

Normally, it could only eat a little leftover scraps, mostly internal organs.

It had not expected that after being gravely injured, Master would actually reward it with precious demon cores.

Ordinary demon races found it very hard to digest the demon cores of other demon beast races, but its Black-Scale Giant Python race was different.

That enormous body and incomparably vigorous vitality all came from its uniquely gifted digestion ability.

Forget demon cores, even rocks and giant trees could be digested somewhat when they had nothing to eat.

Its previous master had been too stingy.

The second-tier demon beasts it hunted had all been swallowed privately by Kun Jiao, who left it no benefits at all.

After changing to a new master, this one had only commanded it like a slave.

It had thought that for the rest of its life, it would have to live so miserably.

It had not expected that this master also had a generous side!

"Many thanks for Master's reward!"

The Black-Scale Jiaolong said ingratiatingly.

"With this batch of demon cores, my foundation will certainly not fall. This lowly one will properly recuperate, then hunt even more demon crabs for Master."

Luo Chen shook his head. In the short term, the Black-Scale Jiaolong would not be able to help him.

Fortunately, there were enough demon crabs hunted before. Many had yet to be refined into Black Emperor Paste, so there was no rush.

Moreover, there was still the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull to command.

With the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull's ability to come and go freely, capturing some low-level demon crabs was still extremely easy.

Just as he was preparing to send the Black-Scale Jiaolong out to recuperate, Luo Chen's heart suddenly stirred.

Foundation?

Suddenly, a jade bottle appeared in his hand, liquid swaying within.

"Take this and try it."

The Black-Scale Jiaolong was puzzled. Half believing and half doubting, it accepted the jade bottle and opened it slightly.

Inside, the liquid gurgled, shining like starlight.

"What is this?"

"Imperial Moonlight Nectar[TERM?]."

...

Giving Imperial Moonlight Nectar to the Black-Scale Jiaolong was something Luo Chen did because interest suddenly came upon him.

Imperial Moonlight Nectar was originally a spirit medicine most suited for demon races to establish their foundation!

Otherwise, Nether Spring would not have taken notice of it.

He had refined a great deal of Imperial Moonlight Nectar on Cangwu Mountain, even pushing its proficiency to Perfected Completion.

Although most of it had been handed to Nether Spring, he himself had also kept a portion.

And its grade was extremely high, all supreme-grade!

He wanted to test what effect supreme-grade Imperial Moonlight Nectar would have on a Black-Scale Jiaolong with terrible aptitude, coarse, fat, and large, only knowing how to eat and drink.

Of course, this was an idle move.

If it had an effect, that would be best. If it had no effect, the loss to him would not be large.

Luo Chen's thoughts were still placed on his own cultivation.

Large batches of third-tier Black Emperor Paste, under his refinement, were placed one by one into black jars.

Even though he consumed them every day, the stockpile grew more and more.

The Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull no longer had its previous idle-cloud, wild-crane ease, and was forced to take over the Black-Scale Jiaolong's work, responsible for capturing second-tier demon crabs for Luo Chen.

Very clearly, however, after learning the Black-Scale Jiaolong's lesson, Luo Chen became even more careful.

Therefore, every time the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull went out hunting, he would remind it to be exceptionally cautious.

Even if it encountered enemies, it should not bring them back home.

For this, he even had Han Zhan make a small method that could be triggered immediately, for Heavenly Pivot to carry at all times.

...

White steeds passed through cracks, and daylight flowed on.

Unknowingly, five years had passed since Luo Chen arrived in the Xuanyan Sea Region.

Over these years, through the combined efforts of the Black-Scale Jiaolong and the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull, they continuously gathered many fresh demon crabs for him.

If counted by number, there were over a thousand!

During this process, the dangers the two demons encountered were also countless.

The Overlord Crab race's encirclement was only one of them.

Once, the Black-Scale Jiaolong tried to capture a second-tier Red Rock Crab for Luo Chen, but accidentally angered the Red Rock Crab race that had come out to sunbathe.

That time, when the Black-Scale Jiaolong barely escaped back, its entire body was red, as if it had been steamed cooked.

Luo Chen spent quite a lot of effort before curing it.

The Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull's side was not safe and sound either.

It knew Luo Chen liked the tender meat of Demon Spider Crabs best, so while hunting, it would also try to pay attention to traces of Demon Spider Crabs for Luo Chen.

However, this kind of demon beast was extremely strange.

The spider silk it spat was incomparably tough. Even the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull, which came and went freely, found it hard to break free once stained by it.

If the Black-Scale Jiaolong had not repaid its life-saving grace by desperately holding off that third-tier Demon Spider Crab until Luo Chen arrived to rescue them, Heavenly Pivot might have fallen under the demon crab's sharp pincers.

The reason danger had come frequently these years was also predictable.

With such large-scale capture of second-tier demon crabs, how could the several great royal demon crab races remain indifferent?

Especially since Luo Chen wanted only live ones!

This gave those third-tier experts among the royal demon crabs enough time to react.

So long as the prey held on long enough, it could delay until the experts of its own race arrived to rescue it.

Thus, the two great demon beasts under Luo Chen's command encountered danger frequently.

Even so, they still advanced one after another, tirelessly enjoying it, doing their utmost to hunt more demon beasts for Luo Chen.

The reason lay in Imperial Moonlight Nectar!

During the four years, after taking Imperial Moonlight Nectar several times, the Black-Scale Jiaolong changed!

Not only did its realm rise somewhat, it even inexplicably comprehended a demon art[TERM?].

Once this art was cast, an enormous suction force would arise within a hundred li.

Even Luo Chen, if he were within ten li, would find that terrifying suction force extremely hard to resist.

Under this terrifying suction force, nothing could not be devoured.

So long as something was swallowed into the Black-Scale Jiaolong's body, it would be refined by the highly toxic acid inside and turned into part of its realm.

Regarding this art, Han Zhan gave an explanation.

"Bloodline inheritance[TERM?]!"

The ancestral bloodline of the Black-Scale Jiaolong was unknown, and the Black-Scale Giant Python was a relatively ordinary demon beast race. Over countless years of reproduction, it had mixed who knew how many foreign bloodlines.

Precisely because its bloodline was impure, this kind of demon beast found it very hard to awaken so-called demon race bloodline inheritance.

When Kun Jiao used a secret method to transform the Black-Scale Giant Python into a third-tier jiaolong, he had merely stimulated one weak jiaolong bloodline within it.

But it was truly too weak, so it could not produce a qualitative change.

Yet under Luo Chen's supply of large amounts of supreme-grade Imperial Moonlight Nectar, the Black-Scale Jiaolong dug out another inheritance from its bloodline.

Very clearly, if that "Devouring" demon art[TERM?] was used well, the Black-Scale Jiaolong's future would certainly be limitless!

Because of this mutation, the Black-Scale Jiaolong began running around and working hard for Luo Chen from the heart, no longer complaining or crying grievance.

The proud Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull, though verbally showing nothing, also worked much harder, hoping Luo Chen would reward more supreme-grade Imperial Moonlight Nectar.

Unfortunately!

After consuming his stockpile, Luo Chen basically had no ability to do more.

The main material for Imperial Moonlight Nectar was easy enough: complete second-tier demon beast flesh, blood, and bones.

But those unimportant auxiliary materials became the limiting factor instead.

He did not have enough auxiliary materials in his storage ring. After later refining two furnaces of supreme-grade Imperial Moonlight Nectar, they were already completely exhausted.

Moreover!

His current focus was not raising two spirit beasts.

After once again sending away the Black-Scale Jiaolong, which had stared at him eagerly, Luo Chen activated the formation to isolate inside and outside.

He swallowed one of the few supreme-grade True Flame Pills and began circulating the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture.

A vigorous aura gradually emerged as spirit qi was continuously swallowed into his physique.

If not suppressed by the grand formation, it would likely have long since covered a hundred li around him.

Outside the formation, Han Zhan, who was mobilizing the thousand-plus second-tier demon souls and two second-tier Ghost Kings in the Myriad Souls Formation[TERM?], subconsciously glanced this way.

"Signs of advancement!"

"Although this trip was for body refinement, with daily accumulation, his qi refinement realm has still been pushed step by step to Core Formation Stage Four!"

"This Luo Chen's perseverance is rare even across my entire cultivation life."

Just as Han Zhan sighed emotionally, his mind suddenly stirred.

With a beckon, a dazed little ghost flew before him.

"Eh? My voice-transmission little ghost[TERM?] is gone. Could Heavenly Pivot have encountered trouble?"

He looked toward Luo Chen, then after leaving a voice transmission talisman, still controlled the Myriad Souls Banner and flew away from Heavenly Pivot Island.

Very clearly, after getting along over these years, Luo Chen did not say it aloud, but he still had some feelings for the two spirit beasts, especially since those two beasts had also taken on the important task of hunting for him.

If possible, Han Zhan still had to preserve them somewhat.

A mass of black cloud flew south.

(End of chapter)
