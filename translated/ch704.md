# Chapter 704: Divine Origin Immortal City, Star-Chasing Demon Beast

"Senior, I have thought it through."

Sang Jinghe stood on the green lawn outside the sinkhole and bowed toward the young man ahead.

"Mm. What have you decided?"

"I am willing to follow at your side, Senior, and serve with all my heart."

Receiving this answer, Luo Chen did not turn back, only smiled softly.

"Very good. Having this decisiveness is quite admirable."

Hearing that laugh, Sang Jinghe felt as if a heavy burden had been lifted.

Making this decision had truly been difficult. It meant he had to give up everything Third Grandfather left behind, including Tumulus Mulberry Sect, a force that had begun to possess some influence within the Myriad Immortals Assembly.

But he had considered it carefully. Although Tumulus Mulberry Sect currently had many disciples and sect members, it had no truly weighty figures.

If he returned, putting aside whether he could achieve Core Formation, merely carrying the sect forward would already be extremely difficult. At most, he could only preserve what existed.

By contrast!

If he followed Daoist Azure Yang, this widely renowned Adept cultivator, he would certainly benefit without end.

In the end, Sang Jinghe understood the intention behind Third Grandfather Sang Jiugong refusing to let him return to Tumulus Mulberry Sect before his death and making him remain long-term on Purple Spirit Island.

It was to tightly embrace Daoist Azure Yang's thigh!

"There is something I did not tell you before. On this journey to Fallen Sea, I offended Moon Wanderer, master of the Myriad Immortals Assembly."

"With her temperament, she will certainly pursue me relentlessly. Those in the Myriad Immortals Assembly who are connected to me will likely be innocently implicated. Tumulus Mulberry Sect is the same."

"If you had not planned to follow me before, I would also have advised you to watch for a long while and not rashly return to Tumulus Mulberry Sect. In gratitude for Fellow Daoist Sang's friendship with me, at most I would have bestowed one or two life-protecting methods on you..."

Listening to the Daoist's calm words ahead, Sang Jinghe unknowingly broke out in cold sweat.

If that were truly the case, then Tumulus Mulberry Sect was probably already surviving in name only!

As his body trembled, Sang Jinghe looked at the Purple Monkey Flowers swaying in the wind below the sinkhole, about to be transplanted, and inexplicably felt a sense of shared fate.

From now on, he too would leave his homeland and become a rootless person, would he not?

...

The black city was towering, banners fluttering.

Pedestrians flowed like woven threads, carriages and horses like streams.

Tall, grand white buildings stood one after another throughout the city.

Cries, calls, and solicitations entered the ear one after another.

A peerlessly beautiful woman stepped alone into the black city.

Looking at this architectural style entirely different from the Northern Sea cultivation world's, the woman gave a cold snort.

"Do the cultivators of Divine Origin City also hide their heads and show only their tails, daring only to peek from the shadows?"

The cold shout was low, but with the support of divine sense, it spread in all directions and enveloped the entire Divine Origin City!

Every Sage who heard it was shocked.

"Late-stage Sage!"

"She really broke through!"

"Who would have thought that within three generations of Penglai Immortal Sect, the one to achieve the realm of a Great Cultivator would not be that highly anticipated Penglai Sect Master, but this traitor instead."

"The rumors were not false. Moon Wanderer truly has the realm of late-stage Sage. But if so, is that so-called spirit treasure rumor also true?"

...

As one of the top five premier immortal cities in the present Northern Sea, Divine Origin City had no fewer than a handful of Sage Sages coming and going year-round.

Especially in recent months!

After the Myriad Immortals Assembly returned from Fallen Sea's secret realm with a great victory, the number of powerhouses coming to Divine Origin City, which bordered the Myriad Immortals Assembly, abruptly multiplied severalfold.

Among them, the Sage Sages, openly and secretly, had even passed into double digits!

One had to know that at the current white-hot stage of the righteous-demonic war, for so many Sage Sages to still come to Divine Origin City was an unimaginable thing.

After arriving in Divine Origin City, they linked up with one another, constantly exchanging information and forming alliances, and more or less all of them were watching the Myriad Immortals Assembly's side.

But no one had expected that the person involved, Moon Wanderer, would actually ignore this power and personally step into Divine Origin City.

She had even uttered mocking words in front of all the watchers.

Such conduct either belonged to someone of great bearing, or someone with absolute confidence and reliance!

Those prying gazes timidly withdrew.

A figure drifted over.

"Moon Wanderer, I trust you have been well since we last met."

Moon Wanderer's slender lashes narrowed slightly as she sized up the newcomer.

"Shen Wandou, I wonder whether you are meeting me as the Grand Elder of the Northern Sea Tianyuan Merchant Alliance, or as an elder of the Star Gate of the Tianyuan Dao Sect?"

White-haired Shen Wandou frowned.

"What difference is there? Though this old man lives idly in the Northern Sea, I am ultimately a Tianyuan disciple."

Moon Wanderer's lips curved slightly. "The difference is enormous. If I am to treat you as the Merchant Alliance's Grand Elder, then I want you to fulfill the promise made when the Myriad Immortals Assembly and Tianyuan Merchant Alliance established relations, and allow me to use Divine Origin City's teleportation formation. If the latter... I do not trust people from other continents."

Shen Wandou fell silent.

After a long while, he stepped aside and raised his hand to usher her forward.

"Please!"

Moon Wanderer smiled. As the hem of her skirt fluttered, her lotus steps shifted lightly.

Two incense sticks later.

Within Divine Origin City's central hall, a pillar of light suddenly shot into the sky.

When the many Sage Sages saw this pillar of light, their expressions could not help changing greatly.

That was the sign of a teleportation formation activating!

Moreover, one glance showed that it was not some short-distance teleportation. At the very least, it was a super-long-distance teleportation formation capable of crossing the Northern Sea from north to south.

"Fellow Daoist Shen, how could you let Moon Wanderer leave so easily?"

"Could it be that she does not even want the Myriad Immortals Assembly she established with her own hands?"

"This is bad. If Moon Wanderer leaves, have we not schemed here for nothing?"

Facing one divine-sense transmission after another, Shen Wandou stood there with a bitter smile on his face.

He did not reply. Instead, he left the teleportation hall and came to the depths beneath Divine Origin City.

A Sage cultivator had been waiting there for a long time.

Seeing Shen Wandou, the young Daoist made a salute with one hand. "Liu Yuan greets Martial Uncle."

Shen Wandou waved a hand. "Since you have already achieved the Sage realm, and have no clan relation with me, the past seniority can be set aside. We may address one another as peers."

Dragon Abyss Sage, whose name was Liu Yuan and whose title was Dragon Abyss, could not help smiling.

"Senior Brother Shen."

"Mm, Junior Brother Liu."

After reestablishing their relationship, both sides relaxed considerably.

Dragon Abyss Sage pointed above. "That woman left?"

Shen Wandou nodded. "She left, but judging by appearances, I fear she will return."

"Return?" Dragon Abyss Sage frowned slightly, but soon relaxed his brow. "Returning is good as well. I observed that Yemo Heaven's seal is on the verge of collapse, and with that archaic beast Ghost Immortal Tower charging into it, the vile creatures within are most likely preparing to break the seal and emerge. When the time comes, the Myriad Immortals Assembly led by Moon Wanderer standing on the front line can also buy enough time for my Tianyuan Dao Sect."

Shen Wandou had the same thought, and then sighed.

"These Northern Sea cultivators, ah. Without the Primordial Demon Sect as the great sky above their heads, every one of them has become unbridled. They actually dare dream of moving against a Great Cultivator. They simply do not know life from death."

Dragon Abyss Sage asked with a smile, "Could it be Senior Brother never had that thought?"

Shen Wandou's expression became somewhat awkward, but he quickly waved a hand. "I would not dare! Unless Junior Brother Fu and the other two return, and together with you, a rising star, form the Five Tribulations Killing Formation with us, there might be a thirty percent chance of defeating Moon Wanderer."

"Thirty percent? That seems a little high!" Dragon Abyss Sage said softly. "I have interacted with Moon Wanderer several times. This woman's cultivation is unfathomable, and her methods are numerous. Now she also has a spirit treasure to anchor her position. Even a Five Tribulations Killing Formation formed by five early-stage Sage cultivators would likely be unable to do anything to her."

Shen Wandou was slightly surprised. He had not expected Moon Wanderer to receive such high praise from this junior brother's mouth.

She was only an abandoned disciple of Penglai!

One had to know that Penglai Immortal Sect was merely an idle chess piece placed by the Tianyuan Dao Sect in the Northern Sea.

Could Moon Wanderer, after betraying Penglai, somehow surpass her origin?

"Forget it. In any case, there is no opportunity to deal with her, and that spirit treasure is not something we can covet. Let us speak of you first!"

Shen Wandou's expression turned solemn. "Have you truly decided to participate in the Dao Sect's great competition held once every five hundred years, and enter that Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven?"

Dragon Abyss Sage also put away his smile, his face becoming serious.

"We children of poor families find it difficult to enter the sect's eyes. In an entire lifetime, we may never touch the opportunity of the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven. Only when advancing to early-stage Sage do we have this one chance."

"I gave up Central Continent's favorable life, left my clan and relatives far behind, and came alone to this Northern Sea to struggle. Step by step, I fought my way from Adept Stage Six to nine-star demon hunter. Borrowing the Myriad Immortals Assembly's resources, only then did I luckily achieve the Sage realm."

"Was all of that not for the chance, when the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven opens once every five hundred years, to obtain that opportunity for Ascendant advancement?"

Shen Wandou frowned deeply. "Since you have such great ambition, why not borrow the power of the Demon-Fall Land?"

"Heh, could it be Senior Brother Shen does not know that the Demon-Fall Land is Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's traveling palace? Even if it contains an inheritance leading directly to Ascendant, in all likelihood it is deeply connected to Devil Lord Heaven-Refining. This demon, it is said, was disliked by the Great Venerable. I absolutely dare not be tainted by his karma."

Dragon Abyss Sage slowly spoke, revealing secrets outsiders did not know.

Including Shen Wandou!

"Where did you learn these things?" His face was full of shock.

Dragon Abyss Sage shook his head. "That is not convenient to say. What I seek now is only to return to the sect as soon as possible, have my name entered into the Dao registry, and make it convenient to enter the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven."

Seeing that he was unwilling to say more, Shen Wandou could not press further.

However, he still asked one more question.

"The Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven is incomparably dangerous. It was transformed from the ancient Great Wilderness. The Dao Sect's great competition is held once every hundred years, but this once-every-five-hundred-years competition is the fiercest, because the venue is set inside the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven. Ordinary participants usually travel in groups and support one another. Do you have someone in mind?"

"I do!" Dragon Abyss Sage said without hesitation, and a cold, lonely figure appeared in his memories.

"Fine! Since you have such resolve, this senior brother will not dampen your spirits. However..." Shen Wandou pointed outside. "The teleportation formation leading to Central Continent and the super-long-distance teleportation formation crossing the Northern Sea are the same one. Moon Wanderer used it just now, and its energy is exhausted. I am afraid you will have to wait several days."

"No matter. I have waited a hundred years. What are these few days?"

Dragon Abyss Sage said with a smile.

His mood appeared calm, but in truth, there was already a touch of agitation.

A hundred years had passed, and he, whom his fellow disciples had once looked down upon, had already achieved the Sage realm.

Now was precisely the time to return home in glory!

...

Above Shell-Leaf Island, deep within the cloud layers, radiance suddenly bloomed.

A graceful figure slowly emerged from within.

Her body staggered once, then steadied in an instant.

Her slightly pale face looked around, and the emotions in her heart surged unstably.

"The Tianyuan Merchant Alliance's cross-sea teleportation formation can actually cover a place this distant. The Merchant Alliance... no, the Dao Sect! What exactly does the Tianyuan Dao Sect intend to do?"

After murmuring once, Moon Wanderer chose a direction.

She raced away like wind and lightning.

Ten days later, her figure appeared above an empty sea region.

She looked left and right, then gave a cold snort.

"A mere eye-blinding trick thinks to fool this Wanderer?"

With a casual point of her finger, towering waves immediately rose.

After the waves fell, cluster after cluster of purple qi spread out from an island.

Sensing the intensely poisonous purple qi, Moon Wanderer could not help letting out a breath.

"So this is Purple Spirit Island?"

"Let me see whether that Azure Yang brat has come."

Her divine sense swept out, crossing the entire island in an instant.

Suddenly!

"Foolish. He actually still dares linger and refuses to leave."

"This is good as well. It saves me most of my effort."

The woman smiled faintly, and cruelty flashed in her eyes.

She stepped forward, and when she appeared again, she was already on Purple Spirit Island's ground.

Her slender, delicate hand stretched out, grabbing toward a figure deep within the earth vein.

Rumble...

Majestic mana broke earth and split stone, breaking the seal in an instant and seizing toward the black-clothed Daoist seated cross-legged deep within the earth vein.

Just then.

Blood light spread across that black-clothed Daoist's body, and he gradually swelled.

"Wrong!"

Moon Wanderer's expression changed slightly, and she was about to shoot into the sky.

However, it was already too late.

Boom!

A destructive power erupted from within the earth vein.

In a single instant, the entire Purple Spirit Island, guided by the formation arranged there for several decades, turned into powder.

Terrifying energy, accompanied by sky-high shattered stone, soil, and wood, vented outward.

An explosion of this sort would kill an Adept cultivator on contact.

Even a Sage Sage, if careless for a moment, would suffer heavy injury.

But before the smoke and dust had even dispersed, Moon Wanderer stepped out from within the endless aftershocks. Looking at her bearing, she was actually completely unscathed.

Her expression was dark, and between her fingertips she pinched a wooden shard that seemed neither gold nor iron, with an aura so thin it was nearly gone still lingering on it.

As she observed it, that aura completely dispersed.

Mana surged, and the wooden shard turned to powder.

Moon Wanderer snorted coldly. "Using a Sage-stage puppet incarnation as bait to lure me onto the island. Quite the courage. This absolutely is not Azure Yang brat's method. It should be the method of that black-robed person who controlled the white tiger puppet to attack me back then."

"However, in the end, you underestimated me."

She took a deep breath. With her back to the towering waves raised by the shattered island behind her, she summoned an ink-colored diagram.

Upon it, phantoms of birds and beasts coiled.

She formed a spirit art and gave a soft shout.

"Star-Chasing Demon Beast, emerge!"

In the next moment, a giant beast like a moth stepped out from the Myriad Beast Diagram.

Facing Moon Wanderer, the Star-Chasing Demon Beast released roars one after another.

"Wicked creature, the artifact spirit has already recognized me as master, yet you dare be so insolent!"

Though she scolded it this way, Moon Wanderer still squeezed out a drop of essence blood and fed it into the demon beast's mouth.

After obtaining the essence blood, this demon beast instantly became obedient.

"Devour all living auras in this place, seek the strongest among them, and track him!"

That moth cried out, then opened its mouth.

Amid the towering explosion behind it, streams of air gathered toward its mouth.

Seeing this scene, Moon Wanderer's face revealed excitement.

The more she sacrifice-refined this Myriad Beast Diagram, the more endless its wondrous uses became. The many demons, beasts, and strange creatures within it were either vicious or powerful. Even the weaker ones had unusual innate talents.

With this spirit treasure in hand, let alone tracking one Adept junior, even ranging across the Northern Sea would pose no difficulty.

Suddenly.

Crack!

A shattering sound rang from her waist.

Moon Wanderer picked up a broken jade pendant in puzzlement. "Something happened to the Myriad Immortals Assembly?"

Unless it was a critical moment, Purple Dowager would not dare crush this jade pendant.

Only when the Myriad Immortals Assembly faced life or death...

For a time, Moon Wanderer hesitated.

After a long while, she made her decision.

She prepared to first return to the Myriad Immortals Assembly. That was the opportunity for her rise, and also the sharp weapon with which she would destroy Penglai and seize overlordship of the Northern Sea!

As for Devil Lord Azure Yang's side...

"With this Star-Chasing Demon Beast, comparable to a late-stage Sage, Azure Yang brat cannot escape."

Murmuring once, she flicked her sleeve. A wisp of ghostly light appeared between her brows and poured into the Star-Chasing Demon Beast.

The Star-Chasing Demon Beast roared, then immediately chased into the endless sea.

*(End of Chapter)*
