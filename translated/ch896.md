# Chapter 896: The Hand Behind the Curtain, Valley Immortal Feng Jiang

On Nine Mushroom Mountain within the Nether Origin Sacred Land.

Chen Mozi, dressed like an old farmer, was bent over in his medicinal garden, carefully observing a lingzhi mushroom.

The bamboo hat on his head concealed his aura well. The high-grade lingzhi, on the verge of gaining sentience, not only did not reject him but instead stretched its leaves and swayed toward him.

Suddenly, a wave of scorching heat swept in from beyond the mountain.

Chen Mozi raised his head and glanced into the distance.

"Xinyisheng, since you're already here, why hide your presence?"

A figure slowly materialized, keeping a great distance and not approaching.

"Old friend, I was afraid of disturbing your young Running Horse Mushroom."

Chen Mozi's expression remained calm. The other party's fire-based techniques were blazing and violent; if he came any closer, it would indeed affect this immature Running Horse Mushroom.

He dripped two drops of green liquid onto the lingzhi, then straightened up, left the garden, and walked out of the mountain.

"Speak. What do you want from me?"

Xinyisheng withdrew his curious gaze from the lingzhi garden and then smiled. "What do you think of Luo Chen?"

Chen Mozi said indifferently, "No particular opinion. As long as he doesn't disturb me, we'll leave each other alone."

Xinyisheng sneered. "But if he truly takes over the Alchemy Saint Hall and then demands to use your mountain's lingzhi for pill refinement, what will you do then?"

Chen Mozi fell silent.

After a long moment of deliberation, he said in a low voice, "He'll have to pass the Alchemy Saint Hall trial first."

"So, isn't that why I came to ask your opinion of him? Do you think he has the ability to pass the trial?"

"A man of great fame has no idle reputation. Since he dares to call himself an Alchemy Grandmaster, he must have some skill. But as for passing the trial... he's too young."

"Youth doesn't mean incompetence. Take Chu Yu, for example—she rose from behind and her alchemy skills are no less than ours."

"Chu Yu is different. She's a member of the Chu family after all, blessed by the Alchemy Saint's legacy."

Xinyisheng frowned. "So you insist that Luo Chen cannot pass the trial? Isn't that a bit too arbitrary?"

Chen Mozi looked at Xinyisheng, who was determined to get an answer, and suddenly sighed.

This former junior had finally become his peer on the Dao, yet now his temperament was growing increasingly biased. He no longer obsessed over the alchemy path but instead sought external things.

No wonder he had no affinity with the Alchemy Saint. No wonder Xuan Yan refused to see him.

Amidst the sigh, Chen Mozi finally gave his reasoning.

"Youth indeed does not mean incompetence. But being young while also mastering multiple skills means being broad without depth, and deep without precision."

"What do you mean by that?" Xinyisheng asked curiously.

Chen Mozi spoke slowly. "A few days ago, we saw him controlling the Mysterious Turtle in the Nether Abyss, displaying considerable skill in beast taming. We also heard he has considerable attainments in talisman arts and formation dao. Especially over the past few decades, his combat ability has been almost unmatched—few among his peers can rival him."

"Such an existence seems born with knowledge, capable of anything."

"But do you truly believe he is capable of everything?"

Seeing the other party lost in thought, Chen Mozi did not wait for an answer and continued on his own.

"Clearly, he is not capable of everything."

"In beast taming, can he compare to the Myriad Beast Sect? Can he compare to the Lu family of Nether Origin?"

"His talisman arts and formation dao are limited to the third and fourth tiers."

"Even in combat ability, how could he surpass Lei Daozi, Azure Frost, and the like?"

"Youth is his greatest advantage, but also his greatest weakness. With a total cultivation time of just over two hundred years, having learned so many things, how good can his alchemy really be?"

"Even if he is a genius who can casually refine high-grade pills, you and I both know that the Alchemy Saint Hall trial emphasizes fundamentals above all else. And that is precisely what he lacks!"

Xinyisheng's mouth fell open as he looked at this old friend, as if seeing him for the first time.

He appeared indifferent to everything, yet he knew Luo Chen's situation like the back of his hand—he simply hadn't shown it before.

In comparison, Xinyisheng's own frantic running around seemed inferior.

But he had to acknowledge the other's words. Youth was Luo Chen's greatest advantage, but also his greatest weakness.

Suddenly.

Chen Mozi said faintly, "Moreover, I can see that his mind is not set on taking over the Alchemy Saint Hall at all. With only a few months until the trial begins, he not only ignores the convenience offered by the Nether Origin Ancestor but instead goes to Sealed Immortal Valley. This alone shows he is not worth worrying about."

"Sealed Immortal Valley?" Xinyisheng was greatly alarmed. "Isn't that the Nether Origin forbidden ground?"

Chen Mozi nodded calmly.

"If you pay a little attention to the commotion on Welcoming Immortal Peak, you would know he has been there for several days."

Xinyisheng was speechless.

Among the five alchemy grandmasters within the Nether Origin Sect, he alone had the weakest foundation and the shallowest connections.

Naturally, he was not as well-informed as Chen Mozi, who had long joined the Nether Origin Sect.

"Is there anything else?" Chen Mozi asked. "If not, please take your leave. I still have some medicinal herbs to tend to."

Faced with this dismissal, Xinyisheng hesitated.

After a moment of wavering, he still spoke. "Regardless of what you think of Luo Chen, or whether he succeeds, this early opening of the Alchemy Saint Hall trial is an opportunity for us as well. I've heard there are four trials in total. Apart from Xuan Yan, we are all stuck at the second trial. Why not take this chance to exchange our experiences from previous attempts?"

At the end, he deliberately added.

"If anyone succeeds, we will all have contributed. If I succeed, the position of Vice Hall Master will be yours alone!"

This was the true purpose of his visit!

Chen Mozi said flatly, "Please return!"

Xinyisheng hadn't expected such a direct refusal.

"Old friend, you..."

Chen Mozi waved his hand and had already drifted back to his mountain.

When the other could no longer see him, Chen Mozi, who had been calm all along, suddenly smiled.

Stuck at the second trial?

He was already confident of passing that trial. Why would he need to exchange experiences with others?

Outside the mountain, Xinyisheng's expression shifted between overcast and clear. In the end, he left Nine Mushroom Mountain.

He first went to Welcoming Immortal Peak. From the disciples there, he learned that Luo Chen had indeed taken his son to Sealed Immortal Valley and had not returned for many days.

He breathed a sigh of relief.

Then he hurried to the Lu family.

This early opening of the Alchemy Saint Hall trial had truly caught him off guard. Many preparations had yet to be made.

But he could wait no longer.

Since Chen Mozi was unwilling, he would seek like-minded people.

Surely Lu Chun, Chu Yu, and the others would not refuse his proposal.

...

There was a high mountain, dwelling in the nine heavens.

Its name was Yanfu. Originally deep in the nether earth, it had been transformed by the great power of a mighty being, allowing it to bathe in heavenly light.

On the mountain were three palaces: Heavenly Nether, Heavenly Abyss, and Heavenly Water, corresponding to the three great ancestors of the Nether Origin Sect—Yu Ming, Tian Yuan, and Hei Ze.

At this moment, within the Heavenly Nether Palace, three aged figures had sat motionless for over ten years.

Suddenly, Tian Yuan Ancestor laughed. "My disciple has come."

Outside the hall, Ye Lingtian received permission and stepped inside.

Seeing the three ancestors, he bowed.

"Disciple pays respects to the three ancestors!"

The other two did not speak. Tian Yuan's separated consciousness asked, "Is there something important?"

Ye Lingtian replied respectfully, "Nothing major. I only wish to report that Luo Chen has entered Nether Origin."

Yu Ming and Hei Ze slowly opened their eyes. The three exchanged glances, all feeling relieved.

For the past ten years, their full attention had been focused on refining Qing Hexian. They had no time to attend to internal sect affairs and truly did not know that Luo Chen had already been brought back.

Ye Lingtian continued, "But Luo Chen does not seem concerned about the Alchemy Saint inheritance. He doesn't even care to learn about the Alchemy Saint Hall trial in advance. Instead, he is focused entirely on resolving the gu insect calamity on his son, Luo Lingxi. He has already gone to Sealed Immortal Valley to see Junior Brother Feng."

Hearing this, Yu Ming Ancestor frowned.

"Gu insect calamity?"

Tian Yuan Ancestor laughed. "You might not know this. Before my incarnation fell last time, I went to Motian Cliff and saw Luo Chen's son. I discovered he had a very powerful Devil Heart Gu on him, so I casually told Xiao Ye about Feng Jiang's situation."

Ye Lingtian nodded. "Thanks to the ancestor informing me of Junior Brother Feng's situation, I was able to spread the news and successfully get Luo Chen to bring his own son to our Nether Origin Sect. This way, his bond with our sect can be deepened."

Yu Ming Ancestor suddenly understood and approved of the approach.

But he was somewhat curious. "Is that gu insect truly so powerful that even your incarnation, Junior Brother Tian Yuan, could not casually remove it?"

Tian Yuan Ancestor chuckled. "I could naturally remove it. But after removing the gu insect, I would probably have to remove Luo Chen's son as well. We can't have him hating us before he even arrives, can we?"

As he said this, he glanced at the silent Hei Ze.

Some of the other's previous actions had been rather improper.

Yu Ming Ancestor seemed to ponder something and murmured, "It seems the Devil Heart Gu is too deeply bound to Luo Chen's son. Within the sect, only Feng Jiang likely has a way to resolve it."

Ye Lingtian, standing nearby, muttered to himself. Did Junior Brother Feng truly have such great ability?

Even the ancestor couldn't solve it, yet he had a way?

But recalling Junior Brother Feng Jiang's experiences, he inexplicably gained some confidence.

His master Tian Yuan Ancestor's voice suddenly came from beside him.

"The matter of Luo Chen's son is not important. What matters is the inheritance of the Alchemy Saint Hall."

"Before we three went into seclusion, we moved the fifth-rank spirit vein of Yanfu Mountain and channeled enough spiritual energy to open the Alchemy Saint Hall trial early. Time is tight. If we miss this opportunity, we may have to wait another hundred years."

"So you need to urge Luo Chen along."

Ye Lingtian nodded in agreement and then respectfully withdrew.

After he left, Tian Yuan Ancestor said bluntly, "The man is now in our Nether Origin Sect, but his heart may not be reined in. If he successfully passes Chu Yan's trial, I suggest that Junior Brother Hei Ze withdraw his divine ability, lest there be a thorn in his heart."

"Hmph!"

A cold snort. Hei Ze did not think he had done wrong.

"That junior clearly has a wild heart. Without a divine ability to restrain him, do you truly think he would willingly come to Nether Origin Sect?"

"But..." Tian Yuan Ancestor frowned.

Hei Ze continued, "Moreover, this child has unclear ties with Green Parasol Mountain, as your incarnation witnessed firsthand. With my divine ability restraining him, he will not dare to harbor second thoughts."

Mentioning the matter of Green Parasol Mountain, Yu Ming Ancestor also acknowledged his junior brother's reasoning.

Tian Yuan Ancestor sighed helplessly. "Let's discuss the specifics after we deal with Qing Hexian and talk to Luo Chen. But I think some appeasement is necessary. Otherwise, if he truly has deep ties with Green Parasol Mountain, we wouldn't dare consume the pills he refines in the future."

Yu Ming Ancestor suddenly asked, "Is that person on Green Parasol Mountain truly that formidable?"

This time, Tian Yuan Ancestor fell silent.

His incarnation had self-destructed on the spot, and the distance was too great to transmit specific information back.

Thus, he had no deep understanding of just how powerful the master of Green Parasol Mountain was.

But for his incarnation to be so desperate as to self-destruct, she must be extraordinarily strong.

In the silence, Hei Ze said faintly, "The matter of the Primordial Demon Sect in the Northern Sea is full of doubts. I absolutely refuse to believe that only Sea Emperor Whale and Great Sage Beili could have destroyed Fellow Daoists Refining Soul and Primordial Demon. There must have been an outsider interfering!"

In the current Mountains and Seas Realm, who could be the "outsider" capable of destroying an entire sect?

The fallen Di Tian could not. The captured Qing Hexian did not have the ability.

The one in the Southern Frontier was trapped at the Life and Death Gate, and the other had directly lost control.

As for the Western Desert, due to the Buddhist sect, they did not understand it deeply, but those bald donkeys should not do such things.

Previously, they had suspected the Tianyuan Dao Sect of being the hand behind the curtain. Judging from the Tianyuan Dao Sect's subsequent deployment of troops to the Northern Sea, they had indeed become the beneficiary.

But in the middle of this process, one point had to be mentioned.

When Hei Ze Ancestor attacked Green Parasol Mountain, its master was not there!

And the timing of her return vaguely coincided with the unsealing of the Arctic Yemo Heaven.

Tian Yuan Ancestor sighed. "Forget it. It's best not to provoke Green Parasol Mountain in the future."

Yu Ming Ancestor shared the same thought. "We'll keep to ourselves. If we don't provoke her, she likely won't dare to provoke us."

Among the three great ancient demons of the Eastern Wilderness, one was dead and one was wounded. Green Parasol Mountain stood alone and was no longer a concern.

The Nether Origin Sect was now truly secure!

...

Within the Nether Origin Sacred Land, there were immortal wonders as well as many perilous forbidden grounds.

The Nether Abyss was one such place, and Sealed Immortal Valley was another.

This forbidden ground was not naturally formed but had gradually appeared over the past five hundred years.

Within it, the earth was cracked open, and magma rolled.

Occasionally, resentful souls wailed and howled, and Yellow Spring water gurgled.

Amidst constant tremors, immortal spiritual energy could not persist for long.

It was as if this place was perpetually on the verge of collapse.

And the reason for all this was attributed to the "Valley Immortal" sealed within Sealed Immortal Valley.

In Sealed Immortal Valley, there was a Valley Immortal. His surname was Feng, his given name Jiang.

Luo Chen had heard the City Lord of Lingtian call him Junior Brother. Even with Alchemy Saint Chu Yan and Lei Daozi before him, he had subconsciously assumed the other's cultivation level might not be too high.

But he had not expected that upon their first meeting, the other's mana fluctuations, even greater than those of Lingtian City Lord, would be so terrifying.

Unconcealed, unrestrained, they nakedly enveloped the entire Sealed Immortal Valley!

Such roiling mana fluctuations were unbearable for ordinary cultivators. Coupled with the chaotic conditions here, it was no wonder this place had become a forbidden ground within the Nether Origin Sect.

Helpless, Luo Chen had to first let Luo Lingxi fall into a deep sleep, then use his mana to shield him, lest Luo Lingxi be affected by the shockwaves.

But after several days of contact, he vaguely sensed that the other's unrestrained release of mana fluctuations was not due to overwhelming strength, but out of necessity.

"Valley Immortal Feng Jiang... it seems he is suppressing something?"

Luo Chen thought to himself.

Just then, Feng Jiang, clad in gray robes, looked at Luo Chen with a ferocious expression.

"To resolve the Devil Heart Gu on this little fellow is extremely simple!"

Simple?

Luo Chen was caught off guard.
