# Chapter 884: The Desired Object, Meeting Nether Spring Again

*Whoosh! Whoosh!*

Two streaks of light flashed and vanished, revealing two figures atop the formation.

One man and one woman—it was Luo Chen and Azure Frost.

Luo Chen instinctively looked down. The glow of the teleportation formation was gradually fading, and those vaguely familiar patterns were also sinking into concealment.

The style of this teleportation formation seemed to be of the same lineage as the super-large one beneath Truth Crossing Hall.

In other words, he had already arrived at Green Parasol Mountain!

Light footsteps sounded as Azure Frost stepped down from the formation platform.

Turning back to look, Azure Frost once again affirmed Luo Chen's level of body refinement.

Back when Luo Chen had fled, he had used her Azure Luan feather to withstand the spatial pressure of ultra-long-distance teleportation.

But this time, even without her protection, and without even being equipped with a teleportation token, Luo Chen showed no discomfort. He hadn't even mobilized any mana, relying purely on his physical body to endure.

Such performance—aside from her and her sister—there was no third person on Green Parasol Mountain capable of it.

Nether Spring had valued him back then; it seemed she truly had some foresight.

"Let's go. First, head to Truth Crossing Hall."

Luo Chen abandoned his study of the teleportation formation and followed her out.

"Doesn't the Primordial Lady want to see me?"

The woman's calm reply echoed off the thick walls of the upward passage.

"She does want to see you, but when she sees you depends on her mood."

"Mm? What does that mean? You force me here, yet won't see me?"

"Forcing? It was merely convenient. My trip to the Medicine King Domain was for the Accumulated Void Flower. Unfortunately, the flower wasn't mature, but I saw you, so I invited you along."

"Quite the 'invitation'..."

There was a hint of laughter in the man's voice, carrying three parts of self-mockery.

The woman seemed oblivious, as if she didn't care at all about the sarcasm beneath those three parts of self-mockery. For her, bringing someone back to Green Parasol Mountain usually meant just sweeping them up in her sleeve. Luo Chen had traveled alongside her today, fully conscious, so it truly could be considered an "invitation."

"I don't understand. Without that chance encounter, how would the Primordial Lady have summoned me? Perhaps you don't know, but according to my plans, I was going to cultivate at the Nether Origin Sect soon."

"Nether Origin?"

Azure Frost repeated the name, her tone pausing for the first time.

But only for a moment, then she said matter-of-factly: "In this realm, if my sister wants to see you, never mind the Nether Abyss—even the Heavenly Abyss couldn't stop her."

Luo Chen was speechless.

He could naturally hear the deep-rooted confidence Azure Frost had in Rosycloud Primordial Lady, coming from her very bones.

But going by rumors, Rosycloud had only risen in the past thousand years, a relatively short time even among the top-tier demon clan experts of the Eastern Wilderness.

At such an age, how great could her power be?

How could she treat a sacred land with three Ascendant powerhouses sitting in it as if it were an empty place?

But if Luo Chen extrapolated from the intelligence he'd obtained in the Demon-Fall Land, and if this Rosycloud was that Rosycloud, then everything seemed to make sense.

Wait?

Demon-Fall Land!

Luo Chen's heart tightened. He vaguely guessed what Rosycloud Primordial Lady wanted.

"You go wait at Truth Crossing Hall for a few days. This Daytime Void Hall doesn't keep guests. You should be familiar with that place."

"Indeed, I am familiar. Heh."

After exiting the underground teleportation formation, Luo Chen stepped into the main body of Daytime Void Hall.

The vast palace was cold and desolate, so silent that even the footsteps of the two were clearly audible.

When they reached the main entrance, Azure Frost flicked her sleeve.

*Creak!*

The bronze gate slowly opened.

Outside the hall, someone was already waiting.

"Hall Master!" A voluptuous, beautiful woman bowed gracefully.

"Mm."

Azure Frost acknowledged. After Luo Chen went out, she waved her sleeve, and the Daytime Void Hall's gate slammed shut.

It looked as if she were shooing away a guest.

"Don't mind her. She's always been like this."

Luo Chen turned around, looking at the familiar woman, and nodded.

Pure Crane carefully examined Luo Chen.

His face was like polished jade, his bearing calm and composed. Even in a dangerous situation, he showed no panic.

Though his complexion was slightly pale from excessive mana depletion, the demeanor of a grandmaster of his generation, standing firm like a mountain, was inadvertently revealed.

Pure Crane's eyes lit up.

"Luo Chen! A hundred years apart, and I never thought you'd become so formidable!"

Luo Chen twitched the corner of his mouth. "Fellow Daoist, haven't you also made great progress, crossing the early Sage stage?"

Pure Crane smoothed her hair and smiled. "Green Parasol Mountain is the sacred land of the Feather Clan, the best place for us feathered types to cultivate. Such progress is only natural. As for you..."

She tactfully stopped there.

Because as they chatted, they had already reached the edge of the white jade plaza before Daytime Void Hall.

Standing on the cliffside without a path, the man gazed at the misty, cloud-wreathed mountain scenery where birds circled, and a look of emotion crossed his face.

He had returned again.

He had felt a similar emotion when he returned to the Eastern Wilderness.

But that time, he had returned with the pride of advancing to Sage, the prestige of defeating the Tianyuan Dao Sect's prodigy, and the confidence to save the Falling Cloud Sect from collapse—like returning home in silken robes.

This time, however, the reality was bitterly cold and stark.

Azure Frost had said "invited," which was relatively polite and courteous.

But Luo Chen understood that if he had refused that "invitation," the consequence would have been a collapsed Sage core and a severe drop in cultivation.

Looking at the familiar scenery now, Luo Chen's heart was filled with complex emotions.

"Let's go!"

"Alright. This way. Hall Master Azure Frost has arranged a cave dwelling for you. The spiritual energy there is extremely abundant—it's the best spot in Truth Crossing Hall aside from the Hall Master's own cave. Even compared to Vice Hall Master Nether Spring's cave, it's not inferior."

Pure Crane was very enthusiastic, explaining everything in great detail.

Luo Chen was somewhat surprised. Had Azure Frost secretly arranged such a good cave for him?

Was it because she had seen his poor state of depleted mana?

When Luo Chen arrived at that cave dwelling in Truth Crossing Hall, Pure Crane began introducing all the furnishings and facilities.

Luo Chen, however, grew a bit impatient.

"Fellow Daoist Pure Crane, that's enough trouble."

Pure Crane was startled, then smiled. "No trouble at all, no trouble at all. It's all my duty. Vice Hall Master Nether Spring is currently in seclusion. That fellow Thunder Prison only recently had his body reconstructed, so I'm temporarily managing Truth Crossing Hall. If you need anything, just send me a message."

As she spoke, she beckoned, and a small, light-yellow-feathered bird flew over.

The bird was very intelligent and landed on Luo Chen's shoulder.

Clearly, this was Green Parasol Mountain's method of communication.

Luo Chen nodded, then closed the cave dwelling's gate.

Outside the cave, the mist gradually gathered, and Pure Crane looked somewhat reluctant to leave.

"Who would have thought that the little brat of yesteryear would grow like this, his appearance even becoming more pleasing to the eye."

"What an excellent furnace vessel!"

"No, at this level, at this age, he's the best candidate for a Dao companion. A thousand times better than that deadbeat Cloud Crane..."

...

Luo Chen didn't know why Pure Crane was so enthusiastic. Back when she had been brought back from the Joyous Union Sect by Nether Spring, she had been quite depressed.

He had no time to dwell on it now. He immediately set up an isolation formation, then took out two items.

One was a flame, the other a jade box.

The flame was cyan-white, with a faint gold within.

The jade box was covered in complex restrictions, so intricate that even with Luo Chen's current skill, he couldn't begin to approach them.

So...

"Is it this?"

"Or this?"

Luo Chen's gaze constantly shifted between the two, uncertainty showing in his eyes.

If this Rosycloud and that Rosycloud mentioned by the Heaven-Refining Cauldron's artifact spirit in the Demon-Fall Land were not just sharing a name but had a profound connection, or were even the same person, then what she sought must be one of these two.

No!

Perhaps there was another item?

Suddenly, Luo Chen remembered another thing he had obtained in the Demon-Fall Land.

He opened his mouth and spat out a small gray cauldron, which floated out slowly.

The cauldron's body was cracked, and it tugged at Luo Chen's heart, as if it too were about to split.

He still remembered that at the time, he had also absorbed a gray light. It was that gray light merging with the Primordial Unity Cauldron that had caused this treasure to undergo a transformation.

Not only had it smoothly advanced to a true artifact, but it had also greatly improved the quality of alchemy and artifact refining, and could even absorb heavenly tribulation lightning.

Thinking of this, Luo Chen examined it closely.

He hadn't looked before, but now that he did, he made an unexpected discovery.

In the previous great battle, the Primordial Unity Cauldron had first taken a direct blow from three Great Demon Emperors, and later, when it released the elk thunder beast to gather the heavenly tribulation, it had been struck again by Huang Man and Bi Zheng.

It had been severely damaged, with cracks even appearing in its extremely hard body.

Yet unexpectedly, in such a short time, those cracks had already shrunk somewhat on their own.

This recovery ability was terrifying!

Compared to it, the Mystic Dust Armor was worlds apart!

Although most magic treasures were refined with some self-repairing materials intentionally mixed in, those recovery abilities usually required the cultivator to nurture them, or even supplement materials to help with repairs before they could be activated.

The materials used for the Primordial Unity Cauldron were excellent, but no matter how good, they shouldn't be this good.

Could this also be an effect of that gray light?

Just as Luo Chen was thinking, a pained voice sounded in his ears.

Luo Chen frowned, put away the three items, and shook his left arm. Black King rolled out.

He tumbled a few times on the ground before struggling to lift his head.

"Are you alright?" Luo Chen asked with concern, taking out some healing pills.

Black King eagerly swallowed them. Though the medicine hadn't taken effect yet, a look of comfort already appeared on his face.

"Fine, fine! Just minor injuries!"

Luo Chen smiled and shook his head. "This time, you worked hard."

Indeed, it had been hard.

In the great battle, aside from Nine Spirits Primordial Lord, Black King had been the only helper Luo Chen could rely on.

For that, he had been severely wounded multiple times, taking several blows from Huang Man's staff, and had even been torn in half by Azure Frost's hands.

If not for the "Undying Body" talent he had gained when advancing to a desolate beast, any ordinary demon beast would have died a thousand times over.

"Being able to risk life and death for my master is this little black's blessing!"

Black King declared with great righteousness, as if even if there were a mountain of blades and a sea of fire ahead, he would lay down his body for Luo Chen to walk over.

However...

Black King's neck shrank, and he looked outside with some fear.

"Master, where are we now?"

"Green Parasol Mountain. Didn't you hear earlier?"

"I heard, but I didn't think it would be this place!"

"What's wrong?" Luo Chen asked, puzzled.

Black King's voice was tinged with fear. "This place... it makes this little black very uncomfortable."

Uncomfortable?

Luo Chen was startled, then suddenly understood.

Black King's original form was a Black-Scale Giant Python, and his natural enemies were powerful flying birds.

Like Heavenjade, who had been at odds with Black King in the early years.

But where was this now?

Green Parasol Mountain, the sacred land of the Feather Clan!

How many powerful flying birds were here, not just one or two!

Even if they didn't deliberately release their auras, accumulated over time, what "crawling creature" wouldn't be terrified?

"No wonder the Whitebone Mystic Serpent cowered before Azure Frost, driving a boat and guarding the gate, so humble that he didn't seem like a generation of Demon Emperor."

Luo Chen shook his head and instructed, "For now, stay put in the cave dwelling. Don't run around. If some bird snatches you away, your master won't have anywhere to plead your case."

Black King repeatedly agreed.

He slithered around the spacious cave dwelling and soon let out an exclamation.

"Master, the spiritual energy here is so rich!"

Luo Chen had anticipated this reaction.

Pure Crane hadn't exaggerated.

The spiritual energy in this cave dwelling was truly abundant, even terrifyingly so!

Luo Chen had seen countless spiritual lands in his life, and almost none could compare to this place.

Even among fourth-rank spiritual lands, this was absolutely the cream of the crop!

Black King even said something like, "If I sleep here for a hundred years, I will surely have it upgraded to a fifth-rank spiritual vein!"

In fact, he didn't even need to help.

Because Green Parasol Mountain itself had a fifth-rank spiritual vein!

Luo Chen walked to the cultivation chamber, taking deep breaths. Every breath seemed to draw in a massive amount of pure spiritual energy.

And this was without him even actively circulating his cultivation technique.

"According to Pure Crane, this cave dwelling is of the same standard as Vice Hall Master Nether Spring's cave in Truth Crossing Hall. It's hard to imagine what the actual Truth Crossing Hall Master's cave must be like."

"Fifth-rank?"

Luo Chen chuckled wryly. At a time like this, he was still thinking about such trivial matters.

Was it because Green Parasol Mountain was treating him too well, or because Azure Frost's words "won't harm him" made him feel emboldened, or because a barefoot man doesn't fear those who wear shoes?

"I'm not barefoot anymore."

Luo Chen murmured to himself as he stepped into the cultivation chamber.

The top priority was still to recuperate from his injuries and restore his condition.

He lit a stick of incense, swallowed a few pills, and as his technique began to circulate, a sea of heaven and earth's spiritual energy poured into him.

...

"Get out here!"

"Back then, you had the nerve to ambush me with others, but now you don't have the nerve to show your face?"

"Luo Chen, get out here!"

A coarse voice, like thunder exploding, rang out continuously.

Pure Crane hurried over with an unhappy expression, speaking in a low voice to the sturdy man.

"Thunder Prison, what are you yelling for? You're disturbing Luo Chen's rest. How will you answer to the Hall Master?"

Thunder Prison Divine Roc suddenly turned his head, glaring viciously at Pure Crane.

"Are you defending that guy? Have you taken a liking to him again?"

Being seen through, Pure Crane was both embarrassed and angry. "What nonsense are you spouting?"

Thunder Prison sneered. "Don't bring that human Joyous Union Sect's ways into Green Parasol Mountain. If not for Daytime Void Hall Master's bitter cultivation and Vice Hall Master Nether Spring not showing her face these years, with your behavior, you'd have long had your feathers plucked, turned into a bald crane, and hung outside Green Parasol Mountain as a warning."

"Bastard!"

Pure Crane was furious and directly swung a claw at him.

Thunder Prison Divine Roc was unafraid and threw a punch in return.

*Boom!*

Under the collision of mana, Thunder Prison Divine Roc staggered back several steps, his face turning pale and then green.

Seeing this, Pure Crane stopped being angry and laughed coquettishly. "Your body hasn't recovered yet, so go catch some bugs in the mountains instead of making a fool of yourself here. You can't even beat me, yet you dare to provoke Luo Chen?"

Thunder Prison was furious. He had long stopped eating bugs!

And!

"Bullying me because my body is newly reconstructed?"

"Wait until I recover to my peak, I'll pluck your feathers first, then kill Luo Chen, and..."

Pure Crane waved her hand dismissively. "Don't bother. Never mind your current state, even if you recover to your peak, what then? Lest you don't know, your friend Seven Rhinoceros was defeated by Luo Chen, Bi Zheng fled in panic, and even Huang Man Hundred Legs died at Luo Chen's hands. If you talk big now, when you see the real person, even your black face will be flushed red with shame."

Thunder Prison was stunned.

He had been busy these years reconstructing the body destroyed by Nine Spirits Primordial Lord and had no time to pay attention to outside affairs.

Pure Crane's words hit him like a thunderbolt, catching him off guard.

"Really?"

"Hah, could it be false? Are you underestimating the intelligence capabilities of Green Parasol Mountain's Feather Clan?"

Thunder Prison subconsciously moved closer to Pure Crane, not to get intimate, but to instinctively distance himself from Luo Chen's cave.

He wouldn't doubt the intelligence capabilities of Green Parasol Mountain's Feather Clan.

But could that mere child Luo Chen really have achieved such feats?

Just then, the flock of birds watching the show in the sky suddenly scattered.

A black shadow swooped down, passing the two of them, and arrived outside Luo Chen's cave.

Her figure was graceful, with curves in all the right places. Just looking at her back, one could tell she was a peerless beauty.

But her true face was hidden beneath a bamboo hat and light veil.

Yet the moment they saw the newcomer, the quarreling Thunder Prison and Pure Crane both fell silent.

"Scram!"

The two trembled, not daring to disobey the woman's words, and hastily retreated.

Not only that, but the birds circling and watching from outside were also driven away by this single shout, not daring to approach any further.

When everything had quieted down, the woman's eyes glared venomously at the cave dwelling covered by mist.

As if sensing something, a low voice came from within.

"Fellow Daoist Nether Spring, please come inside for a chat."

Nether Spring gritted her teeth and let out a sharp voice.

"Fine!"
