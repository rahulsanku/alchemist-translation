# Chapter 992: The Demoness and the Moluo Battle the World

Ripples of void energy undulated as a graceful silhouette drifted down like a falling feather.

With a light tap of her toes, Azure Frost stood firmly upon Luo Chen's broad shoulder.

Her peripheral vision swept downward, and her brow furrowed slightly.

"So ugly!"

All three faces of Luo Chen simultaneously registered stunned bewilderment.

He had never expected that Azure Frost's first words upon emerging from seclusion would be these.

But with powerful enemies surrounding them on all sides, he had no time to critique this woman's aesthetic sensibilities. A deep, rumbling voice emerged from his throat.

"If you had come out any later, forget about this ugly face—you might not even have found my corpse."

"Is it really that difficult?" Azure Frost said dismissively.

Luo Chen sneered. "This ancient grand formation was specifically arranged for Ascendant-level powerhouses. Why don't you try it yourself?"

Azure Frost's gaze swept indifferently over the many monks surrounding them. She first nodded in acknowledgment, but then the corner of her mouth twitched.

"Now that I've recovered to my peak state, even if this formation were to reappear, it wouldn't be able to do much to me."

Luo Chen fell silent.

He wanted to argue, but thinking of Azure Frost's methods, he realized she was actually right.

Most formations in this world required space to be arranged, and the more participants a large formation involved, the more stringent the spatial environment requirements became. For Azure Frost, who was well-versed in the dao of space, such formations held no sway over her when she was at full strength.

With this thought, Luo Chen's confidence in this battle grew even stronger.

"I've expended quite a bit earlier. I'll trouble you to put in more effort from now on."

Azure Frost glanced at Luo Chen, the corner of her mouth lifting as she let out a light "heh" of laughter.

In the next instant, a vast demonic aura surged up from her body, shrouding the surroundings in all directions.

Luo Chen did the same. A Sage domain centered on him began to spread wildly. Everyone caught within it felt their spiritual power obstructed and their bodies weighed down immensely.

And after Azure Frost's appearance, the enemies had already changed color!

Just dealing with the three-headed Moluo alone had been difficult enough. Now that the demoness—reputed to be the number one under the Ascendant level—had appeared, the situation had completely deteriorated.

The way the two of them conversed as if no one else was present was proof enough of their confidence.

Now that they had joined forces, their momentum was rising step by step. How could this battle continue?

The Abbot of the Prajna Hall and the Abbot of the Dharma Hall exchanged a glance, but the determination in their eyes did not waver in the slightest.

"Form the Vajra Demon-Subduing Formation!"

With a roar, the surrounding monks immediately shifted positions.

Grouping in threes, they drew circles on the ground, completely surrounding Luo Chen and Azure Frost.

In response, Luo Chen merely let out a contemptuous laugh.

This so-called Vajra Demon-Subduing Formation was merely a fourth-rank formation that gathered the power of three people. It was far inferior to the Arhat Grand Formation, which could condense the strength of thirty-six people into one.

If the Thirty-Six Arhat Grand Formation could not handle him, how could this inferior Vajra Demon-Subduing Formation?

He took a large stride forward, gripping the Yuantu Blood Sword—which was like a mountain ridge—and slashed downward.

Azure Frost lightly stepped forward, vanishing into the void. When she reappeared, she was already above the white-faced monk.

Capture the chieftain first to capture all!

In this battle, it was clear that this Abbot of the Prajna Hall was the leader of the Hanging Temple forces.

As long as he was killed, things would become much simpler.

Facing Azure Frost's attack, the white-faced monk flew into a rage. Wrapping his prayer beads around his hand, he punched out.

Vajra Prajna!

As their fists clashed, the white-faced monk was sent flying back a thousand zhang, while Azure Frost remained motionless.

After a brief pause in place, Azure Frost's expression unchanged, she continued pursuing her enemy.

Behind them, Luo Chen was advancing unstoppably, sweeping all before him.

Blood blossoms bloomed like fireworks, constantly bursting forth!

Lives vanished like ephemeral night-blooming cereus, gone in an instant!

Even with the seven hall abbots attacking simultaneously, they could not suppress Luo Chen's rampant arrogance.

Even when Buddhist treasures and true artifacts struck his thousand-zhang true body, Luo Chen paid them no heed. As origin power circulated, the injuries to his physical body healed in an instant.

Instead, every swing of his sword and every smash of his staff brought unimaginable damage.

The Life-Seizing Golden Feathers constantly left his body to pierce, each time gouging out massive bloody holes.

For a time, demonic might rampaged across the field, ethereal shadows drifted, and the situation showed a one-sided trend.

The onlookers led by the Heavenly Brahma Son saw this scene, their faces all ashen, their eyes filled with shock and fear.

Compared to the other four continents, the Western Desert could be considered the most resource-poor continent.

Therefore, the number of high-level powerhouses was also the smallest.

Among the human race, those at the Sage level and above numbered less than a hundred in total, far fewer than the other four continents.

And the thirty-six eminent monks that the Hanging Temple had mobilized this time could almost be considered half of the Western Desert's high-end combat power.

No!

Not almost—it had already exceeded half.

The eight hall abbots were each existences at the Great Cultivator level.

These eight alone were enough to match ten, twenty, or even thirty or forty ordinary Sage cultivators.

The Hanging Temple monks led by them could be said to be the strongest combat power in the current Western Desert. Unless an Ascendant appeared, no one could handle them.

But even so, they were being suppressed by the demoness and the Moluo. This was truly too hard to accept.

"We... should we help?"

A difficult voice sounded beside the Heavenly Brahma Son. It was Xiao Yan, the ruler of the Great Liang Kingdom.

The Heavenly Brahma Son asked back, "Help whom?"

Xiao Yan answered without hesitation: "Naturally, the Buddhist friends of the Hanging Temple!"

The Heavenly Brahma Son asked again, "Why?"

Xiao Yan was puzzled. "They are eminent monks of the Buddhist faith, representatives of our Western Desert's holy land. If we don't help them, are we supposed to help the demoness and the Moluo from outside continents?"

The Heavenly Brahma Son let out a "ha" and delivered a soul-searching question: "The Glazed Pure Land is also part of the Hanging Temple. Dharma Master Xuan Du was once the Abbot of the Medicine King Hall. The Demon Emperor Azure Frost and the Alchemy Sect's Luo Chen are helping Dharma Master Xuan Du. This battle is clearly an internal conflict within the Buddhist holy land. How do you intend to intervene?"

Xiao Yan opened his mouth but had no words to reply.

"Moreover..."

The Heavenly Brahma Son's gaze fell upon the moonlight-glazed hall, his eyes filled with envy.

"Dharma Master Xuan Du is about to step into the Arhat realm. The Hanging Temple is about to welcome its second true Buddha!"

At this moment, among the onlookers, those wavering were not just Xiao Yan and his group.

The batch that had entered later were also the same.

But after careful consideration, no one still stepped forward to help.

Most cultivators in the Western Desert indeed believed in Buddhism, but both sides involved in this battle were true inheritors of the Buddhist faith. They could not help either side, so they could only watch helplessly as the two sides decided the victor.

And this so-called victor, after Azure Frost joined the battle, had already begun to tilt rapidly.

Accompanied by a despairing scream, everyone followed the sound and saw a shocking sight.

In the bamboo sea, the white-faced monk sat slumped on the ground, his prayer beads scattered messily around him, while a slender hand hovered above his head.

His eyes looked despairingly at the indifferent woman before him. He opened his mouth, but before he could say anything—

Azure Frost flipped her wrist, and an incomparably sharp surge of spiritual power erupted from her palm.

It struck the crown of his head, penetrated his divine court, and shattered both soul and spirit!

Azure Frost turned her head, looking at the thousand-zhang giant in the distance.

As if sensing each other's unspoken understanding, under her gaze, the thousand-zhang giant suddenly changed his attack methods and rhythm.

He put away the Heaven-Confounding Staff and the Life-Seizing Golden Feathers, holding the Yuantu Blood Sword with one hand.

Opening his mouth, he spat out five streams of light.

Then, all five hands simultaneously began forming sword seals.

The Grand Five Elements Sword Formation, rise!

Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, Earth—five elements, five swords. Radiant light flowed, and thousands upon thousands of sword qi poured down like the Heavenly River inverting, enveloping dozens of people on the field.

Sword threads spun, sword qi surged. Wherever they passed, blood rain flew and screams followed one after another.

In the distance, Azure Frost watched this scene, her expression solemn.

Compared to the last time, the sword formation Luo Chen had just displayed was several times more powerful.

Was it because he had more hands? Or because his divine soul was more condensed? Or perhaps because his spiritual power was purer?

Azure Frost could not know.

She only knew that although she had made up for her shortcomings in the divine soul aspect, she had always been protected by the Nether Soul Pearl, so her overall strength had not actually increased much.

But Luo Chen's improvement was visible in every aspect.

"I wonder, after I refine the Accumulated Void Flower, will I be able to surpass him?"

For some reason, Azure Frost suddenly developed a desire to compare herself with Luo Chen.

But before she could observe much, Azure Frost's heart suddenly tightened.

Without hesitation, she stepped into the void.

But her direction was not toward the battle group where Luo Chen was, but toward the moonlight-glazed hall!

...

The Vajra Demon-Subduing Formation had never been able to handle Luo Chen. When the Grand Five Elements Sword Formation appeared, it became a one-sided massacre.

In his ears were the screams of fellow disciples falling.

The Abbot of the Dharma Hall, Huineng, showed a compassionate expression. His gaze pierced through the sword formation, landing on the moonlight-glazed hall that was so close yet unreachable.

He pressed his palms together and chanted a Buddhist title.

"Six junior brothers, lend me your strength!"

The other six hall abbots seemed to understand his intention. Immediately, they formed circles of three, protecting him in layers within.

Seeing this, Luo Chen realized what the other party intended to do.

He raised the Yuantu Blood Sword high and slashed down.

Hum! Hum!

Two rings of light appeared, blocking the path.

The Yuantu Sword could not advance.

"Hmph!"

Luo Chen snorted coldly. The power of the sword formation around him converged.

Five colors flowed on the blood sword.

His giant hand added force.

"Break!"

With a forceful press, one layer of golden light ring collapsed with a crash.

The three outermost hall abbots were struck as if by a heavy blow, all falling weakly to the ground, their faces as pale as gold paper.

The residual power of the Yuantu Sword did not cease, slashing toward the inner defensive golden ring.

Seeing this, the remaining monks also launched attacks, intending to force him to defend what he must.

Luo Chen paid them no heed, relying solely on his physical body to withstand them. He was determined to stop what the Abbot of the Dharma Hall was doing.

However!

After enduring dozens of attacks and forcibly breaking through the second ring of light, that sword strike missed.

"No tree, no platform; unstained by dust!"

The Abbot of the Dharma Hall calmly watched the massive blood sword brush past his shoulder. His entire body floated up, unaffected by the suppression of the Grand Five Elements Sword Formation, and escaped outside the formation.

The moment he left the formation, he struck a palm strike from afar toward the moonlight-glazed hall.

With one palm strike, his arm broke off at the middle.

A golden arm, containing terrifying Buddhist power and shining with golden light, pressed down toward the moonlight-glazed hall.

Behind him, Luo Chen's sword slashed over.

In front of him, Azure Frost stepped out of the void, without hesitation revealing her true form—a thousand-zhang divine bird.

Swish!

The sword light struck his body. Without any protection, the Abbot of the Dharma Hall's physical body instantly turned into a mist of blood.

The golden hand broke through the air, directly piercing through Azure Frost's body, intending to press down on the white-robed monk in the hall.

That was definitely not the arm of the Abbot of the Dharma Hall!

Ignoring her injuries, Azure Frost let out a sharp cry. Her wings flapped, stirring the void.

That golden hand, containing terrifying Buddhist power, suddenly seemed to sink into a quagmire.

Behind, the thousand-zhang giant took large strides, crossing space, and reached out with all six arms to grab the golden hand.

"Come, back!"

With force added, Luo Chen forcibly pulled the giant hand backward.

Terrifying Buddhist power frantically scoured Luo Chen, draining away all his strength, causing his thousand-zhang true body to rapidly shrink.

Luo Chen's face twisted, his pupils contracting sharply.

"Whose hand is this, exactly?"

Azure Frost's true form changed, transforming into human shape. She came to Luo Chen's side, reaching out to grasp the golden hand together with him.

The two joined forces, forcibly pulling the golden arm backward.

"Not enough! Still not enough!"

Luo Chen gritted his teeth. Within his body, the Nirvana Sacred Flame transformed into a small golden bird, suddenly flying out and beginning to burn around the hand.

At this point, he could no longer care about accidentally injuring Azure Frost.

For a time, both Luo Chen and Azure Frost, along with the golden hand, were enveloped in the Nirvana Sacred Flame.

This was Luo Chen's final trump card now.

The Withering-Bloom Divine Fire had protected him all the way from the Yellow Spring Sea, leaving little power remaining. Even after absorbing the flesh and blood of the Refined Silver Peng, it could only help him hold on for a short while under the Arhat Grand Formation.

And the relatively weaker Nirvana Sacred Flame had become his last resort.

When Azure Frost saw the Nirvana Sacred Flame, her solemn expression momentarily lost focus.

Luo Chen did not notice. He only felt it was still not enough. Opening his mouth, he spat out a mouthful of essence blood onto the Nirvana Sacred Flame.

The firelight immediately blazed intensely!

And that terrifying Buddhist power, under the burning of this sacred flame, rapidly dissipated.

Seeing this, Luo Chen could not help but feel joy.

But just as the Buddhist power was about to disperse, a surge of pitch-black demonic qi, as if suppressed for a long time, suddenly burst forth.

Bang!

The demonic qi trembled, instantly blasting Luo Chen and Azure Frost apart. It turned into an arrow leaving the string, drilling into the moonlight-glazed hall, directly piercing through the chest of the white-robed monk sitting cross-legged in the center of the hall.

Outside the hall.

Luo Chen, his body wreathed in golden flames, was knocked to the ground, continuously retreating.

Long furrows were carved into the earth around him.

Figures flew toward him from all directions, intending to attack him.

But Luo Chen ignored them. After forcibly stopping, he looked toward the hall.

"In the end, I still couldn't protect him?"

At that moment, the wind and clouds in the sky shifted.

A condensed primordial spirit descended from the heavens, entering the white-robed monk's body.

Immediately after, an incalculable amount of heaven and earth origin qi surged toward the white-robed monk along with that primordial spirit.

Receiving this external force, Dharma Master Xuan Du, clad in a moon-white monk's robe, slowly opened his eyes.

He lowered his head to look at the demonic, sinister arm embedded in his chest, revealing a trace of regret.

But this regret vanished in an instant.

"Enough!"

This single "enough"—whether spoken to himself or to the remaining dozen or so Hanging Temple monks—no one knew.

The entire Glazed Pure Land, after these words, suddenly fell silent.

Dharma Master Xuan Du calmly rose and walked out of the moonlight-glazed hall.

The aura on his body no longer concealed itself in the slightest, beginning to rise continuously.

Streams of divine sense crisscrossed back and forth, transforming into spiritual thoughts, making everyone feel as if their entire being, past and future, had become thoroughly transparent.

Luo Chen stood up, looking at Dharma Master Xuan Du with uncertainty, and at the black left hand still embedded in his chest.

"Did he succeed, or fail?"

Azure Frost slowly descended to his side, standing shoulder to shoulder with him, and said softly, "It should be a success."

Success was success. What did "should be" mean?

Azure Frost did not explain. But Dharma Master Xuan Du, in front of everyone, slowly pulled the left hand out of his chest.

"This is the left hand of Mahakala. Fellow junior brothers, look carefully—is this the Buddha's left hand, or the demon's left hand?"

The Hanging Temple monks were stunned. All their battle intent and sorrow were suppressed by the terrifying demonic qi on that arm.

Dharma Master Xuan Du did not explain further. He merely waved his sleeve, and streams of airflow appeared in the Pure Land, rolling up the Heavenly Brahma Son and his group toward the exit.

"Fellow Daoists, this humble monk must cleanse the sect. I won't see you off."

In the blink of an eye, a group of Sage cultivators, without any power to resist, were "sent" out of the Glazed Pure Land.

And the entrance that had been open for many days also closed in an instant, disappearing without a trace.

The Heavenly Brahma Son and the other cultivators floated in the void, under the gaze of the crowd, looking at each other in bewilderment.

They had never expected the final outcome to be like this.

The demoness and the Moluo had suppressed the Hanging Temple monks, defeating all eight hall abbots.

Mahakala had bestowed his left hand, heavily wounding Dharma Master Xuan Du just as his primordial spirit was about to enter his body.

But that left hand clearly contained incomparably terrifying demonic qi!

Now that the Glazed Pure Land had closed, what would happen next?

Thinking of the words Dharma Master Xuan Du had said before sending them out.

Cleanse the sect!

Could it be...

As they exchanged glances, they already had their answer.

"The Hanging Temple!"
