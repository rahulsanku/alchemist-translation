# Chapter 1186: Luo Chen's Thunder Astral Power, Earth Vein Primordial Magnetism

The newcomer was clad in a green robe, and on the exposed parts of his face were patches of green scales, making him appear even more sinister and cold.

When he spoke, the forked tongue he revealed sent chills down one's spine.

But before Luo Chen and Yuan Qingzang, he seemed somewhat inadequate.

Yuan Qingzang spoke bluntly: "Green Robe, how is it that you're the one in charge? Where's that old fellow Bei Feng?"

The man called Green Robe narrowed his eyes as he swept his gaze over the two, his voice cold: "Yuan Qingzang, our ancestor was invited by the Wuyou Realm Lord and left a hundred years ago. Our clan is currently in a state of seclusion. You wouldn't be thinking of taking advantage of the situation to loot, would you?"

Invited by the Wuyou Realm Lord?

Hearing this, Luo Chen's expression turned thoughtful.

It seemed that the Wuyou Realm Lord had indeed prepared thoroughly for this exploration of the Ancient God's Dao Field!

Not only had he taken Mu Bugui away, but he had also previously invited Yuan Qingzang, and now even the ancestor of the Sorrowful Abyss, Bei Feng, had gone with him.

Did this mean that the top experts of other forces within the Guixu had also received invitations?

Beside him, Yuan Qingzang let out an angry snort.

"Why speak so harshly? I merely came to visit an old friend. And even if I were taking advantage of the situation to loot, so what? You, Green Robe, are merely a newly advanced Martial Dao Human Immortal. Do you really think you can stop me?"

"You can try!"

Green Robe was fearless.

As he spoke, his entire body emitted a chilling aura.

Behind him, tens of thousands of warriors sat down cross-legged, streams of astral power rising from them, ultimately converging onto Green Robe alone.

An incredibly complex astral power rose from Green Robe's body, then drilled into those abysses that resembled gaping, blood-filled mouths.

In an instant, the area within a hundred li became treacherous ground.

Clusters upon clusters of inexplicable forces, accompanied by those astral powers, were spat out from the abysses, watching covetously from the side.

Seeing this, Yuan Qingzang's expression shifted slightly, his brows furrowing.

"Old Man Bei Feng even taught you this technique?"

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow, looking at Yuan Qingzang.

The latter didn't hide anything and explained: "This technique is somewhat similar to my *Raging Torrent Extinction Realm*. It can draw upon the strange powers within the earth abyss for one's own use, achieving a hundredfold amplification in might."

With that explanation, Luo Chen understood.

In fact, he had already seen some clues. Such techniques all relied on geographical advantages to enhance one's own abilities.

With the support of geographical advantages, Yuan Qingzang, at the late Human Immortal stage, could contend with a Destruction Realm powerhouse. Now, Green Robe, at the early Human Immortal stage, could threaten an opponent at the late Human Immortal stage.

Yuan Qingzang wasn't confident he could take him down, hence the look of wariness.

On the opposite side, Green Robe's attention had focused on Luo Chen the moment he spoke.

In his eyes, the leader of the two wasn't the famous Lord of the Vaulted Sky Pagoda Forest, Yuan Qingzang, but rather this white-robed young man.

In the Guixu, the strong are revered!

The hierarchy of status was enough to show that Luo Chen's strength must be above Yuan Qingzang's.

If it were just one person, he had some confidence. But if the two attacked together... so for now, he could only hold his killing move, ready but not released.

Green Robe said coldly: "Sir, please do not bring harm upon yourself!"

Luo Chen smiled.

"Daoist Yuan, how about letting me handle this?"

Yuan Qingzang remembered the technique Luo Chen had used to confront him back then, and nodded with slight curiosity.

"Please!"

Luo Chen took a step forward, standing at the front.

Facing tens of thousands of warriors alone, astral power overflowed from his body, but his aura suddenly changed.

From that lawless, selfless, objectless aura, it transformed into another kind of majestic, grand, inviolable presence.

There was even an illusion of a majestic heavenly might about to descend.

This scene made Yuan Qingzang's expression shift.

"This is..."

*Crackle!*

A bolt of lightning suddenly flashed.

Then, endless thunder erupted in a torrent.

In an instant, Luo Chen had become a figure like a god of thunder amidst the lightning.

"Thunder Astral Power, Raging Torrent Extinction Realm!" Yuan Qingzang murmured to himself, his face full of disbelief.

As he murmured, Luo Chen had already made his move.

A cataclysmic thunderstorm, like a vast ocean current pouring out, surged straight towards Green Robe.

The latter, with a look of doubt and uncertainty, glanced at Yuan Qingzang, then gritted his teeth and struck out.

"You go too far!"

In his roar, countless strange currents of air shot up into the sky.

*Boom!*

*Boom!*

*Boom!*

In an instant, thunderbolts and air masses collided fiercely, shaking the surroundings, making heaven and earth tremble.

On the ground, more and more cracks appeared, and more abysses opened their mouths, spewing out more air currents.

Luo Chen stood in the wind, watching this scene impassively, unmoved.

He merely unceasingly urged the inexhaustible Blood-Refined Divine Astral Power, along with the Soul-Shattering Divine Thunder he had previously drawn from the Chaotic Thunder Pool.

Even under the enhancement of his martial dao divine will, the astral power and divine thunder reacted, producing thunderous sounds that shook the mind and spirit.

It was the Soul-Shattering Thunder Sound!

*Rumble!*

The stalemate between the two sides lasted only a moment.

Accompanied by a miserable cry, Green Robe finally couldn't hold on, falling to the ground covered in blood.

And the tens of thousands of Abyss Dweller warriors who had provided him with power also collapsed one by one.

Luo Chen drifted down to the ground.

Green Robe's face was full of hatred: "Just you wait! When our ancestor returns, he will surely avenge the clan's extermination!"

Luo Chen smiled. "Don't make it sound so irreconcilable. I merely knocked them unconscious; I didn't kill them."

Green Robe was stunned.

Before he could react, Luo Chen had already stepped past him, heading towards the direction of the Sorrowful Abyss.

"I'm going to take a look at your clan's territory. Please forgive any discourtesy."

Yuan Qingzang flapped his Thunderlight Wings and flew over, pursing his lips, his face full of curiosity.

"This is Yuan's first time deep in the Sorrowful Abyss. I'd like to see just what kind of environment Old Man Bei Feng cultivates in."

The two, one after the other, eventually disappeared into the abyss.

Green Robe opened his mouth, his face blank.

If it was just for this, then what was the point of this fight, of him being so heavily injured?

...

Entering the Sorrowful Abyss, the light flickered uncertainly, the environment dark and damp.

Between the cliffs, half-human, half-snake creatures covered in green scales occasionally slithered about, all staring outwards as if facing a great enemy.

Luo Chen knew that these were the so-called "Abyss Dwellers," all descendants of Old Man Bei Feng's bloodline.

According to Yuan Qingzang, Old Man Bei Feng's original form was likely a great snake demon, and these Abyss Dwellers were the result of his interbreeding with humans.

Besides continuing his bloodline, their purpose was also to help him explore the Sorrowful Abyss.

This was somewhat similar to how the Wuyou Realm Lord controlled the seventy-two cities of the Great Destruction Realm, selecting high-level martial artists to train in the Destruction Nine Rings.

Luo Chen restrained his aura and continued downwards.

Yuan Qingzang caught up, looking at Luo Chen's profile with an expression of wanting to speak but hesitating.

Luo Chen vaguely knew what he wanted to ask, but still asked with a smile: "Daoist Yuan, do you have any advice?"

"Advice is too strong a word." Yuan Qingzang sighed, then said quietly: "That move you used earlier, was it my *Raging Torrent Extinction Realm* that I had held back?"

Luo Chen didn't hide it, admitting frankly: "It was!"

Having received confirmation, Yuan Qingzang's doubtful feelings settled, but he became even more amazed.

"I remember I never taught you that move. I only discussed some cultivation insights and martial dao concepts with you?"

Luo Chen smiled. "Before I entered the Guixu, I was a high-level cultivator. I had already mastered techniques related to domains. That move of yours, or even the earlier *Wild Thunder Wilderness*, *Violent Thunder Raging Waves*, and so on, can all be considered domain-type killing moves. Such techniques themselves aren't particularly exquisite. As long as one grasps the basic techniques and the relevant energy to activate them, they can be replicated."

Yuan Qingzang opened his mouth, then fell silent.

What Luo Chen said was correct. Those techniques and killing moves were all things he had figured out while facing the power of thunder day and night in the Vaulted Sky Pagoda Forest.

Although their power was great, they relied more on the surrounding field.

Once he left the Vaulted Sky Pagoda Forest, their power would be greatly reduced.

On the contrary, Luo Chen, having absorbed an entire Chaotic Thunder Pool, was like a walking thunder pool of a different kind. Releasing such techniques was actually more powerful than him.

In his silence, Yuan Qingzang felt a bit unconvinced.

"Actually, Yuan also has his own unique single-target divine ability."

"Oh? Then I'd like to see it sometime." Luo Chen laughed, but his pace quickened. "As for now, exploring the Sorrowful Abyss is the priority!"

With that, his figure disappeared into the depths of the Sorrowful Abyss.

...

The endless abyss, with unceasing sorrowful cries.

That was the origin of the name "Sorrowful Abyss."

The deeper one went, the more strange sounds continuously emanated, like the weeping of living beings.

Two figures shuttled through, no one knew how long had passed, nor how many zhang deep into the earth they had gone.

All they knew was that in the sunless environment, the strange air masses around them surged more and more.

Those air masses contained a complex power, seemingly like the Five Elements, yet not within the Five Elements.

Once a martial artist's astral power touched them, it would inexplicably lose control.

The deeper Yuan Qingzang went, the greater the pressure he felt.

On the contrary, the deeper Luo Chen went, the more a sense of familiarity grew within him.

"This power..."

Suddenly, the long-dormant Primordial Unity Cauldron within him seemed to sense something, emitting a slight tremor.

"Luo Chen, I can't go any further!"

The voice of Yuan Qingzang came from behind.

Luo Chen stopped and looked back.

He saw Yuan Qingzang's face showing difficulty, a great radiance of thunder bursting around his body, with a tendency for his natal astral power to be pulled and leaked outwards.

Luo Chen's gaze swept over the surroundings, his expression thoughtful.

"Very well, Daoist Yuan, wait for me outside. I'll go and return shortly."

"Alright, I wish you success."

Yuan Qingzang offered a blessing, then hurriedly left.

Only when he had completely left the underground abyss did he heave a sigh of relief.

The strange power that had been pulling at him from all directions finally disappeared.

Yuan Qingzang spat out a mouthful of turbid qi, muttering to himself: "Such a dangerous place. If I went any deeper, I'd probably explode. Who was it that said there was a way out of the Guixu here?"

"That's precisely why our ancestor was forced to seek other methods and follow the Wuyou Realm Lord away!"

Yuan Qingzang turned his head. Green Robe was standing on a protruding cliff rock, watching him coldly.

Yuan Qingzang chuckled. "How come you didn't follow?"

Green Robe snorted coldly, not explaining, but just looking in the direction from which the other had come.

"And that one?"

...

"Indeed!"

When Luo Chen's feet landed on the solid ground, he understood where that sense of familiarity came from.

His gaze fell on the gray-white stones on the ground, and Luo Chen blurted out.

"Earth Vein Primordial Magnetism!"

He actively released his Blood-Refined Divine Astral Power.

Immediately, a strange power emanated from the ground, beginning to pull and guide Luo Chen's astral power.

In just a short moment, the Blood-Refined Divine Astral Power he had released was actually disintegrated and separated!

One must know that Luo Chen's Blood-Refined Divine Astral Power was formed by fusing the Ninefold Earth Fiend and the Unforgiving Heavenly Wind, with his own pure source blood as the catalyst.

Apart from that chaotic astral power of the Turbid World Martial Ancestor back then, he had almost never seen anything that could disintegrate the Blood-Refined Divine Astral Power.

Even Yuan Qingzang's Thunder Astral Power couldn't do it!

But these powers naturally emitted from the earth veins could achieve such a feat.

Throughout the world, there was only one special thing that had this effect.

That was Primordial Magnetism Divine Light!

No, even Primordial Magnetism Divine Light wasn't enough. Only Primordial Magnetism Divine Power, refined to the extreme, could reach such a level.

Luo Chen looked around. In the pitch-black environment, abysses stretched endlessly, cutting through the entire earth.

It was hard to imagine how vast this earth vein primordial magnetism must be to have condensed such terrifying Primordial Magnetism Divine Power.

Even harder to imagine was that Old Man Bei Feng could actually refine and use this power for himself!

Amidst his amazement, Luo Chen felt regret.

The reason for his regret was not complicated.

Where there was earth vein primordial magnetism, the world's barriers were often the most solid, and ordinary means couldn't break them.

That was why the Primordial Demon Sect of the Mountains and Seas Realm had established their mountain gate in the Arctic Yemo Heaven back then, because there was a Primordial Magnetism Divine Mountain there, serving as an impenetrable portal barrier.

And here, it meant that this earth vein primordial magnetism, which ran through who knew how many ranges, could not possibly have an exit leading out of the Guixu.

Outsiders thought there was an exit here, but it was merely a wrong judgment caused by the Primordial Magnetism Divine Power's characteristic of attracting and disintegrating everything.

Luo Chen sighed and was about to leave.

After all, staying here too long would put even his powerful True Spirit fleshly body under unbearable strain.

If his apertures were to open wide and all his astral power was pulled out, all his previous efforts would be wasted.

But just as he was about to leave, Luo Chen's heart suddenly stirred.

"The Primordial Unity Cauldron had a reaction earlier because I had fused a Primordial Magnetism Divine Mountain into it before ascending."

"In other words, I can actually control simple Primordial Magnetism Divine Power."

"If I activate the Primordial Unity Cauldron and absorb the Primordial Magnetism Divine Power here..."

Luo Chen's eyes lit up, and his impulse immediately turned into action!

He looked around, and after confirming no one was watching, he summoned the Primordial Unity Cauldron with a thought.

*Thump!*

The small cauldron landed, growing larger in the wind, becoming over a zhang in size.

Its form wasn't large, but it was as heavy as a mountain!

The surrounding Primordial Magnetism Divine Power surged over, wanting to disintegrate the Primordial Unity Cauldron, but couldn't budge it in the slightest.

Of course, it was also very difficult for the Primordial Unity Cauldron to absorb the Primordial Magnetism Divine Power, because the nearby earth vein primordial magnetism was too vast. Trying to forcibly absorb it would be like an extremely mismatched tug-of-war.

But Luo Chen had another way!

He drifted and sat cross-legged in the air.

His astral power circulated around his body, and a great radiance of thunder blazed on his back, enveloping the vicinity.

Then, he carefully formed a hand seal.

"Awaken!"

In an instant, golden waves rippled within his sea of consciousness.

His main soul, dormant for centuries, slowly awakened.

Throughout this process, Luo Chen's movements were extremely slow, constantly paying attention to the outside.

After a long while, he finally breathed a sigh of relief.

"Indeed, with the Soul-Shattering Divine Thunder as cover, it didn't attract the Guixu's characteristic Soul-Shattering Thunder Sound."

Then, he released a strand of divine sense, beginning to drive the Primordial Unity Cauldron to actively absorb the nearby Primordial Magnetism Divine Power.

In an instant, the earth veins stirred, and mournful sounds echoed continuously.
