# Chapter 1103: Innate Shackles, Demon-Phase Body Refinement

The banquet resumed, seemingly as before.

Yet the still-turbulent water vapor and the freshly served spirit wine and fine delicacies all bore witness to the lingering influence of the earlier battle.

Especially when the assembled demon cultivators saw Luo Chen, who had previously arrived as a menacing, trouble-seeking guest, now seated as an honored guest on equal footing with the Sea Emperor—everyone was stunned. At the same time, they understood the outcome of the clash between these two Ascendants.

A draw?

At least a draw!

More likely, Luo Chen had prevailed over Sea Emperor Whale; otherwise, there was no explanation for such treatment.

There was little to discuss about the banquet's content—it was nothing more than mutual flattery.

After confirming that killing Sea Emperor Whale would require an enormous cost, Luo Chen had already made his decision.

Cease hostilities and make peace!

He was naturally unafraid of Sea Emperor Whale, but he also had a weakness—the Luo Tian Sect.

If the other party went mad and attacked the Luo Tian Sect, the casualties would be incalculable.

Moreover, after witnessing Luo Chen's strength, Sea Emperor Whale had become sincerely convinced and had given sufficient compensation.

Most of the various resources plundered from the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect had fallen into Sea Emperor Whale's mouth, which was why his cultivation had advanced so greatly.

But Luo Chen actually didn't care much about those resources.

On the contrary, the remaining portion—cultivation techniques, scriptures, secret arts, and inheritances—was, in Luo Chen's view, the real highlight.

If he could fully digest and absorb them, the Luo Tian Sect's future would be promising.

...

Leaving the Sea Emperor's Palace.

Gazing at the Giant Whale Sea Region after the great battle, Luo Chen sighed softly.

The Kongchan beside him was quite puzzled. "Master has just become an Ascendant and already overwhelms those of the same rank—why do you look disappointed?"

Luo Chen shook his head and sighed lightly. "It's just that I've always been accustomed to crushing those of the same rank, killing and slaughtering at will. But at the Ascendant level, achieving this is extremely difficult. As such, interactions between great powers will inevitably rely more on verbal exchanges, interest trades, and even mutual compromise."

Kongchan opened his mouth, but found himself speechless.

Fighting between those of the same rank had always made it hard to determine life or death.

Not to mention the Ascendant realm—even at the Sage realm, this was the case.

It was only because Luo Chen had been so abnormally powerful before that he could dominate the Sage realm.

Now that he had returned to normal circumstances, this was the norm!

But Luo Chen didn't think so.

During the Sage realm, he could easily kill those of the same rank. Apart from his own superb strength and numerous methods, another special point lay in his magic treasure.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron!

Since its refinement, his innate magic treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, had possessed an extremely strong binding and imprisoning effect on Adepts and Sages.

Thus, Luo Chen had been able to personally slay those enemies who had been defeated by him in countless battles.

However, this marvelous effect had weakened to the extreme after he broke through to Ascendant.

An Ascendant cultivator's primordial spirit was too powerful, easily able to stir the origin qi of heaven and earth. Relying solely on the Primordial Unity Cauldron itself could no longer restrict their freedom of movement.

"Ultimately, my cultivation speed has been too fast, causing my innate magic treasure to fail to keep up with my advancement. Up to now, it's still at the true artifact level."

"Furthermore, the Primordial Unity Cauldron's potential has been nearly exhausted; it's very difficult to advance further!"

"If I want to nurture it to the spirit treasure level, it will take at least several hundred years, even a thousand years."

Luo Chen thought silently to himself.

From the perspective of the former Adept and Sage realms, the Primordial Unity Cauldron could be considered an excellent magic treasure.

Unified in offense and defense, with many marvelous uses, it could be called comprehensive!

But its foundation was ultimately the refining method of a mere Adept-level sect. Even though a massive amount of precious resources had been invested during its refinement, its upper limit was still there.

If he hadn't gradually refined some Thousand-Year Thunder Essence and Xirang into it over time, increasing its power, it probably wouldn't have been of any use at all in his earlier fight with Sea Emperor Whale.

Comparatively speaking, aside from daily alchemy, this innate magic treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, could no longer be mentioned in the same breath as the Yuantu Sword, the Burial Bone Umbrella, or even the Great Five Elements Sword Formation, which focused solely on slaughter.

Sometimes it was like this—comprehensiveness either meant perfection in every aspect, or it meant mediocrity.

And what couldn't keep up with Luo Chen's realm was mediocrity, was a chicken rib!

But Luo Chen couldn't abandon this object.

An innate magic treasure was not something that could be easily discarded.

It was a treasure that had been cultivated in tandem with his very life essence for hundreds of years.

"Perhaps I need to consider how to upgrade my innate magic treasure."

Luo Chen pondered silently, countless thoughts flashing through his mind.

With his level as a fourth-rank artifact refiner, his insight into the Dao of artifact refinement was already considerable. Even though the Primordial Unity Cauldron's foundation came only from the Qingdan Valley, he still had some ingenious ideas to enhance its power.

The only thing to consider was: in which direction to enhance it?

And in what aspect would it be sufficiently helpful for his Ascendant realm?

In fact, when Luo Chen had such thoughts, he had already realized he would have to abandon the comprehensiveness of his innate magic treasure.

This realization was not difficult for him.

Because he already had many treasures in hand; the missing parts of his innate magic treasure could be compensated for by other means.

In his ears came Kongchan's questioning voice.

"Master, where are we going next? Shall we go to Suspended Blade Peak?"

"Suspended Blade Peak?" Luo Chen recited this name, then slowly shook his head.

Another great demonic power of the Northern Sea, Great Sage Beili, lived on that Suspended Blade Peak, which was said to be like a heavenly blade hanging alone for eternity.

The terrain there was complex and extremely perilous. Even when the Primordial Demon Sect still existed, no one dared to disturb it.

Even if Luo Chen could break through Suspended Blade Peak and face Great Sage Beili directly, what then?

Another repeat of the story with Sea Emperor Whale?

"Forget it. Back then, outside Heavenly Earth Island, Great Sage Beili was still relatively restrained. Today, I won't go looking for trouble with him. Besides, there's someone waiting for me."

Luo Chen smiled faintly, then merged into heaven and earth with Kongchan.

...

Soon after, on a nameless small island.

Luo Chen's figure materialized as he looked at the young man quietly waiting below, smiling slightly.

"I've kept you waiting, young friend."

"I wouldn't dare. I have always remembered the favor you showed me back then, Senior."

The one who had been waiting for a long time was none other than Layman Fengling.

During the banquet earlier, with so many people and much talk, the two had no opportunity to exchange words, so they had specially arranged to meet here.

As for the favor he mentioned?

To Luo Chen, it was merely a lift of the hand.

Within the Arctic Yemo Heaven, Luo Chen had used the Nirvana Sacred Flame to extinguish that corpse giant, helping Layman Fengling cultivate his demonic art and ultimately condense his demon body.

Luo Chen laughed heartily. "When I led people to attack the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, didn't you also lend a hand? The favor between us has been cleared."

When he said this, Kongchan behind him couldn't help but nod.

In that battle, it was Kongchan, Deng Taiyue, and Layman Fengling who joined forces. The three of them worked together to completely block the Dao Sect's reinforcements from the Northern Continent, giving the Eastern Wilderness cultivators on the Eastern Continent time to retreat.

Therefore, although the three had little contact, the bond formed from fighting side by side once made them appreciate each other.

Especially Layman Fengling's strength, which had earned the recognition of both Kongchan and Deng Taiyue.

It was a combat style completely different from his handsome and demonic appearance—wild, tyrannical, and thick with demonic qi.

Layman Fengling suddenly bowed deeply.

"Last time, my assistance was only right and proper. But meeting today, I must truly express my gratitude. If you hadn't helped me at a critical moment, Senior, how could I have cultivated the True Demon Body? This great kindness and virtue, Fengling will never forget."

"True Demon Body?" Luo Chen became interested and suddenly asked, "I wonder if you can drive demonic qi? The purest kind of demonic qi?"

Layman Fengling asked, "Is Senior referring to the demonic qi of the Primordial Demon Sect lineage?"

"More or less!" Luo Chen nodded.

Layman Fengling revealed a smile. "Let Senior know, compared to the Primordial Demon Qi of the Primordial Demon Sect, my True Demon Power, obtained from Devil Lord Heaven-Refining, is still three parts purer."

As he spoke, a flicker appeared at his fingertip.

A wisp of demonic flame rose into the air.

Its terrifying temperature was startling.

Seeing this, Luo Chen couldn't help but feel joy in his heart.

"In that case, please help me with a small favor!"

A statue appeared before the two of them—a figure with two horns on its head, pitch black all over, with only blood vessels visible in vivid detail.

Even though the statue had no aura, just one glance gave people a sense of power.

This was an extraordinary treasure that Black King had excavated from the depths of the Primordial Demon Sect ruins back then.

Based on Black King and Luo Chen's speculation, this statue most likely recorded a set of body refinement techniques.

But relying solely on dharma power, source power, or even qi and blood power, it was very difficult to fully activate it.

Back then, Luo Chen had guessed that perhaps demonic qi was needed to deduce the complete movement route.

Now that Layman Fengling was before him, Luo Chen could take advantage of his strength.

Since his benefactor had a request, Layman Fengling naturally wouldn't be stingy.

He shook his body, and an extremely pure power flowed from him, surging toward the demon statue.

Luo Chen's eyes sparkled with brilliance, and a trace of surprise appeared on his face.

So this was the so-called True Demon Power?

Compared to the remnants of the Primordial Demon Sect he had seen back then, it was more than three parts purer!

Combined with Layman Fengling's earlier mention of "obtained from Devil Lord Heaven-Refining," Luo Chen vaguely guessed at the other's opportunity.

Six hundred years ago, Layman Fengling, Li Canghai, and others had explored the Demon-Fall Land together. Compared to others who could enter Bright Radiance Heaven, Layman Fengling was unfortunately ambushed by a Violent Barbarian Demon Jiao and fell into the Demon-Falling Abyss that lay between the Five Elements Heaven and Bright Radiance Heaven.

Luo Chen had also briefly entered that Demon-Falling Abyss once.

It was to help the Tai Sui escape its predicament.

But that time, his entry distance was limited, only between the cliff caves, never reaching the bottom of the abyss.

But just looking at it from afar had been terrifying.

That Layman Fengling could walk out alive, though he went mad for several hundred years and earned the title of Mad Wanderer, after he completely awakened, that misfortune became a stroke of luck.

But Luo Chen didn't inquire.

Everyone had their own opportunities. Especially those who could achieve Sage and Ascendant realms—whose opportunities were small?

If he became envious at the sight of others' opportunities, Luo Chen's Crimson-Eyed Golden Pupils would turn into red-eye disease.

On the small island, it was quite quiet.

Only the subtle sound of Layman Fengling driving his demonic qi could be heard.

His movements were very gentle, as if afraid of damaging the statue, but the speed of the demonic qi flow was quite fast, far faster than when Luo Chen had used source power to explore the statue back then.

Half an incense stick later.

Suddenly, a low shout was heard.

"Open!"

The next moment, the three seemed to hear the sound of some shackle being unlocked.

Blood flowed on the statue, black qi rolled, and the entire demon figure seemed to come alive.

A phantom demon suddenly expanded and enlarged, roaring silently toward the sky.

On the demon's body, a large number of unfamiliar characters swirled like living things, looking like talisman patterns at a glance.

Seeing this, Luo Chen's eyes lit up, knowing this method was extraordinary.

"Withdraw!"

Layman Fengling withdrew his fingertip, and the demonic qi dissipated, but a clump remained inside the statue.

He breathed a sigh of relief and began to introduce it to Luo Chen.

"Senior, this demon statue indeed records a set of techniques."

"Its name is—Demon Phase Art."

"As for its specific rank, Fengling does not know, but this Demon Phase Art is quite marvelous. Its foundation lies in tempering qi and blood. When facing an enemy, it can convert qi and blood, condensing a demon phase outside the body. This demon phase has vast power. As long as the cultivator's qi and blood power is not exhausted, the demon phase is immortal and indestructible—truly wondrous."

After speaking, he smiled and cupped his hands toward Luo Chen.

"Congratulations, Senior, on obtaining a marvelous technique!"

Luo Chen laughed heartily. "This still relies heavily on you. Now that you have also cultivated the True Demon Body, this Demon Phase Art should be useful to you as well. Copy a copy for yourself!"

Layman Fengling hesitated for a moment, then finally nodded. "Then Fengling will not refuse. Thank you, Senior, for bestowing the technique."

Luo Chen took the statue. The blood vessels were still crystalline, but the bloody halo that had enveloped the statue had dissipated. The demonic qi restriction on this object had been broken by Layman Fengling.

Not only that, but a clump of demonic qi remained inside.

Luo Chen glanced at Layman Fengling with considerable approval.

The other party had taken the initiative to leave behind a clump of demonic qi, specifically so that Luo Chen could conveniently peruse the techniques recorded on it before he had mastered this method.

He put the statue away, to be cultivated later when he had time.

With this matter concluded, Luo Chen looked at Layman Fengling with increasing satisfaction.

This man knew gratitude, advanced and retreated with propriety, was not only powerful but had also survived great calamities, indicating he had considerable luck on him.

If he could recruit this man to the Luo Tian Sect...

"Young friend, the Myriad Immortals Assembly is no more. I wonder where you plan to go now? Do you have any intention of finding a sect to settle down in?"

Layman Fengling hesitated for a moment.

Luo Chen seemed to understand what he was worried about and waved his hand. "If you're unwilling, that's fine. I won't force you."

Layman Fengling bowed again. "Then thank you for your kindness, Senior. I am accustomed to being alone, drifting across the four seas."

Luo Chen smiled faintly. "In that case, until we meet again."

Luo Chen was a man who cherished talent, but now he no longer needed to use coercion or inducement to force others to join the Luo Tian Sect.

Times had changed.

Back then, when he recruited Ling Fengzi, it was merely to huddle together for warmth in chaotic times.

He did not wish to repeat the old deeds of Mi Shuhua or Black Marsh Ancestor.

...

High in the nine heavens.

The Frost Eagle Shuttle sped through the air. Kongchan piloted the shuttle while Luo Chen studied the statue.

After a long while, he put the statue away.

Although this Demon Phase Art was a demonic body refinement technique, it was equally upright and grand, seeking the same expansion of qi and blood as human body refinement.

Even the qi and blood power that constituted the external demon phase had to be at least at the astral qi level.

This was a bit troublesome for him.

All his qi and blood power had been converted into source power, the purest form of physical strength.

Astral qi was a bit problematic.

He could take it back for Wang Yuan to reference. Since advancing to the fourth rank of body refinement, Wang Yuan had very few body refinement techniques to reference, and his progress had been quite slow.

With this Demon Phase Art, perhaps Wang Yuan could advance further.

After putting away the statue, another object appeared in Luo Chen's hand.

It was the artifact refinement technique that Li Canghai had gifted him earlier, recording a Soul-Extracting Spirit-Binding Art.

Combining his own artifact refinement skills with some earlier thoughts, an idea was gradually taking shape in Luo Chen's mind.

"Kongchan!"

"What are your orders, Master?"

"Don't go to the Swallow Sea Region first. Turn toward the Arctic Yemo Heaven!"

(End of Chapter)
