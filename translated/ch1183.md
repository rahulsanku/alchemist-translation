# Chapter 1183: Facing the Empty Gate, the Tower of the Firmament

The second ring.

A figure slowly materialized, the chaotic energy around him surging like tidal waves.

The white-robed figure stood silent for a long time, as if adapting to the environment.

After a long while, he finally took a step.

The Ring of Annihilation was divided into three cultivation areas—upper, middle, and lower. But even within the same upper three rings, the pressure between the second and third rings was as different as heaven and earth.

Under normal circumstances, without reaching the Annihilation realm, it was extremely difficult to remain in the second ring for long.

Thus, for so many years, only Lord Carefree had cultivated alone in the second ring.

There was only one cultivation stone here!

Before ascending, Luo Chen had already prepared himself. With his true spirit fleshly body, now far surpassing the Great Vajra physique, he should be able to withstand the external pressure.

Especially since his martial dao realm of late-stage Human Immortal was far beyond ordinary.

On the surface, Luo Chen was at late-stage Human Immortal.

But in reality, his apertures housed seven hundred and twenty martial dao gods, each one comparable to an ordinary martial dao Human Immortal.

With seven hundred and twenty stacked together, even an Annihilation realm expert inspired no fear in him.

As he expected, it took only a short while to adapt to this pressure.

"With such dense chaotic energy, it should contain even more fragments of laws."

"If I could cultivate here long-term, perhaps the Primordial Unity Cauldron could gather more origin power of laws."

"But time..."

Luo Chen sighed and quickened his pace.

Finally, he arrived at a cultivation stone.

Unlike the vast, island-like cultivation stones of the third ring, this single stone in the second ring was quite small, barely enough for one person to dwell alone.

Yet it was the only one that could withstand the erosion of chaotic energy for millions of years without being destroyed.

Luo Chen had once been curious about what material these cultivation stones were made of.

But none of the scriptures he had studied contained any relevant records.

Even Lord Carefree didn't understand, only roughly speculating that they might be some kind of chaotic product.

After the incident with the Primordial Unity Cauldron, Luo Chen had become increasingly sensitive to chaotic products.

If it could withstand the erosion of chaotic energy, could it also qualify as a dao artifact embryo?

Others had no way to deal with these things, but Luo Chen was different.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron could even refine chaotic energy; it should also be able to refine these cultivation stones.

If he ever had the chance to leave the Netherworld, Luo Chen planned to take one or two with him to study carefully outside.

But for now...

Standing on the cultivation stone, Luo Chen looked up at the heights.

Within the endless, storm-like chaotic energy, a massive eye was faintly visible, looking down from above.

As their gazes met, Luo Chen even felt as if his sight, his will, and even his hidden divine sense were being devoured.

"That is the Gate of Annihilation!"

With his Crimson-Eyed Golden Pupils fully activated, Luo Chen was shocked to discover that it was not shaped like a gate at all.

It was a terrifying spatial vortex!

Not a fissure, nor a single spatial node, but a composite product of countless spatial fissures and spatial nodes.

If only it were stable!

But the spatial fissures within rose and fell, crisscrossing in chaos.

Those spatial nodes were also extremely unstable. In the short time Luo Chen observed, he didn't know how many had collapsed and perished.

Every time a node collapsed, it created intricate spatial storms.

Then the spatial storms would generate even more spatial fissures.

Ultimately, it formed a spatial vortex whose depths could not be glimpsed at all!

Chaotic energy was expelled under these conditions.

Gradually, Luo Chen seemed to sink into it.

Three days later.

His entire body trembled, and he woke up in a daze.

His face, which would not waver even if a mountain collapsed before him, was now filled with shock and bitterness.

"Such a spatial vortex... even if Azure Frost, who excelled in spatial laws, were here, I'm afraid she would struggle to maintain herself and eventually be drawn in and ground to dust!"

He sighed.

Luo Chen turned and left.

He now understood why Lord Carefree had guarded the Gate of Annihilation for three thousand years but ultimately gave up.

Unless one possessed absolute overwhelming power, no one would dare attempt to cross this spatial vortex unless absolutely necessary.

And this was still the spatial vortex in its "dormant" state, as Lord Carefree had described.

If it truly erupted, the danger would increase by a thousandfold.

Moreover, there was something even more dangerous and complex hidden within, inspiring a deep, instinctive fear.

Luo Chen knew deeply that this was his last resort. Unless there was no better choice, he would not leave from here.

Exiting the second ring, Luo Chen did not stop.

Without greeting anyone, he descended all the way to the starting point—the ninth ring.

Unnoticed, he took out a golden page from his storage ring.

This was the Gold-Erosion Array Diagram!

The writing on it was a special kind of Gold-Erosion script unique to the Immortal Realm. After three years of instruction from Lord Carefree, Luo Chen could roughly recognize the important parts.

But that wasn't important.

What mattered was that the Gold-Erosion Array Diagram could control the Ninefold Immortal Tower!

He struck out a stream of Blood-Refined Divine Astral, and the Gold-Erosion Array Diagram emitted a dense golden light, enveloping Luo Chen's entire body.

With a flicker, Luo Chen's figure vanished from the ninth ring.

When he reappeared, he was already at the formation nexus of the Ninefold Immortal Tower.

Looking at the still-operating, damaged formations, Luo Chen fell into deep thought.

After a long while, he finally made a decision.

He formed a hand seal, and with a thought, a martial dao god flew out from his body and landed on the ground.

As they gazed at each other, the martial dao god gradually became infused with spirituality.

Luo Chen activated a sliver of divided soul sleeping within it!

"You will sit here in my stead for two hundred years!"

The martial dao god nodded calmly upon hearing this, then took the Gold-Erosion Array Diagram from Luo Chen's hand and sat down before the formation nexus.

Luo Chen turned and left.

His divided soul?

A clone?

No, it was more appropriate to call it a divided spirit.

With a divided spirit holding the Gold-Erosion Array Diagram stationed here, no major problems would arise for one or two hundred years, and the Annihilation Nine Rings could continue to operate normally.

As for after two hundred years?

By then, none of this would have anything to do with him.

...

Another hundred years passed.

The Ninefold Immortal Tower opened again, and many martial artists walked out.

Luo Chen was among them.

Of the three martial dao Human Immortals still alive in the Great Annihilation Realm, Gu Xun chose to make one final sprint, while Fang Qingxue had to come out due to some mundane affairs.

These so-called "mundane affairs" were not complicated.

In the past hundred years, chaos had gradually arisen among the seventy-two cities!

They fought endlessly among themselves, slaughter running rampant.

At its core, it was because when the Ninefold Immortal Tower last closed, some martial artists had not returned.

Back then, Luo Chen hadn't bothered with it, which led to these martial artists running rampant outside.

Fang Qingxue came out to swiftly purge the petty villains and restore order.

After all, the Fang family was the true master of the Great Annihilation Realm, and their ancestor Fang Wuyou was even called "Realm Lord."

Luo Chen had no interest in these matters. After coming out, he first went to Sanxiang City.

But unexpectedly, he did not see his old acquaintance there.

Of course, he also didn't hear news of her passing.

Luo Chen's thoughts turned, and he roughly guessed where she had gone.

...

Half-Mountain Moon Village.

On a cold, clear night, a figure trudged alone along a mountain path.

Compared to the abandoned mountain village he had seen last time, Moon Village now had over a dozen more households.

Most importantly, the light was still on in the small house where Luo Chen and Jing Lan had once lived.

At some point, Luo Chen was already standing outside the door.

But he didn't knock directly, hesitating instead.

This journey was far away, with no fixed return date.

If he saw his old acquaintance again, it would only add to their troubles.

But this woman, who had become friends through battle, was one of the few people in the Netherworld with whom he had any real bond. There should be some closure.

Most importantly, when they parted ways back then, they had made a verbal agreement.

Forget it!

Since he was already here!

Luo Chen raised his hand.

At that moment, the person inside seemed to sense something and opened the door.

Seeing the man with his hand raised as if to knock, the old, weathered woman was momentarily stunned.

"You..."

"You really are here."

The shock in Xiang Fei's eyes turned to joy, and her wrinkled hand grabbed the hem of Luo Chen's hanging robe.

"It really is you!"

Luo Chen smiled. "Won't you invite me in to sit?"

Xiang Fei's nose tingled slightly. "This was always your home. There's no need to speak of 'inviting.' Welcome home!"

Home?

Did he have a home in the Netherworld?

Luo Chen walked in with a sigh.

Under the dim lamplight, the fragrance of tea that had been waiting for two hundred years rose in a mist.

That night, Luo Chen and Xiang Fei talked for a long time.

He learned why the abandoned Moon Village had become lively again. The dozen or so households had moved back from the Abandoned Immortal City to escape the war.

And the Abandoned Immortal City had long since fallen, the culprit being a seventh-ring martial artist who had once cultivated in the Annihilation Nine Rings.

Faced with such an existence, Xiang Fei had been powerless to protect it.

She hadn't returned to Sanxiang City either, ultimately choosing to settle in Moon Village.

The reason for this decision was not complicated: she was old.

When she first returned, she had been severely injured. Even though Luo Chen later healed her, her origin qi had long been damaged, so naturally she couldn't live to the eight-hundred-year lifespan limit.

At dawn.

In the early morning, before Jing Lan's grave.

Watching Luo Chen burn a stick of incense, Xiang Fei's vision blurred.

"You're leaving again?"

"Yes." Luo Chen didn't turn around, speaking frankly. "I will travel the Netherworld, seeking a way to leave."

Xiang Fei sighed. "That's right. You were never from here, so naturally you want to leave."

Luo Chen turned around, looking at the melancholy old woman with complicated eyes.

In the end, he only said, "Take care."

In the Mountains and Seas Realm, his steps had never stopped.

Now in the Netherworld, naturally, they would not stop either.

Being able to pause for a moment for an old acquaintance was already a sign of cherishing the past.

On the lush green mountain, the old woman guarded the tombstone, watching the figure gradually receding into the distance in the midair, her vision slowly blurring.

...

The Netherworld was not large, but its environment was intricate and complex.

Even with the seventy-two cities of the Great Annihilation Realm standing tall, there were many dangerous places within.

Beyond the Great Annihilation Realm, the situation was even worse.

Barren mountains and harsh rivers were only the most superficial descriptions. Scorched earth stretching a thousand li with no sign of human habitation was the norm.

Luo Chen recalled the map Gu Xun had given him and headed west.

Out of concern for the Soul-Shaking Thunder Sound, he didn't fly very high, nor was his speed particularly fast.

But his direction was unwavering.

His first target was the Tower of the Firmament Forest!

Not only was it along the way, but it was also said to be close to the extreme heavens, surrounded by thunder all year round.

Luo Chen wanted to know how humans could survive in such a dangerous environment.

Half a year later.

Luo Chen's figure appeared in a valley.

He looked up, gazing into the distance.

Towers rose and fell one after another, like stone pillars piercing straight into the clouds.

At the extreme heavens, dark clouds gathered densely, with thunder and lightning flashing from time to time.

Occasionally, a thunderbolt would strike down, seemingly attracted and drawn into one of the towers.

White light flashed, then disappeared.

"An interesting design!"

"Even more interesting is that the martial artists here dare to use thunder to temper their bodies!"

Luo Chen dispersed his astral qi, and the attached divine will observed every detail of the scenery within his sight.

But this was limited to the area near the ground.

He dared not observe too much at higher altitudes.

That was where the Soul-Shaking Thunder Sound was most concentrated. Even though he had already split his soul into hundreds of pieces, his main soul's foundation was still vast.

If he carelessly attracted the Soul-Shaking Thunder Sound, the consequences would be dire!

Just as Luo Chen was observing, his heart suddenly stirred.

"An interesting person!"

A divine will of pure yang and supreme strength had already detected Luo Chen's prying and met it head-on without any evasion.

"Who dares to spy here?"

A voice like thunder exploded, spreading across the wilderness.

The next moment.

At the highest tower in the Tower of the Firmament Forest, lightning gathered, and a figure gradually materialized.

Even from a thousand li away, the man's piercing eyes locked onto Luo Chen.

"It seems it wasn't an illusion."

Luo Chen was startled.

Martial dao divine will attached to astral qi was not as subtle as a cultivator's divine sense. Those with deep foundations could make it undetectable to those weaker than them.

But that was only under the premise that the gap between the two sides' realms was not large.

Luo Chen's martial dao divine will had the characteristic of selflessness. He had restrained it as much as possible just now, yet he was still discovered.

In other words, the other party not only had a realm comparable to his but also a divine will intensity that was nearly identical.

Was it because of years of cultivation under the Soul-Shaking Thunder Sound?

As Luo Chen speculated, he slowly revealed his form and spoke in a gentle tone:

"Fellow Daoist..."

A thousand li away, the figure amidst the lightning, like a divine being within the thunder, locked his gaze firmly onto Luo Chen.

Before Luo Chen could finish, he immediately roared in anger.

"Ha!"

A single roar was like thunder.

The next moment, the thunder god spread his lightning wings and shot forward like an arrow from a bowstring.

Not only was his form like an arrow, but he also drew a bow and nocked an arrow in his hand. A lightning arrow condensed from astral qi flew out in a zigzag pattern, straight for Luo Chen.

Faced with such a "strike first, ask questions later" attitude, Luo Chen raised an eyebrow but showed no panic.

He merely extended his right hand lightly and pointed out.

Like a delicate hand picking a flower, he tapped three times.

Pfft!

Pfft!

Pfft!

After three taps, the lightning arrow lost its momentum and slowly dissipated.

And the newcomer had already arrived before him!

His hair and beard bristled, his eyes wide with fury, his face red tinged with blue—a truly ferocious and majestic sight.

Seeing Luo Chen break his charged attack with three finger taps, he was first startled, then grew even more enraged.

A palm struck down fiercely!

Terrifying astral qi surged across the wilderness, accompanied by flashing lightning, shaking the heart.

Even the divided souls hidden in Luo Chen's apertures felt a hint of terror at this attack.

Luo Chen took a deep breath, his expression turning cold.

You want to fight? Then let's fight!

He, Luo Chen, was not one to have a good temper either!
