# Chapter 700: Golden Bell Explodes, Nascent Soul Shatters, Stars Fall, Night Bright Radiance

"The Heaven-Refining Cauldron!"

A startled cry rang beside Moon Wanderer's ears.

Moon Wanderer, who had just pointed a finger to kill Old Daoist Jiyuan and was slowly withdrawing her fair jade hand, paused.

"Diagram spirit, what are you saying?"

The Myriad Beast Diagram's artifact spirit paused, then said, "I saw wrongly. That cauldron merely resembles the Heaven-Refining Cauldron. Its grade and might are far inferior."

Moon Wanderer released a breath.

She thought as much. Not long ago, she had personally seen the Heaven-Refining Cauldron self-detonate above the cloud sea. How could a second cauldron appear?

However!

If even the Myriad Beast Diagram's artifact spirit mistook it in a daze, the great cauldron floating above Devil Lord Azure Yang's head was likely not weak.

For a time, she too wanted to carefully observe the battle over there.

"Little sister, stop. Old Daoist Jiyuan is already dead, and Blood Nightmare Moluo probably cannot last the time it takes to drink a cup of tea under Blood Wanderer. Your alliance has already shattered. Although the Primordial Demon Sect is gone and Penglai Immortal Sect cannot be returned to, I established a Myriad Immortals Assembly. Come back with me, and I will protect you."

Holding her painting scroll, Fairy Feileng stood surrounded by layer after layer of beast shadows and fell silent.

Seeing her this way, Moon Wanderer shook her head and carefully withdrew the Myriad Beast Diagram.

This spirit treasure was indeed powerful, but its demand and consumption of mana were far too great. With her current realm as someone who had only just entered late-stage Sage, forcefully driving it was still incomparably strenuous.

Yes, late-stage Sage!

No one in the world had imagined that among the Northern Sea's Three Great Wanderers, the comparatively weakest Moon Wanderer had already advanced quietly to late-stage Sage, becoming a true Great Cultivator!

By comparison, Mad Wanderer, also at late-stage Sage, had long since gone mad, while the domineering and arrogant Blood Wanderer had lost three Blood God Children during this journey and no longer possessed the strength to contend with a Great Cultivator.

She, Moon Wanderer, had leapt to become the strongest wandering cultivator of the Northern Sea!

And this was the main reason she had been able to quickly kill Old Daoist Jiyuan and capture Fairy Feileng alive.

The might of a Great Cultivator suppressed those of the same tier, unmatched and irresistible!

Yet even so, the way a spirit treasure squeezed a Sage cultivator's mana still exceeded expectations.

Seeing that Fairy Feileng no longer resisted, Moon Wanderer, as her elder sister, stopped attacking and raised her eyes toward the battlefield where Luo Chen stood.

With one glance, she let out a soft sound of surprise.

"This cauldron actually has the power to restrain a cultivator's Nascent Soul void-shifting?"

Within her sight.

That gray little cauldron suddenly bloomed with five-colored brilliance, spreading across heaven and earth like exquisite silk.

Feiyunzi's Nascent Soul, hugging the broken golden bell, kept shifting everywhere.

But no matter where he flew, streams of five-colored ribbon seized at him like chains.

Not only that, beneath the high-hanging gray cauldron, clouds of azure flame poured down.

Within that azure flame, points of golden light spread. It seemed beautiful, but beneath that lovely surface hid endless danger.

A cultivator's Nascent Soul did not dare touch even the slightest bit.

Under layer after layer of pressure, Feiyunzi's range of movement grew shorter and shorter. He could not display the Nascent Soul void-shifting might of fleeing a thousand li in one move at all.

Only a clear laugh was heard from the plaza.

The nine-zhang giant grasped through the void, and countless five-colored ribbons suddenly closed together. Feiyunzi's Nascent Soul was locked directly in the void, unable to move in the slightest, its little face filled with unbelieving, deathly gray despair.

Seeing this, Moon Wanderer could not help nodding slightly.

"This gray cauldron is indeed extraordinary. It seems no more than an upper-grade magic treasure, but it is only one baptism of heaven and earth away from becoming a true artifact. If it truly achieves the true artifact rank, I fear that when we Sages appear naked before it, we will have no power to fight back."

How had such a heavy treasure been refined?

The Myriad Beast Diagram, in truth, possessed a similar effect.

But the Myriad Beast Diagram was a spirit treasure, and driving it was incomparably difficult.

If she obtained Devil Lord Azure Yang's gray cauldron and refined it a little...

For a time, a trace of greed flashed through Moon Wanderer's beautiful eyes.

Suddenly.

A low voice sounded beside Moon Wanderer's ear.

"Elder Sister, that gray light involves the secret of Devil Lord Heaven-Refining advancing to the Body Integration stage. Do you not want it?"

Whoosh!

Moon Wanderer abruptly looked toward Fairy Feileng.

"Truly?"

Fairy Feileng met her gaze with a calm expression. "Although this painting in my hand is not a spirit treasure, it contains a painting spirit. It accompanied this place's master day and night and knows some secret matters. I absolutely would not lie to you."

Moon Wanderer still half believed and half doubted, but next she did not seek confirmation from Fairy Feileng. Instead, she asked the Myriad Beast Diagram's artifact spirit, which she had obtained not long ago.

Facing her question, the Myriad Beast Diagram's artifact spirit was somewhat evasive.

Moon Wanderer grew slightly angry. "Since you have already recognized me as master, how can you conceal anything?"

The diagram spirit said in a deep voice, "Every palace has its own inheritance rules. Whatever inheritance one wants, one must display the corresponding value and accept the corresponding trial. That gray light is a treasure the old master left for the Pill Palace inheritor. You should not set your mind on it."

"Hmph!"

Moon Wanderer asked no further.

From the other party's attitude, her sister's words had undoubtedly already been confirmed.

Then next...

In an instant, Moon Wanderer shot into the sky, heading straight for the gray light.

Layers of moonlight bloomed from her body, and all who glimpsed them could not help developing the urge to submit.

Luo Chen, who had only just seized Feiyunzi's Nascent Soul, saw this, and a flash of anger crossed his brow.

That was an inheritance object he had obtained only after exhausting countless hardships, even to the point where his fleshly body was destroyed.

Now every person who could move was setting their sights on it.

Was there any law left in this world?

Naturally, there was no law in the cultivation world. The only laws were the rules great sects set within their own territories.

And this Demon-Fall Land was precisely a place great sect power could not cover.

But clearly, it would not be easy for Moon Wanderer to subdue that gray light either.

Without the Myriad Beast Diagram's assistance, she used several spells in succession and took out several different magic treasures to try to collect the gray light, but all failed.

The few magic treasures that looked quite extraordinary were strangely annihilated after touching the gray light.

Not only that, the gray light extended outward, actually showing a tendency to refine Moon Wanderer.

For a time, Moon Wanderer cut a sorry figure in the sky.

On the plaza, Fairy Feileng's eyes flickered.

Elder Sister has been held up.

Next, as long as she could delay until the teleportation formation activated, perhaps she could escape her control.

And the trigger for that activation was right now!

Her gaze landed on the two still-shining suspended great palaces.

Light Palace and Artifact Palace!

The light upon them flickered in and out, clearly showing that the inheritors inside had reached the end of accepting their inheritances.

According to the painting spirit, as long as Bright Radiance Heaven's inheritance missions ended, this heaven would send its masters outside.

And those cultivators placed within Five Elements Heaven, if lucky enough to find a stone hut with a spirit-gathering formation, would likewise be teleported out at the same time.

Those with poor luck would probably remain in this Demon-Fall Land for the rest of their lives, assimilated into existences like those strange beasts and spirits.

"Only, it must be quick!"

Fairy Feileng shouted inwardly.

She did not like the Primordial Demon Sect. Although the Primordial Demon Sect had been the blessed land where she advanced to Sage, it was also the site of her first half life's humiliation.

She did not like Penglai Immortal Sect. The people there had mouths full of benevolence and righteousness, but at the crucial moment they were the most cold and faithless.

As for this elder sister of hers, that desire to control was so intense it was almost physically repulsive. Fairy Feileng disliked her even more!

"Since I have already formed my Nascent Soul, I should roam freely through the world and from now on be restrained by no one."

Under her expectation, the lights of the Light and Dark Palaces gradually reached their height.

...

"Luo Chen, how are things on your side?"

"I am fine. Although less than one-tenth of my mana remains, it is barely enough to suppress Feiyunzi's Nascent Soul."

Though he said it was enough, it was still extremely forced.

At the very least, Luo Chen could only watch with open eyes as Moon Wanderer tried to forcibly subdue that gray light in front of him.

And he could do nothing.

Or rather, even at his peak, he would not dare provoke an old monster like Moon Wanderer.

"What about your side?"

"Although Cape Heaven Matron's realm is not poor, she is extremely bad at fighting. This old man is not strained."

Luo Chen looked in the direction of the voice.

Sure enough, just as Han Zhan had said, he had formed an overwhelming suppression over Cape Heaven Matron.

Seven strange puppets of unknown origin each held a Seven Baleful Sword, forming a Seven Baleful Sword Array that trapped Cape Heaven Matron firmly within.

Not only that, the Sage-stage puppet Han Zhan personally controlled also sat at the sword array's core, preventing the other party from escaping.

Under Luo Chen's attention, he surprisingly discovered that Han Zhan seemed to still have strength to spare.

"It seems he also obtained great benefits inside Bright Radiance Heaven!"

Luo Chen pondered.

But in the next moment, his mind was immediately drawn to the sky.

He saw Moon Wanderer abruptly turn back and once more unfurl that ancient ink-painting-like diagram. A huge mouthpart, like that of a mosquito, was drilling out from the ancient diagram.

At this moment, Moon Wanderer's grace and elegance were gone; only madness remained.

She intended to forcefully drive the spirit treasure that had recognized her as master not long ago and seize the gray light!

That mouthpart, who knew what demon beast's attack method it was, could actually resist the gray light's refining intent for a short time.

If this stalemate truly continued, then perhaps...

"Damn it!"

"In the end, I am still too weak!"

Luo Chen's heart was unwilling, and he clenched his fists tightly.

Even though he had defeated the enemy Feiyunzi, he could not withstand Moon Wanderer's intervention.

In this way, the opportunity he obtained in Bright Radiance Heaven would be greatly discounted.

Not only had he failed to obtain the heaven-reaching spirit treasure, even this inexplicable gray light would change hands.

If one counted the Five-Spirit Dao Body he had obtained then lost, the destroyed second Origin Pill, the heavily injured Heavenjade, and the Black King whose life and death were unknown...

Luo Chen's breathing grew heavy.

"How can you be distracted?"

An eerie, distant voice came from above the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Luo Chen was startled and subconsciously urged what little mana remained in his Golden Core.

But it was already too late!

In the void, the three-cun Nascent Soul bound by five-colored ribbons raised the golden bell.

With absolute resolve, he spat a stream of mana onto the golden bell.

The golden bell, already covered in cracks, suddenly expanded again, forcibly pushing open the five-colored ribbons.

Only a clang was heard!

The golden bell!

Under Luo Chen's eye-splitting gaze, it split open with a crash.

The might of a true artifact's self-detonation formed a small storm, centered on Feiyunzi's Nascent Soul and pushing out in every direction.

Luo Chen was the closest and suffered a heavy blow.

His enormous body staggered continuously backward under the storm's push.

His lifebound magic treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, was blasted by the qi waves, spinning repeatedly in the air, faint cracks visible upon it.

Pfft!

With his lifebound magic treasure damaged, Luo Chen's divine soul suffered incomparable pain, and he involuntarily spat a mouthful of fresh blood.

But that fresh blood was instantly annihilated beneath the storm of the true artifact's self-detonation.

On the cloud-sea plaza, the two battlefields where Blood Wanderer and Han Zhan had been fighting also had no choice but to temporarily use methods to withstand this impact.

The former was especially dissatisfied.

"A withered bone in a grave can still let him overturn the heavens. This Azure Yang brat is truly useless!"

But how would he know that this was Luo Chen's first time using the Primordial Unity Cauldron's power to seize a cultivator's Nascent Soul, and after undergoing fleshly nirvana, his vast mana had strangely disappeared, causing him to drive the Primordial Unity Cauldron poorly.

Add to that...

Of course, even if Blood Wanderer knew these things, he would only scold Luo Chen for insufficient battle experience.

At this moment, Luo Chen had no mind to argue.

He was both shocked by Feiyunzi's resolve in self-detonating a true artifact and furious at his own distraction and carelessness.

"You are seeking your own death!"

He shouted fiercely, and his body abruptly stopped in the air.

Blood-colored source power suddenly erupted from him. In an endless surge, it condensed into a huge blood claw like an eagle's talon, charging against the current and tearing through the air to grab.

Cloud-Seizing Divine Claw!

This most practiced move, added to the backlash brought by Feiyunzi's self-detonation of a true artifact, and with him at the storm's center...

At this moment, Feiyunzi was already at the end of the road.

The blood-colored divine claw ignored the storm's impact and feared no cuts from the golden bell fragments. It plunged straight into the eye of the storm and grabbed at the three-cun little man whose face was like gold paper.

The little man was still resisting stubbornly.

A sword pellet madly emitted sword qi, piercing countless holes through the incoming blood-colored divine claw.

But still... it was futile!

Luo Chen laughed savagely, ignoring the intense pain, and forcibly gripped the sword qi and the three-cun Nascent Soul within his giant claw.

Then he squeezed violently...

"Father!!!"

A shrill scream rang through heaven and earth as Luo Chen's claw closed.

Before Luo Chen's ferocious, vicious smile had faded, he raised his head fiercely and saw before the gate of the Light Palace in the sky a white-skirted young girl wearing a golden crown, covering her mouth and watching this scene with eyes full of tears.

At the same time.

The sound of another palace gate opening rumbled.

From within the Dark Palace, a young man in black fitted clothes walked out, his appearance sixty to seventy percent similar to Feiyunzi.

Separated by several hundred li, he stared fixedly at Luo Chen.

When the Light and Dark Palaces opened, it meant Bright Radiance Heaven's inheritance missions had reached their end.

The Pill, Servant, Light, and Dark Palace inheritors had all successfully accepted their inheritances.

Even though an accident had occurred on Luo Chen's side, ended was ended.

The spirit-gathering grand formation that had lasted twenty years suddenly reversed at this moment.

Bright Radiance Heaven lost its light source.

The suspended thirteen palaces, along with another half-ruined Pill Palace, slowly fell like dead stars.

At this moment, Bright Radiance Heaven was shrouded by night, turning into Night Bright Radiance.

And the cloud-sea plaza, amid darkness, rumbled upward.

The teleportation formation had activated!

Luo Chen withdrew his hand and recalled his lifebound magic treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, with a thought.

Yet from the corner of his eye, he discovered a gray light chasing after the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

"This..."

"That belongs to me!"

A cold, eerie female voice drifted over.

Moon Wanderer actually ignored the cloud sea's teleportation, determined to take the gray light.

With a Great Cultivator charging over, Luo Chen's face turned iron-blue. The muscles throughout his body tensed incomparably, and he had already prepared for a desperate fight.

"Retreat!"

Han Zhan's voice entered his ear.

Luo Chen understood intuitively. He swallowed the shrunken Primordial Unity Cauldron, together with the gray light mixed into it, while rapidly retreating.

Opposite him, a gigantic black shadow carrying the might of wind and thunder brushed past his shoulder, opened its mouth, and spat out a huge ball of energy light, blasting from afar toward Moon Wanderer as she pursued.

Luo Chen saw clearly that the black shadow was a tiger.

A tiger?

Before he could think further, Han Zhan had already reached his side.

"Go!"

*(End of Chapter)*
