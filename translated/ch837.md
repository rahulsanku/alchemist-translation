# Chapter 837: Millennia-Aged Five Temple Immortal Brew, the Heavenly Capital Domain Boils

The Absolute Spirit Domain!

No!

At that time, that region was still called the Overlord Blade Domain!

After Luo Chen had finished refining his mana and thoroughly stabilized his cultivation base, he had once sought out a secluded place beyond the pass to experiment with various techniques.

Unintentionally, his divine sense had extended to its maximum range, thereby perceiving some of the situation in the Overlord Blade Domain.

At that time, a white-robed, cold-natured demonic cultivator, along with another Great Demon Emperor, had simultaneously discovered his divine sense probing.

The other person, Luo Chen had later learned, was Seven Rhinoceros, one of the Nine Great Commanders.

But that white-robed demonic cultivator, Luo Chen had never seen appear to this day, and he did not even know his name or origin.

Yet he still remembered the feeling of a natural enemy that the other had given him!

That feeling of a Sage's cowardice, of a soul trembling, Luo Chen could not avoid even from several thousand li away.

Clearly, that was an extremely dangerous existence.

Adding to that a certain rumor that had circulated among Sage-level experts for over a hundred years, combined with the successive strange situations now, Luo Chen began to match them up.

And his drastically changed expression also fell into the eyes of the other two at the first moment.

Sage Golden Feather was still somewhat uncertain, but upon seeing Luo Chen's reaction, he could not help but directly ask: "Luo Chen, do you already know who is behind this?"

Luo Chen raised his eyes. "Golden Feather, you should also have an idea in your heart!"

Fairy Wind and Moon showed dissatisfaction. "What riddle are you two playing? Just say it directly!"

Golden Feather's expression was somewhat fearful. He took a deep breath and slowly said: "Do you know why, back when my Five Elements Divine Sect was destroyed, the Supreme Elder, Sage Divine Origin, did not make a move?"

"Wasn't it because Sage Divine Origin was injured in the Green Parasol Mountain battle, so he couldn't make a move?" Fairy Wind and Moon said this, subconsciously glancing at Luo Chen.

Golden Feather also made the same gesture, but the answer he gave was slightly different.

"No matter how severe the injury, it wouldn't have been enough to make him sit idly by and watch his disciples be slaughtered, even forced to abandon a stronghold he had managed for seven hundred years. What truly made Senior Brother Divine Origin wary was someone else!"

"According to what Senior Brother Divine Origin said, at the time there was a ghostly thing secretly spying, bringing him an immense sense of threat. The other party didn't move, so he didn't move either, which led to the tragic result of two of our five peak True Men dying and one being injured."

"And if my guess is not wrong, the ghostly thing that Senior Brother Divine Origin described is definitely related to the current situation of the True Men from the two great upper sects, Wind and Cloud and Die Yun, dying tragically."

"Perhaps, it is even the same person!"

Fairy Wind and Moon's eyes showed disbelief, and she murmured: "What kind of existence could make even a Grand Cultivator so extremely wary?"

As for Luo Chen, his expression was strange.

That thing had indeed made him feel like a thorn in his back, bringing a sense of threat, but it wasn't to the point of not even having the courage to fight!

Especially since Old Thief Divine Origin was a late-stage Sage Grand Cultivator!

What Golden Feather said was too exaggerated.

He suppressed the doubts in his heart, cleared his throat, and said: "In any case, among the enemies, there is something specifically targeting us Sages. If we encounter it, we must each be careful during battle."

"What the Alchemy Sect says is very true!" Fairy Wind and Moon acknowledged. The anger brought by her junior sister's death earlier gradually calmed down.

Golden Feather lightly hummed in agreement. Although he was not as confident as before, he had not lost his courage either.

In the end, he and Luo Chen were both powerful existences who had achieved the feat of killing a Demon Emperor of the same rank head-on.

When they had not truly encountered an opponent they could not defeat, they would not be afraid before the battle even started!

...

After a brief exchange, the three did not stay long at Die Yun Sect and continued chasing in the direction of the Seven Hollows Domain.

However, unlike the full-speed flying at the beginning, the three's flying speed had noticeably decreased.

This way, they could conserve mana and face any battle that might break out at any time in a relatively better state.

During the journey, the three did not communicate again but instead spread out their divine senses to fully guard their surroundings.

At this moment, Luo Chen felt a bit of regret.

He had been somewhat careless when setting out.

If he had brought both of his divided souls, that would have been his peak state. Now, he was a bit inferior.

But fortunately, it was not too late to mend the fold after the sheep is lost. At least he had gone to Bai Li Qingchuan to bring Black King along.

With Black King's assistance, whether in battle or travel, it would be much more convenient for him.

Moreover, there was even an unexpected surprise!

"Master, a total of eighty jars of spirit wine. Among them, the Five Temple Immortal Brew you mentioned amounts to fifty jars, most of which are above a millennium in cellar age. The remaining spirit wines all have names attached, and they are clearly not ordinary products."

Black King's voice entered his ears.

Luo Chen's expression was calm, but his heart was already pleasantly surprised.

Earlier, when they arrived at Die Yun Sect, in order to investigate how the demon clan had broken the formation, he had released Black King.

Black King had the talent of absorbing spiritual energy, so he naturally knew how to distinguish mana. That was why he could help Luo Chen clearly identify the Demon Emperor mana auras left on those broken formation foundations.

It was also during the investigation process that Black King discovered a cellar whose seal had been broken because Die Yun Sect was destroyed.

Large quantities of top-grade spirit wine were stored there!

These spirit wines were not the cheap stuff that Die Yun Sect used to fool the world, but rather tribute that even the Nether Origin Sect would regularly send people to collect!

The Five Temple Immortal Brew that Elder Lu of Shouyang Mountain had mentioned earlier was stored in large quantities there.

Although Luo Chen had not yet inspected the specific quality, based on what Black King said, its age was over a thousand years, making it definitely the finest of wines!

With the three True Men of Die Yun Sect dead in battle, its decline was already inevitable.

Rather than letting these spirit wines benefit others, it was better to take them conveniently and use them to help Luo Chen temper his body.

"I'll have the opportunity later to look after the disciples of Die Yun Sect a bit, as compensation!"

Thinking this, Luo Chen felt no guilt in his heart.

That was how things were in the world. If he didn't take them, others would. To possess a heavy treasure without the power to protect it was like a child carrying gold through a busy market—it was a crime to possess such jade.

By taking it himself, he saved them from unnecessary trouble.

Three streaks of light, like shooting stars, streaked across the sky. A day later, they set foot in the Seven Hollows Domain, where the Seven Hollows Mountain was located.

Unlike when they first arrived at Die Yun Domain, where many small forces were rushing toward Die Yun Sect,

this time, the situation was the opposite.

A large number of cultivators, like locusts, were fleeing outward, as if something terrible was happening inside.

As for what could make them feel terrible?

The answer was self-evident.

Before a collapsed great mountain, three figures stared silently at the ruins of six mountain peaks still burning with fierce fire in the distance.

After a long time, Fairy Wind and Moon spoke with a hoarse voice: "If Mr. Medicine saw this scene, he would probably be utterly disheartened!"

These seven empty, clean, and natural great mountains were precisely the foundation of the Seven Hollows Mountain.

At its peak, each of the seven mountains even had a Sage, and its prestige was in no way inferior to ancient sects like East Sun Sect.

Unfortunately, as time passed, the Seven Hollows Mountain gradually declined.

At that time, Mr. Medicine, named Yao Ping, was still a core disciple of the Red Cinnabar Peak. He had outstanding talent and great prospects on the Dao.

In order to save the declining trend of the Seven Hollows Mountain, he resolutely devoted himself to the Dao of alchemy, attempting to use the power of pills to restore the glory of the Seven Hollows Mountain.

For this, he had to slow down his cultivation speed and only barely advanced to the Sage realm at an advanced age.

But he had indeed done his utmost within his capabilities!

Relying on his attainments as a fourth-tier alchemist, he internally cultivated a newly promoted Nascent Soul Sage, and externally built a wide network of connections, even having strong experts like Deng Taiyue willing to be driven by him.

Given time, under his careful management, the Seven Hollows Mountain might not have been able to return to the ranks of the top sects of the Eastern Wilderness.

But now, everything was destroyed!

His limelight in the Dao of alchemy had already been snatched away by the newly rising Luo Chen, and now even the foundation of the seven mountains was gone.

Mr. Medicine himself was already very old. He would probably never live to see the day the Seven Hollows Mountain rose again.

"Let's go. Head to the Heavenly Extreme Domain!" Golden Feather said.

Without any pause, the three did not even alert the surviving cultivators of the Seven Hollows Mountain and hurriedly rushed to the next domain.

Luo Chen was interested in the various spiritual herbs that the Seven Hollows Mountain had collected over the years, but now was not the time.

...

The Heavenly Extreme Domain. This region was not particularly famous in the Eastern Wilderness.

But if you changed its name, everyone would know it.

Heavenly Extreme Chan Sect!

In this domain, there was a Sage-level upper sect, but it was not a Daoist sect; it was a Buddhist force.

The founder of the sect was Monk Huiping, rumored to be one of the one hundred and eight eminent monks of the Hanging Temple in the Western Desert.

After leaving the Hanging Temple, in order to spread the Dharma, Monk Huiping came alone to the Eastern Wilderness and, with the permission of the Nether Origin Sect, occupied a small domain and established the Heavenly Extreme Chan Sect.

Here, everywhere were bald-headed monks.

There were even female cultivators tired of the mortal world who joined the Buddhist faith, establishing nunneries and becoming so-called nuns.

According to Monk Huiping's plan, by gradually promoting Buddhist concepts and propagating the Dharma, he would sooner or later gather a large number of believers in the Eastern Wilderness.

But all of this depended on him being alive, suppressing one side with his Grand Cultivator power!

In the Green Parasom Mountain battle, Monk Huiping and the Joyous Union Ancestor died in battle on the spot.

Without him, this top-tier expert, the Heavenly Extreme Chan Sect, which had no deep foundation to begin with, was left leaderless, unable to even produce a Sage-level expert.

Even though, under the care of the Nether Origin Sect, this domain was still named Heavenly Extreme and no other major force forcibly interfered, in reality, it had long become chaotic.

Many low-level cultivators, coveting the resources of the Chan Sect, pretended to become monks, but in reality, they only sought to take.

As for the Dharma, with people's hearts in chaos, how could there be a Buddha's heart to speak of?

According to the plan of the three, Golden Feather, the leaderless Heavenly Extreme Domain would be unable to resist the Demon Emperor invasion and might have already become a hell on earth.

But when they actually set foot in the Heavenly Extreme Domain, what they saw and heard was a scene of "peaceful times," with no sign of a great battle having occurred.

In a Chan forest full of birdsong and floral fragrance, three figures walked slowly.

Spreading out their divine senses, they glimpsed inside a monk's room. A pot-bellied monk had tightly closed the door and was painstakingly refining a blood-red ring-blade, with resentful souls occasionally howling around him.

Fairy Wind and Moon withdrew her divine sense and sighed: "Where is this still a pure land of Buddhism? It is clearly a place harboring filth!"

Golden Feather sneered: "In this world, strength is paramount. When Heavenly Extreme Huiping had strength, he could promote his faith and propagate the Dharma. Now that he is dead, how could the world believe in a Dharma that cannot even protect itself?"

Fairy Wind and Moon nodded: "What you say is correct, Daoist friend. We seek immortality, but we only ask about the sword in our hand. If, as the Buddhists think, we should be without desires or demands, then cultivating to seek immortality is the greatest greed. This self-contradictory faith is simply laughable!"

Saying this, she looked at the silent Luo Chen.

"Alchemy Sect, what do you think?"

Luo Chen raised his head, his expression indifferent: "I am not good at debating scriptures, nor do I want to criticize the truth or falsehood of faith. I only know that anything that helps my great Dao of immortality is a good thing."

"Your principle is quite flexible." Golden Feather twitched the corner of his mouth, then asked seemingly casually: "You were also present during the great battle at Green Parasol Mountain. Did you witness the battle situation firsthand? What kind of things did the three Grand Cultivators face, resulting in two deaths and one injury?"

What kind of things?

Luo Chen glanced at him and fobbed him off by saying that his cultivation base was too low at the time to see the true face of the great battle.

But in his heart, he could not help but think of that golden flame that had spread across the wilderness, burning everything.

That kind of overbearing flame, even the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire, which had already advanced to the fifth rank, was still far inferior!

Fairy Wind and Moon suggested:

"Let's rest for a while!"

"After many days of travel, our mana has been somewhat depleted."

"Since nothing has happened to this Heavenly Extreme Chan Sect, it is clear that the group of Demon Emperors led by Splendid Embroiderer truly have the Heavenly Capital Sect as their goal. The battle might be tomorrow; we cannot be negligent."

Luo Chen and Golden Feather had no objections. They restrained their auras and each found a place with abundant spiritual energy to begin recovering their mana.

Inside a resplendent Chan room.

Luo Chen glanced at the sleeping Adept-stage monk and assumed a meditative posture.

But he was not actually meditating. The past few days of full-speed travel had indeed depleted his mana, but he often held a top-grade Hongyuan Pill in his mouth, so it had hardly any effect.

This rest was merely a breather before the great battle.

In his heart, however, he was pondering the true intentions of the group of Demon Emperors led by Splendid Embroiderer.

As Fairy Wind and Moon said, at first glance, their goal seemed to be to destroy all the Sage-level forces along the way, but that was not the case.

Their true intention should be the Heavenly Capital Sect!

The Heavenly Capital Sect was the top formation grand sect in the Eastern Wilderness. Even the great formation of Lingtian Pass was presided over by the Heavenly Capital Sect's Supreme Elder, Heavenly Capital Master.

If the Heavenly Capital Sect was destroyed, how much heart would Heavenly Capital Master have left to hold Lingtian Pass?

And the targets of the other two routes should also be famous top-tier sects.

For example, the Void Emperor Sect, for example, the East Sun Sect!

As long as these targets were taken down, the many cultivators at Lingtian Pass would surely be panicked and have no will for a prolonged battle.

"To be honest, this method of taking advantage of the fact that the strong experts of various sects are rushing to support Lingtian Pass, leaving the rear empty, and deliberately harassing the rear, is more like our sudden attack on the Absolute Spirit Domain back then, or like Deng Taiyue taking advantage of the demon beasts pouring out of their nests to go on a sweeping spree for resources in the Million Great Mountains."

"But, aren't they afraid of the Nether Origin Sect making a move?"

Regarding the upcoming battle, Luo Chen and the others still had some confidence.

This confidence was not only based on their own strength but also on the Lingtian City Lord's statement that the Holy Land would make a move.

Otherwise, how would they dare to rashly chase for ten thousand li?

As long as this Demon Emperor unit's speed of destruction slowed down, with three mid-stage Sage cultivators blocking their retreat from behind and the strong experts of the Nether Origin Holy Land encircling them from the front, it was not impossible to annihilate them completely.

At the very least, they could drive these Demon Emperors out.

After resting for a moment, Luo Chen quietly rose and went outside to meet with Fairy Wind and Moon and Golden Feather, then flew toward the Heavenly Capital Domain.

And in that Chan room, the monk woke up faintly, looking around in panic, but found nothing.

He had no idea what had happened earlier.

Only a pair of light golden pupils seemed to be imprinted deep in his heart.

"Although this is a blessed land, anyone can come and go freely."

"This Chan Sect, I can't stay any longer!"

The monk was terrified, packed his things, and planned to run away.

...

What does it mean to be at a critical moment of life and death? What does it mean to be at a time when a great mansion is about to topple?

It is like this moment, like the present!

Inside the Heavenly Capital Sect, the four Sage True Men, who had long received a message from the Supreme Elder, had, a few days ago, desperately activated the mountain-protecting grand formation.

And what would it look like for a top-tier grand sect, famous for its formations, to be fully activated?

With a towering Heavenly Pillar Mountain at its center, the light of the mountain-protecting formation began to spread outward.

Wherever it passed, formation lights rose one after another, echoing each other.

Large formations contained small formations; small formations formed large formations. It stretched as far as the eye could see, with no edge in sight.

Vast spiritual energy was extracted from the earth veins by the giant formation, and the entire Heavenly Capital Domain, tens of thousands of li in radius, began to boil.

This was a rare sight in the world!

This was also the millennia-deep foundation of the Heavenly Capital Sect!

It was not just defense. Countless formations also contained many killing formations, all ready and waiting under the urging of the hundreds of thousands of disciples of the Heavenly Capital Sect!

Until seven figures stepped into the Heavenly Capital Domain.

The earth began to tremble, the sky began to roar. Boundless spiritual energy transformed into terrifying killing moves, surging wave after wave toward the seven Demon Emperors.

After waving his hand to scatter a giant tree, a Daoist with a gourd-shaped topknot and wearing colorful floral robes had a bitter expression.

"Commander, this Heavenly Capital Sect is more troublesome than we imagined!"

Behind him, two Daoists, also wearing colorful floral robes and gourd-shaped topknots, also had faces full of worry.

The leader was a beautiful woman in a palace dress.

Her expression was calm as she looked at the attacks surging in from all around, without showing any surprise.

"The Heavenly Capital Sect also participated in the ancient great war. Although not very famous, it has a deep foundation. Over three thousand years, it has naturally managed this domain like an iron bucket, as solid as gold."

"You three brothers don't need to worry about these attacks. We will block them for you."

"Your only task is to find the meridians of the great formation and locate the formation eye!"

"Speed is of the essence. As long as the Heavenly Capital Sect is destroyed, we can retreat."
