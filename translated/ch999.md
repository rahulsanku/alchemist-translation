# Chapter 999: Mastering the Nether, Swarming Out

"It's that guy!"

The moment the Azure Yang Great Handprint appeared, Yan Qianji instantly recognized its user.

It was the nightmare that had brought him ten years of suffering!

From his panicked cries, the others roughly understood that the newcomer might be the same expert who had severely wounded Yan Qianji a decade ago. But under the crushing pressure of the handprint, there was no time to think further.

Each of them unleashed their ultimate techniques—some to wear it down, some to counterattack, some to defend with all their might—everything to block that heaven-shaking palm print.

In an instant, the foot of Yanfu Mountain was filled with ear-splitting explosions.

Boom! Boom! Boom! Boom! Boom! Boom! Boom!

Seven blasts erupted simultaneously.

The terrifying shockwave even flipped over the prostrate Mystic Turtle.

If Bai Meiling hadn't been protected by that Xuanhuang Seal, she would have been obliterated, body and soul.

The violent tremors shook heaven and earth, transmitting all the way up the mountain.

It seemed the mountain's inhabitants heard the commotion as well. Wisps of green ghost-fire gazed down toward the mountain's foot, but the residual restrictions made it hard to see clearly.

They couldn't see clearly, but the participants in this battle saw everything with perfect clarity.

Streams of scattered ghost qi gathered together amidst howling winds, reforming into their original bodies.

But compared to before, they had thinned out far too much.

Even Yan Qianji, already severely wounded by Wang Yuan earlier, could no longer coalesce his ghostly form.

Seven pairs of eyes snapped upward.

A handsome young man in resplendent feathered robes stared down at them coldly.

His eyes were deep and profound, making one involuntarily sink into them.

His gaze was brilliant, as if it could pierce the Nine Nether, daring none to meet it directly.

His aura was so overwhelming that even the yellow-robed man beside him was unconsciously overlooked.

Gasps escaped the mouths of the Ghost Emperors.

"Who is he?"

"How could the Nether Abyss have such a powerful being?"

"One palm—just one palm—and it destroyed the protective treasure I worked so hard to find!"

Their exclamations were filled with terror.

Because none of the seven Ghost Emperors had managed to withstand that palm strike aimed at each of them.

This meant the feathered-robed man's strength surpassed not just any single one of them, but all of them combined!

Just then!

The cold man finally spoke.

"Trying to run?"

He reached out and made a grasping motion in the void, as if seizing something.

From a certain patch of empty space, a hazy figure stumbled out. No matter how the surrounding ghost mist tried to gather around him, he couldn't break free from the overwhelming pressure.

He shrieked, "Spare me! Spare me!"

It was Yan Qianji!

The Ghost Emperors hadn't expected that his inability to coalesce his soul body was merely a feint.

In truth, he had already secretly withdrawn, trying to escape this place.

Such caution and cunning—truly worthy of Yan Qianji, who had originated from an old softshell turtle.

But what terrified them most was that none of them had noticed, yet the feathered-robed man had detected it in advance and even forcibly dragged him out.

What kind of profound soul foundation did that require?

The next moment.

The feathered-robed man's lips twitched slightly, then he uttered softly.

"Too late!"

His right hand clenched suddenly.

Yan Qianji's screams cut off abruptly, leaving only a wisp of green smoke dissipating into Yanfu Mountain.

Massive amounts of ghost qi, bereft of soul cohesion, began to surge and howl in the surroundings.

Seeing this scene of death-dealing judgment, the six surviving Ghost Emperors were all terrified.

Ghost cultivators abandoned their physical bodies, focusing on cultivating their yin souls, using vast quantities of ghost qi to form their shapes.

Though this method had many drawbacks—slow cultivation, fear of extreme yang and rigid forces, weak attack power—it also had benefits that ordinary cultivators couldn't enjoy.

For instance, they had strong immunity to physical attacks like magic treasures, true artifacts, spells, and martial strikes.

After all, even their forms were composed of ghost qi; if scattered, they could simply reform quickly.

But now, this advantage had become utterly meaningless before this man.

His mana was majestic, upright, supremely yang, supremely rigid!

His spells carried a wisp of cyan flame, and the moment that cyan flame touched their soul bodies, it released vigorous life force, rapidly eroding the death qi, ghost qi, and even yin qi they relied upon.

"This man is too strong—we cannot fight him head-on!"

This thought flashed through the minds of the six Ghost Emperors simultaneously.

The next moment!

Someone struck, summoning a black jiao dragon with bared fangs and claws, charging at Luo Chen.

Someone fled without hesitation toward the outside.

Someone else fled upward, their entire body dissolving into mist, making it impossible to track their true form.

They scattered like startled birds!

The feathered-robed man snorted coldly, raised both hands, and uttered softly.

"Go!"

A fire phoenix soared up from behind him, extinguishing the incoming black jiao dragon, incinerating the slowest Ghost Emperor, then, with undiminished power, pounced on the remaining enemies.

In just a few breaths, successive screams rang out from the foot of Yanfu Mountain.

Finally, the fire phoenix, its power greatly reduced, chased after the last one, pursuing them up the mountain.

Luo Chen paid no attention to the final outcome. His gaze swept over a distant figure that had reformed its soul body and quickly vanished, and his expression darkened.

"These ghost cultivators are truly hard to kill!"

Beside him, Deng Taiyue, clad in yellow Daoist robes, opened his mouth but didn't know how to respond.

One move severely wounded seven Ghost Emperors!

One move killed five ghosts, with only one escaping Yanfu Mountain and one fleeing up the mountain, life or death unknown.

With such results, you say they're hard to kill?

What more do you want!

In the end, he could only force out five words.

"Fellow Daoist is too modest!"

Then, Deng Taiyue reached out and beckoned. The Xuanhuang Seal that had been shielding Bai Meiling shrank, flew spinning into his hand, and vanished in an instant.

It was he who had acted earlier to protect Bai Meiling.

"Thanks."

Luo Chen said his thanks while looking at the woman floating over.

"Xiao Bai, are you alright?"

"Master!" Bai Meiling blinked her lively, intelligent eyes, overjoyed. Those two simple words seemed to contain a thousand unspoken sentiments.

Thump!

A dull sound came from below.

The Mystic Turtle, which had been flipped over earlier, had finally managed to right itself.

Wang Yuan stood below, looking up at Luo Chen.

"You're back."

Luo Chen nodded in acknowledgment. "Yes, I'm back!"

Suddenly, he looked up toward the summit of Yanfu Mountain, a fierce glint flashing in his eyes.

The others didn't understand why.

But soon, they heard a muffled sound coming down from above, carrying with it the mournful cry of the fire phoenix before it dissipated.

Someone had struck, breaking Luo Chen's Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain technique!

"There's an expert on the mountain!" A fierce light flashed in Luo Chen's eyes, but the corners of his mouth involuntarily lifted.

Bai Meiling's joy at seeing Luo Chen again vanished instantly. Her face filled with anxiety as she said, "Master, we must stop them quickly. The Netherwater Spring Eye is about to condense. If those Ghost Emperors get their hands on it, our Luo Tian Sect will have no foothold left in the Nether Abyss!"

"Oh?" Luo Chen raised an eyebrow. "Tell me the details!"

Bai Meiling nodded and began to recount the specific situation in detail.

...

Inside the Dan Saint Blessed Land.

The high-ranking members of Luo Tian Sect were gathered together, worry written on every face.

At the head, Li Yingzhang spoke solemnly. "Six months!"

He looked around at everyone, his tone heavy yet carrying a hint of resolve.

"Elder Bai and Elder Wang have been gone for six months now, and we've received no news from them. We must organize a force to go and support them!"

Below sat several people, all familiar faces.

Ling Fengzi sat on the left, Canglong on the right—the two remaining Sage-level elders of Luo Tian Sect.

Next was Gu Caiyi in her palace dress, and Heavenjade. One was the Dao companion of Supreme Elder Luo Chen, the other his former spirit pet. Both could represent a portion of the Supreme Elder's will.

As for the Supreme Elder's other Dao companion, Sima Huiniang, she was inconveniently absent.

Rumor had it that before the Supreme Elder left, she had been ordered to remain confined for ten years.

Though the confinement period had nearly passed, she hadn't been seen much in public.

Further down sat the two "younger generation" members of Luo Tian Sect.

Luo Chen's disciple, Qu Lingjun, and Wang Yuan's disciple, Baleful Dragonzi, Zeng Yilong.

One had endured hardship and finally advanced to the Adept stage, overseeing all alchemy matters in Luo Tian Sect.

The other had achieved success in body refinement, managing the sect's punishments and patrols.

Seven people in total!

With Luo Chen absent and Wang and Bai unreachable, these seven were the core leaders of Luo Tian Sect.

Facing Sect Master Li Yingzhang's proposal, Ling Fengzi stroked his beard and spoke first. "The place they went to is Yanfu Mountain, which is infested with high-level ghost cultivators. Although our Luo Tian Sect has greatly increased its strength through years of recuperation, we are still far from a match for them. Sect Master Li, this proposal of yours is somewhat reckless. In my opinion, we should wait until Senior Brother Luo Chen returns!"

Li Yingzhang said coldly, "This is not a proposal—it's a decision!"

Ling Fengzi frowned. "But if the support force also meets with misfortune, what will Luo Tian Sect do then?"

Li Yingzhang countered, "And if a powerful Ghost Emperor seizes the Netherwater Spring Eye, where will Luo Tian Sect go from there?"

This question immediately silenced Ling Fengzi.

In recent years, news about the Netherwater Spring Eye had spread more and more fiercely among the countless ghosts of the Nether Abyss.

Legend had it that in ancient times, the Mountains and Seas Realm did not have the Nether Abyss.

This vast abyss, which imprisoned countless ghosts, had appeared only ten thousand years ago.

It was born from a single spring eye!

The reason the Nether Origin Sect had established its mountain gate here was twofold: one, the natural protection of the Nether Abyss, and two, that very spring eye.

A Ghost Emperor had once said that the Netherwater Spring Eye contained the power of creation!

The Nether Origin Sect had produced Ascendants without interruption for three thousand years, and its younger disciples had successively produced geniuses who comprehended the true meaning of laws ahead of time—all related to the Netherwater Spring Eye.

If Rosycloud hadn't suddenly attacked the Nether Origin Sect, forcibly dispersing the Netherwater Spring Eye to rescue Qinghe Immortal, the Nether Abyss wouldn't have fallen into such chaos.

Fortunately, Rosycloud hadn't focused all her power on that spring eye, so after it collapsed for just a few decades, it had gradually begun to condense again, relying on the residual restriction power of the three Yanfu Heavenly Palaces on Yanfu Mountain.

At this moment, the Netherwater Spring Eye was at its weakest and easiest to subdue.

Once subdued, one could master the Netherwater.

If the host themselves were strong enough, this vast Nether Abyss would also fall under their control.

At that point, the Dan Saint Blessed Land hidden within the Nether Abyss would be exposed and discovered instantly.

All the high-ranking members of Luo Tian Sect naturally understood this intelligence, which was why they were so restless.

Faced with this life-or-death decision for the sect, the usually gentle Sect Master Li Yingzhang had made a resolute determination.

Even Ling Fengzi, honored as a Sage elder, found it hard to defy him.

A gentle, warm voice rang out, like a clear spring soothing the agitation in everyone's hearts.

"Sect Master Li is right. Supporting Elders Wang and Bai is not only about protecting our fellow disciples but also about safeguarding the sect. This journey must be undertaken. Canglong is willing to act."

Everyone looked at the gentle woman who had spoken, and their hearts calmed considerably.

They weren't very familiar with Fairy Canglong.

But everyone knew that the Supreme Elder had saved her life and, with his support, she had broken through her bottleneck a second time and forcibly succeeded in forming her Sage core.

In a sense, she too could represent the Supreme Elder's will.

Some even thought she might become the Supreme Elder's third Dao companion!

Now that she had spoken in agreement with the Sect Master's decision, the others had no more hesitation.

"I second the motion!"

"I second the motion!"

Finally, Gu Caiyi, after careful consideration, also approved Li Yingzhang's decision.

Everyone turned their gaze to the old man who, though aged, still carried an elegant bearing.

Seeing this, Ling Fengzi could only smile bitterly.

"Since that's the case, this old man naturally has no objection either."

Li Yingzhang breathed a sigh of relief. "Then let us now discuss the composition of the support force! I propose that the two elders lead the team, and we dispatch all disciples above the Adept stage from within the sect to participate in the battle."

At this, Canglong, who had previously been quite supportive of Li Yingzhang, shook her head.

"Adepts may be strong, but on Yanfu Mountain, they are nothing but ants. In my view, only Adept-stage late-phase combat power can influence the battle situation to any degree. Additionally, Elder Min Longyu, who is at the early Adept stage, can also participate. With his formations, we can maximize our strength. Finally, if we fail, the remaining large number of Adepts can at least preserve some foundation for Luo Tian Sect."

She too had once led a sect; her grasp of the overall situation was no less than Li Yingzhang's.

Faced with this proposal, Li Yingzhang hesitated. "This..."

Ling Fengzi nodded. "Elder Canglong is right. You are not Sages, so you do not know the capabilities of Sages. Ordinary Adepts have little use in battles at the Sage level. Even Adept-stage late-phase cultivators are barely adequate."

Since both Sage elders had spoken, the others naturally wouldn't object.

Li Yingzhang was thoroughly familiar with everything in the sect, and he immediately named the support personnel.

"Min Longyu, Elder Heavenjade, Jueqing Fairy, Ye Suifeng..."

Apart from Min Longyu, the names called out were all of Luo Tian Sect's current Adept-stage late-phase experts.

Ye Suifeng was a genius cultivated by Ling Fengzi's Misty Peak over the years, and in his early days, he had been an outstanding true disciple of the Misty Sect.

However, when he reached the end of the list, Li Yingzhang seemed somewhat hesitant.

But while he hesitated, the person in question did not.

Under everyone's gaze, Gu Caiyi stood up gracefully.

"I have received the sect's grace and my husband's gifts. Three years ago, I already stepped into the Adept-stage late phase. Now, it is only right that I contribute to the sect!"

"But..."

"No buts!"

Canglong watched the bright, graceful woman and couldn't help but silently praise Luo Chen's good judgment!

This Dao companion, who had supported him from their humble beginnings, might not have outstanding talent and was somewhat jealous in daily life, but when it came to major matters of right and wrong, she was not selfish at all.

With her taking the lead, the others naturally had no objections.

"Very well, then. Gather the personnel and set out immediately!" Li Yingzhang sighed and made the final decision.

But even so, he couldn't suppress the worry in his heart.

Supporting Wang Yuan and Bai Meiling was secondary; seizing the Netherwater Spring Eye was the true priority.

But the combat power Luo Tian Sect had forcibly assembled was far too weak compared to the Nether Abyss's thousands of years of accumulation.

"I've had too little time!"

"If only I could have trained a few more Adept-stage late-phase grand cultivators, we wouldn't be so stretched thin now."

Amidst his sighs, his gaze shifted between Ling Fengzi and Canglong, and finally, he made a decision.

"Elder Canglong, please come with me for a moment!"

A moment later.

In the rear hall, Fairy Canglong stared in surprise at what Li Yingzhang had taken out.

"This!"

"I heard that Elder was once on good terms with the Falling Cloud Sect. I wonder if you have any knowledge of puppet arts?"

Fairy Canglong took a deep breath. She truly hadn't expected that Luo Tian Sect, established less than five hundred years ago, possessed such hidden resources.

Reaching out, she accepted the heavy storage pouch and nodded firmly.

"I will make good use of this!"
