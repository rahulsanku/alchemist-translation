# Chapter 1111: Hunyuan Perfection, World Rejection

"Kill!"
"Fight into the Eastern Wilderness, annihilate all those who once dared to invade our sect!"
"The name of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect cannot fall in our hands—we must take revenge!"
"I agree with Elder Shi Ju's suggestion. Our Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's situation is now precarious; we must establish our authority and intimidate the world."
...
"Are you all mad? With that one guarding the Eastern Wilderness, going there is just seeking death! That one dared to challenge an Ascendant-level powerhouse at the Sage realm—who can defeat him?"
"Fu Qinglan, why beat around the bush? Just say Luo Chen directly. Since he dared to challenge our sect's powerhouse at the Sage realm, why can't we? Is the gap between Sages really greater than the gap between a Sage and an Ascendant? Or are you afraid, or perhaps your old feelings for him haven't faded?"
"Shi Ju, don't provoke me. Everyone knows—and has personally experienced—that Luo Chen suppressed us even while heavily injured. If you had that ability, you wouldn't have been utterly defeated by Nine Spirit Primordial Lord, without even a chance to face Luo Chen directly."
"You!"
...
"Enough, stop arguing. Today's gathering is to resolve the recent disputes among the various great houses, not to launch an external war. Moreover, our sect's vitality has been severely damaged, split in two. At least for a thousand years, we will have no strength left to wage war externally."
"The Temple Master speaks sense. Now that the Tiannan Continent has become incredibly chaotic due to your arrival, we must eventually discuss a plan."
"Hmph, I still insist on external warfare. This barren Tiannan Continent simply isn't enough for all our families to divide."
"Let's discuss this later. At the very least, we must first cultivate a new generation of experts. The ten true disciples of the Sexagenary Grand Competition, plus over thirty Sages, all perished in the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, nearly severing our sect's younger generation. If we continue fighting without restraint, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect will truly be destroyed in our hands."
"This..."
...
Within the Tiannan Dao Palace, in the Houtu Hall.
A graceful figure flew back in fury, and none of the surrounding disciples dared to approach her.
In recent years, Master Qinglan's temper had grown increasingly strange. While not exactly irritable or explosive, she was moody and unpredictable.
Often, while talking, she would suddenly think of something, and her mood would plummet, making people afraid to approach her easily.
Only Fu Qinglan herself knew the reason for all this.
Luo Chen!
The woman's eyes burned with extreme hatred as she stared into the void, as if she could pierce through millions of li to glare at that man.
Everything was because of Luo Chen!
Because of him, the sect's only Ascendant-level powerhouse had fallen.
Because of him, the True Lord of the Mystic Realm couldn't descend, and the last chance to unify the Mountains and Seas Realm had vanished.
And because of him, the Dongyuan Continent had been submerged by a world-destroying tsunami, forcing the surviving cultivators of various great houses to scatter to the Tiannan and Libei Continents—hence today's fierce arguments.
In truth, the fierce arguments were still manageable. The private struggles were the most terrifying.
The Tiannan Continent had always been the most resource-poor among the four continents split from the Central Continent. This was evident from the fact that only two of the Twelve Great Houses—the Zhan family and the Mo family—had settled here initially.
Both the Zhan and Mo families had suffered the heaviest losses during the great calamity of the Central Continent, leaving them with the weakest remaining strength.
Naturally, after the war, they could only settle in the most resource-barren places.
But now, even this single region had seen the influx of the Shi family and the Ke family.
For the peace of their clansmen and for future development, the four great houses, along with various Sage-level families, had begun internal struggles over resource competition.
This struggle had lasted eighty years, during which the number of cultivators who fell was no less than those lost in any war launched in the past.
Fortunately, in recent times, the intensity of the internal strife had finally begun to ease.
Whether it was because resource division had reached a certain point, or because the number of cultivators had been too greatly depleted in the infighting, they had finally arrived at a situation where everyone could sit at the same table and negotiate.
And all of this stemmed from that man!
Everyone knew that the best way to resolve the current situation was to launch an external war, redirecting the internal conflicts of the Tiannan Continent.
But the current Heavenly Origin Dao Sect was utterly incapable of sustaining a cross-continental war.
And the strength of the culprit was too despairing.
So despairing that anyone with a shred of sense couldn't muster even a thought of resistance.
Fu Qinglan's current anger, beneath this despair, was gradually turning into endless regret.
"If the Fu family hadn't brought him into the sect back then, would things have come to this?"
"If the Sect Master hadn't taken his life soul, would he have made his move?"
"Even with all those premises, if I had reported to the sect when I discovered his traces in the Burial God Valley where he suppressed the ghost deity, would the sect have been prepared when the Eastern Wilderness cultivators invaded?"
One question after another arose spontaneously.
Examining her conscience, the woman's heart grew more and more guilty. Her sharp nails had already pierced her palm, dripping fresh blood.
Suddenly.
"Who's there?"
Fu Qinglan released her divine sense, sweeping the surroundings.
A gentle woman walked in, her eyes filled with worry as she looked at her.
"So it's Miaoyin!"
Fu Qinglan murmured, her rigid body collapsing limply into her seat, curling up.
Shi Miaoyin watched this scene, her eyes full of heartache.
"Master, the sect's decline is not your fault. Why torture yourself like this?"
Fu Qinglan shook her head. "You don't understand. You don't understand anything."
Shi Miaoyin opened her mouth, then changed the question. "Little junior brother..."
"Don't mention him!"
Fu Qinglan instantly erupted in fury. The powerful Sage pressure directly sent Shi Miaoyin flying, crashing into the palace wall.
Plop!
Shi Miaoyin fell to the ground, spitting blood.
Fu Qinglan stared blankly at this scene. "I, I..."
Shi Miaoyin struggled to stand up. "Disciple takes her leave. Please take care of yourself, Master."
Fu Qinglan watched helplessly as her disciple left, her thoughts in chaos. Within her heart, an extremely contradictory and complex torrent of thoughts spread wildly, almost devouring her whole.
It was extreme hatred, frenzied resentment, complete guilt, and the most powerless despair.
Everything was devouring this pitiful woman.
Tears fell silently.
At some point, the woman had fallen asleep.
Perhaps this was also the best sleep she had truly had in eighty years.
In her dream, it seemed everything had returned to the time when they were preparing for the Grotto-Heaven Grand Competition, to the moment she first met Luo Chen.
...
The sound of Buddhist chanting echoed, moonlight fell like snow.
In the night, only a soft sigh was heard.
Luo Chen gazed at the woman sleeping soundly within, his mood quite complex.
But in the end, he did not reveal himself.
Some things—different paths, no common goals. No matter how good the previous relationship had been, if it obstructed his Dao, he would crush it without mercy.
All he could do was use an illusion realm, a single *Moonlight Heart-Cleansing Mantra*, to soothe her soul and prevent it from being completely devoured by inner demons.
But Fu Qinglan's future achievements would likely never rise again.
At the very least, Ascension was not in her destiny.
"Let it be."
Luo Chen turned and left, no longer concerning himself with the affairs of this place.
He had observed the current situation of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect and today's daytime meeting from the sidelines, seeing everything clearly.
From beginning to end, he had not interfered.
Even if the current Heavenly Origin Dao Sect gathered the strength of both the Tiannan and Libei regions, it would not be a match for the Eastern Wilderness.
Especially after a large amount of resources and inheritances had been plundered by the three great powerhouses of the Northern Sea, the sect's foundation was even more incomplete.
Even if they had the heart for revenge, it would be a thousand years later.
By then, although he would no longer be here, the Luo Tian Sect should have grown enough to face them.
If the Luo Tian Sect still couldn't face them by then, it wouldn't be Luo Chen's fault—it would only mean that mud couldn't be plastered on the wall.
Leaving the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect as a potential threat might actually give the Luo Tian Sect some pressure.
...
Amidst the starry night, crossing the vast ocean.
Luo Chen's figure descended upon a desolate island.
This island was extremely barren—no vegetation remained, no spiritual energy existed, only a vast expanse of white mist covering the surroundings.
Stepping into it, ripples of water could faintly be felt beneath his feet.
Looking down, it was clearly streams of black water flowing wantonly.
Luo Chen's gaze fell upon a certain spot, and his brow involuntarily raised.
"Is the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven unable to seal itself anymore?"
If he wasn't mistaken, this white mist was clearly caused by the leakage of mirage qi from within the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven.
And this black water was the most familiar of all—Netherwater!
After a moment's thought, Luo Chen roughly understood why this change had occurred.
Back during the Grotto-Heaven war, his clone had not hesitated to detonate the Netherwater spring eye, destroying the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard, one of the sect's three great spirit treasures, in mutual annihilation.
Although it had prevented Chess Saint Ke Lianshan from breaking through to Ascension, it had also caused tremendous damage to the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven.
"I wonder if the exaggerated time flow within, so different from the outside world, still exists?"
Luo Chen mused for a moment, but wasn't particularly concerned.
His purpose in coming to the Heaven-Earth Island this time was only one!
Heaven-Earth Peak!
Looking up, the number one peak of the Mountains and Seas Realm, which had weathered the ravages of war, still stood tall.
This mountain had stood in the Central Continent for eons, witnessing the changes of sun, moon, and stars, accustomed to the wind sweeping away the clouds.
Archaic beasts had fallen at its feet, and Ascendant powerhouses had ascended to immortality above its summit.
It had once served as a bridge of communication between the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect and the Mystic Realm.
The wonders of this mountain were countless, worthy only of the four words: "Reaching Heaven, Penetrating Earth."
"If I can refine it into my lifebound treasure, not only can I form a tripod-like balance, maintaining the equilibrium between the Mi Realm Mountain and the Yuan Magnetic Mountain, but I can also use the Heaven-Earth Peak's characteristic of absorbing world origin qi to give the Primordial Unity Cauldron similar traits, thus laying the most solid foundation for my lifebound treasure's future advancement and growth."
"Of course, without this mountain, and without the Star Gate, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect of the Mountains and Seas Realm and the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect of the Mystic Realm will completely lose their communication channel."
"Such a one-stone-three-birds affair—how could I not do it?"
Luo Chen hesitated no longer. He immediately formed hand seals and chanted incantations, striking out one restriction after another onto the Heaven-Earth Peak.
Collecting this mountain was very different from collecting the Yuan Magnetic Mountain and the Mi Realm Mountain.
The difference lay in the fact that this mountain had once been broken in half!
That was the work of Rosycloud.
That break had nearly destroyed the mountain's foundation.
If not for the great formation of the Liancheng powerhouse, which absorbed the spiritual mechanism of the Central Continent and nourished it with earth-origin dharma power, it might not have been restored to its original state.
Although it appeared restored, Luo Chen still had to be extremely careful.
If the collection was incomplete, it wouldn't just fail to aid the Primordial Unity Cauldron—it could also damage Luo Chen's lifebound treasure.
What Luo Chen was doing now was first using the mountain restriction among the seven great restrictions of Mountain, Water, Wind, Cloud, Obscurity, Brightness, and Void to stabilize this mountain. Then, he would convert all his dharma power into earth attribute, using dharma power containing earth-origin to refine it. Finally, he would reach the stage of collecting it with the Primordial Unity Cauldron.
This preparation process far exceeded that of collecting the Yuan Magnetic Mountain, and the effort required was not comparable to collecting the Mi Realm Mountain.
Time passed, bit by bit.
Several months went by.
The Heaven-Earth Peak, shrouded in mist and corroded by black water, gradually emitted a new radiance.
It faintly possessed some of the former grace of connecting heaven and earth, overlooking all the mountains of the world!
Having reached this step, the preparations were considered complete.
"Next is the true collection."
Luo Chen took a deep breath, soared into the sky, and at the height of the nine heavens, released the Primordial Unity Cauldron with a solemn expression.
The process was the same as before.
But unlike before, the current Primordial Unity Cauldron had undergone some changes in appearance.
Although it still maintained the round form of a three-legged, two-eared cauldron, a gray light permeated the cauldron's body, and beneath the gray light were distinct lines crisscrossing its surface.
That gray light seemed capable of suppressing everything, while the intersecting lines appeared to outline small worlds.
The Primordial Unity Cauldron itself had always possessed a considerable internal space, once serving as Luo Chen's tool for storing Han Zhan's Sage infant and Black King's physical body. After absorbing the Mi Realm Mountain, countless spaces of various sizes had been opened up within it.
But these spaces were not yet stable enough.
The addition of the Heaven-Earth Peak was meant to stabilize all of this!
"Collect!"
When the Primordial Unity Cauldron had expanded to its limit, Luo Chen let out a low shout.
As before, the scene repeated itself. The Heaven-Earth Peak beneath his feet, amidst rumbling tremors, gradually rose from the ground.
Finally, within the hazy gray light emitted by the Primordial Unity Cauldron, it was drawn inside.
The moment the Heaven-Earth Peak was collected, Luo Chen immediately extended his divine sense into the cauldron to observe its condition.
"Chaos opens, turbid qi sinks, achieving an incomparably heavy intent; clear qi rises, agile and lively. Countless internal spaces gradually take shape and stabilize..."
"Indeed, this is exactly the result I predicted!"
"As for extracting the soul and forming a spirit, condensing an artifact spirit, there's no rush for that. After all, Li Canghai spent several hundred years on it."
"Moreover, after refining these three spiritual mountains, this cauldron seems to have another wonderful use."
Luo Chen's gaze turned toward the azure sky, where a wisp of faint light broke through the void and automatically drilled into the cauldron.
Luo Chen recognized that faint light, and was more familiar with it than ninety-nine percent of the world.
It was Azure Firmament Qi!
One of the nine great firmament qi, standing alongside Red Firmament Qi, Purple Firmament Qi, and others.
"This is an unexpected bonus."
"Although my perfected *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon* can already condense sword seeds, greatly reducing the loss of sword qi, I still need to replenish it. Now that this Primordial Unity Cauldron can automatically attract the nine firmament qi, it saves me a lot of trouble."
An unexpected bonus, yet within reason.
Originally, the Yuan Magnetic Mountain of the Primordial Demon Sect had the function of attracting the nine firmament qi, which was why a large number of firmament insects that fed on firmament qi had gathered there.
Now that this mountain had been refined and truly integrated, the Primordial Unity Cauldron would naturally develop similar functions.
"There should be some other wonderful uses that need to be discovered bit by bit through nurturing and refinement."
Luo Chen let out a long breath of turbid qi. With this, his modification of his lifebound treasure could be considered complete for now.
What followed was truly preparing for ascension.
His gaze swept across the surroundings, the entire world entering his eyes.
He wouldn't be able to stay in this world much longer.
The moment he took away the Heaven-Earth Peak, the feeling of being bound by shackles grew even stronger. He could even sense that this world was beginning to reject him.
"Was Li Canghai in such a hurry to ascend because of this as well?"
Luo Chen thought silently, his gaze unconsciously falling upon the incomplete Heaven-Earth Island.
In that giant pit, large amounts of seawater and black water were beginning to converge, about to submerge the island.
Suddenly.
A glimmer of light shone brilliantly.
Luo Chen's gaze focused, and he let out a soft exclamation of surprise.
"Oh? What is that?"

(Chapter End)
