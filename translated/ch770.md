# Chapter 770: Troops to the Northern Sea, Here and Now!

"Is he leaving?"

Inside a quiet secret chamber, Fu Qinglan slowly opened her eyes.

Fu Chaosheng, who had only just entered, nodded.

Fu Qinglan pressed her lips together and asked faintly, "Is he going to find his Dao companion?"

Fu Chaosheng sighed. "Indeed. Previously, I only took him for a Northern Sea rogue cultivator, and the spirit pets and attendants following him also came from the Northern Sea. I never expected that he actually came from the Eastern Wilderness. If he had not taken the initiative to say it, I fear we would still be kept in the dark."

"Eastern Wilderness... Wilderness Wanderer..."

Fu Qinglan murmured Luo Chen's self-chosen Daoist title and could not help laughing softly.

So from the very beginning, Luo Chen had already stated his origins.

And thinking of how he had personally told her grandfather that he already had a Dao companion, Fu Qinglan could only sigh that the decision she had made earlier was correct.

Leaving aside whether Luo Chen was willing to marry into the Fu family, the mere fact that he already had wives and concubines meant she was destined not to share one husband with another.

"When does he leave?"

Fu Chaosheng said, "He has already reported to Elder Seven Lamps of Star Gate. At present, he has already left Heaven-Earth Peak. But before leaving the Central Continent, he still needs to return to the Wangwu Mountain Range. He will bring along his spirit pets and attendants from there, and at the same time hand over the large amount of cultivation resources I prepared for him earlier."

After hearing this, Fu Qinglan sighed. "He is still so thorough in his considerations..."

Afterward, she fell into a long silence.

Fu Chaosheng, standing beside her, did not say much either. Only, whenever his eyes looked toward his granddaughter, there was a sense of distance and unfamiliarity.

Ever since she came out of Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, she had changed a great deal.

Not only in cultivation realm, but also in temperament.

She had become much more mature.

Especially after entering Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak, this change had become even greater.

Of course, in Fu Chaosheng's eyes, this change was good. His lifespan was nearing its end, and he could not protect his granddaughter for a lifetime. If Qinglan could shoulder matters early, that was naturally what he most wished for.

Abruptly.

A yellowed beast-hide booklet appeared in the woman's hand.

"Send this book to him!"

Fu Chaosheng frowned. "Why give another gift? Although he paid fifty years of lifespan, the benefits he obtained from us are already many. Especially last time, according to your intention, I sent him the formation book and sword canon."

As he spoke, his gaze fell on the beast-hide booklet, and his expression could not help freezing.

"Netherpassage Collection? Isn't this your favorite chess manual?"

Fu Qinglan stroked the yellowed pages. The pages tanned from the hide of some unknown demon beast were so soft, and there was also some reluctance in her eyes.

But when she thought of Luo Chen's clumsy appearance when he had played chess with her back then, the corner of her mouth could not help lifting slightly.

"It is only a chess manual. It can be bought anywhere in the Central Continent, and to me now, it is even more a minor path. Give it to him as a farewell gift. I hope that when we meet again someday, his chess skill will not be so unbearable."

As she spoke, she lightly brushed her hand.

The Netherpassage Collection drifted to Fu Chaosheng.

Fu Chaosheng said nothing more this time. It was only an ordinary chess manual, and the Central Continent lacked this type of book least of all.

The Netherpassage Collection might be a little special, having been passed down from the upper realm, but that was all.

In the end, it was still only a miscellaneous book unrelated to Great Dao cultivation.

"I will have someone take the teleportation formation and bring the chess manual back to the family to hand to Luo Chen. As for your side, have the Sect Master and the ancestors made arrangements?"

Fu Qinglan smiled brightly. "Grandfather need not worry about me. Since I have already entered Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak, Qinglan has a Great Dao to look forward to. Once the remaining few people come out of the grotto-heaven, the sect will discuss rankings and distribute rewards."

"Then that should be soon."

Fu Chaosheng stroked his beard, looking forward greatly to that day.

And that day did indeed arrive quickly.

About half a year later, inside the great hall of Heaven-Earth Peak, two figures appeared at the same time.

One man and one woman. The man was Sage Dragon Abyss, and the woman was true disciple Mingyan.

The result was somewhat shocking.

Sage Dragon Abyss, whom many people had favored, had not successfully comprehended origin power. Instead, Mingyan, who came from the master-disciple lineage, had gained a great harvest!

The explanation at the time, according to Mingyan, was that Sage Dragon Abyss had suffered one sword from Luo Chen at Three-Rainbow Falls and carried injuries, causing his later sudden enlightenment to be difficult.

But in the eyes of those sharp-sighted representatives of aristocratic clans and great houses, Sage Dragon Abyss had clearly become a stepping stone for the master-disciple lineage.

Now, the master-disciple lineage had gained a prodigy who had comprehended origin power, and from now on, there was hope of revival.

As for the humble Liu family from which Sage Dragon Abyss came, its rising momentum had been cut off.

Even if they advanced to an aristocratic clan in the future, they would only be the most ordinary kind. Whether they could still keep their family standing a thousand years from now would be hard to say.

After them, Gu Yuan also came out.

And the result likewise surprised people.

Gu Yuan had not comprehended origin power!

One had to know that according to the true disciples who had come out earlier, Gu Yuan had not encountered any obstruction from beginning to end, and his Dao protector Gu Chen had protected him the whole way.

With things going so smoothly, he was instead inferior to Fu Qinglan, Gu Daosu, and Mingyan?

And what shocked people even more was that under these circumstances, the old Gu clan head, always strict in his style, did not blame Gu Yuan in the slightest, but instead praised him for doing well.

Such a contradictory matter left everyone unable to make sense of it.

But this doubt did not last long.

Because Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven began to close.

Beneath Heaven-Earth Peak, the clouds and mist in Passing River began withdrawing on a large scale, entering that great mouth.

Everyone left the great hall in unison and looked toward the grotto-heaven entrance.

There was still one person who had not come out!

Just as the grotto-heaven was about to close completely, a golden sun slowly floated out from the entrance.

It was Shi Ju!

Leaving aside everyone's congratulations, Shi Ju's eyes were like torches as they swept the surroundings. He did not see the person he wanted to see.

"Has he hidden?"

Just then.

An aged voice slowly rang out.

"The grotto-heaven closes. Return to Rotten Axe-Handle!"

Without thinking, everyone raised their escape lights and left Heaven-Earth Peak.

Heaven-Earth Peak, which had been bustling for a year, instantly fell quiet.

Countless people's attention all fell upon Qingyun Plaza in the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range.

Myriad rays of magnificent light, golden threads, and jade steps.

Figure after figure of disciples who had participated in this competition appeared one after another upon the jade steps.

Everyone looked upward until Sect Master Mo Shouzhuo appeared.

He waved his hand, and the child attendant behind him took out a scroll and slowly unfurled it.

"Ge Ling, ranked tenth in the Adept competition. Awarded ten thousand high-grade spirit stones, one mid-grade Daoist robe, five bottles of Qionghua Pills, and fifteen days of study in the Scripture Pavilion."

"Li Zizhuang, ranked ninth in the Adept competition. Awarded twenty thousand high-grade spirit stones, one mid-grade Daoist robe, ten bottles of Qionghua Pills, and fifteen days of study in the Scripture Pavilion."

"Liu Changfeng..."

One name after another was called, and each person, along with the family behind them, became excited.

Especially Liu Changfeng. For this junior from the humble Liu family to enter the top ten truly brought glory to his ancestors.

The rewards aside, the half-month of study inside the Scripture Pavilion was extremely precious!

Because after entering, one could not only search for suitable cultivation methods, but also learn some formidable spells. These things could even be passed down within one's family!

Sage Dragon Abyss's failure had already cast a shadow over the Liu family's future. With Liu Changfeng's supplement, some of the gloom was finally washed away.

Sage Dragon Abyss, standing outside the jade steps, looked slightly better.

Next, the ranking rewards for the Adept disciples were distributed one after another.

But starting from sixth place, those ahead were basically all disciples of Great Houses.

Only in second place was there an exception.

The second-place winner was surnamed Wang.

He came from the Bochuan Wang clan, which was the Wang family of the true disciple forced out of Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven by Luo Chen's sword.

This person was quite extraordinary. Alone with a single sword, he ranged through the outer region of the grotto-heaven and hunted large numbers of third-tier illusion beasts.

His brilliance was not inferior to the disciples of Great Houses at all!

But Sage Dragon Abyss no longer paid attention to any of this. His gaze rested only on the two rows at the top of the jade steps.

In the second row stood three people.

Gu Daosu stood in the middle, Shi Ju on the right, and the back of the woman on the left made his gaze extremely complicated.

Was his hundred years of tempering in the Northern Sea only to make her succeed?

Mingyan seemed able to feel the burning gaze behind her, but she did not move at all.

Shi Ju on the right, however, was rather restless, continuously looking left and right.

That person had not appeared!

Even at the time of this grand ceremony, he still had not appeared.

The representatives of the other families, as well as the Dao protectors of the true Sage disciples, had all arrived in full. Only Luo Chen was nowhere to be seen.

"Has he hidden?"

"You cannot hide. You wounded my lightning clone and ruined my grand plan of dual cultivation in lightning and fire."

"I will definitely come to collect this debt from you!"

As he murmured inwardly, he finally cast a gaze that wished to choose someone and devour them toward the graceful woman in front.

"Bitch!"

These two words were not hidden and were spat out in a low voice.

Fu Qinglan seemed not to notice and simply stood there, only straightening her back a little more.

She had once faced Shi Ju directly!

She had once seen how wretched he was under Luo Chen's hand!

Her former timidity had long since vanished like smoke.

Moreover, she was no longer the Fu Qinglan of before, but first place in the competition, and the first person of her generation to enter Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak for cultivation!

"Shi Ju comprehended origin power. Awarded Flamecloud True Robe and Taihe Pill, entry into Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak, and study of Upper Mystery Cavern-Bright Blazing Flame Scripture. In the Scripture Pavilion, he is granted the divine ability Haoyi Scarlet Firmament Qi!"

The child attendant's voice was clear and distant.

As soon as it rang out, everyone was shocked.

Even if it was within expectations, truly hearing this reward still made countless people envious.

However, Shi Ju himself had an incomparably gloomy expression.

It should not have been like this!

Clearly, according to the plan, he would have been able to choose both lightning and fire divine abilities to cultivate.

Within the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, this would absolutely have been treatment never before seen.

Everything had been ruined by that man!

Above the auspicious clouds, Mo Shouzhuo glanced at Shi Ju, whose emotions were showing outward, and could not help sighing.

"Using simultaneous cultivation of lightning and fire in an attempt to comprehend a supreme law. Even with a seedling cultivated from childhood, it still does not work! Especially this temperament..."

These words were not spoken to himself, but were part of a conversation.

Very soon, someone answered him.

"In terms of temperament, we did indeed protect Shi Ju too well. But the one who caused his failure was Fu Qinglan's Dao protector!"

Another voice joined in.

"Luo Chen? That boy is quite good. He was valued by Chu Yan and also comprehended the true meaning of the laws. In the future, perhaps he can become one of Heaven-Earth Peak's banners."

Toward this, Mo Shouzhuo did not agree.

"You are thinking wrongly. Luo Chen's path has already gone astray."

Gone astray?

The several fellow disciples did not refute Mo Shouzhuo. They could all see that the true meaning of the laws Luo Chen comprehended indeed did not match his own Dao.

At the very least, Luo Chen could not resonate with the origin qi of heaven and earth!

Mo Shouzhuo spoke again. "The experiment on Shi Ju ends here, unless he can comprehend lightning origin on his own."

After speaking, he no longer participated in the discussion of his fellow disciples.

Beside him, the child attendant read out the reward for the first true Sage disciple, Fu Qinglan.

"Fu Qinglan comprehended origin power. Awarded Sala True Robe and Taihe Pill, entry into Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak, and study of Azure Wood Supreme Spirit Transformation Scripture. In the Scripture Pavilion, she is granted the divine ability Great Heaven Creation Seal!"

"In addition, she may enter Ancestor Chunyuan's seat and cultivate for one hundred years!"

When these words fell, the crowd was shocked!

If the previous rewards were already generous, with the three before her as foreshadowing, they were no longer enough to surprise people.

But personal instruction from an Ascendant great power made this reward far too weighty.

Unlike the Ascendant sacred lands of the other four continents in the Mountains and Seas Realm, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect was not based on master-disciple inheritance, but primarily on family inheritance.

Even after joining the Dao Sect's mountain gate, most cultivation still depended on oneself.

The sect's teachings were more often lectures by high-level cultivators, treating all alike.

And now, Fu Qinglan, merely someone who had just entered the Sage realm, could actually receive one hundred years of personal instruction from an Ascendant great power!

Given time, she might not be unable to become a new generation Ascendant ancestor!

For a time, every person stared at Fu Qinglan's back with incomparable envy, wishing they could replace her.

Under everyone's attention, the woman bowed slightly and spoke in a clear voice.

"Thank you, Sect Master. Thank you, sect!"

In her heart, however, that white-robed figure flashed past. In truth, she should thank him even more!

At this point, the competition was completely over.

Mo Shouzhuo waved his hand, and the child attendant closed the scroll and withdrew.

The old man looked down upon the densely packed cultivators below the mountain, his expression calm.

But the words he spoke were shocking enough to split stone.

"Since our sect came to this world, ten thousand years have passed. Our predecessors endured hardship, opened territory and expanded land, and from countless Archaic beasts and demon races, seized this fertile land of the Central Continent for us to dwell and cultivate in."

"After antiquity, for three thousand years, our sect relied on the Central Continent's abundant resources to stand at the summit of the Mountains and Seas, looking proudly upon the four continents."

"But the vast Central Continent no longer has the strength to support our continued cultivation. Just as the Northern Sea is suffering demon chaos and has no leader, someone must stand forth to turn back the raging tide. This moment is precisely the best time for us to inherit our predecessors' will and carry the sect to greater glory."

"Therefore, troops to the Northern Sea, here and now!"

The matter of marching on the Northern Sea had already been communicated among the great families of the Central Continent before the competition.

At this moment, spoken from the Sect Master's mouth, it was thoroughly settled.

For a time, after Mo Shouzhuo's words fell, beneath Qingyun Mountain, responses rose like mountains roaring and seas surging.

"Troops to the Northern Sea, here and now!"

"Troops to the Northern Sea, here and now!"

"Troops to the Northern Sea, here and now!"

...

Within Wangwu Mountain.

Unlike the bustle of the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range, this place seemed especially quiet amid the crisp clicking of an abacus.

Luo Chen quietly watched as Heavenjade and the Fu family cultivators handed over cultivation resources filling more than a dozen storage bags.

The number of spirit stones involved had to be counted in the hundreds of millions.

After this, Luo Chen's savings were also mostly exhausted.

However, everything was worth it.

Next, even if Luo Chen left the Central Continent, he would not have to worry about the resources needed for cultivation.

Outside the door, hurried footsteps rang out.

It was Sang Jinghe.

He hurried inside, and when he saw Luo Chen, his worn face instead showed great joy.

"Sage, I found a method to cultivate the Demon-Devil Heart-Linking Tree!"

*(End of Chapter)*
