# Chapter 660: Spirits of Heaven and Earth, the Tai Sui Calamity

On the uneven cliff, an iron tree stood.

On the iron tree, branches, leaves, and vines spread everywhere, and at this moment, a bright, evil flower bloomed.

Looking carefully, how was it a flower? It was clearly a giant python flicking its tongue.

"Black King, well done!"

Hearing Luo Chen's praise, Black King hissed softly twice, seeming rather pleased with himself.

Correct. The Black Gold Viper [TERM?] that had swallowed Luo Chen was actually Black King transformed in disguise.

Luo Chen had never been stingy toward the two demons who followed him.

Whether magic treasures, pills, or some practical spells, he would teach them.

For example, the Concealment Spirit Art [TERM?] Luo Chen had used most frequently in his early days had been studied by Black King and Heavenjade very early.

The two progressed differently, but Black King showed extremely high talent in this area.

Perhaps it was related to his awakened lifebound demonic art, the devouring divine ability. Long ago, Black King had cultivated this art to a level comparable to Luo Chen's early Great Perfection, possessing the wonders of concealing aura and form and borrowing objects to transform qi.

This was also why Luo Chen had dared let Black King go to Purple Spirit Island alone to scout.

It was not only that Black King was stronger than Heavenjade, but also that he could better restrain his aura and disguise himself.

This time, while exploring this world in advance, Black King continuously activated his aura-concealment art.

After finding this group of third-tier vipers, he received Luo Chen's instructions, forcefully collected the relevant aura, disguised himself as a Black Gold Viper [TERM?], and mixed into the snake swarm.

Just as Luo Chen had once disguised himself as a demon wolf to explore the Howling Moon Mountain Range.

Moreover, Black King himself came from the python category and possessed natural advantages.

With this convenience, the later operation became even simpler.

During one instance of Cave Master Moyun "itching hands," Black King provoked the Black Gold Viper [TERM?] swarm into moving out on a large scale, chasing the three off course until they reached the territory guarded by the fourth-tier strange beast, the Nine Yang Golden Eagle [TERM?].

And Luo Chen's arrangement clearly did not stop there.

"Heavenjade, are you all right?"

Inside the spirit beast pouch, Heavenjade's light cough came.

"That golden eagle seems demon yet not demon, and does not look like a desolate beast either, but it is extremely powerful. Fortunately, over these years, I cultivated the Ninety Thousand Li [TERM?] Master taught me to great accomplishment. Otherwise, I fear I could not have lasted until you arrived."

"You worked hard." After comforting her, Luo Chen asked again, "Can you still act next?"

"Yes."

Receiving an accurate answer, Luo Chen revealed a satisfied smile.

Perhaps it was somewhat heartless, but exploring ancient cultivator ruins this time was full of danger. Luo Chen had to use all available strength.

For this, even sacrifice would not be regretted.

After confirming the two demons' condition, Luo Chen placed all his attention on his two companions now fighting fiercely.

Facing that fourth-tier golden eagle, Ding Yi and Cave Master Moyun had already displayed their abilities.

The first to enter his eyes was Cave Master Moyun.

The number of methods this person possessed made one click the tongue.

Golden thread nets, sharp flying swords, one disposable explosive bead after another, flags, spears, halberds, countless items.

These things might not be high grade, but their numbers were vast. Deployed again and again, they repeatedly obstructed the golden eagle's attacks.

Luo Chen was dazzled and even shocked.

"Does Cave Master Moyun have such vigorous mana? He can actually control so many magic treasures!"

Even if he was not controlling them simultaneously, each one he used looked proper and effective, which was still shocking.

Because this meant every magic treasure had undergone no short period of refinement.

Moreover, one needed at least an extremely deep soul foundation to achieve this degree.

In a short time, Cave Master Moyun had launched seven or eight magic treasures to attack the golden eagle without showing decline.

Han Zhan's voice came, "He should not have refined every magic treasure. After all, even we Sage cultivators would not waste too much mana refining so many magic treasures."

Luo Chen did not understand. "Then how do we explain that he can control so many magic treasures and appears to have an endless supply?"

Han Zhan thought for a moment. "This old man once heard that Tianfan City formerly had something called the Myriad Treasures Array [TERM?]. It could refine more magic treasures with the same portion of mana. At its limit, it could even turn hundreds or thousands of magic treasures to one's own use. This method is especially suitable for wealthy people."

"One portion of mana to refine more magic treasures, even hundreds or thousands?" Luo Chen's eyelids twitched wildly. Was there truly such a wondrous formation in the world?

Over these years, he had collected many powerful magic treasures, but due to the limitations of mana and soul, and lacking sufficient energy to refine them, while also being unwilling to sell them, he could only shelve them.

If he could master this formation, then...

For a time, his heart was greatly moved.

Han Zhan seemed to sense Luo Chen's temptation and laughed softly. "Do not think about it. Such a wondrous formation naturally has strict limitations."

"Oh?"

"First, the cultivator practicing this formation must have an extremely full understanding of the magic treasures they want to refine. Ideally, the best case is that they personally refined them. Second, once included in this formation, all magic treasures lose their future advancement opportunities, and their own might cannot be perfectly displayed."

Just hearing these two points made Luo Chen frown.

And when he heard the third limitation, Luo Chen directly dispelled the thought.

"Finally, the Myriad Treasures Array [TERM?] must be maintained long-term, and the mana it consumes is not necessarily small. Cave Master Moyun is already so old and still stuck at the seventh level of Golden Core. It may well be due to the effect of maintaining this formation long-term."

Luo Chen sighed, somewhat regretful.

Among the hundred arts of cultivation, anyone who could achieve something in any art certainly had outstanding qualities.

Although he also cultivated artifact forging, he clearly would not place all his energy on it. The Myriad Treasures Array [TERM?] had no fate with him.

After watching Cave Master Moyun for a while, Luo Chen turned his attention to Ding Yi.

That battle pattern of valuing quantity over refinement, though having no obvious weakness, clearly had no great strength either. He would not take it.

But Ding Yi's overly single battle pattern also made him frown.

From beginning to end, Ding Yi had not activated any other magic treasures. He had always fought using that yellow-skinned gourd that spat golden sand.

Indeed, the yellow-skinned gourd was of excellent quality, at least a middle-grade or even upper-grade magic treasure.

But Ding Yi had reached the ninth level of Golden Core and had roamed the cultivation world for several hundred years. No matter what, he could not only possess this little method.

"Does he not want to, or can he not?"

Luo Chen murmured, narrowing his eyes slightly.

As if he wanted to see through Ding Yi.

Han Zhan, after observing for a while, gave a soft sound of surprise.

"Luo Chen, have you noticed that after entering these so-called Thousand-Ren Mountains [TERM?], the living beings we have encountered are somewhat strange?"

Luo Chen gave a hum. "Naturally strange. They are said to be demon beasts, yet lack intelligence. But if said to be desolate beasts, they lack that powerful physique. Earlier, Ding Yi said this ruins is called the Demon-Fall Land [TERM?], so I tried linking these strange beasts to the legendary demonic beasts, but there is not the slightest demonic qi on them."

At the end, Luo Chen summarized in confusion, "Such beings that seem demon yet not demon, desolate yet not desolate, and are not demonic beasts either, I have never seen in my life."

"No!"

A decisive voice entered his ear.

"Perhaps you have seen them."

Luo Chen was stunned. "I have seen them?"

Han Zhan's tone was solemn. "You and I should both have seen them."

Luo Chen was puzzled. While his eyes stared at the huge golden eagle wreaking havoc among the mountains, he crazily searched his mind for similar memories.

Gradually, he really did find a familiar feeling.

He hesitantly said, "My second Origin Pill, Hidden Dust Sand?"

"Correct!" After Han Zhan voiced this guess, he seemed even more certain.

"Correct. If this old man has not seen wrong, the strange beasts we have seen along this road are clearly wood-stone spirits and spirits of heaven and earth like Hidden Dust Sand!"

So-called wood-stone spirits and spirits of heaven and earth were rare and strange kinds of life.

They were born without souls and without fleshly bodies. They might be stubborn stones, flowers and trees, mountains and rivers, or flames and light winds.

Under the changes of time and the influence of spiritual qi, after a long time, intelligence was born, forming a kind of life similar to demon beasts.

To distinguish them from demon beasts with fleshly bodies, immortal cultivators named them spirits and phantoms [TERM?].

For example, the second Origin Pill in Luo Chen's qi sea had originally been a pearl spirit evolved from a sea pearl.

After death, it revealed its original form and became a huge pearl.

If compared this way, the various strange beasts they had seen in the Thousand-Ren Mountains [TERM?]... the Golden Melon Mosquitoes [TERM?] were actually Golden Melon Stones [TERM?], the Grinding Iron Black Ants [TERM?] were Grinding Iron Ash [TERM?], the Clear Deer [TERM?] was Clear Light Sand [TERM?]...

Luo Chen swallowed and looked toward the golden eagle.

"Then the Nine Yang Golden Eagle [TERM?] is essentially Nine Yang Geng Metal [TERM?]?"

"It should be correct. Earlier, you also heard that junior say this beast loves solar fire qi and devours geng-metal mineral deposits. So-called Nine Yang Geng Metal [TERM?] is itself a variant of geng metal, named Nine Yang because it absorbed large amounts of solar fire qi."

After receiving Han Zhan's confirmation, Luo Chen felt clear, yet also cold all over.

A chill rushed straight to his head.

In these tens of thousands of li of Thousand-Ren Mountains [TERM?], over one thousand types of mineral deposits were spread. If every mineral deposit had a corresponding strange beast guarding it, did that not mean there were over one thousand types of spirits inside?

And each kind was not limited to one or two. They very likely appeared in groups.

"This must be a joke. How could there be so many spirits of different races gathered in one place?" Luo Chen's scalp tingled, somewhat unwilling to accept reality.

Han Zhan was also silent.

At this moment, the outside battle had reached a white-hot stage.

The battlefield of the two men and one eagle had shifted several times.

Black King roamed with his enormous body, always following within a controllable range.

Boom!

With a loud sound, the Nine Yang Golden Eagle [TERM?] flapped its wings and swept away the golden sand filling the sky.

The golden sand that had once been ever victorious posed no danger before this golden eagle.

The golden eagle shrieked and dove toward Cave Master Moyun.

Its speed was extremely fast, like an arrow shooting forward.

Cave Master Moyun waved repeatedly, and several flying swords blocked in front, but they failed to accomplish anything.

Seeing this, he gritted his teeth and slapped the back of his head.

Opening his mouth, he spat out a small furnace.

The small furnace rose into the air and grew with the wind.

Not only that, large amounts of white flame flew from the furnace mouth.

As soon as the white flame appeared, the void seemed about to melt.

Clearly, this was an origin true fire refined by Cave Master Moyun. It was also by relying on this flame that he gained great assistance on the artifact-forging path.

The white flame spread through the void, becoming flying birds one after another that flew toward the golden eagle.

However!

The Nine Yang Golden Eagle [TERM?] merely opened its mouth and inhaled, and countless tiny birds fell into its mouth, making its might even stronger.

Seeing this, Cave Master Moyun's face turned deathly pale.

"No, even five-element restraint cannot restrain it!"

Before his words fell, the golden eagle was already upon him.

With a rumble, it knocked Cave Master Moyun into the incomparably hard ground.

If not for the fire-spitting furnace protecting him, that strike alone might have caused Cave Master Moyun to perish and his Dao to vanish.

In the sky, Ding Yi, who had a gourd tucked at his waist and had been slapping it wildly, changed expression.

"Idiot. I already said it lives by absorbing solar fire qi. Even if its essence is metal, how could it possibly fear your White Cloud Vast Flame [TERM?]?"

At this moment, the Nine Yang Golden Eagle's [TERM?] violent eyes fixed on him.

"Damn it!"

Ding Yi took a deep breath, glanced in a certain direction, then without hesitation abandoned the yellow-skinned gourd and pressed his palms together.

"Burn!"

The next moment, his round, ball-like body began trembling wildly.

Rolling waves of flesh surged across his entire body.

Amid this wave-like surging, a shadow slowly floated out.

Looking carefully, it seemed like an umbrella, and also like a mushroom.

After this shadow condensed, Ding Yi's whole body directly slimmed by a large circle, seeming no different from an ordinary person.

His face was full of pain, but his eyes also held joy.

"It should be enough."

With a thought, that umbrella-shaped shadow immediately enveloped the enormous golden eagle not far away.

"Show your form!"

With a fierce shout, Ding Yi clapped his palms.

Swish!

The giant umbrella closed in an instant.

The golden eagle cried mournfully and fell from the sky, crashing into the earth.

When the smoke and dust scattered, Cave Master Moyun looked at the golden ore on the ground the size of a millstone and froze.

"Nine Yang Geng Metal [TERM?]? Such a huge piece?"

Not only large, but white and lustrous in color, without any golden yellow, clearly top-grade among top-grade.

If this material was added to an offensive magic treasure, it would surely become one of the sharpest things under heaven.

His whole body trembled.

"Fellow Daoist Ding, look quickly..."

Cave Master Moyun raised his head, only to discover Ding Yi did not care at all about the golden eagle's original body after death. Instead, his eyes burned as he stared at a certain place.

And his posture of pressing his palms together had not relaxed at all.

"Devil Lord Azure Yang, roll out here!"

A sky-reaching sound wave rolled out.

Cave Master Moyun was startled. Hadn't Devil Lord Azure Yang died in a snake's mouth?

Following Ding Yi's gaze, he also looked in that direction.

On the rugged ground, a giant figure swam over rapidly in a zigzag pattern.

As it moved, its body and appearance continuously changed.

Black scales, white belly, single horn, vertical pupils. It was Black King, the spirit pet Devil Lord Azure Yang raised in the depths of Peng Lake.

On the jiao-python's head, a red-robed Daoist stood with his robes snapping.

Facing Ding Yi's rude words, the red-robed Daoist's expression showed neither sorrow nor joy. He merely curled his fingers and formed a spiritual seal.

An aura like heavenly might spread from his body.

Cave Master Moyun's expression shifted continuously. As he thought quickly, he seemed to understand something.

He hurriedly shouted, "Devil Lord, we are companions and should work together. Why fight among ourselves?"

"Companions?" Luo Chen glanced at him and sneered. "I fear our true companion Ding Yi already died inside Leaving Forgetting Heaven [TERM?]."

"Am I right, Tai Sui?"

As soon as the words Tai Sui were spoken, Luo Chen shot into the sky and charged straight toward Ding Yi.

Cruelty surged in Ding Yi's eyes, and his palms clapped together.

In the void, the giant umbrella shadow appeared again.

It covered Luo Chen and began closing.

However, halfway closed, it seemed blocked by a layer of invisible power.

Ding Yi gritted his teeth, and the little remaining fat on his body began trembling again.

As he exerted force, the invisible power protecting Luo Chen also revealed its true form. It was clearly towering crimson mana.

"Domain?" Ding Yi's expression changed slightly.

Luo Chen gave a low shout.

"Out!"

In the next instant, the sword case behind him rang together.

Seven sharp swords flashed like meteors and appeared behind him, emitting waves of sword cries.

Not only that.

A long banner flew into the sky. As black mist surged, Ghost Kings walked out from within, covering the surrounding hundred li.

Once Luo Chen acted, he acted with full strength.

Within the rolling black mist, Luo Chen fully unfolded the All-Encompassing Fire Prison [TERM?].

Behind him, the Seven Baleful Swords rang, their sword tips angling upward and pointing at the deathly pale Ding Yi.

"Since Luo has come to this Demon-Fall Land [TERM?], I will naturally explore it properly. But I cannot trust an inhuman thing like you."

"Here, I will end this future trouble forever."

*(End of Chapter)*
