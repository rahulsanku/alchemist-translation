# Chapter 775: Luo Tian Internal Strife, Sima Usurps Luo

*Cry! Cry! Cry!*

Under the bright, clear sky, cranes soared in formation, circling endlessly, their cries loud and crisp.

Such a magnificent sight was almost indistinguishable from the legendary descriptions of the Immortal realm.

But in the vast Eastern Wilderness, only a few places could boast such a scene.

The White Crane Domain was one of them.

Within this domain, there was a Nascent Soul Superior Sect called the White Crane Sect. Its inherited cultivation art was the *Crane's Cry Piercing the Nine Heavens*, complete from Qi Refinement all the way to the Nascent Soul stage.

Disciples who entered the sect would, upon reaching the Initiate stage, be gifted a spirit crane.

The two would cultivate together, granting them considerable combat power.

By the time they reached the Nascent Soul stage, they could even use their bonded spirit crane to traverse the nine heavens and roam the azure skies!

Of course, it wasn't common to see so many spirit cranes dancing in the skies of the White Crane Domain at ordinary times.

Now, with the five domains connected, jointly resisting the demon race, and to guard against a sudden attack by the demon army, so many cranes had been released for aerial surveillance.

Suddenly!

The flock of cranes in the air halted abruptly, their sharp eyes all fixing on a palace deep within a great mountain.

From there, a powerful, sharp aura spread out without concealment.

Several cultivators were gathering towards that spot.

In the distance, within a fourth-tier cave dwelling, a hale and hearty old man with white hair and a beard slowly opened his eyes.

"That's the teleportation formation at Eastern Sun Sect. Which fellow Daoist has arrived at this hour?"

As he pondered, he drifted out of his cave dwelling.

The moment he stepped into the air, a white crane naturally flew beneath him, carrying him towards the teleportation palace.

In no time, he arrived at the palace.

As soon as he arrived, the guarding disciples around bowed and greeted him.

"Supreme Elder!"

"Supreme Elder!"

"Master!"

The old man nodded to the middle-aged man who had given the last greeting, then looked at the figure gradually materializing within the teleportation formation.

Inside the formation, a man in white robes flashed his eyes. Before the spiritual energy had even stabilized, he spoke unhurriedly.

"Though the cold pine grows old, its noble bearing remains; though the wild crane hungers, it drinks and pecks at leisure. You must be Leisure Crane Sage, the friend of Sage Lu of Eastern Sun Sect!"

Leisure Crane Sage's expression froze slightly!

The teleportation formation hadn't fully stopped yet; vast spiritual energy in the void was still buffeting the cultivator's physique.

At this moment, the cultivator inside was at their most vulnerable. They not only had to resist the pressure of the void but also guard against external enemies.

Under such circumstances, for the other party to speak so calmly and leisurely?

His whisk duster draped over his arm, Leisure Crane Sage made a Daoist salute. "Indeed, this old Daoist is he. May I ask your esteemed name, friend?"

The man in white lifted his foot and, in a single step, forcibly stepped out of the teleportation formation.

"I am Luo Chen!"

With just one word, Luo Chen arrived opposite Leisure Crane Sage.

A name without a title, no known origin?

Leisure Crane Sage frowned slightly, but recalling the other party's mention of Sage Lu of Eastern Sun Sect, he deemed him trustworthy.

"This is no place for conversation. Would you like to come this way, friend?"

"Perfect. I also have some matters on which I seek your guidance, friend."

Luo Chen smiled faintly and followed Leisure Crane Sage and his disciple out of the teleportation palace.

As soon as they emerged, the old man waved his whisk duster, and two spirit cranes flew over, landing in the square before the palace, bowing their bodies to them.

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow. Third-tier spirit cranes?

Especially the one beneath Leisure Crane Sage—it exuded the aura of a third-tier Great Perfection level, even higher in realm than the old man's disciple.

"Please!"

"Thank you for your hospitality, friend."

Luo Chen stepped onto the back of the spirit crane and followed Leisure Crane Sage to a pavilion on a high peak.

"Zhaowu, serve tea!"

"Yes, Master!"

The middle-aged man attending them immediately took out a tea set on the stone table and began brewing spirit tea.

Taking this opportunity, the two sides sized each other up.

Luo Chen also quickly reviewed the information he had on the White Crane Sect.

The cultivators of this sect were skilled in battle, but not fond of it!

The reason they weren't fond of battle, aside from their cultivation of nature and temperament, was also because they invested a portion of their resources into raising their bonded spirit cranes. Consequently, the number of cultivators in this sect was relatively small.

Back then, when the twenty-one Nascent Soul Superior Sects of the Eastern Wilderness rear area had taken out five domains, including the White Crane Domain, as resettlement points for the Death-Desperate Army, this was also a consideration.

Among the Nascent Soul Superior Sects within these five domains, the White Crane Sect had few cultivators; the Splendid Wind Palace mostly had female cultivators and was also small in number; the Mu Manor was a Nascent Soul-level cultivation clan force, content to keep to itself; and the Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion also adhered to the principle of quality over quantity, with few disciples.

It could be said that within the five domains, aside from the Myriad Beasts Sect, which specialized in dealing with demon beasts, the other four sects were chosen because they had few people and didn't occupy much territory, allowing them to free up living space for the survivors who had fled the front lines.

Of course, geographical factors also played a major role.

As far as Luo Chen knew, the White Crane Sect currently had only two Nascent Soul Sages!

One of them, he had heard, was a transformed great demon, not an orthodox human cultivator.

As the fragrance of tea filled the air, Luo Chen suppressed his inner urgency and inquired about the matters that concerned him.

"Friend, have you heard of the Luo Tian Sect?"

Leisure Crane Sage thought it was some major event. When he heard the other party was only asking about a sect, he couldn't help but breathe a sigh of relief.

However, Luo Tian Sect?

He looked puzzled.

It was the disciple, Zhaowu, attending beside him, who hesitated for a moment before suddenly speaking up. "The Luo Tian Sect Sage Luo asks about—could it be the one that recently held the Ten-Decisive Duels at Lingtian Pass?"

Ten-Decisive Duels?

Luo Chen's brow instantly furrowed, like two small swords, causing one's heart to tremble.

He was not stingy. With a flick of his hand, a jade bottle appeared on the table.

"This medicine is beneficial for enhancing spirit beast cultivation. Little friend, why don't you tell me in detail about this so-called Ten-Decisive Duel!"

Leisure Crane Sage also became interested. "Zhaowu, tell us in detail. Your master has been secluded lately, comprehending secret arts, and doesn't know the current affairs of the cultivation world. I am also quite curious."

With his master's permission and the pill Luo Chen had produced, which was supposedly beneficial for spirit beasts, Zhaowu had no more reservations.

He pondered for a moment, then slowly began:

"As far as I know, the Ten-Decisive Duels are an internal strife within a sect called the Luo Tian Sect. I heard that the sect master's lineage split, both sides wanting to consolidate power. After a century of disputes, they had no choice but to resort to arms and settle it through force."

"One side wants to uphold the orthodox Luo Tian lineage; the other says internal pacification must precede external resistance, and they need to establish a core leader before the demon army presses in."

"Of course, from different perspectives, the essence isn't that simple."

"Based on my junior's estimation, this Luo Tian internal strife isn't as straightforward as it seems on the surface. It's more of a joint probe by Motian Cliff and the Five Elements Divine Sect against the Splendid Wind Palace!"

Saying this, Zhaowu couldn't help but look at Leisure Crane Sage.

"Master, our White Crane Domain has a similar situation. Many relocated survivors have intact sect strengths. To seek better spirit veins, they constantly test the limits of our White Crane Sect. They want to take over our nest!"

Leisure Crane Sage smiled. "You wouldn't be talking about the already-defunct Jade Cauldron Sword Sect, would you?"

Zhaowu nodded. "Master cultivates his nature and ignores external affairs, so you might not know. Although the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect has lost Sage Jade Cauldron and poses no threat, don't forget that they originally came from the Ten Thousand Swords Domain. With the backing of the Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion, plus that genius of the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect who broke through to the Nascent Soul stage on the battlefield, known as the 'Lone Swan of the Sword Realm,' their threat to our White Crane Sect is considerable!"

The old man's smile faded, and he silently muttered those names.

Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion, Jade Cauldron Sword Sect, Lone Swan of the Sword Realm!

On Luo Chen's side, however, he was eager to learn more.

Zhaowu reminded his master, then returned to the main topic, discussing the Luo Tian Sect's affairs.

"Speaking of which, the Luo Tian Sect is said to have originated from the former Jade Cauldron Domain, serving the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect as its superior sect."

Luo Chen hummed in acknowledgment. This was naturally true.

Any faction that had developed in the Jade Cauldron Domain back then, big or small, nominally regarded the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect as their leader.

Perhaps there was no formal master-servant distinction, but to make a living, earn resources, and enter and leave various immortal cities, they had all contributed spirit stones to the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect at some point.

"What's the situation with this internal strife within the sect master's lineage you mentioned?"

Zhaowu thought for a moment, then said uncertainly, "This matter probably traces back to when the Luo Tian Sect's founding sect master, Sima Huiniang, passed the position to her senior disciple, Yao Mingyue. At that time, the Luo Tian Sect was in flight, and talents emerged one after another. Not to mention old-timers like War Devil and Tian Kuizi, the younger generation also had promising seedlings like Si Wenjun and Baleful Dragonzi."

"Logically, the position of second-generation sect master should have been passed to Si Wenjun, who was closest to Sima Huiniang and had the highest cultivation."

Saying this, Zhaowu looked at Luo Chen and smiled. "Perhaps you don't know, but Si Wenjun was actually Sima Huiniang's own younger brother, his birth name being Sima Wenjie."

Luo Chen's expression stiffened.

"But somehow, Sima Huiniang didn't pass the sect master position to Si Wenjun, who had the highest cultivation among the younger generation, nor to Baleful Dragonzi, who had the strongest combat strength. Instead, she gave it to her unremarkable senior disciple, Yao Mingyue."

"From then on, the rift was born!"

...

Lingtian Pass!

A majestic, strategic pass, like an iron gate, wedged itself into the only route between the front lines and the rear, amidst the lofty mountains and ridges.

This city was not some ancient city with deep foundations.

Rather, it was a war city built day and night over the last decade, spearheaded by the Nether Origin Sect, funded by the twenty-one Nascent Soul Superior Sects, and manned by the cultivators from the twelve domains lost at the front lines.

It was said that with this city here, even a late Nascent Soul Great Demon Emperor couldn't forcefully breach it!

Thus, it became the second line of defense for the surviving humans against the demon army.

As for the first line?

That would naturally be the three domains still suffering the ravages of war at the front.

Looking down from a high altitude, one could see the deep cannon muzzles on the green-black city walls, the forest of banners, the faintly visible formations, and the orderly rows of cultivator figures patrolling the walls.

Inside the city, however, it wasn't divided into commercial streets like ordinary immortal cities. Instead, there were fortified strongholds, open grounds, and countless large war flying boats crouching low.

Everything seemed poised to strike, ready for battle at any moment.

The establishment of the Death-Desperate Army, while forging these "motley crew" into a cohesive force, also made everyone understand what they were about to face.

An unending, life-or-death struggle with the demon race, without retreating an inch!

Thus, the cultivators walking within also wore fierce expressions, keeping others at a distance.

But!

At this moment, in the central square of Lingtian Pass, a sea of people was filled with morbid, fanatical shouts.

"Kill!"

"Kill!"

"Kill!"

A squad of fully armored cultivators stood amidst the crowd, blocking the surging masses from approaching the high platform that spanned a full square li.

Compared to those morbidly fanatical cultivators, this squad was much calmer.

Because they weren't homeless refugees. They were law enforcement teams formed from disciples and members drawn from the five sects of the five domains.

They were specifically responsible for maintaining order within Lingtian Pass.

Listening to the fanatical shouts nearby, the law enforcement cultivators all wore solemn expressions.

"How many times is this?"

"Countless!"

"Indeed. When disaster strikes, everyone flies their own way. Under the century-long onslaught of the demon race, who knows how many cultivators abandoned their sects to seek higher branches? And how many fished in troubled waters, robbing and killing others? Now they've all gathered in the five domains, settling old scores and vendettas. Those purging their sect's doors are even more numerous. The establishment of this Dueling Platform was to provide them with a fair channel for resolution. Since its establishment, the fighting has never stopped."

"Such internal attrition—don't the higher-ups care?"

"How could they control it? We cultivators value a clear mind and unobstructed thoughts. If they forcefully suppress it, it might lead to private violence, harming innocent bystanders."

"What the junior brother says is indeed reasonable. But this time, the Luo Tian Sect's Ten-Decisive Duels are probably the largest in history, right?"

"Indeed! Ten duels, all battles between Adepts!"

"That Luo Tian Sect—are they really the sect that survived the first wave of demon tides from the Xiaoyue Mountain Range? In such chaos, after a century, not only have they not declined, but they have more and more Adepts."

"Who says otherwise? Not only are they capable of standing on their own, but they also have wide connections. That Si Wenjun, who wants to set up his own sect, was actually able to recruit so many Adept guest elders to fight for him?"

"I'm afraid it's not for him, but for the two superior sects behind him! You might not know this, but one of Si Wenjun's guest elders, as far as I know, should be from the Five Elements Divine Sect..."

"Don't talk about that. Guess who will win this fifth duel?"

"Who's fighting who?"

"Qi Emei against Baleful Dragonzi!"

Upon hearing this, those in the know were stunned.

Weren't those two rumored to be a loving couple, like an immortal pair?

Back in the battle at Border City, when they were still at the Initiate stage, they had joined forces to slay the third-tier Blazing Eye Fierce Bull King, a tale that was once widely praised.

Later, they often hunted demon beasts together, coordinating flawlessly.

How had it come to this, facing each other with blades today?

Amidst the crowd's astonishment, two figures had already appeared on the high platform.

One was valiant and spirited, tall and slender, with a red ribbon fluttering behind her head, accentuating her handsome and beautiful face.

The other was clad in heavy armor, with two metal wings spread, holding a broadsword, his lips tightly pressed together. Although his face was resolute, his gaze was somewhat evasive, as if he dared not look at the woman opposite him.

Under the gaze of the multitude, the woman slowly spoke. "Zeng Yilong, why won't you look at me?"

Baleful Dragonzi kept his head down, staring at the hilt of the broadsword he held with both hands.

"What is there to see? We each serve our own masters now. There's no going back."

Qi Emei twitched the corner of her mouth. "If I win, if Si Wenjun wins the Ten-Decisive Duels, the Luo Tian Sect will be merged into my Five Elements Divine Sect. Everything can still go back."

Baleful Dragonzi opened his mouth, then finally sighed.

"Emei, you are no match for me. You never have been. And now, with this terrain restricting us, even less so."

"Oh?" The woman sneered. "You underestimate me too much. And you underestimate the secret arts of the Divine Sect too much."

As she spoke, strands of red thread began to emanate from her fingertips.

The battle was about to begin!
