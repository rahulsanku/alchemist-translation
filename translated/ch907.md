# Chapter 907: Rosycloud, Jambu Mountain Falls

"Related to me?"

Luo Chen was stunned. How could the honored guests of the Nether Origin Sect have anything to do with him?

Filled with intense curiosity, Luo Chen remained on Jambu Mountain.

Tan Sheng walked out from the Heavenly Nether Palace and came to Luo Chen's side.

"Wait a moment. Those guests are still far away; it'll be a while before they arrive."

Luo Chen asked curiously, "May I ask who is coming?"

Tan Sheng smiled. "You'll know when you see them."

Alright, still being secretive at a time like this.

Luo Chen was a bit speechless and changed the direction of his complaint.

"Fellow Daoist Tan, your cultivation is not shallow. Why bother with all this welcoming and seeing off on Jambu Mountain? With your ability, whether you cultivate in seclusion inside or go out to guard a region, you wouldn't be wasted."

Tan Sheng shrugged and said helplessly, "Naturally, I don't want to do this. But Jambu Mountain is currently under martial law. All the disciples on the mountain have been sent down. There always needs to be one or two disciples that the ancestors trust and find convenient to keep by their side."

His words carried helplessness, but his tone was quite proud.

Among the vast Nether Origin Sect, the three elders trusted only him. On the surface, it was being worked like a horse, but wasn't it also a unique honor?

Under the ancestors' grace, perhaps he would be the next Chongming, the next Lei Daozi!

Among the Sage cultivators Luo Chen had met, this Tan Sheng was relatively simple.

He wasn't like those old monster Sages from the Northern Sea or the Eastern Wilderness who had weathered many storms. Instead, he was somewhat similar to that batch of newly promoted Sages from the Tianyuan Dao Sect.

All were flowers grown in a greenhouse, lacking tempering.

Their so-called competitions were mostly conducted within the bounds of rules, unlike the way they fought without any scruples.

Luo Chen had only shown a slight goodwill earlier, and Tan Sheng's arrogant attitude had vanished, becoming much more familiar.

Using this to extract information would be even easier.

"Why the martial law?" Luo Chen asked with his thick brows and big eyes.

Tan Sheng hesitated for a moment, then lowered his voice. "You should know about the Azure Grain Immortal matter, right?"

Luo Chen's brows shot up, and his heart shook violently!

As expected!

From the powerful pressure occasionally leaking out when he stepped onto the Heavenly Nether Palace's steps, to the violently churning waves in the black water pool inside the palace, everything indicated that besides the three ancestors, there was another powerful existence.

Now, with Tan Sheng's confirmation, Luo Chen could finally be certain that the one being suppressed was Azure Grain Immortal, one of the three great ancient demons of the Eastern Wilderness!

"A dignified Ascendant ancient demon, ending up so miserable. If I hadn't agreed to join the Nether Origin Sect back then, would I have ended up the same?"

Luo Chen asked himself and quickly gave the answer.

Naturally not!

Because with his strength, the three ancestors wouldn't need to go through such trouble.

A flip of the hand and he would be erased!

Thinking about it this way, Black Marsh Ancestor's somewhat impolite treatment of him was already relatively restrained?

Just as the two were conversing in detail, three beams of light approached from afar.

Tan Sheng went out of the mountain to welcome them, leading the three through layers of formations and onto Jambu.

And Luo Chen, with his good eyesight, had already recognized the arrivals.

Indeed, they were related to him.

All were familiar faces!

One of the Star Gate's nineteen elders, Sage Seven Lamps; the head of the Fu family, Fu Chaosheng; and the chief of the four great elders of the Eastern Wilderness Tianyuan Merchant Alliance, Gu Zuokai.

Under Tan Sheng's guidance, the three ascended.

When they looked up, they met the gaze cast down from the mountain, and that white-robed figure.

When they stopped, Sage Seven Lamps let out a cold laugh.

"Luo Chen, you're still well, I see!"

Luo Chen opened his mouth, but could only force a bitter smile.

"In the Eastern Wilderness, one's circumstances are beyond one's control. Please forgive me, elder."

"Hmph!"

Sage Seven Lamps snorted coldly, waved his hand, and strode past Luo Chen. Under Tan Sheng's guidance, he stepped onto the high stairs and headed toward the Heavenly Nether Palace.

Fu Chaosheng, who had grown even older, and Gu Zuokai remained behind.

The three of them faced each other, quite awkward.

Gu Zuokai spread his hands, as if to say, "I told you so."

Luo Chen shook his head gently, meeting Fu Chaosheng's complicated gaze.

"Fellow Daoist, long time no see."

Fu Chaosheng said with a complex tone, "It has been a long time. I still remember your spirited and proud appearance when you defeated all the Dao Sect's prodigies on Heaven-Earth Peak. Who would have thought that in just a few decades, you would be standing on Jambu Mountain."

Heaven-Earth Peak, Jambu Mountain—both were the pinnacles of this world.

The same Luo Chen, the same spirited and proud demeanor.

But the camps had already changed!

Luo Chen repeated, "Circumstances beyond my control, not my wish."

"Understandable, but!" Fu Chaosheng said in a low voice, "You entered Heaven-Earth Peak first. You were first a member of the Tianyuan Dao Sect!"

Luo Chen smiled bitterly in helplessness.

The identity of a Star Gate guest elder of the Tianyuan Dao Sect was useful everywhere, except at the Nether Origin Sect.

First, the Dao Sect had lured away the Nether Origin Sect's Alchemy Saint, Chu Yan. Now, the Nether Origin Sect was repaying in kind by recruiting Alchemy Saint Luo Chen into their sect. Wasn't this just tit for tat?

But while the Nether Origin Sect could endure it back then, the Tianyuan Dao Sect might not be able to swallow this insult!

Even if they didn't really care about an unknown nobody like Luo Chen.

"Why are you the ones here today, and not Dao Sect disciples?"

"Aren't we Dao Sect disciples?" Gu Zuokai joked from the side.

Luo Chen waved his hand. "That's not what I meant. I'm just curious why Sage Seven Lamps is the leader. Is it just because I'm a Star Gate guest elder?"

Fu Chaosheng nodded. "Yes. If they directly sent a Dao Sect representative to negotiate, it would be too straightforward. Sage Seven Lamps' identity is just right. He is one of the Star Gate's nineteen elders, and he had a decent relationship with you before. First, he came to make clear the stance that the Star Gate's dignity cannot be compromised. Second, he wanted to see if he could bring you back."

"That's naturally impossible." Luo Chen didn't even need to think before giving the answer.

If a mere Great Sage came to demand someone, and the three Ascendant ancestors handed him over, wouldn't that become the biggest joke in the Eastern Wilderness?

Besides, Luo Chen had already been publicly enfeoffed as the master of the Alchemy Saint Hall in front of everyone!

Handing Luo Chen over now would be repeating the Alchemy Saint incident.

The Alchemy Saint had at least made his own choice. Luo Chen hadn't.

No one in the Nether Origin Sect would swallow that insult.

"If negotiations fail, it will be courtesy first, then force." Fu Chaosheng said calmly.

In his calm words, turbulent waves surged.

Luo Chen's expression turned serious. "Does the Tianyuan Dao Sect really dare?"

Gu Zuokai said confidently from the side, "There's nothing they don't dare. My Tianyuan Dao Sect is the strongest force in the entire Mountains and Seas Realm. They cannot afford any loss of face!"

Luo Chen frowned. "But as far as I know, the Dao Sect is currently attacking the Northern Sea, and they've even sent people to the Southern Frontier and the Western Desert. Do they really dare to make enemies on all sides?"

Fu Chaosheng explained, "We haven't invested much force in the Western Desert. In the Southern Frontier, we only sent one Ascendant ancestor. As for the Northern Sea, we've already reached a cooperation with the great power Li Canghai. As long as he helps us repel the Northern Sea Demon Alliance, the Tianyuan Dao Sect promises to give him the Arctic Yemo Heaven as his cultivation dojo."

Gu Zuokai added, "It won't be long before my Tianyuan Dao Sect can free up their hands. If the Nether Origin Sect remains stubborn, I believe the Sect Master won't mind starting another war!"

Luo Chen hissed, drawing in a cold breath.

He really hadn't expected the Tianyuan Dao Sect to act so swiftly and with such boldness.

But on second thought, was it really just because of an insignificant nobody like him?

It was probably just a pretext for a righteous cause!

They had likely been interested in the Eastern Wilderness for a long time.

If the Tianyuan Dao Sect really succeeded, wouldn't it be a feat of swallowing the entire Mountains and Seas Realm?

Fu Chaosheng advised, "Luo Chen, listen to me. It's not too late to turn back now. Just follow the precedent of Sage Dragon Abyss joining the Myriad Immortals Assembly back then. Consider it a temporary stay. We'll also give the Nether Origin Sect a way to back down."

Sage Dragon Abyss joining the Myriad Immortals Assembly was merely for experience, not a true change of allegiance.

With this reasoning, both sides could barely save face.

But it was too forced!

One side had likely coveted the Eastern Wilderness for a long time.

The other absolutely could not afford another loss of face, not even the slightest stain!

Luo Chen exhaled a turbid breath. "Forget it. I can't make decisions about this matter. You two, don't bother persuading me. Tell me about your granddaughter. How is Qinglan doing now?"

Seeing that Luo Chen no longer wanted to discuss the matter, Fu Chaosheng was also quite helpless.

If it weren't for his deep connection with Luo Chen, why would he have come on this trip?

But in the face of the general trend, personal relationships could indeed hardly move anything.

He said with some relief, "With your help, Qinglan won first place in the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven Grand Competition. She is now cultivating at Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain. She has become a disciple of Ancestor Chunyuan of Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak, received true scriptures, was taught divine abilities, and her cultivation is about to reach mid-Sage stage. After she comes out of seclusion, she should go out to gain experience. If you have the chance, you can meet her then."

"Almost mid-Sage? That cultivation speed is really fast!"

Luo Chen was also quite relieved. That woman was doing so well now; it wasn't a waste of his efforts back then.

Fu Chaosheng explained, "Sages who comprehend the true meaning of laws early on naturally cultivate much faster than ordinary Sages. Because they can use the true meaning of laws to leverage the origin qi of heaven and earth, absorbing and refining the spiritual energy most suitable for them. Their cultivation efficiency is a thousand miles a day."

A simple sentence, yet it explained the confusion Luo Chen had been stuck on for decades in his cultivation.

Was this the foundation of the strongest force in the Mountains and Seas Realm?

Luo Chen now naturally understood this principle.

If he had also comprehended the true meaning of fire-attribute laws back then, his cultivation efficiency would only have been faster than Fu Qinglan's.

But unfortunately, he had taken a wrong path, which led to him having a vast foundation but walking with difficulty.

"What about the others?" Luo Chen asked about past events.

He had left the Central Continent quite hastily back then and didn't know the final outcome of the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven Grand Competition.

The intelligence he had received over the years had been intermittent due to the Dao Sect's military campaigns. The fact that a letter from Sage Heavenly Mountain took so long to arrive was proof enough.

"Besides Qinglan, the final successful ones in the Grotto-Heaven Grand Competition were only Mingyan, Gu Yuan, and Shi Ju."

"Mingyan goes without saying. She was the representative of the master-disciple lineage. Sage Dragon Abyss ultimately made a wedding dress for her."

"As for Gu Yuan's situation, you should have heard Fellow Daoist Gu talk about it, right?"

"As for Shi Ju..." Fu Chaosheng paused and warned, "You need to be careful of this kid. He holds grudges. You destroyed his Thunder Dao avatar. This grudge won't be easily resolved. Especially since the Shi Great House dotes on this junior the most."

Luo Chen laughed dryly. "If you say that, then I'm even more afraid to return to the Tianyuan Dao Sect."

Just as they were conversing, two figures had already appeared above the Heavenly Nether Palace.

The three stopped talking.

Soon, Sage Seven Lamps hurried down.

His face was pale, as if he had been shocked, but his overall aura was still fine.

Even when two armies are at war, they don't kill the envoy.

Back then, Chongming of the Nether Origin Sect had gone to the Tianyuan Dao Sect to observe the ceremony and returned unscathed.

Now, Sage Seven Lamps making this trip naturally had no danger.

But this pale face was probably from facing a great power and being severely reprimanded.

Sage Seven Lamps glared at Luo Chen with resentment.

"The mountains are high and the rivers are long. We will meet again. Farewell!"

Before he finished speaking, he flicked his sleeve and left, carrying both anger and shock.

Fu Chaosheng looked at Luo Chen and said with a complex tone, "Luo Chen, take care."

Luo Chen opened his mouth, but in the end could only say, "Fellow Daoist, take care!"

Tan Sheng watched this scene with a smile. When the three were blocked by the formation, he said to Luo Chen, "I'll go see them off. The Alchemy Saint Hall Master can go about his business. Treat the Nether Origin Sect as your own home."

Luo Chen smiled helplessly.

He had another home now!

With a heavy heart, Luo Chen also descended Jambu Mountain and flew toward the Alchemy Saint Hall.

One south, one north, two beams of light, going in opposite directions.

That Heaven-Earth Peak, he could never return to.

Suddenly!

On Jambu Mountain, an angry roar exploded across the sky.

"Coming uninvited—that makes you an unwelcome guest!"

Luo Chen spun around sharply, thinking that Sage Seven Lamps and the others had done something to anger the Nether Origin Ancestor.

But the next moment, a stunning golden fire phoenix shot up into the sky!

That golden fire phoenix filled everything in his field of vision.

Its speed was swift, like a thunderclap.

Its momentum was violent, like raging waves.

Where it passed, the void shattered inch by inch, spreading out thousands of feet of pitch-black cracks.

The tightly guarded Jambu Mountain grand formation, which isolated everything, disintegrated in an instant under this impact.

Three auras, like the might of heaven and earth, rose simultaneously, each sending an attack toward the golden fire phoenix.

Boom!

Only a deafening collision sound was heard, echoing for millions of miles.

Luo Chen felt he could hear nothing. Blood dripped from his ears, his features were twisted and fierce, and his powerful physical body was torn apart in the terrifying turbulence of origin qi.

In his blurry vision, Tan Sheng, Fu Chaosheng, Gu Zuokai, who hadn't gone far and were blocking the path, and even Sage Seven Lamps at the late Sage stage, all instantly turned into powder, vanishing without a trace!

A great alarm blared in his mind. Luo Chen forcibly stabilized his physical body and fled in panic like a madman.

And in his heart, there was only one answer.

"Rosycloud has come!"

Crack!

Crack!

Crack!

In a series of unpleasant sounds, void cracks danced like black snakes, and vast origin qi swept in all directions.

And Jambu Mountain, which had stood unyielding in the nine heavens for three thousand years, crashed down with a roar!

Three figures shot out of Jambu Mountain like arrows from a string, standing in front of a stunningly beautiful woman, their faces a mix of shock and anger.

"Master of Green Parasol Mountain!"

"Ancient Demon Phoenix!"

"Rosycloud!"

Facing the successive angry shouts and accusations, the woman was unmoved.

A red robe, standing at the edge of the sky, her gaze seemed contemptuous, yet also as if she didn't see them at all.

Unlike the lazy, just-awakened demeanor Luo Chen had seen at Green Parasol Mountain back then, Rosycloud was now filled with an overbearing aura that brooked no disobedience.

Her sword-like brows slanted into her temples, and at the corners of her eyes hung three phoenix feathers with gilded dark patterns. Her phoenix eyes, half-closed like an ancient pool reflecting the moon, suddenly opened wide, revealing amber vertical pupils with needle-sharp glints.

She paid no attention to the three at all, only casting her gaze toward the falling Jambu Mountain. In her pleasant voice, she issued a faint rebuke.

"I told you not to get involved in the matters of Emperor Heaven. Otherwise, how could you have fallen to this state?"

Black waves surged on Jambu Mountain, and a wisp of green light slowly rose.

From within came a weak voice.

"Azure Grain had no intention of contending for hegemony between the two races, but I had to win a place for the Azure Grain Spirit race to inhabit."

"Overestimating yourself!"

Rosycloud snorted coldly. With a sweep of her large sleeve, the green light offered no resistance and entered her sleeve.

Then, on her bright and imposing face, her willow brows stood upright.

"Now, it's time for this Origin Lord to have a word with you about the repeated provocations against my Green Parasol Mountain!"
