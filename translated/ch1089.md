# Chapter 1089: Sleeve Universe, Relinquishing the Sect Leader Position

[My elder sister was originally a True Lord of the Xuan Realm. For reasons unknown, she descended upon Mountains and Seas. On the verge of death, she employed the Nirvana Secret Art to be reborn and cultivate anew.

In a thousand years since her rebirth, Sister Rosycloud cultivated step by step to the late-stage Ascendant realm. Yet, suppressed by the laws of this world, she could never break through to the Body Integration stage. Moreover, a binding force of the world's laws always constrained her—not only preventing breakthrough but also making ascension exceedingly difficult. She even had a premonition that if she did not leave soon, she would be completely trapped and die within the Mountains and Seas Realm.

According to my sister's observations, the Mountains and Seas Realm is a small world newly born not long ago. Its spiritual qi is quite abundant, and its spiritual opportunities are beyond counting. This greatly aids living beings in comprehending the true meaning of laws. The five sects of the Xuan Realm discovered this world and employed great means to forcibly descend and open it up, plundering resources and nurturing outstanding juniors.

She conceived the idea of emulating them. Over a thousand years, she successively nurtured me and Qinghe, helping her ascend.

Your foundation is even more profound than mine. I expect that breaking through to the Ascendant realm is just around the corner. However, you must be careful. After the great battles of ancient times and even the present, the Heavenly Dao consciousness of this world has gradually become aware of the harm caused by Ascendant cultivators. The more frequently one manipulates the origin qi of heaven and earth, the easier it is to sink deep into this world, becoming indistinguishable from the Heavenly Dao.

Therefore, after reaching Ascendant, ascend quickly to avoid obstructing your immortal path!]

[Azure Frost]

Azure Frost was as taciturn as ever.

On the feather, only a few hundred characters of information remained, briefly explaining Rosycloud's origins and introducing the Mountains and Seas Realm.

But after reading this letter, combined with his own past speculations, the fog in Luo Chen's heart finally dissipated.

This Rosycloud was indeed that Rosycloud!

The so-called "descent for reasons unknown" was precisely her battle with Devil Lord Heaven-Refining, where she unfortunately drifted into this small world.

Nurturing Qinghe Immortal and Azure Frost was also to help her escape and leave.

However, the introduction to the Mountains and Seas Realm surprised Luo Chen somewhat.

This world's spiritual opportunities were beyond counting!

Could it be that other worlds—for instance, the Xuan Realm, a famous great thousand world—had very few spiritual opportunities?

Without comparison, Luo Chen could not know.

But based on the intelligence Luo Chen knew, the number of Ascendant great powers in the Mountains and Seas Realm was indeed somewhat excessive.

Then, once he himself became an Ascendant, should he immediately ascend, or should he learn from the Ascendants of the five ancient holy lands and linger here for a long time?

Moreover, should ascension follow tradition and go to the Immortal Realm, or should he smuggle himself into the Xuan Realm like Rosycloud and Azure Frost?

The Divine Separation Lord had once gifted them a [Xuan Realm Secret Record], which contained some general information about the Xuan Realm.

One part covered local customs and practices, while another part described the top experts.

According to what was written there, the Xuan Realm was an unadulterated great thousand world. The highest cultivators could train to the Great Vehicle stage. Once they ascended, they would become true immortals, honored as True Immortals!

In contrast, cultivators from ordinary small worlds could at most train to the Ascendant realm.

Although they could still rely on the world's origin power to lift them and ascend to the Immortal Realm, after going up, they would still only be Ascendant cultivators. At that point, they were far from being so-called immortals; they were merely the lowest existence.

This reasoning was easy to understand.

The top student from a small place, upon arriving at a top-tier prestigious school—let alone being outstanding—would already be lucky to maintain their position and not fall to the bottom.

But there were exceptions.

True geniuses were geniuses everywhere!

"Will I be that one-in-ten-thousand cultivation prodigy?"

Luo Chen asked himself.

Then, with a confident expression, he answered himself.

"Yes!"

With a five-spirit root, he formed his core in a hundred years, reached Sage in two hundred, and now, at less than five hundred, he was only one step away from Ascendant.

If he wasn't a genius, then who was?

Especially since, at the Sage realm, he had already comprehended the true meaning of three different attribute laws. If he went to the Immortal Realm, he would only shine even brighter.

On the contrary, if he went to the Xuan Realm, which lacked spiritual opportunities, it might even affect his subsequent cultivation.

Most importantly, Luo Chen did not have the method to smuggle himself into the Xuan Realm.

Therefore, after some deliberation, Luo Chen had made up his mind.

Once he became an Ascendant, he would choose the most natural ascension, allowing the mysterious power of the Heavenly Dao to guide him to the Immortal Realm.

For now, however, there was no rush.

His injuries had not yet fully healed, and the matter of breaking through to Ascendant could be postponed for the time being.

Moreover, he was quite interested in the other two things Azure Frost had left within the feather.

One was a teleportation formation—the super-large teleportation formation buried beneath Green Parasol Mountain!

Azure Frost had left behind the corresponding formation diagram, which was extremely complex and contained countless spatial restrictions.

If he could master this formation diagram and combine it with the corresponding materials, Luo Chen might also be able to replicate a similar large-scale teleportation formation.

The other was a divine ability.

[Sleeve Universe]

"How did she know that I was very eager for this divine ability?"

Luo Chen was quite pleasantly surprised as he perused the cultivation method for Sleeve Universe.

However, after some research, Luo Chen was very helpless.

This divine ability could only be cultivated after comprehending spatial laws.

Despite his helplessness, Luo Chen was not discouraged. With the original divine ability text, even if only for reference, he believed that with his own capabilities, he could create a [Sleeve Universe] suitable for himself.

Caressing the feather, Luo Chen sat on the soft couch, looking at the empty hall.

This was once Rosycloud's perspective, and also her foundation.

Now, it had all been left to him.

"But in the end, it is not a foundation I built myself!"

Luo Chen sighed faintly, stood up, and left the Truth Crossing Hall.

When the doors slammed shut, two people were already waiting outside.

Or rather, one person and one demon.

Moon Wanderer and Pure Crane.

"Hall Master!"

Luo Chen nodded slightly, his gaze sweeping over the two, ignoring Pure Crane's eager look, and his eyes lingered on Moon Wanderer.

Faced with this scrutiny, Moon Wanderer's mood was extremely tense, and her body even trembled.

A dignified late-stage Sage great cultivator, one who had even broken through Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's slave path inheritance, yet her performance was so pathetic.

Just how much of a shadow had Rosycloud left on her?

But her current behavior also had its reasons.

She had a grudge against Luo Chen!

And a big one!

Luo Chen's friend Duan Li in the Northern Sea had died at her hands.

Luo Chen's industry in Peng Lake, along with its affiliated Zhang family, had also been destroyed by her.

There was also the ten-thousand-li pursuit back then, which had almost forced Luo Chen into the Yellow Spring Sea.

Now that Rosycloud was gone, and Luo Chen's strength had long surpassed hers, if Luo Chen wanted to kill her, no one could stop him!

The terror of having one's life and death in another's hands—no matter who it was, their performance would not be better than Moon Wanderer's.

After a long while.

Under Moon Wanderer's tense emotions, a voice like heavenly music reached her ears.

"Moon servant, guard the Truth Crossing Hall. Do not let outsiders disturb it."

"Yes!"

"Pure Crane, please take care of the Daytime Void Hall for a while."

"Hall Master, will you not select a new Daytime Void Hall Master?"

"Not for now. The Primordial Lord has departed, Azure Frost is not here. Who in this world can still take over the positions of the two hall masters? Let them remain vacant! Now, Pure Crane, take me to the Green Parasol Treasury. I need to inventory the materials in the treasury; I have great use for them."

Pure Crane hummed in acknowledgment and followed behind Luo Chen, heading toward the Green Parasol Treasury.

Behind them, Moon Wanderer was as if relieved of a heavy burden, and the corners of her mouth could not help but curl up.

Sure enough!

The Primordial Lord had just left, and Luo Chen was not yet ready to make a move. Her gamble had paid off.

...

Green Parasol Treasury.

Luo Chen had been a frequent visitor here before.

During the three years he was imprisoned at Green Parasol Mountain, he often came here to select medicinal herbs and refine Transformation Pills.

Therefore, he was quite familiar with this place.

Now, with Pure Crane personally leading him, many fine materials that he had not noticed before came into Luo Chen's view.

Thousand-year soul-nourishing wood, nine-leaf rosy cloud grass, top-grade Five Elements Lotus Platform...

It had to be said that Rosycloud was exceptionally generous. When she left, she had left all these good things behind. Perhaps she didn't think much of them, but now they all benefited Luo Chen.

His hand touched a bone that retained a powerful aura.

The bone was charred black, with a lingering scorching heat. It should have been left behind after being burned by a powerful flame.

On the straight bone, there were holes, like a long flute.

"A fifth-rank desolate beast bone?"

Pure Crane, on the other side, heard Luo Chen muttering to himself and looked up to explain: "That bone should be from a Flute-Chanting Beast. When it roars, it sounds like a flute, bewitching the mind. It was left behind by the Primordial Lord when she personally killed a fifth-rank desolate beast in the desolate lands in her early years."

The desolate lands—a place distinct from the five continents of the Mountains and Seas Realm.

They were the remnants of ancient times, the complete losers of the ancient great war, driven to the desolate lands to eke out a living.

Although it was a barren land, the desolate lands were vast in scope, no less than any continent in the Mountains and Seas Realm.

Countless years had passed, and no one knew how many top-tier desolate beasts still survived within.

Luo Chen's heart stirred slightly, and he inquired, "Is there a map?"

"It seems there is one. The Primordial Lord casually drew it in her early years, but I don't know where it was thrown. I'll find it for the Hall Master later!"

"Mm, continue searching for that thing I need."

Not long after.

Pure Crane let out a delighted sound. From a pile of boxes, she lifted a small stone.

"Hall Master, the thing you need has been found, and there's a lot of it!"

It was a crystal-clear existence like a piece of crystal.

Its name was Spatial Prism Aperture!

It was a very precious spatial material with excellent ductility.

Whether it was for making storage artifacts at the magic treasure level or large teleportation formations, this item was essential.

Luo Chen quickly walked over, his gaze falling into that box, and a look of joy spread across his face.

Sure enough!

As he had expected, since Green Parasol Mountain could set up such a super-large teleportation formation, there would inevitably be a large amount of Spatial Prism Aperture.

What he hadn't expected was that so much remained.

Enough for him to set up several large teleportation formations in the Eastern Wilderness.

Pure Crane breathed a sigh of relief, looked at Luo Chen, and suddenly asked curiously, "Hall Master, that Moon servant seems to have some friction with you, doesn't she?"

Luo Chen, while inspecting the quality of the Spatial Prism Aperture, gave a noncommittal hum.

Pure Crane became even more curious. "If that's the case, why doesn't the Hall Master punish her?"

"Not yet the time," Luo Chen said calmly.

Pure Crane seemed to understand. "Is it because the Primordial Lord has only recently ascended, so the Hall Master finds it inconvenient to make a move against the people she left behind?"

"That's part of the reason," Luo Chen acknowledged this explanation.

Pure Crane smiled slightly. She hadn't guessed wrong. But she was also puzzled—besides this factor, were there other reasons?

Luo Chen waved his hand and stored the entire box of Spatial Prism Aperture into his storage ring.

He turned around, his gaze falling elsewhere, but his vision was not focused, appearing somewhat ethereal.

"Tell me, how many demon cultivators on Green Parasol Mountain are truly loyal to me now?"

Pure Crane was startled. She instinctively wanted to say all of them, but recalling the earlier situation, her expression turned somewhat ugly for a moment.

She forced herself to say, "You are the Truth Crossing Hall Master. You inherently have the authority to command all the demons. How dare they disobey?"

Luo Chen shook his head. "This authority stems from Rosycloud. Now that the Primordial Lord has departed, the mere title of Truth Crossing Hall Master is not enough to command obedience! Moreover, the vast million mountains are not just this small patch of Green Parasol Mountain!"

"Hall Master, do you mean... to lure the snake out of its hole?" Pure Crane vaguely guessed Luo Chen's intention.

Luo Chen chuckled lightly. "That woman has never been a peaceful one, never. Since we have to settle accounts with her sooner or later, let's use her hand to remove some minor irritants!"

Pure Crane suddenly understood, revealing a bright smile.

"The Hall Master is wise!"

As she spoke, she secretly leaned toward Luo Chen, her fragrant, alluring body almost pressing against him.

However, an invisible force gently pushed her away.

Pure Crane's expression stiffened.

"Do your work for me, and I will not treat you unfairly. Next, inventory all the medicinal herbs remaining in the treasury. I have another use for them."

Pure Crane was as if relieved of a heavy burden, and her stiff expression instantly became natural again.

Since Luo Chen had no interest, she would not force it, lest she offend him.

The work of inventorying the Green Parasol Treasury was somewhat heavy. After all, this was the thousand-year accumulation of Green Parasol Mountain. It took Luo Chen a full three days to barely get a general idea.

Some unimportant things were left at Green Parasol Mountain.

But those rare treasures were all taken by him, filling ten storage magic treasures and over twenty storage pouches.

With this heavy harvest, Luo Chen temporarily left Green Parasol Mountain.

And after he left, the undercurrents of Green Parasol Mountain began to surge under the instigation of those with ulterior motives.

Luo Chen didn't care.

This was all to be expected, the same anywhere.

Those demon cultivators, even if they had faced Luo Chen's might and heard of his feat of slaying a great power in reverse, Luo Chen was ultimately just a human.

Moreover, when Liancheng fell back then, the outside world thought Azure Frost had also intervened. This feat of slaying a great power in reverse had been somewhat diminished in its deterrent effect.

Coupled with the enviable treasure land that was Green Parasol Mountain...

Let them stir for a while!

In the face of absolute strength, so-called surging undercurrents were nothing more than trickling streams, easily extinguished with a flip of the hand.

...

Shangyou City.

The current temporary location of the Luo Tian Sect's mountain gate.

It was a sea of people, with streaks of flying light like threads, bustling and prosperous—a sign of a thriving great sect.

Only, it lacked a bit of the immortal's quiet cultivation atmosphere.

Luo Chen's return did not disturb many people.

For the cultivators of the Luo Tian Sect, the Supreme Elder's frequent appearances were a thing of two hundred years ago. Now, they were accustomed to the Supreme Elder's style of staying out of sight, not moving unless necessary, and shaking the world when he did!

The Luo Tian Grand Hall stood tall and majestic.

The young Luo Lingxi stood guard outside the hall, her face faintly excited.

Inside the hall, Li Yingzhang was listening to Luo Chen's words.

Regarding the construction of a teleportation formation directly connecting Shangyou City to Green Parasol Mountain, he had no objections. Everything would follow the Supreme Elder's instructions.

But after Luo Chen finished giving his orders, he proactively brought up a matter.

"You want to relinquish the position of Sect Leader?"

Luo Chen was somewhat surprised, not expecting this development.

Li Yingzhang was frank and open. "Yes. I assumed the position of Sect Leader two hundred years ago, during the time of Baili Qingchuan. Now, the time has come."

Luo Chen said regretfully, "You have done very well. In these years, the Luo Tian Sect has produced several Sages, and the number of Adept cultivators has increased to over thirty. While some of this is due to my pill medicines, your management skills cannot be overlooked."

Li Yingzhang smiled. "Thank you for the praise, Supreme Elder. That was all within my duties. However, I also have selfish motives. I also wish to become one of those several Sages!"

Luo Chen suddenly understood. He had forgotten about this.

Calculating carefully, Li Yingzhang's age was already considerable. He didn't have many years left before the five-hundred-year limit for Adept cultivators.

And his cultivation had long reached the ninth layer of the Adept realm for quite some time. It was also time to prepare and make a breakthrough to the Sage realm before the great limit arrived.

"Do you have confidence?" Luo Chen asked with concern.

Li Yingzhang said freely, "If even I, the Sect Leader of the Luo Tian Sect, do not have confidence, then who would dare to have confidence?"

Luo Chen smiled slightly. He had been overthinking.

Whether it was pill medicines, formation arrays, cultivation techniques, or even spiritual vein secret arts, Sage-forming insights and experiences, Li Yingzhang's preparations were now more thorough than Luo Chen's back then!

"It's a pity about Min Longyu." Li Yingzhang suddenly sighed, his expression slightly dim. "If he hadn't been severely injured in his early years, damaging his foundation, he would probably have succeeded in forming his Sage before me. In the end, he failed in his breakthrough and perished, his path cut short."

Min Longyu's fall had occurred during the twenty years Luo Chen was recuperating from his injuries.

He was older than Luo Chen and the others, so his great limit came earlier.

Stimulated by the feat of the Luo Tian Sect's Sages following Luo Chen to attack the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, he attempted to forcefully break through to the Sage realm before his great limit, using his late-stage Adept cultivation.

The final result was regrettable.

The atmosphere inside the hall was somewhat oppressive.

Luo Chen sighed. "Don't think too much about it. Don't let it affect you. However, before you step down, have you thought about the candidate for the next Sect Leader?"

(End of this chapter)
