# Chapter 655: Entering the Sunken Sea, the Desolate Beast Ghost Immortal Tower

A pale golden sky, a pitch-black sea.

Where sea and sky met, there was boundless emptiness without human trace.

Only hundreds of vortexes rose and fell above the endless ocean, devouring heaven and earth spiritual qi in great mouthfuls.

Luo Chen stood in the air, robes snapping loudly, gazing at this scene in amazement.

"So this is golden sky and black sea? Hearing of it a hundred times is inferior to seeing it once. It truly is peerlessly magnificent!"

Facing the breeze, Cave Master Moyun stroked his beard and was likewise amazed.

"I wonder what kind of great divine ability the owner of the ruins used to arrange this formation, to actually change the environment of the entire Sunken Sea and even keep it operating automatically a thousand years after his death."

Both were famous artifact forgers and possessed considerable formation attainments.

Before such a divine, world-moving peerless grand formation that could trigger celestial phenomena, it was natural their hearts could not remain calm.

Only Ding Yi was full of impatience at this moment.

"Enough, enough. It is fine for Devil Lord Azure Yang to marvel, but Cave Master, you and I have already come to see this several times. Why act like this?"

Cave Master Moyun chuckled. "Repeated views still feel new."

"Hmph."

Ding Yi snorted and, in front of the three, summoned a beast from the storage bag at his waist.

As soon as the beast landed in the water, it made a thunderous sound, as if a continent had fallen from the sky and smashed into the sea.

If Ding Yi had not arranged a sound-isolation art in advance, some nearby wandering cultivators reluctant to leave might have been drawn over.

Luo Chen looked carefully and could not help giving a soft sound of surprise.

"Hm? Is this that Treasure Basin Fish?"

Ding Yi nodded. "You are not mistaken. It is indeed that middle third-tier Black-Green Treasure Basin Fish. This is the treasure I prepared to cross the Sunken Sea."

Within his sight, an enormous fish several dozen zhang long and wide rose and fell on the sea surface. The exposed scales were covered with moss.

Without doubt, this was clearly the Black-Green Treasure Basin Fish that had caused a small stir at the Jadecold City auction.

It was known as a mobile spiritual vein and portable cave residence.

But unlike when Luo Chen had seen it back then, there were no rocks, soil, flowers, grasses, or trees on its back. Instead, there was a demon beast like a turtle and softshell turtle lying there.

The two were fused together, inseparable, and looked very unusual.

Seeing Luo Chen's surprise, Cave Master Moyun revealed a proud smile.

"Daoist Azure Yang, what do you think of this old man's method?"

"This is your method?"

Cave Master Moyun smiled smugly. "Even in ancient times, this method of refining living things as artifacts was rarely used. I refined the Shore Softshell Beast and the Black-Green Treasure Basin Fish together while alive. In this way, it gained many wondrous uses. Most importantly, the Shore Softshell Beast contains Black Tortoise bloodline and can resist the Void Burning Wind!"

Luo Chen's eyes lit up. The other party's creativity was indeed good.

To move through the Sunken Sea, one had to face the black turbid seawater that corroded everything and the Void Burning Wind.

The former corroded mana, and the latter disturbed the soul.

Clearly, this Treasure Basin Fish, known as a mobile spiritual vein, did not lack spiritual qi at all. And with the Shore Softshell Beast resisting the Void Burning Wind for it, cultivators could rely on it for movement and ensure their condition remained complete.

"Wonderful! Truly wonderful!" Luo Chen praised sincerely.

Ding Yi had not spoken much, instead casting spells at the side.

The enormous treasure fish below began continuously shrinking from several dozen zhang until finally only one zhang in size.

Only then did he free his hands and look at Luo Chen.

"Wonderful it may be, but during the refining process, this beast lost much vital energy. I nurtured it for over ten years before it barely recovered. Now, it can carry at most two people. So Devil Lord, you will have to manage yourself."

Luo Chen raised a brow but did not grow angry.

"Do not worry. I have my own preparations and will not drag you down."

"That is good!"

As the words fell, Ding Yi landed directly within the Shore Softshell shell on the Treasure Basin Fish's back and walked inside.

Cave Master Moyun cupped his hands apologetically to Luo Chen with a smile, then also flew inside.

One zhang in size, it held two people without seeming crowded.

Luo Chen smiled and slapped the storage bag at his waist.

"Come out."

Black King appeared again.

As soon as he appeared, he looked left and right, then finally looked fearfully at the dark ocean.

"Master, do we really have to do this?"

"After devouring and digesting the Sea-Overturning Demon Jiao, did you not also gain the ability to temporarily resist the corrosion of black turbid seawater?"

Black King was troubled. "But you also said it was temporary! This journey is long. If my strength cannot hold..."

Luo Chen glared at him irritably. "Relax. When your strength cannot hold, I will act. I will not tire you out."

Black King perked up a little, but then twisted his body.

"What about the Void Burning Wind?"

Luo Chen helplessly shook his head and pointed at the Daoist robe on his body. Black armor immediately spread out from it, gradually covering Black King's enormous body.

"Shrink a little."

"Oh."

Black King obediently reduced his body to around five zhang. Any smaller would affect his immediate combat strength.

From this point, Cave Master Moyun's living-refined Treasure Basin Shore Softshell Beast did indeed have some uniqueness.

"Devil Lord Azure Yang, are you ready?"

"Coming!"

Luo Chen answered and stepped onto Black King's back, sitting cross-legged.

The next moment, a green fish and a black jiao advanced side by side, swimming into the black, sunken seawater.

Inside the Shore Softshell shell, Ding Yi looked through a gap and frowned slightly at the black jiao outside.

"I have never heard of a jiao transformed from a Black-Scaled Giant Python possessing the ability to resist the Sunken Sea's water. This Daoist Azure Yang is lucky."

Cave Master Moyun stood at the side, staring outside with burning eyes.

After a while, he gently shook his head.

"Do not worry. That incompletely transformed black jiao is still inferior to our Treasure Basin Shore Softshell Beast."

In outsiders' eyes, Black King indeed belonged to an incomplete transformation. He clearly had jiao dragon horns, yet lacked dragon claws, and his body shape was closer to a snake or python.

And Cave Master Moyun's words were indeed verified.

After swimming in the sea for half a day, Black King showed signs of fatigue.

Whenever this happened, Luo Chen, sitting on his back, would proactively release a mana barrier to resist the black turbid seawater coming from every direction.

One man and one jiao alternated resisting the outside world like this, ensuring their speed did not drop.

Ding Yi watched for a long time and saw the trick, but he was also somewhat puzzled.

"Then how are they resisting the Void Burning Wind?"

Cave Master Moyun pointed without thinking at the black armor covering both Luo Chen and the jiao dragon.

"It should be the work of that item."

On Black King's back, Luo Chen's eyes shone as his gaze surveyed all directions at any moment.

In this seawater, divine sense was suppressed and could not spread far.

His eyes, nourished for many years, could instead see more through the layers of black turbid seawater.

Suddenly, Luo Chen's entire body shook, and his soul became restless. Black King beneath him was the same.

Just now, it seemed a clear breeze had brushed past.

"So that is the Void Burning Wind?"

Luo Chen pressed one hand against Black King's back to calm him and used the other to touch the hot Black Dust Armor.

Just now, when the Void Burning Wind blew past, this upper-grade magic treasure seemed to have been summoned and automatically activated for an instant.

That activation speed was so fast it was like instinct.

No other secret art could withstand this wind when it came soundlessly and tracelessly.

"An echo from the Black Tortoise race's bloodline?"

Luo Chen was thoughtful. Only this reason could explain that situation.

If cultivators without Black Tortoise treasures had been in that situation just now, their souls would probably have been affected instantly, causing them to lose control of their mana and be buried in the boundless Sunken Sea.

It could be said that with this point alone, the ancient cultivator ruins on the far shore of the Sunken Sea could avoid being entered by the world's countless cultivators.

In today's cultivation world, the desolate beast Black Tortoise had long since vanished.

Demon beasts containing Black Tortoise bloodline were scattered across the world, but they were also gradually declining.

One could imagine that if these demon beast races could not grow in the future, after they gradually declined, later generations would find it even harder to enter those ruins.

"Perhaps I was not wrong to come on this trip."

Luo Chen smiled and casually swallowed a supreme-grade True Flame Pill.

After the pill was refined, waves of pure spiritual qi spread through his qi sea.

The Golden Core hidden in his lifebound magic treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, spun and began absorbing this spiritual qi.

Within the qi sea, his second Origin Pill remained still and did not compete for this pure spiritual qi.

Three people and two beasts advanced rapidly toward the depths of the Sunken Sea.

Their speed was very fast, far surpassing many wandering cultivators who had not prepared enough.

On the way to the vortex entrance Ding Yi spoke of, Luo Chen also saw several unfamiliar Golden Core cultivators slowly swimming through the Sunken Sea using strange magic treasures.

One among them seemed to run out of mana. As he swam, he suddenly froze, then silently sank into the endless seabed.

Some matters could not be hidden from others.

As more and more people entered the Sunken Sea one after another, the relevant solutions naturally spread out.

Treasures with Black Tortoise bloodline.

If one deliberately searched, one could always find a few.

After all, this thing was mostly found on turtle and softshell-type demon beasts, and these demon beasts were also the most suitable materials for refining defensive weapons.

However, these people had clearly thought too simply.

Black Tortoise bloodline treasures found in haste were ultimately not specially made. They might allow one to advance for a time in the Sunken Sea, but the deeper one went, the harder it became.

Unless they were like Luo Chen's Black Dust Armor.

Even though it was not specially made, its material was a genuine fourth-tier Black Tortoise remains, allowing it to forcibly withstand the Void Burning Wind in the depths of the Sunken Sea.

As they advanced, Luo Chen and the others gradually surpassed many latecomers, drawing ever closer to the vortex Ding Yi sensed.

According to Ding Yi, the Sunken Sea had three hundred and sixty vortexes devouring heaven and earth spiritual qi.

And the vortex he had identified was certainly one of the entrances connected to the ancient cultivator ruins' spirit medicine garden.

As long as they entered smoothly, they could easily obtain the things they wanted.

Tai Sui and the Five Elements Lotus Platform.

Whether others knew this secret, Luo Chen was unclear. But his own heart was incomparably hot.

The closer they came to the destination, the greater his anticipation.

On this day, more than a month had passed since Luo Chen and the others entered the sea. Calculating their full-speed advance, they had long since entered the deepest region of the Sunken Sea.

Black King's forward momentum suddenly stopped.

"Master, what is wrong?"

The one asking this question was also Ding Yi, not far away.

"Devil Lord Azure Yang, why have you stopped?"

Luo Chen's expression shifted uncertainly as he stood on Black King's back and looked into the distance.

"Ahead, there seems to be something."

"Something?" Ding Yi did not understand and spread his divine sense as far as possible, but discovered nothing.

Cave Master Moyun took out a mirror-like magic treasure, struck several spiritual seals, and a misty, turbid scene immediately appeared within it.

"There seems to be something blocking the front?"

He hesitated and asked, "Should we move a little closer to look?"

Ding Yi asked, "Devil Lord Azure Yang, what do you say?"

Luo Chen hesitated somewhat. This concerned the spirit medicine garden, and it left no room for too much hesitation.

"Then let us get a little closer."

That was what he said, but Black King's movements imperceptibly fell behind the great fish, clearly at Luo Chen's signal.

Ding Yi did not notice this point and controlled the spirit beast to continue forward.

Closer and closer.

Until two red suns entered their eyes.

"What is that?" Ding Yi was stunned.

How could there be red suns in the dark seabed?

The other two were also puzzled.

Luo Chen took a deep breath and wiped a finger across his eyes, activating Spiritual Eye Art to the extreme.

Not only that, even the origin power born after advancing to Ancient Wilderness Fourth Tier not long ago attached to his eye membranes.

Under blood-colored vision, Luo Chen vaguely saw something.

A colossal creature several hundred zhang tall stood before a tiny vortex, continuously reaching tentacles inside.

It seemed to want to enter.

Just as Luo Chen looked over, that fellow suddenly moved.

Two red suns met Luo Chen's eyes.

Boom!

In an instant, Luo Chen's mind shook violently.

The Rotten Ke Black Chess flickered fiercely, and black halos continuously overflowed, yet still could not block that strange pressure.

Within his sea of consciousness, Luo Chen immediately fell into boundless illusion.

There were layers of buildings, dense ghost qi, countless immortals coming and going, fairy maidens scattering flowers, and even among the jade towers and jeweled halls, an immortal looked down on heaven and earth, deriving a boundless cosmos.

"Wake!"

A low roar, like a needle stab, fell heavily into the depths of Luo Chen's soul.

He suddenly woke and discovered that only an instant had passed.

Within his sight, the blurred scene grew clearer.

Those two red suns were no suns at all. They were clearly the monster's eyes. Looking carefully, they did not seem like eyes, but like two living things.

Blood Lanterns.

Yes, Blood Lanterns, a type of third-tier Great Demon King Luo Chen had once killed.

Only compared to that powerful Demon King, the two Blood Lanterns in this monster's eyes were even larger, fully at fourth-tier level.

Not only that, its hands were not hands at all, but the tentacles of Great King Squids.

The monster lived at the bottom of the Sunken Sea, like a tall building. Blood Lanterns lived between its eyes, and Great King Squid tentacles covered its body, becoming its hair.

When Luo Chen woke from the illusion, the monster seemed somewhat surprised.

The hair behind it drifted out, turning into a mass of clawing tentacles that grabbed toward Luo Chen and the others.

Before it made this move, Luo Chen had already shouted sharply.

"Run!"

Without any hesitation, he had already been the first to flee.

Black King was slow, so Luo Chen directly stored him inside the spirit beast pouch. Braving the seabed environment full of mana-corroding water and Void Burning Wind, Luo Chen almost fled without choosing a path.

Behind him, Ding Yi and Cave Master Moyun also sensed something wrong and fled frantically.

But their movements were still a little slower.

That mass of tentacles directly grabbed the slower Treasure Basin Fish and dragged it back.

When the Treasure Basin Fish was taken, Ding Yi's face turned deathly pale.

Fortunately, Cave Master Moyun took out a pearl that transformed into a light screen covering the two of them.

"I will block the Void Burning Wind. Use your mana barrier to protect me, and run at full speed!"

The two cooperated seamlessly and immediately coordinated, accelerating their escape.

Seeing the mass of tentacles behind them return, the two used all their strength to flee. Cave Master Moyun even repeatedly smashed several bright red beads behind them.

Boom! Boom! Boom!

Violent explosions caused the surging seawater to boil.

Those tentacles first suffered the red bead explosions, then were corroded by the seawater. Their speed finally slowed.

Seizing this chance, Ding Yi formed a spiritual seal, and a piece of flesh on his body suddenly exploded.

"Escape!"

The next moment, red light filled the sky and wrapped around him and Cave Master Moyun, like an arrow released from a bowstring, cutting through countless seawater toward an unknown direction.

Nor was it unknown, because there was still a guide in that direction.

Ding Yi looked at Luo Chen's back with wings trembling and gnashed his teeth. This fellow ran far too fast.

Beside him, Cave Master Moyun said lowly, "We need to find an entrance immediately. The Black Tortoise bloodline contained in this Origin-Returning Pearl is not much. It can only serve as backup and needs time to accumulate strength."

Ding Yi naturally knew this.

At that exact moment, Luo Chen's figure ahead suddenly stopped.

A vortex neither large nor small was rising from the seabed.

This was its starting point, so its size was smaller. The vortex grew larger as it went upward. Who knew how spectacular it would be at the sea surface?

Luo Chen looked back at the two, then jumped in without hesitation.

Ding Yi and Cave Master Moyun also did not hesitate much. After rushing over, blood light flashed and they disappeared inside.

After the three vanished, several tentacles slowly flew over.

They circled the vortex once, then unwillingly withdrew.

Almost visible wind scraped across his entire body.

Luo Chen's whole body was covered by the qi armor of the Black Dust Armor, yet his soul was still extremely restless.

Suddenly, his body loosened.

Thud!

Luo Chen's feet stepped onto solid ground.

The next moment, two figures arrived close behind.

Crack!

Cave Master Moyun frowned at the Origin-Returning Pearl in his hand. It had shattered.

Beside his ear came Luo Chen's uncertain question, "What exactly was that monster?"

Ding Yi, collapsed on the ground and almost a whole size thinner, panted heavily with an extremely ugly expression.

"What else could it be? It was the legendary desolate beast that wanders the Northern Sea, the Ghost Immortal Tower!"

Ghost Immortal Tower?

Luo Chen was startled. Wasn't that the name of the second layer illusion of his Mirror Flower Water Moon? It had come from what the Primordial Demon Sect's white-robed cultivator had said.

Cave Master Moyun's expression was solemn. "Legs like elephant feet, body like a red tower. When it sits or stands, ghosts overlap in layers; when it runs, mountains and seas collapse. That truly was the legendary desolate beast: Ghost Immortal Tower!"

*(End of Chapter)*
