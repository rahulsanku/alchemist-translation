# Chapter 722: Split Heaven Makes Trouble, Domains Collide

"Brother Bi [TERM?], what did that unfamiliar Fellow Daoist want with you just now? Was it some Moluo Stream scoundrel looking for trouble?"

Bi Dan [TERM?] waved a hand, a gratified smile appearing on his face.

"Not that. The man called himself Jinghe. In the past, he was a neutral rogue cultivator at the Initiate realm. Because of the righteous-demonic war, he did not want to be dragged in, so he hid in a small place and cultivated in seclusion. Recently, he succeeded in forming his core, and after hearing that the righteous and demonic sides had stopped fighting, he returned."

"Jinghe?" His companion repeated the name. It was extremely unfamiliar, almost impossible to match to anyone.

Bi Dan [TERM?] smiled. "What he is called is not important. Rogue cultivators are all like this, hiding their heads and showing their tails. However, this person had a good friend who was once a Feiyun Ravine disciple. After hearing that Feiyun Ravine was destroyed, he came specially to ask me about the details. If we really speak of it, we can be considered united by shared hatred."

"So that is how it is!"

His companion came to a sudden understanding, then sighed.

"Who could have imagined that the famous Feiyun Ravine would turn to ash overnight?"

Bi Dan [TERM?] also sighed endlessly.

If Feiyun Ravine's master, Sage Feiyunzi, had not vanished for several decades, leaving that vast power without anyone to preside over it, a Sage-level upper sect would never have fallen to such a state no matter what.

What a pity about his younger sister...

...

"Sage, I have inquired clearly."

Sang Jinghe [TERM?] stood respectfully behind Luo Chen and reported everything he had discovered in full detail.

"In order to resolve the internal conflict among the human race as quickly as possible, the Cang Hai Alliance began a large-scale counterattack thirty years ago. Without the Penglai Immortal Sect's support, Moluo Stream retreated again and again..."

This matched.

Luo Chen remembered that when he had crossed human territory to go to Purple Spirit Island, he had seen how fierce the Cang Hai Alliance's offensive was.

In order to deal with Split Heaven Stream Lord [TERM?], they had even dispatched three Sage cultivators in one breath!

One could see the Cang Hai Alliance's determination.

"The unwilling Moluo Stream cultivators plundered wantonly before retreating. It happened that news of Demon-Fall Land had leaked at the time, and Feiyunzi was killed by Azure Yang..."

At this point, Sang Jinghe [TERM?] looked at Luo Chen with reverence.

"Continue!"

"Because of Feiyunzi's fall, Feiyun Ravine, this masterless Sage-level upper sect force, became the greatest target. Several Adept powers led their people into Feiyun Ravine, seizing its inheritance and plundering its resources. To prevent a snake from surviving the blow and retaliating, they were so deranged that they slaughtered all three thousand disciples and members from top to bottom."

"Were there no survivors?" Luo Chen asked.

"Naturally, there were, but they were all insignificant small figures who escaped by luck."

"What about the Gu siblings I asked you to investigate?"

Sang Jinghe's [TERM?] spirit lifted, and he explained carefully, "I pretended to ask about them casually. In that battle, from beginning to end, no one saw the Gu siblings. Even afterward, no trace of them appeared. To ensure the news, I also went to ask among the Initiate cultivators, but the situation was largely the same."

"Largely the same? Where was it different?"

Sang Jinghe [TERM?] froze, then said, "Someone did mention the Gu siblings, but it was only suspected. They said a woman suspected to be Gu Xiaolian appeared at the ruins of Feiyun Ravine, but she vanished very quickly."

"You may go."

"Jinghe withdraws."

On the tall building, Luo Chen rested his hand on the railing and tapped it lightly, his expression calm.

In his mind, he was thinking back to the final scene in Bright Radiance Heaven.

The furious Moon Wanderer, the nearly mad Blood Wanderer, and those two entirely different gazes at the palace gate.

One was a young woman covering her mouth as pearl-like tears filled her eyes. The other was a youth who said nothing, his killing intent overflowing to the heavens.

He remembered those two. When hunting Ring-Headed Turtles in the Xuanyan Sea Region, he had once met them. One was called Gu Shaoshang, and the other Gu Xiaolian.

They were siblings.

Their father was Feiyunzi!

If it were an ordinary time, Luo Chen naturally would not care. He had even killed Feiyunzi, much less two Adept juniors.

But he remembered very clearly that those siblings had respectively walked out from the halls of light and darkness!

What did that mean?

The answer was beyond doubt.

Especially the golden crown on Gu Xiaolian's head. It undoubtedly matched the demonic lord's legacy treasure, the True Yang Crown, that the Heaven-Refining Cauldron's artifact spirit had spoken of.

That was a powerful weapon ranked alongside the Heaven-Refining Cauldron and Black Devil Jar as a heaven-reaching spirit treasure [TERM?].

For Gu Xiaolian to obtain the True Yang Crown's recognition in the Hall of Light meant she had obtained the Hall of Light's inheritance.

Since Gu Shaoshang had walked out from the Dark Hall [TERM?], he had most likely obtained a corresponding inheritance as well.

In this way, their future potential was immeasurable!

And Luo Chen had killed their father right in front of them. This ocean-deep blood feud was an obstacle that could not be crossed no matter what.

If he could erase them before they grew up, that would settle everything once and for all.

But if he did not act in advance...

"Originally, I had wanted to make a trip to Feiyun Ravine and search for those siblings. Now that Feiyun Ravine has been destroyed, I have lost the target."

Luo Chen sighed. It was like a fishbone stuck in his throat.

The feeling of being watched by someone hidden in the dark was always unpleasant.

The only slightly better news was that after coming to the Northern Sea, he had always used an alias, and even his Daoist title had been Devil Lord Azure Yang.

The other party did not know his true identity.

After leaving Dragon Abyss Island, Luo Chen had resumed using his real name and was now traveling under the name Wilderness Wanderer [TERM?].

As for his appearance, he had made natural adjustments through his own body-refining art.

It would be difficult for anyone to connect him to the famed Devil Lord Azure Yang.

"Perhaps I should continue showing myself under the name Devil Lord Azure Yang and lure the siblings to come seek revenge on their own?"

This thought subconsciously flashed through Luo Chen's mind, but he quickly scraped it away.

Compared to the threat of those siblings, the name Devil Lord Azure Yang was more likely to attract that woman Moon Wanderer, and Luo Chen did not want another great escape across the Northern Sea.

"Forget it. So long as I cultivate properly and always walk ahead of them, they will be helpless against me."

"If they do not show themselves, all well and good. If they do, I naturally will not indulge them."

...

The heavy rain showed no sign of stopping.

The vast curtain of rain filled heaven and earth, as if it wished to wash clean the bloodshed of the hundred-year righteous-demonic war.

Luo Chen, dressed in white, walked with Sage Langya along a bluestone path, step by step toward a side hall inside Molan Court [TERM?].

The afternoon gathering of Sage cultivators would be held there. Sage Langya had already introduced Luo Chen, and he had obtained the qualifications to attend.

At this moment, Sage Langya was introducing to Luo Chen the likely origins of the powerful figures who might attend.

"On the Cang Hai Alliance's side, quite a few Sage cultivators have come to attend the War-Ceasing Assembly [TERM?], as many as seven or eight. However, because the one in charge is the mid-stage Sage Mu Lan Gaoxiong [TERM?], these seven or eight do not possess especially high realms. At most, they are only about equal to Mu Lan Gaoxiong [TERM?]."

"Do not look down on them because their realms are not high. Almost all of them dominate a region and are famous throughout the Northern Sea cultivation world."

"For instance, that Four Seas Sage [TERM?]. His name is Shang Sihai [TERM?], and under him is the Four Seas Merchant Alliance [TERM?], a rather enormous force. Within the merchant alliance are also two early-stage Sage guest elders. It can be said that the Four Seas Merchant Alliance [TERM?] itself is an extremely powerful force within the Cang Hai Alliance. For this gathering, he is the one on the Cang Hai Alliance's side threading the needle."

"You should have heard the name Old Ghost Nanqi [TERM?]."

Luo Chen thought for a moment, then slowly nodded. "Is he that old monster who was once refined into a Soul-Refining Banner by a Primordial Demon Sect cultivator, then devoured his master in return and stole his cultivation?"

"Correct! Fellow Daoist is indeed widely learned, no ordinary rogue cultivator."

Sage Langya smiled, then his face turned solemn. "This person's true background is very likely from the Southern Frontier, and he is skilled in the dao of gu insects [TERM?]. After all, how could an ordinary cultivator refined into a soul banner turn defeat into victory, not only remaining unharmed, but even advancing greatly in cultivation?"

Old Ghost Nanqi [TERM?], a Southern Frontier cultivator?

Luo Chen nodded slightly and remembered the description of this person's appearance. In the future, he intended even more not to interact with him much.

"Madam Mi [TERM?] comes from..."

"Mu Lan Gaoxiong [TERM?] should not attend this gathering. On his side..."

"The Tianyuan Merchant Alliance elder has also not appeared much recently, but it is said that this person is very enthusiastic about befriending fellow cultivators. There is a possibility he will show himself this time."

As Sage Langya spoke, he changed direction and began introducing the people from Moluo Stream who might arrive.

"Netherfire Stream Lord [TERM?] will certainly appear! He was the Stream Lord who was heavily injured among the one dead and one wounded under the furious Mu Lan Gaoxiong [TERM?] that year. This Sage gathering was also called and initiated by him. His purpose should be to seek resources at the gathering that can resolve his injuries."

"This person originally had extremely strong combat power. At his peak, he was on par with Mu Lan Gaoxiong [TERM?]. After that battle, not only was he heavily injured, his prestige within Moluo Stream also plummeted, and his own faction shrank considerably."

"There is also a woman named Du Feiyan [TERM?]. Wanderer, you must treat her carefully as well. This woman is the only female among Moluo Stream's Twelve Stream Lords. To stand alongside a crowd of demons, her ability absolutely cannot be underestimated."

"I need not say much about Split Heaven Stream Lord [TERM?]. He was the person who stood out most during the hundred-year righteous-demonic war. Though his realm is only the third level of the Sage stage, his wind-attribute cultivation art is incomparably sharp. He comes without trace and leaves without shadow, and his ability in combat is absolutely no weaker than a mid-stage Sage. To deal with him, the Cang Hai Alliance once dispatched three Sage cultivators in one breath, yet still could not take him down.

"Most importantly, he is one of the few people within Moluo Stream who was very close to Blood Nightmare Moluo. Now that Blood Nightmare Moluo has become a stray dog, his own days inside Moluo Stream are not easy. Logically, he should be hiding his light and biding his time, yet for some reason, he has actively come to attend this gathering."

One name after another, all famous throughout the Northern Sea cultivation world, fell into Luo Chen's ears, making his heart turn solemn.

In the past, these figures had been like stars in the sky.

Now they were each about to truly appear before him, and for a moment, his heart even felt a little excited.

But very quickly, Luo Chen calmed his emotions.

The fact that he could participate in this gathering meant he was an existence of the same level.

Naturally, he would no longer look upon these people with an upturned gaze.

Especially Sage cultivators?

He had fought them before, even killed them!

There was no need to be overly cautious!

As the two whispered and strolled leisurely, the bluestone path had already come to an end.

A spacious red-jade plaza instantly entered their sight.

Beneath the dim rain curtain, this red-jade plaza seemed rather oppressive.

At this moment, streaks of escaping light were arriving from every direction, sweeping over the red-jade plaza and flying into that palace.

Return Origin Palace [TERM?]!

It was said that in the past, this palace had been used by Blood Nightmare Moluo to house the Primordial Demon remnants who had come to join him.

No one had expected that Blood Nightmare Moluo would personally refine these younger disciples into Blood God Children.

It was as if the red of this plaza had been dyed by the blood of those Primordial Demon disciples.

"Let us go in as well. The people should nearly all have arrived."

Sage Langya spoke and was about to step forward.

Yet he discovered Luo Chen had not moved at all, but was calmly gazing at the sky.

Sage Langya followed his gaze and looked in that direction.

There were two figures standing in the rain-filled sky, staring at them.

"That is... Split Heaven Stream Lord [TERM?]!"

Immediately after, Sage Langya's expression changed slightly. "Why is he together with Old Ghost Nanqi [TERM?]?"

At this moment.

Split Heaven Stream Lord [TERM?] stepped forward, looking down from above at Mei Yiqi and Luo Chen, his gaze fixed especially on Luo Chen.

His eyes were contemptuous, and his lips thin and sharp.

"Nowadays, any stray nobody dares to crawl out, and even some nameless person fantasizes about sitting at the same table as us."

Sage Langya frowned. "Split Heaven, you go too far. Wilderness Wanderer is my friend. His attendance at this gathering was also approved by Four Seas Sage [TERM?]."

"Shang Sihai [TERM?]? This is not his Four Seas Merchant Alliance [TERM?], and it is not his place to decide. Mei Yiqi, with that overly nice temperament of yours, do you intend to stand up for him?"

"You!"

Sage Langya's expression turned ugly, and he lifted his right hand.

Before he could speak, Luo Chen behind him pressed it down.

Luo Chen stepped forward and calmly looked at the sinister man in the sky.

This targeting had come far too without reason!

He said mildly, "Split Heaven, what do you intend?"

Split Heaven Stream Lord's [TERM?] mouth tugged into a cold smile. "In the vast Northern Sea, I have only ever heard of Blood Wanderer, Moon Wanderer, and Mad Wanderer. I have never heard of any Wilderness Wanderer. What ability do you have to presume you can stand alongside them?"

"What ability does this wanderer possess? Do you want to see it?"

"I do!"

Before the word had fully fallen, the rain curtain between heaven and earth abruptly stopped, and an incomparably sharp aura came tearing heaven and rending earth!

Luo Chen smiled faintly. His white robes snapped loudly in the raging wind as a blazing, domineering aura rose like fierce fire.

Sage Langya retreated several steps, worry on his face.

Inside Return Origin Palace [TERM?], the Sage cultivators who had already arrived naturally sensed the sudden eruption of two auras.

They all extended divine sense to look.

Seeing that it was Split Heaven Stream Lord [TERM?] making trouble, all of them were speechless.

This person's wild and perverse temperament was rare even among Sage cultivators.

No wonder he had been able to draw close to someone like Blood Nightmare Moluo.

Only, to vent the anger accumulated over these days of negotiation onto a newly advanced rogue Sage cultivator was truly a little excessive.

"That fellow called Luo Chen is probably going to have bad luck."

"Then he deserves bad luck. Right now, Blood Wanderer is chasing Split Heaven Stream Lord's good friend Blood Nightmare Moluo all over the world. Split Heaven Stream Lord does not dare provoke Blood Wanderer, but taking his anger out on this so-called Wilderness Wanderer should be fine."

"He could have taken any title, yet he had to call himself Wilderness Wanderer. Where does he get the confidence to stand alongside the Three Great Wanderers? This concubine once met Moon Wanderer in years past and was astonished as though seeing a celestial. Now she has reached late-stage Sage, only one step from Ascendant..."

Ignoring how everyone in the palace looked down on him.

Shang Sihai [TERM?], one of the two initiators of this gathering, had a somewhat ugly expression.

He looked at the sickly red-haired elder opposite him and said coldly, "Netherfire, are your people not going a little too far?"

The sickly red-haired elder lifted his eyelids and said casually, "Split Heaven, no fighting during the War-Ceasing Assembly [TERM?]. Do not provoke everyone's displeasure."

His words were mild, yet they rang out like thunder.

This amounted to defining the matter: so long as they did not start fighting, it was fine.

And if that Wilderness Wanderer performed too shamefully, presumably he would have no face to continue attending this gathering.

He knew Split Heaven Stream Lord's [TERM?] thoughts perfectly well.

Old Ghost Nanqi [TERM?] watched with a chuckle as Split Heaven Stream Lord [TERM?] unfolded his Sage domain, roughly guessing the other party's idea.

In the hundred-year righteous-demonic war, Split Heaven Stream Lord [TERM?] had made many enemies.

His good friend Blood Nightmare Moluo had also become a stray dog.

It could be said that whether on Moluo Stream's side or the Cang Hai Alliance's side, he was effectively isolated.

Especially in the negotiations of the War-Ceasing Assembly [TERM?], several clauses targeted him, requiring him to provide compensation.

Under such circumstances, Split Heaven Stream Lord [TERM?] had to show his toughness to make later bargaining easier.

Targeting Wilderness Wanderer was not really venting anger. It was merely killing a chicken to warn the monkeys.

So be it!

If he wanted to obtain that thing, he still had to rely on Split Heaven. Later, if anyone blamed Split Heaven, he would casually say a few good words for him.

At this moment, as one of the people involved, Split Heaven Stream Lord [TERM?] once again felt that strange sensation arise in his heart.

His dislike for Luo Chen was absolutely not without reason.

It might have been because of the Wanderer title, or because he wanted to kill a chicken to warn the monkeys, but the true reason that made him decide to target Luo Chen was that seemingly real yet false sense of familiarity in his heart.

"I have absolutely seen him somewhere!"

"Since you want to use a false name and false surname to conceal yourself, I will force you to reveal your true body and see what divine being you really are!"

His mind settled. His Nascent Soul opened its eyes, and magic power surged wildly out, drawing in the spiritual qi of heaven and earth to form a violent Sage domain.

Countless wind-attribute strands of spiritual qi gathered from every direction and crashed down toward the white-robed cultivator.

He was very confident. Under the suppression of his Sage domain, Luo Chen, a mere first-level Sage, absolutely could not resist.

If Luo Chen still wanted to participate in this gathering, he would have to reveal some clue no matter what.

But the next instant, Split Heaven Stream Lord's [TERM?] eyes abruptly widened!

Vast spiritual qi of heaven and earth gathered over, forming two great Sage domains, one azure and one red.

The originally dominant azure Sage domain not only failed to suppress the red domain, but instead retreated step by step as the other side constantly rose.

Not only that, Split Heaven Stream Lord [TERM?] could even sense that the wind-attribute spiritual qi he controlled was being continuously melted away, continuously collapsing.

No matter how he repeatedly mobilized magic power, he was completely unable to stop this trend of defeat.

The originally paused torrential rain curtain fell again amid the clash of the azure and red domains.

Only, each raindrop, because of the scorching high temperature, transformed into boiling, terrifying steam that rolled toward Split Heaven Stream Lord [TERM?].

"Such nerve!"

Split Heaven Stream Lord [TERM?] swept his sleeve and fanned away the scalding steam, and a flying sword abruptly appeared in his hand.

"Split Heaven."

Netherfire's weak, breathless voice came again.

Split Heaven Stream Lord [TERM?] took a deep breath and had no choice but to put the flying sword away.

On the red-jade plaza, Luo Chen smiled faintly, and the Forest of All Things Fire Prison [TERM?] appeared and vanished in a flash.

"Thank you for yielding!"

"Hmph. Wilderness Wanderer, is it? I will remember you!"

Split Heaven glared fiercely at Luo Chen, then flicked his sleeve and entered the palace.

Before entering, Old Ghost Nanqi [TERM?] looked deeply at Luo Chen, rather astonished.

Not only them, even the Sage cultivators inside the palace found it unbelievable.

Split Heaven Stream Lord [TERM?], at the third level of the Sage stage, had actually lost to the first-level Sage Luo Chen in a domain collision.

Inside the silent palace, a soft sigh sounded.

"From now on, the Northern Sea will have legends of Four Great Wanderers [TERM?]."
