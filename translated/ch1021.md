# Chapter 1021: Mirror Flower Water Moon, the Dao Sect's Heavenly South

Once upon a time, the Twelve Great Houses of Central Continent stood high above all.

Not only did they occupy most of the Dao Sect's power, but they also held the twelve elder seats within the Star Gate organization. Even the renowned Heavenly Origin Five Elders all came from the Twelve Great Houses.

It could be said that the Twelve Great Houses were the most core existence of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. Each Great House possessed strength comparable to a top-tier Sage-level sect.

And the Shi family was one of them.

Coincidentally, this clan had more or less some grudges with Luo Chen.

To be precise, *they* had grudges with Luo Chen. Luo Chen himself didn't really want to have anything to do with them.

A proud young man's face flashed through his mind. Luo Chen focused his attention on the Shi family Sage before him, who was at the early Sage stage.

Mirror Flower Water Moon unfolded. With Luo Chen's terrifying divine soul foundation, Shi Chuanting had almost no resistance and was deeply trapped within it.

This was the performance Luo Chen's illusion arts should have!

As long as he didn't encounter a divine soul defense spirit treasure, or the interference of a large formation formed by multiple people, among those of the same rank, there were very few who could block his attack, let alone look him straight in the eye without being affected.

Perhaps even an Ascendant facing a Sage would be no more than this!

Following his will, Luo Chen excavated everything Shi Chuanting knew about the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect of Central Continent.

The intelligence from Green Parasol Mountain was ultimately from over thirty years ago, and it was collected from the demon race's perspective. How could it be as clear as what a party involved knew?

Luo Chen was going to Central Continent next, so naturally, he had to be fully prepared.

"Name?"

"Shi Chuanting."

"Position?"

"Temporary executive elder of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect stationed in the Eastern Wilderness, responsible for gathering intelligence on the Eastern Wilderness and supervising various Sage-level sects in paying tribute."

"How many existences like you are there?"

"Four in total, all from the Great Houses."

"Decades ago, when Rosycloud attacked Central Continent, were the Twelve Great Houses damaged after the battle?"

"Although the casualties were heavy, the Twelve Great Houses all remain. None were removed from the roster."

Hearing this, Luo Chen's expression changed slightly.

He hadn't witnessed that battle personally, but he knew how brutal the war had been.

Even Heaven-Earth Peak, which had the "Severing Heaven-Earth Passage Grand Formation" laid upon it, had been broken off. The entire Central Continent had been shattered into pieces. Yet the Twelve Great Houses were still intact. This was truly astonishing.

One could only say they deserved to be called ancient families!

From this news, Luo Chen also understood why, despite suffering such a great calamity, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect as a sect organization still hadn't collapsed.

Because the Twelve Great Houses were the core that formed the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect of the Mountains and Seas Realm.

Luo Chen suppressed his shock and continued questioning: "You say the casualties were heavy. How heavy exactly?"

An uncontrollable expression of sorrow appeared on Shi Chuanting's confused face.

"Central Continent had many fifth-rank spirit lands, but Lanke Mountain and Heaven-Earth Peak were the most important. Coupled with the Star Gate system, when the incident occurred, the supreme elders of each Great House were all on Heaven-Earth Peak. They were connected to the grand formation, sharing the fate of the spirit peak. When the formation collapsed and the mountain broke, they fell with it. This was the greatest loss."

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow, quite agreeing with this explanation.

When he had joined the Star Gate, he had glimpsed the nineteen Star Gate elders.

Apart from Chu Yan, the Ascendant-stage Alchemy Saint, the other eighteen were all late-stage Sage Grand Cultivators. This included those high-cultivation supreme elders from the Twelve Great Houses.

Although these people were advanced in age and didn't hold specific positions in the Twelve Great Houses, they were undoubtedly the strongest martial guarantee and spiritual symbols for their families. The fall of each one was, in significance, comparable to the fall of Divine Origin to the Five Elements Divine Sect.

"In the great battle of Central Continent, it was a battlefield for great powers. Ordinary people couldn't intervene at all. Instead, with the aftershocks of the great powers' attacks, the cultivators of various families were all affected, suffering heavy casualties."

"The Mo family among the Twelve Great Houses was the clan of Ancestor Mo Shouzhuo, the current sect master. They were responsible for guarding key nodes and positions, so they suffered the heaviest losses. They were almost wiped out in that battle. It was only after Mo Shengxing returned from the Northern Sea battlefield that the Mo family was rebuilt."

"The Zhan family, known as the strongest combat force among the Twelve Great Houses, also fell from grace. Their ancestor's soul was severed at the Yellow Spring Sea. Their grand elder died on Heaven-Earth Peak. The Zhan family disciples exhausted all their strength to kill the archaic beast Netherworld Earth Dragon. In the end, only the second elder, Zhan Bingtao, who was also at the late Sage stage, hastily returned to reorganize the family. But Zhan Bingtao was severely injured on the Southern Frontier battlefield and now doesn't bother much with family affairs."

"Furthermore, the Gu, Tan, and Ancient Gu families were not much better off. Their family bases were at the core of the battlefield. Even using all their strength, they only managed to preserve a few clansmen."

Although Shi Chuanting was far away in Central Continent, he was quite familiar with the situation within the sect and the current strength of each family.

Thus, as he spoke now, his reasoning was clear and organized.

It was under this detailed report that Luo Chen learned just how weak those families, which had once been so powerful he dared not look them in the eye, now were.

At their peak, the Twelve Great Houses had no less than a double-digit number of late-stage Sage Grand Cultivators. The number of Sages who could take action was a terrifying figure.

But now, they had fallen to the point of having only a few cats and kittens.

One could see how devastating the blow Rosycloud's surprise attack on Central Continent had been to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

"Currently, among the Twelve Great Houses, the ones in slightly better condition are our Shi family, the Ke family with the Chess Saint, the Li family with the largest population, and... the Lian family, where the Ascendant ancestor resides."

When mentioning "our Shi family," a struggling expression faintly appeared on Shi Chuanting's face.

"Hmph."

Luo Chen snorted coldly. His divine sense spread out, and the power of Mirror Flower Water Moon grew stronger. Shi Chuanting's struggle was forcibly suppressed.

"Do you know the current distribution of each family?"

Shi Chuanting answered without thinking: "The Lian, Ke, Shi, and Li families are all on the Eastern Origin Continent, rebuilding their family territories with the original Lanke Mountain Range as the core. The Gu, Tan, and Ancient Gu families are on the Libei Continent. The Mo and Zhan families, whose strength was most weakened, have fallen to the Heavenly South Continent."

Luo Chen nodded repeatedly. Part of this information matched what he already knew, confirming it.

It was clear that Shi Chuanting wasn't lying.

But Luo Chen waited for a while and still didn't hear the locations of the remaining three Great Houses.

He subconsciously asked: "What about the other three?"

Shi Chuanting replied blankly: "They are already gone!"

"Hmm?" Luo Chen was stunned. Hadn't he said earlier that all twelve Great Houses still existed?

But then he suddenly reacted. He had asked about the time when Rosycloud attacked Central Continent. At that time, indeed, all twelve Great Houses had barely survived.

However, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's blows hadn't stopped at just that one time.

After the Central Continent war, the top-tier archaic beast of the Mountains and Seas Realm, the Diaohun Archaic, was awakened by the Heavenly Origin Five Elders to fight Rosycloud. Wherever it passed, mountains and seas overturned, and heaven and earth cracked apart.

The original Central Continent, which had split into five pieces, had one of its western continents directly rammed and sunk by it. All living beings on it were swept into the Yellow Spring Sea.

If nothing unexpected happened, the remaining three Great Houses—those three families that had stood for thousands of years and even survived the battle that shattered Central Continent—had all died in this unexpected disaster.

Luo Chen asked, and sure enough, Shi Chuanting gave the same answer.

After verification, even Luo Chen, who had anticipated this, couldn't help but sigh with emotion.

Shi Chuanting continued to divulge the intelligence he knew.

"Central Continent is vast and fertile. Even though it has split into several pieces, each piece is still a rare cultivation paradise."

"Besides the nine Great Houses, the surviving various aristocratic clans and many humble families are also distributed across the three continents."

"Furthermore, some guest elders who once joined the Star Gate, because of the collapse of Heaven-Earth Peak, were forced to descend from the mountain and cultivate on the three continents. Especially those guest elders who originally had blood descendants and disciples. Because Ancestor Liancheng relaxed the restrictions, their descendants also gained the opportunity to truly integrate into the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect."

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow. "Elaborate! Why did Ancestor Liancheng relax the restrictions?"

Shi Chuanting answered as a matter of course: "Once upon a time, Central Continent was the richest territory under heaven. Even after splitting into several pieces, it was still coveted. Our Dao Sect was severely damaged this time. To guard against potential dangers, besides recalling all scattered cultivators, we naturally had to cultivate a new generation of strong individuals. Relaxing restrictions on Star Gate guest elders was only one aspect. The top priority was to vigorously distribute the sect's thousands of years of accumulated resources and exhaust all efforts to cultivate a new generation of powerhouses. Ancestor Liancheng wants to use a hundred years of effort to lead the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect out of its current quagmire!"

As he spoke, an expression of "pride" that Luo Chen was very familiar with appeared on Shi Chuanting's face.

It was the pride that all holy land disciples wore on their faces.

Even if the Primordial Demon Sect was destroyed, even if the Nether Origin Sect was annihilated, even if the Dao Sect's ancestral court of Central Continent was shattered by someone, all these holy land disciples were still incomparably proud, as if the sect's glory had always been there.

"A hundred years of effort, out of the quagmire?"

Luo Chen muttered this phrase. His eyes, flickering with illusory light, revealed a hint of mockery.

He wanted to ask more, but his brow twitched as he sensed something.

The next moment, Luo Chen slowly disappeared from the spot.

A powerful divine sense swept over from afar, patrolling the surroundings for a long time, but still found nothing.

"Eh?"

The owner of the divine sense couldn't help but let out a light exclamation, as if he didn't understand where the previous strange feeling had come from.

His attention fell on the Dao Sect cultivator Shi Chuanting, who was still somewhat dazed in the courtyard.

"Fellow Daoist Shi?"

Shi Chuanting suddenly woke up, his face blank, his expression dazed.

He subconsciously responded, "Grand Elder."

"Hmm? Fellow Daoist Shi, are you alright?"

Shi Chuanting shook his head. "I'm fine. Grand Elder, is there something you need from me?"

"Nothing major. I just wanted to invite you for tea and Dao discussion, to exchange some experiences and insights."

Shi Chuanting tightened his face and pursed his lips as he replied, "Let's forget about today. I just finished drinking with Brother Gu. Tomorrow, tomorrow I'll make an appointment. I'll personally go to Chuyang Mountain to invite the Grand Elder to drink my Shi family's Thousand Autumn Brew."

"Very well!"

The aged voice gradually faded away.

And in the courtyard, Shi Chuanting's body trembled uncontrollably, his eyes filled with terror.

What had just happened!

It was as if he had dreamed of the Shi family's supreme elder, who had asked him about matters concerning the sect and family. He had answered everything he knew.

But he was a Sage!

How could he suddenly dream?

Heart demon confusion? Had he been hit by an illusion, bewitched by someone?

The former was terrifying, the latter even more unimaginable.

Unknowingly, Shi Chuanting's face was deathly pale, his body drenched in sweat.

On Chuyang Mountain!

The Grand Elder of Dongyang Sect had originally been puzzled, but the divine sense remotely monitoring Shi Chuanting fed back to him that the previous strangeness was definitely not without cause.

"Could it be that someone really infiltrated my Dongyang Sect silently?"

"Under my very nose, what did they do to Shi Chuanting?"

"Not even the secret method I left on Shi Chuanting was triggered! Just what kind of existence could do this silently?"

The Dongyang Grand Elder took a deep breath, closed his eyes, and injected his divine sense into a golden bronze mirror in his hand that was like a blazing sun.

A beam of golden light leaped into the air.

In this evening filled with sunset glow, it instantly enveloped an area eight thousand li in radius.

A figure unhurriedly descended the clean white stone steps of the mountain. He looked up and saw the scene of sunset glow filling the sky and golden light reflecting off the ground.

He couldn't help but softly praise: "Not a weak divine soul cultivation!"

But it was only a single praise. He let the golden light fall and left Dongyang Sect on his own.

A moment later, the golden light gradually faded, and the sunset glow sank into the night sky.

...

Under the night sky.

The salty sea breeze slapped against his face, making his mind especially clear.

Luo Chen sat outside the flying shuttle, quietly contemplating his next moves.

His plan was very simple from the start, so he would take things step by step according to the specific situation. Now that he had relatively specific information, he naturally had to make new arrangements.

The more complex a plan, the easier it was to fail, because there were too many variables.

A simple plan, for Luo Chen who now possessed strength nearly unmatched in the world, actually had more feasibility.

"I can't go to the Eastern Origin Continent. Not only is the Dao Sect's only great power stationed there, but also because the four Great Houses of Lian, Ke, Shi, and Li exist. The competition there is too fierce. If my clone stands out too much, the targeting it would attract would be unimaginable."

"The Libei Continent has three Great Houses. The situation isn't as harsh, but Libei also has the largest number of entrenched aristocratic clans. If I want to obtain sufficient resources, it would be the same."

Luo Chen didn't plan to personally go to retrieve his life soul.

No matter how well he concealed it, since it involved his life soul, he would inevitably have to deal with the Lanke Chessboard. And such a treasure, once the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's number one spirit treasure, would inevitably receive the attention of Ancestor Liancheng. Luo Chen didn't have enough confidence to fool an Ascendant great power.

Therefore, sending a clean, blank-slate clone to infiltrate was the best method.

As for how to use a clone to fool the spirit treasure's master and the Ascendant great power, for Luo Chen, whose divine soul had now greatly matured, especially after experiencing the complete Nine Tribulations Three Nirvanas, he had a certain degree of confidence.

And to have the clone reach the realm where the Dao Sect would collect its life soul in a short time, without too many accidents occurring in this process, was the importance of Luo Chen's current choice of direction.

"Only the Heavenly South Continent!"

"Although there are also two Great Houses there, they are the weakest Zhan and Mo families. Although there are also many competitors there, their origins are complex, mostly former Star Gate guest elders, that is, cultivators from outside continents."

"Moreover, from Shi Chuanting's memories, I saw an old friend is on the Heavenly South Continent!"

Muttering softly, Luo Chen made his decision.

Immediately, with a thought, he gave an order to Bai Meiling, who was piloting the Frost Eagle Flying Shuttle.

"Xiao Bai, turn southeast!"
