# Chapter 901: Returning to the Old Face, Returning to Dust

The opening of the Dan Saint Hall trial seemed to have stirred no commotion within the Nether Origin Sect.

After all, hardly anyone had even come to observe on that Dan Saint Mountain.

But this was merely the surface appearance.

Because Chu Yan was a figure not suitable for open discussion within the Nether Origin Sect. How could the crowd openly display eager interest in the legacy she had left behind?

Yet in truth, countless eyes were secretly watching this place.

Whoever obtained the Dan Saint's legacy would inevitably inherit the position of Dan Saint Hall Master in the future, commanding all alchemy affairs of the Nether Origin Sect.

And even centuries later, they might become a transcendent alchemy grandmaster like Chu Yan herself!

Who would dare to truly ignore such a potential great figure?

Lu Chun's elimination had been somewhat swift, but the overall result had not surprised anyone.

He was, after all, not a direct disciple of the Dan Saint. The alchemy scriptures he had obtained were merely twenty or thirty percent of what the Chu clan had passed down. Coupled with his restless temperament, the so-called "Little Grandmaster" title was more a flattery forced upon him by others.

What kind of grandmaster only knew how to refine two or three types of fourth-grade pills!

As for Xin Yisheng, his shallow foundation was common knowledge. That he had lasted longer than Lu Chun was already an outstanding display of alchemy talent.

The remaining three were the ones upon whom the greatest hopes rested.

However, everything changed when Master Xuanyan appeared and barged into the Dan Saint Hall without any obstruction.

"Didn't he say he would no longer seek the Dan Saint's legacy?"

"At his advanced age, he could ascend on a crane at any moment. If he doesn't obtain the legacy, fine. But if he does, it would only harm our Nether Origin Sect."

"Surely they won't let him carry Chu Yan's legacy into the Yellow Springs!"

"The ancestor is muddle-headed!"

"It's hard to say. Doesn't Master Xuanyan still have a direct disciple who went to gather medicine in the Million Great Mountains? I've heard that although that one keeps a low profile, he has indeed already reached the Little Grandmaster realm. If Master Xuanyan passes the legacy on to his disciple, we won't have drawn water with a bamboo basket for nothing."

"Er... then what's your relationship with the cultivators on Xuanyan Peak like?"

"Relationships... can be mended afterwards!"

The discussions of these hidden observers, to some extent, also represented the shared thoughts of the Nether Origin Sect's three ancestors.

The Dan Saint's legacy was right there. Whoever took it, took it. As long as the meat stayed in the pot.

The prerequisite was that it had to be brought out!

If a promising junior obtained it, that would naturally be good.

Even if Master Xuanyan, whose lifespan was nearly exhausted, obtained it, as long as it could be passed down, it would still be harmless.

...

The four people inside the Dan Saint Hall were completely unaware of the outside discussions.

They were all immersed in the arduous trial of the second level.

"Thump!"

A dull sound suddenly exploded in the empty room.

Chu Yu opened the furnace lid with a heavy heart.

As expected, inside was a mass of filthy black, with not a trace of clarity to be seen.

She raised her hand, staring blankly at her own jade-white fingers.

"Still not working?"

The ancestor had left many things for the Chu clan: alchemy scriptures, pill formulas, alchemy tools, and even three flames of source-less fire, the most needed by alchemists!

Most of those things had been taken away by the sect, leaving only some alchemy insights that the Chu clan could copy.

Chu Yu had relied on these alchemy insights to grope her way forward, step by step, achieving the Grandmaster realm at less than four hundred years of age.

But the greatest gain was not these!

After failing the Dan Saint Hall trial last time, she had returned and gone over the ancestor's alchemy insights again and again, vaguely discovering a hidden alchemy technique within.

It appeared ordinary, but was actually a composite technique formed from dozens of basic alchemy techniques!

After first obtaining it, Chu Yu had spent some effort cultivating it. Upon entering the gate, she was shocked to discover that this alchemy technique was not used for refining pills at all, but for decomposing materials.

At the time, she had not understood what significance such a technique held.

But after passing the first level this time, she finally understood its meaning.

The world was filled with countless heavenly treasures and earthly rarities. For an alchemist, all could be used as medicine.

The saying "refine heaven, refine earth, and refine man" was no empty boast!

Yet there was only one heaven, one earth, and one man.

Many of those heavenly treasures and earthly rarities that contained majestic and wondrous power were unique existences in the world.

Once they failed in alchemy, alchemists often had to wait hundreds or thousands of years, or even their entire lives, for a second qualified heavenly treasure to reappear.

They could only be like a clever woman without rice, sighing in vain, missing the only opportunity.

In such circumstances, the alchemy technique left by Ancestor Chu Yan became extremely important.

From the failed waste pill, the main material could be decomposed through a reverse-refining technique, thus granting a second, or even a third, chance.

Not only that, for some lost pill formulas, one could also reverse-refine existing pills to decompose the raw materials, determine their original material composition, and thus deduce the original formula.

This was the meaning of the technique called "Returning to the Old Face."

Unfortunately, only now did Chu Yu fully understand its importance.

"If I had mastered this technique before participating in the trial, this second level would have been as easy as walking on flat ground for me!"

"I only hate that the trial was opened early because of Luo Chen's arrival..."

This resentment vanished in an instant.

Because Chu Yu could not deceive herself. She had not been very interested in that technique before.

Her alchemy realm was still in a stage of climbing upward. How could she be passionate about a technique that reversed and returned to the essence?

Moreover, even if she had devoted her energy to learning that technique, she would probably have had difficulty passing the first level.

"I wonder how Luo Chen is doing?"

"The Dan Saint Hall was opened early for you. If you stop at the second level, that would be quite amusing."

Though she did not hate him, there was always resentment.

Chu Yu shook her head and waved away the pile of medicinal dregs in the furnace.

She had failed and could no longer move forward.

She could only return the way she came.

In the hall, a gentle surge of mana came, wrapping around her and pulling her outward.

She did not resist, so there was no possibility of injury.

But while crossing the first level's trial, she was surprised to find a large amount of dense white light flying in the same direction.

The two passed each other, and a ruddy-faced old man nodded kindly at her.

It was Master Xuanyan!

Chu Yu was astonished, but instinctively offered a blessing: "May your alchemy path flourish, Master!"

"Haha, the future is yours. May your alchemy path flourish!"

Master Xuanyan smiled freely and stepped into the second level.

At this moment, only a few days had passed since he entered the hall.

...

"Being one step ahead is only temporary. Reaching the finish line first is what makes a hero."

"Luo Chen, after all, your foundation is too shallow!"

In a hall filled with scorching heat, Chen Mozi's weary expression was full of joy.

He had reached the third level!

The first level had taken him a year.

The second level had trapped him for a full two years!

But for him, this time was already extraordinarily fast.

If not for the failure of the previous trial, which had allowed him to prepare in advance, he might never have crossed the second level by the time the trial ended.

His gaze swept across the hall, and Chen Mozi was not surprised at all.

Because the layout of this hall was a very common scene for alchemists.

Earthfire vents, bellows, humanoid puppets...

This was the standard single-person alchemy room for high-grade alchemists of the Nether Origin Sect.

Only the space was larger, enough to accommodate multiple alchemists refining pills together.

"What is the test of the third level?"

Chen Mozi was extremely curious, because he had never been to this third level before.

In the entire Nether Origin Sect, only Master Xuanyan had ever been here.

Just as he was wondering, the earthfire in the hall surged, forming characters in the void that only he could see.

"Refining a new pill?"

"Fourth-grade?"

"A bit troublesome, but still manageable. Not difficult for me."

Chen Mozi seemed relieved, as if he could already see himself passing this level.

But just as he was about to begin, he suddenly froze.

"The conditions are this harsh?"

...

On the second level, in an empty room.

A white-robed Daoist sat cross-legged, surrounded by scattered medicinal dregs.

The hair bun, which had been neatly arranged with Huiniang's help earlier, was now loose and disheveled.

A pair of golden pupils shone brightly, but the whites of his eyes were covered in bloodshot veins.

Those veins were not from activating source power, but from exhaustion and excessive mental strain.

At this moment, the white-robed Daoist stared blankly at a pile of dregs before him, muttering to himself.

"Almost, almost, still almost."

"What exactly is missing?"

Suddenly!

His nose twitched slightly, sniffing the air.

Amidst a jumble of mixed odors, he caught a familiar scent.

Luo Chen froze, then waved his hand.

Instantly, a clump of dregs from a failed reverse-refinement flew into his hand.

Looking down, Luo Chen immediately understood.

This was a third-grade pill called White Angelica Paste, specifically used to dispel cold. It had an excellent healing effect on those suffering from extreme cold qi invasion.

The main ingredients of this White Angelica Paste should be stream white angelica, qi lotus, drifting duckweed, and other special water-attribute herbs.

He remembered that his reverse-refinement had failed.

But why could he still smell a trace of qi lotus fragrance?

"Qi lotus itself has the property of emerging unstained from mud. But since it already failed, why is there still a qi lotus fragrance?"

Luo Chen puzzledly poked at this pile of dregs, using his unformed alchemy technique to separate the other impurities, finally obtaining a clump of charred black liquid.

Staring at this liquid, Luo Chen fell into deep thought.

After a long while, as if struck by sudden enlightenment, Luo Chen snapped awake.

A look of wild joy appeared on his face.

"That's it!"

"That's it!"

"Before, I only valued the form, not the meaning. I was too deliberately pursuing the original appearance of the herbs."

"But in truth, when we alchemists refine pills, we often do so for a single special substance within certain herbs. That is the essence of selecting herbs. No matter what form it takes, it still exists."

"Just like our cultivation. We inhale the spiritual qi of heaven and earth to achieve majestic mana. But once we fall, the Sage scatters, and the vast mana still returns to its essence, becoming free spiritual qi dispersed between heaven and earth."

"When dust returns to dust, and earth returns to earth, in the end, all the splendor is but a handful of fine sand."

"What I need is not the pearls that make up the crown, but only those purest grains of sand!"

"So, this technique should not be like this. It should be..."

In his ecstasy, Luo Chen's hand seals suddenly changed. He gently wiped the charred black liquid.

This wipe was like a groom lifting the veil to see his bride, like the wind blowing away dark clouds to reveal the bright moon. Luo Chen finally saw the essence of that scent.

A hint of pure white, faintly visible.

The fragrance of qi lotus drifted faintly!

As mana was infused into it, even a lotus flower slowly bloomed.

At this moment, on the attribute panel that had been still for a long time, a line of text slowly appeared.

[Unknown Alchemy Technique: Beginner 1/100]

Luo Chen collapsed to the ground as if all his strength had left him, staring at the white lotus, grinning widely.

"Let it be called the Dust-Returning Technique!"

Dust returns to dust, earth returns to earth. By performing this technique, one could see the essence buried beneath the splendor.

From now on, most pills in the world would no longer be mysterious to him, even those he had never seen before!

Then...

Luo Chen's gaze swept over the remaining twenty-some pills, his eyes full of anticipation.

These pills were at least third-grade, and most were even fourth-grade.

If he remembered correctly, these pills had been refined using many herbs, enough to cover the ones he had failed on earlier.

This Dan Saint Hall trial could still continue!

...

Half a year later, in the brightly lit hall, a cloud of smoke suddenly drifted in.

Chen Mozi, sitting on the ground with a worried expression, looked up and saw the white-robed man appear.

A head of ink-black hair was casually draped behind his back. Though his face showed fatigue, his eyes were extremely bright.

At this moment, the other party was curiously examining this hall.

In his faintly golden eyes, it seemed as if no secret in the world could be hidden.

It was Luo Chen!

Chen Mozi opened his mouth, full of bitterness.

He had not expected the other party to have passed the second level!

He had previously underestimated Luo Chen, thinking that for someone of his foundation, passing the first level was already a genius-level performance.

But now it seemed that being able to calmly pass two levels on his first encounter with the Dan Saint Hall trial was far more than just genius!

"As expected of the Alchemy Sect's reputation!" he said in a low voice.

Luo Chen heard the voice and turned his gaze, nodding slightly.

"You flatter me, Fellow Daoist. You are ahead of me."

Chen Mozi shook his head, unwilling to explain how much preparation he had made for the second level.

Luo Chen asked curiously, "What is the test of the third level?"

This time, Chen Mozi did not hide anything and casually replied, "Refining a pill!"

Luo Chen was stunned, then smiled.

Refining a pill!

For him, that was the simplest thing.

As long as there were materials...

Fire qi rose, forming characters.

Luo Chen looked up, raising an eyebrow. But the more he read, the more solemn his expression became.

When the fire curtain dissipated, Luo Chen looked at Chen Mozi again.

No wonder the other party had arrived at this alchemy hall early but had hesitated to act, not daring to make a move.

He had not expected the third level trial to be so harsh!

"Use the raw materials reverse-refined earlier to refine a fourth-grade pill."

"The specific pill does not matter, but there is only one chance!"

Everyone knew that the success rate was hard to control.

Being able to produce a finished pill counted as being passable.

Controlling the success rate to around twenty or thirty percent was worthy of being called a master alchemist.

For grandmasters like Chen Mozi and Chu Yu, they could at most stabilize the success rate at around fifty percent, and that was for relatively low-grade pills they were skilled at.

How difficult was it to succeed in one try?

Even Luo Chen!

Known for refining a hundred pills with mastery over all, he never dared to claim he could succeed in one try with a fourth-grade pill.

When refining pills for others, he always required a sufficient amount of raw materials.

And this time, he even had to construct the pill formula himself!

"With such a trial, even if I forcibly master it using the attribute panel, there is still a ninety percent failure rate!"

Luo Chen was in a difficult position.

Constructing the pill formula was one problem. Having to succeed in one try was another. These two challenges—who in the world could solve them?

In his entire life, the number of times he had succeeded on the first try with an unfamiliar pill formula could be counted on one hand.

This third level trial seemed like an open-book exam, simple and familiar to every alchemy grandmaster, yet no one could guarantee success.

Just as the two were bitterly pondering, an old, hearty laugh came from outside.

"Two Fellow Daoists, why hesitate and lose confidence in yourselves?"

(End of chapter)
