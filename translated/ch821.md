# Chapter 821: The Demoness’s Gaze, Sage Fourth Layer

*Boom!*

A deafening explosion resounded through the sky.

A massive demon beast plummeted from the heavens, its speed so great that fierce flames had already ignited from the friction around it.

From afar, it looked like a meteorite falling from beyond the sky.

The already shattered Blade Domain was once again thrown into upheaval by the beast's descent.

Countless spirit veins on the verge of collapse—whether high-grade or low-grade—shattered in that instant, unleashing unimaginable spiritual energy that swept in all directions.

Luo Chen and the others did not hesitate. They mounted their escape light and shot into the sky.

At that very moment.

*Whoosh! Whoosh! Whoosh!*

Twelve blood-red great banners flew as streaks of light toward the heavens.

A figure, holding a jade-white broken horn, drifted down from the nine heavens.

It was none other than the Heavenly Abyss City Lord!

After he appeared, he said nothing. Instead, with an extremely grave expression, he stared at a certain patch of void.

There, the light twisted, and the void rippled.

Vaguely, a figure like a solitary, cold moon slowly emerged.

"Demon Emperor Azure Frost!"

The four words were uttered coldly from the Heavenly Abyss City Lord's mouth.

They fell upon everyone present like hoary frost, instantly chilling the hot blood that had been boiling from the great battle.

The legends surrounding this demon were the most formidable of the past hundred years!

Some might have been indignant, but after personally facing Demon Emperor Azure Frost just now, even someone as strong as Nine Spirits Primordial Lord could only remain silent.

The woman stood there, utterly alone.

Yet, facing seven Sage True Persons, her expression remained indifferent, showing not a trace of fear.

She did not look down at the Seven Rhinoceros Commander, who was struggling to crawl out of the depths of the earth. Nor did she glance at the Thunder Prison Divine Roc's Nascent Soul clutched in her hand. Her cold, moon-like eyes, like hooks, fixed directly on the white-robed man among the crowd.

The others immediately sensed something amiss. Their gazes snapped in unison toward the white-robed man standing beside Nine Spirits Primordial Lord.

Luo Chen's sword-like brows furrowed. He took a deep breath and met her gaze.

He did not flinch or avoid it!

Their eyes locked. Azure Frost imperceptibly pursed her lips, then reached out and pushed to the side.

As if opening a door, she stepped into it with eerie grace and vanished completely.

From beginning to end, she did not speak a single word.

But with her departure, it was as if a great stone had been lifted from their hearts. Everyone breathed a sigh of relief.

Golden Feather seemed to dislike that feeling intensely. "That demoness is incredibly arrogant! She treats us as if we were nothing!"

Elder Fu sighed. "Legend has it that this demon is a peerless genius that the Eastern Wilderness demon race—or even the entire Mountains and Seas Realm—might not see once in ten million years. She reached the late Sage stage at the age of two hundred and possesses unmatched combat power. With such stunning brilliance, a bit of arrogance is only natural."

Pavilion Master Li's sharp eyes were now somewhat dazed. "On her, I could find no flaw at all. No opening to exploit, no sword to strike."

Nine Spirits Primordial Lord cradled his left arm, his lips pressed tightly together, not saying a word.

But in his pupils, there was also a hint of fear.

Ever since he had perfected his *Great Nine Heavens Xiao River* technique, he had considered himself no weaker than any grand cultivator!

His battle with the Thunder Prison Divine Roc had only solidified that confidence.

But after that brief contact with Demon Emperor Azure Frost, that confidence had been shattered. It was like... an ant trying to shake a tree?

Where had he fallen short?

Was it a difference in realm?

If he truly advanced to the seventh layer of the Sage stage and entered the late stage, could he then fight her?

The thought of the late Sage stage made Nine Spirits Primordial Lord turn his gaze to the Heavenly Abyss City Lord.

The other party was a late-stage Sage grand cultivator, and he came from a Divine Transformation sacred ground. From the fact that he dared to face Demon Emperor Azure Frost directly, he must have had some confidence.

He wondered what evaluation the other party would give.

But at that moment, the Heavenly Abyss City Lord said nothing. Instead, he looked at Luo Chen with a somewhat puzzled expression.

"Alchemy Sect..."

Just as he began to speak, a figure slammed heavily into the ground.

Before the dust had even settled, a mocking laugh came from within.

"Seven Rhinoceros, how did you end up so miserable?"

Accompanying this voice, streaks of flying light flew in from all directions.

Upon entering the Blade Domain, they split into two distinct groups. One flew toward the Seven Rhinoceros Commander's side, the other toward the Heavenly Abyss City Lord's side.

With no one to stop them, they moved with incredible speed.

In the blink of an eye, both sides had assembled.

One side had only nine people, while the other had as many as fifteen.

The dust on the ground suddenly dispersed. A man in a yellow robe was now looking mockingly at the middle-aged man half-kneeling on the ground.

That middle-aged man was the Seven Rhinoceros Commander. But now, he was in a terrible state. His body was pitted and scarred, as if he had suffered extremely severe injuries.

Most shocking of all, the horn that had once protruded from his head had been forcibly torn off.

A fist-sized hole on his skull was a shocking sight.

Blood gushed out like a fountain.

He slowly stood up, his eyes blazing with fury as he swept his gaze across this battlefield that looked like a hellscape.

"Yellow Barbarian, you stood by and did nothing!"

The yellow-robed man had a sallow complexion and looked sickly. But at his companion's accusation, his face darkened.

As his face darkened, an innate majesty radiated from him.

"Don't throw mud at me. It's not that I sat on the sidelines and watched. The truth is, there was a great formation separating us on the inside, and powerful enemies blocking us on the outside. You were the ones who weren't skilled enough to hold out."

The Seven Rhinoceros Commander clenched his fists tightly, his gaze venomous as he looked at the sky.

Fifteen figures stood there, staring coldly at them.

Divine Origin True Person stepped forward, his sinister voice ringing out.

"Now is the perfect opportunity. Why not wipe them all out in one fell swoop!"

Fifteen against nine, the advantage was theirs!

And at this level of battle, low-rank demon beasts could not easily make up the difference with mere numbers.

However, the Heavenly Abyss City Lord's lips moved behind him, and after a brief telepathic message, Divine Origin True Person's body stiffened.

"She's nearby?"

"Not sure. Perhaps she hasn't gone far."

Divine Origin True Person fell silent.

The Heavenly Abyss City Lord shook his head. His gaze swept over the seven terrified Demon Emperors, then fell on the two commanders on the ground.

"The objective of this battle has been achieved. Let's stop here!"

After saying this to everyone, the Heavenly Abyss City Lord was the first to leave.

The dozen or so human Sages did not linger either. They quickly withdrew.

In an instant, the previously lively battlefield fell silent.

Only the mournful bellow of an old ox echoed through the sky, shrill, agonizing, and full of pain.

...

Fifteen Sage True Persons returned triumphantly to Heavenly Abyss Pass.

The defending True Person, who had already received orders, welcomed them inside.

Only then did the cultivators within the city learn what had caused the earth-shattering commotion outside.

First, the Seven Rhinoceros Commander had failed in his strong assault on Heavenly Abyss Pass and retreated without success.

Then, the Heavenly Abyss City Lord had led a group of True Persons in a surprise attack on the Blade Domain, wiping out an entire tribe in a single battle.

This achievement quickly spread throughout Heavenly Abyss Pass and even began to spread to the rear areas.

It was true that the demon beast forces were overwhelming. But the human race had stood on this land for thousands of years, carving out such a large territory from the mouths of demon beasts. They were no pushovers either.

Because of this battle, the confidence of the human cultivators who had been fleeing for a century was greatly boosted.

Certain major powers that had been sitting on the sidelines in the rear also began to send their experts to Heavenly Abyss Pass.

The world bustles, all for profit; the world hustles, all for gain.

When the demon beast army's advance was unstoppable, everyone thought only of how to protect themselves.

But if someone could break that momentum, then these countless demon beasts would no longer be a danger, but an opportunity, a resource, the very sustenance for cultivators pursuing immortality!

The Heavenly Abyss City Lord had gathered a group of experts and forcibly won this battle. Its impact on the course of the human-demon war was absolutely critical!

But let's set aside the subsequent developments for now.

That day, within Heavenly Abyss Pass.

At the victory banquet, Luo Chen received a private summons from the Heavenly Abyss City Lord.

The Heavenly Abyss City Lord first praised Luo Chen for killing countless demons, then complimented his formidable strength, saying that few of his peers could match him.

Luo Chen was no stranger to such flattery. He was a man of the world himself. He immediately praised the City Lord's extraordinary methods, saying that even the famous Seven Rhinoceros Commander was no match for him.

"He is a water-attributed great demon, while I primarily cultivate the earth element. Coupled with my sect's divine techniques and wondrous methods, defeating him was never a difficult task."

"If I hadn't needed to focus on controlling the *Twelve Capitals Divine Fiend Grand Formation*, taking his ox head would not have been an empty boast."

The Heavenly Abyss City Lord said this with a smile, his words brimming with confidence.

And he did have the right to be so confident!

When the battle had ended, the rhinoceros horn he held in his hand was the best proof.

As they chatted and laughed, the Heavenly Abyss City Lord's tone suddenly shifted.

"Daoist friend, it seems you know that Demon Emperor Azure Frost?"

Luo Chen's expression remained calm. "Who among the prisoners of Green Parasol Mountain doesn't know that woman?"

The Heavenly Abyss City Lord shook his head. "I'm afraid it's not that simple. Yesterday, in front of everyone, her eyes were only on you."

"Being targeted by such a powerful figure, City Lord, you don't think that's a good thing, do you?" Luo Chen countered.

The Heavenly Abyss City Lord frowned.

He also couldn't figure out why Demon Emperor Azure Frost had singled out Luo Chen. Logically, when Luo Chen was trapped in Green Parasol Mountain, he was only a mere early-stage Adept. What could he have done to catch the eye of a great Demon Emperor?

Luo Chen took the opportunity to speculate. "Perhaps she was curious about how I managed to survive the great battle at Green Parasol Mountain? Otherwise, I can't think of any other reason why she would be so interested in me, out of all the fellow Daoists."

Curious?

The Heavenly Abyss City Lord seemed lost in thought.

"Apologies, but I overexerted my mana in yesterday's battle. I would like to return and recuperate properly."

"Oh, go ahead!"

The Heavenly Abyss City Lord said this, but just as Luo Chen was about to leave, he suddenly called out to him.

"Don't worry about your cave dwelling. As long as you are in Heavenly Abyss Pass, that fourth-grade, mid-rank cave dwelling is yours alone to use."

Luo Chen had always remembered this point and had been somewhat worried about it.

Now, having received the assurance, he couldn't help but smile.

"Thank you, City Lord!"

"Don't thank me. Thank the Nether Origin Sect instead!"

A flicker of surprise crossed Luo Chen's face, but he quickly understood the other party's meaning.

The Nether Origin Sect's desire to recruit him had never ceased.

Even though he already held the status of a guest elder of the Star Gate of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!

...

Back in his cave dwelling, Luo Chen did not immediately sit down to meditate and regulate his breathing.

Overexerting his mana?

That was merely an excuse!

The Azure Yang Great Handprint appeared to be immensely powerful and consumed a vast amount of spiritual energy, but in reality, most of it was formed by absorbing the ambient free-floating spiritual energy.

And in his battle with the Lightless Beast, he had not used any techniques that required a great expenditure of mana from beginning to end.

The real reason he was so eager to return was only one thing.

At the tip of the Yuantu sword, a single drop of crimson.

Like a mosquito's blood, or perhaps a cinnabar mole.

"So, she is not invincible after all!"

Luo Chen murmured, his expression dazed.

In the final battle, even with everyone joining forces, they had failed to stop Azure Frost from rescuing the Thunder Prison Divine Roc's Nascent Soul.

Even Nine Spirits Primordial Lord's furious strike had been knocked to the ground.

But that battle had not been without gain.

Relying on the sharpness of the Yuantu, Luo Chen had managed to wound her.

Even if it was a wound so insignificant it was like a mosquito bite to a mortal, for Luo Chen, its significance was entirely different.

In this era where Divine Transformation experts could not easily make a move, late-stage Sage grand cultivators were the pinnacle of combat power in the Mountains and Seas Realm.

And this woman, Azure Frost, ignored the threats of her peers, roamed freely, and was unstoppable in the world.

In the hundred years since, even though they had never met, the image of her being "invincible and undefeated" had become increasingly clear in Luo Chen's heart.

After forming his Nascent Soul, his own style of action had become more direct and decisive. Wasn't that, in part, an unconscious imitation of her?

But the more he imitated, the greater the shadow that loomed over his heart.

She was invincible!

Even wounding her seemed impossible.

Until this single, insignificant drop of blood appeared!

"I am growing, and at a rapid pace. The gap between us is constantly shrinking."

"Now, with the help of others, I can wound her. When my own Dao is fully accomplished, I may yet be able to fight her, and even defeat her!"

Luo Chen's eyes grew brighter and brighter. A thread of confidence had finally and completely dispelled the shadow over his heart.

...

The impact of the Blade Domain battle continued to spread.

On the human side, major powers from the sixteen rear domains continuously sent people to join Heavenly Abyss Pass.

On the demon beast side, the impact was even more profound.

A large number of demon beasts began to retreat.

Due to the Sage-level battle, the Blade Domain had been completely destroyed, and it would be unsuitable for living beings to cultivate there for at least several hundred years.

Yellow Barbarian led his forces in retreat, even abandoning their garrison at the Fire Ridge.

All their troops were consolidated within the single domain of Azure Sky.

They no longer sent large numbers of demon beasts to assault Heavenly Abyss Pass as before. Instead, they quietly waited for the arrival of other demon beast armies.

According to intelligence, the Hundred-Foot Commander would arrive with his army in half a year.

Then, two years later, the great Demon Emperor Ox Ghost would lead a million-strong army and station themselves in the Taihu Domain, where the former Taihu Palace had been located.

Thus, with the three commanders all present, commanding tens of millions of demon beasts, they faced off against Heavenly Abyss Pass from tens of thousands of li away.

It was only then that the demon side began sending small units to harass Heavenly Abyss Pass again.

The flames of war seemed ready to reignite.

Luo Chen paid no attention to any of this.

Ever since he had received the Heavenly Abyss City Lord's promise—that the cave dwelling was his to use permanently—he had begun to go into seclusion to cultivate.

Apart from first negotiating the conditions for refining the *Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid* with the Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion's Pavilion Master Li, he declined all visits from the many powerful experts who came to pay their respects.

The mundane affairs of the Luo Tian Sect were handled internally by Li Yingzhang.

Externally, they were handled by Wang Yuan.

The liaison with the Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance was managed by his two capable wives, Sima Huiniang and Gu Caiyi.

Only then could Luo Chen devote himself wholeheartedly to cultivation, without any distractions.

Time passed, bit by bit.

One year, two years, three years...

During this process, Heavenly Abyss Pass occasionally issued Sage-level missions, but the Heavenly Abyss City Lord never called on Luo Chen.

This gave Luo Chen a peaceful environment for cultivation.

One day, five years later.

Inside the cave dwelling, a white-robed Daoist with disheveled hair sat cross-legged on a lotus platform.

A vast amount of fire-attributed spiritual energy gathered, making the cultivation chamber look like a volcanic cavern.

Streams of spiritual energy, like rosy clouds, continuously surged into the Daoist's body with each rhythmic breath he took.

Suddenly!

It was as if some restriction had been broken. The Daoist's aura began to climb uncontrollably.

*Boom!*

Spiritual energy surged, mana raged.

His black hair fluttered without wind, his robes rustled, and Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes.

He exhaled a long breath of turbid qi, then, with a thought,

The surging mana fluctuations were instantly withdrawn into his body, revealing not a trace of strangeness.

But as he looked inward, his Nascent Soul was full, his divine soul lively. His realm was solidly at the fourth layer of the Sage stage!

(The chapter ends)
