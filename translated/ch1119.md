# Chapter 1119: Foundation for Ten Thousand Ages, Before Ascension

Within the Heavenly Nether Palace.

At the center of the great hall, under the activation of the formation, that pool of black water once again emerged.

Luo Chen, clad in black armor, stood with extreme solemnity beside the deep pool, striking one hand seal after another toward the black water pool.

Gradually, the black water pool began to boil.

Gurgle! Gurgle!

As waves churned, vast amounts of Netherwater flowed forth in bubbling streams.

Seeing this, Luo Chen's expression relaxed slightly.

"After two hundred years, aided by formation guidance, I have finally condensed a Netherwater Spring Eye once more within this Heavenly Nether Palace."

"This is the root of the Nether Abyss!"

Where in the Nether Abyss was there no Netherwater? Where was there no root?

But the true source of rootless water was none other than the Netherwater Spring Eye.

Back then, the reason the Nether Origin Sect's mountain gate was devoured by the abyss was precisely because Rosycloud, in order to rescue Qinghe Immortal, had shattered the Netherwater Spring Eye, causing the Nether Abyss to spiral out of control. Subsequently, because Ancestor Heavenly Origin had employed a technique of mutual destruction, matters had reached their current state.

Thus, among the countless ghost cultivators of the Nether Abyss, a saying had always circulated—"He who controls the Netherwater Spring Eye can become the master of the Nether Abyss!"

However, in Luo Chen's view, this statement had certain misconceptions.

The master of the Nether Abyss must have grasped the Netherwater Spring Eye, but one who held the spring eye was not necessarily the master of the abyss, because there was another prerequisite: one must be an Ascendant great power, and moreover, an Ascendant great power who had comprehended the Water Origin!

Otherwise, even the most supreme Sage, holding the Netherwater Spring Eye, would be unable to control the entire Nether Abyss.

Take Luo Chen, for example!

He had been strong enough before, once matching Ascendant great powers as a Sage, and he had even comprehended the true intent of the Water Law.

But even when he held the Netherwater Spring Eye back then, he still could not master the Nether Abyss.

Now, however, things were completely different.

Luo Chen had not only comprehended the Water Origin and achieved Ascendant status, but he had also waited for the first Demon-Devil Heart-Linking Fruit to ripen!

Outside, ten years; within the grotto-heaven, a thousand years.

Nourished by the heart of a fifth-rank desolate beast, the first Heart-Linking Fruit had ripened and fallen.

Luo Chen consumed it, and in the ninth year, within the empty Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land, he quietly broke through to the fifth rank of the Archaic stage.

He became the first human in the history of the Mountains and Seas Realm to possess a desolate beast's physique!

"With my current dual fifth-rank in qi refinement and body refinement, my profound and abundant soul foundation, and the formidable strength of having grasped four Law True Intents, I am enough to create a new world with my own strength!"

Murmuring to himself, Luo Chen closed his eyes.

His divine sense, centered on Yama Mountain, continuously spread outward.

A hundred li, a thousand li, ten thousand li, until it reached places beyond reach.

The vast Nether Abyss, with its countless hidden ghost cultivators, was at this moment as if naked, exposed bare and undisguised within Luo Chen's divine sense.

Among them, a certain little ghost sensed something amiss and its expression changed drastically.

"Damn it, a great power's divine sense!"

It did not hesitate to flee toward deeper Nether depths.

Its speed far surpassed that of a fourth-rank Ghost King.

But fast as it was, Luo Chen's divine sense was faster, and the origin qi of heaven and earth he manipulated was faster still!

Based on the four Law True Intents, he leveraged the immeasurable origin qi of heaven and earth by multiples, and with a crash, he enveloped the entire Nether Abyss.

At this moment!

Luo Chen became the sole sovereign of the endless abyss!

By the ears of all living beings, only a low shout was heard.

"Rise!"

The next moment, a terrifying thing happened.

The vast Netherwater spread in all directions, forming waves a thousand zhang high.

And in the central area, a stretch of land, vast and spanning a full hundred thousand li in circumference, gradually revealed its true form.

On this stretch of land, not a blade of grass grew, and there was no trace of verdure.

All that remained were decayed bricks and stones, crumbling buildings, and the devastation left behind after a great battle.

It was precisely the former site of the Nether Origin Sect's mountain gate!

Its area was equivalent to four or five Jade Cauldron Domains, and this was after a portion had been eroded by the Nether Abyss following the great battle.

And this stretch of land was the future mountain gate of the Luo Tian Sect that Luo Chen favored most.

The Nether Origin Sect was no more, but the land had ultimately been preserved, and the spirit veins and spirit apertures it contained far surpassed any location in the Eastern Wilderness.

As the foremost sacred land of the Eastern Wilderness continent, the Nether Origin Sect naturally occupied the most essential location.

In the future, this place would become the foundation for ten thousand ages of the Luo Tian Sect!

After the great waves were pushed aside, the land that had sunk into the abyss began to tremble.

The earth's crust shook, and mountain ranges arched and rose.

The vast land continuously heaved upward.

At such a sight, anyone would understand what Luo Chen was doing.

He wanted to sink the Nether Abyss, he wanted Yama to rise again!

Such a great divine ability, nearly on par with creating heaven and earth, existed only in legends; ordinary people could hardly imagine it.

Even the most top-tier experts could only gaze from afar, never reaching it.

Ascendant great powers indeed possessed the terrifying destructive force to burn the seas and boil the oceans, to shatter mountains and sink continents.

But destruction was easy; creation was difficult.

Had not Ancestor Liancheng back then required the cooperation of the entire Heavenly Origin Dao Sect merely to attempt to reunite the Central Continent?

And now Luo Chen was doing it with the strength of one man!

The difficulty involved was imaginable.

But before the eyes of the eyewitness Deng Taiyue, what he saw was matters progressing step by step in the direction Luo Chen had envisioned.

A sea of origin qi of heaven and earth was manipulated by him, pushing aside the Nether Abyss and lifting the continent.

The formations that Luo Tian Sect disciples had laid down on the continent earlier stabilized this land, preventing it from collapsing during this process.

Deng Taiyue's only concern was this space.

After observing carefully for a long time, although there were ominous clouds gathering and scenes of thunder and lightning like doomsday, he did not see any spatial cracks appearing.

"Was this also within his expectations?"

Over the past few hundred years, the space above the Nether Origin Sect had finally been repaired by the power of the world, becoming increasingly stable.

Thus, even though Luo Chen was performing such heaven-defying deeds, no spatial cracks that could tear everything apart appeared.

"Then next, we only need to guard against the counterattack of the Nether Abyss."

Deng Taiyue turned around and looked outside Yama Mountain.

Those towering waves, piled up like thousand-ren mountains, still hung suspended above the continent.

...

At the same time.

Above Nether City, all the high-ranking cultivators of the Luo Tian Sect flew out together, gazing into the endless Nether Abyss.

Led by Kongchan, the four great spirit lords, coordinating with the Luo Tian Sect's mountain-protecting grand formation, guarded the banks of the Nether Abyss tightly.

But those great waves, like towering city walls, carrying infinite Netherwater, always gave people a heart-pounding feeling.

If these waves were to crash down, how many Earth-Connecting cultivators in the entire Luo Tian Sect would survive?

And what kind of immense pressure would the Supreme Elder Luo Chen, at the very center, have to bear next?

No one knew.

They could only see that after being pushed aside by individual power, the great waves angrily roared and crashed down toward the central area.

The next moment, something unexpected happened.

The previously ferocious great waves, after crashing down, suddenly became docile.

One after another, the waves obediently poured into a massive vortex, without causing any earth-shattering commotion.

This process continued for a long time.

When all the great waves had dissipated, the entire Nether Abyss seemed to have sunk by a hundred zhang.

The immeasurable Netherwater had disappeared to who knows where.

And a majestic continent appeared before everyone's eyes.

Suddenly!

A rumbling sound echoed through the universe.

As everyone's eyes widened, they saw a thousand-zhang giant standing tall between heaven and earth, roaring at the sun and moon.

It had three heads and six arms, each with a different aura.

Mist and smoke filled the air, and black armor covered its body.

The exposed skin was bronze-colored, covered with lines like branches or demonic scales.

Then, the giant embraced the void, lifted, and pulled out a great mountain.

Holding the mountain in its hand, the giant soared into the sky.

The surrounding mist and smoke surged upward, wrapping around the base of the mountain, forming thick clouds.

When it was all over, the high mountain hung suspended among the clouds, and the continent was settled above the Nether Abyss.

This heaven and earth was completely renewed!

Everyone looked at each other, then without hesitation transformed into beams of light, like mayflies, flying toward that immortal mountain among the clouds.

...

On Yama Mountain.

Deng Taiyue looked at Luo Chen, who had drifted down, his expression extremely complicated, ultimately transforming into full-hearted admiration.

He bowed and offered words of congratulations.

"Congratulations, Senior, for creating a new divine wilderness and laying the foundation for ten thousand ages of the Luo Tian Sect!"

Luo Chen smiled freely and walked toward the Heavenly Nether Palace.

"It was only to be expected. As for the so-called foundation for ten thousand ages, it depends on how future generations develop it. There is only so much I can do."

His words were light, but his steps carried an indescribable heaviness.

Deng Taiyue watched Luo Chen's retreating figure. Although the other had just pulled up an immortal mountain, for some reason, Deng Taiyue felt another great mountain weighing down on Luo Chen.

Deng Taiyue fell into thought.

Was this the consequence of Luo Chen having just exerted his full ability, manipulating the endless origin qi of heaven and earth?

That method, nearly matching the power of heaven and earth, had clearly and openly contended with this world's Heavenly Dao for authority.

In his current state, every move Luo Chen made was watched by this heaven and earth, and thick shackles had already fallen upon him.

...

In the main hall of the Heavenly Nether Palace, Luo Chen sat on the upper chair, resting with his eyes closed.

He was sensing.

Sensing everything inside and outside himself.

After a long while, he let out a long sigh.

"So this is what Li Canghai called the shackles of heaven and earth?"

"So heavy!"

"If I continue to act without restraint in the future, I fear it will not just consume my magical power, but my lifespan. Moreover, in daily cultivation, the difficulty of absorbing origin qi of heaven and earth will greatly increase, let alone comprehending the true intent of laws."

"It seems the time has indeed come to leave."

Amidst his sighs, Luo Chen opened his eyes.

At that moment, streaks of flying light shot in.

In but a moment, the great hall was filled with people.

One by one, the Luo Tian Sect's Sages looked at Luo Chen with excited and adoring eyes.

Before they could speak, Luo Chen reached out and pressed down in the void, his gaze falling on the middle-aged man at the head.

"Lingxi!"

Luo Lingxi stepped forward and bowed deeply. "Father, I am here!"

Luo Chen's expression was slightly weary as he said, "Handle a matter for me. Perhaps it will be the last one."

Luo Lingxi was startled, and the others were also stunned.

"In ten years, I will ascend. You are to arrange an ascension ceremony for me, inviting all the top experts of the Eastern Wilderness's various sects to come and observe."

Hearing this, Luo Lingxi was bewildered.

Counting it all up, his father Luo Chen was barely over five hundred years old. Even among Sages, he was considered young, let alone an Ascendant great power with a conventional lifespan of two thousand years.

According to recorded legends, most Ascendant great powers in the Mountains and Seas Realm ascended leisurely only after reaching a thousand years of age.

For someone like the demoness Azure Frost, who ascended at a mere six hundred years of age, was extremely rare, practically an anomaly.

And now, his own father, the Luo Tian Ancestor, was about to set a historical record by ascending at five hundred years old?

He hesitated. "Father, isn't this too hasty?"

Luo Chen rubbed his brow. "Just do as I say. As for this former Nether Abyss site, how to restore its vitality in the future is a matter for the sect's disciples. You handle it as you see fit!"

Having said this, he calmly rose and left the Heavenly Nether Palace.

Inside the hall, the Luo Tian Sect Sages, who had been excited by Luo Chen's earlier feat, could no longer care about their excitement. They were all shocked by the news that Luo Chen was about to ascend.

Only Gu Caiyi knew some of the inside story.

She bit her lip and chased after Luo Chen's departing direction.

But just after taking two steps, she stopped and turned back to look at another woman.

"Canglong, you come too!"

...

Luo Chen!

Ascendant Ancestor, Supreme Elder of Luo Tian, the undisputed number one of the Eastern Wilderness!

About to ascend!

This news spread like wildfire, instantly setting the entire Eastern Wilderness cultivation world ablaze.

Whether ancient sects, established powers, or newly risen strongmen, all were shocked by this news.

How old was that man!

The legendary experiences of Luo Chen did not even need interested people to dig them up; they were already engraved in the hearts of every cultivator in the Eastern Wilderness.

Naturally, his age was also a topic of great interest.

Entered the Dao at sixteen, Initiate at thirty, Adept at a hundred, Sage at two hundred.

In less than five hundred years, he had achieved the honor of Ascendant.

Now, before he had even fully enjoyed the extreme glory of being at the pinnacle of a realm, he was about to ascend in haste.

Although it was creating history, it was still incomprehensible.

After this news spread, countless low-level cultivators in the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world spread the word, all wanting to obtain a pass to go to the Luo Tian Sect to watch Luo Chen's ascension.

That senior, although decisive in killing, had a rather gentle style toward low-level cultivators, always bearing the air of a modest gentleman of high virtue and prestige.

If they could witness the ceremony with their own eyes, it would surely become an experience they could talk about for their entire cultivation career.

But among the high-level cultivator community, the reaction was different.

"Why does Luo Chen insist on me coming to observe before his ascension?"

This question lingered in the hearts of all the top experts of the Eastern Wilderness.

The current Eastern Wilderness had a pattern of one superpower and multiple strong powers.

This level of strength completely suppressed the other four continents of the Mountains and Seas Realm.

Because aside from the superpower Luo Tian Sect, the various other flourishing sects all had many who had comprehended Law True Intents!

The root cause of this situation lay in the batch of Initiation Pills Luo Chen had given away back then.

Although the effect of the Initiation Pills was not as good as the Heavenly Secret Pills refined by the Alchemy Saint, let alone the Law-Derivation Pills, under the vast numbers, they had still allowed a portion of talented and gifted Sages to successfully comprehend their own unique Law True Intents!

After comprehending Law True Intents, they not only possessed combat power far exceeding their peers but also had the qualification to charge into the Ascendant realm in the future!

In other words, this batch of people were all Ascendant seeds!

It could be imagined that once Luo Chen ascended and no one was left to suppress the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world, these people would inevitably engage in a frenzied struggle for fifth-rank spirit veins in order to break through to the Ascendant stage.

If someone truly broke through to that realm, the power structure of the Eastern Wilderness would change again.

And if the Luo Tian Sect did not produce a second Ascendant great power, could it maintain its transcendent status?

With this foreseeable future, it would be fine if Luo Chen did not ascend, but he insisted on leaving this realm.

And before leaving this realm, he specifically invited all the strong to observe the ceremony.

What was his purpose? It inevitably made one ponder.

Was it a warning before departure, a suppression, or a ruthless method to eliminate future troubles forever?

For a time, low-level cultivators of the Eastern Wilderness flocked to the Luo Tian Sect, while high-level cultivators hesitated and wavered, unwilling to advance.
