# Chapter 801: An Ancient Fortress Through the Ages, The Schemes of Nether Origin

"They're here, they're here!"

"The Pill Sect is coming! I saw his carriage!"

"How much further?"

"Only about ten li away, he'll be here in an instant!"

"We need to get closer, try to show our faces before the Pill Sect. If we can get him to recognize us, it'll be easier to ask for pills later."

"Haha, 'get him to recognize us'? Who cares about that sort of thing?"

"Fellow Daoist, you don't quite understand. I spent a lot to learn about the Pill Sect's life experiences. This man has the bearing of a gentleman and is never stingy about helping juniors. There were rumors back then that he coveted a small family's medicinal herb business and swallowed it up. But that's just hearsay. In reality, that family still exists under his care, and even the current Luo Tian Sect's sect master is a cultivator from that family."

"Letting an outsider become sect master?"

"That's not all. I heard that when he formed his core, he left his insights in a poem and recited it freely to all the cultivators in the city. Even now, there are still Adept-level rogue cultivators in the Jade Cauldron Domain who remember the Pill Sect's kindness."

"Core formation insights, given away for free? He's that generous?"

"Yes! That's why in such a short time, he's built countless connections, with many Sage-level experts backing him. This time, the Pill Sect has personally come to Lingtian to carry out the three-year rotation patrol mission. If he's in a good mood and lets a little bit of pill medicine slip through his fingers, we who receive it will benefit endlessly."

"I see! Let's go together, let's go together!"

Outside Lingtian Pass, amidst a flurry of discussions, many cultivators crowded toward the only road leading into the city.

Those with requests surged forward eagerly.

Those who were indifferent still didn't want to fall behind.

And there were also those purely here to watch the excitement, wanting to see what the much-hyped Pill Sect of the past two years actually looked like.

After all, although Luo Chen had made a shocking appearance at the Battle Arena back then, it was too brief. Not everyone knew what he looked like.

In ordinary times, seeing the true face of a Sage was extremely difficult.

But today was different!

As a wartime fortress city, Lingtian Pass had a no-fly formation set up inside, arranged by the various Sage-level forces according to the formations passed down by the Nether Origin Sect.

Anyone wanting to enter the city had to get off their flying boats and walk in.

As long as the Pill Sect, Luo Chen, didn't deliberately hide his face, this was the perfect opportunity to personally behold a Sage's jade-like countenance.

The crowd surged, and two graceful figures were swept along within it.

One had an indifferent expression, the other was gentle and refined.

But if you looked closely, both women's eyes held a hint of unease and anxiety.

"Master, will he see us?"

"He should."

"If Senior Brother Luo is willing to see us, that would be wonderful. With his current strength, he might be able to help us."

"Xiaolan, he's no longer that Initiate-stage cultivator. If we meet again, you should address him as Sage..."

When she said the word "Sage," the older female cultivator's eyes were filled with complex emotions.

The young woman called Xiaolan quickly nodded.

She was just used to the old way of addressing him, but she was no ignorant fledgling who didn't understand propriety. She naturally knew how to properly treat a powerful figure.

Suddenly!

Xiaolan's eyes lit up as she pointed into the distance.

"He's here!"

The older female cultivator took a deep breath, released her mana, and pushed forward through the crowd.

Xiaolan followed closely behind.

In the sky, a cloud carriage flew swiftly. As it approached, its speed dropped sharply, and it began descending toward the ground.

Figures of cultivators flew down from it, lining up on both sides amidst the crowd.

Whoosh!

A burly man, holding a long halberd and with wings on his back, circled once in the sky. His vast blood energy turned into a wave of qi that slowly pushed outward.

"Everyone, our Grand Elder is entering the city. Please make way."

After speaking, he landed like an arrowhead at the front of the procession.

It was Baleful Dragonzi!

With just one glance, people recognized this quite famous rising star.

His strength at the third realm of body refinement was in no way inferior to early-stage Adepts.

But the crowd's focus was clearly not on him at the moment.

Everyone looked toward the cloud carriage.

A figure in a white robe with gold patterns walked out from within.

His face was handsome, his nose bridge high and straight.

A pair of thin lips seemed to smile, yet carried a hint of mockery.

But what was most eye-catching were the eyes beneath his tall crown—like a pool of cold water, an abyss.

When his gaze swept over, few dared to meet his eyes.

Even those who forced themselves to look back, neither humble nor arrogant, found themselves dazed and confused.

Was this the Pill Sect, Luo Chen?

So young, yet with such a unique temperament. Even amidst the vast crowd, he stood out, sharp and brilliant!

Although the crowd had been pushed aside by Baleful Dragonzi, upon seeing Luo Chen's true face, people couldn't help but want to surge forward.

But just as they were about to move, a voice sounded from behind, accompanied by a heaven-like pressure that overwhelmed the entire scene.

"Fellow Daoist Luo, you've finally arrived! I've been waiting for you!"

As the words fell, the figure appeared like a ghost before Luo Chen.

Luo Chen was momentarily startled, then a smile appeared on his face.

"Fellow Daoist Lingfengzi, welcoming me like this is an honor that overwhelms me."

The elegant and handsome middle-aged man laughed heartily and extended his hand forward.

"Please!"

Luo Chen nodded and walked shoulder to shoulder with him into the city.

Behind them, Yang Pingdu put away the Sixfold Imperial Flying Cloud Carriage and, together with Chu Kui, led the Luo Tian Sect cultivators into the city.

As for the onlookers, under the pressure of two Sages, they fell silent, no longer making any noise.

When the two figures had completely entered Lingtian Pass, the commotion outside the city erupted once more.

"So that's Senior Pill Sect? I thought he'd be a white-haired old man. I didn't expect him to be so young!"

"You don't understand. The Pill Sect achieved Sagehood at only a little over two hundred years old, they say. He's truly a top-tier prodigy under heaven."

"Two hundred years? Good heavens, I was still trying to break through to the Adept stage at that age!"

"With such cultivation speed and his peerless alchemy skills, even the disciples of the Holy Land probably can't compare!"

"No wonder the Grand Elder of the Misty Void Sect was so solemn, personally coming out of the city to welcome him. If it were me, I'd also place great importance on Senior Pill Sect."

"First, Nine Spirit Lord stood for a long time at Daqing Mountain, earnestly seeking an audience. Now, Lingfengzi personally comes ten li out of the city to welcome him. In today's Eastern Wilderness, no one has a greater reputation than Senior Pill Sect!"

Among the crowd, the two female cultivators woke as if from a dream.

Under the suppression of Lingfengzi's Sage domain, no one dared to speak, for fear of disturbing the Sage.

But now that the person they wanted to see had entered the city, what were they to do about the matter they sought?

Even a Sage-level expert like Lingfengzi had to personally come to welcome him, seeking the first opportunity.

It was easy to imagine that Luo Chen's cave dwelling in Lingtian Pass would be bustling with visitors. People like them, without any reputation, simply couldn't get a chance to see Luo Chen.

The older female cultivator, who usually had a cold face, now showed a rare look of bewilderment.

It was the young woman who, in surprise and uncertainty, whispered, "Master, I think I saw Sixth Brother."

"Sixth Brother?"

"Mm. He was just now in the Luo Tian Sect's formation. It's hard to see Sage Luo, but if I go find Sixth Brother, maybe I can get in through the back door."

Upon hearing this, the older female cultivator's eyes suddenly lit up.

...

"As the number one fortress city in the Eastern Wilderness today, or even the strongest pass in the Eastern Wilderness's thousand-year history, the construction of Lingtian Pass was a massive undertaking."

"Cutting across the borders of five domains, pooling the financial resources of sixteen domains, and using the vast labor force of refugees from the twelve fallen domains in front, it covers a full eight thousand li in area!"

"The number of cultivators inside has already exceeded ten million, with Initiate-stage cultivators numbering a full hundred thousand!"

"According to statistics from the city lord's mansion, the openly known Adept-stage cultivators number around three thousand. This is an extremely formidable force. Apart from the Holy Lands of the Ascendant stage, there is hardly any force that can simultaneously possess such a large number of Adepts."

Luo Chen listened as Lingfengzi introduced the situation at Lingtian Pass. Those cold, hard numbers gave him a concrete impression of this majestic city.

However, even though he had expected it, he still couldn't help but narrow his eyes when he heard the number three thousand Adepts.

"How can there be so many?"

Lingfengzi replied matter-of-factly, "When a great calamity comes, the ones who survive most easily are those with high cultivation. Low-level cultivators die in wave after wave, but Adept-stage cultivators don't fall so easily."

"Moreover, when many people die, more resources are freed up. Under external pressure, Initiate-stage experts with sufficient resources will naturally fight desperately."

"You've also experienced forming a golden core, Fellow Daoist. You naturally understand that the most important thing in breaking through to the Adept stage is that spirit of unwavering determination, right?"

Luo Chen nodded.

When cultivators refine qi, each realm's breakthrough has different emphases.

Rising from Qi Refinement to Initiate emphasizes pure spiritual power and building the Dao foundation.

Rising from Adept to Sage pursues profound foundations, the unity of the three treasures, and also requires great methods to deal with the thunder tribulation.

The breakthrough from Initiate to Adept is also different. The methods of core formation are incredibly varied, with countless paths leading to this realm.

But one thing is absolutely necessary!

That is the unwavering determination to succeed or die trying!

If you fail, like your old friend Tan Jin who died in seclusion, or Chu Kui who was severely wounded and nearly perished.

If you succeed, you can let out a long roar: "Once a golden core is swallowed into the belly, my fate is mine to command, not heaven's!"

Under extreme external pressure, if resources are sufficient, it will indeed give rise to a large number of Adept-stage cultivators.

The reason Luo Tian Sect, like his own, had produced nine Adepts in a hundred years was also due to this factor.

However!

Although such Adepts had reached the realm, they were often unqualified in many aspects.

For example, their mana wasn't tempered enough, their techniques weren't converted in time, or they had hidden injuries from forced breakthroughs.

Even their natal magic treasures probably didn't have the leeway to be properly chosen.

Three thousand Adepts?

Perhaps less than half were truly elite.

"As for Sage-level cultivators, Lingtian Pass has five permanently stationed, rotating every three years."

"Can they rotate in time?"

Lingfengzi smiled slightly. "Naturally, they can. The five domains of Lingtian currently have twenty Sage-level colleagues. Once the war situation becomes critical, the five Sages can instantly increase to ten, even twenty! Furthermore, the sixteen domains in the rear will also send Sage-level experts to quickly reinforce, ensuring Lingtian Pass is not lost."

So many?

Luo Chen was somewhat surprised.

But upon careful calculation, there was no major discrepancy.

Just at his Sage ascension ceremony two years ago, twelve Sage-level cultivators had come.

If you added those guarding their sects, stationed at Lingtian Pass, and those in secluded cultivation, the number of twenty was only natural.

Such a number made Luo Chen involuntarily compare it to the hegemon-level forces of the Northern Sea period.

The Cang Hai Alliance was probably no more than this!

If you added the three parties of the Moluo Stream, the Myriad Immortals Assembly, and the Penglai Immortal Sect, they would be roughly equivalent to the number of Sage-level experts in the current sixteen domains of the Eastern Wilderness.

The destroyed Primordial Demon Sect and the existing Nether Origin Sect roughly canceled each other out.

Looking at it this way, the Eastern Wilderness and the Northern Sea were actually similar in terms of high-level cultivator numbers.

Oh!

He had missed one point. The Northern Sea's islands were scattered, with many forces like the Ice Pole Sect that hadn't gotten involved in the war between the three hegemons for control of the Northern Sea.

Therefore, the number of Sage-level cultivators in the Northern Sea in the past was actually greater than in the Eastern Wilderness.

This was worthy of the Northern Sea's reputation for abundant resources.

Luo Chen was comparing the two continents like this.

But subconsciously, he didn't bring the Central Continent into the comparison.

Because there was really no comparison.

The Central Continent—one sect's territory!

Without any consumption, even he didn't know how many Sage-level experts the Central Continent had.

A hundred was definitely not the limit!

Two hundred was just the surface number.

Three hundred, or even four hundred, was also possible.

If this tiger of the Central Continent were to break free from its cage, it would probably take the other four continents of the Mountains and Seas Realm to barely match it!

"This is the benefit of dominating a single continent—you can develop to your heart's content."

"If the Nether Origin Sect were to control all thirty-six domains, or even clear out the millions of mountains in the barbaric wilderness, its foundation would probably be able to catch up..."

Thinking of this, Luo Chen's brow couldn't help but twitch.

In the current situation, even Nine Spirit Lord could see that it was as precarious as a pile of eggs, yet the Nether Origin Sect still hadn't sent out large numbers of cultivators.

They only had the Lingtian City Lord alone stationed at the front line.

Couldn't they see that the momentum of the Eastern Wilderness Demon Race was growing stronger and stronger?

With the precedent of the Northern Sea's Primordial Demon Sect's destruction before them, weren't they afraid of repeating the same mistake?

Gathering so many cultivators in one place, forming a so-called "Death-Defying Army"—in the end, who would be the ones to survive after being pushed to the brink? Would it be their various forces, large and small, or the Nether Origin Sect?

"They can't be thinking of letting us and the Demon Race wear each other down, and then they'll step in to clean up the mess, imitating the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect and bringing the vast Eastern Wilderness under one sect, can they?"

Thinking of this possibility, Luo Chen couldn't help but quietly clench his fist.

He subtly glanced at Lingfengzi, who was talking animatedly beside him.

Did they know?

Or rather, had they considered this possibility?

Or, under the pressure of the Holy Land, they had no choice but to do so, trying to find a chance of survival amidst death, fighting for a sliver of hope for their own inheritance?

There was no answer, and Luo Chen didn't dare to ask.

Lingfengzi, after a round of goodwill, finally stopped beating around the bush and stated his request.

He wanted Luo Chen to personally refine a fourth-order pill for him!

This was only to be expected. When Luo Chen was in seclusion refining the Nine Li Pill for Nine Spirit Lord, the other party had sought an audience twice.

Luo Chen naturally knew his intentions.

Especially today, when the other party had taken the initiative to come out of the city to welcome him, imitating Nine Spirit Lord by giving him enough face and helping to boost his reputation.

But if Luo Chen was to refine pills, the price had to be discussed.

As for the specific price, Luo Chen had mentioned it back then. With examples like Elder Fu and Nine Spirit Lord before him, as long as one took the trouble to inquire, there was no need for Luo Chen to say it himself.

However, Lingfengzi showed a look of embarrassment on his face.

Luo Chen instantly understood in his heart—so he was a poor man!

But he didn't show any contempt on his face, still smiling cheerfully.

"No rush. Let me first meet with the fellow Daoists in the city, and then we can discuss the details in private."

Lingfengzi breathed a sigh of relief. The other party's words were very face-giving, indicating that this matter could be accomplished. He couldn't help but feel grateful.

Unknowingly, the two had arrived at the city lord's mansion.

This was Luo Chen's second time here.

But unlike the previous time, this time Luo Chen was much more relaxed and at ease.

The few divine senses emanating from inside were no longer sharp, but friendly.

As soon as he entered the hall, four figures immediately came into view.

The Lingtian City Lord at the head went without saying.

The other three were Xiao Shiniang, the master of Fireworks Ancient Island, whom he had met once before; Qin Baixiang, the sect master of the Myriad Beasts Sect; and Luo Chen's "good friend," Sage Xianhe.

This arrangement was very appropriate. Counting Lingfengzi, among the four Sages carrying out the patrol mission, either they had good relations with him or had requests of him. The other two were casual acquaintances with no ill intentions.

Luo Chen's mind turned. He formed a hand seal with his left hand, held it upright before his chest, and gave a slight bow.

"Fellow Daoists, Luo Chen has arrived late!"

(End of this chapter)
