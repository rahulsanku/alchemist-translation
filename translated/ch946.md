# Chapter 946: Demon-Suppressing Buddha, Crossing Sumeru, Purple Robe Before Him, Overlord Cultivation Unmatched

The "few days" Zheng Guan spoke of were not just a few days.

A full half-month of waiting passed before the time came to enter the Sumeru Realm Mountain.

On that day.

Minglei Cave Master arrived before Luo Chen's cave dwelling and waited quietly.

Before long, the cave dwelling's gates dispersed, and Luo Chen's unusually tall figure emerged.

The moment he saw Luo Chen, Minglei Cave Master couldn't help but speak: "The heavens and earth have indeed changed!"

Luo Chen looked at him and smiled. "You only just noticed now?"

Minglei Cave Master smiled bitterly. "Having grown accustomed to being a wandering cultivator living from hand to mouth, I rarely cultivate when alone outside. Even when I occasionally practice, it's only a shallow taste—I never get that feeling. In the last half-month, I tried deep cultivation, only to find my Nascent Soul restless beyond measure, unconsciously slipping out of my Purple Mansion. If I hadn't prepared in advance, I might have lost myself in this world."

Luo Chen nodded. This experience was exactly the same as his own.

The only difference was probably that his Purple Mansion barrier far surpassed ordinary cultivators, not only defending well against the outside world but also locking in his own Nascent Soul.

This was likely one of the advantages that gradually emerged for five-spirit-root cultivators after reaching higher realms!

Minglei Cave Master sighed. "Before, I complained that the Wind-Thunder Gorge's spirit vein was insufficient to support my cultivation after Nascent Soul. Now it seems that being inferior also has its benefits!"

"Perhaps that's also why Feng Buyan set his sights on your Wind-Thunder Gorge?" Luo Chen speculated.

Minglei Cave Master grunted in acknowledgment.

Wind-Thunder Gorge's environment was harsh, its earth qi extremely chaotic.

Though it had spirit veins, they were broken everywhere. The only fourth-order spirit aperture could only support one Sage's cultivation.

In normal times, it was considered an extremely poor cultivation ground.

But in the current Southern Frontier, where heaven and earth had changed so drastically, it had instead become an excellent spirit vein unaffected by the chaos.

"Let's go!" Luo Chen said lightly, heading toward the City Lord's Mansion.

The entrance to the Sumeru Realm Mountain was hidden within the City Lord's Mansion, where Zheng Guan Daoist presided.

Minglei Cave Master followed beside him, casually mentioning some recent events.

More and more experts were gathering at Nine Haze Immortal City, their numbers exceeding imagination.

From the initial six or seven people, it had gradually grown to over a dozen.

Such a terrifying number made one uneasy.

Luo Chen, however, was unimpressed.

From the Eastern Wilderness to the Northern Sea, to the Central Continent and Southern Frontier, he had seen too many major powers.

A lineup of over a dozen Sages might sound formidable, but in the end, it was nothing but loose sand.

At the Molan Court, when the Moluo Stream and the Cang Hai Alliance held their assembly, the number of Sage-level experts was no less than this. During the most intense battles at Lingtian Pass, the numbers far exceeded this.

Not to mention the several sacred lands.

What was truly frightening wasn't the number of Sages in Nine Haze Immortal City, but the Fierce Mountain Corpse Alliance!

If they could unite these experts, forging them into a single rope, then the Southern Frontier would gain a superpower!

A superpower capable of challenging the Life and Death Gate.

"No, it's still lacking," Luo Chen muttered to himself.

To contend with a sacred land, one needed top-tier combat power equivalent to an Ascendant-level existence.

Therefore, the Fierce Mountain Corpse Alliance's confidence in provoking the Life and Death Gate definitely didn't rely solely on these recruited Sages.

"Then where does their reliance lie?"

With these miscellaneous thoughts, Luo Chen and his companion soon arrived at the City Lord's Mansion.

Zheng Guan Daoist's disciple, Hu Jin, personally came out to welcome them, leading the two through twists and turns within the mansion until they finally appeared before an unremarkable hall.

**[Chengjun Hall]**

Luo Chen gazed at the plaque above the hall, silently reading the name.

Only then did he notice the others waiting outside the hall.

Thirteen people, all Sage-level experts!

Among them was the Luo Sect's Left Envoy, Bei Puti, whom he had met before.

At that moment, Minglei Cave Master beside him bristled like a startled cat, his domain suddenly flaring wide.

Another domain, equally righteous and evil, surged forth to meet it.

The two clashed.

On the flat plaza, the sound of wind and thunder suddenly erupted.

*Boom!*

Minglei Cave Master staggered, glaring viciously at the newcomer.

"It's you!"

A white-robed monk dotted with traces of blood-red plum blossoms stood with one palm pressed together, his eyes filled with murderous intent despite his calm appearance.

"You still dare leave Wind-Thunder Gorge?"

As he spoke, that domain—both righteous and evil—pressed down again.

Minglei Cave Master's expression shifted slightly. Within Wind-Thunder Gorge, he wouldn't fear this man. But having left it, he was merely an ordinary early-stage Sage.

Against this being who was both demon and buddha, he truly was no match.

Just as he gritted his teeth, preparing to resist, a tall figure suddenly appeared before him, blocking his way.

One step!

Luo Chen took only a single step, and he stood in the way.

No matter how fiercely the white-robed monk's domain surged, no matter how vast his momentum, Luo Chen was like an unyielding reef, firmly blocking the torrent.

"Enough!"

The white-robed monk's pupils contracted. "Qi Tian, Minglei and Feng Buyan conspired to harm me. Are you standing up for him now?"

Luo Chen sneered contemptuously. "Ning Yuzhuo, if you wish to die right now, this lord wouldn't mind granting your wish."

His tone was extremely arrogant, as if he could accomplish it simply by willing it.

The watching Sages couldn't help but look at Luo Chen with considerable surprise.

The domain Ning Yuzhuo emitted was extremely powerful, far surpassing an ordinary early-stage Sage.

This was evident from how Minglei Cave Master had been completely outmatched in their domain clash.

Yet unexpectedly, Luo Chen, who revealed nothing, was completely unaffected.

Not only that, his words showed not the slightest regard for his opponent.

So what realm was this black-armored man named "Qi Tian"?

Among the crowd, familiar Sages began murmuring among themselves.

"The name Qi Tian is unfamiliar, yet it sounds somewhat familiar."

"Such overbearing behavior, such a contemptuous gaze—could he be the legendary overlord cultivator Qi Tian?"

"The one who killed one Sage of the Shadow Teaching and forced another to flee?"

"That should be him!"

"Rumor has it that his body-tempering skills are no less than our Sage-level cultivation. Even the Luo Sect's Left Envoy holds him in high regard. It seems he truly has some accomplishment!"

"Body tempering? Is anyone still walking that path now? Not to mention that body-tempering has declined, but after experiencing the calamity of the Devil Heart, cultivators in the Southern Frontier are reluctant to temper their bodies!"

The one who spoke this last sentence was the master of a major sect.

His realm wasn't particularly high, but his knowledge was extensive.

After he finished speaking, he felt a gaze sweep over him. When he followed it, it had vanished without a trace.

Finally, he fixed his eyes on Luo Chen.

"Was he looking at me just now?"

"He's in the middle of a domain confrontation with Ning Yuzhuo, yet he can still spare attention to listen to our discussion?"

Leaving aside these murmurs, under the watchful eyes of the crowd, Ning Yuzhuo seemed somewhat caught between a rock and a hard place.

Across from him, Luo Chen grinned ferociously and was about to step forward.

Just then.

*Creak, creak, creak...*

As if bearing the weight of ten thousand jun, the palace gates slowly opened.

Zheng Guan Daoist's figure appeared before the crowd.

His first glance fell upon the two locked in confrontation, Luo Chen and Ning Yuzhuo.

His horsetail whisk swept once, and a gentle force cut through the stalemate between them.

Ning Yuzhuo, knowing when to stop, withdrew his Sage domain like receding tide.

"Hmph!"

Luo Chen snorted coldly, too lazy to make a move.

Zheng Guan Daoist draped his whisk over his arm and smiled. "Fighting within the city, I might turn a blind eye. But once we enter the Sumeru Realm Mountain, I advise everyone not to make too much trouble."

The words were spoken with a smile, but the warning within was clear to all.

Everyone responded in agreement.

"Thank you for the guidance, Fellow Daoist."

"With Sage Songyang presiding over the Sumeru Realm Mountain, how would we dare act recklessly? You worry too much, Fellow Daoist Zheng."

"Wouldn't dare, wouldn't dare!"

Zheng Guan Daoist smiled and looked at Luo Chen.

This overbearing man clasped his hands behind his back and twitched the corner of his mouth.

"Qi is no brute. If Ning Yuzhuo hadn't offended my friend, I wouldn't have bothered to get rough with him."

"Very well then!"

Zheng Guan Daoist nodded, then raised his whisk.

"Everyone, please!"

One by one, figures filed through the gates of Chengjun Hall.

Once inside.

Everyone revealed expressions of surprise.

Before them, an entrance like a vortex slowly rotated within the hall.

Array flags rustled all around. On the floor and ceiling, array patterns flickered with streaks of light.

Dense spiritual energy was continuously drawn in and constantly crushed.

"A spatial passage!"

With a single glance, Luo Chen recognized the composition of this vortex entrance.

Strictly speaking, it wasn't a spatial passage.

It was a spatial rift!

After being modified by an expert's array, it had been expanded to a size barely passable for people.

This method wasn't particularly profound or exquisite, far inferior to the teleportation array from the Demon-Fall Land to Bright Radiance Heaven, or the super teleportation array at Green Parasol Mountain.

But behind its crudeness lay practicality.

"There are also array experts within the Fierce Mountain Corpse Alliance!"

As Luo Chen muttered inwardly, Zheng Guan Daoist beside him continued with his instructions.

"The Sumeru passage is unstable, so it can only be opened at regular intervals."

"When entering, I will use a special treasure to shield you all. But to avoid any gaps in my protection, everyone should still do their best to defend themselves."

"Once through this Sumeru passage, you will reach the Sumeru Realm Mountain."

As he spoke, he produced a Dao register, channeled a stream of magic power into it, and the characters on the register flickered before revealing over a dozen large "Protect" characters that descended toward the crowd.

After some hesitation, everyone allowed these "Protect" character spiritual lights to cover them.

Except for one person.

"Fellow Daoist Qi?" Zheng Guan Daoist was puzzled.

Luo Chen smiled, then, to everyone's astonishment, took a single step and plunged straight into the Sumeru passage.

When his figure vanished, the hall erupted in clamor.

"Courting death!"

"That Sumeru passage contains spatial cutting force—who dares touch it?"

"He didn't even open a magic power shield, just waltzed right in?"

"Too ostentatious! Even if he has some means to protect himself, I've never seen such an arrogant existence. I wonder how he's survived until now?"

Zheng Guan Daoist frowned, interrupting everyone's words.

"Let's go!"

His Dao register unfurled, covering the group as they all entered the Sumeru passage.

...

Within the passage.

Luo Chen guarded his mind, extending his divine sense to the utmost.

Not only that, his Crimson-Eyed Golden Pupils had already been activated.

Those people were right—the Sumeru passage did contain spatial cutting force.

But compared to the chaotic spatial rifts above the Eastern Nether Abyss, it was much more orderly.

Within it, relying on his previous experience studying the appearance and disappearance of spatial rifts, he avoided each dangerous spot, allowing the vast spiritual energy to carry him toward the end.

Of course, there were places he couldn't pay attention to during this process.

But relying on his powerful physique, he could still resist the wisps of cutting force.

"If I am to have another chance to fight Azure Frost in the future, then spatial passages and even rifts will be my best experimental subjects for studying how to counter her."

"Therefore, how could I miss the opportunity to personally experience it?"

Murmuring to himself, Luo Chen focused his attention to the extreme.

...

Within the Sumeru Pavilion.

A purple-robed Daoist sat cross-legged, his expression wooden.

But in his heart, he was quite active.

"I wonder how many fellow Daoists will join the Corpse Alliance this time to jointly attack the Life and Death Gate?"

"I hope some formidable ones come, not always just trash."

To outsiders, Sages were lofty and exalted, but in this man's mouth, they became mere trash. Truly shocking.

But this man was indeed qualified to say so.

As the Supreme Elder of the Purple Blood Sect, at the fifth layer of the Sage realm, and having personally slain a Sage of the Life and Death Gate—all of this was enough for him to look down on ordinary Sages.

This time, he was here to welcome the newcomers, not as a mere formality, but to intimidate.

The reputation of Purple Robe Marquis was still quite resounding in the Southern Frontier.

As he muttered to himself, a disturbance suddenly arose in the Sumeru Pavilion.

Layers of ripples spread slowly through space.

Purple Robe Marquis looked up. "They're here?"

The next moment!

The ripples turned into waves, surging outward.

*Sssss...*

Large swaths of mountain trees were snapped, their broken ends smooth as glass.

Purple Robe Marquis's expression changed slightly as he cursed under his breath, "What is Zheng Guan doing, making such a big commotion?"

Cursing, he retreated in embarrassment.

Such spatial fluctuations were not something he dared to face head-on.

Suddenly.

A figure twisted into view within the vortex.

*Thump!*

That figure landed heavily like a meteor, ignoring the spatial ripples behind him.

A pair of golden-glowing eyes first glanced at Purple Robe Marquis, then turned to look up at the mountainside.

"Is this Sumeru Mountain?"

What met his eyes was not particularly majestic, somewhat small and delicate.

But faintly, layer upon layer, it seemed as if countless worlds converged within.

This was a phenomenon of spatial overlap!

Behind him, Purple Robe Marquis's pupils contracted as he barked, "Who goes there? Why do you force your way into the Sumeru Realm Mountain?"

"Force my way?"

Luo Chen chuckled lightly.

Before he could explain, slight spatial fluctuations spread behind him.

Then, one by one, figures materialized.

The moment Zheng Guan Daoist landed, he looked at Luo Chen.

"Fellow Daoist Qi, are you alright?"

The others also looked over curiously.

When they saw Luo Chen completely unharmed, one by one, they couldn't help but draw in a cold breath.

Although they had passed through the Sumeru passage safely under the protection of Zheng Guan's treasure, deep within it, they could still feel the omnipresent danger.

One could imagine that without a precious treasure's protection, crossing the Sumeru passage personally would require paying a tremendous price.

They never expected Luo Chen to emerge without a single injury.

Only then did Purple Robe Marquis realize that Luo Chen hadn't forced his way in—he had simply gone ahead.

Thinking of this, he was even more shocked.

"Zheng Guan, who is this man?"

Hearing this voice, the others finally shifted their attention to the purple-robed Daoist.

Those familiar with him blurted out directly.

"Purple Robe Marquis!"

The Supreme Elder of the Southern Frontier's major sect, the Purple Blood Sect!

This identity naturally drew gasps of surprise.

Even such an expert had quietly joined the Fierce Mountain Corpse Alliance?

But to their shock, Purple Robe Marquis no longer cared.

He only wanted to know the name of the man who had walked out of the spatial fluctuations relying purely on his physical body.

"He is the Overlord Cultivator Qi Tian!"

Zheng Guan introduced him.

Purple Robe Marquis raised an eyebrow, his wooden face already showing a hint of fervor.

This was a true expert, a worthy opponent!

However, Luo Chen paid him no heed, already striding toward Sumeru Mountain.

(Chapter End)
