# Chapter 831: Sword Array First Display, Demon-Slaying Sage

"Kill!"

"Kill!"

The sounds of battle filled the mountains and plains, deafening beast roars echoing everywhere.

Along with the seemingly endless explosions, they formed a complex yet unified grand symphony on the battlefield.

Ten figures stood high in the sky, gazing into the distance at that towering, majestic city.

The formation's light rose and fell without rest, cultivators gathered like clouds.

Looking further up, stars flickered in the nine heavens, radiating a captivating yet dangerous light.

"With the Black Tortoise Four Directions Formation ahead and the Zhou Tian Star Dipper Formation above, this Lingtian Pass is truly a turtle shell—nowhere to strike!"

A sigh arose among the crowd, drawing echoes of agreement.

"Forget it, there's no need for us to go all out. Following the commander's orders, we'll focus on attrition."

"Let's make our move!"

"Be careful of the Sages inside the city. If you can't win, don't linger!"

"I'll head to the southern city."

"I'll go to the western city!"

...

One after another, figures exuded vast demonic auras. Demonic winds swept forth, carrying massive demon clouds that surged from all directions toward Lingtian Pass.

Had this horrifying scene occurred at the war's beginning, it would have terrified everyone.

But amid the day-after-day, year-after-year conflict, it was no longer considered a major event.

It was just another instance of fourth-rank Demon Emperors attempting to harass Lingtian Pass and drain the city's resources.

Low-level cultivators need not worry—Sage-level experts would naturally come forth to resist.

And indeed!

The moment the Demon Emperors launched their assault, Lingtian Pass seemed to receive a signal. Figures soared into the air, transforming into streaks of light as they flew toward different opponents.

These Demon Emperors were intercepted before they could even approach Lingtian Pass.

A burly man in black robes, his face dotted with mottled spots and his eyes filled with brutality, charged forward swiftly and fiercely.

Suddenly!

A massive cyan palm print shot out from Lingtian Pass.

Its speed was neither fast nor slow, but its momentum was immense and powerful—clearly not something to be trifled with.

The mottled-faced man snorted coldly. Before the palm print could expand, he suddenly threw a punch.

*Boom!*

The palm print instantly collapsed, sparks flying everywhere like blooming fireworks.

A figure had appeared before him, unknown when.

"This Sect Master is Luo Chen. May I ask your esteemed name, Fellow Daoist?"

Hearing the name Luo Chen, the mottled-faced man's pupils contracted sharply.

His forward charge came to an abrupt halt.

He looked cautiously at Luo Chen. "This Emperor is Taobao, Lord of the Blue Amber Great Grassland."

The Hundred Thousand Mountains was a general term, not all mountains.

Within it were mountains and waters, great rivers flowing, countless lakes and seas.

Naturally, where there were high mountains, there were valleys; where there were hills, there were grasslands.

Luo Chen nodded. "The Lord of Blue Amber should stay well in the great grassland. Why come here to stir up dust?"

Taobao sneered coldly. "Lest you not know, five thousand years ago, my clan's territory was right beneath our feet."

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow.

Looking down, he had to admit—if not for Lingtian Pass, this man-made towering city rising from the ground, the tens of thousands of miles around were indeed quite flat, very suitable for low-growing vegetation.

But because of the yearly great battles, hardly any green could be seen.

"So, you insist on entering Lingtian Pass?"

"Enough talk. Let's settle this with our hands!"

As the words fell, Taobao leaped forward, his figure exploding with speed. In his mad dash, he manifested several afterimages, indistinguishable from the real one.

Luo Chen sighed, but there was no fear in his eyes. Instead, he looked eager to try.

His eyes, no longer amber but pale gold, swept across the growing number of afterimages.

Suddenly, the corner of his mouth lifted. He lightly spat out a beam of light.

Not only that, his fingers rapidly formed seals, mana surging wildly.

Just as all the afterimages reached ten zhang before him, a phoenix phantom flew out from behind him with a sharp cry.

It circled once, and all the afterimages shattered instantly.

Then the phoenix charged headlong into the depths of the void.

*Bang!*

With an explosion, a yellow light burst forth.

Taobao was shocked. "He really can find me. The rumors are true!"

In the midst of his shock, he suddenly frowned, turned, and pointed a finger.

*Zheng!*

A sword pellet was forcibly stopped by him.

Then a lotus bloomed, sword light spilling forth.

"A sword cultivator?" The mottled-faced man was startled at the sight of the sword pellet, but the sword light emerged far too slowly. He couldn't help but sneer. "A false alarm. Such crude methods pose no threat to me!"

"Break!"

He curled his fingers into a fist and smashed down.

The sword pellet had no power to resist and was forcibly driven into the dust.

Watching the man over there, who was gathering power to cast a new spell, Taobao took a deep breath.

Since the war began, any notable experts from both human and demon races knew something of each other.

Luo Chen of the Pill Sect, with three recorded battles and two direct kills, had his intelligence long memorized by many Demon Emperors.

"This man excels in fire techniques, can break illusions, and is skilled at discerning truth from falsehood. The Illuminationless Demon Emperor, who excelled at concealment, and the Frost Eagle Demon Emperor, who was adept at void escape, both died under his hand."

"To fight this man, one must engage in close combat and leverage the demon race's powerful physical advantage!"

With this thought, Taobao wildly raised his demonic yuan, yellow wind gathering beneath his feet.

His speed suddenly doubled.

He transformed into an arrow leaving the string, charging straight at Luo Chen.

Not only that, he waved both hands, continuously striking out claw-shaped attacks.

Faced with this, Luo Chen's expression remained calm and indifferent.

"Trying to close the distance?"

"Don't they know I'm also skilled in body refinement?"

"Forget it. I won't fight bare-handed today. Let me try my new methods!"

Luo Chen stepped with Moon Step, his figure ghostly, dodging and weaving to avoid each attack.

Even those he couldn't avoid, he withstood with his Mystic Dust Armor.

Not only that, he opened his mouth and spat out sword pellets one after another.

*Chi! Chi! Chi! Chi!*

Unfortunately, these sword pellets were not as well-controlled as those of orthodox sword cultivators. They lacked the thousand changes, swiftness, and sharpness, and were often knocked away before even approaching Taobao's body.

Luo Chen was not discouraged. When there was no room to dodge, he watched coldly.

*Boom!*

A punch smashed down, passing by.

Taobao was stunned. A strike locked on by divine sense—how could it miss?

But in an instant, he suddenly realized it was an illusionary soul technique that had caused his attack to deviate.

In a duel between experts, a hair's breadth difference meant missing by a thousand li.

Fearing Luo Chen would seize the opportunity to counterattack, he pulled his right hand sharply, and a massive blade light slashed horizontally.

Luo Chen lightly dodged, having no intention of pressing the advantage.

This scene seemed like mockery.

Taobao held his long blade, momentarily caught in a dilemma.

"This man is not only skilled in breaking illusions with techniques, but his movement is also elusive and unpredictable. Moreover, he has a set of precious armor protecting him. Ordinary attacks simply cannot harm him."

"A full-power strike is also easily missed due to that strange illusion technique."

"To kill him, I alone cannot do it in a short time."

After understanding this, Taobao made up his mind. Without hesitation, he turned and fled.

Behind him came the man's mocking voice.

"Fellow Daoist, you come and go as you please. Don't you think that's a bit disrespectful to this Sect Master?"

"What do you want..."

Before he finished speaking, Taobao suddenly sensed something was wrong.

A sharp aura began to spread inexplicably between heaven and earth.

Like a lotus blooming, or an old tree sprouting new buds, strands of sword qi began to fill the world.

White-gold, cyan-green, azure blue, murky yellow, crimson red...

Five-colored light, dazzling to the eye.

"It's those sword pellets!"

Taobao suddenly realized. Those seemingly harmless sword pellets were actually hidden traps.

Without any delay, Taobao slashed his long blade horizontally, instantly cutting out a blade light hundreds of zhang long, creating a vacuum zone in the sword-qi-filled space. Then he transformed into an arrow leaving the string and broke through the air.

Luo Chen watched this scene, unafraid that he would escape. His ten fingers frequently moved.

Sword seals continuously struck out. The five carefully refined sword pellets began to display a different style.

The sword array rose!

Sword light turned into threads, interweaving heaven and earth.

The vacuum zone that had been cut open was instantly sealed again by sword threads.

Crisscrossing, fine sword marks appeared on Taobao's mottled face.

Not only that, the mana that Luo Chen had changed in attribute beforehand transformed into five elements—wood, fire, earth, metal, water—continuously interweaving and merging, generating a tremendous suppressive force within the sword array.

The Great Five Elements Sword Array was not famous for sharpness. The so-called sharpness was merely the incidental power of true-artifact-level sword pellets.

What this sword array was truly formidable at was the terrifying suppression of enemies through the mutual generation and restraint of the five elements.

Visibly, Taobao's speed within the sword array became much slower.

No matter how his long blade slashed, cutting out paths of escape, his speed prevented him from following those paths out.

Instead, his powerful physical body began to show wounds one after another.

Blood rich with qi and blood began to spray across the sky.

In the blink of an eye, the demon became bloodied and disheveled, utterly miserable.

Luo Chen controlled the sword array, using his agile eyes to observe the enemy's movements while absorbing experience and insights.

"Using the methods from the *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon* to refine the sword pellets is slow to show effect, but the connection is tight—no less than orthodox sword cultivation sects like the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect's Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion."

"Of the five sword pellets, the White-Gold Sword Pellet and the Cyan Wood Sword Pellet are the main attackers. The other three... have flaws, insufficient suppression."

"Simultaneously controlling five sword pellets puts too much pressure on the divine soul. My divine soul foundation is now comparable to a fifth-layer Sage, even approaching the sixth layer, yet I still feel strained. I need to improve further."

"No wonder Pavilion Master Li so desired the Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid. A good pair of eyes can greatly reduce the pressure on divine sense."

Inside the sword array.

Taobao charged left and right but could not escape.

He suddenly stopped, then sprayed blood around him. Each drop of blood transformed into a figure identical to him.

Then they charged in all directions.

Unable to break through by force, he tried to split into avatars to find an opportunity?

Unfortunately!

Under the grinding of thousands of sword threads, each avatar shattered without resistance.

Among them, one avatar received focused attention, frantically ground down.

Clearly, under Luo Chen's eyes that could see through illusions, such tricks could not fool him.

Suddenly!

The remaining avatars abruptly charged toward the heavily attacked clone. As they merged *shua shua shua*, Taobao's hundred-zhang true body was revealed.

It was a mottled leopard, standing on black clouds with four feet, a desolate gold blade held in its mouth, majestic beyond compare.

But this majestic leopard was now covered in deep wounds, utterly disheveled.

He let out a low roar, then instead of retreating, he charged toward Lingtian Pass, the desolate gold blade in his mouth slashing out a terrifying blade light.

Seeing this, Luo Chen couldn't help but sigh.

"No wonder Han Zhan emphasized that five same-level true-artifact sword pellets were necessary."

"This flaw really cannot be hidden from Sage-level experts!"

"But, it's enough!"

Luo Chen curled his finger, beginning to accumulate another spell. Not only that, a blood light transformed in his hand, and a small sword quietly flew out.

*Boom!*

With a loud noise, the giant leopard, carrying countless sword qi, returned to the clear sky.

Seeing the blue sky again, he was overjoyed.

"Luo Chen of the Pill Sect, this sword array cannot trap this Emperor!"

The golden blade flew back, held in his mouth.

With the desolate gold blade in his mouth, Taobao laughed wildly. Not lingering to fight, he charged toward the high sky.

Unable to enter Lingtian Pass, he would borrow the path of the nine heavens to kill his way out!

Throughout this process, he constantly watched Luo Chen's movements.

In the corner of his eye, the white-robed Daoist reached out and recalled the five sword pellets, watching him coldly.

"Giving up?"

"Good!"

Taobao couldn't help but breathe a sigh of relief. He had laughed wildly, but his fear of Luo Chen had not diminished in the slightest.

If he hadn't spent a great price to find the flaw in the sword array, he might have truly been trapped inside and slowly flayed alive.

He had already decided that once back, he would sell the intelligence on Luo Chen of the Pill Sect at a high price.

If one encountered this man, without the realm of a mid-fourth-rank or even a Great Demon Emperor, one must never linger in battle.

Just as his mind relaxed a little because Luo Chen was not pursuing, a sudden change occurred in the sky.

Fire clouds gathered, radiant light myriad.

A huge dragon head emerged from within, fixing its gaze on Taobao with one look.

"Roar!"

Flame Dragon Descends!

Caught off guard, the flame dragon charged straight down, colliding most directly with Taobao's true body, which had no room to dodge.

Blade light appeared again, but this time, beneath the flame dragon, it seemed so fragile and insignificant.

*Boom!*

The fire gradually extinguished. More than half of his body gone, Taobao's eyes were filled with terror.

But this time, he didn't even dare look at Luo Chen, only thinking of fleeing for his life.

However...

*Chi!*

A blood sword flew out, severing the desolate gold blade, plunging into the leopard's head like a blood thread, deepening continuously until it pierced the Nascent Soul.

"Ahhh..."

The chilling screams spread for a hundred li, causing countless demon beasts and many cultivators inside and outside Lingtian Pass to shiver from the bottom of their hearts.

Everyone knew that another Demon Emperor had fallen.

...

*Thump!*

A heavy object crashed to the ground, stirring up a cloud of dust.

Under a multitude of awed gazes, Luo Chen waved his hand, signaling the cultivators of the Luo Tian Treasure Pavilion to come out and clean up the half of the demon emperor's corpse.

He himself, amid a buzz of discussion, drifted back to Danluo Mountain.

"How many is that?"

"The third Demon Emperor! Under the Pill Sect's hand, three Demon Emperor-level existences have fallen. He is truly number one at Lingtian!"

"Such achievements are not just number one at Lingtian. In the entire Eastern Wilderness, can a second be found?"

"Why call him Pill Sect? With such slaughter, calling him Demon-Slaying Sage is not an exaggeration!"

"Demon-Slaying Sage, how fitting!"

As Luo Chen made his moves time and again, the title Demon-Slaying Sage began to spread gradually among countless cultivators.

Even at the demon clan's headquarters, his name grew increasingly renowned. Ordinary Demon Emperors simply did not want to encounter him.

There was even a rumor: those who see the Demon-Slaying Daoist may flee without guilt.

Luo Chen saw these effects but did not take them to heart.

He knew that as his reputation grew, the enemies he would face in the future would only be stronger, their methods more numerous.

Too frequent appearances would instead expose his trump cards.

Therefore, he specifically requested from the Lord of Lingtian Pass not to dispatch him too often for battle.

Given his brilliant achievements, the Lord of Lingtian Pass agreed to this request.

Thus, over the next two years, although Luo Chen occasionally made moves, he was much more restrained compared to other Sages.

Moreover, he slightly curbed his killing intent, no longer pursuing utter annihilation.

Once, when facing an enemy Sage who abandoned his physical body to escape, under the watchful eyes of other Demon Emperors, he chose to give up the pursuit.

One day.

On Danluo Mountain, Luo Chen sat cross-legged before the Primordial Unity Cauldron, his eyes filled with anticipation.

"Calculating the time, the Heart-Inquiring Mirror should be born!"
