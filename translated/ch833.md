# Chapter 833: A Great Dream, the Boundless Demon Emperor

In a secluded courtyard, a dignified middle-aged man knitted his brows in deep worry, his gaze occasionally falling upon an orchid-scented room as he let out soft sighs.

Outside the courtyard, hurried footsteps suddenly sounded.

Before long, a young girl rushed in breathlessly.

Her small face was round and chubby, her skin fair with a rosy flush. On her adorable baby-fat cheeks, surprise and delight were written all over.

"Father, I've found out! That legendary immortal land really exists. Mother can be saved!"

The middle-aged man shook his head. "Ranran, give it up! Your mother's illness is beyond medicine or stone. Instead of tormenting her further, it's better to spend more time with her and let her live out these last two years in peace."

The girl's round face was unusually stubborn.

"I won't!"

Faced with those persistent eyes, the middle-aged man's face showed anger, but it slowly softened.

"Tell me the place. I'll send people to seek the immortals."

The girl broke into a smile. "Ordinary people won't find it. I have to go myself!"

...

In a frozen wasteland, a straggling group of people trudged forward with difficulty against the wind and snow.

The pack beasts pulling the carts snorted hot air from their nostrils, refusing to take another step.

A servant's voice came from behind: "Miss, we can't go any further. Ahead lies the legendary Death Ice Sea."

The girl at the lead was wrapped tightly in a fluffy white coat.

Hearing this, she waved her small hand.

"No, we must press on. Only by crossing the Death Ice Sea can we truly meet the immortals."

The servants had no choice but to obey the young miss's orders and continue forward.

Yet the heavy snow buried people, the biting wind cut to the bone, and everyone struggled to move forward, their vision growing worse and worse.

It was midday, but the sky inexplicably darkened.

Whoosh...

The wind rose without warning.

A blizzard, unnoticed by anyone, charged into the convoy, scattering everyone in disarray.

When the martial grandmaster leading the group finally managed to gather most of the people, he discovered in horror—

"Miss Xie is missing!"

...

"I will definitely find the immortals!"

"I will definitely save Mother!"

"It's just ahead, just ahead!"

The girl shivered all over, her lips trembling, her teeth chattering audibly.

But all around was a vast expanse of white. Where was any immortal paradise?

The girl walked alone on the ice field, driven forward by an obsession. But the further she went, the more lost she became.

Suddenly, a flash of red caught the corner of her eye.

She hurried toward it. As the distance closed, the source of the red light gradually came into view.

It was clearly a large dog whose fur was like a ball of flame!

It trod on white ice, majestic and divine.

It stood quietly on the ice field, a pair of golden pupils fixed on the running girl.

"Big doggy, do you know— Ah!"

The girl called out as she ran, but her footing was unsteady. With a careless step, she fell into an ice crevice.

Her frail body plummeted endlessly.

Freezing liquid surged around her, swallowing her up.

This was no ice field at all—it was a great lake, merely frozen over by the extreme cold.

Ignorant mortals had named it the Death Ice Sea.

The golden-eyed red dog still stood quietly by the lake, watching the girl fall until her figure was lost from sight.

No one knew how long passed.

Whoosh!

Suddenly, a beam of treasure light shot up into the sky.

After the light, cracking sounds rang out continuously.

Large sheets of ice shattered and collapsed. Amidst the surging waves, an unconscious girl frozen into an ice block floated up from the lake bottom, clutching a bright pearl in her arms.

The golden-eyed red dog looked up at the sky and finally moved.

It walked across the waves as if on level ground, approaching the girl.

With a mouthful, it tossed her backward, and the ice-block girl landed on its back.

Without even discerning the direction, the golden-eyed red dog carried the girl on its back and headed straight north.

As it trudged along alone, great heat emanated from its body. The ice figure on its back began to melt continuously. The girl's eyelashes fluttered faintly, showing signs of waking.

Not long after they left, a beam of light suddenly descended at the spot where the treasure light had appeared.

It was a beautiful woman.

Arriving at the place, she plunged without hesitation into the broken ice lake.

A moment later, she flew back out.

The beautiful woman's face showed suspicion and uncertainty.

"That's not right. That treasure light was clearly the Grandmaster's lifebound artifact, the Canglong Treasure Pearl. It vanished when the Grandmaster passed away. Now that it's finally seen the light of day again, how could it have mysteriously disappeared?"

"Could it be... that someone took it?"

Thinking of the latter possibility, the woman's face turned murderous. How could her sect's heirloom treasure be left outside!

She mounted her flying sword and shot into the sky, frantically patrolling the area around the ice lake at extreme speed.

Even the storm that had swept through earlier—she charged straight into it and searched a full circle.

No result. No result. Still no result!

Suddenly turning her head, the woman looked down. On the white snow, a patch of red was moving unhurriedly forward.

Thud!

The beautiful woman landed heavily, her mana surging, her long sword pointing at that patch of red.

But after truly seeing it clearly, her expression softened.

A young girl was holding a white treasure pearl, sitting listlessly on a large red dog.

"Big doggy, did you save me?"

"Where are you taking me?"

"I need to find immortals. They can save my mother. Can you take me to them?"

The girl's words were like sleep-talking.

But upon seeing the beautiful woman descend from the sky, the confusion in her eyes swept away.

"Are you an immortal?"

The beautiful woman walked over step by step, staring at the girl, a strange light in her eyes.

"I am not an immortal, but I should be what you call an immortal."

Even as she said this, her heart began to pound.

*Ultimate-grade Ice Spirit Root!*

*Seven-Orifice Exquisite Body!*

No wonder she had stumbled upon the Grandmaster's treasure by chance.

This was clearly the Grandmaster's blessing from the beyond, sending a heaven-chosen one to my sect!

Drawing near, the beautiful woman reached out and stroked the girl's baby-fat cheeks.

"What is your name?"

The girl instinctively wanted to say her name, but when she opened her mouth, she froze.

"What... is my name?"

"It doesn't matter. You'll remember later. For now, I'll give you a name. Let's call you Canglong!"

"Canglong..."

The girl repeated the name, her dazed eyes gradually clearing.

"Would you be willing to return to the sect with me? Learn immortal arts, master celestial techniques. Once you've learned, you can save your mother!"

"Mm-hmm, I'm willing!"

"Then let's go!"

"Where to?"

"The sect!"

"What is our sect called?"

"Icecastle!"

"Oh, oh! Can I bring Big Doggy? He saved me."

The beautiful woman looked down at the dog that was like a ball of flame. Meeting its golden pupils, she nodded as if possessed.

Three figures gradually faded into the wind and snow, until they disappeared from sight.

...

Hum...

Inside the ice chamber, Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes.

There was some fatigue, but more joy.

He had expended considerable effort and finally succeeded in getting Canglong into the illusion.

His original plan had been to wake her within the illusion and then teach her the *Soul-Splitting Art*.

But after carefully understanding the wonders of the Karmic Fate Immortal Mirror, he abandoned that initial, crudest plan.

The illusions created by the Karmic Fate Immortal Mirror inherited the Ghost God Heart-Inquiring Mirror's ability to simulate and excavate, while also possessing the almost-real illusion-inducing effect of a fourth-order illusion core.

If the geniuses of the Tianyuan Dao Sect could use such illusion cores to comprehend and experience the true meaning of laws,

Why couldn't he use this function to let Canglong's soul unconsciously learn the *Soul-Splitting Art* within the illusion?

Moreover, outside, Luo Chen would gradually have her take soul-nourishing medicines like Netherpassage Pills, Tranquility Powder, and Great Soul Pills.

Thus, when she woke from her dream, the great work would be accomplished!

The only drawback was that someone needed to guide her during this process, lest Canglong's soul detect something amiss and interrupt the illusion cultivation.

And the guide, naturally, was Luo Chen!

He had two divided souls, sharing his thoughts and ideas, and they too had mastered the *Soul-Splitting Art*.

They could take turns controlling the Karmic Fate Immortal Mirror and guiding Canglong's cultivation within the illusion.

"Let it be a great dream!"

Luo Chen exhaled a turbid breath, then poured a large amount of mana into the Karmic Fate Immortal Mirror to keep it running continuously.

Fortunately, Canglong's cultivation level had not yet reached the Sage stage, so the mana consumption was not great.

But to guard against any oversight on his part, Luo Chen infused enough mana to maintain the mirror's minimum operation for many years.

...

Leaving the ice chamber, Luo Chen spent several days recuperating.

He restored his mana and adjusted his state.

Waiting for the great battle to come!

This was not Luo Chen crying wolf. The current battle situation was truly as Wang Yuan had said—growing increasingly fierce, with no one able to stand aloof any longer.

The appearance of casualties among Sage-level cultivators at Lingtian Pass was proof enough!

In this demonic calamity, the initial attackers were the demon race.

As they charged out from the Eastern Wilderness's Million Mountains and continuously conquered human domains, human experts began to fall one after another.

This forced a large number of cultivators to keep retreating.

Until the establishment of Lingtian Pass!

With this majestic city as a stronghold, Sage casualties dropped sharply.

Not only that, but with formations backing them up, the Sage cultivators had confidence when fighting outside the city. At worst, they could retreat to Lingtian Pass if they couldn't win.

Thus, in the past decade of fighting at Lingtian Pass, almost no Sages had died.

On the contrary, the demon side, lacking formation support and continuously assaulting Lingtian Pass, began to suffer casualties one after another.

Luo Chen's brilliant battle record was proof of the past decade's war situation.

To be honest, if not for the protection of the Black Tortoise Four Directions Formation and the Zhou Tian Star Dipper Formation, he would not have dared to act so freely.

But now, the stalemate was slowly being broken!

The demon side simply had too many high-level experts. Even with Lingtian Pass backing them up, a slightly weaker Sage could still fall if they weren't careful.

Four Great Commanders, thirty Demon Emperors—this force was enough to destroy any Sage-level force in the world.

The appearance of Sage casualties was a very bad sign!

...

That day, Luo Chen arrived at the city wall.

Looking at the human cultivator army outside the city, continuously routed under the assault of a sea of demon beasts, he couldn't help but take a deep breath.

"On the western side, the number of low-level demon beasts has grown this much?"

The Sage standing beside him was none other than Mu Zhenren, the master of the Mu Estate.

His expression was grave as he said solemnly, "Yes. I don't know what's going on. The number of high-level experts on the demon side hasn't increased, but the quantity of low-level demon beasts has far exceeded our imagination. The strategic buffer zone we originally fought for is being gradually eaten away under the impact of such massive numbers. If this continues, Lingtian Pass will be surrounded again in just a few days."

Luo Chen frowned. "Let me take a look!"

Mu Zhenren was puzzled.

Then he watched as Luo Chen, right before his eyes, formed hand seals and chanted incantations, casting a spell.

Large patches of cloud qi surged forth with Luo Chen's mana, rising into the sky.

As if responding, the sky, which had been cleared of clouds by the fierce battle, inexplicably gathered great swaths of clouds.

These clouds converged, then exploded apart, transforming into countless small cloud clusters that drifted in all directions.

Like chess pieces scattered across a chessboard.

And before Luo Chen—

"Appear!"

A water mirror suddenly materialized.

Scenes of various battlefields began to appear on it one after another.

"What spell is this? Its coverage range is so vast—observing ten thousand li from a single corner!"

Mu Zhenren was utterly shocked.

Luo Chen smiled slightly and spoke only four words: "Cloud-Chess Water Mirror!"

This spell was one Luo Chen had created himself during his time in Central Continent.

The principle was not particularly profound, but its subtlety was rare in the world.

It combined the low-level Water Mirror Art, the cloud patterns from basic formation theory, and even inspiration from the Peach Garden chess formation set up by that Chess Saint in Central Continent.

It could greatly compensate for a cultivator's insufficient divine sense and limited eyesight, allowing them to observe a much larger area.

And its strength lay in concealment!

At this moment, the clouds in the sky were like chess pieces scattered across the battlefield, gathering all the scenes together and imprinting them on the water mirror before Luo Chen.

And the scenes from various parts of the battlefield vividly fell into Luo Chen's nimble eyes.

If he hadn't looked, he wouldn't have known. But looking gave him a shock!

Compared to the other four directions, the sea of demon beasts on the western city was nothing.

To the north and south, it was pitch-black masses, demon beasts like locusts.

And at the eastern city, where the defensive pressure was greatest, some massive siege beasts had even begun to appear, fearlessly charging at the city walls.

Especially beyond the maximum range of the Cloud-Chess Water Mirror, countless low-level demon beasts were still pouring in endlessly.

This number—was it merely tens of millions?

It had likely already reached hundreds of millions!

Luo Chen swallowed his saliva. "Have they gone mad? Do they intend to use the lives of low-level demon beasts to smash through Lingtian Pass?"

Mu Zhenren watched the water mirror beside him. This was also his first time viewing the battlefield from a global perspective.

Horror filled his eyes.

"Can Lingtian Pass be defended?"

The answer was unknown.

After all, the number of cultivators in Lingtian Pass was only around ten million.

The only thing they could rely on were the killing formations set up in advance!

Under the control of cultivators from the Heavenly Capital Sect, the formations activated.

They fired beams of death at the most dangerous parts of the battlefield, and large swaths of demon beasts fell like cut grass.

Then, Demon Emperors would suddenly appear, attacking the formations from over ten li away.

Seeing this, Luo Chen realized that he might have to make a move soon.

After all, they couldn't allow Demon Emperors to recklessly attack the formations.

And indeed!

One day, two days...

As time passed, the pressure on the western district grew heavier and heavier. Many cultivators were forced to retreat into the city, and a massive number of demon beasts gathered within the ten-li range restricted by the no-fly formation.

One killing formation after another began to activate.

Vaguely, figures of great demons appeared in the sky.

And Luo Chen's Cloud-Chess Water Mirror had long been destroyed by a great demon with keen spiritual senses.

"Alchemy Sect, you'll have to make a move," Mu Zhenren said.

Luo Chen's expression was calm. "No matter. I'm used to this kind of thing."

Without hesitation, he released his divine sense toward an unfamiliar Demon Emperor who was about to attack a formation.

His divine sense locked on, and Luo Chen shot into the sky.

The Demon Emperor seemed to sense it, his battle-filled eyes meeting Luo Chen's.

"State your name, newcomer!"

"Luo Chen of the Alchemy Sect!"

The tall, thin Demon Emperor blinked and couldn't help licking his lips.

"So you're the Demon-Slaying Sage? I am Boundless. I was just looking for a chance to test my skills against you."

As he spoke, he reached behind his back.

Chi...

A sharp sound of flesh tearing rang out as a sword covered in thorns was pulled from his spine.

Shua!

The limp sword flicked and straightened, the bloody aura on it furrowing brows.

This was no sword at all—

It was clearly his own spine!
