# Chapter 895: Riding the Mystic Turtle, Crossing the Nether Abyss—Petty Tricks Are Especially Laughable

Within the endless abyss, black waters suddenly parted, revealing a vast expanse of greenish-yellow.

It resembled solid land.

Looking closely, however, one could see that although it was rounded, it also had edges and corners.

*Splash!*

Amidst the boundless waves, an enormous neck suddenly emerged.

A serpent-like head rose high, spewing black water, opening its mouth to emit a low roar.

"Moo!!!"

A sound like a deep ox bellow, yet also like a suppressed snake hiss, slowly spread outward.

*Boom!*

The black water submerged back into the abyss once more. The giant beast rose and fell amidst the water mist, quietly gazing down at the few people standing at the edge of the Nether Abyss.

An old man walked out tremblingly from above, bowing slightly to the Heavenly Nether City Lord.

"Senior Brother Ye, please!"

The Heavenly Nether City Lord nodded in acknowledgment. "I'll trouble Junior Brother Lu."

Then he looked at Luo Chen, making an inviting gesture. "Fellow Daoist, please!"

Luo Chen gazed at the giant beast, a thoughtful expression on his face, but his steps were unhurried. Taking Luo Lingxi by the hand, he gracefully ascended onto the beast's back.

He glanced at the old man beside him. The Heavenly Nether City Lord had already begun introducing him.

"This is Junior Brother Lu Xianfeng, who has been responsible for the sect's ferry duties for several hundred years."

The old man cupped his hands toward Luo Chen.

"And this is Luo Chen of the Alchemy Sect!"

Luo Chen returned the greeting.

Thus, the two parties became acquainted.

Only then did Luo Chen have the chance to ask about the giant beast carrying them.

"Is this none other than the legendary Mystic Turtle of ancient times?"

Lu Xianfeng nodded calmly. "Good eyes, Fellow Daoist. This is indeed a desolate beast, a Mystic Turtle."

Luo Chen's lips curled slightly. "A desolate beast Mystic Turtle? In my opinion, it looks more like a Mystic Turtle evolved from a Ring-Headed Turtle, and the transformation is incomplete—some characteristics of the Cliff Serpent have yet to fade away."

At this, Lu Xianfeng's calm expression finally showed a flicker of surprise.

Recognizing a Mystic Turtle was not unusual.

But being able to identify what it had evolved from was truly astonishing.

The Heavenly Nether City Lord beside him laughed. "The Alchemy Sect's knowledge is indeed vast. You saw through this beast's origins at a single glance."

The great turtle beneath their feet swam unhurriedly, parting the black waters as it moved steadily forward.

Its movements were subtle, appearing slow, and its broad back felt like solid land, seemingly not moving at all.

Yet in the perception of the Sage-level cultivators, the scenery on both sides was changing at an extremely rapid pace.

"In this age, desolate beasts are hard to find, mostly hidden in the borderlands and wilderness. True Mystic Turtles are exceedingly rare."

"This beast was carefully cultivated by our sect from a Ring-Headed Turtle with rich bloodline. Our original intention was indeed to raise it into a Great Desolate Mystic Turtle as one of the sect's foundations. Unfortunately, it grew up in the Nether Abyss, was too deeply influenced by it, and its transformation became incomplete, ending up like this."

"In the end, we gave this defective product to the Lu family to handle ordinary ferry and transport duties."

The Nether Abyss beneath their feet gave one the feeling it could devour all things.

Yet this Mystic Turtle could move freely within it. Calling it a defective product seemed somewhat excessive.

But having a fourth-rank Mystic Turtle perform such menial tasks also offered a glimpse into the tip of the iceberg that was the Holy Land's foundation.

Lu Xianfeng cast a deep glance at Luo Chen, then took his leave and withdrew.

Luo Chen watched his retreating figure and asked curiously, "The Lu family? Could it be the family of that alchemist you mentioned earlier, Lu Chun?"

The Heavenly Nether City Lord nodded and began explaining some of the Nether Origin Sect's internal affairs to Luo Chen.

Although the Nether Origin Sect differed from the family-clan-dominated Tianyuan Dao Sect, over thousands of years of propagation, families had unknowingly sprung up within its ranks.

There were families like the Lu family, who specialized in beast taming; the Chu family, which had risen through alchemy; and even the Ye family behind the Heavenly Nether City Lord himself.

Perhaps the only difference was that these families did not have strict sectarian views. They still allowed their clan members to apprentice under experts from other families, continuing the orthodox tradition of master-disciple inheritance.

Luo Chen was very curious. "Are there any families left behind by Ascendant-level powerhouses, like the Twelve Great Houses of the Tianyuan Dao Sect?"

The Heavenly Nether City Lord shook his head. "Within our sect, once one achieves Ascension, the concept of family ceases to matter. Everything is based on the sect's interests. Even if a great power's family has the substance of a Great House, it lacks the name and cannot enjoy more privileges. It's not much different from an ordinary small family."

Luo Chen smiled but did not refute. Clearly, he did not truly believe it.

Taking himself as an example, because of his personal influence, his two Dao companions and his only bloodline, Luo Lingxi, all held extremely special positions within the Luo Tian Sect.

All resources were available to them as they wished.

How could a family relying on an Ascendant-level powerhouse be no different from others?

Setting this topic aside, Luo Chen casually asked the Heavenly Nether City Lord about other matters.

"Why haven't I seen Fellow Daoists Thunderchild and Chongming?"

"Are you displeased that they didn't escort you all the way?"

"Haha, how could that be?"

"Just a joke. I'm sure the Alchemy Sect wouldn't be so petty. Thunderchild returned to the sect long ago. As for Junior Brother Chongming, he had just emerged from seclusion and, feeling restless, decided to go on a journey. His visit to the Luo Tian Sect to call on you was merely incidental. By now, he has probably already left the Eastern Wilderness."

"Tsk tsk, the Holy Land's disciples are truly carefree! Cultivation is cultivation, travel is travel—unlike us mortals, who toil and bustle all day long."

"The Alchemy Sect is too modest."

Just as the two were chatting idly, the Mystic Turtle, carrying the group, was gradually approaching the shore.

Luo Chen stopped talking and gazed into the distance.

What he saw was a towering mountain standing tall, seemingly no different from an ordinary sect.

But that mountain was floating in midair, with no foundation connecting it to the ground below.

Such a strange sight greatly astonished Luo Chen.

"That is Jambu Mountain, where our sect's three ancestors cultivate in seclusion," the Heavenly Nether City Lord explained, then recounted the mountain's origins. "This mountain possesses geomagnetic force and was originally deep within the earth's core. Three thousand years ago, a great power from our sect discovered it and, using extreme methods, activated it, causing it to reverse its momentum and soar into the sky..."

Suddenly!

The Heavenly Nether City Lord's expression changed. He shouted, "Lu Xianfeng!"

No one answered.

And the giant beast beneath their feet, with a low roar, began to sink into the depths of the Nether Abyss.

Vast amounts of pitch-black liquid surged in from all directions.

Without hesitation, Luo Chen spread his mana protective barrier, enveloping Luo Lingxi within it.

At that moment, he felt the sounds of wailing ghosts and howling spirits outside his mana shield.

It was as if thousands of fierce ghosts and resentful souls were assaulting him.

Among them were countless Ghost Generals and Ghost Kings.

Even Ghost Emperor-level existences were lurking in the distance, poised to pounce at any moment.

"So the so-called Nether Abyss is said to have been drawn from the Nine Nether realms. But now it seems it is clearly a place where resentful souls and fierce ghosts are trapped. Could it be that the Nether Origin Sect has cast the souls of powerful ancient demons they slew into this endless abyss?"

A strange realization surged into Luo Chen's heart.

He looked around and clearly sensed the power of a formation that was both suppressing and enslaving.

The Nether Origin Sect had indeed carried out a massive man-made transformation project on this natural peril.

But what did it mean for them to confront him with this now?

The corners of Luo Chen's mouth lifted slightly, as if in contemptuous mockery, or perhaps disdain.

A young boy's panicked voice reached his ears.

"Father..."

Come to think of it, he hadn't actually heard Luo Lingxi call him "Father" many times, had he?

Luo Chen no longer ignored the giant turtle sinking into the abyss, the black water engulfing them.

He took a deep breath, his abdomen swelling like a frog's, then slowly exhaled.

A long, drawn-out breath, yet containing a complex rhythm.

At the same time, the black armor worn by the Nascent Soul miniature figure in his Purple Mansion emitted the aura of origin.

Moreover, surging source power rippled through his physique.

With the combination of these three, within the abyss, a hundred ghosts gave way, as if a great desolate beast that devoured demons had appeared.

The Heavenly Nether City Lord's pupils contracted sharply as he stared at Luo Chen in shock.

At this moment, he clearly sensed that the man before him had undergone a strange change in aura.

He no longer looked human, but like an ancient desolate beast devouring the sun and moon!

Most importantly, the Mystic Turtle, which had been sinking, shuddered violently and stopped its descent.

Then, opening its mouth to breathe, its frequency and rhythm matched Luo Chen's breathing perfectly.

"This..."

In the shadows, the old man Lu Xianfeng was greatly alarmed. Luo Chen could actually command the Mystic Turtle that the Lu family had communed with for a thousand years.

"Rise!"

With a light shout from the white-robed Daoist, the great turtle, as if understanding his words, obediently swam upward.

*Splash!!*

Within the Nether Abyss, the green land rose once more.

The white-robed Daoist held the young boy's hand, his posture elegant, not a speck of dust upon him.

Lu Xianfeng appeared in panic. "Senior Brother Ye, Alchemy Sect, the Mystic Turtle just now went mad and lost control. Please forgive me."

"Went mad?" The Heavenly Nether City Lord's expression was stern, his face iron-blue. He pointed at the turtle's head, which was now circling its neck and rubbing affectionately against Luo Chen. "So obedient, and you call that going mad?"

Lu Xianfeng was speechless.

"I will report this matter to the ancestor. Let your Lu family head explain it himself!"

Hearing this, the old man's face turned pale.

The Heavenly Nether City Lord snorted coldly and walked toward Luo Chen, his expression somewhat awkward.

"Alchemy Sect..."

"It's nothing. It seems some people simply don't welcome this sect."

Luo Chen waved his hand, then led Luo Lingxi ashore gracefully.

On the shore of the abyss, several cultivators holding large nets stared blankly at this scene, unsure whether to cast their nets or withdraw them.

Luo Chen laughed. "Were you planning to fish me out just now? How thoughtful!"

Those cultivators' faces turned pale, their lips dry.

The Heavenly Nether City Lord hurriedly disembarked from the turtle's back. "Shameless wretches, get out of here!"

The group of low-level cultivators fled in disgrace.

"Alchemy Sect, my apologies. I didn't expect such a thing to happen."

Luo Chen's gaze was deep, looking toward the empty sky, as if someone was watching from the shadows there.

"I also didn't expect your sect's way of welcoming people to be so... unique."

Unique was an understatement—it was laughable.

The Heavenly Nether City Lord found it both amusing and infuriating. The juniors in the sect had such childish ways.

Such tricks, to a seasoned cultivator like the Alchemy Sect, were no different from a monkey show—they only made the performers look foolish.

He sighed. "Let's go. Let's settle in first."

"Mm. I'm also eager to meet that fellow Daoist who has been to the Southern Frontier."

Luo Chen's expression was calm, not at all angered by what had just happened.

He merely looked down at his son.

"Were you scared just now?"

The fear in Luo Lingxi's eyes had not yet faded, but under his father's gaze, he instinctively puffed out his chest.

"I wasn't scared!"

Then he added another sentence.

"With you here, I'm not afraid of anything!"

Luo Chen laughed heartily. "Being afraid isn't shameful. If you're scared, then be scared. But with your father here, you really don't need to fear these things."

Amidst laughter, the three figures gradually faded into the distance.

Their flying light was neither fast nor slow. As they passed, lofty mountains, great marshes, islands, and land all came into view.

Amidst swirling clouds and mist, auspicious energy pervaded the air.

Pavilions and towers were hidden among them, and from time to time, cultivators could be seen flying back and forth on flying swords.

A magnificent scene—truly the sight of a Holy Land!

Not long after they left, above the empty shore of the Nether Abyss.

A flash of green light appeared, and then the figures of Lu Chun and Chu Yu materialized.

Lu Chun looked bewildered.

"That Mystic Turtle listens to no one except the three ancestors of the sect and Uncle Feng, who cares for it day and night. Even when I was a child and wanted to get close to it, I couldn't. Why was it so obedient just now?"

Chu Yu naturally couldn't give him an answer, but inwardly, she nodded in approval.

"The Alchemy Sect's bearing is truly extraordinary. So this is the person our ancestor had his eye on?"

---

In ancient times, there was a great turtle called the Mystic, with a lifespan of thirty thousand years!

Legend has it that during normal activity, it breathed only three times a day.

If it fell into deep slumber, it would breathe only once every three hundred years.

During those three hundred years, it would be as still as a mountain, like a dead thing, without any sign of life. When it finally took a breath after three hundred years, it would swallow up an entire region's heaven and earth—a truly astonishing sight.

"Your father obtained a crude technique in his early years called the *Turtle Spirit Shell Armor Art*. It can imitate this beast's breathing, regulate the leakage of essence and qi, and conceal one's own mana fluctuations."

"Although this technique is crude, I later cultivated a body-tempering method similar to it, and I also have a protective armor made from a Mystic Turtle's remains, which can emit a Mystic Turtle's aura."

"Lingxi, you may not know this, but creatures like the Mystic Turtle like to live in groups when alive and share the same burial ground after death. If a fellow creature emits its aura, they will respond to each other."

"Just now, that big fellow mistook me for a kindred spirit, and one with a purer bloodline and seniority at that, so it was willing to be commanded by me."

Perhaps because Luo Lingxi had called him "Father," Luo Chen was in an exceptionally good mood tonight.

Before the boy went to sleep, Luo Chen took the opportunity to explain the earlier events to him.

Luo Lingxi lay in bed, listening as if to a myth, his eyes shining brightly as he stared at Luo Chen with adoration.

"Father, can I learn that technique? Lingxi also wants a big turtle to carry me across the abyss and the sea."

"You, of course, can..." Luo Chen stopped mid-sentence.

He looked at the boy's thin, frail body and smiled gently.

"Of course you can!"

A crude technique—at his current level, he could easily modify it.

Whether or not Luo Lingxi could cultivate it in the future, if he could master a guiding method to regulate his body, it would ultimately be a good thing.

"Sleep now! Tomorrow morning, I'll teach you."

Luo Lingxi nodded excitedly. With anticipation in his heart, drowsiness surged up instantly.

Children are like that—one moment full of energy, the next fast asleep.

Especially after a lively day.

Luo Chen rose from the bedside. He didn't go far, only to the window, his gaze shifting from the unfamiliar scenery below the mountain to the bright moon in the sky.

He chuckled softly.

"The moon in this Holy Land doesn't seem any different, does it?"

---

The next morning.

On Welcoming Immortal Peak, the Heavenly Nether City Lord arrived.

As he descended from the air, he saw two figures—one old, one young—sitting cross-legged under a large pine tree. Their lips moved slightly, their breaths faint and elusive.

But one breathed long and deep, unceasing; the other's breath was scattered, impure and unrefined.

Seeming to sense his arrival, the elder rose quietly and walked toward him.

"City Lord, good morning!"

The Heavenly Nether City Lord smiled helplessly. "I told you long ago, now that you've entered the Nether Origin Sect, we are fellow sect members. Especially now that I'm no longer stationed at Heavenly Nether Pass, you can call me Senior Brother Ye."

Luo Chen smiled. "Senior Brother Ye."

Ye Tianming looked curiously at the young boy still breathing and exhaling. "Your son shouldn't be twelve yet, right? Isn't it too early to have him start cultivating?"

Luo Chen explained, "That's not a cultivation technique, just a breathing method. Strictly speaking, it's a qi-guiding technique I tailored for him to regulate his own breathing rhythm."

A qi-guiding technique?

Ye Tianming looked again with curiosity.

This time, he actually saw some mystery in it.

Luo Lingxi's breathing was more about internal self-regulation than interaction with the outside world.

Although he wasn't very proficient yet, if he kept at it for a year or two, he would achieve some results.

This method was very suitable for children under twelve who weren't yet ready to cultivate.

First regulate oneself, then interact with the external heaven and earth spiritual qi—when the time came to embark on the path of cultivation, the foundation would be extremely solid.

But Ye Tianming remembered that Luo Lingxi hadn't been like this before.

And if Luo Chen had such a technique, why hadn't he taught it to his son earlier?

Could it be that he had only created it in the past two days?

He asked tentatively, "This technique is quite wonderful. If popularized, it could lay the foundation for the sect's young seedlings. Did you develop it yourself?"

Luo Chen waved his hand. "It's just a crude technique, nothing exquisite, and not worth popularizing."

Then he changed the subject and asked about the purpose of the visit.

Ye Tianming didn't dwell on the trivial matter and stated his reasons.

"Two things!"

"The Alchemy Saint Hall's inheritance trial was opened early by the ancestor before you arrived. You can go observe and attempt it at any time. By the time the formal trial day arrives, you'll be well prepared."

"Also, my junior brother who went to the Southern Frontier has emerged from seclusion. You can choose a day to visit him."

After speaking, he winked.

"These two matters have different priorities. I suggest you check out the Alchemy Saint Hall first."

Luo Chen gave his answer without hesitation.

"Senior Brother, please first introduce me to that junior brother."
