# Chapter 988: Southern Desert Shaken, Divine Power First Achieved

The Glazed Pure Land had no fixed abode, hidden within the vast cosmos, difficult to seek out.

But as the spirit treasure absorbed the spiritual mechanisms of the Western Desert, the entrance to the Pure Land became fixed, and the so-called legend turned into reality.

The sky was deep and dark, the moon clear and bright.

Over a hundred figures lingered around the entrance, their faces filled with curiosity and wariness.

A low-level cultivator couldn't resist approaching the glazed land shrouded in moonlight, but before he could even enter within a hundred zhang, he was pushed back by a tremendous force.

That force was extremely grand and upright, yet quite gentle, containing no malice whatsoever.

Someone nearby laughed. "Liu San, don't even think about it. At your Initiate realm, you can't even cross the threshold."

Liu San's face flushed red. "Does the Glazed Pure Land require the Adept realm to enter?"

As he spoke, he subconsciously glanced at the few Adept realm cultivators nearby.

But they all averted their eyes from his gaze.

Clearly, even Adept realm cultivators weren't qualified!

Just then, a slow-moving light of escape light appeared in the distance, and a grand, ornate ceremonial procession gradually came into view.

The cultivators looked up and saw a large banner flying high.

【Liang】

"Someone from the Great Liang Kingdom has come!"

"Judging by the ceremonial specifications and that traveling palanquin, could it be that the visitor is Xiao Yan, the current ruler of Great Liang?"

"Very likely. If we're talking about which of the many courts in the Southern Desert most reveres Buddhism, Great Liang ranks in the top three. That ruler Xiao Yan once even cultivated in seclusion at Mount Wutai, and after returning, he built eight hundred Buddhist temples throughout the country."

"Unexpectedly, the appearance of the Glazed Pure Land has even attracted a nation's ruler."

"This is the Glazed Pure Land, after all. A small sacred land rumored to contain a true Buddha. Most of the Southern Desert courts believe in Buddhism. With this place appearing, who knows how many experts it will attract?"

As the outside world buzzed with discussion, the man inside the ornate palanquin remained unmoved.

Through the curtain, he gazed at the entrance to the Pure Land, a look of hesitation on his face.

"That demoness and Moluo opposed Mount Wutai and destroyed the physical body of Master Cihai. They are true backsliders from Buddhism. Why does the Glazed Pure Land take them in?"

"Should I enter?"

Because of this hesitation, the Great Liang ruler did not act rashly but waited quietly outside with his retinue.

As time passed, more and more cultivators gathered outside the Glazed Pure Land.

After all, the place where the entrance to the Glazed Pure Land appeared was itself near the Southern Desert courts.

And the Southern Desert courts were the most populous and vastest region in the Western Desert.

Within it were thirty-six kingdoms, large and small, most of which believed in Buddhism, and many cultivation families also had some connection to the Western Desert Buddhist Sect.

The Glazed Pure Land was a treasure of the first abbot of the Hanging Temple, rumored to contain a true Buddha.

But it had left the Hanging Temple several hundred years ago and had been wandering outside ever since.

The world said that whether one entered the Glazed Pure Land or obtained this treasure, they would have the opportunity to escape the sea of suffering and become that true Buddha.

Now that it had appeared, who could remain unmoved?

The appearance of Xiao Yan of Great Liang was just a signal.

As time slowly passed, the number of Southern Desert experts gathered outside had exceeded everyone's expectations.

A team emitting a strange fragrance and playing music arrived in a grand procession—that was the Incense Kingdom.

Its ruler, Tanba, was a genuine Sage realm expert.

Several hundred monks, ugly in appearance and simply dressed, came walking step by step from afar in ascetic practice—that was the Ascetic Kingdom, which used the bitter cultivation of the physical body as its practice method.

These several hundred monks were all of the Tuluo crowd.

A group descended from the sky riding a golden divine bird. The leader was extraordinary in appearance, like a heavenly god.

As soon as he arrived, bursts of exclamation arose.

"It's the Brahma Heavenly Son!"

"The strongest in the Southern Desert, the number one under the Hanging Temple. The Brahma Heavenly Son, who once declined the position of head of the Prajna Hall of the Hanging Temple, has actually come too!"

The appearance of the Brahma Heavenly Son completely shook the entire scene.

Even Xiao Yan of Great Liang, Tanba of Incense, and the leader of the Tuluo crowd, who had been sitting firmly before, all appeared one by one to greet him.

"In my opinion, there's no need to wait any longer."

The Brahma Heavenly Son's face was handsome, like a heavenly god, and his words seemed to contain Buddhist tones.

His gaze fell on the entrance, his eyes full of curiosity.

"I wonder, where has Fellow Buddhist Xuan Du reached in the five hundred years since he left the Hanging Temple?"

As he spoke, he personally tested the barrier.

That previous force pushed against him again.

The Brahma Heavenly Son's lips curled slightly, but his forward steps never stopped.

A hundred zhang, fifty zhang, ten zhang...

Just as he was about to step into the entrance, Master Xuan Du's gentle voice came from within.

"Fellow Buddhist, once you enter this place, you will face the Buddhist Tribulation. Have you thought it through?"

The Brahma Heavenly Son frowned.

"What is the Buddhist Tribulation?"

There was no further explanation from within.

But then, the Brahma Heavenly Son remembered what the Great Liang ruler Xiao Yan had said—that the Glazed Pure Land had taken in two demons.

Now that the entrance to the Pure Land was wide open, as time passed, eminent Buddhist monks would surely come to subdue the demons and vanquish the evil.

A great battle was inevitable.

Judging by this posture, it seemed that the master of the Glazed Pure Land, Master Xuan Du, intended to protect those two?

The Brahma Heavenly Son didn't care. With his abilities, he had shaken the Southern Desert for centuries. What scene hadn't he seen?

Surviving tribulations without falling was just a common occurrence!

With a slight smile, he immediately stepped into the Glazed Pure Land.

Behind him, Xiao Yan, Tanba, and the others could no longer hold back and followed one by one.

But the moment they entered, everyone was stunned.

In the Buddhist temple shrouded in endless moonlight, a white-robed monk was slowly floating in the air. As his monk's robes fluttered, his body absorbed streams of vast spiritual energy.

Not only that!

In the void, it seemed as if a passage was opening, and a glazed soul rose and fell in the emptiness.

The Brahma Heavenly Son blurted out, "Spiritual mechanism entering the body, primordial spirit escaping into the void—this is a sign of breaking through to the Ascendant realm!"

For a moment, everyone looked at each other in shock.

...

In the central region of the Western Desert.

Mountains surrounded the area, coiling like jade dragons.

The grass was all green, a scene full of vitality.

And atop those lofty mountains was a place surrounded by endless fierce winds, blocking all external prying.

That was the Green Mist Screen!

A perilous place that had protected the Hanging Temple for thousands of years.

For thousands of years, the Green Mist Screen had blocked all external dangers, never having been broken from the outside.

Even a few years ago, when the demon army led by the Ancient Red Sand Scorpion marched all the way from the extreme west, it was ultimately blocked outside the Green Mist Screen.

The Ancient Red Sand Scorpion himself was slapped from afar by the master of the Hanging Temple, Arhat Mahakala, severely injured, and fled back to the extreme west in disgrace.

This demonic chaos that had plagued the region for a hundred years was finally ended by the personal intervention of the master of the Western Desert's sacred land.

The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, which had long coveted the Western Desert, also returned empty-handed, completely abandoning their plan to enter the Western Desert.

It was rumored that when the Ancient Red Sand Scorpion retreated, he once roared, "King Golden Peng has misled me!"

Thus, some speculated that if the Ancient Red Sand Scorpion had joined forces with the Golden-Winged Great Peng, they might have had a chance to break the Green Mist Screen.

But during that battle, the Golden-Winged Great Peng indeed did not appear.

The Green Mist Screen still maintained its reputation as one of the world's most perilous places.

Especially since its two equally famous perilous places, the Arctic Yemo Heaven and the Nether Abyss, had both been breached, the reputation of the Green Mist Screen as a world-class perilous place had increased by three points.

Therefore, the saying that as long as the Green Mist Screen was not broken, the Hanging Temple would forever guard the Western Desert became even more deeply ingrained.

And at this moment.

The Green Mist Screen, with its fierce winds howling, suddenly weakened considerably.

Figures walked out from behind the screen, each exuding a rich Buddhist power—a symbol of strength!

After appearing, they did not leave directly but waited for something.

They didn't wait long before a low voice came from behind the Green Mist Screen.

"The backslider from Buddhism wishes to attain the Arhat fruit in the Southern Desert. This seat is not convenient to act. All monks, capture him."

Thirty-six figures chanted Buddhist titles in unison and thundered their assent.

The next moment, someone behind the screen performed a world-shaking divine power, manipulating the wind and clouds to form a rainbow bridge that enveloped the monks.

"Go!"

"Remember, bring back the Glazed Pure Land!"

With a push of a large hand, the thirty-six monks entered the rainbow bridge and flew south at an unimaginable speed.

...

Whoosh! Whoosh!

The wind howled fiercely, and snow and ice covered the ground.

A figure sat cross-legged amidst it all, as still as a rock, unmoving as a mountain.

But upon closer inspection, one could see the strange tremors of the physical body beneath the snow and ice.

The skin was blue-black, covered in pustules, and a continuous "jiu jiu" sound came from the mouth.

Suddenly!

This miserable physical body began to shatter and split.

Like a lotus blooming, the entire physical body split open into petal after petal of bloody flowers.

And in this process, a vast amount of heaven and earth spiritual energy surged into the man's body from the outside, maintaining it in a half-shattered state.

"It's somewhat similar to when I underwent physical nirvana back then?"

Luo Chen thought this as he refined the massive influx of spiritual energy into mana.

This spiritual energy was not only vast in quantity but also extremely pure, containing almost no impurities.

He only needed to circulate it once or twice before it was marked with his imprint and became his mana.

The withered Sage in his Purple Mansion devoured the newly converted mana in large gulps, its state climbing day by day.

From initial surprise to later familiarity, Luo Chen had already understood the origin of this pure spiritual energy.

It was a gift from Master Xuan Du!

The other party was at the stage of breaking through to the Ascendant realm, with his primordial spirit diving into the ocean of origin qi, able to mobilize vast amounts of heaven and earth origin qi.

Sharing a little with Luo Chen was not a difficult matter.

But whether this would affect the other party's breakthrough was something Luo Chen didn't know.

"The time is ripe. It's time to cultivate that divine power!"

Luo Chen glanced at the panel.

The proficiency level of *Three Heads and Six Arms* had, over these few months, crossed into the [Familiar] level, just one step away from [Proficient].

This kind of progress probably surprised even Master Xuan Du himself.

But if one really looked into it, it was nothing more than the old saying of "accumulating thickly and thinly spreading."

Luo Chen's physical foundation was simply too strong. Even without cultivating this divine power, he was still one of the top body cultivators in the world.

Especially since he possessed the talent of Flesh and Blood Rebirth, he frantically pushed the evolution of the *Three Heads and Six Arms* divine power through the constant process of physical rupture and reorganization.

In the end, this divine power focused on the path of transformation.

And when it came to transformation, whether it was beast-like transformation or giant spirit transformation, Luo Chen had cultivated them to perfection.

Using this to switch to cultivating *Three Heads and Six Arms* was only natural.

Next, he would take the final step!

"Heh..."

With a low shout, Luo Chen activated all the source power in his body, and his physical body began to swell.

As the body expanded, the pustules and cracks also grew larger.

Especially on the shoulders, four large tumors emerged, and on both sides of the neck, two slightly smaller tumors appeared.

"Out!"

With a thought from Luo Chen, a large amount of source blood impacted the six tumors.

But as if there were some obstruction, the tumors only swelled but never split open.

"Still lacking something?"

Luo Chen calmed himself, changed his approach, and began to sort out the various forces within him.

He moved all his source power to the small tumor on his left shoulder.

"Break!"

With a deep shout, that small tumor suddenly split open, and then grew at an unimaginable speed into a face identical to Luo Chen's, except that the skin was not as fair as the main body's, but rather extremely ruddy.

The Red Head appeared!

Luo Chen was still unsatisfied. He began to mobilize the mixed poison power, the pure baleful qi and pill qi, and the demonic qi that had somehow formed, gathering them together to impact another tumor.

But this impact became somewhat difficult and obstructed.

The process of breaking through was extremely long.

It felt like a long time had passed, or perhaps just a few breaths.

Accompanied by Luo Chen's suppressed, beast-like low roar that reached its peak.

"Break!"

Pfft!

The tumor on the right side also split open from within.

The features began to transform, the flesh and blood began to reorganize.

In the blink of an eye, another head appeared.

But this time, it was not the same face.

A blue-black face, sharp fangs, ugly enough to be shocking.

It was exactly the image of the Shura Yaksha from before!

After doing all this, Luo Chen couldn't help but breathe a sigh of relief.

White face, red head, Yaksha face.

These were his three heads!

Each head represented the power he controlled.

Mana, source power, and various mixed forces.

The next thing would be relatively simpler.

Luo Chen pursed his lips, and the main head's soul trembled as he immediately split off six divided souls, which entered the Red Head and the Yaksha face.

Three heads, three kinds of the same thought, were being driven simultaneously.

Pfft!

Pfft!

Pfft!

Pfft!

On the shoulders, front, back, left, and right, thick limbs grew.

Four arms burst out of the flesh.

Six eyes rotated in unison, constantly scanning the body, as if feeling something was not quite right.

With a thought, the physical body began to swell, adjusting the body bit by bit.

A few days later.

In the bitterly cold Eight Cold Little Hell, a figure with three heads and six arms sat cross-legged on the icy ground.

Reflected in the solid ice was the eerie feeling of that figure.

It was completely different from the three heads and six arms cultivated by normal Buddhist Vajras!

In a normal three heads and six arms, the extra two hands on the same side were only at the waist and ribs.

But Luo Chen's three heads and six arms presented a state of three faces sharing one body.

The three heads looked in three directions respectively.

The six hands were each on three sides.

If viewed from a single side, it was almost no different from a normal person.

But if someone looked from a distance, it was like three people standing back to back, all mixed together.

If one had to describe it, only four words would suffice.

Frightening!

"Is this what the divine power looks like when first achieved?"

Three voices came from three mouths simultaneously, with a faint rumbling echo.

Luo Chen slowly stood up from the ice, feeling indescribably wonderful, his whole body full of comfort.

Only in his chest and abdomen was there a slight stifling breath.

He couldn't help but open all three mouths at the same time and let out a long roar.

"Roar!"

The vast roar spread far and wide, and wherever it passed, icebergs collapsed, revealing the original appearance of the tall white pagoda.

The solid ice melted, and rolling spiritual energy poured out in all directions.

Freedom, exhilaration, and a body full of power!

(End of chapter)
