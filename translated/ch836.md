# Chapter 836: Black King Returns, Ten Thousand Li Pursuit

A Sage was not so easily slain!

This principle was well known to all.

Because once a cultivator reached the Sage realm, even if their physical body was destroyed, their divine soul could still use their Nascent Soul to teleport a thousand li in an instant.

As long as the Nascent Soul escaped, even without the protection of the Purple Mansion, there was still some chance of rebuilding the Dao.

Seizing a body, reconstructing a physical form, converting to the path of ghost cultivation, and various other wondrous methods.

The reason Luo Chen had been able to claim the lives of so many Sage-level experts along his journey was because he possessed the Primordial Unity Cauldron—a top-grade true artifact capable of sealing space and capturing a cultivator's golden core or Nascent Soul.

Others did not have such means.

Even if the gap in strength was enormous, one could at most kill, but it was very difficult to capture a Sage alive.

And once a Nascent Soul shattered, the vast amount of mana that constituted the Nascent Soul body would sweep across the area, forming a chaotic and terrifying spiritual energy storm.

Back when the eight Demon Emperors had attacked the pass, the few Demon Emperors who fell had stirred up powerful spiritual energy storms that swept through Lingtian Pass. It was only because Lingtian Pass was equipped with a large-scale formation that absorbed these spiritual energy storms; otherwise, countless buildings and living beings would have been destroyed within them.

When Luo Chen killed Feiyunzi in the Demon-Fall Land, a similar scene had occurred. If he hadn't possessed an Archaic fourth-stage physical body back then, he probably wouldn't have been able to withstand the ravages of the spiritual energy storm either.

This was the scene of a shattered Nascent Soul during battle.

As for Sages who died naturally of old age and sat in meditation, although they would not create a devastating spiritual energy storm, their mana would slowly dissipate, transforming into pure spiritual energy that returned to heaven and earth.

Although Luo Chen had never seen such a scene, he had learned a great deal about it on Heaven-Earth Peak.

The reason the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect in Central Continent was so powerful was because they had absorbed many elderly Sage cultivators from other continents. After these cultivators passed away, Central Continent could absorb the vast amount of spiritual energy condensed by these elderly Sages throughout their lives.

Accumulated bit by bit, the concentration of spiritual energy in Central Continent far surpassed that of the other four continents, creating the flourishing scene where talents emerged in abundance from Central Continent.

But now!

Luo Chen's divine sense spread out, covering an area of several thousand li.

Nothing!

There was absolutely no residual mana left behind from the Second Palace Master's shattered Nascent Soul!

Moreover, she had fallen here, and the battlefield was also on the Fenghua Palace side. It was impossible for her to abandon thousands of Fenghua Palace disciples and escape alone with her Nascent Soul.

So, where had the Second Palace Master's Nascent Soul gone?

For a moment, an uncomfortable feeling rose in Luo Chen's heart, and he subconsciously gripped his right arm.

Golden Plume Sage also looked puzzled. "Could it be that the Second Palace Master isn't dead yet?"

Fenghua Fairy said hatefully, "My junior sister is definitely dead; my perception is not wrong. As for where her Nascent Soul went, that must be asked of those beasts!"

Carefully placing the Second Palace Master's corpse on the ground, she stood up and said softly, "Shunxi, Xialan, Fengyue, come in!"

Three figures walked in from amidst a pile of ruins.

Pale-faced Fengyue could barely walk, while another woman had to be supported by someone else to enter.

"Senior Uncle, you must avenge my master!"

"Xialan, what are you saying? Have you gone mad?"

"Master!"

Clearly, Shunxi and Xialan were both disciples of the Second Palace Master, so they addressed Fenghua Fairy as Senior Uncle.

Hearing their mournful voices, like children seeking help from an adult, Fenghua Fairy couldn't help but clench her fists tightly.

"Xialan, you are responsible for collecting your master's remains and preparing the appropriate funeral arrangements."

"Shunxi, you are in better condition. Go gather our sect's disciples and calm their emotions. Tell them that I am still here, and Fenghua Palace will not fall!"

"Fengyue, you tell your master, what exactly happened back then? How many enemies were there, and who killed the Second Palace Master?"

Fengyue pursed her lips and, in the presence of the three Sages, quickly recounted what had happened earlier.

The content was actually very brief, because in the eyes of these Core Formation cultivators, the battle had only occurred in an instant.

"Junior Uncle sent a message that a massive, unidentified demonic aura was heading straight for Fenghua Palace, and ordered us to activate the mountain-protecting formation."

"Junior Uncle went alone to meet the enemy."

"When we hurriedly activated the grand formation, Junior Uncle fell from the sky, her chest and abdomen pierced through, with no sign of life left. The killer was unknown."

"The mountain-protecting formation was broken in an instant. It was three demons with similar appearances and identical clothing who joined forces to attack."

"Our sect's grand formation connected the nine palaces of Fenghua and seventeen earth veins. Once the formation was broken, the earth veins shook, mountains collapsed, and countless low-level disciples died instantly. Among the main formation controllers, including myself, there were seven Core Formation cultivators. Four sisters exploded and died. Senior Sister Xialan's qi sea was shattered. Senior Sister Shunxi and I suffered mana backlash, our meridians were severely damaged, and we had no more strength to resist."

"However, after destroying Fenghua Palace, those Demon Emperors did not take the opportunity to strike. Instead, under the orders of a leading female demon, they quickly fled to an unknown location."

After speaking in one breath, Fengyue panted slightly. Clearly, the rampaging mana had severely damaged her meridians.

She swallowed her saliva and pleaded with fear in her eyes, "Master, there are seven invading enemies in total. You are definitely no match for them. Please don't go!"

A fierce glint flashed in Fenghua Fairy's eyes.

Not go?

How could she possibly not go!

Not to mention that this was a mission issued by Lingtian Pass, just the death of her own junior sister alone made it impossible for her to stand idly by.

"It's Splendid Immortal," Luo Chen said softly.

Fenghua Fairy and Golden Plume Sage looked at Luo Chen. His eyes narrowed slightly, and a look of wariness appeared on his face.

"According to Fengyue's account, the invading enemies consisted of seven Demon Emperors, led by a female demon. Among the three commanders attacking the rear, Bi Zhen and Juan Kuang were both male demons; only Splendid Immortal was female."

"Not only that, she was also one of the only two female demons among the nine commanders!"

"To be ranked alongside great Demon Emperors like Seven Rhino and Yellow Brute, her strength cannot be underestimated!"

It was far more than just "cannot be underestimated." Such an existence could only be matched by a Grand Cultivator; ordinary Sages were no match at all.

Moreover, based on Fengyue's account, this demon was extremely purposeful. After killing the Second Palace Master and destroying Fenghua Palace, she did not engage in mass slaughter but immediately rushed to the next domain.

Clearly, this enemy was not one to linger in battle; she only sought to destroy the stable rear.

To deal with such an existence?

Difficult!

Golden Plume snorted coldly, "The three of us joining forces can even fight a Grand Cultivator face to face. What is there to fear?"

Luo Chen shook his head. "I'm not afraid; I'm worried."

"Worried about what?" Fenghua Fairy's delicate face now showed a hint of ferocity. "She killed my junior sister and destroyed my Fenghua Palace. I will make them pay!"

Luo Chen sighed. "But our enemy is not just Splendid Immortal alone."

"And their enemy is not just us alone!" Fenghua Fairy said in a low voice.

Then, without further ado, after instructing Fengyue and the other disciples, she soared into the sky on her flying light, heading for Dieyun Domain, which bordered Fenghua Domain.

Golden Plume Sage glanced at Luo Chen and said confidently, "Dieyun Sect is not weak. The three Sages, Flowing Cloud, Rosy Cloud, and Colorful Cloud, all have considerable skill. Relying on their geographical advantage, they might be able to delay them for a while. If we move quickly, we still have a chance to intercept them."

After speaking, he also flew out of the ruined Fenghua Palace.

Luo Chen looked around, feeling a sense of melancholy.

He met Fengyue's timid, pleading gaze.

"Sage Luo, my master is now somewhat swayed by emotions and may make mistakes. I beg you, in this critical moment, to look after her a little."

"I'll see what I can do."

Luo Chen made no promises, merely giving a perfunctory reply before mustering his mana and quickly catching up with the other two.

Seeing Luo Chen follow, Fenghua Fairy and Golden Plume Sage said nothing, but they were clearly relieved.

Luo Chen's glorious battle achievements over the years were enough to prove his formidable strength.

If they lacked his support, the two of them truly wouldn't have the confidence to face Splendid Immortal.

But just as he caught up, Luo Chen made a request.

"Detour for a moment! I need to go to Bai Li Qingchuan!"

Fenghua Fairy frowned. "Are you worried about Luo Tian Sect?"

Golden Plume Sage waved his hand. "There's no need. Clearly, this group of Demon Emperors only targets Sage-level sects. Your Luo Tian Sect's remaining cultivators are even fewer than those of my Five Elements Divine Sect. They won't bother with them."

"I need to go!"

Luo Chen insisted, and the other two couldn't forcefully refuse, so they had to go with him.

But obviously, they all increased their speed considerably.

And during this flight, they were both astonished that Luo Chen could keep up with them so effortlessly.

After all, one of them specialized in wind techniques, the other in metal techniques—both were among the best in their realm in terms of speed. Yet, under full-speed flight, Luo Chen, a third-layer Sage, could still keep up, and even seemed to have plenty of strength left.

How could they not be shocked?

...

Bai Li Qingchuan was not far from Fenghua Palace's mountain gate.

After all, this place was originally one of Fenghua Palace's subsidiary industries, later handed over to Luo Tian Sect for management.

With the three's speed, they arrived in no time.

When they arrived, the vast plain was devoid of any human figures.

But within Daqing Mountain, brilliant light flickered and spiritual energy surged, as if they were facing a great enemy.

Clearly, the mountain-protecting formation had been pushed to its limit.

Observing the concentration of spiritual energy, Fenghua Fairy was momentarily stunned.

"How can it be this strong? It's almost approaching the spiritual energy concentration of Fenghua Palace's third palace. Bai Li Qingchuan shouldn't have such high-quality spirit veins!"

Luo Chen ignored the woman's stunned expression and flew straight to the sky above Daqing Mountain.

His familiar divine sense spread out, and he spoke in a clear voice.

"The Supreme Elder has returned. Why is there no welcome?"

Silence fell over Daqing Mountain, as if everyone inside was holding their breath. Only after Luo Chen's voice rang out did movement begin within.

A gap opened in the formation's light curtain, and Li Yingzhang and Min Longyu walked out together. When they saw Luo Chen, they couldn't help but breathe a sigh of relief.

"Supreme Elder!"

"You're finally back! Fenghua Palace over there..."

Luo Chen raised his hand, interrupting Min Longyu's words. "No need to say more. I already understand the situation. It's good that the sect is safe."

In the clouds, Fenghua and Golden Plume watched this scene with some impatience.

Just to check on Luo Tian Sect's situation, they had to waste time detouring. This alchemy sect was somewhat lacking in understanding of the bigger picture.

They knew that most of Luo Tian Sect's personnel were stationed at Lingtian Pass, with only about a hundred people left here. Even the mountain gate belonged to Fenghua Palace, so its destruction would be insignificant.

But the next moment, both of their eyes widened.

Without seeing Luo Chen make any move, Daqing Mountain began to shake violently.

Then, amidst a crowd of horrified gazes, a black dragon broke through the thick earth, roaring as it flew out from deep underground.

Arriving before Luo Chen, it wagged its head and tail, looking extremely docile.

Then it transformed into a streak of black light and disappeared into Luo Chen's left shoulder.

"You all continue to maintain the formation. Do not let anyone enter."

Luo Chen gave the order, then withdrew and returned to the other two.

"Let's go!"

Fenghua Fairy swallowed her saliva and asked hesitantly, "Alchemy Sage, what was that just now?"

"Just my spirit pet," Luo Chen said indifferently.

Seeing that he didn't want to explain, Fenghua Fairy didn't press further. Beside her, Golden Plume Sage's expression grew solemn, and he increasingly felt that Luo Chen was hiding his true depths.

In fact, Golden Plume Sage had already decided that after this battle, he would definitely tell Senior Brother Shenyuan about that black dragon.

...

Under the full efforts of the three, they finally left Fenghua Domain by the second day and set foot in the neighboring Dieyun Domain.

The master of Dieyun Domain was Dieyun Sect.

This was no obscure minor sect; it had a considerable reputation throughout the Eastern Wilderness.

The sect had over ten thousand core disciples, hundreds of thousands of outer disciples, and even three Sage-level experts!

Moreover, because the sect brewed top-grade spiritual wine, it had a very good relationship with the Nether Origin Holy Land.

The large domains bordering it were Qitong Domain and Fenghua Domain.

The former had declined severely in recent years, with many of its interests openly and secretly encroached upon by Dieyun Sect. Even some Core Formation great sects in Qitong Domain had become vassals of Dieyun Sect.

If not for Fenghua Palace having two Palace Masters and Fenghua Fairy's considerable strength, their fate might not have been any better than that of Qitong Mountain in Qitong Domain.

The three entered Dieyun Domain.

They soon sensed something unusual.

On the ground below, many low-level cultivators were rushing toward Dieyun Sect!

"Dieyun Sect must have already been attacked, which is why they sent out a distress signal to all their vassals," Fenghua Fairy said with certainty.

Golden Plume Sage replied confidently, "These small forces are still heading there, which means Dieyun Sect might not have fallen yet."

Fenghua Fairy nodded. "I have a fairly good personal relationship with Colorful Cloud Sage. I know their situation quite well. Their Supreme Elder, that old fellow Flowing Cloud Sage, has changed his lazy ways in recent years and worked hard on cultivation, breaking through to mid-Sage. If they rely on their geographical advantage and join forces with three Sages, these Demon Emperors might not be able to break through quickly."

Luo Chen said nothing. He didn't know Flowing Cloud or Colorful Cloud Sages.

But he had met Rosy Cloud Sage, at the Shouyang Dao Discussion.

Back then, the other party had bought the heart of a Lightless Beast from him, saying it was for brewing some kind of spiritual wine.

Because the material was scarce, the other party had taken the time to return to Dieyun Sect after obtaining it.

That was also why Rosy Cloud Sage wasn't among those trapped at Lingtian Pass.

Based on his observations at the time, Rosy Cloud Sage was at the second layer of the Sage realm—neither high nor low—but it was hard to judge his actual combat skills.

Seven Demon Emperors joining forces, including a commander-level existence, and especially with a clear purpose in mind.

Could just three Sages hold them off?

Luo Chen wasn't optimistic.

Dieyun Domain was much larger than Fenghua Domain, which was related to Dieyun Domain's encroachment on Qitong Mountain's territory over the years.

And Dieyun Sect's mountain gate was established at the center of the domain.

The three flew at full speed without any rest, spending an entire day and night before finally arriving outside Dieyun Sect.

But what met their eyes was a familiar scene that told them they were too late.

Once again, mountains and rivers were shattered, and devastation was everywhere!

There was even a huge pit, shockingly lying at the foot of Dieyun Sect's mountain.

This scene left Fenghua Fairy frozen in place, and made Golden Plume Sage's earlier confidence waver.

"Let's go down and take a look first!"

Luo Chen said this, his mana light shield protecting his entire body as he crossed the pit and slowly descended to the ground.

Some surviving low-level cultivators recognized his identity as a Sage through the domain pressure he emitted, but Demon Emperors could also take human form, so no one dared approach after Luo Chen appeared.

It was only when Fenghua Fairy appeared that a Core Formation cultivator who recognized her face cried out in surprise and ran over.

Fenghua Fairy composed herself and began asking about the general situation.

Luo Chen and Golden Plume Sage exchanged glances, then separated to carefully observe the remnants of Dieyun Sect.

During this process, black light flowed from Luo Chen's left shoulder, sinking into the ground like soft water.

About the time it takes to burn an incense stick later.

The three gathered again to share the intelligence they had collected.

Luo Chen spoke first.

"There are also capable individuals among the Demon Emperors! Dieyun Sect's mountain-protecting formation, unlike Fenghua Palace's which purely relied on earth veins, was also connected to the clouds above."

Luo Chen pointed to the sky and spoke eloquently.

"The foundation of formation dao lies in the seven major prohibitions. Although Dieyun Sect is not famous for formations, they are not inferior to any major formation sect in terms of cloud-pattern prohibitions. The grand formation built with this as its core, once activated, is unpredictable. If combined with wind prohibitions and obscuring restrictions, even a Sage would find it difficult to locate the formation eye in a short time."

"Under such circumstances, to break it by brute force would require a disparity of at least ten or even a hundred times."

"And based on my observations, Dieyun Sect's grand formation was not broken by brute force, but with considerable skill. At the broken formation base, there were only two traces of mana aura. One was mixed, the other pure to the point of being terrifying. The former was the guide, the latter was the main force!"

This explanation left the other two confused.

Fenghua Fairy even looked at Luo Chen with suspicion. "Dieyun Sect once sought advice from my sect on wind prohibitions. To ease relations, I did indeed exchange some knowledge on this subject with them. But how can you see through it so clearly, Fellow Daoist? Could it be that you are also proficient in formation dao?"

Luo Chen avoided the question and instead explained carefully, "My point is simple. Among those seven demons, there is someone skilled in breaking formations. It was because he found the formation eye that another top-tier expert could break the formation with a single strike!"

With this explanation, the two immediately understood.

Golden Plume Sage said solemnly, "The one who broke the formation with great mana must be Splendid Immortal, the Great Commander!"

Through this information, a very frightening reality was laid before the three.

Human sects, able to resist enemies several times their number, often relied on the geographical advantage provided by formations.

Especially since demons were not skilled in formations, which was why there had been the proud record of many forces holding out for years or even over a decade over the past century.

But now, based on Luo Chen's observations, among this group of Demon Emperors, there was someone skilled in breaking formations.

Then their speed in destroying Eastern Wilderness sects would undoubtedly be much faster.

Could they catch up?

Could other sects react in time?

Could the stronger forces further back, even the Nether Origin Sect's reinforcements, arrive in time to rescue them?

Luo Chen sighed. "I'm not optimistic about Qitong Mountain, and even less so about the leaderless Tianji Zen Sect. Perhaps only Tiandu Sect can truly stop them."

"So, the place where we will fight them is at Tiandu Sect?" Golden Plume asked.

"Very likely!" Luo Chen nodded.

For a moment, the three fell silent.

Too fast. This group of Demon Emperors moved too fast. It could be described as swift as the wind and aggressive as fire.

In this silence, Fenghua Fairy suddenly spoke. "I asked about the situation of Dieyun Sect's three Sages. The strongest, Flowing Cloud Sage, left no corpse behind. His Nascent Soul self-destructed. The large pit we saw earlier was created by his self-destruction. This old fellow's temper is still so fierce, just like in the old days."

After a moment of emotion, the woman's words did not stop but grew heavier.

"I saw the corpses of Rosy Cloud Sage and Colorful Cloud Sage. Their chests and abdomens had holes, the same as my junior sister. Their Nascent Souls were nowhere to be found."

At these words, even the most obtuse person would realize something was wrong.

Especially Luo Chen, whose previously calm expression had now changed drastically.

In his mind, the palpitation he had felt when he once spied on the Spirit Severance Domain instantly resurfaced!

(End of this chapter)
