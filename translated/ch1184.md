# Chapter 1184: Raging Current Annihilation, Chaotic Thunder Pool

A thunderous palm strike descended from the heavens, its momentum vast and overwhelming.

The white-robed man was unhurried. From below, he swept his sleeve upward, overturning the sky.

*Boom!*

The two attacks clashed, and qi surged in all directions, shaking the surrounding wilderness.

Only then did the thunder god, bearing wings on his back, stand opposite Luo Chen.

The two were separated by no more than a hundred zhang. With a single glance exchanged, without further words, they met with ultimate techniques.

But this time, it was Luo Chen who struck first.

He curled his fingers and tapped out repeatedly, sending streaks of Blood God Tribulation Finger shooting forth, interwoven with other finger techniques.

In an instant, the midair was filled with crimson light.

Faced with such an attack, the blue-faced man revealed a contemptuous expression.

He clenched one hand, and electric currents shot out in all directions.

With exquisite precision, he intercepted each finger strike.

Watching this, Luo Chen was not surprised.

The Blood God Tribulation Finger was straightforward and direct, easily dodged or intercepted.

But if this was all he had, then he would be underestimating this man.

The next moment.

Amidst the countless annihilating blood light, more than a dozen other streaks of qi suddenly separated, their trajectories erratic.

It was Immortal Returning to Penglai!

Luo Chen had long since cultivated all the martial techniques from the *Divine Astral Combat Qi* to Great Perfection, allowing him to unleash them with effortless variation.

The sudden change startled the blue-faced man, but he merely opened his clenched hand, forming a claw that grasped at the empty air.

Instantly, bolts of lightning, like wild beasts baring their fangs, raced through the air at incredible speed.

Any astral qi that approached him was devoured and worn away one by one.

Seeing this, Luo Chen's eyes lit up.

"Excellent technique!"

"If even a mere Lightning Fang counts as an excellent technique, then I'm afraid you won't be able to withstand what I have next!" The blue-faced man sneered coldly, his body blazing with thunderous light.

The next moment.

Overbearing astral qi flowed from his body, transforming into violent electric currents that surged outward.

For several li in every direction, the sky and the wilderness were instantly engulfed in a thunder domain!

Luo Chen, standing within it, clearly sensed a powerful martial divine will pressing down on him from the pervasive thunderous astral qi.

He was naturally unafraid, instinctively releasing his own Blood-Refined Divine Astral Qi to carve out a foothold.

Looking around, Luo Chen seemed thoughtful.

"It resembles a domain technique for dividing the battlefield. I take it you have a finishing move prepared?"

For the first time, the blue-faced man showed a hint of appreciation for Luo Chen.

"Good eyesight. After the Wild Thunder Field comes the true killing blow. Let's see how you handle this move of mine—Raging Thunder, Surging Waves!"

The man roared low, and with both hands, he suddenly pushed forward.

In an instant, the surrounding thunderlight rose and fell, layer upon layer, converging into wave after wave of thunder, pressing down on Luo Chen with incomparable ferocity.

Behind him, the blue-faced man's body flickered with thunder, unleashing even more thunderous astral qi to join the storm-like thunder waves.

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow and suddenly reached out his right hand.

Blood-Refined Divine Astral Qi surged, transforming into massive blood-colored dragons.

It was the Great Luo Five Dragon Hand!

With the great hand's appearance, the five dragons roared in unison.

"Go!"

The five blood dragons stretched their forms and charged into the waves with unstoppable momentum.

The same technique, when executed at the Leakless Realm versus the Human Immortal Realm, had vastly different power.

Especially since it contained the divine will of Selflessness, which perfectly complemented the exquisite changes of the Great Luo Five Dragon Hand.

Facing the relentless battering of thunder waves, the five blood dragons not only held their ground but grew fiercer with each passing moment.

The blue-faced man's expression shifted slightly. He sensed a strange change within them.

His own power was too dispersed. Though the attack was comprehensive, it posed no real threat to his opponent.

Occasional thunderbolts that struck Luo Chen merely left a scorch mark on his white robe, which vanished in an instant.

Instead, his opponent's five blood dragons grew stronger as they fought.

In terms of technique finesse, he was outmatched!

Then let it be a contest of raw power!

"Condense!"

The blue-faced man intoned solemnly.

The next moment, amidst the violent thunder tide, an electric thunder dragon instantly coalesced, roaring as it charged toward the five blood dragons.

For a time, the thunder dragon and blood dragons vied for supremacy, neither gaining the upper hand.

From finesse to raw power, the battle had finally evolved into a pure contest of astral qi between the two sides.

The more intense the stalemate, the more shocked the blue-faced man became.

His thunderous astral qi was supremely yang and rigid, with few rivals at the same level. It could even affect an opponent's martial will in fierce combat.

Yet the white-robed man's martial will remained unmoved.

Even in a pure contest of astral qi, his thunderous astral qi was gradually losing ground.

It was even being worn away and devoured.

"What kind of astral qi is this? What materials have been fused into it?"

Amidst his shock, the blue-faced man inadvertently glanced at Luo Chen.

He found the other's expression still calm, as if he still had strength to spare.

A trace of wariness surfaced.

"No good. I need to unleash another ultimate technique!"

The blue-faced man hardened his heart. He no longer maintained the Raging Thunder, Surging Waves technique and retreated.

The moment he withdrew, the thunder dragon lost its support, letting out a mournful cry as the five blood dragons quickly entangled and strangled it.

Luo Chen waved his hand, and the five blood dragons immediately withdrew. He did not press the advantage.

He looked from afar at the man retreating with flapping wings, a rare appreciation in his eyes.

This man, whether in astral qi strength or combat technique, was among the best Luo Chen had seen among the martial artists in Guixu.

If it were anyone else, they would not have been able to withstand that previous attack.

So, what technique would come next?

Luo Chen walked forward leisurely.

The blue-faced man, meanwhile, kept retreating.

When he was a hundred li from the Azure Dome Pagoda Forest, his figure suddenly halted.

"Sir, do you truly intend to force your way into my Azure Dome Pagoda Forest?"

Luo Chen paused, then smiled. "I only wanted to pass by and take a look. Since you say I'm forcing my way in, then I suppose I am! And it just so happens that I'd like to see the legendary Chaotic Thunder Pool!"

He had long since passed the stage of needing to justify or defend himself.

The blue-faced man's expression hardened. "Then you will regret this decision!"

As he spoke, his figure rose abruptly, standing high in the sky.

The thunder wings behind him flapped, and dark clouds gathered in the heavens.

Not only that!

Within the three-thousand-pagoda forest, each tower seemed to respond, emitting bolts of lightning that converged toward the man.

In a few breaths, it took on the appearance of a world-destroying phenomenon.

From one extreme, he went to another.

"Raging Current Annihilation!"

As this technique first took shape, the blue-faced man showed a hint of pride.

This move, blessed by the time and place, and harmonized by his own presence, had its power increased a hundredfold.

At this moment, even if a Destruction Realm expert stood before him, he was confident he could repel them!

Without this trump card, how could he, Yuan Qingzang, with his late Human Immortal Realm strength, dominate this region?

This man was strong today, but he was also unlucky to have run into him.

However, when he looked down.

He saw the young man's robes rustling in the wind, his hands crossed back and forth in a grinding motion.

Two enormous millstones were slowly taking shape from his body.

As the giant millstones appeared, wind and clouds stirred.

The countless violent thunder currents he had gathered were involuntarily drawn toward them, then gradually worn away.

Not just the leaking thunder currents.

But also the astral qi he emitted, his body, and the three-thousand-pagoda forest behind him!

This was still the charging state before the technique was unleashed!

If he truly let his opponent release this move, even if his own Raging Current Annihilation could contend with it, the aftermath of the battle would likely affect hundreds of thousands of li in all directions.

At that time...

"Brother, stop, stop, stop!"

Luo Chen held his technique without releasing it, smiling lightly as he looked upward. "Why should I stop?"

Yuan Qingzang replied calmly, "Your strength has already earned my recognition. There's no need to continue fighting."

Luo Chen twitched the corner of his mouth. "But I don't care about your recognition!"

As he spoke, the two giant millstones spun even faster, and the range of their influence grew wider.

Yuan Qingzang was taken aback. Seeing the other's indifferent expression, he quickly said, "Don't be like that! I only wanted to test your skills. I've been holding back the whole time, with no intention of killing. Besides, didn't you want to visit the Azure Dome Pagoda Forest and the Chaotic Thunder Pool?"

"Oh..."

Luo Chen let out a soft sound, then casually flicked his hand.

The two slowly condensing giant millstones vanished instantly, as carefree as could be.

Seeing this, Yuan Qingzang hesitated instead.

His opponent had already withdrawn his charged killing move, while his Raging Current Annihilation still hung in the void. If he took advantage and struck down...

In his hesitation, he caught the faint, knowing smile on the other's face.

Yuan Qingzang gave a bitter smile and slowly withdrew his technique.

The surrounding electric currents returned along their original paths, sinking back into the three-thousand-pagoda forest.

The dark clouds gathering in the sky also dispersed quickly.

Under a clear, bright sky, the man descended and landed opposite Luo Chen, cupping his fists. "Yuan Qingzang, Lord of the Azure Dome Pagoda Forest. May I ask your name?"

"Luo Chen, of the Great Destruction Realm!"

"Under Lord Carefree Realm?" Yuan Qingzang was somewhat surprised.

Luo Chen asked back, "What of it?"

Yuan Qingzang shook his head. "Nothing, just a bit surprised. The Great Destruction Realm is quite close to my Azure Dome Pagoda Forest. I know most of the experts there, but I never expected there was a hidden master like you. I suppose it's only fitting that the Great Destruction Realm, one of the three largest human settlements in Guixu, truly produces one expert after another."

Luo Chen smiled but did not elaborate.

He was no hidden expert. It was simply that his cultivation speed was too fast, reaching the late Human Immortal Realm in just three hundred years.

Such a cultivation speed, no matter where it was mentioned, would be too shocking.

"Brother Luo, please, come inside!"

"After you!"

...

Two figures entered the Azure Dome Pagoda Forest from outside.

As soon as they entered, several figures gathered around, but before they could ask anything, Yuan Qingzang waved them away.

"Our Azure Dome Pagoda Forest is not a major force. With three thousand pagodas for protection, a population of less than a million, and only me as a Martial Dao Human Immortal. To prevent troublemakers, I tend to be a bit vigilant. Please forgive my earlier rude probing, Brother Luo."

"No harm done. I understand."

As Luo Chen listened to Yuan Qingzang's introduction, he looked around.

The towers, dark blue-black, were built from a rare lightning-absorbing stone, standing in a basin spanning ten thousand li.

They isolated the inside from the outside, yet reached straight into the sky.

Especially the lightning power that occasionally leaked from those towers, drawing attention.

It showed that the lightning power accumulated within was beyond imagination.

And this was actually one of the reasons Luo Chen had chosen to stop earlier.

Yuan Qingzang's technique, though not unleashed, was undoubtedly a shocking martial divine ability.

Luo Chen was naturally unafraid and dared to counter with *Heaven-Earth Inverse Mill*.

But he had no great confidence in the outcome.

Even if he killed Yuan Qingzang, he would likely suffer some injuries.

That would not be suitable for the upcoming journey.

Why would he want to be injured as soon as he left?

So, when Yuan Qingzang gave him an out, Luo Chen gladly took it.

They were both "cellmates" trapped in the same cage. Why fight and kill?

"Brother Luo, with such profound cultivation yet previously unknown, you must be someone who practices in seclusion. Now that you have taken the initiative to leave the Great Destruction Realm, could it be that what Lord Carefree Realm said is true?"

Luo Chen paused in his steps and looked at Yuan Qingzang, who wore an expression of seeking instruction.

After a moment of thought, he nodded slightly.

Then, he asked Yuan Qingzang in return, "Have you met Lord Carefree Realm?"

"A hundred years ago, I did." Yuan Qingzang nodded, then said with a melancholy air, "At that time, he passed by the Azure Dome Pagoda Forest and met with me. He even invited me to the Ancient God Dao Field."

"Why didn't you go?"

"Because the Ancient God Dao Field is too treacherous. Countless martial experts have died within it. How would I dare to take such a risk?"

"But the cycle of eras is approaching. The return of all things to nothingness is the greatest danger!"

"Yes, indeed! In fact, even if you hadn't come, I was planning to leave recently. I was going to go to the Great Destruction Realm first to try that legendary Door of Destruction. But now, seeing both you and Lord Carefree Realm leaving one after another, I suspect that path is also a dead end."

"Leave? Does the path out of the Azure Dome Pagoda Forest have no hope either?"

"We're here!"

Yuan Qingzang stopped.

Luo Chen stopped as well, then looked up at the towering pagoda before him.

It was the most majestic of the three thousand pagodas, covered in thunderlight. Ordinary martial artists could not even approach it; just one glance would make their hearts tremble.

Yuan Qingzang extended his hand in an inviting gesture. "Please, Fellow Daoist. Once you see it, you'll understand why this path is also impassable."

Luo Chen stepped inside.

As soon as he entered the pagoda, thunder roared.

*Rumble!*

*Rumble!*

Thunderous sounds echoed through the surroundings. Each one was no weaker than the roar Yuan Qingzang had let out earlier.

It even reminded Luo Chen of the Thunder Prison Roc's demon art—Thunder Roar—from back in the Mountains and Seas Realm.

But the thunder here was far, far stronger than the Thunder Prison Roc's Thunder Roar.

Even with Luo Chen's powerful martial divine will, he could not help but feel his mind tremble.

Especially within his sea of consciousness!

Luo Chen took a deep breath, his eyes focused, and his Blood-Refined Divine Astral Qi surged out, enveloping his entire body, isolating inside from outside.

Yuan Qingzang had not warned him earlier. Now, seeing Luo Chen's quick reaction, he could not help but praise, "Fellow Daoist is indeed formidable. To react so quickly on your first entry into the Azure Dome Pagoda. Unlike me, who made a great fool of myself back then."

"You flatter me." Luo Chen waved his hand, his gaze fixed on the heights above.

Yuan Qingzang sighed. "It seems you've noticed as well. The thunder within the pagoda is but a drop in the ocean compared to the extreme heavens. Standing here, we must be cautious. How could we possibly break through the omnipresent Soul-Shaking Thunder and leave Guixu?"

Luo Chen was silent for a moment.

Finally, he nodded reluctantly.

The effect of the Soul-Shaking Thunder on martial artists was far less than on him. The stronger the soul, the more constrained one was. Attempting to escape Guixu from this place was far more dangerous than traversing the Door of Destruction!

Dejected, he turned his gaze to the bottom of the pagoda, to a three-foot-square thunder pool.

"Is this the Chaotic Thunder Pool?"

Yuan Qingzang replied, "Yes. This is the lightning power accumulated over the years by the Azure Dome Pagoda filtering the Soul-Shaking Thunder. We martial artists here use it to temper our astral qi. Those who reach the Human Immortal Realm, like me, can even attempt to fuse it into their bodies, cultivating thunderous astral qi."

Luo Chen suddenly asked, "May I give it a try?"

"Try what?" Yuan Qingzang was puzzled, then quickly understood. He exclaimed in surprise, "Do you also want to fuse thunder into your astral qi?"

Luo Chen nodded lightly. (Chapter End)
