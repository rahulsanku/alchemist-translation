# Chapter 780: Fellow Daoist, Let’s Talk This Over!

"Who?"

The moment Luo Chen appeared, the City Lord of Lingtian was the first to sense something amiss.

When the explosive boom rang out and that figure abruptly materialized, he couldn’t help but stare intently.

It wasn’t any Eastern Wilderness Sage he recognized.

He was about to ask the nearby Divine Fire Sage, only to find the man’s expression drastically change as he deployed his escape art, transforming into a wind-fire wolf and flying toward the combat platform.

The City Lord of Lingtian was taken aback, then the corners of his lips curled slightly.

"Looks like there’s another good show to watch!"

Under the gaze of the crowd, someone had forcibly broken through the isolation formation and entered the combat platform. Aside from the Sages watching the battle, most people hadn’t even registered what was happening.

But a man’s clear, mocking laughter reached everyone’s ears.

"Since you lot have no regard for martial virtue, don’t blame me, Luo, for returning the favor in kind!"

The crowd was baffled.

But the next moment, they saw the white-robed Daoist—one hand holding Motian Cliff aloft with a phantom grip, the other tossing out a small mountain that spun as it flew.

The small mountain grew as it caught the wind, instantly transforming into a towering, perilous peak.

Stuck haphazardly into that peak were all kinds of broken sabers, spears, swords, and halberds—like the remnants of various magic treasures.

But those weapons weren’t the key.

The key was that the moment this mountain appeared, just like Motian Cliff before it, it pressed straight down toward another man on the combat platform.

Yin Sixiang felt a fierce wind slam into his face. He could barely breathe, and even mobilizing his mana was extremely difficult.

He barely managed to summon his lifebound treasure, the Four Spirit Banners, but they held for less than a single breath before all four snapped in succession.

"Fellow Daoist, let’s talk this over!"

A shrill cry came from outside the combat platform.

Divine Fire Sage, who had rushed over with the speed of wind and fire, hadn’t even stopped before he released his Sage domain, trying to hold back that bizarre mountain.

Not only that, but he opened his mouth and spat out a red light, attempting to save his disciple.

A sharp gleam flashed in Luo Chen’s eyes.

"Talk? I’m always reasonable. Let Yin Sixiang talk to me himself!"

In those deep, dark eyes, it seemed as if a mirror flower, water moon suddenly bloomed.

Divine Fire Sage froze.

It was only a moment of distraction, but the outcome was already sealed.

"Ah!"

All that was heard was a single scream. When he looked again, the spot where Yin Sixiang had stood on the combat platform was empty. Only a slowly shrinking mountain remained.

And—

A pool of crimson liquid, gurgling outward.

Seeing this, Divine Fire Sage’s eyes were filled with disbelief.

Yin Sixiang was the strongest fighter below the Sage realm in their Five Elements Divine Sect, the one with the highest cultivation, and the most influential figure among both the younger generation and the middle-aged. In the future, he was destined to advance to the Sage realm and become either the Sect Master or an Elder.

And he had died just like that?

Originally, the Sect hadn’t even wanted to let Yin Sixiang fight. They had planned to let him weather this period and attempt a breakthrough to the Sage realm.

It was Divine Fire Sage himself who had insisted on bringing him here.

Now Yin Sixiang had been crushed like an ant by this unfamiliar Sage.

How was he supposed to explain this to the Sect?

Divine Fire Sage’s lips trembled, his fingers shaking as he pointed at Luo Chen, his eyes blazing with uncontrollable fury.

"You villain! Bullying the weak like this! Where is your dignity as a Sage? Where is your—"

"Bullying the weak? Hahaha!!!"

His words were cut off by Luo Chen’s wild laughter.

Then, they watched as Luo Chen stood on Wang Yuan’s shoulder, his massive, mana-conjured hand gripping Motian Cliff tightly as it trembled and struggled to break free.

"I have never seen a magic treasure that can still operate on its own after its master has fallen."

"Have any of you?"

"Have you, Motian Old Ghost?!"

The last sentence struck like a thunderbolt, slamming heavily into a tall building several hundred meters away from the combat platform.

Countless watching cultivators turned their gazes there.

On that building, an old cultivator with his hair completely combed back was sweating profusely, his face twisted in a vicious expression as he frantically formed hand seals.

Seeing this bizarre sight, combined with the words of the white-robed cultivator on the platform, everyone finally understood the truth.

The one who had truly bullied the weak, acted despicably, and shown no dignity as a Sage was none other than Motian Old Ghost!

That Motian Cliff had never been Yin Sixiang’s treasure!

In fact, it hadn’t even been Yin Sixiang controlling it during the battle!

Wang Yuan hadn’t just been fighting Yin Sixiang alone—he had also been enduring a Sage channeling his lifebound true artifact!

Instantly, all kinds of uproar and contemptuous gazes were directed at Motian Old Ghost.

If looks could kill and doubt could pierce the heart, Motian Old Ghost would have been riddled with holes by now.

But the man himself didn’t care about their opinions at all.

He frantically mobilized his mana, remotely controlling his lifebound true artifact, trying to forcibly recall it.

But for some reason, that towering cliff seemed to have been fixed in place.

No matter how hard he strained, he couldn’t pull it back even a fraction.

Motian Old Ghost grew frantic.

"Fellow Daoist, let’s talk this over!"

Another "let’s talk this over"?

A sneer curled at the corner of Luo Chen’s mouth. This time, he didn’t waste words. Instead, he opened his mouth and gently exhaled.

*Whoosh…*

A wisp of flame, a mix of cyan and white that constantly shifted in color, drifted out from between his lips.

As it flew into the air, the flame’s color completely transformed into a stark, bone-white.

The bone-white flame spread along Luo Chen’s mana-formed hand and crept onto Motian Cliff.

*Sizzle, sizzle, sizzle…*

Silently, the towering cliff melted into a pool of golden liquid under the horrified gaze of countless onlookers.

"How is that possible?!" The City Lord of Lingtian’s eyes nearly popped out of his head.

"What kind of strange fire is that? Melting metal and breaking iron, refining it in an instant!" The Grand Palace Master of Fenghua Palace exclaimed in shock, the words tumbling out.

"Hiss!"

"Hiss!"

The other Sages watching this battle couldn’t help but suck in a breath of cold air, their minds shaken.

A Sage’s lifebound true artifact, one they had refined for centuries, had been melted into a pool of liquid in an instant?

"Pfft!"

With his lifebound true artifact destroyed and centuries of soul-bond severed, Motian Old Ghost was gravely wounded on the spot. He couldn’t even stop himself from spitting out a mouthful of blood.

The immense fury in his mind had clouded his reason.

He transformed into a streak of light, staggering toward Luo Chen, wanting to avenge the destruction of his treasure.

Halfway there, another man stopped him.

"Calm down, Old Ghost! Calm down!"

It was Divine Fire Sage.

His urgent words were like a bucket of cold water, dousing the raging fire in Motian Old Ghost’s heart and snapping him back to his senses.

Only then did he come to his senses, staring at the young man who had just floated down from the giant’s shoulder on the combat platform as if he were a monster.

Someone who could refine his lifebound true artifact in an instant was not someone he, gravely wounded and without his treasure to protect him, could contend with.

All he could do was swallow the bloody taste in his mouth and glare at Luo Chen with utmost hatred.

"Who are you?"

Divine Fire Sage’s expression was equally grim, but with a hint of suspicion in his eyes, he shouted loudly:

"This battle is an internal affair of the Luo Tian Sect! What right do you have to interfere?"

Faced with this challenge, Luo Chen smiled.

Behind him, Wang Yuan, who had shrunk back to his normal size, walked up to his back.

Without any hesitation, in front of the countless onlookers, he bowed to Luo Chen.

"Wang Yuan greets the Supreme Elder!"

Luo Chen was a little surprised by this bow.

But when he saw Wang Yuan’s eyes, he understood.

Meanwhile, below the platform, several figures rushed over like madmen.

Luo Chen looked up. Though a hundred years of wind and frost had changed many people’s appearances, he could still recognize most of them.

Especially the one in the lead—her face streaked with tears, disbelief mixed with a joy so great it felt like a treasure lost and found.

It was Caiyi!

Luo Chen let out a long, soft sigh. He reached out, wrapped an arm around her waist, and half-embraced her.

Qu Lingjun, Min Longyu, and the others knelt on one knee.

"Greetings, Master!"

"Supreme Elder, you’ve finally returned!"

"Yilong greets the Master-Uncle!"

Meng Qiner, who had been scheduled to fight that afternoon, stood awkwardly among the group, standing out like a crane among chickens.

But after Qu Lingjun tugged at her sleeve, she knelt without hesitation.

"This junior greets Sage Luo!"

Compared to the tens of thousands of spectators, the Luo Tian Sect members were few, a drop in the ocean.

But at this moment, each shouted title rang through the crowd.

And those titles made those who knew a little about the Luo Tian Sect realize Luo Chen’s identity.

The Supreme Elder of the Luo Tian Sect—and its founding ancestor!

The legendary prodigy who had single-handedly taken a ragtag group of Qi Refinement cultivators and step by step built them into a sect at the Adept level!

Among the crowd, someone even shouted out "Dan Chenzi," a Dao name so old that Luo Chen himself had almost forgotten it.

"You… how…"

The woman in his arms was already sobbing too hard to speak.

Luo Chen patted her shoulder, then gently pushed Gu Caiyi away.

"This matter isn’t over yet."

At those words, the woman wiped her tears and, knowing her place, stepped back.

But as she looked at Luo Chen’s back, her eyes were filled with nothing but tenderness.

Luo Chen stepped down from the platform and walked step by step toward Divine Fire Sage and Motian Old Ghost.

"Now do you know who I am?"

"So, do I still have the right to meddle in the Luo Tian Sect’s affairs?"

Divine Fire Sage took a deep breath and lowered his head slightly.

"Since it’s your family matter, I won’t question it. But you killed a disciple of my Five Elements Divine Sect. You still need to give an account!"

"An account?"

Luo Chen’s expression hardened, and he struck out without warning.

"You’re the ones who need to give *me* an account!"

A massive mana-formed palm print suddenly shot out, catching the two Sages off guard.

"How dare you! No private fighting is allowed within Lingtian Pass!"

"Luo Chen, what are you doing?"

Having witnessed Luo Chen’s terrifying methods earlier, the two Sages already harbored some fear. Faced with his lawless, sudden attack, they instinctively dodged.

As they retreated, a man who had been hiding behind them was exposed.

The mana palm print rushed toward him like a galloping horse, blowing his hair back and revealing a pale, anxious face full of terror.

"Get back here!"

Luo Chen barked. The massive palm print suddenly transformed into a giant claw, grabbed the man, and tossed him behind Luo Chen like a chick.

Min Longyu vaguely remembered Luo Chen’s style of conduct. Seeing him act, he understood the intent.

He struck out instantly, planting a restriction on the man.

Finally, he let out a long, quiet sigh.

"Wenjie, did you ever think it would come to this?"

Sima Wenjie gritted his teeth, not daring to look at the man’s back, but he didn’t show weakness either. He simply closed his eyes.

"Alas…"

Min Longyu sighed, then paused.

Every member of the Luo Tian Sect heard Luo Chen’s voice transmission in their ears.

They exchanged glances, then pushed through the crowd and left.

But unlike the decades of anxiety and insecurity they had felt before, now their hearts felt incredibly solid.

Even Gu Caiyi, when she looked back at that man, had a smile on her tear-streaked face.

Luo Chen smiled and waved at her.

"Go back. This time, I won’t disappear."

…

Luo Chen didn’t stay in the public eye for long.

In his view, it wasn’t a show of force—it was just airing the family’s dirty laundry.

From White Crane Domain to Lingtian Pass, he had shot straight into the sky, his escape art at full speed, crossing an entire domain in a single day to reach the front lines.

If he hadn’t needed the White Crane Sect to provide the entry token for the pass, he would have arrived even sooner and cleaned up this mess faster.

But clearly, his swift, decisive action had truly intimidated everyone in the city.

Including the group of Sages now gathered in the City Lord’s mansion!

In a side courtyard, Luo Chen and Wang Yuan stood facing each other, then exchanged a smile.

Luo Chen asked, "How long has it been?"

Wang Yuan replied with a hint of melancholy, "A hundred and ten years, exactly. Since you left, the days have felt like years for the Luo Tian Sect."

Luo Chen opened his mouth, a thousand words on his lips, but in the end, only one came out.

"You’ve worked hard."

Indeed, Wang Yuan had worked hard.

From what Luo Chen had learned from Zhao Wu, the senior disciple of Leisure Crane Sage, most of it was just fragments. But even from those scattered pieces of information, Luo Chen could tell how much effort Wang Yuan had put into protecting the Luo Tian Sect.

When the Luo Tian Sect was adrift without Luo Chen, their ally Diwu Qi had tried to swallow them up. Wang Yuan had fought a great battle against him, expelled him from the original Luo Tian Alliance, and restored the Luo Tian Sect’s independence.

Later, when Diwu Qi made a move in the shadows, Wang Yuan had charged into the enemy camp alone and forcibly killed him.

All to deter the petty and stabilize the hearts of the sect.

If anyone else had done such things, Luo Chen would have assumed they were greedy for the power and position of the sect’s high office.

But the one who did this was Wang Yuan!

The same Wang Yuan who had once taught Luo Chen, "Don’t let power go to your head, don’t let sect and family ties drag down your own cultivation."

He had never been interested in sect power.

To still make so many heroic efforts to protect the Luo Tian Sect under these circumstances, it was clearly because he was holding fast to the "righteousness" in his heart.

Fighting for the bond he and Luo Chen had forged through life-and-death trials, through shared honor and disgrace.

Fighting for the time Luo Chen had charged alone into the Nine Mountains of Accumulated Thunder, risking offending a Superior Adept, just to save him.

Between men, many things don’t need to be said. But they are remembered in each other’s hearts.

Luo Chen didn’t pester him with nagging questions about the details. He just asked with concern:

"Your path… how did it change?"

He remembered that Wang Yuan had always insisted on the orthodox path of body refinement, disdaining the demonic beast path.

Back then, when the *Heavenly Roc Transformation* was placed before them, Wang Yuan had only sneered and said, "A beast-like transformation? I’ll have none of it!"

Later, during his century of cultivation in the Northern Sea, Luo Chen had gradually realized that beast-like transformations were indeed unsuitable for the human true body.

Thus, in his repeated cultivation sessions, he had gradually adapted the Heavenly Roc True Body into a human giant form more suited to himself.

But just now, on the combat platform, the form Wang Yuan had taken was almost on the verge of some kind of demonic beast transformation.

Faced with this question, Wang Yuan waved his hand, his expression calm.

"Forced by circumstances. I had no choice."

Needless to say, during the century of guarding the sect, he must have encountered some insurmountable difficulty and been forced onto a shortcut he didn’t like, just to gain enough power.

Luo Chen wanted to say more, but in the end, he could only say one thing.

"From now on, I’m here!"

Only then did Wang Yuan finally smile.

"Go. They must be waiting anxiously."

"Yeah."

With Wang Yuan, Luo Chen left the side courtyard and headed for the main hall of the City Lord’s mansion.

Wherever he passed, cultivators were silent and fearful, bowing in respect.

Clearly, the story of Luo Chen destroying an enemy’s treasure and cleaning house on the combat platform had already spread.

Especially his protective act of disregarding Sage dignity to return the favor and crush the junior Yin Sixiang—it made those of lower cultivation fear him from the bottom of their hearts, even daring not to offend him in the slightest in their etiquette.

Before he even reached the main hall, Luo Chen paused.

Outside the hall, an old acquaintance was looking at him with a complicated expression.

It was Wind Moon Fairy, whom he had dealt with a few times.

Luo Chen said nothing. He only nodded, then pushed open the heavy doors of the main hall.

With a rumbling sound, countless gazes fell on the tall man walking in against the light.

At this moment, the sun was high in the sky, its rays blazing.

A white robe, a spine straight as a sword. In the hazy, dust-filled light, the man bared his teeth in a brilliant smile.

"Everyone, I’m back!"

Though his voice was not loud, it landed in each person’s heart like a mountain’s weight, a river’s flow.

It seemed as if he wasn’t speaking to the cultivators in the hall, but announcing his return to all of the Eastern Wilderness.
