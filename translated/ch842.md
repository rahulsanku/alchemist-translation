# Chapter 842: Eastern Wilderness Righteousness, Gao Chang's Origins

"Luo Chen!"

The gaze was cold, the voice indifferent.

Yet it was laced with barely contained fury!

The newcomer was none other than the Supreme Elder of the Five Elements Divine Sect—Sage Divine Origin!

Luo Chen's gaze was calm as he met those eyes.

"Shen Yuan, what advice do you have?"

Beneath the black hat, Sage Divine Origin's face could not be seen, only the faint hint of a stern, cold smile at the corner of his lips. He reached out directly toward Luo Chen.

"Return my sect's Jin Ling's remains to me!"

Luo Chen shook his head, speaking with feigned innocence. "The world knows that Fellow Daoist Jin Ling died in the battle to reinforce Heavenly Capital Sect, his form and spirit utterly destroyed. How could there be remains? There is one lifebound golden feather left, but it was seized by the Thousand-Cord Demon Emperor. You should seek him out, not me."

Sage Divine Origin's fury turned to laughter. "Well said—'the world knows.' Do you truly think I, old as I am..."

His words were cut short by a heavy cough.

"Cough, cough!"

A pale-faced, haggard old man slowly walked into the hall.

His voice was heavy as he spoke. "Fellow Daoist Shen Yuan, I know you bear the pain of a fallen sect brother, but this matter has nothing to do with the Alchemy Sect. Why take your anger out on him?"

"Heavenly Capital, you speak lightly. Jin Ling fell precisely to relieve the siege on Heavenly Capital!"

Sage Divine Yuan turned around, staring fixedly at the old man.

Heavenly Capital sighed. "I hold this grace in my heart, but the elders in the sect have sent word—Jin Ling's corpse and bones are truly gone. Stop making a scene."

A scene?

Sage Divine Yuan looked as if he had heard something utterly unexpected, momentarily stunned.

Then he saw Heavenly Capital walk over to Luo Chen's side, lips moving as they began to converse.

The meaning of this scene became clear to him in an instant.

Luo Chen watched him from the corner of his eye, inwardly relaxing slightly.

Reinforcing Heavenly Capital Sect had been a mission. Even if there was grace owed, his own contribution was no less than Jin Ling's.

And what value did the dead hold compared to the living?

Especially when he was an Alchemy Sect master!

Now that Heavenly Capital Sect had only two of its five Sages remaining, its strength had plummeted.

Under these circumstances, Heavenly Capital had made the most correct choice—risking offense to Shen Yuan to protect him.

And it wasn't just Heavenly Capital!

Even earlier, when Sage Divine Yuan had intended to make his move, Nine Spirit Primogenitor, Deng Taiyue, Lingfengzi, and others had instinctively sided with Luo Chen.

This was what it meant to have become a force to be reckoned with!

"Accept my condolences!"

"Though your sect has lost three elders, at least the mountain gate and spirit lands of Heavenly Capital Sect are preserved. Most of the Golden Core disciples and below have also survived."

"Once the war subsides and you recuperate for a hundred years or so, you will surely recover your vitality."

Luo Chen offered these words of comfort.

Heavenly Capital's smile was strained. "I accept your kind words. May it be so!"

Returning kindness for kindness, Luo Chen generously promised on the spot.

"After the war, if you have any need for alchemical pills, feel free to seek me out, Elder. Luo will not refuse!"

Heavenly Capital gave Luo Chen a deep look. He knew that his earlier stance had indeed won Luo Chen's approval.

The two, who had not been particularly close before, had now formed an alliance.

Such was the way of the world.

You showed grace to my Heavenly Capital Sect, and I helped cover up the matter of Jin Ling's hidden remains. Back and forth, it became what was called friendship.

Only...

"After the war... I fear that day is still far off!"

Heavenly Capital sighed and walked toward the upper end of the hall.

There, unlike usual, five chairs had been arranged.

Heavenly Capital took one.

Sage Divine Yuan, after giving Luo Chen a dark glare, also sat in one of the armchairs.

The remaining three chairs were, unsurprisingly, reserved for the three great cultivators of the Nether Origin Sect.

And indeed!

When most had arrived, the experts of the Nether Origin Sect began to appear.

Thirteen in total!

Aside from the familiar Lingtian City Lord, the other twelve were complete strangers.

But the powerful auras emanating from each of them commanded respect.

Among them were early-stage Sages and mid-stage Sages, each bearing an air of pride and self-importance.

These were the disciples of the Holy Land—the cultivators of Nether Origin!

Luo Chen sat in a relatively forward position, with Nine Spirit Primogenitor beside him. This showed Luo Chen's current status in the Eastern Wilderness—nearly equal to a great cultivator like Nine Spirit Primogenitor.

If one considered his reputation as an "Alchemy Sect master," his status might even be slightly higher.

But as Luo Chen scrutinized those unfamiliar Nether Origin Sect cultivators, a doubt arose in his mind.

"What's wrong?" Perhaps seeing the confusion on Luo Chen's face, Nine Spirit Primogenitor took the initiative to ask.

Luo Chen casually cast a soundproof barrier and transmitted his voice. "The numbers don't add up!"

"Numbers?"

"Yes, I heard earlier that there were over a dozen Nether Origin Sages just here at Lingtian Pass. And at the rear, at Heavenly Capital Sect, there were another eleven. I imagine the number of Nether Origin Sages on the other two fronts is also considerable!"

"Yes, there were six Nether Origin Sages helping on my front, including one great cultivator."

"That's right. The Nether Origin Sages on Shouyang Hermit's front should also be numerous. So why haven't these people arrived at Lingtian Pass yet?"

"It seems they have other tasks."

"Oh?"

Luo Chen was puzzled. Now that the Nether Origin Sect had joined the fray, the situation had eased.

Shouldn't they all gather at Lingtian Pass, regroup, and launch a counterattack against the demon race?

What kind of task could keep someone as powerful as Thunder Daozi from appearing even now?

Pursuing those demon emperors?

Doubts arose. Looking at the hall, which was missing many familiar faces, Luo Chen's spirits remained low.

Deng Taiyue, on the other hand, seemed eager to chat, repeatedly asking if Luo Chen had been injured.

He was probably worried that if something happened to Luo Chen, there would be no one to help him research the formula for the Awakening Pill!

"Speaking of which, where is Shouyang Hermit?" Luo Chen asked curiously. He hadn't heard news of his death, but neither had he seen him.

Deng Taiyue shook his head. "Him? During the great battle with us at Void Emperor Cult, his physical body was destroyed. Only a Sage embryo survived by luck. He's probably already returned to Eastern Sun Sect. Didn't you notice? The one representing Eastern Sun Sect today is another Sage elder."

Luo Chen opened his mouth but ultimately could say nothing.

He remembered that when they left Lingtian Pass, Shouyang Hermit had hoped everyone would return safely, so they could gather at Shouyang Mountain and continue their discussions.

But now, of the nine who went, four returned, two were severely wounded, and three had died!

And those three were no ordinary figures!

Jin Ling, Hua Tianshi, Pavilion Master Li...

Just as Luo Chen was lamenting, those at the head table seemed to have reached a consensus.

At the sound of Lingtian City Lord knocking on the table, the noisy hall fell silent, and everyone's attention converged.

"Cough, cough!"

"First, as City Lord, I express my remembrance and apology for all the fallen fellow daoists in this battle."

Lingtian City Lord stood and, facing everyone, bowed deeply.

This scene was quite unexpected.

What realm and status did Lingtian City Lord have, to act in such a manner?

The new representative of Eastern Sun Sect immediately said, "City Lord, you need not blame yourself. Life and death are fated for our generation; you cannot be held responsible."

Lingtian City Lord spoke with remorse. "In the end, it was my negligence that led to this great mistake."

His words seemed sincere, coming from the heart.

Everyone appeared somewhat moved.

But what each person truly thought was anyone's guess.

"Second, now that Lingtian Pass has been breached, reconstruction must be put on the agenda immediately, to prevent another attack by the demon army!"

After mourning the past came the most pressing matter of the present.

This was within everyone's expectations.

Lingtian City Lord spoke with great righteousness. "For this reconstruction of Lingtian Pass, all resources will be provided by my Nether Origin Sect! Fellow daoists need not spend a single spirit stone."

Luo Chen and the others exchanged glances. Such good fortune?

But then they quickly understood—this was only proper!

When Lingtian Pass was first built, the resources had come from the savings of various sects and families, and even the territory had been carved out from the five domains of Splendid Wind Palace and Mu Manor.

Nominally, the city of Lingtian Pass belonged to all the forces of the Eastern Wilderness.

The name "Lingtian" had been given merely as a courtesy to the Nether Origin Sect, plus they had provided the complete city-building plan and the city-protecting grand formation.

But now, with the Nether Origin Sect funding the reconstruction, and the name remaining, who would Lingtian Pass belong to in the future?

Would it belong to all the forces of the Eastern Wilderness, or to the Nether Origin Sect?

Luo Chen's heart grew cold. "Has it already begun?"

Lingtian City Lord's speech continued.

"Of course, although the various families need not contribute materials, they must still assist with manpower."

"Furthermore!"

Suddenly, his tone sharpened!

"From this sneak attack by the demon race on our rear, it is clear that their ambition is not limited to a single city or region, but the entire Eastern Wilderness—the very soil on which our human race survives!"

"If we cannot thoroughly defeat the demon army and reclaim our lost territory, we will have no place to stand. Your sects and families will not be able to pass down your heritage for a thousand generations."

"Therefore, this City Lord proposes that all sects in the rear send their full strength to reinforce Lingtian Pass and the surrounding five domains. At the same time, we conscript all intact Golden Core great sects to participate in the coming war!"

"This battle is not the affair of a single sect or family, but a matter of the rise and fall, the survival or extinction, of the entire Eastern Wilderness human race!"

...

For the first time, Luo Chen felt that the bold and generous Lingtian City Lord was so hypocritical.

Those words might have come from Lingtian City Lord's personal heart.

But how much sect interest was mixed in? They, who had lived for centuries as Sage elders, knew it all too well!

Yet beneath the high-sounding rhetoric lay an irresistible tide, one that no one could swim against. It was righteousness that crushed all, and no one could refute it.

Even after leaving the City Lord's mansion, Luo Chen still felt a suffocating oppression.

This was the first time since he had achieved the Sage realm!

"For the rise and fall of the Eastern Wilderness human race?"

"Hmph!"

"I'm afraid that after we truly drive away the demon race, the various sects will wither and decline. After a great reshuffling, the strongest Nether Origin Holy Land will be able to completely control the Eastern Wilderness, just like the Tianyuan Dao Sect."

Recalling the content of the meeting, Luo Chen smiled bitterly.

To dispel that oppressive feeling, after the meeting ended, he took the initiative to visit the severely wounded Talisman Elder, accompanied by Ye Lan.

...

The content of the City Lord's mansion meeting, spread by those with intent, quickly reached everyone's ears.

It contained not only righteousness and stakes, but also various attached benefits.

Rebuild Lingtian Pass, regroup, exhaust the strength of the Eastern Wilderness human race, and launch a counterattack.

After driving back the demon race, the ownerless spirit lands would be the best rewards!

This alone was enough to move many.

Coupled with the precedent of the demon emperors' massive harassment of the rear, the forces in the sixteen rear domains, which had been sitting idle, could no longer stay put.

The number of cultivators at Lingtian Pass, after a sharp decline, began to be heavily replenished.

Moreover, the nearby five domains of Lingtian also saw a daily increase in experts.

Luo Chen kept an eye on all this, but did not pay it too much mind. Instead, using the excuse of injuries from the previous battle, he closed his doors to visitors.

It was not until the return of a friend that he opened his gates to receive guests.

"Gao Chang, did you get him?"

The moment he saw Splendid Wind Fairy, Luo Chen asked this question.

"No!"

Splendid Wind Fairy shook her head decisively, then said with a grave expression, "That fellow is not simple!"

Luo Chen was not surprised. "Naturally, he is not simple. Although this demon's attack methods are frequent, his threat to our Sage realm is rarely seen in the world. His origins must be extraordinary."

"It's not just that!"

Splendid Wind Fairy took a deep breath and began to recount the situation she had encountered.

"After leaving Heavenly Capital Domain, I followed the Nether Origin Sect cultivators in pursuing and killing Splendid Brocade and the other demon emperors."

"During this process, we had the upper hand. I even destroyed Yellow Wind Guest's physical body with the help of the Nether Origin Sect cultivators."

"Thunder Daozi also displayed his renowned, peerless strength. Even someone as powerful as Splendid Brocade could not withstand him. But..."

Here, a hint of fear appeared in her eyes.

"But when facing Gao Chang, whom he completely countered, he kept pursuing without killing."

"At first, I thought it was out of caution. Later, when other commander-level demon emperors came to the rescue, it seemed to confirm my thoughts."

"But it wasn't until Gao Chang, under siege, unleashed a killing move of terrifying power—far beyond what a Sage could wield—that I realized something was wrong."

"With that one move, he killed five Nether Origin Sect Sage elders!"

Luo Chen's expression changed drastically!

Gao Chang had such a means, capable of instantly killing five Sage elders?

And these were Sage experts from the Holy Land?

"At that point, I began to retreat, no longer intending to continue participating in this pursuit. I thought the Nether Origin Sect cultivators would do the same, but..."

Splendid Wind Fairy spoke word by word. "But despite such tragic casualties, Thunder Daozi still did not give up. He even summoned more Sage experts to surround and kill Gao Chang."

"I realized they were determined to get him. And with repeated probing, pursuing without killing, using lives to pile up, it was as if they were trying to lure someone out."

Luo Chen ventured, "Besieging a point to ambush reinforcements?"

"Yes, that was the feeling." Splendid Wind Fairy nodded, but confusion showed in her eyes. "But Gao Chang's threat was already great enough. The commander-level demon emperors he attracted included top experts like Huang Man and Niu Gui, yet they still didn't seem to be Thunder Daozi's main target. Who else could it be?"

Stronger than Huang Man and Niu Gui—there were indeed such beings among the Eastern Wilderness demon race.

For example, Ao Kuang, or Azure Frost!

But intuitively, Luo Chen and Splendid Wind Fairy dismissed this possibility.

"You said that Gao Chang, at a critical moment of life and death, unleashed a killing move far beyond what a Sage cultivator could wield? Doesn't that mean it was a means of the Ascendant realm, a great power's divine ability?"

Faced with Luo Chen's question, Splendid Wind Fairy nodded subconsciously, then her eyes widened.

"You suspect that Thunder Daozi and the others are trying to use Gao Chang to lure out the Ancient Demon Emperor, Di Tian?"

Luo Chen hummed in acknowledgment, then took a deep breath. "Legend has it that the Ancient Demon Emperor Di Tian is a White Tiger who attained the Dao in the Eastern Wilderness. Thus, he pacified the Million Great Mountains, commanded the myriad demons, and was titled Lord Pingshan. He has a favored consort, Splendid Brocade, and a great general, Huang Man. Even old-timers like Ao Kuang have been subdued by him. As for Gao Chang, he might also be someone Di Tian values. Otherwise, it would be hard to explain why he carries an Ascendant-level means on him."

"You may not know, but to break through Lingtian Pass, Huang Man also used an Ascendant-level means once!"

"For Gao Chang to receive treatment on par with Huang Man shows his importance."

"With Thunder Daozi and the Nether Origin Sect cultivators acting as they did, their intention could not be clearer!"

Splendid Wind Fairy was puzzled. "Even if they lure out the Ancient Demon Emperor Di Tian, what then?"

"Kill him, and wouldn't that resolve the current Eastern Wilderness crisis?" Luo Chen countered.

Splendid Wind Fairy shook her head. "The Ancient Demon Emperor is on the same level as an Ascendant. How could he be so easily killed? Besides, isn't it said that Ascendant great powers cannot fight in battle?"

Cannot fight in battle?

Luo Chen seemed to recall certain scenes, and a light scoff escaped his lips.
