# Chapter 768: Dao Body Revives, the Change in Law

While everyone was still envying the future prospects of the Fu grandfather and granddaughter, Sage Seven Lamps was sincerely envying Luo Chen.

He even skipped the superficial effort of circling around the topic and directly stated his request!

"I wonder if Fellow Daoist Luo could bear to part with that Heavenly Secret Pill?"

On the mountain path, Luo Chen stopped.

So it was for this!

No wonder. Earlier, he had still been wondering why Sage Seven Lamps would stand up for him under the pressure of so many families. It turned out the man had set his eyes on his Heavenly Secret Pill.

And the timing of his appearance was just right, neither early nor late.

It was as if he had been waiting for Luo Chen to come out all along. Clearly, he had planned for this for a long time.

Sage Seven Lamps took two more steps, and when he noticed Luo Chen had stopped, he also stopped.

Turning back, he said frankly, "You may not know this, but the Heavenly Secret Pill concerns the key to late-stage Sage cultivators like us advancing to the Ascendant realm. At your current realm, it is of no great use. At most, taking it would increase your magic power and raise you by one or two minor realms."

"Such a treasure is something late-stage Sage cultivators dream of, including those experts from the great families. Previously, their attention was merely focused on the grotto-heaven competition, and with too many others present, it was not convenient for them to speak. Once they come back to their senses, there will certainly be people who come to your door."

"At that time, you will face the pressure of the great families. Perhaps even those great powers of the Tianyuan Dao Sect will be interested."

"You cannot protect it!"

The corner of Luo Chen's mouth lifted slightly, and he asked in return, "Then what about you, Elder? Can you protect it?"

Elder Seven Lamps nodded confidently.

"Naturally I can protect it! By virtue of my late-stage Sage realm, by virtue of my being one of Star Gate's five great elders, and by virtue of this pill coming from the hands of the former Star Gate elder, the Alchemy Saint!"

Luo Chen nodded in understanding.

With his early-stage Sage realm, as well as his status as an external affairs guest elder who had only just arrived, it would indeed be very difficult to keep the Heavenly Secret Pill.

But whether in strength, identity, or even his connection to the Alchemy Saint, no one in the whole Central Continent would forcibly seize it from the other party.

Seeing Luo Chen nod, Elder Seven Lamps felt joy in his heart.

"You agree?"

Luo Chen suddenly asked, "Since this pill's value is so high, and even concerns the key to us Sages advancing to the Ascendant realm, what will you use to exchange for it?"

Elder Seven Lamps hesitated. He had originally wanted to use the favor of having spoken for him earlier as compensation, but it was very clear that this Wanderer was not such a dull person.

Even if he had not spoken earlier, as long as Tianyuan Sect Master appeared, no one would have dared throw rules into disorder.

Therefore, that favor was dispensable.

But clearly, he had made full preparations!

"In this old man's life, I have cultivated for more than nine hundred years, and my footprints have covered three continents. I have collected countless treasures."

"Fellow Daoist has only recently achieved the Sage realm, and you come from a rogue cultivator background. I imagine you lack suitable magic treasures. I have more than ten magic treasures for you to choose from, and also a true artifact specialized in killing."

"Not only that, this old man also has three fourth-tier cultivation methods and one incomplete fifth-tier cultivation method, enough to satisfy Fellow Daoist's cultivation needs during the Sage stage."

"Does Fellow Daoist lack medicinal pills..."

As expected of an old Sage who had accumulated through many years, Elder Seven Lamps truly had a considerable fortune. The moment he opened his mouth, he presented all kinds of dazzling conditions.

At the end.

"Fellow Daoist is currently still an external affairs guest elder, and cultivating at Heaven-Earth Peak is rather inconvenient. If you agree, I will persuade the other elders to let you become a true Star Gate guest elder, and even promote you to the position of offering elder after two hundred years!"

Facing condition after condition that could move a person's heart, Luo Chen seemed slightly silent.

Because of this silence, Elder Seven Lamps grew happier and happier. This meant the other party was moved.

However, the result...

"My apologies. I have already taken that pill."

Elder Seven Lamps froze, then disbelief appeared on his face.

"Although you had fifty years inside the grotto-heaven, you had to protect..."

A deep, powerful aura slowly appeared, cutting off the old man's words.

Third-level Sage!

He had not sensed wrongly. The other party possessed the realm of a third-level Sage.

Although the fluctuations of his magic power seemed somewhat impetuous, it was precisely this impetuousness that made this soaring realm appear more believable.

Everyone knew that before entering Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, Luo Chen's realm had been first-level Sage.

But now, in one year outside and fifty years inside the grotto-heaven, he had soared by two minor realms. That was absolutely not something that could be achieved by bitter cultivation. Only by taking a miraculous pill like the Heavenly Secret Pill could one accomplish it in one step.

No wonder he could slaughter in every direction inside the grotto-heaven, with no one his match.

The third-level Sage realm, among peers who had broken through less than a hundred years ago, already possessed crushing ability!

Only, in this way, had his earlier posture not seemed laughable in the other party's eyes?

Just as displeasure rose in Sage Seven Lamps's heart, his expression suddenly changed, and he subconsciously retreated several steps away from Luo Chen.

"No?"

"More than that!"

Beside Luo Chen, a strange power was slowly spreading outward.

The moment he was lightly tainted by it, he felt that his fleshly body, already declining in blood and qi because of his excessive age, had a withered, aged feeling.

It was like an old tree shivering when autumn and winter arrived.

Sage Seven Lamps swallowed. "You comprehended it?"

Luo Chen smiled faintly and formed a spell. The outward-spreading magic power fluctuations and the true meaning of Withering-Bloom were immediately forcibly suppressed into his body.

"My apologies. Although I am very moved by the conditions you offered, Elder, Luo is powerless."

Elder Seven Lamps opened his mouth, and in the end, he sighed in dejection.

He had constantly paid attention to the grotto-heaven competition and had come to trade with Luo Chen before anyone else, yet he was still one step too late.

Indeed, when it was not in one's fate, forcing it was useless!

He shook his head, and the displeasure in his heart also vanished completely.

Whether this was only because his attitude changed after failing to obtain the Heavenly Secret Pill, or because Luo Chen had revealed the potential of comprehending the true meaning of the laws in advance, was unknown.

"Since that is so, then let it be."

Luo Chen cupped his hands, sincerely thanking the other party.

"Earlier, Elder spoke righteously on my behalf. Luo will remember this kindness."

Elder Seven Lamps smiled with some effort. That small favor and little benefit had no great use.

But Luo Chen's words were not finished.

"Just now, Elder mentioned that you could raise my guest elder status to formal guest elder. Is that true?"

Elder Seven Lamps grunted, but he was no longer as enthusiastic as before.

"If you can still take out a precious pill like the Heavenly Secret Pill, I will naturally run about for you."

Clearly, he could not!

Even though Luo Chen still had a Law-Derivation Pill more precious than the Heavenly Secret Pill, he absolutely could not take it out.

Perhaps sensing that his tone was somewhat stiff, Elder Seven Lamps slightly adjusted his mood and asked curiously, "Actually, at your age, whether Star Gate's guest elder status is formal or not should not be so important, right?"

"For me personally, it is indeed not that important. A spirit land and a cave residence are already enough. But..."

Luo Chen first nodded, then changed the direction of his words.

"But Luo is not completely alone either. I also have a Dao companion and disciples, spirit pets and servants. They also need a place to stand."

So that was it.

Sage Seven Lamps instantly understood what he meant.

Compared to that external affairs guest elder, a formal guest elder of Star Gate still had some authority.

One of these points was the ability to bring close people into Star Gate.

Even if they did not cultivate on Heaven-Earth Peak, the Dao Sect could plan out a territory for them and let them settle there.

And if Luo Chen stayed in Star Gate for five hundred years, his juniors would also have the opportunity to enter the Tianyuan Dao Sect for cultivation.

Clearly, Luo Chen's pursuit of formal guest elder status was in consideration of this.

Sage Seven Lamps thought for a moment. Perhaps remembering that the other party had revealed, at such a young age, potential no less than a Tianyuan true disciple, he still decided to sell a favor and reveal some inside information.

"This matter is not difficult. The reason you cannot become a formal guest elder is because you are too young and have not established the slightest merit."

"Right now, there is an excellent opportunity for you to become a formal guest elder through merit."

An excellent opportunity!

Luo Chen froze, thinking of something.

Before he entered Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, through Elder Seven Lamps's introduction, he had met Hui Yuanhong. The other party had now indeed filled the vacancy left by the Alchemy Saint's ascension and become one of Heaven-Earth Peak's nineteen elders.

At that time, to draw Luo Chen in, the other party had also said similar words.

He had said that Luo Chen had come at precisely the right time.

And now, Elder Seven Lamps said the same.

"May I ask what that opportunity is?"

Elder Seven Lamps drew in a deep breath and no longer spoke aloud. Instead, he transmitted with divine sense.

"The Tianyuan Dao Sect has already decided that once Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven closes, troops will be sent to the Northern Sea and Western Desert continents. The former is to conquer the Northern Sea immortal cultivation world and bring it into Tianyuan's territory. You came from the Northern Sea, so you are presumably quite familiar with that side. At that time, you can follow the Tianyuan army to the Northern Sea, storm cities and seize strongholds for Tianyuan, split mountains and open seas. With your ability, so-called merit will certainly be easy to obtain."

"At that time, with merit to your name, I will speak for you on Star Gate's side, and Fu Qinglan will presumably not be stingy about putting in a few good words for you on the Dao Sect's side."

"In this way, not only formal guest elder status, but even the position of offering elder will be within easy reach."

After saying this, Elder Seven Lamps had nothing more he wanted to say and left with somewhat waning interest.

After he left, Luo Chen's brows furrowed slightly.

He had long expected that the Tianyuan Dao Sect might send troops to the Northern Sea.

But did he still have to return to the Northern Sea?

...

Ever since Luo Chen emerged from the grotto-heaven, the flow of time seemed to have slowed.

It was not a change in lifespan, but that waiting always made one feel as though days passed like years!

The competition was still continuing.

As long as the true disciples inside had not come out, or had not fallen, it had to continue.

After roughly calculating, the people inside could still persist for at least fifty years before reaching the limit. In the outside world, that was half a year.

Only after everyone came out would the so-called competition rankings be judged, deciding the sect's rewards.

But no matter what, Fu Qinglan, the first to comprehend the true meaning of the laws and come out early, was already certain to be first place!

This also meant that Luo Chen had perfectly, and even beyond imagination, fulfilled his promise to Fu Chaosheng.

At the same time, it meant that Luo Chen no longer had any attachments in the Central Continent!

Regarding the Tianyuan Dao Sect's matter of conquering the Northern Sea, opening territory and expanding land, Luo Chen carefully thought it over in private and found that it was indeed a good opportunity for him.

On the Northern Sea side, he could not be considered unfamiliar, and he still had many grudges and favors unresolved.

If he followed the army as a Star Gate guest elder, he could accomplish two things at once.

But before that, he still planned to first return to the Eastern Wilderness and bring those people in his memory, whose faces were almost growing blurry, back to the Central Continent.

Luo Chen had already roughly decided the date of departure.

When the competition ended would be when he left the Central Continent and returned to the Eastern Wilderness.

For now, the changes in the outside world and the discussions about Luo Chen, Fu Qinglan, and the others had nothing to do with him.

Alone, he quietly cultivated in Cave Residence Seventy-Eight of Heaven-Earth Peak.

Just as the cultivation he had revealed before Elder Seven Lamps showed, at this moment, he truly possessed the realm of a third-level Sage.

But it was not, as Elder Seven Lamps thought, that he had just entered third-level Sage!

His realm had truly risen somewhat too quickly!

Originally, he had already lingered for a long time at the peak of first-level Sage, and had even used the Withering-Bloom Divine Seal to suppress the rapid increase of his cultivation.

After entering the grotto-heaven, in only a few years, he naturally advanced to second-level Sage.

For the next several decades, he was basically consolidating his realm.

Logically speaking, with his realm increased to this point, Luo Chen was already satisfied.

But during sudden enlightenment, he entered the depths of this world's sea of origin qi. After comprehending the true meaning of Withering-Bloom, he even obtained a return feeding of origin qi.

That was this world's fundamental origin!

It was the aggregate of spiritual qi!

Even if ordinary people obtained origin qi's return feeding, because of the different attributes of the magic power they cultivated, they could not perfectly refine it and could only absorb part of it.

However, Luo Chen absorbed eighty or ninety percent!

The vast origin qi directly pushed his realm in one breath to third-level Sage, and even for a time made it climb toward fourth-level Sage!

At first, Luo Chen thought this situation was caused by the Withering-Bloom True Flame advancing to the fifth tier, but later he discovered it was caused by his own constitution.

At this moment, Luo Chen looked at the attribute panel, at the entry "Five-Spirit Dao Body" that had quietly hidden beside "Fire Spirit Body" for a long time, and saw it faintly emitting light.

"Why is it glowing?"

"Is it because I comprehended the true meaning of the laws?"

"But this true meaning of Withering-Bloom of mine is likewise a fire-attribute true meaning of the laws. It should not... hm?"

Inside the cave residence, Luo Chen revealed a look of surprise that was very rare for him.

This was the first time he had opened the attribute panel after sudden enlightenment.

Originally, he wanted to use the data on the panel to see his true situation, and he was also very curious why the Five-Spirit Dao Body would suddenly awaken.

But the changes on the panel were somewhat unexpected.

Not only was there a new attribute entry, [Law], but below that entry, the prefix of [True Meaning of Withering-Bloom] was not fire, but wood!

This truly caught Luo Chen off guard.

Ever since he switched his lifebound cultivation method to the fire attribute in the Golden Core stage, for the next one hundred and sixty-some years, he had abandoned every other attribute and immersed himself solely in the fire path.

Logically speaking, the true meaning of the laws he comprehended should also have been fire attribute.

But this true meaning of Withering-Bloom, on the attribute panel, was indeed classified as wood attribute.

He, Luo Chen, might be wrong, but the attribute panel had never been wrong.

Then why had this situation occurred?

Luo Chen pondered deeply.

"Is it because when I was in Qi Refinement and Initiate, my main cultivation was wood attribute? My foundation lies in wood?"

"Or did I receive the influence of Fu Qinglan, who comprehended wood origin, inside Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven?"

"Or perhaps the Withering-Bloom True Flame itself also has an attribute, and it is classified as wood?"

"If that is truly so, then the true meaning of Withering-Bloom being categorized as wood attribute is understandable. After all, during my comprehension, what I saw and heard was mostly life and death transformation, the rise and fall of all things, not the explosive tyranny of fire, but more like the withering and blooming of wood."

"In this way, I can also understand the awakening of the Five-Spirit Dao Body. I broke free from the shackles of the Fire Spirit Body and comprehended the true meaning of the laws of another attribute."

"Then does that mean, if I can comprehend the true meaning of the laws of other attributes, the Five-Spirit Dao Body can fully awaken and cover the Fire Spirit Body?"

Faintly, Luo Chen seemed to see a path to recultivating his Dao body.

Even if that path seemed somewhat illusory, even unrealistically ambitious.

Just as Luo Chen intended to carefully study the changes on the attribute panel so he could adjust his next cultivation plan, his cave residence welcomed its first visitor.

Luo Chen's heart moved, and with a beckon, a transmission talisman flew in.

After a while.

Luo Chen stared at the ruddy-faced old man before him with a smile that was not quite a smile.

"Fellow Daoist Fu, you hid things from me so bitterly."

*(End of Chapter)*
