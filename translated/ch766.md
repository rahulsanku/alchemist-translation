# Chapter 766: The Sword Breaks Fright, the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger

"Defeated foe?"

When those four words fell into his ears, Lin Jingtang's face abruptly flushed red.

That most unbearable scene surged back like a tide!

His birth had not been very good. He did not even know who his parents were, but his path of growth was enough to make him proud.

He was adopted by the Lin clan head and taught like a true son. Because of his outstanding talent, he had revealed his extraordinary nature very early.

Even though he had not joined the Tianyuan Dao Sect, many people had always compared him to the Dao Sect disciples of his generation.

He had not failed everyone's expectations either. Step by step, he advanced through Qi Refinement and Initiate, finally achieving the Sage realm!

That moment was the happiest day of his life. The Lin family held a Sage-forming ceremony for him, many friendly humble aristocratic clans came to congratulate him, and even from the Dao Sect, quite a few peers came to observe the ceremony.

He knew all of this was because the Lin clan head wanted him to exert himself for Lin Bufan in his most crucial trial, but he was willing.

In his heart, he had also long since prepared to protect Lin Bufan with all his strength, to repay the Lin family's great kindness.

He also had this confidence!

But all of this was cast under a shadow by Luo Chen's palm strike when they had first entered the grotto-heaven!

That palm forced him back a hundred li and left him heavily injured.

And that palm had merely been something the other party did casually!

This was unbearable for Lin Jingtang, who had always placed himself alongside Tianyuan's true disciples.

If he could not even match a rogue cultivator, how could he speak of standing together with Tianyuan's true disciples above the blue sky?

If not for Lin Bufan's Dao path being before him, making it impossible for him to act out of personal anger, he would long since have thought of washing away that humiliation.

Originally, he had already suppressed that anger.

But now...

"Seeking my own death?"

Lin Jingtang grinned, blood threads looking especially savage against his white teeth.

"Even if I die, I will drag you down to hell with me!"

The shattered Sage actually drilled straight out of the Purple Mansion, exposed bare beneath broad daylight and countless gazes.

Its tender little arm landed on the golden bell, which was completely disproportionate to it, and it charged downward violently without looking back.

Smash Luo Chen to death!

Boom!

With only one blow, the great mountain beneath Luo Chen collapsed and shattered.

But to everyone's surprise, he still floated quietly in the air and was not affected in the slightest.

Seeing this scene, Lin Jingtang, who had already fallen to the mountain base, could not help freezing.

But with his rich experience, he quickly realized what had happened.

His strike had missed and only smashed beside the other party.

And the reason all of this had happened lay in the many ghostly shadows and illusions that had suddenly appeared around the other party when they brushed past just now.

"An illusion art that can affect divine sense?"

Someone answered this doubt for him.

Qi Xiongcheng's face was grave as he flew to Shi Ju's side.

"That move again! And what is different is that he is clearly in a state of sudden enlightenment, yet can still cast this move. This means he must have cultivated a divided soul art or a second Sage!"

"Second Sage?" Shi Ju shook his head. How could such a profound method be something Luo Chen, a wilderness rogue cultivator, could master?

"It is a divided soul art! Didn't you see that his earlier attack was mainly a sneak attack, unlike before, when he fought in such broad, sweeping fashion? This shows that right now, he simply cannot drive the vast magic power within his Sage, much less mobilize the spiritual qi of heaven and earth."

There was a clear distinction between a divided soul art and a second Sage.

The former was merely a split-off soul, possessing consciousness and behavioral logic identical to the main body, but in the end, it was only a soul.

That it could cast soul arts and barely mobilize a tiny bit of magic power already showed how deep Luo Chen's attainments in soul refinement were.

But a second Sage was almost equivalent to a cultivator's second life!

A complete Sage, a complete soul, and if paired with a corresponding physique, it could become the legendary external incarnation!

This kind of incarnation was not something puppet incarnations or magic-power incarnations could compare with.

Only the orthodox Blood God Child of the Primordial Demon Sect in legend could barely match it, and even then, only barely. In the end, a Blood God Child was still not transformed from the cultivator's own Sage.

Once a cultivator mastered such a secret art, it was equal to gaining another duplicate self. In battle, the two would be connected in mind and cooperate without gaps, achieving a result where one plus one was greater than two, or even multiplying battle power severalfold!

At this moment, the conversation between Shi Ju and Qi Xiongcheng was not concealed at all. It spread openly across the four fields, and also fell into Lin Jingtang's ears.

The small Sage figure with a great hole in its chest came to realization, and its fury grew even more overwhelming!

He had actually been ambushed earlier by a divided soul.

Now his fleshly body was heavily wounded, his Purple Mansion shattered, and even his Sage true body was on the verge of collapse.

He had to avenge this extraordinary humiliation!

Ignoring Lin Bufan's constant attempts to stop him from far away, Lin Jingtang, whose mind had been overwhelmed by anger and hatred, kicked his little foot against the great bell.

The Fright Bell rose into the sky and covered the air above Luo Chen.

Lin Jingtang formed incantations with both hands. His magic power, which had already been constantly draining away, now poured out completely like a great river breaking its banks.

His body was shrinking and breaking apart, but his eyes were incomparably mad.

"Grow!"

"Grow!"

"Grow!"

The golden great bell began swelling madly: ten zhang, fifty zhang, seventy zhang, until it reached a hundred zhang!

It was like an enormous pot lid, and also like a giant umbrella propping up the sky, completely covering the hundred-zhang radius around Luo Chen.

No matter how strange and exquisite your soul art was, under such a range, where could you escape?

The Fright Bell suddenly fell.

In the distance, Fu Qinglan ignored her injuries and shot into the sky, her sword and leaf arriving even before she did.

But having received Shi Ju's instruction, Wang Yifu and Qi Xiongcheng separately stopped the two weapons, while Shi Ju himself blocked in front of Fu Qinglan.

"Move!"

Fu Qinglan shouted sharply and struck out with one palm.

Shi Ju was unhurried. With a wave of his folding fan, he blocked this palm, and a cold smile appeared on his face.

"Do you truly think yourself important!"

His empty left hand formed a spell, its speed extremely fast. In a blur, thunderclaps seemed to explode.

Crack!

Fu Qinglan seemed to be struck by lightning and fell into the earth.

When she stood again, dense silver-white arcs of electricity flickered over her dress.

She gave a muffled groan, and green magic power immediately scattered those arcs.

In her eyes, there was some startled uncertainty.

Was the other party not a pure Nine Yang Body? Why could he also use lightning arts?

"You cannot save him."

By her ear came Shi Ju's cold laugh.

Fu Qinglan raised her head and looked. The golden great bell had already fully descended, covering Luo Chen's figure within.

Lin Jingtang's Sage, meanwhile, slowly floated upward. Ignoring the shattering of the Sage, he was continuously forming incantations and chanting spells.

As he cast, the Fright Bell constantly emitted rumbling sounds.

Even though she was not at the center of the attack and was extremely far away, Fu Qinglan felt the illusion that her soul was trembling and her fleshly body was breaking apart.

Lin Jingtang meant to shake Luo Chen to death while he was alive!

Fu Qinglan's heart chilled.

Looking around, Shi Ju and the other two were poised to attack, the two from the Gu family watched like tigers eyeing prey, and high in the ninth heaven there was also the faintly visible figure of a white eagle anxiously flapping its wings.

Everything could no longer be saved.

In the end, she could not protect Luo Chen safely in return as he had protected her.

If so, then preserving his life was enough!

Her magic power surged, her soul resonated, and Fu Qinglan shouted sharply.

"Luo Chen, activate the illusion chess piece!"

As soon as these words came out, everyone's expression changed slightly.

Activating the illusion chess piece meant directly leaving Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven. With the ability of these cultivators present, they still could not stop the methods of an Ascendant great power.

Were they to watch Luo Chen leave safe and sound with their own eyes?

The other party had a divided soul on him, so even if his main soul was silent, he could still activate the illusion chess piece.

Shi Ju's expression changed repeatedly. If that happened, they truly would draw water with a bamboo basket and end up with nothing.

Wang Yifu was even more furious. Not only could he not kill Luo Chen, but Fu Qinglan had successfully comprehended the true meaning of the laws. How could he speak of avenging his hatred?

Lin Bufan, however, let out a breath of relief. If Luo Chen truly left, then Jingtang would likely no longer persist. As long as his Sage could be returned to his body and they also left the grotto-heaven to return to the Mountains and Seas Realm, with the methods of the Tianyuan Dao Sect's experts, perhaps they could still preserve the other party's Sage realm.

On Gu Yuan's side, he shook his head, and the interest that had originally been raised in him also lowered its banners and ceased.

This show had ended with a strong beginning and a weak end!

Perhaps Luo Chen's only loss was probably having his sudden enlightenment interrupted!

Lin Jingtang, one of the involved parties, thought differently from them.

"You want to leave? How could it be that simple!"

With a thought, amid Lin Bufan's cry of alarm, the lower half of Lin Jingtang's Sage abruptly collapsed, turning into sky-flooding magic power that fell into the Fright Bell.

"Scatter for me!"

Clang!

The great sound of the enormous bell shook the universe!

Under such a blow, the cultivator inside would certainly die. Even if there was a backup like the illusion chess piece, his mind would be thrown into chaos, and he would have no time to activate it.

Just then.

"You truly want me dead."

A low, hoarse voice sounded faintly.

Amid everyone's shock, they saw a blood-red sword tip emerge from the top of the great bell.

Hiss!

An ear-piercing sound rang out continuously.

Under Lin Jingtang's terrified gaze, that sword seemed to stab into his heart. Inch by inch, it broke open his lifebound magic treasure, bit by bit cutting apart the Fright Bell.

Until!

"Open!"

Only a crack could be heard, and the great bell turned into two halves.

A man holding a blood sword, stepping on a gray cauldron, and wearing black Mystic Dust Armor appeared in everyone's eyes.

His jet-black long hair drifted behind him, blowing in the piercing cold wind like black serpents dancing madly.

His fair skin was like congealed fat, no longer bearing the wrinkled, middle-aged weariness of before.

Only the eyes that swept over everyone were like a cold pool with no visible bottom, reflecting the fear in every person's heart.

The moment he appeared, he moved.

The blood sword slashed diagonally!

Lin Jingtang, already suffering agonizing soul pain because his lifebound magic treasure had been broken, had no power to resist under this sword.

Even though he had activated the illusion chess piece in his earlier panic, it was already too late.

The sword fell, and half of his Sage, along with his soul, instantly shattered.

Only his ruined body vanished with a vortex.

On the other side, Luo Chen stomped one foot.

The gray cauldron beneath his feet suddenly sent nine scarlet chains bursting out in unison, striking toward Shi Ju's side.

Shi Ju's brows furrowed, and the earlier scene appeared again.

Firelight flashed, his body vanished inch by inch, and when he appeared again, he was already beside Qi and Wang.

He glanced sideways and saw the nine scarlet chains wrap around Fu Qinglan and carry her flying back.

"Hmph!"

Luo Chen did not even look at the others. Instead, he lowered his head and looked at Fu Qinglan, whose clothes were stained with blood.

"Since you have already comprehended origin power, why are you still in such a sorry state?"

Shame appeared on Fu Qinglan's face.

In the end, she was not good at combat. Even though she had grasped a higher-level power, she lacked battle experience, and she had no corresponding methods to drive that power. She could only use it in the shallowest way.

However, the ending was still good after all!

Her purpose on this trip had already been fulfilled, and Luo Chen had not suffered any injuries. The only regret was...

"I did not protect you and caused your sudden enlightenment to be interrupted. It is this little sister's incompetence. But now all is well. We only need to activate the illusion chess pieces and leave this world, and our merit will be complete."

Sudden enlightenment interrupted?

Luo Chen laughed despite himself.

He did not explain anything to Fu Qinglan, and his gaze swept over everyone present.

"Leaving is inevitable. But before leaving, I would like to borrow all of you to test the method I newly comprehended. It can also complete the unfinished battle from Three-Rainbow Falls."

As soon as these words came out, everyone was shaken.

He was not leaving, but instead still wanted to fight?

Was he angry that everyone had disturbed his opportunity?

"Pay with your life for my Wang clan true disciple!"

Wang Yifu roared and charged toward Luo Chen holding his bamboo staff.

Qi Xiongcheng remained silent and followed close behind.

Shi Ju's eyes were uncertain, but since the other party gave him this chance, he could not possibly retreat.

Taking a deep breath, he rushed toward Luo Chen in a three-sided encirclement.

Seeing this, Fu Qinglan's face turned cold, and she raised the autumn-water longsword in her hand again, only to be pressed down by a thread of Luo Chen's magic power.

"You won't let me act?" Fu Qinglan was puzzled.

She admitted Luo Chen was very powerful, but one person's strength was limited after all.

She had comprehended wood origin, yet under those three working together, she was not their opponent. Moreover, Shi Ju seemed to have hidden methods he had not yet used.

Did Luo Chen truly have such confidence?

Luo Chen watched the enemies charging from three directions, unmoved, and then looked toward the distant Gu Yuan, Gu Chen, and Lin Bufan.

"And you?"

After watching Lin Jingtang fall with his own eyes, Lin Bufan was already filled with grief and fury. But his extraordinary spiritual perception made him detect the hidden threat within Luo Chen's body.

He gritted his teeth and did not answer, but his body rapidly retreated.

Watching this scene, Fu Qinglan's expression grew complicated, and she only murmured the words "Little Lin."

On Gu Yuan's side, he and Gu Chen exchanged a look and nodded in tacit understanding.

Since the other party could enter a state of sudden enlightenment while protecting Fu Qinglan, there was a very high possibility that he had taken that Heavenly Secret Pill.

Regardless of whether he had comprehended anything, he had gained something in the end.

For example, the "new method" the other party had just mentioned.

"I still have family tasks on me, so I will not meet Fellow Daoist with blades and weapons. After we go out, we may drink and speak happily instead."

Gu Yuan said with a smile, bringing Gu Chen slowly back.

Regret appeared on Luo Chen's face, but very soon his interest rose again.

In the sky, that white eagle, which had been circling for a long time, finally could no longer hold back and dove toward Luo Chen.

"Just in time!"

"Three men and one demon are enough for me to test the sword of my new art!"

Although he spoke of testing a sword, he put away the Yuantu sword, and even swallowed the Primordial Unity Cauldron into his mouth.

Luo Chen quietly stood in the void, slowly raising his right hand. His fingers joined like a sword and gradually fell.

As his finger descended, the winds and clouds of the sky abruptly changed!

Large swaths of cloud and mist tore apart, and endless wild winds churned as though about to roll this sky backward.

Majestic spiritual qi of heaven and earth surged in from every direction.

The snowy mountains that had collapsed because of the previous great battle had gathered into a vast ocean of snowmelt, and because of Luo Chen's finger, it also began boiling.

Between heaven and earth, everything was restless!

The three great cultivators originally charging toward Luo Chen sensed even more that their own magic power was becoming restless beyond their control.

Within their vision, Luo Chen's aura was rising madly. His figure became like a blazing sun, so brilliant and dazzling that no one could look directly at him. He also seemed like a great tree connecting heaven and earth, spreading a dense crown that shrouded sky and sun!

Shi Ju, who had been immersed in many family inheritances since childhood, revealed disbelief in his eyes the instant this abnormality appeared.

"Could he also have comprehended the true meaning of the laws?"

After this thought appeared, he no longer had any intention of hiding his methods.

In his right hand was the folding fan. In his left appeared a rattle-drum.

The next moment, the folding fan opened, and flame currents rolled.

The rattle-drum shook, and two bells struck the drum face, releasing streaks of lightning thunderbolts.

Amid the madness in Shi Ju's eyes, lightning and flame currents gathered together, becoming a fused current of lightning and fire, and a sense of destruction immediately appeared.

Not only him, the other two also sensed something wrong.

Wang Yifu abruptly stopped, thrusting his bamboo staff straight into the air. One emerald green bamboo after another rose from the void, turning into a bamboo forest that enveloped him.

Qi Xiongcheng lifted all the magic power in his body. The black saber in his hand trembled violently and turned into an enormous saber light of a thousand zhang, arriving late yet striking first toward Luo Chen.

Facing these attacks, Luo Chen made no other move, still slowly pointing one finger.

From his mouth came a light shout.

"Withering-Bloom!"

*(End of Chapter)*
