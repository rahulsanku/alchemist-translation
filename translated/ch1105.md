# Chapter 1105: Moon Inviting Forms Nascent Soul, Muxian Blocks the Gate

The Swallow Islands.

The most remote sea region east of the Northern Sea, barren in resources.

And because it was close to the Southern Ocean, which teemed with numerous gu insects, living creatures found it difficult to approach, making it quite dangerous. Thus, for many years, it had remained obscure, with only a few low-ranking immortal cultivation families gathering here.

But in recent years, this place had grown increasingly prosperous.

To evade the tide of demon beasts, a large number of low-ranking, weak cultivators either gathered at the Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance, where great powers sat, or hid in remote places.

The Swallow Islands had become a suitable location.

Especially since there was a great cultivator at the late Adept stage stationed here. As long as one was unlucky enough not to encounter a Sage-level Demon Emperor, one could be safe enough to protect oneself.

Moon Inviting Fairy was that great cultivator at the late Adept stage!

Rumor had it that this woman had once been the Dao companion of a powerful Northern Sea expert and had received gifts from him. Although she had always kept a low profile and rarely appeared before others, her realm had steadily increased.

Now, at over four hundred years old, she was already at the Great Perfection of the Adept realm, only one step away from forming a Nascent Soul!

And as for forming a Nascent Soul, under the speculation of those in the know, it was estimated to be soon.

In recent years, under the joint efforts of formation masters from various families, the Swallow Islands had been linked together. The method used was the island-linking formation technique that the great power Li Canghai had spread throughout the Northern Sea back then.

After several hundred years of nurturing, coupled with the frequent wars in the Northern Sea and the shifting of earth veins, the Swallow Islands had unknowingly already shown signs of a fourth-order spirit vein.

To mortals with short lifespans, this was the changing of seas into mulberry fields, a geographical transformation.

But in the eyes of cultivators, it was the result of Moon Inviting Fairy's excellent management, enabling her to nurture a fourth-order spirit vein.

Now, with a fourth-order spirit vein already present, and Moon Inviting Fairy at Adept Great Perfection, the opportunity to form a Nascent Soul had arrived.

The scattered cultivators of Swallow Islands who had received her protection were all looking forward to that day.

If the Swallow Islands could produce a Sage, and with Moon Inviting Sage in charge, this place would truly become their paradise for cultivation.

However!

When forming a Nascent Soul, heavenly omens would appear, and the commotion would be enormous.

The cultivator themselves had to regulate the three treasures of essence, qi, and spirit, and there might even be a sudden heart demon tribulation, leaving them completely unable to deal with the outside world.

If those Nascent Soul heavenly omens attracted any powerful enemies, the cultivator forming the Nascent Soul would fall into the most dangerous situation.

Such incidents were common in the immortal cultivation world.

Just like what Luo Chen had experienced back then when Icecastle's Canglong first crossed his tribulation. He had encountered interference from the Whitebone Mysterious Serpent and ultimately failed.

This was also one of the reasons why it was difficult for scattered cultivators and cultivators from small forces to reach the Sage realm.

Even if their accumulation was sufficient, when it came time to take that final step, there was still the risk of outside interference.

Therefore, at this critical juncture, these people would usually invite as many trustworthy friends as possible to act as Dao protectors.

Unfortunately, Moon Inviting Fairy had a cold and aloof nature. She had lived in Ten Mile Pinghu for many years and had very few friends.

Therefore, the matter of Dao protection fell upon the Cheng family, where Moon Inviting Fairy resided, and the many scattered cultivators of the Swallow Islands.

Starting a year ago, the Swallow Island cultivators had frequently gone out to clear out nearby low-ranking demon beasts, ensuring that when Moon Inviting Fairy broke through, she would have a peaceful place undisturbed by outsiders.

But in the Northern Sea now, where was there any peace to be found?

On Black Swan Island.

Inside the Cheng family's council hall, three Adept cultivators had grim expressions on their faces.

"According to reports from several scattered cultivators, seven hundred li west of Swallow Islands, there is a massive herd of beasts flying in our direction. I'm afraid it will affect the Ancestor's Nascent Soul formation!"

"Have you determined how many high-ranking demon beasts are inside that herd?"

"Reporting to the Patriarch, the scattered cultivators are of low cultivation. They could only observe from afar and were completely unable to get close for a detailed look. But the existence capable of driving a herd of over ten thousand is definitely at the third-order Demon King level. Moreover, the Northern Sea demon clans nowadays often act in groups to fight against us cultivators. I'm afraid there is more than one Demon King inside."

The Cheng family patriarch frowned, his face darkening. "No matter how many Demon Kings there are, we absolutely cannot let them affect the Ancestor's Nascent Soul formation. This matter is not just the Ancestor's affair; it is a major event for our Cheng family! Pingqiu, you will lead all the Initiate and Qi Refinement cultivators of the clan, except for those maintaining the grand formation, and go seven hundred li to meet the enemy. Stop the demon beasts from interfering with the Ancestor's Nascent Soul formation."

Cheng Pingqiu was stunned. "Send out the entire clan? Isn't that too dangerous? If we are not careful and all of them are lost, our Cheng family will suffer a severe blow to its vitality, and might even exist in name only."

The Cheng family patriarch glared at him fiercely. "Is the substance of the Cheng family us? Is it those low-ranking cultivators? No! It is only the Ancestor. She is everything to the Cheng family. As long as she is still here, the Cheng family will always exist!"

Cheng Pingqiu disagreed with this statement, but another Adept cultivator nodded in agreement.

"The Patriarch is absolutely right. Four hundred years ago, during the great chaos of the Swallow Islands, the previous patriarch of our Cheng family fell. At that time, our once-great family only had two or three Initiate true cultivators. But because of the Ancestor's presence, talents have emerged one after another over the years, and now we have the three of us, Adept cultivators. If the Ancestor is no longer here, let alone advancing the family further, even just maintaining our position as the number one immortal cultivation family of the Swallow Islands would be difficult."

The current Swallow Islands not only had scattered cultivators but also the three families that had survived back then.

Those three families had also benefited from the upgrading of the local spirit vein and had successively produced several Adept cultivators.

The reason they were willing to remain subordinate to the Cheng family was only out of fear of the Ancestor.

Ten Mile Pinghu, Moon Inviting Fairy!

She was the true number one person of the Swallow Islands!

Although she rarely appeared, the strength she displayed in the few times she had struck out to repel third-order Demon Kings was awe-inspiring.

Especially!

According to rumors, Moon Inviting Fairy seemed to have a very deep relationship with a certain demonic path powerhouse.

That made it even more unwise to provoke her!

"Alright, I will do my best in this battle. But the other three families cannot just stand by and watch. Second Brother, go and inform them. Each family is to send a hundred elite cultivators to join our Cheng family in meeting the enemy." Cheng Pingqiu accepted the task. Having made the decision, he no longer hesitated, but a hint of hope appeared on his face. "If the rumored Sage who has a close relationship with the Ancestor were to come and act as her Dao protector, why would we need to exhaust ourselves like this?"

"Go. Don't think about these things. One must rely on oneself." The Patriarch waved his hand, his tone firm. "Moreover, since the Ancestor dares to form her Nascent Soul at this time, she must have other preparations."

"I hope so!"

Cheng Pingqiu muttered to himself, then hurried out the door to begin mustering troops and preparing to meet the invading demon beasts.

The Second Elder also did the same, rushing off to the other three families.

Seven hundred li seemed far, but for high-ranking cultivators, it was only a matter of moments.

In fact, the combat range of some powerful Adept cultivators was more than that.

Fortunately, those demon beasts were acting in groups, and the low-ranking ones were slow, so they would not impact the Swallow Islands for a while.

Cheng Pingqiu's preparation time was not long. He soon gathered a group of elite Cheng family cultivators.

The number was quite large, over a thousand.

Among them, there were a full twenty Initiate cultivators!

The three families did not shirk the Cheng family's request for aid either. Each sent over a hundred elite cultivators.

All were led by Adept cultivators, each dispatching ten Initiate true cultivators.

Especially the Xu family, which had a very good relationship with the Cheng family. Their Supreme Elder, a mid-stage Adept, personally led the team!

Thus, in no time at all, the Swallow Islands had organized four Adepts, fifty Initiate true cultivators, and over a thousand elite Qi Refinement late-stage cultivators.

Not only that, but the scattered cultivators of the Swallow Islands who had received the news also rushed to join the support.

A force of several thousand people gathered together, heading west in a mighty procession, intending to annihilate the invading demon beasts outside.

And on the Swallow Islands, within Ten Mile Pinghu, the heavenly omens of a Nascent Soul were already faintly visible.

A large amount of spiritual energy was gushing out from various earth veins, converging towards the small island in the center of the lake.

...

The Swallow Island cultivators assembled extremely quickly. Under the organization of the Cheng family, they boarded large ships and set off rapidly.

Seven hundred li was covered in less than half an hour.

When Cheng Pingqiu led his men to the location, the number of demon beasts they saw almost covered the entire sea area.

The reason he used the word "covered" was because what floated on the sea surface were all corpses!

Blood flowed like a sea, and corpses piled up like mountains.

Only one cultivator in a green robe stood amidst the mountain of corpses, holding a long sword, his posture calm and composed.

All the invading demon beasts had been completely annihilated by this one person!

Everyone was shocked.

What level of strength was this person?

Could it be the Sage realm?

The green-robed cultivator slowly turned around, revealing a handsome face with an immortal aura and a refined, elegant temperament.

His gaze fell upon the crowd, especially on the clan emblems on the robes of the cultivators from the four families.

"Are you the Swallow Island cultivators?"

Cheng Pingqiu stepped forward proactively, speaking with reverence. "We are indeed Swallow Island cultivators. May I ask the Senior's esteemed name, and why you have helped us eliminate this beast horde?"

The green-robed cultivator smiled. "I have been away for too long. It seems none of you recognize me anymore!"

The crowd was stunned, not understanding his meaning.

But among the crowd, the oldest Supreme Elder of the Xu family had a look of doubt on his face, which gradually turned into one of disbelief.

He staggered out of the crowd and said with incredible joy, "Could it be Brother Muxian?"

The green-robed cultivator looked at the white-haired Xu family Supreme Elder. "You know me? You are..."

"I'm Xiaoqi, Xu Wei! The one who used to follow behind you, asking you for pills to eat."

At this moment, the Xu family Supreme Elder's words were excited, and his demeanor was exactly like a child's.

But no one was puzzled by this attitude.

Only because the person before them had a very high probability of being a Sage.

Otherwise, it could not explain how he could so easily annihilate over ten thousand demon beasts, especially when there were at least five powerful auras among them, suspected to be third-order Demon Kings.

If this person was related to the Xu family, then the name of the number one family of the Swallow Islands might have to change.

As for Cheng Pingqiu, after his shock, he vaguely felt that the name "Muxian" was somewhat familiar.

It seemed that in the history of his Cheng family, there was also such a figure?

But that person was not very important, so no matter how hard he racked his brain, he couldn't recall for a moment.

Xu Muxian smiled slightly. "So it's Xiaoqi. I didn't expect you to have formed a core as well."

The Xu family Supreme Elder shook his head. "This is nothing. My lifespan is almost up, and I have no hope of breaking through. What use is forming a core? But Brother Muxian... are you at the Sage realm?"

Xu Muxian's face turned serious. "As you can see, through a stroke of fate, I have indeed achieved the Sage realm. I have returned this time specifically to act as a Dao protector for my master's wife, to clear away all external enemies for her!"

The words "master's wife" instantly cleared the fog in Cheng Pingqiu's memory.

He finally knew why the name Xu Muxian was somewhat familiar to him.

Everything pointed to that powerhouse who had once had a relationship with the family Ancestor, that demonic path giant who had become a taboo on the Swallow Islands.

Azure Yang Demon Lord!

In years past, the Azure Yang Demon Lord had taken over the Swallow Islands, first slaying the demonic path Adept, then subduing the various families.

The Ancestor had once become his Dao companion and spent many years with him.

Xu Muxian, who had originally been unimportant to the family, had caught the Azure Yang Demon Lord's eye because of his alchemy talent. He was accepted as a nominal disciple and taught the art of alchemy. Later, he even became a marriage prospect between the Cheng and Xu families.

Later, the Azure Yang Demon Lord left quietly, and after his Dao companion died of old age, Xu Muxian also followed in his master's footsteps, leaving the Swallow Islands to roam the Northern Sea immortal cultivation world alone.

Because the Azure Yang Demon Lord had made a great name for himself in the Northern Sea and had offended many major powers, the Swallow Islands rarely mentioned this name, lest they be implicated.

Naturally, as his nominal disciple, Xu Muxian, who was only at the Initiate stage back then, was even less remembered.

Yet, four hundred years had passed.

The Demon Lord had not returned, but his nominal disciple had come back instead.

And he had come back specifically to act as a protector for the family Ancestor.

"So this is the backup plan the Ancestor prepared?"

Cheng Pingqiu felt relieved. Having a Sage in charge was truly the best possible outcome.

He asked cautiously, "Senior Xu, with your help, the beast horde has been eliminated. There should be no more external worries for my family Ancestor's Nascent Soul formation, right?"

However, Xu Muxian's face showed no sign of relief.

The long sword in his hand had never been sheathed; he held it tightly.

"I'm afraid it's not that simple!"

Cheng Pingqiu was taken aback. "This horde of over ten thousand beasts and five Demon Kings have all perished under your sword. Is there something even more troublesome than this?"

Xu Muxian let out a faint sigh. "Have you ever heard of the name Split Heaven Stream Lord?"

The crowd was stunned, then their expressions changed drastically.

Split Heaven Stream Lord!

The infamous demonic path giant of the current Northern Sea!

With incredibly fast escape speed and formidable strength, he had especially obtained a set of evil techniques for refining the souls of living people alive to enhance his own soul foundation. In recent years, he had frequently struck out to hunt high-ranking cultivators.

The number of Adept cultivators who had fallen at the hands of Split Heaven Stream Lord had long since numbered in the hundreds or thousands.

As for Demon Kings, they were too numerous to count!

Even Sage cultivators, who were said to be the most difficult to kill, had records of falling at his hands.

Although the former Moluo Stream had existed in name only after Blood Nightmare Moluo disappeared without a trace, the name Split Heaven Stream Lord had become increasingly prominent, once rivaling the rising star Black Demon.

They were known as the two great demonic path giants of the current Northern Sea!

Their methods were cruel and ruthless, and few who fell into their hands ever survived.

Cheng Pingqiu's voice was dry. "Why would this old demon set his sights on my family Ancestor?"

Xu Muxian said calmly, "In the current Northern Sea, there is no longer any peaceful place. The name of the Swallow Islands has long since spread far and wide. If my master's wife does not succeed, then so be it. But once she does, she will become the type of nascent Sage that Split Heaven Stream Lord most favors. There is a high probability that he will come to hunt her, extract her soul, and seize her spirit to enhance his own cultivation."

"But..."

"There are no buts!" Xu Muxian waved his long sword, his gaze firm. "If he does not come, then fine. But if he truly does come, with me blocking the gate, I will never let him harm a single hair on my master's wife!"

The crowd swallowed their saliva, their eyes filled with a glimmer of hope.

Their emotions could be described as a great joy followed by a great fall, fluctuating endlessly.

Seeing Xu Muxian kill the demon beasts, they were overjoyed, and because he was a Sage from the Swallow Islands, they were even more excited.

But after hearing the great name of Split Heaven Stream Lord, they became uneasy and terrified.

A person's reputation is like a tree's shadow.

If Split Heaven Stream Lord truly came, could this unknown Sage Xu really stop him?

(End of this chapter)
