# Chapter 619: Honored Guests at the Door, the Daoist Feeds the Dragon

"What right does he have?"

Inside a cave dwelling rich with spiritual qi, Huangfu Song smashed the teacup in his hand, his face full of anger.

A mere lower-grade defensive magic treasure. What right did it have to dare list a price of five hundred thousand? Did they truly think cultivators in the cultivation world were fools?

Lord Gui, who had been reporting the news from the side, looked helpless.

He roughly understood what Huangfu Song was angry about.

Back then, showing weakness before Devil Lord Azure Yang had once been the greatest humiliation in Huangfu Song's heart. Even though he often used the principle that those who accomplish great things can bend and stretch to convince himself, the fact that he still paid attention to Devil Lord Azure Yang's movements from time to time showed that he had not put the matter down.

Now that Devil Lord Azure Yang had refined a second defensive magic treasure, it meant the other man's success was not coincidence.

Such treasures had a strong market, and their prices had always remained high.

In this way, Devil Lord Azure Yang's source of wealth would flow endlessly. The plan of hoping the other man's reserves would be insufficient to support the enormous rent, forcing him to withdraw from Peng Lake, had ended without a cure.

With these two things added together, Huangfu Song had lost composure as he had now.

Lord Gui said in his sharp voice, "Master, some things should be put down when they must be put down. Do not delay our own great undertaking."

Huangfu Song glared at him from the corner of his eye. "Why should I put it down? Do you truly think I am inferior to him? That time, we were in the Dao field he had managed for many years, with a grand formation empowering him, while our side was instead restrained. If we changed locations, it remains unknown who between him and me would die."

"A child worth a thousand gold does not sit beneath the eaves. Why risk your life?"

"Hmph. Do I not still have all of you? Moreover, the Purple Dowager also promised, when I begged with all my strength, that she would act once for me."

"Cough, cough. Master, I heard recently that the Purple Dowager took the initiative to send a calling card to Peng Lake."

Huangfu Song froze.

What taking the initiative to send a calling card meant, he understood better than anyone.

When it was not courtesy first and force afterward, it meant the other party had the intention to befriend Devil Lord Azure Yang.

Thinking of this, Huangfu Song's face twisted even further.

"That vile woman..."

He could merely refine defensive magic treasures. Was there a need to shamelessly go up and curry favor like this?

...

Inside Dragon Abyss Immortal City.

Outside Azure Yang Hall.

The Moya Cave Master paced for a long while, then finally made up his mind.

"This old man wants to see what quality this magic treasure you crafted truly has. For this, even paying five hundred thousand spirit stones will not matter."

One subordinate did not understand why Daoist Azure Yang had set such a high price.

Regarding this, the Moya Cave Master curled his lips.

"He placed it there as the shop-suppressing treasure, so of course the price had to be set high. Five hundred thousand. Foundation Establishment cultivators cannot afford it, and some newly advanced Golden Core cultivators are also short on funds. Only established Golden Core cultivators who have operated for many years can take out this sum of spirit stones. But such people have usually already obtained suitable protective treasures over many years of cultivation. Why would they set aside the near and seek the far, buying another treasure?"

The subordinate suddenly understood.

No wonder a lower-grade defensive magic treasure had been priced so high, yet not so high as to be absurd.

This was an excellent price stage, making people retreat from difficulty while not truly treating them as fools.

But now, their Cave Master wanted to purchase this treasure and study it carefully. This so-called shop-suppressing treasure had not suppressed the shop for even two days before it was about to be bought away.

The Moya Cave Master strode forward, pushing through the bustling crowd and entering Azure Yang Hall.

The crowd behind him had all come because of the shop's reputation. At this moment, when they saw the Moya Cave Master, those who recognized him immediately called out his identity.

For a time, the crowd fell into an uproar.

Even the Myriad Immortals Assembly's most famous artifact-forging master, the Moya Cave Master, had personally come. Was this a disguised acknowledgement of Master Daoist Azure Yang's artifact-forging art?

Could it be that Master Daoist Azure Yang could already compare to the Moya Cave Master?

The Moya Cave Master paid no attention to the discussions behind him and walked straight to the counter.

He said to the gentle woman there, "Where is the Bright Yellow Boulder Armor?"

Wen Xiu recognized the other party's background and felt somewhat nervous, but her face maintained as much neither-humble-nor-arrogant composure as possible.

"Master, I fear this treasure cannot be handed to you for viewing right now."

"Hm?" The Moya Cave Master frowned.

Before he could become angry, Wen Xiu hurriedly explained clearly, "A buyer is upstairs discussing it with my husband. We must first..."

"Hmph. I would like to see who dares compete with me for a treasure."

The Moya Cave Master swept his sleeve and strode up the stairs.

Wen Xiu followed closely behind.

Thud, thud, thud.

The moment the Moya Cave Master entered the VIP room, a soft laugh came.

"Fellow Daoist Moya, it is only a lower-grade magic treasure. Why make such a great mobilization? Why not yield it to Huo this time?"

"Huo Fang!"

Seeing the middle-aged Daoist speaking calmly and smiling, the Moya Cave Master blurted out.

It was precisely Huo Fang, the deputy city lord of Dragon Abyss Immortal City.

"Do you not have a mid-grade defensive magic treasure, the Heaven-Guarding Ring? Why would you still..."

Before his words finished, he realized something.

"You are purchasing this item for Wuzhong Valley?"

Huo Fang smiled and nodded, his expression composed and calm, as if he were not at all worried the other man would raise the price and fight him for it.

The Moya Cave Master, however, grew somewhat conflicted.

Although Huo Fang was the deputy city lord of Dragon Abyss Immortal City, he himself was not a dependent under Dragon Abyss True Being, and he was not a demon hunter like the Angler or the Purple Dowager in the Myriad Immortals Assembly.

This person came from Wuzhong Valley.

Everyone knew that the Myriad Immortals Assembly claimed to be a holy land for wandering cultivators, but it was not so pure.

Apart from the Three Great Wanderers and Dragon Abyss True Being, who had advanced from nine-star demon hunter, there were three other Nascent Soul true beings.

Those three already controlled forces behind them.

The Tianqu Chen clan, the Coral Sea Zhou clan, and a sect called Wuzhong Valley.

Huo Fang came from Wuzhong Valley. Apart from his identity as deputy city lord of Dragon Abyss, he was also the third disciple of Spirit-Control True Being of Wuzhong Valley, and concurrently served as Wuzhong Valley's transmission elder.

The reason he had come to Dragon Abyss Immortal City to take office had to do with a rule established when the Myriad Immortals Assembly was founded in the early years.

The three great forces could not interfere too deeply in the Myriad Immortals Assembly's internal affairs and were only considered allies.

To compensate them for their losses, the Three Great Wanderers had deliberately yielded part of the benefits of Dragon Abyss Immortal City.

The price was the position of deputy city lord.

After discussion, the three great forces decided to let Huo Fang serve as deputy city lord and seize the benefits belonging to them on behalf of the three great forces.

These benefits were not only as simple as cultivation resources. More importantly, there was another point: recruiting talent.

Among the grass and wilderness, heroes emerged. Among wandering cultivators, talented people also appeared.

Within the countless wandering cultivators, there would always be some figures with special talents.

The significance of Huo Fang serving as deputy city lord lay in drawing in the formidable wandering cultivators within the Myriad Immortals Assembly.

Why did Moya know this?

No other reason. He was one of those drawn in.

Over these years, Moya Cave had refined so many magic instruments and magic treasures, but only a portion had flowed into the wandering cultivator community. More had been purchased by the three great forces.

If not for the Moya Cave Master's loose nature and unwillingness to be bound, the three great forces had even been willing to promise him the position of guest elder and support him with heavy resources.

And now.

Huo Fang had personally come to Azure Yang Hall to purchase this lower-grade magic treasure that was useless to him. What this meant, the Moya Cave Master instantly tasted.

What he valued was not the Bright Yellow Boulder Armor, but Daoist Azure Yang, who refined it.

Just like when they had valued him years ago.

Seeing the Moya Cave Master sink into thought, Huo Fang suddenly spoke.

"You want this item only to study it. This does not contradict our purpose. Rather, it can help us appraise this item, and even save you quite a few spirit stones."

"Fellow Daoist Moya, yield it to me. It is a mutually beneficial matter. Later, I will also give you time to study this treasure."

The Moya Cave Master thought for a moment, then agreed without hesitation.

Outside the room.

Zhang Jiadi and Wen Xiu did not dare breathe loudly.

Even if they wanted to privately discuss, they feared being overheard by the Golden Core masters' divine sense.

After a while, laughter and conversation came from inside the room.

The door opened, and the Moya Cave Master walked out, no longer mentioning purchasing the Bright Yellow Boulder Armor.

"Deputy City Lord!" Zhang Jiadi respectfully saluted.

Huo Fang gave a sound of acknowledgement. His gaze fell on Zhang Jiadi and his wife, and his eyes blinked.

"I hear that Fellow Daoist Azure Yang does not have many capable assistants at his side, and it has always been you husband and wife helping him manage mundane affairs?"

Zhang Jiadi hurriedly nodded. "Yes. This little one established a cultivation family and relies on my lord to live and work in peace. In ordinary times, I help my lord handle some matters."

"Is that so..."

Huo Fang pursed his lips, and the smile in his eyes grew brighter and brighter.

...

One month later.

A cyan calling card was delivered into Heavenjade's hands on Peng Island.

"Master said he is in seclusion and will not see outside guests. What is Little Zhang doing?"

Over these years, countless people had tried to visit Luo Chen, and the calling cards delivered to the door were piled like small mountains.

Among them were quite a few formidable beings famous among wandering cultivators.

Even so, Luo Chen had not met a single person.

Especially after releasing the second defensive magic treasure, he had anticipated the subsequent reaction and specifically instructed Heavenjade to keep visitors outside Peng Lake and not let them affect his cultivation.

Under these circumstances, Zhang Jiadi actually still dared deliver a calling card up?

Under Heavenjade's displeased gaze, Zhang Jiadi swallowed.

"The person who gave this calling card has a somewhat special identity."

"Hm?"

"It is Lord Huo Fang, deputy city lord of Dragon Abyss Immortal City."

"Huo Fang?"

Heavenjade recited this name, and her expression quickly changed.

Huo Fang did not represent Dragon Abyss Immortal City, but Wuzhong Valley, as well as the Tianqu Chen clan and Coral Sea Zhou clan.

In other words, what he represented were the three Nascent Soul true beings in the Myriad Immortals Assembly.

Her master could ignore those powerful Golden Core wandering cultivators, but how could the face of three Nascent Soul true beings be brushed aside so casually?

Taking a deep breath, Heavenjade received the calling card and hurried toward the alchemy-and-forging hall.

When she arrived there, however, she saw no movement.

"Master is not refining pills or forging artifacts?"

Without thinking, she arrived outside Star-Dipper Hall and stood there quietly for a long time.

Even with a matter to report, she did not dare rashly disturb her master's cultivation.

Fortunately, Luo Chen's divine sense was powerful, and there was a special spiritual connection between spirit beast and master. He soon detected her arrival.

"What is it?"

"Master, Deputy City Lord Huo Fang of Dragon Abyss has sent a calling card."

"Give it to me."

The heavy copper door slowly opened, revealing a gap. A stream of mana swept out and rolled the card held in Heavenjade's hands inside.

Afterward, the copper door slowly closed again.

It seemed calm, but in that instant, Heavenjade's expression changed drastically, her heart sea churned, and even the demon qi she had restrained for many years could not be suppressed and leaked out.

"Gulp."

"What aura was that?"

Heavenjade could not help swallowing.

In that instant just now, it was as if she had sensed a savage beast awakening.

Natural rank suppression made her almost unable to breathe.

"What exactly is Master cultivating?"

...

Inside Star-Dipper Hall.

Luo Chen's eyes were tightly closed, but inside his body, it seemed rivers, lakes, and seas were rushing.

On his body, large bulges occasionally rose one after another, as if someone were striking a cowhide drum.

Sounds like great bells and drums rang out now and then inside the starlit hall.

Listening carefully, all these sounds came from within his body.

"Initial body tempering begins with external methods turning inward. Small success is the Vajra Realm."

"After entering the hall and mounting the stairs, blood qi surges like rivers. This is the Blood River Realm."

"After that, one understands the transformations of qi and blood, embraces the core and gathers intent, condenses astral qi, and achieves the third realm of body tempering, the Furnace Realm."

"This is the traditional body-tempering path. I, however, created another path that imitates savage beasts, not emphasizing qi and blood transformations, only caring about bodily strength."

"After years of cultivation, I have exchanged blood eight times and shaped bones two hundred and six times. With the aid of Black Emperor Ointment, my physique is stronger than a puppet body. But in the end, the cultivation is too mixed and cannot become perfectly unified."

"Now, I use artifact-forging methods to forge and cast my precious body. I take the meaning of tempering through a thousand hammerings, carrying out the melting and refining of limbs, bones, extraordinary meridians, and eight channels. In the future, I will surely achieve the sight of swallowing and exhaling the great wilderness."

As thoughts rose and fell, the qi and blood inside his body, accompanied by the burning refinement of the Withering-Bloom True Flame, formed surges of powerful force that struck Luo Chen's organs.

Under this sort of impact, those organs and meridians became increasingly tenacious.

This was precisely the body-tempering method Luo Chen had newly researched.

Using qi and blood as firewood, burning it with the Withering-Bloom True Flame, forming even stronger internal power, and then using that power to hammer the physique.

Supplemented by large quantities of blood food to increase qi and blood.

And with the external fourth-tier Black Emperor Ointment continuously improving the strength of his physique.

In this way, his body-tempering path became increasingly complete, no longer blindly following ancient methods, only seeking what suited his own body.

And the change was the body-tempering progress on the attribute panel rising faster and faster.

He was only a dozen or so points of progress away from Archaic Tier Four. It was close at hand.

After one complete circulation finished, Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes.

His gaze landed on the calling card in his hand.

After reading for a long while, he smiled.

"Then let us meet."

...

Three days later, a large boat slowly set out from the shore of Peng Lake.

Zhang Jiadi, head of the Zhang family of Cailian Island, personally steered it, and Fairy Heavenjade personally accompanied the guests.

The honored guests on the boat were not limited to Huo Fang alone.

He had also brought a Foundation Establishment junior from the Tianqu Chen clan and a Foundation Establishment junior from the Coral Sea Zhou clan.

Apart from this, there were also the artifact-forging master Moya Cave Master, and the two eight-star demon hunters, Purple Dowager and Angler.

The latter three visits counted as borrowing Huo Fang's light.

However, since they had already boarded the boat, it represented Luo Chen's acknowledgement.

The reasoning was simple. Before, he had treated everyone equally and seen no one. Now that he was going to see Huo Fang, it meant a precedent had been opened. He might as well meet Angler, Purple Dowager, and the others at the same time. After all, they were the very top figures among the Myriad Immortals Assembly's wandering cultivators. If he could avoid offending them, it was best to avoid it.

At this moment, the group stood on the boat, facing the wind and appreciating the scenery of Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake.

In ordinary times, mist and waves stretched vast, with no human figures visible.

Now, with the formation opened, water vapor and rosy mist dispersed, revealing green mountains, small islands, and emerald lake water. It could be considered a blessed immortal land.

Especially beneath the boat, Golden Dragon Carp swam over from time to time, gradually gathering into the sight of ten thousand carp leaping together, a magnificent spectacle.

Huo Fang stood at the front, watching this scene and nodding repeatedly.

"It seems Daoist Azure Yang has truly managed Peng Lake very well."

The others also nodded in agreement.

A young female cultivator with an oval face suddenly let out a startled sound.

"Hm? Why are the Golden Dragon Carp here black-gold?"

Heavenjade glanced at her and said calmly, "Living here long-term, they were tainted with jiaolong qi and underwent some mutation. It is within reason."

Jiaolong qi?

That young female cultivator froze, and the Foundation Establishment cultivator from the Tianqu Chen clan beside her quietly explained, "The master of Peng Lake has a spirit pet, a Black-Scale Giant Jiaolong. It once nearly swallowed a mid Golden Core cultivator in one bite and is truly incomparably formidable."

The woman suddenly understood.

With a jiaolong living here long-term, even the Golden Dragon Carp, which carried a faint trace of dragon race bloodline, had evolved. She simply did not know what the black-gold dragon carp tasted like.

These words fell into the ears of the Golden Core masters, and each had different thoughts.

The Purple Dowager: "Daoist Azure Yang has managed Peng Lake so prosperously, and now even Huo Fang has come to cooperate with him. His position in the Myriad Immortals Assembly is becoming more and more stable. This time, I came to visit proactively to break the ice over the previous misunderstanding. Only then will it not hinder my future Dao path."

Having one more friend was always better than having one more enemy.

As for Huangfu Song, who had some connection to her?

He was currently still tossing around on a piece of territory controlled by a Golden Core sect. Small-scale tinkering, in her view, truly amounted to nothing.

Who knew what year or month it would be before he could restore the Great Zhou dynasty?

Moreover, even in the Great Zhou dynasty's peak period, its Nascent Soul ancestor had to hide like a rat, unable to see the light.

As for so-called imperial glory, the Purple Dowager did not want to enjoy even the slightest bit of it in this life.

The Angler was different. He only wanted to see Daoist Azure Yang and ask clearly when the other man would end seclusion and be able to move around normally.

He wanted to exchange with the other man more and learn more things related to Nascent Soul formation from his mouth.

As for the Moya Cave Master, who stood alone in one corner?

At this moment, his face was full of hesitation, and he seemed rather impatient.

After studying the Bright Yellow Boulder Armor, his heart felt as if claws were scratching it, itching almost to death.

How exactly had Daoist Azure Yang achieved perfect fusion between the formation and magic treasure embryo?

The materials had clearly been only the most ordinary, yet under that strange method, it had defensive power capable of resisting early Golden Core cultivators and even mid Golden Core attacks.

He had never seen or heard of such a method.

If he could learn even a little of it, it would certainly bring a qualitative improvement to his artifact-forging art. Perhaps in his lifetime, he might even have hope of refining the legendary true artifact.

Huo Fang held a smile at the corner of his mouth and watched this scene calmly.

"Fellow Daoist Heavenjade, how much longer?"

Heavenjade replied, "After passing this Flying Falcon Island, we will arrive."

Huo Fang looked at the island ahead. Upon it were short-tailed black birds perched among the forest trees, their movements fierce and swift as they took off and landed.

Unfortunately, they were not spirit birds. Otherwise, if cultivated a little, many people would want them.

Just as he sighed inwardly, the large boat turned, passed around Flying Falcon Island, and a stretch of mirror-like water entered their eyes.

As the breeze blew, there seemed to be dragon roars in waves.

Everyone focused and looked. A white-robed Daoist stood at the water's edge, scattering pills into the water. From the water, a huge black dragon head suddenly leapt up, joyfully swallowing those pills into its mouth.

At just this moment, the white-robed Daoist turned.

He patted his hands, as if brushing away crumbs, and also as if dismissing the black dragon.

When the black dragon rolled over and dove to the lake bottom, splashing ripples across the surface, the white-robed Daoist laughed loudly.

"When friends come from afar, is it not a joy?"

"Fellow Daoists, Daoist Azure Yang offers greetings."

*(End of Chapter)*
