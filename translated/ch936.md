# Chapter 936: Invincible Under Ascension, Can Gu Refining the Body Prove the Dao?

Wind Thunder Gorge!

Since ancient times, this had been a perilous location in the Southern Frontier.

Here, the earth veins were chaotic. A vast amount of turbid earth qi continuously clashed and merged, leaking outward to form a fierce, bone-scraping gale within the long, narrow gorge.

That gale, formed from turbid earth qi, possessed a strong destructive power against a cultivator's protective qi barrier. Like blades, a careless cultivator could be torn apart within it. Hence, it was also called Blade Wind.

Above, as the earth qi rose and rubbed against the void, thunder and lightning fields constantly accumulated.

It was precisely because of the year-round Blade Wind and lightning fields that this place was named Wind Thunder Gorge.

Ordinary people would never come here to cultivate.

It wasn't until several hundred years ago that a cultivator, fleeing pursuit, escaped here and lived for a long time, gradually uncovering the mysteries of this place.

Whether it was the Blade Wind or the falling thunder, they only existed in the empty spaces of the gorge.

As long as one didn't recklessly fly through the middle, but instead climbed along the steep cliffs on either side, the risk of being struck could be greatly reduced.

Over time.

More and more cultivators with nowhere else to turn gathered here.

They carved out caves on the cliff walls on both sides, using them as places of cultivation.

Eventually, a large number of rogue cultivators came, attracted by the free, scattered spirit veins.

Everyone who carved out a cave could be called a Cave Master.

And the thirty-six strongest among them were known to the outside world as the Thirty-Six Cave Masters of Wind Thunder Gorge.

At this moment!

Under Cheng Bai's guidance, Luo Chen slowly walked along a simple path carved into the cliff face.

Everything about Wind Thunder Gorge was also being narrated by Cheng Bai, giving Luo Chen an initial impression.

"Blade Wind?"

Luo Chen was greatly intrigued after hearing this.

Under Cheng Bai's terrified gaze, he boldly reached his hand out of the path.

*Chi...*

A gust of wind, as if discovering prey, surged towards him.

"Senior!!!"

Cheng Bai screamed in fright, but the next moment, Luo Chen's hand, extended in the air, twisted slightly and slowly withdrew.

Luo Chen chuckled lightly, "It's a bit itchy."

Cheng Bai was stunned.

The Blade Wind, which made countless cultivators tremble in fear and dare not touch it at all, only made this senior feel "itchy"?

For a moment, his perception nearly deviated, and he even wanted to try it himself.

Fortunately, Luo Chen's next words interrupted his delusion.

"No need to dawdle. Just take me directly to see Cave Master Ming Lei. He must have been waiting for a long time."

As he said this, he looked at Cheng Bai with a faint, knowing smile.

The other man swallowed his saliva. "Senior Ba Xiu, did you know I secretly informed the Cave Master?"

Luo Chen smiled but said nothing more.

He had noticed the other's little movements but simply didn't care.

Suddenly!

A powerful aura erupted violently from a protruding stone platform on the steep cliff wall.

The aura was astonishing, causing many cultivators on both sides of the cliff to turn their heads.

"What's wrong with Cave Master Ming Lei?"

"Could he have broken through in his cultivation?"

"No, it seems like he's actively declaring his presence."

Amidst the murmurs, Luo Chen looked up.

He saw a middle-aged man, spanning over ten li, looking towards him from afar.

His aura was overwhelming, even stirring the wind and clouds, causing a large amount of lightning and thunder to crash down.

But in Luo Chen's eyes, it was merely "blustering".

With merely the first layer of the Sage realm, the reason he could stir the wind and clouds and change the celestial phenomena was solely due to the special geographical environment nearby.

Luo Chen grinned fiercely, and his source power vibrated.

*Boom!*

A wolf smoke of blood and qi shot straight into the sky.

Under his majestic might, the gale retreated, and the thunder fell silent. He forcibly broke through Cave Master Ming Lei's Sage domain.

Then, he took a step forward.

*Dong!*

The void exploded with a bang. The next moment, Luo Chen's figure shot out like an arrow from a bowstring, landing directly on the huge stone platform protruding from the mountain.

"Ming Lei, is this your way of welcoming guests?"

As he spoke, the tall Luo Chen looked down at the diminutive Cave Master Ming Lei before him.

An aura that seemed ready to devour rushed towards him!

Cave Master Ming Lei instinctively stepped back twice, moving out from under Luo Chen's shadow.

His lips parted slightly, and there was clearly a hint of panic in his eyes.

This person before him hadn't revealed any trace of his magical power realm, yet that devouring aura made him mistakenly believe he was facing a primordial ancient ferocious beast!

He took a deep breath and cupped his hands in a salute to Luo Chen.

"Apologies, I was merely testing Fellow Daoist Qi Tian's capabilities."

Luo Chen sneered contemptuously, "And what is the result of the test now?"

"Certainly above mine!" Cave Master Ming Lei frankly admitted his inferiority to Luo Chen.

After all, his prepared Sage domain had been shattered by the other's aura at a single touch, showing the vast gap between them.

But...

Cave Master Ming Lei shook his head. "However, Feng Buyan, the grand elder of the Feng family who covets this Wind Thunder Gorge, is a seasoned Sage. He won't be so easy to deal with!"

Luo Chen snorted, "If he were truly that formidable, could you have held him off?"

Cave Master Ming Lei said frankly, "Outside, I am indeed no match for him. But here in Wind Thunder Gorge, he can't do anything to me either."

As he spoke, his abdomen bulged slightly, like a frog.

The next moment, a thunderclap exploded within his body.

The world seemed to respond, and then lightning flashed and thunder roared in the void. Silver-white bolts of lightning danced wildly like dragons and snakes.

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow, putting away his contempt, and looked outside with a serious expression.

What kind of method was this, allowing his Sage domain to merge with heaven and earth, actually guiding the power of heavenly thunder?

Then, his gaze fell back on Cave Master Ming Lei's lower abdomen.

"A Gu insect?"

Cave Master Ming Lei didn't hide it and said frankly, "Exactly! I possess a relatively special Thunder Spirit Root. A hundred years ago, I refined a Thunder Frog Gu and used it to break through to the Sage realm. With the geographical advantage of Wind Thunder Gorge, I dare to fight even a mid-Sage realm expert! And this is precisely why that old bastard Feng Buyan fears me."

So that's it!

Luo Chen couldn't help but look at the man before him with a bit more respect. Being able to fight across realms, even if it relied on a special Gu insect and favorable timing and location, was still impressive.

But Cave Master Ming Lei wasn't proud of it. Once he left Wind Thunder Gorge, he was just an ordinary early-stage Sage.

Even if his attack methods were a bit sharper, they ultimately couldn't surpass the scope of the early Sage realm.

"Fellow Daoist Qi Tian, lest you don't know, the Feng family is determined to take this Wind Thunder Gorge. That Feng Buyan is still widely inviting fellow cultivators, trying to attack and take it in one battle. Are you sure you want to wade into this muddy water?"

Luo Chen countered, "Don't you want my help, Cave Master?"

Cave Master Ming Lei shook his head. "It's not that I don't want it; on the contrary, I'd be more than happy to have it. But I'm more afraid that at a critical moment, you'll pull out, leaving me to fight alone. Rather than that, it's better to clarify your intentions early. At worst, I'll abandon this place where I attained the Dao and find another place to cultivate."

He was a rogue cultivator who had attained the Dao.

Abandoning a good cave dwelling was certainly a pity.

But as long as he kept his life, with his Sage realm, he could go anywhere under heaven.

So, there was no need to stake his life on a place that wasn't particularly excellent for cultivation.

The help of this unfamiliar strongman, Ba Xiu Qi Tian, was an unexpected joy, but if it became a shackle dragging him down, that would be bad.

Luo Chen had also been a rogue cultivator, so he naturally heard the sincerity in the other's words.

Without much hesitation, he said confidently, "No matter how many people they call, as long as it's not an Ascendant powerhouse, I can handle them all for you."

Cave Master Ming Lei's pupils constricted slightly. Could he be so confident?

Or was it arrogance?

Luo Chen clasped his hands behind his back, paced to the edge of the cliff, and gazed down at the deep, unfathomable scenery below.

"But the prerequisite is that you must offer a reward that satisfies me."

Cave Master Ming Lei thought for a moment and said, "That depends on what you want, Fellow Daoist, and what I have."

Luo Chen smiled slightly. "It's not difficult, not difficult at all!"

...

As the only Sage in Wind Thunder Gorge, Cave Master Ming Lei's cave dwelling was naturally the best among them.

A fourth-rank spirit vein ran through the underground, and the spirit acupoint was dense and rich, right inside his cave.

As a guest who had come to help, Luo Chen was given preferential treatment and was arranged to stay in the best room that very day.

In the dead of night, Luo Chen slowly withdrew from his cultivation state.

He frowned slightly, a look of deep confusion on his face.

"Strange!"

"Although this cave dwelling does have a fourth-rank spirit vein, it's hardly excellent. It's just the most ordinary kind of fourth-rank spirit vein."

"Moreover, because of the chaotic earth qi in Wind Thunder Gorge, the spiritual qi drawn into the cave is quite impure, requiring constant focus during cultivation and refinement."

"A spirit vein of this grade might be a dream come true for a Core Formation cultivator trying to break through to the Sage realm, but for someone like me who has already attained the Dao, it's far from good."

"So why would Feng Buyan, a seasoned Sage, set his sights on a place like this?"

After experiencing it himself, Luo Chen was quite puzzled.

According to Cheng Bai and Cave Master Ming Lei, the Feng family, where Feng Buyan belonged, was the largest force within ten thousand li.

In terms of heritage, it had been passed down for a thousand years!

A force like that must have deep-rooted, excellent spirit veins. Why would they covet a place like this?

After some thought, Luo Chen still couldn't find the answer.

He didn't dwell on it. This place was just his first stepping stone into the Southern Frontier. Through the introduction of local Southern Frontier experts, he would gradually integrate into the high-level cultivator circles of the Southern Frontier cultivation world.

Then, it would be more convenient to inquire about the whereabouts of the Devil Heart Gu King.

He flicked his wrist, and a jade box appeared before him.

Opening it, a sleeping little insect came into view.

"Strength Gu!"

In ancient times, the path of body refinement was prevalent in the Mountains and Seas Realm.

In the Southern Frontier, there was also a special path of body refinement.

By using some special Gu insects, one could increase a cultivator's purest physical strength.

This first-rank Strength Gu was the most common and representative of them.

After a cultivator refined it, they could gain a bit of strength, making their fists and feet more powerful.

After a quick inspection, Luo Chen confirmed that Cave Master Ming Lei hadn't tampered with it. Then, he wrapped it in source power and slowly swallowed it into his abdomen.

The technique circulated, and it was refined in an instant!

Luo Chen clenched his fist. He felt nothing!

But he did sense a subtle improvement.

"It should be because my body refinement realm is too high, so such a low-grade Gu insect has no effect on me."

He savored the process of refining the Strength Gu earlier and deduced the principle by which it increased a cultivator's strength.

Qi cultivation had two paths: one was to absorb the spiritual qi of heaven and earth, taking it from the outside. The other was Wang Yuan's early method of refining essence into qi, extracting spiritual qi and magical power from one's own powerful essence.

This Strength Gu was clearly a Gu insect containing a large amount of spiritual qi within.

After the cultivator refined it, instead of directly converting it into spiritual power or magical power, they relied on their body refinement technique to do the work of "refining qi into essence."

Refining essence into qi was for qi cultivation; refining qi into essence was for body refinement.

It was the reverse!

"Unfortunately, it's useless for me!"

Luo Chen said it was a pity, but there was clearly a look of anticipation on his face.

Since someone could create this kind of Gu insect, and it was still circulating in the world, it meant that the path of body refinement in the Southern Frontier hadn't been completely severed like in the Eastern Wilderness.

A complete cultivation system that was still preserved was the best goal for him!

If a first-rank Strength Gu was useless, what about higher ranks?

"And that secret technique Cave Master Ming Lei mentioned!"

For a moment, Luo Chen recalled a piece of information revealed by the other party during their straightforward conversation that day.

After becoming a Sage, Cave Master Ming Lei naturally wasn't content to just cultivate behind closed doors.

Therefore, he often went out to travel, trying to befriend other Sages in the Southern Frontier and exchange cultivation experiences.

It was during this process that he was noticed by the Feng family's grand elder.

Leaving aside their grievances, the Sage gathering that Cave Master Ming Lei had previously attended was quite noteworthy.

At that gathering, as a rogue cultivator, Cave Master Ming Lei was suffering from a lack of Sage-level cultivation techniques, so he spent a great price to purchase some techniques and secret arts that were useful to him.

*Flash Thunder Art* was one of the gains from that time.

According to him, at that gathering, someone had also offered a high-level body refinement technique. However, due to its extremely harsh and exceptionally dangerous cultivation conditions, no one ended up taking it.

If Luo Chen wanted to go further on this path, Cave Master Ming Lei believed that body refinement technique might be useful to him. At the very least, it could serve as a reference.

As long as Luo Chen helped him resolve the current crisis, he promised to introduce Luo Chen into that circle and help him obtain that high-level body refinement technique.

"I need to enter that circle, and I also need to get my hands on that high-level body refinement technique!"

Luo Chen pursed his lips, slowly closed his eyes, and continued his cultivation.

Even though the spiritual qi concentration here was average, and refining it was troublesome, cultivation was a matter of daily accumulation, a painstaking process that couldn't be neglected for a moment.

...

In another room not far away, Cave Master Ming Lei gently put down the box in his hand.

Cheng Bai was full of anticipation, but seeing this scene, his heart involuntarily sank.

"Cave Master, is the Formless Gu not working?"

Cave Master Ming Lei sighed. "It's not that it doesn't work. If I catalyze it with a secret technique, it can indeed help me cultivate the *Flash Thunder Art*. But there's not enough time."

Cheng Bai was stunned, then immediately thought of the agreed time between the Feng family and Cave Master Ming Lei.

It was indeed almost here!

By then, if Cave Master Ming Lei didn't voluntarily hand over Wind Thunder Gorge, the other side would resort to force.

In such a short time, relying on the secret technique to catalyze the Formless Gu, it would be very difficult to even get started on the *Flash Thunder Art*, let alone master it.

Cheng Bai said cautiously, "With the help of Senior Qi Tian, whom I introduced, we should be able to get through this calamity, right?"

"I'm afraid it will be very difficult!" Cave Master Ming Lei wasn't very optimistic about this upcoming battle.

If he could find helpers, how could Feng Buyan, a seasoned Sage, lack connections?

If Qi Tian hadn't boasted so arrogantly, claiming he could handle anyone short of an Ascendant, he might have had a bit more confidence.

But with such a boast, his impression score was greatly reduced.

In a cultivator's duel, realm was one thing, various magic treasures and secret arts were another, and combat experience couldn't be ignored at all.

Qi Tian was a body refiner, a path that had long been in decline.

Presumably, he was also quite lacking in magic treasures.

And with such a rampant temperament, he was likely to be easily lured into a trap during battle.

"I still need to contact those fellow daoists I met at the last gathering and see if they can spare the time to help me out!"

As Cave Master Ming Lei thought this, he noticed Cheng Bai's eager expression.

He smiled slightly. "You've been following me since before I formed my core. It's been a hundred years now, hasn't it?"

Cheng Bai nodded vigorously.

A hundred years was long, yet also very short.

In the blink of an eye, it felt like yesterday.

Cave Master Ming Lei laughed. "In these hundred years, you have contributed greatly to me. Now you've found me the Formless Gu and introduced me to a helper. You've truly been thoughtful."

Cheng Bai said sincerely, "This is what this humble one should do. Without you, Cave Master, I wouldn't have been able to break through to the Foundation Establishment stage."

Cave Master Ming Lei accepted this gratitude as a matter of course.

But he changed the subject.

"If this Wind Thunder Gorge can't be kept, what will you choose then? Join the Feng family, or flee far away with me?"

Cheng Bai didn't hesitate. "Naturally, I will follow you, Cave Master!"

"You need to think carefully. The Southern Frontier is in turmoil right now. The Life and Death Gate has closed its mountain gate and ignores worldly affairs. Internally, the Fierce Burial Stiff Alliance is causing trouble, and externally, the Tianyuan Dao Sect is making moves. I am a lone man, drifting outside. Following me will only be fraught with danger!"

Cheng Bai's gaze flickered; he was wavering.

Cave Master Ming Lei waved his hand. "Forget it. Go back and think it over again! I also need to use more methods. Wind Thunder Gorge is, after all, the place where I attained the Dao. How could I bear to give it up so easily?"

After dismissing Cheng Bai, Cave Master Ming Lei took out a few message Gu insects. However, after sending them out, they were like stones sinking into the sea, with no reply.

He was, after all, a rogue cultivator with no background. Who could help him?

His dazed, wandering gaze fell upon the lonely jade box on the table.

Inside, the juvenile Formless Gu lay quietly prostrate.

In an instant, a fierce glint flashed in Cave Master Ming Lei's eyes.

"Cheng Bai, come back for a moment!"

(End of this chapter)
