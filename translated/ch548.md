# Chapter 548: Demon Emperor Shed Husk, the Struggle for Xuanyan

The waters around Heavenly Pivot Island.

Half a day after Luo Chen left, although the weather had originally been calm and sunny, the great sea began to surge with violent waves.

As the seawater fluctuated violently, figure after figure emerged from the sea.

Compound eyes and white markings, crab claws and spider silk.

Looking out, they were densely packed Demon Spider Crabs, numbering in the thousands, and there were almost no weaklings among them.

The five enormous Demon Spider Crabs at the head were especially conspicuous.

At this moment, they stepped out over the sea. Several thousand Demon Spider Crabs spread out, directly surrounding the not particularly large Heavenly Pivot Island layer upon layer.

A Demon Spider Crab suffused with an aura nearly at third-tier Perfected Completion moved sideways over. Wherever it passed, all crabs retreated, and hidden reefs shattered.

Finally, its steps stopped on an ordinary patch of ground.

An aged and hoarse voice came from its mouth.

"Is it here?"

Someone beside it said quietly, "It is here. Although it was hidden very well, the area below has already been investigated. It is indeed a cave residence in the style of a human cultivator, and from the looks of it, it was used for no less than five years."

Five years?

The Demon Spider Crab race's patriarch[TERM?] muttered this and slowly nodded.

"It matches the time when changes began in the Xuanyan Sea Region these past few years."

This sea region's former overlord had been the Ring-Headed Turtle race.

But that was built on the premise that the Ring-Headed Turtles had a Demon Emperor[TERM?] at the transformation stage[TERM?].

At that time, the so-called seven royal demon crab races were nothing more than prey for the Ring-Headed Turtles.

Only when that Demon Emperor neared the end of his lifespan over a thousand years ago and shut himself away did the demon crab side gradually begin to rise.

Even so, afterward, in order to seize the overlord position of the Xuanyan Sea Region, the demon crab side also paid a tremendous price.

The seven royal demon crab races were directly reduced to five.

From then on, the many demon crabs became the overlords of this place, and situations where they were hunted were rare.

Even if human cultivators occasionally came to hunt demons, they merely did one job, made a quick profit, and ran. There had almost never been a tragedy where they were hunted on a large scale.

But five years ago, everything changed.

At first, no one paid attention. One or two demon crabs disappearing was truly normal.

But gradually, the numbers grew larger and larger. The Demon Spider Crab race especially suffered heavy losses.

After many ambushes and lures, they finally found out it was done by a jiaolong and a gull joining hands.

Looking at it now!

"So it is again a despicable human cultivator!"

The dense fuzz was gray-white and dry, and the voice he spat out was also aged and hoarse.

The Demon Spider Crab patriarch sighed. "If it is a cultivator, then Little Seven, Zhu Sha[TERM?], and Zhu Mo[TERM?] falling is only natural."

Although he said this, the sorrow in his eyes remained extremely dense.

Zhu Qi was the junior he had regarded most highly.

Although his cultivation was not particularly powerful, his wisdom was not inferior to outstanding humans.

Especially since he was also proficient in the formation dao.

He had even already prepared to pass the position of patriarch to Zhu Qi after a few hundred years, once Zhu Qi cultivated to late third-tier.

He had not expected this kind of incident to happen.

Hearing the patriarch sigh, a late third-tier Demon Spider Crab beside him said quietly, "I have already sent over a hundred sons to follow the aura. However, that person's concealment methods are superb. Even with our race's extraordinary perception ability, I fear it will still take tremendous effort to find one or two traces."

A streak of escaping light flew from afar and landed on the island with a boom.

It was impressively another late third-tier Demon Spider Crab.

"Patriarch, I have already negotiated terms with the Hidden Dust Sand race of the Xuanyan Sea Region. They will help search for the enemy who killed Little Seven and the others."

Hidden Dust Sand was one of the most common demon beasts in the endless Northern Sea.

Calling them demon beasts was not too accurate either.

Strictly speaking, Hidden Dust Sand existed between demon beasts and stone spirits[TERM?].

In ancient times, creatures such as great clams, giant clams, oysters, and sea oysters held sand grains in their flesh. Over time, those evolved into dazzling pearls.

Such pearls were treasures demanded by both the mundane world and the cultivation world.

But because of the workings of the Heavenly Dao and the permeation of spirit qi, pearls could also gain spirituality.

Some special grains of sand, while deriving pearls, would in turn absorb the vitality of hosts such as great clams and giant clams, gradually giving birth to consciousness. Over a long period, they became a different kind of existence between demon beasts and stone spirits.

Some called them pearl demons[TERM?], some called them sea demon seeds[TERM?].

But the most famous name was "Hidden Dust Sand."

This was because this kind of demon beast could not reproduce, but after giving birth to spiritual intelligence and cultivating to high realm, the experts of the Hidden Dust Sand race also developed a sense of race.

They would combine a portion of their own bodies with special grains of sand, separate them, and parasitize them inside creatures like great clams and giant clams, letting them absorb vitality and naturally evolve.

This instinctive action, over time, formed the Hidden Dust Sand race.

This race was spread throughout the Northern Sea. Though hidden in dust, they were like Ganges sand[TERM?], impossible to count. Thus they gained the name Hidden Dust Sand.

Because of their enormous numbers and hidden way of living and cultivating, one could say they were the most developed race in the sea for gathering intelligence.

If someone truly needed intelligence, after paying a certain price, they would mostly seek out the Hidden Dust Sand race, and would often gain something.

At this moment, hearing the clansman say he had negotiated terms with the Hidden Dust Sand race, the Demon Spider Crab patriarch asked in surprise, "You saw that old fellow Yuan Yin[TERM?]?"

The clansman said in a muffled voice, "Not exactly. Although that fellow's strength is not great, his concealment skill is extremely strong, and I could not see his true body. I only passed the message through low-level Hidden Dust Sand on Slippery Bone Island, and the other party also agreed to help search for the enemy."

The Hidden Dust Sand's main base in the Xuanyan Sea Region was precisely Slippery Bone Island, which had large numbers of shellfish to provide them parasitic lodging.

Hearing that he had not seen Yuan Yin, the Demon Spider Crab patriarch shook his head in disappointment.

"Forget it. It is probably just perfunctory again."

Hearing that it was perfunctory, the clansman became somewhat anxious.

"Are we truly going to let that murderer go? He did not merely kill Little Seven and the others. These years, the sons who died under his hands number over a hundred. This is a blood feud!"

The Demon Spider Crab patriarch slowly said, "We definitely must pursue traces of the murderer. Whether on the Hidden Dust Sand side or our side, we must continue. However..."

When he spoke the word "however," his tone became solemn.

"The most urgent matter at present is not this!"

His gaze projected far into the distance, as if passing through ten thousand li and landing on the most remote corner of the Xuanyan Sea Region.

"I obtained news that the Xuanyan Demon Emperor[TERM?] actually reached the end of his lifespan as early as three hundred years ago and went to the Fallen Sea's Black Tortoise ancestral land to return to the void. The Demon Emperor aura emanating from Xuanyan Island is actually only a shed husk[TERM?] he left behind."

The clansman was greatly shocked. "A shed husk?"

The Demon Spider Crab patriarch gave an mm. "Yes. This news came from the Nine-Clawed side. Although I also do not know why he did not take the shed husk with him when he went to return to the void, the Xuanyan Demon Emperor's death is real."

What the death of a region's Demon Emperor meant needed no further explanation!

It meant that the other party's cultivation land, a fourth-tier spirit vein, had been vacated.

If this place were seized, then the many late third-tier great demon kings among the five great royal demon crab races might have hope of breaking through to fourth tier and achieving the transformation-stage Demon Emperor realm!

One could even say that if they obtained the shed husk left by Xuanyan Demon Emperor, they could also concurrently cultivate the inheritance he left behind.

One had to know that the Ring-Headed Turtle race and the demon crab races were actually extremely similar on the cultivation path.

Strictly speaking, the initial cultivation methods of the five great royal demon crab races of the Xuanyan Sea Region had all been obtained from Xuanyan Island!

"On the Overlord race's side, Jin Hun[TERM?] long ago brought Golden Armor to Xuanyan Island. Four years ago, Golden Claw came out of seclusion and also went straight to Xuanyan Island."

"The Azure Emperor race sealed their residence and shut their gates, ignoring the murderer on Heavenly Pivot Island, but in truth, they also quietly went to Xuanyan Island to seek the shed husk."

"Nine-Clawed gave me face, but the news was heavily delayed. Their Nine-Clawed Poison King Crab race is most likely also prepared in advance."

"I do not know whether Red Flame[TERM?] will be moved, but this old man also intends to go to Xuanyan Island and contend!"

At this point, the Demon Spider Crab patriarch moved his eight crab legs and slowly walked toward the great sea.

"This matter concerns the rise of the Demon Spider Crab race, and is above pursuing the murderer. I will go first. Return and make preparations, then bring people over as well!"

Among the ten great elders of the Demon Spider Crab race, there were three great demon kings.

The rest were all at mid and early third-tier.

If they left manpower to guard the clan land and pursue the murderer of Heavenly Pivot Island, they could perhaps dispatch eight third-tier demon king-level existences.

Such a lineup might not compare to the Overlord Crab race with its formidable combat power, but it could absolutely contend with the Azure Emperor Crab side.

And contending for opportunity did not merely depend on strength. It also depended on luck.

The other two great demon kings looked at one another, then also made up their minds.

This time, they must fully assist the patriarch!

...

Above the vast great sea,

a demon-hunting ship drifted alone.

Escaping lights entered and exited from time to time, displaying the mighty strength of this ship.

Inside an incomparably luxurious palace covered in cloud patterns and water patterns,

seven Core Formation cultivators gathered together, discussing a certain topic.

"Strange. Did people not always say the Xuanyan Sea Region had large numbers of Ring-Headed Turtles moving around? Why have we gained almost nothing on this trip?"

"Could those Ring-Headed Turtles have all been slaughtered by demon crabs?"

"No, no, no. Master said that the Xuanyan Sea Region is actually controlled by a Ring-Headed Turtle power with a Demon Emperor-level existence. Even if there were rumors someone saw that Xuanyan Demon Emperor flee into the Fallen Sea, a dead tiger's remaining might is still there. How could the race he left behind be so easily slaughtered clean?"

"No matter what, we must find a way to collect the large number of Ring-Headed Turtle shells Master needs. What should we do next?"

Listening to her senior brothers discuss, Gu Xiaolian hesitated and said, "Why not buy them?"

Toward this naive suggestion, several senior brothers could not help laughing.

"Little Junior Sister, you are thinking wrongly. Materials from the several great turtle races containing Black Tortoise bloodline have always been popular goods[TERM?] for making defensive magic treasures. Normally they cannot be collected, let alone purchased in large amounts."

"Yes. In the past, no one dared come to the Xuanyan Sea Region. We only dared come here for a quick profit because we received the news from Master that Xuanyan Demon Emperor had entered the Fallen Sea. Otherwise, even the chance to hunt in this place probably would not have fallen to our Feiyun Ravine."

Gu Xiaolian's face flushed slightly. She knew her knowledge was insufficient and had caused a joke.

However, her senior brothers were teasing her with goodwill, so she was not angry.

Just as everyone was discussing, the richly dressed young man at the head let out a soft sound of surprise.

At the same time, Gu Xiaolian's expression also changed slightly, and she hurriedly took out a talisman from her storage bag.

As soon as the talisman emerged, it ignited without wind and turned into a pile of ash.

Seeing this scene, the others could not help freezing.

"This is..."

Gu Shaoshang raised his brow. "My third-tier Demon-Suppressing Talisman was broken."

When the secondary talisman was broken, the main talisman naturally had no object of control, so it turned into ash.

One Core Formation cultivator said in surprise, "Eldest Senior Brother is a Core Formation Stage Seven great cultivator. A Demon-Suppressing Talisman cast by you was actually broken in just one day. It is hard to imagine how strong the one who broke the talisman is!"

The others also began speculating based on this information.

"Could it be the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull's master?"

"Having offended such an expert, could they come seeking vengeance?"

Listening to their discussion,

Gu Shaoshang's brows furrowed slightly, and he also sank into thought.

Looking now, that Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull was a spirit beast with a master!

And its master was impressively also a late Core Formation existence not weaker than him.

This point could be seen from the Myriad Souls Banner the other party drove.

"Myriad Souls Banner... could it be a remnant evil of the Primordial Demon Sect?"

Gu Shaoshang murmured. The worry he originally felt gradually dispersed because of this guess.

Their Feiyun Ravine was a part of the Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance.

The Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance was now the number-one force in the Northern Sea and was irreconcilable with remnants of the Primordial Demon Sect!

If the other party truly dared come to his door, Gu Shaoshang would not mind properly teaching them a lesson. Perhaps he could even take the other party's head and go to the Cang Hai Alliance to claim a bounty!

Waving his hand, Gu Shaoshang said solemnly, "Leave this matter aside for now. Return to the matter of hunting Ring-Headed Turtles."

Once he spoke, everyone immediately gathered their minds and quietly listened to what came next.

"Father needs large numbers of Ring-Headed Turtle shells. He must have a use for them!"

"We must complete this matter perfectly!"

"Since we cannot find Ring-Headed Turtles in these ordinary sea regions, then we will go directly to... Xuanyan Island!"

Once these words came out, everyone's expressions changed greatly.

The youngest, Gu Xiaolian, even blurted out, "Elder Brother, you mustn't!"

Gu Shaoshang looked over. Although his face was gentle, his gaze was somewhat cold.

"Little Sister, I can indulge you in other matters, but matters concerning Father's important affairs cannot be treated willfully."

Gu Xiaolian hurriedly waved her hands. "It is not that I am willful. It is just that Xuanyan Island is the Ring-Headed Turtles' main base, and there must be countless experts within. This time, our Feiyun Ravine only brought seven Core Formation cultivators in total. If we attack their door, I fear we will suffer disastrous losses!"

The other Core Formation cultivators swallowed and also nervously looked at the richly dressed young man.

Gu Shaoshang stubbornly shook his head. "This trip must be made!"

Seeing everyone's faces turn pale, his tone softened slightly.

"Of course, I will not have you throw your lives away. We will first go nearby and look, then act according to circumstances. If it truly will not do, I can pay some price and invite a few friends over to jointly enter Xuanyan Island. That is not impossible."

Huu...

Inside the palace, one could clearly hear someone let out a breath of relief.

As expected, Eldest Senior Brother was still Eldest Senior Brother. In conduct and action, he was never blindly impulsive.

Thinking of their earlier cowardice, these Core Formation cultivators of Feiyun Ravine hurriedly spoke, directly saying they would spare no effort in the upcoming mission.

Gu Shaoshang's expression was indifferent, but inwardly he sneered.

Aside from Second Junior Brother, Sixth Junior Brother, and Little Sister, the other three had all brought their arts with them and joined later[TERM?]. They were normally kind and amiable, but at important moments, sure enough, they were not of one heart with the Gu family.

As expected, changing from a cultivation clan into a sect power was a long and heavy road.

Their Feiyun Ravine still lacked some foundation of a Sage upper sect!

Since the matter had already been settled, the demon-hunting ship no longer wandered pointlessly through these ordinary sea regions. It raised high sails, changed direction, and headed straight for Xuanyan Island.

And on a sea surface tens of thousands of li away,

a streak of escaping light broke through the water from the deep sea. Endless waves reflected dazzling brilliance beneath the sunlight, and that escaping light's handsome figure was also revealed.

After its enormous body circled in the air, it seemed to have found a target and chased toward the demon-hunting ship with beating wings.

(End of chapter)
