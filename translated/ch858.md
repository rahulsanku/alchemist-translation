# Chapter 858: Straddling the Golden Tiger, Entering Lingtian, Welcomed by the Multitude

*Thump!*

A dull sound rang out as the vast mist that had once shrouded Flowing Cloud Mountain suddenly dispersed.

Many low-level cultivators among the peaks stared blankly at this scene.

Then, soon enough, someone realized what had happened.

"The Cloud Deep Unknown Grand Formation has been broken!"

"Could it be that Uncle-Master Kang has undone the Supreme Elder's formation?"

"It must be him!"

Figures began to converge toward Flowing Cloud Sage's cave dwelling.

At the cave entrance, an aged figure had already entered impatiently.

He had been here before, so he knew the general layout.

Passing by one unimportant room after another, Kang Ledao finally arrived at the study room.

After searching for a long while, he eventually stopped before a bookshelf.

In a conspicuous spot, a jade slip was placed there.

*Wine God Art!*

Kang Ledao's face lit up with joy. This was precisely the secret Nascent Soul formation method passed down through the Dieyun Sect, and it had indeed been stored in the Supreme Elder's cave dwelling.

With this secret art, combined with the Dieyun Sect cultivators' own techniques, their chances of breaking through to the Nascent Soul stage would surely be much higher.

His persistence over these past few years had not been in vain!

Kang Ledao's nose stung, his emotions complex.

He was happy to have recovered the sect's secret art, pleased that his years of effort had borne fruit, and yet there was also a trace of disappointment that he was already too old to attempt the Nascent Soul stage himself.

"Forget it. As long as the sect has successors, that is the greatest fortune."

Sighing, Kang Ledao carefully put away the *Wine God Art* and turned to leave.

But as he turned, the corner of his eye caught something.

"Oh? Why is there a jade box beneath the *Wine God Art*?"

He hadn't noticed it before, all his attention absorbed by the *Wine God Art*. Only now did he see that jade box.

Curious, Kang Ledao picked up the jade box and swept his divine sense over it. He found no restrictions.

Unprotected?

It seemed like nothing too important.

Thinking this, Kang Ledao opened the jade box.

Inside, three dragon-eye-sized pills lay quietly.

Beside them was a small slip of paper with three hastily scrawled characters.

**[Nascent Soul Pill]**

Kang Ledao felt as if struck by lightning.

Nascent Soul Pill?

The legendary pill that could greatly increase a Golden Core cultivator's chances of condensing a Nascent Soul?

Was it real or fake?

How could something so important be placed so casually, without even setting up any restrictions?

Countless questions came with the immense shock.

But when he saw those three scrawled yet familiar characters, he recognized the Supreme Elder's handwriting.

That was right. The Supreme Elder had always been carefree by nature. Besides his love for drinking, he didn't care much about cultivation. Why would he care about Nascent Soul Pills?

But for the current Dieyun Sect, which had lost its generation of True Men, three Nascent Soul Pills were worth more than everything!

"Our Dieyun Sect has hope of revival!"

Kang Ledao murmured, his expression dazed.

---

Luo Chen had left behind three Nascent Soul Pills.

Merely as a small compensation.

The batch of spirit wine that Black King had stolen from the Dieyun Sect was of enormous value, arguably the most important and greatest foundation of the Dieyun Sect.

If used well, Luo Chen would benefit immensely.

He had entertained similar thoughts of looking after the Dieyun Sect's disciples before.

But after witnessing the sect's bleak situation with his own eyes, he realized that no amount of care could compare to helping them produce one more Nascent Soul Sage!

If they had a Nascent Soul Sage, the Dieyun Sect would not have fallen from the ranks of upper sects, would not have been driven out of the Dieyun Domain.

If they had a Nascent Soul Sage, they could suppress a region, and no petty villains would dare covet them.

If they had a Nascent Soul Sage, united as one, they might return to their peak.

Thus, Luo Chen personally found for them the secret art *Wine God Art*, hidden deep within the restrictions and beneficial for condensing a Nascent Soul, and left behind three Nascent Soul Pills.

"Perhaps I still owe the Dieyun Sect much, but for now, this is the best I can do."

Luo Chen sighed, transformed into a streak of red light, and crossed the boundary between the Dieyun Domain and the Fenghua Domain.

---

On this day.

On Jihua Mountain, a figure had been concealed for a long time.

Suddenly, the mountain's formation dispersed, and the figure drifted inside.

"Brother Luo, you're back!"

Seeing the newcomer, Fenghua Fairy revealed a genuine smile.

Luo Chen made a Daoist salute. "Three years have passed. Congratulations, Fellow Daoist, on your great advancement in cultivation!"

Fenghua Fairy waved her hand with a smile. "It's not much of an advancement. I merely had some free time, reflected on myself a little, and made some changes. But Brother Luo..."

A hint of hesitation appeared in Fenghua Fairy's eyes. She couldn't help but feel a sense of awe from him, as if he had grown much stronger again.

But Luo Chen had already been terrifyingly strong before. Could he have improved again in just three short years?

For Nascent Soul cultivators, combat was often something to be avoided, as excessive fighting could affect one's own cultivation.

But without the tempering of blood and fire, how could the flaws in one's techniques be uncovered?

In these three years, Luo Chen had not only restored his mana and physical body to their peak state but had also created a fire escape technique most suited to himself.

This was reflected in his greater confidence and the ease in his demeanor.

Luo Chen avoided the topic and asked about the Luo Tian Sect's situation.

Fenghua Fairy recounted the details, her tone carrying a hint of praise.

"Although your sect has few disciples, each one has firm resolve and aspires to the Great Dao. Given time, this batch of cultivators, tempered by hardship, will surely become the pillars of the Luo Tian Sect!"

"If a few more Nascent Soul True Men emerge, even my Fenghua Palace might not be able to compare."

Luo Chen smiled modestly. "You flatter me."

But inwardly, he was quite satisfied.

These two hundred or so members of the Luo Tian Sect had been selected from nearly ten thousand at its peak. Their loyalty to the sect was extremely high.

A century of exile had not changed their hearts; living under another's roof had not shaken their resolve.

Twenty years of the most intense warfare had tempered their skills and cultivation to the limit.

According to Fenghua Fairy, in just three years of rest, two more Golden Core cultivators had emerged within the Luo Tian Sect.

One was Yao Mingyue. The other was somewhat unexpected—a female cultivator from Icecastle brought over by Jueqing Fairy.

This was only three years. Given more time, the situation would surely improve!

After a moment of emotion, Luo Chen turned his attention to the current overall situation.

The transmission jade had limited material and could not carry much information. Fenghua Fairy had many things left unsaid.

Now, under Luo Chen's inquiry, she told him the detailed situation.

"That great battle at Lingtian Pass, which lasted nine days and nine nights three years ago, was far more brutal than we imagined."

"Of the tens of millions of cultivators involved, less than sixty percent survived the post-battle tally! Our sects were fortunate to have avoided that war."

"Perhaps this doesn't resonate deeply with you, Brother Luo. Let me talk about the Nascent Soul cultivators at our level."

"First, the five Lingtian domains. The Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion's mountain gate was destroyed, and their sole remaining Nascent Soul sword cultivator perished together with the enemy. Fellow Daoist Qin Baixiang of the Hundred Beasts Sect survived, but his carefully cultivated fourth-rank spirit beast, the Dusk Monkey, was beaten to death by the opposing Demon Emperor. The elder sent to guard the Mu Mansion by the Dongyang Sect fled with severe injuries. Among the five domains, only our Fenghua Domain and the White Crane Domain suffered relatively little damage. But I heard that Crane Emperor of the White Crane Sect is not in good condition either."

"Then there's Lingtian Pass. As far as I know, a total of seven Nascent Soul cultivators died in battle. One of them, you should know—Fellow Daoist Ye Lan, the new sect master of the Divine Talisman Sect."

Hearing the name Ye Lan, Luo Chen was stunned.

That was the junior whom Talisman Elder had placed great hopes in.

He was also the future hope of the Divine Talisman Sect!

For him, Talisman Elder had paid a great price to exchange for a Nascent Soul Pill from Luo Chen.

He had been so promising, and now he had fallen?

Luo Chen dared not imagine how Talisman Elder felt now.

In silence, Luo Chen asked about the Nether Origin Sect's situation.

A smile once again appeared on Fenghua Fairy's face.

"It turns out that the sacred land cultivators aren't as invincible as imagined! Of the seven Nascent Soul cultivators who died at Lingtian Pass, a full five were from the Nether Origin Sect."

Luo Chen was stunned, but then understood.

This result seemed counterintuitive—how could cultivators from a sacred land of Ascendants be so incompetent?

But having personally dealt with cultivators from another sacred land, Luo Chen knew this was normal.

Thanks to their extremely high status in the cultivation world, abundant resources, and best techniques, sacred land cultivators often cultivated very quickly, reached high realms, and had many techniques.

But their overly favorable external environment meant they lacked life-and-death tempering.

To solve this, most sacred lands encouraged internal competition and various competitions.

But in the end, these competitions could not compare to the tempering of blood and fire.

The Nether Origin Sect had sent a large number of Nascent Soul cultivators this time—over thirty!

With such a large base, losing five was perfectly normal.

But Luo Chen knew that the survivors could no longer be treated as "flowers in a greenhouse."

They had experienced war, seen death, and their techniques, along with their combat experience, would be condensed and integrated.

With the sacred land's foundation, these surviving Nascent Soul True Men would surely surpass ordinary Nascent Soul cultivators in the cultivation world.

Losing five might seem like a heavy loss in numbers.

But for the Nether Origin Sect's overall strength, it was actually a great improvement.

Luo Chen shook his head inwardly but did not refute Fenghua Fairy's schadenfreude on the surface.

After all, for this human-demon war, how many Nascent Soul True Men from various Eastern Wilderness sects had died? Far more than five!

They deserved to gloat!

They deserved to kick someone when they were down!

Adjusting his mood, Luo Chen slowly spoke:

"Let's talk about the Ascendant great powers."

The smile on Fenghua Fairy's face gradually faded.

"Back then, Ancient Demon Emperor Ditian, seeing the demons unable to break through for a long time, personally attacked Lingtian Pass, trying to reclaim the Corpse Ghost. It was Black Marsh Ancestor of the Nether Origin Sect who stopped him."

"The two exchanged only one move, neither gaining the upper hand."

"Then Ancient Demon Emperor Ditian ordered the demons to retreat, pulling back hundreds of thousands of li in one go, leaving the empty nine domains as a buffer zone."

"According to reliable information, the human and demon races might, after ancient times, once again sign a truce treaty!"

A truce treaty?

Luo Chen took a deep breath. "Is the information really reliable?"

"Reliable. I got it from Nine Spirit Primordial Lord." Fenghua Fairy spoke with certainty. After a moment of hesitation, she lowered her voice. "And when Lingtian City Lord visited last time, I tested him."

"What did he say?"

"The Eastern Wilderness will once again welcome peace!" Fenghua Fairy said thus.

Luo Chen nodded. Lingtian City Lord had an excellent reputation and should not lie.

Moreover, not only was his realm high, but his seniority within the Nether Origin Sect was also extremely high. He was from the same generation as the Alchemy Saint.

He should have access to the most core inside information.

If the truce treaty was finalized, the Eastern Wilderness might, after ancient times, once again obtain peaceful tranquility.

This peace was also what countless cultivators and major sects had long yearned for.

"Were there any general terms mentioned?" Luo Chen asked.

Fenghua Fairy shook her head. "Lingtian City Lord didn't go into detail, but it should involve negotiations over the ownership of the fifteen lost domains, the conditions for initiating war, and how the two races will coexist in the future."

"When?"

"Probably very soon."

Luo Chen frowned. "Very soon?"

"Yes!" Fenghua Fairy confirmed, a look of astonishment on her face. "That's also why they asked you to see the Nether Origin Ancestor. After all, the Ancient Demon's son is enslaved by you."

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow. "You mean my spirit pet, Di Tu, might also be part of the negotiation terms?"

Before Fenghua Fairy could answer, Luo Chen laughed to himself.

"Indeed. How could the son of an Ancient Demon be reduced to a human's mount?"

Fenghua Fairy hummed in agreement. She had only later learned that the Demon Emperor Luo Chen had enslaved was actually the Ancient Demon's son.

That made the battle that day three points more impressive.

Fighting five Demon Emperors alone!

Killing three, capturing the Ancient Demon's son, and defeating the Great Demon Emperor!

Such a brilliant achievement was no less than that of Thunder Daozi, who had roamed freely across the Lingtian Pass battlefield!

Of course, she also understood why Luo Chen had left in a hurry three years ago.

He didn't want to implicate others, fearing the Ancient Demon's wrath would fall on the Luo Tian Sect and Fenghua Palace cultivators.

The culprit had left; the Ancient Demon's son had stayed.

Even if Ditian came in person, with the Ancient Demon's dignity, he would not vent his anger on low-level cultivators without reason.

"Alchemy Sect Luo Chen is not only powerful but also has feelings and righteousness!"

Fenghua Fairy sighed thus in her heart.

---

Luo Chen did not go to see the Luo Tian Sect members. He returned alone to Daqing Mountain.

As Di Tu looked on in surprise, he said only one sentence.

"Follow me!"

Then, Luo Chen ordered him to manifest a miniature form and carry him to Lingtian Pass.

This time, Luo Chen did not conceal his tracks.

Instead, he openly straddled the golden tiger and traveled through the air.

Wherever he passed, the Demon Emperor's vast pressure terrified countless low-level cultivators returning from their journeys.

But upon seeing the figure on the golden tiger's back, their hearts were greatly relieved, filled with reverence.

Some, with broad knowledge, recognized Luo Chen after three years of rumors.

From afar, they bowed to Alchemy Sect.

Luo Chen smiled, continuing to parade ostentatiously.

For three years, because of Di Tu, he had been fearful and had even fled thousands of li.

Now, by returning Di Tu, he would once again increase his prestige. It was also making the best use of waste.

As for whether Di Tu, after returning, would resent Luo Chen for this "humiliation"?

Heh, whether he hated or not, it wouldn't matter this time!

And Luo Chen deeply understood that the great power's summons was not merely for the truce treaty.

It meant that one of the three most supreme ancestors of the Nether Origin Sacred Land was extending an invitation to him.

Would Luo Chen accept?

The answer, in fact, needed no further words.

"Master, Lingtian Pass has arrived!"

Di Tu's deep voice came from beneath him.

Luo Chen looked ahead. The once majestic and grand ancient pass now had collapsed city walls and was covered in scars.

This was the relatively better West City District.

With his terrifying eyesight, Luo Chen could even see the other districts.

The devastation after the great battle was even more shocking to the eye.

One could imagine how fierce the nine-day-nine-night siege had been.

"Not participating in that battle is both a fortune and a regret!"

Luo Chen murmured, patting the golden tiger.

"Enter the city!"

One man, one tiger, advanced slowly.

Countless eyes followed closely.

"Is that Alchemy Sect?"

Slaying Demon Emperors, repelling Seven Rhinoceroses, invincible and unrivaled.

Straddling the golden tiger, entering Lingtian, welcomed by the multitude.

This scene, filled with countless stories, was destined to be remembered by many cultivators for a lifetime!

(End of chapter)
