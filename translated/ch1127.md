# Chapter 1127: The Sinister Green Lamp

Luo Chen could not be kept.

He had come with the wind, and with the wind he would eventually leave.

This truth, Jing Lan had already understood when Luo Chen proposed going to Green Mountain Town.

She could not force him to stay. She could only agree.

Yet, there were some inexplicable feelings in her heart, which she firmly suppressed.

Luo Chen seemed to know nothing, or perhaps pretended not to know. He simply continued doing what he could.

Autumn harvest—cutting rice, drying grain.

With his help, in just a few short days, the meager harvest was completely gathered.

To celebrate, and also to reward him, Jing Lan specially took time to go to Green Mountain Town, saying she would buy some meat and fetch some wine.

Luo Chen did not idle either. He felled some trees and carried back some dried straw, planning to repair the dilapidated roof.

Under the blazing sun, an old man hobbled past the doorway with a cane, trembling. Seeing the man on the roof, he struck up a conversation.

"That one from A-Lan's family…"

"Just call me Luo Chen."

Luo Chen recognized him. He was a neighbor, surnamed Pu. The villagers called him Third Grandpa Pu.

Earlier, Jing Lan had mentioned that the villager beaten by the new manager of the tobacco workshop was him.

Third Grandpa Pu sat in the shade, avoiding the scorching sunlight, and looked at Luo Chen with satisfaction. "Having a man in the house really makes a difference. In years past, A-Lan would barely have harvested three-tenths of her crops by now. Not like now, with your help. Not only is the grain gathered, but you can also fix up the house. From now on, if you support each other and walk the path together, A-Lan won't be all alone anymore."

Luo Chen seemed unafraid of the sun as he hammered one wooden wedge after another into the beams and pillars.

Hearing the old man's rambling, he asked casually, "Has Jing Lan always been alone?"

Third Grandpa Pu sighed with deep emotion. "Yes, she's an orphan. Abandoned by her parents, raised by the village. The child has a kind heart and is hardworking and uncomplaining. Everyone usually helps her out a bit, which is how she managed to survive. But living alone—what kind of life is that? With you here, I can see she's much happier. Lately, she's even been learning to dress up."

Luo Chen was silent, then changed the subject.

"I've heard Jing Lan mention the Three Great Calamities, but I've never really understood the Annihilation Mist. Do you know anything about it, Elder?"

Third Grandpa Pu scratched his head. "I'm not too clear on that either. I've only heard of such a thing, but in the hundred years this village has existed, no one has ever seen it. They say that whenever the Annihilation Mist appears, a great fog covers the entire world, and all sorts of strange beasts emerge. Our village was built on the mountain, following tradition, to guard against beast attacks."

"Beasts?"

Luo Chen frowned unconsciously.

"Alright, it's too hot outside. I'll head back first. We'll talk another time."

Third Grandpa Pu patted his backside, stood up, and hobbled shakily toward his home.

His movements were very slow. Clearly, the beating from the new manager of the tobacco workshop had been severe. Months had passed, and he still hadn't fully recovered.

Luo Chen crouched on the roof, his gaze looking into the distance. Clearly visible at the foot of the mountain was a figure returning with a smile on her face.

…

Dinner was very sumptuous.

At least, it was the most lavish meal Luo Chen had eaten since arriving in this world.

There was not only vegetables and meat, but also wine.

Although the wine was low in alcohol content and contained no spiritual energy, it was quite smooth on the palate.

A man and a woman ate in silence. Luo Chen did not speak, and Jing Lan did not know what to say.

It was only after they had finished eating and put down their bowls and chopsticks that Jing Lan finally spoke.

"In two days, I'll take you to Green Mountain Town… and also to the tobacco workshop, to pick up some work that can be done at home."

Luo Chen replied without looking up. "Alright!"

After a simple wash, the sky had darkened.

Unlike the heat of the day, the village was quite cold at night, especially near the ground.

Luo Chen wondered how Jing Lan had managed to sleep on that pile of straw in the corner all this time.

He, of course, paid no attention to the temperature. As he had done these past few days, he sat cross-legged on the straw, closed his eyes, and regulated his body.

Using blood and qi to perform the Healing Art was now second nature to Luo Chen.

Most of the blocked meridians in his body had been cleared.

The broken parts had also undergone simple repairs.

Only a few major acupoints were still being restored.

A soft grunt escaped him.

"Hmm…"

Luo Chen's tightly furrowed brow finally relaxed.

"The Divine Court acupoint is open!"

With this, blood and qi could circulate without obstruction.

Even though his magical power was completely empty, relying on his formidable physique and the source blood hidden deep within his flesh, he finally had a third of his self-protection ability.

In Luo Chen's estimation, as long as he didn't encounter an existence equivalent to the Sage realm of the Mountains and Seas Realm, he would not be in mortal danger.

If he were willing to risk severely wounding his physical body again, he could even fight against beings stronger than that.

Across the fire pit, the woman stared blankly at Luo Chen's sharply defined features. When she saw his brow relax, she unconsciously let out a sigh of relief, then turned over and went to sleep.

…

Early the next morning.

Two figures, taking advantage of the dim light of dawn, quickly descended the mountain.

They crossed the great river that lay between the green mountains and headed east.

Unlike the uneven, rugged land on the mountain, the land Luo Chen saw along the way was all flat and vast.

Clearly, this land was far, far better than that on the mountain.

But it was not planted with rice or grain. Instead, it was covered with green plants only as tall as an adult's thigh.

"These all belong to the Green Mountain Tobacco Workshop. They're planted with *midie*. When the *midie* flowers bloom, they bear fruit. Those fruits are the raw material used to make Mi Immortal Smoke."

As they passed a large field of *midie*, Jing Lan explained.

Luo Chen nodded and looked out. The fields stretched almost endlessly before him.

Clearly, the Green Mountain Tobacco Workshop was powerful, able to occupy such a large area of fertile land solely for planting *midie*.

One could imagine the immense profits from the Mi Immortal Smoke made from *midie* fruit.

And according to Jing Lan, *midie* wasn't just useful for its fruit. Its bark, after special tanning, could be made into extremely tough rope called Thousand-Link Rope. This time, when she went to the workshop, she planned to collect a batch of bark to take home and tan into Thousand-Link Rope during her free time.

Green Mountain Town was soon reached.

Luo Chen's first impression was one of surprise.

The outer perimeter of the town was mostly built of piled stone, forming low city walls. It gave a feeling of solidity and security, as if guarding against something.

Instinctively, Luo Chen thought of the "beasts" brought by the Annihilation Mist that Third Grandpa Pu had mentioned.

But although the city walls of Green Mountain Town were dark and ancient, they bore no bloodstains. Clearly, these walls had never been used to repel beasts.

"You go to the workshop on your own. I'll look around."

Jing Lan gave Luo Chen a serious look, then nodded. "Be careful. If anyone questions you, say you're from Half-Mountain Moon Village."

Luo Chen said he understood, then strolled off along the street.

…

Green Mountain Town was large, with nearly a thousand households.

Most had shops facing the street. In the early morning, some people were already setting out their wares.

Luo Chen walked and observed.

His expression did not change, but inwardly he was somewhat disappointed.

"All ordinary things. Nothing related to cultivators."

Clutching at a lingering hope, he continued along the street, which was neither wide nor narrow. Finally, he stopped in front of a small building called the [Myriad Manifestations Pavilion].

Looking up at the signboard, Luo Chen was a little surprised.

Such a small place, yet it bore such an imposing name.

Without hesitation, Luo Chen stepped inside.

As he entered, the wind chime at the door let out a crisp ring.

Then, hurried footsteps approached.

"Esteemed cus…" A middle-aged, fat man with a mercenary face walked out. But when he saw Luo Chen's simple linen clothes, he immediately changed his tune. "Is there something you want to sell?"

Luo Chen said calmly, "Just looking."

The middle-aged man frowned. "Suit yourself."

Luo Chen paid him no mind. He scanned the shelves in the shop, reading quickly.

As Jing Lan had said earlier, there were indeed people in Green Mountain Town who specialized in collecting things—items swept in by the heavenly winds.

This was probably why the shopkeeper had directly asked Luo Chen what he wanted to sell. Someone dressed as shabbily as him couldn't afford to buy anything; they usually came to sell things they had found.

Luo Chen was also curious about what the Myriad Manifestations Pavilion sold.

"Sabers, swords, rulers… rice, beast hides… is this a spirit stone?"

Luo Chen's gaze stopped on a rhomboid crystal. But unfortunately, the crystal was just an empty shell. Its spiritual energy was long gone.

But even an empty spirit stone shell made his spirits lift.

The existence of such things meant that this Great Annihilation Realm must have things related to cultivators.

Luo Chen's gaze continued to sweep. Suddenly, his expression changed slightly.

"Shopkeeper, where did this come from?"

A green lamp hung from his hand, and eerily, a faint, flickering flame had ignited within it.
