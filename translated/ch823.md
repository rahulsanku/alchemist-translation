# Chapter 823: Cruel Demon Arts, an Old Acquaintance Passes Away

The old shopkeeper appeared unremarkable, but in truth his cultivation was not low—he was at the Adept realm.

Out in the world, low-level cultivators would have to respectfully address him as "Superior."

Thus, his discernment was by no means poor!

After obtaining the *Soul-Plucking Essence Art* from the gray-robed man, he had only flipped through it for a moment before instantly realizing the extraordinary nature of this technique!

It recorded a secret art that could extract essence from the souls of living beings.

Souls—something unique to sentient beings.

Seemingly formless and intangible, yet truly existing.

And their uses were many, arguably the most important thing in the process of cultivation, bar none!

The path of immortal cultivation, on the surface, was about refining qi, but in reality, it was about refining the spirit.

From the soul, to the derived spiritual sense, and then after the Adept realm, transforming into divine sense that could interfere with the outside world—this was a complete path of transformation.

If one could achieve the Sage realm, they could even temporarily use their nascent soul as a second vessel for their spirit.

Rumor had it that the Ascendant realm was named "Ascendant" precisely because the nascent soul could transform into a primordial spirit.

At that realm, the spirit and nascent soul completely merged. Even if the physical body was destroyed, the cultivator could rely on the primordial spirit to survive alone, no longer fearing assimilation by heaven and earth, or natural and earthly calamities.

Why had it been said since ancient times that only the Ascendant realm could ascend?

It was precisely because of the existence of the primordial spirit. During the process of ascension, the powerful primordial spirit could lock onto the guiding spiritual light and ascend to the Immortal Realm.

But that was getting off track.

Back to the *Soul-Plucking Essence Art*!

This secret art could refine the soul essence from living beings. Not only high-level existences with accomplished spirits, but even some low-level creatures could have their weak and hard-to-find soul essence extracted using this technique.

Truly vicious and evil to the extreme, a demonic path!

While questioning the gray-robed cultivator, the old man eagerly flipped through the pages.

This tattered little booklet looked ancient, something from who knows how many years ago.

But the secret art recorded within was quite complete. Even the tool needed to refine soul essence, the "Soul-Nourishing Banner," had its manufacturing method written in detail.

The old man quickly realized the importance of this technique.

According to the description, soul essence was quite peculiar. It could be used both for cultivation to increase one's spiritual foundation and for practicing certain sinister soul-path secret arts.

As long as he could produce it, there would be no shortage of buyers!

Perhaps it could even become the treasure of his shop!

As for the biggest problem with refining soul essence—it was definitely not the difficulty of practicing the secret art or making the Soul-Nourishing Banner, but rather the acquisition of raw materials.

But this problem, placed in the current situation, was not a problem at all.

What time was it now?

The war between humans and demons, a racial conflict!

The most intense and direct collision between the human cultivators, the masters of the Eastern Wilderness, and the demon race that had been amassing power for three thousand years.

Every day, who knew how many living beings perished, and how many souls vanished like smoke.

Those were all the best raw materials!

Facing the old man's urgent questioning, the gray-robed cultivator reached out with a wary expression.

"Just tell me, are we trading or not? If not, give it back to me!"

The old man immediately clutched the tattered beast-skin book tightly and said with a flattering smile, "Why be so nervous? I was just asking a couple more questions. What if this secret art of yours was obtained from some famous sect? If I took it, wouldn't I be inviting endless trouble?"

The gray-robed cultivator suddenly understood, then explained, "You don't need to worry about that. I got this book from an ancient cultivator's cave dwelling. I guarantee there won't be any loose ends."

An ancient cultivator's cave dwelling?

The old man carefully studied the beast-skin paper and the handwriting on it, then couldn't help but nod.

It did indeed look ancient.

In terms of the value of this technique, let alone exchanging it for a *Azure Blood Grass* worth three thousand spirit stones, even if the price were ten or a hundred times higher, it would still be completely worth it!

This Initiate Realm junior truly didn't know the value of what he had!

In that case...

"Deal!"

...

The gray-robed man, having obtained the *Azure Blood Grass*, looked delighted. As soon as he stepped out, the shop door behind him slammed shut with a bang, and even the small window on the front was closed.

As if afraid he might change his mind.

But the gray-robed man didn't care at all. The smile on his face gradually faded.

He casually put the *Azure Blood Grass*, which he had previously valued quite highly, into his storage pouch, then turned and walked towards another dark street.

There were many, many such remote trading markets within Lingtian Pass.

Most of the transactions involved resources that couldn't be brought into the light.

There were herbs brazenly picked from demon territory, minerals secretly dug up, and even stolen goods obtained by killing and robbing in the chaos.

Previously, these were all traded underground. It was only recently, because of the measures taken by the Sage of the Eastern Sun Sect, that they had slightly come to light.

Based on information he had gathered along the way, the gray-robed cultivator kept entering and exiting those remote shops within Lingtian Pass.

For most transactions, he chose to barter.

And the item he frequently offered was that tattered beast-skin booklet.

In just a short day or two, he had distributed dozens of copies of the *Soul-Plucking Essence Art*!

And all of this had not been discovered yet in such a short time.

As for what would happen if it was discovered?

...

Inside the pill sect cave dwelling in the western city, the gray-robed man, now with a calm expression, activated the formation, blocking any detection.

Then, his entire body crackled and popped as his figure rapidly grew taller.

His originally sallow complexion also turned fair.

His facial features twisted, finally forming Luo Chen's young and handsome face.

Scanning the remaining beast-skin booklets in his storage ring, he abandoned the idea of continuing to spread the soul-essence refining secret art.

"That should be enough."

"For a secret art like this to work, it needs a large number of people to be effective."

"They will help me continue spreading it."

This was Luo Chen's groundwork for his future continued practice of the *Soul-Splitting Art*.

Splitting off souls would affect the strength of his current main soul, plunging him into a period of weakness.

But there was another drawback that was the reason Luo Chen had suppressed the increase in the number of split souls.

That was the *Great Soul Pill*!

He didn't have the complete method of the *Soul-Splitting Art*, so he couldn't quickly raise the realm of his split souls. He had to rely on the *Great Soul Pill* as an aid.

And to refine the *Great Soul Pill*, he had to use precious soul essence.

The amount of soul essence he currently had was more than enough to support the cultivation of his main soul and two split souls.

But if the number of split souls increased—three, four, five...

By then, even if he had a large number of split souls, their overall quality wouldn't be able to keep up, and they would become a burden instead.

This could actually be seen from Han Zhan's example.

Han Zhan had begun practicing the *Soul-Splitting Art* at the Initiate realm, and within a hundred years of stepping into the Sage realm, he had acquired nine major split souls.

It seemed extremely powerful, but in reality, it was "fragile and easily broken."

As Luo Chen's realm increased, his understanding of Han Zhan's nine split souls from back then became clearer. Each one seemed to have the level of an early Sage realm, but they fell one after another under his *Dragon-Slaying Art*, with no power to resist.

Obviously, the strength of those split souls could not be considered true Sage realm souls.

They were even less comparable to the two split souls Luo Chen had carefully nurtured.

With this in mind, Luo Chen began to prepare for the future.

He spread the soul-essence manufacturing method from the Primordial Demon Sect that he had obtained from Fu Chaosheng, using the environment of war to let those desperate outlaws produce soul essence for him.

Once a large amount of soul essence was produced, someone would inevitably put it up for sale.

Perhaps many high-level cultivators would be interested in soul essence, but they didn't have the correct method to use it.

At that point, Luo Chen could begin to openly harvest it.

As for why Luo Chen had to personally disguise himself to do this, instead of using cultivators from the Luo Tian Sect?

And why he had to go to the trouble of imitating the Hundred Crafts Mountain Master's old methods, using alchemy techniques to age the beast-skin books?

The reason was simple: the *Soul-Plucking Essence Art* was, after all, a demonic secret art!

Using the souls of living beings as materials was heinous and extremely vicious.

Even the Primordial Demon Sect had only distributed it to their vassal forces back then, rather than doing it themselves, which showed its harmfulness.

The Luo Tian Sect was fragile compared to the Primordial Demon Sect.

If he let his subordinates do this, once discovered, the consequences would be endless.

Even the reputation Luo Chen had painstakingly built would easily be stained with blemishes.

"It's a bit underhanded, I suppose. But for the sake of longevity, it's not worth mentioning."

Luo Chen smiled faintly and temporarily set the matter aside.

His attention then turned to the refinement of the *Heart-Inquiring Mirror*.

He unfolded his attribute panel.

With a thought, he spent ten achievement points and directly mastered it!

In a vague feeling, some new experiences emerged.

"I should take advantage of this free time to refine this thing. As for the required materials..."

Luo Chen's eyes narrowed slightly as he made some calculations in his mind.

...

Sometimes, a pillow arrives just when you're feeling sleepy.

And coincidentally, many people were thinking of handing him a pillow right now!

That day.

Jueqing Fairy had just seen off an Adept realm cultivator and walked back holding a visiting card.

There was a look of helplessness in her eyes.

Yet another Sage had sent a disciple to deliver a visiting card.

Over the past five years, there had been many such cards, and quite a few were from people she couldn't easily offend.

Especially today's one.

"Sage Lu of the Eastern Sun Sect, who claims to have an old acquaintance with him, has come specially to invite him. But he's in seclusion, and I can't very well agree on his behalf."

Just as Jueqing Fairy was feeling troubled, the mist shrouding the mountain suddenly dispersed.

She was stunned for a moment, then instantly realized what was happening. She immediately mounted her flying magic and flew up.

...

Inside the cave dwelling, Luo Chen was flipping through a stack of visiting cards.

[To the Supreme Pill Sage of the Luo Tian Sect: I have long heard of your great name, skilled in alchemy and medicine, with no lack of expertise... If you have time, I hope we can meet and I humbly request your guidance. Yao Ping, First Seat of the Red Dan Peak, Seven Peaks Mountain]

Seven Peaks Mountain was an early Sage-level sect from the Eastern Wilderness's thirty-six domains.

Its founding time was roughly the same as the Eastern Sun Sect, and it had also participated in the ancient human-demon war.

But by now, it had long declined, no longer having the grand scene of seven peak first seats all being Sages.

However, Yao Ping, the first seat of the Red Dan Peak, did have some alchemy skill and was respectfully called "Master Yao."

His visiting card seemed to be asking to consult Luo Chen about alchemy, but the tone inside wasn't very good.

It was full of flattery meant to set Luo Chen up, and also hinted at wanting to compete with him.

Luo Chen only glanced at it twice before setting it aside.

With his current status and reputation, he couldn't be bothered to compete in alchemy with others.

Winning was to be expected, and losing would only enhance the reputation of this declining Seven Peaks Mountain.

He opened another visiting card.

This one was familiar. It was from some force wanting to ask Luo Chen to refine a type of cultivation pill that their sect had lost.

Luo Chen would generally refuse such requests.

Cultivation pills often required more than just one or two batches.

They needed large and stable supplies, which would take up too much of his time.

Even if this force was willing to pay a large number of spirit stones and the *Scarlet Firmament Qi* he needed.

But Luo Chen still carefully read the contents of the visiting card, stroking his chin with a thoughtful expression.

*Scarlet Firmament Qi*, a resource that only Sages could collect, was extremely precious, but it seemed it wasn't actually that rare?

Most Sages could produce one or two strands.

Later, he even discovered that the great technique of the Nine Spirit Primogenitor, that river of light, was actually the product of condensing nine types of *Scarlet Firmament Qi* with magic power.

That was why that technique was so domineering, even the physical body of the Thunder Prison Roc was ground away by it.

The Nine Spirit Sect, where the Nine Spirit Primogenitor belonged, wasn't very ancient. With their foundation, they could accumulate a large amount of *Scarlet Firmament Qi* for the Nine Spirit Primogenitor to cultivate this technique, and even have enough left over to trade some to Luo Chen.

What about the more ancient Seven Peaks Mountain, the Eastern Sun Sect, or even the Ascendant Sacred Lands?

Had his circle of friends been a bit too limited before?

Luo Chen pondered, then asked casually, "Wasn't it Xu Xiao Liu who used to deliver the visiting cards, and you who received the visitors? Why are you personally delivering them today?"

Although Jueqing Fairy had joined the Luo Tian Sect and had accepted the reversal of their status, she still subconsciously avoided meeting him most of the time.

Embarrassment!

Her personally delivering the cards today was rather unusual.

Unexpectedly, upon hearing this, a complex expression appeared on the woman's cold face.

She said softly, "He has passed away."

Luo Chen was stunned, his movements freezing for a moment.

"When?"

"In the third year of your seclusion. He died of old age, his wife and daughter saw him off, and his body was buried in Bai Li Qingchuan. Sect Leader Li, in the name of an elder, broke the rules and had his spirit tablet enshrined in the Luo Tian Sect's ancestral hall."

Luo Chen opened his mouth, but in the end, only let out a soft sigh.

Five years—for someone of his realm, it was merely an insignificant period of quiet cultivation.

And the goal wasn't even cultivation, but merely breaking through the bottleneck between the third and fourth layers of the Sage realm.

Yet, just like that, a person by his side had quietly reached the end of their lifespan and passed away.

If he were to break through a major realm in the future, which would take decades or even centuries, how many familiar faces would still be there to celebrate for him when he emerged from seclusion?

Shaking his head, Luo Chen quietly picked up the next visiting card.

And this one was exactly the pillow he needed.

"Sage Lu of the Eastern Sun Sect!"

Jueqing Fairy explained from the side, "This person claims to have an old acquaintance with you, so even though his card only invites you to a gathering, I still singled it out."

Luo Chen hummed in acknowledgment, confirming the matter.

He did indeed have an old acquaintance with Sage Lu, though it was only a brief meeting.

Back when he borrowed the teleportation formation from the Eastern Sun Domain to the White Crane Domain, Gu Zuokai, the intermediary, had sought out Sage Lu of the Eastern Sun Sect.

At the time, Luo Chen had even specially gifted him a bottle of high-quality fourth-tier *Hongyuan Pills*.

He still remembered that Sage Lu had been quite tempted back then, repeatedly asking Luo Chen if he had any intention of continuing to sell this pill.

"The Eastern Sun Sect has a very deep connection with the Nether Origin Sect, and can even be seen as a directly subordinate vassal of the Nether Origin Sect."

"Therefore, as soon as this Elder Lu arrived at Lingtian Pass, he received strong support from the Lingtian City Lord, and encountered no obstacles in doing anything."

"He promoted the formation of the Lingtian Trading Fair, established the relevant rules, and even sent people to maintain order. He has a very good reputation among low-level cultivators. Not only that, but he also regularly holds exchange meetings for the Adept realm and Sage realm in the name of the Eastern Sun Sect."

"He really wants you to attend one in person!"

As Jueqing Fairy narrated, Luo Chen learned the full picture of the matter.

And that kind of exchange gathering was precisely the perfect environment for high-level cultivators to exchange resources with each other.

Luo Chen didn't hesitate and immediately asked:

"When is the next gathering?"

"Three days from now!"

"Good! You reply on my behalf. Luo will definitely attend on time."

(Chapter End)
