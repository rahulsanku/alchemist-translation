# Chapter 1088: Three Demons Ascend Together, Lord of Green Parasol Mountain

"Kongchan, stay in the Northern Sea and investigate the situation regarding Black Devil and the Gu siblings for me."

Leaving behind these words, Luo Chen hurried away under Kongchan's bewildered gaze.

The super teleportation formation on Green Parasol Mountain was far-reaching, with established teleportation points spanning almost all five continents of the former Mountains and Seas Realm.

From what Luo Chen knew previously, there was one point in the Xingtian Sea region of the Northern Sea and two at the Buddha Mother Temple in the Western Desert.

Not far from the Northern Sea, near a hidden location close to the Central Continent, there was also another teleportation outpost.

This was intelligence Luo Chen had only grasped after becoming the Lord of Truth Crossing Hall.

Now, with Pure Crane's transmission arriving, Luo Chen did not hesitate to activate this teleportation formation.

During his journey, Luo Chen's expression was gloomy, his heart filled with confusion.

Regardless of how the outside world speculated about Rosycloud's situation, at least in his view, it was absolutely not good!

Having endured several major battles, each time contending against complete holy lands, the terrifying foundation of a Divine Transformation holy land had left severe injuries on her body.

Especially the battle at Broken Soul Cliff!

The Five Elders of Heavenly Origin had used the spirit treasure Burial Bone Umbrella to set up a grand formation, fighting her to the death, and at the final moment had awakened the Void Refinement Great Desolate Soul-Howling Desolation!

That was an existence capable of injuring Rosycloud's primordial spirit.

Now, after only a little over a hundred years of recuperation, she was completely healed?

Enough to withstand the pressure of ascension?

Luo Chen harbored great doubts about this.

Under Luo Chen's full-speed flight, disregarding mana consumption, it took only half a day to find the teleportation outpost, hidden deep within an undersea trench—quite secretive.

After inspecting it and confirming there were no issues, Luo Chen held the Truth Crossing Great Seal and activated it in an instant.

As with the last time he rode the Northern Sea teleportation formation, he remained conscious throughout the process.

And this time, he saw even more clearly how he was shuttling through the void—one spatial node after another appearing before his eyes, layer upon layer of spatial ripples spreading out like ripples.

"Short-distance teleportation never produces this phenomenon. This kind of cross-continental ultra-long-distance teleportation, on the contrary, allows me to clearly perceive the changes within. Somehow, the spatial talent Azure Frost bestowed upon me seems to have grown again."

But at this moment, Luo Chen had no intention of dwelling on these trivial details.

Guarding his mind tightly, when he reappeared, he was already in a different world.

The moment he stepped out of the teleportation formation, a colossal force pressed down heavily.

Before Luo Chen could react, he stumbled two steps involuntarily.

This shocked him!

What kind of pressure could cause him to lose his composure like this? Not long ago, he had faced the great power Liancheng directly.

Not only that, but the surroundings were filled with invisible pressure, preventing even his divine sense from extending too far.

"Could the ascension have already begun?"

Luo Chen's expression turned grave as he quickly moved toward the ground.

As soon as he emerged from Truth Crossing Hall, he saw demon cultivators sitting cross-legged on the ground, all looking up at the distant sky.

Luo Chen advanced step by step, his pace swift, causing those demon cultivators to stare wide-eyed.

Under the terrifying pressure before ascension, they could not even stand, let alone walk, yet Luo Chen moved as if flying.

But thinking of Luo Chen's feat twenty years ago, it seemed only natural.

As he advanced, Luo Chen looked up at the sky, his brow furrowing deeper, his eyes filled with confusion.

Wrong!

Completely wrong!

He had personally witnessed the ascension of Divine Transformation great powers, and more than once.

But the situation above Green Parasol Mountain at this moment felt very off.

"Pure Crane!"

Luo Chen's footsteps stopped on the empty square in front of Truth Crossing Hall, where Pure Crane was sitting cross-legged with difficulty.

Hearing Luo Chen's voice, Pure Crane said joyfully, "Hall Master, you're back."

"Mm." Luo Chen looked down and asked, "Tell me, what's going on?"

Without hesitation, Pure Crane recounted the changes over the past few days and even the last few decades.

"A few days ago, Vice Hall Master Nether Spring told us directly that they would be ascending soon and that we should not wander around recklessly in the coming days."

"Upon hearing this news, we were all caught off guard."

"But in reality, over these decades, Green Parasol Mountain has been undergoing subtle changes. Spiritual energy from tens of thousands of li around has been drawn into the Profound Truth Hall. The Primordial Lord and the others must have already made preparations."

Luo Chen listened intently, his face taut.

Suddenly!

He caught a key phrase.

"Them?"

Before Pure Crane could answer, Luo Chen abruptly looked up at the void, golden light blazing in his eyes as he fully activated his Crimson Eye Golden Pupil.

*Boom!*

Green Parasol Mountain shook violently.

An ancient aura spread out, as if this great mountain that had stood in the Eastern Wilderness for countless years had come alive.

A domineering aura erupted from within Profound Truth Hall.

Amidst swirling clouds and howling wind, a solid figure appeared before everyone's eyes.

It was a majestic phoenix!

Its body was covered in multicolored feathers, bathed in a red halo, and atop its head was a ring of golden flame condensed into a crown.

It merely hovered in the void, barely fluttering its wings, yet it gave the illusion of being about to ride the wind away.

But at this moment, Luo Chen had another strange feeling.

Yes!

Rosycloud's primordial spirit was now released to the limit Luo Chen had ever known.

Whether it was the newly ascended Divine Transformation alchemy saint Chu Yan back then, or the half-step Void Refinement Youli Divine Lord, their primordial spirits were far inferior to Rosycloud's at this moment.

But?

"There's no spiritual opportunity!"

"Why is there no spiritual opportunity!"

Luo Chen stared fixedly at that spot, blood vessels crazily spreading in his golden-glowing eyes, as if trying to see everything clearly.

Yet in his observation, the most abnormal anomaly appeared.

When a Divine Transformation cultivator ascends—whether actively or forced by reaching the limit of their realm—the world would draw in a vast amount of spiritual opportunity to aid the ascension.

Its name—Radiant Ascent!

Alchemy Saint Chu Yan was like this, and so was Youli Divine Lord.

But after Rosycloud released her primordial spirit, she did not attract any spiritual opportunity. Instead, strands of electric light began to spread across the nine heavens.

Thunder Tribulation!

"Why are there signs of thunder tribulation again?" Luo Chen bit his lip, his face tense. "When Chu Yan ascended back then, it went smoothly. It was only because the Dao Sect Master Mo Shouzhuo forcibly intercepted a portion of the spiritual opportunity into the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven that thunder tribulation signs appeared. Now, why is Rosycloud facing the same?"

Just as Luo Chen was frantically pondering, another change occurred in the outside world without his notice.

*Hum...*

A faint blue light spread from within Profound Truth Hall.

That blue light was so weak, like roadside weeds, easily overlooked.

But as it continuously spread and rose, it became tougher and denser, until it filled the entire space, even covering Rosycloud.

The majestic fire phoenix offered no resistance, allowing the blue light to envelop her.

After this, the thunder tribulation signs in the sky seemed to lose their target, showing signs of dissipating in confusion.

"This blue light?"

Luo Chen was stunned.

"It's Lady Azure Grain Immortal." Pure Crane rarely showed reverence in her eyes. Even toward Rosycloud, she only felt fear, but under that blue light, she was filled with respect. To have cultivated from the weakest Azure Grain Grass in the Million Great Mountains, step by step, to the sentient Divine Transformation stage—even if her combat power was not strong, she was still worthy of respect.

Pure Crane shifted her gaze, looking at Luo Chen's raised chin.

"Didn't you want to ask who 'them' refers to?"

"The answer is right before you."

"This ascension is not of one person, but of three!"

As if to confirm her words, the next moment, a clear cry resounded through the universe.

A white light broke through the air, shooting straight into the nine heavens.

*Boom!*

With a single charge, the inexplicable thunder tribulation dissipated into nothingness.

The white light swiftly returned, revealing a slightly illusory, handsome blue bird figure.

She elegantly soared in the air, circling the motionless giant fire phoenix.

As she danced, as the blue light spread, the world became peaceful once more.

Especially under Luo Chen's Crimson Eye Golden Pupil, he detected countless spiritual opportunities surging from all directions, turning into patches of rosy clouds, falling upon the soaring blue bird, upon the swaying grass, and also upon... the divine fire phoenix!

Luo Chen suddenly clenched his fist.

Vaguely, he seemed to understand something.

Rosycloud was not a native demon cultivator; she was a great demon descended from the Mystic Realm.

She not only plundered the power of this world but also stirred up several great wars, destroying mountains and burning seas, turning heaven and earth upside down.

If the Mountains and Seas Realm had a potential consciousness, how could it let her leave, let alone help her ascend?

In Luo Chen's view, if Rosycloud stirred up trouble again, this world might very well unleash the most severe heavenly punishment, obliterating her body and soul.

But Azure Frost and Azure Grain Immortal were genuine native beings.

Their success was gratifying. If they were to travel far, the heaven and earth, as their parents, would naturally give them "travel expenses."

Sending spiritual opportunity, lifting with rosy clouds—that was the greatest help.

Rosycloud must have used some method to bind the two with herself, confusing the heavenly mechanism, intending to ascend together.

"But can relying solely on the spiritual opportunity drawn by two Divine Transformation cultivators help her escape this realm and ascend to the Immortal Realm?"

After the shock, Luo Chen began to doubt whether Rosycloud could succeed.

The amount of spiritual opportunity drawn during Radiant Ascent was indeed vast, but it was usually only enough for one person.

Azure Frost and Azure Grain Immortal ascending together might be different, but the scope would not be much larger.

At least from Luo Chen's observation at this moment, the amount of spiritual opportunity outside the three primordial spirits was only two or three times more than when Chu Yan ascended, far from reaching a qualitative change that could lift another person.

"Wait!"

"What if it's not ascending to the Immortal Realm?"

"Profound Truth Hall... Mystic Realm!"

Just as Luo Chen was making this bold guess, a wisp of starlight came from afar, landing within Profound Truth Hall.

That starlight seemed to have a guiding force, forming a massive pillar of light with Profound Truth Hall.

Three figures sat facing each other inside, slowly rising.

Outside the pillar of light, the three massive primordial spirits, as if summoned, all flew back into their bodies.

Bathed in the brilliant starlight, the three figures slowly flew into the sky, and a door had already opened a crack in the nine heavens.

Luo Chen hoarsely uttered two words: "Star Gate!"

Yes, if he wasn't mistaken, that familiar starlight passage was exactly the one that appeared when Alchemy Saint Chu Yan ascended, formed by the Star Gate.

Moreover, if he wasn't wrong, Rosycloud's plan was actually very simple: borrow the spiritual opportunity drawn by Azure Grain Immortal and Azure Frost's ascension to help her escape this world's suppression, then use the Dao Sect's spirit treasure Star Gate to open a path to the Mystic Realm. In this way, Rosycloud could leave this small world cage that had constrained her everywhere, like a caged canary.

Her past actions were all for seizing the Star Gate.

Forcing her way into the Nether Origin Sect to rescue Azure Grain Immortal, vigorously cultivating Azure Frost—the purpose was also to borrow the power of their ascension.

Luo Chen had long anticipated the matter of seizing the Star Gate.

But the latter—only at this moment did he suddenly realize it.

However...

Luo Chen unconsciously touched his own heart, feeling a slight melancholy.

"Too hasty."

"I haven't yet reached Divine Transformation, haven't yet undone the life-and-death gu with Azure Frost."

"Now, I haven't even said a word of farewell face to face, and we're already separated like this."

As if by telepathy, just as Luo Chen was feeling this melancholy, the silent, blue-robed woman sitting cross-legged in the starlight passage opened her eyes and looked at him from afar.

Across a thousand li, their eyes met—everything was said without words.

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow, then cupped his hands in a bow.

"I wish you well in ascending to the upper realm. May we meet again in the Immortal Realm!"

The others, not knowing the truth, now offered their congratulations.

"We congratulate the Primordial Lord on her Radiant Ascent!"

They only thought the three were ascending to the Immortal Realm, carried by rosy clouds as in the legends.

But in reality, they had only gone to a larger Mystic Realm.

When the starlight passage gradually faded and the three figures finally disappeared, the terrifying pressure that had accumulated for tens of thousands of li around Green Parasol Mountain suddenly vanished.

Everyone felt relieved.

Countless birds let out joyful cries, forming a grand procession, soaring above Green Parasol Mountain.

A hundred birds paying homage to the phoenix—but unfortunately, the phoenix had already departed.

Pure Crane's excitement gradually calmed. She stood up. "Hall Master..."

"Wait here!"

Leaving these words, Luo Chen floated up and headed toward the towering Profound Truth Hall.

Behind him, a group of demon cultivators looked at each other, their hearts inexplicably filled with unease.

Rosycloud had already ascended, and with her went Azure Frost and Azure Grain Immortal, two great powers.

Thus, Green Parasol Mountain no longer had a single Divine Transformation great power. Who should they serve as master now?

Pure Crane seemed to sense the restlessness in others. She turned sharply and glared at everyone.

"The Lord of Truth Crossing Hall is still here. Do any of you harbor second thoughts?"

"From now on, the master of Green Parasol Mountain is Luo Chen!"

"Who among you dares to disobey?!"

Under her glare, everyone bowed their heads.

Or rather, what made them bow their heads was more Luo Chen's formidable martial power, demonstrated more than once.

...

*Whoosh!*

The clouds scattered like cotton wool as a figure landed before the gates of Profound Truth Hall.

His gaze fell upon a woman kneeling before the gate.

Even kneeling, she displayed a graceful figure, especially the round, full moon-like buttocks that contrasted sharply with her slender waist.

"Moon Slave pays respects to the Lord of Truth Crossing Hall!"

Luo Chen squinted, staring at this woman.

"Raise your head."

The woman slowly lifted her head, revealing a pure face like a moon palace fairy, a stark contrast to her naturally alluring, fiery figure. But at this moment, her eyes were filled with fear.

"Moon Wanderer, you were the Primordial Lord's moon-viewing maid. Now that the Primordial Lord has ascended, you are free to leave Green Parasol Mountain. Why are you still here?"

"Without orders, Moon Slave dares not leave."

"Is it that you dare not, or that you are unwilling?" Luo Chen asked with a half-smile.

Moon Wanderer lowered her head in fear. "The Hall Master is overthinking."

"Hmph!"

Luo Chen snorted coldly, too lazy to bother with her for now.

With both hands, he pushed open the heavy gates.

Without any hesitation, Luo Chen strode inside.

Behind him, Moon Wanderer cautiously raised her head, but only slightly, just enough to vaguely see Luo Chen's back.

The fear in her eyes had long vanished, replaced by a hint of greed and jealousy.

Inside the main hall of Profound Truth Hall, it was already empty.

Even Nether Spring, whom Luo Chen had expected to find, was not there. Perhaps she had also followed Rosycloud and the others.

At the top of the hall, on the soft couch before the low table, a single cyan feather lay quietly.

Luo Chen sat upright on the soft couch, took a deep breath, and picked up the cyan feather.

The message stored within flowed into his mind like a cold spring stream.

[Luo Chen, when you see this, I have already left the Mountains and Seas Realm...]

(End of chapter)
