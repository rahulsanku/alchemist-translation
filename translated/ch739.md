# Chapter 739: Withered-Source Fire Marrow, True Flame Advances

The four jade bottles were different colors.

Luo Chen was very familiar with the two largest ones. Their bodies were glazed, clear and transparent, with wisps of golden mist coiling within. These were the glazed jade soul bottles [TERM?] specially used in the Northern Sea to preserve soul essence.

The soul essence Old Monster Netherfire had traded to Luo Chen before had also been stored in this kind of special bottle.

Clearly, this was the reward Fu Chaosheng had promised in the contents of their cooperation.

Only, at the time, the agreement had been that after the matter was accomplished, he would send the remaining two bottles to Luo Chen.

But now, the moment they arrived in Central Continent, the other party took them out without the slightest hesitation.

Luo Chen felt a trace of joy in his heart, but his brow furrowed slightly.

"The matter has not yet been accomplished, yet Fellow Daoist Fu is so at ease handing me the payment in advance?"

Fu Chaosheng's expression was solemn. "What is there to be uneasy about? Will you leave without saying farewell and abandon your promise? Or when the time comes, will you show up but not work, and handle the matter perfunctorily?"

Luo Chen was speechless.

He truly was not that sort of person.

However, Fu Chaosheng truly did judge people accurately!

Since that was so, he would accept them without worry.

Compared to the Hongyuan Pill, the Great Soul Pill was much more difficult to refine. Even with the guarantee of system proficiency, the proficiency still rose relatively slowly.

Luo Chen estimated that the previous bottle would be exhausted after another few years of use.

With these two bottles as replenishment, he would not panic at all. Moreover, he could refine pills freely, improve his Great Soul Pill refinement proficiency, and strive to consume high-grade Great Soul Pills as soon as possible.

Afterward, he looked in puzzlement toward the other two jade bottles.

These two bottles were also extremely different in color.

One was pure white, and the other deep red.

"And these are?"

"Taihe Pill [TERM?], Flameflow Marrow [TERM?]."

Confusion appeared on Luo Chen's face.

Fu Chaosheng did not keep him in suspense and directly explained the origins and uses of the two kinds of pills and materials one by one.

"The Taihe Pill is one of my Tianyuan Dao Sect's most famous high-tier pills, specially provided for Sage cultivators to consume. It can greatly increase magic power."

"This pill has hidden dangers. If too much is taken and not thoroughly refined, it will cause the Purple Mansion barrier to thin, and even the Nascent Soul body will become mixed and impure."

"Therefore, it was already abandoned five hundred years ago. The Taihe Pill currently circulated in the sect is actually the improved version created by Alchemy Saint Chu Yan. That kind of pill is supplied only to the sect's core Sages."

"However, this is also a good thing. Many of the old-version high-grade Taihe Pills abandoned back then were divided among our great families."

"The bottle of Taihe Pills I am giving you is the final bottle remaining in this old man's hands, and its grade is high-grade. I know my Dao path has no further hope. If Qinglan performs well in the grand competition, she will naturally have the sect-issued new Taihe Pills, and will have no use for this object. I might as well take it out as a personal favor to help you improve your cultivation in the short term."

"You need not worry about its hidden danger either. If it is only one bottle, spending a bit more effort refining it will not affect your foundation."

Luo Chen grew interested and opened that jade bottle.

Sure enough, a rich and surging medicinal power, so dense it could barely be suppressed, seeped out from within.

Luo Chen pinched a white pill. Before consuming it, he could not sense its true effect, but purely from the surface, it seemed to surpass his low-grade Hongyuan Pills by quite a bit.

As he observed the Taihe Pill, Fu Chaosheng's words came to his ears.

"Flameflow Marrow comes from the Western Desert. It is the remnant product of a flame river in the boundless desert that once gave birth to a fifth-tier source-less fire [TERM?]. Its own might is not high, for the essence was all absorbed by that fifth-tier fire spirit. But this Flameflow Marrow has the trait of promoting a cultivator's source true flame advancement."

"In earlier years, you were famous throughout the Northern Sea under the title Devil Lord Azure Yang, and this old man has long heard of your fire-control arts. With this Flameflow Marrow, perhaps your source true flame can advance another level!"

Luo Chen froze.

It was not that he doubted the truth of Fu Chaosheng's words.

It was only because, based on the other party's description, he found the corresponding medicinal material name in the alchemy classics left behind by Devil Lord Heaven-Refining.

Its name was Withered-Source Fire Marrow [TERM?]!

When a certain source-less fire produced powerful spirituality and left, this kind of resource would be born at the withered source.

Devil Lord Heaven-Refining called it Withered-Source Fire Marrow, while the Mountains and Seas Realm called it Flameflow Marrow.

The former was a general term. Clearly, this kind of resource did not exist only in that one place in the Western Desert. Other powerful fifth-tier fire spirits could also give birth to this kind of fire marrow.

For example, in Blazing Refinement Prison, within Vermilion Bird Mountain!

Fu Chaosheng was actually willing to give such a precious treasure to him for free?

It seemed he was staking everything on this five-hundred-year grand competition for his granddaughter!

In addition, judging from the Central Continent family competition the other party had described, the pressure was likely enormous as well, which was why Fu Chaosheng continued increasing the stakes beyond the contents of their cooperation.

At this moment, Luo Chen should have said, "You have been thoughtful."

But when his hand touched the deep-red jade bottle containing Flameflow Marrow, the words at his lips suddenly changed.

"Within Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, Luo will certainly exert his full strength!"

The moment the words left his mouth, he saw the solemn expression on the old man's face across from him bloom like a flower, revealing a brilliant smile.

Clearly, these were the words Fu Chaosheng wanted to hear.

...

Wangwu Mountain Range [TERM?].

The Fu family's clan land was located here.

It was extremely close to Star Island and extremely far from the Tianyuan Dao Sect.

From this, one could also see that although the Fu family bore the name of an Aristocratic Clan, it was already ranked last among the Thirty-Six Aristocratic Clans.

Otherwise, its clan land should have been in the central region of Central Continent, where the essence was richest.

But one could not say it was far from the Tianyuan Dao Sect either.

The entire Central Continent was the Tianyuan Dao Sect's mountain gate, so how could there be any such thing as distance?

On a mountain peak wreathed in clouds and mist, Fu Chaosheng watched the cave abode's doors slowly close, a relieved expression appearing on his face.

Fu Hongzhang stood beside him, but instead wore a pained expression.

"Family head, why go this far?"

Fu Chaosheng clasped his hands behind his back and leisurely walked down the mountain.

"Why can we not do this?"

Fu Hongzhang followed step by step behind him. "Let us set aside the previous cooperation. Even if we suffered some losses, compared to Qinglan's future and the family's honor or disgrace, I can accept it. But you have delivered payment early, gifted pills for free, and now you have even lent him the fourth-tier cave abode you use for your own cultivation. This investment is truly somewhat too great. Oh, and the trouble caused by Wilderness Wanderer's spirit pet is also being borne by our family. Are you looking for a helper, or recruiting a grandson-in-law?"

"Grandson-in-law?"

Fu Chaosheng laughed despite himself.

"If he is willing, all suitable women in the clan may be chosen by him. If Qinglan is satisfied, I agree too, but he would have to marry into our family."

Fu Hongzhang was helpless. How could ordinary women enter Wilderness Wanderer's eye?

As for marrying in?

The other party would rather be a rogue cultivator in the Northern Sea than join a great force. Clearly, he was someone who loved freedom. How could he do something so devoid of dignity?

Abruptly.

The old man ahead stopped, and Fu Hongzhang heard only a faint sigh by his ear.

"Hongzhang, do you know why, when my lifespan is nearly exhausted, I still hold both the positions of clan elder and family head and have long delayed handing the position of family head to you?"

Fu Hongzhang froze, then subconsciously said, "It is because Hongzhang lacks ability and cannot support the rise and fall of a clan."

Polite words met a direct answer.

Fu Chaosheng nodded. "You are right. However, it is not that you are incapable. You have ability, but you lack a bit of boldness."

"Uh..."

Fu Chaosheng looked down at the hazy scenery below the Wangwu Mountain Range. His divine sense spread out, sensing the auras of one Fu family clansman after another.

Leaving the family for two hundred years felt like only yesterday to him, but to low-level cultivators, it had already been a vast change of seas and mulberry fields.

Among those auras, most had already become unfamiliar. Only the response from the bloodline made him feel close.

However!

Although unfamiliar, they were full of vitality!

"You said it yourself. Compared to Qinglan's future and the family's honor or disgrace, it is fine if we suffer a bit. That is why I had you wholeheartedly collect all kinds of resources for Luo Chen."

"Since that is so, why can we not suffer a little more loss within the range we can bear?"

"So long as Qinglan has hope of becoming an Ascendant seed, all the losses will be returned tenfold and hundredfold after a thousand years!"

Fu Hongzhang nodded. "I understand the reasoning, but..."

Fu Chaosheng shook his head. "No, you do not understand. You do not understand Luo Chen's thoughts at all. His purpose is not the Heaven-Earth Peak Star Gate guest elder position, nor is it joining the Tianyuan Dao Sect. As for those resources, although troublesome to collect, if Sages like us put our minds to it, we can always find them. Surely you do not think Luo Chen lacks that ability?"

There were indeed people in the world who cultivated purely and did not understand worldly affairs.

Clearly, Wilderness Wanderer was not that kind of person.

Fu Hongzhang had interacted greatly with his subordinates. Whether it was honest Sang Jinghe or capable, sharp Heavenjade, both had the ability to hold a side alone.

He also had assistance from that powerful fourth-tier desolate beast comparable to a Sage.

Even Zhang Jingqing, who was only at the Initiate stage and looked pitiful, was in truth clever, perceptive, and quick-witted.

For Wilderness Wanderer to gather these people under his command, if he spent a little effort forming a force, searching for resources would be no more than a matter within reach!

Fu Hongzhang hesitated and asked, "Then what are his thoughts?"

"This old man does not know either."

"Uh..."

"It is precisely because I do not know that I must keep increasing the stakes, so he temporarily places his thoughts completely on Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven."

So that was how it was!

Fu Hongzhang suddenly understood.

When Wilderness Wanderer's thoughts were unclear, continuously increasing the stakes from one side would draw the other party's attention over.

He had seen this kind of method before while following the family head in business; only this time, the family head's boldness was even greater.

Whether it was the old-generation high-grade Taihe Pills, the Flameflow Marrow found from the Western Desert, or even the rearranged Wangwu Peak [TERM?] cave abode, none were things ordinary Sage cultivators would easily bear to take out.

Only, under such stakes, Fu Hongzhang was also somewhat worried.

"What if he takes the things but does not work?"

To this, Fu Chaosheng was rather confident.

"How would he not work? Leave Central Continent directly? This is the Tianyuan Dao Sect! As for what you truly worry about, that he will show up but not exert himself, this old man is not so worried."

"I investigated very clearly. The two human cultivators following Luo Chen actually do not have especially close relationships with him. One is the inheritance disciple of a nodding acquaintance from the Myriad Immortals Assembly. The other is the daughter of an affiliate he casually accepted, and a daughter he did not value much at that."

"For this kind of person, because of affection, he did not hesitate to bring them along. He even had his own spirit pet brave the risk of demon beasts besieging the Coral Sea to specially rescue someone. This shows he is a person who values feelings and keeps promises."

"Speaking of spirit pets, when that desolate beast Black King dirtied Star Island's teleportation grand formation, Luo Chen was also willing to take out spirit stones to settle it..."

"Most importantly, he still remembers the guidance this old man gave him back then in Jadecold City on Coldlight Island!"

So that was how it was.

Fu Hongzhang understood again, as if enlightenment had been poured over his head.

Only, he was puzzled again. If that was so, why had the family head not handled things like this earlier, and instead had to wait until they reached Central Continent?

He did not know that Fu Hongzhang's change of mind had occurred only in an instant.

On Star Island, Luo Chen's sentence, "I do not wish to trouble you too much," had awakened him as if from a dream.

"Hongzhang, organize the intelligence gathered over these years and send it up. In addition, have the clansmen over at Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain [TERM?] continue collecting all intelligence related to the five-hundred-year grand competition!"

"Yes, family head!"

...

Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range [TERM?]!

It stretched for who knew how many ten thousand li, the mountains rising and falling like a gray dragon. Within it, peaks overlapped and strange summits emerged one after another.

There were also lakes, seas, and rivers winding through, rippling with water and adorning the mountains.

The enormous mountain range had crouched upon the earth for countless ages, as though the firmest guardian between heaven and earth, silently watching the mortal world's changes.

The dim yellow sunset sprinkled over the ridgeline, golden light rippling, dust drifting, telling the ancient stories of this land.

This was Central Continent's most central and essential region.

The Tianyuan Dao Sect was located here.

In the neighboring land, there was only one lone peak connecting heaven and earth. That place was Heaven-Earth Peak.

At this moment, three people walked out from Heaven-Earth Peak.

One man and two women, all of them handsome and beautiful, tall and slender.

The man silently followed behind, his expression dazed, as if he were still immersed in some matter.

The two women in front chatted intermittently.

"Qinglan, after meeting Sage Skywatch [TERM?] just now, what did you think?"

Fu Qinglan thought for a moment and said with words as precious as gold, "Severe presence, abundant magic power!"

Yu Xu [TERM?] smiled brightly. "For him to receive such high praise from you, it seems the dao-protector my family found for me truly is extraordinary. But that is only natural. He is different from Sage cultivators raised by our aristocratic clans and great houses. He comes from the most mysterious Southern Frontier, and was the final cult master of the Heavenly Mountain Cult [TERM?]. Not only is his Dao attainment formidable, his battle ability has long since matured. With him protecting me, on the trip to Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, I will not need to worry too much."

If this matter were spoken outside, it would certainly make many jaws drop.

A cult master of a Sage upper sect had actually given himself up and joined the Tianyuan Dao Sect. This was truly somewhat unexpected.

However, it had also let her Yu family pick up a bargain.

Yu Xu suddenly asked, "Qinglan, have you found your dao-protector?"

Fu Qinglan shook her head.

Yu Xu said anxiously, "You cannot imitate the master-disciple lineage and enter Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven alone!"

Fu Qinglan bit her lip and said nothing.

Yu Xu's eyes rolled. "What do you think of Qi Xiongcheng [TERM?], the one I introduced to you before? Although he was born as a servant of the Shi Great House [TERM?], he was lucky enough to achieve the Sage realm and has not yet had time to go out and establish his own door. Now, less than a hundred years have passed, so he completely satisfies the conditions to enter Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven. You are familiar with Shi Yue [TERM?] as well. If you ask him for the man, I imagine it would not be difficult."

Fu Qinglan said quietly, "My grandfather has his own arrangements for this matter."

"Your Fu family has declined for a long time, and Elder Fu was in the Northern Sea for so long. How could he so easily find you an excellent dao-protector? In my view, he may not even be as good as Qi Xiongcheng."

"Let us discuss it later. My grandfather said he will return soon." Fu Qinglan shook her head, seeming unwilling to discuss this topic further.

Only, when the clear wind brushed across her cheeks and stirred her hair, there were traces of confusion in her eyes.

The letter said that he was a very formidable rogue cultivator.

But how formidable could a rogue cultivator be?
