# Chapter 524: Demon Sea!

---

On the calm surface of the sea, Luo Chen had changed into a fresh set of black close-fitting clothes. The affected air of a weathered sage was gone; in its place was the spirited bearing of a dashing young hero.

Standing at the center of the flying boat, he gauged its speed — roughly a third slower than flying through open air.

Yet he made no move to take to the sky.

The reason was simple enough.

Looking back the way he had come, a flock of large white seabirds was circling in the sky. They were an unidentifiable species of white fowl — mostly first-tier, with a fair number of second-tier among them — and in shape they somewhat resembled the mundane albatross.

More notably, at the very center of the flock, the faint aura of a third-tier demon beast drifted and spread, enveloping the entire group.

*"I wonder whether these seabirds were deliberately raised by the demon cultivators of Green Parasol Mountain to guard the place and watch over the teleportation formation."*

*"If so, with the Concealment Veil Formation at full power, I should be able to slip away without being detected."*

*"And if not..."*

A quiet unease settled in Luo Chen's chest.

He had barely arrived and had already encountered a pack of demon beasts led by a third-tier Demon King. That alone spoke to how dangerous this region was.

What he needed right now, urgently, was to find living people — and get a clear picture of where he was.

He stamped his foot lightly; a surge of mana pushed the flying boat faster across the surface of the sea. The Concealment Veil Formation kept it invisible. To a sharp-eyed seabird watching from a distance, it would look like nothing more than a white line skimming the waves.

The sun rose and the moon drifted up: a full day and night slipped past.

He had no idea how far he had traveled, nor whether his direction was right. Luo Chen was going on instinct alone, heading away from whatever directions set his senses on edge, pressing straight ahead.

Morning mist settled around him, a faint chill soaking into his skin.

Luo Chen retrieved a gourd from his storage ring and tipped a mouthful in.

A sweet liquid — rich as aged wine, fragrant as fresh fruit.

He hung the gourd back at his waist with a sigh.

*"What a shame."*

This monkey wine had been gathered for him from deep in the wilds by a demon bird on Green Parasol Mountain — one that had learned alchemy at his hands. It was a proper third-tier spirit wine, well suited for recovering mana. Nothing remarkable by a Sage's standards, but a genuine treasure for an Adept.

It could also be counted as that demon bird's formal disciple's gift.

He had intended to bring out a bottle for Heaven Smelter to taste — a gesture of thanks for forging his life-bound treasure.

But then everything had gone sideways.

*"He never got to enjoy it. All the more for me, I suppose."*

Luo Chen shook his head.

He released his divine sense, as had become his habit, sweeping the surroundings for signs of life.

At Golden Core Stage Three, his divine sense — when pushed to its limit — had long since surpassed the five-hundred-li range of a newly formed Adept. Extended fully now, it reached over seven hundred li in a straight line.

Combined with his spirit eyes, the situation ahead lay completely open before him.

In an instant, his brow rose.

*"Finally — living people."*

*"Though their situation looks rather grim."*

*"All the better."*

With a thought, the flying boat launched forward like an arrow loosed from a bow. Sea wind lashed at his face, and the smile it found there was brilliant.

——

Out on the open sea, a demon-hunting vessel twenty zhang in length lay becalmed on the still water.

Aside from those keeping watch, most of the crew were still deep in sleep.

But that peaceful sleep was shattered in an instant by a sharp cry of alarm.

"Enemies!"

Every last person jerked awake.

Because they worked at sea and were always exposed to sudden crises, most had slept fully clothed. Once roused, while the first group — the muscular mortal sailors — stumbled about in some confusion, the cultivators among them were mostly composed.

Under their direction, everyone began to move in orderly fashion.

The man in command burst from the cabin: a large black-faced man gripping a long white banner. "What enemy?" he barked.

A watchman answered in a panicked rush: "Blue-Ringed Sea Serpents!"

The black-faced man's brow shot up. "Any Blue-Ringed Fang Serpents among them?"

A Blue-Ringed Fang Serpent was still a Blue-Ringed Sea Serpent — but their ranks were worlds apart.

A Blue-Ringed Serpent that had grown its great fangs had already crossed into second-tier demon beast territory. One alone was manageable; a large number of them was beyond even his ability to hold off for a whole ship of people.

The watchman shook his head quickly. "None spotted yet."

The black-faced man frowned. "Then why the panic?"

"The numbers!"

The watchman raised a trembling finger toward the deck's edge, toward the deep and lightless sea in the pale half-dark before dawn.

"What about the numbers?"

"There's no counting them!"

The black-faced man fixed him with a glare, then strode briskly to the railing and released his spirit sense.

One sweep was all it took. The color drained from his face instantly.

Without a moment's hesitation, he roared his orders.

"First mate, second mate — activate the formation!"

"Third mate — distribute the spirit-bonded weapons to the mortal crew!"

"Little Seventeen — rally everyone. All hands. Prepare for battle!"

"Cheng Ji — raise the sails, full speed, head back now!"

The commands came clear and steady, urgent but unrushed. The crew that had been stumbling in confusion snapped into motion.

A black curtain of light erupted from the deck's edges, wrapping the ship layer upon layer in dark mist. Grotesque shapes occasionally thrust heads and limbs through the mist before retreating.

The mortal sailors, frightened but crackling with excitement, grabbed swords and sea-tridents from the cultivators' hands. As they channeled their inner qi into the strange weapons, the blades began to glow with a faint, hazy light.

On deck, three great sails rose with a loud snap, ready to catch the wind.

Seeing his orders faithfully carried out, the black-faced man allowed himself a brief moment of relief.

But when he looked down at the water below — at all those blue-and-white shapes shifting endlessly beneath the surface — his lips went dry and his throat itched.

*"With this many Blue-Ringed Sea Serpents... the family's demon-hunting vessel isn't going to survive."*

*"No help for it. I'll do what I can, then think from there."*

——

The sky grew pale.

The sea mist drew back.

Out on the open water, the great black ship had stopped moving entirely and was spinning in wild, helpless circles. The dark mist surrounding it gave it some measure of defense.

But beneath the surface, countless great serpents churned the water into a raging vortex. A massive whirlpool had formed in the sea, and even the mist-protected vessel was being steadily dragged downward.

Luo Chen watched from a distance, eyes flickering.

He marveled at how the ship had held out against the ceaseless assault of so many sea-floor demon beasts — its defensive formation was remarkably strong.

He was equally struck by how these low-tier demon beasts had mastered the art of cooperation. Unable to break through by direct assault, they had stirred up a whirlpool to harness the raw force of the sea and drag the vessel to the depths.

It was easy to imagine what would follow once the ship went under: with tens of thousands of low-tier demon beasts swarming it, the dark mist formation would collapse within a few breaths.

*"What demon beast is this?"*

*"Somewhat similar to the Black-Ringed Snow Pythons of the Great Snowy Mountains."*

*"Though far smarter than an ordinary Black-Ringed Snow Python."*

Luo Chen's gaze settled on a spot not far from the fighting — the relative rear of the battlefield.

A small, round gray cauldron settled into his palm.

One hand cradled the cauldron. The other formed a seal.

"Go."

In the next instant, bright crimson chains shot out of the Primordial Unity Cauldron and plunged into the dark water below.

*Hissss—*

A hissing shriek rose from the deep, carrying unmistakable fury and fear.

Then, bound fast by crimson chains, a massive serpent broke the surface: nine zhang long, sporting two enormous fangs, almost like a small flood dragon in build.

In the daylight, the blue rings along its body were vivid and deep, and its fangs were terrifyingly sharp.

One look was all it took.

*"Didn't get a clear view earlier — turns out it's a second-tier late-stage Great Demon. Bigger than I thought."*

At that moment, the calm sea erupted.

From the north, south, east, and west — four thick white streaks came rushing toward Luo Chen's small boat.

And from the distance, the serpent swarms that had been maintaining the whirlpool turned and surged toward him as well.

The answer was obvious. He had gone for the leader. He had caught their commander-in-chief.

Luo Chen glanced once at the four white lines, then raised his fingers in rapid succession.

Four more crimson chains shot out from beneath the surface and dragged up four more serpents — each four to five zhang in length, each bearing grotesque fangs, each thrashing its body furiously and writhing to break free.

Luo Chen paid no attention to the five great serpents' struggles. His eyes swept out over the open sea — toward the countless demon beasts now surging toward him from every direction — and a greedy gleam entered his gaze.

*"Today, I eat well."*

He flicked out his tongue, moistened his lips, and rose slowly into the air, his robes stirring in a wind of their own making. Both hands pressed outward against the open sky.

Azure flame erupted across his entire body.

Fed by mana, the azure fire swelled and blazed, bleeding from blue into blazing red.

In that moment, the azure-red above and the blue-white below became the only colors in the world.

——

Aboard the demon-hunting vessel.

The black-faced man's eyes were bloodshot with fury and despair.

In under half an hour, the demon-hunting vessel — representing two-tenths of his family's finest strength — had been gutted.

Every mortal sailor was dead.

The Qi Refinement cultivators he had counted on were more than half killed or wounded.

And old Cheng Ji — the Initiate cultivator who piloted the ship — had tried to force the vessel forward, only to suffer a formation backlash. He now lay collapsed in the helm cabin, unconscious.

At this rate, they were all going to die.

The black-faced man drew a long breath and abandoned any thought of going down with the ship. His white banner exhaled a billow of mist, coiling around the unconscious Cheng Ji, preparing to carry him skyward and flee alone.

Then—

"They're retreating!"

"The sea serpents are retreating!"

"We made it — we're alive!"

Cries of wild joy and weeping erupted across the deck.

The black-faced man froze, bewildered, his spiritual power less than half remaining. He looked around.

And indeed: the tens of thousands of sea serpents that had encircled the demon-hunting ship, impenetrable as a wall, had abruptly abandoned their assault and were streaming eastward.

He followed that direction with his gaze.

The sun had just risen, a brilliant disc climbing slowly from the sea.

*"I've never heard of Blue-Ringed Sea Serpents being unable to move in sunlight..."*

He was still muttering in confusion when something struck him like a blow.

That wasn't the sun.

That was a person.

A living, breathing person.

In the clear morning light, a figure hung in midair, gazing down over the vast sea. Boundless azure-red flames blazed from his body, pouring in torrents across the ocean surface.

Tens of thousands of sea serpents launched poison-laced water arrows, firing them upward at the figure.

Every one of those arrows dissolved in the azure-red fire before it came close.

And the fire itself — an endless, surging mass of it — descended like a great dome clapped over the world, covering a hundred li in a single breath.

Beneath it, the great serpents thrashed and screamed in the burning water.

The cries were hideous, impossible to ignore.

The black-faced man and the dozen-odd survivors aboard the demon-hunting vessel stared, slack-jawed.

One person. Holding down ten thousand sea serpents.

What a sight.

The serpents packed together in the water were like eels dropped alive into boiling water — no amount of thrashing in that extreme heat could win them free.

Even separated by dozens of li, everyone on the ship could feel the rolling waves of heat washing over them.

Through their nostrils drifted a distinct, rich smell — scorched flesh.

*Gulp.*

Someone swallowed. The sound was absurdly loud in the silence.

Then, almost as one, everyone else swallowed too.

Not a single person could look away. Necks stiff, hearts hammering.

"Who is that?"

"Could it be a Dao Demonist from the Primal Demon Sect?"

"Impossible. The Primal Demon Sect was destroyed long ago. Even if a supreme expert like this had survived, their name would be known across the islands by now. How could we not have heard of them?"

Voices rose, low and uncertain. No one could place the figure.

No one dared to move or flee.

It was obvious the man was deliberately restraining his azure-red fire — had he not, those aboard the ship would have been well within range.

Every survivor, having escaped death by the thinnest of margins, watched in stunned silence and waited for the battle to end.

A long, long time seemed to pass.

It felt like a full turning of the seasons. In truth, barely the time it takes to burn an incense stick.

The morning sun finally lifted clear of the horizon.

The young man in black descended slowly from the sky, both hands raised at his sides, and landed on the demon-hunting vessel.

He stepped onto the deck and said nothing — only closed his eyes, as though sensing something.

The black-faced man and the others all bowed in unison, not daring to disturb him.

But their eyes wandered: one moment to the cloud-white boots on his feet, untouched by a speck of grime; the next to the charred and blackened sea stretching in every direction.

Apart from the handful of Blue-Ringed Sea Serpents that instinct had driven to flee, every one of the tens of thousands was dead — wiped out in a slaughter that had been entirely one-sided.

Scorched black carcasses drifted on the surface. The waves came and broke them into ash, swallowing them beneath the sea.

Even the remnants left floating had been reduced to such fragility that the slightest force reduced them to cinders.

At last, every gaze returned to the young man in black.

Waves of powerful aura ebbed and flowed from him, rising and falling like breath — a vital, surging life force that made something in people stir toward him involuntarily.

Yet the spiritual pressure he made no effort to conceal pressed down on every person there, bowing their heads and bending their backs.

Spiritual pressure.

An Adept.

The newcomer's cultivation level, completely unmasked, stood revealed before all of them.

Then—

The young man opened his eyes. A sharp, brilliant light swept across every face.

Those with weaker cultivation — even without being deliberately targeted — simply crumpled where they stood, dropping to the deck.

He drew a slow breath and withdrew his Adept's spiritual pressure.

The black-faced man let out a long, relieved exhale. Then a voice reached him:

"What is this place?"

The black-faced man moistened his dry lips. "Senior, this is the Xingtian Sea — eight thousand li from the Swallow Islands. It falls within the uncontrolled region of the Demon Sea."

*Xingtian Sea.*

*Swallow Islands.*

Luo Chen's brow furrowed faintly — but the moment he heard the words *Demon Sea*, his eyes lit up.

"The Northern Sea?"

Half question, half certainty.

The black-faced man paused, then nodded. "Senior's insight is correct. This is indeed the Northern Sea."

*(End of Chapter)*
