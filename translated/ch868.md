# Chapter 868: Heavenly River Soars, Luo Tian Sets Sail

Within a secluded valley on Green Duckweed Mountain.

Somewhat chilly mountain winds blew in from outside, making a sobbing sound.

A woman with a light veil covering her face paced back and forth within the valley, as if waiting for something.

Suddenly, the sound of flying through the void came from beyond the mountain.

The woman's heart rejoiced, and she quickly went out to greet the visitor.

Upon seeing who had arrived, she performed a graceful bow with practiced familiarity. "Tao Wan pays respects to Sect Master Li!"

Li Yingzhang waved his hand and smiled. "Why must Sect Master Tao be so polite? Our two sects have deep ties from the past; there's no need to be so distant."

Beneath her veil, Tao Wan smiled bitterly.

Once upon a time, the Green Dan Valley had been a prestigious Adept Grand Sect with formidable strength. Back then, the Luo Tian Sect was still the Luo Tian Society, merely a second-rate force.

Now, the Green Dan Valley had only three Adept cultivators left, barely maintaining its status as a grand sect.

But the Luo Tian Society, which once had to rely on the Green Dan Valley's goodwill, had become the lofty Luo Tian Supreme Sect.

A tragic thick barrier had long since formed between them!

The only connection that allowed the two sides to converse was probably the slight ties between her and the Luo Tian Sect's Supreme Elder.

Sighing inwardly, Tao Wan pulled herself together and asked, "Will Sage Luo be willing to see me?"

Li Yingzhang said apologetically, "Unfortunately, the Supreme Elder has been very busy lately and cannot find time to meet you."

Tao Wan's mouth opened slightly, and beneath her veil, her face was full of disappointment.

But Li Yingzhang's next words swept away her dejected mood.

"However, rest assured. For this expansion war, the Supreme Elder has agreed to let the Green Dan Valley participate. Moreover, after a domain is secured, you will be permitted to select a third-rank spirit land as a place to continue your sect."

Upon hearing this, Tao Wan was overjoyed.

"Thank you, Sect Master Li! And please also thank your sect's Supreme Elder for me!"

Li Yingzhang waved his hand. "It's nothing. Just a small matter. I still have some business to attend to, so I'll take my leave now."

"Sect Master Li, go ahead. Don't worry about me. One year from now, the Green Dan Valley will fully participate in the war!" Tao Wan said happily, and took out a storage pouch, respectfully offering it up. "Although Sage Luo is unwilling to see me, there are some things my Green Dan Valley has not forgotten. Please, Sect Master Li, pass this on on my behalf."

Li Yingzhang hesitated as he looked at the storage pouch.

Tao Wan said earnestly, "This is what my Green Dan Valley owed Sage Luo back then. Although it may be of little use to him now, we cannot break our promise."

Li Yingzhang no longer refused. He accepted the storage pouch and then drifted away.

After Li Yingzhang left, Tao Wan finally let out a sigh of relief.

She did not stay here any longer. She only cast a glance toward the top of Green Duckweed Mountain before hurriedly leaving this place.

She headed straight for Lingtian Pass.

At the top of Green Duckweed Mountain, Luo Chen stood together with Sima Huiniang, jointly inventorying the batch of magical treasures that would serve as the Luo Tian Sect's foundation, discussing how to distribute them later.

Li Yingzhang hurried over, reported the situation, and finally handed over a storage pouch.

"I understand."

Luo Chen accepted the storage pouch and nodded faintly.

Li Yingzhang did not linger any longer and respectfully withdrew.

After he left, Sima Huiniang chuckled lightly. "I didn't expect the Green Dan Valley to still exist. There were rumors before that they had been wiped out."

Luo Chen, while using source power to remove the flesh-and-blood imprints left on the treasures by the Hundred-Foot Great Demon Emperor, replied, "Rumors are just rumors after all. These long-established Adept Grand Sects all have their ways of surviving, more or less."

As he said this, a hint of emotion flickered across Luo Chen's face.

As the front line bearing the brunt of the beast tide's assault, the Jade Cauldron Domain had suffered no small losses.

Of the Seven Sects of Jade Cauldron, the Sword Sect's Sage had died tragically, and only Lin Qingxuan, a rising star, had stepped up to hold things together.

The Falling Cloud Sect had been destroyed, and its Supreme Elder, Han Zhan, had fled for his life to the Northern Sea.

Ailao Mountain had collapsed, the Hundred Flowers Palace had withered, Icecastle had been reduced to less than one percent of its former strength, and the Luo Tian Sect had fled ten thousand li.

As for the Green Dan Valley, it had first endured the beast tide's assault, then suffered internal strife. A rebellion by Xuan Yunzi had stolen the Nascent Soul Pill formula.

Afterward, it was rumored that a large number of Green Dan Valley disciples had been absorbed by the Medicine King Sect, and then, amidst the chaos of the strong taking advantage of the situation, they had died or fled.

Thus, the Green Dan Valley could already be considered erased from the history of the Eastern Wilderness.

Yet unexpectedly, the final sect master, Tao Wan, had managed to preserve a branch lineage!

Over the years, like the Heartless Fairy, she had led several dozen Green Dan Valley disciples in hiding, scattering and blending in among the crowds of Lingtian Pass.

They had even concealed the name "Green Dan Valley," calling themselves only rogue cultivators.

Luo Chen could roughly guess their intentions behind this behavior.

They were simply afraid that someone would covet the Nascent Soul Pill formula that the Green Dan Valley had once obtained—the crime of possessing a jade that invited disaster, being targeted by others.

Now that the situation had stabilized, Tao Wan wanted to use Luo Chen's powerful backing to rebuild their mountain gate.

"Husband, you are quite kind-hearted, agreeing to let this fledgling Green Dan Valley participate." Sima Huiniang looked at Luo Chen with a half-smile, meaningfully teasing, "I've heard that Superior Tao Wan has never married to this day, all alone and quite pitiful."

"Heh, I used to think only Caiyi was jealous, but now even the usually reasonable Huiniang has become like this!" Luo Chen stopped what he was doing.

Sima Huiniang aggrievedly lowered her head.

Luo Chen sighed and weighed the storage pouch that the Green Dan Valley had offered up.

"After all, Tao Wan and I have a past connection. How could I turn a blind eye to their miserable state?"

"Besides, they are so perceptive and sensible—far more understanding than those other Adept Grand Sects."

The storage pouch opened, revealing a copied fourth-rank Nascent Soul Pill formula inside. There was also a jade slip, which recorded the Green Dan Valley's former Five Peaks Valley-Canopy feng shui formation for condensing a fourth-rank spirit vein.

The former was not exactly given for free.

Back then, when Luo Chen had agreed to refine the Nascent Soul Pill for the Green Dan Valley, their Supreme Elder, Qing Danzi, had promised the Primordial Unity Cauldron's refining blueprints and the Nascent Soul Pill formula as conditions.

The former had been given to Luo Chen before the refining began, while the latter was to be given after Luo Chen reached the late Adept stage.

Unfortunately, many things had happened later, and Luo Chen had even ended up drifting to the Northern Sea, so the promise had fizzled out.

Now, Tao Wan, on behalf of the Green Dan Valley, was delivering the formula, thus fulfilling the promise from back then.

Having a beginning and an end—that was how to achieve perfection!

As for the Five Peaks Valley-Canopy feng shui formation, it was clearly a deliberate offering tailored to the Luo Tian Sect's imminent establishment of its sect.

Sima Huiniang curiously looked at these two items and had to admit that Tao Wan's actions were indeed generous.

Either one of these could be considered a precious inheritance in the hands of an Adept Grand Sect. To so generously contribute them to Luo Chen, all she sought was a third-rank spirit land and the Luo Tian Sect's protection.

She awkwardly mumbled, "It was this wife who overthought things."

Luo Chen smiled gently and affectionately squeezed her fair, delicate hand.

"It's nothing. Once the Luo Tian Sect is settled, before I go to the Nether Origin Holy Land, I will naturally help you fulfill what is on your mind."

Sima Huiniang's eyes lit up.

"Really?"

"Would your husband lie to you?"

"Mm... Actually, this wife doesn't mind at all if you take multiple wives and concubines. I think Tao Wan, the Heartless Fairy, and even Fenghua..."

"Stop!"

Luo Chen proactively ended this topic.

He knew that Huiniang's temperament had changed greatly over the years, but he hadn't expected it to be so different.

In the end, it was the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu affliction that prevented her from cultivating, making her thoughts more and more complicated with each passing day.

He wondered when Sage Heavenly Mountain would reply.

Luo Chen shook his head and reached out to summon two true artifacts, namely the Thunderclap Gloves and the Golden Binding Rope.

The Hundred-Foot Great Demon Emperor, with the power of one being, controlled over a hundred magical treasures and even six or seven true artifacts. Such methods completely exceeded a cultivator's imagination.

But clearly, this method had a natural flaw.

That was, the control over these treasures and true artifacts was not through divine sense imprints, but through flesh-and-blood imprints.

This method was convenient, but its drawback was that it could not bring out the full power of the treasures.

Thus, in that battle, although the Hundred-Foot Great Demon Emperor's treasure light filled the sky, facing Luo Chen's one cauldron, one sword, and one armor, it was ultimately powerless.

Of course, these flaws became advantages in front of Luo Chen at this moment.

Soul imprints were difficult for him to erase quickly.

But flesh-and-blood imprints?

Luo Chen's mastery over flesh and blood surpassed even the Hundred-Foot Great Demon Emperor's. Removing the imprints was truly simple.

"These Thunderclap Gloves, I'll give them to Wang Yuan. He lacks a suitable offensive weapon. Coincidentally, this item is also compatible with the path of qi and blood, making it most suitable."

"As for this Golden Binding Rope..."

Sima Huiniang suggested, "Why not give it to Elder Lingfengzi?"

Luo Chen turned his head, frowning slightly, somewhat reluctant.

At this moment, Sima Huiniang had regained some of the bearing she had when she was the Luo Tian Sect's sect master.

She spoke eloquently, "Elder Lingfengzi joined the Luo Tian Sect under your half-coercion, half-beguilement. He inevitably harbors some resentment in his heart. Especially now that the situation is settled, even a fallen sect like the Green Dan Valley has a chance to rebuild its mountain gate. Why wouldn't he be itching to do the same?"

"Giving him a true artifact shows your respect for him and can serve as some compensation."

"After you leave for the Nether Origin Holy Land, the Luo Tian Sect will still need to rely on this Nascent Soul powerhouse to hold things down in the short term. We cannot afford to neglect him."

After hearing this, Luo Chen fell into thought.

He had to admit that what Sima Huiniang said made some sense.

Moreover, he thought about it. Lingfengzi excelled at concealing his aura, hiding his form, and wind-escape fleeing techniques, but his frontal combat ability was somewhat weak.

If he had this Golden Binding Rope as an aid, it could greatly increase his strength.

"Then I'll listen to you, my wife!"

Luo Chen no longer hesitated.

That very day, he summoned Lingfengzi.

To Lingfengzi's surprise, he gave him the Golden Binding Rope true artifact, which had once bound Luo Chen.

What he got in return was a sentence from Lingfengzi, spoken with utmost sincerity.

"Thank you, Senior Brother!"

As for whether he still had other thoughts in his heart, Luo Chen would not delve into it.

As long as he was still here, the title of Alchemy Sect would weigh on the other party, and the Golden Binding Rope would firmly tie him to the Luo Tian Sect's ship.

Afterward, Luo Chen summoned Wang Yuan and handed the Thunderclap Gloves to him.

Not only that, but Luo Chen also temporarily took back the Hundred Crafts Mountain.

"Before the expansion war, I will reforge this treasure. At that time, with the Thunderclap Gloves and the new Hundred Crafts Mountain, relying on your Great Perfection Furnace Realm cultivation, you can fight even against ordinary Nascent Soul cultivators!"

Wang Yuan had no doubt about himself, nor would he doubt the credibility of Luo Chen's words.

As for how to reforge the Hundred Crafts Mountain, Luo Chen already had a plan in mind.

In the Alchemy-Artifact Hall.

The Hundred Crafts Mountain, reduced to its normal size, sat motionless.

Luo Chen waved his hand, and several streams of light flew out from his storage ring.

There were shattered rocks, densely covered with forbidden formations. This was the mountain true artifact that had been smashed to pieces by Luo Chen's Primordial Unity Cauldron, once relied upon by the Hundred-Foot Great Demon Emperor.

A jade bottle opened, and black liquid flowed out from it. Although it was in a flowing state, it gave people an extremely heavy feeling.

This was the true artifact of the Moya Old Ghost—Moya Cliff!

On that battle platform, it had been instantly refined by the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire that Luo Chen had released and then withdrawn.

Not only that, but there were also several medium-grade and high-grade magical treasures, resembling large seals, inkstones, and stone tablets, floating beside Luo Chen.

Luo Chen looked at all of this, satisfaction showing in his eyes.

For a long time, he had entertained the idea of personally refining a magical treasure suitable for being driven by source power.

But in this world, few cultivators could cultivate source power, let alone have refining blueprints they could reference.

In comparison, there were relatively more blueprints for qi-and-blood weapons made for orthodox body cultivators.

He now planned to test some ideas while refining a qi-and-blood weapon for Wang Yuan.

If successful, not only would Wang Yuan obtain a powerful weapon, but Luo Chen would also find it easier to refine source-power weapons in the future.

"First, I'll melt all these materials!"

With a thought, the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire roared out, splitting into multiple strands, enveloping all the materials and treasures.

Even the intact Hundred Crafts Mountain was wrapped by the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire, beginning to shrink gradually as a large amount of impurities were refined out.

...

Years are hard to keep; time is easily lost.

A mere year might seem long to mortals, but to cultivators, it was merely a simple seclusion.

Even though the war preparations were extremely cumbersome, with the joint efforts of many cultivators, everything was gradually put in place.

Many forces, eager to return to their homeland, had already set out ahead of time.

In comparison, because the Luo Tian Sect was conducting its first expansion war, it was much slower.

But no matter how slow, when the appointed time came, they finally set off.

On this day, in Fenghua Domain, ten thousand cultivators gathered!

Countless cultivators looked toward Green Duckweed Mountain, where a stream of light flew into the sky, then grew with the wind, transforming into an ultra-giant flying ship over a thousand zhang in length!

Like a massive dark cloud, it covered Green Duckweed Mountain and part of the nearby Fenghua Palace buildings.

On the flying ship, palaces rose high, pavilions stood in rows, landscapes flowed, and spiritual energy was dense.

At the edges of the flying ship, exquisite patterns were carved, as if containing mysterious power. These patterns occasionally flickered with faint light, giving people a contradictory feeling of both safety and danger.

On both sides of the flying ship, there were even huge oars, as if once they started rowing, the ship could roam the Heavenly River at any time.

Gasps instantly rang out everywhere.

"What kind of flying boat is that?"

"Phew, could this be the legendary Heavenly River Soaring Void Ship, comparable to a true artifact?"

"Heavenly River Soaring Void? Isn't that a treasure of the Tiandu Sect? Why is it here?"

"I heard that many sects invited the Tiandu Sect to participate in the expansion war this time, but they all refused. They only wanted to close their gates and recover. Yet unexpectedly, they chose to help the Luo Tian Sect!"

"Yes, they even lent out one of their sect's guardian treasures, the Heavenly River Soaring Void Ship. It shows how much the Tiandu Sect values the Luo Tian Sect."

"With this ship here, this expansion war is as good as won!"

Below the mountain, discussions buzzed. On the mountain, however, conversation was light and cheerful.

"Senior Alchemy Sect, our sect's Supreme Elder is currently in seclusion recovering from injuries, and Elder Tianquanzi is busy managing the sect, so they cannot come in person. Please forgive us." Ji Wanqian bowed respectfully.

Luo Chen stood with his hands behind his back, looking up at the enormous flying ship, a smile on his face.

"That's enough. Your sect's intentions have been received. From now on, I'll trouble you and your fellow disciples."

As he spoke, he tossed out a bottle of pills.

Ji Wanqian, a late-stage Adept, initially wanted to refuse, as this was something personally arranged by the senior members of his sect.

But a wisp of pure pill fragrance leaking from the bottle made him subconsciously swallow his saliva.

Top-grade third-rank pills!

The Alchemy Sect was so generous!

He no longer refused. Accepting the bottle of pills as if they were a precious treasure, he respectfully said, "Please, Senior Alchemy Sect, board the ship first. Leave the rest to us."

Luo Chen smiled faintly, lifted his foot, and under the gaze of countless people, ascended the ship as if climbing a heavenly ladder.

Afterward, the Luo Tian Sect cultivators, led by Lingfengzi and Wang Yuan, boarded the ship one after another.

When all the Luo Tian Sect disciples had boarded, nearly ten thousand cultivators also flew out from Fenghua Palace, led by the female cultivator Fenghua Fairy, and flew up.

After these two parties were settled, Li Yingzhang exchanged a few words with Ji Wanqian, then stood at the bow of the Heavenly River Soaring Void Ship, looking down below.

He infused his magic power and spoke lightly.

"Fellow Daoists, if you wish to save some effort, please board the ship!"

As the words fell, a commotion stirred below the mountain.

Then, streaks of flying light, like mayflies and shooting stars, flew together toward the giant ship.

There were also those unwilling to share the ship, who piloted flying boats of various sizes, following alongside.

When everything was ready,

Li Yingzhang took a deep breath and shouted loudly.

"Target: Motian Domain!"

"Set sail!"

With these words, the Heavenly River Soaring Void Ship trembled. Patterns flickered with light one after another, and vast spiritual energy surged into the sky.

Splash! Splash!

One by one, the huge oars began to row. The surrounding clouds and mist were pushed aside by the powerful airflow, forming ripples.

The great ship slowly started moving, like a dark cloud heading eastward.

On the Heavenly River Soaring Void, Fenghua Fairy stood facing the wind, her eyes flickering with light.

"What a Motian Domain. I thought this assistance would just be a formality, but it seems there will be a good show to watch."

Her disciple, puzzled, asked, "Isn't the Motian Domain vacant?"

Fenghua Fairy smiled slightly. "Not long ago, the Motian Cliff cultivators, led by their Supreme Elder, the Moya Old Ghost, prepared to return to their homeland. Not only that, but this domain will also serve as the rear base for the Five Elements Divine Sect's recovery of the Five Elements Domain."

The disciple's eyes widened in shock. "If the Luo Tian Sect goes there, their biggest opponent will no longer be those insignificant demon beasts, but a large group of human experts led by the Five Elements Divine Sect!"

"Exactly. One side has the claim of ancient tradition, the other has the decree of the holy land." Fenghua Fairy's heart surged with excitement, a hint of anticipation in her eyes.

In terms of righteousness, the two sides were evenly matched.

But who would ultimately win the Motian Domain would depend on their respective methods.

Alchemy Sect Luo Chen's choice was clearly aimed at the Motian Cliff and the Five Elements Divine Sect.

Coincidentally, during the years these two sects had resided in Fenghua Domain, they had also had some conflicts with her Fenghua Palace. It was time to settle them all at once!

(Chapter End)
