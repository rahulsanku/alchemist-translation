# Chapter 717: Nirvana Scripture Great Completion, Extreme Control

All along, Luo Chen had discovered that he was very prone to suffering "inner demon assaults."

During Qi Refining, he had suffered a heart tribulation.

During Foundation Establishment, he had fallen into demonic obstruction.

For this reason, he had painstakingly cultivated related cultivation arts and secret arts.

Whether it was Bright Spirit Breaking Balefulness [TERM?] or the Fine Dust Primordial Art, both had the effect of tempering will and state of mind.

Unknowingly, they had gradually forged a powerful heart that could endure a hundred setbacks and not be invaded by outer demons.

This state-of-mind cultivation did not usually show, but whenever major events arose, Luo Chen could always face them calmly and make the correct choice amid countless moments.

Whether he was trapped in Green Parasol Mountain [TERM?], surrounded by powerful enemies in the Fallen Demon Land [TERM?], or pursued across ten thousand li by the late Nascent Soul Moon Wanderer, he had still maintained inner calm amid wildly surging emotions.

Thus, through the entire Golden Core realm, he had never again encountered anything like the inner demon assaults of Qi Refining and Foundation Establishment.

However!

Luo Chen had never expected that when inner demons did not arise, an outer demon would attack.

After breaking through to Nascent Soul and crossing the tribulation, Han Zhan erupted!

That battle in the sea of consciousness could also be regarded as an outer-demon great calamity.

Now more than ten years had passed. Even though he was safe and sound, and his cultivation was stronger than before, lingering fear still remained.

"Among all cultivators under heaven, am I the only one like this, or is everyone like this?"

In idle moments, Luo Chen had also asked himself this.

But in the end, he had obtained no answer.

Unable to force it, Luo Chen could only let it be and do everything he could to strengthen himself.

And now, after calamity had turned to fortune, his gains also exceeded ordinary imagination.

A complete formation diagram for the Great Five Elements Sword Formation [TERM?], Feiyunzi's sword-pellet refining method, and even a metal-attribute true-artifact sword pellet without any trace of will attached!

Pinching that sword pellet, Luo Chen fell into thought.

This should have been a backup weapon Feiyunzi prepared for himself.

From this point, Feiyunzi, this sword cultivator, did not seem quite so pure.

A sword cultivator cultivated only one sword pellet in life.

If the sword lived, the person lived; if the sword perished, the person perished.

From the very beginning, he had left himself a way out. No wonder his sword Dao lacked that slight flavor and was far from possessing the grand aura of one sword breaking ten thousand arts.

"But in the end, I am the one who benefited."

Luo Chen chuckled and stored the formation diagram, artifact-refining record, and sword pellet together.

He was still missing four. If he spent time gathering them in the future, perhaps he could master a powerful sword formation.

Afterward, he began inventorying the other resources inside Feiyunzi's storage belt.

At first glance earlier, he had not felt much, but now that he counted them one by one, Luo Chen's mouth could hardly close.

All kinds of rare medicinal ingredients and medicinal pills suited for Golden Core and Nascent Soul cultivators filled his sight in dazzling profusion.

There were also a dozen or so classics covering every aspect, and most were rarely seen in the outside world.

For example, one jade slip without any restriction applied recorded exactly what Luo Chen currently needed most.

Northern Sea Geography Record [TERM?]

Inside, the geography of the Northern Sea was recorded in detail, along with the corresponding names of its powers.

In particular, before his death, Feiyunzi had even marked on the jade slip the power structure of the Northern Sea after the Primordial Demon Sect.

Moluo Stream occupied twelve sea regions, corresponding exactly to the twelve Stream Lords.

The Penglai Immortal Sect [TERM?], returned from the Demon Sea, occupied three great sea regions alone.

The Myriad Immortals Assembly, centered on the Fulong Mountain Range [TERM?], occupied eight sea regions in scattered fashion. It seemed like many, but three of them were located on the Three Oceans defense line [TERM?] and had to face demon beast incursions year-round, so outsiders did not covet them at all.

Another three had originally been ancient territories of Nascent Soul powers.

In other words, the Myriad Immortals Assembly truly controlled only two wealthy sea regions.

The strongest power was the Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance [TERM?]!

With the strength of one alliance, it occupied twenty-nine complete sea regions, each with a Nascent Soul cultivator stationed there!

If this power were not an alliance of interests, relying solely on the prestige of the great expert Li Canghai [TERM?] to suppress it, with its people's hearts disunited internally, then even if Moluo Stream joined forces with the Penglai Immortal Sect [TERM?], it probably would not dare contend with it head-on.

Luo Chen did not pay much attention to the other scattered small powers.

Only when withdrawing from the jade slip did his divine sense deliberately linger on the sea regions occupied by the Tianyuan Merchant Alliance [TERM?].

Seven. Not many.

But the Tianyuan Merchant Alliance [TERM?] had grown increasingly influential over the years, gradually spreading through the righteous, demonic, and rogue cultivator factions. The entire Northern Sea could feel this merchant alliance's presence.

Especially the Tianyuan Dao Sect [TERM?] behind it!

That was a colossal entity standing in distant Central Continent!

Even when the Primordial Demon Sect still existed, the two had merely looked at each other across the sea and kept their wells from interfering with each other's rivers.

Placing the jade slip alone in an easy-to-reach place inside his storage ring, Luo Chen let out a breath.

"With this map, when I return to the cultivation world later, I can avoid many detours."

Afterward, he continued inventorying Feiyunzi's relics.

If one asked what was most abundant inside Feiyunzi's belt, what was it?

There was no doubt: things related to magic treasures!

One high-grade magic treasure, the Heavenly Dipper Furnace [TERM?], with no combat power and born only for artifact refining and alchemy.

Three high-grade magic treasure flying swords.

Twelve mid-grade magic treasures.

As for low-grade magic treasures and magic artifacts, there were very few.

Every single one had been refined by Feiyunzi's own hands, and each bore the unique cloud-pattern mark he had left.

If all these treasures were taken out, they would be enough to arm a smaller Nascent Soul sect. If it were a Golden Core sect, they could arm a major Golden Core sect to the teeth.

Luo Chen reached out and took out a long painted halberd, examining it with interest.

This was an unconventional magic treasure. Few cultivators in the cultivation world used such things.

The reason he cared so much was that this magic treasure's grade was not high, only low-grade, yet it had been placed among the high-grade magic treasures, side by side with the three flying swords.

Once in hand, Luo Chen's brow lifted.

Heavy!

An extreme weight made his wrist bend, and the painted halberd dropped straight toward the ground.

But what sort of person was Luo Chen? Even without using source power, he possessed unmatched divine strength.

He gripped the halberd shaft with one hand and the handle with the other. His muscles rose, and he slowly lifted it before his eyes.

"So heavy. It must carry the force of ten thousand jun [TERM?]."

"A cultivator's divine sense might be able to control it with difficulty, but it would be far too strenuous. This does not seem like a magic treasure refined for immortal cultivators."

"Could it be specially refined for those body refiners?"

Luo Chen's heart stirred, and a crimson power flowed from his arm to cover the painted halberd.

He did not have a body refiner's astral qi [TERM?], but he did have source power.

He wondered whether it could...

The next moment, source power covered it, and the painted halberd abruptly became lighter in his hand. Although it was still heavy as a mountain, he could at least swing it freely.

Luo Chen paused, then naturally revealed a smile.

Previously, after forming his Nascent Soul, he had still been thinking of specially refining a weapon that could withstand source power and amplify it.

Unexpectedly, the pillow had arrived just as he grew sleepy.

Its grade might be a little low, but merely relying on that weight of ten thousand jun [TERM?], no ordinary cultivator would dare bump against it.

"I wonder what material this magic treasure was refined from."

Luo Chen grew interested. His divine sense fell upon the painted halberd's dark shaft, as well as the crescent blades and spear tip flickering with faint gloomy light.

Relying on his own knowledge, he recognized some of the materials.

Geng Metal, Thunder Sand [TERM?], and a large amount of Traceback Mud [TERM?], but he could not identify the main material.

Hiss!

A wisp of azure flame emerged, and the Withering-Bloom True Flame covered the halberd shaft.

As the terrifying heat of the Withering-Bloom True Flame burned it, a special aura gradually surfaced.

It was a solid, earthlike feeling!

"Primordial Heavy Sky Ridge Stone [TERM?]!"

Luo Chen was stunned. He had never expected that the main material of this painted halberd would actually be the outer-heaven meteorite recorded in ancient texts, Primordial Heavy Sky Ridge Stone [TERM?]!

In ancient times, such a meteorite had descended from beyond the heavens.

It was not large, but after falling to the earth, it could not be shaken or pulled up by strength.

Therefore, someone had taken the idea of one yuan of weight [TERM?] and a mountain range descending from the heavens, giving it this vivid name.

The main material of this painted halberd was unmistakably Primordial Heavy Sky Ridge Stone [TERM?]. In fact, because the material was so special, Feiyunzi had been unable to engrave enough formations on it, which affected its grade.

"Wait, stop, stop!"

Luo Chen beckoned hastily and withdrew the Withering-Bloom True Flame.

The fire spirit had been playing too enthusiastically just now and had almost truly melted the painted halberd.

"I know you are impressive. Happy now?"

Luo Chen glared at the Withering-Bloom True Flame and put it away.

Ever since the Withering-Bloom True Flame had awakened spirit, it had always been very lively. In the past, Luo Chen had still suppressed its spirituality, but after forming his Nascent Soul, Luo Chen felt he could allow it to grow, so he had not suppressed it much.

Now it had grown even more unruly.

Ignoring the Withering-Bloom True Flame's grievance, Luo Chen happily planted the painted halberd into the earth.

Next, he would have to draw out some time to sacrificially refine this painted halberd as well, adding another method to his arsenal.

Finally, Luo Chen counted the spirit stones Feiyunzi had left behind.

Whoa!

It was also no small sum!

Adding together spirit stones of every grade, they amounted to a full five hundred million low-grade spirit stones.

If the thirty thousand high-grade spirit stones Luo Chen had obtained before were included and converted, Luo Chen's current fortune had reached eight hundred million!

And that did not even count Han Zhan's ten supreme-grade spirit stones.

Those counted as strategic resources and could not be measured as normal cultivation-world currency.

"Tsk, tsk! Sure enough, a horse does not grow fat without night grass, and a man does not grow rich without windfall wealth. I worked hard managing things for so many years, with spirit stones coming and going and being constantly consumed, and even after two hundred years I might not have had this much wealth. Yet with only a few fights, I leapt into enormous riches and no longer need to toil over spirit stones."

Luo Chen was deeply emotional.

But if he truly had to choose, he would absolutely not be willing to make his fortune this way.

Too dangerous!

After placing his own mark on Feiyunzi's cloud-pattern belt, changing it to red, and happily tying it around his waist, Luo Chen continued cultivating.

...

Time slowly passed.

The spiritual qi on Dragon Abyss Island, after its dying flash, finally began gradually declining.

Countless spiritual qi scattered day and night into heaven and earth and into the sea.

Even with formations blocking it, the formations had been born relying on the earth veins, and fundamentally could not stop the collapsing trend of spiritual qi venting outward.

This was exactly prosperity reaching its peak and then declining!

Luo Chen's cultivation also gradually approached its end.

In the third year after his realm stabilized, he optimized the Azure Yang Great Handprint [TERM?] and also unlocked Han Zhan's storage bag.

In the fifth year, he unlocked Feiyunzi's storage belt and gained a tremendous harvest.

In the seventh year, he completely refined the Soul-Summoning Banner [TERM?] of true-artifact grade.

In the eighth year, after several failed attempts to refine the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion [TERM?], Luo Chen found another path, using Han Zhan's early method to sacrificially refine the Octagonal Pavilion [TERM?] and obtain a certain level of authority.

The reason this true artifact was so difficult to refine was because it was an inherited true artifact.

Although the Penglai Immortal Sect [TERM?] had not completely handed it over to any single person, they had left extremely complex restrictions upon it.

With Luo Chen's current attainments, if he wanted to refine it completely, he would need at least several decades.

However, this portion of authority alone was already enough, sufficient to support Luo Chen's ordinary use.

When the ninth year arrived, Luo Chen's primary cultivation art reached Great Completion.

The Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture had reached Great Completion!

On this day, Luo Chen looked at the attribute panel, where the progress bar had reached [Grandmaster 100/100]. The entry flickered and transformed into the words [Great Completion].

No change?

Amid hesitation, Luo Chen once again circulated his lifebound cultivation art despite the spiritual qi being less than abundant.

He split his attention, sensing the absorption of external spiritual qi.

"Half again as much as before!"

"A small qualitative change, barely enough to support my cultivation in the Nascent Soul stage."

"In this way, changing my primary cultivation art is not so urgent."

Luo Chen breathed a sigh of relief.

This cultivation art was worthy of being called the number one fire-element cultivation art under heaven. Merely its Golden Core chapter possessed many wondrous uses.

Even after reaching the Nascent Soul stage, relying on Great Completion proficiency, it could still support a Nascent Soul cultivator's training.

Suddenly.

Luo Chen's heart stirred.

While absorbing the spiritual qi of heaven and earth, it seemed something had changed.

With a thought, his Nascent Soul domain abruptly unfolded!

The drifting fire-attribute spiritual qi of heaven and earth outside surged over one after another, and a powerful pressure enveloped the earth abyss.

At the same time, Luo Chen gave a soft shout.

"Come out!"

The next moment, the Withering-Bloom True Flame quietly poked its head out.

It looked at Luo Chen while trembling and unfurled its branches.

"As expected!"

Luo Chen controlled those branches formed from true flame and fused them into his Nascent Soul domain.

In an instant, an extremely destructive power began spreading outward.

Within the earth abyss, countless rocks began melting.

Luo Chen withdrew and rose, leaping into the sky above Dragon Abyss Island.

"Black King!"

Black King, who was cultivating on the island, responded and moved.

"Master, are you testing a move again?"

Luo Chen looked down at Black King and smiled faintly.

"This time, it is simple. Reinforce your whole body with source power and try resisting my Forest of All Things Fire Prison [TERM?]."

Black King gave an "oh" and was not particularly surprised.

A Nascent Soul domain, was it? Against ancient beasts like them, it did not have much effect.

Source power was something naturally unafraid of domain suppression.

Luo Chen had comprehended this before and had also taught the method to Black King.

At this moment, facing the blazing fire prison, Black King waited calmly for the domain suppression.

But very soon, his expression changed.

Looking at the streams of azure smoke rising from his body.

"How can this be?"

Huu...

Luo Chen exhaled at length.

Looking at his domain, his brows filled with joy.

The Great Completion Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture did not provide a particularly large cultivation bonus, at least for a Nascent Soul cultivator. It was only barely usable.

But the other effect it produced was unexpected.

Toward fire-attribute spiritual qi of heaven and earth, it possessed an extraordinary power of control!

That was drifting spiritual qi that had never been refined and had not been branded with his own mark!

Not only toward heaven and earth spiritual qi, even toward tangible fire, it possessed terrifying control, as though a superior naturally suppressed an inferior.

The Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit had been extremely arrogant before, and Luo Chen still needed to scold it.

But now, it trembled and allowed Luo Chen to control it.

Luo Chen simply fused it into the Forest of All Things Fire Prison [TERM?]... not a simple fusion, but a fusion of its innate property of burning and refining the vitality of living beings as well.

Therefore, the current Forest of All Things Fire Prison [TERM?] could not only suppress enemies, it had gained an additional divine ability.

Melting the Void [TERM?]!

It could melt away the spiritual qi of heaven and earth of other attributes, and even Black King's source power, which had not feared a Nascent Soul domain!

One could imagine that in battle, once the Forest of All Things Fire Prison [TERM?] emerged, enemies would be unable to control the spiritual qi of heaven and earth, while Luo Chen could control it. With one side weakened and the other strengthened, victory would be established.

"My hundred years of bitter cultivation were not in vain!"

A hundred years was no exaggeration!

From the moment Luo Chen obtained the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture and cultivated it all the way until now, a hundred years had already passed.

Looking across all the cultivation arts and secret arts Luo Chen had learned in his life, no other art had consumed so much time.

And the final gain truly satisfied Luo Chen immensely.

"I wonder what wonders the Nascent Soul chapter of the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture possesses?"

Luo Chen indulged in that thought for a moment, then returned to the earth abyss.

...

Without realizing it, the tenth year arrived.

The spiritual qi on Dragon Abyss Island had already fallen to a level fundamentally unsuitable for a Nascent Soul Perfected Being's cultivation.

Even in the earth abyss, only the cave dwelling Luo Chen protected with all his strength barely retained the quality of a fourth-tier spirit vein, and it was still continuously falling.

This year, Luo Chen was two hundred and twenty-six years old!

In the morning, he slowly opened his eyes and blinked.

"It is time to go!"

Luo Chen leisurely rose and left the earth abyss.

Outside, Black King and the others, who had been notified in advance, were already prepared.

Luo Chen extended his right hand, and an octagonal pavilion slowly appeared in his palm.

"Jinghe, take this treasure and collect all the spirit soil on the island. Do not leave behind a single inch!"

Sang Jinghe [TERM?] had already succeeded in forming his core. At this moment, he possessed an early Golden Core realm and could barely control the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion [TERM?].

He took the treasure and left.

"Heavenjade, go into the earth abyss and extract all the spirit stone raw ore we have discovered."

Heavenjade gladly accepted the order.

Even though those raw ores had insufficient internal spiritual qi because the spiritual qi was venting away, their grade remained. In the future, as long as they were arranged properly, these raw ores could create a spirit land.

"Black King!"

"Here!"

"Go take away all the Dragon Earthworms [TERM?] on the island!"

Black King laughed and twisted his enormous body toward the northern side of Dragon Abyss Island.

If it were another demon beast or ancient beast, taking away so many Dragon Earthworms [TERM?] would be very difficult.

But he was different.

When he had been healing thirty years ago, the blood pool had been infused with the blood of several million Dragon Earthworms [TERM?], and he had completely absorbed it.

In a certain sense, he also possessed the bloodline of the Dragon Earthworms [TERM?].

Previously, because the Dragon Earthworms [TERM?] had been slaughtered in large numbers, many on the island had fled, but later many Dragon Earthworms [TERM?] returned one after another. Apart from the island's leaking spiritual qi, part of the reason was also the high-level Dragon Earthworm [TERM?] aura Black King naturally emitted.

Kin drew close to kin!

The Rosy Hill [TERM?] of the past was now only an enormous pit.

There, a huge white tiger stood quietly.

Twenty years had passed. Wind and rain had battered it, but they had not changed its restrained killing intent.

Moss and vines covered it, adding a bit of ancient desolation.

Looking at this huge white tiger, Luo Chen took a deep breath and leapt onto its head.

Sitting cross-legged, his fingertips cast out restriction after restriction, landing on the white tiger.

He also knew puppet arts!

Having stayed beside Han Zhan for so long, even by mere exposure he would have learned some.

Perfectly controlling a puppet might be difficult, but subduing one was simple.

Especially!

He now had a split soul!

When all the restrictions had been laid down, Luo Chen moved his mind, and the split soul entered the puppet's head.

Vaguely, he could sense that deep within the puppet, there was a dormant soul.

This white tiger puppet was not a dead object!

Within it was a bound tiger soul. Facing that tiger soul, Luo Chen's split soul slowly approached.

...

Cape Heaven Matron [TERM?], wearing a green-and-floral Daoist robe, walked out from the mountain.

"Matron!"

"Matron!"

Cape Heaven Matron [TERM?] nodded, looking at her children and grandchildren with a satisfied smile.

"Not bad. These years, you have not neglected your cultivation. Next, follow me to the Fallen Abyss Sea Region [TERM?]!"

One month later.

Cape Heaven Matron [TERM?], who had traveled day and night, brought more than a hundred elite clansmen outside Dragon Abyss Island.
