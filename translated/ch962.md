# Chapter 962: A Contest from Afar, Flying Heavenly Yaksha

Amidst a scattering of white bones, Luo Chen slowly rose to his feet.

Looking at the gu insect in his hand, a look of surprise appeared on his face.

This place was practically indistinguishable from the legendary Netherworld.

A great number of cultivators and demon beasts had died here unjustly, their resentment unable to find release. Over time, this had become an extreme yin land.

As a result, after these creatures had been dead for a thousand years, they naturally grew into all kinds of ghosts, monsters, and zombies.

Along the way, Luo Chen had seen countless white zombies and black zombies.

What he hadn't expected was to find even a Maozombie here, comparable to an Adept cultivator.

Maozombies, after centuries of cultivation and sedimentation, had tough fur and hair growing from their decaying flesh, and their bones had become as hard as steel.

They moved swiftly, had incredible escape speed, possessed astonishing defensive power, and were unafraid of a cultivator's true fire. Even in the cultivation world, they were a fearsome existence.

Of course, in front of someone like Luo Chen, who could be considered a top existence in the human world, they were nothing to worry about. He could destroy them with a flip of his hand.

What surprised Luo Chen was that these zombies actually had Corpse Poison Gu living on them.

Zombies and ghostly creatures from other continents wouldn't have such things.

"Was this also a method left behind by the great power Yin Yangzi before his fall?"

From Kuangnanzi, the Second Ancestor of the Golden Lion, Luo Chen had obtained some simple information about this realm.

He knew how unwilling and how mad Yin Yangzi had been before his death.

He had implanted various gu insects into the bodies of living cultivators and demon beasts, raising gu with living creatures. He had even reversed the process, planting living gu into dead things, trying to reverse the state of life and death.

As a result, within this Nether Mountain, a large number of strange and bizarre creatures had been left behind.

Maozombies were just the most insignificant among them.

"As long as I don't run into a Flying Heavenly Yaksha, it'll be fine."

Luo Chen muttered to himself, then casually put this Corpse Poison Gu into his storage ring.

This was a good thing. After the Hundred Poison King Gu devoured and refined it, it would release a large amount of corpse poison that could temper the physical body.

As the most powerful type of Flying Zombie, a Flying Heavenly Yaksha could naturally give birth to an even more powerful Corpse Poison Gu. But such an existence wasn't easy to deal with.

Luo Chen had no intention of provoking one, so he deliberately avoided places that emitted powerful auras.

Following the traces left by Li Tian Shi and his group, he changed his aura and pursued closely.

Occasionally encountering unexpected situations, he would cut through the knot with a swift blade, resolving them instantly.

The distance between them was constantly closing.

...

"Qi... Luo Chen has caught up."

Purple-Robed Marquis said with a somewhat unnatural expression.

The others were stunned, seemingly unable to believe it.

Only Li Tian Shi looked as if it were only natural.

"With this kind of personality, repaying kindness and revenge, it really is him." Li Tian Shi muttered, recalling the intelligence about the destruction of the top Initiate Sect, the Five Elements Divine Sect, in the Eastern Wilderness.

Then, he looked at Old Woman Redleaf.

She nodded slightly. "I have left five large and small formations. They should be enough to trap and kill him, without affecting our subsequent plans."

"Kill him?" Li Tian Shi shook his head. He didn't quite believe that, but as long as they could trap him for a while, that was enough.

His eyes gazed into the distance, and faintly, a gloomy palace appeared in his field of vision.

"The Yin Yang Tomb is almost here. Redleaf, I'll leave the rest to you."

Old Woman Redleaf's eyes lit up, and her wrinkled face almost relaxed.

"Leave the formations of the Yin Yang Tomb to me. But if the Ten Thousand Array Gu really exists, then this gu must be given to me according to the agreement!"

Li Tian Shi nodded. "I won't take any of the gains outside the tomb. Everyone can rely on their own abilities!"

As he said this, he took out several jade boxes from his storage ring.

"Purple-Robed Marquis, this is the Blood Transformation Gu I promised you!"

Purple-Robed Marquis took it with a look of anticipation. With this gu, even without the orthodox techniques of the Blood Sea lineage of the Primordial Demon Sect, he might still be able to learn the Blood Transformation Art.

In his ears, Li Tian Shi's voice of instruction came again.

"If Luo Chen breaks in, I hope you can hold him off for a while."

Purple-Robed Marquis hesitated for a moment, then, looking at the jade box in his hand, he nodded heavily.

"I will live up to your trust!"

The others also received similar rewards.

This trip to the Yin Yang Remnant Realm was different from a normal secret realm exploration. It was a team that Li Tian Shi had assembled using his personal connections.

For this trip, he had prepared a lot and paid a lot.

Now, he was just handing over the rewards in person.

Relatively speaking, if it hadn't been necessary for Luo Chen to exert great effort in breaking the door, the Hundred Poison King Gu shouldn't have been handed over to him so early.

After everything was ready, Li Tian Shi and Old Woman Redleaf entered the gloomy, illusory palace complex together.

Only four people were left outside.

They exchanged glances, then each found a spot to guard.

...

"Is this a backup plan left by Old Woman Redleaf?"

In a mountain stream where spiritual light flickered, Luo Chen stood amidst it, a look of realization on his face.

The style of the formation here was completely different from the other incomplete formations in Nether Mountain.

Those formations, rather than being meant to block external enemies, were more like prisons specifically used to trap Yin Yangzi's original research subjects.

It was just that as time passed, those formations gradually became incomplete, and the bound research subjects ran out, becoming the ghosts and monsters everywhere now.

But the formation under his feet was exceptionally complete, and its intent to suppress and kill was extremely strong.

Facing thousands of sword lights and blade shadows, Luo Chen acted as if he didn't see them.

A golden light flashed in his eyes. He stepped with the Moon Step, his figure flickering as he suddenly appeared in one spot. He pointed a finger.

"Break!"

A cracking sound suddenly came.

Thousands of sword lights and blade shadows fell like rain, and the true appearance of this place was instantly revealed.

Luo Chen smiled slightly. "To be able to set up a fourth-grade formation like this in a hurry, Redleaf's formation attainments are indeed impressive. But trying to trap me is still far from enough."

...

Inside the palace complex, Old Woman Redleaf, who was busy, suddenly paused in her movements.

Li Tian Shi, who was beside her, frowned. "What's wrong?"

Old Woman Redleaf exhaled a turbid breath. "Nothing, just that another one of the five formations I left behind has been broken. And it was the Blade and Spear Formation, which is said to have the strongest killing intent."

Li Tian Shi was stunned.

Another one broken?

"That makes it the fourth one, right?"

Old Woman Redleaf nodded. "Indeed, it's the fourth. I didn't expect Luo Chen's formation attainments to be so outstanding, completely on par with mine. The formations I set up in a hurry have too many flaws... I underestimated him."

She had indeed underestimated him.

But who could have known that Luo Chen, who was unmatched in body refinement, had outstanding Dao techniques, and was known as an Alchemy Grandmaster, could also have such attainments in the Dao of Formations?

Old Woman Redleaf took a deep breath. "But it doesn't matter. He won't be able to break the fifth formation so easily. If he doesn't break it, he can't get through. If he does break it, he'll be even more unable to get through!"

Li Tian Shi frowned. "Is it the formation you spent half a day setting up earlier?"

Old Woman Redleaf smiled smugly. "That Spirit-Trapping Formation wasn't originally set up for him. But if he walks into it, let him stay there!"

After saying this, she stopped paying attention to what was happening behind her and focused on probing the formation in front of her.

Li Tian Shi subconsciously glanced back, and his heart settled down.

If Luo Chen was trapped inside, that would be for the best.

After they took out the things from the Yin Yang Tomb, they could then spare the time to deal with him.

...

A vast swamp, with no place to set foot.

A figure, like a flying swallow, crisscrossed back and forth.

But after a long time, he returned to the same spot.

Luo Chen frowned deeply. His Crimson-Eyed Golden Pupils scanned the surroundings, trying to see the flaw in this formation.

But everything he saw was a flaw!

"This formation... it's really hard to know where to start!"

Most formations had one or two vital points. If you broke them, it would affect the natural operation of the grand formation, and the trapped person could naturally escape.

But having too many flaws had instead become a major obstacle for the person trying to break the formation.

"Was it a feint, or was it intentional?"

Luo Chen hesitated.

If he made a move, it could easily trigger a chain reaction.

If he triggered something that looked like a flaw but was actually an ambush point left intentionally by the other party, he would be in trouble.

"I need to test it out, but I can't test it myself!"

Luo Chen thought for a moment, and a thought stirred.

A black light emerged from his left arm, slowly coiling around his left shoulder.

Seeing this, Black King had a bitter look on his face.

But under Luo Chen's pressing gaze, he had no choice but to move.

"Don't worry, I'll act to protect you when the critical moment comes."

How could Black King be reassured? But now that things had come to this, he had to grit his teeth and do it.

Gnashing his teeth, he followed Luo Chen's instructions and charged straight into a patch of swamp on the ground.

Boom!

With the impact, mud shot into the sky, and a water column rose from the ground.

Under the scouring of the water column, the originally dim sky suddenly brightened.

Luo Chen was stunned. "The formation is broken?"

Black King rolled over in the swamp, feeling frustrated. "Master, you're just overthinking it. Why not just break the formation directly?"

Luo Chen didn't know whether to laugh or cry.

He hadn't expected that the formation left by Old Woman Redleaf was actually a "puzzle formation" specifically set up for clever people.

With this kind of formation, the more you thought about it, the easier it was to be constrained.

If you forced your way through like Black King...

"No, that's not right!"

Luo Chen seemed to think of something. His expression changed instantly, and he suddenly reached out to grab Black King.

Black King was still ignorant, but the next moment, a large amount of black fog suddenly erupted from the mud beneath him.

The black fog condensed into ghostly hands, grabbing at Black King.

"Oh my god!"

Black King let out a strange cry, twisting his body to dodge, but he was still entangled by the ghostly hands.

Just then, Luo Chen's Cloud-Seizing Divine Claw broke through the fog.

He grabbed Black King and pulled him outward.

"Come out!"

With the addition of immense force, Black King cried out in pain as he was forcibly pulled out.

In the swamp, a large amount of fog, having lost its target, seemed somewhat furious.

"Huff..."

A huge breathing sound arose, and the boundless black fog suddenly contracted back.

Deep in the swamp, a creature slowly opened its eyes.

As soon as it opened them, the wings on its back spread out, lifting it into the air. Its emotionless black eyes stared fixedly at the man opposite.

"Hiss, it's a zombie!" Black King shouted, still hissing in pain from the claw marks on his body.

Looking at the monster in front of him, whose spread wings almost cast a shadow over the earth, Luo Chen's mood was somewhat heavy.

This was not a simple ordinary zombie.

With wings on its back, ghostly fog pervading, and even karmic fire rising.

This was the most powerful existence among Flying Zombies, the Flying Heavenly Yaksha!

In terms of realm, it was comparable to a human Sage at the late stage.

Especially its physical body, which possessed extremely strong spiritual energy defense, could be said to be invulnerable to all techniques!

Sure enough, Old Woman Redleaf's backup plan was not that simple.

She had set up a suspicious formation to waste his time and energy, and had also secretly set up a Spirit-Summoning Formation.

When the suspicious formation was broken, it would inevitably awaken the Flying Heavenly Yaksha sleeping deep underground.

Once such a peerless fiend came out, its anger and malice would be directed at whom, it went without saying.

"Hu...man..." The Flying Heavenly Yaksha stared at Luo Chen, its mouth laboriously making two sounds.

Luo Chen, whose face had been gloomy, suddenly smiled.

"Redleaf, if you think you can trap me with just a Flying Heavenly Yaksha, then you're thinking too simply."

Without any visible movement from Luo Chen, his robes fluttered without wind.

A wisp of golden light slowly emerged behind him.

"Caw!"

A clear cry suddenly sounded.

A fire phoenix rose into the air.

It was not a spell, but the manifestation of the Nirvana Sacred Flame!

As soon as this flame appeared, the malicious Flying Heavenly Yaksha's face clearly showed a look of cowering.

Without saying a word, without doing anything, it unhesitatingly fell to the ground and disappeared into the vast Nether Mountain.

Flying through the sky, invulnerable to all techniques, it was indeed the ultimate Flying Zombie, the Flying Heavenly Yaksha!

Seeing this, Luo Chen couldn't help but breathe a sigh of relief.

He put away the Nirvana Sacred Flame.

Black King felt a bit regretful. "That thing wanted to eat me just now. Master, aren't you going to teach it a lesson?"

Luo Chen glanced at him, and Black King couldn't help but shrink his head.

Luo Chen waved his hand and said, "This thing isn't much of a threat to me, but it would take a lot of effort to completely deal with it. Right now, I don't have the mind to tangle with it."

The strength of a Flying Heavenly Yaksha lay in its immunity to spells and its physical body, which could withstand magical treasures.

If an ordinary great cultivator encountered one, it would indeed be very troublesome, even leaving them without a way to start.

But for Luo Chen, who was already powerful in body refinement, it was just a hand-to-hand fight.

But that would take too much time.

Since that was the case, why not use the Nirvana Sacred Flame, which could restrain ghostly fog, to intimidate it, scare it off, and be done with it simply.

"Above the ruins of the Nether Origin Sect, the sacred flame suppressed the sea, and all ghosts could not come out. It shows that the Nirvana Sacred Flame has this kind of restraining effect. Now, after experimenting, it's indeed the case."

With a thought, Luo Chen's deep gaze fell into the distance.

"Li Tian Shi, how many more methods do you have to stop me?"

...

Outside the Yin Yang Tomb.

Two figures stood together, chatting in low voices.

After a while, they flew off in different directions.

White Bone Lord was sitting cross-legged on a green stone. When he saw Purple-Robed Marquis and the Hidden Sword Pavilion Master approaching, he raised his eyelids slightly.

Purple-Robed Marquis didn't want to deal with this person. The people of the White Bone Sect had quite extreme temperaments, and it was best not to provoke them if possible.

He just walked towards Meng Shentong.

However, before he could get close, a bone spike suddenly emerged from the ground.

"What do you mean?" Purple-Robed Marquis frowned. "I have something I want to discuss with Fellow Daoist Meng. It has nothing to do with you!"

White Bone Lord said coldly, "Fellow Daoist Meng is recovering his mana. No one is to disturb him."

Purple-Robed Marquis was stunned. "On the way here, Meng Shentong didn't exert much effort. What mana does he need to recover?"

The Hidden Sword Pavilion Master beside him suddenly flicked his hand, and a wisp of sword qi shot out.

Pfft!

Meng Shentong, who was sitting cross-legged and resting, shattered like a bubble.

Seeing this, Purple-Robed Marquis's expression changed slightly.

"Where did Meng Shentong go?"

Under his questioning, White Bone Lord slowly stood up.

A bone sword suddenly appeared in his hand.

Purple-Robed Marquis was on high alert, a purple light trembling all over his body. "What are you going to do?"

White Bone Lord didn't respond, just looked up in the direction they had come from.

"Instead of being curious about where Meng Shentong went, the two of you should think about how to deal with that one from the Eastern Wilderness!"

The wind and clouds converged, and the ghostly qi dispersed.

A black figure appeared abruptly.

Standing high in the sky, he looked down at the three people below with a calm expression.

"You want to stop me?"
