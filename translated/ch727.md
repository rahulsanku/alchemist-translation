# Chapter 727: Wanderer Moluo, Burning the Sea and Ending the World

The first time Luo Chen had seen a Blood God Child was also on Coldlight Island [TERM?].

At that time, Blood Nightmare Moluo had possessed He Yuan [TERM?] and personally recruited Luo Chen.

But the Blood God Child that truly left a deep impression on Luo Chen had been in Withered Wood Ridge [TERM?].

Blood Wanderer had refined a Sage cultivator of the Primordial Demon Sect into a Blood God Child and used it to capture the fifth-tier Withered Blood Vine.

That time, the Blood God Child's strength had been unforgettable to him.

The Blood God Children he had seen both times shared one common characteristic: there were two entirely different auras on the Blood God Child.

One belonged to the original owner, while the other belonged to the controller.

Logically speaking, such a profound method should not have such an obvious flaw, but they had indeed displayed themselves this way.

It was precisely because of this that when Luo Chen saw an Adept cultivator of the Wu clan erupt with blood light and reveal two kinds of aura, he instantly recognized its essence.

"Is it Blood Wanderer or Blood Nightmare Moluo?"

At the first moment, Luo Chen thought of these two.

He waved a hand, signaling Heavenjade and Sang Jinghe [TERM?] to retreat.

And the wretched-looking early-stage Adept Blood God Child now raised its empty eye sockets and stared fixedly at Luo Chen.

"Why did you come disturb me?"

It was Blood Nightmare Moluo!

With only one sentence, Luo Chen recognized the other party.

His contact with Blood Nightmare Moluo was not deep, while Blood Wanderer had left a deeper impression, so he quickly judged that this was not Blood Wanderer.

And in the current Northern Sea, the only powerful figures openly still refining Blood God Children were these two.

Was this fellow not being madly hunted by Blood Wanderer?

How had he run to Coldlight Island [TERM?] to hide?

This place was extremely close to both Moluo Stream and the Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance, and could be considered their border... the most dangerous place was the safest place?

While a hundred thoughts turned in Luo Chen's mind, a cry of alarm came from behind him.

"Master, look!"

Luo Chen's pupils shrank slightly. He saw the mortals on Baiyang Island [TERM?] melting!

One mortal after another turned into masses of filthy blood and merged into the ground.

The nearby Qi Refinement and Initiate cultivators were all horrified, panic-stricken, and shouting wildly.

And such changes were clearly not limited to Baiyang Island [TERM?].

Luo Chen raised his eyes and looked far away. On every island of the shattered Coldlight Archipelago [TERM?], large and small, this scene was unfolding.

Tens of millions of mortals silently transformed into masses of filthy blood, without even time to let out a single cry.

"Why did you disturb me!"

A fierce wind blew at his face.

Luo Chen pointed out with one finger, sword qi crisscrossing and surging, instantly shredding that early-stage Adept Blood God Child into chunks of flesh.

The chunks fell to the ground and also turned into muddy blood, merging into the earth.

Luo Chen abruptly lowered his head!

His divine sense began spreading wildly with Baiyang Island [TERM?] as the center.

On every island, blood light shot into the sky.

The originally azure sea was tinged with an eerie crimson.

Countless low-level cultivators attempted to fly away from their islands, but before they could escape high into the sky, they were enveloped by streams of blood light.

Luo Chen took a deep breath, his gaze sharp. "It is a formation. Blood Nightmare Moluo has altered the island-linking formation, enveloping the tens of millions of mortals and over a hundred thousand low-level cultivators of the Coldlight Archipelago [TERM?], and affecting them without their knowledge."

"We cannot remain here!"

"Go!"

With a sweep of his sleeve, Luo Chen rolled up Heavenjade and Sang Jinghe [TERM?] and was about to rush into the sky.

At this moment, the dome of heaven became like a furnace, and a red canopy covered their heads.

Without thinking, Luo Chen struck out with an Azure Yang Great Handprint.

In an instant, the red canopy churned, and a five-fingered bloody handprint appeared in the sky.

Luo Chen himself, however, was forced to fall back onto Baiyang Island [TERM?].

"That red canopy is a Sage domain?"

Luo Chen's expression changed slightly. He had already seen it clearly.

This Blood Nightmare Moluo had, like him, merged some kind of power into his domain and formed a special transformation.

Sacred-land disciples were truly troublesome!

The earth beneath his feet trembled.

Around him, it was no longer only mortals being affected. Strange changes began appearing one after another on Qi Refinement cultivators and Initiate cultivators as well.

Swish! Swish! Swish!

One gaze after another, one spiritual sense after another, gathered toward Luo Chen.

Almost at the same time, Luo Chen felt as though thorns were pricking his back.

"Damn it. Has Blood Nightmare Moluo gone mad? Not only did he refine tens of millions of mortals, he even planted devil seeds [TERM?] in more than a hundred thousand low-level cultivators."

Luo Chen had already seen that these low-level cultivators did not deserve to be called Blood God Children at all. They had merely lost their minds under the influence of the formation.

And this formation was clearly irreversible. Once it was fully completed, everyone within it would turn into filthy blood just like those mortals.

Luo Chen glanced at Heavenjade and Sang Jinghe [TERM?], his brows creasing slightly.

They were too much of a hindrance!

Sang Jinghe [TERM?], his face full of panic, had all his attention on Luo Chen. Seeing Luo Chen's gaze at this moment, his heart could not help but lurch.

"Sage, what do we do now?"

Luo Chen drew a deep breath. The ten fingers of both hands flew and each formed a seal.

Three breaths later, the seals fell, and his hands pressed together.

Afterward, his right palm pushed out horizontally.

A palm-sized azure handprint slowly flew out. It contained Luo Chen's pure magic power, and carried a powerful enslaving force, absorbing the wandering fire-attribute spiritual qi between heaven and earth.

As the azure handprint continued flying forward, its size grew larger and larger.

Rumble...

Wherever the handprint passed, blood light vanished, islands sank, and the boundless ocean surged in wild waves.

A passage appeared.

Luo Chen lightly patted the void behind the two of them with both hands, and his magic power pushed them to shoot out along the vacuum passage opened by the Azure Yang Great Handprint.

Heavenjade turned her head in surprise. Seeing Luo Chen's white robes whipping as he stood in place, she was instantly filled with worry.

"Master, leave with us!"

"Heavenjade, take Jinghe and go first. I will follow soon."

The Azure Yang Great Handprint transformed after the Sage stage was far more powerful than before, and because it had incorporated the Spirit-Enslaving Seal, once struck out it was completely outside Luo Chen's control and would grow stronger and stronger.

If no one forcibly interrupted it, the Azure Yang Great Handprint, constantly gathering momentum as it advanced, was enough to open a passage.

Luo Chen no longer looked at the two, but spread out all his divine sense, searching for Blood Nightmare Moluo's true body.

He could not leave now.

Because that red-canopy-like domain was locking onto him tightly.

And because of the formation's support, unless Luo Chen could teleport over a range of hundreds or thousands of li, the other party could keep him locked.

Behind Luo Chen, the Azure Yang Great Handprint traveled north all the way, finally leaving the Coldlight Archipelago [TERM?] and striking out a thread of daylight in the space stained deep red.

And this daylight closed in an instant.

Luo Chen lightly stomped the ground and slowly flew into the air.

Beneath his feet, Baiyang Island [TERM?] began to melt.

Countless streams of filthy blood flowed into the ocean.

Figures suffused with blood light rose from every direction and rushed toward Luo Chen.

Seeing this, Luo Chen snorted coldly.

"Hiding your head and showing your tail, and you are worthy of being called a Moluo of your generation?"

His right hand lifted in support, and a round blazing sun slowly rose.

When it reached above his head, the blazing sun trembled and abruptly shattered into tens of thousands of sparks.

Luo Chen flicked a finger, and the sparks shot out, flying toward those low-level cultivators with identical expressions.

Hiss! Hiss! Hiss! Hiss! Hiss! Hiss!

Piercing sounds rang out without end.

They were merely a hundred thousand or so low-level cultivators!

Before absolute individual might, numbers had already lost meaning.

Blood Nightmare Moluo's attempt to attack Luo Chen using these degraded versions of Blood God Children was simply wishful thinking.

But after successively killing more than ten thousand cultivators and carving out an empty zone without people, Luo Chen's expression changed slightly.

Between heaven and earth, an invisible force seemed to be gathering toward him.

And that force was almost exactly the same as the one that had previously enveloped the low-level cultivators.

"He wants to refine me into a Blood God Child too?"

With a thought, the Withering-Bloom True Flame inside Luo Chen stirred and enveloped his exterior, blocking the erosion of those forces.

Not only that, the Forest of All Things Fire Prison [TERM?] spread open, melting the red-canopy domain overhead.

And Luo Chen himself somewhat awkwardly formed a set of complex hand seals.

"This time, I want to see whether you come out or not!"

Luo Chen's eyes were cold. An illusory fire phoenix spread its wings behind him and leisurely flew out.

The fire phoenix was lifelike, its proud posture beyond compare.

The moment it flew out, it skimmed along the sea.

Wherever it passed, the blood sea evaporated, islands melted, and countless living beings were taken before they could react.

It was precisely the fourth-tier spell Luo Chen had only recently entered the door of: Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain [TERM?].

Under ordinary circumstances, Luo Chen absolutely would not use such a high-level spell, only recently learned and requiring a great deal of time to cast, during battle.

But Blood Nightmare Moluo not showing himself gave him enough time.

Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain [TERM?], cast calmly, made Luo Chen rather satisfied with its power.

It truly had a sense of burning the sea and destroying the world!

His divine sense probed the changes in the rolling blood waves.

Suddenly, Luo Chen's gaze condensed.

"So you were here!"

Luo Chen pointed with two fingers like a sword. The fire phoenix abruptly turned and plunged into the sea region where Jadecold City's ruins had once stood.

Boom!

A violent explosion instantly rang out.

There was no smoke or dust, only boiling mist soaring to the heavens.

Luo Chen's spirit eyes were like lightning, piercing through the fog and seeing a figure slowly emerge within the blood sea.

White hair, red robes, and mad eyes.

It was precisely Blood Nightmare Moluo's true body, whom he had once seen in Bright Radiance Heaven!

The moment he appeared, the red canopy covering the sky pressed down overhead.

Luo Chen was not afraid in the slightest. The Forest of All Things Fire Prison [TERM?] spread out, melting the power of the void and burning hole after hole into that red canopy.

Because of these holes, the wandering fire-attribute spiritual qi between heaven and earth drilled inside and gathered toward Luo Chen.

In their first direct collision, neither side won or lost, and neither domain could do anything to the other.

The madness in Blood Nightmare Moluo's eyes paused. Looking at Luo Chen's face, he found him somewhat unfamiliar.

"Who... are you?"

Luo Chen froze. He did not recognize him?

But with a turn of thought, it was normal. Their contact had been limited to begin with.

That previous recruitment had also been conducted with the other party controlling He Yuan [TERM?], that Blood God Child, to meet him.

Even when he later ordered the Hundred Crafts Mountain Master [TERM?] to hunt Luo Chen, it had only been a casual sentence.

Especially now that Luo Chen's appearance had changed and was entirely different from his appearance in Bright Radiance Heaven.

This was good. Since the other party did not recognize him, this battle was currently unnecessary.

Although there was hatred, Luo Chen had only just entered the Sage realm, while the other party had not only formed his Nascent Soul for many years, but was also a sacred-land disciple with extraordinary methods. The fact that he had survived Blood Wanderer's pursuit for so many years showed that his strength was not ordinary.

This grudge could be repaid another day!

Luo Chen had the intention to leave, but heaven did not follow human wishes.

Blood Nightmare Moluo shook his head by himself. "You disturbed my sleep and ruined my blood food. Since that is so, stay."

As he spoke, he beckoned with an empty hand.

The enormous banyan tree [TERM?] growing like a giant umbrella over the sea suddenly cracked and split apart.

A streak of sword light flew out from the trunk and landed in his hand.

It was that blood sword!

Luo Chen's heart trembled. He knew this sword. Back then at the Jadecold City auction, it had been called a spirit treasure.

Even though Han Zhan later explained that it was only a pseudo-spirit treasure [TERM?], the fact that its power could threaten Blood Wanderer meant its killing power could not be underestimated.

Moluo raised the sword and cut down with an utterly plain slash.

A brilliant sword light shot out.

Luo Chen did not dare be careless and moved both hands at once.

Seven fire rings layered one over another in front of him.

Sevenfold Blazing Heaven Rings!

Not only that, his robes billowed, and armor appeared.

All defenses were already prepared.

Yet the result...

Blood Nightmare Moluo held the God-Slaying Blood Sword [TERM?] horizontally before him, looking at a thread of crimson blood on it, and the madness in his eyes that had originally receded became even denser.

The blood sword trembled faintly.

"It seems you like his blood very much!"

Luo Chen looked at the sword wound across his palm and lowered his head without speaking.

That the Sevenfold Blazing Heaven Rings could not block this sword had been within his expectations.

But the Mystic Dust Armor, which had only recently advanced to a true artifact, also had no power to resist before this sword. That truly surpassed his imagination.

Luo Chen raised his head, his eyes narrowing slightly.

That sword...

Facing his gaze, the blood sword rang and trembled incessantly, as if excited.

It wanted to drink his blood?

Luo Chen smiled savagely, slapped a hand at his waist, and a painted halberd fell into his hand.

"You forced me!"

Before the words had fallen, the void exploded.

When he reappeared, Luo Chen was already in front of him.

The Ten-Thousand-Jun Halberd [TERM?] smashed down over his head.

As if he had not expected Luo Chen to possess such extreme speed, Blood Nightmare Moluo, unable to react in time, only managed to raise his sword to block.

Clang!

With one strike, Blood Nightmare Moluo was smashed into the blood sea.

Madness filled Luo Chen's expression. "If you will not let me leave, then I will not leave. Perfect. I also have an account to settle with you!"

Holding the halberd in one hand, he struck a palm with the other.

The Azure Yang Great Handprint fired instantly, pressing straight down on the blood sea.

Bang!

With the Great Handprint opening the way, Luo Chen charged directly into the blood sea.

A great battle unfolded in the depths of the dark sea.

Several breaths later, two figures rushed out from the sea surface again and flew into the sky.

Blood Nightmare Moluo attempted to pull away, but Luo Chen pursued him like a maggot in the bone.

After Blood Nightmare Moluo finally opened a tiny bit of space, Luo Chen kicked over, as fast as thunder and lightning, blasting a large hole through Blood Nightmare Moluo's body.

"Bastard!"

Red light exploded from Blood Nightmare Moluo's eyes and shot directly into Luo Chen's sea of consciousness.

Afterward, a wisp of pure devil qi [TERM?] was mobilized from his body, transforming into a heaven-propping great hand that abruptly slapped down.

But the next instant, Luo Chen, as if heavily struck, instantly woke clear, entirely unaffected by the soul attack.

Facing the giant heaven-propping hand, Luo Chen opened his mouth and spat.

A blue-gold flame flew out, transforming into a fire tree that wrapped around it.

The movement of his hands was not slow either. The Ten-Thousand-Jun Halberd [TERM?] was drawn back behind him like a javelin, then hurled out.

Waves of blood rose into the sky, blocking the Ten-Thousand-Jun Halberd [TERM?].

The God-Slaying Blood Sword [TERM?] floated up, hidden within the blood waves, and stabbed toward Luo Chen.

"Since you like drinking my blood so much, I will let you drink your fill!"

Luo Chen roared angrily. His left hand instantly swelled larger, his five fingers spread like claws, and with one grip through the void, he seized the several-zhang area where the God-Slaying Blood Sword [TERM?] was located.

Cloud-Seizing Divine Claw, released and withdrawn as he wished.

Luo Chen felt the piercing pain, and the madness on his face grew thicker.

But that enormous force and piercing sword qi kept erupting in his hand.

Bang!

The blood sword shot out like a dragon, escaping this claw.

Luo Chen was not discouraged. The wings behind him spread, and his speed soared by another three parts.

Not only that, the Withering-Bloom True Flame completely enveloped his whole body, turning him into a fire god.

At this moment, apart from the Archaic true body [TERM?], Luo Chen had already entered his strongest state. Wherever he passed, all surrounding blood qi evaporated.

He charged toward Blood Nightmare Moluo again.

Between the two of them, there was an indelible difference in realm. Blood Nightmare Moluo was at the third level of the Sage stage, while he was at the first.

The various spells and combat experience Luo Chen had been proud of in the past did not hold much advantage compared to this elder from the Primordial Demon Sect.

Since that was so, he would bring out his own strengths and engage in close combat as much as possible!

From the very beginning, Luo Chen had already settled the battle plan.

The direction of the subsequent battle, apart from Blood Nightmare Moluo's sword being too sharp and his body being too strong, which were somewhat unexpected, was basically about the same as Luo Chen had predicted.

In terms of fleshly body, Luo Chen guessed the other party had likely also cultivated some body-refining art.

But Luo Chen was even more confident in his own physique.

Especially since he still had a trump card!

In midair, spiritual light erupted from Luo Chen's eyes.

Mirror Flower Water Moon!

Blood Nightmare Moluo, who had been watching his movements at all times, was instantly struck.

But after one instant of stiffness, he recovered.

Fu Chaosheng had said that Blood Nightmare Moluo had gone against his master's ancestral teachings and attempted to cultivate all three lineages together.

Now it seemed this was not false in the slightest.

Whether it was the life-bound cultivation art of the Blood Sea lineage, the pure devil qi [TERM?] that transformed into the heaven-propping giant hand, or even the methods of the soul-refining lineage now, he had mastered them all.

But Luo Chen had never counted on Mirror Flower Water Moon to succeed.

What he wanted was only this instant of stiffness.

Crackle!

In the void, a silver serpent trembled.

A wisp of lightning arc struck Blood Nightmare Moluo's body.

The power of tribulation lightning!

Because of this sudden attack, Blood Nightmare Moluo's whole body stiffened.

And Luo Chen had already fully closed the distance.

His ten fingers flicked repeatedly. Overlord Removes Armor appeared again, injecting streams of source power into the other party's body.

Each strike prevented Blood Nightmare Moluo from regaining control of his body.

Afterward, Luo Chen's hands became claws and clamped onto the other party's shoulders.

Then he tore fiercely!

"Open for me!"

Riiip...

The complete body immediately split into two halves.

At this moment, the blood sword was sharp in vain, with no opponent left!

Luo Chen stood in the air, but there was little joy on his face.

"Where is the Nascent Soul?"

The two halves of the body in his hands turned into filthy blood, falling into the sea like torrential rain.

He lowered his head and saw the blood waves in the sea below roll even more violently.

A huge shadow was faintly discernible beneath the sea surface.

"As long as the blood sea remains, you are undying and indestructible?"

The madness in Luo Chen's eyes gradually receded. He subconsciously glanced in a certain direction, then made a decision.

"I will settle accounts with you later!"

With a thought, the Ten-Thousand-Jun Halberd [TERM?] was summoned back.

Just before leaving, his figure paused, and his gaze turned toward the ancient great sword that wished to plunge into the blood sea.

"But I will collect the interest!"

He reached out a claw, and the Cloud-Seizing Divine Claw tore through the air.

Sensing danger, the blood sword erupted with sword light and directly broke through this claw.

However, behind this claw, a small cauldron was spreading nine chains, tightly trapping it.

The blood sword trembled madly, but that suppressive force was unprecedented and difficult to imagine.

"Go!"

Luo Chen no longer hesitated. He stomped the void, activating Voidburst Step, and when he reappeared, he was already a hundred zhang away.

His wings trembled lightly, and his speed became even more unimaginable.

From within the sea, a furious voice rang out.

"Return the sword to me!"

What answered him was Luo Chen's cold laugh, and an angry roar like a nightmare.

"Found you!"

On the distant horizon, a deeper blood light shot over like a meteor descending from the heavens.

Boom!

Blood light descended, and the blood sea trembled.

Blood Wanderer, his face covered in thick beard, reached one hand toward the sea.

"Roll out for me!"

*(End of Chapter)*
