# Chapter 733: Blood Sword Yuantu, Deep-Planted Soul Seal, Sword-Halberd Sets Sail, Divine Origin Seals the City

A pitch-black hilt, a blood-red blade.

A speck of cold light flickered dimly, like a solitary star. Only when one looked carefully would one suddenly realize it was the incomparably sharp sword tip.

This was the God-Slaying Blood Sword, which had once shaken an era.

God-Slaying was not its true name. It was only because rumor said this sword had drunk its fill of Ascendant blood.

Luo Chen looked at the object, somewhat hesitant in his heart.

By now, he was no longer that impoverished Qi Refinement rogue cultivator of Dahe Market from back then.

As an honored Sage, he could be said to possess countless magic treasures at his side.

But among his many treasures, there were in truth only three that could truly enter his discerning eye.

The first needed no explanation. It was naturally the Primordial Unity Cauldron, his life-bound treasure that he had nourished for a long time. This treasure's foundation had been abundant to begin with, and it had successively fused with nameless gray light and the power of tribulation lightning. After advancing to a true artifact, he could use it as easily as moving his own arm, and its effects were extraordinary.

In external battle, the Primordial Unity Cauldron could imprison and suppress, its might limitless. In assisting internal cultivation, it could temper the divided soul and also refine pills and forge artifacts. Especially with regard to the latter, aside from his superb alchemy, the Primordial Unity Cauldron had rendered great service in allowing Luo Chen to refine the Great Soul Pill and Hongyuan Pill in such a short time.

The second treasure Luo Chen cherished and valued was that White Tiger Puppet.

Battle strength enough to rival a late-stage Sage. So long as he could control it, it meant Luo Chen could instantly gain a great cultivator-level assistant.

Originally, Luo Chen had also wanted to take control of the White Tiger Puppet first. Cultivating the divided soul and studying puppet arts were both preparations for this.

However, after careful consideration, Luo Chen temporarily suppressed this impulse.

There was no other reason. The white tiger spirit soul within that puppet was truly far too powerful. With Luo Chen's present divine-soul strength, he might not be able to enslave it perfectly.

Even Han Zhan back then had spent enormous effort before he could barely drive it once or twice, and even then, he had been unable to unleash its full might.

Luo Chen believed that whether in divine-soul strength or puppet-art attainment, he was still some distance from Han Zhan at his peak.

Furthermore, if he wanted to refine this puppet, he would need sufficiently spacious room.

If he openly took it out on someone else's territory, Little Qiong Mountain, it would likely invite no small trouble.

For instance, the scene not long ago when he exchanged on the Dao with Zhou Yunshen and their Nascent Souls collided had certainly fallen into the eyes of quite a few interested people.

For the time being, Luo Chen did not want to expose this hidden card.

Thus, after some thought, Luo Chen set his sights on this blood sword, which was in no way weaker than the White Tiger Puppet.

"A true artifact capable of killing an Ascendant great power. Even if it is not a spirit treasure, it cannot be far off."

"But unlike refining a spirit treasure, which requires the artifact spirit's recognition, this sword, in the end, remains within the category of true artifacts. It can be forcibly refined!"

"Even if the refinement fails and I destroy the spirit soul inside it, it will not affect this sword's might in the slightest."

"Blood Nightmare Moluo was likewise an early-stage Sage cultivator. What he could do, I may not be unable to do."

"And if I can once take command of this sword, my battle strength will surely undergo a qualitative leap!"

Luo Chen's hopes were not arrogant self-importance.

All along, although his strength had been great and his battle ability far exceeded those of the same realm, he had relied more on his own abilities and not much on external objects.

To put it unpleasantly, Luo Chen had never had any truly powerful offensive weapon.

During the Qi Refinement stage, his main battle method had been spells. It had been the same during the Initiate stage. In the Adept stage, although he had many treasures, there had been no handy offensive magic treasure either.

Over all these years, only that Mystic Flame Sword had been rather compatible with him.

But unfortunately, the Mystic Flame Sword had been a crudely made item, and its grade had been only low-grade.

This blood sword in his hand had killed an Ascendant great power as a true artifact. It was absolutely a supreme item among supreme items, and perhaps in the future, it even had hope of advancing to spirit-treasure level.

At present, Luo Chen's realm was only Sage, and this sword was also relatively suitable. Once refined, there would be no embarrassing scene like the Heaven-Refining Cauldron self-destructing at every turn.

As for how to refine the blood sword spirit soul, Luo Chen already had some ideas.

The hesitation gradually faded. Amid solemnity, his expression grew ever firmer.

His divine sense spread out, invading the blood sword bit by bit.

Even though blood light wrapped around it, it could not stop Luo Chen's persistence.

Within the Primordial Unity Cauldron, Luo Chen was master!

This sword was nothing more than a prisoner. How far could it resist?

Very quickly, Luo Chen found that wisp of spiritual light.

"Is this the blood sword's spirit soul?"

It seemed human yet not human, beast yet not beast, curled together like a placenta, with a faint stench lingering around it.

Luo Chen frowned. He could not find any corresponding race similar to this object in his memories.

Demon beast? Devil beast? Even a man-made strange beast, or perhaps some naturally intelligent spirit creature?

Setting aside what this thing belonged to, his divine sense began probing along the spirit soul's entire body, attempting to find an opening that would be easy to break through.

Time passed bit by bit.

Gradually, Luo Chen found some clues on this motionless spirit soul.

"Three layers of seals in total!"

"One layer has already been broken. Blood filth spreads over it, extremely corrosive. If my guess is correct, this should be the work of the Primordial Demon Sect's Blood Sea Ancestor. He attempted to refine this sword, but was heavily injured instead. Afterward, his lifetime's Dao attainment lodged upon this sword until Blood Nightmare Moluo inherited it."

"The second layer of seal is filled with gaps. Is this Blood Nightmare Moluo's method?"

"That is also good. Following these gaps, perhaps I can plant the soul seal onto the spirit soul."

Luo Chen withdrew his divine sense, realization appearing on his face.

Previously, Blood Nightmare Moluo must have had thoughts of thoroughly refining this sword, but he failed. The gaps on those seals were the evidence.

Then could Luo Chen succeed?

The corners of Luo Chen's mouth rose slightly. He already had a plan in his heart.

...

Three days later.

Luo Chen sat upright before the Primordial Unity Cauldron, both eyes closed, his hands forming seals.

Within his Purple Mansion, the tiny Nascent Soul seemed to answer him from inside and outside. Its plump little white hands formed the same seal.

As he breathed and circulated energy, the muscles of Luo Chen's face trembled. Gradually, a wisp of golden light slowly flew out from between his brows.

Looking closely, it was a mark shaped like flame.

This was the soul seal condensed according to puppet arts.

Once planted into a puppet, it was far more convenient than thread-suspension control, magic-power control, or divine sense forming threads.

However, ordinary puppet masters could not condense such a lifelike soul seal with a complete form.

Only those who had mastered the method of divided souls and whose own divine-soul foundation was powerful could do this.

As the soul seal flew out, Luo Chen did not slacken in the slightest and directly cast it into the cauldron.

In an instant!

Blood light erupted inside the cauldron. The blood sword seemed to realize something, and layers of spiritual light burst out as it attempted to stop the soul seal's invasion.

Was it very unwilling?

Did it not want to sit and wait for death?

At this moment, Luo Chen gave a cold snort.

"Suppress!"

The Primordial Unity Cauldron trembled, and five colors, gold, green, blue, red, and yellow, intertwined like soft ribbons, binding the blood sword layer upon layer.

Not only that.

Crackle!

Wisps of silver-white lightning arcs crackled inside the cauldron, like whips constantly lashing and threatening beside the blood sword.

In fact, within the silver-white lightning arcs, strands of black lightning could faintly be seen.

The power of tribulation lightning the Primordial Unity Cauldron had absorbed back then had been a transformed black lightning beast!

The silver-white arcs were only the coarse surface.

The black lightning arcs were the essence of tribulation lightning's power.

Even Luo Chen, when fighting enemies normally, did not dare easily summon black tribulation lightning. At this moment, he was merely driving it slightly.

Under this powerful suppression and threat, the blood sword trembled constantly, sword cries ringing again and again.

Only, in Luo Chen's ears, those sword cries sounded more like powerless wails of resistance.

"Submit to me."

Luo Chen chuckled softly, controlling the soul seal to follow those gaps, drill into the second layer of seal, and begin fusing with the spirit soul.

During this process, some images appeared in his mind.

A small beast was born from its mother's womb and devoured eight sibling brothers, its vicious nature full and fierce.

A dazzling treasured sword emerged from a furnace and was nourished on Weapon Mountain.

Then one day, the treasured sword broke out of the mountain and flew straight into a gloomy, dark world.

In that world, countless strange beasts existed, and visitors came in complex variety.

Many people attempted to subdue the treasured sword, but in the end, they failed with regret.

There were also those who briefly succeeded, but in the end, they did not leave that world either. Instead, they became sacrifices to the treasured sword.

Among them was an old man wearing plum-blossom blood robes in a style Luo Chen found somewhat familiar, who personally grasped this sword.

The images shifted.

The blood sword was carried out of that dark world in the mouth of an unintelligent dragon soul, yet halfway through, it struggled free and fled. In the end, it fell into a woman's slender hand.

That woman's ability was insufficient, and she had no way to refine the sword. She could only use it as bait to fish up a great catch.

The blood sword was empty sharpness, leaving a faint sword mark between the brows of a middle-aged man.

Under the influence of another consciousness, it was forced to fly back into that dark and lightless world again, until it was brought out once more by another person.

Within these images were also contained the various methods those people from the past had used when attempting to refine this sword.

Dripping blood to recognize a master, magic-power imprisonment, soul enslavement, even offering up one's own body to nourish it!

Under the actions of so many methods, the blood sword spirit soul had been polluted, becoming incomparably foul.

Now, Luo Chen had come to do the same!

The spirit soul slowly opened its eyes. Its beast pupils looked at Luo Chen with cruelty and deep affection, then slowly closed again.

At this moment, Luo Chen sensed a resonance between this sword and himself.

"It succeeded!?"

Luo Chen's eyes suddenly opened, both startled and delighted.

But afterward, he shook his head.

Deep within the blood sword, the thickest third layer of seal still had not been broken. He had only barely obtained the authority to control this sword.

The proof was...

"Sword, come!"

With a beckon of his hand, the Primordial Unity Cauldron's five-colored ribbons loosened, the silver-white lightning arcs withdrew, and the blood sword fell into his hand.

The sword tip trembled, actually attempting to turn traitor and point toward Luo Chen himself.

A thick desire for blood spread across it.

"You still want to drink my blood? Wishful thinking."

Luo Chen was perfectly unafraid. Holding the sword in one hand, he swept his other hand across the blade. Magic power surged, and the blood light instantly drew back.

At this moment, without the blood light affecting his divine sense and sight, he finally saw the two ancient engraved characters at the junction of blade and hilt.

[Yuantu]

"So this is your true name?"

Luo Chen released a breath and casually swung the blood sword twice. He only felt that it suited his hand perfectly, sharp beyond compare, as though even space itself were about to be cut apart.

"It is a good name. The Primordial Demon Sect's Blood Sea Ancestor was indeed slaughtered by you."

Although he knew that when this sword had been given its engraved name, it had absolutely not meant this, Luo Chen was willing to attach this layer of history to it.

Afterward, Luo Chen placed the Yuantu Sword across his knees. Magic power like an ocean enveloped it, and his divine sense continuously drilled into the sword body.

Soul-seal recognition was only the first step.

If he wanted to truly unleash this sword's power, he would still need constant sacrificial refinement, deeper familiarity, and even to find the rhythm by which it absorbed the spiritual qi of heaven and earth.

He would take it one step at a time!

...

Luo Chen intended to take it step by step, but clearly, things went against his wishes.

Because Fu Chaosheng had returned.

Some matters were just like this. Often, they did not shift according to an individual's will.

Previously, Luo Chen had been anxious about when Fu Chaosheng would return so they could set out for Central Continent. Now, he hoped Fu Chaosheng would come back a little more slowly.

But since Fu Chaosheng had returned, he ultimately had to face that reality he both anticipated and guarded against.

"There is not much time. We leave tomorrow!"

Fu Chaosheng said this.

Seeing Luo Chen's confusion, he explained, "News has come from the sect. A great power is soon to ascend. I must return one step earlier to make some preparations."

The bellyful of questions Luo Chen had all turned to nothing before the four words "great power is soon to ascend."

He asked only one thing. "Who is it?"

Fu Chaosheng shook his head. "When the time comes, all of you will naturally know. Prepare yourselves. We set out directly tomorrow morning."

...

In Divine Origin City, Fu Chaosheng's return was not concealed in any way.

Likewise, the news of his departure was also released that same day.

It was so urgent, as if forcing people to make their stances known.

Some high-level cultivators who had originally still been watching had no choice but to make decisions in a short time.

Early the next morning, a party of over a thousand people marched grandly out of the city and arrived before a great ship in Giant Whale Harbor.

Luo Chen looked up at that great ship and actually sensed from it a powerful aura no weaker than his own.

That was not some living person, but the aura emitted by the ship itself.

Zhou Yunshen said in a low voice from beside him, "Rumor says this ship's entire body was made from the corpse of a Sword-Halberd Whale. That Sword-Halberd Whale once reached the fourth-tier Demon Emperor realm while alive. That is why it gives cultivators like us such a soul-stirring feeling."

"Using a whale as a ship?" Luo Chen was rather surprised. "Would the Sea Emperor Whale, head of the Northern Sea whale race, not be enraged by this?"

But the moment he said this, he laughed self-mockingly.

This was the Tianyuan Dao Sect's ship!

Let alone a fourth-tier Demon Emperor, even if the Sea Emperor Whale personally appeared, the Tianyuan Dao Sect likely would not give him face.

It was not that Luo Chen exaggerated the Dao Sect's mighty name. Rather, Divine Origin City's present incompatibility with the Northern Sea explained everything.

Countless demon beasts circled it, yet not a single demon dared attack the city.

Even now, when the Sword-Halberd was about to carry people on a long voyage, those demon beasts only dared watch from afar and did not dare approach in the slightest.

Black King, hidden in Luo Chen's sleeve, was rather curious. "Why do we not take the teleportation formation and go directly to Central Continent?"

To this, Luo Chen shook his head.

If it were merely a few people, the teleportation formation would naturally be convenient.

But now...

He looked around. Thousands of people waited densely packed at the harbor, gathered like clouds.

Among them were Sages with profound Dao attainment like him, low-realm Qi Refinement cultivators, and even infants held in women's arms.

The powerful pressure brought about during transmission by such an ultra-long-distance cross-sea teleportation formation was not something low-level cultivators could endure.

Taking a ship was faster and more convenient.

According to Fu Chaosheng, when they reached the halfway point, they would also split up. A portion of cultivators would take a short-distance teleportation formation to cross a natural barrier and directly reach Central Continent, while another portion would continue sailing step by step.

In Luo Chen's perception, he very quickly figured out the number of Sage cultivators traveling to Central Continent.

Including him and Zhou Yunshen, there were seven Sages in total!

The remaining five were all at decent realms and were rather old. In the past, each had dominated a region in the Northern Sea and possessed considerable fame.

Over these years, some had made the decision to leave their homes and go to Central Continent to rely on the Tianyuan Dao Sect because of the righteous-demonic war, some because of the Demon Alliance's invasion, and some for the sake of their own Dao paths.

"On this single trip alone, the Tianyuan Dao Sect is taking seven great Sages into its net. In the three thousand years since antiquity, who knows how many outside-continent experts the Tianyuan Dao Sect has absorbed."

Luo Chen took a deep breath and cast his gaze toward Fu Chaosheng, who was conversing with others.

There were four people in total, precisely the four great elders of the Northern Sea Tianyuan Merchant Alliance.

No one knew the contents of their exchange, but when Fu Chaosheng cupped his hands and bowed farewell, the three great elders headed by Shen Wandou all had solemn expressions.

"Everyone, board the ship!"

Fu Chaosheng was the first to move, directly entering the Sword-Halberd.

The remaining people did not swarm aboard. Instead, under the guidance of Dao Sect disciples, they boarded one by one.

"Sage Luo, this way, please!"

To everyone's surprise, among the Northern Sea's seven great Sages, the first to be led aboard was not Zhou Yunshen, who revealed the highest realm, but Wilderness Wanderer, the youngest and lowest in realm.

Looking at Fu Hongzhang's sincere invitation and sensing the gazes on his back like sharp thorns, Luo Chen's expression remained calm.

"Let us go!"

He stepped aboard.

Heavenjade, Sang Jinghe, and Zhang Jingqing followed closely behind.

Zhou Yunshen, who stood relatively near, watched this scene with a complicated expression.

From the order of boarding, one could already see some things.

And he had been placed last among the seven Sages.

"Too late. Everything is too late. I came too late, and my years are late as well. I seem to have the highest realm, but in truth, I am the least valued. Only after paying out the Zhou clan's countless years of accumulation did I barely obtain a place aboard. I do not know whether this trip will be worth it."

Zhou Yunshen turned back and looked at the clansmen behind him. Each had a different expression. Some were reluctant to leave their homeland, while others yearned for and anticipated the place they were going.

"Forget it. Being able to find them a place to rest and live is already the last thing this old man can do. Children and grandchildren have their own fortunes. What happens later will depend on themselves."

"Board the ship!"

He spoke steadily, calling for his clansmen to follow the Primordial Demon Sect disciples onto the Sword-Halberd.

After the Zhou clan members all boarded, there were no more passengers.

At the bow, Fu Chaosheng nodded distantly toward the three people at the harbor, then disappeared into the depths of the great ship.

The Sword-Halberd hummed and trembled. Spiritual light burst forth, parting great waves as it slowly set sail.

Beneath the rolling layers of white waves, sails hung high, and the Sword-Halberd was like an enormous whale swimming toward the far horizon.

After they left.

Shen Wandou released his divine sense with full force, sweeping across the surroundings, a mocking smile on his face.

"You lot, born from fur and armor, dampness and eggs, have already formed a great momentum, yet you still shrink back and dare not advance. In the end, you will never accomplish great things."

"Return. From this day on, Divine Origin shall seal the city!"

Following his command, countless cultivators of Giant Whale Harbor retreated like the tide into Divine Origin City, and the enormous city gates closed with a rumbling boom.

One Tianyuan Spirit Energy Cannon after another extended from the city walls, their thick black muzzles gazing distantly over the Northern Sea.

*(End of Chapter)*
