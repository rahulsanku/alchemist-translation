# Chapter 967: Settling Wind and Waves, Slaying Li Tian, Nether Earth Dragon

One finger pointed, and heaven and earth transformed.

Vast, surging origin qi swept in like crashing waves against the shore.

The countless gu insects that had been hidden within the Wind-Wave Pavilion were forced to reveal themselves under the unceasing assault.

One by one, the gu insects rose and fell within the wind and waves, bobbing uncertainly.

A pair of sharp eyes gazed at those despairing, struggling gu insects, but had no intention of striking directly.

He simply allowed the vast death qi to transform into a massive amount of life force, letting these gu insects devour it greedily.

With each round of devouring, the power of the Withering-Bloom Finger gradually weakened.

But those gu insects...

*Bang!*

A gu insect that resembled a withered cicada had its wings turn emerald green, its body swelling like a ball until it was finally burst apart alive, turning into a puff of blood mist.

This was not an isolated case.

As if a chain reaction had been triggered, once one gu insect exploded, a continuous series of detonations rang out, like firecrackers.

*Crackle, pop!*

With their minds connected, Li Tian's originally ferocious expression underwent a violent change.

His soul foundation was very strong—among the Sage-level cultivators Luo Chen had encountered, he was an outstanding one. Even when the Liuli Gu was destroyed earlier, he could easily suppress the soul backlash.

It was precisely for this reason that during the battle, Luo Chen had directly abandoned using soul-bewildering techniques like "Ghosts and Gods Give Way."

The "Ghosts and Gods Give Way" soul art was very useful, but if an opponent saw through it during combat, it would instead cause one, who thought themselves safe, to suffer attacks they could have otherwise dodged.

However, no matter how strong the soul foundation, how could it withstand the continuous destruction of hundreds of spiritually connected things?

Under wave after wave of impact, Li Tian staggered backward, his expression ferocious.

"How can this be?"

"What kind of spell is that? Why can't the Wind-Wave Pavilion absorb it?"

Faced with his confusion and bewilderment, Luo Chen naturally would not explain.

Since his battle with Divine Origin Sage, his understanding of the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger divine ability had reached perfection.

When used normally, life force transformed into death intent, capable of injuring enemies, even causing their physical bodies and mana to stagnate and dissipate.

But if the divine ability were reversed, death qi transforming into life force would be enough to deliver a saturation strike against these enemies who loved to devour and digest.

This was the so-called cycle of withering and blooming, the transformation of life and death!

And the death qi within the Yin-Yang Remnant Realm—how vast, how immense was it?

At this moment, under his guidance, how could a mere Wind-Wave Pavilion absorb it all?

He stood quietly in the void. The shockwaves from the self-destruction of countless gu insects around him came crashing in, but under the protective divine light emitted by the Pingshan Crown, he stood as immovable as a mountain.

His powerful divine sense spread outward, searching for something.

After a moment, Luo Chen's eyes lit up, and he reached out into the void.

*Whoosh!*

A platinum light drifted back.

It was the Platinum Sword Pellet that had been bound earlier.

But Luo Chen did not stop there.

His gaze suddenly froze, falling upon the two yin-yang fish freely swimming within the storm of origin qi.

"The Same Life Shared Death Gu!"

His eyes lit up, and he took steps, moving closer to the two gu insects.

Suddenly, a shocking sword light slashed down.

Luo Chen did not dodge or evade. The Yuantu Sword in his hand reversed and parried.

*Boom!*

Under the impact of the tremendous force, Luo Chen remained as immovable as a mountain.

But in the air, that rotting corpse sword developed several cracks with a barely audible crisp sound.

"Without the Wind-Wave Pavilion to suppress me, what else can you do, Li Tian?" Luo Chen said calmly, a smile on his face. The corner of his eye caught a glimpse of the two-tailed yin-yang fish swimming not far away, jostled by the residual waves of battle.

In mid-air, Li Tian's arm trembled slightly as he gripped the rotting corpse sword, his face full of unwillingness.

"If not for this Same Life Shared Death Gu not reaching the fifth rank, if not for the Wind-Wave Pavilion lacking a fifth-rank divine gu to suppress it, how could you have broken it with a single move?"

Luo Chen never dwelled on "ifs."

In battle, he only cared about the present.

Seeing the two-tailed yin-yang fish getting farther and farther away, his expression froze slightly, and he was about to give chase.

Although the Same Life Shared Death Gu taken from the black coffin was not a fifth-rank existence as Meng Shentong had exaggerated, the fact that it could make a generation of great power guard it until their death meant its potential must be enormous.

Such a treasure—since he was already here, why not take it as a bonus?

Seeing Luo Chen's movements, Li Tian's eyes nearly split with rage.

Not only had his natal Devil Heart Gu been taken, but this man even coveted the Same Life Shared Death Gu he had painstakingly sought.

"You... push people too far!"

With an angry roar, his expression turned frantic as he formed a hand seal.

"Explode!"

With that one word, a myriad of buzzing sounds arose.

The remaining gu insects that had formed the Wind-Wave Pavilion instantly self-destructed, turning into a terrifying storm that surged toward Luo Chen.

Taking this opportunity, Li Tian shot out like a meteor, charging toward the yin-yang fish.

The terrifying storm arrived. Luo Chen was caught within it, his figure flickering in and out of existence, staggering left and right, unable to advance even an inch.

Even the protective light curtain of the Pingshan Crown and the protective qi armor of the Mystic Dust Armor began to waver and crumble under such an impact.

Luo Chen sighed, his expression suddenly turning solemn.

He opened his mouth and spat out a small gray cauldron, which spun out.

The small gray cauldron grew with the wind, in the blink of an eye transforming into a hundred-zhang giant.

Powerful spiritual light flickered within it, and a butterfly pattern seemed ready to take flight.

The giant cauldron stood amidst the storm, like an indestructible reef.

Only a low shout was heard in the void.

"Suppress!"

The vast storm fell silent in an instant.

The immeasurable wind and waves vanished in a flash.

Many of the gu insects that were in the process of self-destructing were forced to cease.

Even the two-tailed yin-yang fish, which had been unaffected by the wind and waves before, somehow cowered to one side after the Primordial Unity Cauldron appeared, no longer continuing to flee.

Even Li Tian, who was flying rapidly, found his body involuntarily slowing down, as if trapped in a quagmire.

Terror filled his eyes as he watched Luo Chen, who had started later but arrived first, cross the void in two or three steps and appear before the two-tailed yin-yang fish.

With a casual scoop, both fish were in hand.

"This sect master will gladly accept them!"

A flash of spiritual light in his hand, and the Same Life Shared Death Gu vanished instantly.

At the same time, a furious, anguished roar came from behind him.

"Give them back!"

*Bang!*

Li Tian's entire body erupted with ethereal flames. Wielding three blade-like arms and holding the rotting corpse sword, he charged at Luo Chen with explosive speed.

He had forcibly broken free from the suppressing power of the Primordial Unity Cauldron!

Facing the frenzied Li Tian, a sneer curled at the corner of Luo Chen's mouth.

"Do you really think I'm easy to bully? Without the Wind-Wave Pavilion, how could you possibly be my match?"

Instead of retreating, he advanced, wielding the Yuantu Sword with ease.

One strike!

*Clang!*

That rotting corpse sword, which Li Tian had secretly refined for hundreds of years, could no longer withstand the unmatched sharpness. It broke into several pieces in the middle, the hilt plunging into the earth.

Luo Chen casually flicked his hand.

Five sword pellets spun out, arrayed in five directions, manifesting the Five Elements.

Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, Earth—mutually restraining, yet endlessly generating each other.

*Boom!*

Vast sword threads spread out.

A sword qi storm, in no way inferior to the earlier self-destruction of the Wind-Wave Pavilion, directly enveloped the charging Li Tian.

*Whoosh! Whoosh! Whoosh!*

Countless sword threads shuttle back and forth, continuously cutting and grinding, forcing Li Tian to dodge left and right within, unable to evade.

In just a few exchanges, his three mantis-like blade arms were severed, leaving only one.

Luo Chen put away the Yuantu Blood Sword, retreated, clasped his hands together, and pushed out a palm.

*Hum...*

A great handprint, like a blazing sun, slowly emerged. Seemingly slow but actually fast, it charged into the Grand Five Elements Sword Formation.

Only a miserable scream was heard.

The Azure Yang Great Handprint broke through the formation, pressing a figure down heavily onto the hard ground.

All of this, though complicated to describe, was merely instinctive combat experience and rapid transitions under Luo Chen's command.

With a thought, the five sword pellets circled around him, the Primordial Unity Cauldron held aloft in front.

Luo Chen looked down from on high at the pitiful figure within the five-finger imprint on the ground.

Though miserable and disheveled, he still had a breath of life left.

Who knew how many people's blood and qi he had secretly refined with the Devil Heart Gu, nurturing his physical body to such a vigorous state that, even under Luo Chen's relentless attacks, he still had strength to spare.

Among the many enemies Luo Chen had encountered, this one was also considered tenacious.

The only pity was that although this man's realm was high and his methods numerous, his combat experience was somewhat rough.

Perhaps this was also related to the fact that he had been forced to hide his true identity for many years, and after succeeding in gu refinement, he had held a high position.

With a high status, opportunities to fight diminished.

Naturally, there was no chance to hone one's combat skills.

For a moment, Luo Chen even felt a sense of dissatisfaction.

*Crack, crack, crack...*

Sounds of breaking free arose.

It was not only Li Tian struggling but also Black King, who had been suppressed by the triple blood shackles earlier.

Luo Chen channeled his mana, and Black King broke free.

"Have you been slacking off in your cultivation too?" Luo Chen asked.

Black King was full of grievance. Although he often slept, that was also a way of cultivating for him. He had never been lazy or idle.

It was just that the enemies his master had encountered recently were all far beyond his strength.

The strongest under the Ascendant realm, Azure Frost Demon Emperor!

The former number one cultivator of the Extreme East, Divine Origin Sage!

The Southern Frontier gu-refining grandmaster, Li Tian!

Which one of these could he deal with?

"Master..."

"Enough, come back!"

Luo Chen could not bear to blame Black King too harshly. Being able to share some of the burden at a critical moment was already his best effort.

Black King hummed in acknowledgment and flew toward him, saying at the same time, "Master, there seems to be a big thing under this tomb."

"A big thing?"

Luo Chen was startled.

But then he realized that something that even Black King would call a "big thing" was definitely not on the level of Red Leaf Matron or the Flying Yaksha.

His expression changed slightly, and without hesitation, he pointed a finger at Li Tian.

Sword qi left his body, arriving in an instant.

*Pfft!*

A large hole suddenly burst open in the center of Li Tian's brow.

For Sage-level powerhouses, the physical body was no longer a fatal weakness. Even if the heart were taken, they could still fight for a short time.

But the head was still relatively fragile, especially as it involved the location of the sea of consciousness and the soul.

Under a single sword qi, the sea of consciousness was completely destroyed.

Following closely, more sword qi came rushing in.

Luo Chen was going to finish him off completely.

A blood-colored Nascent Soul, the size of a child, crawled out of Li Tian's corpse. Facing the dense sword qi, its eyes were full of resentment.

But that was all it could do—just resentment.

Because the omnipresent suppressing power of the Primordial Unity Cauldron still existed, preventing his Nascent Soul from merging with heaven and earth.

"Luo Chen, I won't let you go even if I become a ghost."

The resentful voice reached his ears, and Luo Chen smiled slightly.

The number of people he had killed in his life was countless.

Among them were not a few who bore resentment. If every one of them were to turn into a vengeful spirit to take revenge on him, he would have died long ago.

"Die!"

Sword qi wove like silk, unceasing.

It continuously broke through Li Tian's Nascent Soul's defenses. Death was in the next moment.

And in the next moment...

*Boom!*

The ground suddenly split open. A bloody maw erupted from the earth, swallowing Li Tian's Nascent Soul in one gulp.

Faintly, the sound of chewing could be heard.

Then, a strange head, greenish-yellow in color, appeared before Luo Chen.

As soon as it appeared, its lively eyes locked onto Luo Chen.

"What kind of monster is this?"

Astonishment arose in Luo Chen's heart, but his hands moved without any delay.

He pressed down with a palm!

Striking that strange head.

*Boom!*

Under the force of the impact, the result was unexpected.

Luo Chen was forced to fly backward, crashing directly into the hard wall.

He fell weakly.

"Master!"

An anxious voice sounded. Black King was about to manifest again.

Luo Chen pressed him down, his mind racing with a hundred thoughts, finally settling on a single idea.

"Flee!"

Origin power regenerated, and his entire body shot upward.

The ground continued to sink. The greenish-yellow, lively-eyed monster lay quietly prostrate on the ground, its long tongue flicking. It watched Luo Chen trying to escape but did not seem in a hurry to stop him.

The next moment.

*Bang!*

The entrance to the Nether Mountain, which had been blasted open by the Flame Dragon Descends earlier, had somehow already closed, forming a tough barrier that blocked Luo Chen.

It blocked not only Luo Chen but also the previous survivor, Red Leaf Matron.

The two exchanged a glance and, without a second thought, unleashed their means.

One held a formation plate, executing a translocation technique.

The other manifested a blood sword again, slashing through the void.

But the translocation technique was interrupted, failing and appearing back within the tomb.

The blood sword was unmatched in sharpness, wherever it passed, the tough wall shattered inch by inch.

However, unexpectedly, the speed at which it broke open was matched by the speed at which it recovered.

*Drip, drip.*

Luo Chen stood on the ground, watching the dripping blood, an uncontrollable thought arising in his mind.

Could this Nether Mountain be the transformed body of some giant beast?

The walls he thought were tough were actually its internal flesh and blood?

Then what was this monster before him?

The greenish-yellow monster struggled to crawl out from beneath the tomb.

Its body was not large, only a dozen or so zhang.

But the terrifying power contained within its physical form was evident from the fact that it could take a palm strike from Luo Chen with its head.

Especially the look in its eyes as it gazed at Luo Chen, which held a hint of urgent greed.

"It's a fifth-rank desolate beast, the Nether Earth Dragon!"

The anxious voice of Red Leaf Matron came from the side.

The four words "Nether Earth Dragon" immediately brought relevant information to Luo Chen's mind.

According to legend, the great power Yin-Yangzi had used the Same Life Shared Death Gu to contract a fifth-rank desolate beast—the Nether Earth Dragon.

It was by relying on this beast's ability to travel between the netherworld and connect yin and yang that he could achieve accomplishments in the dao of yin and yang.

But hadn't that Nether Earth Dragon already died long ago due to the end of its lifespan?

Could this be a contingency plan left behind by the other party, feigning death to be reborn?

Luo Chen had no time to think, because the greedy gaze fixed on him was becoming more and more urgent.

"The Same Life Shared Death Gu!"

In an instant, Luo Chen understood everything.

This young Nether Earth Dragon was Yin-Yangzi's contingency plan. Wasn't the Same Life Shared Death Gu hidden in the black coffin also one of his plans?

It was just that, for some reason, the gu insect had failed to advance to the fifth rank as hoped, delaying the resurrection of Yin-Yangzi.

But since it had awakened now, merging the two into one was a necessity.

"Return the Same Life Shared Death Gu, or stay?"

Luo Chen glanced at the Nether Earth Dragon and made a decision in an instant.

He was afraid that even if he gave it to him, it wouldn't let him go.

In that case...

A fierce glint flashed in Luo Chen's eyes. He raised a single palm before his chest.

"Come forth!"

The next moment, the phantom of a small sapling appeared in the tomb hall.

Immeasurable azure-white flames, centered on Luo Chen, swept over everything.
