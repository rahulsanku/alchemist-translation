# Chapter 1069: Submitting the Pledge of Allegiance, Ascending to Divinity in a Single Day

*Thump!*

*Thump!*

Two figures were sent flying back into the hall by the Xuanhuang great palm, tumbling across the smooth, bright floor like rolling gourds, utterly disheveled.

Lian Buwei had barely struggled to sit up when he couldn't help but spit out a mouthful of blood.

*Pfft!*

He stared in disbelief at the figure standing at the entrance of the hall, as imposing as a mountain.

"Who are you, daring to attack us?"

Deng Taiyue paid him no heed, merely looking at Luo Chen, as if waiting for him to state his position.

This sudden turn of events happened so swiftly that no one had time to react.

Almost the moment Luo Chen uttered the words "Heavenly Origin Dao Sect," Lian Buwei and Shi Chuanting sensed something was wrong and instinctively rushed outward.

They understood very well that since Luo Chen had so blatantly spoken such words, he would never allow news to leak before making his move, nor would he let the two of them leave this place alive.

But someone was faster than them!

Deng Taiyue of Taishan Sect, who had not appeared until now, struck out fiercely, catching them off guard and heavily wounding two Sage cultivators in one blow!

Luo Chen's gaze fell darkly, the murderous intent within it so blatant and unrestrained.

Shi Chuanting, who had remained silent since arriving, suddenly shrieked, "Luo Chen, have you gone mad? The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect is not someone you can afford to provoke!"

Yes!

Had Luo Chen gone mad?

He actually wanted to make a move against the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect—that was the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!

It was a sacred land of Ascendants!

It was the number one power in the Mountains and Seas Realm!

With countless experts, profound foundations, and a living Ascendant great power personally overseeing it!

Was there truly a madman in this world who dared to rebel against such an existence?

Someone opened their mouth, wanting to speak, but Luo Chen had already nodded slightly at Deng Taiyue.

Deng Taiyue stepped forward.

One person blocked his path.

The Dongyang Grand Elder's expression was tense, a hint of pleading in his eyes.

"You can't do this, Sage Taiyue. Perhaps there's some misunderstanding here. Let's all sit down and properly..."

*Boom!*

A colossal pillar of light descended from the heavens, piercing through the dome, and enveloped the Dongyang Grand Elder before he could finish speaking.

No matter how the Dongyang Grand Elder urged his mana, he could not break free from that terrifying suppression.

He could no longer stop Deng Taiyue in the slightest.

Deng Taiyue's pupils contracted at the sight of the light pillar. He glanced at Luo Chen, who sat as steady as a mountain above, then stepped past the Dongyang Grand Elder and arrived before Lian Buwei and Shi Chuanting.

Knowing they had lost all hope of escape, the two men's faces were filled with despair.

Then, despair turned into madness.

"The Dao Sect will avenge us!"

"Together, let's go to hell!"

The two roared, their mana suddenly contracting inward, converging toward their nascent souls.

An extreme aura began to expand, containing a destructive force within.

Someone cried out in alarm.

"They're going to self-destruct their nascent souls!"

"Run!"

"Get out of here!"

But before Luo Chen, how could they self-destruct their nascent souls so easily?

However, Luo Chen did not act. He left everything to Deng Taiyue.

And it had to be left to Deng Taiyue!

Today, these two Dao Sect Sages had to die here. Sealing the news was the first reason, killing the chicken to scare the monkeys was the second, and this final point was to serve as a pledge of allegiance!

Deng Taiyue's pledge of allegiance, the pledge of everyone present to turn a blind eye to the deaths of these two!

Deng Taiyue formed a hand seal, then flipped his hand and pressed down.

"Seal!"

In an instant, a mysterious aura appeared on the two men. The destructive aura that had been climbing and swelling suddenly vanished, as if it had been locked away.

Under the wide-eyed gazes of Lian Buwei and Shi Chuanting, Deng Taiyue extended a finger from each hand and lightly tapped them on the tops of their heads from a distance.

*Chi!*

*Chi!*

Two holes appeared, and the two figures collapsed limply to the ground.

Deng Taiyue clapped his hands, and a burst of earthy yellow spiritual light emanated from his body.

That earthy yellow spiritual light rolled on the ground and then transformed into a burly, imposing man.

"Shi Gandang pays respects to the Sect Master!"

"Dispose of these two. Be careful of the nascent souls I've sealed—don't hurt yourself."

Shi Gandang gave a muffled acknowledgment, then carried the two limp corpses toward the exit of the hall.

The Dongyang Grand Elder, who had been facing away from everyone, trembled violently when he saw Shi Gandang walk past him carrying the two limp bodies.

But Deng Taiyue paid no attention to this. He brushed his sleeves and cupped his hands toward Luo Chen.

"Daoist, the mission is accomplished!"

Luo Chen smiled slightly. "You've worked hard. Please, take a seat!"

Unbeknownst to anyone, Xiaobai had already brought over a cushion, placed to the left of Luo Chen.

Deng Taiyue lifted his Daoist robe and sat down calmly.

When Sage Taiyue had taken his seat, the previously quiet hall suddenly erupted.

"He really killed them?"

"Those were disciples of the sacred land!"

"How dare Sage Taiyue... the Pill Sect Master..."

"We've stirred up big trouble, a huge mess. Those two were the stewards specially left by the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect to collect resources in the Eastern Wilderness. Now that they're dead, the Dao Sect will surely be furious and send troops."

"What should we do?"

...

Amidst the chaos and panic, a heavy cough sounded.

Everyone fell silent, then instinctively looked toward the old man at the front, as if finding a pillar of support.

Tian Duzi ignored these gazes, his eyes fixed on Luo Chen with shock and uncertainty.

"Pill Sect Master, have you already broken through to the Ascendant realm?"

The corners of Luo Chen's mouth lifted slightly. "What makes you say that?"

Tian Duzi swallowed his saliva and pointed at the Dongyang Grand Elder, who was still completely immobilized by the pillar of light.

Only then did everyone react.

Luo Chen had used some unknown means to easily suppress a long-established late-stage Sage grand cultivator!

It wasn't a spell, nor a divine ability, nor a magic treasure. They hadn't even seen Luo Chen make a move.

How had he done it?

Although rumors claimed Luo Chen was the number one under the Ascendant realm, on par with that demoness Azure Frost, the world was more inclined to believe it was due to Luo Chen's extraordinary combat prowess.

As for Luo Chen's cultivation realm, they didn't think much of it.

After all, Luo Chen had been cultivating for too short a time, and his cultivation path was traceable.

Yet, without a direct confrontation, Luo Chen had suppressed the Dongyang Grand Elder, to the point where the other couldn't even turn around, standing stiffly like a wooden stake with his back to everyone.

A Sage cultivator could never achieve this step.

Only an Ascendant!

Tian Duzi took a deep breath, then slowly exhaled.

"I am well-versed in formations, even capable of setting up fifth-rank formations. I know how to borrow qi from heaven and earth."

"Your method contains extremely mixed spiritual qi within it. It is not the way we Sages can only control corresponding attribute free-floating spiritual qi. In other words, what you are manipulating is the origin qi of heaven and earth!"

"And manipulating the origin qi of heaven and earth is the most distinctive characteristic of an Ascendant, the means of a great power!"

"Have you truly broken through?"

At these words, everyone was shocked.

Even Nine Spirit Primogenitor's eyes were wide with disbelief.

How old was Luo Chen? How could he possibly have broken through to the Ascendant realm?

He remembered that when they first met, he was at the sixth layer of the Sage realm, and Luo Chen was only at the third layer. How could Luo Chen have surpassed him in such a short time and broken through to an even higher realm?

But reality seemed to be right before their eyes?

Luo Chen let out a soft laugh, nodded slightly, and then slowly shook his head.

"It is Ascendant, yet it is not Ascendant."

Tian Duzi frowned. "What does that mean?"

Luo Chen smiled. "This Sect Master has merely mastered a method that allows me to briefly enter the Ascendant realm."

Everyone was bewildered. What did it mean to briefly enter the Ascendant realm?

If you haven't broken through, you haven't broken through. If you have, you have.

Could you go up and then fall back down, repeating the cycle? What did he take cultivation for?

Faced with everyone's confusion, Luo Chen did not explain too much.

In truth, regarding his own situation, Luo Chen had only gradually come to fully understand it over the past hundred years.

He could use the *Moonlight Heart-Cleansing Incantation* to condense his soul and merge it with his nascent soul, forming a temporary primordial spirit. This state had gone from the initial half-hour, one hour, to now, after a hundred years, lasting an entire day!

Then, he had comprehended three types of Law True Intent. In the primordial spirit state, he could manipulate even more origin qi of heaven and earth than an ordinary Ascendant great power.

Coupled with his self-created Withering-Bloom Dao Art, the Three Heads Six Arms he had cultivated, and the *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon*—these three divine abilities.

With all this, what difference was there between him and an Ascendant great power?

Nascent Soul to Ascendant, Law True Intent, manipulating origin qi of heaven and earth, mastering divine abilities—all these things combined. Even if it was only for a single day, Luo Chen was still a one-day Ascendant great power!

Leaving others to their guesses, Luo Chen turned his attention to the stiff old man before him.

With a thought, the heavenly light pillar vanished instantly.

The Dongyang Grand Elder, suddenly losing the heavy pressure, erupted with the mana he had been trying to break free with. This was the mana of a long-established late-stage Sage, unmatched in power, surpassing even Deng Taiyue, Nine Spirit Primogenitor, and Sage Pengxiang. Perhaps only Tian Duzi could rival it. Yet, compared to the origin qi pillar Luo Chen had casually summoned earlier, it was like a firefly. Especially the scorching gaze behind him, which made him feel as if a knife were pressed against his back.

His aura surged and then immediately retracted. The old man staggered a couple of steps before turning around.

He looked at Luo Chen with fearful eyes, opened his mouth to speak, but felt that whatever he said would be meaningless.

In the end, he could only force a bitter smile. "Pill Sect Master, why must it come to this?"

The people in the hall nodded inwardly. Yes, why must it come to this?

Before matters were even settled, he had already resorted to killing. This left everyone with no way back.

Earlier, everyone had recommended Luo Chen as the representative of the Eastern Wilderness. Now, you take the lead in doing something like this—aren't you pushing everyone to their deaths?

Luo Chen replied calmly, "It had to be this way. Between me and the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, this battle is inevitable."

"But what are your chances of victory?" the Dongyang Grand Elder asked, utterly dejected. "Yes, you are strong—stronger than anyone imagined! But you are ultimately not an Ascendant great power, and what you face is the number one sacred land in the Mountains and Seas Realm, with ten thousand years of foundation and three thousand years of development since the ancient era. What are you going to use to fight them? Just relying on us?"

Luo Chen chuckled lightly. "This Sect Master has never fought a battle without certainty in my life. Since I have decided to make a move, I will certainly be prepared."

The Dongyang Grand Elder looked at Luo Chen blankly. "Prepared?"

Luo Chen tapped the table, drawing everyone's attention—or rather, there was no need to draw it; he just made them pay more attention to what he was about to say.

"In this battle, the elite forces of the Eastern Wilderness will be fully deployed. Not just you and my Luo Tian Sect, but also the Eastern Wilderness demon race—a total of twenty-four Demon Emperors. Among them are great Demon Emperors like Bi Zheng, Niu Gui, Juan Kuang, and the Thunder Prison Divine Peng!"

Everyone was stunned. The human and demon races joining forces?

Then, they instinctively recalled what Lian Buwei of the Dao Sect had said earlier—"Luo Chen has colluded with the demon race!"

But this statement, in the face of Luo Chen's previously displayed strength comparable to an Ascendant great power, no one dared to voice.

Tian Duzi cautiously asked, "How did you manage that?"

Luo Chen countered, "I can refine Nascent Soul Pills and Initiation Pills for you. Can't I refine pills for them as well?"

The demon race needed pills for cultivation?

Everyone looked confused.

But He Huang, who was sitting behind Sage Xianhe, said thoughtfully, "Transformation Pills?"

Back then, He Huang's transformation had been incomplete. It was only thanks to the Transformation Pill Luo Chen had given him that he succeeded in reaching the Sage realm.

Luo Chen nodded. "Yes, Transformation Pills, but not just Transformation Pills. And you must understand one truth: after signing the ceasefire treaty, the two races will enjoy a brief period of peace. Exchanging goods and resources with each other will be better than attacking and killing one another. Think about it—how many rare flowers, exotic herbs, ores, and treasures are there in the Million Great Mountains? These can all be exchanged with magic treasures and pills without going through battle."

Luo Chen said a lot, but it was all just an excuse.

The group of demon experts he had invited required no payment at all.

The Lord of the Truth Crossing Hall had the authority to summon all beings under his command!

Others didn't know this, but they began to ponder whether, if the human and demon races joined forces, they would have the capital to face the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

Luo Chen spoke eloquently, continuing, "This battle gathers the peak combat power of the Eastern Wilderness, but it is not about confronting the entire Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. After the great calamity in the Central Continent, the entire continent was split apart. My target is only the Dao Sect's ancestral court on the Eastern Origin Continent. As long as we are fast enough, we can leave calmly before reinforcements from the other two continents arrive."

Hearing this, the others visibly relaxed.

But the Dongyang Grand Elder still shook his head.

"Today, the Dao Sect's ancestral court has Liancheng Ancestor sitting in command within, and the four great top-tier great houses—Lian, Ke, Shi, and Li—protecting it from without. There are also quite a few Sage aristocratic families. Relying solely on us is still insufficient."

Luo Chen was not surprised by the Dongyang Grand Elder's understanding of the Dao Sect, after all, he had once been the first native force in the Eastern Wilderness to submit to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

Luo Chen simply uttered two more names.

"Li Canghai! Azure Frost!"

There was some confusion among the crowd, but those with well-informed connections recognized the two names.

"Li Canghai—could he be that great power from the Northern Sea?"

"Yes, he and I have an understanding. He will make a move together with us at the appointed time."

"This..."

"And Azure Frost. Perhaps you don't know, but she has already broken through to the Ascendant realm, achieving the body of an ancient demon!"

At these words, everyone in the hall was shaken to their core.

That number one demon under the Ascendant realm, who had roamed the Eastern Wilderness for three hundred years, had broken through to the Ascendant realm.

So fast, yet so natural, as if it was always meant to be.

And thus, everyone suddenly understood, and even felt fortunate, that they had concluded the ceasefire treaty earlier.

With Azure Frost's abilities, she had already roamed the Eastern Wilderness freely before. Now that she was truly making a move, who in the vast Eastern Wilderness could stop her?

"Green Parasol Mountain and the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect have an undying, irreconcilable hatred. The reason the demon race is willing to cease hostilities with us and even join forces with me is precisely because we share a common enemy."

"Everyone, with two great powers personally taking action, plus the peak combat power of the Eastern Wilderness, are you still afraid of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect?"

Luo Chen's gaze burned as he looked at everyone, his words filled with immense confidence.

That Initiation Pill appeared before everyone's eyes once again.

"Sage Taiyue took the Initiation Pill refined by this Sect Master, thus breaking through to the late Sage realm. Not only did he comprehend the Earth Origin, but his strength also soared. You should have seen the signs of this from his earlier actions!"

Deng Taiyue had just heavily wounded the two Dao Sect Sages and, with a backhanded move, sealed their nascent souls' suicidal self-destruction. Such methods were no longer something ordinary spells could achieve.

Only by comprehending Law True Intent!

"As long as you are willing to make a move for me once, you too can obtain an Initiation Pill... including you, Dongyang Grand Elder!"

The Dongyang Grand Elder's throat bobbed. His earlier dejected demeanor had dissipated considerably.

"I know you want to submit to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, become a guest elder of the Star Gate, and seek a chance to charge into the Ascendant realm in the future."

"But do you know that the Star Gate has long been a hollow shell?"

"Do you know that the former Star Gate elders have all been sidelined and left idle?"

"Now, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect invests all its resources in the elite disciples and the next generation within the sect. Will they open the door wide for an outsider like you?"

"The Initiation Pill does not guarantee absolute success, but it is already your last chance!"

Luo Chen's words were like the whispers of a demon, constantly filling the Dongyang Grand Elder's ears.

And lingering in everyone's hearts.

The comparison of strength between the two sides, two Ascendant great powers plus Luo Chen, who could briefly enter the Ascendant realm, and the temptation of a divine pill like the Initiation Pill—all of this caused everyone to waver.

The atmosphere in the hall grew heavy once more. Everyone was thinking.

Thinking about whether to participate.

Thinking about what would happen to them if they failed, and what would happen to the sects, descendants, relatives, and friends behind them.

Thinking about what they could gain if they succeeded.

In the midst of this silence, a resolute and firm voice suddenly rang out.

"I am a mere rogue cultivator, fortunate to have received the grace of the Pill Sect Master, and thus had the opportunity to break through to the Sage realm. Now, Senior Pill Sect Master has pointed out the path to Ascension. How can I hesitate any longer?"

"Bo Hanjun is willing to go all out!"

(End of chapter)
