# Chapter 615: Wandering Cultivators Are Paupers; Immortal Heirs Are Gold Mines

On the jiaolong's back, the red-robed Daoist looked up into the sky, sea wind whipping his sleeves with a snapping sound.

Under gazes that were curious, wary, and more than anything reverent, his expression remained calm. His figure slowly rose and finally boarded the gorgeous flying boat.

His gaze swept past the young cultivators one by one, finally stopping on the old man whose hair and beard were both white and whose face was covered in wrinkles.

"Elder Sang, long time no see. Have you been well recently?"

Sang Jiugong's wrinkled face squeezed together. Whether it was a bitter smile or something else was hard to say, but it always carried a forced feeling.

He cupped his hands to Luo Chen. "Good or bad, there is little to say. There have merely been some twists and turns. Come, this way. Let us speak inside."

Luo Chen's eyes swept over him. He felt Sang Jiugong's aura was somewhat different from before.

But since the other party invited him, he naturally would not refuse.

When arriving at a new place, if one wanted to cultivate peacefully, one had to build many connections.

It did not count as forming factions. In any case, once one had many contacts, many things became much more convenient.

Immediately, under Sang Jiugong's invitation, Luo Chen followed him into the flying boat's great hall.

After they left, the young Foundation Establishment cultivators from Divine Origin City on the flying boat finally let out breaths and became less restrained.

"That one is Devil Lord Azure Yang, right?"

"Definitely. When I glimpsed him hurriedly last year, I did not connect the Dao title Daoist Azure Yang with Devil Lord Azure Yang. Looking at him now, this Golden Core master has restrained bearing and deep eyes. He seems ordinary, but in truth, standing there, he is like a deep pool whose depth cannot be seen, giving people a fear that sinks into the marrow."

"Indeed. My Huaqing Sect is also a famous Nascent Soul upper sect in the southwest. There are quite a few Golden Core cultivators inside, but no mid Golden Core senior gives as much pressure as this Devil Lord. Only one late Golden Core great cultivator senior barely compares."

"Anyone who can make a name among countless wandering cultivators cannot be weak. This sort of existence may not have especially exquisite methods, but their experience in killing and battle far exceeds the strong cultivators of our sects. The reason we are actively going out to temper ourselves now is to erase this gap and make our strength equal our realm, or even greater than our realm."

"Rumor says this person is also proficient in artifact-forging. If we invite him to join my Shangyuan Xu clan..."

"Haha, stop dreaming. This kind of demonic path expert has unclear origins and many enemies. Your Golden Core clan could not bear that kind of karma."

"I wonder why Elder Sang took the initiative to invite this person aboard?"

...

Inside the hall, Luo Chen sat down freely.

Sang Jiugong personally poured him a cup of tea before sitting down at the side.

Luo Chen touched the scalding teacup and smelled the misty tea fragrance. He knew this tea was absolutely a fine product among teas.

The other party had invited him so warmly and lowered himself to pour tea and water for him. If this was merely idle conversation, that would be self-deception.

Since that was the case, Luo Chen did not need to deliberately posture.

"Elder Sang, if there is something, speak directly."

"Daoist Azure Yang is so frank and straightforward. That saves this old man from circling around."

Clearly, Luo Chen's directness made Sang Jiugong let out a breath.

"Your term should be complete, and you should be returning to the Fulong Mountain Range, yes?"

"Mm." Luo Chen nodded.

"This old man is as well." Sang Jiugong echoed. The missions he and Luo Chen had accepted were close in duration, which was why the coincidence occurred that one arrived before the other, and they left at about the same time. "And what this old man seeks is very simple. I hope Fellow Daoist will travel with me and return together."

That was all?

Luo Chen frowned. "I am accustomed to traveling alone. With so many people on your boat, it will inevitably slow me down."

Sang Jiugong advised, "Is Fellow Daoist not afraid of encountering robbers on the road?"

"Uh..."

For a moment, Luo Chen was actually stumped by the question.

"Elder Sang, are you joking? I am a Golden Core cultivator. Under normal circumstances, who would dare rob me? Moreover, even if someone dares provoke me, as long as they are not late Golden Core great cultivators, I am confident I can come and go freely. And if late Golden Core great cultivators stoop to becoming robber cultivators, would that not be far too beneath them?"

Sang Jiugong shook his head. "Daoist Azure Yang, do not imagine the Northern Sea to be too beautiful. You must know that the term robber cultivator originated from our Northern Sea cultivation world. Who says there are no examples of great cultivators lowering themselves to robber cultivator work?"

Luo Chen was stunned. "There truly are?"

Sang Jiugong lowered his voice. "Could it be Fellow Daoist does not know that Blood Wanderer, one of our Myriad Immortals Assembly's Three Great Wanderers, did similar things in his early years when he was at late Golden Core?"

Very well.

This time, Luo Chen was truly shaken by this secret.

Blood Wanderer had actually once been a robber cultivator.

And when he was late Golden Core. That was truly... unconcerned with minor details.

Seeing Luo Chen's expression, Sang Jiugong relaxed inwardly and hurriedly struck while the iron was hot. "Sea routes are dangerous, especially now that the Northern Sea situation is unstable. There are many cultivators so poor their eyes have reddened, willing to take desperate risks. Fellow Daoist cannot ignore precautions. Why not return with us? It may be a little slower, but safety will certainly be more assured."

After some hesitation, Luo Chen finally looked at Sang Jiugong.

"Elder Sang, our friendship has not reached that point yet. You cannot be thinking only of my welfare, can you?"

This sentence pierced the old man's verbal smokescreen like a sharp sword.

Elder Sang's expression grew somewhat unnatural. He wanted to argue a little, but under Luo Chen's burning gaze, he finally sighed.

"This old man is injured. Protecting this boat full of people is somewhat beyond my strength, so I need a stronger guarantee."

"Injured?" Luo Chen's gaze swept over the other man, and he immediately understood where the feeling of discord between Sang Jiugong's aura and body had come from when he first saw him earlier.

Elder Sang no longer concealed it and spoke frankly. "I accepted a commission from this group of Divine Origin City prodigies, responsible for protecting them as they killed demons and tempered themselves on the first defensive line. It should have been an easy matter, but I did not expect these high sect disciples and clan cores to be so ignorant of the height of heaven and depth of earth. Several of them actually joined hands and tried to strangle a third-tier Demon King."

"To be honest, they did have some ability. An early third-tier Demon King truly was not their match."

"But the commotion was too great, and the battle dragged on too long, attracting even more powerful existences."

"To prevent anyone from dying, this old man had no choice but to personally act and fight several Demon Kings with all my strength. Although I killed them all, this old man was also wounded rather heavily."

"Returning to Dragon Abyss Immortal City will take three to five days if fast, seven or eight if slow. That distance is neither far nor short. If we truly encounter villains, and a few people on this boat are lost, this old man absolutely cannot bear the questioning of the forces behind them."

"Therefore, I had no choice but to ask Fellow Daoist."

These words were spoken slowly and clearly.

Though Luo Chen did not know how much of the inner truth was real, it did have beginning and end.

Luo Chen listened quietly and gave no opinion.

Seeing Luo Chen remain unmoved, Sang Jiugong's tone rose slightly. "Fellow Daoist once made even Huangfu Song retreat from difficulty. Your abilities must be no less than those of a great cultivator. With you protecting us, we will surely be safe."

"Furthermore, the chance of trouble on this return route is not high. It is not certain that we will truly run into anything troublesome."

"Daoist Azure Yang, how about treating it as this Sang Jiugong owing you a favor?"

First praise, then state that the danger level was not high, and finally offer a favor.

His posture was low enough.

Luo Chen looked at the other man with a faint smile.

Favors from Golden Core cultivators were not so cheap.

But after thinking again, Luo Chen faintly realized Sang Jiugong's true intention.

Sometimes, owing someone a favor was not necessarily a bad thing.

So-called friendship was built through back and forth. When there was no overlap of interests to begin with, taking the initiative to suffer a loss and owe someone a favor meant the two sides now had a connection.

Clearly, this request for an escort was only a small matter.

The favor owed would also only be a small favor. Repaying it later would not require great effort, much less reach the level of repaying with life and death.

But this was a good beginning.

This Sang Jiugong was clearly using a request for escort as a pretext to properly befriend Daoist Azure Yang, master of Peng Lake.

After thinking this through, Luo Chen laughed inwardly.

And he had said he would not circle around. This was clearly a very large circle.

However, such a matter ultimately had a hundred benefits and no harm for Luo Chen. There was no reason to refuse.

Under Sang Jiugong's gaze, Luo Chen slowly nodded.

"Since that is so, let us travel together."

Hearing this answer, Sang Jiugong completely let out a breath, and a brilliant smile appeared on his face, like a chrysanthemum blooming.

Afterward, Luo Chen sent out a divine sense transmission.

Black King, bored in the sea, muttered a sentence, shrank his body, flew up onto the flying boat, and found a room to coil up in.

On Sang Jiugong's side, he gave instructions to the people under him. The flying boat started moving again, while he returned to the hall and began exchanging cultivation insights with Luo Chen.

In the cultivation world, so-called exchange of cultivation insights was not merely talk.

But such matters mostly occurred among sect and clan cultivators.

They even did more than verbal exchange. They regularly organized so-called small competitions, great competitions, and even contests between forces to verify what they had gained from cultivation.

By comparison, the frequency of exchanges among wandering cultivators was much lower.

In the five or six years Luo Chen had been in the Myriad Immortals Assembly, he had only occasionally exchanged with the Angler, and even then it was mostly the other party seeking advice from him on body-tempering insights.

This time, Luo Chen did not hold much expectation for exchanging with Elder Sang. After all, the other man was one minor realm lower than him.

Unexpectedly, Elder Sang, who had cultivated for long years, truly did give him some small gains.

...

"The path of spirit plants sounds like it has many rules and a high threshold, but in truth, it does not have that many mysteries."

"To become a good spirit planter, first one looks at the spirit plant's own potential, second at the spirit plant's growth environment, and only last at the spirit planter's own accomplishments in spirit-plant techniques."

"The reasoning is simple. If it is only the most ordinary wildflower or weed, and only barren yellow earth, then even if we had heaven-reaching ability, we could not cultivate a sky-propping Jianmu tree."

"In the early years, why was the Northern Sea so enthusiastic about launching expansion wars? The reason was that in the deep seas occupied by demon beasts, there were too many high-grade spirit lands that had not yet been explored. Those spirit lands not only assisted our cultivation, but on their territories, many spirit plants also grew."

"These spirit plants had never been harvested by us human immortal cultivators. Demon beasts have long lifespans and can afford to wait. Some demon beast races even specially guard certain types of spirit plants and continuously harvest their fruits. This leads to those spirit plants having extremely long growth ages, often hundreds or thousands of years, even tens of thousands. One could say that once an expansion war succeeds, the profit from spirit plants absolutely counts as one of the largest gains."

"Fellow Daoist may not know, but the current Splitting Heaven Stream Master of Moluo Stream participated in an expansion war in his early years and harvested a precious fifth-tier medicinal material from a deserted island. He contributed it to the Primordial Demon Sect, and the Primordial Demon Sect's Ascendant ancestor was greatly pleased, specially cultivating the Splitting Heaven Stream Master from Golden Core all the way to Nascent Soul. Tsk, tsk. Such fortune is simply what cultivators like us dream of."

Apart from his Golden Core Stage Four realm, Sang Jiugong himself was also a spirit planter.

It was precisely because he could not bear to take his exquisite spirit-planting art into the coffin that he wanted to leave behind a legacy before his lifespan neared its end.

Over these years, he had already begun accepting disciples and establishing a sect.

The site of his sect was not within the Fulong Mountain Range, but on an ordinary third-tier spirit vein island within the Myriad Immortals Assembly's territory.

Strictly speaking, he was no longer a wandering cultivator.

But no one cared about such things.

After all, among the Myriad Immortals Assembly's seven Nascent Soul Sages, three had sects or clans of their own.

Listening to the other man's eloquent talk, Luo Chen's memories related to spirit planters and spirit plants surfaced again.

He was not unfamiliar with this path.

In his early years at the Luo Tian Sect, under him had been Yuan Dongsheng, a spirit planter who had once cultivated spirit trees and spirit flowers outside his Tianlan Peak cave dwelling.

That man had passed away, but the things he left behind still remained in Luo Chen's mind.

At this moment, listening to Sang Jiugong speak, Luo Chen agreed while also asking about matters of cultivating certain medicinal materials he cared about.

The other man truly deserved to be a spirit planter who had once cultivated fourth-tier medicinal materials. He answered Luo Chen's questions one by one. Even on things he was not skilled in, he gave conjectures and suggestions within his ability.

As they reached the end of the conversation, Sang Jiugong sighed.

"It is not that this old man covets profit, taking risks and violating rules to bring these Divine Origin City prodigies to the Three Oceans defensive line. It is truly that these people have extraordinary origins and rich fortunes. Befriending them also benefits the Earth Mulberry Sect under this old man."

Luo Chen did not understand. "But these people are not cultivators of my Myriad Immortals Assembly. Why do you not befriend the wandering cultivators in the Myriad Immortals Assembly?"

Regarding this, Sang Jiugong gave a cold laugh.

"Wandering cultivators?"

"Those people are not worth trusting."

"There is a saying: those with constant property have constant hearts, and those without constant property lack constant hearts. If they lack constant hearts, there is no wickedness they will not do. Daoist Azure Yang, I imagine you should understand the meaning of this sentence."

Luo Chen fell silent.

How could he not understand this principle?

Wandering cultivators had no fixed residences and drifted everywhere.

So-called good and evil were but a thought apart. In front of people, they could be modest gentlemen, and behind backs, they could turn into masked robber cultivators. How could they be easily trusted?

By contrast, cultivators with fixed properties were restrained by the sects and clans behind them, which determined that they could not act recklessly. Even if they truly did evil, they had to bear the consequence that a monk might run, but the temple could not.

If it were Luo Chen, he would also be more willing to befriend sect cultivators.

However...

"Elder Sang, you seem to regard me differently."

"Haha!"

Sang Jiugong laughed loudly. "Others may not know you, Daoist Azure Yang, but this old man knows a little. Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake, I imagine, has not been left empty these years."

Luo Chen raised a brow, then smiled knowingly.

His gaze turned outside, and he sighed.

"In that case, these cultivators from Divine Origin City are treasures."

"Indeed. They are all the future hope of their various forces. Given time, as long as they do not die, they will surely hold power over one side. Befriending them brings tremendous benefit not only to ourselves, but also to our descendants."

At this point, Sang Jiugong suddenly pointed outside.

"Daoist Azure Yang, are you interested in lowering yourself to befriend them?"

"Me?" Luo Chen hesitated.

Sang Jiugong nodded. "Yes. With the name Devil Lord Azure Yang spreading outside, these children of tomorrow, who have been protected under their elders' wings since childhood, feel not only reverence but even more curiosity toward you. If you take the initiative to lower yourself and befriend them, they will surely be delighted."

This time, Luo Chen truly became a little interested.

It was not for some future network of descendants, but because he recalled the sentence Sang Jiugong had spoken earlier: these people had extraordinary origins and rich fortunes.

In other words, they were very wealthy.

His previous plan was to make Azure Yang Hall's signboard famous so he could earn the money of the Myriad Immortals Assembly's wandering cultivators.

But wandering cultivators were mostly paupers.

How could earning paupers' money compare to earning the money of these "immortal second-generation heirs"?

*(End of Chapter)*
