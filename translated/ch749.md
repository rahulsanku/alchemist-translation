# Chapter 749: Intercepting Spiritual Impulse, Flying Star Gate; Mirage Dragon Wakes, Grotto-Heaven Opens

Above Heaven-Earth Peak, the hazy phantom covered the sky like a canopy.

Any cultivator with some knowledge realized what it was.

Primordial spirit!

The path of qi refinement pursued long life, which was spiritual immortality.

Therefore, one refined essence into qi, refined qi into spirit, and continually accumulated divine soul foundation from the scattered sea of consciousness.

But ultimately, the divine soul leaned toward yin and emptiness, and could not face the might of heaven and earth directly. At most, one could release a little divine soul outward, forming so-called spiritual sense or divine sense.

However!

When cultivators reached the Sage realm, the divine soul combined with the body's essence qi and magic power, giving birth to a special thing called the Nascent Soul.

The Nascent Soul symbolized the union of essence, qi, and spirit, the three treasures!

It could temporarily house the divine soul and serve as a temporary dwelling for the divine soul after leaving the sea of consciousness.

But at this time, its symbolic meaning was greater than its practical meaning. Without the Purple Mansion's protection, a naked Nascent Soul still dared not remain long beneath the blue sky and blazing sun.

Thus, cultivators began to pursue yin soul crossing into yang, transforming from a yin-empty state to a yang-solid state.

Once one reached the yang-solid realm, the divine soul could completely merge into the Nascent Soul body. At this time, the Nascent Soul was no longer called a Nascent Soul, but a primordial spirit!

The outward manifestation of a cultivator's divine soul was no longer called divine sense, but divine thought.

And this realm as a whole was called "Ascendant"!

This was a fundamental transformation.

From this point on, cultivators no longer feared the blue sky and blazing sun or the gaze of the Heavenly Dao. They could cultivate without restraint and freely sense the three thousand Great Daos and countless laws!

When Chu Yan, the Alchemy Saint, revealed a complete primordial spirit identical to her appearance and no longer in infant form, everyone understood that she was already a genuine Ascendant great power.

She had comprehended laws, could become god or devil with a thought, and also possessed the qualification to ascend!

What she was doing now was preparing to ascend.

Everyone held their breath and focused all their attention together, wanting to engrave every later change firmly in their hearts.

Luo Chen was no exception!

He not only concentrated his mind, but also mobilized magic power to pour into his eyes, activating Spirit Eye Art to the greatest extent in his history, striving not to miss the slightest change.

His black pupils shone with brilliant spiritual light. If everyone's attention had not been focused on the Alchemy Saint, it would likely have drawn many sidelong glances.

Under Luo Chen's burning gaze, the enormous primordial spirit filling the air slowly closed its eyes.

As she closed her eyes, majestic spiritual qi surged over from all directions, from thousands and tens of thousands of li away.

All attributes were present, not limited to any single attribute.

This spiritual qi gathered together, wrapping that enormous primordial spirit layer upon layer.

Luo Chen watched this scene thoughtfully.

If Sage cultivators could control heaven and earth spiritual qi corresponding to their own attributes, then after reaching Ascendant, such existences could already mobilize and absorb the collective body of spiritual qi between heaven and earth: origin qi.

The original qi of heaven and earth!

According to his understanding, if this world were viewed as a living being, origin qi would be the breath it exhaled and inhaled. The various attributes of spiritual qi were merely individual components within origin qi.

Ascendant cultivators evolved the primordial spirit to stand alongside heaven and earth, comprehended the true intent of laws, and grasped part of the Great Dao's authority.

At this moment, in terms of rank, Ascendant cultivators already stood on the same level as this world's heaven and earth. Only because of the difference in individual strength did they still appear small.

But in essence, they could already be considered part of heaven and earth, and naturally could absorb and use the original qi breathed by heaven and earth.

After absorbing and refining this powerful force of origin qi, a cultivator's magic power would surely possess limitless might.

Compared with the weak beings of this realm, they already possessed the great power to burn heaven and boil seas, chase stars and seize the moon!

Therefore, the honorific for Ascendant cultivators no longer used words like True Cultivator or Superior, which at lower realms expressed cultivation principle and status. Replacing them was a term symbolizing the world-destroying power they possessed.

The world called them great powers!

Luo Chen watched the majestic origin qi surge from the Central Continent land, nearly drowning the entire Heaven-Earth Peak. Thoughts rose and turned a hundred times in his heart, his mind exceptionally active.

Even though he kept circulating the Fine Dust Primordial Art to suppress emotional fluctuations, his imagination still ran wild.

"How can this be? Is it because I am seeing this feat and cannot control myself?"

But in the next moment, he understood everything from a record in the countless classics he had read.

"It is caused by the other party drawing on heaven and earth's power to assist her ascension!"

It was not so simple for Ascendant cultivators to ascend.

There were active and passive forms.

The latter required cultivation to late-stage Ascendant, when one's personal might made this world feel fear and the world would actively force the other party to ascend.

The former, because the realm was insufficient and personal strength inadequate, required heaven and earth to give a push.

And how did heaven and earth exert force?

The majestic origin qi drawn by the primordial spirit was one portion of power, while the other portion...

Luo Chen took a deep breath. The source power obtained from body cultivation began slowly mobilizing within him, gradually rising to his eyes.

This was a very dangerous method.

At ordinary times, Luo Chen would never use source power to cast spells. After all, source power and magic power were products of two different cultivation systems, and the arts suited to them were entirely different.

But at this moment, he needed to glimpse this world's truth.

There was no power more suitable than source power!

Because source power was only born in desolate beasts, and desolate beasts were the original favored children of the Mountains and Seas Realm.

When source power rose to his eyes, Luo Chen's eye sockets immediately reddened, and the scene he saw became entirely scarlet.

And within that scarlet, he vaguely detected some extraordinary existences flying from Central Continent toward the Alchemy Saint's primordial spirit.

"Are these spiritual impulses?"

Luo Chen murmured inwardly.

He had once seen a description of "spiritual impulse" in an ancient classic.

"The universe's spiritual impulse is distant and obscure, chaotic and dim. Thus the Great Dao is hard to seek, and living beings remain benighted. Only when heaven grants spiritual impulse, bright and unobscured, can clouds be parted, mist seen through, and true meaning obtained!"

That so-called spiritual impulse was not a kind of power, but more like the consciousness born from a world.

This consciousness drove the growth of all things in a world and evolved all kinds of Great Dao laws.

Therefore, when people often said heaven and earth had spirit, this "spirit" never referred to spiritual qi, but to the ethereal and unfathomable thing called spiritual impulse.

At this moment, the Alchemy Saint released her primordial spirit mainly to attract these spiritual impulses.

Only, the spiritual impulse she absorbed seemed a little too much?

"Why is the range so large?"

In Luo Chen's vision, spiritual impulse spread across several thousand li.

And beyond tens of millions of li, where his eyes could not see, it seemed that still more mysterious spiritual impulse was surging over.

If Luo Chen's realm were higher, he would discover that at this moment, not only Central Continent, but even the other four continents had faint spiritual impulses actively flowing toward Central Continent.

For now, he only assumed that ascension by an Ascendant great power was like this, so he did not dig into it and continued observing the Alchemy Saint's actions.

The process of her drawing spiritual impulse continued for a very long time.

Almost half a day.

The majestic pressure even pressed all low-level cultivators on Heaven-Earth Peak to the ground.

Only Sages could sit high in the clouds, but the pressure on them was still enormous.

Luo Chen, whose eyes were scarlet, could no longer support using source power to keep activating Spirit Eye Art and lowered his head.

"It is still not over? Why has the auspicious sign of ascension not appeared?"

Ancient classics recorded that when an Ascendant cultivator ascended, it was like a wanderer leaving home. Heaven and earth, as "parents," would create all kinds of auspicious signs to send them off.

Rosy light lifting them up was the most common scene.

There were also auspicious signs such as golden lotuses surging from the earth, ten thousand spirits cheering, and colored clouds chasing the moon.

Just as Luo Chen waited with anticipation, the long-awaited auspicious sign finally appeared.

Amid the hazy world, a strand of rosy light pierced through Heaven-Earth Peak like a sharp sword and shone upon the Alchemy Saint's primordial spirit.

As if sensing it, the Alchemy Saint's primordial spirit trembled, and a frail body floated up from the depths of Heaven-Earth Peak.

Next, if nothing unexpected happened, the primordial spirit should return to its position, and she should ascend to the immortal realm on rosy clouds, right?

This was what Luo Chen expected.

But he did not notice that some people had no such expectation at all.

Even the Alchemy Saint did not return her primordial spirit to its position. Instead, she kept it outside, continuously drawing more spiritual impulse from the Mountains and Seas Realm.

Luo Chen waited for a long time and did not see the scene in his heart.

In confusion, he subconsciously raised his head.

The Alchemy Saint still made no move. The voice that answered his and others' doubts was that of Tianyuan Dao Sect Master Mo Shouzhuo.

"You have worked hard. Leave the rest to me."

One calm sentence drew everyone's gaze to the old man in black-and-white Daoist robes.

He beckoned behind him.

Another old man behind him respectfully presented an ancient go board.

Only then did people remember the two figures who had appeared together with the Tianyuan Sect Master earlier but had been ignored all along.

The white-haired one had a resounding reputation: Chess Saint Ke Lianshan.

The beautiful but forlorn one belonged to the Tianyuan Dao Sect's master-disciple lineage, the current most outstanding true disciple, Mingyan.

The Tianyuan Sect Master took the go board from Chess Saint Ke Lianshan. Holding it up with his left hand, he used two fingers of his right like a sword and traced patterns in the void.

Luo Chen could not help activating Spirit Eye Art with source power once again.

Under his gaze, he saw strand after strand of invisible, formless spiritual impulse gathering at the Tianyuan Sect Master's right fingertips.

Very quickly, a miniature vortex formed.

Then the Tianyuan Sect Master bent a finger and pressed. That vortex fell onto the very center of the ancient go board like a go stone.

Tianyuan!

At this moment, the Alchemy Saint seemed relieved of a heavy burden. Her primordial spirit returned to its position, sinking into her frail body.

At this moment, the ancient go board trembled slightly, starlight flickering without cease.

Also at this moment, heaven and earth shook, and wind and cloud howled!

The rosy light that had descended from the sky instantly scattered.

Heavy black clouds madly gathered above Heaven-Earth Peak, and silver-white arcs of lightning flickered within them.

Seeing this, Luo Chen was dumbstruck.

"What is he doing?"

"These spiritual impulses are meant to lift Chu Yan, the Alchemy Saint, to the immortal realm!"

"Could that go board be the Tianyuan Dao Sect's sect-guarding spirit treasure, Rotten Axe-Handle?"

"Rumor says that this go board alone can suppress a continent's spiritual impulse. But now, it has become a treasure for interception, taking the spiritual impulse that belongs to the Alchemy Saint into the board."

Luo Chen had no time to keep watching the Tianyuan Sect Master's actions. He hurriedly looked toward the Alchemy Saint.

Interrupting the ascension opportunity and intercepting heaven and earth's spiritual impulse for private use. Though he did not know what benefit this brought the Tianyuan Dao Sect, for Chu Yan, the Alchemy Saint, it was absolutely an act of great rebellion that angered heaven and earth.

The consequences...

Black clouds filled the sky. Tribulation lightning gathered its might!

In a cultivator's life, they would only trigger heavenly tribulation three times.

Once in the Sage stage.

Once in the Body Integration stage.

Once in the Tribulation Crossing stage.

Each time had three rounds, commonly called the three-nine heavenly tribulation.

Ascendant ascension was originally a joyous matter and should not have drawn heavenly tribulation at all.

But now, the tribulation that had abruptly formed possessed terrifying might beyond imagination!

How would Chu Yan, the Alchemy Saint, face it?

Amid Luo Chen's worry, the frail woman's expression was calm, and she was actually unmoved.

Sitting cross-legged in the void, she slowly stood.

Plain white Daoist robes covered her exquisite body, and on her slightly round little face, the corners of her mouth rose.

She flicked her sleeve, and the majestic origin qi around her immediately trembled.

Rumble!

The origin qi was scattered, turning into spiritual rain that fell upon every cultivator on the cloud-sea plaza.

The low-level cultivators who had been weak and collapsed drank it like sweet dew, only feeling clear-minded and refreshed, their Dao attainments improving.

Some Initiate great-perfection cultivators near the Adept stage even unknowingly condensed qi vortices within their bodies and began automatically forming cores.

Some cultivators with hidden injuries had old ailments vanish in this moment, their whole bodies becoming light.

Even the Sages seated high in the clouds felt their magic power increase greatly.

Luo Chen, who had originally been a little short of the second level of Sage, faintly felt that bottleneck loosen. If he quietly cultivated for a period afterward, he should be able to break through to second-level Sage with ease.

"Is this her grace?" Luo Chen thought.

But Chu Yan's movements had not ended.

She grasped at the void. As the Tianyuan Sect Master frowned, she seemed to catch something.

Then she exhaled clear qi, and her ten fingers repeatedly formed seals in the air.

Looking at this scene, Luo Chen felt somewhat dazed.

"Why does this feel so familiar?"

"Those hand seals are pill arts?"

"She is refining a pill!"

"But why do I find this pill-refining method somewhat familiar?"

Suddenly.

Chu Yan stopped moving. Five fist-sized light spheres hovered before her.

"Sect Master Mo, my apologies. Before leaving, this little woman still wishes to leave behind something."

The Tianyuan Sect Master's brows, with his fingers pressed against the go board, slowly relaxed. "No matter. A little spiritual impulse does not affect the overall situation."

The reason he said this was because among the five light spheres, two fell among the Central Continent clan representatives, and one flew toward the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range. This represented that the Tianyuan Dao Sect received the most from the Alchemy Saint's final gift.

The ownership of the remaining two light spheres was somewhat unexpected.

One flew into the hands of the young man from the Eastern Wilderness Nether Origin Sect.

"Martial Aunt..."

"Accept it. This is a little compensation from me to the Nether Origin Sect."

The man pressed his lips together and put away the light sphere.

The final light sphere, under ten thousand gazes, fell into the hands of a gray-haired middle-aged man among the many Star Gate guest elders.

Luo Chen was somewhat stunned.

Facing one gaze after another, especially Elder Seven Lamps's burning gaze nearby, he felt uncomfortable all over.

"Senior Alchemy Saint, this?"

Chu Yan smiled slightly. "Your true flame's transformation is only one step away. With this Heavenly Secret Pill[TERM?] to assist, success will follow naturally. This also counts as my goodwill toward Star Gate."

Luo Chen froze. How did she know his Withering-Bloom True Flame was about to complete transformation? And why give this gift to him, the newest external affairs guest elder, rather than other Star Gate guest elders and offering elders?

There was no time left to think.

Because the Tianyuan Sect Master said in a vicissitude-filled voice, "You should go."

And the Alchemy Saint went!

Boom!

Above Heaven-Earth Peak, Star Gate opened wide.

A brilliant pillar of light shot into the sky, scattering the black clouds and rushing out a mysterious gate in the azure depths of the ninth heaven.

The frail woman walked upward along the brilliant pillar of light. Under the world's gaze, she pushed open that gate and disappeared.

Without her figure, heaven and earth's anger had nowhere to vent.

In the end, black clouds vanished and tribulation lightning scattered.

Now green mountains stood, rivers flowed, clear moon and wind remained, but the beauty was gone.

Luo Chen stared blankly at the ninth heaven. Toward this woman whom he had met twice but only truly seen once, he unexpectedly felt a strange reluctance.

At this moment, beneath the night sky, a deep dragon roar suddenly sounded.

"Spiritual impulse is sufficient. Mirage Dragon wakes. Tianyuan disciples, enter the grotto-heaven!"

Luo Chen abruptly turned back and gazed down below Heaven-Earth Peak.

Beneath Heaven-Earth Peak, by the Passing River, vast white mist suddenly rose, and a great mouth formed of cloud and mist slowly opened.

The great mouth was deep, connecting reality and illusion, as though leading to another space.

*(End of Chapter)*
