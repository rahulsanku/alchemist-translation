# Chapter 540: Nine-Zhang Giant Cauldron, Hundred-Pills Simultaneous Refinement

Inside the quiet cultivation chamber,

only the long breaths spaced far apart proved that someone was within.

After another wave of medicinal balefulness was completely spat out, the crimson color on Luo Chen's face slowly faded.

Although the redness faded a great deal, because he had been taking True Flame Pills year-round and expelling medicinal balefulness, his face still appeared flushed red.

If his build were a little more robust, once he went outside, he would probably earn the simple nickname "red-faced big man."

That was not important.

In cultivation, there were ten thousand different paths, and each relied on their own methods to walk toward the final Great Dao of longevity.

It was precisely because of this that, since cultivators cultivated different strange arts and unusual methods, outward phenomena often manifested.

For example, red faces, purple faces, or sickly wax-yellow complexions.

There were also some whose cultivation went awry, turning their hair white overnight or making their hair dry and withered. Such cases were not rare either.

Luo Chen did not much care about the complexion of his face, but through the changes in his facial color, he could indirectly see just how tyrannical the medicinal power of this supreme-grade True Flame Pill was!

"One pill is enough to equal ten low-grade ones!"

Luo Chen muttered to himself, his eyes full of pleasant surprise.

For ordinary pills, according to their differing grades, the medicinal power often showed a trend of doubling.

One, two, four, eight, respectively corresponding to the four levels of low-grade, middle-grade, high-grade, and supreme-grade.

This was something Luo Chen had figured out himself.

Other alchemists?

Sorry, among ten thousand alchemists, one might not necessarily be able to refine a supreme-grade pill. Naturally, they had no way to explore this rule.

It was precisely because of this that Luo Chen was so pleasantly surprised.

Because the medicinal power of the True Flame Pill was special, it had slightly broken this rule.

The increase in its medicinal power lay within the levels of one, two, five, and ten.

In other words, after the True Flame Pill's grade reached high-grade, it produced a qualitative change, reaching its greatest limit at the supreme-grade level!

This kind of change far exceeded the Starfield Pills he had taken for years before.

And this was only the difference in change. If one truly compared medicinal power, a single low-grade True Flame Pill had fifty percent more medicinal power than a Starfield Pill.

The detailed data within was rather complicated, and only Luo Chen, the pill-taker, could have a clear grasp of it.

After a round of calculation, his pleasant surprise gradually faded, leaving only threads of regret.

"Its cost-performance ratio is actually quite high, but unless I draw the bones and suck the marrow of the Swallow Islands, they cannot support my cultivation."

If following the most perfect method, he would naturally take supreme-grade True Flame Pills every day to cultivate. In that way, his cultivation speed could reach its maximum.

One supreme-grade pill equaled ten low-grade True Flame Pills, and fifteen low-grade Starfield Pills.

So long as his financial strength could support it, and no drug resistance appeared, then the time Luo Chen needed to cultivate to Core Formation Perfected Completion could be controlled within two hundred years even with only a third-tier low-grade spirit vein, and might even require only a little over a hundred years.

The problem lay precisely in that "financial strength."

Very clearly, the Swallow Islands could not support his daily consumption.

Just the previous situation alone had already made the various great clans sigh and groan with complaints.

If he took supreme-grade True Flame Pills every day for cultivation, the entire thirty-six Swallow Islands would be sucked dry by him.

Sooner or later, rebellion would occur!

Therefore, after weighing things, Luo Chen could only control the grade of the pills he took daily at the high-grade level.

In this way, ten portions of raw materials, at a fifty-percent success rate, could produce five portions of True Flame Pills, which meant fifty high-grade pills, enough for him to use for nearly two months.

This was relatively cheaper than ten portions producing only one portion of supreme-grade True Flame Pills, which meant ten pills.

The medicinal power he could absorb, without considering time, would also be somewhat greater.

Both were cost-performance ratios. One targeted cultivation speed, the other financial strength.

After comprehensive consideration, Luo Chen chose the plan most suited to the present situation.

Feeling the Golden Core within his body slowly absorb the remaining medicinal power, Luo Chen naturally thought of yesterday's conversation with Wu Qi.

Moluo Stream.

Blood Nightmare Moluo.

The Twelve Stream Lords.

The Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance.

Finally, his thoughts stopped on the "ten years" mentioned by the other party.

According to the current situation, ten years later, he should be able to steadily step into Core Formation Stage Four, which was the mid Core Formation realm.

At that time, his strength would also undergo a qualitative change.

When he faced Wu Qi then, his chances of victory would occupy seventy to eighty percent.

But that was only Wu Qi!

And it was a Wu Qi who had not yet exhausted all his methods.

"In the Northern Sea cultivation world, demons and devils range everywhere. The combat abilities of the cultivators here are especially superior to the domains of the Eastern Wasteland. Perhaps only those cultivators of the new domains near the million-li Barbarian Wilds Great Mountains are not weak against peers in battle."

Two exchanges with local same-realm cultivators had already made Luo Chen faintly sense the strength of "Demon Sea" cultivators.

He also naturally revised his assessment of his own strength.

At Core Formation Stage Three, he could battle mid Core Formation, but against a late Core Formation great cultivator, he definitely could not win, though he could run.

This barely matched the previous idea of "enough to protect himself."

And it was only barely. He absolutely had to run.

If he wanted to withstand them, that would have to wait until after mid Core Formation.

This made Luo Chen somewhat dissatisfied.

He had bitterly cultivated many spells, had done his utmost to sacrificially refine his magic treasures day and night, and also cultivated a technique known as the ancestor of fire arts. Yet in the end, he could only reach this degree.

It truly dealt a bit of a blow to his confidence.

If outsiders knew these thoughts of his, they would probably angrily curse him.

At early and mid Core Formation, you are already thinking of battling late-stage great cultivators by force? What do you take other people's several hundred years of bitter cultivation for?

"Not enough!"

"Far from enough!"

"Now that my cultivation speed is guaranteed, I must once again strengthen my combat ability."

"Having hope of longevity is not worth rejoicing over. Surviving a hundred battles is the true principle."

After many years of cultivation, Luo Chen's goal was no longer merely as simple as longevity.

So-called longevity was nothing more than a change in lifespan.

Once realm rose, endless lifespan would naturally be added.

But such cultivators likewise faced the risk of falling.

Therefore, under endless lifespan, possessing endless combat strength was "true longevity"!

Especially since, after several assessments, Luo Chen felt it was very possible that he would cultivate to Core Formation Perfected Completion within two hundred years.

In that case, resources related to forming a Nascent Soul had to be planned for as early as possible, lest he become flustered and run around everywhere when the time came.

He did not want to imitate Fu Jiusheng, only going out to seek Core Formation resources after reaching Foundation Establishment Perfected Completion.

Therefore!

Ten years later, regardless of whether he joined Moluo Stream, he had to leave the Swallow Islands and go befriend more Core Formation cultivators in the Northern Sea cultivation world.

From their hands, he would trade for more resources related to Nascent Soul formation.

Especially the three main materials for the Nascent Soul Pill: Divine Iris, Purple Monkey Flower, and Five Elements Lotus Platform.

Perhaps opening up connections would involve risks, but as Han Zhan had said, how could the path of cultivation always be smooth sailing? Risk was inevitable!

"Among my current methods, aside from refining my lifebound magic treasure, most have basically advanced as far as they can."

"If I want to increase combat strength further, I can only place it on other aspects."

Luo Chen evaluated his current state.

In the end, his idea landed on body refinement.

With the supplementation of large amounts of high-quality blood food, his physique had already reached [Archaic Tier Three 40/100], solidly at the mid third-tier level.

In five years, it had increased ten points of progress, surpassing several decades in the Jade Cauldron Domain.

Yet even so, Luo Chen still felt it was slow.

After all, in the beginning, his body refinement realm had walked ahead of his qi refinement realm. Now, it was about to be caught by his qi refinement realm.

Especially since the further body refinement went, the harder the physical transformation became. Perhaps its speed would even decrease.

Therefore, Luo Chen had long been searching for external auxiliary resources better than blood food and spirit meals.

With Han Zhan's awakening, this problem had been solved.

Black Emperor Paste!

As for how to refine Black Emperor Paste, Luo Chen had already formed a plan over these years.

The Swallow Islands would not do. Their conditions were insufficient. He had to go out, to another place.

"But before traveling, some matters still have to be prepared ahead of time."

"Putting aside everything else, I must prepare enough True Flame Pills for my use in advance."

The moment he thought of refining True Flame Pills, a faint smile appeared on Luo Chen's face.

At present, his True Flame Pill proficiency had already been pushed to Perfected Completion. It was just right to try that so-called "hundred-pills simultaneous refinement"!

...

After the battle between the Wu clan ancestor and Demon Lord Qingyang, the Swallow Islands sank into a strange silence.

The previous complaints and grievances were no longer seen!

If Demon Lord Qingyang killing Yan Nantian had displayed ability surpassing the previous lord of Swallow, that had still been within the estimates of Cheng Dou and the others.

After all, without a diamond drill, how would one dare take on porcelain work?

The strength Luo Chen displayed at that time was merely at the normal level of a Core Formation cultivator.

But after Luo Chen decisively acted and forced back Wu Qi, the famous Wu clan ancestor, everything became different.

That was a major figure at Core Formation Stage Six!

If late-stage cultivators did not appear, he was an existence who could walk sideways in the Northern Sea cultivation world.

Even such an existence had to "talk things out" with Demon Lord Qingyang.

They, this batch of Foundation Establishment cultivators, were usually only offering up some medicinal materials. What right did they have to so many complaints?

Under this "silent atmosphere," the various great clans tacitly increased their tribute to Luo Chen.

All kinds of True Flame Pill materials were gathered as much as possible and handed into the hands of Invitation Moon Fairy Cheng Haixin.

Luo Chen was extremely satisfied with this!

Was this not just falling asleep and meeting a pillow?

He was just preparing to carry out several rounds of large-scale pill refinement. The medicinal materials he had stored previously could only support three attempts.

His own plan was at least five attempts, and ideally seven or eight.

Only this way could he gather the quantity of True Flame Pills needed for ten years.

Now that those below were so diligent, they perfectly completed his plan, sparing him extra effort in persuasion.

Time flowed bit by bit.

Three months had already passed since that great battle.

During these three months, the various clans took action, doing their utmost to repair the losses brought by that battle.

Even so, four islands had still permanently sunk into the seabed.

Those four islands had no high-grade spirit veins, only first-tier low-grade ones. Ordinary mortals usually lived upon them. This was also why, after the battle, the various great clans did not want to spend effort digging them back out.

In this way, the thirty-six Swallow Islands became thirty-two islands.

The missing four islands, on the map, happened to be the "swallow's" wing sections.

If viewed from high above, the two wings of the flying swallow had become somewhat asymmetrical.

At present, no one cared about this.

Everyone cared about only one thing.

That was "the Demon Lord refining pills"!

...

On this day, above the ten-li calm lake, the white mist completely dispersed.

The blazing sun hanging high in the nine heavens scattered endless golden radiance, spilling across the lake surface and making it look like golden waves rippling.

Along the banks encircling the lakeside, at some unknown time, thousands upon tens of thousands of people had gradually gathered.

Most were Qi Refinement cultivators.

They gathered before one true Foundation Establishment cultivator after another, their gazes fixed on the small island at the center of the lake while they quietly discussed.

"Is the Demon Lord truly going to refine pills publicly?"

"Yes. I heard the display might be very grand!"

"Tsk tsk, how grand can it be? Could it be grander than those pill refinement assemblies held by the legendary great alchemy sects?"

"Hard to say. In any case, I know that for these several months, Demon Lord Qingyang has not refined pills. He has been conserving his strength and waiting for those below to process all kinds of raw materials for him in advance."

"What are you muttering about? Being able to watch a third-tier alchemist personally refine pills might be the only story worth boasting about in your entire lives. Why are you not watching properly?"

"Senior's lesson is right! We were rash."

"Exactly. If we are lucky enough to learn one or two moves, passing them down to descendants would also count as a family craft."

Amid this tide-like wave of discussion,

on that quiet standing small island, figures walked out one after another like flowing water under the orderly command of a tall female cultivator.

Each stood in a position, then took various medicinal materials from their storage bags.

If one observed carefully, the medicinal materials each person took out were all of the same type, without the slightest deviation.

There were eighteen people in total, separated by great distances, but on close inspection, they had clearly formed a large circle.

In the middle, an empty space was left.

Everyone looked at one another. Could that empty space in the middle be the place for refining pills?

But no special treatment could be seen!

There were none of the fire-avoidance bricks of ordinary pill chambers, no fire-enhancing formation, and no other auxiliary tools.

How was Demon Lord Qingyang going to refine pills?

Just as everyone was doubtful, an uproar came.

At some unknown time, in the center of that circular plaza, a man wearing a wide white Daoist robe was already standing there.

He merely stood there quietly, yet his tall back was like a high mountain, making people hold their breaths and look up.

It was precisely Demon Lord Qingyang!

Suddenly,

the Demon Lord waved his great sleeve, and a small cauldron spun out.

The small cauldron was pale gray in color, as if outside the Five Elements, yet also as if all things of heaven and earth, yin-yang and the Five Elements, were contained within it.

As soon as it flew out, it grew in the wind.

Three cun, seven cun, one chi, one zhang, three zhang...

By the lakeside, some low-level cultivators could not help speaking aloud.

"This damn thing is an alchemy cauldron?"

In the end, under everyone's dumbstruck gazes, it actually transformed into a giant cauldron nine zhang tall, like a tall building, and stood with a boom on Invitation Moon Island.

Three legs, two ears, a round body, and rich pill fragrance coiling around it. It truly was an alchemy cauldron. It was just that its size was indeed much larger.

Luo Chen looked at this scene in satisfaction, then opened his mouth and spat.

A wisp of azure flame leisurely emerged.

Under the support of mana, it likewise grew in the wind, transforming into raging fire that completely wrapped the Primordial Unity Cauldron within.

Not only that, he leaped and flew into the high sky, looking down from above.

His great sleeves unfurled. The prepared medicinal materials nearby fell continuously into the Primordial Unity Cauldron like streams of light.

One portion, two portions... only after a hundred portions of raw materials had all gathered within one cauldron did Luo Chen stop moving.

At this point, his expression gained several degrees of solemnity.

The test had come!

If hundred-pills simultaneous refinement could succeed, then on his future cultivation path, he could save vast amounts of time wasted on pill refinement.

He could refine many pills in a single attempt, then use the saved time elsewhere.

Inside Qingyang Hall on Invitation Moon Island, a black banner leaned beside the door.

On the black banner, a pair of ghost-qi-condensed eyeballs burned as they gazed at the figure above the giant cauldron.

"Such a grand scene was something only Medicine King Sect held once every ten years in the past."

"Even Azure Pill Valley, a great alchemy sect, would rarely see it."

"Such a display, such bearing, such grace. It is not the slightest bit inferior to those fourth-tier alchemists of Sage upper sects!"

"Luo Chen, haha... the cultivators of this place only know your name as Demon Lord Qingyang, but do not know your dao title Dan Chenzi. That is truly a pity."

(End of chapter)
