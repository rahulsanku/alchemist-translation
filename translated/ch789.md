# Chapter 789: Alchemy Grandmaster, Extensive Connections

After the earlier minor incident, the atmosphere within the hall eased.

Today's Nascent Soul Ceremony had been different from ordinary ones from the very start.

Luo Chen had formed his Nascent Soul decades ago, and not in the Eastern Wilderness at that—how could there be any talk of fellow Daoists coming to congratulate him?

Today's gathering was not large, nor could it be called a grand sect-wide celebration.

The various True Persons who came had not brought any disciples or juniors to listen to Luo Chen's teachings.

Clearly, everyone understood the purpose of this Nascent Soul Ceremony that Luo Chen had deliberately arranged.

He wanted to build connections!

Given the claim on the invitation—"Refining a hundred pills, proficient in all"—the fellow Daoists of the Five Regions were willing to give him this face.

But that face was given by others.

After that incident with Jin Ling, Luo Chen's momentum had not been suppressed; on the contrary, it had risen to new heights.

At this point, he no longer needed others to give him face. Relying solely on his own strength, he could completely establish himself in the Eastern Wilderness!

Of course, Luo Chen had never liked fighting and killing. He was not one for conflict.

"You may think my words on the invitation were somewhat exaggerated."

"Luo does not take offense, for such bold claims do sound arrogant."

"But excessive modesty is itself arrogance. In the Dao of alchemy, I do have some pride and confidence."

"Honored guests, please observe!"

When Luo Chen proactively brought up the matter of alchemy, everyone's attention converged on him.

After a bit of self-praise, Luo Chen directly presented evidence that could prove his alchemy level.

One by one, Hongyuan Pills of top-grade quality were delivered before everyone by his magical power.

Instantly, as they held the pills, expressions of shock appeared on their faces.

"This pill fragrance is so rich, the medicinal power contained within is so vast!"

"Fourth-tier, top-grade!"

"True Person Luo, did you refine these?"

"Was it by chance, or..."

Facing each question, Luo Chen answered with a smile.

And when asked whether he could stably refine fourth-tier top-grade pills, though he did not fully admit it, he stated that he had great confidence in his success rate and finished product yield.

The Lord of Lingtian City, who came from the Nether Origin Sect, held the Hongyuan Pill, also showing disbelief.

Fourth-tier pills, top-grade?

Even the Alchemy Saint was no more than this at his peak!

He remembered very clearly that Chu Yan had not been able to stably refine fourth-tier top-grade pills at first. Only with luck could she occasionally produce top-grade ones.

It was not until later, when she fortuitously refined a batch of fifth-tier divine pills and thereby earned the title of Alchemy Saint, that she could, with a high-level perspective and skill, stably refine fourth-tier top-grade pills.

In other words, Luo Chen's current alchemy level was almost equivalent to Chu Yan's final stage as the Alchemy Saint in the Eastern Wilderness?

Even if he could not yet refine fifth-tier divine pills and could not use the extreme title of Alchemy Saint, the appellation of Alchemy Grandmaster was absolutely no lie!

How could such an existence be allowed to become a dog of Heavenly Origin, a guest elder of the Star Gate!

The Lord of Lingtian City couldn't help but look up at Luo Chen.

Facing gazes of astonishment, reverence, and even disbelief, Luo Chen accepted them calmly.

Hide one's light?

No need!

Before a behemoth like a Holy Land of Ascendants, hiding one's light was even more unnecessary!

If they were unreasonable and simply crushed you to death, that would be ridiculous.

Only by displaying sufficient value could one gain the attention of superiors, the flattery and respect of peers, and the admiration of inferiors.

And this value lay in being beneficial to others.

In this time of chaos, fourth-tier pills that could stably enhance the strength of Nascent Soul True Persons were undoubtedly the most beneficial and valuable.

"This pill is not suitable for you all; it is merely a personal cultivation pill for Luo's own use."

"But Luo's alchemy Dao has already reached great accomplishment. As for other pills, as long as there is a complete pill formula, sufficient raw materials, and enough time for me, refining them is not a difficult matter."

Luo Chen spoke lightly, as if discussing something trivial.

But the content of his words stirred up a huge commotion in everyone's hearts.

Immediately, someone eagerly asked.

"Can fourth-tier pills be refined?"

Luo Chen glanced at the questioner. He wore a gourd-shaped topknot and flowing wind robes, exuding an air of ethereal transcendence. It was none other than the Grand Supreme of the Misty Sect, Lingfengzi.

Luo Chen nodded slightly. "Yes!"

Lingfengzi was overjoyed!

Another person asked.

"Can breakthrough pills be refined?"

This time, it was Elder Fu who asked.

His eyes revealed an urgent eagerness.

The Divine Talisman Sect, in terms of wealth within the Extreme East Six Regions, had once been on par with the Medicine King Sect.

But later, the Medicine King Sect left them far behind, and even Tianfan City caught up and surpassed the Divine Talisman Sect.

The reason for all this was the talent gap in the Divine Talisman Sect.

Elder Fu was already nine hundred years old!

He had formed his Nascent Soul five hundred years ago!

For a full five hundred years, the Divine Talisman Sect had not produced a second Nascent Soul True Person.

This not only hindered the sect's development but even led to its gradual decline.

If not for Elder Fu being an old hand who knew when to keep his head down, the sect would likely have been destroyed in the repeated sect competitions and demon beast rampages.

The meaning of his question now was self-evident.

Facing that eager gaze, the corners of Luo Chen's mouth lifted slightly.

"That too is possible!"

*Thump!*

The sound of a table being struck abruptly rang out.

Luo Chen glanced sideways. The Lord of Lingtian City's right hand was somewhat undignifiedly resting on the table, his expression complex.

Everyone saw this scene, but no one paid it any mind.

They were more concerned about the truth of Luo Chen's words.

The Nine Spirit Primordial Lord suddenly stood up. "Fellow Daoist Luo, for an incomplete fourth-tier pill formula, can you still refine the finished pill?"

Luo Chen smiled freely. "I have never seen it, so I dare not boast. But if I do refine it, that will be a different price. Primordial Lord, you should think carefully before coming."

The Nine Spirit Primordial Lord was overjoyed.

Others suddenly realized one thing.

The price!

Yes, Luo Chen's willingness to help them refine pills was already a great favor.

But one thing was another; compensation was a separate matter!

After all, these days, it wasn't just about having money to get things done; you also needed the other party to give you that face.

Immediately, someone asked about Luo Chen's conditions for taking action.

Luo Chen was not coy and generously stated a few things.

Red Firmament Qi, large amounts of Red Firmament Qi!

High-level body refinement techniques, no time limit!

Various pill formulas, no grade limit.

These three were the general conditions.

Anyone holding any one of these three could come to Luo Chen, but the specific details and additional costs would need to be discussed further.

After all, the difficulty of refining each pill formula, the time and energy required, were all different, so the prices naturally varied.

After Luo Chen released these conditions, the atmosphere in the hall cooled slightly. Everyone was thinking about whether they had anything that met the requirements.

But a few people had their minds elsewhere.

For instance, the Divine Fire Sage, seeing this scene, couldn't help but recall the words Jin Ling had said earlier.

Luo Chen was really about to become a force to be reckoned with!

In a short time, he had become the center of attention.

If he really started refining pills for everyone, then once the favors were given out, people's hearts would naturally be in his grasp.

If the Five Elements Divine Sect wanted to deal with Luo Chen again, it would probably...

"Divine Fire, I heard that your sect's Pure Pool Elder died under the siege of the Demon Emperors. Now, can anyone in your Divine Sect still refine the Great Five Elements Divine Pill?"

Divine Fire suddenly looked up, meeting Luo Chen's smiling eyes, feeling a lump in his throat.

As the strongest sect in the Extreme East Six Regions, the Five Elements Divine Sect at its peak could rival those ancient powerful sects.

And the elders of the five peaks within the sect each had their own strengths.

Jin Ling was skilled in battle, Linmu in nurturing, and he, Divine Fire, was adept at artifact forging.

But if one were to say who was the most important among the five peak elders, it would undoubtedly be Pure Pool.

She was the chief alchemist of the Five Elements Divine Sect. Whether it was the Ruoshui Pills used by Initiates, the Five Elements Divine Pills commonly used by Adepts, or the Great Five Elements Divine Pills used to assist their Nascent Soul True Persons in cultivation, all were produced by Pure Pool.

But in that battle back then, of the five peak elders, two died and one was injured. Pure Pool died under the siege of several Demon Emperors.

With her death, the sect's pill supply was cut off.

Now Luo Chen brought this up, it was almost like publicly picking at a scab!

Divine Fire suppressed the anger in his heart. "Luo Chen, what do you mean by this?"

Luo Chen smiled slightly. "My meaning is simple. If your sect has no one who can refine the Five Elements Divine Pills, I wouldn't mind helping you out."

"Oh, are you that kind-hearted?" Divine Fire was puzzled.

Luo Chen shrugged. "I have always been kind-hearted and cannot bear to see others suffer. As long as your sect's Grand Supreme Elder personally comes with the pill formula for the Great Five Elements Divine Pill, what harm would it be for me to refine a batch of spiritual pills for you?"

Divine Fire was stunned, but quickly reacted.

"Apologies, my sect's Grand Supreme is unwell and does not see outsiders."

With that, harboring anger, he found an excuse to take his leave and depart.

Watching his retreating figure, Luo Chen's eyes narrowed slightly.

...

This Nascent Soul Ceremony did not last long, concluding at dusk.

The strong from various sects and factions left one after another.

Before leaving, Qin Baiming of the Myriad Beast Sect glanced a few times at the calm and composed Idle Crane True Person.

"This old fellow came early and now looks like he's not planning to leave. Luo Chen hasn't known him for long; can they really have that good a relationship?"

Qin Baiming was puzzled, but he still left quickly.

He planned to go back and discuss with his fellow sect members whether to take out some resources and ask Luo Chen to refine a few batches of pills beneficial for raising spirit beasts.

On Great Green Mountain.

Idle Crane True Person walked side by side with Luo Chen, chatting and laughing.

"I heard earlier that you instantly defeated Motian and overpowered Divine Fire within Lingtian Pass. At the time, I was half-convinced. But today, in the domain battle, you forcibly defeated Jin Ling. It truly opens one's eyes."

"Haha, you flatter me! That old thief Jin Ling also said that domain battles are but minor arts; the real test will be when they meet on the battlefield."

"Fellow Daoist Luo is so carefree; it seems you are also confident in your practical skills!"

"After all, he is a mid-stage Nascent Soul cultivator a whole level above me. How can there be any talk of certainty? I'm just putting up a strong front."

Idle Crane True Person glanced at Luo Chen, seeing no sign of someone pretending to be tough, only full confidence.

He shook his head, stopped beating around the bush, and got straight to the point.

"Can the Imperial Moonlight Nectar be supplied in large quantities to my White Crane Sect?"

Luo Chen seemed to have expected this question and asked with a smile, "I presume you have already tested this medicine?"

Idle Crane True Person draped his horsetail whisk over his arm and stroked his beard. "Indeed, as you said, this medicine has great benefits for the advancement of demon beasts. If we can obtain a large amount of Imperial Moonlight Nectar, my White Crane Sect's strength will surely reach new heights."

Luo Chen smiled knowingly. "Fellow Daoist Idle Crane, is your experimental result limited to this?"

Idle Crane was startled, tightened his grip on his beard, and his expression turned slightly unnatural.

Luo Chen continued on his own. "Imperial Moonlight Nectar has an excellent effect on the advancement of demon beasts, but for Qi Refinement cultivators, it also has a twenty to thirty percent efficiency in breaking through to the Initiate stage. Its wondrous uses are in no way inferior to orthodox Initiate spiritual medicines. I have never heard of any sect supplying such resources in large quantities."

Since ancient times, breakthrough-type pills have been far more valuable than same-tier cultivation-type pills.

In the black market or among loose cultivators, the premium could reach ten times, even a hundred times!

Even in normal transactions, the price would be exorbitant.

Exposed by Luo Chen, Idle Crane awkwardly lowered his hand.

"There is indeed a somewhat similar effect."

Then, his tone turned serious.

"Fellow Daoist Luo, just name your price!"

"Name my price?"

Luo Chen chuckled.

"I don't care much for ordinary spirit stones. If you truly want to trade, why not bring out a batch of well-trained, accomplished spirit cranes!"

"That's impossible!" Idle Crane True Person refused outright. "Spirit cranes are the foundation of my sect. How can they be sold to outsiders?"

Luo Chen was indeed somewhat covetous of the White Crane Sect's spirit cranes.

They were highly intelligent, far surpassing ordinary demon beasts, and could even patrol and guard the mountain gate on their own.

Moreover, their flight speed was extremely fast. Among the same tier, probably only powerful avian demon beasts like the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gulls could be considered superior.

But the White Crane Sect's spirit cranes were social creatures, existing in large flocks.

Unlike the solitary Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gulls.

Although his Luo Tian Sect had lost Xu Haizhen, they had retained the techniques for controlling flying birds from earlier years.

If they could obtain a large batch of well-trained spirit cranes, they could instantly enhance the strength of their disciples, at least greatly increasing their self-defense capabilities.

Moreover, these spirit cranes could also compensate for the disadvantage of the Luo Tian Sect's insufficient number of disciples.

In his position, he sought the governance of his office.

Now that Luo Chen had returned to his identity as the Grand Supreme of Luo Tian, he was destined not to be as carefree as before.

Nor did he find it troublesome; it was just a matter of moving his lips.

Moreover, in this process, he could build his own network of relationships.

A lone ranger was carefree, but when trouble came, he could only bear it alone. How could that compare to the ease of having allies to fight side by side!

At this moment, facing Idle Crane True Person's outright refusal, Luo Chen was not angry.

He said with a smile, "Fellow Daoist, if you so eagerly want Imperial Moonlight Nectar to enhance your disciples' strength, I presume you are also afraid of the war spreading here, right?"

Without waiting for Idle Crane True Person to answer, he continued, "The front line in the three regions has been deadlocked for several years. Collapse is only a matter of time. Once the three regions all fall, the demon army's spear will surely point directly at Lingtian Pass. At that time, everyone within the five regions will be drawn into the most dangerous great war between humans and demons!"

"And this war is not only the most dangerous but also leaves no room for mercy. The demon race will not be soft-hearted, and the Nether Origin Sect behind the Eastern Wilderness will not allow us to take a single step back."

"Therefore, every person and every faction is doing their utmost to enhance their strength, hoping to survive the future war, or even seize more benefits."

"Fellow Daoist Idle Crane, am I right?"

Facing Luo Chen's gaze, Idle Crane True Person opened his mouth, then finally sighed softly.

"Fellow Daoist, you have only just returned to the Eastern Wilderness, yet you have such a clear and insightful view of the situation here."

Luo Chen smiled and shook his head, pacing a few steps, suddenly falling silent as if lost in thought.

Idle Crane True Person was puzzled. To avoid an awkward silence, he said dryly, "My White Crane Sect's spirit cranes are limited in number. It is indeed inconvenient to trade them. Why not change to another..."

Suddenly!

After pondering for a long while, Luo Chen raised his head and stared at Idle Crane True Person.

"Last time at your sect's mountain gate, I sensed the presence of another Crane True Person, but he did not appear. Could it be that the rumor is true?"

Idle Crane True Person's expression changed drastically!

"Fellow Daoist Luo Chen, what do you mean by this?"

This reaction confirmed Luo Chen's speculation.

Among the five regions of Lingtian, the five Nascent Soul-level powers, aside from the weakest Mu Manor, the other four were actually of similar strength.

The Elegant Wind Palace had two Palace Masters, the Myriad Sword Pavilion also had two Nascent Soul True Persons, and the Myriad Beast Sect, besides Qin Baiming, also enslaved a Demon Emperor, counting as two Nascent Soul-level combatants.

Then there was the White Crane Sect.

Rumor had it that the White Crane Sect also had two Nascent Soul True Persons.

Idle Crane True Person, and a transformed spirit crane.

However, whether from external rumors or the intelligence given by the Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance, no one had ever seen that transformed spirit crane, and peers could only sense his aura.

Over time, a rumor emerged.

That Crane True Person of the White Crane Sect had indeed passed the thunder tribulation but failed in transformation, making it inconvenient for him to appear in the world.

Passing the thunder tribulation but failing in transformation sounded contradictory.

In reality, it was not difficult to understand.

It was simply that during the tribulation, someone used external force to help the tribulation-crosser. Although they survived the great life-and-death tribulation, they lacked the baptism of heaven and earth, thus failing to achieve complete success.

The subsequent effects were that the demon cultivator could not use human form to cultivate quickly, and because their original body was too large, it was inconvenient to walk abroad, among other disadvantages.

The rumor about the White Crane Sect might not be false.

The Myriad Sword Pavilion secretly supporting the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect and sending Lin Qingxuan to test the White Crane Sect was evidence of this.

Otherwise, why would the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect, now with only a few cats and dogs, dare to provoke the White Crane Sect, which had two Nascent Soul True Persons?

Now, having obtained Idle Crane True Person's reaction, Luo Chen had a clear idea in his heart.

"My meaning is not complicated."

"Relying on Imperial Moonlight Nectar to cultivate the strength of your disciples requires daily accumulation over time. You don't have that much time now."

"On the contrary, if that Crane True Person of your sect could easily walk among men, that would undoubtedly be the greatest and most immediate enhancement for you, wouldn't it?"

*Whoosh!*

A pill appeared in Luo Chen's hand.

"This pill is called the Transformation Pill. I am willing to offer this pill as a gift to forge good relations between our two sects!"

Discharged from the hospital!

Nothing much to say, it's time to fight from here on out.

(The End)
