# Chapter 1076: Azure Yang Emerges, Ghosts and Gods Appear, Killing into the Dao Sect

Beyond the Tiannan Continent.

Above a stretch of sea, a massive flying boat sped as fast as possible in a direction away from the Tiannan Continent.

On the flying boat, figures flickered in and out of view.

On every face were expressions of bewilderment, unease, and confusion.

They could not understand why their family, which had finally settled down, had to suddenly leave White Egret Marsh.

And what made them confused and uneasy was—where were they going next?

Inside the flying boat, in a private room, four figures sat clustered together, their expressions grave.

"The Eastern Wilderness?"

"Yes, we're heading to the Eastern Wilderness. Our destination is Calm Mountain Sea!"

"Calm Mountain Sea? Where is that?"

"It's said to have once been the territory of an Adept Supreme Sect, but later it was caught in the aftermath of a battle between Ascendant-level powerhouses and turned into an inland great lake. Adjacent to it are the Tai Lake Domain, which has many mortals, and the Azure Sky Domain, which is rumored to be a land of no living beings. We can go there to peacefully grow our family, and since it's not far from the prosperous Lingtian Pass, trading with the outside world will be much easier in the future."

"Arranged so thoroughly? Was it arranged by that Fairy Gu, or by the rumored Pill Sect?"

"Neither. Calm Mountain Sea was personally chosen by our ancestor for our Zhou family. Trust the old ancestor!"

Mentioning the only Sage of the Zhou family in a thousand years, the four fell into a difficult silence.

Regarding the deal between their ancestor Zhou Yunshen and Luo Chen of the Pill Sect, Gu Caiyi had already told them when she quietly visited the Zhou family.

That was why the Zhou family could, in such a short time, recall clansmen from all over, take all their cultivators aboard, and flee the Tiannan Continent together.

But in their view, wasn't it also because of their own inadequacy that their old ancestor had to plan ahead even before his passing?

The family head, Zhou Junxiao, sighed. "If only eldest brother hadn't failed in his breakthrough to Sage and died, we wouldn't be without a strong one to rely on."

Elder Zhou Shuzhen still had doubts. "But don't we still have Fu You? He's become the new generation's leading figure of the Tiannan Dao Palace. I heard he even took first place in the Dao Sect's Sexagenary Grand Competition. With him there, our Zhou family's future within the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect is bound to be prosperous. Now we're not only defying the Dao Palace's orders by leaving without permission, but we're also abandoning Fu You."

Speaking of Zhou Fu You, Zhou Junxiao's eyes showed deep regret.

He too had once thought that child was the Zhou family's hope for the future.

But after truly learning the inside story, he realized that child was both hope and a source of calamity.

Only by leaving Tiannan could the Zhou family have hope.

Staying in Tiannan, Zhou Fu You would bring utter destruction upon the Zhou family!

Seeing him silent, Zhou Shuzhen couldn't help but speak again. "Even if we ignore Zhou Fu You, what about those dozen or so excellent clansmen still cultivating in the Tiannan Dao Palace? Are we just abandoning them?"

Zhou Chongxiao beside her also said with great worry, "We've violated the Tiannan Dao Palace's prohibition. With the entire continent's formation activated, we left rashly. Those clansmen will definitely be purged afterward."

Zhou Junxiao pressed his lips together, his face resolute.

"That is their fate!"

Just as the four Zhou family Adepts were conversing, a crisp, melodious voice suddenly came from outside.

"Where are you going?"

The voice was pleasant to hear at first, like a spring breeze, but the questioning tone within it was filled with a chilling meaning.

The faces of the four Zhou family Adepts changed color simultaneously.

A Dao Sect Sage had arrived!

On the flying boat's deck, the Zhou family clansmen were in chaos, looking at the green-clad woman who had caught up and was hovering in the sky, their eyes filled with awe and fear.

Under the pressure of heavenly might, the flying boat, which had been moving swiftly, was forcibly halted.

Fu Qinglan looked at these panicked low-level cultivators, her delicate brows slightly furrowed.

How could these people alone dare to violate the prohibition?

Behind the Zhou family's relocation of their entire clan, there must be someone pulling the strings.

Just then!

*Clang!*

The sound of a sharp blade tearing through cloth rang out sharply.

The Sage domain that had been suppressing the flying boat revealed a flaw. The flying boat started up again and fled into the distance.

Fu Qinglan did not give chase, because someone had blocked her path.

It was a woman, mature and voluptuous, with a slender figure. Her bright and generous temperament carried a hint of a seeker's firmness.

Fu Qinglan had never seen this person before, but for some reason, the other woman gave her a familiar feeling.

"Who are you?"

"Who I am is not important. What's important is that the Central Continent is about to reunite, yet Fairy Qinglan is not stationed in the Tiannan Continent maintaining the formation. If something goes wrong, can you bear the responsibility?"

Fu Qinglan's expression changed slightly. The formations on the three continents were all activated simultaneously now, so naturally, it couldn't be hidden.

But the matter of the Central Continent's reunion was not something that could be seen on the surface.

How did this woman know about it, and why did she choose this time to take away the Zhou family clansmen?

Fu Qinglan's face turned cold.

"No matter who you are, the Zhou family is my disciple's family. I cannot allow you to put him in an unfilial position!"

As she spoke, her Sage domain suddenly pressed down on the other woman.

The woman, intending to test her strength, also released her own Sage domain.

But with just one collision, it crumbled and retreated in defeat.

If not for the sword qi swirling around her, protecting the small area around her, she probably wouldn't even be able to grasp the free-floating spiritual energy of heaven and earth.

She was ultimately still too newly formed a Sage, no match for the other.

"Is this the Dao Sect's prodigy, the first place winner of the Sexagenary Grotto-Heaven Grand Competition?"

The woman praised, then raised her hand and lifted it. A formation diagram floated lightly into the sky.

Fu Qinglan's expression remained unchanged. "Formation cultivator?"

The next moment!

In her line of sight, five firefly-like lights shot out, and countless sword qi erupted, instantly forming a vast and boundless storm of sword qi.

A massive sword formation had taken shape!

Fu Qinglan's expression changed slightly. "So it's a sword cultivator!"

She made a finger gesture like picking a flower, and a verdant green leaf appeared before her, tightly wrapping around her body.

This was her lifebound magic treasure, which she had refined for centuries. It had already been elevated to the true artifact level. Its defensive power might not be the strongest, but it was enough to block some ordinary sword formations.

However, when the countless sword qi transformed into threads and densely entangled and sliced over, fine sword marks began to appear on the verdant green leaf.

Fu Qinglan's face changed dramatically. "A sword formation composed of five true artifact-level sword pellets!"

With this thought, she dared not hold back any longer.

If she remained this passive, once the sword formation was fully formed, even she would be injured.

With a thought, Fu Qinglan formed hand seals and chanted a spell. The mana within her body circulated, and the origin qi outside her body surged.

She did not look at those sword pellets that were shooting endlessly and crisscrossing back and forth.

With her experience, she could naturally see that this woman's cultivation level was insufficient, her mana was particularly weak, and it was impossible for her to meticulously control each sword pellet.

What truly made this great formation operate was that formation diagram!

Fu Qinglan suddenly looked up, her eyes fierce, and struck out with a palm.

Divine Art—Great Heaven Creation Seal!

The verdant handprint pushed aside layers of sword qi, snapped countless sword threads, and advanced forward without any obstruction.

Only a loud explosion was heard in the air.

*Boom!*

The formation diagram flipped over, cracks appearing on it.

The five sword pellets scattered chaotically in all directions, and the great formation that had been maintained with all its might collapsed in an instant, revealing the stunned expression on the bright woman's face. She blurted out:

"Impossible! How could the Great Five Elements Sword Formation bestowed by my husband be broken?"

But then she came to her senses. In the end, her cultivation level was insufficient, and she couldn't skillfully control this sword formation. Moreover, the enemy she faced was no ordinary Sage.

The power of that palm strike was definitely at the level of a single strike from a Great Cultivator.

However, the time she had bought for the Zhou family was enough.

Since that was the case, it was time to retreat!

The formation diagram rolled up and protected the woman's side. The five sword pellets merged into one, turning into a rainbow of sword light that wrapped around both the woman and the diagram, then shot into the distance.

Before leaving, the woman curiously looked back.

That Dao Sect prodigy was standing there in a daze, actually not giving chase.

"How strange?"

In the sky, a rainbow of sword qi broke through the air and disappeared.

The green-clad woman stood there in a daze, looking at the sword mark on her own palm.

From her mouth came a faint, murmuring voice.

"This formation diagram style..."

"This mana aura..."

"No mistake, it's his handiwork!"

Then, an uncontrollable unease surged in Fu Qinglan's heart.

"Why did he send someone to take away the Zhou family clansmen?"

"And the person he sent called him husband. No wonder I felt that woman was somewhat familiar. When I was looking for him in the Eastern Wilderness back then, I saw a portrait of that woman. It's Gu Caiyi!"

"Sending such a close confidant to carry out this task, why didn't he come himself?"

"Why was the Zhou family willing to leave with Gu Caiyi? They had already taken root in the Tiannan Continent, and they had someone like Zhou Fu You, who could be seen as the contemporary leading figure of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, to rely on. Why go far and seek the near, going to the Eastern Wilderness to rely on him?"

Too many questions arose after breaking that formation diagram refined by a certain someone.

Too much unease evolved in the end into an unspeakable fear.

Why do this?

Why at this particular time?

The Zhou family, Zhou Fu You, the Burial God Valley, Black King attacking Zhenhai City...

When the distant rainbow of sword qi streaked across the sky and disappeared, leaving only a long empty trail in the sky, the green-clad woman finally snapped out of her unspeakable fear.

"Luo Chen!"

Without hesitation, Fu Qinglan turned and left, heading straight for the Tiannan Continent.

Her speed was extremely fast, the fastest she had ever been in her life.

Even after entering the inland, she did not stop at all, completely disregarding the task of patrolling the four directions and guarding the formation, going directly to the Tiannan Dao Palace.

Along the way, she even frequently used short-distance teleportation formations, ignoring the prohibitions.

Finally, half a day later, she returned to the Tiannan Dao Palace.

Upon arrival, without waiting for the Palace Master Zhan Bingtao to question her, she blurted out: "Someone is going to harm the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect! We must immediately reinforce the Dao Sect's ancestral court!"

Zhan Bingtao's expression changed abruptly.

The Mo family head, Mo Liheng, beside him, was also stunned.

Who in the world would dare... no, there was indeed someone who dared. Over a hundred years ago, someone had already killed their way to the Central Continent.

"Is Rosycloud making a comeback?"

Fu Qinglan took a deep breath. "No, it's Luo Chen!"

Upon hearing this, Mo Liheng instead breathed a sigh of relief. "So it's him!"

Zhan Bingtao smiled. "Junior Sister, although Luo Chen has the reputation of being the number one under the Ascendant, that was only because the Western Desert Buddhist Land had no experts stationed there, allowing him to make a great name for himself. Moreover, even if he truly has the strength to be invincible under the Ascendant, compared to our Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, he is nothing more than an ant. Where does he get the guts?"

Fu Qinglan shook her head, her face full of worry. "No, you don't understand him. Beneath his seemingly peaceful personality lies extreme madness. Once someone blocks his path, no matter who it is, he will eliminate them. Back then, in the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, Shi Ju was like that. Now, our Heavenly Origin Dao Sect is the same!"

Zhan Bingtao became a little more serious. "Junior Sister, isn't this a bit exaggerated?"

"Not at all exaggerated!" Fu Qinglan said firmly. "He has already made his move. Or rather, he started making his move a long time ago!"

Zhan Bingtao and Mo Liheng looked at each other, at a loss.

Next, Fu Qinglan laid out her discoveries over the years one by one. Linking them together, especially the fact that the Zhou family clansmen were taken away at this critical juncture, all corroborated her speculation.

"To retrieve his life soul, for the path of longevity, even if it means going against our Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, Luo Chen will never give up! In fact, based on my understanding of him, he will exhaust all his strength to achieve his goal!"

After hearing this explanation and even speculation, Zhan Bingtao's expression had become extremely grave.

"If there were signs back then, why didn't you speak up earlier?"

"I... I..." Fu Qinglan's pale lips trembled, her face full of shame.

Zhan Bingtao slammed the table, smashing the sturdy table into powder.

"Liheng, you guard the Tiannan Dao Palace!"

"Alright, leave it to me!"

"Qinglan, summon the three hall masters, and also notify the four families, Wen, Bu, Hu, and Di, of their Sages. We will immediately rush to the Dongyuan Continent!"

"Alright!"

Fu Qinglan responded heavily.

She had to stop Luo Chen. Compared to certain inexplicable feelings, the sect and even her family were more important to her.

However!

Just as Fu Qinglan's foot stepped out of the palace, the entire earth shook with a loud rumble.

The sudden tremor, at a time when her mind was already in chaos, caused her to lose control of her body for a moment, stumbling involuntarily.

Fu Qinglan grabbed the door pillar, her face full of confusion.

"It stopped?"

Under the pull of the Heaven-Gathering Continent Formation, the Tiannan Continent, which should have been continuously converging towards the Heaven-Earth Island, had stopped at this moment?

What exactly had happened on the Dongyuan Continent?

...

Time goes back slightly.

Outside the Dongyuan Continent, facing the direction of the Eastern Wilderness.

Luo Chen, clad in a black Daoist robe, stood in the sky, overlooking the vast sea.

A giant turtle pushed aside layers of waves, slowly emerging from the unfathomable ocean.

On the turtle's back, dozens of figures stood tall, each exuding a powerful aura.

Seeing this, Luo Chen smiled slightly.

"You've come."

The crowd looked solemn and spoke in unison: "Greetings, Pill Sect!"

Luo Chen nodded. "Good. Since you're here, let the battle begin! I will go first. You all will charge in afterward, kill the enemies, and break the formation!"

With that, he turned to face the direction of the Dongyuan Continent.

His ten fingers nimbly flew, forming hand seals and chanting spells.

Mana circulated throughout his body, and his primordial spirit resonated with the Dao of heaven and earth.

One hand formed the Nirvana Seal, the other the Spirit-Enslaving Seal. The two seals combined into one—his signature technique, the Azure Yang Great Handprint!

After brewing for a long time!

As he slowly pushed his right palm forward, a tiny handprint appeared in the sky.

With a light shout from Luo Chen.

"Go!"

This handprint flew slowly towards the Dongyuan Continent. Along the way, it continuously absorbed the vast origin qi of heaven and earth. The handprint grew larger and larger, its speed increasing.

By the end, wherever the handprint passed, huge waves rolled, the sea parted, revealing a thousand-zhang deep seabed.

The ocean could not block its advance, and the earth was even more unimpeded.

The massive Azure Yang handprint advanced all the way, pushing horizontally for thousands and tens of thousands of li.

Along the way, it destroyed countless formations and countless mountains. Many experts of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect appeared, but under this terrifying palm, they did not even have the courage to be a mantis trying to stop a chariot. They could only watch helplessly as that huge Azure Yang handprint pushed from east to west, all the way.

Until it reached the Dao Sect's ancestral court, the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range!

Several figures had already been notified and were on high alert, fully activating the mountain-protecting grand formation.

"Everyone be careful!"

"That cyan handprint, after continuously absorbing the origin qi of heaven and earth, its power has reached the level of a full-force strike from an Ascendant powerhouse. Do not take it head-on."

"Activate the mountain-protecting grand formation to block it, then guard the formation eye of the Heaven-Gathering Continent Formation!"

Amidst a series of shouts, a brilliant light curtain rose continuously from the Rotten Axe-Handle mountains, turning into layer after layer of city walls blocking the path of the Azure Yang Great Handprint.

Faced with all this, the Azure Yang Great Handprint, which could no longer be controlled after being released, paid no heed and simply charged forward.

*Boom!*

*Boom!*

*Boom!*

Layer after layer of light curtains shattered continuously.

The huge handprint also gradually shrank amidst the constant impacts.

When it finally reached the Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak of the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range, it was only the insignificant size of a normal person's palm.

*Smack!*

This tiny handprint imprinted itself on Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak, leaving only a shallow mark.

Seeing this, the many cultivators of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect finally breathed a sigh of relief.

They had blocked it!

But the next moment.

A sound of cracking, inch by inch, eerily spread to everyone's ears.

Then, an ear-splitting roar, as if from a demon howling in hell, exploded.

"*Roar!*"

Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak, which had stood in the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range for millions of years, collapsed with a loud crash before the disbelieving eyes of countless people.

A behemoth, a full thousand zhang tall, wreathed in countless ghostly qi, slowly stood up.

It was like an ox without horns, standing on one leg.

Its entire body was mottled with sword scars.

Its huge eyes were filled with the desire for violence and destruction.

It looked at the countless stunned Dao Sect cultivators, simply opened its mouth, and let out a booming sound.

"*Roar!*"

One figure after another fell like rain under this roar.

"Everyone, follow me to kill this ghostly god! Repair the formation eye with all your might!"

The late-stage Great Cultivator stationed here, his eyes cracking with rage, let out a furious roar.

And far away, on the coast.

Facing a crowd of awestruck gazes, Luo Chen's expression remained unchanged.

"The rest is up to you!"

His figure gradually disappeared into the void. A teleportation formation had long been prepared here. When he reappeared, he was already outside the Heaven-Earth Island.

After he left, the group of Eastern Wilderness experts looked at each other, glanced around, and finally turned their gazes towards that continent, now filled with the smoke of war and in turmoil.

Wang Yuan took the lead, his voice heavy.

"Kill!"

Then, the group of cultivators, like a swarm of locusts, charged into the Dongyuan Continent in a sky-darkening tide.

Majestic fleeing lights crisscrossed, vast domains unfolded, and powerful mana began to be unleashed upon this land, once the most prosperous in the Mountains and Seas Realm.

(End of this chapter)
