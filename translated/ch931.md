# Chapter 931: Kindred Spirit, Madness at the End

The Grand Five Elements Sword Formation!

The formation diagram originated from Han Zhan's improved Yin-Yang Five Elements Sword Formation. It was not necessary to be constrained by puppets holding swords; sword pellets could be used instead.

The sword pellets for the formation had been gathered from three sources: Feiyunzi of the Northern Sea, Pavilion Master Li of the Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion, and Lin Qingxuan of the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect.

Unfortunately, because the sword pellets were of varying grades, the formation always had flaws.

In previous battles, when Luo Chen deployed this sword formation, he could never achieve complete success.

It was not until recently, when Canglong underwent his tribulation, that Luo Chen used the power of heavenly thunder to refine three of the sword pellets to the true artifact level in one go. Only then did the perfect sword formation conceived by Han Zhan fully take shape.

And the first battle for this fully-formed sword formation was against the enemy Luo Chen had longed to face for years!

Swoosh! Swoosh! Swoosh! Swoosh! Swoosh!

Five sword pellets, like swimming fish, dove into the void.

The next moment, sword light blazed!

Centered on the enemy, Sage Divine Origin, countless sword threads burst forth, spreading in all directions like a weaving loom.

The sword threads changed in a thousand ways, crisscrossing as they twisted and killed toward Sage Divine Origin.

That crystalline flying sword was a true artifact that Sage Divine Origin had painstakingly refined for centuries. Its killing power was in no way inferior to his lifebound Five-Colored Treasure Pagoda.

Yet within this Grand Five Elements Sword Formation, it seemed to have lost its direction.

No matter how the flying sword charged left and right, severing countless sword threads, the threads were inexhaustible, showing no sign of decreasing.

Chi! Chi! Chi!

As the sword threads touched his body, deep marks appeared on Divine Origin's protective robe.

The hat covering his head was directly cut open, revealing his strange and contradictory face.

"So sharp?"

Sage Divine Origin's pupils contracted. He had never imagined the sword formation's power could be so keen.

What he did not know was that although the formation originated from Han Zhan, its upper limit had completely surpassed Han Zhan's conception. Because Luo Chen had integrated that piece of top-grade Nine Yang Geng Metal into it. The sharpness of the sword threads formed by the sword pellets was rarely matched in the world, except by a few treasures.

Luo Chen's ten fingers moved continuously, forming sword seals rapidly. The complete Grand Five Elements Sword Formation, now deployed, gave him a very smooth feeling.

Finally, there were no more flaws!

He no longer had to worry about the enemy finding a weakness.

Since the old thief Divine Origin had entered the sword formation, he could just wait to die!

However, when he saw the face under the other's hat, Luo Chen paused involuntarily.

"It changed!"

"That's not the face!"

He remembered very clearly. When they had fought last time, the face under the hat belonged to another person. According to them, it looked very much like the deceased Sage Linmu.

But now, this face gave Luo Chen the feeling of... the old ghost Motian?

"You're distracted!"

In the sinister laughter, the crystalline flying sword transformed into a rainbow light, wrapping around Sage Divine Origin as it directly broke through layers of sword thread entanglement, attempting to escape the formation.

Luo Chen frowned, suppressing his doubts. The sword seal in his fingertips suddenly changed, and he let out a low shout.

"Slash!"

The next moment, countless sword threads converged into one, forming a giant golden light sword.

As Luo Chen's sword finger shot forward, the light sword chased and slashed down.

Facing this sword strike, Divine Origin's eyes nearly split apart. Instead of retreating, he advanced, flicking his finger.

"Go!"

The crystalline flying sword wrapped around him detached from his body, using its tiny sword body to resist the giant golden sword.

Clang!

The clanging sound of swords rang out in all directions.

The instant collision unleashed boundless power.

In this collision, the Grand Five Elements Sword Formation was forcibly smashed open, breaking itself.

The five sword pellets spun and flew back. Luo Chen grabbed them in one hand, not dissatisfied that the formation had been broken.

Because the enemy's loss was very satisfying to him.

Crack, crack, crack...

A breaking sound was heard faintly.

Divine Origin held the sword hilt, his face flushed red.

The next moment.

Pfft!

He spat out a mouthful of blood. The crystalline flying sword, connected to his heart and mind, had broken into pieces.

This flying sword was no ordinary item.

It had been forged by the most supreme master weapon smiths using countless precious materials.

Then he had painstakingly nurtured it for centuries. Even during the great battle at Green Parasol Mountain, it had not been destroyed.

But now, it had been destroyed by Luo Chen's sword formation attack.

"Bastard!"

Ignoring the severe pain in his soul, Divine Origin was furious. He pointed a finger at Luo Chen.

A hazy green light suddenly burst forth!

This move again?

Luo Chen remembered this move. Last time, the other party had used it to fight his Withering-Bloom Dao Finger to a standstill.

At that time, he thought it was a divine ability technique.

But now, it seemed similar yet different, more like...

"I see!"

Luo Chen was thoughtful. He raised both hands, and a fire phoenix soared from behind him, carrying unmatched momentum. With a sharp screech, it pounced toward the hazy green light.

The two colors, green and red, collided in the void.

The hazy halo continuously invaded the other side, also continuously expanding. Heaven and earth trembled violently. The vast interior of the Divine Sect was repeatedly affected by the fierce aftermath.

Luo Chen watched the collision of the two great spells, and his guess was completely confirmed.

When he had comprehended the origin of fire, he discovered that spells of the corresponding attribute had also been enhanced.

At the time, he thought it was because the true meaning of the law had been integrated into them.

Only later did he realize that was not the case.

Spells in that state were higher than ordinary spells, yet slightly lower than great divine ability techniques.

It was just that the origin qi of heaven and earth moved with them, causing ordinary spells to be elevated, their power becoming more magnificent.

True divine abilities were those that could mutually integrate and verify with the true meaning of the law.

Clearly, the old thief Divine Origin had not comprehended the true meaning of the law. He had merely fused ordinary spells with the origin qi of heaven and earth, thus creating a qualitative change.

The reason he had been able to withstand Luo Chen's Withering-Bloom Dao Finger back then was simply that he had the advantage of higher cultivation and deeper mana.

"If that's the case..."

Luo Chen pursed his lips. Gazing at the furious Sage Divine Origin in the distance, he instantly cast a spell.

"Since you gave me one, it's only polite to return the favor. Take this too!"

The next moment!

A dragon's roar sounded from the nine heavens. A huge dragon head suddenly appeared, roaring as it charged toward Sage Divine Origin.

Wherever it passed, the void trembled, and a massive amount of origin qi of heaven and earth continuously poured into it.

Seeing this, Divine Origin's expression changed slightly, and his body began to move quickly.

But no matter how he flew, that flame dragon still locked onto him firmly, never missing.

A spell attack with divine sense lock!

In just a couple of breaths, Divine Origin realized the essence of this move.

He stopped dodging, stood still, reached down to grab, and lifted.

Then, the earth surged.

Countless yellow earth flew up, forming two large hands in the void that shielded him.

Roar!

The flame dragon crashed into the two yellow earth hands without hesitation, then dissipated bit by bit, returning to pure spiritual energy between heaven and earth.

Swoosh!

Luo Chen followed closely, determined not to let the other escape his attack range.

But seeing this scene, his brow furrowed again.

Instant-cast high-level spell?

In three hundred years of cultivation, only he himself could achieve such a level!

Even with the proficiency panel, he had to practice spells day and night to increase their proficiency, only then could he instantly cast various powerful spells with a flick of his fingers.

But in just a few exchanges with Sage Divine Origin, he found that the other's spell mastery was in no way inferior to his own!

Whether it was the green light finger or this earth-attribute defensive spell, from beginning to end, he had not seen the other form many hand seals. Like himself, he could cast them instantly with a single thought.

"According to legend, the Five Elements Divine Sect relied on Divine Origin alone to reach the level of a top-tier major power in the Eastern Wilderness."

"Countless high-level spells that came from within the Five Elements Divine Sect were, in all likelihood, created and developed by Sage Divine Origin himself."

"In the past, I thought it was an exaggeration. But now, it seems the reputation is well-deserved."

"Just the speed of his spellcasting alone shows how profound his mastery is in this path!"

As these thoughts flashed through his mind, Luo Chen could not help but feel a sense of mutual appreciation.

Spells contained the basic understanding of the Dao. Often, the creation and use of a spell required extremely proficient mastery of the corresponding basic components.

Compared to spells that were slow to release and consumed a lot of mana, normal cultivators preferred to rely on magic treasures that took effect quickly and consumed less mana.

Even cultivators who had advanced to the Sage stage would, due to past habits, not pay too much attention to spell cultivation.

Along this journey, Luo Chen had experienced countless battles.

But the vast majority were collisions between magic treasures.

Battles like this one, where spells were frequent and each side displayed their exquisite skills, were extremely rare.

Not only did Sage Divine Origin share the same five-spirit-root constitution as Luo Chen, but even his cultivation direction and battle mode were so similar.

How could Luo Chen not appreciate him?

However!

After a brief moment of mutual appreciation came overwhelming killing intent!

Such a person deserved to die even more!

Luo Chen hesitated no longer. He opened his mouth and spat out, unleashing his ultimate move.

Whoosh!

Amidst the fierce wind, a small green-white tree suddenly appeared in the void.

Its roots plunged deep into the void, spreading to unknown places.

A massive amount of origin qi of heaven and earth poured into the green-white tree, along with Luo Chen's countless reserves of dense mana.

The Withering-Bloom Divine Fire swayed its branches, wantonly revealing its true form.

Infinite scorching heat burned the void.

Sage Divine Origin, trapped within, even began to have his physical body melt.

He continuously cast spells, but whether they were earth-attribute defensive spells or water-attribute defensive spells, none were effective.

Fifth-grade divine fire had already surpassed the resistance range of Sage cultivators.

Even the powerful Great Demon Emperor Huang Man had been helpless against this move.

"Divine Origin, farewell!"

Luo Chen controlled his mana output, continuously pouring Gold Wind Jade Dew spiritual wine into his mouth to replenish the rapidly depleting mana.

Even though he had broken through to Sage sixth layer, even though he had cultivated the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture*, and even comprehended the origin of fire, relying on the Sage realm to activate the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire was still extremely strenuous.

Being able to make the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire reveal its true form was already the limit.

He believed that under this move, Sage Divine Origin would surely die.

However!

The battle's change once again exceeded Luo Chen's expectations.

Gurgle!

Gurgle!

In the hazy, illusory flames, Sage Divine Origin's withered hand held a glazed jade pure vase.

A stream of clear spring was continuously emerging from the bottle's mouth.

It formed a large bubble, wrapping him inside.

No matter how the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire burned, it could not get within three feet of him.

Luo Chen's gaze froze, and he said hoarsely, "What is that?"

Divine Origin seemed somewhat strained in activating this spring water, but he was especially pleased with Luo Chen's shock.

"There are rare treasures in the world. Fire has the Sourceless Fire, water has the Rootless Water."

"This rootless water of mine is called the Inexhaustible Spring. It is ever-growing, endlessly flowing. Obtained from the wild lands, after many years of cultivation, it has this power."

"Luo Chen, I know the power of your divine fire. Even Lei Daozi, Huang Man, and others were helpless against it. But since I dared to fight you, how could I come unprepared?"

"How long can your mana sustain the activation of this divine fire?"

As he spoke, the bubble even began to expand, slowly pushing back the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire.

Luo Chen gritted his teeth and continued to increase his mana output.

The Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit also seemed somewhat exasperated. It was actually being blocked by a puddle of spring water!

In the past, it was capable of burning the heavens and boiling the seas!

With Luo Chen's mana support and the mad infusion of origin qi of heaven and earth, the green-white tree's branches swayed even more violently.

Pressing the bubble to shrink continuously.

Seeing that Luo Chen's entire mind was focused on controlling the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire, Sage Divine Origin's eyes flashed with a gleam.

Now!

With one hand holding the bottle and the other forming hand seals, he slowly closed both eyes.

Hum...

A strange power suddenly appeared.

Without anyone noticing, a transparent attack struck Luo Chen.

Luo Chen's whole body trembled. His eyes unconsciously closed, and even the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire wilted by three points.

Seeing this, Divine Origin was overjoyed.

Ever since Luo Chen had fought the three great Demon Emperors right under his nose and personally killed Huang Man, he knew that this man's strength was completely on par with a Great Cultivator.

Ever since Luo Chen had returned safely from Green Parasol Mountain and even joined the Nether Origin Sect, he knew that this child had soared to the heavens and was no longer someone he could covet.

Especially, although the other's life experience showed a gentlemanly style, he also repaid grievances with grievances.

He himself had targeted the Luo Tian Sect so much and even plotted against the other. How could things go smoothly?

This battle between them was inevitable!

For this, he had prepared many things.

The Inexhaustible Spring was to counter the fifth-grade divine fire.

And the soul technique just now was aimed at Luo Chen's short cultivation time, his soul foundation surely not as strong as his own.

But the other still had an extremely prominent strong point that he had to deal with!

"It's just a pity for this extremely precious physical body."

Murmuring, Divine Origin slapped the glazed jade pure vase with one palm.

The next moment, the clear spring flowed, gurgling out.

Endless spring water immediately broke through the encirclement of the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire.

Not only that, the Five-Colored Treasure Pagoda, which had been locked in combat with the Primordial Unity Cauldron, also flew back with a spin, spitting out a large swath of yellow light.

Divine Origin grabbed with both hands in the void. The crystalline flying sword fragments that had been shattered by the Grand Five Elements Sword Formation continuously converged toward him, turning into streams of golden light.

He puffed out his cheeks and spat out a mouthful of pure Sage True Flame, formed from mana. It intertwined and rotated with the golden light, the yellow light, and even the green light of the Inexhaustible Spring.

"Gold Flame Nine Turns, Xirang Fills the Sea!"

Spells in the world are divided into many types and attributes.

The basic ones are merely for dusting off dust and cleansing the body. The powerful ones can destroy heaven and earth, known as divine abilities.

And in between, there is a relatively special type of spell.

Its name is the combination spell!

For example, Luo Chen's Mountain Collapse technique was a combination of fire and earth systems. The Mirror Flower Water Moon was actually a combination of water and wood systems.

For the Five Elements Divine Sect, which specialized in the path of spells, how could they not have similar spells?

The Great Handprint of Primordial Qi was just the simplest arrangement and combination, not considered profound.

Back then, on the battle platform, the Four Symbols Boundary deployed by the Five Elements Divine Sect's Adept Great Cultivator Yin Sixiang was the true pinnacle!

However, limited by cultivation, Yin Sixiang still needed many external aids to deploy the Four Symbols Boundary, and it was very slow.

But Sage Divine Origin did not need that!

His move, Gold Flame Nine Turns, Xirang Fills the Sea, whether in terms of subtlety or power, was in no way inferior to the divine ability techniques created by those Ascendant great powers.

The only drawback was probably that it had not integrated the true meaning of the law!

But in this world, how could anyone simultaneously master the true meanings of the four laws of gold, fire, water, and earth, and even fuse them together?

"Killing Luo Chen is enough!"

Divine Origin slowly and with difficulty unleashed this spell.

Instantly, a great river of flame flowed between heaven and earth, washing away everything in its path.

Without Luo Chen's control, the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire could barely withstand its power and could only retreat to protect its master.

But in the continuous washing, without Luo Chen's mana support, the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire grew increasingly wilted.

"Master, wake up quickly!"

Pressed to the limit, the ignorant Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit actually sent out a warning with a similar meaning.

And to this...

Swish!

Luo Chen suddenly opened his eyes. In his right hand, a blood sword suddenly appeared.

He slashed down!

The blood sword traversed the long sky, cutting open the great river of flame, going upstream, and touching Divine Origin's body.

Just one sword!

Under the terrified gazes of countless Five Elements Divine Sect disciples, that aged body was directly split in two.

Even the huge, mixed Sage embryo was dumbfoundedly cleaved in half.

Luo Chen withdrew the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire and stood holding the sword.

As the fierce wind stirred, Luo Chen's face was cold and stern: "What gave you the illusion that my soul foundation is inferior to yours?"

Within his sea of consciousness, three divided souls each stood in their own domain, the Zhenyu Soul Technique always on guard.

Let alone one soul technique from Sage Divine Origin.

Even if three or five Great Cultivators launched soul attacks together, Luo Chen would not be afraid in the slightest.

His earlier stillness was merely feigning weakness to find a flaw when the other unleashed his ultimate move, then strike with one sword.

This old thing had lived for nearly a thousand years. He had too many trump cards, too many methods. If they continued to fight, who knew what strange things he might still have.

Only a single fatal blow could end this grudge.

The aged body fell to the earth, splashing up patches of dust.

The Sage embryo, cleaved in two, floated stupidly in the air, seemingly unaware of what had happened.

How could the other be completely unaffected by the soul technique?

How was his most proud spell instantly broken?

What exactly was that sword?

Was he going to stop here?

It shouldn't be!

It shouldn't be!

"It shouldn't be!!!"

Luo Chen watched the little Sage figure continuously dissipate and disintegrate in the void, and his thoughts gradually became clear.

Ignoring the desperate, unwilling voice, ignoring the countless resentful and hateful gazes below, he recalled the Primordial Unity Cauldron, turned, and prepared to leave.

Suddenly!

The wind stopped! The clouds did not move!

Luo Chen whirled around, staring fixedly at the Sage embryo that had been cleaved in two.

In his gaze, the Sage embryo actually fused together. Not only that, its body began to expand rapidly, and a sea of origin qi of heaven and earth poured into it.

Even!

"My disciples and grandchildren, with a great enemy before you, why are you unmoved?"

"Are you afraid? Or do you not recognize me as your Supreme Elder?"

"If so, then return your power to me!"

"Let me, destroy this thief!"

The huge Sage embryo spanned the void. Countless spiritual energy formed streams converging into it.

As his eyes opened and closed, and his lips moved, the protective sect formation that had originally enveloped the Five Elements Divine Sect in an attempt to trap Luo Chen also trembled.

Beams of light scattered, sweeping over one low-level disciple after another.

Every time one was swept, a life was extinguished.

Then their mana, blood qi, and even souls followed the beams of light into that Sage embryo.

"Senior Brother, no!"

The Divine Fire Sect Master cried out in anguish.

But Divine Origin paid him no heed. He continued on his own way.

The huge Sage embryo body gradually expanded, even giving Luo Chen a familiar feeling.

"Primordial spirit?"

Back then, on Heaven-Earth Peak, before the Alchemy Saint Chu Yan ascended, he had once manifested his primordial spirit.

That was no longer an infant body, but a huge soul giant that could cover heaven and earth.

He knew that was the hallmark of an Ascendant great power. The transformation of the Sage embryo into the primordial spirit was called the primordial spirit!

At this moment, Sage Divine Origin was clearly walking this path in his death throes.

Luo Chen frowned. Holding the Yuantu Sword, he slashed out.

Swish!

The sword light was fierce. With one sword, the illusory, huge primordial spirit was split in two.

But immediately, with the massive infusion of origin qi of heaven and earth and the countless disciples of the Five Elements Divine Sect, it quickly fused again.

Luo Chen was stunned. His Yuantu Blood Sword had successively killed two Ascendant great powers, Blood Sea Ancestor and Black Marsh Ancestor. It was a top-tier true artifact, only one step away from a spirit treasure.

How could it be ineffective against Divine Origin's primordial spirit?

"Could it be..."

Luo Chen's mind moved. His red-gold pupils reappeared, looking into the void.

Threads of spiritual mechanism were merging into Divine Origin's body along with the sea of heaven and earth spiritual energy.

Assimilation!

Sure enough, although Divine Origin appeared to be charging into the Ascendant realm at this moment, he was actually in the process of being rapidly assimilated by heaven and earth.

At this moment, he was this realm of heaven and earth!

Although the Yuantu Sword could kill Ascendants, it was far from reaching the level where it could cut through a realm of heaven and earth.

Luo Chen began to retreat.

The same went for the few Sage cultivators of the Five Elements Divine Sect.

"You all flee quickly! Senior Brother has gone mad!"

"As long as you live, the Divine Sect still has hope!"

The Divine Fire Sect Master cried out in anguish.

Qi Emei and the other two looked at each other in terror, retreating in panic, not even caring about Luo Chen who was also retreating.

To this.

The terrifying primordial spirit, spanning the void and having expanded to a thousand zhang in size, let out a low laugh.

"You can't escape."

"Ever since you cultivated the techniques I gave you, you have never been able to escape for your entire lives."

"Come back, all come back!"

With his low laughter, like a great bell, Qi Emei and the other two Sages were horrified to find that their mana was out of control, and they could not even escape their Sage embryos from their bodies. They were forced to fly toward the huge primordial spirit.

"It's the Nascent Soul Absorption Art!"

The Divine Fire Sect Master knelt on the ground, instantly understanding everything.

"Senior Brother, you left a hidden hand in it!"

Sage Divine Origin paid him no heed at all. The terrifying suction force was also not very effective against this junior brother, the Divine Fire Sect Master. It only worked on low-level cultivators and the three, Qi Emei and the others, who had cultivated the *Nascent Soul Absorption Art*.

Amidst the three women's miserable pleas for mercy, Divine Origin completely devoured them.

Then, the thousand-zhang huge primordial spirit instantly locked onto the white-robed man.

"Luo Chen, it's your turn!"
