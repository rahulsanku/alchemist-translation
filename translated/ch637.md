# Chapter 637: Sitting Beside the Abyss, Luo Chen Is Lost

The great battle erupted at a touch!

The demon beasts of this place lived in a remote outer sea and had almost no chance to communicate with humans.

Even though they had reached third-tier and refined the transverse bone in their mouths, they still did not understand human speech.

Amid reckless roars, no one knew what they were saying.

Luo Chen and the others had even less desire to waste words with them.

What this battle needed was speed!

Resolve it quickly, and as much as possible, prevent the battle aftermath from spreading, thereby affecting the island's terrain and the Purple Monkey Flowers' future growing environment.

Therefore, when this beast tide, numbering over a thousand, came rolling over, Luo Chen, the three humans, and one jiaolong did not hesitate to strike ruthlessly.

Duan Li activated a jade ruler, transforming it into countless phantom images that encircled large groups of demon beasts. Every time the jade ruler fell, a great burst of blood mist exploded.

Sang Jiugong slapped the earth, and immediately over a hundred black vines broke out from the ground, directly binding three Purple Qi Poison Monkeys. Judging by this method, it had some similarities to Luo Chen's use of Vine Art to activate Thorn Poison Vines during Foundation Establishment, but in terms of power, it was far greater. Three early third-tier Demon Kings roared madly under the black vines' entanglement, but could not break free. Their physiques even began slowly tightening, nearly torn apart. Not only that, Sang Jiugong also took out a stone stele and pressed it to the ground, forcefully turning the earth into a muddy swamp. Countless low-level demon beasts sank within and could not extricate themselves.

On Lord Lushan's side, by comparison, things were much more free and easy. She controlled a blue flying sword, moving vertically and horizontally. Her left hand held a horsetail whisk, and though she did not seem to use it, as the white strands drifted, a killing intent was ready to burst out.

As for the blade lights in the field, those were Black King's methods controlling the two middle-grade magic treasures, Yin Slaughter and Yang Severance. However, very clearly, his magic treasure control methods were quite crude. In his hands, the two middle-grade magic treasures were not as powerful as those of Duan Li and the others. Later, Black King grew somewhat impatient, opened his bloody maw, and began using his lifebound talent to swallow low-level demon beasts in large mouthfuls.

And among these people, the most domineering was undoubtedly Luo Chen, who fought openly and forcefully.

He did not use the Broken Moon Wings, but his speed remained extremely fast.

Especially every time an explosive sound came from the void, he would shift dozens of zhang horizontally out of nowhere, land atop a third-tier Demon King's head, and smash down with a hammer.

Every strike meant a Demon King would either die or be injured.

Under his rapid movement, in only a short while, three or four Purple Qi Poison Monkeys had perished under the Flowing Fire Hammer.

"Screech!"

In the sky, a sharp bird cry rang out.

Luo Chen raised his head and looked coldly. This was the only avian demon beast among the many third-tier Demon Kings Duan Li had mentioned in his investigation.

Judging by its appearance, it should be a Golden-Crowned Flying Eagle, famous for boundless strength.

"Hmph!"

Luo Chen snorted coldly, and nine chains suddenly emerged from his body, flying toward the sky.

Seeing this, the Golden-Crowned Flying Eagle was unafraid, reaching out with giant claws to tear at those chains.

However, the nine mana chains rolled upward instead, wrapping around half its body.

"Get down here!"

Luo Chen shouted lowly, and surging giant strength abruptly flew. His own body also used the force to fly into the sky.

The Golden-Crowned Flying Eagle encountered giant force and further felt that the demon power in its body was not circulating smoothly. As it fell, it let out a panicked bird cry.

In its golden eyes, it suddenly saw a person leap over its head, then smash down with a hammer.

Boom!

In the sky, blood and flesh suddenly scattered, and sunset-colored feathers filled the air.

This sequence of movements ended extremely quickly, like a rabbit rising and a falcon falling.

From when everyone heard the eagle cry to Luo Chen attacking, only several breaths had passed.

When they looked again, only blood rain spilling from the sky remained.

Seeing this, Black King could not help drooling pitifully. Master, what a waste!

The other three still had strength to spare while fighting, so they also saw how swiftly and violently Luo Chen dealt with a troublesome third-tier flying bird. Each of their hearts tightened.

Duan Li: 'Brother Luo Hai's combat methods have advanced further and further, with quite some sense of returning to simplicity.'

Sang Jiugong: 'So these are Devil Lord Azure Yang's methods? Yet I still have not seen him use that rumored giant cauldron magic treasure, and he is already this formidable.'

Lord Lushan's eyes blinked, and her heart was also incomparably shocked.

'Did he not just fight a Golden Core Stage Eight great cultivator for an entire night on Seven Stars Island not long ago? Why is his mana still this abundant?'

In the sky, after Luo Chen finished dealing with the Golden-Crowned Flying Eagle, his gaze fell on the earth, taking in the entire battlefield.

His brows could not help furrowing.

Under the slaughter of four people and one demon, this demon beast group entrenched on Purple Spirit Island had no power to resist.

At this moment, it had already revealed signs of defeat.

The demon beasts' instinct made them flee in all directions.

One could imagine that once the demon beasts scattered, although solving them might become even easier, it would also more easily affect other places.

With a thought, Luo Chen called out loudly.

"Fellow Daoists, do not hold back. Deal with the demon beasts here as quickly as possible, and do not let them escape back to their nests!"

Once these words fell, everyone sped up their movements.

Duan Li intensified his mana output, while Lord Lushan swept horizontally with the horsetail whisk in her hand. Wherever the flying threads passed, demon beasts were immediately torn apart. Sang Jiugong struck the stone stele before him with a palm, and the stele immediately enlarged, then pushed and crushed forward with unstoppable force.

Black King was even simpler. His body swelled greatly, and he killed into the demon beast group. With a swing of the jiaolong tail, he swept away a large stretch.

Luo Chen was not idle either. He put away the Flowing Fire Hammer, stood in the air, and flicked all ten fingers repeatedly.

Fireballs fell like meteors, as if fire rain had begun.

Any being below third-tier, once his Fireball landed, transformed into a huge ball of fire and was burned to ash amid screams and wails.

As for those few remaining third-tier Demon Kings, Luo Chen did not let them go either.

Great Azure Yang Handprints came out again and again, and whatever they touched turned to meat paste.

There were exceptions!

Luo Chen's gaze paused, falling on a huge crocodile fleeing in panic.

"So this is that third-tier Abyss Crocodile? Its defensive ability is indeed outstanding. It can actually withstand my Great Azure Yang Handprint head-on."

With a thought, Luo Chen dove downward, heading straight for that giant crocodile comparable to a small mountain.

Sensing the enemy chasing it, the Abyss Crocodile was unwilling to sit and await death. Its savage tail swung out, shattering rocks in front of it like dried mud.

The crocodile tail swept horizontally, heading straight for Luo Chen.

"Hmph!"

Luo Chen snorted lightly, and his two palms suddenly swelled larger. In the critical instant, he forcibly grabbed the crocodile tail.

Both feet pierced into the earth, unmoving as a mountain.

With a low roar.

"Rise!"

Both hands exerted force. Under the Abyss Crocodile's terrified gaze, he actually lifted this giant crocodile horizontally into the sky, then smashed it down fiercely.

Boom!

The earth trembled, and the wilderness shook.

From the distance, Sang Jiugong gave a startled cry. "Daoist Azure Yang, be careful!"

This was fear that Luo Chen would use brute force and destroy this not-very-large Purple Spirit Island.

A fierce light flashed in Luo Chen's eyes. His figure shot violently to the top of the giant crocodile's head, and his huge palm slapped down.

Mountain-Opening Monument-Breaking Palm!

Domineering mana surged out.

The giant crocodile seemed to suffer a heavy blow and rolled endlessly on the ground.

Yet its tyrannical defense still preserved its life under this palm.

How was this a crocodile? It was clearly a turtle!

Luo Chen's eyes trembled. Mirror Flower, Water Moon immediately activated, and the giant crocodile stiffly lay on the ground.

Seizing the chance, Luo Chen slapped both palms repeatedly from the giant crocodile's head to its tail, a distance of several dozen zhang, one palm every three zhang.

One palm! Two palms! Three palms... until after nine palms.

Luo Chen's hands transformed into claws, grabbing the giant crocodile's sturdy scales and armor, and he shouted fiercely.

"Overlord Removes Armor!"

Sss!!!

The sound of bone and flesh separating rang out without cease.

The sight of bloody flesh was shocking.

Everyone looked over after hearing the sound, only to see on the earth, there was impressively a flesh crocodile without scales and armor, leaving only fresh red flesh, madly rolling around.

Rocks and mud fell onto the blurry flesh, making it even more savage and disgusting.

As for the red-robed Daoist, he merely stood there dragging an empty shell of armor.

Seeing the flesh crocodile roll, already about to affect an area where Purple Monkey Flowers grew luxuriantly, he casually struck out with a palm and killed it.

After finishing all this, Luo Chen panted lightly in small breaths, a slightly comfortable look on his face.

Lord Lushan was the first to resolve the demon beasts on her side. At this moment, she looked up and saw Luo Chen's comfortable expression, and could not help frowning.

Daoist Azure Yang was a little perverse!

Only Luo Chen himself knew why he had been so violent just now.

In the Withered Wood Ridge battle, Sage power had made him feel powerless.

In the Seven Stars Island battle, he had been ambushed, restricted everywhere, and beaten passively the entire time.

This made him, who had been smooth sailing ever since arriving in the Northern Sea, feel stifled to the extreme.

Therefore, he had vented his anger in this battle.

Using Voidburst Step's extreme speed and pure giant strength, he crushed these demon beasts bewitched by Purple Monkey Flowers, venting the anger in his heart.

Only now, after flaying the Abyss Crocodile's scales alive, had he vented it.

Catching Lord Lushan's displeased gaze, he casually threw down the Abyss Crocodile shell.

"Apologies. I lost my composure a little just now."

Lord Lushan smiled with difficulty and nodded without speaking.

It was loss of composure, but it also showed the domineering tyranny of the other party's methods.

If these methods were used on her, she feared she could not withstand even a few exchanges.

"Such a powerful person can only be befriended, not made an enemy!"

Luo Chen did not care what she was thinking now. He casually cast a cleansing spell, washing away the smell of blood on his body.

He looked around.

The battlefield situation had long since been settled.

Duan Li and Black King were chasing down small groups of demon beasts that had escaped.

Sang Jiugong was collecting the shattered demon beast corpses, gathering them into a pile. This was related to the ripening art he would later use.

Overall, this battle could be said to have been taken very easily.

It did not exceed Luo Chen's expectations. After all, every step had long since been calculated in his heart.

And next, things would be even simpler.

...

Three days later.

Duan Li and Lord Lushan took their leave from Luo Chen and left together.

On Purple Spirit Island, only Luo Chen and Sang Jiugong remained.

Black King roamed the nearby sea region, wantonly hunting low-level demon beasts in the sea and bringing the corpses back to Purple Spirit Island.

Inside the valley that resembled a giant sinkhole, Luo Chen and Sang Jiugong squatted on the ground and carefully plucked three Purple Monkey Flowers that looked like monkey brains.

"Adding the one on the cliff face, these four Purple Monkey Flowers that reached fourth-tier are now in my hands."

Luo Chen carefully stored them in jade boxes.

Afterward, he watched as Sang Jiugong buried shattered demon beast corpses beneath the sinkhole one after another, including the body of that third-tier Abyss Crocodile.

All valuable parts of these demon beasts had already been removed by Luo Chen.

Demon cores, scales and armor, wings.

Only blood, muscles, and bones he did not touch in the slightest.

Within his field of vision, after Sang Jiugong buried large batches of demon beast corpses, he covered them with great mounds of soft soil. Then, before Luo Chen, he took out a jade bottle and poured roughly a sea-bowl's worth of green liquid onto the soil.

After finishing all this, Sang Jiugong could not help letting out a breath.

"Purple Spirit Island's spirit vein grade is ordinary, and after being absorbed without restraint by four fourth-tier Purple Monkey Flowers, it long since lost the possibility of advancing. Now this can be regarded as making use of waste."

"Using vast amounts of living flesh and blood, plus my uniquely prepared Golden Mulberry Green Liquid, this place can become the most fertile growth environment for Purple Monkey Flowers within ten years."

After hearing him finish, Luo Chen revealed confusion.

"Ten years, is that enough?"

Sang Jiugong smiled faintly. "Naturally, it is not enough. If you want to ripen these third-tier Purple Monkey Flowers and let them save the strength of a thousand years of natural growth, how could it be this easy? Rest assured, I still have some auxiliary methods that can maximize the soil's fertility, enough to last for several dozen years!"

Luo Chen's eyes brightened. "Oh? Then Luo will wait and see!"

Sang Jiugong did not lie.

In the following days, he truly displayed many high-level spirit planter methods that Luo Chen had only heard of in rumors.

Taking boundless seawater, fusing Luo Chen's one million spirit stones, then using formations to arrange a circulating Great Cloud Rain Art, causing a spiritual rain to fall once every three months.

Heaven-reaching fire burned all of Purple Spirit Island. Countless ancient trees turned to ash and were piled inside the sinkhole. This was the Bing Fire Burning Wasteland Art, best at improving soil fertility.

Black King was even used as hard labor, diving deep into the sea to collect many shells for Sang Jiugong, then crushing them into powder with demon power and evenly scattering them inside the sinkhole.

All sorts of varied methods left Luo Chen somewhat dazzled.

They also made him deeply understand that any skill, once reaching profound levels, was not necessarily any simpler than alchemy or artifact-forging.

By the time the Purple Monkey Flower "growing base" in the sinkhole was cultivated, nearly half a year had already passed.

Only now did Sang Jiugong begin the official Purple Monkey Flower transplanting work.

He wanted to transplant all the Purple Monkey Flowers on the entire Purple Spirit Island over.

Using the strength of the entire group, he would feed the one hundred-plus third-tier Purple Monkey Flowers, allowing them to cross the time of a thousand years of natural growth and advance early to the fourth-tier level.

Apart from assisting Sang Jiugong at the very beginning by using origin true fire to cast the Bing Fire Burning Wasteland Art, Luo Chen did not interfere again.

Black King became hard labor, summoned and ordered around by Sang Jiugong.

For a time, Luo Chen instead became idle.

Calling it complete leisure would not be accurate either. He traveled around Purple Spirit Island, checking the terrain and arranging suitable island-protecting formations to prevent powerful demon beasts from breaking in and destroying the growth of the spirit medicines here in the days after he was gone.

During this process, Luo Chen began reflecting on the deficiencies in his current combat methods.

Although he was skilled in spells, and those low-level spells still had no small power when used by him.

As his realm grew, low-level spells ultimately developed limitations. Against cultivators of the same rank, or even enemies of higher realms, their threat greatly decreased.

Mountain Collapse and Blazing Sun Art, previously used as killing moves, even though constantly optimized, no longer had decisive one-hit-kill effects.

His signature method, Great Azure Yang Handprint, could still be used. But its advantage of wide range had become a flaw of large scope and insufficiently concentrated power before high-level enemies.

Unless it was an equally huge-bodied thing, when facing agile human cultivators or powerful defensive magic treasures, Great Azure Yang Handprint had very little effect.

Before a cold pool radiating chill, there were traces of a huge creature crawling. It should have been that Abyss Crocodile's dwelling place in life.

Rather than calling it a pool, it was better to call it an abyss.

Luo Chen planted a formation flag, then looked at the deep, bottomless pool-abyss and sank into thought.

"Third-tier spells have, in truth, already become somewhat useless to me."

It was not that third-tier spells were not strong. Under his diligent study and bitter practice, plus system deduction, Great Perfection third-tier spells could still erupt with powerful might.

This level of power was still rather effective against early Golden Core and even mid Golden Core.

But very clearly, Luo Chen had long since ceased to fear that kind of enemy much.

His current imagined enemies were late Golden Core great cultivators, and even possible Nascent Soul Sages!

In his calculations, fourth-tier spells might be able to become new trump cards.

But fourth-tier spells rarely circulated on the market. Only Nascent Soul Sages could grasp them.

Right now, he had mastered only two fourth-tier spells in total: the Dragon-Slaying Art and Origin Pill Art.

Yet neither of these two belonged to offensive spells. Even though the Dragon-Slaying Art had evolved the effect of severing divine souls, it was still limited within Luo Chen's sea of consciousness and could not be released outward.

Origin Pill Art, by contrast, was extraordinary. The Origin Pill Luo Chen refined provided him with a "second hidden energy source."

The reason he could stalemate with the Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft for an entire night on Seven Stars Island, then open slaughter on Purple Spirit Island, was that the Origin Pill within his body apart from his Golden Core played a major role.

His current depth of mana was completely no less than late Golden Core figures!

The Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft should not have lost that miserably in a contest of mana foundation, but the other party had to operate the Northern Dipper Seven Peaks Array while simultaneously controlling seven middle-grade magic treasure flying swords to arrange a sword array. How could that last long?

By contrast, after Luo Chen used many methods, Xuanchen Armor could absorb wandering earth-attribute spiritual qi and earth-vein qi.

So in the end, he could calmly defeat the other party and still have strength to spare.

"But these are ultimately foundations. They cannot directly show powerful killing force!"

Luo Chen bitterly discovered that on the spell path he was most proficient in, he had fallen into a bottleneck.

Even with his deep accumulation, he could create spells himself, but clearly, the spells created would inevitably not reach the fourth-tier level in grade and power.

It was not only spells, but also magic treasures!

He had many magic treasures in hand. Whether the Primordial Unity Cauldron, Broken Moon Wings, Treasure-Dropping Copper Coin, or Flowing Fire Hammer, they were all fine items.

He also had unusual magic treasures of marvelous power, such as the Rotten Axe-Handle Black Chess Pieces and the Dragon-Nurturing Basin.

But in the end, these magic treasures leaned more toward support and defense.

The Flowing Fire Hammer did have decent offensive power, but its speed was too slow. Throwing it out to smash people was not as fast as holding it in hand and flying over to smash with a hammer.

As his lifebound magic treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron had extraordinary potential, and in many respects, had wondrous effects.

Only in the path of attack was it somewhat lacking.

Luo Chen had long anticipated this situation. All-round development meant all-round mediocrity. The Primordial Unity Cauldron had deep foundation and certainly would not be mediocre, but at least in the short term, it could not become the sharp weapon in his hands for breaking through tough points.

In truth, this sort of predicament also appeared among most Golden Core cultivators.

As objects of dual cultivation of life and destiny, Golden Core cultivators valued their lifebound magic treasures' deep foundation and comprehensiveness more, causing them to be somewhat lacking in attack.

Therefore, after many Golden Core cultivators raised their realms and had surplus mana, they would do their utmost to grasp a second offensive magic treasure.

For example, Angler. Luo Chen did not know what his lifebound magic treasure was, but clearly, the combined set of magic treasures including the Mystic Gold Rod, Sound-Capturing Bell, and Heaven-Remnant Line was something he had specifically refined to fill the gap in attack.

Another example was the Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft, whom he had encountered not long ago. His lifebound magic treasure was the extremely heavy Hundred Craft Mountain, but in attack, he used the baleful, sharp, and unmatched Seven Baleful Swords.

Luo Chen had also walked this path before. The Primordial Unity Cauldron was his lifebound magic treasure, emphasizing comprehensiveness, and separately, he refined Xuanchen Armor to defend his whole body.

Before, he had the Mystic Flame Sword, and later, the Flowing Fire Hammer, as offensive tools.

In the past, he had felt this was enough, and it truly was enough. After all, he had many methods and almost no shortcomings. When encountering enemies, there was always a method to counter them.

However, after facing late Golden Core enemies, he discovered the gap was rather large.

"I lack a pure offensive magic treasure!"

Beside the cold pool-abyss, Luo Chen sat cross-legged and found a problem he had previously ignored.

Finally, through the reflection in the water, he saw his own sturdy body.

"This physique of mine is clearly extremely powerful, far surpassing third-tier demon beasts and enough to compare to true archaic beasts!"

"However, my use of it is too rough. It seems I have simply not displayed its true potential."

In the Seven Stars Island battle, Luo Chen had been suppressed from beginning to end, with only one chance to counterattack.

For this to happen, the Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft's Northern Dipper Seven Peaks Array was one aspect. Luo Chen's development and use of his own body was another.

He believed that if Wang Yuan, with such a physique, encountered that situation, even without many spells and without powerful magic treasures, he would not have been so passively beaten.

In his mind, for some reason, the evaluation Wang Yuan had given the Heavenly Peng Transformation cultivation method appeared: a transformation imitating demons, how can it compare to my human race's true body?

"Is the Heavenly Peng true body unsuitable for frontal battle?"

When space was suppressed and speed was restricted, the Heavenly Peng true body indeed seemed to be only a living target.

Then what would happen if it were Wang Yuan's Giant Spirit Transformation?

One question after another was found by Luo Chen one by one, like basins of cold water splashing over him.

Years of cultivation, constant progress, everyone's admiration, the flattery of those at his rank, had long since made Luo Chen, who was drunk on success, suddenly awaken at this moment before the cold pool-abyss.

Looking at it now, he actually discovered that as he became stronger and stronger, his flaws and openings were also increasing.

"That should not be..."

The Daoist sat beside the abyss, murmuring to himself, bewilderment seeming to appear in his eyes.

*(End of Chapter)*
