# Chapter 1121: Ascension (Part 2)

Nine Spirit Primordial Lord had come in haste and departed in haste.

But all those who paid attention to his movements had precisely seized upon this timing.

A secret conversation unknown to the world took place in some uninhabited location.

After that, representatives from various major Nascent Soul sects began to respond to Luo Tian Sect's invitations one after another, stating that they would definitely come in person to watch Luo Tian Ancestor's Ascension Ceremony!

A formless undercurrent that might have overturned the entire Eastern Wilderness cultivation world thus dissipated into nothingness as well.

...

Ten years passed in the blink of an eye.

On Green Parasol Mountain.

Truth Crossing Hall Master Pure Crane and Daytime Void Hall Master Huamei waited quietly.

When the teleportation array emitted light, they looked inside with curiosity.

Three figures slowly materialized.

A middle-aged Daoist guarded the front, while behind him were a matronly woman and a young girl.

"Fellow Daoist Xu, could this be...?"

Xu Muxian stepped out of the teleportation array, cupped his hands toward the two, and introduced them: "As you think, this is my master's wife, Moon Inviting Fairy, and this is my master's daughter."

Toward the former, his expression was one of utmost respect.

Toward the latter, he was quite cautious, not even carefully revealing her name.

Huamei carefully examined the young woman, from her brows and eyes to the corners of her mouth, the contours of her features, her bearing and temperament.

"So similar, truly too similar! Everyone says the current Luo Tian Sect Master bears a strong resemblance to his father, but it's only in spirit. Yet the young miss shares eight or nine parts of the master's features—truly carved from the same mold! Only these clear eyes, unstained by the slightest dust, are unlike the master's unfathomable depths."

Her heart was full of emotion as she then respectfully bowed.

"Disciple Huamei pays respects to Master's Wife, pays respects to the Young Miss."

Cheng Haixin calmly nodded. Before coming, Xu Muxian had already explained the situation here clearly, so she was not surprised that Luo Chen still had such a disciple.

As for the young girl, her clear eyes were curiously looking around.

"Let's go. According to my calculations, the day is approaching. If we hurry now, we'll still make it in time." Xu Muxian did not wish to dwell on old times.

Huamei Fairy acknowledged with an "mm," gestured for them to step off the teleportation array first, then adjusted the formation together with Pure Crane.

Soon, a light flashed, and the five vanished from Green Parasol Mountain.

When they appeared again, the five were already walking on a bustling street.

Xu Muxian acted as guide, introducing the situation here to everyone, especially Cheng Haixin and her daughter.

"This is Shangyou City, the outer sect of Luo Tian Sect. Don't let the 'outer sect' label fool you—there is still a massive fourth-order spirit vein here for disciples to cultivate, and a Nascent Soul Sage is always stationed here on guard."

"Leaving the city, we must take a Du You Ferry Boat to Luo Tian Continent."

"The true mountain gate of Luo Tian Sect is on Luo Tian Continent, surrounded by the Nether Abyss."

The young girl asked curiously, "Uncle Xu, didn't you say Father's ascension would be on Yanfu Mountain?"

Xu Muxian smiled gently. "Yes, but don't worry, Xiao Tong. I've already arranged a place on Yanfu Mountain. Then you can witness your father's cloud-riding ascension with your own eyes."

Hearing the word "father," the young girl instead grew somewhat nervous.

"Let's go!"

One man and four women, three humans and two demons, followed the lively crowd in Shangyou City toward the Nether Abyss.

They seemed merely one part of the countless curious onlookers who wanted to personally witness Luo Tian Ancestor's ascension.

...

On Yanfu Mountain, coiled dragon pillars stood tall, and banners fluttered in the wind.

Twelve great bells, arranged according to the Heavenly Stems and Earthly Branches, were solemnly placed in various positions.

Beams of escape light broke through the air and arrived. Upon entering Yanfu Mountain, they withdrew their escape light and walked on foot along the mountain's white jade paths to show respect.

From time to time, acquaintances met and greeted each other warmly.

None of their conversations could avoid today's grand event.

All they talked about were past events of the Pill Sect, all they discussed was today's Ancestor's ascension.

Among the crowd were no lack of Nascent Soul Sages, sect leaders, and supreme elders.

There were even Northern Sea demon cultivators who suddenly appeared, attracting attention.

One was a late-stage Divine Transformation great Demon Emperor wearing an insignia of a wind-hanging blade!

When he appeared, it caused a stir among the crowd, until the leader of Luo Tian Sect's Four Great Spirit Lords, the demon monk Kongchan, appeared to receive him, and only then did the crowd quiet down.

Soon, news spread.

Great Sage Beili of the Northern Sea cultivation world had sent someone with congratulatory gifts to personally witness Luo Tian Ancestor's ascension.

And this was only the beginning!

Not long after, representatives from the Life and Death Gate of the Southern Frontier and the Hanging Temple of the Western Desert also arrived, receiving personal reception from Luo Tian Sect's top experts.

From this point, it was clear that Luo Tian Sect was now a genuine Ascendant sacred ground, on equal footing with sacred ground powers from other continents.

Among the crowd, Xu Muxian and his group threaded their way through.

Suddenly.

Xu Muxian, walking in front, stopped.

The young girl behind him was puzzled. "Uncle Xu, what's wrong?"

Xu Muxian did not turn back but instead cupped his hands toward a group approaching from the opposite direction, especially the leader.

"Sect Master!"

Luo Lingxi nodded and led the sect's high-level members toward the Luo Tian Grand Hall.

But as they passed each other, his steps suddenly halted.

A pair of majestic eyes met those clear, spring-water-like eyes.

Then, he carefully examined the girl's beautiful yet exceedingly familiar face.

The Luo Tian high-ranking officials following him initially did not notice, but gradually, upon seeing the young girl's face, they began to stir.

Gasps rose softly.

Under the gaze of a group of top experts, even though the young girl was bold, she couldn't help feeling flustered.

Xu Muxian realized what was happening and instinctively stepped forward to block.

"Sect Master..."

"It's fine!"

Luo Lingxi pushed Xu Muxian aside, his eyes flickering as he stared at the young girl, finally softening his expression.

"May I ask this little... young lady's name?"

The girl was quite flustered and said nervously, "My name is Qingtong."

"Little friend Qingtong?" Luo Lingxi looked at that face so reminiscent of his father and understood everything. "Meeting is fate. This jade pendant can calm the heart and ward off evil. I'll give it to you."

Watching the still-warm jade pendant fly before her, the girl Qingtong hesitated. She instinctively looked at her mother.

Cheng Haixin nodded calmly.

Only then did the girl Qingtong solemnly accept it.

Luo Lingxi smiled faintly, then patted Xu Muxian's shoulder.

"Senior Brother Xu, take good care of her."

Xu Muxian nodded. "I understand."

With that, Luo Lingxi led the crowd away and entered the Luo Tian Grand Hall directly.

The Luo Tian high-level officials behind him followed closely, but from time to time, someone couldn't help turning back to look.

That face, except for the clear, spring-water-like eyes of one inexperienced in the world, was almost identical to the Ancestor.

Could she really be the Ancestor's bloodline from outside?

"Is she my sister?"

Luo Lingxi asked himself this, but the answer was already quite obvious.

Suppressing his inexplicable emotions, he finally ascended the last step.

Inside the hall, two figures also walked out at the same time.

Two women, each dressed in magnificent palace attire, with dignified bearing, noble and elegant.

Luo Lingxi bowed deeply, then asked, "Father, are you ready?"

Gu Caiyi's expression carried reluctance. "Ten years, your father's magical power has long recovered, his state has reached its peak. There's really no need to prepare anything. Ascension is merely following Heaven's will."

Canglong's gaze swept over the crowd, finally landing on Luo Lingxi.

"Go in."

Luo Lingxi answered heavily and strode quickly into the hall.

In the square below, Xu Muxian's group had only just squeezed out of the crowd, heading toward a flying-corner pavilion on Yanfu Mountain.

The girl named Qingtong held the still-warm jade pendant, momentarily at a loss.

"Uncle Xu, that person you called Sect Master just now, why..."

Before Xu Muxian could answer, Qingtong gradually realized.

The current Luo Tian Sect Master, his name was Luo Lingxi, his father Luo Chen.

She gripped the jade pendant tightly, suddenly understanding.

"So he's my elder brother!"

Xu Muxian shook his head at this and didn't explain anything, simply leading the group up the Star-Gazing Tower.

Passing by a fair-skinned man, he glanced curiously but didn't pay much attention.

At such a grand event, too many prominent Eastern Wilderness experts had come to observe. It was normal for him not to recognize a Nascent Soul Sage, since he hadn't been in the Eastern Wilderness long.

Bo Hanjun withdrew his gaze from Xu Muxian's group, a thoughtful expression on his face.

"Is that a blood descendant related to Senior Luo Chen? She looks so much like him?"

Before he could ponder this thought further, Bo Hanjun's gaze suddenly sharpened.

On the distant white jade steps, a figure stepped out from the crowd and spoke in a grand voice.

"Begin!"

The next moment, coiled dragons wrapped around pillars, sutra banners fluttered.

Twelve bell sounds rang out in succession, clear chimes spreading throughout Yanfu Mountain. With the formation's enhancement, they layered and spread across the entire Luo Tian Continent.

Threads of spiritual energy drifted in from all directions, surging into the Luo Tian Grand Hall.

The multitude fell silent.

Suddenly, clouds and mist rose. A vast phantom slowly ascended from the hall, revealing itself before the world.

The phantom was enormous, like a tree canopy blocking the sky and covering the sun.

Those with some knowledge understood its essence at a glance.

"Yin soul crossing to yang, Nascent Soul transforming into spirit—this is the primordial spirit!"

As for the uninvited Great Demon Emperor from the Northern Sea's Hanging Blade Peak who had come to observe, his expression could no longer be controlled, revealing shock.

"Such a vast primordial spirit! It far surpasses my clan's ancestor! How is this possible? Hasn't he only just become an Ascendant?"

The primordial spirit phantom paid no attention to the commotion among the observing crowd. It simply stood quietly in the void, allowing its main body to draw in the vast origin qi of heaven and earth.

In its eyes, countless formless spiritual mechanisms could be clearly seen coming from all directions.

Their quantity far exceeded anything he had seen in this life.

Whether it was Chu Yan's ascension, or Youli Divine Lord's ascension, or even the ascension of the three Green Parasol Mountain demons, the spiritual mechanisms they had drawn were far inferior to what he was drawing now.

Luo Chen's heart was touched, and he suddenly understood everything.

All of this was because he had comprehended the true meanings of the four laws of Earth, Fire, Water, and Wood.

If he continued to grow, he would master the authority of these four laws, becoming a unique existence in this world, nearly equal to the Heavenly Dao of this small realm.

Therefore, at the time of his ascension, he could draw such vast spiritual mechanisms.

It was both a send-off and an expulsion!

"Since I should leave, then I shall leave!"

Murmuring, Luo Chen released the restrictions of his Purple Mansion, letting his divine sense spread across the wilderness, allowing the power of heaven and earth to empower him.

The next moment.

From the boundless Nether Abyss, black lotuses surged forth.

On the earth, spiritual energy gushed forth like springs.

Not only that, but in the sky, colorful clouds gathered and chased after him, like evening clouds chasing the moon.

Radiant light, at this moment, spontaneously arose.

Countless cultivators watched this scene, their hearts captivated.

Black lotuses born from the sea, spiritual springs gushing from the earth, colorful clouds chasing the moon, myriad rays of light!

Four great phenomena appeared simultaneously. Such an ascension was unprecedented since ancient times.

Luo Chen cast a reluctant glance downward—this was his last look at the Mountains and Seas Realm.

A mysterious resonance made his heart tighten.

His gaze fell upon the Star-Gazing Tower at a corner of Yanfu Mountain, and Luo Chen instantly understood.

Joy mixed with a trace of guilt.

Without a word, he flicked his finger.

On the Star-Gazing Tower, the girl who had been holding her breath watching this scene suddenly had dazed eyes, losing focus for a moment.

When she came to, the vast primordial spirit phantom had already dissipated.

Only an endless pure qi originating from outside this realm descended, forming threads that scattered across Luo Tian Continent.

Then came a second thread of pure qi, a third, a fourth... until there were thousands, countless beyond measure.

Pure qi descended, the immortal path opened wide!

This was the omen of ascension recorded in ancient texts!

Amidst myriad rays of light, a figure gradually receded into the distance, finally disappearing into the vast cosmos.

The girl rubbed her brow, looking blankly at the dazed woman beside her.

"Mother, has Father left?"

Cheng Haixin's eyes were filled with tears. She bit her lip and gave a heavy "mm."

The girl was somewhat disappointed, but then a smile broke across her face.

"Father saw me in the end. He even spoke to me."

Cheng Haixin was stunned.

At that moment, the twelve clear chimes rang out again, distant and far-reaching.

A gentle voice, familiar to the girl from before, sounded, spreading in all directions.

"The ceremony is complete!"

"Respectfully seeing off the Ancestor as he ascends to the Immortal Realm!"

Then came a sound like mountains roaring and seas howling, rising in unison.

From Yanfu Mountain to Luo Tian Continent.

Wherever people were, they chanted in unison.

"Respectfully seeing off the Ancestor as he ascends to the Immortal Realm!"

(End of chapter)
