# Chapter 806: Heavenly Origin Agent, Fulfilling an Old Friendship

Inside the Warm Jade Pavilion of Heavenly Fragrance Tower.

After several cups of fine wine, with the subtle fragrance of beauties in his nostrils and the sight of singing and dancing before his eyes, Gu Zuokai's recent pent-up frustration had eased considerably.

He hadn't expected Luo Chen to invite him to a place like this for entertainment.

In his impression, Luo Chen had once been a Northern Sea Wanderer, presumably someone who preferred quietude. Now, with the addition of his Dao title from the Pill Sect, it only further suggested a life of ascetic hardship—after all, without sufficient diligent study and practice, how could an ordinary person master alchemy at the Grandmaster level?

This pleasure district truly seemed somewhat incompatible with Luo Chen.

And indeed, it appeared that way.

Though Luo Chen across from him wore a smile, he sat upright and proper, barely touching the wine and food the women beside him carefully poured and offered.

Clearly, his arrangement of such entertainment for the other party was not merely about catching up.

After three rounds of wine and some casual conversation, Gu Zuokai set down his cup.

"Brother Luo, if you have something to say, just say it directly!"

Luo Chen smiled faintly, dismissed the attendants, leaving only the three women serving beside them.

"I heard Brother Gu almost established a Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance branch here in Lingtian Pass?"

Gu Zuokai sighed. "That's right. I had already negotiated with the five major sects of Lingtian's Five Regions, even selected a location, but at the last moment, the Lingtian City Lord stopped it."

"What did they say?" Luo Chen asked.

Gu Zuokai said helplessly, "What else could they say? They made excuses and delayed, and now they simply refuse to see me. In short, they won't allow our Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance to set up shop."

Luo Chen looked thoughtful. "I suspect this is the Nether Origin Sect's doing."

The Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance wasn't merely engaged in commerce. They also gathered intelligence from various continents and recruited talent for the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

As Luo Chen speculated, the Nether Origin Sect wanted to exhaust the Eastern Wilderness cultivators, then personally step forward to repel the demon army, ultimately imitating the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect by dominating the Eastern Wilderness in one fell swoop!

Under such circumstances, if the Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance made contact with various Sage experts, it would undoubtedly provide them with an escape route.

In that case, who would remain to resist the demon army's advance?

Moreover, after the Alchemy Saint incident, how could the Nether Origin Sect sit idly by while the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect continued to grow?

In recent years, Sage experts from the Northern Sea, Southern Frontier, and even the Western Desert had been continuously recruited by the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. Only in the Eastern Wilderness had the Nether Origin Sect maintained a tight defense, with few Sages entering Heaven-Earth Peak.

Oh, Luo Chen was an exception.

Gu Zuokai clearly understood that the suspension of the merchant alliance's construction involved the Eastern Wilderness's sacred land, the Nether Origin Sect. Otherwise, with all the gifts he had sent to the Lingtian City Lord, anyone would have been tempted.

The fact that the other party had returned everything and refused to budge meant there must be a stronger directive behind it.

Luo Chen looked up and asked, "Then if the Lingtian City Lord never agrees, will you just stay stuck in Lingtian Pass indefinitely?"

Gu Zuokai's hand, which had been reaching for his wine cup, froze.

This matter of establishing a merchant alliance branch amid the chaos was also a task passed down from the Dao Sect.

If he abandoned it halfway, even as a member of the Gu Great House, one of the Twelve Great Houses, he wouldn't escape unscathed.

"Brother Gu, have you considered settling for second best?"

Gu Zuokai frowned. "You mean going to the rear?"

Luo Chen nodded. "Exactly. If they won't let you operate in Lingtian Pass, then go to the rear. That way, you won't compete with the shops of the Nether Origin Sect and the established Eastern Wilderness sects within the city. At worst, it'll be a bit roundabout and troublesome."

Gu Zuokai considered this suggestion for only a brief moment before rejecting it.

"According to the Lingtian City Lord's meaning, we're not allowed to use the Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance's name, even in the rear!"

That restriction was rather strict.

The Nether Origin Sect wanted to completely sever the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's reach!

Luo Chen offered another suggestion. "Then what about not using the Heavenly Origin name but still conducting business?"

Not using the Heavenly Origin name, yet still doing business?

Gu Zuokai was puzzled.

Luo Chen smiled freely. "Just find an agent, isn't that simple?"

As he said this, he pointed to himself.

"I've temporarily established a foothold in the Eastern Wilderness, and I have a sect, neither large nor small, at my disposal. Entrust the Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance's business to my Luo Tian Sect. Let my sect handle matters on your behalf, and then you can settle accounts with us separately."

Gu Zuokai was stunned.

But this time, he didn't immediately reject the idea. Instead, he carefully considered the feasibility of this suggestion.

First, Luo Chen was relatively trustworthy among the vast Eastern Wilderness—he held the status of an external guest elder of the Star Gate!

Second, this would circumvent the Lingtian City Lord's prohibition.

Finally, although he hadn't fully completed the task assigned by the sect, he would at least have made some progress to report to the Dao Sect. Even if questioned by the Central Continent, he could use the excuse of proceeding gradually to buy some time.

However, did the current Luo Tian Sect have the capability?

After all, the Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance's business touched every aspect, essentially competing with those established Eastern Wilderness sects within Lingtian City.

Relying solely on a Luo Tian Sect that even he looked down upon...

Gu Zuokai's gaze fell on Luo Chen, and he suddenly understood.

The other party was referring to himself first!

He was not only the former Wilderness Wanderer who had defeated the Dao Sect's prodigies but now also the renowned Pill Sect of the Eastern Wilderness, sought after by countless experts!

One man alone possessed unparalleled appeal!

If they used his name as the main banner, supplemented by the Luo Tian Sect's personnel, and backed by his own support, they indeed had a chance to establish the merchant alliance's business.

Having figured this out, the gloom that had lingered between Gu Zuokai's brows for days instantly dissipated.

"Brother Luo, this suggestion of yours is truly brilliant!"

Luo Chen smiled modestly. "It's merely mutual benefit. My Luo Tian Sect has long lacked financial resources. I can't support them on my own; I need to find them something to do. Besides, this isn't difficult for you at all. The only thing lacking was a trustworthy person."

Gu Zuokai couldn't help but agree. After all, apart from the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, there were no Star Gate guest elders in other continents.

But he still had concerns.

"If you act on our behalf, won't the City Lord's Mansion..."

Luo Chen shook his head. "I don't think so. With so many cultivators forced to remain in Lingtian Pass, the rules won't be that restrictive. Look at this Heavenly Fragrance Tower—the City Lord's Mansion hasn't ordered it closed, has it?"

Gu Zuokai looked around and couldn't help but chuckle. He had been overthinking.

The specific details of cooperation couldn't be discussed here, so after a few words, they changed the subject.

This time, it was Luo Chen who had another request.

"You're talking about artifacts made from illusion cores?"

"Yes. Ever since I witnessed the wonders of illusion beasts in the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, I've been fixated on illusion cores. But as you know, I'm not a Dao Sect disciple, so I can't use fourth-tier illusion cores, and third-tier ones are useless to me. So I have to settle for second best and see if there's anything remarkable about magical artifacts and treasures made from third-tier illusion cores."

"Illusion core artifacts are quite rare, usually held by the various great families of the Central Continent. Under normal circumstances, I could at most find one or two for you. But now, it's not difficult. The five-hundred-year Grotto-Heaven Grand Competition just ended, and many illusion cores have flowed out, naturally accompanied by corresponding artifacts. If you want some, I'll have people search for a few pieces."

"Then I'll thank Brother Gu in advance!"

Gu Zuokai waved his hand dismissively, unconcerned about this matter.

Compared to the trouble Luo Chen might help him resolve, a few low-tier illusion core artifacts that were useless to Sage experts were utterly insignificant.

On the contrary, he was quite pleased.

Back then, following his clan's instructions, he had deliberately cultivated a relationship with Luo Chen and even helped him find a teleportation formation.

Now it seemed he had made the right choice.

This Luo Chen was someone who clearly distinguished between kindness and grievance, arriving like a timely rain when Gu Zuokai was at a loss.

With both matters settled, the atmosphere became even more relaxed.

Even the singing and dancing that had stopped earlier resumed at Luo Chen's signal.

Late into the night, when the wine had run its course and the guests dispersed.

"This jade token is my personal seal. There's also Tianxuan in the sect whom you know; they'll recognize it."

"Then I'll go to Baili Qingchuan to discuss this matter in detail with your Luo Tian Sect's sect master. Today's banquet was most enjoyable!"

"Brother Gu, wait!"

"Hmm? Is there something else?"

Just as they were about to part, Luo Chen suddenly called out to Gu Zuokai.

Amid the other's confusion, Luo Chen produced an envelope.

The envelope was utterly ordinary, without even a seal or prohibition, suggesting it contained nothing secret that needed guarding from prying eyes.

"During the Grotto-Heaven Grand Competition, I made a good friend named Sage Heavenly Mountain. I have some cultivation doubts that only he might be able to resolve. So, if it's convenient for Brother Gu, could you use the merchant alliance's channels to deliver this letter for me?"

Gu Zuokai laughed heartily. "Such a trivial matter? It's no trouble at all!"

Taking the letter, he strode away.

Leaving behind, in the Warm Jade Pavilion, the two female cultivators who had served him for so long, their faces filled with disappointment.

And within that disappointment, a hint of resignation.

How could a Sage expert possibly take an interest in fallen women like them?

"You two may leave. Yu Xiang, stay."

The two were startled, their gazes involuntarily falling on the woman who had remained silent and quiet all evening.

Could it be that the renowned Pill Sect had taken a liking to her?

Why didn't they have such luck!

Was it merely because, during the selection, she had deliberately acted coy, arousing the Sage's interest?

Cursing inwardly at the injustice and silently scolding Yu Xiang for her cunning, they departed. The Warm Jade Pavilion fell silent.

Only one man and one woman remained.

The woman's bowed body was visibly trembling.

Until a cup of warm wine was placed before her.

Yu Xiang was startled. The Sage was pouring wine for her?

Hesitantly, she raised her head. What met her eyes was a face as warm and smooth as jade, and a pair of clean, clear eyes.

"Miss Yu Xiang, it's been a long time."

Yu Xiang took the wine cup. At the sound of this gentle voice, tears flowed uncontrollably down her face.

At this moment, her emotions were far more complex than any outsider could understand.

Once, a Qi Refinement rogue cultivator who could barely make ends meet, who had to pull strings just to see her, had now become a generation's Sage expert.

While she, who had once had a promising path ahead, had fallen from an inner disciple of the Joyous Union Sect to this state of selling smiles and accompanying drinks just to survive.

How unpredictable the world was!

She wiped her tears. "You flatter me, Sage. I am no longer a 'miss.' If not for taking a Beauty Preservation Pill in my early years and being skilled in beauty maintenance techniques, I would already be old and decrepit, no longer young."

Luo Chen smiled, acknowledging her words.

Strictly speaking, Yu Xiang was actually a few years younger than him.

But no matter how young, she didn't have much time left before the lifespan limit of an Initiate cultivator.

To put it bluntly, she could already be considered an old woman.

Compared to the vibrant vitality of Caiyi and the others, who had formed their cores and had promising paths ahead, she was far behind.

He thought of Gu Caiyi, and she clearly did too.

"Is Caiyi... doing well?"

Luo Chen spoke of Gu Caiyi's situation.

Hearing that the other had successfully formed her core and had a promising path ahead, Yu Xiang's emotions grew even more complicated.

In truth, there was no point comparing herself to Luo Chen—their circumstances were different, and there was no gap to speak of.

But Gu Caiyi? She had once been merely a dancer at Heavenly Fragrance Tower, inferior to Yu Xiang in both cultivation and background.

Yet...

"Could you tell me about the destruction of the Joyous Union Sect?" Luo Chen asked with some curiosity. "I heard that Azure Frost Demon Emperor single-handedly stormed the Joyous Union Sect and killed your sect's only remaining Sage?"

Yu Xiang hadn't expected him to be curious about this matter. But that event was the nightmare that had plunged all Joyous Union Sect cultivators into the dust, and she remembered it vividly.

Steeling herself, she recounted the battle from back then in detail.

Luo Chen listened carefully, his brow furrowing from time to time.

Clearly, Yu Xiang's cultivation level at the time had been insufficient to witness the full scope of the battle.

Her account was mostly limited to the breaking of the mountain-protecting grand formation and the image of the Adept elder exploding to death.

The battle details of Azure Frost Demon Emperor, which Luo Chen cared about most, she knew nothing about. She only mentioned that after the Sect Ancestor Sage let out a miserable scream, Azure Frost Demon Emperor coldly glanced down at them and then calmly departed.

"She is still that powerful..."

Luo Chen murmured to himself, then stood up calmly.

Yu Xiang looked up at him, hesitating and timidly asking, "Would the Sage like to stay the night?"

Luo Chen looked down at her.

With just one glance, Yu Xiang felt as if her entire body had been seen through.

She couldn't help but lower her head, cold sweat forming on her back.

A jade bottle landed on the table with a crisp sound.

"Your cultivation is sufficient; you are only one step away from forming a core. As a former inner disciple of the Joyous Union Sect, I imagine you don't lack methods for core formation. This Condensed Liquid Pill, refined by my own hand, will give you a helping hand!"

Yu Xiang opened her mouth. When she looked up again, Luo Chen was already gone.

Biting her lip, she showed a hint of unwillingness.

But when she opened the jade bottle, she froze.

Third-tier, mid-grade!

And personally refined by the Pill Sect!

Perhaps with this single pill, she could attempt that golden core realm she had always feared yet yearned for!

In an instant, amidst her unwilling emotions, a thread of hope emerged, making her feelings even more complex.

...

Leaving Heavenly Fragrance Tower, Luo Chen walked through an alley in Lingtian City under the moonlight.

Unlike the Heartless Fairy, he had never had any intention of taking Yu Xiang into the Luo Tian Sect.

Joyous Union Sect cultivators had devious minds and were hard to control.

He had no deep connection with Yu Xiang.

The reason he had given her a mid-grade Condensed Liquid Pill was out of consideration for the time when she had sold him her unused Purple Cloud Copper Furnace, solving his urgent need when his alchemy cauldron had exploded.

"Consider this as fulfilling an old friendship."

Luo Chen smiled, in quite a good mood.

He had found a source of income for the Luo Tian Sect and also gained a lead on reforging the Ghost God Heart-Inquiring Mirror.

If he could obtain a batch of illusion core artifacts for study, he might discover how to utilize this material, and then he could try to figure out how to refine the Ghost God Heart-Inquiring Mirror himself.

As for the letter he had sent out?

Its contents were simple—it was about the mutation of the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu on Sima Huiniang.

Sage Heavenly Mountain hailed from the Southern Frontier and was quite knowledgeable about gu insects. He might have a corresponding solution.

Just as Luo Chen stepped out of the alley, the smile on his face froze.

"Fellow Daoist Luo, have you just come from... Heavenly Fragrance Tower?"

Ling Fengzi, who happened to be passing by, hesitated as he glanced back at the alley behind him.

Under the moonlight, they stared at each other, and Luo Chen felt a strange sense of being caught red-handed.
