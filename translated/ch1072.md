# Chapter 1072: Stake Everything, Cross the Sea for Battle

"So this is the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven?"

Among the crowd, Zhou Fuyou gazed at the unfamiliar territory.

Yet to his disappointment, the Dao Sect's foremost grotto-heaven, which the classics recorded as profoundly mysterious, held nothing particularly remarkable. Even the most iconic "illusion beasts" were nowhere to be seen.

As one of the Adept cultivators participating in this Grand Competition, he basically knew that those who secured a top-ten ranking would have the opportunity to enter the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven to cultivate. Thus, most had consulted the relevant ancient texts beforehand. But what they saw before them now left them with the same doubts as Zhou Fuyou.

Apart from the ten core Adept disciples, thirty-six Sages had also entered this time.

Perhaps sensing the confusion among the younger Adept cultivators, someone explained, "This time, the grotto-heaven was opened through an unconventional backdoor method, so the internal scenery is vastly different. There's no need for you to be surprised."

"Uncle Li, is it also normal for the illusion beasts to be absent?" someone asked curiously.

Uncle Li laughed. "Of course not. Illusion beasts are actually formed from the Mirage Dragon's cloud qi combined with spiritual mechanism. Unless the disciples from the previous grotto-heaven Grand Competition killed them all, how could they not exist? The reason you don't see any now is simply that this exit area has already been cleared by Ancestor Liancheng."

Having said this, his expression turned slightly serious.

"Don't be curious about these things. Your purpose in entering here is purely to accelerate your growth. The Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven has a time-flow difference of one hundred to one compared to the outside world. A hundred years in the grotto-heaven equals one year outside. You must make good use of this hundred years to break through to the Sage stage as quickly as possible, so that after you leave, you can become the new generation of leaders for our Dao Sect!"

"Remember, do not venture too deep into the grotto-heaven. Large numbers of high-rank illusion beasts dwell there, with strength comparable to Sages. They are not something you can handle at your current level."

The assembled disciples acknowledged with fervent promises, all voicing their agreement.

Uncle Li's gaze fell upon the somewhat aloof white-robed man among the crowd.

"Zhou Fuyou!"

"Disciple is here."

"You are the top-ranked winner of this Sexagenary Grand Competition. The other rewards have already been distributed to the Tiannan Dao Palace and your clan. As for your personal rewards, you should have received them by now, correct?"

A list of rewards flowed through Zhou Fuyou's mind: the Thousand-Year Thunder Essence that could be added to his lifebound treasure to enhance its power, the Profound Origin Heavy Water most suitable for water-attribute cultivators, and a wealth of resources and secret techniques to aid in forming his Sage core, among others.

He nodded lightly. "I received them yesterday."

Uncle Li's eyes showed expectation. "I hope that when the grotto-heaven opens again, you will have become one of us, a fellow Daoist on the same path. Additionally, there will be another opportunity at that time. I hope you seize it."

Zhou Fuyou was startled. Another opportunity when the grotto-heaven opened again?

He wanted to ask further, but Uncle Li and the other thirty-six Sages had already grown impatient.

After giving the group a general briefing, they hurriedly mounted their flying light and departed.

They all headed in the same direction, very decisively, as if they had entered with a specific mission.

Connecting this to the name "Ke Lianshan" that Ancestor Liancheng had mentioned before they entered, Zhou Fuyou's face showed a thoughtful expression.

The name Ke Lianshan was not well-known among the current generation of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, but it carried great renown among the older generation. He was once a figure of equal fame to the Alchemy Saint, known throughout the Central Continent as the Chess Saint.

He was the one most likely to become an Ascendant!

Having vanished from sight for over a hundred years, no one expected that he had somehow entered the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven at some unknown time.

"The Chess Saint Ke Lianshan, plus thirty-six Sages... what great undertaking are they planning?"

Zhou Fuyou thought to himself, his mind momentarily adrift.

When he came back to his senses, he found that the other nine companions were all looking at him with peculiar expressions.

Was it because of Uncle Li's special treatment earlier?

Zhou Fuyou snorted lightly, unconcerned. As the top-ranked winner who had overpowered his fellow disciples in this Grand Competition, he had the right not to care about anyone's opinions.

With a leap, he transformed into a streak of light and flew into the distance.

Seeing him act so willfully, the others found it hard to comment. The elder among them merely said, "Everyone, go seek out your own treasured cultivation sites," before they all dispersed.

Ten figures, transforming into ten streaks of light, headed off in different directions.

Compared to the previous five-hundred-year grotto-heaven Grand Competition, this batch of Dao Sect core disciples was clearly much more relaxed.

Even entering the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven came with no attached competition tasks. It was purely a reward for the Sexagenary Grand Competition, allowing them to enter and cultivate peacefully for a hundred years.

Zhou Fuyou rushed ahead, and after only two or three days, he found a spiritual site to his liking.

It was a place of scenic beauty with a three-tiered waterfall where white waves crashed against the sky. Its only flaw was probably that the mountain body was scorched black, as if burned by a fierce fire.

However, taking up residence there proved quite troublesome.

Because it was occupied by a fourth-rank jiaolong illusion beast, comparable to a Sage cultivator!

Zhou Fuyou was not afraid of this.

If he wanted to break through to the Sage stage, he would need the assistance of a fourth-rank spiritual vein. And with his strength, he was fully qualified to kill this fourth-rank illusion beast across ranks.

Zhou Fuyou raised both hands, and vast mist erupted from his body. Thick black fog enveloped the entire area for ten li, within which the sound of water crashing faintly echoed.

*Qi Sea Floating Heaven, Myriad Phenomena Majestic!*

Not only that, but Zhou Fuyou opened his mouth and spat out a pearl that spun and flew out, striking at the jiaolong-like fourth-rank illusion beast.

The two became locked in battle, stirring the spiritual energy for dozens of li in all directions.

In the distance, a woman watched this scene with a horrified expression.

She had originally thought that when Zhou Fuyou overpowered his peers in the Sexagenary Grand Competition, his strength was already outstanding.

But she never expected that was merely the tip of the iceberg.

The power he was displaying now was almost indistinguishable from that of a true Sage.

"Forget it. This Three-Tiered Waterfall was never my goal anyway. Let him have it!"

Seeing that the battle was about to reach a conclusion, the woman quietly retreated.

*Boom!*

With a violent roar, the boundless cloud qi suddenly collapsed.

The enormous illusion beast jiaolong fell with a mournful cry.

Zhou Fuyou's figure flickered, and he grabbed a beast core, his face showing surprise and delight.

"This should be the legendary illusion core!"

"According to my master, it contains opportunities that seize the wonders of heaven and earth. By cultivating with an illusion core, one can hope to comprehend the true meaning of laws."

"No rush. I haven't broken through to the Sage stage yet. I'll keep it safe for now."

Zhou Fuyou carefully stored away the fourth-rank illusion core and cast a glance into the distance.

"Gu Muyu?"

She was one of his defeated opponents in the Sexagenary Grand Competition. He hadn't expected that she, too, had ignored Uncle Li's warning and ventured so deep into the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven.

The reason Zhou Fuyou dared to go deep was twofold: first, to seek a better spiritual vein as his cultivation environment for the next hundred years; second, because he trusted his own martial prowess and wasn't afraid of the threat from fourth-rank illusion beasts.

But Gu Muyu didn't have that capability.

Frowning slightly, Zhou Fuyou didn't bother with her spying. After all, Gu Muyu had already taken the initiative to retreat.

He raised his hand and scattered several formation flags, setting up a simple isolation formation, and then began carving a new cave dwelling within the Three-Tiered Waterfall.

Unexpectedly, after his flying sword had been drilling for only a short while, it suddenly broke through.

Inside, there was already a self-contained cave dwelling!

Walking through it, Zhou Fuyou could detect traces of someone having once been active here, though they were already very faint.

"Perhaps it was a Sage core disciple from my master's generation."

Since there was already a convenient cave dwelling, Zhou Fuyou no longer insisted on doing everything himself. He took out a pile of items from his storage ring and arranged them around him.

Once everything was set up, he sat down cross-legged in peace.

"Currently, I'm at the ninth layer of the Adept stage. Next, I'll go all out to sprint for the Great Perfection of the Adept stage, nurture my essence, energy, and spirit, and prepare for breaking through to the Sage stage."

Zhou Fuyou murmured to himself, his entire being gradually entering a state of pure tranquility and non-action as he began to absorb external spiritual energy into his body.

His cultivation level was also visibly rising rapidly.

...

Outside Heaven-Earth Island.

Witnessing the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven opening once again, Luo Chen's expression shifted slightly.

"What's wrong?"

Li Canghai didn't understand and looked at Luo Chen.

Luo Chen shook his head. "I've been inside that secret realm before. I didn't expect that Liancheng would open it before the scheduled time."

Li Canghai was puzzled. "Is there something special about that secret realm?"

The Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven was quite famous, but it was mostly known among Dao Sect disciples and through the reputation of the grotto-heaven Grand Competition.

Outsiders rarely knew the specifics of the secret realm itself.

Luo Chen gave a brief, perfunctory explanation, but his heart remained unsettled.

The Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven had two special features: one was its abundance of illusion beasts, and the other was its different time flow.

The illusion cores from the former helped those newly entering the Sage stage comprehend the true meaning of laws. That should be the reason for that batch of Sages entering.

But the latter...

"If Liancheng placed the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard inside to nurture it, with a hundred years outside, the effect inside would be no less than ten thousand years. Even if the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard was severely damaged during the Central Continent war, it should be able to recover by now!"

"Especially since the Chess Saint Ke Lianshan is also inside!"

"Rumor has it that the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard is under the control of the Dao Sect's Sect Leader, but its true master is the Chess Saint Ke Lianshan. Could it really be as I imagined? The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect forcibly opened the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven to nurture this spirit treasure for ten thousand years?"

"If that's the case, then this upcoming battle..."

Luo Chen took a deep breath, his eyes showing determination.

At this point, the arrow had been shot and must fly. He couldn't afford to hesitate or look back, and he couldn't share this information with Li Canghai either, lest the other party get cold feet.

He turned around and said, "Senior, I don't know what agreement you have with the Northern Sea Demon Race, but I, Luo, have already contacted the Eastern Wilderness Demon Race. In this upcoming battle, it will be a joint effort of humans and demons from the Eastern Wilderness—the strongest lineup possible. This battle cannot be allowed to fail!"

Li Canghai withdrew his suspicious gaze and smiled. "You don't need to worry about my side. When the time comes, the Northern Sea Demon Race will go all out to attack the Beili Continent. No one will be able to come to the aid of the Dongyuan Continent. As for the Tiannan Continent, its strength is the weakest. Even if they send reinforcements, they won't be a threat."

Luo Chen nodded. "Alright. In that case, let's separate for now and start gathering our forces to prepare. I hope that when the time comes, you and I can fight Liancheng together on Heaven-Earth Island."

Cupping his hands, Luo Chen transformed into a streak of light and drifted away.

Li Canghai turned back to look at the entrance of the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven on Heaven-Earth Island, his eyes showing both suspicion and a hint of greed.

He was puzzled as to why Liancheng had sent thirty-six family Sages into the secret realm at such a critical moment. As for the greed, it was directed at the secret realm itself.

"Although I am a mighty Ascendant revered by tens of thousands, among my peers, my foundation is still too shallow—far inferior to the Ascendants of these sacred lands. If I could also take this secret realm into my possession, perhaps I could make up for some of that deficiency."

Murmuring to himself, Li Canghai's figure gradually vanished into the void.

...

Eastern Wilderness, Dongyang Domain.

At the coastline where land met sea, a once-bustling port was eerily quiet today.

Only a few scattered figures were arriving from all directions.

Yet, despite their small number, the forces they represented and the strength they possessed were enough to surpass any prosperous port in the Eastern Wilderness.

Looking around, every one of them was richly dressed and exuded an extraordinary bearing—all top-tier great figures of the Eastern Wilderness.

At the forefront was the Grand Elder of the Dongyang Sect, leading four Sages from the sect. Among them was Shouyang Hermit, whose body had been reshaped, his face full of excitement and hope.

He had thought that when his physical body was destroyed and his Sage core wounded during the human-demon war, even after reshaping his body, there would be no hope for the Great Dao. Unexpectedly, the Alchemy Sect's Luo Chen possessed the Heaven-Transforming Initiation Pill that could seize the wonders of heaven and earth.

Thus, when his senior brother brought back that news, Shouyang Hermit agreed to this operation without the slightest hesitation.

Shouyang Hermit swept his gaze across the crowd: the Nine Spirit Primogenitor of the Nine Spirit Sect, the Heavenly Capital Master of the Heavenly Capital Sect, a group of Daoists from the White Deer Grotto...

Every Sage of any renown in the Eastern Wilderness was gathered here.

"As expected of the Alchemy Sect! This power of rallying support is terrifyingly strong!" Shouyang Hermit exclaimed in admiration.

The Dongyang Grand Elder ahead of him was still shaken, but he also sighed, "Not to mention the Alchemy Sect's personal abilities, just this power of influence alone means no one in the Eastern Wilderness dares to oppose him. With a single command, there are countless Sages willing to follow him, and countless Adepts and Initiate cultivators ready to run errands for him."

Their conversation wasn't soundproofed, so the Sages from other sects could hear it.

But no one disputed their words.

The impression from the previous Dao Discussion Conference hadn't been deep enough. Only when it was truly time to mobilize did they realize the terrifying mobilizing power of the Alchemy Sect.

Seeing that most people had already arrived, someone asked Taiyue Sage when they would set out.

Deng Taiyue only said one word: "Wait."

Wait for whom?

The answer became self-evident not long after, when demonic qi swept in from all directions.

*Swish! Swish! Swish!*

Figures descended from the sky, each exuding surging and powerful demonic qi.

The human Sages of the Eastern Wilderness were no strangers to these beings.

In the blink of an eye, most of them were recognized.

Thunder Prison Roc, Bi Zheng, Kuang Kuang, Bull Demon, Blood Vulture Daoist, White Bone Mystic Serpent, White-Browed Immortal Ape...

None were weaklings. Almost all had earned fearsome reputations during the two-hundred-year human-demon war.

And beneath their glorious reputations lay mountains of human bones.

The moment the two sides met, an atmosphere of tension and confrontation formed.

Deng Taiyue stepped forward, placing himself between them.

His meaning was clear.

On the demon side, Thunder Prison Roc issued orders on behalf of Luo Chen, restraining the various demons of the Truth Crossing Hall from clashing with the Eastern Wilderness humans.

The two sides glared coldly at each other until, in the distant ocean, waves rolled and a massive figure, as vast as land itself, slowly surfaced from the water.

A desolate beast: the Nether Abyss Mystic Turtle!

The group of experts from the Luo Tian Sect stood atop it, their expressions solemn.

Cang Long stepped forward and bowed gracefully to the crowd from across the distance.

"This journey across the sea is long. To avoid wasting mana needlessly, this turtle can serve as our transport."

"Fellow Daoists, please!"

The Nine Spirit Primogenitor laughed heartily. "Lady Cang Long is so generous. Then I won't stand on ceremony."

With that, he led the two Sages of the Nine Spirit Sect onto the turtle's back.

Seeing this, the others no longer hesitated and leaped up one after another.

Even the group of demon experts, led by Thunder Prison Roc, set foot on the turtle's back, which was as thick as land itself.

Simultaneously bearing nearly sixty Sage-level beings, the honest and simple Nether Abyss Mystic Turtle seemed unfazed. It merely snorted water columns from its nostrils, paddled its four limbs, and carried the entire combat strength of the Eastern Wilderness across the ocean, heading toward the Dongyuan Continent.

At the same time, in the direction of the Northern Sea.

Vast numbers of low-rank demons surged toward the Beili Continent, and another human-demon war erupted once more.

In the ruined city of Taohai, the Chanting Monk, who had been reciting sutras and chanting Buddha's name, suddenly stopped the wooden fish in his hand.

He took out a jade token, its surface flickering with faint light.

The Chanting Monk's expression was somewhat complicated. In the end, he gritted his teeth and left this Buddhist hall that seemed like a pure land.

And not long after he left, a sudden explosion rang out from the ruined city of Taohai. Amidst the trembling of countless low-rank demon beasts, a beam of blood light shot up into the sky.

(Chapter End)
