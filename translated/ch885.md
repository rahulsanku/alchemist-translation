# Chapter 885: The Primordial Lord Awakens, Ascends to Profound Clarity

The spacious cave dwelling was filled with dense spiritual qi, the atmosphere quiet and serene.

Until a light laugh broke the silence.

"Fellow Daoist Nether Spring, why do you look as if you bear a deep-seated hatred?"

"Fellow Daoist?"

Nether Spring chewed on the word, a hint of mockery in her voice.

"A former prisoner now dares to address me as fellow Daoist?"

As she spoke, her divine sense swept outward.

A sudden gust of wind rose in the hidden chamber, blowing Luo Chen's black hair wildly like dancing snakes.

A smile curled at the corner of his mouth as he likewise released his divine sense.

When they clashed in the void, the smile on Luo Chen's face faded slightly.

In contrast, Nether Spring let out a soft exclamation of surprise.

"Oh!"

"I never expected that not only have you improved your cultivation realm quickly, but your divine sense is also this powerful?"

"I thought you might have taken some shortcut."

Divine sense was the external manifestation of one's soul foundation. Powerful divine sense indicated a strong soul.

Cultivators refined both qi and spirit. Qi was easy to refine, but the soul was not so easily enhanced.

Apart from realm breakthroughs, it often required long years of accumulation and gradual transformation.

For Luo Chen to have a soul foundation comparable to a great cultivator while rapidly advancing his realm was truly remarkable.

"Now, may I address you as fellow Daoist?" Luo Chen asked calmly.

And because the sudden gust of wind had lifted a corner of her veil, he had already guessed the reason for Nether Spring's angry visit.

"Hmph, it's just a form of address. Call me whatever you like." Nether Spring snorted coldly, acknowledging Luo Chen, then immediately began to interrogate him. "The outside world spreads your reputation as the Alchemy Sect. In this hall master's view, it seems somewhat exaggerated. The pills you refined..."

Before Nether Spring could finish, Luo Chen suddenly spoke up.

"Could it be that the Transformation Pill from back then had a flaw?"

Nether Spring was taken aback, then replied, "Indeed, there was a problem."

Luo Chen smiled. "What problem? Could it be that the transformation was incomplete?"

Nether Spring's gaze suddenly turned fierce as she stared intently at Luo Chen. "You knew the Transformation Pill had this issue?"

Her implication was clear: "And you still dared to give it to me?" Had he deliberately schemed against her?

Luo Chen quickly waved his hands. This doctor-patient relationship was getting out of hand. He didn't want to ruin his reputation as the Alchemy Sect.

After a moment of deliberation, Luo Chen spoke slowly. "The Transformation Pill itself had no problem. My alchemy skills were also not a major issue."

"Then is it my problem?"

"No, neither. The problem lies in the pill formula itself!"

Seeing Nether Spring's confusion, Luo Chen explained in detail. "The Transformation Pill formula you gave me back then was intended for demon cultivators below the fourth rank, so they could transform into human form before reaching the fourth rank and switch to practicing human techniques. Therefore, the main ingredient used was the Nascent Soul essence blood of a Sage cultivator. At the time, my alchemy skills were just beginning, and my understanding was shallow. I didn't realize the inherent limitations. Now that I have achieved mastery in the alchemy path, I naturally perceive the drawbacks."

Nether Spring listened with some impatience, wanting only to hear the conclusion. "What drawbacks?"

"Fourth-rank demon cultivators who take this pill will experience a conflict between the human Sage's essence blood and their own Nascent Soul, resulting in incomplete transformation."

Having said this, Luo Chen shrugged casually. "The Transformation Pill I refined back then was absolutely fine. One of my spirit pets successfully transformed."

Nether Spring chose to believe this explanation.

Because later, a third-rank demon bird at Green Parasol Mountain had also taken the Transformation Pill Luo Chen left behind and successfully transformed ahead of schedule.

Moreover, Luo Chen's explanation...

"It seems you're not lying to me. Huamei said the same thing."

Luo Chen asked curiously, "Who is Huamei?"

Nether Spring sneered. "You don't even remember your own disciple?"

Now Luo Chen was truly bewildered. When had he ever had a disciple named "Huamei"?

But Nether Spring had no interest in explaining to Luo Chen. She cut straight to the point. "Since you are now accomplished in the alchemy path and know the drawbacks of the Transformation Pill, do you have a subsequent solution?"

Luo Chen hesitated. "I have some ideas, but it would be best to first examine your condition, Vice Hall Master."

This time, it was Nether Spring's turn to hesitate.

Luo Chen did not urge her, waiting quietly.

After a long while, Nether Spring somewhat awkwardly removed her bamboo hat and black veil.

The moment Luo Chen saw Nether Spring's true appearance, he laughed.

Of course, inwardly. His face remained quite serious.

A crow's head on a human body, a short black beak, and even her nose was covered by feathery down, obscuring all details.

Under Luo Chen's gaze, Nether Spring became even more uncomfortable.

"Have you seen enough? Is there a solution?"

Luo Chen nodded. "It's manageable. The problem isn't major. Once I refine a new batch of Transformation Pills, you will be able to fully transform into human form."

Hearing this, Nether Spring felt as if a great weight had been lifted.

For demon beasts, they did not despise their original forms nor covet the human body.

But an incomplete transformation—half-beast, half-human—was something that would invite discrimination from powerful members of the demon race!

Unless it was a deliberately retained racial characteristic during transformation, no one would end up with a ridiculous appearance like a beast's head on a human body or a human head on a beast's torso.

"How long will it take?"

"Three days will suffice. I have the materials prepared."

"Fine. In three days, I will send someone to collect them!"

Nether Spring could not wait to put her bamboo hat and black veil back on. Without any intention of catching up with Luo Chen, she rose and left directly.

Luo Chen also stood to see her off, maintaining proper etiquette.

Their harmonious farewell at the cave dwelling's entrance was witnessed by many people from Green Parasol Mountain.

He Qingzi, who had been somewhat worried, relaxed upon seeing this scene.

Leiyu was quite unwilling. He had hoped Vice Hall Master Nether Spring would go to interrogate Luo Chen, but instead, it had turned into a scene of "mutual delight"?

Standing at the entrance of the cave dwelling, Luo Chen could naturally sense those prying gazes but remained perfectly composed.

As for how to solve Nether Spring's problem?

It was actually not a problem at all.

The Transformation Pill formula from Green Parasol Mountain back then did indeed have natural limitations.

But the batch of Transformation Pills he had refined in the Demon-Fall Land using the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's formula had no flaws whatsoever.

The Crane Emperor of the White Crane Sect had failed in its transformation, yet it was still resolved by the Transformation Pill Luo Chen had sent out. So why would Nether Spring be any different?

The reason he needed a three-day buffer was merely deliberate on Luo Chen's part.

Waiting was always hard to bear. Things obtained too easily were never appreciated.

Three days would give Nether Spring time to calm down. By the time he cured her, the resentment she had accumulated for over a hundred years could be greatly reduced.

After all, the murderous intent she had shown when they first met was unmistakable.

"I didn't expect her divine sense to be so powerful, even surpassing mine!"

Luo Chen paced back into his dwelling, a hint of gravity on his face.

While Nether Spring was surprised by his rapid soul enhancement, Luo Chen was equally shocked by the depth of Nether Spring's soul foundation.

Moreover, their earlier exchange had only been the simplest collision of divine sense, without any soul arts involved.

Nether Spring's original form was a Netherworld Yin Crow, most skilled in soul-path techniques.

If she were to employ some sinister soul arts, he might suffer a great loss if caught off guard.

"I need to put on the agenda the matter of splitting my three souls and practicing the *Prison Suppression* soul art, which I've been putting off for years."

Luo Chen thought this, but did not rashly begin splitting his three souls.

The top priority was still to restore himself to peak condition and then meet with Rosycloud Primordial Lord!

Fortunately, the spiritual qi in this cave dwelling was rich, greatly accelerating his recovery. He believed it would not be long before he was fully restored.

...

Days passed one by one.

News of Luo Chen's disappearance had spread throughout the Luo Tian Sect.

If this had happened in years past, chaos would have ensued.

But this time, the situation stabilized quickly.

With Wang Yuan and Lingfengzi overseeing matters above, and Li Yingzhang handling affairs below, neither the Luo Tian Sect nor the Luo Tian Domain could fall into disorder.

Moreover, the appearance of Luo Lingxi had raised many people's expectations.

Of course, the main reason the Luo Tian Sect's high-level members remained steady was the personal visit of the Nether Origin Sect's ancestor.

Rumor had it that the Nether Origin Sect's ancestor had personally gone to find Luo Chen!

On this day, deep within the million mountains, atop a peak ten thousand li from Green Parasol Mountain, two figures stood gazing into the distance.

"Luo Chen is there!"

Heaven Abyss Ancestor said this, his usually gentle expression turning grave.

The Heavenly Execution City Lord looked in that direction. Countless birds circled among the mountain ranges, a rather spectacular sight.

He asked in confusion, "Since you've found Luo Chen, why not go directly?"

Heaven Abyss Ancestor shook his head. "I cannot go. I cannot go. That place is not something an incarnation like me can breach."

Taking a deep breath, Heaven Abyss Ancestor said in a low voice, "You might have been fooled by Luo Chen back then. His relationship with Green Parasol Mountain is deeper than you imagine."

The Heavenly Execution City Lord was momentarily stunned.

Just then, Heaven Abyss Ancestor's expression suddenly changed drastically.

"Go!"

Without a second thought, the Heavenly Execution City Lord instinctively fled.

But Heaven Abyss Ancestor unexpectedly remained where he was, forming a hand seal.

"Explode!"

His entire body became suffused with vast mana, beginning to swell wildly.

Having fled several li away, the Heavenly Execution City Lord was extremely puzzled. What kind of existence could make his master's incarnation treat it with such caution, even resorting to self-destruction to block the enemy?

"Send word to Nether Origin: Rosycloud has returned!"

These were the last words left by Heaven Abyss Ancestor's incarnation. Then came a terrifying explosion that swept in all directions, also completely erasing the aura left by the Heavenly Execution City Lord.

Amidst the self-destruction, smoke and dust filled the air. The chaotic origin qi of heaven and earth made it impossible for any living being to approach.

It seemed to have become a dead zone.

Suddenly!

A delicate jade hand materialized out of thin air, reaching gently into the air.

The chaotic origin qi of heaven and earth instantly calmed. A blurry figure emerged in confusion, grasped in the palm of that jade hand.

Whoosh...

A light breeze blew. The mysterious jade hand dissipated, as if nothing had happened.

Only the shattered peak bore witness to the brief yet fierce collision that had occurred here.

...

Inside the cave dwelling, Luo Chen looked curiously at the woman before him.

Her brows were like a painting, her delicate body carried a fragrance—and it was the scent of pills, the one he was most familiar with.

"You are Huamei?"

Huamei smiled gently, making a graceful bow to Luo Chen.

"Your disciple pays respects to her master."

Luo Chen quickly waved his hand. "I am unworthy, unworthy."

Huamei laughed. "Back then, when you publicly refined pills at Green Parasol Mountain and taught us your alchemy techniques, many of us benefited greatly. Especially later, when the Vice Hall Master rewarded me with the Transformation Pill you left behind, I used it to successfully transform and formally embark on the alchemy path. The title of master is not undeserved."

Luo Chen suddenly understood. Indeed, that had happened.

During his time at Green Parasol Mountain, he had often refined pills openly.

But he hadn't expected that someone had actually learned something genuine from it.

Moreover, from the thick pill fragrance on Huamei's body and Nether Spring's casual mention of her judgment on the Transformation Pill, it was clear that Huamei's alchemy skills were considerable.

Suddenly interested, Luo Chen posed a few questions to test her.

Huamei answered fluently, even able to draw inferences and apply them in reverse.

Her overflowing talent far surpassed that of Qu Lingjun, whom Luo Chen had personally taught.

This also made Luo Chen realize that demon beasts were not as foolish and ignorant as imagined. They were merely constrained by their physical forms at low cultivation levels, preventing them from engaging in the myriad arts of cultivation.

The Transformation Pill was truly the most supreme elixir for the demon race!

Once the physical constraints were resolved, their intelligence was not inferior, their lifespans were long, and they could even achieve accomplishments far beyond those of humans at the same cultivation level!

For a moment, Luo Chen's mood became complicated.

"In my brief glance earlier, I saw many figures moving about Green Parasol Mountain, but none had reached the Sage realm, and their auras were not human."

"The Transformation Pills I left behind back then only numbered four."

"These third-rank transformed demon cultivators—are they all your work?"

Huamei smiled gently. "Your guess is correct. I gained the Vice Hall Master's favor, and many personnel from the Truth Crossing Hall were placed at my disposal. The million mountains are filled with countless spiritual herbs. That is how my alchemy skills were able to advance by leaps and bounds."

Luo Chen shook his head, even feeling a hint of envy.

Supported by a holy land, with the million mountains as a medicine garden, and having an alchemy master like himself laying the foundation for her alchemy path—such treatment was truly favored by heaven.

Coupled with her overflowing alchemy talent, she had refined fourth-rank pills in just over a hundred years...

"Wait. The Transformation Pill formula of Green Parasol Mountain requires human Sage essence blood. Where did you get that batch of materials?"

Luo Chen suddenly thought of this question. Had Green Parasol Mountain captured Sage cultivators to use as medicine ingredients?

Had they taken advantage of the chaos during the human-demon war to capture them?

But aside from Azure Frost's occasional one or two interventions, he hadn't heard of any major actions by Green Parasol Mountain!

Even Azure Frost's position as commander was merely a nominal title.

Huamei laughed. "Back then, wasn't a batch of Adept cultivators captured and imprisoned underground? Some of them survived the great war. Later, the Vice Hall Master invested resources to cultivate them into Sage cultivators, specifically for me to draw blood for pill refining."

Hearing this answer, Luo Chen fell silent.

Among those captured with him back then, there had indeed been many Adept cultivators—probably twenty or thirty.

And these people were by no means unknown nobodies. Most were genius figures from various major sects.

There was Wei Wuya, a late-stage Adept great cultivator from Motian Cliff; Yu Jiaolong, a heavenly spirit root from Meng Quan Cave; and the Hundred Flowers Fairy and Food Fragrance Master, who had come from the Joyous Union Sect but established their own sects.

Even among the rogue cultivators, there was Haoranzi, a powerhouse just one step away from the Sage realm.

They had participated in the pioneering war precisely to seize the opportunity to break through to the Sage realm.

Such things were common. Cloud Crane Sage had done it before, and Lin Qingxuan had risen amidst the chaos of war. Even cultivators from holy lands often gave up their comfortable cultivation environments to train in dangerous places.

If anyone had truly survived the great chaos at Green Parasol Mountain, with their talent and Nether Spring's unrestrained investment of resources to solve the Transformation Pill problem, they indeed had the chance to achieve the Sage realm.

But who would want such a Sage realm?

Unable to rule a region and establish a sect, unable to enjoy the prestige of a true Sage, instead being treated as a medicinal ingredient, having blood drawn day after day.

This fate... Luo Chen inexplicably thought of the demon beasts the Luo Tian Society had once raised.

There was the Explosive Ape, from which blood was drawn to refine Blood Baleful Pills.

There was the demon deer, from which milk was extracted to make milk tea.

There were spirit silkworms, from which silk was unraveled to make dharma robes.

In the silence, Luo Chen asked in a low voice, "Who survived?"

Huamei was about to answer when Luo Chen suddenly raised his hand to stop her.

A voice transmission was echoing in Luo Chen's mind.

"The Primordial Lord has awakened. In three days, ascend to the Profound Clarity Hall!"
