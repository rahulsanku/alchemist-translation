# Chapter 560: Return to the Swallow Islands, Blood Nightmare Pays a Visit

Atop a Sala tree, Luo Chen stood at the very crown, gazing toward a distant horizon.

This was already the Xingtian Sea.

At full speed, the Swallow Islands lay no more than two or three days' flight away.

The reason he had stopped here was mainly this: when the teleportation formation on Green Parasol Mountain had sent him here all those years ago, this was precisely where he had landed.

After so many years, he found himself wondering how that section of the formation he had destroyed was faring.

"Do you want to go take a look?" A voice reached his ears.

Luo Chen's expression remained calm. He slowly shook his head.

He was no criminal with a habit of returning to the scene of his crimes to observe the results — only to be caught in the act.

Even though he was genuinely curious about the state of that formation, Luo Chen suppressed the feeling.

His gaze drifted slowly downward.

"I plan to wait here a few days."

"Wait?" Han Zhan asked, puzzled.

After a moment's thought, Han Zhan spoke with dawning understanding: "You're afraid there might be an ambush waiting at the Swallow Islands — something unfavorable laid out for you?"

Luo Chen nodded.

That was exactly his thinking.

The Swallow Islands were his base — but before his arrival, they had nominally been part of the Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance, controlled by the wandering cultivator Yan Nantian.

He had killed Yan Nantian, and more than ten years had since passed. There was no guarantee the Cang Hai Alliance hadn't sent someone to look in on things.

If they were displeased with what he had done and had set an ambush to catch him walking into their trap, that would be trouble.

And then there was Moluo Stream. Wu Qi had come once, trying and failing to recruit him. He had no idea what had been said since.

Six years.

The ten-year deadline Wu Qi had spoken of was drawing closer, and that kind of thing had a way of producing complications.

So he intended to send someone ahead to scout first.

"Heavenjade!"

"What does Master need?"

"Following the directions in this jade token, go take a look at that island cluster. Remember — do not land. There is a grand formation on those islands, and at full activation it would be more than you can handle."

The Swallow Islands held the island-linking formation method passed down from the Cang Hai Alliance. Luo Chen had studied it once.

At full activation, the formation's power was genuinely formidable. A demon beast without formation knowledge — even one a full major realm above — could easily be killed by it.

So he only instructed Heavenjade to circle the area around the Swallow Islands for a full day without concealment.

One day of unguarded circling would draw out any Golden Core or higher cultivators lying in ambush — they would have to act to drive Heavenjade away or kill her.

And if that happened, Luo Chen would feel it.

At that point, he naturally would not walk into the trap himself.

With her orders received, Heavenjade beat her wings and flew toward the Swallow Islands.

Luo Chen descended, approached the thick trunk of the tall Sala tree, and pressed two fingers together like a sword.

As mana flowed in and out, a large hollow was quickly carved into the trunk.

He slipped inside and waited quietly.

The Xingtian Sea was home to many demon beasts, though not primarily sea-dwellers — the region was populated by great flocks of albatrosses.

These creatures were gentle by nature, and so long as one did not provoke them, they generally would not attack.

Of course, if truly provoked, an entire colony of albatrosses was not to be taken lightly.

Once inside the tree hollow, Luo Chen was not idle.

He retrieved the pearl from his storage ring.

This was the physical form left behind by the Hidden Dust Sand called Yuan Yin.

In the years Luo Chen had spent in the Northern Sea, he had never abandoned his habit of reading. He therefore had some familiarity with the commonly encountered materials of this corner of the cultivation world.

Hidden Dust Sand — also known as pearl demon, or sea demon seed.

Though called "demon," their essential nature was that of a stone spirit.

Like wood spirits and fire spirits, creatures born of heaven and earth's essence — by fundamental nature they possessed neither blood nor flesh, but were special beings who had found their own path to the dao.

They were called "demons" primarily because once they developed intelligence, their cultivation method involved parasitizing the flesh of bivalves — giant clams, oysters, and similar shellfish — hence the name.

This material was extraordinarily common throughout the Northern Sea.

Even some physically robust mortal warriors could dive to find first-tier Hidden Dust Sand.

After all, the creatures had essentially no combat ability whatsoever. At first or second tier, they could only hide — once discovered, they were entirely at others' mercy.

But to set that aside.

Because they were so common, their uses had long since been thoroughly catalogued.

As medicine, in treasure-crafting, for laying formations, for talismans — they could even be ground into powder and spread over spirit soil to nourish spirit plants.

Their breadth of application was arguably unmatched among all known materials.

Luo Chen happened to have one or two pill formulas that called for Hidden Dust Sand — though both were quite low-grade.

And especially —

"This Hidden Dust Sand is third-tier Perfected Completion. Even placed among the finest stock of major shops, it would be the most prized item on offer — any ordinary fourth-tier material would fall well short of half its worth."

"To use it for refining low-grade pills would be an unconscionable waste."

Gazing at this Hidden Dust Sand, Luo Chen felt a certain helplessness.

A treasure in hand with no suitable application — truly a happy kind of trouble.

"Senior Han, do you have any suitable method for making use of this Hidden Dust Sand?"

From within the Myriad Souls Banner, Han Zhan said bluntly: "My understanding of this creature is shallow — I know it by sight, nothing more. Ask me how to use it properly, and I genuinely have nothing for you."

Luo Chen frowned and reluctantly set aside this near-perfect-grade Hidden Dust Sand for the time being.

Perhaps by exchanging knowledge with Northern Sea Adept cultivators later, he might find someone with a better approach.

If all else failed — he could refine it into a magic treasure.

Its technique before death, that final outburst of force, bore some resemblance to the jade marrow waste pearls he had used back during his Qi Refinement days. If he were to refine a magic treasure from a Hidden Dust Sand of this quality, the power it could produce would certainly not be weak.

...

The Swallow Islands.

This isolated corner of the world, after passing through several upheavals, had finally found a brief period of peace once Devil Lord Azure Yang arrived.

But beneath that peace, a festering problem remained.

In order to obtain the Core Formation secret art from the Azure Yang Alchemy Canon, the great clans had spent their resources like men possessed.

In the short term this was manageable, but as time stretched on, some clans had begun to buckle under the strain.

Especially given Devil Lord Azure Yang's demands — boundless as a bottomless pit — and no one could understand why a Golden Core cultivator would need to consume so many resources.

None of the other Golden Core cultivators they'd heard of were this resource-hungry.

Still, that frenzied pace had gradually eased in the six years since Devil Lord Azure Yang's departure.

With the principal party absent, no matter how much they tried to ingratiate themselves, the effort was wasted.

And so the Swallow Islands had enjoyed six years of quiet.

That quiet, however, had recently been broken.

A demon king-level Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull had appeared somewhere in the area — drawn, it seemed, to the Swallow Islands — and had been lingering in its proximity without leaving.

With a demon king's aura flooding the surroundings, the island cultivators were on high alert.

The leaders of the great clans had quickly reached a unified decision: activate the island-defense formation.

If that Fighting Gull tried to land, they would cut it down without mercy.

But strangely, the Fighting Gull showed remarkable intelligence — it simply circled outside the islands, never attempting to land.

Because the creature moved with extraordinary speed, the island cultivators dared not take the initiative to activate the formation's killing arrays. If they failed to kill the demon and enraged it instead, that would only invite a worse disaster.

On Black Swan Island.

Over thirty Initiate cultivators had gathered to discuss the crisis before them.

"That bird has been circling for nearly a full day. What does it want?"

"Could there be something in our territory attracting it?"

"That seems unlikely. The Swallow Islands are resource-poor — nothing of note but a third-tier spirit vein. But the spirit qi overflow from that land has been masked by Immortal Venerable Azure Yang's formation array. A demon king of this caliber shouldn't be able to sense it."

"Then what does it want? It won't come in, it won't leave — just lingering outside and watching like a tiger eyeing its prey."

After considerable heated discussion, one weathered, steady elder shifted his gaze toward the woman in palace robes seated at the head of the assembly.

"Invitation Moon Fairy — what do you think we should do?"

The Invitation Moon Fairy, among the assembled clan-head level cultivators, was a relative newcomer.

But no one dared underestimate her.

Beyond the terrifying talent she had displayed — reaching Foundation Establishment Stage Five in barely ten-odd years of cultivation — what mattered most was the figure she represented.

Invitation Moon Island. Devil Lord Azure Yang.

Now, under everyone's gaze, Cheng Haixin smoothed her hair and showed a small, rueful smile.

"Fellow daoists are too kind. Against a great demon of this caliber, I have no more means to handle it than anyone else here."

Seeing her deflect, the others exchanged looks of skepticism.

"Surely — before Immortal Venerable Azure Yang departed, he left you some offensive measures, did he not?"

"Even if he did, how could they be spent on a single demon king — and moreover, one that hasn't actually attacked us?" said the Invitation Moon Fairy, deflecting the inquiry.

She understood perfectly. They were testing her.

Whether Devil Lord Azure Yang had left behind any measures was the question that interested these clan heads most.

With the Devil Lord gone so long, Invitation Moon Island was masterless land — and everyone wanted a share.

Especially with that stone stele containing the Azure Yang Alchemy Canon sitting there.

In the crowd, someone sighed: "If only our Swallow Islands had a true, locally-born Golden Core cultivator."

"Indeed — what a pity about Fellow Daoist Cheng Dou. He was the most promising candidate for Core Formation, and in the end he failed at the final step, dying on the spot."

"I heard he was too impatient?"

"He cultivated the Azure Yang Alchemy Canon for only ten years before attempting Core Formation in haste. He must have rushed it and suffered the backlash."

"In that case — doesn't the Azure Yang Alchemy Canon itself have flaws?"

Watching the conversation drift further and further astray, Cheng Haixin's expression grew colder and colder.

Over these years, the great clans' dissatisfaction with the Black Swan Cheng clan had deepened steadily.

When Devil Lord Azure Yang was present, no one had dared show a trace of it.

But with the Devil Lord gone so long and no sign of his return — and her eldest brother, a Foundation Establishment Perfected Completion cultivator, having failed Core Formation and died on the spot — the Cheng clan had lost its most powerful senior cultivator.

Over time, the resentments that had built up in these clans' hearts had begun to show themselves.

Testing whether the Devil Lord had left behind any measures. Discussing her late brother openly in front of her face. Even beginning to cast doubt on the Azure Yang Alchemy Canon itself — all of this was a form of provocation.

*If only the Devil Lord were still here.*

Taking a slow breath, the woman said in a cool voice: "Fellow daoists — let us focus on the matter of that Fighting Gull."

She forcibly redirected the conversation.

"With a demon king lingering alongside us, refusing to leave — that is bad for every clan's daily operations."

"Whether it is internal cultivation, work at sea, or traveling commerce — none of it can proceed."

"Until this problem is resolved, every one of us sits in a cage."

The faces in the assembly grew equally grim.

Finally, someone proposed: "What if we activate the formation and force the kill?"

That was the final option.

Once taken —

Just as everyone was wavering, the door opened and a cultivator stepped inside.

"It's gone!"

"The demon king — it suddenly left!"

The assembly froze.

Then everyone filed out together, flew into the sky, and scanned the horizon in all directions — and indeed, not a trace of the great demon remained.

How strange.

They exchanged glances all around.

But even with the demon king gone, the clans did not dare relax their guard.

The restriction on leaving was not lifted immediately. The island formation remained drawn but unloosed, ready to respond at any moment.

If the demon king had done this deliberately — lulling them into lowering their guard before returning to strike — they needed to be ready to react.

But clearly —

This state of tension could not continue indefinitely.

No one had the stamina for it.

And so, in that stifling atmosphere —

On the third day.

Suddenly, a bird's cry rang out from the sky above the harbor.

A sharp cry that carried in every direction, startling the many Initiate cultivators who were already on edge.

Over a hundred Initiate cultivators of the Swallow Islands mobilized as one, rising into the sky.

The demon king had returned — and this time, it seemed there would truly be no avoiding battle.

A Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull, the most aggressive and battle-hardened of all sea-going demon birds — once the fighting broke out, the casualties would not be few.

In the midst of this general dread, Cheng Haixin at the front suddenly brightened.

"That's—"

Before anyone else could react, she flew directly out past the formation's defensive boundary toward the Fighting Gull.

"Haixin respectfully welcomes the Immortal Venerable's return!"

Only then did everyone notice: atop that enormous demon bird's back stood a figure in white daoist robes.

The figure's aura was perfectly concealed — still as a piece of dead wood, calling no attention to itself whatsoever.

In an ordinary moment, one might have taken him for a mortal.

But clearly — if he could make a demon king bow as his mount, he was anything but mortal.

The assembled cultivators were thunderstruck.

The Devil Lord had gone out once, and returned with a Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull as his mount — that alone was beyond their imagination.

His cultivation had surely advanced considerably from before.

In that moment, every cultivator without hesitation flew out from the islands to welcome Devil Lord Azure Yang's return.

Faced with the waves of voices welcoming the Devil Lord, Luo Chen's expression remained cool.

"Disperse."

With that word, he released a flow of mana, swept up Cheng Haixin, and rode the Fighting Gull directly toward the Ten-Li Flat Lake at the center of the islands.

Upon arrival, he shook his wrist.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron expanded — and from within, a black jiaolong swam out.

Demon qi surging, the Black-Scale Jiaolong's form expanded and stretched to its full length, and with the sheer pleasure of it let out an involuntary roar.

"ROAR!"

Quite unable to help itself.

Watching Black King swim into the Ten-Li Flat Lake, while Heavenjade settled on Invitation Moon Island to preen her feathers, Luo Chen finally felt the tension in his chest ease.

Home at last.

Cheng Haixin watched the scene, her eyes twitching.

A Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull was shock enough.

The Devil Lord had actually also tamed a jiaolong.

The former leader of the Jiaolong Gang, Kun Jiao, had owned a black jiaolong as well — but that one had been quite ordinary in strength; her late eldest brother Cheng Dou had not even been particularly afraid of it.

Nothing like this creature, whose massive form and vigorous aura spoke plainly of something not to be trifled with.

Even as she was turning this over in her mind, Luo Chen turned to face her.

"These past few years — have there been any major events on the islands?"

Cheng Haixin's expression became serious. "Nothing of great scale — but someone did come to pay you a visit."

"Who?"

"That person called themselves Blood Nightmare. I could not determine their exact realm, but I dared not look at them directly — I imagine they are extraordinarily powerful."

*Blood Nightmare.*

Luo Chen's expression shifted slightly.

That such a figure had personally come calling was genuinely unexpected.

*(End of Chapter)*
