# Chapter 1092: Human Strength Has Its Limits, Heaven's Mandate Has Its End

Li Yingzhang's successful Nascent Soul formation seemed like a signal.

It was a signal of his accumulated strength finally bursting forth, and also a signal of the surging tide of powerful cultivators within Luo Tian Sect.

Three years later, the heavenly omen appeared again!

Unexpectedly, this time the one forming a Nascent Soul was the youngest among them all—Meng Qiner.

Born in Fenghua Palace, she had always been an exceptionally talented individual, having successfully formed her core at a mere hundred years old.

Ever since becoming Dao companions with Qu Lingjun, she had followed her husband wherever he went, coming to Luo Tian Sect to cultivate. Of course, she hadn't completely severed ties with Fenghua Palace. It could be said that this woman played a very important role in the close relationship between Luo Tian Sect and Fenghua Palace.

After arriving at Luo Tian Sect, her training never stopped. All kinds of resources were allocated according to the standard configuration for Adept cultivators.

In fact, perhaps because she was the Dao companion of his disciple Qu Lingjun, Luo Chen showed a bit of favoritism, specially granting her a true artifact with a lower refinement threshold but excellent quality to help her through the heavenly tribulation.

The final result lived up to expectations.

Under the gaze of countless onlookers, the valiant and spirited Meng Qiner successfully passed the heavenly tribulation, becoming the ninth Sage of Luo Tian Sect.

This number excluded body cultivator Wang Yuan, ghost cultivator Xiaobai, and wild beasts like Black King Xuanyuan Turtle, counting only those who had condensed Nascent Souls through the path of qi refinement, including the demon cultivator Tianxuan.

Nine was the ultimate number!

Even the most top-tier Nascent Soul superior sects could hardly possess more than this number of Sages.

And Luo Tian Sect had passed through this checkpoint almost without pause.

Another two years later, the Nascent Soul heavenly omen appeared again.

Qin Yuanjiang successfully formed his Nascent Soul!

The son of the former acquaintance Qin Liangchen, he had left home at a young age to join Falling Cloud Sect. During the great war between humans and demons, he joined Luo Tian Sect because of his mother Murong Qinglian.

At over four hundred years old, he finally became the tenth Sage of Luo Tian Sect!

Three consecutive successful Nascent Soul formations stirred no small waves within the Alchemy Saint Blessed Land, and when the news spread out, the entire Luo Tian Sect was set ablaze with excitement.

Sect Master Luo Lingxi had even planned to hold a grand Nascent Soul ceremony for the three newly promoted Sage elders.

Such a grand event naturally couldn't be celebrated behind closed doors; it had to be announced to the world.

This was also a manifestation of Luo Tian Sect's current strength!

But it seemed that good fortune came to an abrupt halt there.

The fourth disciple attempting to condense a Nascent Soul failed.

He was a Adept cultivator from Misty Peak, the most valued successor of Lingfengzi, practicing the ultimate techniques of the former Misty Sect.

Before attempting Nascent Soul formation, he had been personally guided by Lingfengzi.

But in the end, it was Lingfengzi who carried the disciple's corpse out of the cave dwelling.

"I had actually expected this long ago. In the old days, the Misty Sect's Sages were always passed down in a single line, rarely having two Sages coexisting. The techniques of the Misty lineage naturally have strong life-preserving abilities, but they are too difficult in terms of realm breakthroughs."

In the Dan Chen Hall, Lingfengzi looked desolate, occasionally coughing as he spoke.

That was an injury he had sustained when following Luo Chen to attack the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, facing off against a Sage of that sect. The injury had damaged his Purple Mansion, and medicine could not cure it; he could only slowly nurture it with his own mana.

He looked at Luo Chen, who sat silently above, and let out a faint sigh.

"Perhaps I was too stubborn, insisting on continuing the Misty Sect's inheritance. If I had let Guangyao absorb more of Luo Tian Sect's cultivation methods, perhaps he wouldn't have gone to such extremes."

Luo Chen comforted him, "It was only a single misstep. You need not take it so hard."

Lingfengzi shook his head. "No, I've already made up my mind. I can no longer work behind closed doors. I will contribute the Misty Sect's inheritance to be included in Luo Tian Sect's Scripture Pavilion and Transmission Hall. From now on, Misty Peak will only be an ordinary mountain peak within Luo Tian Sect."

Hearing this, Luo Chen actually felt relieved.

In this way, Misty Peak, which had set up its own independent faction within Luo Tian Sect, would be completely integrated into the sect, no longer maintaining its special status.

This would also be a great advantage for Luo Lingxi in controlling the sect in the future.

Just as the two were talking, Qi Yi hurriedly arrived outside the hall.

"Supreme Elder, this disciple has a matter to request an audience."

"Come in!"

Soon, Qi Yi entered with a worried expression.

Luo Chen stopped his conversation with Lingfengzi and looked at Qi Yi with a gentle expression. "What's the urgency? What matter do you have?"

Qi Yi bowed deeply, then spoke with some concern, "Does the Supreme Elder still remember Sang Jinghe?"

"Him..." Luo Chen thought for a moment, then smiled. "I naturally remember. What about him?"

Qi Yi's next words made the smile disappear from Luo Chen's face.

Among this batch of Adept cultivators attempting Nascent Soul formation was Sang Jinghe.

He was a descendant of an old acquaintance Luo Chen had brought back from the Northern Sea. His learning originated from Sang Jiugong, and he was proficient in the path of spiritual plant cultivation.

After arriving at Luo Tian Sect, he got along well with the alchemists.

Qi Yi was his close friend.

And this time, he had resolutely decided to enter a death-seclusion to break through to the Nascent Soul realm.

Qi Yi was quite concerned about his friend's movements, frequently going to the vicinity of the other's secluded cave dwelling.

About half a year ago, a faint spiritual energy convergence phenomenon had appeared near Sang Jinghe's cave, but it came and went quickly.

Perhaps influenced by Misty Peak's Cui Guangyao's failed Nascent Soul formation, Qi Yi had recently felt increasingly uneasy, so he had come to seek Luo Chen's help.

"Jinghe set up the most rigorous formation around his cave for his death-seclusion."

"I don't know if he's still in the preparation stage or if something has gone wrong, so I wanted to ask someone to enter and investigate."

That formation is too tight. If someone else barges in rashly, it might disturb him. Therefore, I beg the Supreme Elder to lend a hand.

Before Qi Yi could say more, Luo Chen had already stood up.

"Lead the way!"

Qi Yi breathed a sigh of relief and quickly flew out of the hall, heading toward Sang Jinghe's secluded cave.

Behind him, Luo Chen and Lingfengzi followed instantly.

"You're worried about that junior?"

"He is, after all, a descendant of an old acquaintance and has helped me a great deal in the past. His aptitude is not particularly good. Back then, he formed his core relying on the Condensed Liquid Pill I refined. I am not optimistic about his chances of breaking through the Nascent Soul realm."

"Since that's the case, why didn't you stop him... Hah, I spoke out of turn."

How could one stop him?

For a cultivator to break through to Nascent Soul was already a matter of nine deaths and one life.

Once a decision was made, it was a commitment to the Great Dao—success or death.

It was difficult for others to intervene.

Just like Qi Yi, who felt his Dao heart was not firm enough and his foundation not thick enough, he simply didn't attempt it, lest he end up in the miserable state of body and Dao perishing together. Others could hardly persuade him to enter seclusion and attempt a breakthrough.

Soon.

Three figures landed in a picturesque medicinal garden filled with lush vegetation.

This was the Spiritual Medicine Garden.

Sang Jinghe's cultivation ground, and also Luo Tian Sect's main center for high-level spiritual plant cultivation.

Outside the Spiritual Medicine Garden, a group of low-level disciples had gathered.

These were all the direct disciples Sang Jinghe had taken in over the years, mostly inheriting his spiritual plant cultivation techniques.

At this moment, everyone was filled with worry, yet also tinged with a hint of hope.

"The Supreme Elder is here!"

Amidst exclamations, the crowd made way.

Luo Chen stepped forward and, under everyone's gaze, entered the Spiritual Medicine Garden.

Inside, paths crisscrossed, and medicinal plots lay in patches.

Various medicinal herbs, all familiar to Luo Chen, were planted in individual plots, separated by formations.

These herbs were all well-cultivated, with appropriate formations, the best soil, and even companion plants specially configured.

It was evident that Sang Jinghe had put great effort into these matters.

Luo Chen walked silently, all the way to a thatched cottage.

That was Sang Jinghe's "cave dwelling."

He worked daily in the garden, cultivated and refined qi there, and ultimately chose to enter seclusion for his breakthrough here.

At this moment, the formation's spiritual light swirled incessantly, enveloping the entire thatched cottage, preventing outsiders from disturbing it.

Even a Sage's divine sense could not penetrate it.

However, the moment Luo Chen arrived, the look of dismay on his face was already clearly visible.

"Alas..."

With a soft sigh, Luo Chen cast a few hand seals and, under everyone's expectant gaze, walked inside.

A moment later, the formation covering the garden suddenly dissipated.

Seeing this, everyone was stunned for a moment, but before they could feel joy, they realized what it meant.

If Sang Jinghe were still in seclusion cultivation, the Supreme Elder would certainly not have removed his formation.

There was only one possibility for this situation.

"Old friend Jinghe!" Qi Yi rushed inside.

Behind him came a chorus of wails and lamentations.

"Master..."

Inside the thatched cottage, after Luo Chen entered, the vast spiritual energy seemed to find an outlet, gushing out of the cottage.

A weathered old man sat cross-legged on a cushion, his face twisted, his expression terrified, as if he had seen something dreadful.

Lingfengzi took two steps closer and sighed regretfully.

"His spirit merged with his qi, and his qi scattered with his essence. He fell at the final step of the Three Treasures Unification, and it seems he encountered a rare heart demon tribulation at that moment, unable to withdraw his spirit, failing even to preserve his life."

"Accept my condolences..."

With just one glance, Lingfengzi had judged the general circumstances of Sang Jinghe's failure.

Luo Chen stood silently by a table inside the room. On it lay three jade slips.

A sweep of his divine sense revealed their contents.

One recorded his lifelong insights into spiritual plant cultivation, along with various exquisite spiritual plant techniques, offered to the sect.

One recorded a cultivation method for a type of incense flower called Chenxiang Hua, a fourth-order spiritual plant that had already gone extinct, specifically used for refining certain pills. It was the subject of Qi Yi's deepest research into fourth-order pills. He had once consulted Luo Chen, but Luo Chen, troubled by the difficulty of cooking without ingredients, had thought of several substitute main materials, but none were as effective as Chenxiang Hua. Clearly, this jade slip was a gift for his good friend Qi Yi.

The final jade slip...

Luo Chen held it in his hand and murmured, "You have a thoughtful heart."

He solemnly placed this jade slip into his storage ring, then turned and left the thatched cottage.

As more disciples surged in, the cries of grief behind him grew louder.

A generation of spiritual plant masters from Luo Tian Sect had fallen.

...

Due to the consecutive failed breakthroughs and deaths of Misty Peak's Cui Guangyao and Spiritual Medicine Garden's Sang Jinghe, the immense joy brought by the successful Nascent Soul formations of Li Yingzhang and the other two was completely washed away.

The people of Luo Tian Sect grew increasingly worried about the remaining few still in seclusion, attempting to break through to the Nascent Soul realm.

Seclusion was not a matter of the longer, the better.

They were not rogue cultivators; they had all undergone the sect's training and received personal guidance from Luo Chen, Wang Yuan, Lingfengzi, and other high-level powerhouses.

Once they decided to enter seclusion to break through to a realm, it had to be a single, concentrated effort. A second attempt would be weaker, and a third would be exhausted.

The longer the time, the greater the chance of failure!

Among those remaining were the rising star Su Wen, a former true disciple of the Divine Talisman Sect who had brought his skills to join Luo Tian Sect, and Yang Pingdu, once the leader of the Myriad Manifestations Sect and now an elder of Luo Tian Sect.

But the one who drew the most attention was none other than Luo Tian Sect's first sect master, the Dao companion of the Supreme Elder, and the mother of the current sect master—Sima Huiniang.

Would they succeed?

...

"Huiniang, although you are the second child, your steady temperament surpasses your elder brother, and your strength and talent surpass your younger brother. In the future, you must help your brothers more. The three of you siblings must advance and retreat together!"

"In the future, I will be the Supreme Elder, and you will be the Sect Master. I am at ease entrusting Luo Tian Sect to you."

"Mother, why can't Lingxi cultivate like them?"

...

"Second Sister, why should that Nether Origin Pill go to Yang Pingdu? Is my foundation inferior to his? Or are my contributions less than his? I am your own blood brother!"

"Old Sect Master, how cruel you are, using us to nurture your son!"

"You are ultimately inferior to me. Only I remain by our husband's side. Admit it, if you hadn't seized the opportunity back then, there would have been no place for you by our husband's side. Give up. Why torment yourself? Luo Lingxi can already cultivate now, and has even become the fourth-generation Sect Master. What obsession do you still hold? End it. If you go, I will take care of our husband, and Luo Lingxi's only stain will depart with you..."

...

In the silent cave dwelling, a gray stone mirror hung high, emitting a hazy spiritual light.

Under that spiritual light's illumination, everything should have been peaceful.

But the mature woman's cheeks still shifted in expression.

Her entire aura was restless and unbearable, the surrounding spiritual energy boiling like water, washing over the woman's body again and again.

Vaguely, a figure stood silently in the corner.

That person's presence was extremely low. Even if someone used divine sense to sweep, eyes to look, or nose to sniff nearby, they would not discover his existence.

Crack!

In the silent cave dwelling, a clearly audible breaking sound suddenly came.

The silently standing figure moved abruptly.

He pointed a finger at the mature woman's brow, and with a low shout that sounded like a great bell or a huge drum.

"Awaken!"

Boom!

The surrounding spiritual energy receded like a tide.

Terrifying yet gentle mana surged into the woman's body in an instant, suppressing the restless blood qi and the broken golden core.

"Pfft..."

A mouthful of blood spat out, and the woman opened her eyes with difficulty.

In her pupils was reflected a familiar face full of worry.

She smiled bitterly, "Husband, I have failed you."

As soon as the words fell, the woman fell unconscious.

Luo Chen held the woman's still trembling body, his gentle mana repeatedly smoothing over the terrible condition inside her. But no matter how he soothed, the horrifying cracks on the golden core in her qi sea continued to grow larger.

Luo Chen's lips moved, but in the end, he only let out a soft sigh.

"Living is what matters, that's what matters..."

...

One year later.

Above the Nether Abyss.

A figure flew rapidly. Reaching the middle point, the figure suddenly descended, deploying a dharma boat to carry him straight into the abyss.

Soon, the young figure burst into the Alchemy Saint Blessed Land.

The two early-stage Adept cultivators guarding the gate were startled when they saw the newcomer, as if they hadn't expected him to appear suddenly.

But the two still respectfully bowed.

"Greetings, Sect Master!"

"Greetings, Sect Master!"

Luo Lingxi suppressed his urgent mood, nodded to the two, and flew toward the Dan Chen Hall.

Behind him, the two men saw this and roughly understood why he was so anxious.

The Old Sect Master had failed in her Nascent Soul formation!

Luo Lingxi flew at great speed, not bothering to conserve mana, heading straight for the Dan Chen Hall.

Before he even set foot on that crimson mountain peak, he saw two figures leaning against each other on a corner of the mountain, watching the setting sun.

He had the urge to rush over directly, no longer maintaining the composure and steadiness of a sect master.

But looking at those two backs, he unconsciously stopped in his tracks.

Only a wetness spread in his eyes.

As if sensing something, the woman in the man's arms let out a voice as faint as a mosquito's hum.

"Is Lingxi back?"

The man, whose hair had turned white at some unknown time and whose face was full of wrinkles, turned his head.

"Come here!"

Luo Lingxi walked over with difficulty, step by step.

"Father, Mother, why did you hide this from me?"

The old woman, looking aged and with a faint aura, smiled kindly. Reaching out to rub the head of the young man kneeling beside her, she said, "Don't blame your father. It was my idea. You have only recently succeeded to the position of Sect Master. Every day, every moment, every step, every action affects your authority as Sect Master. Even if you came back, what good would it do?"

"But, but..." Luo Lingxi's lips trembled. He instinctively cast his pleading gaze upon the towering mountain he had looked up to his entire life. "Father, is there truly nothing you can do?"

Luo Chen was silent.

Human strength had its limits; this was Heaven's Mandate.

Sima Huiniang was already several decades older than him. He himself was over four hundred years old, so what of her?

Especially since she had suffered a great injury in her early years. Even though he had spent immense resources to extend her life, she had ultimately reached the limit of a Adept's lifespan.

The failure of Nascent Soul formation had only accelerated everything.

Even the Beauty Preservation Pill could no longer maintain the decline of blood qi and the change in appearance brought on by the decay of mana.

In fact, in this final year, Luo Chen had forcibly maintained her vital signs, only adding to her suffering.

Sima Huiniang smiled and tapped Luo Lingxi on the head.

"Don't rely on your father for everything. You've grown up now."

"The path ahead depends on you."

"You've finally made something of yourself. I'm very happy, but I regret that I won't be able to see the day you achieve Nascent Soul, or even Ascension."

"What a pity..."

The woman's voice gradually weakened, until her hand gently fell.

Luo Lingxi knelt heavily, unable to speak through his sobs.

Luo Chen silently watched all this, only holding the body in his arms a little tighter.

(End of this chapter)
