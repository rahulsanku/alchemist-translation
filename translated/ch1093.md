# Chapter 1093: Truth Crossing, Ascending to Divinity; Law-Derivation, Soul-Scouring

The funeral was held at the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land.

The scale was not grand, after all, the sect was currently in a phase of rapid development, striving toward the position of the number one sect in the Eastern Wilderness.

But everyone who should come, came.

All the high-ranking members of the Luo Tian Sect, whether on stage or behind the scenes.

Even those cultivators who had already reached the Sage realm all rushed back to participate in this funeral, to see Sima Huiniang off on her final journey.

Such solemnity was not only because she was Luo Chen's Dao companion, but also because she was the founding sect master of the Luo Tian Sect!

She was the sect master who had led the Luo Tian Sect, which at that time had only a handful of Initiate true cultivators, all the way to becoming a major Adept sect.

She was also the heroine of that chaotic era who, when the demon invasion scattered people's hearts, gritted her teeth and twisted the leaderless Luo Tian Sect into a single rope, holding on until Luo Chen's return!

Although she had made some muddled decisions later on, they could never overshadow her contributions to the sect.

Before the grave.

Figures stepped forward one after another to pay their respects, and among them were not a few sighs of regret.

Even Gu Caiyi, who had competed with her for a lifetime and had once become Sima Huiniang's heart demon during her Sage formation, could only let out a single sigh before the grave.

Not knowing what to say, she inserted a stick of incense and silently withdrew from the crowd.

Outside the crowd, Luo Chen, dressed in black with a silent expression, stood alone under a tree, lost in thought.

Unlike his usual young and handsome appearance, over the past year Luo Chen's hair had grown white at the temples, and many wrinkles had appeared on his face, making him look no different from a middle-aged man.

Gu Caiyi walked to his side and said softly, "Husband, please accept my condolences."

Luo Chen pressed his lips together and said nothing.

Grief?

A little, but far less than one might imagine.

Perhaps it was the moment Huiniang failed to form her Sage core that he had guessed this day would come.

Or perhaps, long before that, he had anticipated that similar things would happen, and keep happening!

If he wanted to advance, it was inevitable that some would be unable to keep up.

In the past, it had been his subordinates, his disciples, his friends, and even his enemies. This time, it was merely his family, one of his closest.

His gaze fell on Gu Caiyi, then shifted to the crowd of mourners not far away.

There, too, were his old friends, his juniors, and his descendants.

Would it be their turn in the future?

Amidst the crowd, the sound of sobbing echoed continuously.

Huiniang's personal disciple, Yao Mingyue, led a group of disciples and grand-disciples to pay their respects.

She was the most sorrowful among them. Sima Huiniang had been like a teacher and a mother to her, not only teaching her cultivation but also teaching her how to conduct herself. Her passing now was heartbreaking.

But this atmosphere of grief would eventually dissipate with time.

Even now, as the mourners and condolence-givers gradually left, the first rays of the morning sun began to dispel some of the sorrow.

Among the sparse crowd, a figure moved against the current.

It was an ordinary person with no spiritual energy at all, dressed in coarse gray linen, attracting no attention whatsoever.

If not for the fact that he was moving against the flow of the crowd, no one would have noticed him.

"How did a mortal climb this mountain?"

Someone was curious, but before they could get to the bottom of it, they were pulled away by an older generation Luo Tian Sect cultivator.

Before the grave.

Luo Lingxi looked at the newcomer with confusion, especially scrutinizing the face that bore a slight resemblance to her mother.

"You are..."

The newcomer lit a stick of incense and bowed solemnly three times before the tombstone.

"Sister, go in peace!"

Finally, he raised his head, carefully examined Luo Lingxi, then looked past the grave toward the man in the distance who was watching him.

Their eyes met, and the gray-clad man averted his gaze.

He hesitated, then reached out and patted Luo Lingxi on the shoulder.

"Work hard. Don't let your mother down."

Luo Lingxi could have avoided that pat. With her Adept realm cultivation, she certainly could have, but for some reason, she didn't.

Because that pat was filled with the concern and admonition of an elder.

As the gray-clad man turned and walked down the mountain with a lonely air, Luo Lingxi was still bewildered.

"Who is he?"

...

"Sima Wenjie is still alive?"

Gu Caiyi looked at the gray-clad man's retreating figure in surprise, as if she hadn't expected his appearance or his existence.

Luo Chen said calmly, "He has always been alive. Under Wang Yuan's nose, in the Scripture Pavilion."

Gu Caiyi was stunned. "But wasn't he condemned as a criminal, his cultivation abolished?"

Luo Chen did not explain much.

With his insight, he could naturally see that Sima Wenjie seemed to have encountered another opportunity. Even though his qi sea was empty and devoid of any mana, that ordinary body seemed as if it could acquire boundless power at any moment.

But ultimately, it had nothing to do with him.

The mistakes Sima Wenjie had made back then had long since dissipated with the severe punishment.

Now that Huiniang had also passed away, there was even less reason to pursue the matter.

Luo Chen was not afraid that Sima Wenjie would cause trouble again in the future. The Luo Tian Sect had plenty of strong cultivators. The sect master, Luo Lingxi, was his own nephew. Whether in terms of strength or sentiment, Sima Wenjie should be able to weigh things clearly.

Letting out a turbid breath, Luo Chen took a step forward.

With that step, his graying temples returned to their original state, the wrinkles on his face vanished, and his once young and handsome countenance reappeared.

And on his body, an aura that had been dormant for over thirty years once again spread out, even stronger than before, carrying a hint of profound mystery.

His injuries had healed.

Looking at his back, Gu Caiyi seemed to realize something and called out instinctively.

"Husband!"

Without turning his head, Luo Chen said, "I am leaving. The sect has been handed over to Lingxi. The Blessed Land will be under your care."

Gu Caiyi's face showed reluctance as she murmured, "Husband..."

Luo Chen's gentle voice came again. "Don't worry. On the day I truly leave, I will return."

This was a contradictory statement.

To return on the day of leaving—ordinary people would not understand its meaning.

But Gu Caiyi understood. Biting her lip, she bowed gracefully to Luo Chen's retreating back.

"Your wife wishes you success in attaining Divinity and a prosperous path of immortality!"

...

That day.

Amidst the grief of the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land, a brief, unnoticed tremor appeared.

Two figures flew out from the Blessed Land, emerged from the surface of the Nether Abyss Sea, and bowed respectfully before Luo Chen.

"Master, the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion has been retrieved. The Demon-Devil Heart-Linking Tree and the Black Obsidian Lotus Pod have both been transplanted inside."

Tianxuan spoke, holding a small building with an octagonal, upturned eave.

Luo Chen glanced at it. "Give me the Black Obsidian Lotus Pod. You take care of the Heart-Linking Tree for now. I may not have much time in the coming days."

"Understood!"

Black King rolled over, stretching his massive body out before Luo Chen.

Luo Chen stepped onto it, and Tianxuan followed closely behind.

"Let's go!"

Soon after.

Within the Luo Tian Sect's mountain gate in Shangyou City, a beam of brilliant light shot into the sky, then briefly dissipated.

Those in the know could tell at a glance that it was the sign of a teleportation array being activated, and judging by its scale, it must have been one of those large-scale arrays.

They couldn't help but marvel that the Luo Tian Sect was becoming more and more prosperous, now even in possession of the kind of large teleportation array that only the most top-tier major powers could have.

And far, far away from the Luo Tian Sect, deep within the millions of mountains of the Eastern Wilderness.

A familiar humming sound arose.

Pure Crane's eyes lit up as she arrived early to wait outside the array.

When three figures appeared on the array platform, she bowed deeply.

"Pure Crane pays respects to the Hall Master!"

Luo Chen walked down from the high platform, asking questions as he headed out.

Pure Crane answered them one by one, her expression slightly serious.

"Since the three of them, including Rosycloud, ascended, the changes at Green Parasol Mountain have indeed been great."

"Ox Ghost stays in his territory. He has faithfully carried out the orders I sent, clearing out some small demon tribes, but he has not agreed to come to Green Parasol Mountain for an audience. There are quite a few demon cultivators like him, such as Blood Vulture Daoist, White-Browed Immortal Ape, and so on."

Luo Chen nodded slightly. "Heeding orders but not summons. Expected."

Pure Crane sighed. "It would be one thing if the others were just watching, but Kuangjuan has left. Not only did he leave Green Parasol Mountain, but he also abandoned his original territory and disappeared into the millions of mountains. I suspect he may have gone to seek out the Divine Heaven lineage."

"The Divine Heaven lineage, Jinxiu and the others?" Luo Chen frowned, but then relaxed. "No matter. They are just insignificant remnants. What about Bi Zheng?"

At the mention of Bi Zheng, Pure Crane's expression turned grave.

"Celestial Wolf Mountain!"

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow. "So he's gone to Aokuang's territory!"

Pure Crane nodded. "Yes. He was always close to Aokuang. Whether submitting to the Ancient Demon Divine Heaven or to Green Parasol Mountain, it was only temporary fealty. In his heart, he has always revered Celestial Wolf Aokuang!"

Celestial Wolf Aokuang, the lord of all wolfkind!

Even the once-proud Wolf Emperor Aoxiao was merely a junior he had taken a liking to.

This man had been famous for a long time, even before Rosycloud, and had once contended with Divine Heaven before he attained the Dao.

Most of the demon clans in the millions of mountains were his juniors and had witnessed his might.

It was only natural for Bi Zheng to revere him.

Now that Divine Heaven had fallen and Rosycloud had ascended, with no Ascendant power sitting in the Eastern Wilderness human race, Bi Zheng would rather go and pledge himself to Aokuang than submit to Luo Chen.

Luo Chen was not surprised. He merely asked about another person.

"Moon Slave?" Pure Crane hesitated, looking somewhat puzzled. "She has made no moves in the Tongxuan Hall. She is obedient and well-behaved."

Luo Chen gave a light laugh.

At a time like this, being so obedient was the greatest sign of disobedience.

He did not ask too much, merely learning from Pure Crane about the demon cultivators still stationed at Green Parasol Mountain.

The main one was the Thunder Prison Divine Peng, supplemented by the two sisters, Huamei and Qinghe, as well as Pure Crane herself.

As for the Whitebone Mystic Serpent, he had been severely injured in the battle at Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain and was recuperating at the Great Snowy Mountains, not coming to Green Parasol Mountain.

"Green Parasol Mountain has become quite desolate!"

Luo Chen sighed, his figure already having left the teleportation array's underground palace.

Soon after.

Having received the news, Thunder Peng, Huamei, and the others rushed to the Truth Crossing Hall to pay their respects to Luo Chen.

The once arrogant and unruly Thunder Peng was now sincerely convinced by Luo Chen. Even after Rosycloud's ascension, he still chose to acknowledge Luo Chen as the master of the Truth Crossing Hall.

Fairy Huamei was nominally Luo Chen's registered disciple, having inherited part of his alchemy.

As for the Qinghe sisters...

"Since Qinghe Immortal has ascended, why haven't you returned to your territory like the others? Why stay at Green Parasol Mountain?"

Hezhi bowed respectfully to Luo Chen.

"The Qinghe clan was originally promoted by Rosycloud, which allowed us to gain a foothold in the millions of mountains. Now that Rosycloud has ascended and our ancestor has left, if we do not wish to become cultivation resources for both humans and demons, we naturally must attach ourselves to the Hall Master."

The Qinghe clan were plants that had gained sentience.

Although they were born from the creation of heaven and earth and the essence of the sun and moon, their combat power was low, making them almost the weakest among their peers.

Low-grade Qinghe demons were often used as firewood for alchemy and artifact forging. Back in the day, Luo Chen had done this quite a bit.

But high-grade, sentient Qinghe demons had even more peculiar uses.

Their essence, a treasure of wood and grass, could both heal hidden injuries and greatly enhance one's cultivation.

Especially when such sentient beings ascended, they would attract a great surge of spiritual opportunity. If someone killed them at that moment, that spiritual opportunity would become a wedding dress for the killer.

Luo Chen's lips curled slightly. "Quite straightforward. Hexiang, do you feel the same way?"

The other of the Qinghe sisters bowed solemnly.

"The Qinghe clan wishes to submit to Green Parasol Mountain and serve you as our master!"

"Good!"

Luo Chen replied with a heavy voice.

His majestic gaze swept over the crowd, and for a moment, there was silence.

In the oppressive silence, Luo Chen suddenly spoke. "Next, I will go into seclusion at Green Parasol Mountain to break through to the Ascendant realm. All of you will stay here, guard this mountain, and protect my seclusion!"

Everyone was shaken. Even though they knew Luo Chen possessed strength comparable to an Ascendant, they had never expected him to go into seclusion to break through to the Ascendant realm so soon.

How many years had he been cultivating?

At most, it was less than five hundred years!

As they looked at each other, their hearts surged with excitement.

If it went smoothly, wouldn't Green Parasol Mountain produce another Ascendant, becoming the only sacred land in the millions of mountains?

For a moment, everyone responded in unison.

After dismissing the crowd, Luo Chen paced leisurely in the Truth Crossing Hall, walking step by step toward that sealed fifth-grade cave dwelling.

He did not choose the Tongxuan Hall, nor the Zhoukong Hall, but stayed in the Truth Crossing Hall.

It wasn't that those two places were bad, but that he felt more at ease here.

In the Truth Crossing Hall, he had once been a prisoner, had once subdued the demon horde, and in the future, he would surely break through to the Ascendant realm here.

Arriving before the cave dwelling, Luo Chen took out the Truth Crossing Seal and opened its restrictions.

Instantly!

The surging spiritual energy stirred his robes, making them rustle!

Luo Chen's eyes lit up. Such dense spiritual energy was truly rare in the world.

After a thousand years, this cave dwelling had been left unused, never touched. The spiritual energy within was so dense it had almost condensed into dew. Taking a mouthful or two would be enough to restore one's entire mana.

Especially the spiritual opportunity!

As his eyes swept over, a vast amount of spiritual opportunity drifted within, clearly visible.

Such a treasure land was a godsend for breaking through to the Ascendant realm!

But Luo Chen was not in a hurry.

Instead, he spat out his lifebound artifact—the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

He was almost certain of breaking through to the Ascendant realm.

But becoming an Ascendant was not just about breaking through a realm; it was also an opportunity.

Legend had it that many Sage cultivators, while breaking through to the Ascendant realm, would roam the heavens and earth, touching upon a fragment of the true meaning of the laws. This would lay a good foundation for comprehending the true meaning of the laws after becoming an Ascendant.

Although Luo Chen had already comprehended three types of law true meanings—Wood's Withering and Blooming, and the two origins of Water and Fire—he still wanted more.

If, while breaking through to the Ascendant realm, he could touch upon, or even comprehend, a fourth type of law true meaning, it would greatly benefit his cultivation path after becoming an Ascendant.

And for this, he had two preparations!

Flicking his finger, he shot out a spiritual seal, and the Primordial Unity Cauldron bloomed with a faint light.

A golden radiance slowly flew out from within.

This was the first preparation—the Law-Derivation Pill!

Back in the final trial of the Pill Realm, he had expended tremendous effort, even gnawing on the Taisui's main body and using his source blood, to refine this fifth-grade divine pill.

Its effects were already recorded in the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's alchemy manual.

It could help an Ascendant cultivator comprehend a completely different law true meaning!

Luo Chen did not doubt this. After all, the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining had shockingly comprehended over a dozen types of law true meanings in his life, and the credit went to this heaven-defying Law-Derivation Pill.

As for the second preparation, it would still cost Luo Chen some time.

With a flick of his hand, a deep, ink-black lotus pod floated before him.

The Black Obsidian Lotus Pod, a spiritual medicine cultivated in the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land for over a thousand years, was already mature.

The place where it grew was free of filth.

It absorbed spiritual energy, removing the dross and keeping the essence.

It could be said that wherever such a spiritual medicine existed, the place itself would be nurtured into a spiritual land.

But its true use was that when used as an ingredient, it was a great tonic for a cultivator's Sage core, allowing it to become more condensed and not be disturbed by external demons.

In fact, even many water-attributed Ascendant cultivators would cultivate this ingredient to temper their primordial spirit.

Luo Chen guessed that this was a spiritual plant that the Alchemy Saint had cultivated for the Black Marsh Ancestor before leaving the Nether Origin Sect. Now, it had fallen into his hands.

The Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's alchemy manual happened to record a fifth-grade pill formula with the Black Obsidian Lotus Pod as the main ingredient, called the Soul-Scouring Pill.

Its effect was precisely to wash away the turbid qi of the primordial spirit and condense the essence of the soul.

"If I also use soul stones as an ingredient, the effect will surely be twice the result with half the effort!"

Luo Chen's eyes were full of anticipation.

Comprehending a law true meaning during the Ascension process was a matter of low probability. One could usually only make contact, leaving a seed, and it was very difficult to truly comprehend it on the spot.

But with the Law-Derivation Pill, his chances of success were high.

The only drawback was probably that the newly formed Ascendant realm's primordial spirit would not be stable enough to persevere.

But if he had the Soul-Scouring Pill, refined from the Black Obsidian Lotus Pod, he could quickly stabilize his primordial spirit, and then use the Law-Derivation Pill to comprehend a new law true meaning.

If he succeeded.

He might be the first existence in history to master four types of law true meanings upon first entering the Ascendant realm!

Luo Chen took a deep breath and began his actions.

Flicking his finger, the Nirvana Sacred Flame shot out and began to envelop the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

The dense spiritual energy around him, like a flood dragon, poured into the cauldron.

Luo Chen moved his hands in rapid succession. The Black Obsidian Lotus Pod was thrown in, along with a fist-sized soul stone and the many auxiliary ingredients he had specially prepared over the years.

With these actions, an intoxicating, soul-stirring aura gradually emanated from the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

(Chapter End)
