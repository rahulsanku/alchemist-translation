# Chapter 547: Confession, New Residence, Black King, Seeking Vengeance

Three demons attacked. One wounded, two dead.

What followed was simple, nothing more than cleaning up the battlefield.

The living Demon Spider Crab was easy to handle. Although it was third-tier, the handling method was no different from the previous first- and second-tier ones.

Then came the two dead Demon Spider Crabs.

One had been blown apart in the chest by Luo Chen's punch, yet still left behind remains: two enormous crab claws and a third-tier water-attribute demon core.

Zhu Qi, by contrast, was rather miserable.

The material of Luo Chen's Primordial Unity Cauldron was excellent. Even though its impurities had still not been completely removed, when thrown with his Archaic physique, it could still erupt with vigorous giant strength.

Moreover, these years of uninterrupted sacrificial refinement and frequent pill refinement had made his control of the Primordial Unity Cauldron increasingly masterful.

With one strike, there was no intact corpse, and even the demon core failed to survive.

However, that compass plate serving as the grand formation's hub, as well as the eight crab legs used to arrange the formation, were preserved intact.

This was also a decent harvest.

Letting the Black-Scale Jiaolong clean up the battlefield for him, Luo Chen carefully savored the previous battle.

Aside from not using spells and similar methods, the combat strength erupted by his physique was clearly visible.

Facing demon beasts likewise powerful in physique, he could suppress early third-tier ones without suspense.

And mid third-tier demon beasts were very clearly also not his match.

The reason he had been trapped in the grand formation was because he had been somewhat careless.

After all, very few people would think demon beasts could use formations, this "crystallization of science" belonging to human cultivators.

This also reminded Luo Chen that when facing any enemy, he could not be careless.

"Myriad Path Convergence, which has already reached Perfected Completion, has derived the function of partial body transformation. It makes up for my pure physical close-combat ability."

"In this way, even without fully using the Heavenly Roc true body transformation, I can still erupt with part of my body-refinement strength."

"And if I transform fully, with late Archaic Tier Three..."

Luo Chen savored it, recalling the battle against that Gold Wolf King on Tiangu Plain.

Looking at it now, if he fully transformed at present, he might be able to withstand the other party's full frontal charge without the slightest injury.

In other words, his current strength was enough to not fear the vast majority of late third-tier great demon kings.

And if he waited until his Core Formation Stage Four realm completely stabilized,

even when facing a late Core Formation great cultivator at Stage Seven, he would also possess enough ability to protect himself.

"Perhaps, if prepared, I could even fight and defeat one?"

Luo Chen muttered.

He was somewhat unable to accurately grasp his true strength.

But no matter how inaccurate it was, he still had the confidence to win forcefully if facing Wu Qi's attacks.

"This physique of mine has cultivated enough, but trained too little. I feel there is still enormous space to develop."

"Moreover, with Black Emperor Paste's support, there is a very high chance of advancing to the fourth tier. If it reaches that time, perhaps a true qualitative change will arrive!"

While Luo Chen summarized, the Black-Scale Jiaolong carefully swam over.

"Master, these are all the spoils."

Luo Chen put these things into his storage ring, then released Cleansing Art across this sea region on a large scale.

But after cleaning for a while, he could not help shaking his head.

Many battle auras were no longer something a low-level spell like Cleansing Art could completely erase.

It could only achieve a temporary concealing effect.

He looked around, made a decision in his heart, and brought the Black-Scale Jiaolong back to Heavenly Pivot Island.

He first glanced at the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull lying on the ground, then frowned.

"What happened?"

The Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull answered lowly, "A late Core Formation great cultivator placed a Demon-Suppressing Talisman on me. I cannot mobilize my demon power at all."

Demon-Suppressing Talisman!

One of the most widely circulated talismans in the Northern Sea cultivation world. From first tier below to fourth tier above, every kind existed.

Its function was also very simple: to suppress a demon beast's inner core and seal all the demon power in its body.

The reason such talismans existed

was because some cultivators liked capturing living demon beasts, then taming and subduing them to become spirit beasts.

Moreover, many liked to use them as rewards for low-realm juniors.

Therefore, to make it convenient for them to subdue demon beasts, Demon-Suppressing Talismans mostly came in pairs. Juniors obtained them and used the talismans to drive demon beasts of higher realm than themselves.

Luo Chen released his divine sense and carefully investigated the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull's body.

With the other party not resisting at all, he quickly found that the third-tier Demon-Suppressing Talisman had already seeped into its demon core.

Without the caster actively undoing it, this talisman was very hard to remove.

Unless they found the main talisman[TERM?].

Luo Chen examined it carefully, then nodded slightly.

"The problem is not large. Even without the main talisman, give me some time, and I can undo it."

His confidence made the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull and Black-Scale Jiaolong both somewhat stunned. Master could even undo Demon-Suppressing Talismans?

"The two of you rest and reorganize a little. Later, leave with me."

The Black-Scale Jiaolong froze. "We are leaving?"

Luo Chen did not answer and returned to the cave residence alone.

As soon as he entered, he saw the black Soul-Refining Banner planted by the entrance.

Han Zhan's voice came at the right time. "Congratulations, young friend, on your dao attainment advancing."

Luo Chen waved his hand. "Mere Core Formation Stage Four, not worth mentioning."

"What I speak of is not merely Core Formation Stage Four, but also this physique of yours! Three demon kings besieged you, especially with a mid-stage demon king among them who actually grasped formations, yet you killed them so easily. Looking across Core Formation cultivators, you can be counted among the outstanding."

The corners of Luo Chen's mouth tugged, and he suddenly asked, "Compared with that Gu Shaoshang of Feiyun Ravine, how am I?"

On the way back, he had learned the details of the previous battle through the Black-Scale Jiaolong.

He had also learned the origin of that ship of cultivators, as well as the name of their leader: Eldest Senior Brother Gu Shaoshang of Feiyun Ravine.

Han Zhan smiled. "I only exchanged blows with him for a few breaths, and the Myriad Souls Banner is not my specialty magic treasure, so it is difficult to judge. But from what I saw, he should be inferior to you."

Luo Chen also smiled.

"According to Senior, he is inferior to me. Yet he, at Core Formation Stage Seven, fought Senior to a draw, while before Senior, I still feel that your wisp of remnant soul is as deep as the abyssal sea. Heh..."

Han Zhan fell silent.

Luo Chen ignored him and went straight into the cave residence.

Mana surged, transforming into red great hands that put away all kinds of miscellaneous medicinal materials, books, wooden racks, and meditation mats into the storage ring one by one.

The commotion from the battle against the three great demon kings of the Demon Spider Crab race had truly not been small.

He had killed three demons, and the Demon Spider Crab race would certainly not let things rest.

These were not some first- or second-tier little crabs!

Heavenly Pivot Island was already exposed!

Therefore, abandoning this base was imperative.

This was also why human cultivators rarely chose wilderness mountain ranges or demon sea territories for cultivation.

Once one or two battles erupted, it was very easy to attract large numbers of demon beasts to spy and attack.

Under such circumstances, quiet cultivation was simply impossible.

If he had not had two subdued spirit beasts, how could these five years have been so peaceful?

Leaving had to happen now.

After packing everything, Luo Chen looked at this cave residence with a small third-tier spirit vein and felt rather reluctant.

"A pity."

With a murmur, he waved his great sleeve.

The spirit-gathering formation flags hidden underground transformed into streaks of flowing light and flew toward him. Cracking sounds came from above the cave residence.

Large sheets of falling stones and gravel cascaded down.

He turned and left.

When passing the entrance, he recalled the Myriad Souls Banner.

...

In the sky, a faint white cloud slowly flew.

Luo Chen held the compass plate Zhu Qi left behind and continuously fiddled with it.

This was a water-attribute formation plate[TERM?].

With his current minor attainment in formation cultivation, he could observe that a full thirteen formations were inscribed upon it.

Ten were common first- and second-tier formations.

The other three were rather excellent third-tier grand formations.

The Sea-Sealing Eight Trigrams Formation was also among them.

This formation's strength lay in extracting endless water vapor from the great sea to form a sealing and imprisoning formation. So long as one entered the formation, if one did not have absolute explosive force, the formation arranger would almost stand in an invincible position.

After all, human strength had limits, but the great sea was boundless.

Luo Chen had relied on his late third-tier Archaic physique and the high-grade magic treasure Primordial Unity Cauldron he had tempered for a long time to violently break it in one stroke.

If it were an ordinary person, they would likely have long since been sealed alive inside and killed by those blood-colored spider threads.

Aside from the Sea-Sealing Eight Trigrams Formation, there was also a Misty Cloud Dense Fog Formation[TERM?], which had the effect of concealment and cover.

As for the final third-tier grand formation, Luo Chen could not see its origin.

While he studied the formation plate, his brow suddenly rose.

With a flip of his wrist, the Myriad Souls Banner stretched across before him.

"Does Senior have something you want to say to me?"

Inside the soul banner, Han Zhan sighed and said, "I only want to tell you that the soul origin remaining in this wisp of remnant soul of mine is very, very little, and it is the key to my future body reconstruction and recultivation. Unless absolutely necessary, I cannot possibly go to war over some weak enemies and consume my origin."

The corners of Luo Chen's mouth curled in disdain.

As expected, this Han Zhan had held something back.

Previously, when dealing with Wu Qi, he had used the excessive depletion of low-level souls in the Myriad Souls Banner as a reason to conceal the fact that he could not stop the other party, forcing Luo Chen to personally act and block Wu Qi's forceful landing on the Swallow Islands.

But over these years, Luo Chen had filled the Soul-Refining Banner with all the souls of the first- and second-tier demon crabs he hunted.

This banner had long since recovered the quality of a Myriad Souls Banner!

One could even say that because Luo Chen had killed Yan Nantian back then, many Foundation Establishment cultivator souls inside this banner had divided and devoured Yan Nantian's soul, giving those hundred-plus Foundation Establishment souls third-tier foundation.

After low-level souls were replenished in large numbers, the soul of one cultivator who, while alive, had been Foundation Establishment Perfected Completion gradually advanced to the third-tier Ghost King level by devouring and merging.

This was also why when Han Zhan activated the Myriad Souls Banner, aside from the original Ghost King, there was a second Ghost King within.

Under such circumstances, this banner was already a genuine Myriad Souls Banner!

With Sage-stage Han Zhan as the controller, even if he were no longer in good condition, how could a mere Core Formation Stage Seven cultivator be his match?

Yet Han Zhan, while blocking Gu Shaoshang, had still required the Black-Scale Jiaolong to use its lifebound demon art before barely rescuing the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull.

Because of this, trouble had also come to his door.

One could say that all along, Han Zhan had consciously held back, hiding his true ability.

Connecting this to his battle against Wolf Emperor Aoxiao at Tiangu Plain and all of his deeds on Cangwu Mountain, this person was truly crafty with age and liked very much to keep one or two trump cards.

Now, hearing the reason the other party gave for holding back, Luo Chen had only a contemptuous smile.

"I do not know whether what you say is true or false, but since Senior has chosen to travel the Demon Sea with me, and is bound by a Great Dao oath, I feel it is best if we cooperate sincerely."

Han Zhan asked in return, "By sincere cooperation, do you mean going all out for the other party?"

Luo Chen pondered for a moment. After weighing gains and losses, he said leisurely, "I hope so."

Han Zhan laughed softly. "Then this old man may as well speak frankly. What I want is something you may not be able to accomplish even if you go all out."

"Speak frankly."

"Recovering Sage cultivation through bitter cultivation is already impossible for me. And if I seize the body[TERM?] of a Core Formation cultivator, I cannot rely on that stolen body to pass the Sage-stage heavenly tribulation[TERM?]. Therefore, I need the fleshly body of a Sage cultivator, and it must be the body of a human cultivator, for me to reconstruct my body, recultivate, and rebuild my dao foundation. Luo Chen, can you accomplish that?"

The leisure on Luo Chen's face vanished.

...

Splash!

On the great sea, bubbles layered over one another. As the ripples dispersed, things gradually returned to calm.

Beneath the sea surface, two figures dove deeper and deeper.

Finally, they arrived at an extremely deep seabed trench.

The Black-Scale Jiaolong stopped and respectfully said, "Master, this is the most hidden of the three backup bases."

Luo Chen looked at this trench several thousand zhang long, with no end visible at a glance. On both sides of the trench swayed clusters of very beautiful red coral.

That was blood coral[TERM?]!

A second-tier spirit plant, intelligent and possessing powerful offensive ability.

Because of its vivid appearance, it often attracted sea creatures, which it then devoured.

Therefore, this place could also be counted as a dangerous land. Ordinary low-level demon beasts would not come here.

He nodded in satisfaction.

Heavenly Pivot Island's exposure had been within his expectations.

Therefore, long before this, he had ordered Heavenly Pivot and the Black-Scale Jiaolong to find backup bases for him.

This place was one of them, and also the most hidden.

The Black-Scale Jiaolong hesitated, but still said, "Master, this place only has second-tier spirit vein spirit qi density, and it is water-attribute. I fear it is not suitable for your cultivation. How about we go to Slippery Bone Island[TERM?] instead?"

Luo Chen waved his hand and walked toward the coral trench.

"Slippery Bone Island is too dangerous. Although it has a third-tier spirit vein, it is occupied by large numbers of Hidden Dust Sand and is adjacent to the Nine-Clawed Poison King Crabs."

"One more matter is worse than one less."

"Moreover, I have already advanced to Core Formation Stage Four. In the short term, stabilizing my realm is the priority. I do not need an especially good spirit vein land."

Entering the coral trench,

Luo Chen searched left and right, finally stopping beneath a rock wall.

"Here."

Before he could take out the Mystic Flame Sword to open a cave residence, the Black-Scale Jiaolong swam over.

"Master, I will do it! I will do it!"

"How could such a small matter be worth Master expending precious mana?"

As it spoke, it crashed into the rock wall, demon power spitting out and transforming into blades that cut the hard stone.

Seeing its bootlicking appearance, Luo Chen could not help laughing.

"Big Black..."

Just as he opened his mouth, Luo Chen recalled the earlier scene when the Black-Scale Jiaolong ignored its depleted demon power and forced itself forward to help him fight.

And these years of hard work from the other party.

Even if it was because it had been enslaved and because of Imperial Moonlight Nectar, one could still say it had worked hard and achieved great merit!

Calling it Big Black was a little casual.

At least that Fighting Gull could be called Heavenly Pivot.

Thinking to this point, Luo Chen asked, "Speaking of which, do you have a name?"

The Black-Scale Jiaolong, currently working hard to open the cave residence, froze for a moment, then answered, "When I was small, my clansmen called me Little Taotie[TERM?], saying I could eat a lot. Later, when I became fat, they called me Big Black. That Kun Jiao also called me that."

Little Taotie, Big Black...

The corners of Luo Chen's mouth twitched. No wonder when he had called it Big Black before, the other party had never sensed anything wrong.

It was truly called this name!

But this was far too casual.

"I will give you a name."

The Black-Scale Jiaolong's movements immediately stopped, and it looked eagerly toward Luo Chen. "Master wants to bestow a name upon me?"

Luo Chen nodded. Looking at the dragon horn atop the Black-Scale Jiaolong's head, its fierce outline already showing, and thinking of the potentially enormous lifebound demon art it had awakened, able to devour foreign things and rely on its powerful digestion ability to strengthen itself,

if it did not fall in the future, it would certainly accomplish something and perhaps possess a kingly bearing.

Thinking of this, he gently said, "You shall be called Black King[TERM?]!"

"Black turtle?" The Black-Scale Jiaolong froze, not quite reacting. Where did it resemble a turtle?

Luo Chen laughed despite himself and repeated, "Black King!"

This time, the Black-Scale Jiaolong heard clearly.

"Black King? Black King!"

After muttering twice, it revealed a look of wild joy.

"Many thanks, Master, for bestowing a name!"

Luo Chen shook his head. "Continue."

Afterward, he walked nearby, and after briefly observing, he formed a rough plan in his heart.

Always using mana to resist deep-sea pressure and block seawater corrosion was not a proper long-term method.

Originally, he had planned to arrange a second-tier Water-Avoidance Formation[TERM?].

But now, he had a better substitute.

As he pondered, he took from his storage ring the compass plate he had obtained not long ago.

First, he used powerful divine sense to erase Zhu Qi's remaining mark on it.

Demon beasts were strong in physique and weak in divine soul.

Although Zhu Qi had been mid third-tier, his divine soul foundation was only early third-tier.

These years, even though Luo Chen had not deliberately trained his divine soul foundation much, as his realm rose, it had gradually grown to a level comparable to ordinary late Core Formation!

He was also proficient in the obscuring restriction among the seven great formation fundamentals.

Now, once he used it, in only three to five breaths, he erased the previous owner's remaining mark.

Afterward, mana poured into it.

The three slowly interlocking layers of the compass plate began to slowly turn. The surrounding earth vein power and water vapor were also drawn in, gradually changing.

Streams of water, centered on Luo Chen, began continuously moving and spinning.

Nearby blood coral swayed its body, releasing an instinct of fear.

Slowly, a water curtain spread outward, pushing aside the seawater here one by one. There were also waves of mist spreading outward.

It was precisely the Misty Cloud Dense Fog Formation!

It had extremely strong concealment and covering effects, and could also merge very well into the local environment.

The spreading mist, after drifting several times, also transformed into streaks of red light, reflecting with the surrounding blood-colored coral. Anyone who saw it would only think it was light reflected by the blood coral.

Seeing this scene, Luo Chen nodded in satisfaction.

"With this, I can move without worry."

"It just consumes a bit of mana. Under normal circumstances, it is better to replace that with spirit stones."

While he arranged the formation, Black King had also opened a rough cave residence.

Luo Chen entered. After praising the other party, he embedded the compass plate in the center of the cave residence, while also beginning to arrange the spirit-gathering formation.

Only after he finished all this did this place initially possess the shape of a cave residence that could be lived in for the short term.

Then, Luo Chen summoned the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Clang!

The great cauldron landed. He formed a spell seal.

The Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull was shaken out of it.

"Next, I will undo that Demon-Suppressing Talisman for you."

As for how to undo it?

For Luo Chen, this was not difficult.

The main point of this talisman lay in the mana mark of a high-level cultivator applied to it. That was why, without the main talisman, it was hard to remove the secondary talisman[TERM?].

He only needed to use the fourth-tier Dragon-Slaying Art to sever that mana mark.

Coincidentally!

Over these years, in order to remove the enormous medicinal balefulness of True Flame Pills, Dragon-Slaying Art, under day after day of use, had already been pushed to grandmaster proficiency and was not far from Perfected Completion!

Severing the mana mark of a Core Formation Stage Seven cultivator was more than enough.

In less than half a day, that mark completely dissipated.

On the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull's forehead, the original form of the Demon-Suppressing Talisman also appeared.

Luo Chen reached out and peeled it off. That talisman drifted to the ground, losing its spiritual light.

Once the talisman was gone, it immediately felt freedom!

The Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull circulated its demon power and only felt its whole body become comfortable.

It lay on the ground and said with delight, "Many thanks, Master, for casting the spell!"

Luo Chen smiled faintly. "You are my spirit beast. This is what I should do."

At this point, the smile on his face suddenly vanished.

When beating a dog, one still had to look at its master. Someone had tried to seize his spirit beast. This matter would not be so easily brushed over.

Especially since, even after Han Zhan acted, the other party had not voluntarily returned it. That was adding crime upon crime.

"Do you still remember the aura of that ship of people?"

Once this question came out, the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull first froze, then said hatefully, "Heavenly Pivot naturally remembers!"

The Fighting Gull race held grudges most of all.

Therefore, it had gained its evil name for loving battle because it remembered grudges.

Remembering the aura of enemies was an instinct carved into its blood.

Luo Chen nodded. "Since that is so, after you rest a little, go look for that ship of people. See where they are, and what they are doing."

A guess rose in Heavenly Pivot's heart. Could Master be planning to avenge it?

Thinking of how it had been toyed with, captured, and suppressed, it could not stop hating the other party.

If it could take revenge, that would simply feel too good!

However...

"Is Master going to avenge me?" Heavenly Pivot hesitated. "On that ship, there are many Core Formation cultivators. There are two mid-stage cultivators like Master, and even one Core Formation Stage Seven great cultivator. This..."

A cold smile hung on Luo Chen's mouth. "You do not need to worry. Go search first. Do not alert the enemy either. It is enough to know the general situation."

Seeing Master's words were firm as iron, the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull naturally did not resist.

Added to its lack of habit with the seabed environment, it immediately left the coral trench.

Watching its departing back, Luo Chen snorted.

He very much wanted to know why, for no reason, a ship of people, even seven great Core Formation cultivators, had traveled far into the demon sea to come to this rather dangerous Xuanyan Sea Region.

If they were passing by, then fine. At worst, he would swallow this anger.

If they were going to handle some major matter in the Xuanyan Sea Region, then this would affect his great plan of refining Black Emperor Paste.

At that time, to prevent mishaps, perhaps he would have to divert disaster eastward[TERM?], borrowing the hands of the five great royal demon crab races to avenge hatred.

Amid war, he could also take the opportunity to hunt some demon crabs.

In recent years, the number of demon crabs hunted had become fewer and fewer. That was not enough for him to refine and store more Black Emperor Paste to support his cultivation plan after leaving in the future.

After thinking through all this, Luo Chen closed the cave residence door.

He had only just broken through to Core Formation Stage Four, and still needed to stabilize it somewhat. He could not let other miscellaneous matters cause this hard-won realm to fall.

Rhythmic breathing slowly sounded within this cave residence in the coral trench.

Changing places seemed to have no effect on Luo Chen at all.

(End of chapter)
