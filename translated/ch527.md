# Chapter 527: At Sea-Watching Cliff, Driving the Tiger to Devour the Wolf

---

Outside Black Swan Island.

When Cheng Dou heard the four words "I am Qingyang," he pursed his lips.

As expected.

It was almost exactly as they had guessed. This young man, whose conduct was so arrogant and overbearing, controlled Qingyang Demon Flame and had even taken it as his Daoist title.

The moment Cheng Dou thought of the conditions required to refine Qingyang Demon Flame, his heart trembled.

That process required essence blood from countless living beings and could only be called cruel.

Such a demonic flame was something only a demon-path expert could resolve himself to refine.

In that case, he absolutely had to receive this person properly and avoid provoking him.

"Senior, this way, please."

Cheng Dou respectfully stepped aside, yielding the path ahead so Luo Chen could walk in front.

Luo Chen glanced at him and said mildly, "I do not yet wish to expose my realm."

Cheng Dou froze, then immediately understood.

He himself was a great cultivator at Initiate Stage Nine, only one step away from Initiate Perfected Completion. Black Swan Island was also the Cheng clan's base camp, with many people and considerable strength. If he behaved this respectfully toward someone who seemed only at middle-stage Initiate, then the slightest rumor would make others guess Luo Chen's identity or realm in a higher direction.

He drew a deep breath, straightened his back, stepped forward, and walked shoulder to shoulder with Luo Chen.

"The evening banquet has already been arranged, but it still needs a little time. While we wait, why does this junior not introduce Black Swan Island and the Swallow Islands to you in detail?"

"As you please."

...

"Senior should know that the Northern Sea is also called the Demon Sea. That is because there are countless sea demons in the great sea and countless demon-path experts from the Primordial Demon Sect ran rampant here, so outsiders gave it that name."

"Over time, even the Primordial Demon Sect itself acknowledged the term. At some point, the Primordial Demon Sect began calling the regions it had already conquered and occupied the Demon Domain, while the places not yet occupied were collectively called the Demon Sea."

"The Swallow Islands became human territory eight hundred years ago. Eight hundred years ago, this place was still within the Demon Sea. At the time, countless Cloud-Piercing Sparrows and Sea-Breaking Swallows nested throughout the islands."

"Eight hundred years ago, the Primordial Demon Sect dispatched subordinate sects to conquer this place and renamed it the Swallow Islands."

"However, because the resources here are comparatively poor, its geographic position remote, and it constantly faces threats from the Xingtian Sea, no large sect was willing to remain here. As time passed, it became a gathering place for itinerant cultivators and small clans."

"My Cheng clan settled here at that time and made our living breeding and selling first-tier demon beasts such as black swans."

Cheng Dou recounted the history of the Swallow Islands one piece at a time.

Its details were far clearer than Cheng Haichang's account.

Luo Chen listened quietly, catching common knowledge that would be useful to him.

For example, the Demon Sea was divided into the Demon Sea and the Demon Domain.

For another example, while the Primordial Demon Sect seemed wildly arrogant and domineering in its conduct, in truth it had also shouldered a task similar to that of the Nether Origin Sect: opening new domains.

If one followed this comparison, did the Ascendant Holy Lands of the Southern Frontier and Western Desert also have similar missions, or perhaps responsibilities?

If that were so, then the original intentions of the five great Holy Lands were actually quite good. Overall, they were all seeking benefits for human cultivators.

Wait, then what role did the Tianyuan Dao Sect, located in Central Continent where there were no outside threats, play?

Leader? One who enjoyed the fruits of others' labor? Or a meritorious predecessor who had already endured hardship, opened an entire continent, and begun to enjoy the rewards?

His thoughts drifted. Without realizing it, this small Adept cultivator had begun thinking rather far afield.

This time, Luo Chen did not cut off the train of thought.

Because before coming to the Northern Sea, he himself had already faintly touched upon the conflict between the human Holy Land, the Nether Origin Sect, and the feathered demon Holy Land of Green Parasol Mountain.

If he one day advanced to Sage, or even Ascendant, he would inevitably return to the Eastern Wilderness.

At that time, he would very likely be swept into it.

Sorting these matters out a little more now would do him no harm.

Beside him, Cheng Dou continued his introduction.

"Although the Primordial Demon Sect was known for acting without restraint and with peerless ferocity, in the eyes of many, it was also an orthodox sect with a bottom line."

"They pursued the law of the jungle and encouraged competition both internally and externally. But when it came to the lower strata, they did quite well. They strictly forbade high-tier cultivators from wantonly slaughtering the lower-level human race. That even included mortals."

"Even the soul-refining lineage within the Primordial Demon Sect, which required large numbers of low-tier souls, was ordered not to massacre mortals or Qi Refinement cultivators. If they needed souls, they could simply hunt in the endless Demon Sea."

"Senior should have noticed as well. Although the mortals of our Swallow Islands live difficult lives, they all have a way forward. The reason lies here. Many years ago, the Primordial Demon Sect showed us how mortals should be used."

"Allow mortals to reproduce in great numbers and give birth to population. Those with spirit roots are taken into sects. Those without spirit roots are taught body-tempering martial methods and given spirit-bonded weapons, allowing them to shine in various tedious mundane affairs. Some innate grandmaster-level mortals who perform well are even exceptionally recruited into cultivation clans like ours and given quite decent treatment."

Luo Chen suddenly understood.

No wonder the mortals he had seen earlier, though weathered by hardship, were overall full of enthusiasm for their work.

They were nothing like the mortals of the Eastern Wilderness, who were filled with numbness.

So it was because they had "hope."

Luo Chen nodded. "From that perspective, the Primordial Demon Sect did rather well."

Cheng Dou's spirits lifted. He had deliberately guided the topic in this direction and openly or subtly praised the Primordial Demon Sect quite a bit. Seeing Luo Chen's reaction, he felt rather satisfied.

In this way, regardless of whether the other party was a remnant of the Primordial Demon Sect, Cheng Dou felt he had handled it well enough.

He coughed and continued:

"Yes. The Primordial Demon Sect was merely somewhat flamboyant and eccentric in its conduct. Even if the world called it a demon sect, and gave its people titles like Demon Domain and devils, they disdained to argue, and were too lazy to crown themselves with grand-sounding names like Holy Sect."

Luo Chen gave a soft sound of acknowledgement. Truly top-level experts rarely cared about distinctions between righteous and demonic, let alone a mere title.

As they chatted, the two had already arrived before the manor beside Little Mirror Lake.

"Senior Qingyang, the banquet is ready. This way, please."

Luo Chen's steps paused slightly. He glanced at Cheng Dou, a half-smile on his face.

"You prepared quite the feast."

Cheng Dou's heart tightened. "It is what this junior should do. I only hope Senior will be pleased."

"You have put thought into it."

Luo Chen chuckled and strode into the manor.

As soon as he entered, he saw a beautiful woman with a graceful figure standing at the entrance of the main hall, hands folded neatly before her.

Seeing Luo Chen's face, her eyes trembled. She lowered her head and bent at the waist in a graceful bow.

"This little woman, Cheng Haixin, pays respects to Senior Qingyang."

As she bent, her skirt slanted slightly, and spring light briefly leaked out.

Leaving aside the curves of waist and hip, merely the alluring snow-white flash at her chest was enough to make a person linger.

Luo Chen glanced at her and said mildly, "Raise your head."

Cheng Haixin pursed her tender red lips, bit her pearly teeth, and straightened. Her long neck was like that of a proud swan.

Only then did Luo Chen discover this woman's advantage.

Tall.

She was fully one meter eighty, almost level with himself.

She was even a head taller than Cheng Dou beside her.

Among all the women he had seen in his life, perhaps only the demon cultivator Azure Frost could compare with her in height.

But compared to Cheng Haixin's fragile, almost little-white-rabbit, little-swan temperament, Azure Frost was far superior.

Cold, powerful. In the three years Luo Chen had spent on Green Parasol Mountain, he had not even dared look directly at her.

The only reason Azure Frost had left such a deep impression of her tall figure on him was probably that during the few times they met, he had kept his head lowered and could only glimpse her long, firm, powerful legs.

Cheng Dou stood carefully at the side, observing Luo Chen's expression.

In that instant, he saw Luo Chen show a trace of distraction.

At that moment, he finally relaxed.

As expected.

Seventh Sister, the pearl of Black Swan Island, was something no one could keep a firm grip on after seeing.

"Senior, please take your seat."

Luo Chen looked at the chair Cheng Dou pulled out and raised a brow. "I am the guest. Is it appropriate for me to sit in the host's seat?"

Cheng Dou hurriedly smiled. "Appropriate. Senior's personal arrival at my Cheng clan is the Cheng clan's honor. We must make Senior feel at home. If even the slightest thing is lacking, it is this junior's fault."

Luo Chen smiled faintly, no longer bothering with false modesty, and sat openly in the main seat.

Cheng Dou also sat at the side.

Once the two sat, Cheng Haixin, still standing in the hall, became rather conspicuous.

Cheng Dou gave her several looks before the woman bit her teeth and sat down beside Luo Chen.

Her reluctant appearance made Luo Chen laugh inwardly.

This Cheng Dou clearly wanted something from him.

"Senior, please taste the specialty dishes of our Swallow Islands."

"Haixin, pour wine for Senior."

Cheng Haixin hummed softly and poured a cup of wine for Luo Chen with movements that were not very practiced.

The moment the fragrance of wine spread, the spirit qi throughout the hall seemed to stir.

Luo Chen's heart moved, and his gaze fell upon the cup.

"For my lord's knowledge, this is Huan Orange Wine. It is a third-tier spirit wine brewed from deep-sea spirit spring water, blood oranges from the Primordial Demon Sect's Blood God lineage, and supreme-grade Huan Jade Marrow. It provides powerful assistance when cultivators break through realms."

Cheng Haixin lifted the cup and offered it before Luo Chen. The blood-colored wine, like deep crimson nectar, seized the soul.

Beauty. Spirit wine. Grand banquet.

Luo Chen smiled.

A banquet with only three people began beneath an ambiguous atmosphere.

Cheng Dou was a skilled conversationalist.

He deliberately found excuses to continue the topic they had not finished earlier.

Under the Primordial Demon Sect's rule, although the Northern Sea cultivation world had been turbulent, overall, cultivation clans like theirs had managed passably well.

But a hundred years ago, after the Primordial Demon Sect was destroyed, everything changed.

The order that had never been particularly good collapsed overnight.

The atmosphere guided by the demon sect for several thousand years erupted completely, and without the Holy Land's absolute suppression, the next seventy years shrouded the entire Northern Sea cultivation world in bloody storms.

It was said that the inland Demon Domain truly became a demon domain.

Devils emerged one after another. Demon-path titans often attacked mountains and exterminated sects. Even low-realm cultivators began acting as robber cultivators.

There were also cultivators who had learned superficial methods from the Primordial Demon Sect and began practicing slaughter on a large scale.

Some used the blood demon methods of the Blood God lineage, others the soul-refining methods of the soul-refining lineage. All of those means required great quantities of essence blood and souls to support them.

In the past, they had all been directed outward, toward the demon races in the endless sea.

But now, with so many soft persimmons in the cultivation world to squeeze, who would still want to risk danger in the Demon Sea?

In short, those seventy years were the bleakest and darkest period in the Northern Sea cultivation world.

Even the remote Swallow Islands had seen several bloody struggles that destroyed clans and families.

The Cheng clan had originally possessed several middle-stage Initiate cultivators, who could be called clan pillars and mainstays, but all had been destroyed during those seventy years of chaos.

Especially with the appearance of Senior Nantian, slaughter was pushed to the extreme.

The moment he descended, he destroyed on the spot a clan that possessed three late-stage Initiates, thereby occupying the only third-tier spirit vein in this place.

If that were all, perhaps it would have been tolerable.

But afterward, he ordered every force in the Swallow Islands to pay him tribute.

Anyone who obeyed in public but resisted in private would be suppressed with bloodshed.

In a short seventy years, three Initiate clans had been destroyed by his hand.

It was only thirty years ago, when the mighty expert surnamed Li established the Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance in the center of the Demon Domain, that a new fragile order finally arose in the Northern Sea cultivation world.

Whether they sincerely submitted or not, surface respect still had to be shown.

In order to obtain the method of linking islands into arrays from that mighty expert surnamed Li, Senior Nantian nominally joined the Cang Hai Alliance.

It was only after that that he restrained his unbridled arrogance.

The Swallow Islands thus welcomed thirty short years of peace.

"However!"

"That Senior Nantian is not a broad-minded person. Once someone in the Swallow Islands reaches a realm that could threaten him, he suppresses them openly and secretly acts to erase them."

"For example, the master of the Jiaolong Gang is a grand cultivator at Initiate Perfected Completion, yet for all these years he has drifted alone overseas and has not dared return to the Swallow Islands."

"As for this unworthy one, I possess dual spirit roots and my cultivation speed is passable. But although I entered Initiate Stage Nine several decades ago, I have never dared step into Initiate Perfected Completion and charge toward a higher realm. What I fear is precisely this."

Cheng Dou spoke with feeling, his tone full of lament.

His gaze fell on Luo Chen, and his voice abruptly grew impassioned.

"But now it is different!"

"Senior has come, and the Swallow Islands can know peace. Senior has come, and Swallow can have a blue sky!"

Luo Chen suddenly raised a hand.

The impassioned words stopped abruptly.

Amid Cheng Dou's confusion, Luo Chen drank the last mouthful of spirit wine.

"The wine is good, and the dishes are quite distinctive. But I am tired."

Cheng Dou opened his mouth, his face flushing slightly.

But since he had managed to survive for decades under Senior Nantian's oppressive might, he still possessed some self-control. He knew he had been too hasty.

Forcing a smile, he said, "This junior was at fault. I forgot Senior came from afar and indeed needs rest. Haixin, take Senior to the inner courtyard to rest."

Cheng Haixin's expression dimmed, and she silently rose.

Luo Chen waved a hand. "No need for the inner courtyard. I find that lone mountain cliff at the tail end of Black Swan Island quite decent. There is also a second-tier spirit vein there. It can support my temporary stay."

Cheng Dou naturally did not dare disobey.

He immediately changed his words. "Senior is a man of refined interests. Sea-Watching Cliff does indeed have magnificent scenery. Haixin, accompany Senior there and open a cave residence for his temporary rest."

Cheng Haixin lowered her head and hummed in agreement.

...

On the solitary cliff, Luo Chen controlled the Mystic Flame Sword. In less than half a double-hour, he opened a simple yet spacious cave residence.

The moment he entered, he released the Primordial Unity Cauldron from his storage bag.

From within the cauldron came bursts of hissing cries.

Cheng Haixin, standing to the side, showed curiosity.

But when Luo Chen formed hand seals and the five Blue-Ringed Fang Serpents appeared, her lovely face paled.

Luo Chen turned and said mildly, "I intend to rest here. Little friend, do as you please."

Cheng Haixin's face shifted between green and red, but in the end she bit her teeth and said, "The nights on Sea-Watching Cliff are cold and windy. If Senior sleeps alone, it may be lonely and chilly. This concubine is willing to warm Senior's bed."

Luo Chen raised a brow and carefully looked her over.

At last, he slowly shook his head.

"Forget it. I am not so delicate."

"But..." Joy rose in the woman's heart, though conflict still showed on her face.

"The days ahead are long." Luo Chen spoke with a deeper implication, then ignored her and began arranging this temporary resting place on his own.

Cheng Haixin hesitated, but in the end, unable to conceal her joy, she left Sea-Watching Cliff.

After she was gone, Luo Chen walked to the cave entrance.

Looking at the cold silver waves and the heavy starry sea, his eyes narrowed slightly.

A moment later, he laughed freely.

"Nothing more than driving a tiger to devour a wolf."

*(End of Chapter)*
