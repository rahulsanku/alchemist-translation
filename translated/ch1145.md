# Chapter 1145: Qi-Mechanism Sensing

The first battle had ended too quickly.

In the span of a rabbit's rise and a falcon's stoop, reversals had come one after another.

But this was merely the view of martial arts experts. For ordinary people who couldn't see the true details of the fight, Granny Yu's defeat was only to be expected.

After all, Wood Wolf Divine Lord's reputation far exceeded Granny Yu's. The fact that she had held out for so long was actually admirable.

As for the second battle, it was even more anticipated.

Whether it was Old Freak Redflame or Silver-Handed Yama, both were local experts of Relic Immortal City who had made their names many years ago.

Although the two had never fought in public, their strength had always been rumored to be nearly equal—matched opponents.

For the audience, battles with too great a disparity were not very interesting.

It was fights like this, between evenly matched opponents, that were the scenes they wanted to see.

And indeed!

The second battle that followed was exceptionally explosive.

Whether it was Old Freak Redflame or Silver-Handed Yama, both excelled at head-on collisions.

As they exchanged blows, fist met palm, astral qi crisscrossed, and the onlookers were all stirred with burning fervor.

Cheers and shouts soared straight into the clouds.

Amidst this clamor and uproar, even Tie Xiong, who had the highest cultivation level in the entire venue, couldn't help but look tense, his heart hanging high. After all, the second battle could greatly influence the overall direction of the situation, so he had no choice but to watch with full concentration.

On the other hand, Luo Chen, who had made many comments during the previous match, had now become much quieter.

He simply watched the two men fight, a hint of doubt on his face.

For martial artists, they did not primarily cultivate the soul, and therefore did not develop divine sense or spiritual awareness. The drawback of this cultivation method was that in battle, lacking the aid of divine sense, they would react slowly to changes in the enemy's movements.

But real combat was not like that!

Whether it was the previous duel between Granny Yu and Wood Wolf Divine Lord, or the current slaughter between Redflame and Silver-Handed Yama, both clearly displayed great method and order.

In response to the enemy's attacks, they would often make relatively perfect countermeasures.

In extremely dangerous situations, they would have prepared counterattacks in advance.

This was absolutely not something that could be achieved by relying solely on eyesight!

As for physical instinct?

Luo Chen had considered this point, but any strong person who could reach the Furnace Realm had already achieved absolute control over their physical body.

Once this advantage offset each other, it was nothing special.

Therefore, the real answer was only one!

"Qi-mechanism sensing!"

This term appeared in Luo Chen's mind once again.

It was not an unfamiliar word, after all, Tie Xiong had just mentioned it once.

But the first time Luo Chen had truly heard this word was hundreds of years ago, from his good friend Wang Yuan.

At that time, Wang Yuan had not yet cultivated to the Furnace Realm, yet when facing a top assassin using a strange magical treasure, he had still easily found the enemy's true body and thus effortlessly defeated him.

Later, in Luo Chen's five-hundred-year combat career, he had once directly experienced this technique.

It was... Azure Frost!

Luo Chen involuntarily thought of that woman, the one who, like him, cultivated both dharma and body.

In the Yellow Spring Sea battle, Luo Chen had relied on his Crimson-Golden Pupils to see through spatial fluctuations and perfectly predict Azure Frost's sudden attacks. He had also relied on his profound soul foundation to execute soul techniques like "Ghosts and Gods Give Way," repeatedly nullifying Azure Frost's forceful attacks.

That time, he should have had the upper hand, but in reality, that advantage was erased after just a few exchanges.

It was only because Azure Frost also used the technique of "qi-mechanism sensing" to counter "Ghosts and Gods Give Way" and truly landed damage on him.

Spirit moves with intent, intent is emitted by qi.

All living beings have qi-mechanism.

Unless the physical body reaches the fourth-stage Leakless Realm, one's own qi-mechanism will constantly emanate outward.

Especially Luo Chen's physical body, which had once been cultivated to the Archaic fifth stage. The magnitude of his qi-mechanism, no matter how well concealed, was still like a bright moon in the dark night.

Therefore, Azure Frost had been able to easily bridge the gap in soul foundation and fight Luo Chen to a standstill.

Luo Chen was not reminiscing about these past events out of nostalgia, but rather having a sudden inspiration.

"If I could master this technique, could it replace the disadvantage of my divine sense being unusable now?"

For a cultivator, having no divine sense or spiritual awareness was almost equivalent to being blind.

Luo Chen was not blind, because he had a pair of Crimson-Golden Pupils that he had been nurturing since the Qi Refinement stage.

But in this world, there were ultimately some existences that the eyes could not see through.

If he could master the method of qi-mechanism sensing, perhaps Luo Chen could gain another pair of "eyes."

As for how to master it?

Luo Chen no longer watched the battle on the martial stage. He closed his eyes, and his own blood qi fell into a strange state of calm.

No one noticed this scene.

Everyone's attention was on the martial stage; why would they care about Luo Chen's changes?

Luo Chen's mind settled, his spirit calmed. His originally boiling blood qi suddenly became like a still lake. The two layers of Zhou Tian Dan Seeds that had condensed earlier also stopped rotating, instead illuminating the universe.

With his body as the center, figures of various people were inexplicably reflected in the still lake.

"It's this simple?"

In an instant, Luo Chen had mastered the top-tier technique that only Furnace Realm martial artists could barely grasp.

But he was not complacent.

Wang Yuan had comprehended this technique ahead of time back in the Blood River Realm.

He himself was inheriting the experience of predecessors, cultivating the book *Divine Astral Fighting Qi*, and had a solid physical foundation. Learning this technique ahead of time was merely a matter of natural progression.

Having just learned this method, Luo Chen was delighted. He subconsciously expanded his qi-mechanism to sense more people.

The first batch, naturally, were the people in the nearest spectator seats.

Unexpectedly, although Xiao Sanniang, General Chu, and others had qi-mechanisms as bright as furnaces, Tie Xiong's qi-mechanism could not be sensed at all.

"No, it's not that I can't sense it, it's just that the other party is concealing it quite well."

Luo Chen concentrated his attention, began to gather his own blood qi, forming a more condensed and powerful qi-mechanism, and diffused it towards Tie Xiong's direction.

"Sensed it!"

A trace of qi-mechanism, extremely well-hidden but like a firefly in the dark night, flickered faintly beside him.

At the same time.

Tie Xiong frowned and subconsciously looked at Luo Chen.

Then, a look of shock appeared on his face.

He felt that the person sitting beside him was no longer a human, but an ancient giant beast, eyeing him covetously.

"This qi-mechanism is too terrifying!"

But Luo Chen's attention to him was only momentary. After sensing him, he shifted to other places.

From left to right, the main viewing platform, the right viewing platform, until the entire martial stage, tens of thousands of spectators!

Countless qi-mechanisms were all reflected in the still lake of blood qi within Luo Chen's body.

Most people were unaware, after all, Luo Chen's qi-mechanism was so powerful that it was beyond the imagination of the world.

This secret observation was like a giant observing ants; how could the ants notice the gaze looking down from high above?

But on the main viewing platform, the originally somewhat languid Carefree Envoy suddenly frowned, his entire body's hair standing on end, and he directly stood up.

"Envoy, what's wrong?"

Puzzled gazes came from beside him.

The Carefree Envoy took a deep breath, his gaze fearfully sweeping across the entire venue, and finally sat down without finding anything.

"Was I hallucinating?"

...

"Redflame has lost!"

A sigh came from beside his ear.

Luo Chen's entire body's qi-mechanism suddenly retracted, a thoughtful expression on his face.

Even the method of qi-mechanism sensing had two types.

One was passive sensing. When the qi-mechanisms of both sides came into contact, one could distinguish friend from foe. This was the method mastered by Furnace Realm martial artists. This type was more like a passive defense mechanism brought about by an increase in realm.

The other was the method he had just used, active sensing, which required the Leakless Realm to master. This method was not difficult, but it required one's own massive qi-mechanism as a foundation.

Thinking about it this way, Wang Yuan was truly a genius of unparalleled talent!

With his shallow foundation back then, being able to cross two major realms and master the active qi-mechanism sensing method ahead of time was truly remarkable.

After sighing in admiration, Luo Chen's gaze swept over the crowd.

Tie Xiong, who appeared unperturbed, actually had an incomplete Leakless Golden Body, which was why Luo Chen had been able to capture a trace of his dissipating qi-mechanism. This might be related to him using a secret technique to forcibly break through to the Leakless Realm. This was not surprising; Luo Chen had encountered a big monk like this before, and that person's end had been torn apart alive by him.

Within the vast martial venue, aside from Tie Xiong, the one with the strongest qi-mechanism was that Carefree Envoy. His strength was even above the famous Tie Futu; he was a strong ant.

As for the seemingly unremarkable Young City Lord Xiao Feng, his realm was not bad. Unknown to the world, he had set foot in the Furnace Realm. His strength was even above his younger brother Xiao Yun!

Furthermore, Luo Chen had even detected a familiar qi-mechanism within the martial venue, belonging to Xiao Zirui, whom he had seen earlier. He was also watching the battle in secret.

Luo Chen was very satisfied with this experiment.

"Although the method of qi-mechanism sensing is not as convenient and fast as divine sense detection, it still has its unique merits."

"In this Great Destruction Realm, it is no different from a second pair of eyes besides these eyes of mine."

"If one day I can leave this spiritless land, when fighting with cultivators of the same realm, the method of qi-mechanism sensing might, like when Azure Frost fought me back then, unexpectedly establish an advantage!"

But unlike his current satisfaction and delight, the atmosphere on his side's spectator stand was quite heavy.

Especially after the dispirited Redflame returned, this oppressive atmosphere reached its peak.

Two consecutive losses!

Tie Xiong glanced at Xiao Sanniang and Luo Chen, then calmly stood up.

"It's my turn."
