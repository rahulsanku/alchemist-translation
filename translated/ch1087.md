# Chapter 1087: The Gu Siblings, Rosycloud's Ascension

*Drip!*

*Drip!*

Water droplets from the rock wall fell onto the bluestone, producing crisp sounds that echoed faintly within the underground chamber.

The white-robed man gazed at the slumbering young woman, a complex expression flickering in his weathered eyes.

Kongchan had not followed him inside, instead standing guard outside.

At this moment, only Luo Chen and Zhou Qingyu shared the chamber.

Without any hesitation, he casually waved his hand, and a beam of spiritual light enveloped Zhou Qingyu.

His divine sense surged like water, penetrating her entire body.

Every single state of Zhou Qingyu's being was clearly reflected in Luo Chen's mind.

"So this is what it looks like after successfully building one's foundation by taking a Spirit-Root Pill?"

Ever since obtaining the Spirit-Root Pill formula in the Demon-Fall Land, Luo Chen had rarely had the chance to experiment with it.

He never expected that the most authentic case would present itself before him in this form.

"Meridians too narrow, acupoints blocked, unable to withstand a large influx of spiritual energy. After prolonged cultivation, there are actually numerous hidden injuries."

"The nine-layered qi sea is too tightly connected, precarious as a pile of eggs. The danger during breakthrough is far higher than for ordinary cultivators."

"Divine sense..."

Under Luo Chen's divine sense scan, Zhou Qingyu's physical condition was laid bare, and the results were shocking.

With such poor aptitude, to have cultivated all the way to the mid-Initiate stage, one could only imagine how many resources Zhou Fuyou had spent for his younger sister back then. Even more, one could only imagine what kind of perseverance Zhou Qingyu had used to forcibly endure the pain of cultivation, step by step, to reach her current state.

But this was as far as she could go.

Whether it was her narrow meridians, her blocked acupoints, or her qi sea as precarious as a pile of eggs, all of these limited Zhou Qingyu from advancing further.

Moreover, her body contained a massive accumulation of pill toxins. It was easy to imagine that Zhou Qingyu had been reckless in her pursuit of cultivation.

The only thing worth praising was probably Zhou Qingyu's divine sense!

Its strength was considerable, comparable to late-stage Initiate cultivators even at her mid-Initiate realm.

Yet even so, her upper limit was merely the Adept stage, and even then, she could only condense a false core!

Luo Chen rubbed the crying infant beast's inner core in his hand, his expression somewhat complex.

This was ultimately his karma.

If he hadn't used Zhou Qingyu to nourish his clone back then, she wouldn't have fallen to such a state.

"Forget it!"

Luo Chen let out a faint sigh. He had originally planned to leave behind a few things and go, but his mindset had shifted slightly at this moment.

With one hand, like a spring breeze turning into rain, he brushed across Zhou Qingyu's body from a distance.

Threads of scorching mana warmed her icy-cold body, making it slightly warm.

The *Pure Source Marvelous Pill Art* unfolded, like a broom sweeping away the accumulated pill toxins within her body.

Those pill toxins had settled in her qi sea and attached to her meridians. The process of sweeping them away was quite painful, and even in her slumber, Zhou Qingyu let out groans of pain.

Before she could wake, Luo Chen changed his technique.

His medical skills, proficient to the point of perfection, were continuously applied from his hands.

Repairing meridians, opening acupoints, stimulating the vitality of the flesh, restoring various hidden injuries on her body.

Under Luo Chen's successive ministrations, Zhou Qingyu's condition rapidly improved.

"Finally, there's the qi sea."

Luo Chen's expression shifted slightly. A stream of light flew from his hand, floating before the woman's lips.

It was a round pearl, swirling with five colors, steam rising from it. Even just floating there, it seemed to have the aura of suppressing the heavens and stabilizing the mountains and seas.

The Calming Sea Pearl!

A natal treasure meticulously refined for Zhou Fuyou by the Tiannan Dao Palace at great cost.

The moment the pearl was formed, it had forcefully crossed the thunder tribulation, reaching the level of a true artifact!

This was a treasure with profound foundation and extremely high potential. If nothing unexpected happened, nurtured for a few hundred years by Zhou Fuyou's Profound Nether Dao Body, after he became an Ascendant, it had a great chance of advancing to the level of a spirit treasure.

Back then, Luo Chen's life soul had managed to escape the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven after the self-destruction of the Netherwater Spring Eye precisely by using this treasure.

"Your life belongs to me, but your legacy... I'll leave it to the person you cherished most!"

Luo Chen muttered to himself, unsure whether he was speaking to the former Zhou Fuyou or to his own conscience.

His fingertip transformed his hand seals, striking layer upon layer of restrictions onto the Calming Sea Pearl.

True artifacts needed to absorb a large amount of the host's mana to continuously advance and improve.

These restrictions sealed the formation patterns on the Calming Sea Pearl, temporarily turning it into a dead object that could not absorb mana. Only after the host herself advanced to the Adept stage could she use her divine sense to unravel the restrictions on the Calming Sea Pearl, thread by thread.

Then, Luo Chen pressed down through the void, and the Calming Sea Pearl sank into Zhou Qingyu's mouth, traveling all the way into her qi sea.

Thus, her acupoints were fully opened, the eight meridians converged, and the precious pearl suppressed her qi sea!

Instantly, with the woman's breathing, faint spiritual energy began to flow toward her body.

Luo Chen assessed Zhou Qingyu's current condition.

Although she still couldn't be considered exceptionally talented, she now had the aptitude of an ordinary person.

With this, Luo Chen's promise to Zhou Fuyou was fulfilled.

But for some reason, just as he was about to leave, Luo Chen's footsteps halted.

Looking at the woman's tightly furrowed brows, in silence, he took out a few items from his storage ring.

The first was a Condensation Liquid Pill, which could help someone form their core.

The second was the *Fine Dust Primordial Art*, which also aided in core formation and was of great help for future Sage condensation. This was a self-created secret art that Luo Chen still occasionally practiced even after reaching the Sage stage.

The third was the *Myriad Path Convergence*, which could forcibly enhance one's physical body with the help of massive resources. If Zhou Qingyu's qi-refining path proved impassable, she might consider switching to body refinement.

Finally, unexpectedly, there was a blank jade slip.

Luo Chen gazed at the jade slip, his divine sense stirring slightly as he engraved sentence after sentence of refined words.

**[Demon Cultivator Secret Record: In the world of immortal cultivation, natural selection, survival of the fittest! If one seeks immortality, one must stop at nothing...]**

Word by word, concise and refined, he recorded the key points of how a demon cultivator—or rather, a rogue cultivator—should survive in the cruel world of immortal cultivation and continuously climb upward.

There were both broad principles and practical operational methods.

Changing one's appearance, concealing oneself, not flaunting wealth, keeping hidden cards, biding one's time and developing, making decisive moves, never fully trusting others, always being vigilant...

"I hope you won't be hunted all the way to your cave dwelling without even realizing it again in the future."

Luo Chen let out a soft sigh, placed all the items before the bluestone where the woman slept soundly, and then left with a melancholy heart.

Exiting the nameless small island, Kongchan greeted him.

"Master, is that young girl a descendant of an old acquaintance?"

Luo Chen nodded ambiguously. "You could say that."

Kongchan was puzzled. "Since Master was willing to travel a thousand li to look after her, why not directly bring her back to the Eastern Wilderness and let the Luo Tian Sect's vast power protect her?"

Luo Chen shook his head. "That wouldn't be true cultivation."

Kongchan was taken aback. How was cultivating within a sect not true cultivation?

How many sect disciples in the world had grown up that way?

Luo Chen didn't explain much.

Everyone had their own path!

For a hundred years, Zhou Qingyu had been under her elder brother's protection, never having weathered storms and trials, and would never be able to step onto her own path.

And she had already cultivated at the Tiannan Dao Palace for decades, experiencing the guidance of elders and the instruction of the sect.

Now, Luo Chen had used elaborate methods to remove the obstacles to her aptitude.

If she truly wanted to gain something from cultivation, she had to endure the hardships of the world and climb to higher places.

Luo Chen had been like that back then, and Sage Dragon Abyss of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect had been the same. He believed Zhou Qingyu could do it.

If she couldn't...

True cultivation also came with the threat of death!

"Let's go!"

...

A day and a night later.

From the underground chamber of the nameless small island came a long, drawn-out groan.

Zhou Qingyu sat up from the hard bluestone, stretching comfortably.

She hadn't slept such a peaceful sleep in a long time.

Waking up, she felt completely refreshed, light and relaxed everywhere.

Even the injuries from yesterday...

"Injuries?"

Zhou Qingyu was startled. She instinctively touched her body. Those wounds had inexplicably healed, and the newly grown skin was like creamy snow.

A sweep of her spiritual sense left her even more shocked!

The broken, narrow meridians had become wide, unobstructed, and incredibly resilient.

One by one, the opened acupoints greedily absorbed the scattered mana.

The once-turbulent qi sea was now orderly, divided into nine layers, each stacked upon the next like the foundation of ten thousand generations.

Within that qi sea, a round pearl with concealed spiritual light rose and fell with the surging spiritual power.

It was precisely because of this pearl's presence that the entire qi sea had become vastly different.

"This is..." The woman's body trembled slightly, her face full of disbelief. "The Calming Sea Pearl, it's big brother's natal treasure!"

She suddenly woke up, her eyes sweeping the chamber. There was no one.

Ignoring everything else, the woman leaped up, flew out of the underground chamber, and frantically flew around the nameless small island.

But in the end, she found nothing.

In her memory, there were some things beside the bluestone where she had woken up.

Desperate yet hopeful, Zhou Qingyu hurried back.

Pills, scriptures, and a scroll of the *Demon Cultivator Secret Record*!

Holding the items, she scanned the words at a glance, but she never saw what she wanted to see.

Tears involuntarily filled her eyes.

"Is it big brother?"

"Since you're here, why don't you want to see me?"

"Or is it that the person who came isn't big brother, but someone bringing his..."

The woman dared not dwell on that last thought. She was afraid.

Staring blankly at the jade slip, tears fell silently.

Suddenly!

The woman wiped away her tears, a look of unprecedented determination shining in her eyes.

"No matter where big brother is, I will find him!"

"But I can't stay here any longer."

Thinking of the contents of the jade slip, the woman realized how many mistakes she had made before. Packing up her things, she hurriedly left the nameless small island.

...

"Master, where are we going next?"

"Since we're in the Northern Sea, let's make a trip. I'll also look for two old acquaintances."

When mentioning "old acquaintances," a rare look of wariness appeared on Luo Chen's face.

Kongchan asked curiously, "Who?"

Luo Chen countered, "Have you ever heard of the Black Devil?"

Kongchan was startled, then suddenly understood.

"Is Master referring to that demon who has gained the greatest fame in the Northern Sea cultivation world over the past two hundred years?"

Luo Chen nodded slightly.

In the Northern Sea cultivation world, ever since the demon beast allied army escaped from Yemo Heaven, it had been plunged into successive great battles.

Countless sects and families had been implicated, in a state of dire hardship.

In such a cruel environment, demonic cultivators and heretical practitioners thrived instead, often doing things that boggled the mind.

Destroying mountains and exterminating clans, refining souls into banners, slaughtering demon beasts and extracting their essence blood to nourish themselves...

Everything one would imagine demon cultivators doing had been happening endlessly in the Northern Sea cultivation world over the past two hundred years.

Among the many demon cultivators and heretics, one person stood out the most!

His true name was unknown to anyone. Because he was skilled in using a black pottery jar and acted with extreme cruelty, he was called the Black Devil.

If it were just that, Luo Chen wouldn't care.

But coincidentally, the black pottery jar described in the intelligence reports had immense power!

Even when several Sage cultivators of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect joined forces to besiege him back then, he still managed to escape.

Moreover, wherever the Black Devil appeared, another woman wearing a golden crown, like a goddess, would often appear. This forced Luo Chen to make associations.

The Black Devil Jar, the golden crown, the Demon-Fall Land, Bright Radiance Heaven!

Back then, during that secret realm expedition, Luo Chen and Moon Wanderer were not the only ones who passed the inheritance trials.

The most unexpected were a pair of siblings at the Adept stage who successfully passed the trials of the Light and Dark Halls.

At that time, Luo Chen was also at the Adept stage. After passing the Pill Realm trial, he had been washed by the Spirit-Molding Sacred Spring, and his aptitude had undergone earth-shaking changes. Not only that, he had also obtained the alchemy inheritance of Devil Lord Heaven-Refining. If nothing unexpected had happened, he should have also obtained a spirit treasure, the Heaven-Refining Cauldron, but unfortunately...

One could imagine that the pair of siblings, also at the Adept stage, after passing their trials, had obtained two spirit treasures. Their future achievements would be immeasurable.

And Luo Chen had a blood-deep enmity with them!

He had killed their father, Feiyunzi!

He didn't believe that others would not seek revenge, just as he himself had endured for ten or a hundred years to achieve peace of mind.

Such a venomous snake lurking in the shadows—if it were before, it wouldn't matter.

But as Luo Chen led the Eastern Wilderness experts to attack the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, his name had spread throughout the world. His past experiences, even his many titles, had been dug up.

Luo Chen had become the most conspicuous target!

With his abilities, he naturally wasn't afraid.

But if the other party learned from him and kept enduring, enduring until Luo Chen became an Ascendant, enduring until Luo Chen ascended, and then struck at the Luo Tian Sect members he left behind, what could he do about it?

For now, the two were definitely no match for him. If he could find them, he might as well uproot the weeds and eliminate them.

If he delayed any longer, until he successfully became an Ascendant, there would be many things to worry about.

Ascendant powerhouses in this realm were not incapable of acting.

But they could not act lightly.

Every time they acted, there was actually a price to pay.

Massively manipulating the origin qi of heaven and earth would cause their primordial spirit, which had not yet condensed a phantom form, to become too closely connected with this world. In the future, ascending and leaving this realm would become extremely difficult.

In fact, this was the real reason why Sage cultivators roamed the Mountains and Seas Realm, but Ascendant powerhouses rarely made a move.

Whenever a powerhouse made a move, there was always a reason, and there would always be a result!

Luo Chen didn't want to wait until that time, so he wanted to take care of this matter while he was passing through.

However, some things never go as one wishes.

Luo Chen's expression suddenly shifted. He took out a large seal.

It was the seal of the Truth Crossing Hall Master.

A slightly anxious voice came from within.

"Hall Master, the Primordial is about to ascend. Please return to Green Parasol Mountain immediately!"

Luo Chen was stunned, a look of disbelief in his eyes.

Rosycloud, in that state, could ascend?
