# Chapter 724: Devouring Netherfire, Using a Favor to Seek Repayment

On the quiet flower-hall walkway, two figures walked slowly, one in front and one behind.

Luo Chen's gaze remained fixed on the scorched footprints left wherever Old Monster Netherfire passed.

Those footprints were extremely conspicuous, but usually after three steps they vanished without trace, as if nothing had happened at all.

But Luo Chen knew this was absolutely no illusion!

"A dignified mid-stage Sage cultivator actually cannot suppress the true flame inside his body and allows it to leak outward?"

This inconceivable thought surfaced in Luo Chen's mind.

He was an expert of the fire dao, so he could naturally see that those scorched footprints were caused by the leakage of some deathly true flame.

Combined with the other party's Daoist title, Netherfire, it was obvious that Old Monster Netherfire was also proficient in the fire dao and had refined some kind of source-less fire into his source true flame.

What was strange was precisely this: how could a cultivator fail to suppress his own source true flame?

Unless...

"Wilderness Wanderer, have you seen some clue?"

The elder's hoarse voice sounded by his ear. Luo Chen's expression remained calm, and he nodded slightly.

For the other party to appear so "wretched" was surely intentional. If Luo Chen still could not see this much, he would not have been invited alone to the rear hall.

Old Monster Netherfire smiled bitterly. "Perhaps you think I am doing this deliberately, but the condition inside this old man's body is already as dangerous as stacked eggs."

Luo Chen asked probingly, "Could it be that the source true flame Stream Lord refined has devoured its master?"

"Mm."

That faint sound contained emotions of extreme complexity.

Old Monster Netherfire sighed and began speaking of the past.

"That Mu Lan Gaoxiong, even though his Dao path was severed for life, used my heavy injury and Frost Willow's fall as stepping stones to once again establish a ferocious name."

"The world only thinks that Frost Willow and I were lacking, but how could they know the inside story?"

Facing Netherfire's sudden desire to talk, Luo Chen did not interrupt.

He knew of Frost Willow. This should be the early-stage Sage Stream Lord that Mu Lan Gaoxiong had personally killed among his many battle achievements.

However, in Luo Chen's view, even under his current heavy injuries, Old Monster Netherfire still possessed a powerful aura contained within. That Mu Lan Gaoxiong could heavily injure him was truly somewhat unbelievable.

Sure enough, Old Monster Netherfire revealed the inside reason from that year.

"With this old man's strength, though I cannot say I could stably defeat Mu Lan Gaoxiong, suppressing him by a head was never a problem."

"Back then, after learning Frost Willow had died in battle, I personally went out to fight. Unexpectedly, during the battle, the true flame inside my body ran wild. I was not defeated by Mu Lan Gaoxiong, but by my own hands!"

"Now, that netherfire roams throughout my body, burning my five viscera and scorching my apertures. It has even entrenched itself inside my Purple Mansion and wishes to usurp the nest!"

Luo Chen's eyelids twitched wildly as he listened. A faint fear even rose in his heart.

As expected, this was almost exactly the same as his guess.

"Stream Lord, could it be that your source true flame has already awakened intelligence?"

Old Monster Netherfire smiled bitterly. "You have indeed seen through it. My netherfire has truly awakened intelligence and gained its own awareness."

Luo Chen did not understand. "Even if true flame awakens intelligence, it should obey its master's will. How could it retaliate? Unless its spirituality..."

A glazed treasure bottle appeared in Old Monster Netherfire's hand.

It was precisely the treasure bottle containing soul essence!

"In the past, the Primordial Demon Sect's soul-refining lineage voluntarily handed the method of refining soul essence to the side-path forces of the Northern Sea, but they did not pass down the method of using it."

"After the demonic sect's destruction, none of us side-path cultivators have not pondered how to use such a strange object as soul essence."

"This old man does not know how others researched it, but back then, this old man took the wrong road and attempted to use a large quantity of soul essence to nourish the spirituality of true flame. This caused the netherfire I refined to surge in intelligence, until later even my own divine soul could no longer suppress it."

"This is the reason it turned against me in battle and devoured my body!"

After receiving this answer, Luo Chen came to a sudden understanding, while also looking as if it was no more than he had expected.

Back then, he had actually almost fallen into this exact predicament.

After all, the higher the spirituality of true flame, the greater its assistance to oneself, whether in alchemy, artifact refining, or battle against enemies.

But in the end, Luo Chen had suppressed that impulse.

He had always suppressed the spirituality of the Withering-Bloom True Flame. Only after he achieved the Sage realm did he thoroughly release the growth of the Withering-Bloom True Flame's intelligence.

If compared to human intelligence, the current Withering-Bloom True Flame's spirituality had grown roughly to that of a seven- or eight-year-old child.

But its own power was genuine, without the slightest discount.

He found it very difficult to imagine to what degree the intelligence of the netherfire inside Old Monster Netherfire's body had grown after absorbing a large quantity of soul essence, that it dared to devour its master and even wanted to usurp the nest!

However, what did this have to do with seeking him out for a private chat?

The other party did not know that his Withering-Bloom True Flame possessed the ability to devour other source-less fires with the assistance of the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture.

As if sensing Luo Chen's doubt, Old Monster Netherfire's steps stopped.

He turned to look at Luo Chen, and an aura rose without warning.

It was a Sage domain!

Luo Chen raised his brow and likewise released his Sage domain.

The two domains drew in the spiritual qi of heaven and earth, and both drew in fire-attribute spiritual qi, engaging in a probing collision within this small garden.

Overall, Luo Chen was at a disadvantage.

But in localized terms, his Encompassing Fire Prison was continuously melting the other party's domain.

Seeing this scene, Old Monster Netherfire released a breath. "As expected, I did not misjudge you. You possess absolute control over your source true flame, even reaching the degree of resonance, causing your domain to undergo a qualitative change. One can imagine that your true flame itself possesses even stronger melting power."

The Sage domain appeared and withdrew in an instant.

Luo Chen also put away his domain and frowned. "Stream Lord, what does this mean?"

"Very simple. I need you to use that source true flame of yours that can melt all things to help me destroy the netherfire inside my body! If this matter succeeds, this bottle of soul essence, condensed from tens of millions of people, will be the payment I give you!"

Luo Chen's expression changed slightly. "Stream Lord, you must be joking. If two true flames collide with each other, the battlefield will be inside your body. Are you not afraid..."

"They will not collide!"

Old Monster Netherfire shook his head. "At that time, I will do everything in my power to suppress the netherfire's spirituality, allowing your true flame to destroy it bit by bit. Even if there are minor injuries, they are within the range I can bear."

Luo Chen opened his mouth. It seemed this truly did make some sense.

He had experienced the pain brought by two kinds of true flame colliding inside his body.

Nine-Refined Beacon Fire and the Nirvana Sacred Flame!

But at that time, the greatest reason had actually been that he had not possessed enough ability to suppress either true flame, which caused the tragic collapse of his fleshly body.

If Old Monster Netherfire truly could suppress the netherfire inside his body and perfectly cooperate with him, then Luo Chen really did have the ability to completely annihilate the netherfire inside him.

However, this meant he had no choice but to consider another matter.

Under such circumstances, Old Monster Netherfire would be almost entirely defenseless, laying everything about himself bare before Luo Chen.

Life and death, taking and seizing, would all be in Luo Chen's hands!

Luo Chen asked hesitantly, "Before today, you and I did not know each other. Stream Lord actually trusts me this much?"

To this, Old Monster Netherfire smiled freely.

"Of course not. This old man is no naive child without the slightest guard against others. The one this old man trusts is not you, but Fellow Daoist Fu."

At this moment, the doors of the rear hall slowly opened without wind.

"Please!"

Luo Chen took a deep breath and stepped into the great hall.

The moment he entered, a wealthy-looking elder holding a yellowed bronze mirror entered Luo Chen's eyes.

Seeing this person, Luo Chen could not help but reveal a smile.

The elder looked at Luo Chen and also smilingly performed a Daoist salute.

"Fu Chaosheng of the Tianyuan Merchant Alliance greets Fellow Daoist Luo Chen!"

He stressed the two words "Luo Chen" especially heavily.

Luo Chen's heart paused, and he likewise returned the salute. "Wanderer Luo Chen greets Fellow Daoist Fu."

Only after the two became acquainted did Old Monster Netherfire say, "Elder Fu is precisely the intermediary of this righteous-demonic War-Ceasing Assembly, responsible for mediation in the name of Central Continent's Tianyuan Dao Sect. With the name of a sacred land and the Tianyuan Merchant Alliance's credibility, with him acting as guarantor, you need not worry that I will do anything unfavorable to you while you heal me, and I need not worry that you will suddenly make a hidden move. Unless, of course, none of us wishes to walk the cultivation world anymore."

"Wilderness Wanderer, what do you think?"

Luo Chen glanced at Fu Chaosheng. The other party smiled and nodded.

"Since that is the case, this wanderer will reluctantly give it a try."

"Good!"

Old Monster Netherfire opened his hand, and the glazed treasure bottle fell into Fu Chaosheng's hand.

The meaning was self-evident.

As long as this matter succeeded, Luo Chen could go to Fu Chaosheng to claim the payment.

With the Tianyuan Merchant Alliance's reputation of doing business across the five continents under heaven, they naturally would not embezzle this small thing.

Luo Chen asked about the time for healing.

Old Monster Netherfire seemed impatient. Rather than choosing another day, they might as well do it today.

Luo Chen naturally had no objection. The sooner the matter was finished, the sooner he could obtain the soul essence.

As for the gathering still continuing in Return Origin Palace, they had already lost interest.

Fu Chaosheng merely said a few simple words, then no longer spoke. Only, his small narrowed eyes kept moving over Luo Chen, revealing an expression of great interest.

...

Inside the quiet secret chamber.

The elder sat cross-legged, his mind absent, like a walking corpse.

It could be seen that all his attention had merged into the Nascent Soul in his Purple Mansion, where he was doing everything he could to suppress the netherfire's awareness.

Luo Chen stood beside him. With a thought, he released the Withering-Bloom True Flame.

A blue-gold fire tree stretched out its branches and slowly enveloped the elder.

"So this is netherfire?"

When one branch of the fire tree drew out a wisp of deathly, pale-white flame, Luo Chen carefully observed it.

Death qi spread over it, and indistinctly, it seemed as if countless dead souls and resentful spirits were wailing endlessly within.

Luo Chen's expression turned solemn. He had never expected that someone would advance the most ordinary ghost fire to such a level.

Yes. Netherfire sounded very unusual, but in truth, it was not rare in the cultivation world.

At its essence, it was ghost fire transformed from the soul, blood, and flesh of living beings after death.

If one truly discussed grade, it was not even equal to the man-made Azure Yang Demon Flame.

Yet Old Monster Netherfire had relied on tremendous ability and vast resources to forcibly push this flame to the fourth tier.

He had even invested a large amount of soul essence to greatly increase its spirituality!

By Luo Chen's judgment, this flame was in no way inferior to most of the fourth-tier source-less fires he had seen in Blazing Refinement Prison, and merely had not yet transformed.

It had not transformed, yet had awakened intelligence. No wonder the netherfire retaliated and wanted to usurp the nest.

Forcing growth was never good.

Luo Chen shook his head and activated the Withering-Bloom True Flame to forcibly melt away this wisp of netherfire in his hand.

Just as he was about to continue, a sudden thought arose in his heart.

"Why destroy it? Why not devour it?"

Thinking of this, Luo Chen was moved.

Netherfire of such quality was rare in the world. It would be far too wasteful to destroy it like this.

If he could devour it, perhaps his Withering-Bloom True Flame could advance another step.

Moreover, this way, it would not harm Old Monster Netherfire's body. The man would still have to thank him.

Without hesitation, Luo Chen immediately circulated the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture. The nature of the Withering-Bloom True Flame changed, and it instantly sank into Old Monster Netherfire's body, beginning to absorb the scattered netherfire spread throughout his fleshly body.

Time passed bit by bit.

One day, two days...

The gathering in Return Origin Palace ended that very night.

After seeing Old Monster Netherfire and Wilderness Wanderer fail to return for a long time, everyone understood something.

Among them, Old Ghost Nanqi's face was dark, and his anger was entirely undisguised.

But Molan Court was Moluo Stream's territory after all, and with his status, he did not dare act recklessly. He could only leave hatefully.

Inside the rear hall of Return Origin Palace, Fu Chaosheng wiped the bronze mirror in his hand. On it, a graceful figure appeared vividly, and seeing this, the smile in Fu Chaosheng's brows and eyes grew even thicker.

...

The ninth day of healing arrived soundlessly.

Old Monster Netherfire also opened his eyes soundlessly.

Sensing the condition inside his body, he exhaled a breath of turbid qi.

On his face there was both a smile of release from heavy burden and a trace of regret, as if he had lost something.

With the deep illness gone, he only needed to recuperate properly for a time, and then he could continue advancing along the Great Dao.

But the netherfire he had nurtured for several hundred years had not left even the slightest wisp behind, which made him feel rather regretful.

"I truly did not misjudge him. That Wilderness Wanderer's attainment in the fire dao is not inferior to mine in the slightest. In a mere nine days, he thoroughly melted away all my netherfire."

"However, where is he?"

Surprised, Old Monster Netherfire walked out of the secret chamber.

Fu Chaosheng happened to be outside.

"Do not disturb him for now. In order to help heal you, Wilderness Wanderer suffered considerable loss and is resting in Ten-Thousand Streams Ravine."

"Is that so?"

Old Monster Netherfire smiled. "I did indeed trouble him. I had intended to thank him face to face, but there are some matters I must handle as soon as possible. I will have to trouble Fellow Daoist to transfer the soul essence to him."

"It was a trade to begin with. There is no need to be so courteous."

Fu Chaosheng spoke smilingly.

After the other party hurriedly left, he slowly walked along the complex palace pathways and finally came to a mountain ravine where countless small waterfalls gathered.

At that time, waterfalls hung suspended and the rain curtain fell like a screen.

A white-robed Daoist sat quietly there with his hair loose.

A surging vitality and a chilling death intent continuously circulated over his body, and blue-gold flames bared teeth and claws as they emerged from him, burning the void until it distorted.

Seeing this scene, Fu Chaosheng took a deep breath, and the previous calm on his face could no longer be suppressed.

"Borrowing fire to entrust the soul, merging spirit with heaven and earth!"

At this moment, the Daoist's whole body trembled, and all the flames withdrew into his body.

Fu Chaosheng was stunned. "Did I disturb you?"

Luo Chen stood up with a frown, a confused look on his face.

"No. I only feel that something is missing."

Fu Chaosheng released a breath. "That is a pity. I thought you were about to comprehend the true meaning of laws."

"How could law power be so easy to comprehend? Fellow Daoist thinks too highly of me." Luo Chen smiled.

But Fu Chaosheng shook his head. "Law power is indeed difficult to comprehend, but within the Mountains and Seas Realm, it is an exception. This realm is abundant in spiritual qi, overflowing with spiritual vitality. There are powerful desolate beasts that can master law power once they reach adulthood, and we humans are not inferior in comprehension. Moreover, you, Devil Lord Azure Yang, advanced from the fourth level of the Golden Core Stage to the Sage realm in a mere hundred years. With talent so terrifying, if you were said to be capable of grasping the surface of laws in early-stage Sage, this old man would believe it."

With these words, the confusion on Luo Chen's face vanished.

He turned to look at the wealthy elder. "I believe I concealed myself well enough, whether in appearance, name, or even the aura of my magic power, all of which are greatly different from a hundred years ago. How did you recognize me?"

Fu Chaosheng laughed loudly, and the bronze mirror appeared in his hand again.

"Your disguise is naturally decent. In a hurry, I definitely could not recognize you. But that transformed demon beast maid of yours, I remember very deeply!"

On the bronze mirror, Heavenjade's figure appeared faintly.

"This is?"

"True Spirit Treasure Mirror! My life-bound true artifact, refined in imitation of a spirit treasure. It can peer through false and real and appraise treasures. Especially for demon beasts, once their aura has been recorded, there is nowhere for them to hide. This righteous-demonic War-Ceasing Assembly is a top-priority matter for Northern Sea cultivators. To prevent a transformed Demon Emperor from sneaking in, I have naturally kept it open at all times."

Luo Chen looked at the bronze mirror and came to a sudden understanding.

He had already done his best to have Heavenjade conceal her movements, precisely because he feared Sage experts would discover her Fighting Gull true body and avoid unnecessary trouble.

Usually, when there were matters to handle, he basically sent Sang Jinghe out to deal with them.

Unexpectedly, someone still discovered the clue.

However, this was not a bad thing. His purpose in coming to the War-Ceasing Assembly had included finding Fu Chaosheng to begin with.

He wanted to use the Tianyuan Merchant Alliance's route to go to Central Continent, then return to the Eastern Wilderness.

Moreover, the fact that the other party knew the name Luo Chen yet did not connect it to matters on the Eastern Wilderness side also allowed Luo Chen to release a breath inwardly.

Regarding his collusion with the demon race, judging from the current situation, it seemed the uproar was not particularly large?

That made sense. Back then, he had only just entered Golden Core. His reputation might have had some weight in the Jade Cauldron Domain, but when spread across the entire Eastern Wilderness, it was nothing.

That matter had also been relatively secret. At present, perhaps outsiders did not know.

At this moment.

Since he had already been recognized, Luo Chen no longer concealed himself, and he cupped his hands in salute toward the elder.

Fu Chaosheng dodged aside. "What is the meaning of this?"

Luo Chen straightened. "Fellow Daoist's guidance back then benefited me greatly. A word of thanks is only proper."

Fu Chaosheng said "oh," then waved a hand. "A few casual words are nothing."

Luo Chen did not think so. "Blood Nightmare Moluo took the initiative to recruit me back then. If not for Fellow Daoist kindly reminding me, I fear I truly would have entered a demon den."

"You mean that..."

Fu Chaosheng smiled faintly. This matter truly had been discovered by him first.

No one else knew that Blood Nightmare Moluo had attempted to unite the three lineages, or even refined the Primordial Demon disciples under him into Blood God Children.

Only he, with the True Spirit Treasure Mirror at his side, had seen a slight clue.

That was why he could remind Luo Chen back then to avoid going to Moluo Stream as much as possible.

"It is not only that. Whether Fellow Daoist's guidance on cultivation, the treasure you bestowed upon my maid, or introducing me to exchange with fellow cultivators, all benefited my later cultivation greatly. That I could achieve the Sage realm today is partly owed to Fellow Daoist's merit."

Luo Chen placed one tall hat after another onto his head. What he said was fact, but now that he had already become a Sage and still praised him like this, anyone hearing it would feel comfortable.

When seeking help, finding someone upon whom one had bestowed favor was actually not easy, but finding someone who had shown favor to oneself was often easier.

This logic was a little counterintuitive, but reality was precisely like this.

But what Luo Chen had not expected was that Fu Chaosheng would "use a favor to seek repayment"!

"Since you are so grateful, why not help me with one matter?"

Luo Chen froze.

*(End of Chapter)*
