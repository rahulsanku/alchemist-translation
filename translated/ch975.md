# Chapter 975: Landing on the Great Desolate, Star Gate Opens, Phoenix Arrives in Central Continent

Another night fell, deep and still.

Luo Chen, who had been sitting for a long time, slowly opened his eyes.

He rubbed his brow, a look of exhaustion on his face.

With the continuous replenishment of soul stones, his main soul had always remained at its peak state. In theory, he could keep dividing it indefinitely.

But by the time he reached the eighth soul, extremely difficult signs had already emerged.

He had wanted to push through in one go and split off a ninth soul. Even if it was weaker, he could slowly nurture it. But precisely because of this, he had been unable to separate the ninth soul for a long time.

Furthermore, he had a premonition that once he split off the ninth soul, something extremely unfavorable would happen.

That kind of accident was something he absolutely did not want to see.

"Where exactly is the problem?"

Luo Chen dared not act rashly. He trusted his instincts.

As one's realm grew higher, some instinctual intuitions became more accurate. Cultivators simply could not afford to ignore them.

He could only carefully analyze, find the problem, and then solve it accordingly.

"Is it a problem with the foundation I laid? Should I have learned from Han Zhan, first splitting off nine souls, then slowly strengthening them?"

Luo Chen didn't quite believe that. If one were to talk about the depth of foundation, he was still above Han Zhan.

"Then is it because I've been splitting my soul too frequently over the past ten years, to the point that my main soul can't sustain it?"

Behind strength lies weakness. The seemingly resilient main soul might have long been unable to bear further division.

Luo Chen wasn't entirely sure about this, because the heaven-defying effects of the soul stones hadn't shown any side effects.

In fact, during his leisure time, he had deliberately collected a large pile of soul stones, preparing for when he would leave the Soul Severing Cliff in the future.

"Or perhaps... is it the inherent incompleteness of the *Soul-Splitting Art* that prevents me from achieving full success?"

Regarding this last speculation, Luo Chen only gave it a fleeting thought.

The *Soul-Splitting Art* did have incomplete parts, but the missing section was about tempering the soul.

And that part, he had already made up for in his early years with the Great Soul Pill.

Moreover, the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture* he had rebuilt also came with a considerable soul-tempering method. The problem shouldn't lie there.

Unable to find an answer despite much thought, Luo Chen could only sigh softly.

Could it be that eight divided souls were his limit?

"Perhaps I've been too greedy. In less than ten years, I've achieved what would take ordinary people hundreds or even thousands of years. I should be satisfied. How can I still crave perfection?"

Exhaling a mouthful of turbid qi, Luo Chen stood up straight, left the stone cave dwelling that sheltered him from wind and rain, and strolled to the mountainside.

Under the night sky, the Soul Island, with no wind or waves, actually felt somewhat eerie.

Vaguely, there seemed to be sounds of ghosts wailing and wolves howling.

Luo Chen's expression was calm, long accustomed to this.

He gazed into the distance, his divine sense slowly spreading out.

One li, two li... ten li.

"Ten li of land?"

Luo Chen nodded slightly. Compared to the hundred zhang of land when he first entered the island, this change was more than ten times the difference.

From this, one could also see the tremendous growth in his soul foundation!

But it was still not enough!

With a thought, golden light flickered in Luo Chen's eyes, and his pupils suddenly turned gold.

Not only that, but source power followed a familiar path into his eyes, and streaks of bloodshot lines spread out.

Instantly, the image of red eyes and golden pupils appeared once again.

Gazing into the distance, it was a full thousand li.

Here, divine sense was greatly suppressed. Although his vision was also affected by the murky sky light, it was relatively much better.

Yet, there was not much joy on Luo Chen's face.

It was only because this pair of eyes of his still could not pierce through the Yellow Spring Sea and reach the outside world.

"Under these circumstances, I have no full confidence in safely leaving the Yellow Spring Sea."

"Should I take a gamble?"

Luo Chen asked himself, appearing hesitant.

But in reality, the moment he posed this question, the answer was already determined.

There was not a trace of spiritual qi on the Soul Severing Cliff. He could not refine qi.

The supplies he carried with him could not support him in advancing further in body refinement.

Even the path of soul refinement, which he had the most hope for, had reached a bottleneck with the obstruction of splitting the ninth soul.

If he continued to stay, he would live out his life and die of old age on this lonely island.

Even with a lifespan of a thousand years, it would be useless.

Taking a risky gamble was necessary.

And his greatest reliance, aside from his greatly increased soul power, was...

Luo Chen looked inward. In his Purple Mansion, the green-white fire tree had already spread its branches.

The Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit, which had been over-consumed earlier, had awakened.

Although it was not at its peak, with the experience of one trip through the Yellow Spring Sea, Luo Chen believed that under his own control, this time going out would be smoother.

Suddenly!

Just as Luo Chen's focus was on himself, his body swayed slightly.

Luo Chen withdrew his divine sense in surprise. Had he not stood steady?

How could that be!

His gaze fell downward.

The dark, dense sand was trembling slightly.

The soul stones, which had become somewhat sparse after his extensive harvesting, were also bouncing lightly.

Not only that...

Luo Chen pursed his lips and looked into the distance.

For the past eight years, the surface of the Yellow Spring Sea, which had been calm every night, was now restless.

The wind came from nowhere, but the waves rose from the Yellow Spring itself.

Splash...

Under the night sky, the crisp sound of waves crashing carried far and wide.

Luo Chen stared at this scene in shock, murmuring inwardly: "What is happening?"

Perhaps the question he should ask was not what was happening, but where the Soul Severing Cliff had drifted to during this time.

And the answer came with the break of dawn, given by this sea area.

Under the light of dawn, the usually murky Yellow Spring Sea actually reflected blurry images.

There was a dense green light, spreading from east to north at a slow but undeniable pace. Within the range of the green light, stalks of green grain were swaying.

There were undulating terrains, as if some giant creature was moving underground, causing great commotion. Wherever it passed, mountain ridges rose high, an image of a thousand mountains bowing in submission.

In the sky, a thousand-zhang golden roc flapped its wings, tearing black lines into the void.

There was also a red giant beast, like a tall building, rushing over from the great sea. Its body was covered in layers of ghostly shadows, and each step it took raised waves that seemed enough to destroy the world.

And in the endless nine heavens above, there were dense dark clouds that made it hard to breathe.

Occasionally, thunder and lightning would fall, striking down tiny figures that charged into the sky.

Within the black clouds, a graceful figure flickered in and out of sight, her true face unseen.

Four giant beasts, landing from the four cardinal directions, charged towards a mountain peak that was so high it seemed to connect heaven and earth, impossible to scale.

And within that mountain peak, stars twinkled, a gate of light opened wide, and figures radiating terrifying auras were walking out from within. Each hazy figure exuded an aura of anger.

Suddenly, thunder and fire intertwined, forming a giant hand of destruction that slapped across the void towards the towering mountain peak that connected heaven and earth, provoking a sky-shaking roar from within the star gate.

Splash...

A giant wave crashed over, shattering the reflected image into pieces, which then completely disappeared.

Luo Chen's mouth fell open, revealing a rare moment of loss of composure in his life. He stood frozen on the spot.

After a long time, he murmured: "Was it an illusion?"

...

"It must be an illusion!"

"Mirage scenes have been recorded since ancient times. Many times, they can even distort space and time, reflecting images from extremely far away or from many years ago."

"That fleeting glimpse was most likely a scene from an ancient great war."

"Wild beasts, great demons, Ascendant-level experts, visitors from beyond the heavens..."

Walking along the mountainside of the Soul Severing Cliff, Luo Chen unconsciously headed towards the summit.

His mind was still filled with the scene he had just witnessed.

He tried to convince himself it was an illusion, but in his memory, that graceful figure flickering in and out of sight amidst the dark clouds and thunder was unforgettable.

Thump!

Luo Chen stopped in his tracks.

The look of shock and doubt on his face slowly faded.

He could not deceive himself. That dignified and noble figure was so familiar. There was absolutely no way he could have mistaken it.

Furthermore, she was a figure from within the last thousand years. It was impossible for her to be linked to an ancient great war.

The ancient demon phoenix, Rosycloud!

"No mistake, it must be her. That kind of bearing was also displayed when she destroyed the Nether Origin Sect."

"And this time, the target has been replaced by the even stronger Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!"

Luo Chen swallowed his saliva, carefully recalling the scene he had glimpsed in a daze.

There were four powerful existences comparable to Ascendant-level experts, landing on the Central Continent from four directions. He could directly determine the origins of two of them.

The one with the dense green light was most likely the Azure Grain Immortal from the Eastern Wilderness, a being who had attained the Dao from grass and trees.

The one whose body was like a red tower, toppling mountains and seas, was the Northern Sea wild beast, the Ghost Immortal Tower, whom he had met a few times. From the aura it revealed, it seemed to have already broken through the fourth rank and become a fifth-rank Great Desolate.

As for the other two.

The thousand-zhang golden roc, could it be the legendary Golden-Winged Great Peng that was wreaking havoc in the Western Desert?

This beast's background was not simple. It possessed a powerful bloodline itself, and had once entered the Buddhist sect, becoming a protector of the Relic Court. Later, it had betrayed its trust, stealing thirty-six eminent monks' relics. It was truly extremely powerful.

It should be correct!

In his early years, when Luo Chen mainly cultivated the *Heavenly Roc Transformation*, it listed several advancement paths such as the Thunder Roc, Fire Roc, and Golden Roc. Luo Chen had once studied this knowledge.

The external features of the Golden-Winged Great Peng were simply too obvious.

As for the last one, its figure was unseen, hidden beneath the earth's surface.

But vaguely, Luo Chen felt a sense of familiarity.

"It's the Netherworld Earth Dragon!"

In an instant, Luo Chen identified the source of that familiar feeling.

His battle with this beast seemed like it was only yesterday. It had once pushed him to the brink of life and death.

Therefore, even without seeing its figure, Luo Chen was certain that the one landing from the south, causing the earth's veins to crack and a thousand mountains to bow in submission, was definitely the Netherworld Earth Dragon!

But there was a point of confusion. When this beast first appeared, it was far from being as powerful as it was in the image.

Especially after the corpse of the old Netherworld Earth Dragon had collapsed, the small Netherworld Earth Dragon that escaped was nearly severely injured in its origin qi. How could it have the almost world-destroying power shown in the image?

Suddenly.

Luo Chen thought of some ambiguous actions of Divine Lord Youli before his ascension.

At that time, Luo Chen had asked him why he had let the Netherworld Earth Dragon go.

Divine Lord Youli's answer was that the Life and Death Gate had no capable people who could control this beast.

With the gap in their realms, Luo Chen could only take his word for it.

But looking at it now, the truth was probably not so!

"Rosycloud attacked the Central Continent because the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's entry into the Eastern Wilderness angered her?"

"If it were the Nether Origin Sect, it might be explainable. But how could there be someone in this world so incapable of enduring provocation, repeatedly making moves to destroy sacred lands?"

"First the Primordial Demon Sect, then the Nether Origin Sect, and now the strongest sect in this realm, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!"

Luo Chen was more inclined to believe that Rosycloud had been planning this for a long time.

This could be seen from the fact that when she attacked the Nether Origin Sect, the first thing she did was rescue the Azure Grain Immortal.

The foundation of a sacred land was incredibly powerful!

When the Primordial Demon Sect was destroyed, it sealed its sect for a hundred years, imprisoning tens of millions of demon beasts.

Before the fall of the three Nether Origin Sect Ancestors, they had spared no expense, nearly dragging Rosycloud down to hell with them.

During the great calamity of the Life and Death Gate, Divine Lord Youli had killed a million cultivators with a single strike.

It could be seen that any sacred land possessed a foundation worthy of its thousand-year existence!

Would Rosycloud dare to take such risks just for a momentary impulse?

It must be because the captured Azure Grain Immortal was of great use to her, which was why she spared no risk and took action herself.

And what was this use for?

The answer had now emerged.

"Everything... was to deal with that behemoth-like existence in the Central Continent?"

Luo Chen recalled the fleeting image, becoming more certain of his speculation.

Gathering four great wild beasts, drawing boundless heavenly tribulation—none of this was accidental.

In that case, would the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect follow in the footsteps of the Primordial Demon Sect and the Nether Origin Sect?

Luo Chen pursed his lips, his expression grave.

In his memory, he hadn't seen the rumored Five Elders of the Dao Sect.

According to the intelligence previously gathered by the Fierce Burial Corpse Alliance, the five Ascendant-level Ancestor Elders of the Dao Sect each presided over a battlefield, with only the Sect Master, Mo Shouzhuo, sitting in the Central Continent.

However, the powerful figures walking out from the star gate on the Heaven-Earth Peak in the image made Luo Chen dare not underestimate them.

"The *Xuanjie Secret Record* records the general situation of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's main sect in the Xuan Realm. The Sect Master, Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign, is a solid Body Integration realm expert. The four Sacred Spirit Elders beneath him are even Refining Void True Lords. Ascendant-level experts number in the hundreds!"

"And according to Divine Lord Youli, the only one within the Mountains and Seas Realm that can still communicate with the upper realm is the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect."

"Faced with such a life-and-death crisis, begging for help from the main sect in the upper realm is only natural."

"Those powerful figures comparable to Ascendant-level existences that I saw should be the reinforcements sent down by the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect from the upper realm."

"With such powerful reinforcements, can Rosycloud destroy the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, and even retreat safely?"

"Most importantly..."

Luo Chen's eyes narrowed slightly. His mind was churning with countless thoughts, and he murmured to himself.

"What exactly is her goal?"

On the lonely Soul Island, no one answered Luo Chen's question. Only the previously calm Yellow Spring Sea roared incessantly, raising monstrous waves.

Luo Chen tried to connect all the information he knew, to analyze Rosycloud's actions.

From the Eastern Wilderness to the Northern Sea, then to the Southern Frontier, and even the Western Desert he had never set foot in—putting all the bits and pieces of intelligence together, no matter how he thought about it, he couldn't find a single thread.

"In the end, it's still because my realm is insufficient!"

A sigh drifted out faintly.

He believed that the ascended Divine Lord Youli must know some inside information.

Even the reason why the other party had been so kind to him might be related to Rosycloud. After all, his own dharma power attribute and the Nirvana Sacred Flame on his body could not be hidden from such a peak existence of this realm.

If he had an Ascendant-level cultivation, perhaps he would be able to know some secrets.

"Forget it!"

Luo Chen paused in his steps, then turned and went down the mountain, heading towards his cave dwelling.

Taking advantage of this turbulent time in the Yellow Spring Sea, the best opportunity for him to leave this Soul Island had arrived.

It was time to make some preparations!

(End of this chapter)
