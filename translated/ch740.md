# Chapter 740: True Flame Mutation, the Alchemy Saint Ascends

Rogue cultivators were not necessarily weak.

Famous rogue cultivators all had a few tricks, more or less.

But cultivators who satisfied the conditions to enter Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven had to meet the requirement of having formed the Nascent Soul less than a hundred years ago.

In such a short time, early-stage Sage cultivators were often still at the stage of consolidating their foundations, nourishing their origins, and exploring their own paths.

If they had an inheritance from a teacher, then naturally that was good. Whether cultivation arts, magic treasures, or cultivation-assisting resources, all would have been planned out long ago.

For instance, the dao-protector her good friend Yu Xu's family had found for her had once been the final cult master of a Sage-level cult in the Southern Frontier. Even if he had declined to the point where he had no choice but to throw himself into the Tianyuan Dao Sect, his arts and attainments were still incomparably solid.

But a rogue cultivator?

To be honest, Fu Qinglan felt no confidence at all.

For such people, successfully forming a Nascent Soul was already a heaven-flooding fortune, so how could one speak of other progress?

Probably most of a hundred years would be spent stabilizing the realm, while the rest would be spent searching for cultivation spirit lands and resources. They might even be lacking in cultivation arts.

In that case, where would they have time to nurture powerful magic treasures and improve battle ability?

Unlike Dao Sect disciples like them, inside Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, the main fighting would be done by these dao-protectors.

Most other dao-protectors were second-level or even third-level Sages.

The rogue cultivator her grandfather had found, according to the letter, was only at the first level of the Sage stage. Could he truly do it?

As for Qi Xiongcheng, the one Yu Xu had mentioned, although he was born as a servant of the Shi Great House, he had received a Great House's cultivation. After forming his Nascent Soul, he had placed all his energy into improving his battle methods. Even if he was likewise a first-level Sage, he was clearly more reliable.

Only, the Shi Great House...

Fu Qinglan secretly shook her head. Gifts from the Shi Great House certainly had a price. She had to revive the Fu family and could not afford such a price.

Just as thoughts rose and fell in her heart, the silent man following behind them suddenly spoke.

"Senior sisters, this junior brother will go on ahead."

Fu Qinglan made a sound of acknowledgment.

Yu Xu, however, was somewhat surprised. Looking at the direction the young man was going, she asked in puzzlement, "Why is he going back to Heaven-Earth Peak again? I remember Little Lin's dao-protector is a collateral clansman specially cultivated by his family and has no connection at all to Star Gate guest elders."

Fu Qinglan was also somewhat puzzled. She subconsciously raised her head and looked toward that majestic lone peak connecting heaven and earth.

"Perhaps he admires the Alchemy Saint."

Yu Xu suddenly understood. "That is true. Little Lin has liked alchemy since childhood. Now that the Alchemy Saint is about to leave, he naturally wants to linger more in the place where the Alchemy Saint cultivated in seclusion. Perhaps there will be some opportunity there."

Afterward, Yu Xu smiled brightly.

"I hear the Seven Disciples of Muyuan [TERM?] have come to the Hall of Eminents [TERM?] and want to challenge Eminent Daoyin [TERM?]. It is today, I think. Qinglan, let us go take a look!"

The moment she heard this news, Fu Qinglan's eyes brightened.

"They are playing today?"

"Yes. Senior Brother Daoyin is very likely going to face all seven alone. It will absolutely be a rare grand event in the go world in recent years. You have always liked playing go, so how could you miss such a gathering?"

"Let us go!"

...

Inside the cave abode on Wangwu Peak in the Wangwu Mountain Range.

Luo Chen's eyes were tightly shut, his face tinged purple. As his lips opened and closed, hot streams of air surged out, steaming the quiet and secluded sealed chamber until it became extremely sweltering.

And as he continuously exhaled scorching air, the purple on his face gradually faded.

Only after a long while did Luo Chen slowly open his eyes.

Sensing the even more condensed Nascent Soul body and the restless, surging magic power, the corners of his mouth could not help lifting slightly.

As expected of a high-grade Taihe Pill!

Just one had caused his magic power to increase greatly.

At this moment, on the system panel, the progress bar in the realm column had already reached [Sage Level One 25/100], growing closer and closer to the second level of the Sage stage.

According to Luo Chen's estimate, the medicinal power of the high-grade Taihe Pill he had refined recently should be around three times that of a low-grade Hongyuan Pill, and compared to a middle-grade Hongyuan Pill, it was also slightly superior.

However, compared to the Hongyuan Pill's powerful applicability with no aftereffects, the Taihe Pill had too many drawbacks.

Pill poison corroded the Purple Mansion barrier, and the pill power would make the Nascent Soul body mixed and impure, requiring him to spend great effort refining it bit by bit.

Especially since this pill's high-grade medicinal power was only slightly superior to a middle-grade Hongyuan Pill, he imagined it would be much inferior compared to a high-grade Hongyuan Pill.

"Even so, this pill still counts as a high-quality pill."

Pills that could still affect Sages were already few and far between in the world, and for the effect to be so obvious showed that the Taihe Pill, which the Tianyuan Dao Sect had mainly used in earlier years, truly deserved its reputation.

In this way, Luo Chen felt even more admiration for Alchemy Saint Chu Yan, who could improve the flaws of this pill formula.

With his present alchemy, so long as he had the corresponding pill formula, he could easily refine it.

But if one spoke of improving this pill formula, he feared it would still be somewhat strenuous.

Shaking his head, Luo Chen no longer thought about these matters.

The Taihe Pill was good, but it was only a supplement. This pill could not be consumed frequently and was far inferior to the Hongyuan Pill.

After exhausting this bottle in his hand, he had no intention of seeking more Taihe Pills.

At present, he placed his attention on that bottle of Flameflow Marrow.

Looking at the deep-red bottle in his hand, Luo Chen probed into it with divine sense.

A red, viscous liquid like bone marrow gurgled and flowed inside.

As bubbles popped, faint flame waves seemed to be born and extinguished.

"Withered-Source Fire Marrow is born in the withered source land after a source-less fire departs, and possesses the wondrous property of nourishing new fire. For cultivators, if a source true flame absorbs it, there is a great chance it can enhance the source true flame's power, or even advance it!"

In his mind, all kinds of information recorded in the alchemy book surfaced.

Within his Purple Mansion, the Withering-Bloom True Flame grew somewhat restless. The fire tree's branches stretched, showing a rather obvious desire.

Judging from the Withering-Bloom True Flame's movement, the alchemy book record had not deceived him, and Fu Chaosheng had not used a fake to brush Luo Chen off.

This bottle of Flameflow Marrow was indeed the Withered-Source Fire Marrow recorded in rumor.

Only, Luo Chen looked at the bottle with regret in his eyes.

"That volume of alchemy records describes several wondrous methods for using Withered-Source Fire Marrow. One of them refines it with a special spirit liquid from the Devil Realm into a pill, with a chance of producing Yin-Yang Extremity Flame [TERM?], where water and fire attributes coexist."

"Unfortunately, I do not have that spirit liquid."

"I can only let you benefit."

As he murmured to himself, Luo Chen opened his mouth and spat out the complete Withering-Bloom True Flame.

In midair, it transformed into a three-foot azure-gold sapling.

Its branches were blue-green, its roots tinged gold.

At the same time, Luo Chen opened the bottle stopper.

The fire tree did not hesitate. Its branches reached into the mouth of the bottle, and wisp after wisp of deep-red marrow liquid was drawn out, flowing along the branches and merging into the fire tree's trunk.

Watching this scene, Luo Chen showed anticipation.

His source true flame was not especially extraordinary in itself, merely a flame used for artifact forging by a major Adept sect.

At the time, the Flame Alliance had wanted to raise this flame's grade, so they constructed the terrain of Incense Burning Valley and cultivated it bit by bit to the fourth tier.

Logically speaking, this was the limit of the Withering-Bloom Fire.

But after Luo Chen later refined it, it devoured large amounts of demon-beast life force, laying an excellent foundation for it, and only then did it have greater potential.

Inside Blazing Refinement Prison, it devoured seven kinds of low-level source-less fire in succession, and even fully devoured the fire spirit of a fourth-tier Burning Heaven Fire.

At this point, the Withering-Bloom True Flame possessed the embryonic form of a physical body.

But if one spoke of developing intelligence, it was still far from enough.

Only after it devoured that trace of Nirvana Sacred Flame source inside the Pill Hall did its spirituality greatly open.

Now, as Luo Chen advanced to the Sage stage and released his suppression on it, its spirituality changed almost every day.

Whether devouring netherfire or suppressing Shark Emperor Liujun's inherited blue flame, it did so with ease.

What kind of change would it undergo after absorbing this bottle of Withered-Source Fire Marrow?

Luo Chen was very expectant!

...

One month later.

Fu Chaosheng looked at Luo Chen as he left seclusion, and his eyes brightened.

"Fellow Daoist's bearing has grown even more magnificent. It seems that in only one short month, your Dao attainment has improved greatly!"

Luo Chen smiled faintly. "It is thanks to your blessing, Elder. That Taihe Pill is indeed a rare cultivation sacred pill in the world and benefited me greatly."

Fu Chaosheng chuckled and stroked his beard. "That is only natural. Even pills my sect has eliminated and no longer uses are first-rate spirit pills and wondrous medicines when placed in the other four continents. If Fellow Daoist still wants them later, after Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, I dare not speak of high-grade ones, but ordinary Taihe Pills are something this old man can find quite a few of if he spends some effort."

Luo Chen blinked and smiled without speaking.

So the hook was here!

First, Fu Chaosheng had given him excellent high-grade Taihe Pills for free, both showing boldness and making him feel grateful, so he would exert his full strength in the later grand competition.

If the matter succeeded, then according to the implication of his words, he could think of a way to supply some ordinary Taihe Pills to Luo Chen.

But at that time, they definitely would no longer be free.

He was a merchant after all, thinking everything through completely no matter what he did.

Unfortunately, Luo Chen's ambition was not here.

He had the Hongyuan Pill in hand. So long as its proficiency continued to rise and he refined high-grade ones later, the effect would be better than this discarded Taihe Pill.

Seeing Luo Chen not bite the hook, Fu Chaosheng found it somewhat strange, but he did not hurriedly press the matter and instead asked about something else.

"I wonder, has Fellow Daoist tried that Flameflow Marrow?"

When this matter was mentioned, Luo Chen's expression became somewhat strange.

"I tried it."

"How was the effect?"

"Hard to say. My source true flame nearly went out."

"Ah!"

Fu Chaosheng was incomparably shocked.

Luo Chen waved his hand. "No matter. I restored it afterward."

Only then did Fu Chaosheng release a long breath. It was good that no major accident had happened.

But he also found it very strange. Flameflow Marrow counted as a rare and precious resource difficult to find in the world. Even if one set aside its positive effects, it should not have extinguished a Sage cultivator's source true flame.

Could the bottle he had found have expired?

"Forget it. This old man was somewhat inconsiderate. I hope Fellow Daoist will not mind."

Luo Chen smiled, looking as though he did not take it to heart.

And inside his Purple Mansion, the Withering-Bloom fire tree, which had once grown vigorously, was now sickly and listless. Its branches no longer stretched out, but curled inward, presenting a dispirited appearance.

If it had been before, Luo Chen would have panicked long ago.

But the Withering-Bloom True Flame had already gained intelligence. When its branches curled, it had notified him of a piece of information.

This was a good change!

A sign before advancement!

Even if, after the advancement completed, it could not rise to the fifth tier, it would certainly be much stronger than before.

After briefly chatting about recent circumstances, Fu Chaosheng explained why he had come.

"We should set out for Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain."

Luo Chen already knew Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain was where the Tianyuan Dao Sect's core mountain gate was located. He was only somewhat surprised.

"Did we not agree that I would cultivate here for a full year before going?"

Wangwu Mountain's main peak had been carefully cultivated by the Fu family, with incomparably abundant spiritual qi, and was truly the first cultivation spirit land Luo Chen had encountered after forming his Nascent Soul.

It far surpassed Longyuan Island's earth abyss and Divine Origin City's Little Qiong Mountain.

He was also very satisfied with this place.

But now, they were going to the Tianyuan Dao Sect early. Could it be that Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven was opening and the five-hundred-year grand competition was being held ahead of schedule?

Unexpectedly, Fu Chaosheng gave another reason.

"That person's day of ascension is near!"

In an instant, Luo Chen's pupils contracted.

...

One month later.

In a majestic city, two people walked out from a cold and quiet great hall.

As he walked, Fu Chaosheng bowed from afar toward the back with a somewhat humble smile on his face.

Only after they were far from that great hall did he release a breath.

"Forgive me for making a fool of myself."

Luo Chen did not understand. Along the way, they had traveled day and night, sometimes taking flying boats, sometimes relying on teleportation formations between great cities, rushing quickly toward Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain.

But not once had Fu Chaosheng been so respectful and humble.

"This is Zhigu City [TERM?], territory controlled by the Gu Great House [TERM?], one of the Twelve Great Houses."

"As one of the Twelve Great Houses, the Gu family's strength is far beyond what people like us can imagine. Not only do they have five great Sages presiding within the clan, they also have the Zha clan [TERM?] and Qu clan [TERM?] among the Thirty-Six Aristocratic Clans at their command. Especially Gu Daoyin [TERM?] of the Gu family. He is not yet five hundred, but already mid-stage Sage, and is praised as one of the three people of this generation with the greatest hope of advancing to Ascendant."

"Facing such a great clan, Fu has no choice but to treat them respectfully."

Luo Chen suddenly understood. So it was one of the Twelve Great Houses!

He was now growing more and more familiar with Central Continent's power structure.

Aside from the Tianyuan Dao Sect, there were no other sects, only families everywhere.

Aside from family cultivators, there were no rogue cultivators. Even though every place was very suitable for cultivation, there were simply no rogue cultivators!

If one wanted to cultivate on those spirit lands as a lone person, one had to throw oneself into the great families.

Some became slaves, some servants, and marriages into or out of the family were even more common.

The great families were not rigid. They gave these people another path: if their Dao attainments became accomplished, they could apply to establish their own doors. After passing the Tianyuan Dao Sect's examination, they could enter the ranks of Aristocratic Clans or lesser houses.

As Fu Chaosheng had said, the Zha clan and Qu clan were newly risen families among the Thirty-Six Aristocratic Clans.

Their patriarchs had come from the Gu Great House.

As for that heaven's favored son Gu Daoyin, Luo Chen was even more interested.

That was because the reason the other party was called an Ascendant seed was not because of exceptional aptitude, but because he excelled at playing go.

He himself was also a famous figure in Central Continent's go circles, equally renowned with Tianyuan Linxian [TERM?] and Chess Saint Ke Lianshan [TERM?].

Yes, in Central Continent, all the hundred arts of cultivation were inferior, and only the Dao of go was supreme.

Unless one could cultivate a certain skill to the level of the Alchemy Saint, one would earn others' admiration and be treated with courtesy.

But in this world, how many people could reach the Alchemy Saint's level?

There was only this one person, and perhaps before long, there would be no such person left in the world.

Luo Chen's eyes were deep. He cast aside the bustling Zhigu City and gazed far into the distance at that heaven-reaching peak, still visible from a thousand li away.

Vaguely, rays of hazy escape light, like mayflies, passed through the azure clouds and disappeared into it.

"Is the Alchemy Saint going to ascend to the upper realm there?"

Fu Chaosheng showed envy on his face and likewise looked toward that place.

"Yes. Right there, one year from now!"

*(End of Chapter)*
