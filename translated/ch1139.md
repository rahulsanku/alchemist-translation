# Chapter 1139: Xiao Family Guest Elder

Within the Luo residence, dozens of maidservants and servants had gathered in the courtyard.

Steward Yan was earnestly explaining the general situation to Luo Chen.

Finally, he pointed to a kindly, honest-looking old man and said, "Senior, Uncle Zhong has served in the City Lord's Mansion for many years. He is reliable and efficient in handling matters. From now on, he will manage the residence for you. Are you satisfied with this arrangement?"

Luo Chen swept his gaze across the crowd and waved his hand. "There's no need for so many people. Leave ten to serve, that's enough."

Afterward, he looked toward Jing Lan, who stood quietly to the side.

"From now on, you follow Uncle Zhong and learn how to manage the residence. Consider it something to keep you occupied."

Jing Lan was somewhat surprised but did not refuse Luo Chen's goodwill. Having just arrived at Relic Immortal City, she was still quite lost, and having something to do was always a good thing.

Having given these instructions, Luo Chen did not bother with anything else. He went to the rear courtyard on his own to rest.

Steward Yan stayed behind to give a few more instructions, but he did not linger long before being hurriedly called away.

...

Inside the City Lord's Mansion.

Steward Yan stood respectfully before the young man, recounting the events of the previous journey in detail, omitting nothing.

This was not something that could be clearly conveyed by simply sending someone with a sealed letter.

Under his vivid description, Xiao Feng could almost picture the battlefield situation at that time.

After he finished speaking, Xiao Feng nodded with a smile.

"You've worked hard on this trip. Not only did you keep the merchant caravan safe, but you also recruited such a formidable helper for me. Old Yan, you're not getting any younger. From now on, stay in the city! As for the matter at Green Mountain Town... I recall you have a capable nephew. Let him handle it!"

Hearing these words, Steward Yan was overjoyed.

Being able to stay in the city was naturally the best outcome for him. Moreover, the post he had held in a remote area was not lost; it had simply been passed on to his junior.

His decision to recruit Luo Chen this time had truly been a clever move.

After expressing his gratitude profusely, Steward Yan slowly withdrew.

Xiao Feng, however, put away his smile and fell into deep thought.

"Uncle Iron, what do you think?"

Uncle Iron did not hesitate to affirm Luo Chen's strength. "The earlier letter only mentioned that he repelled them, but now it seems he actually slew them in battle—and killed every single one. This is not something an ordinary Dan Jin expert could accomplish. Even a Furnace Realm expert who has cultivated Gang Qi would not have found it so easy. That Luo Chen is truly unfathomable!"

Xiao Feng also sighed with emotion. "Indeed, one cannot underestimate these cultivators, even if they have fallen on hard times!"

Uncle Iron asked, "Then will you agree to his conditions?"

Xiao Feng took a deep breath and said firmly, "Naturally. Some books are merely lifeless objects. At this critical juncture, if I can use such lifeless things to win over a top-tier expert, it is the most cost-effective deal imaginable. Tomorrow morning, I will visit him personally to show my respect!"

...

Early the next morning, Xiao Feng personally went to the Luo residence to pay a visit.

When he returned, he brought Luo Chen with him.

Outside the City Lord's Mansion's Scripture Pavilion, Xiao Feng clasped his hands in a salute. "I hope Senior will find what he seeks."

Luo Chen grunted in acknowledgment and was about to push open the door and enter. Before stepping inside, he paused slightly, then said without turning his head, "If there is anything you cannot handle, feel free to seek me out."

Xiao Feng's spirits lifted. "Then I shall trouble Senior from now on."

The corner of Luo Chen's mouth lifted slightly, and he had already entered the Scripture Pavilion.

Having agreed to be a guest elder, and since Xiao Feng had shown him sufficient respect, he did not mind occasionally lending a hand.

These trivial matters quickly faded from Luo Chen's mind once he entered the Scripture Pavilion. All his attention fell upon the books housed within.

In the shadows, several gazes fell upon him—martial arts experts responsible for guarding the Scripture Pavilion.

Luo Chen acted as if he were unaware and began browsing the first floor directly.

This surprised the hidden guards.

Everyone knew that the City Lord of Relic Immortal City was a martial arts fanatic. He had collected a vast number of martial arts texts and had even expanded the Scripture Pavilion to five floors!

The first floor was the largest, with the most books, but they mostly covered geography, local customs, and martial arts anecdotes.

The second and third floors housed low-level martial arts techniques.

To enter the fourth and fifth floors and glimpse the high-level martial arts texts was far more complicated and required the City Lord's approval.

This new guest elder was favored by the Young City Lord and had been granted the right to freely browse the collection. Yet, unexpectedly, he did not head straight for the fourth or fifth floors but stopped on the seemingly useless first floor.

And he stayed there for an entire morning!

By noon, he finally stopped, and a table was piled high with a towering stack of books.

The clerk responsible for recording the borrowing and returning of books felt somewhat numb when he saw the half-man-high pile of ordinary books.

"Sir, are you sure you want to borrow all of these?"

Luo Chen asked back, "Is there a limit?"

The clerk quickly shook his head. "Ordinary people naturally have a limit on the number of books they can borrow, but you are different, sir. The Young City Lord specifically instructed that you may borrow as many books as you wish, with no time limit."

Having said that, he swiftly began recording the names of each book.

On the white paper, as his brush strokes spread, the titles were recorded one by one.

*Heavenly Wind Scroll*, *Ghost Rain Scroll*, *Mist Scroll*, *Travel Notes of the Seventy-Two Cities*, *Relic Immortal Essays*, *Worry-Free Strange Tales*, *The Mystery of the Mummy*...

Luo Chen watched the clerk write quickly and felt a sense of relief.

With these texts, he could quickly understand the general situation of this world and act with purpose in the future.

This was far more useful than obtaining some profound martial arts manual.

After all, powerful martial arts techniques required time and effort to practice. But understanding the operating model of a world and the levels of its experts, combined with his own strength, would make his actions far more convenient.

"So many books. This will keep me busy for a while."

Luo Chen thought to himself, but he felt no pride—only a hint of bitterness.

If he could use his divine sense, his efficiency in browsing these texts would be far higher. He could even have filtered out all the books he wanted to borrow the moment he entered the Scripture Pavilion, without wasting an entire morning.

However, restoring his divine sense was not something to be rushed. At the very least, he could not act rashly until he reached the Leakless Golden Body realm.

"Sir, it's done!"

The clerk's respectful voice came from beside him, and Luo Chen waved his hand.

"Arrange for someone to deliver them to my residence!"

Having given this instruction, Luo Chen turned and left.

As he walked out of the City Lord's Mansion, Luo Chen's brow furrowed slightly, but his steps did not falter.

That feeling of being secretly watched was far too obvious.

"Is it that man called Uncle Iron?"

Luo Chen thought to himself. That person was the only one present who could draw his attention.

Although the other party had hidden himself extremely well, under Luo Chen's sharp eyes, he could tell at a glance that this man's cultivation far surpassed the many experts in the City Lord's Mansion. He was undoubtedly a powerful existence at the fourth realm of body refinement.

Luo Chen did not mind the surveillance. He harbored no ill intentions, so he acted openly and aboveboard. If the other party wanted to cause trouble, he would not mind crossing blows with him.

Although his apparent cultivation was only at the Blood River realm, this fleshly body of his—tempered through countless refinements and even having endured drifting through the cosmos—was a genuine Archaic Fifth Grade!

Even if a Leakless realm expert stood before him, Luo Chen would leave them with a memory they would never forget.

Luo Chen departed calmly.

From a high tower in the City Lord's Mansion, Uncle Iron thoughtfully withdrew his gaze.

And at a street corner Luo Chen had not noticed, a gaze that was practically spitting fire also withdrew.

Xiao Zirui slammed his fist heavily against the wall.

"Why! This man has only just arrived, yet he can freely borrow all the books in the collection. I have served Xiao Feng for a long time, yet I still haven't obtained the second half of the *Unfixed Art*. Is this what it means to give to outsiders but not to one's own servants?"

(Chapter End)
