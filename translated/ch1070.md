# Chapter 1070: Ancestral Court of the Dao Sect, Meeting Canghai Again

The Shouyang Dao Discussion Conference had ended!

When they arrived, dozens of flying lights had converged like flowing stars.

When they departed, they scattered in twos and threes, drifting away with the wind.

Luo Chen stood with his hands clasped behind his back at the summit of Shouyang Mountain, gazing into the distance at those fading escape lights. His expression no longer held the confident, knowing air of before; instead, it was somewhat gloomy.

Behind him, Canglong and Gu Caiyi gazed with admiration at his tall, broad back.

The list of attendees from earlier was now held in Canglong's hand.

"Thirteen Upper Sects, plus the rogue cultivator Fellow Daoist Bo Han, twenty-five people in total. Everyone who left their name agreed to help you, Senior Brother, this time."

"Additionally, there are four Nascent Soul Sages from Dongyang Sect who remain at their mountain gate. The Grand Elder of Dongyang has also promised to bring them along when the time comes."

"Senior Brother, we will definitely succeed this time, won't we?"

Luo Chen did not reply. He merely paced slowly along the edge of the cliff.

Gu Caiyi was the most adept at reading people. At this moment, she caught a glimpse of Luo Chen's gloomy profile and felt puzzled.

"Husband, why are you unhappy now that you've brought this together? Is there some unforeseen variable?"

A variable?

Luo Chen paused mid-step and let out a soft sigh.

Of course, there were variables!

What had truly made everyone agree to this plan were the two Ascendant-level existences!

Whether it was Deng Taiyue's daring act of killing two Dao Sect Sages as a "blood oath," the powerful alliance between the human and demon races of the Eastern Wilderness, or the temptation of the Enlightening Pill's opportunity for Ascension—these were all merely inducements.

The participation of Li Canghai from the Northern Sea and Azure Frost from Green Parasol Mountain—these two individuals—were the true anchors of reassurance in everyone's hearts.

A human Ascendant powerhouse and a fifth-rank ancient demon from the demon race. In terms of sheer numbers, they were no less than the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, which currently only had a single Ascendant powerhouse.

It was precisely this intuitive comparison that had instantly overwhelmed everyone's wavering thoughts.

However!

"Li Canghai and I are merely nodding acquaintances. This so-called tacit understanding still depends on his willingness."

"As for Azure Frost... whether she will actually make a move, she hasn't given me a definitive answer either."

"In the end, I still have to rely on myself!"

Luo Chen thought this in his heart.

But verbally, he did not voice these things.

Instead, he turned around, his gaze soft as he looked at the two women.

"Canglong, how is your refinement of that White Tiger Puppet going?"

Canglong smiled slightly. "I've mastered it completely. Although this puppet was crafted with extreme ingenuity, its control methods are more in line with the Falling Cloud Sect's style. In my early years, I had a good relationship with Han Zhan and received some guidance from him. A hundred years has been enough time for me to refine it to perfection."

Luo Chen hummed in acknowledgment, not surprised. "With your current mid-Nascent Soul cultivation, you should be able to use this puppet for prolonged combat. Additionally, I'll give you fifty fourth-rank talismans. When the critical moment comes, just throw them out."

Luo Chen actually had a very substantial stash on hand—a massive quantity of fourth-rank talismans!

They were byproducts from back when he was comprehending the true meaning of fire-attribute laws by making talismans in the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land. Most were the fourth-rank spells Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain and Flame Dragon Descends, which had been sitting in his hands without any use.

These talismans were not inferior goods; they were the finest of the finest!

One must know that Luo Chen had cultivated both of those spells to the Great Perfection level. Their power was unmatched among those below the Ascendant stage. An ordinary Nascent Soul Sage couldn't even withstand a single one.

Icecastle specialized in talisman crafting and usage—Luo Chen had known this since the early days from the Heartless Fairy.

He believed that with this batch of talismans, plus a late-Nascent Soul White Tiger Puppet, Canglong's combat power would be in no way inferior to any Grand Cultivator.

Gu Caiyi, standing nearby, was stunned by what she heard. "You're at mid-Nascent Soul?"

Canglong nodded slightly and smiled at Luo Chen. "I have to thank you, Senior Brother, for your gift. That thing you left inside my body back then—I've refined most of it over the past hundred years. Reaching mid-Nascent Soul was only natural."

Gu Caiyi's eyes widened. What did she mean by "the thing you left inside my body"?

Luo Chen coughed awkwardly and explained in detail. "It was a portion of the origin qi of heaven and earth, plus some converted water-attribute origin dharma power. Back then, I was too eager for results. While comprehending the Water Origin, I triggered a clash between the Water and Fire Origins, which even caused a celestial anomaly. You should know about that incident, Caiyi. The situation was extremely critical at the time. Although I managed to avoid disaster using a secret technique, my body was flooded with too much origin qi. It was Canglong who sacrificed her innocence, using her special constitution and a dual cultivation method, to save my life. After the matter was resolved, I left a portion of the excess origin qi in Canglong's heart aperture. That's roughly what happened."

After this explanation, Gu Caiyi finally understood why Canglong had advanced so rapidly over the past hundred years, leaping from just forming her Nascent Soul to the mid-Nascent Soul stage.

At the same time, she was very grateful to Canglong for sacrificing her innocence... to save her husband.

Wait. Sacrificing innocence? Dual cultivation method? What was this again?

Gu Caiyi suddenly came to a realization. No wonder, after reuniting with her husband, Canglong had always stayed by his side, accompanying her without any attempt to avoid suspicion.

Even during the Shouyang Dao Discussion Conference, she had sat behind her husband.

She, wanting to display magnanimity, hadn't gotten too angry, only thinking that her husband was trying to "put up a front."

Now that she thought about it, during that hundred years when she was quietly breaking through to the Nascent Soul stage, she had unknowingly overlooked many things!

Faced with Gu Caiyi's vaguely resentful gaze, Luo Chen pretended not to see it. Instead, he opened his mouth and spat out a ball of five-colored light.

He rubbed his hands together, and the five-colored light dispersed into five individual sword pellets.

Canglong took one look and exclaimed in surprise.

"Are these the batch of sword pellets you specially refined using heavenly thunder when I was undergoing my tribulation back then?"

Luo Chen nodded gently, then looked solemnly at Gu Caiyi.

"These are the Five Elements Sword Pellets. Each one is a true artifact in its own right, painstakingly refined by your husband."

"Individually, they are incomparably sharp. Combined, their power is vast and grand. They can even form the Grand Five Elements Sword Formation, capable of sweeping through the Nascent Soul stage without issue!"

"Caiyi, you have just formed your Nascent Soul. Your foundation of dharma treasures is insufficient, and none have been tempered by thunder tribulation into true artifacts, so they won't be of much help in your current battles. I will temporarily give you these five sword pellets, along with a formation diagram and a large amount of dharma power. At that time, you can forcibly activate the Grand Five Elements Sword Formation to deal with any enemy!"

Gu Caiyi's face showed hesitation, and she did not reach out to take them.

She looked at Luo Chen with confusion. "Such a powerful treasure would be even more formidable in your hands, Husband. Why give it to me?"

Luo Chen smiled gently. "Remember that old man who went to the Luo Tian Sect?"

Gu Caiyi subconsciously said, "Zhou Yunshen?"

Luo Chen chuckled, as if recalling past events. "Yes. He is the ancestor of the Zhou clan of the Central Continent. I knew him even before I went to the Central Continent, back when their clan was still in the Northern Sea. I secretly formed a cooperative relationship with him. Once we attack the Central Continent, the Zhou clan will inevitably be caught in the aftermath. Therefore, I need you to do something for me. While we are attacking the Dongyuan Continent, you will go to the Tiannan Continent, take the important members of the Zhou clan, and bring them back to the Eastern Wilderness."

Gu Caiyi subconsciously nodded and reached out to take the five-colored sword pellets.

But the moment her hand grasped the sharp pellet, she suddenly lifted her head.

"Husband, are you deliberately sending me away, not wanting me to participate in the main battlefield?"

Luo Chen shook his head with a smile. "How could you think that? This matter is my promise. If the Zhou clan suffers disaster, wouldn't I be breaking my word? Besides, Zhou Yunshen traveled thousands of li to come to the Luo Tian Sect before his great limit arrived, which shows the importance of this matter. There aren't many people I can fully trust. Caiyi, you won't disappoint me, will you?"

Gu Caiyi accepted the sword pellets with a mixture of doubt and belief. "Caiyi will complete this task successfully. I will not let you down, Husband."

"Mm. Then hurry up and do a simple refinement of these five sword pellets and this formation diagram. There are still three years left; that should be barely enough."

Gu Caiyi took the formation diagram from Luo Chen's hand and then hurriedly left.

Canglong stood to the side, a hint of envy in her eyes.

She could feel the special affection Luo Chen had for Gu Caiyi, a gentle love different from what was between him and herself.

This was something she had never seen between Luo Chen and Sima Huiniang. Towards Sima Huiniang, Luo Chen had shown more a sense of responsibility.

As for that formation diagram, Canglong felt a sense of familiarity again.

The Grand Five Elements Sword Formation?

Plus the strong Falling Cloud Sect puppet control style on the White Tiger Puppet from before.

Canglong brought up Han Zhan at an inopportune moment.

"Senior Brother, you and Han Zhan..."

Luo Chen said frankly, "There are some entanglements of grievances. It's a long story."

Canglong said thoughtfully, "In that case, there's no need to tell it."

"It's fine. It doesn't hurt to tell you." Luo Chen's gaze was distant as he spoke of events from years past.

From being imprisoned together on Green Parasol Mountain, to the Northern Sea, and finally to their life-and-death struggle on Longyuan Island in the Demon Sea.

Bit by bit, drop by drop, by the time his words were finished, the moon was high in the sky.

The more Canglong listened, the more silent she became. That old friend of hers had actually been so deeply calculating and harbored such ill intentions.

Finally.

Canglong sighed softly. "You can know a person's face but not their heart. I never imagined it would come to this."

Luo Chen waved his hand. "The tallest tree in the forest is the first to be felled by the wind. Some people use my alchemy, some covet my physical body, and others fancy my formidable combat power. I'm used to it, truly used to it."

He took a step forward, standing on the edge of the cliff, facing the mountain wind, his robes rustling.

"But after this battle, no one will ever disrespect me again!"

The woman gazed at that tall, straight figure that seemed capable of supporting heaven and earth, her eyes filled with reverence.

...

After everyone in the Shouyang Hall had agreed to help Luo Chen, the Dao Discussion Conference came to an end.

To prevent news from leaking, everyone made a Great Dao oath together.

Only after the matter was concluded could they reveal the inside story to outsiders.

And before launching the attack on the Central Continent, Luo Chen left everyone two years.

These two years were for them to return to their respective sects and arrange all the major and minor affairs, to prevent chaos and a leaderless situation within their sects if someone fell on the battlefield.

Additionally, Luo Chen made a not-insignificant promise.

Aside from the Enlightening Pill, if anyone fell in this battle and their sect's strength was greatly diminished, he, Luo Chen, and the Luo Tian Sect would protect that sect.

The scope of this promise covered the thirteen Nascent Soul Upper Sects, with a duration of one thousand years.

As long as Luo Chen still lived, as long as the Luo Tian Sect still existed!

Only then did everyone feel at ease and leave Shouyang Mountain with high ambitions.

With the return of these people, some relatively important information about the Dao Discussion Conference also spread throughout the Eastern Wilderness.

Under the vigorous promotion of the Alchemy Sect, the human and demon races had finally reached a ceasefire treaty!

The great war between humans and demons, which had lasted for two hundred years and then been suspended for another hundred, was slowly drawing to a close. The Eastern Wilderness cultivation world would now usher in a peaceful time lasting one thousand years.

A thousand years was a long period for any existence.

Even for a Nascent Soul Sage, their lifespan was only about a thousand years at most.

Therefore, no one thought about what would happen after a thousand years. People only knew that they no longer had to worry about sudden attacks from large groups of demon beasts and could live in peace.

The reputation of the Alchemy Sect rose once again because of this matter.

It was even elevated to an unimaginable level!

Whether it was the so-called Nascent Soul Perfection Grand Elder of Dongyang, the formation master Tian Duzi whose skills were superb, or the invincible Nine Spirit Primordial Lord, none could be compared to Luo Chen.

The entire Eastern Wilderness was singing the praises of Luo Chen's prestige and his various deeds, great and small.

And while the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world was celebrating the ceasefire, Luo Chen's figure had already crossed the seas and arrived once again in the "Central Continent."

Only this time, it was no longer the Tiannan Continent, but the ancestral court of the Dao Sect—the Dongyuan Continent!

This region was not unfamiliar to him.

In the past, when he participated in the Grotto-Heaven Grand Competition, it was in the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven beneath Heaven-Earth Peak. And Heaven-Earth Peak was located along the Passing River, upstream from the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range.

Later, when he left the Central Continent to return to the Eastern Wilderness, he had also traveled along the Dongyuan Continent route.

Now, he was merely retracing his steps.

Merging into the Dongyuan Continent was not difficult for Luo Chen. He had always been skilled in concealment and qi-narrowing techniques.

Coupled with the fact that the Dongyuan Continent was currently hosting a Sexagenary Grand Competition, which had been delayed for a hundred and sixty years, it was even more suitable for him to infiltrate.

Luo Chen found a down-and-out Foundation Establishment cultivator from a minor clan and replaced him.

Through his meticulous mastery of body refinement, he altered his appearance to match. He obtained the other's cultivation technique, practiced it briefly, and reached the perfect master level, his dharma power aura completely identical.

In this way, two years later, Luo Chen finally entered the ancestral court of the Dao Sect—the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range!

The Sexagenary Grand Competition was in full swing!

This competition was held one hundred and sixty years after the previous five-hundred-year Grotto-Heaven Grand Competition.

It should have been held a hundred years later, but due to the Central Continent Calamity, it had been delayed. Thus, it was only being held again after sixty years.

Under these circumstances, the Dao Sect placed great importance on this competition.

It was both a way to select good seedlings and to enhance the sect's cohesion.

Through martial competition, they aimed to demonstrate that the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect was still strong, still the most powerful human sacred land in the world!

Therefore, from beginning to end, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect strove for perfection in this competition.

It was divided into three major stages: Qi Refinement, Foundation Establishment, and Core Formation.

Then, within these three major stages, the selection and competition of outstanding disciples were held separately across the three continents of Dongyuan, Tiannan, and Libei.

Each continent selected ten people for each cultivation realm.

Ultimately, only the top ninety people could compete in the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range.

The victors would receive substantial rewards.

Luo Chen did not care about the rankings. He only cared about one person.

That white-clothed man on the arena who had forcefully defeated an opponent—Zhou Fuyou.

This was the first time Luo Chen had seen his clone.

His appearance was completely different from his own. His personality was also entirely unlike what Little Bai had reported. Even his dharma power was so deep and profound, surpassing even Luo Chen's own at the same realm.

The only somewhat similar point was probably his exceptionally outstanding spellcasting!

"Unfortunately, he's only at the ninth layer of Core Formation. He hasn't reached the Nascent Soul stage after all."

Luo Chen sighed softly in his heart.

But he knew he couldn't ask for too much.

All things considered, Zhou Fuyou wasn't even a hundred years old yet.

For an ordinary person to form a core at a hundred was already a genius among geniuses. How could they possibly form a Nascent Soul at a hundred?

He shook his head secretly, and without paying any attention to the high-ranking Heavenly Origin Dao Sect experts above, he quietly withdrew from the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range.

He walked alone to the bank of the Passing River, which flowed eastward without return, and waited in silence.

He did not know how much time had passed before a figure appeared behind him.

"You've come."

"Yes, I've come."

"I thought you, Senior, might not come."

"Why are you so impatient, Young Friend? There is still a year until the formation is activated. By then, Liancheng will be stretched thin, and all the Nascent Soul powerhouses of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect must be stationed at various formation nodes. That will be the best time for us to strike."

As he spoke, the owner of the weathered voice had already walked up beside Luo Chen, gazing together at the rolling river beneath their feet, flowing eastward.

It was the great power of the Northern Sea, Li Canghai!
