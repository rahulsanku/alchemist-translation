# Chapter 916: A Time of Turmoil, Harvesting Soul Essence

The former Jianchuan Shen family could now be renamed the Tuohe Shen family.

The river flowing before the clan was no longer the soaring, surging, sword-like Jianchuan, but had become the muddy, turbid Tuohe, filled with sand and gravel all day long.

But all of this was done out of necessity.

Under the moonlight, Luo Chen stood by the window, sighing with emotion.

"I never imagined that in just three short years, the outside world would change so drastically."

The changes in the Eastern Wilderness were something no one had anticipated.

The Nether Origin Sacred Land being submerged by the Nether Abyss, filled with countless ghostly creatures, was already shocking enough.

But the fact that the demon beasts from the Million Great Mountains had returned and launched another attack on the human cultivation world was even more unexpected.

And!

Leading this war was none other than the feathered clan's sacred land, Green Parasol Mountain!

Led by Nether Spring, the deputy hall master of Truth Crossing Hall, they had assembled an elite lineup of three great demon emperors—Bull Ghost, Bi Zheng, and Mad Frenzy—along with a vast horde of low-level demon beasts, charging into human territory.

In just a few short months, they had once again amassed their forces outside the Six Extreme Eastern Domains.

Compared to the last time, the momentum of this demon clan offensive was not as grand.

But on the human side, after over a hundred years of bitter war, they were also severely weakened.

Especially the backing behind them—the Nether Origin Sacred Land—had collapsed.

For a time, panic spread among the people.

The only unexpected thing was that the demon clan's attack was not as ferocious as the last time. They merely stationed their troops outside the Six Extreme Eastern Domains, unmoving.

It looked more like a deterrent than a full-scale offensive.

"Green Parasol Mountain taking the initiative to meddle in this matter—could it be related to Rosycloud?"

Luo Chen pondered the connections, combining this with the Nether Origin battle, and vaguely formed a few guesses.

When Rosycloud left, she was definitely not unharmed.

Three great powers, heavenly tribulation and earthly calamity, plus billions of ghostly creatures charging at her—even if she were made of iron, she would have suffered some damage.

Especially since, according to many ghostly rumors, that woman had left with a tribulation cloud hanging over her head.

"So, she had Green Parasol Mountain lead this war, most likely to intimidate human experts, lest any petty villains disturb her while she weathered the heavenly tribulation at Green Parasol Mountain?"

Luo Chen wasn't entirely sure about this guess.

But the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect descending upon the Eastern Wilderness, though unexpected, was actually quite reasonable!

They had long harbored great ambitions for the entire Mountains and Seas Realm.

Deploying troops to the Northern Sea, laying plans in the Western Desert, sending spies to the Southern Frontier—their every action revealed a wild and unrestrained posture of swallowing the entire realm.

Perhaps cultivators in the Eastern Wilderness, being far away, didn't feel it deeply.

But Luo Chen had once stayed in the Central Continent and remembered clearly the bone-deep confidence and arrogance of Heavenly Origin Dao Sect cultivators.

Especially this time, the Eastern Wilderness had given them a legitimate excuse to intervene.

A Star Gate elder at the late Sage stage from the Dao Sect delegation, a great elder of the Eastern Wilderness Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance, a clan patriarch, and dozens of low-level disciples had all fallen within the Nether Origin Sacred Land.

With such a major incident, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect would certainly demand an explanation.

Sending a large number of experts to invade the Eastern Wilderness was perfectly justified.

And the Dongyang Domain, adjacent to the coast, became their first bridgehead for landing.

The Dongyang Sect and the Jianchuan Shen family, among others, had truly suffered a great misfortune.

Even the last demon clan attack hadn't harmed them in the slightest, but now they had been driven from their homes.

"Perhaps it's not entirely an undeserved calamity?"

Luo Chen recalled what Shen Qiuhua had just told him.

A Dao Sect great power had personally visited the Dongyang Sect, forcing its great elder to submit. The signal sent by this incident was far from simple.

Everyone knew that the Dongyang Sect was a vassal of the Nether Origin Sect, its number one "lackey."

Now that the Nether Origin Sect had been destroyed, and the Dongyang Sect had submitted to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, didn't that mean the sky over the entire Eastern Wilderness had completely changed?

"The days ahead will likely be turbulent."

Just then, dark clouds obscured the bright moon.

Luo Chen felt a premonition.

"Is Rosycloud really that weak right now?"

Murmuring to himself, Luo Chen's form flickered, and a spark of fire dissipated into the night sky.

...

"Big brother, you and I can both see how weak the Eastern Wilderness is now."

"Those Sage sects ahead, after the last great war, have long been severely weakened. Relying on them to stop the demon beast tide is impossible."

"In this situation, if the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect wants to gain a foothold, they'll have to take this assault head-on."

"But are they willing to sacrifice their own disciples?"

"I doubt it. Ten to one, they'll follow the Nether Origin Sect's example and drive other cultivators ahead of them as cannon fodder. We're bound to become expendable."

"So, I've been recruiting a large number of guest elders recently, to strengthen the Shen family's power, so we won't have to send you to the front lines again like last time."

In a quiet secret chamber, Shen Qiuhua spoke passionately.

His face was flushed, his emotions agitated, his sincere concern for the family evident.

Shen Qiubai, dressed simply in plain white robes, remained calm. He said flatly, "I understand your thoughts. But are the people you've recruited reliable?"

Shen Qiuhua was stunned for a moment, then subconsciously replied, "They should be reliable!"

Shen Qiubai shook his head. "I can't see through this Fellow Daoist Gui Xuan who came today."

Shen Qiuhua looked puzzled. "What's there to see through? His conduct, his speech—he's definitely the style of a rogue cultivator!"

Shen Qiubai rose calmly and, under his younger brother's confused gaze, walked out.

"What I mean by 'can't see through' refers to his strength."

"Just as the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect wants to use us as cannon fodder because our strength can't compare to theirs, if our Shen family recruits outside guest elders as cannon fodder, we must also have absolute suppressing power."

"But that Gui Xuan, though he appears to be at the early Adept stage on the surface, the sense of threat he gives me is no less than those old Sage monsters I once saw on Shouyang Mountain at Lingtian Pass."

"If you bring in such a top-tier expert, our Shen family would be inviting a wolf into the house."

Shen Qiuhua's mouth fell open, his face full of disbelief.

"A rogue cultivator can become a Sage?"

Shen Qiubai didn't answer him. He walked to the courtyard where Luo Chen had been lodged and cautiously knocked on the door.

"Shen Qiubai, patriarch of the Shen family, requests an audience with Fellow Daoist. I hope you will grant it."

There was no response.

Shen Qiubai pushed open the door.

It was empty. Only the silvery moonlight spilled down after the dark clouds had passed.

Shen Qiuhua released his divine sense, sweeping the area. "He left without saying goodbye?"

His elder brother, Shen Qiubai, instead let out a relieved sigh.

"It's good that he's gone. Good that he's gone!"

...

Luo Chen did not go to the Dongyang Domain to take a look at the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's arrivals.

Even though the two places were quite close given his flying speed, he had no such intention.

As far as he knew, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect was continuously sending people across the vast ocean to land in the Eastern Wilderness.

This act of amassing troops had already revealed their intention to take root in the Eastern Wilderness.

That meant, in the future, whether it was with the remnants of the Nether Origin Sect, the various local powers of the Eastern Wilderness, or even the covetous demon clan, there would be all kinds of friction and conflicts.

He had absolutely no desire to get involved in matters between these major powers anymore.

Being deep within them always led to being swept along.

Whether you were at the Initiate, Adept, Sage, or Ascendant stage, there would always be personal factors or coercion from others that forced you to make decisions against your will.

In contrast, he chose to distance himself from the mundane world.

This trip had only one purpose.

To take away the Luo Tian Sect!

There were people there he couldn't let go of, matters he couldn't cut off. As his cultivation level grew, they had gradually become a weakness of his.

How could such a weakness be exposed to the outside world?

Only by hiding it away could he act freely.

From the Tuohe River to the Luo Tian Domain was a distance of a million li.

To avoid being discovered, Luo Chen chose a shortcut.

Ascending through the nine heavens, entering the blue void.

A dark, matte flying shuttle shot forward like an arrow from a string.

Under his full power, it took only a month to reach Lingtian Pass.

It could have been faster, as the modified Frost Eagle Shuttle was far faster than before.

The delay of a few days was due to the dangers within the blue void. There were creatures like the Rosy Cloud Beast from time to time, preventing him from flying at full speed.

But once he reached Lingtian Pass, the Luo Tian Domain was not far away.

Luo Chen did not go back directly. Instead, he chose to land in a secluded place and then headed towards Lingtian Pass.

Since he planned to retreat far from the world, it was necessary to do some large-scale purchasing beforehand. He also wanted to inquire about the situation at the Luo Tian Sect, lest he blindly rush into some unknown danger.

Having changed his appearance and aura, Luo Chen revisited the old place and entered Lingtian again.

Contrary to the imagined desolation and decline of a war fortress after the human-demon war, this ancient, thousand-year-old heroic city, after experiencing its brilliance, had instead taken on a different luster.

The thick, tall city walls were gone, and they hadn't been rebuilt after the war.

The mountains and rivers within were laid bare for everyone to see.

Buildings stood in abundance, with pavilions and towers hidden among them.

The wide streets were bustling with traffic, people coming and going, shoulder to shoulder.

The sounds of hawking, soliciting, shouting, and talking were endless.

Clearly, this war fortress had now smoothly transformed into a commercial center connecting the east and west.

Every day, a vast amount of resources and treasures gathered here.

Luo Chen paused for a long time, then, following his memory, found a shop established here by a Sage sect.

"This Fellow Daoist looks unfamiliar!"

An old man waved away the maid and took the initiative to greet him.

It was because Luo Chen's exposed magical power fluctuation was at the Adept stage, not something a mere Qi Refinement maid could receive.

Luo Chen's gaze swept over the goods in the shop. The restrictions and formations were like nothing to him. He casually dealt with the old man verbally.

"Lingtian Pass has cultivators like clouds and rain, Fellow Daoist. Surely you can't recognize everyone?"

"This old man naturally doesn't have that kind of eyesight, but I rarely fail to recognize Adept cultivators."

"Heh, I've only recently advanced to Adept. It's normal for you to find me unfamiliar, Fellow Daoist."

"I see. May I ask what this Fellow Daoist has come to buy? Resources to consolidate the Adept stage? Our establishment doesn't lack those."

Unexpectedly, Luo Chen suddenly transmitted his divine sense.

"Do you have any soul essence?"

The old man's face stiffened, and then he said with a complex expression, "That kind of thing is against the natural order. We don't have any here."

"None at all?" Luo Chen looked at him with a half-smile, then slowly shifted his gaze upstairs.

This action made the old man's expression change drastically. In a secret chamber upstairs, there was indeed a large amount of collected soul essence stored.

"You!"

Luo Chen waved his hand. "Don't get excited. I'm utterly mediocre in everything else, except my divine sense is somewhat powerful. I heard that soul essence has great benefits for enhancing the foundation of the soul, so I wanted to buy a little, to see if I could make further progress."

"Is that so?" The old man was half-convinced, his gaze constantly scrutinizing Luo Chen.

But due to Luo Chen's concealment technique, he couldn't see his true cultivation level. He only felt that his magical power fluctuation was indeed ordinary, just like a very common early Adept cultivator.

He hesitated for a moment and said, "This thing called soul essence—many people can see its great value, and many experts secretly purchase it. But few have mastered the specific method of using it, so there has never been a definite valuation."

Priceless but without a market?

Luo Chen savored this. "Then how do you usually trade it?"

"A large amount of high-grade spirit stones, or bartering!" the old man said.

Luo Chen nodded. "In that case, I happen to have a treasure I want to sell. I wonder if you, Elder, would be interested."

The old man showed a curious expression. What kind of thing could be called a treasure?

"Let's find a quiet room to talk!"

Luo Chen's gaze swept over the bustling shop, and he said calmly.

The old man naturally wouldn't refuse. He quickly led Luo Chen to a quiet room.

About half a day later, Luo Chen left the shop, his face showing neither satisfaction nor disappointment.

But the old man was beaming with joy.

It was because their shop was about to gain a treasure to anchor the establishment.

"A true artifact!"

He never imagined that Luo Chen would be so generous, actually taking out a true artifact for trade.

Although this true artifact had been defiled by some unknown power, causing it to lose most of its spirituality.

As long as they were willing to spend time, using a dozen years to gradually remove the defiled power, they could always restore some of its power.

The appraiser who had just been invited was now playing with the flying sword in confusion.

"It seems to be the tainted power of Netherwater?"

The old man was stunned, then thought of the Nether Origin Sect incident.

"Could that person just now have been someone who returned from treasure hunting at the Nether Abyss?"

As he thought.

The incomplete true artifact that Luo Chen had just sold was indeed something he had accidentally picked up while crossing the Nether Abyss.

Not just one, but several.

Most of them were at the magic treasure level, with only two or three being incomplete true artifacts.

The Xuanyan Turtle, seeing his fondness for picking up such things, had even deliberately taken him to places with similar treasures.

If Luo Chen hadn't been in a hurry to travel, he might have collected even more.

Such things were utterly useless to Luo Chen.

Even if he bestowed them upon his disciples, they would need to spend a lot of effort to remove the filth and carefully refine them because they had been defiled by Netherwater.

It was better to sell them off in exchange for what he needed.

"It's just that the quantity of soul essence is too small, and the trading process is too troublesome."

Luo Chen's mind moved, and he headed towards certain remote corners of Lingtian Pass that he remembered.

These big shops had too many rules.

Far less convenient than trading on the black market.

Although black market trading had other risks, with Luo Chen's strength, he could completely ignore them.

"The method for making soul essence was also spread from these black markets back then. Now that I want to harvest the fruits, I should start from the ground where the seeds were planted."

"Besides, their information is more up-to-date. I can inquire about the situation in the Luo Tian Domain."

...

Just as Luo Chen was moving through Lingtian Pass, collecting resources and gathering information.

On the coast of the Dongyang Domain, the Demon-Reflecting Treasure Mirror, which had hung high for thousands of years, reflected a scene from afar.

A long, narrow ship, shaped like a Sword-Halberd Whale, cut through the wind and waves, speeding forward.

Figures were faintly visible on the ship, and banners fluttered in the wind.

As they were about to reach the coast, a young girl ran to the front of a palace hung with a sign reading [Kun Xiao].

"Senior Sister, the Eastern Wilderness is here!"

The palace gates rumbled open, and a graceful figure slowly walked out.

She wore a moon-white cloud-patterned silk long skirt, with silver thread flowing on the hem to form dark orchid patterns. She moved as if carrying mist and clouds.

Her black hair was styled into a morning cloud near-fragrant bun, with a slanting hairpin and swaying dangling hair ornament.

A few small pearl floral ornaments adorned her temples, and a light green tender leaf was painted between her brows, making her skin look even whiter than snow and frost.

Compared to her former ladylike demeanor, Fu Qinglan now carried an additional noble, unapproachable, aloof aura.

She looked at the Eastern Wilderness continent, so close at hand, then turned her limpid autumn-water eyes towards the vast sea behind her.

"Shi Ju will probably arrive soon too. I can't let him get ahead of me."
