# Chapter 911: Within Arm’s Reach, a Man Is an Enemy to the Whole Realm

The outside world was turned upside down, but within the blessed land, time flowed peacefully.

Yet, the moment those four heart-piercing words—“leading the wolf into the house”—were spoken, an atmosphere of slaughter spread uncontrollably.

Luo Chen’s eyelids lifted. He said in a deep voice, “Ancestor, you are going too far.”

Heize Ancestor grinned, his broken and battered body radiating a dense, dangerous aura.

“I cannot be mistaken!”

“Back then at Green Parasol Mountain, it was this golden flame. Outside just now, it was also this golden flame. And now… how do you intend to explain yourself?”

Before Luo Chen could defend himself.

Heize Ancestor sneered again. “I said, how could the Demon Phoenix instantly locate Mount Yanfu and even shatter the formation’s central hub with a single palm strike? So it turns out you were the traitor, coordinating with her from within.”

He looked around, his gaze falling on the array flags planted everywhere.

“With this level of formation mastery, who in the world would know that Luo Chen, a Grand Alchemy Master, is also a brilliant Formation Grandmaster?”

Luo Chen spoke once more. “Heize Ancestor, you are overthinking things.”

Heize Ancestor shook his head. “Am I overthinking? If I had thought enough back then, I would never have watched Green Parasol Mountain fly away, nor would I have brought you, this lackey of the Demon Phoenix, into the Nether Origin Sect.”

Luo Chen sighed and slowly rose to his feet.

Many things could not be explained, nor were they easy to explain.

What he displayed to the outside world was the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame, but what he had just manifested was the Nirvana Sacred Flame.

Hiding it so deliberately—who would believe he harbored no ill intentions?

Especially now, when the sect was suffering disaster and had suffered grievous losses.

But clearly, it all started when you, venerable ancestor, took the initiative to provoke Green Parasol Mountain!

He could see that Heize Ancestor had never regretted provoking Green Parasol Mountain. What he regretted was only that he had let Green Parasol Mountain escape back then, instead of keeping it there.

He regretted that Luo Chen had led Rosycloud inside, not that he had been defeated by her.

If the Nether Abyss Grand Formation had been relied upon, with Mount Yanfu’s terrain as support, and the combined strength of three great powers and countless Nether Origin Sect cultivators, who in this realm could have forcibly broken into the Nether Origin Sect?

Defeat often begins from within.

It was Luo Chen, that chess piece, who had caused the center to bloom, putting them in a passive position.

As Luo Chen stood up, a vast wave of mana fluctuations burst forth.

His Sage domain also expanded, drawing in the fire-attributed spiritual energy of the Alchemy Saint Blessed Land.

Thanks to Rosycloud, some of the Nirvana Sacred Flame had seeped in earlier, making the fire-attributed spiritual energy in this realm quite abundant.

Now, as it merged into Luo Chen’s domain, a spiritual pressure of terrifying intensity rippled outward.

Heize Ancestor watched this scene, a hint of admiration flashing in his eyes.

“Merely at the fifth layer of the Sage realm, yet your mana fluctuations are no less than those of any late-stage Great Sage. The intensity of this domain’s spiritual pressure—even if Chongxuan and Hanyang were still alive, I fear they could not compare to you.”

But the more admiration he felt, the thicker the anger on his face became.

“Although we knew you had ties to that Green Parasol Mountain, we still wanted to give you a chance. Time reveals a person’s heart. We even thought that if you performed well, we might help you achieve the Ascendant realm in the future.”

“But you, of all things, should never have committed the act of betraying your master!”

“Now, putting on such a show—do you still intend to make a move against me?”

Luo Chen slowly raised his right hand, fingers pressed together like a sword. Wisps of heaven and earth origin qi began to gather at his fingertip.

He asked calmly, “Heize, what kind of performance would be considered good enough to satisfy you? As for so-called betrayal—was joining the Nether Origin Sect something I did willingly? And finally, how would I, a junior, dare to make a move against a mighty Ascendant? I am merely seeking self-preservation.”

“Hahaha!”

Hearing these words, Heize Ancestor could not help but burst into laughter.

But as he laughed, he could not suppress a cough, choking the laughter in his throat.

“Cough, cough, cough…”

His steps still did not stop. He continued to approach Luo Chen, step by step.

His eyes fixed on Luo Chen’s finger, sensing the true intent of the law within it.

“That flame was the Nirvana Sacred Flame, and this law’s true intent is remarkably similar to Rosycloud’s Nirvana True Intent. You said you had received her guidance—you weren’t lying about that.”

“Luo has always treated others with sincerity!”

“Even if my spiritual treasure has been broken, my mind and spirit wounded, and I am being hunted by Rosycloud, in my current poor state—do you think you are a match for me?”

Luo Chen stared at the black-robed elder’s tattered body. It was like a torn cloth sack, leaking mana outward.

But at the same time, more heaven and earth origin qi was converging toward him.

Luo Chen understood. The other party was letting him gather his divine ability because he himself was also recovering his strength.

Killing Luo Chen might only take a single strike.

But how could Luo Chen just sit and wait for death?

A golden light flashed in his eyes. Before the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger could be unleashed, the cyan-white flame exploded outward from Luo Chen’s center, turning into a wave of fire that swept toward the opponent.

Facing this move, Heize Ancestor did not dodge or evade. He merely let a black halo rise from his body.

The Withering-Bloom Divine Flame crashed against that black halo like waves hitting a solid reef—able to pass over it, but unable to break it.

Heize Ancestor walked forward against the surging tide of flames, getting closer and closer to Luo Chen.

“This killing move of yours might pose a threat to any Ascendant, but you’ve unfortunately met me—the one most capable of countering fire.”

In an instant, Luo Chen’s expression turned ugly.

On the surface, he was gathering power for a divine ability technique, but in truth, his greatest killing move was the fifth-order Withering-Bloom Divine Flame.

But just as the other party had said, without an absolute gap in strength, fire is restrained by water.

This was the most basic principle of the Five Phases’ mutual restraint.

Yet at this moment, it was being displayed so thoroughly!

The elder’s body stopped one zhang away from Luo Chen.

“It’s over!”

“After you die, your son will go down to accompany you. The entire Luo Tian Sect, from top to bottom, will also accompany you.”

“Throughout the entire Eastern Wilderness, anyone who has any connection with you will not fare well!”

“Heavenly One…”

Heize Ancestor pushed his right fist forward. When it reached mid-air, he slowly opened it.

He murmured softly:

“Birth Water!”

The next moment!

A black halo, of the same origin as Heize Ancestor’s, erupted from Luo Chen’s body.

Like a spring of water, it gurgled and boiled.

And Luo Chen’s physical body, as if corroded, began to melt in an instant.

Having done this, Heize Ancestor could not help but breathe a sigh of relief.

It seemed that activating the divine ability he had left on Luo Chen had also cost him a great deal of strength in his current state.

The gathered heaven and earth origin qi began to slowly recede. He dared not let it linger, or else, in this state, he would surely suffer backlash and fall into the fate of being assimilated by heaven and earth.

“Everything… is over.”

As he said this, a deep weariness appeared on his dispirited face.

What did killing Luo Chen matter?

It was merely venting his anger.

But the Nether Origin Sect had, after all, suffered a devastating blow.

Post-war reconstruction was inevitable, but whether it could return to its peak was another matter.

Moreover, with the Nether Origin Sect so weakened, it was hard to avoid covetous eyes from the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

He lowered his heavy head and looked at the ground.

“At least there is this blessed land. It leaves a root for the Nether Origin Sect.”

At that very moment!

A figure walked out like a ghost from the black halo of melted snow. His fingers pressed together like a sword, and he thrust forward.

Within arm’s reach, a man is an enemy to the whole realm!

Caught off guard, Heize Ancestor instinctively used his remaining mana to inflate his dharma robe, which billowed high.

But then!

The sharp, unmatched sword tip pierced through like tearing cloth, stabbing into his body without resistance.

Pfft!

His balloon-like body deflated.

Heize Ancestor raised his head in disbelief, meeting Luo Chen’s red-eyed, golden-pupiled gaze.

“How did you break free from my divine ability’s control?”

Luo Chen did not answer him. There was only madness in his eyes.

A sword tip extended from his finger, frantically destroying Heize Ancestor’s body. It deeply pierced through the Purple Mansion barrier and stabbed into the already weak and exhausted primordial spirit.

“It’s a substitute puppet!”

Heize Ancestor’s thoughts flashed, and he instantly understood Luo Chen’s method.

There was no time to think. Only a furious roar escaped him.

“Get away!”

Mana surged, directly blasting Luo Chen’s body away.

He tumbled like a gourd across the ground, rolling endlessly, crashing into countless rocks and mountains.

But as Luo Chen was forced back, the stabbing pain in his primordial spirit did not diminish in the slightest. Instead, it grew more agonizing.

Heize Ancestor lowered his head and saw a blood sword embedded in his chest.

He reached out with trembling hands, trying to pull the blood sword out.

But it was already too late!

A bloodthirsty, mad will burst forth from within it, rushing toward the primordial spirit in his Purple Mansion and swallowing it in one gulp.

It was the Yuantu Sword Spirit!

When cultivators refine qi and refine spirit, upon reaching the Ascendant realm, the Nascent Soul merges with the soul to form a powerful primordial spirit.

In that state, even without a physical body, as long as the primordial spirit survives, the Great Dao remains unimpaired.

But once the primordial spirit is destroyed, everything is over.

And killing an Ascendant was not the Yuantu Sword’s first time.

Only the last time, the one who died under its blade was Blood Sea Ancestor of the Primordial Demon Sect.

This time, it was Heize Ancestor of the Nether Origin Sect.

Now, having drunk its fill of blood and primordial spirit, the Yuantu Sword Spirit let out a sharp, piercing sword cry—as if rejoicing, or perhaps like a belch after a full meal.

And Heize Ancestor stood there, eyes wide open, like a wooden statue or clay sculpture.

“Ancestor!!!”

A shrill, piercing voice suddenly rang out.

Ye Ruoli, who had been protected within the ring of fire, had been suppressed by Luo Chen’s domain earlier and could not speak.

Only now, after this brief yet perilous battle had ended, could she make a sound.

She could not believe it, nor was she willing to believe that the revered sect ancestor she admired had died right before her eyes, his aura completely extinguished.

In the distance.

A tall figure struggled out from a pile of rubble.

Under Ye Ruoli’s terrified gaze, he walked step by step toward Heize Ancestor.

“Hall Master Alchemy Saint, no!”

“The Ancestor cannot die!”

Luo Chen stopped in his tracks. Blood trickled from the corner of his mouth, and his aura was in disarray.

“If he does not die, then I die.”

Hearing these words, the young woman staggered, almost touching the ring of fire beside her. Fortunately, Luo Lingxi grabbed her hand.

“Sister Ruoli, Father had no choice.”

Luo Lingxi said this, but in her mind echoed the words Heize Ancestor had spoken before he tried to kill Luo Chen.

Everyone connected to Luo Chen would die!

Father had done nothing wrong!

Ye Ruoli’s face was pale, and her lips trembled uncontrollably. “But… but…”

There were no more “buts.”

Luo Chen walked up to Heize Ancestor and reached out to pull out the sword.

As early as the battle in the Overlord Blade Domain, he had realized the bloodthirsty nature of the Yuantu Sword Spirit.

Later, during the great battle in the Medicine King Domain, he had used his own source blood as a price to unlock the third seal of the Yuantu Sword, fully releasing the sword spirit.

But when Azure Frost arrived later, the Yuantu Sword Spirit had been utterly useless.

Azure Frost’s spatial divine abilities were too exquisite for the Yuantu Sword to find an opportunity to strike.

But this time!

It was an unparalleled, once-in-a-millennium opportunity!

A severely wounded Ascendant, with an attack distance of mere inches.

To land this sword strike, he had first used the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame, a substitute puppet, and even the divine ability Withering-Bloom Dao Finger as decoys, finally achieving this unexpected result.

And now…

Luo Chen gripped the sword hilt and slowly pulled it out.

Within it, the sword spirit transmitted a maddening urge, as if it wanted to slaughter everything.

Endless blood light, as the sword body was drawn out, splashed around like countless ribbons, carving deep and shallow ravines into the earth.

Luo Chen’s face turned fierce. “You have already acknowledged me as your master—do you still intend to rebel?”

As he said this, the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame directly enveloped the Yuantu Sword.

It seemed that if the sword said another word, he would refine it on the spot.

Threatened by the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame, the Yuantu Sword Spirit let out an unwilling, trembling cry, and finally chose to submit.

The stone in Luo Chen’s heart finally fell.

This was the drawback of nurturing the Yuantu Sword within the acupoint of his arm.

The connection between his mind and the sword was not close enough, and the sword’s own consciousness was too strong.

Unlike the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit, which, even after reaching the fifth order, willingly let Luo Chen control it—only demanding a large amount of mana as a condition.

After pulling out the Yuantu Sword, Luo Chen held it before him, looking at the gleaming blade with satisfaction in his eyes.

This God-Slaying Blood Sword, once famous on Coldlight Island, had become even more devastatingly powerful.

Once it digested Heize Ancestor’s primordial spirit, it might even take the final step, breaking free from the shackles of a pseudo-spirit treasure and achieving true spirit treasure rank.

At that time, he, Luo Chen, would also possess a killing weapon of his very own!

“Rest well!”

With a flash of blood light, the Yuantu Sword disappeared.

And Heize Ancestor’s shell crashed heavily to the ground, stirring up a little dust.

From not far away came the sound of sobbing.

Ye Ruoli wept uncontrollably. “Why… why…”

She did not understand why everything had changed.

In a single night, the flourishing sect had been devoured by war.

The revered ancestor, whom she had admired so much, had died at the hands of the Hall Master Alchemy Saint.

For her, who had grown up in the Nether Origin Sect under the protection of her clan’s elders, this was all too great a shock.

Luo Chen looked over and met Ye Ruoli’s eyes.

A trace of venomous hatred flickered through them.

Luo Chen acted as if he had not seen it. He lowered his head and looked at Heize Ancestor’s corpse.

Why?

Perhaps it was simply because the other party had not respected him enough?

To the lofty great powers of the Nether Origin Sect, all cultivators in the Eastern Wilderness could be taken or discarded at will, summoned or dismissed with a shout.

Was it he who had led the wolf into the house?

No!

It was merely they who had provoked the Demon Phoenix.

He, a mere Sage, was nothing more than a chess piece that could be moved at will before an Ascendant.

“A Sage is not a Sage; a great power cannot achieve greatness. If one wishes to roam free and live long, only by becoming an immortal can it be done.”

Murmuring to himself, Luo Chen released a wisp of Nirvana Sacred Flame and burned Heize Ancestor’s corpse to ashes.

This way, even if the strong of the Nether Origin Sect asked, he could simply say that Rosycloud had killed him.

In truth, Heize Ancestor’s fate today was indeed thanks to Rosycloud.

Luo Chen’s sword strike was merely the last straw that broke the camel’s back.

After doing all this, Luo Chen waved his hand behind him.

Thump!

Luo Lingxi stared blankly at the woman before him, who had fallen beside him. It seemed he could still feel the lingering warmth of her hand.

He looked at the extinguished Ye Ruoli, then turned his head in confusion toward the tall, back-facing man.

“Father, why?”

“That is not a question you should ask.”

Luo Chen did not turn around to look at his son. He merely shook his left shoulder, and a small dragon flew out, circling before him.

“Protect Lingxi. I will go and return shortly.”

“Yes, Master!” Black King replied with rare decisiveness, a hint of fear in his eyes.

Luo Chen reached out and summoned the token from the ground. He activated it with his mana, and his entire body was enveloped in mist and smoke, disappearing from the Alchemy Saint Blessed Land.

In the vast Alchemy Saint Blessed Land, silence now reigned.

Only the black dragon coiled and crouched. Only the golden ring of fire burned slowly.

The youth sat slumped within it, guarding the woman’s gradually cooling body.

(Chapter End)
