# Chapter 1024: Innate Spirit Body, Mayfly Between Heaven and Earth

Mountains and Seas Calendar, year 3681.

The Zhou clan, having relocated to White Egret Marsh, ceased all external conflicts, focusing solely on recuperation and restoring their strength.

The Zhou clan's ancestor, whose great limit was rumored to be approaching, took out his lifelong accumulated savings to assist the clan's high-ranking cultivators in breaking through to higher realms. At the same time, he encouraged some lower-realm clan members with mediocre aptitudes to relax restrictions on childbirth, increasing the clan's population.

This was a two-pronged approach—supplementing high-level combat power from above while replenishing the foundational population from below.

Not only that, but the Zhou clan's ancestor personally arranged a meeting with the clan leaders of the Green Parasol Lin clan and the Cold River Valley Yin clan outside White Egret Marsh, reaching an alliance for harmonious coexistence and joint development.

The process went smoothly.

The Green Parasol Lin clan and the Cold River Valley Yin clan were merely ordinary members of the countless humble families under the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. Their so-called clan leaders were only Adept cultivators. Faced with the condescending friendship of a Sage like the Zhou clan's ancestor, they naturally accepted with delight.

Thus, the Zhou clan's external instability seemed to have settled down.

At the very least, before the Zhou clan's ancestor passed away, this alliance formed by one Sage clan and two Adept clans was temporarily stable on the Tiannan Continent. In terms of strength, they had the Zhou clan's ancestor, a sixth-layer Sage, overseeing them. In terms of connections, the Lin and Yin clans were also forces under the Dao Sect. No one would deliberately come looking for trouble.

As for when the Zhou clan ancestor's great limit would arrive?

Although there was much speculation from the outside world, seeing the Zhou clan's ancestor so active, no one could pinpoint the exact time.

After the three clans formed their alliance, the Zhou clan's ancestor no longer went out. However, he did not go into seclusion to recuperate either. Instead, he frequently appeared within the clan, personally instructing three Adept cultivators and some Initiate Realm juniors who showed promise of breaking through to the Adept stage.

It must be said that the Zhou clan's ancestor was indeed a seasoned Sage. Under his personal guidance, the Zhou clan gained two more Adept cultivators in just one year!

This pace of restoring their strength aroused envy from surrounding forces.

Knowing how to cultivate was one thing, but knowing how to teach others to cultivate was another matter entirely.

However, they were not entirely clear on the inside story.

---

On this day.

Deep within White Egret Marsh, where miasma flourished and poisonous insects and venomous snakes abounded, a guest arrived.

Dressed in plain white robes embroidered with scattered coral branches, his face wrinkled but ruddy, it was none other than the Zhou clan's ancestor, Zhou Yunshen.

As he gradually penetrated deeper into White Egret Marsh, the surrounding miasma parted as if it had a will of its own, clearing a path for him, as if welcoming him.

Zhou Yunshen watched this scene, his expression somewhat complex.

The power casually displayed by the one dwelling deep within White Egret Marsh was already so miraculous and formidable.

This naturally formed miasma had the special effect of corrupting spiritual power. Cultivators dared not let it touch them at all. He himself had the strength to deal with it, but at his advanced age, he could not spare the attention. Yet in that person's hands, this miasma had become an utterly docile pet, freely commanded at will.

Suddenly.

A voice like clashing metal and stone rang out from within.

"Old friend, you've come."

When the miasma had fully dispersed, Zhou Yunshen exhaled a turbid breath and stepped into a water-curtain cave.

Gazing at the man sitting cross-legged like a sharp sword, he spoke with a complex mix of gratitude and reverence. "Thanks to your pills and the cultivation insights and scriptures you gave me, the Zhou clan gained two new Adept cultivators a few months ago."

The black-robed man kept his eyes closed, speaking calmly. "The Central Continent collapsed, yet they still managed to survive. Attaining this realm now is merely a small blessing after surviving a great calamity."

Zhou Yunshen shook his head. "I still need to thank you."

"If it were only to thank me, old friend, why would you have come today?" A blood-colored longsword lay horizontally across the black-robed man's knees, wreathed in strands of three-colored Nine Firmament Qi. As he circulated his mana, that firmament Qi gradually sank into the longsword. "Speak. Has there been progress on my matter?"

Zhou Yunshen seemed to have made up his mind. He spoke heavily, "Found it!"

Luo Chen's fingertip froze, pressing down on the sword. With a *shua*, his eyes snapped open.

Zhou Yunshen shook his head and spoke slowly. "Over the past year, with the Zhou clan lifting all resource restrictions, a total of thirty-seven women have become pregnant. Using the top-grade water lotus seeds you provided, we tested and found that nine fetuses possess considerable water-attributed spiritual energy affinity. In other words, these nine children may have water spirit roots."

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow. "That high a probability?"

Zhou Yunshen smiled bitterly. "I had all clan members who had no possibility of breaking through to the Adept stage set aside all mundane affairs and focus on procreation. Moreover, during the process, we used the special spirit-gathering formations you provided, which is how we achieved these results."

When mortals bore children, it was difficult for even one in a thousand to have a spirit root.

But if both parents were cultivators, after their bodies had undergone marrow cleansing and decades of spiritual energy infusion, it became much easier to conceive children with spirit roots.

This was why families flourished in the Central Continent, and those with spiritual root talent emerged in an endless stream.

And if, during the process of conception, the fetus was also influenced by the specially modified formations Luo Chen had provided, there was a high chance of conceiving a fetus with a water spirit root.

However, before the fetus was born, and even before it grew to eleven or twelve years old, it was actually very difficult to detect the spirit root. But this was not difficult for Luo Chen. He had taken the fourth-rank top-grade Five Elements Lotus Platform's water lotus seeds and, combined with the corresponding techniques, could vaguely sense the existence and approximate attribute of the fetus's spirit root.

It could be said that from the very beginning, Luo Chen had put great effort into the plan to seize the life soul.

In Zhou Yunshen's view, reaching this step should have been sufficient. He did not understand why Luo Chen had specifically reminded him that he *must* come to inform him at this time.

*Swoosh!*

The blood sword flew up, circled once, and finally disappeared into Luo Chen's right arm.

The black-robed man rose to his feet, clasped his hands behind his back, and walked outside.

"Take me to see those nine children!"

Zhou Yunshen was puzzled, not understanding Luo Chen's purpose. But having received Luo Chen's benefits and his promise for the future, he naturally would not oppose such a trivial matter.

The two walked one after the other, leaving the depths of the White Egret Marsh's foul swamp and heading towards the Zhou clan's settlement.

---

"It's not nine, it's ten."

In a quiet room, a gentle young woman slept soundly.

The young man and the weathered old man stood before the bed, gazing at this last pregnant woman, speaking in low voices.

Zhou Yunshen instinctively avoided Luo Chen's eyes, which were gleaming with golden light, and said in surprise, "Could it be that Jingshu is carrying twins?"

Luo Chen nodded slightly. "Not just twins, but a dragon and phoenix pair."

Zhou Yunshen stroked his beard. "I didn't notice that. But judging from the earlier test with the water lotus seed, Jingshu's pair of children have only an average affinity for water spiritual energy. They can't compare to Xiaoqi's child."

However, Luo Chen shook his head, a strange smile appearing on his face.

"No, it's him!"

Zhou Yunshen was confused.

Luo Chen's lips curled up slightly. "The fetuses in the womb are one male and one female, one yin and one yang, perfectly complementary. I have a technique, combined with medicinal decoctions, that can take from the strong to supplement the weak, transferring the innate qi of one child to the other. At that time, the child who receives the supplement will be born ahead of everyone else, possessing an unimaginable foundation."

"There is such a secret technique in this world?" Zhou Yunshen was extremely surprised.

Luo Chen hummed in acknowledgment. "This technique is called the Life-Fostering Art. The required medicinal decoctions are also not complicated."

Zhou Yunshen clicked his tongue in wonder. "Such a secret technique, unheard of. Truly worthy of the Alchemy Sect!"

Luo Chen smiled. This technique was not actually his own creation. It originated from a record in the seventy-two volumes of alchemy scriptures left by the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining.

It was a method of the demon race!

The demon race's reproductive ability was far inferior to that of the human race. Often, when a single pregnancy produced multiple offspring, the resulting children would have a clear disparity in strength.

The demon race pursued an elite policy. The weaker offspring, once born, were mostly left to fend for themselves and received little cultivation.

In view of this, someone simply adhered to the principle of not wasting resources. While the multiple fetuses were still in the mother's body, they would gather the innate qi and even the root-bone attributes of the weaker ones onto the stronger one.

In this way, there was a high chance of producing a peerless genius.

Zhou Yunshen curiously asked another question. "And what about the child whose innate qi is drained?"

Luo Chen said coldly, "They become a cripple or a mediocrity, unable to cultivate for their entire life."

The old man was stunned, instinctively tugging at his graying beard.

Luo Chen's action would not only use one of the Zhou clan's children but also sacrifice the future of another child!

"What, has your heart softened?" Luo Chen turned around, looking at the old man.

Zhou Yunshen opened his mouth, his eyes full of heartache.

In recent days, he had given up on his own cultivation, focusing entirely on instructing and guiding his descendants. In those repeated interactions, the previously vague blood ties gradually became stronger.

Even though Lin Jingshu was a woman sent from the Green Parasol Lin clan for a marriage alliance, the child in her womb was still of Zhou clan blood!

By his ear came Luo Chen's increasingly indifferent voice.

"Old friend, I gave you pills, gifted you cultivation insights, and spent a great price to help extend your lifespan. You should have known long ago what you would have to pay."

"But..."

"Forget it. I won't let her nourish my avatar for nothing. I will give her another path. Although that path will be very difficult, it is still a path."

Zhou Yunshen raised his head in pleasant surprise.

Luo Chen had not promised anything, but since he had said it, Zhou Yunshen was willing to believe him.

"Next, old friend, please step outside the room for a moment!"

Zhou Yunshen nodded, pursed his lips, and withdrew from the room, leaving only Luo Chen silently gazing at the gentle young woman.

He lifted the hem of the young woman's garment, revealing her fair, rounded belly.

Slowly raising his right hand, he bit his fingertip and squeezed out a drop of essence blood.

The drop of essence blood fell onto the rounded belly, gradually seeping into it.

Luo Chen brought his fingers together like a sword, tracing in the air, outlining mysterious and complex patterns.

Those patterns also gradually sank into the woman's abdomen.

After doing all this, Luo Chen opened his mouth and spat out the Primordial Unity Cauldron, which flew out with a *drip*.

Gazing at the Primordial Unity Cauldron, Luo Chen hesitated for a moment, but ultimately steeled his heart.

Within a hundred years, to cultivate a Sage from nothing was not something that could be achieved by merely accumulating resources.

A single mouthful of vigorous innate qi was not enough.

A single water-type heavenly spirit root was still not enough.

Even with the help of the top-grade water lotus seeds, it was still too slow.

Only an innate spirit body would do!

And to create an innate spirit body, the Luo Chen of the past could not have done it. But the Luo Chen of the present had some confidence.

The key lay in the object within the cauldron.

"Come out!"

In a murmur, a gurgling spring eye slowly flew out from the gray small cauldron.

The Netherwater Spring Eye!

It contained the origin of water, a naturally formed creation object. As long as it was dispersed and integrated into the fetus, it could, through continuous nurturing, forge an innate water spirit body.

As for whether the fetus could withstand it?

This was not very difficult for Luo Chen, given that he had already refined the Netherwater Spring Eye and grasped the true meaning of the water origin law.

Luo Chen pointed out a finger, and the floating Netherwater Spring Eye shattered in an instant.

In the quiet room, a light rain began to fall.

And outside the room, the sky was without wind or clouds, without thunder or lightning, yet suddenly a torrential downpour began.

The entire White Egret Marsh was shrouded in a vast curtain of rain.

Zhou Yunshen stared blankly at this scene, vaguely feeling his Sage soul stir restlessly.

He did not know how long passed before he heard the sound of a wooden door closing beside him, along with a faintly weary voice.

"After the child is born, remember to give him that water lotus seed."

Zhou Yunshen was jolted awake, a sense of loss on his face, as if he had just missed something.

Under the eaves' rain curtain, he looked at the man's tired face, not knowing what the other had done.

He could only couldn't help but remind him, "You said you would give the other child a way out."

"I won't forget. Before I leave, I will leave the things in the White Egret Marsh's foul swamp."

After leaving these words, Luo Chen stepped into the rain curtain, flickering a few times before completely disappearing from sight.

---

And half a year later.

In that room, a loud cry was heard.

On that day, over the vast White Egret Marsh, another torrential downpour fell.

The heavy rain came without reason and disappeared without a trace, as if it had suddenly cleared up after the infant's cries ceased.

Zhou Baihan and Lin Jingshu, first-time parents, did not notice this strange phenomenon at all, completely immersed in the joy of having a son and a daughter.

But after the joy and excitement, a sense of doubt and regret arose.

"Brother is so robust, why is sister so thin and frail, light as a feather in my arms."

"Husband, it's normal for a girl to be slender. Won't brother protect her well from now on?"

"Haha, yes! From now on, you must protect your little sister well!"

Amidst laughter and joy, the Zhou clan gained another pair of children.

And three days later, the Zhou clan's ancestor, feeling grateful for the clan members' hard work over the past two years, distributed generous rewards. To Zhou Baihan's family, he specially bestowed a water lotus seed.

And he personally named the dragon and phoenix twins.

The girl was called Zhou Qingyu (Light Feather).

The boy was called Zhou Fuyou (Mayfly).

---

"Master, are we leaving?"

"It's time to go. We've been here for a long time."

"Then where are we going?"

"Before we leave, let's travel around the Tiannan Continent."

Within the White Egret Marsh's foul swamp, the voices of a man and a woman answered each other.

Luo Chen looked at the scenery inside the water-curtain cave and took out a series of items from his storage ring.

Pills, ores, cultivation insights, and some small techniques for using spells.

That was all. There was nothing else.

Little Bai roughly guessed who Luo Chen had left these things for, and couldn't help but ask curiously, "Is it that simple?"

Luo Chen shook his head. "It's enough. Interfering too much would let people detect my traces. I believe that the soul split from my Nirvana Primordial Spirit, even without my memories or will, will not have an ordinary life."

"And what about that girl?"

"It's in there too. Don't worry."

Luo Chen sighed, rising to his feet. Little Bai followed behind him, step by step, as they left the water-curtain cave.

(End of chapter)
