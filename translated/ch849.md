# Chapter 849: Stepping into the Void, Wind God Without Trace, Three Souls Return, Full-Power Posture!

Baili Qingchuan once again welcomed its master.

All the cultivators of the Luo Tian Sect had returned!

However, compared to the "peak" of over three hundred people before, now, at most, there were only about two hundred left.

A loss of one-third!

From this small example, one could see the bigger picture. Looking at the entire Eastern Wilderness, the losses in this war were probably even greater than this!

But no one had time to mourn or grieve anymore.

Everyone's mind was focused on constructing defensive fortifications and arranging various offensive and defensive formations.

Luo Chen handed over all the defensive arrangements of the Luo Tian Sect to Min Longyu, with Sect Leader Li Yingzhang leading the entire sect's manpower to assist him.

Everything, everything, was in preparation for the upcoming final decisive battle.

As for Luo Chen, he was together with Wang Yuan, beginning to deduce and complete the move "Wind Leg" from the *Wind, Moon, Thunder, Cloud Steps to Immortality*.

The *Wind, Moon, Thunder, Cloud Steps to Immortality*, strictly speaking, was a complete body-tempering technique, focusing on the lower limbs of the human body.

Among them, the four moves—Wind Leg, Moon Step, Cloud Shoes, and Thunder Kick—corresponded to four aspects: rapid attack, combat movement, long-distance travel, and instantaneous burst.

Unfortunately, when Luo Chen obtained this technique, only the Thunder Kick move was complete; the other three forms were all incomplete.

Later, with Wang Yuan's deduction, the Moon Step was finally completed.

Luo Chen had practiced the Moon Step and was quite satisfied with its effect.

In battle, unless he encountered formation restrictions, once he executed it, his dodging, shifting, and teleportation were so fast that even the divine sense of a Sage could hardly keep up.

Thus, Luo Chen was even more looking forward to the Wind Leg move.

He possessed a powerful physique, which had repeatedly performed meritorious deeds in countless battles.

However, Luo Chen's development and application of this physique had never been perfect.

Back then, the *Devil Lord's Seven Scattered Hands*, which he had integrated from the strengths of many schools, focused more on the development of upper body techniques. If he could also develop powerful lower body techniques, it would undoubtedly be a great improvement for facing enemies in battle.

...

"The *Three Forms of the Wind God* from the Misty Cloud Sect are extremely fast, with extremely strong force, and can be described as domineering and fierce. Once executed, they are as violent as a storm."

"But this technique has a flaw: it emphasizes form over intent, it can release but cannot retract."

"In terms of upper limit of power, it cannot compare to the original Thunder Kick. In battle, the inability to control it freely can easily threaten oneself. Therefore, this technique is quite useless."

"On the other hand, the *Void-Stepping Steps* you obtained from the White Crane Sect emphasize intent over form, focusing on self-control of the body and mind."

"The two indeed have the potential to complement each other."

"If we can use the general outline of the Wind Leg to fuse these two body arts, we might truly be able to complete this ancient body art!"

On a plain in Baili Qingchuan, Wang Yuan spoke eloquently to Luo Chen.

As a martial arts grandmaster, although Wang Yuan might have been surpassed by Luo Chen in the realm of body tempering, in terms of understanding martial arts techniques, he was still above Luo Chen, who was a "borrower."

In these two hundred years, without guidance from predecessors, Wang Yuan had relied on his own insight and comprehension to create multiple techniques.

The Giant Spirit Transformation was a grand technique of physical transformation!

The Blood God Tribulation Finger, Immortal Returning to Penglai, and the Blood-Transforming Demon Finger—these three divine, immortal, and demonic fingers were an ultra-high-level utilization of blood and qi changes!

After reaching the Furnace Realm, he had even fused thirty-six strands of divine wind and thirty-six earthly balefulnesses to create a technique called *Divine Astral Fighting Qi*.

Back then, on the Lingtian Pass dueling platform, Wang Yuan had used the Divine Astral Fighting Qi to forcefully break the Four Symbols Dharma Realm of Yin Sixiang from the Five Elements Divine Sect.

One must know that the Four Symbols Dharma Realm was quite similar to the Encompassing Fire Prison that Luo Chen had researched during his Adept period, both imitating the domain power of Sage cultivators.

Once executed, few below the Sage level could withstand it.

Yet even so, it was forcefully broken by Wang Yuan, forcing Yin Sixiang to use the Motian Cliff of Old Monster Motian.

This fully demonstrated the power of the *Divine Astral Fighting Qi*.

It could be said that Wang Yuan's ability to reach his current state was largely due to his powerful comprehension!

Unlike Luo Chen, whose so-called body tempering was more about the crazy accumulation of resources.

At this moment, the two were deducing the *Wind Leg* together, with Wang Yuan taking the lead.

But Luo Chen was not useless either.

For example:

"Pour all your source power into your left leg, standing like a heavenly pillar. The source power in your right leg passes through the Liver Meridian of Foot Jueyin and the Kidney Meridian of Foot Shaoyin, focusing on stimulating the Taixi, Jiaoxin, Zhaohai... these dozen or so acupoints. Twist your waist, swing your foot, and strike twelve times in succession."

"Try it!"

As Wang Yuan's words fell, Luo Chen began the experiment without any hesitation.

Any cultivator has a good understanding of the body's meridians and acupoints, especially Luo Chen, who was also a decent physician, with an even deeper understanding of the human body than ordinary people.

In an instant, he understood Wang Yuan's meaning.

Using source power instead of blood qi, he infused it into his left and right legs respectively, and then...

*Smack! Smack! Smack!*

After nine consecutive kicks, at the tenth kick, Luo Chen's expression changed drastically.

*Boom!*

A puff of blood mist burst from his wildly swinging right leg.

Then came a *crack* sound that made the listener's heart tremble.

Luo Chen landed on the ground, standing on one leg like a golden rooster.

His right leg was a bloody mess, hanging limply there.

Broken!

Luo Chen's flesh was extremely strong, his sinews extremely tough, and his bones extremely hard, all rare in the world, yet he had managed to break it himself during the experiment.

Such a terrifying scene was commonplace for Wang Yuan. He quickly approached and began to examine Luo Chen's broken right leg.

"The Taixi acupoint cannot withstand the massive energy; this is the key point where your right leg failed to hold up."

"The Zhaohai acupoint needs to take on more of the burden."

"This meridian should be fine; the source power should go through this one..."

"That's all I have for now. Luo Chen, tell me how you feel!"

Luo Chen nodded, his expression calm as he described his feelings just now.

"The conflict in the body represents the contradiction between form and intent. There's a problem with the fusion of the Void-Stepping Steps and the Wind God Leg. One must be chosen as the dominant side, otherwise similar situations might occur again."

Wang Yuan stroked his chin, showing a thoughtful expression.

Seeing him thinking, Luo Chen didn't waste any time.

With a thought, his flesh and blood began to slowly mend, and in a short while, his right leg was restored to its original state.

Wang Yuan caught a glimpse of this from the corner of his eye. Even though he had seen it many times recently, he still couldn't help shaking his head, a look of envy on his face.

Flesh and blood regeneration, reconnecting severed limbs!

This ability was the most coveted by body tempering cultivators.

Even those who later cultivated to the Leakless Realm rarely possessed it.

In his estimation, at least orthodox body tempering cultivators would need to reach the fifth realm to master such a method.

But Luo Chen, who practiced body tempering as a side discipline, could achieve this level.

It was truly infuriating to compare!

However, this method was most suitable for their current research on techniques.

This was one of Luo Chen's current roles!

Relying on his ability to regenerate flesh and blood and reconnect severed limbs, he could recklessly experiment with the various hypotheses and conjectures Wang Yuan proposed.

He didn't need to worry about the damage to his body from experimenting with techniques.

Trying again and again, eliminating wrong answers one by one, step by step moving towards the finish line of success.

The progress of deducing the Wind Leg, under their seamless cooperation, advanced by leaps and bounds at an unimaginable speed.

...

One month later.

Still on that plain, two figures were flipping up and down, moving left and right, their leg shadows raining down like raindrops.

They were fighting, testing moves.

From time to time, terrifying sounds of legs and feet colliding echoed in the void, like thunder.

Rolling waves of air pressed down, making the grass and trees on the plain unable to lift their heads.

Especially on Wang Yuan's side, when he kicked out, leg shadows condensed from astral qi would even appear in the void, leaving huge craters on the ground when they landed.

On the other hand, Luo Chen's side was unremarkable, showing no signs of power.

Suddenly!

The two figures, after a violent collision, separated and landed on the ground.

Wang Yuan staggered back, leaving deep footprints on the earth with each step, his face flushed red.

Luo Chen raised his hand and tossed a gourd over.

Wang Yuan forcefully steadied himself, caught the gourd, opened it, and took a sip of spirit wine, his flushed face easing.

He couldn't help but shout.

"What a thrill!"

After saying that, he was about to throw the gourd back.

Luo Chen waved his hand, indicating that he had another Purple Gold Gourd at his waist.

"Keep that gourd for yourself; I have something better here!"

Taking a small sip of the thousand-year Five Temple Immortal Brew, Luo Chen also showed a satisfied smile.

Wang Yuan stomped his slightly numb legs and asked, "You weren't going all out just now, were you?"

Luo Chen smiled. "Indeed, I was holding back."

Wang Yuan sighed with emotion. "Thinking back to when we used to spar, I was the one who had to hold back. Now it's you who has to restrain your strength, and in my proudest field of body tempering, no less. Tsk tsk..."

Although it was a sigh of regret, there was no jealousy in it.

Luo Chen put down his gourd and walked over lazily.

"Finally succeeded. Stepping into the void, Wind God without trace!"

Stepping into the void, Wind God without trace!

These simple eight words were the best summary of the completed Wind Leg.

Once executed, it was as fast as a storm, as strong as raging waves. It lacked a bit of fierceness and dominance, but the attacks were denser and could strike from various angles. It could even use source power and blood qi to condense footprints for long-range attacks.

Not only that, after dozens of experiments where Luo Chen had blown up his own thighs, this move could also be perfectly linked with Moon Step and Thunder Kick.

Truly unpredictable in its changes, unfathomable in its power!

"Do we still need to complete the Cloud Shoes?" Wang Yuan asked.

Luo Chen waved his hand. "Not for now. The *Ninety Thousand Li* we learned before is already a very good long-distance travel method. Of course, after we survive this great war, when we have time in the future, we can research new techniques together like this."

Wang Yuan nodded, smacking his lips, also very satisfied with their recent collaborative research.

His ultra-high comprehension in martial arts, combined with Luo Chen's terrifying body that could be experimented on at will, was truly a match made in heaven for researching techniques!

The fact that they had deduced the incomplete Wind Leg in just over a month was proof.

If there were no war, he would wish to continue living like this. Perhaps he could even find the opportunity to cross the fourth realm of body tempering.

Wang Yuan sighed. "The final decisive battle... I wonder when it will come?"

"Soon!"

Luo Chen replied.

Wang Yuan looked over, and Luo Chen repeated solemnly.

"Soon!"

Unlike Wang Yuan, who focused all his mind on researching techniques, Luo Chen, while experimenting, also paid attention to the situation at Lingtian Pass.

With Lingfengzi occasionally sending him intelligence, he also had some understanding of the movements of the demon army.

A year had passed since the battle where Lingtian Pass was breached!

In that year, Lingtian Pass had continuously replenished its cultivators, and the demon side had done the same.

The eight great commanders had all gathered!

Dozens of fourth-rank Demon Emperors were watching covetously!

The low-rank demon beasts under their command were countless, no longer measurable in the millions. The nine surrounding great domains were all filled with demons of all sizes, like waves in the ocean.

Such a terrifying force, though powerful, could never be sustained.

Even the Ancient Demon Emperor, Di Tian, could not maintain it any longer.

Therefore, the day of the final decisive battle was imminent!

...

Being able to complete the Wind Leg before the final decisive battle was excellent news.

On the very day it was completed, Luo Chen had already entered the gate.

And after multiple sparring sessions with Wang Yuan, he had brushed his proficiency up to the Mastery level.

The reason why his cultivation speed was faster than that of Thunder Kick and Moon Step was mainly because this body art had been experimented on Luo Chen himself from the very beginning, making it the most suitable for his body.

Naturally, the cultivation speed was beyond imagination.

Not only that, Luo Chen had also taken the opportunity to raise his Moon Step to the Grandmaster level.

This was also why he could control his moves freely during his sparring with Wang Yuan.

However, after reaching these two levels, the speed inevitably slowed down, requiring more effort to practice.

To prepare for battle, Luo Chen had to maintain his state, so he stopped.

At the summit of Daqing Mountain, within the Luo Tian Grand Hall.

An ice castle emitting extreme cold power stood quietly.

Luo Chen's divine sense entered it and saw an ice coffin placed inside, with Canglong still sleeping.

However, her usually furrowed willow-leaf brows had long since relaxed under the hazy light emitted by a mottled stone mirror.

With a thought, Luo Chen contacted his second soul, which had entered the stone mirror!

...

"Canglong, I never expected your soul foundation to be so powerful. I originally planned to teach you the *Ten Directions Extreme Freezing Dao* after you reached the Adept stage. Now it seems you already have the qualifications to learn this technique at the Initiate stage."

"The *Ten Directions Extreme Freezing Dao*? Could it be the most powerful technique of my Icecastle that is rumored?"

"Yes, this technique reaches the fourth rank and contains the method for forming a Nascent Soul. Only those with both Water and Ice spirit roots are qualified to learn it. Moreover, one must have a powerful soul foundation to proceed without hindrance. You can now try to learn it."

"Thank you, Master!"

Canglong took the original copy of the technique and happily left her master's cave dwelling.

Along the way, she encountered gazes of jealousy, envy, respect, and admiration.

She paid them no mind until she returned to her own cave dwelling, where she carefully opened the *Ten Directions Extreme Freezing Dao* and began to study it intently.

"Woof!"

Suddenly, a dog bark came from beside her.

She looked up and saw a huge red dog with golden pupils walking out.

Canglong smiled sweetly. "Mingzun, thank you for teaching me the *Soul-Splitting Art*. It made my soul foundation strong. My master just gave me the core technique of Icecastle in advance."

Mingzun, the God of Fire!

The red dog, called Mingzun, was like a burning flame, walking step by step towards the exit of the cave dwelling.

Hearing the woman's words, he turned back to look at her.

Canglong was stunned for a moment, then realized something and immediately asked, "Are you leaving?"

Mingzun nodded and then continued walking out of the cave dwelling.

The woman was a little anxious and chased after him.

"Practice the *Soul-Splitting Art* well. I will return."

When she chased out of the cave dwelling, the red dog was nowhere to be seen, leaving only a dog bark in her ears.

The woman stood there in a daze for a long time before walking back into the cave dwelling dejectedly.

...

*Whoosh!*

A ray of dim light flew out from the ice castle and entered Luo Chen's crown.

After a long time, Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes.

After many years, with the help of the Dustbound Immortal Mirror and his second soul, Canglong had constructed a complete dream, and it was relatively stable.

Thus, his divided soul no longer needed to stay inside all the time.

In the future, when he had time, he could go in again to help stabilize the dream and guide Canglong to continue practicing the *Soul-Splitting Art*.

As for now, Luo Chen naturally had to retrieve all his divided souls to ensure he was at his peak combat state.

Luo Chen sank deep into Daqing Mountain and came to the spirit cave guarded by Black King.

The puppet, Jin San, was sitting cross-legged in the spirit cave, absorbing the qi of heaven and earth.

Luo Chen observed carefully for a while and showed a satisfied expression.

"The nurturing is going well. At this rate, it can be used for battle after the ten-year period is up."

But!

That was still ten years away.

And for now, this puppet couldn't help Luo Chen much.

With a thought, a ray of dim light flew out from the puppet and entered Luo Chen's sea of consciousness.

The third soul had returned!

The puppet, Jin San, still sat quietly in the spirit cave. Without the dominant divided soul, the speed of absorbing the qi of heaven and earth immediately slowed down.

However, due to the formations inscribed on it, it was still slowly absorbing the surrounding spiritual energy.

Black King swam beside Luo Chen. Seeing this action, he asked curiously, "Master, is something big about to happen? Recently, the cultivators of the Luo Tian Sect have been frequently extracting the earth vein's spiritual energy. Now you've taken back your divided souls. You're so solemn. Is there going to be another fight?"

"Mm."

Feeling the fullness in his sea of consciousness, Luo Chen responded casually.

After years of nurturing, the strength of his second soul had reached about the third layer of the Sage stage. Even the third soul, which was split off later, had been promoted to about the second layer of the Sage stage with the help of the Great Soul Pill.

With both of them returning, combined with the powerful foundation of his main soul, Luo Chen felt that if he fused them all, his strength would be no less than that of an ordinary sixth-layer Sage cultivator.

"With this level of strength, I might still be unable to withstand the attacks of a Grandmaster skilled in soul arts, but against ordinary soul attacks, I can already forcibly endure them."

"Moreover, with two divided souls, I can multi-cast spells while multitasking, catching the enemy off guard!"

"This is my full-power posture!"

For the upcoming war, Luo Chen, who was in the rear, was relatively safe.

But one must consider the unexpected!

If a troublesome enemy appeared, even threatening the Luo Tian Sect, he would have to consider killing them quickly.

Luo Chen could no longer bear to watch his friends, family, and disciples die before his eyes.

He was ready for battle.

His gaze fell on Black King, and Luo Chen suddenly smiled.

"I almost forgot about you. Come up!"

With a wave of his hand, Black King's body shrank, and he obediently climbed onto his left arm, turning into a dragon-shaped tattoo, disappearing into his left shoulder.

Thus, having learned the Wind Leg, with his three souls returned and Black King attached to his body, Luo Chen had reached the peak of his combat power in this life!

Just how strong was he? Luo Chen didn't know.

But he knew that as long as it wasn't a Grandmaster-level existence, he was confident he could kill them!

Even against a Grandmaster, he had a certain chance of escaping unscathed!

"I wonder which unlucky bastard will run into Baili Qingchuan?"

Luo Chen dusted off his robe and drifted back to the Luo Tian Grand Hall.

He lit a top-quality Miao Shen Ye that Lingfengzi had given him and placed it in an incense burner.

Under the curling faint fragrance, the white-robed Daoist sat cross-legged, approaching stillness, silent and soundless.

(End of Chapter)
