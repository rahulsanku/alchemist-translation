# Chapter 840: Golden Plume Falls, His Intent Deserves Death

Left and right, the cyclones spun wildly.

Black turned to white, yin and yang interweaving.

Extreme dharma power followed opposing trajectories, erupting with terrifying grinding force.

Yet Luo Chen stood within it, surging blood qi exploding forth like an ancient desolate beast reborn, fearless of the vast disparity between heaven and earth, roaring in counterattack.

Mountain-Breaking Form, Mountain-Uprooting Form, Mountain-Crashing Form…

The Devil Lord's Seven Scattering Hands, which drew upon the strengths of a hundred schools, now unleashed unimaginable might under the torrential support of source power, battering the two great millstones until they swayed precariously.

The three Lu brothers watched this scene, their jaws nearly dropping to the ground.

Was this still a frail human cultivator?

But Luo Chen's attention was not on them at all at this moment. A pair of pale-gold divine pupils stared fixedly at the white light flying out from Heaven Pillar Mountain.

Alarm bells screamed in his mind!

Wrong! Wrong!

Completely wrong!

That white-robed man from before had merely been contaminated with a trace of this demonic creature's aura; he was by no means the one from the Overlord Blade Domain back then!

This white light was the true body!

It was the ghostly, sinister thing that Old Thief Shen Yuan had spoken of, the culprit who had harmed the Second Palace Master of Splendid Radiance Palace and the two Sages of Stacked Cloud Sect, and moreover, the bizarre existence that had struck fear into many during the century-long human-demon war!

Now appearing again, it had already devoured the nascent souls of three elders from Heavenly Capital Sect.

At this moment, it was flying straight toward Golden Plume.

In his line of sight, Golden Plume had clearly sensed the arrival of danger.

Originally, two golden feathers had been battling Thousand Rope Demon Emperor, but now he suddenly opened his mouth and spat out a beam of golden light.

The *Three Life-Seizing Golden Feathers*, which he had painstakingly cultivated for centuries, revealed themselves in their entirety for the first time in this world.

With a single strike, one golden feather heavily pierced through Thousand Rope Demon Emperor's shoulder. The formidable destructive force bored countless wounds through the demon emperor's body from the inside.

In an ordinary battle, Golden Plume could have pressed the advantage to pursue, but now he could only barely force the demon emperor back. Then he abruptly turned, holding two golden feathers, and thrust them straight forward.

Gold light and white light intersected in an instant.

Then, the white light seemed to ignore all defenses and plunged into Golden Plume's body.

Golden Plume gripped the two golden feathers, staring blankly downward, his eyes filled with disbelief.

"How… is this possible?"

In his murmuring voice, his body fell straight toward the earth like a kite with its string cut.

A beam of white light pierced through his chest and flew out in a flash.

Faintly, one could even hear the chilling sound of chewing and swallowing.

After killing Golden Plume, the white light showed no sign of stopping. It flew straight toward another enemy in the same direction.

It was Luo Chen!

The nine-zhang giant faced this scene, exhaling and opening his voice.

"Break… open!"

Overlord Removes Armor was unleashed once more, striking the already tottering yin-yang millstone, delivering the final blow that broke the camel's back.

*Boom!*

The yin-yang mill was shattered. The three Lu brothers, as if by prior agreement, spat blood simultaneously and flew backward.

Like Golden Plume, Luo Chen had no time to press the advantage. Black light wildly spread across his body as Mystic Dust Armor covered him entirely.

Not only that, but a thick layer of qi armor also stood guard outside.

Even his pale-gold pupils blazed with light as he stared fixedly at that streak of white light.

Mirror Flower Water Moon, open!

With just one exchange of glances, Luo Chen saw through the white light to that cruel and mocking gaze within.

Luo Chen let out a muffled grunt, his brow deeply furrowed.

His soul foundation was inferior to his opponent's. Not only did the illusion technique fail to take effect, but it also backlashed against him.

Now!

The white light flickered, shooting toward Luo Chen like an arrow.

The thick qi armor offered no defensive effect whatsoever, being sliced through like tofu.

The solid Mystic Dust Armor, eroded by the earlier Nine Chapters Bone-Melting Grand Formation, had become pitted and full of holes.

The white light followed these gaps, about to burrow into Luo Chen's body.

*Thump!*

As if crashing into some impenetrable bronze wall, the white light froze for a moment.

Only then did it realize that it was Luo Chen's powerful physical body. What was distributed across his flesh and blood was not dharma power, but rather something like the source power of a desolate beast?

"Is this man not human, but a desolate beast?"

Just as it hesitated, a deafening roar suddenly reached its ears.

"Get out!"

A dragon head emerged from Luo Chen's body, opening a bloody maw and forcibly driving the white light away from Luo Chen's side.

Black King appeared, saying nothing, and opened his mouth to inhale fiercely.

A terrifying devouring force spread through the surroundings.

The white light's ambush had failed, and it had no further opportunity to strike. It could only hastily retreat.

*Suck…*

Black King took a deep breath, revealing his hundred-zhang body as he coiled in midair.

And Luo Chen stood atop his dragon spine, his dark, sunken eyes staring fixedly into the distance.

A palace-skirted woman, beautiful as a flower.

A tall, gaunt man like a paper-cut figure, dressed in white.

The two stood side by side.

One revealed the aura of a Great Demon Emperor, suppressing the Heavenly Capital Sect battlefield, his gaze indifferent.

The other, though not a Great Demon Emperor, exuded a bizarre aura from head to toe. His gaze toward every person was like that of a predator eyeing prey—cruel and mocking.

*Swish!*

A beam of cyan light descended before Luo Chen.

It was Splendid Radiance Fairy, her expression extremely ugly.

"Luo Chen, what do we do now?"

The situation had completely spiraled out of control!

This was evident from the fact that she, who would normally address him respectfully as "Alchemy Lord," now called him directly by name.

Luo Chen pressed his lips tightly together, his gaze sweeping across the surroundings.

Thousand Rope Demon Emperor, Yellow Wind Guest, the three Lu brothers—these people had also returned one after another to Splendid Brocade Immortal's side.

Though each bore injuries, their combat power had not been significantly weakened.

On his own side, Golden Plume had fallen in battle. Of Heavenly Capital Sect's four great elders, only Heaven Pivot could barely fight; the rest had already perished.

As for those low-level cultivators…

For a moment, an atmosphere of despair began to spread across the entire battlefield.

…

"Gao Chang, this is the first time I've seen you fail."

Splendid Brocade Immortal glanced at Luo Chen with some surprise, speaking in a flat tone to the white-clothed man beside her.

Gao Chang twitched the corner of his mouth, pulling it into an exaggerated arc as if trying to split his entire face open.

"This man has seen me before, so he's been on guard."

Splendid Brocade Immortal was taken aback, then her gaze fell on a battlefield that had been leveled to the ground.

"No wonder when they first struck, they didn't kill Thousand Rope, didn't kill Yellow Wind Guest, didn't even bother with the three Lu brothers, but specifically targeted the Bai Lan you were parasitizing. So that's it!"

"Never mind. This man is merely tough in the flesh, plus he has a protective demon… desolate beast?"

As Gao Chang spoke, his gaze suddenly fixed on Black King, a look of uncertainty spreading across his face.

A fourth-rank desolate beast?

Where had this thing crawled out from some ancient wasteland?

Splendid Brocade Immortal paid no attention to this. Looking at the utterly silent battlefield, impatience appeared on her cold face.

"The Heavenly Capital Domain has delayed us too long. Finish this quickly!"

With a wave of her long sleeve, the other demon emperors immediately received their orders.

A great battle was about to erupt once more.

It was easy to imagine that the coming battle would be a one-sided slaughter!

But just as Splendid Brocade Immortal issued her command, a look of terror appeared on her face.

Instinctively, she swept her sleeve, pushing aside the white-clothed man beside her.

Then she saw a bolt of thunder descending from the heavens, grazing dangerously between the two of them, even destroying her sleeve.

The thunderbolt struck the ground, splitting the earth open.

Only then did the rumbling sound of thunder roll and spread in all directions.

"As expected of the favored consort of the ancient demon emperor, Splendid Brocade Immortal truly lives up to her reputation. But can you save this little ghost for a moment, or for a lifetime?"

A voice like thunder fell from the sky.

Everyone looked up to see ominous clouds gathering in the nine heavens above.

A tall, straight figure stood leaning on a pitch-black great sword, gazing down upon the vast earth.

"Thunder Daozi!"

Splendid Brocade Immortal spat out the newcomer's dao title, her expression changing drastically.

Without a second thought, she issued an order.

"Retreat!"

Even before her command, Gao Chang, who had been regarding everyone as prey, had already fled first.

Faced with this scene, Thunder Daozi sneered coldly, cast a white net toward Gao Chang, then waved his hand.

"Pursue!"

"Leave these beasts who dare to invade the Eastern Wilderness buried in this land!"

Behind him, the ominous clouds scattered, revealing figures one after another.

There were fully ten of them!

And each one was at the Sage stage!

From the insignia on their robes, these people were clearly cultivators of the Nether Origin Sect!

After issuing his orders, Thunder Daozi did not lag behind. With a flash of lightning, he chased after Gao Chang.

From beginning to end, he did not exchange a single word with Luo Chen or Splendid Radiance Fairy.

Ignoring them, as if looking down on them!

Splendid Radiance Fairy's previously stiff body suddenly relaxed.

Then, anger surged across her face.

"Fellow Daoist Luo, killing just one demon cannot vent the hatred in my heart. I wish to assist the Nether Origin cultivators in pursuing these fiends. You…"

Luo Chen shook his head, his body rapidly shrinking.

"You go ahead. I won't join in. Since reinforcements have arrived, our mission is mostly complete. What follows is merely perfecting the outcome."

Returning to his normal state, Luo Chen was still clad in a white robe, spotless, as if this brief and extremely dangerous battle had caused him no harm at all.

Splendid Radiance Fairy took a deep look at Luo Chen, clasped her fists, then transformed into a beam of cyan light, chasing after the enemy.

After they left.

Luo Chen slowly descended to the ground.

Black King swayed his massive body and swam over.

"Master, that fellow is a bit strange. He seems like a ghostly thing, yet somewhat like a desolate beast."

"Mm."

Luo Chen hummed softly. In that brief moment of contact just now, he too had sensed that the true body of the one called "Gao Chang" was quite bizarre.

But he hadn't sensed it as deeply as Black King.

His footsteps stopped before a corpse with a gaping chest. Luo Chen sighed faintly, his expression complex.

Sage Golden Plume had fallen here!

From their very first meeting, Luo Chen had never given this man a good face.

Not only because of Old Thief Shen Yuan's schemes against him, but also because Duan Feng and his wife, whom he had befriended in his youth, had fallen in the Two Instruments Tin Forest support battle in the Boundless Domain due to this man's orders.

The reason Golden Plume had specifically transferred Duan Feng there was merely because Duan Feng was skilled at identifying ores.

For the sake of his friends, Luo Chen could never get along amicably with this man.

Let alone turn an enemy into a friend like Pavilion Master Li or Lin Qingxuan.

But later, after fighting side by side with Golden Plume several times, the formidable strength he displayed had, to some extent, earned Luo Chen's recognition.

This was no weakling!

Even in today's battle, facing Thousand Rope Demon Emperor, whose fighting style somewhat countered his own, Golden Plume had suppressed the demon emperor from start to finish.

A pity…

"A pity he didn't die by my hand!"

Luo Chen waved his hand, and the two remaining golden feathers fell into his grasp.

Then he sent a command to Black King, who opened his mouth and swallowed Golden Plume's corpse into his belly.

After doing all this.

A beam of light flew out from the shattered Heaven Pillar Mountain and arrived before Luo Chen.

"Alchemy Lord!"

Luo Chen looked at the slightly pale middle-aged man and made a bowing salute. "It is I!"

Heaven Pivot looked at the giant dragon behind him, a hesitant expression on his face.

Luo Chen smiled. "This is my spirit pet. No need to worry."

Heaven Pivot shook his head. "That's not what I meant. I wanted to ask about Fellow Daoist Golden Plume's corpse…"

Luo Chen's smile faded. "You don't need to worry about that. To the outside world, just say he died at the hands of the demon emperor, his body and bones utterly destroyed."

Heaven Pivot was stunned. With so many people watching, would anyone believe that?

Luo Chen didn't want to discuss this matter further. Suddenly, he said, "I have an impertinent request. I would like to see the remains of your sect's three fellow daoists. May I?"

Heaven Pivot frowned, instinctively wanting to refuse.

But considering Luo Chen's assistance to Heavenly Capital Sect today, and the fact that he hadn't fled even when at an absolute disadvantage before the Nether Origin Sect reinforcements arrived, he ultimately agreed.

Before long, Luo Chen appeared in an empty hall.

The remains of the three fallen Sage elders of Heavenly Capital Sect were laid out here.

Unlike the previous people, these three had no wounds piercing through their chests and abdomens.

"How did they die?"

"Ambushed. When we released our nascent souls to reinforce the complete Eight Trigrams Diagram, he suddenly appeared and devoured the nascent souls of three junior brothers. If I hadn't reacted quickly and withdrawn my nascent soul back into my Purple Mansion, I would have fallen to his hand as well."

"Didn't he launch a follow-up attack on you?"

"No time. I activated the grand formation and briefly teleported him out of Heaven Pillar Mountain."

…

Luo Chen did not stay long at Heavenly Capital Sect. After inquiring about the specific circumstances at the time, he had Black King carry him as they slowly flew toward Lingtian Pass.

On the dragon's back, Luo Chen sat cross-legged.

A Great Soul Pill in his mouth, he healed the soul injuries caused by the illusion technique's backlash.

The pitted Mystic Dust Armor on his body also began to slowly recover under the expulsion of dharma power.

When night fell, Luo Chen finally woke up.

In this battle, aside from a slight backlash to his soul, he had actually suffered no real injuries.

With the Great Soul Pill, that minor injury was nothing to fear.

If not for Gao Chang's sudden ambush, Luo Chen, having broken out of the formation, would have been fully capable of killing the three Lu brothers without injury.

"Gao Chang…"

Sitting cross-legged on the dragon's back, Luo Chen slowly spat out this name.

"Desolate beast, ghostly thing!"

"Ignores protective shields and qi armor, unafraid of physical treasure attacks."

"Extremely fast, can break through a cultivator's Purple Mansion barrier, feeds on nascent souls."

"Countermeasure… a powerful physical body—no, a powerful physical body possessing desolate beast source power—and thunder power specifically designed to counter yin ghosts!"

The final point of summary was deduced from Gao Chang's terrified reaction when Thunder Daozi appeared.

Thunder Daozi Zhang Che had been famous for a long time, but what truly elevated him to the pinnacle of Eastern Wilderness prodigies was his comprehension of the true meaning of the thunder dao.

From his actions today, even Splendid Brocade Immortal dared not clash head-on with him.

His reputation was by no means undeserved!

"But he had clearly arrived long ago, yet he watched our bitter struggle, allowing four Sage Sages to die in battle on the spot."

"His intent deserves death!"

Luo Chen's eyes narrowed slightly, his murmuring voice filled with cold harshness.
