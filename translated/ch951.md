# Chapter 951: Bottleneck Loosens, Storms Rise Again

In the quiet cave dwelling.

Luo Chen stood before his desk, meticulously studying the Hundred Poison King Gu sealed within that amber.

Because of the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu incident back then, Luo Chen was quite cautious when it came to gu insects, afraid of once again encountering one that had been tampered with.

Just like when he had previously refined the Strength Gu and the Message Gu, he had only chosen to refine them after being extremely careful.

Compared to low-grade gu insects like the Strength Gu and Message Gu, this Hundred Poison King Gu was of a slightly higher grade, a full third-tier, making the inspection process very troublesome.

Fortunately, as Luo Chen's cultivation realm grew deeper, he increasingly understood one principle.

Refinement paths were not separate!

Whether it was alchemy, artifact forging, or even gu refinement, they all shared some common principles.

Leaving a hidden hand in pills, magical treasures, or even gu insects was not that simple.

One needed sufficient understanding of the corresponding object and a method to restrain it before one could leave a backup plan.

At least, from his inspection so far, this Hundred Poison King Gu was clean and pure, with no hidden dangers visible.

"Right, it's just a third-tier gu insect. There's no need for a top Southern Frontier gu refiner to resort to such dirty tricks."

Luo Chen laughed at his own caution, then shook his head.

The other party had used just a third-tier poison gu and a matching cultivation technique to exchange for a single move from him, the famous "Overlord Cultivator." They had truly gotten a great deal!

Of course, Luo Chen wasn't overly angry either.

It was true that the Hundred Poison King Gu was only third-tier, but its future potential was considerable.

Once this gu was exposed to various poisons, its grade would rapidly increase, with fourth-tier being the ideal state.

In fact, under Li Tian Master's wild imagination, if the host could withstand it and there was enough powerful poison support, this Hundred Poison King Gu might even have a chance to advance to fifth-tier one day.

From this, one could also see that this was an experimental path Li Tian Master had taken for refining a fifth-tier divine gu.

Not refining it directly, but relying on the cultivator's acquired efforts to cultivate it, step by step, pushing it upward.

The reason Li Tian Master later abandoned the Hundred Poison King Gu was simple.

The two conditions required were far too rare for anyone in this world to satisfy!

"Can I withstand it?"

Luo Chen asked himself, then smiled knowingly.

If even he couldn't withstand it, how many people in this world were qualified?

Azure Frost? Wang Yuan?

Neither of them would choose this kind of "deviant path." Even if the king gu and the technique were placed before them, they would probably just dismiss them with contempt.

Only someone like himself, who had hit a bottleneck in body refinement, would choose to try this as a last resort.

"Let me see what use you really have!"

With a thought, a slight stinging sensation came from Luo Chen's fingertip.

A drop of essence blood, exuding a powerful aura, was squeezed out.

Wobbling slightly, a sound was heard—

*Drip!*

The essence blood fell onto the amber, and the amber sealing the Hundred Poison King Gu instantly began to melt like snow.

Stimulated, the Hundred Poison King Gu gradually began to awaken.

Before it could make any move, Luo Chen's essence blood had already touched its body.

"Hiss..."

A soundless sharp cry echoed in his heart.

In his sight, the plump white king gu began to roll violently, flashes of blood appearing on its body as it struggled continuously.

When the blood color gradually faded, Luo Chen knew the first stage of refinement was successful.

Next...

Without hesitation, Luo Chen pointed two fingers together and slashed across his upper abdomen.

A fierce wound appeared, and he grabbed the Hundred Poison King Gu, stuffing it directly inside.

Under his powerful self-healing ability, the fierce wound began to heal rapidly.

And in his perception, the plump gu insect, once inside his body, headed straight for his stomach and internal organs.

"Fortunately, I've consumed a large amount of Five Temple Immortal Brew over the years, cultivating my five organs to a rare and powerful state. Otherwise, I wouldn't be able to provide a stable home for this Hundred Poison King Gu."

Muttering to himself, within Luo Chen's Purple Mansion, light also swirled on the brow of his Nascent Soul little man.

It was the Illumination Spirit Gu he had refined earlier!

To prevent any unexpected changes Luo Chen couldn't predict, the Illumination Spirit Gu needed to be activated at all times, monitoring this Hundred Poison King Gu.

So far, everything was going smoothly.

Once the Hundred Poison King Gu entered his stomach, the feeling of discomfort instantly became like a fish in water when it encountered Luo Chen's terrifying stomach acid, which seemed capable of dissolving everything.

And a feeling of hunger, connected through their spiritual bond, was transmitted to Luo Chen's consciousness.

"Impatient already?"

Luo Chen smiled slightly, then took a black jar from his storage bag.

Opening the jar, a small green snake was slowly swimming inside, flicking its tongue.

This was a Green Scale Snake, a first-tier demon beast, containing deadly poison.

Any Qi Refinement cultivator who accidentally came into contact with it would die of heart paralysis.

And this was also one of the most basic hundred first-tier poisons required for the introductory practice of the *Hundred Poisons Body Refinement Art*.

One hand was placed before the small green snake.

The Green Scale Snake didn't hesitate, biting down immediately.

But with one bite, it couldn't even leave a white mark on Luo Chen's hand.

Luo Chen shook his head. "Too troublesome."

He pinched with two fingers, and the Green Scale Snake's venom gland was removed.

Instantly, the Green Scale Snake became listless.

And Luo Chen, facing this poison that countless Qi Refinement cultivators would avoid at all costs, swallowed it with a light laugh.

The poison followed his esophagus down into his stomach.

The Hundred Poison King Gu was attracted, continuously absorbing it.

Its plump white body gradually turned pitch black.

But this change was very brief, quickly returning to a jade-like white.

Seeing this, Luo Chen began to circulate the *Hundred Poisons Body Refinement Art*.

As the technique flowed, the spiritually connected Hundred Poison King Gu also reacted accordingly.

A hazy mist emanated from its body, gradually eroding Luo Chen's flesh and blood.

A tingling, numbing, unbearably itchy sensation arose.

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow, allowing the painful, itchy feeling to spread throughout his body, remaining as immovable as a mountain!

Gradually, under the erosion of the poison, Luo Chen's flesh and blood seemed to wither slightly.

But his powerful physical self-healing ability instantly restored it.

The painful, itchy sensation also disappeared in a flash.

Seeing this, Luo Chen fell into deep thought.

After consulting the Illumination Spirit Gu's memories once more, Luo Chen gradually came to an understanding.

"I see!"

No wonder this Hundred Poison King Gu could become the core of this high-grade body refinement technique, and even the *Hundred Poisons Body Refinement Art* was specifically created to match it.

Its very nature was extraordinary!

It was a top-tier poison, but its toxicity was extremely internalized, causing no harm to the cultivator.

However, after absorbing other poisons, that toxicity would fluctuate.

At this moment, circulating the *Hundred Poisons Body Refinement Art* to stimulate the king gu would cause the poison it had swallowed to be released many times over, thereby tempering the host's own flesh.

The Hundred Poison King Gu could be seen as an intermediary, or as a converter.

To give a real example...

Luo Chen thought for a moment, then recalled a technological product from his previous life—a transformer.

Just as a transformer could step voltage up or down, the Hundred Poison King Gu could amplify or reduce the toxicity of the poisons fed to it.

After feeding it back to the host, it could temper the physical body.

And this tempering was also different from the normal meaning of tempering.

It was a slow erosion, a continuous dissolution.

If the host's own flesh wasn't strong enough, this erosion and dissolution would instead harm them.

Only with a sufficiently powerful physical body could one, in the process of continuous erosion, dissolution, and recovery, gradually become stronger!

"No wonder Li Tian Master didn't think much of this high-grade body refinement technique. Its principle is so simple. Even without the *Hundred Poisons Body Refinement Art*, as long as I have this king gu, I could create matching thousand-poison, ten-thousand-poison body refinement methods myself."

After this examination, Luo Chen understood many things.

If one wanted this technique to improve quickly, three conditions were needed.

First, the host's own physical strength; second, the degree to which the technique stimulated the Hundred Poison King Gu; third, the supply of external poisons.

"These aren't difficult!"

Smacking his lips, another wave of hunger came from within his body.

Luo Chen smiled lightly, took out a large number of low-grade poisons from his storage bag, extracted their toxins, and fed them to the Hundred Poison King Gu.

At the same time, the already mastered *Hundred Poisons Body Refinement Art*, under the control of his main soul, naturally began to circulate.

A vast amount of dense poisonous mist, centered on his stomach, began to spread throughout Luo Chen's entire body.

...

After joining the Fierce Peak Corpse Alliance, Luo Chen's life of rushing around suddenly stabilized again.

Every day, he was either cultivating the *Hundred Poisons Body Refinement Art* in his cave dwelling, or wandering up and down Mount Mi Realm, ignoring the gazes of others, studying the ubiquitous natural spatial restrictions.

During this period, the matching poison resources that Li Tian Master had promised were also sent over one after another.

Of course, these resources were not free; Luo Chen had to pay a certain price to buy them.

Fortunately, before leaving the Eastern Wilderness, Luo Chen had gained a lot from the Five Elements Divine Sect and also obtained some benefits from the Shadow Image Sect, so paying for these low-grade poisons was more than enough.

With the supply of a large number of low-grade poisons, coupled with Luo Chen's terrifying physical self-healing ability, his proficiency in the *Hundred Poisons Body Refinement Art* increased rapidly, changing almost daily.

From beginner to proficient took only a month or so.

From proficient to master level was just around the corner.

After the Hundred Poison King Gu had swallowed a large amount of poison, its own grade was also gradually changing.

The only thing that made Luo Chen a little dissatisfied was that although his body refinement bottleneck had loosened and he was beginning to advance toward the late fourth-tier, the improvement was quite limited.

If an ordinary person obtained this method, under such terrifying poison stimulation, their body refinement skill would surely advance by leaps and bounds.

But his physical foundation was simply too strong. It was a existence that could contend with great demons and rival desolate beasts. The physical enhancement from this constant destruction and reconstruction was just icing on the cake.

He understood that, in the end, the Hundred Poison King Gu's own grade was too low.

Only by raising it to the fourth-tier level could it truly benefit Luo Chen.

First- and second-tier poisons clearly couldn't raise the Hundred Poison King Gu's grade.

Third-tier poisons might be effective, but poisons of this level were already considered high-grade resources, and their quantity was quite limited.

If one wanted immediate results, perhaps only fourth-tier poisons, which could threaten Sage cultivators, could cause a qualitative change in the Hundred Poison King Gu!

But poisons of this level were far too rare in the world.

Luo Chen couldn't rush it; he could only slowly grind away at it.

Icing on the cake was still an improvement; at least he had found a direction to move forward.

And during this process, Luo Chen's understanding of natural spatial restrictions became deeper and deeper.

He even used the materials on hand to privately craft some spatial treasures to verify his learning results.

In a mountain stream shrouded in clouds and mist, Luo Chen looked at the storage bag in his hand.

This was a spatial treasure he had refined himself. The materials weren't particularly expensive; the exquisite part was the technique.

"No wonder the price of storage bags has always remained high."

"What's expensive is never the raw materials, but the craftsmanship of refining a storage bag!"

Luo Chen sighed with emotion, then suddenly struck!

A squeeze!

*Bang!*

The storage bag in his hand, which contained scattered spirit stones and worthless magical artifacts, suddenly shattered.

Luo Chen's eyes widened as he stared intently at the place where the storage bag had broken.

A storage bag has its own space, covered with restrictions. Once it breaks, it connects with the external space.

In the past, Luo Chen had seen this situation many times.

After all, in battle, it was not uncommon to accidentally smash an enemy's storage bag.

At that time, he hadn't paid close attention to the specifics, only occasionally noticing after the battle, when checking the spoils, that some things were missing.

Now, looking closely, he found some strange phenomena.

In the process of the internal space shattering and colliding with the external space, a large number of spatial restrictions, like ripples, continuously collapsed and regenerated in the void.

Of the various items hidden in the internal space, some were cut into powder by the terrifying spatial restrictions, some were fortunately preserved, and another part simply disappeared strangely into the void, their whereabouts unknown.

"Where did they go?"

Muttering to himself, Luo Chen swept his powerful divine sense across the empty mountain stream, but found nothing.

Inside that storage bag just now, besides the worthless magical artifacts, he had deliberately left something of value.

A green lamp!

Obtained from the Shadow Image Sect, it was something Black King had secretly collected for him.

In terms of grade, it should be at the magical treasure level.

Luo Chen was too lazy to study its effects, but he had left his divine sense mark on it, specifically to test the results.

But now, no matter how powerful his divine sense was, he couldn't find that "Broken Form Green Lamp."

Frowning, Luo Chen shifted his focus to the changes in the spatial restrictions from before.

"A Sage's ability to shrink the earth into an inch and teleport thousands of miles is actually an instinctive use of spatial restrictions, but it's a natural act of conforming to spatial changes."

"Azure Frost, on the other hand, defies common sense, traversing the void with her physical body, appearing and disappearing mysteriously. Clearly, this is a method of defying spatial changes..."

"Breaking, reorganizing, finding gaps in the brief process of spatial restrictions recovering, one can freely enter and exit."

"This heaven and earth is like a tightly woven net. Sages are like small shrimp, able to freely shuttle through the mesh. The reason my Primordial Unity Cauldron can suppress Sages is because it has its own space, blocking many of the shuttle meshes. And Azure Frost, that big fish, destroys the nodes of the net, then traverses with her physical body, reaching a level where the net is broken but the fish doesn't die."

"Is that it?"

Luo Chen's expression was dazed as he muttered to himself.

No one gave him an answer; he could only rely on his subsequent research to verify it himself.

From time to time, a painful, itchy sensation came from his body, but Luo Chen suppressed it with his main soul, not letting it affect his focus.

Ending today's research, Luo Chen returned to his cave dwelling with a heart full of doubts.

Just as he arrived, he saw Purple Marquis waiting outside the cave.

Luo Chen was startled, then quickly put away his thoughts and took the initiative to greet him.

"Fellow Daoist, sorry to keep you waiting."

Purple Marquis waved his hand carelessly. "No problem. You know, those of us who have been branded with the Fierce Peak Seal have a much lower cultivation efficiency than before. Rather than doing that futile work, I might as well come out for a walk."

Luo Chen smiled. This was probably the pain of most Southern Frontier Sage cultivators now!

Clearly, the world's spiritual energy had increased, but they dared not cultivate recklessly.

To lift this restriction, one had to find the source to restore the days of freedom.

After laughing, he asked, "Is there any important business today?"

"No important business." Purple Marquis shook his head, then took out a jade slip. "Here, this is the information you wanted."

A look of joy appeared on Luo Chen's face. "Is it about the Devil Heart Gu Emperor?"

"Part of it, but not all. It also includes some changes in the major events of the Mountains and Seas Realm collected by the Fierce Peak Corpse Alliance from various channels."

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow and took the jade slip.

The first thing he saw made his mind focus.

"The Eastern Wilderness... has finally started fighting!"
