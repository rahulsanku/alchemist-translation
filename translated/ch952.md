# Chapter 952: Unlucky Nine Spirit Lord, Extreme Luo Chen

The Eastern Wilderness was once again embroiled in war!

This was an expected development, not a surprising one.

After all, the demon race had been amassing troops in the six easternmost domains for many years. Under prolonged confrontation, friction was constant, and it was only a matter of time before things spiraled out of control.

What surprised Luo Chen was the trigger for the war's resurgence.

[The Great Demon Azure Frost, violent and lawless, destroyed the Five Elements Divine Sect, slaughtered thousands of cultivators, and treated human experts as nothing. The Tianyuan Dao Sect, bearing the will of the people, raised an army to punish them.]

"The Five Elements Divine Sect was destroyed by Azure Frost Demon Emperor?"

When he saw this line, Luo Chen felt a sense of unreality.

It was like when he was in the Northern Sea and saw the intelligence report that Azure Frost Demon Emperor had destroyed the Joyous Union Sect.

But times were different now. He was certain that the Five Elements Divine Sect had been destroyed by himself and Divine Origin.

Beside him, Purpleclad Marquis let out a snort of derision. "Just take a look and move on. Although the Stiff Alliance has plenty of intelligence, we still have to judge its authenticity ourselves. For instance, regarding the destruction of the Five Elements Divine Sect, according to reliable information from my Purple Blood Sect, it wasn't Azure Frost's doing at all, but rather caused by an Eastern Wilderness top-tier expert named Luo Chen."

"Eastern Wilderness top-tier expert, Luo Chen?" Luo Chen asked hesitantly.

Purpleclad Marquis nodded. "Yes, he's an alchemy dao grandmaster on par with Li Tian Master. Rumor has it he had some grudge with the Five Elements Divine Sect and suddenly appeared after disappearing for many years to annihilate them."

Hearing his own legend from someone else's mouth, Luo Chen felt quite uncomfortable for a moment.

Both embarrassed and proud.

Purpleclad Marquis continued, "But although the deed was done by Alchemy Sect's Luo Chen, few actually witnessed his actions. The Five Elements Divine Sect was a top-tier Sage sect of the Eastern Wilderness. Such a massive force, gone in an instant, needed some explanation to the world. The Tianyuan Dao Sect had just entered the Eastern Wilderness and was looking for an excuse to kill the chicken to scare the monkeys and establish their authority. They simply pinned the blame on the demon race, stirring up public anger to launch an all-out war against the Eastern Wilderness demons!"

Public anger?

There should indeed be anger.

Nearly two hundred years of the Eastern Wilderness human-demon war had destroyed countless sects and families.

They had barely settled down, and now the demons were marching in again. Who wouldn't be afraid? Who wouldn't be angry?

And Azure Frost Demon Emperor had a history of such ruthless acts—penetrating deep into human territory, destroying sects and exterminating clans.

Since the Tianyuan Dao Sect said so, the major and minor forces of the Eastern Wilderness naturally believed it.

With Azure Frost's cold and aloof nature, she probably wouldn't bother explaining much to others. But she wasn't the type to just endure and let things slide. This matter probably wouldn't be resolved so easily!

Luo Chen pursed his lips and asked about what happened next.

Purpleclad Marquis shrugged. "What else could happen? They just had to fight it out. As far as I know, facing the dirty water splashed on her by the Tianyuan Dao Sect, Azure Frost Demon Emperor didn't bother defending herself, but her actions were extremely ruthless. Not only did she thoroughly defeat the newly emerged Nine Spirit Lord, but she also chased him for over a hundred thousand li, killed the Dongyang Sect Sage, severely wounded Tianyuan experts, and destroyed branch divisions of the Divine Talisman, Dongyang, and Void Emperor sects."

Speaking of the latter, he pointed at the jade slip.

"This matter is also recorded here. You can take a closer look."

"Mm."

Luo Chen hummed in acknowledgment and quickly scanned the subsequent text on the slip.

Sure enough, regarding these overt intelligence matters, the jade slip recorded them in considerable detail.

After scanning through, Luo Chen could only sigh. This was probably exactly the kind of thing that woman Azure Frost would do.

You splash dirty water on me? I won't refute it.

But if I actually make it real, don't come crying.

You say I destroy sects and exterminate clans? Then I'll really destroy sects and exterminate clans for you to see.

It was just that his good friend Nine Spirit Lord was a bit unlucky.

After the Medicine King Domain great battle, he had gone into seclusion to heal his injuries.

He had finally recovered, and even turned misfortune into fortune, truly advancing to the late Sage stage, becoming a genuine Grand Cultivator. Yet, upon emerging from seclusion full of ambition and satisfaction, he ran into the most unreasonable peak expert in this realm.

He was probably quite helpless too!

"But as long as he's alive and well, and not seriously injured, that's fine."

The intelligence didn't record that Nine Spirit Lord was severely wounded, only that he was defeated by Azure Frost Demon Emperor.

Setting this aside, Luo Chen sank into deeper contemplation.

The resurgence of war was within reason.

But the party initiating the war was unexpected.

It was actually the Tianyuan Dao Sect?

Shouldn't it be the Green Parasol Mountain side?

What Rosycloud had done to the Nether Origin Sect was enough to make the remaining holy lands of the Mountains and Seas Realm vigilant.

That was a shocking feat—one person flattening an entire holy land!

Wasn't the Tianyuan Dao Sect afraid of repeating that disaster?

Or were they testing?

Testing whether Rosycloud still had the strength to make similar moves after the Nether Origin great battle?

Testing whether Rosycloud could still act under the suppression of heavenly tribulation?

Putting himself in the position of a holy land-level force, Luo Chen vaguely understood the Tianyuan Dao Sect's thinking.

As the foremost existence in this realm, discovering that there were beings capable of threatening them—how could they rest easy?

A simple probe or two, then follow-up actions.

Moreover, this probe wasn't very dangerous.

The battlefield was in the Eastern Wilderness, the manpower and resources used were also local Eastern Wilderness cultivators. It was like playing chess—the board was set on another chessboard. Even if Rosycloud struck out in fury and disrupted the situation, the Tianyuan Dao Sect could slip away in the chaos and watch from the sidelines.

The fear was that they underestimated Rosycloud's ruthlessness!

Luo Chen sighed inwardly and shifted his attention to the later intelligence.

The Eastern Wilderness intelligence was only part of it.

Later records also included many major events concerning the Northern Sea, Western Desert, and even the Southern Frontier itself.

And from these major events, Luo Chen was shocked to find the Tianyuan Dao Sect's figure appearing frequently.

He couldn't help but exclaim, "The Tianyuan Dao Sect is striking out on all fronts, spreading their forces thin. Isn't this a major taboo in military affairs?"

Purpleclad Marquis, however, had a different view, speaking with admiration, "Isn't this the grand ambition to swallow the world? Moreover, they have the strength, and they've encountered an unprecedented golden opportunity!"

Luo Chen opened his mouth but ultimately had nothing to say.

Because what Purpleclad Marquis said wasn't wrong.

The Primordial Demon and Nether Origin sects had been destroyed, the Northern Sea and Eastern Wilderness were without masters, and the Hanging Temple and Life and Death Gate had successively closed their mountain gates, leaving the Western Desert and Southern Frontier leaderless.

Under these circumstances, if the Tianyuan Dao Sect could seize the opportunity, even if they suffered defeats in one or two theaters, their overall gains would still exceed imagination.

Most importantly, the chess pieces had been placed!

Luo Chen shook his head, no longer concerning himself with the grand trends of the world, and flipped to the parts related to him.

"The Devil Heart Gu Emperor has disappeared for centuries, rarely showing his face. The world rumored that he died from his demon gu backlashing on him."

"If he is not dead, given the heaven-defying effect of the Devil Heart Gu in accelerating cultivation, after centuries, the Devil Heart Gu Emperor must have advanced to late Sage, ranking among the Grand Cultivators."

"For such an expert to continue cultivating, he must have a matching high-grade spirit vein."

"The number of high-grade spirit veins in the Southern Frontier has been explored over thousands of years, and all have owners. Therefore, if the Devil Heart Gu Emperor still lives, he must be the master of one of these spirit veins."

"The Southern Frontier Grand Cultivators occupying high-grade spirit veins, excluding the hidden experts of the Life and Death Gate, are as follows: Songyang Sage, Redleaf Matron, Burial Life Elder, Luo Sect Supreme, Xuanji Fairy..."

Li Tian Master's method of searching for traces of the Devil Heart Gu Emperor for Luo Chen was actually very simple.

It was just elimination!

Just as mortals need to drink water and eat to live, if a Sage cultivator wants to attain the Ascendant Great Dao, they need high-grade spirit veins matching their own realm to assist.

If the Devil Heart Gu Emperor didn't want to delay his cultivation, he would inevitably be mixed in among them.

Thus, the scope of suspects was greatly narrowed.

And this was Luo Chen's first intuitive understanding of the Southern Frontier Grand Cultivator group.

"Not weaker than the various sects of the Eastern Wilderness at their peak!"

Or rather, not just not weaker, but surpassing them.

At the peak of the Eastern Wilderness, the number of Grand Cultivators on the surface was five: the Dongyang Sect Supreme, Heavenly Capital Sect's Heavenly Capital Son, Tianji Zen Sect's Monk Huiping, the Joyous Union Sect Ancestor, and the Five Elements Divine Sect's Divine Origin Sage.

But now, the Southern Frontier had as many as eight Grand Cultivators!

Just the Fierce Corpse Stiff Alliance side had gathered four of them.

From this, it could be seen that the Southern Frontier's reliance on gu insects to assist cultivation did have some remarkable effects.

But there was also an awkward point: in the last two to three hundred years, no new late-stage Sage Grand Cultivators had emerged for a long time.

Unlike the Northern Sea, which produced Moon Wanderer, and the Eastern Wilderness, which produced Nine Spirit Lord and other newly advanced Grand Cultivators.

"No wonder the Southern Frontier cultivators blame the changes in heaven and earth here."

Luo Chen found an excuse for them, then, based on the intelligence given by Li Tian Master, focused his attention on these seven or eight people, beginning to analyze carefully which one might be the Devil Heart Gu Emperor.

First, he eliminated Redleaf Matron and Xuanji Fairy.

Because the rumors said the Devil Heart Gu Emperor was a male cultivator.

Of the remaining six, Songyang Sage could also be directly eliminated, because the Songshan Sect's inheritance had never been interrupted, and Songyang Sage had been famous since youth—there was no possibility of someone impersonating him.

"The remaining five..."

Luo Chen's eyes narrowed slightly.

He had to admit, Ming Lei Cave Master's suspicion before leaving had some basis.

Burial Life Elder was indeed very suspicious. Burial Life Valley had too little contact with the outside world, and Burial Life Elder's style of conduct was quite mysterious and strange.

The Luo Sect Supreme was also within the scope of suspicion. Since the Luo Sect internal strife back then, the Luo Sect Supreme had been in seclusion, never showing any trace.

Rumor had it he was injured?

But what kind of injury would require centuries to heal?

Even now, with the Southern Frontier cultivators attacking the Life and Death Gate in such a major event, he was indifferent and never showed his face.

By his ear, Purpleclad Marquis's puzzled voice sounded.

"Speaking of which, Fellow Daoist Qi is so enthusiastic about searching for the Devil Heart Gu Emperor. What is it for?"

Luo Chen replied with a natural expression, "Just looking for another path."

Purpleclad Marquis was confused.

Luo Chen pointed at his own body. "Although I have considerable body refinement attainments, the path of body refinement has long declined, and the road ahead is unclear. Moreover, a body refiner's lifespan is far shorter than a Sage's. If I don't want to regretfully sit in meditation when my great limit arrives, I must find a path to re-cultivate."

Purpleclad Marquis hesitated. "You want to use the Devil Heart Gu to switch to the path of qi refinement?"

"Exactly!" Luo Chen said calmly. "The principle of the Devil Heart Gu accelerating cultivation is orthodox essence refinement into qi. I possess a vast essence that no one can match, which will not dissipate even in death. If I have a high-grade Devil Heart Gu to assist me, when body refinement can advance no further, I can forcibly convert my massive essence to assault the Sage realm, thus gaining a few more hundred years of life."

"This, this..." Purpleclad Marquis hadn't expected such an answer. It was both reasonable and shocking.

Who, having achieved success in body refinement, would think of making such an extreme conversion to another path?

Even if some Sages dabbled in body refinement techniques, it was only as a side practice, not an extreme conversion.

Similarly, for Fellow Daoist Qi to have such a life-or-death idea was shocking yet admirable.

"Quite the bold vision!"

Purpleclad Marquis sighed in admiration, then advised, "Now that you've obtained the *Hundred Poisons Body Refinement Art*, your path ahead may not be hopeless. The Devil Heart Gu is too convenient and easily causes one to lose their mind. Unless absolutely necessary, I still advise you to cherish your current cultivation."

Luo Chen hummed in acknowledgment. "I understand, Fellow Daoist Qi."

...

"Is that what he said?"

"Absolutely true, and his demeanor didn't seem feigned."

"I didn't expect the domineering Qi Tian to have such meticulous foresight!"

In a gu-refining room thick with dry and irritable qi, Li Tian Master turned around from a miniature pavilion.

Purpleclad Marquis looked at the rather familiar small pavilion and said, "Fear of death is human nature. But being willing to abandon one's current path and make such an extreme conversion to another is still admirable."

Li Tian Master chuckled. "Then I still hope that day doesn't come. After all, to enter the Yin-Yang Remnant Realm, we still need to rely on Qi Tian's body refinement skills."

As he spoke, he waved his hand behind him.

Instantly, several streams of light flew out from the small pavilion.

"Take these to him. I suspect it's because his progress with the *Hundred Poisons Body Refinement Art* hasn't been great that his thoughts have become so extreme."

"What are these?" Purpleclad Marquis looked curiously at the several sealed gu insects.

Li Tian Master explained, "These are three types of fourth-rank poison gu that I've treasured. If Qi Tian can refine and feed them to the Hundred Poisons King Gu, it should be enough to advance it to the fourth rank. Thus, his body refinement path can be further elevated."

"This is quite a generous gesture from you, Elder!" Purpleclad Marquis took the three gu insects with some envy.

Li Tian Master sighed emotionally. "The great matter is imminent. The Yin-Yang Remnant Realm not only contains the gu formulas I need but also involves how to open the Life and Death Gate. I cannot afford to be negligent!"

Purpleclad Marquis nodded. "Then does Qi Tian need to provide corresponding resources in return?"

"He's a man who knows his limits. As long as it's roughly equivalent, just accept it on my behalf."

Purpleclad Marquis suddenly asked again, "Regarding the Blood Transformation Gu I need, how is your progress?"

Li Tian Master seemed to have anticipated this and answered methodically, "Based on the materials you provided and the Blood Sea lineage techniques of the Northern Sea Primordial Demon Sect, I've roughly developed the gu formula. After entering the Yin-Yang Remnant Realm, I should be able to refine the Blood Transformation Gu for you."

Purpleclad Marquis immediately felt relieved.

With that Blood Transformation Gu, perhaps he could emulate the experts of the Primordial Demon Sect's Blood Sea lineage and refine his own Blood God Child.

Moreover, he could use this method to evade the changes in heaven and earth and accumulate cultivation to reach late Sage.

Before leaving, he couldn't help but look back at the small pavilion Li Tian Master was operating.

He muttered to himself.

"How is it so similar to the Wind-Wave Pavilion at the entrance of the Mi Jie Mountain?"
