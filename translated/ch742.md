# Chapter 742: The Seven Elders Decide; Temporary Guest Elder

"My name is Chu Yan. The cultivators of the Mountains and Seas Realm honor me as the Alchemy Saint."

Even after Luo Chen withdrew from that gate, that warm and gentle sentence still echoed in his mind like spring wind and fine rain.

No wonder he had felt close to her the moment he first saw her.

They were both from the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world, both alchemists, and had even both once accomplished the astonishing feat of refining a fifth-tier pill at the Sage realm.

When all these similarities merged together, that subconscious closeness was born.

Only, some aspects were beyond expectation.

In Luo Chen's imagination, the Alchemy Saint should have been an old cultivator with a flowing white beard and an immortal bearing. Yet judging from that illusory figure, she was clearly a frail, slender young woman in the bloom of youth.

Of course, from the moment she abruptly asked, "Can you refine fifth-tier pills?" Luo Chen could not possibly regard her as any weak woman.

This was an Ascendant great power who was about to rise on rosy clouds and ascend!

She had even faintly perceived that he had once refined a fifth-tier pill, which was why she asked that question.

Luo Chen naturally would not publicize the matter of the fifth-tier Law-Derivation Pill, so he muddled through.

The questioning inside Star Gate had become less important from the moment he saw Chu Yan, the Alchemy Saint. It had only been a matter of repeating the phrasing Fu Chaosheng had instructed him in beforehand.

With a calm face, Luo Chen left Star Gate and returned to Welcoming Immortals Hall.

Fu Chaosheng, who had waited for a long time, immediately came out expectantly to greet him, and also brought over a true "young woman in the bloom of youth."

"This is my granddaughter, Fu Qinglan. Qinglan, why have you not greeted Fellow Daoist Luo?"

Looking at the young Luo Chen, three parts hesitation appeared on Fu Qinglan's fair cheeks.

Luo Chen, however, saw through her awkwardness.

It was merely the issue of seniority in the cultivation world.

He interacted with her grandfather as an equal, but in terms of realm, he and Fu Qinglan were roughly equivalent. This made forms of address rather difficult.

"Let us each go by our own reckoning. Fellow Daoist Fu... Little Fellow Daoist Fu?"

When the words reached his lips, Luo Chen also felt a little awkward.

Fu Qinglan pressed her lips together and smiled. "Those who have attained are first. I will call you Dao Brother. Dao Brother Luo should not call me Little Fellow Daoist Fu. Just call me Qinglan. My friends all call me that."

Because of this smile, the awkward atmosphere in the room dispersed at once.

Fu Chaosheng was skilled at adjusting the mood. Just now, he had deliberately watched from the wall to give the two of them some time to interact. Seeing that the conversation had now opened, he naturally would not remain aloof. He warmly invited Luo Chen to the prepared banquet, while asking him about the Star Gate Seven Elders' assessment.

...

In that space where starlight flickered bright and dim, the seven illusory figures began discussing after Luo Chen left.

"A Northern Sea cultivator who calls himself a rogue cultivator and has no sect inheritance. Yet his magic power is majestic and upright, and his hidden soul foundation far surpasses ordinary first-level Sages. This man's background is unclear. Xiang believes he should not pass!"

This was the representative of the Thirty-Six Aristocratic Clans, the Xiang family grand elder.

He opened his golden mouth first, seemingly intending to set the tone.

But very soon, the others refuted him.

"Star Gate has always chosen talent without regard for class. It can even contain demon cultivators, let alone a rogue cultivator. Every person has secrets. We were able to become late-stage Sage great cultivators, so who among us has not had a few fortuitous encounters? Must we dig to the roots of everything?"

"Indeed. If Star Gate wishes to grow, it must uphold the principle of embracing all rivers and swallowing the four continents like a whale. From the moment Wilderness Wanderer entered Star Gate, the fire-attribute spiritual qi in this space became extremely active. He seems to possess a Fire Spirit Body. Add his age, which is at most under three hundred, and he is plainly an Ascendant seed. I believe he is very suited to become a Star Gate guest elder."

"Leaving aside his talent and youth for now, I see this man as rootless duckweed. If Star Gate can accept him, over time it will gain his recognition. In the intelligence, Fu Chaosheng also said that this man values loyalty and emotion..."

Three Heaven-Earth Peak elders from outside continents spoke in succession, and very quickly shifted the tone set by the Xiang family grand elder.

The so-called Seven Elders' assessment consisted of five outside-continent Heaven-Earth Peak elders, one representative of the Thirty-Six Aristocratic Clans, and one representative of the Twelve Great Houses.

Now that the outside-continent elders were speaking with such a unified voice, it was clear that Luo Chen's impressive bearing had not truly moved them.

Those words had been proper and ordinary. How could they speak of bearing?

If one thought carefully about the inside story, it was not complicated.

It was simply that among the five great outside-continent elders, Chu Yan, the Alchemy Saint, was about to ascend, leaving an empty seat.

And her departure would also greatly weaken the influence of outside-continent cultivators within Star Gate.

That was why they were now so eager to recruit outside-continent cultivators who shared their experience.

Taking this as a beginning, it would also be smoother later when promoting a new elder.

Unexpectedly, when the fourth person spoke, the direction abruptly changed.

"All of you speak of his aptitude, his temperament, and constantly say he has hope of becoming an Ascendant."

"But are there few such people on Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain?"

The three outside-continent elders' expressions changed at once.

"Dongfang Bai[TERM?], what do you mean?"

Dongfang Bai sneered. "You must first understand what Star Gate's principle is! To absorb outside-continent talent, prioritizing immediate combat strength, prioritizing profound realms, and prioritizing advanced age. To put it bluntly, it absorbs the thick branches and trunks of the Mountains and Seas Realm's five continents and turns them into nourishment to supply this land, to supply this continent's younger generation. These words may sound unpleasant, but you and I both know it in our hearts. It is only because the Dao Sect is benevolent and has given us withered bones in the grave a hope, a hope leading to Ascendant long life, that we accept it gladly."

"That child Luo Chen is so young, with such aptitude. Do you want him to come and consume Star Gate's resources, plundering Central Continent's spiritual opportunity?"

"Therefore, this old man believes he should not pass!"

No one expected a crack to appear among the outside-continent elders, who should have been united as iron.

The three were momentarily speechless and subconsciously looked toward the frail woman.

Dongfang Bai, however, spoke. "In this matter, we have always only provided suggestions. The one who truly decides must still be the representative of the Twelve Great Houses. Elder Zhan[TERM?], what do you think?"

The person called Elder Zhan had an overbearing tone.

"I naturally uphold Star Gate's principles and do not wish Luo Chen to enter Heaven-Earth Peak. However..."

As soon as the word "however" emerged, Dongfang Bai and the Xiang family grand elder sensed something wrong.

Sure enough, Elder Zhan's tone softened by several degrees.

"Alchemy Saint, this time, let the decision be yours."

"Elder Zhan!" Dongfang Bai cried out softly.

Elder Zhan glanced at him, his gaze icy.

The other three-continent elders laughed inwardly. The emperor was not anxious, but the eunuch was. The local Central Continent cultivator himself did not mind, yet an outsider was more worried than anyone.

For example, the Xiang family grand elder was now composed and calm, following Elder Zhan's lead.

Though they did not know why Elder Zhan had suddenly handed the decision to the Alchemy Saint, the three felt it was practically certain.

They were all outside-continent cultivators, so surely the Alchemy Saint would generously approve.

The frail figure spoke leisurely.

"Heaven-Earth Peak has too heavy a scent of decay."

"It would be rather good for such a young person to join."

"However, Dongfang Bai's considerations are also not wrong. Accepting young outside-continent talent into Heaven-Earth Peak violates Star Gate's principle. Once this opening is made, it will not only cost Central Continent's younger generation resources, but also cause the top great sects of the four continents east, south, west, and north to resent it and join hands to resist the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, just as when I left the Eastern Wilderness back then..."

"Therefore, giving him a temporary guest elder status should be enough."

Temporary guest elder status?

The three outside-continent elders looked at one another.

Dongfang Bai was stunned and somewhat surprised.

The Xiang family grand elder frowned.

Elder Zhan said in a deep voice, "There is no precedent for this."

It either was or was not. How could there be a temporary version?

Chu Yan laughed softly. "Elder Zhan, did you not say I would decide?"

Elder Zhan paused, then laughed freely.

"Since that is so, then we shall follow the Alchemy Saint's words. Bestow upon Wilderness Wanderer Luo Chen temporary guest elder status. He may freely enter and leave Heaven-Earth Peak. However, no resources shall be opened to him. They must be exchanged for corresponding contributions."

"As for his cave abode... number Jia Seventy-Eight."

...

"Luo Chen, you must be tired today. Let the drinking stop here. We will not disturb you further."

Facing the Fu grandfather and granddaughter's farewell, Luo Chen readily agreed.

He was indeed a little "tired."

Among the Seven Elders of Star Gate, six were late-stage Sage great cultivators comparable to Moon Wanderer, and one was an Ascendant great power about to ascend.

In front of them, no matter how neither servile nor overbearing Luo Chen appeared, the pressure was still immense.

Had Chu Yan, the Alchemy Saint, not dispelled with laughter and conversation the pressure the others brought him, the questions and answers inside the Star Gate space might not have gone so smoothly.

A banquet had relaxed him considerably.

But no matter how relaxed he became, it could not compare to quietly cultivating in private for a while.

Only, before leaving, he deliberately glanced at Fu Qinglan.

He was somewhat surprised by this woman.

He had seen many Sages, and every one possessed an austere bearing.

This woman also counted as rather graceful, but that quality was hard to connect with a Sage.

How to put it?

She had both the tenacity of someone prepared to bear the rise and fall, honor and disgrace of her clan, and also a few parts of innocent naivete untouched by worldly affairs.

And he had seen this kind of temperament repeatedly in Central Continent.

Regarding this, Luo Chen could only attribute it to the fact that this place was simply too peaceful.

Without tempering through blood and fire, the cultivators here were high in realm, but somewhat immature in conduct.

Just like Changfengzi, Guan Xiaoping, and the others he had seen back then at the Myriad Immortals Assembly.

Somewhat coincidentally, when Luo Chen looked over, Fu Qinglan was also secretly looking at him.

When their gazes met, Luo Chen was calm and composed, while the latter unnaturally shifted her gaze away.

"A fledgling."

On the way back to his room, Luo Chen laughed despite himself.

If someone like this were placed in the Eastern Wilderness or Northern Sea, he could sell her and she would probably still count the money for him.

There was a smile on his face, but how could there not also be a little jealousy in his heart?

Someone like this could actually reach the Sage realm?

...

Two streaks of escaping light left Heaven-Earth Peak beneath the moonlight and flew toward Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain across the river.

Under the night, Fu Qinglan bit her lip lightly and suddenly spoke.

"Grandfather, can that man really do it?"

Fu Chaosheng, who had been in a good mood, suddenly froze. First, he was puzzled. "What do you mean, can he do it?"

But very quickly, he reacted.

"Are you doubting your grandfather's eye?"

Fu Qinglan shook her head and said worriedly, "It is not that I doubt you. It is simply that this competition is different from previous ones. Leaving aside the sect's Adept and Initiate participants, there are a full thirteen newly advanced Sages alone. Every Dao protector they selected is either a strong expert meticulously cultivated by a clan, the master of a great outside-continent teaching, or a local overlord. By comparison, Luo Chen is too..."

"Too what?" Fu Chaosheng's face held anger.

Fu Qinglan took a deep breath and said without fear, "Too young. Though I call him Dao Brother, I even feel he is younger than me. Can such a person truly protect me while I focus on comprehending the true intent of laws inside Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven?"

"Young?" Fu Chaosheng laughed in extreme anger. "Since when does the cultivation world judge ability by age? Your grandfather has lived an empty nine hundred years, and to this day I am only at the third level of Sage. Do you think Eminent Gu Daoyin, who is not yet five hundred, is stronger, or am I stronger?"

Fu Qinglan pressed her lips tight. "That is different. Senior Brother Daoyin is a true genius unseen in a sacred land for a thousand years."

"There are three such geniuses in this generation of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!" Fu Chaosheng's words rang like metal and stone, his eyes sharp. "Moreover, in my view, Wilderness Wanderer's ability is not below any sacred-land prodigy. Do not underestimate him."

Fu Qinglan opened her mouth, not knowing why her grandfather had such confidence in that man.

Seeing that she was still half convinced and half doubtful, Fu Chaosheng's anger vanished.

He had protected her too well.

If, like that boy Liu Yuan, she had gone to the Northern Sea to temper herself for eighty or a hundred years, she would know what it meant that a person could not be judged by appearance and the sea could not be measured with a dipper.

With a sigh, Fu Chaosheng tossed out a jade slip.

"It was inconvenient to explain in detail in the letter before. Now take this and read it!"

"What is this?"

"It records Luo Chen's battle achievements, large and small. After reading it, you should understand why Grandfather is certain that no one but this person will do."

Fu Qinglan hesitantly probed into the jade slip with divine sense.

Slowly, her expression became wonderfully colorful.

...

At mid-stage Adept, he comprehended the method of forming a Nascent Soul on his own.

In the battle on Coldlight Island, he instantly killed someone of the same level, and under the pursuit and interception of several Adept cultivators, he defeated them despite crossing a minor realm.

Within the Myriad Immortals Assembly, a late-stage Adept great cultivator was suppressed by him.

Beside the Fallen Sea, the name Devil Lord resounded in all directions.

There was even the feat of suspected cross-realm killing of a Sage cultivator!

In a mere hundred years, without help from any sect or clan, he formed his Nascent Soul alone.

The moment he stepped into the Sage realm, he displayed uncommon ability. Langya took the initiative to befriend him, Split Heaven suffered a small loss, and even Shen Wandou, also from the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, could not grasp Luo Chen's ability.

The name Wilderness Wanderer had risen only recently, and was not as famous as the Three Great Wanderers of the Northern Sea.

But in terms of speed of rise and future potential, he was even above the Three Great Wanderers!

When Fu Qinglan once again arrived at Welcoming Immortals Hall on Heaven-Earth Peak, Luo Chen's resume from the jade slip kept surfacing in her mind.

Every item and every deed spoke of those harsh, dangerous, and thrilling cultivation roads.

By comparison, the sect competitions and secret clan pressure she had thought very dangerous were simply too childish.

"No wonder, when he sized me up then, his eyes held such complicated emotions. Some curiosity, some disdain, and even a faint trace of envy and jealousy."

"Such a person should be enough to be my Dao protector."

"But I cannot let him look down on me too much. Today, I must always speak openly with him once."

When Fu Qinglan arrived at Luo Chen's small courtyard, she happened to see an outside-continent elder of Star Gate handing over all the necessary tokens to him.

Had he become a Star Gate guest elder?

Yet from Luo Chen's strange expression, she sensed that something was wrong.

"Dao Brother Luo, did something unexpected happen?"

Luo Chen weighed the jade token in his hand with confusion. "Does Star Gate have something called an external affairs guest elder[TERM?]?"

Fu Qinglan also grew confused.

Within Heaven-Earth Peak, there were only three kinds of existence.

Star Gate guest elders, Heavenly Origin offering elders, and Heaven-Earth Peak elders.

The first was the simplest. They had the name of guest elder and could enjoy some rights and benefits.

As for offering elders, the addition of Heavenly Origin to the name meant the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect would also support their cultivation.

Heaven-Earth Peak elders were rather special. People of that level could participate in certain decisions of Heaven-Earth Peak, and even the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

But external affairs guest elder? She had never heard of it.

Judging by the literal meaning, could it be a guest elder specially responsible for external affairs?

Seeing that she also could not explain it, Luo Chen stopped asking.

"First, let us see my cave abode."

He smiled freely and strode out of the courtyard.

Fu Qinglan hurried after him.

Without realizing it, she had already begun taking the other party's thoughts and actions as the lead.

*(End of Chapter)*
