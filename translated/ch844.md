# Chapter 844: Immortal Brew Nourishes the Five Viscera, Enemy Refined into a Puppet

At first glance!

Luo Chen did not look at the wine jars. Instead, his gaze fell upon the corpse.

A large hole in the chest, all breath gone, a look of stunned disbelief still frozen on the pale face.

It was precisely Jin Ling’s corpse!

Whoosh…

A gentle breeze seemed to stir within the great hall.

Jin Ling’s corpse slowly floated upward.

Luo Chen brushed his hand through the air from a distance, smoothing closed those eyes that had died with resentment. Then, to Black King’s confusion, he began forming hand seals toward the corpse.

“What is Master trying to do?”

Black King watched the scene, unable to fathom his intent.

Not until Luo Chen suddenly paused, and a certain aura emanated from him that made Black King tremble, did he realize Luo Chen’s intention.

“No way…”

The next moment, Luo Chen’s mana fluctuations took on a rhythmic cadence, as if his entire being were resonating with heaven and earth.

A streak of azure-white light slowly flew out from Luo Chen’s body and carefully landed upon Jin Ling’s corpse.

*Shua!*

Those eyes opened once more!

Black King’s entire body shuddered, and his heart cried out: “Is Master going to refine him into a puppet?”

*Thump.*

The corpse, which had seemed to come back to life for an instant, suddenly fell heavily to the ground, as if nothing had happened.

Luo Chen stared at the corpse, his eyes deep and unfathomable, lost in thought.

Black King did not even dare to breathe, respectfully lying to the side.

Only when Luo Chen spoke did he suddenly snap to attention.

“What?”

Luo Chen no longer looked at the corpse. He pointed at the densely packed jars beneath his feet.

“Have you tested these spirit wines?”

Black King nodded. “I’ve basically tested them all. There are seven kinds of spirit wine in total. Aside from that one with the *zhenling millet* label, which I dared not try, the other six are all beneficial for cultivation and contain no toxins whatsoever.”

With a single glance, Luo Chen spotted the *zhenling millet*.

He waved his hand, and the small jar landed in his grasp.

“What is so peculiar about this wine that even you dare not drink it?”

Originally, Black King had come from the Black-Scale Giant Python clan. After someone stimulated his potential, he had transformed into a Black-Scale Jiaolong—thick-skinned and tough, immune to a hundred poisons. After all these years of cultivation, his resistance to toxins was unparalleled.

Back then, when Luo Chen had sent him to explore Purple Spirit Island, his high poison resistance had already been evident.

That was why Luo Chen had given him this small task—to test whether these spirit wines contained any deadly poison.

He had never expected there to be a poisoned wine that even Black King dared not touch!

Black King looked at the wine jar, a trace of fear in his eyes.

“I don’t know what kind of poison was used to brew this wine. It smells extremely fragrant and tempting, but it has a powerful corrosive effect on the flesh and blood of our kind.”

“If you hadn’t warned me beforehand, Master, I might have drunk it.”

“Later, to resist that temptation, I was forced to seal it away with my stomach acid. That helped a lot.”

Hearing this description, Luo Chen couldn’t help but raise an eyebrow.

He gently lifted a corner of the seal, and immediately a fragrance of wine wafted out, making his throat bob as he fought the urge to drink.

Right in front of Black King, Luo Chen took out a single drop and sipped it into his mouth.

“Master, don’t…”

Nothing unusual!

Just as the poisoned wine entered his stomach and Luo Chen felt nothing, a sudden sharp pain erupted in his lower abdomen.

His expression unchanged, he opened his mouth and spat!

*Chi…*

That undigested drop of spirit wine was wrapped in a stream of ashen-white flames and expelled, incinerated in midair.

Luo Chen stared at the wine jar, a look of wariness in his eyes. “What kind of wine is this? Even my fourth-rank mid-stage Archaic physique has no resistance to it, and that was just a single drop.”

He did not dare try again. Carefully, Luo Chen stored the wine jar separately in his storage belt.

This stuff couldn’t be drunk, but keeping it wouldn’t hurt. It might come in handy someday.

Then, his gaze fell upon the remaining eighty jars of spirit wine.

Sweet Mead, Jade Nectar, Longevity Spring, Golden Wind and Jade Dew, Blooming Rosemallow, Five Temple Immortal Brew!

Six kinds of spirit wine in total, all cellar-aged treasures of the Dieyun Sect over countless years.

Under Black King’s explanation, Luo Chen learned the general purpose of each.

“Sweet Mead is a bitter wine. The moment it enters the mouth, it’s bitter. Drinking a mouthful is more bitter than all the hardships Little Black has endured in his life. But after it goes down the throat, a great sweetness returns, lingering for days without end. It’s the most unforgettable of all the spirit wines.”

“Has your life really been that bitter?”

“Uh…”

“Continue!”

“Alright. Jade Nectar has extremely heavy yin energy. It’s not very suitable for males like us. It should be a wine brewed specifically for female cultivators. As for its exact effects, Master will have to find a female cultivator to test it on. Tianxuan would be a good choice.”

“Longevity Spring, as the name suggests, should have the effect of extending life. But exactly how many years it can extend, Little Black has no way of knowing.”

Luo Chen’s gaze fell upon the single jar of Longevity Spring, a thoughtful look in his eyes.

If it truly had the effect of extending life, he could give it to those in the sect who had rendered great service but were nearing the end of their lifespan—Qu Lingjun, for instance.

But for now, it was better not to send it.

Without pressure, that kid might lose his drive for fierce cultivation.

Keep it for emergencies!

“Golden Wind and Jade Dew should be very useful to Master. This wine contains terrifying spiritual energy. A single sip can instantly restore a large amount of mana!”

Luo Chen’s eyes lit up. He reached out and summoned a jar, taking a small sip to savor it.

Sure enough, it contained a vast amount of pure, exquisite spiritual energy.

Moreover, it possessed all five elements, extremely close to what was called origin qi.

And after a thousand years of cellar-aging, it was even more mellow and rich in spiritual energy.

Just one small sip had restored about a thousandth of his mana.

What about a large gulp?

What about an entire jar?

And there were ten whole jars of this Golden Wind and Jade Dew!

In other words, as long as Luo Chen didn’t exhaust all his mana in one go, with these wines, he could have virtually inexhaustible mana for a certain period of time!

Truly, when Golden Wind and Jade Dew meet, they surpass countless worldly pleasures!

“As for the Five Temple Immortal Brew…”

“You don’t need to introduce that one. I know.”

What Luo Chen valued most was the Five Temple Immortal Brew, which was the most abundant in number.

He had drunk this wine before—the hundred-year vintage. It had a decent nourishing effect on the five viscera.

And the thousand-year cellar-aged version would only be more effective!

With this batch of Five Temple Immortal Brew, he might be able to make some progress in his body refinement realm, which had stagnated for years.

“And this Blooming Rosemallow—why aren’t you introducing it?” Luo Chen suddenly asked.

A look of embarrassment appeared on Black King’s face. He twisted awkwardly and said, “This wine is rather… base.”

“Poisonous?”

“Not poison, it’s just… well, after drinking it, one really wants to do *that* sort of thing.”

Luo Chen was stunned for a moment. He looked at the utterly ordinary, unremarkable wine jar and slowly understood.

“You mean this jar is for… arousal?”

“Mm-hmm.”

Black King nodded repeatedly.

Heaven knew that recently he had been tossing and turning underground because he had drunk a little of this wine, making him restless and agitated.

The Luo Tian Sect cultivators just thought he was turning over in his sleep.

Luo Chen didn’t know whether to laugh or cry, but he quickly realized the implications.

If it could stir desire in a fourth-rank desolate beast, this Blooming Rosemallow was no ordinary wine.

He had built his fortune refining the Myriad Delight Pill, so he knew well the wonders of such things.

“In a certain sense, the Dieyun Sect had quite a deep foundation. Their mastery of wine-brewing was truly at its peak.”

“No wonder the Nether Origin Sect valued them so highly!”

Murmuring to himself, Luo Chen carefully sorted and stored the various wines.

The most dangerous *zhenling millet* wine and the most important Longevity Spring were placed together, not to be used unless absolutely necessary.

The Sweet Mead for satisfying the palate, the Jade Nectar that might be useful for female cultivators, and the Blooming Rosemallow for arousal were grouped together. These wines were of little use to him, so they would just be kept as collectibles.

As for the Golden Wind and Jade Dew and the Five Temple Immortal Brew, Luo Chen placed them where they could be retrieved most easily.

“You may withdraw!”

Luo Chen waved his hand, and Black King obediently vanished.

After he left, Luo Chen took out a jar of Five Temple Immortal Brew. Without any hesitation, he took a large gulp.

Then he activated *Myriad Path Convergence*, directing the refined wine’s power toward his heart, liver, spleen, lungs, and kidneys—the five viscera.

A faint tipsiness came and went.

The five viscera churned like a blazing cauldron!

In an instant, Luo Chen felt a tempestuous upheaval within his body, as if his five viscera had gained intelligence and wanted to break free from his body.

“Miscalculation.”

“I drank too much!”

Luo Chen inwardly cursed his mistake. He had used his experience with the hundred-year Five Temple Immortal Brew to judge the thousand-year cellar-aged version, unconsciously overlooking the fundamental difference between them.

Or perhaps not overlooking, but rather having too much confidence in his own powerful physique.

But he hadn’t expected the surging blood energy contained in this thousand-year Five Temple Immortal Brew to be so terrifying.

“I wonder what methods the Dieyun Sect used to brew these spirit wines. If I get the chance, collecting some recipes wouldn’t be a bad idea.”

“It’s a pity that Chu Kui left. He was fond of such drinks.”

Luo Chen liked wine but wasn’t addicted to it. He had drunk many fine wines in his youth.

Old Yuan’s yellow pear wine, the monkey wine from the Gu Yuan Mountains, the Taibai wine from Taibai Market, and even some local waters from the Northern Sea.

Having drunk so much, even if he couldn’t brew, he could still taste.

With just one sip, he recognized the extraordinary quality of the thousand-year Five Temple Immortal Brew.

But the level of pain was still within his tolerance, so he had the leisure to let his mind wander.

Reining in his thoughts, Luo Chen began to focus on refining the immortal brew.

…

Three days later, Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes.

The churning five viscera had settled.

What remained was a brand-new sensation.

With each breath, the atmosphere surged, blood and qi flowed, and his heart pounded with vitality.

Luo Chen subconsciously stroked his abdomen, a thoughtful look in his eyes.

His physique was incredibly powerful.

He had first followed the orthodox path of body refinement, then borrowed beast-like body refinement techniques to cross the third rank. Later, he had relied on the Black Emperor Ointment, meant for puppets, to reach his peak.

But over the years, he had always felt something was off.

Only now did he suddenly realize.

“Everything is on the outside, nothing is within!”

“My strength comes from lightning strikes and fire refinement—supreme yang and hardness, excelling on the surface.”

“Those weaker than me shatter on contact. But if I encounter a truly powerful body refiner, even if my limbs and surface can withstand it, my five viscera and six organs remain fragile.”

Powerful body refiners didn’t rely on simple external attacks.

Take Wang Yuan, for example. Early on, he had created a Blood Transformation Demon Finger, which could inject force into an opponent’s body and then detonate it instantly.

That was just the most obvious example. There were countless techniques like striking through the ox to hit the mountain, shattering the heart and lungs, and so on.

That was why, after laying a solid foundation, body refiners no longer pursued pure hardness but instead turned to internal cultivation.

The Furnace and Leakless realms were exactly for this purpose.

Luo Chen didn’t understand the transformations of blood and qi. The source power within his body came from his physical origin and wasn’t as versatile as force energy or astral energy.

Thus, his internal body was far weaker than his external body, which had been tempered by Black Emperor Ointment for over a hundred years.

“Excessive hardness breaks easily; excessive softness leads to weakness. Hardness and softness combined—that is how things are accomplished.”

“If I can use the Five Temple Immortal Brew to nourish my internal organs, I can make up for this deficiency.”

Having understood all this, Luo Chen felt greatly relieved.

Considering that the thousand-year Five Temple Immortal Brew was too potent, he took a clean Purple Yang Gourd, poured a jar’s worth into it, and hung it at his waist.

This way, he could drink it at any time.

And he wouldn’t drink too much at once, causing internal pain.

Step by step, he would gradually nourish his five viscera until they reached perfection.

After doing all this, Luo Chen did not continue cultivating.

Instead, he turned his attention to the corpse.

The remains of a Sage—even if his Purple Mansion had been broken and his Nascent Soul was gone, it was still a Sage’s body!

Wasting such material would be a terrible shame.

Luo Chen had always had respect for the strong.

But Jin Ling was not only a strong opponent; he was also his enemy. That meant making the most of him.

“Perfect timing. After all these years of studying puppet arts, it’s time to put them into practice.”

“I’ll use you for practice!”

Luo Chen muttered, then patted the crown of his head. A streak of dim light flew out.

It was his third soul!

Over the years, he had been studying the way of puppets, trying to master the White Tiger Puppet, which was comparable to a late-stage Sage.

In terms of his current puppet mastery, he was still not qualified to refine the White Tiger Puppet.

But making ordinary puppets—Luo Chen was confident he had some skill.

As for how to process Jin Ling’s corpse, Luo Chen already had a plan.

There were many types of puppets, divided by shape, material, spirit-hosting, and control methods.

Generally speaking, they could be roughly divided into two categories: mechanical puppets and flesh-and-blood puppets.

Mechanical puppets were made from various precious materials—wood, minerals, and so on. These were relatively more difficult and required a high level of skill from the puppeteer.

Flesh-and-blood puppets were simpler but also more taboo.

As long as one had the right raw materials, one could start refining them.

As for what those raw materials were, there was no need to say more.

Wasn’t one right in front of him?

“Back then, Tian Xingzi of the Falling Cloud Sect—who enjoyed the honorary status of elder even after dying at the Initiate realm—had refined Mi Shuhua’s remains into a flesh-and-blood puppet. I even cursed him for being cruel.”

“Now I’m walking the same path. It’s like a boomerang hitting myself.”

There was a hint of shame in his words, but his face showed no emotion.

Luo Chen pointed his finger, and a wisp of ashen-white flame flew from his fingertip, enveloping Jin Ling’s corpse entirely.

Under the scorching flames, a burnt stench immediately filled the hall.

The third soul beside him was not idle either. It began searching the Accumulated Thunder Treasure Ring, which held his materials, for suitable auxiliary components.

Even a flesh-and-blood puppet required auxiliary materials.

Working on both fronts, time passed quickly.

It took a full seven days and seven nights before Luo Chen finished refining this Sage’s corpse.

At this point, Jin Ling’s corpse had shrunk by nearly seventy percent, withered like human skin.

Luo Chen was very satisfied with this result.

Next came filling in the materials.

With a thought, Luo Chen took out the Primordial Unity Cauldron, threw in various prepared mineral materials, and the Withering-Bloom True Flame responded.

When he had fully refined a basin of metallic liquid, Luo Chen stopped.

He did not immediately fill in the materials. Instead, his gaze fell upon the large hole in Jin Ling’s chest.

After hesitating for a long while, Luo Chen steeled his heart.

“Remove armor!”

The Mystic Dust Armor fell away, revealing his muscular upper body.

Luo Chen moved his right hand, and the Yuantu Sword materialized. He slowly cut into his own chest.

Blood flowed, and the Yuantu Sword began to tremble, letting out a series of chirping sounds.

“What are you so happy about? Be still!”

Luo Chen barked. The Yuantu Sword let out an unwilling whimper but obediently allowed itself to be driven by Luo Chen.

Seeing this, Luo Chen breathed a sigh of relief.

After he had practiced the sword dao and saturated it with demon blood, the sword spirit of Yuantu had become increasingly accepting of him.

That was a good thing.

But the sword spirit’s desire to drink his blood was also an undisguised fact.

Luo Chen would not indulge it. He would rather feed the sword spirit with Demon Emperor blood than sacrifice his own body to the sword.

That would only increase the sword spirit’s demonic nature and blur the line between master and servant!

After suppressing the sword spirit, Luo Chen endured the discomfort of self-mutilation and slowly cut off a large piece of flesh from his rock-hard chest.

“Hnng…”

With a muffled groan, Luo Chen immediately placed that piece of chest flesh onto Jin Ling’s corpse.

On his own body, a large amount of source blood gathered, and the flesh regenerated as if it had a will of its own.

It was the Archaic physique’s talent for reattaching severed limbs and regenerating flesh!

Though not as good as Black King’s Undying Body, it was still quite useful.

Ignoring his own discomfort for the moment, Luo Chen carefully covered the chest flesh over the large hole in Jin Ling’s remains.

After it was fully embedded, he poured in the prepared metallic liquid.

Not only that, but he also used his mana to drive golden threads, like worms, into the corpse’s meridians and blood vessels.

When everything was ready, the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame appeared once more, enveloping Jin Ling’s entire corpse and beginning to refine it together.

During this process, Luo Chen occasionally used the Withering-Bloom True Intent he had comprehended to stimulate the vitality brought by his own flesh on the corpse, making it increasingly lively.

And the third soul sat beside him, silently watching everything.

…

A full month passed in the blink of an eye.

From within the Luo Tian Great Hall came a low shout.

A majestic aura burst forth.

But a moment later, it vanished without a trace.

Inside the hall.

Luo Chen looked at the completed puppet, a satisfied expression in his eyes.

“Enter!”

The third soul did not hesitate at all. It directly drilled into the puppet and even took out a top-grade metal-attribute spirit stone to embed in the chest.

“Not enough.”

“At this moment, this puppet is not even half as strong as Jin Ling at his peak.”

Luo Chen observed for a moment and understood the shortcomings.

Without a Nascent Soul, it could not connect with heaven and earth or manipulate free-floating spiritual energy.

Without a magic treasure, it lacked combat power.

But these shortcomings could be remedied.

Luo Chen raised his hand, and two golden feathers fell into the puppet’s hands.

The puppet took them and swung them without any hindrance. A terrifying sharpness immediately arose in the hall.

These were originally Jin Ling’s natal magic treasures. As long as the soul imprint was erased, there would be no obstruction.

Not only that, Luo Chen patted the armrest.

“Come out!”

Black King appeared again, looking at Luo Chen with a resentful expression.

He had just fallen asleep, and he was woken up again.

“Take him to the place with the richest spiritual energy in the Hundred Li Green River to nourish him!”

Black King looked at the puppet and couldn’t help swallowing his saliva.

His earlier guess had come true.

Master really had refined that guy into a puppet.

He quickly said, “The Fenghua Palace has been breached, and the formation that sealed the spirit vein has a flaw. I’ve drawn a lot of spiritual energy over here. At the bottom of this Great Azure Mountain, a small fourth-rank spirit acupoint has already condensed. It’s the most suitable place to nourish Master’s puppet!”

Luo Chen was stunned. An unexpected bonus?
