# Chapter 539: Blood Nightmare Moluo, the Twelve Stream Lords

"This one is Wu Qi. Greetings, Fellow Daoist."

"I am Qingyang, temporarily lord of Swallow."

On the ten-li calm lake, misty white vapor steamed upward.

On the small island of Invitation Moon, two Daoists sat facing one another.

Staring at Luo Chen, Wu Qi's eyes revealed a look of emotion. This Demon Lord Qingyang's methods were truly extraordinary!

In just a few short exchanges, the other party had revealed absolutely powerful ability in both the soul-refining dao and the dao of spells.

Combining that with the other party's previous rumors...

He could not help asking aloud, "Fellow Daoist Qingyang, could you truly be a true inheritance disciple[TERM?] of the Primordial Demon Sect who survived outside?"

Luo Chen's expression was calm. He asked in reply, "The Primordial Demon Sect has already scattered like wind and clouds. Whether I am or not, what difference is there?"

Wu Qi's expression froze.

That was indeed the logic, but if one could act under the Primordial Demon Sect's name, then when walking in this cultivation world once controlled by the Primordial Demon Sect, without doubt...

Suddenly,

he came to his senses.

If one truly did so, there would naturally be benefits, but also drawbacks.

Under the Primordial Demon Sect's rule of several thousand years, although it had the name of a Holy Land, it had not much imitated the Holy Lands of other continents by exercising strong control over the Northern Sea. Add to that the overbearing style of its disciples and members, and no one knew how many powerful people it had offended, openly and in secret.

Even if, on the surface, no one was beating a fallen dog,

in the dark, who knew how many people were exterminating remnants of the Primordial Demon Sect to the last.

No one dared provoke those Sage-stage Primordial Demon Sect experts.

But someone like Demon Lord Qingyang was much easier to attack.

Judging from the other party's character, hiding in such a remote place as the Swallow Islands, he was someone who greatly disliked trouble. Naturally, he would not invite even greater trouble for a few conveniences.

Believing he had thought it through, Wu Qi had already pinned the title of Primordial Demon Sect true inheritance disciple onto Luo Chen.

This was precisely the effect Luo Chen wanted.

He gave his origins a source, while avoiding the corresponding risks as much as possible.

Some things were best left unsaid outright.

But!

There were some things that were still better said clearly.

He looked at the black-robed Daoist with a high topknot and coldly said, "Fellow Daoist Wu comes from Moluo Stream?"

Wu Qi smiled. "Indeed. Among the Twelve Moluo Streams[TERM?], I dwell on Shaman God Island[TERM?] under Blood Nightmare Moluo[TERM?], and unworthily serve as the current supreme elder of the Wu clan."

In a single sentence, he laid out his name, address, origin, and backer.

Luo Chen savored the information within.

The Twelve Moluo Streams referred to the fact that within the force known as Moluo Stream, there were a total of twelve Sage Perfected Beings, collectively called the Twelve Stream Lords[TERM?].

Rumor had it that among them, there were also top great cultivators at late Sage stage.

But this force's name originated from an early-stage Sage cultivator, precisely that Blood Nightmare Moluo.

This person was a proper direct-line inheritor of the Primordial Demon Sect!

Within the demonic sect, he was also an outstanding figure of the Blood God lineage[TERM?], and had even advanced to become an elder of the demonic sect.

When the Primordial Demon Sect was destroyed, he had been outside handling affairs for the sect and had luckily escaped calamity.

After surviving, he thought at every moment of reviving the sect.

After obtaining the support of a group of Sage Perfected Beings, he founded the force called Moluo Stream under his dao title, attempting to recover the Primordial Demon Sect's territory through it.

At present, Moluo Stream was also one of the major top forces mainly opposing the Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance!

Very clearly, when Wu Qi bluntly stated that he served under Blood Nightmare Moluo, it was both to show sincerity and to hint at Luo Chen.

"I have a great backer!"

Luo Chen naturally understood the meaning within the other party's words, but his expression remained calm.

"Fellow Daoist came here presumably for that Yan Nantian!"

Wu Qi nodded. "Indeed. When Moluo Stream was first established, there were also other forces wanting to fish in troubled waters. The Yan clan of Laboring Swallow Island was foolish and ignorant, used by others, thereby offending my Moluo Stream. That thief Yan Nantian had rather some strength, and unfortunately was both bold and meticulous, injuring quite a few people of our faction. Killing him was an assigned mission within the stream."

"Then that is correct!" Luo Chen said coldly, a trace of displeasure appearing on his face.

Wu Qi was stunned. "What is correct?"

Luo Chen looked at him coldly. "I have never disrespected your faction, and I killed your enemy Yan Nantian for you. Whether in feeling or reason, you should all thank me. Yet why did you come to my door and immediately make such a big show of force? I need an explanation."

His posture was neither servile nor overbearing, and even carried a trace of forcefulness!

As if he did not care in the slightest about Blood Nightmare Moluo behind Wu Qi.

Wu Qi's expression changed slightly.

Could it be that before the Primordial Demon Sect's destruction, this Demon Lord Qingyang had occupied a high position? One where he did not even care about Blood Nightmare Moluo?

After careful observation, when he saw the other party's extremely young face, he faintly gained a little certainty.

So young, yet already possessing the respectable realm of Core Formation Stage Three.

Even within the demonic sect, he would be a heaven's chosen figure.

Perhaps the other party truly did not fear Blood Nightmare Moluo?

Many thoughts circulated. In the end, Wu Qi's expression turned solemn, and he cupped his fists toward Luo Chen.

"Apologies. Wu was also misled by a petty person. I will certainly give Fellow Daoist an explanation for this matter!"

After the words fell,

in front of Luo Chen, he released a divine-sense voice transmission without the slightest concealment.

Afterward, he quietly drank spirit tea with Luo Chen.

Luo Chen did not hurry him. He wanted to know what kind of explanation this would be.

Before long,

a streak of escaping light flew in from outside the island, circling above the ten-li calm lake, hesitating and not advancing.

On Invitation Moon Island, shrouded in white mist, as if sensing the arrival, a small path voluntarily opened.

That escaping light hesitated for an instant, then flew in.

Following the guidance, Kun Jiao, gang leader of the Jiaolong Gang, arrived at Qingyang Hall in nervous unease.

As soon as he entered, he felt two burning gazes fall upon him.

That unrestrained spiritual pressure pressed down like mountains.

His face gained a trace of pallor.

With a thud,

he knelt on the ground and gritted his teeth. "Kun Jiao greets the two Seniors!"

Just as he was about to raise his head, he heard a laughing voice by his ear.

"Fellow Daoist, this is the explanation I will give you."

That voice seemed to be right by his ear.

When he listened again, he was blank and at a loss.

Kun Jiao saw only a headless corpse kneeling on the white jade floor tiles.

A perfectly good human head rolled four or five times through the hall.

Luo Chen looked toward Wu Qi with a smile that was not quite a smile.

"Just this?"

Wu Qi said frankly, "The one who deceived me with fawning words was him. Killing him is entirely reasonable. Of course, perhaps Fellow Daoist still has some dissatisfaction. In that case..."

As he spoke, he beckoned with his hand.

The two bags at Kun Jiao's waist flew over.

One was a storage bag, the other a spirit beast bag.

Both bags landed before Luo Chen.

Wu Qi pointed at the two bags and said, "As a gang leader, Kun Jiao should have some small savings. In addition, inside this spirit beast bag is an early third-tier Black-Scale Jiaolong. Even if it cannot compare to us Core Formation cultivators, it is enough to handle the vast majority of Foundation Establishment juniors. I see that Fellow Daoist's cultivation place has only four or five second-tier Blue-Ringed Fang Serpents as guards. It is truly a bit shabby. Using this Black-Scale Jiaolong to guard the home and watch the gate can also decorate your facade a little."

His words were logical and well-founded, neither servile nor overbearing.

He had killed someone to provide Luo Chen a way down, and also offered profit as benefit.

At this point, if Luo Chen still chased after the matter and refused to let go, that would truly be somewhat failing to appreciate favor.

Luo Chen was not someone with low emotional intelligence.

After a moment of silence, he slowly nodded.

"So Fellow Daoist was deceived by a petty person. In that case, the past grudge between you and me is fully dissolved."

"Haha, indeed, indeed!" Wu Qi laughed twice, as if he did not care in the slightest about what had happened earlier.

Luo Chen gained a few parts of understanding in his heart.

Perhaps this was also one of the customs of the Northern Sea cultivation world.

In the Eastern Wasteland, one first declared one's background and origin, made clear each side's position as enemy or friend, then decided how to act.

But in the Northern Sea, right and wrong did not matter. First, fight a round!

If you had the strength to make me acknowledge you, only then would there be the follow-up of sitting opposite each other, talking and laughing, and exchanging backgrounds and origins.

Very clearly, his previous performance had obtained the other party's recognition.

After all!

Luo Chen absolutely did not believe he was much stronger than Wu Qi.

During that brief exchange of one move, he himself had also felt pressure. Otherwise, he would not have taken out the Mystic Flame Sword while preparing to fight with his full strength.

As for Wu Qi, after retreating back to the sea, although he had yielded verbally, his aura was instead three parts stronger than it had been on land.

Otherwise, Luo Chen would not have invited him onto Invitation Moon Island.

While Luo Chen deeply understood these principles, Wu Qi was also constantly examining the surrounding environment, his nose occasionally sniffing.

In the end, his somewhat shocked gaze settled on Luo Chen.

Faced with this strange gaze, Luo Chen frowned. "Yan Nantian is dead, and you have given me an explanation. There should be no other matters. Does Fellow Daoist still have business?"

This was driving the guest away.

Wu Qi hurriedly waved his hand. "There is business, there is business!"

He finally could not resist asking, "Fellow Daoist Qingyang, you are a third-tier alchemist, yes?"

Luo Chen tugged at the corner of his mouth. He did not answer, but he did not deny it either.

Wu Qi struck while the iron was hot. "From my observation, I fear Fellow Daoist can be called a master-level existence even among third-tier alchemists!"

"Oh? How do you see that?" Luo Chen asked in reply.

Wu Qi sniffed and, with a few traces of reminiscence, said, "Within Moluo Stream, one Stream Lord concurrently cultivates alchemy and has already reached the third tier. Once, he refined a third-tier high-grade pill and, in his delight, bestowed it on those below."

"Although this one did not obtain it, I was lucky enough to observe it once or twice from another person."

"The pill fragrance of that high-grade pill was simply like orchid and musk, as if... but even so! It did not reach even one or two tenths of the residual pill fragrance on Fellow Daoist's body!"

At this point, his expression grew excited.

"Fellow Daoist, could you refine supreme-grade third-tier pills?"

Luo Chen's thoughts stirred, and he seriously sniffed the scent on his body.

Sure enough!

Before the battle, the fragrance of the supreme-grade True Flame Pills he had refined still lingered faintly on his body.

In the past, he would habitually use Cleansing Art[TERM?] to remove it.

But after arriving in the Northern Sea, in order to preserve the baleful aura of killing people and demons on his body to deter petty villains, he had reduced the frequency with which he used Cleansing Art.

And because he had long resided in a room full of orchids and no longer smelled their fragrance[TERM?], he had accidentally overlooked these pill scents.

He had not expected Wu Qi's observational ability to be so sharp, glimpsing some of his true and false depths.

Since that was so, he did not need to conceal it.

"I merely succeeded once by luck. I do not deserve the mistaken praise of master."

Wu Qi hurriedly shook his head. "No, no, no. Although that Kun Jiao deceived me on some matters, he mentioned Fellow Daoist Qingyang's attainments in the alchemy dao many times."

"At that time, I only took him for a rustic wandering cultivator with shallow knowledge, mistakenly treating mediocrity as divinity."

"Looking now, he was indeed a rustic wandering cultivator with shallow knowledge. He only knew Fellow Daoist was a third-tier alchemist, but did not know that third-tier alchemists are also divided into high and low."

"With master-level alchemy like yours, in my Moluo Stream, you could be an honored guest. Even the exalted Twelve Stream Lords would presumably not spare their dignity and would lower themselves to befriend you."

The corners of Luo Chen's mouth twitched. Was this praise not a little exaggerated?

But thinking again, an existence capable of refining supreme-grade third-tier pills would be a first-rate figure even in the Eastern Wasteland!

That Qing Danzi and Meng Changshou, at their peak, could only refine third-tier high-grade pills, and the success rate could not be guaranteed.

Although they could also refine one or two types of fourth-tier pills, those were extremely simple ones, like the Transformation Pill.

Even so, Qing Danzi and Meng Changshou were famous existences in the Eastern Wasteland cultivation world, absolutely not inferior to ordinary Sage Perfected Beings.

After thinking this through, his mood became even calmer.

Seeing his calm expression, Wu Qi took a deep breath.

As expected!

No wonder this Demon Lord Qingyang had not cared at all earlier when Wu Qi mentioned Blood Nightmare Moluo as his backer.

An existence capable of refining supreme-grade third-tier pills was definitely someone any Sage-level great force would compete to invite.

Blood Nightmare Moluo's background was great, but Demon Lord Qingyang's background was also not small.

The other party also possessed this incomparably exquisite alchemy, not the slightest bit weaker than the other's Sage realm.

Which was higher and lower could be seen at a glance. The other party truly did not need to lower his head.

Swallowing, he suppressed all the greed he had held before coming and said seriously, "Fellow Daoist, are you interested in joining my Moluo Stream?"

"Not interested!"

Without the slightest hesitation!

Luo Chen immediately refused, giving the other party no face at all.

If an ordinary person were so rude, Wu Qi would certainly fly into a rage.

But before him was a master-level alchemist!

Proud because of talent, as it should be!

He did not take offense and continued inviting him warmly.

He listed one by one how enormous Moluo Stream's power was, how the Twelve Stream Lords were each awe-inspiring in all directions, and how countless broad resources lay under their jurisdiction.

There were also several hundred Core Formation cultivators, tens of thousands of true Foundation Establishment cultivators, and Qi Refinement cultivators scattered like cloud and rain.

Once he joined Moluo Stream, he would surely become chief alchemist[TERM?], with a status so honored that it could not be added to further!

Seeing that Luo Chen had become slightly moved,

he struck while the iron was hot and said, "Master Qingyang, it is a fact that you killed Yan Nantian. Although Yan Nantian was not that important, in name, he had at least joined the Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance. That is a Holy Land-level force with an Ascendant mighty figure. Are you not worried they will come trouble you?"

If tempting him with profit did not work, then lay out the harm!

Unexpectedly, Luo Chen scoffed.

"With my ability, compared to Yan Nantian, it should be the difference between the bright moon and a firefly. The cultivators of the Cang Hai Alliance are not fools. Once I reveal my alchemy attainment, will they not likewise treat me as an honored guest? Besides, from what you said, that is a force with an Ascendant mighty figure. Is it not better than Moluo Stream?"

Wu Qi was dumbfounded.

Unwilling to give up, he thought carefully again.

After a long while, he said with some uncertainty, "Everyone knows that although the Cang Hai Alliance is a righteous force, it is extremely loose. That mighty figure surnamed Li lives in seclusion and never handles affairs. Having him is the same as not having him. As for the forces opposing the Cang Hai Alliance, most either have a Primordial Demon Sect background like our Moluo Stream, or have overseas forces participating. In my judgment, this Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance will certainly not last long. Fellow Daoist, why join a force destined sooner or later to become yesterday's flower[TERM?]?"

Luo Chen raised his brow. His understanding of the great momentum of the Northern Sea cultivation world gained three more parts.

No wonder these forces without Ascendant mighty figures dared oppose the Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance.

Primordial Demon remnants were the Northern Sea's orthodoxy!

Overseas forces must be Ascendant Holy Lands from the other four continents.

In this way, it also explained why the mighty figure surnamed Li who founded the Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance lived in seclusion, ignored worldly affairs, and handed all miscellaneous alliance matters to the Sage Perfected Beings below.

He likely did not want to offend those inland Ascendant Holy Lands too deeply either.

But in that case, it was even less suitable for Luo Chen to join Moluo Stream.

When great powers contended, it was easiest for the fish in the moat to be harmed.

Seeing Luo Chen unmoved, Wu Qi's heart filled with a strong sense of defeat.

The other party's strength was no worse than his, and he also possessed outstanding alchemy attainment.

If he had no desires and no demands, then being without desire truly made one strong...

Wait!

Wu Qi recalled carefully. When he had spoken of Moluo Stream's vast resources and numerous people, the other party seemed slightly moved.

All master-level alchemists often had many followers, for them to drive at will.

This showed the nobility of master-level alchemists, but from the side, it also proved that such existences very much needed manpower to run errands for them.

Although this Demon Lord Qingyang appeared to have no desires and no demands,

if he were truly an idle cloud and wild crane, why would he have acted so decisively to kill Yan Nantian, who had no grudges or enmity with him?

What he sought was still the manpower and resources of this territory, the Swallow Islands, was it not?

After thinking this through, his entire body relaxed.

A smile once again spread across his face.

"Fellow Daoist Qingyang, joining my Moluo Stream grants many benefits. I naturally need not say more."

"But the Twelve Stream Lords are all Sage Perfected Beings. They can always produce a few portions of Nascent Soul formation insights[TERM?]. Does that truly not move your heart?"

"Of course, perhaps you fear trouble. But no matter what kind of trouble it is, it will not fall onto an alchemist. In this aspect, you need not worry."

"How about this!"

Wu Qi did not act like a nagging woman, chattering without end.

He took out a blood-red rhombus-shaped token from his storage bag.

"This is a Blood Nightmare Token[TERM?]. It is a token Blood Nightmare Demon Lord grants to those who have rendered great service to him. If you have the intention to join my Moluo Stream, ten years from now, you may hold this item and go to Feileng City to find me."

"Ten years?" Luo Chen was puzzled.

Wu Qi nodded. "I am not too certain either. In any case, around ten years. During that period, I will definitely make a trip from Shaman God Island to Feileng City. Of course, if Fellow Daoist has the intention, you may also go directly to my Shaman God Island."

As he spoke, he also gave Luo Chen a map.

At this point, he had no further lingering intent.

After exchanging a few polite words with Luo Chen, he drifted away.

Standing on Invitation Moon Island and watching the back of that escaping light as it left, Luo Chen held the blood-colored token, a rather hesitant look in his eyes.

An aged voice came from behind him.

"Tempted?"

Luo Chen did not turn his head. "A little, but I fear trouble even more."

"Cultivation will inevitably involve trouble. It cannot possibly always be smooth sailing forever."

Luo Chen shook his head. He lowered his head and carefully examined the token in his hand, then put it into his ring.

"We will speak of it later!"

"With my current ability, and your current state, I cannot withstand those troubles very well yet."

At this point, behind him, Han Zhan, hidden inside the Soul-Refining Banner, let out an awkward laugh.

"Actually, if you replenish the souls within the Myriad Souls Banner for me, this old man still has a couple of tricks."

These words received no answer.

Luo Chen had already returned to the cave residence and taken out that bottle of supreme-grade True Flame Pills.

(End of chapter)
