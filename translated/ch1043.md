# Chapter 1043: What Is Heavenly Origin, Demonic Art Dao Body

In the early morning.

The verdant branches and leaves stretched out their blades, the dewdrops condensed upon them trembling as if about to fall.

Reflected in the clear dewdrops, Fairy Qinglan stood before the Morning-Evening Palace, gazing at the breathtaking scenery of the Dao palace below the mountainside.

A voice like flowing spring water poured from her lips.

"Zhou Fuyou, do you know what Heavenly Origin is?"

Zhou Fuyou answered without hesitation: "On the go board, the central star position is called Heavenly Origin."

"Correct, but not entirely correct." Qinglan said unhurriedly, "The core of the universe, the highest status—that too is Heavenly Origin."

Zhou Fuyou was startled, then suddenly understood.

The term Heavenly Origin was indeed not merely a Go term; it had always held a lofty position in astronomy and astrology.

"Our Dao Sect takes Heavenly Origin as its name, and over the past several thousand years, it has lived up to this grand and majestic name."

"Dominating an entire continent, standing proudly as the foremost of the five sacred lands of the Mountains and Seas Realm. Even after enduring great calamities, it remains the strongest power under heaven."

"But these are not all that the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect encompasses!"

The woman's words came unhurriedly, unhurriedly, unfolding like a tale, beautiful to hear and captivating.

Zhou Fuyou had already caught on—his master had come early in the morning to give him a lesson.

And this first lesson was to introduce the sect's history, heritage, glory, and related matters.

Perhaps for many cultivators from Central Continent's immortal cultivation clans, this lesson was entirely unnecessary, but for Zhou Fuyou, Qinglan believed it was very necessary.

Because the Zhou clan had previously only been a vassal force brought in by a Star Gate guest elder, not fully integrated into the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

Under these circumstances, making the most outstanding younger generation of the Zhou clan develop cohesion with the sect was undoubtedly the best approach.

Zhou Fuyou, well-versed in classics and historical texts, understood this method very well.

But he was still shocked by what Qinglan said next.

"Above this realm, there is another realm called the Mystic Realm. There too, there is a Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. Within it are countless experts, unending great cultivators and true lords, and we are a part of that Mystic Realm Dao Sect."

Qinglan turned around, her gaze falling on the astonished Zhou Fuyou, satisfied with his reaction.

"Master, then how did we come down?"

Qinglan smiled: "According to the sect's ancient records, our Dao Sect, including the other four sacred lands of the Mountains and Seas Realm, all descended from the Mystic Realm to the Mountains and Seas in ancient times. Then our ancestors blazed trails through thorns and brambles, opening up this realm step by step into the human immortal cultivation world."

"Descended?"

"Yes, through a spirit treasure!"

Zhou Fuyou's heart tightened, inspiration striking, "Could it be the spirit treasure you mentioned yesterday that can summon many great cultivator avatars to descend to this lower realm?"

"Mm, the Star Gate." Qinglan walked slowly back and forth before the Morning-Evening Palace, one hand behind her back, explaining the doubts to this disciple: "The Star Gate is a spatial spirit treasure, but not merely a spatial spirit treasure. It has no offensive power, no defensive ability. Its function is to roam the universe, shuttle between two realms, more like the flying treasures we commonly use."

Speaking thus, she pointed distantly in a certain direction.

"For millions of years, that gate has stood atop Heaven-Earth Peak, connecting the Mountains and Seas Realm and the Mystic Realm, allowing us to maintain simple contact with the main sect in the upper realm."

Zhou Fuyou stopped walking and also looked in that direction.

That was once the core area of Central Continent—Heaven-Earth Peak!

But now, Heaven-Earth Peak had collapsed.

And...

"Where is the Star Gate now?"

A trace of gloom appeared on Qinglan's face, "I heard it has been taken. The demon phoenix's attack on Central Continent was for the Star Gate. Even in the view of some senior brothers and sisters in the sect, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's deployment of troops everywhere, attacking the other four continents, all had the demon phoenix's shadow. It was precisely because our strength was divided five ways, experts scattered, that Central Continent fell into emptiness, and the demon phoenix seized the opportunity to take the treasure."

The next moment, Qinglan shook her head slightly, shaking off this dejection.

"But our Dao Sect's foundation is so deep that even having lost the Star Gate, two of the three sect treasures remain intact, still enough to intimidate the world!"

At this moment, however, Zhou Fuyou's thoughts were not only on these matters.

He could not yet speak of sect cohesion.

He was merely curious about one thing.

"Your disciple knows that over the past three thousand years in Central Continent, many great cultivators have ascended, and even a hundred years ago, the Alchemy Saint ascended with rosy clouds under the gaze of countless people. I am very curious—when the Star Gate still existed, did these Dao Sect predecessors fly to the legendary Immortal Realm, or to the Mystic Realm where the main sect resides?"

This question was somewhat abrupt, but Qinglan was not flustered. She smiled slightly.

"Naturally, the Mystic Realm."

Zhou Fuyou frowned.

The Immortal Realm of myth and legend was a place everyone dreamed of.

The Dao Sect predecessors did not go to the Immortal Realm but flew to the Mystic Realm—what was the reasoning?

However, his master did not explain in detail, merely mentioning one sentence.

"You will naturally know in the future."

Having said this, Qinglan once again stepped forward, leading Zhou Fuyou into the main hall of the Morning-Evening Palace.

She sat generously in the seat of honor.

Facing Zhou Fuyou, who stood tall and upright below, she formed a Daoist hand seal.

A green light gently enveloped him.

Zhou Fuyou showed a surprised expression, feeling a vast amount of spiritual energy surging toward him with incomparable affinity.

This scene was not unfamiliar.

A few years ago, at the Spirit Testing Ceremony, it had been the same.

Clearly, his master was personally testing his aptitude.

"Just as I thought, a water-attribute heavenly spirit root!"

As the probing continued, the surprise on Qinglan's face grew deeper and deeper, until later turning into delight.

In the end, she withdrew the green light, and around Zhou Fuyou, a mass of dense, dark black spiritual energy had already gathered.

"Your innate Dao body is the Profound Nether Dao Body recorded in the classics!"

Zhou Fuyou asked curiously: "Our clan's ancestor also said this, but your disciple has perused many classics and found no detailed records of this Dao body. I wonder what its advantages and disadvantages truly are?"

Qinglan smiled: "There are many advantages. This Dao body greatly enhances your cultivation efficiency, and can even automatically exclude the interference of other spiritual energies, saving you the effort of exhausting your spirit and energy during cultivation."

Just this first point was something countless people dreamed of!

In heaven and earth, there were countless free-floating spiritual energies, mixed with various attributes.

When cultivators breathed and refined qi, they often had to expend great mental effort.

During the Qi Refinement stage, they absorbed everything in one go, then bit by bit refined, transformed, and purified it, finally turning it into spiritual power suitable for themselves.

After the Initiate stage, cultivators could release their spiritual sense, identifying and selecting spiritual energies of corresponding attributes from the outside during absorption, thus improving efficiency somewhat.

Clearly, Zhou Fuyou, possessing the Profound Nether Dao Body, could directly bypass these processes.

As long as the spiritual vein grade was high enough and the spiritual energy concentrated enough, he could simply breathe in large quantities.

From his master's mouth, Zhou Fuyou also learned other advantages of the Profound Nether Dao Body.

Those with this constitution often had meridians several times wider than ordinary people, allowing them to circulate mana with great force and high output efficiency.

The same applied to dantian acupoints, the places where mana was stored.

This also perfectly corroborated the qi sea, like a bottomless abyss, that Zhou Fuyou had opened when he established his foundation!

Additionally, the Profound Nether Dao Body had a certain resistance to potent toxins and filthy qi, making it a relatively rare "hundred poisons impervious" constitution. If one used this constitution to cultivate certain poison-dispelling methods, the effects would be outstanding.

Zhou Fuyou thought back to his childhood, when his seventh aunt had tried to harm him with "Spirit-Strengthening Soup," and his sister had reacted violently after mistakenly consuming it, while he had no reaction at all. Even after vomiting it out later, he felt nothing.

Clearly, his master was correct.

"As for disadvantages, I am not too clear, because this constitution appears very rarely."

"Among water-type spiritual constitutions, the most common are low-grade Jade Water Body, Ninefold Winding, Cold Soul Body, and so on. High-grade ones are Innate Water Spirit Body, Yellow Spring Dao Body, and Grand Yin True Water Body."

"A constitution like your Profound Nether Dao Body is almost as rare as the ultimate Water Origin Dao Embryo—extremely scarce, seen once in tens of thousands of years."

Zhou Fuyou was astonished. He had not imagined that even cultivator constitutions had so many distinctions.

What shocked him even more was the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's accumulation of knowledge in this area.

From the fact that his master could casually name so many constitutions, one could see the depth of the Dao Sect's foundation.

After examining Zhou Fuyou's innate constitution, Qinglan also began preparing for his future cultivation.

"What cultivation art did you practice before?"

Zhou Fuyou did not hesitate and directly told the truth.

"The Dew Cicada Art."

Qinglan was momentarily bewildered, but when Zhou Fuyou added "a Northern Sea demonic art," she suddenly reacted.

"A speed-cultivation method?"

The woman's brows visibly furrowed.

"No wonder you are only twenty-one yet have already established your foundation early, and even displayed the mana fluctuations of late Initiate stage—you took such a shortcut."

"Who taught you?"

"Could it be Fellow Daoist Zhou Yunshen?"

Zhou Fuyou bowed respectfully, "Please do not blame the ancestor, Master. This art was my own choice when I was young; no one else is to blame."

Qinglan's delicate brows remained tightly furrowed, "I thought Fellow Daoist Zhou would not be so unwise. This kind of demonic art, though extremely fast in cultivation speed, has great defects and hidden dangers. With your talent, your cultivation speed is already very fast. You could completely practice some arts that are slower in cultivation but produce vigorous mana and a solid foundation. That way, you could fully develop your constitution and have higher potential!"

As she spoke, she waved her hand, and five streams of light floated in the great hall.

Zhou Fuyou looked at them; each stream of light revealed dazzling characters, clearly some high-grade cultivation arts.

Three of them he even recognized—they were precisely the ones given to him by Dean Mo Wanting of the Jade Measure Academy before she left.

"These are the five best water-type cultivation arts of our Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, each with the potential to directly reach the Ascendant stage. While you have not yet formed your core, switch to a destined art!"

However, Zhou Fuyou refused.

"Master, I feel the Dew Cicada Art suits me well. I do not need to switch."

Qinglan said displeasedly, "Do you only care about progress and not about your foundation?"

"But your disciple's foundation is not bad, is it?" Zhou Fuyou countered.

As he spoke, the vast mana fluctuations on his body manifested again.

This time, Qinglan was surprised once more.

In truth, earlier at the Heavenly Origin Observatory, they had all observed Zhou Fuyou's mana situation, and had only been surprised at his high realm.

But now, knowing that he had only practiced the Dew Cicada Art, that surprise turned into shock.

To cultivate such abundant mana with a demonic art known for sparse mana—this defied common sense.

"Could it be because of the Profound Nether Dao Body?"

Qinglan was utterly perplexed.

A constitution was merely a talent, allowing one to accommodate more things and learn more things during growth, but it would not produce more things for no reason.

Zhou Fuyou's situation was a bit too bizarre.

The only explanation was that the Profound Nether Dao Body was extremely compatible with the Dew Cicada Art, using innate advantages to compensate for the acquired defects of the art.

"Do you insist on cultivating the Dew Cicada Art?" Suppressing the shock in her heart, Qinglan asked solemnly.

Zhou Fuyou was equally solemn, "I insist! My only request is that Master obtain for me the subsequent cultivation methods of the Dew Cicada Art for the Adept stage and beyond."

He trusted his own feelings and the judgment of his clan ancestor.

Just as his master had said, the Profound Nether Dao Body appeared far too rarely, and the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect had no relevant examples.

Since that was the case, who could be more aware of their own situation than the person themselves?

Qinglan scrutinized him carefully for a long while, then nodded slightly.

"Very well, I will apply for the subsequent methods of the Dew Cicada Art from Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain. But I will also observe your cultivation progress. Once problems arise, you must listen to your master and immediately switch to a destined art!"

"Additionally, this art is not skilled in combat. Our path of cultivation also requires means of protecting the Dao."

"Legend has it that the Profound Nether Dao Body is a constitution naturally suited to bearing Nether Water. If you can command Nether Water in battle, you will have few opponents at the same level."

Hope also shone in Zhou Fuyou's eyes.

He knew the great name of "Nether Water" from the classics.

"Master, is it the Nether Water controlled by the Nether Origin Sect in the Eastern Wilderness?"

"Yes!"

Qinglan nodded slightly, a momentary daze on her face.

She thought of her grandfather who had perished in the Nether Origin Sect, and of that man in the Eastern Wilderness, and now the disciple she had personally taken on would also control Nether Water.

In the unseen, was there some fate?

She subconsciously glanced at Zhou Fuyou, suddenly understanding why she had felt a moment of warm familiarity when first seeing him.

But it was not him!

Zhou Fuyou was a legitimate member of the Zhou clan; from childhood to present, everything was traceable, with no connection to that man.

Moreover, one had a strong, imposing appearance, the other was handsome and delicate—they were completely unrelated.

"Perhaps I am overthinking."

Sighing, Qinglan prepared to leave.

Today she had come for three things: to increase her disciple's understanding and cohesion with the sect, to grasp Zhou Fuyou's specific situation, and to arrange his future cultivation direction.

But before leaving, she asked one thing curiously.

"Why did you ultimately choose me as your master?"

Zhou Fuyou was stunned for a moment, then said naturally, "Everyone outside says you are one of the few in the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect most likely to break through to the Ascendant stage. Your disciple naturally wanted a great cultivator as a master!"

"Heh."

Qinglan shook her head. This disciple was indeed candid, and the reason was well known.

After giving a few simple instructions, Qinglan drifted away.

Before the gate of the Morning-Evening Palace, Zhou Fuyou saw her off, then calmed his mind.

The reason for choosing a master?

What he had just said was indeed one reason.

The other was that Zhou Fuyou subconsciously did not want to be too close to others.

Qinglan was young, outstanding in talent, and had comprehended the true meaning of laws. To break through to Ascendant, she would inevitably devote most of her time to bitter cultivation.

Thus, her guidance of him would not be too frequent.

If he had chosen one of the other three hall masters, he feared it would not be so peaceful.

"With the ancestor's half-year of close guidance, he has already clarified all the barriers from Initiate to Adept for me. Now, with the generous spiritual energy environment of the Morning-Evening Palace, I will devote myself fully to cultivation, striving to complete the Initiate stage and break through to the Adept stage as soon as possible!"

The white-robed man murmured to himself, his eyes filled with strong confidence.

Qi Refinement and Initiate were merely the two most insignificant minor realms on the path of cultivation, counting for little in the immortal cultivation world.

Others now thought highly of him only because of his talent and potential.

Only by forming an Adept core could he have a certain say.

At that time, he would legitimately take charge of the Zhou clan, and also have the opportunity to bring his sister to the Dao palace for cultivation.

As for other matters, Zhou Fuyou did not care much.

(Chapter End)
