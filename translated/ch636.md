# Chapter 636: Arranging the Formation to Kill Demons, Controlling the Flower Island

"So this is the Purple Spirit Sea Region?"

A huge python-jiaolong slowly surfaced from beneath the sea where undercurrents surged.

On his black back, four figures sat cross-legged.

Luo Chen looked at the purple fog drifting over the sea surface and could not help frowning.

Beneath him, Black King gave a low voice. "It was not like this when I came the first time. At that time, this purple qi poison miasma covered only Purple Spirit Island. Half a month ago, when I brought everyone here, we discovered that the purple qi poison miasma had begun spreading from the island and covering this sea region."

Duan Li beside him added, "Not only that, some low-level demon fish nearby seem to have been bewitched and have begun being full of aggression toward strangers."

Luo Chen was puzzled. His understanding of Purple Monkey Flowers was limited to classics, and he seemed never to have heard that they possessed such strong spirituality!

Sang Jiugong, proficient in spirit plant cultivation, gave an explanation at this moment.

"Purple Spirit Island is very large, and it has many Purple Monkey Flowers. This kind of situation where a group clusters together often produces an extremely strong leader. Purple Monkey Flowers themselves only attack with floral fragrance poison. Once one escapes the coverage range of their flower poison, there is not much danger."

"Last time, Black King escaped Purple Spirit Island, which likely gave these muddled Purple Monkey Flowers a sense of crisis, so they deliberately expanded the coverage range of the flower poison."

Muddled?

The three looked at one another. With such intelligence, could it not be considered awakened?

Luo Chen compared them to the Withered Blood Vine King on Withered Wood Ridge. In comparison, Purple Monkey Flowers were still weaker by a level and still needed to bewitch other creatures to fight for them. They themselves did not have much power.

However, one was fourth-tier and the other fifth-tier. A difference was very normal.

"Master, what should we do next? Directly kill our way onto the island?" Black King asked.

Luo Chen waved his hand. "No hurry. Let us secretly land and investigate first."

Both were landing on the island, but directly killing their way up and going up secretly were still different.

Most directly, they would not come into conflict with the island's creatures at the first moment.

Luo Chen pointed at his own body, and the Concealment Array fully activated, covering his aura to the extreme.

Looking at the other three, they also began using qi-concealing methods.

After placing a talisman on herself, Lord Lushan looked at Luo Chen with some surprise.

"Fellow Daoist's qi-concealing method is truly exquisite. Not only are the movements swift beyond compare, even the aura concealment is this marvelous."

At this moment, Luo Chen stood before the three. If not for his physical body being here, no one would be able to detect his existence.

Luo Chen smiled lightly. His qi-concealing method was naturally exquisite.

Engraving an entire third-tier grand formation onto his body was equivalent to using a whole formation to conceal him.

Without advance targeting, it would be extremely difficult to discover his movements.

Not long ago, in the battle against the Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft, the other party had suffered this hidden loss.

While Luo Chen frantically cast Great Azure Yang Handprints, he silently launched the Treasure-Dropping Copper Coin, and the other party did not detect it at all.

By the time the other party reacted, the Treasure-Dropping Copper Coin had already struck his body-protecting shield.

Thus, it broke the stalemate and allowed Luo Chen to gain the initiative.

Speaking of this, one had to mention Luo Chen's rich battle experience and keen battle awareness.

The Treasure-Dropping Copper Coin was only a lower-grade magic treasure, and its own power was actually rather ordinary.

Back on Cold Light Island, the cultivator holding it had used it against Luo Chen and achieved nothing.

Yet in Luo Chen's hands, it could use weakness to defeat strength, striking a flaw into a defensive shield at the upper-grade level.

From this, one could know that a magic treasure's power did not depend only on its own grade, but also on how its controller used it.

"Let us go!"

Luo Chen spoke and flew toward the island covered in purple fog, the three behind following.

Black King, under their gaze, slowly sank beneath the sea surface.

...

"The island is over seventy li long and around forty li wide. Its overall terrain is concave inward, like a basin. There are tall mountains on the island's side, blocking the sea wind, and also creating the strange sight of poison fog lingering without scattering."

"I imagine Purple Spirit Island can have this many Purple Monkey Flowers reproducing because of this unique geographical environment."

"If one wants to arrange a formation here, one can use the Inverted Jade Bowl Grand Formation. This does not differ much from the previous deduction."

Piece after piece of information was calculated in Luo Chen's heart.

As his eyes shone brightly, Luo Chen still carefully paid attention to whether the island had any hidden methods.

After learning that the news of Purple Monkey Flower was deliberately released by the Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft to lure the snake from the cave, he had carried an added worry toward Purple Spirit Island throughout the journey.

Now, after observing it on site, that worry finally dispersed.

For the sake of the information's authenticity, the Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft truly had not done anything to Purple Spirit Island.

Of course, it was also possible that he had been unable to.

In the short half day just now, Luo Chen had already sensed how formidable the purple qi poison miasma on the island was. Fortunately, he had long prepared the improved fourth-tier Poison-Resisting Pills.

Because of this, he could move unhindered through Purple Spirit Island.

After ascertaining Purple Spirit Island's overall terrain, Luo Chen did not stop moving, but walked once each in the four directions of east, south, west, and north.

Everywhere he went, he took out the Sealing Divinity Compass to check the earth qi circulation and spirit vein distribution.

If he wanted to arrange a grand formation, relying only on his own resources would consume too much.

Only by combining geographical advantage could he achieve twice the result with half the effort!

At the same time, he also had to pay attention to controlling the formation's power to a certain degree, not drawing spiritual qi without restraint and ruining this spirit island where Purple Monkey Flowers grew.

Like this, only when the setting sun's afterglow scattered did he leave Purple Spirit Island and meet with the other three.

Duan Li was the first to report the investigation situation.

"There are seventeen third-tier demon beasts in all, all early third-tier, and they are scattered in distribution, not worth worrying about. Among them, Purple Qi Poison Monkeys are the most numerous, eleven in total."

"There are not many first-tier and second-tier beings. Most are apes and monkeys, dependent beneath the Purple Qi Poison Monkeys."

"However, we must pay attention to an Abyss Crocodile that can live both in water and on land. Its scales and armor are quite hard and can withstand late Golden Core attacks. If we truly fight this beast, we must consider the range of destruction."

Everyone quietly listened. Afterward, Lord Lushan also reported the investigation situation one by one.

However, her side was not important.

The purpose of inviting her to travel with them was only the talismans she made.

Most important was Sang Jiugong!

"Elder Sang, what about your side?"

Under Luo Chen's gaze, Sang Jiugong coughed dryly, cleared his throat, and slowly spoke.

"I have roughly understood the growth distribution of Purple Monkey Flowers."

"In the central region's hollow, there are only three plants, but they are the three highest-grade plants among all the Purple Monkey Flowers on the entire island. In my observation, their growth years are at least over a thousand, and they already have fourth-tier level."

"In addition, on a cliff face, I also discovered one fourth-tier Purple Monkey Flower with no poor appearance."

Luo Chen frowned. "Only four plants in all?"

He had studied the Nascent Soul Pill formula for many years and was long familiar with the medicinal ratios and portions of every medicinal material.

Apart from the Five Elements Lotus Platform being usable in medicine multiple times, Purple Monkey Flower and Divine Iris both belonged to the kind where one plant could only be refined once.

Four plants meant he had only four chances to refine the Nascent Soul Pill.

This number was far too few compared to Azure Pill Valley back then.

Although comparing an individual with a sect's foundation was somewhat unsuitable, Luo Chen naturally hoped the more raw materials, the better.

Seeing Luo Chen worried, Sang Jiugong smiled.

"Although fourth-tier ones are few, the number of third-tier Purple Monkey Flowers is truly enormous!"

"I did not count them in detail, but with only a rough estimate, there are fully over one hundred. Combined with countless first-tier and second-tier Purple Monkey Flowers, this Purple Spirit Island can indeed be called the kingdom of Purple Monkey Flowers."

Lord Lushan beside them was somewhat curious. "But what Daoist Azure Yang needs are fourth-tier Purple Monkey Flowers. Are these low-tier ones useful?"

"Useful, of course they are useful!" Sang Jiugong said with certainty.

"With my ripening secret method, combined with the Purple Monkey Flower's growth habits, as long as I am given fifty years, I can cultivate these one hundred-plus third-tier Purple Monkey Flowers to the fourth-tier level."

Lord Lushan was greatly shocked, disbelief appearing on her clear and beautiful face.

"That formidable?"

Sang Jiugong chuckled, but in a flash, he solemnly said to Luo Chen, "However, once they are ripened according to my method, it will cause a destructive blow to this place's Purple Monkey Flower ecology. Daoist Azure Yang, you should consider it carefully."

Luo Chen waved his great sleeve and calmly said, "I only need fourth-tier ones, no fewer than ten flowers. Anything extra can be regarded as your elder's compensation."

Sang Jiugong's eyes brightened, and he subconsciously rubbed his hands.

An ancient poison flower like Purple Monkey Flower was not known by ordinary people, but as a spirit planter proficient in spirit plant cultivation, he knew how formidable it was.

Especially third-tier and fourth-tier ones.

Once formed, they could be sold to high-level cultivators who raised poison-attribute demon beasts, exchanging for large amounts of spirit stones.

If Luo Chen truly kept this promise, then this trip would not count as coming for nothing!

He had both repaid the favor and obtained benefits, while also drawing closer to Daoist Azure Yang. Was that not wonderful?

"In that case, I will carefully tell everyone the places to pay attention to later."

Sang Jiugong took out the rough map he had drawn before and pointed to each place.

"Here, this must be avoided during battle. Fourth-tier Purple Monkey Flowers grow inside."

"This place must also be noted. The terrain is too fragile and easily affected by battle aftermath."

"And this hill. Although no high-tier Purple Monkey Flowers grow there, such a large number of low-tier Purple Monkey Flowers is extremely important for my later ripening. Do not let too many be damaged."

"Here... here..."

"All right, that is about it. As for the other scattered low-tier Purple Monkey Flowers, if they are destroyed, then they are destroyed. It will not affect the overall situation."

Sang Jiugong swallowed to moisten his dry throat.

Luo Chen nodded. "Elder, you were thoughtful."

Then, he looked toward Lord Lushan.

The other party realized something and nodded to him.

"I have already prepared ten third-tier Spirit-Suppressing Talismans. For the extra places, I will use other talismans to protect them. I will make sure it does not affect the overall situation."

"Then I will trouble Fellow Daoist when the time comes." Luo Chen cupped his hands, then finally looked toward Duan Li.

The latter solemnly took out a flower basket from his storage bag and delivered it before Luo Chen.

Duan Li instructed, "This treasure is an upper-grade magic treasure inherited by my Duan family. It has dual earth and wood attributes and excels at breaking formations and spatial restrictions. If you want to reverse its use and arrange a formation with it to protect the earth vein, it is not impossible. But you must pay attention: this treasure is slightly damaged, and I have not had time to repair it."

"Damaged?" Luo Chen frowned. "Why did you not say so earlier?"

Duan Li was helpless. "This treasure was damaged in one petal during the battle against that white-robed cultivator of the Primordial Demon Sect last time. Later, I was busy recovering from injuries and doing missions, and truly had no time to repair it. And the time you notified me to come to Purple Spirit Island was too urgent, where would there be time to repair it? But rest assured, a little damage does not affect this treasure's overall power."

After hearing this, Luo Chen carefully examined it and ultimately nodded in acknowledgment.

It indeed did not affect it much.

And with his artifact-forging attainments, repairing this treasure would not be difficult. Adding some wood-attribute materials to mend it, then nourishing it for some time, would restore it to its original state.

"After this matter is completed, I will find time to personally repair this treasure for you."

Duan Li froze, then was overjoyed.

"In that case, many thanks, Brother Luo!"

Luo Chen hummed, his gaze looking toward the deep night and the purple fog drifting over the sea.

Wait until dawn!

...

They were similarly gathering materials as a group, but Luo Chen's party and Angler's group had completely different methods.

The latter did not care at all about destructiveness to the local environment and only sought to take down the fifth-tier Withered Blood Vine's main body. They even wanted to make as much commotion as possible, forcing the Vine King to recover Withered Wood Ridge's vitality and make the Vine King's main body more spiritual.

The former, however, had to be extremely cautious, striving to preserve Purple Spirit Island's original appearance as much as possible so the Purple Monkey Flowers here could continue growing.

The same goal, different methods, and this caused a world of difference in the process of action.

When the rising sun climbed high and sunlight struggled through the purple qi poison miasma, spilling over Purple Spirit Island.

Luo Chen's group began to act.

Lord Lushan was alone, heading to the places Sang Jiugong had marked. Every place she reached, she arranged talismans to protect the local Purple Monkey Flowers.

Luo Chen, the other three, and Black King rushed toward the four directions of east, south, west, and north.

During this process, Luo Chen handed to Duan Li, Sang Jiugong, and the others the formation flags and formation plates he had frantically refined during the months before departure.

As for Luo Chen himself, he went straight to the valley at the very center of Purple Spirit Island.

Before the three fourth-tier Purple Monkey Flowers in the valley, which trembled frantically, he took out two objects.

One was the Sealing Divinity Compass, which he drove into the depths of the ground.

The other was Duan Li's flower basket, which went straight into the sky.

When voice transmissions entered his ears from the four directions, he rose into the air under the gazes of third-tier demon beasts rushing over from all directions.

"Formation, rise!"

With a low shout, his sleeves billowed without wind, and pure spiritual power surged in all directions.

The Sealing Divinity Compass deep underground began spinning at high speed, drawing the earth-vein qi from the four directions.

In the four directions of east, south, west, and north, with the aid of one hundred and sixty formation flags, ten thousand rays of light emerged and finally gathered above Luo Chen.

In the sky.

The gorgeous flower basket magic treasure opened petal after petal, slowly hanging upside down.

Its main body constantly enlarged, and it even transformed a phantom image, finally seeming to become an inverted porcelain bowl, completely covering the entire Purple Spirit Island.

The Inverted Jade Bowl Grand Formation took shape!

During this process, demon beasts launched attack after attack at Luo Chen, but under the fully activated Xuanchen Armor's protection, they could not injure him in the slightest.

Even if there were physical attacks forming powerful rebound force, Luo Chen's formidable physique could ignore them.

After the formation took shape, Luo Chen rushed toward the northeast without thinking.

Behind him, more and more demon beasts gathered into a small beast tide and pursued.

A moment later, Luo Chen descended onto a plain where sparse Purple Monkey Flowers grew, turned back to look at this beast tide, and smiled faintly.

"Fellow Daoists, let us act together!"

The beast tide that had come chasing Luo Chen suddenly became the surrounded side after Lord Lushan, Black King, and the others revealed themselves in other directions.

Although their number was many, at this moment, they instead seemed even weaker.

There was no hesitation. After Luo Chen gave the order, the simplest, purest attacks came from each person's hands.

For a time, blades and swords flashed, mana surged, and the entire Purple Spirit Island seemed about to fall apart.

Yet under the suppression of the upside-down flower basket in the sky, everything was as steady as Mount Tai.

*(End of Chapter)*
