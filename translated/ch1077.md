# Chapter 1077: Central Continent War, Reunion with Heaven and Earth

"Who are you people?"

A sharp cry of anger demanded an answer from the newcomers.

An Initiate Sage guarding a coastal formation node looked at the approaching figures and asked instinctively.

Wang Yuan, charging at the very front, neither replied nor answered. His entire body erupted with astral qi as he rushed forward.

Seeing the situation was wrong, that Initiate Sage sent a flying sword shooting out.

Wang Yuan punched it with his bare hand!

*Clang!*

Fist and sword clashed, producing the sound of metal striking metal.

The enemy's eyes widened in disbelief. Bare-handed resistance against his true-artifact-grade flying sword?

"Senior Brother Wang Yuan, are you alright?" A concerned inquiry came from behind.

It was Jadewater Dragon.

Wang Yuan didn't look back. "Don't stop! Destroy every formation node you can, prevent the Dao Sect disciples from repairing them, and don't let them reach Heaven-Earth Island!"

As he spoke, his divine will erupted, firmly locking onto the enemy's qi signature.

Behind him, the Luo Tian Sect cultivators had already descended upon the Eastern Origin Continent.

Jadewater Dragon joined forces with Haoranzi, while Lingfengzi rode the wind.

*Thump! Thump! Thump!*

A massive White Tiger Puppet strode proudly across the land, with Canglong, white as snow, standing atop it.

"Fellow disciples, if you encounter danger, remember to activate the fourth-rank talismans bestowed by Senior Brother Luo Chen! Don't be stingy!"

When the Initiate Sage entangled by Wang Yuan saw this scene, his heart and gall trembled violently. What he saw in his line of sight was not just these few from Luo Tian Sect.

In the distance, a giant turtle was landing, a black dragon was ascending to the sky, and a seductive Ghost King drifted forward.

In other directions as well, streaks of powerful light were charging into the Eastern Origin Continent.

After the trembling of heart and gall came uncontrollable rage.

"How dare you offend the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect! You deserve to die!!!"

Wang Yuan's expression was cold. "Come, let Wang see what the disciples of the number one sacred land under heaven can do!"

Without further words, a life-and-death battle unfolded between the two.

Powerful dharma power and astral qi exploded in all directions. The low-rank cultivators nearby simply couldn't intervene.

This great war was not merely a conflict between Luo Chen and the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, but a war between the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world and the Central Continent cultivation world.

The battlefield was divided according to various Eastern Wilderness sect forces, unfolding like pieces placed on a chessboard across the Eastern Origin Continent.

Heavenly Origin Dao Sect cultivators rushing to reinforce were constantly intercepted by powerful experts from all sides along the way.

As Luo Chen's own sect, Luo Tian Sect naturally had to lead the charge and be at the forefront.

Wang Yuan and the others fulfilled this task admirably, fearless of life or death, being the first to kill their way into the Eastern Origin Continent.

Naturally, the resistance they encountered was also the fiercest.

Wang Yuan's situation was manageable. Since he wasn't a qi refiner and his realm couldn't be measured by Adept or Sage standards, his first opponent wasn't too strong—merely an early-stage Sage.

But other places weren't so "easy."

Especially where Jadewater Dragon was.

Three massive white beams of light struck in rapid succession.

Jadewater Dragon unfolded her Sage domain, trying to slow down these attacks, but with little effect.

In haste, she could only deploy defensive measures.

*Boom!*

Just one beam of light heavily wounded her.

Seeing the two beams following closely, despair crept into Jadewater Dragon's eyes.

Just then.

"Dodge!"

A bamboo scroll unfurled before her, blocking one beam of light. Then, a surge of righteous qi erupted violently, and within it, a fire phoenix spiraled out, attacking the newcomer.

A figure stood before Jadewater Dragon—it was Haoranzi, who had been imprisoned with her on Green Parasol Mountain for two hundred years.

"Junior Sister, be careful. There are too many Sage cultivators on this battlefield now, and the dharma power and spiritual qi are chaotically mixed. Sage domains can no longer be used normally. Everything depends on your own offensive and defensive abilities."

Haoranzi said this, his gaze solemnly fixed on the approaching figure.

A mid-stage Sage cultivator.

Elsewhere, Lingfengzi was also locked in a bitter struggle. Fortunately, his escape speed was extremely fast, and he had mastered the killing move "Wind Cave," barely holding on without being defeated.

As for Canglong, in a short time, she had already broken through a formation maintained by over a hundred people.

Facing the Dao Sect Sage who came at her, she was unhurried. The light of the Canghai Zhenlong Sword in her hand flashed, and the talismans bestowed by Luo Chen were activated from time to time, firmly suppressing the Dao Sect Sage.

The late-stage Sage White Tiger Puppet beneath her feet strolled leisurely toward the next formation node.

In the sky.

Dozens of Eastern Wilderness human and demon experts did not stop. Since the Luo Tian Sect cultivators had already torn open the defensive line, they only needed to drive straight in.

For so many years, the Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion had only one Sage—Lin Qingxuan, who had absorbed the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect back then.

Whether the Pavilion's failure to produce a second Sage afterward was due to Lin Qingxuan's deliberate suppression was unknown, but on this battlefield, he stood alone, sword in hand.

A figure with the bearing of a mountain, possessing the demeanor of a grandmaster, stood before a formation node.

Lin Qingxuan's sword pellet orbited beside him. He knew the newcomer was no easy opponent, and his gaze was solemn.

"Your name!"

The newcomer flipped his sleeve, and a long sword fell into his hand. "Liu family, Liu Yuan!"

Lin Qingxuan flicked his finger, and the sword pellet radiated countless sword lights.

"Lin Qingxuan of Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion. Please enlighten me!"

...

Tao Wan's heart was somewhat uneasy. This was her first battle since advancing to the Sage realm, and one she had never imagined.

The scale of combat involved on both sides was no less than the racial war between Eastern Wilderness humans and demons back then. In fact, due to the excessive concentration of Sage experts, it was even more dangerous.

But she had to come for this battle.

Back then, Luo Chen had acted for her, seizing the Dao Seed competition of the Azure Pill Valley and ensuring her safe breakthrough to the Adept realm.

Later, being able to luckily bid for a Nascent Soul Pill at the Lingtian Auction was not unrelated to Deng Taiyue's consideration of her relationship with Luo Chen.

Therefore, even if it weren't for the Ascension opportunity of the Enlightenment Pill, she still had to come and lend Luo Chen a hand.

However, the slaughter on the Sage battlefield was more brutal than she had imagined!

A single streak of fire rose from the sky, tearing through her defenses and heavily wounding her.

A figure like a flame demon slowly rose from the ground, filled with rage.

"Where is Luo Chen?"

Tao Wan couldn't answer. Smashed to the ground, she barely recovered before spitting out a large mouthful of blood.

*Pfft!*

Her entire dharma power felt as if it had been ignited, boiling and restless, on the verge of exploding.

She looked at the person who had appeared, her expression terrified.

"Shi Ju!"

It was the Dao Sect prodigy who had once gone to the Eastern Wilderness, now the head of the Shi family, Shi Ju!

Rumor had it that he had comprehended the true meaning of laws ahead of time and cultivated the Dao Sect's divine abilities, not losing even when facing late-stage Great Demon Emperors.

How could someone like her contend with such a person?

That single palm strike just now had nearly instantly killed her.

Or rather, it wasn't that he couldn't do it, but that he deliberately spared her life to ask about Luo Chen's whereabouts.

But Tao Wan couldn't answer, nor did she want to.

She could only watch helplessly as Shi Ju stepped through the air, walking toward her step by step, his expression ferocious and furious.

*Swish! Swish!*

Two figures flew over, blocking Shi Ju from left and right.

Tao Wan wiped the blood from the corner of her mouth and staggered into the air, looking at the two backs and saying with difficulty, "Changxi, Meng Ye, it's you."

Shi Ju continued forward as if no one else was there.

"You can't stop me. Even if the three of you join forces, you are no match for me. Tell me, where is Luo Chen? I know he has already come!"

That palm print that had crossed the sea and nearly pierced through half the continent earlier—he would never mistake it.

It was the signature technique of the Wilderness Wanderer Luo Chen—the Azure Yang Great Handprint!

Changxi's expression was calm as she held her sword, guarding Tao Wan.

"Your opponent is not us. It's Senior Brother Jiuling."

Shi Ju suddenly looked up!

In the void, a dreamlike river of light was flowing slowly.

Within it, the figure of Jiuling Yuanjun was faintly visible.

"You are not worthy of being Luo Chen's opponent. Let me spar with you!"

Shi Ju grinned ferociously. "Fine. Once I kill you all, Luo Chen will eventually appear."

As his words fell, he shot into the sky, his Solar True Flame erupting as he forcibly charged into the Great Nine Heavens River.

After he left, Changxi and the other two breathed a sigh of relief.

Shi Ju was a true Dao Sect prodigy, absolutely not someone they could contend with.

Even ordinary Great Cultivators might not be able to suppress Shi Ju.

Perhaps only Jiuling Yuanjun, once hailed as the number one under the Great Cultivators, could fight him.

"Then what about us?" Tao Wan asked.

Changxi smiled slightly, her gaze falling on the immortal city in the distance.

There happened to be a formation node inside.

But this smile quickly faded.

Because within the immortal city, figures were appearing one after another. Although they were only at the Adept realm, relying on the city's geographical advantage and the assistance of formations, their killing power was enough to threaten Sage cultivators.

"Kill!"

...

Meanwhile, beside a large lake.

Four figures descended from the sky.

The aged Tianduzi walked at the front.

By the lakeside, two Dao Sect Sages stood their ground without any retreat, blocking their path.

"Senior Brother, leave these two to us!" Tianquanzi said so.

Tianduzi nodded, his steps not pausing as he headed toward the lake.

Tianquanzi led two newly advanced Sage junior brothers, deploying a formation to envelop the Dao Sect Sages.

Tianduzi didn't even look at the outcome of this battle, silently gazing at the calm lake.

After a moment, his eyes flickered, and he pointed a finger.

*Crack!*

A wisp of dharma power struck deep into the lake. Then, ripples spread, and spiritual light bloomed.

*Bang!*

Like a dream bubble, the lake shattered in an instant. Deep at the bottom, formation patterns were clearly visible.

However, the light of these formation patterns had already gradually dimmed.

This formation node was broken by him with a flip of his hand.

Tianduzi didn't leave immediately. Instead, he carefully examined those dim formation patterns. After a long while, a thoughtful expression appeared on his face.

"Although it's a sixth-rank formation, it's composed of individual fourth-rank formations, forming fifth-rank grand formations on each continent, and then all combined to create the Heaven-Vault Continent-Gathering Formation."

"Indeed. With cultivators of this world not reaching the Void Refining stage, how could they easily arrange a sixth-rank formation?"

"This is a good approach—formations linked in rings, multiplying power. But the drawback is also obvious: touch one part, and the whole is affected. If the formations on one continent are mostly damaged, success becomes impossible."

"Such an obvious flaw—how could the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect overlook it? Were they too disdainful to care, or did they not expect anyone to come and destroy it?"

As he pondered, the nearby battle had already ended.

Three figures from the Tiandu Sect emerged. One sat on the ground with a withered aura, one staggered and finally leaned against a rock, panting heavily. Only Junior Brother Tianquanzi remained as before.

As for the two Dao Sect Sages, they had already turned into a pool of blood.

Tianduzi frowned. "Three of you joining forces, with the Three-Phase Extermination Formation I customized for you, why are you still so strained?"

Tianquanzi smiled bitterly. "The two junior brothers are ultimately lacking in realm and experience. Moreover, the Dao Sect experts were unexpectedly tenacious. It's already fortunate that we could kill them without paying with our lives."

Tianduzi understood and then instructed, "I'll give you the time it takes an incense stick to adjust your breathing. Then we'll head to the southeast node. According to my estimation, there's a locally important node there. Once it's broken, we can paralyze seven nearby formation eyes!"

"Understood!"

...

As time passed quickly, battles had already erupted across half of the Eastern Origin Continent.

The situation on each battlefield was completely different.

Some, like Luo Tian Sect, charged forward and fell into bitter struggle. Some, like Jiuling Sect, met their match. Sects with weaker forces and fewer Sage cultivators mostly relied on surprise attacks after joining the battle.

The Tiandu Sect appeared more purposeful, so each of their moves seemed quite composed.

But if one were to talk about true dominance, it had to be the two strongest sects in the current Eastern Wilderness cultivation world.

The Eastern Sun Sect and the White Deer Academy!

One had five Sages, led by the Eastern Sun Grand Elder, a Sage at the Great Perfection stage.

The other had six Sage cultivators. Apart from the newly advanced Kongtongzi, the other five were seasoned Sages of many years, extremely powerful. For instance, Sage Tieguan had fought alongside Luo Chen on the Overlord Blade Domain battlefield back then, earning great fame. And their Grand Elder, Sage Pengxiang, had also reached the late Sage stage. When encountering troublesome Dao Sect experts, he personally stepped in to forcefully kill them.

These two major forces drove straight in, heading directly for the core of the Eastern Origin Continent—the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range.

According to Luo Chen's intelligence, that was the most important place!

It was both the Dao Sect's ancestral court and the core formation eye of the Heaven-Vault Continent-Gathering Formation.

Only by breaking that place could all worries be resolved.

To kill his way into the Dao Sect ancestral court, Luo Chen had long arranged for a fifth-rank ghost spirit to cause chaos inside.

However, although the Kui Ox Ghost Spirit was powerful, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's foundation was not to be underestimated. It was hard to guarantee that they wouldn't activate some Ascendant-level means to quickly suppress it.

Therefore, subsequent reinforcements were extremely important.

At this moment, on the Eastern Origin Continent, three forces, like three arrows, were charging straight toward the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range.

The reason for saying three arrows was that, aside from the Eastern Sun Sect and the White Deer Academy, there was also the side of the demon clan experts.

Forced to be conscripted by Luo Chen in the name of the Truth Crossing Hall Master, these demon clan experts had long been filled with resentment.

This resentment couldn't be vented on Luo Chen, nor could they defy the orders of Green Parasol Mountain. So they could only suppress it, suppress it, until they set foot on the Eastern Origin Continent!

The towering resentment erupted violently!

Over twenty Demon Emperors, upon stepping onto this continent, brought about a storm of blood and gore.

Led by the Thunder Prison Roc, with the three Great Demon Emperors Bi Zheng, Niu Gui, and Juan Kuang as support, leading a host of Demon Emperors, they could be said to kill anyone who stood in their way, slay any god who opposed them.

Sage cultivators who dared to resist were all torn to pieces, blasted into dust.

The immortal city strongholds blocking their path were all smashed into ruins.

Their goal was only one: to strike straight at the heart, killing their way into the Dao Sect ancestral court!

Along the way, these three forces advanced extremely quickly, encountering almost no decent resistance. Occasionally, Dao Sect Sages appeared, but only in twos and threes.

This number was somewhat puzzling.

Too few!

Although the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect had suffered an unprecedented calamity, according to estimates by those in the know, there were still at least a hundred remaining Sage experts.

Over the past hundred-plus years, with vigorous cultivation, there would inevitably be many more.

Even if scattered across three continents, as the Dao Sect's ancestral court, the Eastern Origin Continent should have at least fifty or sixty.

However, the resistance encountered on the battlefield was not that great.

Sects like Fenghua Palace, White Crane Gate, Hundred Beasts Sect, and even Dieyun Sect, Seven Peaks Sect, and others were carrying out their task of destroying formation nodes very smoothly.

In fact, before the demon cultivators of the Truth Crossing Hall and the two top human Sage sects had even killed their way into the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range, most of the formation nodes on the Eastern Origin Continent had been destroyed within a single day.

Such severe damage was already enough to affect the operation of the overall grand formation.

With a earth-shattering roar, the Eastern Origin Continent, which had been moving toward Heaven-Earth Island, came to a grinding halt.

The earth shook, the oceans overturned, and boundless monstrous waves surged into the sky.

Outside Heaven-Earth Island.

Luo Chen turned his back to the giant waves, looking at a place where no one stood.

In the void, ripples undulated, and a figure slowly emerged.

It was Li Canghai!

"Senior indeed did not break his promise."

Li Canghai smiled slightly and looked back. "I didn't expect you to be able to make such a big move. So many Sage experts came at your invitation. Tsk tsk, is this the pulling power of an Alchemy Grandmaster?"

Luo Chen smiled but said nothing.

This was not just the pulling power of an Alchemy Grandmaster; it was also related to the good reputation he had maintained over the years.

Take Li Canghai, for example. As a grandmaster of formation dao, didn't he also possess pulling power?

But his reputation, aside from his legendary cultivation experience, was not particularly good.

At least, he hadn't extended much help to his former peers.

After advancing to Ascendant, he didn't seem to guide many younger juniors.

The so-called Canghai Righteous Alliance was merely a tool in his hands.

When the demon army broke free from Yemo Heaven, he disregarded righteousness and instead seized the spiritual mountain to refine treasures. When the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect entered the Northern Sea, Li Canghai could abandon the Righteous Alliance. After the Dao Sect retreated and the demon clan grew powerful, he could turn around and cooperate with demon clan grandmasters.

Such fickleness, even with heaven-defying skills, wouldn't make many people willing to risk their lives for him.

Sometimes, when a person possesses great strength and provides sufficient benefits to others, reputation is also one of the keys to cooperation.

Seeing this, Li Canghai didn't ask what Luo Chen had done to call so many helpers. He also shifted his gaze to the island before them, which was like a small continent.

"Heaven-Earth Island!"

"The true formation core. The Dao Sect Ancestor Liancheng is also inside."

"Young friend, do you dare to enter with me and meet him?"

Luo Chen's smile faded, and his entire body exuded a murderous aura.

"Why wouldn't I dare!"

With that, he took a step forward, crossing ten li of ground, and set foot on the hard soil of Heaven-Earth Island.

Li Canghai raised an eyebrow, seemingly admiring Luo Chen's courage, and then followed him into Heaven-Earth Island.

The first thing they did upon entering the island was to simultaneously release their divine senses, casting them toward the first peak that had once dominated the world.

The broken Heaven-Earth Peak was shrouded in clouds and mist.

A young man sat cross-legged on the flat mountaintop, slowly opening his eyes.

(End of this chapter)
