# Chapter 923: Ocean of Origin Qi, Tribulation Gu Appears Again

The celestial mechanisms of the universe are profound and obscure, vast and indistinct.

Thus the Great Dao is difficult to seek, and living beings remain in chaos.

Only when Heaven bestows celestial mechanism, clear and unobscured, dispelling clouds and mist, can one attain the true essence!

"Celestial mechanism" is everywhere, yet extremely difficult to discover.

Even though Luo Chen cultivated both Dharma and body, tempering a pair of crimson eyes with golden pupils that could glimpse the flow of celestial mechanism, and even possessed the alchemy art "Refining Heavenly Secrets" left by the Alchemy Saint, he had never found any clue on how to draw celestial mechanism into his body and use it for cultivation.

The only thing he knew was that only when a cultivator experienced enlightenment would celestial mechanism actively come to them, allowing them to comprehend certain bottlenecks.

He himself had experienced enlightenment many times, but often knew the what without understanding the why, never grasping why any difficult puzzle could be solved in that state.

Now, having understood its principle, even without being in a state of enlightenment, he could still capture certain threads.

His natal technique, the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture*, began to circulate quietly.

The Nascent Soul opened its eyes and mouth, greedily devouring the free-floating spiritual energy between heaven and earth.

The phenomenon of crimson eyes and golden pupils also manifested above the Nascent Soul.

A pair of bright eyes stared fixedly at the vast spiritual energy surging through the void, faintly visible within it were strands of celestial mechanism.

Not much, completely incomparable to what the Alchemy Saint had drawn when ascending.

But it was enough!

The Alchemy Saint had drawn vast amounts of celestial mechanism to ascend in broad daylight.

He merely wanted to use it to comprehend the true meaning of the laws!

The only unexpected thing was that these celestial mechanisms lingered outside, not very willing to merge into Luo Chen's body.

The little Nascent Soul blinked, and as if inspired, formed a hand seal.

The alchemy art *Refining Heavenly Secrets* suddenly reappeared.

At this moment, Luo Chen was actually treating his own Nascent Soul as a pill, attempting to refine celestial mechanism into his body!

In this semi-active, semi-passive state, those lingering celestial mechanisms finally followed the rolling currents into Luo Chen's body, devoured in large gulps by the Nascent Soul.

As time passed, Luo Chen's mind gradually became transparent and clear, growing more and more lucid.

His divine sense spread uncontrollably, penetrating his body, piercing through the cave dwelling, extending endlessly toward the depths of the void, exploring the source of heaven and earth's spiritual energy.

The so-called true meaning of laws was the essence of nature.

The hundred arts of immortality also had the essence of the Great Dao, but ultimately narrow, and the true meaning of laws derived from them was relatively scarce.

But the foundation that constructed this world—life and death, yin and yang, the five elements, wind and thunder—was far more complex.

Fortunately, Luo Chen was not pursuing any profound true meaning of laws this time. He only wanted to comprehend the simplest and most fundamental type within the fire-attribute law's true meaning—the origin of fire!

And this process, as long as he could once again enter the ocean of origin qi in the void, would naturally be accomplished.

Fu Qinglan had done this when she comprehended the origin of wood back then.

Luo Chen had also done this when he comprehended the true meaning of withering and blooming by borrowing the Withering-Bloom True Flame.

His vast divine sense, without any concealment, spread toward the void, guided by celestial mechanism, going against the current, seeking the root and tracing the source.

Wherever it passed, whether it was Alchemy Mountain, or Misty Peak, War God Peak, or various parts of the Luo Tian Sect, everything was reflected under Luo Chen's divine sense.

He passed by hastily, not paying much attention.

Only vaguely did he sense that there was a warm and vast ocean, like a mother's embrace, right before his eyes.

Just then, Luo Chen looked back in confusion, lowering his head to gaze below.

Because of this backward glance, the celestial mechanism suddenly dispersed.

The ocean of origin qi that was right before his eyes suddenly closed its open door to him.

How could the Great Dao turn back?

His vast divine sense abruptly retracted, the Nascent Soul returned to his body, and Luo Chen's eyes snapped open.

"Failed!"

His face showed both shock and anger.

But not because of failing to enter the ocean of origin qi and comprehend the origin of fire this time.

He was not Deng Taiyue, who would lose his chance forever after one failure.

For Luo Chen, who had grasped the method, at worst he could try again, twice, three times, and always succeed eventually.

What shocked and angered him was something else entirely!

Without even looking at the fourth-rank talisman slowly floating down from the air, Luo Chen rose to his feet and transformed into a crimson rainbow, shooting into the distance.

In just a few breaths, Luo Chen arrived at a scenic mountain.

Because his movements were quite conspicuous, the people in the mountain had already discovered him and hurried out to greet him.

"Husband, why have you come?"

Luo Chen didn't even look at the flustered Sima Huiniang. With a wave of his sleeve, the door of a cave dwelling suddenly opened wide.

Stepping inside, with just one glance, Luo Chen fixed his gaze on the young man whose body emitted dense spiritual energy.

"Luo Lingxi!"

The young man who had been meditating opened his eyes in surprise. The moment he saw Luo Chen, his face turned pale.

"Father."

Luo Chen said nothing, only stared fixedly at the strands of spiritual energy on his body, and his cultivation level of the fourth layer of Qi Refinement!

Four years ago, Luo Lingxi had been nothing more than an ordinary mortal with no cultivation whatsoever.

Yet in just four years, he had not only embarked on the path of cultivation but had also leaped to become a mid-stage Qi Refinement cultivator.

This change, if Luo Chen's divine sense hadn't been amplified by celestial mechanism and spread throughout the entire Luo Tian Sect, he would never have discovered it.

Luo Lingxi's lips trembled, and finally, under Luo Chen's eyes that seemed capable of seeing through everything, he lowered his head.

"Your child was wrong. Please punish me, Father!"

Just then, Sima Huiniang rushed in frantically, shielding Luo Lingxi behind her.

"It's not Lingxi's fault, it's mine!"

"It was my decision to split the Devil Heart Gu from his body and plant it on the low-level disciples within the sect."

"Husband, if you want to blame someone, blame me. If you want to punish someone, punish me!"

Luo Chen said coldly, "Do you know how great the harm of this action is?"

Sima Huiniang was stunned.

Luo Chen shook his head. "Our Luo Tian Sect doesn't have many disciples to begin with, and the low-level disciples are the foundation of the sect. Using them to plant demon gu as cauldrons for Lingxi's cultivation is cutting off the sect's foundation. If this were discovered by others, the sect would become divided and disloyal. Do you want the Luo Tian Sect to fall apart?"

Sima Huiniang opened her mouth, her face deathly pale.

Her lips moved a few times before she barely managed to say, "But what else can Lingxi do? He is our son. He longs so much to cultivate. He also wants to fly through the skies and see the beautiful scenery of the Eastern Wilderness. Do you really want him to live a life of mediocrity and turn into a handful of yellow earth after a hundred years?"

"Luo Chen, he is your son too!"

"You clearly have methods that could allow him to cultivate, yet you only focus on your own secluded cultivation. Do you deserve to be a father?"

"Don't think I don't know what you wanted to do when Lingxi was born!"

As she spoke, Sima Huiniang became more and more agitated, even calling Luo Chen by his given name.

This was the first time in all their years together that they had clashed so fiercely.

A flash of anger crossed Luo Chen's face, but in the end, he took a deep breath and suppressed the fire in his heart.

He said solemnly, "Huiniang, Lingxi is our child. But isn't the Luo Tian Sect also a child that you and I have raised with our own hands? It contains your friends, your disciples, and countless juniors who respect and love you as the founding sect leader. If you continue to act recklessly like this, will you next use Initiate disciples and Adept disciples as cauldrons for Luo Lingxi's demon planting?"

Sima Huiniang was stunned.

Scenes of her exhausting every effort for the Luo Tian Sect, rising early and sleeping late, flashed through her mind.

When had she started becoming so indifferent to the sect?

Luo Chen shook his head and let out a long sigh.

"The fault lies not with you, nor with Lingxi. It lies with me."

"I have neglected too much."

"Fortunately, it's not too late to mend the fold after the sheep are lost."

Having said that, a fierce look flashed across his face as he reached out and grabbed toward Luo Lingxi from afar.

"Come out!"

Luo Lingxi closed his eyes in despair.

Sima Huiniang also cried out in despair, "No!"

The next moment, a gray light struggled and reluctantly flew out of Luo Lingxi's body.

After this object left his body, Luo Lingxi immediately felt much lighter.

He opened his eyes in confusion. "I'm not dead?"

Sima Huiniang was also stunned. "Husband, what you extracted wasn't the Devil Heart Gu?"

Luo Chen did not answer them. Instead, after the gray light appeared, he began to continuously form hand seals, striking layers of restrictions upon it, sealing it bit by bit.

In the end, Luo Chen even took out his natal treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, and strands of tiny chains extended out, binding it tightly.

Only then did the black light mass reveal its blurry form.

It was something resembling a butterfly.

Luo Lingxi looked at the object in confusion, not understanding why such a thing existed on his body.

"Father, what is that?"

Only after the Primordial Unity Cauldron had completely suppressed it did Luo Chen breathe a sigh of relief, cold sweat beading on his forehead.

It was clear that sealing this object was no easy task for him either.

He explained in a low voice, "Do you still remember the elder I once took you to meet, Valley Immortal Feng Jiang?"

Luo Lingxi said dazedly, "I think I met him once, but I fell asleep as soon as I saw him."

Luo Chen nodded. "This object was left by him. It is definitely not something you can control at your current level!"

Strictly speaking, this object did not belong to Valley Immortal Feng Jiang, but to the Life and Death Gate of the Southern Frontier.

A fifth-rank divine gu—the Tribulation Gu!

It possessed the terrifying power to attract calamities, bring disasters, and destroy heaven and earth.

Even with the foundations of Valley Immortal Feng Jiang and the Nether Origin Sect, it was very difficult to subdue.

They could only use the fleshly body as a prison, suppressing it within the Sealing Immortal Valley.

Yet even so, the scene of continuous earthly disasters in the Sealing Immortal Valley was still shocking to behold.

Later, to deal with Rosycloud, Valley Immortal Feng Jiang had used his life as the price to fully activate this gu, and with the help of the Nether Origin Sect's sect leader, Ancestor Yuming, they had summoned a terrifying heavenly tribulation that forced even Rosycloud to retreat.

After the great battle, the weakened Tribulation Gu had disappeared.

Unexpectedly, it had quietly slipped into the Alchemy Saint's blessed land and attached itself to the most inconspicuous Luo Lingxi, who had no cultivation at all.

In truth, Luo Chen should have discovered it long ago. The first time he returned to the blessed land, he had vaguely felt that something was wrong with Luo Lingxi, something that even made him uneasy.

But at the time, he hadn't paid it much attention.

It was only during this attempt to comprehend the origin of fire, when he had drawn a large amount of celestial mechanism and his divine sense had spread out, that he detected the anomaly.

Something was secretly competing with him for the celestial mechanism!

He had arrived in haste, discovered that the Tribulation Gu and Luo Lingxi were not deeply bound, and decisively extracted it.

But even so, Luo Chen couldn't help but be secretly alarmed by this object's mysterious power to attract calamities and bring disasters.

Simply by borrowing an insignificant mortal like Luo Lingxi, it had unknowingly planted a great hidden danger within the Luo Tian Sect.

If Luo Lingxi had continued like this, as his cultivation level increased, he would inevitably and uncontrollably turn his attention to other disciples of the Luo Tian Sect.

What Luo Chen had said earlier about Initiate cauldrons and Adept cauldrons was not an exaggeration!

Once that time came, the sect's order would collapse. Even if Luo Chen could suppress it with his strength, a divided and disloyal Luo Tian Sect would have no cohesion. It would exist in name only!

Sima Huiniang did not understand the terror of the Tribulation Gu. She only cared about her son.

"Then what should we do about Lingxi now?"

Luo Chen glared at her. "A doting mother spoils the child! If you ever use that method of splitting and planting demon gu again..."

"Father, I won't cultivate anymore. Please don't blame Mother."

Luo Lingxi interrupted Luo Chen's words, his face desolate, but there was still some unwillingness in his eyes.

Luo Chen waved his hand and sighed. "Why rush? I started my path at sixteen, but I truly began cultivating at twenty-seven. You still have time. Once I am fully prepared, I will naturally solve the Devil Heart Gu problem on your body. I might even make your future cultivation speed soar, allowing you to become a genius of the immortal path."

Luo Lingxi's eyes lit up. "Really?"

Sima Huiniang guessed at a possibility and said in a trembling voice, "Husband, are you going to the Southern Frontier to seek the Devil Heart Gu King?"

Earlier, when Luo Chen had returned to Motian Cliff, he had once given Sima Huiniang a book about solving the Devil Heart Gu problem, so she knew the relevant methods.

At the time, Luo Chen had only wanted to soothe his Dao partner's anxious heart.

Unexpectedly, under the influence of deep maternal love and the Tribulation Gu, she had nearly committed a great mistake.

Luo Chen sighed faintly. "Let's first clean up the mess you've made!"

Afterward, Luo Chen ordered Sima Huiniang to be confined for ten years, then took Luo Lingxi and left in a hurry.

...

On Alchemy Mountain, four small figures waited in confusion.

Xiao Xuan asked in confusion, "Why has the sect leader brought us to Alchemy Mountain? This is the Supreme Elder's cultivation retreat."

Zhao Lang said excitedly, "Could it be because our recent cultivation speed has been extremely fast, far surpassing our peers, and has attracted the Supreme Elder's attention?"

The only female disciple among the four, Yin Baiqiu, also became happy.

"I heard that the Supreme Elder only has one disciple. Now that the sect has settled down, could he be planning to take on more disciples and personally train them?"

The Supreme Elder personally taking disciples?

As soon as this statement was made, it sparked intense discussion among the three.

Only a dark-faced middle-aged man seemed to be in a daze.

Yin Baiqiu asked curiously, "Senior Brother Zang, why are you silent?"

Zang Yuzhi suddenly woke up. Facing the three junior brothers and sisters staring at him, he hesitated, then finally shook his head.

"Our aptitudes aren't that good. Our recent rapid progress was only due to the help of high-quality spirit veins. The Supreme Elder wouldn't think much of us."

Zhao Lang said displeasedly, "Why are you so self-deprecating? We all entered together, but only we have made such rapid progress. Doesn't that say enough?"

Zang Yuzhi said nothing, but there was a hint of worry in his expression.

At this moment, in Luo Chen's cave dwelling, Li Yingzhang was reporting the situation of the four people outside one by one.

"They are all new disciples with clean backgrounds and no connections within the sect."

"Among the four, Xiao Xuan and Zhao Lang come from the mortal world, with three-spirit-root aptitudes. Yin Baiqiu is the descendant of a fallen family. That family originally wanted to attach themselves to our Luo Tian Sect, but we followed your orders and didn't accept vassals. We only took Yin Baiqiu in because she was full of spiritual nature."

"Zang Yuzhi is somewhat special. He obtained a fortuitous encounter in his childhood. I investigated—he accidentally entered the fallen site of an Adept cultivator and received some legacy. But it wasn't valuable, so the sect didn't interfere."

After hearing this, Luo Chen nodded slightly.

"A disciple's fortuitous encounters are their own. As long as they are loyal to the sect, there's no need to covet them."

Li Yingzhang also thought the same way. Everyone had their own secrets, whether it was a fortuitous encounter helpful for cultivation or a defect that was hard to speak of.

The sect was like a parent, but there was no need to meddle in everything. Otherwise, it would go against the principle that immortal cultivation ultimately depended on oneself.

He asked curiously, "Supreme Elder, you specifically called these four here. Do you have some plan?"

Luo Chen opened his mouth, then sighed, and told him everything his family had done.

There was no concealment in the middle, and he even told Li Yingzhang about his future plan to leave the Alchemy Saint's blessed land.

After hearing all this, Li Yingzhang was greatly shocked.

Such a family scandal—why had the Supreme Elder told him?

But then, he immediately understood.

Although Sima Huiniang and Luo Lingxi were Luo Chen's closest family, the several hundred people of the Luo Tian Sect were also Luo Chen's family.

And as the acting head of the family, he needed to know all this.

"Huiniang failed to set a good example as an elder. I have ordered her to be confined for ten years, and she can no longer interfere in any matters of the Luo Tian Sect. You can also find ways to suppress the influence of the founding sect leader. Consider this a slight punishment."

Li Yingzhang hesitated. "Isn't that too harsh? She is, after all, the founding sect leader!"

Luo Chen avoided the topic. Right now, Sima Huiniang's Dao heart was in great turmoil. She was completely unsuitable to be a respected elder of the sect.

"As for Luo Lingxi, considering his youth and that he was influenced by the gu insect and his mother, unconsciously taking the wrong path, I have chosen to let bygones be bygones. Yingzhang, what do you think?"

Li Yingzhang said hesitantly, "And what about the four disciples he harmed? How should they be handled?"

"This is why I asked you to bring them here today."

Luo Chen already had an idea and said slowly, "Later, I will personally remove the Devil Heart Gu that hasn't been deeply planted in them yet. This process might damage their cultivation level and foundation. Afterward, I will bestow a large amount of resources as compensation. In the future, please help me take care of them a bit. If I can help them establish their foundations and extend their lifespans, I will feel a little better."

Li Yingzhang breathed a sigh of relief. He had been afraid that the Supreme Elder would secretly dispose of these four.

"The Supreme Elder is merciful!"

Luo Chen laughed self-deprecatingly. Merciful?

If Luo Lingxi and Sima Huiniang were not his family, if this were any other sect committing such a great crime, they would have been killed with a single palm strike long ago.

"Call them in!"

Li Yingzhang nodded and personally went out to bring the four in.

The moment the four saw Luo Chen, they fell into the Mirror Flower Water Moon illusion, unable to extricate themselves.

The process of removing the Devil Heart Gu also caused them no pain.

It was only afterward that they noticed their cultivation levels had dropped and felt a vague sense of loss on their bodies.

As if something had left them.

Dazedly, the four left Alchemy Mountain.

Their only impression of the Supreme Elder was his gentle smile, the large amount of cultivation resources suitable for them that he had bestowed, and his encouragement to guard against arrogance and impatience and cultivate well in the future.

That was it?

Not yet!

"Yingzhang, from now on, keep an eye on the entire sect. Make sure this doesn't happen again." Luo Chen said this, then sealed the extracted Devil Heart Gu and handed it to Li Yingzhang as well.

Li Yingzhang naturally understood Luo Chen's intention. He was to help monitor Luo Lingxi and prevent him from causing chaos in the sect.

Thus, this melodramatic ethical drama involving family and sect finally came to an end under Luo Chen's swift and decisive action.

When everyone had left, Luo Chen exhaled a breath of turbid air.

"They say even an upright official finds it hard to settle family disputes. This is more troublesome than comprehending the true meaning of the laws!"

After muttering a few words, Luo Chen also felt the pressure of time.

Before leaving the Alchemy Saint's blessed land, there were two things he had to accomplish.

One was to comprehend his own true meaning of the laws. The other was to awaken Canglong.

On Canglong's side, having obtained his divided soul personally controlling the Dust Fate Immortal Mirror, progress was extremely fast. The day of awakening was not far off.

As for himself, his comprehension had been interrupted, just one step away from the threshold.

"This time, I'll try drawing the talisman for the fourth-rank spell 'Flame Dragon Descends'!"

Luo Chen made a decision in his heart. He planned to enter that previous state again while pushing another fourth-rank spell to great completion.

But before starting, he first took out the Primordial Unity Cauldron to observe it.

This natal treasure of his had never fully recovered since the battle in the Medicine King Domain.

Using it to suppress the Tribulation Gu—he wondered if there would be any problems.

The moment he looked, his expression changed.

"Where has the Tribulation Gu gone?"
