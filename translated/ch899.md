# Chapter 899: The Brush Moves with Spirit, Accomplished in One Go

*Whoosh!*

In the void, a large sleeve swept across.

Yet the feeling upon contact was still empty.

Luo Chen slowly withdrew his hand, a golden light blooming in his eyes.

"Another illusion?"

But soon, he realized that those existences emitting dense white light were not illusions, but real.

It was just that if he tried to subdue them by brute force, they would become slippery and difficult to grasp.

"It seems that forcibly collecting these protected pills and sniffing them carefully is impossible."

With his eyes that could pierce through illusions, he had already seen that these white lights were actually pills.

Just like those "Pill Scriptures" in the side hall earlier, they originated from the same illusion technique.

As things stood, forceful collection was impossible. Only by truly identifying the corresponding pill could one actually obtain it.

Luo Chen suppressed his restless thoughts and cast his gaze upon the other four people not far away.

At first, they were also somewhat surprised by the specific test question of this trial.

But perhaps because they had experienced similar things before, after the initial panic, they stabilized.

They began to display their respective abilities!

Chen Mozi took out a gourd, poured some unknown liquid from it, and dripped it onto his nose.

Xin Yisheng's red light shone brightly, reflecting and shimmering with those white lights, quite splendid.

Lu Chun simply pinched a spell and sat cross-legged in the void.

Chu Yu was the simplest, merely putting on a white veil, as if it could filter out the other miscellaneous pill qi.

And with such efforts, progress gradually began.

Lu Chun's face lit up with joy. He woke from his meditative state and wrote the name of a pill on the desk in front of him.

*Whoosh!*

Just as the pill's name appeared, a white light rushed towards him, finally landing on the jade plate.

This meant he had succeeded once!

Lu Chun was walking at the forefront, feeling immensely proud in his heart.

Looking around, he happened to catch Luo Chen's observing gaze. He instinctively covered the first-grade pill on his jade plate.

Luo Chen smiled freely, turned sideways, and stopped looking closely.

In reality, this place had special arrangements. Relying solely on the naked eye, one couldn't distinguish the pills the others had harvested.

After laughing, Luo Chen fell into thoughtful contemplation.

Identifying by scent—this was a required lesson for every alchemist.

Many times, distinguishing the quality of a pill wasn't just about tangible methods like cloud patterns, appearance, taste, texture, or feel.

Pill fragrance, or pill qi, was also a very important part of it.

For an experienced alchemist, sometimes they didn't even need to look. A single gentle sniff was enough to identify what pill it was, how much medicinal power it had, and what its quality was.

Even Luo Chen, who came from an unorthodox background, had considerable skill in this area.

One type of pill he had refined in his early years was called the Blood Baleful Pill.

After the pill was formed, its fragrance was burnt and thick.

For other orthodox pills, one might think it had failed.

But for the Blood Baleful Pill, having that kind of fragrance was actually a sign of success.

Luo Chen had also relied on that burnt pill fragrance back then to confirm his success.

But what truly demonstrated Luo Chen's skill in this area wasn't the fragrance of a single pill, but a spell.

The Cleansing Art!

In his early years, to prevent enemies from targeting him through his residual aura, Luo Chen had practiced the Cleansing Art to the Great Perfection level.

Every time he finished alchemy, he would subconsciously cast it on himself to remove the aura on his body.

That casting process wasn't just about removing all auras. It required precisely identifying the residual pill qi of the refined pill among the mixed auras on his body, so he could accurately remove it.

Over three hundred years, Luo Chen had reached a point where, for the pills he had refined, he could identify the type just by smelling them.

His gaze swept over the hall where white light was darting about, and his brow furrowed slightly.

If the scope of identifying pills was limited to those he had refined, then this test was nothing to fear.

But here, the number of white lights was in the thousands!

To accurately identify a hundred types of pills from among them without a single mistake, the scope was a bit too large...

"Scope?"

Luo Chen suddenly realized. Those "Pill Scriptures" he had read in the side hall of the Pill Saint Hall earlier—didn't they encompass all the pills in this trial?

"The scope of this test question is too broad. Acting rashly might seem like getting ahead of everyone, but in reality, it's a lower-class approach."

"One careless mistake, and all previous efforts are wasted."

"Only with absolute certainty can one put brush to paper!"

Luo Chen took a deep breath and, like Lu Chun, sat cross-legged in the void.

His divine sense spread out, enveloping the endless white lights.

His nostrils gently flared. Whenever a white light flew by, he would carefully identify it.

Most importantly, he compared these pill fragrances with the memories from when he had read the Pill Scriptures.

Through repeated identifications, Luo Chen gradually came to understand that Pill Saint Chu Yan using the pills themselves to transform into Pill Scriptures wasn't just showing off his skills.

Some things in the Dao of alchemy couldn't be fully summarized just by using words and phrases to describe them.

For instance, pill fragrance. Occasionally, one or two types could be accurately described, but what about ten, a hundred, or even a thousand types?

Descriptions would always run out of words. Different pills might also have the same description.

What seemed like a slight difference was actually a vast error!

Only after specifically smelling them could an alchemist have a concrete concept.

Luo Chen did not put brush to paper for a long time, remaining seated cross-legged in the hall.

This unconventional behavior naturally attracted the attention of others.

But being in the midst of the trial, no one paid continuous attention. The most important thing was their own situation.

Lu Chun had put brush to paper first and had the fastest progress.

More than ten pill names were already on the white paper, and the corresponding ten-odd pills were indeed placed on the white jade plate.

Xin Yisheng's progress was slower, but each time was very accurate.

Chu Yu was unhurried. That white gauze scarf magic treasure seemed to be able to filter out some miscellaneous odors. Therefore, she was also the second fastest person here.

As for Chen Mozi, after his initial brushstroke, he seemed to have hit a bottleneck, unable to identify a second pill for a long time.

For a time, three completely different scenes appeared in the hall.

Lu Chun was bustling with activity, his progress rapid.

Xin Yisheng and Chu Yu were neither fast nor slow, but their brushstrokes were calm.

Chen Mozi and Luo Chen were completely motionless, like wooden carvings or clay sculptures.

Time passed bit by bit. Being in the great hall, unable to see the passage of the sun and moon, or discern the changes in light, no one knew how long had passed.

The trial process of the first level had unknowingly reached its middle stage.

"*Huu...*"

A long exhale. Chen Mozi woke from his silence.

He looked at the surrounding situation. The one who had started fast and was now struggling to write was Lu Chun.

Those who maintained their original identification speed but whose expressions were growing heavier were Chu Yu and Xin Yisheng.

For a moment, he couldn't help but sneer inwardly.

"The more obvious ones are easier to identify, but what's left are all those with similar auras."

"There are even several types of pills that are colorless and odorless."

"Add to that the constant pressure of the trial, the unconscious competition in progress between each person. In the later stages, who can stabilize their mind and fully unleash their strength?"

"Only after making thorough preparations can one wield the brush like a spirit!"

"Fortunately, I discovered the problem from the start and didn't follow the fixed experience of the previous trials."

He was about to put brush to paper.

But before he did, he inadvertently glanced at Luo Chen.

The other party was still deep in thought.

"I underestimated Luo Chen. He must be the same as me, perhaps even earlier than me in discovering the true method to pass this trial."

"The title of Pill Sect is not false!"

"However, youth is still his weakness. His insufficient foundation is shackling his start at this moment."

With a slight smile, Chen Mozi wrote the name of the second pill on the rice paper.

A white light drifted over.

Its fragrance was extremely faint, almost impossible to smell, but he had indeed identified it.

Just then, a fire suddenly ignited for no reason.

Lu Chun stared blankly at the desk in front of him. That piece of paper, filled with dozens of pill names, had self-immolated.

And all the pills in the white jade plate flew away.

This meant he had failed.

"How could this be?"

"Clearly, the fragrance recorded in the classics is rich, like plum and osmanthus—that's the Fragrant Flower Pill! It has the effect of prolonging life and preserving beauty. Why was it wrong?"

"No!"

"There is also a third-grade pill called Lily, described the same way. But the two have an essential difference. That one is a demonic pill that stimulates potential and exchanges for powerful strength."

"Wrong... wrong!"

Muttering, Lu Chun regretted not being so hasty in putting brush to paper.

Just then, a surge of immense force pushed him, sending him flying out of the hall.

Before leaving, his gaze swept over everyone in the hall.

Some were indifferent to his departure, some looked as if they had expected it, and others showed a sneer.

Only one person was completely unmoved.

*Boom!*

The scene shifted. Someone fell out, staggered a few steps, and then stood dejectedly in front of the Pill Saint Hall.

Ye Lingtian was specifically in charge of this Pill Saint Hall trial. Seeing this, he couldn't help but be surprised.

"So fast? It's only been less than half a year!"

And the others had already gathered around.

"Young master!"

"Cousin, why are you out so early?"

"Did you fail?"

Faced with his clan members' questions, Lu Chun was deeply ashamed.

Knowing that the facts were already settled, and seeing Lu Chun silent, everyone stopped asking, so as not to embarrass him further.

But seeing that Lu Chun wasn't leaving, as if he wanted to wait for the results, someone else asked curiously.

"How is the trial going?"

Lu Chun sighed and recounted the situation he had seen before leaving, along with his judgment, one by one.

Ye Lingtian listened quietly, getting a general idea in his heart.

Chen Mozi was the most mature and steady, his progress relatively the slowest.

Chu Yu and Xin Yisheng were walking more steadily, with hope of passing the trial first.

As for Luo Chen, whom he had the highest hopes for, a full half year had passed, and he still hadn't made a move.

"What is he doing?"

...

"What are you thinking about?"

During her rest, Chu Yu secretly observed Luo Chen.

Another two months had passed, and the other party still hadn't acted.

If she had thought he was being pretentious at first, she wouldn't see it that way now.

Because the further along it got, the harder it became. Accurately identifying a specific pill from the vast multitude had already left her mentally and physically exhausted.

At this point, she also understood that the method of unraveling layer by layer and steadily advancing was not the best way to pass.

Only after comprehensive consideration could one succeed in one go.

But the knowledge accumulation and alchemical foundation required for that were too terrifying.

Among those present, perhaps only Chen Mozi had such a foundation. This was evident from the rice paper that was almost filled with names.

In this situation, why was Luo Chen forcing it? Why not just use the slow method to find the corresponding pills one by one? At least he wouldn't lose so badly!

Xin Yisheng rubbed his brow. Catching a glimpse of Luo Chen's calm profile, he sneered inwardly.

"Truly young. Not up to the task!"

But upon seeing Chen Mozi's progress, his heart grew anxious.

The other had started later but arrived first. How had he gotten ahead of him?

Chen Mozi had no time for other concerns. He was currently focused on chasing two white lights that were entangled with each other.

"Fishy like crucian carp, nauseating to smell, entangled with each other, life and death unceasing."

"This must be the legendary Yin-Yang Pill!"

"But which is Yin, and which is Yang? Where is the difference between the two?"

"Do I have to gamble on it?"

Sweat beads had unknowingly appeared on the man's forehead.

For a Sage, no matter how little they trained their bodies, their physique had been cleansed and tempered by countless spiritual energy and mana. How could they sweat?

This represented an immense pressure!

And this struggle only ended after Chen Mozi had agonized for a full month.

He gambled once!

And succeeded!

With the pressure lifted, his confidence soared, and even his subsequent progress became faster and faster.

Seventy-one... eighty-two... ninety-three...

He was only single digits away from identifying a hundred types of pills.

Just as the one-year deadline was approaching, Luo Chen suddenly awoke, catching everyone off guard.

His gaze swept over the vast white light. Holding a brush, he calmly dipped it in ink.

"Jade Blossom!"

A white light arrived and landed on the jade plate.

Revealing the true form of the pill.

Almost transparent, colorless yet dazzling.

Odorless, yet upon being revealed, it made one feel fragrant and lush from the depths of their soul.

This was a fourth-grade pill, and an extremely rare one in the world for nourishing the primordial spirit.

No one in the current era could refine it. Only the ascended Pill Saint Chu Yan had refined a small furnace of them.

Luo Chen glanced at it, his expression calm and unconcerned.

As if everything was under control.

Once he put brush to paper, it was like a surging river, flowing endlessly.

Luo Chen wrote quickly, pill names appearing on the paper one after another.

"Five Accumulations"

"Extreme Heaven Return to Spring"

"Purple Frost Condensed Radiance"

"Jade Dew"

"Thousand Bones"

...

"Nourishing Qi!"

When Luo Chen wrote the last name, the desk in front of him suddenly dissipated. The white paper fluttered forward like a guide.

Luo Chen first put away the hundred pills on the white jade plate in front of him, then stepped forward to follow.

Behind him, three gazes followed closely.

They had noticed him from the moment he started writing.

But they never expected that he could identify a hundred pills in one go.

What kind of foundation, what kind of confidence was that!

*Pfft...*

Blood sprayed. Xin Yisheng, his whole body blazing with firelight, was pushed backward by the mighty force of the Pill Saint Hall, flying out.

Bloodstains were at the corners of his mouth. He hated his own mistake and was shocked by Luo Chen's passage.

Seeing the other getting faster and faster, he had panicked and misjudged a certain pill.

In the end, one misstep led to eternal regret.

"Chen Mozi has misled me!"

In a reluctant low growl, the Alchemy Saint Hall's doors closed once more.

Chen Mozi opened his mouth to speak, but Luo Chen was already gone ahead, leaving only the unwilling expression of Xinyi Sheng echoing behind him.

In the end, there was only a soft sigh.

"In this world, there has never been a shortage of geniuses, old friend!"

Chu Yu blinked, then took a deep breath, pulling all his attention back to himself.

For the upcoming "Smelling Fragrance to Identify Pills," I need to be more cautious.

...

Identifying pills by scent—it sounded simple.

But to accurately identify a hundred types of pills from a chaotic mix of thousands upon thousands was extremely difficult.

It required the alchemist to possess a deep foundation, vast experience, and control over details to possibly succeed.

Fortunately, Luo Chen no longer had any shortcomings in this area.

He stood in a room filled with a large number of tools and many materials, a look of bewilderment on his face.

"Second level, what does it test?"

*Ding-dong!*

The white jade plate he had just put away flew out uncontrollably.

As Luo Chen watched in surprise, the hundred pills he had identified through his own knowledge surrounded him as if they had a spirit.

As clouds and mist swirled, a line of familiar text appeared once more.

**[Return to the Origin, Trace Back to the Source, Refine in Reverse to Pass]**

This time, Luo Chen's brow furrowed deeply.
