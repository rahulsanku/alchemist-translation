# Chapter 933: Blood and Rain, Far to the Southern Frontier

The rain poured in torrents, the spiritual energy thick and hazy.

Terrified figures emerged one by one from their hiding places, facing this vast celestial anomaly, utterly at a loss. Some Qi Refinement cultivators, bold as newborn calves, reached out a hand to daringly touch the rainwater.

The raindrops were slightly cool, dissolving upon contact, yet faintly, the spiritual power within their bodies stirred a few points.

"It's spiritual energy!"

"This heavy rain contains abundant spiritual energy!"

Cries of delight washed away the crowd's panic. Instantly, many rogue cultivators took out vessels to collect the rainwater.

Some knowledgeable individuals from minor sects also noticed this startling change, but their methods were more formidable. They activated their mountain-protecting formations to draw in large amounts of rainwater, enriching their own spirit vein foundations.

Of course, the Five Elements Domain was vast in scope, and most of the rainwater naturally merged into the mountains and great rivers. Some barren lands that originally had no spirit veins, nourished by this great rain, gradually saw their qi transform into spiritual energy, giving birth to spirit apertures. It seemed that before long, a spirit vein would naturally take shape.

Different factions had varying reactions to such a celestial anomaly.

Some Adept cultivators, however, clearly sensed what was happening.

When had the world ever given an unearned gift?

This spiritual rain was clearly just the tip of the iceberg of the massive spiritual energy released from the fourth-order spirit vein that the Five Elements Divine Sect had monopolized for nearly a thousand years.

Coupled with the earlier disturbance of heaven and earth's origin qi, the entire Five Elements Domain had felt as if it were shrouded in a world-ending phenomenon. Many people began to harbor some unpleasant guesses.

In trepidation, some strong individuals called their friends and companions, carefully making their way toward the Five Elements Divine Sect's mountain gate.

Before long.

Figures stepped dazedly into the interior of the Five Elements Divine Sect.

What met their eyes was a world turned upside down!

High mountains had collapsed, great rivers had changed course, and vast stretches of palace complexes lay in ruins.

"What kind of battle happened here?" someone groaned in question.

They couldn't imagine what level of combat could cause such destruction. Even an ordinary Sage would probably not be able to create even twenty or thirty percent of this devastation, right?

But no one answered that question!

Because it wasn't important!

What was important were the lifeless corpses lying among the ruins.

And the magical artifacts, treasures, and storage pouches beside them!

"It's mine!"

"I clearly discovered it first!"

"Damn it, give it back to me!"

A storm of blood and rain erupted once more.

As more and more Five Elements Domain cultivators, seeking answers, arrived at the sect's mountain gate, the battles over the inheritance of the sect's cultivators grew increasingly fierce.

Under the starry night and the curtain of rain, the sounds of brutal slaughter drowned out the mournful sobs emanating from this fallen top-tier sect's ruins.

When Shi Ju arrived with his people, this was the scene he witnessed.

He curled his lips in disdain.

A swarm of flies fighting over carrion.

With a wave of his hand, the cultivators under his command immediately dispersed.

"Investigate!"

"Find out who did this!"

The Shi family experts accepted the order and departed, delving deep into the sect's mountain gate interior. Any insignificant Eastern Wilderness cultivator who dared to offend was killed on the spot without question.

Shi Ju sat in his ornate cloud carriage, a beauty by his side, but his mood was far from good.

Although he looked down on Eastern Wilderness cultivators, that fellow Shen Yuan was a genuine late-stage Sage, a renowned figure. Such an existence had some reputation even within the powerhouse-filled Tianyuan Dao Sect.

Not long ago, his subordinates had told him that Shen Yuan might join the Tianyuan Dao Sect as a guest elder of Heaven-Earth Peak. He had realized this was a good thing. If he acted as the intermediary to bring him into the Star Gate, the Shi family would gain a powerful ally!

A Great Sage wasn't some roadside cabbage; even the Shi family only had one.

Once Shen Yuan entered Heaven-Earth Peak through his, Shi Ju's, efforts, there was even a possibility of pushing him to the position of the nineteenth elder of the Star Gate. The Shi family's voice on Heaven-Earth Peak would also greatly increase.

Most importantly, there was another point!

Rumor had it that Sage Shen Yuan and Luo Chen didn't get along!

The two had crossed swords, even having physical altercations in public.

An enemy's enemy was a friend, wasn't that right?

It was precisely for this reason that Shi Ju had taken this matter quite seriously, far more seriously than the sect's important task of conquering the Eastern Wilderness!

But then, the bad news came instantly.

The Five Elements Divine Sect had been destroyed?

When he first heard the news, Shi Ju almost couldn't believe it.

His instinctive thought was that the demon race had invaded and reached the Five Elements Divine Sect.

But the answer was clearly not that.

Hence, this day's journey, where he personally led his people to the Five Elements Domain.

"It wasn't the work of demon race experts. Which force in the entire Eastern Wilderness has this strength? Which expert can go toe-to-toe with Shen Yuan?"

"Dongyang Supreme? That old fellow doesn't have that kind of temper, and he has no enmity with Shen Yuan."

"Heavenly Capital? No, he was summoned by the Ancestor and hasn't had the time recently."

"Who could it be?"

The experts of the Eastern Wilderness were too unfamiliar to Shi Ju. He had only heard of a few outstanding top-tier figures. In such a short time, he simply couldn't figure out who possessed such heaven-defying ability.

Soon, a beam of light flew rapidly outside the cloud carriage.

"Young Master!"

"Found out? Which force killed the sect's over a hundred thousand disciples, like pigs and dogs, without them even putting up a fight?"

The subordinate hesitated. "It seems they weren't killed by an external enemy, but died under their own mountain-protecting grand formation."

"Hmm?"

Shi Ju was startled. He lifted the curtain and strode out.

Even though the rain curtain was continuous, standing there, he was like a blazing sun, dazzling to the eye.

The subordinate swallowed his saliva and quickly added, "Furthermore, we found some traces at the center of the battlefield that can roughly infer the origin of the enemy who invaded the sect."

"Who is it?" Shi Ju asked.

The subordinate's lips moved, but he dared not make a rash assertion.

"Hmph!"

Shi Ju snorted coldly and strode directly into the battlefield of Shen Yuan Peak.

As soon as he arrived, he felt a strange sense of familiarity.

An inexplicable familiarity made him somewhat uncomfortable.

"It's the residual power of Law True Intent, and moreover, it's the fire origin power of the same attribute as mine!"

As expected of a genius of the Dao Sect, in an instant, he recognized some traces of the enemy who had invaded the sect. After realizing this, the disdain in Shi Ju's heart immediately retracted.

An existence capable of comprehending Law True Intent, regardless of their realm, was absolutely a powerhouse not to be underestimated, irrespective of their background or origins. Even a rogue cultivator was a top-tier expert comparable to their Dao Sect geniuses!

Soon, under his subordinate's guidance, he arrived before a huge pit.

At first glance, it was just a lake filled with rainwater.

But as his divine sense spread out, Shi Ju observed the shape of the lake from an overall perspective, and a look of shock and anger gradually spread across his face.

A five-finger imprint, azure fire qi, palm strength vast and mighty, with an intent to suppress all living beings.

Azure Yang Great Handprint!

In all the world, there was only one person capable of executing such a spell!

"Luo Chen!!!"

A low roar burst from Shi Ju's throat.

Between each word, golden flames erupted violently, burning away the rainwater in the five-fingered lake, revealing the bare rock strata beneath.

The surrounding Shi family cultivators quickly retreated, not daring to touch the Young Master's bad luck.

"Hu..."

A deep breath, and Shi Ju woke from his fury.

"I underestimated the depth of the enmity between Shen Yuan and Luo Chen. I never thought he would dare to appear at such a sensitive juncture and kill his great enemy."

"I also never imagined his strength had progressed so rapidly that even a Great Sage was no match for him."

Back then, he, Shi Ju, was no match for Luo Chen.

He had thought that with the support of both the Dao Sect and his family's resources, he had advanced to the mid-Sage stage in just a few decades, even able to briefly contend with a Great Demon Emperor like Bi Zheng, and had completely surpassed that old enemy.

But he never expected Luo Chen's cultivation progress to completely exceed the world's imagination.

The rumors were indeed true; the legend of Luo Chen once fighting three Demon Emperors to a standstill and surviving was real!

This meant...

Amidst his rage, a chill ran down his spine!

The other party's cultivation talent was simply too heaven-defying, wasn't it?

He himself was a favored son of heaven, supported by the greatest force in the Mountains and Seas Realm and the resources of his entire family, yet he couldn't keep up with the other's progress.

Moreover!

"I remember that the Law True Intent he comprehended in the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven was of the wood attribute!"

But the faint Law True Intent left behind on this very battlefield was the power of fire origin, the one Shi Ju was most familiar with!

How could there be a Sage in the world who possessed two True Intents at once?

If he was allowed to continue growing, then who would he, Shi Ju, seek revenge from for the destruction of his Thunder Dao clone back then?

"Find him for me!"

An order was issued in a voice almost like a roar.

The Shi family experts were shaken and immediately accepted the order, using various means to collect Luo Chen's aura from this place.

But the harvest was meager!

With Luo Chen's methods, he had already cleansed his own aura before leaving. Perhaps others could identify his techniques through some battlefield traces, but tracking him by following the trail was absolutely impossible.

The only oversight was probably the residual power of fire origin!

He had comprehended this Dao's Law True Intent not long ago, and due to realm limitations, he was often in a state of being able to release but not retract. Shi Ju, also an existence who had comprehended this origin, could only perceive him because of this.

Seeing his subordinates' fruitless tracking, Shi Ju was furious.

"A bunch of useless trash!"

Then, he soared into the clouds, roughly discerned a direction, and vaguely tracked southward.

But this pursuit didn't last long before he was pulled back by a voice transmission.

"Take command of the Shi family cultivators and prepare to take the initiative to attack the demon race!"

Shi Ju stopped in astonishment, countless doubts flooding his mind.

This time, the Tianyuan Dao Sect had landed in the Eastern Wilderness to conquer this vast continent while the Nether Origin Sect was destroyed. The Eastern Wilderness cultivation world was enough for the Tianyuan Dao Sect to digest for now; the Million Great Mountains were not in the plan.

Why take the initiative to attack?

But he dared not disobey the Ancestor's order, so he had to turn back.

Towards the direction he had sensed, Shi Ju muttered harsh words to himself.

"You're lucky!"

Then he turned and returned, his escape light extremely fast.

However, if anyone were there, they might have sensed a hint of relief and lightness in that swift beam of light.

Was Luo Chen, who could destroy a sect alone and leave calmly, someone Shi Ju could contend with now?

This question was hard for outsiders to know, but Shi Ju already had the answer in his heart.

The pursuit was merely a reckless act driven by fury, and a fear that Luo Chen would further widen the gap between them in the future.

On the way back, Shi Ju gradually calmed down.

When he arrived at the ruins of the Five Elements Divine Sect, his subordinates gathered around.

"Young Master, why are you back so soon?"

Shi Ju glanced at him. The Sage cultivator, sensing his impropriety, quickly shut his mouth.

Shi Ju snorted. "The Ancestor has ordered that we must not linger here. What are you dawdling for?"

Although that cultivator had a Sage realm, he had only recently advanced and was formerly a servant of the Shi family. He said humbly, "I heard that the Five Elements Divine Sect's Dao techniques are exquisite, considered a unique art in the Eastern Wilderness. I've just reached the Sage realm, so I have the qualifications to establish my own clan. I was thinking of collecting some of the sect's Dao techniques to prepare for building a family in the future."

"Tch..."

Shi Ju sneered, unconcerned.

Exquisite Dao techniques?

The Dao techniques of his Tianyuan Dao Sect were second to none in the world. Why would he care about the inheritances of these minor sects?

Just as he was about to turn and leave, he suddenly remembered something.

That Luo Chen didn't seem to have any impressive background, and his Dao technique inheritance was even less traceable.

Yet, the other party had been able to surpass him step by step and make a great name for himself.

Could it be that these neglected minor arts also held some profound mysteries?

He reached out a hand!

"How much have you collected? Let me have a look!"

The subordinate clearly didn't react to this outstretched hand and stood frozen in place.

Shi Ju frowned. "Just lending it to me for a look. Do you think this Young Master would covet such a paltry side-door technique?"

The subordinate quickly waved his hands. "How could I have such a thought? It's just that after searching for a long time, the harvest is meager. The vast inheritance of the sect has vanished without a trace."

Shi Ju was stunned for a moment.

Then he thought, could it have been looted by Luo Chen?

...

Layers of clouds, vast and trackless.

A black light shot through, stirring up countless wind and clouds.

Luo Chen sat cross-legged on the Black Dragon's back, a pill in his mouth, quietly meditating and regulating his breath.

The battle with Shen Yuan had left him unscathed!

It seemed effortless, but the danger within was hard for outsiders to know.

Whether in spells, soul arts, or the manipulation of magical treasures and true artifacts, Shen Yuan was definitely a standout among Great Sages. Even Huang Man and Bi Zheng were inferior to him.

But his greatest weakness was his physical body!

In the battle at Green Parasol Mountain back then, Shen Yuan's physical body had been destroyed by the Nirvana Sacred Flame left behind by Rosycloud. The body he later used was one he had refined himself, not well-matched with his own soul.

Therefore, during battle, there were always some clues to follow.

For the same instant-cast spell, Luo Chen's thought would arrive instantly, while Shen Yuan still had to consider many things, lacking smoothness and fluidity.

Before a Qi refiner cultivated their primordial spirit, the physical body was still the precious raft for protecting the Dao, extremely important.

Even so, Luo Chen had consumed his mana and risked his realm dropping to kill him, which showed how strong Shen Yuan was.

"Fortunately, the enemy is gone, and my thoughts are finally clear!"

Murmuring, Luo Chen opened his eyes.

The loss of mana could not be quickly recovered by relying on a few pills.

Even the Golden Wind and Jade Dew Wine, if taken in large quantities, would cause damage to the meridians.

Only by finding a good spiritual land could he recklessly recover to his peak state.

Luo Chen was not in a hurry. With the little mana he had recovered now, an ordinary Sage could not harm him.

He looked at the vast, boundless road ahead and sighed, "This journey to the Southern Frontier is long and distant. The Devil Heart Gu Emperor I seek is even more elusive, appearing and disappearing without a trace. I don't know where to start."

Having resolved the great enemy Shen Yuan that had weighed on his heart, Luo Chen now had only one thing left to do.

Fulfill his duty as a father!

Find the Devil Heart Gu Emperor and obtain a fourth-order Devil Heart Gu from it, thereby resolving the trouble on Luo Lingxi's body and allowing her to truly cultivate.

As for whether the Devil Heart Gu Emperor was good or evil, or whether it would willingly hand over a fourth-order Devil Heart Gu?

Luo Chen didn't care!

But where should he start?

Luo Chen recalled the letter from Sage Heavenly Mountain, some of Gu Xianfeng's words, and the intelligence about the Southern Frontier he had secretly gathered over the years. His heart grew heavy once more.

Suddenly!

Black King's voice, seeking credit, rang in his ear.

"Master, I have some good things here. Would you like to take a look?"
