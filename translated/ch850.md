# Chapter 850: Armies at the Gates, the Calamity of Luo Tian

“Fellow Daoist Lin, it’s been some days. How have you been?”

Leaning against the city wall with a desolate expression, Lin Qingxuan heard the voice and shifted his eyes slightly.

Within his line of sight, the new sect master of the Divine Talisman Sect, Night Wave Sage, was walking toward him, greeting him with a chuckle.

His tone was quite familiar, as if the two of them had a very good relationship.

But in truth, there wasn’t much interaction between them—only some exchange during the last Shouyang Dao Discussion hosted by Luo Chen.

If it were any other time, Lin Qingxuan naturally wouldn’t have paid him much mind.

Night Wave was, after all, merely a newly formed Sage. How could he compare to Lin Qingxuan, who had already made a name for himself?

Yet, beneath Night Wave Sage’s somewhat humble, overly familiar smile, Lin Qingxuan somehow seemed to recall his former self—also ingratiating himself with established experts in this same way, just to build connections for his sect.

Lin Qingxuan straightened up and nodded at the newcomer.

“Not too good, but not bad either. I heard that Elder Fu of your sect has stabilized?”

Night Wave walked up to him, stopped, and spoke:

“Thanks to the pills gifted by the Alchemy Sect during that visit, my master’s injuries have indeed stabilized and haven’t worsened. But as for saying he’s well…”

Night Wave sighed, his expression heavy.

Lin Qingxuan patted his shoulder and comforted him, “The older generation has already done enough. In the end, we’ll have to rely on ourselves from now on.”

Then he asked with some curiosity, “Does the Alchemy Sect still refine healing pills?”

Night Wave snapped out of his grief over his elder’s injuries and forced a smile. “The Alchemy Sect’s abilities are truly extraordinary. Even ordinary healing pills refined by him have effects far better than those on the market. I heard you’re quite close with him—you could try asking for some, to have on hand for emergencies.”

*Close with him?*

*Ha!*

Lin Qingxuan let out a self-deprecating laugh in his heart.

Night Wave looked around and suddenly asked with some confusion, “Speaking of which, the storm is about to break. Why did the Alchemy Sect leave Lingtian Pass and go to the rear?”

Luo Tian Sect’s departure couldn’t be hidden from anyone.

Especially in recent years, Luo Chen had been acting quite conspicuously.

Many thought he would make a big showing in the coming war, but no one expected that before the fighting even started, he had already “slipped away.”

Lin Qingxuan narrowed his eyes and casually brushed it off: “Perhaps he’s afraid of getting hurt in battle. With his alchemy skills, even in a holy land of Ascendants, he would be an honored guest. How could someone so precious stand beneath a crumbling wall?”

Though he said this, Lin Qingxuan was actually quite relieved in his heart.

Luo Chen’s reputation was already too great!

If Lin Qingxuan traded with him, their statuses would be completely unequal.

If Luo Chen rose even higher through military merit, the gap between them would only widen, and their fragile cooperative relationship might not even be maintainable.

Night Wave frowned, seemingly dissatisfied with Lin Qingxuan’s words.

Such a statement rather belittled the Alchemy Sect.

In his heart, the Alchemy Sect’s Luo Chen was truly a person of high virtue and great skill—how could he flee before battle?

Especially during the Shouyang Dao Discussion, when he bore the pressure of the Nether Origin Sect and took a blow from Thunder Daozi head-on—that image still lingered in Night Wave’s mind, unforgettable.

*A Sage should be like that!*

“Fellow Daoist Lin, you’re being a bit…”

Just as he was carefully choosing his words to speak, his expression suddenly changed.

A talisman appeared in his hand, spontaneously combusting into ashes.

“That’s my disciple’s life-saving talisman. How could this be? He was patrolling outside the city, could it be…”

Lin Qingxuan had no time to answer him.

He leaped into the air, transforming into a beam of light, standing high in the sky.

Biting his fingertip, he then pressed his bloodstained fingers together like a sword and wiped them across his eyes. His narrowed eyes suddenly flew wide open.

A pair of blood-red pupils instantly gazed thousands of li away.

Within his sight, a shocking scene was unfolding.

A sea of demon beasts, like surging waves, was pouring out from between the mountains and ridges, charging toward Lingtian Pass.

The number of these demon beasts was enormous—from afar, they looked like swarms of ants.

But the vast demonic aura that filled the sky—how could that be compared to tiny ants?

“They’ve come?”

Lin Qingxuan took a deep breath, and the next moment, a warning thunderclap rang out.

“Enemy attack!”

The roar was like thunder, and after leaving his mouth, it rolled toward Lingtian Pass.

As if they had been prepared all along, waves of Sage-level mana began to surge up from all over the city.

Lin Qingxuan had no time to count the number of experts in the city; instead, he instinctively touched his eyes.

This pair of blood-red pupils was the final result of taking the Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid personally refined by Luo Chen!

Its effects were extraordinary—being able to see a thousand li into the distance was just one of them.

In battle, it had many more uses!

If he could continue to improve it, it would only help him more.

And to continue improving, he absolutely could not do without that Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid.

“I spoke too hastily earlier. I need to maintain my relationship with Luo Chen. From now on, in front of outsiders, I should speak well of him.”

Thinking this in his heart, Lin Qingxuan took another look at the black tide of demonic qi rolling in the distance.

With just one glance, he exclaimed in surprise.

“Eh?”

Within his sight, the massive black tide, as it advanced, was subtly splitting apart.

Four or five smaller tributaries branched off, charging toward other directions of Lingtian Pass.

Seeing this scene, Lin Qingxuan instantly understood.

The cultivators of the Nether Origin Sect were right—the demon race had truly gone all out in this battle.

They weren’t just going to smash Lingtian Pass; they would also sweep through the surrounding regions.

If that was the case, then Luo Chen, who was in Fenghua Domain, wasn’t as safe as he had imagined.

Shaking his head, Lin Qingxuan gathered his focus, raised his qi, and steadied his spirit, preparing to face the life-and-death battle ahead!

If he survived, he would be reborn—he could even rebuild the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect!

If he didn’t, then everything would be over!

…

Rumble!

Rumble!

The sun and moon changed color; the earth trembled.

Beneath the rolling dust and smoke was an endless tide of beasts!

And this was only Fenghua Domain, located behind Lingtian Pass.

It was hard to imagine how many demon beasts were at Lingtian Pass itself, which was under direct attack.

But the cultivators of Fenghua Domain had no time to worry about others—all their energy was focused on resisting the invading beasts.

One grand formation after another rose along the undulating mountains and rivers.

But beneath the massive beast tide, the weaker sects were wiped out in an instant.

Blazing Wind Valley, Peacock Isle, Golden Mountain Temple…

It wasn’t until this massive beast tide encountered Fenghua Palace that it was finally halted.

Fenghua Fairy, who had returned to Fenghua Palace even before Luo Chen, struck out at the first moment, summoning divine wind that tore countless leading demon beasts to shreds.

Then she ordered her disciples to activate the formations and fight using the terrain.

She herself flew out of Fenghua Palace and into the nine heavens.

“Fellow Daoist, stop here. This is not a place you should be!”

In the vast mist and clouds, a figure revealed itself. He clasped his hands in salute to Fenghua Fairy.

“Are you Fenghua Fairy in person? I am Canlou, under Lord Pingshan’s command. Greetings!”

Fenghua Fairy’s expression changed slightly. This demon emperor who called himself Canlou revealed a cultivation level around the mid-fourth tier.

Most importantly, he was not under some commander’s banner, but under Lord Pingshan’s!

Lord Pingshan!

That name alone brought tremendous pressure.

And it meant that demon emperors directly under the Ancient Demon Emperor Heaven’s command had also joined this battle.

Canlou smiled faintly and waved behind him.

“Leave this woman to me. You go quickly and wipe out the other forces in Fenghua Domain, then surround Lingtian Pass!”

“Yes!”

“As you command!”

“Understood!”

Several voices came from behind, some of which even felt familiar to Fenghua Fairy.

Was a mere Fenghua Domain worth investing so many demon emperor-level experts?

The massive beast tide, which had been blocked by Fenghua Palace, seemed to receive orders and began to split again, spreading left and right to other places.

This posture truly aimed to conquer the entire Fenghua Domain!

But Fenghua Fairy had no time to think further—all her attention was on the man before her.

“I heard that Yellow Wind Guest’s physical body was destroyed by you?” Canlou asked with a smile.

Fenghua Fairy twitched the corner of her mouth but did not explain.

Back then, Gao Chang had parasitized Yellow Wind Guest’s body, turning into rolling yellow wind to try to escape the encirclement. It was she who had torn apart Yellow Wind Guest’s true body, forcing Gao Chang to reveal himself.

Since the other party said this, he clearly already knew something about her combat methods.

This battle would likely be extremely difficult.

Fenghua Fairy’s heart began to sink without end.

…

A “small” beast tide advanced madly along the southwestern direction.

“Small” here meant in comparison to the massive beast tides attacking Lingtian Pass and Fenghua Palace.

In reality, the number of demon beasts in this tide was already over ten thousand.

Even some moderately powerful sects would find it hard to face.

However, as this beast tide surged into a plain, it was met with rolling black mist. Before even seeing the enemy, the harassment of that black mist alone caused the demon beasts to fall one after another.

Corpse after bloody corpse lay prostrate on the vast earth.

No wailing, no roaring.

Swish!

A tall figure landed on a hundred-li stretch of green riverbank.

Heavenjade held a deep, pitch-black large banner in her hand, forming hand seals with her fingers. The black mist swept up countless vengeful souls and returned them to the banner.

“This Myriad Souls Banner bestowed by the master is truly useful in group battles!”

Heavenjade checked briefly, and a joyful expression appeared on her face.

She found that this Myriad Souls Banner was advancing toward becoming a Ten-Thousand Fierce Souls Banner. The only thing lacking was probably a Sage-level main soul!

Suddenly!

Her brow furrowed, and her divine sense spread in all directions.

In her perception, an even greater number of demon beasts were charging toward the hundred-li green riverbank.

Not just one wave—at least four waves of beast tides!

The total number was likely over a hundred thousand!

What had the mere Luo Tian Sect done to deserve attracting so many demon beasts?

Heavenjade had no time to think. She let out a sharp whistle to transmit a message.

The next moment, on the distant Great Green Mountain, cultivators began to perform their arts and activate the grand formation.

Layers of earthen-yellow light screens spread like water ripples, covering ten li, fifty li, gradually enveloping the entire hundred-li green riverbank.

The demon beasts showed no fear of death and charged headlong into the massive formation.

On Great Green Mountain.

Min Longyu sat cross-legged inside a great hall.

Below him were nine Initiate cultivators, helping him control the various formation eyes.

Above the hall hung lifelike water mirrors, reflecting the specific situation outside.

“The Cloud-Chess Water Mirror left by the Supreme Elder is an excellent supplement to formations—it saves the burden of divine sense monitoring.”

Min Longyu sighed with emotion, but his hands moved without any delay.

He guided the formation with hand seals, harvesting the low-level beasts that entered, while also observing the threatening enemies within the formation.

“More and more demon kings are entering the formation!”

“They will destroy the formation foundations I’ve laid. Someone needs to go and eliminate them one by one.”

“I’ll give the specific locations and approximate strength. You relay them to the Sect Master. After he judges, he will assign the various elders to eliminate them!”

The nine Initiate disciples below immediately acknowledged.

Min Longyu took a deep breath and began to call out names one by one.

“On Moonlight Ridge, there is a centipede-type demon beast entrenched. Should be around early third tier.”

“At Gray Pool, an earth dragon has broken through the ground. Should be around early third tier.”

“So fast! A great demon king has ignored the formation’s restrictions and killed its way to the Blackwater Riverbank!”

…

In the Sect Master’s hall.

Li Yingzhang received the transmissions and quickly made judgment after judgment.

“Baleful Dragonzi, go to Moonlight Ridge and slay the centipede demon king!”

“Understood!” Zeng Yilong accepted the order and left.

“Elder Gu, go to Gray Pool… be careful.”

“Understood!” Gu Caiyi, holding her coiled long whip, eagerly left the hall.

“Elder Wang, that great demon king is yours!”

Wang Yuan nodded silently and leaped out of the hall.

Li Yingzhang’s orders continued to be issued.

None of Luo Tian Sect’s experts disobeyed his words.

When there was no one left in the hall to dispatch, Li Yingzhang couldn’t help but shake his head.

In the end, their foundation was too small, and they lacked manpower.

The low-level disciples had all been sent to guard the various formation nodes, leaving only a handful of Adept-level elders for him to command.

“Since that’s the case, let those who can do more, do more!”

Facing the constant stream of enemy information from Min Longyu, Li Yingzhang quickly made relatively accurate judgments and assigned them to Luo Tian Sect’s Adept elders one by one.

Wang Yuan was assigned the most tasks and performed the best, often eliminating enemies with lightning speed.

Jueqing Fairy and Heavenjade, who had both advanced to late Adept stage, also had no easy tasks—not only did they have to deal with demon kings, but they also had to reinforce various locations.

Even Meng Qiner, who was nominally still a Fenghua Palace disciple, was used by Li Yingzhang as a fighter, trapped in bitter combat.

But the powerful enemies were simply too many!

Luo Tian Sect’s strength had decreased considerably in recent years. Among the Adept elders, Chu Kui and Fu Jiusheng had died in battle at Lingtian Pass, and no new Adepts had been added to replenish them.

At this moment, Li Yingzhang truly felt like a clever woman with no rice to cook.

He wished he could throw himself into the battlefield, but he had to sit at the center and oversee the overall situation.

“I finally understand how Sect Master Sima felt back then—having strength but nowhere to use it.”

Li Yingzhang revealed a bitter smile.

However, he was also curious—why hadn’t the Supreme Elder made a move yet?

With the Supreme Elder’s strength, even dozens of demon kings, let alone the overwhelming tide of over a hundred thousand beasts, should be easily wiped out with a flick of his sleeve!

What was he waiting for?

…

In the void.

Four figures stood in the clouds, overlooking the slaughter below.

A burly man carrying a golden ring-blade on his back showed impatience on his face.

“We’ve lured over a hundred thousand children here and gathered twenty-eight demon kings. Why are we still sitting idle? Can’t you three brothers break this formation below?”

Lu Changsheng flattered, “Lord Ditu, don’t be anxious. The formation below is naturally simple, but our target on this trip is not these weak humans.”

Ditu snorted coldly but did not refute.

Other regions had only sent one or two demon emperors each. Only Fenghua Domain had received so much attention from his father.

Not only did he send that brat Canlou to lead, but he also dispatched Ditu himself and the three Lu brothers.

Such a lineup would be considered luxurious even in normal times.

Even now, it should have been a great help in the strong assault on Lingtian Pass.

Yet it was placed in Fenghua Domain instead!

What they sought was naturally the rumored Alchemy Sect’s Luo Chen!

That fellow possessed heaven-defying alchemy skills, and there were even rumors that he had connections with Green Parasol Mountain.

If they could capture this man, it would surely be a great merit.

Whether his elder brother Huang Man could take Lingtian Pass or not, he didn’t know—but he was determined to capture the Alchemy Sect and successfully complete his father’s mission!

But until now, he hadn’t seen that fellow show his face.

Was the siege of a hundred thousand demon beasts on the hundred-li green riverbank still not enough pressure?

Ditu frowned and looked down, his gaze involuntarily falling on a figure that was charging back and forth, stained with countless demon blood.

“That fellow…”

Suddenly!

Above the hundred-li green riverbank, fish-silk sword threads spread out, vast sword qi cutting through the void, cooperating with those Adept cultivators to methodically slaughter the third-tier demon kings one by one.

“He can’t sit still any longer!”

Lu Changsheng’s eyes lit up, and he shouted.

“Move!”
