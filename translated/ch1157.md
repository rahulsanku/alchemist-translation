# Chapter 1157: First City of Divine Astral, Phantom Dream Ghost Locust

Within the Luo Estate, halls and pavilions stood in abundance.

The place where Xiao Sanniang had once lived now bore a different plaque.

Xiangshui Pavilion.

Xiangfei resided here, a mere stone's throw from the current Yixian City Lord, Jing Lan's, dwelling.

Clearly, this arrangement took into account matters of close protection.

To say Xiangfei did not mind being subordinate to a mortal would be utterly impossible. But with Luo Chen's restrictions binding her, and her love of life, she could only accept it.

At this moment, Xiangfei stood atop the pavilion, gazing into the distance at Ten-Thousand Carp Lake, a hint of trepidation in her eyes.

In her perception, whether it was the gold or the black astral qi, both far surpassed the astral qi of ordinary Leakless Realm martial artists.

It was only then that she realized incorporating things like Divine Wind Baleful Qi into astral qi could yield such unimaginable power.

Amidst her admiration, what truly shocked her was that Luo Chen was still unsatisfied, attempting to merge these two powerful astral qi into one.

"Was he not afraid of failure, of the astral qi clashing and destroying his foundation furnace?"

This was the most basic common sense.

Astral qi was formed from the extreme blood qi of a martial artist; a single mistake could gravely injure oneself.

What Luo Chen was doing now was truly audacious.

Xiangfei could only attribute his reckless confidence to that strange blood bead he had spat out.

"Forget it. Why should I worry about this? If he dies, I can regain my freedom. For now, I should focus on the tasks Luo Chen assigned! I heard that tree has already been found."

...

Before Ten-Thousand Carp Lake, within the Tide-Watching Pavilion.

Luo Chen stared fixedly at the fusion of the two astral qi before him, not daring to be the least bit careless.

Lacking the fine, meticulous assistance of divine sense, he did not have absolute confidence in such matters.

Fortunately, his proficiency in the *Divine Astral Combat Qi* had reached the Grandmaster level, and he had thoroughly memorized Xiao Lin's *Thousand Astral Hundred Rotations*, mastering every detail of controlling astral qi.

Only then did he dare to take this risky gamble.

Most importantly!

"Even if my origin blood is not as good as that drop of True Spirit Origin Blood I burned back then, its grade is still extremely high, enough to suppress both astral qi!"

With his mind settled, Luo Chen carefully controlled the fusion of the two astral qi.

Whenever he encountered signs of instability, he would activate his lifebound origin blood for absolute control and suppression.

Gradually, the two astral qi began to truly merge.

A trace of blood-red light, as if about to drip, began to bloom faintly before Luo Chen.

The process of refining astral qi was a delicate task.

It allowed for neither the slightest negligence nor excessive slowness.

Time passed in a flash. Three months later, a hearty laugh rang out from the Tide-Watching Pavilion.

Affected by it, countless koi leaped from the lake, forming a scene of ten thousand carp paying homage.

Luo Chen stood by the lakeshore, looking at a wisp of blood-colored astral qi floating before him, his face full of pride.

Blood-Refined Divine Astral, complete!

Without any visible movement from him, the astral qi merely trembled slightly.

*Hum!*

A strange, clear chime sounded. The Tide-Watching Pavilion, which had stood by Ten-Thousand Carp Lake for who knew how many years, instantly turned to powder.

"Heavy as a mountain, light as the wind. Between motion and stillness, its might is ten thousand jun!"

This single wisp of Blood-Refined Divine Astral was enough to crush mountains and hills!

A mere Tide-Watching Pavilion was naturally nothing.

It was a pity that Luo Chen currently had no true artifact or spirit treasure to use as a test subject.

Otherwise, he truly wanted to try the effect Wang Yuan had hypothesized for the perfected Divine Astral Combat Qi—invincible, unstoppable, and overwhelming.

Of course!

He could still roughly estimate the effects of his personally refined lifebound divine astral.

The Nine Tribulations Earth Fiend and the Unforgiving Heavenly Wind were not ordinary items.

Especially since they had been refined with thirty-six drops of origin blood.

Using this divine astral to execute ordinary martial techniques would greatly amplify their power.

Especially that one improved and perfected forbidden art, *Heaven-Earth Reversal*!

Using ordinary astral qi to drive it, the power could already be doubled.

Previously, using the Nine Tribulations True Astral and the Unforgiving Heavenly Astral, its power was about ten times greater. It was by relying on this move that Luo Chen had easily killed the five City Lord-level martial artists.

Now that the Blood-Refined Divine Astral was initially formed, its power would likely be even more terrifying!

"Once I reach the Leakless Realm and go to Carefree City, there should be plenty of people to test my new moves on!"

A smile curled at the corner of Luo Chen's mouth as he put away the hard-won Blood-Refined Divine Astral.

At the same time, a figure approached gracefully from behind.

"Congratulations, Senior, on your divine art's great success!"

Luo Chen turned around, looking at the sincerely convinced Xiangfei, in a good mood.

"What brings you here?"

Xiangfei bowed respectfully. "Senior, the red-leaf tree you previously requested has been found. I personally went to inspect it. During the season shrouded by Ghost Rain, just as Senior predicted, a large number of Ghost Nightmares have gathered there."

Hearing this, Luo Chen's eyes lit up.

"Excellent!"

Today was truly a double blessing!

Not only had his cultivation art succeeded, but one of the conjectures in his plan had also been confirmed.

Xiangfei, however, was somewhat puzzled. "How did Senior know that the red-leaf tree would attract Ghost Nightmares?"

During the Ghost Rain, ordinary people simply would not go out, so no one should know this secret.

Especially since Luo Chen hadn't even seen it himself, how could he know?

At this, Luo Chen was slightly proud.

This stemmed from when he first obtained the Red Leaf Balefulness.

Back then, he had heard Chu Zhaonan mention the legend of the red-leaf tree.

It was an ancient tree that seemed to have developed intelligence, attracting large numbers of livestock and wild beasts to come under it of their own accord, where they would then die mysteriously.

Such a thing, if placed in the Mountains and Seas Realm, would not be particularly strange.

Whether it was a great demon or a spirit creature, they could all accomplish such enticement.

But this was the Guixu!

Where could so many demons, monsters, and spirits come from?

As for what could make livestock and beasts of low intelligence do such strange things, Luo Chen could only think of one answer.

Ghost Nightmares!

The ghostly nightmares that accompanied the Ghost Rain!

These ghostly beings could enter dreams, causing people to sink into them until they died without anyone knowing.

They were fully capable of doing this.

The reason no one had thought of this before was that when the Ghost Rain fell, no one chose to go out.

And once the sky cleared, the Ghost Nightmares would disappear.

Since Luo Chen had made this connection, he naturally wouldn't give up this opportunity.

Hence, Xiangfei's investigation.

"Now that it's confirmed, I should make a trip myself!" Luo Chen murmured.

Xiangfei was surprised. "Senior is going out?"

Luo Chen nodded. "For this trip, I'll have Chu Zhaonan lead the troops to accompany me. You stay in Yixian City and assist Jing Lan."

"May I ask how long this trip will take?" Xiangfei hesitated for a moment, then couldn't help asking. As if afraid Luo Chen would be angry at her for overstepping, she quickly added an explanation. "I was severely injured in Carefree City. Although I'm slightly better than Desert Wind City Lord and the others, my current situation is truly not good. If Senior is away for too long, I'm afraid I won't be able to hold on until Senior returns."

Luo Chen frowned. He had forgotten about this.

Most Returners were not far from death.

Desert Wind City Lord was like this.

The reason Xilong City Lord and the others had taken the risk to besiege him was also because of this.

He thought for a moment, then suddenly said, "Open all your acupoints!"

Xiangfei did as she was told.

The next moment, a wisp of blood-colored astral qi coiled around Luo Chen's fingertip—it was the newly refined Blood-Refined Divine Astral.

Luo Chen's finger slowly pointed towards the space above Xiangfei's brow.

Seeing this, Xiangfei couldn't help but swallow nervously. The point between her brows was the Yintang. And above it was the first major acupoint concerning a martial artist's essence and qi switch—the Tianting acupoint!

If it were broken, the Leakless Golden Body would collapse in an instant.

In severe cases, it could even lead to instant death.

Just like Xiao Feng, who had been struck there by Luo Chen's Blood God Tribulation Finger.

But Xiangfei did not dodge, allowing him to touch her Tianting acupoint.

The moment contact was made, the divine astral at Luo Chen's fingertip suddenly split apart. Strands of astral qi, like earthworms, drilled into her body through the opened acupoints.

A tingling, numbing sensation arose from Xiangfei's body.

"Was he examining my body?"

After a long while, Luo Chen slowly withdrew his finger.

Of the hundred arts of immortality, Luo Chen was proficient in most.

Although many skills on his panel had been sealed along with the collapse of his primordial spirit, two were still usable.

One of them was medicine.

Without divine sense assistance, relying on his astral qi, which moved as he willed, he could barely perform some medical techniques.

After this examination, Luo Chen was now thoroughly familiar with Xiangfei's condition.

"Your injuries are indeed severe. Several major acupoints are blocked by unknown energy and show signs of collapse. More than half of your internal meridians are broken, preventing the blood qi furnace from functioning normally. If this continues, no matter how well you maintain yourself, your golden body will collapse in less than thirty years, and you will die."

Xiangfei gave a bitter smile. "Senior sees clearly."

Luo Chen smiled and said, "But this injury is not incurable."

Xiangfei was stunned.

Luo Chen clasped his hands behind his back and paced slowly along the lakeshore.

"As long as someone is willing to act as a sacrifice, drawing out those unknown energies, and then using good pills to repair the meridians, you can recover as before."

Faced with this answer, the hope that had been kindled in Xiangfei's heart immediately plummeted again.

Not to mention what kind of top-grade pill could repair her severely damaged meridians, just the step of drawing out that energy was something no one dared to do.

This energy was precisely the chaotic energy she had absorbed when she attempted to step into the Ninth Ring of Destruction!

Its terror was such that ordinary people simply could not suppress it. Once it erupted, even a martial artist who had cultivated a Leakless Golden Body could not withstand it.

Suddenly!

Xiangfei's gaze fell on Luo Chen.

In the Battle of Huifeng Mountain Range, the strength of Luo Chen's physical body was still fresh in her memory.

If it were him...

"Come to my room tonight. Before I leave, I'll draw out those unknown energies for you. That way, at least you can extend your life by a hundred years."

From living less than thirty years to extending life by a hundred years sounded exaggerated.

But for a Leakless Realm martial artist who already had a maximum lifespan of eight hundred years, this was not a false statement.

Even with damaged meridians, a Leakless Realm martial artist could still circulate astral qi through their acupoints.

As long as the acupoints were truly unblocked, Xiangfei could indeed live much longer.

Suddenly, Xiangfei found that being under Luo Chen's control didn't seem like such an unacceptable thing?

That night.

Xiangfei entered Luo Chen's room alone. Soon after, sounds like muffled thunder came from within, causing everyone in the Luo Estate to look askance.

The next day.

Chu Zhaonan led a hundred elite Mysterious Armor Soldiers to escort Luo Chen as he left Yixian City.

On the city wall, two graceful figures stood side by side.

One bore signs of age, with a slightly dignified and noble air.

The other was beautiful and radiant, like a spring breeze.

Jing Lan watched the distant figures until Luo Chen's party disappeared from view, then turned her gaze back to Xiangfei.

Facing her jealous yet bitter look, Xiangfei smiled charmingly.

"City Lord Jing, don't misunderstand. Last night, Senior Luo was merely treating my injuries."

"I wasn't thinking anything!" Jing Lan turned around, a hint of relief beneath her unnatural expression.

She would rather believe that Luo Chen was not interested in women than believe she was inferior to another woman.

Letting out a turbid breath, Jing Lan refreshed her spirits.

"Although Brother Luo has left, the task of gathering training resources for him must not stop. In fact, we must work even harder."

With this thought, Jing Lan faced Xiangfei again.

"Regarding the Swallow Fat matter, it's time for you to put pressure on Sanxiang City!"

This time, Xiangfei did not refuse.

"Naturally!"

...

Inside the carriage.

Luo Chen's eyes were tightly shut, all his attention focused within his body.

In the blood qi furnace, a stream of gray, hazy gas was rampaging wildly, causing muffled sounds to echo within Luo Chen's body.

"So this is the so-called chaotic energy?"

Luo Chen observed for a long time but could not discern any clues.

Ultimately, lacking the assistance of divine sense, he could not see the details.

But from the violent state of this gas, he analyzed why cultivators could not train in the Ninth Ring of Destruction, and why Carefree City only allowed Leakless Realm martial artists from other major cities to go there.

Chaotic energy could penetrate anything, slipping into any gap.

For cultivators, who focused on opening all acupoints to draw in the origin qi of heaven and earth, encountering chaotic energy would instantly explode their physical bodies, let alone refining it for their own use.

Only Leakless Realm martial artists, who controlled the opening and closing of their acupoints, could absorb a small amount of chaotic energy and then close their acupoints to prevent being instantly overwhelmed.

However, some weaker martial artists could not control the scale, leading to excessive residual chaotic energy, which in turn severely injured themselves.

Like Xiangfei and the others.

"Leaving it in the body is ultimately a hidden danger. I'll refine it!"

The blood qi furnace began to rotate slowly. Strand after strand of powerful Blood-Refined Divine Astral rushed into it, unraveling and refining that chaotic energy bit by bit, like peeling a silk cocoon.

In this process, some impurities were also expelled by Luo Chen.

A few days later, Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes.

"My blood qi has increased quite a bit."

"If the Ninth Ring of Destruction contains a large amount of this kind of energy, it can indeed nurture many high-level martial artists."

"That place... I'm becoming more and more curious about it."

...

"Whoa!"

The sound of reining in horses came from outside.

Then, the heavy footsteps of armored soldiers approached.

"Master, we have arrived at Red Leaf Ridge."

The carriage curtain was lifted, and Luo Chen, dressed in white, stepped out.

Chu Zhaonan dared not meet his gaze and quickly lowered his head.

He was the sole survivor of Xiao Feng's faction.

Although he did not know why Luo Chen had spared his life afterwards, his fear now was only deeper.

Luo Chen paid no attention to his state of mind, instead looking into the distance.

On a lonely, desolate ridge, only a single tree about three zhang tall swayed in the wind.

Beneath the tree, cracks crisscrossed the ground, and wisps of fierce baleful qi occasionally emerged.

There were also bleached white bones, inspiring fear.

The moment he saw this tree, a diagram appeared in Luo Chen's mind.

"This is no red-leaf tree; it's clearly a Phantom Dream Locust!"

According to the *Secret Records of the Xuan Realm*, there was a top-tier force called the Nether Capital, filled with Phantom Dream Locusts, which day and night attracted demons and monsters.

The tree did not show its grandeur; it was usually only about one zhang in size, looking like an ordinary mortal tree.

Unexpectedly, in this spiritless land of Guixu, there was a Phantom Dream Locust that had grown to three zhang!

This explained why this tree could attract ghostly beings like the Ghost Nightmares.

After carefully examining the Phantom Dream Locust, Luo Chen jumped down from the carriage.

Under the tense gazes of over a hundred Mysterious Armor Soldiers, he strode directly into the range of Red Leaf Ridge.

The baleful qi surged from the cracks in the earth, but Luo Chen seemed oblivious. He simply opened his crimson-golden eyes and meticulously observed the surrounding terrain.

As he observed, he muttered to himself.

"Not enough!"

"Still not enough!"

"I need to make some more detailed arrangements!"

"Moreover, I lack many materials. I need to send someone back to Yixian City to fetch them."

This behavior seemed inexplicable to the Mysterious Armor Soldiers.

But if his former subordinate, Min Longyu, were here, he might know what Luo Chen was going to do.

Set up a formation!

Luo Chen was going to set up a grand formation, transform the terrain of Red Leaf Ridge, and amplify the characteristics of the Phantom Dream Locust a hundredfold!

He wanted to attract more, and more powerful, Ghost Nightmares here!
