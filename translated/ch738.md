# Chapter 738: Thousand-Year Aristocratic Clans, Ten-Thousand-Year Great Houses

Luo Chen had indeed been negligent.

Recently, he had been busy developing the soul art Ghosts and Gods Give Way, while also keeping a number of other concerns in mind, which caused him to forget one thing.

That was that Luo Chen's party was not only five people!

On Black King's body, millions of dragon earthworms were parasitizing!

Adding that when they were on Sun-Moon Island, Yu Ziyou had focused on Zhang Jingqing, and Luo Chen had assumed he could use the dual support of source power and magic power to protect Zhang Jingqing through the teleportation formation, he had not thought of the hidden danger on Black King.

Black King had not mentioned it either!

In short, after they teleported over, this scene appeared.

Black King could not bear the heavy burden. His source power was excessively depleted, yet he still failed to protect the million dragon earthworms parasitizing his body.

Under the enormous pressure, ninety-nine percent of the dragon earthworms were crushed into powder. Black King could not hold back and vomited out great mouthfuls of their flesh and blood.

At this moment, the underground palace stank terribly, like a slaughterhouse.

After hearing Luo Chen's explanation, Fu Chaosheng could not help pressing a hand to his forehead with a headache.

He had also forgotten this matter. Powerful desolate beasts often had parasitic beasts on their bodies.

If the number were small, it would have been fine, but unfortunately, no one had expected Black King's body to contain such a terrifying number of parasites.

When he heard that they were dragon earthworm demon beasts, he felt even more incomparably regretful.

A million dragon earthworms, even if all of them were only first or second tier, were valuable enough to be worth one or two billion spirit stones.

For all of them to be destroyed at once truly made one's heart ache.

Winter Mountain Hermit and Lu Qiuzhen were now staring at Luo Chen in extreme shock. They had never imagined the other party still had such hidden enormous wealth.

A million dragon earthworms were enough to be called a complete demon-beast race.

Who knew where the other party had obtained them? At such a young age, his wealth already surpassed old Sages like them.

But now that it was all gone, their hearts, which were watching the show, seemed to balance somewhat.

"Enough, enough. In the end, it is only a small matter. It is good that everyone arrived safely." Fu Chaosheng waved his hand and comforted Luo Chen. "Afterward, I will order my clansmen to clean Star Island's underground palace, and then compensate the sect with a sum of spirit stones."

He said this lightly, but the pained expression on his face could not deceive anyone.

Luo Chen did not quite understand.

But when he saw the Dao Sect disciples stationed on the island hurrying over, he understood why Fu Chaosheng would look so pained.

Overloaded teleportation required enormous spiritual qi!

Not only had the several supreme-grade spirit stones normally kept inside the underground palace been drained dry in one breath, even the island's fourth-tier spirit vein had been drawn for a large amount of spiritual qi and would be difficult to restore in the short term.

In this way, it affected the normal operation of the teleportation formation on Star Island.

The spirit stones the Fu family had to compensate were clearly not a small sum.

...

On Star Island, the four Sages were all checking the conditions of their clansmen, disciples, and subordinates to ensure they had not suffered heavy injuries.

Luo Chen withdrew his divine sense from Zhang Jingqing, a satisfied expression on his face.

With his source power protecting her body and his magic power shielding her, Zhang Jingqing had ultimately endured it.

At this moment, only her viscera had suffered some damage.

To Luo Chen, injuries of this degree did not count as anything at all.

He transformed his magic power and consecutively cast medical arts several times, including the Spring Breeze Turns to Rain Art and Five Organs Return to Origin Art. Zhang Jingqing's deathly pale little face gradually became rosy.

"Thank you, Sage!"

After thanking him, her worried gaze fell onto Luo Chen's shoulder.

Black King was lying there listlessly.

Luo Chen waved his hand. "Go to Heavenjade for now. Nothing serious has happened to Black King."

Only after Zhang Jingqing left did Black King speak weakly. "Master, Black King made a mistake."

Luo Chen shook his head. "You did somewhat overestimate yourself. I spent considerable effort protecting Qingqing, yet you still wanted to rely on your own strength to shelter a million dragon earthworms. You are lucky you did not ruin yourself as well."

"I thought that with them hiding inside my body, with my powerful physique resisting the outside pressure and source power protecting them, they should be able to make it through. I did not expect... I caused Master such heavy losses."

Facing Black King's words of guilt, Luo Chen was calm.

This trip truly counted as a heavy loss to him.

A million dragon earthworms were extremely valuable!

Originally, he had wanted to bring them back to the Eastern Wilderness and use them to revive the Luo Tian Sect, but now they had all been lost inside.

It could not be said they were completely lost. Black King had absorbed a large amount of dragon earthworm blood back then and was naturally close to these crawling creatures, treating them as his descendants. In the moment of danger, he had ultimately spent enormous effort to preserve several thousand second-tier dragon earthworms.

With this batch of essential seeds, the scale of the race could be more or less cultivated back up later.

Most importantly, nothing major had happened to Black King himself.

"Rest for now. I will go speak with Fu Chaosheng."

Black King made a sound of acknowledgment and listlessly drilled into Luo Chen's sleeve.

Luo Chen summoned Fu Hongzhang, asked where Fu Chaosheng was, then hurried over.

When he arrived, he saw Fu Chaosheng speaking with several cultivators wearing black-and-white Daoist robes. Naturally, he would not go up and disturb them, so he waited quietly at the side.

After a while, Fu Chaosheng returned dispirited.

Seeing Luo Chen waiting, he opened his mouth, wanting to speak but stopping.

Luo Chen was considerate and asked directly, "Do you have to compensate many spirit stones?"

Since the skylight had already been opened, they might as well speak plainly.

Fu Chaosheng sighed. "More than I expected. Originally, I thought compensating eight or ten million spirit stones would be enough. I did not expect them to open their mouths like lions and demand I take out thirty million spirit stones, otherwise they will report this matter to the sect."

Only thirty million?

Luo Chen froze, having thought it would be some astronomical price.

But very quickly, he reacted. This was indeed not a small sum.

Do not look at how he himself possessed several hundred million in wealth despite his youth; those spirit stones had not been earned through his own step-by-step management.

They all came from killing people and seizing treasures!

Back when Luo Chen had painstakingly operated businesses, the greatest amount of savings on his whole person had never exceeded thirty million.

Although he now had enormous wealth, some of the supreme-grade and high-grade spirit stones within it were strategic resources and would not easily be used as currency in transactions.

By comparison, Fu Chaosheng seemed enormously wealthy, but beneath him was a vast family, and he still needed to spend resources supporting his granddaughter at early-stage Sage.

Taking out thirty million all at once counted as cutting into flesh and bone for him.

After pondering briefly, Luo Chen said straightforwardly, "This matter arose because of me, so it should be my responsibility. I will pay this sum of spirit stones."

Fu Chaosheng was stunned. Even though he knew Luo Chen was rather wealthy, he had not expected the other party to be so open and upright.

"This is not very appropriate. After all, I invited you here..."

"There is nothing inappropriate about it. You and I are in a cooperative relationship. Aside from the contents of our cooperation, I do not wish to trouble you too much."

Fu Chaosheng subconsciously nodded.

But very quickly, he came back to himself.

If Luo Chen did not want to trouble him too much, then by implication, he also did not want Fu Chaosheng to trouble him too much outside the contents of their cooperation.

"How about this. You pay half the spirit stones, and this old man will pay the rest. No need to refuse. This matter also bears this old man's responsibility for not reminding you properly. Moreover, when my Fu family clansmen come to clean the underground palace, the accumulated dragon earthworm blood can also be recovered and used, counting as some compensation."

Luo Chen glanced at Fu Chaosheng in surprise, but did not insist further.

It was important to grasp the proper measure in getting along. Half and half would not damage harmony, nor would it entangle them too deeply.

Only, when leaving Star Island, Fu Chaosheng subconsciously looked back toward the underground palace.

"Just returning to Central Continent and already encountering a blood calamity. This omen is somewhat inauspicious."

...

The moment he set foot on Central Continent, Luo Chen vaguely sensed the difference between this place and the Eastern Wilderness or Northern Sea.

First was the omnipresent spiritual qi of heaven and earth!

Its abundance, even on a wilderness path they merely passed through, reached first-tier level.

Any place where humans gathered had second-tier level spiritual qi.

Third-tier spirit veins, which Luo Chen had sought bitterly back then and had even not hesitated to join an opening war to obtain, numbered no fewer than a dozen that he personally saw during the short half-day journey!

One could imagine that under such a spiritual qi environment, fourth-tier spirit veins were probably common sights as well.

This discovery alone left Luo Chen, as well as Winter Mountain Hermit and Lu Qiuzhen, the two Sages from the Northern Sea, incomparably shocked.

They were all lands of the five continents. Was the gap between Central Continent and the other four continents truly this enormous?

Apart from this, another special point also made Luo Chen click his tongue in wonder.

Where his divine sense swept past, he saw no few Initiate and Adept cultivators.

Unlike cultivators of other great continents, who cautiously guarded against one another, these people were relatively harmonious even when they clearly did not come from the same family.

This strange sight could only be attributed by Luo Chen to the fact that Central Continent had only one sect, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

As for the other differences, such as broadly similar clothing styles and the simple architectural style based on black and white, compared to the first two, these were not worth mentioning.

On the Fu family's flying boat, Fu Chaosheng briefly introduced some basic information about Central Continent to Luo Chen and the other two.

"Unlike the Eastern Wilderness, where immortal domains are divided under joint sect governance, and unlike the Northern Sea, where immortal islands are scattered and rogue cultivators are numerous, Central Continent's situation is extremely simple."

"Across the entire Central Continent, there are countless families, but only one sect, and that is my Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!"

"The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect is not only Central Continent's sole sect, but also the common master of all Central Continent's families. Looking across the world, it is also the strongest force!"

"If you can join Star Gate, that is my sect's generosity, and also your honor!"

Unlike his usual smiling face, Fu Chaosheng now rarely revealed a trace of pride.

That was the pride of a successor of the world's foremost holy land.

This pride gave him calm and unhurried confidence, whether facing the Northern Sea's righteous and demonic paths, the late-stage Sage demon monk Kongchan, or Luo Chen and the other two before him.

Ordinarily, because he had to travel outside conducting business, he was amiable toward everyone.

But now, he was no longer that merchant, but a Dao Sect successor, so naturally he did not need to tread carefully and make harmony for profit.

"Star Gate is located at the very center of Central Continent, established atop Heaven-Earth Peak."

"Winter Mountain Hermit, you hold Elder Shen's recommendation token. Fellow Daoist Lu, you hold my recommendation token. Bring your clansmen and disciples, and you can travel there without obstruction. At that time, Star Gate elders will naturally send people to examine you."

"Fellow Daoist Luo, come with me to the Fu family. This old man will naturally arrange what comes afterward."

Fu Chaosheng made arrangements for the next stage of the journey.

Luo Chen naturally had no objection.

The other two looked at one another, and at the moment of parting, each expressed thanks to Fu Chaosheng for bringing them along on this trip.

After the two left, the pride on Fu Chaosheng's face immediately withdrew, and he returned to his previous appearance.

"Forgive me for making a fool of myself."

Luo Chen shook his head. "It is nothing. After all, they are not fellow travelers with us."

They were indeed not fellow travelers.

Those two were rather advanced in years and did not meet the conditions to enter Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven. If they went one step ahead, they could only stop at Heaven-Earth Peak.

Luo Chen was different. He was qualified to become a Dao Sect disciple's dao-protector and enter Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven.

According to Fu Chaosheng, this kind of person, even after likewise joining Heaven-Earth Peak, would have a much greater chance of becoming an offering elder or even a Heaven-Earth Peak elder later.

Luo Chen was more curious about the things he had observed earlier, so he simply asked.

Fu Chaosheng did not conceal anything and directly resolved his doubts.

"Central Continent was originally the place in this realm where spiritual opportunity is most flourishing. Abundant spiritual qi is only normal."

"In addition, because this continent is solely controlled by my Dao Sect, there is also a complete plan for cultivating spirit veins. Situations like those on other continents, where rogue cultivators or sect cultivators overmine spirit stone veins, do not appear."

"Finally, Heaven-Earth Peak's Star Gate is wide open, broadly recruiting Sages from across the world. When these people are alive, they are used by my sect. After they pass away in meditation, the vast magic power across their bodies feeds back into Central Continent's heaven and earth. This is also why Central Continent's spiritual qi grows more and more flourishing."

Luo Chen listened and nodded repeatedly, and the doubts in his heart were resolved.

Cultivators accumulated personal greatness through long years of cultivation, but once they fell, the terrifying magic power accumulated over a lifetime would not disappear for no reason. It would return to heaven and earth.

Fu Chaosheng had gone to the Northern Sea for two hundred years and brought back seven Sages in one return trip.

Even if that counted as a relatively large number, under normal circumstances, there would always be two or three people defecting to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

This was the number for two hundred years. The Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance had been established after antiquity and had existed for three thousand years until now!

How many Northern Sea Sages had it attracted in that time?

What about the cultivation worlds of the Eastern Wilderness, Southern Frontier, and Western Desert?

If one added the Sages cultivated by the Dao Sect itself, then that number...

Merely imagining it briefly made reverence rise in one's heart!

Since Luo Chen was young, he had heard the rumor that the Eastern Wilderness Alchemy Saint had gone far away to Central Continent and joined the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. Looking at it now, he could be said to be imitating a predecessor.

It was not only that Alchemy Saint. Luo Chen had also once heard that a powerful figure had left the Southern Frontier, joined the Central Continent Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, achieved Ascendant, and even ascended to the upper realm.

All these things showed that the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect had carried out its plan to absorb outside-continent talent for many years.

Among so many people, those who ascended were ultimately a minority.

Most Sages, when the limit of their lifespans arrived, could only turn into a handful of yellow earth, adding bricks and tiles to Central Continent's strength.

"Naturally, I will not stay long. The so-called joining of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect is merely to obtain the identity of a Star Gate guest elder, so I can return to Central Continent."

Luo Chen smiled in his heart. He had no intention of devoting his life to Central Continent.

As for how he would return when the time came, Fu Chaosheng would naturally arrange it for him.

Afterward, Luo Chen asked about the strange situation of cultivators in this place getting along harmoniously.

"Everyone reveres the Dao Sect as master, so naturally they will not fight to the death."

Fu Chaosheng explained.

But very quickly, he shook his head again.

"It only looks harmonious on the surface. In truth, competition between the great families is extremely intense."

"You should have some understanding of Central Continent's family hierarchy, yes?"

Luo Chen nodded. "I have heard a little. It seems families are distinguished by great houses and aristocratic clans according to the size of their strength."

"That is somewhat crude. This old man will explain it to you in detail!"

Fu Chaosheng waved his hand and softly recited, "Twelve are the Great Houses, Thirty-Six the Aristocratic Clans. Countless lesser houses all together forge the Heavenly Origin Sect."

"This is the structure of Central Continent's families. The Twelve Great Houses stand high above, thousand-year aristocratic clans enjoy glory for a time, and fallen lesser houses all strive upward."

This simple little poem aptly explained the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's situation.

Through his introduction, Luo Chen also understood the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect more and more deeply.

This Ascendant holy land that dominated Central Continent and watched the world was different from a traditional sect based on master-disciple inheritance. Its interior was mainly based on family inheritance.

Although there was also a small amount of master-disciple inheritance, the mainstream was still the great families distributed across Central Continent.

The so-called Great Houses were top clans that had produced Ascendant great powers.

The so-called Aristocratic Clans were great clans that had produced Sages.

The so-called lesser houses were measured by the number of Adept cultivators.

As for those beneath lesser houses?

They no longer entered the Dao Sect's eye, yet they could not be summarized as rogue cultivators either. Such small households often could only attach themselves to great families, and after their realms had some accomplishment, they would see whether they should establish their own doors.

Luo Chen listened in incomparable shock and at one point lost his voice as he asked, "The Twelve Great Houses. Does that mean Central Continent today has twelve Ascendant great powers?"

Fu Chaosheng was speechless and could only remind him, "They have produced them, not that they presently exist."

Luo Chen smiled awkwardly. He rarely lost composure like this.

It was truly that the coexistence of twelve great powers was somewhat frightening.

One had to know that on the Northern Sea side, the Primordial Demon Sect had only three Ascendant great powers in total, and even adding Northern Departure Great Saint and the Sea Emperor Whale on the demon side, there were only five.

As for the Eastern Wilderness, rumor said the Nether Origin Sect also had three Ascendant great powers. On the demon side, as far as he knew, there was an ancient demon named Di Tian, and even adding Rosycloud, there were only five.

Perhaps the two continents still hid powerful existences of this realm, but their numbers were absolutely not many.

If Central Continent suddenly pulled out twelve, that would truly be somewhat terrifying.

Suddenly.

Fu Chaosheng said in a low voice, "If my sect wished it, it would not be impossible for there to exist a number of great powers counted on both hands at the same time, or even more."

Luo Chen's eyes suddenly widened.

However, the other party had no intention of continuing to explain and instead changed the subject.

"Ten-thousand-year Great Houses are not immortal, and thousand-year Aristocratic Clans will also sink. Within my sect, even great aristocratic families undergo regular examinations."

"Let us set Great Houses aside for now. Aristocratic Clans are assessed once every five hundred years, and lesser houses once every three hundred years."

"If a Sage passes away in meditation and his descendants are unworthy, failing to produce a second Sage within five hundred years, then the Aristocratic Clan name will automatically be canceled and fall into the ranks of lesser houses. Lesser house assessments follow this pattern."

"To ensure family inheritance and talented people in every generation, competition between families over cultivation resources is extremely intense!"

"And the scope of this resource competition is not limited to family territories. It also takes place among the clansmen who enter the Dao Sect."

"The sect grand competition every hundred years is an open competition of real blades and true swords."

"And the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven grand competition once every five hundred years is the most intense one!"

"My Fu family had decent luck. When I grew old and weak, Qinglan rose up. But after her, can that luck continue? This old man does not know and does not dare gamble. Therefore, I must seize this chance and ensure Qinglan can obtain more resources and a higher position inside the Dao Sect!"

"For this reason, I hope Fellow Daoist will exert your full strength when the time comes and help Qinglan accomplish this!"

As he spoke to the end, Fu Chaosheng, whose ruddy face could not hide its wrinkles, stared at Luo Chen with burning eyes.

Not only that, with a wave of his hand, spiritual light flickered, and four jade bottles appeared before Luo Chen.

*(End of Chapter)*
