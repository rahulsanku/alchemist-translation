# Chapter 1006: Family Reunited, Fourth-Order Initiation

The Alchemy Saint's blessed land, which had enjoyed over a decade of peace, welcomed a touch of liveliness today.

But soon, that liveliness returned to calm.

Three figures separated from the crowd and flew swiftly toward the southeast.

"She's been staying at Little Stone Flat all these years, never taking a single step outside." Gu Caiyi's voice was soft as she spoke against the brushing wind.

Luo Chen nodded after hearing this, his expression somewhat complicated.

Little Stone Flat.

The various peaks and cave dwellings in the Alchemy Saint's blessed land originally had no names. Most had been named by Luo Tian Sect cultivators based on past experiences after they moved in.

Thus, many place names felt familiar.

And Huiniang naming her residence Little Stone Flat was undoubtedly a way to commemorate that cave dwelling in Baili Qingchuan.

What was there to commemorate about that place?

Was it the hundred years of waiting for him in vain, or the painful memories of enduring mana burning her body, or perhaps resentment toward him?

Canglong spoke up from the side: "A year after you left, after I returned to the blessed land, she sought me out through her disciple Yao Mingyue and earnestly begged me to use the Extreme Ice Seal method to seal Luo Lingxi."

Luo Chen was momentarily stunned, but quickly realized what that meant, and his brow furrowed instantly.

Back when he had been trying to break Canglong's seal, he had also studied the *Ten Directions Frozen Extreme Dao*, that Icecastle inheritance technique.

He naturally had some understanding of its sealing art—the Extreme Ice Seal.

It was a high-level sealing technique that could preserve a cultivator's current state.

Its advantage was maintaining the sealed person's condition before sealing, but the drawback was that the cultivator could not use this seal to recover from injuries, and the process required a constant external supply of extreme cold energy.

The only reason Luo Chen frowned was this: "Lingxi's cultivation is too low. How could she withstand the Extreme Ice Seal?"

Canglong spoke with admiration in her tone: "I advised Fellow Daoist Sima against it at the time, but she was very insistent. She said outright that your departure had no fixed time. If you returned too late, or if you failed to find a fourth-order Devil Heart Gu, then Luo Lingxi would miss the optimal age for cultivation. Only by using the Extreme Ice Seal to seal him could there be any leeway left. As for how the weak Luo Lingxi could withstand the Extreme Ice Seal's cold energy..."

Speaking up to here, Canglong glanced at Luo Chen and said in a low voice: "Sima Huiniang chose to use her own blood and qi to shield Luo Lingxi from the cold's invasion. A mother's love for her child—nothing surpasses it."

Luo Chen pressed his lips together and did not ask further.

The three moved extremely fast. Under Gu Caiyi's guidance, they soon arrived at Little Stone Flat.

Though it shared the same name, the scenery was vastly different.

Luo Chen had no heart to look closely. With a slight wave of his hand, he dissolved the protective formation and strode inside.

The moment he entered the cave dwelling, their eyes met, and Luo Chen's stern expression involuntarily softened.

"Why did it come to this?"

Back when Luo Chen had treated her, Sima Huiniang had recovered as if the medicine had banished the illness. Even the lost lifespan had been replenished through longevity-extending spiritual medicines. After subsequent careful nurturing, she had not only been able to continue cultivation but had also regained a fresh and radiant appearance.

But now, Sima Huiniang's face had withered considerably.

Her originally lustrous black hair had also taken on a touch of grayish white.

Maintaining Luo Lingxi with her blood and qi day after day for ten years had nearly depleted her vital energy.

Sima Huiniang looked at Luo Chen. No matter how much resentment she had harbored in her heart, it all dissipated like smoke at this moment. She murmured, "Husband, you're back."

Luo Chen sighed, stepped forward, and pulled her into his embrace.

Gu Caiyi watched this scene from behind. Even though she was usually prone to jealousy, she could not muster any such feeling now.

Huiniang placed her hopes on their son Luo Lingxi, but she herself was different. She had made up her mind to cultivate diligently, break through to the Sage realm in the future, and stay with Luo Chen forever.

If love endures, what need is there for day and night together?

For cultivators, this was even more true.

Let her husband share some of his affection now—what harm was there?

Gu Caiyi turned and nodded to Canglong. "Next, I'll trouble you."

Canglong smiled faintly and walked toward another room filled with a chilling cold.

Seeing this, Sima Huiniang was jolted awake and quickly asked, "Husband, have you already retrieved the Devil Heart Gu?"

Luo Chen released her and nodded solemnly. "I have indeed retrieved it. Today, I will lift the restriction on our son."

For a moment, Sima Huiniang was overcome with excitement.

Canglong opened the stone door, and a wave of cold air rushed out.

Seeing the young man frozen inside the blood-colored jade, she raised an eyebrow.

"Blood permeating the mystic ice—Fellow Daoist Sima has truly paid a great price!"

Sima Huiniang paid no attention to this. With a hopeful look on her pale face, she said, "Please, Elder Canglong, undo the seal on my son!"

Canglong nodded and slowly began forming hand seals.

Mana flowed, gradually seeping into the blood-colored jade.

Slowly, the ten-thousand-year mystic ice began to melt, turning into trickling streams that flew toward Canglong's fingertips.

After about the time it takes an incense stick to burn, Canglong twisted her slender fingers, pinched a blood bead, and withdrew from the room.

"The rest is up to you."

"Thank you!"

Luo Chen rose calmly and entered the room.

Luo Lingxi, in his youthful state, blinked his eyelashes as if about to wake. Luo Chen pressed a finger to his brow.

"Sleep a little longer. When you wake up, you can begin the cultivation you've dreamed of."

As he spoke, Luo Chen took a vigorously beating heart from his storage ring.

The Devil Heart Gu was inside this demon heart!

*Bang!*

The great door slammed shut, blocking the view from outside.

Sima Huiniang's body trembled slightly, her worry evident. Gu Caiyi comforted her from the side.

"Don't worry. With your husband handling it, Lingxi will surely be fine. Instead of worrying, you should think about how to guide his cultivation once he's well, so he doesn't fall short of his father's name."

Sima Huiniang's eyes lit up.

Yes!

From now on, her son could cultivate!

She needed to prepare all kinds of cultivation resources for him.

Canglong chuckled lightly. "Brother Luo Chen saved my life and helped me achieve the Sage realm. He's too formidable now, and there's little I can do to repay him. Why don't I extend that gratitude to his son? I'll use this ten-thousand-year mystic ice, which contains a vast amount of your blood and qi, to craft a protective treasure tailored for Luo Lingxi!"

Sima Huiniang looked at the elegant and beautiful woman before her and nodded gratefully. "I, your junior's wife, am deeply grateful to Elder Canglong on behalf of my son."

Canglong waved her hand, her gaze falling on the tightly shut door.

Once Luo Lingxi recovered, he would be doted on by all and would surely achieve great things in cultivation.

The Luo Tian Sect should have a worthy successor!

...

The calamity of the Luo family's Devil Heart Gu, strictly speaking, had lasted about a hundred and thirty years.

It began when Luo Chen's physical body underwent nirvana and his mana eroded Sima Huiniang, later passing on to their son Luo Lingxi.

The physical and psychological pain endured by two generations was something outsiders could hardly imagine.

From the once capable and intelligent Sima Huiniang gradually becoming extreme in temperament, even developing resentment, one could see the toll it had taken.

Until today, there was finally a solution.

Luo Chen looked at his son lying before him and let out a faint sigh.

"I hope everything can return to how it was!"

He hesitated no longer. The Withering-Bloom Divine Flame appeared in his palm and began to burn the heart that still beat vigorously despite being separated from its owner.

A scorched stench instantly filled the entire room. Large amounts of flesh and blood burned to ash, and the terrifying energy concentrated into a single point. The heart beat more and more violently.

Suddenly!

A strange fragrance that made one's mouth water appeared. The heart in his palm had transformed into a crystal-clear little insect with clearly visible blood vessels.

It was the Devil Heart Gu!

A demonic gu that the Li Tian Master had painstakingly nurtured for centuries, its grade nearly crossing into the fifth order.

With a solemn expression, Luo Chen slowly pressed this nearly fifth-order Devil Heart Gu toward Luo Lingxi's chest, where his heart lay.

The moment they touched, the little insect eagerly burrowed inside on its own.

Instantly, Luo Lingxi's body began to tremble violently.

Even in his slumber, the young man let out agonized roars, like a wild beast's howl.

This process was the most critical. It required drawing out the third-order Devil Heart Gu that had coexisted with Luo Lingxi, then having it devour the top-grade fourth-order Devil Heart Gu that Luo Chen had refined.

Once successful, Luo Lingxi would no longer be controlled by anyone and could even claim the powerful gu for his own use.

If he were more extreme, he could even cultivate child gu and plant them in other cultivators, then plunder their hard-earned cultivation.

But for the inferior to overcome the superior was no easy matter.

The pain involved could only be borne by Luo Lingxi himself.

All Luo Chen could do was fully suppress the fourth-order Devil Heart Gu, allowing Luo Lingxi to instinctively devour that thing.

Time passed minute by minute.

A full half-month later, the tightly shut stone door finally rumbled open.

Sima Huiniang, who had been waiting outside, rushed forward without hesitation, grabbing Luo Chen's broad hands, her eyes full of longing.

"Husband, how is Lingxi?"

Luo Chen smiled and nodded.

"Everything went smoothly!"

Sima Huiniang was so relieved that she nearly collapsed.

Luo Chen infused a stream of mana into her and said gently, "Go see him. The Devil Heart Gu's grade is too high for him to control now. After it devoured the old one, I sealed it, but normal cultivation is no longer a problem."

Sima Huiniang rushed inside. On the cushion, the young man had opened his eyes, his expression dazed.

"My son, my son, you're finally well..." Sima Huiniang hugged the young man, already sobbing uncontrollably.

Luo Lingxi looked at the woman with graying hair and wrinkled face and instinctively said, "Mother."

Then he raised his head to look at the tall figure at the door.

"Father."

Luo Chen nodded gently in response. "Mm."

...

Nighttime.

Inside the Little Stone Flat cave dwelling, under the glow of night pearls, three figures sat together, enjoying a sumptuous dinner.

The aroma of the food, the woman's gentle voice, the young man's silent chewing—all stirred ripples in Luo Chen's calm heart.

In his previous life, he had never experienced this.

In this life, even when the three of them had lived harmoniously like this, Huiniang's face had always carried a faint, lingering worry.

Now, Huiniang had lost her radiance, her beauty faded, but the smile at the corners of her mouth was utterly sincere.

This feeling was rather nice.

Luo Lingxi ate another bowl of spirit-rich rice—something he had never dared to eat freely before. Now, he could finally indulge.

After eating his fill, he couldn't help but look at Luo Chen.

"Father, did you go to the Southern Frontier?"

"Mm."

"To find the Devil Heart Gu... it must have been very hard and dangerous, right?"

"It was fine."

Luo Chen said "fine," but thinking back on his journey to the Southern Frontier, he felt quite emotional inside.

Even if it wasn't a matter of life and death, it had still been an arduous ordeal.

Of course, in front of his family, he wouldn't say much.

Now that Sima Huiniang's resentment toward Luo Chen had completely dissipated, she had returned to her former understanding and considerate state. Seeing that Luo Chen didn't want to dwell on it, she quickly changed the subject.

"Husband, now that Lingxi can cultivate normally, shouldn't we find him a good master?"

Hearing this, Luo Lingxi looked at Luo Chen with eager anticipation.

Who in the world could compare to his own father?

And he happened to have a water-fire dual spirit root, perfectly suited to inherit his father's supreme fire-attribute Dao lineage!

But facing this expectation, Luo Chen shook his head.

"I'm going to be very busy from now on. I won't have time to teach you carefully. Besides, you're not suited for cultivating fire-attribute techniques."

Hearing this, Luo Lingxi was somewhat disappointed.

But he didn't lose heart. Being able to cultivate at all was already wonderful—how could he ask for more?

Sima Huiniang hesitated for a moment, then asked, "If fire techniques won't work, then water techniques are the way. Logically, I could teach Lingxi, but I didn't break through to the Adept realm normally back then. I'm unclear on some details, and I'm afraid I'd teach him wrong. Husband, what do you think of Elder Canglong?"

Luo Chen put down his chopsticks and said calmly, "Canglong only advanced to the Sage realm ten years ago. She still needs time to polish her cultivation. Lingxi needs to build his foundation quickly, and we can't afford delays. So we need to find another master."

Sima Huiniang asked curiously, "Do you have someone in mind, husband?"

"Let him study under Yingzhang!"

"Under Sect Master Li?" Sima Huiniang murmured, seeming a bit puzzled.

Luo Chen explained patiently, "There are many water-attribute cultivators in the Luo Tian Sect. Those above the Adept realm include Canglong, Li Yingzhang, Caiyi, and others. But if we're talking about the most stable cultivation and the most abundant mana, it has to be Yingzhang. Moreover, his temperament is very steady, and he manages the sect in an orderly manner. Lingxi needs to quickly charge toward the Initiate realm, but he can't neglect his foundation. Studying under Yingzhang will not only lay a solid foundation and temper his character but also expose him to the affairs of the Luo Tian Sect. This is the best choice!"

After Luo Chen's explanation, Sima Huiniang also began to think that Li Yingzhang was the best choice as her son's master.

She had been too hasty, only thinking of finding the most powerful master for her son, forgetting that the most powerful wasn't necessarily the most suitable.

And most importantly!

Li Yingzhang was now the Sect Master of the Luo Tian Sect!

If Luo Lingxi could learn something, it would greatly help him take over the foundation Luo Chen had built in the future.

At the very least, she could offer guidance from the side based on her rich experience.

Dinner gradually ended amid the casual conversation of the three.

Watching the woman clearing the dishes, Luo Chen took his leave.

"Senior Deng Taiyue is probably getting impatient. I need to go see him. As for Lingxi's apprenticeship, you can go talk to Li Yingzhang yourself. He won't refuse."

Sima Huiniang smiled. "I understand, husband. Go ahead."

After Luo Chen left, Sima Huiniang continued tidying the table.

When she came to the bronze basin to wash up, she suddenly realized how old and haggard the face reflected in the water was.

...

Under the night sky, on Alchemy Mountain.

Deng Taiyue suddenly opened his eyes and walked out of the great hall, seeing a figure approaching under the moonlight.

He let out a breath of turbid qi and broke into a broad smile.

"Disturbing Brother Luo's family reunion is my fault."

Luo Chen waved his hand. "It's nothing. Though we of the Dao path don't claim to be free of desires, family affection is ultimately but a moment's pleasure. How could I let personal matters delay a fellow Daoist's prospects?"

Deng Taiyue's expression turned serious, and he stopped beating around the bush.

"I've prepared all the medicinal herbs you need. Can the Initiation Pill really be refined now?"

(End of Chapter)
