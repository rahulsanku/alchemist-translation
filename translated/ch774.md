# Chapter 774: Eastern Wilderness Situation, Alchemy Grandmaster Luo Chen

Aboard the cloud carriage, Luo Chen’s divine sense swept over the jade slip, and the geographical location of Divine Origin City became clear at a glance.

After reading it, a thoughtful expression appeared on Luo Chen’s face.

Many things could be discerned from the surface alone.

The Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance’s Divine Origin City in the Eastern Wilderness was not independent and autonomous like in the Northern Sea; instead, it was constrained.

The nine major immortal cities surrounding it formed a pattern of three front, three back, and three right, tightly encircling it.

And the gap left behind was precisely where the East Yang Sect’s mountain gate was located.

From this alone, one could see how deeply the Nether Origin Sect guarded against the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!

To prevent the Dao Sect from infiltrating the Eastern Wilderness through the Merchant Alliance, they had not only placed the Merchant Alliance headquarters within the territory of the East Yang Sect, which the Nether Origin Sect trusted most, but had also achieved encirclement and constraint in the details.

He just didn’t know if it had been like this from the start, or if it had become this way because of the Alchemy Saint incident.

Luo Chen couldn’t be bothered to ponder deeply.

So-called Merchant Alliance was merely a tool for the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect to gather wealth and intelligence, having nothing to do with his position as a guest elder of the Star Gate.

On the contrary, he was quite happy.

Because it was very close!

With a thought, his divine sense transmitted sound into the ears of others.

“You all rest. I’ll take over controlling the cloud carriage!”

As he spoke, he had already taken over control of the Sixfold Imperial Flying Cloud Carriage from a distance.

Vast mana surged into it. The cloud carriage paused for a moment, then burst forth with brilliant light, suddenly shooting out, tracing a bright arc across the sky.

Under his control, the cloud carriage’s speed was more than three times what it had been, no less than the full-speed flight of a late-stage Adept.

Even though the East Yang Domain was far larger than some smaller cultivation domains, at this speed, crossing half of the East Yang Domain would take less than a day.

Of course, if Luo Chen were flying at full speed himself, this time would be even faster!

But with Zhang Jingqing, a Initiate cultivator, and Sang Jinghe, who had only recently formed his core, Luo Chen couldn’t do that.

After all, he had endured two hundred years; he could afford a day or two.

While multitasking to control the cloud carriage, Luo Chen continued to refine the Crimson Firmament Qi.

This kind of naturally formed spiritual essence, even after being subdued, still retained its wild nature.

If not for the Primordial Unity Cauldron helping with the refinement, the loss would have been extremely heavy.

Following the method in the sword scripture, Luo Chen first used the Primordial Unity Cauldron to refine the violent parts of the Crimson Firmament Qi, and after refining it to a certain degree, he then integrated it into the Yuantu Sword.

The Crimson Firmament Qi merged with the Yuantu Sword, slowly nurturing into sword qi.

In this process, Luo Chen himself was also slightly affected.

His edge was revealed, his aura compelling.

The reactions of Black King, Chi Zhong, and others had already indirectly indicated the subtle changes in Luo Chen.

…

No distinction between day and night, no pause between dawn and dusk.

Under the vast starry sky, the Sixfold Imperial Flying Cloud Carriage wobbled to a stop outside an immortal city.

“After all, it’s just the seat of a Golden Core Grand Sect’s sect master. Splendid enough, but lacking in grandeur. Under my full force, it’s almost falling apart.”

Luo Chen muttered a few words and stored the cloud carriage into his storage ring.

Heavenjade and the others gathered around.

“Master, it’s already late at night. Should we enter the city?”

“Of course!”

Without hesitation, Luo Chen strode toward the city gate.

The towering black city wall, under the starlight, resembled a crouching giant beast.

Luo Chen walked step by step to the city gate, where a cultivator was already waiting.

“Halt, who goes—”

Luo Chen casually tossed out his Star Gate guest elder identity token.

The unique mana belonging to Luo Chen was infused into it, and it immediately shone with light.

This meant it was not a forgery, but legitimately held!

The cultivator was startled for a moment, but quickly reacted.

“Adept, please wait a moment. This humble one will open the city gate for you immediately.”

“Mm.”

The heavy black gate slowly opened before Luo Chen’s eyes.

Meanwhile, in a cave dwelling within Divine Origin City, a cultivator received a transmission talisman with surprise.

“Strange, at this hour, why would someone come from the Central Continent? Shouldn’t the focus be on the Northern Sea and the Western Desert?”

As the transmission talisman slowly burned, the cultivator’s brow furrowed slightly.

“Star Gate guest elder, Luo Chen?”

But the next moment, his eyes lit up.

“Could it be that Wilderness Wanderer?”

With this thought, the man’s eyes rolled, and he immediately rose.

…

By the time Luo Chen arrived at the city lord’s mansion, someone was already waiting for him under the starry night.

Wide robes and large sleeves, hair bound in a high crown.

His smile was warm, like a spring breeze.

Luo Chen straightened his clothes and made a Daoist salute. “Disturbing you, Fellow Daoist, at such a late hour. This is truly Luo’s discourtesy.”

“No matter. I, Gu, had nothing important to do anyway. Come, please enter!”

Hearing the surname Gu, Luo Chen couldn’t help but raise an eyebrow slightly.

On the way in, the two introduced themselves to each other.

As expected, this cultivator named Gu Zuokai was indeed from the Gu Great House, one of the Twelve Great Houses!

The Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance was divided across four continents, each with four elders at the Sage level.

However, those elders were basically from aristocratic clans, rarely involving members of the Great Houses.

Now, the Grand Elder of the Merchant Alliance in the Eastern Wilderness was actually sent by the Gu Great House.

From this, one could see how much importance the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect placed on the Eastern Wilderness.

Luo Chen was initially very careful in his conversation with the other party, but gradually discovered that the other party was very easy to get along with!

This so-called “easy to get along with” was different from Fu Chaosheng’s earlier friendliness; rather, it seemed like the other party was deliberately trying to befriend him.

Was it because of his outstanding performance in the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven?

Luo Chen’s guess was not far off.

Gu Zuokai looked at the young man before him, who was like an unsheathed sword, and couldn’t help but admire inwardly.

“The clan said this man was extraordinary and worth befriending. Now that I see him, he’s even more impressive than the rumors. If I can really pull him into my Gu family, he will surely be a powerful asset!”

While he was secretly admiring, Luo Chen, however, did not beat around the bush and directly asked about the current situation in the Eastern Wilderness.

Gu Zuokai was not coy either and immediately explained the situation in the Eastern Wilderness in detail.

“The demon beast calamity in the Eastern Wilderness has lasted for a hundred years. The former thirty-six domains have long lost their glory.”

“Now, among the thirty-six domains, twelve have completely fallen, and three are suffering the ravages of war. The major forces that survived the front lines have gathered ahead, using the five domains of the Mu Manor, Fenghua Palace, and others as their main bases to form the ‘Desperate Army,’ using the three front-line domains as the battlefield, engaged in a bloody war of attrition.”

“As for the Extreme East Six Domains you were asking about, they have long been destroyed!”

Hearing this, Luo Chen’s hand under his sleeve clenched tightly.

Even though he had already known from intelligence that the Extreme East Six Domains, closest to the Million Great Mountains, had fallen, hearing the result confirmed from someone else’s mouth still made his heart tighten.

He asked in a deep voice, “What is this ‘Desperate Army’ you speak of?”

Gu Zuokai smiled slightly and explained the origin of this so-called Desperate Army to Luo Chen in detail.

The demon beasts were powerful, and the various domains and sects were scattered, unable to face the vast tide of demon beasts head-on.

But cultivators were not dead men; they knew how to run. They also understood the principle: lose land, keep people; both land and people are preserved. Lose people, keep land; both land and people are lost.

Therefore, after determining that they could not stop the demon beasts, they would flee to the rear.

When too many people fled, they became like locusts and refugees, harassing the various major sects in the rear.

According to Eastern Wilderness history, the further back the sect forces were, the earlier they were actually established, with deep foundations.

They were not about to indulge these “refugees.”

After watching twelve domains fall and three become battlefields, to avoid following the same path, the major forces in the rear joined forces to stop the fleeing cultivators from retreating further.

They ordered them to stop at the front line of the human-demon beast battlefield.

To this end, they even vacated five domains to serve as their wartime headquarters.

These five domains were: the Mu Manor where the Mu family was located, the Fenghua Domain where Fenghua Palace was, the White Crane Domain where the White Crane Sect was, the Hundred Beasts Domain where the Hundred Beasts Sect was, and the Sword Domain where the Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion was.

They provided territory, provided resources, established rules, integrated this scattered and vast force, and formed a cultivator army to resist the demon race.

This was the origin of the Desperate Army.

To be placed in a desperate situation and then survive!

Either die on the human-demon beast battlefield, or be suppressed and annihilated together by the twenty-one Sage-level upper sects in the rear!

Only by turning around and charging forward, killing out a bright and clear sky, could they return to their homeland!

After understanding the origin of the so-called Desperate Army, a glimmer of hope rose in Luo Chen’s heart.

Before the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu disappeared, he had clearly known that Sima Huiniang was not dead. If they were smart enough to flee to the rear, they had a chance to survive.

Perhaps, they were in this so-called Desperate Army.

While harboring this hope, Luo Chen asked, “The situation in the Eastern Wilderness is so dire. Doesn’t the Holy Land have anything to say about it?”

“What is there to say?” Gu Zuokai sneered. “Who knows how many demon beasts are actually in the Million Great Mountains? In the early years, they carried out that ‘Pioneering War,’ encroaching step by step on demon territory. Although they did indeed conquer many territories, just like those people on our front lines now, some smarter and more powerful demon beasts fled to the rear. Accumulating like this for several thousand years, they have become a force to be reckoned with! Now, the Nether Origin Sect is holding its troops still, merely stretching the front line, enlarging the battlefield, using the lives of countless sects, families, scattered cultivators, and common people as the price to test the depths of the demon race!”

Luo Zhang opened his mouth. From his perspective, the inaction of the Holy Land was, in the other’s view, the Eastern Wilderness Holy Land Nether Origin Sect’s overall strategic probing.

But when this strategy fell upon individuals, upon small sects and small factions, it was a devastating blow!

Of course, if he were in that position, he might do the same.

What was interesting was Gu Zuokai’s disdain for the “Pioneering War,” a strategy of expanding territory.

Luo Chen thought deeply and quickly understood.

The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect was not so slow!

In ancient times, they fought a single battle to conquer the Central Continent, purging the demonic aura, thus ushering in three thousand years of peace and prosperity.

The cultivators blanketing the Central Continent he had seen, with Adepts like clouds and Sages appearing frequently, were the abundant fruits of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect’s decisive conquest of the Central Continent back then.

“Of course, the Nether Origin Sect hasn’t been completely inactive. In this process, they occasionally dispatch Sage-level sects, and sometimes send their own disciples into the battlefield to kill demons.”

“At least, from a moral standpoint, they can’t be faulted.”

After saying all this, Gu Zuokai’s expression turned serious.

“In my view, the Nether Origin Sect’s approach is more like pulling back their fist to strike more powerfully.”

“Contracting the front line is one thing, ordering the formation of the Desperate Army is another. Next, there might be an even bigger move!”

A bigger move?

Luo Chen fell silent.

The so-called big move was probably to have those survivors counterattack the demon race, while the Nether Origin Sect itself would also send out its army to recover lost territory.

But in this process, the Desperate Army would definitely be at the very front, and the casualties would be unimaginable!

If Huiniang and the others were really still alive…

Luo Chen suddenly looked up. “Is there a teleportation formation within Divine Origin City?”

Gu Zuokai was stunned for a moment. “You want to go to the front line?”

Luo Chen nodded.

Gu Zuokai spread his hands. “I’m sorry to say, but the Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance is not permitted to establish teleportation formations in the Eastern Wilderness. If you want to go to the five front-line domains, you’ll have to borrow the East Yang Sect’s teleportation formation. But at this time, the cost of a single teleportation is a bit expensive. Moreover, under wartime conditions, it’s hard for ordinary people to borrow it…”

“Please, Fellow Daoist, help me out.”

As he spoke, Luo Chen handed over a jade bottle.

Gu Zuokai waved his hand dismissively. “Wanderer, you’re being too polite. Even without…”

The bottle stopper was removed, and a thick pill fragrance wafted out.

Gu Zuokai’s expression changed slightly.

He carefully took the jade bottle, examined it with his divine sense for a long time, and finally said in a deep voice, “I will do my best!”

…

Shangyang City!

A major city directly under the East Yang Sect, on a scale no less than Divine Origin City, and one of the top ten immortal cities in the Eastern Wilderness!

Here, an ultra-long-distance teleportation formation was arranged, which could reach the White Crane Domain.

It was said that because the East Yang Sect and the White Crane Sect had a good relationship and exchanged with each other, they could have this kind of two-way teleportation formation.

Standing outside a great hall, Luo Chen said to Gu Zuokai, “These attendants of mine are different from ordinary people. It’s not very suitable for them to walk around the Eastern Wilderness at this time. I would appreciate it if you could arrange for someone to escort them.”

Gu Zuokai looked at Heavenjade and the others but didn’t see anything unusual.

However, when the black dragon pattern on the white robe of Luo Chen’s left arm suddenly came to life, turning into a small dragon that leaped onto Zhang Jingqing’s shoulder, his expression changed slightly.

At that moment, he sensed the aura of a fellow Sage!

The rumor that the Wilderness Wanderer had a fourth-order Demon Emperor level spirit pet was indeed true!

Such an existence was indeed not suitable for walking openly in the Eastern Wilderness at this time.

But for the sake of that bottle of Hongyuan Pills, he still agreed to the matter.

“Fellow Daoist, go ahead. With the speed of the merchant caravan, as long as there are no accidents, they will reach the White Crane Domain in at most two or three years.”

“Then, many thanks, Fellow Daoist!”

Luo Chen cupped his hands, gave a few more instructions to Heavenjade and Black King, and then strode toward the heavily guarded great hall.

The cultivators made way, and the gate opened.

Luo Chen stepped inside, and a Sage cultivator greeted him.

“Please!”

“I’ll trouble you, Fellow Daoist Lu.”

“No trouble at all. The few bottles of pills you took out earlier, Fellow Daoist Luo, were quite good. I wonder if you have any more to spare?”

“Unfortunately, those were the last ten bottles I had on me. However, I am proficient in the method of refining this pill. If Fellow Daoist Lu wants more in the future, feel free to come find me!”

“Then I thank Fellow Daoist Luo in advance!”

Sage Lu’s face was full of joy.

For Sage cultivators, external things like pills were no longer of much use.

But that was only for ordinary pills!

The ten bottles of Hongyuan Pills that Luo Chen had taken out earlier as the teleportation fee were all of middle grade. He had taken a small amount and had yet to refine them fully.

It could be imagined that after complete refinement, his mana would have considerable growth!

The third layer of the Sage realm that had troubled him for years might be broken through with these ten bottles of Hongyuan Pills. If he could continue to take them afterward, the late Sage realm might not be impossible!

After handing Luo Chen a jade token and watching him enter the teleportation formation, Sage Lu personally activated the formation.

When a flash of light passed, Luo Chen’s figure had completely disappeared.

Sage Lu breathed a sigh of relief. No accident had occurred.

He strode out of the teleportation hall, chatted with Gu Zuokai for a while, and then returned to his residence, intending to continue refining that Hongyuan Pill.

But before he could sit down, he heard a report that a junior disciple was requesting an audience.

“Is it Chi Zhong?”

Sage Lu was a little curious. Normally, this junior disciple from the Nether Origin Sect wouldn’t come looking for him.

He didn’t put on any airs and quickly summoned the other party.

When Chi Zhong explained his purpose, Sage Lu showed a hesitant expression.

The person he had just personally sent off was actually a guest elder of the Star Gate?

No wonder Gu Zuokai had personally vouched for him.

He smiled and said to Chi Zhong, “Rest assured, I will report this news to your sect.”

“Then, many thanks, Uncle-Master.” Chi Zhong bowed respectfully and withdrew.

After he left, Sage Lu took out a jade slip and began to inscribe its contents.

“Star Gate guest elder, named Luo Chen.”

“Early Sage realm, extraordinary bearing, suspected sword cultivator.”

He paused, hesitated for a moment, and then added the information he knew.

“Capable of refining fourth-order middle-grade cultivation pills, suspected Alchemy Grandmaster!”

(End of Chapter)
