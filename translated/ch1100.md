# Chapter 1100: Devil Lord’s Malice, Ascendant Cultivation

The Demon-Fall Land truly contained an abundance of opportunities rarely seen in the world.

Even in Luo Chen’s eyes, now that he had achieved the Ascendant realm, there were still things within that made his heart burn with desire.

Take the inexhaustible mineral deposits in the Thousand-Ren Mountains, the heavenly soil within the Flowing Sand Sea, the fifth-rank Vermilion Bird Heavenly Flame in the Blazing Refinement Prison, not to mention the high-grade spirit medicines that could be seen everywhere in the Wood Heaven Plain.

These were all excellent treasures that greatly benefited the Ascendant realm!

When Luo Chen had gone there back then, he had been in a hurry, his cultivation insufficient, and he had to constantly guard against the Taisui accompanying him. The treasures he had discovered were likely only the tip of the iceberg.

At the very least, many of the inheritances within Bright Radiance Heaven still remained unclaimed!

High-level cultivators often drew on the strengths of many schools. Others might not know, but Luo Chen was confident he had the ability to obtain other inheritances.

As long as the Demon-Fall Land opened again!

But achieving that would be somewhat difficult with his current means.

Li Canghai was different. He was a grandmaster of formation dao, and what he had learned happened to originate from the lineage of Devil Lord Heaven-Refining. If Li Canghai had the intention, he could certainly take the initiative to open the Demon-Fall Land.

When that time came, Luo Chen could not only eradicate the root of the problem but also sweep through the place, making ample preparations for his later ascension to the Immortal Realm.

It was never wrong to bring more traveling expenses before leaving home.

However, facing Luo Chen’s proposal, Li Canghai’s expression turned grave.

“Luo Chen, I advise you not to make such greedy plans!”

Luo Chen’s expression grew serious, and he sat up straight. “Please, Fellow Daoist, enlighten me.”

Li Canghai answered with a question: “Have you not seen the lesson of Blood Sea Ancestor?”

Luo Chen frowned. “Does the Demon-Fall Land restrict Ascendant cultivators?”

Li Canghai nodded slightly, his tone heavy. “Indeed. Back then, most of those who entered were Adepts and Sages. The only Ascendant was Blood Sea Ancestor of the Primordial Demon Sect. Logically, he should have dominated everything, but he was greatly suppressed inside, able to exert no more than twenty or thirty percent of his full strength. So when competing for resources in the Five Elements Heaven, that old monster Hatred Heaven dared to rise up and contend with the Primordial Demon Sect cultivators, even killing three of their Sage elders. But Hatred Heaven ultimately underestimated an Ascendant cultivator. Even suppressed, he was still personally slain by Blood Sea Ancestor—his body and soul utterly destroyed. Yet Blood Sea Ancestor fared no better. Inside the Demon-Fall Land, he was suppressed terribly, and the strange beasts and living beings there harbored immense malice toward him! Just to cross the Flowing Sand Sea, he was forced to self-detonate a desolate beast stone dragon soul from his sect. Outside the Wood Heaven Plain, he was besieged by three fifth-rank sentient powerhouses—the Taisui, the Wood Man King, and the Dusk Vine Crow Ancestor… By the time he finally struggled through the Five Elements Heaven, he was already severely wounded, and ultimately fell in a small inheritance world.”

Hearing these secrets, Luo Chen couldn’t help but draw in a sharp breath.

Especially since some of the events vaguely matched his own later experiences.

For instance, how the Taisui had escaped the Demon-Fall Land, or why the remnant soul of the stone dragon had been left behind in the Flowing Sand Sea, or even how the Yuantu Sword, which had still been at the true artifact level back then, had managed to stain itself with Ascendant blood and was once hailed as the God-Slaying Blood Sword.

If all this was true, then the Demon-Fall Land’s malice toward Ascendant cultivators was truly rare in the world.

Li Canghai finished recounting the details of that past event in one breath, took a slight pause to steady his breath, and then continued, "After I achieved Nascent Soul, I actually also had designs on the Demon-Fall Land, especially since I urgently needed the mineral deposits inside to refine a spiritual treasure. But after some research, I ultimately gave up."

He gave a bitter laugh. “Achieving Ascendant is not easy. I don’t want to die an obscure death inside. Fellow Daoist Luo, I advise you not to entertain such greedy thoughts either.”

Luo Chen nodded solemnly. “I understand. Thank you for your warning, Fellow Daoist.”

Although he was eager to eradicate the root of the problem, Luo Chen was not one to act recklessly.

The black demon Gu Shaoshang was already dead. Whether his sister Gu Xiaolian could still walk out of the Demon-Fall Land alive was another matter entirely. The enmity between them could be put to rest for now.

When it came to life and death, and without the immense benefits that compelled him to act, like the fate soul, he had no reason to risk his life.

The Demon-Fall Land’s malice toward Ascendant cultivators might be even greater than he imagined.

Otherwise, with Blood Sea Ancestor’s abilities, he would not have met such an end.

Luo Chen actively changed the subject and began to consult Li Canghai on cultivation insights for the Ascendant realm.

Although both were at the early Ascendant stage, Li Canghai had been in this realm for four hundred years. Even without guidance, he had accumulated considerable cultivation experience.

Luo Chen had no one to guide him either, so the two simply spoke freely and exchanged ideas.

And indeed, some of Li Canghai’s insights verified Luo Chen’s own guesses and confusions after breaking through.

“You ask about the relationship between origin power and the true intent of the same attribute’s laws?”

“I am not talented, but I do have some conjectures.”

“If we compare a complete law of the Dao to a person, then origin power is the foundation—equivalent to the instinctive eating, drinking, breathing, and sleeping that a person is born with. The other true intents of laws are like the active or passive learning, training, communication, and even tool mastery that occur during growth. This is the way of change!”

“Origin is the foundation; true intent is change. A complete person naturally has countless changes. To cultivate a law to perfection, one must master both the instinct and all the changes.”

“If someone truly could completely master all foundations and changes, that person would surely be an existence who could claim supremacy and patriarch status in that law.”

“They could become an immortal, a Dao Ancestor!”

“Of course, that is far too distant from us. In my estimation, even if one only cultivated the most basic origin power to a state of great accomplishment, their realm would likely be no lower than the Body Integration stage.”

…

“As for how to cultivate origin and true intent, I have experience with the former, but only conjectures about the latter.”

“Origin power can be seen as a person’s instinct. To cultivate and strengthen it, one need only repeat it day after day and grasp the patterns. Let the primordial spirit roam the ocean of origin qi, observe the manifestation of origin laws, and it can gradually strengthen. But in this process, the cultivator is immersed in heaven and earth. Even if the primordial spirit is stable, repeated day after day still carries the danger of being assimilated. Therefore, we cultivators must combine heaven and earth origin qi, condense a dharma form, and protect ourselves.”

“In my view, the cultivation of the Ascendant realm is the process of tempering the primordial spirit and condensing the dharma form. When the dharma form is complete, one can completely overcome the danger of assimilation by heaven and earth. Then, roaming the ocean of origin qi becomes a matter of following one’s heart.”

“As for the true intent of laws, as I said earlier, that is the way of change. It cannot be learned through observation and imitation alone. It must be realized—comprehended, enlightened, and even awakened!”

…

A single conversation with Canghai was worth a hundred years of study.

Luo Chen’s guesses were confirmed one by one, and all his confusion was swept away in that exchange.

No wonder his three origin powers of water, fire, and earth had made no progress, while his Withering-Bloom True Intent had unknowingly improved a great deal.

In the five hundred years he had walked this path, he had witnessed far too much withering, blooming, rise, and fall.

All things in heaven and earth, the birth and death of spirits.

Families and sects, waxing and waning.

Everything was nothing more than the appearance of withering and blooming, cycling endlessly.

He had once known the what but not the why. But after comprehending the Withering-Bloom True Intent, these experiences and insights gradually became nourishment for the growth of that true intent in his understanding.

It sounded like improving these true intent changes was much simpler than improving origin power.

But upon careful thought, Luo Chen understood that the two were not the same at all.

Even if he had the realization to comprehend the changes of true intent, it did not mean he could necessarily understand them, because sometimes enlightenment required the right opportunity.

That single word “realization” had stumped countless brilliant talents throughout the ages!

Luo Chen was not necessarily that one-in-ten-thousand genius.

In comparison, the cultivation of origin power had a traceable path based on the summaries of predecessors.

Ascendant: the soul and Sage embryo merge to form the primordial spirit. Then, using internal dharma power and the self-primordial spirit, one connects to the external heaven and earth origin qi, weaving a self-dharma form, achieving a medium of communication with heaven and earth. Only then can one advance further without interference.

Luo Chen drew inferences and extended his thinking.

“Does that mean the next realm after Ascendant requires refining the hard-won dharma form?”

Li Canghai smiled and nodded. “Fellow Daoist’s guess is correct, though the details differ slightly. The dharma form condensed by us Ascendant cultivators is merely a phantom form. What the Void Refinement stage requires is to refine the phantom into reality, a complete transformation and rebirth.”

Luo Chen fell into thought.

Sage to Ascendant, weaving a phantom form, refining phantom into reality… step by step, while maintaining self-will, gradually mastering the laws of the Dao, neither being assimilated by heaven and earth nor equaling heaven and earth, grasping a portion of the authority of the laws.

Compared to the simple accumulation and elevation of dharma power during Qi Refinement and Initiate, once the realm grew higher, it became profoundly mysterious.

But this mystery inexplicably stirred a bit of excitement and thrill in his heart.

With a long sigh, Luo Chen revealed a smile.

“Indeed, when three walk together, there is always a teacher. Today’s exchange with Fellow Daoist is enough to save me several hundred years of detours.”

Li Canghai waved his hand. “Fellow Daoist Luo flatters me. With your talent, what need is there for several hundred years?”

Luo Chen laughed heartily. One good turn deserves another, and he proactively shared his insights on the dao of soul refinement.

It was clear that Li Canghai was extremely curious about his powerful primordial spirit.

Luo Chen had not spoken earlier because the atmosphere was not right. Now that the other party had shown such sincerity, he could not hoard his knowledge.

As he shared, Li Canghai, like Luo Chen before him, raised questions about points of confusion.

“Illusion realm soul refinement? I have experimented with this method. The fifth-rank illusion formations I set up do have remarkable effects.”

“Splitting the primordial spirit, strengthening both parts simultaneously, then merging them to double the foundation? That is a very ingenious method. But our primordial spirit is of utmost importance. One careless mistake could lead to eternal damnation!”

“Internal refinement and external consumption, supplemented by the way of external alchemy? That is indeed a solution. But pills capable of nourishing the soul are all rare treasures of the world, hard to come by.”

“Great Soul Pill, Soul-Scouring Pill… this… this… Fellow Daoist, is this not too precious!”

By the end of the conversation, Luo Chen had actually given away a bottle of Great Soul Pills and a Soul-Scouring Pill he had previously refined.

After briefly examining the two pills, Li Canghai’s face showed shock.

Especially the Soul-Scouring Pill, which could cleanse the primordial spirit of distracting thoughts. He finally understood why Luo Chen’s primordial spirit was so powerful and stable even when he had just entered the Ascendant realm.

“This pill can only be taken once in a cultivator’s lifetime. I have already left some for the sect. Today, having received your generous guidance, what harm is there in giving you one?” Luo Chen said openly and generously.

Li Canghai swallowed his saliva, unable to utter words of refusal.

He carefully put away the pills, a sincere smile appearing on his face.

“To be honest, the reason I spoke so freely with Fellow Daoist earlier was merely out of guilt for abandoning you on Heaven-Earth Island back then. I never expected you to repay kindness with kindness. This has caught me somewhat off guard.”

Li Canghai thought for a moment, then suddenly said, “I have nothing to offer in return. I noticed Fellow Daoist frequently eyeing my Mountain-Seal Seal earlier, clearly curious about it. Since that is the case, I will gift you a refining method similar to this treasure!”

As he spoke, a flash of spiritual light appeared in his hand, and a black jade slip appeared before Luo Chen.

“This…” Luo Chen hesitated slightly.

Li Canghai waved his hand. “Fellow Daoist need not worry. This refining method mainly records soul extraction and spirit binding, which has remarkable effects on mountains and seas. As for what spirit treasure you intend to refine with it, that is entirely up to you. It does not have to be a Mountain-Seal Seal like mine.”

Luo Chen’s eyes lit up, and he put away the black jade slip.

“Since that is the case, I will not refuse.”

Next, Li Canghai carefully explained some key points of “soul extraction and spirit binding” as well as various taboos.

Luo Chen listened attentively.

There were no sun or moon within the Mountain-Seal Seal.

From the time Luo Chen entered the Mountain-Seal Seal, this exchange of Dao had already lasted quite a while.

The demon monk Kongchan, waiting outside, had begun to worry whether Luo Chen had met with some accident.

A month later, a figure suddenly appeared before him, much to his pleasant surprise.

Upon seeing Luo Chen, who was brimming with energy, he could not help but congratulate him. “It seems the master’s visit to the great cultivator Li was quite fruitful!”

Luo Chen smiled and nodded, his mood quite relaxed.

This visit had indeed benefited him greatly.

The so-called “soul extraction and spirit binding” method was secondary. The cultivation insights for the Ascendant realm that Li Canghai had shared were the true priority, pointing out the direction for Luo Chen’s Ascendant cultivation.

Nor did he doubt that Li Canghai’s cultivation insights had any major issues in their general direction.

Before him were Rosycloud and later Youli Divine Lord. Both were the most top-tier Ascendant powerhouses in the Mountains and Seas Realm, and both had condensed dharma forms.

From this, it was clear that the Ascendant realm indeed required condensing a dharma form to walk the path of the laws faster, more steadily, and further!

Seeing Luo Chen in good spirits, Kongchan took the initiative to ask, “Master, should we now return to the Fallen Sea?”

To this, Luo Chen decisively shook his head.

“The matter of the Fallen Sea ends here. We are going to the Sea Emperor’s Palace!”

“The Sea Emperor’s Palace?” Kongchan was stunned, then realized something and his face changed. “Master, that Sea Emperor Whale is a great demon of the demon race, an ancient demon of a generation!”

Luo Chen looked at him with a half-smile. “What, are you afraid?”

Kongchan gave a bitter smile. “This humble monk is naturally afraid. I just think the master need not provoke that old demon.”

Luo Chen snorted coldly. “I am not afraid, so why should you be? Lead the way. You should know where the Sea Emperor’s Palace is.”

Kongchan had no choice but to tremble with fear as he took the lead for Luo Chen.

Inside the Mountain-Seal Seal.

Li Canghai watched Luo Chen’s departing figure, his expression somewhat wistful.

Newly ascended to great power, full of vigor and spirit, roaming the mountains and seas—how similar this was to when he himself had formed the Canghai Righteous Dao Alliance and driven the cultivators of the Northern Sea.

It seemed that Luo Chen and the Sea Emperor Whale would inevitably have a confrontation, one that would be splendid to behold.

Unfortunately, he would not be able to witness that scene.

He looked down at the Soul-Scouring Pill he was rubbing in his hand, a feeling of elation rising in his heart.

Just as he was about to completely refine the Mountain-Seal Seal and prepare for ascension, Luo Chen had sent him such a great gift. It was truly a pillow arriving just as he was feeling sleepy!

“Luo Chen, until we meet again!”

(End of chapter)
