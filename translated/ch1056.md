# Chapter 1056: Great Demons Submit, Phoenix Burns the Spirit

"Master?"

When Little White cried for help, the three enemies were all momentarily stunned, then their killing intent surged!

This woman was not alone!

The three exchanged glances, then tacitly separated, forming a triangular formation to surround Little White.

Little White naturally refused to sit idly and await death. As she retreated at extreme speed, her body transformed into countless ghostly shadows.

"So she's a ghost cultivator."

The demon monk Kongchan was surprised for a moment, then a divination slip container appeared in his hand, and he casually scattered its contents.

Swish! Swish! Swish!

Countless spirit slips flew out from the bamboo tube, transforming into streaks of fine light that shot toward the ghostly shadows.

Puff! Puff! Puff!

Only the sounds of breaking could be heard as the countless ghostly shadows were all shattered, revealing Little White's true form hidden within.

In her panic, Little White let out a delicate cry and struck a palm strike to the side.

Light flashed, and a large blue palm met hers precisely.

Boom!

The two clashed, and Little White, being slightly weaker, was forced to stop.

Shark Emperor Liujun withdrew his hand, clicking his tongue in admiration: "To withstand my palm strike with a ghostly body, this female ghost's cultivation is not ordinary. But this is as far as she goes."

In his line of sight, a green escape light suddenly appeared behind Little White, instantly transforming into countless grass and wood arrows.

Tai Sui snorted coldly and pointed a finger.

The countless grass and wood arrows converged together, shooting toward Little White in a concentrated volley.

Faced with this scene, Little White's face turned pale. Even against a late-stage Sage Grand Cultivator, she could barely hold on for a moment.

But the three besieging her now included a Great Demon Emperor, one who completely suppressed her in terms of soul foundation.

As for the other two, one had superb combat experience and blocked her escape route. The other was cunning in nature—even with three attacking one, he still resorted to sneak attacks.

Seeing the attacks about to land, Little White desperately defended while activating the protective armor that Luo Chen had temporarily bestowed upon her.

A hazy yellow qi shield instantly spread out.

Ding! Ding! Ding!

The dense sounds of collision rang out as countless wooden arrows madly struck the hazy yellow qi shield, shattering it in moments before hitting the black armor itself.

At this point, the previously unstoppable grass-and-wood arrows were rendered ineffective, falling away one after another.

The moment he saw the black armor, Tai Sui was subconsciously stunned.

"Xuanyan Armor?"

In that moment of hesitation, Little White seized the opportunity, her form flickering as she slipped through his interception.

In an instant, Little White broke free from the three's encirclement, transforming into a streak of light flying back the way she came.

Behind her came the demon monk Kongchan's questioning voice.

"Tai Sui, what are you doing?"

"Don't let that female ghost escape!" Shark Emperor Liujun, sensing something amiss, immediately gave chase. While pursuing, he raised his hand and produced an oil lamp.

Pinching his fingers, a small flame flew out with a *drip*, crossing several hundred zhang at an unimaginable speed, about to land on Little White.

Forced to turn around, Little White opened her mouth and spat out streams of ghostly qi.

Unfortunately, these pure ghostly qi had no effect against the blue flame, continuously burning to ashes.

Just as Little White was about to fall into despair, a shocking scene occurred.

The blue flames that had landed on her body scattered like flowing water, eventually reverting to the earlier small flame.

"This!?" Shark Emperor Liujun was stunned, his face full of disbelief.

That azure flame was nurtured from the Shark Emperor Lamp, a true artifact passed down through the sharkfolk clan, using sharkfolk oil. Although mainly effective against wild beast physical bodies, it also had considerable effects against soul forms.

But now, the Shark Emperor True Flame was so obediently floating on that female ghost's body.

Impossible!

"No, it's not you!"

Just as Shark Emperor Liujun realized the truth, a figure walked out from behind Little White. The familiar yet different face made Shark Emperor Liujun's eyes instantly widen.

"It's you!"

"Little White, you've worked hard. Leave the rest to me!" Luo Chen gently patted Little White's shoulder and stood before her.

"Master, be careful. These three are not easy to deal with," Little White said worriedly, taking off the Xuanyan Armor and returning it to Luo Chen.

The Xuanyan Armor merged into his body, and Luo Chen smiled faintly, his gaze falling on the three.

No, strictly speaking, it was two demon beasts and one spirit creature that had usurped a human cultivator's physical body.

He ignored the strongest among them, the demon monk Kongchan, and instead greeted the other two.

"Long time no see, Shark Emperor Liujun, and... Fellow Daoist Tai Sui!"

The moment he faced Tai Sui directly, Luo Chen finally understood why this person gave him a familiar and warm feeling.

How could it not be warm and familiar?

How could it not be warm and familiar?

Back then, he had personally swallowed and refined Tai Sui's original body.

Relying on that fifth-grade medicinal ingredient—Tai Sui's original body—he had been able to refine the fifth-grade Law-Derivation Pill and even pass the second stage of the Nine Tribulations Three Nectars, the physical body nirvana.

In Luo Chen's view, the reason he could defy common sense and use a Fire Spirit Body to comprehend the Withering-Bloom True Intent within the wood-attribute laws was also greatly related to having consumed Tai Sui's original body back then.

A fifth-grade spirit-attuned Tai Sui inherently contained wood-attribute laws!

Shark Emperor Liujun looked at Luo Chen warily, holding the oil lamp in his hand, its azure flame beginning to rise.

"So it was you who secretly infiltrated Yemo Heaven earlier, Azure Yang Demon Lord!"

Behind him, Tai Sui looked at Luo Chen with fear, his feet already retreating.

When the Xuanyan Armor appeared, he had roughly guessed who Little White's master was.

Now that he truly saw him, he couldn't sense any aura from the other party.

But precisely because he couldn't sense anything, his fear grew even greater!

He had once fought alongside Luo Chen and knew how powerful this guy was.

Later, he watched the other party break through the Pill Realm, obtain the Heaven-Refining Devil Lord's alchemy inheritance, and after so many years, he would inevitably have grown even stronger.

He even suspected that the famous Eastern Wilderness Pill Sect was none other than Luo Chen!

Luo Hai, Luo Chen—just a single character difference. Both possessed formidable alchemy skills. With the Heaven-Refining Devil Lord's alchemy inheritance, Luo Hai could indeed have reached his current level.

If that were the case, then he had to consider the achievements under another title of Pill Sect Luo Chen.

Pill Sect Luo Chen, also known as Formless Moluo, was hailed as the strongest under the Ascendant level, alongside Demon Emperor Azure Frost!

His small retreating movements were all noticed by Luo Chen.

Luo Chen smiled freely, took a step forward, and walked toward the three as if strolling through a garden.

"Fellow Daoist Tai Sui, I haven't yet congratulated you on seizing a new body and advancing to the Sage stage. Why do you want to leave without saying goodbye as soon as we meet?"

At these words, the two Demon Emperors both looked at Tai Sui.

The demon monk Kongchan frowned and said, "Tai Sui, what is there to fear? He's just one person. Adding that female ghost, it's only two against three."

"Is that so?" Luo Chen chuckled, shaking his arm.

Immediately, a black light flowed, coiling along his left arm. A dragon head with prominent horns emerged, staring fiercely at the three.

The moment he saw the black dragon, Tai Sui shrieked, "It really is you!"

Then, ignoring his two companions entirely, he fled without hesitation.

"Flee?" The corner of Luo Chen's mouth lifted as he struck out with his right palm from afar.

Azure Yang Great Handprint, appearing in the world once more!

A massive palm print rolled forward, pulling at the mixed spiritual qi of heaven and earth, chasing after Tai Sui with overwhelming momentum.

The moment this giant palm appeared, the demon monk Kongchan's expression changed.

So strong!

A casual strike from the other party actually possessed such power!

Shark Emperor Liujun's face also changed completely. He frantically activated his mana, and the azure flame on the oil lamp burned fiercely.

But before he could unleash the Shark Emperor True Flame, Luo Chen merely glanced at him, and all his movements ceased.

Liujun stood frozen in the air, his eyes dazed.

And the azure flame beside him, without its master's control, instantly went berserk. It transformed into a long, slender blue fish, and with a single pounce, swallowed Liujun whole.

This devouring encompassed his mana, physical body, Sage infant, and soul.

From beginning to end, Liujun made no sound.

He allowed the true flame that the sharkfolk clan had worshipped for countless ages to completely consume him.

On this desolate glacial wasteland, only the crackling sound of the azure flame burning fiercely could be heard.

The air was filled with the fragrant smell of burning oil.

The demon monk Kongchan watched this scene, his body beginning to tremble uncontrollably.

He himself was an expert in soul attacks and knew what level of soul cultivation was required to make a Sage-level Demon Emperor completely powerless to resist, even unable to feel pain as he was burned alive.

That was absolutely not something a Sage could achieve—only an Ascendant Great Power!

Then was the man before him an Ascendant Great Power?

The demon monk Kongchan watched the black-robed Daoist approaching step by step. He subconsciously raised his staff, but when faced with those eyes emitting strange light, he tremblingly lowered it again.

Until the two stood face to face.

Luo Chen gently patted the demon monk Kongchan and said approvingly, "Quite sensible."

With that, he brushed past and headed toward the center of Yemo Heaven.

In the distance came violent rumbling sounds. With words of praise ringing in his ears, the demon monk Kongchan's face flushed red, full of humiliation and unwillingness, but in the end, he still dared not attack.

He also dared not flee.

Because when they passed each other, the pat on his shoulder from Luo Chen had already imprinted a soul restriction.

Just this single soul seal made Kongchan understand the vast gap between them in terms of soul foundation.

Little White stayed behind, monitoring the demon monk Kongchan and the azure oil lamp that still burned faintly.

Black King detached himself from Luo Chen's shoulder and chased in the direction Tai Sui had fled.

As for Luo Chen, he arrived at the shore of that eerie blood lake.

A strange battle was unfolding within the blood lake.

A giant formed from countless corpses was walking toward the center of the lake. Massive waves of blood rolled in, washing away the countless corpses that made up the giant.

But no matter how many were washed away, the giant could condense just as many.

Wave after wave, always advancing!

Luo Chen silently watched this scene, his gaze heavy, lost in thought.

After a long while, Black King flew back, his mouth carrying a broken, mangled body.

"Master, this fat guy has tenacious vitality. He actually survived a direct hit from your Azure Yang Great Handprint."

How could he not be tenacious?

Tai Sui had once cultivated to the fifth rank!

Even if his soul had been suppressed by the Heaven-Refining Devil Lord for a thousand years, even if his physical body had been consumed by Luo Chen, his vitality was still among the rarest in the world.

Luo Chen looked at the person who was constantly "leaking qi" and said indifferently, "Tai Sui, I heard you've been looking for me all these years?"

A single Azure Yang Great Handprint had shattered Tai Sui's physical body. Moreover, because of the fire-attribute law power contained within, even his Sage infant could not self-destruct. Now lying at Luo Chen's feet, Tai Sui's eyes were full of resentment and unwillingness.

"I only regret not finding you sooner, letting you grow into a force to be reckoned with!" Tai Sui said hatefully.

Luo Chen shook his head. "Why bother? If you hadn't set your sights on me back then, we might still be friends who braved the Fallen Sea together."

Tai Sui shrieked, "Dare you say that even if I hadn't set my sights on you, you wouldn't have wanted to make a move on me?"

Luo Chen was speechless.

Tai Sui's original body was a top-grade medicinal ingredient rarely seen in the world. Anyone would be tempted.

Without Tai Sui's original body, Luo Chen would likely have struggled to survive the physical body nirvana stage.

But a gentleman judges by deeds, not intentions. It was indeed Tai Sui who made the first move.

"Enough!" Luo Chen sighed, his right hand moving slightly. "If you die, the grudges of the past will be settled once and for all."

Though his voice was light, his killing intent was absolute.

At this moment, all of Tai Sui's resentment and unwillingness vanished, replaced by only fear.

"Don't!"

Boom!

Golden flames erupted from Luo Chen's palm, forming a pillar of fire that pierced through Tai Sui's physical body.

From within came a series of miserable, blood-curdling screams.

Black King subconsciously retreated a few zhang. Even though he knew his master's fire-controlling abilities were formidable, facing that golden flame still filled him with a fear that came from the depths of his soul.

This fire could burn souls and melt spirits!

After a moment, a pile of black ash remained on the ground. With a gust of wind, all the ashes disappeared, leaving only a human-shaped imprint.

Black King swallowed his saliva. "Master, that was a bit wasteful."

It was indeed somewhat wasteful—both the Sage infant condensed from this seized physical body and Tai Sui's soul, which, though not yet condensed into a primordial spirit, still possessed the essence of a fifth-rank spirit creature, were decent resources.

But Luo Chen had no mind to deal with these things right now.

He slowly raised his hand, and the brilliant Nirvana Sacred Flame transformed into a golden phoenix, obediently standing on his palm, gently pecking at Luo Chen's thick, solid flesh.

The Nirvana Sacred Flame, the most supremely yang and unyielding flame in the world, rivaling even the legendary Solar True Flame.

Especially after Luo Chen completed his soul nirvana, it could have a terrifying burning effect on the souls of living beings.

This was vividly demonstrated when he destroyed Tai Sui.

"Although I haven't yet advanced to the Ascendant stage, from my brief exchange with Li Canghai and the various means I possess, I already have the strength to truly fight an Ascendant Great Power!"

With this thought, Luo Chen's gaze fell upon the blood lake.

He opened his hand and tossed.

"Go!"

*Qiangqiang!*

The phoenix fire spirit let out a resounding, clear cry, circled Luo Chen twice, then soared into the sky.

As the phoenix fire spirit flew out, its body began to expand and grow, transforming into a massive firebird thousands of zhang long. It nimbly dodged wave after wave of blood, pouncing toward the corpse giant.

This fire spirit seemed to be the natural enemy of the corpse giant. As it pounced, large amounts of green, ghostly soul fires began to dissipate.

Countless corpses fell with a *clatter* into the blood lake.

After doing all this, Luo Chen did not recall the phoenix fire spirit. His gaze fell upon the person rising and falling within the blood lake.

That person had a face that was both very old and very young, constantly shifting amidst the surging blood waves.

When Luo Chen's gaze fell upon him, as if sensing something, that face slowly opened a pair of eyes.

They were filled with deep weariness, yet within the calm lay an unwilling madness.

"Without madness, no survival?"

Luo Chen murmured to himself, then reached out and beckoned. The phoenix fire spirit whistled back.

He gently nodded to Mad Wanderer, then turned and left.

Mad Wanderer didn't know what had happened, but he knew he had escaped a calamity.

As Luo Chen's figure gradually faded into the distance, Mad Wanderer cast a grateful glance at his back, then closed his eyes.

The surrounding blood waves surged even more violently, gradually forming a massive blood cocoon that completely enveloped Mad Wanderer.

A moment later.

Luo Chen returned to the place where he had fought earlier.

"Master!" Little White called out happily.

"Mm."

Luo Chen then looked at the demon monk Kongchan, saying nothing, just watching.

Under his gaze, the demon monk Kongchan was drenched in sweat. Finally, he knelt and prostrated himself. "Little demon Kongchan is willing to acknowledge you as master!"

"Good!"

Luo Chen smiled, then finally turned his gaze to the azure flame still burning faintly.

The Shark Emperor True Flame, was it?
