# Chapter 1135: Peak of Dan Force

Perhaps because they relied on the prestige of the City Lord's residence, this merchant caravan had no experience of being attacked over the years, and they were still somewhat at a loss after the great battle.

Fortunately, there were still some capable people within the caravan. After calming down, under Steward Yan's command, they managed to clean up the mess and get back on the road.

After giving all his instructions, Steward Yan could not wait to head toward the carriage where Luo Chen was.

When he lifted the curtain, what he met was a pair of eyes that seemed to be smiling yet not.

Steward Yan's heart tightened. From the corner of his eye, he caught a glimpse of the man lying on the floor. The man's face was a terrified pale, and he was gasping for air in great gulps, as if he had suffered some kind of torture.

Steward Yan immediately cupped his hands and bowed. "Thank you, Senior, for your assistance. If the goods had been robbed today, the consequences would have been unimaginable."

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow. "Earlier, you said your master was lenient in temperament. Even if the goods were stolen, you all probably wouldn't face a life-threatening punishment, right?"

A bitter smile appeared on Steward Yan's face. "If it were just our lives, that would be a small matter. But this batch of goods—especially the Miexian Smoke inside—must be delivered in fixed annual quantities to other cities. If our portion is missing, the impact would be great. At that time, the Young City Lord might not vent his anger on our families..."

Luo Chen listened to his explanation and finally nodded.

"Take this man away!"

Steward Yan looked at the trembling, cowering man, who no longer bore any trace of the imposing martial valor of an eight-foot-tall strongman. He asked cautiously, "Does Senior not wish to learn more?"

Luo Chen said with a faint smile, "Everything I wanted to know, he already told me."

Steward Yan was stunned.

In the time it took to clean up the mess, Luo Chen had already interrogated everything he wanted to know?

Was it that this strongman had a weak will, or was Luo Chen's method too formidable?

With a wary heart, Steward Yan dragged the eight-foot-tall strongman out of the carriage. On the man's exposed skin, he could faintly see streaks of blood qi writhing like earthworms beneath the surface. He couldn't help but draw a sharp breath.

"Hiss, such a sinister method!"

...

"Meteor Bandits, leader Tong Huai Jin, hailing from Xilong City, formerly a general under the Xilong City Lord. He came to Yixian City seven years ago, founded the bandit group Meteor Bandits, with five peak Hua Jin experts under him and a force of several hundred bandits. That eight-foot-tall strongman was one of the five peak Hua Jin experts, originally from the Red Armored Army of Mofeng City. This operation was a simultaneous strike at year's end, targeting five City Lord's residence caravans..."

The intelligence obtained from the eight-foot-tall strongman flowed through Luo Chen's mind one by one.

Gradually, the corners of the young man's mouth lifted.

The composition of this bandit group entrenched around Yixian City was far from simple!

The bandit leader was actually a Furnace Realm expert, with a lifespan far exceeding ordinary people and extraordinary strength. A heavyweight figure anywhere, yet he was willing to become an outlaw.

Moreover, the many experts under his command also belonged to different factions.

The target of this operation was far too unified, perfectly timed to target the Young City Lord Xiao Feng.

Putting all this together, the scent of conspiracy was far too obvious.

With Luo Chen's experience, combined with the intelligence obtained from Steward Yan, he could quickly piece things together and sort out a general picture.

The master of Yixian City had two sons and three daughters. Ten years ago, the City Lord left Yixian City, leaving the two sons to compete for the position of City Lord.

The eldest, Xiao Feng, had strong foundations. Relying on the City Lord's residence's power base, he had long held the substance of the Young City Lord.

The second son, Xiao Yun, was unwilling to be left out. He sought external help and built his own power base. Not only that, but external forces also seemed to covet Yixian City. Bound by certain surface-level rules, they secretly provided Xiao Yun with substantial assistance.

The Meteor Bandits were a very suitable knife for such tasks.

For instance, this time, at a critical moment, they launched a large-scale raid on Xiao Feng's caravans. When it came time for the goods to be traded, if Xiao Feng couldn't produce them, his reputation would inevitably suffer, and he might even lose his position as Young City Lord.

Thus, the second son, Xiao Yun, would have an excuse to make trouble and scheme for the true position of City Lord.

"Tsk tsk, brothers fighting each other, and with such ferocity."

Luo Chen smacked his lips, but he was clearly unconcerned.

Jing Lan beside him looked somewhat frightened, obviously scared by Luo Chen's casual interrogation of the enemy earlier. The silent, agonized struggle of that eight-foot-tall strongman was truly horrifying.

Hearing Luo Chen's inexplicable remark, she finally couldn't help but speak.

"Brother Luo, what exactly happened?"

Luo Chen said calmly, "It has nothing to do with you. Don't worry."

With that, he slowly closed his eyes.

Jing Lan opened her mouth but dared not ask further. A feeling of powerlessness spread throughout her body.

Luo Chen paid no attention to Jing Lan's thoughts, immersing himself in his own world.

He was continuing to convert source blood into surging qi and blood power, then twisting it into strands of pure force, and finally condensing those forces into tiny, egg-like existences.

This was precisely what was called the Dan Force at the peak of the Blood River Realm!

Contrary to what Steward Yan thought, Luo Chen's body refinement realm had not broken through to the third realm, Furnace Realm. He was still in the Blood River Realm.

But his Blood River Realm was completely different from that of ordinary body refiners!

Ordinary body refiners, after undergoing the "physical foundation building" of the Blood River Realm and understanding the changes in qi and blood to derive the four types of force, would then attempt to "gather Dan into Gang and transform the body into a furnace."

This step required gathering all the qi and blood in the body, deriving infinite force, and merging will and force into one, forming an unstoppable, powerful Gang Qi!

That was energy on the same level as a Golden Core cultivator's mana.

But Luo Chen's foundation was simply too terrifying.

Even though the matter with Rosycloud had caused him to burn the only drop of fifth-rank true spirit source blood, there were still a large number of ordinary source bloods within his body.

Each drop of source blood contained a vast amount of qi and blood power, accumulated by Luo Chen over centuries of cultivation, digesting massive resources.

Since Luo Chen intended to also cultivate the orthodox path of body refinement, he needed to convert all these ordinary source bloods one by one.

Perhaps doing so would be more cumbersome, and breaking through realms would be slower.

But once successful, Luo Chen would become the most powerful existence in the third realm of body refinement in history!

With the source blood of a fifth-rank desolate beast's physique, how vast would the quantity of qi and blood converted be? How terrifying would the force and Gang Qi condensed from it be?

Of course, some might lament the source blood accumulated bit by bit, and might even think it wasn't worth it, since the source power converted from source blood was itself not weak.

But strength or weakness also depended on the environment.

In this Great Destruction Realm, devoid of spiritual qi, even if Luo Chen could barely maintain his fifth-rank archaic fleshly body, the archaic body refinement method from the Mountains and Seas Realm could no longer continue. Moreover, the use of source blood and source power was too crude and wasteful. In fact, it had always been merely a supplementary product under Luo Chen's qi refinement cultivation system.

If he wanted to leave this world in the future, he had to find another path and climb new heights!

As things stood, the orthodox body refinement method was the mainstream cultivation system in this Great Destruction Realm. Luo Chen resolutely decided to switch to it.

He also believed that, with his knowledge, experience, unmatched foundation, and the proficiency panel he could still use normally, quickly cultivating to the "Human Immortal Realm" that could shatter the void was not a delusion.

At this moment.

As Luo Chen continued his conversion, his originally jade-white face instantly turned crimson, and wisps of hot steam rose from the top of his head.

Pure source blood was converted into vast qi and blood, then transformed into force, and finally condensed into seeds of Dan Force.

Such an operation was astonishing.

The so-called four types of force were merely different changes in qi and blood.

Dan Force was an instantaneous combination of forces that had to be unleashed in the next moment.

But the Dan Force that Luo Chen was holding back within his body at this moment was far more than a single strand!

If a single strand of Dan Force was a bomb with its fuse lit, then Luo Chen had buried countless bombs inside his body.

Once he gathered Dan into Gang, the Furnace Realm he would break through to would no longer be an ordinary physical furnace, but a furnace of heaven and earth comparable to a realm of its own!
