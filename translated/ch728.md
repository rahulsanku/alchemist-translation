# Chapter 728: Spirit of the Blood Sword, Divine Origin's Myriad Manifestations

Huu...

Clear wind washed over him, and turbid qi was softly exhaled.

The moment Luo Chen stepped out of Mo Gan Mountain [TERM?], he felt like a bird entering the blue sky, a fish entering the sea.

That oppressive feeling completely vanished.

At this moment, when he looked back.

Within his spirit eyes, he could vaguely see two blood seas of similar color scouring one another. The heaven-surging waves raised by the fierce battle even smashed and shattered some of the remaining small Coldlight islands [TERM?] completely.

For Sage cultivators who could control the spiritual qi of heaven and earth, if they fought without restraint, the destructive power was this terrifying.

At the lighter end, they destroyed mountains and severed peaks. At the heavier end, islands sank into the sea.

It was like Luo Chen's Azure Yang Great Handprint, which had incorporated the Spirit-Enslaving Seal. If left uncontrolled, its power would grow stronger and stronger like a rolling snowball, and one palm breaking a city would not be an exaggeration.

"Because of me, Blood Nightmare Moluo, who was hiding here, was awakened, thereby drawing Blood Wanderer, who has been hunting him all this time."

"Both are Sage experts long famous in the Northern Sea. In the past, I felt they were lofty beyond reach, deep as the ocean. Now that I look, they do not seem so strong that they cannot be touched!"

Luo Chen watched this battle between the two Bloods [TERM?] that had continued for decades, bright light flashing continuously in his eyes.

He did not know whether his own realm had risen and shortened the distance between them, or whether these two had both suffered grave damage to their vitality from years of death struggle. In any case, in his eyes, neither of them was so strong that he was completely unable to fight them.

Just like how he had torn Blood Nightmare Moluo's body apart a moment ago.

Even if the other party was undying and indestructible and could evolve a new body from the blood sea, Luo Chen could split and mince him again and again!

If it truly did not work, he could fully activate the Withering-Bloom True Flame and burn this blood sea away!

Especially since he had not even manifested his strongest state, the Archaic true body [TERM?]. He had more than enough confidence.

"Should I pick up a bargain?"

Luo Chen's mind stirred.

He could see that the battle between the two Bloods [TERM?] had directly entered a white-hot stage from the beginning. Each displayed blood-dao methods, neither willing to stop until death. There was a great possibility that this battle would declare the final result.

No matter who the final victor was, if Luo Chen could profit like a fisherman...

In the end, Luo Chen still dispelled this greedy thought.

Within his divine sense perception, a considerable number of Adept cultivators were rushing over from every direction.

These Adept cultivators were not frightening, but their arrival meant that the commotion from the battle at the Coldlight Archipelago [TERM?] had already been sensed by the outside world.

After these people, the top experts of the righteous and demonic paths would probably arrive one after another.

Luo Chen did not want to create more trouble, and hurriedly left in one direction.

...

A splendid carriage appeared and vanished among the clouds.

In the blink of an eye, it was already far away on the horizon.

Heavenjade drove the Sixfold Imperial Flying Cloud Carriage, her worried gaze occasionally falling on the figure behind the curtain.

Ever since Master fought his way out of the Coldlight Archipelago [TERM?], he had remained silent. Had something happened?

At this moment, Luo Chen's eyes were tightly shut, his divine sense drilling into the Primordial Unity Cauldron inside his Purple Mansion [TERM?].

The cauldron body was still as dull gray as before.

Yet unlike its former rough appearance, its entire body was now rounded and perfect, without even the slightest visible trace of formation engraving.

All its materials had fused into one whole.

It was as if this Primordial Unity Cauldron had not been refined by human hands, but had been naturally born of heaven and earth.

Even the Mystic Dust Armor, which had also advanced to a true artifact, was far inferior by comparison.

The power displayed by the Primordial Unity Cauldron was indeed not something the Mystic Dust Armor could reach.

The tribulation lightning power generated after swallowing heavenly lightning was small in quantity, but at critical moments, it was impossible to guard against.

And its own functions were truly beyond expectation.

Even a pseudo-spirit treasure [TERM?] like the God-Slaying Blood Sword [TERM?], which could injure Luo Chen's fleshly body, could be forcefully suppressed and taken in by the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

"Is it because of that gray light it absorbed in Bright Radiance Heaven?"

Luo Chen muttered inwardly and once again searched for traces of the gray light, but found nothing.

It was as if it had vanished into thin air?

With a sigh, Luo Chen placed his attention back on the God-Slaying Blood Sword [TERM?] and studied it carefully.

Among the weapons he had seen in his life, if discussing the highest grade, then without question it was the Heaven-Refining Cauldron, which was said to be at the level of a heaven-reaching spirit treasure [TERM?].

But at that time, because the Heaven-Refining Cauldron had been damaged by the thousand-year struggle between Nine-Refined Beacon Fire and the Nirvana Sacred Flame, and because it was not itself a killing-type spirit treasure, it had not left a deep impression in terms of destructive power.

Therefore, this God-Slaying Blood Sword [TERM?] had become the number-one killing weapon among all weapons Luo Chen had seen!

The beginning of the Northern Sea righteous-demonic war had been Moluo Stream's three Sages plotting against Mu Lan Gaoxiong [TERM?], but what truly drew Mu Lan Gaoxiong [TERM?] into the game had been this blood sword.

Because of this sword, Mu Lan Gaoxiong's [TERM?] Dao path was severed, and he would remain forever at mid-stage Sage.

Also because of this sword, Blood Nightmare Moluo could wield it to contend against Blood Wanderer, the most ferocious in reputation among the Three Great Wanderers.

One could see how sharp this sword was.

However, in Luo Chen's intuition, Blood Nightmare Moluo did not seem to have fully activated the sword's power.

According to what Han Zhan had said, during the battle at Bright Radiance Heaven's cloud-sea plaza, Blood Nightmare Moluo still had to sever a finger to feed the sword when he wanted to activate it. Clearly, he had not truly obtained this sword's recognition either.

"Is it because of the sword spirit?"

Luo Chen tried to use divine sense to contact the sword spirit, but a thick mass of blood light blocked him tightly.

Although the God-Slaying Blood Sword [TERM?] was trapped within the Primordial Unity Cauldron and could not escape, it could still isolate inside from outside and prevent Luo Chen from contacting it.

Such spontaneous awareness indeed seemed like something possessing a spirit.

However, Luo Chen remembered very clearly that the God-Slaying Blood Sword [TERM?] was ultimately not a spirit treasure that had given birth to self-awareness. The spirit body inside it was more like certain special magic treasures he had encountered at lower realms, where a spirit had been bound into the object.

Therefore, if he wanted to master this sword, he should obtain the recognition of the spirit body inside the sword.

Or forcefully control it?

For some reason, Luo Chen thought of the White Tiger Puppet [TERM?] stored away and long ignored in his storage ring.

He slowly opened his eyes, looking thoughtful.

"It seems I must properly seek out some profound puppet classics."

...

Back then, traveling from the Coldlight Archipelago [TERM?] to Giant Whale Harbor [TERM?] had taken a full three years.

This time, however, the long journey required only a few months.

The Sixfold Imperial Flying Cloud Carriage's extremely high speed was one aspect, and the smooth road after the righteous-demonic war ended was another.

Yes, the righteous-demonic war had already been declared over.

On the road, Luo Chen had learned the result of the peace negotiations at Molan Court [TERM?] from some cultivators.

The two sides reached agreement in the broad direction. The war had to stop, and Moluo Stream publicly announced that Blood Nightmare Moluo was the chief culprit of this war, which counted as finding someone to take the blame.

The Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance let bygones be bygones and even passed the new complete island-linking formation method researched by Ascendant great power Li Canghai to Moluo Stream.

Luo Chen had no way to know the detailed particulars.

But in his view, the reason the two sides were so eager to stop the war and even cooperate all came from outside pressure.

The Northern Sea Demon Alliance [TERM?]!

And Luo Chen had already directly felt this pressure.

Giant Whale Harbor [TERM?], once bustling, was now much more desolate, with no sign of the former crowds coming and going.

Sang Jinghe [TERM?] softly reported the information he had gathered beside him.

"Divine Origin City is indeed surrounded by large numbers of demon beasts."

"But these demon beasts are quite organized. They have not completely sealed Divine Origin City off. Instead, they surround three sides and leave one open, leaving Giant Whale Harbor [TERM?], the large harbor leading to the human hinterland."

"It is precisely because of this that we have the opportunity to enter Divine Origin City safely."

Luo Chen listened quietly. His powerful divine sense spread outward, and more than a thousand li away, there were indeed some powerful auras faintly discernible.

In terms of realm, those existences were not inferior to him.

"Surrounding three sides and leaving one open?"

Luo Chen was puzzled. "Are these demon beasts trying to make this heavy city of the Northern Sea harbor hope, divide its power, and then destroy it piece by piece? Or do they not dare provoke the Tianyuan Merchant Alliance, and thus only make a superficial encirclement, not daring to strike to kill?"

Divine Origin City was the headquarters of the Northern Sea Tianyuan Merchant Alliance, and the power behind it was the distant Central Continent Tianyuan Dao Sect!

The Northern Sea Demon Alliance [TERM?] clearly was not a motley crowd. The experts within it must fear the Tianyuan Dao Sect to some degree.

Therefore, Luo Chen's latter guess was clearly closer to the truth.

Thinking of Fu Chaosheng agreeing to meet him at Divine Origin City...

Luo Chen settled his thoughts and walked toward that majestic immortal city of black and white.

Before setting out, his sleeve trembled slightly, and a small snake dropped from it, swimming into the sea. A moment later, it vanished from sight.

...

The city walls were pitch-black, like a black giant dragon that enclosed several hundred li of land within.

Inside the city, distinctive pure-white buildings stood everywhere.

There were small bridges and flowing water, lakes and rivers, and tall towers standing upright.

This architectural style was truly the first Luo Chen had seen in his life.

It was majestic and grand, yet not lacking in exquisite detail, as if everything had been planned from the very beginning of construction.

Especially the black-and-white matching colors, which brought people an enormous visual impact.

Rumor said Central Continent loved chess, especially the Tianyuan Dao Sect, whose disciples all knew a move or two of go [TERM?]. Perhaps this architectural style had also been influenced by go.

What drew Luo Chen's attention most, however, was the spiritual qi concentration within Divine Origin City.

It was very high!

Even on the main streets, it had the level of a weak second-tier spirit vein.

One could imagine that the shop towers arranged with spirit-gathering formations were at least second tier at baseline.

As for the specially opened cave abodes inside the city, there were surely no few third-tier ones.

Luo Chen could even sense at least ten locations emitting a strong attraction toward him.

Those represented cave abodes possessing spiritual qi concentration at the level of fourth-tier spirit veins!

This city alone was no less than the Fulong Mountain Range.

He did not know how the Primordial Demon Sect, when it still existed, had allowed the Tianyuan Dao Sect to obtain such a hinterland location and establish the merchant alliance headquarters there.

"Master, what do we do now?" Heavenjade asked.

Luo Chen smiled faintly. "Naturally, find a place to stay."

As he spoke, a token appeared in his hand. It was precisely the merchant alliance distinguished guest identity plaque Fu Chaosheng had personally given him back then.

"Jinghe, take this to the tall tower in the city and rent a fourth-tier cave abode from them."

"Master, why will you not let me go again?" Heavenjade did not understand. She believed she handled matters neatly and had rarely made mistakes over these years, but along the road, Luo Chen had basically entrusted matters large and small to Sang Jinghe.

Luo Chen handed the token to the respectful Sang Jinghe [TERM?] and patted his shoulder, signaling him to go first.

Afterward, while walking leisurely along the broad street and looking around, he explained softly.

"The current situation is different. Relations between humans and demons are tense. Although you have transformed, in essence you are still a Golden Core-stage demon cultivator. If someone sees through your foundation, it will easily cause trouble."

"Jinghe is a human cultivator and has also come to Divine Origin City before. It will be much better for him to show himself."

"As for you... just accompany me for a stroll."

Heavenjade came to a sudden understanding, then saw Luo Chen step into a shop.

She hurriedly followed.

The moment they entered, they heard an attendant warmly come forward to greet them.

"Senior, do you have any specific needs?"

"No. I will just look around."

"Then Elder has come to the right place. Our shop is not large, but our resources are incomparably complete, not inferior to the Tianyuan Merchant Alliance at all!"

"Oh?"

Luo Chen became interested. This shop's tone was actually this big?

He remembered that before entering, the signboard had read Myriad Manifestations Tower [TERM?].

The attendant's spirit lifted, and he began introducing it.

"Behind our Myriad Manifestations Tower [TERM?] stands the Xiang family [TERM?], first among the Tianyuan Dao Sect's Thirty-Six Aristocratic Clans [TERM?], a top great clan second only to the Twelve Great Houses [TERM?]!"

"Our business runs through north and south."

"Here, you can not only see local Northern Sea specialties, but also every resource from the Southern Frontier!"

Luo Chen snorted inwardly. Their tone had been so large before, but now it seemed quite exaggerated.

The Tianyuan Merchant Alliance's business spread across the Mountains and Seas Realm. It was not merely as simple as running north and south.

However, for this so-called Xiang family [TERM?] to extend business to the Southern Frontier, their strength must be abundant.

He put away the contempt in his heart and asked, "If that is so, do you have Southern Frontier gu insects [TERM?]?"

The attendant spread a hand in invitation.

"Senior, this way, please! Gu insects [TERM?] are mysterious. To prevent them from affecting cultivators, the storage location is arranged in the back."

Luo Chen smiled and followed him inside.

Jar after jar successively appeared before him.

The attendant's introductions also continued without end.

"This is the Moonlight Gu [TERM?]. Its grade is not high, only first tier, but it has the ability to guide qi and open meridians. Some people who possess spirit roots but are born with blocked meridians can refine this gu and break through the restriction to cultivate. Even those born with clear meridians will not stint on refining one Moonlight Gu [TERM?] to assist their Qi Refinement."

"This is Wine Pouch Gu [TERM?]. If spirit wine is placed inside it, the quality of the spirit wine will grow better and better, and the flavor incomparably mellow. It is truly the first choice for those fond of wine! It is also one of the best-selling low-level gu insects [TERM?] in our Myriad Manifestations Tower [TERM?]."

...

"First-tier Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu [TERM?]! Senior may not have heard this name, but you have surely heard of its predecessor. That is the Devil Heart Gu [TERM?], a demonic-path gu insect [TERM?] that can parasitize inside a cultivator's body; once the host dies, all cultivation is devoured by the master."

Hearing the name Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu [TERM?], Luo Chen's thoughts stirred.

Not only had he heard the name, he had used this gu before.

In fact, under the nourishment of his powerful qi and blood, that Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu [TERM?] inside his body had once been nurtured to the third tier.

Later, after his fleshly-body nirvana, this gu vanished along with all his vast magic power... vanished.

Suddenly, Luo Chen guessed a possibility.

A third-tier Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu [TERM?] no longer had only the function of a meeting of hearts, but could advance to the level of Devil Heart Gu [TERM?].

Although it could not transfer an entire body's cultivation and make wedding clothes for another, it could transmit half the magic power through the air to the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu [TERM?] mother gu.

Then his body's magic power back then very likely had not vanished without cause, but had been transmitted through the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu [TERM?] to Sima Huiniang [TERM?], who had likewise been planted with this gu!

At this thought, Luo Chen's body subconsciously trembled.

He himself knew best how domineering the magic power of his body had been.

If Sima Huiniang [TERM?] had been at the Golden Core Stage, perhaps she could have barely endured some of it. But if she had not yet broken through to the Golden Core Stage at that time, then perhaps...

The attendant had been paying attention to Luo Chen the whole time. They were best at reading expressions.

Seeing Luo Chen's body tremble, he hurriedly explained, "Senior need not worry. No one has dared refine Devil Heart Gu [TERM?] for a long time. Even if Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu [TERM?] has a king gu [TERM?] controlling it, my Central Continent Xiang family [TERM?] also has capable people and has long since dealt with the influence of the Spirit-Rhinoceros King Gu [TERM?]. You may certainly buy a pair and plant them in the bodies of those close to you, so you can sense their life and safety."

At this moment, Luo Chen's emotions surged so intensely that he could no longer really listen to the other party's introduction.

He took a deep breath and beckoned to Heavenjade behind him.

"Buy one of every gu insect [TERM?]. Heavenjade, you pay."

After saying this, Luo Chen left Myriad Manifestations Tower [TERM?] in a distracted state.

Standing on the desolate street, Luo Chen subconsciously wanted to activate the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu [TERM?], but there was no longer that little insect inside his body, so naturally he could not sense Huiniang's condition.

A melancholy mood surged into his heart for a time.

At the end of his line of sight, Sang Jinghe [TERM?] hurried over, and behind him was a middle-aged man in splendid clothes.

From a distance, that middle-aged man cupped his hands toward Luo Chen in salute, his bearing incomparably respectful.

"Junior Fu Hongzhang [TERM?] greets Senior Wilderness Wanderer!"
