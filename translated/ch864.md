# Chapter 864: Meeting an Old Friend, Falling Leaves Return to Their Roots

"Xiaoling?"

Luo Chen asked softly, at the same time extending his thick arm.

The great python nodded repeatedly, slithering up along that arm.

When it landed on his hand, there was a slight weight, yet it still felt as light and ethereal as if it were nothing.

This was not because Luo Chen disregarded that weight, but because the other party was truly that ethereal and airy.

For although this great python appeared to have a solid body, it was in fact merely pure ghostly qi gathered together.

Thus, this python was a ghost!

But Luo Chen did not care in the slightest, only because the other party was an old friend of his—Bai Meiling!

Luo Chen slowly raised his arm, looking at Bai Meiling, whose long, slender form was filled with delighted surprise, and asked curiously, "How did you end up here? And you've even condensed a ghost infant, reaching the Fourth Stage Ghost Emperor realm?"

After confirming it was Luo Chen, Bai Meiling was overjoyed. Her lithe body coiled tightly around Luo Chen's arm, as if afraid he would run away.

"Master, it's a long story."

Luo Chen smiled faintly. "A long story, then take your time telling it! I need to clean up the battlefield anyway."

As he spoke, Luo Chen patted his body, enduring the pain.

*Puff! Puff! Puff! Puff!*

Four black shadows were forced out.

They were precisely the four black spikes.

Then, reaching out with his hand, he beckoned, and a black light flew over from afar.

The four black spikes seemed to sense the call, like fledgling birds returning to the nest, merging with that black light.

Borrowing the moonlight, Luo Chen raised his hand and looked at this thick, large black spike.

A heavy treasure of Tianfan City—the Law-Suppressing Five Element Spikes!

It was truly wondrous. After being driven into a cultivator's body, it severed the eight extraordinary meridians, sealed the Purple Mansion barrier, making it difficult for the cultivator's Sage to mobilize their power.

And clearly, Hundred Leg's artifact control technique was not exquisite enough; he had not pushed this true artifact to its limits.

With just a little probing, Luo Chen knew that if this spike were driven into a cultivator's Sage, they would have no power to resist at all.

It was like before, when Luo Chen had used his two divided souls as the main force, mobilizing the dharma power within his acupoints, and imperceptibly pried loose the suppression of the four Law-Suppressing black spikes.

This was also why, during the final strike, Luo Chen had enough dharma power to execute the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger!

From beside his ear came Bai Meiling's voice, recalling past events, carrying a hint of heaviness.

"Back then, when the demon tide erupted, the Luo Tian Sect was forced to relocate. I was unfortunately separated from them and ended up wandering alone outside."

Luo Chen nodded. This matter was recorded in the *Luo Tian Records*.

"Fortunately, I was just a lonely wandering ghost. In the midst of the great chaos, no one paid me any attention. After all, everywhere was filled with ghosts and fierce spirits transformed from the resentful thoughts, obsessions, and evil thoughts of dead cultivators and demon beasts."

"Also, thanks to the Soul-Nourishing Banner and ghost cultivation method you bestowed upon me in the early years, I cultivated while searching for Gu Caiyi and the others."

Luo Chen formed a hand seal and tapped it on his chest.

Instantly, a mist of blood spread.

His massive body also rapidly shrank under the rolling blood mist, returning to its original form in the blink of an eye.

Luo Chen examined himself. This battle had been quite intense and had taken a long time, lasting from noon until the bright moon hung high.

His Mystic Dust Armor was now tattered and uneven.

In several places, it had even been pierced through.

With this level of damage, relying solely on the Mystic Dust Armor's automatic recovery ability would not be enough.

After returning, he would need to find some materials to fill in the gaps and then nurture it properly.

As for the injuries on his body—flesh turned outward, bones exposed—they looked ferocious and terrifying.

But to Luo Chen, these wounds were not considered severe.

After casting a few medical arts, swallowing a few pills, and combining them with his powerful flesh-and-blood self-healing ability, they were no longer much of a problem.

Bai Meiling's ghostly qi surged and contracted, and her Charm Python ghost body also slowly shrank, coiling up on Luo Chen's shoulder.

"By the time I returned to the Jade Cauldron Domain, I discovered it had been completely destroyed, reduced to a ghostly realm."

"But it was also fortunate that it was a ghostly realm. I found a yin cave filled with ghostly qi, cultivated bitterly for several decades, and finally broke through to the Third Stage Ghost King realm in one go."

"After I advanced to the Third Stage Ghost King realm, I finally had some confidence to walk the world. I learned that the Luo Tian Sect had stopped in the Taihu Domain, so I hurried over."

"Who would have thought that when I arrived, I found it had already been occupied by the demon race, with countless cultivators having fallen."

Luo Chen, who was in the middle of collecting various scattered magical treasures, paused slightly upon hearing this.

The *Luo Tian Records* indeed contained this account.

In fact, there was even a simple sentence within it that had once made Luo Chen sigh.

[Yuan Xiaoyue, disciple of Gu Caiyi, died while fleeing west of Taihu Lake.]

Luo Chen sighed and came before Hundred Leg's corpse.

Even though it was already dead, its body sunk into the great lake, a small part was still exposed.

The corpse of a Great Demon Emperor!

And one who was proficient in body refinement!

The value of this corpse was immeasurable.

The only flaw was that the most essential blood within it was now completely gone, all absorbed by the Yuantu Sword.

Oh well!

Being able to kill him was already a great fortune; how could he covet perfection?

Luo Chen spat out a white-gold sword pellet, which transformed into sword threads, and began to rapidly cut through the corpse.

One by one, the arms were cut off.

The internal organs were also sorted out.

Even the two eyeballs, Luo Chen specially dug them out.

Under the night sky, Luo Chen transformed into a merciless butcher, performing the bloody and filthy work of dismemberment.

After selecting all the valuable materials from the corpse, he did not waste the remaining centipede flesh.

"Xiao Hei, it's all yours!"

He patted his left arm, and amidst Bai Meiling's panic, a black light flew out.

Black King landed on the ground, but did not directly look at the big meal. Instead, he somewhat aggrievedly lay down before Luo Chen.

"Master, why didn't you call me out to help this time either?"

Luo Chen laughed heartily. "It's not that I look down on you. It's just that this was also my first time revealing my complete true body. If I lost control of my strength and flattened you into a meat patty, that would be truly unfortunate."

"Next time, I'll definitely call you next time!"

*Losing control of his strength and flattening me into a meat patty—isn't that still looking down on me?*

Black King muttered to himself, then manifested his hundred-zhang true body, activated his Devouring talent, and swallowed Hundred Leg's body into his own.

Swallowing a seven-to-eight-hundred-zhang giant centipede with a hundred-zhang body was indeed a bit like a snake trying to swallow an elephant.

But after Black King swallowed it, his body only became one size rounder.

"Burp!"

He let out a satisfied belch, curiously glanced at Bai Meiling, and then burrowed back into Luo Chen's arm.

Bai Meiling timidly stared at the black dragon tattoo on Luo Chen's arm. "This is…?"

Luo Chen smiled faintly. "A spirit pet I took in over the years. A simpleton."

Bai Meiling flicked her snake tongue, quite astonished.

The aura that Black King had revealed when he swallowed Hundred Leg's body just now was not that of a weakling.

In her opinion, even some Sage cultivators would be inferior.

Her master was becoming more and more formidable!

Luo Chen stopped what he was doing and frowned slightly as he looked at Bai Meiling's Charm Python ghost body.

"I remember the technique I gave you back then was an orthodox human ghost cultivation method. How did you end up like this?"

Mentioning this, Bai Meiling, whose conversation had just been interrupted, immediately became interested.

"Master, come with me!"

She flew off Luo Chen's body and plunged directly into Taihu Lake.

"Oh?"

Luo Chen blinked, then curiously followed her down.

At this moment, Taihu Lake, due to the previous great battle, had become extremely turbid.

Waves surged, silt churned.

Everywhere they passed, there were even large rocks and jade pillars stuck upside down in the lakebed.

Soon, Bai Meiling's steps stopped at a place with dense water plants.

"It's right here!"

She pushed aside the water plants, waiting for Luo Chen.

Luo Chen swept his divine sense over it; there was no life force inside.

With doubt in his heart, he nevertheless entered the secluded, clear cave dwelling covered by water plants.

The moment he entered, Luo Chen's body stiffened.

Inside the water cave, a young woman lay on a stone bed, her appearance unchanged, just like in the old days.

Luo Chen let out a faint, deep sigh. "It's Xiaoyue!"

Bai Meiling followed him in, crawled onto the stone bed, and began to recount the events of that year in detail.

Back then, she had followed the traces of the Luo Tian Sect and arrived at the Taihu Domain.

Here, she still failed to successfully reunite with the Luo Tian Sect. Instead, she witnessed the demon race slaughtering the cultivators who were hiding everywhere.

She was also about to leave.

But on the way, she saw "Yuan Xiaoyue" killing in all directions.

Bai Meiling was very puzzled and took the initiative to reveal herself, but the other party simply did not recognize her.

One must know that Bai Meiling had also stayed on Danxia Mountain for a very long time. Any high-ranking member of the Luo Tian Sect would more or less recognize her.

Yuan Xiaoyue's status was not low, and since they were both from Dahe Market, she naturally knew Bai Meiling as well.

After a bout of fighting, Bai Meiling finally understood why.

Yuan Xiaoyue had long since died.

Her body had been taken over by a lonely ghost.

And that lonely ghost had no will, only a pure desire to kill.

In order to preserve her friend's physical body, Bai Meiling fought a great battle with it, and was ultimately forced, at the Ghost King realm, to devour the other's soul.

This kind of devouring method was something all Ghost Kings could do.

But only those cruel and violent Ghost Kings would use it. Orthodox ghost cultivators disdained and dared not casually absorb other souls.

It was only after devouring it that Bai Meiling became trapped in this Taihu Domain.

"That soul was a part of the Nine Nether Charm Python!"

Bai Meiling said this.

Luo Chen was thoughtful, recalling another past event.

Back then, when the Taihu Domain was invaded by the demon race, several Demon Emperors had charged in.

Among them, the leader was the Nine Nether Charm Python under the command of the Yellow Barbarian Commander!

That battle was extremely fierce.

The Supreme Elder of Taihu Palace, together with Lingfengzi and others, had managed to kill the Nine Nether Charm Python, even shattering its Sage and splitting its soul.

Lingfengzi and the others had even divided the remains of the Nine Nether Charm Python.

One of its large sinews had been given to Luo Chen as payment for refining the Wind Acupoint Pill.

What Bai Meiling had encountered was a wisp of the Nine Nether Charm Python's soul scattered in the Taihu Domain.

After devouring this soul, Bai Meiling's already not-very-high ghost dao realm suffered a backlash.

Driven by the instinct of the Nine Nether Charm Python's soul, she began searching for other scattered soul fragments in the Taihu Domain.

During this time, Bai Meiling held onto her original heart, bit by bit grinding away the faint wills within those souls, improving her own cultivation.

But her cultivation direction also deviated under the instinct of the Nine Nether Charm Python.

Ultimately, she became the Charm Python ghost body she was now!

"The Nine Nether Charm Python is different from ordinary Demon Emperors. Not only is it proficient in water-type demon arts, but it is also skilled in the dao of soul charm. The former is obtained through cultivation, the latter is innate. Just like the 'Nine Nether' in its name, its origin is a demon beast born in the damp, dark places of the Nine Nether."

"That's why, under the siege of many Sages, it could die but its soul would not be extinguished."

"It has instead benefited you."

Luo Chen sighed with emotion, pityingly rubbing Bai Meiling's solid ghost body.

Bai Meiling was quite pleased, but also somewhat troubled.

"Even though the will within the Nine Nether Charm Python's scattered soul was very faint, it still placed a great burden on me. To grind them away, I often had to fall into slumber, spending my days in a daze. Earlier, when I sensed my master's aura, I didn't dare to recognize you for a moment."

Luo Chen smiled gently. Wasn't he the same?

If he hadn't sensed Bai Meiling's soul aura earlier, why would he have bothered to fight a great battle with Hundred Leg? He would have long since disregarded everything and used the Fire Concealment Art to flee far away.

"It's fine. I'll help you solve these minor problems later."

Bai Meiling nodded happily. She believed in Luo Chen!

But looking down at Yuan Xiaoyue's physical body, she felt a little sad again.

"Xiaoyue... she is ultimately beyond saving."

Luo Chen silently looked at this corpse.

Suddenly, he took out a piece of mystic ice!

This mystic ice was originally prepared as a spare for Canglong, but now it had another use.

Sword qi burst from his fingertip, and ice shavings fell from the mystic ice.

In an instant, an ice coffin took shape.

Luo Chen bent down, picked up Yuan Xiaoyue's body, and placed it inside.

"Everyone must die eventually. To have a complete corpse and for the fallen leaves to return to their roots—that is a kind of completeness."

Putting the ice coffin into his storage ring, Luo Chen beckoned to Bai Meiling.

"Let's go. This place can't be stayed in any longer."

Bai Meiling stared blankly at that scene. She found that Luo Chen had become much more indifferent to the death of friends.

As if he had already become accustomed to it?

"Hm?" Luo Chen turned back to look at her.

Bai Meiling quickly followed.

Coiling on Luo Chen's shoulder, Bai Meiling, in order to dispel that trace of confusion in her heart, chattered away lively as she had back then.

"Master, that Soul-Nourishing Banner you gave me is useless now."

"I know. That Soul-Nourishing Banner was originally only at the magical treasure level, and a damaged one at that. How could it possibly hold you, a Fourth Stage Ghost Emperor? Don't worry, your master has something better here. A Soul-Nourishing Banner from the Primordial Demon Sect of the Northern Sea, at the true artifact level!"

"Really?"

"Would I lie to you?"

Perhaps because he had slain a Great Demon Emperor in battle, or perhaps because he was influenced by the lively nature of his old friend, Luo Chen was in a very good mood at the moment, even joking with Bai Meiling.

But when he leaped out of the lake, this wonderful mood vanished without a trace.

The great battle had ceased for a long time.

Yet Taihu Lake was still surging with turbulent waves, rolling with fury.

The bright moon reflected on the swaying lake surface could not even form a solid image.

Sometimes it shattered, sometimes it folded.

Luo Chen turned back to look in the direction of the Bikong Domain. Separated by tens of thousands of li, it seemed he could still see the Netherworld Yellow Spring Diagram that blotted out the sky and covered the sun.

A full two days!

Was the Ascendant battle still ongoing?

It had already begun to affect the Taihu Domain now.

He dared not stay, and flew further away into the distance.

...

A towering cliff stood suspended amidst the clouds, as if leveling the sky.

A white-robed man sat cross-legged upon it, watching the clouds roll and unfurl beyond the sky, his heart at peace.

Beneath his feet was Motian Cliff!

Once the mountain gate stronghold of a Sage sect, a place that ordinary people dared not climb.

This sect was very interesting. Its name was Motian Cliff, its inherited true artifact was also called Motian Cliff, and even its mountain gate stronghold shared the same name.

But when Luo Chen arrived here, he understood why they had such an obsession.

The spiritual qi was so abundant that it surpassed ordinary Fourth Stage spirit veins.

Not only that, the spiritual qi on Motian Cliff was extremely pure and clear, very suitable for cultivators to absorb and refine, without much effort.

The only pity was that such a pure and clear place was only a small patch, and Luo Chen had only found it by luck using his Illusion-Breaking Eye.

The rest of the place was just an ordinary Fourth Stage spirit vein.

"That old ghost of Motian has occupied this place for many years, yet his realm is stuck at the early Sage stage. Truly a waste!"

Luo Chen sighed with emotion, then closed his eyes and continued to concentrate fully on sensing the flow of the origin qi of heaven and earth.

This was his only way to determine when the Ascendant battle would end.

Three days had passed since the Biyou Mountain negotiations, and the Ascendant battle had also lasted a full three days.

Was that Ancient Demon Emperor Ditian really so difficult to deal with?

The three great ancestors of the Nether Origin Sect had joined forces, yet after three days of fierce battle, they still couldn't take him down?

Luo Chen couldn't help but doubt whether the Nether Origin Sect he was about to join was actually up to the task.

Just as this thought arose in his mind, the sea of clouds beneath his feet suddenly rippled.

Luo Chen was startled. "Again?"

But the next moment, the ripple disappeared.

Luo Chen suddenly stood up, a spiritual light blooming in his eyes, piercing through the thick layers of origin qi to look thousands of li away.

"Is everything finally over?"
