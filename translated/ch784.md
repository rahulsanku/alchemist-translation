# Chapter 784: Luo Tian’s Past, Withering-Bloom Enlightenment Once More

The dao of gu insects was passed down from the Southern Frontier.

In the world of immortal cultivation, it was something that made people’s faces change colour at the mere mention, and their hearts tremble at the sight.

But this was only true for wandering cultivators with little experience.

For someone like Luo Chen, whose cultivation was profound and whose knowledge was vast, he naturally understood that the myriad things of heaven and earth were never divided into good and evil, right and wrong—only into useful and useless.

At the very least, from what he had learned, many gu insects could play a significant role in assisting normal cultivators with their training.

Take, for example, a type of first-rank gu called the Moonlight Gu.

Its rank was not high, but its effects were extremely miraculous—it could guide qi and open meridians.

For those who possessed a spirit root but whose meridians were naturally blocked, refining this gu would allow them to break free from their shackles and engage in normal Qi Refinement training.

Even those whose meridians were naturally unobstructed would not begrudge refining a Moonlight Gu, if conditions permitted, to assist their qi cultivation.

Over in the Central Continent, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect had once purchased Moonlight Gu in large quantities from the Southern Frontier. It was only later, when the Southern Frontier became increasingly closed off, that such trade gradually ceased.

One could say that the widespread circulation of the Moonlight Gu in the past had contributed no small part to the large number of cultivators in the Central Continent.

And the Moonlight Gu was merely one type among many strange gu insects.

The Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu planted in Luo Chen and Sima Huiniang was also one such type, and its origins were even greater.

Legend had it that it was a simplified version of an ancient fifth-rank gu called the Life-and-Death Gu!

That was an extreme gu insect that could bind an Ascendant-level great cultivator and a Qi Refinement cultivator together, sharing life and death.

However, as time passed and the seas changed, the name of the Life-and-Death Gu gradually faded from memory. Meanwhile, another evolutionary path of the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu once stirred up a great storm in the Southern Frontier’s immortal cultivation world.

The third-rank demon gu, the Devil Heart Gu!

There were those who had lost all reason, refining large batches of Devil Heart Gu and planting them in low-rank cultivators. Then, when the gu matured, they would use its miraculous properties to devour the cultivators’ cultivation realms!

It was precisely because of this incident that the Devil Heart Gu was thoroughly suppressed, and even the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu gradually became rarer.

Back then, Luo Chen was about to embark on a “long journey.” Fearing a loss of contact, and after carefully inspecting the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu to confirm there were no problems, he planted it in Sima Huiniang.

As it turned out, the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu’s ability to create a “heart-to-heart connection through a single point of understanding” had indeed brought many benefits.

Whether it was the two years Luo Chen was trapped in the White Night Cave on the Accumulated Thunder battlefield, the three years he spent as a prisoner beneath Green Parasol Mountain, or the hundred years of drifting in the Northern Sea—all those periods of lost contact—the existence of the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu allowed Sima Huiniang to maintain sufficient faith.

Faith that he would return!

If not for that faith, the Luo Tian Sect would likely have long since crumbled. The few cultivators who remained, without the protection of the sect, might have been favoured by the great sects for their outstanding talents and absorbed into them. But most of the mediocre ones would more likely have gradually scattered and disappeared into the vast world of immortal cultivation.

Not everyone could be like Luo Chen, a drifting wandering cultivator who only got better and better the more he wandered.

Later, after Luo Chen’s journey to the Demon-Fall Land, his physical body underwent Nirvana, and the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu along with his dharma power vanished. For a time, Luo Chen was very worried about Sima Huiniang’s situation.

That was why, in the Myriad Manifestations Tower of Divine Origin City in the Northern Sea, he had purchased a batch of low-rank gu insects for research.

After arriving in the Central Continent, following the end of the Grotto-Heaven Grand Competition, Sage Heavenly Mountain, who hailed from the Southern Frontier, had also chatted with him. Taking the opportunity, he asked about the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu.

Whether it was his own research results or the evaluation of a gu dao expert, neither indicated any major problems with the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu.

This was also why Luo Chen had previously believed that resolving the injuries on Sima Huiniang’s body would not be too troublesome.

But now!

An anomaly had occurred!

Under Luo Chen’s observation, the golden core formed by the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu inside Huiniang’s body was like a living thing, connected to her heart, and even to her sea of consciousness and soul!

This was the reason Luo Chen had uttered that phrase: “The Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu connects the heart and also connects the spirit.”

Because of these two connections, Luo Chen dared not forcibly eradicate it.

If he did, not only would her heart rupture, but her soul would also be extinguished at the same time.

“No wonder Caiyi said earlier that Huiniang’s body had changed. This was caused by the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu absorbing her heart-blood. And that pain, which even a golden core cultivator could not suppress, was somewhat alleviated after she cultivated the *Bright Spirit Shattering Balefulness*. Clearly, it also strengthened her own soul’s endurance, allowing her to barely withstand the devouring of her soul.”

“But how exactly did this situation come about?”

Luo Chen wanted to ask Chu Kui if there were any other hidden dangers when he had gifted the pair of gu insects back then.

But as soon as this thought arose, he dismissed it.

Given Chu Kui’s character, if there had been any hidden dangers, he would have mentioned them long ago.

Moreover, neither the two hundred years prior, nor his own research, nor Sage Heavenly Mountain’s insights had indicated any problems with the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu.

“Then the problem can only lie with Huiniang and… me.”

Luo Chen’s eyes narrowed slightly, and a single drop of blood appeared on his fingertip without any visible cause.

The Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu was refined using essence blood and fed on the qi and blood power that circulated daily. Apart from the time of refinement, it did not affect the cultivator’s daily training.

And his, Luo Chen’s, qi and blood…

On his fingertip, the blood was crystal clear, exuding an incomparably vigorous life force.

Even without understanding the changes in qi and blood, this single drop of Luo Chen’s blood was in no way inferior to the blood that Wang Yuan had tempered thousands of times.

This was what the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu had absorbed, growing bit by bit from the first rank to the third rank.

“My blood…”

“Blood Nightmare Moluo once said that the Yuantu sword liked my blood very much.”

“Black King also said he liked to stick to me, because the vigorous qi and blood I emitted made him very comfortable.”

“And the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu, soaked day and night in my qi and blood, made me so accustomed to it, so neglectful of it, that I forgot how far it had grown. When I underwent Nirvana, the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu shattered, but it carried my dharma power with it, revived inside Huiniang’s body, and condensed into a golden core.”

“But now, the host is no longer the physically formidable me, but the frail Huiniang. Therefore, in order to continue growing, it devours her heart and spirit!”

In the depths of his mind, Luo Chen understood everything.

As he murmured to himself, his finger landed on the lips of the woman who had fallen asleep beside him.

That single drop of squeezed-out blood immediately stained her pale, thin lips.

Under the surveillance of his divine sense, within her qi sea, the golden core trembled slightly, but was restrained by the restriction Luo Chen had placed.

Luo Chen released the restriction at the right moment, and then saw a surge of dharma power roll that drop of blood away.

The golden core’s colour deepened!

The woman’s delicate brows, however, completely relaxed!

Seeing this, Luo Chen opened his mouth, but in the end, he lowered his head and let out a soft sigh.

“In the end, it was my negligence.”

…

“Zhou Yuanli fell at the Xiaoyue Mountain Range, during the outbreak of the beast tide.”

“Bai Meiling’s soul disappeared during the escape.”

“Xing Zonghan, having forcefully cultivated the *Fine Dust Primordial Art*, suffered a qi deviation and lost much of his cultivation. During a full-sect retreat, he voluntarily chose to hold the rear, and his fate is unknown.”

“Mi Li and her son Qu Jiu waited in vain within the sect for Qu Lingjun for ten years, and ultimately died with resentment.”

“Duan Feng and his wife, skilled in identifying ores, were ordered by Sage Jinling of the Five Elements Divine Sect to go to the Two Modes Tin Forest support battle in the Wuji Domain. They fell there, leaving no descendants.”

“Xu Haizhen died on the Sanchuan River battlefield.”

“The Falling Cloud Sect’s Supreme Elder disappeared, the Xiaoyue Mountain Range was breached, and a large number of disciples and followers fell. Coupled with Han Zhan’s monopolisation of too many of the Falling Cloud Sect’s resources in his early years in order to break through to the Sage realm, and the failure of the expansion war, internal strife erupted within the Falling Cloud Sect. To seize power, the sect split into three factions: the Sect Leader’s lineage, the lineage of Han Zhan’s grandson Han Zheng, and the lineage of Grand Elder Cheng Yan, who had been left behind at the Tianguyuan. The three factions fought, resulting in countless deaths and injuries. Qin Yuanjiang, who belonged to the Sect Leader’s lineage, had his dao partner Ruan Huan’er unfortunately die in the internal strife, and his mother Murong Qinglian was severely injured. Disheartened, Qin Yuanjiang switched his allegiance to the Luo Tian Sect.”

“Murong Qinglian died within the Luo Tian Sect.”

…

Leaves fell, and the wind sobbed.

Luo Chen sat on the small stone flat outside Huiniang’s cave dwelling, reading the *Luo Tian Records* of the Luo Tian Sect from these past years.

This material had been compiled ever since the Luo Tian Sect was still the Luo Tian Association.

It had always been kept in three copies: one stored in the Sutra Library, one carried by the current Sect Leader, and one held by Luo Chen.

Luo Chen’s copy had stopped a hundred years ago.

What he was reading now had been sent over by Yao Mingyue’s people.

It recorded little about events outside the sect; most of it concerned the major changes and events within the Luo Tian Sect itself.

And what Luo Chen was paying attention to were those names, both familiar and unfamiliar.

Ever since he had arrived at Baili Qingchuan, his divine sense had already swept over this area, detecting the auras of many old acquaintances, but also sensing the absence of many more.

At this time, not being present in the sect meant… Luo Chen had long been mentally prepared for what it signified.

But when he actually saw the contents of the *Luo Tian Records*, he still pressed his lips together, clenched his teeth, and his brow twitched uncontrollably.

He didn’t know what he felt in his heart.

Regret? Grief? Pain?

Or perhaps anger?

Anger at the invasion of demon beasts, anger at the oppression from multiple forces, anger at the Five Elements Divine Sect for not treating the Luo Tian disciples as human beings, anger at the Falling Cloud Sect’s internal strife that led to the death of a respected friend.

And even, anger at his own inaction!

A pressure, like the one before a thunderclap, emanated faintly from his body.

Thump!

Xu Xiao Liu, who had delivered the book, was too close and couldn’t withstand the pressure, falling to his knees.

“Alas…”

Xu Xiao Liu, his hair already white, heard a sigh in his ears, and then felt the heaven-like pressure dissipate in an instant.

He cautiously raised his head.

“Little Six, you’re still as slippery as ever. They’re all dead, and you’re the only one still alive.”

Xu Xiao Liu was stunned, not knowing how to answer.

Then he saw the young man smile self-deprecatingly and mutter to himself, “That’s right. Only people like you and me, slippery as we are, can survive a hundred bends and breaks, like loaches and earthworms, no matter where we go.”

Xu Xiao Liu’s heart relaxed, and he said in an aged voice, “How could the Supreme Elder be the same as Little Six? I am merely a mayfly of the vast sea, barely clinging to life. You, sir, are the clear sky and bright moon, no matter the wind or rain, never losing a shred of your lustre.”

“You, at this age, still know how to flatter just like back then.”

Luo Chen smiled and sent out a surge of dharma power.

He lifted the white-haired Xu Xiao Liu and sat him down on the stone chair opposite him.

Xu Xiao Liu placed his hands on his knees, sitting upright. The man opposite him was as young as ever, and his attitude towards others was as warm as a spring breeze.

It seemed as if nothing had changed, yet everything had changed.

But it had been many years since anyone had called him Little Six, and he found it exceptionally warm and familiar.

“Little Six, tell me about the current situation within the sect.”

“Mm.”

Perhaps because of Luo Chen’s gentle attitude, Xu Xiao Liu relaxed a great deal and began to recount the current state of the Luo Tian Sect in detail.

“Our sect now has three hundred followers, with over two hundred Qi Refinement cultivators and mortals, and about thirty Initiate-level cultivators. However, the number of golden core cultivators is exceptionally high, with a full nine Golden Core Superiors!”

“The nine golden cores are: Old Sect Leader Sima, Elder Fu Jiusheng, Elder Chu Kui, Elder Gu Caiyi, Elder Min Longyu, Elder Li Yingzhang, Elder Yang Pingdu, and Elder Qin Yuanjiang.”

Wang Yuan and Baleful Dragonzi, though possessing golden core combat power, were not, strictly speaking, golden core cultivators.

They were body refiners.

But!

Luo Chen was curious. “That’s only eight, isn’t it?”

Xu Xiao Liu said somewhat awkwardly, “If you count Sima Wenjie, then it’s nine.”

Luo Chen gave an “oh” and gestured for him to continue.

But in his heart, he was sighing. He had deliberately left behind the *Fine Dust Primordial Art* back then and had Wang Yuan study a simplified version—he had indeed made the right choice.

Without that all-encompassing *Fine Dust Primordial Art*, how could the small Luo Tian Sect have produced so many golden core cultivators?

Among the nine, Sima Huiniang needed no further mention.

Fu Jiusheng and Chu Kui had formed their cores earlier. Gu Caiyi had successfully broken through by using the Dark Origin Pill he had left behind. Li Yingzhang had accumulated steadily and then burst forth.

Qin Yuanjiang had succeeded in forming his core by inheriting the legacy of the Falling Cloud Sect. Min Longyu, with the help of Qu Lingjun, had managed to form his core by relying on formations.

The only unfamiliar one was Yang Pingdu!

This man was the son of Yang Wenchang, the Sect Leader of the Myriad Manifestations Sect, who had been secretly murdered by Diwu Qi during the expansion war.

In order to keep a handle on Diwu Qi, Luo Chen had forcibly kept the members of the Myriad Manifestations Sect.

Later, the Myriad Manifestations Sect’s senior disciple, Zong Jiulang, died on the Tianguyuan battlefield.

Yang Pingdu then became the leader of the Myriad Manifestations Sect.

After the beast tide erupted, in order to preserve the sect, Yang Pingdu led a group of disciples and completely joined the Luo Tian Sect. He then exerted himself wholeheartedly in the subsequent battles.

To reward him and to unite people’s hearts, Sima Huiniang, after successfully forming her own core, bestowed the remaining Dark Origin Pill upon Yang Pingdu.

He succeeded!

It was because of this matter that Sima Wenjie rebelled!

He sought refuge elsewhere, obtained the help of a Sage, successfully formed his core, and then brought outsiders to try and seize the Luo Tian Sect.

A messy account, impossible to calculate clearly, impossible to reason through. Xu Xiao Liu’s droning voice filled his ears.

“Among the younger generation, Qin Yuanjiang and Zeng Yilong are the most promising, but the others are not bad either.”

“Mei Ruoqi, Yuan Yijun, Qu Jiu, and others are gradually showing their talents. Su’e has died, but her disciple Suwen has inherited the medical tradition. After Qu Lingjun left, Qi Yi took up the banner of alchemy for the Luo Tian Sect. It is precisely because of the many Initiate-level spiritual medicines he refined that our sect’s Initiate True Cultivators have emerged one after another…”

When Luo Chen heard the name Qi Yi, a thoughtful expression appeared on his face.

This was an alchemist he had personally recruited in his early years, and he had been a disciple of an enemy!

He remembered that at the time, he had recruited him together with his senior or junior brother?

His alchemy talent was beyond doubt.

But refining many Initiate-level spiritual medicines?

Could it be the Imperial Nectar?

But he had only passed that pill formula on to Qu Lingjun.

Could it be that Qu Lingjun had secretly taught it before he left?

If that were the case, then it would make sense.

During the great war between humans and demons, there was no shortage of various demon beast corpse materials. Using them to refine large quantities of Imperial Nectar would indeed ensure a continuous supply of Initiate True Cultivators for the Luo Tian Sect.

Looking at it this way, his only personal disciple had indeed been loyal to the sect, considering all aspects.

“Sect Leader Yao goes without saying. After succeeding as Sect Leader, although she is not as capable as the old Sect Leader, she has been steady and stable, diligently maintaining this foundation of the Luo Tian Sect.”

“The daughter born to this old man and Shilan is now studying by Sect Leader Yao’s side.”

At the end, Xu Xiao Liu suddenly mentioned his daughter.

This intention…

Luo Chen glanced at him. The other man looked a bit embarrassed, but his expression was frank.

Luo Chen didn’t mind and instead asked curiously, “Shilan? Could it be that girl from the Icecastle back then?”

“Exactly! At that time, the Jade Cauldron Domain was in chaos, and the Icecastle also encountered upheaval. I saved Shilan’s life, and later she became my dao partner.”

Speaking of this matter, Xu Xiao Liu was quite proud, as if being able to be with that woman was the proudest achievement of his life.

But then, his tone dropped.

“Unfortunately, later on, Shilan left her daughter behind and departed without saying goodbye, going to find her fellow disciples from the Icecastle.”

“What happened to the Icecastle?”

“That’s a long story. The Icecastle’s Supreme Elder failed to break through to the Sage realm, and many strong members of the sect fell…”

Luo Chen listened half-heartedly as Xu Xiao Liu spoke of past events, but he didn’t pay much attention.

Right now, just the matters of the Luo Tian Sect were enough for him to consider.

Those old friends and past events of the Jade Cauldron Domain were truly not an urgent priority. He would just take it as gaining some understanding.

Xu Xiao Liu, perhaps also noticing Luo Chen’s distraction, gradually wound down his topic.

“Of the former Seven Sects of the Jade Cauldron, apart from our Luo Tian Sect, the others have long since scattered or perished. If not for the Supreme Elder’s timely return, the name of Luo Tian would likely have been scattered to the winds as well.”

Luo Chen comforted him, then calmly asked a new question.

“How many years of your lifespan remain?”

Xu Xiao Liu was startled, and subconsciously stroked his beard as he said, “Little Six’s lifespan is two hundred and forty-seven years. My end is near.”

Then, he habitually began to flatter.

“I have already seen through matters of life and death. Shilan and I were once deeply in love, and my bloodline remains in this world. Even dying before I have seen your return, sir, would make Little Six die willingly.”

“You can’t die now. I still have a task for you.”

Luo Chen smiled slightly, and while the other man was puzzled, he spoke a few words.

After hearing them, Xu Xiao Liu’s eyes immediately sparkled with light.

He clapped his hands excitedly and said, “I accept this task! I will not let you down, sir!”

After Xu Xiao Liu left, Luo Chen closed the *Luo Tian Records*.

Standing on the small stone flat, he gazed at the mountains and waters of Baili Qingchuan, his mind filled with thoughts of the Luo Tian Sect’s affairs.

As he thought deeply, he couldn’t help but murmur:

“Generation after generation, people age and die, leaving behind those who either continue to climb the peak or flourish and thrive.”

“Just like that small tree, facing winter and welcoming summer, shedding the complexity to preserve the simple, retaining the trunk and branches, eventually becoming a towering giant that scrapes the sky.”

“The dao of withering and blooming—is it not just like this?”

The wind stopped, the leaves fell, and the forest rustled, as if the mountains and waters here were responding to him.
