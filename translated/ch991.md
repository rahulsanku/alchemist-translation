# Chapter 991: Wrathful Aspect, Deep Practice, Three-Headed Moluo

Luo Chen entering the formation was both a necessity and an inevitability.

If he wanted the Hanging Temple experts to not disrupt Xuan Du's breakthrough, he had to hold these people back.

With the strength of one man, he drew the attention of thirty-six eminent monks!

The moment he entered the formation, Luo Chen's long sword swept out, unleashing streak after streak of cunning sword light.

The sword light was fierce, not only with tricky angles but also with a grand bearing. Yet every sword strike sank like a stone into the vast sea, vanishing without a trace.

Luo Chen was not surprised by this. Instead, he looked as if he had expected it all along.

"As I thought!"

"That earlier moment, when a single ordinary monk could block my full-powered strike, was no illusion."

"Under the guidance of this Arhat Grand Formation, the thirty-six men appear to be arrayed in four directions, but in truth, they are fused into one. One prospers, all prosper; one suffers, all suffer. Every strike from any one of them can be seen as the combined power of all thirty-six."

"If that's the case…"

Luo Chen's eyes widened, his pupils spinning at an incredible speed as he frantically searched for potential weaknesses.

Suddenly!

His white figure moved like thunder, striking toward the northeast corner.

The speed was so fast it was difficult to track.

Yet the next moment, a deafening clang of metal rang out.

A heavy precept-blade had blocked the path of the Yuantu Sword.

It was the Abbot of the Precept Court!

Luo Chen smiled faintly, withdrew, and attacked elsewhere.

The Abbot of the Precept Court glanced at the precept-blade in his hand. A deep sword scar marked its surface.

"What a fine demon sword!"

Half praise, half anger stirred in his heart. He was unimpressed by Luo Chen's probing attack.

Breaking the surface by striking a single point—how could it be so simple?

Indeed!

In the following moments, no matter how ethereal and illusory Luo Chen's movement technique was, no matter how swift and dragon-like, every single one of his attacks was met by a Great Cultivator-level Abbot of the Eight Courts, who moved through the formation to personally block him.

Of the Eight Court Abbots, aside from the Prajna Hall and the Dharma Court, who were holding the line, the other six Abbots had all personally integrated into the formation.

It seemed like Luo Chen was breaking the surface by striking a single point, but in reality, every time he was clashing head-on with the strongest point. And that point not only possessed the strength of a Great Cultivator itself but could also rapidly gather the power of the entire Thirty-Six Arhat Grand Formation.

How difficult was it for Luo Chen to break through?

The Great Brahma Heavenly Son, watching from outside, saw this and couldn't help but shake his head regretfully.

"Luo Chen is strong, certainly, but in the end, he is too arrogant."

At the entrance, the Nascent Soul old monsters who had followed them in, upon witnessing the might of the Arhat Grand Formation, had to suppress many of their schemes.

Facing such a top-tier formation, they couldn't possibly fish in troubled waters or seek any advantage.

However, they were quite shocked that Luo Chen, inside the formation, was not suppressed but instead was launching attacks frequently.

"Is that the Alchemy Sect's Luo Chen?"

"I consider my own mana to be quite abundant, but facing a formation that ties together dozens of Nascent Souls, I would surely be trapped in a quagmire. Yet that Luo Chen seems completely unaffected?"

"Still calling him the Alchemy Sect? Didn't you hear the Hanging Temple eminent monks call him a Moluo?"

"Moluo? Is it Luo Chen who has fallen to the demonic path, or a great demon who seizes lives, destroys dharma, and disrupts the roots of virtue and merit?"

"Perhaps both!"

"What a pity. A generation of demonic titans, before even revealing his demonic might, is about to be suppressed."

None of them were optimistic about Luo Chen, even though on the surface he had not yet fallen into a major disadvantage.

Their judgment had its reasons.

Once inside the Arhat Grand Formation, Luo Chen could not leave.

Moreover, the formation had not yet fully activated, its power had not reached its limit, and two of the Eight Court Abbots had not even joined yet.

At that moment, the white-faced monk, Abbot of the Prajna Hall, glanced at the situation inside the formation, his expression utterly calm.

"He can't stir up any waves. Senior Brother Huineng, prepare to join me in dealing with Xuan Du!"

The Abbot of the Dharma Court nodded.

The two of them were not only holding the line but were also the main force targeting Dharma Master Xuan Du.

Luo Chen's appearance, though somewhat unexpected, was still within controllable bounds.

But the next moment, the white-faced monk's expression changed.

A sudden change occurred within the formation!

Luo Chen, who was attacking the Abbot of the Arhat Hall, suddenly transformed into specks of fire and appeared on the other side, stabbing his sword at the Abbot of the Mind Meditation Court.

As the two clashed, Luo Chen's footwork turned bizarre. He shifted again, appearing before a monk of roughly early Nascent Soul cultivation, and struck out with a heavy palm.

Such a rapid succession of form changes, happening in the blink of an eye, left everyone unable to react.

If he succeeded, the Thirty-Six Arhat Grand Formation would instantly reveal a flaw.

A figure abruptly blocked Luo Chen's path, pushing out a single palm.

Boom!

The two palms clashed, stirring up a boundless gale, forcibly driving Luo Chen back.

The Abbot of the Dharma Court had personally entered the formation!

His half-open monk's robe rustled loudly, and his withdrawn right hand shone like gold.

Chi! Chi! Chi!

In the void, three afterimages slowly dissipated, finally merging into Luo Chen's main body.

He held his long sword inverted in his left hand, his right hand pressing down on the empty air, suppressing the fierce palm wind.

Raising his eyes to look at the broad-faced, large-eared monk and the other white-faced monk, the corners of his mouth lifted.

"If you don't go all out, this formation of yours can't trap me!"

The Abbot of the Prajna Hall's expression turned somewhat gloomy.

He hadn't expected Luo Chen's formation mastery to be so outstanding, nor his combat experience to be so remarkable.

The Arhat Grand Formation, which originally had no flaws, had, under his three consecutive attacks, forcibly produced flaws that shouldn't have existed.

The Abbot of the Dharma Court beside him spoke up: "Formations are dead; people are alive. By using feints, he forced the formation's power to constantly shift. In that process of shifting, flaws appeared. The six Court Abbots alone couldn't react in time."

Luo Chen, hovering in the air, heard this and couldn't help but look at the old monk of the Dharma Court with a bit more respect.

That was indeed what he had done earlier.

The formation's power gathering at a single point was indeed the greatest strength of the Arhat Grand Formation, but it was also its greatest weakness.

As long as his attacks were strong enough and fast enough, he could force the formation to constantly shift its power in response to his movements.

In that case, flaws would appear everywhere!

Of course, that was no longer the case.

With the remaining two Court Abbots entering the formation, its operation became smoother, making it far more difficult to create an opportunity like before.

"So, are you still holding back?" Luo Chen asked with a smile, as if catching up with an old friend.

But this expression of his, in the eyes of others, was nothing short of arrogant and overbearing.

As if the Hanging Temple monks not going all out against him was an insult.

The white-faced monk squeezed his prayer beads and said coldly, "This demon is stubborn and hard to convert. Let us grind him down, then stop Xuan Du!"

As he spoke, he and the Abbot of the Dharma Court also fully integrated into the formation.

And the Thirty-Six Arhat Grand Formation began to change.

When it flowed, it was like moving clouds and water.

When it paused, it was as heavy as a mountain.

The spiritual pressure linked together was like a giant millstone, trying to grind everything inside into powder.

Deep within it, Luo Chen's originally high movement speed was greatly suppressed, and even his Fire Concealment Art became difficult to use.

His smile gradually faded, and he looked calmly around him.

So this was the true Thirty-Six Arhat Grand Formation?

Its power was still climbing!

Suddenly!

A monk in a kapok robe appeared, raising his staff to smash down at Luo Chen.

Luo Chen countered with his sword, using four ounces to deflect a thousand pounds, parrying the staff.

"Abbot of the Mind Meditation Court?"

Above, saber qi swept across the sky, as if to split mountains and crack stones.

Luo Chen raised a single hand to the heavens, using his bare palm to forcibly send that familiar precept-blade flying.

"Abbot of the Arhat Hall!"

Suddenly, a thunderous sound exploded, then transformed into boundless Buddhist true words.

"Om! Ma! Ni! Pad! Me! Hum!"

As the Buddhist sounds chanted, the world seemed to fall into a hell of red dust.

But strangely, Luo Chen stood amidst it, seemingly unaffected. Instead, his eyes blazed with brilliant light, conjuring Mirror Flower Water Moon.

Buddha heads fell, celestial maidens undressed.

Wine, meat, sound, color, the desires of all beings—none were lacking.

Using spiritual-eye illusions against true-word illusion realms—this was the most extreme and dangerous collision. One wrong move and one would sink into it.

And the result came swiftly.

A compassionate old monk, blood at the corner of his mouth, retreated into the formation with lingering confusion in his eyes.

"His soul foundation is too powerful to be affected!"

Protected by the *Prison Suppression* soul art, Luo Chen wasn't too affected. But the moment he escaped the true-word illusion, a massive palm print struck him from behind.

Luo Chen couldn't react in time and thrust his sword back.

Clang!

The killing move of the Prajna Hall Abbot was broken. A surge of anger rose on his fair face.

"Wrathful Aspect, Deep Practice, Vajra Prajna!"

Another palm struck out, its power stacking.

Luo Chen pushed out a palm as well, but this time he couldn't block it.

Boom!

Under the impact of the two palms, Luo Chen was sent flying backward.

And within the formation, attacks came one after another. All kinds of spells and magic treasures rained down on him.

His entire figure was swallowed by the boundless ocean of Buddhist qi.

Outside the formation, everyone watching this scene felt a chill in their hearts.

Luo Chen's sudden outburst had nearly broken the famed Arhat Grand Formation, astonishing them.

Could the power of a single man be so strong?

But the Arhat Grand Formation, supplemented by the Eight Court Abbots, also shook their minds.

The attacks were ever-changing, too many to take in, and as the formation continuously operated, its power was still madly stacking.

Could there truly be a Nascent Soul cultivator in this world who could withstand this peerless formation?

"Luo Chen is in danger!" The group of Nascent Soul old monsters at the entrance reached this conclusion after discussion.

The Great Brahma Heavenly Son initially agreed with this assessment, but recalling the scene when he first met Luo Chen, he became somewhat uncertain.

Especially when his gaze fell upon that bamboo grove, he found it hard to make a judgment.

"The outcome is still unknown."

The Gandharva beside him was puzzled, but soon thought of that fleeting scene from earlier. A look of doubt and uncertainty appeared on her charming face.

"Can that one move withstand the Arhat Grand Formation?"

As if to confirm their suspicions.

Luo Chen, after being repeatedly struck within the formation, no longer concealed his methods.

With a long roar, a thousand-zhang true body rose from the ground.

Three heads, ferocious: a white face, a red head, and a wrathful yaksha aspect.

Six arms, bizarre: stirring the winds and clouds, churning the formation.

And unlike the initial six empty arms, this time the six hands were gripping several magic treasures.

One hand held a sword, two hands gripped a staff, and two golden feathers were attached to two arms.

Only one hand was empty.

Seeing this, the Hanging Temple monks were all shaken.

Three heads and six arms—this was a Buddhist divine ability!

"Senior Brother Xuan Du, the dharma is not transmitted outside the six ears. How could such a divine ability be lightly taught?"

The Prajna Hall Abbot recognized the essence of Luo Chen's transformation technique at almost the first glance. After his loud questioning, he received no response.

He grew even angrier!

"All monks, follow me in subduing the demon!"

Within the formation, deep red chains evolved, descending from the sky, smashing toward Luo Chen's thousand-zhang true body.

Luo Chen was unafraid. He pointed a finger at his chest, at his Purple Mansion.

Clatter! Clatter! Clatter!

One crimson chain after another burst forth from his body, curling back into the void, knocking away those chains.

Stretching his limbs slightly, Luo Chen's three pairs of eyes looked in all directions, revealing a ferocious expression.

"Now, it's my turn!"

The Yuantu Sword's light scattered like rain.

The Hundun Staff churned the seas.

The two life-taking golden feathers pierced endlessly, appearing and disappearing unpredictably.

Under his simultaneous execution, the entire formation was moved by him.

It was like a massive whirlpool in a boundless ocean, the ocean moving with it, waves sweeping across the sky.

This scene truly shocked the watching experts.

If earlier it was a peerless formation besieging the tiny Luo Chen, now it was a thousand-zhang giant clashing head-on with the formation.

Luo Chen, who had been at a disadvantage, had once again seized the initiative.

Of course, the Hanging Temple's sect-guarding formation, famed through the ages, was no idle boast.

Although Luo Chen had pushed it to the brink, the formation was still operating, and growing stronger.

One attack after another was unleashed, bringing much damage to Luo Chen's massive, hard-to-dodge body.

Especially the ugly yaksha aspect, its demonic qi rolling. Every time it was struck by an attack containing Buddhist power, it would wither considerably.

Buddha and demon, each restraining the other.

When Buddha arose, demon waned; when demon grew, Buddha perished.

Between them, there was only a difference of strength and weakness.

Unfortunately, the demonic power on Luo Chen's body was too scarce, only partially converted from the Yuantu Sword.

In the continuous stalemate, it was gradually suppressed by the formation, even becoming a key target.

Luo Chen quickly realized this.

"This body still has flaws!"

With this thought, the Yuantu Sword in Luo Chen's hand grew more furious, its flourishing sword light scattering recklessly.

And that empty arm began to form hand seals with a single hand.

In the void, the ocean of origin qi that Dharma Master Xuan Du had drawn down was attracted, sending down a vast stream of heaven-earth origin qi.

Luo Chen pointed a finger.

Divine Ability—Withering-Bloom Dao Finger!

Green light spread, death qi abundant.

Under this finger, the originally smooth-flowing Arhat Grand Formation became sluggish and stiff, less agile than before.

Some of the monks even showed wrinkles of age on their faces, their hair turning white.

Faced with this situation, an old monk stepped forward and also pointed a finger.

"Amitabha!"

Under his finger, the deathly still, pale death qi actually reversed course, splashing onto Luo Chen, causing his fleshly body to wither.

But in an instant, source power flowed, and his body recovered as before.

Luo Chen took a deep look at that old monk of the Dharma Court, already having an idea.

Within the Buddhist sect, there were also those skilled in the dao of Withering and Blooming!

Another nun from the Medicine King Court gently sprinkled her willow branch. As the rain and dew fell evenly, the monks affected by Luo Chen's Withering-Bloom Dao Finger began to recover quickly.

This formation, once fully operational, truly had no flaws!

No matter what Luo Chen did, the other side always had a way to deal with it.

In the end, it was still lacking something.

Luo Chen knew deeply that to break the formation, he needed overwhelming power.

And on his body, there was only one thing.

"Come forth!"

Luo Chen roared to the sky. A towering green fire tree extended outward, its branches spreading in all directions, as if to merge into the void.

Fifth-Order Divine Fire!

Faced with this move, the monks in the Arhat Grand Formation were quite surprised, but they were not flustered.

The thirty-six of them opened their mouths and spat out at the same time.

Relic True Fire surged and spread, instantly twisting into a single strand, pouncing toward the green fire tree.

As the two collided, ripples spread through the void, as if to burn everything.

Luo Chen was also surprised, but thinking about it, how could a formation used to deal with Ascendant-level experts lack methods to counter the true fire of an Ascendant?

His eyes flickered as he saw the trend of the clash between the two flames.

The Withering-Bloom Divine Fire was gaining the upper hand!

In the end, its quality was still higher.

But this upper hand was not significant.

The quantity of the Relic True Fire was simply too great.

As time passed, the east wind would turn around and overwhelm the west wind.

He, Luo Chen, was ultimately using the mana of one man to contend with thirty-six eminent monks, and the other side had the formation's blessing.

"Can the Nirvana Sacred Flame break the deadlock?"

Just as Luo Chen's mind was turning, his gaze suddenly froze.

Where the void ripples surged, a pure white fist emerged.

Bang!

With one punch thrown, the already stiff formation immediately revealed a flaw.

Luo Chen seized the opportunity, and the Yuantu Sword slashed out.

The vast sword light, like a river of stars, surged forward, following the flaw to cut a bloody path.

Boom!

As the void shook, the Arhat Grand Formation was broken!

Dozens of figures stumbled and flew, scattering in all directions.

The Eight Court Abbots were both shocked and furious, looking up.

A tall figure was walking out of the void, slowly descending onto the shoulder of the thousand-zhang giant.

(Chapter End)
