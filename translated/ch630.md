# Chapter 630: Lord Lushan Arrives, Severing Mortal Ties

On this day, within the Fulong Mountain Range, a mass of clear cloud slowly flew over from far away.

On the cloud, two graceful figures stood elegantly.

A faint breeze brushed past, sending hair drifting.

The one standing in front had a calm expression, seeming to rather enjoy the mountain breeze blowing across her.

The woman behind her, however, could not stop smoothing her hair. Her face seemed calm, but her eyes were constantly turning.

"Ah Xiu, your heart is no longer still." A gentle voice came from the female Daoist in front.

Hearing this, Wen Xiu could not help smiling bitterly.

Who could truly keep their heart as still as water when faced with timidity upon nearing home?

"Master, why must it be so decisive?"

Lord Lushan said indifferently, "Since you have entered my sect, you should sever mortal ties and be indifferent to fame and profit. Your talent has the potential for Golden Core, especially after switching to my cultivation method. You should have advanced a thousand li in a day, but these years you have stopped at Foundation Establishment Stage Six, unable to advance an inch. Previously, you concealed it well, and I only thought you had encountered a temporary difficulty. Only after your daughter came to seek you that time did I understand the reason."

Wen Xiu lowered her brows and eyes, murmuring, "But how can blood relations be so easily severed? Little Qing is the child I carried for ten months and gave birth to by spending essence blood. I cannot do it... Master, did you not also fail to do it back then?"

Lord Lushan frowned.

Back then, she indeed had not severed those so-called mortal ties. It was only after her close family passed away, and through the power of time, that she gradually forgot those bonds.

Being pointed out by her disciple at this moment was truly embarrassing.

But Lord Lushan did not grow angry.

She sighed softly. "It is precisely because I experienced it that I know what miraculous effect achieving that step in advance will have on cultivating the Book of Fading Memory [TERM?]."

Wen Xiu was lost and helpless.

Lord Lushan explained slightly, "Unlike ordinary immortal cultivators whose cultivation emphasizes resources, our Daoist lineage methods focus on cultivating the heart. At the beginning, cultivation speed may be somewhat slower, but as long as the state of mind rises, one will catch up from behind. According to the introduction in the Book of Fading Memory, this cultivation method's true wonder can only truly be displayed after the Sage stage."

Was it truly like this?

Wen Xiu felt uneasy. Secular Daoist traditions seemed not to forbid marriage, funerals, weddings, and such things.

Could it be that their lineage was especially different?

Glancing at the young married woman whose hair was in disarray, Lord Lushan shook her head.

"On this trip, apart from having you actively settle the origins between you and the Zhang family, this master also wants to visit a friend. When the time comes, first accompany me to Peng Island, then return to Cailian Island."

"Mm."

As they spoke, the clear cloud had already crossed a thousand mountains and finally landed beside the vast, misty great lake.

The moment the cloud descended, cultivators from Wind-Wave Pavilion [TERM?] by the lakeside took the initiative to come forward and welcome them.

Lord Lushan's spiritual pressure spread outside, immediately revealing her Golden Core realm, so the pavilion-guarding cultivators behaved very respectfully.

"Senior, may I ask whether you are here to visit Peng Lake's lord or the Cailian Zhang family... Madam Wen?"

As the old cultivator spoke, his gaze subconsciously fell on Wen Xiu behind her, and he could not help blurting out the last words.

Lord Lushan waved her sleeve. "Ah Xiu, you handle this!"

"Mm."

Wen Xiu stepped forward and negotiated with the old cultivator she knew.

She had lived in Peng Lake for a long time, and she knew the rules here perfectly.

Especially after stating that Lord Lushan had come at the personal invitation of Peng Lake's lord, Daoist Azure Yang, the cultivators of Wind-Wave Pavilion directly opened the door of convenience.

After Lord Lushan took Wen Xiu by boat toward Peng Island, the old pavilion-guarding cultivator in Wind-Wave Pavilion had an uncertain expression for a while, then ultimately headed toward Cailian Island.

On the small boat.

Lord Lushan's divine sense naturally took all the situation behind them into her eyes, and her divine sense voice transmission sounded in Wen Xiu's ears.

"See? Matters in the world are just like this."

"Back then, when you managed the Cailian Island Zhang family for Zhang Jiadi, you were gentle internally and often bestowed kindness. Even after you later left, you never mistreated them."

"But now, with your sudden return, those people who once received your kindness still chose, after hesitating repeatedly, to send word to the people on Cailian Island."

"When a person leaves, the tea grows cold. It is no more than this."

"Family affection and love may be a little deeper, but they also cannot withstand testing. Severing them early is the proper path."

Behind her, pain appeared in the woman's eyes, and her pearly teeth bit her lip, as if she were unwilling to accept the truth.

Or rather, she had long since understood these principles.

It was only that in the past, she had thought of yielding. After yielding failed, she thought of escaping, until she entered Lord Lushan's sect in the name of admiring the Great Dao.

But in the end, she was still escaping!

Lord Lushan had precisely seen through her thoughts, and only then chose to bring her back and have her face all this.

If the other party still could not let go, then she would not mind arriving as a pair and leaving alone.

At some unknown moment, a gentle laugh sounded beside Wen Xiu's ear.

"Fellow Daoist, please!"

"Daoist Azure Yang is too courteous. Why come so far to welcome this poor Daoist?"

"Haha, I merely walked a few extra steps. How can that be called coming far to welcome you? Come, this way, please. Previously, I heard Fellow Daoist likes touring scenery. My Peng Lake also has a mountain, and atop it there is a Lake-Viewing Pavilion where one can look down and fully enjoy the lake and mountain scenery. Let us go there to admire the view while discussing matters."

"Then this poor Daoist will follow the host's convenience. Ah Xiu, wait here for a while."

Wen Xiu nodded. After the two left, she stood alone at the edge of Peng Island, her eyes gazing toward one direction, her expression changing without pause.

...

Roughly half a day later, Luo Chen and Lord Lushan returned together.

Both had smiles on their faces. Presumably, their discussion had gone smoothly.

After Lord Lushan landed beside Wen Xiu, she smiled faintly at Luo Chen and said, "You will decide the specific departure time. I will hurry to produce the required talismans, and I will certainly not delay your important matter."

Luo Chen smiled and nodded. "There is no hurry. It will still take a while. On my side, I also still need to invite another fellow Daoist to travel with us."

Lord Lushan was somewhat surprised. Apart from Sang Jiugong, he actually still needed one more person?

From this, it seemed Daoist Azure Yang was truly determined to obtain that Purple Spirit Island!

But after another thought, she felt more people was also good. If they truly encountered trouble then, it would be safer.

After all, it was deep in the Demon Sea, where the demon race gathered.

"In that case, this poor Daoist will no longer disturb you." Lord Lushan saluted and prepared to take Wen Xiu away.

Luo Chen spoke, "Do you need Luo to serve as an intermediary?"

Lord Lushan hesitated briefly, but ultimately agreed.

The Cailian Island Zhang family was not worth fearing, merely a Foundation Establishment family.

But behind it was the Sage force, the Coral Sea Zhou clan.

A mid Golden Core cultivator like her could not suppress them, though she had not planned to use force in the first place.

But Luo Chen was different. He was an honored guest of the Zhou clan, and also the Zhang family's lord. If he came forward, everyone had to give him some face.

Next, it would depend on Wen Xiu's thoughts.

"Ah Xiu, have you decided?"

...

Cailian Island.

Luo Chen's gaze fell on this island rich in spiritual qi, and he could not help raising a brow.

He remembered very clearly that this small island originally had only one second-tier lower-grade spirit vein. But now, viewing it with Spiritual Eye, it possessed second-tier middle-grade spiritual qi intensity, and even faintly showed signs of reaching upper-grade.

Yet strangely, this additional spiritual qi was not entirely seized from Peng Lake's main vein.

His gaze wandered, falling on those gradually thinning lotus leaves and lotus flowers, as well as the Emerald Coral spread across the lake bottom. Luo Chen felt thoughtful.

"Previously, I only heard Heavenjade say that after Zhou Shanshan married in, the Zhang family grew increasingly prosperous. At the time, I only thought this was a change in wealth and population."

"Now it seems that in every respect, it has become different."

On Wen Xiu's side, however, her heart tightened more and more.

The once endless green lotus leaves stretching to the horizon had now become withered lotus roots and ruined lotus flowers, cold and desolate in autumn.

Things had truly remained, but people had changed.

For a time, the tightened mood also inexplicably gained a bit of openness.

People had changed, and things had changed too. Why should she still cling bitterly?

Perhaps because they had learned of Wen Xiu's return in advance, and because Luo Chen's and Lord Lushan's escape lights had not been concealed in the slightest, by the time they arrived, a group of people had already gathered in advance in the Zhang family's jade plaza at the center of Cailian Island.

As Luo Chen descended, the Zhang family cultivators led by Zhang Jiadi and Zhou Shanshan all bowed in worship.

"Greetings, Azure Yang!"

Over a hundred cultivators gathered together, and the sound they produced was like mountains calling and seas roaring.

Luo Chen had seen many grand scenes and did not care much about this.

He waved his hand. "Today is your family affair. This one will temporarily act as an intermediary. Just resolve it harmoniously."

After saying this, he walked straight toward the Zhang family's main courtyard, looking entirely casual and not treating himself as an outsider at all.

Behind him.

Zhang Jiadi looked with eyes full of tenderness at Wen Xiu, who did not glance sideways.

Zhou Shanshan pursed her lips and stared fixedly at Wen Xiu, as if someone were stealing food.

Wen Xiu, who had gradually thought things through, was instead strangely calm at this moment. She followed behind her master, Lord Lushan, and entered the courtyard.

Zhang Jiadi waved his hand to dismiss the family cultivators, bringing only Zhou Shanshan inside with him.

...

For this so-called Zhang family matter, Luo Chen watched from beginning to end and saw quite the spectacle.

It was nothing more than Wen Xiu wanting to separate peacefully from Zhang Jiadi and take away the portion of property that belonged to her.

Zhang Jiadi was naturally unwilling.

And this unwillingness did not lie in the property, but in feelings.

He even said such bitter words as "abandoning husband and daughter" and "what a cruel heart."

The more Wen Xiu watched him perform, the more disgusted she became. How had she taken a liking to such a person back then?

Therefore, her intent to separate became increasingly firm.

Zhang Jiadi's bitter persuasion failed, and ultimately, under the witness of two Golden Core superiors, he agreed to sign the separation document.

Zhou Shanshan was somewhat unwilling. The property Wen Xiu wanted to divide away was not a small amount. Even if it did not wound the current Zhang family to the bone, that was "her money"!

But with Azure Yang before her, she also did not dare make a scene.

Although in ordinary times, when facing other Golden Core cultivators, even Fairy Heavenjade, she could remain neither servile nor overbearing.

But Azure Yang was different. He was an existence even the Zhou clan ancestor on her maiden family's side greatly admired. At the ancestor's eight-hundredth birthday last time, they had even had someone send Azure Yang an invitation.

After the separation document was signed, and the converted spirit stones were delivered into Wen Xiu's hands.

From this moment on, she and the Cailian Island Zhang family no longer had any connection!

At this moment, night was already deep, and the deeper night dew was heavy.

Lord Lushan refused Zhang Jiadi's attempt to make them stay and took Wen Xiu to return.

However, when they walked out of the small courtyard, several people's steps stopped.

Beneath the moonlight, a graceful young girl stood there, on the verge of tears.

"Mother, do you not want me anymore?"

...

"Is this the cultivation method of you Daoist cultivators?"

"Not quite. Other orthodox Daoist cultivators do not have so many taboos. It is only that this poor Daoist's lineage is rather special."

"It seems a little too focused on form, no?"

"Wen Xiu's realm has not reached that level, so naturally she cannot be informal about small matters. Whether form or deliberate action, at least it cuts off her path of retreat."

"Haha, you truly do your utmost as a master."

"I have made Fellow Daoist laugh."

Beneath the moonlight, atop a white cloud, one man and one woman stood side by side, chatting idly.

Their gazes fell on the mother and daughter leaning against each other outside Cailian Island.

Lord Lushan suddenly said, "In truth, people like us all arrive at the same destination by different roads. Those whose hearts face the Great Dao are destined for solitude! Daoist Azure Yang, after hundreds or thousands of years, how many people will still be able to follow by your side?"

Luo Chen's smile gradually faded.

Qi Refinement cultivators had a lifespan of one hundred years, and even the long-lived were only one hundred and twenty-five.

Foundation Establishment true cultivators had doubled lifespans, but even those proficient in life-nurturing arts could not break through the great limit of three hundred.

As for Golden Core cultivators like them, sitting in death at four or five hundred years old was normal.

To ordinary people, this was already extremely long-lived.

But to those whose hearts faced the Great Dao, they still felt time was insufficient.

During the process of climbing this peak, there would always be people constantly falling behind.

Even if some people could keep pace with him at a certain stage, if one extended the view across time, in the end there would ultimately be only one person, alone.

If one spent all day sleeping in the past, thinking of old friends, one's own cultivation would be affected, and in the end, one would also become one of those who fell behind.

Only by becoming accustomed to solitude could one press forward with perseverance!

What Lord Lushan was doing now was merely helping her disciple Wen Xiu take that step in advance.

What about himself?

Luo Chen pursed his lips, confusion appearing in his eyes.

At some unknown time, Lord Lushan had already left with Wen Xiu.

Zhang Jingqing, who had already grown into a young woman, stood dazed at the edge of Cailian Island. When the moon reached the middle of the sky, she threw herself into the lake and, like a swimming fish, dived toward the depths.

...

"Heavenjade, do you know how old you are?"

Inside the Pill-Artifact Hall, while waiting for the medicinal properties of a spirit pill to settle, Luo Chen asked this question.

Heavenjade, holding the Snow-Blowing Fan [TERM?] to control the furnace fire for Luo Chen, was somewhat surprised.

Then she gave an answer.

"It should be over three hundred years, I think!"

Luo Chen was stunned. "That young?"

Heavenjade smiled faintly. "It is not young. Although demon beasts are all said to be born long-lived, it still depends on what demon race they are. We Fighting Gulls are born fierce and fond of battle, and can be considered a short-lived species among the demon race. As far as I know, third-tier Fighting Gulls can live at most around eight hundred to one thousand years."

"That is already very long, comparable to my human race's Sage Perfected Beings." Luo Chen shook his head, then asked about her family and friends.

Toward this, Heavenjade behaved at a loss.

Their Fighting Gull race was too fond of battle, which made it very difficult to gather and live in groups. Any member with second-tier strength would leave the flock and live alone.

To her, things like family and friends truly gave her no feeling.

At the end.

Heavenjade sighed. "If I had not encountered Master, I would have either died under the hands of other demon beasts, or cultivated muddleheadedly to fourth-tier."

Luo Chen could not help asking, "Have you never considered the meaning of living?"

"Meaning?"

Heavenjade froze, then asked in return, "Is the meaning of living not living?"

Luo Chen was stunned.

Just then, an unusual sound came from within the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Luo Chen struck out mana, and immediately a stream of light flew out like a river of stars.

The improved fourth-tier Poison-Resisting Pill had finally succeeded!

Holding the pill, Luo Chen savored Heavenjade's words. Slowly, the corners of his mouth rose slightly.

The meaning of living was living.

In other words, was the purpose of cultivation not long life?

What was he, a mediocre person, disturbing himself over?

Was this not his aspiration from the very beginning!

As for so-called mortal ties, so-called solitude?

If they did not affect his cultivation, why care about them?

Leave everything to time!

And for now, he should still walk every step steadily and practically!

Holding the Poison-Resisting Pill, Luo Chen asked:

"Where is Duan Li now?"

Heavenjade stood. "He should have ended his mission and returned to the Demon Hunter Office to report completion."

The Demon Hunter Office?

Luo Chen nodded. With a sweep of his great sleeve, he walked outside.

"He owes me a favor. Let him repay it this time!"

*(End of Chapter)*
