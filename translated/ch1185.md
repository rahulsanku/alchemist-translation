# Chapter 1185: Origin of Thunder, Abyss of Lamentation

A martial artist's astral qi initially has no attributes to speak of.

Only upon reaching the Human Immortal realm, giving birth to martial divine intent, can one then incorporate external objects into the body without adverse effects.

This was the mature martial arts system developed over many years in the Great Destruction Realm.

There were also forbidden aspects within it.

For instance, the chosen external object should best match one's own martial divine intent, and after selection, one should not incorporate other things, lest their own astral qi become impure.

Yuan Qingzang did not know what kind of bloody astral qi Luo Chen possessed, but he was very clear about the strength of Luo Chen's bloody astral qi.

It was absolutely not inferior to his own thunder astral qi, and might even surpass it!

An existence like this was already strong enough on its own.

Adding external objects would instead be counterproductive.

He didn't believe Luo Chen didn't understand the principle that greed leads to indigestion.

Moreover, the thunder power in his own Chaotic Thunder Pool was accompanied by dangerous Soul-Shaking Thunder Sounds, which ordinary people simply could not subdue.

Luo Chen's move was overly greedy and arrogant.

Yuan Qingzang intended to let Luo Chen know his limits and back off, so he no longer obstructed him.

"Brother Luo wants to give it a try, then go ahead and try. But be careful not to hurt yourself!"

Luo Chen smiled and stepped forward.

Looking at the thunder pool below with its surging electric light, his eyes gradually narrowed.

This observation lasted for most of the day.

It was so slow that Yuan Qingzang almost thought Luo Chen had lost his nerve, when suddenly he moved.

Raising his hand, five dragons roared forth.

The Chaotic Thunder Pool seemed to sense this provocation. Amidst rumbling thunder, bolts of lightning flowed backward along the five dragon-shaped astral qi streams, counterattacking Luo Chen.

The moment it touched his body, Luo Chen shuddered all over, his expression somewhat twisted.

Seeing this, Yuan Qingzang spoke up: "Brother Luo, know your limits!"

"Thank you for your concern, Fellow Daoist Yuan. I am fine!"

Such a hard mouth?

Yuan Qingzang complained inwardly—Luo Chen was clearly showing signs of great discomfort.

But the next moment, his expression changed drastically.

At some point, with his back to him, Luo Chen's figure began to shimmer with a white light.

On that spine as upright and towering as a mountain, a single vertebra seemed to come alive.

All the thunderbolts that had pounced on Luo Chen actively surged toward that spinal bone, where they were absorbed and devoured.

And this speed was getting faster and faster!

It was as if Luo Chen himself had turned into a bottomless pit, frantically devouring the external thunder.

Gradually, Luo Chen was no longer satisfied.

He actually sat down cross-legged, slowly floating above the Chaotic Thunder Pool, letting the pool below roar and surge.

Looking at the figure in midair that resembled a thunder god, Yuan Qingzang opened his mouth wide, his face full of disbelief.

"Is this Luo Chen also cultivating a thunder-attribute art?"

...

Inside the Azure Dome Tower.

Luo Chen sat motionless above the thunder pool, letting the lightning continuously bombard his body.

But inside his body, unimaginable changes were taking place.

That thunder bone, dormant for many years, had come alive!

Like a sponge, it was gulping down the external thunder power in large mouthfuls.

Under the continuous devouring, it became filled with explosive power.

The excess overflowed, flowing into his body, beginning to temper and grind against the Blood Refining Divine Astral Qi.

Under this grinding, the Blood Refining Divine Astral Qi also became more pure and condensed.

The latter change was within Luo Chen's expectations.

Just as cultivators in the Great Destruction Realm used chaotic energy to cultivate, the martial artists here in the Sky Dome Forest could also use thunder power to train.

Absorbing thunder power into the body not only allowed one to refine the energy contained within but also simultaneously tempered one's own astral qi.

Over time, it formed an effect similar to a cultivation accelerator.

Luo Chen had initially had this idea—to temper his Blood Refining Divine Astral Qi.

But during the continuous absorption process, another change occurred.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron moved!

After sensing the hint of hunger emanating from the Primordial Unity Cauldron, Luo Chen actively activated it.

In an instant!

The Primordial Unity Cauldron also began to operate, absorbing the thunder power that came in through the thunder bone.

This change was exactly the same as when it had absorbed chaotic energy within the Destruction Nine Rings!

Even in Luo Chen's observation, he faintly noticed that on the walls of the Primordial Unity Cauldron, streaks of lightning patterns were slowly taking shape.

"Could it be the origin of thunder?"

At this thought, a new idea arose in Luo Chen's mind.

Not enough!

The thunder power being absorbed was far from enough!

He needed more thunder power!

The next moment, Luo Chen's figure, suspended in midair, suddenly descended, firmly landing into the Chaotic Thunder Pool.

In an instant, electric light surged, and thunder roared!

Yuan Qingzang, watching from the edge of the thunder pool, was almost numb.

Even he himself dared not enter the thunder pool to cultivate, yet this Luo Chen was completely unafraid.

What kind of monster was he?

Beyond his shock, Yuan Qingzang also felt some relief.

Fortunately, in that battle with Luo Chen earlier, he had stopped in time and voluntarily ceased hostilities.

Otherwise, if it had continued, even with the advantage of time and place, and the assistance of the three thousand towers, he would likely have lost to Luo Chen.

Especially the faint, ambiguous smile Luo Chen had revealed when he voluntarily stopped and withdrew the Blood-Colored Great Mill—that image now involuntarily surfaced again in Yuan Qingzang's mind.

"Forget it!"

"Yuan will help others achieve their goals. This fortune is yours."

Yuan Qingzang sighed, then fixed his gaze. Raising his hand, he struck a palm towards the top of the Azure Dome Tower.

Amidst the roar, the entire tower moved.

Brilliant light shone, thunder flashed and lightning crackled.

And above the tower, at that extreme height, the Soul-Shaking Thunder Sound that had been intermittently appearing suddenly became concentrated, wildly striking the Azure Dome Tower with violent lightning.

Such a major commotion could not be ignored by the martial artists of the Sky Dome Forest.

In shock, they flew out one after another, converging on the Azure Dome Tower.

"Could the chief be about to break through to a higher realm?"

But before they could get close, they heard Yuan Qingzang's proactively issued rebuke.

"Everyone, retreat a hundred li from the Azure Dome Tower!"

...

A year later.

All the high-level martial artists of the Sky Dome Forest gathered together to pay homage to Yuan Qingzang.

After an internal meeting, Yuan Qingzang, with three parts reluctance and seven parts relief, drifted away from the Azure Dome Tower. Luo Chen was already waiting outside.

"Let's go!"

"Everything settled?"

"Just some trivial matters of power transfer. At this stage, no one would bother with a mere Sky Dome Forest."

Saying this, Yuan Qingzang looked helplessly at Luo Chen.

"Especially since the Chaotic Thunder Pool has almost been drained dry by you. There's nothing left in the Sky Dome Forest to tempt anyone."

Luo Chen showed a look of guilt.

"Brother Yuan, my apologies."

Luo Chen really hadn't expected this. He had originally planned to use the selfless divine intent characteristic of his Blood Refining Divine Astral Qi to temper his astral qi with the help of the Chaotic Thunder Pool.

But with the empowerment of his thousand-tempered thunder bone, he had instead absorbed a massive amount of thunder power in reverse.

Especially the Primordial Unity Cauldron!

That thing was the real glutton!

Without Yuan Qingzang noticing, it had nearly drained the entire thunder pool dry.

And the result was that a lightning pattern had successfully appeared on the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Luo Chen understood the meaning of that better than anyone.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron had truly condensed the origin of thunder out of thin air!

Luo Chen had also incidentally mastered the Soul-Shaking Divine Thunder.

The only regret was that Luo Chen had merely mastered the Soul-Shaking Divine Thunder; he had not used this to comprehend the true law meaning of the thunder origin.

Even after the Primordial Unity Cauldron had eaten its fill, it had not returned this kind of law power to him.

Luo Chen suspected this was related to the fact that he himself had no thunder dao insights.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron could only reciprocate the true law meaning that Luo Chen himself had already comprehended. If he had no relevant insights, it would not reciprocate.

This was bad news, but also good news.

Luo Chen had previously worried that there were too many strange and bizarre law fragments in the Guixu. If the Primordial Unity Cauldron could absorb them all and feed them back to him after eating its fill, then his dao would inevitably become chaotic and lead him down the wrong path.

Even if he could leave the Guixu later, the moment he emerged, he would suffer a terrifying backlash.

Faced with Luo Chen's apology, Yuan Qingzang just waved his hand.

"Actually, this place has long lost its meaning for me. As I told you before, I was already planning to travel outside. Your actions this time just accelerated my decision."

He turned back to look at the Sky Dome Forest, a trace of reluctance in his eyes.

After all, it was his homeland!

"Actually, you draining the Chaotic Thunder Pool dry is a good thing. The thunder power accumulated there for thousands of years was simply not suitable for martial artists below the Human Immortal realm to cultivate. Now that the thunder pool is nearly dry, but not completely gone, it's just right for my clansmen to cultivate while slowly nurturing a new thunder pool."

With that, he looked curiously at Luo Chen.

"Next, according to your plan, where are you going?"

Luo Chen smiled slightly. "First, I'll take a stroll around the Abyss of Lamentation, then follow the route through the Corpse Remnant Immortal Domain and the Star Sea."

"That's good. Apart from the Corpse Remnant Immortal Domain, this route is considered safe."

Yuan Qingzang had already decided to travel with Luo Chen.

But after analyzing this route, he suddenly reacted.

"You also want to go to the Ancient God Dao Field?"

Luo Chen smiled without speaking.

...

Along the journey, Luo Chen and Yuan Qingzang found more and more common ground.

Yuan Qingzang, who appeared fierce and vicious on the surface, was actually a simple and honest man at heart.

The reason he had fought Luo Chen at the drop of a hat earlier was merely his survival strategy in the Guixu.

As a leader, one could not be too weak.

Faced with someone of unknown motives, fight first.

If you beat them into submission, then talk. If you couldn't, you could still use that move, *Mad Current Annihilation Realm*, to deter the enemy, and then sit down and talk properly.

But for those who truly had ability, Yuan Qingzang often held the highest respect.

Take Luo Chen, for example!

The more they interacted, the more Yuan Qingzang admired Luo Chen's unfathomable and endlessly varied understanding of martial arts.

Many things, the other party would grasp with just a single point.

And some cultivation difficulties that had puzzled him for years, Luo Chen could also offer good advice.

Especially the "selfless" martial divine intent that Luo Chen had actively revealed—this truly stunned Yuan Qingzang.

Using a self-centered heart to harness a selfless intent.

When facing an enemy, he could even transform into various characteristics of martial divine intent.

For instance, Yuan Qingzang's thunder divine intent, Luo Chen had once imitated it, and because he had mastered the Soul-Shaking Divine Thunder, the power he unleashed was not at all inferior to Yuan Qingzang's.

"If that's the case, you really should have come out earlier to travel and experience various different martial divine intents."

After their discussion, Yuan Qingzang made such a remark.

Luo Chen didn't feel much about it.

His Blood Refining Divine Astral Qi was strong enough; he didn't need to imitate those ordinary martial divine intents.

The only ones worthy of his attention were peculiar ones like the thunder divine intent.

Mentioning this, Yuan Qingzang became interested.

"If you want to see peculiar martial divine intents, then the Abyss of Lamentation we're heading to next will definitely not disappoint you."

"Oh? Tell me more!"

...

The Abyss of Lamentation!

An unremarkable settlement of living beings in the Guixu.

The beings living here were not human, but a peculiar existence that was half-human, half-snake.

Their leader was a martial artist at the Human Immortal realm called Old Man Beifeng.

Because the territories of the Sky Dome Tower and the Abyss of Lamentation were too close, the two groups often had friction.

The two leaders had also fought a few times.

But neither had gained the upper hand!

Because Old Man Beifeng's realm was similar to Yuan Qingzang's, and their specific strength was also similar.

The outcome of a battle was determined by the environment.

If near the three thousand towers, Yuan Qingzang could easily defeat Old Man Beifeng.

But if near the Abyss of Lamentation, then Yuan Qingzang would have to spread his wings and flee.

Luo Chen had roughly guessed what Old Man Beifeng relied on.

It was nothing more than a martial dao divine ability similar to Yuan Qingzang's *Mad Current Annihilation Realm*, which could guide external forces to attack the enemy.

And this kind of divine ability was often slow to activate. With his *Heaven-Earth Inverse Reincarnation* in hand, he was not afraid at all.

Luo Chen was just curious to see the "Yuan Magnetic Divine Intent" that Yuan Qingzang said was unique to Old Man Beifeng.

A month later.

Two figures stopped above a plain.

Luo Chen's blood-colored wings behind him fluttered, his eyes sparkling with sharp light as he looked down.

When he saw the cracks on the earth that looked like mouths, a look of understanding appeared on his face.

"No wonder you said the Abyss of Lamentation is unremarkable, and even a passing Human Immortal martial artist would easily overlook it."

The people here didn't live on the surface, but underground!

Yuan Qingzang laughed. "Yes, this area contains a large number of mineral deposits, including plenty of lightning-absorbing stones. As you can see, every lightning tower in my Sky Dome Forest requires a large amount of lightning-absorbing stones to build. Our two sides have had quite a few frictions and conflicts over this."

As he spoke, Yuan Qingzang no longer hid his aura.

Opening his mouth, he roared, a thunderous sound exploding across the wilderness, straight into the underground abyss.

"Old Man Beifeng, an old acquaintance has come calling. Why not come out and meet me?"

The rumbling sound spread across the four fields, causing the earth to tremble.

Even black qi surged out from the abyss.

Then, figures emerged one after another, exactly the abyss dwellers in their half-human, half-snake form!

In a short time, over ten thousand abyss dwellers had appeared on the earth, swaying their snake tails and flicking their tongues as they coldly stared at the two in the sky.

But after seeing the two figures standing in the air and their powerful auras, these abyss dwellers couldn't help but stir restlessly.

Luo Chen watched this scene and nodded inwardly.

The clan's strength was not weak.

Among the over ten thousand abyss dweller warriors, there were as many as twenty or thirty martial artists at the Leakless realm.

Although not comparable to the Great Destruction realm, it was still considerable.

Most importantly, deep underground, a powerful aura was rapidly rising.

"Is that Old Man Beifeng?" Luo Chen frowned, looking puzzledly at Yuan Qingzang.

The other party was also quite puzzled.

Old Man Beifeng's aura shouldn't be this "weak."

Although it had the aura of a Human Immortal, it was at most at the early stage, and even the realm was somewhat unstable.

The next moment, a figure shot into the sky, relying on the ten thousand clan warriors to coldly face Luo and Yuan.

"Two gentlemen, my clan's ancestor has already traveled far. I'm afraid I cannot entertain you."

The tone was icy cold, without the slightest courtesy. (Chapter End)
