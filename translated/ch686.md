# Chapter 686: Cleansing Qi and Scouring the Soul, Cape Heaven Matron

The void swayed, and his vision twisted.

Unable to tell whether it was a change in the outside world or an illusion in his own perception, Luo Chen was transported from within the stone house to a new realm.

The second trial!

Before Luo Chen had even steadied himself, he subconsciously sent his spiritual sense into his storage ring.

Those precious bamboo slips could not be taken away, but what about the excess Spirit-Root Pills and other pills he had personally refined?

Under the perception of his spiritual sense, Luo Chen let out a breath of relief.

"Good. They are all still there!"

These three kinds of pills were not high in grade, only first and second tier.

But the auxiliary materials needed to refine them were extremely difficult to find in the Mountains and Seas Realm.

Several among them were things Luo Chen had never even heard of before, only recognizing them through the bamboo slips within the Pill Realm.

In other words,

Even after leaving Demon-Fall Land, it would be difficult for him to reproduce and refine these pills in the outside world unless he spent enormous effort to improve the formulas.

While feeling fortunate, Luo Chen also felt all the more regretful.

"If these pills could spread through the Mountains and Seas Realm, perhaps I could change the structure of this world!"

The Mountains and Seas Realm was dominated by immortal cultivators.

But the true majority in number was the countless, immeasurable mass of mortals.

In the Eastern Wilderness, these mortals were raised by great sects as logistics, helping collect basic materials and providing seedlings for immortal cultivation. In the Northern Sea their utilization was somewhat higher, but they were still only expendable labor assisting forces large and small with miscellaneous tasks.

But if this vast population of mortals could use the Spirit-Root Pill to cultivate, even if only to early or middle Qi Refinement, it would bring earth-shaking changes to the cultivation world.

At that time, everyone could cultivate, everyone would be like a dragon. Such a prospect...

"I am probably thinking too much. Even if it could truly be done, there would not be such abundant resources to supply their cultivation."

Luo Chen sighed and turned his attention to the new realm where he had just steadied himself.

"So this is the second trial?"

He walked forward slowly, his gaze falling on the strange flowers and unusual grasses spread out beneath him.

Like the first trial, all of them were medicinal materials.

The difference was that the spiritual qi contained in the medicinal materials below was far stronger than in the first trial.

By his estimate, their overall age had risen one level from five hundred years or below. They had not reached a thousand years, but the outstanding ones among them were around seven or eight hundred years old.

Higher-grade medicinal materials meant that the spirit pills refined from them would also be higher in grade.

This was within Luo Chen's expectations.

It was normal for the second trial to be a little more difficult than the first, was it not?

Aside from his eyes, Luo Chen's spiritual sense continuously spread outward, as though trying to find someone.

In the end,

When he arrived at a stone house similar to the one in the first trial, his spiritual sense had still not discovered a second person besides himself.

Facing this result, Luo Chen did not know whether he should feel relieved or worried.

Relieved, naturally, because he had not encountered his competitor, avoiding a possible battle.

Worried, clearly because the other party was very likely walking ahead of him. Not only had they passed the first trial, they might even have already crossed the second.

Counting the days,

It had been roughly four years since Luo Chen entered Demon-Fall Land.

He had spent two years in Oblivion Heaven and Five Elements Heaven, then another two years refining pills in the Pill Realm of Bright Radiance Heaven.

But in reality, Demon-Fall Land had already been open for five years!

Luo Chen had delayed a year before setting foot in this place.

This also meant that the advance entrant had spent at least two years, and at most four or five years, in the inheritance land.

To break through two trials in such a short time showed that the other party's alchemy attainments were extraordinary!

In that case, Luo Chen had to consider one matter: would the other party pass all assessments before him and obtain Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's alchemy inheritance in advance?

Facing this possibility, an invisible urgency hung over his heart.

"Forget it. I should first look at the examination question!"

Creak...

The red-robed Daoist pushed open the two stone doors. A stone path and the three rooms of pill, book, and herb came into view.

The same arrangement meant that the subsequent assessments would largely follow the same pattern, differing only in the question.

...

[Humans are the spirit of all things, and demons are the true essence of heaven and earth.

Yet humans and demons walk different paths. Brief lifespans grant the human race clever intelligence, while long years leave the demon race dull and clumsy. At the same realm, the demon race is absolutely no match for the human race.

In my view, the gap between them is not merely in the use of external objects. The greater problem lies in the difference between their cultivation paths.

We alchemists... refine corresponding pills to break the divergence between human and demon.]

At the same redwood desk, Luo Chen held the jade slip and slowly read the question left upon it.

Only after finishing, his face was full of doubt.

In his view, the gap between humans and demons truly did lie in the use of external objects!

When Qi Refinement cultivators fought first-tier demon beasts, Initiates fought second-tier demon beasts, and even Golden Core cultivators faced third-tier Demon Kings, the reason humans could occupy the advantage was because they had assistance from external objects such as law devices, magic treasures, formations, and talismans.

How was he supposed to refine a specific pill to smooth over that gap?

Should they not be teaching low-tier demons how to skillfully use external objects instead?

Confusion covered his face.

But at least there was one point that Luo Chen faintly grasped.

In the first trial, Devil Lord Heaven-Refining wanted him to open the divide between immortal and mortal, aimed at the human race.

In the second trial, the so-called divergence between human and demon placed the emphasis on the demon race.

According to what Tai Sui had said... assuming Tai Sui had not deceived him, Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's origin was very peculiar. One could say he was neither human, nor demon, nor devil, yet one could also say all three were complete in him.

Because the other party was a "mixed blood" who simultaneously possessed the bloodlines of human, demon, and devil.

Saying it this way might be somewhat disrespectful.

But that was the truth.

If so, then since the first two trials focused respectively on humans and demons, the third trial was very likely to focus on the devil race.

In that case, a problem appeared.

Luo Chen was very familiar with the human race and the demon race.

But the devil race...

"I am thinking too far. Better to focus on the present!"

Taking a deep breath, Luo Chen picked up the jade slip again and repeatedly savored and pondered it.

How could one smooth over the gap in combat strength between humans and demons of the same realm?

Only by first grasping the essence of the problem could he prescribe the proper medicine and refine a targeted pill.

...

Time slowly flowed. Several days later, Luo Chen faintly worked out some clues.

The so-called difference between humans and demons did not actually lie in the aptitude of the body, but in wisdom and soul.

Humans were born with short lifespans.

In order to seek longevity, they had no choice but to rack their brains and study the Great Dao.

The use of external objects was essentially an extension of wisdom.

The demon race was different. Most demon beasts were born with long years ahead of them, which left them without such urgent cultivation needs.

Therefore, the demon race's cultivation path was, from the very beginning, not aimed at the divine soul, but at the accumulation of power itself.

It was precisely because of the difference in starting point that the subsequent cultivation paths of humans and demons gradually diverged.

Although humans refined qi, they placed greater weight on the divine soul. When they reached higher realms, body and mana were both less important than the divine soul. Han Zhan was an example: even if his body had been destroyed, he could still persist in the world.

But the demon race relied on enormous bodies, accumulating terrifying spiritual qi within themselves. They could even condense demon cores at second tier, one step earlier than humans.

One must know that humans could only condense Golden Cores at the Golden Core stage.

If power were compared to work, then the demon race was actually the one that, from the beginning, used tools by instinct.

"My first intuitive feeling before was wrong."

"Because of their innate bodily advantage, the demon race instinctively relies on external objects, meaning power, from the start, and neglects tempering the essence of the divine soul. This makes them slow at the first step, and slow at every step thereafter."

"Power needs divine soul wisdom to control it. If one has vast demon power but cannot command it freely, and cannot even rely on tools to multiply its output, then of course one cannot defeat a same-tier human."

"If I want to change this problem, strengthening the divine soul will suffice."

After sorting this point clearly, Luo Chen gradually drew a thread out of the chaos.

That he could "break the question" so quickly benefited from Luo Chen's long dealings with Black King and Heavenjade.

Especially Heavenjade. Luo Chen had personally inspected her body and tailored a cultivation method for her.

It could be said that among the human immortal cultivators of the present world, if one discussed the degree of understanding of demon beasts, Luo Chen could absolutely be counted among the foremost.

Now that he had broken the question, solving it became simple.

He truly knew how to refine pills that strengthened the divine soul. He even had some on hand!

Spiritual light flashed in his hand, and a second-tier supreme-grade Netherpassage Pill appeared.

Luo Chen observed the surroundings.

There was no change.

"Hm?"

"Could it be that the assessment result can only be sensed inside the Pill Room?"

Hesitating, Luo Chen came to the Pill Room.

But even after he took out the Netherpassage Pill, there was still no change.

The weak voice with its unconcealable frailty did not appear.

"What does this mean?" Luo Chen's expression sank.

Surely the answer he brought with him did not count?

The Netherpassage Pill was not something only immortal cultivators could use. He had once given it to Heavenjade as well, and it had indeed been effective!

Feeling vexed, Luo Chen returned to the study again.

His gaze swept over the bookshelves, paused, and finally returned to the jade slip on the desk.

His spiritual sense entered it, and the examination question echoed by his ear once more.

"Could it be that I went off topic?"

...

Unable to find the answer, Luo Chen pondered bitterly for another day, then chose to start from the beginning.

What did starting from the beginning mean?

Naturally, it meant reading the bamboo slips on the bookshelves within the study.

Compared with the first trial, there were far fewer bamboo slips in this second trial.

Not ten thousand scrolls, only a thousand or so.

Moreover, their contents no longer recorded only medicinal material information. What surprised and delighted Luo Chen was that he discovered several unique alchemy arts among them.

Yes, alchemy arts!

They were like Luo Chen's earliest Pure Heart Hand and Jade-Breaking Hand.

They differed from pill-refining methods. An art was a minor Dao, focusing more on the handling of medicinal materials.

Every kind of alchemy art targeted different medicinal materials.

For instance, Pure Heart Hand excelled at processing wood-attribute medicinal materials. Certain materials that could not touch the ground or be stained by dust had to use this art.

Jade-Breaking Hand and Star-Plucking Hand excelled at processing earth-attribute and mineral-type medicinal materials. Some pills that used jade as material required them.

Luo Chen had learned many basic alchemy arts in his early years, but the further he went, the fewer basic alchemy arts he obtained.

The last time he had learned a new alchemy art had been when Mu Yunyan of the Falling Cloud Sect imparted Burning Crane Hand to him during an exchange.

But this time, the several alchemy arts Luo Chen obtained made him quite pleasantly surprised.

Qi-Control Art, specifically used to process all kinds of "spiritual qi type" medicinal materials.

What were spiritual qi type medicinal materials?

Some pills did not rely only on the medicinal materials themselves. Before refinement was complete, they also required large amounts of special spiritual qi to be introduced.

But the spiritual qi of the world was mixed together, and there were few places as singular and pure as Five Elements Heaven.

Unless one used something like a supreme-grade spirit stone, which contained a large amount of a single type of spiritual qi.

Clearly, no alchemist would be willing to use supreme-grade spirit stones to refine pills.

Under such circumstances, one could only guide spiritual qi from the outside world into the pill, relying on formations to filter and screen it, bringing in one kind of spiritual qi alone.

But even the best formation would have leaks.

It was like when cultivators cultivated. Even a single-attribute heavenly spirit root would passively absorb other attributes of spiritual qi into the body during cultivation. This had advantages and disadvantages: the advantage was that a single spirit root cultivator could cast spells of other attributes, while the disadvantage was that it affected cultivation speed.

Returning to the topic, Qi-Control Art was clearly an alchemy art that could accurately distinguish the spiritual qi of heaven and earth and harvest it into medicine.

If he could proficiently master this art, it would be a qualitative leap for Luo Chen's alchemy.

It would even provide considerable benefit to cultivation.

Aside from Qi-Control Art, there was also the Light-Gathering Method, Formless Hand, and other alchemy arts that Luo Chen found profound and unfathomable at a glance.

According to the bamboo slips, the Light-Gathering Method could gather all kinds of natural light into medicine. It was deeply mysterious.

As for Formless Hand, it was said to command the void without form, refining illusory, incorporeal existences into medicine. As for what counted as an illusory, incorporeal existence?

The bamboo slips briefly listed several kinds: the seven emotions and six desires, resentment, hatred, parting, and so on.

These descriptions truly fascinated Luo Chen and broadened his horizons.

Even emotions could be treated as medicinal materials and used to refine pills?

Of course, such unimaginable alchemy arts also had extremely high cultivation requirements. Luo Chen could not learn them in a short time, and could only firmly memorize them.

It was during this process of reading widely that Luo Chen gradually gained a new understanding of the question Devil Lord Heaven-Refining had left behind.

On this day,

Before the desk, Luo Chen sat silently for a long time, then slowly lifted his brush.

He wrote down the names of medicinal materials one after another.

"Earlier, I fell into a misunderstanding, thinking only that to resolve the difference between human and demon, strengthening the divine soul would suffice."

"In reality, because of long lifespans and the accumulation of years, the foundation of a demon beast's divine soul is completely no weaker than a same-tier human expert's."

"Their weakness lies not in quantity, but in quality!"

"That vast divine soul becomes turbid and unbearable because of the vicissitudes of years. Even if it is large, it is still not as refined as the human divine soul."

"Therefore, to solve this problem, the key is not strengthening the soul, but purifying it."

"According to the records on the study's bamboo slips, Soul-Scouring Grass has the effect of cleansing the divine soul and clearing the mind. Using it as the main material, perhaps I can refine a corresponding pill for scouring the divine soul. The auxiliary materials should be Redcloud Flower, Rotten Ghost Vine... If this pill succeeds, perhaps I can also benefit somewhat."

"Scouring the soul alone is still insufficient. Demon cultivation methods have always been rough, and the reason they leave demon cultivators with vast divine soul foundations yet fragile control also lies in the impurity of their mana. So I must also refine demon cultivator magic power. In this regard, I have the Spirit-Cleansing Pill commonly used by immortal cultivators as a blueprint, and can create a formula specially for the demon race to purify demon power."

...

"Cleansing qi and scouring soul. These two pills are the answer I give for this assessment!"

One year later.

Inside the Pill Room, Luo Chen held one black and one white pill and recited in a clear voice.

This time, that weak voice appeared again, faint and leisurely.

"Qi-Cleansing Pill. Pill formed: third-tier lower-grade. It can help demon cultivators purify mana."

"Soul-Scouring Pill. Pill formed: third-tier lower-grade. It can help demon cultivators clear the mind, stabilize nature, and scour the divine soul."

"Assessment result: superior-high. Passed!"

Luo Chen, already confident, smiled slightly and tightened his hand around those two pills.

The scene before him began twisting and collapsing.

Between illusion and reality, his figure gradually appeared on a mountain peak.

Below the mountain, an old woman carrying a herb basket was diligently gathering spirit fruits.

In an instant, two gazes collided.

Luo Chen cupped his hands, looking down from above. "Devil Lord Azure Yang greets Fellow Daoist!"

The old woman slowly straightened her back. "This old body, Cape Heaven Matron, greets Fellow Daoist!"

*(End of Chapter)*
