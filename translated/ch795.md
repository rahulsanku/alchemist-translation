# Chapter 795: Nine Spirits Enter Sagehood, Nine Separations Form Nascent Soul

*Rustle, rustle, rustle…*

Mountain wind swept through the hall, stirring the red paper talismans so they fluttered and snapped.

In the great hall where Luo Chen was drawing talismans, sheets of talismans were affixed to wooden racks. Now, stirred by the wind, the terrifying power contained within them made Qu Jiu’s heart uneasy.

He couldn’t help but look at those talismans—over a hundred of them, all painstakingly drawn by Grandmaster Luo Chen over these past days.

Most of them were Blazing Sun Talismans!

Fluttering like this, they resembled a blazing sun about to fall. If they all exploded at once, even an Adept cultivator would likely be blown to smithereens!

“Little Jiu, your heart is not at peace.”

A gentle voice came from beside him. Qu Jiu looked over and saw that Luo Chen was still focused on drawing talismans, not even looking at him.

He clenched his jaw, forcing an ugly smile.

“My mental cultivation is insufficient.”

Luo Chen still didn’t look at him, continuing to draw talismans, but he spoke calmly: “I know your life experience has been tortuous, and even now you still resent your father. This has made your temperament extreme, rarely finding peace. This kind of disposition is not conducive to cultivation, nor to talisman drawing, and it has also hindered you from being able to draw second-grade talismans for so long.”

Qu Jiu fell silent.

The path of talisman drawing was not as simple as it seemed. It had thresholds and required talent.

And the most important talent was a unified mind and spirit, a mentality that remained unchanged even if a thousand mountains collapsed before you.

Without this talent for controlling emotions, it was easy for one’s mind to waver when drawing talismans. At the very least, the talisman patterns would go astray, wasting all previous effort. At worst, the mana output would be improper, causing the talisman to activate on the spot and backfire on oneself.

Previously, he had only been slightly interested in the talisman path, but had never delved into practice.

In recent days, under Luo Chen’s guidance, his talisman-drawing technique had advanced by leaps and bounds, yet he remained stuck at second-grade talismans.

The main reason was his poor mental cultivation!

“Have you studied the *Fine Dust Primordial Art*?”

“Not yet. That art has extremely high requirements for the divine soul. Even with Elder Wang’s improvements, one still needs at least the late Initiate stage to barely cultivate it.”

“In that case, I will teach you the *Settling Wind and Waves*. Practice it first, learn how to cut off distracting thoughts and not be moved by external things. Later, when your realm improves, you can seamlessly transition to the *Fine Dust Primordial Art*!”

Qu Jiu was stunned. Grandmaster was passing down a secret technique to him?

Just then, a mountain-visiting sound came rumbling from outside the hall.

“Nine Spirit Primordial Lord requests an audience with the Alchemy Sect!”

In that moment, Qu Jiu’s expression changed drastically.

The Nine Spirit Primordial Lord was quite famous throughout the Lingtian Five Regions, and even the entire Eastern Wilderness.

With his mid-Sage cultivation, he was already considered deeply accomplished in the Dao. But what was even more remarkable was his extraordinarily profound mana!

There were rumors that Nine Spirit Primordial Lord’s mana was not inferior even to late-stage Great Cultivator Sages.

For this reason alone, Nine Spirit Primordial Lord stood above many Sage cultivators. Even within the current Lingtian Five Regions, only the Lingtian City Lord and the secluded Divine Origin Sage could suppress him.

Other mid-Sage cultivators like Fenghua Fairy and Golden Feather Sage were not as profound in mana as Nine Spirit Primordial Lord.

Back when the Nine Spirit Domain was attacked by the demon race, three Demon Emperors had joined forces to besiege him, yet still failed to keep him there—a testament to his ability!

For such a powerful figure to visit Grandmaster at this time, so openly—one could even say “arrogantly”—what did he intend?

Qu Jiu couldn’t help but look worriedly at Luo Chen.

But the white-robed Daoist remained unmoved, still focused on drawing talismans.

Only when the talisman patterns were inscribed to the final stroke did he slowly raise his eyes.

“Hoo…”

He gently blew a breath, looking with satisfaction at the blue-and-red talisman in his hand.

A third-grade Azure Yang Talisman!

Within it was inscribed his most proud signature spell from his Adept period—the Azure Yang Great Handprint!

After over a thousand attempts at drawing low-grade talismans, he could finally easily draw third-grade talismans.

At this point, he was a true third-grade talisman master!

He casually tossed the Azure Yang Talisman in front of Qu Jiu.

The terrifying mana aura that had not yet fully settled within the talisman startled Qu Jiu into a fluster, afraid he might accidentally activate it.

“This one is for you.”

“As for all the other first and second-grade talismans, go find the Sect Master. Have him send someone to inventory them and store them in the treasury for wartime use.”

“This jade slip contains the *Settling Wind and Waves*, along with my cultivation insights. Take it and study it well.”

After giving these instructions, Luo Chen patted his already spotless white robe and walked out of the great hall with a smile on his face.

…

Under the watchful eyes of many, both overt and covert, Nine Spirit Primordial Lord stood outside the gates of the Luo Tian Sect, waiting quietly.

Time passed, minute by minute.

In the shadows, no one knew how much criticism was brewing.

That Alchemy Sect Luo Chen had quite the airs about him!

How formidable was Nine Spirit Primordial Lord? He had voluntarily set aside his pride and announced his visit, yet he was kept waiting for so long!

These onlookers were even anticipating the scene of Nine Spirit Primordial Lord leaving in anger.

Unfortunately, after about half an incense stick’s worth of time, movement finally came from the Great Green Mountain.

“Is it not a joy to have friends come from afar!”

“I have kept you waiting, Fellow Daoist.”

“Please!”

Wind and clouds converged, forming a rainbow bridge.

Spanning a hundred li, it landed at the feet of the arriving guest.

With this single display, Luo Chen revealed his profound mastery of spell techniques.

Nine Spirit Primordial Lord looked down at the rainbow bridge beneath his feet, his gaze sharpening. Then he smiled and stepped onto the bridge, entering the mountain.

At this, countless disciples within the Luo Tian Sect who had been craning their necks returned to normal—dust to dust, earth to earth, no more clamor.

Instead, it was the onlookers outside the mountain who exchanged glances.

Did the Alchemy Sect Luo Chen truly possess such ability?

He had kept the renowned Nine Spirit Primordial Lord waiting for a good while, and the other party still willingly entered the mountain?

For a moment, everyone couldn’t help but re-evaluate Luo Chen’s current status in the Eastern Wilderness.

Though he had returned in haste, though he was only at the early Sage stage, though he lacked deep foundations… his status was already no less than that of an established powerhouse like Nine Spirit Primordial Lord.

…

“Fellow Daoist Luo, we meet again.”

“Half a year apart, and you are still as impressive as ever, Fellow Daoist Nine Spirit. I have been waiting for you.”

“Oh, you knew I would come back to find you?”

“Heh, at the grand ceremony back then, aside from Elder Fu and Fellow Daoist Lingfengzi, you were the one most concerned about my alchemy skills. The only thing that surprised me was that you came before Lingfengzi.”

“You are highly perceptive, Fellow Daoist! I do have a request—I would like you to refine a furnace of pills for me.”

“Easy enough. As long as the price is right, I have always been accommodating!”

Having received Luo Chen’s verbal agreement, Nine Spirit Primordial Lord finally breathed a sigh of relief.

His earlier willingness to set aside face and “humbly” visit the mountain in front of everyone had yielded some return.

He understood Luo Chen’s intentions very well!

Luo Chen simply wanted to use the matter of refining pills for others to build his reputation and secure a place in the Eastern Wilderness.

And he, Nine Spirit Primordial Lord, also had needs in this regard.

So, he would play along, using his own long-accumulated reputation to elevate the other’s status.

Even if the other party deliberately kept him waiting for a short while, he didn’t mind—as long as the other could truly refine what he needed!

He wanted substance; Luo Chen wanted face. That was all.

“Fellow Daoist Luo, the price is negotiable. I can give you twenty strands of Red Firmament Qi, ten strands of Crimson Firmament Qi, and ten strands of Purple Firmament Qi. I also have some of the other six types of firmament qi. If you want them, we can discuss it.”

Luo Chen was somewhat surprised.

The other party actually had this much firmament qi on hand?

What’s more, he even had reserves of the other six types of firmament qi?

Most importantly, this person seemed to understand the uses of all nine types of firmament qi. When Luo Chen had only asked for Red Firmament Qi, the other now proactively offered Crimson Firmament Qi and Purple Firmament Qi.

According to the sword scripture, Luo Chen could indeed refine all three types of qi simultaneously!

The reason was that Crimson Firmament Qi and Purple Firmament Qi also had partial fire attributes.

Nine Spirit Primordial Lord clearly knew this, which was why he specifically mentioned it.

Luo Chen calculated. If he obtained a total of forty strands of the three types of qi from Nine Spirit Primordial Lord, plus the ten strands he already had, that would be halfway to the hundred strands needed for the intermediate stage of the *Thousand Firmaments Myriad Transformations Sword Scripture*.

This deal could be made!

“The price is acceptable. I can take on the commission. But the specifics will depend on what kind of pill you want refined. If it requires too much effort, I will have to raise the price.”

Without further hesitation, he directly asked about the other’s needs.

Seeing Luo Chen agree, Nine Spirit Primordial Lord suddenly became hesitant instead.

“I want to refine a fourth-grade pill called the Nine Separations Pill. Can you do it?”

Luo Chen was exasperated. “Knowing only its name, not its substance, is too vague. Even if I had the ability of a Grandmaster, I couldn’t give you an answer like that!”

Nine Spirit Primordial Lord thought for a moment, then solemnly took out an object.

Luo Chen looked closely. It was a bronze statue.

The statue was somewhat strange. Its form was that of a person sitting in meditation, but on its eyes, ears, mouth, navel, and the two scrotal sacs below, there was a tumor growing on each.

Nine in total.

The statue suddenly turned over, revealing its bronze back.

“The specific pill formula for the Nine Separations Pill is recorded here. This pill formula is obscure and difficult to understand, and due to the special nature of this treasure, it cannot be copied, so you can only examine it in person.”

Nine Spirit Primordial Lord said this.

Luo Chen made an “oh” sound, no longer paying attention to those tumors, but focusing on the pill formula on the bronze back.

As he looked, his brow immediately furrowed.

Seeing his reaction, Nine Spirit Primordial Lord grew worried.

“Fellow Daoist Luo…”

“It’s nothing. Just some uncommon medicinal names.”

Luo Chen smiled, giving the other a reassuring expression.

But in his heart, he couldn’t help muttering.

“These medicinal names… they follow the naming conventions of the demon race!”

Ever since obtaining the alchemy inheritance of Devil Lord Heaven-Refining, Luo Chen would occasionally flip through those alchemy books, memorizing many medicinal ingredients he had never heard of before.

Devil Lord Heaven-Refining had extensive knowledge, even labeling some demon-realm spiritual medicines that were the same as human ones with two different names.

After this perusal, he understood about seventy or eighty percent of it.

Time passed, minute by minute. Luo Chen continuously memorized the pill formula above, while pondering the refining taboos within.

It wasn’t until the middle of the night that he came to his senses.

Throughout this process, Nine Spirit Primordial Lord had not left his side, closely observing his reactions.

“Fellow Daoist Luo, can it be refined?”

Luo Chen first nodded, causing the other to be overjoyed.

Then he shook his head violently, leaving the other stunned.

“What do you mean? I don’t understand.”

Luo Chen pointed at the pill formula and said, “It can be refined, but there are a few problems.”

“Speak freely!”

“The ingredients. Do you have the ingredients listed here? I don’t have any spares.”

So that was it!

Nine Spirit Primordial Lord breathed a sigh of relief. “The ingredients on the pill formula are indeed very obscure. Even my Nine Spirit Sect couldn’t collect them all. However, successive generations of sect predecessors have continuously explored and found corresponding substitutes. I have barely managed to gather a few sets here.”

“Oh?” Luo Chen raised an eyebrow. “Let me have a look.”

Nine Spirit Primordial Lord immediately handed Luo Chen a storage pouch.

Taking it, Luo Chen probed it with his divine sense, occasionally taking out an object, examining it while secretly nodding.

“Using Thorn Sunflower to replace the demon realm’s Cultivation-Free Seed—the medicinal properties are quite similar.”

“Snow Pine Emerald to replace Blood Jade Great Pan? There’s such a substitution method? Obviously… ah, it takes the similar medicinal properties of the pan jade. The predecessors of the Nine Spirit Sect do have some skill.”

“And this Silver Soul Stone—how is it to be substituted? Is it a combination of these two ingredients?”

…

After an inventory, Luo Chen’s mind was somewhat hazy, but he generally understood that the ingredients Nine Spirit Primordial Lord had brought out did indeed have some chance of refining the Nine Separations Pill recorded in the formula.

If he were to refine it, after studying the formula for half a year and then conducting dozens or hundreds of detailed experiments, he should be able to produce it.

But that would be both time-consuming and labor-intensive, hardly befitting the illustrious reputation of the “Alchemy Sect.”

Luo Chen wasn’t worried, though. It was, after all, a fourth-grade pill formula, within his capabilities.

Spending ten achievement points to directly master it would save even more time and effort.

By dawn, Luo Chen asked the second question.

“This pill is related to your cultivation, isn’t it?”

Nine Spirit Primordial Lord knew he couldn’t hide it from Luo Chen, because the pill formula clearly stated that it required the corresponding Sage’s essence blood from the person taking the pill.

He frankly nodded in acknowledgment.

Luo Chen asked again, “Then do you know from which part of the Sage’s body the essence blood should be taken?”

Nine Spirit Primordial Lord was stunned, a look of confusion on his face.

Luo Chen wore an expression of “I knew it.” The content of the Nine Separations Pill formula was too obscure; ordinary cultivators simply couldn’t understand it.

Even a normal human fourth-grade alchemist might not grasp its meaning at first glance.

Only he, having received Devil Lord Heaven-Refining’s alchemy inheritance that spanned the human, demon, and monster races, could quickly comprehend it.

Luo Chen pointed at the bronze human statue. “The essence blood must be taken from the nine tumors on the Sage. Fellow Daoist Nine Spirit, do you have these nine tumors on your Sage?”

At these words, the middle-aged man before him reacted like a cat whose fur had been ruffled. His expression changed instantly.

A majestic aura surged forth like ocean waves.

*Boom!*

Luo Chen, facing him, felt his hair fly and his white robe rustle loudly.

He knew that this was because he had bluntly spoken of the other’s special cultivation method, which counted as touching a taboo, hence such a strong reaction.

But Luo Chen’s expression did not change. He only said softly:

“I refine pills for all fellow Daoists. It is very normal for me to know some secrets of various families. I will not spread them around. If you do not want to refine this pill, if you do not trust me, then let it end here!”

Nine Spirit Primordial Lord took a deep look at Luo Chen, then slowly withdrew his overwhelming aura.

“Nine Spirit has never trusted others easily, but this pill involves a major technique of mine, and it is also related to my path to advancing to the late Sage stage. So, I can only trust you this once.”

“Alchemy Sect, do not disappoint me.”

As he spoke, he slowly closed his eyes.

Luo Chen grinned. This fellow’s mana was unexpectedly profound!

According to rumors, his realm was only at the fifth layer of the Sage stage, similar to Golden Feather, but the depth of his mana was almost comparable to the Moon Wanderer back then.

He remembered that the Moon Wanderer had secretly concealed his realm, actually having just entered the late Sage stage.

That is to say, Nine Spirit Primordial Lord’s mana was almost as deep as a late-stage Sage.

Luo Chen, who prided himself on his vast mana, even if Golden Feather Sage was two minor realms higher than him, he was confident he could contend with him.

But the aura Nine Spirit Primordial Lord had just released had completely overwhelmed him.

“I wonder how he cultivated it?”

As he was pondering this, a deformed monster suddenly emerged from the top of Nine Spirit Primordial Lord’s head.

It was an infant.

A cultivator’s Sage.

But now, on the infant’s eyes, ears, mouth, navel, and the two scrotal sacs below, there was a fleshy tumor growing on each.

Looking at it as a whole, it was truly horrifying.

The infant stared at Luo Chen and slowly spoke.

“I have cultivated the Nine Spirits Enter Sagehood method of our sect’s founding ancestor, absorbing the spiritual essence of the nine firmaments into my body, obtaining boundless mana.”

“I need this Nine Separations Pill to help me separate the nine essences and form nine spiritual infants.”

“Alchemy Sect, if you want to take the essence blood, do it now.”

(The chapter ends)
