# Chapter 1047: Suppressing the Sea's Flowing Waves, the Sea-Patrolling Yaksha

**Sea-Suppressing City!**

A war fortress stood at the estuary on the coast of the Eastern Sea, built specifically to resist the invasion of coastal demon beasts.

It was not constructed relying on elegant and winding mountain terrain, but was poured from the coldest ten-thousand-jun black-sinking rock, combined with the lava from a perpetually erupting volcano somewhere on the Tiannan Continent.

The city wall was like an ancient dragon's spine, stretching for hundreds of miles without visible beginning or end, forcibly snatching a foothold from the ferocious sea!

The wall was so high that to look up at its peak, one nearly broke their neck. Upon it were arrayed rows of Heavenly Origin Spirit Energy Cannons, their maws dark and oppressive, as if they could destroy everything.

Below were nine enormous sluice gates, named the Sea-Suppressing Sluices. Each one was dozens of zhang thick, covered in crude and ferocious barbs and the dangerous light of formations. These Sea-Suppressing Sluices were layered one upon another, not only completely protecting Sea-Suppressing City but also blocking large numbers of low-grade sea demons from rushing upstream directly from the estuary into the interior.

Zhou Fuyou, having drunk his fill the night before, walked through the various checkpoints of Sea-Suppressing City, watching the Sea-Suppressing Sluices rise and fall to let them pass, his heart filled with shock.

Beside him, Second Senior Brother Fu Bin saw this and couldn't help but laugh. "Little Junior Brother seems quite astonished?"

Having grown accustomed to the inland's grand cities, mainly elegant, ancient, and majestic in style, now facing the ferocious Sea-Suppressing City, which resembled a war beast, Zhou Fuyou indeed found it hard to hide his shock.

He praised, "Truly a mighty city! Completely different from the elegant style of our Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!"

"Then Little Junior Brother, you are mistaken." Fu Bin laughed heartily, then said with feeling, "Three thousand years ago, our Heavenly Origin Dao Sect was the only Ascendant sacred ground on the five continents to have completely purged demon beasts. Three thousand years later, our Heavenly Origin Dao Sect has even launched campaigns to conquer the other four continents and unify the Mountains and Seas Realm. When it comes to martial spirit and iron-bloodedness, who in the world can compare to our Heavenly Origin Dao Sect?"

Each "our Heavenly Origin Dao Sect" was filled with pride and reverence.

Not only Zhou Fuyou, but even the group of Initiate true cultivators beside him were also infected.

"Magnificent is our Heavenly Origin!"

They too were disciples of the Dao Sect!

When another sluice gate let them pass, Zhou Fuyou flew out, skimming low along the river channel.

He asked curiously, "Early this morning, Sage Wen left in a hurry, and Second Senior Brother, you urgently summoned us to the battlefield. Has something happened?"

Fu Bin's smile faded, and his expression turned serious.

"Indeed, something has happened. It is said that a great battle erupted in the sea last night, of a very high level, definitely at the Sage level."

At these words, Zhou Fuyou and the group of Initiate true cultivators also changed color.

"A fourth-order Demon Emperor joined the battle?"

At the peak of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, demon beasts were almost extinct in the seas near the Central Continent; there was hardly any demon calamity to speak of.

But after the Central Continent Catastrophe, when the continent shattered into several pieces, demon beasts that had lain low for many years began to appear again one after another.

The first few decades were not so bad, mostly first and second-order low-grade demon beasts.

But in recent years, a large number of third-order Demon Kings had surfaced one after another.

And now, even a fourth-order Demon Emperor had joined the battle!

How could they not be stunned?

Fu Bin said solemnly, "That should be the case, otherwise Sage Wen would not have left so hastily. He is stationed at Sea-Suppressing City. Although there are other fortress cities along the coast, Sea-Suppressing City is definitely the most important and cannot be lost. Therefore, he must investigate the specific situation."

"Then what about us?"

"Don't worry. Demon Emperor-level experts will naturally be dealt with by Sage true men. Our purpose is to seal off other places besides the estuary and protect some formations from being damaged."

Fu Bin briefly explained, and everyone was slightly reassured.

*Rumble!*

Another Sea-Suppressing Sluice opened, and the elites from the Four Imperial Halls of the Tiannan Dao Palace took the opportunity to fly through.

Fu Bin's gaze swept behind him, then he said to Zhou Fuyou, "Little Junior Brother, I have already assigned your team. After we leave the city, you will lead them to patrol the eight-hundred-li radius of Liubo Mountain."

Zhou Fuyou had previously looked at the coastal map and roughly knew where Liubo Mountain was.

So, after confirming his position, he asked about the specific mission.

"Just the twenty of us?"

The Four Imperial Halls had dispatched a total of four hundred Initiate true cultivators and twenty Adept superiors this time, each Adept superior leading a team, divided into twenty large squads. Within each large squad, they were also divided into five small teams of four people each.

That added up to a hundred small teams.

Fu Bin shook his head. "Liubo Mountain is so vast, of course it can't rely on just you twenty. Normally, there are cultivators from various great families stationed there; they are the real main force. You are the mobile force, to go and support wherever an emergency arises."

Zhou Fuyou understood.

This kind of arrangement was relatively freer and better at honing the independent action abilities of the Dao Palace geniuses, far more effective than rigid, fixed missions.

"Alright, the ninth sluice is about to open, which also means we are completely out of Sea-Suppressing City. From now on, while you slay demons and earn merits, don't forget to protect yourselves."

After giving this instruction, Fu Bin accelerated and rushed to the very front.

When the ninth sluice gate, which was like a mountain, opened, the Dao Palace geniuses flew in all directions like shooting stars.

Zhou Fuyou looked back at the ninth sluice. Unlike the previous ancient and solid gates, this one was covered in slimy mucus and fragments, reeking of blood. Clearly, these were the flesh and blood left by countless low-grade demon beasts that had charged Sea-Suppressing City.

His gaze gradually fell upon the hundred Initiate true cultivators following him.

Among them were a few acquaintances, or rather, relatives.

Zhou Yuwei, Zhou Yuhao, Zhou Yuqi, and his cousin Lin Qingtong.

They were all disciples who had successfully built their foundations over the years and had also taken refuge under the Houtu Hall.

Second Senior Brother had assigned them to his team, also to make it convenient for him to look after them.

Facing Zhou Fuyou's gaze, the crowd stirred slightly.

Zhou Fuyou's lips trembled slightly, and he uttered two words.

"Let's go!"

...

Liubo Mountain, a hundred li from the sea.

The mountain was tall and steep, with clouds gathering. Whenever the sea breeze blew, large amounts of mist rolled and flowed like waves.

Hence the name Liubo Mountain!

Zhou Fuyou's arrival was met with a grand reception by the leader stationed at this mountain.

A full six Adept superiors came together to welcome him. This showed respect, and also demonstrated the status of the Tiannan Dao Palace geniuses.

The leader was a late-stage Adept grand cultivator named Jidu, from a humble family.

After grandly receiving Zhou Fuyou, he began to introduce the specific situation of Liubo Mountain.

The terrain was vast, eight hundred li in radius, and steep.

Although it was close to the sea, it was a good place that was easy to defend and hard to attack.

A total of six Adept superiors and over ten thousand low-grade cultivators guarded it together.

While listening to his introduction, Zhou Fuyou observed the various details of Liubo Mountain.

He noticed that the cultivators here wore different clothes with different emblem markings.

"It seems, just like my Zhou family back then, these are cultivators sent by various great families of the Tiannan Continent to carry out sea-patrol missions."

Zhou Fuyou thought of his parents, who had also carried out such missions and then unfortunately fallen.

Suddenly!

Zhou Fuyou's steps stopped at the foot of the mountain. There, a formation light screen was covering an area.

"This is?"

Superior Jidu spread his hands. "I don't know. Sea-Suppressing City also told us not to interfere with these, only to guard Liubo Mountain well."

Zhou Fuyou frowned. "Since it is the place we are guarding, how can there be an existence of unknown origin?"

He reached out and touched the light screen.

But what his hand encountered was extremely tough, difficult to penetrate.

Zhou Fuyou snorted lightly. His mana suddenly changed, and a wisp of black qi coiled around his fingertip.

*Chi chi chi...*

Superior Jidu's expression changed slightly. He, a dignified late-stage Adept grand cultivator, actually felt a hint of danger from this brat before him.

What was that black qi?

Just as Zhou Fuyou was about to break through the great formation, the light screen suddenly split open a hole large enough for one person to pass through, and then a figure walked out.

With just one look, he glared fiercely at the two of them.

"Haven't you received the order from Sea-Suppressing City? Do not interfere with the local formations, just guard well!"

Jidu seemed to recognize the newcomer and quickly said, "Senior Brother, my apologies. Fellow Daoist Zhou has just arrived for mobile support; he is not yet aware of these matters."

Zhou Fuyou raised an eyebrow. He remembered that before leaving Sea-Suppressing City, Second Senior Brother had briefly mentioned guarding some local formations.

So these were them?

He took a step back and withdrew his mana.

"Junior Brother Zhou Fuyou, greetings, Senior Brother!"

The man's eyes lit up. "Is it Zhou Fuyou of the Tiannan Dao Palace?"

"Indeed!"

"So it's Junior Brother Zhou. I am Lian Yuanshan. Let's exchange ideas when we have time. As for today's matter, ignorance is not a crime. Just be more careful in the future."

After a brief greeting, Lian Yuanshan retreated back into the formation.

The scene fell silent for a moment, with only the formation light screen slowly emitting spiritual energy still present.

"Let's go," Jidu said quietly beside him, as if afraid of disturbing the people inside.

Zhou Fuyou hummed in acknowledgment and followed behind.

After they had distanced themselves a bit, he voiced the doubt in his heart.

"Superior Jidu, your cultivation level is much higher than that Senior Brother Lian Yuanshan's, so why do you address him with such respect?"

Superior Jidu said helplessly, "He is a true disciple of the Dao Sect from the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range, not comparable to us ordinary family cultivators who nominally belong to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect but have never actually entered Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain."

Zhou Fuyou was startled, then immediately understood.

No wonder the man's attitude changed after confirming that he was from the Tiannan Dao Palace.

The Tiannan Dao Palace was also part of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, and the disciples of the Four Halls of the Dao Palace were naturally Dao Sect disciples as well. Moreover, for a genius like himself, there was a high chance of entering the Dao Sect's ancestral court in the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range in the future and becoming a true Dao Sect true disciple!

That "Junior Brother Zhou" was sincere, without a trace of falsehood!

Superior Jidu changed the subject. "Besides, didn't you see his family emblem?"

Zhou Fuyou recalled what he had glimpsed earlier. The emblem was just a few wavy lines strung together, which inexplicably held some Dao resonance.

This family crest...

Zhou Fuyou immediately understood.

On the former Central Continent, there were thousands of large and small families, each with different family emblem markings.

No one would deliberately memorize all these family crests.

Except for twelve family crests, which were recorded in the classics, and everyone would incidentally remember them.

Because those twelve family crests represented the twelve giant families that stood high above and had always dominated the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

The Twelve Great Houses!

And the family crest with the connected lines from just now was one of them.

Zhou Fuyou blurted out, "Lian Yuanshan, the Lian family!"

Superior Jidu spread his hands. "Yes, it is the Lian family, one of the Twelve Great Houses, and also the original family of our Dao Sect's Liancheng Ancestor!"

No wonder Superior Jidu was so respectful and humble towards someone whose cultivation level was far inferior to his own.

Zhou Fuyou looked back at the place where the formation had been.

His divine sense, with unyielding force, drilled down from above into the depths of Liubo Mountain.

Within the earth veins, formation patterns lay spread out like ravines.

The entire Liubo Mountain sat upon them, and those formation patterns even showed signs of expanding outward.

A thoughtful look appeared in Zhou Fuyou's eyes. "What we are to guard is probably not Liubo Mountain, but these secretly laid formations!"

...

Over the vast sea, the dark clouds gradually dispersed.

A burly figure, holding a azure trident, stood on the sea surface. Looking at the dispersing clouds, he roared angrily, as if venting his frustration.

The surrounding sea was stimulated, exploding like a boiling tsunami.

He glared hatefully to his left and right, then calmed down and returned to his underwater palace.

At that moment, the clam maidens were still singing, and the mermaids danced joyfully.

In the great hall, that black dragon was still cheerfully drinking Dry Blue Ice Wine, enjoying the beautiful songs and dances.

"Saibo, how did it go? Did you catch that fellow who was spying on you?"

Saibo put away his trident and snorted coldly. "Failed at the last moment."

The black dragon raised its head, its whiskers swaying, clearly very surprised.

"You are of the Sea-Patrolling Yaksha clan, in the great sea your mana is inexhaustible. Even with you personally making a move, you couldn't capture the enemy. Is that fellow that powerful?"

Saibo's face was filled with anger. "That fellow is also skilled in controlling water, and moreover, it's an evil ghost, extremely slippery."

The black dragon was stunned. "An evil ghost?"

"Yes!" Saibo nodded. "Its original form was a great python, but after I broke it, I discovered it was condensed from pure ghost qi. It is actually a fourth-order Ghost Emperor. I didn't notice at first, and it relied on the special nature of a ghostly body to be formless and form, barely dodging my strongest strike."

A great python?

A Ghost Emperor?

A trace of confusion flickered in the black dragon's eyes.

"Most importantly, that old fellow from Sea-Suppressing City came out, forcing me to stop pursuing that evil ghost."

Then, Saibo slammed the table.

"We can't wait any longer. Since that old fellow from Sea-Suppressing City knows we are here, his defenses will definitely be tighter from now on. We have to act early!"

"Fine!" the black dragon said nonchalantly. "Since my master sent me here to contribute, just give the orders!"

Saibo took a deep breath. "Sea-Suppressing City, on the surface, seems tightly defended. But in reality, there is only one Nascent Soul Sage. On our side, there are three Demon Emperors, plus you, taking it down is no problem. Originally, I wanted to wait for a few more helpers to charge directly into the inland, but now we have to launch early. Later, I will contact the other two Dao friends and attack Sea-Suppressing City from three directions. You will sneak over from the Liubo Mountain direction, use your desolate beast body to smash through the nine sluice gates, so that my clan's children can charge into Sea-Suppressing City and slaughter freely!"

Finally, he looked at the black dragon.

"No problem?"

The black dragon split its bloody maw into a grin. "Don't worry, a small matter!"

...

The dark clouds in the sky gathered and dispersed, dispersed and gathered.

Amidst the lightning and thunder, a faint light descended with the electric light onto a small island.

The faint light transformed into a form, revealing Xiaobai's graceful figure.

She spat out a mouthful of turbid qi, a look of lingering fear on her face.

"A mid-fourth-order Demon Emperor, and the most troublesome Sea-Patrolling Yaksha at that. I almost fell for it."

While feeling fortunate, Xiaobai recalled the situation she had detected earlier, her face hesitant.

But in a moment, she showed a determined expression.

"Absolutely no mistake. That greedy drunkard is Black King!"

With this thought, Xiaobai immediately took out a jade token and sent a detailed voice transmission into it.

The first sentence was:

"Master, Black King has appeared!"

(End of this chapter)
