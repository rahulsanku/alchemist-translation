# Chapter 997: Sage Taiyue, Forcing Entry into the Nether Abyss

Shock and anger lasted only an instant before Luo Chen's expression returned to normal.

Through that brief contact, he had already discovered that the green enslavement seal bore some resemblance to the enslavement seals he himself produced.

Both aimed to plant the seed of servitude deep within, without affecting Di Tu's cultivation and growth.

In this way, it would neither hinder Di Tu's cultivation progress, nor would it prevent the eventual reaping of a peak Great Demon Emperor, or even a fifth-stage ancient demon, in the future.

And to be able to forcibly traverse the Golden Wind Astral Qi and arrive here was no feat an ordinary Sage could accomplish.

Luo Chen's gaze swept the surroundings before finally settling once more on Di Tu's brow.

A leaf-shaped enslavement seal flickered faintly in and out of sight.

"The methods of an Ascendant-level powerhouse!"

"If my guess is correct, this must have been done by Ancestor Chunyuan, the great power the Tianyuan Dao Sect sent to oversee the Eastern Wilderness back in the day."

"Now that Chunyuan is dead, perhaps I can break the seal he left behind!"

Luo Chen did not know the extent of Rosycloud's injuries from the battle at Severed Soul Cliff, but the Tianyuan Dao Sect's losses were appalling to behold.

Of the Five Elders of Tianyuan, four had fallen, leaving only the youngest, Liancheng, to survive by sheer luck.

Among them, Ancestor Chunyuan had also failed to walk out of Severed Soul Cliff alive.

Since the man was already gone, the methods he left behind naturally could not remain at their peak.

Especially since this enslavement seal was not particularly coercive to begin with.

Holding a sliver of anticipation, Luo Chen's eyes blazed with golden light. While staring fixedly at Di Tu, his divine sense slowly extended outward, gradually sinking into the leaf-shaped enslavement seal.

Removing seal restrictions was Luo Chen's specialty.

Over the past three hundred years, he had unlocked residual seals on countless storage rings, magical treasures, and artifacts.

Thus, he had developed a unique set of personal insights—both fast and accurate.

As he analyzed and peeled away layer by layer, his understanding of Ancestor Chunyuan's methods grew deeper. He even detected a strand of law power from the Wood Origin within it.

This power was silently influencing Di Tu's soul.

The day Di Tu achieved his goal would also be the day he was completely enslaved.

"What a fine 'moistening things silently and without sound'!"

Three days passed in the blink of an eye.

Luo Chen closed his sore, swollen eyes and gently pressed his fingers against his temples. Confidence had already taken root in his heart.

For now, he could not break this enslavement seal.

Not because Ancestor Chunyuan's methods were particularly brilliant, but because the soul-restriction that constituted the seal was too solid!

Even if only a wisp of soul power remained, its extreme condensation was enough to withstand the repeated battering of Luo Chen's vast divine sense.

And if he mishandled it, he might even startle Di Tu awake, interrupting the process of absorbing his father Di Tian's Gold Origin power.

"Time!"

Luo Chen quickly arrived at the best method for breaking it.

Chunyuan was dead. The enslavement seal he left behind was gradually weakening with time. Luo Chen need do nothing, and the seal would dissipate on its own.

Of course, that would also allow Di Tu to regain his freedom.

What Luo Chen needed to do was to wait for the right moment—just as the seal was about to fade but had not yet fully dispersed—and imprint his own enslavement seal over it.

He cast one last deep look at the slumbering golden giant tiger, then turned and left.

...

When Luo Chen returned to the flying boat, Hao Ranzi and Jadewater Dragon, who had been worrying for three days, finally breathed a sigh of relief.

Three short days with no news at all—they had truly feared something had happened to Luo Chen.

Although they had now achieved Sagehood, the longer they remained at this realm, the more they understood the vast gap between Sages.

They were still in a weakened state. Without Luo Chen's protection, whether now or in the future, their path would be exceedingly difficult.

Thus, the two of them were the ones who least wanted anything to happen to Luo Chen.

Seeing Luo Chen return looking somewhat fatigued, they wanted to ask but ultimately kept silent out of tact.

If he didn't want to say, they dared not pry.

The flying boat skirted around the Blue Sky Domain and set sail once more.

Luo Chen closed his eyes, sinking his mind into his sea of consciousness.

His massive main soul sat at the center.

Eight powerful divided souls surrounded it in a circle.

Through his clash with the soul-seal left by Ancestor Chunyuan, he had gained another insight.

He had recognized the gap between himself and Ascendant-level powerhouses in terms of soul cultivation.

Not in quantity, but in quality!

The journey to Severed Soul Cliff could be considered the second greatest opportunity of Luo Chen's life, second only to the Demon-Fall Land in the Northern Sea.

In terms of tangible gains, the explosive expansion of his soul foundation in such a short time gave an even more striking impression than the continuous, far-reaching effects of the Demon-Fall Land's bounty.

Luo Chen's soul foundation, after years of nurturing, had long surpassed that of his peers.

After absorbing a large number of soul stones at Severed Soul Cliff, it had expanded more than tenfold!

At one point, Luo Chen believed his soul foundation could rival that of an ordinary Ascendant.

But from the looks of it now, he still fell slightly short.

The gap lay in quality.

"Is it the difference between a Sage's Nascent Soul and an Ascendant's Primordial Spirit?"

Luo Chen murmured to himself, his thoughts drifting back to not long ago.

In the Western Desert, he had briefly clashed with Dharma Master Xuan Du before his breakthrough.

That was a collision on the level of divine sense!

At that time, although Xuan Du's soul foundation was far inferior to Luo Chen's, the clash had ended in a draw.

Xuan Du could not overcome Luo Chen, and Luo Chen could not shake Xuan Du.

And Xuan Du's reliance was precisely his prematurely condensed Primordial Spirit.

"A soul is ethereal and cannot be relied upon; it merely resides in the sea of consciousness as a shelter. But once it fully merges with the Nascent Soul, it is equivalent to having the most perfect parasitic vessel."

"If that's the case, dealing with the Tianyuan Dao Sect won't be so easy."

The Tianyuan Dao Sect still had an Ascendant powerhouse guarding it!

And Luo Chen's strength, as tested by the Thirty-Six Arhat Formation of the Hanging Temple, was also very clear.

He still fell short of the might of an Ascendant.

Thus, a head-on confrontation with the Tianyuan Dao Sect was absolutely out of the question!

"I'll have to think of a long-term plan—a foolproof way to retrieve my life soul without alerting the great power Liancheng."

Luo Chen pursed his lips, silently contemplating.

...

The flying boat sailed slowly, passing over Lingtian and crossing Dieyun. Along the way, it passed by Fenghua Palace, Tiandu Sect, and many other forces friendly to Luo Chen, but did not stop.

It only paused briefly when returning to the Nether Abyss and when considering a detour to Mount Tai Sect.

But in the end, Luo Chen still did not go to Mount Tai Sect.

He had promised Deng Taiyue of Mount Tai Sect to refine the Enlightenment Pill for him, but his own matters were more pressing.

As for the Enlightenment Pill's formula, after years of research—especially with the reference alchemy books provided by Mr. Xuanyan—he had pieced together a rough version. If he had enough medicinal ingredients for experiments, with the confirmation of his attribute panel, he might be able to truly restore it.

But that would take time!

And time was exactly what Luo Chen lacked right now, so he was reluctant to divert his attention.

Thus, the flying boat did not change course. After flying unhurriedly for a year, it finally returned to the Nether Abyss.

However, though he wished to avoid it, others had been waiting for this matter for a long time.

On the shore of the pitch-black Netherwater, a steady middle-aged Daoist looked in surprise at the direction of the Nether Abyss, then turned back in astonishment to look at the three people on the flying boat.

He took a deep breath and cupped his hands in salute: "Alchemy Sovereign, it has been a long time!"

Luo Chen put away his equally surprised expression and returned the salute: "Sage Taiyue, how have you been lately?"

Deng Taiyue gave a bitter smile. "I have not been well, Fellow Daoist Deng. If I had waited any longer for you, I was planning to force my way into this Nether Abyss, which is said to be impassable for living beings."

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow, wondering how the other party had determined that the Luo Tian Sect was hidden within the Nether Abyss.

It must have something to do with the techniques he specialized in!

He adjusted his mood slightly and asked abruptly, "Brother Deng, is this about the Enlightenment Pill?"

Deng Taiyue nodded eagerly. "Exactly! Over the years, I have gathered a large number of medicinal ingredients related to the Enlightenment Pill, waiting for you to collect them. But you have made no move. And I have suffered enough from being trapped at the sixth layer of Sagehood—I truly cannot sit still any longer."

Deng Taiyue was actually quite old, and he had become famous early on.

But in his early years, because he missed the opportunity to comprehend the true meaning of the laws, he became trapped by it, and his cultivation level stagnated.

Even Nine Spirit Primogenitor had surpassed him—how could he endure it?

After news came from the Central Continent about Luo Chen's battle with Demon Emperor Azure Frost, he learned how active Luo Chen had been.

Using his unique secret technique, he tracked Luo Chen to the shore of the Nether Abyss, but could not enter and had to wait in vain.

He had not expected to wait for a Luo Tian Sect cultivator to come out from within, but instead for Luo Chen to return from outside.

What a coincidence!

Luo Chen's mind turned, and he nodded slightly. "I have made some progress on the Enlightenment Pill. However, some essential materials are left inside the sect. Brother Deng, if you don't mind, why not accompany me to the Luo Tian Sect as a guest?"

Deng Taiyue's eyes lit up, and his face shone with brilliance. "Since Brother Luo invites me, it is my long-cherished wish!"

Luo Chen nodded and immediately stopped by the shore. While chatting with Deng Taiyue, he waited for the Xuan Turtle's guidance.

During this time, he also introduced Hao Ranzi and Jadewater Dragon to Deng Taiyue.

However, it was clear that Deng Taiyue was not very interested in these two. He only gave them a polite response out of respect for Luo Chen.

Hao Ranzi and Jadewater Dragon naturally harbored no resentment. Such existences were peerless experts they could only look up to in the past. Even now, at the same great realm, they could not foolishly hope for equal treatment.

But they gained an increasingly clear understanding of Luo Chen's current influence.

Those who came and went were all top experts of the Eastern Wilderness, and their comings and goings were always to sacred grounds or forbidden lands.

Gone was the cautious, timid Dan Chenzi of yesteryear. In his place was the Alchemy Sovereign's bearing—one who shook the Eastern Wilderness and whose name weighed heavily on the age!

If they could attach themselves to such an existence, even if their foundations were damaged, there might still be a sliver of a chance to continue pursuing the Great Dao in the future.

...

"Shangyou Immortal City actually still has its foundation. It's a waste for it to be occupied by scattered cultivators now. With a little tidying up, it might not be impossible to use it as a foundation for a major sect. I don't know what blessed land your sect has within the Nether Abyss as a refuge, but for sect development, being too isolated from the world is not conducive to inheritance. If Brother Luo is interested, my Mount Tai Sect can lend a hand and help you relocate to Shangyou Immortal City. Of course, returning to the Luo Tian Domain is also acceptable."

"This place is quite close to the Dongyang Sect. If the Luo Tian Sect occupies it, the Dongyang Sect will surely have thoughts about it, won't they?"

"Haha, with Brother Luo's strength, what need is there to fear the mere Dongyang Sect?"

"That is true. I, Luo, would also very much like to meet the great elder of the Dongyang Sect."

As the two chatted and laughed, they carried an air of treating the heroes of the Eastern Wilderness as nothing.

The current Eastern Wilderness, after a series of great battles, was utterly hollow.

If the Luo Tian Sect wished to emerge into the world, there were plenty of territories to choose from.

Whoever dared to block them would have to weigh the gap in strength between themselves and Luo Chen!

Amidst their conversation, Luo Chen's brow furrowed slightly.

"Why has there been no response for so long?"

He had a deep connection with that Xuan Turtle. After sending a secret communication, a response should come within a short time at most, or a few days at worst.

But three days had passed since he sent the signal, and there was still no reaction.

Could something have happened?

Deng Taiyue saw that Luo Chen was waiting for someone to receive him and offered words of comfort: "I've heard that the Nether Abyss is filled with countless yin spirits and ghostly beings. Perhaps your sect members have been delayed!"

Luo Chen shook his head. "If it were before, I would believe that. But now, it shouldn't be like this!"

Deng Taiyue did not understand.

But Luo Chen was quite confident in his judgment.

Because back when Rosycloud attacked the Central Continent, aside from the four great desolate beasts, she also commanded nine Ghost Emperors.

Ghost Emperors were fifth-stage ghostly beings.

Even above Ghost Kings and Ghost Royals.

Such a large number of fifth-stage ghostly beings could not have appeared out of thin air.

Thus, the source of those nine Ghost Emperors could only be one place.

The Nether Abyss!

Before his death, Ancestor Tianyuan had undone the restrictions, releasing countless ghostly beings that had been suppressed beneath the Nether Abyss for thousands of years, attempting to use them to besiege Rosycloud.

But unexpectedly, Rosycloud paid a great price to avoid a mutually destructive outcome.

Not only that, but she returned years later, using the Myriad Beast Diagram handed over by Moon Wanderer as a foundation to collect the powerful ghostly beings within the Nether Abyss.

With this as leverage, she forcefully attacked the Tianyuan Dao Sect.

Therefore, although the Nether Abyss still appeared terrifying, its danger had actually been reduced to a minimum.

With the Xuan Turtle roaming the abyss, and Little White's Ghost Emperor body as a guide, contacting Luo Chen should have been simple.

For there to be no response for so long, there was only one explanation.

Something had happened to the Luo Tian Sect!

Taking a deep breath, Luo Chen leaped into the air.

Seeing this, Deng Taiyue exclaimed in surprise, "Brother Luo, what are you doing?"

"Crossing the Nether Abyss to return to the Luo Tian Sect." Luo Chen looked down and spoke thus.

Deng Taiyue was horrified. "How can you be so reckless? The Nether Abyss is filled with Nirvana Sacred Flame, and above the former Nether Abyss site, there are unpredictable spatial rifts. We are merely Sages—how can we cross it?"

He did not mention sneaking through the abyss, as that was even more unreliable.

The ubiquitous Netherwater would seep into a cultivator's mana, gradually polluting it. If even a little was brought back into the body, it could corrode the Purple Mansion and harm the Nascent Soul itself.

Faced with this dissuasion, Luo Chen narrowed his eyes and gazed into the boundless Nether Abyss.

"My mind is made up. Brother Deng, the two of you wait for me here. After I have dealt with matters within the sect, I will send someone to fetch you."

With that, he glanced at Hao Ranzi and Jadewater Dragon.

The two had no objections. Whether as the Lord of Truth Crossing Hall or under the title of fellow disciple, they were bound to obey his words.

Deng Taiyue thought for a moment, then gritted his teeth and leaped up to follow.

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow. "Brother Deng, what is this?"

Having made his decision, Deng Taiyue instead relaxed. He smiled and said, "The Enlightenment Pill I have dreamed of is right before my eyes. If something happens to you, where would I find a second Alchemy Sovereign? Naturally, I must follow you and lend whatever strength I can."

Seeing the other's determined expression, Luo Chen was speechless, then nodded.

"Then follow me!"

With that, he transformed into a streak of escaping light and charged into the Nether Abyss.

Deng Taiyue followed closely behind, a thick layer of profound yellow light tightly protecting him.

"Brother Luo Chen, be safe!"

Behind them, Hao Ranzi called out a worried farewell, then exchanged glances with Jadewater Dragon, who had also remained behind.

"What do we do now?"

"Waiting here is useless—just wasting time." Jadewater Dragon's eyes rolled cunningly, and a sudden idea came to her. "Why not take a trip to Shangyou Immortal City?"
