# Chapter 702: Han Zhan Refines the Treasure, the Devil Lord's Blood

What did it mean for something to be hard to explain in a few words?

When matters were winding and complex, and could not be made clear in a single sentence, that was what it meant.

Han Zhan did not understand.

As he saw it, the mana fluctuations Luo Chen revealed outwardly were no longer at Adept Stage Six, as when he had first entered the Demon-Fall Land, but at Adept Stage Eight!

As for what lay within, Luo Chen's terrifying physique, which could forcibly withstand attacks from a true-artifact-grade sword pellet, had undoubtedly made tremendous progress compared to when he left Peng Lake.

Combining the two, one could see what wondrous fortune Luo Chen had obtained after the two of them separated in Bright Radiance Heaven.

And that was not even mentioning the inheritance gains the other man had obtained from the Pill Palace.

No matter how one looked at it, this was a good thing!

In the silence, Luo Chen also did not know how to explain it to Han Zhan for a time, or perhaps, subconsciously, he did not want to speak of those matters.

Because that involved his immortal-path lifebound cultivation method, the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture.

At this moment, two entries were gleaming brightly on his attribute panel.

[Realm: Adept Stage Eight 99/100, Archaic Tier Four 35/100]

Two simple entries, yet they implied many details.

The explosive rise in his qi cultivation realm undoubtedly came from that mysterious spring water. After soaking in it for more than a year, Luo Chen's realm had silently risen to a point only one step away from Adept Stage Nine.

That gain had saved him at least several decades of bitter cultivation!

To an ordinary person, this would already have been an enormous fortune.

But to Luo Chen, the change in his body cultivation realm was what concerned him most.

From just having entered Archaic Tier Four, without any tempering at all, directly from 1 to 35. What sort of concept was that?

Put another way, this was no less than an early-stage Sage cultivator instantly climbing from Sage Stage One to Sage Stage Three... no, Stage Four!

That progress bar of thirty-five had already exceeded one-third of the whole. This was also the decisive reason Luo Chen had been able to press down and beat early-stage Sage Feiyunzi once he seized the opportunity back then.

Such earth-shaking change clearly originated from that "nirvana"!

Misfortune is where fortune rests!

Because of the struggle between the Nine-Refinement Beacon Fire and the Nirvana Sacred Flame, his second Origin Core had been destroyed, and the tremendous power had almost dragged him into an abyss beyond redemption.

Luo Chen seized that opportunity and circulated the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture, forcefully beginning a physical nirvana.

With his lifebound Golden Core, the terrifying life essence of Tai Sui's true body, and that wisp of source Nirvana Sacred Flame, he had completed, by the slimmest margin, three tribulations and one nirvana among the Nine Tribulations and Three Nirvanas.

Blood tribulation, flesh tribulation, bone tribulation, physical nirvana!

This sequence of actions had not only let Luo Chen escape danger and reshape a complete physique, it had also allowed his physique to instantly cross the early period of Archaic Tier Four and reach the middle-period level.

Not only that, it had even transformed his constitution into a fire spirit body, making it even more suited to the cultivation of fire-type arts.

However!

Fortune is where misfortune lurks!

Compared to these gains, the things Luo Chen had lost made his heart ache even more.

The Five-Spirit Dao Body that could cultivate all arts together!

The foundation from which, after advancing to the Sage realm, a second Nascent Soul might have been born: the second Origin Core!

One could even say that Tai Sui's true body, which contained boundless vitality, had been so crudely consumed and refined alive that for Luo Chen, a fourth-tier alchemist, this too was an enormous loss!

If used properly, that one Tai Sui true body alone could have let him refine who knew how many divine pills and spirit medicines.

Even if it were used solely for body cultivation, Luo Chen could, through alchemy and supplemented by other medicinal materials, bring out its medicinal nature severalfold, tenfold, or even a hundredfold, enough to supply his body cultivation needs for the entire Archaic Tier Four stage.

Eating it directly was simply a reckless waste of heavenly treasure!

In short, Luo Chen summarized the changes to his current body in eight words: "gain in the present, loss in the future."

"Forget it. Now is not the time to think about these things."

Luo Chen shook his head hard, as if flinging the thousand tangled threads in his mind away.

There were many things he needed to do now, but the most important was actually only one!

Mana surged at his fingertip, and a faint light emerged from his storage ring.

"Senior, can you refine this object for me?"

Han Zhan looked over. The object was only a square cun in size and could be held in one palm.

Its flying eaves stood high, its eight corners were exquisite, and its windows and doors were lifelike, just like a miniature wood carving that could rest in one's hand.

It was precisely the Penglai Immortal Sect's inherited true artifact, the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion!

The heavy treasure Luo Chen had forcibly seized from the hands of the Myriad Immortals Assembly and Penglai Immortal Sect powerhouses back in Wood Heaven Plain.

It could gather in spirit medicines and let them grow freely, earning it the reputation of a portable spirit medicine garden.

Han Zhan looked at Luo Chen in surprise. "Are you in such a hurry? This treasure has all five elements, along with the three variant powers of wind, thunder, and ice. After you form your Nascent Soul, you can refine it yourself. Why must I do it for you?"

Luo Chen shook his head. "I cannot wait that long. I need to be able to use this object within a year, no, within half a year. The sooner the better!"

Han Zhan did not understand.

But seeing Luo Chen so urgent, his thoughts stirred, and he roughly guessed the other man's intentions.

"You are worried about Moon Wanderer?"

Luo Chen grunted in acknowledgment, his face filled with solemnity.

"That woman has an extremely strong desire for control, not only over people, but also over things. Although I do not know what that gray light is, the fact that she would rather abandon Fairy Jadecold to forcibly take it shows how determined she was to obtain it!"

"That gray light is now... in short, Moon Wanderer will not let me go."

"So I must stay far away from her and give her no chance for revenge."

"Once she returns to the Myriad Immortals Assembly, there is a great chance she will mobilize the strength of its countless wandering cultivators to search for me. She may even use those on friendly terms with me to discover Purple Spirit Island's location!"

"The Purple Monkey Flowers Sang Jiugong cultivated for me on Purple Spirit Island concern my chance to form a Nascent Soul. I absolutely cannot abandon them."

"Therefore, before I leave, I must take the Purple Monkey Flowers there with me!"

Han Zhan suddenly understood.

But immediately afterward, he had another doubt.

"If you want to take the Purple Monkey Flowers, then take them. You do not necessarily need to use the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion!"

Han Zhan had not personally participated in that journey into the Demon Sea.

In addition, after returning, Luo Chen had not said much, only briefly mentioning that Mountain Lord Hundred Craft had set an ambush to trap and kill him.

Luo Chen patiently explained, "Most of the Purple Monkey Flowers on Purple Spirit Island are not mature, and have not reached the basic standard for refining Nascent Soul Pills. By my estimate, they still need at least ten or twenty years to mature. If they are picked midway, all previous effort will be wasted."

At this, Han Zhan thoroughly understood.

This portable spirit medicine garden, the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion, had the effect of transplanting all kinds of spirit plants and medicinal materials together with their roots and soil, without damaging the medicinal materials' spirituality.

Luo Chen intended to use the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion to take away all the Purple Monkey Flowers on Purple Spirit Island, in case Moon Wanderer found a chance to chase him there.

Thinking of the strength Moon Wanderer had displayed in Bright Radiance Heaven at the time, Han Zhan's heart also chilled.

Even after he had used the late-stage Sage white tiger puppet he gained in the Puppet Realm and prepared an attack in advance, he had still failed to injure her, which showed just how powerful Moon Wanderer was.

For Luo Chen to have provoked such an enemy was truly an endless future calamity!

"Fine!"

Han Zhan beckoned, and that miniature pavilion fell into his hand.

His divine sense probed inside, carefully observing while analyzing the possibility of refining it.

"It is indeed a true artifact, without question. It can communicate with the spirit qi of the inner and outer worlds."

"However, this treasure should not have been refined by anyone for over a thousand years. There are quite a few divine-sense restrictions left on it, but they do not belong solely to one person. They belong to many people."

"According to what Moya Cave Master said, this treasure ranks among the Penglai Immortal Sect's inherited true artifacts and had dedicated personnel maintaining it, but it did not recognize any single master. The Penglai Immortal Sect has been passed down for countless years, and because it has wandered year-round in the depths of the Northern Sea wilderness, it often encounters naturally grown spirit medicines. Thus, it frequently dispatches disciples and sect members to hold the Octagonal Pavilion and transplant spirit medicines. For that reason, they did not specially set up powerful restrictions, making it convenient for sect members to use."

"But there is one point. I have discovered several mana auras at the core formation area, sealing the deepest core restriction. If I wish to refine that place in a short time, it is absolutely impossible. Even with my powerful divine soul, it would take several decades."

"You need not worry either. If all you want is to achieve the same level as those Adept-stage Penglai disciples, like Yu Gao, when they collected Divine Iris, then it is not difficult for me. I only need to refine a few key restrictions."

"Leave it to me!"

After receiving Han Zhan's promise, Luo Chen could not help letting out a breath.

The Purple Monkey Flowers could not be lost!

Among the thirty-six pill formulas he had obtained in the Pill Palace, there was one Nascent Soul Pill formula for the human race, but the medicinal materials listed on it were varied and numerous, and the refinement points involved were also all different. In the short term, Luo Chen had no way to master that pill formula.

But he had possessed the ancient Nascent Soul Pill formula obtained from Green Pill Valley and Han Zhan for several decades already!

His study of it was so deep that he could act to refine it at any time.

Especially since, among the medicinal materials required, the Five Elements Lotus Platform was already in his possession.

Inside the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion, there was a large patch of high-quality Divine Iris, originating from Wood Heaven Plain and transplanted inside by Penglai Yu Gao.

As for the remaining miscellaneous supplementary materials, he had gradually collected and prepared them over these years.

As long as the Purple Monkey Flowers matured, he could refine Nascent Soul Pills!

Watching Han Zhan's focused movements as he refined the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion, Luo Chen could not help looking a few more times at the other man's puppet body.

The other man had reaped rather extraordinary gains in Bright Radiance Heaven's Puppet Realm!

Aside from failing to obtain the most important puppet inheritance, he had obtained a full nine powerful puppets!

Seven peak Adept puppets, one Sage-stage puppet suitable for Han Zhan's Nascent Soul to reside in, and its entire body was made of spirit wood, allowing his mana to flow and release smoothly.

Aside from these, there was also that white tiger puppet that had appeared only in a stunning glimpse!

If Luo Chen had not seen wrongly, it was absolutely a powerful existence comparable to a late-stage Sage!

It could be said that as long as a cultivator had walked out of the Demon-Fall Land alive, they had more or less obtained substantial gains.

This was true of Luo Chen, true of Han Zhan, and needless to say true of everyone else.

Only, because of this, Luo Chen was very puzzled.

The Primordial Demon Sect had monopolized the Demon-Fall Land for a thousand years. The treasures within it must have been seized in countless numbers.

Even if Ascendant mighty ones did not dare enter personally because of the risk of falling, relying only on the countless brilliant and peerless juniors of a Holy Land generation after generation, they should still have earned a pot full and a bowl full.

After a thousand years, the Primordial Demon Sect should also have become even more flourishing.

Under such circumstances, how had it actually been destroyed in a single battle by the Northern Sea demon race alliance army?

Was it only because Blood Sea Ancestor had fallen inside Bright Radiance Heaven?

Or was it that these endless Northern Sea demon races truly had supreme existences even the Primordial Demon Sect's mighty ones could not match?

No!

That should not be the case.

If such existences truly existed, how could they have been sealed within Arctic Yemo Heaven by the ancestor of the soul-refining lineage?

He could not understand it!

Luo Chen shook his head. Since he could not understand it, he would set it aside for now. In any case, this matter had nothing to do with him.

His attention withdrew from Han Zhan and fell upon the silent little gray cauldron in his qi sea.

Looking at that quiet little cauldron, Luo Chen's heart could not help sinking as well.

Previously, on Bright Radiance Heaven's cloud-sea plaza, Feiyunzi had taken advantage of the moment when Luo Chen was distracted, self-detonating his true-artifact golden bell and forcibly breaking free from the Primordial Unity Cauldron's restraint.

The might of that true artifact's self-detonation could be called terrifying beyond compare.

If Luo Chen's body had not contained Tai Sui's terrifying vitality, continuously repairing his flesh, he likely would have suffered grievous injury as well.

He had survived.

But his lifebound magic treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, had suffered incomparably severe damage, even injuring Luo Chen's divine soul through their connected mind.

For the Primordial Unity Cauldron, with its excellent materials and profound foundation, this was undoubtedly the first time anything like that had happened.

At the time, Luo Chen had even feared it would fall apart.

After all, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, which was infinitely close to a true artifact, still was not a true artifact, and there was an absolute difference in level between it and that golden bell.

But now!

What Luo Chen saw was that the Primordial Unity Cauldron in his qi sea was as intact as ever!

Not a single crack could be found, not one damaged formation could be seen. Only its size had shrunk by about one-third compared to before.

It was as if it had repaired itself automatically.

Although the Primordial Unity Cauldron and the Mystic Dust Armor both had self-repairing functions, that function was actually relatively weak. The owner needed to supplement materials and use mana true fire to sacrifice-refine them day and night before they could recover as before.

There was only one reason it had undergone such a shocking change!

That gray light!

When the teleportation of Bright Radiance Heaven began, the gray light had actively chased after him and voluntarily merged into the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

At that time, Luo Chen had no time to think carefully and instinctively withdrew his own lifebound magic treasure.

Unexpectedly, after several months, he had not found the slightest trace of the gray light within the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

It was as if it had completely merged in!

There was no rejection whatsoever!

Regarding this event, Luo Chen could not understand it no matter how he thought.

Observing carefully at this moment, Luo Chen still gained nothing.

"The gray light that even Moon Wanderer could not subdue, why would it throw itself toward me? Could there really be such a saying as heavenly treasures going to those fated to receive them?"

"How could the Primordial Unity Cauldron and the gray light be so compatible? They merged with each other without the slightest rejection. Could it be because the gray light was previously suppressed inside the Heaven-Refining Cauldron, which was likewise a cauldron-type magic treasure?"

"But that is not right either. The Heaven-Refining Cauldron's artifact spirit saw through the truth that I cultivate Rosycloud's cultivation method, and would rather self-detonate than let me obtain the Heaven-Refining Cauldron and that gray light."

"What exactly is that gray light?"

"A thing left behind by Devil Lord Heaven-Refining. Could it have been refined by him personally?"

"If so, then its voluntary turn toward the Primordial Unity Cauldron means my lifebound magic treasure should have some connection to Devil Lord Heaven-Refining."

"No, this cauldron, from nothing to existence, from blueprints to materials to refinement, never had any connection to Devil Lord Heaven-Refining..."

Luo Chen recalled the process of refining his lifebound magic treasure, and all the events of the past surfaced in his heart.

Abruptly!

A faintly discernible clue linked together in his mind.

If the battle between Devil Lord Heaven-Refining and Rosycloud was true, then it was only natural that Rosycloud's Nirvana Sacred Flame existed within Bright Radiance Heaven.

Changing direction, Rosycloud should also have possessed something belonging to Devil Lord Heaven-Refining.

Perhaps a damaged magic treasure, perhaps a severed limb or remnant body... and essence blood!

Once, when the Primordial Unity Cauldron was being refined, an unexpected accident had occurred.

That accident had caused all of Luo Chen's materials to be consumed. Even Heaven Smelter had taken out his own treasured Azure Duckweed Nether Wind, and at the same time, it had created the Primordial Unity Cauldron's terrifying foundation.

On the day the cauldron was completed, it was already upper-grade!

And that accident originated from unknown blood that had stained a supreme-grade Fire Spirit Diamond.

At that time, Luo Chen had only felt that the aura of the blood was terrifyingly strange.

Heaven Smelter had felt that the blood seemed very suitable for artifact forging, and so allowed it to remain without expelling it.

"Could that blood have belonged to Devil Lord Heaven-Refining? Is that why the gray light, likewise left behind by the Devil Lord, voluntarily came over after the Primordial Unity Cauldron cracked and revealed the blood's aura?"

"Also, in my early years, when I borrowed the Primordial Unity Cauldron for alchemy and artifact forging, it had rather miraculous effects. I only took that to be the Primordial Unity Cauldron's merit. Looking at it now, the credit should belong to that blood. Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's ability in both alchemy and artifact forging reached the heavens. Even a single drop of blood possessed wondrous power!"

Many people said Luo Chen had profound fortune, and Luo Chen often teased himself in the same way.

But teasing was all it was.

Luo Chen had never believed himself to be some chosen child of heaven. Everything he had came from his own effort and calculations.

For that reason, he did not believe those immortal-family anecdotes about heavenly treasures voluntarily throwing themselves toward someone would happen to him.

If he had to find a reason for that final opportunity in Bright Radiance Heaven, Luo Chen could only use this conjecture to explain it.

In short, whether true or false, for him at present it could not be counted as a good thing.

Because he could not drive the Primordial Unity Cauldron!

That little cauldron had quietly taken root in his qi sea just like that, devouring the mana Luo Chen already had little of at this moment.

This was his own lifebound magic treasure!

If he could not activate it, what was that supposed to mean?

Especially since he would still need to refine Nascent Soul Pills later. If the most handy Primordial Unity Cauldron could not be used, the trouble would be enormous.

After a long time.

Luo Chen slowly withdrew his mind.

"Forget it. For now, the Primordial Unity Cauldron being restored as before is already an excellent thing. As long as it does not cause any strange mishaps, there will always be a way to solve this problem later."

"Right now, the most important thing is still for me to recover my mana quickly and restore my strength to its peak."

Luo Chen took out a supreme-grade True Flame Pill, swallowed it, and slowly refined it.

Without a third-tier spirit vein, relying solely on pills to recover mana was this slow.

Especially now that he was at Adept Stage Eight, the mana he required was more than twice what he had needed before.

The accumulation of True Flame Pills in his storage ring was already low.

"Where exactly did all that mana of mine go?"

As he meditated and circulated his art, this question surfaced once again.

...

"Turn the rudder! Turn the rudder! Turn the rudder!"

"They're fighting again up ahead!"

"What is going on? Wasn't this route already under Moluo Stream's control?"

"Who knows? Recently, the Cang Hai Alliance has been counterattacking Moluo Stream on a large scale, and for some unknown reason, Penglai Immortal Sect has reduced its troops, leaving Moluo Stream to fight alone. In any case, forget the rest for now. Avoid this battlefield first, lest we get swept up in it."

Three days later, amid towering waves, the merchant ship belonging to this Adept family hurriedly changed course.

The leader was an early-stage Adept cultivator named Sui Jie.

He looked at the distant scene of multicolored magic treasures clashing in midair, and at the waves raised over the sea by countless powerhouses, his expression incomparably panicked.

If even a little of the aftershock from a great battle of this level reached his side, there would certainly be no good end.

For this reason, even if the delivery date was delayed, they had to turn aside.

Just then, from the corner of his eye, he glimpsed a black-clothed man walking out from the passenger cabin and coming to the edge of the deck.

"Little friend Gu Chen, fierce fighting lies ahead. It would be better to go inside and take shelter."

The black-clothed man glanced at him, then cupped his hands.

"Many thanks for Fellow Daoist Sui's hospitality these past days. I have other urgent matters and cannot afford delay, so I shall take my leave here."

Sui Jie frowned. A mere Initiate cultivator actually addressed him as "Fellow Daoist"; it was rather lacking in self-awareness.

But in the next moment, his eyes widened.

Powerful mana fluctuations abruptly bloomed from the black-clothed man named "Gu Chen," not inferior to his own at all.

No!

As the mana surged layer upon layer, its pure and domineering force made him involuntarily take a step back.

This person's cultivation far surpassed his by who knew how much!

With just one glance, Sui Jie realized that the other man's mana was vast as a deep sea, absolutely something only those old monsters among the Adept stage who had cultivated for three to five hundred years could possess.

His tone turned stiff. "It was I who failed to recognize Mount Tai. I wish you smooth travels, Fellow Daoist."

Luo Chen smiled and nodded, then leapt into the air.

Suspended in midair, a wooden kite one and a half zhang long appeared beneath his feet.

He formed a spirit art, and the wooden kite circled once around the merchant ship before racing toward the center of the battlefield.

In Sui Jie's eyes, the wooden kite carried the black-clothed Daoist like a moth to flame, plunging straight into the towering waves and sky-filling radiance.

"Just what sort of urgent matter would make him disregard his own life, unwilling to detour, and insist on forcing his way through this battlefield between righteous and demonic forces?"

*(End of Chapter)*
