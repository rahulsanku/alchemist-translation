# Chapter 870: The Three-Battle Pact, Destroying Motian Old Ghost’s Fleshly Body

Flying boats crossed the sky, banners fluttering.

Cultivators gathered, the aura of slaughter permeating the heavens.

A single sentence—"Pill Sect, you have gone too far"—was met with a light chuckle.

"Old ghost, back then when you interfered in my Luo Tian Sect's internal affairs, did you think *you* had gone too far?"

Under the gaze of the crowd, Luo Chen paced forward.

A white robe, standing tall at the prow of the Heavenly River Void-Treading Ship.

Motian Old Ghost's expression shifted slightly, and he subconsciously glanced toward the side where the Five Elements Divine Sect cultivators were stationed.

He took a deep breath and said, "That was then, this is now. The Motian Domain is the ancient territory of my Motian Cliff, and since ancient times, it has belonged to my sect!"

"Haha!"

Luo Chen laughed loudly, as if no one else was present.

"A fine 'since ancient times' indeed!"

Abruptly, his laughter ceased, and his voice turned cold. "When the demon beasts occupied the Motian Domain earlier, why didn't you argue with them about the righteousness of your claim?"

"You—!"

"Me?" Luo Chen's lips curled slightly as he produced a black scroll. "I have a decree from the Nether Origin Holy Land. I can also open up the Motian Domain. What do you intend to do about it?"

Motian Old Ghost pointed a trembling finger at Luo Chen.

His lips quivered, even his withered white beard shaking.

At this moment, everyone understood that Pill Sect's trip was not merely to find a piece of spirit land for the Luo Tian Sect, but also to settle the grudges of yesteryear.

Regarding the internal strife of the Luo Tian Sect back then, many were aware of some of the details.

The Motian Cliff had been involved, and another sect had also interfered!

Moreover, that other sect was also present on the scene, likewise in need of the land beneath their feet as a rear base camp for future expansion.

For a moment, many gazes unconsciously turned toward the five-colored treasure ship of the Five Elements Divine Sect.

Would Divine Origin Sage intervene?

The answer was, yes!

A vicissitudinous and hoarse voice slowly spread through the air.

"Pill Sect, the demon chaos has not yet been completely pacified. Do you wish to defy the will of all under heaven and provoke a civil war among the human sects?"

On the five-colored treasure ship, cultivators parted to both sides, clearing a path.

A figure shrouded in a black robe slowly flew out. It was the Supreme Elder of the Five Elements Divine Sect, the one known as the number one cultivator of the Extreme East—Divine Origin Sage!

After his appearance, Motian Old Ghost visibly breathed a sigh of relief.

The current Pill Sect was too strong, its reputation too great. The psychological pressure of facing him directly was something ordinary people could not fathom.

Now that a Grand Cultivator had appeared, Pill Sect should surely restrain his arrogance a little, right?

Luo Chen gazed at the figure, his eyes narrowing slightly.

Towards this potential enemy, after all these years, he had never seen the other's true face!

Today might present an opportunity.

Pursing his lips, Luo Chen gave a carefree smile.

"Daoist Divine Origin, that's quite a heavy hat you're trying to put on me."

"What virtue or ability do I possess to dare provoke a sect war?"

"How about this: to avoid rivers of blood, let this dispute be confined to us Nascent Soul cultivators!"

Divine Origin Sage stared at Luo Chen, saying blandly, "What do you propose?"

This was letting Luo Chen lay down the rules.

Without hesitation, Luo Chen raised three fingers.

"Each of our two sides will send out three people. Victory or defeat will determine who is superior, and the outcome will decide the fate of Motian."

"Three battles decide Motian?" Divine Origin Sage chuckled lowly. "Am I included in that?"

"Of course!" Luo Chen said, full of confidence.

At these words, several Nascent Soul cultivators on Luo Chen's side changed expression slightly.

Especially Nine Spirit Primogenitor, whose expression became extremely serious, his mana fluctuations faintly discernible.

In his view, if Divine Origin Sage were to participate in the battle, the only one capable of opposing him would be himself—no second choice.

But he was still one step short of the Grand Cultivator realm.

He could fight a Grand Cultivator, but winning would be quite difficult.

Of course, he didn't think Luo Chen's plan was problematic at all.

Apart from himself, the remaining people—whether Luo Chen, Deng Taiyue, or Fenghua Fairy—were all of profound cultivation and formidable combat prowess.

Winning two matches would be a simple task!

On the contrary, Motian Old Ghost showed a worried expression. The other side had a wealth of talent. If they used a horse-racing strategy, they wouldn't have such conditions.

Therefore, he secretly transmitted a message.

"Daoist Divine Origin, you cannot agree!"

Divine Origin Sage glanced at him. "Do you not trust me, or do you not trust White Deer Cave's Sage Peng Xiang, or is it that you lack confidence in yourself?"

Motian Old Ghost opened his mouth, his face alternating between pale and green.

While he hesitated, Divine Origin Sage had already spoken. "Fine, I'll go along with what Pill Sect says!"

Luo Chen clapped his hands in admiration. "As expected of a Grand Cultivator, quick and decisive. To avoid any future gossip, I think we should discuss the selection of combatants carefully!"

Divine Origin Sage became interested and asked in a hoarse voice, "How do you wish to discuss it?"

Luo Chen smiled slightly. "It's very simple. This battle is, at its core, a dispute between Luo Tian and Motian. Therefore, our two sects must have a direct confrontation."

Divine Origin Sage's gaze swept over the crowd on the Heavenly River Void-Treading Ship, pausing for a moment on Ling Fengzi beside Luo Chen. He asked with interest, "Will you fight, or will Ling Fengzi fight?"

Luo Chen waved his hand. "Ling Fengzi has only recently joined my Luo Tian Sect. Sending him out wouldn't be quite appropriate."

"Then you will fight?"

Before Luo Chen could answer, Motian Old Ghost blurted out.

"No, Pill Sect fighting would be bullying the weak. It's too unfair!"

These words drew a few snickers from the crowd.

Pill Sect had been cultivating for less than three hundred years in total. How could that be considered bullying the weak?

When had you, the Supreme Elder of Motian Cliff, become so lacking in self-confidence?

Even Luo Chen couldn't help but chuckle softly.

But he quickly nodded.

"What Old Ghost says is not wrong. I am at the mid-Nascent Soul stage, which is indeed a bit like bullying the weak."

"How about this!"

"I'll send someone. I believe you will definitely accept!"

Amidst everyone's confusion, the tall and sturdy Wang Yuan walked out from the back, step by step, until he stood beside Luo Chen.

"Brother Luo, this..." Fenghua Fairy was startled and instinctively wanted to dissuade him.

On the opposite side, Motian Old Ghost was already overjoyed and acknowledged, "You're right. This battle is between Luo Tian and Motian. I am willing to fight the famous War Demon, Wang Yuan!"

Pah!

Shameless!

This thought unconsciously appeared in many people's minds.

Even the disciples of Motian Cliff felt their faces burning.

Divine Origin Sage was somewhat stunned. He hadn't expected Luo Chen to be so arrogant as to send Wang Yuan.

The name War Demon was indeed resounding, but in the end, he was not a Nascent Soul cultivator!

He took a deep look at Luo Chen, his brow furrowing. What exactly was the other party plotting?

But since both sides had agreed, he naturally couldn't refuse.

Composing himself, Divine Origin Sage slowly spoke. "For the second battle, let our side nominate the candidate. Daoist Peng Xiang of White Deer Cave, would you be willing to fight on our behalf and test their skills?"

A Daoist in a black robe, with a solemn brow, made a bowing gesture.

"I, this poor Daoist, offer my greetings!"

The appearance of Sage Peng Xiang drew gasps of surprise.

This man was not widely known, but his strength was considerable, possessing the profound cultivation of the Nascent Soul sixth layer.

His identity was even more noteworthy: he was the senior brother of Iron View Daoist, a comrade-in-arms who had followed Luo Chen and the others into the Overlord Saber Domain back then.

Among those present, the only one who could be said to be certain of defeating him was the Grand Cultivator, Divine Origin Sage.

As for the Luo Tian Sect side?

The Nascent Soul cultivators looked at each other, a few showing faint dissatisfaction.

If they hadn't sent Wang Yuan in the first match, but instead sent Fenghua Fairy, Taiyue Sage, or even Lin Qingxuan, whose cultivation was comparable to Motian Old Ghost's, the arrangements wouldn't have been so difficult.

As if he hadn't seen their dissatisfaction, Luo Chen's gaze fell on Nine Spirit Primogenitor.

"Daoist Nine Spirit, can you handle this man?"

Nine Spirit Primogenitor was full of confidence. "Thanks to the blessing of your pills, after cultivating the Great Nine Heavens Xiao River, I have yet to lose to any of my peers. I know Sage Peng Xiang. In the past, we might have been evenly matched, but now he is definitely no match for me. However..."

Nine Spirit Primogenitor changed his tone. "If I fight the second match, who will deal with Divine Origin Sage?"

"Simple. Isn't there still me?" Luo Chen said with a carefree smile.

Everyone was stunned.

They couldn't help but look Luo Chen up and down.

Everyone knew that Luo Chen had earned the Dao title of Demon-Slaying Sage through his combat prowess.

He had even repelled the Seven-Rhino Great Demon Emperor with a single finger!

But in the end, Luo Chen was only at the Nascent Soul fourth layer, separated from the Grand Cultivator realm by a heavenly chasm.

How could he be so confident?

Nine Spirit Primogenitor was a little anxious. "Why not let Daoist Deng take on Peng Xiang, and let me deal with Divine Origin Sage instead!"

Deng Taiyue also chimed in. "Deng is also willing to contribute his strength today."

Luo Chen shook his head. "Nine Spirit, can you defeat Divine Origin?"

Nine Spirit Primogenitor's expression froze. He dared to fight a Grand Cultivator, but to say he could win for sure—that would be too arrogant.

"You can't, can you?" Luo Chen smiled slightly. "In that case, it's better for you to secure a victory for me. If Wang Yuan also wins his match, it might not even be my turn to fight."

That logic was sound, but...

There were no buts. Luo Chen had already announced the candidate for the third match in front of everyone.

"For the third match, I will personally take the field!"

Divine Origin Sage laughed.

So this was the other party's arrangement—just a reasonable excuse to fight him!

Indeed, with the full support of the Nether Origin Holy Land, where couldn't the Luo Tian Sect go?

A mere Motian Domain—gain or loss meant nothing.

It just so happened that he also wanted to test Luo Chen's true depths.

Since that was the case, he would grant Luo Chen's wish!

"For the third match, I am willing to have a bout with you!"

The candidates for the battle were settled. No matter what anyone thought, the result was already unchangeable.

Soon, the battlefield was also chosen.

It was in the Taihu Domain, adjacent to the Motian Domain!

...

The treasure ship turned around, the flying boats changed course.

Amidst the rise and fall of flying lights, countless cultivators had already spread across the border between the two domains.

Water Mirror techniques were cast among the crowd, specifically for the low-level cultivators whose divine sense was weak and eyesight insufficient.

But for the Nascent Soul powerhouses, these were not really necessary.

Luo Chen stood at the bow of the treasure ship, facing the wind, looking down at the earth below.

The yellow soil was exposed, covered with tortoise-shell cracks. For a thousand li, there was no smoke from human habitation; for ten thousand li, no living creature was seen.

Beside him, Ling Fengzi said with confusion, "Logically, the Taihu Domain is quite far from the Bi Kong Domain where the Ascendants fought. It shouldn't have been so severely affected. Yet its degree of damage is no less than the adjacent Jiuling Domain. It's truly strange."

Elder Fu also chimed in, "Indeed. I was originally planning to choose this place as the cultivation ground for the Divine Talisman Sect, but I didn't expect that even the only fourth-order spirit vein here was destroyed. Such a place is no longer suitable for cultivator cultivation. Even after being transformed by cultivators, it can at most support mortal habitation."

Luo Chen listened on the side, smiling without speaking.

The Taihu Domain shouldn't have been destroyed so badly.

The primary cause was undoubtedly the Ascendant battle, but his own fight with the Hundred-Legged Great Demon Emperor was the main reason the fourth-order spirit vein at the foot of Taihu Palace was destroyed.

But there was no need to mention this to outsiders.

Of course, such a vast piece of land couldn't be wasted.

Later, they could have cultivators make some minor modifications, divert water veins, and then let a large number of mortals thrive here, serving as a supplementary source of fresh blood for the immortal sect.

As for the present?

Two figures burst out of the air and landed on two remaining mountain peaks.

One was Motian Old Ghost, the other was the War Demon, Wang Yuan!

"Junior, surrender early and spare yourself some physical pain," Motian Old Ghost said, his face full of confidence.

Wang Yuan slowly put on a pair of silver-white fist gloves, his eyelids drooping, his tone icy cold.

"Back then on the dueling platform, you lent your life-bound true artifact to Yin Sixiang to suppress me. Today, I will repay that grudge tenfold, a hundredfold!"

Motian Old Ghost was taken aback. He had almost forgotten about that.

But Wang Yuan had never forgotten.

After a brief exchange, Wang Yuan made his move.

Stepping through the air, a sound like thunder exploded.

Boom!

His figure flickered, and he had already crossed several hundred zhang, smashing down towards Motian Old Ghost.

Such a swift movement left many stunned.

Only Luo Chen looked as if he had expected it all along.

This was exactly the Voidburst Step he had created in his early years, inspired by Azure Frost Demon Emperor's spatial teleportation technique.

Now that Wang Yuan was executing it, its momentum was no less than his own back then. In fact, due to the enhancement of Divine Astral Fighting Qi, it was even more violent in its motion.

Although Motian Old Ghost was caught off guard by this move, his divine sense spread out, and he ultimately reacted.

He first released an earthen-yellow shield to protect his surroundings.

Then, opening his mouth, he spat out a flying sword that shot straight towards Wang Yuan's head.

But the next moment.

Thud!

Visibly, the earthen-yellow shield was smashed into a deep depression by the fist that came flying through the air.

This scene made even Motian Old Ghost change expression, and he couldn't help blurting out.

"A true artifact!"

That silver-white fist glove was actually a weapon of the true artifact level!

This time, he no longer dared to stand still and take the opponent's attack head-on. His figure flew backward.

Wang Yuan punched the flying sword away and followed closely behind.

At this moment, his astral qi erupted, and he continuously executed the Voidburst Step. His escape speed was not at all inferior to that of a Nascent Soul cultivator.

The distance between the two was constantly closing.

Motian Old Ghost's expression was horrified. He suddenly released his Nascent Soul domain.

But the domain's power could not suppress Wang Yuan at all.

At this moment, Motian Old Ghost felt a surge of regret for being tricked.

The spirit veins of the Taihu Domain had collapsed, and the spiritual qi was scattered. How could a Nascent Soul cultivator easily condense their Nascent Soul domain?

Moreover, this enemy specialized in body refinement. There was hardly any mana in his body. How could he be suppressed by the spiritual qi of heaven and earth?

"A fine move, Pill Sect!"

Motian Old Ghost cursed Luo Chen under his breath for choosing this battlefield. While in mid-air, he launched an active attack.

The flying sword multiplied: one became ten, ten became a hundred, forming thousands of sword lights that then converged on Wang Yuan.

Not only that, but two more magical treasures flew out from his hands in succession.

A ring spun with a *drip-drop* sound, and a bell jingled.

One was meant to imprison the physical body, the other to disturb the mind.

Visibly, Wang Yuan's movements slowed down. The flying swords struck his body, producing a clattering sound of collision.

Suddenly.

A sharp shout was heard.

"Open!"

The ring flew backward. Wang Yuan's body began to expand continuously, and a three-zhang-tall little giant manifested before the world.

Opening his mouth, he spat out a rumbling thunder sound that suppressed the bell's noise.

Flicking his ten fingers, bloody astral qi sprayed like rain.

After breaking through the thousands of sword lights, the giant stepped with a Moon Step, his figure inexplicably taking on a somewhat ethereal and unpredictable quality.

This time, Motian Old Ghost's divine sense could barely keep up.

"No good. This person is too fast, and his will is firm. He is not affected by my soul-bewitching arts at all. I must first immobilize him."

Motian Old Ghost gritted his teeth, extended his right hand, and lifted it upward.

A great seal flew out with a *drip-drop* sound, then expanded and grew, becoming a hundred zhang in size, pressing down towards Wang Yuan.

A terrifying power of suppression instantly sealed the space.

At that moment, Wang Yuan let out a cold laugh and charged straight towards the great seal without any fear.

"Break!"

One punch!

The main body of the great seal trembled violently.

Wang Yuan withdrew his hand, his astral qi surging, and threw three more punches.

"Break!"

"Break!"

"Break!"

This time, the result was completely different.

The three moves, derived from Luo Chen's Mountain-Breaking Style, drove his domineering astral qi to its fullest extent. The power was condensed into a single point, amplified by the Thunderclap Fist Gloves.

Only a sound of explosion was heard.

Boom!

The great seal, to the shock of everyone, was blasted into pieces.

Wang Yuan charged out in one go, straight up to the nine heavens.

With his magical treasure destroyed, Motian Old Ghost suffered backlash, blood trickling from the corner of his mouth. He felt quite unwilling.

If his life-bound true artifact, Motian Cliff, were still here, how would this Wang Yuan brat dare to be so presumptuous?

Forget it!

To win against this man, he would have to rely on his profound mana to outlast the opponent.

He reached out with his hand, and the flying sword leisurely returned to his grasp. Then, he flew towards the nine heavens.

But the next moment, Motian Old Ghost's expression changed drastically.

Above the nine heavens, there was a lofty mountain, like a meteor, rumbling down.

"That's my Motian Cliff!"

The unfamiliar mountain body, the familiar aura, made him blurt it out.

But his divine sense control received no response.

In panic, he activated the shield to its maximum, holding it above his head.

Not only that, but he continuously formed hand seals. His robe moved without wind, and a layer of yellow light enveloped his entire body.

Bang!

The shield magical treasure shattered at the first touch.

The mountain pressed down from above. Even with the yellow light protecting his body, it still forced Motian Old Ghost to descend continuously, finally stopping on the earth amidst his furious roar.

Wang Yuan stood atop the mountain, stamped his foot, and stomped.

Boom!

The immense pressure reappeared, shaking the yellow light apart by several more fractions. Motian Old Ghost's face turned a deep red.

Wang Yuan perceived this scene, an indescribable feeling of satisfaction welling up in his heart.

Back then, you suppressed me with Motian Cliff. Today, I suppress you with Motian Hundred-Craft Mountain. Truly, the one who started it all—does he have no descendants!

He stamped his foot again. The Divine Astral Fighting Qi traveled along his body and was injected into the Motian Hundred-Craft Mountain.

Boom!

Motian Old Ghost's pupils widened. His protective robe suddenly shattered, revealing his withered, old, and shriveled body.

And that yellow light became even fainter.

It seemed that with just one more stomp, it would shatter.

Without thinking, he roared, "I concede!"

Wang Yuan acted as if he hadn't heard, and stomped once more.

Boom!

Whoosh...

The fierce wind stirred, and the yellow light canopy collapsed with a crash.

A large spray of blood mist scattered across the dry yellow earth.

A streak of escaping light shot out, crossing a hundred li, escaping the suppression range of the Motian Hundred-Craft Mountain.

The tender, white infant's face showed a venomous expression. It formed a hand seal, glaring fixedly at Wang Yuan.

"I want you dead!"

Wang Yuan floated up, reached out with his hand, and the Motian Hundred-Craft Mountain rapidly shrank, falling into his grasp.

"I can destroy your main body once, and I can kill your Nascent Soul once!"

He stretched out his right hand from afar, his five fingers flexing as if to grasp.

The Nascent Soul little man's expression changed. He felt blood threads appearing on his tender body, and his eyes instantly filled with terror.

At that moment, a surge of mana enveloped his Nascent Soul.

Wang Yuan clenched his fist, detonating the astral qi, but got no reaction.

A fierce look appeared on his face as he looked into the distance.

Divine Origin Sage waved his hand, and Motian Old Ghost's Nascent Soul flew back to his side.

Seeing this, Luo Chen couldn't help but smile slightly.

"This first battle counts as a win for my Luo Tian Sect, right?"

As his words fell, the multitude fell silent, not a sound to be heard.

(End of Chapter)
