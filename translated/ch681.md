# Chapter 681: A Cornered Beast's Struggle, the Black Nail Breaks the Soul

The contingency formation laid down by a generation's Void Refining True Lord was exceedingly exquisite.

So long as Tai Sui's main body did not die, this Soul-Hooking Spirit-Nailing Formation would not cease.

That was because the formation's energy came from the immense vitality within Tai Sui's fleshly body, then borrowed that very power to nail Tai Sui's soul and spirit in place.

Just hearing of such a method was enough to make one's thoughts reel.

It was equivalent to using Tai Sui's own body as a prison to trap Tai Sui's soul, truly making the body into a jail, hooking the soul and nailing the spirit.

Under normal circumstances, Golden Core cultivators like Luo Chen and the others had no way to break it.

However!

Tai Sui, who had suffered for many years under the pain of having his soul and spirit trapped, had thought of a method.

As long as the power of the main body was weakened, the might of the Soul-Hooking Spirit-Nailing Formation would inevitably weaken as well.

At that time, with inner and outer forces cooperating, they could transfer the main body's divine soul into Ding Yi's avatar body. Once the Soul-Hooking Spirit-Nailing Grand Formation lost its target, it would collapse on its own without being attacked.

Afterward, they would take out the main body and merge it with the divine soul. That would complete the golden cicada shedding its shell.

After Tai Sui explained this idea, Luo Chen and the others acknowledged its feasibility.

The formation had succeeded because of the terrifying vitality contained within Tai Sui's main body, and it would also fail because its source of power was Tai Sui's main body.

What Luo Chen and the others had to do now was weaken the strength of Tai Sui's main body.

As for that,

Tai Sui had planned for many years and had long since prepared a countermeasure.

"This Thirty-Six Golden Altars Rebirth Formation [TERM?] restrains my wood-attribute fleshly body the most. As long as we proceed little by little, slicing flesh with a blunt knife, and combine it with the cooperation of my avatar, we will certainly reach the point where my soul and spirit can escape."

Tai Sui spoke leisurely.

Behind him, Moya Cave Master was moving among one golden altar after another, activating the formations.

At the core of each formation, there was also a lower-grade magic treasure serving as a suppressing object.

In total, there were fully thirty-six magic treasures!

Even if they were only lower-grade, such a display was astonishing enough, because all of them were metal-attribute lower-grade magic treasures, or at least contained metal-attribute power.

Not only that, before entering the formation, Tai Sui had handed Moya Cave Master an entire storage bag of upper-grade spirit stones, along with several metal-attribute supreme-grade spirit stones.

For today, he had schemed painstakingly for three hundred years!

The grand formation had not yet been fully activated. Luo Chen still had some leisure at this moment. While marveling at this great expenditure, he asked about the details.

"Was Ding Yi willing to spend so much of his accumulation?"

Tai Sui closed his eyes and said in a muffled voice, "Of course he was not willing. He was thinking every moment of freeing himself from the influence of my remnant thought. Buying Dustfall Pills from you was only one meaningless struggle."

"However, how many people in this world can withstand my temptation?"

Luo Chen was surprised. "Temptation?"

Tai Sui smiled faintly. "Yes. The name of the Tai Sui spirit herb is known throughout the cultivation world. It is said to revive the dead, flesh white bones, and possess the power of creation. Of course, such rumors are somewhat exaggerated, but the terrifying vitality contained in my main body is real. It was accumulated by being touched by the power of the wood law."

"Do you truly think Ding Yi came to Demon-Fall Land to resolve the influence of my remnant thought? In truth, he coveted my main body and wanted to refine it into medicine, producing a spirit herb that could help him break into the Sage realm."

Luo Chen was stunned.

Yet looking at the white flesh lump before him, like a mass of meat jelly, he had no choice but to believe it.

He could sense that the thing truly contained unfathomable vitality, many times greater even than his Archaic Tier Four body and soul.

The power of the wood law?

Luo Chen murmured the words.

At this moment, the Thirty-Six Golden Altars Rebirth Formation was already prepared.

Moya Cave Master gave a low shout, and a beam of golden light rose up, covering Tai Sui's main body.

Among the five elements, metal restrained wood.

As one formation pressed down, Tai Sui's main body immediately began to resist.

But the Tai Sui remnant thought within Ding Yi's body issued a command and forcibly suppressed the main body's resistance.

Clang!

The golden light fell. Amid the trembling of the white flesh ball, its aura instantly dropped.

Visible to the naked eye, a lump of flesh was cut away.

The Golden Altars Rebirth Formation had taken effect.

Seizing this opportunity, Luo Chen's eyes bloomed with spiritual light as he stared fixedly at Tai Sui's main body.

He observed the changes in the Soul-Hooking Spirit-Nailing Formation upon it.

It seemed to have weakened somewhat?

"Not enough!"

"Cave Master, please continue!"

Tai Sui gave a low shout.

Behind him, Moya Cave Master naturally did not hesitate. He continued activating the next Golden Altars Rebirth Formation.

Under his control, golden lights rose and fell one after another, continuously landing upon Tai Sui's main body.

To suppress the main body's resistance, the remnant thought also exerted all its strength, and his expression grew increasingly fierce.

Luo Chen watched this scene from the side the entire time, but most of his attention remained on observing the formation.

Under his focused attention, he faintly glimpsed a tiny bit of the formation's essence.

"Devil Lord Heaven-Refining drew out Tai Sui's vitality and transformed it into seven illusory rust-marked golden nails, nailing down Tai Sui's seven spirits."

"Then he used the body as a prison to lock down its three souls."

"With that, he constructed a flawless formation."

"Can I also arrange a similar formation?"

Luo Chen's thoughts rose and fell. Many understandings of formation dao gathered together as he attempted to learn by analogy, or at least comprehend a little of the surface.

But the result left him somewhat frustrated.

He had no idea how to transform the tangible essence qi and vitality of the flesh into illusory spiritual power.

Those illusory golden nails were something he could not condense.

Perhaps Han Zhan could?

He told Han Zhan this guess, and Han Zhan gave an answer.

"I cannot achieve such a degree of turning substance into formlessness either. That is a method involving the level of laws. It can even be said that if Tai Sui himself were not assisting, and if you were not using a method that removes the firewood from beneath the cauldron and destroys the foundation of the formation, you would not have even the slightest chance of breaking this formation."

"However, this formation has given me a little inspiration..."

Luo Chen grew curious. "What inspiration?"

"Heh. Inspiration is inspiration precisely because it has not yet been implemented. This old man does not know whether it is right or wrong, so how could I recount it to you?" Han Zhan chuckled, then asked in return, "What, do you want to replicate this formation?"

Luo Chen said, "I would not dare."

Han Zhan did not think too deeply on it. Instead, he said, "In my view, such methods are applications of the essence of power. Low-level cultivators like us may not understand the essence of power, but we can borrow tools and achieve similar effects while knowing the result without knowing the principle."

That was a little like clouds and mist to him.

But the Luo Chen of today was no longer the young Qi Refinement cultivator who understood nothing back then. He could faintly grasp Han Zhan's general meaning.

Tools...

"Could Senior be referring to soul-type magic treasures like the Rotten Axe-Handle Black Chess Pieces?"

"You are rather clever." Han Zhan praised him, then continued, "However, such magic treasures are rare in the world. Even my Falling Cloud Sect, which excels in soul-refining arts, lacks the relevant treasures. The reason my Split-Soul Art has been unable to reach great completion for so long is partly due to this."

To cultivate the Split-Soul Art to great completion required related soul-type magic treasures.

Luo Chen memorized this information.

Afterward, he reflected on himself.

Soul-type magic treasures were indeed rare, especially defensive ones.

But attacking soul-type magic treasures did occasionally appear in the cultivation world, even if their grades were usually not very high.

As it happened, he had one on him.

"If I can learn even three parts of the essence of this Soul-Hooking Spirit-Nailing Formation, then borrow an external object to use it, perhaps it can replace the illusion technique Mirror Flower, Water Moon."

Mirror Flower, Water Moon was very powerful. In battle, it could make a person fall for it without knowing.

But once this technique suffered backlash, the danger was enormous.

Therefore, when Luo Chen fought above his level, unless he had no other choice, he rarely used this art.

As for battles against opponents of the same Golden Core realm, his other methods were already enough to crush them head-on.

Over time, this illusion art, which he had painstakingly cultivated to great completion and rooted in both eyes, had gradually become tasteless.

If he could find a replacement method, and one that borrowed an external object without backlashing onto himself, it would make up for Luo Chen's flaw in soul attacks.

With this thought, Luo Chen observed even more meticulously.

His pair of spiritual eyes did not shift in the slightest, and even began to feel dry and astringent.

Time slowly passed.

Inside the Falling Demon Abyss cave, more than half of the thirty-six Golden Altars Rebirth Formations had already been activated.

Three of the supreme-grade metal spirit stones in Moya Cave Master's hands had been drained dry, to say nothing of that full storage bag of upper-grade spirit stones.

On Tai Sui's side, the changes were even more obvious.

The main body had taken on a fragmented appearance. Of the seven nails, four had already dissipated, and the remaining three had grown increasingly illusory, clearly on the verge of collapse.

The remnant-thought avatar occupying Ding Yi's fleshly body was like a dying candle in the wind, his waist bent and his back hunched.

Sweat drops the size of pearls continuously fell from his face, striking the ground and breaking into petal-like fragments.

One could imagine how difficult suppressing the main body's resistance was for the remnant thought.

Ugh...

When another golden nail dissipated, Tai Sui groaned and slowly said, "Devil Lord Azure Yang, prepare on your side."

"Mm."

The answer to him was a simple "mm."

Tai Sui said no more. He gave a low roar and formed a spirit seal.

The next moment, clusters of lingzhi and green moss flew in from outside the cave, gathering toward his body.

These were all powers Tai Sui had forcibly split from his main body over the past thousand years in order to reduce the force of suppression.

Now, one by one, they converged upon Ding Yi's body as reserve power.

"Hm?"

Luo Chen gave a soft sound. The light of the protective barrier around him flickered.

Vaguely, there were tiny things, invisible to the naked eye, lingering outside.

At this moment, Tai Sui's voice came again.

"Do not resist. Those are also powers I split off. Later, when you use the Spirit-Blocking Grand Formation, you may suffer attacks from the Soul-Hooking Spirit-Nailing Formation. With this power on you, it will not treat you as an enemy."

Luo Chen hesitated somewhat.

Tai Sui said in a low voice, "This is a battle between me and it. If an outsider interferes, it is easy to be accidentally injured. It is best for you to carry my aura."

Luo Chen curled his lip, then opened the protective barrier and allowed those invisible things to attach to him.

His spiritual sense spread out and saw only extremely tiny particles.

Spores?

This thought had just emerged when Luo Chen's attention was drawn by Moya Cave Master's movement.

Another Golden Altars Rebirth Formation had activated!

...

Time flew like a shuttle, like a white colt passing a crack.

When the final Golden Altars Rebirth Formation lit up inside the cave, it represented that this operation had reached its end.

Tai Sui's eyes were full of anticipation.

He could feel that the might of the Soul-Hooking Spirit-Nailing Grand Formation had already been weakened to the extreme.

Even though his main fleshly body was broken apart, the divine soul that had been silent for many years was gradually awakening.

Only one step remained!

Suddenly, Moya Cave Master held his seal and did not release it.

"Now that we have reached this moment, you should hand me the method to enter the inheritance land, should you not?"

Tai Sui seemed to have expected this and was not too surprised. He was only angry for an instant.

"You truly cannot stay calm."

Afterward, he transmitted a message with his spiritual sense.

Luo Chen watched this scene but did not pay much attention to it, because the next step was his turn. Whether they could isolate the pursuit of the Soul-Hooking Spirit-Nailing Formation would depend on his Spirit-Blocking Grand Formation.

Behind him, after Moya Cave Master finished listening to what Tai Sui said, uncertainty appeared on his face.

"Inside the tornado? Are you deceiving me? I can sense how dangerous that place is even without going in."

These words were not transmitted, but asked directly.

Even Luo Chen heard them clearly.

Tai Sui said in a deep voice, "As long as you follow the method I taught you and drive the magic treasure you refined in advance, you can enter the inheritance land without obstruction. Do not worry that I am deceiving you."

Moya Cave Master hesitated for a moment, then his gaze gradually firmed.

"I believe a being like you would not use this sort of matter to deceive me."

After speaking, he released the hand seal.

The next moment, a golden axe chopped down through the air.

There was no great movement, but in the hearts of all three, a cracking sound seemed to echo.

The final illusory nail was destroyed.

"Azure Yang, now!"

Tai Sui gave a low roar and pressed a finger to his own brow.

In an instant, violent wind howled through the cave.

A divine soul that seemed almost tangible slowly floated out from the fragmented Tai Sui main body.

Along with its appearance came clattering sounds.

One illusory chain after another, each bearing a hook, was still hooked onto that massive divine soul.

The enormous divine soul flew out, moving with extreme slowness as it drifted toward Ding Yi's body.

Wherever it passed, the illusory hooked chains dragged at it, making its progress stagger.

Luo Chen looked at this scene and could not help swallowing.

Had the divine soul of a fifth-tier Tai Sui already reached such a degree, showing signs of condensing into substance even while still illusory?

His amazement lasted only an instant.

The Spirit-Blocking Grand Formation had already activated.

Under his control, the formation wrapped around Tai Sui's divine soul like a filter, little by little stripping away those weakened hooked chains that had lost their vast vitality resource.

The enormous divine soul advanced bit by bit and escaped its shackles bit by bit.

At last, it left the Spirit-Blocking Grand Formation and arduously merged into Ding Yi's body.

Almost at the exact instant of fusion, the fragmented Tai Sui fleshly body lying at the center of the cave flashed with green light and returned to its original state in the blink of an eye.

It was as though the damage inflicted by the Thirty-Six Golden Altars Rebirth Grand Formation had been no more than a drizzle.

At that same moment, Luo Chen's expression changed slightly.

The same green lights flickered on his body.

They were the Tai Sui powers that had attached to him earlier.

"What is the meaning of this?"

Luo Chen shouted angrily.

Tai Sui turned a deaf ear, still closing his eyes as though adapting to his own body.

At the cave entrance, Moya Cave Master, pale-faced, glanced at the two of them, then rushed out without a word.

He did not even look back.

After he left, Tai Sui slowly opened his eyes. A covetous gaze fell upon Luo Chen.

"Ding Yi's fleshly body, even after years of adjustment, is still far too strained to carry my immense divine soul. Before I leave Demon-Fall Land, I also cannot merge with my main fleshly body."

"Your archaic beast body is clearly a better vessel. It may even be the best vessel."

"Fellow Daoist, I will accept it with a smile."

As he spoke, he slowly raised a finger toward Luo Chen.

Yet before he could activate those green lights, clusters of azure flame emerged from Luo Chen's body.

Those weak green lights, under the azure flames, were like dry wood meeting raging fire and instantly began to burn.

Between the flicker of fire and smoke, the red robe became black armor.

Luo Chen's eyes were ice-cold. He rose abruptly and punched out.

"Did you truly think Luo had not guarded against you at all?"

In an instant, wind and thunder erupted in the cave.

The distance between the two was already extremely close, only one or two zhang.

At such a distance, the violent power inside Luo Chen's fleshly body could be displayed to the fullest.

With only one punch.

Boom!

Tai Sui was blasted backward, smashing into the mountain wall like a torn sack.

A huge hole appeared between his chest and abdomen.

Luo Chen slowly withdrew his hand, gripping a Golden Core, then gently squeezed.

Crack!

The Golden Core that Ding Yi had bitterly cultivated for several hundred years shattered abruptly.

Pure magic power poured out, forming a surging wave of qi inside the cave.

If this were a normal person, even a Sage Perfected Being would have died beyond any hope of further death under that strike.

However.

Tai Sui drifted down to the ground and merely glanced at the huge hole in his chest and abdomen, then shook his head.

"Why bother? It is nothing more than a cornered beast's struggle."

At that moment, a sharp sword cry rang out.

Behind Luo Chen, seven baleful lights emerged from the sword case and flew out like meteors.

Seeing this, Tai Sui sneered.

"Such a shallow method dares to display itself before an expert?"

Yet in the next moment, his expression changed.

Those seven baleful lights crisscrossed through the qi waves, faintly constructing a sword formation.

That sword formation had chilling baleful qi and crisscrossing sword energy, its might drawn taut but not yet released.

No, this was definitely not Luo Chen's method!

He remembered very clearly that the previous times Luo Chen used this set of flying-sword magic treasures, their power had been quite ordinary.

But now.

Could it be the method of the other person hidden on Luo Chen?

Suddenly, his eyes nearly split with rage.

That sword formation was not coming for him. It was rushing toward his main body.

"Stop!"

With an angry roar, Tai Sui staggered and rushed straight over.

But after charging forward only several zhang, he realized something was wrong.

Attack what he must save, but the true target was still the original owner.

Sure enough, the sword formation left and returned, dropping down over Tai Sui's fat body.

In an instant, flesh and blood flew, and white bones were exposed.

"I underestimated you."

Tai Sui gnashed his teeth. He pressed his palms together and opened a great green umbrella.

He could feel that this sword formation's might was enormous, no weaker than any method of a Sage Perfected Being.

Even if his main body faced it personally, he would have to be cautious, let alone this avatar body that had only just fused with him.

Amid the surging qi waves, a black light tore through the air.

"Hmph!"

Tai Sui gave a soft snort and exhaled a breath. The black light immediately slowed and revealed its form in midair.

It was a black nail.

A nail?

Tai Sui's entire body trembled, and instinctively he felt very uncomfortable.

At this moment, Luo Chen rushed out from the qi waves, spread his five fingers, and turned them into a fleshy palm.

One palm slapped down on... that black nail!

With terrifying force added to it, the black nail broke through layer after layer of resistance and landed on Tai Sui's brow in an instant.

"Aaah!"

The shrill scream almost shattered the eardrums.

Luo Chen snorted coldly and withdrew, with no intention of killing him to the last.

Tai Sui's divine soul was far too enormous. Relying only on this lower-grade Soul-Breaking Nail might not completely kill him.

It was enough to suppress him for a short time while he could only command part of Ding Yi's former power.

His hand touched something.

Cold and soft.

Spiritual light flickered, and that thing vanished from where it was.

"Fellow Daoist, I will accept your body with a smile."

"No need to see me off!"

Before the words had finished, Luo Chen had already rushed out of the cave. Before leaving, he even turned and struck a palm backward, collapsing the mountain cave.

After he left, huge rocks rolled down, and large amounts of rubble crashed down, burying the cave completely.

Several breaths later.

Bang!

With a great sound, Tai Sui stepped out from the smoke and dust. The black nail in his brow had already been forced out by three parts.

His face, dark as water, was now filled with fury.

His main body had been stolen!

He had set his eyes on Luo Chen's archaic beast body, and the other party had likewise set his eyes on the Tai Sui body he had cultivated for over ten thousand years.

The so-called oath and promise had ended the moment his divine soul escaped.

Now, he had truly lost both the lady and the soldiers.

He stretched out his hand, grasped the black nail, and pulled it out bit by bit.

"Ah..."

The continuous painful cries went on until finally, with a clang, the black nail fell to the ground.

Looking into the dark abyss where no daylight could be seen, Tai Sui growled,

"You cannot escape!"

With a thought, green glows suddenly emerged throughout the originally black, oppressive abyss.

They were all the powers he had split off over the past several hundred years, spread throughout the entire Falling Demon Abyss.

Devil Lord Azure Yang was still in the jar!

...

Within the abyss, a figure shot upward.

Luo Chen's face was full of joy.

It was in hand!

This fifth-tier Tai Sui had finally fallen into his hands.

Tai Sui had coveted his archaic beast body. How could he not have had similar thoughts?

All along the way, Tai Sui had probed him again and again, even committing excessive acts, and he had endured them. Was this not what he had been aiming for today?

As for the battle process, although there had been accidents, the result was still within expectation.

As early as Blazing Refinement Prison, he had noticed that the other party seemed to dislike that sort of environment.

In other words, fire spirits had an extremely strong restraining effect on Tai Sui.

Luo Chen had originally planned to pour out all his Withering-Bloom True Fire and fight him, but after observing the Soul-Hooking Spirit-Nailing Formation, he had another idea.

The Soul-Breaking Nail!

This magic treasure came from the Great Elder Helian Yun of the Lianyun Merchant Alliance, who had bestowed it during the battle at the Great Snowy Mountains.

Back then, the other party had released three black nails in one breath, the Qi-Breaking Nail [TERM?], the Armor-Breaking Nail, and the Soul-Breaking Nail.

After the other party died in battle, Luo Chen naturally gained the corresponding spoils.

In later battles, both the Qi-Breaking Nail and Armor-Breaking Nail had already been ruined, and the Soul-Breaking Nail had also been shelved by Luo Chen.

This time, Luo Chen had a sudden flash of insight and used it in coordination with methods similar to the Soul-Hooking Spirit-Nailing Formation, striking Tai Sui's divine soul. It produced an unexpected effect and bought him time to collect Tai Sui's fleshly body.

"You should feel fortunate that his power has been continuously scattered over several hundred years, and that Ding Yi's Golden Core was destroyed by you. Otherwise, this dangerous act of snatching chestnuts from the fire would very likely have failed even with this old man's help."

Han Zhan's voice echoed beside his ear.

Luo Chen laughed heartily. "If he had not revealed such weakness, how could murderous thoughts have arisen in me? Now that the matter is done, let us make ourselves scarce!"

Amid the hearty laughter, Luo Chen's wings trembled, and he flew away even faster.

...

One incense stick later.

Luo Chen's face was gloomy as he fled into the abyss. The smile on his face had long vanished, replaced by hidden formations all fully activated.

Han Zhan smiled bitterly. "How could such an existence be so easy to deal with? You should have expected this long ago."

Luo Chen said nothing. Azure flames erupted from him again.

Sizzle!

On his arm, the mushroom that had just grown out instantly turned to ash.

Just now, when he tried to fly out of the abyss, a giant mushroom had appeared from nowhere and blocked his path.

That mushroom was very powerful, but under Luo Chen's full-force eruption, he still did not fear it.

Yet after destroying that mushroom, small mushrooms somehow began growing from his body.

These mushrooms were continuously drawing magic power from him.

If the Withering-Bloom True Fire did not restrain them, Luo Chen might already have become a mushroom man.

And existences like these were beginning to appear continuously inside and outside the abyss.

Luo Chen understood that these things should all be powers Tai Sui had forcibly split away over the past several hundred years in order to escape, just like the remnant thought on Ding Yi.

The powers Tai Sui had split off were not so strong that they could not be resisted, but that strange force...

Could that be the so-called power of law?

But was it not said that ordinary wood and stone spirits, even if they reached fifth tier, could not use the law power within them?

Another mushroom grew from his head.

Luo Chen brushed a hand over it, azure flame winding around and releasing an unusual fragrance.

If given enough time, with his strengthened Withering-Bloom True Fire, he could certainly eliminate this force.

But what he lacked now was precisely time.

"I cannot keep fighting these fellows. I cannot kill them, and they will only make my magic power thinner and thinner."

Han Zhan asked, "Then how will you leave this abyss?"

Luo Chen's gaze swept around.

At the cliff top, countless Tai Sui avatars were beginning to gather. If Tai Sui's divine soul commanded and controlled them, those avatars would inevitably become even more dangerous.

As for the bottom of the abyss,

Luo Chen only glanced once before withdrawing his gaze with a chill in his heart.

Finally, he looked toward the enormous spiritual qi tornado that connected heaven and earth.

He gritted his teeth and turned in a different direction.

Yet after the time it took to drink a cup of tea, several green lights flew over faintly, and vague voices emerged from them.

"You cannot escape. You cannot escape."

"Return it to me. Return my body to me!"

Looking at these Tai Sui avatars, weak ones comparable to Golden Cores, strong ones rivaling fourth-tier fire spirits, Luo Chen was forced backward.

He did not fear direct hardness. Even if a Sage Perfected Being stood before him now, he would dare fight.

But that force, once it contaminated him, was like a maggot clinging to bone, drawing out his magic power and even his essence blood. He did not want to touch it again at all.

Compared with a frontal battle, this sort of clinging erosion was far more disgusting.

It was not strong enough to kill him immediately.

But every delay made the situation worse.

"You are ruthless!"

Luo Chen cursed under his breath and rushed straight toward the heaven-reaching tornado.

Han Zhan silently watched this scene and murmured inwardly, 'I fear this is the choice your subconscious already hoped to make.'

...

Several breaths later.

Tai Sui controlled the fat body and arrived outside the spiritual qi tornado.

His robe was whipped noisily by the wild wind, and the flesh on his face constantly trembled.

"Damn it, he really went in!"

After cursing, Tai Sui fell into a dilemma.

Finally, he looked at the growing number of green lights around him, already several hundred strong.

"If I want to leave Demon-Fall Land, I can only pass through Bright Radiance Heaven."

"The Soul-Hooking Spirit-Nailing Formation is gone, and without the main body's attraction, the True Lord's restrictions should not trigger again."

"Let me try!"

Gritting his teeth, Tai Sui opened his mouth.

He had no better choice.

The next moment, the several hundred green lights around him began converging toward him.

His body also began expanding wildly as though inflated.

The hole Luo Chen had punched through his chest and abdomen gradually filled with flesh and blood.

His aura also began to climb continuously!

In the blink of an eye, it surpassed the Golden Core realm, directly reaching early-stage Sage, middle-stage Sage, and then the peak of middle-stage Sage.

When it reached that final step, however, it could not cross no matter what.

Tai Sui cursed under his breath again, then carefully stepped into the tornado.

Spiritual qi swept around him. After only a few rotations, Tai Sui's figure vanished completely.

*(End of Chapter)*
