# Chapter 912: True Fire Mutates, Returning in Force

Rising and falling, deep and dark.

The world was pitch-black, direction impossible to discern.

Suddenly, a glimmer of light flickered.

The creatures that had been wandering nearby for a long time were drawn to it, instinctively gathering closer.

Yet what greeted them were sword lights erupting from fingertips.

After casually slaying these insignificant little ghosts, Luo Chen cautiously emerged from the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land.

The moment he saw the scene outside, he froze entirely.

He looked up at the sky, then down at the ground, feeling as though seas had turned to mulberry fields.

"Am I still in the Nether Origin Sacred Land?"

As far as the eye could see, there was only an expanse of black water, even giving him the feeling of having returned to the Fallen Sea.

But unlike the immensely heavy waters of the Fallen Sea, the black water here brought him not pressure, but a sense of chaos and disorientation.

Suddenly.

Luo Chen abruptly sensed the protective barrier surrounding him. Its crimson color was turning black, and it was continuously dissipating.

He instinctively circulated his mana, urging his protective qi barrier.

But as soon as a portion of his mana flowed back, Luo Chen decisively cast it away, a look of disgust appearing on his face.

"This is the Nether Water of the Nether Abyss!"

The waters of the Fallen Sea primarily corrode. A cultivator's protective qi barrier would be continuously digested and corroded, but it had no real effect on the essence of the cultivator's mana.

But the Nether Water of the Nether Abyss was different—filthy, dirty, and utterly defiling!

Once a cultivator's mana, magical treasure, or even physical body was contaminated, it would be corrupted.

Luo Chen had instinctively withdrawn his flagging mana, and immediately sensed something was wrong.

That portion of mana had been completely defiled, utterly unsuitable for recovery and reuse.

Moreover, there was no space here to replenish mana.

After identifying the Nether Water, a thought suddenly arose in Luo Chen's mind.

"Has the Nether Abyss been opened, completely flooding the Nether Origin Sacred Land?"

No one answered him; he could only explore on his own.

Although the Nether Water would defile mana, the degree of defilement was limited. With Luo Chen's profound mana reserves, he could hold out for a while.

He pressed forward, ten li, a hundred li, a thousand li...

Throughout this process, ghostly creatures would occasionally appear in his line of sight.

But because Luo Chen had concealed his aura, even changing the color of his mana barrier to blend in with the Nether Abyss, he was rarely discovered.

Those insignificant little ghosts were fine; Luo Chen paid them no mind.

What truly put him on guard was a Sage-level ghost he had glimpsed earlier!

Legend had it that countless evil ghosts and resentful spirits were suppressed within the Nether Abyss.

It was an ancient forbidden land that had flowed for tens of millions of years before the Nether Origin Sect even established its sect here.

Later, after the Nether Origin Sect experienced the ancient war of expansion, they took the easy way out and simply threw all the demon beasts and ghost souls that were difficult to convert during the war into the Nether Abyss.

In this way, they not only saved the effort of converting the ghosts but also strengthened the threat of the Nether Abyss to protect the sect.

But now that the Nether Abyss had flooded the Nether Origin Sacred Land in reverse, those countless ghostly creatures had been completely released.

A Sage-level ghost was no coincidence.

Within this boundless Nether Sea, who knew how many Sage-level ghosts there were, or even Ascendant-level ghosts.

Luo Chen dared not force his way across the Nether Abyss.

When his mana had been consumed to a certain extent, he hesitated for a moment, then swam upward.

This process was quick.

But just as he was about to touch the surface, Luo Chen stopped.

Golden flames were burning above!

Luo Chen's pupils contracted, and the words slipped out.

"Nirvana Sacred Flame!"

...

The Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land opened again.

Luo Chen walked back in with a grim expression.

Black King immediately greeted him. "Master, what's it like out there?"

Luo Chen waved his hand. "Gone. All of it."

Black King was stunned. "What's gone?"

"The Nether Origin Sect is gone!"

Having said this, he paid Black King no further attention. He walked over to the ring of fire, casually beckoned, and the ring transformed into a small golden bird that flew back onto Luo Chen's shoulder.

Glancing at Luo Lingxi, who was still sitting dazedly beside Ye Ruoli's corpse, Luo Chen said nothing, his face taut.

This was fine, too. It saved him the trouble of explaining to the Nether Origin experts.

As for his own son?

The other party simply couldn't cultivate; he wasn't stupid. He would naturally understand that under those circumstances, what Luo Chen had done was necessary.

Perhaps the shock of seeing someone familiar die before his eyes was too great, and he couldn't accept it for the moment.

Time would dilute everything.

And right now, time was the one thing Luo Chen was not short of.

"I'm trapped inside the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land!"

Luo Chen muttered gloomily to himself, then soared into the air and chose a nearby mountain peak.

This place had a fourth-order spirit vein, with relatively rich spiritual qi, enough for him to recover his mana.

After Luo Chen left, Black King still hadn't fully reacted.

That powerful Nether Origin Sect was gone?

He had a deep understanding of the Nether Origin Sect's strength. There were countless experts within, and many who inspired fear in him.

Not to mention the three Ascendant Ancestors, even Gu Xianfengjiang, whom he had seen upon entering the sect, possessed the power to destroy him.

But all of this was gone, just like that?

"What about the three Ancestors?"

...

"Of the three Ancestors, one died by my sword, one died at the hands of Rosycloud, and the last one..."

In just a few days, relying solely on his daily circulation of his cultivation technique, Luo Chen's mana had fully recovered to its peak.

This showed how much his cultivation efficiency had improved after switching techniques.

At this moment, he was sitting cross-legged in a temporary cave dwelling he had opened in the mountain, his mind replaying the information he had extracted from a certain Adept-level ghost earlier.

Although it was very chaotic, there was always some useful intelligence within.

Especially the fates of the other two Ancestors.

"The Nether Origin Great Power has fallen; we are free!"

This sentence had been spread by a certain Ascendant-level ghost. Any ghost with a shred of awareness knew this news.

This was the reason why the Nether Abyss was now so chaotic.

Without the Nether Origin Sect, without the suppression of a Great Power, this place had become a sea of chaos.

And Luo Chen was still sighing with emotion over the fate of Heaven Abyss Ancestor.

He had never imagined that the old man, who seemed rather kind-faced, would choose to feed himself to the ghosts, opening the Nether Abyss's restrictions in an attempt to shatter jade and stone together with Rosycloud.

But the result seemed to be one that would make Heaven Abyss Ancestor die with his eyes open.

Rosycloud had ultimately escaped.

Not only that, but during the great battle in the Nether Abyss, those powerful ghostly existences that the little ghost Luo Chen had soul-searched had once prostrated themselves in worship had almost all come to a bad end.

Dead, wounded, or fled.

Although many still lived, they were hiding in various corners of the Nether Abyss, licking their wounds.

"That woman is truly unreasonably strong!"

Luo Chen sighed, then began to ponder his current predicament.

Forcing his way across the Nether Abyss was absolutely impossible.

He didn't have such profound mana reserves to traverse hundreds of thousands of li. Even an Ascendant Great Power probably couldn't do it.

And on the sea surface, the technique Rosycloud had used before leaving had spread an incalculable amount of Nirvana Sacred Flame throughout the sea, temporarily suppressing these evil ghosts.

It wouldn't dissipate anytime soon.

"Can the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire break through Rosycloud's Nirvana Sacred Flame?"

Luo Chen murmured to himself. In his Purple Mansion, the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit seemed to sense something and sent out a signal of fear.

"You can't do it either?"

Luo Chen shook his head and considered other methods.

In the end, his thoughts settled on the strand of Nirvana Sacred Flame he had refined.

"If I can strengthen it a bit, could it become a tool for me?"

In the past, Luo Chen naturally wouldn't have had this idea.

Strengthening a source true flame could only be done by nurturing it with mana, requiring the accumulated effort of days and months.

Or, one could find corresponding food for the source true flame to accelerate its growth.

For example, the Withering-Bloom True Flame had been nurtured by the Burning Incense Valley in the Yan Alliance in its early years. After Luo Chen cultivated the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture*, he hunted demon beasts for it in the Northern Sea, absorbing life force, and even devoured large amounts of high-grade source-less fires.

But none of this worked for the Nirvana Sacred Flame.

Its own rank was too high; it simply wasn't interested in those low-grade source-less fires.

Unless it was something like the fifth-order Withering-Bloom Divine Fire, the Vermilion Bird Divine Fire in the Blazing Refinement Prison, or even the Nine-Refined Beacon Fire within the Heaven-Refining Cauldron—only then would it have an appetite.

But now, Luo Chen had a little bit of capital.

He shook his hand!

A pill appeared in his palm.

The pill was formed in black and white, containing the two poles of water and fire within, with a hint of yin-yang rhythm flowing over it.

It was precisely the Two Extremities Pill that Luo Chen had refined in the third level of the Alchemy Saint's Trial!

"Since I refined it using you, using it on you now can be considered putting it to good use."

Without hesitation, Luo Chen swallowed the Two Extremities Pill.

Mana circulated, and he began to refine the pill's medicinal power.

A stream of powerful medicinal energy poured out from the pill. Guided by his mana, it entered his Purple Mansion, but instead of heading toward his Nascent Soul, it landed on the small golden bird perched on the green-white fire tree.

In an instant.

The small golden bird bathed in black and white light, comfortably squinting its eyes.

Seeing this, Luo Chen's heart was stirred.

"It works!"

Next, he just had to wait for the Nirvana Sacred Flame to fully absorb the medicinal power of the Two Extremities Pill and see to what extent it could grow.

One day.

Two days.

Three days...

Time passed slowly.

Although the medicinal power of the Two Extremities Pill was vast, it shouldn't take this long to absorb.

Luo Chen realized something might be wrong.

"Where is the problem?"

Just as Luo Chen was pondering deeply, a furious thought came from his Purple Mansion one day.

His Nascent Soul trembled, and he opened his eyes to look.

The small golden bird had woken up at some point and was lowering its head to peck at the branches and leaves of the green fire tree beneath it.

The furious thought came from the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit.

At this moment, the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit was enraged. Green and white flames suddenly burst forth, wanting to kill the small golden bird.

The small golden bird was unafraid. Although its aura was weaker, it dodged and weaved through the onslaught of green-white flames, even occasionally pecking a mouthful of green-white flame into its body, making its golden light even more dazzling.

Luo Chen was stunned. He could feel two thoughts.

One was the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit's shock and anger. It seemed to have never expected that the little creature it had humbled itself to raise as a nest would actually want to eat it!

The other was the first dawning thought of the Nirvana Fire Spirit—hunger!

"But no matter how hungry, you can't eat the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire!"

Luo Chen was anxious!

He couldn't just watch the divine fire he had painstakingly cultivated for hundreds of years be devoured by another little creature, nor could he sit idly by while the two divine fires fought in his Purple Mansion.

With a thought, his mana wrapped around the Nirvana Fire Spirit.

No matter how it struggled or resisted, Luo Chen first moved it out of his Purple Mansion.

This one was the most restless!

Only after separating them did Luo Chen breathe a sigh of relief.

"You almost tore the house down!"

Luo Chen sent a reprimanding thought to each of them.

The green-white little tree could only feel wronged, drooping its branches and leaves, sulking in silence.

The small golden bird flapped its wings, completely unaware of Luo Chen's anger, only chirping ignorantly.

"Hungry!"

"Hungry!"

Luo Chen frowned as he watched this scene. The Two Extremities Pill had apparently fully awakened the fire spirit of the Nirvana Sacred Flame, greatly increasing its spirituality.

But what was this hunger all about?

Just as his mind was churning through the information about the Two Extremities Pill recorded in the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's alchemy manual, the golden fire bird flying in the sky suddenly paused, then flew out under Luo Chen's bewildered gaze.

Luo Chen curiously followed.

In a moment, he stared in surprise at a scene.

The golden fire bird was pacing in the flowing magma, carefully pecking at another flame that was even more brilliant and powerful than itself.

Luo Chen swallowed his saliva.

That was a strand of Nirvana Sacred Flame that had seeped in before the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land's gate had fully closed.

There wasn't much of it, scattered in various places.

And because Black Marsh Ancestor had suddenly barged in, the gate had closed, cutting off the subsequent seepage of Nirvana Sacred Flame.

But the portion that had entered earlier still remained within the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land.

Luo Chen had never had time to deal with it, nor did he know how to.

But now!

"Using this as food... is that really okay?"

Luo Chen hesitated.

...

"Is this Shangyou Immortal City?"

After the great battle at Lingtian Pass, Shouyang Mountain Hermit, whose physical body had been injured, had quickly repaired his dharma body with the help of the sect.

This was his first time leaving the mountain in many years.

But when he arrived at a familiar place, the sights and scenes before him were astonishing.

The East Yang Sect disciple beside him quickly replied, "Reporting to Elder, this is indeed where Shangyou Immortal City is located, but why it has become like this, this disciple does not know."

Shouyang Mountain Hermit swallowed his saliva and looked out. The entire city was in ruins.

Among the broken walls and collapsed buildings, flames surged, blazing hot.

A massive palm print almost covered the entire Shangyou Immortal City. Those broken walls and collapsed buildings were merely the bits and pieces revealed between the fingers.

"Who destroyed Shangyou Immortal City?"

"And with just one strike!"

No one could answer him.

And even more shocking things were yet to come.

"Elder Lu, the Grand Elder requests your presence!"

Shouyang Mountain Hermit composed himself and nervously headed toward the Nether Abyss.

When he arrived, the three senior brothers and sisters of the sect had already been standing there for a long time, all silent.

The leader wore a set of sunrise brocade robes and a rosy cloud crown on his head.

He was the Grand Elder of the East Yang Sect, and also a renowned late-Sage Grand Cultivator of the Eastern Wilderness.

Before Shouyang Mountain Hermit could even offer his greetings, his gaze froze again.

The Nether Abyss was boiling, covering ten thousand li.

Golden flames swirled, dazzling and piercing.

In the void, black dragon-snakes would suddenly appear, then abruptly vanish.

Anyone with a bit of knowledge would understand what those things were.

Space cracks!

Boom!

A thunderbolt fell, exploding on the sea surface, splashing countless waves and sparks.

Above the nine heavens, dark clouds lingered, never dispersing, with lightning flickering within from time to time.

Because of this falling thunderbolt, everyone seemed to wake from a great dream.

The East Yang Sect Grand Elder slowly turned around, his dignified face now full of terror.

"The Nether Origin Sacred Land... has been destroyed!"

The others were about to ask who had done it, when Shouyang Mountain Hermit suddenly took out a sound transmission jade talisman.

An urgent voice came from within, interrupting everyone's attention.

"The million great mountains—the demon race is stirring. They seem to be returning in force!"

(Chapter End)
