# Chapter 551: Breaking Formations and Drowning Islands, Fishing in Troubled Waters

"This is a Ring-Headed Turtle?"

In the secrecy of an unnamed underground cavern, Luo Chen stared in surprise at the creature prostrated before him.

A turtle's body, a serpent's head, and a neck impossibly long and slender.

When he channeled his mana to prod it, the beast was forced to extend that neck fully — and at its greatest stretch, it was long enough to coil once around its own body.

"Is this a turtle or a giant snake?!"

Luo Chen was taken aback. Even to his senses, there was something about the Ring-Headed Turtle that faintly recalled the legendary divine beast of myth — the Black Tortoise.

The difference was that the mythological Black Tortoise was depicted as a tortoise and a serpent intertwined, two creatures existing in tandem.

This Ring-Headed Turtle, by contrast, wore a tortoise's shell atop a serpent's head — an uncomfortable blend of both.

Black King, who had shrunk himself down beside him, spoke up: "Master, lower-tier Ring-Headed Turtles are all like this. Only by the third tier does the difference emerge."

"Oh? Change at the third tier?"

The specimen before him was a first-tier Ring-Headed Turtle he had quietly captured recently — low in rank, barely a flicker of intelligence.

He had assumed demon beast lineages would simply vary by cultivation level, not undergo genuine transformation as they progressed.

Black King flicked his tongue and replied: "Unlike ordinary demon beasts with muddled bloodlines, Ring-Headed Turtles are a true ancient-blooded strain, traceable to a genuine ancestral lineage. According to the elders of my Black-Scale Giant Python clan, Ring-Headed Turtles carry both the Black Tortoise and Cliff Serpent bloodlines. Dilute though they may be, their bloodline concentration still far exceeds our own ordinary demon kin."

"As their realm rises, they refine their bloodlines through their demon power, gradually purifying one bloodline to its utmost, slowly drawing closer to the image of their ancestors."

"The third tier is the threshold. At that point, a Ring-Headed Turtle will truly commit to one path of cultivation, in preparation for the transformation stage."

Years at Luo Chen's side had left Black King fluent in the human tongue.

He spoke now with measured clarity, organized and articulate.

Luo Chen listened quietly, nodding from time to time.

Purifying the bloodline, reaching back toward ancestral power — that was precisely how demon beast cultivation worked.

Black Tortoise and Cliff Serpent were both legendary archaic demons of the Mountains and Seas Realm.

With those two bloodlines as their guiding paths, Ring-Headed Turtles enjoyed a far cleaner cultivation direction than bloodline-muddled kin like Black-Scale Giant Pythons or demon crabs.

Rubbing his chin thoughtfully, Luo Chen mused: "So the legendary Xuanyan Demon Emperor, when it underwent transformation, must have already refined its bloodline to the ultimate extreme — achieving a full Black Tortoise form."

His gaze drifted down to the Ring-Headed Turtle's shell.

One wondered whether such a carapace could be used to refine Black Emperor Paste, and if so, what kind of result it might produce.

But the thought was quickly set aside.

Refining Black Emperor Paste from demon crab shells had required careful preparation and repeated experiments with supporting ingredients before it came to fruition.

Switching the primary material would inevitably mean switching all the supporting materials too.

Far too much trouble.

His current priorities lay elsewhere.

"Black King, Tian Xuan — what is the situation outside right now?"

"Reporting to Master: the five great royal demon crab clans have assembled in full around Xuanyan Island, ten million soldiers strong, and by all signs they intend to wipe out the Ring-Headed Turtle clan entirely." Black King answered.

Hearing that number, Luo Chen couldn't help but twitch.

He had known the demon crab clans were enormous in number.

After all, their reproductive capacity was extraordinary — even surpassing humanity, itself famed for proliferation.

That was one of the main reasons he had come to the Xuanyan Sea Region to hunt demon crabs in the first place.

*You lot breed that many crabs, and I'm just taking a few to refine into medicine — surely that won't attract notice?*

But he hadn't expected the numbers to be *this* staggering.

Ten million soldiers.

Even if ninety-nine percent of them were negligible first-tier demon crabs, sheer numbers could bury anyone. Ants, in enough numbers, chew an elephant to death.

Five great royal clans working together — the Ring-Headed Turtles were in grave peril.

"Master!"

Tian Xuan's clear voice chimed out, bearing news that made Luo Chen blink.

"I found something. Those human cultivators — they seem to have allied with the demon crabs."

...

Over the vast and rolling sea, the sounds of battle shook heaven and sea alike.

Luo Chen, Concealment Veil Formation fully activated, leaked not a trace of his presence — hidden within the surging currents of the deep.

His keen eyes blazed with spiritual light, piercing through blood-dyed water to scan in every direction.

Countless crab soldiers and crab generals, led by one third-tier demon crab king after another, launched assault after assault on island after island.

On each island, Ring-Headed Turtle warriors held their ground, locked in a battle to the death, refusing to yield a single step.

Though hopelessly outnumbered, the islands had been woven into the grand formation over years of Ring-Headed Turtle occupation, drawing on every vein of spiritual qi in the terrain. By cycling through offensive and defensive rotations with the formation's power, the turtle warriors had managed to hold the line.

For the moment, the scales were balanced.

But in Luo Chen's eyes, that balance was tipping as living strength bled away.

He watched a while, a frown of regret forming on his face.

"These three islands — they'll fall soon."

He could see it plainly: the Ring-Headed Turtles had a grand formation, but the number of warriors who could wield it like an extension of their own arms was vanishingly small.

Given the absolute numerical disparity, defeat was only a matter of time.

Unless Xuanyan Island itself sent reinforcements.

Reinforcements?

He dared not extend his divine sense, lest he draw the attention of someone whose divine soul foundations ran deeper than his own.

But his exceptional eyes could see far.

In the direction of Xuanyan Island, several enormous demon clouds were billowing toward him.

Only second-tier demon beasts and above could ride demon clouds in flight.

And only third-tier demon kings could command clouds of such breadth and mass.

The Ring-Headed Turtles had dispatched third-tier demon kings as relief forces.

Yet before the clouds could draw anywhere near the three embattled islands, a dozen streaks of escape-light burst out from the surrounding waters to intercept.

Battle was joined on the spot.

And within just half the time it takes a stick of incense to burn, the outcome was clear.

Five great royal clans, waiting in readiness — they could never allow relief forces to breach their encirclement so easily.

Shortly after, the handful of lights that survived the outer battle were forced to retreat toward Xuanyan Island.

Back on the three island battlegrounds, the sounds of slaughter slowly dimmed.

Then a roar of defiance erupted, and several beams of escape-light burst simultaneously from all three islands, trying to break through.

But this time, there was no escaping for the Ring-Headed Turtle warriors.

Several nearby demon crab kings moved as one, suppressing them back onto the island battlefields.

"How many times has it been now?"

The scene felt both familiar and strange, as though from another life.

Luo Chen watched from the shadows, inwardly unsettled.

He hadn't been surprised by the five royal clans acting in concert — he had expected a mindless flood tide once battle broke out.

What he hadn't expected was how methodical their formation-breaking had proven.

Divide and annihilate. Besiege the point, intercept the relief. Press from outside inward, advancing step by calculated step.

The so-called Nine Palaces Eight Trigrams Formation was losing ground under the demon crabs' relentless erosion. Nearly thirty islands had already fallen — just past the halfway mark.

Lose half, and the entire structure would collapse.

Then the demon crabs would only need to drive straight in, punch through the inner thirty-six islands, and strike directly at Xuanyan.

"This is absolutely not how demon beasts fight."

Luo Chen drew a slow breath, and a name surfaced in his mind.

Gu Shaoshang.

Tian Xuan's earlier intelligence had been clear: a demon king from the Demon Spider Crab clan had personally boarded the Feiyun Ravine demon-hunting ship, and when they departed, Gu Shaoshang had gone with them. When he returned, he came alone — but without a scratch.

And the communication between the two sides after that had been frequent.

What did it all mean?

Feiyun Ravine and the demon crabs of the Xuanyan Sea Region had allied.

Together, they were targeting the Ring-Headed Turtle clan of Xuanyan Island.

As Luo Chen saw it, only Gu Shaoshang could have commanded this vast crab army with such orderly precision — patient and unhurried, grinding down the formation piece by piece.

Thinking of the man, Luo Chen felt a flicker of unease.

This complicated his plan to fish in troubled waters — to quietly snatch a few heavily wounded third-tier demon crab kings for himself.

The way things were going, there was a very real chance he would spend all this time watching the royal crabs carry off their spoils, while he went home empty-handed.

At the thought, he found himself muttering that name again.

"Gu Shaoshang..."

What kind of offer could have convinced a human cultivator to ally with demon beasts?

And wasn't the man afraid the demon crabs would eventually go back on their word?

With the absolute power gap, Feiyun Ravine's seven — no, six — Adept cultivators couldn't hope to make so much as a ripple.

...

On one island.

Five demon crab kings radiating powerful auras had gathered.

Then a streak of sword-light tore through the sky.

"It's Golden Claw!"

*Thud.*

A corpse the size of a small mountain was flung to the ground.

Golden Claw grinned savagely: "Reporting to the patriarch — Longevity Three Islands have been taken. The formation master Huan Shou's body is here."

Jin Hun looked at the massive turtle carcass and nodded in satisfaction.

"Well done. Rest for now — when we move on the inner thirty-six islands, we'll need you again."

"And the body?"

"You killed it. It's yours."

Golden Claw let out a low laugh, swept a provocative gaze across the other four, and strutted off in triumph.

As his taunting eyes swept past, all four demon crab patriarchs' expressions shifted.

When he was gone, Red Flame snorted coldly: "Jin Hun, that Golden Claw of yours grows more insufferable by the day."

Nine Claw gave an equally cold harrumph. "The ignorant brat — does he truly think we are so old and feeble we couldn't handle him?"

Zhu Xiang of the Demon Spider Crabs said nothing, but as he watched Golden Claw's retreating figure, a hint of envy glimmered in his eyes.

The Overlord Crab clan produced the strong in wave after wave.

Golden Claw's battle power was barely a step below the five patriarchs themselves.

He was already, by unspoken consensus, the next Overlord Crab patriarch. The Demon Spider Crab clan, by comparison, had found itself between generations — talent thin, prospects uncertain.

*Little Seven...*

Jin Hun made no reply to the two demons' provocations. Instead, he spoke quietly: "The outer thirty-six islands are essentially ours. Next comes the inner thirty-six, and that will be a hard fight."

Lüxiu nodded. "The Ring-Headed Turtle clan's full strength is concentrated in the inner islands — a brutal battle is unavoidable. Still, with Fellow Daoist Gu's assistance, breaking the formation and taking the island is only a matter of time. Elder Jin, there is no need for concern."

Jin Hun shook his head. "That, I do not worry about. What occupies my mind right now is something else."

As he spoke, his gaze came to rest — by some unspoken accord — on Zhu Xiang of the Demon Spider Crabs.

"Zhu Xiang, if word spreads that our demon clans joined hands with human cultivators to attack the Ring-Headed Turtles — I fear the name of Xuanyan's demon crabs will be forever fouled throughout the Northern Sea."

Zhu Xiang fell silent.

The other three demons reacted at once.

Using Gu Shaoshang to break the Nine Palaces Eight Trigrams Formation, seizing the legacy troves of Xuanyan — that was for the future of all five royal clans.

But if the five royal clans' reputation rotted from within demon-kind... even if a crab emperor truly arose someday, those days would not be easy.

For a moment, every gaze converged on Zhu Xiang.

A long pause.

Then old, unhurried words emerged from his lips.

"This alliance was for mutual benefit. Nothing more."

"The day we strike Xuanyan Island is the day the cooperation ends."

"On that day, 'Fellow Daoist Gu Shaoshang' will cease to exist. In his place: the villain who invaded the Xuanyan Sea Region and brutally slaughtered fellow demon-kind — Gu Shaoshang."

"Such a man — we must kill him together."

At those words, faces around him lit with quiet satisfaction.

And at that very moment, a streak of sword-light came cutting through the sky.

A flying sword bearing a message?

Jin Hun reached out and caught the small blade. A transmission embedded within it flowed into his mind.

Before he had even finished reading, his expression had already turned to fury and shock.

"Human cultivators — treacherous and contemptible indeed!"

...

A demon-hunting ship, borne on a strong wind, raced away from the Xuanyan Sea Region at tremendous speed.

The young woman Gu Xiaolian looked back at the waters behind them, then turned to the young man at her side with a puzzled frown.

"Brother, the cooperation isn't over yet, is it?"

Gu Shaoshang smiled. "I've already given them the formation-breaking method. How they implement the rest is up to them — I can hardly go on directing an army of demon beasts forever. Not exactly a tenable position."

That made sense. Legitimacy would have been an issue.

Gu Xiaolian understood.

But then her gaze drifted to the mountain of Ring-Headed Turtle corpses piled across the ship's deck, and uncertainty crept back in.

"We've spent months collecting over a hundred second-tier Ring-Headed Turtle bodies and five third-tier ones. The number is large, but it's still short of the nine-turtle count Father requested by quite a margin. How do we explain that to Father?"

At this, the smile on Gu Shaoshang's face slowly faded.

"Don't worry. I'll handle the loose ends myself."

Gu Xiaolian blinked.

"Brother, do you mean to say..."

Gu Shaoshang nodded. "That battlefield is far too dangerous, and we're isolated out here with no backup. I couldn't be certain they wouldn't betray us in the end. You all go ahead — I'll head back once more and collect the goods our agreement promised."

"But alone, Brother?" Gu Xiaolian's face creased with worry.

Gu Shaoshang let out a cold laugh. "No matter. Even if I can't best them outright, Father has given me the means to ensure they can't hold me. I'll have no trouble leaving."

Feiyunzi, lord of Feiyun Ravine, was a genuine Sage Perfected Being.

With the trump cards his father had given him, even an ordinary third-tier demon king couldn't hold him.

That was the foundation of Gu Shaoshang's confidence.

He instructed a few of his fellow disciples to escort Gu Xiaolian and deliver the shipload of materials back to Feiyun Ravine — then leapt upward, transforming into a streak of escape-light, and slipped back into the Xuanyan Sea Region.

...

Half a month later.

On a massive island, the sounds of battle were the only music.

Wave after wave of low-tier demon crabs surged from the sea to make landfall, encircling the island on all sides.

Seven Red Rock Crab kings moved in concert at the vanguard, their attacks directed at the Ring-Headed Turtle warriors holding the position.

The battle burned white-hot.

Perhaps sensing that survival itself was at stake, the Ring-Headed Turtles fought back with ever-fiercer desperation, drawing on ley-line spiritual qi without restraint.

Despite their disadvantage in numbers, the formation's power granted them parity with the Red Rock Crab clan's strongest fighters.

*Parity?*

Yes — from the initial rout, the battle had stabilized into something close to equal footing.

The strange reversal came partly from the Ring-Headed Turtles' do-or-die resistance, and partly from the demon crabs' own fractured command structure.

Where before they had fought in joint formation, the five great royal clans had now reverted to single-clan units, each unwilling to expose their backs to the others.

But that caution came at a price. Without the synergy between the five clans' different abilities, the battles grew steadily harder.

The fighting expanded outward.

From the islands to the sea itself — demon qi boiling, surging in uncontrolled waves.

And this was not confined to a single island. The same scene played out in every direction.

On one stretch of open sea —

*BOOM.*

With a thunderous crash, a massive Red Rock Crab was sent plunging into the sea.

It had fallen. Slain by a third-tier Ring-Headed Turtle.

The instant its body hit the water, streaks of fire burst across it.

The Ring-Headed Turtle warrior gasped for breath. "It tried to self-destruct — thank goodness..."

Beneath the sea's surface, Luo Chen laced Blazing Fire Miasma through the water to mask all perception. Both hands reached through to seize the body of the fallen third-tier Red Rock Crab.

"My luck holds."

"The first two times, I had to ambush my targets. This one I got for free — already dead. The shell of a third-tier demon king is still serviceable for refining even after death."

Pleased, he tucked the corpse into his storage ring.

He looked upward through the billowing curtain of Blazing Fire Miasma at the thick-shelled Ring-Headed Turtle above.

A Ring-Headed Turtle demon king who had committed to the Black Tortoise path.

And gravely wounded.

Should he...?

Just as he wavered, his pupils snapped to pinpoints.

A figure blinked into existence from the void above — one hand closing in a grab, threads converging from every direction like cables drawn suddenly taut.

In a single instant, the unprepared Ring-Headed Turtle demon king was dead.

As the figure gathered the remains, his gaze fell to the water below.

And for a moment, his brow furrowed ever so slightly.

*(End of Chapter)*
