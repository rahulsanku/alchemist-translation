# Chapter 898: The Pill Saint Hall Opens, Five Sects Enter

*Creak…*

The ancient gate, as if sealed for ages, let out an indescribably grating sound when pushed open.

And this was merely a side hall.

The moment he stepped inside, Luo Chen stood frozen on the spot.

The interior space soared as high as the heavens, with bookshelves rising layer upon layer like stairways to a celestial kingdom. On those shelves, half-illusory, half-solid scriptures were arranged in neat categories.

For the first time, Luo Chen vividly understood the phrase "so many books they fill a house to overflowing."

A mere side hall, and it could store so many Daoist alchemy classics?

Even Luo Chen, who had experienced the alchemy inheritance of Devil Lord Heaven-Refining, could not help but marvel inwardly at the accumulation of Pill Saint Chu Yan.

He reached out with a hand, and from the nearest black bookshelf, a volume that seemed condensed from white clouds flew over.

Before he could even open it, a soft exclamation of surprise rang through the hall.

"Oh?"

"This book?"

Luo Chen stroked the volume carefully. It felt as if it were alive.

His Crimson-Eyed Golden Pupils looked down from above, and the cloud qi swirling around the book could no longer conceal its true nature.

Luo Chen slowly raised the volume, focusing his gaze.

"This is actually a pill!"

Unexpectedly, this book, which appeared outwardly to be a conventional scripture, was in essence a pill.

The reason it had that external form was that someone had used exceptionally masterful illusion techniques, borrowing abundant spiritual qi to manifest an illusion, thus deceiving outsiders.

"The methods of Pill Saint Chu Yan?"

"She had such a superb illusion skill?"

In the cultivation world, illusion arts were often confused with soul arts, but in truth, they were completely different.

The essence of illusion was manipulating the five senses—making what one saw, heard, smelled, touched, or even tasted produce false judgments.

Soul arts, on the other hand, were fundamental methods targeting the primordial spirit. Even if one sealed off the five senses, they were still hard to guard against.

Take Luo Chen's early proud achievement, "Mirror Flower Water Moon." In essence, it was merely a water-type illusion art, using spiritual qi flow to misjudge everything the eyes saw.

All that is seen is but illusion, nothing more than a mirror flower, a water moon!

It was precisely because of this fundamental difference that even when Luo Chen later tried to optimize this art toward soul arts, its upper limit of power remained insufficient.

Against those of lower cultivation, it was invincible.

When facing peers, a sudden execution could also have miraculous effects.

But against those of higher cultivation, it often failed, even backfiring and injuring his own primordial spirit.

This flaw only improved slightly after Luo Chen later created the soul art "Ghosts and Gods Give Way," drawing inspiration from the desolate beast Ghost Immortal Tower.

However, "Ghosts and Gods Give Way" was based on self-defense, misleading a cultivator's divine sense and visual judgment. Therefore, when facing great demon emperors like Huang Man and Bi Zheng, whose primordial spirits were not inferior to his own, it was insufficient. During battle, he basically never considered using this move.

But that was a digression.

At this moment, the illusion techniques left behind by Pill Saint Chu Yan had clearly reached an extraordinary level.

Using the pill itself as a foundation, borrowing the hand of spiritual qi, anyone who touched this pill—whether through the tactile sensation in their hand, the scene before their eyes, or even lowering their head to smell it—would find it difficult to detect its true essence.

It was only because Luo Chen had this pair of long-cultivated Crimson-Eyed Golden Pupils that he could realize the truth in such a short time.

"Leaving behind a batch of pills, why go to such trouble?"

"Is it showing off, or is there another purpose?"

Holding the "pill book," Luo Chen fell into contemplation.

But this contemplation lasted only a moment before Luo Chen laughed to himself.

He had come here to understand the Pill Saint Hall trial in advance, the same principle as cramming before an exam. Why bother thinking about the materials and techniques the examiner used to create the study materials?

Shaking his head, Luo Chen lowered his head and began to flip through the pill book.

The pill in his hand was not unfamiliar to him—it was merely the most common first-grade pill, the Qi-Nourishing Pill.

It was also the pill Luo Chen had consumed the most during his Qi Refinement period.

The pill book recorded everything about the Qi-Nourishing Pill!

From principles to materials, to refining techniques, heat requirements, and even the appearance, color, and storage taboos after formation—all were listed in detail.

Such thoroughness! If it fell into the hands of someone with even a little alchemy talent, obtaining this book would be no less than having a famous teacher guide them.

Coupled with a large number of refining attempts, they would surely enter the door with ease.

Luo Chen had never seen anyone summarize a low-grade pill so meticulously. Therefore, even though the grade was not high, he read with great interest.

But no matter how interesting, it was, after all, only a first-grade pill. Luo Chen soon skimmed through it at a glance.

He looked up at the tens of thousands of pill books.

"Are these all study materials?"

"I was a bit too arrogant earlier, putting off scanning the questions."

"There are only a few months left until the Pill Saint Hall trial begins. Should I still be able to make it in time?"

Luo Chen had some doubts in his words, but his heart was full of confidence.

For alchemists at their level—alchemy grandmasters—quantity was never the problem. The problem was high-grade pills that exceeded their own alchemy skill.

Especially for Luo Chen, whose alchemy foundation had been tested by Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's Pill Realm.

Therefore, he was not too worried.

One pill book after another entered his hands. The specific details of each type of pill poured into his mind like seawater flooding in.

Days passed one by one. The unread pill books in the side hall grew fewer and fewer.

From the vast ocean of first-grade pills to the handful of fourth-grade pills, Luo Chen's understanding grew deeper and deeper.

Stuffing so much into his brain in a short time would have overwhelmed an ordinary person under the impact of such vast knowledge.

But Luo Chen felt no discomfort.

Whether it was his tolerance for pain or his vast primordial spirit, both were enough to support him in cramming large amounts of knowledge.

Not to mention, in this rote-reading process, although Luo Chen seemed to gain little, he actually benefited quite a bit.

Along his journey, his alchemy skills were mostly self-taught, with little guidance from famous masters.

The phrase "wild path" was a mockery, but also the truth.

This situation only improved slightly after Luo Chen came into contact with the alchemy of Azure Pill Valley, and was completely reversed after he passed the Pill Realm trial and obtained Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's alchemy inheritance.

But there were hidden drawbacks here as well.

Azure Pill Valley's alchemy was too low-level, mostly first and second grade.

Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's alchemy inheritance was too mixed and too ancient.

It was said to span the three races of humans, demons, and devils, but an ordinary alchemist mastering one school would be enough to be called a master or saint. How could anyone master them all?

Even Luo Chen only sought the knowledge useful to him, absorbing it bit by bit.

And the word "ancient" was no joke.

Among Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's inheritances, the shortest alchemy knowledge was at least a thousand years old.

How intelligent were cultivators? Qi refinement methods and alchemy techniques complemented each other, changing with each passing day. If Luo Chen clung to his old knowledge, he would inevitably be eliminated by the new mainstream alchemy.

The pill books left by Chu Yan were all from within the last thousand years. Through massive reading, Luo Chen's last weakness was gradually filled.

...

Five months later!

Luo Chen walked out of the side hall. Ye Lingtian, who had made an appointment, was waiting for him outside.

"Are you confident?"

Luo Chen pointed to his head. "It's all in here."

Ye Lingtian breathed a sigh of relief.

But Luo Chen's next sentence lifted his heart again.

"But whether it works depends on the specific content of the trial."

Ye Lingtian could only smile bitterly. "I don't know what Junior Sister Chu Yan was thinking. The last three trials are extremely difficult. Although the first trial is the simplest, it changes every time. Otherwise, I wouldn't have let you study hard for five months, using painstaking effort to deal with the first trial."

Luo Chen nodded, deeply agreeing.

If it were before, he would never have believed that the first trial's questions would change in a trial held once every hundred years.

What ordinary cultivator had such means?

But after witnessing the Pill Saint's illusion techniques, he could no longer doubt.

Her alchemy skills were transcendent, and her illusions were enough to pass off falsehoods as truth. Combining the two, what difficulty was there in setting up some small alchemy tests?

Of course, such arrangements must be extremely delicate and could not withstand violent destruction.

This was probably why the inheritance had been left vacant within the sect, and the three great ancestors of the Nether Origin Sect had not forcibly taken it!

"The first trials of the previous three times were respectively appearance, materials, and medicinal effects. I wonder what it will be this time?" Luo Chen murmured, a hint of curiosity on his slightly tired face.

Ye Lingtian waved his hand. "Go back and rest well first. In a couple of days, when the time is ripe and the trial fully begins, you'll naturally know."

"Mm."

Luo Chen nodded slightly and subconsciously looked back at the Pill Saint Hall shrouded in clouds and mist.

His Crimson-Eyed Golden Pupils flashed, and he vaguely saw another wisp of spiritual mechanism slip into the Pill Saint Hall.

It seemed that to activate the inheritance trial left by the Pill Saint, this spiritual mechanism was an indispensable part.

Reasoning backward, when Pill Saint Chu Yan, who had set up these strict trial conditions, was at the Sage realm, she could already attract spiritual mechanisms?

Thinking of this, Luo Chen felt a chill inwardly.

The spiritual mechanisms of the universe were profound and obscure, vast and indistinct. Even Ascendant cultivators found it difficult to easily attract them. Before leaving the Nether Origin Sect, Pill Saint Chu Yan was only at the Sage realm. How could she have achieved that step?

Suppressing the doubts in his heart, Luo Chen returned to Welcoming Immortal Peak.

Half a year had passed, and Luo Lingxi's appearance had not changed much. She was still that thin, weak, fair, and clean little girl, with almost no growth.

Under the guidance of that gentle woman from the Ye family, Luo Lingxi happily called out, "Father."

Luo Chen rubbed the little one's head, and the thought uncontrollably arose again.

"In the future, should I implement the Valley Immortal Enfeoffment method?"

...

Three days passed in the blink of an eye.

Under the secret anticipation of many, the main hall of the Pill Saint Hall opened in a desolate scene.

When Luo Chen arrived, there were only a few people around.

He swept his gaze over them and immediately understood the situation.

The one in white-patterned ink robes was Chen Mozi.

The one in golden-threaded cloud robes was Xin Yisheng.

The one surrounded by several cultivators embroidered with dragon and phoenix patterns, like stars surrounding the moon, was Lu Chun.

The last to arrive, but with an urgent expression, was a woman—she must be Chu Yu.

Luo Chen's gaze lingered longest on Chu Yu, because she gave him a familiar feeling, just like that woman who had stepped into the Star Gate back then.

Was this the descendant of Pill Saint Chu Yan?

While he was sizing them up, they were naturally sizing him up too.

Chen Mozi thought to himself, "He really is young!"

Xin Yisheng revealed a smile. "He only stayed in the side hall for five months. We all stayed for at least a full year. He probably didn't learn much. Forget the last three trials—I'm afraid the first trial alone will make him retreat. This kid is nothing to fear!"

Lu Chun's face showed obvious shock, because in the activated state of the Pill Saint Hall, a large amount of spiritual pressure surrounded the cultivators, making it impossible for anyone to conceal their cultivation level.

And the cultivation level Luo Chen revealed was at least Sage Fifth Layer!

Among the five grandmasters, he stood alone at the top!

For a moment, Lu Chun's heart grew uneasy. No matter what others said, in his view, the Pill Saint Hall's inheritance trial heavily depended on the alchemist's own cultivation level.

Luo Chen had the highest cultivation level—didn't that mean he had the greatest chance?

Facing Luo Chen's gaze, Chu Yu lowered her head at first—this was the instinctive reaction of the Chu family cultivators when facing Nether Origin Sect disciples after their ancestor, the Pill Saint, had left.

But thinking of what she planned to do today, she stubbornly raised her head again and glared fiercely at Luo Chen.

"The ancestor's inheritance should naturally be taken by the descendants. How can it be entrusted to you?"

Amidst this undercurrent of tension, a low, heavy voice suddenly descended from the sky.

"Ten years!"

"You only have ten years!"

"After ten years, the Pill Saint Hall will automatically close. At that time, whether or not you obtain the inheritance, you will be forcibly expelled."

"Do not disappoint the sect's expectations of you."

It was the voice of Nether Origin Sect's sect leader, Ancestor Imperial Nether.

But no one sought the source of the voice, because the main gate of the Pill Saint Hall was slowly opening.

Five figures, without distinction of front or back, simultaneously slipped into the hall.

Outside the hall, it suddenly fell silent.

Someone quietly asked, "Will this time succeed?"

There was no answer.

High in the sky, on Mount Yanfu, Ancestor Imperial Nether withdrew his attention.

Attracting spiritual mechanisms was also a very difficult task for Ascendant cultivators.

Unless one released their primordial spirit, recklessly communicating with the origin qi of heaven and earth, exploring the vast ocean for spiritual mechanisms.

But doing so, no matter how strong the primordial spirit, would not withstand the assimilation of heaven and earth.

Therefore, to attract spiritual mechanisms, Ascendant cultivators in the Mountains and Seas Realm had only two methods.

One: during ascension, manifest the primordial spirit, and in that state of rosy cloud ascension, attract a large amount of spiritual mechanisms. Since one was about to ascend anyway, why fear the assimilation of one realm?

The second... that would depend on fifth-grade spiritual veins.

As the highest-grade spiritual veins in this realm, their value was not merely in the abundance of spiritual qi.

Such spiritual veins would spontaneously attract a small amount of spiritual mechanisms.

As long as an Ascendant cultivator was willing to expend the vein's foundation, they could borrow the hand of a fifth-grade spiritual vein to attract a considerable amount of heavenly spiritual mechanisms.

For the deeply entrenched Nether Origin Sect, such expenditure was usually bearable.

But the situation was different now!

The Nether Origin Sect's fifth-grade spiritual vein was located on Mount Yanfu, and Mount Yanfu's main function at present was to suppress and refine Qinghe Immortal.

"Ten years is the limit!" Ancestor Imperial Nether exhaled a turbid breath. He had some hope for this result, but also some pessimism.

Ancestor Heavenly Origin was puzzled. "Senior Brother seems not very optimistic about them?"

Ancestor Imperial Nether nodded. "Chen Mozi lacks talent, Xin Yisheng lacks foundation, Lu Chun's mind is distracted, and Chu Yu... is too impatient."

"Isn't there Luo Chen?" Ancestor Heize suddenly spoke.

Ancestor Imperial Nether hummed in acknowledgment. "That's why I made the decision to open the Pill Saint Hall trial early. After all, Luo Chen was favored by Chu Yan. But in the end, he came too late."

Ancestor Heavenly Origin frowned. "If not for that trip to Green Parasol Mountain, Luo Chen would have arrived ten years earlier. It would have been almost certain."

Ancestor Imperial Nether shook his head, a wizened figure flashing through his mind.

"Ten or eight years is nothing to Sage-level juniors. If we're talking about certainty, it would have to be Mr. Xuanyan. Unfortunately, he..."

"Perhaps he will also enter the Pill Saint Hall!" Heize spoke again.

"What do you mean?"

"Why do you say that, Junior Brother?"

The two great ancestors asked in unison.

Heize helplessly said, "Have you forgotten? His life-extension methods have reached their limit. And the alchemy classics he authored have already been handed over to us. Now that his end is near, wouldn't he want to see what was left behind by the one who walked before him?"

That seemed to make sense?

Ancestor Imperial Nether and Ancestor Heavenly Origin exchanged glances, a hint of expectation appearing on their faces.

And at this moment, inside the Pill Saint Hall.

Five figures suddenly stopped.

Strands of white light crisscrossed through the void, flying back and forth like living things.

A rich, mixed fragrance of pills assailed the nostrils, almost making the nose uncomfortable.

Several desks were placed in the hall, with brush, ink, paper, and inkstone laid out on them.

A line of text slowly emerged.

**[Identify the Pills by Scent, Pass with a Hundred Correct]**

Was this the first trial?

Luo Chen reached out without hesitation, grabbing toward a strand of white light.
