# Chapter 644: Black Turbid Seawater, Void Burning Wind, Where Is My Huge Sea-Overturning Demon Jiao?

"So this is the Sunken Sea?"

Within the dim and gloomy seawater, a figure walked alone.

His body emitted a complex demonic aura. If someone whose soul was not strong glanced at him, they would dazedly mistake him for a low-rank demon beast.

This person was Luo Chen.

Using the concealment arts he had learned, he had stolen the auras of several demon beasts to disguise himself. Only in this way could he smoothly pass through the surveillance of the outer demon beasts and enter the Sunken Sea directly.

As soon as he entered, he detected something wrong.

The mana barrier around his body that isolated the seawater was dissolving at a speed thirty to forty percent faster than usual.

And the farther he went into the Sunken Sea, the faster this dissolution became, forcing him to constantly send out mana to maintain the barrier.

This situation was inconceivable, but it was within Luo Chen's expectations.

As the most famous dangerous land among the Northern Sea's many perilous places, the Sunken Sea's taboos had long since spread over the years.

The most famous among them, and the one most harmful to cultivators, was this "black turbid seawater."

Within the black turbid seawater, a cultivator's body would suffer extremely strong corrosion. Only by opening a barrier and isolating inside from outside could this corrosive effect be avoided.

But the black turbid seawater was also extremely corrosive toward barriers.

Therefore, for cultivators to move within the Sunken Sea, they had to maintain a barrier at all times, and they also had to pay attention to the remaining amount of their mana or spiritual power, lest they reach the halfway point with their strength nearly exhausted and die within the Sunken Sea.

Such powerful seawater would surely be an extraordinary material in the cultivation world.

In fact, over the years, many people had indeed set their sights on the Sunken Sea's black turbid seawater and repeatedly tried to transport it out.

But no matter what secret arts or storage artifacts they used, once the black turbid seawater left the Sunken Sea's range, it would strangely lose that corrosive nature and become the most ordinary seawater.

Some people guessed that the corrosiveness of the black turbid seawater was not its own trait, but was created by the special geography of the Sunken Sea region. Others guessed that some artificial grand formation had been arranged inside. Some went even further, guessing that the Sunken Sea had evolved from weak water at the beginning of heaven and earth.

Luo Chen did not research this much. He was merely going to find that Sea-Overturning Demon Jiao.

As he advanced, Luo Chen occasionally raised his eyes to the sky.

Since the Sunken Sea's seawater was so dangerous, why not fly over from the sky?

There was also an answer to this question.

Naturally, it was because the sky was even more dangerous. That was why cultivators had no choice but to take the sea route, whose dangers were still within their control.

As for what danger existed in the sky?

Luo Chen stopped and stared fixedly upward, his eyes shining with spiritual light.

An ignorant seabird, with no demonic spirit aura and no rank, merely the most ordinary seabird, flew over the sky above the Sunken Sea at this moment.

It traveled several li safely, as if the sea below it were not the Sunken Sea that made the world pale at its mention, but an ordinary stretch of ocean.

Then suddenly!

The seabird plunged straight down from the sky, falling into the seawater. Its feathers and flesh were corroded away in an instant, becoming one part of this sea's nourishment.

Throughout the entire process, no one had attacked it.

Nor did any special phenomenon appear.

But Luo Chen knew why the seabird had died.

"Void Burning Wind!"

Luo Chen frowned tightly and spat out those four words.

This was the reason the Northern Sea's countless cultivators did not dare cross the Sunken Sea through the air.

They did not know why, and they did not even know what had attacked them.

Luo Chen had not known in the past either, but after he came into contact with Ding Yi, the other party had told him what had attacked the seabird.

A strange "wind."

It was formless and immaterial, soundless and traceless. Its appearance followed no pattern, and the strength of its power was completely uncertain, ranging from mortals below to Ascendants above.

Once struck by this wind, a living being's soul would seem to catch fire and fall into extreme chaos, causing it to lose control of its body and fall into the seawater, sinking there forever.

This intelligence had come from Primordial Demon Sect disciples and was obtained by Ding Yi.

According to rumor, the Primordial Demon Sect had spent enormous effort to research these traits. With great manpower and resources invested, they slowly found a few patterns of the Void Burning Wind.

This wind was most powerful in the air.

Within the seawater, it was weakest. It also rarely appeared in the outer regions, while the deeper one went, the more frequent the Void Burning Wind became.

Finally, there was also a corresponding method to counter this wind.

That method was a magic treasure containing Black Tortoise bloodline.

According to the Primordial Demon Sect's research, this wind was very likely formed from the corpses of large numbers of Black Tortoises buried in the Sunken Sea. Therefore, treasures with Black Tortoise bloodline could effectively resist it after the seawater weakened it.

"Although I have the Black Dust Armor on me, I am not yet prepared to face this wind. On this trip, I will not go into the Sunken Sea's inner region. I will only roam around the outer area."

Luo Chen withdrew his gaze with lingering fear, maintained his mana output, and continued swimming forward.

With Luo Chen's speed, even in water he was unaccustomed to, he advanced extremely quickly.

Around half an hour later, his figure appeared in a dark region.

This place was over a thousand zhang beneath the sea surface.

The powerful water pressure seemed as if it wanted to crush a person into powder.

Luo Chen relied on his powerful physique and felt nothing. Only the speed of his mana consumption slightly shocked him.

If he truly entered the Sunken Sea together with Ding Yi and Cave Master Moyun next time, the first thing he would have to do was refine large amounts of pills to replenish mana.

Otherwise, this constant mana consumption alone would be enough to exhaust an ordinary Golden Core cultivator's entire mana.

For now, Luo Chen did not think about this matter. He spread out his divine sense, wanting to search for traces of the Sea-Overturning Demon Jiao.

According to the demon race force distribution map, ordinary first- and second-tier demon beasts were all guarding outside the Sunken Sea. Ordinary third-tier Demon Kings were only located along the dividing line. Only a few powerful Great Demon Kings would enter the Sunken Sea alone.

Such unusual solitary behavior naturally puzzled people.

After all, if they wanted to guard the Sunken Sea and prevent the human race from interfering, they should group together and maximize their strength, rather than separate the strong and weak from one another.

Many wandering cultivators of the Myriad Immortals Assembly could not understand it.

Luo Chen had another guess.

"Could this deep-sea hill also become one of the entrances to the ancient cultivator ruins in the future?"

He murmured to himself.

According to his guess, the demon race had deployed troops at the Sunken Sea to occupy the ancient cultivator ruins.

Since that was their purpose, there was no need to guard the entire vast Sunken Sea. They only needed to guard the corresponding entrances to the ruins.

The demon race in the Arctic Yemo Heaven had obtained the Primordial Demon Sect's research legacy on the Sunken Sea. It was very possible that they had found the corresponding ruin entrances. Therefore, arranging powerful Demon Kings to occupy and guard the corresponding regions was the most logical choice.

At this moment, however, under Luo Chen's divine sense probing, he did not feel anything unusual about the deep-sea hills below.

The only oddity was that the range of his divine sense was invisibly suppressed by something. Luo Chen's vast divine sense, which could originally extend several hundred li, could at most be released several li now.

Weakened a hundredfold.

In other words, if an ordinary Golden Core cultivator without much soul foundation came here, he probably could not even investigate these few li.

"Is the Sea-Overturning Demon Jiao not here?"

Luo Chen murmured and began moving quickly.

In the end, his steps stopped above a seafloor basin.

Within his sight, a colossal creature was coiled inside the basin, unmoving.

"So you are here!"

Luo Chen smiled faintly and released his concealment art.

When his aura spread, the demon jiao in the sea slowly opened eyes as large as lanterns. Two blue vertical pupils fixed on Luo Chen.

Rustle...

Its mountain-like stacked body began to unfold, revealing a long physique. Four giant claws sprawled on the ground, and a hard dragon horn towered toward the sky.

A long exhalation stirred up many shattered stones and white bones in the sea as the demon jiao opened its mouth.

"Human, are you provoking this dragon?"

Luo Chen took a deep breath and did not answer politely. Instead, he formed a spiritual seal and struck it against the qi sea at his lower abdomen.

When this spiritual seal landed, it was as if some restriction had been released.

In an instant!

Heaven-shaking mana surged out from Luo Chen's body.

At that moment, at the bottom of the Sunken Sea, a volcano erupted with a roar.

"Seeking death!"

The Sea-Overturning Demon Jiao gave a low roar, rolled its enormous body, and charged toward Luo Chen.

Not only that, during this process, the surrounding black turbid seawater also surged in from all directions with its movements.

Seeing this scene, Luo Chen's pupils shrank. But a fierce light flashed in his eyes. The Flowing Fire Hammer landed in his hand, and instead of retreating, he advanced, likewise charging toward the Sea-Overturning Demon Jiao.

Around him, within a range of one hundred zhang, crimson mana rose like flames, forming a sea of fire prison.

The next moment, the two sides collided like heavenly thunder striking earthly fire.

Around the time it took an incense stick to burn later.

A figure slowly walked out from the smoke and dust.

In his hand, he carried a broken great hammer. On his body, the black armor covered in complex patterns had several edges that were slightly scorched yellow.

"Huff!"

Luo Chen exhaled a long breath of turbid air. Looking at the nearly destroyed Flowing Fire Hammer, his brows could not help furrowing.

He turned back, swept his large sleeve, and the smoke and dust filling the seabed immediately scattered.

A jiao dragon corpse that seemed to have been cooked lay quietly there, emitting an enticing meat fragrance.

After putting away the magic treasure, Luo Chen extended his left hand toward the jiao dragon corpse.

On his five fingers, his thumb wore a black iron ring, while his index finger wore an ink-green ring.

Light flickered on his thumb, and a powerful suction immediately drew the jiao dragon corpse inside.

The ink-green ring was Mountain Lord Hundred Craft's relic. It was named the Ink Ring, an orthodox storage ring that had reached the level of a low-grade magic treasure. It was now Luo Chen's commonly used storage ring.

As for Luo Chen's previously commonly used Thunder Accumulation Treasure Ring, it had now retreated to the second line. Most important resources inside had been transferred into the Ink Ring.

After all, although that thing had the level of a low-grade magic treasure, it had originally only been used to hold Thunder Essence. It was not so orthodox in storage.

By comparison, the Ink Ring not only had a larger storage space, but was also sturdier. Back then, Mountain Lord Hundred Craft had been besieged by Lord Lu Mountain and two others, and had taken a blow from Black King, being beaten to pieces, yet the Ink Ring had still remained. Its power was obvious.

At this moment, however, the Thunder Accumulation Treasure Ring also had a use.

It was just right for holding this jiao dragon corpse.

After putting away the corpse, Luo Chen did not bother cleaning the battlefield and directly returned by his original route.

After spending more than half an hour, from the heavily guarded shore of the Sunken Sea, a streak of escape light quietly flew out and finally landed in an ordinary small mountain with little spiritual qi.

He released an artifact flying sword and casually carved out a cave residence.

Luo Chen threw out the Sealing God Compass, arranged the Billowing Cloud Dense Mist Array, and only then calmly stepped into the cave residence.

As soon as he entered, Luo Chen immediately swallowed pills, regulated his breathing, and meditated.

The sun set and the moon rose.

Around midnight, Luo Chen leisurely woke, his aura somewhat recovered.

Only now did he have time to recall that previous battle.

"The killing move has only just formed. I was a little too arrogant."

Luo Chen smiled bitterly. This trip to test his sword had almost capsized.

When he was at the early Golden Core stage, and later at the fourth level of Golden Core, he had once faced late third-tier Great Demon Kings.

One was the Golden Wolf King, who had crushed him.

The other was the Golden-Armored Tyrant Crab of the Xuanyan sea region. They had been evenly matched, and in the end, Luo Chen had returned after leaving and injured the Golden-Armored Crab King heavily with a fully charged palm, regaining face.

Now he was at the sixth level of Golden Core. His mana was stronger than before, and his many spells were more exquisite. Especially not long ago, he had created a killing move of his own. Logically speaking, against the Sea-Overturning Demon Jiao, which was also late third-tier, he should have crushed it.

But the actual situation was not so.

The Sea-Overturning Demon Jiao not only possessed a powerful physique, but could move freely at the bottom of the Sunken Sea, and could even briefly control the black turbid seawater.

Its rumored water-control ability had received the greatest possible enhancement from the Sunken Sea's geographical advantage.

This battle had been rather dangerous for Luo Chen.

He fully deployed the All-Encompassing Fire Prison, briefly transformed into the true body of the Heavenly Peng during battle, and finally destroyed the Flowing Fire Hammer. Relying on the Black Dust Armor's excellent defense, he had forcefully taken it down.

Taking out the Flowing Fire Hammer, which now had no possibility of repair, Luo Chen inwardly lamented its loss, yet also came to a realization.

No wonder the Sea-Overturning Demon Jiao dared occupy the Sunken Sea.

It could actually briefly control the black turbid seawater.

If he had not been empowered by the All-Encompassing Fire Prison and pushed his combat strength to the maximum, he probably would not have been able to take the opponent down.

However, through this real battle, he became more familiar with this new killing move.

"Once the All-Encompassing Fire Prison unfolds, it can greatly enhance my attacks. Within a hundred zhang, whether spells or magic treasures, they are several degrees fiercer than in a normal state."

"But this is not the key!"

"The key lies in the fact that enemies inside the fire prison suffer suppression similar to a Sage domain. But my 'suppression' is somewhat different. It makes the enemy's mana become restless, preventing it from being freely mobilized as usual, thereby creating great flaws in battle."

"If I seize those flaws, I can easily defeat the enemy."

The suppression of Luo Chen's pseudo-domain did not make the enemy's mana difficult to mobilize and slow their speed. Instead, it did the opposite, making the enemy's power more berserk and thereby producing an effect where it became difficult to control.

It could be considered the same destination by a different path.

The reason the Sea-Overturning Demon Jiao had forced Luo Chen to transform into the true Heavenly Peng body was because apart from its vigorous demonic power, it also possessed an outstanding physique.

When its mana became difficult to control, it chose to fight Luo Chen in melee.

Only, it had chosen the wrong opponent.

Luo Chen merely transformed briefly and easily took it down.

"As expected of a Great Demon King that survived the battle against the Primordial Demon Sect. It truly had some genuine ability. Its whole strength was no weaker than an ordinary late Golden Core human cultivator."

Luo Chen sighed and took out the force distribution map.

"I will have to choose the next sword-testing target carefully."

This trip was for testing his sword. This "sword" was the new killing move, the All-Encompassing Fire Prison, not a life-or-death struggle to break through his limits.

There was no need for him to specifically choose especially powerful enemies.

The best choice would be one with some ability, yet unable to threaten Luo Chen's life.

In addition, during the selection process, Luo Chen this time deliberately avoided Demon Kings whose descriptions included something similar to "water-control ability."

He did not want to meet another fellow like the Sea-Overturning Demon Jiao, who could briefly control the Sunken Sea's black turbid seawater.

Five days later.

A sturdy figure appeared inside the Sunken Sea basin.

"This should be the territory occupied by that demon jiao. Killing it will not only earn large amounts of merit and spirit stones, but will also let me use blood baleful power to upgrade my Golden Suanni Saber once."

Dao Lan gripped his long saber, his mind occasionally recalling another blade from Withered Wood Ridge.

True artifact, the Divine Dipper Blood Blade.

What Blood Wanderer could do, Dao Lan might not be unable to do.

As long as he killed all the way forward, his Golden Suanni Saber would also surely break through the shackle of magic treasures and become an unsurpassed famous blade.

While his heart brimmed with ambition, the taciturn Dao Lan froze on the ruined battlefield.

Where had such a huge demon jiao gone?

He clearly remembered that when he had discovered this Demon King last time, it had been here.

Relying on that intelligence, he had even obtained a fine amount of merit at Taiping Fortress.

Dao Lan frowned and spread his divine sense, searching the nearby situation.

Under the corrosion of the surging black turbid seawater, the lingering aura of the battlefield had long since been cleared away.

But!

Dao Lan lowered himself and took a blood-stained scale from the mud.

The blood was the Sea-Overturning Demon Jiao's blood.

But there was also a strand of unusual residual mana aura on it.

Very faint, but extremely domineering.

"Such domineering, blazing mana... him?"

Dao Lan was startled, and a figure faintly appeared in his mind.

"But how is that possible?"

Although that person was formidable and comparable to a late cultivator, in Dao Lan's view he was only equal to someone at the seventh level of Golden Core.

And this Sea-Overturning Demon Jiao was an existence that even Dao Lan, a ninth-level Golden Core eight-star demon hunter, had only dared come for alone after making thorough preparations.

Could it be that person's strength was already comparable to someone at the ninth level of Golden Core?

Amid uncertainty, Dao Lan softly spat out a few words.

"Devil Lord Azure Yang..."

He turned and headed east.

There, another Scorchflame Sawtooth Shark existed, a late third-tier Great Demon King like the Sea-Overturning Demon Jiao.

It was also a fine whetstone.

If he killed it, he could also use its body of metal-attribute sawteeth to repair the accumulated damage his Golden Suanni Saber had suffered over these years of battle.

One day later.

Dao Lan stood alone above a sea region full of hidden reefs, his face blank.

"Where is my Scorchflame Sawtooth Shark?"

*(End of Chapter)*
