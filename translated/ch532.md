# Chapter 532: The Qingyang Alchemy Canon

---

On Sea-Watching Cliff.

Night lay heavy, and the sea wind blew gently.

Amid the rolling sound of waves, there was a different sort of stillness.

With a flip of Luo Chen's wrist, the black long banner immediately planted itself in the center of the cave residence.

His divine sense entered it. The slaughter inside was still ongoing.

Over a hundred powerful resentful souls surrounded Yan Nantian's remnant soul, constantly tearing and gnawing at it.

Especially that Ghost King remnant soul at the Adept realm. Under its great mouthfuls of devouring, its originally dim spiritual light began to recover again.

Aside from that, there was nothing else.

Watching this scene, Luo Chen revealed a thoughtful look.

"Although cultivators borrow external resources to accelerate cultivation, in essence, they still walk the same path as ancient qi refiners: refining essence into qi, refining qi into spirit."

"At Initiate, spiritual sense is projected outward. At Adept, spirit becomes divine. But when carefully weighed, the essence of the divine soul still has not undergone qualitative change."

"Without the physical body, this precious raft for crossing the world, an Adept cultivator's divine soul is like a naked child. Wind, frost, rain, and snow can all break it, let alone attacks from resentful souls and fierce ghosts that have fallen into the ghost path."

"This situation may only improve slightly at Sage. The divine soul can drive the Nascent Soul, forming a second precious raft. Even so, it still cannot be considered qualitative change. The true qualitative change should come at Ascendant, when the Nascent Soul and divine soul unify into one and form the primordial spirit[TERM?]."

Luo Chen did not know whether his guess was correct.

But based on records from old classics, exchanges with fellow cultivators, and the current situation of Yan Nantian's remnant soul having no power to resist, he arrived at this conjecture.

As his thoughts spread, he wondered: if he himself fell to such a state, how could he preserve his remnant soul to the greatest extent?

The answer was: he could not.

Or rather, there was one and only one way, which was to continue strengthening the foundation of his divine soul.

"Unfortunately, the Tongyou Pill[TERM?] no longer has any effect on me. The soul-refining effect of the Fine Dust Primordial Art is also negligible."

Luo Chen had judged for himself that after advancing to Adept Stage Three, his current divine soul strength still had not exceeded that of an ordinary late-stage Adept cultivator.

It was roughly equivalent to an Adept Stage Five cultivator, or perhaps a slightly weaker Stage Six.

He thought again of the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture, this top-tier fire cultivation method.

It also recorded no divine-refining method.

But he felt that a divine art rumored to lead directly to the Great Vehicle stage[TERM?] should not have such a flaw.

"In other words, if such secret methods truly exist, they should be in the Sage section, or even the Ascendant section."

Pressing his lips together, Luo Chen dispelled those foolish fantasies.

He did not plan to return to Green Parasol Mountain, so naturally he had no chance to obtain higher-level follow-up methods.

After thinking for a moment, Luo Chen squeezed out a drop of essence blood and let it fall onto the Myriad Souls Banner.

Mana surged out, and divine sense coiled around it.

Luo Chen sat cross-legged and began refining it.

This was an excellent treasure, especially since it was a soul-path treasure outside the five-element system. He could use it without obstruction.

Most importantly, its foundation was quite good.

Even though the tens of thousands of low-tier souls inside had been exhausted in the previous battle, the most central hundred-plus resentful souls, eight great split souls, and Adept main soul had all been preserved.

Going forward, so long as he spent a little effort replenishing low-tier souls, this treasure would not lose its power.

It could even be said to have quite decent growth potential.

Above a Myriad Souls Banner, what came next again?

A Hundred-Thousand Fierce Souls Banner[TERM?], perhaps.

Time flowed little by little.

When dawn arrived, Luo Chen stopped his movements with a somewhat tired expression.

He reached out, and the black long banner planted in the ground fell into his hand.

A faint connection, barely there, reached from the long banner into the depths of his divine soul.

"Although I still cannot use it directly, as long as I refine it persistently, this treasure can be used by me in a few months. By then, Yan Nantian's soul should also have been completely divided up."

Luo Chen smiled in satisfaction and put the Soul-Refining Banner into his storage ring.

Afterward, another object appeared in his hand.

They were two weapons resembling short halberds, yet narrower and longer. Their tips were sharp, and blue light flowed across them like water.

Water-Parting Spikes.

Low-grade treasures.

They contained the killing move of parting water and transforming into dragons.

This was also one of the few spoils Yan Nantian had left Luo Chen in the previous battle.

Fire spells were too violent in power.

Under such extreme confrontation, even Yan Nantian's body could not be preserved, let alone his storage bag.

The Myriad Souls Banner and Water-Parting Spikes were everything.

"Their quality is decent. They are nearly comparable to the Tianyue Purple-Gold Wheel[TERM?] I used often before."

"Unfortunately, I cannot use them."

Shaking his head regretfully, Luo Chen put the Water-Parting Spikes away.

Now that he mainly cultivated fire, his mana was indeed purer and more tyrannical, but the shortcomings were also obvious.

High-quality treasures of the other four elements could not be used flexibly by him.

Even if he forcibly refined and drove them, the power they displayed in battle would only be five or six tenths of their original strength, and would greatly harm the treasures themselves.

With gain, there must be loss.

Luo Chen had long since prepared himself mentally. After all, he had already abandoned even the Tianyue Purple-Gold Wheel, into which he had poured great effort, let alone these newly obtained Water-Parting Spikes.

Then Luo Chen pushed out with his hand. A round gray little cauldron spun from his palm, growing from small to large before landing in front of him.

Looking at the little cauldron, Luo Chen sank into indescribable silence.

This was the life-bound treasure he had pursued bitterly, spending who knew how much energy before finally managing to create it.

But now, among all his treasures, the Primordial Unity Cauldron was in the most awkward position.

He was not an artifact-forging master and did not know whether the methods Heaven Smelter left behind still remained.

Because of that, after leaving Green Parasol Mountain, he had delayed swallowing this treasure into his qi sea and nurturing it day and night. He could only keep it in his storage ring.

Precisely because of that, the refining process of this thing had remained stuck.

Leaving aside its ugly appearance, the terrifying impurities contained within it could not be refined away and hindered the treasure's advancement in grade.

Even so, the Primordial Unity Cauldron's power still made Luo Chen praise it in wonder.

Whether imprisoning Han Zhan's soul, binding and capturing Blue-Ringed Fang Serpents, or nurturing Blazing Fire Miasma, all of it showed the strength of this "masterpiece" of Heaven Smelter.

One could imagine how great its potential would be if its impurities were truly removed completely.

It could even continue to fuse with Thousand-Year Thunder Essence[TERM?] and ore materials of each element afterward. What level would its grade reach then?

High-grade treasure?

No. That was far from the Primordial Unity Cauldron's limit.

True artifact[TERM?]... spirit treasure[TERM?]...

After a long silence, heaven and man warred within Luo Chen's heart. Finally, he clenched his fist fiercely.

"What am I worrying about? Heaven Smelter is already dead. Even if he left some contingency, no one else can activate this treasure."

"Since when was looking before and after like this my, Luo Chen's, nature?"

With a thought, Luo Chen opened his mouth and inhaled.

At once, the round little cauldron rapidly shrank, finally transforming into a spinning streak of light that flew into Luo Chen's mouth and came to rest within his qi sea.

Pure mana coiling in every direction surged up at once. Source true fire also wrapped around it, burning and refining impurities.

In the end, Luo Chen still could not bear to part with his life-bound treasure.

Leaving aside how much energy he had spent for this treasure, merely the coming grand alchemy enterprise required him to control it.

The True Flame Pill was not something the incomplete Shangshu Kettle[TERM?] could support for long-term, high-frequency refinement.

After inspecting the spoils, Luo Chen left the cave residence and strolled along Sea-Watching Cliff.

Looking at the endless sea and the slowly rising blazing sun, his weary mind seemed to ease.

Yan Nantian was dead.

After personally witnessing his great display of divine might, the cultivators of the Swallow Islands should have no unusual thoughts for a short time and could be used by him.

In this way, Luo Chen finally had a temporary place to settle and continue cultivating.

What came afterward would instead be simple.

It was nothing more than organizing manpower, collecting resources, refining pills, and cultivating bitterly behind closed doors.

Looking at the sea swallows circling over the ocean, Luo Chen felt carefree.

"Strictly speaking, Yan Nantian was the first proper life-and-death enemy I fought after forming my Core."

"He was not weak. He had reached Adept Stage Four."

"Unfortunately, he had just broken through when he received one of my Qingyang Great Handprints, and the rest of the battle remained entirely under my control."

"He probably felt quite wronged. He had no grudge or enmity with me. I merely took interest in his spirit land, and he suffered this undeserved calamity."

"If carefully weighed, his methods were actually quite good and extremely comprehensive. He himself could also fully control two treasures at the same time. His greatest mistake was forcing a realm breakthrough..."

On the cliff, the young man paced slowly, summarizing the gains and losses of the previous battle.

The salty sea wind struck his body. When it lifted his black hair, it seemed to carry away the restless air around him as well.

...

One month later.

At the very center of the thirty-six islands.

The ten-li calm lake rippled once again. The little Invitation Moon Island rose from deep underground, and dense heaven-and-earth spirit qi began to gush forth anew.

Figures moved through the area, preparing for today's grand ceremony.

Not a single mortal was present. The lowest cultivation there was Qi Refinement.

From time to time, people cast glances toward the little island at the heart of the lake, fear showing on their faces.

In this short month, the name Demon Lord Qingyang had already spread among the hundreds of thousands of mortals and cultivators of the thirty-six islands.

Everyone knew the sky over the Swallow Islands had changed.

It was no longer "Nantian," Southern Sky, but "Qingtian," Azure Sky.

No one knew what the future would look like.

But for now, the first signs had already appeared.

Merely gathering spirit and stabilizing the vein, rebuilding a third-tier spirit land, had consumed much wealth from dozens of clans across the thirty-six islands.

Some time ago, forty-nine Initiate true cultivators, led by the three great formation masters of Swallow, had forcibly pulled Invitation Moon Island up from deep underground. The commotion had been in no way inferior to Luo Chen blasting Invitation Moon Island down.

One could imagine that in the future, Demon Lord Qingyang might make the Swallow cultivators do who knew what.

Buzzing discussion constantly rose as the crowd moved.

But as noon arrived, the discussion gradually faded, and all cultivators below Initiate withdrew.

Suddenly.

A mass of red cloud came from the distance and stopped above Invitation Moon Island.

Upon the red cloud stood a tall man dressed in black Daoist robes. His black hair was neatly bound by a jade hairpin. Although his face was young, his spirit eyes seemed like cold pools, deep abysses, vast oceans, and ravines, bottomless.

Seeing the arrival, every Initiate cultivator beside the lake bent and saluted.

"Greetings, Demon Lord Qingyang!"

Though only a hundred voices, gathered together they possessed the momentum of mountains calling and seas roaring.

Luo Chen's spirit eyes looked down on the lakefront cultivators, slowly sweeping over them.

At last, his gaze stopped on Invitation Moon Island.

A satisfied smile appeared on his face.

"Not bad. I am very satisfied."

"And not one of you is missing. There are even a few more. I am even more satisfied."

Hearing this, everyone could not help relaxing slightly.

With a sweep of his sleeve, Luo Chen said clearly, "There is no need for many other words. What sort of style I, Qingyang, possess, everyone will naturally come to understand through future dealings."

"Today, I will speak of only three matters."

"First, from now on, the Cheng clan of Black Swan Island will be my proxy in the Swallow Islands. All secular affairs will be handled by them."

Hearing this, even though the cultivators had long since prepared themselves mentally, faint disappointment still rose in their hearts.

The Cheng clan had truly ascended to heaven in one step, clinging to an enormous thigh. From now on, they would stand beneath one person and above ten thousand.

At the front of the crowd, the seven Initiates of the Cheng clan all showed self-satisfied expressions.

Suddenly, a spiritual light flew from Luo Chen's sleeve and drifted before the seven Cheng cultivators.

A treasure.

Unlike in the Jade Cauldron Domain, where low-grade treasures overflowed, treasures were still very rare in the cultivation world.

Apart from great sects and old cultivation clans, ordinary small forces had almost no powerful treasures worth presenting.

From Luo Chen's previous observations, the Initiate cultivators here mostly used high-grade or supreme-grade artifacts.

Even a stronger clan like the Cheng clan had only one soul banner with growth potential.

Seeing that treasure light, Cheng Dou's heart moved. He wanted to step forward and receive it, but unexpectedly, the spiritual light automatically fell into the hands of the woman beside him.

"This is my token. Cheng Haixin shall hold it. Seeing her is as seeing me."

Cheng Haixin looked at the pair of Water-Parting Spikes in her hand, dazed.

Especially when she saw the fleeting gloom on her elder brother's face, her heart trembled even more.

"Demon Lord, I..."

The words that followed stopped abruptly. Under Luo Chen's deep gaze, she swallowed the words of refusal.

Before a Senior, where was there room to refuse?

"The second matter."

Luo Chen looked coldly at the crowd, his tone turning slightly chilly.

"Next, I will enter seclusion to cultivate and need to refine a third-tier pill in large quantities. The medicinal materials required for this pill will be searched out by all of you for me. From now on, you need not offer tribute to me. You only need to collect medicinal materials for me."

"I have already given the list of medicinal materials to the Cheng clan. They will assign tasks. Once you receive tasks, simply collect what is required."

Everyone's expressions shifted slightly.

They would not need to offer yearly tribute to him like they had to Yan Nantian before, only collect medicinal materials?

In that case, it seemed somewhat better.

The pill resources used by an Adept cultivator for one year of cultivation would not be too many. With the thirty-six islands working together, the expense should not be great.

Cheng Dou looked at this scene with a cold smile. If they knew the quantity of medicinal materials on the list, they probably would not be so optimistic.

The quantity of medicinal materials Demon Lord Qingyang required, if converted to spirit stones, exceeded the yearly tribute Yan Nantian had demanded before.

He wondered whether, after he later issued the tasks and these people discovered the gains and losses involved, they would stir up some trouble.

How would Demon Lord Qingyang pacify the dissatisfaction in their hearts?

The next moment, Luo Chen's voice sounded again.

And this time, feverish expressions appeared on everyone's faces.

Luo Chen flipped his wrist, and a stone stele floated beside him.

On the stone stele, the characters were written with dragon-like strokes, as though some divine art or wondrous method had been engraved there. Yet they were covered by spiritual light, preventing everyone from seeing their true content.

Only four characters entered everyone's hearts.

Qingyang Alchemy Canon[TERM?].

"Earlier, I learned of the Swallow Islands' history. In a hundred years, not a single Adept cultivator has emerged. Even over eight hundred years, only two Adepts have walked out."

"Such a laughable Core Formation success rate. Could it be that everyone's aptitude is poor? Or that your temperaments are too lacking?"

"I do not think that is the case."

"Previously, all of you gathered the spirit vein for me. In the future, you will also travel in every direction on my behalf. Your hard work and merit can be said to be great."

"Therefore, I pass down this middle-grade Core Formation secret art[TERM?]. Each clan may exchange for it using contributions and merits earned toward me. This is the third matter."

Luo Chen smiled faintly, and spiritual light bloomed from his hand.

Layer after layer of obscuring restrictions[TERM?] struck the stone stele. The hazy spiritual light above it instantly shot into the sky before vanishing into nothingness.

The writing on it also completely disappeared.

But everyone knew the writing had not vanished. Rather, under Demon Lord Qingyang's great methods as an Adept cultivator, it had only been temporarily hidden.

If they wanted to exchange for it in the future, they would have to take out sufficient contributions and merits.

And how would those merits be obtained?

If nothing unexpected happened, it would depend on how many rare medicinal materials each clan could collect for Demon Lord Qingyang.

Luo Chen struck down with one hand, and the stone stele immediately sank, planting itself on Invitation Moon Island.

After finishing all this, Luo Chen smiled faintly.

"Everyone, the Great Dao is difficult. Let us encourage one another."

When the words fell, his figure gradually dispersed.

At the same time, layer upon layer of white cloud and mist appeared over Invitation Moon Island, completely covering it.

Beside the lake, more than a hundred Initiate true cultivators looked at one another, their eyes filled with feverish obsession.

The Qingyang Alchemy Canon, a middle-grade Core Formation secret art.

From now on, the Swallow cultivators had hope for the Golden Core Great Dao.

*(End of Chapter)*
