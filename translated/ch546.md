# Chapter 546: Sea-Sealing Eight Trigrams, the Power of One Cauldron

Splash!

White waves rolled, mud and sand cascading down.

In the shallows, three figures slowly emerged.

As they appeared, their originally hidden auras began to climb continuously, and the bodies of the three began to expand without cease.

With this posture, if one word did not agree, they were about to open a slaughter.

Seeing this scene, the Black-Scale Jiaolong lying on the beach could not stop its heart from sinking.

"Damn it. When I came back, I actually did not notice someone following."

Deep-sea travel was already extremely concealed, so this matter truly could not be blamed on the Black-Scale Jiaolong.

Especially since the Demon Spider Crab race had sharp perception and were also top-class experts at concealing aura.

The Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull looked dispirited and spat out crisp human words. "Big Black, my mana has been suppressed. Go help Master fight."

It saw clearly that all the newcomers were third-tier demon kings. Especially the thin-bodied Demon Spider Crab at the head, whose aura was even stronger, seeming to be at mid third tier.

If the three joined hands, Master would likely suffer a major loss.

If the Black-Scale Jiaolong could hold back one or two enemies for Master, perhaps the situation could be reversed.

However,

facing its request, the Black-Scale Jiaolong could not help pouring out its own bitterness.

"I would like to!"

"But to save you, I have very little demon power left. How could I still fight?"

What it said was the truth.

Breaking through the protective formation of the giant demon-hunting ship under the noses of seven Core Formation cultivators and forcefully rescuing Heavenly Pivot was not so simple.

The Black-Scale Jiaolong had purely relied on the Devouring demon art it had awakened earlier.

But that demon art had awakened only a year or two ago, and its mastery was not very skilled. Normally, it only showed off its might against small fish and shrimp.

Such large-scale activation was a devastating drain on its demon power.

Being able to bring Heavenly Pivot back had already completely exhausted the demon power stored in the demon core within its body.

Seeing those three Demon Spider Crabs ready to strike, the Black-Scale Jiaolong gritted its teeth. "Fine. When the time comes, I will go up and fight physically, doing my best to entangle one enemy and create an opening for Master. In any case, my skin is rough and my flesh is thick. So long as I do not die, Master will definitely save me back. Maybe he will even reward me with two more bottles of Imperial Moonlight Nectar."

Having made up its mind, it moved its enormous body and swam toward Luo Chen.

However, after only a few steps, it stopped.

"Go back. Leave this place to me."

Luo Chen, with his back to the Black-Scale Jiaolong, waved his hand. Between the light flutter of his great sleeves, he was calm and unhurried.

The Black-Scale Jiaolong froze, flicking its tongue as if wanting to say something, but its mind subconsciously recalled that battle in the Swallow Islands several years ago.

That stunning palm!

Since Master could fight evenly with Core Formation Stage Six Wu Qi, what need did it have to worry?

Holding expectation and trust toward Luo Chen, it slowly retreated to the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull's side, trying to undo the Demon-Suppressing Talisman's seal for it.

...

By the shore,

Seventh Elder of the Demon Spider Crab race looked at the man opposite him with alarm and uncertainty.

"So this evil jiaolong and thief gull are spirit beasts under your seat?"

Luo Chen calmly said, "What if they are this seat's spirit beasts?"

Seventh Elder's two huge eyeballs rolled. "Then it makes sense. The Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull and Black-Scale Jiaolong are natural enemies, yet they could join hands. So you were the mastermind behind the scenes."

At this point, his face showed a hateful expression. "In other words, those sons of mine actually all died by your hand?"

Demon Spider Crabs?

The corners of Luo Chen's mouth rose slightly, and his sleeve lightly lifted.

"To tell the truth, in over a hundred years of cultivation, I have eaten no small number of delicacies. Only the crab roe inside the heads of you Demon Spider Crabs can be called a rare delicacy of the human world!"

Once these words came out, Seventh Elder flew into a rage.

"Demon!"

Luo Chen's face turned cold. "Monster! I have broken through in realm and was just thinking of trying the taste of third-tier Demon Spider Crabs. Today, since you have come uninvited, you can serve as my congratulations!"

As he spoke, he stamped one foot and charged toward the three demons with a boom.

At some unknown time, a black-red great sword was already in his hand.

His speed was so fast that only an explosive boom could be heard, and in the next moment, he arrived before Seventh Elder.

One sword suddenly shot out.

Xiu!

Facing this menacing sword, Seventh Elder put away his furious emotions and withdrew backward.

Boom!

A great mass of waves exploded, and endless water droplets fell.

Seventh Elder looked at that long sword sinking into the sea, and the taut string in his heart could not help loosening by three parts.

"No need to worry. This villain has Core Formation Stage Four realm in name, but he has only temporarily broken through. His realm is not yet stable, and his mana control is also unsatisfactory."

"The quality of his magic treasure flying sword is also not particularly good."

"Let us attack together and quickly take him down!"

As he spoke, the three demons rushed toward Luo Chen from three directions.

Pop...

Luo Chen lightly landed on the water surface. Looking at his wrist, his brows furrowed slightly.

For the qi refinement path, breaking through right before battle was troublesome in this way.

Methods once familiar would become relatively unfamiliar because of the new realm, stronger mana, and stronger divine sense.

To put it exaggeratedly, it was like a child suddenly having to swing a great saber to chop someone, unable to weigh his own true strength clearly.

Failing to injure the enemy and instead easily suffering backlash oneself.

This was also why cultivators, after breaking through in realm, mostly specially spent some time in seclusion to stabilize the newly broken-through realm.

The purpose was not merely stabilization, but also familiarizing themselves with combat methods under the new realm.

Very clearly, this time, the enemy had not given him that chance.

His two eyeballs turned left and right, divine sense spreading around him. Looking at the enemies charging horizontally from three directions, the corners of Luo Chen's mouth rose slightly. In the enemies' eyes, it looked like the ferocious smile of a demon.

"Do you think this is all I have?"

As he muttered to himself, a pair of scarlet wings abruptly unfurled behind him.

"Perfect. I will also use you to test just how strong my late third-tier Archaic physique is!"

With a ferocious smile, his wings trembled.

The next moment, Luo Chen abruptly flew high into the sky.

Seeing this, the enemies did not hesitate. Each opened their mouths and spat silk.

Thick streams like white rainbows shot toward him together.

Luo Chen twisted and shifted in the air, his figure agile like a swimming dragon, dodging the three attacks by the narrowest margin.

Afterward, he transformed into a startling rainbow and charged straight toward the weakest of the three demons.

Seeing Luo Chen not only not widen the distance to fight, but instead wanting to engage in close combat,

that Demon Spider Crab could not help laughing loudly. "Fighting my demon race in the flesh? You truly seek death."

Amid its wild laughter, its two giant claws covered in hideous fuzz smashed toward Luo Chen.

Spiritual light in Luo Chen's eyes bloomed to the utmost, allowing him to maintain the precision of his physical attacks even under ultra-high-speed movement.

Seeing the giant claws' ferocity, he neither feared nor dreaded. He stepped out with Voidburst Step, and another explosive sound rang between sea and sky.

In an instant, his entire body abruptly rushed forward several dozen zhang, and then he struck out with a palm.

As the palm struck, the muscles in his arm erupted and swelled by several times.

His five fingers were solid, slightly bent, grabbing down like claws.

This palm was not the Azure Yang Great Handprint, but the Cloud-Seizing Divine Claw!

Originally, in human form, Luo Chen's Cloud-Seizing Divine Claw only had thirty to forty percent of its power in the Heavenly Roc true body.

Unless necessary, he would not use this move in human form.

But as Myriad Path Convergence reached Perfected Completion, and his physique reached late Archaic Tier Three, he grasped a technique for partial transformation of the fleshly body.

At this moment, when he swung a claw, there was almost no difference compared to when he transformed into the Heavenly Roc true body.

And what power did the Perfected Completion Cloud-Seizing Divine Claw possess?

Very soon, everyone witnessed it.

Crack! Crack!

That sudden claw grabbed the two giant claws head-on, then fiercely pulled.

Under the terrified gazes of the many compound eyes of that Demon Spider Crab, its two strongest giant claws were forcibly torn off, revealing incomparably delicious white tender meat.

The largest crab pincers also possessed the tenderest crab meat!

Only at this point did the afterimage left behind by Voidburst Step slowly disperse.

"Ah! It hurts so much!!!"

Amid the screams of intense pain, the three demons focused their gazes again on the white-robed man, the shock and fury in their eyes completely impossible to conceal.

Luo Chen held the two enormous crab pincers and licked his lips.

"This meat should be incomparably delicious!"

"Only, these screams are not suitable to accompany wine."

"Then I will slaughter you first and make a delicious whole-crab banquet!"

As he spoke, he erupted with extreme speed again.

This time, Seventh Elder quickly responded and shouted sternly, "This demon's physique surpasses even us demon race members. You absolutely must not clash with him head-on. Use Thousand Spiders Myriad Demons Hand!"

After receiving the reminder, the other two demons immediately understood his meaning.

In an instant, the fuzz on each crab leg beneath them began growing continuously, transforming into threads of white spikes. They also opened their mouths and spat wildly, huge masses of spider silk surging out.

Luo Chen flashed with Voidburst Step, descending before another demon beast, and swung a claw.

Whoosh! Whoosh! Whoosh!

Masses of spider silk wrapped around his arm.

At the same time, two incomparably hard giant claws swung over.

"First use spider silk to overcome hardness with softness, then use giant claws that destroy anything?"

Luo Chen sneered and likewise opened his mouth to spit.

Hiss...

A wisp of azure flame drifted out.

In only an instant, it coiled around the endless white spider silk.

Azure and white colors intertwined. Without doubt, the fourth-tier azure flame won completely.

Great masses of spider silk turned to ash and fell to the ground.

His palm loosened and regained freedom. Luo Chen's two giant claws simultaneously gripped the two giant claws.

One man and one demon began a contest of strength.

"Open for me!"

Luo Chen shouted softly. With boundless enormous strength added, he actually forcibly pried open those two giant claws.

And he himself gained the opening, pressing close and coming face to face with that third-tier demon crab.

This was the end!

Once he was close to the face, it was no longer as simple as breaking legs and tearing claws.

Luo Chen suddenly struck with a palm, surging giant strength splitting mountains and cracking stone.

Boom!!!

Blood suddenly appeared. A third-tier Demon Spider Crab was actually blown apart on the spot by him.

Even its protective light barrier could not block the slightest bit.

Amid flesh and blood scattering through the sky, Luo Chen's peripheral vision caught sight of the Demon Spider Crab whose two claws he had torn off earlier withdrawing backward.

"Trying to run?"

With a cold snort, he flicked his finger.

Three black lights flew out in an instant.

They were precisely the three Soul-Breaking Nails!

That Demon Spider Crab's expression changed greatly, and it abruptly drilled beneath the sea surface.

But the next moment, a scream rang out.

"Ah!"

Bang!

The water surface exploded. An enlarged sword light pierced through the Demon Spider Crab's chest and leaped from the water surface, charging into the sky.

This was clearly the Mystic Flame Sword Luo Chen had struck into the seawater earlier.

He had not retrieved it before only because it was drawn and waiting without release.

Under his multitasking control, it had issued a lethal strike.

At this point,

in a short period, amid a sudden rapid exchange, two demons were already one dead and one gravely wounded.

Luo Chen turned back and looked toward the "thin" Demon Spider Crab that had remained standing motionless the entire time.

Mid third-tier.

"Your turn."

A humanlike sorrow flashed across that Demon Spider Crab's face, probably because it had not expected its two companions to suffer such heavy injuries.

It said hoarsely, "Yes, my turn. One against three, and at the first moment of battle, you used the method of defeating them one by one. Your battle talent is truly unparalleled."

Luo Chen's brows furrowed slightly.

This demon beast was somewhat extraordinary.

Its human speech was clear and orderly, almost no different from a cultivator.

"But you forgot why I stood motionless."

"It is not that I am cold-blooded and indifferent to my clansmen's miserable deaths."

"In truth, it is because I must kill you!"

At the end, that sorrowful tone became impassioned and furious.

The next moment, it leaped up, white silk transforming into clouds to support its body, while it held a brown compass plate in its hand.

It was also at this moment that Luo Chen discovered something was wrong.

"Where are his legs?"

Crabs had eight legs, but at this moment, this Demon Spider Crab seemed to have none.

It was also at this moment that huge water pillars shot out from the sea surface.

As the water fell, eight black crab legs covered in white markings were revealed.

Luo Chen's expression changed slightly. "A formation?"

Eight legs as the formation, arraying the sea and sealing it.

The instant the formation took shape, endless blood-colored spider silk shot toward Luo Chen from all directions.

In the sky, Seventh Elder of the Demon Spider Crab race manipulated the compass plate, his expression calm.

"My name is Zhu Qi. When I was young, I had a fortuitous encounter and obtained the inheritance of a human Core Formation cultivator on an overseas immortal island. Since then, with an innately deficient body, I cultivated all the way to mid third-tier and ranked as Seventh Elder of the Demon Spider Crab race."

"This formation is named the Sea-Sealing Eight Trigrams Formation, and it also comes from that inheritance."

"I fused it with my racial talent, making its power even greater. Its sealing power cannot be broken by ordinary great demon kings."

"Human, before you die, state your name! In the future, when I transform at fourth tier and achieve the honored status of Demon Emperor, you may become a unique memory in my cultivation life."

Sea-Sealing Eight Trigrams Formation?

Within the grand formation, Luo Chen's expression was full of alarm and uncertainty.

Facing the terrifying spider silk shooting toward him, endless azure flames surged from all around his body.

However, those spider silks that had collapsed upon contact before actually withstood it this time.

Although they still could not advance an inch, very clearly, a unique change had occurred.

...

By the shore,

seeing this scene, the Black-Scale Jiaolong could no longer sit still.

"Heavenly Pivot, retreat to the rear island. I will help Master."

As he spoke, without waiting for Heavenly Pivot to answer, he moved his enormous body and charged toward that mass of white cloud in the sky.

Facing this scene,

Zhu Qi's expression did not change. He fiddled with the compass plate.

Immediately, a huge water pillar shot into the sky from the sea, transforming into a Demon Spider Crab water giant. Eight legs emerged together, tightly entangling the Black-Scale Jiaolong.

"This formation was originally prepared for that thief gull."

"I did not expect she also had a master."

"This is fine too. I will catch all of you in one net and exchange it for the peace of countless demon crab clansmen in my Xuanyan Sea Region."

As he spoke, his movements controlling the compass plate grew faster and faster, vigorous demon power pouring into it.

And on the great sea, water vapor surged with increasing violence.

...

Within the formation,

Luo Chen's spiritual eyes turned rapidly, and finally he glimpsed the truth.

No wonder this white spider silk could withstand his fourth-tier Withering-Bloom fire.

It had borrowed the power of the great sea, fusing with endless water-attribute power.

Thinking to this point, Luo Chen could not help sighing softly in his heart.

Abandoning metal, wood, water, and earth to mainly cultivate fire had greatly increased his battle strength.

Yet he had come to the Northern Sea cultivation world, called the Demon Sea.

The mainstream among the cultivators of this world was water arts.

Water and fire did not coexist, and their mutual restraint was extremely severe.

But so-called restraint merely depended on who was stronger and who was weaker.

However, the cultivators of this world possessed the countless great seas as geographical advantage. Relying on geographical advantage, they could greatly increase their own strength.

Yan Nantian had been like this. Wu Qi had been like this.

He had not expected that a demon beast could actually use geographical advantage to the utmost too.

One could imagine

that as the grand formation operated, its power would certainly become stronger and stronger.

And the energy source of this grand formation came from the boundless great sea, which could be called endlessly continuous.

If this continued for a long time, his azure flame would inevitably lose follow-up power, fall into disadvantage, and continue until he died.

Thinking of that scene, Luo Chen could not help laughing.

Zhu Qi, who was presiding over the grand formation, could not help freezing when he saw this.

"Why can he still laugh?"

The next moment, in his vision, he saw Luo Chen take out an object.

That was a cauldron!

The cauldron entered the grand formation and grew in the wind.

Three zhang square, like a small mountain.

The white-robed Daoist held the cauldron in one hand. Azure flame coiled around it, transforming into a huge fireball. From afar, it looked as if the Daoist was holding up an azure sun.

He raised his head, looked toward Zhu Qi, and lightly spat:

"My name is Azure Yang. Fellow Daoist, may your road beneath the Yellow Springs be smooth!"

When the words fell, he turned sideways, bent his arm, and his locally enlarged arm curved into the shape of a crescent moon.

Then, he hurled it with force!

In an instant, the giant cauldron flew out, like a meteorite rolling back into the horizon, drawing a majestic trail amid rumbling thunder.

Zhu Qi mobilized the formation's power. Vast white spider silk gathered together, trying to pull this azure sun into the great sea.

However, that terrifying giant strength was unstoppable.

Not only that, when the giant cauldron spat azure flames, it was not affected by the great sea's water vapor at all, as if it itself was also part of the great sea. At this moment, it was merely like a fish in water, a dragon swimming through abyssal seas.

Could this Core Formation cultivator skilled in fire arts be using a water-attribute magic treasure?

Amid his doubts, the great cauldron came closer and closer.

Finally, boom!

A huge hole was directly smashed through the grand formation barrier, and the giant cauldron burst out.

Looking at the giant cauldron coming straight at him, Zhu Qi, without his eight legs, absolutely could not escape that tracking trajectory with speed.

His face paled. Only one thought remained in his mind.

"My life is over!"

Bang!

A rain of blood scattered, and scorching water vapor filled the void.

Luo Chen drifted lightly out. One hand recalled the Primordial Unity Cauldron, which spun and shrank, while the other held that brown compass plate.

"A pity about this mid third-tier Demon Spider Crab."

It was unclear whether he was lamenting the other's fleshly materials, or the collapse midway of the other's dream of becoming a Demon Emperor.

Amid his emotion, his gaze landed on the other Demon Spider Crab, which had been gravely wounded by the Mystic Flame Sword and suppressed by the Soul-Breaking Nails.

Fortunately, it was not drawing water with a bamboo basket and ending empty-handed.

(End of chapter)
