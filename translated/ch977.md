# Chapter 977: Golden Flames Scorch the Sea, Rosycloud Descends

"I shouldn't have coveted that desolate beast's body!"

Hidden in his place of concealment, Luo Chen rarely showed regret.

But this feeling came quickly and left just as fast.

With someone interfering, he couldn't possibly force his way across the Yellow Spring Sea.

Moreover, who knew why a great power from Heavenly Origin Dao Sect would suddenly come here? From the looks of it, they were even planning to use this place as a battlefield to fight it out with Rosycloud.

Just because Rosycloud had launched a fierce attack on Heavenly Origin Dao Sect?

That was reason enough.

But Rosycloud's ferocity had already been fully displayed when she retreated in the face of descending Ascendants from the Mystic Realm. Under such circumstances, the remaining Five Elders of Heavenly Origin still wanted to fight to the death?

Luo Chen didn't know what conviction of certain death they held to prepare for this battle.

He only knew that he was now caught between a rock and a hard place.

Leave? He couldn't!

If he made even the slightest noise, with Ancestor Chunyuan overlooking the entire island from their position, they could easily discover him.

And stay?

Once the battle erupted, he would likely become collateral damage.

"No!"

"Actually, there is a chance."

Luo Chen's eyes narrowed slightly, a plan for escape gradually forming in his mind.

...

Life on Soul Island was extremely cold and lonely.

Entering this place was difficult enough; leaving was even harder.

This fact had been confirmed by the great power Chunyuan after multiple explorations.

The faint hope she had harbored for the upcoming battle gradually sank to the bottom along with the results of her investigations.

"Choosing this island as the battlefield means we must determine life and death with that Rosycloud!"

Uttering a soft sigh, her steps slowly brought her to the north side of Soul Severing Cliff.

When she saw that large inward-curving rock, surprise appeared on her face.

But soon, that surprise turned into ruthlessness.

"Someone has been living on this island recently!"

With that thought, her vast divine sense poured out instantly.

Her entire body soared into the air, flying around the eight-hundred-li Soul Island without stopping.

Even though this place naturally suppressed divine sense, with her moving and scanning like this, no living creature could escape her eyes.

Yet after flying back and forth three or four times, she found nothing.

Ancestor Chunyuan's expression wavered, her gaze turning toward the undulating Yellow Spring Sea.

"Could they have already left?"

From her observation, the residual aura of that cave dwelling hadn't reached the Ascendant level; it was likely someone below Ascendant.

If such an existence were still on Soul Island, they definitely couldn't hide from her.

With no results from this investigation, this should be the only answer.

But to be safe, Ancestor Chunyuan still couldn't let it go. She leaped up and searched the island once more.

Only when night fell did she return to Azure Frost, who had been sealed and bound.

As soon as she landed, she heard Azure Frost's faint voice.

"Being this cautious?"

Ancestor Chunyuan paid no attention to the hidden sarcasm in this question. She said very solemnly, "That sister of yours is no ordinary person. There is almost no one in the entire Mountains and Seas Realm who can match her. If we want to kill her, we must gather all three elements: heavenly timing, earthly advantage, and human harmony."

"Because of the three Nether Origin Sect ancestors' deathbed counterattack, heavenly tribulation was added to her, and later she destroyed Central Continent, causing earthly qi to entangle her. It can be said that heavenly timing is not on her side."

"This Soul Severing Cliff is said to have been formed from the desolate soul-devouring beast. Born in ignorance, it delights in eating souls. This place imprisons the origin qi of heaven and earth and suppresses divine sense and thoughts. Moreover, as a native great desolate of the Mountains and Seas Realm, its realm has broken through to the sixth-level Body Integration stage. Even if Rosycloud once had the ability of a peak Body Integration stage, how much can she display here?"

"Finally, we five Heavenly Origin elders pay with our lives, using you as bait. Human harmony is on our side."

"This is the only time, and the most hopeful chance to kill her. I cannot allow any link to go wrong!"

Having said this, she glanced at Azure Frost.

"The reason you were captured by me is because of a mistake in your cultivation. Although extremely subtle, details determine success or failure. I don't want this kind of situation to appear in my Heavenly Origin Dao Sect."

Upon hearing these words, Azure Frost's originally calm expression changed slightly.

If she hadn't been captured due to her mistake, today's situation wouldn't have arisen.

Even if Heavenly Origin Dao Sect wanted revenge, at most they would have attacked Green Parasol Mountain.

At that time, at least two of the three elements—heavenly timing, earthly advantage, and human harmony—would have been on her sister's side. How could it have come to this?

With a heavy heart and being naturally disinclined to speak much, Azure Frost didn't say another word the entire night.

Ancestor Chunyuan also stopped talking, simply waiting patiently.

When the sky was about to dawn, her eyes suddenly brightened.

In the distance, a massive streak of light was shuttling through the Yellow Spring Sea, step by step approaching Soul Severing Cliff.

"She's here!"

That massive streak of light wasn't particularly fast, but its direction wasn't affected by the waves at all, moving unswervingly toward Soul Severing Cliff.

About the time it takes an incense stick to burn, the flying light split into four, revealing themselves on the soft sand.

Instantly, four men with distinctly different temperaments came into view.

The most prominent was a majestic, stalwart old man with a bristling beard, a ruddy face, and a powerful aura emanating from his entire body.

As soon as he landed, he looked up at the cliff top and let out a hearty laugh.

"Junior Sister Chunyuan, sorry to keep you waiting!"

Above, Ancestor Chunyuan chuckled lightly. "Senior Brother Zhan, please come up!"

Zhan Buxiu laughed heartily, waving his hand without looking back.

"Three junior brothers, let's go!"

Unlike his surging battle intent, the other three had different expressions.

Tan Fengyue, dressed in white scholar's robes and holding a feather fan, still appeared elegant, but there was always a hint of melancholy between his brows.

The youngest, Lian Cheng, who looked the most youthful, had an extremely gloomy expression, even with a hint of panic, and his steps forward were quite heavy.

*Slap!*

An aged hand landed on his shoulder.

Lian Cheng turned his head to look, seeing Senior Brother Mo.

But the usually dignified sect master senior brother now had encouragement in his eyes.

"Don't worry, we've prepared enough for this battle. We can win."

Lian Cheng's voice was hoarse and dry. "But during the Central Continent war, nine Ascendant senior brothers sent down from the Mystic Realm all died at the hands of that demon phoenix! Can the five of us really do it?"

Mo Shouzhuo shook his head. "That was different. Crossing the star gate and forcibly descending to the lower realm itself consumed most of their power. Being picked off one by one by Rosycloud, who was waiting at her leisure, was predictable. This time, we've been prepared for a long time. The advantage is on our side."

Lian Cheng wanted to say something more.

Tan Fengyue, ahead of them, spoke gloomily, "This is a death order from the main sect in the Mystic Realm, a matter conveyed across realms by White Tiger Elder before the star gate was taken. Now that things have come to this, where do we have room to resist? Don't overthink it. Better to be a bit more carefree!"

Lian Cheng opened his mouth, quite unwilling. "I've cultivated bitterly for hundreds of years, finally achieving Ascendant. As soon as I emerge from the mountain, I'm first defeated by You Li, and now I have to fight to the death with the demon phoenix. How can you tell me to be carefree?"

"Heh." Tan Fengyue laughed, seemingly carefree, but the bitterness in it was obvious to all.

Zhan Buxiu at the very front stopped walking, turning to look at the two, his face stern.

"Fearing battle before it even begins—have these years of comfortable life made you too comfortable? Where is the demeanor of Ascendant great powers? Where is the qi-nourishing skill from centuries of cultivation?"

Lian Cheng lowered his head in shame, while Tan Fengyue curled his lips.

Although they addressed each other as senior and junior brothers on ordinary days, when it came down to it, Heavenly Origin Dao Sect in the Mountains and Seas Realm was primarily based on clan power, and their relationships weren't that deep.

Zhan Buxiu was throwing his weight around here, but not everyone would buy it.

In the end, it was Mo Shouzhuo, the one in charge of Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, who stepped forward.

With a light cough, he drew everyone's attention, then said sternly:

"This battle must succeed, not fail. Even if we cannot defeat the demon phoenix, we must severely wound her and force her to linger in this realm. The follow-up cleanup will be handled by the main sect in the Mystic Realm. White Tiger Elder has already prepared to send a slaughter avatar to forcibly descend into this realm."

"Anyone who harbors wishful thinking, even if they escape with their lives for a moment, cannot avoid the main sect's punishment."

"Therefore, during the great battle, do not hold back. Be sure to exhaust all your strength and slay the demon phoenix!"

The men tightened their faces, pursed their lips, and nodded heavily in agreement.

Soon, the four of them gathered together with Chunyuan.

After gathering, the five Heavenly Origin elders didn't rest for a moment, immediately beginning various arrangements.

As for Azure Frost, the bait to lure Rosycloud here, her five senses and divine sense were blocked, and her entire body was covered with restrictions.

Everything was ready, only waiting for the demon phoenix to arrive!

...

Would she come?

This question surfaced deep in Luo Chen's soul.

Knowing it was an ambush, would that woman come?

If it were himself, Luo Chen certainly wouldn't fall for it.

But thinking of that woman's pride, that phoenix figure who had forcibly charged into the Nether Origin Sect, Luo Chen subconsciously arrived at the answer.

She would come!

Moreover, since the five Heavenly Origin elders dared to use Azure Frost as bait, it proved that this woman was extremely important to Rosycloud, and they weren't afraid she wouldn't take the bait.

This was an open scheme.

So, she would definitely come!

And the opportunity for him to leave lay at the very beginning of their engagement!

Not before the great battle, not after it, and certainly not during it.

Before the battle, if he rashly appeared, who knew if someone would attack him.

After the battle, whether he could even survive was another matter.

During the fierce battle, the same logic applied.

Only at the start of the battle, by charging into the Yellow Spring Sea without regard for anything, would no one have the spare hands to deal with him.

And this way, he could avoid being caught in the aftermath of the battle.

"Hope everything goes smoothly!"

Rocks were arranged in special positions, without any spirit stones as energy drivers, just the simplest secular mystical divination.

Luo Chen's physical body was hidden among them.

He hadn't set up any peculiar concealment formation.

In front of Ascendant great powers, the spiritual qi emitted by any ordinary formation would be sensed instantly. Instead, a maze formation without any spiritual qi could provide some cover.

His entire aura was restrained to the extreme, and even his divine sense gradually fell into slumber.

He only left a warning formation on his body using the Myriad Formation Gu.

Once the great battle outside began, and on Soul Island without any spiritual qi, he sensed a powerful spiritual qi fluctuation, this warning formation would awaken him.

At that time, he would flee into the Yellow Spring Sea at all costs!

And this opportunity came three days later.

...

"She's here!"

Zhan Buxiu, the strongest among the five Heavenly Origin elders, was the first to notice the anomaly in the Yellow Spring Sea.

In that murky yellow waves, a golden light was crossing the sea.

It was Rosycloud!

Zhan Buxiu's face showed high battle intent. He spoke, "Let me test her first, see what condition she's in. Then you activate the spirit treasure, trap her inside, and refine her together!"

Everyone nodded in agreement.

Although Rosycloud was strong, limited by the suppression of this world, the strength she could display was only at the Ascendant level.

Perhaps she had once possessed peak Body Integration stage attainments, but under this suppression, they were not incapable of fighting.

The fact that the three Nether Origin Sect ancestors had left severe wounds on her before dying was clear proof.

Among the five, Zhan Buxiu had the highest realm and the strongest strength. He could completely try to fight her head-on.

However!

As that golden light's speed skyrocketed, everyone's expressions grew increasingly grave.

A piercing shriek overpowered the endless roaring of the waves.

Gusts of wind surged, raising a thousand layers of waves.

Golden flames spread, as if wanting to scorch dry everything between heaven and earth.

The Yellow Spring Sea, which could drown all living beings, make Luo Chen sink, and even force the five Heavenly Origin elders to combine their artifacts to cross, seemed to be just an ordinary sea before Rosycloud, offering no resistance at all?

Two brilliant wings shuttled through the vast waves.

As if in just an instant, that lofty voice appeared at everyone's ears.

"I have come!"

The words were still echoing when a Nirvana Sacred Flame had already descended.

Zhan Buxiu charged into the sky, a vast sword light splitting everything, wanting to slay the demon phoenix.

*Boom!*

Without drawing on any heavenly and earthly spiritual qi, just the simplest collision of spells, the most direct impact of mana, in an instant stirred heaven and earth to change color, wind and clouds scattering.

Even the void seemed unable to bear this terrifying power, tearing open crack after crack.

Zhan Buxiu charged up as fast as he fell back down.

Seeing this, Mo Shouzhuo's pupils contracted, and he blurted out.

"Kill Azure Frost first!"

Chunyuan didn't hesitate, pointing out a finger.

At the cliff edge, the woman in green had ethereal hair and a calm expression, seemingly indifferent to all this.

The next moment!

From a void rift, a pure white wrist emerged, grabbing the woman in green and directly pulling her into the void rift.

Ancestor Chunyuan didn't dare touch this void rift and quickly withdrew her hand.

When she looked again, two figures were slowly emerging from the void.

One was proud and dignified, her gaze looking down on everything.

The other was cold and aloof, as if indifferent to everything outside.

And all this happened in the blink of an eye, too fast for anyone to react.

Rosycloud looked at the five Heavenly Origin elders, the corners of her mouth slowly lifting.

"Are you ready to bear my wrath?"

At that moment, Mo Shouzhuo slapped his palm toward the ground.

"Activate the formation!"

In an instant, immeasurable spiritual light burst forth from Soul Severing Cliff, rapidly spreading, wanting to cover the entire Soul Island.

Chunyuan's hand seals changed even faster, pointing a finger at Azure Frost across the void.

"Iron Tree Blossoms!"

A hazy green light emanated from Azure Frost's body, as if vegetation were sprouting branches and blooming flowers.

Rosycloud seemed to have anticipated this. Standing within the formation, she showed no fear.

Facing the anomaly on Azure Frost's body, she merely frowned slightly, pressing one hand on Azure Frost's shoulder.

One press!

That hazy green light was immediately suppressed, no longer showing signs of sprouting and blooming.

Another wipe!

Instantly, crackling sounds came from Azure Frost's body, and all the restrictions of varying sizes directly dissipated.

"Go," she said lightly.

Azure Frost nodded slightly, her form beginning to slowly dissipate into the void.

Seeing this, the five Heavenly Origin Dao Sect Ascendants' hearts sank.

This place made it difficult to communicate with heaven and earth's origin qi, and ordinary law true intents were mostly useless.

But the true intent of spatial laws seemed unaffected.

After all, time and space have existed since ancient times, omnipresent.

They had originally planned to kill Azure Frost first after luring Rosycloud here, but their pre-prepared methods failed one after another.

Thus, Azure Frost, who had been stably controlled, instead became a variable in the upcoming battle.

Just as their hearts were heavy, a spiritual light suddenly erupted in the distance, rushing toward the Yellow Spring Sea with lightning speed.

"Oh?"

Rosycloud exclaimed in surprise, a hint of wonder in her eyes.

Then she smiled, looking at the five Heavenly Origin elders.

"That one—isn't he a traitor to your Heavenly Origin Dao Sect? Are you just going to watch him escape like this?"

At this moment, the formation had just been activated, and indeed no one could spare a hand to deal with Luo Chen.

But!

Mo Shouzhuo's eyelids drooped, and he sent out a low voice, carrying far into the distance.

"Luo Chen, do you still want your life soul?"

At these words, the figure that had already merged into the Yellow Spring Sea suddenly froze in place.
