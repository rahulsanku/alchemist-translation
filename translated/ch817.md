# Chapter 817: The Raid List, Seven Sages Join Forces

"Three thousand years have passed since the ancient era!"

"Our Eastern Wilderness human race has blazed a trail through hardship, opened up territories, and carved out thirty-six domains, laying the foundation for a flourishing Eastern Wilderness cultivation world."

"Yet the demon beasts, wild and untamable, have taken the initiative to break the ancient covenant..."

After all the Nascent Soul cultivators had arrived, the City Lord of Lingtian began a lengthy speech.

His words sounded pleasant enough, but everyone present understood the truth—it was all about establishing a righteous pretext for action!

Each person appeared to listen attentively to his oration, but in reality, they were all waiting for the real content to arrive.

The City Lord of Lingtian did not keep them waiting long. After a pause, he spoke in a deep voice:

"The demon race's wolfish ambitions should be perfectly clear to all of you. They do not merely desire a single city or territory, nor one domain or ten—they want to swallow the entire Eastern Wilderness and exterminate our human race to the last!"

"Therefore, our Nether Origin Sect has taken the lead in building Lingtian Pass here, to block the advance of their demon army."

"As the saying goes, 'At the first drum, the morale rises; at the second, it flags; at the third, it is exhausted.' As long as we hold them back, those countless low-grade demon beasts—with their low intelligence and wild nature—cannot be driven for long, no matter how powerful the Demon Emperor. That will be the moment for our counterattack!"

"Clearly, the demon side also has capable individuals. They see Lingtian Pass as a thorn in their flesh, a nail in their eye, and wish nothing more than to uproot us."

"The encirclement two months ago was merely a probe!"

"If they had truly succeeded, Lingtian Pass would have become an isolated city. Without support, its fall would have been only a matter of time."

"We absolutely cannot sit idly by and wait for death!"

"Therefore, this City Lord intends to launch a surprise attack—to make them feel pain. At the same time, we will carve out a strategic buffer zone for Lingtian Pass."

A surprise attack!

At these three words, everyone's attention sharpened.

Someone asked, "Since it's a surprise attack, will we need to penetrate deep behind enemy lines?"

The City Lord of Lingtian glanced at the speaker and answered crisply, "Correct! And the tactical objective is simple—annihilate the Seven Rhinoceros Great Demon Emperor's division and send them into utter disarray!"

The demon race had nine great commanders, each of extraordinary strength, each leading their own army.

Among these nine commanders, the Seven Rhinoceros Commander controlled the largest number of demon beasts, making his momentum the fiercest—he had once charged ahead of everyone else.

The other commanders might have only five or six, or six or seven demon emperors assisting them.

But the Seven Rhinoceros Commander, at his peak, had ten demon emperors under him!

However, after several major battles, his losses had been considerable.

Within and outside Lingtian Pass alone, four demon emperors had fallen.

Clearly, the City Lord of Lingtian's thinking was straightforward: injuring ten fingers was not as good as cutting off one.

Taking advantage of the fact that the nine great commanders had not yet all arrived at Lingtian Pass, he wanted to sever the Seven Rhinoceros division and achieve the greatest possible results.

This was what he meant by "making them feel pain!"

The assembled cultivators' expressions shifted as they weighed the feasibility of the plan.

Nine Spirit Primogenitor suddenly spoke: "If we want to annihilate an entire army, the commotion will inevitably be enormous. Will the Yellow Barbarian Commander simply look the other way?"

Clearly not!

And there was one most critical issue.

"Once the operation begins, who will hold off two commander-level great demon emperors? Can Lingtian Pass's Four Directions Mysterious Swords strike enemies from ten thousand li away?"

At these words, everyone's expression changed drastically!

The deterrent range of the Four Directions Mysterious Swords was limited to a thousand li around Lingtian Pass.

But the battlefield for the surprise attack would necessarily have to enter places like Overlord Blade Domain—territory already occupied by the demon army.

If no one could restrain those two great commanders, any ordinary Nascent Soul cultivator who went there would be seeking death.

To this question, the City Lord of Lingtian glanced at the black-robed figure on his right and said slowly, "In this battle, this City Lord will personally take the field! With that, can you all rest assured?"

What!

The City Lord of Lingtian was going to fight personally?

Everyone was stunned.

"Not only that, but Fellow Daoist Shenyuan of the Five Elements Divine Sect will also go with us," the City Lord of Lingtian said, pointing to the black-robed figure beside him.

Shenyuan Sage's gaze swept over the crowd, and he spoke in a hoarse voice.

"City Lord Lingtian will personally lead this surprise attack to crush the Seven Rhinoceros army."

"I will take people to intercept the demon emperor reinforcements from Yellow Barbarian's side, ensuring that the main battlefield of the surprise attack is not disturbed by outsiders."

"Even if Yellow Barbarian himself makes a move, I will hold him back!"

With the two of them playing off each other, none of the assembled Nascent Soul cultivators could raise further objections.

After all, these were two great cultivators—one representing the holy land of Nether Origin Sect, the other long-renowned in the Eastern Wilderness with great prestige.

If anyone still tried to back out now, they would never be able to hold their head high among their peers again.

Moreover, looking at the several unfamiliar Nascent Soul cultivators from the sixteen rear domains who had appeared in the hall, it was clear that the City Lord of Lingtian was determined to see this battle through.

He had already made his plans—that was why this meeting had been called!

Seeing no one object, the City Lord of Lingtian broke into a smile.

"Excellent! Since no one opposes, let us now select the personnel for the battle."

"First, the main assault force!"

His gaze swept over the crowd as he effortlessly named several individuals.

"Nine Spirit Primogenitor, Pavilion Master Li, Golden Feather Sage, Iron View Daoist, Elder Fu, and Lingfengzi."

As soon as these six names were spoken, everyone understood the implications.

Among the six, aside from Lingfengzi, there was hardly a weak one!

Nine Spirit Primogenitor was said to have mana comparable to a great cultivator, and it was rumored that with the help of the Alchemy Sect recently, he had cultivated a great technique, advancing even further.

Pavilion Master Li of the Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion was also at mid-Nascent Soul stage, an orthodox sword cultivator with unmatched combat power.

Golden Feather Sage came from the Five Elements Divine Sect and cultivated the sect's most lethal technique, the *Three Golden Feathers of Death*. Back when the Five Elements Divine Sect was broken, two Nascent Soul cultivators had died, and Forestwood Sage had nearly perished as well. It was Golden Feather Sage who had fought fiercely against a powerful demon emperor to save the other's Nascent Soul.

Iron View Daoist was also quite renowned, hailing from the White Deer Cave in the sixteen rear domains. That sect had few disciples, but each was exceptionally skilled, and Iron View Daoist was among the best.

The remaining two were the only early Nascent Soul cultivators.

However, Elder Fu had governed the Divine Talisman Domain for centuries, possessed immense wealth, and had numerous tricks up his sleeve—even ordinary mid-Nascent Soul cultivators dared not underestimate him.

Only Lingfengzi was somewhat weaker.

But everyone knew, more or less, that Lingfengzi had been gathering intelligence for Lingtian Pass all these years. If anyone was most familiar with the demon armies, it was undoubtedly him!

From the composition of these six, it was clear how determined the City Lord of Lingtian was to win this battle.

An ordinary demon emperor was only equivalent to an early Nascent Soul cultivator.

The City Lord of Lingtian had immediately selected six such formidable individuals—he was clearly set on annihilating the enemy in a single battle!

"Does anyone have any objections?"

After reading out the list for the assault team, the City Lord of Lingtian asked with a chuckle.

Among the six, aside from Lingfengzi's bitter smile, there were no major objections.

"Next, Fellow Daoist Shenyuan, please select the seven who will accompany you to intercept the reinforcements!"

Shenyuan Sage nodded, his gaze sweeping over the crowd.

"Yellow Barbarian originally had eight demon emperors under his command, but the Nine Nether Enchanting Python died at Taihu Palace, so now only seven remain."

"Therefore, I will choose seven people."

"You need not worry—our task is to intercept and delay, not to fight to the death. We just need to buy enough time for the main assault force."

"Divine Fire!"

The first name he called was that of Divine Fire Sage, his own disciple.

If Golden Feather Sage, who had already joined the assault team, was included, then the Five Elements Divine Sect's high-end combat power was essentially being deployed in full.

With that, the others who were called had even less reason to refuse.

"Lin Qingxuan!"

"Qin Baixiang!"

"Cold Face Sir!"

"Thunderclap Daoist!"

"Fellow Daoist Chu Sheng!"

In the blink of an eye, six names were read out.

The last three were Nascent Soul cultivators from sects in the sixteen rear domains.

Only one position remained.

Shenyuan Sage's cold, gloomy gaze fell upon the white-robed man who sat calmly and unperturbed in the hall.

"I have heard that the Alchemy Sect has the glorious feat of slaying a demon emperor in battle. If you were to join us, you would surely become an unbreachable wall of bronze and iron that no one could cross!"

In an instant, everyone's eyes fell upon the white-robed cultivator.

Luo Chen of the Alchemy Sect, slayer of a demon emperor in battle!

Such a proud achievement was rare even among Nascent Soul cultivators.

For cultivators at their level, in times of peace, after forming their Nascent Soul, they rarely engaged in combat, and their battles against peers of the same rank were few and far between.

Let alone any notable combat records worth mentioning.

When people said so-and-so was formidable, it was usually based on friendly sparring between peers—never a life-or-death struggle.

But Luo Chen had risen like a blazing sun, and everything he did, even in the current chaotic times, still drew attention.

In the several major battles at Lingtian Pass, some had made names for themselves.

Lin Qingxuan, hailed as the "startling swan of the sword world," had the decent record of forcing back a demon emperor.

But compared to Luo Chen, it amounted to nothing.

At this moment, Shenyuan Sage's naming was entirely reasonable.

Under the gaze of the crowd, Luo Chen raised his head and blinked.

"To contribute to the survival of the human race is indeed our duty as cultivators."

"However, since you know I have the ability to slay demons, why not assign me to the assault team?"

"Fellow Daoist Lingfengzi, I would be willing to exchange places with you. Do you agree?"

Instead of seeking the relatively safer interception mission, he was volunteering for the assault mission that would inevitably involve life-or-death combat?

Luo Chen's move left everyone stunned.

Lingfengzi was the first to react. He looked at Luo Chen with considerable gratitude.

"The Alchemy Sect's abilities leave me deeply ashamed of my own inadequacy. This battle cannot afford the slightest mistake—the capable should take the lead. I am willing to exchange with you!"

Luo Chen smiled and nodded, then looked up at the man seated in the center.

"City Lord, what do you think?"

The City Lord of Lingtian's expression remained unchanged. "Since Fellow Daoist Luo is willing to place himself in danger, I naturally will not refuse. Fellow Daoist Shenyuan, let it be arranged this way!"

Shenyuan Sage was somewhat silent.

His black hood completely concealed his features, making it impossible to read his expression.

But as everyone waited, he finally gave his answer.

"Agreed!"

"Excellent!"

The City Lord of Lingtian clapped his hands in approval. "The battle roster has been decided. I will notify you of the specific time. The remaining fellow daoists will hold fast at Lingtian Pass, guarding against the enemy possibly launching a counter-raid on our now-weakened defenses."

Heavenly Capital Master, who had been mostly silent, chuckled and said, "With this old man here, Lingtian Pass is hardly weakened!"

"Haha, you're right. I was overthinking things."

"Then this old man wishes all of you fellow daoists a triumphant return, safe and sound!"

The previously tense atmosphere relaxed.

The Nascent Soul cultivators who had not been selected for battle all offered words of blessing, wishing Luo Chen and the others a safe return.

Luo Chen and the others responded in different ways, mostly with smiles on their faces and no trace of fear.

But what they truly thought in private was anyone's guess.

...

As he left the City Lord's residence, Luo Chen could feel a gaze fixed on his back.

He knew it was that old scoundrel Shenyuan.

A wise man does not stand beneath a dangerous wall. Even though, after weighing his own strength, he had the confidence to face a great cultivator, he had no desire to follow that man's orders in battle.

Therefore, he had volunteered for the more dangerous mission.

Still, a lingering disgust churned in his heart.

"Old scoundrel, pushing me step by step like this—do you really think I'm a soft persimmon?"

"Just don't let me find an opportunity!"

...

Regarding the matter of the surprise attack on the demon beast headquarters, the City Lord of Lingtian had ordered it kept secret.

Thus, life within Lingtian Pass continued as usual.

No one knew that soon, a great battle that might change the situation of Lingtian Pass's encirclement was about to unfold.

After returning, Luo Chen immediately went into seclusion to cultivate.

He only regretted that time was insufficient. If he could reach mid-Nascent Soul stage, he would be far more composed in everything he did.

War had always been like this!

In major battles, mana consumption was heavy. Aside from body cultivators who grew stronger through combat and needed battle to temper themselves, it often affected one's cultivation progress.

This was, in fact, one of the reasons for the decline of body cultivation.

After the ancient era, when the war between humans and demons ceased, body cultivation at low realms could not extend lifespan, so it naturally declined.

Now, with the Eastern Wilderness human-demon war lasting over a hundred years, demon beast flesh and blood—helpful for body cultivators—had appeared on a large scale, and the path of body cultivation was gradually emerging once more.

However, if other Nascent Soul cultivators knew of Luo Chen's complaints, they would probably just curse him for being ungrateful.

He at least had top-grade Hongyuan Pills and a fourth-rank mid-grade cave dwelling to support his cultivation. His progress might be slow, but he was still climbing step by step.

What conditions did others have?

Not having their cultivation realm drop was already fortunate.

While waiting for the notification, people from the Luo Tian Sect on the side of Bai Li Qingchuan were sent again.

They had been conscripted.

This time, Wang Yuan led the team, with Gu Caiyi and Qin Yuanjiang as assistants.

Luo Chen found time to meet with them.

"The body cultivation techniques from the Misty Sect that you gave me have indeed helped to supplement the Wind Leg, but they are still lacking something. I heard that some decent body cultivation techniques have recently surfaced at Lingtian Pass, so I came personally to collect a few," Wang Yuan said.

Under Luo Chen's observation, the other's blood and qi were even more vigorous than before.

It seemed that the demon emperor flesh and blood he had sent back was indeed a great tonic for him!

"Don't worry about matters back at the sect. Li Yingzhang is managing things, and Black King is sitting in the shadows. Nothing major will happen."

Luo Chen shook his head. "Of course, I'm not worried about the rear. As long as Lingtian Pass does not fall, the demon army will not dare to recklessly attack the rear."

After chatting with Wang Yuan for a while, he spoke privately with Gu Caiyi.

"Why did you come too? I told you to stay in seclusion and consolidate your realm."

"I've been in seclusion for six years! My realm has long been stable!" Gu Caiyi said with dissatisfaction. "Besides, you and Huiniang are both here at Lingtian Pass. How could I possibly cultivate in peace back there?"

Luo Chen was helpless, but he knew she wanted to share his burden.

He handed her the long whip magic treasure he had refined earlier and explained its general properties in detail.

"Does it have a name?"

Gu Caiyi flicked the whip a few times, her face full of delight—clearly, she was very satisfied with the treasure.

"It's your life-bound magic treasure. Name it yourself!"

Gu Caiyi thought for a moment. "Since it's made from the main sinew of the Nine Nether Enchanting Python, let's name it after that. The Nether Python Whip—what do you think?"

Luo Chen smiled. He wanted to say it wasn't great.

But thinking of his own life-bound treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, which he hadn't even renamed and had simply kept the name from the Azure Pill Valley's naming convention, he held back.

"You've given me such a fine gift—how should I repay you?"

"We're an old married couple. What repayment is there to speak of..."

Before he could finish, the woman's voluptuous body coiled around him like a great python, her fragrance filling the air, spring colors everywhere.

Luo Chen did not restrain himself and kissed her.

...

"Fellow Daoist Luo, you're in a good mood today!"

"Oh? How can you tell, Elder Fu?"

"Your face is full of spring breeze, and there's a smile in your brows. Anyone can see you're in a fine mood! Have you broken through a realm, or come up with a new pill formula?"

"Haha..."

Luo Chen laughed it off and did not elaborate to Elder Fu.

But he had to admit, certain primal activities could indeed ease the mind.

The exhaustion of many days, the constant scheming—all had unknowingly left Luo Chen's heart troubled and his brows furrowed.

Caiyi was indeed someone who understood him well.

Savoring the woman's fragrance in his mind, he walked with Elder Fu into a great mountain within Lingtian Pass.

That morning, he had received notice from the City Lord of Lingtian.

The battle would be today.

Along the way, they passed through a deep, hidden tunnel and finally stopped in a cave covered with array patterns.

With a single glance, Luo Chen recognized the nature of the formation at the center of the cave.

"Who told me there were no teleportation formations inside Lingtian Pass?"
