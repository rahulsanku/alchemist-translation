# Chapter 550: Nine Palaces and Eight Trigrams, Inviting the Wolf Into the House

"The area ahead is Xuanyan Island?"

High in the nine heavens, clouds covered the sky, black clouds spread everywhere, and wind and rain seemed about to arrive.

One dark cloud hid among them.

Luo Chen stood atop the cloud. The Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull, its body shrunk, stood under his feet and nodded like a chick pecking rice.

A sea chart landed in his hand. Luo Chen carefully examined it for a while, then pursed his lips thoughtfully.

The Xuanyan Sea Region was very large, its area even far surpassing the Jade Cauldron Domain he was familiar with. If truly calculated, it was as vast as two to three Jade Cauldron Domains.

But Xuanyan Island, from which the name originated, was actually not large.

As he looked down from afar with his spiritual eyes, on the blue great sea, island after island was scattered like stars, densely dotting the area.

The small island at the very center was foremost among them.

As a whole, it seemed to form a kind of maze.

One, two, three... seventy-two!

Counting carefully, there were actually a full seventy-two islands of varying size, arranged in rows around Xuanyan Island.

Comparing the sea chart with the real terrain, Luo Chen suddenly let out a soft sound of surprise.

"A formation?"

With his current formation attainment, he could already see some clues.

The islands below, combined with water veins and reefs, faintly formed a natural grand formation.

However, Luo Chen could only see a rough outline.

He immediately took out the Myriad Souls Banner and awakened Han Zhan.

After Luo Chen stated his guess, Han Zhan carefully examined it for a while and finally gave an answer.

"It is indeed a formation, but it was not naturally formed. There are human factors within it, and at minimum, it was personally arranged by a Sage-stage Fellow Daoist."

Nascent Soul Sages had boundless mana.

They were existences that could truly move mountains, fill seas, destroy cities, and sever peaks.

With the ability of such people, moving sea islands and arranging a grand formation with special terrain was not difficult.

Luo Chen humbly asked for instruction. "Can Senior see what grand formation this is, and what its effects are?"

"Eight Trigrams..." Han Zhan shook his head. "No, it should be a Nine Palaces Eight Trigrams Formation, secretly matching the number seventy-two. As for its effects..."

After pondering for a long while, Han Zhan slowly said, "Hiding wind and gathering water, moving qi and taking in spirit. It is the most common method of gathering veins. Although the Xuanyan Sea Region is vast, its spirit veins are scattered. This place's master arranged a grand formation, concentrating the spirit qi of an entire region here and forming a fourth-tier spirit vein. After several thousand years of evolution, it is already no different from a natural spirit vein."

At this point, he added, "In addition, this Nine Palaces Eight Trigrams Formation also possesses both offensive and defensive abilities. The seventy-two islands are interlocked, strong outside and weak inside. But if one ignores the outer islands and directly attacks the interior, it will instead form a desperate counterattack posture."

"If I were the master of this place, I would assign capable subordinates to the outer islands and station one person to guard the Central Palace."

Hearing this, Luo Chen was somewhat puzzled.

"Wouldn't this make the central hub empty, letting someone strike straight at the yellow dragon and carry out a decapitation strategy?"

Han Zhan laughed despite himself. "What if the one in the Central Palace is the strongest?"

Luo Chen suddenly understood.

That was right. In this way, the so-called strong outside and weak inside became a balanced posture.

If a truly powerful enemy struck straight at the yellow dragon, not only would they be met head-on by the expert in the Central Palace, they would also, under the encirclement of the seventy-two islands, form an awkward situation where they could neither advance nor retreat.

If the one sitting in the Central Palace were a Sage existence, then even if powerful enemies of the same tier invaded, unless they were several times more numerous, they probably could not break through Xuanyan Island.

After this analysis, he finally understood why, under the siege of the five great royal demon crab races for several years, the Ring-Headed Turtle race could still remain as stable as Mount Tai.

"That Xuanyan Demon Emperor truly acted meticulously, leaving not the slightest flaw!" Luo Chen said with emotion.

Han Zhan, however, held a negative opinion.

"Formations are dead, people are alive. From what I see, although there is a Demon Emperor aura on Xuanyan Island, it is extremely thin and rich in death qi."

Luo Chen froze.

"Xuanyan Demon Emperor is already dead?"

"At the very least, he is not in this land. Otherwise, he would not allow death qi to leak out."

Now Luo Chen understood why the five great royal demon crab races dared attack Xuanyan Island.

This was a case of no tiger in the mountain, so monkeys wanted to call themselves kings.

The demon crab side intended to overthrow those above them and become the true overlords of the Xuanyan Sea Region.

But in this case, it gave him an opportunity to exploit!

The Ring-Headed Turtles were at least a great race that had produced a Demon Emperor. Even if the Demon Emperor had already gone, their foundation still was not comparable to ordinary demon races.

As the two sides fought viciously, there would certainly be losses.

This gave Luo Chen the chance to reap the fisherman's profit.

However, right now, the priority was still to find a base and settle down.

Since he had already called Han Zhan out, Luo Chen naturally would not let go of such a helper.

"Senior's formation attainment, vision, and knowledge are all above mine. Why not find me a hidden and suitable place to settle?"

Han Zhan laughed. "Why would this matter need to trouble me? You have a sharp tool in your hands and can solve it yourself."

Luo Chen froze.

Then he saw an object drift out from the Myriad Souls Banner.

It was precisely the formation plate he had previously given Han Zhan.

Originally, he had planned to let Han Zhan modify it somewhat so that he could arrange some formations suitable for himself.

At this moment, seeing the formation plate fly out, he was somewhat puzzled.

Han Zhan immediately explained to him, "There are nine small formations and three grand formations upon it. Aside from the Sea-Sealing Eight Trigrams Formation and the Misty Cloud Dense Fog Formation, I have already thoroughly studied the final formation."

"Oh? I ask Senior to instruct me." Luo Chen's gaze flashed, and he felt moved.

"This formation is called Search Divinity Seek Spirit. It seems third-tier, but its effect is no lower than an ordinary fourth-tier formation."

"Search Divinity Seek Spirit?"

Hearing this name, Luo Chen could not help murmuring it again.

...

Above the clouds, the blurred Daoist sat cross-legged with closed eyes.

His hand supported a compass plate, whose mechanisms slowly turned.

This region's terrain, under the depiction of his divine sense, entered the compass plate one by one.

Suddenly!

The Daoist formed a seal with one hand and slowly tapped the compass plate.

In an instant, the mana-condensed compass needle began turning wildly. An inexplicable qi movement, under continuous traction, radiated in all directions.

Finally, the mana needle slowed and gradually pointed toward one place.

Luo Chen opened his eyes, revealing a trace of joy.

His figure moved, not leaking the slightest aura, and he flew in that direction.

After a long while, Luo Chen bypassed some patrolling demon beasts and slipped onto a nameless deserted island.

On the deserted island, there was only a half-abandoned first-tier spirit vein. Aside from some beasts without much intelligence, there were almost no living beings.

Luo Chen looked around and felt incomparably satisfied.

This island was within the formation, but it was not one link of the grand formation, nor was it among the seventy-two islands.

No one patrolled it, and no one paid attention to it.

It happened to be suitable for him to settle.

Thinking of this, Luo Chen could not help carefully examining the formation plate he had obtained from Zhu Qi.

Good thing!

Putting aside its offensive and defensive abilities, for cultivators traveling and training outside, it was simply a sharp tool!

The Misty Cloud Dense Fog Grand Formation was suited for concealing and covering a cave residence.

The Search Divinity Seek Spirit Grand Formation was suited for searching the qi movement of treasures and spirit veins. Once activated, it would automatically explore for the direction nearby where qi movement was strongest. Luo Chen did the opposite, thus finding the place where qi movement was weakest as the base for his next actions.

As for the Sea-Sealing Eight Trigrams Formation, in his eyes now, perhaps it was not used for battle either. Its original function should have been to seal a spirit land and prevent qi movement from leaking outward.

The reason it had suppressed Luo Chen within during the previous battle was actually because Zhu Qi had modified it and combined it with the limbs of his own body, forming a so-called suppressing formation.

But doing that also created a natural flaw, and thus Luo Chen broke it in one stroke.

Looking at it now,

this formation plate combined three major functions: concealing and covering a cave residence, searching and exploring the qi movement of spirit treasures, and sealing and suppressing. It was truly the perfect sharp tool for home, travel, murder, and arson!

"Good treasure. I do not know what you were called before, but from now on, you shall be called the Sealing Divinity Compass!"

Inspired by the names of the three grand formations and their effects, Luo Chen gave this treasure a new name.

Then he began busily working on this deserted island.

...

In the depths of the ocean,

a five-colored clam almost a hundred zhang in size slightly opened its shell.

A figure rode the ocean current and arrived with a boom.

He raised his head and glanced at this five-colored clam, snorting coldly. "Tch. It is merely the corpse of a five-colored clam that failed transformation. Wherever that old fellow Jin Hun goes, he brings it along like a treasure."

Although he said this, the envy in his eyes could not be faked.

As if it knew someone had arrived, colored light flashed on the five-colored clam, and the light screen opened.

That person moved his eight legs and walked inside.

As soon as he entered, a deep voice sounded from the warm, moist hall.

"Fellow Daoist Red Flame has arrived!"

Red Flame raised his head and looked. Three figures sat in three directions.

One was golden throughout, as if covered in golden armor, filled with tyrannical bearing.

One was wreathed in azure, adorned with several green algae, faintly revealing elegance.

As for the final demon, though it had shrunk its body, its sharp mouth protruded, and paired with eight legs and two claws, it gave people a ferocious and terrifying feeling.

If a human cultivator proficient in demon beast catalogues came here, they could identify the races of these three demons from their appearances. They were impressively the Overlord Crab, Azure Emperor Crab, and Nine-Clawed Poison King Crab races.

"Jin Hun, Green Sleeves, Nine-Claws, normally one never sees a trace of you, but this time you came so early!" Red Flame sneered, his words carrying a few traces of mockery.

Jin Hun did not speak, but Nine-Claws returned sarcasm with sarcasm.

"You old fellow normally hide in a dead volcano at the bottom of the sea, unwilling even to go ashore and bask in the sun. This time you ran out so eagerly, and you have the nerve to talk about us?"

Red Flame's anger immediately rose, and just as he was about to refute, a gentle and beautiful voice sounded.

"Stop arguing. Everyone has gathered here for the future of our demon crab races. Why make such a commotion?"

It was Green Sleeves, also the only female crab patriarch among the five great royal demon crab races.

Hearing this, Red Flame snorted coldly and no longer wasted words with Nine-Claws.

Seeing that everyone had quieted, Jin Hun, who presided over this meeting, slowly spoke. "We only await Zhu Xiang's arrival."

The Zhu Xiang he spoke of was precisely the Demon Spider Crab race's patriarch.

However, although Zhu Xiang had not yet arrived, Green Sleeves softly said, "Brother Jin, why not first explain the situation in detail? We can discuss it."

Jin Hun looked at the three demons. "The matter is not complicated. It is only the matter of attacking Xuanyan Island."

Red Flame snorted coldly. "We cannot even see Xuanyan Island's face. How can we speak of attacking it?"

"That is precisely what must be discussed." Jin Hun sighed. "No one expected that although Xuanyan Demon Emperor had gone, he still left the Ring-Headed Turtle race a life-saving grand formation. Formations... for us demon race members, they are simply a heavenly chasm, difficult to cross."

This emotion sank deeply into everyone's heart.

Over these years, in the battles with the Ring-Headed Turtle race of Xuanyan Island, they had experienced countless battles large and small.

The best attempt had only taken down twenty-seven sea islands.

But after the other side activated the formation to counterattack, six or seven tenths of those twenty-seven islands were retaken within a few days. At present, only nine islands were in their hands.

If this progress were spoken of, people would laugh themselves to death.

The five great royal demon crab races?

Just this?

Red Flame frowned, then snorted. "If it truly will not do, I will use my race's foundation and detonate the underwater volcanoes, washing over this sea region!"

Green Sleeves hurriedly stopped him. "That cannot be done. If it affects the fourth-tier spirit vein here, how will we advance to the transformation stage in the future?"

Red Flame was incomparably irritated. "This won't do, that won't do. You want the Xuanyan shed husk, but you also want to preserve the fourth-tier spirit vein. Do you not know the human saying that fish and bear's paw cannot both be obtained?"

Nine-Claws sneered. "The spirit vein can be shared, and the shed husk goes to the destined. How can both not be obtained?"

"You!" Red Flame's anger surged.

"Enough. Stop arguing."

Jin Hun coldly said, "All of us are either trapped at third-tier Perfected Completion or only one step away from the transformation stage. We came here for the opportunity to advance. No matter who obtains the opportunity in the end, there is at least some chance of advancing to the transformation stage. At that time, the remaining races will also count as having a backer. Right now is precisely the time for sincere cooperation. How can we act on emotion?"

Red Flame was stunned. "Your Overlord Crab race is the most domineering and overbearing. Jin Hun, since when have you considered the overall situation like this?"

Jin Hun glanced at him and coldly said, "With no Demon Emperor presiding over the Xuanyan Sea Region, it will sooner or later become a hunting area for humans. It is only these hundred years, with the human Holy Land collapsing, that we have had breathing room. Even so, have not cultivator forces received news and come here to hunt? If within the next few hundred years, none of us produces a transformation-stage Demon Emperor, then when the humans recover, I fear this Xuanyan Sea Region will have to change its name!"

This long speech came from the old crab's mouth. Although cold and indifferent, it carried a mountain-like pressure.

All the others fell silent after hearing it.

They all knew there was a demon-hunting ship wandering nearby.

It was only because the cultivators on the ship were hunting Ring-Headed Turtles. Otherwise, they would have long since joined hands and destroyed it.

Even so, they had already had several close clashes.

There was even one time when Golden Claw, an expert of the Overlord Crab race, violently tore apart a Core Formation cultivator alive.

If both sides had not held misgivings, they probably would have already used their full strength and fought to the death.

Seeing that everyone understood the general situation, Jin Hun let out a breath and continued, "The grand formation of Xuanyan Island is rather mysterious and complicated. With our knowledge, we cannot break it, and can at most attack by brute force."

"But the Ring-Headed Turtle race was once the number-one great race of Xuanyan Island!"

"The number of third-tier demon kings within far exceeds any one of our races. Only if the five races join hands can we suppress them."

"With the support of the formation, brute force cannot break it. Therefore, we must return to how to cleverly break the formation."

At this point, it seemed to become a dead end.

But Jin Hun's words turned. "As far as I know, Zhu Xiang has a junior he values highly. That junior obtained a human inheritance and knows a little of formations."

Everyone was somewhat surprised. There was actually a demon crab that could learn a human inheritance?

Green Sleeves hesitated. "Could it be that little fellow Zhu Qi?"

She knew the Demon Spider Crab race had produced a genius.

It was said that that junior had been born innately deficient and rather thin and weak, often bullied within the race.

Later, once, while wandering outside, he mistakenly entered a human cultivator's cave residence and obtained an inheritance. From then on, he began to rise.

After several hundred years, he had already become a third-tier expert!

Because of this, she had once wanted to arrange a marriage alliance with him, using a second-tier Azure Emperor Crab to rope him in.

"Precisely!" Jin Hun gave confirmation.

Green Sleeves suddenly understood. "If it is him, I feel there may be some feasibility."

Nine-Claws said sinisterly, "Here comes the problem. If we have Zhu Xiang's people act to break the formation, does that mean the spoils must be divided more to them afterward?"

Jin Hun was puzzled. "Nine-Claws, aren't you on the best terms with Zhu Xiang?"

Nine-Claws sneered. "Friendship is friendship, benefits are benefits. Everyone has simply not transformed yet. We are not ignorant wild beasts. I can still distinguish this much."

"Actually, giving them a little more is no problem. After all, what we want most..."

Just as the patriarchs of the four great royal demon crab races were discussing how to divide the benefits inside the mobile palace made from the five-colored clam corpse, the light screen flashed.

A figure walked in.

"There is no need to consider this matter. My Little Seven has already fallen."

Everyone followed the voice and looked. It was precisely the Demon Spider Crab race's patriarch, Zhu Xiang!

As expected of the Demon Spider Crab race with the sharpest perception. Even while outside, he had heard every word of the conversation inside the five-colored clam.

However, the information in his words: Zhu Qi had already fallen?

At first hearing this, everyone was greatly shocked.

Jin Hun's whole body flashed with golden light, and he said angrily, "How could this be!"

This time was the demon crab races' only chance to rise.

After all kinds of efforts and attempts, success or failure could basically be said to rest entirely on Zhu Qi alone.

Now he was being told that Zhu Qi had already fallen?

He could not accept it!

Zhu Xiang shook his head, a trace of grief in his eyes.

Immediately, he told everyone the details of Zhu Qi's fall one by one.

After a long while,

Jin Hun gritted his teeth and said, "It was actually the master behind that evil jiaolong and thief gull. Damn it! I felt something was wrong before and had Golden Claw, who had come out of seclusion, go resolve this matter. I did not expect we still missed the mastermind behind the scenes!"

Nine-Claws was somewhat at a loss. These years, among them, only the Nine-Clawed Poison King Crab race had not suffered Luo Chen's hidden attacks.

Therefore, his feelings on this were not deep.

Green Sleeves was also somewhat furious at this moment. "If we had not placed our attention on Xuanyan Island, we would definitely have sent out an army to catch that mastermind and tear him into pieces!"

Her Azure Emperor Crab race had also lost many low-level clansmen over these years.

Red Flame chuckled and did not particularly care about this.

Their Red Rock Crab race lived in underwater volcanoes, and when they occasionally came ashore, they moved in groups. They were rarely attacked.

He had heard that one time, they encountered a Black-Scale Jiaolong, but it had nearly been beaten to death by the Red Rock Crab elder leading the group.

Now that he heard the tragedies of the other three races, he actually felt some schadenfreude.

However, Zhu Qi was dead. How would they break the formation?

His schadenfreude also withdrew.

Zhu Xiang coldly watched the scenes in the hall.

Suddenly,

"I have a way to break Xuanyan Island's grand formation. But I have one condition. After the matter succeeds, you must all help me pursue and kill that mastermind!"

This condition did not matter.

And the method he spoke of drew everyone's attention.

For a time, the four demons stared at him without blinking, wanting to know what the method was.

Zhu Xiang stomped one leg on the ground, issuing a crisp sound.

This sound traveled along the five-colored clam shell and slowly reached the outside.

"Jin Hun, please open the door."

Jin Hun's expression moved slightly, and he stared seriously at Zhu Xiang.

His mouth moved slightly.

"I hope you are not inviting the wolf into the house."

The next moment, the light screen trembled, and a tall, upright figure entered their eyes.

His clothing was gorgeous, his face handsome.

As soon as he entered, he cupped his fists in greeting.

"Gu Shaoshang of Feiyun Ravine greets all Fellow Daoists!"

It was a human cultivator!

(End of chapter)
