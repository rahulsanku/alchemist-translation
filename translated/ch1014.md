# Chapter 1014: Life and Death Together, Soul Light Dims

Endless ocean, the vast Northern Sea.

Within an ancient palace at the bottom of the sea, three figures sat facing each other, the atmosphere oppressively heavy.

After a long while, two of the strange-looking individuals nodded solemnly, as if agreeing to something.

The elderly, refined scholar opposite them smiled faintly, cupped his hands toward the two, then calmly departed.

After he left, the remaining two exchanged a glance, their expressions extremely peculiar.

"Li Canghai actually willing to hand over the Northern Sea cultivation world—this truly caught this Emperor by surprise," Sea Emperor Whale said with a click of his tongue. "Beili, what do you make of this?"

Great Sage Beili pondered for a moment, then slowly said, "It's actually not hard to understand. With the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's army withdrawing, even if we haven't yet had the chance to continue our advance into the human heartland, the Northern Sea will sooner or later become our bag. Although Li Canghai possesses earth-shattering formation mastery, he can only hold one place. On the other hand, the fractured Central Continent holds boundless opportunities—that place suits him better."

Sea Emperor Whale grumbled, "Then are we really going to help him?"

Great Sage Beili tapped the table and said decisively, "Help!"

Sea Emperor Whale hesitated. "But that's the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!"

Great Sage Beili laughed heartily. "The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect of the past might have inspired fear, but the current Dao Sect is merely an empty name. Besides, we don't need to act personally—at most, we'll dispatch some sea beasts to cause them trouble. And the Northern Sea? We never lack sea beasts!"

This was indeed true.

Among the five continents of the Mountains and Seas Realm, the region with the most demon beasts was not the barren Western Desert, nor the Eastern Wilderness with its claim of a million mountains, but the Northern Sea, which occupied the majority of the realm's territory.

Back in the battle that destroyed the Primordial Demon Sect, the Northern Sea had assembled a billion-strong demon beast army.

That war annihilated the Primordial Demon Sect, and the demon race suffered heavy losses. Yet in just three hundred years, they had recovered much of their vitality.

If the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect hadn't intervened, the Northern Sea would have been completely theirs by now.

Now that Li Canghai understood the situation and was wise enough to yield the Northern Sea cultivation world, they were happy to avoid a major conflict.

As for what he sought in the Central Continent, Great Sage Beili and Sea Emperor Whale were willing to send demon beasts to harass the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, as long as they didn't have to act personally.

After reaching consensus, the two began discussing Li Canghai himself.

"His cultivation level is inferior to ours, but his formation skills are truly miraculous. Defeating him is easy, but killing him is far too difficult."

"Indeed. Especially the Mountain-Sea Seal he personally refined—it actually reached the grade of a spirit treasure. That's simply inconceivable. How long has it been since he achieved Ascension?"

"Infuriating! We've been Ascendants for nearly a thousand years and still don't have a single spirit treasure to our name, yet he refined one in just a few hundred years. Are human cultivators simply born more suited to refining spirit treasures than we are?"

"At least after conquering the Primordial Demon Sect, we gained something. My Sea Emperor Halberd, after another century or two of nurturing, can be upgraded. Beili, why haven't you moved to refine a spirit treasure? Are you still coveting that Yuan Magnetic Divine Light? Don't think about it. That place was tampered with by that old soul-refining bastard before he died—it can never be integrated into a magic treasure. In my opinion, you should listen to Rosycloud's advice and steadily refine a spirit treasure."

"Rosycloud... she cannot be trusted. As for the spirit treasure, I'll think about it some more."

...

That figure emerged from the Sea Emperor Palace, each step carrying him a hundred li away.

The slightly fishy, salty sea breeze blew against his face, lifting some graying hair and revealing the old man's refined, weathered features.

He was the Northern Sea's sole human Ascendant—Li Canghai!

He glanced back toward the Sea Emperor Palace and let out a soft laugh.

What did humans or demons matter to him?

All he sought in this life was to ascend and become an immortal.

To ascend, one needed a massive surge of spiritual energy to lift them, allowing them to ride the clouds and ascend.

And in the Mountains and Seas Realm, the place with the most abundant spiritual energy had always been the Central Continent!

Even now, with the Central Continent shattered, the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain still retained its foundation.

As long as he seized Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain, he would ascend and leave—let the world drown in chaos if it wished!

To him, whether allying with the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect in the past or with the two great ancient demons now, it was merely a choice in his cultivation path.

So-called racial distinctions had never affected him.

But before that, he could use the banner of human righteousness to mobilize the Cang Hai Alliance, wearing down the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's strength. Ideally, he would force the remaining Liancheng great power to act, exposing some weakness.

Thoughts flickering through his mind, Li Canghai stepped forward one stride at a time, moving farther from the Sea Emperor Palace and closer to the human heartland.

Suddenly!

When he reached a certain canyon, his figure paused slightly.

Looking down, he heard roars echoing.

Li Canghai was startled. "A desolate beast?"

He revealed his form and descended.

Soon, the scene before him fully entered his view.

Countless black-scaled giant pythons lay stunned, scattered haphazardly across the canyon.

And a black dragon, fully six hundred zhang long, rose and fell in the churning seawater, its aura visibly and rapidly declining.

Li Canghai remarked in surprise, "This beast is clearly in its prime, with abundant blood qi—why does it appear to be on the verge of death and fall?"

Gazing at the majestic, handsome black dragon, Li Canghai felt a desire to subdue it.

Although he couldn't identify its origin, such a desolate beast must be an ancient remnant. If raised well, he might one day gain a fifth-rank desolate beast mount.

A fifth-rank desolate beast was no weakling!

The collapse of the Central Continent had involved four great desolate beasts.

In the Mountains and Seas Realm, desolate beasts were even more favored than human cultivators.

With this thought, Li Canghai hesitated no longer. He reached out and released a small seal.

On the seal, towering mountains and scattered lakes and seas were carved.

In the blink of an eye, the Mountain-Sea Seal enveloped the boundless canyon, covering the black dragon.

A faint, dark light slowly descended, isolating the inside from the outside.

The black dragon struggled to open its pupils and saw Li Canghai.

At this moment, its aura was extremely weak. It only managed to say "Master" before falling unconscious.

Li Canghai was taken aback. "Master? Does this beast already have an owner?"

...

Central Continent!

After the brutal battle twenty years ago, the once-dominant Central Continent of the Mountains and Seas Realm had shattered into five landmasses.

One of them, ten years ago, had even been rammed and sunk by the legendary top-tier great desolate, the Mourning Soul Desolate!

All the families and cultivators on it, along with the vast land, had been swallowed by the Yellow Spring Sea, their whereabouts unknown.

Of the remaining four landmasses, the Heaven-Earth Peak at the original center was now completely unsuitable for human habitation or cultivation.

Heaven-Earth Peak had broken!

That place had once been a fifth-rank spirit vein, no weaker than Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain.

Due to the mountain's fracture, violent spiritual energy had been released, constantly sweeping the surroundings.

If an Ascendant great power were willing to spend time and carefully regulate it, they might restore it to its former state. But the current Heavenly Origin Dao Sect clearly lacked the manpower and energy.

Of the former Five Elders of Heavenly Origin, only one remained.

And having escaped from Broken Soul Cliff, he had expended a great deal of his origin qi—how could he spare the effort to restore Heaven-Earth Peak?

Inside Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain.

Liancheng, his face slightly pale, looked at the old man opposite him with hope in his eyes.

"Ke Lianshan, are you confident?"

Chess Saint Ke Lianshan faced the ancestor's earnest gaze and nodded solemnly.

"Although the destruction of the Peach Garden caused me to fail in comprehending the true meaning of the laws, centuries of cultivation have ultimately given me the foundation to condense my primordial spirit. When the time comes, I only need to open the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard and retrieve my life soul, and I will be able to smoothly break through to the Ascendant realm."

Hearing this, Ancestor Liancheng couldn't help but cough heavily several times.

"Take out the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard and let me have a look."

Chess Saint Ke Lianshan did as instructed, placing a square, rotten wooden chessboard before Ancestor Liancheng.

Compared to its peak state, this rotten wooden chessboard now had a broken corner.

In particular, the white stones on it numbered fewer than one hundred and eighty!

In the battle that year, to protect the Central Continent, Sect Master Mo Shouzhuo had exhausted himself driving the spirit treasure Rotten Axe-Handle, but ultimately could not prevent the continent's collapse.

Not only that, even the white stones, renowned as offensive treasures, had been mostly consumed, leaving only eighty-one.

And the black stones, which had soul-protecting effects, had all become dim.

All of this was thanks to Rosycloud!

Ke Lianshan couldn't help but ask, "Ancestor, can the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard be opened now?"

Ancestor Liancheng shook his head, his tone despondent. "It's very difficult. This spirit treasure was originally used to suppress the spiritual energy of the Central Continent. The continent's collapse dealt it unimaginable damage, and the artifact spirit has automatically entered a dormant state. To awaken it, either Senior Brother Mo Shouzhuo must still be alive, or you, who have gained its recognition, must advance to Ascension."

Ke Lianshan's aged face showed difficulty. "But if I can't open it and retrieve my life soul, how can I possibly advance to the Ascendant stage?"

Unlike other sects in the Mountains and Seas Realm, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect placed very few restrictions on its disciples.

This had led to the proliferation of aristocratic families and great houses.

But this surface-level leniency concealed the most extreme form of control.

That was: once a Dao Sect cultivator reached the Sage realm, a portion of their life soul would be extracted and kept within the sect.

This was not the conventional "soul lamp" or "life tablet."

Ordinary soul lamps and life tablets merely captured a wisp of a disciple's soul aura, used to monitor the life status of important disciples.

Their warning function far outweighed their control function.

But the life soul extracted by the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect was truly an important component of a cultivator's soul!

Without this piece, it was nearly impossible to break through to the Ascendant stage.

Thus, countless sects across the world had varying degrees of defectors, even holy lands like the Nether Origin Sect and the Primordial Demon Sect.

But the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, in three thousand years, had never had a single Sage-level cultivator defect.

What they relied on was this extreme form of soul control!

For this reason, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect even used this method on their guest elders.

That is, those who joined the Star Gate and cultivated on Heaven-Earth Peak as foreign cultivators.

Of course, as long as a cultivator showed hope of breaking through to the Ascendant stage, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect would return that portion of their life soul to its host.

And the vessel that stored these life souls was the spirit treasure—the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard!

But now, a problem had arisen!

Only two people could activate this spirit treasure: the former Sect Master Mo Shouzhuo, and Ke Lianshan, who had gained the artifact spirit's recognition.

Mo Shouzhuo had already fallen. And with the artifact spirit asleep, Ke Lianshan, at the Sage realm, simply could not open the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard.

An awkward impasse!

*Thump!*

Ancestor Liancheng slammed the table before him heavily, anger rising on his face.

"Demon Phoenix!"

Ke Lianshan knew who the "Demon Phoenix" his ancestor was cursing referred to. He asked curiously, "The five of you joined forces, and she had already been severely wounded in the two battles at Nether Origin and Central Continent. Rumors say she even borrowed the power of the top-tier great desolate, the Mourning Soul Desolate, at the time. Even so, you couldn't kill her?"

"She is a phoenix from the upper realm, an existence that even the Great Sovereign could not subdue. For us to kill her—how could it be easy?" Ancestor Liancheng spoke with anger and a hint of fear, slowly revealing some secrets. But then, he sneered, "Although she didn't die, she is certainly not well. Once you advance to Ascension and reunite the Central Continent, and I rebuild Heaven-Earth Peak to receive the White Tiger Elder's avatar descending to this realm, we will surely obliterate her!"

Reunite the Central Continent? Rebuild Heaven-Earth Peak?

How easy was that!

Ke Lianshan looked helplessly at his ancestor. You need to open the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard for me first!

Ancestor Liancheng seemed to realize the problem and fell into deep thought.

After a long while, he had a plan in mind.

"You may withdraw first. I have a general plan, but there are still some preparations to make."

Ke Lianshan reminded him, "Ancestor, my time is limited. This matter must be carried out before my lifespan runs out."

After speaking, he bowed respectfully and withdrew.

After he left, Ke Lianshan remained immersed in his earlier thoughts.

In his view, restoring the Rotten Axe-Handle artifact spirit was not something that could be accomplished overnight. What was lacking was simply time.

And time... the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven!

Just as he was thinking, footsteps suddenly sounded outside the door.

Ancestor Liancheng frowned without looking up. "What is it?"

The disciple outside said respectfully, "From the Eastern Wilderness, news has come regarding Luo Chen. This person is one you specifically ordered to be watched, so upon receiving the information, this disciple came immediately to report."

"Luo Chen!" Ancestor Liancheng exclaimed in surprise. "He's still alive?"

The disciple outside received permission, entered, and respectfully presented a jade slip.

Ancestor Liancheng swept his divine sense over it and understood the gist.

Then, his face turned gloomy.

Not only was Luo Chen not dead, but he had appeared in the Western Desert and had even joined forces with the Demon Phoenix's younger sister, Azure Luan, to fight against powerful enemies.

The thirty-six eminent monks of the Hanging Temple had formed a formation capable of dealing with Ascendant great powers, and the two of them had broken it together.

Now, the world was spreading their fame, claiming that the two of them together could slaughter all the Sages under heaven!

Truly exaggerated!

"I knew that brat was an ungrateful wolf. He verbally agreed to Senior Brother Mo Shouzhuo, then turned around and made peace with Azure Luan."

"Fine. Once I open the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard, I will find his incomplete life soul, destroy his soul, and cut off his chance at Ascension!"

As he muttered to himself, the disciple below had not yet withdrawn.

"Is there something else?"

The disciple lowered his head and said, "Luo Chen not only appeared in the Western Desert but also seems to have returned to the Eastern Wilderness, with some traces showing. The elder we left in the Eastern Wilderness asks whether we should persuade him to return to the sect?"

Ancestor Liancheng raised an eyebrow. Execute him, or take him in as a dog?

The image of Luo Chen's battle with Azure Frost back then vaguely surfaced in his mind.

Such talent must never be allowed to advance to the Ascendant stage, or he would be difficult to control.

But if taken in as a subordinate, he might make a good enforcer?

Hesitating, his gaze gradually fell upon the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard.

Suddenly, the corner of his eye twitched.

One of the black stones had become even dimmer.

The recovery of the Rotten Axe-Handle artifact spirit was far less optimistic than imagined!

Luo Chen's matter was of little concern. The urgent task was to restore this spirit treasure, which was the foundation of the Dao Sect!

...

Eastern Wilderness!

Green Parasol Mountain.

The massive Azure Luan's true body had been lying within the Daytime Void Hall for ten years now.

A hazy white light continuously radiated from within her body, making her seem illusory, as if at any moment she would merge into the void and disappear from this world.

Suddenly!

Azure Luan began to tremble violently. Threads of blood spread from her heart, permeating her entire body.

The blood threads were fragile, as if they would snap at any moment.

Above the Daytime Void Hall stood the thousand-year-old Profound Passage Hall.

Its master seemed to sense something, forcibly awakening from slumber and casting a dazzling golden light that enveloped Azure Luan.

"Something happened to Luo Chen?"

A proud, domineering voice sounded, tinged with faint confusion.

But soon, the owner of the voice realized what was happening.

"What boldness. I thought he would never dare to undergo a soul nirvana in this lifetime."

Though the voice was mocking, compared to its usual lofty indifference, it now carried three parts of approval.

The golden light gradually faded, as if unconcerned with the divine bird Azure Luan's life or death.

And indeed, that was the case. The suddenly appearing blood threads vanished into Azure Luan's body after a moment.

The trembling Azure Luan slowly calmed, once again letting the hazy white light envelop her.

As if nothing had happened.
