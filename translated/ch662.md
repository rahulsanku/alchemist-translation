# Chapter 662: Neither Human, Nor Demon, Nor Devil; He Is called Devil Lord Heaven-Refining

Within the Thousand-Ren Mountains [TERM?], countless strange beasts occupied the land.

A green squirrel leapt onto a jade tree, squeaking as it gnawed the tenderest branch.

Suddenly, it stopped chewing and looked around in confusion.

It seemed a light breeze had brushed past.

Whoosh...

When it looked again, it saw nothing. The green squirrel puzzledly scratched its mouth.

A dark light moved close to the ground and glanced back at the small squirrel.

"A strange beast with very sharp perception. But it still cannot compare to that red-robed Daoist. He actually sensed me and nearly found where my true body was..."

Youya lowered her speed, still feeling somewhat afraid.

Red robes, domain, flying swords, azure palms, domineering and peerless fire-attribute mana, especially... that pair of eyes containing stars and moon.

When had the Northern Sea cultivation world produced such a figure?

He could instantly kill three cultivators of the same rank and reverse-kill a ninth-level Golden Core late cultivator while only at the sixth level of Golden Core.

If spoken aloud, probably no one would believe it.

"I even wanted to divert calamity eastward, borrow another's blade to kill, then act as the oriole behind and seize that supreme-grade Nine Yang Geng Metal [TERM?]. Yet I was almost kept there."

"Those eyes..."

Youya secretly swallowed, her slender body trembling slightly.

High Man Red Corpse [TERM?] definitely was not weak.

Even if she faced him head-on, the odds between her and High Man Red Corpse [TERM?] were only sixty-forty. Especially since this person controlled two refined corpses comparable to late Golden Core cultivators. Once they formed an encirclement, forget sixty-forty; even thirty-seventy would be hard to say.

Yet such an existence had only lasted three exchanges in that red-robed Daoist's hands before perishing.

"A strange range spell that affects mana mobilization, somewhat like a Sage's domain."

"Instantly erupting extreme speed, arriving like thunder exploding through the air."

"An illusion art that unfolds soundlessly as long as eyes meet, and especially extremely sharp perception."

"This person completely counters me. If I do not encounter him later, fine. If I do, I absolutely cannot make him an enemy."

In a short moment, Youya relied on her memory of secretly observing earlier and analyzed many of the red-robed Daoist's methods, finally reaching a conclusion.

Suddenly, her figure stopped at an abandoned cave.

Uncertainty appeared on Youya's face.

"Now that this secret realm has opened, it has drawn countless powerful experts to explore. Even a Golden Core cultivator I have never heard of is this formidable, let alone those famous figures."

"I originally thought I could move like a fish in water in this secret realm by relying on my great accomplished Shadow Escape Art [TERM?]. Now it seems I was somewhat fanciful."

"No, my current methods are insufficient. I must go one step further."

"Fortunately, I have the treasure in hand to bring my escape art to a higher level."

The woman lifted her hood, revealing an exquisite but dark and shining face. With a flick of her hand, that cluster of Blue Evolution Lead [TERM?] appeared.

With this item, her concealment methods could not only become more powerful, she could even artificially create shadowed places and escape the innate shackles of her shadow escape art.

If she truly cultivated to that level, forget the red-robed Daoist discovering her. Even Sages would likely have difficulty detecting where her true body was.

After making her decision, Youya hesitated no longer and stepped into that abandoned cave.

At the very center of the Demon-Fall Land [TERM?], a sky-reaching tornado stirred wind and clouds, rotating without cease.

Several figures shot over.

Raising their heads to look at the enormous spiritual qi tornado, all their eyes held awe.

The leader was a handsome man dressed in blood-red robes.

After looking at the distant tornado for a while, he said faintly, "I did not expect the demon race experts to seize the lead and enter the inheritance land early."

The cool young matron behind him said coldly, "If you had not insisted on collecting resources, how could Cape Heaven Matron [TERM?] and the others have taken the lead?"

The handsome man smiled. "Feileng, do not worry. If this inheritance were truly so easy to obtain, my Primordial Demon Sect would have taken it all long ago over the past thousand years. How would it be our turn?"

This cool young matron was surprisingly Fairy Feileng, who was quite famous in the Northern Sea cultivation world.

She frowned. "Blood Nightmare, I do not care about any inheritance. I only want to find what was lost inside back then."

"What exactly is it that makes you think of it so constantly?" Blood Nightmare Moluo was extremely curious.

"Only a painting."

Fairy Feileng gave a simple answer, then looked as though she did not want to say more.

Blood Nightmare Moluo smiled. "Fine. Since I promised you, this seat will naturally fulfill the promise. Besides, I also want to find where my sect ancestor died. Let us go. We enter the teleportation array first. The inheritance land is in another heaven. Without the teleportation array, we cannot enter."

With a wave of his hand, he took the lead forward.

Fairy Feileng followed step by step, but after walking two steps, she frowned and looked back.

"Third Elder?"

Behind them was an old man with immortal bearing.

His name was Jiyuan, and he came from the Penglai Immortal Sect.

At this moment, he looked unwillingly at the scenery behind him. "A pity about the countless heavenly materials and earthly treasures in this Demon-Fall Land [TERM?]."

Fairy Feileng frowned. She knew very well that once they entered the inheritance land, there would be no way to return.

And in this secret realm, countless treasures rarely seen outside were hidden. Even for Sages like them, they were extremely precious.

Elder Jiyuan was entering for the first time, so some reluctance was understandable.

Without turning his head, Blood Nightmare Moluo said, "Let the juniors collect a few external objects for us. What matters most now is entering the inheritance land and seeking the opportunity to become Ascendants!"

Once the words "opportunity to become Ascendants" came out, Old Daoist Jiyuan finally dispelled his greed.

Not dispelled, but replaced by greater greed.

"Fellow Daoist Blood Nightmare is correct. This old man was shortsighted. Let us go."

Old Daoist Jiyuan smiled and followed the three to the end where the sky-reaching spiritual qi tornado gathered.

It was inside a dark abyss.

As soon as they landed, Old Daoist Jiyuan nervously examined the surroundings.

The abyss was silent, without sound and without the aura of living beings.

Only mushrooms grew in the cracks of the mountain walls.

"Strange."

Old Daoist Jiyuan picked a mushroom, full of doubt.

"This place is where spiritual qi flows backward. Why do these mushrooms have no spiritual qi at all?"

Blood Nightmare Moluo was also puzzled. His understanding of the Demon-Fall Land [TERM?] mostly came from sect records. This was his first time entering.

If one wanted to speak of someone familiar with this place, it had to be Fairy Feileng, who had been here once before.

Seeing their gazes come over, Feileng snorted softly.

"Do not look at me. This place is strange. How could I know? Moreover, when I came here back then, I was only late Golden Core and was treated as..."

She did not continue, but a poor expression had already appeared on her face.

Blood Nightmare Moluo waved his hand. "Forget it. Let us not mention that for now. Prepare to enter."

"How do we enter?" Old Daoist Jiyuan was curious.

Blood Nightmare Moluo took a deep breath and pointed at the abyss with no visible bottom.

"This abyss has no bottom. Entering it normally causes one to become lost inside. Only when the ruins open and spiritual qi flows backward to form a tornado will the teleportation array appear."

"We only need to jump into that tornado and move against the flow to find the teleportation array."

"What I said is correct, Fairy Feileng?"

The woman nodded. "Indeed. But I must point out one thing. When entering, do not resist. Treat yourself as part of heaven and earth spiritual qi. Otherwise, you will be rejected by the formation."

Hearing this suddenly, Old Daoist Jiyuan's expression changed slightly.

"Such violent heaven and earth spiritual qi. If we do not resist, even if we do not die, we will be heavily injured."

Blood Nightmare Moluo calmly looked at him, his eyes seeming somewhat speechless.

Fairy Feileng said softly, "Very simple. Unfold your Sage domain and briefly merge with heaven and earth."

So that was how.

Old Daoist Jiyuan suddenly understood.

Although merging with heaven and earth carried great risk for a Nascent Soul, if it was only for a short time, it would be fine.

"Let us go."

Blood Nightmare Moluo waved his sleeve and leapt into the violent tornado. The other two followed close behind.

As the three entered, faint rumbling sounds came from the abyss.

And amid that rumbling, in a crack of the cliff, a thing like a lump of flesh trembled.

As its body trembled, some dust scattered out.

Most fell into the abyss.

A small portion clung to the rock wall, either forming green moss or turning into small mushrooms.

Only an extremely small amount was carried out of the abyss by the wild wind formed by the spiritual qi tornado.

As these things were shaken out, the lump of flesh's aura weakened somewhat.

It resentfully glanced at the bottomless abyss, then looked from the lightless crack toward the outside world.

"They have come. They are coming."

"The Demon-Fall Land [TERM?] has three heavens. One is Leaving Forgetting Heaven [TERM?], one is Five Elements Heaven [TERM?], and another is named Night Bright Radiance [TERM?], also called Bright Radiance Heaven [TERM?]."

"You have seen Leaving Forgetting Heaven [TERM?]. It is covered in countless illusions, bewildering in five colors. One moment of carelessness and one will sink inside. Especially those with unstable Dao hearts, or existences with flawed souls, are easily trapped there. Ding Yi died like that. His soul was injured by my remnant thought in earlier years, which caused him to be unable to pass through Leaving Forgetting Heaven [TERM?]."

"That place is also the natural barrier separating this place from the outside world. According to rumor, Leaving Forgetting Heaven [TERM?] was not built to guard against cultivators, but against the legendary heavenly demons. Inside, you did not trigger Leaving Forgetting Heaven's [TERM?] greatest power at all. What you saw was only the tip of the iceberg."

"As for Bright Radiance Heaven [TERM?], I have not been there. But that is what you human cultivators call the inheritance land. Over the past thousand years, I have seen many Primordial Demon Sect experts enter it, including Ascendant great powers!"

Three streaks of escape light carefully flew through the Thousand-Ren Mountains [TERM?].

They tried to avoid those towering peaks and, as much as possible, avoid stepping into regions where strange beasts existed.

Tai Sui, who occupied Ding Yi's fleshly body, satisfied Luo Chen's curiosity and explained the layout of this secret realm.

Luo Chen listened seriously. When he encountered something unclear, he interrupted Tai Sui and asked in detail.

When he learned that they had not triggered Leaving Forgetting Heaven's [TERM?] greatest power at all, a trace of relief arose in his heart.

Tai Sui was somewhat surprised. "You said you encountered the Heart-Dimming Illusion [TERM?]?"

Luo Chen did not understand. "Is something wrong with that?"

Tai Sui said uncertainly, "I remember the Heart-Dimming Illusion [TERM?] seems to be a barrier set up for heavenly demons. You..."

Cave Master Moyun's eyes were somewhat frightened. "Devil Lord, you would not be a heavenly demon, would you?"

Luo Chen's expression chilled, then he snorted. "Have you ever seen a heavenly demon only at the Golden Core stage?"

That was true.

The legendary heavenly demons were formless and bodiless, able to cross boundary power and travel.

Under ordinary circumstances, they would not appear at all. Only during tribulations, when heaven and earth fused with a cultivator's soul, would heavenly demons seek an opportunity to arrive.

Luo Chen was only at the Golden Core stage. How could he possibly be a so-called heavenly demon?

Tai Sui also came back to his senses. "It was probably because your killing was too heavy and your heart was covered in dust, causing you to accidentally trigger the Heart-Dimming Illusion [TERM?]."

Luo Chen curled his lip. "Tell me about Five Elements Heaven [TERM?], one of the three heavens."

His face showed indifference, but a few shadows covered his heart.

Tai Sui cleared his throat and explained what Five Elements Heaven [TERM?] was.

"This place's master had supreme cultivation, profound beyond measure. With the power of the five-element laws, he divided five domains. These five domains are Thousand-Ren Mountains [TERM?], Wood Heaven Plain [TERM?], Crossing Vast Billows [TERM?], Blazing Refinement Prison [TERM?], and Flowing Sand Sea [TERM?]. Each place condenses large amounts of five-element essences. Not only that, that person used great divine ability to enlighten five-element essences into spirits, causing the five domains to circulate and endlessly generate."

"Together, these five domains are Five Elements Heaven [TERM?]."

These few words were like thunder exploding.

Especially Luo Chen and Han Zhan residing on him were filled with disbelief.

His figure paused, stopping in the air.

"How is that possible? Even if he was a Void Refining True Lord, it is impossible to be proficient in five types of law power, and even enlighten spirits!"

Tai Sui also stopped and said faintly with a strange look, "He was a Void Refining True Lord, but not an ordinary True Lord. He was the strongest Void Refining True Lord from ancient times to now."

What kind of gaze was that?

Awe, worship, childish longing, mixed with resentment and fear.

Many emotions, all different.

Cave Master Moyun shook his head. "If this is true, being proficient in five types of laws is truly terrifying."

"More than five!"

Amid Luo Chen and Cave Master Moyun's shock, Tai Sui said softly, "True Lord was a peerless genius rarely seen in ten million years. Cultivation was as simple to him as eating and drinking. At least, I saw him use no fewer than ten types of law power."

The corner of Luo Chen's mouth twitched. "Fellow Daoist Tai Sui, are you still describing a person?"

He already felt the other party was deliberately exaggerating this place's owner to frighten them.

However, Tai Sui nodded matter-of-factly.

"He indeed was not human, but a devil, half human and half devil. He even had heavenly demon bloodline! Of course, you can also say he was an existence neither human, nor demon, nor devil."

This time, Luo Chen fell completely silent.

Only after a long time did he ask softly, "What was his name?"

"How could a tiny being like me know True Lord's personal name? But I once heard an absolute peak expert who entered this realm call him Devil Lord Heaven-Refining [TERM?]!"

Devil Lord Heaven-Refining.

Silently repeating this Dao title, Luo Chen started moving again.

No wonder this place was called the Demon-Fall Land [TERM?].

It turned out this place's owner was a devil race powerhouse.

He wanted to ask more information, but Tai Sui was unwilling to say more.

Or rather, he did not know that much.

According to Tai Sui's explanation, most living beings in this realm were spirits enlightened by Devil Lord Heaven-Refining [TERM?] using great divine ability. A small number were sentient beings like Tai Sui, who had lived here for long periods and gradually developed intelligence.

For example, the Five Elements Lotus Platform could be considered Tai Sui's junior.

Only, it had finally developed a tiny bit of intelligence and had not yet advanced to fifth tier when Luo Chen and these people set their eyes on it.

Because Tai Sui had been in this realm since the beginning of his consciousness, he did not know very much about Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's [TERM?] life.

What he knew was only the tip of the iceberg.

Five days later, three figures descended on a low hill.

Behind them were the Thousand-Ren Mountains [TERM?], like a forest of sharp swords.

"Within Five Elements Heaven [TERM?], the Thousand-Ren Mountains [TERM?] are the richest, but also the most dangerous. Over these years, even when Primordial Demon Sect experts entered to explore, they did not dare act too wantonly. Because inside are many powerful fourth-tier strange beasts, and even a sleeping fifth-tier iron-devouring strange beast [TERM?]."

"The route we take next is Blazing Refinement Prison [TERM?]. Your Five Elements Lotus Platform is currently in the prison."

Tai Sui exhaled and slowly spoke.

Luo Chen looked far into the distance. He did not see a prison, only a sand sea.

"Let us first cross this Flowing Sand Sea [TERM?]."

*(End of Chapter)*
