# Chapter 764: Wood Origin, Withering-Bloom Awakens

Summer-Knowing Grass!

It divided into two leaves, one on the left and one on the right, and usually grew at the beginning of summer.

Although its grade was not high, it contained a current of warm flame power within, making it especially suitable for crafting certain first-tier healing pills, and it had a rather good curative effect on yin-cold poisons.

The moment that touch of green appeared, with Luo Chen's experience, he recognized it instantly.

This sort of medicinal herb was not rare.

What was rare was that, in the depths of this cold winter day, it had somehow grown out!

Looking at the tender green leaf petals that slightly parted, Luo Chen was somewhat surprised.

The reason Summer-Knowing Grass was called "summer-knowing" was precisely because it only grew when early summer arrived. When people saw this grass, they knew summer had come.

And once summer and autumn passed, Summer-Knowing Grass would wither, while accumulating strength and quietly awaiting the arrival of the next summer.

He reached out to touch those leaf petals.

Suddenly, the Summer-Knowing Grass flopped limply into the white snow.

"It is actually this fragile?"

Luo Chen smiled, unconcerned in his heart, only treating this Summer-Knowing Grass as having misjudged the season and grown in a season unsuited to it.

He withdrew his hand, but a flash of insight passed through his mind, and his hand froze in midair.

Spiritual light trembled faintly in his eyes as he looked toward the various places across the mountains covered in white snow.

At some unknown time, specks of green had already appeared.

Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven was a secret realm that formed its own grotto-heaven, but the turning of the four seasons followed the outside world and was almost no different.

In such a cold winter season, this region should not have so much green!

"Could it be!"

Luo Chen's heart shook, and he subconsciously looked toward a low hill not far away.

It seemed perfectly ordinary, but in truth, its surroundings were filled with hidden formations laid down by Luo Chen!

Perhaps in terms of power, these formations could not compare to the Mountain-Suppressing Balance-Guarding Formation Gu Daosu had set up nearby during his sudden enlightenment, but in terms of concealment, they were first-rate.

Fu Qinglan was cultivating inside that mountain!

Luo Chen formed an incantation, and the formation slowly opened. Everything that entered his sight was green!

The closer it was to the cave residence where Fu Qinglan cultivated in seclusion, the more flourishing the vegetation became. Its vigorous vitality could be felt by Luo Chen even from very far away.

He subconsciously pursed his lips, wonder appearing in his eyes.

"Could this be the so-called wood-attribute origin power?"

Only this explanation fit!

Sage cultivators were powerful indeed, but they were far from reaching the degree where they could affect the external environment. Even when controlling the spiritual qi of heaven and earth, they still had to coordinate with their Sages.

These plants and trees were born according to the four seasons and the turning of sun and moon, relying precisely on the fundamental origin qi between heaven and earth.

At this moment, great numbers of plants and trees were being forced to grow out of season. There must be a great amount of the fundamental origin qi they needed gathering here.

But the Heavenly Dao had its constants. How could fundamental origin qi be born for no reason?

There was only one explanation: Fu Qinglan had comprehended origin power, and had thereby seized a great amount of corresponding origin qi from the sea of origin qi. Because her own control was improper, it leaked outward, finally affecting these plants that should have grown in spring, summer, and autumn, causing them to break through the earth in the cold winter day.

"She has finally entered the proper path!"

"In that case, my decades of running about, laboring, and keeping lonely watch over one place were not wasted."

Relief appeared on Luo Chen's face, and then he slowly closed his eyes.

What came next was simple. He only needed to stand the final watch and wait for Fu Qinglan to emerge from seclusion.

This length of time was hard to judge. Perhaps it would be very long, but it could also be very short. In the end, however, there was already an answer.

Only, just as Luo Chen closed his eyes, his mind shook.

Within the Purple Mansion!

The weak withered-white flame was turning from white to green, the flame plume rising without cease.

What the appearance of this scene meant went without saying!

The Withering-Bloom True Flame had awakened!

Its aura began to climb. Great amounts of azure flame steamed into the sky above the Purple Mansion, as though forming an azure cloud that enveloped Luo Chen's Sage.

At this moment, because Luo Chen had previously laid down the Withering-Bloom Divine Seal, the Sage was unlike its usual fair, tender self and appeared wrinkled instead.

Luo Chen's soul sank into it, and the Sage suddenly opened its eyes.

It trotted over to the Withering-Bloom True Flame, and the surging vitality shone upon the Sage's little face until it was rosy red.

"It succeeded after all!"

"Earlier, in the outside world, I thought it would only need a few years to recover. But ever since entering Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, the recovery of the Withering-Bloom True Flame became incomparably slow."

"I thought this was because this heaven and earth were different from the outside world, causing such a situation, and I was even thinking I would wait for it to awaken after we went out."

"Yet I never expected..."

The Sage murmured to itself, feeling the surging vitality emitted when that true flame awakened. It felt exceptionally comfortable.

Abruptly, Luo Chen thought of a possibility.

"Was it the fundamental origin qi leaked by Fu Qinglan being unconsciously absorbed by me, causing the Withering-Bloom True Flame to awaken early?"

The more Luo Chen thought about it, the more he felt this was so!

Because the transformation of the Withering-Bloom True Flame was from flourish to wither, then from wither to bloom.

What the latter half of its evolution needed was a great amount of vitality.

Previously, Luo Chen had feared the Withering-Bloom True Flame absorbing vitality and affecting his Sage. Because of the cultivation method he had created himself, Luo Chen had even specially set the Withering-Bloom Divine Seal upon the Sage to prevent the Sage's vitality from leaking out.

Now, Fu Qinglan had comprehended wood origin, which had filled this gap for him.

Next, he only needed to wait for it to transform naturally.

Luo Chen smiled faintly, left a divided soul stationed within the Sage, and returned his main consciousness to his sea of consciousness.

He looked forward greatly to what level the fully recovered Withering-Bloom True Flame would reach.

...

Luo Chen still sat cross-legged atop the snowy mountain, alert to his surroundings in case anyone came to interrupt Fu Qinglan's cultivation.

However, another portion of his attention was focused on the transformation of the Withering-Bloom True Flame.

Time flowed by day after day, and the endless mountains also began passing through season after season.

But unlike before, the plants and trees flourished in spring and summer, and in autumn and winter they also remained lush with green. All of this was because of Fu Qinglan's influence.

The speed of Luo Chen's Withering-Bloom True Flame transformation also benefited from the origin power Fu Qinglan leaked outward, increasing greatly.

From the time this flame was refined, it had possessed the level of early fourth tier.

Afterward, Luo Chen hunted large numbers of demon beasts. The vast majority of their vitality was absorbed by the Withering-Bloom True Flame, and only a small portion fed back into Luo Chen's fleshly body.

Only with this foundation could the Withering-Bloom True Flame absorb many second- and third-tier strange fires in Blazing Refinement Prison, and even absorb a fourth-tier Burning Heaven Fire Spirit!

If it had only been so, that would have been one thing, but afterward the Withering-Bloom True Flame also absorbed a trace of Nirvana Sacred Flame, as well as all of Old Monster Netherfire's sentient Netherfire.

It could even be said that when Black King returned from the Coral Sea back then, the fourth-tier blue flame of the sharkfolk race attached to his fleshly body had also been swallowed by the Withering-Bloom True Flame.

So many flames supplemented it, creating the terrifying foundation of the Withering-Bloom True Flame.

If judged by realm, it was at least at the late fourth-tier level!

But it was precisely because there were too many flames that primary and secondary were indistinct, and the interior was too mixed.

Therefore, when Luo Chen was cultivating, the Withering-Bloom True Flame had actively entered the state of withering and blooming in cycle.

It tried to use the opportunity of the withering-bloom transformation to thoroughly digest those flames and make itself pure and singular.

Looking at it now, this step had indeed been the correct one.

In only a year or two, the grade of the Withering-Bloom True Flame was pushed to the late fourth-tier level, and it began spreading step by step toward the fifth tier!

Generally speaking, even when sentient beings reached the fifth tier, they still could not compare to Ascendant cultivators.

If one had targeted methods, even Sage cultivators could capture fifth-tier sentient beings.

For example, at Withered Wood Ridge back then, Blood Wanderer subdued the fifth-tier Withered Blood Vine!

But sentient beings of the fire attribute were exceptionally different.

Such existences naturally possessed enormous destructive power. Once they achieved the fifth tier, their formidable aspects far surpassed ordinary sentient creatures. Even Ascendant great powers who had not yet fully comprehended law power might not necessarily be their opponents.

Back then on Vermilion Bird Mountain in Blazing Refinement Prison, Shark Emperor Liujun had not dared provoke the Vermilion Bird fire spirit for this very reason.

If the Withering-Bloom True Flame could cross this final step and reach the fifth tier, then Luo Chen would thoroughly possess a support with which to range across the Mountains and Seas Realm!

Only, this hope went awry in the third year.

"How could this be?"

Inside the Purple Mansion, the Sage looked at the azure flame that rose again and again, only to lower its banners and cease again and again, and his brows knitted tightly.

He could feel that the Withering-Bloom True Flame very much wanted to cross that step, but it could never step over.

The weak consciousness of the Withering-Bloom fire spirit also transmitted a vague, unclear emotion of anger.

It was as if something had restricted it?

And furthermore, it was powerless to do anything about it?

Luo Chen pondered deeply. As its master, he naturally wanted to help the Withering-Bloom fire spirit.

Was its own accumulation still insufficient?

That should not be. If it were insufficient, it would not have transformed automatically. Such sentient beings were most sensitive to their own state. Unless they encountered external calamity, they could often naturally complete the transformation process.

Was it because it had absorbed too many strange fires?

But after a cycle of withering and blooming, they should have long been refined.

Suddenly!

The Sage's bright eyes fixed upon that speck of brilliant gold within the azure flame!

Even amid such azure clouds spreading everywhere and roaring firelight, that point of gold had not faded at all and remained rooted within.

"Nirvana Sacred Flame!"

Luo Chen's expression changed slightly.

He had never expected this thing to obstruct the final step of the Withering-Bloom True Flame's transformation.

This wisp of Nirvana Sacred Flame had come from the Heaven-Refining Cauldron.

Originally, its scale had been larger, but during its thousand-year struggle with the Nine-Refined Beacon Fire, it had been continuously worn down, and its spiritual wisdom had long since collapsed.

Therefore, when Luo Chen completed the nirvana of his fleshly body, the Withering-Bloom True Flame had actively absorbed it.

It could be said that the reason the Withering-Bloom True Flame could now automatically walk the path of transformation was also influenced by the "Nirvana Sacred Flame."

But this flame was both the cause of success and the cause of failure!

Its grade was simply too high. Even if it was unconscious, even if only a bit remained, it was not something the Withering-Bloom True Flame, which was still only fourth tier, could absorb and refine.

On the contrary, because of its existence, it obstructed the Withering-Bloom True Flame from continuing to climb to a higher realm.

"Does that mean it can only stop here?"

Luo Chen's Sage murmured to itself.

An anxious emotion transmitted over. It was the unwillingness of the Withering-Bloom fire spirit.

How could Luo Chen be willing either?

They had clearly already reached this step.

In the end, Luo Chen made his decision!

"You and I have accompanied each other for two hundred years. I once lost much lifespan because of you, and once achieved great success in physique because of you. Along my path of cultivation and battle, I have benefited greatly from you."

"Now that you have obtained this opportunity to attain the Dao, as your master, I naturally should push you forward!"

As he murmured, all of Luo Chen's mind returned to the sea of consciousness.

Swish!

His eyes opened, and a jade box appeared before him.

He opened the lid. The Heavenly Secret Pill shrouded in white light was emitting a fatal temptation toward Luo Chen.

Perhaps this was the meaning of its existence!

If it were any other time, Luo Chen would have taken this Heavenly Secret Pill himself, trying to do as Fu Qinglan and the others had and comprehend the true meaning of the laws, achieving personal mighty power.

But now, this pill would be refined by the Withering-Bloom fire spirit!

Luo Chen had thought very clearly, or rather, he saw very clearly.

These true Sage disciples of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect all had outstanding aptitudes, were guided by elders, and had made all kinds of preparations in advance, yet it was still difficult for them to comprehend the true meaning of the laws.

Even if he took the Heavenly Secret Pill, his chance of successfully comprehending the true meaning of the laws would only be fifty-fifty.

But if he gave it to the Withering-Bloom fire spirit, the other party's chance of advancing to the fifth tier would be far greater than his chance of comprehending the true meaning of the laws.

And once the Withering-Bloom fire spirit succeeded, the improvement to Luo Chen's strength would absolutely not be inferior to mastering the true meaning of the laws!

The reason he was so certain lay in the words Alchemy Saint Chu Yan had transmitted to Luo Chen when he ascended.

[Your true flame's transformation is only one step from crossing the threshold. With the Heavenly Secret Pill's assistance, it will follow naturally.]

From the very beginning, this pill belonged to the Withering-Bloom fire spirit.

Luo Chen no longer hesitated and swallowed the Heavenly Secret Pill in one gulp.

The pill entered his body. Without any magic power refining it, it went straight into the Purple Mansion, then was wrapped up impatiently by the Withering-Bloom fire spirit.

At that moment, Luo Chen clearly sensed a great amount of spiritual resonance spreading outward.

The Sage stirred with eagerness, but could only watch helplessly as that spiritual resonance was devoured by the Withering-Bloom True Flame.

"So be it! So be it!"

Luo Chen sighed and stared blankly at the Withering-Bloom True Flame's transformation and rise, hoping that day would come quickly.

...

Seven years later.

Inside a cave residence, Shi Ju suddenly opened his eyes.

"Where?"

Qi Xiongcheng, who had lost one arm, stood respectfully at the side and said in a low voice, "The outer region of the grotto-heaven, the far north."

Shi Ju raised a brow. "They ran outside. No wonder there was no news of them before. Are you certain they are there?"

Qi Xiongcheng nodded. "It should not be false. This is news sent by Wang Yifu. He said the environment there is extremely strange. Even when great snow covers the land, the plants and trees remain dense, like spring or summer. According to Dao Sect records, such strangeness only appears when a cultivator cannot control origin power properly. Wilderness Wanderer seized many illusion cores, and with Fu Qinglan having this assistance, it is reasonable for her to have reached that step."

Shi Ju revealed a thoughtful look.

Origin power was the most basic kind within the true meaning of the laws. It looked simple, but in truth, controlling it also required considerable skill.

It was only normal that Fu Qinglan's improper control would cause origin power to leak outward.

The only unexpected thing was that her progress was somewhat fast. Even Gu Daosu had not reached this step earlier.

In that case...

"Let's go. To the far north of the grotto-heaven!"

Qi Xiongcheng hesitated and subconsciously touched his severed arm.

With a Sage cultivator's methods, reconnecting a severed arm should be commonplace, but for some unknown reason, the severed arm cut off by that blood sword could not be reattached by ordinary methods.

Shi Ju saw his small movement and frowned. "Are you afraid of Wilderness Wanderer?"

Qi Xiongcheng hurriedly shook his head. "No. It's only that Young Master has already collected enough illusion cores. You should be calmly cultivating. Why go seek trouble with them?"

Shi Ju's expression was savage, and he swept a broad hand.

"If I do not go look for them, they will run! As for cultivation, I have never worried about it in my life. What are you worrying about?"

Qi Xiongcheng was left speechless.

The other party's words did indeed have some reason. Once Fu Qinglan succeeded and activated the illusion core, she could directly leave Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven.

After going out, matters would have to be handled according to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's rules.

As for Shi Ju's cultivation, it was indeed not his place to worry.

The other party had never fretted over cultivation in his life. Even comprehending the true meaning of the laws, something everyone considered difficult, was treated by Shi Ju as a matter already nailed down.

"Could it be that I truly am afraid of Wilderness Wanderer?"

While he questioned himself and reached toward the truth pressed down in his heart, which he did not dare face, Shi Ju had already strode out of the cave residence.

"Notify Wang Yifu. He is not to act rashly. Wait for us to arrive. He is too weak and is not Wilderness Wanderer's opponent. If he alerts the prey, I will hold only him responsible!"

"Yes!"

...

"Is it here?"

"It should be. I followed that white eagle here. Look, it keeps circling nearby. Clearly, it has detected the presence of its enemy."

Listening to Gu Chen's words, Gu Yuan lowered himself under the great snow.

He reached out and pulled up a withered yellow blade of grass.

"No, this grass is withered, almost devoid of vitality. It does not look much like something tainted by wood-attribute origin power."

Gu Chen was also somewhat puzzled and could only comfort him. "Perhaps it is too far away, and this side has not been affected?"

Gu Yuan stood up and looked at the huge white eagle in the sky.

"Most likely this beast searched wrongly. Counting the time, thirty years have already passed. Even if Fu Qinglan's aptitude is poor, with so many illusion cores assisting her, she should have comprehended the Dao long ago and left Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven. Why would she still remain in these boundless snowy mountains?"

"How about we go a little farther in and look?"

"Fine. In any case, I am not in a rush, and I can also investigate the grotto-heaven's situation for the family along the way. If they really are inside, perhaps there will be a lively show to watch."

Gu Yuan was indifferent. He concealed his aura well, following the white eagle's path as they gradually entered the mountains covered in great snow.

Two days later.

Their footsteps stopped.

Looking toward one mountain peak, they saw two men standing shoulder to shoulder there, gazing at them from afar.

"It's those two from the Lin family."

"Why are they here? Could they also have come because of Wilderness Wanderer?"

"Unclear. Perhaps they came for this white eagle. I heard earlier that the boy Lin Jingtang mastered a method for hunting illusion beasts, and has gained quite a harvest over these years. Perhaps he has set his eyes on this extremely conspicuous white eagle."

"Ignore them. Search a little more. If we cannot find them, we will return. I have also settled myself about enough."

Gu Yuan said this and continued forward.

In the distance, Lin Bufan and Lin Jingtang watched them, doubt on their faces.

The two from the Gu family continued forward, and the scenes they saw grew stranger and stranger.

In this cold winter season, there were indeed unusual green plants appearing in violation of the season.

Yet among those greens, there were also large amounts of withered yellow.

Without external force, such abnormalities were truly difficult to understand.

Suddenly!

Gu Chen's footsteps paused, and his golden robe snapped loudly as he blocked Gu Yuan behind him.

Gu Yuan was surprised. "What is it?"

Gu Chen's expression was startled and uncertain. "For some reason, I have an extremely uncomfortable feeling. If we go farther forward, something bad may happen."

A feeling?

Gu Yuan frowned, spread out his divine sense, and looked around.

The mountains rose and fell, some tall and some short.

Great snow covered them, wrapping everything in silver.

Amid the vast whiteness, there was no human figure to be seen.

Aside from the abnormal plants and trees, there was nothing else.

"Protect me for a moment."

Gu Yuan gave an instruction, sat cross-legged on the ground, and let his Sage flee out.

In this way, he fit more closely with this heaven and earth. And because he had previously tried cultivating with illusion cores, he was even more sensitive to this world's spiritual resonance.

The most eye-catching was the white eagle circling in the sky.

But apart from the white eagle, there were two other auras that drew attention.

The Sage returned to his body, and Gu Yuan opened his eyes.

"Found it. The place where spiritual resonance gathers!"

But after saying this, doubt appeared on his face.

"But why are there two currents? Shouldn't only Fu Qinglan be cultivating?"

Amid his confusion, he called Gu Chen, and they quietly advanced.

After about the time it took to drink a cup of tea, the two stopped in a gorge where the accumulated snow was half-melted and dripping water murmured.

The two raised their heads and looked. From a mountain peak that seemed to have no one on it came an aura that made one's hair stand on end.

Gu Yuan swallowed, and a possibility surfaced in his mind.

"How did he also enter a state of sudden enlightenment?"

At this very moment, the white eagle screamed, and the mountains rumbled.

Three figures, making no attempt to conceal their auras, broke through the air and arrived.

Wherever they passed, vast magic power shook down great swaths of snowy mountains. The commotion was so great that it drew sidelong looks.

"Wilderness Wanderer, found you!"

The arrogant voice spread across the four fields, echoing endlessly among the mountains and shaking still more snowy mountains into collapse.

Gu Yuan drew in a deep breath.

"Arriving early is not as good as arriving at the right time. Shi Ju and the others have come knocking!"

*(End of Chapter)*
