# Chapter 913: Obsidian Lotus Pods, Leaving the Nether Abyss

The outside world was in turmoil, but the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land remained as tranquil as ever.

A figure streaked across the sky, then suddenly halted, descending onto a lake.

The white-robed man surveyed his surroundings, a look of doubt in his eyes.

A flash of golden light flickered in his pupils, and his brow involuntarily lifted.

His fingertips moved slightly, striking a series of hand seals into the empty void. One after another, formation flags flew from his sleeve and plunged into the lake.

The calm surface of the lake, stirred by his actions, gradually began to ripple.

When all preparations were complete, the white-robed man let out a low shout.

"Open!"

The next moment, the tranquil lake surface, which had been reflecting the blue sky and white clouds, slowly revealed its true appearance.

Stalks of black lotus pods came into view.

They bloomed on the clear water, bathed in mist, inhaling and exhaling the purest water-attributed spiritual qi.

As if sensing an outsider, these black lotus pods all dove beneath the water's surface in unison.

Extremely spirited!

Seeing this scene, a look of both surprise and delight appeared on Luo Chen's face.

"Obsidian Lotus Pods!"

"To think that such high-grade spiritual herbs still exist in this realm!"

"No wonder the free-floating spiritual qi in this area felt different from other places when I passed by earlier."

The Obsidian Lotus Pod was a high-grade spiritual herb recorded in the seventy-two volumes of alchemy scriptures left by the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining.

It grew only in places free of filth.

It absorbed spiritual qi, refining the coarse and retaining the pure.

It could be said that wherever such a spiritual herb existed, the land itself would be nurtured into a blessed ground.

But its true purpose was that when refined into medicine, it greatly nourished a cultivator's Nascent Soul, making it more solid and impervious to external demonic disturbances.

In fact, even many water-attributed Ascendants cultivated this herb to temper their primordial spirits.

"The token didn't record this kind of herb earlier?"

Luo Chen murmured to himself, wondering why the Alchemy Saint had concealed such a thing.

Then, a possibility gradually occurred to him.

The herbs the Alchemy Saint had specifically marked were probably those she had left behind herself.

These hidden high-grade spiritual herbs were likely cultivated for someone else.

As the inheritor of the Alchemy Saint's insights, it was only natural for him to automatically claim what she had left. But what others had entrusted to her was a different matter.

"Then who is the true owner of these Obsidian Lotus Pods?"

The answer was obvious.

For the Alchemy Saint, Chu Yan, to go to such lengths to arrange a blessed land, set up formations, and specially cultivate these herbs, the only ones in the entire Nether Origin Sect with that kind of face were the three founding ancestors.

And among those three great powers, Ancestor Yuming primarily cultivated wind-attributed techniques.

Therefore, the owner of these Obsidian Lotus Pods could only be either Ancestor Tianyuan or Heize.

Luo Chen was more inclined to believe it was the latter.

Ancestor Tianyuan had achieved Ascension earlier; his primordial spirit was already stable.

Only Ancestor Heize, having become an Ascendant more recently, had a primordial spirit that was not yet strong enough and required external assistance.

This was evident from how the Yuantu Sword had devoured his primordial spirit.

Luo Chen glanced once more at the empty lake surface and retrieved the formation flags he had deployed.

The barrier that concealed this place, now unobstructed, activated once again.

Luo Chen's sharp eyes continued to stare inside. After a long while, those spirited black lotus pods surfaced again.

He turned and left!

It wasn't that he had no way to deal with these Obsidian Lotus Pods, nor that he lacked the relevant pill formulas. It was simply that the time was not yet right to use them.

Although Luo Chen often mortgaged the future to boost his short-term strength, he usually only did so when forced by circumstances.

Overall, he was someone who liked to plan for the long term.

But most of the time, he was compelled by the situation to focus on the present.

Now, with no one pressuring him and the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land being relatively safe, why waste these Obsidian Lotus Pods?

When he achieved Ascension in the future, refining a batch of pills to stabilize his primordial spirit would save him centuries of effort!

That would be the optimal time to use them.

Luo Chen rode his flying light, traversing the Blessed Land. It wasn't small, but to him, it was by no means large.

Following the guidance on the token, Luo Chen found one spiritual herb garden after another.

In particular, he located the Hong Spirit Grass he needed for his cultivation.

The growth was excellent, the age was high—it was the best batch of Hong Spirit Grass Luo Chen had ever seen.

This time, Luo Chen harvested it without hesitation!

Using this to refine pills would surely yield a large batch of top-grade, even supreme-grade Hongyuan Pills, greatly accelerating his cultivation efficiency.

Considering that he would soon have to forcefully cross the Nether Abyss, he could not afford any shortcomings in his magical power.

Naturally, Luo Chen would not waste this opportunity.

Of course, he also left seeds for the herb garden, replanting some scattered, immature Hong Spirit Grass.

Luo Chen had some knowledge of the herbalist's craft.

After harvesting the Hong Spirit Grass, Luo Chen, carrying a medicinal basket larger than himself, headed back toward the cave dwelling.

At this moment, Luo Chen somewhat missed the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion.

Freshly harvested Hong Spirit Grass was not suitable for storage rings. Only a true artifact-level spatial storage treasure like the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion, which could communicate with the spiritual qi of heaven and earth, would not damage the herbs.

"I'll have to make a trip back to the Luo Tian Sect."

...

In a large mountain.

A dragon, hundreds of feet long, slowly swam through the air.

As if sensing a familiar aura, it followed the mountain ridge, climbed to the peak, and "gently" said to a youth, "Little master, the master has returned."

Luo Lingxi stopped what he was doing and looked in the direction Black King had indicated.

Although he had not cultivated any immortal arts, Luo Lingxi's eyesight was now far beyond that of an ordinary person.

This was thanks to Luo Chen regularly giving him diluted Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid.

In his line of sight, his young father, carrying a medicinal basket, was returning like an old farmer coming home after a hard day's work under the setting sun.

A happy expression appeared on his face, but he pursed his lips, suppressing the smile, and began to practice a set of boxing techniques on his own.

The movements were vigorous and well-structured.

Luo Chen was extremely fast. The pause earlier had only been to observe the Nirvana Sacred Flame's feeding process.

After returning, seeing his son's actions, he couldn't help but frown.

"You taught him this?"

His eyes were on Luo Lingxi, but his words were directed at Black King.

His tone was calm, but carried a hint of reproach.

Black King shrugged his neck. "I taught him."

Luo Lingxi stopped his movements and nervously explained, "Black King saw that I was too tired going up and down the mountain, so he taught me this crude Climbing and Leaping Method. It's not some profound cultivation technique."

Luo Chen twitched the corner of his mouth. This was far from a crude climbing method.

It contained the essence of some physical arts he had cultivated back when he was at the Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake of the Myriad Immortals Assembly, only omitting the need for magical power.

If Luo Lingxi possessed great power and used this technique, the force he could unleash would be unimaginable.

Luo Chen looked at Black King and said softly, "You have a good heart."

It was unclear whether he was praising Black King for adapting his technique or for specifically teaching this method to Luo Lingxi.

These words made Black King quite nervous.

But Luo Chen had already carried his medicinal basket into the cave, leaving only a parting remark: "Come back early for dinner."

After he left, both the dragon and the youth breathed a sigh of relief.

Luo Lingxi laughed. "Big Black, see? I told you Father wouldn't scold you."

Black King was helpless. "You don't understand. Your father doesn't say many things out loud; he keeps them in his heart. He'll probably scold me later. In the future, don't ask me to teach you things anymore."

Luo Lingxi's eyelashes trembled, and a hint of dejection appeared in his lively eyes.

His father was indeed someone who didn't show his emotions on his face.

As the atmosphere grew tense, Black King went down the mountain, and the youth continued practicing the boxing technique from earlier.

It truly was more than just a climbing method.

With every punch and kick, the wind howled. Though the youth was merely a mortal, his movements stirred up sand and stones, drawing attention.

Only after he had completely finished the set of techniques did a voice call out to him, and he trotted back to the cave.

"Time to eat."

Luo Chen said simply, then sat down on a chair.

On the table was a single, simple dish—wild greens from the Blessed Land, stir-fried plainly. Even the rice in their bowls was just common millet.

It was a bit bland.

But there was no helping it.

Luo Lingxi was only a mortal; he couldn't handle heavily nourishing food, nor could he be given too many fasting pills. So Luo Chen had to cook for him from time to time.

This wasn't much trouble for Luo Chen.

Having lived two lives, he had spent most of his time alone. Cooking was a habit for him.

It was only during these three hundred years of cultivation that he had neglected this skill.

Father and son ate their dinner quietly in the brightly lit cave.

A chopstick of greens, a chopstick of rice.

With only one dish, the youth ate quickly, and their chopsticks would occasionally clash.

After wolfing down three bowls of rice, the youth put down his bamboo chopsticks.

"Father, I'm full."

Luo Chen grunted in acknowledgment. "If you're full, go wash up and sleep. Don't stay up too late reading my travel journals."

Luo Lingxi obediently nodded. Before leaving, he hesitated, then said, "Father doesn't have to cook for me every day. It takes too much time from your cultivation. I can cook for myself, and Black King will help me find ingredients."

Luo Chen paused in his action of picking up food, looking at Luo Lingxi.

The other bit his lip and insisted, "I'm sixteen now! I've grown up!"

After a moment of silence, Luo Chen simply nodded.

"Then cook for yourself! I'll cook when I have the time."

Luo Lingxi, as if relieved, returned to his own room.

After he left, Luo Chen's expression was somewhat dazed.

Sixteen, huh?

That little ball of flesh had now grown up.

Apart from being a bit shorter than him, he was perfectly healthy.

This also meant that nearly six years had passed since they arrived at the Nether Origin Sect, and a full year had been spent in the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land.

He had never realized time passed so quickly.

A deep seclusion, a breakthrough in realm—these often took years or even decades.

But watching a child grow up before his very eyes made the passage of time feel so clear.

"Perhaps, as Lingxi said, I really can't keep delaying."

Luo Chen cleaned up the dishes and returned to his own room.

He didn't start cultivating immediately. First, he processed the batch of freshly harvested Hong Spirit Grass, which he would need for alchemy in a few days.

Only then, until dawn, did he begin to meditate and refine qi.

...

In the wilderness, a youth ran swiftly, like a tiger or leopard.

Spotting a large tree, he charged straight at it, then, gauging his direction, swooped down like an eagle into a ravine.

In no time, he emerged holding a rabbit.

"Big Black, see? I told you I could catch one myself without you suppressing the beasts with your aura!"

Black King slowly crawled out.

The still-living rabbit fainted just from looking at him.

Black King said helplessly, "Too much trouble."

Luo Lingxi insisted, "It's no trouble. In the future, I'll catch tigers and leopards, and shoot down birds."

Black King was curious. "Is this a way for you to train yourself?"

Luo Lingxi nodded instinctively, then shook his head. "No, it's just that Father has been stir-frying greens for a year. The taste is too bland."

Black King was stunned.

Cultivators naturally preferred light food.

Even when they ate meat, it was mostly for the abundant spiritual qi in demon beast flesh.

It seemed the master had somewhat neglected his son's dietary needs?

Suddenly, he looked up into the distance.

"What's wrong?" Luo Lingxi asked curiously.

Black King's voice was somewhat awed as he said, "It seems the master's new origin true flame has been successfully completed."

Luo Lingxi subconsciously tightened his grip on the rabbit ears in his hand.

Had Father mastered another great divine ability?

As powerful as he was, he was still constantly improving, never stopping!

Could his own insignificant training ever hope to catch up to his footsteps?

...

In a volcano with flowing magma, Luo Chen stood atop the peak, letting the hot wind buffet his face.

He stared intently at the golden firebird spreading its wings and flying over the sea of magma.

The more he looked, the stronger the sense of déjà vu.

"Phoenix!"

"How similar it is to the dharma manifestation that Rosycloud inadvertently revealed when she attacked Yanfu Mountain!"

"Was it right or wrong to let the fire spirit devour the Nirvana Sacred Flame she left behind?"

As he murmured, the golden firebird had already flown back. It circled Luo Chen a few times, then shrank its body and perched affectionately on his shoulder, using its beak to preen its golden feathers formed of flame.

He had no answer for his own doubts.

But the fire spirit's affectionate behavior seemed to provide one.

He only knew that he could now try to leave the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land and head for the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world.

...

That evening.

Father and son met again at the dinner table.

But unlike the usual single dish, this time there was an extra soup and a heavily oiled and salted stir-fried rabbit meat.

"Father, try my cooking?" Luo Lingxi invited proudly.

Luo Chen took a couple of bites and nodded with a smile.

"Very good. The flavor is almost catching up to your mother's."

"Really?" Luo Lingxi was very happy to receive his father's approval, but then his expression darkened. "It's been a long time since I've seen Mother."

Luo Chen put down his chopsticks and said solemnly, "Soon, you will see her."

Luo Lingxi was overjoyed. "Are we going out?"

Luo Chen shook his head slightly. "I am going out."

The youth was stunned.

Luo Chen didn't explain, only instructed, "I'll leave Black King here. Wait for me. It could be a few months, or a few years. If I don't... I will return."

Having said that, he left the living room.

Luo Lingxi bit his lip. The delicacy he had carefully prepared, his father had only taken one bite.

Most importantly, why wouldn't Father take him? Was he afraid he would be a burden?

The moon was bright in the clear sky, and the night wind gently blew.

Black King asked curiously, "Master, why not take me? The Nether Abyss is quite dangerous. At least I can carry you part of the way."

Luo Chen sighed. "You know it's dangerous too! Even I only have a seventy or eighty percent chance of successfully getting out this time. If you were to fall in there too, who would take care of Lingxi?"

Black King was speechless. That was indeed true.

Luo Chen's words continued to drift on the night wind.

"Besides, I need you to stay in the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land and guard the gate, lest any powerful ghost creatures break in."

As he spoke, a token fell before Black King.

Black King knew this was the key to the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land.

He carefully put it away and asked, "Master, when do you depart?"

Luo Chen's expression was solemn.

"Tomorrow morning!"

...

In the dark, turbulent abyss, ghostly figures drifted to and fro.

Suddenly, a sword light burst forth from the gloom, silently slashing away the specters and monsters.

After clearing the ghostly creatures outside the Blessed Land, a figure in white gradually materialized.

Luo Chen took a deep breath, his crimson eyes and golden pupils locking onto a certain direction, and he departed calmly.
