# Chapter 915: Upper Nether Ruined City, Dao Sect Arrives in Eastern Wilderness

"A living being!"

Facing those blood-red eyes, Luo Chen discerned his enemy in an instant.

It wasn't one of the ghostly beings common to the Nether Abyss, but a living creature, radiating a powerful aura no less than Black King's.

If it were only that, it wouldn't matter. With Luo Chen's strength, he had no fear of such an existence.

But this was the Nether Abyss!

Once a fight broke out, it would inevitably attract the attention of other powerful beings. If that happened, Luo Chen's fate would likely be grim.

"How could a living being exist in such a desperate place?"

A look of confusion crossed Luo Chen's face as he slowly retreated, signaling that he had no intention of fighting the enemy.

But as he retreated, the enemy advanced.

Rolling waves surged forth, and the master of those blood-red eyes revealed its true form.

A broad, bluish-yellow back as wide as land itself, rounded overall but angular in the details.

A long, slender neck, like a giraffe's, or perhaps a giant python's.

A turtle-snake image?

Seeing this, Luo Chen froze.

His heart stirred. Could it be...

"That mystic turtle from the Nether Origin Sect's Lu family!"

In an instant, Luo Chen recognized it.

He also understood why a living creature could exist in the Nether Abyss, where life could hardly survive.

It was because this beast had grown up here from birth. Its incomplete transformation, due to its environment, meant the turtle and snake aspects had never fully separated.

At that moment, the massive mystic turtle charged at Luo Chen with overwhelming force, its eyes filled with bloodlust.

This time, Luo Chen did not retreat.

He exhaled, and slowly pushed out a single palm.

*Boom!*

The two collided. Violent power erupted from the point of contact, surging in all directions, forming wave after fierce wave.

Yet these waves, capable of shattering mountains and rocks, were mere ripples before the two of them.

Luo Chen pressed one hand against the triangular tip of the turtle's head, terrifying power continuously erupting from his physical body.

The mystic turtle was not to be outdone. Its hard head suddenly became soft, its long neck flexibly circled upward, then lunged down at Luo Chen, jaws wide.

It wanted to swallow Luo Chen!

At that moment, Luo Chen's aura exploded outward.

Origin blood surged, Mystic Dust Armor clad his body, and a wave of qi, sharing the same origin as the mystic turtle's, spread out.

"How dare you act presumptuously, little one!"

The roar of a superior being made the turtle's open jaws freeze in midair.

Its blood-red eyes showed a hint of fear, a trace of confusion, and a sense of waking from a dream.

Suddenly!

*Woo...*

A ghostly wail exploded.

The shrill, piercing sound nearly made one's soul faint.

The next moment, a pitch-black light shot out from the top of the turtle's head, straight at Luo Chen.

Its speed was incredibly fast.

Throughout this process, Luo Chen seemed completely unaffected by the soul-shaking impact of the ghostly cry.

The Flat Mountain Crown on his head scattered hazy golden light, the Mystic Dust Armor on his body emitted a yellow qi shield, and his eyes revealed the illusions of Mirror Flower Water Moon and the Towering Ghost Shadow.

Yet the black light was equally unaffected. It broke through the golden light and qi shield in succession, touching Luo Chen's body. Only the illusions briefly blocked it for a moment.

That moment was enough for Luo Chen to react.

"Come out!"

The next instant, two flames simultaneously emerged from Luo Chen's Purple Mansion.

One was azure-white, the other pure gold without blemish.

*Boom!*

In an instant, Luo Chen became a man of fire.

The black light, as if seeing something terrifying, hastily retreated, desperate to avoid it.

After casting a fearful glance at Luo Chen, it fled directly into the depths of the darkness.

Only then did Luo Chen exhale in relief, though the lingering dread in his heart refused to dissipate.

"That was..."

Luo Chen said hoarsely, "A Devouring Origin Beast?"

Though it had no physical body, no tangible form, the aura it emitted was one Luo Chen would never forget.

It was clearly the high-ranking wraith that the ancient demon emperor Di Tian had once released to ravage the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world—Gao Chang!

Its original form was a Devouring Origin Beast, a rare species of archaic beast. It fed on the origin qi of heaven and earth, and most of all, it loved cultivators' dao fruits. Whether Adept cores or Sage infant souls, both were its favorite food.

No one knew how many Sage cultivators had died in this wraith's belly.

Jin Ling, who had once fought alongside Luo Chen, had his Sage infant soul taken by this creature, leading to his fall. Luo Chen had even refined his physical body into a substitute puppet, which took a fatal blow from Black Marsh Ancestor in Luo Chen's place.

"How is this thing here?"

Just as Luo Chen was puzzled, the mystic turtle pushed through the water and approached him.

Its triangular head leaned toward Luo Chen, gently nudging him.

The movement was soft and affectionate, as if seeking favor from an elder.

Luo Chen looked down. Its eyes had become clear, completely free of the earlier blood-red color.

"You were controlled by that wraith, forced to attack me, weren't you?"

The mystic turtle let out a "moo," its tone quite aggrieved.

Luo Chen reached out and rubbed its smooth head. "It's alright. I don't blame you. From now on, you'll have to..."

Luo Chen's words caught in his throat.

Clearly, the Nether Abyss's Netherwater had an extremely strong defiling ability. Relying on his own mana, it would be very difficult to persist through the crossing.

And above the water surface, spatial tears appeared from time to time, with heavenly thunder striking down. The danger was even higher than in the abyss!

This beast had grown up here from birth, completely unafraid of the Netherwater's defiling power.

Long before Luo Chen arrived, it had been the best beast of burden for Nether Origin Sect cultivators to ferry between the two banks.

If he could use it as a mount, crossing the Nether Abyss would save him a great deal of effort, and even be faster.

"From now on, give me a ride!"

Luo Chen leaped lightly, landing on the turtle's back.

"Take me to the way I came. You still remember, right?"

The turtle called out, its tone affirmative.

It had a deep memory of Luo Chen, considering him an elder of its own race, one who had merely taken the path of human transformation.

Now that the elder wanted a ride, it was only right to serve.

Moreover, its earlier collision had been terribly rude. It had to make amends.

The giant turtle swung its neck, churning up countless waves of Netherwater, and carried Luo Chen toward the opposite shore.

On the turtle's back now, there were no more pavilions, towers, or palace buildings. It revealed its most essential nature.

Luo Chen felt no discomfort. He sat cross-legged, letting the turtle's source power cover him.

In his eyes, a contemplative look appeared.

"In the battle at Azure Sky Domain, Di Tian fell and perished. Azure Grain Immortal was captured and suppressed in the Nether Abyss. The rest of the negotiators fled or scattered."

"I thought Gao Chang had died in that battle too, but it seems not."

"During the time it was captured, the Nether Origin Sect's ancestor had already laid hands on it. How could it have escaped? Moreover, rumor had it that Di Tian tried to advance this wraith to the Ascendant stage, to make it his external incarnation. With such a valuable tool, how could the Nether Origin Ancestor have been indifferent? He must have brought it back."

"In fact, I can guess that the ancestor who wanted to refine it into a clone was most likely Tianyuan Ancestor, who specialized in the dao of clones."

"But as Tianyuan Ancestor fed himself to the ghost, activating the Jade Shattering Restriction, this wraith gained its freedom instead."

"Looking at its chaotic state, its spiritual wisdom has probably been erased by Tianyuan Ancestor, leaving only the instinct to attack cultivators."

"Now it's like a blank slate. If I could capture it and refine it into a clone..."

At this thought, Luo Chen's heart stirred.

After his brief clash with Black Marsh Ancestor, he understood the benefits of a clone all too well.

Such a thing could assist in cultivation, handle trivial matters, and in critical moments, whether for battle or as a substitute for death, it had unexpected effects.

Looking back on his journey, anyone who possessed a clone had top-tier survival abilities.

Whether it was Han Zhan, Blood Wanderer, Shi Ju, Tianyuan Ancestor, or others.

Regardless of whether their clones were puppets, Blood God Children, or self-refined through great divine abilities.

In short, clones were good things!

But Luo Chen suppressed this thought almost as soon as it arose.

Not to mention how dangerous the wraith transformed from the Devouring Origin Beast was—just its instinct had forced Luo Chen to use two trump cards, the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire and the Nirvana Sacred Flame. The endless abyss alone was not a place where Luo Chen could easily find its hiding spot.

"Even great powers dream of such an existence. How could I, in my current state, easily covet it?"

Luo Chen shook his head. Sitting on the turtle's back, he took a sip of spirit wine to quickly recover his mana.

The road ahead would not be peaceful. He had to stay at his peak at all times.

...

Half a year later.

A young man in black robes, with dark skin, stood before a stretch of ruins.

His face showed surprise, his eyes filled with disbelief.

"Is this Upper Nether Immortal City?"

A figure rushed out from the ruins. Passing the black-robed youth, he heard the muttered words and paused.

"Even the Nether Origin Sacred Land was destroyed. What's a mere Upper Nether City?"

The black-robed youth looked at the other. He was clearly an Adept cultivator.

At this moment, Luo Chen's displayed mana fluctuations were also controlled at the Adept stage.

He cupped his hands and humbly asked, "I am Gui Xuan, a wandering cultivator."

The middle-aged man descended from the air, somewhat surprised. "An Adept-level wandering cultivator?"

But then, he smiled self-deprecatingly.

Yes. In recent years, with the fierce demon war, how many lofty sect sacred lands had collapsed? A large number of wandering cultivators, who had suffered from a lack of resources, had instead seized the opportunity to soar.

The Adept stage was not so unattainable.

He cupped his hands in return and introduced himself, "Shen Qiuhua, from the Sword River Shen family."

Luo Chen looked confused, but as if recalling something, he tentatively asked, "The Sword River Shen family beside Divine Origin City in the East Sun Domain?"

Shen Shuhua was surprised. "I didn't expect that Fellow Daoist would know of my humble Shen family?"

Luo Chen waved his hand. "The Shen family is hardly humble. Your clan leader, Shen Qiubai, shone brilliantly in the human-demon battle at Lingtian Pass. He was hailed as having hope for Sagehood and boundless prospects on the Great Dao. If Clan Leader Shen breaks through, your clan may also hope to open a domain and establish a sect."

He remembered this because of the Longevity Sun Dao Discussion hosted by that Longevity Sun Hermit from the East Sun Sect back then.

The most numerous participants were not Sage cultivators, but Adept powerhouses from all over.

Longevity Sun Hermit often put items that Sage cultivators couldn't trade up for sale among the Adepts. This solved the Sage cultivators' difficulty in selling and also gathered popularity.

Among them, Shen Qiubai's name had reached Luo Chen's ears.

He was considered one of the outstanding Adept powerhouses of that time.

"Shen Qiubai is my elder brother!"

Mentioning the clan leader, Shen Qiuhua also showed a sense of pride.

Then, he pointed to the ruins behind him. "Is Fellow Daoist Gui here for the first time?"

Luo Chen nodded. "I heard that the Nether Origin Sacred Land had changed, so I came to see. I didn't expect that even the famous Upper Nether Immortal City had become ruins. I wonder what kind of power could have such city-destroying might. After all, I've heard that this city was always guarded by several Sage cultivators."

Speaking of this, Shen Qiuhua couldn't help but sigh.

"What kind of power? It was just the last ancient demon of the Eastern Wilderness making a move."

"The last ancient demon of the Eastern Wilderness?" Luo Chen looked confused.

Shen Qiuhua frowned. Such common knowledge—why did this Fellow Daoist know nothing about it?

But thinking of the other's self-proclaimed "wandering cultivator" identity, he found the lack of information understandable.

He explained, "Her name is Rosycloud. Her original form is the divine bird, the Phoenix!"

Luo Chen showed shock on his face, but in his heart, he was enlightened.

So it was her!

He hadn't expected that after leaving the Nether Abyss, she would vent her anger on Upper Nether Immortal City.

He didn't know how many low-level cultivators had suffered this undeserved calamity.

He asked curiously, "I saw Fellow Daoist just coming out from inside. What were you doing?"

Shen Qiuhua smiled self-deprecatingly. "Just scavenging some waste. Nothing glorious."

Luo Chen laughed heartily. "I used to do that kind of thing often too, Fellow Daoist. No need to feel inferior."

Shen Qiuhua also smiled naturally. In his understanding, that was indeed what wandering cultivators did.

And inside the ruined Upper Nether City right now, there were indeed many low-level wandering cultivators, risking their lives to search for treasure.

Perhaps someone might find a powerful cultivator's relic, advancing their cultivation or even breaking through a realm.

"Brother Shen, how much more do you know about Rosycloud?" Luo Chen showed curiosity.

Shen Qiuhua wanted to speak, but looked at the sky and showed hesitation.

Seeing this, Luo Chen didn't press. "If it's inconvenient, then forget it."

Shen Qiuhua thought for a moment, then carefully examined Luo Chen.

"Fellow Daoist Gui Xuan, if you don't mind, why not come to my Shen residence as a guest? We can talk at length by candlelight."

Luo Chen was taken aback. "Sword River? That's probably a three-to-five-day journey from here, isn't it?"

If he remembered correctly, the Sword River Shen family was located beside Divine Origin City, the former headquarters of the Tianyuan Merchant Alliance in the East Sun Domain.

Though not far from here, it was certainly not close.

At an Adept's flying speed...

Shen Qiuhua showed a gloomy expression. "The Shen family left Sword River long ago."

"Oh?"

"There, and the entire East Sun Domain, have been occupied by the Tianyuan Dao Sect. Everyone, including the East Sun Sect, the overlord of the East Sun Domain, has been driven out. We are already homeless dogs."

As Shen Qiuhua expected, Luo Chen's face showed astonishment.

It was the kind of shock that came without warning, completely undisguised.

(Chapter End)
