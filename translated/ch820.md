# Chapter 820: Slaughter Like Butchery, A Snow-White Fist

How deep was Luo Chen's killing intent?

Impossible to estimate!

In this life, he had slain people in droves, fought bloody battles without end, and was especially fond of large-scale offensive spells.

The number of living beings who had died by his hand was incalculable.

During ordinary cultivation, because he feared the residual blood baleful qi from slaughtering living beings would affect him, he often used Cleansing Art and Dragon-Slaying Art to remove the blood baleful qi from his body.

But although the baleful qi was gone, his killing intent had become even more pure and condensed.

For so many years, this killing intent had been suppressed.

Until this moment!

When that overwhelming killing intent spread without any concealment, all those of comparable realms were shocked to the core, as if needles were pricking their backs.

Especially when they saw that the darkness that had previously enveloped Luo Chen had disappeared. What this scene meant was clear to everyone.

The Lightless Demon Emperor had fallen!

Another fourth-rank Demon Emperor had died at his hands!

Then, within the Overlord Blade Domain, who could still stop him?

Once he joined any battle group, who on the demon side, already struggling to hold on, could still have any confidence?

Almost the moment Luo Chen's killing intent spread, the situation in every battle group changed slightly.

The Demon Emperors' attacks grew fiercer, their range of movement wider.

The residual waves from Sage-level combat brought incalculable impact to this land.

Boom!

Boom!

Boom!

Earth veins were shattered, scorching magma shooting into the sky.

Amidst the bloody wind and rain, another blaze of brilliant warfire was added.

Luo Chen stood atop a pillar of magma fire, with no intention whatsoever of joining any same-rank battle group.

His sparkling spirit eyes swept in all directions.

The blood sword in his hand clanged and rang.

"Are you excited too?"

"Excited by my killing intent, held back not at all?"

Luo Chen slowly raised his right hand. The Yuantu sword reflected his young and eerie face.

"Since that's the case, then go!"

As if talking to himself, Luo Chen casually tossed the sword and began forming hand seals.

The Yuantu sword let out a low hum, then suddenly shot through the air.

Transforming into a beam of blood light, it killed into the groups of low-rank demon beasts below, who were like headless flies.

Chi chi chi...

Its speed was so fast it was barely visible to the naked eye.

Its momentum was so sharp that physical bodies could not withstand it.

Wherever the sword light passed, no living mouths remained.

A third-rank Demon King dozens of feet long opened its bloody maw and forcibly swallowed the sword light.

But in an instant, the Yuantu sword burst out from its body.

That enormous Demon King froze in place, then crashed down with a boom.

The blood in its body, as if drawn by some force, chased after the sword light.

The sword light showed no sign of stopping. Centered on Luo Chen himself, within a thousand li radius, it rampaged freely, meeting no opponent that could withstand a single exchange.

Luo Chen stepped on pillars of flame, constantly moving rapidly across the brutal battlefield of bloody wind and rain, warfire and black smoke.

In his sight, there was only the blood cloud that grew thicker and thicker.

Shua!

A brilliant sword light, transforming into a thousand or even ten thousand feet tall, slashed down with a boom.

The beast tide that had originally surrounded the Iron View Dao Temple lost a quarter of its numbers under this single slash. The remaining demon beasts completely collapsed, their instinct for survival driving them to flee outside the Overlord Blade Domain.

Luo Chen exchanged a glance with Iron View Daoist, said nothing, and continued to change battlefields.

The latter accepted this favor, then took the opportunity to recall his two temple-guarding Dharma Protectors, the Turtle and Snake, while simultaneously pincering the bare-chested burly man Demon Emperor. The scales of victory had completely tipped.

Luo Chen didn't care much whether the other acknowledged the favor or not.

It was only because the number of demon beasts gathered here earlier was large enough that he had struck this sword.

All along the way, he controlled the Yuantu sword to pursue and kill low-rank demon beasts, and the blood cloud following behind the sword light grew thicker and thicker.

Suddenly!

A razor-thin sword brushed past his face.

Truly brushed past his face, nearly piercing through his skull.

From the void came a cicada's cry, as if extremely incredulous that this sure-kill strike had missed.

Luo Chen's expression did not change. He reached out to grab that sword.

Golden light erupted violently, as if it could cut through everything.

But Luo Chen didn't stop at all. Seizing the moment before that sword could escape, he caught it at the critical juncture.

He yanked hard!

Splash...

A great deal of blood spurted from the void.

Looking again at the razor-thin sword in Luo Chen's hand, it was no flying sword at all. It was truly just a thin, golden cicada wing!

"Six-Winged Golden Cicada, Dark Chirp Demon Emperor?"

In the void, Dark Chirp appeared in a sorry state, but no longer with the grandeur of six wings—only five cicada wings remained.

Hatred showed in her eyes.

"Bullying the weak, wantonly slaughtering low-rank demon beasts. Pill Sect, do you have no dignity as a Sage at all?"

"Dignity?"

Luo Chen smiled contemptuously. "Attacking me by surprise without warning—is that the dignity you, a Demon Emperor, possess?"

Then, he suddenly shouted, "How many low-rank disciples of my sect died at the hands of your Demon Kings and Demon Emperors? Did you speak of dignity then?"

Dark Chirp was stunned, and before she could reply, an extremely dangerous intuition came to her.

Chi!

A sword light, from behind, suddenly slashed down.

Dark Chirp dodged at the very last moment, but another golden cicada wing was left in the void.

Just as Dark Chirp reappeared in panic, a golden-armored giant, like a heavenly soldier descending, fell from the sky and grabbed at her.

Elder Fu stood panting on top of the golden-armored giant, looking apologetically at Luo Chen.

"Apologies, I failed to stop this demon, allowing her to disturb you, Fellow Daoist."

Luo Chen held two cicada wings in his hand and smiled slightly. "No matter. Leave the rest to you."

With that, he put the still-steaming cicada wings into his storage ring and stepped into the blood cloud.

The Yuantu sword let out a ringing sound and began flying toward the edge of the Overlord Blade Domain.

Too many low-rank demon beasts, not daring to face Sage-level combat, had been forced to flee to the edge.

They wanted to run!

But because of the restrictions of the Twelve Nether Gods Baleful Grand Formation, they were trapped within the Overlord Blade Domain.

At this moment, before Luo Chen, they were lambs waiting for slaughter.

The Yuantu sword rose and fell, its dharma power spraying and swallowing.

Luo Chen could sense that the sword spirit sealed in the third layer was now radiating a lively will.

Luo Chen looked at the blood cloud beneath his feet, a thoughtful expression on his face.

Compared to the forcibly refined Scarlet Firmament Sword Qi, it seemed that this blood cloud born from slaughter was what the Yuantu sword spirit truly liked?

Why would that be?

"When this sword first emerged, it was named the God-Slaying Sword, because it was rumored to have slain an Ascendant-level great power."

"And that Ascendant, if nothing unexpected, should be Blood Sea Ancestor of the Primordial Demon Sect."

"So, did the Yuantu sword spirit also inherit Blood Sea Ancestor's habits?"

Just as Luo Chen was pondering, a urgent shout came to his ear.

"Fellow Daoist Luo, be careful!"

Luo Chen suddenly looked up. In his line of sight, a massive fist, piercing through the formation light curtain, came crashing down at him from beyond the domain.

Without any hesitation, he threw a punch as well.

Boom!

The void trembled, the blood cloud collapsed.

Luo Chen stood firm in the air, letting the blood cloud beneath his feet gather again.

And his eyes were fixed on the slowly retracting massive fist, meeting the furious gaze of its owner.

A fourth-rank Demon Emperor who cultivated both dharma and body?

It was a bald-headed man, who now, seeing the sword light that still hadn't stopped its slaughtering rhythm, roared angrily, "Come out!"

Luo Chen twitched the corner of his mouth, not responding to him at all.

Instead, he looked at another figure who had arrived hurriedly, one step too late.

"Is this the help you said you'd provide to intercept?"

Divine Fire Sage smiled sheepishly. "Apologies, I let this demon find a loophole. It won't happen again."

"Hmph!"

Luo Chen snorted coldly, turned, and left.

The blood cloud spanned the sky, its shadow covering the earth.

The slaughter continued.

The warfire still blazed.

A miserable, earth-shaking scream rang out.

It signified that another fourth-rank Demon Emperor had completely fallen.

Luo Chen had once personally watched Goldfeather Sage shatter a Demon Emperor's physical body. The Demon Emperor's Sage infant kept teleporting, but was ultimately blocked by the Twelve Nether Gods Baleful Grand Formation.

A single golden feather pierced through the Sage infant, stringing it up like a candied hawthorn.

Looking at that infant, shaped like a young beast, Goldfeather Sage said contemptuously in front of Luo Chen, "Not of my human race, yet you learn the methods of transformation and spirit refinement. In the end, you end up neither fish nor fowl."

Then, with a shake of the golden feather, the Sage infant suddenly shattered.

Goldfeather Sage gave Luo Chen a deep look before drifting away to pursue another Demon Emperor who had previously besieged him.

Luo Chen's expression was as usual, completely unconcerned by this provocative act.

Standing atop the blood cloud, he continued his butchery.

But unlike the earlier, unrestrained slaughter, as he controlled the Yuantu sword in battle, he grew more and more familiar with the techniques of the *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon*.

Sword light differentiation, sword qi transforming into dragons.

Myriad sword threads, even the Great Sword Art, Sword-Melting Escape...

These techniques, which had once seemed so profound, became increasingly practiced in battle.

The Wan Jian Pavilion's Pavilion Master Li, who had long since dealt with his opponent, had somehow followed behind Luo Chen, watching him experiment and explore, his eyes filled with complex emotions.

Others had all considered this a dangerously sudden assault battle.

But for Luo Chen, it had turned into something like a trial to hone himself.

"Treating a life-and-death battle as a whetstone to sharpen oneself—such a mindset secretly aligns with my path of the sword cultivator."

"No wonder he agreed to discuss the matter of Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid with me in detail!"

"This medicine is of great use to him as well!"

Luo Chen didn't care about Pavilion Master Li's spying.

His current actions were also quite different from his usual style.

He rarely treated life-and-death battles as a means to temper himself, often going all out instead.

The reason he was doing this now was simply that the overall situation was already decided!

When the Twelve Nether Gods Baleful Grand Formation had fallen, when the six mid-Sage combatants, and even Nine Spirit Primogenitor, a powerhouse comparable to a Great Cultivator, had joined forces to kill into the Overlord Blade Domain, the outcome had long been sealed.

Buzz...

A terrifying cicada cry suddenly spread for several thousand li.

Luo Chen looked up and saw a golden-armored giant shatter with a boom.

Then, Elder Fu's sorry figure emerged from within.

Although sorry, he had ultimately survived.

And the Six-Winged Golden Cicada, after having two of its wings broken by Luo Chen, was no match for Elder Fu.

Shua!

A Daoist holding an iron ruler flew to where Luo Chen and Pavilion Master Li were.

It was Iron View Daoist.

"My side is resolved."

Pavilion Master Li praised, "Iron View Fellow Daoist has good methods! Under the siege of so many demon beasts, you still managed to kill the Demon Emperor strong enemy."

Iron View Daoist was neither sad nor happy, his expression calm. "You flatter me. My meager methods are not as fast as Pill Sect's instant annihilation of enemies, cannot compare to Goldfeather Fellow Daoist's one-against-two, and even Pavilion Master Li, who personally killed one of the only two fourth-rank mid-stage Demon Emperors here, is not someone I can match."

Pavilion Master Li laughed heartily, stroking his flowing beard, somewhat self-satisfied.

But as his gaze swept over the Sages scattered in various places, a hint of wariness arose in his heart.

In times of peace, everyone kept a low profile, only judging each other by realm.

Because of the sharp killing power of sword cultivators, over time they had gained the reputation of being the strongest at the same rank.

But when it came to true life-and-death combat, every single one of these people had endless tricks up their sleeves. No one had truly proven weaker than their sword-cultivating counterparts.

Especially that Nine Spirit Primogenitor!

Previously, everyone said he had abundant dharma power but poor technique.

But now, with the means to trap the Thunder Prison Roc and fight fiercely for a whole day and night, who would dare say his technique was poor?

The five Sages each stood in one place, looking down at a battle group that had somehow already fought deep into the earth's veins.

The nine-colored light river still flowed and churned.

The thunder light surged, but grew weaker and weaker.

"This demon will be defeated!" Pavilion Master Li observed for a long while before concluding.

The others nodded in tacit agreement.

It was rumored that this Thunder Prison Roc had once fought with the Five Elements Divine Sect's Supreme Elder, Divine Origin Sage, and survived, so it should have the capability of a Great Cultivator.

Now it seemed, they had overestimated it.

Luo Chen's eyes flickered, muttering to himself.

A hundred years had passed, and the Thunder Prison Roc's realm hadn't improved much!

Was demon race cultivation inherently slow? Or had the injuries from that battle a hundred years ago been too severe, so it had spent the last century recuperating without progressing?

If Nine Spirit Primogenitor really killed it, that would be a good thing.

No matter how stupid this demon was, it should still remember him.

If news of him were to spread back to Green Parasol Mountain, it would only invite unnecessary trouble.

"Whether Nine Spirit Primogenitor can kill him or not, as long as he's still alive, I'll add a strike to shatter him to pieces!"

Just as Luo Chen was muttering, the battle deep in the earth was nearing its end.

Thump! Thump! Thump!

The earth's crust shook, the ground split open.

Countless magma spewed out, then poured back in.

The originally faint thunder light suddenly flared up.

Boom!

With a single explosive sound, the nine-colored light river was broken open.

A stream of light flew out from within.

Looking closely, there was no majestic lightning roc bird at all, only a ball of light flickering.

It was the Thunder Prison Roc's Sage infant!

"We cannot let this thief escape!"

Luo Chen made a prompt decision and shouted loudly.

The others also struck without hesitation.

In the night sky, sword light, iron ruler, golden feather, and talismans all struck at the Sage infant from various directions.

Luo Chen's attack was among them, the Yuantu sword transforming into a beam of blood light, shooting through the air.

At this critical moment, the formation light curtain covering the Overlord Blade Domain instantly dissipated.

Ripples undulated in the void.

A snow-white fist slowly extended from the void ripples.

The fist opened, grasping the Thunder Prison Roc's Sage infant light ball, seemingly ignoring the attacks coming at it.

Boom!

With a single explosive sound, all the attacks bloomed like fireworks.

Beautiful, yet utterly harmless.

The moment Luo Chen saw that snow-white fist, his pupils contracted sharply, but he did not stop.

The blood sword spun its tip madly, stabbing onto the fist.

Ding!

The blood sword, as if struck heavily, was bounced back.

From deep in the earth came a muffled roar. Nine Spirit Primogenitor, like a god, shot into the sky surrounded by nine colored light bands.

But as fast as he appeared, he returned just as fast.

Almost no one could see how the owner of that snow-white fist had struck. The next moment, they saw Nine Spirit Primogenitor fall into the dust, his shoulder hanging limply.

The ripples in the void slowly dissipated.

The six cultivators, who had achieved such bountiful battle results, now looked at each other in dismay.

The turbulent Overlord Blade Domain was still in chaos, but here, it was terrifyingly quiet.

Luo Chen gripped the blood sword, his right hand trembling uncontrollably.

From the corner of his eye, he caught a glimpse of a red dot on the sword's tip.
