# Chapter 1017: Luo Chen Emerges from Seclusion, Arranging His Final Affairs

By the shores of the Nether Lake.

The number of gathered people had grown larger and larger.

From the initial few Sage elders, it had reached the point where almost every important figure of the Luo Tian Sect was present.

Such as Luo Chen's disciple Qu Lingjun and his Dao partner Meng Qin'er, as well as the Heartless Fairy and Shi Lan from the Icecastle lineage.

The older generation of Adept-stage cultivators had all arrived, while some newly promoted Adepts had also set aside their affairs and come in an unending stream.

Everyone knew that the Supreme Elder was about to emerge from seclusion.

When the raging waves on the Nether Lake finally subsided, the crowd stared with eager anticipation.

Especially Luo Chen's two Dao partners, who could barely restrain themselves from rushing in.

However, they were quickly stopped by Qu Lingjun and his wife.

"Masters' wives, the Nether water is filthy. Adept-stage cultivators cannot touch it at all!"

"Master's wife Caiyi, the master is blessed by heaven and will surely be fine. Please wait a moment; he will come out soon."

The couple each moved to persuade the two young women.

And the commotion in the arena seemed to quiet down along with Sima Huiniang and Gu Caiyi's patient waiting.

*Splash, splash…*

Ripples rose once more, but this time they were not raging waves; rather, it was as if the Nether water was actively receding.

Soon!

Amidst layer upon layer of waves, two figures stepped across the waves and emerged from the lake, coming into everyone's view.

With just one glance, all attention fell upon the tall, handsome man wearing feathered robes and a tall crown, as majestic as a heavenly god.

"Congratulations to the Supreme Elder on emerging from seclusion!"

Dozens of voices erupted like a mountain roar and ocean tide, their excitement and joy overflowing.

Facing everyone's congratulations, Luo Chen smiled. "I've made you all worry. This is not a place for lengthy conversation. Let us move to the Luo Tian Grand Hall and talk further!"

Everyone assented in unison.

Gu Caiyi wanted to step forward, but her foot paused, her gaze falling directly on the woman behind Luo Chen.

Cloud-like hair, flower-like face, ice-like bones, jade-like skin.

As she lightly stepped between the turbid waves, there was a hint of belatedly realized mature and graceful charm.

It wasn't just Gu Caiyi.

In fact, many people had noticed the woman silently following behind Luo Chen.

Canglong Fairy of the Luo Tian Sect's Heavenly Mountain lineage—this was not unfamiliar.

But what was unfamiliar was the temperament she now revealed, and the affectionate eyes with which she looked at Luo Chen.

Luo Chen seemed not to notice, offering no explanation, yet he appeared to have observed everything and directly gave the answer through his actions.

He extended his left hand, suspending it before the woman.

Canglong hesitated for a moment, then still extended her frost-white jade-like wrist.

Luo Chen smiled faintly, squeezed the woman's cold little hand, and flew toward the Luo Tian Grand Hall through the path the crowd had opened.

Such an act was equivalent to declaring everything.

Gu Caiyi was both shocked and angry. She instinctively looked at Sima Huiniang, only to find her expression perfectly calm.

She did not follow the main group but immediately stopped and blocked Sima Huiniang.

"What exactly is going on?"

Facing the interrogation, Sima Huiniang was exceptionally calm, as if she had known this question would come. She said in a low voice: "Exactly what you saw. Canglong has become our husband's third Dao partner. It's that simple."

Gu Caiyi was furious. "You knew this might happen long ago? Were those rumors also spread by you?"

Sima Huiniang did not avoid the question. "The rumors about Canglong within the Luo Tian Sect did not originate from me. But after our husband returned, the rumors not only did not stop but grew even fiercer—that did have some contribution from me."

"You!" Gu Caiyi was quite angry. When it came to matters involving Luo Chen, she found it hard to control her temper. "Our husband is not one to indulge in sensual pleasures. What made you think mere rumors would move his heart?"

Sima Huiniang countered, "Isn't the result exactly what I thought it would be?"

Gu Caiyi was speechless, momentarily unable to refute.

Sima Huiniang waved her hand instead. "From the very beginning, I never intended to move our husband's heart. The rumors were targeted at Canglong. The fiercer the rumors, the harder it was to erase the debt of gratitude from when our husband saved and helped her back then. Once she made up her mind and threw herself into his arms, with our husband's personality of not taking the initiative but not refusing either, what do you think the result would be?"

Before Gu Caiyi could answer, she said decisively: "Moreover, in recent years, our husband has shown signs of almost going mad with demonic possession in his pursuit of strength. I happened to learn that Canglong possesses a Seven-Aperture Exquisite Body, so it was destined that he would not refuse. With our husband's style of not initiating, not refusing, but being very responsible... tsk tsk. Sister Caiyi, we're about to have another sister. Oh, she's older than us and higher in cultivation than us both—should we call her elder sister?"

By the end, Sima Huiniang's words carried a hint of mockery.

Her gaze toward Gu Caiyi also held a somewhat playful meaning.

Gu Caiyi took a deep breath. She didn't care about titles like elder or younger sister; she simply didn't want to share Luo Chen's already scarce affection with more people.

She coldly looked at Sima Huiniang and spat out three words: "Why this time?"

Sima Huiniang did not answer her this time, merely shrugged.

Gu Caiyi sneered. "All for nothing was the time we supported each other through those difficult years. I didn't expect that when times got better, you would become more and more degenerate. In cultivation, in matters of the heart, it's the same. Fine. From your initiative to bring in Canglong, it's no different from declaring defeat. I'll wait until I achieve the Sage realm, then compete with Canglong!"

Having said this, she shot into the sky and flew toward the main peak of the Luo Tian Sect.

Behind her, after she finished those words, Sima Huiniang almost instinctively clenched her fists.

The sharp nails of the woman's hand deeply pierced her palm, drawing blood.

She raised her hand, and the blood-stained fingers caressed her carefully maintained but still aging face.

"If I weren't old and fading, if I hadn't spent ten years of my vitality protecting Lingxi to the point where forming a Sage is hopeless, why would I have invited a wolf into the house!"

"Fine. If you want to compete, then compete. I brought Canglong in precisely so she would compete with you."

"Compete, then. You two compete. Just wait until my son Lingxi grows up!"

...

In the Luo Tian Grand Hall.

Everyone sat below, while Luo Chen sat alone at the head.

Regarding various major and minor matters of the sect, they were reported one by one while Luo Chen had the time.

Of course, most reports only mentioned the good and omitted the bad.

Occasionally, when bad news was reported, it was to request more resource allocation.

For example, Lingfengzi had been persistently requesting that the Grand Pill Academy allocate more cultivation pills to the Misty Peak disciples.

But the Luo Tian Sect had been cut off from the outside world for decades, relying entirely on self-sufficiency for medicinal herbs. There was no way to give freely to a single peak, so Li Yingzhang had been refusing.

Now that Luo Chen was present, he naturally gave his judgment.

However, the judgment was not a specific number but a statement supporting Li Yingzhang.

"Li Yingzhang is the Sect Master. Everything he does is for the sect's development. You must respect and execute his decisions and orders!"

Li Yingzhang was overjoyed, his tone becoming more confident.

Lingfengzi fell silent, no longer insisting on more pills.

Watching this scene, Luo Chen merely sneered inwardly.

Although his journey so far seemed solid, to those around him and his enemies, it had been too fast and too steep.

Even describing it as earth-shattering would not be an exaggeration.

This had led to those following him, unable to keep up, developing inappropriate thoughts and making small moves, which was normal.

Perhaps it wasn't just the subordinate disciples and attendants; even his Dao partners and disciples might have some extra thoughts.

Regarding their thoughts and various actions, Luo Chen saw them but did not take them to heart.

The current Luo Tian Sect, for him, no longer provided much assistance, only remaining a responsibility.

For the sake of this responsibility, delaying his own cultivation too much would not be worth it.

Therefore, Luo Chen merely watched coldly.

Even if his bed partners occasionally had jealous little moves, as long as they didn't affect him, Luo Chen was too lazy to correct them.

His only consolation now was probably that the Luo Tian Sect had been living in seclusion in the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land for these decades!

Otherwise, with the fearsome reputation he had earned in the Southern Frontier and Western Desert, his disciples might have been "acting like bullies backed by power," causing him a mountain of trouble.

He did not want to do the kind of "clean up after the younger generation" work.

As Luo Chen received reports from the Luo Tian Sect's high-level members about their respective situations, time passed bit by bit.

By the end, Luo Chen had a thorough understanding of the entire sect's condition.

The current Luo Tian Sect, aside from his own Supreme Elder lineage, had five other branches.

They were: the Misty Peak branch led by Lingfengzi, the War God Peak branch led by Wang Yuan, the Ghost Emperor Peak branch where Bai Meiling resided, the Heavenly Mountain Icecastle branch inherited by Canglong Fairy, and the Haoranzi branch stationed outside at Shangyou City.

Apart from these five branches, there were numerous actual institutions below.

Such as the Grand Pill Academy, Enforcement Hall, Transmission Hall, Treasure Artifact Hall, and so on.

Although it sounded like many departments, the total number of people was only a little over a thousand, with many mixed elements.

For example, disciples from the six branches would all serve in various halls and academies.

A typical case was Luo Chen's disciple Qu Lingjun, who served as the Dean of the Grand Pill Academy.

This situation, viewed positively, was "small but complete"; viewed negatively, it was factionalism.

Regarding this, Luo Chen was also powerless.

After all, the emergence of this situation was not unrelated to him.

Initially, to attract people like Lingfengzi and Canglong to join, he had specially allowed them to establish their own branches within the Luo Tian Sect and continue their original traditions.

Originally, this situation would have gradually changed with Luo Chen's careful governance, completely integrating them into the sect.

But in recent years, Luo Chen had either been traveling outside or focusing on his own cultivation, not paying much attention to sect governance, resulting in the current situation where the tail wags the dog.

"Forget it!"

"As long as I am still here, the more branches and the stronger the people below, the better the Luo Tian Sect will be."

"And if I am no longer here..."

This situation would require specific analysis—whether it was a case of violent death or a glorious ascension. The two scenarios would have completely different impacts on the sect.

Luo Chen no longer wanted to think about it. He gently coughed.

The hall instantly fell silent.

"I have three things to say!"

"First, if you all have requests in the future, you don't have to look inward; you can also look outward. I will batch-refine a special type of magical artifact for you to roam the Nether Abyss."

Upon hearing this, everyone's eyes lit up.

The Nether Abyss was terrifying to hear about, but that was because of the ubiquitous Nether water.

If Luo Chen could refine artifacts that allowed people to roam the abyss, then the Nether Abyss would no longer be something to fear but a place to covet.

After all, this abyss buried the ruins of a generation's Ascendant-level sacred land!

No one knew exactly how many treasures and opportunities it contained.

But everyone knew that the earlier they explored, the more benefits they would gain.

Luo Chen was very satisfied with everyone's reaction. He didn't care how much energy refining that batch of artifacts would cost him.

After refining the Nether Water Spring, he already had great confidence in this matter.

Mastering the Nether Water Spring was equivalent to mastering the Nether Abyss—this was no empty talk.

Even if Luo Chen had not yet reached the level of directly controlling the Nether Abyss, letting his disciples roam the abyss without pressure was still a simple matter.

After everyone had digested the first matter, Luo Chen continued.

And this time, his gaze fell on Canglong Fairy.

"I intend to formally form a Dao partnership with Junior Sister Canglong. I wish to hold a celebration ceremony to announce it publicly and share the joy with all disciples."

Everyone had obviously guessed this matter.

But before they could offer their congratulations, a graceful figure suddenly stood up.

Luo Chen calmly looked over. "Caiyi, do you have something to say?"

Under his gaze, Gu Caiyi's face was somewhat pale, but her words were quite steady.

"Congratulations to my husband for gaining another confidante. Caiyi stood up only to, as your wife, volunteer to handle this matter. I hope my husband will grant permission."

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow, a smile appearing on his face.

"Very good. So understanding—truly worthy of being my woman. Then this matter..."

"Senior Brother, in my opinion, let's forget the celebration ceremony!"

Luo Chen frowned slightly, his gaze falling on the woman who had spoken.

Canglong, who had been silent all along, smiled and said, "Although the Luo Tian Sect has been peaceful for a long time, there's no need for such extravagance and waste. Since there are no outsiders to participate anyway, everyone will naturally come to know in time."

Gu Caiyi was puzzled. "Back then, my husband gave Sima Huiniang and me a grand Dao partnership ceremony. Don't you want to openly and legitimately become my husband's Dao partner?"

Faced with this question, the most agitated were the few people standing behind Canglong.

The Heartless Fairy and Shi Lan from Icecastle.

Canglong Fairy smiled and shook her head. "Thank you for your kindness, Sister Caiyi. Canglong does not like ostentation. Let this matter rest!"

Gu Caiyi was still puzzled.

But Luo Chen was beginning to understand.

It wasn't that Canglong didn't want this recognition, but she was completely different from Gu Caiyi and Sima Huiniang.

Those two were "wives who shared hardships" with Luo Chen. No matter how great the favor, they could bear it.

In the eyes of those below, it was what they deserved.

But Canglong Fairy had not contributed much to the Luo Tian Sect's development. Waking up and immediately achieving the Sage realm, then becoming the Supreme Elder's Dao partner—this was more like someone coming to pick the ripe fruit.

Under such circumstances, holding a celebration ceremony would more easily provoke displeasure from those below.

"Since that's the case, let it be as Junior Sister wishes!"

Luo Chen said indifferently, then spoke of the third matter.

"I am leaving the Luo Tian Sect. This time, the duration is uncertain, and the return date is unknown!"

Those who had previously shown indifference to Luo Chen's family affairs were suddenly thrown into chaos. The grand hall erupted in uproar.

(Chapter End)
