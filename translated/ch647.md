# Chapter 647: The Demon Emperor Descends, a Palm Sinks the Pillar Mountains, the Wind Rises!

Luo Chen's recent behavior truly matched the style of a demonic path powerhouse.

Massacring demon beasts on a grand scale was only one part of it.

Outside the Sunken Sea, those low-rank demon beasts were also mission targets for other wandering cultivators. By hunting those demon beasts and trading them to the Demon Hunter Office, they could earn spirit stones, raise their demon hunter star grade, or temper themselves through battle.

But as soon as Luo Chen arrived, the Myriad Souls Banner unfolded and directly cleared the field.

Other wandering cultivators below Golden Core had to get lost immediately.

As for Golden Core cultivators, unless they were seven-star demon hunters or late Golden Core cultivators, after weighing matters a little, they would also fear Luo Chen's devilish might and retreat far away.

Then again, there were not many late Golden Core cultivators in the Myriad Immortals Assembly to begin with.

At this moment, among those active on the Sunken Sea battlefield, aside from Dao Lan, they were even fewer.

Thus, wherever Luo Chen went, large and small groups of demon beasts were all swallowed up by him alone.

It was precisely such tyrannical conduct that caused the name Devil Lord Azure Yang to spread rapidly.

At this moment, the red-robed man stepped into the range of Three Pillars Gorge. Seeing the scattered battles between cultivators and demon beasts all around, his expression was as still as an ancient well.

He did not make any great movement. He merely raised his hand, and a black banner suddenly enlarged, directly inserting itself into the seawater.

Great waves of ghost qi emerged from the banner and formed one Ghost General after another on the sea surface, even Ghost Kings with solid bodies.

The man gave a low shout.

"Go!"

Immediately, the ghosts flew in all directions, covering the surrounding ten li.

The wandering cultivators currently fighting demon beasts saw this rumored scene. How could they not know what was happening?

"Devil Lord Azure Yang is here!"

"Ten thousand ghosts clear the field, and the group demons offer their heads!"

"Retreat! Retreat! Retreat!"

"Leave the range of the Myriad Souls Banner immediately. Once you are caught inside, the Devil Lord will not show mercy."

Amid cries of alarm, streaks of escape light rose and fell as they fled outward.

The demon beasts that had been fighting them bitterly looked at this scene in blank ignorance, not knowing what had happened.

There were also some powerful demon beasts that sensed something wrong and tried to attack the Ghost Generals and Ghost Kings. The effect was indeed not bad.

Under their attacks, those Ghost Generals and Ghost Kings were easily defeated.

This made those demon beasts relax somewhat.

Those humans were far too timid. These few ghosts were completely unable to withstand a single blow.

Standing atop a tall peak, Luo Chen watched this scene coldly and was not surprised.

His control methods for a widely circulated magic treasure like the Myriad Souls Banner had always been very simple.

He could neither use Ghost Kings to form arrays like Han Zhan, nor reach the level of that white-robed cultivator who condensed a ghost domain.

This meant that the ghosts he released through the Myriad Souls Banner could only act by instinct.

While alive, these ghosts may have had a few skills, but after death, their strength had greatly declined, and the enemies they faced were elite troops who had experienced the battle against the Primordial Demon Sect.

With one side falling and the other rising, it was only normal that they could not withstand a blow.

His current goal was only to drive away the human cultivators first, then focus on dealing with these low-rank demon beasts and that early third-tier Demon King.

"Around two thousand in number. This Three Pillars Gorge battlefield is divided into many smaller battlefields."

"That makes things a little more troublesome."

Luo Chen muttered inwardly. Looking at the demon beast group below gradually concentrating as time passed, a thought rose in his mind.

"I have more or less mastered the All-Encompassing Fire Prison. Using this art to kill low-rank demon beasts is truly using a butcher's cleaver to kill chickens. Why not change methods and try that move?"

As soon as the thought arose, his hands were already moving.

As spiritual seals struck out one after another, the second Origin Pill in his body continuously spat out pure spiritual qi.

One spiritual seal, two spiritual seals... in the blink of an eye, under Luo Chen's butterfly-like spellcasting, hundreds of spiritual seals struck the black banner in the sea.

Suddenly!

A low shout came from the sky.

"Fuse!"

The next moment, within several li of Three Pillars Gorge, waves violently erupted, fell as rain, wind and clouds trembled, and what seemed like thunder sounded. Listening carefully, it was filled with the howling and wailing of countless ghosts.

Amid this miserable wind, bitter rain, and ghostly wailing, the sea suddenly shook.

Over one thousand demon beasts widened their eyes and looked in horror at a monster slowly standing up from beneath the sea surface.

It was a terrifying monster with more than a dozen heads and over a hundred hands.

Half of its body was in the sea, and half above the surface.

After it appeared, the surrounding ghost mist continuously gathered toward it and fused into its body, making its form increasingly solid.

Without the ghost mist obscuring vision, the wandering cultivators who had lingered and refused to leave naturally saw the true state of the battlefield.

When they saw this terrifying monster, discussions erupted.

Luo Chen paid them no attention. His eyes stared fixedly at the ghost standing motionless in the sea.

As if sensing something, the head in the center of the ghost turned and stared toward Luo Chen with what seemed both confusion and resentment. As black mist sprayed from its mouth, it continuously emitted heavy breathing.

"I cannot drive it?"

Luo Chen snorted coldly inwardly.

This art originated from a soul-fusion secret art that Angler had selected from the white-robed cultivator's inheritance after the first assessment mission was completed, and that Luo Chen had exchanged for.

Over these years, he had occasionally researched it, but every time the soul fusion succeeded, a similar situation appeared.

There was no problem with the secret art. The problem was that Luo Chen's cultivation method did not match it.

He had not cultivated the Primordial Demon Sect Soul Refining lineage's techniques, which was why this awkward scene appeared.

But after exploring it over the years, Luo Chen had already prepared a backup plan.

His eyes closed slightly. When they opened again, flowers seemed to bloom and wither inside them, while the moon waxed and waned.

At once, the resentment in the terrifying ghost's eyes faded by more than half, and its confusion grew even denser.

Not only that, Luo Chen pinched a spiritual seal with one hand, and Mirror Flower Water Moon rose another level.

Layered towers stood grimly, ghostly shadows looming.

Celestial immortals drifted endlessly, dreamlike and illusory.

In an instant, the fused will of that ghost was lost inside this tower of ghostly illusions, and Luo Chen's divine sense took the lead.

"Kill!" Luo Chen shouted lowly.

"Kill!" the Ghost King responded.

Then the enormous Ghost King stirred the waves and charged toward the thousand demon beasts.

Seeing this, Luo Chen could not help letting out a breath.

"As expected, if I use illusions to confuse the fused will, I can drive it for my own use."

He could roughly guess why the fused will resisted him.

After all, most of the original ghosts in this Myriad Souls Banner had been killed cleanly by him, leaving only a dozen or so second-tier Ghost Generals.

As for the tens of thousands of ghosts added later, they were either demon beasts killed by him or immortal cultivators who had died at his hands.

These fellows all had enmity with him.

Ordinarily, their wills were scattered and suppressed by the Myriad Souls Banner, which was fine. But once they fused, they easily occupied the lead and resisted Luo Chen.

This was not baseless speculation, but traceable.

Most notably, among the dozen or so heads of the Ghost King comparable to a late Golden Core cultivator, several belonged to people Luo Chen knew well.

Yan Nantian.

The Demon Spider Crab.

Qian Ting, seventh level of Golden Core.

There were also several Golden Core cultivators who had died at the Mo clan fishery in the battle on Cold Light Island, such as the Wu clan's second son, the old woman of Hundred Craft Mountain, and others.

Luo Chen's gaze was cold. Looking at the one-sided slaughter below, he thought even more.

He did not master this soul-fusion secret art very deeply. At least, he could not fuse a Ghost King with only one head like the white-robed cultivator had.

However, a fused Ghost King comparable to a late cultivator could also serve as one of his trump-card killing moves.

In this final battle at Three Pillars Gorge, he would practice it more to avoid being rusty when the time came to use it.

After watching for a while, Luo Chen moved.

Standing atop the tall peak, his sleeves snapped loudly. Strands of azure flame flowed down from his fingertips, landing on demon beasts that were gravely injured but not dead.

Burn-refine. Devour.

When the azure flames returned, majestic vitality leapt within them.

The price was clumps of ash swallowed by the sea.

The nearby Myriad Immortals Assembly wandering cultivators who personally watched this battle were all horrified.

They both feared the powerful monster Luo Chen had fused and marveled at the subtlety of his fire control.

In particular, this leisurely slaughter made every person's back sweat and heart grow cold.

"So this is Devil Lord Azure Yang?"

Amid their shock, everyone looked at the man standing atop the tall peak with growing awe, and even fear.

The battlefield was moving.

Luo Chen leapt down from the peak and followed behind the fused Ghost King, burn-refining demon beasts while driving the Ghost King into other regions of Three Pillars Gorge.

The geography here was special, a place where three seas converged. Therefore, large-scale demon beasts were stationed here.

Luo Chen advanced all the way, capturing nearby demon beasts as he went.

Massive vitality flowed into his body. The Withering Glory True Fire became increasingly vigorous, long past its state when the Flame Alliance had painstakingly cultivated it and it had only just reached fourth tier.

Luo Chen, who received the feedback, also gained tremendous benefits.

His already crimson face was now so dark red it seemed it might drip blood.

Luo Chen did not know what would happen if he continued devouring like this, but in the unseen depths, he clearly knew this act would definitely benefit him.

The opportunity to break through to Ancient Wilderness Fourth Tier might be on this trip.

On a surging sea surface, two men and one woman formed a formation and tightly surrounded a demon beast resembling a toad.

Joy gradually grew in Guan Xiaoping's eyes. As she controlled the flying sleeve in her hand, she said, "Senior Brother Changfengzi, as long as we kill this River-Swallowing Toad, we can advance to four-star demon hunters when we go back, right?"

Wu Tao at the side was also excited. "A middle third-tier Demon King, and one soon to advance to late-stage. It will definitely exchange for plenty of merits. Junior Sister, let us put in more strength!"

"All right."

Changfengzi stood at the formation eye and reminded them, "This is a battlefield. The situation is strange and ever-changing. Do not consume too much mana."

The young man and woman gave a hum, but clearly did not take it too seriously. The mana they released was several degrees greater than before.

In their view, they were merely dealing with these stupid demon beasts. Why keep anything in reserve? Wouldn't it be fine to kill it quickly and leave?

Changfengzi shook his head, holding a talisman tightly in his hand without releasing it, in case of accident.

Gradually, the River-Swallowing Toad trapped in the middle grew increasingly weak, and its struggles became smaller and smaller.

Success was at hand.

Just as joy appeared on the brows of the three...

Suddenly!

An enormous suction force came from beneath their feet.

"Ah!"

"Senior Brother, save me!"

"What is going on?"

Among the three, Guan Xiaoping was the first to be pulled into the deep sea by that terrifying suction.

Immediately after, Wu Tao, whose mana was nearly depleted, was also dragged down.

Changfengzi released all his mana and wiped a finger across his eyes. Spiritual light blazed in his eyes, and he instantly saw the scene in the deep sea.

A golden toad only about one zhang in size crouched on the deep seabed, its mouth wide open.

The terrifying suction came precisely from it.

Moreover, this golden toad clearly looked exactly the same as the River-Swallowing Toad they had controlled, except that its skin color was different.

"Shedding a golden cicada's shell?"

No, it was a golden toad shedding its shell.

This Demon King's true body was not a River-Swallowing Toad at all, but a Sea-Containing Golden Toad.

This was a remnant of an ancient desolate beast. Rumor said it possessed a lifebound divine ability to swallow and spit oceans and drive water and waves.

Even if it was currently only middle third-tier, it was still not something ordinary early Golden Core cultivators could contend with.

Information flowed through Changfengzi's mind in an instant, but his hands were not slow. He directly activated that talisman.

Immediately, a wave of azure light erupted, enveloping all three.

Each person felt their body lighten considerably, almost creating the illusion that they could ride the wind away.

It was the Liu clan's Wind-Riding Talisman.

Guan Xiaoping and Wu Tao were immediately overjoyed, and their figures moved nimbly toward the sea surface.

As long as they left this sea, the Sea-Containing Golden Toad could do nothing to them.

Croak!

A toad cry came from the Sea-Containing Golden Toad's belly.

The next moment, the River-Swallowing Toad, which had lost all sign of life, suddenly opened its eyes and also opened its mouth.

A heaven-reaching flood surged from its belly and immediately knocked the three into the ocean.

"This is bad!"

"My life is over!"

"No, we will die like this. We must use the method bestowed by the clan ancestor."

Just as despair arose in the three hearts, a rumbling sound broke through the sea.

Within their sight, a giant black arm pierced through layer after layer of waves and grabbed toward them.

Just as it was about to seize them, the arm suddenly collapsed. In an instant, it broke through the space around them and reformed behind them.

This time, its target had abruptly changed to that small golden toad.

Croak!

Another toad cry rang out. The sea immediately surged and blasted that black hand into wisps of black smoke.

The golden toad's eyes flickered. Through the turbid waves, it glanced at the terrifying monster on the sea surface, then no longer lingered and swam into the deep sea.

"A pity. I let such a desolate beast remnant escape. Otherwise, if I had subdued it, it could have become a great helper."

Luo Chen shook his head. His gaze fell on the enormous River-Swallowing Toad corpse.

There was no soul aura.

For such an existence, even if the Withering Glory True Fire burn-refined it, it could not extract much vitality.

In his view, this River-Swallowing Toad had died many years ago. It was merely an attached puppet of that Sea-Containing Golden Toad.

Three bedraggled figures escaped from the seawater and immediately looked toward the terrifying monster standing in the sea.

"Hiss, what kind of ghost is that?" Guan Xiaoping was incomparably shocked.

Changfengzi did not care about this and immediately looked at Luo Chen.

"So it was Fellow Daoist Daoist Azure Yang."

Luo Chen glanced at them and said nothing. He beckoned to the fused Ghost King and stepped away over the wind and waves.

Changfengzi's expression changed slightly, but he still bowed deeply toward Luo Chen's back from afar.

"Many thanks, Fellow Daoist, for saving our lives!"

Wu Tao disapproved. "Why thank him? Even if he had not come to save us, as long as we used the treasures bestowed by the clan ancestor before we left, we could still have survived."

Why had they not done so earlier?

Under the crisis of life and death, he probably would not even have been able to think clearly about what method to use.

Changfengzi straightened and shook his head. "That is not how it should be said. To him, it was only a casual act, but in the end, it saved us from using one trump card. We have only just arrived at the Myriad Immortals Assembly. If we squander our life-preserving methods so easily, how will we continue tempering ourselves later?"

Wu Tao opened his mouth but had no words to refute him.

The always lively Guan Xiaoping said nothing at this time. She merely looked at that gradually distant back, and a few complex emotions appeared in her gaze.

Saving Changfengzi and the others had merely been a casual act.

Luo Chen's original intent had been to capture that Sea-Containing Golden Toad. He wanted to know where desolate beasts that did not walk the path of demon core transformation were truly strong.

Was it their powerful physiques, with demonic power evenly distributed throughout the whole body as rumors said?

What difference was there between such existences and his own Ancient Wilderness physique?

"Forget it. I will find opportunities to collect one or two pieces of information related to desolate beasts later."

"Speaking of which, after Black King consumed large amounts of Emperor Flow Nectar, signs of ancestral reversion appeared, and he also seems to be walking the path of desolate beasts. But he still retained his demon core. Should I go back and study his body?"

Luo Chen rushed to the various demon beast gathering places in Three Pillars Gorge while thinking about these random matters.

While slaughtering demon beasts everywhere and harvesting enormous vitality, he gradually made a new discovery.

The demon beasts stored here seemed much stronger than those in other places.

Earlier, there had been a middle third-tier Demon King. Although Luo Chen had erased it, before it died, it had forcefully blown apart the fused Ghost King's body.

Due to this misfortune turning into a blessing, Luo Chen comprehended a few small tricks of the soul-fusion secret art, allowing portions of the fused Ghost King's body to gather and scatter as he wished.

And the River-Swallowing Toad he had just encountered had actually had a true body that was the Sea-Containing Golden Toad, a powerful desolate beast.

And these were only the ones guarding the periphery.

What about the most powerful Great Demon King?

Just as doubt arose in Luo Chen's heart, his divine sense suddenly trembled.

Within the black sea, dazzling saber light broke through the water, cutting out a vacuum without water for several dozen li.

A tiny black dot staggered out from the sea.

Following closely behind were countless thick tentacles.

The tentacles were covered with disgusting suckers, dense and packed together. Just looking at them made the soul feel extremely uncomfortable.

"Is that the rumored Great King Squid? It actually forced Dao Lan to this extent?"

Although he had not seen its true body, he had long heard of this powerful demon beast.

"Devil Lord, lend me a hand!" Dao Lan's shout came over.

Luo Chen was moved and was about to step forward when he suddenly turned his head.

He activated Spiritual Eye Art to its limit. A thousand li away, a drifting figure crossed the sea.

Where he passed, the ocean boiled, and dark clouds moved with him.

"A Sage?"

"No, it seems to be a Demon Emperor!"

Luo Chen cried out in alarm, his expression changing greatly. He flew at extreme speed out of Three Pillars Gorge.

The Moonbreaker Wings spread out.

Mana surged wildly, and his physique transformed.

In an instant, a giant peng bird spread its wings and swept across the sea.

In the distance, a handsome man saw this scene and blinked.

"He runs quite fast."

After offering that praise, he appeared above Three Pillars Gorge in the next instant.

Such a huge disturbance had naturally long since alarmed the Myriad Immortals Assembly wandering cultivators performing missions here. They also discovered the dense demonic qi emitted by the handsome man and had already begun scattering in flight.

But how could the escape speed of Golden Core and Foundation Establishment cultivators compare to a fourth-tier Demon Emperor's?

Even though they had been given a thousand li head start, they were still within sight.

The man smiled. Without any visible movement, the wandering cultivators in his sight fell into the sea like dumplings.

There were exceptions.

Three people, after falling, staggered back up and then shot away from Three Pillars Gorge at even greater speed.

"These human cultivators..."

The handsome man muttered, but did not attack again.

His gaze fell on the three tall mountains beneath him.

"So this is one of the formation eyes? It can be changed, but not destroyed."

He murmured, then pressed down with his palm.

Boom! Boom! Boom!

Only three rumbling sounds were heard. The three sky-supporting peaks standing in Three Pillars Gorge were forcefully sunk into the ocean.

They inserted straight into the sea just like that.

After the mountain peaks were submerged by seawater, the handsome man raised his head and looked toward the lifeless Sunken Sea.

There was clearly nothing there.

Yet he murmured,

"The wind has risen."

*(End of Chapter)*
