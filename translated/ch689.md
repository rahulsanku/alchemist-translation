# Chapter 689: The Final Trial, the Strongest Fifth-Tier Divine Pill!

The fourth trial of the Pill Realm assessment was finally different from the first three.

It was not a difference in the examination question itself, but in the direction of the assessment.

The first three trials examined an alchemist's foundational skill. From medicinal materials, pill principles, and pill-refining methods to all kinds of details, all were included.

Summed up, it was self-creation of pill formulas.

Self-creating pill formulas was not something one could do unless one was a grandmaster or possessed extraordinary talent.

For an ordinary alchemist, the first three trials alone would leave them stuck for an unknown number of years.

Luo Chen knew that even though his foundation was solid, if he did not have the system helping him screen and audit, and relied only on his own derivation and trial and error, the time required would not have been merely six or seven years.

Whether the Spirit-Root Pill formula or the demon-soul pill formulas, not one was something an ordinary alchemist could derive in several decades or a hundred years. Some might even spend their entire lives and still fail.

He had taken a shortcut. Only because of that could he arrive at the fourth trial one step ahead of Cape Heaven Matron.

Yet he had not expected this fourth trial to be even "simpler" than he had imagined!

"It tests alchemy technique?"

Luo Chen laughed softly, his heart becoming increasingly relaxed.

He had to admit that for him, with a cheat on his person, the Pill Realm assessment was like walking on a level road!

The first three trials had perhaps caused him great trouble because they required self-created pill formulas. But this clearly more difficult fourth trial did not amount to much.

With concrete pill formulas present, even if they were fourth-tier pills, as long as he was willing to spend achievement points, he could easily enter them no matter how difficult they were.

Especially because

He himself was a fourth-tier alchemist. For entering pill formulas of the same tier, the achievement point cost was fixed at ten points.

And on his current panel, he still had plenty of achievement points remaining.

"However, I still cannot use them carelessly."

Luo Chen closed his eyes and carefully savored the three fourth-tier pill formulas transmitted by that voice just now.

Devil-Transformation Pill, Demon-Transformation Pill, Human-Transformation Pill...

According to the descriptions on the formulas, after the user consumed the pills refined according to them, they could perfectly transform their form.

Devil into demon, demon into human, human into devil.

They were truly wondrous beyond compare.

What startled Luo Chen most was that the so-called Human-Transformation Pill felt seventy to eighty percent familiar to him.

"Transformation Pill?"

After some thought, Luo Chen could not help nodding.

As expected, this Human-Transformation Pill and the Transformation Pill Nether Spring Demon Emperor had given him on Green Parasol Mountain were indeed almost the same.

Aside from a small number of auxiliary materials, the greatest difference lay in the main material.

The main material of the Green Parasol Transformation Pill was the Nascent Soul essence blood of a Sage Sage, while the Human-Transformation Pill of this fourth trial used a fourth-tier spirit medicine called Crooked Infant Fruit instead.

"Crooked Infant Fruit can replace Nascent Soul essence blood? Why have I never seen a similar description in any classics?"

Unlike those other medicinal materials he did not recognize, Luo Chen knew Crooked Infant Fruit. This kind of spirit medicine existed in the Mountains and Seas Realm.

But its descriptions had not the slightest relation to a Nascent Soul.

In other words, if this fruit could replace Nascent Soul essence blood, then either it possessed an effect cultivators of the Mountains and Seas Realm had never developed, or a special effect was stimulated when paired with the other auxiliary materials.

For a time, Luo Chen suppressed the impulse to directly enter the formula through the system and instead sank his mind down to study this pill formula carefully.

He had once successfully refined a furnace of Transformation Pills. If he could thoroughly study the new formula, he could save ten achievement points no matter what.

"It was somewhat wasteful that I spent thirty achievement points entering those three body-tempering formulas earlier."

Luo Chen glanced at the remaining two hundred and seventy achievement points on the system and estimated whether they would be enough for the next assessments.

However, considering that he had passed the third trial one step ahead of Cape Heaven Matron and created psychological pressure for her, those thirty achievement points could be considered worthwhile.

Next, within the scope of his ability, saving even one point was one point.

Because Luo Chen also did not know whether after the fourth trial, there would be a fifth trial, a sixth trial...

...

Within the Pill Realm, medicinal materials were countless.

Overall, these medicinal materials seemed to have been prepared for the formulas in the examination question.

Luo Chen had previously tried gathering some extra medicinal materials from the earlier trials and putting them into his storage ring, attempting to take them away.

But he discovered that every time he reached a new trial, the medicinal materials in his storage ring would vanish into thin air, as if they had never existed.

Only the finished pills he had refined through the assessments could be preserved.

Up to now, eight kinds of pills had appeared in his storage ring in total.

They were the first-tier Five Viscera Origin-Nurturing Pill, the first-tier Snow Spirit Meridian-Opening Powder, the second-tier Spirit-Root Pill, the third-tier Qi-Cleansing Pill, the third-tier Soul-Scouring Pill, and the third-tier Stone-Skin, Thunderbone, and Mercury-Blood Pills.

Their quantities were not large, and their grades were not high.

But they were extremely comprehensive!

Most importantly, even if these refined pills could not be reproduced in the outside world, they still brought Luo Chen enormous inspiration.

If he could slightly improve one or two of the formulas and make them usable in the Mountains and Seas Realm, perhaps they could let Luo Chen's cultivation advance another level.

Leaving aside the others, the Soul-Scouring Pill refined for the demon race, if improved for his own body, would certainly bring tremendous benefit to the foundation of his divine soul!

Perhaps his divine soul could not grow stronger, but refining it a little should still be possible.

Of course, Luo Chen had no time to improve those formulas right now.

It had already been a full eight years since he entered Demon-Fall Land!

According to Tai Sui, in past years, the portal to Demon-Fall Land usually remained open for around forty to fifty years.

But this time, the portal's opening had clearly happened ahead of schedule.

That Shark Emperor Liujun had taken control of Sunken Three Pillar Gorge, causing changes in the Sunken Sea grand formation, which was why Demon-Fall Land opened early.

Therefore, no one knew when Demon-Fall Land would close.

Luo Chen had to seize the time and complete the Pill Realm assessment.

Otherwise, if he ended up stranded here, who knew what unknown troubles might occur?

Within the Pill Realm, Luo Chen's figure began appearing in various places.

He gathered the medicinal materials needed for the three great fourth-tier transformation pills.

This time, there were not only all kinds of spirit plants. Many mineral jades and liquids were also involved.

He even discovered some prepared devil-race bones in the stone house at the center of the Pill Realm. Those were main materials needed to refine the Devil-Transformation Pill.

Clearly, for this fourth trial, Devil Lord Heaven-Refining had already prepared all materials for the person who accepted the inheritance.

During rest, Luo Chen had also wondered why Devil Lord Heaven-Refining went to such painstaking effort to arrange such complicated inheritance assessments.

What was the purpose?

Were the other dozen or so inheritance lands assessed in the same way as the Pill Realm, peaceful and safe?

From what he had seen, these trial assessments did not seem at all like arrangements hastily made by a cultivator before sitting in death. They were far too complicated and detailed.

Or perhaps this place had originally been a True Lord's traveling palace, and these things had been arranged while the owner was still alive, specially prepared for others?

Many questions had no clue.

Luo Chen occasionally speculated, but did not think too deeply.

His energy was still placed on processing the medicinal materials for the three transformation pills.

This time, processing the materials in advance alone took him a full four years.

Such length was truly somewhat beyond expectation.

It was because several medicinal materials could not reach the initial standard for refinement through ordinary processing methods alone. They still required the precipitation of time.

Moreover, for every kind of pill, Luo Chen prepared at least ten sets of materials or more, only to succeed in one effort when the time came.

During this process, Cape Heaven Matron followed closely half a year later.

In the end, she had still caught up to Luo Chen's footsteps.

When the two met, this time they did not exchange any unnecessary words.

Presumably, the moment the other party entered the fourth trial, she had also received the three concrete pill formulas.

For someone without a cheat, even if Cape Heaven Matron styled herself an alchemy grandmaster, one glance was enough to know those three formulas were extremely complex.

If she truly wanted to refine each of them one by one, who knew how much time she would need for trial and error?

Therefore, the moment she entered, she did the same as Luo Chen, busily gathering medicinal materials.

One could even say her movements were larger than Luo Chen's. For every kind of medicinal material, she prepared several dozen sets.

In any case, for them, processing one set of materials was processing, and processing dozens of sets at once was also processing. It was better to prepare thoroughly.

Luo Chen did not pay attention to Cape Heaven Matron's actions, and instead focused on his own little plot of land.

One day four years later,

A strange fragrance spread outward.

Looking at five golden pills the size of pearls, from which faint infant cries seemed to echo, Luo Chen put them away with immense satisfaction.

The Human-Transformation Pill had succeeded!

This was within reason, and he was not the least surprised.

After resting for one night, tomorrow he would directly use achievement points to enter the Devil-Transformation Pill and Demon-Transformation Pill. The materials had already been prepared, so he might as well strike while the iron was hot and refine them in one breath.

He pushed open the Pill Room door, and a pair of vicissitude-filled eyes looked over at him.

It was Cape Heaven Matron.

Luo Chen nodded to her. Without the previous verbal clash, he went straight to rest.

To him, once they reached the fourth trial with exact pill formulas, his speed, already one step ahead of Cape Heaven Matron, became even faster.

The other party had not yet begun refining pills. One could imagine how many failures she would still have to experience afterward.

But Luo Chen had already seen the future of success.

In the small courtyard, Cape Heaven Matron watched Luo Chen's unrestrained back, her expression gloomy, words wanting to come yet stopping.

The other party's pill-refining progress had completely exceeded her expectations.

How long had it been? He had to laboriously process medicinal materials and study the formula, yet the result was that he seemed to have spent no effort and experienced few failures before directly refining one pill.

Such attainment was simply unimaginable.

For a time, Cape Heaven Matron even suspected that the other party was some top human alchemy grandmaster.

But among the few alchemy grandmasters standing at the peak of the Northern Sea, she had heard of all of them and had even privately discussed alchemy with one or two. There was absolutely no person called Devil Lord Azure Yang among them.

Could he be from another great continent?

Yet even so, as fellow top figures, there should not be such a vast gap between them.

She was doubting Luo Chen, and at the same time, she was doubting herself.

Could it be that her so-called reputation as a grandmaster of the Dao of Pills was undeserved?

This doubt intensified in the days that followed, until it reached its peak.

Three months later, Luo Chen successfully refined a furnace of Demon-Transformation Pills!

At that time, Cape Heaven Matron had only just begun refining what she considered the relatively simplest Demon-Transformation Pill.

Comparing the two, Cape Heaven Matron's mind fell into great chaos.

In the field she excelled at most, she was directly crushed by someone else. There was no greater blow in the world.

When the fifth year of the fourth trial arrived, Luo Chen, after more than ten failures, successfully refined the Devil-Transformation Pill.

That increasingly weak strange voice descended upon the Pill Realm once more.

This time, Cape Heaven Matron almost gave up hope.

She could no longer catch up.

She had not even refined a single Demon-Transformation Pill yet. By her estimate, she would need at least another year or two.

But the other party was far too fast!

She found many reasons for herself. Demons, devils, and humans had innate differences in bodily structure. She had wasted too much time processing medicinal materials. No one was assisting her... and so on.

But in the end, the essence was still that her skill was weaker by a measure!

Whether there were other trials afterward, she did not know.

She only knew that in the alchemy inheritance, this opponent she had encountered was someone she could only watch from afar, unable to match.

"Could it be that I will stop here?"

As Luo Chen's figure vanished before her eyes, Cape Heaven Matron fell into a bewilderment she had not felt in several hundred years.

...

In a quiet stone house,

Luo Chen sat cross-legged on the ground, quietly listening to that devilish voice pour into his ears.

A new pill formula was firmly memorized by him.

He had passed the fourth trial without expending too much effort, and had also obtained more than ten special Transformation Pills. Especially since he had left Cape Heaven Matron, his competitor, behind, he should have been delighted.

However, the examination question of this fifth trial, which was also the final trial, made his heart thoroughly restless.

The fifth trial was also the final trial.

As long as he passed, he would obtain the true alchemy inheritance of Devil Lord Heaven-Refining!

Everything was within reach.

However, the examination question of the fifth trial was astonishingly to refine a fifth-tier pill!

A fifth-tier pill!

This was a question that lay within reason, yet outside expectation.

Generally speaking, an alchemist's level was linked to the cultivator's realm.

Even if there were occasionally low-tier cultivators capable of refining pills one tier higher, they were few and far between.

But this time, he had to cross an entire major alchemist rank to refine a fifth-tier pill specially prepared for Ascendant mighty figures!

How could Luo Chen not be shaken? How could he not be anxious?

At the same time, he was wildly delighted that he had obtained a fifth-tier pill formula out of nowhere!

Especially because the completed pill effect described by this fifth-tier formula was simply wondrous beyond compare, worthy of being called the foremost among fifth-tier divine pills!

Even though Luo Chen had never seen or heard of any fifth-tier pill before, just by looking at that pill effect description, he directly crowned it the greatest of the fifth tier!

There was no other reason.

Only because this pill was named: Law-Derivation!

[Law-Derivation Pill: After consumption, it can let an Ascendant cultivator merge the spirit with heaven and earth, evolve law principles, and deepen comprehension of laws. If used by a Sage cultivator, there is a chance to comprehend law power across realms.]

The instant he obtained the formula,

Luo Chen finally understood why Devil Lord Heaven-Refining, as described by Tai Sui, had been praised as the strongest Void Refining True Lord from ancient times to the present.

He also did not doubt in the slightest whether the other party had truly comprehended and mastered more than ten kinds of law power at the same time.

If this pill formula was real, and Devil Lord Heaven-Refining had truly refined it,

Then after consuming it in large quantities, how could he fail to comprehend many laws and reach heaven-reaching Dao attainment?

As for whether the pill formula was true or false?

Luo Chen naturally had a way to distinguish it.

With a thought, the attribute panel fell like a waterfall.

At the same time, an extremely simple line of information slowly appeared before his eyes.

[Detected complete Law-Derivation Pill formula, fifth tier.]

[Enter?]

Crack!

In an instant, Luo Chen clenched his fist, and blue veins bulged beneath his sleeves.

Extreme wild joy erupted uncontrollably from his eyes.

It was real!

This pill formula was real!

*(End of Chapter)*
