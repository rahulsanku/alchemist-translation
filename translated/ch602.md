# Chapter 602: Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake — May It Serve as My Cultivation Ground

Within the Fulong Mountain Range, countless reclusive cultivators dwelt, and streaks of escaping light could be seen flickering through the air at any hour.

But on this particular day, wherever three streaks of light passed — each radiating a steady, measured spiritual pressure — every other cultivator gave way.

The reason was simple.

Each of the three was a Golden Core cultivator. Most importantly, the one at their head bore a familiar face that prompted respectful greetings from every cultivator who caught sight of it.

Eight-star demon hunters. Of these, the Myriad Immortals Assembly currently had exactly three.

Not one of them fell below Golden Core Stage Nine, and every one was a battle-hardened cultivator of formidable experience.

Their power was imposing; their standing, nearly unmatched — second only to the handful of Nascent Soul true beings who presided far above.

For the Angler — an eight-star demon hunter — to personally accompany someone, that person was either an exceptionally powerful cultivator or a newly enrolled Golden Core member of the Assembly.

Either way, neither was someone to be trifled with.

Before a low, squat cave entrance, Duan Li hesitated.

Just as he was about to give up and move to the next candidate, Luo Chen suddenly called after him.

"This place is actually quite fine."

Duan Li looked at him with uncertainty. "Azure Yang, what do you see in it?"

Luo Chen smiled. "The scenery may be lacking, but the wind-sheltering and qi-gathering here are excellent, and the terrain's flow is favorable. By my read, the spiritual energy has been accumulating in the earth beneath for years, holding itself in reserve. To cultivate here would add perhaps a century's worth of progress at minimum."

Duan Li was surprised. "I didn't expect you to have knowledge of earth-vein surveying and dragon-point location as well."

Luo Chen shrugged. "You give me too much credit. I'm only passing along borrowed flowers."

As he spoke, he pointed toward a solitary peak several li away, wreathed in cloud and mist, its true shape hidden.

"Fellow Daoist Xiao Buhuan's Hengyue Cave is just over there. During our half-month on Quyang Island, he and I spoke quite a bit — including the state of the cave dwellings around here."

"Of course, whether you trust it is up to you."

"The next one might be even better."

Duan Li hesitated for a while. In the end, under the steady gaze of Luo Chen's sincere eyes, he gave a small nod.

He stepped out of the cave and told the idly waiting Angler his decision to rent Hidden Wind Cave.

"You're certain you want Hidden Wind Cave?"

The Angler frowned. "Are you aware that while this cave is not on high ground, it sits directly above several underground wind vents? Short-term cultivation here is tolerable, but extended residence will cause one's form and spirit to grow unstable — it carries a hidden drawback for long-term cultivation."

Duan Li wavered again.

On instinct he glanced toward Luo Chen, but this time the other man had deliberately averted his gaze.

After lengthy deliberation, he gritted his teeth and made up his mind.

"Then this is the one. Having Fellow Daoist Xiao Buhuan for a neighbor is a fine thing in itself."

The Angler studied Duan Li carefully, then let his gaze drift — without drawing attention to it — toward Luo Chen.

He recalled: before Duan Li had made up his mind, the man had instinctively looked toward Luo Chen. It seemed likely that his choice of this excellent Tier-Three lower-grade spirit land had been swayed by Luo Chen's words.

*So Azure Yang also knows earth-vein surveying?* the elder mused.

He took a jade slip from his storage pouch, recorded Duan Li's name, then wiped the glowing dot marking Hidden Wind Cave from the map.

"Take your time looking it over. If you need anything installed, let me know. The Demon Hunter Office doesn't collect rent for nothing — basic instruments and formation arrays come with the residence."

"Then I thank the Elder for his generosity," Duan Li said, bowing in thanks.

With that settled, the Angler — who had grown somewhat weary of the proceedings — departed the area with Luo Chen.

...

Two streaks of light floated through the sky, drifting slowly forward.

Map in hand, the Angler introduced the remaining Tier-Three cave dwellings to Luo Chen. Occasionally he pointed out restricted zones within the Fulong Mountain Range — cultivation sites of the Myriad Immortals Assembly's Nascent Soul true beings — warning that cultivators without a personal connection had best stay well away.

Through the elder's remarks, Luo Chen also learned the precise count of true beings currently within the Assembly.

Seven in total.

With the Three Great Wanderers at the head.

Three more Nascent Soul true beings were not, strictly speaking, wandering cultivators — they were powers unto themselves who had joined the Assembly in a wandering cultivator capacity, seeking strength through numbers.

Beyond these, the remaining true being was the newly advanced Longyuan True Being — formerly the only nine-star demon hunter in the Office's history.

Upon hearing this count, Luo Chen found himself quietly turning it over in his mind.

Seven Nascent Soul true beings in the Myriad Immortals Assembly — genuinely impressive, by any reckoning.

But set against Moluo Stream's twelve Stream Masters or the Cang Hai Alliance's score of true beings, the number fell rather short.

More importantly, these three factions combined still had far too few Nascent Soul true beings altogether.

By Luo Chen's understanding of the Eastern Wasteland — where across its thirty-six domains, every domain had at least one true being in residence, many with several — the total there easily surpassed fifty.

Add to that the unknown count of Nascent Soul true beings within the Mingyuan Sect, and the number only climbed higher.

By comparison, the Northern Sea — with its greater abundance of resources — should not have so few.

But the answer came to him almost immediately.

The Primordial Demon Sect had been destroyed.

A transcendent sect of Ascendant rank was, by nature, the power with the greatest concentration of Nascent Soul true beings. The moment it fell, how many brilliant Nascent Soul cultivators had perished with it — reducing the Northern Sea's highest-tier strength by more than half overnight.

Beyond that, the Northern Sea's geography fostered a culture of deep hermitage. Many powerful cultivators chose solitary seclusion — either as independent powers, or as recluses who kept entirely to their own affairs.

Emerald Cold Fairy and Langya True Being, for instance, had not been counted among the three factions until the battle on Cold Light Island.

And the Myriad Immortals Assembly was too young to have absorbed every noteworthy wandering cultivator.

Taking it all together, the Northern Sea's cultivation world still held staggering depth.

"The Three Great Wanderers all cultivate here in the Fulong Mountain Range. Longyuan True Being spends most of his time stationed at Longyuan Immortal City. The other three are largely based in their own territories — it would take something major to bring them here."

"The details you can look up in the Demon Hunter Office's intelligence archive when the time comes. I'll spare you the full account now."

The Angler said all this in an unhurried drawl.

Luo Chen watched the old man's manner, his gaze drifting to the map in the elder's hand. He smiled inwardly.

Without further ceremony, he produced a jade slip with a casual flick and held it out to the elder.

"What's this?" the Angler asked, feigning surprise.

Luo Chen smiled. "I've noticed that Elder has been impressed by this body of mine on more than one occasion. I assumed you have some interest in body tempering. This slip contains my notes on the subject, along with an attached Tier-Three body cultivation method. Think of it as a small offering — perhaps something in it will prove useful."

"During the assessment mission, Elder looked after me well and shared much of what he knew."

"This is a modest token of my gratitude. I hope you won't refuse it."

For the first time, the Angler was genuinely taken aback.

He accepted the jade slip with some hesitation, not examining it immediately, but looking carefully at Luo Chen.

"A gift of this value is not modest at all."

Luo Chen gave a small nod.

It truly was not a modest gift.

Even accounting for the fact that he had omitted certain core insights, and that the attached Tier-Three technique was one of the more rudimentary among his collection of spoils — together, the two were worth well over five hundred thousand spirit stones.

Given the right cultivator and sustained practice, there was a real chance of building a formidable physique within a hundred years.

Especially for a late-stage Golden Core cultivator.

Advancing from Golden Core to Nascent Soul required the three treasures of essence, vitality, and spirit to be fully refined and merged — reaching what was called the Three Flowers Gathering at the Crown.

The first of these — "essence" — referred precisely to blood essence and physical constitution.

Without a sufficiently resilient and powerful body, there was no vessel capable of containing a Nascent Soul.

Even cultivators who had never deliberately trained their bodies could not afford to neglect physical conditioning at the higher stages.

The Angler was at Golden Core Stage Nine. He could not have avoided thinking about these things.

Seeing the sincerity in Luo Chen's manner, the elder thought it over and accepted.

He had been genuinely impressed by Luo Chen's physique on multiple occasions — that had come from the heart. He simply hadn't expected Luo Chen to have noticed, and to have found so apt a way to make use of it.

"You're a thoughtful one. That said, this gift far exceeds anything I've done for you. Is there something else you need? Say it plainly."

Luo Chen did not stand on ceremony, and pointed directly at the map.

"I want the information on the Tier-Three middle-grade and upper-grade cave dwellings. I hope Elder won't hold back."

The Angler raised an eyebrow, studied Luo Chen for a long moment, then slowly nodded.

A brush of his hand across the map.

The spiritual light trembled — and a dozen more bright points appeared.

Another batch of unclaimed dwellings, far superior in quality to anything shown to Duan Li.

Luo Chen relaxed inwardly.

As expected. The old fellow would not release a hawk before spotting the hare.

Without a gift, he would share only the basics, keeping the finest in reserve.

The dwellings they'd visited earlier had been mostly mediocre. Hidden Wind Cave — the one Luo Chen had pointed out — had its flaws, but it was the best of the available lot.

He had read it right.

"One thing I should tell you." The Angler patted the map with a measured gravity. "This batch of caves is of excellent quality — but the rent is not something the average wandering cultivator can sustain."

Luo Chen smiled. "Isn't there the demon hunter star-rank discount? And of course, with Elder's help —"

The Angler shook his head. "Even discounted, the price is absurd. These caves were priced by the true beings. There's only so much I can do for you. Better to just take you to see them first."

His spiritual energy surged; the riding cloud accelerated.

He had no concern about whether Luo Chen could keep up.

Under his guidance, Luo Chen began surveying the truly premium cave dwellings hidden within the Fulong Mountain Range.

...

Dead of night. The chill was deep.

Above a broad expanse of mist-veiled water, two figures stood motionless at length.

"Are you certain you want to rent here?"

"Why not?"

"You've read the map's details. Peng Lake has three lower-grade spirit veins and twenty-seven Tier-Two veins, but they run in scattered directions with no coherent whole. Its listing among top-tier dwellings is purely on account of its sheer size — territory fit to serve as a clan or sect's foundation. The Assembly is made up mostly of wandering cultivators, but even wanderers are people, and people think about legacy and succession. This dwelling was effectively prepared for that kind of purpose. If you truly have the resources for it, why not choose one of the two Tier-Three middle-grade dwellings I mentioned earlier? Better spiritual concentration, and cheaper besides."

Luo Chen looked out over Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake, his gaze settling on the central island in the middle of the water.

This landscape bore some resemblance to his dwelling on the Swallow Islands — the Ten-Li Flat Lake, the small island commanding a view of the moon.

Only dozens of times larger.

Truly a foundation worthy of a clan or sect.

His expression firmed. "This is the one. My mind is made up."

Seeing the elder hesitate, Luo Chen smiled. "I have a method for aggregating spirit veins and raising their grade. To me, this place is worth no less than a Tier-Three upper-grade dwelling. No need for Elder to worry on my behalf."

The Angler was startled. "An art for raising spirit vein grades? I thought only upper-tier Nascent Soul sects held those secrets."

This time, Luo Chen smiled without answering.

The Angler had little room to press. After all, he had already taken the gift.

Still, he added one last word of caution.

"You're not the only one with eyes on this place — others have only been deterred by the cost. If you're truly going to rent it, I'd recommend paying five full years upfront. That way no one can use a renewal deadline against you."

Luo Chen frowned. "Not even with Elder's backing?"

The Angler sighed. "In the Myriad Immortals Assembly, I am not the only eight-star demon hunter."

The implication was clear: whoever had also set their sights on Peng Lake had an eight-star demon hunter backing them as well.

Under the night sky, Luo Chen's expression shifted through several shades.

In the end, staring at the thin mist drifting across the surface of the water, he made up his mind.

"Five years' rent is nothing. I happen to have a small fortune to my name."

The Angler nodded inwardly.

An ordinary wandering cultivator would never command resources like that.

As expected — this Azure Yang was indeed the Devil Lord Azure Yang of rumor.

Only someone who had slain a number of same-level cultivators — including regional lords among them — and seized vast amounts of spoils could amass wealth like this.

And only someone like that would stand unbothered before an enemy backed by an eight-star demon hunter.

After a moment's thought, he added: "Don't worry. If anyone tries to contest it, they'll have to come themselves. Their backer — I'll deal with that side."

After all, he had taken the gift.

This was an unexpected boon.

Luo Chen clasped his hands in gratitude. "Then I am much obliged, Fellow Daoist."

...

Three days later.

Within the Fulong Mountain Range, above Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake.

Three figures arrived on drifting light.

Wen Xiu — her figure noticeably rounder than before — stared out at the lake stretching to the horizon without end, and could not shake her shock even after her feet touched the ground.

Only when a stream of instructions reached her ears did she slowly come back to herself.

"Heavenjade — have you memorized everything on the list? Not one item missed."

"The million spirit stones I've given you — don't be sparing with them. Buy finished formation flags wherever you can."

"If the shops in the city can't supply the volume, use my demon hunter medallion and place a bulk order through the Demon Hunter Office."

"I intend to aggregate the spirit veins here and raise their grade to Tier-Three upper rank — a full-speed cultivation ground."

Wen Xiu watched Heavenjade receive her orders with perfect deference, and couldn't help swallowing.

Senior Immortal Azure Yang was truly bold.

When he acted, he acted grandly — renting a spirit land of this size as a cultivation base without a second thought.

In all her years alongside her husband and among Golden Core cultivators, she had never encountered one with this kind of bearing.

Luo Chen's footsteps fell firm on the solid earth of Peng Island, deep in thought.

The listed rent for Peng Lake was three million per year. After the four-star demon hunter discount, it came to one million eight hundred thousand.

Five years: nine hundred thousand — which would swallow most of his eighteen-million liquid funds.

And this was only the first five years.

Five years was also a number with practical significance. Golden Core demon hunters were permitted to take on new assignments roughly once every three to five years. Setting the lease at five years ensured that if a Golden Core demon hunter died on an assignment, the financial loss wouldn't be excessive.

Meanwhile, the sky-high base rent and modest discounts pushed lower-ranked demon hunters to actively take on dangerous assignments to raise their star level.

*These Nascent Soul true beings are a savvy lot — every angle accounted for.*

With that quiet observation, Luo Chen stepped to the lakeshore.

He opened his mouth and exhaled. A small gray cauldron spun lazily out, then caught the wind and swelled to a hundred zhang in size.

The enormous object drew Wen Xiu's gaze immediately.

In the next moment, an earth-shaking dragon's roar rang out.

A black flood dragon — sixty zhang in length, with a girth as wide as a mountain ridge — burst through the air, stretching its massive body with every sign of pleasure.

"Finally free!"

Black King let out a great roar, then, with a wag of its tail, lowered its head before the man who had recalled the cauldron.

"Black King greets Master."

Luo Chen gave a calm nod. "Go. From now on, Peng Lake is your home. No outsiders are permitted to enter without my leave."

Black King turned and beheld the vast, mist-veiled lake — eyes alight with excitement.

"Understood!"

With that, it dove headlong into the water with an enormous splash.

Wen Xiu stared, utterly at a loss.

Beyond Heavenjade — the striking Golden Core female cultivator who served Senior Immortal Azure Yang — the Senior Immortal apparently also had a Tier-Three mid-stage demon king for a spiritual beast companion.

While her mind was still reeling, a calm voice reached her.

"Wen Xiu — I heard from young Zhang that you intend to found a family and build a lasting lineage. Is that right?"

Wen Xiu nodded, somewhat dazed.

"Starting a cultivation family is no easy thing — especially without a suitable spirit land."

Luo Chen clasped his hands behind his back, expression mild.

"We have a connection, you and I. Why not find a spot here on Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake — a Tier-Two spirit land — and establish the Zhang family."

The woman was at a loss for words.

But clearly, Luo Chen's tone carried not only an inquiry but a command.

"Heavenjade — while there's time, take Wen Xiu and show her around."

Heavenjade received the order and led the bewildered woman on a tour around Peng Lake.

Once they were both gone, Luo Chen let out a slow breath of relief and drew a black piece of wood from his person.

"Senior — the matter of aggregating the spirit veins and raising their grade. I must trouble you."

Han Zhan's resigned voice came from within the soul-nourishing wood. "You, boy — knowing full well that I've been busy cultivating secret arts and working on this soul-nourishing wood, you still have the nerve to bother me."

Luo Chen said pleasantly, "There's nothing to be done. Of all those I know, Senior is the only one with the means for this. I've already had Heavenjade fetch the necessary formation instruments. From here, it's all in Senior's hands."

Ordinary sects had no such methods.

But Han Zhan was different.

The Falling Cloud Sect he had once belonged to had successfully obtained authorization to open a conquest campaign, earning the qualifications for an upper-tier Nascent Soul sect. Many secret arts unknown to wandering cultivators had been passed down by the Mingyuan Sect — including techniques for crafting regulation spirit stones, raising spirit vein grades, aggregating spirit veins, and establishing large-scale immortal cities.

The vast Shattered Moon Immortal City within the Wailing Moon Mountain Range was a living testament to what such methods could build.

These methods were not Han Zhan's to pass on — but performing the work himself was another matter.

Luo Chen had made full use of that.

Against Luo Chen's shameless request — which Han Zhan had already agreed to beforehand — the elder said nothing further, though he had already extracted the Sealing Divinity Compass from Luo Chen as the formation's cornerstone before beginning.

With that treasure in hand, Han Zhan guided the soul-nourishing wood off the island and began surveying the vein lines of the landscape.

Once he was gone, Luo Chen allowed himself a smile of relief.

Strolling with leisurely ease across the solid ground, his gaze sweeping the surroundings, he murmured to himself.

"Years of rushing about — cultivation neglected — my realm crawling along at a tortoise's pace, still stuck at Golden Core Stage Four."

"At last. A temporary resting place."

"From here on — time to accumulate, and then to bloom."

With those words, Luo Chen walked deeper into the island.

Beneath the light mist rising off the water, the figure of the red-robed Daoist grew hazier and hazier, until it vanished entirely.

*(End of Chapter)*
