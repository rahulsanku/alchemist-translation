Chapter 905: White Robe Ascends the Heavens, Red Robe Faces the Abyss (Thanks to the Flagship Alliance Leader for the Million-Point Tip!)

The moment Luo Chen used the Heaven-Refining Art to draw a strand of spiritual mechanism into the token, a change erupted instantly!

In that first instant, Luo Chen knew he had grasped the key to this realm.

The entire miniature topography of the "Pill Saint Realm" hidden within the Pill Saint Hall flooded into his mind. It wasn't particularly vast—only about half the size of the Jade Cauldron Domain.

No wonder Xuan Yan could find him the moment he entered.

Within it were planted many rare flowers and exotic herbs seldom seen in the outside world.

Take the Hong Spirit Grass Luo Chen used to refine the Hongyuan Pill—several acres of it were planted here. This was an unexpected delight for Luo Chen.

Why had he previously set aside spirit-land caves and medicinal pills and liquids when calculating his cultivation speed?

Wasn't it precisely because he had no leads on a spirit-land cave, and there was a shortage of Hongyuan Pills as well!

The batch of Hong Spirit Grass he had acquired from the Northern Sea back in the day had long been nearly exhausted after years of heavy consumption.

Even though Sang Jinghe was cultivating Hong Spirit Grass for him, the time was too short, making it like trying to douse a cartload of burning firewood with a single cup of water.

In truth, it wasn't just the Hong Spirit Grass needed for Hongyuan Pills—even the soul essence required for Great Soul Pills was becoming scarce.

Achieving his current fifth-layer Sage realm wasn't solely due to Luo Chen's diligent cultivation and fortunate encounters; it was more the accumulation of vast quantities of resources!

Of course!

At this moment, compared to the alchemy insights left behind by Chu Yan, these exotic flowers and herbs—even the entire Pill Saint Realm—were utterly insignificant.

What truly mattered were only those insights!

"So these are the true meanings of the laws related to alchemy?"

Luo Chen murmured, gazing at those illusions.

To the onlooker Xuan Yan, they were merely illusions.

But to Luo Chen himself, the one experiencing it, it felt as if he were there in person.

Through the token, Luo Chen could seemingly step into Chu Yan's perspective at any time, accompanying her through the mental journey of each pill refinement, experiencing every subtle change.

Even the moment Chu Yan comprehended the true meaning of the laws had been preserved.

It was precisely the scene Xuan Yan had witnessed—a small world being refined by Chu Yan!

And it was during that very refinement that Chu Yan had grasped the "Heaven-Refining Art," this alchemy technique, learning the method of capturing and guiding spiritual mechanisms that ordinary cultivators could scarcely imagine!

The Dao of Heaven is divided into three thousand.

So-called "three thousand" is merely a general term.

This means that any existence has the opportunity to comprehend a Dao unique to itself.

It is not limited to the most basic Five Elements, nor the renowned Wind, Thunder, Yin-Yang, Space, Time, and so forth.

Even plants and spirits, qin-chess-calligraphy-painting, demons and monsters, even alchemy-formations-artifacts-talismans, puppets and beast-taming—any skill, when taken to its highest level, has the chance to touch the essence of the Dao.

This is the principle behind "skill can approach the Dao."

Of course, the essence of the Great Dao is absolutely not something the weak can fully grasp.

But by comprehending just a little, one's art can reach the divine. By thereby awakening the true meaning of the laws and stepping into the Ascendant realm, it becomes a matter of natural progression.

At this moment!

Within the insights left by Chu Yan, Luo Chen's alchemy, already at the Grandmaster level, began to integrate and penetrate from the inside out, from the small to the great.

And through Chu Yan's insights, Luo Chen was still climbing upward!

As long as he was willing to continue accepting these insights, he would inevitably comprehend the true meaning of the Alchemy Dao, beyond the Withering-Bloom true meaning.

He would inevitably become a rare—perhaps even the only—dual-true-meaning Sage in the Mountains and Seas Realm!

A dual-true-meaning Sage might not sound impressive, but one must understand that ordinary Sage cultivators already find it extremely difficult to comprehend the true meaning of the laws in advance.

They often have to elevate their Sage to the Primordial Spirit level, entering the Ascendant realm, before they can resist the assimilation of heaven and earth relatively well and comprehend the true meaning of the laws.

To comprehend the true meaning of the laws before reaching Ascendant is already a genius rarely seen in the world.

Let alone dual true meanings!

As long as Luo Chen was willing, he could take this step!

But just as Luo Chen was about to step into the scene of Chu Yan refining that small world, he stopped!

Amidst the hazy chaos, Luo Chen's thoughts grew increasingly clear.

"These are Chu Yan's alchemy insights, not my own."

"Although the Withering-Bloom true meaning was also obtained opportunistically when I comprehended it during the advancement of the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame, it still belongs to my own Great Dao—merely having gone astray."

"What would it mean to steal another's insights now?"

"With my current alchemy foundation, comprehending an alchemy true meaning uniquely my own in the future will be a natural progression. Why borrow another's hand!"

With a shift of his mind, the three souls in Luo Chen's sea of consciousness simultaneously activated the Aperture-Thatching Art.

"Open!"

As his divine soul trembled, Luo Chen had already broken free from the illusions left by Chu Yan.

At that moment, the vast spiritual mechanisms that had been attracted also began to slowly dissipate.

"What a pity."

Luo Chen sighed regretfully, but he wasn't particularly disappointed.

After experiencing Rosycloud's casual guidance, he had come to a profound realization: shortcuts may be good, but they are difficult to lead to the destination.

A Sage must have the heart to choose and let go!

Fortunately, the control over this Pill Saint Realm was still in his hands, so this trip was not without gain.

Opening his eyes, Luo Chen met Xuan Yan's dim gaze.

The other man opened his mouth, then silently lowered his head.

To have persisted until now in a state of returning radiance was already extremely difficult.

Having seen the relics left by his old friend, he could depart without regret.

The stream babbled, its crisp, clear sound flowing into the distance.

Accompanied only by the young man's words:

"Sir, may your journey be peaceful!"

...

The rain at the Nether Origin Sect lasted a full nine days.

Whether it was because this place was naturally prone to rain, or because the clouds and smoke that had shrouded the Pill Saint Hall for centuries had dispersed, no one could say for certain.

But as cultivators, everyone was willing to believe it was the latter.

They also believed that when all the clouds and smoke had dissipated, they would see the new Pill Saint Hall Master!

And so it was!

Under the watchful gaze of Lingtian City Lord, the tightly closed main gate of the Pill Saint Hall was slowly pulled open.

A tall figure calmly walked out.

"I've kept you all waiting," the white-robed Daoist said with a smile.

"It's Luo Chen!"

"He actually passed the Pill Saint Hall trial!"

"And Xuan Yan?"

"What exactly is the Pill Saint inheritance?"

At some point, the square outside the Pill Saint Hall had already gathered countless cultivators.

Those who could stand here were at least Adept cultivators.

Among them were no fewer than a dozen Sage experts, all holding high positions within the Nether Origin Sect.

Amidst the clamor of discussion, a vast voice came from the nine heavens above.

"Hereby decreed: Luo Chen, born of the wilds, of exceptional talent, having passed the trial with the ability of an Alchemy Saint."

"From this day forward, he shall serve as the Pill Saint Hall Master. Bestowed: one Fortune Cauldron!"

"Additionally, granted: the Level Mountain Crown, the Snowfall Frost Robe, the Nine Radiance Jade Belt, and the Cloud-Treading Boots."

"Order Luo Chen to proceed immediately to Mount Yanfu to pay respects to the three Ancestors!"

The vast voice was still echoing.

A figure had already appeared above the Pill Saint Hall.

Luo Chen glanced at Lingtian City Lord.

Ye Lingtian quickly transmitted a message to introduce him: "This is Junior Brother Tan Sheng, one of Imperial Nether Ancestor's personal disciples."

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow. He had only recently arrived at the Nether Origin Sect and was completely unfamiliar with the people and affairs here.

Let alone the internal factional differences.

For instance, the three who had come to invite him from Motian Cliff—he had initially judged them to be representatives of the three Ancestors, but later discovered that wasn't quite right either.

Take Lei Daozi, for example—he wasn't actually a disciple of Imperial Nether Ancestor.

He inherited the legacy of the fallen great power Bai Ye of the Nether Origin Sect. Strictly speaking, he was an independent faction, merely leaning toward Imperial Nether Ancestor's lineage.

Composing himself, Luo Chen cupped his hands in greeting: "Thank you for the Ancestor's generous gifts!"

Tan Sheng waved his hand, and disciples behind him stepped forward carrying trays.

On each tray was an item.

Spiritual light flowed, making them appear extraordinary at a glance.

And on that head crown, Luo Chen vaguely felt a sense of familiarity.

"Change into this set first, then go see the Ancestor!" Tan Sheng glanced at Luo Chen, frowning slightly. "Don't lose your decorum."

Luo Chen smiled freely, unconcerned by the other's tone.

After all, the man was here to deliver gifts!

After the five-year trial, his appearance was indeed somewhat unkempt.

Carefree was fine, but meeting seniors in such a state was improper.

"Please wait a moment!"

Ye Lingtian, standing nearby, transmitted a message to a young woman who had hurried over.

Then he said to Luo Chen, "Let this young lady of the Ye family attend to the Pill Saint Hall Master's grooming!"

Luo Chen nodded, then beckoned to the youth beside the young woman.

"Come in with me!"

Among the crowd of cultivators, the youth seemed quite out of place.

There was no fluctuation of spiritual power on his body—anyone could see he was an ordinary mortal.

But at this moment, aside from Ye Ruoli, who was to attend to Luo Chen's grooming, he was the only one allowed to enter the Pill Saint Hall.

As the three figures disappeared, the cultivators outside continued their discussions.

"Is that youth Luo Chen's son?"

"Still calling him Luo Chen? From now on, address him as Pill Saint Hall Master!"

"He is indeed the Pill Saint Hall Master's son, but I wonder why Hall Master Luo, whose alchemy skill is nearly transcendent and saintly, has a descendant who cannot cultivate."

"That shouldn't be!"

"Did you see that small cauldron? That was the Fortune Cauldron the Pill Saint used for a long time before forming his Sage!"

"The Fortune Cauldron... The Ancestor bestowed this item on Hall Master Luo. It seems their intentions remain unchanged."

"I heard that to prevent a repeat of the Pill Saint Hall's closure, the hall will henceforth have one principal and two deputy hall masters. Now that the principal hall master is set in stone, who do you think the remaining two deputy hall masters will be?"

"Needless to say, they'll be chosen from among the four Alchemy Grandmasters like Chu Yu and Lu Chun!"

"Heh, there's nuance to that. First, does Chu Yu even qualify to be a deputy hall master? And two of the four are outsiders..."

"Who said you must be proficient in alchemy to be a deputy hall master?"

At that sudden rhetorical question, the discussion fell into silence.

Then it erupted into even louder debate!

Meanwhile, inside the Pill Saint Hall.

Luo Chen stood before a large mirror, letting Ye Ruoli help him into the Snowfall Frost Robe and fasten the Nine Radiance Jade Belt.

With the comfortable Cloud-Treading Boots on his feet, a faint feminine fragrance drifting past his nose, Luo Chen's attention was on the youth beside him.

"You've grown taller."

Five years—a mere snap of the fingers for a cultivator.

But for Luo Lingxi, who could not cultivate, it had passed so slowly.

Looking at Luo Chen, whose appearance hadn't changed at all from five years ago, his voice choked with emotion.

"Father."

Luo Chen could see the admiration and worship in the youth's eyes.

He beckoned, and the youth obediently came over.

Luo Chen ruffled his hair. "Have you been diligently practicing the guiding technique I taught you these past few years?"

Luo Lingxi nodded emphatically. "Never slacked off!"

Luo Chen smiled. He could naturally see the other's condition.

Though he still looked somewhat thin, he was indeed much better than before.

The youth seemed to want to say something more, but Luo Chen had already sat down in a chair.

Ye Ruoli glanced at the youth, then asked hesitantly, "Hall Master, what hairstyle would you like?"

Luo Chen casually waved his hand, and the spiritual power in the air coalesced into an image of his previous appearance.

"Follow this hairstyle, with some slight adjustments to match the crown."

Ye Ruoli carefully studied the spiritual power portrait, then nodded, picking up a comb to arrange Luo Chen's hair.

Luo Lingxi could see clearly—that hairstyle was exactly the one his mother, Sima Huiniang, often used for his father.

While sitting still, Luo Chen didn't idle. He picked up the crown.

"The Level Mountain Crown?"

"Just as I thought!"

With a brief inspection, Luo Chen understood why it felt familiar.

This crown was clearly the one worn by the ancient Demon Emperor Di Tian, merely with the jade bead curtain removed.

It lacked imperial majesty but retained dignity, still bearing the air of a gentleman.

"The three Ancestors are quite generous!"

Luo Chen murmured. With his knowledge of artifact refinement, he could tell at a glance that this crown was extraordinary.

It was clearly a powerful defensive true artifact!

When he infused his mana into it, a protective barrier immediately formed, capable of enveloping his entire person.

It could fully defend against physical attacks and mana impacts.

The defensive strength was not yet known, but a treasure worn by the ancient Demon Emperor Di Tian could not possibly be weak.

It was just missing the jade bead curtain!

In Luo Chen's judgment, if those jade beads were still there, they should also be usable against divine soul attacks.

"But it's enough. I have the Town Prison Soul Art, so I don't lack those minor divine soul defense methods."

When his hair was done, Luo Chen solemnly placed the Level Mountain Crown on his head, inserting a jade hairpin crosswise.

He rose and walked out.

"Wait here for your father to return!"

"As for your cultivation matter, I already have an idea. Don't worry."

Luo Lingxi, who had been feeling downcast, suddenly brightened up.

...

When Luo Chen appeared at the gate of the Pill Saint Hall, the square, which had been buzzing with discussion, suddenly fell silent.

Countless gazes shot over simultaneously.

His feet trod the pure Cloud-Treading Boots, his waist encircled by the Nine Radiance Jade Belt, the Snowfall Frost Robe fitting perfectly and making him appear ethereal and otherworldly.

Especially that crown, which made the youthful-faced Luo Chen look extraordinarily handsome and distinguished.

He seemed like a banished immortal!

Ye Lingtian praised, "This is our Nether Origin Sect's new Pill Saint Hall Master!"

Instantly, a chorus of responses arose.

"Greetings, Pill Saint Hall Master!"

Among the hundreds present, aside from Ye Lingtian, Luo Chen knew none of them.

Even Lu Chun, Chen Mozi, and the others were merely nodding acquaintances, even former competitors.

But carried by the atmosphere, Luo Chen appeared delighted.

"When I return, I shall host a feast, and we shall all celebrate together!"

With that, he stepped onto a cloud and drifted up into the nine heavens.

That Mount Yanfu was up above!

...

Within Shangyou City, traffic flowed like a river, people coming and going.

In the crowded throng, a flash of red passed by, leaving the crowd and arriving alone outside the city.

A bottomless abyss, like a slumbering giant beast, stood silently before her.

The red-robed figure walked along the abyss, seeming somewhat troubled.

Suddenly!

A slender, well-defined jade hand gently pointed into the abyss. Instantly, the giant beast opened its mouth, and the abyss parted.

A path leading to the Eastern Wilderness's Ascendant sacred ground, the Nether Origin Sect, opened!

Flagship Crazy Store Daoist is truly too crazy!

Another Silver Alliance Leader tip, too precious!

Thank you, thank you!

Happy New Year!
