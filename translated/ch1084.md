# Chapter 1084: The Great Battle Ends, Farewell

They had not shown themselves before the battle to declare their stance.

Nor had they intervened during the fight to aid either side.

Yet they chose to appear together after the great battle, facing Luo Chen, whose qi and strength were exhausted, and Azure Frost, who had expended a vast amount of mana.

Such an attitude was no different from sitting on the mountain watching the tigers fight, then reaping the fisherman's profit!

Great Sage Beili and Sea Emperor Whale exchanged a glance, seemingly not expecting that Luo Chen, under such circumstances, would still choose to lay the matter bare directly. Was he not afraid of provoking a new round of battle?

Or did he have some other trump card yet to be used?

The scene during the battle just now, though they were far away, had been fully witnessed by them.

Every technique Luo Chen had displayed was immensely powerful, enough to threaten Ascendant-level great powers.

Whether it was the Dao-finger divine technique or the fusion of three flames, especially that final sword strike—even though they had not faced its edge directly, a chill had risen in their hearts.

That was a sword strike capable of slaying an Ascendant great power!

Could he still unleash that sword strike now?

If he truly could, which of the two of them would have to withstand it?

And Azure Frost...

Standing beside Luo Chen, Azure Frost held her umbrella, eyes slightly narrowed, her form becoming illusory—as if she were in this world, yet also not in this world.

Between the illusion and reality of her form, the pressure she exuded grew ever heavier.

She had been the number one under the Ascendants before Luo Chen, having grasped the most terrifying spatial laws at the Sage stage. Now that she had quietly broken through to the Ascendant stage, she was likely even more formidable.

This was evident from Azure Frost's resistance against the Zhan Realm True Lord across worlds.

Clearly, Azure Frost was now standing on Luo Chen's side.

Sea Emperor Whale's expression turned fierce as he took a step forward.

With just that one step, the Luo Chen opposite him raised the corners of his mouth.

"It seems Fellow Daoist Sea Emperor Whale has made a choice."

As he spoke, the Scarlet Firmament sword qi slowly spread across the ancient sword. Not only that, but strands upon strands of golden flame also emerged on his body.

When he saw that golden flame, Sea Emperor Whale's expression shifted slightly.

"Nirvana Sacred Flame, Rosycloud?"

Not only that, but he also felt a sensation of needles pricking his back—an intangible threat from Azure Frost, who was about to vanish into the void, a bone-piercing, heart-stabbing icy killing intent.

"Beili, what say you?" Sea Emperor Whale asked without turning his head.

Before Great Sage Beili could answer, a light laugh sounded from afar.

"Two fellow daoists, do not let momentary greed blind your eyes. These two are from Green Parasol Mountain in the Eastern Wilderness!"

As the words fell, Li Canghai had already arrived nearby.

He first cupped his hands toward Luo Chen, speaking with full apology: "My apologies for leaving you to face the enemy alone earlier."

Luo Chen showed an unconcerned expression. "No matter. Before you left, Senior did ultimately advise me. How could I hold a grudge?"

Li Canghai waved his hand. "No need to be so distant. Let us address each other as peers."

Luo Chen chuckled and nodded, addressing him as: "Fellow Daoist Li."

Hearing this form of address, Li Canghai finally breathed a sigh of relief.

He turned around and looked at the two demon clan great powers, whose expressions were shifting between uncertainty.

The name of Green Parasol Mountain in the Eastern Wilderness made them quite wary.

This was something they had not considered before.

But Azure Frost's appearance, along with the Nirvana Sacred Flame Luo Chen had displayed, all indicated that Green Parasol Mountain's shadow loomed behind this battle.

And now, Li Canghai's attitude was very important.

Sea Emperor Whale said in a muffled voice: "Li Canghai, you reached a consensus with us. What are you trying to do now?"

Li Canghai said helplessly: "But I also have a cooperative relationship with Fellow Daoist Luo Chen. Before this battle is completely over, it stands to reason that I should fight alongside him. This is truly frustrating!"

Faced with this explanation, anger appeared on Sea Emperor Whale's face.

In his judgment, Luo Chen was surely exhausted of qi and strength, unable to fight further. Azure Frost was likely not much better—that slash from White Tiger True Lord, though across two worlds, could not have been easy to bear.

Two against two, they held a huge advantage.

But if it became three against two...

Li Canghai was in perfect condition, and this man was especially cunning. If he truly intended to help Luo Chen, the situation would become chaotic.

Behind him, Great Sage Beili gave a light cough.

"Whale Emperor, let it go!"

"But..." Sea Emperor Whale's face was full of reluctance, his gaze shifting uncertainly between the long sword in Luo Chen's hand and Azure Frost's bone umbrella.

Li Canghai seized the opportunity to say: "Fellow Daoist Beili speaks truly. Today has already been very bloody. Why must we stir up more conflict? Moreover, now that the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect has lost its leader and has been severely wounded by Fellow Daoist Luo Chen's people, it is like a weak lamb. That was once the number one sacred land under heaven. Are the two of you not tempted?"

As he mediated, he also shook his hand, producing a great seal.

"The Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range has been taken by me. The rest I leave to the two fellow daoists."

At these words, both Great Sage Beili and Sea Emperor Whale's hearts were moved.

Rather than taking the risk of offending Green Parasol Mountain here and fighting a great battle with Luo Chen and Azure Frost, it would be easier to go and pluck Luo Chen's victory fruits, seizing the rich spoils of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

The two exchanged a glance and reached an understanding.

"We have offended you today. Farewell!"

Without hesitation, the two departed at the same time.

One headed toward the Northern Continent, the other toward the Southern Continent.

After they left, Li Canghai finally breathed a sigh of relief.

He turned around and looked at Luo Chen again.

"Apologies. Those things should have belonged to you. I overstepped by making decisions on your behalf."

Luo Chen gave a carefree smile: "They are all merely external possessions. Besides, I sought only one thing on this journey. Beyond that, I seek nothing."

Such magnanimity?

Li Canghai subconsciously glanced at the blood sword in the other's hand and the golden flame that had never been withdrawn from head to toe, and couldn't help pursing his lips.

"The great battle is over. I imagine you still have matters to settle. I won't disturb you further."

"The camaraderie of fighting side by side today, I, Luo, will remember. If I am fortunate enough to achieve Ascension, I will personally visit you."

"I wish you smooth Ascension and a flourishing immortal path!"

Li Canghai cupped his hands, then vanished from Luo Chen's sight.

After he left, Luo Chen still stood still. Azure Frost reappeared, her gaze falling on the golden flame that was still being maintained.

"Camaraderie of fighting side by side? I'm curious what state of mind you used to say such words."

Divine sense spread everywhere. When he confirmed that Li Canghai had completely left, Luo Chen slowly withdrew the Nirvana Sacred Flame. With a flick of his hand, the Yuantu Sword also disappeared.

"Cough, cough..."

He clutched his chest and coughed dully.

"What state of mind? The other party apologized. In my current state, can I pursue Li Canghai for fleeing the battle?"

"And later?"

"Considering that he stepped in afterward to mediate between the two great ancient demons of the Northern Sea, I won't pursue it too harshly."

"Too harshly? Doesn't that mean you still want an accounting?"

"An accounting? Yes, I want one, but not from Li Canghai—from those two fellows in the Northern Sea."

"I knew it. With your temperament, how could you tolerate others snatching food from the tiger's mouth in front of you."

Perhaps because her mind was relaxed after the battle, Azure Frost's words were a few more than usual, but only a few.

Her gaze looked into the distance, where streaks of light were flying toward this direction. At the tail of the group, there seemed to be lingering aftershocks of battle.

"I will take the demon cultivators from Truth Crossing Hall away later. As for you..." Azure Frost exhaled a turbid breath, looking at Luo Chen with a somewhat complicated expression, and handed over the bone umbrella.

Luo Chen subconsciously took the bone umbrella, not understanding why the other party showed such an expression.

He said freely: "Fellow Daoist, don't worry. After I achieve the Ascendant stage, I will personally undo the Life-and-Death Gu between us. We will meet again then."

Azure Frost shook her head faintly.

"It's better if we never see each other again!"

What did this mean?

While Luo Chen was puzzled, Azure Frost had already turned and left.

Facing the returning streaks of light, amidst a series of exclamations, Azure Frost waved her large sleeves repeatedly, taking in one demon clan expert after another.

Luo Chen held the bone umbrella, watching this scene with some envy.

This "Sleeve Universe" technique was truly elegant!

Especially after Azure Frost had advanced to the Ascendant stage, using it made her appear even more ethereal and immortal. If only he could learn it.

"Actually, it shouldn't be too difficult. With my current attainments in spatial restrictions, combined with the spatial talent I've shared from Azure Frost, and if I match it with suitable robes, perhaps I could create a similar technique?"

Luo Chen had a sudden inspiration, vaguely feeling an insight.

Just as he was thinking this, the Eastern Wilderness cultivators had already withdrawn from the Dao Sect continent and, led by Heavenjade, arrived where Luo Chen was.

"Master, Heavenjade has not disgraced your trust!"

Upon seeing Luo Chen, Heavenjade excitedly stepped forward to pay respects.

Luo Chen nodded, his gaze falling on the group behind her.

His first attention was on the Luo Tian Sect cultivators.

A total of six Luo Tian Sect cultivators had participated in this battle, plus the two wild beasts, Black King and Xuanyan Turtle. Heavenjade had been a scout, while Emperor Slaughter had been assigned to the Southern Continent side to block reinforcements.

Looking now, the lineup was intact.

Wang Yuan was drenched in blood, but his spirit was full.

Canglong's Dharma robe was as white as snow, as if he had suffered no damage at all.

Lingfengzi, Haoranzi, and Jadewater Dragon's auras were somewhat wilted. Especially Jadewater Dragon, whose injuries were completely uncontrollable. She was only kept from falling by Canglong's support.

"As long as no one fell, that's good." Luo Chen said with relief.

Canglong smoothed her hair and said with emotion: "Thanks to Senior Brother's intelligence, the number of Sage-stage cultivators on the Eastern Origin Continent was less than we imagined. Although each was very powerful, in the case of many fighting few, the fallen made up only a very small proportion of those who went to battle, and most were on the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range battlefield."

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow and looked at Little White.

She had also gone to that place.

Little White explained in a low voice: "The Dao Sect's foundation is unfathomably deep. Even with the Kui Ox Ghost God's detonation at the forefront, they still activated great power techniques to forcibly kill it. Then, facing the siege of demon clan experts and the two top sects of the Eastern Wilderness, they still fought to the death, causing many casualties. If not for Great Power Li Canghai forcibly taking the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range and using his divine ability to throw everyone out, even more would have died."

"It seems I still underestimated the Dao Sect's foundation." Luo Chen murmured, his gaze passing over the crowd to look into the distance.

Streaks of blood-soaked escape lights were gradually withdrawing from the Eastern Origin Continent. Even with the tsunami raging, Dao Sect experts were still pursuing and killing from behind.

Those Dao Sect experts were not only from the Eastern Origin Continent but also from the other two continents.

Clearly, relying solely on Emperor Slaughter and Deng Taiyue's two locations could not achieve perfect interception.

Luo Chen understood this and flicked his finger toward the distance.

"Go!"

The Yuantu Sword, as if receiving a command, transformed into a massive sword light and descended with a boom.

This single sword strike just managed to separate the pursuers from the retreating side.

Zhan Bingtao and the other Dao Sect experts in charge of pursuit instinctively stopped, staring in shock at that blood sword.

A figure slowly descended from the sky, the tip of his foot landing on the sword hilt, looking coldly at the crowd.

"You all, stop here!"

"Die, villain!"

Someone charged up in anger, only to be met by a beam of Scarlet Firmament sword qi.

Pfft!

The sword qi passed through the body, piercing through the other's Purple Mansion Sage Core in a single strike.

Overwhelming, utterly overwhelming.

Now, no one dared to continue the pursuit. Especially the familiar aura emanating from that blood sword.

It was the aura of Ancestor Liancheng!

Could it be...

A great terror spread uncontrollably in everyone's hearts.

Luo Chen calmly looked down at the Dao Sect cultivators. "Our sect has no deep hatred with you. This battle was forced upon us. If you take one more step forward, do not blame me for opening a great slaughter."

His gaze swept over the crowd, pausing for a moment on a woman whose eyes were full of resentment, then passing over.

"Farewell!"

After he left, the crowd remained oppressive.

Both because they were shocked by Luo Chen's single sword strike and because of the possibility that the Ancestor had fallen.

That was Ancestor Liancheng, who had survived the Southern Frontier great battle and the battle at Soul-Breaking Cliff to besiege the Demon Phoenix!

Could he have died at the hands of that man just now?

This was too unbelievable!

But with the massive tsunami that had submerged most of the Eastern Origin, and the Ascendant great power's unscrupulous act of taking the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range, everyone had to consider that possibility.

After a long time, there was commotion in the crowd.

Until Zhan Bingtao gave an order.

"First, go to Heaven-Earth Island!"

Without any further hesitation, the Dao Sect cultivators, not even bothering to clean up the Eastern Origin Continent battlefield, headed straight for Heaven-Earth Island.

Yet what they saw and heard only plunged them deeper into the abyss.

The massive Heaven-Earth Island had been cut in two by a single sword strike.

Heaven-Earth Peak stood alone on the other side.

At the entrance to the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, a few figures sat cross-legged, their auras miserable.

"Junior Sister Mingyan!"

Zhan Bingtao shouted.

Mingyan raised her head, revealing a face of despair.

"Senior Brother, we... have failed."

Even though Zhan Bingtao had anticipated it, he still found it hard to believe: "With Senior Brother Ke Lianshan inside, Ancestor Liancheng outside, plus two spirit treasures and three great formations supporting us, how could we have failed?"

Mingyan clenched her fists in pain, her sharp nails digging deep into her flesh.

"It was Zhou Fuyou, no, it was Luo Chen..."

Among the crowd, Fu Qinglan, her blue clothes stained with blood, listened to Mingyan's accusations one by one, her body trembling uncontrollably.

The expression on her face kept changing—pain, despair, guilt, self-blame... until endless resentment filled her heart.

"Luo Chen!!!"

...

On the azure sea.

A giant turtle floated, swimming slowly.

Luo Chen stood on the turtle's back, letting the salty sea breeze blow his hair and rustle his robes.

His aura was weak, far less than one or two percent of his peak, but his mood was more relaxed than ever before.

Behind him, dozens of gazes occasionally turned to him. Even though everyone could see how poor Luo Chen's condition was, the deep fear and reverence in each person's eyes could not be faked.

At the Sage stage, to reverse-slaughter an Ascendant great power!

This unprecedented feat, he had accomplished it!

How could this not inspire awe and worship?

Canglong moved lightly to his side and asked with concern: "Senior Brother, are you alright?"

Faced with this question, Luo Chen let out a light laugh.

"Heh..."

The laugh was very soft, but it came from the heart, then grew from small to large, gradually filling the universe.

In the vast sea, only the daoist's unrestrained laughter resounded through heaven and earth.

(Chapter End)
