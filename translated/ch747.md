# Chapter 747: Heaven-Earth Peak Opens, All Cultivators Gather; The Alchemy Saint Ascends, Mountains and Seas Offer Congratulations

Two streaks of escaping light slowly flew out of the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range along the bank of the Passing River.

Because their speed was not fast, they repeatedly encountered low-level Dao Sect disciples along the way.

When those disciples recognized Fu Qinglan, they often bowed and greeted her. Fu Qinglan responded with the same aloof appearance she usually maintained.

But the flicker in her eyes exposed the turmoil in her heart.

Finally, when they were about to reach Heaven-Earth Peak, she could not hold back.

"Elder Qi lost to you in a single exchange?"

"Mm."

Luo Chen answered casually, his expression incomparably calm.

Facing this indifferent reaction, as though he had merely crushed an ant in passing, Fu Qinglan's emotions grew even more stirred.

That was Qi Xiongcheng!

A loyal servant carefully cultivated by the Shi Great House!

Originally, he had been trained as a guardian warrior, so from childhood he cultivated body-refining arts.

Later, after reaching the Furnace Realm, the third realm of body cultivation, he had been sent to the West Desert, where he handled secular affairs for the Shi family while studying Buddhist body-refining methods.

Yes, in the present Mountains and Seas Realm, body cultivation had long declined. Only the West Desert Buddhist schools still persisted in this aspect of cultivation.

But no one expected that when Qi Xiongcheng returned from "advanced study" in the West Desert, he would reveal Sage cultivation!

Dual cultivation of magic and body was something no one had expected.

Precisely because of this, Qi Xiongcheng's reputation suddenly grew.

Many people believed he would rely on his Sage realm to leave the Shi family and establish his own household.

It could be said that Qi Xiongcheng was very strong. His body-refining attainments had earned him the nickname Old Ghost Qi in the resource-poor West Desert, and dual cultivation of magic and body made his name resound.

If one added the rich combat experience he had gained throughout his journey, his strength was something even untempered Dao Sect prodigies could not look down on.

Yet such an existence had not lasted even one move under Luo Chen!

Fu Qinglan had seen very clearly that when Qi Xiongcheng failed with his first move, a huge opening appeared. If Luo Chen had wished it, he could have taken his life in an instant.

At the very least, Qi Xiongcheng would have been badly injured!

"Is this why Grandfather has such confidence in him?"

Fu Qinglan vaguely sensed that Luo Chen's method, utterly free of smoke and fire, leaned toward divine-soul illusion arts. But that he could deploy it at the critical instant with such perfect timing still shocked her beyond measure.

She even could not help wondering, if she faced Luo Chen, what would her chances of victory be...

This imagining did not continue at all.

Because Luo Chen spoke.

"Next, I will stay on Heaven-Earth Peak and not go out. I will only wait for Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven to open. If there is no important matter, do not disturb me."

Fu Qinglan hesitated, but seeing Luo Chen's firmness, she understood he disliked these lively events.

Rogue cultivator background, after all. It was like this.

It was just somewhat regrettable. She had still wanted to introduce Luo Chen to her two friends.

But looking at Luo Chen's tall back as he departed, her heart filled with a sense of safety.

"With him this powerful, do I have hope of gaining more in Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven?"

...

When returning to Heaven-Earth Peak, Luo Chen saw a streak of escaping light drifting unsteadily out, heading toward the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range upstream along the Passing River.

"Only going to the Peach Garden at this hour?"

Luo Chen muttered.

But when he was about to enter the mountain, he subconsciously looked back at that escaping light.

The uncovered face within felt somewhat familiar.

"It seems to be Lin Bufan[TERM?] of the Lin family from the competition intelligence?"

He had some impression of this person, because Lin Bufan was Little Linzi in Fu Qinglan's mouth and also one of Fu Qinglan's friends.

"Why did he come out of Heaven-Earth Peak? I remember his Dao protector is his own family servant."

Among Tianyuan great clans, countless servants and attendants were cultivated.

Among these people, high-level cultivators occasionally appeared.

But most came from the Twelve Great Houses.

For forces like the Thirty-Six Aristocratic Clans, cultivating Adept servants was already decent. Sage-level ones were basically impossible.

After all, with those resources, it would be better to pour them into their own descendants.

However, the Lin family was an exception. The family head had taken in a child in earlier years. He was called a servant, but in truth was an adopted son.

Though he did not have the qualification to enter the Tianyuan Dao Sect, the family still cultivated him with all its strength and brought him to early-stage Sage.

Such a person had a fully known background and unquestionable loyalty.

Thus, that servant named Lin Jingtang was Lin Bufan's Dao protector.

As thoughts flowed through his mind, Luo Chen floated up the mountain.

When passing the huge plaza before Star Gate, he saw Sage Seven Lamps[TERM?].

Luo Chen deliberately stopped to greet him. "Elder Seven Lamps!"

"So it is Wilderness Wanderer. Where have you been?" Sage Seven Lamps, whose face was withered yellow, asked with a smile.

Luo Chen pointed in one direction. "I just returned from the Peach Garden."

Sage Seven Lamps understood. "That makes sense. When I first came to Central Continent, I was also quite interested in this continent's so-called go matches. But over time, I lost the desire to join the excitement."

Sage Seven Lamps came from the West Desert.

His exact age was unknown. He had joined Heaven-Earth Peak more than three hundred years ago, and his current realm was late-stage Sage.

But none of that was important. What mattered was that he was one of Heaven-Earth Peak's nineteen elders!

At the same time, he was also the outside-continent cultivator of Heaven-Earth Peak who had the most contact with Luo Chen.

Previously, when the Seven Elders decided to give Luo Chen external affairs guest elder status and arrange his cave abode, this man had participated in the entire process and had even personally handed things over to Luo Chen.

This was why Luo Chen took the initiative to stop and greet him.

Curious, Luo Chen asked, "Since Elder is not interested, why stand here?"

Surely not for a stroll!

Sage Seven Lamps shook his head. "Driving people out."

"Driving people out?" Luo Chen was stunned.

Sage Seven Lamps pursed his lips. "Did you not see that Lin family boy when you returned?"

"You mean Lin Bufan?"

"Yes. That boy loitered on Heaven-Earth Peak for more than half a year, which did not conform to the rules. I could only drive him away."

"Oh."

Luo Chen nodded. Though he did not understand why Lin Bufan had loitered on Heaven-Earth Peak, at least this explained why he was leaving Heaven-Earth Peak now.

Since they had met, they naturally exchanged a few pleasantries.

Luo Chen simply stopped and chatted with Sage Seven Lamps.

Unexpectedly, this casual talk truly let him learn some inside information.

The time was roughly half a year later.

The place was Heaven-Earth Peak, on the very plaza beneath Luo Chen's feet.

The person was Chu Yan, the Alchemy Saint!

"At that time, Central Continent's Twelve Great Houses and Thirty-Six Aristocratic Clans will all be present to observe the ceremony, and countless humble families will also choose representatives to come. Experts from the four continents will be seated to respectfully send off the Alchemy Saint."

"Many experts of our Heaven-Earth Peak, aside from those away from home, will basically show themselves."

"I hear Fellow Daoist will enter Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven as a Dao protector. At that time, it is best for you to be present."

Luo Chen was shocked by how quickly time had passed, and also curious why it would be better for him to be present.

Regarding this, Elder Seven Lamps only smiled slightly.

"I have been in Heaven-Earth Peak for only three hundred years, and did not see the last Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven opening. But the classics record it this way. Presumably, Family Head Fu will notify you then."

Carrying his doubts, Luo Chen returned to cave abode Seventy-Eight.

He very much wanted to know what connection the Alchemy Saint's ascension ceremony had with the opening of Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, but he was only an external affairs guest elder, not even an ordinary Star Gate guest elder, so he had no authority to consult related classics. He could only let it go.

For the following time, Luo Chen did not intend to go out again.

It was not merely that he was uninterested in such excitement, but that he did not want to be drawn into pointless disputes.

He had only gone out once today, and it had already brought Qi Xiongcheng's probe. That had absolutely not been some rash action born from anger that Luo Chen had taken the Dao protector position!

In his view, it was much more likely to have been secretly indicated by Shi Ju.

As the competition approached, other clan disciples might very well have similar thoughts.

After all, opponent intelligence only remained on paper. If there was an opportunity to personally probe someone, no one would miss it.

Under such circumstances, preserving a certain mystery around himself would benefit his later actions more.

Luo Chen's plan remained step by step as before.

Under the condition that daily cultivation could not be performed, he would raise the proficiencies of Hongyuan Pill and Great Soul Pill to Grandmaster level.

The latter had already reached the target.

So he could take high-grade Great Soul Pills to promote the divided soul's growth.

At the same time, Luo Chen constantly watched the changes of his own source true flame. The Withering-Bloom True Flame was continuously moving toward withering and dead silence. Calculating by time, it should soon complete the step of flourishing reaching its peak and declining.

...

As Luo Chen expected, as the competition drew closer, investigations into the participants' intelligence grew more intense.

Especially intelligence on the Dao protectors of the Tianyuan Dao Sect participants!

Among these Dao protectors, Luo Chen was the least conspicuous, yet also the most glaring!

He was least conspicuous because there was relatively little intelligence on him. His rise had been too short, and the major clans had not yet had time to collect information related to him.

But he was glaring because of his rogue cultivator identity.

The other Dao protectors were either great-clan servants with known backgrounds, or famous experts from major outside forces invited from abroad. Only Luo Chen had no roots as a rogue cultivator.

Logically, rogue cultivators were not worth fearing, but for the Fu family to push away a strong expert like Qi Xiongcheng showed the Fu family's confidence in Luo Chen's strength.

Most people knew little about Luo Chen.

But some people always had their own channels.

For example, in the Peach Garden, after Shi Ju listened to Qi Xiongcheng's description, an extremely interested look appeared on his face.

Proficient in divine-soul illusion arts?

Even able to cause deviation in a Sage's attack?

Just these two points were enough to make someone take precautions.

This intelligence had been obtained by Shi Ju sending someone to personally probe Luo Chen.

And in a secluded cave abode in the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range, Sage Dragon Abyss accepted a jade slip from Changfengzi.

The moment divine sense probed into it, dense information surged over.

Only after a long while did he set down the jade slip, half believing and half doubting.

"Is this intelligence real?"

Changfengzi hurriedly nodded. "It should not be false. This was personally sent by Elder Shen Wandou, who has long resided in Northern Sea's Divine Origin City. Part is confirmed beyond doubt, and part is reasonable inference. Even the inferred parts match completely after I used Central Continent's Shen family power to investigate."

"The first time he appeared, he suppressed Split Heaven Stream Lord in a domain clash and even made Old Monster Netherfire value him greatly. When Senior Brother Shen personally tested him, his divine sense performed unusually. He took the title Wilderness Wanderer himself, comparing himself to the Three Great Wanderers of the Northern Sea... This Devil Lord Azure Yang reached such a stage in only a hundred years?"

Sage Dragon Abyss found it somewhat unimaginable.

Regarding Wilderness Wanderer Luo Chen's Sage-level performance, he only considered the other party's strength uncommon.

But in the intelligence, Shen Wandou of the Northern Sea Tianyuan Merchant Alliance linked him with Devil Lord Azure Yang, who had risen to fame years ago in the Myriad Immortals Assembly.

When Devil Lord Azure Yang first entered the Myriad Immortals Assembly, he had only been at mid-stage Adept!

How could it have come to this?

But judging by Wilderness Wanderer's fire arts, as well as the black jiaolong spirit pet that had attacked the Coral Sea and relieved the Zhou clan's siege, he did indeed match some characteristics of Devil Lord Azure Yang.

Especially since the Zhou clan ancestor took the initiative to befriend him, and he also accepted a young female cultivator who was not from the Zhou clan. That female cultivator named Zhang Jingqing had a great connection to the Zhang family of Peng Lake's Lotus-Gathering Island. The Zhang family had once been Devil Lord Azure Yang's vassal.

Under the investigation of his clansman Changfengzi, that junior female cultivator and another maidservant were now both staying at the Fu family clan land in Central Continent.

Combining all kinds of intelligence, Wilderness Wanderer was very likely Devil Lord Azure Yang!

And Luo Hai was naturally Luo Chen.

Changfengzi said tentatively, "Perhaps his enormous change is connected to his trip to the Demon-Fall Land?"

Sage Dragon Abyss frowned. "That is possible. After all, anyone who walked alive out of Demon-Fall Land must have gained something."

Changfengzi said regretfully, "If Demon-Fall Land truly had such great fortune, Family Head, if you had gone there back then..."

After returning to Central Continent, Sage Dragon Abyss had completely grasped power over the humble Liu family and become its current head.

Facing his clansman's regret, he sharply glared at him.

"What do you know? Anyone may go to that place, but only my Tianyuan Dao Sect disciples must not!"

Changfengzi froze.

Sage Dragon Abyss did not explain further.

First, he disdained obtaining Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's inheritance in Demon-Fall Land. Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven naturally had his own fortune.

Second, some secrets told to him by the Dao Sect's master-disciple lineage meant he absolutely could not set foot in Demon-Fall Land.

There was no need to tell his clansman these matters.

"If he truly is Devil Lord Azure Yang, then you should thank him for saving the three of you in the Fallen Sea back then."

Changfengzi subconsciously nodded.

Back then, he, Guan Xiaoping, and Wu Tao had gone to the Northern Sea to temper themselves. Their original intention was to imitate their clan uncle, Sage Dragon Abyss, and rely on Northern Sea resources and life-and-death tempering to obtain a chance of advancing to Sage.

But later, the Demon Alliance broke free from Arctic Yemo Heaven, and the situation became far too dangerous.

The three of them were merely Adept cultivators, and under that great tide, they could hardly accomplish anything. They could only return to Central Continent by a winding path.

Changfengzi still remembered clearly the most dangerous tempering experience in the Fallen Sea.

Devil Lord Azure Yang had shown him kindness.

But Wilderness Wanderer...

"He is now Family Head's enemy. How could I feel gratitude toward him? After the competition ends, I will thank him then," Changfengzi said.

Sage Dragon Abyss shook his head. "You are narrow-minded."

Changfengzi insisted on his view. "Then let this nephew be narrow-minded. But regarding this person, Family Head, are you confident?"

Sage Dragon Abyss pondered for a moment, then suddenly smiled.

His smile was full of confidence.

"He and I both formed our Nascent Souls in the Northern Sea. Before that, when I was at the Adept stage, I had no opponent at the same realm. Now that we are both Sages, and I advanced one step earlier than him, how could I fear him?"

"Moreover, I am different from others. Mingyan and I will assist each other. Inside Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, no matter who we face, we do not fear them!"

Hearing the name Mingyan, Changfengzi's expression grew solemn.

Though the Tianyuan Dao Sect's master-disciple lineage had declined for a long time, in the end it still stood parallel to the clan lineage.

As the most outstanding prodigy of the current master-disciple lineage, with Martial Aunt Mingyan's help, Family Head would surely seize the Great Dao opportunity in Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven this time!

"Do not worry about me. You should return and prepare properly as well! I hope that when the time comes, you too can make your name in Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven and display the might of my Liu family!" Sage Dragon Abyss encouraged.

Changfengzi nodded with confidence.

Though born from a humble family, he also had the ambition to emulate his clan uncle!

Especially since he had gone to the Northern Sea to temper himself and had seen blood. He might not necessarily be weaker then than those disciples from Great Houses and Aristocratic Clans.

...

Central Continent became thoroughly lively.

Whether because of the Alchemy Saint's ascension or the Tianyuan Grand Competition, great clans and forces from every side continually streamed toward the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range.

The nearby immortal cities were already packed to capacity.

All kinds of news continually spread.

Yet the center of all this news, Heaven-Earth Peak, grew increasingly quiet, refusing all visits from outsiders.

Driving Lin Bufan off the mountain before had only been a negligible little matter before the mountain closed.

Everyone knew that the Alchemy Saint's ascension was imminent. Even when she occasionally appeared, it was mostly as an avatar.

She was wholeheartedly cultivating, seeking that final step!

This quiet environment also spared Luo Chen some potential troubles.

Every day, he refined pills. Every month, he ate one Great Soul Pill and circulated the Soul-Splitting Art, sensing the divided soul's growth.

In leisure time, he basically read books.

All kinds of books!

Those related to main cultivation arts, those related to puppet arts, those related to alchemy classics, and even some go records Fu Qinglan had given him. He occasionally flipped through those as well.

Before Heaven-Earth Peak closed, Fu Qinglan also delivered the magic-tool imitation of the Rotten Axe-Handle spirit treasure.

Luo Chen spent a day studying it and concluded that its power was extremely weak, but it indeed shared a similar principle with the Ghost God Valley Rotten Axe-Handle black stone he had obtained years ago.

In this way, he became even more certain that the Eastern Wilderness's Ghost God Valley had some connection with Central Continent's Tianyuan Dao Sect.

As for what connection?

Luo Chen leaned somewhat toward the relationship between the Northern Sea's Penglai Immortal Sect and the Tianyuan Dao Sect.

Only, Northern Sea forces were relatively scattered, while the Eastern Wilderness seemed divided into dozens of cultivation domains that did not govern one another, yet were all under the Nether Origin Sect's control. This could be seen from how the Nether Origin Sect's Ascendant great power had once mobilized the grand elders of three Sage upper sects.

Therefore, the Tianyuan Dao Sect could not insert Sage-level great forces into the Eastern Wilderness, and could only use a small force like Ghost God Valley to slowly seep in.

Perhaps this plan had long ago been discovered by the Nether Origin Sect, which had unknowingly squeezed out Ghost God Valley and ultimately sent it off to a development war, where it was destroyed in one stroke.

Luo Chen himself was startled by this imagining.

If that was truly the case, then the Tianyuan Dao Sect's schemes were truly astonishingly vast.

But judging by Heaven-Earth Peak's grand plan of absorbing Sages from the four continents, his deduction had a great possibility of standing.

"Forget it. In the end, it has nothing to do with me. I am merely a passing guest."

Luo Chen consoled himself this way.

Then he placed his attention on the changes of the source true flame within him.

It had already been a year since leaving the Fu family's Wangwu Mountain Range.

After digesting the Withered-Source Fire Marrow, the Withering-Bloom True Flame had continuously weakened.

The weakening had originally been slow, but Luo Chen discovered that after he came to Heaven-Earth Peak, the speed accelerated greatly.

Now that a year had passed, the Withering-Bloom True Flame in his Purple Mansion had weakened to the limit, leaving only a wisp of flame colored green with gold.

"After flourishing reaches its peak and declines, next should be revival from dead ashes, right?"

So Luo Chen thought.

But he did not wait for dead ashes to reignite. Instead, he waited for the ringing of a bell and the beating of a drum.

One bell rang, resounding through heaven and earth.

Nineteen drumbeats sounded, and Heaven-Earth Peak opened.

An aged, vicissitude-filled, mellow voice entered Luo Chen's ears like spring wind and fine rain, and entered the ears of everyone within tens of thousands of li of Heaven-Earth Peak.

"Heaven-Earth Peak opens. All cultivators gather. The Alchemy Saint ascends. Mountains and Seas offer congratulations!"

Luo Chen took a deep breath. Without any hesitation, he released a cleansing art upon himself and tied his loose hair with a wooden hairpin.

He slowly stood, straightened the robe corners that had no wrinkles to begin with, then floated out of the cave abode toward the cloud-sea plaza above.

Wherever he passed, spiritual qi rose, cheering and leaping.

As far as the eye could see, ten thousand streaks of escaping light, like tiny mayflies, gathered toward Heaven-Earth Peak towering into the clouds.

*(End of Chapter)*
