# Chapter 799: Primordial Spirit Wound, Personally Arriving at Lingtian

"Please!"

With that single word, all sounds fell silent.

Sage Primordial Spirit gazed at that solitary figure and fell into silence.

One, two, three—a full three Sage-level combat forces.

Nine Spirit Lord's magical power had always been highly praised.

According to intelligence, Luo Chen also possessed an extremely overbearing strange flame, suspected to be close to fifth rank.

Especially that unknown aura hidden in the shadows, which had already merged with the heaven and earth of this place, and had been the first to detect his traces.

"In the end, he has grown into a force to be reckoned with!"

He had originally come to observe in secret, and the so-called "guest visit" was merely a test.

Under such circumstances, it was clearly no longer advantageous for him.

Sage Primordial Spirit took a deep look at Luo Chen, then quietly withdrew.

Luo Chen did not stop him.

As for fear—it couldn't really be called fear. He knew that this day of facing Sage Primordial Spirit directly would come sooner or later.

Besides, he had allies now.

The reason he didn't strike was also due to his own considerations.

On the surface, the two sides had not truly torn apart their relationship. The other party's intentions toward him had not been made public either.

Under such circumstances, striking rashly would only fail to kill the snake and instead leave future trouble.

Moreover, after truly meeting Sage Primordial Spirit in person, Luo Chen no longer had absolute confidence.

"An enemy late-stage Sage with hostility—the pressure he gave me was that immense!"

Luo Chen gazed in the direction the other had departed and let out a long breath of turbid air.

...

"He wasn't brought by me!"

As soon as he returned, Nine Spirit Lord immediately distanced himself from the matter.

Luo Chen nodded and did not pursue it further.

Nine Spirit Lord breathed a sigh of relief, then asked with a hint of wariness in his eyes: "Do you have a grudge with the ancestor of the Five Elements Divine Sect?"

"Having killed the sect's most promising disciple for reaching the Sage stage in front of countless people, what do you think?" Luo Chen asked with a smile.

Nine Spirit Lord frowned, not quite believing this explanation.

Let alone a single disciple!

Back when the Five Elements Divine Sect was forced to leave the Five Elements Domain, countless outstanding disciples had died, including two of the Five Peak Elders, with another wounded—yet that old fellow had never personally intervened.

Luo Chen seemed to be in a relaxed mood at the moment. He asked with a smile: "Regarding this Sage Primordial Spirit, what do you know about him?"

Nine Spirit Lord thought for a moment and replied: "This man is extremely powerful. Among the countless cultivators of the Eastern Wilderness, aside from the experts of the Nether Origin Sect, he stands at the very pinnacle. If not for the severe injuries he suffered in the battle at Green Parasol Mountain back then, the Five Elements Divine Sect would not have fallen to its current state."

"Before that battle, he could almost be said to have single-handedly elevated the declining Five Elements Divine Sect to the level of a Sage-level sect."

"He led his disciples and followers in waging expansion wars, and later personally taught five Sage cultivators, completely dominating an entire domain."

"It can be said that Sage Primordial Spirit is exceptionally outstanding in both personal strength and leadership charisma. Even the Joyous Union Ancestor and Tianji Huiping, who were once his equals, could not compare to him. Only the Supreme Elder of the Dongyang Sect could rival him."

"Precisely because of this, even though the world spreads rumors that his injuries are difficult to heal and the Five Elements Divine Sect is in decline, no one dares to provoke him."

Luo Chen listened quietly to Nine Spirit Lord's description of that enemy.

There might be some bias in it, but Nine Spirit Lord was also a long-established top-tier expert of the Eastern Wilderness. The admiration in his words was genuine.

For someone like him to hold such high regard—it showed Sage Primordial Spirit's influence.

And such an existence was precisely his enemy!

"If you have a conflict with him, I think it's best to clarify it as soon as possible." Nine Spirit Lord hesitated for a moment, then said seriously: "If needed, I can act as an intermediary to mediate."

Luo Chen asked with a smile: "If the conflict between him and me is very, very great, would he give you face?"

Nine Spirit Lord frowned.

But soon, he said confidently: "Not now, certainly. But if I obtain the Nine Separation Pill and cultivate that great technique, I believe Sage Primordial Spirit will give me face."

Luo Chen laughed heartily.

"Enough. Let the future take care of itself! This time, you were willing to help me, and I, Luo, am truly grateful."

As he spoke, he pushed the nine jade bottles containing the pills one by one in front of the other.

"I have not failed your trust!"

Nine Spirit Lord took a deep breath, accepted the nine jade bottles, and examined them one by one.

Combining the records in his sect's classics, he observed the pills' color, patterns, and the overflow of medicinal power.

Finally, his face was filled with joy that could no longer be suppressed.

He put away all the jade bottles in one sweep and clasped his hands in a respectful bow.

"Fellow Daoist Luo, my thanks!"

Luo Chen waved his hand with a smile. "If you have needs in the future, feel free to come find me. On the path of cultivation, we should support each other and walk the Great Dao together, shouldn't we?"

"Indeed! Indeed!"

Faced with Luo Chen's gesture of goodwill, how could Nine Spirit Lord refuse it?

Especially after witnessing the other's courage in facing Sage Primordial Spirit head-on at the early Sage stage, he had gained a new level of respect for him.

A man of such caliber, as long as he didn't fall, would surely become one of the top experts of the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world in the future.

"Then I shall take my leave."

"Please!"

Luo Chen personally escorted him down the mountain, all the way to a hundred li beyond the Green River.

He had fully fulfilled the duties of a host.

Before leaving, Nine Spirit Lord suddenly spoke.

"In my view, Fellow Daoist's magical power far exceeds that of your peers, and is not inferior to even ordinary mid-stage Sages. In the future, if you simply cultivate step by step, by the time you reach my realm, you will surely be no weaker than me. Why seek extremes?"

"That technique of mine, though it sounds good, actually has great drawbacks. Absorbing extreme substances into the body disturbs the Three Treasures and causes immense pain. The world only speaks of my great magical power, but does not call it a great divine ability—that is one of the prices."

"Now, fortunately, I have obtained the Nine Separation Pill to help me shed my shackles. I am deeply grateful."

"Therefore, I specially advise you: do not bring harm upon yourself!"

Having said this, he did not wait for Luo Chen's reaction and drifted away.

Only Luo Chen was left standing there, lost in thought.

...

In his cave dwelling.

Luo Chen silently pondered Nine Spirit Lord's final words. A glimmer of insight seemed to flash, but he could never grasp the key point.

Just then, mist rose from the ground, and Black King cautiously emerged.

"Is that guy gone?"

Luo Chen did not look up. "Are you talking about Nine Spirit, or Sage Primordial Spirit?"

Seeing his master so calm, Black King also breathed a sigh of relief.

"Definitely that old bastard Sage Primordial Spirit!"

Luo Chen stopped dwelling on Nine Spirit Lord's final warning and looked at the awakened Black King.

"How did you detect him? Neither Nine Spirit Lord nor I sensed his arrival. If you hadn't notified me, I would still be in the dark."

As he said this, a hint of lingering fear appeared on Luo Chen's face.

No matter how much outsiders said about how powerful a late-stage Sage was, it was just a concept—too vague.

But when he faced Sage Primordial Spirit directly, he felt that wordless pressure.

It wasn't just magical power—it was also the oppression of the soul!

The other party could approach the Green River a hundred li away without being detected by them, and even the mountain-protecting grand formation here was not alerted.

This showed how strong his soul was!

But precisely because of such concealed movements, he was discovered by Black King.

Faced with Luo Chen's question, Black King was first stunned, then became smug.

"He was so conspicuous, like a candle flame in the dark night. I was awakened by him immediately."

Luo Chen was taken aback. "Conspicuous?"

"Right! When that old fellow moved, the nearby heaven and earth spiritual energy couldn't wait to rush toward him. Although he forcibly suppressed this tendency, the spiritual energy within a hundred li of the Great Green Mountain was all drawn here by my breathing. Someone wanted to compete with me for control, so naturally I sensed it."

As he said this, Black King still looked quite angry.

The abundant spiritual energy within a hundred li of the Great Green Mountain was all attracted by him while he slept.

Someone trying to snatch control from him—he certainly wasn't happy about it.

"Are you sure it was only spiritual energy?"

Black King was stunned for a moment, then carefully recalled, gradually becoming uncertain.

"It seems... it wasn't just spiritual energy?"

Luo Chen did not press further, but a certain definite guess had already formed in his mind.

That was not spiritual energy—it was the world's primordial origin qi!

In other words, *yuan qi*.

It sounded like a good thing for the world's origin qi to come to one willingly.

But in reality, it was not good at all!

Origin qi was pure and overbearing, yet also extremely mixed.

It was the fundamental energy that gave rise to all things in the world, containing various types of spiritual energy with different attributes.

Sage cultivators simply could not effectively use this power.

Luo Chen had absorbed a strand of origin qi in the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven after comprehending the true meaning of Withering-Bloom, and he still hadn't fully refined it to this day.

That showed how difficult it was!

It was precisely because of this experience that Luo Chen's understanding of origin qi had deepened.

This power could only be manipulated and used by Ascendant cultivators, who could extract the spiritual energy of the attributes they desired from it like drawing silk from a cocoon.

Sage cultivators, on the other hand, should avoid it as much as possible, preferably using the Purple Mansion as a barrier to isolate inside from outside.

If a mere Sage cultivator were truly enveloped by a large amount of world origin qi, there would only be two outcomes.

Either use it to advance to the Ascendant stage!

Or have their Sage Embryo assimilated by the world and become part of the origin qi!

Given Sage Primordial Spirit's realm, he was still far from the Ascendant stage.

Therefore, only the latter possibility existed.

"It seems that in that battle at Green Parasol Mountain back then, he didn't die, but he didn't come out unscathed either!"

"Origin qi actively surges toward him, indicating that his physical body has suffered tremendous damage, to the point where even the Purple Mansion that shelters his Sage Embryo has developed flaws, unable to perfectly isolate inside from outside. So, when facing the three of us, he chose to retreat voluntarily. Otherwise, if a real fight broke out, it's hard to say who would win, but it would definitely worsen his injuries."

"If that's really the case, then dealing with him might not be as difficult as I imagined?"

As he pondered, Luo Chen unconsciously curled his fingers.

A trace of profound and mysterious meaning flickered faintly.

Black King's eyes immediately went wide!

...

The days of secluded cultivation eventually came to an end.

When living under someone else's roof, one cannot eat and drink for free without paying a price.

The Luo Tian Sect had to send people to the Lingtian Pass to perform patrol duties—it could no longer be delayed.

Originally, it should have been the Luo Tian Sect's turn two years ago.

But the Lingtian City Lord had suppressed it, which was why they had enjoyed these two years of peace.

Now, it was finally their turn.

In the Luo Tian Grand Hall, Li Yingzhang faced the Nether Origin Sect disciple who was directly under the Lingtian City Lord's command, his demeanor respectful.

But in his words, he argued based on reason.

"Why do we need to send so many people this time?"

That young cultivator in black Daoist robes replied coldly and arrogantly: "Times have changed. Before, you were merely a Core Formation force, but now you have a Sage expert. Naturally, you should contribute more."

Li Yingzhang was left speechless.

He never expected this to be the reason.

Just then, Luo Chen walked into the hall.

Li Yingzhang quickly went to greet him.

"Supreme Elder!"

"Mm, I already know about the matter."

Luo Chen waved his hand and looked at the young cultivator.

The other's cold arrogance slightly receded as he bowed to Luo Chen.

"Ye Hong pays respects to Sage Luo!"

"Ye Hong? Are you a descendant of the Lingtian City Lord?" Luo Chen asked with a smile.

Ye Hong nodded. "The City Lord is indeed my grandfather."

Luo Chen seemed thoughtful, then asked: "Is this also the Lingtian City Lord's intention?"

Ye Hong immediately explained: "Indeed it is. For the past two years, my grandfather used the excuse that the Luo Tian Sect had recently advanced to a Sage-level sect and needed time to reorganize, thus delaying the patrol duty. But now, the pressure at the Lingtian Pass is gradually increasing, so you can no longer continue to rest and recuperate."

So that was it.

Luo Chen understood the Lingtian City Lord's good intentions.

If his Luo Tian Sect wanted to enjoy the prestige of a Sage-level sect, it naturally had to bear the corresponding responsibilities.

Otherwise, enjoying only the benefits without taking on responsibilities—other sects would not stand for it.

Two years, under the current situation, was already long enough.

Luo Chen's reputation had gradually been established.

"In that case, my Luo Tian Sect will send the appropriate personnel!"

Ye Hong breathed a sigh of relief. "Sage Luo is truly understanding. This junior will now return to report."

"Heavenjade, see him out."

"Understood."

Heavenjade, who was standing nearby, personally escorted Ye Hong out of the Great Green Mountain, also saying some pleasantries to ease the previously tense relationship.

After they left, Li Yingzhang told Luo Chen the number of personnel the Luo Tian Sect had to send this time.

One hundred late-stage Qi Refinement cultivators; if insufficient, double the number of mid-stage Qi Refinement cultivators could be substituted.

Twenty Initiate true cultivators; if insufficient, triple the number of late-stage Qi Refinement cultivators could be substituted.

Three Core Formation cultivators—there was no concept of "insufficient" for them. Any sect with a decent foundation would have at least three Core Formation cultivators.

If they didn't have this number, they wouldn't be worthy of being called a Core Formation great sect.

The Luo Tian Sect currently had only a little over three hundred people. Sending so many at once would drain nearly half their strength.

If anything went wrong, the Luo Tian Sect would suffer a severe blow to its vitality.

After learning these conditions, Luo Chen also asked about the situation of other Sage-level sects sending personnel.

The answer was—even more severe!

"It seems the Lingtian City Lord has already treated us quite favorably." Luo Chen nodded slightly.

Li Yingzhang smiled bitterly. "We can send three Core Formation cultivators. Twenty Initiate cultivators can be managed with gritted teeth. But as for the lowest-level late-stage Qi Refinement cultivators, where can the Luo Tian Sect scrape together a hundred of them now?"

To this, Luo Chen was not worried.

"Pick fifty. That will be enough."

Li Yingzhang was puzzled.

They were already sending fewer than other Sage-level forces, and now they wanted to reduce further?

Then he saw Luo Chen stand up and wave his sleeves elegantly.

"I will personally make the trip. That alone can make up for fifty Qi Refinement cultivators."

Li Yingzhang was stunned.

For the Supreme Elder to personally go—let alone fifty Qi Refinement cultivators, even five hundred or five thousand could not compare to a single finger of his.

But...

Luo Chen smiled. "Tired of stillness, I long for movement. I should make a trip to the Lingtian Pass and see for myself what the situation is on the front lines. I imagine the people there will welcome me quite warmly!"

A generation's Alchemy Saint, personally arriving at the front lines.

Who would refuse that?
