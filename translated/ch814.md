# Chapter 814: The Alchemy Saint Is Here, Entering Lingtian Is Forbidden!

The human-demon war at Lingtian Pass had resumed after more than a month of silence.

Unlike the initial tension, anxiety, and trepidation, the cultivators within the city had truly grown accustomed to this rhythm. They now treated it as routine as a daily meal.

It was just that this meal was a bit hard to swallow.

One careless bite could choke you to death.

Luo Chen still remained in the relatively safe Western District, guarding his own small patch of territory.

From time to time, he learned of the frontline battle situation through his disciples.

For instance, the demon beasts had committed even more troops than before.

Or that a Sage and a Demon Emperor had clashed outside the city. The specifics were unknown, but Lingtian Pass claimed they had easily repelled the enemy.

He also heard that Commander Huang Man had personally come to observe outside Lingtian Pass. After a long silence, he had retreated.

That last piece of news, in particular, lifted a heavy stone from many people's hearts.

They truly could not breach Lingtian Pass's defenses!

Even the joint efforts of the two great commanders dared not act rashly.

Luo Chen kept a close watch on all of this, but his main focus remained on his own cultivation.

He had gradually figured out the changes in the *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon*.

Just as he had imagined, after his proficiency had risen to the next level, the speed at which he refined sky qi into sword qi had increased, saving a great deal of time.

Based on his specific assessment, this reduction was a full doubling!

In other words, what previously took half a year to refine a single strand now only required three months.

Luo Chen understood the implications better than anyone.

Originally, reaching the Great Completion of the Thousand Skies Converging Stream realm would have taken five hundred years. Now, it would only take two hundred and fifty.

Furthermore, if his proficiency continued to improve, he might be able to shorten this time even further.

"This sword art was practically tailor-made for me!"

After refining his eleventh strand of sword qi, Luo Chen spoke with immense excitement.

He could see the hope of mastering this canon within his lifetime.

Moreover, the increased efficiency could also compensate for the consumption of Red Firmament Sword Qi.

However, another problem now presented itself to Luo Chen.

The quantity of the three types of sky qi required!

Even a clever housewife cannot cook a meal without rice. Having the proficiency was useless without a sufficient quantity!

And sky qi was not something that could be artificially produced. It had to be gathered from the blue heavens, and only a Sage could do so.

Therefore, there was basically no market for such a resource. The only way to obtain it was through bartering!

"Searching for it on my own would be too troublesome."

"It seems I'll have to spread the word and wait for a seller to come to me."

As for how to spread the word, that was simple.

Within Lingtian Pass, interactions between Sage cultivators were very frequent, and various private gatherings were often held.

Luo Chen usually received an invitation to these gatherings.

The name of the Alchemy Saint carried considerable weight. If he attended, it would bring the host considerable face.

At such an event, he could casually mention his need for the three types of sky qi, and the news would naturally spread among the Sage cultivators.

---

For low-level cultivators, a large-scale war felt like a year dragging on.

But for high-level cultivators like Luo Chen, time passed in the blink of an eye.

The deadline for his final patrol duty approached unnoticed as Luo Chen cultivated.

The one who came to relieve him was the Second Palace Master of Fenghua Palace.

"Guarding the Western District is a good assignment. It's the safest here, with the fewest demon beasts," Luo Chen chatted cheerfully with the Second Palace Master as they handed over the duty.

The other party was clearly satisfied with this assignment.

"Thanks to Lord Lingtian's consideration for not assigning me to one of the other three districts. That would have been troublesome."

"Haha, I suppose it's also because you're familiar with this area!"

Luo Chen smiled. After his subordinates completed the handover, he led Zeng Yilong, Yang Pingdu, and the others down from the city wall.

From now on, he wouldn't have to keep a daily watch here.

As for his next arrangements, according to normal procedure, he could return to his sect for a period of rest.

But unfortunately, this was not a normal time.

So, like all the other Sages who had come to reinforce Lingtian Pass, he had to remain within the pass, on standby at all times.

To put it simply, Luo Chen's next role was that of a mobile reserve!

This status seemed leisurely, but when trouble actually arose, he would have to grit his teeth and step up, no matter how unwilling he was.

After descending the city wall, Jueqing the Fairy and Chu Kui also gathered around.

Luo Chen led the four of them, enjoying a rare leisurely stroll down the main street of Lingtian's Western District.

Cultivators came and went all around them, their hurried figures a stark contrast to their leisurely pace.

Jueqing the Fairy, usually taciturn, suddenly let out a faint sigh.

"We cultivators, even if not seeking tranquility far from the bustling world, should at least be dining on morning clouds and drinking evening dew, moving among the azure clouds. How have we fallen to this state, like common mortals fighting day after day?"

Yang Pingdu nodded in agreement, looking as if he shared the sentiment.

Chu Kui sneered at this. "You speak as if cultivation should be without desire or ambition. Don't you know that the path to immortality is crowded? Everyone wants to take a few more steps forward. And how do you do that? By stepping over the corpses of others, by seizing their resources, their territories, even their disciples! That's how you go further than the rest!"

"Uncle-Master Chu is right!" Zeng Yilong agreed strongly.

Luo Chen listened to the conversation behind him and smiled knowingly.

It was normal for his disciples to have differing opinions. Jueqing and Yang Pingdu had both come from established sects. Although they weren't pampered, even their competitions were mostly conducted under an orderly framework.

Now that order had been broken. Cultivators from lofty sects had to obey others' commands and live amidst the blood and fire of war.

Even if they had adapted, they still complained.

In contrast, Chu Kui, a rogue cultivator who had always toiled for cultivation resources, was very accustomed to this environment.

It was just that before, it was man against man. Now, it was man against demon beast.

However, Zeng Yilong's agreement with Chu Kui's thoughts surprised Luo Chen a little.

But after thinking about it, he understood. It was probably Wang Yuan's teachings from childhood that had influenced him.

And what about himself?

What kind of life did he want?

To hold great power, revered by billions, like Heavenly Origin Sect Master Mo Shouzhuo, lofty and untouchable?

To travel far and wide, fighting different opponents, enjoying the thrill of life-and-death battles?

Or to dine on clouds and drink dew, tranquil and detached, without desire or ambition amidst green mountains and white clouds?

No!

All he sought was immortality. Any kind of life was merely a fleeting scene on his journey towards that goal.

To achieve this goal, he could abandon all other desires.

Because immortality itself was the greatest desire in the world!

After this deep reflection, Luo Chen's expression became calm and detached, neither happy nor sad.

His footsteps stopped at his cave dwelling.

"You may go and rest. If you are summoned, make sure to protect yourselves."

"Yes, Supreme Elder!"

The four acknowledged and dispersed at the foot of the mountain.

Faint snatches of their discussion drifted on the wind.

"Have you noticed? The Supreme Elder is becoming harder and harder to read."

"Heh, as if you've ever been able to see through the Supreme Elder before."

"Junior Disciple Zeng is right. When the Supreme Elder first returned to the Eastern Wilderness, his aura was extremely sharp, with a 'keep away' attitude. He was strong, but I only thought of him as an ordinary Sage. But over the years, the Supreme Elder has kept a low profile, and his aura has been completely restrained. Although he has become more approachable, my reverence for him has only deepened. I wouldn't even dare to guess what he's thinking."

"Jueqing the Fairy, what do you think?"

"Me? I can't see through him. Before, I could guess some of his thoughts, but now I can't figure him out at all. And I don't dare to try."

---

After his patrol duty ended, Luo Chen suddenly had a lot of free time.

Returning to his cave dwelling, he set aside all mundane affairs and devoted himself entirely to cultivation.

He was getting closer and closer to the mid-Sage stage, and he rarely had the opportunity to use this fourth-rank mid-grade spirit vein cave dwelling. Naturally, he wanted to make the most of it before Lord Lingtian took back the usage rights.

His Fire Spirit Body gave him a cultivation speed comparable to a Heavenly Spirit Root.

His deep Purple Mansion barrier allowed him to ignore the danger of being assimilated by heaven and earth, absorbing external spiritual energy without restraint.

The top-grade fourth-rank Hongyuan Pills each contained immense medicinal power. Refining one was equivalent to an ordinary Sage cultivating for over a month.

Coupled with his fifth-rank Withering-Bloom True Flame, which could help him purify impurities in his mana.

With all these advantages, after consolidating his realm, Luo Chen began to cultivate with complete abandon.

His realm began to skyrocket at a terrifying speed.

One month later.

The vast spiritual energy within the cave dwelling was drawn in by Luo Chen, almost coalescing into a sea of misty clouds.

A massive amount of spiritual energy was absorbed into his body.

Suddenly!

The misty clouds dispersed as Luo Chen let out a long breath of turbid qi.

He glanced briefly at his attribute panel. **[Sage Layer 3: 90/100]** .

"Just one step away from the mid-stage realm. Who dares to disturb me now?"

Luo Chen frowned, displeased, and picked up his waist token, which was flashing.

He injected his mana into it, and Tianduzi's calm, weathered voice came through.

"The Western District is under attack. We must not let the fourth-rank Demon Emperors destroy the city's major and minor formations. Fellow Daoist Luo, please go and assist."

Luo Chen's frown deepened.

The Western District was considered a relatively safe area. Were the demon beasts now expanding their attack range to this side?

It seemed they realized that if they couldn't cut off Lingtian Pass's connection with other forces, reinforcements would keep pouring in.

That was why they were spreading their forces westward.

Luo Chen hesitated no longer. He rose to his feet.

As soon as he stepped outside his cave dwelling, he saw a transmission talisman swirling with wind, flying around aimlessly like a headless fly within the cave's maze formation.

He casually pointed a finger, and the talisman ignited.

The urgent voice of the Second Palace Master of Fenghua Palace immediately rang out.

"Enemy attack! There is more than one fourth-rank Demon Emperor! I implore the Fellow Daoist to assist!"

Luo Chen snuffed out the talisman with a pinch.

"The situation seems... a bit grim!"

Murmuring to himself, Luo Chen shot into the sky, transforming into a streak of fire as he sped towards the Western District.

---

At the Western District.

It was usually peaceful, with only scattered demon beasts appearing occasionally.

But now, it had become a place of raging stench and violent spiritual energy.

Looking into the distance, countless demon beasts were surging forward like an ocean of beasts.

Figures of cultivators rose into the air, their expressions tense as they looked outside.

Their gazes fell upon the city wall.

Disciples of the Tiangdu Sect were leading the effort to activate the formations, launching attacks outside the city.

Meteors fell from the sky, black wind and fire rained down, beams of light swept across...

When the formations were pushed to their limits, the Tiangdu Sect disciples would immediately go to replace the spirit stones and repair the formation gaps.

It was during these intervals that the cultivators within the city had to hold the line, buying them time.

The Second Palace Master of Fenghua Palace spread her divine sense, precisely transmitting each command to the leading Core Formation cultivators amidst the chaotic battlefield. They, in turn, directed the lower-level cultivators to resist the demon beast attacks.

The Second Palace Master's gaze, however, frequently drifted to the sky ten li away from Lingtian Pass.

There, black clouds gathered, and demonic qi surged!

As the fierce winds swirled, they would occasionally reveal several shadowy figures.

Demon Emperors. More than one!

At least four!

The Second Palace Master smiled bitterly. "When did my Western District become attractive enough to draw so many Demon Emperor-level existences! If the other Fellow Daoists don't arrive soon, I'll have to apply to Tianduzi to activate the city's main defensive formation."

The Black Tortoise Four Directions Formation was incredibly powerful!

But it was not something that could be activated casually. Each activation came at a tremendous cost.

Lord Lingtian had only activated it when he had no other choice.

Just as the Second Palace Master was growing anxious, three streaks of light suddenly descended upon the city wall.

She looked over and exclaimed with surprise.

"Fellow Daoist Jin Ling, you've finally arrived!"

The newcomers were Jin Ling, Divine Fire, and Motian the Old Ghost. They were representatives of two of the three great Sage-level sects residing in Fenghua Domain.

As soon as he landed, Jin Ling narrowed his eyes, looking into the distance.

Beside him, Divine Fire's expression shifted slightly. "Four Demon Emperors? Such a grand formation?"

Motian the Old Ghost, however, looked around and, not seeing the person he wanted to see, sneered.

"And where is that brat Luo Chen? Has he taken fright in the face of a great enemy?"

The Second Palace Master also felt some resentment inwardly, but she didn't show it on her face. She said urgently, "Those fellows have been watching for a long time. They're about to make their move. When they do, I ask you all to hold them back. If necessary, I will also join the fight."

Without turning his head, Jin Ling said, "That mid-fourth-rank demon cultivator is mine. You can divide the rest among yourselves!"

Upon hearing this, the Second Palace Master felt a little more at ease.

As long as Jin Ling made his move, this battle would be much more secure.

But why hadn't Luo Chen arrived yet? She had clearly sent him a transmission talisman!

---

In the distance, the four demon beasts fixed their gazes on the city wall of Lingtian Pass, silently watching their subordinate demon beast army attack.

As the formation lights flickered, they searched for weaknesses.

"Lingtian Pass has high walls and deep moats, with cultivators as numerous as clouds. But what restricts our advance are actually these large and small formations."

"As expected of the crystallization of human wisdom, able to unleash power comparable to the Core Formation and Sage stages using only various materials and spirit stones as energy sources."

"We can't wait any longer. Our children are dying in increasing numbers. We should try to break these formations, large and small. Once these formations are broken, especially the no-fly formation, our children can charge into the city in one fell swoop."

"Remember, we must not force our way into the city, lest we trigger the city's main defensive formation!"

After a brief exchange, the four demon beasts struck simultaneously.

Vast demonic power filled the sky.

Giant palm prints, blazing fireballs, black gales, and even lightning bolts descended from the heavens.

Four massive attacks surged forward.

Shua!

A light barrier rose from the city wall in response.

It forcibly blocked these four attacks.

But after a moment, the light barrier also began to weaken.

A Tiangdu Sect disciple shouted urgently, "Sage! This fourth-rank defensive formation has temporarily exhausted its energy. We need to replenish the spirit stones. Do not let the Demon Emperors' attacks land!"

Before the Second Palace Master could speak, she saw three figures fly out from the city wall, each heading towards an enemy.

"It's my turn too!"

The Second Palace Master took a deep breath, then opened her mouth and spat out a small round fan into her hand.

Just as she was about to set off, she suddenly saw a red light flash over her head.

A voice came from within.

"Fellow Daoist, it's better for you to stay and hold the line. Leave the remaining Demon Emperor to me, Luo!"

It was Luo Chen!

The Second Palace Master's expression relaxed slightly.

But when she saw the realm of the remaining Demon Emperor, her face changed slightly.

Jin Ling and the others had chosen three opponents. The one left behind was actually at the Sage Layer 3 level.

"If Luo Chen hadn't come, would this demon have been left for me?"

She was only at Sage Layer 2!

Every minor realm difference between Sage cultivators represented an improvement.

Similarly, every minor realm gap represented an absolute difference in mana.

Without a powerful trump card, even a single minor realm could crush you!

What had Jin Ling and the others intended by leaving this one for her?

The Second Palace Master had no time to think further. Her gaze followed Luo Chen intently.

It was rumored that the Alchemy Saint Luo Chen had once withstood the mid-Sage Jin Ling during his Core Formation Ceremony, and had even single-handedly faced a Thunder Roc during the defense of the pass.

Could he stop this demon?

Ten li was nothing for a Sage cultivator; they arrived in an instant.

Shua!

The flying light suddenly stopped. A white-robed, tall-crowned Daoist made a bowing salute to the hawk-nosed Demon Emperor.

"The Alchemy Saint is here. Entering Lingtian Pass is forbidden."

"Fellow Daoist, I greet you!"

(The chapter ends)
