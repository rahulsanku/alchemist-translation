# Chapter 982: Ceasefire and Reconciliation, Cooperate to Destroy the Enemy

The blazing sun seemed shrouded in a layer of gray haze, the world between heaven and earth nothing but a murky yellow.

The sound of flying sand and rolling stones pierced the eardrums.

In the pitch-black darkness, only the wild sandstorm swept through everything in the world.

At the very core, an even larger dragon-shaped sand funnel connected heaven and earth, scouring all living creatures.

From time to time, broken demon beast carcasses would be swept in, becoming part of the expanding sand funnel.

And all of this was caused by the collision of ultimate techniques between two of the world's top powerhouses.

The chain reaction from a single clash between the Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations and the Sub-Empty Slash was utterly terrifying.

When the silver-threaded, black-robed man delved deep into the center of the sandstorm and saw all the traces left behind, his heart nearly leaped into his throat.

"Under such a terrifying attack, can a Sage truly survive?"

He was both shocked by the strength of those two unfamiliar powerhouses and secretly delighted that they had engaged in such desperate, life-or-death combat.

Such great means were not something that could be casually unleashed.

Once it came to this point, it was almost certain to be a fight to the death, with no quarter given.

"Could it be mutual destruction?"

Refining Silver Roc licked his dry lips and returned to the Sand-Trapping Valley.

His divine sense spread out, carefully probing in all directions, and finally followed the lingering traces deep underground.

Only when he reached a depth of a thousand zhang did Refining Silver Roc's eyes suddenly light up!

"Found them!"

He struck out with a palm.

The vast, flowing sand immediately parted to both sides, and the scene within the deep pit gradually came into view.

A man, and a woman!

The man was completely naked, his hair disheveled, one arm severed, one leg limp and powerless, his entire body covered in large and small wounds that could not heal. The longest was a full foot and a half, cutting across his waist and abdomen—a truly shocking sight.

The woman was not much better off. Her entire body was bruised black and blue, covered in scars, and a golden needle was faintly visible on the crown of her head. Her appearance was utterly miserable.

The two were entangled in an ambiguous posture, kissing each other's necks and clawing at each other's hearts.

"What a perfect mutual destruction!"

Seeing this scene, Refining Silver Roc was overjoyed.

But then, as his divine sense probed further, he felt a trace of regret.

"No more aura. Their Sages must have already dissipated. What a pity—I've lost the greatest prize."

Then, his gaze fell upon the two bodies.

For powerhouses like these, the resources they carried on them were sure to be abundant.

To be safe, Refining Silver Roc let demonic aura gather at his fingertips and struck several attacks at the two corpses.

*Puff! Puff!*

*Puff! Puff!*

The heads of the man and woman instantly burst like melons.

Only then did Refining Silver Roc finally breathe a sigh of relief.

The brain was the seat of the sea of consciousness. Even if a cultivator's soul could temporarily reside in their Sage, no one would be willing to sacrifice their brain as bait.

From the looks of it, they really had perished together!

"What a windfall for me!"

Refining Silver Roc was overjoyed and charged straight down.

Suddenly!

"No!"

Refining Silver Roc's expression changed drastically. A violent crackling sound erupted from his body, as if something was breaking through him.

His eyes suddenly turned blood-red, and the surrounding scenery began to change dramatically.

From illusion to reality, it took only an instant.

What came into view was a woman in green robes, reaching out with her hand, forcibly breaking through layer after layer of defenses he had pre-arranged on his body.

"It's a trap!"

Refining Silver Roc's soul fled in terror. He mustered his mana, and immeasurable silver light sprayed out in all directions.

But the woman in green paid it no heed, still reaching out to grab him.

Wherever her slender hand passed, the silver light parted like mercury, her mana unstoppable.

When her palm touched his head, Refining Silver Roc panicked.

"My elder brother is the Golden Roc King, overlord of the Western Desert! Spare my life!"

The Golden Roc King was the title of the fifth-rank desolate beast, the Golden-Winged Great Peng.

Hearing this name, the woman in green paused slightly in her movements.

At that moment, a muffled grunt came from behind her.

Refining Silver Roc's eyes went blank.

The woman in green hesitated no longer. Her right hand pierced through metal and shattered stone, crushing his brain in one grip.

His soul scattered, blood blossomed.

The remaining body lost consciousness and uncontrollably transformed back into its original form.

It was a massive roc bird, several hundred zhang in length, its entire body pure silver, fierce and majestic.

*Boom!*

The roc's corpse crashed heavily onto the sand dune.

Only then did heavy panting come from behind the woman in green.

She turned around and looked at the man leaning against the wall, blood tears streaming from his eyes. A flicker of admiration passed through her gaze.

She seemed astonished that Luo Chen, in such a state, could still unleash a soul art that could easily control a fourth-stage demon cultivator.

Seeing the admiration in the woman's eyes, Luo Chen closed his eyes with difficulty and smiled bitterly. "Even though my soul foundation far surpasses those of the same rank, it's still useless against you, isn't it?"

Azure Frost did not explain. After wiping the blood from her hand, she walked over to him.

"Can you still move?"

Luo Chen kept his eyes closed. "I need to regulate my breathing for a while."

Azure Frost hummed in acknowledgment, then paced over to Refining Silver Roc's side. After observing for a moment, she suddenly reached out and grabbed.

*Rip!*

Flesh and blood flew everywhere as a storage pouch flew out.

"There might be something you need in here."

Luo Chen did not refuse. He took the storage pouch and wiped his hand over it. The fragile soul imprint on it instantly dissipated.

Then, several bottles and jars flew out.

Even with his eyes closed, Luo Chen's examination allowed him to clearly identify the properties of the pills inside.

"Agada Pills—can revive the dead and restore flesh and bone."

"Relic Pills—can restore a large amount of a cultivator's mana."

"White Bone Fat—a fourth-stage pill for repairing meridian injuries."

"This demon roc really had some good stuff on him!"

Luo Chen's own pills had been almost completely consumed over the years. With this batch, he could recover somewhat.

He began to take them, categorizing them by type, and his aura, which had been extremely weak, gradually began to rise.

But after rising to a certain point, it could no longer advance.

Luo Chen raised his head. Though his eyes were closed, he was still staring in Azure Frost's direction.

"Since the gu insects have already been planted, and we are bound by restrictions and a Great Dao oath, why not help each other?"

Azure Frost was silent.

Luo Chen sighed helplessly. "Daytime Void Hall Master, we are unfamiliar with this place. Human Sages and fourth-stage demon emperors appear at every turn. Do we really want to face the enemy again in this broken state? Help me, and I will help you remove the hidden danger I have left on you."

"Hmph!"

Azure Frost snorted coldly, walked to a spot not far from Luo Chen, and extended her right hand.

Instantly, strands of the foreign mana lingering on Luo Chen's body were drawn away.

"*Hoo...*"

Feeling this, Luo Chen breathed a sigh of relief.

Ever since he had fought Azure Frost, every time her attacks hit him, they left behind a strange and powerful mana that prevented him from repairing his body.

Especially the move that severed his left arm—even when he used his perfected Dragon-Slaying Art, he could not remove the residual mana.

It was extremely troublesome!

It was also during this battle that Luo Chen realized that the talent of flesh and blood regeneration was not only useless against devastating attacks or excessive self-consumption, but could also be greatly weakened by certain peculiar techniques.

With the restriction on his body lifted, Luo Chen's recovery speed increased dramatically.

He took out his previously severed hand from his storage ring, inserted it, twisted it, and let his source power flow. The hand immediately fused back together, and even the lingering wounds on his body began to gradually heal.

After adjusting a little, Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes.

Looking at the woman who still remained silent, he smiled helplessly.

"If you don't mind, come a little closer!"

Azure Frost bit her lip, hesitating.

Luo Chen shrugged. "There's no other way. I can't recover much mana in a short time. You need to be closer so I can help you."

Azure Frost's gaze wavered, but in the end, she walked over to Luo Chen's side and sat down cross-legged.

Luo Chen focused his mind.

*Whoosh! Whoosh! Whoosh! Whoosh! Whoosh!*

Instantly, five streams of light flew out from her body.

It was the Law-Suppressing Five Element Cones!

Not only that, but Luo Chen extended his hand, hovering it above Azure Frost's head, and unleashed a suction force.

Azure Frost's body trembled slightly, but she let him do as he pleased.

A gleam of golden light slowly emerged from the crown of her head.

During this process, Luo Chen spread out his divine sense and finally detected something unusual.

A strange gray light was tightly wrapped around her sea of consciousness.

It was precisely this gray light that rendered Luo Chen's Soul-Weaving Needle and all kinds of illusory soul arts completely useless against Azure Frost.

A name slipped from his lips.

"Soul-Defense Spirit Treasure!"

After extracting the Law-Suppressing Five Element Cones and the Soul-Weaving Needle, Azure Frost also seemed much more relaxed.

She looked at him with a complicated expression. "This is the Nether Soul Pearl, a spirit treasure. It is nourished by the physical body and soul to protect the spirit. The stronger the body, the stronger its defensive power. In my continuous battle with you, my body grew weaker and weaker, which is why your golden needle was able to penetrate deeper and deeper."

Luo Chen was astonished. Such a treasure existed in the world?

But in the next moment, he vaguely sensed that the refining style of that spirit treasure had a somewhat familiar, yet elusive, quality.

"This spirit treasure wasn't refined by you, nor by Rosycloud, was it?"

"You guessed it?" Azure Frost asked in return.

Luo Chen hesitated, then uttered a name: "Devil Lord Heaven-Refining?"

Azure Frost nodded. "Exactly. That Devil Lord. After his battle with my sister back then, all his spirit treasures were brought out, and most of them were destroyed by her. The Nether Soul Pearl was also burned to a wreck by the Nirvana Sacred Flame. Later, my sister obtained it, gave it a simple refinement, and passed it on to me. Nourished since childhood, it gradually recovered some of its power."

Luo Chen exhaled a turbid breath, thinking to himself that it was just as he had suspected.

He had once heard some past events from the Heaven-Refining Cauldron's artifact spirit in the Bright Radiance Heaven Pill Hall.

Devil Lord Heaven-Refining had refined many spirit treasures in his lifetime, among them the Nether Soul Pearl.

Although it was a spirit treasure, its function and value were by no means inferior to those few inherited heavy treasures.

Unfortunately, after the battle with Rosycloud, all five spirit treasures were destroyed.

He had never expected that one of them had been preserved by Rosycloud.

Luo Chen cast a slightly envious glance at Azure Frost.

She cultivated the true meaning of spatial laws while simultaneously tempering her divine beast body, which already made her unmatched in offense and defense. And now, she also had this soul-defense spirit treasure protecting her.

With this, she had almost no weaknesses.

No wonder she had earned the title of the number one under the Ascendant stage.

With the Nether Soul Pearl protecting her, even ordinary Ascendant powerhouses would have trouble dealing with her soul in a short time!

Shaking his head, Luo Chen began to help Azure Frost remove the residual potent poison on her body.

That was a "mixed poison" he had cultivated after mastering the *Hundred Poisons Body-Tempering Art*.

Luo Chen hadn't given it a specific name yet, but the poison was so strong that it surpassed even the likes of Li Tian's Corpse Rot Poison.

It was precisely because of this mixed poison that he had been able to continuously weaken Azure Frost's strength during the battle.

As Luo Chen worked, the mixed poison gradually separated from Azure Frost's body and was absorbed by him.

The black and blue color on her body faded, and the wounds on Azure Frost's body began to heal. Even her severed leg started to regrow from the inside out.

Watching Luo Chen, who was focused on extracting the poison, she hesitated but ultimately remained silent.

Luo Chen noticed this and smiled. "If you have something to say, just say it directly."

Azure Frost opened her mouth, then finally said flatly, "That last sword of yours was very strong!"

Luo Chen chuckled lightly. "That move of yours wasn't weak either."

Azure Frost wrinkled her nose. "If I hadn't been refining the Accumulated Void Flower, preventing me from achieving full power, that move could have been even stronger!"

Accumulated Void Flower?

Luo Chen was startled, then vaguely understood something.

He had felt that during this battle with Azure Frost, her movements were not as elusive as before, and when she used her Void Concealment Divine Art, it had become traceable.

While part of the reason was that he had mastered a large number of spatial restrictions and had the assistance of his Crimson-Golden Pupils, the main cause was that Azure Frost herself had a problem.

He had never expected it to be because she was refining the Accumulated Void Flower.

According to legend, this flower could allow a cultivator to open a second Purple Mansion, and in the hands of a cultivator who practiced spatial laws, it could even open a strange internal space within the body.

That was what Azure Frost should have been doing.

But the refinement process was too long, causing flaws in her otherwise perfectly seamless combat system.

It had even affected her divine-ability killing moves!

Of course, he wouldn't show weakness in front of her either.

"That move of mine, the Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations, requires the use of Nine Firmament Qi to unleash its strongest state. What you saw was merely activated with mixed demon blood, its power less than ten or twenty percent of the original."

Azure Frost sneered. "Bragging!"

"You can try it again sometime."

Luo Chen clapped his hands and stepped back calmly.

The mixed poison on her body had been completely drained by him.

With that, both of their lingering ailments were gone, and they could quickly recover their state, thereby gaining some ability to protect themselves.

They wouldn't have to join forces to kill a mere fourth-stage early demon cultivator like Refining Silver Roc again.

For a time, there was no more noise in the underground cavern, only the faint sounds of breathing as the two regulated their health.

It was quiet and peaceful.

As if they hadn't been trying to kill each other just moments before.

...

A few days later.

Two figures in the underground cavern prepared to leave.

"Here!"

Azure Frost spoke flatly, tossing an object to Luo Chen.

As if expecting this, Luo Chen caught the object, and it disappeared into his right arm.

His gaze fell on the massive silver roc corpse beside them.

"Can't let this go to waste!"

With a thought, a wisp of blue-white flame emerged from his mouth, enveloping the roc's hundred-zhang body.

In just a moment, Refining Silver Roc's corpse turned into a pile of ashes.

In its place, the recovered Withering-Bloom Divine Fire had gained a bit more vitality.

Seeing this, Azure Frost's lips moved slightly, but she said nothing.

Luo Chen had acted first and asked for permission later. He then inquired, "This Refining Silver Roc was the younger brother of the Golden-Winged Great Peng. That Golden-Winged Great Peng went with your sister to attack the Central Continent. Were they master and servant, or were they cooperating?"

Azure Frost did not hide the truth and answered calmly, "Cooperating."

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow. "So, the other three Ascendant-level desolate beasts were also cooperating?"

"Not all of them. The Ghost Immortal Tower was sent by the Sea Emperor Whale and the Great Sage Beili. Sister Qinghe achieved the Ascendant stage thanks to my sister's kindness; she was repaying a favor. As for the Netherworld Earth Dragon, it was simply captured and forcibly enhanced in strength using secret techniques. It belongs to the enslaved category."

Luo Chen immediately understood.

Then, he smiled. "Since the Golden-Winged Great Peng is cooperating with your sister, and we not only killed his little brother but also refined his corpse, will there be any consequences?"

"You've already refined it, so why bother asking?"

Azure Frost curled her lips, said no more, and leaped out of the underground cavern.

Luo Chen felt the gradually reviving Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit in his Purple Mansion, and the heavy stone in his heart finally fell.

Looking up, he chased after her.

As soon as they emerged, the wild sandstorm around them slammed into their bodies. Luo Chen steadied himself and pursued Azure Frost's direction.

In the sky-covering, earth-blanketing sandstorm, two figures moved unhurriedly forward, leaving behind a trail of footprints.

"With this kind of terrain, and demon beasts like Refining Silver Roc, we must have ended up in the Western Desert?"

"Soul-Severing Cliff, Soul-Hanging Wasteland... worthy of being one of the top great wastelands of the Mountains and Seas Realm, famed alongside the Mirage Dragon since ancient times."

"Is there a quick way to return to the Eastern Wilderness?"

Faint, brief exchanges could be heard, but they were instantly swallowed by the howling sandstorm.

(End of chapter)
