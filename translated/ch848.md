# Chapter 848: Nether Origin Charges the Void, Yielding Danluo

After the Shouyang Discourse, the entire Lingtian Pass was plunged into a fervent atmosphere.

The final battle was approaching!

On this point alone, no one dared to slack off.

The strong from surviving sects in the rear led countless low-level cultivators, surging toward Lingtian Pass in an endless stream.

It wasn't just the Nascent Soul upper sects; a large number of Adept-level sects, and even some Initiate-level forces that couldn't even make the grade, were all included within the scope of conscription.

The number of cultivators in Lingtian Pass and the five surrounding domains, which had plummeted to below ten million after the previous city-breaking battle, was now swelling like an inflated leather ball, crazily expanding with daily supplements of tens of thousands.

Even over at the Nether Origin Sect, the number of low-level cultivators below the Nascent Soul stage had unknowingly reached a hundred thousand!

So many cultivators were being stationed in Lingtian Pass and the nearby five domains, beginning to construct defensive works, set up formation fortresses, and quietly await the assault of the demon army.

The reason for waiting in defense rather than taking the initiative to attack was actually quite easy to understand.

In terms of numbers, the human side was at an absolute disadvantage. Once the battlefield was spread too wide, they would fall into a situation of the few fighting the many, which was too dangerous.

But after the Nether Origin Sect joined the battle, in terms of high-end combat power, especially the number of Nascent Soul Sages, they had begun to surpass the demon side.

Coupled with holding such a great killer weapon as formations, waiting for the enemy to throw themselves at their doorstep was undoubtedly the best choice.

...

"Crane Emperor, you are too kind."

"It's nothing, merely a white feather I shed during my transformation, containing only a single Nascent Soul-level strike. Compared to your help in completing my transformation, it's hardly worth mentioning. If you don't accept it, that would be looking down on me."

"Yes, Alchemy Saint, just take it! Even if you have no use for it yourself, it's not bad to bestow it upon a promising disciple or junior. And this *Void-Stepping Step*, I heard you've been collecting such body-tempering techniques. Though it's incomplete, it might offer you some inspiration."

Facing the coordinated persuasion of Leisure Crane Sage and the Crane Emperor, Luo Chen smiled and generously accepted the white feather and the incomplete *Void-Stepping Step*.

Speaking of it, ever since the Crane Emperor of the White Crane Sect had fully transformed, he had always said he wanted to visit Luo Chen.

But so many years had passed, and the two had never had the chance to meet.

It was only after this Shouyang Discourse that the other party took the initiative to come calling, and even presented a rather valuable gift.

From this point of view, it was undoubtedly also stimulated by Luo Chen's greatly increased prestige, a sign that things were different now.

"The battle is imminent. The City Lord's Mansion has decided to send us back to the White Crane Domain, hoping we can fully leverage the power of the White Crane Domain we've managed for over a thousand years. For the time being, I'm afraid we won't have the chance to meet again, Fellow Daoist." Leisure Crane Sage revealed a piece of news.

Luo Chen thought for a moment. "That stands to reason."

With a large number of cultivators from rear forces pouring into Lingtian Pass, and many high-level Nascent Soul Sages stationed there, Leisure Crane Sage and the Crane Emperor were no longer as crucial.

Sending them back to the White Crane Domain was undoubtedly the most correct decision.

Then, he smiled and said, "Compared to Lingtian Pass, although the connected five domains are also targets, they are relatively safer. This trip of yours is a bit more relaxed."

Leisure Crane Sage nodded slightly, stroking his beard and smiling. "Indeed it is. It's just that you, Fellow Daoist, are highly skilled, and Lingtian Pass is reluctant to let you go, so you can't go to the rear."

Luo Chen shrugged helplessly. "There's nowhere to go even if I wanted to. Baili Qingchuan was just a temporary lodging; where else could my sect retreat to?"

As he spoke, his helpless expression gradually turned into one of high fighting spirit.

"I wasn't present at the last Lingtian Pass battle, and could only regretfully hear the news of the fall of Fellow Daoists like Xiao Shiniang and Mu Zhenren. This time, this sect master is personally here. If the demons want to enter the city, they'll have to pay a sufficient price!"

Leisure Crane Sage and the Crane Emperor exchanged a glance, their eyes showing approval.

Luo Chen's reputation as the Demon-Slaying Sage was quite resounding.

The number of fourth-tier Demon Emperors who had died under his hands was the highest among his peers. As long as he didn't encounter a commander-level Great Demon Emperor... no, judging from how Luo Chen had withstood Lei Daozi's strike at Shouyang Mountain, it seemed even a Great Demon Emperor couldn't easily kill Luo Chen!

The three chatted for a while longer until Luo Chen personally saw the two off from Danluo Mountain, ending this private meeting.

Luo Chen stood amidst the mountains, watching his old friends gradually fade into the distance, his expression filled with emotion.

Although the White Crane Domain was safe, it was only relatively so.

After the final battle, whether they could meet again depended on luck.

But regarding this decisive battle, Luo Chen was generally optimistic.

With the Nether Origin Sect taking the initiative to step up and rally everyone, the strength of the Eastern Wilderness had been consolidated, which was far better than the scattered sand of the Northern Sea cultivation world.

Dozens of Nascent Soul Sages—this alone was something the current Northern Sea cultivation world could not compare to.

Although he was optimistic about this battle, after comparing it to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, Luo Chen couldn't help but shake his head.

The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect had ruled the Central Continent for three thousand years, forging it into a solid block of iron. A single continent could field over two hundred Nascent Soul Sages, overwhelming the other four continents!

Now, they were even sending elites to vigorously conquer the Northern Sea.

Unlike the Nether Origin Sect, which was forced onto the defensive.

"Forget it. Instead of thinking so much, I'd better focus on myself."

"Many people have taken the initiative to visit me recently, boosting my prestige even further. That's a good thing. When I face the Nether Origin Sect's recruitment later, I'll have more leverage. But this does take up my cultivation time."

There were gains and losses, and Luo Chen couldn't complain too much at the moment.

He could only use his spare time to improve his strength as much as possible.

In terms of realm, even with the help of top-grade Hongyuan Pills, he couldn't cross the threshold to Nascent Soul fifth layer in a short time.

Therefore, Luo Chen focused on shoring up his weaknesses.

The Moon Step, which had previously been stuck at the perfect level, was now rapidly climbing toward the master level under his constant practice.

If he could master a master-level combat technique before the final battle, it would be a tremendous help.

Not only that, but he also took fragmented time to study and complete the Wind Leg with Wang Yuan, trying to master another body art.

This *Void-Stepping Step* came at just the right time.

...

That day.

The Luo Tian cultivators on Danluo Mountain gathered together, waiting for Luo Chen's appearance.

Soon, Luo Chen walked out.

"What's going on? Weren't you supposed to be patrolling the west city of Lingtian Pass? Why are you all back?"

Faced with Luo Chen's inquiry, Zeng Yilong stepped forward.

"The Nether Origin cultivators pulled us out."

"Oh?" Luo Chen was puzzled.

Zeng Yilong continued, "Our duties were all taken over by those female cultivators from the Divine Sound Mansion. They have numbers and don't need our help."

Not letting the Luo Tian Sect cultivators do anything—what did this mean?

Jueqing Fairy beside him said in a low voice, "Preparations for battle at Lingtian Pass. All shops have been ordered to close. Guild Master Sima Huiniang and Elder Gu are also leading their disciples toward Danluo Mountain."

This wasn't surprising. Those shops were all built in strategic locations. Once the battle started and demon beasts broke into the city, they would be very suitable defensive fortifications.

The Luo Tian Treasure Pavilion had also been included in the scope of conscription.

But when all these things were connected, Luo Chen felt something was off.

Suddenly!

His brows twitched, and he walked out of the meeting hall, his eyes gazing into the sky.

The crowd was puzzled, but when a huge flying boat flew over from the direction of the City Lord's Mansion, they realized what was happening.

Someone from the City Lord's Mansion had arrived!

Luo Chen gave a signal, and Zeng Yilong took the initiative to fly up.

"Junior Baleful Dragonzi, may I ask which senior is present?"

"I am Nether Origin Chongxuan. Tell your master to come up and speak!"

Zeng Yilong was stunned.

But after a voice transmission from Luo Chen, he immediately obediently withdrew.

A figure leaped into the sky, and under the curious gazes of the low-level Nether Origin Sect cultivators, walked toward a palace in the center of the flying boat.

As soon as he entered, several gazes were cast upon him.

A total of six people, all at the Nascent Soul stage.

Especially the one at the head, whose powerful aura of a late Nascent Soul cultivator was not concealed in the slightest.

Chongxuan Sage!

Luo Chen knew this person's reputation. He was the Nether Origin Sect reinforcement that the Nine Spirit Primogenitor had encountered back then.

This great cultivator had personally led five Nether Origin Sect Sages to expel Demon Emperors like Bi Zheng from the Xuhuang Great Domain.

Chongxuan Sage had white hair, but his face was imposing. He stared at Luo Chen and said coldly, "So you are the Alchemy Saint, Luo Chen?"

Luo Chen's expression was calm. "It is this sect master."

Chongxuan Sage's voice was loud. "I heard you took a blow from Junior Brother Zhang and came out unscathed?"

Junior Brother Zhang?

Luo Chen immediately reacted. The Junior Brother Zhang the other party spoke of was Lei Daozi.

His real name was Zhang Che. Although he had the most famous reputation among the Nether Origin Sect's traveling disciples, his time in the Dao was not long.

Therefore, despite his profound realm, his seniority within the Nether Origin Sect was not very high.

People like the Lingtian City Lord and Chongxuan Sage could all be considered his senior brothers.

Luo Chen said in a deep voice, "That is indeed the case. What, does Fellow Daoist Chongxuan also want to try his hand?"

As soon as these words were spoken, the curious gazes of the other five Nascent Soul Sages instantly turned sharp.

The atmosphere in the room, due to Luo Chen's boldness, became rather tense.

Chongxuan Sage narrowed his eyes and stared at Luo Chen for a long time.

Suddenly!

He shook his head. "There may be an opportunity to become fellow sect members in the future. Starting a fight now would only harm our harmony. I came here today only to inform you of one thing. Danluo Mountain must be handed over to us."

"Oh?"

Luo Chen let out a heavy sound of doubt.

Chongxuan Sage explained, "I'm sure you have a clear view of the upcoming battle. It's not just Lingtian Pass; the five surrounding domains are also within the attack range of the demon army. Currently, there are many strong cultivators within the pass, and we don't lack one or two Nascent Soul Sages. However, the other five domains are somewhat lacking. Therefore, you can take the Luo Tian Sect cultivators and return to Baili Qingchuan to set up defenses."

"Is this the intention of the City Lord's Mansion?"

"Yes. Don't take it the wrong way. I don't care about a mere Danluo Mountain. On the contrary, the rear is relatively safe and can avoid the first wave of impact."

Luo Chen fell silent. Not long ago, he had been telling people he would guard Lingtian Pass and make those Demon Emperors pay a price.

Seeing Luo Chen silent, Chongxuan Sage's tone softened, and he spoke persuasively.

"Alchemy Saint, perhaps you have extraordinary abilities, but what about the cultivators under your command? If you don't consider yourself, you must consider them! This arrangement is already generous enough for your Luo Tian Sect."

Someone beside him chimed in, "Yes, Alchemy Saint. I heard that the disciples of your Luo Tian Sect are few and far between, and probably can't withstand too much turmoil. Retreating to the rear is undoubtedly a good thing."

Faced with this coordinated persuasion, Luo Chen suddenly laughed.

"Since it is the intention of the Nether Origin Sect, and this Danluo Mountain was only mine to occupy for so long thanks to the Lingtian City Lord's many years of favor, then I will yield it!"

"Please wait a moment!"

After saying this, Luo Chen flicked his sleeves and left.

After he left, Chongxuan Sage frowned.

He hadn't said it was the Nether Origin Sect's intention; he had said it was the City Lord's Mansion's intention earlier.

But it didn't matter!

...

A cloud carriage, ornate but not particularly grand, slowly rose from Danluo Mountain.

Flying to a high altitude, it passed by the huge Nether Origin Sect flying boat.

Luo Chen stood on the deck of the cloud carriage, a smile on his face, nodding in greeting to Chongxuan Sage, who was looking down at Danluo Mountain from the boat.

From now on, aside from its name, this Danluo Mountain would have little to do with Luo Chen.

After the cloud carriage and the flying boat passed each other, the smile on Luo Chen's face gradually faded.

"Uncle-Master, are we going directly back to Baili Qingchuan?" Zeng Yilong asked.

"First, go pick up your two uncle-masters' wives." Luo Chen ordered calmly.

Zeng Yilong nodded and immediately went down to instruct the cultivators to change course.

Wang Yuan walked over to Luo Chen's side and asked in a low voice, "Are you alright?"

Luo Chen retorted, "What could be wrong? Retreating to the rear is a good thing for us, isn't it?"

Wang Yuan shook his head. "It's a good thing for the Luo Tian Sect. For you, I'm afraid it's not so good."

Luo Chen opened his mouth but didn't object.

If the battle started, with his abilities, as long as his luck wasn't too bad, he would surely slay demons and make a name for himself again.

Combined with the prestige he had already accumulated, he could rise to another level.

Especially now that, aside from the Nether Origin Sect, all the other major and minor forces in the Eastern Wilderness had declined significantly. He could ride this momentum to gain even higher prestige and status.

At that time, even if the Nether Origin Sect wanted to forcibly recruit him, they would have to pay a sufficient price.

But now, under the pretext of preparing for the final battle, he was being transferred away from the front line of combat. How could he accumulate battle merits or even greater fame?

He didn't know if this was a deliberate arrangement by the Nether Origin Sect or an unintentional one.

Or perhaps a deliberate unintentional one?

But he couldn't object.

Wang Yuan sighed. "We are holding you back."

Luo Chen waved his hand. "Don't say that. Without you all, there would be no me as I am today."

Wang Yuan's expression was gloomy. "But now, we have indeed become your burden."

When Luo Chen was at his weakest, relying on sticking together for warmth, the Luo Tian Sect had protected him and helped him grow.

But as Luo Chen's realm advanced by leaps and bounds, they had indeed become a burden by now.

Why could Tianduzi represent the various sects in negotiations with the Lingtian City Lord, and even get the Nether Origin Sect to aid the other sects?

Not only because he had the strength of a great cultivator, but also because he had a powerful Tiandu Sect behind him!

Why did the Shouyang Mountain Lord initiate the Shouyang Discourse, and even lift the commercial restrictions of Lingtian Pass, gaining immense prestige?

Wasn't it also because of the Dongyang Sect behind him?

But for Luo Chen, at the moment he wanted to take a step further, the weak Luo Tian Sect was holding him back.

Seeing Wang Yuan, who was always strong-willed and never admitted defeat, showing such a dejected expression, Luo Chen pursed his lips tightly.

"No!"

"It is true that the Luo Tian Sect is weak, but it is also true that I am not strong enough!"

"If I were strong enough, I alone could stand above all other cultivators!"

Wang Yuan stared blankly at Luo Chen. At this moment, he did not see the complacency of the past few days on the young man's face, but only the same insatiable ambition he had shown when his realm was lowly back then.

"Let's go. After we return, we'll complete the Wind Leg together. I have a new understanding of the *Void-Stepping Step*. Combined with the *Three Styles of the Wind God* from the Misty Sect, we might be able to deduce a new Wind Leg!"

Luo Chen said loudly, his words devoid of the dejection of being transferred away from Lingtian Pass. Instead, he was ambitious, with the air of someone about to make a big move.

Haha, it was just completing a single technique.

Wang Yuan smiled. "Then let's do it together!"

(End of Chapter)
