# Chapter 1159: The Envoy Returns Again, Immortal Realm Origin Stone

The stage of polishing apertures was not particularly difficult for Luo Chen.

This was thanks to the solid foundation he had laid during his Initiate stage.

If not for Xiangfei's fear that his polishing speed might be too fast, causing his realm to become unstable, Luo Chen's polishing speed might have been beyond imagination.

Even so!

Under the fine and meticulous control of his Blood-Refining Divine Astral, this stage was still advancing at a slow but steady pace.

Within one year, he had polished thirty-six apertures, averaging one every ten days.

The second year was even faster, reaching two every ten days, for a total of seventy-two large and small apertures polished.

Such progress drew continuous praise from Xiangfei.

However, for a certain reason, Luo Chen began to actively slow down the pace of aperture polishing starting from the third year.

The quantity of astral qi!

Put another way, this could also be understood as resources.

A martial artist's astral qi originates from the conversion of blood and qi, and blood and qi are not generated from nothing—they are refined bit by bit by drawing upon external resources.

The scarcity of resources in the Great Destruction Realm was an old, oft-repeated matter.

Even with the full support of the Relic Immortal City, and relying on Luo Chen's influence to siphon resources from the surrounding major city districts, it was still like trying to put out a blazing cartload of firewood with a single cup of water for the vast amount of blood and qi required by Luo Chen's physique.

The polished apertures could not be left idle.

They needed a portion of pure astral qi to be permanently stationed within, only then could they open and close freely, achieving a body without leaks.

Especially since Luo Chen's Blood-Refining Divine Astral was itself of extremely high quality, requiring an even greater amount of converted blood and qi, which was why Luo Chen's cultivation speed had to slow down.

Reaching this point, Luo Chen finally understood why those martial artists of the Great Destruction Realm, once they cultivated to the Leakless Realm, would all head to the Carefree City.

It wasn't just that they were forcibly conscripted; they also had the will to go willingly.

The efficiency of converting resources through food supplementation was far too low.

And that chaotic energy containing different properties, even if dangerous for martial artists, was at least a great tonic that could be more directly and easily converted into a large amount of blood and qi!

After understanding this, Luo Chen was also helpless.

But he was not in a hurry either.

The Leakless Realm was not his final destination; the higher realm that would allow him to leave the Guixu was the peak he needed to climb.

Therefore, Luo Chen did not contend for every moment.

Moreover, the formation at Red Leaf Valley still needed the polishing of time to take shape.

So, he would cultivate while waiting!

Time flowed forward like water in Luo Chen's low-efficiency cultivation life.

Until another twenty years had passed.

The Relic Immortal City welcomed the seventieth birthday of its current city lord, Jing Lan.

At the birthday banquet, guests were like clouds, and gifts piled up like mountains.

The protagonist of the grand celebration, Jing Lan, sat at the head seat, her face kind yet dignified, calmly enjoying the congratulations of one important figure after another.

At seventy years old, she had not married, and naturally had no children.

But having governed the Relic Immortal City for thirty years, even without any martial arts skills, she had accumulated enough prestige.

This was evident from the fact that important figures from the seventy-two cities came to offer their congratulations.

And in the midst of this lively scene, a name announcement shocked everyone.

"Carefree Envoy Fang Zhibai has specially come to congratulate City Lord Jing on her birthday!"

This shock was filled with countless emotions.

There was shock that the Carefree City had sent a special envoy to personally show Jing Lan such importance.

There was also fear that the Carefree City would hold Jing Lan accountable!

After all, Jing Lan relied on Luo Chen's power and prestige to sit in the city lord's position as a mortal.

Whether in legal principle or procedure, there were flaws.

If the Carefree City were to pursue the matter, then those major and minor forces that had actively befriended the Relic Immortal City over the years would likely not escape blame either.

Unexpectedly, however, after Fang Zhibai arrived, he merely offered congratulations on Jing Lan's birthday and mentioned nothing else.

Did this mean that the Carefree City was also recognizing Jing Lan's position as city lord for the first time?

Some wanted to probe through the Carefree Envoy.

But midway through the banquet, that young but immensely authoritative envoy had already disappeared.

...

Weichen Pavilion!

Luo Chen's private residence.

Two people dressed in white sat facing each other, the aroma of tea swirling before them.

Fang Zhibai looked at Luo Chen with a hint of surprise, praising him repeatedly.

"Fifty years apart, and Brother Luo's progress has been swift. From the peak of the Blood River Realm back then, you are now only one step away from the Leakless Realm."

Luo Chen's expression was calm. "Not as good as the Envoy, who has already achieved true Leakless throughout the body."

Fang Zhibai gave a wry smile. "Brother Luo flatters me. When we first met back then, I was actually already close to breaking through. That tour of the Great Destruction Realm was merely a journey to temper my will before the breakthrough."

Breaking through to the Leakless Realm seemed to only require the accumulation of astral qi and the polishing of apertures, but in reality, it also involved a heart demon barrier similar to that faced by Adept-stage cultivators breaking through to the Sage stage.

However, Luo Chen had never considered this.

In his five hundred years of cultivation in the Mountains and Seas Realm, he had experienced such situations countless times.

Therefore, there was no need to think about it at all.

Luo Chen poured a cup of tea and pushed it in front of Fang Zhibai.

"Please, have some tea!"

Fang Zhibai raised the cup and took a light sip, his eyes still unable to stop sizing up Luo Chen.

When he put down the teacup, he praised, "After leaving the Relic Immortal City back then, I went into seclusion to break through. Fortunately, my luck was good, and with the guidance of my ancestor, I successfully broke through to the Leakless Realm. At that time, I was quite proud of my achievements. But I never expected that upon leaving seclusion, I would hear of Brother Luo's feat of fighting six alone and killing five Leakless Realm experts. That arrogance of mine vanished like smoke."

Luo Chen asked, "So, has the Envoy come to the Relic Immortal City today to seek an explanation for that battle?"

"An explanation?" Fang Zhibai let out a disdainful laugh. "Just a bunch of losers. Dying at Brother Luo's hands is their honor. Do they deserve me coming to ask for an explanation?"

"Oh?"

Luo Chen examined Fang Zhibai for a moment, a rare curiosity arising in his heart.

"I wonder what business Brother Fang has here today? It couldn't possibly be just to congratulate Jing Lan on her birthday, could it?"

Facing Luo Chen's change of address from "Envoy" to "Brother Fang," Fang Zhibai's expression brightened slightly.

But when it came to official business, he had to put on a serious face.

"Fang has come today for three matters."

"Please, speak!"

Fang Zhibai tapped the table and slowly said, "First, I ask Brother Luo to show mercy and stop using force to oppress the other major cities, extracting vast resources through unequal commercial dealings."

Luo Chen did not seem surprised by this. From the moment Jing Lan had begun using his influence without restraint to launch a series of unequal cooperation treaties with the outside, oppressing many major city districts to extract resources, he knew the Carefree City would not sit idly by.

The only thing he hadn't expected was that the Carefree City had endured for a full thirty years before sending someone to mediate.

He only asked calmly, "Why?"

Fang Zhibai sighed. "The resources of every major city district are limited. Being able to support two or three Leakless Realm experts within a limited time is already a stretch. With your actions, it has greatly affected the birth of a new generation of young experts. If this continues, I'm afraid there will be a gap in the ranks of Leakless Realm experts."

Luo Chen twitched the corner of his mouth, not expecting the Carefree City to consider such a thing as sustainable development.

But he was also clear that his own cultivation's demand for resources far surpassed that of ordinary martial artists.

One portion of Blood-Refining Divine Astral was worth the condensed astral qi of a hundred or more Leakless Realm experts.

Naturally, wanting to convert enough Blood-Refining Divine Astral would also affect the growth of a large number of potential experts.

However!

Luo Chen's gaze turned cold. "Then where am I supposed to find the resources I need for cultivation?"

Fang Zhibai was also used to dealing with all kinds of people and could naturally detect the change in Luo Chen's tone.

But as a Leakless Realm expert with the backing of the Carefree City, he was not afraid of Luo Chen either.

Instead, he said forcefully, "Thirty years of indulgence is enough. Luo Chen, you should stop now!"

"Not enough." Luo Chen said decisively.

Fang Zhibai suppressed his anger. "Are you not afraid that the Carefree City will send experts to find trouble with you? Perhaps you are not yet clear, but within the Nine Rings of Destruction, there are true Martial Dao Human Immortals!"

"Martial Dao Human Immortals?" Luo Chen was not only unafraid, but a rare look of eagerness to try appeared on his face. "I would like to see what they are capable of."

Fang Zhibai was caught off guard by this answer.

But after carefully examining Luo Chen, he could only show admiration.

This person's foundation, as his own ancestor had guessed, was indeed far beyond that of ordinary cultivators.

Not only in his resource needs for martial arts cultivation, but also in the strong confidence brought by his own strength.

"Forget it!"

Fang Zhibai no longer insisted on that meaningless pride.

Or rather, in the eyes of such top-tier experts, his pride in relying on his background would only make people laugh.

He took a deep breath, and with great solemnity, took out a stone box from his bosom.

The moment he saw the stone box, Luo Chen was momentarily distracted.

But Fang Zhibai did not notice; all his attention was on the box.

He squeezed a drop of blood containing astral qi from his fingertip, let it fall onto the stone box, and then formed seals one after another.

The stone box trembled slightly, revealing streaks of red light.

A gap opened slightly, and then an inexplicable aura that instinctively made one feel a sense of longing poured out.

The moment this aura appeared, the world around them suddenly became heavy, as if trying to force the aura back inside.

Snap!

Fang Zhibai quickly closed the jade box and once again formed seals to seal the stone box.

"With this item, it should be enough to exchange for Brother Luo stopping."

Luo Chen stared blankly at the stone box.

This stone box was extraordinary, very much matching a certain precious material he had learned about from an enemy in the Mountains and Seas Realm.

The seals Fang Zhibai had just performed were also quite peculiar, clearly immortal restrictions, yet executed with astral-infused blood, bearing similarities to the blood qi restrictions he had improved and applied to Xiangfei.

But all of this was nothing compared to what he had glimpsed when the stone box was opened.

"What is that?"

"Its name is Immortal Origin Stone!"

"Immortal Origin Stone?"

"Indeed! As you can see, a fingernail-sized piece of Immortal Origin Stone contains extremely pure immortal qi. It is an existence even more precious than the legendary immortal jade."

After explaining, Fang Zhibai carefully pushed the stone box in front of Luo Chen.

"With this piece of Immortal Origin Stone, it should be enough for you to refine and advance to the Leakless Realm, right?"

Luo Chen licked his lips, his mouth and tongue feeling a bit dry.

No wonder he had instinctively felt a sense of longing just now.

It wasn't just his physical body; even his sealed divine sense had trembled for a moment.

After a cultivator reached the Ascendant stage, they could ascend and become an immortal.

Legend had it that upon reaching the Immortal Realm, one would undergo a baptism of immortal qi.

Dharma power would transform into immortal power, the primordial spirit would become nearly indestructible, the mortal body could become an immortal body, and lifespan would explode to an unimaginable degree.

He himself had once been close to that place.

But he never expected that instead of ascending, he would encounter Immortal Origin Stones, which only existed in the Immortal Realm, in this Guixu, which was said to be destined for destruction.

A fingernail-sized piece of Immortal Origin Stone was not enough to baptize his entire body at his peak, but it was more than sufficient just for breaking through to the Leakless Realm.

Looking at the stone box placed before him, Luo Chen closed his eyes.

After the incident with Rosycloud, he would never again believe in any unearned favors or goodwill.

When he opened his eyes again, there was an inexplicable change in his tone.

"Why?"

Fang Zhibai's whole body trembled, and then he murmured, "This item is given to you, firstly for the matter mentioned earlier, to prevent you from excessively squeezing the other major city districts and affecting the birth of future experts. Secondly, my ancestor thinks very highly of you, believing you have the chance to grow to his level and find a way to leave the Guixu together with him."

"Is that so?"

Fang Zhibai woke up as if from a dream, just in time to see Luo Chen muttering to himself as he picked up the jade box and examined it carefully.

Fang Zhibai was greatly alarmed. Although what he had just said was what he was supposed to say, it was clear that the state he was in was not natural or from the heart.

"What did you do to me?"

Luo Chen smiled. "Apologies, just a minor trick that can't be put on the table, to prevent someone from deceiving me."

This was no minor trick.

The move just now was a sonic art Luo Chen had summarized by drawing on Xiangfei's flute sound for soul-summoning, as well as classics he had obtained in the Mountains and Seas Realm, such as the "Immortal by the River Melody" and the Buddhist "Lion's Roar."

Actuated by astral qi, with a special rhythmic cadence, it influenced others' will, achieving an effect similar to a soul-capturing technique.

"What about the third matter?"

Fang Zhibai's expression was not very good, and naturally his tone was not pleasant either. "The third matter is that my ancestor hopes you will break through soon and come to the Carefree City to jointly explore the Great Dao. You should have guessed by now—my ancestor is none other than the Carefree City Lord!"

Luo Chen nodded. "Alright, I understand. Brother Fang, would you like to stay for dinner?"

"No, I have important matters to attend to and will leave immediately."

Fang Zhibai's mood was not very pleasant.

He was dissatisfied with Luo Chen's little trick earlier, and also alarmed that he had fallen for it so easily.

After leaving Luo's residence, he found cold sweat seeping from his back.

"No wonder even the ancestor thinks highly of him!"

...

At Weichen Pavilion.

Luo Chen was still seated.

The aroma of tea was hard to disturb his mind; his attention remained on the stone box.

After a careful examination, he confirmed that he had not been mistaken.

This stone box was cast entirely from "Void Sprout Crystal," with a self-contained space within.

It could be seen as an alternative storage treasure that could not expand space by hundreds of times!

Its advantage was that it was unaffected by the outside world, and even the divine sense of an Ascendant-stage great power could not penetrate it.

Another use of Void Sprout Crystal was known to very few people.

It could be used as food to feed a type of fourth-grade gu insect called the Vacuum Gu, accelerating its growth to the fifth grade!

And Luo Chen had one Vacuum Gu on him, hidden within his Purple Mansion.

It was obtained back then from Old Ghost Nanqi, originally the town-sect treasure of the Luo Sect in the Southern Frontier!

Of course, at this moment, Luo Chen's attention was mainly focused on the item inside the box.

"Immortal Origin Stone..."

Luo Chen's eyes narrowed slightly, his thoughts churning.

This Guixu place contained both the broken-phase blue lamp, a magical artifact he had lost during his experiments with spatial fluctuations back then, and Immortal Origin Stones, which were only produced in the Immortal Realm.

What kind of peculiar place could gather both things at the same time?

And was the legend of its eventual destruction true?

All things return to emptiness—was this the Guixu?

"Thinking hard is useless. I should refine it as soon as possible and convert it into my own strength!"
