# Chapter 753: Waiting by the Stump for the Rabbit, Effortlessly Done

Rustle!

A clear wind blew past, rolling through the branches and leaves of the forest. From afar, it looked like layer upon layer of waves.

Luo Chen sat cross-legged on a blue stone jutting out from the mountain, overlooking the rolling tree waves below his feet, then looked back toward the cave abode behind him, hesitation in his eyes.

"Is the wind moving them, or is she moving them?"

Three or four months had already passed since Fu Qinglan began absorbing the illusion core to cultivate.

During this time, she had not taken a single step outside, and Luo Chen had remained constantly at the cave abode's entrance, guarding it without leaving.

At first, nothing had seemed unusual, but gradually Luo Chen sensed something strange.

Inside the cave abode, Fu Qinglan's cultivation seemed completely undefended?

Her Nascent Soul had directly escaped the Purple Mansion and was swallowing the spiritual qi of heaven and earth in great mouthfuls with its true body.

Such behavior, under ordinary circumstances, was basically courting death!

Without the Purple Mansion's protection, exposing the Nascent Soul like this while entering deep cultivation was simply waiting for this heaven and earth to assimilate her.

Yet the other party had no scruples whatsoever!

At that time, Luo Chen remembered certain things Fu Chaosheng had once said.

The top-tier Great Desolate Mirage Dragon swallowed clouds and exhaled mist, transforming sun, moon, and stars, and its body formed a grotto-heaven of its own. This world was nearly no different from the outside world, yet because it was isolated by the Mirage Dragon's own fleshly body, a special membrane had formed.

If cultivators cultivated within it, they could avoid being assimilated by heaven and earth to the greatest degree.

Even merging the spirit with heaven and earth was the same!

Thus, Luo Chen understood why Fu Qinglan was now acting so recklessly.

But another problem followed!

Although the spiritual qi in this world was abundant, it was not condensed and concentrated. If the only purpose was to borrow the advantage of not being assimilated by heaven and earth and deliberately enter the grotto-heaven to cultivate, and this chance came only once every five hundred years, was that truly necessary?

Fu Qinglan's later changes gradually gave Luo Chen an explanation.

The other party was not refining qi and cultivating at all. Instead, through the Nascent Soul as an intermediary vessel, she was causing her divine soul to interact constantly with heaven and earth, attempting to comprehend a certain thing.

Luo Chen knew that thing was the so-called true intent of laws.

Only, this cultivation mode was often something only late-stage Sage great cultivators would carry out. For Fu Qinglan to begin this step so early would probably be futile.

Yet everything became different once she had the special resource of a fourth-tier illusion core.

After Fu Qinglan persisted in cultivation for several months, it seemed she had truly come into contact with some sort of law true intent?

At present.

Luo Chen watched the changes in the outside world while also observing Fu Qinglan's cultivation.

After several months passed, he had already understood certain clues.

"No wonder the chance from the five-hundred-year grand competition is not given to those older-generation great cultivators who are only one step away from the Ascendant realm, but is instead specially reserved for newly advanced Sages like Fu Qinglan."

"No wonder, before entering Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, aside from consolidating their realms, their sect elders ordered them not to cultivate excessively."

"Everything is to preserve that state of having just entered the Sage realm!"

When cultivators first enter the Sage realm, they subconsciously draw close to heaven and earth, and there is not much separation between them.

In later cultivation, however, they continuously strengthen the Purple Mansion barrier to prevent themselves from being assimilated by heaven and earth.

In this way, their realm steadily rises, and their safety is also guaranteed.

But at the same time, they gradually grow estranged from heaven and earth, thereby losing some of the Nascent Soul's instincts.

Put this way, it might be somewhat obscure and difficult to understand.

Take a human infant as an example!

Before an infant is born, it dwells all along in amniotic fluid, and at that time humans naturally know how to swim.

But in the process of growing after birth, in order to avoid drowning, the instinct to swim is constantly suppressed, and they also grow increasingly resistant to water, gradually becoming unable to swim.

If they want to grasp this innate skill again, they instead have to undergo acquired learning and training.

If one compared the Nascent Soul to an infant, heaven and earth to water, and comprehending the true intent of laws to swimming, it became easy to understand.

This was the intention of the Tianyuan Dao Sect's sages.

They wanted the new generation of Sage geniuses, while their Nascent Souls had not yet rejected heaven and earth and still retained their instinctive closeness, to grasp the ability to "swim freely" within heaven and earth.

They might even achieve it in one stroke and truly grasp a law true intent suited to them!

And in this process, the trace of spiritual impulse inside a fourth-tier illusion core served much the same purpose as the tube that supplied oxygen to an infant in the womb.

"The Tianyuan sages truly put in painstaking effort and thought far ahead!"

Luo Chen sighed with great feeling.

This was the advantage of possessing a master's lineage and inheritance.

Even how to cultivate afterward, how to take shortcuts, and how to grasp powerful forces in advance had all been handed down to later generations one by one.

He could only envy it, but he could not give rise to any covetous thoughts.

It was simple. Those methods certainly contained many key tricks.

For example, how to use Mirage Dragon power, how to open the illusion core and absorb that trace of heavenly secret, and the related methods for borrowing that wisp of heavenly secret to comprehend laws, and so on.

Most importantly, every illusion core in Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven belonged to Tianyuan Dao Sect disciples!

Dao protectors like them could only look, not use them!

If they truly seized them by force, once they left, they would have more than they could bear.

Leaving aside those Ascendant great powers, the Tianyuan Dao Sect's hundreds of Sages alone were not something Luo Chen dared look down upon.

What he could do now was simply eat a few Hongyuan Pills prepared in advance to raise his cultivation a little while helping guard her.

From this point of view, Luo Chen was not entirely without anything to do.

Inside the grotto-heaven, spiritual qi was scattered and distributed unevenly, making it less suitable for Sage cultivation, and the result was only half the gain for twice the effort.

But with high-grade Hongyuan Pills assisting him, Luo Chen's cultivation would not be held back.

Adding the strand of origin qi bestowed upon him when the Alchemy Saint ascended earlier, he felt that breaking through to the second level of Sage was already very close.

...

Another two months passed.

On this day, crisp footsteps came from inside the cave abode.

Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes and looked toward the person arriving.

Fu Qinglan pressed her lips together, her face full of disappointment.

"Did you fail?"

"Mm. I have disappointed Dao Brother."

"No matter. We only need to find another fourth-tier illusion core!"

Luo Chen said this calmly, then rose to his full height, his white robe fluttering lightly.

Fu Qinglan opened her mouth, but in the end, she could only nod with some difficulty.

"I will trouble Dao Brother. Only, fourth-tier illusion beasts are hard to find. I am afraid we must go out and slowly search for them."

Unexpectedly!

Luo Chen merely smiled faintly, as if he did not care about this problem at all.

"Come with me."

Fu Qinglan was puzzled. She rose into the air and followed Luo Chen.

Only, when manipulating the spiritual qi of the outside world, she actually staggered for an instant.

Luo Chen stopped and looked at her with a frown.

Fu Qinglan hurriedly explained, "My Nascent Soul was outside for a long time. Now that it has returned, my communication with the outside world's spiritual qi is a little unsmooth. It is nothing serious."

Luo Chen nodded, accepting her explanation.

The true body of the Nascent Soul could naturally control the spiritual qi of heaven and earth with ease, but after being outside for a long time, then suddenly returning, the physical body existed as an intermediary, so things would become unfamiliar. This was very normal.

Thinking of this, he also understood why this hundred-year grand competition was entirely different from ordinary hundred-year grand competitions and required Dao protectors to participate.

At this moment, people like Fu Qinglan were at their most fragile. Their hearts were set wholly on seeking the Dao, and they lacked the strength to protect themselves, requiring others to guard them.

Only, this made him very curious. Back then, what had Shi Ju relied on to dare enter the grotto-heaven alone without bringing a Dao protector? Although later, when he joined the grand competition, he had brought Qi Xiongcheng, his initial decision proved that Shi Ju truly did possess such confidence and could protect himself.

What was he relying on?

...

Eighty li away from Fu Qinglan's cultivation cave abode.

There was a vast plain, quite broad in scope, ten li in both length and width, roughly equivalent to a small town.

Yet from the angle of view, this plain did not seem especially wide.

That was only because three great mountains surrounded it in an encircling formation, wrapping it tightly and leaving only one exit.

Luo Chen and Fu Qinglan stopped here.

Fu Qinglan looked around in confusion. "Is there a fourth-tier illusion beast here? It is hidden so deeply that I did not discover it even after arriving right before it?"

Luo Chen's expression was calm. He bent down and placed his hand on the ground covered in green grass.

"There is none now, but there will be before long."

A jade box lined with soft silk was placed on the ground by him.

Fu Qinglan's gaze focused, and her expression changed slightly.

Inside the jade box, a white pill emitted dense light.

If she had not seen wrongly, it should be one of the five pills personally refined by the Alchemy Saint before her ascension.

Why was Luo Chen taking this thing out now?

For such a precious object, should he not preserve it carefully?

Amid her confusion, she happened to see Luo Chen straighten and look back at her, his gaze carrying a meaningful quality.

Fu Qinglan froze, then shook her head.

"Dao Brother Luo Chen, do not worry. This is your possession, and this little sister has absolutely no covetous intent."

Luo Chen twitched the corner of his mouth and said nothing.

Instead, he leaped upward into the sky.

His ten fingers flew, and restriction after restriction shot from his hands into the three surrounding mountains.

Not only that, one small triangular flag after another shot into the ground like arrows.

Fu Qinglan blinked. Luo Chen was actually proficient in formation-setting arts as well?

Moreover, judging from the calm ease with which he acted, he had clearly already gained sufficient understanding of the trend of the mountains, rivers, and earth veins in this place.

Only, relying merely on these magic-power restrictions and the formation laid down with formation flags, could he limit a fourth-tier illusion beast that gathered and scattered without form?

The next moment, Fu Qinglan's pupils shrank.

In the void, a small gray cauldron spun out.

The cauldron mouth inverted, covering this stretch of the void like a lid.

The white-robed Daoist pointed at the small gray cauldron, and the little cauldron immediately vanished into the void, no longer showing any trace.

"Next, we only need to wait by the stump for the rabbit."

Luo Chen said this from the sky.

Fu Qinglan's gaze moved continuously between him and the glowing mass of the open jade box on the ground, her face half convinced and half doubtful.

...

One day.

Two days.

As time gradually passed, the doubt on Fu Qinglan's face grew stronger and stronger.

At last, she could not help seeking out Luo Chen, who was cultivating quietly on a mountain peak.

"Dao Brother, can this truly work?"

Luo Chen opened his eyes and calmly stared at her.

He said nothing, simply staring like that until the other party's heart grew uneasy.

After a moment, Luo Chen suddenly sighed.

"Since we have nothing to do, would you tell me about cultivation in the Tianyuan Dao Sect? Luo has been a drifting cloud and wild crane since childhood, and I am quite curious about sect cultivation."

As he spoke, he waved a hand, and a table and a cushion appeared before him.

On the table were wine, dried meat, and some fragrant beans.

Fu Qinglan hesitated a little, then sat down.

Luo Chen filled her cup, and she began talking about some anecdotes of sect cultivation, taking it as satisfying Luo Chen's curiosity while also passing the boring time.

"When I was twelve years old, I was tested and found to possess dual water and wood spirit roots. I passed the Dao Sect's test and entered the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range to cultivate. From then until now, it has already been more than two hundred years, almost three hundred."

"Cultivation within the sect is actually very dull and tedious."

"Every day there are morning lessons, evening lessons, reciting classics, opening meridians and guiding qi..."

"There are seventy-two famous peaks in the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range, and basically each is occupied by a Sage for cultivation. Most of these Sages also come from aristocratic clans and great houses. We do not mainly rely on master-disciple transmission. Aside from listening to Dao Sect elders lecture, ordinary cultivation is carried out individually."

"If there is truly anything interesting, it is probably the small competitions held within each clan every ten years, and then the whole-sect grand competition once every hundred years. At that time, the outstanding juniors of each realm from the great clans all gather to compete. Heh, this kind of matter counts as top priority to us, but in Dao Brother's eyes, I imagine it is just children playing house."

"These beans are delicious... bitter hazelnuts? They are handled so well that they are not inferior to some of my sect's exquisite foods."

"It was not as if everything along the way was smooth sailing. The sect periodically issues missions. Central Continent has no demons, but there are still demon beasts entrenched in some places near Central Continent, and the sect regularly issues such missions for us to carry out. In addition, there are all sorts of other missions, large and small. The more distant ones even require going to other continents..."

"Because the Tianyuan Dao Sect is mainly composed of cultivation clans, internal oppression within the sect is not so severe, but struggles between clans have never stopped. The reason my grandfather went to the Northern Sea to serve as an elder of the merchant alliance was also because he wanted to use the status of a merchant alliance elder to avoid clan competition and thereby buy me time to cultivate to the Sage stage."

"From this point of view, Grandfather truly protected me too well. If this little sister usually does anything childish, I ask Dao Brother to be more tolerant."

...

Luo Chen drank wine while listening to Fu Qinglan speak of sect cultivation.

The other party spoke with some enthusiasm, but halfway through, Luo Chen found it somewhat dull.

Quiet, detached, with few storms.

Even when there was occasional competition, it mostly remained within the rules.

Even Fu Qinglan, after long years of cultivation made her feel bored, had gradually searched for things to pass the time, such as go.

But later, Luo Chen's attention focused again.

He could not help imagining: under circumstances without large-scale life-and-death battles, after the Tianyuan Dao Sect had rested and built strength for several thousand years, how many cultivators of every realm had it accumulated?

And although Central Continent was vast, could it truly accommodate so many cultivators dividing up these resources?

Combining this with the matters of the Northern Sea's Penglai Immortal Sect and the Eastern Wilderness's Ghost God Valley, Luo Chen had no choice but to think in the worst direction. The crouching tiger that was Central Continent was probably already unable to resist breaking free of its cage and reaching toward the other continents of the Mountains and Seas Realm.

Suddenly.

Luo Chen waved his hand, and the wine and snacks between the two vanished.

"It is here!"

He rose lightly, and the spiritual qi of heaven and earth around him began converging toward him.

Fu Qinglan followed his gaze into the distance.

Between the mountains and ridges, a streak of white light came racing like wind and lightning.

Without the slightest confusion, it rushed straight toward this ten-li plain!

She looked at Luo Chen with eyes full of wonder.

The formation he had laid had magnified the Heavenly Secret Pill's medicinal power and spread it across a thousand li, while controlling it at an extremely suitable degree so that only high-level illusion beasts could sense it.

Now it seemed he had truly waited by the stump until the rabbit arrived.

And Luo Chen's soft laugh confirmed her thought even more.

"It really is a rabbit!"

That white light was extremely fast, but as it ran, it hopped and leaped, crossing several li with each bound.

Looking closely, amid the gathering and scattering cloud qi, it had two pointed ears and lively features, no different from a mundane rabbit.

This rabbit arrived extremely quickly, rushing into the plain surrounded by three mountains without regard for anything, heading straight for the jade box resting quietly on the ground.

It was at this moment that the jade box abruptly vanished.

The rabbit froze, then raised its head and looked toward the sky.

A giant fire phoenix spread its wings and pounced down toward it with a shriek.

The rabbit grew anxious.

Large amounts of cloud qi gathered, and its body began swelling madly.

Five zhang, ten zhang, fifty zhang, a hundred zhang...

A giant rabbit more than a hundred zhang tall charged angrily at the fire phoenix.

In Fu Qinglan's eyes, however, she seemed to have already seen its ending.

"It is merely seeking death!"

Boom!

The giant rabbit and the fire phoenix collided.

Enormous spiritual qi fluctuations surged outward in all directions like waves.

The fire phoenix gradually dispersed.

The giant rabbit, meanwhile, was blasted into cloud and mist that filled the sky.

Within it, a condensed streak of white light appeared and disappeared.

Luo Chen stood high in the void, looking at this scene, and nodded inwardly.

Just as he had thought, a fourth-tier illusion beast's strength was at most comparable to an early-stage Sage cultivator who had not grasped any formidable methods. Its only troublesome point was probably its characteristic of gathering and scattering without form, making it difficult to kill.

And the Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain he had cast was enough to rival an ordinary early-stage Sage cultivator's full-force strike.

Under circumstances where he had prepared in advance, scattering its body was not difficult.

The true difficulty should come afterward.

Fu Qinglan said anxiously, "It wants to run!"

Luo Chen waved a hand. "It cannot run."

Luo Chen clenched his hand in the void!

The next moment, a giant cauldron descended rumbling from the void, completely covering this stretch of sky.

Not only that, the three surrounding mountains also emitted sheets of light, sealing the ten-li plain completely.

Within it, a streak of white light charged left and right, cloud and mist scattering then gathering, gathering then scattering.

After a stalemate lasting the time of one incense stick, it finally lost strength to continue.

"Collect!"

The cauldron mouth opened wide, dragging that illusion core inside. Afterward, the Primordial Unity Cauldron rapidly shrank and finally landed in Luo Chen's hand.

Watching this scene, Fu Qinglan could not help praising, "Dao Brother's magic treasure truly possesses formidable power of suppression and sealing. Even among true artifacts, I imagine it ranks near the front!"

"You flatter me. Compared with your noble sect's Rotten Axe-Handle Go Board, it is no more than the light of a firefly."

As Luo Chen said this, he grabbed the white rabbit illusion core.

If Lin Jingtang had been here and seen Luo Chen hunt a fourth-tier illusion beast so easily, his jaw would probably have dropped in shock.

He had spent enormous effort just to defeat that withered-tree illusion beast, yet still allowed its illusion core to escape.

Luo Chen, however, had expended hardly any effort at all.

"Take it."

Luo Chen tossed it, and the illusion core landed in Fu Qinglan's hand.

"With this one, I imagine comprehending the true intent of laws will be a bit easier for you."

Fu Qinglan tightly gripped the illusion core in her small hand and nodded heavily.

"I absolutely will not fail Dao Brother's high hopes!"

The two returned as if nothing at all had happened on this trip.

Half a year later.

In the mountainside cave abode, Fu Qinglan walked out again, as if nothing had happened.

Her face was utterly dispirited.

Seeing her like this, Luo Chen's brows could not help drawing deeply together, almost forming the character for "river."

"Still not enough?"

*(End of Chapter)*
