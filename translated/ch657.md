# Chapter 657: Luo Chen, Are You Willing to Enter My Pill Hall?

In the quiet corridor, three sets of footsteps echoed crisply.

Suddenly, there was a crack.

Ding Yi stopped and looked at that bone.

"So this is the so-called Turtle Ruins Land?"

That bone was clearly the remains left behind after the death of a desolate beast Black Tortoise.

Cave Master Moyun sighed at the side. "Over tens of millions of years, large numbers of Black Tortoise corpses were buried here, thereby forming the Void Burning Wind. That True Lord used artifact-forging and formation methods to expand this wind until it covered the entire Sunken Sea. It truly is inconceivable great divine ability!"

As he spoke, he and Ding Yi tacitly glanced from the corners of their eyes at Luo Chen walking in the middle.

They saw the other party looking straight ahead, seemingly not interested in these Black Tortoise remains at all.

Of course, the Black Tortoise remains scattered in the corridor had long since become waste material and were of no use. The true place where the Void Burning Wind formed and expanded was not here.

At this moment, Luo Chen was rapidly conversing with Han Zhan without the two noticing.

"Senior already knew this place was the fall site of a Void Refining True Lord?"

"I had this guess. Otherwise, I would not have taken the initiative to come."

"On what basis?"

"Very simple. Do you remember what I told you back then about secret realms?"

Luo Chen's memories surged, and he gradually found the clue.

"You once said that the death place of an Ascendant great power is not something Sages can easily enter. Because inside are the law powers the great power comprehended in life, and they can easily affect Sage cultivators who have not mastered law power."

"Correct. That is how it is. Your memory is not bad." Han Zhan smiled, and his tone carried a little more anticipation. "So when I learned of ruins like these, where even an Ascendant Holy Land was helpless, yet Sages could enter and leave, I understood that they had to be a secret realm left behind by at least an existence above Ascendant."

Luo Chen did not understand. "Would the burial place of a True Lord not affect Sage cultivators' cultivation?"

"You, in the end, do not understand the two words Void Refining."

"Oh? This junior is willing to hear the details."

Han Zhan did not stint on instruction. "So-called Void Refining means refining illusory laws into concrete objects, gaining absolute control over the mysterious and unfathomable power of laws. Therefore, this realm is also called the Phenomenon Realm or Law Manifestation Realm. Under that terrifying control, even after death, the laws left behind will not affect those who have not comprehended laws. Instead, Ascendant cultivators who have just begun touching the Dao of laws will suffer absolute suppression."

Void Refining? Laws? Phenomenon? Absolute control?

Luo Chen's head swelled listening to these high-realm terms that he seemed to understand yet not understand.

He could not help asking, "Senior is only one realm above me, and you are not a Holy Land disciple. Why do you know these secrets so well?"

"This counts as detailed?" Han Zhan laughed softly. "It is merely the simplest summary."

After a pause, he continued, his words carrying a few sighs.

"The founding ancestor of my Falling Cloud Sect obtained a corresponding inheritance from an ancient cultivator's cave residence and only then established the sect. Among that ancient cultivator's relics were descriptions of these realms. This old man is only repeating another's wisdom."

So that was how it was.

In the ancient human-demon war, countless ancient cultivators had fallen.

The seas changed into mulberry fields, people and things passed away, and who knew how many accomplished True Cultivators were buried in the dust of history.

Later generations who obtained their legacies might rise for a time or establish sects. All of it was fortune.

In the past, Luo Chen feared secret realms and dangerous lands, not because he was unmoved, but because he remembered that risks existed alongside gains.

So-called ancient cultivator ruins were not watched by him alone.

Even this time, he planned to withdraw depending on the situation once he obtained the Five Elements Lotus Platform.

However, Luo Chen still had many doubts in his heart.

"Then Ding Yi said the ancient cultivator who died in this Demon-Fall Land may have been the strongest Void Refining True Lord from ancient times to now. I do not know why he would be called the strongest. And how could such a powerful figure fall in this realm? Isn't the Mountains and Seas Realm only able to support cultivation up to the Ascendant stage? How could it contain him? In addition, where does the word 'demon' come from? Could this person also have been a demonic cultivator? If so, the ruins he left behind may not be so friendly."

Luo Chen had asked these many doubts before, but Ding Yi could not give concrete answers.

At this moment, Han Zhan also seemed unable to figure it out and remained silent for a long time.

Silent...

"Hm?"

Luo Chen's footsteps stopped, and he abruptly raised his head.

Blood flowed everywhere, and corpses piled like mountains.

A young man stood with a sword in hand, back facing Luo Chen.

He slowly turned around and looked at Luo Chen in confusion.

"Devil Lord Azure Yang? Luo Hai?"

Looking at that unfamiliar yet somewhat familiar face, Luo Chen hesitantly said, "He Yuan?"

But in the next instant, a terrifying feeling rushed to the top of his head.

"Blood Nightmare Moluo!"

Blood Nightmare Moluo nodded. "It seems you still remember this old man."

His sword tip trembled and pointed diagonally at Luo Chen.

"I will give you another chance. Submit to this old man."

Luo Chen frowned and did not hesitate at all.

The Golden Core in his body rotated, the Primordial Unity Cauldron amplified it, and vast mana immediately spread out.

The All-Encompassing Fire Prison appeared again.

Luo Chen stood within it, raised a hand, and struck a palm through the air.

An enormous hand seal surged out grandly.

Blood Nightmare Moluo wanted to resist, but under the fire prison, he was shocked to find his mana restless and difficult to mobilize.

He hurriedly dodged.

However, the azure great hand seal was already overhead.

Boom!

With a great sound, Luo Chen stepped to his side and looked at the broken, dying young man, shaking his head slightly.

"If Blood Nightmare were before me, perhaps I would need to deal with him carefully. But you are only an incarnation, and still dare speak so wildly? Heh..."

With a cold laugh, azure flames bloomed in his hand, and the man beneath his feet vanished into ash.

Luo Chen did not stop. His figure fled toward the distance.

At some point, the sky had dimmed, and a bright moon gradually hung high.

Luo Chen looked up at the moon. Beneath his feet was a small island surrounded by a ten-li calm lake.

Raising a cup to invite the bright moon; with my shadow, we become three.

A sallow-faced man knelt half before Luo Chen. "Fellow Daoist, spare Yan's life! You attacked Moon Invitation Island this time only for this cultivation place. Yan can hand it over. I only ask to preserve my life!"

Looking at Yan Nantian's lowered head, Luo Chen's expression showed slight movement.

After a long while, he said coldly, "I can spare your life, but you must submit to me and serve me for one hundred years. If you refuse, this seat's sword will not spare you."

As he spoke, the Profound Fire Sword in his hand rose slightly.

Facing this shameless bandit who had forcibly seized his property and now wanted him to submit, Yan Nantian's face was ugly. But after a long silence, he ultimately agreed powerlessly.

Luo Chen smiled.

"In this way, I gain one more Golden Core cultivator to run for me. Excellent."

Without him noticing, the aura on his body continuously scattered and soon fell below Golden Core.

Descending from the sky, he arrived in a rugged, rocky place.

Ahead was an empty valley surrounded by mountains.

Several Foundation Establishment True Cultivators fought separately.

Luo Chen charged to the sky with sword in hand, aiming to behead a man.

That man looked terrified. "I am the son of a Golden Core cultivator of the Flame Alliance. You cannot kill me!"

The sword stopped at the man's neck.

Yan Leizi panted heavily and quickly explained his background.

Opposite him, Luo Chen's expression shifted uncertainly as he listened.

"Provoking a Golden Core cultivator for this person is probably not worth it."

He put away the Profound Fire Sword. Yan Leizi, as if relieved of a great burden, thanked him and hurriedly left.

When he reached a very distant place, he looked back resentfully.

"Dan Chenzi, I will remember you. Wait for death!"

Half a month later, Luo Chen, who was slaughtering on the battlefield, encountered an aged old man who stepped through the air carrying golden clouds filling the sky.

"Luo Chen, pay for my son's life!"

Luo Chen's expression was terrified. "I spared him. You cannot kill me!"

The old man laughed contemptuously. He pointed with fingers like a sword, and the golden clouds filling the sky immediately transformed into golden swords that broke through the air.

Luo Chen fled in panic, and Chu Kui at his side helped him resist.

However, this time, another mirror appeared in the old man's hand, fixing Chu Kui in place. Golden swords like swimming fish landed before Luo Chen.

Looking at the sword tips about to reach him, Luo Chen's eyes widened.

"No!"

With a shout, Luo Chen abruptly woke before a desk.

A graceful woman worriedly helped him up.

"Chairman, what happened?"

Luo Chen was drenched in sweat. Looking at the worried Sima Huiniang, he continuously panted.

"I had a dream. I dreamed I was hunted by a Golden Core High Man and almost died. In that dream..."

He spoke on and on, and Huiniang listened quietly.

Only after a long while did the woman's voice sound in the silent Luo Heaven Hall.

"Chairman, you are under too much pressure."

"All the Luo Heaven Creed's burdens press on you, which is why you had such a nightmare. Actually, there is no need. Now you manage external matters and I manage internal matters. We also have a high person like Heartless Fairy escorting us. Our Luo Heaven Creed will certainly become better and better."

Listening to the gentle woman's comfort, Luo Chen gradually calmed.

But looking at the woman's gorgeous palace dress, ink-black hair, and the patch of snow-white skin at her chest, he involuntarily swallowed.

The woman seemed to sense it and gently leaned against the man's shoulder.

"Chairman, the sky is already late. Do not go back."

Luo Chen had just wanted to wrap an arm around her shoulder when the scene from the dream suddenly emerged in his heart.

Thinking of the long immortal path, where even he could not guarantee his own safety, why drag another into the water?

He sighed softly and gently pushed away the woman's fragrant shoulder.

"Chairman, I..."

"It is not your fault. You are very good."

Luo Chen rose and left the hall.

Inside the solemn hall, the bustle of the Foundation Establishment ceremony during the day no longer remained. There was only cold quiet and faint sobbing.

When Luo Chen walked out of the hall, he felt empty inside.

Looking at the bright moon outside, he seemed to feel that he had lost something.

At this moment, an old man looked over with a smile.

"Luo Chen, what do you think?"

What did he think?

Luo Chen's drifting gaze landed beside the old man, on the weak young girl holding a relative's corpse and crying.

It was Mi Li.

Without thinking, Luo Chen said, "Naturally, kill her!"

Chi!

A sword crossed the air.

The young girl died with eyes open.

Qin Liangchen at the side widened his eyes and looked at Luo Chen in disbelief. "You!"

"Me?"

Luo Chen's mind shook. "How could this happen?"

The old man Miao Wen walked past him, patted his shoulder, and cold words fell into his ear.

"You are very good."

Under that cold gaze, that gaze of disbelief, and those dead eyes that could not close, Luo Chen fell into chaos.

No!

It should not have been like this!

How could it be like this?

The aura on his body also continuously scattered amid this chaos.

He staggered backward, feeling his head ache as if it would split.

As he retreated, Luo Chen rubbed his head, only to discover that he was moving forward.

The bright moon was still that bright moon.

On the open bluestone path, a wealthy-looking old man suddenly turned. Half his face was covered by shadow, while the other half smiled at Luo Chen.

"You are Luo Chen, yes?"

"In my Broken Mountain Gang, there are now the Seven Tigers Hall, and another Jiao Hall, with Wang Yuan as hall master. Only the Pill Hall lacks a master."

"Are you willing to enter my Pill Hall?"

*(End of Chapter)*
