# Chapter 1058: Heavenly Tribulation Seizes the Treasure, Thousand Skies Converge

The sky roared with wind, black clouds spreading thickly.

The earth was ringed by majestic, towering mountains.

Palaces dotted the continuous mountain ranges in twos and threes. Today, many cultivators had emerged from their palaces, gazing up at the sky above the Tinnan Dao Palace.

Their eyes were fixed on the central Heavenly Origin Observatory. There, a white-clad figure sat cross-legged in the void, drifting ethereally like an immortal descended from heaven.

Beneath the oppressive dark clouds, the cultivators looked upon that white-robed man with complex emotions.

There was reverence, admiration, and even envy and jealousy.

The great battle at the East Sea coast had raged on for three years, one after another.

After the Zhenhai City Lord, Sage Wen, joined forces with the Houtu Hall Master, Bluejade Fairy, to defeat the three Great Demon Emperors, the outcome of the subsequent war was already sealed.

The reason it dragged on so long was that the various Sages no longer intervened, leaving the opportunities to the lower-ranked cultivators, especially the true disciples of the Dao Palace.

This could be seen as a test!

A different kind of sect competition!

Measured by the number of demons killed, cultivation level, and even the recovery of lost territory, it would determine the ranking of strength among this generation of Tinnan Dao Palace true disciples.

In this way, they could eliminate the demon threat while tempering the combat experience of the younger disciples without internal conflict.

And in these three years of demon-hunting trials, the white-robed man's performance was the most outstanding!

He first defended Liubo Mountain at the early Adept realm. Then, he survived an attack from a desolate beast that had broken through the sixth level of the Sea-Suppressing Gate. His subsequent achievements in hunting demon beasts were also extremely brilliant, whether in terms of quantity or the number of Demon King-level experts, far surpassing his peers among the true disciples.

So, by the time the true disciples returned to the Tinnan Dao Palace, the white-robed man was already considered the nominal number one of his generation.

The reason it was only nominal was that there were still a batch of powerful Adept true disciples who had not participated in the battle at the East Sea coast.

But no one cared about that!

Because the white-robed man was too young. Having advanced to the Adept realm at the mere age of forty-something, he was already an unprecedented genius in the Mountains and Seas Realm for a thousand years. With his monstrous cultivation talent, it was only a matter of time before he caught up to the realms of his senior brothers and sisters.

As for his strength, no one doubted him.

In the three years of demon-hunting trials, the white-robed man's divine sense and magical power were unmatched. Even his combat skills improved at a visible, frenzied rate. A Great Demon King was easily slain by his precious ruler under his very eyes. That dazzling combat talent seemed innate, drawing everyone's attention.

To praise his outstanding performance in the demon-hunting trials and to encourage him to continue his efforts, the Observatory Master, Zhan Bingtao, decided to personally forge his lifebound magical treasure!

Yes, despite such insane performance, he didn't even have a lifebound treasure yet.

Such a grand spectacle today was precisely for the purpose of refining a magical treasure!

"Zhou Fuyou, are you ready?"

A vigorous, rough voice came from the Heavenly Origin Observatory below.

That was the Heavenly Origin Observatory Master, Zhan Bingtao, the current strongest cultivator on the Tinnan Continent, and also a master of artifact refinement!

A good warrior cannot be without weapons. When it came to refining magical treasures and weapons, the Zhan family, one of the Twelve Great Houses, had always been the leader, and Zhan Bingtao was the best of the best.

Zhou Fuyou sat cross-legged in the void, his eyes half-open, half-closed.

"Observatory Master, this disciple is ready!"

"Then let us begin!"

With these words, a faint light slowly emerged from the top of Zhou Fuyou's head, flying towards the boundless sky shrouded in black clouds.

At the same time, from the four cardinal directions, a powerful stream of magical power struck from afar, pouring into the Heavenly Origin Observatory.

Layers of formation light paths unfolded, and Zhan Bingtao let out a low shout.

"Heavenly light reveals the sun, divine thunder descends upon the world, open!"

Boom!

In the next instant, a massive pillar of light, as thick as a water bucket, shot up from the Heavenly Origin Observatory.

As if in response, within the thick black clouds, lightning danced like dragons and serpents, silver light flickered, and a grand bolt of thunder also emerged with a roar.

This scene startled quite a few people.

Heavenly tribulation!

This celestial phenomenon was almost identical to the heavenly tribulation drawn when a cultivator broke through to the Sage realm.

The Heavenly Origin Observatory Master, Zhan Bingtao, truly lived up to his reputation as a master of artifact refinement. By joining forces with the four Hall Masters, he could forcibly draw forth the divine thunder of heavenly tribulation.

For a moment, many low-level cultivators breathed rapidly. If only the magical treasure being refined by the combined efforts of the five Sages were theirs!

The pillar of light and the thunderbolt connected in an instant, enveloping the faint light that Zhou Fuyou had released earlier.

Not only that, but from all directions, snakes and dragons of lightning, baring their fangs and claws, came rushing towards that faint light.

Zhou Fuyou watched calmly, his magical power fully raised, ready to collect the treasure at any moment.

The precious materials used in refining this Sea-Calming Pearl were unimaginable to ordinary people.

Just one of the main ingredients, the Water Lotus Seed, was a treasure produced from the Five Elements Lotus Platform, and its grade far exceeded that of an ordinary fourth-grade Five Elements Lotus Platform. According to the Observatory Master's inspection, that Water Lotus Seed could only have been produced by a Lotus Platform that was about to reach the fifth grade.

As for the other materials, they were not cheap either.

There was the Withered-Source Fire Marrow Diamond left by the ancestor Zhou Yunshen, and a small piece of top-grade Nine Yang Geng Metal.

There was the Ginseng-Tong Wood gifted by his master, Bluejade Fairy.

Finally, the Observatory Master, Zhan Bingtao, had expended great effort to obtain a piece of Reverse Scale from the Netherworld Earth Dragon that had been slain by the combined forces of the Zhan family's experts back in the day. Although the Reverse Scale was inferior to the Great Dragon's Spine and Dragon Heart, it was still the best fourth-grade earth-attribute material.

Any one of these main ingredients could be used to refine a mid-grade, or even a top-grade, magical treasure.

If they were all fused together, and with the power of the heavenly tribulation's divine thunder, what kind of treasure would be refined?

Zhou Fuyou's breath quickened for a moment, but he quickly suppressed it.

Because once such a treasure was born, its foundation would inevitably be profound. He had to refine it himself.

Suddenly!

A thunderous reminder came from below.

"If you don't seize the treasure now, when will you!"

Zhou Fuyou leaped up, facing the falling thunderbolts, and plunged straight into the depths of the clouds.

Countless eyes stared complexly at that patch of clouds, where lightning flickered and magical power surged.

No one knew how long it took, or perhaps it was just an instant.

A feeling that could suppress the heavens, yet also seemed to embrace all things, arose in everyone's hearts.

The thunder and lightning abruptly ceased.

The vast dark clouds dissipated in an instant.

The white-clad figure lightly descended into the Heavenly Origin Observatory.

As soon as Zhou Fuyou landed, he drew the gaze of five pairs of eyes.

He took a deep breath, extended his hand, and a perfectly round blue pearl appeared before everyone.

"Master, uncles, Fuyou has not failed your expectations!"

Bluejade Fairy nodded with satisfaction. "Refining treasures with heavenly tribulation is the most dangerous thing. But once successful, the benefits are unimaginable. The Sea-Calming Pearl in your hand has already surpassed the level of a magical treasure; it is a powerful true artifact!"

Upon hearing this, Hui Yuanhong, who was opposite, said with slight excitement, "Using a true artifact as a lifebound treasure, combined with Zhou Fuyou's already formidable strength, he will surely dominate the Dao Sect's Sexagenary Grand Competition!"

However, Zhan Bingtao, the refiner of the Sea-Calming Pearl, poured cold water on them.

"Don't think it's too easy. Magical treasures are driven by magical power. True artifacts are the same, but to unleash their maximum power, they need to absorb the free-floating spiritual energy of heaven and earth. Zhou Fuyou is only at the early Adept realm."

The elders above chatted one after another, and Zhou Fuyou precisely captured the key information within.

"Sexagenary Grand Competition?"

This was something he had never learned about from any of the numerous classics.

Seeing his confusion, his master Bluejade Fairy specially explained.

It wasn't that Zhou Fuyou was ignorant, but that the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect had indeed never had a "Sexagenary Grand Competition" in its past history.

This so-called Sexagenary Grand Competition was actually another form of the Centennial Grand Competition.

The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect held a grand competition every hundred years, and every five hundred years, they would open the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven for a Grotto-Heaven Grand Competition.

But a hundred years after the last Grotto-Heaven Grand Competition, no Centennial Grand Competition was held.

Everyone knew the reason in their hearts. At that time, the Central Continent was in turmoil after the great calamity. Where was the time and energy to organize competitions between disciples?

But as time passed, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect gradually recovered its vitality, and this ancient tradition should continue.

Zhou Fuyou roughly calculated the time. Indeed, more than a hundred years had passed since the last Grotto-Heaven Grand Competition, and no competition of any kind had been held during that period. Even this internal ranking competition of the Tinnan Dao Palace was conducted on the battlefield between humans and demons.

"The Sexagenary Grand Competition is an exception, a product of special times. It will certainly not continue in the future, because the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect will surely return to its proper course."

Zhan Bingtao took over the conversation, speaking eloquently.

"Special products naturally come with special rewards."

"Unlike previous competitions where the top-ranked disciples would receive rewards such as magical treasures, pills, techniques, guidance, and even benefits for their families, this Sexagenary Grand Competition, besides the disciples and their families, the Dao Palaces they belong to will also receive more resource allocation!"

Zhou Fuyou suddenly understood. No wonder the other Hall Masters cared so much about this matter.

If he could perform well in the Sexagenary Grand Competition, the originally weaker Tinnan Dao Palace might receive a large amount of resource allocation and then revive its momentum.

Regarding the help of resources for cultivation, Zhou Fuyou didn't actually feel it deeply.

His cultivation speed was extremely fast. Apart from the necessary spiritual vein conditions, he hardly used any external objects.

But he was very clear about how attractive those resources were to ordinary cultivators.

Not to mention anything else, just his own little sister, if she wanted to continue her cultivation on the path of Qi Refinement Immortality, she would inevitably need a vast amount of resources, even more than ordinary cultivators!

Zhou Fuyou secretly resolved to perform well in this Sexagenary Grand Competition.

But Zhan Bingtao hadn't finished speaking.

"Most importantly, the top ten of each cultivation level in the Sexagenary Grand Competition will also have the opportunity to enter the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven for cultivation!"

Zhou Fuyou was slightly bewildered, not understanding the significance of entering the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven for cultivation. He instinctively looked at Bluejade Fairy.

His own master was the number one of the last Grotto-Heaven Grand Competition!

Bluejade Fairy smoothed her hair and smiled slightly. "The heaven-defying opportunities inside can only be enjoyed by Sages. But for ordinary cultivators, there is another great opportunity. I only need to say one thing: the difference in time flow!"

"One day outside, a hundred days in the grotto-heaven. One year outside, a hundred years in the grotto-heaven!"

"If you can enter and cultivate there, even for just one year, you can gain a hundred years of cultivation accumulation faster than your peers!"

Zhou Fuyou's lips parted slightly, his eyes shining. He rarely lost his composure.

With his cultivation speed, if he could span a hundred years of time, what realm could he reach?

"Zhou Fuyou, go and cultivate well! The Sexagenary Grand Competition is still decades away. With your cultivation speed, this time is enough for you to raise your realm again and also unleash more of the Sea-Calming Pearl's power. I hope that in the Sexagenary Grand Competition, you can win a good ranking for the Tinnan Dao Palace!" Zhan Bingtao said slowly, his eyes full of expectation.

Zhou Fuyou bowed deeply. "Fuyou will do his utmost and will not disappoint the expectations of my master and uncles!"

Leaving the Heavenly Origin Observatory, although Zhou Fuyou's heart was extremely excited, his expression had returned to calm.

Oblivious to the envious glances cast at him from all directions, he returned to his new Zhaoxi Palace on his own.

There, many people were waiting for him.

Senior Sister Shi Miaoyin, Second Senior Brother Fu Bin, Fifth Senior Sister...

Clanswoman Zhou Yuwei, Zhou Yuqi who was in a wheelchair, cousin Lin Qingtong.

And his sister, Zhou Qingyu!

After returning, everyone celebrated his acquisition of a lifebound treasure with a grand banquet.

During the banquet, other senior brothers came from time to time to offer their congratulations.

Among them were many junior brothers and sisters he had saved on the battlefield at the East Sea coast.

Unknowingly, Zhou Fuyou had gathered a considerable network of people around him. These people might not seem outstanding within the Tinnan Dao Palace, but if placed on the Tinnan Continent, or even the entire Central Continent, they would all be lords of their own domains in the future.

And Zhou Fuyou was the most dazzling existence among them.

After the celebration, Zhou Fuyou returned to his cultivation chamber as usual.

The words of the Observatory Master Zhan Bingtao and his master during the day still echoed in Zhou Fuyou's mind.

When he was very, very young, he had a craving for power.

This craving, later on, even became urgent.

He craved power, urgently desired a higher status, not only for himself but also for the people around him.

This urgent craving seemed to disappear after he broke through to the Adept realm.

But on Liubo Mountain, the gaze of that enormous black dragon made him understand that an Adept was nothing!

Only Sage, only Ascendant, could roam freely in this world and protect those he cherished.

Once again, he had motivation.

"I must win a good ranking in the Sexagenary Grand Competition. I must enter the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven!"

"One year outside, a hundred years in the grotto-heaven."

"If I can walk out from there, I will be a Sage!"

Zhou Fuyou thought this, his resolve unshakable!

...

In the mountains, there are no days or months; in the world, a thousand years have passed.

Unknowingly, fifty years had gone by since Luo Chen arrived at the Arctic Yemo Heaven.

This once sacred mountain gate had long lost its former scene of shimmering auroras and towering peaks scraping the sky.

The air was filled with cold wind and snow, and the sky seemed to be stained with a layer of gray, forming a thick iron curtain.

On this day!

Above the iron curtain sky, a vast, mighty star stream surged forth, rolling past.

Where the star stream passed, the iron curtain split open, and wind and snow retreated.

Even the blood pool within the glacier boiled uncontrollably.

For a moment, it felt as if heaven and earth were being destroyed.

But in an instant, the vast star stream disappeared.

The iron curtain closed again, the wind and snow remained, the cold persisted, and the blood pool returned to calm.

Sensing this anomaly, Xiao Bai and Black King had long since returned from other parts of Yemo Heaven, waiting at the foot of a mountain.

Swish!

A sword light shot up into the sky, then landed before them.

The newcomer was tall, with a hard-lined face, slightly curled lips as if in contempt or disdain, and a pair of black eyes like bottomless abysses, making one involuntarily sink into them.

He stood there quietly, like an unsheathed sharp sword, almost piercing the sky.

After standing still for a moment, the black-robed Daoist exhaled a turbid breath.

With this breath, that sharp, exposed aura gradually receded, until it returned to its initial warmth like polished jade.

Xiao Bai and Black King exchanged a glance, then spoke in unison.

"Congratulations, Master, on emerging from seclusion!"

Luo Chen smiled slightly and waved his large sleeve.

"Let's go, we're returning to the Eastern Wilderness!"
