# Chapter 1010: Yin Soul Crosses Yang, Nascent Soul Becomes Ascendant

The proficiency panel had one fundamental logic: to increase proficiency, one must practice the skill as much as possible.

Luo Chen had understood this logic ever since his Qi Refinement days.

Whether it was alchemy, martial arts, qi refinement, or even cultivating spells, one had to practice with utmost effort. If one was perfunctory, proficiency would still increase, but only by a very small margin.

Conversely, the higher the concentration during practice, the faster the proficiency would rise!

As for the Buddhist-Daoist technique *Moonlight Pure Heart Mantra*, its purpose was to remove distracting thoughts from the soul, making it more condensed, until a complete primordial spirit was finally condensed.

And the larger the soul, the more distracting thoughts it had!

Especially since Luo Chen not only possessed a vast soul foundation but had also split off eight divided souls.

Although all the divided souls were dominated by his main soul, he still could not withstand the complexity of thoughts and the frequency of distracting ideas.

The *Fine Dust Primordial Art*, which originally had the effect of gathering the mind and discarding distracting thoughts, had gradually failed to keep up with his strength.

As a result, Luo Chen's mind had grown increasingly profound over the years.

Perhaps for ordinary people, thinking more and considering things comprehensively was a good thing. But for an expert focused on breaking through to the Ascendant realm, concentration was the path of utmost sincerity!

Luo Chen's purpose in cultivating the higher-level *Moonlight Pure Heart Mantra* was to cut away some unnecessary distracting thoughts.

At this moment, under the pressure of the vast, seemingly countless ghostly souls, Luo Chen's pressure multiplied.

Even his *Prison-Suppressing Soul Art*, which had always been as firm as a rock, began to waver faintly.

But this was exactly the situation Luo Chen wanted.

He chanted the *Moonlight Pure Heart Mantra* with all his might, inwardly cutting away the myriad distracting thoughts, outwardly resisting the seductive ghostly sounds.

In this way, his concentration was fully maximized!

Cultivating the *Moonlight Pure Heart Mantra* became twice the result with half the effort, reflected in the intuitive data as a rapid increase in proficiency.

Time passed bit by bit, and he chanted over and over again.

Later on, there was even a faint sound of Buddhist chanting, echoing through the empty hall.

If an outsider had been present, they would have noticed that the hidden ferocity on Luo Chen's body was also dissipating with each recitation.

---

As time passed since moving into the Dan Saint Blessed Land, the Luo Tian Sect's development of this blessed land space had become increasingly complete.

Starting from the five main areas—the Dan Mountain where the Supreme Elder resided, the three peaks where the three great elders lived (Misty Peak, War God Peak, Ghost Emperor Peak), and the Great Luo Mountain where the many disciples resided—more places had gradually been developed.

For example, the Xuanyan Lake where the Xuanyan Turtle lived, the Heavenly Mountain of the Canglong lineage, and the Divine Elegance Peak additionally opened by the Grand Alchemy Court.

But no matter how the Luo Tian Sect changed, the towering Dan Mountain remained the most revered place in the sect.

Only because the one who lived inside was the Supreme Elder who had founded the Luo Tian Sect!

Ordinary disciples would not easily set foot there, and even seeing it from afar, they mostly harbored feelings of reverence and worship.

However, in recent years, many in the Luo Tian Sect had noticed great changes on Dan Mountain.

At first, yin qi assailed it, and ghostly qi was dense.

Gradually, from time to time, sounds of ghostly wailing and wolf howling, sounds that seized one's soul, came from within.

Some disciples who went to pay homage to Dan Mountain even fell victim, suffering damage to their souls for a time.

Later, it was Sect Leader Li Yingzhang who recalled Elder Bai from Ghost Emperor Peak. After an investigation, he set up a corresponding formation on Dan Mountain to isolate external prying, thus preventing some low-level disciples from suffering undeserved calamities.

But this situation gradually eased over time.

What replaced it was the occasional sound of Buddhist chanting that leaked out.

Anyone who heard it found their mind and spirit at peace, losing their desire for slaughter and conquest.

The many disciples did not know the reason, only remembering to obey the Sect Leader's orders and not approach Dan Mountain easily.

Five years later.

The sound of Buddhist chanting on Dan Mountain grew even more majestic.

Even the great hall seemed to have become solemn and dignified, like a Buddhist shrine.

Even more astonishing was that the large number of ghost cultivators inside had stopped making those soul-seizing ghostly wails and howls.

More than thirty Ghost Emperors had restrained their forms, prostrating themselves in the hall, reverently guarding the figure at the center.

Feather robe, high crown, expression peaceful.

The originally sharp contours of his face had also become much gentler, set off by the Buddhist chanting.

Suddenly!

The feather-robed Daoist chanted "Svaha" three times in succession.

A strange change appeared on his body.

Beams of golden light emanated from between his brows, covering his entire body.

All the Ghost Emperors looked up together, and there was even a sense of overlapping phantoms in their eyes.

That Daoist now seemed to have transformed into two bodies, one inside and one outside.

The outer body still wore a feather robe and high crown with a peaceful expression, while the inner phantom was clad in black armor, holding a cauldron, with a look of completion.

If a knowledgeable expert had been present, they would have immediately identified the essence of this phenomenon at a glance.

Primordial spirit entering the body!

At this moment, Luo Chen had clearly achieved the initial unification and fusion of his soul and Nascent Soul, condensing a primordial spirit.

And what was most eye-catching was that the golden light emanating from his sea of consciousness was still continuing!

A massive amount of soul power surged from his sea of consciousness, continuously merging into his Nascent Soul, causing the primordial spirit, which was originally the same size as his physical body, to grow even larger.

This was the advantage revealed when condensing the primordial spirit, stemming from his soul foundation that far exceeded his peers and was comparable to Ascendant-level great powers.

From the very beginning, the primordial spirit he condensed was stronger than that of ordinary great cultivators, and was even climbing towards the level of Ascendant great powers.

The overlapping phantoms appeared staggered!

The primordial spirit overpowered the physical body!

It even surpassed the physical body, continuously expanding and manifesting outside.

Gradually, it filled the entire hall, until it manifested externally, covering the entire Dan Mountain!

This scene almost had a hint of the feeling when Dan Saint Chu Yan ascended, with his primordial spirit floating in the sky!

At this point, the primordial spirit finally stopped its expansion trend.

It seemed that Luo Chen's Nascent Soul had completely accommodated the vast soul power.

It was also at this moment that the spiritual qi accumulated over many years in the Dan Dust Hall surged towards Luo Chen's primordial spirit. Not only that, a large amount of fire-attribute spiritual qi from the Dan Mountain's earth vein also seemed to be attracted, all rushing towards the primordial spirit that floated and covered the mountain.

Even more, a massive outward suction force directly collapsed the formation that Bai Meiling and Li Yingzhang had set up on the periphery of Dan Mountain back then.

This suction force connected heaven and earth, beginning to extract from the entire sky.

Such a scene directly shook the entire Dan Saint Blessed Land!

Those with strong cultivation, such as Ling Fengzi, Cang Long, and Wang Yuan, were immediately alarmed. They flew into the sky in unison and gazed into the distance at this place.

Upon seeing Luo Chen's massive primordial spirit phenomenon, they all drew in a cold breath.

"Yin soul crosses yang, Nascent Soul becomes Ascendant—this is a sign of primordial spirit condensation!"

"Spiritual qi surges, pulling heaven and earth—what is Senior Brother Luo Chen trying to do?"

"Could it be that he wants to advance to the Ascendant realm?"

Even Jadewater Dragon, who had only recently joined the Luo Tian Sect, stopped her seclusion and hurriedly followed out.

Seeing this scene, her eyes went wide.

She had always considered herself supremely talented, a rare heavenly spirit root cultivator in the world.

If she hadn't been trapped on Green Parasol Mountain back then and turned into a medicine slave, if she had cultivated normally, she would definitely not be inferior to any of her peers.

But facing the unfathomable Daoist practice that Luo Chen had revealed, she had to admit that even without those unfortunate experiences, she could not have achieved such accomplishments at such an age.

She even groaned with a hint of doubt about life: "Luo Chen is less than three hundred years old, yet he can condense a primordial spirit. Could he be a reincarnated immortal?"

As the four Sage-level combat forces left behind in the Dan Saint Blessed Land, they naturally discovered the anomaly first.

And as the massive primordial spirit covering the mountain slowly opened its eyes, the tremor of this space became more and more obvious, causing even some Adept-level cultivators to notice something was wrong.

But before the low-level cultivators could react, the massive primordial spirit that enveloped Dan Mountain suddenly disappeared.

The four of them, Ling Fengzi, looked at each other in confusion.

Facing each other, they still didn't understand what had happened.

"So... what exactly happened to Luo Chen?"

---

Inside the Dan Dust Hall.

The Soul-Nourishing Banner was placed to the side, all the ghosts had been collected inside, and Luo Chen was still sitting cross-legged on the cushion.

His eyes were closed, but his eyelids kept twitching, showing the turmoil in his heart at this moment.

Just a moment ago.

He had forcibly condensed his primordial spirit, from no-thought giving birth to ten thousand thoughts!

Even more, at the moment of condensing the primordial spirit, he had stirred heaven and earth, showing signs of advancing to the Ascendant realm!

This was an unexpected situation.

But if viewed from many classics, it was a change that made sense.

Only because his primordial spirit was far larger than this realm should allow.

Moreover, he possessed two true meanings of the Dao—the withering and blooming of wood, and the origin of fire.

But in the end, he failed.

There were many reasons for the failure.

Luo Chen didn't need to think too much to list a few.

Insufficient mana, foundation still slightly lacking.

The spiritual vein grade was not high enough, resulting in insufficient spiritual opportunity. The reason he could stir heaven and earth was merely the blessing of the two true meanings of the Dao.

And the most important point was that his primordial spirit was forcibly condensed and could not withstand the might of heaven and earth.

Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes, his lips slightly parted, and a stream of air like a white dragon slowly exhaled.

Xuan Du didn't lie to me. Although the *Moonlight Pure Heart Mantra* can temporarily condense the primordial spirit, the duration is too short to support me in forcibly breaking through to the Ascendant stage. Even though I have cultivated this technique to the Grandmaster level, even though I have comprehended the true meaning of the *Repairing Deficiency Mantra*, my destiny soul is still incomplete!

"But that *Repairing Deficiency Mantra* is really effective. If I cultivate the *Moonlight Pure Heart Mantra* to the Great Perfection realm, can I ignore the missing destiny soul and forcibly break through to the Ascendant stage?"

Luo Chen carefully considered this possibility.

The *Moonlight Pure Heart Mantra* contained a most core mantra, called *Repairing Deficiency*, which meant to repair what is missing.

It was by relying on this mantra that he could make up for the missing destiny soul.

In these five years of intense cultivation under great pressure, Luo Chen had comprehended the *Repairing Deficiency Mantra*, relying on it to break through to the Grandmaster level of proficiency in the technique, and even to condense the primordial spirit.

But after constant deliberation, he still couldn't make up his mind.

Because he wasn't sure how long it would take to break through to the Ascendant realm, and whether his forcibly condensed primordial spirit could support him until the moment of complete success.

Moreover, after his *Pure Heart Mantra* reached the Grandmaster level, this batch of ghosts could no longer give him any pressure, and the speed of improving the *Pure Heart Mantra*'s proficiency instantly plummeted.

If he wanted to cultivate to the Great Perfection level, he didn't know how many years it would take.

"So, in the end, I still have to go to the Central Continent!"

Luo Chen sighed, setting aside the plan to forcibly break through to the Ascendant stage using the Great Perfection proficiency of the *Moonlight Pure Heart Mantra* as a backup. Retrieving his destiny soul was still the first choice.

He composed himself, examined his own state, and the feelings of regret and emotion were gone, replaced by a trace of joy.

Besides successfully cultivating the *Moonlight Pure Heart Mantra* to the Grandmaster level, there was an unexpected bonus.

He was about to reach the seventh layer of the Sage realm!

On the panel, the progress of the qi refinement column had reached [Sage Sixth Layer 90/100].

He was only one step away from the late Sage realm!

This was an unexpected joy without any discount.

Cultivation in the Sage realm became more difficult the further one went. It could be said to be step by step, layer by layer of heaven.

Therefore, the Sage realm, like the Adept and Initiate realms below, was divided into nine small layers, unlike the Ascendant and Void Refining realms, which were only divided into three stages: early, middle, and late.

Luo Chen had not expected to approach the late Sage realm so quickly, after all, his cultivation speed was already very, very fast.

But this time, when condensing the primordial spirit and stirring heaven and earth, he had swallowed all the spiritual qi accumulated on Dan Mountain over the years in one gulp, and even absorbed most of the spiritual qi within ten thousand li through the spirit-gathering formation.

Only by such a massive devouring could he take such a solid big step.

It could be imagined that as long as he cultivated properly for another ten or twenty years, stepping into the late Sage realm would be a natural progression.

Of course, Luo Chen was someone who could distinguish priorities.

Compared to improving this one small realm, the previously planned refinement of the Nether Water Spring Eye was the top priority.

"Now that the primordial spirit is formed, even if I cannot break through to the Ascendant realm, grinding away the will of the spring eye will be a piece of cake."

Luo Chen smiled slightly, his gaze falling on the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

---

Outside Dan Mountain.

Several figures gathered, waiting quietly.

After a long time, a gentle voice came from inside.

"I am fine, everyone need not worry."

Everyone breathed a sigh of relief.

Besides the four Sage-level combat forces, Ling Fengzi and the others, there were also four Adept-level cultivators.

They were Sect Leader Li Yingzhang, Luo Chen's disciple Qu Lingjun, and Luo Chen's two Dao companions, Sima Huiniang and Gu Caiyi.

Ling Fengzi, the most experienced and steady, comforted them: "I said Senior Brother Luo Chen is blessed by heaven and won't have any problems. You insisted on following along."

Li Yingzhang shook his head. "The Supreme Elder is the pillar of our Luo Tian Sect. No amount of concern is too much."

Qu Lingjun, on the other hand, spoke towards Dan Mountain: "Master, the *Tihu Pills* have been nurtured to completion!"

As he spoke, he took out a large gourd.

Instantly, the gazes of Ling Fengzi, Cang Long, and Jadewater Dragon all fell upon it, their eyes showing barely restrained greed, and even their breathing became a little rapid.

This was the famous ancient pseudo-fifth-grade divine pill!

It had miraculous effects on Sage realm cultivators.

Inside the mountain, Luo Chen chuckled lightly.

"You have worked hard."

Then a stream of mana came through the air, grabbing the large gourd and flying back to Dan Mountain.

"Next, I will continue my seclusion. Unless there is a matter of life and death, do not disturb me."

After leaving these words, Dan Mountain had no further movement.

Ling Fengzi looked regretfully at the large gourd flying away, his motion of stroking his beard becoming much faster.

That Luo Chen had refined *Tihu Pills* was common knowledge in the Luo Tian Sect.

And the quantity was quite large, a total of fifteen pills!

According to legend, a cultivator could only take one *Tihu Pill* in their lifetime.

Deng Taiyue would at most only ask for one.

The rest would naturally belong to the Luo Tian Sect!

Wang Yuan was a body cultivator and couldn't use them, but he and Cang Long and the others could always use them, right?

But there was no sign of Senior Brother Luo Chen distributing any to them, the Luo Tian Sect cultivators. This made Ling Fengzi's heart itch unbearably.

"Your husband has his own plans. Elder Ling Fengzi, why be impatient? What is yours will be yours, and what is not yours cannot be forced." Gu Caiyi said lightly, neither humble nor arrogant in the face of the Sage realm cultivator who was a rank above her.

Sima Huiniang smoothed things over: "Since the pill formula has been researched, with your husband's alchemy skills, once we find more medicinal ingredients, he can refine as many as he wants. Elder Ling Fengzi, there really is no need to rush."

Faced with this one playing the bad cop and the other the good cop, Ling Fengzi had nothing to say in response.

After all, they were Luo Chen's Dao companions. What could he say? Especially since what they said did make some sense.

Jadewater Dragon was currently at the first layer of the Sage realm and wasn't in a hurry to advance to the Ascendant realm, so she didn't care much.

But Cang Longxianzi showed a hesitant expression.

"Senior Brother Luo Chen has just made a breakthrough. He should be relaxing, so why is he going into seclusion again?"

"He seems very anxious!"

Faced with this statement, everyone was stunned.

It did seem to be the case!

Since Luo Chen returned, although he had handled everything in an orderly manner, it was precisely under this orderly manner that a suppressed urgency was revealed.

Deng Taiyue came and went.

Bai Meiling was sent out to sweep the Nether Abyss.

It seemed like he was preparing something.

And after Luo Chen returned, besides treating Luo Lingxi and refining the *Tihu Pills*, he had basically no interaction with others.

Even with his two Dao companions, there was no basic intimacy.

He didn't give much guidance to his disciples and followers.

He even left all the big and small affairs of the Luo Tian Sect to Li Yingzhang.

In everyone's hearts, Luo Chen was definitely not an unfeeling person, but why was he so indifferent to everything?

"There must be a powerful enemy lurking, which is why he is acting like this!"

Suddenly, Wang Yuan said abruptly, with a somewhat decisive tone.

Everyone was stunned.

With the strength Luo Chen had shown, what powerful enemy would make him so anxious?

Wang Yuan said solemnly: "I know him. In the past, whenever there was a powerful enemy, he acted like this. And the reason he doesn't tell us is most likely because he thinks we can't help much, so he puts all the pressure on himself."

Sima Huiniang blurted out: "Then what should we do?"

Wang Yuan took a deep breath, looking up at the lonely Dan Mountain.

"Only by hard cultivation, improving our strength, can we possibly help him a little!"

(End of this chapter)
