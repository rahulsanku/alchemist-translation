# Chapter 971: Crossing the Southern Ocean, Turbid Waves Swallow the Flames

"Farewell to the Ancestor's ascension!"

...

The mountain-shaking, sea-roaring sounds of farewell still faintly echoed in his ears, but gradually dissipated with the lapping of the waves beneath his feet.

Luo Chen gazed at this unfamiliar sea region. No birds were seen, thick fog filled the air, and there was a faint sense of familiarity.

"This is... the Southern Ocean?" Luo Chen murmured hesitantly.

Suddenly!

"Hnng..."

A muffled groan escaped him, and Luo Chen's expression shifted slightly.

Only at this moment did that extreme sense of weakness suddenly emerge.

His divine sense swept outward, examining his own body, and the terrible state of his physical condition instantly became clear.

His Nascent Soul was withered, his dharma power depleted.

His limbs and bones, his five viscera and six bowels, even his meridians and acupoints—all transmitted intense pain.

These were the injuries sustained from the battle in the Yin-Yang Remnant Realm!

It was just that during the brief time spent with the Divine Lord Youli, the other party had suppressed them with great divine abilities, so he hadn't felt them.

Now, having left that cloud-sea precipice, with no one else suppressing them, the backlash against Luo Chen began instantly.

The only good news was that the meager source power remaining had silently increased by quite a bit, giving Luo Chen at least three-tenths of his self-protection ability.

Source power and dharma power were different. Dharma power sought externally, returning to oneself. Source power, however, was the physical strength refined from powerful flesh and blood. As long as the physical body was not destroyed, source power could be endlessly generated.

The "source" character in source power was taken from the origin power of the physical body, and also carried the meaning of being inexhaustible.

Of course, there was no truly infinite power in the world.

The price of inexhaustibility was the constant squeezing of the physical body.

If consumption was excessive, it would greatly reduce one's lifespan.

On the attribute panel, Luo Chen's original fifteen-hundred-year lifespan—which had once looked down upon his peers—had also been reduced due to the battle in the Medicine King Domain and the battle in the Yin-Yang Remnant Realm.

After personally experiencing these two incidents, Luo Chen understood more and more why the path of body refinement had gradually declined in the Mountains and Seas Immortal Cultivation Realm.

Every intense battle led to a loss of lifespan. Who would still be willing to cultivate such a method?

If he hadn't also concurrently cultivated the path of qi refinement, with additional lifespan supplementation, he probably couldn't withstand such consumption either.

Spitting out a mouthful of turbid qi, Luo Chen also understood why the Divine Lord Youli had sent him to the Southern Ocean sea region.

In his current state, he was extremely withered.

If he were in the Southern Frontier Immortal Cultivation Realm and encountered a powerful Sage, it would be hard to avoid accidents.

Although the Southern Ocean sea region was equally strange and dangerous, for his natal dharma treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, which still carried the aura of a fifth-rank divine gu, it wasn't actually that frightening.

"That senior truly thought of everything!"

Luo Chen sighed with emotion.

Even though there were still many doubts in his heart, he greatly admired the other's actions.

Before ascending, he had even helped the Life and Death Gate resolve the invading great enemy.

Now that Tianyuan had retreated and the Fierce Peak Stiff Alliance had collapsed, the entire Southern Frontier Immortal Cultivation Realm could be said to have suffered severe damage, leaving it empty.

Under such circumstances, the Life and Death Gate, with its completely intact strength, could dispatch its entire force and unify the Southern Frontier!

What the Nether Origin Sect had once wanted to do but couldn't accomplish, the Divine Lord Youli had nearly completed with just a single strike.

Especially since the hidden danger of the Yin-Yang Remnant Realm had been eliminated, the Death Gate could also return and merge with the Life Gate. The Life and Death Gate now had a complete defensive barrier.

Even if the Southern Frontier cultivators counterattacked the Life and Death Gate in the future, they could advance and retreat with proper strategy, no longer reduced to the situation of having enemies break into their mountain gate.

"It's still a bit regrettable."

"Although the trip to the Southern Frontier successfully achieved its goals, it was hurried coming and going. There were too many figures and sights I didn't get to appreciate."

Amid his sighs, Luo Chen turned his attention to the sea region where faint black mist drifted.

Back when he had first entered the Sage realm, he had accidentally entered this sea region, attempting to use it to cross the Eastern Wilderness. Unfortunately, due to insufficient strength and a cautious nature, he hadn't ventured deep.

This time, he could give it a try.

But to be safe, he still needed to make some preparations.

Luo Chen's mind stirred, and his left shoulder shook.

Black light flickered, and his spirit pet reappeared.

Looking at Black King, who lay prostrate in the water waves, withered and listless, Luo Chen asked calmly, "Can you still move?"

"I can't move anymore!" Black King drooped his eyelids and said weakly, "I'm almost completely torn apart by that big fellow."

"Stop pretending. On this trip, I need you to serve as my mount."

Though he said this, Luo Chen was actually quite distressed for him.

If it had been an ordinary battle, having part of Black King's body shattered wouldn't have had much impact.

But the Netherworld Earth Dragon was ultimately a fifth-rank desolate beast, and it seemed to be of the same earth element as Black King.

Being heavily injured by that kind of thing, Black King certainly couldn't be in good shape.

With a flick of his wrist, streaks of light emerged from his storage ring—all healing pills Luo Chen had treasured.

Some he had refined himself, others he had deliberately collected before leaving.

At this moment, without carefully identifying them, he directly stuffed a large handful into Black King's mouth.

Black King closed his eyes and swallowed them all in one gulp.

Not only that, but he also pretended to cough with difficulty.

"Master, it's a bit choking!"

Luo Chen was helpless, understanding what the other was angling for.

But considering Black King's proactive protection of his master under dangerous circumstances earlier, he no longer held back. Light emerged from the storage ring again.

A wine jar appeared before him.

Faintly, a rich aroma of wine surfaced.

Black King opened his drooping eyelids, his eyes full of gleam.

"This is the last jar of thousand-year Five Temple Immortal Brew. I'll reward it to you!"

"Thank you, Master. Thank you!"

Black King directly tilted his head back, and a large amount of wine gushed out from the jar, swallowed in big gulps, his face full of satisfaction.

This was indeed the last jar of thousand-year Five Temple Immortal Brew.

Although the Dieyun Sect had a large stock of this spirit wine, it couldn't withstand Luo Chen's continuous consumption over thirty years!

There were only a few hundred jars in total, and they had long been nearly exhausted.

Of course, with such prodigious drinking, the benefits it brought Luo Chen were undeniable.

After making no progress in *Myriad Path Convergence*, the reason Luo Chen could still cultivate to the late-stage Archaic fourth-rank level wasn't just relying on the *Hundred Poisons Body Refinement Art* alone.

The foundation brought by thirty years of Five Temple Immortal Brew had created the miracle of accumulated thickness bursting forth.

Now that the last jar of Five Temple Immortal Brew was given away, Luo Chen felt pained but not too distressed.

First, the recipe for this wine had been roughly deduced by the Luo Tian Sect's cultivators, so it could always be brewed again.

Second, he had the *Hundred Poisons Body Refinement Art*. As long as the cultivation materials for toxins kept up, he wouldn't lack this kind of body-tempering effect.

"I've heard that the Southern Ocean is the birthplace of the Southern Frontier's gu insects. Over thousands of years, countless wondrous gu insects have originated from this sea region."

"I can pay attention along the way. If I can pick up a few high-grade poison gu for free, I can assist the Hundred Poisons King Gu in refinement."

Gazing at the boundless, unfathomable sea region, Luo Chen was lost in thought. Then he directly sat cross-legged on Black King's smooth, scaly back.

"Head northeast. Strive to return to the Eastern Wilderness as soon as possible!"

After issuing the command, Luo Chen took out a top-grade Hongyuan Pill and began to recover his dharma power.

Black King let out a shout and immediately broke through the black mist, swimming toward the vast ocean.

...

One month later.

On the boundless sea, a blue-green hornbill bird circled in the sky, its sapphire-like eyes fixed on the black shadow slowly moving below, like a patient hunter waiting.

On the massive black shadow sat a white-robed Daoist.

Having left the Southern Frontier, Luo Chen had restored his original appearance.

The Mystic Dust Armor he had been wearing was also put away into his Purple Mansion, where his Nascent Soul continuously emitted dharma power to refine and nurture it.

After the battle with the Netherworld Earth Dragon, the Mystic Dust Armor was severely damaged. Without suitable materials to repair it, he could only rely on dharma power to slowly nurture and restore it.

However, the Pingshan Crown still remained on his head.

This dharma treasure not only had an excellent appearance but also possessed outstanding defensive capabilities. After all, it was once used daily by the ancient demon lord Pingshan, Emperor Ditian.

The reason it hadn't shown its brilliance during the previous battle was that Luo Chen wasn't familiar enough with it.

If he became well-acquainted with it, its defensive power should surpass that of the Mystic Dust Armor.

At this moment, Luo Chen had just finished his meditation practice and was concentrating on the treasure in his hands.

It was a vigorously beating heart!

The heart of Master Li Tian!

Even though it had been separated from its owner for many days, this heart remained ruddy in color, beating in a cycle of five breaths.

And as it beat, it emitted a large amount of qi and blood, filling the blood vessels on the heart.

Such strangeness was truly astonishing.

Luo Chen even compared it to his own heart, concluding that it was no less than his own.

"Fortunately, I suddenly attacked and dug out Master Li Tian's heart. Otherwise, with this heart aiding him, under the suppression of that Wind-Wave Pavilion, Master Li Tian's strength would have been three points higher." Luo Chen muttered, not without praise in his words.

But this praise was not for Master Li Tian's body refinement skill, but for the Devil Heart Gu that had actively fallen into slumber within the heart!

After Luo Chen personally erased the divine sense imprint on the Devil Heart Gu, this peculiar gu insect had fallen into dormancy.

Yet the powerful qi and blood it naturally emitted in its dormant state was still breathtaking.

It was hard to imagine what level this gu insect had reached after centuries of nurturing by Master Li Tian.

It was probably only one step away from the fifth rank.

"Perhaps the reason Master Li Tian craved the Life-and-Death Sharing Gu, and didn't even care that it was only fourth rank, was because of this Devil Heart Gu!"

"The two were originally of the same origin. If I refine this Devil Heart Gu into the fourth-rank Life-and-Death Sharing Gu, would there be a chance for it to break through to the fifth rank?"

Luo Chen didn't know the answer, as he had no great skill in the art of gu refinement.

But with Master Li Tian's methods, achieving that should not have been difficult.

"Never mind. I don't need a high rank. As long as I can take it back and remove the restrictions on Lingxi, that's enough."

Luo Chen cast a series of restrictive spells, temporarily sealing this heart.

Then he began to inventory his other spoils of war!

Indeed, although the trip to the Yin-Yang Remnant Realm had ended abruptly and inexplicably, he had indeed harvested a large pile of things.

Not to mention the source blood of the Golden Dragon Lion, even some of the "miscellaneous items" the Divine Lord Youli had fished out were of great use to him.

A long knife, shimmering and crystalline like crystal.

This was the true artifact left by Meng Shentong, named the Void Severance Blade.

When attacking, it was formless, imageless, impermanent, and indefinite.

It could freely change its form and also teleport through space, unleashing great power.

Its only drawback was probably that it needed to be used with the Vacuum Gu to double its power.

A piece of tortoise shell, with ancient patterns and countless restrictions.

This was exactly the item that Matron Redleaf had craved.

When she was caught in Luo Chen's Withering-Bloom Divine Fire, most of her treasures had collapsed and disappeared along with her storage dharma treasure. Only this piece of tortoise shell had been preserved.

Later, when the Divine Lord Youli used the "Fishing the Moon from the Realm" divine ability, a large number of things were swallowed up, and this item was given to Luo Chen.

Now, with Luo Chen's knowledge, he couldn't even tell whether this item was a dharma treasure or a gu insect.

It had both the aura of a gu insect and the methods of formation dao, all borne on a single piece of tortoise shell.

He only knew that this item seemed to be a living being. After a cultivator refined it, they could use the seven basic prohibitions of formation dao on it to quickly set up most of the formations in the world.

Although it was of true artifact rank, its practicality was probably not inferior to that of a spirit treasure.

Luo Chen happily left his divine sense imprint on the tortoise shell.

From now on, setting up formations would be much more convenient.

While Luo Chen was inventorying his spoils, Black King beneath him finally couldn't hold back.

"Master, that Blue-Eyed Ghost Hornbill has been following us for three days. Let's kill it!"

Without looking up, Luo Chen replied, "The Southern Ocean is full of dangers. If we can avoid fighting, we will. Unless it actively comes to provoke us."

Black King said gloomily, "But don't you need treasures containing potent poison? The Blue-Eyed Ghost Hornbill itself is a poisonous demon beast. Since it appears in the Southern Ocean, it might even have parasitic poison gu on it. Aren't you tempted?"

Luo Chen frowned and instinctively looked up at the azure bird circling in the sky.

As Black King said, this demon was indeed a great poisonous demon, and also a good material for him to cultivate the *Hundred Poisons Body Refinement Art*.

But this was the Southern Ocean...

"Forget it. I still have a good stock of Poisonous Millet. It's enough for me to cultivate for a while. Better not make trouble."

The azure bird seemed to sense something, aware of the danger. It flapped its wings and flew forward, distancing itself from the two.

"Sharp instincts."

Luo Chen smiled and withdrew his gaze.

Then he estimated the time.

It had been a month since entering the Southern Ocean.

Because both he and Black King were injured, their forward speed wasn't too fast, to avoid bumping into some formidable creatures in the Southern Ocean.

But now, except for the Mystic Dust Armor, which couldn't be restored due to lack of materials, everything else was essentially no different from its peak state.

Black King was also about the same.

So next, they could go all out.

The sooner they returned to the Luo Tian Sect, the sooner his son Luo Lingxi could begin to recover and cultivate.

It was time to speed up!

With this thought, Luo Chen immediately issued the command.

Black King let out a cheer and accelerated.

The Southern Ocean was not friendly to him. Whether in the black misty air or the surging seawater, gu insects appeared from time to time.

Especially the deeper they went, the more gu insects there were.

Even though Luo Chen constantly emitted the aura of the Calamity Gu remaining on the Primordial Unity Cauldron, it couldn't prevent some low-intelligence, low-rank gu insects from bumping into them.

Black King couldn't wait to leave early.

His massive body twisted, pushing aside the waves, moving forward quickly.

Wherever he passed, startling waves rose like a tsunami.

From time to time, some formidable creatures were startled, but under the Calamity Gu aura Luo Chen emitted, none dared to approach.

This feeling of rampaging without restraint made Black King occasionally let out long roars and dragon cries.

This day.

Feeling bored with the journey, Luo Chen took out the *Secret Record of the Xuan Realm* that the Divine Lord Youli had given him and began to read.

He also wanted to seriously understand what kind of scenery lay behind the origins of the five sacred lands of the Mountains and Seas Realm.

But before he could start reading, Black King's voice came again.

"Master, that blue-feathered beast is haunting us again. It's appeared."

Luo Chen frowned and looked up.

Sure enough, several li away in the sky, the Blue-Eyed Ghost Hornbill was flapping its wings, circling back and forth.

"Wretched beast, do you truly seek death..."

As he spoke, Luo Chen's expression gradually changed.

That wasn't the waiting of a hunter, but the cowering of fear!

"No! Fall back quickly!"

Black King's reaction was a bit slow. He asked in confusion, "Why has the seawater turned yellow?"

Then, he felt a great force suddenly come from his back.

It was Luo Chen, grabbing through the air, seizing his massive body and flinging it backward.

Black King was in midair, full of shock. He looked back.

"Master?"

Vast dharma power surged into the sky but dissipated in an instant.

Golden flames spread out, but were extinguished completely by a single wave of turbid yellow seawater.

Between the rising and falling waves, blue-white flames flickered and gradually disappeared.

Watching this scene, Black King's mouth fell open, and his entire body was completely seized by fear.

"Master!"
