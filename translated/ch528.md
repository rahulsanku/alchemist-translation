# Chapter 528: Rebuilding the Combat System

---

Driving the tiger to devour the wolf.

Cheng Dou, head of the Cheng clan, had made his thoughts quite obvious. One could even say he had barely bothered to disguise them.

All he wanted was for Luo Chen to act against Senior Nantian, who occupied the Swallow Islands.

As for whether removing Nantian would mean inviting another wolf into the house?

That concern was likely something the other party no longer cared about.

Luo Chen's eye was sharp. The instant they met, he had discovered Cheng Dou's realm.

It was in a suppressed state, only one step from Initiate Perfected Completion.

So long as Cheng Dou released that suppression, he could enter Perfected Completion and then charge toward Adept.

Perhaps the suspended sword of his realm was nearly driving Cheng Dou mad, which was why he had resorted to such desperate measures.

Moreover, even if it was inviting a wolf indoors, the Cheng clan would still receive far better treatment than the other clans of the Swallow Islands.

"His knowledge is limited, but his eye is decent."

Luo Chen chuckled.

The other party's demands were actually aligned with his own objective.

It was just that if he wanted to scheme for the Swallow Islands, he still had to take the long view.

At the very least, he needed to learn Senior Nantian's methods clearly first.

In addition, during his time on Green Parasol Mountain, being trapped and unable to act freely had caused many of Luo Chen's methods to stagnate. This period was perfect for cultivating them properly.

After switching to fire cultivation, his combat system had also become rather chaotic. It needed sorting out.

Once he made up his mind, Luo Chen first looked at the black wooden figurine inside a small compartment of the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Han Zhan had still not awakened.

"It seems Nirvana Sacred Flame dealt him extremely severe damage."

Inside the black wooden figurine, a wisp of remnant soul clung to life. In every movement, it no longer possessed the bearing of a Sage true person from days past.

With only this remnant soul left, he might never have any possibility of recovery.

In truth, this was also why Luo Chen dared keep Han Zhan at his side.

Weak. Declining. Easy to control.

He sent out mana to isolate the Primordial Unity Cauldron, then shifted his gaze to the five Blue-Ringed Fang Serpents confined by the cauldron's chains.

Even though their bodies had been forcibly shrunk to the extreme, their savage might remained undiminished.

Just releasing them earlier had made Cheng Haixin, newly entered into Initiate, lose color in her face. Their ferocity was plain.

However, when Luo Chen's gaze landed on them, of the five sea serpents, all except the late-stage second-tier one showed fear. Their bodies trembled uncontrollably.

Luo Chen frowned, then realized something.

"Is it because I killed over ten thousand of their fellow sea serpents in one breath, leaving dense baleful qi on me?"

So-called baleful qi was not limited to the vicious energy born in filthy and dangerous places of heaven and earth.

It could also refer to the great amount of special "aura" left on one's body from long involvement in slaughter, after enemies died one after another.

Within that aura were one's own killing intent and hostility, as well as the resentment, fear, agitation, and terror enemies left before death.

All of these gathered together, forming a particular "spiritual balefulness."

Baleful qi condensed from thoughts and emotions.

This sort of spiritual balefulness was felt most deeply by one's own kind.

When Luo Chen killed people in the past, baleful qi would also be left on him, but most of the time he removed it afterward with a Cleansing Art.

This time, things had been rushed, and he had not removed it.

Or rather, he had done it deliberately.

In a place like the Demon Sea, if one did not look vicious, trouble would easily arise.

If one looked especially "fierce," many troublesome matters would instead be avoided.

Just like now.

"Perfect. Borrowing their fear of me, I can enslave them as guards."

Luo Chen smiled faintly. With one hand, he reached out. Mana formed a palm and captured an early second-tier Fang Serpent alive.

He flicked a finger, sending a pill into the serpent's mouth, then drove it with mana and forcibly refined it.

In an instant, once the pill took effect, the Fang Serpent's body went rigid and lost part of its ability to resist.

But only part.

"This batch of Beast-Enslaving Pills I obtained in my early years is truly too low-grade. They are only lower second-tier."

"Fortunately, combined with their fear of me, the Spirit-Enslaving Seal should be quite smooth to use."

With a thought, Luo Chen's ten fingers danced like embroidery needles. One incantation after another silently left his lips, then he abruptly struck out with his palm.

In the void, an illusory palm seal appeared.

It was precisely the Spirit-Enslaving Seal.

The palm struck down and landed on the Fang Serpent's crown.

At once, the Fang Serpent twisted and struggled on the ground with savage ferocity, shrieking madly.

But everything was futile.

In only the time it took to drink a cup of tea, this Blue-Ringed Fang Serpent quietly crouched on the ground.

"Come here."

Luo Chen beckoned, and the sea serpent hissed as it crawled toward him, coiling at his side and revealing an ingratiating, obedient manner.

Seeing this, Luo Chen could not help showing satisfaction.

The Myriad Beast Sutra, this beast-enslaving method, had been obtained years ago from the Zhenlong Black Market.

It contained a beast-enslaving mental method, matching beast-enslaving pills, and special control commands.

In his early years, he had relied on this method to enslave a second-tier Explosive Ape, and during the battle to attack Little Huan Mountain and destroy the Duan clan, it had produced excellent results.

That enslavement had been extremely difficult and consumed much of his mind. It had even nearly failed.

But this time, it was so easy it could be called catching them by hand.

The gap between Adept and Qi Refinement was simply too great.

The same cultivation method or secret art produced effects as different as heaven and earth when used on enemies of different levels.

After enslaving one sea serpent, Luo Chen did not feel tired. His gaze fell on the remaining four.

"One by one, then."

...

Time flowed little by little.

Sea-Watching Cliff, five hundred zhang tall, towered above the boundless sea, shielding Black Swan Island from violent sea winds and threats from the depths.

In previous years, the Cheng clan would send low-tier cultivators to patrol and guard the island.

But at some point, Sea-Watching Cliff had become a forbidden ground of the Cheng clan, and unrelated people were prohibited from entering.

Not only that, clusters of white cloud and mist now rose there, blocking outside prying.

At the manor beside Little Mirror Lake.

Cheng Dou looked anxiously at the tall woman standing before him.

"Seventh Sister!"

Cheng Haixin curled her lip, looking utterly carefree.

Tilting her head to look at her elder brother, she smiled. "Calling me is useless. That Senior has been in seclusion recently. I have no way to curry favor with him."

Cheng Dou frowned. "Is it that you have no way? Or that you don't want to? Didn't he give you a token allowing free entry to Sea-Watching Cliff? You should go there more often and walk around..."

"Walk around where?" Cheng Haixin's smile gradually faded. "Let your own sister walk into his bed?"

That was an ugly thing to say.

Cheng Dou shook his head and said with frustration, "Although that Senior has a heavy killing nature, he still acts with principles. Entrusting yourself to him would not be humiliation. Besides, becoming the concubine of an Adept cultivator is a fortune countless low-level female cultivators could not obtain even if they envied it."

"Then I'll give you this fortune. Do you want it?" Cheng Haixin retorted without yielding.

Cheng Dou's expression froze, but soon he pointed to himself.

"I only hate that I was not born a woman."

Cheng Haixin stared, almost thinking she had misheard.

Cheng Dou sighed. "It is not that your big brother is not human. It is truly that Yan Nantian, that wretch, has put too much pressure on me. If I keep holding back from Perfected Completion, problems will arise in my realm. But once I reach Initiate Perfected Completion, Yan Nantian will inevitably persecute me. I also cannot imitate the Jiaolong Gang's leader and flee far overseas. If I leave, this vast Cheng clan will have no great cultivator holding it down and will instantly fall to the ranks of minor forces. Our circumstances then will only be worse."

"Seventh Sister, you must understand your big brother."

This time, Cheng Haixin fell silent.

One who does not hold the position does not plan its policies.

But her elder brother's restraint, effort, and struggle over the years had all been seen by their brothers and sisters.

For the development of the Cheng clan, everyone had exhausted their strength.

Second Uncle Cheng Ji was already old, yet he still personally went to sea and steered the ship.

Fourth Brother Cheng Haichang was clearly stuck at Initiate Stage Three, yet he still boarded the demon-hunting ship to open new sources of wealth for the Cheng clan, without even time for quiet cultivation or seclusion.

By comparison, she had spent these years under the clan's protection, smoothly cultivating to Initiate, and had never contributed to the clan.

In that respect, she truly had wronged the others who had worked so hard.

Seeing her silence, Cheng Dou's spirits rose, and he added more fuel to the fire.

"As long as your relationship with Senior Azure Yang goes one step further and you persuade him to drive away Yan Nantian and seize the Swallow Islands, then the thirty-six islands of Swallow will belong to my Cheng clan."

"And I can take advantage of the momentum to charge into Adept. Once I succeed, I will be an Adept cultivator, able to stand equal with Senior Azure Yang."

"Seventh Sister, think about it. When the time comes, your Dao companion will be an Adept Senior, and your elder brother will also be an Adept cultivator. Forget the Swallow Islands, even in all the nearby islands large and small, you could walk sideways."

"Would that not be good?"

Cheng Haixin was wavering now.

The cultivation life she had imagined was not like this.

She had thought that once she reached Initiate, she would be a true cultivator and could roam the seas, comprehending the Great Dao.

But now the burden of the clan pressed down on her, making it hard for her to breathe.

Striking while the iron was hot, Cheng Dou grasped her arm and said with heartfelt sincerity, "Whether the Cheng clan can rise and unify the thirty-six Swallow Islands rests on your single thought. Seventh Sister, do not disappoint me, and do not disappoint the spirits in heaven of the Cheng clansmen who died in battle over the past century."

Cheng Haixin opened her mouth, but in the end, only dejection remained.

"Then I will go up the mountain and try again."

...

The sea wind was salty, white mist ethereal.

A stunningly beautiful woman carrying a trace of desolation held a token and walked alone along the mountain path of Sea-Watching Cliff.

She walked and thought, her mind in turmoil.

Before she realized it, she had arrived at the rough cave residence that had only been opened three months ago.

She activated the token subconsciously and entered the cave residence.

But the empty cave made her freeze.

"Senior Azure Yang isn't here?"

"Where did he go?"

"He can't have already left, can he?"

In an instant, pallor appeared on her cheeks.

She did not dare immediately report to her elder brother, lest she disappoint him. Instead, she decided to wait here for a while.

Judging by the cave residence's furnishings, Senior Azure Yang should not have left yet.

She would wait a little longer.

...

Upon the endless azure sea.

Five enormous sea serpents, each several zhang long with bodies fully extended, swam through the deep.

The positions they occupied formed a circle, enclosing more than a hundred li of sea.

It seemed they were standing guard.

Their gazes occasionally turned toward the sky, toward the black-robed man standing against the wind in empty air.

Suddenly.

The black-robed man lightly pushed out his right palm. The palm print faced downward, toward the sea.

This palm carried not the slightest trace of smoke or fire.

But the instant the palm print appeared, it shifted from white to red and emitted boundless heat.

Not only that, a wisp of azure appeared upon it.

When the palm print landed on the calm sea surface, stillness turned to motion in a single instant.

Boom!

A heaven-shaking explosion rang across several hundred li of sea.

Deep in the ocean, an enormous five-fingered depression emerged.

Within the hollow, not a single drop of seawater remained. Only endless azure flame coiled there.

When the air waves surged into the sky, seawater from every direction began rushing in to fill the depression.

Yet no matter how much seawater poured in, those azure flames could not be extinguished.

A flame that even immeasurable seawater could not put out?

If anyone saw this bizarre sight, their eyes might fall out in shock.

In the sky, Luo Chen watched the scene with satisfaction, surprise and delight showing in his eyes.

The Great Handprint of Primordial Qi had finally reached Perfected Mastery.

Not only that, during his practice, he had combined it with the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture and gradually fused his azure flame into this third-tier spell.

At this moment, the Great Handprint of Primordial Qi he unleashed was composed of incomparably pure fire-attribute mana and source true fire.

Its power was extremely great.

By Luo Chen's assessment, this single palm was enough to contend with the late third-tier Gold Wolf King that had charged into him years ago.

Moreover, he had also grasped the optimal speed for casting this art.

A wave of his palm was enough.

He did not even need to borrow the Mystic Flame Sword and use the method of controlling spells through an artifact.

Or perhaps the Mystic Flame Sword, that inferior treasure flying sword, was already incapable of bearing the power of this palm.

"Calling it the Great Handprint of Primordial Qi now is clearly unsuitable. I'll change its name to the Azure Yang Great Handprint."

"In addition, the limits of the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture are far beyond this."

"Through this cultivation method, I should be able to use source true fire in every aspect."

"Once I grow familiar with it, my combat strength will not fall but rise."

All along, Luo Chen had secretly held this worry.

After cultivating the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture, all his mana had converted into fire-attribute mana. The other four elements no longer existed.

That meant many methods he excelled in could no longer be displayed.

If that happened, he would become what people called an embroidered pillow: possessing realm, but no matching combat strength.

Especially over the last few months, those worries had become reality when he cast other spells.

The defensive spell Glazed Heavenly Canopy, which he had cultivated to Perfected Mastery in his early years, could no longer be cast.

Kusanagi Geng Metal Sword Art, Wave Art, Entangling Art, and others had all developed problems.

Even though he could rely on Perfected Mastery proficiency to barely cast them with preparation, who would give him that much preparation time in battle?

Fortunately, loss came with gain.

Gradually, through practice on the open sea, Luo Chen slowly explored a combat path more suited to his current self.

Although most of it was fire-based, it was also becoming comprehensive.

For attack, he possessed a series of Perfected Mastery fire spells: Blazing Sun Art, Mountain Collapse, Azure Yang Great Handprint, and Blazing Fire Miasma.

In addition, he could pair them with the Mystic Flame Sword. Its function was singular, but its power was even greater.

As for defense, there was little to say. In his early years, Luo Chen had relied on Glazed Heavenly Canopy and his own physique to withstand attacks.

Now that Glazed Heavenly Canopy was gone, he had the Primordial Unity Cauldron to supplement it. In any case, his body could endure the rebound force.

Moreover, Luo Chen had already begun developing fire-based defensive spells.

Back when he was in Qi Refinement, he had held similar concepts for fire-based defensive spells. He simply had not continued exploring them then.

Now, with the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture and his proficiency panel, he was confident he could derive the defensive art best suited to him.

Aside from offense and defense, for movement, he had Nine Thousand Li empowered by the Broken Moon Wings.

This art was now even faster than before.

Especially because after so many years of constant refinement, the Broken Moon Wings had already shed all impurities and become perfectly unified. Among all his treasures, they had the highest degree of refinement.

It was even possible that others would mistake them for Luo Chen's life-bound treasure.

That covered long-distance movement. For short-distance movement, Luo Chen had combined Free and Unfettered Wandering, Wang Yuan's Rising Dragon Step, and his own powerful physique to work out a method of shifting form and swapping shadows.

He called it the Voidburst Step.

With a thought, Luo Chen leapt backward, then charged forward, swaying left and right in lateral movement.

In an instant, fiery afterimages appeared in the void.

As he moved, explosive cracks rang through the air.

If Tao Wan of Azure Pill Valley saw this scene, she would likely be greatly shocked.

It looked very much like her Nine Gale Transformations.

But clearly, the essence of this art did not stop there.

"Azure Frost..."

Luo Chen stopped and revealed envy.

Relying on her racial talent, Azure Frost had grasped the barest surface of spatial laws at the Sage stage and combined it with body cultivation, thereby suppressing peers in speed.

When she moved, she appeared and vanished like a ghost, impossible to guard against.

Luo Chen had only glimpsed her method in passing, but that woman's bearing had left a deep impression on him.

He had imitated Azure Frost's method and worked out Voidburst Step.

Using body cultivation as the foundation, supplemented with Free and Unfettered Wandering, Rising Dragon Step, and the mana cultivated through the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture.

In this way, his Voidburst Step also possessed similar effects.

In short-distance movement, it was filled with explosive power, as though a blazing sun burst in the air.

Although it was only the surface of Azure Frost's method, at the Adept level, it could definitely be considered an excellent method, and ordinary people could not cultivate it.

After all, no one else possessed Luo Chen's physique or his body of fire-attribute mana filled with extreme explosive power.

"I no longer lack attack or movement. I also have Mirror Flower Water Moon to support combat."

"Once I thoroughly develop the defensive side, I can be said to have no remaining weak points."

Luo Chen let out a long breath.

From this point on, the hidden worry he had felt after first arriving in the Northern Sea completely dispersed.

With a movement of divine sense, he sent out an order.

Before long, the five Blue-Ringed Fang Serpents gathered in one place.

Luo Chen descended from the sky, stepped onto the serpent king's head, and pointed in a direction. The serpent king carried Luo Chen away in leisurely travel.

Standing on the serpent's head with sea wind striking his face, Luo Chen thought about his next plans.

It was time to seek a third-tier spirit land for himself.

No matter how good a cultivation method was, it was still only the software.

Without the hardware support of "spirit land," his cultivation speed could not rise.

In addition to spirit land, he also had to reconsider pills.

The medicinal power of the Starfield Pill was too weak, and too mixed.

It was no longer very suitable for his current self, who cultivated only fire-attribute mana.

As for what pill to change to?

Luo Chen already had a plan in mind.

...

When he reached the base of Sea-Watching Cliff, Luo Chen issued an order, and the five Blue-Ringed Fang Serpents dispersed.

With Sea-Watching Cliff as their center, they roamed and swept the nearby waters while remaining alert for approaching high-tier demon beasts.

Luo Chen leapt up and flew toward the cave residence.

"Hm?"

White mist parted. Looking at the woman sitting on the threshold, deep asleep, Luo Chen showed surprise.

She was still an Initiate true cultivator, after all.

Although Cheng Haixin had fallen asleep while waiting bitterly because she was worried over clan matters and conflicted about her own future, now that Luo Chen had returned and had not concealed his aura, she woke immediately.

The woman stood nervously. "Senior..."

Luo Chen walked into the cave residence on his own. Seeing her still waiting outside, he shook his head.

"Stop standing foolishly outside. Come in."

Joy stirred in the woman's heart, and she quietly walked in.

Seeing her nervous appearance, Luo Chen could not help smiling helplessly.

"Is there something? Or do you want to warm my bed again?"

In an instant, the woman's face flushed crimson.

She pinched the corner of her robe, then seemed to remember something and hurriedly took an object from her storage bag.

It was a jade slip.

"Senior, my elder brother entrusted me to deliver this to you."

After Luo Chen accepted it, she added somewhat awkwardly, "Actually, warming the bed is not impossible."

Luo Chen chuckled and browsed the jade slip in front of her.

Gradually, the corners of Luo Chen's mouth rose, forming a pleasing arc.

That Cheng Dou truly could not hold himself back.

*(End of Chapter)*
