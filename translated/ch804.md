# Chapter 804: Canglong’s Wound, Reforging the Ghost God Heart-Inquiring Mirror?

How to treat Canglong?

This matter was extremely tricky for an Adept-stage cultivator.

The reason was that her injuries stemmed from a failed breakthrough to the Sage stage, with all three treasures—essence, qi, and spirit—damaged.

To survive, Canglong had forcibly sealed herself using Icecastle’s secret arts.

To undo that sealing art, one needed strength surpassing Canglong’s own realm.

That meant the Sage stage!

This single condition alone had blocked the self-rescue methods of Jueqing the Fairy and her companions.

Moreover, under Luo Chen’s observation, another restriction barred outsiders from probing with divine sense.

“It was left by Han Zhan!”

In the cave dwelling, Luo Chen released his divine sense, gently penetrating the ice coffin.

He had not yet learned the sealing art called “Ice Pole Seal,” so he could not undo Canglong’s seal and forcibly wake her. But with his formidable divine sense, he could already inspect her condition.

He recognized the soul restriction left by Han Zhan at a glance.

This restriction was not difficult; it could be undone with the Aperture-Thatching Art.

The hard part was: after undoing the restriction and the seal, how would Canglong manage herself?

“The qi sea is damaged, the golden core is dormant. It can be repaired with Canghai Pills, supplemented by other mild elixirs for slow recovery.”

“Her dharma power is not lacking. There is indeed a hidden, vast dharma power at the heart aperture. Once Canglong regains her will, she can instantly revive the golden core.”

“What truly limits her is the injury to her soul.”

In Luo Chen’s perception, there were faintly two souls within Canglong’s sea of consciousness.

It looked very much like the divided-soul method he himself cultivated, but in reality, both souls were incomplete.

After a brief thought, Luo Chen understood the reason for this condition.

To break through to the Sage stage, a cultivator must unite the three treasures and shatter the golden core to form the nascent soul!

These three treasures—essence, qi, and spirit—required balance.

Take Luo Chen back then: his physical body was at the fourth tier, his dharma power at golden core great perfection was nearly comparable to a newly ascended Sage, and his soul was no weaker—always surpassing his peers. All three had reached an extreme balance, so the nascent soul he condensed was exceptionally powerful.

But for ordinary cultivators, the so-called balance of the three treasures placed the lowest demand on the physical body.

What mattered most was the game between divine sense and dharma power.

Canglong’s dharma power was too strong, but her soul was slightly weak, breaking the balance of essence, qi, and spirit.

Moreover, when she attempted to form the nascent soul, the environment was not safe. The Whitebone Dark Serpent threatened from outside, adding more concerns to her breakthrough.

In her attempt to forcibly control the dharma power, under the bombardment of thunder force, her soul was actually split apart.

Logically, under such circumstances, a cultivator would be doomed.

But fortunately, Han Zhan—an expert in the dao of souls—was by her side. He forcibly gathered her remnant souls and supplemented them with the ice coffin, allowing her to linger on until now.

Once Luo Chen understood this, a path to treat Canglong became traceable.

Repair her soul!

In this regard, Luo Chen actually had experience!

Black King was saved by him.

But the problem came: the real credit for saving Black King went to the remnant soul of the Archaic Stone Dragon. Where could Luo Chen find another remnant soul to use now?

Even if he had one, Canglong was human. Luo Chen dared not casually fuse it with her.

For a moment, Luo Chen was caught in a dilemma.

But when his divine sense swept over the woman’s brain in the ice coffin once more, he suddenly recalled something.

When treating Black King, Han Zhan had mentioned that he had tried to research a set of methods to treat Canglong.

Unfortunately, by the time they reached the Northern Sea, that method could only be used on Black King.

Strengthen the injured party’s own soul, letting it save itself.

Luo Chen had no other resources back then, so he could only throw in the Archaic Stone Dragon’s remnant soul and treat it as a desperate gamble.

But now, he actually had abundant resources!

Great Soul Pills!

This pill was precisely the miraculous medicine Luo Chen had specially used to strengthen his own divided souls.

Both of his divided souls had been gradually built up from the Adept stage.

Luo Chen extrapolated and had even more ideas.

“Right now, Canglong’s main soul is split. This is actually the perfect time for her to cultivate the *Soul-Splitting Art*, just like I did back then. If she can take the opportunity to cut out a divided soul and supplement it with Great Soul Pills, she can not only repair her soul but also gain an extra divided soul.”

“Furthermore, once the soul is strengthened, she can perfectly control the vast dharma power within her body.”

“As long as her physical body is preserved intact, she might even have a chance to forcibly break through to the Sage stage again when she awakens.”

At this thought, Luo Chen’s heart stirred.

But the problem came again.

Canglong, now unconscious, how could she cultivate a new technique? She probably didn’t even have the energy to refine a Great Soul Pill.

“Am I just wishful thinking?”

Luo Chen felt somewhat dejected.

He sighed, waved his hand to cast a restriction, and properly sealed the ice coffin.

The corresponding treatment plan would have to be studied bit by bit, ensuring no mistakes.

…

The defection of the Icecastle disciples did not add any trouble for Luo Chen.

Nowadays, within Lingtian Pass, many people were willing to give him face, let alone just a dozen or so insignificant female cultivators.

On the contrary, because of Jueqing the Fairy’s involvement, the Luo Tian Sect gained considerable benefits.

The reason was simple!

Hadn’t Luo Chen distributed a large batch of talismans earlier?

Those talismans were basically allocated to low-level disciples for combat readiness.

But talismans were not as simple as they seemed.

Making talismans and using talismans—both were full of knowledge.

The Divine Talisman Sect sold so many talismans yet still held a lofty position in the dao of talismans precisely because they had a separate core method for how to use them.

This kind of method was something Luo Chen had always wanted.

But neither Lord Lushan nor Talisman Elder would easily give it to him.

This time, however, Jueqing the Fairy took the initiative to teach the Luo Tian Sect cultivators how to use talismans properly.

She was used to dealing with Luo Chen based on a transaction model.

But this time, Luo Chen had saved her senior sister without asking for any reward, which made her a bit uneasy.

Jueqing the Fairy herself was a talisman expert. Back then, she had given Luo Chen third-tier Ice God Talismans and Ice Talismans, showing her skill.

Under her guidance, the Luo Tian Sect cultivators quickly learned some techniques and experience for using talismans.

How to activate them in advance yet hold back the release, how to unleash them quickly without damaging the talisman’s power, how to combine different talismans for use, and so on.

What Luo Chen had once sought in vain was now delivered to him in this manner.

“So it’s that simple?”

Luo Chen looked at the Blazing Sun Talisman in his hand, which was activated but not yet released. He could feel the power that could be detonated at any moment.

Originally, to activate a talisman, one needed to inject dharma power, use divine sense to control it to fly toward the enemy, and try not to harm oneself.

But according to Jueqing the Fairy’s method, one only needed to separate a dharma power seed in advance and attach it to the talisman. When it was time to use it, a single thought of divine sense could activate it.

This skipped the process of injecting dharma power—a process that seemed short but was actually very long in battle.

Of course, some things looked simple, like a layer of window paper.

But without an experienced person to point it out, one might spend a lifetime unable to poke through that layer.

Moreover, attaching the dharma power seed also required special techniques. It was not simply a matter of striking a dharma power in advance.

“This is just Jueqing the Fairy’s personal opinion. The Divine Talisman Sect must have even more exquisite talisman-control arts!”

Luo Chen sighed and put away the Blazing Sun Talisman.

For his current realm, these were ultimately minor arts, just a supplement.

His focus was still on his previous plans.

Lingfengzi’s pills were not urgent.

His reputation had already risen. If he refined them too quickly, others might ask him to refine pills for them.

That would instead take up too much of his time.

Stretching out the pill-refining time could also highlight the difficulty of his work.

For now, he planned to first refine Caiyi’s lifebound magic treasure.

With the blueprint and main materials, this was not difficult for him, especially since he had the Primordial Unity Cauldron in hand!

This treasure had shown extremely special effects when refining the Mystic Dust Armor back then, with an exceptionally high success rate.

Later, Luo Chen figured out that the credit came from the drop of Devil Lord Heaven-Refining’s blood.

After entering the Demon-Fall Land, he understood even more clearly why the devil lord’s blood had such heaven-defying effects. The other party was a polymath, with extraordinary skill in the dao of artifact refinement as well.

From this perspective, although Luo Chen had only obtained the other’s alchemy inheritance, hadn’t he also stolen a bit of the legacy in artifact dao?

“Nine Nether Charm Python, water-attributed.”

A wisp of dharma power seeped into the large sinew, faintly stirring blood qi.

The blood qi had a delicious fragrance, refreshing the mind and intoxicating.

“It carries a charming fragrance that can confuse a cultivator’s sense of smell, even affecting divine sense. And this is just one of its large sinews. If the Nine Nether Charm Python were in its prime, even Sage-stage cultivators might be affected.”

Luo Chen praised, then took out a jade bottle from his storage ring.

Facing this jade bottle, Luo Chen’s expression became noticeably more solemn.

Because inside was the most famous Nether Water of the Nether Origin Sect!

Legend had it that this water was one of the world’s extraordinary waters. Once a living being touched it, their flesh would rot. Even magic treasures and sharp weapons could hardly resist it.

Its mysterious effects surpassed those of Profound Origin Heavy Water, Ren Water, and True Water of Heaven.

The Yellow Spring around the Soul-Breaking Cliff and the black water in the Fallen Sea were probably slightly inferior to it.

And such rare and extraordinary water, the Nether Origin Sect had plenty!

It was an entire abyss that completely surrounded the Nether Origin Sect’s mountain gate, making it hard for living beings to approach!

From this perspective, among the five sacred lands of the Mountains and Seas Realm, aside from the Tianyuan Dao Sect in Central Continent, the other four sacred lands were built within perilous forbidden grounds, easy to defend but hard to attack.

The Primordial Demon Sect’s Arctic Yemo Heaven, the Nether Origin Sect’s Nether Abyss, the Hanging Temple’s Green Screen, and the Life and Death Gate’s… Life and Death Gate.

The Life and Death Gate was somewhat peculiar; the forbidden ground it occupied had the same name as the sect.

But let’s set that aside.

Luo Chen looked at the Nether Water in the bottle, looking eager to try.

“I wonder if my Primordial Unity Cauldron can withstand the erosion of Nether Water?”

He wanted to compare, but in the end, he did not put it into practice.

If he damaged his lifebound magic treasure, he would have nowhere to cry.

“After the patrol mission ends, Caiyi should also finish her seclusion. Giving her a lifebound magic treasure when we return would be just right.”

Luo Chen smiled slightly and no longer hesitated, beginning the preparatory work for refining the magic treasure.

…

Time flowed slowly.

In the blink of an eye, more than a year had passed since Luo Chen arrived at Lingtian Pass.

For the cultivators at Lingtian Pass, this year was relatively peaceful.

Although small patrol teams were occasionally attacked by demon beasts, they never encountered large groups of demon beast troops.

And in the hearts of high-level cultivators, they naturally did not want to fight to the death with those cruel demon kings and demon emperors.

Luo Chen’s whereabouts were rarely seen within the city. Even some Sage-stage cultivators found it inconvenient to disturb him.

Because everyone knew that he had accepted Lingfengzi’s commission and was wholeheartedly refining an important pill for him.

To prevent Luo Chen from being disturbed, Lingfengzi even took the initiative to accept a small mission for him—leaving the city to investigate the demon beast situation in a fallen great domain.

This made Luo Chen, who learned of it later, feel quite guilty.

He still hadn’t refined the pills for Lingfengzi yet.

Although that pill formula had already been thoroughly studied by the Red Dog Puppet, his main energy this past year had been spent on refining the Scarlet Firmament Qi and Gu Caiyi’s lifebound magic treasure.

This day!

Luo Chen floated in midair, his sharp eyes fixed on the great cauldron below.

Firelight flickered, spiritual energy steamed.

The cauldron’s mouth was like a small world.

Inside, a long, snake-like object kept swimming, occasionally spitting out wisps of demonic qi.

“If I want this treasure to reveal the demon emperor’s true form in the future, I must preserve its demonic power. But to prevent the cultivator from being affected by the demonic power during the refining process, I need to add extra restrictions and formations.”

“For me, it’s not difficult!”

Luo Chen smiled slightly as he struck out streams of hand seals, sinking into the long object.

A large amount of blood qi was suppressed and sealed by him.

This large sinew seemed to still have remnant spirituality, unwilling to become another’s weapon. It suddenly unleashed its last bit of power.

Luo Chen, who was applying the hand seals, inadvertently inhaled a little.

But he did not mind. With his physique, such power could not affect him in the slightest.

The illusions that were about to appear vanished before they could form.

“Perhaps if the Nine Nether Charm Python itself were here, I would need to be careful. But just the remnant power of a single large sinew wants to make me hallucinate and disturb my mind… Hmm?”

Luo Chen’s hand paused slightly.

In that pause, the long whip rose up, manifesting a colorful python phantom in the cauldron.

Was this the true form of the Nine Nether Charm Python?

Luo Chen snorted coldly and struck out the final restriction.

In an instant, all the formations and restrictions fused into one, generating a powerful pulling force that forcibly dragged the colorful python phantom into the long whip.

Luo Chen’s mind moved, and the Withering-Bloom True Flame immediately dissipated.

He reached out and summoned the long whip into his hand.

A brief perception made him show a look of regret.

Middle grade!

It was only a hair’s breadth away from upper-grade magic treasure!

This should not have happened.

Back then, using the Xuanyan Turtle’s remains, he had refined the upper-grade Mystic Dust Armor.

Now, this long whip, also using a demon emperor’s remains as the main material and even adding such rare substances as Nether Water, had only reached middle grade.

The reason was not that his artifact refinement skill was lacking.

But at the final critical moment, he had been slightly distracted, causing the embedding of restrictions and formations to deviate, thus affecting the treasure’s grade.

What had distracted him was precisely the charm python’s demonic qi emitted from the sinew at the last moment.

Logically, such demonic qi could not affect him at all.

And indeed, Luo Chen was not affected.

But at the moment when the illusion was about to appear but had not yet appeared, he felt a sense of déjà vu, which triggered reverie.

In a single thought, all his efforts were wasted.

Holding the hot long whip, Luo Chen captured the sudden inspiration from just now.

Illusion… illusion realm.

Charm python demonic qi… illusion beast cloud qi!

His left hand shook, and a core shrouded in mist appeared in his palm.

It was precisely the spoils from the final battle before leaving the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven—a fourth-tier mid-stage illusion beast’s illusion core.

He had secretly brought it out. The Tianyuan Dao Sect did not know, and even Fu Qinglan had not mentioned this matter to the sect.

Looking at this illusion core, Luo Chen fell into a long silence.

“If I use this treasure as the core, can I reforge the Ghost God Heart-Inquiring Mirror?”
