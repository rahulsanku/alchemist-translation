# Chapter 1028: Deep and Vast Great Clans, Precocious Youth

Drip!

Drip!

For several years now, the sky over the three-thousand-li White Egret Marsh had been perpetually overcast.

During this chilly spring season, the dreary rain fell without end.

On the eaves, fine raindrops gathered into trickling streams, continuously falling to form beaded curtains. Even beneath the sheltered corridor eaves of the three-courtyard compound, water droplets condensed from moisture dripped down with a steady patter.

Thump! Thump! Thump!

Faint but crisp footsteps sounded urgently, drawing closer from afar, gradually growing more dense.

Soon, a little girl with a pair of goat-horn braids and a face no bigger than a palm came running in from outside.

The little girl was very cute, with large eyes like pure black gemstones, a pert little nose, and thin lips. Only her complexion bore a sickly pallor, utterly devoid of rosiness.

She was dressed very warmly, her small body wrapped in a white velvet silk robe.

But even the white swan-down garment could not compare to the paleness of her complexion.

A voice of tender affection came from behind.

"Maomao, slow down, don't fall."

The little girl was very obedient. She paused her steps, leaning against a thick corridor pillar, panting for breath.

She said excitedly to the person behind her, "Mother, Big Brother's lessons are almost over today. I want to go play with him."

After catching her breath a little, she set off again, running through the long corridor toward a waterside pavilion hidden behind a rockery.

Behind her, a gentle young woman smiled and shook her head, following at a leisurely pace.

Although there was some distance between her and the little girl called Maomao, the girl's figure never left her sight.

Just as she was about to enter the waterside pavilion, a plainly dressed middle-aged woman bowed and withdrew.

Seeing the gentle young woman, she respectfully performed a bow.

"Greetings, Madam."

Lin Jingshu looked at her face with some confusion, hesitating, "You're from Seventh Brother's household?"

The middle-aged woman nodded. "Yes, my master heard that Young Master Fuyou was studying hard. Thinking of his good relationship with Young Master Yuqi, he specially ordered this servant to send some tonics."

Lin Jingshu suddenly understood. Her son indeed had a good relationship with Zhou Yuqi from Seventh Brother's household.

Moreover, her husband had an excellent relationship with Seventh Brother; they had once braved life and death together.

But being well-educated and sensible, she still said, "This is too extravagant."

The middle-aged woman squeezed out a smile. "It's nothing. The main thing is that Young Master Fuyou is too young. Studying so diligently might harm his body."

Lin Jingshu smiled. "Then many thanks. When I have time, I will personally visit to express my gratitude."

"Then this servant will take her leave."

Lin Jingshu nodded, her eyes fixed on the middle-aged woman as she walked through the long winding corridor.

Her husband was quite popular within the clan!

With a soft sigh, she walked through the corridor toward the waterside pavilion.

Before she even entered, she heard laughter and chatter from inside.

"Big Brother, Big Brother, is this character pronounced 'feng,' like the wind that blows?"

"That's right, Maomao."

"Then what's the next character?"

"'Che,' as in 'wind-che-dian-che'—the 'che' of 'swift as the wind and quick as lightning.' This is also one of the characters used in our Zhou clan's generational naming. 'Cang-kun-yun-xiao, hai-yu-jing-feng, che-yu-qu-cheng, lei-dian-ni-xia'... It carries the meaning of riding clouds, commanding the seas, and breaking through thunder and lightning. Our Zhou family is a cultivation clan, originating from the far northern Coral Sea. Thus, we aspire to immortality and roam the four seas. Our ancestors left behind this verse of auspicious characters."

"So complicated! And why don't our names have any of these characters, but Brother Yuqi's does?"

"Actually, you do have one. You were originally called Zhou Qingyu, but the Old Ancestor changed it to the homophonous character 'yu' for feather."

"And what about you, Big Brother?"

"Me..."

The little girl's voice was always full of curiosity and liveliness, while the boy's voice, though soft and sharp, was unhurried and remarkably steady.

But when it came to why his own name wasn't among the clan's generational characters, his tone was also filled with confusion.

Lin Jingshu chose that moment to enter. Looking at her adorable daughter and handsome son, she smiled and said:

"It's because the Old Ancestor has high hopes for your brother. Taking the meaning of 'mayfly's insignificance' is meant to urge and remind him not to slack off."

The young boy, dressed in a simple plain white Daoist robe, stood up, gently set aside the little girl clinging to him, and bowed to the woman.

"Mother!"

Lin Jingshu shook her head slightly. "This is at home. No need for such formalities. How have your lessons been lately?"

Zhou Fuyou said confidently, "I've thoroughly read thirty-two volumes of the Daoist Canon, fully understood the clan's precepts and classics, and also delved into local customs and strange tales..."

The young boy spoke unhurriedly, his words full of calm confidence.

Especially when matched with his physique, which had developed far beyond that of his twin sister, it was almost impossible to believe he was merely a five-year-old child!

Yes.

At this moment, Zhou Fuyou was only five years old, but his reputation as a child prodigy had already spread throughout the entire Zhou clan.

He could run at one, speak eloquently at two, and had mastered poetry and song by three.

Later on, no longer satisfied with ordinary literary classics, he began to delve into martial scriptures and Daoist canons related to cultivation.

Almost every elder and senior of the Zhou clan who had seen him believed that as long as he possessed a spiritual root, he would surely become a cultivation genius of the Zhou family in the future!

Lin Jingshu looked at her son, her eyes filled with pride and gratification.

But when she looked at her daughter, who was even thinner and paler than other children her age, with a complexion like white jade coral, her heart ached with unspeakable tenderness.

Born of the same mother, with barely any difference in birth date, Zhou Qingyu had been frail and sickly since childhood, and her intelligence had awakened late.

Despite the couple spending considerable resources, they had not managed to nurture her well.

To keep her free from illness and worry, they had even followed the custom of common folk and given her the humble nickname "Maomao," to make raising her easier.

Sighing, suppressing the pity in her heart, Lin Jingshu asked her son about another matter.

"What about the Chess Classic?"

Zhou Fuyou's face showed hesitation.

Lin Jingshu's expression darkened slightly. "Our Heavenly Origin Dao Sect places the greatest emphasis on the art of Go. Those who achieve anything in it are all proficient in Go. How can you be so negligent?"

Zhou Fuyou smiled wryly. "Child truly has no interest in playing Go. I have disappointed Mother."

Lin Jingshu frowned slightly, thinking that her son might have inherited only her husband's temperament.

Her Lin family were native-born commoners of the Dao Sect, but her husband's Zhou clan were guest elders of the Star Gate, having come from the Northern Sea.

Perhaps, deep in his bones, he simply disliked the affairs of the square chessboard.

Shaking her head, Lin Jingshu dropped the matter and took the initiative to say, "Your father has sent an urgent message. Traces of demon beasts have appeared along the coast. The combined forces of the Zhou, Lin, and Yin families are insufficient, so I must go to help as well."

Zhou Fuyou asked, "And the household?"

Lin Jingshu waved her hand. "Don't worry. The Old Ancestor is guarding the clan; no one would dare to offend us. I've arranged for Granny Lin to take care of things here. Though she's old, she's still at the late Qi Refinement stage. My only concern is your sister."

Zhou Fuyou understood and nodded firmly. "Mother, rest assured. I will take good care of Sister!"

Lin Jingshu smiled with relief and beckoned them over.

The previously obedient little girl ran over, and Zhou Fuyou also drew closer.

The young woman scooped up the girl with one arm and ruffled the boy's head with the other.

"It won't take long. Your father and mother will be back soon. You two behave at home."

"Mm!"

"Mm!"

Then, Lin Jingshu carried the little girl as she walked out, the young boy following closely behind.

Leaving the waterside pavilion, the previously mentioned Granny Lin was waiting quietly, her back hunched.

"Wait for us to return."

The young boy took his sister from his mother's arms and waved goodbye to her.

Lin Jingshu felt reluctant, but time was pressing. She summoned a flying sword and stepped onto it.

In the distance, a huge flying boat was already circling in the sky, with figures gathering from all directions.

Lin Jingshu smiled and waved at the children, then flew her sword up to the flying boat.

Soon, the flying boat was ready. It circled for a moment, then gradually flew away toward the coast.

On the ground.

A light drizzle fell. Zhou Fuyou could no longer see the flying boat. He picked up his sister.

"It's too cold outside. Let's go home!"

"Big Brother, when will Mother come back?"

"Soon." Zhou Fuyou rubbed the little girl's head, then said to Granny Lin beside him, "The tonics sent by Seventh Uncle's household are in the waterside pavilion. Please trouble Granny to decoct them when you have time. I'll take them tonight while I study."

Granny Lin was not a member of the Zhou family; she was a maidservant who had come with Lin Jingshu's dowry from her maiden home. So, family matters did not trouble her. But in caring for the Fuyou siblings, she was quite attentive.

Hearing this, she nodded and showed a kind smile.

"Young Master is truly diligent!"

That night.

Zhou Fuyou read by candlelight while drinking the tonic decocted by Granny Lin. He felt his spirits greatly lifted, reading ten lines at a glance.

He thought to himself that the things sent by Seventh Uncle's household were really quite good.

But on the third day, something went wrong.

Crack!

A porcelain bowl fell to the ground, shattering into several pieces.

Zhou Fuyou, who was reading, turned sideways and saw the little girl lying on the ground, her face flushed bright red, with soup at the corner of her mouth.

"Maomao!"

Zhou Fuyou's expression changed drastically. He scooped her up and at the same time rang the bell hanging by the waterside pavilion.

Soon, Granny Lin's figure appeared in the room.

"How did this happen?"

Zhou Fuyou's face showed some panic. "I don't know. Just now I was reading, and Maomao was playing beside me. Then she..."

His eyes fell on the broken bowl on the floor. There had been half a bowl of tonic left, but now only a little liquid remained.

"It's this medicine!"

Granny Lin examined Zhou Qingyu's condition and breathed a slight sigh of relief.

"There's still life in her. Don't panic. We'll go to Clan Elder Haibo. He's the most skilled in medicine."

With that, she took out a talisman and stuck it on herself, then picked up the little girl with one arm and took the young boy's hand with the other, transforming into a streak of cyan light that shot out of the mansion.

...

Late at night.

An old man said cheerfully to Granny Lin and Zhou Fuyou, "It's nothing serious. Just that her body was too weak to absorb the nourishment. This medicine has too strong a fire nature. It's actually not very suitable for children, especially Qingyu, whose constitution is too deficient. I've used medical techniques combined with pills to save Qingyu. Just let her rest and recuperate for a while."

Hearing this, Granny Lin completely breathed a sigh of relief.

She looked at Zhou Fuyou, who was full of self-blame. "Young Master Fuyou, you mustn't blame yourself."

"How can I not blame myself? Mother specifically told me to take good care of Sister before she left." Zhou Fuyou looked at his sister with heartache. The delicate, jade-like face had lost its flush, but the pallor was even worse than before, and even the translucent blood vessels were faintly visible. "It's all my fault. I shouldn't have let her touch that bowl of medicine."

Although Zhou Fuyou looked like a young boy, in truth, he had only developed too quickly. He was still only five years old.

Heartache, anxiety, panic, self-blame—many emotions showed uncontrollably on his face.

Clan Elder Haibo, who had superb medical skills, asked curiously, "This medicine strengthens the spirit and brightens the eyes, but it's actually not suitable for children. And the preparation is complex. Xiao Lin, why did you decoct it for little Fuyou to drink?"

Granny Lin hesitated. "It was sent by Seventh Master's household. And Young Master Fuyou felt very good after drinking it, so I would decoct two bowls every day."

"Oh, is that so?" Clan Elder Haibo showed a thoughtful expression, then waved his hand. "Don't brew this stuff anymore in the future. Also, I have a Fire-Dispelling Pill here. Little Fuyou, take it and eat it."

After explaining everything, he hurriedly left the room.

Granny Lin examined the pill, then handed it to Zhou Fuyou.

Zhou Fuyou looked at his still-sleeping sister and shook his head. "I'll eat it later."

...

"Old Ancestor, Haibo requests an audience!"

In a deathly silent hall, Zhou Haibo, who had been all smiles earlier, now wore a slightly solemn expression. He stood respectfully outside, waiting for instructions.

Creak...

The door slowly opened a crack, and Zhou Haibo entered.

"What is it?" A voice, weathered beyond measure, came from the pitch-black depths of the hall.

Zhou Haibo bowed his head respectfully and recounted the previous events in meticulous detail.

The weathered voice asked calmly, "It was just a mistaken use of medicine. Why the urgent need to see me?"

Zhou Haibo swallowed his saliva. "But that medicinal decoction, while appearing to strengthen the spirit, actually damages it. Cultivators below the Adept stage simply cannot see through it. If I were not skilled in medicine, I probably wouldn't have noticed either."

The weathered voice asked, "You mean to say that someone within the clan is plotting to harm a fellow clansman?"

Zhou Haibo dared not answer.

Silence fell within the hall.

After a long while, a single candle flame flickered to life, revealing an extremely aged face.

It was Zhou Yunshen.

His face was gloomy, and he sighed. "We've only been settled for a few years, and the family has just begun to rise. How can someone already be harboring petty schemes?"

Zhou Haibo said in a low voice, "Perhaps it's because little Fuyou's brilliance is too sharp. At only five years old, he has already shown heaven-granted talent. Coupled with the fact that you, Old Ancestor, bestowed upon him a precious Water Lotus Seed at his birth, it has made some people envious."

"I'm not dead yet!" Zhou Yunshen snorted coldly.

Zhou Haibo asked carefully, "Then how should we arrange for Zhou Fuyou next? His parents have gone out on a mission, and there's only a Qi Refinement-level maidservant at home to look after him."

"He cannot come to harm!" Zhou Yunshen said first, decisively, then added with some contradiction, "But we also cannot interfere too much in his growth."

Zhou Haibo was puzzled. Fuyou was just a child. Even if he was somewhat intelligent, why would the Old Ancestor pay him such attention?

He had even secretly instructed Haibo, the most skilled in medicine, to regularly check his health.

And now, why did the Old Ancestor say outright that they could not interfere too much in Zhou Fuyou's growth?

A long time passed.

Zhou Yunshen's voice slowly reached his ears.

"It's not convenient for me to show myself. Have Junxiao secretly look after Fuyou on my behalf."

The Zhou clan currently had five Adept-stage cultivators.

The strongest was naturally Zhou Cang, but he bore the heavy responsibility of breaking through to the Sage stage.

Next was Zhou Junxiao, a vigorous member of the second generation of the Xiao generation!

For the Old Ancestor to have Zhou Junxiao step forward showed how much he valued Zhou Fuyou.

But his words also contained the word "secretly," which was truly contradictory and complex.

Zhou Haibo had many doubts in his heart, but in the end, he accepted the order.

...

Back in the earlier medical clinic, just before dawn, Zhou Fuyou swallowed the pill.

At first, nothing happened.

But not long after.

"Ugh!"

A mouthful of black blood was spat onto the ground.

Zhou Fuyou stared blankly at this sight.

Although he was young, he was exceptionally intelligent. Seeing this black blood, he instantly understood what that medicinal decoction was.

"Poison!"

Combined with Clan Elder Haibo's seemingly gentle but repeatedly probing words earlier...

"Seventh Uncle's household wants to harm me?"

Murmuring, the young boy was almost incredulous.

Beside him, the little girl stirred, curling up as if she was very cold.

Zhou Fuyou covered her with a thick blanket. Watching his sister's brow relax, his heart was both warm and cold.

Warm because his sister was fine. Cold because why would Seventh Uncle's household, with whom they had always had a good relationship, want to harm him?

Clan Elder Haibo had clearly seen through it, so why didn't he speak plainly?

And when would Father and Mother return?

Zhou Fuyou unconsciously clenched his fists, looking at the deep, unfathomable Zhou clan. He felt lost and helpless, as if danger lurked everywhere.
