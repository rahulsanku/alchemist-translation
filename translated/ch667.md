# Chapter 667: Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana, Vermilion Bird Sacred Fire

One day later.

Inside a low, silent dead volcano.

The red-robed Daoist sat cross-legged, eyes peacefully closed, his aura long and steady.

Large amounts of heaven and earth spiritual qi lingered around him and continuously poured into his body.

The dense spiritual qi infusion formed whirling currents of wind that stirred the sleeves of his red robe until they rustled.

"The richness of the fire-attribute spiritual qi here is simply unimaginable!"

As his cultivation cycle neared its end, Luo Chen's thoughts became clearer. He increasingly felt that the marvel of this cultivation environment was the strongest he had ever encountered in his life!

Even though the small volcano he had chosen now only had a spiritual qi density around the lower third tier, the efficiency of absorption and refinement was enough to compare with the Star-Dipper Hall painstakingly constructed through formations across Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake.

What created such a wondrous sight was clearly the enormous difference in the density of spiritual qi drifting through heaven and earth.

If the heaven and earth spiritual qi outside was uniform...

Primarily the five elements, with wind and thunder present as well, while all kinds of different attributes of spiritual qi were mixed within it.

Cultivators relied on excellent cultivation methods and different spirit root attributes to capture and refine spiritual qi suitable for themselves from the vast world, purifying it into their own mana.

Then, within Blazing Refinement Prison, the uniform distribution of spiritual qi had been completely broken!

It was no longer uniform. Instead, fire-attribute spiritual qi occupied more than ninety percent!

In this situation, cultivators of the fire path gained a tremendous increase in the efficiency of capturing and refining qi.

One could even say there was no need to deliberately capture it. They only had to inhale with great mouthfuls and refine madly.

The small amount of other impurity spiritual qi did not affect the purity of the spiritual qi after refinement at all.

It was precisely because of this that, in only one short day, paired with a supreme-grade True Flame Pill, Luo Chen had completely replenished the mana of his second Origin Pill.

Although the newly replenished mana was still slightly restless, it did not affect ordinary combat use at all.

"A mere lower third-tier spirit land in Blazing Refinement Prison can compare to an outside upper third-tier one. Then if I find one or two middle-grade or upper-grade places, could they have a chance to rival that supreme-grade cave residence on Green Parasol Mountain?"

"If I could keep cultivating here, the Golden Core Great Perfection realm I originally estimated would still require more than a hundred years. Could I complete it in one breath and ascend in one step?"

With his cultivation cycle complete and spirit, essence, and qi abundant, Luo Chen sat in the hot cave residence and felt as comfortable as a fish in water.

In such a delighted mood, it was hard to avoid thoughts rising and surging.

Presumably, Moya Cave Master had also noticed the marvel of this place!

Unfortunately, they had entered to seek treasure, not to cultivate. Such a superior environment was being wasted.

Luo Chen sighed deeply, his face full of regret.

Just as he sighed...

Suddenly!

"Hm?"

Luo Chen gave a soft sound of surprise, and a wisp of azure flame appeared at his fingertip.

Just now, he had sensed the Withering-Bloom True Fire stirring restlessly.

Rather, from the moment he had stepped into Blazing Refinement Prison, he had felt something faintly. It was not only him. Moya Cave Master had been the same.

At that time, he had merely treated it as the natural reaction of an origin true fire under an extreme environment.

But now, he clearly sensed the agitation from the Withering-Bloom True Fire inside his qi sea becoming increasingly obvious. There was even a faint signal of "hunger" coming from it.

It was like an empty stomach, or like an itch at the back of the head.

The azure flame at his fingertip drifted and flickered. At times it rose, and at times it stretched longer, seeming somewhat confused.

In the next moment...

Hiss...

A wisp of green smoke rose from the cave wall.

"Inside?"

Luo Chen looked at the cave wall, somewhat hesitant.

He instinctively looked outside. Moya Cave Master and Tai Sui were still waiting for him.

"Then let them wait first!"

The current him had the qualification to make others wait.

Luo Chen pursed his lips, put away the Withering-Bloom True Fire, and beckoned with his hand. In an instant, a Seven Baleful Sword flew from the sword case behind him.

With a slight command, the Seven Baleful Sword immediately began rotating rapidly and stabbed into the cave wall.

Buzz!

Buzz!

Buzz!

Like a drill, the sharp sword tip continuously bored deeper into the stone wall.

Large sheets of stone chips and earth fell rustling down.

Luo Chen followed behind, step by step, walking inward.

The simple cave residence he had opened earlier was already located within the mountain belly, so heading deeper now was not difficult.

After some unknown amount of time...

Bang!

With a muffled sound, a section of stone wall was directly opened into a large hole.

Holding the long sword, Luo Chen slowly stepped out from the cave and lowered his gaze.

Gurgle...

Gurgle...

Gurgle...

The dense firelight reflected across Luo Chen's fair skin, turning it flushed red, as though he had returned to the state of the red-faced Daoist famous throughout the Myriad Immortals Assembly before he broke through to Archaic Tier Four.

But at this moment, Luo Chen's eyes suddenly widened.

The scene before him was a small magma pool flowing quietly in the depths of the earth's core. Bubbles formed and burst one after another within the pool.

At the center of that small pool, a crystalline red lotus quietly stretched three petals.

"Five Elements Lotus Platform?"

"No!"

With only a second glance, Luo Chen recognized what this object truly was.

Red Lotus Earthfire [TERM?]!

A supreme grade among earthfires!

There were many mysterious things in the world, such as rootless water, sourceless fire, stainless wind, and soundless thunder.

Luo Chen understood sourceless fire the most.

So-called sourceless did not mean that it had no source. Rather, as this kind rose in rank, it had already broken free from environmental restrictions and could search for its own dwelling place.

Opposite to it was the most common "earthfire" under heaven.

Once this kind of flame left its fire vein, it could not survive on its own. It had to have a place of origin in order to exist.

Refining sourceless fire into origin true fire was an extremely dangerous matter. Even if artifact-forgers, alchemists, and some people who cultivated fire-attribute methods knew the wonders of origin true fire, they did not dare refine origin true fire lightly.

Most of them used earthfire to refine pills and artifacts.

Although controlling it was more troublesome, it could still accomplish the purpose.

Yet not every earthfire in the world was weaker than sourceless fire.

There was one existence that, although it still had to rely on underground fire veins to survive, was not at all weaker in might than sourceless fire.

That was Red Lotus Earthfire!

It was the essence of earthfire, the supreme grade of mortal flame.

If one obtained it, a clan or sect could flourish!

And at this moment, the lotus quietly spreading three petals beneath Luo Chen's feet was precisely the legendary Red Lotus Earthfire.

Moreover, it had reached the third tier!

The instant Luo Chen's breathing grew heavy, the Withering-Bloom True Fire inside his qi sea became even more restless, almost unable to wait.

"You want... to eat it?"

In the unseen depths, Luo Chen seemed to feel the Withering-Bloom True Fire's meaning.

As Luo Chen asked, the Withering-Bloom True Fire suddenly rose several degrees, expressing affirmation.

Luo Chen frowned slightly.

"How do you eat it?"

While hesitating, he opened his mouth and spat out a strand of pure azure flame, which slowly flew toward that Red Lotus Earthfire.

As if sensing danger, the three petals quietly spread upon the Red Lotus Earthfire began trembling.

The small pool, roughly three zhang across, also began boiling at that moment.

Waves of fire shot into the sky.

Toward this, the Withering-Bloom True Fire neither dodged nor avoided. It went straight in and wrapped the Red Lotus Earthfire in a single breath.

The fire pool shook even more violently.

Luo Chen's eyes were bright. Connected to it by mind, he could clearly sense the ravenous feeling of the Withering-Bloom True Fire facing a feast.

Only... the speed was a little slow.

If he truly let the Withering-Bloom True Fire completely devour this Red Lotus Earthfire, it would need at least ten days or half a month.

"I cannot wait that long!"

Luo Chen murmured.

At that moment, his heart suddenly stirred, and he remembered an old matter.

Back then, when he had first come to the Myriad Immortals Assembly, he participated in the demon hunter assessment mission.

Together with Duan Li and the other two, he had besieged the white-robed cultivator, a remnant of the Yuan Demon Sect.

That person had many methods. What left Luo Chen the deepest impression was the ghost domain activated with the Myriad Souls Banner. It was also because of that scene that Luo Chen's inspiration had been stirred and he created the All-Encompassing Fire Prison, the killing move that ran rampant through the Golden Core stage.

But besides that, the white-robed cultivator had another method at the time, one he relied on as a killing move and attempted to use to instantly finish Luo Chen.

Azure Fire Bead [TERM?]!

A disposable attack treasure condensed from large amounts of supreme-grade Azure Yang Demon Flame.

If it had been an ordinary Golden Core cultivator, under the Azure Fire Bead's attack, they would certainly have died or been injured.

Luo Chen was different. Not only was he uninjured, he even seized the Azure Fire Bead.

After returning later, he researched the Azure Yang Demon Flame inside the Azure Fire Bead a little but gained nothing.

With the idea of making use of waste, he used the Withering-Bloom True Fire to test the strength of the Azure Yang Demon Flame, only to discover that the Withering-Bloom True Fire had devoured some of the Azure Yang Demon Flame.

At that time, he thought it was caused by the Withering-Bloom True Fire's own characteristics.

But clearly, the cultivation world did not have any saying that sourceless flames could devour one another.

If their origins were different, how could they merge?

Then why could the Withering-Bloom True Fire devour Azure Yang Demon Flame?

As if blessed by sudden insight, Luo Chen sat cross-legged in the air and once again skillfully circulated the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture.

As his cultivation method circulated, more and more Withering-Bloom True Fire flowed backward out of his qi sea and surged toward the Red Lotus Earthfire.

When the two sides touched, there seemed to be both a mournful cry and the sound of great devouring at the same time.

"So that is how it is!"

Luo Chen's eyes grew increasingly brilliant, and his heart was incomparably excited.

This Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture, said to be the strongest fire-attribute cultivation method under heaven and even capable of leading directly to the Great Vehicle stage, was even more extraordinary than Luo Chen had imagined.

Not only could it transform spirit root attributes, replacing the five elements with a single element and allowing cultivation with the aptitude of a false heavenly spirit root, it could even control true fire to devour other sourceless flames.

If he cultivated in this direction, perhaps he could become a sovereign among flames!

"Yes. Is the phoenix not said to be reborn through fire and known as sovereign of ten thousand flames?"

"How did I only understand this principle now? Truly stupid to this extent!"

Though he blamed himself, he could not hide his wild joy.

After Luo Chen circulated the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture, the Withering-Bloom True Fire's speed in devouring the Red Lotus Earthfire suddenly increased, more than ten times faster than before.

If so, Luo Chen no longer needed to worry.

Within the mountain belly, above the fire pool.

Magma rolled. The Daoist sat cross-legged in the air, forming seals, controlling azure flame with all his strength as it devoured red flame.

Time slowly passed.

Two days later.

Outside the small mountain.

Tai Sui breathed out scorching air and looked at Heavenjade with dissatisfaction.

"Your master has delayed too long!"

Heavenjade had already recovered her human form. Facing Tai Sui and Moya Cave Master, two great Golden Core cultivators, her expression was neither servile nor overbearing.

"When Master leaves seclusion is for him to decide. But you absolutely may not barge in."

"You!"

Tai Sui was extremely impatient.

Within Five Elements Heaven, the two places he disliked most were the Thousand-Ren Mountains and Blazing Refinement Prison.

One had metal sharpness too keen, while the other had fire intent too fierce. Both made his divine soul extremely uncomfortable and even made his mana difficult to mobilize.

Back in the Thousand-Ren Mountains, the reason he had looked so wretched before Luo Chen and the Nine Yang Golden Eagle was partly because of this.

By contrast, if he were in Yue Canglan [TERM?], he would be much better.

Especially Wood Heaven Plain. That place was his home ground!

If he were there, even as a remnant-thought avatar, he would have confidence to face anyone.

"Tai Sui, wait a little longer," Moya Cave Master said, shaking his head. At this moment, with spirit rounded and qi full, he could understand Luo Chen the best. "This place is truly too suitable for our cultivation. Devil Lord Azure Yang probably saw prey and became delighted, becoming somewhat absorbed in cultivation."

"I fear precisely that he has become absorbed!"

Tai Sui turned back and shouted loudly.

His voice was so loud that it spread across the wilderness, as if he wanted it to pass through the formation and enter Luo Chen's ears.

Swish!

A plantain fan appeared in Heavenjade's hand. The woman said coldly, "Please conduct yourself with restraint!"

Tai Sui was about to say something when his eyes saw the scene reflected in Moya Cave Master's pupils: firelight shooting into the sky.

He abruptly turned around.

A pillar of fire rose into the sky.

Boom!

The volcano erupted.

Large sheets of magma fell like rain.

Thick black smoke spread in all directions.

"Master!"

Heavenjade screamed. The erupting volcano was precisely the little mountain where her master was cultivating!

Holding the plantain fan, she charged straight into the sky, wanting to fan this volcano out.

"That is not right. This is a dead volcano. How could it suddenly erupt?"

Moya Cave Master was shocked by the might of heaven and earth while also puzzled.

Tai Sui swept his divine sense across and suddenly cried out.

"He is there!"

Heavenjade's steps suddenly halted.

In her eyes was reflected the red-robed Daoist sitting cross-legged within the pillar of fire.

His black hair flew, and his eyes were closed.

Above his red robe, a layer of qi armor appeared faintly, resisting the impact of the scorching magma outside.

Behind him, azure and red constantly tangled and intertwined, gradually seeming to form a... tree?

At that moment, Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes.

The azure and red lights also instantly rushed into his body.

Luo Chen lowered his head and glanced at the three of them. Then his gaze settled on the erupting volcano.

"An earth vein capable of nurturing Red Lotus Earthfire is a good place. It would be too much of a pity to destroy it."

With a thought, the All-Encompassing Fire Prison fully unfolded, covering several dozen li.

Afterward, his fingers formed seals, and he struck restriction after restriction into various parts of the small mountain.

With both measures working together, the small volcano that had been about to erupt completely gradually quieted down.

Luo Chen leapt down and landed before the three.

"Let us go!"

Seeing him calm a volcanic eruption and straighten the disordered earth qi with such casual ease, Heavenjade's eyes were full of worship.

Moya Cave Master, however, was deathly pale. He deeply understood the gap between himself and the other party.

Tai Sui looked deeply at Luo Chen. He faintly sensed that something seemed to have changed here, that a trace of "vitality" was missing.

But he did not understand what Luo Chen had done.

Taking a deep breath, Tai Sui pointed in a direction.

"Go that way. The Five Elements Lotus Platform must be there!"

Luo Chen and the others followed his finger and looked over. That was the central region of Blazing Refinement Prison. A mountain peak that seemed about to spread its wings and fly was faintly visible.

What kind of mountain peak looked as if it were about to spread wings and fly?

"That place is?"

"Vermilion Bird Mountain [TERM?]. It contains fifth-tier Vermilion Bird Sacred Fire [TERM?]. If the Five Elements Lotus Platform wishes to advance to the fifth tier, it must separately obtain the true essences of the five elements. It has already absorbed the other four attributes. In the place where it is now, there is one and only one possibility: it is there!"

Looking at that incomparably lively mountain, Luo Chen's eyes narrowed slightly.

Fifth-tier Vermilion Bird Sacred Fire?

*(End of Chapter)*
