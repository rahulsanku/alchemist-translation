# Chapter 1026: Top-Grade Soul Stone, Great Desolate Kui Ox

Spirit Burial Valley.

Located at the extreme northern border of the Tiannan Continent, half of it lay on land while the other half was deeply submerged beneath the sea.

Such a peculiar geographical structure was not naturally formed. Legend had it that it was created by the fierce battle between that fifth-rank ghost deity and a Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign back in the day.

In that battle, the fifth-rank ghost deity's ghostly body had been utterly shattered, and even its primordial spirit had been split into several pieces, suppressed within Spirit Burial Valley.

It was only because the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect was currently desperately short of Ascendant-stage cultivators that they hadn't yet made a move to completely eradicate the remnant primordial spirit of this fifth-rank ghost deity.

Back then, the Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign had hastily set up a simple suppression method before departing in a hurry, thereby leaving a great hidden danger for this continent. Although ghost deities were not truly immortal and indestructible, they were not afraid of blazing sun or harsh winds, and were extremely difficult to eliminate naturally. In just over thirty years of suppression, the changes in Spirit Burial Valley had grown increasingly severe.

A large number of unconscious ghost souls that had fallen on the Central Continent were attracted and gathered in Spirit Burial Valley.

Then, from the inside out, they began to gradually expand.

This was precisely the trend of a ghost domain forming!

Moreover, this ghost domain was not led by some Ghost King or Ghost Emperor. One could imagine that once the ghost domain fully expanded, the revival of that ghost deity would not be far off.

The high-level cultivators of the Tiannan Continent had no good solutions. They could only set up formation boundary markers to delay the ghost domain's expansion as much as possible, waiting for the Dao Sect's ancestral court to send someone to deal with it.

However, these formations might stop ordinary cultivators, but to Luo Chen, they were as good as nonexistent.

Entering Spirit Burial Valley was like walking into an empty land.

Little White followed closely beside him, watching as Luo Chen strolled leisurely, advancing deeper into this dark and eerie valley.

Occasionally, low-level ghostly beings would attack, but Luo Chen paid them no heed.

However, as the two went deeper, the ghosts appearing around them grew stronger and stronger.

When a third-rank Ghost King brushed past them, Little White was already shocked to her core. They hadn't even reached the very center yet, and third-rank Ghost Kings were already appearing.

Did that mean that in the central area, there might even be fourth-rank Ghost Emperors?

Suddenly!

A fire meteor shot from Luo Chen's fingertip ahead, heading straight for the Ghost King that had just passed by.

Silently and without a sound, that Ghost King's form was shattered, turning into a puff of extremely pure ghostly qi that spread out in all directions.

"Master?"

Little White looked at this scene in confusion. He had let that Ghost King go just now, so why did Master suddenly strike?

Unbeknownst to her, Luo Chen had already activated his Crimson-Golden Pupils, gazing thoughtfully at the dissipating ghostly qi.

As if to verify some conjecture, in the following journey, Luo Chen was no longer as harmless as before. Instead, he struck out frequently.

Whenever a high-level ghostly being appeared, he would unhesitatingly shatter its form without hesitation. Even if it was a fourth-rank Ghost Emperor comparable to a Sage-stage cultivator, he still would not let it go.

At first, Little White didn't understand Luo Chen's intention behind such "bloodthirsty" behavior. But when she noticed that those high-level ghostly beings whose bodies had been shattered did not reform their bodies but instead converged toward the center, she sensed something was wrong.

"Generally speaking, high-level ghost cultivators naturally master the means of gathering and dispersing at will. Why aren't these ghosts reforming their bodies?"

"Moreover, their displayed intelligence is far too low. They don't resemble high-level ghost cultivators who have awakened their intelligence at all."

"Why are all the high-level ghost cultivators that Master shattered instinctively gathering toward the center?"

Faced with these doubts, Luo Chen let out a soft "Heh."

"Heh, indeed, Little White, you've also noticed the strangeness!"

Little White didn't understand the reasoning and looked at Luo Chen curiously. She knew that since Master was speaking now, he intended to explain it to her.

A fierce wind howled, striking Luo Chen but feeling no more than a gentle breeze brushing his face.

He stepped onto a high, protruding cliff and looked down from above. The scene on the other side of Spirit Burial Valley was fully revealed.

Luo Chen fully extended his divine sense, finally taking in the entire picture.

"Just as I thought!"

"If this ghost deity were given another twenty or thirty years, it might very well break free completely."

Luo Chen chuckled lightly as he waved his hand. Several small flags appeared before him, and then he formed a hand seal, sending them flying toward all directions of Spirit Burial Valley.

Little White watched the positions where those formation flags landed, and vaguely, they seemed to outline a giant body.

Luo Chen's voice drifted over lightly from beside her, clearing away all her doubts.

"The Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign who killed this ghost deity was truly formidable. Not only did he destroy its ghostly body, but he also cut its primordial spirit into nine parts. Four parts were on land, and five parts were deep in the sea."

"However, this person's suppression and sealing methods were rather crude. He merely set up casual arrangements to suppress the nine divided souls separately. Of course, it's also possible that the battle on the Central Continent was too critical at the time, and this person had no time to set up more exquisite means."

"In any case, the crude seal gave this ghost deity an opportunity to escape!"

"It disregarded the risk of losing its self-awareness, splitting its own soul into thousands of parts, parasitizing those high-level ghostly beings that were attracted here."

"The more it split, the greater the risk of it sinking into oblivion, possibly even degenerating into an unintelligent beast. But with this massive splitting, the suppressive force against it also grew weaker and weaker. By the time the weak external divided souls merge together, it will be able to completely break free from the Great Sovereign's seal."

Little White suddenly understood.

These fifth-rank ghost deities were truly hard to kill. Even reduced to this state, they still had methods like the lizard shedding its tail or the cicada shedding its shell.

But then, she became curious.

"Master, how did you discover this?"

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow. "Don't you think this is quite similar to the *Soul-Splitting Art* that I primarily cultivate?"

Both involved splitting the soul!

But the difference was that Luo Chen's main soul held the power to dominate everything, and he wouldn't suffer from fragmented consciousness due to having too many split souls.

The only exception was probably the weak divided soul that Luo Chen had recently split off using the Nirvana method!

That divided soul had been residing in a member of the Zhou clan since childhood. As it gradually grew and merged, it would develop self-awareness and carry the risk of independence.

However, Luo Chen also had a way to deal with this.

As long as he encountered the severed destiny soul of Luo Chen in the future, he would awaken his past-life wisdom and return to Luo Chen's control.

At this moment.

Luo Chen was quite curious about what the true form of this sealed ghost deity actually was, that it even knew such a method?

After understanding everything, Little White asked curiously, "Master, are you going to release it ahead of time now?"

"If I don't release it, how can I subdue it?" Luo Chen said with a smile, then produced an object in his hand. "Not only will I help it break free, but I will also help it escape the predicament of being split into nine parts, allowing it to condense into one!"

Little White's expression changed slightly.

Her gaze fell on the object in Luo Chen's hand.

It was a stone.

Dark and profound, yet crystal clear, about the size of a baby's fist.

She recognized this thing! She had even consumed it before!

It was a soul stone!

Moreover, it was far more pure than the soul stone Luo Chen had previously rewarded her with—a true small top-grade specimen.

Back then, just using a small soul stone had greatly increased her soul foundation.

If she were given such a large soul stone now, she might even be able to break through to the mid-Ghost Emperor stage in one go!

"Master, isn't this a bit too extravagant and wasteful?" Little White swallowed her saliva, a hint of envy in her tone.

"If you're not willing to cast the bait and lay the trap, how can you catch the big fish?"

Luo Chen smiled faintly, his gaze falling on this top-grade soul stone.

Before leaving Broken Soul Cliff back then, he had almost completely scraped clean all the soul stones exposed on the beach.

From those as fine as sand grains to those as large as fists, he had swept them all up.

Even though he now possessed a "sea" of soul stones, this one in his hand still ranked among the top five!

A long time ago, Luo Chen had speculated about the origin of soul stones.

Broken Soul Cliff and the Yellow Spring Sea were both part of the Refining Soul Wasteland of the Lianxu Great Desolate. The Yellow Spring Sea swept away all living beings, devouring their flesh and blood until it disappeared. What about the souls of those living beings?

Soul stones were the final refined essence left behind by these souls!

Therefore, cultivators could refine them to strengthen their soul foundation.

Luo Chen was like this, and Little White was like this.

For the currently extremely weak fifth-rank ghost deity, it should be even more so!

Luo Chen casually tossed it, and that crystal-clear black soul stone slowly floated to the center of Spirit Burial Valley.

A strange power emanated from the formation flags that had been thrown out earlier, lifting the soul stone and beginning to attract a large number of ghostly beings to converge.

They scrambled to devour this soul stone, then devoured each other, continuously growing stronger.

Scenes of hungry ghosts devouring souls and cannibalizing their own kind played out one after another.

Below the valley, waves continuously crashed against the cliff walls. An inexplicably chaotic will began to gradually converge, grow, and even awaken!

Little White watched this scene, feeling that will as vast as the ocean, her body trembling uncontrollably.

She couldn't even maintain her human form, her legs turning into the tail of a Nether Serpent.

Swoosh!

A large black flag was planted in front of her.

Little White was confused.

"After it fully awakens, take this banner and subdue it."

Little White was stunned, pointing at her own face.

"Me?"

Luo Chen laughed heartily, soaring into the sky on a beam of light, leaving only one sentence behind.

"Trust me!"

Little White's heart trembled with fear as she gripped the Soul-Refining Banner.

The roaring sounds in her ears grew louder and louder, and the entire sea seemed to tremble uneasily. But she still suppressed the urge to flee, willing to trust Luo Chen.

Time slowly passed.

That top-grade soul stone, after being devoured and digested by countless ghosts, had completely disappeared.

The mutual devouring among the ghosts was still ongoing.

A month later, Spirit Burial Valley had completely changed.

Extremely dense black mist had converged together, completely shrouding the boundary between the sea and the land.

On this day!

Shrill howls came from all directions, and one formation flag after another exploded.

Seals began to shatter one after another.

And within that black mist, a pair of enormous eyes slowly opened.

Little White, standing by the cliff, felt like she was facing a great enemy, but she remembered Luo Chen's words and immediately unfurled the Soul-Refining Banner.

In an instant!

A million ghost souls poured out like a flood.

Fourth-rank Ghost Emperors roared, filling heaven and earth.

A roar like a wild beast came from within the black mist.

"What a bold little junior!"

Little White's face was pale, and without a word, the lower half of her body had completely transformed into the body of a Nether Serpent. Holding the Million-Soul Refining Banner, she soared into the sky.

With both hands shaking the banner, the earth cracked and the sea surged.

The entire sky seemed about to shatter with her shaking of the banner.

Those enslaved million ghost souls launched fearless attacks.

This ghost deity had not yet fully broken the seal, and its soul was scattered and chaotic. Faced with such a mighty onslaught, it was beaten back step by step.

A mass of black mist covering a hundred li in radius was gradually driven by Bai Meiling into the Eastern Sea.

But that was all.

Once in the vast ocean, that mass of black mist seemed to possess inexhaustible power. No matter how many vengeful spirits and fierce ghosts attacked, they could not shake it.

And the will that had been somewhat chaotic earlier gradually became clear.

"Little junior, stop struggling. You can no longer prevent this sovereign's revival."

Little White gritted her teeth, but this Soul-Refining Banner was almost about to advance to a Million Baleful Soul Banner. With her strength, activating it a few times was already a stretch. Now, she was completely exhausted and could no longer stop the black mist from condensing.

The next moment.

Nine explosive sounds came from Spirit Burial Valley in succession, and nine pillars of light shot up from all directions.

That mass of black mist suddenly descended, following the nine pillars of light, sinking into the depths of the ocean.

The moment the black mist disappeared, heaven and earth fell silent!

Then, a deafening sound filled Little White's mind, drowning out all other sounds.

A vast wave surged into the sky, and large swaths of cliffs and valleys collapsed.

A cyan-colored giant beast slowly rose from the depths of the sea.

The giant beast was like an ox but had no horns. It stood on a single leg in the ocean, yet it seemed like a heavenly pillar capable of supporting heaven and earth.

Its mottled cyan body was covered with dense wounds, with nine sword scars being the most conspicuous.

Those nine sword scars almost evenly divided its entire body into nine pieces. Even the connection between its neck and head had a neat ring of sword scars.

When this beast appeared, all the ghosts were stunned, unable to muster any thought of attack or resistance.

Little White looked at this scene in terror, only murmuring, "A fifth-rank ghost deity!"

That giant beast seemed to pay no attention to the insignificant Little White, nor did it care about the thousands upon thousands of ghosts. It simply raised its head and roared to the sky, venting the joy of breaking free.

First suppressed by the Nether Origin Sect for three thousand years, then enslaved by Rosycloud as consumable material, and finally slain and sealed by the Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign.

It had had enough!

Now that it had finally broken free, how could it not be overjoyed?

With its roar, wind and rain raged, thunder and lightning flashed, and the entire world seemed to have fallen into an apocalypse.

Until a voice slowly descended from the nine heavens above.

"So it's a desolate beast with the bloodline of a Kui Ox!"

The hornless, single-legged giant beast was suddenly startled, instinctively looking up at the high sky.

It saw a giant cauldron, expanded to its extreme, a full thousand zhang in size, descending from the heavens.

Boom!

The cyan giant beast was unwilling to be suppressed and resisted with all its might.

However, that giant cauldron ignored wind, rain, thunder, and lightning, suppressed the ocean waves, and enveloped it entirely with an unyielding posture.

What terrified the giant beast the most was that a vast primordial spirit, like a blazing sun, coldly looked down upon it from the nine heavens. Above its head hung a gray-white bronze mirror, and between its eyes, visions flew about and illusions constantly emerged.

In a daze, the cyan giant beast's mind was filled with numerous figures.

There was a black-robed old man stirring the Nether Abyss, a red-clad woman burning the heavens and boiling the seas, and a young man wielding a single sword, roaming freely.

These were the beings it feared most in its life.

With just one moment of distraction, it lost its power to resist.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron crashed down with a boom, then began to shrink rapidly.

From within the cauldron came an angry, unceasing voice.

"I am not willing!"

But Luo Chen paid it no heed. After landing, he reached out and beckoned.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron landed in his hand. Little White, her face pale, held the Soul-Refining Banner and came before him.

"Let's go. We can't stay on the Tiannan Continent any longer."

With a thought, flames flickered, and the master and servant had already disappeared from the spot.

Not long after.

Several beams of light broke through the air from the south, landing at the devastated Spirit Burial Valley. They looked at each other, their faces full of disbelief.

(End of this chapter)
