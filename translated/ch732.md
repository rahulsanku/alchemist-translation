# Chapter 732: Unimaginable Cultivation Speed, Late Blooming

Sang Jinghe left, and very soon returned.

When he returned, he also brought back Fu Chaosheng's recent situation.

The day of his return was near, but the exact date was still unknown. Fu Chaosheng still had many loose ends in his Northern Sea business that needed to be resolved one by one.

Especially on the righteous and demonic paths' side. Previously, he had acted as intermediary to mediate the war, and that was not as simple as sitting down to eat and be a guest. It also involved a large amount of cooperation between the Tianyuan Merchant Alliance and the righteous and demonic paths.

In truth, from this, one could also see that although the Tianyuan Dao Sect's merchant alliance in the Northern Sea was nominally governed jointly by four great elders, Grand Elder Shen Wandou clearly held a position one level higher.

Shen Wandou did not need to run around outside. He only needed to remain in the headquarters, Divine Origin City, and oversee the overall situation.

Unlike Fu Chaosheng and the other two, who rarely had leisure and spent their days traveling outside.

The reason this situation had formed was not merely as simple as realm. It was also related to the "family background" behind them.

The Fu family [TERM?] had declined, and only Fu Chaosheng and the newly advanced Sage Fu Qinglan [TERM?] supported its facade.

Even if the Shen family was not among the foremost of the so-called Thirty-Six Aristocratic Clans, there were still other clansmen inside the family who had advanced to Sage many years ago. Its own strength was abundant.

Because of this gap, it was reflected in the two men outside, Shen and Fu: one leisurely, one busy.

This was not the capable doing more work; it was the weak doing more work.

Luo Chen had no desire to investigate the truth within. After learning that Fu Chaosheng's return would still require some time, he suppressed the urgency in his heart and placed all his energy on his own cultivation.

Using the power of tribulation lightning to refine the soul was far too dangerous. If one thing went poorly, it could easily damage the spirit and exhaust the soul, so he needed to control the degree within.

The Great Soul Pill was still taken once a month, while his main energy was placed on his current cultivation.

According to the progress bar on the attribute panel, he roughly judged his present cultivation speed.

The result he obtained, however, left him extremely astonished.

Even though the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture at Great Completion was merely usable, so long as he had a normal fourth-tier spirit vein supporting him, he could completely advance to the next realm within ten years, which was to say, the second level of the Sage stage!

If he had the medicinally powerful Hongyuan Pill [TERM?] to assist him, taking one every half month and refining it slowly, this speed would even have a chance to improve to seven years, six years, or even half of that!

Such a result completely overturned the common sense of all the realms before Sage.

Should cultivation in the Sage realm not become even more difficult?

Was the doubled lifespan limit that appeared out of nowhere not precisely meant to prepare for long cultivation?

Luo Chen was deeply puzzled by this.

"Could it be caused by this constitution of mine, the Fire Spirit Body [TERM?]?"

"But among those in the world capable of becoming Sages, which one is not outstanding in aptitude and unshakably resolute in temperament? Yet for them to improve by one realm, time is counted in decades or centuries."

"Especially those who were already somewhat old when they broke through to Sage. Throughout their entire lives, they cannot even touch the threshold of late-stage Sage."

Regarding this question, Luo Chen still could not understand it even after reading through a large number of classics.

This made him understand that without guidance from a famous teacher, without sect inheritance, and without exchange with fellow Daoists, even if he cultivated bitterly behind closed doors, he would merely know that something was so without knowing why it was so.

Therefore, Luo Chen developed the thought of exchanging with cultivators of the same realm.

At present, there were quite a few Sages stranded in Divine Origin City, and many among them wanted to join the Tianyuan Dao Sect and use the opportunity to leave the Northern Sea.

However, Luo Chen had no intention of forming deep ties with these people. He set his sights on Zhou Yunshen.

It was not because he was bullying an honest man. A person capable of becoming a Sage and founding a great clan could never be described by the word "honest."

The main reason was that there was "history" between them.

Because of this tangled history, if Luo Chen took the initiative to ask for advice, not only would it not annoy the other party, it could instead draw their relationship closer.

The ways of human sentiment were just like this. Sometimes, others very much wanted you to ask them for a favor.

After Zhou Yunshen simply settled his clansmen, he also began frequently visiting the Sage cultivators of Divine Origin City. Faced with Luo Chen's invitation, he gladly attended the appointment.

"The daily cultivation of Sage cultivators?"

Facing the question Luo Chen raised, Zhou Yunshen was rather surprised.

Luo Chen did not avoid the matter and said frankly, "I am originally a rogue cultivator without the inheritance of a teacher or sect. I know very little of these common-sense things. Previously, I did not feel it, but the more frequently I cultivate, the more confused I become. Therefore, I ask Fellow Daoist to give me some guidance."

Zhou Yunshen hesitated for a moment, but looking at Luo Chen's gaze, thirsting for knowledge, he still laughed freely.

"Very well. This does not count as any core secret transmission, only some words from experience."

After laughing, his expression turned solemn.

"Before that, please release your Sage domain and touch it against mine."

Luo Chen nodded. With a thought, the Nascent Soul inside his body opened its eyes slightly, and its aura passed through the Purple Mansion, drawing on the spiritual qi of heaven and earth.

He did not add the Melting the Void [TERM?] method, but simply activated his Sage domain with full force.

At once, a large amount of fire-attribute spiritual qi of heaven and earth converged, forming a crimson cloudglow above Little Qiong Mountain, like clouds burning in the sky.

At the same time, two streams of spiritual qi, one blue and one green, rushed over and formed another Sage domain.

The two collided, and in only an instant, the fire clouds were pressed into constant retreat.

From this, one could see the enormous gap between the two men.

The two great domains touched and then withdrew. Luo Chen praised, "Fellow Daoist's Dao attainment is profound. I am far from your match!"

Unexpectedly, Zhou Yunshen's heart was even more shocked.

In that direct collision, he could sense how solid the other party's domain was and how vast the spiritual qi of heaven and earth it drew over was.

Even if it could not compare to his own, it was still far beyond what an ordinary first-level Sage cultivator could possess.

Of course, on the surface, his expression did not reveal this.

After withdrawing his Sage domain, he asked, "Did you perceive anything special within?"

Luo Chen froze. What difference was there? Was it not simply a domain collision?

But immediately, he seemed to realize something.

In that direct collision just now, apart from the free spiritual qi drawn over from heaven and earth, there was also the magic power of the two cultivators themselves.

Yet in his perception, although Zhou Yunshen's magic power was abundant, the difference between them was absolutely not heaven and earth.

The reason his own domain had collapsed in disarray was actually the result formed jointly by the cultivator's magic power and the spiritual qi of heaven and earth.

Luo Chen hesitated. "If we simply compare magic power between you and me, the gap does not seem to be that large?"

Zhou Yunshen smiled. "As expected of Wilderness Wanderer, who could still break through to the Sage stage as a rogue cultivator. Your comprehension is extraordinary, and you can see the whole from the smallest hint."

"Fellow Daoist, please speak plainly!"

Zhou Yunshen put away his smile and said seriously, "For cultivators like us, if one compares only magic power, then unless one is a late-stage Sage great cultivator, the difference between one another is not large. The reason different realms form such obvious gaps during battle is because the process of using magic power to move external spiritual qi magnifies the gap in magic power several times over."

"Battles between Sage cultivators are no longer simple clashes of magic treasures or exchanges of fists and feet, but are chiefly embodied in moving the spiritual qi of heaven and earth."

"Whether it is the Sage domain or true artifacts capable of communicating with external spiritual qi, both are manifestations of this method."

Luo Chen furrowed his brow, carefully thinking over what the other party had said.

If he understood it through the quantitative thinking he habitually used, it should roughly be one, two, three, four, five, six, each respectively corresponding to the quantity of magic power at each level of the Sage stage.

Between one and two, the difference was indeed not great. Even one compared to five, or one compared to six, remained within a normal range.

But once it involved battle, one could not use simple addition and subtraction to compare.

A magic-power magnitude of one could basically move only one measure of the spiritual qi of heaven and earth.

But a magic-power magnitude of two could move at least two measures of spiritual qi, perhaps even three or four, or more.

Under this combined situation, one times one would be less than two times two, or two times three.

If the span of realms widened further, one times one would become far, far smaller than five times five, five times six, seven, eight, and so on.

This was also why, when he had not used the Melting the Void method just now, he was completely no match for Zhou Yunshen in their domain collision.

Working backward from this, when he had previously encountered Blood Nightmare Moluo and even the Split Heaven Stream Lord, his domain had in fact been at a disadvantage. He had relied entirely on the self-realized secret art Melting the Void to smooth out the gap.

Following this line of thought, Luo Chen asked several more questions.

"Before late-stage Sage, there is no absolute gap in magic power, and the true gap is reflected in moving the spiritual qi of heaven and earth?"

"Yes."

"Is there a way to reduce this gap?"

"Naturally there is. High-level spells, innate divine abilities, secret arts, constitutions, and even the true artifacts we commonly use can all do so! As for the specific circumstances, that varies from person to person."

"Forgive the presumptuous question, but was that your full strength just now?"

"Hehe, this old man is old. If I used my full strength, I would damage bones and tendons. Besides, Wanderer, did you not also hold something back?"

The extended topic ended there.

Luo Chen returned to the original question, but became even more puzzled.

"For cultivators like us to raise our realms, the most basic requirement is an increase in magic power. Since the magic-power gap between each realm is not large, why does a Sage cultivator's cultivation time so often count a single minor realm in centuries?"

Zhou Yunshen pointed at the sky and said as though it were only natural, "The higher the realm, the stronger the magic power, and the more spiritual qi of heaven and earth one moves. But the more spiritual qi one moves, the greater the backlash, and the easier it becomes for the cultivator's Nascent Soul to lose itself and be assimilated by heaven and earth. Therefore, the more bravely and rapidly cultivators like us advance, the more carefully we must act. We must constantly comb through our own magic power and cannot allow the slightest neglect."

"But is there not still the Purple Mansion barrier [TERM?]?"

"Haha, could it be Fellow Daoist does not know that the Purple Mansion barrier originates from the evolution of the spirit root?"

Luo Chen froze, then abruptly understood.

Human cultivation required spirit roots, but spirit root was only a general term. Its own nature was divided into three parts.

One was at the tip of the tongue, one was in the dantian, and one was in the essence chamber or womb.

The first affected sensitivity to external spiritual qi, absorption efficiency, and so on. There were people in the world with lame legs, broken feet, and incomplete limbs, but high-realm cultivators with severed tongue roots were rare.

The last was not so crucial; one only needed to preserve primordial yang or primordial yin before Initiate advancement.

The most important was the dantian.

As the realm grew higher and higher, the dantian would continuously change, from the initial opening of the dantian, to the dantian transforming into a sea, and finally to the derivation of the Purple Mansion.

Therefore, if one truly calculated it, the Purple Mansion was indeed the product of the spirit root's evolution!

Zhou Yunshen spoke of the most basic matters, and the reasoning was simple and plain.

"The fewer the spirit roots, the easier it is to precisely sense the corresponding spiritual qi attribute, the easier it is to absorb, and naturally, the faster the cultivation speed."

"Therefore, single-spirit-root heavenly spirit root cultivators are called favored children of heaven and earth."

"But that is limited to before the Sage realm!"

"After Sage, the spirit root evolves into the Purple Mansion. The fewer the spirit roots, the smaller the Purple Mansion, and the thinner the barrier. It may seem more intimate with heaven and earth, but how would we dare completely merge into heaven and earth?"

Zhou Yunshen smiled as he looked at Luo Chen and asked, "You should have heard the most widespread rumor in the cultivation world regarding the cultivation speed of heavenly spirit root cultivators, yes?"

Without thinking, Luo Chen said, "Ten years to Initiate, a hundred years to form the core, two hundred years to Nascent Soul, five hundred years... to Ascendant."

As he spoke the last part, his tone gradually grew hesitant, while a feeling like enlightenment poured through his mind.

Combined with the simple exchanges he had with Sage Langya [TERM?] during the days they traveled together, the question that had puzzled him for many days was instantly resolved.

Zhou Yunshen did not yet know Luo Chen had already understood, and still continued his simple explanation.

"Those with a single spirit root possess an enormous advantage in the early stages. Even if their cultivation path has some slight twists and turns, if calculated fully, when they form their Nascent Soul, their age is only around three hundred."

"So young, and still so far from the thousand-year lifespan limit, one can say their time is abundant."

"However, from the Sage stage to the Ascendant stage, it requires a full five hundred years!"

"The obstruction within lies in the fact that the Purple Mansion barrier evolved by heavenly spirit root cultivators is truly far, far too thin. They simply do not dare act with brave and rapid advancement!"

"In fact, upon reaching this realm, they do not even dare take too many cultivation-type pills. Because pill poison damages the Purple Mansion barrier, causing an already flimsy thatched cottage to leak wind from every side, leaving the Nascent Soul with no shelter."

At this point, even Zhou Yunshen could not help sighing with endless feeling.

Heaven and earth were fair, and the Great Dao treated all equally.

However much convenience it gave you in the early stage, it would give you the same amount of obstruction later.

And if cultivation was filled with difficulty from the very beginning, then after one's realm became profound, it was like holding on until the clouds parted to reveal the moon, with the road ahead smooth and unobstructed.

His own aptitude was not poor. In terms of spirit roots, he could count as having dual spirit roots, which was how he formed his Nascent Soul at four hundred years old.

But during the next four hundred years, he walked as though on thin ice. For a time, his Nascent Soul sank and he lost himself, trapped at the fourth level of the Sage stage for many years.

This was also one reason the outside world believed he was only at the fourth level of the Sage stage.

Later, even though he broke through that bewildering barrier, he only advanced by two minor realms.

Now that his lifespan had reached eight hundred years, if he wanted to rely on the remaining two or three centuries to break through late-stage Sage and assault the Ascendant realm...

Zhou Yunshen smiled bitterly as he looked toward Luo Chen. He could sense the other party's youth, and also knew that when Luo Chen was in the Myriad Immortals Assembly, he had only been at mid-stage Adept.

So young, and having advanced to the Sage stage so quickly, he was likely a single fire-attribute spirit root.

In the past, Zhou Yunshen would have considered him a sect genius or a clan pillar.

But now, he could only feel a trace of sorrow for the other party.

After crossing the hardest hurdle, he had discovered that there was still a high mountain ahead. In his heart, Luo Chen was probably incomparably lost and despairing.

The reason he had taken the initiative to seek Zhou Yunshen out for answers today likely lay here!

Feeling Zhou Yunshen's pitying gaze, Luo Chen blinked. He did not explain anything, but instead began exchanging on other questions.

"Detain Spirits and Dispatch Generals [TERM?], enslaving spirit souls?"

"In this area, I truly understand a thing or two."

"Endless Valley's Spirit-Control Sage was my good friend. His Daoist title was Spirit-Control, which is enough to explain everything."

"In the past, he told me some little tricks. After I explored them, I merged them into the Coral Sea's Blood Coral Grand Formation [TERM?], and the effects were extraordinary."

"I can explain a few things, but these tricks vary from person to person. If you wish to use them, you must consider them carefully."

Luo Chen had not expected that a casual question would actually give him a small pleasant surprise.

Recently, while studying puppet arts, he had been somewhat at a loss regarding how to control the powerful spirit souls inside puppets.

The Spirit-Control Sage's little tricks that Zhou Yunshen shared gave him great inspiration.

After the exchange ended, Luo Chen personally saw the other party out.

The two walked down Little Qiong Mountain together.

At the moment of parting, Luo Chen suddenly asked, "Fellow Daoist Zhou, where do you plan to go in the future?"

"The future?"

A trace of blankness spread across Zhou Yunshen's aged face, and then he said firmly, "I must find a way out for my Zhou clan descendants. I plan to go far away to Central Continent and enter the Dao Sect's Heaven-Earth Peak. You should have the same plan, yes? When the time comes, we can support one another. After all, Central Continent may seem to absorb the world's experts, but it is also extremely exclusionary. If we do not huddle together for warmth, I fear we may become consumables."

Luo Chen smiled. No wonder the other party had told him everything earlier.

Repaying Black King's rescue was one reason; the experience he transmitted was shallow was the second. The true purpose was still to befriend him, making it convenient for them to move together in the future.

Although Luo Chen had no intention of staying long in Central Continent, if he had someone whose roots and history he knew to cooperate with then, it would likely be somewhat safer.

Since that was so, he should also return the favor.

With a flick of Luo Chen's wrist, a storage bag was handed over.

"This is?"

Zhou Yunshen was puzzled and did not accept the storage bag.

Luo Chen smiled. "Inside are three low-grade defensive magic treasures. Some were personally crafted by me, and some I obtained by chance. I hear that the Zhou clan's young talents suffered heavy losses in the Coral Sea battle. Consider these items my gift to those juniors, for their self-defense."

Zhou Yunshen hesitated. "One should not accept reward without merit. Fellow Daoist, this gift is somewhat..."

"How can you say there is no merit? Our earlier exchange can save me who knows how many wrong turns. A mere three low-grade magic treasures useless to me, and I even feel they are a little shabby. Besides..."

Luo Chen smiled faintly. "Back then, your Zhou clan sent me a batch of medicinal herbs that assisted me in refining pills. At that time, I promised to tailor-make a defensive magic treasure for your Zhou clan's newly advanced Adept. Unfortunately, later I fled far into the Demon Sea, and this promise was never fulfilled. Now, this is only a slight compensation. I hope Fellow Daoist will not disdain it."

Since things had been said to this point, Zhou Yunshen no longer acted coy.

Three magic treasures truly did not count as too precious.

But defensive magic treasures were indeed what the Zhou clan's younger descendants currently lacked.

There truly were not many people left. If they suffered more losses, at his age, he might not be able to bear it.

"Since that is so, I will accept with thanks."

Zhou Yunshen smiled and accepted the storage bag, then cupped his hands and took his leave.

After he left, the smile on Luo Chen's face did not fade. He did not use magic power either, but slowly walked along the mountain path.

His confusion had been very simple, and in truth, Sage Langya had already given the answer long ago. But it had been too general, causing one leaf to obscure his vision.

With Zhou Yunshen, an old Sage of many years, sorting it out, the answer had instantly become clear.

"My Purple Mansion barrier appears to be formed from a single fire spirit root, but in reality, it was formed from five-phase spirit roots."

"No!"

"My spirit roots were also raised by the Spirit-Molding Sacred Spring [TERM?] in the Bright Radiance Heaven Pill Hall [TERM?]. They are truly superb beyond measure."

"The Purple Mansion derived from this has no equal beneath the heavens."

"With this Purple Mansion protecting me, when I cultivate, I do not need to care about heaven and earth's influence on me at all. At the same time, the Fire Spirit Body ensures my perfect absorption of fire-attribute spiritual qi, which is why I can possess such fast cultivation speed."

"Once the Hongyuan Pill's proficiency rises, and I refine middle-grade or even high-grade Hongyuan Pills, my cultivation speed can increase explosively once more!"

"Haha, all my life I was trapped by low aptitude and spent countless resources. I never expected that after succeeding in becoming a Sage, I would instead leap up and achieve so-called genius aptitude. This truly is somewhat wondrous."

Luo Chen was in an excellent mood.

After returning to the mountain, even his progress studying puppet arts greatly increased along with it.

One month later, Luo Chen, whose condition had risen to its peak, took out the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

With a thought, his divine sense locked onto the thing inside the cauldron!
