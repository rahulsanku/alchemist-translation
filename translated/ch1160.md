# Chapter 1160: Jinglan Passes Away, The God in the Valley

Inside the quiet room of the Fine Dust Pavilion.

Luo Chen carefully undid the restrictions on the stone box.

The moment the immortal origin stone appeared, he once again felt that violent surge of energy draining away.

This situation was almost identical to what the ancient texts recorded about a cultivator's spiritual power rapidly dissipating upon entering the Great Extinction Realm!

"Even immortal qi, a higher level of energy, cannot resist the suppression of the Guixu?"

The thought flashed through his mind as Luo Chen quickly swallowed the immortal origin stone.

Waste was shameful!

The moment the immortal origin stone entered his body, strands of astral qi immediately clung to it, binding it tightly and dragging it into the furnace of his blood and qi.

*Boom!*

A sound like muffled thunder rang out as the entire blood-qi furnace began to spin rapidly.

The Blood-Refining Divine Astral tightly coiled around that milky-white immortal origin stone, refining it wave after wave.

But contrary to the smooth progress he had imagined, even with Luo Chen pushing his technique to its utmost limit, the fingernail-sized immortal origin stone was still being refined at an agonizingly slow pace.

A full three days passed, and the immortal origin stone's size hadn't changed at all.

If he couldn't feel a clear increase in his own blood and qi power, Luo Chen might have suspected he was wasting his effort.

But this result left Luo Chen quite satisfied.

It showed that the energy contained within the immortal origin stone was extremely vast!

Even a single strand could transform into a massive amount of blood and qi.

The reason the refinement was so slow wasn't the stone's fault—it was merely because his own cultivation level was too low.

"Step by step, then!"

"If I can refine it completely, I might be able to break through directly to the Leakless realm!"

Luo Chen settled his mind and began circulating the *Divine Astral Combat Qi* over and over again.

The blood-qi furnace rumbled as it spun, the Blood-Refining Divine Astral scoured the immortal origin stone again and again, and his body's acupoints shone like stars, radiating brilliant light.

Meanwhile, outside.

The festive atmosphere of the Relic Immortal City Lord's seventieth birthday had gradually faded.

Following Luo Chen's instructions before seclusion, Jinglan had also halted the commercial activities that could almost be called extortion and exploitation.

The city, which had been running at a frantic pace for decades, finally had a chance to catch its breath as Luo Chen's demand for resources decreased.

Jinglan had no children, and Luo Chen had no disciples or friends. Considering her advancing age, Jinglan began to cultivate a successor for the city lord position.

The choice of successor surprised everyone.

It was actually a woman from the Xiao family!

In truth, the Xiao family bloodline had never been severed.

As mentioned before, the old city lord Xiao Lin had left behind five children before he departed.

Two sons and three younger sisters.

The eldest and second sons were Xiao Feng and Xiao Yun, both long since fallen.

But the three younger sisters were still alive, and Luo Chen had never troubled them.

Jinglan training one of the three women as the city lord candidate was, in a sense, returning things to their original owners.

As for whether they would hold a grudge over past grievances?

Jinglan wasn't worried about that.

She knew very well that Brother Luo Chen didn't care much about Relic Immortal City at all—he was merely using it to gather cultivation resources.

Once Luo Chen's cultivation reached a certain level, he would naturally leave this place.

All Jinglan sought was peace of mind.

While training her successor, Jinglan would also occasionally come to the Tide-Watching Pavilion, gazing from afar at the quiet Fine Dust Pavilion.

Often, she would sit there for half a day.

But she never saw Luo Chen emerge from seclusion.

Only the koi fish, foraging for food, would occasionally swim up to her, leaping and splashing in the water.

...

Twenty years later.

Within the Xiao residence, aside from the patrolling Mysterious Armor soldiers and a few servants, the place had grown sparse.

The reason lay in the Fine Dust Pavilion at the center of the estate.

In recent years, sounds of turmoil had been emanating from the pavilion from time to time.

Like frog croaks and ox bellows, like muffled thunder, and occasionally like great rivers surging.

These noises had persisted for a long time, making it impossible for ordinary people to live nearby.

In the last half month, the sounds had become increasingly frequent.

Xiangfei had dismissed all the servants, leaving only a small number of Mysterious Armor soldiers to guard the outer estate, while she herself kept watch outside the Fine Dust Pavilion.

"He's finally about to emerge!"

Inside the Fine Dust Pavilion.

At some point, the originally spacious cultivation chamber had become cramped.

A blood sphere, like a cocoon, stood within it!

The blood cocoon seemed alive, its heart beating vigorously, transmitting the earth-shaking sounds that had been heard outside.

The blood cocoon began to tremble violently, sometimes expanding, sometimes contracting.

Suddenly!

The blood cocoon expanded to its limit, nearly filling the entire cultivation chamber, and then, under a strange suction force, it collapsed inward wildly.

A blurry human figure gradually emerged.

The figure sat cross-legged in midair, completely naked. His entire body was like a sponge, absorbing the surrounding blood cocoon through every pore and inch of skin.

When all the blood energy had vanished, only skin as smooth as satin remained, as tender as milk.

The figure opened his mouth slightly, and his originally thick, hard teeth began to fall out one by one.

At the same time, new teeth grew from the roots where the old ones had fallen.

The new teeth were full and plump, like snow or crystal, seemingly fragile, yet as if they could bite through the hardest gemstone in the world.

His originally thick hair was now growing wildly like weeds, each strand as tough as python sinew or dragon spine.

The changes weren't limited to the exterior!

Inside his body, transformations were occurring that ordinary people could hardly imagine.

Seven hundred and twenty acupoints emitted a scarlet light, blurry yet translucent. Clusters of astral qi coiled within them, like stars hanging in the cosmos.

His meridians had been widened to their limit, allowing streams of astral qi to surge through them freely.

Even the bones that had been tempered before were now cracking like exploding bamboo joints.

Especially the great spine at his back!

At this moment, it blazed with intense lightning!

Strands of silver-white electric arcs were almost bursting out of his body.

As if responding?

Outside the pavilion, dark clouds gathered in the high sky.

But the next moment, as Luo Chen suddenly opened his eyes, the originally pitch-black sky slowly dispersed.

Luo Chen gazed through the wall at the void above.

After a long while, he lowered his head.

"Even within this Guixu, a lightning tribulation cannot form?"

Just as a cultivator reaching the Sage stage would face their first Three-Nine Heavenly Tribulation, the same was true for martial artists.

In fact, the tribulation a martial artist had to undergo was even more dangerous than that of a cultivator.

After all, heavenly tribulation descended to punish and destroy.

Compared to the fragile primordial spirit, the physical body cultivated by martial artists was much harder to destroy, so the external lightning tribulation would naturally be more violent and powerful.

But within this Guixu, just like the martial artists of old recorded in the texts, Luo Chen did not suffer a lightning tribulation.

"A pity, really. If there had been a lightning tribulation, it would have meant that this world still possessed heavenly Dao consciousness."

Luo Chen exhaled a turbid breath and turned his attention to his own body.

He was already intimately familiar with the changes inside and out.

Only the transformation of his spine surprised him.

Lightning Bone!

This was not something unfamiliar.

Back when he was at the Initiate stage, in the White Night Cave Mansion of Accumulated Thunder Nine Mountains, he had once used lightning power to temper his body, giving birth to a lightning bone.

At that time, he had even hoped that this bone would prove special when he later faced a lightning tribulation.

Later, when he ventured into the Demon-Fall Land and used the Nine Tribulations Three Nirvanas method, his body underwent nirvana and everything was reorganized.

That lightning bone had still remained!

When he crossed his Sage lightning tribulation, it had indeed absorbed a small amount of lightning power.

But it seemed to have stopped there, never giving Luo Chen much more help.

Even later, when his physical body stepped into the fifth rank of the Archaic stage, the lightning bone in his spine had shown no special changes.

Who would have thought!

Now that he had stepped into the fourth realm of body refinement and cultivated the Leakless Golden Body, the spine at his back had responded with such a clear change.

"Without cultivating any lightning techniques, without a lightning spirit root, yet I can advance a lightning bone using blood and qi power?"

"Does this mean that as my blood and qi continue to transform, this lightning bone, bathed in it, will also transform along with it?"

Luo Chen had no answer.

He closed his eyes briefly, then opened them again.

All his acupoints suddenly closed tightly, and a membrane of qi, generated from the Blood-Refining Divine Astral, enveloped his entire body.

Looking at him, he seemed like an eerie blood god!

This was his Leakless Golden Body!

Luo Chen formed a blade with his right hand, dispersing the blood membrane, and then slashed down on his left arm.

The qi membrane was tough and unmoving.

Luo Chen was stunned for a moment, then couldn't help but smile.

That slash just now had used thirty percent of his physical strength, roughly equivalent to the power of a Sage-stage cultivator wielding a true artifact.

And the result was quite pleasing.

No wonder Wang Yuan had delved into how to strengthen astral qi, even adding wind and earth balefulness to it.

Just this membrane derived from the Blood-Refining Divine Astral was enough to block attacks at the true artifact level.

If defense was like this, then attack power must be even stronger!

With a soft laugh, Luo Chen slowly landed on the ground. His waterfall-like hair, as if animated, retracted to its normal length, hanging down his back.

He changed into a white robe, then stepped out.

Outside the Fine Dust Pavilion.

Xiangfei's eyes sparkled with joy as she called out from afar.

"Congratulations, Senior, on completing your arduous cultivation and achieving the Leakless Golden Body!"

Luo Chen walked up to her, breathing the fresh air, feeling as if his body had become its own little world, and his heart was light and carefree.

Twenty years, refining the immortal origin stone, breaking through to the fourth realm of body refinement.

It could indeed be called an arduous cultivation.

"Where's Jinglan? Why don't I see her?" Luo Chen asked casually.

Xiangfei opened her mouth, hesitating.

Luo Chen was taken aback, then understood everything.

Mortals, after all, cannot withstand time.

For him, it was merely an ordinary period of seclusion, but for Jinglan, her life had already run its course.

Although he had avoided the melancholy of her final moments, Luo Chen's mood could not help but darken now.

"Come, take me to see her."

Xiangfei softly acknowledged, and the joy of Luo Chen's breakthrough also quieted down.

As they left the residence, someone was kneeling at the gate.

It was the new city lord of Relic Immortal City, Xiao Yuxin.

She knelt there, not daring to raise her head even slightly.

Luo Chen only glanced at her briefly. He had no objections to Jinglan's final arrangements.

Relic Immortal City was indeed unimportant to him.

The Xiao family and he had no deep blood feud. If Xiao Feng hadn't been disobedient, Luo Chen wouldn't have done anything to him. As long as this new city lord didn't do anything foolish in the future, he wouldn't bother to interfere.

As for whether Xiao Yuxin could secure her position as city lord, that would depend on her own fortune.

...

South of Green Mountain Town, an unremarkable green hill stood quietly.

A small stream flowed at its base. Though there were houses on the hill, there was no sign of human habitation.

Halfway up the hill, a grave mound stood alone.

"The villagers of Moon Village were moved out long ago by City Lord Jinglan. This place is now a deserted mountain. On her deathbed, she instructed me not to hold a grand funeral, but simply to send her remains back here for burial."

"The tombstone is nameless, presumably waiting for you, Senior, to inscribe it."

"There is also a letter."

Listening to Xiangfei's soft words, holding the thin letter in his hand, Luo Chen's gaze moved from the grave to the green hill, and finally settled on the letter.

"Brother Luo, please read this personally:

Seeing this letter is like seeing me. By the time you read this, Xiaolan will likely have already passed on..."

It was a very plain letter. It did not entrust any final affairs, nor did it speak of longing. It was merely some everyday words Jinglan had left behind when she felt her end was near.

Yet the plainer it was, the heavier Luo Chen's heart grew.

After reading it all, Luo Chen could only let out a long sigh.

He carefully put away the letter, then pressed his fingers together like a sword, slowly inscribing on the nameless tombstone.

**[Grave of Beloved Friend Jinglan — Erected by Luo Chen]**

Xiangfei stood behind, quietly watching this scene. She was curious about the words "Beloved Friend" but did not ask.

She could only sigh inwardly. For an ordinary woman like Jinglan to meet Luo Chen and live an extraordinary life—was this fortune or misfortune?

"Let's go!"

"Back to Relic Immortal City?"

"No, straight to Redleaf Ridge!"

Luo Chen flicked his sleeve and drifted down the mountain.

...

The Redleaf Ridge of old, now the Redleaf Valley.

Surrounded by five peaks, the lake was emerald green, and red leaves fluttered in the breeze.

Standing atop one of the peaks, Xiangfei looked down into the valley and could faintly see, among the crisscrossing ravines on the earth, a peculiar character.

It looked somewhat like an ancient character used by cultivators in the texts.

While Xiangfei was still curious, Luo Chen's voice drifted over.

"We've waited several days. The time has come. Send the death row prisoners into the valley!"

Xiangfei looked up at the sky and could faintly feel a chill approaching.

This was the season when the Ghost Rain was about to fall!

She dared not delay. She descended the mountain herself and went to a prison a hundred li outside Redleaf Valley.

This prison had been built by Jinglan's orders while she was still alive.

It held criminals accumulated over the past fifty years from Relic Immortal City and other major cities.

These criminals were all death row prisoners who had violated the law with force. They should have been executed long ago, but had been imprisoned here.

All for this day!

Soon, Luo Chen could see from afar Xiangfei leading the Mysterious Armor soldiers, bringing a large group of death row prisoners into Redleaf Valley.

Once inside, they were arranged into that dreamlike azure lake. Each person was bound in shackles, unable to float up or sink down.

Chaotic, panicked voices filled the air.

Luo Chen's expression was cold, as if he heard nothing.

When everything was arranged, Luo Chen descended the mountain and personally stepped into Redleaf Valley.

"Senior, do you have any further instructions?"

Luo Chen stood with his back to her, his gaze fixed on the Illusion Dream Locust tree.

"No. Leave. Remember, before I leave this valley, no one is to enter, not even you!"

Xiangfei trembled all over, bowed, and departed.

After she left, Luo Chen cut his wrist. Crimson blood flowed out in torrents, following the ravines and spreading.

Gradually, a blood-red character for "God" appeared vividly in the valley!

And in the azure lake, which had been noisy, it gradually quieted down. The death row prisoners hung their heads, as if falling into a deep sleep.

Luo Chen exhaled a slight turbid breath and formed several hand seals.

Instantly, the balefulness that had accumulated for decades gathered toward the Illusion Dream Locust tree. The red leaves began to rustle.

The Blood River Demon Cloud Grand Formation activated.

The Illusion Bubble Grand Formation activated.

Only the last formation, the Five Ghosts Locking God Grand Formation, remained poised and ready.

And at that moment.

*Plop!*

A raindrop fell onto the lake's surface, stirring up tiny ripples.

(End of chapter)
