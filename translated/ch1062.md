# Chapter 1062: Luo Chen’s Might, Subduing the Host of Demons

In the Truth Crossing Hall.

Demon aura filled the air, and voices clamored like a boiling cauldron.

Figures sat or stood within, conversing loudly, all discussing the matter of the current Truth Crossing Hall Master.

“The three halls of Green Parasol Mountain are the sacred grounds that govern the Feather Tribe, and even all the demon clans of the Eastern Wilderness. Every hall master possesses extraordinary ability; none are weak.”

“The Sovereign controls the Profound Mystery Hall, destroyed the Nether Origin Sect, ruined the Central Continent, and is unmatched among Ascendants.”

“Azure Frost controls the Daytime Void Hall, the number one under the Sage stage, with a brilliant record of achievements. There are even rumors that Azure Frost Hall Master has already advanced to the Ascendant stage and qualified for ascension.”

“The Truth Crossing Hall Master, who stands alongside these two great figures, cannot possibly be weak, nor can he be an unknown nobody. How could he still be hiding his head and tail, nowhere to be seen?”

Someone said, “Perhaps he is busy with cultivation?”

But the words had barely left his mouth when another retorted, “If that were truly the case, why would he summon us here for an audience?”

Those present were dissatisfied with the Truth Crossing Hall Master’s arrogant posture. Having not shown himself for an entire year, he had kept everyone in suspense.

Therefore, his identity had become a subject of much speculation.

If he turned out to be someone who could not command respect, then this year of high-handedness would backfire upon their first meeting.

“Is it Lord Nether Spring? She has long served as Vice Hall Master; being promoted to full Hall Master would only be natural.” A woman in a black dress adorned with starlight made this guess.

However, the Blood Vulture Daoist across from her waved his hand. “Night Star Pupil, your guess is wrong.”

Night Star Pupil raised an eyebrow and smiled. “Blood Vulture, it seems you know something?”

Blood Vulture Daoist spread his hands helplessly. “What would this poor Daoist know? I initially thought the same as you, but when I arrived at Green Parasol Mountain, Fellow Daoist Whitebone denied it.”

The denial of the Whitebone Mystic Serpent?

Those present did not doubt the truth of his words.

Among the earliest demon clan experts to attach themselves to Green Parasol Mountain was the Whitebone Mystic Serpent, who had even once guarded the teleportation array leading directly to the Great Snowy Mountains for Green Parasol Mountain.

If anyone understood the situation at Green Parasol Mountain best, it was none other than the Whitebone Mystic Serpent.

Since he had said so, the current Truth Crossing Hall Master could not be Nether Spring.

Then who could it be?

“Perhaps it is Lord Green Grain Immortal?”

Everyone was startled, then nodded one after another. If it were this one, then it would make sense.

Although Lord Green Grain Immortal was not a demon clan expert in the conventional sense, as a plant that had gained sentience, she was also classified under the Eastern Wilderness demon clan.

Moreover, Lord Green Grain Immortal had a close relationship with Green Parasol Mountain. It was rumored that even her Ascension had received some guidance from Rosycloud.

If it were the Ascendant-level Green Grain Immortal governing the Truth Crossing Hall, whether in terms of realm or strength, she would be convincing enough.

However, this conjecture was immediately shattered by two women from the Green Grain clan.

“It is not our ancestor.”

“Our ancestor has been recuperating from injuries since the Central Continent war. Our sisters came to Green Parasol Mountain for this audience under our ancestor’s orders, to pay respects to the new Truth Crossing Hall Master.”

Thus spoke the Twin Beauties of Green Grain.

Everyone exchanged glances. Not Nether Spring, not Green Grain Immortal—then who could it be?

“It couldn’t be Lord Aoxiao, could it!”

Aoxiao, the Wolf Clan’s supreme ruler!

He had taken human form over a thousand years ago, with outstanding combat prowess, once evenly matched with Azure Frost.

Even back when the ancient Demon Emperor Tian was still alive, he had treated Aoxiao with considerable courtesy.

But after Emperor Tian fell and Rosycloud unified the demon clans, Aoxiao did not submit to Green Parasol Mountain. How could he possibly become the Truth Crossing Hall Master?

Everyone instinctively turned their gazes toward a fierce, murderous-looking man sitting in the front row.

Once, this man had been extremely close to Aoxiao. Together with the Hundred-Foot Great Demon Emperor, he could be considered Aoxiao’s right-hand man.

But after Hundred-Foot inexplicably fell, this man had left Aoxiao’s command and joined Green Parasol Mountain.

If anyone knew Aoxiao’s movements best, it would be none other than this man among those present.

Facing everyone’s gaze, Bi Zheng’s eyes were half-open, half-closed as he said coldly, “It is not Brother Ao!”

The crowd fell silent, and the hall descended into stillness.

This new Truth Crossing Hall Master was neither the experienced Nether Spring, nor the high-realm Green Grain Immortal, nor even the powerful Aoxiao. Then who in the Eastern Wilderness was still qualified to become the Truth Crossing Hall Master and command the demon clans?

After all, although there were only over twenty Nascent Soul demon cultivators present, the surviving demon cultivators of the entire Eastern Wilderness were not limited to this number.

Moreover, even among those present, which one was not a lord of their own domain?

In particular, Bi Zheng, Kuang Kuang, and Niu Gui—these three were at the Great Demon Emperor realm, equivalent to human late-Nascent Soul Grand Cultivators.

If it were merely an unknown nobody, let alone commanding the demon clans, even these three Great Demon Emperors would likely be impossible to drive.

The three men sitting in the front seats, Bi Zheng, Kuang Kuang, and Niu Gui, exchanged a knowing glance.

Their attachment to Green Parasol Mountain was a matter of following the general trend, but also a necessity.

Aoxiao’s power was waning, so Bi Zheng had chosen to defect to the powerful Green Parasol Mountain.

Kuang Kuang and Niu Gui had once been under Emperor Tian’s command, but with Emperor Tian’s fall and the demon court’s split, Jinxiu Immortal had gone into hiding with the remaining forces. They always had to find a place for themselves.

Taking shelter in the Truth Crossing Hall was not a bad choice.

If the new Truth Crossing Hall Master turned out to be someone incompetent, that would actually be a good thing for them. That way, they could be relatively free and not have to be completely at someone else’s beck and call.

Just as everyone was speculating, heavy footsteps came from behind the hall.

Everyone looked up and saw a dignified man in a purple robe walking out.

“Thunder Prison?”

“You are the new Truth Crossing Hall Master?”

Amidst the exclamations were mixed a few notes of doubt.

The Thunder Prison Divine Roc snorted heavily but did not admit it, standing on the steps of the hall.

Everyone exchanged glances. Could it not be Thunder Prison?

If it were him, everyone could reluctantly accept it.

After all, the Thunder Prison Divine Roc was the third most powerful figure at Green Parasol Mountain, after Rosycloud and Azure Frost. His methods were overbearing and unmatched, and it was even said that he had made a great breakthrough in his realm in recent years.

But his posture of standing on the steps as a guard clearly indicated he was not the new Truth Crossing Hall Master.

Then who could it be?

After the Thunder Prison Divine Roc appeared, the commotion in the hall subsided.

Therefore, when the Whitebone Mystic Serpent, Pure Crane, and Fairy Huamei appeared one after another, no one made any unusual moves. Instead, they held their breath one by one, waiting for the main character to appear.

Fairy Huamei and Pure Crane exchanged a glance and called out in clear voices, “The Truth Crossing Hall Master has arrived. All cultivators, rise and pay homage!”

Rustle, rustle, rustle!

The sound of robes shifting and chairs scraping rang out continuously.

One by one, figures stood up and bowed toward the figure slowly emerging from behind the hall.

“We pay homage to the Truth Crossing Hall Mas—”

The final word “ter” never came out.

In an instant, the hall fell into a strange atmosphere.

A young man in a feathered robe curled his lips slightly and sat down lightly on that ornate and noble chair.

“Is this how you welcome your Hall Master?”

In response to this question, the hall erupted in cries of disbelief.

“It’s you!”

“The Eastern Wilderness Pill Sect!”

“The Demon-Slaying Sage!”

“The Formless Demon Lord!”

The young man waved his hand. “Stop, stop, stop. This chair can’t hold so many people. Your Hall Master travels under one name and never changes it. Luo Chen is my name!”

The two words “Luo Chen” shook the entire hall.

As for those earlier Daoist titles, they were achievements the same person had made in different fields.

Pill Sect was Luo Chen’s good name in the Eastern Wilderness; Demon-Slaying Sage was the fearsome reputation he had earned among the demon clans during the two-hundred-year human-demon war; as for Formless Demon Lord, that was a new title he had earned in the Western Desert Buddhist lands—a symbol of strength comparable to Azure Frost, having suppressed the Western Desert Buddhist lands.

But no matter how many good names, fearsome names, or even prestigious names, none could change one fact.

Luo Chen was human!

How could such an existence become the master of the Truth Crossing Hall, commanding the demon clans?

All the demon cultivators straightened their backs, raised their heads, and stared coldly at the figure above.

The pressure from this gaze was so great that even the Thunder Prison Divine Roc and the Whitebone Mystic Serpent could barely withstand it, instinctively stepping aside.

Luo Chen, however, seemed oblivious, a hint of a smile curling at the corner of his mouth.

“You are not convinced?”

As he spoke, a vast pressure, centered on him, instantly enveloped the spacious Truth Crossing Hall.

A Sage domain!

In an instant, everyone felt as if they had entered a prison of flames.

Boundless, impossible to escape.

Scorching and intense, their bodies felt as if they would burn.

Everyone instinctively released their mana, trying to connect with the world’s spiritual energy and release their own Sage domains.

But within this terrifying, all-encompassing fire prison, no matter how they tried to connect with heaven and earth, they received no response.

One man suppressing all the demon emperors with his own strength?

A flash of blood light shot up into the air.

“The Truth Crossing Hall Master’s seat belongs to the capable, but one must also consider the difference in race!”

Luo Chen glanced at that person. Just one glance.

*Plop!*

The Blood Vulture Daoist fell to the ground, collapsing into a heap, his eyes vacant, his face pale. With just one look, the other party had rendered him completely powerless to resist.

What kind of ability was this?

“Difference in race?” Luo Chen sneered, his gaze sweeping over the crowd. “Every one of you here is a creature of scales, shells, moisture-born, or egg-hatched. Are you even of the same race? What you call the demon clan is merely a collective term we humans coined after we became strong. As for this Truth Crossing Hall, what it ferries are true people and true cultivators. You and I are all Nascent Soul true people. Who ferries whom depends solely on each person’s ability. Are you dissatisfied with your Hall Master’s ability?”

“Hmph!”

Luo Chen gave a heavy snort, and his vast Sage domain pressed down violently, as if a corner of heaven and earth had overturned, leaving no one with the slightest will to resist.

Those of slightly weaker strength even fell to their knees with a *plop*.

Some of the more powerful ones barely managed to remain standing, but the trembling of their bodies showed they were on the verge of collapse.

His gaze swept across the room, finally landing on the three Great Demon Emperors in front.

“Who is not convinced?”

“Is it you, Bi Zheng, or you, Niu Gui?”

“Back then, the three of you Great Demon Emperors joining forces were no match for your Hall Master. Now, even if you all attack together, your Hall Master can still suppress you with a flip of my hand!”

When Luo Chen appeared, Bi Zheng and Niu Gui’s faces changed color simultaneously.

In the battle at the Medicine King Domain, the three Great Demon Emperors had joined forces, not only failing to take down Luo Chen, but one had fled and another had been severely injured. Even Emperor Tian’s son, Huang Man, had died miserably at Luo Chen’s hands.

Now, over a hundred years had passed in the blink of an eye. Not only had Luo Chen’s strength skyrocketed, but he had also become the Truth Crossing Hall Master, standing above them. How could they accept this?

Yet, under this overbearing domain suppression, they actually felt a great terror, as if their life and death were in Luo Chen’s hands.

It was a terror that even Azure Frost, the former number one under the Ascendant stage, had never given them!

But to submit to Luo Chen, they could not get past that hurdle in their hearts.

How could they kneel and bow to a former mortal enemy?

They could only muster their mana, grit their teeth, and stubbornly persist within the overbearing domain.

Amidst the creaking sounds.

Luo Chen grinned fiercely, his primordial spirit suddenly condensed, his mana drawing upon the origin qi of heaven and earth. His already overwhelmingly powerful Sage domain, now bolstered, seemed to transform into a divine domain.

“Kneel before me!”

*Boom!*

The divine domain was overbearing, blazing and ostentatious.

All the demon emperors who had been stubbornly persisting could no longer withstand this violent power, collapsing to the ground one after another.

Even the three Great Demon Emperors, Bi Zheng, Niu Gui, and Kuang Kuang, could not withstand Luo Chen’s pressure.

*Plop.*

The three bent their knees and bowed, half-kneeling on the ground, their faces full of humiliation and unwillingness.

The Whitebone Mystic Serpent watched this scene, his heart filled with desolation. Was he not feeling the same way?

A junior he had once looked down upon had now become an existence that could decide their life and death at will.

Beside him, the Thunder Prison Divine Roc closed his eyes, as if he could not see this scene. He knew he would never catch up to Luo Chen’s strength in this lifetime, and no longer harbored any thoughts of competition.

Pure Crane’s beautiful eyes shimmered with layers of strange light. Suppressing peers of the same rank, invincible—this was what a Sage should be!

Huamei’s heart surged with emotion. Holding a great seal in her hand, she stepped forward.

“My master, Luo Chen, is the Truth Crossing Hall Master appointed by the Sovereign herself. He governs the register of demon cultivators and commands the myriad mountains and ten thousand demons. Is there anyone else who is not convinced?”

With the Truth Crossing Seal before them, Luo Chen wearing the Hundred-Feather Robe symbolizing the Truth Crossing Hall Master, and the extraordinary ability he had just displayed, who would dare to be unconvinced?

In the midst of the silence, an aged voice was the first to emerge.

“Whitebrow, pays respects to the Hall Master!”

Everyone turned sharply to look at the Whitebrow Spirit Ape. This old fellow had defected at the first opportunity!

However, after him, clear voices of homage rang out in the hall.

“Hezhi, pays respects to the Hall Master!”

“Hexiang, pays respects to the Hall Master!”

The Twin Beauties of Green Grain, under Green Grain Immortal’s command, also acknowledged Luo Chen’s identity.

Influenced by this, or perhaps unable to bear the pressure any longer, voices of homage rang out one after another in the hall.

Crimson Demon Jiao, Illusion Fire Bird, Eight-Limbed Ape Demon… By the end, only the three Great Demon Emperors remained.

Under Luo Chen’s gaze, Kuang Kuang did not hesitate for long.

“Kuang Kuang, pays respects to the Hall Master!”

Luo Chen nodded slightly, looking at Bi Zheng and Niu Gui, with whom he had once had a life-and-death enmity.

Watching their stubbornly persisting expressions, Luo Chen was helpless.

“Very well. You have spirit. Since you are not convinced by me, then be removed from the register of demon cultivators!”

“Niu Gui, pays respects to the Hall Master!”

“Bi Zheng, pays respects to the Hall Master!”

One voice was honest and simple, the other sharp, ringing out the moment Luo Chen uttered the words “removed from the register.”

Between life and death, there truly was great terror!

Luo Chen was startled, then smiled lightly.

With a thought, his Sage domain receded like a tide, and everyone felt as if a great weight had been lifted.

But they still knelt on the ground, not daring to rise.

“All of you, rise and sit. Since everyone has acknowledged my position as Hall Master, let us now discuss another matter.”

Luo Chen’s smile faded, and a strong sense of danger emanated from his eyes.

…

Half a month later.

At Green Parasol Mountain, Luo Chen’s figure disappeared into the teleportation array.

Inside the Daytime Void Hall, Nether Spring stood beside Azure Frost, her face full of indignation.

“He just up and leaves, but the Truth Crossing Hall completely ignores my commands now.”

Azure Frost said calmly, “You were only acting as the Truth Crossing Hall Master in the first place.”

Nether Spring’s eyes widened. “Acting is still being Vice Hall Master! Now that Luo Chen wants all the experts in the hall to attack the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, I’ll really be left all alone.”

“In this coming battle, the demon cultivators of Green Parasol Mountain are not going to their deaths and not returning.” Azure Frost’s expression was cold and distant. After the light of the teleportation array gradually dimmed, her gaze also seemed slightly dim. “Even if they all die, so what?”

Nether Spring pouted.

“You really don’t care.”

After muttering to herself, she seemed to remember something and suddenly asked:

“Will you go?”

Azure Frost was silent, only raising her head to look upward.

Her gaze seemed to pierce through the high dome, looking toward the Profound Mystery Hall standing above Green Parasol Mountain.

(End of chapter)
