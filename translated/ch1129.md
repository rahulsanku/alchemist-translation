# Chapter 1129: Prison for Cultivators

"Brother Luo... Senior!"

The word "brother" on the tip of his tongue was swallowed back by Steward Yan, replaced by the slightly more respectful address of "Senior."

"Senior knew I would come?"

After speaking, Steward Yan stared intently at Luo Chen, determined not to miss the slightest change in his expression.

To his disappointment, Luo Chen's expression was as calm as a still lake.

"I punished Lai Er right in front of you. If you had any discernment at all, you should have noticed something amiss and sought me out. If you lacked even that much insight, then it wouldn't matter if you came or not."

Steward Yan suddenly understood—Luo Chen had indeed acted deliberately back then.

And his own lateness was due to a cautious investigation that had delayed some time.

He had investigated Jing Lan, investigated Luo Chen's origins, investigated the bizarre injuries on Lai Er's body, and even thoroughly investigated everything Luo Chen had done that day in Qingshan Town.

Only after completing all this preparatory work did he feel he had some measure of confidence to visit.

Steward Yan weighed his words carefully and asked, "Senior, are you a cultivator from beyond this realm?"

Luo Chen's eyes lifted, and his calm expression finally shifted.

The other party had used the term "cultivator" rather than "practitioner" or the ignorant mortals' "immortal," which showed he clearly understood the fundamental differences between cultivators and other beings.

In other words, within this Great Destruction Realm, there were either orthodox cultivators or records pertaining to them!

"What if I am? What if I'm not?"

Steward Yan lowered his eyes deferentially. "Whether you are or not, it doesn't affect my invitation for Senior to become a retainer of the Young City Lord. It's just that after confirming Senior's origins, I can promise certain conditions on his behalf."

"Young City Lord?" Luo Chen studied the other man curiously. "You're from the Immortal Relic City?"

Steward Yan puffed out his chest with pride. "Yes, I serve under the Young City Lord of Immortal Relic City, managing his properties in Qingshan Town."

But when he spoke the words "Qingshan Town," a hint of bitterness crept into his proud expression.

Clearly, coming to this remote Qingshan Town was not his own choice.

Luo Chen had no interest in probing the inside story, but he was even more curious about the matter of cultivators.

"You practice the path of body refinement, not the path of qi cultivation, yet you know about cultivators. So, within this Great Destruction Realm, are there any true cultivators?"

"Perhaps there were, but generally speaking, there are none now," Steward Yan answered.

Luo Chen's brow furrowed deeply. "What do you mean by that?"

Steward Yan said frankly, "Senior must have noticed that within this realm, there is no so-called spiritual energy in heaven and earth. It is completely unsuitable for cultivators to train. Even if someone like Senior occasionally enters the Great Destruction Realm, they can only stagnate for their entire lives."

Luo Chen nodded. From his observations, the external environment was indeed like this.

To put it more severely, it wasn't just stagnation—there was also the risk of realm regression!

Without external spiritual energy to replenish, a cultivator's mana would decrease with each use, their realm would continuously fall, and even their lifespan would be affected.

Steward Yan's answer continued.

"Furthermore, according to some records from predecessors, the environment of the Great Destruction Realm is extremely special and imposes great restrictions on cultivators. The lack of spiritual energy is one thing, but even the origin qi of heaven and earth here has undergone tremendous changes. When cultivators unleash grand techniques or great divine abilities, once they are deployed, their power will be sharply reduced by seventy to eighty percent on the spot, as if annihilated within heaven and earth. Not only that, if one is unfortunate enough to encounter the Soul-Shaking Thunder Sound, the divine sense a cultivator releases externally will also suffer severe damage. At the very least, the soul will be harmed; at worst, it will be destroyed on the spot."

Luo Chen blurted out, full of disbelief: "How could that be possible!"

Steward Yan spread his hands. "I don't know if it's true or false either. These things were all told to me by the Young City Lord when I followed him before. There should be relevant records in the classics, but unfortunately, I don't have any copies here."

Luo Chen took a deep breath to calm his mind.

His entire body was devoid of mana, so he hadn't experimented with releasing spells or even divine abilities, and didn't know the specific situation.

But what Steward Yan had just said about the "Soul-Shaking Thunder Sound" reminded him of the ghost rain he had encountered when he first woke up.

Before that ghost rain, there had been peals of heavenly thunder.

At that time, he had felt as if his soul was about to be destroyed.

Now, this precisely confirmed what the other party had said!

Thinking of this, Luo Chen felt a chill run through his body.

If the entire Great Destruction Realm was like this, then for cultivators, it was nothing less than a natural prison.

From divine sense to mana, from cultivation to combat—almost everything was severely restricted.

Once a cultivator entered this realm, no matter what earth-shattering, mountain-toppling, sea-overturning abilities they once possessed, their strength would be drastically reduced. Not to say they would be at others' mercy, but at the very least, they would be completely disoriented.

What Steward Yan had just said—that generally speaking, there were no cultivators—was probably not an empty claim.

With this thought, Luo Chen's mind raced as he asked, "If that's truly the case, and I am indeed a cultivator, then I wouldn't be considered particularly powerful in this world. Why would Steward Yan bother to invite me?"

Steward Yan revealed a smile. "Senior is being modest. The methods you used to punish Lai Er earlier are not something ordinary practitioners possess. I imagine that even if Senior only has ten or twenty percent of his original ability left, he still far surpasses the mundane. If I can recommend an expert like you to the Young City Lord, I might be able to redeem myself and leave this Qingshan Town."

Having said this, his smile faded slightly as he cupped his hands to Luo Chen. "Of course, before that, I humbly request to test Senior's strength, so that I don't exaggerate in front of the Young City Lord and incur his wrath instead."

Luo Chen pondered for a moment, then nodded lightly. "I grant it!"

Steward Yan's expression turned slightly solemn as he leaped to a spacious area, his entire body gathering momentum.

Layers of red qi force surged forth from his body, gradually forming a massive tiger's head.

Standing three zhang away, Luo Chen watched this scene, his brows twitching slightly.

"Transforming force within the second realm of body refinement?"

"No, transforming force is about refining all the body's strength into every part of the body, moving freely and with perfect mastery when activated. This method of manifesting qi force into form is more like an imitation of the third realm's astral qi manifestation."

"He's taken the wrong path!"

Luo Chen did not follow the orthodox path of body refinement, but that didn't mean he was ignorant of the theoretical knowledge of this system.

Not to mention the many ancient body refinement techniques he had collected in the Mountains and Seas Realm, his good brother Wang Yuan was an orthodox body cultivator.

After Wang Yuan had cultivated to the fourth realm of body refinement, the Leakless Golden Body, he had compiled the relevant techniques and cultivation insights into a book and placed it in the Luo Tian Sect's Scripture Pavilion for disciples to study and research. Luo Chen had naturally read it thoroughly.

Therefore, the moment Steward Yan revealed his skills, Luo Chen saw many flaws.

Steward Yan finished gathering his momentum and let out a loud shout:

"Senior, watch out!"

Then, he threw a punch, and the roar of a fierce tiger nearly exploded across half of Moon Village on the mountain.

Jing Lan, who was hiding behind the door inside the house listening, was so shaken by the proximity that she fell flat on her bottom.

Luo Chen looked at the surging layers of qi force hurtling toward him, unhurriedly raised his hand, and blocked it in front of him.

The next moment, a scene that left Steward Yan dumbfounded appeared.

The overbearing, violent tiger qi force actually crouched obediently under Luo Chen's hand, then gradually dissipated.

Then, Luo Chen moved.

Without any visible motion, he simply took a step and appeared before Steward Yan.

One finger, lightly tapped on the center of his palm.

Pfft!

The qi force that had been condensed throughout Steward Yan's body collapsed like a huge water bag that had been punctured, shattering completely.

Steward Yan staggered back a few steps, an unnatural flush spreading across his face as if he were drunk.

After he steadied himself, he subconsciously swallowed his saliva.

"Senior, your blood and qi are incredibly powerful!"

Indeed, when Luo Chen blocked his move just now, he hadn't used any exquisite technique at all—purely relying on his abundant blood and qi to withstand it.

And the subsequent defeat of Steward Yan was accomplished by using pure speed and strength to scatter his condensed qi force.

These minor methods were not difficult for Luo Chen. In fact, even without using any techniques, purely relying on the terrifying speed and power his physical body could unleash, he could tear apart a fourth-rank demon emperor with his bare hands!

Luo Chen clasped his hands behind his back and said calmly, "Is this test result satisfactory to you?"

Steward Yan nodded repeatedly. "I am at the late stage of the Blood River Realm. Since Senior can defeat me so easily, he is at least a powerful existence at the third stage of body refinement, the Furnace Realm. That is more than enough to make you a distinguished guest of my master."

Having said this, he pointed to the various items the servants had carried up earlier.

"Senior joining my master's service is only a matter of time, and the corresponding rewards will be decided by my master. These are some extra tokens of my esteem. Furthermore..."

Steward Yan gritted his teeth and took out a thin booklet from his bosom, then respectfully handed it to Luo Chen.

"This technique came into my possession by chance. It has some wonderful uses for cultivators who accidentally enter the Great Destruction Realm. Senior may accept it; perhaps it will add a bit more to your strength."

Luo Chen glanced at him in surprise, then took the booklet.

"The Art of Dissolving Power?"

(End of chapter)
