# Chapter 980: Ten Thousand Miles of Wild Sand, No Rest Until Death

The golden flame was none other than the Nirvana Sacred Flame.

Using this flame was also a last resort for Luo Chen.

Being able to escape from the frenzied Nether Spring Sea had once again exhausted the merit of the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire.

But in doing so, the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit had, as expected, once again fallen into a period of lethargy.

To deal with a top-tier powerhouse like Azure Frost, who was below the Ascendant stage, ordinary methods were completely ineffective. He had to rely on items of great power.

Although the Nirvana Sacred Flame was still relatively young, its foundation was high enough, and it was very compatible with Luo Chen's Fire Spirit Body.

Of course, if it were only that, it would be one thing.

Luo Chen had another trick up his sleeve!

*Screech!*

Amidst the piercing cry, a massive fire phoenix soared into the sky.

The divine, majestic blue bird was furious in its heart, yet its eyes were incomparably cold. It flapped its wings without the slightest fear.

*Swish!*

Instantly, the fire phoenix was split in two.

After all, the vessel carrying the Nirvana Sacred Flame was merely an ordinary fourth-rank spell.

But in the next moment, Azure Frost's cold gaze changed.

The fire phoenix that had been split in two did not directly dissipate. Instead, it eerily replenished half of its body on each side, surrounding her from left and right with undiminished momentum.

Azure Frost let out a low cry, both wings trembling simultaneously.

*Swish! Swish!*

Two enormous wind blades, in no way inferior to the fire phoenix, tore through the air.

After meeting the two fire phoenixes, they sliced them in half.

Surely that would settle it, right?

But unexpectedly, the four halves of the fire phoenixes eerily replenished and grew strong again, restoring themselves to their original state.

In an instant, four phoenixes hovered in the sky!

The already distorted light became even more flickering and uncertain amidst the circling dance of the four massive fire phoenixes, making even Luo Chen's distant, tiny figure seem ethereal.

Azure Frost was no fool. Seeing this bizarre scene, she quickly began to think.

Soon, she arrived at an answer.

Her eyes, which seemed capable of seeing through the void itself, fell upon the endless desert beneath her feet.

"Your power comes from this heaven and earth!"

In the distance, Luo Chen stood motionless, his hands maintaining a hand seal.

The vast fire energy from the surrounding earth veins was extracted by him and fused into the Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain spell.

Not only that, but strands of heaven and earth origin qi from the void were also drawn by him, becoming an endless source of power to aid him.

If it had been before, he would never have been able to achieve this level.

But after comprehending the Fire Origin, he had gained the qualification to empower ordinary spells with heaven and earth origin qi.

The eight years at Broken Soul Cliff had also expanded his divine soul to its utmost limit.

Therefore, he could execute such a great technique that was not a divine ability, yet rivaled the power of one.

Four phoenixes hovering in the sky was not the end!

Luo Chen let out a low shout, the hand seal between his fingers changing. Vaguely, another four fire phoenixes were about to split off.

Each one possessed the power of Luo Chen's full-strength strike.

If all eight were to take shape at once...

He looked from afar at that divine, elegant blue bird in the sky and said in a deep voice, "Since we have already escaped the Nether Spring Sea, continuing to fight holds no meaning. How about we stop here?"

"You're afraid?" The four solid fire phoenixes, plus four illusory ones, surrounded her from all directions, above and below, but Azure Frost's face still showed no fear. She only asked with a hint of mockery in her voice.

Luo Chen twitched the corner of his mouth. "Having fought you to this extent earlier, do you think I would still be afraid of you?"

"Since you're not afraid, why seek peace?" Azure Frost retorted, then answered her own question: "You're weighing things. Weighing gains and losses! People like you spend your entire lives calculating, even needing to find a meaning in battle."

Luo Chen laughed heartily. "What meaning is there in a meaningless battle?"

Azure Frost shrieked, "But my sister is all the meaning to me! You harmed her!"

As the words fell, she actually ignored the encirclement of the eight fire phoenixes and transformed into an arrow loosed from a bowstring, charging straight at Luo Chen.

Luo Chen's expression changed drastically, a ruthless glint flashing in his eyes.

He changed his hand seal again!

*Boom!*

The four leading fire phoenixes exploded one after another, unleashing a boundless tide of spiritual energy, with a vast golden sea of flames surging and spreading.

Azure Frost faced all of this, ignoring everything, just charging forward fiercely.

*Swish!*

Wreathed in wisps of golden flames, Azure Frost emerged from the sea of fire, bathed in flames. Ignoring the four fire phoenixes chasing her from behind, her twin claws lunged at Luo Chen's tiny figure.

One strike!

*Thump!*

The earth shook, and yellow sand collapsed.

Within a radius of several li, at a speed unimaginable to mortals, it instantly sank a hundred zhang.

The four fire phoenixes pursued closely, diving into the deep pit.

"Roar!"

A furious roar sounded.

Then, a figure nearly a thousand zhang tall, holding two sharp claws aloft with its hands, rose from the ground. A large amount of sand and gravel fell from its body with a rustling sound, like a downpour of yellow sand.

It was Luo Chen, revealing his true body!

But unlike before, although this true body still bore some resemblance to him, it now had a green face and fangs, skin like dark, hard iron, and a faint, foul odor emanating from it.

His appearance was somewhat ugly, vaguely resembling the legendary *yaksha*.

On the earth, the thousand-zhang giant firmly grasped the divine, majestic blue bird. The four fire phoenixes broke through the air and slammed into the blue bird.

*Boom! Boom! Boom! Boom!*

Explosions rang out one after another, raising a mushroom cloud in the endless desert, with surging air waves sweeping for hundreds of li.

Amidst the rolling waves of air, the two figures were forcibly separated.

*Bang!*

Luo Chen was directly flung dozens of li away. His massive body, like a bulldozer, kept sliding across the desert.

Wherever he passed, hills were instantly leveled, and mountains were broken apart.

When he finally managed to stop, the thousand-zhang giant let out a muffled groan, and then... *Pfft!*

In an instant, the massive giant shrank to its extreme, returning to a normal eight-foot-tall, imposing stature.

A mouthful of stagnant blood uncontrollably spewed forth. Luo Chen staggered and half-knelt on the ground.

The jet-black hair, once bound by the Flat Peak Crown, was now disheveled, dancing wildly in the wind like silver snakes.

His robes had long since burst and scattered due to the transformation into his true body.

"In the end, the battle at Nether Spring Sea consumed too much source power. I can't even sustain the Giant Spirit Transformation anymore?"

Luo Chen wiped the blood from the corner of his mouth with one hand, slowly stood up, and looked gloomily into the distance.

The divine beast, the blue bird, flapped its wings amidst the surging air waves and hovered in the sky once more.

"Do you really think I'm afraid of you? At worst, we'll both perish!"

Seeing Azure Frost charging to kill again, Luo Chen pressed his fingers together like a sword. Five streams of light shot out from his mouth.

*Whoosh! Whoosh! Whoosh! Whoosh! Whoosh!*

As the streams of light emerged, sword qi appeared.

Amidst the ringing of swords, a terrifying storm of sword qi soared into the sky, transforming into a peerless sword formation that stood in the way.

Azure Frost was extremely fast and had no time to dodge. She plunged straight into it.

In an instant, she was subjected to the extreme grinding and killing of the sword formation.

This Grand Five Elements Sword Formation, infused with the top-grade Nine Yang Geng Metal, was unparalleled in sharpness under heaven.

Since Luo Chen had successfully completed this formation, it had been invincible in every encounter.

Whether it was Sage Divine Origin or the Purple-Robed Marquis and others, when facing this formation, they either had to self-destruct their lifebound treasures or suffer severe injuries.

However, an unexpected scene appeared.

Trapped within the sword formation, Azure Frost charged and rampaged, seemingly unaffected.

The sound of clanging, like metal striking metal, rang out continuously from within.

Feeling the rapidly depleting mana in his body, Luo Chen's expression grew increasingly gloomy.

He hadn't expected Azure Frost to have tempered her true body to such a terrifying degree, almost like a peerless divine weapon.

The Grand Five Elements Sword Formation, while powerful, was not a concentrated point-break attack, but a continuous, crisscrossing cut.

Faced with Azure Frost's all-around, impenetrable hardness, he found no way to strike.

"Truly infuriating!"

Luo Chen gritted his teeth, uttering each word.

Then, with a wave of his hands, the five streams of light entered the grand formation. Using the sword qi as cover, they silently stabbed towards the divine, majestic blue bird.

Unlike the sword qi, which was blocked on the outside, these five streams of light followed the flow of mana and entered the divine bird's body.

*Pfft!*

Luo Chen's expression turned fierce. "Suppress!"

The Five Suppression Origin Awls activated simultaneously, beginning to suppress Azure Frost's mana throughout her body.

Not only that, but Luo Chen, whose divine soul foundation was immensely powerful, unleashed another move.

He casually raised his hand, and a golden light shot out, plunging straight into the blue bird's head.

It was the true artifact, the Soul-Weaving Needle, also obtained from the Hundred-Legged Great Demon King, specializing in attacking the divine soul.

Unexpectedly, after this Soul-Weaving Needle entered halfway into the blue bird's head, it became stuck, unable to advance further.

"I refuse to believe I can't suppress you!"

Luo Chen grew ruthless. Ignoring the rapid consumption of his mana, he opened his mouth and spat.

A grey light flew out, spinning.

As it flew, it grew with the wind, instantly expanding to a hundred zhang in size.

*Thump!*

The giant cauldron sat in the void, emitting a dull sound.

Crimson chains extended from within, wrapping around Azure Frost's physical body.

Not only that, but a waterfall of brilliant light poured down, pushing aside the omnipresent sword qi, completely enveloping Azure Frost.

The sword orbs provided support, the black awls suppressed her mana, the golden needle locked her soul, and the Primordial Unity Cauldron sealed the void.

Luo Chen had almost used all his methods. He was going to refine Azure Frost alive!

His mana was consumed at an unimaginable speed, and Luo Chen's aura grew increasingly weary.

But he showed no sign of stopping. The surrounding heaven and earth origin qi also seemed to have released their restrictions, rolling towards him in torrents.

Within the sword formation, the divine bird Azure Frost's gaze remained cold. Even under such terrifying suppression, she still stared fixedly at Luo Chen.

Through the void, their eyes met, each filled with fierce determination.

The intent of no rest until death was almost palpable.

This battle had clearly reached its final stage.

Either Luo Chen, having exhausted all his methods, would refine Azure Frost, or Azure Frost would break the formation and kill Luo Chen, whose source power was depleted and mana was about to run dry.

The outcome of their struggle was about to be decided.

...

Inside a camp tent, a young girl was listening intently, a look of doubt on her face.

She blinked and looked at the middle-aged man beside her.

"Brother Zhou, did you hear any commotion?"

Zhou Mingxuan's expression had long been grave. He nodded and strode out of the tent.

Across the void, he looked into the far distance.

Then, he spread his divine sense and sent out a summons, gathering all the nearby guards.

"Although this is a border region, we must guard against a sudden attack by the demon race. Everyone, follow me!"

*Whoosh! Whoosh! Whoosh!*

One after another, beams of light shot into the sky, numbering in the hundreds, each exuding a considerable aura.

Looking around, the lowest realm among them was at the Initiate stage.

And the leading Brother Zhou was a powerful early-stage Sage.

Under his command, this team advanced rapidly. After half a day, they crossed a high sand dune under the moonlight.

In an instant, a scene of devastation burst into their view.

The earth was cracked, and rolling yellow sand continuously flowed into the deep abyssal fissures.

In some places, the yellow sand had been burned into a red magma, flowing and bubbling.

And in the middle of a magma lake, ten li in circumference, a man with tattered clothes and wild black hair was forming hand seals, his complexion deathly pale.

In front of him was a terrifying sword formation surrounded by sword qi, and a giant grey cauldron hanging high in the sky.

All of this was suppressing a divine, majestic blue bird.

The young girl who had sensed the anomaly earlier was first shocked, then overjoyed.

"So a great demon is indeed breaking through its realm, trying to attack the Buddhist territory. Fortunately, this senior is here to block this great demon for us. But he looks very strained. Let's go help him!"

The others seemed accustomed to similar scenes, taking out their treasures and heading towards the magma lake.

Zhou Mingxuan, the leader, had the same thought.

However, as they approached a certain distance, his expression suddenly changed drastically.

"No!"

"Everyone, fall back quickly!"

Unfortunately, this warning came too late.

The man sitting cross-legged in the void, his face pale, opened his mouth and spat out a mouthful of blood.

The surrounding sword formation could no longer operate and instantly shattered.

The terrifying sword qi scattered in all directions.

Wherever it passed, figures turned into clouds of blood mist.

Amidst the *puffing* sounds, the disheveled man waved his hand, summoning the five sword orbs and the giant cauldron towards him. Then, he glared hatefully at Zhou Mingxuan and the others.

"Incompetent fools who ruin everything!"

With those words, he shot off into the distance without the slightest hesitation.

Zhou Mingxuan's face was deathly pale. He had clearly realized what had happened.

"Luo Chen, you can't escape!"

Within the magma lake, the blue bird, whose aura had already weakened to its limit, slowly floated up into the sky.

It cast a cold glance at Zhou Mingxuan and the others, then chased after Luo Chen.

After the man and the demon had left.

The surviving young girl trembled all over, her eyes filled with terror at the instant death of hundreds of companions and the countless broken limbs and bones on the ground.

"Brother Zhou, we clearly came to help that senior. Why did he turn around and harm us?"

Zhou Mingxuan let out a bitter laugh but found it hard to explain.

Help?

They had been a hindrance!

That unfamiliar man and demon had clearly been in the most dangerous stalemate—one suppressing and refining, the other resisting and gathering strength.

Their own group's sudden entry into the battle had disrupted the mind of the man with the disheveled hair, even drawing the surrounding sword formation towards them, weakening the suppressive force and allowing the great demon to break free.

Otherwise, why would he curse them as "incompetent fools who ruin everything" before leaving?

And all of this, in the final analysis, was not the young girl's recklessness, but his, the presiding Sage's, failure to detect the signs earlier.

He found it inconvenient to explain and could only mutter to himself.

"Good intentions were repaid with ingratitude!"

The young girl was indignant, especially the tragic death of so many companions, which was hard for her to accept.

"This person is so ungrateful. I must report this to the Precept Court and the Arhat Hall!"

Zhou Mingxuan opened his mouth but did not stop her.

He just kept repeating the name the blue bird had roared before leaving in his heart.

"Luo Chen?"

...

Under the night sky.

Two figures, one after the other, ceaselessly chased each other across the boundless desert.

A fierce wind surged towards them. The man in front turned and slashed with his sword, cutting the gale in two.

From time to time, fire phoenixes and flame dragons would be born from the void, attacking the divine bird behind.

But compared to their initial attacks, both the man and the blue bird were much weaker.

Occasionally, snatches of conversation could be heard from their brief clashes, gradually dissipating with the night wind.

"Luo Chen, you can't escape!"

"Azure Frost, why this relentless pursuit?"

"When you tried to refine me earlier, why didn't you say that?"

"Continuing like this will only leave us both grievously wounded, and we'll perish together!"

"The one who dies will only be you!"

"The mixed poison on your body must be uncontrollable by now! And my Five Suppression Origin Awls, though they can't completely seal your mana, will sink deeper and deeper as your mana circulates. And the Soul-Weaving Needle—if you don't force it out soon, your divine soul will also collapse..."

"So what? My sister's entrapment at Broken Soul Cliff was all your doing! I could have saved her, but you betrayed us!"

"Betrayal? Where does betrayal come in?"

"At Green Parasol Mountain, my sister guided your cultivation, kept you from going astray, and later lent you her blessed land and cave dwelling for cultivation. Is this how you repay us?"

"That was merely a transaction. Do you think my Dao Union Pill was so cheap that I would just give it away? And as for the matter at Broken Soul Cliff, I was forced into it. My life and death were in the hands of others. What could I do?"

"If you couldn't do anything, then you should have died!"

Perhaps it was the many battles and multiple brushes with death, or perhaps it was the guilt and concern for Rosycloud, but Azure Frost, usually silent and taciturn, was now somewhat agitated.

Unable to communicate, she even ignored Rosycloud's early warnings and mobilized her origin power.

"Void Severance Slash!"

Under the night sky, a pitch-black crack suddenly appeared.

Luo Chen, who had been on guard, sidestepped.

But he was still too slow.

*Crack!*

His left arm was severed cleanly in the middle.

Luo Chen's expression changed slightly. He grabbed his severed arm, intending to reattach it, but a strange force prevented him from successfully connecting it.

He no longer had the surplus source power to regenerate the arm, so he had to put it into his storage ring.

Behind him, after unleashing this strike, Azure Frost's form staggered slightly, and in her stagger, she seemed somewhat illusory, as if about to disappear from this space.

Luo Chen gave her a dark look, then, holding the Yuantu Sword, fled once more.

Azure Frost quickly stabilized her condition and resumed her pursuit.

In this chase, the two had long since penetrated deep into the boundless desert.

As dawn was about to break, Luo Chen fell like a meteor to the foot of a high mountain.

*Boom!*

The moment his foot touched the ground, an uncontrollable wave of mana directly leveled the mountain.

And below, figures seemed to sense something, all looking up at the same time.

A foul stench, cruel gazes, stretching as far as the eye could see.

No one knew that such a massive army of demon beasts was gathered in this desert.

High in the sky, the blue bird chased after him again.

"Luo Chen, how will you escape this time?"

Luo Chen let out a ferocious grin. He no longer fled. Instead, he raised his hand.

The Yuantu Sword transformed into a beam of blood light, charging straight towards that army of demon beasts.

(End of Chapter)
