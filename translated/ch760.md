# Chapter 760: Luo Chen Sets the Trap, All Enter the Net

"Senior Brother, is the number enough?"

"Counting this harvest, there are fifteen illusion cores in total."

"Only fifteen?"

In a quiet mountain cave, a man and woman conversed in low voices.

When she heard the number fifteen, obvious conflict appeared on the woman's face.

"Senior Brother, why not use this batch of illusion cores first and attempt to roam the sea of origin qi and comprehend origin power!"

"How could I do that? Before entering, we agreed that we would first find twenty illusion cores together, and at that time, you and I would each cultivate. Now we are still five short. How could I adapt in advance? Besides, if anyone is to use them first, it should be you, Mingyan!" Sage Dragon Abyss said.

Seeing the woman remain conflicted, he shook his head and comforted her. "Inside Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, the flow of time is greatly different from the outside world. We still have ample time. Moreover, because of those juniors' encirclement and slaughter in the outer region, large numbers of second- and third-tier illusion beasts will be forced into the interior, where they will devour and merge with one another, giving birth to more fourth-tier illusion beasts. We have many opportunities."

At this point, Sage Dragon Abyss could not help smiling.

"If not for your master-disciple lineage telling me the trick that illusion cores need to be absorbed together in concentration, I fear that even if I labored inside this grotto-heaven for a hundred years, I might not gain anything."

Mingyan gave a sound of acknowledgment. This secret had been summarized by several elders of their master-disciple lineage who had trained inside Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven.

Unlike the clan lineage, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's master-disciple lineage was weak and isolated, and those who participated in the five-hundred-year grand competition often entered the grotto-heaven alone.

Without a Dao protector guarding them, they did not dare lightly absorb illusion cores and enter deep cultivation.

One elder, in order to prevent disturbance, simply hunted illusion beasts all along the way, hoarded large numbers of illusion cores with a secret technique, then after making layer upon layer of protective preparations, absorbed them all at once to cultivate, and finally successfully comprehended origin power!

Whether the other clans knew this trick, Mingyan did not know. But this time, the master-disciple lineage had told this trick to Sage Dragon Abyss, who was on very good terms with her and was especially well regarded by the elders of the master-disciple lineage.

Only, even with the trick, illusion beasts were hard to find!

These illusion beasts were generated by Mirage Dragon power and were naturally compatible with this heaven and earth, possessing extremely powerful concealment.

In a full twenty years, the two of them working together had only successfully harvested fifteen illusion cores.

They were still five short of the goal of ten per person!

"I calculated it. The remaining five will require at most another ten years. As for our cultivation, each of us will set a ten-year limit. In this way, the total is only fifty years. If we still have not succeeded by then, we will still have fifty years to collect enough illusion cores and carry out a final attempt!"

Sage Dragon Abyss spoke with full confidence.

Sage Mingyan nodded. "Then we will follow Senior Brother's words!"

Just as the two were discussing, Sage Dragon Abyss's eyes suddenly brightened, and he went straight out of the cave.

"Senior Brother, what is it?"

Mingyan hurriedly followed him out, but the scene that entered her eyes instantly made her small mouth fall open.

She saw a white argali sheep as huge as a small mountain crushing through layer after layer of forest, heading east.

"Fourth-tier illusion beast!" she cried out subconsciously.

"Go. Follow it. Find an opportunity to take its core!"

Sage Dragon Abyss was extremely decisive, and a long sword was already gripped in his hand, as if he were ready to act at any moment.

Mingyan followed behind without thinking.

...

On a mountain summit.

Two figures slowly appeared.

A young man in luxurious long robes, holding a white jade fan, lowered his head and looked toward the abyss beneath the peak.

Heavy mist churned, and poisonous miasma was dense.

"Old Ghost Qi, are you certain there is a fourth-tier illusion beast below?"

Beside him, the stooped Qi Xiongcheng carried a purple jade gourd almost larger than his body on his back.

He answered solemnly, "This old slave absolutely did not see wrongly. Three years ago, a bird-type illusion beast hunted large numbers of third-tier illusion beasts outside, then after eating its fill, flew alone into this abyss. In this old slave's view, that illusion beast's body was extremely majestic, and even among fourth-tier illusion beasts, it counts as strong. Its illusion core must contain abundant spiritual impulse!"

Toward this answer, Shi Ju not only did not praise him, but revealed dissatisfaction.

"Since you discovered it, why did you not capture and kill it, and insist on waiting for me to leave seclusion? Do you not know that this time, I entered not only to comprehend origin power, but also to borrow the abundant time inside the grotto-heaven to cultivate the Superior Profound Cave-Bright Blazing Flame True Scripture, so that after the grand competition ends, I can directly learn one of the sect's Seven Great Divine Abilities?"

In his words, he showed an attitude of being determined to obtain the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's Seven Great Divine Abilities.

But every great-clan expert knew that Heavenly Origin divine abilities were never lightly transmitted. Even if one's aptitude was excellent, there was still a threshold to learning them.

Only Ascendant ancestors and Sages who had comprehended law true intent in advance could learn them.

In other words, Shi Ju was not worried at all about whether he could comprehend law true intent. He had already regarded it as a certainty.

Qi Xiongcheng lowered his head even further and said quietly, "If it were an ordinary fourth-tier illusion beast, this old slave would be confident in taking it down, but this one is too strong, so Young Master must help. When you and I join hands, we will be able to seize it with ease."

"Enough!"

Shi Ju waved a hand and closed the fan in his hand.

"Since I am here, do not speak of joining hands. In a moment, I will scatter its form, and you will use the Purple Yang Gourd to collect the illusion core."

The young master would personally act?

Qi Xiongcheng did not hesitate and accepted this plan, without the slightest doubt about Shi Ju's strength.

Among the thirteen true disciples who entered the grotto-heaven this time, the young master was definitely among the outstanding. Not only was his aptitude exceptional, but he also possessed powerful battle strength.

Even Liu Yuan, who had trained in the Northern Sea for many years, had merely been defeated by the young master back then!

The only thing that needed consideration was probably activating the Purple Yang Gourd in a moment!

This true artifact had been specially refined by the Shi family for this grotto-heaven trip. Aside from powerful defensive effects, it also possessed suppressive and absorptive power specifically targeting illusion beasts.

However, Qi Xiongcheng himself had not refined it for very long, so he had to be especially careful when activating it.

Huff...

By his ear came Shi Ju's soft exhalation.

That airflow carried a scorching heat.

Qi Xiongcheng looked at Shi Ju's back, sleeves swelling and white fan slowly opening, and had already made his preparations.

Just as Shi Ju was about to act, he suddenly asked:

"Have you found any traces of that Wilderness Wanderer I asked you to inquire about?"

Hesitation appeared on Qi Xiongcheng's face.

Because of this hesitation, Shi Ju grew displeased.

"Speak!"

Qi Xiongcheng did not dare delay and hurriedly said, "I did indeed learn news of this person from Yu Xu. He is also still inside the grotto-heaven."

"Good that he is still here!" Shi Ju pressed his lips together, greed appearing in his eyes.

Qi Xiongcheng kindly warned, "Young Master, this person is extremely troublesome. Not only does he have considerable attainments in the divine soul path, according to Yu Xu, he is also extremely formidable in spells and body techniques. I think it would be better if we did not provoke..."

"Hm?"

Qi Xiongcheng instantly fell silent with fear and dared say no more.

Shi Ju sneered. "I am determined to provoke him! That Heavenly Secret Pill personally refined by the Alchemy Saint before she left contains who knows how much vast spiritual impulse gathered from illusion cores. If I obtain it, it will surely add wings to a tiger in my comprehension of fire origin. It may even give me a chance to comprehend some other, more powerful law true intent!"

Origin power was merely the most basic form of law true intent, and also the easiest form for cultivators to comprehend.

Above this foundation, there were still other, more powerful law true intents.

After all, the Great Dao of laws seemed singular, but beneath the same law, there would be all sorts of different variations.

He, Shi Ju, either would not comprehend law true intent in the Sage stage, or if he did, he would definitely comprehend something better!

"Step aside and watch how I force out that beast in the abyss!"

At some unknown time, the white jade fan had already turned crimson, its ribs like red-hot glass. Wisps of ethereal flame appeared and disappeared upon it.

Shi Ju held the fire fan and was about to wave it down.

It was at this moment!

"Shriek!"

A sharp bird cry pierced the eardrums.

The next moment, an incomparably divine and spirited great eagle rose proudly from below like an arrow leaving the string.

Countless clouds and mist trailed behind it, forming a long tail wing.

The moment it flew out, it did not even look at the two people on the mountain peak and flew west without hesitation.

Swish!

The fire fan immediately closed. Shi Ju stared at that departing figure in shock and uncertainty, with three parts lingering fear. This illusion beast's might was so strong! If he had rashly acted just now and accidentally fallen to its method...

"Young Master's cultivation art seems to have advanced another step. Your killing moves are freely withdrawn and released..."

Before the flattery could finish, Shi Ju angrily said:

"Why say such useless nonsense? Chase!"

Qi Xiongcheng smiled awkwardly and followed behind in pursuit, but his eyes were rather helpless.

For such a powerful illusion beast to suddenly leave its nest, there had to be something rather dangerous.

With his personality of running about outside for many years and being cautious in all things, he did not want to join the excitement at all.

But who made him a servant of the Shi family?

Even if outsiders viewed him as famous and possessed of Sage honor, before the Shi family, he still could not raise his head.

"Forget it. I will do my best to protect him!"

...

If someone had a complete map of the grotto-heaven, they would discover that the very center of Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven was shockingly Three-Rainbow Falls!

Who knew how the Gu family had discovered that core region.

But in any case, Three-Rainbow Falls was located in the center and covered the four directions.

At present, from east, south, west, north, and several other directions, whether tens of thousands of li or even hundreds of thousands of li away, smoke and dust rolled, and white clouds surged.

Illusion beasts of varying strength set out from their nests and charged toward Three-Rainbow Falls.

Everywhere they passed, large numbers of trees collapsed. There were even natural barriers and great mountains spanning entire regions that were smashed straight through by those unreasonable giant beasts that possessed only instinct, as they charged out across them.

And Gu Daosu, the person at the center of all this, knew nothing.

His Dao protector, Madam Xu, finally discovered something was wrong.

Boom!

Ten li away from the waterfall.

A cold and beautiful married woman in a long black dress struck back a hundred-zhang scorpion with one sleeve.

The other party rolled continuously across the earth like a ball, showing how powerful Madam Xu's sleeve strike was.

Madam Xu's expression was as usual. She only assumed this was an illusion beast drawn over by her nephew Gu Daosu's state of sudden enlightenment.

This sort of situation had occurred occasionally over the past two or three years and was not strange.

Killing it might take some effort, but forcing it back was not difficult.

These illusion beasts were all very cowardly and would flee when they saw the situation turn bad.

But after the scorpion steadied itself, it did not retreat. Instead, it still watched Madam Xu like a tiger eyeing prey.

"So stubborn?"

Madam Xu frowned.

The next moment, her expression changed slightly.

Within her divine sense, another illusion beast was rushing over from the southeast, and its target was shockingly Three-Rainbow Falls.

She drew a deep breath, released towering killing intent, and shouted lowly at the scorpion illusion beast ahead.

"Scram!"

After releasing this threatening word, she hesitated no longer and hurriedly returned to defend Three-Rainbow Falls.

After she left, the scorpion illusion beast hesitated somewhat.

That towering killing intent let it vaguely understand that it was completely no match for this person.

But its instinctive impulse suppressed any resistance to its desire for that "delicacy" ahead.

Leave? Or advance?

In its muddle, the scorpion illusion beast carefully moved forward.

One day!

Two days!

Madam Xu appeared frequently within a hundred li of Three-Rainbow Falls.

Every time she appeared, she would surely use a thunderous strike, either forcing back or scattering the illusion beast, and she actually relied on her strength alone to keep many illusion beasts from approaching Three-Rainbow Falls.

But she also gradually noticed what was wrong.

"I clearly laid down the formation bestowed by the clan head, which can isolate most spiritual impulse from leaking out. Why are there still so many illusion beasts charging at Su'er?"

"One or two would be one thing, and I could treat them as having entered by accident, but this time the number is truly too great."

"Could someone be causing trouble?"

After another ruthless strike that forcefully scattered a fourth-tier illusion beast, the cold and beautiful married woman flew onto a towering mountain peak.

She formed a spell seal, and her aura began to surge.

From the second level of Sage, it began advancing toward the third level of Sage, but at the crucial moment, she suppressed it again.

However, her vast divine sense had already spread out.

When the perceived scene returned, Madam Xu's expression changed drastically.

Within her divine sense, she shockingly discovered that with Three-Rainbow Falls at the center, no fewer than ten fourth-tier illusion beasts had unknowingly gathered within several thousand li!

There were even more dangerous auras charging over from distant places.

In addition...

"Petty wretches, get out here!"

She shouted loudly, and her sharp cry spread across several thousand li.

Two figures slowly walked out from a mountain stream.

The leader had a youthful face, and behind him, a middle-aged man in golden robes followed step for step.

"I wondered what commotion was stirring up an illusion-beast riot. So it was Junior Brother Gu! That makes sense. He has always possessed extraordinary comprehension, and he has an elder brother like Senior Brother Daoyin to seek a Rotten Axe-Handle Black Chess Piece for him. For him to walk ahead of us now is truly worthy of congratulations!"

The young man praised as he stepped into the sky.

When Madam Xu saw the arrivals, especially the golden-robed middle-aged man behind him, her pupils could not help shrinking.

Gu Yuan!

Dao protector Gu Chen!

Madam Xu drew a deep breath. "Is this your doing?"

Gu Yuan froze. "Was this not caused by Junior Brother Gu's sudden enlightenment state?"

Madam Xu sneered. "Su'er has been in sudden enlightenment for three years, and nothing like this happened before. Yet you still say it was not your doing?"

Gu Yuan furrowed his brows.

Gu Chen beside him also shook his head.

Madam Xu was unwilling to say more, because in her divine sense, another illusion beast was charging over from afar.

Madam Xu increasingly felt matters were becoming troublesome. Gritting her silver teeth, she suddenly continued the unfinished spell seal from before.

Her realm began to surge!

It crossed the third level of Sage in one stroke and even advanced toward the middle stage of Sage.

A hairpin was taken from her head and tossed casually. The jade hairpin hung high above Three-Rainbow Falls, like a sharp sword, intimidating the four directions!

Because of this, those restless illusion beasts had no choice but to suppress the instincts in their hearts.

The golden-robed middle-aged man looked at this scene and could not help nodding slightly.

"Madam Xu has used a secret technique to forcibly raise her realm and use it to intimidate enemies. But this secret technique absolutely cannot last long, and the aftermath is quite severe. To protect her nephew, she is risking her life."

Listening to this introduction, Gu Yuan could not help nodding as well.

Suddenly, he glanced in a certain direction.

"More fellow disciples have arrived!"

Whoosh! Whoosh!

Two escape lights abruptly stopped.

Sage Dragon Abyss stopped a thousand li away with a solemn expression.

"There are people ahead!"

Mingyan's face was slightly pale. "There are beasts on the ground! And many, many of them!"

Perhaps because more and more Sage experts had appeared, the even more numerous illusion beasts instead did not dare launch an attack.

But also because of their numbers, and the instinctive desire within them, they were unwilling to retreat just like this.

For a time, a stalemate formed.

And during this stalemate...

Rumble!

Rumble!

More and more illusion beasts came from afar.

The Sages drawn over also became more and more numerous.

Every arrival had their attention drawn by Gu Daosu, who was currently in sudden enlightenment, and every person's thoughts were placed on the vast group of illusion beasts nearby.

If they could catch all these illusion beasts in one net, it would save decades of effort!

If they broke Gu Daosu's sudden enlightenment state, they could remove the strongest opponent in this competition!

Madam Xu knew perfectly well what they were thinking.

It was precisely because she understood these things that her expression grew more and more unsightly.

"This stalemate cannot continue. I must use thunderous methods to kill the chicken and warn the monkeys!"

Just as she was about to act, her already ugly expression suddenly turned deathly pale.

Shriek!

Between heaven and earth, a piercing bird cry rang out.

An enormous white eagle waved its wings and broke through the air.

Its powerful might was absolutely no weaker than a middle-stage Sage cultivator.

It did not pause in the slightest. The moment it arrived, it flew straight toward Three-Rainbow Falls, treating the hanging jade hairpin as if it were nothing.

If it were only this, that would be one thing.

But the white eagle's arrival was like a spark falling into a pile of dry firewood.

Those high-level illusion beasts that had been eyeing everything fiercely could no longer hold back and charged together with the white eagle!

Madam Xu stood on the mountain peak, looking down at the dozen or so giant beasts charging at the same time across the earth. Her eyes were despairing, and her face was deathly pale.

It was not only her. All the Sages present changed color together.

At this moment, the attacking and defending positions had changed!

It was no longer cultivators hunting illusion beasts, but illusion beasts charging at cultivators in a group!

*(End of Chapter)*
