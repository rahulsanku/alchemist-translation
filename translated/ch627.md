# Chapter 627: Deep in the Sunken Sea, an Ancient Cultivator Ruin

Meeting Ding Yi again, the body shape was familiar, and the warmth was familiar too.

But Luo Chen could not help frowning.

After a brief exchange of pleasantries, Luo Chen could not help asking, "Fellow Daoist Ding, why has the flesh on you still not gone down?"

Ding Yi laughed loudly. "Daoist Azure Yang is this attentive?"

"I am just afraid my Dustfall Pill was ineffective, and worried Fellow Daoist would come to me for after-sales service," Luo Chen said helplessly.

Ding Yi waved his hand. "You are overthinking it. Back then, money and goods were settled cleanly. How could there be any talk of pursuing responsibility afterward? Moreover, that Dustfall Pill was indeed effective for me."

If it was effective, why had this body of fat still not been reduced?

Luo Chen looked one more time in surprise, but did not continue asking.

Perhaps the other party cultivated some secret art, which was why he maintained this body shape.

It was just like Luo Chen himself, who had the nickname red-faced Daoist in the outside world because he cultivated by taking True Flame Pills, leaving the skin of his face crimson year-round.

For this sort of matter, mentioning it once was fine. Asking too much would instead make people unhappy.

Potbellied Ding Yi walked on Peng Island, his gaze looking around from time to time, his mouth constantly producing sounds of praise and occasionally sounds of regret.

"This vast Peng Lake, in less than twenty years, has been managed into something quite decent. Ordinary wandering cultivators would be endlessly envious if they saw it."

"It is only a pity that some places still have not been managed properly. Otherwise, how many more spirit stones could be earned each year!"

Luo Chen smiled. "I have made Fellow Daoist laugh."

His thoughts over these years had not been on managing a spirit land in the first place.

For it to reach this scale was already the result of Heavenjade and the Zhang family doing their utmost.

If he had truly managed it himself, then in nearly twenty years, Peng Lake would have long since changed heaven and earth.

At the very least, there would not be only this few people!

Although the spirit veins of this spirit land were scattered, there were many of them, and their quality was not low.

It was most suitable for establishing a sect or gang force.

Only with enough manpower could enough industries be opened up.

These were all words from experience.

Luo Chen naturally knew these things, and he had even managed things like this before. It was only that his aspirations were not here now.

"In the battle of Cold Light Island back then, Daoist Azure Yang, your prestige was truly great! If not for the world's gaze all being drawn away by the Sage battle, the one who truly became famous in that battle should have been you, Azure Yang... Devil Lord."

"Fellow Daoist praises me too much. I merely taught a few Golden Core cultivators a lesson. It cannot be called any sort of prestige."

"Tsk, tsk. Among that group who surrounded you, the others were still fine, even Qian Ting was merely so-so. But that mountain lord of Hundred Craft Mountain was not some weakling. Even Ding would have to expend quite some effort against him. Yet under such a siege, Daoist Azure Yang, you could leave calmly and even counter-kill several people. My praise is not excessive at all."

Luo Chen tugged the corners of his mouth. He naturally understood better than anyone how formidable the mountain lord of Hundred Craft Mountain had been.

If he had not seized the opportunity, and the Heavenly Peng had risen with a thousand-jun strike to send him flying into the distance, then had he truly been entangled by the other party, unless Han Zhan acted, his life and death would have been hard to predict.

However, although Ding Yi spoke modestly, within his words he still had the confidence that he could fight and defeat the mountain lord of Hundred Craft Mountain. That was a little unexpected.

He carefully sized up this fatty.

The other party also did not hide anything. His mana fluctuations slowly spread outward.

Luo Chen raised a brow.

Golden Core Stage Nine!

This person had previously hidden quite deeply. The cultivation he revealed outwardly was only around mid Golden Core.

Now, without concealing it, the depth of his realm was like an abyssal sea.

Among all the people Luo Chen had seen in this life, within the Golden Core stage, only Angler was a bit stronger than him. Purple Dowager and Ghost Spirit Child, whom he had met hurriedly once or twice, were both inferior to Ding Yi.

No, there was still one person who could definitely firmly suppress Ding Yi by a head!

Icecastle's supreme elder, Superior Canglong!

At that time, Luo Chen had still been in the Foundation Establishment realm and could not sense the specific strengths and weaknesses of Golden Core cultivators. Superior Canglong had also restrained his spiritual pressure extremely well before him in ordinary times.

Now, recalling it carefully from the perspective of a Golden Core cultivator, that was clearly a sign of initial fusion of essence, qi, and spirit, the three treasures, at Great Perfection!

"I did not expect that you were the one hiding your depths."

Luo Chen sighed, and his steps stopped beneath a willow tree by the lakeside.

Ding Yi chuckled. "When traveling the Northern Sea, demons and devils are everywhere. If one does not hide a little cultivation, and others arrange targeted measures, would that not become disaster? Besides, Daoist Azure Yang, are you not the same? With Golden Core Stage Four... eh!"

Ding Yi's tone stalled, only because the mana fluctuations released by the white-robed man by the lakeside grew increasingly strong, the cultivation of a genuine Golden Core Stage Six.

In fact!

Because that pure and domineering mana caused extremely strong spiritual pressure, it forced him to involuntarily use his own mana fluctuations to resist.

The collision of the two spiritual pressures touched and immediately withdrew, without showing high or low.

Yet Ding Yi was both shocked and delighted.

"Golden Core Stage Six!"

"Fellow Daoist's Dao progress has advanced to this degree!"

The domineering spiritual pressure slowly receded into Luo Chen's body like the tide.

His bright eyes looked toward Ding Yi. Luo Chen was determined not to miss the slightest change in his expression.

"Since Fellow Daoist has come to see me, why keep talking east and west? Why not go straight to the main topic?"

Ding Yi's smile grew even wider. "I have always acknowledged your battle strength. Now that you have revealed this realm, I am even more satisfied."

Luo Chen asked, "As far as I know, you should have arrived at the Myriad Immortals Assembly long ago."

Ding Yi did not argue, but frankly nodded. "Indeed. Fourteen years ago, I arrived at the Fulong Mountain Range and asked the Moya Cave Master to refine a secret treasure for me."

"Secret treasure?"

"Yes. That secret treasure concerns a dangerous place I am about to go to. If the secret treasure were not completed, I would not go, and I would not come to find you."

"Find me?" Luo Chen raised a brow, understanding that the topic had finally entered its core. "Could it be that you want to invite Luo to travel with you?"

"Fellow Daoist is indeed clever!" Ding Yi openly admitted his purpose.

Walking to Luo Chen's side, one tall and one fat, the two stood shoulder to shoulder, looking at the lake surface, calm as a mirror and without ripples.

"That dangerous place is extremely dangerous, surpassing even Wuzhong Valley, Soul-Severing Cliff, and many other dangerous places in the Northern Sea. In fact, in my view, even the former Arctic Yemo Heaven was not as dangerous as that place."

Luo Chen was shocked inwardly.

The names Ding Yi mentioned were all famous dangerous places in the Northern Sea cultivation world.

Wuzhong Valley was known as the place where ten thousand demons did not return. Demon beasts of third-tier and below were all buried within, and even fourth-tier Demon Emperors did not dare set foot there lightly.

Soul-Severing Cliff was a forbidden land deep in the Demon Sea. It had been discovered by a wandering cultivator in the early years, and afterward, the Primordial Demon Sect organized a group of Sage-stage and Golden Core-stage powerhouses to go there, only for more than half to perish within. Thus, it gained the name "Soul-Severing."

If these still did not count for much, then Arctic Yemo Heaven required explanation.

This was the foremost dangerous place in the Northern Sea. With the Sunken Sea, born once every thousand years, behind it, and naturally formed geomagnetic auroras enveloping its left and right, whoever went there would end in bitter regret.

"If it is more dangerous than all these places, would you not be seeking death by going?"

"Haha, how is that seeking death? Did you not see that even Arctic Yemo Heaven was taken down by the demon race allied army?"

Luo Chen laughed despite himself. "That was the Northern Sea demon race's great powers gathering billions of demon beasts and forcefully attacking it. You and I are merely Golden Core cultivators. How can that be compared?"

Ding Yi shook his head.

"That is not how the words should be spoken. Dangerous places are born to be conquered. Wuzhong Valley was broken through by Spirit-Control Sage and became a blessed cultivation land. Arctic Yemo Heaven was transformed by the Primordial Demon Sect into a sect grotto-heaven. As for the dangerous place I want to go to, over so many years, people have also already groped out its patterns. If those people can go, then naturally I can go too."

Fine!

Looking at that firm expression on the fatty's thin-cheeked face, Luo Chen could not continue persuading him and instead directly asked where the dangerous place was.

"Deep in the Sunken Sea!"

"Mm?"

"I said, that place is deep in the Sunken Sea!" Ding Yi stared fixedly at Luo Chen, saying each word clearly.

Luo Chen was doubtful and uncertain. "Is the Sunken Sea not the Primordial Demon Sect's back garden? Is there still something inside that you are thinking of?"

"Heh. The world only knows that the Sunken Sea guarded Arctic Yemo Heaven's rear, but does not know that the danger within not only blocked enemies, it also cut off the Primordial Demon Sect itself." Ding Yi spoke with confidence and ease.

Luo Chen did not understand. "If there truly were good things inside, the Primordial Demon Sect would have taken them long ago. How would there still be your turn?"

"Did I not say it? The Sunken Sea also blocked the Primordial Demon Sect's cultivators. Moreover, much of the experience of entering and leaving the Sunken Sea was slowly groped out by Primordial Demon Sect members bit by bit. They themselves were also exploring. It was only that before they could fully develop that secret realm, their sect was already destroyed and their people dead."

Put that way, it was relatively reasonable.

Luo Chen nodded faintly, but still had doubts in his heart.

"I know the Sunken Sea is dangerous, but what exactly is in its depths that makes you so fixated?"

This time, Ding Yi became somewhat cautious and mysterious.

"The specifics, I do not know either, but at least within it there is the ruin of an ancient cultivator's sitting death."

The sitting-death ruin of an ancient cultivator?

For some reason, Luo Chen thought of White Night's traveling palace in the Nine Accumulated Thunder Mountains.

A ruin that even the Primordial Demon Sect had persisted in exploring had to have been left behind by at least an Ascendant-level great power.

The remaining treasures inside, even if only insignificant items, would be priceless treasures to Golden Core cultivators like them.

Just like Thousand-Year Thunder Essence!

Seeing Luo Chen reveal a thoughtful expression, Ding Yi did not urge him. There were many origins involved here, and he could not explain everything immediately.

Only if he confirmed Luo Chen was going would he tell everything.

But after half a day, Luo Chen still did not state his position. Thinking of how Luo Chen's words before and after had only mentioned him, Ding Yi, and not "them," Ding Yi became somewhat urgent.

"Daoist Azure Yang, are you truly not moved?"

Luo Chen stayed calm and still said nothing.

Ding Yi carefully persuaded him, "There must be heaven-shaking treasures within. Over these years, through my inquiries, I have learned that no small number of high-realm figures in the Northern Sea are actively exploring the Sunken Sea."

"Are there Sage Sages?" Luo Chen asked.

Ding Yi did not hesitate. "Naturally there are. One could even say Sage Sages are the main force!"

"In that case, where would there be leftovers for you and me to eat?"

"But there have also been Golden Core cultivators who walked out from inside!"

"Who?"

Ding Yi spat out a name.

Fairy Jadecold!

Seeing Luo Chen disbelieve him, Ding Yi quietly said, "As far as I know, Fairy Jadecold was actually only the furnace cauldron of a Primordial Demon Sect Sage elder back then. She was taken deep into the Sunken Sea, to that ancient cultivator ruin. After coming out, she broke through to the Sage stage. Only then did the Primordial Demon Sect elder later bestow Jadecold City upon her and let her escape the identity of a furnace cauldron."

That lofty, unreachable Fairy Jadecold had actually once been only a furnace cauldron?

Hearing this secret, Luo Chen was truly shocked.

And what shocked him even more was that deep in the Sunken Sea, there was an opportunity for Sage formation!

The moment he thought of this, he understood why Ding Yi insisted on going there.

He wanted to seek an opportunity for Sage formation!

Clearly, this information in his words was also something Ding Yi had deliberately revealed to Luo Chen.

After saying all this, he looked at Luo Chen with an old god's calm.

He did not believe Luo Chen would not be moved!

However, after waiting for half a day, Luo Chen's expression went from shock to anticipation, then to hesitation and uncertainty, and finally, as if he had made up his mind, he shook his head.

"Apologies. Luo was born steady, and generally does not do things he lacks confidence in. After considering that secret realm you spoke of, it is simply too dangerous. I fear it is not something I can touch."

Ding Yi's face instantly became somewhat ugly.

He had discovered that this Devil Lord Azure Yang did not have the bearing of any demonic-path powerhouse at all. He clearly acted with incomparable steadiness.

There was none of the domineering spirit from the Cold Light Island battle, where he arrogantly faced several powerhouses of the same rank alone.

In truth, Luo Chen had genuinely weighed things.

So what if there was a Sage formation opportunity?

He had already comprehended a method of Sage formation and had the Nascent Soul Pill formula in hand. So long as he refined the Nascent Soul Pill, what he lacked most was not any Sage formation opportunity at all, but merely a fourth-tier spirit land.

Under such circumstances, why take risks?

Was it for those unknown treasures?

Luo Chen had lived his life steadily. He knew of several secret realms and ruins, but whether it was Ghost God Valley's supreme elder ruin close at hand during Qi Refinement, or the Ascendant cave residence he had accidentally entered during Foundation Establishment, he had never actively explored them.

It was precisely because of this steady personality that he could walk step by step to where he was now.

He did not do things he had no confidence in!

Seeing Ding Yi's face look somewhat poor, Luo Chen comforted him, "Within the vast Myriad Immortals Assembly, there are quite a few people whose ability surpasses mine. Why must Fellow Daoist insist on me? As I see it, the Moya Cave Master, with his many magic treasures, is quite formidable."

"He is already one of the traveling companions!"

"Uh... Then others can do as well. Ghost Spirit Child's great limit is near. If he knew of such an opportunity, he would surely risk it. Angler is also quite good, the head of the three great eight-star demon hunters. His abilities are still above mine. And there is also Huo Fang, deputy city lord of Dragon Abyss..."

"Ghost Spirit Child is merely a withered bone in a grave, insufficient to scheme with. Angler is Blood Wanderer's subordinate. Even if I do not invite him, eight or nine times out of ten he will still go to the Sunken Sea. As for Huo Fang? A sect man. Ding does not trust him!"

Every person Luo Chen named was rejected by Ding Yi one by one.

Listening further, Luo Chen gradually tasted something in it.

It was not that those people would not do, but that those people were not easy to control?

By comparison, his own battle strength was strong enough, while his realm was relatively weaker. In this way, he would not seize Ding Yi's leadership.

If the other party truly had such a plan, then within the vast Myriad Immortals Assembly, it seemed he really was the most suitable traveling companion for Ding Yi.

Thinking carefully, the Moya Cave Master seemed to satisfy this condition as well.

Many magic treasures and outstanding methods, but a mere Golden Core Stage Seven realm, which would not threaten Ding Yi's leadership.

Thinking of this, Luo Chen's face no longer looked pleasant.

Even when he had been affected by the heart demon back then and taken the initiative to deal with the Yan Alliance, Luo Chen had still connected people from the middle and occupied the leading position.

The more one risked danger, the more one needed leadership. Otherwise, one would not know how one died.

Just as he planned to send the guest away.

Beside his ear, four words suddenly came.

"Five Elements Lotus Platform!"

"Mm?"

Brilliant light burst from Luo Chen's eyes, and he stared fixedly at Ding Yi.

The latter chuckled. "As far as I know, over these years, you have constantly been asking for news of the spirit medicine Five Elements Lotus Platform! Have you collected even one or two?"

Luo Chen did not answer, only staring at him.

Ding Yi shrugged. "I will not hide it from you. In that ancient cultivator ruin, there are Five Elements Lotus Platforms, and no small number of them. I do not know what you need this item for, but I know this thing is used by Sage Sages in cultivation. Only Sage Sages trade it among themselves. If you delusionally hope to collect Five Elements Lotus Platforms from low-level wandering cultivator groups, I fear you could toil for a lifetime and still not obtain two. But if you go to that place with me, as long as we encounter Five Elements Lotus Platforms, they will all belong to you."

After saying this, he blinked.

"Daoist Azure Yang, will you truly not consider it?"

This time, Luo Chen fell into a long silence.

Silence represented hesitation, and hesitation meant his heart had moved.

Ding Yi's face once again revealed a smile. He plucked a willow twig and casually waved it.

"I will be at Moya Mountain refining the secret treasure. If you finish considering it, come find me!"

"But I must explain in advance that the opening time of that secret realm is unstable. You need to make a decision early."

"In addition, the Sunken Sea itself is also dangerous. To cross this sea, one needs to prepare corresponding treasures."

"Decide earlier, and I will teach you the method of refining the treasure, lest you all drag me down at that time."

His words stretched out gradually, one by one.

In Luo Chen's vision, the fat figure had already left Peng Island, traveling farther and farther through the sky.

By the lakeside, Luo Chen stood for a very long time.

Until Heavenjade quietly approached.

"Master, Black King has returned."

*(End of Chapter)*
