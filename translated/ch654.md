# Chapter 654: The Devil Lord's Seven Loose Hands, Three Cultivators Travel North

The phenomenon at Peng Lake lasted only an instant in Ding Yi's eyes.

But he remained beside Peng Lake for a full hour.

Only after a divine sense transmission came from inside did he move.

"We leave in half a month?"

With every step, he looked back repeatedly, still staring in uncertainty at the great lake shrouded by cloud and mist, as if some terrifying existence were inside.

Inside Star Dou Hall, after a long time, Luo Chen once again opened the attribute panel.

Lifespan: 165/800

Spiritual Roots: Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, Earth

Realm: Golden Core Sixth Level 57/100, Ancient Wilderness Fourth Tier 1/100

Cultivation Methods: Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture Perfect 457/500, Ten Thousand Daos Converge Great Perfection, Myriad Beasts Scripture Grandmaster 500/1000

Spells: Fourth Tier: Dragon-Slaying Art Great Perfection, Origin Pill Art Great Perfection, All-Encompassing Fire Prison Perfect 317/500

Third Tier: Blazing Heaven Sevenfold Ring Great Perfection, Life-Severing Finger Beginner 15/100

Physical Arts: Voidburst Step Great Perfection, Devil Lord's Seven Loose Hands: Cloud-Probing Divine Claw Great Perfection, Overlord Removes Armor Proficient 201/300, Thunder Kick Perfect 350/500, Mountain-Suppressing Form Grandmaster 510/1000, Mountain-Pulling Form Perfect 460/500, Mountain-Breaking Form Grandmaster 501/1000, Mountain-Colliding Form Perfect 390/500

Achievement Points: 334

Looking at the attribute panel, Luo Chen revealed a satisfied smile.

These were the gains of nearly thirty years since he came to the Myriad Immortals Assembly.

And this was only in cultivation. If he opened panels such as alchemist, artifact forger, and formation master, his gains would be even more terrifying.

After only one glance, Luo Chen's smile grew even brighter, and the corners of his mouth nearly reached his ears.

Eight hundred.

Eight hundred years of lifespan.

In legend, even a Sage had only one thousand years of life.

Yet Luo Chen, while merely at the Golden Core stage, could possess a terrifying lifespan of eight hundred years.

Among all cultivators of this age, who else but him?

Such a terrifying change in lifespan clearly came from the body-refinement realm he had just advanced to.

Ancient Wilderness Fourth Tier, even without the innate racial bonuses of orthodox desolate beasts, had still increased his lifespan limit by a full three hundred years.

Even though he had expected it, after seeing the result, Luo Chen still grinned with joy.

"That massive investment was worth it after all!"

On the path of body refinement, Luo Chen had spent countless resources, not necessarily less than on qi refinement.

The Black Emperor Paste alone contained at least over one thousand second-tier demon crabs and a dozen third-tier Crab Kings.

If one added the tens of thousands of low-rank demon beasts he had burned-refined and devoured with origin true fire over these years, the spirit stones consumed by these two items alone would total over one hundred million after conversion.

Terrifying resource investment had created the current monster.

A three-hundred-year lifespan increase was only natural.

The only thing Luo Chen found a pity was probably the improvement of his qi-refinement realm.

In recent years, to break through to Ancient Wilderness Fourth Tier, he had no choice but to slow his qi-refinement cultivation, causing a realm that might originally have advanced to become stuck halfway through the sixth level of Golden Core.

Of course, body refinement could not be entirely blamed for consuming time.

Over these years, he had forged artifacts on a grand scale, gone out seeking treasure, and frequently deployed the All-Encompassing Fire Prison, which consumed enormous mana. All of these seriously delayed his qi-refinement cultivation.

It could only be said that Luo Chen might be able to multitask, but he could not split one person into two bodies and attend to everything.

Of course, the regret was only a little.

In Luo Chen's view, cultivation had tens of thousands of paths, and in the end, all roads returned to the same goal: seeking longevity.

So-called longevity meant lifespan.

Now body refinement had taken the first step and truly granted him an increase in lifespan. What was there to regret?

Especially since there was also an increase in strength.

Among fourth-tier spells, Dragon-Slaying Art and Origin Pill Art had both reached Great Perfection. The All-Encompassing Fire Prison had also stepped into the Perfect level on the Sunken Sea demon beast battlefield and could be released as he wished.

As for physical arts, Luo Chen gathered together the many scattered incomplete killing moves he had obtained over these years and gave them the name Devil Lord's Seven Loose Hands.

The reason they were Seven Loose Hands was naturally because there were only seven moves.

Cloud-Probing Divine Claw appeared and vanished unpredictably.

Overlord Removes Armor had complicated forms. When fused together, they could still be completed in one breath and strip away an enemy's defenses.

Thunder Kick was a leg technique, swift as rushing thunder. If combined with the Great Perfection Voidburst Step, its power was even greater. Han Zhan had once said this move could not hit a Sage, but now that Luo Chen was already Ancient Wilderness Fourth Tier, he wanted to know whether any Sage dared take this move head-on.

Mountain-Suppressing, Mountain-Pulling, Mountain-Breaking, and Mountain-Colliding came from the Extreme Mountain Six Forms. But now, when Luo Chen displayed them, they were similar yet different, and their might exceeded the descriptions in the records. The reason was that Luo Chen had combined his own circumstances with some low-rank moves he had once excelled in and made corresponding improvements.

Of course, if he merely collected them together and called it a self-created cultivation method or battle technique, that would indeed be somewhat shameless.

The good thing was that in these final years, for the sake of body refinement, Luo Chen had crazily practiced these physical killing moves, causing the proficiency of the Seven Loose Hands to reach a considerable level.

At a glance, aside from the most complicated Overlord Removes Armor being at Proficient level, the others were either Perfect or Grandmaster.

One had to know that the system's evaluations were very strict.

Grandmaster level on the panel, if placed outside, might be what others called great accomplishment.

Even beginner levels such as Skilled and Proficient were often evaluated outside as minor accomplishment or entering the hall.

As for Great Perfection, that was at least the level of reaching the peak and creating something new.

"Relying on these Devil Lord's Seven Loose Hands, I may be able to maximize the power of this current tyrannical body."

Luo Chen murmured, his eyes carrying a trace of ambition.

Previously, after transforming into the Heavenly Peng true body, his attack methods were too direct and too lacking.

In the past, he could still carry his lifebound magic treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, and smash things with it. But now the Primordial Unity Cauldron had to assist the Golden Core in deploying the All-Encompassing Fire Prison, so it could not be used lightly.

He had also once considered using the Hundred Craft Mountain left behind by Mountain Lord Hundred Craft as an attacking weapon.

But Hundred Craft Mountain was actually the mountain lord's lifebound magic treasure. After its master's death, its might was damaged, and Luo Chen had not had time to refine it over these years, so he could not display one hundred percent of its power.

By comparison, these scattered killing moves he had collected and organized, once gathered together, were more reliable and easier to use.

If he had opportunities in the future to collect more powerful killing moves, perhaps the Devil Lord's Seven Loose Hands would become Eight Loose Hands, Twelve Loose Hands, Twenty-Three Loose Hands, and so on.

In short, the future was promising.

Finally, Luo Chen's gaze landed on the Achievement Points column.

334.

It was a number that made one somewhat numb.

He had not used achievement points in a very long time.

Yet over these years, he had never stopped alchemy, artifact forging, practicing formations, and learning all kinds of killing moves. Over time, it had accumulated to more than three hundred.

He remembered clearly that the last time he checked the panel, it had only been one hundred fifty points.

In the blink of an eye, it had unknowingly accumulated to more than three hundred.

Apart from surprise, it brought him even more security.

In the future, if he encountered high-level pill formulas, cultivation methods, or spells that he could not understand through research, these achievement points would be his universal key to force entry.

Even some incomplete cultivation methods had the chance to be deduced and completed through achievement points.

For example, the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture.

This peerless cultivation method, said to lead directly to the Mahayana stage, had only reached Luo Chen in its Golden Core chapter.

Even the Golden Core chapter was broad, profound, and wondrous.

It changed spiritual root attributes, purified mana, greatly increased control over flames, and so on.

At present, the Golden Core chapter was absolutely enough. In Luo Chen's view, with the scripture's wondrous nature, it was more than enough even for his Sage-stage cultivation.

But Luo Chen had not reached that realm after all and did not know how Sage-stage cultivation differed.

Therefore, the incomplete Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture became a future worry in Luo Chen's cultivation.

He had originally wanted to cultivate this method to Great Perfection and see whether the system would automatically deduce and complete it, just as the Yi Wood Medicine King Scripture had automatically been completed into the Everlasting Evergreen Scripture after reaching perfection.

However, this method's proficiency rose too slowly. After decades, it was only at Perfect level and had not yet reached Grandmaster.

And this was only a backup.

The Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture's grade was too high. If the system could not automatically deduce the follow-up, he would have to consider whether to spend massive achievement points to forcefully complete it.

As for switching cultivation methods after Sage stage?

Luo Chen had considered this, but in the end, he shelved the plan.

Cultivators frequently switching their main cultivation method caused great harm to the spiritual roots. It was best to remain consistent from beginning to end.

So-called spiritual roots were faint and untraceable, yet truly hidden within the cultivator's body.

Specifically speaking, they were not some definite object, but a cultivator's affinity toward a certain heaven and earth spiritual qi.

Why did those with fewer spiritual roots cultivate faster?

The reason was that the fewer spiritual roots one had, the higher the affinity toward the corresponding attribute of heaven and earth spiritual qi.

The more spiritual roots one had, the more they split and affected affinity toward any single attribute's spiritual qi, causing absorption efficiency to greatly decline.

When a cultivator cultivated a certain attribute's method for many years, his body and spiritual roots would become more and more compatible with the corresponding attribute's spiritual qi.

Once he switched methods, that compatibility would be broken and injure himself.

If the methods were of the same type and attribute, the harm would decrease, but there would still be an effect. After all, his body had already grown accustomed to that meridian circulation route over decades or centuries of cultivation.

Luo Chen possessed five spiritual roots. Switching between the five attributes seemed possible.

But the higher his realm, the less he dared switch lightly.

After all, his five spiritual roots did not have high affinity toward heaven and earth spiritual qi.

Unless he could achieve the "Primal Embryo Dao Body" from a certain madman's idea back then.

With that mythical physique, forget switching cultivation methods, one could absorb heaven and earth origin qi to cultivate even within the turbulent void. It truly made one yearn.

Shaking his head, Luo Chen's mood gradually cooled from his previous wild joy.

"Now that everything is ready, it is time to set out."

"Those two must have been waiting anxiously for a long time."

Luo Chen laughed softly. The restless feeling of power throughout his body currently gave him confidence to look down on everything.

Ding Yi? Cave Master Moyun?

Heh.

Half a month later, beside misty Peng Lake, two figures stood on the shore, quietly waiting for something.

Suddenly, a clear breeze arrived.

A red-robed Daoist, carrying a sword case on his back, stood on the spine of a jiao-python and crossed the lake.

When he reached the lakeshore, the Daoist leapt down, held a spirit beast pouch, and beckoned to the enormous jiao-python.

"Go in."

Black King mumbled unwillingly, shrank his body, and flew inside.

Hanging the spirit beast pouch at his waist, Luo Chen saluted the two.

"Fellow Daoist Ding, Cave Master, I have made you wait."

They had indeed waited a long time.

The Sunken Sea phenomenon had been born for over a year. Those who should go had long since gone. The three of them had planned for years, yet had been unable to set out. All of this was thanks to Luo Chen.

Previously, Ding Yi had long been full of complaints and had even nearly torn off all pretense by trying to force his way into Peng Lake.

However, now that he truly saw Luo Chen, Ding Yi only snorted coldly and said nothing more.

His gaze as he sized up Luo Chen was full of dread.

That terrifying aura from that brief instant back then could not be false.

Now, looking carefully, there seemed to be nothing unusual. Even Luo Chen's realm did not seem to have broken through to late Golden Core.

Then what exactly had that terrifying aura been?

Some killing move? Or was it caused by the Peng Lake grand formation? It could not be that he had deliberately frightened Ding Yi, right?

"Hm? Devil Lord, your face?"

Cave Master Moyun was also sizing up Luo Chen. A quick sweep gave him no feeling, yet he felt he had missed something.

When he looked carefully again, he finally discovered that the crimson skin on the other party's face had vanished.

In its place was white skin like jade, like a baby's congealed fat.

Ding Yi also abruptly realized it. He had focused only on trusting his perception and divine sense, neglecting what his eyes saw.

Devil Lord Azure Yang's face.

His skin was like congealed fat, his face like crowned jade, and his pitch-black eyes were deep like a cold abyss, able to reflect the filth in the depths of a person's heart.

Especially set against the great red Daoist robe, that skin color appeared even more striking.

Before the lake, morning mist spread. The Daoist carried immortal grace, like an exiled immortal.

Luo Chen smiled freely and waved his hand.

"Only a slight change in complexion. For people like us, why care about appearance? Let us go. Luo is also impatient."

After speaking, he passed the two and headed north.

Ding Yi and Cave Master Moyun looked at each other. Looking at Luo Chen's back, which even seemed a few degrees taller, they realized that some unknown change must have occurred in him.

But with departure imminent, they could not spare attention to guess and hurriedly followed his steps.

Two hours after they left, the sky brightened and the rising sun appeared.

Warm light scattered across the clear lake surface, reflecting the eight hundred li of Peng Lake into breathtaking beauty. When early-rising cultivators rowed past and raised ripples, it showed even more of an immortal family's calm and peace.

No one realized that the Billowing Cloud Dense Mist that had once covered the eight hundred li of Peng Lake had scattered without trace today.

Nor did anyone discover that the lord of Peng Lake, Daoist Azure Yang, had quietly left.
