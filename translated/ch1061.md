# Chapter 1061: The Demons Pay Court, Secrets of the Fifth Order

*Whoosh!*

A streak of blood light shot across the sky, moving at an incredible speed.

Beneath it lay verdant green mountains, stretching endlessly without bound.

Suddenly, the blood-colored flying light dropped sharply in speed, its gaze fixed upon a great mountain ahead.

A thunderous rumbling sound rang out as that mountain split cleanly in two. A burly man with a pair of horns atop his head strode forth, looked up at the sky, and met the gaze of the man in the blood light.

"Blood Vulture Daoist, long time no see."

Blood Vulture Daoist clicked his tongue. "Shake Mountain Que, you're still this irritable. That mountain didn't do anything to you."

"The mountain didn't offend me, but *you* have angered me." Shake Mountain Que's eyes blazed with murderous intent as he stared fixedly at Blood Vulture Daoist. "Back when I had yet to attain the Dao, you ambushed me and gnawed on my flesh and blood. I haven't forgotten that grudge one bit."

Before his words had even faded, his thick arms swept inward from both sides. Two enormous phantom palms materialized in the void, slapping toward Blood Vulture Daoist.

The might of those clapping palms seemed capable of crushing everything in their path.

Yet Blood Vulture Daoist merely raised an eyebrow, allowing the palms to strike his body.

*Bang!*

Blood light exploded outward. After the palms vanished, the blood light suddenly coalesced again, and Blood Vulture Daoist emerged completely unharmed.

Seeing that Shake Mountain Que was about to attack again, Blood Vulture Daoist waved his hand helplessly. "Stop, stop, stop. Back then, neither of us had taken form or attained the Dao. Our spiritual wisdom was blocked, and we were merely acting on instinct. Why hold a grudge for so long?"

Shake Mountain Que ignored him. He reached out, and a black-jade mixed-iron staff fell into his hand.

Blood Vulture Daoist's gaze sharpened. That staff was actually a true artifact!

He took a deep breath. "You stubborn ox, you won't listen to reason no matter what I say. This is an order sent by the Truth Crossing Hall of Green Parasol Mountain! If you delay any longer, do you want to be punished by the Sovereign?"

Green Parasol Mountain! Truth Crossing Hall!

The moment those two names were uttered, Shake Mountain Que finally stopped.

He shot a vicious glare at Blood Vulture Daoist, then turned and left.

"Don't follow me, or I'll devour you alive!"

Blood Vulture Daoist curled his lip, too lazy to engage in a battle of pride with the other. His flying light rose, and he continued flying toward Green Parasol Mountain.

Below, Shake Mountain Que walked with heavy, deliberate steps. Each stride carried him ten li, and neither mountain nor earth could hinder him in the slightest.

This technique of "shrinking the earth into inches" was an extremely advanced form of earth escape art.

Even among the human cultivator world, few could cultivate earth escape techniques to such a level. Yet Shake Mountain Que executed it repeatedly, his mana flowing smoothly without any stagnation.

Because this technique was the innate demon art of the Shake Earth Divine Ox clan.

Step by step, he advanced, drawing closer and closer to Green Parasol Mountain.

And in the process, his divine sense perceived more and more powerful auras in the vicinity.

Some were weaker than him, but there were also those far stronger!

"Is Green Parasol Mountain planning to attack the human sects of the Eastern Wilderness again?" Shake Mountain Que muttered to himself.

Beneath the nine heavens, the closer he got to Green Parasol Mountain, the more Blood Vulture Daoist gradually lowered his flying altitude.

Thus, the scenery for thousands of li around unfolded before his eyes.

The flickering figure of Shake Mountain Que was naturally one of them.

But elsewhere, other top experts from the Million Mountains were also showing themselves.

To the east, eight apes carried a sedan chair, their aura ethereal and immortal-like. That should be White-Browed Immortal Ape, the old fellow who occupied Immortal Ape Peak.

Behind the eight-ape sedan, two streaks of green light traveled together, drifting in from afar, accompanied by wafts of fragrant breeze that seeped into the heart. Those must be the Green Grain Twin Beauties.

On a mighty river in the distance, waves churned as a spirited great jiaolong swam upstream against the current. Low-level demon beasts fled in all directions. The Crimson Demon Jiao had come too!

In just a short moment, his divine sense had captured a full five Nascent Soul demon cultivators!

Further away, black clouds filled the sky as demonic aura rolled in.

Another one!

Blood Vulture Daoist's heart tightened involuntarily.

"What exactly is Green Parasol Mountain planning, summoning so many top experts at once? Are they attacking the human sects of the Eastern Wilderness? But this time, they haven't gathered enough low-level demon beasts!"

Lost in his confusion, Blood Vulture Daoist's escape speed was extremely fast, and he arrived at Green Parasol Mountain first.

He descended in altitude as a sign of respect.

Blood Vulture Daoist withdrew his flying light and landed on the empty plaza outside the Truth Crossing Hall. Someone was already waiting there.

A white bone dharma robe, a handsome face with an eerie temperament.

"White Bone! Long time no see!"

White Bone Mystic Serpent narrowed his eyes. "I knew you'd be the first to arrive. Wherever war breaks out, you bloodthirsty vulture are always the first to sense it."

Blood Vulture Daoist laughed heartily and didn't deny it.

"Go on in. We've already arranged lodgings for you." White Bone Mystic Serpent turned and walked inside, and Blood Vulture Daoist quickly followed.

Their relationship was quite good among the demon clan's powerful figures.

This had to do with the habits of their original forms before transformation.

One was a bloodthirsty vulture, craving flesh and blood. The other was a white bone mystic serpent, feeding on bones.

There was no conflict of food sources between them, nor were they natural enemies. They had even fought side by side before, with excellent results.

After catching up, Blood Vulture Daoist asked curiously, "What important matter does the Sovereign have in summoning us here?"

White Bone Mystic Serpent shot him a glance. "It wasn't the Sovereign who called you here, but the Truth Crossing Hall Master. Didn't you read the order carefully?"

Blood Vulture Daoist was startled. Of course, he had read the order brought by Green Parasol Mountain's messenger birds carefully.

The signature at the bottom was [Truth Crossing Hall Master].

But hadn't the Truth Crossing Hall Master always been Nether Spring?

And Nether Spring represented none other than Rosycloud Sovereign!

But soon, he realized his mistake. The former Nether Spring had only been the Vice Hall Master of Truth Crossing Hall and had never used the title [Truth Crossing Hall Master].

Blood Vulture Daoist licked his sharp, protruding lips. "Daoist White Bone, may I ask who the current Truth Crossing Hall Master is?"

In response to this question, White Bone Mystic Serpent snorted coldly, as if greatly displeased.

"You'll know when the time comes!" He stopped walking and pointed at an ordinary-looking side hall. "Alright, you'll stay here for the next few days."

Blood Vulture Daoist blocked White Bone Mystic Serpent, who was about to leave. "It's been a long time since we last met. Now that we've rarely encountered each other, I've brewed a pot of blood wine. Won't you have a cup?"

Blood wine!

White Bone Mystic Serpent pursed his lips, seemingly quite tempted.

But in the end, he shook his head. "Don't try to pry information about that person from me. You'll know when you see him. I still have to go greet the other fellow daoists."

Blood Vulture Daoist didn't insist. His eyes darted around, and instead of entering the hall to rest, he followed behind White Bone Mystic Serpent, keeping a distant tail.

From afar, he watched as demon clan experts arrived at Green Parasol Mountain one by one.

The ones responsible for the reception were not just White Bone Mystic Serpent alone. There were also two women.

One had a seductive temperament and a fiery figure. Blood Vulture Daoist recognized her as Pure Crane. Rumour had it that she had once betrayed the demon clan and defected to a human sect, only to be later captured and brought back by Daytime Void Hall Master Azure Frost.

The other had features like a painting, with a gentle, tranquil disposition.

This one, he didn't quite recognize.

But whoever she received, the demon clan experts all wore smiles on their faces, and their words even carried a hint of eagerness to please.

Even old fellows like White-Browed Immortal Ape were deliberately currying favour with that beautiful woman.

Seizing an opportunity, Blood Vulture Daoist stopped White-Browed Immortal Ape and asked about the woman's background.

"You mean Painting-Brow Fairy! In the Million Mountains today, who doesn't know her?" White-Browed Immortal Ape looked at Blood Vulture Daoist with a puzzled expression, but soon he chuckled. "Ah, right. You, Blood Vulture, are a loner. It's only natural that you haven't received her favour before."

Blood Vulture Daoist was still confused.

"Old White-Brow, explain in detail."

White-Browed Immortal Ape smiled kindly. "Although Painting-Brow Fairy's cultivation isn't high—she only formed her Nascent Soul in the last two years—her status in Green Parasol Mountain, and even in the Million Mountains, is very high. Because she possesses an extremely refined alchemy skill, and she can even refine pills that allow our younger generations to transform early, helping them embark on the path of orthodox cultivation ahead of time."

Alchemy!

Hearing this, Blood Vulture Daoist's eyes lit up.

For the demon clan, unless they had a racial advantage, it was very difficult to master the hundred arts of human cultivation.

That gentle, tranquil female demon was actually a powerful alchemist!

No wonder her status was so exalted!

That old fellow White-Browed Immortal Ape had a vast number of monkey descendants under his command, and this clan was quite intelligent, but they were limited by their ape physique. If they truly obtained the help of Painting-Brow Fairy's Transformation Pills, they could break free from the limitations of their original form and develop rapidly.

Thinking back to the eight young apes who had carried his sedan chair earlier, now transformed into human forms and following behind him, it was clear they had already received Painting-Brow Fairy's blessing. No wonder Old White-Brow was so kind and benevolent toward Painting-Brow Fairy, whose cultivation and strength were far inferior to his own.

Blood Vulture Daoist sighed with emotion. "As expected of Rosycloud Sovereign. She can even cultivate such a skilled alchemist."

Unexpectedly, White-Browed Immortal Ape stroked his beard and smiled.

"Blood Vulture, you've got it wrong this time. Painting-Brow Fairy's teacher is not the Sovereign. Her esteemed master is the Truth Crossing Hall Master!"

"Ah!?"

Blood Vulture Daoist was even more astonished this time.

Green Parasol Mountain's reputation had been circulating in the Million Mountains for over a thousand years.

But to be honest, over that millennium, its momentum had always been suppressed by the Demon Court established by Ancient Demon Emperor Heaven.

It wasn't until Ancient Demon Emperor Heaven fell, and Rosycloud Sovereign personally acted to annihilate the Nether Origin Sect, that Green Parasol Mountain finally rose to prominence.

And it was at this point that a large number of top-tier demon clan experts came to pledge their allegiance.

It was also at this time that the many powerful figures of the demon clan discovered just how different and extraordinary Green Parasol Mountain was compared to ordinary demon clan factions.

The demon cultivators on the mountain, whether in terms of strength, cultivation techniques, or even combat methods like magical artifacts and demon arts, far surpassed their peers of the same rank in the Million Mountains.

Even when compared to the true disciples of the human holy lands, they were in no way inferior.

But Blood Vulture Daoist had never imagined that Green Parasol Mountain also possessed such a powerful figure in the Dao of alchemy.

"I wonder just what kind of existence the Truth Crossing Hall Master is?" he murmured, a hint of longing in his eyes.

...

The first batch of Nascent Soul demon cultivators to arrive at Green Parasol Mountain numbered seven.

They were all relatively close to Green Parasol Mountain, so they had rushed over immediately upon receiving the order.

Then, over the course of half a year, Green Parasol Mountain welcomed more than a dozen more Nascent Soul demon cultivators one after another.

But throughout this process, that mysterious Truth Crossing Hall Master never showed his face. Even information about him was kept tightly under wraps by the demon cultivators of Truth Crossing Hall.

Some didn't dare to speak, and some didn't want to.

And so, the Truth Crossing Hall Master remained hidden behind the scenes.

Until another half year passed, and three great Demon Emperors descended upon Green Parasol Mountain together.

Only then did the demon cultivators finally lose their patience!

To make so many powerful figures wait idly—wasn't that Truth Crossing Hall Master far too arrogant?

Deep within the Truth Crossing Hall, in the most central cave dwelling, the great doors were tightly shut.

Luo Chen sat cross-legged within, surrounded by dense spiritual energy that surged toward his body.

His mana was also growing rapidly.

Ever since he had comprehended the Water Origin a hundred years ago and his cultivation had skyrocketed to the ninth layer of the Nascent Soul stage due to an unexpected incident, Luo Chen's cultivation had not changed much.

Firstly, because his travels through the azure sky to capture celestial qi had left him with insufficient time for cultivation. Secondly, refining the Crimson celestial sword qi consumed a great deal of mana. But most importantly, his cultivation had soared too quickly back then. He dared not pursue further progress, so he had been suppressing it all along, tempering his surging mana.

Now, a hundred years had passed, and the ninth layer of the Nascent Soul stage had long been firmly stabilized.

Having now obtained the assistance of a fifth-order spirit vein, his cultivation had once again begun to rise at a visible rate.

But Luo Chen actually didn't care much about this increase in mana. He was concerned about something else.

A fifth-order spirit vein would naturally attract spiritual energy!

This was something Luo Chen had known before.

But what Luo Chen didn't know was just how vast the amount of spiritual energy attracted by a fifth-order spirit vein was!

Immersed within it, whether it was refining mana, circulating thoughts, or even comprehending the true meaning of laws, everything was many times faster than outside.

Spiritual energy was the best auxiliary resource for cultivators to comprehend the true meaning of laws and enter a state of enlightenment. Even advancing to the Ascendant stage required a large amount of spiritual energy assistance.

It existed everywhere, yet was difficult to find. Its quantity in heaven and earth was pitifully scarce.

Only a fifth-order spirit vein, with spiritual energy dense to the extreme, could naturally attract a large amount of spiritual energy to linger for a long time.

The moment he entered this Truth Crossing cave dwelling, Luo Chen, who possessed the Crimson Eye Golden Pupil, understood why a fifth-order spirit vein was necessary to advance to the Ascendant stage.

Such an abundance of spiritual energy was enough for a Nascent Soul Sage to take that final step!

For an entire year, Luo Chen lived within, setting aside all distracting thoughts and entering a state of intense cultivation.

Not only did his mana increase, but he even entered a state of enlightenment once.

That time, he felt surging golden flames all around him, a feeling of life and death intertwined, of reincarnation and rebirth, filling his heart.

Although he ultimately didn't comprehend anything from it, after exiting the state of enlightenment, his control over his own Nirvana Sacred Flame had greatly improved.

Luo Chen speculated that if he had succeeded that time, what he might have comprehended would have been the "Nirvana True Intent" within the Law of Fire.

Just as he had once used the Withering-Bloom True Flame to comprehend the Withering-Bloom True Intent, now, with the Nirvana Sacred Flame at his side and the enlightenment aided by spiritual insight, comprehending the Nirvana True Intent was only natural.

Unfortunately, this law's true intent seemed too powerful and too high-level, causing Luo Chen to fail at the last moment.

"The Great Dao has three thousand paths, and laws are countless!"

"Each law contains different true intents."

"From the most basic origin power, to the changes in true intents of the same attribute but different natures—if one could ultimately comprehend them all, would that represent true attainment of the Dao?"

Luo Chen was very certain that the Nirvana True Intent and the Fire Origin were of the same attribute, both belonging to the Law of Fire. It was just that one was high-level and the other was basic.

Within the Law of Wood, the relationship was the same as between the Withering-Bloom True Intent and the Wood Origin.

And thus, the above conjecture arose in his mind.

Suddenly, his thoughts stirred, and he reached out.

A transmission talisman flew in from outside the cave dwelling and ignited spontaneously, revealing Painting-Brow's gentle voice.

"Teacher, all the demon cultivators on the roster have arrived."

Luo Chen came to his senses. He extinguished the transmission talisman, changed into the Hundred-Feather Robe that represented the Truth Crossing Hall Master, and stepped out of the Truth Crossing cave dwelling.

As soon as he emerged, he saw Painting-Brow Fairy standing nearby, looking at him with gentle, adoring eyes.

"You've worked hard."

"It's no trouble. Painting-Brow has benefited greatly from Teacher's instruction in alchemy. Doing a few small things for Teacher is nothing."

Regarding this cheap disciple, Luo Chen smiled and shook his head. "Where are they now?"

Painting-Brow Fairy hesitated for a moment, then quietly explained the situation with those demon cultivators to Luo Chen.

After listening, Luo Chen's sword-like brows lifted slightly.

"Dissatisfied with me?"

With a light chuckle, the man in the feathered robe swept his wide sleeves and walked calmly toward the Truth Crossing Hall.

(Chapter End)
