# Chapter 995: Leaving Green Parasol Mountain, Seeing Nine Spirits Again

Along the way, Luo Chen had made countless enemies.

But as his strength increased, most of his enemies had been slain by his own hand, with a few still alive simply because he hadn't encountered them yet.

Among these were the two great demon emperors, Bull Demon and Bi Zheng!

Back then, they had joined forces with Huang Man to deal with Luo Chen, once forcing him into a perilous situation. He had even been invited back to Green Parasol Mountain by Azure Frost and compelled to hand over the formula for the Dao Union Pill.

This grudge, Luo Chen had always remembered.

Who would have thought that fate played such tricks—they were now under Luo Chen's management?

"I wonder how those two would react if they learned of this news?"

Luo Chen chuckled softly, his eyes glinting with a dangerous light.

But in an instant, a hint of confusion arose in Luo Chen.

Wild Frenzy and Bull Demon were one thing—these two demons had previously served under the ancient Demon Emperor Di Tian. With Di Tian's fall, their submission to Green Parasol Mountain was not surprising.

But Bi Zheng?

"As I recall, wasn't he close to the Wolf Clan's sovereign, Aokuang? Yet he also joined Green Parasol Mountain's side? Because his true form carries a trace of the ancient fierce bird, Bifang's bloodline, so he submitted to the feathered clan's sacred ground?"

Luo Chen shook his head, too lazy to ponder the changes in the internal power dynamics of the demon race.

His gaze swept quickly across the light screen, committing to memory the names of some familiar or unfamiliar Sage-level demon cultivators.

Thunder Prison Divine Peng, Pure Crane, White Heron King, Blood Vulture Daoist... Night Star Pupil, Shake Mountain Que, White-Browed Immortal Ape.

All together, there were twenty-five Sage-level demon cultivators!

Among them, a few familiar names were permanent residents on Green Parasol Mountain. The rest, Luo Chen had heard of more or less during the nearly two-hundred-year-long human-demon war in the Eastern Wilderness.

They had originally been lords of their own territories, each with their own domains.

Previously, they had been vassals under the ancient Demon Emperor Di Tian, but with Di Tian's fall, they had followed Bull Demon, Wild Frenzy, and other great demon emperors in submitting to the Green Parasol Mountain sacred ground.

However, Rosycloud probably looked down on these vassals. The fact that she had faced the sacred grounds twice alone was proof enough.

Now, these Sage-level demon cultivators, who had once been shouting for blood and battle, were all under Luo Chen's control!

For a moment, he felt a sense of absurdity.

"Even I find it absurd. If they found out, who knows what kind of uproar they'd cause."

Luo Chen suppressed the thought of summoning these demon cultivators to announce his existence, and even put away the demon cultivator roster deep into his storage ring.

Rosycloud had given him the position of Truth Crossing Hall Master, and he could not refuse it.

But the authority of the Truth Crossing Hall Master was something Luo Chen dared not display before others unless absolutely necessary.

Whether it was the demon race or the Eastern Wilderness humans, his position was too awkward.

By the same token, that [Ceasefire Treaty] was not something his current prestige could allow him to sign on behalf of the Eastern Wilderness humans.

"Keep them all as bargaining chips for when the time comes!"

Luo Chen sighed and carefully put away the scroll as well.

Finally, Luo Chen's attention fell on the glazed fire fan that Rosycloud had bestowed upon him.

"Blood Jade Three-Flame Fan, pseudo-spirit treasure grade."

Muttering the name, he stroked the warm glazed fan handle and carefully extended his mana.

The moment they merged, there was a brief resistance, but the mana quickly and easily poured in.

Nether Spring had not deceived him.

During the refining of this fan, a trace of fire origin had indeed been incorporated.

Was it Rosycloud's?

Luo Chen stroked the fire fan, his eyelids drooping low, lost in thought.

...

A few days later.

Three figures walked out of the Truth Crossing Hall, boarded an ordinary magical treasure flying boat, circled briefly, and headed west, disappearing into the sky in an instant.

On a peak of Green Parasol Mountain, where the Daytime Void Hall stood.

Two gazes followed the distant shadow of the flying boat, the atmosphere somewhat oppressive.

Finally, Nether Spring could not hold back.

"Azure Frost, are you really letting him leave so easily?"

Azure Frost's expression was cold, her voice crisp. "We cannot keep him."

Nether Spring frowned. "But you share life and death with him. If he roams outside and unfortunately falls, what then? In my opinion, it would be better to forcibly keep him on Green Parasol Mountain and help him break through to Ascendant. Then you can dissolve the life-and-death gu. That way, you'll have no more worries."

Azure Frost shook her head but did not explain the specific reason.

Luo Chen staying on Green Parasol Mountain would make it impossible for him to break through to the Ascendant realm.

"Hmph!"

Nether Spring snorted coldly, shifting her gaze upward to the majestic palace hanging high in the nine heavens.

"How is the Lady's body?"

There was a hint of genuine concern in her words.

Azure Frost's expression dimmed, but she quickly rallied. "Don't worry. With Sister's astonishing abilities, no one in the Mountains and Seas Realm can threaten her."

Was there truly no one who could threaten Rosycloud?

Whether it was the Soul-Sealing Grand Formation of the Nether Origin Sect's Soul-Refining Ancestor in the Northern Sea, or the heavenly tribulation summoned by the Nether Abyss Sect's Abyss Ancestor sacrificing everything before his death, both had brought great trouble to Rosycloud.

Especially the battle with the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, where Ascendants from the upper realm descended like rain, and the Heavenly Origin Five Elders awakened a Far Ancient desolate beast at the Body Integration stage, nearly causing Rosycloud to be buried in the Yellow Spring Sea.

After a series of fierce battles, the Lady's current condition was probably not as good as imagined.

Nether Spring was deeply worried, and even the expression on her face could not be concealed.

Azure Frost took a deep breath. "The next step is up to me!"

Nether Spring was startled, vaguely sensing something, but still couldn't help asking:

"What are you going to do?"

"This realm suppresses Sister too much. To have no obstacles, the only way is to ascend to the upper realm. And Sister is not a native Ascendant, so she cannot trigger the spiritual mechanism to ascend. Therefore... Ascendant, I must become an Ascendant!"

"But you have cultivated for too short a time, your heart-mind is insufficient. How will you condense your primordial spirit?"

At this, Azure Frost pursed her lips.

"It doesn't matter. As long as I refine the Accumulated Void Flower, everything will fall into place."

Will it really work?

Nether Spring was half-convinced.

But seeing Azure Frost's resolute expression, she found it hard to dissuade her further. Perhaps the Lady had given her some secret technique before going into seclusion.

She simply turned her gaze back to the distance.

For some reason, compared to Azure Frost, she had more faith in Luo Chen.

That kid's cultivation progress was simply too fast!

...

High in the sky, a streak of light flew from east to west, unhurriedly.

A figure stood at the bow of the boat, his gaze like a torch looking down at the familiar yet unfamiliar scenery below.

Once, these scenes had been familiar to Luo Chen.

But under the influence of successive great battles, rivers had changed course, mountains had collapsed and risen again, and with the passage of time, they had gradually become strange.

The so-called vicissitudes of life were nothing more than this.

Thinking of the intelligence Nether Spring had sent, and the description of the current state of the Central Continent, Luo Chen couldn't help but feel a pang of melancholy.

The destructive power of high-level cultivators on this world was simply too great!

The collapse of a single city or region was commonplace.

When battles reached their fiercest, even shattering a great domain was not surprising.

Especially when Ascendant-level powerhouses, without any restraint, caused continents to sink and the sky to crack—that was the ultimate terror.

If that level of war happened a few more times, even this realm, with its abundant spiritual mechanisms and dense spiritual energy, would not be able to withstand such wanton destruction and abuse, and would eventually become unsuitable for cultivation!

Of course, this melancholy and sighing were just a sudden whim.

At least for now, it did not affect Luo Chen.

His gaze fell on a certain place, and his mind stirred.

"Wait for me here for a moment. I will go down to see an old acquaintance."

"As the Hall Master wishes!"

Two voices came from within the boat, and then the flying boat stopped in midair, two gazes filled with complex meaning watching Luo Chen descend below.

...

Joyous Union Domain!

The domain where the Joyous Union Sect, one of the six great sects of the extreme east, had once been located.

In the early years, due to the invasion of the demon emperor, it had been battered and broken.

But as two hundred years passed in the blink of an eye, even the greatest wounds gradually healed.

Especially with the establishment of the Sage-level Nine Spirits Sect, under the deployment and nurturing of the Nine Spirits Sect's cultivators, this domain gradually showed signs of vitality.

Although it could not yet compare to the prosperity of the Joyous Union Sect's heyday, it still had a sense of a hundred things waiting to be revived, full of vitality.

The once-shattered main peak of the Joyous Union Sect had also been restored to stand in the world through the great means of the Nine Spirits Sage, who had channeled earth veins and dredged spiritual energy. It had become one of the symbols of the Nine Spirits Sect, replaced by the new name, Nine Origin Peak.

Today, the Nine Origin Peak, shrouded in clouds and mist all year round, seemed somewhat different.

Amidst the surging clouds, a figure slowly entered.

Halfway there, a low, muffled shout came from within the peak.

"Who?"

Luo Chen, dressed in a magnificent feathered robe, smiled faintly and released his aura.

"Old friend, a returning acquaintance—are you not welcome?"

Inside a cave dwelling on the peak, the Nine Spirits Sage suddenly opened his eyes, a look of surprise and uncertainty on his face.

"Luo Chen?"

Luo Chen's lips curled slightly. "It is I!"

As his words fell, the sea of clouds before him drifted gently, as if guided by some intelligence, making way for a wide path.

Luo Chen walked forward calmly, and soon saw a figure standing against the wind on the mountain top, watching him from afar.

It was the Nine Spirits Sage, whom he had not seen for many years!

Stepping onto the mountain top in one stride, Luo Chen cupped his hands. "Fellow Daoist Nine Spirits, long time no see."

The Nine Spirits Sage's eyes held a hint of amazement as he slowly spoke: "I thought you were dead."

Luo Chen laughed heartily. "How could I be someone with such a short life?"

The Nine Spirits Sage shook his head. "That's true. Back then, surrounded by three great demon emperors, you still survived. There should be few in this world who can match you. However, I heard that you fought a desperate battle with the demon emperor Azure Frost, and both of you disappeared into the Yellow Spring Sea. I thought you had perished."

Speaking thus, the Nine Spirits Sage could not help but look Luo Chen up and down.

The three great demon emperors were strong, but they were not of one mind. Moreover, he had been there to hold back Bull Demon, so the three demon emperors could not fully unite against Luo Chen.

But the demon emperor Azure Frost was a genuine top-tier powerhouse of the Eastern Wilderness, known as the number one under the Ascendant realm.

Luo Chen had fought a desperate battle with her and still survived.

And...

The Nine Spirits Sage shook off his thoughts. The person before him was, after all, the living, breathing Luo Chen.

He extended a hand in invitation. "Please, come inside!"

Luo Chen, with a smile on his face, followed him into the cave dwelling. Behind them, the sea of clouds gradually closed, and no one knew that Luo Chen had come to Nine Origin Peak.

...

When old friends meet, a round of reminiscing is inevitable.

Recalling past events was one thing; commenting on the present was another.

"After you destroyed the Five Elements Divine Sect and left, the human-demon war broke out again. But compared to the previous time, this one was much less intense."

"Although less intense, the losses for our various Eastern Wilderness sects were not small. The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect practically used us as cannon fodder, and on the demon side, there were powerhouses like Azure Frost, whom almost no one could match. So we suffered repeated defeats."

"Until the upheaval in the Central Continent came!"

"At that time, the Ascendant Chunyuan, who was stationed in the Eastern Wilderness, personally took action without hesitation, capturing Azure Frost alive and forcing back the demon army."

"The war was thus declared suspended, and we gained a chance to catch our breath."

"After that, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect cultivators stationed in the Eastern Wilderness all returned to the Central Continent, leaving only a few Sage-level true persons behind. They used the Dongyang Sect to remotely control the Eastern Wilderness, ordering us to hand over no small amount of resources every year."

Speaking of the losses during the human-demon war, the Nine Spirits Sage was full of grief.

And when it came to the current tribute of resources and offerings to the Dao Sect, he was even more filled with hidden anger.

Luo Chen asked curiously, "Since they've all left, are you still willing to pay tribute?"

The Nine Spirits Sage smiled bitterly. "Who would be willing? Everyone wishes to recuperate and use all resources to restore the sect's strength. But that is the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!"

Luo Chen frowned. "Since you know about the upheaval in the Central Continent, don't you know that the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect has now split into five—no, four parts? Moreover, only one Ascendant-level great power remains. They can barely take care of themselves, so how can they have the spare energy to control the Eastern Wilderness?"

Suddenly hearing this intelligence, the Nine Spirits Sage was stunned.

The Dao Sect that once dominated the Mountains and Seas Realm had split into four?

The once-famous Heavenly Origin Five Elders now only had one left?

Seeing his reaction, Luo Chen knew that the cultivators of the Eastern Wilderness did not have very detailed information about the Central Continent.

That made sense. His own intelligence came from the Green Parasol Mountain sacred ground.

As an enemy of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, no one knew the specific changes on the other side better than Green Parasol Mountain.

And the major forces of the Eastern Wilderness, leaderless, how could they cross the sea and keep up with the changes on the other side in just a few short years?

Luo Chen shook his head and sighed. "If it were the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect at its peak, they wouldn't need you to voluntarily pay tribute. They would have already moved into the Eastern Wilderness like before, instead of using their lackey, the Dongyang Sect."

Looking back on the changes of the past few years, the Nine Spirits Sage realized that this did seem to be the case.

The Dongyang Sect was merely a fox borrowing the tiger's might.

And the few Dao Sect true persons left in the Eastern Wilderness also seemed somewhat outwardly strong but inwardly weak.

For a moment, the Nine Spirits Sage's expression shifted uncertainly.

Luo Chen did not disturb his thoughts, drinking the fine wine stored by the Nine Spirits Sect at his leisure.

He hadn't enjoyed such good things for years. The spirit wine of the Diedie Cloud Sect that he had once stored had been completely exhausted during his journey to the Southern Frontier and his cultivation on Broken Soul Cliff.

Savoring it now, it was indeed refreshing.

After a long while, the Nine Spirits Sage finally recovered.

"Thank you for telling me this news."

Luo Chen smiled. "So, what do you plan to do next?"

The Nine Spirits Sage twitched the corner of his mouth. "What I do doesn't matter. What matters is what the other fellow Daoists of the Eastern Wilderness do. After all, it is still the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!"

Luo Chen shrugged, unconcerned. "Then suit yourself! Besides catching up, I have another favor to ask this time."

"Oh?" The Nine Spirits Sage was extremely surprised. "The esteemed Pill Sect actually has something to ask of me?"

Luo Chen was helpless. He was not omnipotent, so naturally, he had his shortcomings.

Straightening his expression, Luo Chen stated his request.

"I would like to borrow your sect's inherited true artifact."

"The Nine Firmament Jar?"

"Yes!"

"Take it!"

The Nine Spirits Sage flicked his hand, and a large black jar appeared beside him, hitting the ground with a clang.

This treasure was the same heavy artifact he had relied on in his battle with the White Deer Cave's Peng Xiang Daoist.

Luo Chen was somewhat surprised. The other party was so straightforward, not even asking the purpose before lending it?

He hesitated for a moment, then took the initiative to explain: "As you know, I have a technique that requires a large amount of firmament qi. But collecting firmament qi from the market is too slow. I heard that your sect's inherited true artifact is best at gathering the firmament qi of the nine heavens, so I took the liberty of asking to borrow it. This loan may take a long time, not just a year or two."

The Nine Spirits Sage laughed heartily. "Luo Chen, you are too polite! Just for the kindness of you coming to the Medicine King Domain without hesitation to save me, and for that you fell into the trap of being surrounded by three great demon emperors—lending you a dead object is nothing!"

Luo Chen was speechless. "In that case, I'll accept it."

The Nine Spirits Sage nodded, at the same time taking out a jade slip and immersing his divine sense into it.

While inscribing with his divine sense, he said, "This treasure has some tricks to its use. To avoid trouble, I'll record a control treasure formula for you first!"

Luo Chen was grateful. "I appreciate the trouble."

The Nine Spirits Sage suddenly paused.

"Trouble is not the word. But I also have an uninvited request."

"Oh?" Luo Chen was curious.

Immediately, he saw the mana fluctuations on the Nine Spirits Sage opposite him roll out like ocean waves, and a vast aura slowly pressed toward him.

Luo Chen narrowed his eyes. "It's been many years, and you have advanced like this. Congratulations on finally breaking through to the Great Sage realm!"

The Nine Spirits Sage's sleeves fluttered without wind, and a hint of eagerness appeared on his face.

"Since becoming a Great Sage, I have never fought anyone. Fellow Daoist, you have contended with the demon emperor Azure Frost and are undoubtedly a top figure in this realm. I wonder how far apart we are?"

Luo Chen smiled slightly and spread his hand.

"Granted!"
