# Chapter 1120: Ascension (Part 1)

**Dan Saint's Blessed Land!**

Due to the sinking of the Nether Abyss and the re-emergence of Yanfu, the once-secluded Dan Saint's Blessed Land had now fully connected with this world.

Yet this place remained tranquil, even quieter than before.

All because the Luo Tian Ancestor, Supreme Luo Chen, resided here.

In these final years before his ascension to the Immortal Realm, unless there was an urgent matter, no one would disturb him.

Everyone wanted to leave him with the most beautiful memories in these last days of the mortal world.

By the lakeside, a man in white robes wore a serene expression, quietly fishing.

His gaze fell upon the clear lake surface. As ripples spread, countless people and events seemed to reflect upon it.

Dahe Market, Tianlan Immortal City, Jade Cauldron Domain, the Million Great Mountains, the Northern Sea...

In the ocean of memory, silver waves rippled, stirring deep emotion.

But the white-robed man's heart was as firm as iron, without the slightest wavering. He simply tugged at his fishing rod and pulled up a fat fish.

After carefully removing the hook, he tossed the fish back into the water.

Two streaks of light streaked across the sky, then landed beside him.

They were two women, both exceptionally beautiful, each with her own unique charm.

One was like a peony—gorgeous and generous.

The other was like a snow lotus—solitary, chaste, and noble.

But at this moment, the gazes they cast upon the white-robed man were filled with tender affection.

"Husband, you're quite content enjoying yourself all alone like this. Because of your ascension, the entire sect is running around with no time to rest."

Luo Chen chuckled. "I've worked hard my whole life. Can't I enjoy myself a little?"

As he spoke, he patted the green stone beneath him.

Canglong lifted her skirt and sat down quietly. Gu Caiyi shot him a glare before sitting down beside him as well.

"Regarding the inheritance matters you instructed, they have been arranged. Wang Yuan is overseeing the overall process, sorting through the many cultivation techniques and scriptures you've left behind."

"For the inheritance of the hundred cultivation arts, several elders including Lingjun, Canglong, and Qin Yuanjiang are organizing them..."

"As for the sect's rules and regulations, you need not worry. After several hundred years, the Luo Tian Sect has its own effective set of regulations."

Gu Caiyi recounted the Luo Tian Sect's recent affairs one by one, leaving nothing out, meticulous in every detail.

Luo Chen smiled as he watched the woman's chatterbox manner, as if she had countless words she couldn't finish saying.

In this period, few dared to disturb him—only Caiyi.

Luo Chen knew that she was reluctant to part with him, so she wanted to seize every moment to cling to him with affection.

"You've worked hard."

Gu Caiyi pouted. "What hardship have I endured? I stopped managing sect affairs long ago. Your son is the one in charge now. I'm just telling you what I've seen and heard."

Luo Chen chuckled softly. "That way, I can leave with a lighter heart, right?"

Gu Caiyi quickly covered his mouth. "You're ascending to the Immortal Realm, heading to a broader world. Don't speak so harshly."

Luo Chen laughed heartily, pulled the woman's waist close, and let her lean against his shoulder.

Gu Caiyi rested her head sideways on the man's broad shoulder and murmured, "You don't need to worry. I'll cultivate diligently, strive to break through to Ascendant, and go find you in the Immortal Realm. Then I'll tell you all about home."

The woman spoke casually, but the determination in her words was unwavering.

Just as she had once braved life and death to forcibly establish her foundation, and later cast everything aside to focus entirely on breaking through to the Sage stage—all so she could stand openly by Luo Chen's side.

Luo Chen never doubted Gu Caiyi, but whether they would truly reunite in the Immortal Realm in the future was something he couldn't predict.

He turned his head to look at the quiet Canglong beside him.

"How is the Spirit Communication Formation coming along?"

Canglong smoothed her hair and nodded gently. "According to your instructions, using Yanfu Mountain as the foundation and drawing upon the large spirit veins of the Luo Tian Continent, the Spirit Communication Formation has been successfully set up. At that time, using bloodline power and the Primordial Spirit Lamp you left behind, the formation can be activated."

After speaking, her beautiful eyes looked at Luo Chen with some curiosity. "Senior Brother, can we truly contact you through the Spirit Communication Formation?"

Luo Chen nodded, then shook his head. "Perhaps yes, perhaps no. I haven't ascended yet, so I don't know if the Spirit Communication Formation will truly work."

The so-called "Spirit Communication Formation" they spoke of was something Luo Chen had imitated.

The object of his imitation was the God-Descending Formation he had seen on Heaven-Earth Island back then!

He didn't have the original formation diagram, so he could only grope his way through based on his own understanding and some information Li Canghai had revealed back then.

Its effects certainly wouldn't be as powerful as the original God-Descending Formation—it couldn't achieve cross-realm descent.

But if it was merely for simple communication, it might have miraculous effects.

For this reason, Luo Chen had specially left behind a Primordial Spirit Lamp.

If the Luo Tian Sect truly wanted to contact him, they could have a descendant bearing Luo Chen's bloodline power use the Primordial Spirit Lamp to activate the Spirit Communication Formation.

"Senior Brother, in the end, you still can't let go of the Luo Tian Sect?" Canglong asked softly.

Luo Chen let out a bitter laugh. "The sect means little to me. What I can't let go of is you, Caiyi, Lingxi, Lingjun, and all the friends who once fought alongside me!"

In the midst of his faint sigh, the evening glow had already filled the sky.

...

The former Tianming Palace, now the Luo Tian Hall.

Late at night, it was still brightly lit.

Luo Lingxi sat high above, reviewing the vast intelligence reports sent back by the sect's disciples.

They concerned the invitation list for the Ascension Grand Ceremony.

He paid little attention to the low-level cultivators and small forces eager to attend the ceremony. What Luo Lingxi focused on were the various Sage-level sects.

In the lamplight, a shadow flickered faintly.

"Sect Master, the various major sects are ambiguous in their stance. They haven't agreed on the spot whether they will attend the ceremony on time."

Luo Lingxi didn't look up. "They're afraid. Afraid that Father will take them with him before he leaves."

In the darkness, the shadow said coldly, "If they're afraid, that means they have guilty consciences!"

Luo Lingxi's lips curled slightly. "In this world, who doesn't have a guilty conscience? What matters is who dares to act on it!"

The shadow showed some concern. "Once the Ancestor ascends, there will be no one to suppress the Eastern Wilderness. The petty and unscrupulous may stir. How will our Luo Tian Sect handle it then?"

Faced with this question, Luo Lingxi slowly raised his head.

His face bore a strong resemblance to Sima Huiniang, but his eyes were exactly like Luo Chen's.

At this moment, in those eyes like cold, deep pools, there was actually a hint of fervor!

"In years past, Father took a minor, insignificant force and step by step built it into the current number one sacred land of the Eastern Wilderness. Having inherited such a vast foundation, how could I let it decline?"

"You worry too much. A sacred land must have the bearing of a sacred land. Even without an Ascendant powerhouse in residence, the Luo Tian Sect remains the number one great sect of the Eastern Wilderness! This sect fears no challenge and has the confidence to face any challenge!"

As he spoke, a powerful aura surged from Luo Lingxi's body, instantly filling the entire hall.

The shadow behind him trembled. "Sect Master, you've broken through again?"

The powerful aura flashed and vanished, as if nothing had happened.

Luo Lingxi lowered his head again and pulled out a visiting card from the thick stack of intelligence.

"Look, isn't there a sect that has proactively extended an olive branch?"

On the visiting card were clearly imprinted three large characters: "Nine Spirits Sect"!

...

In the current Eastern Wilderness, the number one figure was undoubtedly the Ascendant Great Power, Luo Chen.

But the title of number one below Ascendant had never been claimed.

Some said it was the Ancient Demon's Son, Di Tu, who had single-handedly intercepted reinforcements from the Tiannan Dao Sect. Others believed that Taiyue Zhenren of Mount Tai Sect was no weaker than anyone. And the demon monk Kongchan, who had joined the Luo Tian Sect at some unknown time, also possessed heaven-defying abilities.

But no matter how the rankings were arranged, one person always ranked among the top three.

That was the Nine Spirits Sect's Supreme Elder, Nine Spirits Primordial Lord!

This man was a representative figure in the cultivation world of those who achieved great success late in life after accumulating steadily.

Initially, he wasn't considered exceptionally brilliant, known only for his profound magical power.

But as the chaotic era unfolded four hundred years ago, Nine Spirits Primordial Lord's performance repeatedly refreshed the world's perception.

Facing a siege by three Demon Emperors of the same rank, he forcibly preserved his sect.

After advancing to the mid-Sage stage, he mastered the sect's inherited great divine ability, the Great Nine Heavens River, and from then on, he was renowned as comparable to a Great Cultivator.

During the human-demon war, his performance was outstanding, surpassed only by the current Ascendant Great Power, Luo Chen.

After he broke through to the late-Sage stage and became a true Great Cultivator, his strength became even more terrifying.

On the Dongyuan Continent, Nine Spirits Primordial Lord drove straight in, and no one in the vast Dao Sect could stop him.

Facing the Dao Sect's prodigy, Shi Ju, even though the latter had comprehended the true meaning of laws, Nine Spirits Primordial Lord still beat him back step by step. If not for the Dao Sect's experts desperately protecting him, Shi Ju's fate would not have been severe injury—he would have died at Nine Spirits Primordial Lord's hands.

And such a naturally formidable existence had also been fortunate enough to comprehend the true meaning of laws. Just how terrifying his strength was now was difficult for the world to fathom.

The world only knew that if a second Ascendant Great Power were to appear in the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world after Luo Chen, it would inevitably be between Taiyue Zhenren and Nine Spirits Primordial Lord.

After the Luo Tian Sect sent out invitation cards for the Ascension Grand Ceremony, the circle of top Eastern Wilderness experts hesitated, not daring to make a decision.

Under these circumstances, almost everyone's attention fell upon the Nine Spirits Sect.

The world knew that in years past, the Dan Saint and Nine Spirits Primordial Lord had been close friends.

Now, when intentions—whether goodwill or malice—were unclear, how would Nine Spirits Primordial Lord choose? How would the Great Power Luo Chen handle it?

Under the watchful eyes of many interested parties, Nine Spirits Primordial Lord made his move.

He came alone, personally ascending Yanfu, seeking an audience with Luo Chen.

...

"You've come."

"Everyone was waiting for me to make the first move. How could I not come?"

"Why do you all fear me like a tiger? When you accepted the Initiation Pills before, each of you was extremely warm and close!"

"Times are different. Even when a secular emperor's reign ends, he often takes a few meritorious old ministers with him. Naturally, among us cultivators, there is no shortage of grand gestures where one takes enemies along when their time is up."

"Then, are you enemies of the Luo Tian Sect?"

Without any roundabout talk, the most straightforward question was asked, shaking the hearts of those present in the quiet hall.

Nine Spirits Primordial Lord was slightly silent, then said with resounding force, "No. Not in the past, not now, and not in the future!"

Above, Luo Chen nodded gently.

"Mm, I trust you."

Nine Spirits Primordial Lord felt relieved, but Luo Chen's next words made his heart rise again.

"But I don't trust them."

Nine Spirits Primordial Lord couldn't help but smile bitterly. "Then what do you intend to do? Do you truly plan to take them with you when you ascend?"

"Hmph, you're thinking too beautifully."

"Uh..."

Faced with Luo Chen's sudden jest, Nine Spirits Primordial Lord was momentarily speechless.

He sighed. "You know, and I know, what this so-called 'taking them with you' means. But you should know even better that the one who starts it sets a precedent. There will always be brilliant talents rising in this world. If every expert who breaks through to the Ascendant stage must act so domineeringly, then there should be no Luo Tian Sect now, and there will be no Luo Tian Sect in the future."

Luo Chen smiled. "Yes, how could I not understand this principle? So, I merely want to impose a slight restraint on everyone, a restraint on all of you."

Nine Spirits Primordial Lord frowned slightly. "How to restrain?"

"It's not difficult. Your generation just needs to give me a promise—that during your lifetimes, you will not make a move against the Luo Tian Sect."

"You fear competition?"

"This seat has never feared competition and encourages competition among my disciples. But I know the overwhelming advantage that those who have comprehended the true meaning of laws possess over those of the same rank. Without a higher major realm, one can hardly even muster the strength to resist. I don't want the Initiation Pills I refined to create all of you, only for you to turn around and harm the lives of Luo Tian Sect disciples. Therefore, I need such a promise! As for the next generation, that will depend on each side's abilities."

Nine Spirits Primordial Lord had no rebuttal for these words.

He had once admired Luo Chen's ability to fight across ranks. A hundred years ago, despite being a full major realm above Shi Ju and possessing lethal techniques, he still hadn't managed to kill him. Now that he had comprehended the true meaning of laws, he finally understood just how terrifying this power was in enhancing a Sage's strength!

Luo Chen's concern was reasonable.

He himself could perhaps guarantee that he would always hold goodwill toward the Luo Tian Sect, but what about others?

The Dongyang Grand Elder had failed in his comprehension and gone mad, but what about the one from White Deer Cave?

And what about the Sword Realm's startling genius, Lin Qingxuan of Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion, and the Virtual Emperor Sect's elder, Bai Xuzi? These two had already comprehended the true meaning of laws.

And their relationship with the Luo Tian Sect was hardly close.

These were only the ones who had revealed themselves. Of the thirteen sects that had originally received Initiation Pills, how many had not shown their true results in secret?

Nine Spirits Primordial Lord didn't know what attitude they would hold toward the Luo Tian Sect in the future, but if he were in Luo Chen's place, he certainly wouldn't want to hand over such a great foundation to others, making a wedding dress for someone else.

Take, for example, the Yanfu Mountain they were on, or the Luo Tian Continent beneath their feet!

"Correspondingly, this seat will also give you a promise."

Suddenly!

Luo Chen spoke these words.

Nine Spirits Primordial Lord raised his head, looking at Luo Chen with anticipation.

"Currently, the Eastern Wilderness has limited fifth-order spirit veins. If any of you reach the point where you must break through the Ascendant bottleneck, you may bring my token and enter Yanfu Mountain to cultivate for a hundred years, attempting the breakthrough. Just like Deng Taiyue now!"

Nine Spirits Primordial Lord was somewhat moved.

Luo Chen continued, "If you fail, so be it. But if you succeed in breaking through to Ascendant, you must absolutely not engage in the act of usurping another's nest. I know that Ascendant Great Powers require fifth-order spirit veins for daily cultivation, so I have specifically sought out several ownerless spirit veins in the wilderness. Although they are somewhat dangerous in the wild, they are not impossible to develop. Moreover, aside from the Eastern Wilderness, the Mountains and Seas Realm is vast. You would do well to set your sights further afield!"

This time, Nine Spirits Primordial Lord was truly tempted.

He understood the meaning behind Luo Chen's latter words. If one wanted an example, it could be seen as a large-scale expansion war.

And such matters were not without precedent. Hadn't the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect done exactly that?

Although they had failed.

"Alright, I have said my piece. If you all agree, then good. If not, then this seat will not hesitate to sacrifice my reputation before my departure for the sake of what comes after."

Nine Spirits Primordial Lord straightened his posture, then pursed his lips and bowed deeply to Luo Chen.

"I will convey this condition to all the fellow daoists of the Eastern Wilderness!"

"Go!"

Luo Chen waved his hand, watching Nine Spirits Primordial Lord respectfully withdraw.

After he left, crisp footsteps echoed in the quiet hall.

"Father, why must you go to such lengths?"

Luo Chen smiled. "I know you have great ambitions, son, but these old fellows are no easy opponents either. And besides..."

At the end, a trace of gloom appeared on the man's face.

"I also don't want the friends who once fought alongside me to end up crossing swords with the Luo Tian Sect."

Luo Lingxi was momentarily stunned. He vaguely realized that this last sentence might be Luo Chen's true feelings.

In truth, it was exactly so.

Luo Chen wasn't actually that afraid of Eastern Wilderness experts turning against the Luo Tian Sect.

In the years before his ascension, although he appeared leisurely on the surface, he had actually arranged everything for the aftermath with perfect clarity.

The cultivation techniques, scriptures, cultivation experience, and summaries of the hundred arts he had accumulated throughout his life had all been passed down.

The magic treasures he no longer needed had been gifted to those close to him.

For example, the complete set of the Great Five Elements Sword Formation had been given to Gu Caiyi, the Heart-Inquiring Mirror to Canglong, the Mystic Dust Armor was now worn by Luo Lingxi, the Chaos Heaven Staff had been given to Zeng Yilong, the White Tiger Puppet to the Sage-stage Qin Yuanjiang, the Life-Seizing Golden Feather to Qu Lingjun's son, Qu Jiu...

The only treasures still on his person were the Primordial Unity Cauldron, the Yuantu Sword, the Burial Bone Umbrella, and a few storage rings.

Aside from these, he had also secretly left behind three killing moves, known only to Luo Lingxi.

These three killing moves were all Luo Chen's most proud techniques!

Thousand Skies Converging, Starfire Burning the World, and an Azure Yang Great Handprint that seemed unremarkable but contained the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire. This technique was nurtured on Yanfu Mountain and could grow with time, eventually becoming endless and inexhaustible.

These three moves—let alone Sage cultivators who had comprehended the true meaning of laws—even an Ascendant Great Power would find few who could withstand them.

In the end, Luo Chen truly did not want to see some old friends harbor improper thoughts, only to end up dying tragically under his divine abilities.

He hoped they would make the right choice!
