# Chapter 759: Nine Heavens and Ten Earths, Transposition Can Reach; Heavenly Secret Daosu, Luring the Illusions

Two days later.

In the hazy morning mist, a man and woman left the valley side by side.

"Brother Luo, where are we going?"

"You will know when we arrive. In any case, I will not harm you."

"Heh, this little sister is not worried about that."

"There is one more matter Luo must inform you of in advance."

"What is it?"

"At that time, I will need you to guard a formation for me, to prevent others from damaging it. If it truly is damaged, you must inform me in advance, so that I do not enter the formation blindly."

"What formation is so important?"

"Let us first go to Three-Rainbow Falls and take a look. I have not fully decided yet."

The sound of their conversation gradually faded into the mist.

Only after one day and one night, when rainbows were still entering their sight from an extremely great distance, did the two stop.

Standing on a high mountain, Fu Qinglan looked at the waterfall in the distance that turned three times, silver arcs falling in three tiers, puzzlement on her face.

Inside Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, there was no detailed map.

Because each time people entered, five hundred years had usually passed.

In five hundred years, seas changed into mulberry fields, and there were also destructive creatures like illusion beasts. Therefore, during every grotto-heaven grand competition, the mountains and rivers inside would undergo enormous changes.

Thus, even though the great clans of the Tianyuan Dao Sect had participated in several Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven grand competitions, they still did not understand the terrain inside in much detail.

They even believed that the changes in the mountains, rivers, and landforms inside had no pattern at all, and were guided by the dead Mirage Dragon body's instincts.

That waterfall in the distance, called Three-Rainbow Falls, was merely a name Luo Chen had casually given it when passing by once.

Who knew whether that waterfall had existed in the previous five hundred years?

Yet it had to be said, Luo Chen's name was truly quite vivid!

The magnificent waterfall poured down in great volume, and because of the rise and fall of the mountain, it split into three slanting falls.

Every tier was like a white silk bolt.

Under the sunlight, each reflected crystalline seven-colored rainbow light.

Three-Rainbow Falls. The scenery matched the name, incomparably magnificent.

Just as she was wondering why they had come here, and why they were not approaching Three-Rainbow Falls, Luo Chen's voice sounded by her ear.

"After twenty years of using illusion cores to cultivate and absorbing large amounts of spiritual impulse, you should be very familiar with that sensation. At this moment, you may as well release your Nascent Soul true body and sense it a little."

Fu Qinglan did as he said!

A female infant with pale skin and delicate hands and feet flew out from above her head.

Such an action, in the outside world, was truly daring beyond measure.

Even inside Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, openly releasing one's Nascent Soul before another person like this was an act of someone who did not know life from death.

But the person beside her was Luo Chen!

Fu Qinglan had no guard at all, and truly did as the other party said.

After a moment, her Nascent Soul returned to her body, and surprise appeared on Fu Qinglan's face.

"There is a large amount of spiritual impulse lingering there. Could it be that there is an extremely powerful fourth-tier illusion beast there?"

It was truly too dense. Even from so far away, she sensed it.

Luo Chen smiled and shook his head. "No matter how strong a fourth-tier illusion beast is, because of the illusion core's restraint, spiritual impulse will not leak out like this. Think carefully again."

"Would it not be easier to say it directly? Why go around in circles like this?" Fu Qinglan rolled her eyes at Luo Chen, but she truly thought carefully.

After pondering in detail, she gradually understood, and even became somewhat uncertain and shocked.

"Someone is comprehending the true intent of laws there?"

"Exactly!"

Luo Chen nodded heavily, and the smile on his face gradually faded.

"Perhaps you are inside the mountain and therefore do not know its shape. These years, I have guarded you from the side and seen it clearly. When absorbing illusion cores to cultivate, the spiritual impulse inside them lingers nearby until it is completely exhausted."

Fu Qinglan understood what Luo Chen meant, but another piece of information revealed by his words shocked her even more:

"You can see spiritual impulse?"

Luo Chen did not explain, instead continuing along his earlier words. "However, the spiritual impulse inside one or two illusion cores would absolutely not be this abundant. If one wants to explain the magnificent phenomenon at Three-Rainbow Falls, there is only one answer!"

Luo Chen's expression turned solemn. "That is that the person cultivating inside the waterfall has also entered a state of sudden enlightenment, thereby actively drawing the spiritual impulse of heaven and earth to gather toward him!"

Fu Qinglan's expression changed drastically!

Spiritual impulse was everywhere, yet distant and impossible to seek.

Often, only when a cultivator entered sudden enlightenment or ascended would it gather in large quantities.

Inside Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, spiritual impulse had been solidified inside illusion cores, allowing Tianyuan true disciples like them to absorb and refine it.

This sort of strange matter was already enough to shock people merely by speaking of it.

But now, there was such an existence who had not only absorbed the spiritual impulse inside illusion cores, but had even personally entered sudden enlightenment... needless to say, it had to be one of the thirteen senior brothers and sisters.

Moreover, once he finished this sudden enlightenment, he might find origin power in one stroke!

At that time, first place in this grand competition would be beyond doubt!

Then, who could it be?

"It is Gu Daosu, the younger brother of Eminent Daoyin, the qilin child of this generation from the Gu clan of the Twelve Great Houses."

Luo Chen calmly named the person cultivating in seclusion within Three-Rainbow Falls.

As for why he knew?

This was very simple.

As mentioned before, when these Tianyuan true disciples cultivated with illusion cores, small amounts of spiritual impulse would linger nearby. Outsiders could not sense it deeply, and even Luo Chen had to circulate source power into his eyes to observe a little, but the illusion beasts of this world were extremely sensitive to scenes where spiritual impulse lingered!

If one remained in a state of absorbing illusion beasts to cultivate for a long time, it was very easy to attract attacks from high-level illusion beasts.

This was also one of the reasons Dao protectors existed: to stop illusion beasts from disturbing Tianyuan true disciples' comprehension of law true intent.

During that year in the secluded valley, it so happened that an extremely fast dragon-louse illusion beast had barged in and attempted to attack Fu Qinglan.

Luo Chen acted and drove it away.

His original intention had not been to drive it away, but to kill it and take its core.

But that dragon-louse illusion beast was extremely fast and unexpectedly escaped while Luo Chen was not paying attention.

How could Luo Chen tolerate a fourth-tier illusion beast in his grasp escaping? He chased it for some distance, then arrived at Three-Rainbow Falls.

At that time, a beautiful married woman had suddenly acted and killed that dragon louse!

Luo Chen recognized that beautiful woman. She was precisely Gu Daosu's Dao protector!

Naturally, he learned that Gu Daosu was cultivating at Three-Rainbow Falls, and his progress was extremely fast, even above Fu Qinglan's.

At that time, Luo Chen had no particular thoughts. After all, Fu Qinglan was still in secluded cultivation and needed him to guard her.

But now, he had some additional ideas.

Moreover, with his current strength, this additional idea could be fully carried out.

"So it is Senior Brother Gu."

"His talent is indeed extraordinary. And his elder brother played against the Chess Saint in the Peach Garden and obtained a Rotten Axe-Handle Black Chess Piece for him. With the black chess piece guarding him, his divine soul is incomparably firm, and it is not easy for him to lose himself while searching for origin power in the sea of origin qi."

"It is understandable that he has walked ahead of us now."

The spirit-treasure-level Rotten Axe-Handle Black Chess Piece also had this kind of effect?

Listening to Fu Qinglan's sigh, Luo Chen was somewhat surprised, but his hands did not stop moving.

Material after material was taken out by him from his storage ring.

Fu Qinglan was somewhat curious and crouched down to look at those materials.

Profound Heaven Crystal, Red Flame Sand, Spirit-Gathering Wood... Finally, she saw Luo Chen somewhat painfully take out a piece of transparent crystal.

"Is this a Spatial Prism Aperture?"

Luo Chen held that crystal. "You recognize this object as well?"

Fu Qinglan nodded. "Of course I recognize it. This object is an extremely precious spatial material. It contains its own space within and has excellent extensibility. It is often used to make storage instruments at the magic treasure or even true artifact level. But generally, it is used only to make magic treasure-level storage instruments. Using it for a true artifact would be too extravagant. My sect has collected many Spatial Prism Apertures, specially for use in making teleportation formations."

Many?

Luo Chen was somewhat astonished.

But thinking of how the Tianyuan Dao Sect had arranged teleportation formations on Star Island and on several islands controlled by the Northern Sea Tianyuan Merchant Alliance, it was natural that they would possess many Spatial Prism Apertures.

However, they were an Ascendant sacred land!

No matter how precious Spatial Prism Apertures were, they always had ways to obtain them.

But in Luo Chen's hands, there was only this one Spatial Prism Aperture!

He had obtained it from Feiyunzi's remains.

Fu Qinglan naturally noticed the pained look Luo Chen had shown earlier. Presumably, the other party did not have many Spatial Prism Apertures saved. But precisely because of this, she became even more curious about Luo Chen's purpose in taking out a Spatial Prism Aperture and these materials.

"Brother Luo, are you refining a magic treasure or making a teleportation formation?"

"The latter!"

Luo Chen no longer kept her in suspense and simply stated his purpose.

"I plan to arrange a small teleportation formation here spanning five thousand li. Actually, calling it a transposition formation would be more suitable."

Teleportation formations and transposition formations sounded roughly the same.

But when implemented in detail, the difference was enormous.

The former were fixed and used frequently, requiring higher demands on terrain, spirit veins, and materials.

The latter were not fixed, and most of the time were one-use items. They did not have such high demands on terrain or spirit veins, and their advantage lay in convenience and speed.

With Luo Chen's current conditions, he could only make a transposition formation. This still had to be credited to his undoing countless formations in the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion, which gave him sufficient attainment in spatial restrictions to have this confidence.

Without further words, Luo Chen immediately began refining the transposition formation!

Magic power surged and withdrew at his fingertips, generating Nascent Soul true fire amid destruction, burning those materials.

As they melted, they continuously fused together.

Luo Chen then constantly added all sorts of materials into it. His entire person sank into the work, all kinds of artifact-refining arts coming easily to hand, yet used with perfect propriety.

Fu Qinglan did not dare disturb him and peacefully watched from the side as Luo Chen worked with complete focus.

Her heart was filled with emotion.

Earlier, she had heard her grandfather say that Luo Chen was a formidable alchemist. When the Alchemy Saint ascended, one of the five Heavenly Secret Pills had been given to Luo Chen, who had just arrived in Central Continent. It was very likely because she felt moved that he was also a person of the alchemy path and therefore left him this blessing.

She then discovered inside Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven that the other party had considerable attainments in formations.

But now, the other party was clearly displaying no ordinary ability in artifact forging as well.

"Is he truly only a little over two hundred years old?"

"So young, with such strength, and he also attends to multiple skills! Or could it be that these things are only the tip of the iceberg among his many abilities?"

"This man truly is like a mystery. Beneath layer after layer of mist, unexpected surprises always emerge."

...

Several days later.

Luo Chen looked at the two formation disks in his hands and exhaled a long breath of turbid air.

His tired face, however, was quite full of satisfaction!

This was an expression Fu Qinglan rarely saw on Luo Chen's face. The other party's ability to control his emotions was extremely outstanding. He had only lost composure back then when learning of the grotto-heaven's differing time flow, and most of the time, he behaved very calmly.

But at this moment, pride was clearly showing.

Her gaze fell on those two formation disks, and realization dawned.

Yes!

Those capable of independently refining formation disks were often existences with powerful attainments in both formations and artifact forging, and those capable of refining transposition formation disks had at least reached the fourth tier in one of those paths!

The other party indeed had reason to be proud!

"Is it done?"

"Naturally!"

"Does this formation have a name?"

"A mere small thousand-li transposition formation, how could it have a nam... Let us call it the Nine Heavens Ten Earths Grand Transposition Formation!"

Fu Qinglan smiled and nodded in agreement.

It was at this moment.

Luo Chen reached out, and one of the two formation disks was handed before her. Thirty-six high-grade spirit stones had already been embedded on it, along with one top-grade spirit stone!

"After I leave, arrange this formation disk inside this mountain's belly! As for the exact location..."

Luo Chen pointed, and a wisp of sword radiance suddenly flew out and entered the ground.

Before long, the sword radiance stopped at a place within the mountain's belly.

Fu Qinglan nodded. The formation the other party had earlier said he wanted her to guard must be this transposition formation.

Carefully holding the formation disk, she asked curiously, "Are you not going to rest for a while?"

Luo Chen brushed at nonexistent dust, straightened his robes, and walked to the cliff edge.

"No."

"I have rested enough inside this grotto-heaven!"

Before his words had fallen, he leaped, already like an arrow leaving the string, plunging down the cliff.

Fu Qinglan hurriedly leaned out to look. After the other party's figure fell into the dense forest below, it vanished completely.

Even his aura was fully concealed.

No matter how she probed with divine sense, she could not glimpse his tracks.

Fu Qinglan withdrew her gaze. "For him to act so carefully, even leaving the Nine... Nine Heavens Ten Earths Grand Transposition Formation Disk as an escape route, the plan he is about to carry out must be extremely dangerous. Although I am not much use, I must still guard this retreat path properly for him."

As she murmured, she released her magic power, and her entire person quietly sank into the mountain and earth.

...

"This is the place!"

"It can connect to Gu Daosu's spiritual impulse without letting his Dao protector discover me. A perfect distance!"

Where silver waterfalls descended from the sky, there would naturally be great rivers and lakes distributed among the mountains and streams.

Luo Chen's figure stopped within a large lake.

He gazed at Three-Rainbow Falls, only a hundred li away, with all his strength concealing his aura, then quietly sank into the center of the lake.

The moment he entered, the Primordial Unity Cauldron was taken out.

Opposite it were even more complex formation materials.

Luo Chen did not hesitate and drove these materials one by one into the lakebed.

A place chosen by a heaven's chosen like Gu Daosu for secluded cultivation was certain to be numinous and beautiful, with dense spiritual qi.

Therefore, even from a hundred li away, the spiritual qi in this large lake was quite good.

At this moment, under Luo Chen's arrangement, formation patterns gradually took shape.

If Fu Qinglan were here, she would definitely find this formation incomparably familiar!

That was right. This formation was exactly the formation they had arranged on the ten-li plain surrounded by three mountains after first entering Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven.

It possessed the wondrous effect of spreading medicinal fragrance and enticing illusion beasts!

Only, unlike back then, Luo Chen's formation this time no longer left any room to attempt trapping illusion beasts. Instead, he placed all its focus on amplifying medicinal power.

If it had been before, he still would not have been able to reach this point.

But now, his formation attainment on the system panel was a genuine fourth tier, fully allowing him to achieve what he imagined.

Moreover, he still had the Primordial Unity Cauldron as the formation-anchoring object!

Borrowing a true artifact's function of drawing the spiritual qi of heaven and earth, he could instantly amplify this formation's effect tenfold, hundredfold!

"The Heavenly Secret Pill's medicinal fragrance is colorless and scentless. Ordinary people cannot smell it at all, perhaps because its main material is spiritual impulse. Only those illusion beasts that crave spiritual impulse are especially sensitive to it."

"So long as I control it well, I can spread the Heavenly Secret Pill's medicinal fragrance from the original thousand-li range to ten thousand li, or even a hundred thousand li!"

"Most importantly, beside me is Gu Daosu, who is in a state of sudden enlightenment. He is the greatest bait!"

Thinking this, Luo Chen revealed a smile that showed everything was already in his grasp.

His plan was actually very simple: borrow the formation's power to spread the Heavenly Secret Pill's medicinal strength and attract powerful illusion beasts from tens of thousands of li around. With Gu Daosu, the obvious bait, added on top, it would surely lure in a large number of illusion beasts at once.

He only had to act at the right moment, and he would have a chance to harvest a large number of illusion cores in one go!

With this batch of illusion cores supporting her, Fu Qinglan could cultivate continuously. The sooner she comprehended origin power, the sooner he could leave Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven.

For this, even paying resources such as a Spatial Prism Aperture and a top-grade spirit stone was worth it.

The earlier he left, the less lifespan he would lose inside the grotto-heaven.

Of course!

The simpler a plan was, the easier it was to succeed.

But if it was too simple, the risk would also be greater!

He could imagine what kind of danger the situation would contain at that time. It might even spiral out of control.

The Nine Heavens Ten Earths Grand Transposition Formation was only an escape route. What truly gave him the confidence to face these dangers was still his own ability.

With a thought, the Heavenly Secret Pill appeared in his hand, and Luo Chen slowly placed it about an inch above the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

The surrounding formation instantly activated!

Looking at the Heavenly Secret Pill spinning and dancing, Luo Chen suddenly thought that if he used the fifth-tier Law-Derivation Pill he had refined in the Pill Realm as bait, would the effect be even better?

"Distracting thoughts have arisen again!"

"Even I do not dare consume that divine pill now. How could I use it as bait?"

"In the end, with three souls standing side by side, distracting thoughts arise when I am tired."

"I must be settled! I must be still!"

Luo Chen drew a deep breath, slowly closed his eyes, and circulated Fine Dust Primordial Art, entering a state of calming his mind and regulating his breath.

The surrounding lake water was clear and cold. Following his rhythmic breathing, ripple after ripple spread across the lake surface, finally returning to stillness.

*(End of Chapter)*
