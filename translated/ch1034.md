# Chapter 1034: Innate Dao Body, the Bearing of a Great Power!

The Spirit Root Testing Ceremony continued.

The number of people detected with spirit roots had increased considerably compared to before, rising from one or two in ten to two or three in ten.

The youths who had not yet been tested could not detect this change; they remained anxious and uneasy.

But for the Zhou clan's strong practitioners, this testing result could already be considered genuinely excellent.

Ordinarily, let alone two or three in ten, even two or three in a hundred was extremely difficult.

The real reason was that among this batch of youths, three out of ten were born from the union of two cultivator parents. Only thus could such a high frequency of spirit root bearers emerge.

Under normal circumstances, cultivators were generally unwilling to bear offspring early. This would harm one's own origin qi and affect one's cultivation.

Only those who had no hope for the Great Dao, who indulged in pleasure, or who lacked emotional anchors, would bear children in their later years.

That such excellent results had been achieved this time was entirely due to the benefits brought by the old ancestor's orders back then.

It could be imagined that after this batch, and the clansmen who would appear in the next ten years or so, cultivated in the future, they would surely become a vital force for the Zhou clan.

Of course, the elders were satisfied, but what the youths in their midst saw was not the luck of two or three in ten, but the elimination rate of seven or eight in ten.

Once without a spirit root, from then on, one was a mortal!

Restlessness, panic, worry—they spread quietly.

"Zhou Yuhao, Water-Wood dual spirit root!"

When the result appeared, the last trace of anxiety in Zhou Yuhao's heart vanished, and he couldn't help but grin widely.

But what surprised him even more was a pleasant voice from above.

"This child may enter the Dao Palace for cultivation."

Zhou Yuhao looked up in ecstasy. That beautiful matron was nodding slightly at him, seeming quite satisfied.

And beside Mo Wanting, the Zhou clan leader Zhou Junxiao's face showed a puzzled expression.

A dual spirit root was enough to enter the Dao Palace for cultivation and become a disciple of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. Then why couldn't someone like Zhou Yusheng, who also had a dual spirit root, do the same?

His gaze kept shifting between the two youths, Zhou Yusheng and Zhou Yuhao, and gradually he came to understand.

There were differences between dual spirit roots and dual spirit roots.

Water-Wood dual spirit root was the Five Elements mutual generation of Water giving birth to Wood. Cultivating Wood-attribute techniques would yield twice the result with half the effort!

But Zhou Yusheng had a Metal-Fire dual spirit root. Fire overcomes Metal, which was a Five Elements mutual overcoming. He could not cultivate Metal-attribute techniques. And if he cultivated Fire-attribute techniques, the Metal spirit root's perception of Metal-attribute spiritual qi would also affect the induction and absorption of Fire spiritual qi.

Because these two attributes, Metal and Fire, were both considered the most violent among the Five Elements.

In a sense, a Metal-Fire dual spirit root was only slightly better than a triple spirit root. It couldn't compare to a normal dual spirit root, let alone Zhou Yuhao's excellently matched dual spirit root.

After figuring this out, Zhou Junxiao had a rough idea of the Tiannan Dao Palace's standards for selecting disciples.

"If that's the case, then Yuwei will definitely get in, but Hong'er will probably fall short."

Sure enough!

In the subsequent tests, the three youths from his own lineage also had their results come out one after another.

One was a quadruple spirit root, barely able to cultivate.

Zhou Yuhong had an Earth-Wood dual spirit root. Although there was a mutual overcoming relationship, it wasn't as extreme as the previous Metal-Fire dual spirit root. It could only be considered a normal dual spirit root aptitude.

Placed in an ordinary sect or clan, this would already be considered excellent aptitude, with hope for Initiation or even Adept in the future.

But the Jade Pivot Academy Head Mo Wanting still did not speak up to accept him.

When it came to Zhou Yuwei, who was arranged near the end, Mo Wanting spoke up again.

"Metal-Earth dual spirit root, not bad. May enter the Dao Palace for cultivation!"

Earth gives birth to Metal. Cultivating Metal-attribute techniques would be both fast and stable. This verified Zhou Junxiao's thoughts.

And from this, he sighed at what the former Heavenly Origin Dao Sect had relied on to be the number one sacred land of the five continents!

Their standards for selecting disciples were actually this high.

Ordinary dual spirit roots were not even considered; at least a dual spirit root with some beneficial mutual generation was required to be qualified.

It could be imagined that back when the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect held the complete Central Continent, brilliant talents were as numerous as fish crossing a river, theirs for the picking. Only thus could they create countless geniuses and a flourishing gathering of strong practitioners.

"Daoist Zhou, your Zhou clan truly deserves to be a Sage clan. To have such a foundation even after experiencing war and chaos. Just one Spirit Root Testing Ceremony has allowed this Academy Head to harvest three good seedlings. In my opinion, it is not inferior to those Sage clans that have been passed down for a thousand years in my Dao Sect."

Perhaps having obtained three good seedlings, Mo Wanting was in a very good mood and took the initiative to converse with Zhou Junxiao.

Zhou Junxiao laughed heartily. "If that's truly the case, then why doesn't the Dao Sect give our Zhou clan the same treatment as those Sage clans?"

Mo Wanting smiled slightly. "Isn't it already treating everyone equally now? After all, in the past, the descendants of Star Gate guest elders had to undergo a five-hundred-year trial in the Central Continent before they could formally enter the Dao Sect's mountain gate."

Zhou Junxiao licked his lips and said with a hint of greed, "You know that's not what I'm talking about."

Mo Wanting raised an eyebrow. "You want fixed quotas?"

"Yes!"

Zhou Junxiao did not beat around the bush.

He was well aware that for the true Dao Sect aristocratic clans, aside from those with excellent aptitudes who could join the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, there was another batch of quotas for those who could audit classes in the Dao Palace.

Among this batch of people, perhaps most would not achieve as much as the former, but there would always be a few who stood out.

Moreover, some who ultimately could not stay in the Dao Sect to cultivate, after receiving instruction from the strongest sacred land of the five continents, could return to their clans and lead them to better development.

This was why the twelve great houses and thirty-six aristocratic clans of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect were increasing in number, never decreasing, and growing more and more.

If his Zhou clan had fixed quotas, then those with ordinary dual spirit root aptitudes like Zhou Yuhong, Zhou Yusheng, and Zhou Yuqi would also have the opportunity to enter the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect for cultivation.

Mo Wanting smiled. "Well, this Academy Head cannot make that decision. After all, the Zhou clan's contributions are insufficient, your time in the Central Continent is short, and you have not yet cultivated more good seedlings for the Dao Sect. So it's not very convenient to issue fixed quotas."

"Contributions? My Zhou clan's old ancestor contributed considerably during the Northern Sea War. As for the time issue, isn't the Dao Palace no longer emphasizing that now? You said so yourself earlier. As for more good seedlings?" Zhou Junxiao's gaze fell on the last few people who had not yet been tested. "I wonder just how many good seedlings it will take to satisfy the Dao Sect?"

Mo Wanting followed his gaze and also landed on those youths.

She covered her mouth and chuckled lightly. "I'm afraid just these few people are not enough."

"Is that so?"

Zhou Junxiao felt some disappointment in his heart.

...

Time had already moved from morning to afternoon.

The harshest sunlight had passed, but the hearts of those behind were agitated to the extreme.

"No spirit root, step down!"

"No spirit root, step down!"

One voice after another came from Zhou Haiye's mouth, having lost the gentleness of a clan elder from the beginning, replaced only by coldness and impatience.

This was related to the fact that his son Zhou Yuqi had not been favored by Mo Wanting and could not enter the Tiannan Dao Palace for cultivation.

He just wanted to finish the Spirit Root Testing Ceremony quickly now!

Fortunately, there were only two people left, and they were two familiar ones.

"Zhou Qingyu!"

"Here!"

Zhou Qingyu took a deep breath, glanced at her brother Zhou Fuyou, and under his encouraging gaze, stepped onto the high platform.

The formation's light swirled.

The girl's heart was anxious, hopeful... and in an instant, despairing!

In just a moment, the formation's light dissipated.

Such a fast speed, such a familiar sight—exactly the same as those peers without spirit roots.

Zhou Qingyu stiffly turned her head to look at Zhou Haiye.

He calmly spoke those four words.

"No spirit root."

The girl walked down from the high platform in a daze, somewhat absent-minded.

The gazes behind her were even hotter than the blazing sun in the sky.

She knew who the owners of those gazes were—her clansmen, Zhou Yuhao who called her "Sister Mao," and the friends she had played with for the past six years.

But from today onward, everything would change.

They could stay in the clan and continue cultivating, becoming lofty cultivators.

As for herself, she would have to go to Coral Town, that mortal settlement, and live an ordinary, mediocre life.

In a daze, a pair of somewhat cold hands landed on her head, making a familiar rubbing motion.

"Don't be afraid. I'm here."

The hand left. That person brushed past her.

Zhou Qingyu stopped in her tracks, her eyes red.

"Brother."

That back, though thin, seemed so tall at that moment, just like their father back then.

The moment he stepped onto the high platform, the formation suddenly activated!

In an instant!

A beam of azure-blue light surpassed the other four colors and rushed straight into the youth's body.

The compass in Zhou Haiye's hand spun wildly, trembling incessantly.

Such a fast reaction caught everyone off guard.

After all, just sensing spiritual qi required twelve-year-old youths to close their eyes, concentrate their minds, and calm their hearts, spending a long time before they could feel the spiritual qi corresponding to their spirit root attributes under the formation's assistance.

How could it be like Zhou Fuyou, who reacted in an instant?

Didn't this mean his affinity for spiritual qi had reached an extreme level?

Moreover, there was only one color!

Could it be...?

"Heavenly spirit root!"

"Water-attribute heavenly spirit root!"

"Zhou Fuyou actually possesses a uniquely favored Water-attribute heavenly spirit root! Doesn't that mean he's a guaranteed Sage in the future?"

"Our Zhou clan has a successor!"

For a moment, the entire venue was in an uproar, with exclamations one after another.

In the vast testing square, everyone's gaze was fixed on Zhou Fuyou alone.

The envy and jealousy of his peers, the delighted cheers of the clan elders, the shocked ecstasy of the Adept superiors.

Zhou Fuyou raised his head to meet Zhou Haiye's gaze.

On Seventh Uncle's face, an uncontrollable succession of expressions appeared: astonishment, shock, regret, and a fleeting murderous intent!

The moment their eyes met, Zhou Fuyou seemed oblivious and looked toward the viewing seats.

Clan leader Zhou Junxiao had a look of sudden realization on his face.

The two clan leaders, Lin and Yin, were much like the others—aside from surprise, there was also a trace of envy.

As for that woman?

Mo Wanting sat in her chair, raised an eyebrow, and a satisfied smile appeared on her face.

Compared to dual spirit root aptitude, a heavenly spirit root was ultimately a cut above.

Although it was said that heavenly spirit root cultivators found it difficult to become Ascendants, for the current Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, the fact that one could smoothly advance to the Sage stage was enough to value Zhou Fuyou.

After all, the current Heavenly Origin Dao Sect was desperately short of Sages. Otherwise, they wouldn't have bent the rules to allow former Star Gate guest elders to join the sect, nor would they have let Hui Yuanhong occupy a place among the four halls of the Tiannan Dao Palace.

As long as she brought Zhou Fuyou back, this trip would be equivalent to her bringing back a Sage.

"Good! This child is called Zhou Fuyou, right?"

Zhou Junxiao exhaled a turbid breath and steadied his mind. "Yes, he is also a fourth-generation clansman of the Zhou family, and has been known as a child prodigy since he was young..."

He wanted to continue introducing Zhou Fuyou's deeds, but the next moment he saw Mo Wanting lose her composure and stand up from her chair.

Zhou Junxiao was startled. Following her gaze, he looked at Zhou Fuyou again.

*Boom!*

Above the square, wind and clouds raged.

Amidst lightning and thunder, a torrential rain arrived abruptly.

And within that formation, a wisp of black light inexplicably appeared.

The moment this black light appeared, the four colors representing the Five Elements spiritual qi were all suppressed at once. Only the blue light of the Water attribute flowed upstream, merging into the black light.

"This..." Zhou Junxiao's mouth fell open. For a moment, he didn't react.

Mo Wanting's face changed color as she exclaimed, "An innate spirit body! And it's the innate spirit body most compatible with the Water spirit root! Is it the Grand Yin True Body? Or the Profound Nether Dao Body? It couldn't be the Water Origin Dao Embryo, could it!"

Names that only existed in ancient texts kept pouring out of the woman's mouth.

Each one represented uniqueness, represented boundless power, and even more so, represented the future of the Great Dao!

Mo Wanting could no longer restrain herself. "Now, immediately, right away, I want to take him back to the Tiannan Dao Palace!"

Zhou Junxiao quickly waved his hand to interrupt her. "Daoist Mo! Academy Head Mo! You are losing your composure!"

Mo Wanting let out a breath. "Apologies. It's truly too unexpected. If it were just a normal heavenly spirit root, I would not be so agitated. After all, the upper limit of a heavenly spirit root cultivator is only the Sage stage; it's very difficult to cultivate to the Ascendant realm. But if paired with a corresponding powerful physique, the shackles limiting the heavenly spirit root will be broken. In other words..."

Her gaze was fixed firmly on Zhou Fuyou.

"This child has the bearing of a great power!"

Zhou Junxiao drew in a sharp breath of cold air, not even noticing the change in her self-address. "Is that truly so?"

Mo Wanting nodded repeatedly. "Even if it's just the weakest Water Spirit Body, it would allow him to touch the threshold of Ascendant. If it's related to the Grand Yin True Body, the Profound Nether Dao Body, or even the legendary Water Origin Dao Embryo, then his path to Ascendant is a smooth road!"

After a brief explanation, Mo Wanting couldn't help herself again. "No, I must take him back to the Tiannan Dao Palace!"

Their conversation was not transmitted through divine sense but was spoken in front of everyone.

The entire square fell into silence.

Until the youth's calm voice came through.

"Senior, Fuyou does not wish to leave the clan for the time being."

Mo Wanting was stunned and asked incredulously, "What did you say?"

"This junior does not wish to leave the clan for the time being."

"Why?"

The youth did not answer. Instead, his gaze fell on the girl who was filled with surprise and adoration.

Mo Wanting frowned. "Because of your little sister? She has no spirit root and cannot cultivate. She is destined not to walk the same path as you."

Zhou Fuyou shook his head.

"You can go to the Dao Palace to cultivate first. Later, when your realm is accomplished, the sect will naturally reward you with pills that prolong life. That way, your little sister can also live for a very long time."

Zhou Fuyou still shook his head.

"Fine. Your little sister can go to the Dao Palace with you. I am willing to set this precedent for you!"

Zhou Fuyou was somewhat tempted. However, recalling the looks and expressions of the friends who had been close to his sister after she was found to have no spirit root, he still shook his head.

In an environment where everyone could cultivate and everyone was a genius, his sister's life would only be more difficult in the future.

Seeing his stubbornness, Mo Wanting couldn't help but flare up with anger.

"Do not think that just because you possess peerless talent, you can act willfully. You must know that there are countless geniuses in this world, but those who truly realize their talent are ultimately a minority. On the path of growth, one needs the guidance of a famous master, the support of resources, and the protection of strong practitioners. And all of this, only the Tiannan Dao Palace can give you!"

Zhou Fuyou hesitated for a moment but still insisted.

"I do not wish to leave now. When the time is right, I will naturally go to the Dao Palace. I hope the senior will be accommodating."

Mo Wanting wanted to say something more, but a vicissitudinous and mellow voice came leisurely, interrupting her.

"Little friend, just respect Fuyou's wishes! With this old man here, there is no need to worry about his safety."

Accompanying this voice came an overwhelmingly powerful pressure.

Everyone understood who the owner of this voice was.

The Zhou clan's old ancestor!

(Chapter End)
