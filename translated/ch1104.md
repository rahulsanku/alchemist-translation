# Chapter 1104: Collecting the Primordial Magnetic Divine Mountain, Refining the Lifebound Treasure Anew

Tidal Sea Demon City!

Once the gateway of the Demon Sect, now it was thick with demonic qi.

Tens of thousands of low-rank demon beasts were stationed within, strictly guarding against any outsider's prying eyes.

But even the tightest defense could not stop internal destruction.

On this day.

A beam of escaping light flew in from afar.

The third-rank demon kings stationed here all came forward to greet him.

"Lord Kongchan!"

"Amitabha!" Kongchan intoned a Buddhist chant, then swept his gaze across the crowd and issued an order that caught everyone off guard. "Everyone withdraw from Tidal Sea City immediately. No delays!"

The stationed demon kings were stunned. "But..."

Kongchan's brow furrowed. "Are you all trying to disobey orders?"

The demon kings exchanged glances, momentarily at a loss.

Kongchan suppressed his impatience. His master was watching his every move, after all.

"I'll give you three days. Lead all low-rank demon beasts and withdraw completely from Tidal Sea City. Otherwise, you'll bear the consequences!"

This time, no one dared to question him. They all assented.

Kongchan paid no further attention to the details of their retreat. He returned to his previous residence on his own.

Inside the Buddhist hall, a figure stood with hands clasped behind his back, silently gazing at a light screen before him.

Lines crisscrossed on the screen, profound and mysterious.

Kongchan stole a glance at the light screen, then cupped his hands and said, "Master, the evacuation order has been issued. In three days, the city will be empty of demons."

Luo Chen simply hummed in acknowledgment, his attention still on the light screen.

Kongchan couldn't help but ask, "Master was already able to freely pass through the Arctic Demon-Sealing Grand Formation before. Why are you studying it so carefully now?"

Without turning his head, Luo Chen said, "This formation was set up by Li Canghai, connecting the mountain and sea veins, drawing upon the creation of heaven and earth. Even though the earth energy from the Fallen Sea that was channeled here once damaged it, it has been gradually recovering under the power of time. The reason I could pass through before was merely thanks to the passage token Li Canghai granted me. But this time, what I am about to do will completely shake this formation. When heaven and earth collapse, the consequences will be unimaginable."

Kongchan nodded thoughtfully. So this was why Luo Chen had ordered him to have the demon race here withdraw?

Fortunately, the news of his submission to Luo Chen had not yet spread. The order still carried weight; otherwise, getting those low-rank demon beasts to leave wouldn't have gone so smoothly.

But what Luo Chen said about shaking the Arctic Demon-Sealing Grand Formation piqued his curiosity immensely.

What kind of major event could completely shake a formation that had once stumped a host of transformed great demons and even ancient demon grandmasters?

Luo Chen did not explain. His gaze continued to roam over the light screen.

With his current level of insight, he could see that this grand formation, possessing heaven-reaching and earth-spanning power, was not merely for sealing demons.

Within it, there was also the power to nurture earth energy and absorb the origin qi of heaven and earth!

The reason it had been briefly broken back then was that the power of the Fallen Sea had been channeled over, temporarily disrupting the balance, creating a gap.

But its self-repairing ability still existed. Hence, three hundred years later, it had gradually recovered to its original state.

And the key to this ability lay precisely in nurturing earth energy!

Thoughts flickered through Luo Chen's mind, leading to many associations.

Given Li Canghai's personality, it wasn't likely he was acting for the benefit of the Northern Sea cultivators.

The formation he had set up clearly wasn't just meant to seal away a group of great demons.

His goal should have been to repair the fifth-rank spirit vein of the Arctic Yemo Heaven, to find a good dwelling place for his future cultivation.

But this plan, after Luo Chen helped Li Canghai successfully claim the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range in Central Continent, had become optional.

Compared to Yemo Heaven, which had been beaten into ruins and needed time to nurture and recover, the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range was one of the world's few fifth-rank spirit veins. Its spiritual energy was abundant, its spiritual mechanisms flowing, far surpassing Yemo Heaven.

Luo Chen had felt this deeply after personally entering the Mountain-Sea Seal.

"So, my next move probably won't affect Li Canghai either."

Luo Chen smiled faintly and waved his hand, erasing the light screen.

The entire outline of the grand formation was now completely etched into his mind.

With the passage token Li Canghai had given him back then, he now had a fair grasp of how to break this formation.

When the time came, there would be no more obstacles!

...

Three days later, Tidal Sea Demon City, once thick with demonic qi, had become an empty city.

All the low-rank demon beasts, led by the demon kings, had withdrawn from the great city.

But two figures moved against the current, stepping into the Demon-Sealing Grand Formation.

Once inside the formation, to Kongchan's surprise, Luo Chen did not head straight for Yemo Heaven. Instead, he frequently wandered about within the formation.

From time to time, he would even strike out with formation flags.

With Kongchan's not-so-advanced knowledge of formations, he could still discern some clues from these unusual actions.

"Is Master blocking the formation eyes, cutting off the flow of spiritual energy?"

"Is he trying to break the formation?"

"But if he's breaking it, why not just destroy the formation eyes directly?"

Doubts filled his mind, but Luo Chen did not explain the details to him. Instead, he took out even more formation tools and had Kongchan help with the work.

Thus, a full three months later, the two finally passed through the entire formation and arrived before the Primordial Magnetic Mountain.

Looking at the Radiant Light Beasts that had once again gathered on the Primordial Magnetic Mountain, chasing the light as they moved, Kongchan felt a surge of emotion.

The former Primordial Demon Sect, backed by the Millennium-Descended Fallen Sea and guarded by the Primordial Magnetic Divine Light that could suppress the Five Elements, had been the most impregnable fortress.

No one had expected it to become the first of the Five Sacred Lands to fall.

"Master, these Radiant Light Beasts are still few in number, nothing to fear. Let's go straight in!"

However.

Luo Chen stopped before the Primordial Magnetic Mountain, his gaze focused.

"No. We'll do it here!"

"Huh?" Kongchan was puzzled.

Luo Chen smiled and pointed at the great mountain before him, which emitted a hazy gray light. "My goal has never been Yemo Heaven, but this mountain!"

Kongchan's mouth fell open involuntarily.

After recovering, he said worriedly, "The Primordial Magnetic Mountain is formed entirely from a Primordial Magnetic ore vein. The Primordial Magnetic Divine Light it naturally emits restrains all Five Elements power under heaven. Although its power is boundless, no one in this world has ever been able to master this force. Even the Primordial Demon Sect only used it as a portal barrier. Legend has it that only an Ascendant grandmaster with five spirit roots could, by borrowing the mutual generation of the Five Elements, counter their mutual restraint. But a five-spirit-root aptitude—let alone reaching Ascendant, even forming a core or a Sage..."

His words came to an abrupt halt.

Because on Luo Chen's body, strands of dharma power of varying colors began to flicker.

The strongest was clearly fire-attribute dharma power.

Then came water and wood dual attributes, followed by the emergence of earth-attribute dharma power.

The final metal-attribute dharma power condensed rather slowly, as if Luo Chen was transforming it bit by bit through some great means, appearing somewhat laborious.

But even so, it still made Kongchan's eyes widen in utter disbelief!

Five Elements dharma power!

Five spirit roots!

This master of his was actually a five-spirit-root Ascendant grandmaster, something rarely seen in the Mountains and Seas Realm for millions of years!

At this moment, Luo Chen's attention was focused on himself.

Indeed!

After comprehending the power of four of the Five Elements laws, his essence as a Five-Spirit Dao Body was no longer suppressed and could begin to display its might.

Even though he still lacked a metal origin to fully unlock the Five-Spirit Dao Body, by borrowing the principle of mutual generation among the Five Elements, he could already smoothly convert metal-attribute dharma power.

Thus, the Primordial Magnetic Divine Light had lost its restraining effect on him.

What he needed to do next was simple.

"Collect the Primordial Magnetic Divine Mountain, refine the lifebound treasure anew, and by borrowing the Primordial Magnetic Divine Light's power to restrain all Five Elements under heaven, enhance the Primordial Unity Cauldron's ability to suppress!"

This was Luo Chen's ultimate goal for this trip!

The wondrous uses of the Primordial Magnetic Divine Light were known to all.

Even when Luo Chen was still in the Qi Refinement stage, he had witnessed a glimpse of it at a small Primordial Magnetic Valley in the Gu Yuan Mountain Range.

And the might of this Primordial Magnetic Mountain before the Primordial Demon Sect's mountain gate was hundreds or thousands of times greater than that small valley!

But unfortunately, for thousands of years, no one in the Demon Sect had been able to drive this great mountain. They could only set it aside, barely using it as a portal barrier.

Luo Chen, however, was different. He possessed five spirit roots, and his physique had once been reshaped into a Five-Spirit Dao Body, making him completely unaffected.

Moreover, his lifebound treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, had been crafted from the very beginning with a comprehensive approach, incorporating a large amount of Five Elements materials, allowing it to perfectly bear the Primordial Magnetic power.

If he could smelt this mountain into his lifebound treasure, then, not to mention anything else, its power of suppression would surely be greatly enhanced.

When that time came!

This treasure might even be able to play a role in battles at the Ascendant level.

The preparations were complete.

Without further ado!

Luo Chen got straight to work!

Opening his mouth, he spat out a streak of spiritual light.

It grew as it caught the wind, and in an instant, it transformed into a gray giant cauldron, hovering high above the divine mountain.

Luo Chen's dharma power surged, and the Five Elements dharma power coalesced into five chains, each a thousand zhang long, binding the Primordial Magnetic Mountain from all directions.

Then, he pointed a finger at his own chest.

Under Kongchan's terrified gaze, Luo Chen's physical body swelled and grew wildly.

In the blink of an eye, a giant nearly a thousand zhang tall stood between heaven and earth.

Among the demon race, there had always been a saying that size determined strength, or rather, that tonnage was combat power!

As a late-stage transformed great demon emperor, Kongchan's original body was already enormous.

But compared to Luo Chen at this moment, he was still three parts inferior.

The meaning of this, Kongchan understood all too well.

In the path of body refinement, Luo Chen surpassed even the demon race by three parts!

But this wasn't the end!

Luo Chen's nostrils spewed hot breath, and the muscles on his body trembled.

On his shoulders and neck, two fleshy lumps bulged, slowly emerging as two heads with identical features but different auras.

At his waist and ribs, like millstone-sized palms, flesh slowly split open, and they extended outward.

Seeing this, Kongchan couldn't help but groan.

"Is this the legendary true form of my master?"

"Three heads and six arms, the Formless Demon Luo!"

Luo Chen stood before the Primordial Magnetic Divine Mountain, his eyes filled with determination.

The next moment!

He clenched his fists and slammed them heavily onto the hard ground.

Strands of blood-colored source power were added, spreading out like waves, layer upon layer, centered on the struck ground.

Boom!

Boom!

Boom!

After twelve consecutive strikes, the earth heaved and the mountains trembled.

The Arctic Demon-Sealing Grand Formation behind him seemed to stir, its formation power about to spread. However, the formation flags previously placed at various formation eyes now played their role.

The flow of spiritual energy was cut off, blocking the power of the Demon-Sealing Formation.

Only now did Kongchan understand the purpose of Luo Chen's earlier actions.

This Arctic Demon-Sealing Grand Formation not only drew upon the origin qi of heaven and earth but had also once connected to the Primordial Magnetic Divine Mountain, stealing the mountain's power of suppression.

That was how, back then, it had cooperated with the Soul-Refining Ancestor's formation to seal the billion-strong demon army within Yemo Heaven.

But now, the connection between the formation and the Primordial Magnetic Divine Mountain had been severed by Luo Chen.

As the divine mountain trembled.

Luo Chen seized the opportunity. His six arms shot out simultaneously, grasping the mountain's waist in the air.

The spiritual power chains formed by the Five Elements dharma power also bound the great mountain tightly at this moment.

His three heads all opened their mouths, exhaling and shouting.

"Rise!"

Under Kongchan's worshipful and awestruck gaze, the Primordial Magnetic Mountain, which had sat here for eons, slowly rose from the ground amidst endless rumbling!

At the same time.

The gray cauldron suspended in the nine heavens began to rain down strands of gray qi, scattering over the ancient mountain.

Time passed, bit by bit.

On the Primordial Magnetic Mountain, large amounts of earth and stone peeled away, and mountain rocks shattered.

Revealing its most core existence.

A great deal of Primordial Magnetic Divine Light also burst forth, but before the Primordial Unity Cauldron, it had little effect.

This scene of refinement lasted for nine days and nine nights.

When the sun rose on the tenth day.

The once magnificent Primordial Magnetic Mountain had vanished, leaving only a round, gray cauldron, as if it had eaten its fill, suspended in the sky.

Luo Chen reached out and beckoned.

The cauldron slowly shrank and descended into his palm.

"Hmm..."

As the cauldron entered his hand, Luo Chen couldn't help but grunt.

His entire body sank, and he staggered a step.

Heavy!

The first feeling was heaviness!

Then, when his divine sense penetrated it, the second feeling was "stagnation."

His lifebound treasure, which had always been responsive and moved at his will, now gave him a hazy feeling, making it difficult to command as effortlessly as before.

"It seems the path of smelting is still long!"

Luo Chen let out a long breath. His massive body rapidly shrank, and there was no worry on his face, only joy.

As for how to completely smelt the Primordial Magnetic Mountain into the Primordial Unity Cauldron, he already had a plan in mind.

The set of artifact-refining methods Li Canghai had given him would play a huge role in the upcoming plan.

Although he couldn't create a spirit treasure like the Mountain-Sea Seal from scratch like Li Canghai could, the Primordial Unity Cauldron itself had a solid foundation. As long as he smelted in compatible materials, it might one day be promoted to the rank of spirit treasure!

"Master!"

Kongchan cautiously approached.

"Congratulations, Master! Congratulations on acquiring the divine mountain!"

Luo Chen laughed heartily, his mood quite cheerful.

He cast out a dharma power shield, enveloping Kongchan.

Kongchan was puzzled.

But then, he understood everything.

Behind them, the Arctic Demon-Sealing Grand Formation, having lost the Primordial Magnetic Divine Mountain—its most important formation eye—began to collapse in an instant.

Boom!

With a single earth-shattering explosion.

This grand formation, which had existed for several hundred years, collapsed and shattered.

A terrifying storm of origin qi swept out.

One side rushed into Yemo Heaven, the other into Tidal Sea City.

The ancient glaciers crumbled layer by layer under this terrifying storm.

The thousand-year-old majestic city, like paper, was leveled to the ground.

Within the storm, a flying shuttle broke through the air, heading straight into the nine heavens.

"Master, are we heading to the Swallow Islands next?"

"Yes. Before returning to the Eastern Wilderness, I need to see an old friend. I think the time has come."

The voices of question and answer gradually faded into the boundless storm.

This storm would continue for decades to come. When it finally dissipated, Yemo Heaven, which had only existed in the legends of the new generation of Northern Sea cultivators, would have its doors thrown wide open.

The ruins of the Primordial Demon Sect, once frozen in ice, would also become a treasure-hunting secret realm that Northern Sea cultivators would flock to.

(End of chapter)
