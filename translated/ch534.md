# Chapter 534: True Flame Pill, Sevenfold Blazing Heaven Rings

---

When Luo Chen killed Yan Nantian and overawed the cultivators of the Swallow Islands into submission, it meant he would possess a long stretch of peaceful cultivation time.

In such a remote place, as long as no large-scale beast tide struck and no extreme oceanic calamity descended, a single Adept cultivator was enough to throw his weight around, proclaim himself king, and rule as hegemon.

Roughly speaking, it was equivalent to Pang Renxiong coming west with one sword and suppressing Dahe Market back then.

The only difference was probably that the Swallow Islands' area and number of cultivators far exceeded Dahe Market, and its overall scale was only slightly inferior to Tianlan.

That was precisely why Luo Chen had taken interest in this territory.

A region only slightly worse than Tianlan, with a natural lower third-tier spirit vein present, was more than enough to support an early-stage Adept cultivator.

Even himself, who was comparable to a gold-devouring beast.

After the Cheng clan delivered the first batch of medicinal materials, Luo Chen immediately entered the work of refining True Flame Pills at full speed.

For this refinement, he still did not use the enormous number of Achievement Points accumulated on his attribute panel.

After refining the fourth-tier Transformation Pill, his panel already evaluated him as a "4th-Tier Alchemist."

Even though he had mastered only that one fourth-tier pill, with his extremely solid foundation in the Dao of alchemy, the title of 4th-Tier Alchemist was still deserved.

Therefore, refining a third-tier pill was not actually too troublesome for him.

As long as he entered the door, everything would be easy to discuss.

From arriving at the Swallow Islands, to killing Yan Nantian, to waiting for medicinal materials, seven months had passed in total.

During these seven months, aside from cultivation, Luo Chen had basically spent all his spare time researching the True Flame Pill formula.

Now that the raw materials had arrived, he could begin practical work directly.

"After Dazzling Light Seeds are peeled, their medicinal nature is preserved for too short a time. Refine them first."

"This Yama Flower is the noble main ingredient of the True Flame Pill. It should be processed with Burning Crane."

"Fire Ginseng is rare, so it can only be substituted with Iron Rock Ginseng, which has a stronger dry nature. But in that case, the involved alchemy techniques must be switched three times."

Every time Luo Chen processed a medicinal material, his mind would produce the alchemy technique, timing, and fire control to use later during refinement.

Even though he had not truly begun refining, his mind had unknowingly simulated the process hundreds and thousands of times.

By the time he finished processing this entire batch of medicinal materials, a full month had passed.

Luo Chen was somewhat dissatisfied. Without anyone to help process medicinal materials, things were this troublesome.

But for now, this was all he could do.

After the medicinal materials were processed, he immediately began refinement without pause.

Once, twice, three times...

The complexity of refining a third-tier pill far exceeded ordinary first-tier and second-tier pills.

Thus, the refinement time also exceeded the usual one or two double-hours.

To refine one furnace of True Flame Pills, Luo Chen estimated that it required at least a full day.

This duration was even longer than the fourth-tier Transformation Pill.

For no other reason than that if the True Flame Pill was to possess enormous medicinal power, aside from the raw materials, once the pill formed, it still had to be "simmered" inside the cauldron with source true fire for a full three double-hours.

Only in this way would the True Flame Pill possess medicinal power far surpassing ordinary third-tier cultivation pills.

Luo Chen had long since mentally prepared himself for this situation.

Alchemy was like cultivation. The higher the realm, the longer a single seclusion lasted. Pills were the same. The higher the grade, the longer refinement might take.

In legend, the Nine-Turn Golden Pill, known as "one swallowed into the belly, straight up to the azure clouds," required a full four thousand nine hundred years to refine one furnace.

Of course, that was myth and could not be trusted.

After all, what pill could make a mere mortal directly ascend to immortality? Leaving aside whether it possessed such great power, the medicinal power contained in such a pill was not something an ordinary person could endure.

But Luo Chen also clearly knew that the higher a pill's grade, the more time refinement consumed.

When refining Nascent Soul Pills in Green Pill Valley, it took as little as three days and as much as five or six days.

And that had been the refinement time without full success.

For the True Flame Pill to require only one day was already quite good.

"No wonder high-tier alchemists always have such high status."

"Leaving aside how useful the pills they refine are, merely the fact that they expend so much energy and time, delaying their own cultivation, is enough for major forces to give high-tier alchemists honored positions."

"But in that case, the number of existences who can ultimately reach sufficient attainment at the peak of the Dao of alchemy will probably grow fewer and fewer."

One day did not seem like much.

But accumulated bit by bit, who knew how many years would be delayed and how much lifespan consumed?

And this was only a third-tier pill. What about Sage pills in the future? Ascendant pills?

In the vast Mountains and Seas Realm, over ten thousand years, only one existence known as the Alchemy Saint had emerged.

Seen from this perspective, such delays truly did affect the realm advancement of high-tier alchemists.

"Perhaps I need to find a way to hand the work of processing raw materials to people below me, so as not to waste more of my time."

"I need to choose someone trustworthy... Actually, they do not need to be very trustworthy. As long as I place them on Moon Inviting Island and monitor them at all times, I need not fear them causing trouble."

"In that case, things will be much easier."

Changing his previous thinking, Luo Chen still planned to form a group of people to solve the tedious work of processing raw materials for him.

And the leader was simple enough. Cheng Haixin, who had only recently reached Initiate, would do.

This woman had a simple mind, like a sheet of blank paper. He could paint on it however he pleased.

As for those doing the assisting work?

That was not difficult either. Among the tens of thousands of cultivators and millions of mortals in the Swallow Islands, selecting some physicians, alchemists, or alchemy apprentices would definitely not be hard.

Perhaps some would be unwilling.

But as long as their work was linked to contributions toward Luo Chen, there should be no complaints.

Perhaps the major clans would even enthusiastically send people over.

After all, the more contribution they had, the earlier they could exchange for the Azure Yang Alchemy Canon on the stone stele.

"Hm? It failed?"

Luo Chen muttered. He had not expected this furnace of True Flame Pills to fail.

He had clearly done everything so perfectly earlier.

While cleaning up the medicinal dregs, he looked at the Primordial Unity Cauldron, which had been burned red-hot, and revealed a thoughtful look.

"So that is how it is."

"The refinement of the True Flame Pill has two major difficulties. Earlier, I only paid attention to the fusion of medicinal power and overlooked the process of simmering the pill over a low flame."

"It looks like a simple finishing step, but in truth it is the step that gradually draws the medicinal power inward and concentrates it."

"I understand."

With those words, a comfortable smile appeared on Luo Chen's face.

He was very confident that the next time he refined True Flame Pills, he would have a ninety percent success rate.

However, he did not strike while the iron was hot and continue opening the furnace.

Another day had passed, delaying cultivation. That would not do.

With his current cultivation speed, every day delayed was no small loss. Luo Chen could not bear that.

Pills were ultimately only support. Daily cultivation was the most critical part of slow, grinding effort. He must not reverse root and branch.

Aside from qi cultivation and meditation, Luo Chen's cultivation schedule was also packed.

Body cultivation.

Refining the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Developing a fire-based defensive spell.

One item after another. Whenever he had free time, Luo Chen never stopped.

And he truly felt the steady, grounded sensation of advancing in every plan.

For cultivation, he had the heavenly-spirit-root speed brought by the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture.

For body cultivation, after obtaining the blood food Cheng Haixin supplied every month, that previously difficult body refinement speed finally began to rise slowly. It was little, but far better than before.

As for refining his life-bound treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, the results became increasingly obvious.

Especially after he began using the cauldron to refine True Flame Pills in large batches. The azure flame burned it for a day at a time, and great quantities of pill qi stained it. The impurities within the Primordial Unity Cauldron grew fewer and fewer, and its connection to his mind and spirit grew deeper.

This precisely confirmed what Tao Wan of Green Pill Valley had told him back then: the Primordial Unity Cauldron could be further nurtured and refined through alchemy.

For an alchemist, this was absolutely the most suitable life-bound treasure, bar none.

Luo Chen even felt that the day when he could freely and perfectly control the Primordial Unity Cauldron was not far off.

Lastly was the development of a fire-based defensive spell. Luo Chen had already found some clues.

When he had still been in Qi Refinement, while researching the Fireball Art, he had displayed a series of embryonic defensive spells such as Fire Shield Art and Fire Ring Art.

Now, after over a hundred years of accumulation and after reading many spell classics, supplementing and perfecting them was not particularly difficult.

The only thing restricting him was probably that Luo Chen wanted to integrate the azure flame into it.

The more he cultivated the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture, the more Luo Chen felt that he could develop and utilize his source true fire in many areas.

This fire was not limited to alchemy, artifact refining, and body refinement.

It could also be applied to both offense and defense.

Several days later.

On Moon Inviting Island.

Thick white mist abruptly parted.

Layer after layer of firelight spread like ripples.

Looking closely, they were fire serpents, one after another, linked head to tail, serpent heads biting serpent tails, sketching rings.

At first glance, there were three rings formed from three enormous fire serpents.

The instant the three rings took shape, a strange force lingered in the void.

A high-grade flying sword came from above and drilled into the fire rings. Before it could pass through the first ring, it turned into a puddle of molten iron and flowed onto the ground.

Looking at this scene, Luo Chen revealed a satisfied smile on the little island.

This three-ring spell was precisely the fire-based defensive spell he had recently researched and roughly formed.

Its foundation was the transformation of the Fireball Art's form. That was not difficult. In his early years, he had already researched "Flying Fire Glowflies." Now it had merely become the shape of fire serpents.

Pure and tyrannical mana was the key component.

But the reason this spell possessed a third-tier core the moment it was first researched lay in the integration of the azure flame.

Because it possessed the azure flame, these fire serpent rings could devour or erase the mana within an enemy's attack when resisting it.

If the enemy's weapon was not of sufficient quality, it would even be directly burned away by the fire serpent rings.

If it were only that, it would be one thing.

But Luo Chen felt that this spell still had much unfinished potential.

Third tier?

It did not seem to be its end.

Looking at the spell section of his attribute panel, where a hazy entry slowly appeared, Luo Chen thought briefly and gave it a name.

"Let us call it Sevenfold Blazing Heaven Rings."

He was saving trouble.

In the cultivation world, there was already a fire-based defensive spell called Sevenfold Blazing Heaven Rings, but its grade was only second tier.

In terms of defensive ability, it was far inferior to Glazed Heavenly Canopy.

Luo Chen had previously browsed the spell manual for Sevenfold Blazing Heaven Rings, but had had no intention of cultivating it. After all, at the time he had already mastered Glazed Heavenly Canopy. There had been no reason to abandon the near for the far.

Now, his self-developed defensive spell had some shadow of Sevenfold Blazing Heaven Rings, so naming it this was normal.

The only abnormal thing was probably that Luo Chen could currently release only three Withering-Bloom Fire Serpent Rings.

"It has only just reached Beginner. Perhaps once I raise its proficiency, I can gain a few more defensive fire rings."

"In addition, this art can still incorporate the principles of some other spells. Perhaps its grade can rise to the fourth tier."

When it came to spells, Luo Chen's confidence exceeded even his confidence in alchemy.

All along, he had considered himself to possess powerful talent in spells. Aside from those whose grade far exceeded his realm, he had almost never used Achievement Points to enter a spell.

If he truly could, at the Adept stage, gather a hundred streams, take the strengths of each, and create a fourth-tier spell, that would also count as an achievement.

It would be no less than when he created Myriad Path Convergence and the Fine Dust Primordial Art.

However, Luo Chen was temporarily powerless to continue raising the spell's grade.

With his accumulation, he had barely reached this point for now. Going forward, he still needed to read more books and practice more.

A third-tier defensive spell was not enough by current standards, but if cultivated to Perfected Mastery and integrated with the azure flame, it would be very much enough.

After it appeared as Beginner on the panel, Luo Chen was no longer anxious. He would slowly raise its proficiency later.

Fortunately, it was a defensive spell. He did not need to find an open area and unleash it at full power. He could practice it on Moon Inviting Island.

He turned and entered the alchemy room.

Clang!

The Primordial Unity Cauldron landed on the ground. Its gray belly was no longer round and plump, having drawn in somewhat.

Although it was still chubby, it had finally gained a tiny trace of solemn dignity.

A cauldron was authority.

Solemn majesty was its essence. How could it have an adorably naive appearance?

After inspecting the Primordial Unity Cauldron, Luo Chen began his daily alchemy.

This time, he was determined to succeed.

...

"Seventh Sister, has the list been finalized?"

"It has. The Xu clan has the best relationship with us, so I gave them three slots. The other thirty-some forces only received fifteen slots in total. However, I told them clearly that if the first batch selected performs poorly, they will be directly screened out and replaced by alchemists from other clans."

Looking at the gentle woman organizing the list, Cheng Dou showed excitement on his face and paced continuously through the flower hall.

He kept muttering to himself.

"You handled it correctly. It does not excessively offend the major forces, while also perfectly completing Devil Lord Azure Yang's requirement."

"I was previously troubled over how to gather the contribution value of six thousand True Flame Pill raw material sets without other clans being willing to pay an even greater price. Now that we have the matter of helping process medicinal materials, perhaps we can greatly shorten the time needed to exchange for the Azure Yang Alchemy Canon."

"Seventh Sister, once you are there, you cannot merely watch. You reached Initiate in your thirties, so your outstanding aptitude goes without saying. However, in the early years, you put all your energy into cultivation, and even now you have no particular skill."

"For cultivators like us, once our realms rise, we more or less cultivate a secondary craft."

"Who would have thought Devil Lord Azure Yang was actually a formidable high-tier alchemist? Take this opportunity to learn properly from him. If you can obtain true transmission, then my Cheng clan..."

"If you cannot become his concubine, being his disciple would be fine too. In any case, both serve before his knees. Those near the water get the moon first."

Seeing her elder brother's words grow more and more absurd, Cheng Haixin's face shifted between green and red.

What did he mean, if she could not become his concubine, being his disciple would be fine too?

Her, Cheng Haixin's, aptitude was first-rate even across the Swallow Islands. Had things really come to this?

"Elder Brother!"

"Hm, what is it?" Cheng Dou raised his head blankly.

Cheng Haixin frowned. The words reached her lips, but she swallowed them back.

Forget it. Why argue with her own brother?

Even if they truly quarreled, it would eventually return to the same arguments, without anything new.

She shook her head and stood, revealing a pair of long, slender legs.

"I am going to Moon Inviting Island. I fear I will no longer be able to look after the clan."

Cheng Dou waved a hand. "Do not worry. Although I am busy with the merchant caravan, Uncle Ji is here at the clan. He is old, so he will not go to sea recently."

Cheng Haixin hummed, stepped on her long legs, and left the flower hall.

Before long, a streak of escaping light rose and flew toward Moon Inviting Island.

As soon as she landed, a dozen voices rang out in unison.

"Greetings, Fairy Haixin!"

"Greetings, Senior Cheng!"

Cheng Haixin looked at the group before her. There were white-haired elders in their dotage, deft-handed women, and young boys not yet capped.

At this moment, all of them bowed respectfully to her.

Cheng Haixin knew their respect was not because of the difference in realm, but because she represented Devil Lord Azure Yang.

A subtle feeling rose in her heart.

The woman pursed her lips and said gently, "Everyone is prepared, yes? Then follow me onto the island."

As she spoke, she flipped her wrist, and one Water-Parting Spike fell into her hand.

After spiritual power was poured in, the Water-Parting Spike drilled into the lake.

The next moment, a blue flood dragon several zhang long emerged, shaking head and tail.

It was precisely the water-parting, dragon-transforming method contained within the pair of Water-Parting Spikes Luo Chen had bestowed.

The woman leapt onto the dragon's head.

At her signal, the others climbed onto the dragon's body one after another.

Then, under Cheng Haixin's control, the water dragon swam across the ten-li calm lake and entered Moon Inviting Island, shrouded in white mist.

She brought the group to the medicine room where medicinal materials were stored.

Before she could take the initiative to pay respects to Luo Chen, she heard a short whistle of heartfelt joy from the neighboring alchemy room.

"What happened?"

Just as everyone was surprised, Luo Chen, dressed in a loose, casual white robe, walked over from the alchemy room next door, smiling broadly.

"You are here."

"This concubine greets Senior." Cheng Haixin bowed gracefully. Looking at Luo Chen's excited face, she followed the thread and asked, "Senior is so delighted. Could it be that your pill refinement succeeded?"

Luo Chen grinned. "Haha, Haixin has quite the clear and clever heart. That's right. Today, I successfully refined the third-tier True Flame Pill. Such a joyous event deserves a great cup of wine."

When these words came out, Cheng Haixin did not react much, but the group behind her all showed shock.

A third-tier pill.

This legendary Devil Lord Azure Yang truly was a third-tier alchemist.

Cheng Haixin herself knew little about the Dao of alchemy and did not understand the weight of it. She only followed her heart and said, "Good wine should naturally be paired with good dishes. This concubine will order people to prepare delicacies and celebrate for Senior."

"Haha, Haixin is thoughtful."

Luo Chen laughed heartily, then waved his sleeve.

"No rush. First, let me see what skill this group has."

As he spoke, he reached out.

At once, a large pile of medicinal materials flew one after another from the shelves in the medicine room.

As though possessing spirituality, the medicinal materials flew before the eighteen people.

Luo Chen's smile faded, and he said seriously, "Next, you will process medicinal materials for me on the spot. I will tell you the corresponding standards in advance. If anyone fails to meet them..."

Luo Chen did not finish the sentence.

But no one present was a fool. If they truly failed to meet the standard, nine times out of ten they would be driven from Moon Inviting Island and lose the chance to earn contribution for their clan.

For a time, the medicine room became a scene of bustling work.

All eighteen were famous figures in the Swallow Islands, and their learned skills were basically related to alchemy.

At this moment, all of them took out specialized tools and corresponding auxiliary materials from their storage bags to process the medicinal materials.

Fortunately, what Luo Chen gave them were the lowest first-tier medicinal materials, and ordinary common ones at that. Processing them was not particularly difficult.

Time slowly passed.

Gradually, people finished one after another.

Those who finished stood anxiously to the side.

Those who remained quickened their pace.

Less than half a double-hour later, Luo Chen stood before the eighteen processed medicinal materials and inspected them one by one.

"Good appearance. Meets standard."

"Meets standard."

"Passable."

"Fire control was inadequate. This medicinal material is already ruined. You..." Luo Chen glanced at the luxuriously dressed middle-aged man and shook his head.

The man's face turned snow-white.

"Devil Lord, I am a first-tier alchemist. I can refine mid-grade Condensed Water Pills. I am very capable."

Luo Chen frowned. What he wanted were assistants. Being able to process medicinal materials was enough.

Alchemists?

Too lazy to say more to him, he gave Cheng Haixin a look, and she immediately stepped forward to handle the person.

Luo Chen moved on, looking all the way down.

Most were still qualified. Only two people failed, and coincidentally, both possessed the identity of first-tier alchemists.

"It seems those small forces are trying to send alchemists over to steal teachings."

Luo Chen laughed despite himself.

Most alchemists raised by these clans began directly with refining pills. The medicinal materials they used had already been processed, so naturally they lacked the relevant ability.

Failure was only natural.

At last, Luo Chen stopped before a red-lipped, white-toothed youth.

He lowered his head and looked at that medicinal material, a strange light appearing in his eyes.

"This Golden Branch seems to have failed, yet some of its medicinal nature remains. A strange processing method."

"What is your name?"

The youth's teeth chattered as he said, "My name is Xu Muxian. I am a collateral member of the Xu clan. I am proficient in processing medicinal materials, and I also know how to refine Fasting Powder and Five-Grain Pills."

Fasting Powder and Five-Grain Pills were both unranked pills and not particularly unusual.

However, at the other party's age of fifteen or sixteen, being proficient in processing medicinal materials while also able to refine two kinds of pills was actually quite decent.

"Can you answer me? What method did you use to process this Golden Branch?"

Xu Muxian hesitated, but still answered, "I soaked it in Red Rock Liquid, but I misjudged its quality, so I failed."

Red Rock Liquid was a fire-attribute spiritual liquid, generally formed when magma from undersea volcanoes fused with undersea spirit springs.

Using fire to counter metal. Not only had he comprehended the principle of mutual restraint among the five elements, he had boldly applied it.

He was a good seedling.

Luo Chen looked deeply at the youth. "You stay."

Xu Muxian, who had already given up hope, could not help revealing extreme surprise and joy upon hearing those three words.

Luo Chen walked back to Cheng Haixin. "Most of them have no problem. Add two replacements later. At that time, there will be no need to trouble me. Have this man help check them."

As he spoke, Luo Chen pointed to the old man in his dotage who had been the first to complete processing.

Cheng Haixin nodded. "Elder Qi is indeed very capable. He is a famous physician throughout the Swallow Islands."

Luo Chen hummed and walked out of the medicine room on his own.

After Cheng Haixin instructed the group on when to begin work, she followed Luo Chen.

Watching her departing back, envy appeared on the face of Xu Muxian among the crowd.

As everyone rode small boats away from Moon Inviting Island, Elder Qi suddenly grabbed the youth's arm.

"Elder Qi?"

"Boy, your fortune has arrived. The Devil Lord looked at you differently. Perhaps in the future, he might teach you a little alchemy."

Xu Muxian asked, half-believing and half-doubting, "Really?"

Elder Qi smiled kindly. "Could that be false? When we processed those medicinal materials, we relied on experience and established methods. You, however, created something different. Although you failed, you showed spirituality. These great figures sometimes favor seedlings with spirituality."

"But I am only at Qi Refinement Stage Two. Can I truly enter the Devil Lord's eyes?" Xu Muxian was still lacking confidence.

Elder Qi shook his head. "Do not worry too much. In that man's eyes, there is little difference between Initiate and Qi Refinement cultivators like us. As long as you work steadily from now on, there will eventually be a day you rise."

At the end, he added:

"If there is anything you do not understand, you can ask me first."

Looking at the youth's ignorant yet delighted appearance, Elder Qi could not help sighing inwardly.

Supposedly, Xu Muxian was not well regarded in the Xu clan.

He clearly had talent as an alchemist, yet no one supported his alchemy, so he could only make a living by rubbing together the lowest-grade Fasting Powder and Five-Grain Pills.

Today, Elder Qi would treat it as doing a good deed and forming a good connection.

If this youth was truly favored by Devil Lord Azure Yang in the future, then his bet today might perhaps bless the descendants of the Qi clan.

In the cultivation world, great figures had the lives of great figures, and small figures had the struggles of small figures.

Luo Chen did not know what kind of life-changing opportunity his casual thought would bring a youth.

He only knew that he was very hot right now.

In the sleeping hall, Luo Chen's face was flushed red, almost like a boiled lobster. Streams of hot air escaped from the top of his head.

Inside his body, a low-grade True Flame Pill was being refined, releasing its medicinal power.

The medicinal power was enormous, striking his extraordinary meridians, eight vessels, orifices, membranes, flesh, and blood like streams of flame.

The gurgling sound was like rivers rushing.

Stimulated by it, even his qi sea churned slightly.

Only after a long while did Luo Chen slowly open his eyes, a wave of hot air gradually leaving his mouth.

"As expected of the True Flame Pill."

"Such enormous medicinal power. Such tyrannical medicinal nature."

Aside from joy and praise, a trace of lingering fear remained in his eyes.
