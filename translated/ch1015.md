# Chapter 1015: Canglong's Intent, Nine Tribulations Three Nirvanas

Within the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land.

Where the alchemy mountain once stood, a vast lake spanning a hundred li now lay.

The lake's surface was calm, without a single ripple. The water was pitch black, as if it could devour everything.

Nether Lake!

That was the name of this lake.

Originally, there was no Nether Lake within the blessed land. It had been born nine years ago.

On that day, the entire Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land had trembled. A massive rift had appeared at the limits of the sky, and a vast quantity of black water and golden flames had poured in from the outside, nearly possessing the momentum to destroy the entire blessed land space.

At that time, Luo Tian Sect's Sect Leader Li Yingzhang had urgently summoned all disciples, already prepared to flee the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land.

But not long after, the heavenly anomaly had vanished.

The rift in the sky had naturally healed itself. All the terrifying golden flames had disappeared. Only the sea of black water remained, forming this awe-inspiring lake.

It was said.

The heavenly anomaly nine years ago had been caused by the Supreme Elder's cultivation, and in the end, it was the Supreme Elder himself who had personally intervened to resolve it.

But precisely because of this, the Supreme Elder had suffered backlash. Gravely wounded, he had fallen into a deep slumber.

Nether Lake was the Supreme Elder's resting place.

As for when he would awaken, the disciples of Luo Tian Sect had no way of knowing.

But the entire sect had designated Nether Lake as a forbidden area, completely sealed off, with powerful cultivators patrolling and guarding it day and night.

This guard was not merely superficial. The arrangement of personnel alone showed how seriously the sect treated this matter.

A permanent Sage elder was stationed there, along with five Adept cultivators. In addition, a large number of Initiate true cultivators constantly roamed the outer perimeter, preventing anyone from approaching.

The rotation for patrol and guard duty was tentatively set at ten years per shift.

For this first round, Canglong Fairy of Heavenly Mountain Peak had volunteered to take on the task.

In her words, she had only recently stabilized her cultivation base. Relying solely on cultivation to improve her first-layer Sage realm was not very effective, and she had not contributed much to Luo Tian Sect. Guarding Luo Chen this time would be her way of doing her small part.

Under the night sky.

Canglong stood quietly by the lakeshore, gazing at the lake surface with a complex expression, lost in thought.

Light footsteps sounded behind her. Without turning her head, Canglong asked, "Have you checked everything?"

Jueqing Fairy hummed in acknowledgment: "All the formations are fine. No one can break in. Besides, we're inside the blessed land, protected by the entire Luo Tian Sect. Who could possibly force their way in?"

Canglong said calmly, "We must still guard against the unexpected."

Jueqing Fairy walked up beside her, curiously studying Canglong's gentle profile. "Senior Sister, are you very worried about him?"

"How could I not be? He is the founder of Luo Tian Sect, its spiritual symbol. It is precisely because of him that everything can be unified. If something were to happen to him, the current Luo Tian Sect would surely fall apart!"

"Sigh, is it that exaggerated?"

"Setting aside everything else, just look at the Misty Peak lineage. Ever since Lingfengzi broke through to mid-Sage, his arrogance has grown considerably. If not for Luo Chen's reputation suppressing him, do you think Lingfengzi wouldn't want to break away from Luo Tian Sect and rebuild the Misty Sect?"

Jueqing Fairy nodded thoughtfully, but then quickly came to her senses. "Senior Sister, I wasn't asking about his importance to Luo Tian Sect. I was asking if you are worried about him as a person?"

As she said this, Jueqing Fairy stared intently at Canglong, unwilling to miss any detail.

Canglong frowned. "What do you mean by that?"

Jueqing Fairy hesitated for a moment, then lowered her voice. "A few years ago, before he returned, there were rumors circulating within the sect about your relationship with him."

"What relationship?" Canglong asked instinctively.

Jueqing Fairy said helplessly, "What else could it be? The disciples below were all saying that you would become his third Dao companion."

"Ah, that..." Canglong let out a light laugh.

Seeing her so dismissive, Jueqing Fairy said angrily, "Senior Sister, that's your reputation! How can you allow others to gossip about it? If you didn't stop the rumors from spreading before, that's one thing. But now that he's back, how can you still sit idly by while the rumors run rampant?"

To this, Canglong did not reply.

She simply stood quietly by the pitch-black lakeshore, the moonlight reflecting off her snow-white, almost translucent skin. She was like a celestial snow lotus.

Jueqing Fairy kept waiting for an answer, but her senior sister remained silent.

Gradually, an expression of disbelief appeared on her face.

"Senior Sister, you don't mean..."

"Junior Sister, do you know what I was thinking at that moment nine years ago, when Luo Chen's soul aura vanished?"

Before Jueqing Fairy could finish her sentence, Canglong interrupted her.

Suppressing the shock in her heart, Jueqing Fairy asked curiously, "Were you worried that Luo Tian Sect would fall apart?"

Such a worry would not be strange.

After all, that was what Canglong had said earlier.

However, Canglong shook her head and said softly, "That was all analysis I made after calming down. At that very moment, there was only one thing on my mind: regret!"

"Regret?" Jueqing Fairy was puzzled. "Regret what?"

Canglong said very naturally, "Regret that I had not yet repaid his kindness. Luo Chen continued the legacy of Icecastle, rescued me, and helped me break through to the Sage realm in one fell swoop. If he had fallen before I could do anything, I would have regretted it for the rest of my life."

Jueqing Fairy said urgently, "But that still doesn't warrant offering yourself to him!"

Canglong countered, "For someone like him now, what do you think I could offer to repay such a debt of gratitude? This Seven-Orifice Exquisite Body of mine might still be of some benefit to him."

Jueqing Fairy opened her mouth, but all the thoughts that came to her lips felt futile.

This was the Alchemy Sage who had fought his way out of mountains of corpses and seas of blood, the Demon-Slaying Sage who had battled three great Demon Emperors without falling, the Luo Tian Supreme who had single-handedly suppressed dozens of Nether Ghost Emperors!

Before a powerhouse of this level, what could an ordinary Sage possibly offer to move Luo Chen?

And her senior sister's Seven-Orifice Exquisite constitution, with its miraculous effect of forcibly raising a cultivator of the same rank by one minor realm, was undoubtedly the most precious form of repayment, surpassing any miraculous pill or medicine.

Jueqing Fairy swallowed her saliva. "So that's why you turned a blind eye to the rumors spreading throughout the sect without stopping them? You want to go with the flow, let things take their natural course, or even half-push, half-accept, and make this happen?"

Canglong had probably already thought through all the ins and outs long ago, so she appeared exceptionally calm at this moment.

"Something like that. As long as Luo Chen brings it up, I won't refuse." Saying this, Canglong's hand, which had been smoothing her hair, tightened slightly. "But after he came back, he never once mentioned this matter, as if he didn't care at all about having helped me back then. I originally thought that if he had no intention, then I would let it go. But observing his actions in recent years, they are filled with a sense of urgency. He exhausts his mind and strength to improve his strength, and even ends up being greedy for merit and causing this kind of heavenly disaster. It shows how great his pressure is. So back then, I wanted to take the initiative. But when I got to him, I couldn't bring myself to say those words of offering myself to him."

Cultivators should have no attachments.

But a woman, after all, has a thin face.

Canglong sighed. "When his soul aura dissipated, I was filled with regret. If I had been braver, his realm might have been one level higher. When faced with the heavenly disaster, he might have been able to handle it more calmly, and perhaps he wouldn't have had to die."

"Senior Sister, it doesn't have to come to this." Jueqing Fairy shook her head, not approving of this behavior. "He already has two openly married Dao companions!"

Back when Luo Chen was promoted to the Adept realm, he had held a Grand Completion Ceremony.

And that ceremony was also the Dao companion ceremony for him, Sima Huiniang, and Gu Caiyi. Coincidentally, it was Jueqing Fairy who represented Icecastle at that time.

In her view, Luo Chen already had two Dao companions, which was already quite excessive for cultivators who pursued a pure and desireless life. How could he possibly add another wife or concubine?

From Luo Chen's actions over the years, it was indeed true that he had no such intention.

If her senior sister threw herself at him, she would only invite gossip.

Especially since Gu Caiyi had a reputation for being jealous!

Canglong smiled. "Since he already has two, why can't he have a third? For a great man like him, even having a house full of wives and concubines would be only natural."

Jueqing Fairy was dumbfounded. She almost didn't recognize her own senior sister.

How could the Canglong Fairy, who had once valued Icecastle above all else and had always been steady and prudent in her dealings, say such wanton words?

Had the years of being frozen damaged her mind?

Or had she let herself go after being freed from the burden of the sect?

Or had Luo Chen secretly used some kind of charm technique on her senior sister during the years he treated her?

Jueqing Fairy had no way of knowing, and Canglong had no intention of explaining. Instead, after saying these words, she visibly relaxed.

"Fortunately, Luo Chen is fine. His soul aura reappeared shortly after it disappeared. When he wakes up, I will directly express my feelings!"

"Senior Sister!"

"Wait!"

Their conversation came to an abrupt halt.

Canglong raised her hand to stop Jueqing Fairy's hesitation, her beautiful eyes fixed on the center of the distant lake.

There, a whirlpool was slowly forming.

"It seems he's awake!"

Canglong said joyfully, then immediately instructed Jueqing Fairy.

"Quickly go and notify the others in the sect!"

After that, she summoned an ice bridge, crossed the increasingly turbulent surface of Nether Lake, and flew towards the center of the whirlpool.

...

At the bottom of Nether Lake.

A great cauldron stood firmly within it.

The surrounding nether water could not corrode the cauldron's body at all. Instead, large amounts of water vapor seeped into it, as if the two were originally of the same origin.

Inside the cauldron, it was unexpectedly dry, even giving one a feeling of intense heat.

A figure sat cross-legged within, a thin layer of golden light covering his body.

If one looked closely, they would find that the golden light was clearly clusters of golden flames, burning continuously.

But these flames were not burning any physical substance, but rather something illusory.

Suddenly!

The cultivator's long-dormant eyelids twitched for a moment, as if they would open at any time.

And within his sea of consciousness, a massive soul had already taken shape!

At this moment, the soul was still illusory, but in terms of appearance and temperament, it was almost identical to Luo Chen's external form, with no discernible difference.

And what was quite different was that this soul was exceptionally solid, as if it were completely unafraid of heavenly thunder, earthly fire, blazing sun, or fierce wind!

This was quite astonishing.

One must know that a cultivator's soul was extremely fragile. Only after cultivating a primordial spirit could one be unafraid of external yang substances.

Hence the saying, "Yin soul crosses yang, Sage becomes Ascendant."

Luo Chen had not expected that even without forcibly condensing a primordial spirit, he could achieve such a level with just his soul alone.

"Is this the transformation brought about by completing the final nirvana of the Nine Tribulations Three Nirvanas?"

"Directly possessing the omen of becoming an Ascendant!"

The *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture* recorded a method of transformation called the Nine Tribulations Three Nirvanas.

The Three Nirvanas corresponded to the Three Treasures of Essence, Qi, and Spirit, namely the Nirvana of the Physical Body, the Nirvana of Dharma Power, and the Nirvana of the Soul.

Each transformation involved three different aspects.

The Nirvana of the Soul included the Nirvana of the Hun, the Nirvana of the Po, and the Nirvana of the Will. Back then, when Luo Chen had first attempted the Nirvana of the Soul on Green Parasol Mountain, the reason for his failure was obvious.

Insufficient will!

At that time, he did not have the awareness to seek life in death, and he had even made the lucky attempt to use a divided soul to undergo the nirvana.

Failure was inevitable!

If he had not possessed a Nirvana Fire Spirit, he would not have been able to even begin the complete *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture*.

And this time, under the threat of life and death, before the collapse of the blessed land, and in the face of the opportunity for the future Ascendant Dao, he had finally taken this step without hesitation.

It was precisely because of this that he had barely managed to endure the final nirvana.

After passing this stage, Luo Chen realized how great the benefits would have been if he had completed the Nine Tribulations Three Nirvanas earlier.

He could have directly possessed compatible dharma power in the Adept realm, a terrifyingly powerful cultivation physique, and a soul with the omen of becoming an Ascendant.

For him to have only taken this step now, at the late Sage realm, was indeed somewhat slow.

"Fortunately, it's not too late!"

"Now that the soul itself is so stable, once it merges with the Sage to form a primordial spirit, it will undoubtedly be even stronger than before."

"Perhaps, I can even use this to directly attempt a breakthrough to the Ascendant realm!"

Vaguely, Luo Chen had this feeling.

With such a soul foundation, he could forcefully assault the Ascendant realm without worrying about the sliver of life soul that had been taken by the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

This was not unfounded arrogance. After completing the Nine Tribulations Three Nirvanas, he could clearly observe the state of his own soul.

This was also one of its wonderful uses.

The three souls of Heaven, Earth, and Life were clearly visible.

The seven po also resided in various parts of the physical body.

And that missing sliver of life soul was extremely, extremely small.

Perhaps it was precisely because it was so insignificant that Luo Chen had not noticed it back when Mo Shouzhuo had secretly used a spirit treasure to sever it.

And for Luo Chen now, that sliver of life soul was merely a drop in the bucket of his vast soul foundation. It might not actually be able to truly affect his breakthrough to the Ascendant realm.

"Should I gamble?"

As soon as this thought arose, Luo Chen immediately extinguished it.

After witnessing that descending dragon of origin qi from the heavens, he would rather fight against people than test the temper of that damned heaven.

What if, just because of that missing piece, his advancement failed? Then he would have no one to cry to.

"I'll just follow the original plan step by step!"

After examining his soul, Luo Chen's attention turned to his body.

He hadn't looked closely before, but when he did, he was greatly alarmed.

His entire body, from his meridians to his acupoints, was filled with a sea of dharma power.

"This dharma power is too impure!"

Luo Chen's heart sank. He quickly sank his mind into his Purple Mansion.

Sure enough!

The Sage, which had previously grown to be identical to him in appearance and size, was now bloated, like a water balloon that could burst at any moment.

A green fire tree was spreading its dense branches, completely surrounding it, continuously releasing a vast amount of vitality to maintain the Sage's state.

The moment Luo Chen's consciousness appeared, the green fire tree immediately transmitted a weary thought.

Luo Chen could only comfort it: "You've worked hard."

Then, the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit slowly retracted its branches, while Luo Chen's soul took over the Sage, beginning to quickly sort out the chaotic and vast dharma power within.

Back then, when the two great law origins of water and fire had clashed, they had attracted the infusion of the ocean of origin qi.

The quantity was simply too much, far exceeding the amount of a single law origin, and far exceeding the endurance limit of Luo Chen's Sage.

The consequence was the current situation.

The dharma power had become extremely impure, the Sage had become bloated, and even the meridians and acupoints had begun to deform.

If not for his accomplished body refinement, which had prevented his physical body from being burst open, and if not for the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit once again automatically protecting its master during the soul nirvana, Luo Chen would probably not have survived these nine years.

But as things stood, there were still endless troubles.

Luo Chen had known for a long time that the origin qi of heaven and earth was a collection of various types of spiritual qi. Compared to a single-attribute spiritual qi, its advantage lay in its vast quantity and its omnipresence.

Although Ascendant greats were said to be able to command the origin qi of heaven and earth in battle, that was only for combat. When they cultivated, they still carefully selected the attribute energy that suited them.

At this moment, Luo Chen's body was filled with a vast amount of unrefined origin qi of heaven and earth, which had directly raised his cultivation from the previous near seventh-layer Sage to as high as the ninth-layer Sage.

But Luo Chen did not want this.

Power that could not be perfectly controlled, no matter how high the realm, was nothing but a castle in the air.

He had to find a way to strip away the impure origin qi of heaven and earth.

Even if this was a gift from heaven and earth after comprehending the laws of the Dao!

Luo Chen began to attempt to strip and dredge, refine and purify, but it was more troublesome than he had imagined.

The quantity was simply too much!

If he had to refine it strand by strand, he didn't know when he would finish.

And a large part of it was biased toward the water attribute, which had been brought about by comprehending the origin of water.

But the problem was that his comprehension of the origin of water was a shortcut, an observation and borrowing from the Nether Water Spring Eye, not his own ability. His entire life's cultivation technique was fire-attributed. As a result, refining and purifying it was like picking out sand with a needle.

From time to time, it would even cause a clash of dharma power, injuring his already precarious meridians and acupoints.

"I had previously thought of transplanting the Nether Water Spring Eye into my Purple Mansion, making it an existence like the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit. Thinking about it now, that would have been suicide!"

After a thought flashed through his mind, Luo Chen couldn't help but smile bitterly.

His Purple Mansion, formed from his five spirit roots, was vast enough to accommodate the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit, the Nirvana Fire Spirit... Eh, when did this Nirvana Fire Spirit become so majestic and large?

One surprise after another. Luo Chen was shocked to find that the Nirvana Fire Spirit, which had previously been like a small sparrow, was now incredibly majestic. Its feathers were distinct, its crest was like a blazing sun, and it fluttered gracefully near the already weary Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit, appearing even more magnificent!

"Could it be because I have completely completed the Nine Tribulations Three Nirvanas?"

"If that's the case, can my Nirvana Sacred Flame now burn a cultivator's soul?"

Every time one completed a stage of the Nine Tribulations Three Nirvanas, the Nirvana Sacred Flame would undergo some wondrous changes.

From burning the physical body, to burning the energy source of dharma power, and finally, to being able to burn the soul!

This was also why Rosycloud had been able to single-handedly suppress the countless ghost cultivators of the Nether Abyss with her Nirvana Sacred Flame.

Luo Chen had no time to experiment with the changes in the Nirvana Sacred Flame, just as he had no time to experiment with the current duration of his primordial spirit.

It was simply that he had just awakened, and there were a thousand threads and ten thousand ends, leaving him at a loss as to where to start.

Just then!

An ice bridge extended from the outside.

Then, an elegant figure stepped onto the ice bridge and entered the Primordial Unity Cauldron, which shielded against the filthy nether water.

"Senior Brother Luo Chen, you really are awake!" Canglong asked joyfully.

Luo Chen had naturally opened his eyes. Looking at the newcomer, he nodded slightly. "I've caused everyone to worry all these years."

Canglong smiled with relief. "It's good that you're fine."

"Fine?"

Luo Chen muttered to himself, a bitter smile involuntarily appearing on his face.

With this bloated, almost immobile body full of impure dharma power, how could he possibly be fine?

Seeing this rarely seen helpless expression on his face, Canglong Fairy couldn't help but ask curiously, "Senior Brother, what's wrong? Is there anything this junior sister can do to help?"

Help?

Luo Chen suddenly thought of something. His burning eyes looked up and down at the elegant and mature woman before him.

"Junior Sister, were those words you spoke earlier by the lakeshore true?"

Swish!

In an instant, the woman who had been so calm before had her slender neck flushed red.
