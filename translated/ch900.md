# Chapter 900: Self-Created Alchemy Art, Master Xuanyan

Regarding the Alchemy Saint Hall trial, Luo Chen had already begun to discern its nature.

Overall, it lacked the danger of various inheritance trials found elsewhere in the cultivation world.

Before the trial even began, a side hall had been specially set aside for participants to prepare in advance.

Such an arrangement was far too lenient.

But upon careful thought, this inheritance was originally left within the Nether Origin Sect for junior sect members. How could it be filled with peril?

Furthermore, Alchemy Saint Chu Yan herself was a gentle and kind person, unlikely to deliberately make things difficult.

The purpose of her trial lay primarily in the word "selection."

Just like the first level he had just experienced, and judging by the content of all previous first-level examinations, the goal was to filter out those with profound alchemical foundations.

Those with shallow foundations were weeded out.

But upon arriving at the second level, Luo Chen found himself somewhat uncertain.

[Return to Origin, Trace Back to Source, Reverse-Refine to Pass]

What was the meaning of this question?

The first level had at least given the hint "pass with a full hundred." So what standard would be used to judge the second level?

Was it merely the words "reverse-refine"?

Taken literally, it should mean reverse-refining pills, tracing back the various materials that composed the pill to their origins.

But to what extent of reverse-refinement, and how many pills, would be considered passing?

The room was empty. No one gave Luo Chen an answer.

Luo Chen fell into deep thought.

Time passed quietly. For a long while, no one entered the empty room.

This shouldn't be the case.

Because when Luo Chen passed the first level, he could clearly sense that the other three were not far from passing as well.

Especially that Chen Mozi, who like Luo Chen had taken the speed-clear method. Perhaps a few pills might stump him, but as long as he made no mistakes, he would always be able to carefully identify them.

Luo Chen believed that a reputation was never undeserved. The other party wouldn't be so incompetent.

Since they hadn't arrived after so long, it likely meant that each person went to a different room after passing.

Luo Chen didn't dwell on this matter. He continued pondering the intention behind the second level's question.

Gradually, Luo Chen seemed to have an epiphany.

"If the goal truly is to select those with broad and profound alchemical foundations, then there shouldn't be a side hall filled with a sea of questions for people to prepare in advance."

"In other words, the scope of the alchemical texts in that side hall also encompasses this second level."

"The hundred pills selected in the first level are the hundred pills each person understands best, is most confident about, and is most familiar with. Even if one has never handled them before, the knowledge absorbed from the side hall would leave a deep impression. If one must reverse-refine, naturally it would be the hundred pills we are most confident about."

"And the various knowledge recorded in the side hall's alchemical texts also corresponds to the medicinal ingredients needed for refining pills, preparation work, alchemy techniques, pill-forming characteristics, nurturing and storage methods, and so on."

"Therefore, the side hall's collection does indeed help trial participants reverse-refine pills, especially unfamiliar ones they've never actually refined."

"So how is a successful reverse-refinement of a pill judged?"

"When the various raw materials that originally composed the pill are reverse-refined out, that can be considered success!"

"The knowledge recorded in the side hall serves as the basis for us trial participants."

"But no alchemist in the world has such confidence that they can succeed every time in refining pills, let alone successfully reverse-refining all one hundred pills."

"Therefore, the standard for passing is not the quantity of pills successfully reverse-refined, but should focus on the raw materials obtained from the final reverse-refinement!"

After this analysis, Luo Chen gradually found the question-setter's intention from a state of confusion.

This second level tested two broad points.

One was still the alchemist's foundation.

The other, however, began to involve alchemical skill.

The former was experience and knowledge; the latter required actual hands-on practice.

Normal refining was difficult enough, let alone reverse-refining!

Even Luo Chen was confident he could reverse-refine some low-grade pills, but he never even considered reverse-refining high-grade ones. Moreover, among the hundred pills he had selected, many were ones he had never handled before.

The key to passing lay in the raw materials!

Luo Chen's gaze swept over the hundred pills surrounding him. Based on his memory, the composition of raw materials for each pill gradually surfaced in his mind.

He suddenly realized that among the hundred pills he had selected, many had similar properties, and some even shared the same medicinal ingredients.

"The hundred pills one knows best and is most familiar with tend toward personal preference, leaving quite a bit of room for maneuver."

Luo Chen now had a clear understanding.

Regardless of the standard, he planned to reverse-refine every single raw material as much as possible.

If some failed, he would preserve the same raw material from other pills.

As for the beginning?

Luo Chen smiled slightly and grabbed a first-grade Qi Nourishing Pill.

This time, he would start from the easy and gradually explore the difficult!

……

Regarding the Alchemy Saint inheritance, Luo Chen initially had no particular thoughts.

Because he simply didn't know about it!

It wasn't until they were on their way to the Nether Origin Sect that Ye Lingtian proactively brought it up, probably afraid that Luo Chen would think Nether Origin Ancestor had gone back on his word and dug a trap within the agreed terms.

This feeling was like applying for a job in his previous life. The boss said, "Come work for me, I'll give you a hundred thousand a month, and you'll be the general manager directly."

But when you showed up for work, the company only then told you that you needed to pass a performance assessment first. Otherwise, the general manager's office wouldn't be yours.

Knowing the truth, Luo Chen wasn't particularly angry.

Because an Alchemy Saint inheritance wasn't that precious to him.

He already had the alchemical inheritance of Devil Lord Heaven-Refining, containing countless alchemical texts, numerous pill formulas, and covering the three paths of humans, demons, and devils.

Even with Luo Chen's occasional research over so many years, he still hadn't fully comprehended it.

Not to mention the fifth-grade, sixth-grade, and even eighth-grade pill formulas within.

Why had Luo Chen agreed so readily when Rosycloud demanded the "Dao Union Pill" formula?

Besides the absolute gap in strength, the fact that Luo Chen couldn't digest the "Dao Union Pill" formula was also an important reason.

Meat that couldn't be eaten in the short term, or even for a long time, had little meaning to keep. It was better to hand it over to save one's life.

But that was a digression.

In short, from the very beginning, Luo Chen's attitude toward the Alchemy Saint inheritance was exactly as Ye Lingtian had observed.

Not proactive, not rejecting, not enthusiastic, and not afraid.

But after successively going through the side hall's studies and the first level's assessment, Luo Chen became increasingly interested in this trial.

No!

It shouldn't be called an inheritance, but rather the trial content!

Luo Chen was somewhat addicted to these levels of trials.

Systematic learning, clearly directed assessments, foundation-focused tests—this couldn't be compared to the sense of achievement from refining a single pill.

It was like... students after nine years of compulsory education, successively passing the college entrance examination.

The college entrance exam doesn't test how many difficult problems you can solve; it mainly tests the foundation laid over those nine years.

It was through these Alchemy Saint Hall trials that Luo Chen was testing his true level accumulated over three hundred years!

In terms of foundation, his depth was far beyond that of ordinary alchemists.

The title "Alchemy Grandmaster" meant to outsiders that he could refine various high-grade pills. To himself, it meant he could effortlessly refine any low-grade pill and casually perform numerous alchemy techniques.

Now, in the second level of the trial, it was precisely the time for the countless alchemy techniques Luo Chen had mastered to display their power!

"Qi Nourishing Pill—the most universally applicable low-grade pill in the cultivation world. Qi Refinement cultivators of all spirit roots and constitutions can take it."

"The materials used are balanced and gentle, mostly low-grade raw materials with five-element attributes and both yin and yang."

"The refining method can be either fire-refining or water-refining. The one left by the Alchemy Saint in my hand uses both."

"The chosen alchemy techniques are nothing more than Cloud-Transforming Hand, Foundation-Nourishing Hand, Qi-Solidifying Style... and seven other basic techniques."

"To reverse-refine, selecting three of them will suffice!"

The same question had different methods of solution.

After understanding the question-setter's intention, Luo Chen quickly selected the method he was best at.

*Dong!*

The Primordial Unity Cauldron appeared again after many years.

Its color was the same as before, but there were still faint cracks on its surface that hadn't healed.

However, for the present purpose of reverse-refining pills, it was more than sufficient.

Placing the Qi Nourishing Pill inside, Luo Chen expertly performed his alchemy techniques.

Spiritual power flowed at his fingertips, hand seals and techniques fluttering like butterflies, elegant and beautiful.

In less than half a day, he obtained thirteen types of raw materials.

Some were solid, some liquid, and even some misty qi.

These were the raw materials of the most widely circulated pill in the cultivation world!

Compared to Luo Chen's early years when refining first-grade pills often yielded only single-digit raw materials, this Qi Nourishing Pill seemed simple but had thirteen types of raw materials. No wonder its universality was so strong.

First attempt, success!

Luo Chen didn't stop. He turned his attention to the second pill.

It was the Jade Dew Pill!

That second-grade pill which had once helped Luo Chen establish himself in Tianlan and was treasured by the Splendid Radiance Palace was also quite popular within the Nether Origin Sect.

Facing this pill, Luo Chen was also full of confidence.

The corresponding raw materials, refining techniques, specific characteristics and properties—all surged into his mind.

Luo Chen moved swiftly, decomposing the Jade Dew Pill step by step without any obstruction.

But at the end, looking at the streams of spiritual light, Luo Chen's expression turned somewhat grim.

Most of these spiritual lights were very clear, without any impurities, representing the purest raw materials.

But one cluster among them was of mixed color and chaotic aura.

The meaning of this went without saying.

"Such a smooth reverse-refinement, and I've already decomposed eighty percent of the raw materials. Yet those last twenty percent failed to decompose and instead became more entangled!"

"Where did the problem lie?"

He stopped his preparations for reverse-refining the next pill and began to reflect on this failure.

Yes, even though he had reverse-refined eighty percent of the raw materials, failing to reverse-refine twenty percent of his most proficient pill was still a failure in Luo Chen's eyes!

One day, two days...

Several days later, Luo Chen woke from his contemplation with some understanding.

"Reverse-refining and normal refining are ultimately different."

"Whether it's the alchemy techniques used, the general process, or even the depth and magnitude of spiritual power mobilization, corresponding adjustments should be made."

Luo Chen took a deep breath, put away the successfully obtained raw materials, and set the failed ones aside.

Then he began reverse-refining the next pill.

During the reverse-refinement process, Luo Chen murmured softly, almost inaudibly.

"If only there were a specialized alchemy technique for reverse-refining pills, that would be much more convenient."

This murmur flashed by. Luo Chen didn't take it to heart, merely a sudden whim.

How could such an alchemy technique exist in the world?

But as successes and failures continued to alternate, this sudden whim surged more and more violently in Luo Chen's mind.

There were so many hundred pills!

The alchemy techniques required were incredibly complex!

The raw materials involved, even if they leaned toward Luo Chen's specialties, were as vast as an ocean.

Even with his profound alchemical foundation, to categorize and reverse-refine each pill one by one, solving all hundred pills, would take seven, eight, or even over ten years.

But before entering, the Nether Origin Sect Ancestor had only given ten years!

Luo Chen stopped his preparations for reverse-refining a third-grade pill, a hesitant expression on his face.

"If such an alchemy technique truly existed, it would solve everything once and for all!"

"I possess numerous alchemy techniques, most of which have reached the pinnacle, the state of great perfection. I've even dabbled in demonic and devilish alchemy techniques."

"Using this as a foundation, taking the strengths of each, incorporating a hundred schools, and creating a reverse-refinement alchemy technique of my own—that's not necessarily a fantasy."

"It's just the matter of time..."

Luo Chen looked at the more than seventy pills remaining, his hesitation growing stronger.

Suddenly!

His hesitation vanished, replaced by a flash of decisiveness.

"The Alchemy Saint inheritance is nothing more than a few high-grade pill formulas, some alchemical texts, and at most some of the Alchemy Saint's residual blessings."

"Compared to the inheritance of Devil Lord Heaven-Refining, it's not that important."

"On the contrary, if I can create such a reverse-refinement alchemy technique, it will be a leap forward in my alchemical path!"

"This place is clearly the most suitable opportunity to create my own alchemy technique. How could I waste it?"

*Hoo...*

Luo Chen exhaled a long, turbid breath, his mind made up.

He would use this sudden inspiration and the suitable environment here to attempt creating his own alchemy technique.

Even if it meant losing the Alchemy Saint inheritance, it would be worth it!

"Let's take this Five Essence Pill as the first experimental target!"

"The alchemy techniques used in its refinement are the Essence-Containing Seal, Five Extremes Hand, and Falling Star Technique. Reverse-refinement should also use these techniques."

"If I reverse the technique seals and incorporate basic alchemy techniques, I wonder if it can succeed?"

……

Outside the Alchemy Saint Hall.

Ye Lingtian walked along the mountain path, arriving ethereally.

This Alchemy Saint Hall trial was presided over by him.

But he didn't devote all his energy to overseeing it. In his spare time, he would still go to a fourth-grade cave dwelling in the mountains to cultivate.

Upon arrival, he saw Lu Chun still sitting motionlessly outside the hall.

Ye Lingtian shook his head and walked over.

"How many years has it been?"

Lu Chun subconsciously replied, "The third year."

"The third year already! I wonder how things are inside?" Ye Lingtian murmured, then frowned at Lu Chun. "Since Junior Brother has already failed, why not return? Waiting here is meaningless."

Lu Chun looked up and said solemnly, "It has meaning. I have relied on my memory to continuously replay the first level I failed in my mind, and I have finally arrived at the answer. If the Alchemy Saint Hall opens again and the first level's trial remains the same, I am fully confident I can pass!"

"Heh heh."

Ye Lingtian smiled but didn't argue.

How many "ifs" were there in the world?

Perhaps this time someone would directly pass and obtain the Alchemy Saint inheritance?

Even if everyone failed, the next time it opened, according to the methods Chu Yan had left behind, the questions inside would certainly not remain unchanged.

But Lu Chun's talent was indeed good. Being able to find the problem through personal review alone.

If this focus had been placed on the alchemical path instead of taking shortcuts to forcibly raise his realm, perhaps the result of this trial would have been different.

Just as the two were chatting, a black wind suddenly swept outside the hall.

Ye Lingtian's expression changed, and he shouted:

"Who?"

The black wind dissipated, revealing an old man in dark robes.

His hair was gray but neatly combed.

His face was lined with wrinkles, but his complexion was ruddy and full, his spirit and energy abundant.

"Daoist Ye, have you forgotten even me?"

Ye Lingtian was momentarily stunned.

But Lu Chun beside him had already risen solemnly and respectfully bowed to the newcomer.

"Junior Lu Chun pays respects to Master Xuanyan!"

Master Xuanyan laughed heartily and waved his hand. "Daoist Lu, why be so polite? You are no longer that junior from back then. You and I are now of the same generation."

Lu Chun shook his head, not accepting this statement.

Ye Lingtian suddenly realized. So it was Master Xuanyan!

No wonder Lu Chun, though at the same Sage realm, addressed him as junior.

The other party's age was unmatched among the Sage True Persons of the Nether Origin Sect.

At a lifespan of one thousand one hundred years, he had already reached the limit of a Sage cultivator's lifespan!

Coupled with having reached the realm of Alchemy Grandmaster very early, it could be said that most alchemists in the Nether Origin Sect, and even the entire Eastern Wilderness, were his juniors.

Even Alchemy Saint Chu Yan, who had already ascended!

He steadied himself. "Master Xuanyan, what brings you here today?"

"What brings me?"

Master Xuanyan laughed heartily and strode toward the Alchemy Saint Hall.

"Before I go, I want to see what that girl left behind."

His laughter still echoed in their ears, but his figure had already vanished into the mist.

Ye Lingtian opened his mouth, his expression changing repeatedly, but in the end, he didn't stop the other's actions.

Master Xuanyan had made great contributions to the Nether Origin Sect. Even Alchemy Saint Chu Yan's rise owed something to him.

Now that he was old and clearly showing signs of a final resurgence, even if obtaining the Alchemy Saint inheritance wouldn't benefit the Nether Origin Sect much, relying on those contributions, he had no reason to stop the dying man from seeing what his old friend had left behind.

But this matter still needed to be reported to the three ancestors.

As he expected, the reply from Yama Mountain was the same.

"Let him go!"
