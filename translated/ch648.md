# Chapter 648: Three Sages Gather, War Reopens

"Faster!"

"Faster!"

"A little faster!"

In his heart, he shouted continuously, and his wings trembled to the extreme.

A giant figure nine zhang long sped madly along the clear-turbid boundary between the Sunken Sea and the Flow Ocean sea region. Occasionally, explosive sounds rang out as it broke through the air, and when it appeared again, it was already a hundred zhang away.

From time to time, Myriad Immortals Assembly wandering cultivators in the surroundings raised their heads to look at this scene. At first, they thought some powerful Great Demon King had come to kill them, but the dense mana fluctuations truly showed this was a human immortal cultivator.

They did not understand why this person flew so fast, almost fleeing several li away in the blink of an eye.

While they were stunned, when they looked again, he was already a thousand li away.

Relying on the Ninety Thousand Li of the Heavenly Peng true body and the Voidburst Steps he occasionally used, Luo Chen finally opened distance from Three Pillars Gorge.

Boom!

Fierce winds rose as the peng bird directly crashed into a solitary peak in the sea.

When the smoke and dust scattered, Luo Chen walked out from the broken solitary peak. His face was somewhat pale, not because his mana was depleted, but because he had been frightened.

For no reason, how could a Demon Emperor descend on the Sunken Sea, and from the most impossible direction, the human race hinterland?

"The most impossible direction!"

Suddenly, Luo Chen's expression changed sharply.

Along the endless horizon, he looked toward a certain place from afar.

There was the legendary forbidden land of the Northern Sea: the Arctic Yemo Heaven.

"How is that possible?"

"Aren't the two grand formations there said to be able to suppress the high-rank demon beasts within for hundreds or thousands of years?"

As he murmured, Luo Chen seemed unwilling to believe it at all.

But the scene he had seen when leaving Three Pillars Gorge forced him to believe.

As soon as that strange, handsome man who seemed like an exiled immortal descended, he quietly killed over one thousand wandering cultivators. Then, with one ferocious palm, he sank three sky-supporting giant peaks.

Even Dao Lan had been knocked into the Sunken Sea in Luo Chen's peripheral vision.

Luo Chen recognized that method. It was the power of a heaven and earth domain that cultivators and demon beasts both mastered after advancing to the Sage stage.

That man was definitely a Sage-level demon cultivator.

"I cannot stay at the Sunken Sea anymore!"

Without further hesitation, once Luo Chen caught his breath, he gave the direction of Three Pillars Gorge a deep look, then flapped the Moonbreaker Wings and flew toward Taiping Fortress.

That night, Luo Chen and Shentu stood outside a great hall.

Both of them had solemn expressions and discussed in low voices.

"You confirm there is no mistake?"

"Luo would never lie. After the others return, you can naturally verify the details."

Luo Chen was not the only survivor of Three Pillars Gorge.

Apart from him, there were also Changfengzi and the other two.

Not only that, because he had used the Myriad Souls Banner to clear the field while hunting demon beasts, some wandering cultivators had been forced to leave Three Pillars Gorge in advance.

With such a great disturbance at Three Pillars Gorge, those wandering cultivators who had gone far away should also be able to discover some of it. They were only slower and could not return to Taiping Fortress as quickly as Luo Chen.

Shentu, the Golden Core garrison of the first defensive line, stared fixedly at Luo Chen. Finally, he slowly nodded.

"This is a first-class matter for the Myriad Immortals Assembly. I believe Devil Lord would not joke about it. Since that is the case, this moment is equivalent to entering wartime status. Devil Lord, please."

Luo Chen's heart eased, and he cupped his fists to Shentu.

"Luo will take the first step. Fellow Daoist, take care."

Shentu smiled bitterly and returned the salute. "Devil Lord, go first. Remember to report the situation to the Demon Hunter Office. I will also notify the Myriad Immortals Assembly here."

Luo Chen gave a hum, swept his sleeve, and strode into the great hall before him.

After entering, he displayed his five-star demon hunter waist token and proactively showed his mana and soul to prove his identity.

Then, holding the waist token, under the gazes of many formation masters, he stepped onto the high platform in the hall.

As light blazed, Luo Chen's figure gradually twisted within it.

When the light dispersed, there was no trace of the Daoist on the intricately patterned high platform.

Outside the hall, the enormous Taiping Fortress seemed like a giant beast awakening from deep sleep, releasing rumbling tremors.

One fierce cannon barrel after another extended from the cannon ports in the city wall.

Not only that.

Within the Flow Ocean sea region, wild winds rose and fierce waves towered.

A super grand formation was ready to unleash.

Beneath the night, Taiping Fortress stood amid towering winds and waves, silently waiting for the enemy's arrival.

Inside Dragon Abyss Immortal City, in a heavily guarded hall of the Demon Hunter Office, seven cultivators sat cross-legged facing a high platform, their faces somewhat bored.

"This is the fifth month, right? How long until our rotation?"

"One month. We all have a half-year mission term."

"Why is Fellow Daoist Xu so anxious? Although guarding the teleportation array is a bitter duty, this place has second-tier upper-grade spiritual qi concentration. It is truly an excellent cultivation environment for people like us. Why hurry to end the mission?"

The mission of guarding the teleportation array was a bitter one in the Myriad Immortals Assembly. The guarding cultivators had to remain still for months or half a year at a time.

At the same time, it was also a desirable post. One could freely enjoy a high-grade cultivation environment.

Ordinary people could not accept such a mission even if they wanted to. They had to possess certain formation attainments and pass an assessment before taking the post.

Before taking the post, they even needed a dedicated person to teach them corresponding teleportation formation knowledge.

The cultivator surnamed Xu said in distress, "Who can stand staying in this little black room for half a year? Apart from when I was establishing my foundation, I have never secluded myself this long in my life. And you don't know, a new shop opened in Dragon Abyss Immortal City. Tsk, that shop..."

When he mentioned the new shop, his eyes almost shone.

"Inside are all top beauties trained by Warm Jade Pavilion! Do you know Warm Jade Pavilion? In the past, it specialized in cultivating high-quality furnaces for the Primordial Demon Sect, a major Golden Core sect."

"Apart from beauties, there is also fine wine said to come from Southern Border. Oh, and there are top-grade spirit chefs inside who can cook all kinds of delicacies. Their ingredients are either spirit plants from Earth Mulberry Sect, Black Gold Dragon Carp from Peng Lake, or even ingredients from the Canghai Alliance and the Moluo Flow. They can gather them all."

"Ah, I, Xu, do not want to stay in this teleportation hall for even one more day!"

Just as he raised his head and howled, bitter over his lack of fulfillment, the high platform before them suddenly emitted a buzzing sound.

The other six were startled.

"In the middle of the night, why would someone still use the teleportation array?"

Although puzzled, their movements were not slow.

Each held a small formation disk and released mana to activate it.

Seven-colored light like silk flew from the formation disks and landed on the high platform.

In an instant, swish, swish, swish!

The entire hall and the high platform merged into one, and a powerful pressure enveloped the whole platform.

Luo Chen, whose figure had just appeared on the high platform, frowned at this scene.

Before the few could question him, he raised his hand and sent out his waist token.

Among the seven, the leading late Foundation Establishment cultivator took out an illustrated register.

After comparing the two, the newcomer's information immediately appeared.

Five-star demon hunter: Luo Hai.

Who was Luo Hai?

Was there such a person in the Myriad Immortals Assembly?

The few looked at one another.

On the high platform, Luo Chen gave a soft snort. "This seat is Daoist Azure Yang. Are you not going to let me pass?"

As soon as the three words Daoist Azure Yang appeared, the seven trembled and hurriedly controlled the formation to disperse.

After the pressure covering him completely disappeared, Luo Chen calmly stepped down from the high platform and directly left the teleportation hall.

Only after the doors closed again did the seven guarding cultivators begin chattering.

"Right, I have indeed heard before that Daoist Azure Yang's surname was Luo. I just did not expect his real name to be Luo Hai. We almost angered him just now."

"And how do you know his true name is Luo Hai? Within the Myriad Immortals Assembly, most wandering cultivators use aliases and usually show themselves through Dao titles or nicknames. Perhaps Luo Hai is also a fake name."

"That is true. But the rumored lord of Peng Lake is truly wealthy. He actually uses teleportation arrays when traveling. This thing costs one million spirit stones for a single one-way trip, even for short-distance teleportation, right?"

"Otherwise, how would he be the lord of Peng Lake and an artifact-forging master famous throughout the Myriad Immortals Assembly?"

"My focus is different. Did you not notice just now? When Daoist Azure Yang emerged from the array, his expression was normal, almost without any abnormality. You should know that when we occasionally received Golden Core High Men from the teleportation array in the past, most staggered, and a few even became nauseous, vomited, and could not control their mana."

"Hiss, now that you mention it, that is true."

"The lord of Peng Lake is unfathomable."

They were still discussing Daoist Azure Yang here, but outside, those who saw Luo Chen were already incomparably awed and called him Devil Lord.

Over the past year, Luo Chen's deeds on the Sunken Sea battlefield had gradually spread as the corpses of those Great Demon Kings were sent back.

Wandering cultivators who worked long-term at the Demon Hunter Office understood even more deeply.

As Luo Chen traveled onward, he sensed that the Demon Hunter Office still had a peaceful atmosphere.

"Could it be that the news from Taiping Fortress has not yet returned?"

While hesitating, he saw three late cultivators with solemn expressions.

Angler, Purple Dowager, and Ghost Spirit Child.

The Myriad Immortals Assembly's three eight-star demon hunters apart from Dao Lan.

As soon as they saw Luo Chen, Angler spoke first.

"We will discuss the specific situation after the three Sages arrive."

So they had already been notified.

Luo Chen gave a hum and found a chair to sit down.

They did not wait long. Before long, a sound of wind came from outside.

The three followed the sound and looked out. A blood light suddenly appeared and landed on the center of the three chairs above.

"Angler greets Sage!"

"Purple Dowager greets Sage!"

"Ghost Spirit greets Sage!"

Luo Chen abruptly stood, then bowed respectfully. "Luo Hai greets Sage!"

"Mm, dispense with ceremony."

A deep voice came from above.

Luo Chen straightened and subconsciously looked toward the head seat.

A tall man sat there.

His appearance was firm and resolute, his face full of beard, and his eyes bright and piercing. He wore a blood robe without any mixed color, and beneath his burly frame, taut muscles revealed endless power.

A wild and domineering temperament arose naturally.

His eyes looked down on everyone, seeming both contemptuous and as if he ignored them entirely.

Was this Pang Bujue, Blood Wanderer, one of the three great wandering cultivators famous throughout the Northern Sea?

Luo Chen's heart grew cold, and his expression became even more respectful.

Blood Wanderer glanced at him and spoke directly.

"Daoist Azure Yang, is it? Explain the Sunken Sea's situation one by one."

Luo Chen was startled. He had thought they would wait for the other two to arrive before speaking, but he had not expected Blood Wanderer to be so urgent.

He did not dare be negligent and immediately explained the specific situation in detail.

When he mentioned that strange, handsome man who seemed like an exiled immortal, a thoughtful expression appeared on Blood Wanderer's firm face.

"Is it that one?"

Before Blood Wanderer could continue asking, wind suddenly rose outside the door.

Everyone subconsciously turned to look and saw a young Daoist with his hair tied in a Daoist topknot step in.

"Wanderer, long time no see."

Blood Wanderer looked at him and nodded slightly. "Dragon Abyss, take a seat."

So this was Dragon Abyss True Being. He had already ended his seclusion, and this was his first public appearance after coming out.

Dragon Abyss had arrived. As for the last person...

Under the moonlight, a woman came walking on moonbeams. Cool moonlight scattered across her simple, ancient, moon-white Daoist robe, making her seem like a fairy from the moon palace descending into the mortal world.

Her face was exquisite, her skin as delicate as congealed fat, and her eyes as bright as stars.

As her eyelashes trembled faintly, she revealed a trace of distance from worldly things.

Yet the two bulging objects at her chest completely destroyed that cool distance, making her appear passionate and gorgeous.

Further down were a slender waist and long, full thighs, enough to make anyone salivate at a glance, regardless of man or woman.

Cool and passionate, distant yet gorgeous.

Two completely different temperaments gathered on one person.

In the entire Myriad Immortals Assembly, or even the entire Northern Sea, perhaps only one person possessed such grace.

Moon Wanderer.

Luo Chen did not dare look too much. After one glance, he lowered his head and joined the others in respectfully greeting the newcomer.

"Greetings, Moon Sage!"

Moon Wanderer did not look at them, nor even spare Dragon Abyss True Being more than a glance. Her gaze directly fell on the resolute, burly man in the center.

"Pang Bujue, you were willing to return?"

Blood Wanderer snorted coldly. "This Myriad Immortals Assembly was founded by me. I return when I wish."

Moon Wanderer laughed softly. Her laughter was gentle yet charming, and when it fell into others' ears, it actually showed signs of shaking their souls.

"I fear you could no longer restrain yourself."

With a smiling voice, she floated into her seat.

Blood Wanderer ignored the mockery in her words and directly told Moon Wanderer and Dragon Abyss True Being the situation Luo Chen had reported earlier.

After digesting the information, Moon Wanderer glanced at Luo Chen, then slowly nodded to Blood Wanderer.

"If Daoist Azure Yang spoke correctly, then he should be Flow Lord."

Blood Wanderer nodded. "Among the transformed demon cultivators of the Northern Sea, as far as I know, only he has such outstanding looks."

The emperor of the jiaoren race: Flow Lord.

Moon Wanderer said softly, "This person is not troublesome. Either you or I can deal with him. The trouble is the Arctic Yemo Heaven."

Blood Wanderer disagreed. "You and I are both solitary wanderers drifting everywhere. Even if the seal of Nightmara Heaven is truly broken, at worst we leave. What trouble is there?"

Moon Wanderer smiled lightly and glanced at the person beside them. "I fear Fellow Daoist Dragon Abyss will not think so."

After sitting, Dragon Abyss True Being, who had not spoken, remained composed and unhurried. His steady temperament did not match his youthful appearance at all.

"For such matters, doing our best is enough. There is no need to force it."

Moon Wanderer curled her lip and did not argue with him.

"Let us first discuss how to handle the Sunken Sea. In my view, we must notify the three families of Wuzhong Valley. In addition, both the righteous and demonic sides, and even Divine Origin City, should be informed of the relevant situation."

"These have nothing to do with me!"

Blood Wanderer waved his large hand and stood from his seat.

"Jiaoren Emperor Flow Lord destroyed Three Pillars Gorge, clearly intending to borrow the Sunken Sea's power to break the formation in Nightmara Heaven. This process may be very long or very short, but he absolutely cannot be allowed to ruin this old man's plan."

"Tomorrow, this old man will personally go to the Sunken Sea and slay Flow Lord!"

"Angler, you three select people from the Myriad Immortals Assembly, then hurry to the Sunken Sea and slaughter all those beasts!"

Moon Wanderer watched this scene and also smiled as she echoed him. "Then this concubine will also lend Fellow Daoist a hand. The three families of Wuzhong Valley have been comfortable for so many years. It is time to make them move."

Blood Wanderer turned and gave the woman a deep look. Finally, he slowly nodded.

"Fine!"

As soon as this word was spoken, the four Golden Core cultivators below realized one thing at the same time.

The Myriad Immortals Assembly had declared wartime status.

*(End of Chapter)*
