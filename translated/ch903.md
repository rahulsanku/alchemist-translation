# Chapter 903: Grandmaster Luo Chen

The so-called Two Modes are the foundation of the Great Dao, referring to Yin and Yang.

Legend has it that when heaven and earth first opened, everything was chaos, also known as the Ultimate Void. The Ultimate Void gave birth to the Supreme Ultimate, the Supreme Ultimate gave rise to the Two Modes, and from the Two Modes came the Four Symbols and Eight Trigrams, and then all things in heaven and earth.

Such knowledge was something even children who had just begun the path of Qi Refinement understood, and Luo Chen naturally knew it as well.

But matters in this world were always easier said than done, and even the "easier said" part was often not simple.

Luo Chen's ability to comprehend the concept of Two Modes Yin and Yang came only after long-term study of formations and the process of setting them up, gradually understanding it from the principles of feng shui.

By drawing inferences from formations, he understood that so-called Yin-Yang opposition existed in other matters as well.

And in the refinement of this Two Modes Pill, this concept had been applied to the extreme extent that Luo Chen had encountered so far!

All materials had to be water and fire opposites, their grades kept consistent, and sufficient balance must be maintained.

Not only that, unlike ordinary pill refining where various auxiliary materials were processed in advance, the refinement of the Two Modes Pill began directly with processing the two main ingredients.

The difficulty of this could well be imagined.

After all, many things started small and grew large, from easy to difficult, advancing step by step to more easily approach success.

Who would directly raise the difficulty to the maximum from the start?

But Luo Chen had to do it this way. Only by fusing the two main materials of water and fire into a balanced state of Two Modes could he accommodate all things.

For this, Luo Chen marshaled his utmost concentration, not daring to slacken in the slightest.

First, he used earth fire to warm the cauldron, conserving his mana. This process lasted a full three days.

Under Luo Chen's fine control, the cauldron's temperature gradually reached the ideal state.

Then, with a flick of his finger, the Withered-Source Fire Marrow and Nether Spring Cold Spring entered the Primordial Unity Cauldron simultaneously.

The moment these two materials of extreme opposing attributes entered, they erupted into the most extreme conflict.

"All things bear Yin and embrace Yang, blending Qi to achieve harmony!"

"To make them coexist peacefully and reach a balanced unified state, one must suppress them with even greater power!"

Luo Chen's thoughts flashed, and he opened his mouth to exhale.

A stream of cyan-white flame slowly flew out, completely enveloping the entire Primordial Unity Cauldron.

It was the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire!

The moment this fire appeared, the entire Earth Fire Hall trembled for an instant.

Chen Mozi, who was tempering auxiliary materials, even looked up at Luo Chen in shock.

"What kind of origin true fire is he using, to have such power that makes all fires tremble?"

Fortunately, this was not a critical stage of Chen Mozi's pill refining, otherwise this single distraction might have caused him to lose all his progress.

Luo Chen paid no attention to others. Once the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire was unleashed, its consumption of mana was incalculable.

When he usually refined pills, he only dared to borrow a wisp or a thread. How could it compare to directly using a whole stream now!

But the effect was surprisingly good!

When the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire enveloped the Primordial Unity Cauldron, the conflicting water and fire extreme materials inside immediately quieted down.

The two began to be fused together bit by bit under the pill art Luo Chen employed.

This process was very, very slow, but if it went smoothly, everything would be fine!

Luo Chen was willing to wait.

One day, two days... unknowingly, three months had already passed.

*Bang!*

A soft sound suddenly arose in the Earth Fire Hall.

Amidst a puff of ash, Chen Mozi stumbled back, covered in dust.

His expression was gloomy, but in the end, he could only smile bitterly.

He had failed after all!

He had anticipated this outcome, since even the pill formula was hastily created from available materials, with no familiarity whatsoever.

But accepting this failure was far more painful than he had imagined.

It wasn't just the failed refinement, nor that the Alchemy Saint inheritance was no longer his. More than that, the step he had just mustered the courage to take had been dealt a solid blow.

"Sir Xuanyan advised me not to stick to old conventions, to dare to innovate, to have the courage to think of change when pushed to extremes. But all things in this world operate according to established patterns. How easy is it to break the rules!"

Creating any pill formula required countless repeated experiments to obtain the optimal ratio.

To succeed on the first try was unimaginably difficult.

He sighed, waved his hand to sweep away the medicinal dregs in the copper cauldron, and prepared to leave in dejection.

But before leaving, he inadvertently glanced at the opposite side.

The cyan-white flame was still burning slowly!

In the illusory space, the focused Luo Chen seemed undisturbed by any external matters.

But his pale complexion was even worse than Chen Mozi's own current state of failure.

"What pill is he trying to refine?"

"Just the first step alone has taken three full months!"

"It's hard to imagine how much longer the later steps will need."

Chen Mozi shook his head, did not disturb Luo Chen, and quietly left the Earth Fire Hall.

The Alchemy Saint inheritance was not for him. Why act the petty villain?

Luo Chen's first step was already so difficult; he would probably find it hard to succeed in refining the pill either. He likely wouldn't see the Alchemy Saint inheritance either.

For Luo Chen at this moment, any thought of the Alchemy Saint inheritance had long been cast aside.

What filled his mind, what his eyes saw, was only the viscous liquid gradually fusing from red and blue colors within the furnace cauldron.

His entire mind was stretched to its tightest!

"Why can't it successfully merge into one?"

"The Withering-Bloom Divine Fire is enough to suppress everything, but beneath the surface harmony of the two materials lies extreme internal conflict. In this conflict, medicinal power is constantly dissipating."

"Where exactly is the problem?"

Luo Chen kept asking himself frantically.

The fusion of the two main materials had gone relatively smoothly at the start.

Perhaps because the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire also contained the principle of Two Modes Yin and Yang, the step Luo Chen thought would be the most difficult had progressed quite smoothly initially.

But at the final critical moment, it simply could not be completed.

For this, he was stuck here, forced to watch the two main materials constantly collide, repel each other, and waste their power in vain.

Suddenly, a consciousness came through, vague and unclear, but its meaning was clear.

"Change the fire!"

It was the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit.

Since gaining intelligence, the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit had never spoken, but it actually had its own consciousness, though most of the time Luo Chen was the dominant one.

This kind of proactive reminder was the first of its kind.

Luo Chen was startled. Change the fire? Change to what fire?

But instinctively, his reflexes had already made the move.

The cyan-white flame suddenly withdrew, and the viscous liquid in the cauldron began to boil violently.

But before it could reach its peak of boiling, a golden flame enveloped the world within the cauldron!

It was the still-young Nirvana Sacred Flame!

This flame had a higher rank, but was still too weak. Even Luo Chen did not dare to force too much mana to stimulate it, lest he disperse it.

But the moment the Nirvana Sacred Flame appeared, the boiling liquid inside the cauldron instantly quieted down.

Luo Chen stared blankly at this scene, vaguely realizing something.

So-called withering and blooming—their roots still remained. They were not absolutely mutually exclusive, only temporarily dormant.

It was more about the endless vitality generated through the cycle of withering and blooming.

Using this to correspond to Yin-Yang Two Modes was only similar in form, not in spirit.

But the Nirvana Sacred Flame was different!

It was the extreme transformation between life and death!

Only through nirvana, seeking life from death, could one reach the pinnacle!

At this moment, Luo Chen entered a state of enlightenment. On his attribute panel, the law entry related to [Withering-Bloom True Intent] was advancing at a visible rate.

But he had no time to pay attention to that. He only knew that in that state, relying on the instinct of an alchemy grandmaster, he controlled the rapid fusion of the two main materials.

The fusion that had been deadlocked for three full months was now completed in just three days.

Luo Chen suddenly snapped back to reality, repeatedly counting his blessings.

If not for the divided soul maintaining the alchemy work, if not for his powerful instinct, this sudden enlightenment alone would have caused him to lose all his progress.

But having crossed this step, what followed was relatively simple.

He only needed to add the other auxiliary materials step by step, and he could foresee the moment of success.

Of course, "simple" was only relative to the first step.

Luo Chen still carefully added various rare materials, controlling the formation of the pill.

Gradually, the red and blue colors inside began to change, finally forming an existence surrounded by black and white qi.

Several days later!

Only a single word was heard: "Collect!"

In the hall, Luo Chen, his face weary but his eyes bright and spirited, waved his sleeve, and a black-and-white pill flew toward him.

The moment the pill entered his hand, before Luo Chen could even examine it carefully, the golden bird formed by the Nirvana Sacred Flame and the small tree of the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit simultaneously stirred restlessly in his Purple Mansion.

Luo Chen smiled wryly.

Such a scene needed no explanation of whether he had succeeded or failed.

The attitude of the two powerful flames was enough to explain everything!

But now was not the time to upgrade them.

Luo Chen put away the Primordial Unity Cauldron, raised the Two Modes Pill toward the empty Earth Fire Hall.

*Hum!*

The sound of a formation operating reached Luo Chen's keen ears, and then he saw a cloud of mist surging toward him, trying to envelop him.

Luo Chen did not resist, letting the formation operate, carrying him to the next location.

Floating ethereally, when he opened his eyes again, the scene had changed to a new world.

...

"Where is this?"

Luo Chen walked out of the teleportation formation in surprise, looking at the world of beautiful mountains, clear waters, singing birds, and fragrant flowers.

A thought emerged in his mind.

"A grotto-heaven?"

No!

In an instant, Luo Chen rejected this idea and gave another answer.

"A blessed land!"

The so-called grotto-heavens and blessed lands were often confused by the world.

But the two were completely different.

Grotto-heavens were often self-contained spaces, independent of the outside world.

Like the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, or the broken small space the Medicine King Sect used to cultivate Accumulated Void Flowers—either internal worlds of living beings, or spaces forcibly cut out by powerful beings through artificial means.

Blessed lands, however, were closely connected to the real world, seemingly independent but actually inseparable.

There was no distinction of high and low between the two. A powerful grotto-heaven like the Mirage Dragon's was formed from a top-grade great wilderness, with derived laws and true intent within, far surpassing ordinary blessed lands. But a weak one like the Medicine King Sect's small space could be shattered by just a few Sage-level experts fighting inside it, how could it compare to this stable world before him?

What surprised Luo Chen was that Alchemy Saint Chu Yan actually had such a blessed land.

"No, this blessed land should belong to the Nether Origin Sect!"

"So she left, but the blessed land remained."

"No wonder the three ancestors of the Nether Origin Sect weren't worried about the Alchemy Saint inheritance for so many years, nor did they forcefully take it, yet they repeatedly sent people to participate in the trial."

"They don't care so much about the Alchemy Saint inheritance; what they care about is reclaiming this blessed land!"

"And the Alchemy Saint trial is the key to opening this blessed land."

*Whoosh...*

Letting out a long breath, Luo Chen stopped thinking about these irrelevant matters. He did not rush to find the so-called fourth trial either.

Instead, he immediately sat down cross-legged, adjusted his posture, and began circulating his technique.

The earlier refinement of the Two Modes Pill, with its heavy use of the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire and Nirvana Sacred Flame, had greatly depleted his mana.

Regardless of what the fourth trial's content was, the first priority was to recover his mana.

As he breathed and exhaled, vast amounts of pure spiritual energy surged toward him like rolling waves.

Moreover, because his understanding of Withering-Bloom True Intent had deepened earlier, the influence of the enlightenment state lingered even after it had dissipated.

Some indescribable things were flowing toward him.

...

By a babbling stream, a white-robed Daoist sat cross-legged.

Divine sense leaped, mana circulated.

Streams of heaven-earth spiritual energy were drawn into his body, first refined through a cycle of meridians and acupoints, then further tempered by the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire, before finally merging into the plump little Sage infant, becoming the purest mana.

Luo Chen was immersed in this, feeling a certain "indescribable subtlety."

Cultivation was bitter and arduous—how could there be such a thing as indescribable subtlety?

But at this moment, he truly felt this way, because compared to his former self, the efficiency of converting mana and recovering mana now was far, far too high.

Almost two to three times what it was before!

This speed was not obtained out of thin air, but precisely calculated by Luo Chen after cultivating the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture* on Green Parasol Mountain.

He clearly knew that his cultivation had embarked on the most correct path. Just the cultivation speed and recovery speed had increased two or three times.

In the past, if he wanted to break through from Sage Fourth Layer to Sage Fifth Layer, he would need at least forty to fifty years. Even with the help of the top-grade fourth-rank cave dwelling on Green Parasol Mountain, it only shortened a portion of that time.

But at his current speed, he could break through in at most twenty or thirty years.

And now, based on Luo Chen's judgment, relying on this cultivation speed, reaching Sage Sixth Layer would probably only take about fifty years.

This was still after deducting the boosts from things like the Green Parasol Mountain cave dwelling, the Alchemy Saint Blessed Land, and various pill and formation aids.

Using only an ordinary fourth-rank spiritual land for cultivation!

If he added those, Luo Chen was confident he could minimize this time to the shortest!

"Sage Late Stage is waving to me."

"The threshold of Ascendant also has great opportunities for me to touch in the future."

"The future is truly bright!"

As his mana gradually recovered to its peak, Luo Chen's mood became increasingly cheerful.

Suddenly!

He abruptly opened his eyes and looked across the small stream.

An old man with graying hair, a face full of wrinkles, and an aura of decay and decline emanating from his entire body was looking at him with a smile.

Luo Chen stood up and said in surprise, "Sir Xuanyan? You..."

He remembered very clearly that when Sir Xuanyan appeared earlier, although he was quite old, his aura was vigorous and his complexion was ruddy. How could he now be in such a state of being at death's door?

Sir Xuanyan seemed unconcerned about his own change. He said casually, "I have long passed the thousand-year lifespan limit of an ordinary Sage cultivator. The reason I've lingered for over a hundred years is merely by relying on the Nether Origin Sect's resources and life-extending methods. The way I looked earlier was just a flash of returning radiance before death."

Luo Chen understood. He had thought Sir Xuanyan's earlier performance was completely different from the rumors.

So it was just a flash of returning radiance.

But why had he suddenly declined now, with even his entire aura growing weaker and weaker?

As he wondered, Sir Xuanyan slowly raised his right hand, and a white token came into Luo Chen's view.

"This is?" Luo Chen was puzzled, but a guess was forming in his mind.

Sir Xuanyan did not keep him in suspense and said directly, "This is precisely the fourth trial, and also what Chu Yan left behind. Only by unlocking it can one be considered to have fully obtained the Dao of Alchemy inheritance."

Luo Chen stared intently at the token, but soon shook his head.

"I don't know what's inside it, but the first three trials have already benefited me immensely."

"Since this inheritance is already in your hands, Senior, Luo Chen congratulates you!"

Sir Xuanyan raised an eyebrow, somewhat surprised by Luo Chen's words.

After all, this was the Alchemy Saint inheritance!

But to Luo Chen, the gains from the first three trials were indeed too great.

The first trial had consolidated his foundation in the Dao of Alchemy.

The second trial had allowed him to create a technique for reverse-refining pills—the Dust-Returning Art—and understand the essence of medicinal materials.

The third trial had allowed him to refine the Two Modes Pill, a high-grade pill that would surely be of great use, and the success on his first attempt had greatly boosted his confidence, leaving him without any fear of unfamiliar pills in the future.

Only at this moment did Luo Chen truly possess the bearing of a grandmaster, from the inside out!

Even when facing the famous Sir Xuanyan directly, he was full of confidence. He might lack some experience, but when it came to alchemy, he considered himself no weaker than anyone!

A mere inheritance—since it had already fallen into another's hands, why forcefully take it?

Sir Xuanyan looked at Luo Chen in surprise, his hand held in the air somewhat awkwardly.

"Unexpected. You are even more outstanding than I imagined!"

"No wonder Chu Yan thought highly of you, and no wonder the three of the Nether Origin Sect opened the Alchemy Saint Hall early for you."

"This item, I give to you!"

*Whoosh!*

As Luo Chen stared in astonishment, the white token was lightly tossed over by the other party, as if this was not an inheritance left by an Ascendant Alchemy Saint, but a worthless Qi-Nourishing Pill.

"Do you know why I came specifically to find you?"

"I do not know, sir."

"Because only you have the chance to open this token. As for me, I ultimately fell short by one step!"

Sir Xuanyan said slowly, his tone somewhat desolate, his demeanor quite lonely.

Luo Chen did not know why the other party said this. His gaze fell on the warm white jade token in his hand, and all his questions were instantly resolved.

(Chapter End)
