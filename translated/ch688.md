# Chapter 688: Becoming Devil, Becoming Demon, and Also Becoming Human

"Xuyi Remnant Race: like snakes and horses, their bodies covered in scales. Four feet grow from the belly. Twelve great sinews and ninety-six minor sinews, matching the number of the minor heavenly circuit..."

"Horned Devil Race: shaped like humans, with towering horns upon the head. A single horn absorbs the power of wind and thunder, making them brave beyond resistance..."

"Strange-Eye Race: a single eye grows between the brows, commanding the three strange powers of petrification, air-ban, and soul-capturing. Also called the Odd-Eye Race. According to legend, they are the propagated bloodline of the Candle Dragon, one of the three great ancient devils of the Devil Realm. They are naturally weak and frail, but their single eye cannot be looked at directly. Their whole body's apertures are blocked, totaling one thousand and eight. When all apertures open together, they complete the devil body and open the devil eye..."

"Po Luo Race..."

As Luo Chen expected, aside from information on all kinds of medicinal materials, the bamboo slips in the study of the third trial also recorded a vast amount of information concerning the races of the Devil Realm.

Some bamboo slips even had illustrations for comparison!

Though made with only a few sparse strokes, the artistry was almost numinous. One look made a person feel as if a great devil stood before their eyes.

In truth, it was not only the artistry. The calligraphy on these bamboo slips was also extremely profound, and contained a special power. Even if Luo Chen did not recognize the characters, one glance allowed him to understand the true meaning within.

If all these bamboo slips had truly been personally written by Devil Lord Heaven-Refining, then the ability contained within them was truly astonishing.

Returning to the topic,

Through reading the bamboo slips, over half a year Luo Chen's understanding of that Devil Realm he had never seen became increasingly deep.

Strange races of every kind, unimaginable abilities, appearances ugly or beautiful... and bodies of incomparable complexity!

More than a thousand races gathered in one realm. The differences between their bodies meant that the pills used by each race were different.

"When I first saw the third trial's examination question, I was beside myself with joy and thought it was merely body-tempering spirit pills, something I could accomplish with ease."

"Now that I understand the distribution of races in the Devil Realm and the structures of most devil bodies, I realize that this simplicity contains no simplicity at all!"

That Luo Chen could feel the matter was thorny showed where the problem lay.

The third trial did indeed require him to refine the simplest body-tempering pills, but the pills he refined had to possess strong "universality."

They had to break the boundaries of devil races, allowing most devil races to use such pills, strengthen their body cultivation realm, establish a good foundation, and pursue the true-devil realm countless devils dreamed of.

In this way, it was no longer simple.

The more ordinary a pill, the more extreme it had to be.

"I was a little too pleased with myself, and underestimated the Pill Realm's inheritance assessment."

Murmuring, Luo Chen put down the bamboo slip and slowly paced within the study.

In his mind, there were many inspirations, but now it seemed that none were especially suitable.

Some were too niche and suited only to one or two races. Others had strong universality, but their medicinal force was low, and might not meet the assessment requirements of this trial. If he spent great effort refining them only to obtain an inferior-low evaluation, it would be a loss not worth the gain.

At this moment, Luo Chen's nostrils twitched lightly.

A faint scorched smell drifted from afar.

He looked in that direction and saw the Pill Room doors open as a cloud of black smoke drifted out.

Then Cape Heaven Matron's soot-covered figure appeared in his vision.

She had failed again!

The corners of Luo Chen's mouth lifted slightly.

In this half year, how many times had the other party failed?

Seventy times? Eighty? Or perhaps more than a hundred times when he had not been paying attention?

How could Cape Heaven Matron fail to notice his schadenfreude? Her face darkened.

"Hmph!"

Seeing that she was about to burrow into the Pill Room again, Luo Chen hurriedly spoke.

"Fellow Daoist, one person's plans are short, while two people's plans are long. Why not take out the formula you have derived and discuss it with me?"

Cape Heaven Matron smiled without warmth. "Then should this old body thank you for your kindness?"

Luo Chen first waved his hand. "No need to thank me. We are fellow travelers, so mutual help is only natural!"

Then he pointed at himself with a self-satisfied face. "With my alchemy attainments, which let me break through two trials in crushing fashion, giving you some guidance should not be a problem."

Cape Heaven Matron's expression sank.

She said coldly, "Did no one ever teach you to respect the old and cherish the young? Even if you do not understand etiquette, you should at least understand the principle that the accomplished come first! When you surpass this old body, you can come point fingers."

"Hmph!"

After speaking, she snorted coldly and burrowed into the Pill Room again.

After she left, the smile on Luo Chen's face gradually faded.

A pity.

He had wanted to look at the other party's research results, to avoid some of the pits of failure.

But after such verbal squeezing, at a time when the other party had just failed, it should leave a rather large psychological shadow in Cape Heaven Matron's heart.

As long as there was some influence, that was enough.

"Next, I should also pare away the complex and keep the simple, and research the foundational pills most suitable for the majority of devil races to refine the body."

Luo Chen waved his sleeve, brushing away the pungent scorched smell in the air, then closed the study doors.

...

"Failed again!"

"How could it fail?"

"Demon and devil body refinement clearly share common points. Why does my formula fail again and again?"

Inside the Pill Room, Cape Heaven Matron constantly questioned herself, confusion appearing on her face.

She did not think the problem lay with her alchemy.

With her strength as a grandmaster of the Dao of Pills, facing a formula she had researched herself, and a comparatively low-tier foundational formula at that, successful refinement should be the simplest thing.

Therefore, the problem must lie in the formula itself!

"Could it be that Abyss Grass has too tyrannical a medicinal force?"

Pinching a clump of black ash between her fingers, Cape Heaven Matron fell into deep thought.

After thinking for a long time, she decided to return to the study and look through those bamboo slips again.

The answer was surely hidden within them!

Creak...

The study door was pushed open. Cape Heaven Matron, heavy with thoughts, looked only once before her pupils contracted slightly.

Unlike the previous half year, this time the human cultivator called Devil Lord Azure Yang was no longer wandering among the shelves. Instead, he sat before the desk, writing swiftly.

This scene made her heart suddenly turn heavy.

"So fast?"

"The knowledge recorded on this place's bamboo slips may not be as vast as a sea of smoke, but it is no less than a mountain of books. He used only half a year to read it all. He has even... already reached the stage of writing a pill formula!"

Thinking of the powerful confidence the other party had revealed in his words earlier, as well as his record of breaking two trials in overwhelming fashion, the old woman clenched her fist almost imperceptibly.

"I cannot see through this person's realm. When facing me, he is neither servile nor overbearing, so he must also be among the ranks of Sages."

"But his alchemy attainments must be extremely profound, especially in creating pill formulas... Could he be an ancestor from some Sage-level upper sect, or even a direct descendant of an Ascendant Holy Land?"

"If that is truly so, then this will be difficult for me."

The old woman wandered between the bookshelves. From time to time, her gaze fell upon the young, handsome man bent over the desk by the window.

Her thoughts rose and fell, and distracting thoughts flew everywhere.

When it came to the Dao of Pills, the human race was absolutely above the demon race.

Although she, Cape Heaven Matron, came from the Cape Heaven clan, which was skilled at recognizing spirit medicines, when it came to alchemy inheritance, she was still lacking.

In her early years, in order to advance her Dao of Pills, she had hunted and killed large numbers of immortal cultivators to obtain alchemy books.

She had even risked herself, personally venturing deep into human territory to attend alchemy gatherings and exchange insights with formidable alchemists.

It was precisely by relying on effort bit by bit that she achieved what she had today.

But if she were truly compared with those upper sects and Holy Lands that had inherited knowledge for thousands or tens of thousands of years, her foundation was ultimately far too lacking.

If Devil Lord Azure Yang truly surpassed her later and broke the third trial ahead of her, then this trip would amount to drawing water with a bamboo basket, all for nothing.

Thinking of this, the old woman's expression grew increasingly gloomy.

"It is all Shark Emperor Liujun's fault. If he had been willing to give me the alchemy books he obtained from Yuan Demon Sect, how could I be struggling like this now?"

"If I obtain the alchemy inheritance this time, then fine. But if I fail, then the friendship between my Cape Heaven clan and the jiaoren race..."

As she thought and thought, Cape Heaven Matron suddenly froze.

"What is wrong with me?"

At that very moment, she looked toward the spot by the window. The handsome young man was raising his head and looking at her quietly.

Their eyes met in silence, then shifted away from each other.

Cape Heaven Matron seemed to understand something and cursed in a low voice.

"Despicable!"

...

No matter how vile the schemes or how base the methods, they did not affect one's attainments in a craft.

The so-called innocent heart or sincere Dao, in Luo Chen's view, merely referred to the degree of investment in a certain path. It had nothing to do with good or evil.

Countless attempts, countless successes, unceasing reading, and more than a hundred years of accumulation had, even if he could not be said to have absorbed the strengths of a hundred schools, laid an extremely solid foundation in the Dao of Pills.

This foundation played a supremely great role in the Pill Realm's three trials on self-created pill formulas!

Regarding devil-race body-tempering pills, Luo Chen spent a year and a half writing several dozen formulas.

Afterward, he also realized that devil-race body refinement was similar yet different from the human race's qi-blood transformations and the demon race's spiritual-qi body tempering. It was closer to the physical polishing of archaic beasts.

Only, in this process, it also mixed in some body-refining concepts from the human and demon races.

Using the body as primary and qi as auxiliary, it walked an extreme, domineering path!

After understanding this point, he suddenly saw through everything.

If he wanted to refine pills for those on this path, then the medicinal power had to be presented with the force of an unrestrained flood, not bound by mud or sand!

Simply put, the auxiliary materials had to be strong, and the main materials had to be even stronger. Only then would the refined pills possess terrifying medicinal force.

The bodies of the devil race were innately stronger than those of humans and demons, and the medicinal force they could bear was naturally greater as well.

Therefore, he only needed to act boldly!

Following this line of thought, Luo Chen screened out the overwhelming majority of formulas, leaving only nine.

Among those nine formulas, most focused on skin, flesh, and bones, while the part concerning blood and apertures occupied only one or two.

Afterward, he went into the Pill Realm to gather spirit medicines and brought them back to grind and process.

During the process of personally handling these medicinal materials, he eliminated another six formulas, leaving only the three with the roughest style.

Then he used the system to verify and confirm their completeness.

At this point, all things were ready. He only had to begin refining!

During this process, Cape Heaven Matron had been coldly watching from the side.

She could naturally see that the medicinal materials Luo Chen gathered were all types with violent medicinal power.

Because of that, amid her bellyful of doubts, she also felt some contempt.

"He does not distinguish host and guest, does not understand how to pair strong and weak. Could it be that I overestimated him?"

While doubtful, Cape Heaven Matron's own hands did not stop.

She had used three years to break open the divide between immortal and mortal in the first trial, one year for demon-soul scouring in the second trial, and now this devil-body foundation tempering had consumed a full three years!

It was time to test the result.

This time, she was very confident!

The Pill Room of the third trial had always been used by her. Devil Lord Azure Yang had never stepped inside even once.

Over three years, after countless failures, perhaps success would be in the very next attempt.

The old woman began refining pills again.

Three months later,

The old woman's eyes were bloodshot as she stared fixedly at the pill furnace burned red-hot, the feeling of imminent success in her heart growing stronger and stronger.

This time, she would certainly succeed. She would certainly pass!

And that boy who had built a hut outside and was refining pills would once again be left behind by her.

"He has only been refining pills for three months. A large amount of trial and error has only just begun. Compared with me, the gap is too great!"

"I fell for this boy's trick before and let him disturb my mind. This time, I am walking ahead of him, and now it should be his turn for his footing to fall into chaos."

Thinking of this, Cape Heaven Matron's face revealed a pleased smile.

Yet before that smile could fully bloom, a weak voice exploded from the air with a rumble.

[Stone-Skin Pill. Pill formed: third-tier lower-grade. It can help low-tier devils temper skin and flesh.]

[Thunderbone Pill. Pill formed: third-tier lower-grade. It can help low-tier devils temper bones and marrow.]

[Mercury-Blood Pill. Pill formed: third-tier lower-grade. It can help low-tier devils temper blood.]

[Assessment result: superior-high. Passed!]

Boom!

Inside the Pill Room, the medicinal furnace exploded.

The old woman could no longer care that everything had been abandoned midway. With a wave of her sleeve, the door opened, and she rushed straight out.

Within her field of vision, a brilliant light enveloped the red-robed Daoist, and space seemed to twist and collapse.

The Daoist looked at the white-haired, bloodshot-eyed old woman and nodded slightly.

"Fellow Daoist, I will go on ahead."

As his words fell, the pillar of light dispersed, and the Daoist's figure had already completely vanished.

Looking at this dreamlike scene, the old woman's whole body trembled, her face full of disbelief.

"How is that possible?"

"How could he refine the correct pills in such a short time?"

"Third tier! With such a high grade, the medicinal power must be terrifying beyond compare. How could low-tier devils endure it for foundation tempering?"

"Impossible. Absolutely impossible!"

The old woman's murmurs gradually grew louder, almost like shouts.

Yet this time, no one responded to her.

The look Luo Chen had given before leaving was calm and peaceful, as though he had never treated her as an opponent in the contest for the alchemy inheritance at all.

Being ignored was the greatest contempt!

Cape Heaven Matron's state of mind had become somewhat unbalanced.

...

Sun and moon turned, and a new heaven replaced the old.

Luo Chen absorbed the dense spiritual qi of heaven and earth within it, feeling his Golden Core leap restlessly inside him.

As expected, after three trials, there was still a fourth, as if endless.

But he understood that the alchemy inheritance of a Void Refining True Lord would naturally not have such simple assessments.

What he needed to do was keep walking step by step, no matter how many trials remained.

Previously in the third trial, his advantages over Cape Heaven Matron, an alchemy grandmaster, had been fully revealed!

Relying on years of body-tempering experience, he broke the question faster. With the system's help, he screened formulas faster. Even the concrete refining process, because he had used achievement points to enter the formulas, took only three months before he directly entered them and refined the first furnace of pills.

Such thunderous momentum must have given that old woman a tremendous shock!

However, Luo Chen could sense that the other party was getting closer and closer to success. His small methods probably could not obstruct her for long.

Next, he had to work even harder.

With a faint smile, Luo Chen's eyes suddenly focused.

A voice entered his mind, like a great bell and vast drum, echoing without end.

"Devils envy the demon's changeability, demons admire human wisdom, and humans covet the devil's purity. As spiritual leaders of heaven and earth, each has its strengths. We alchemists enter the Dao through pills, and should break the limits between devil, demon, and human."

"Please refine the three pills of Devil-Transformation, Demon-Transformation, and Human-Transformation."

"The formulas are as follows..."

In an instant, Luo Chen stood still where he was, falling into contemplation.

This trial did not test foundational alchemy understanding, but concrete pill-refining technique?

*(End of Chapter)*
