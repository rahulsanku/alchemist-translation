# Chapter 675: Ghost Vines, Old Trees, Dusk Crows, and a Third Person

The crying of crows rose and fell in the forest.

Hoarse, desolate, as though people were wailing.

As the sound entered his ears, Luo Chen glanced at the dark treetops around him. Every ancient tree here had a strange shape. Twisted trunks rose from the earth like old bones, and black vines hung down from them in dense curtains, swaying even when there was no wind.

Occasionally, a dusk-black bird would perch among the branches, its eyes shining with a dim yellow light. When it opened its beak, the mournful cry that echoed through the forest came from it.

"Those are Duskvine Crows," Tai Sui said as he walked ahead.

Luo Chen's gaze moved slightly. "A demon beast?"

"Not exactly."

Tai Sui shook his head. "They are a kind of demon spirit unique to Hundred Crow Ghost Forest. One body, two souls. One soul belongs to the ghost vine wood spirit, and the other belongs to the dusk crow demon beast. The two depend on one another and also parasitize one another. Over time, they fuse into a single entity."

Moya Cave Master, who had been looking everywhere with greedy eyes, could not help asking, "How does a vine and a crow become one thing?"

Tai Sui smiled faintly. "Ghost vines love death qi. Wherever ghost vines grow, dusk crows often gather to nest. Dusk crows live on carrion and death qi, while their droppings and corpses nourish the vines. After thousands upon thousands of years, there will naturally be some unusual changes. When a ghost vine gains spirit and wishes to leave its rooted place, it will lodge its soul in the body of a dusk crow. From then on, vine and crow share life and death. That is a true Duskvine Crow."

Luo Chen looked at the black vines hanging from the trees. "Then the vines we are seeing now..."

"Some are ordinary ghost vines. Some may be the bodies of Duskvine Crows."

Tai Sui's tone grew more serious. "Do not touch them."

Moya Cave Master quietly withdrew the hand he had just stretched toward a gray-white branch.

Tai Sui continued, "Duskvine Crows are not physically powerful, but they are extremely troublesome. Their innate illusion art is called the Dusk Curtain [TERM?]. Once you fall into it, you will repeatedly see the sun sinking into the western mountains. Your mind will lose its sense of time. You will think only a moment has passed, but outside, your body may already have been strangled into dried meat by ghost vines."

Luo Chen's heart tightened slightly.

Illusion techniques again.

Ever since he entered Demon-Fall Land, he had encountered too many methods that targeted the soul and the mind.

First there was Liujun's bewitching art, then the demon-devil echo in the Demon-Devil Heart Tree cave, and now this Dusk Curtain.

He had cultivated for so many years and had always taken his spiritual sense to be quite solid. Yet when placed before truly brilliant soul arts, that foundation seemed far less reassuring than he had imagined.

Han Zhan's voice sounded in his sea of consciousness. "Do not stare too long at any one crow."

Luo Chen immediately drew back his gaze.

Tai Sui did not notice the exchange. He led them deeper through the ghost forest.

The deeper they went, the stranger the trees became.

There were spirit woods that resembled blazing torches, their leaves faintly glowing like morning sun. Tai Sui called them Rising Sun Wood [TERM?].

There were silver-white deadwoods with lightning marks crawling across their bark. Those were Flash Thunder Wood [TERM?].

There were gloomy trees whose trunks seemed to contain distorted faces. Tai Sui said those were Hell Illusion Trees [TERM?], and even he told them to keep away.

There were also frost-blue trees with needles like crystal. They were Frostlight Wood [TERM?].

Many more spirit woods Luo Chen could not identify at all.

What surprised him most was not the rarity of these spirit woods, but the fact that they could all grow together in one place.

Some liked blazing yang qi. Some liked thunder. Some needed extreme yin and death qi. Others required cold, damp soil.

According to ordinary spirit-planting principles, trees with such different requirements should never have been able to coexist in such harmony.

Yet here, they crowded together as though the contradictions between their natures had been smoothed flat by an invisible hand.

'This place is not just a forest,' Luo Chen thought. 'It is more like a living spirit-planting array.'

Suddenly, his steps paused.

Not far away stood a black ancient tree.

Its trunk was neither pine nor locust, yet somehow resembled both. The bark was as dark as ink, and its crown spread out like a cloud. There were no flowers or fruits on it, but the aura it emitted was extraordinarily gentle. Just looking at it made Luo Chen feel a faint coolness pass through his soul.

Han Zhan's voice rose sharply. "Soul-Nourishing Tree!"

Luo Chen's eyes narrowed.

He knew this tree.

Soul-Nourishing Wood was one of the rarest materials for nurturing remnant souls and stabilizing primordial spirits. The small wooden tablet Han Zhan lived in had once been made from a piece of Soul-Nourishing Wood.

But before him now was not a piece of wood. It was an entire Soul-Nourishing Tree.

Even Tai Sui stopped and looked back. "Your eyes are not bad. This Soul-Nourishing Tree has lived for more than three thousand years. Even in the old days, it was a rare thing."

Han Zhan's breathing seemed to grow heavier in Luo Chen's sea of consciousness.

Luo Chen looked carefully and saw a shallow scar on the trunk, roughly three inches long.

It was very close in size to Han Zhan's wooden tablet.

His expression moved slightly.

At that instant, the entire world turned dim yellow.

The forest disappeared.

A vast sunset hung at the end of the horizon. The dying light was thick and hazy, like a curtain of yellow gauze covering heaven and earth.

Luo Chen's heart jolted.

"Dusk Curtain!"

He had clearly guarded his mind, and Han Zhan had been watching from within his sea of consciousness. Even so, he had still been drawn into the illusion without warning.

Han Zhan's voice rang out coldly. "Do not panic. This is a powerful illusion, but there is no killing intent."

Luo Chen steadied himself.

The sunset before him was too real.

The clouds were red. The wind carried the warmth of the day's last light. Even his own shadow stretched long across the ground. If he had not known where he was, he might truly have believed that he stood on some wilderness plain, watching dusk descend.

"Can an ordinary second- or third-tier Duskvine Crow do this?" Luo Chen asked inwardly.

"No," Han Zhan said. "Not even an ordinary fourth-tier one. This is most likely the work of a fifth-tier Duskvine Crow."

Luo Chen's expression sank.

A fifth-tier existence.

In other words, a being comparable to a Sage.

"But it has no malice," Han Zhan added. "It is only screening us off."

Luo Chen understood at once.

Tai Sui had brought them into Hundred Crow Ghost Forest not merely to pass through.

He was meeting old acquaintances.

Since those old acquaintances did not wish outsiders to overhear, they had casually lowered this Dusk Curtain.

Luo Chen let out a breath and simply stood where he was. "Soul arts, charm arts, illusion arts... Senior Han, after this trip, I truly feel like my soul is full of holes."

Han Zhan gave a light laugh. "You say that, but you yourself also use Mirror Flower, Water Moon."

"That is different. I use it to bully others. I do not like being bullied by others."

"A very honest double standard."

Luo Chen did not deny it. "Do you have a method to defend against or break free from such arts?"

Han Zhan was silent for a moment, then said, "If the gap in soul foundation is too large, prevention is difficult. But after being struck, there are still ways to awaken oneself. I know three methods."

Luo Chen's eyes lit slightly. "Please teach me."

Han Zhan did not immediately agree.

After a while, he said slowly, "Do you still remember why I came to Demon-Fall Land?"

Luo Chen's expression became serious.

Borrowing a body. Rebirth.

Han Zhan wanted to find a suitable body here and seize it, restoring himself to life.

"At the critical moment, I will need you to help me take a human Sage body," Han Zhan said plainly. "Not a demon beast. Not a spirit. A human Sage. It may be dangerous."

Luo Chen was silent.

Han Zhan continued, "Over the years, I have helped you many times. Even if I said nothing, when that moment came, you probably would not stand aside. But some things should be spoken clearly in advance. Favors are favors, and conditions are conditions."

Luo Chen did not answer immediately.

The yellow sunset remained before him, sinking by a hair's breadth with each breath, as though an eternity would pass before it disappeared.

At last, Luo Chen said, "Teach me first."

Han Zhan laughed. "Good. The first method is called the Aperture-Opening Art. It is especially suitable for you. You cultivated the Origin Pill Art, and you also have the Locked Pearl Curtain. With those two as foundation, engraving this art on the second Origin Pill will not be difficult."

While Han Zhan began explaining the method in Luo Chen's mind, another conversation was taking place elsewhere in the forest.

Deep within a dense cluster of black vines, Tai Sui was bound layer upon layer.

Those vines did not tighten, nor did they injure him. They merely wrapped around him like old arms.

An aged voice came from the depths of the vines.

"You returned."

Tai Sui smiled bitterly. "Old Ghost [TERM?], long time no see."

"Since you escaped, you should not have come back."

"A remnant thought escaping hardly counts as escape," Tai Sui said. "My main body is still trapped in Falling Demon Abyss."

The aged voice was calm. "The True Lord said we must not touch the inheritance. You were too greedy."

Tai Sui's smile disappeared. "I only did not want to guard this place forever. Besides, the True Lord is already dead."

"The True Lord is unmatched. Heavenly Origin could not suppress him. Rosycloud could not kill him. He merely took the wrong path and used another's hand to begin again."

Tai Sui sneered. "You still believe that?"

"I do."

Tai Sui did not argue further. He knew this old thing's temperament.

He said, "I came this time not to ask you to help me. I only hope you and Woodman will not block my way."

"Have you found a way to leave?"

"Thirty percent certainty," Tai Sui said. "The True Lord's countermeasures are nothing more than small arrangements. If I pay enough of a price and borrow several formation techniques, I can leave Falling Demon Abyss. After that, I will use the teleportation array in the inheritance land to go outside."

The vines trembled slightly.

Tai Sui lowered his voice. "Old Ghost, the outside world is very good. The spiritual qi has not dried up. The Mountains and Seas Realm still exists. There are even archaic beasts alive."

For the first time, the aged voice showed emotion. "Archaic beasts..."

"If you want to leave, I can give you the method. Give one copy to Woodman as well. Whether you dare use it is your own business."

Two jade slips flew from Tai Sui's hand and sank into the black vines.

After a long time, the aged voice said, "Those three are your helpers?"

Tai Sui was about to nod, but suddenly froze.

"Three?"

Aside from Moya Cave Master and Devil Lord Azure Yang, where had a third person come from?

The dim yellow sunset before Luo Chen's eyes suddenly shattered.

The forest returned.

Tai Sui was standing not far ahead, looking no different from before.

He said lightly, "The ancient trees here have spirits. Do not touch anything."

Moya Cave Master, who had quietly taken out a small shovel before a fourth-tier Diamond Wood [TERM?], stiffened. He laughed dryly and hid the shovel behind his back.

Luo Chen's gaze drifted past the Soul-Nourishing Tree and no longer lingered on it.

Tai Sui looked at Luo Chen for a moment longer than usual.

Luo Chen smiled. "Fellow Daoist Tai Sui, you promised to bring us through safely."

"There was a small accident," Tai Sui said.

"A fifth-tier accident?"

Tai Sui pretended not to hear the sarcasm. "Since we are already here, after leaving Hundred Crow Ghost Forest, there will be Wood Heaven Plain. Many spirit herbs grow there. Some are useful for forming the Sage. Some are body-tempering herbs from the demon world. Fellow Daoist Luo, you may be interested."

Moya Cave Master's eyes immediately brightened.

Luo Chen's heart also moved slightly.

His original plan was indeed simple. Cross Hundred Crow Ghost Forest, cross Wood Heaven Plain, pass through Yue Canglan, and then enter the third heaven, Bright Radiance Heaven, where the inheritance lay. After fulfilling his promise to Tai Sui, he would wait for the reverse passage to open and leave Demon-Fall Land.

But if there were useful body-tempering spirit herbs along the way, he had no reason to ignore them.

Tai Sui smiled when he saw their expressions.

"Then let us go."

The group gradually left the depths of the forest.

After their figures disappeared, the black vines behind the ancient trees slowly retracted.

A glossy black crow descended from the sky and landed on a half-withered, half-flourishing old tree.

The bark of the old tree wrinkled and gradually formed an old human face.

"Why did they come?" the old tree asked.

The black crow opened its beak, and a hoarse voice emerged. "The three-hundred-year spiritual intake period has arrived. The gates of the traveling palace will open. Tai Sui returned for this."

"He wants to leave with his main body," another old voice said from the ground, where black vines twisted like snakes.

The old tree was silent for a long time.

"Can he succeed?"

"Who knows?"

The black crow lowered its head and preened its wing.

The ancient tree face slowly closed its eyes.

"Then let him try. If he succeeds, perhaps one day we will also see the world outside."

The crow cried once.

Its voice was still hoarse and mournful, but beneath that endless dusk, it seemed to carry a trace of longing.

*(End of Chapter)*
