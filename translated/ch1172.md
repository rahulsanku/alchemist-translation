# Chapter 1172: The Vulture Alliance, Great Luo Five Dragon Hands

"The third ring?"

Faced with Luo Chen's sudden visit, the Purple Haze City Lord appeared somewhat caught off guard, especially by the question he asked.

She knew better than anyone what that meant.

The Purple Haze City Lord hesitated for a moment, then couldn't help but advise: "Brother Luo, you've only been in the Nine Rings of Ruin for a mere ten years. Why not consolidate your realm a little longer before rushing to a more dangerous place?"

Luo Chen smiled, maintaining his resolute stance.

The Purple Haze City Lord sighed and ceased her persuasion, instead beginning to explain her understanding of the middle three rings.

"The chaotic energy follows a spiraling downward decreasing trend. The lower you go, the thinner the concentration; the higher you go, the denser it becomes. Correspondingly, the realm requirements for martial artists are also higher."

"Therefore, to set foot in the middle three rings, one must at least have reached the mid-stage of the Leakless Realm."

"Otherwise, it's very difficult to withstand the erosion of chaotic energy."

"But!"

"Having the realm alone is not enough, because the number of cultivation stones in the middle three rings is extremely limited."

"As far as I know, the sixth ring has no more than thirty-six cultivation stones in total, the fifth ring has only eighteen, and it's rumored that the fourth ring, which only late-stage Leakless Realm martial artists can set foot in, has already dwindled to a single-digit number of cultivation stones!"

...

As Luo Chen listened to the Purple Haze City Lord's introduction, he recalled Fang Zhibai's earlier reminder to him.

Cross-referencing the two, there wasn't much discrepancy.

In other words, the martial artists in the sixth ring were at least mid-stage Leakless Realm experts.

Moreover, because the number of cultivation stones in the fourth ring was limited, there might even be some late-stage Leakless Realm martial artists forced to remain in the fifth and sixth rings.

This phenomenon wasn't particularly strange.

After all, in any cultivation system, the further one progresses, the slower the speed.

He himself was the exception!

Because of the Primordial Unity Cauldron, he could greatly enhance his cultivation efficiency.

What would take ordinary people over a hundred years to barely break through a minor realm, he could achieve in just a few years.

Of course, there was a prerequisite!

That was that the external energy supply must keep up.

And this was precisely the main reason Luo Chen frequently changed cultivation stones.

The Purple Haze City Lord's introduction continued.

"The transfer of cultivation stones follows that set of so-called unwritten rules, but there is actually some room for manipulation. If you have familiar friends, during the gap period, you can proactively initiate a challenge, put on a show of force, and bypass the rules."

"It's because I lacked friends that I fell into the predicament of being forcibly challenged by Old Monster Qu ten years ago."

"But the martial artists in the middle three rings are different. Apart from a few, most of them are older-generation martial artists who entered the Nine Rings of Ruin two hundred years ago, or even three or four hundred years ago. They've long since figured out this unwritten rule, or rather, this rule was created by them. To avoid trouble, they've formed alliances, united externally, and never engage in internal conflict."

"For example, the Vulture Alliance was initiated and established by Dugu Cheng, the master of the Single Vulture Merchant Alliance, and it has no fewer than a dozen martial artists within it."

"If Brother Luo wants to enter the middle three rings, it's best not to offend the Vulture Alliance, or trouble will come one after another."

Luo Chen recalled the old man he had briefly met during the trading fair.

Dugu Cheng?

He had actually established a force within the Nine Rings of Ruin.

From this perspective, the martial artists here weren't solely focused on cultivation.

Thinking of this, Luo Chen became curious about one thing.

"Cultivation within the Nine Rings of Ruin is quite dangerous. At the very least, one can be injured or severely wounded, enduring a hundred years of suffering before being able to leave and seek spiritual medicine. At worst, one can fall on the spot, disappearing without a trace. If it were those with firm Dao-seeking resolve, it would be nothing more than a bitter cultivation session. But there are all kinds of people in the world. There must be those with weak wills, or those who see their own potential limits. Why don't they leave the Nine Rings of Ruin and return to their original cities to enjoy life?"

Faced with this question, the Purple Haze City Lord was somewhat surprised instead.

"Brother Luo, do you think the Carefree Token is so easy to accept?"

Luo Chen suddenly understood and blurted out four words, "The Carefree Realm Lord?"

The Purple Haze City Lord nodded gently, her tone even becoming softer.

"Every hundred years, the envoys representing the Carefree Realm Lord tour the seventy-two cities and issue Carefree Tokens to every martial artist who breaks through to the Leakless Realm. The holder must head to Carefree City when the hundred-year period expires, and finally enter the Nine Rings of Ruin for cultivation."

"Unless one is inherently unsuited to this environment and returns after failure, one cannot easily leave the Nine Rings of Ruin. But those people, after returning, usually don't live much longer anyway."

"In the past, there were those who disobeyed the order, but their fate was often to be eliminated."

"Over time, no one dared to ignore this prohibition anymore."

This explanation was perfectly reasonable.

In fact, it also explained why Fang Zhibai had previously advised him to reduce his exploitation of other major cities.

The Carefree Realm Lord desperately sought fellow Daoists who could study the way of leaving the Guixu together.

To this end, he imposed almost harsh restrictions on Great Destruction Realm martial artists.

High-level martial artists could not affect the growth of low-level martial artists, and the acquisition of resources had to be controlled to a certain extent.

Once low-level martial artists grew up, they had to enter the Nine Rings of Ruin for cultivation, striving to reach higher realms and become fellow Daoists of the Carefree Realm Lord.

Anyone who defied the order would likely face the personal intervention of a Martial Dao Human Immortal to be eliminated.

Thinking about it this way, Xiang Fei had exploited a loophole.

She should have returned to await death, but because she met Luo Chen, who healed her injuries, she was able to avoid the harsh cultivation environment.

But on second thought, Luo Chen felt it was just an extra hundred or so years of life.

Xiang Fei's aptitude was mediocre to begin with. She barely broke through to the Leakless Realm at over three hundred years old, waited a hundred years to enter Carefree City, cultivated bitterly for thirty years, failed and returned, and then was enslaved by Luo Chen for another hundred years.

Calculating carefully, she had unknowingly reached the age of six hundred.

In the Great Destruction Realm, the lifespan limit for Leakless Realm martial artists was only eight hundred years, and there was a lack of methods to extend life.

Perhaps the next time he went out, Xiang Fei would have already passed away.

The Purple Haze City Lord looked at Luo Chen and offered a suggestion.

"The transfer of cultivation stones doesn't necessarily have to be obtained through challenges."

Luo Chen asked, "What do you mean? Like before, can I exchange pills for them?"

The Purple Haze City Lord shook her head. "I imagine Brother Luo doesn't have many of those precious pills on hand. However, there is another path. The martial artists in the middle three rings, and even the upper three rings, are mostly older, and many are nearing their lifespan limits. Brother Luo could wait a few more decades, and perhaps a vacancy will open up."

Having said this, the Purple Haze City Lord smiled sheepishly.

"I don't plan to force my way into the sixth ring. I want to use this method to wait for the next opportunity."

However, Luo Chen waved his hand. "I'm sorry, but I can't wait."

With that, he turned and left.

Before leaving, he informed the Purple Haze City Lord that she could go to her previous cultivation stone.

This was considered payment for the information she had exchanged.

Not long after.

Luo Chen arrived at the central platform leading to the sixth ring.

Looking up at the space above, his calm face was full of confidence.

"Among the thirty-six cultivation stones, there should be a place for me!"

...

The space within the Ninefold Immortal Tower was not boundless; in fact, it wasn't very large.

But those within it often felt a sense of boundlessness.

Especially when cultivating alone for hundreds of years, that feeling of loneliness and insignificance became increasingly pronounced.

Cui Zhuo was such a person.

For this reason, he had actively joined the Vulture Alliance, so he could find people to exchange ideas and discuss the Dao during his leisure time, alleviating his loneliness and boredom.

Over time, he had become very popular within the alliance.

He even had a considerable reputation outside.

Moreover, joining the alliance had the added benefit of not having to worry about being challenged and having his cultivation stone taken.

Especially during the centennial period!

Ordinary people would only think it was the stage when a large number of new martial artists entered the Ninefold Immortal Tower, but in the eyes of these older-generation martial artists, they knew that after a hundred years, the previous batch of martial artists who persevered would often have achieved something. Then these people would become restless and try to obtain a better cultivation environment.

Challenges often occurred during this stage.

He had heard that this situation had already appeared in the sixth ring ten years ago, with the challenger being a female martial artist from the seventh ring.

To prevent himself from being disturbed, Cui Zhuo had specifically asked his friend to put on a show three years ago, so he could enjoy some peaceful time.

As long as this stage passed, there would be no need for such trouble later.

"Today, who should I invite for tea and discussion?"

Cui Zhuo felt a bit restless. Ten years ago, he had bought a lot of good tea and fine wine in Carefree City and brought them in to pass the boring time.

But not every martial artist was as indifferent to cultivation as he was.

Therefore, he had to rotate who he invited for tea to avoid becoming annoying.

Just as he was pondering, the corner of his eye suddenly froze.

A blood-red escape light shot straight towards him from the dark void.

"A stranger?"

Cui Zhuo frowned, then put on a smile and took the initiative to stand up and greet him.

"You look unfamiliar, sir. I am Cui Zhuo. Would you care to stop and chat, perhaps make a friend?"

The white-clothed man paused, his gaze bypassing him and constantly examining the huge boulder beneath him, which was a full three zhang in size.

Unlike other cultivation stones, this boulder had a stone membrane constantly deployed, shielding the storm-like chaotic energy nearby.

Furthermore, a small pavilion had been built on it, complete with a stone table, tea set, cattail hassock, incense burner, and so on.

It was said that one shouldn't strike a smiling face, but the visitor's words instantly wiped the smile off Cui Zhuo's face.

"This place is not bad. I've taken a liking to it."

Cui Zhuo suppressed the anger in his heart and said as calmly as possible, "Perhaps you don't know, but I was only challenged three years ago. If you want this place, you'll have to wait another seven years!"

However, the white-clothed man was quite domineering, waving his sleeve.

"No need to wait seven years. Let's do it now!"

Cui Zhuo said angrily, "Do you want to break the rules? Aren't you afraid that the one who started it will suffer the consequences, and that endless trouble will follow? I'm afraid you'll make a mistake, so I must tell you, I have many friends."

"Troublesome!"

The white-clothed man said, then suddenly reached out his right hand, and streaks of blood-red astral qi condensed into form.

Cui Zhuo understood that today's matter could not be resolved peacefully. No matter how good his temper, he couldn't help but lose his cool.

"Very well! Let me see what you're capable of!"

As he spoke, he flew away from the cultivation stone, first setting up an astral qi protective shield around his body, then drawing his long sword, ready for battle.

In his line of sight, the astral qi on the white-clothed man's right hand transformed into long, snake-like shapes, vivid and lifelike.

This move looked familiar.

"Old Monster Qu's Five Dragons Flourishing Spirit?"

But in the next moment, he saw the five giant pythons covered in scales, with sharp, angular horns growing on their heads, and even four five-clawed dragon claws sprouting from their bellies.

In a moment of shock, the five blood dragons, baring their fangs and brandishing their claws, roared forward.

Wherever they passed, even the chaotic storms of the sixth ring were completely pushed aside.

This scene startled Cui Zhuo, but his long sword had already slashed out.

Chi!

Chi!

Chi!

One after another, sword qi crisscrossed, forming a large net that enveloped the five dragons.

Being able to make so many friends wasn't just because of his good personality; his considerable strength was the real reason people respected him.

This "Sword Net" technique was one of his three great killing moves!

Those unfamiliar with it, even late-stage Leakless Realm martial artists, would easily suffer a great loss upon first encounter.

But then a scene that made Cui Zhuo's eyes burn with rage appeared.

The five blood dragons roared and chose to meet the sword net head-on.

Although they were shattered inch by inch under countless sword qi strikes, and in the blink of an eye only two of the five blood dragons remained, these two had already broken through the sword net, charging straight at him from front and back.

Cui Zhuo certainly didn't intend to offer his neck for the slaughter. He held his long sword and soared into the sky.

His body merged with the sword light, achieving a unity of man and sword, and pounced straight at the blood dragons.

Chi!

In the blink of an eye, one blood dragon was pierced through from head to tail.

But before he could execute his next move, the last blood dragon grabbed down at him.

Boom!

With just one strike, amidst the clash of astral qi, it broke through his protective shield, and the five-clawed dragon hand grasped him in its palm.

All of this, though lengthy to describe, happened in an instant.

The outcome was already clear.

Cui Zhuo never imagined he would be defeated so quickly, so easily.

From beginning to end, the opponent had only used one move!

His face bitter, he asked, "What is this move called?"

The white-clothed man replied, "Didn't you say it? Five Dragons Flourishing Spirit."

Cui Zhuo shook his head. "I've fought Old Monster Qu. His Five Dragons Flourishing Spirit absolutely cannot reach this level. Moreover, the subtle changes within it surpass Old Monster Qu's."

The white-clothed man stroked his chin, not expecting this martial artist, who rarely used weapons, to have such keen insight.

Indeed!

This move originated from the *Five Dragons Flourishing Spirit*, but he found the original technique too crude and had added some of his own understanding, making its changes more agile.

Even if Old Monster Qu were resurrected and saw this move, he would only find it similar in form but not in spirit.

This move was still in its early stages of creation. He intended to continue perfecting it, to bring it to a higher level.

"Then let's change the name. You can call it the Great Luo Five Dragon Hands."

"Great Luo Five Dragon Hands..." Cui Zhuo was stunned, only to find that the dragon claws on his body had loosened. "You're not going to kill me?"

The white-clothed man smiled. "Although we can't be friends, there's no need for a life-or-death confrontation. You may go!"

Clearly, it was Cui Zhuo's initial friendly attitude that made him show mercy.

Cui Zhuo's face alternated between red and white. Finally, he cupped his hands and asked, "May I ask your esteemed name?"

"Are you thinking of finding someone to take revenge?" the white-clothed man asked, but before he could answer, he said casually, "I am who I am, and I never change my name. Luo Chen is my name!"

"Luo Chen?"

Cui Zhuo muttered the name, feeling it sounded somewhat familiar, but he was sure he had never seen this person before.

Could he be a rising star who had just entered the Nine Rings of Ruin?

But it had only been ten years. How could he have cultivated so quickly and already broken into the middle three rings?

Finally taking a deep look at Luo Chen's young face, Cui Zhuo turned and left.

(End of this chapter)
