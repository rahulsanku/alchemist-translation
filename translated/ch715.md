# Chapter 715: Lifespan Surges, Bones Ground and Ashes Scattered

Strange peaks overlapped, and jagged rocks stood everywhere.

Hidden by the forest, the waters of the emerald pool were deep and quiet.

The white-robed Daoist sat upright on a blue stone, his back to the pool.

Below him, Black King, whose body had shrunk, stood respectfully with Heavenjade and Sang Jinghe [TERM?].

Their faces all carried excitement and joy.

Luo Chen, however, was somewhat emotional.

How fallen he had become!

In the past, whenever his realm rose, whether a Foundation Establishment ceremony or a Golden Core ceremony, which time had not had disciples rushing about, guests gathering, and lively celebrations held for him?

He still remembered that during the Golden Core ceremony, various sects had come to congratulate him, and Danxia had seen unprecedented splendor...

Now, after finally breaking through to the Sage realm and becoming one of this realm's first-class great figures, only a few stray cats and dogs had come to welcome him out of seclusion.

Of course, while wandering in exile, there was no need to worry too much about these minor points.

No matter what, this was a day of great joy. Even if there was no need to share his Nascent Soul formation insights, the blessings that ought to be shared still had to be shared somewhat.

Thus, Luo Chen first examined the cultivation situation of his dependents.

"Jinghe [TERM?], your cultivation progress is not bad. In such a short time, you have already reached Initiate Perfected Completion."

Sang Jinghe [TERM?] respectfully said, "All thanks to Sage's grace in raising me!"

Calling it short, in truth, it was not short. Without realizing it, thirty years had already passed since coming to Dragon Abyss Island [TERM?].

Benefiting from the island's abundant spirit medicines and dense spirit qi, Sang Jinghe's [TERM?] progress was only natural.

Luo Chen smiled. "Since you have already reached completion, next you should begin preparing for Core Formation. I have already bestowed three Condensed Liquid Pills [TERM?] on you. Do you have a suitable Core Formation secret art?"

Sang Jinghe [TERM?] nodded. "Before Third Grandfather sat in meditation and passed away, he gave me one. Only, my aptitude is dull, and I can only cultivate it to minor success, so I do not have much confidence in Core Formation."

"Oh, let me see that secret art."

Sang Jinghe [TERM?] did not hesitate, immediately offering a jade slip respectfully.

Luo Chen probed inside with divine sense and swept through it, already having a grasp in his heart.

If judged by grade, the Core Formation secret art Sang Jiugong had left behind was fairly decent, among middle-grade arts.

However, in Luo Chen's current view, it seemed somewhat insufficient.

After a little thought, combined with Sang Jinghe's [TERM?] own aptitude situation, Luo Chen's divine sense moved.

In no more than the time to drink a cup of tea, additional content appeared on that jade slip, involving the key points of the intersection between spiritual sense and mana.

"Take it and cultivate properly. There is no need to start over. Skip the subsequent content of Sang Jiugong's secret method and directly switch to the method I revised. It should increase your Core Formation chance by another ten or twenty percent."

Luo Chen said this with a smile and pushed the jade slip back into Sang Jinghe's [TERM?] hands.

Holding the thin jade slip, Sang Jinghe [TERM?] was incomparably excited.

This was a secret art personally bestowed by a Sage!

Especially since it could be considered a Core Formation secret art tailor-made for him!

Luo Chen smiled faintly and looked toward Heavenjade.

This time, he did not ask about her cultivation progress, instead directly raising his hand and handing over a new jade slip.

Heavenjade accepted it in surprise, and with one glance, read in confusion, "Huan Yu Secret Canon?"

"Correct!"

Luo Chen slowly said, "Different from the first version, I have revised the Huan Yu Secret Canon this time. Part of it involves how to transform your constitution and use that constitution to accelerate cultivation. You must study it carefully. Perhaps before forming your Nascent Soul, you will have the chance to cultivate a true Xun Wind Body [TERM?]!"

When these words came out, surprise appeared on Heavenjade's face.

The Xun Wind Body [TERM?] on her was incomplete!

This point, Han Zhan had also said before.

After soaking in the Spirit-Molding Sacred Spring [TERM?], Heavenjade's bones and aptitude had indeed improved a great deal, but she had only slightly gained signs of the Xun Wind Body [TERM?].

Luo Chen had felt the benefits of constitution improvement.

The Fire Spirit Body let his cultivation speed become extremely fast during ordinary cultivation. When advancing to the Sage realm, relying on this constitution, he could make the Origin Embryo [TERM?] condensed by the Golden Core greatly mobilize external heaven and earth spirit qi, thereby forming a resonance.

By analogy, other single-attribute spirit bodies should also have this effect.

The Xun Wind Body [TERM?] was no exception!

Therefore, combining the characteristics of his own Fire Spirit Body and the evolution path of an archaic beast flesh body, he deduced the Huan Yu Secret Canon from a higher perspective and produced an even more optimized version.

As long as Heavenjade cultivated properly, she would have the chance to achieve a true Xun Wind Body [TERM?], making her future advancement to the Sage realm smoother as well.

Black King stared at this scene, and for some reason, a common saying among human cultivators appeared in his mind: when one person attains the Dao, even chickens and dogs rise to heaven.

At this moment, did it not perfectly fit this saying?

Then what about him?

Facing Black King's eager gaze, Luo Chen laughed and scolded him.

"Do not think about it. Ever since you changed to the archaic beast path and stepped into fourth tier, I have not studied your body. How could I guide you through your confusion?"

Black King's raised little head drooped, listless.

However, Luo Chen's next words made him begin showing off.

"Tell me, after reaching fourth tier, what changes do you have?"

Immediately, Black King began showing off.

The Undying Body [TERM?] was one of them, which had already been revealed before.

And this time, Black King displayed several new abilities.

One was becoming large or small.

When large, he could swallow clouds and spit mist; when small, he could hide in the smallest space and conceal his form.

This ability seemed ordinary, but in truth it was extraordinary.

In the past, although Black King could also change his body size, there were limits. He often felt stifled and complained endlessly because he had shrunk his body.

But this time, he could shrink to palm size and lie on Luo Chen's shoulder very comfortably.

When growing larger, his body could even exceed a hundred zhang, reaching three to five hundred zhang, and he still had strength left, seeming able to continue swelling further.

At this size, if combined with the devouring demon art born when he was third tier, its might was simply unimaginable.

Luo Chen also felt fortunate. If when Han Zhan suddenly attacked back then, the controlled Black King had enlarged his body to the limit at once and used his devouring demon art, perhaps Luo Chen's Nascent Soul would have been swallowed into his belly in an instant.

Aside from this extreme form-changing ability of becoming large or small, Black King also displayed another ability that made Luo Chen look sideways, even greatly startled.

"Master, wait a moment!"

Black King returned to his normal hundred-zhang body, then lay prone on the ground.

His eyes slowly closed, his entire body relaxed freely, and before anyone knew it, he was making thunderous snores.

Falling asleep so quickly?

False Sleep Art [TERM?]!

With one look, Luo Chen understood Black King's small method, but he found it strange. Was the other party not going to display an ability? Why had he fallen asleep in front of him?

But in the next moment!

Luo Chen's expression became startled and uncertain. He only felt countless drifting strands of heaven and earth spirit qi gather toward Black King.

The range covered a hundred li, a thousand li, and even drifting spirit qi from ten thousand li away was spontaneously gathering toward Black King.

Although the farther away it was, the less spirit qi gathered over, there was indeed a tendency like young swallows returning to the nest!

So much spirit qi surged over. A small portion merged into Black King's body and was absorbed by him.

But most of it coiled near Black King's body and slowly sank into the ground.

"This is the Stone Dragon remnant soul's ability!"

Luo Chen spoke one word at a time, and wild joy arose spontaneously in his heart.

He had never expected that when he had only intended to waste one incomplete fifth-tier soul to save Black King, Black King would actually inherit this ability.

After he fell asleep, his natural breathing and exhalation could actually draw a response from heaven and earth and gather drifting spirit qi.

Looking at it now, the true coverage range of this ability was within a thousand li.

If he found a way to expand it and stabilize it within ten thousand li, combined with an already high-grade spirit vein, could the idea he had once imagined be realized through Black King?

The ox-like snoring stopped abruptly, and Black King woke.

"I did not discover this ability before either. But when I rested in the sea, every time I woke, many, many low-tier demon beasts had gathered around me. At that time, I did not think much of it and treated them as food delivering itself to my door."

"But some time ago, when Dragon Abyss Island [TERM?] experienced that strange spirit qi eruption, I moved onto the island to cultivate. As a result, the spirit qi gathering effect became better and better, and the affected range also grew larger and larger."

As he spoke, Black King suddenly paused.

"Right, I forgot to tell Master that I found the reason for Dragon Abyss Island's [TERM?] spirit qi eruption!"

Luo Chen withdrew that wild joy and asked curiously.

"What is the reason?"

Of course he knew the changes Dragon Abyss Island [TERM?] had undergone in the past ten years. Compared to before the tribulation, it was simply as different as heaven and earth.

If outsiders saw it, they would think it was the carefully managed mountain gate of some great sect.

Everywhere, spirit qi was abundant, strange flowers and unusual grasses grew vigorously, and even the Dragon Earthworms [TERM?] that had previously fled had subconsciously moved back, along with some other wild low-tier demon beasts.

Black King's face became somewhat solemn. "The earth's crust moved, and the earth vein fractured!"

As soon as these words came out, Luo Chen's heart sank.

Without asking further, he directly released his divine sense.

Its coverage area instantly surged from the few hundred li of the Adept stage to a thousand li away.

Not only that, this range continued to expand.

One thousand, two thousand, until three thousand li, where it slowly stopped.

Feeling that vast divine sense, Black King's dragon eyes widened instantly.

Master's divine sense was actually this strong?

He did not know that Luo Chen's divine soul foundation had always been outstanding among peers!

The stronger the foundation, the greater the increase after raising realm.

Added to the fact that during these ten years, Luo Chen had refined quite a lot of Han Zhan's soul power and supplemented an additional portion.

Therefore, ordinary early-stage Sage cultivators' divine sense coverage was only around one thousand li, and the stronger ones only about two thousand li.

Yet Luo Chen had directly broken through this limit at Sage Stage One!

Wait, Master's realm?

Not mentioning Black King's shock, Luo Chen's mood slowly sank.

He leapt into the air and circled Dragon Abyss Island [TERM?] at extreme speed, finally reaching a conclusion similar to Black King's.

The earth vein of this spirit island had indeed shown signs of fracture, and they were irreversible!

Originally, this island's earth-vein pattern was one of concealed feng shui and sunken spiritual potential.

Shown externally, this meant large amounts of spirit qi were compressed within the earth abyss, rarely manifesting outside.

But now, large amounts of spirit qi were erupting outward, creating this flourishing scene that looked like brocade flowers but was in truth oil boiling over fire.

If compared to a person, this was final radiance before death, not far from death.

Only, because this island's pattern was very large and its foundation sufficient, this final radiance lasted extremely long, to the point that even after ten years, it had not reached its end.

"The culprit should be my tribulation, followed by the battle between Black King and the white tiger. Combined, they accidentally shook the spirit vein pattern of this place."

Luo Chen murmured.

In his heart, the thought that this place could not be stayed in for long had already arisen.

As for his mood, it was neither good nor bad.

Because he had not intended to continue cultivating in seclusion on Dragon Abyss Island [TERM?] in the first place.

There were many reasons to leave: deducing a main cultivation method after Sage advancement, auxiliary resources for cultivating the Soul-Splitting Art [TERM?], the dangers lurking everywhere in the Demon Sea, and certain people and matters he cared about.

Taking a deep breath, Luo Chen returned to the emerald pool.

Immediately, he issued two orders.

"Black King, I allow you free entry into the earth abyss. Search for all supreme-grade spirit stone raw ore, and also determine the locations of upper-grade ones."

"Jinghe [TERM?]... forget it. Jinghe [TERM?], focus on cultivation and strive to advance to the Adept stage within ten years. Heavenjade, you handle this matter. Collect all graded spirit soil on Dragon Abyss Island [TERM?] in full!"

As soon as he said this, everyone understood Luo Chen's meaning.

He intended to leave.

Perhaps tomorrow, perhaps next year, or perhaps not long from now.

Heavenjade said softly, "Master, that white tiger puppet..."

"I will find time to subdue it."

"Then how should this be handled?"

Spiritual light flashed in Heavenjade's hand, and a withered corpse appeared before them.

Han Zhan's flesh body... no, Feiyunzi's flesh body!

On this joyful day of emerging after Nascent Soul formation, seeing such a corpse truly made one unhappy.

Heavenjade had originally not intended to bring this matter up today. It was mainly that Luo Chen's display of urgency had affected her.

Luo Chen looked at the flesh body at his feet, his eyes narrowing slightly.

He was not worried that Han Zhan had left any backup outside.

Possession was a matter that required full commitment. Under those circumstances, Han Zhan could not possibly have had any remaining strength.

But this flesh body involved not only the grudge between Luo Chen and Han Zhan, but also Feiyunzi's descendants.

At his fingertip, a point of azure flame floated out.

Before the three of them, the flesh body of a Sage Sage of his generation turned into black ash on the ground.

"Scatter it."

"A Northern Sea person, a Northern Sea soul."

"Burying him in the sea can also count as fallen leaves returning to their roots."

Black King waved a claw, scraping away three inches of earth and carrying those ashes with it.

"Leave this to me!"

Before long, Black King appeared above the sea and casually scattered large amounts of soil mixed with ashes into the seawater.

Not only that, demon fish and sea beasts gathered competitively, pecking at those ashes.

Ashes formed from a Sage's flesh body were a great supplement to them!

Watching this scene, Black King chuckled.

He had no thought of respecting powerful enemies. He only knew to share Master's worries and solve his problems.

This flesh body, whether when it was Feiyunzi alive or old thief Han Zhan after death, had troubled Master endlessly. It deserved to have no bones left!

...

After returning to the cave dwelling, Luo Chen calmed his mood.

Then he naturally opened the attribute panel.

The originally complicated panel data also gradually became clear at a glance under his editing.

[Spirit Roots: Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, Earth]

[Constitution: Fire Spirit Body (Five-Spirit Dao Body Hidden)]

[Realm: Sage Stage One 1/100, Archaic Tier Four 40/100]

[Soul Arts: Mirror Flower Water Moon [TERM?] Great Completion, Spirit-Awe Art [TERM?] Great Completion, Mao-Opening Art [TERM?] Proficient 240/300]

Looking at the brand-new panel, Luo Chen felt comfortable in his heart.

The greatest change was none other than the surge in lifespan once again.

One thousand five hundred years...

Hahaha!

Luo Chen could not help laughing. Compared to the fantasy of longevity from his youth, this could be considered a solid, real step forward!

It was rumored that after cultivators broke through to the Sage realm, their lifespan doubled and would reach a thousand years.

He had now not only reached a thousand years, but even had five hundred more.

The change within should be because of his Archaic Tier Four flesh body.

From this point, as long as he did not recklessly start conflicts, this lifespan alone was enough to outlive those of the same realm.

Of course, that would truly be too lacking in ambition.

Smiling, Luo Chen examined the complicated data.

His realm reaching Sage Stage One was within expectations.

The heaven and earth phenomenon during his tribulation had been rare in the world.

Golden lotuses surged from the earth, and the heavenly ladle poured dew.

After he passed the lightning tribulation, these two great phenomena had poured who knew how much pure spirit qi over his newly condensed Nascent Soul, both helping him condense the Nascent Soul body and causing his cultivation to advance greatly.

Added to absorbing the pure mana left behind on Han Zhan's Nascent Soul body, it was only natural that he had stepped solidly into Sage Stage One.

This was good!

When normal cultivators formed a Nascent Soul, merely consolidating the realm and stepping into Sage Stage One would take thirty to fifty years.

Was that not precisely why Dragon Abyss True Being of the Myriad Immortals Assembly had secluded himself for a full fifty years and rarely gone out?

Back then, after Han Zhan formed his Nascent Soul, he had also quietly cultivated for several decades before daring to come out and challenge Jade Cauldron True Being [TERM?].

In this regard, Luo Chen had truly saved far too much time.

And this saved time naturally had great use.

His main cultivation method!

Looking at the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture entry, only one hundred proficiency away from Great Completion, Luo Chen fell silent.

Inside Bright Radiance Heaven [TERM?], after obtaining the Five-Spirit Dao Body [TERM?], he had once wanted to change his main cultivation method after forming his Nascent Soul.

But subsequent changes had caused him to fall from the Five-Spirit Dao Body [TERM?] to the Fire Spirit Body.

Even if the system panel displayed that the Five-Spirit Dao Body [TERM?] was only hidden and not completely lost, his current constitution also meant that he could only continue down the fire-attribute path.

In that case, the main cultivation method suitable for the Sage stage would be restricted.

Only after a long time did Luo Chen sigh.

"First cultivate this Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture to Great Completion and see whether there are any changes!"

"If after Great Completion it still maintains a decent cultivation speed, then I will continue this method. If not, after returning to the cultivation world, I must consider collecting other fourth-tier fire-attribute cultivation methods."

The latter was a backup plan.

And merely thinking about it, it would be extremely difficult.

Fourth-tier cultivation methods were priceless no matter where they were placed!

At lightest, they were the core of personal cultivation, and at heaviest, they were the sect inheritance treasure. How could they be so easy to collect?

Luo Chen then looked toward the Soul-Splitting Art [TERM?]. It had already reached Beginner.

It had not been in vain that he had endured the torment of soul splitting day and night for these ten years.

At this moment, within his sea of consciousness, aside from the main soul, there was another formed soul fragment.

The two had the same aura, but the difference in strength was clear at a glance.

Luo Chen's current soul fragment was roughly at the Adept stage in strength, and was still too weak.

There were two reasons for this. First, he had cultivated the Soul-Splitting Art [TERM?] for too short a time. Second, the cultivation method lacked the corresponding secret art for accelerating growth.

Regarding the latter flaw, Luo Chen already had a solution in mind.

That was the True Lord alchemy inheritance!

A pill formula appeared in Luo Chen's hand.

Three words were written on it: Great Soul Pill [TERM?]!

This was a fourth-tier pill specially for human cultivators to strengthen their divine soul foundation, and it was also among the thirty-six pill formulas.

Previously, when Luo Chen was treating Black King, he had once considered this pill formula.

But at that time, he feared Black King could not withstand the Great Soul Pill's [TERM?] powerful medicinal force, and he also lacked the relevant medicinal materials, so he had dismissed the idea.

Now, however, this pill formula had a use.

"The main material, I have seen it, yes. But collecting such a large quantity will be somewhat troublesome."

As Luo Chen murmured, a trace of worry appeared between his brows.
