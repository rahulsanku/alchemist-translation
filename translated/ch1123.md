# Chapter 1123: Drifting Alone, a Maiden in a Broken Hut

In the lightless, colorless deep universe, endless dust floated.

These specks of dust varied in shape and size.

The smallest were barely visible, while the largest were like landmasses, bearing no trace of any living creature. They drifted silently through the cosmos, wandering in solitude, quietly awaiting the erosion of time.

But it was not always so quiet.

Occasionally, a fortunate piece would pass by a star, only to be pulled in by its powerful gravity, finally settling down—dust returning to dust, earth returning to earth.

Within this endless dust, a thousand-zhang giant drifted silently.

The aura of death was no different from any other piece of debris, but aside from that, a faint layer of green-white flame spread like the veins of a tree across its body, protecting it.

This flame was very dim. It seemed that merely shielding the giant had exhausted all its strength, leaving it unable to do anything else.

Suddenly!

The green-white flame seemed to sense something and trembled violently.

In the extremely distant distance, a flash of light appeared and vanished in an instant.

Following closely behind, waves of mysterious power rolled in like a tide.

Wherever they passed, dust was swept up, utterly powerless to resist.

Fragments of planets and rocky mineral landmasses were mixed within, constantly colliding. The fragile ones were ground into powder.

The giant was also caught in the tide.

Its body was indestructible; anything that came close was shattered.

But the tide surged for an immeasurably long time. Under the relentless collisions, the giant's unconscious body gradually became damaged.

The green-white flames that covered its entire body could, in the end, only protect a few key areas.

In this cosmic drift, time had lost all meaning.

No matter how eternal an existence might be, it would eventually be worn away.

Even though the giant's body was extremely sturdy, even though it was protected by the green-white flames, under the constant cosmic tide, its destruction was only a matter of time.

Suddenly!

A wisp of gray light rose from the giant's broken body.

This gray light appeared abruptly, yet it seemed to have existed for a long time.

It gradually changed shape, eventually transforming into a gray butterfly.

If the giant's consciousness had been awake, the moment it saw this gray butterfly, it would have understood its essence—it was the power of a Tribulation Gu left behind by a certain Divine Lord from long ago.

At this moment, the gray butterfly seemed to realize something and flapped its wings with great force.

As the butterfly's wings stirred, the surging cosmic tide inexplicably shifted course.

In the end, the gray butterfly vanished completely, and the giant was pushed by the tide toward an unknown destination.

Blessings dwell in misfortune; misfortune lurks within blessings.

Within a deadly tribulation, new life was hidden!

...

In the midst of chaos, golden ripples suddenly spread.

Then, countless thoughts, as numerous as the sands of the Ganges, came flooding in.

Colliding with each other, they felt like stars exploding, bringing an intense sense of impact.

"Ugh..."

A pained groan sounded clearly in the cramped room.

On a bed made of stacked bricks and wood, a man lay.

If one looked closely, they would see his eyeballs trembling beneath his eyelids, accompanied by low moans of pain.

Suddenly!

His eyes snapped open, revealing a pair of pupils that were majestic and unfathomable, deep and inscrutable.

"Huff!"

When his eyes opened, the turbid qi that had accumulated in his body was also expelled in a single breath.

But just this one action seemed to pull at every part of his body, sending countless sharp pains from every corner.

An ordinary person would likely have fainted by now.

But the man's will to endure pain far exceeded anyone's. Though large beads of sweat broke out on his face, he did not cry out again.

He just lay quietly on the stone-and-wood bed, staring at the low ceiling.

His eyes did not move, as if he were in a daze, or perhaps thinking.

Time passed continuously, and the light filtering through the cracks in the roof grew dimmer.

Boom!

A sudden clap of thunder rumbled from outside.

The thunder seemed to startle the man, and his brow furrowed instinctively.

This thunder carried an awe-inspiring, unpredictable majesty, completely different from any thunderclap he had ever heard.

It was as if all living beings must submit to it; to dare to touch it would surely mean the destruction of both body and soul!

The man had to be grateful that he had not released his divine sense, or perhaps a single clap of thunder might have shattered his soul.

But what made him even more bitter was that he was not lucky enough to have refrained from releasing it—he simply could not extend it at all.

His vast, majestic soul, like a prison, seemed to be sealed away, completely unable to be mobilized.

Helpless, the man had to laboriously shift his gaze to look around.

A low, leaky roof, walls made of broken planks, a table made of a single stone, an iron chain hanging from the center of the room, and in the corner, some tools that looked like sickles and hoes... Finally, there was the cold, hard stone-and-wood bed beneath him and the blanket with a special fragrance covering him.

Everything was simple and clear at a glance.

And just this simple act of observing his surroundings seemed to have drained all the man's energy.

When he withdrew his gaze, he could already hear the sound of pouring rain outside.

Inside the house, there was also the rustling sound of rain.

Drip!

Drip!

Under the heavy downpour, rainwater gathered into streams, and some droplets fell into the room through the cracks in the roof.

For a moment, a feeling of darkness and dampness arose.

The man quietly felt all this, and a hint of bitterness twisted his lips.

In his entire life, even in his most difficult times, had he ever fallen into such a dire predicament?

And all of this was thanks to that person!

"De..."

The overwhelming hatred was stuck in his mouth, which could barely open.

At this moment, even speaking was difficult for him.

The man's eyes slowly closed.

Just then, amidst the heavy rain outside, hurried footsteps approached.

Then came the clattering sound of metal, as if reflecting the flustered movements of the person opening the door.

Click!

As the lock fell, the wooden door was pushed open.

A flash of light came and went, and the door was quickly closed again.

A figure entered the house, shivering as they spoke.

"So cold, so cold!"

As soon as they entered, they quickly put down what they were carrying and went to the center of the room.

It seemed they bumped into something, letting out a cry of pain, accompanied by the slight clinking of the iron chain.

Chi...

A wave of warmth spread as a fire was lit.

Then came the rustling sound of taking off clothes, and the sound of fetching water to wipe the body.

After this person entered the house, all kinds of sounds arose, but they were all very soft, as if afraid of disturbing something.

Finally, as the fragrance of rice spread, it dispelled the unpleasant, cold, and damp feeling in the room.

"Time to eat!"

As if talking to themselves, or perhaps calling out to someone, the person carried a bowl and sat down on the edge of the bed.

They scooped up a spoonful of thin porridge and brought it to the man's mouth.

Just then, a pair of eyes slowly opened, staring quietly at the other person.

This caught them off guard.

The woman was startled, and the spoonful of porridge slipped, spilling onto the corner of the man's mouth.

The woman panicked and quickly took a cloth to wipe his mouth.

As she wiped, she asked, "You're awake? When did you wake up? What's your name? Did I burn you?..."

A barrage of questions came one after another. Her only answer was the man's hoarse voice, like sandpaper scraping, coming from his throat.

"Luo... Chen."

(End of chapter)
