# Chapter 1091: The Devil Lord's Handiwork, Spirit Treasure Buries Bones

The Dao Sect's three great spirit treasures—Star Gate, Burial Bone Umbrella, and Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard!

The Star Gate connected heaven and earth, penetrating the mystic realm, an invaluable spatial spirit treasure.

The Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard was even more miraculous, possessing the ability to gather spiritual energy and suppress the fortune of entire continents. In fact, after absorbing the memories of his clone Zhou Fuyou, Luo Chen's admiration for this treasure had only grown. He clearly remembered that when he had gone to the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven back then, the spiritual veins scattered everywhere were hardly suitable for cultivation. Yet when Zhou Fuyou's batch of disciples entered for the Sexagenary Grand Competition, they found many excellent third-order spiritual veins, fourth-order spiritual veins, and even a fifth-order spiritual vein that helped Ke Lianshan break through to Ascendant!

Such a drastic change was merely because the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard had nurtured the place for ten thousand years.

Thus, in Luo Chen's view, the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard also had the wondrous effect of transforming spiritual apertures and accumulating qi into veins.

In truth, this function should have been discovered long ago.

The Central Continent, with just one continent, possessed the Heaven-Earth Island, the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range, the two great Dao Palaces of Tiannan and Libei, and the sunken Western Continent—a full five fifth-order spiritual veins!

It crushed the other four continents with absolute superiority!

This was by no means natural formation. One could imagine what role the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard had played in this.

The only question was whether this spirit treasure had survived the explosion of the Netherwater Spring Eye.

As for the Burial Bone Umbrella?

Luo Chen gazed at the somewhat illusory bone umbrella beneath the blazing flames, uncertainty flickering in his eyes.

As if to verify something, he raised his hand.

An ancient great sword appeared in his grasp.

It was the Yuantu Sword!

Now, after Luo Chen had relaxed restrictions and nurtured it with his Sage-level true body's mana for a hundred years, the Yuantu Sword had finally taken the last step and achieved the rank of spirit treasure.

This was also the crucial factor behind why that final strike had been able to cut down Lian Cheng so effortlessly.

Using the sharpest spirit treasure flying sword under heaven to execute the perfected Thousand Skies Converging Streams—its power was far from a simple one-plus-one-equals-two. Lian Cheng's defeat under that strike was not unjust.

Now, as sword and umbrella reflected each other, Luo Chen's eyes lit up.

"Indeed!"

"The refining styles of the two are nearly from the same lineage."

"Could this umbrella be the handiwork of Devil Lord Heaven-Refining?"

Luo Chen had some knowledge of the art of artifact refining.

He could not be called a grandmaster, but he was at least at the level of a master refiner, having produced no small number of magic treasures and true artifacts in his hands.

Thus, he had a certain ability to distinguish the refining styles of various schools.

Now, in his view, both the Yuantu Blood Sword and the Burial Bone Umbrella possessed extremely distinct refining styles.

One emphasized slaughter, unmatched sharpness!

One emphasized defense, impervious to water and fire, invulnerable to blades and weapons!

This extreme refining style was relatively rare in the current cultivation world.

The magic treasures refined by most cultivators tended to pursue diversity and comprehensiveness, intending to use a small number of treasures to deal with all kinds of unexpected situations.

For instance, Luo Chen's lifebound treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, combined suppression, trapping, nurturing and refinement, as well as offensive and defensive effects.

Another example was Zhou Fuyou's Sea-Settling Pearl, which leaned toward defense but also had functions like suppression and restraint.

Luo Chen had only ever seen this kind of refining style—pursuing only a single power—from one refiner.

Devil Lord Heaven-Refining!

"According to what the Heaven-Refining Cauldron's artifact spirit said back then, Devil Lord Heaven-Refining refined a total of thirteen spirit treasures in his life. One was given to the Jade Pool Merchant Woman, one fell into the hands of Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign, and one remained within his own clan. Of the remaining ten, five were destroyed by Rosycloud, leaving only five stored in Bright Radiance Heaven."

"Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign is an ancestral figure of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. Could it be that this Burial Bone Umbrella was one of the spirit treasures obtained from Devil Lord Heaven-Refining?"

"Bestowed by him and brought into the Mountains and Seas Realm."

The more Luo Chen thought, the more his guess seemed plausible.

Otherwise, he could not explain why the style of the Burial Bone Umbrella was so completely different from the other two Dao Sect's town-sect treasures.

Now, recalling that battle with Lian Cheng, Luo Chen felt a trace of luck.

Using the Yuantu Sword to execute Thousand Skies Converging Streams was arguably Luo Chen's strongest killing move. Yet even when facing Lian Cheng, who was fully driving the Burial Bone Umbrella, it was still blocked for an instant.

The defensive power of this spirit treasure was truly beyond imagination.

However, unexpectedly, the Burial Bone Umbrella itself was flawed!

That unparalleled defensive ability had a negligible crack, as if it had been forcibly torn open by someone before.

Following that crack, Luo Chen had been able to strike decisively and forcibly slay Lian Cheng.

"I wonder what earth-shattering method could tear open such a defensive spirit treasure, ultimately creating an opportunity for me?"

Murmuring to himself, Luo Chen's gaze once again fell upon the bone umbrella.

He coveted this spirit treasure and had once tried to subdue it.

However, the reason spirit treasures were spirit treasures was that they possessed artifact spirits.

If the artifact spirit was unwilling, not to mention Luo Chen, even a true Ascendant great power would find it very difficult to forcibly subdue it.

In the twenty-plus years since obtaining this item, its artifact spirit had been hiding, refusing to meet with Luo Chen, clearly unwilling to acknowledge him as its master.

This greatly infuriated Luo Chen.

Because he needed to heal his injuries, he did not want to employ any gentle methods. Thus, he had simply chosen the most extreme approach—burning it day and night with earthly baleful fire.

Yet even so, the Burial Bone Umbrella's artifact spirit continued to resist stubbornly, as if preferring to be shattered jade rather than remain intact.

Now, Luo Chen vaguely had a method to subdue the Burial Bone Umbrella's artifact spirit.

"Little Tu, come out!"

With a thought, a bloody halo flickered on the Yuantu Sword, and a ferocious, savage little beast's phantom emerged.

Though ferocious and fierce, it appeared close and obedient before Luo Chen.

This was the Yuantu Sword's sword spirit!

After fully nurturing this sword to the spirit treasure level, the artifact spirit had emerged from ignorance and awakened its intelligence.

Along with this, some memories from the beast's past life had also been imparted to Luo Chen.

Its origin was very special—a type of demonic beast from the Demon Realm called a "Tusu."

This beast rarely appeared. When born, it would often devour its mother's power to strengthen itself. It was said to bear the evil reputation of a great ferocious beast even in the Demon Realm.

But Little Tu's fate was somewhat tragic.

When it was born, it still had eight other brothers, causing its power to be too dispersed.

Nine young Tusu cannibalized each other, and in the end, only Little Tu survived, but it was on the verge of death.

Devil Lord Heaven-Refining had encountered it by chance and fused it into a carefully refined true artifact sword. He had both the intention of making use of waste and seemed to want to experiment with creating an artificial artifact spirit.

Later, this sword passed through many hands and eventually fell into Luo Chen's possession. After drinking countless amounts of blood and undergoing sword qi refinement, it finally reached the spirit treasure rank.

Now, the Yuantu Sword had already claimed the lives of three great powers. That trace of ferocity had been thoroughly aroused.

Sensing Luo Chen's intention, it glared fiercely at the bone umbrella within the blazing flames.

With a low roar, it charged straight in.

Luo Chen's method was simple.

Since the umbrella spirit did not want to communicate with him, he would let the sword spirit, also an artifact spirit, communicate with it.

Both were artifact spirits, and both originated from Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's hands. They could be considered to have some connection.

It should be better than him forcibly subduing this treasure.

Watching the sword spirit Little Tu enter, Luo Chen waited with a smile on his face.

However, that smile disappeared from Luo Chen's face as the bone umbrella slowly opened.

He did not know what was happening inside.

He only knew that within the tightly closed bone umbrella, a sleeping aura had awakened, pressing down on the sword spirit with a powerful posture.

Although the sword spirit was at a disadvantage, that ferocity could not be suppressed.

The two collided continuously, causing the surrounding flames to shake unceasingly.

Seeing this, Luo Chen's expression carried a hint of worry.

"Maybe... forget it?"

"The sword spirit was only recently condensed. If it is damaged inside, the loss would outweigh the gain."

Just as Luo Chen was about to give up, the floating, tightly closed bone umbrella suddenly opened.

Whoosh!

A beam of blood light rushed out.

It manifested as the little beast's phantom, wagging its head and tail before Luo Chen, looking extremely pleased with itself.

As if asking for credit?

"Did it succeed?"

Luo Chen looked expectantly at the bone umbrella, which had opened like a flower.

A white, illusory figure gradually condensed into form.

It was a young girl, as beautiful as a flower, as stark as bone, with a sorrowful expression on her face.

Seeing Luo Chen, she gave a graceful bow.

"Little Bone greets Master."

Just like that?

Luo Chen was stunned and instinctively looked at the sword spirit.

Little Tu was rolling around on the Yuantu Sword, utterly carefree.

Truly, when one exhausts all schemes to no avail, success comes without any effort!

Luo Chen breathed a sigh of relief and then released a strand of divine sense. The Burial Bone Umbrella's umbrella spirit did not refuse and opened its own restrictions.

The moment they made contact, some vague memory fragments surfaced in Luo Chen's mind.

The most recent was a river of sword qi, like flowing stars, washing over.

The happiest was when the bone umbrella opened, and the young girl smiled radiantly.

And the most painful...

A lone island, a perilous cliff, the origin qi of heaven and earth trembling—a fire phoenix forcibly tore apart the tough umbrella surface!

Boom!

Luo Chen was struck as if by a heavy blow, suddenly jolting awake, shock in his eyes.

"Rosycloud!"

Looking again at the desolate and sorrowful young girl, Luo Chen already understood what had happened to her.

Back in the battle at Soulseverance Cliff, the Five Elders of Heavenly Origin had used this spirit treasure as the foundation to construct a peerless grand formation, attempting to trap and kill Rosycloud.

But the final result was a failure!

Rosycloud had forcibly broken the formation, slain four Ascendant great powers, and even escaped from Soulseverance Cliff under the attack of the Void Refining Great Desolate Condor.

Lian Cheng had survived by a fluke and brought the Burial Bone Umbrella back to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

But the severe damage Rosycloud had inflicted on the Burial Bone Umbrella could not be healed in a short time. Thus, it had created the opportunity for Luo Chen's heaven-startling sword strike to break through.

"It has been hard on you."

After the shock, Luo Chen's expression was complicated as he reached out and summoned the Burial Bone Umbrella.

So close!

It was only by a hair's breadth that this Burial Bone Umbrella would have become the sixth Devil Lord spirit treasure destroyed by Rosycloud.

Fortunately, the battle at Soulseverance Cliff had taken place within the Mountains and Seas Realm, which had constrained Rosycloud's power, thus preserving the Burial Bone Umbrella.

If it had been in the mystic realm where Rosycloud and Devil Lord Heaven-Refining had fought back then, the unrestricted peak Void Refining power would have been enough to destroy the Burial Bone Umbrella itself.

"From now on, you will follow me. I will find a way to repair you."

Caressing the barely visible yet shocking crack on the umbrella's surface, he could vaguely sense a trace of scorching power.

It was not from the earthly fire of the Dan Chen Hall, but a familiar flame.

Nirvana Sacred Flame!

If it was a wound caused by this flame, he might have a way to slowly repair it.

The white-clad young girl nodded gently and then slipped into the bone umbrella.

With a thought from Luo Chen, two streams of light flew into his body.

Within the Purple Mansion.

The miniature Sage sat cross-legged in the center, clad in black armor, holding a blood sword in hand, with a bone umbrella slowly opening above its head. Strands of mana emanated from the miniature Sage and were continuously absorbed by the bone umbrella.

In the distance, a fire bird soared and circled, while a withered fire tree drooped listlessly.

...

A day in the mountains, a thousand years in the world.

Within the Alchemy Saint's blessed land, even without a special time flow difference, the high-level cultivators cultivating here still felt no passage of time.

In a daze, a decade had passed since Luo Chen's return.

In those ten years, Luo Chen had both healed his injuries and used his own mana to nurture the Burial Bone Umbrella, deepening the connection between them.

The Initiation Pills he had refined in batches were also ready. Luo Tian Sect's cultivators would come to collect them and then deliver them to the various Eastern Wilderness sects Luo Chen had previously promised.

On this day!

Luo Chen was in a pavilion on a beautiful mountain peak, flipping through a scroll of diagrams.

The scene depicted on it was quite familiar.

It was the complete picture of Yanfu Mountain!

This was a panoramic map of Yanfu that Little Bai had painstakingly drawn over several decades, avoiding various formation restrictions and mobilizing countless little ghosts.

It marked the locations of each cave dwelling and indicated the approximate power of each formation.

In particular, two places were specially depicted.

One was the Heavenly Origin Palace, and the other was the Tianze Palace!

These were the former Dao fields of two of the three great ancestors of the Nether Origin Sect—Ancestor Heavenly Origin and Ancestor Heize—whose status was no less than that of the sect master's Tianming Palace.

The Tianming Palace had already been opened due to the great battle back then, the subsequent ghost attacks, and the emergence of the Netherwater Spring Eye. However, the Heavenly Origin Palace and Tianze Palace were still protected by formations. What treasures lay within made many people's hearts stir.

But unfortunately, since Min Longyu's fall, the person most skilled in formations in the Luo Tian Sect was Luo Chen.

And Luo Chen had to heal his injuries and refine his treasures, so he naturally could not spare the time to break those formations.

If he were to hire formation experts from outside, such as Tian Duzi and his ilk, there might be a chance to break them one by one. But then, he would inevitably have to share some of the contents within.

These formations are quite interesting. Perhaps after I become an Ascendant, I can try to break them.

Not perhaps—it should be said, definitely!

Yanfu Mountain was the true mountain gate Luo Chen intended for the Luo Tian Sect.

He could not allow Yanfu Mountain to remain buried forever in the Nether Abyss.

And to restart Yanfu Mountain, he would naturally have to break some of the large formations within.

Just as Luo Chen was pondering, his expression suddenly shifted, and he looked with joy toward a certain location.

A mysterious aura emanated from within a mountain peak.

Heaven and earth resonated, as if responding.

Between the sky and the earth, dark clouds surged, gradually forming a scene of "wind filling the tower, rain about to come."

This was the heavenly omen of Sage formation!

Logically, one could not undergo tribulation within a blessed land.

Like Canglong back then, Luo Chen had specifically taken him outside to undergo the Sage heavenly tribulation.

But now, the Luo Tian Sect no longer needed to hide. The blessed land's portal had been opened and was kept in a constant state of activation.

Moreover, because of Luo Chen's comprehension of the Water Origin back then, which had caused heaven and earth to tremble and connect with the outside world, the Alchemy Saint's blessed land and the outside world were now essentially inseparable.

Now that the heavenly omen had appeared, someone must be about to take that final step.

When lightning flickered, figures flew out from all over the blessed land, gazing toward that location.

Whoosh! Whoosh!

Two beams of escaping light landed before Luo Chen.

"Husband!"

"Senior Brother!"

Looking at Gu Caiyi and Canglong, Luo Chen smiled and nodded. He walked out of the pavilion with leisurely steps.

"It's Yingzhang!"

Gu Caiyi's face was full of joy. "Senior Brother Li has finally reached this step!"

Canglong's expression carried a hint of worry, as if recalling the dangerous situation when she had formed her Sage. If Luo Chen had not helped, she would have found it difficult to survive the thunder tribulation.

"Can Sect Leader Li succeed?"

Luo Chen smiled, his expression calm.

"No need to worry. Yingzhang's foundation is extremely solid. Few of the same rank can surpass him. With the ample preparations he has made over the years, it would be strange if he did not succeed."

Li Yingzhang was a true representative of accumulating steadily and bursting forth later.

Whether in the Qi Refinement stage or the Initiate stage, his cultivation speed was not fast, but every step he took was solid.

After reaching the Adept stage, due to sect affairs, his cultivation speed was dragged down considerably.

But there was also a benefit—in the day-to-day tempering, he had almost no weaknesses.

Whether in mana or divine soul, he could be said to be the foremost among the Luo Tian Sect's Adept cultivators now.

This was evident from the fact that he dared to be the first to declare a closed-door death seclusion.

Not long after that aura appeared, the heavenly tribulation took shape in the blink of an eye.

The subsequent development did not exceed Luo Chen's expectations.

Facing the heavenly might, he weathered the thunder tribulation, and golden lotuses surged from the earth to mold the Sage.

Amid the expectations of countless people, a long roar like a dragon's cry resounded through the blessed land.

As the dragon's cry faded.

The crowd flew toward Qinhua Mountain.

Spiritual energy dense, clouds and mist shrouding.

A refined middle-aged man walked out from within. At this moment, his face had lost some of its former steadiness and gained a bit of spiritedness.

"When the sky breaks, the spirit soul shines; in the depths of the nine firmaments, a jade pendant hangs!"

"Everyone, Li has not failed expectations!"

(Chapter End)
