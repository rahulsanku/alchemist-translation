# Chapter 932: Parting in Life and Death, Clouds Scatter and Rain Falls

When Divine Origin Sage's physical body was destroyed and his Nascent Soul forcibly transformed into a Primordial Spirit, countless streams of heaven and earth origin qi were attracted, forming tidal surges that converged toward him.

Where the tidal surges passed, the void trembled, earth dragons turned over, and the formations binding the spirit veins were shattered one after another, vast amounts of pure spiritual qi gathering within.

In the nine heavens above, fierce winds suddenly arose, tearing through countless layers of clouds.

The Frost Eagle Flying Shuttle sat amidst all this like a solitary boat on a vast ocean, rocking and swaying as it was battered by the gale-force winds and towering waves.

Inside the shuttle, Canglong's face was deathly pale, disbelief written in her eyes.

Was this the kind of heavenly phenomenon a Sage cultivator could trigger?

She had only just formed her Nascent Soul, not even having had time to stabilize her realm. Her understanding of this stage was far too shallow.

But even a veteran Sage like Elder Fu was equally stunned at this moment.

"Madness!"

"Divine Origin has gone mad!"

"Even if his physical body is destroyed and his Nascent Soul shattered, he still has the chance to walk the ghost path. By recklessly absorbing heaven and earth origin qi like this, his Nascent Soul will inevitably be assimilated by heaven and earth. When that happens, his divine soul will return to nothingness—not to mention the ghost cultivation path, even the path of reincarnation will be severed."

Canglong said hoarsely, "What is he seeking?"

It was difficult for ordinary people to understand Divine Origin's thinking.

Take Elder Fu, for example. Facing his approaching end, though unwilling, his reluctance was only for the uncertain future of his sect. He himself was relatively at peace with death.

But for Divine Origin, calmly accepting the end of his lifespan and sitting in meditation until death—that was absolutely unacceptable!

In this world, only those with spirit roots could cultivate.

A single spirit root was called a child of heaven and earth; dual spirit roots could be called cultivation prodigies; triple spirit roots were ordinary; and those with four spirit roots were often called "pseudo spirit roots," with little prospect on the Great Dao.

Five spirit roots were even below four spirit roots!

They were called waste spirit roots!

Whether it was breathing and absorbing spiritual qi for cultivation, breaking through bottlenecks, or even the most basic task of finding a suitable cultivation technique, everything was several times—even dozens of times—more difficult than for ordinary people.

And Divine Origin Sage was exactly such a waste spirit root constitution.

Yet relying on this very constitution, he had cultivated step by step all the way to the Sage realm, and had led an obscure little sect to become a top-tier power in the Eastern Wilderness.

To call him astonishingly talented was no exaggeration!

For such a figure, facing the Ascendant realm so close at hand, how could he not have hopes? How could he not have covetous desires? How could he not have an obsession!

For this, he had schemed far too much.

Spiritual lands, pills, techniques, secret arts...

Above, he could grovel before Ascendant holy lands; below, he could secretly scheme against Luo Chen, who was still a minor figure at the time. Even his own sect's disciples and descendants, and secret dealings with demon beast powerhouses—none of these were off limits to him.

But in the end, he had fallen at the very threshold!

Perhaps in the entire Mountains and Seas Realm, the only one who could understand this obsession of his was Luo Chen, who also possessed a five spirit root constitution!

To break through to the Initiate realm, Luo Chen had researched too many methods of foundation establishment—blood path foundation establishment, soul path foundation establishment, alchemy path foundation establishment...

To break through to the Adept realm, he had studied various core formation secret arts over and over, even creating his own *Fine Dust Primordial Art* suited to himself.

To break through to the Sage realm, this man who was always cautious, who in his youth wouldn't even go to an Adept relic that was right under his nose, had actually broken character to dare enter the Demon-Fall Land.

Every major realm breakthrough was far, far too difficult for a five spirit root constitution.

Therefore, people like them treasured their hard-won cultivation all the more.

When there was no further progress to be made, even if only one path remained, they would gamble their lives on it.

*Floating clouds may obscure the sun, but cannot hide it; seeking the Dao, even unto death, the heart is like iron!*

Beneath Divine Origin Sage's desperate and frenzied display, Luo Chen saw this essence.

His divine sense could perceive that the range of the violent heaven and earth origin qi was about ten thousand li in circumference, enveloping the entire Five Elements Divine Sect.

This display might not compare to an Ascendant powerhouse casually drawing the origin qi of four or five great domains.

But for the Sage realm, this was indeed beyond the performance of ordinary Sage cultivators.

Perhaps only when Thunder Daozi fell, using the power of the Nether Origin Sect's grand formation, could it compare.

Caught in the midst of it, Luo Chen knew he couldn't escape.

He had already been completely locked onto by this heaven and earth!

At this moment!

Divine Origin Sage's enormous Primordial Spirit was still expanding, gradually merging with heaven and earth, walking the path toward assimilation.

But within that massive body, blurry faces kept emerging.

Limbwood Sage, Sky-Scraping Old Ghost, Qi Emei...

And many, many people Luo Chen didn't recognize.

These faces were murmuring, raging, roaring.

Though scattered, these wills allowed Divine Origin Sage to barely resist the process of heaven and earth assimilation.

The massive face stared fixedly at Luo Chen, not letting this culprit who had caused him to reach this step prematurely escape.

"Luo Chen, it's your turn!"

A voice containing countless complex tones, as if thousands of people were speaking this sentence together.

The only commonality was the endless hatred!

Luo Chen stopped in his tracks, no longer trying to flee.

He couldn't escape anyway. This ten-thousand-li circumference was now the other's domain, the other's world.

The only thing he could do was face it!

Magical treasures were useless!

Spells probably wouldn't have much effect either.

The Withering-Bloom Divine Fire could burn everything and absorb life force, but the other party didn't even have a physical body anymore.

What about the Nirvana Sacred Flame?

He still hadn't completed the divine soul nirvana of the Nine Tribulations Three Nirvanas, so this Nirvana Sacred Flame wouldn't have much effect on a Primordial Spirit either.

Then the only way to break the situation was...

When the enormous Primordial Spirit surged toward him and swallowed him in one gulp, Luo Chen slowly raised his finger, formed a hand seal, and held it beneath his nose.

*Thump!*

The void trembled. The massive Primordial Spirit formed by Divine Origin Sage stood still in the void, as if using all its strength to absorb Luo Chen's divine soul.

But the process wasn't going smoothly.

Under the protection of the Nether-Purging Soul Art, Luo Chen had enough reaction time.

In contrast, even more heaven and earth origin qi came rolling in abruptly.

Divine Origin Sage's lingering obsession suddenly sensed something was wrong.

Controlling the heaven and earth origin qi within ten thousand li was already his limit. Any more, and he would be stretched to bursting!

He wanted to stop.

But no matter how he restrained his divine thoughts, he couldn't do it.

"Why is this happening?"

"I calculated this step countless times. Using my natal Primordial Spirit as an anchor, supplemented by the divine souls of countless cultivators as pulling cables, so that I wouldn't lose myself in heaven and earth assimilation, gradually merging everything to form a true Primordial Spirit and step into the Ascendant realm."

"But why, at this moment, can't I stop?"

Suddenly, he thought of Luo Chen, whom he had just swallowed!

"Get out of me!"

Amidst the roar, countless streams of divine sense rushed toward the hazy white-robed man.

But Luo Chen stood firm within, like a reef, letting the waves crash against him, unmoved.

He simply held that hand seal tightly!

Divine Origin Sage suddenly felt that the hand seal was extremely familiar.

"That divine ability!"

"During the battle in Taihu Domain, he used this very divine ability!"

"No, that's not right? It seems different?"

"Yes, it is! It is!"

Chaotic thoughts and anxious emotions made Divine Origin Sage feel only restlessness and irritation. A sense of danger kept surfacing within his divine soul.

He couldn't even distinguish what method Luo Chen was using now.

Nor could he find a way to stop him.

Time passed bit by bit. The agitation of heaven and earth origin qi gradually spread throughout the entire Five Elements Domain.

A sea of heaven and earth spiritual qi poured in, causing Divine Origin Sage's Primordial Spirit to grow even larger, like a giant splitting heaven and earth apart, spanning the world.

But that insubstantial Primordial Spirit was like a water balloon, expanding madly. What would happen when it reached the critical point was easy to imagine with a little thought.

Divine Origin Sage was now panicked to the extreme, letting out pleas for mercy.

"Luo Chen, stop!"

"Stop, quickly!"

Inside the Primordial Spirit, Luo Chen finally opened his eyes.

He ignored the enemy's pleas, focusing solely on this move—the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger!

Yes, it was still that self-created divine ability!

Only, unlike the ordinary Withering-Bloom Dao Finger, he was now reversing this move!

The normal Withering-Bloom Dao Finger used Luo Chen's dharma power as a guide, drawing upon the vast life force of nature, relying on the Withering-Bloom True Intent to transform it into death intent. When struck on an enemy, it would cause their magical treasures to shatter, their qi and blood to decay, and even their divine soul to wither. Especially for those with hidden injuries, resistance under this move was even more difficult.

But now, Divine Origin Sage had no physical body to speak of. Even his Nascent Soul had transformed into a Primordial Spirit, connected to one world. Luo Chen simply couldn't make an entire world decay.

Therefore, he reversed the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger!

No longer seeking to transform into death intent, nor forcing the other's Primordial Spirit to weaken.

Instead, he helped it grow stronger, helping it absorb even more heaven and earth origin qi.

Luo Chen saw it very clearly. Divine Origin's current state was absolutely not the normal state of a cultivator transforming into an Ascendant.

He couldn't even maintain his original mind. Just suppressing the miscellaneous thoughts and wills of others within his Primordial Spirit had exhausted most of his mental energy.

How could he have the energy to resist heaven and earth assimilation?

As expected!

When heaven and earth origin qi beyond Divine Origin's control limit surged in, the ever-expanding Primordial Spirit had completely lost control.

Facing all this, Luo Chen's crimson-golden pupils had already seen the outcome.

His lips moved slightly as he slowly spoke:

"Fellow Daoist Divine Origin, may your journey be peaceful!"

The next moment!

*Boom!*

Like a thunderclap exploding, the towering, heaven-supporting Primordial Spirit suddenly collapsed.

Countless origin qi, like wild horses breaking free of their reins, roared and vented throughout the entire Five Elements Domain.

A torrential rain composed of spiritual qi poured down!

The principal, Divine Origin, had long since vanished without a trace, not even a wisp of remnant soul left behind.

The divine souls of those low-level cultivators also dissipated in an instant.

Only the remnant souls of the seven Sage cultivators flickered in and out of existence within the void.

Qi Emei, Yao Hong, and the unfamiliar male cultivator of the same generation were still somewhat frightened, somewhat dazed, watching helplessly as their remnant souls dissipated beneath the curtain of rain.

Sky-Scraping Old Ghost cast a complex glance at Luo Chen, then slowly dissipated with the rain.

An old man whom Luo Chen felt was somewhat familiar revealed an expression of relief, looked at Luo Chen, and gave a slight bow.

"This old man is Limbwood. Greetings, Fellow Daoist."

Luo Chen now understood where the sense of familiarity came from. This was the real Limbwood Sage, also one of the former Five Peaks Seat Masters within the Five Elements Divine Sect.

One does not strike a smiling face. Luo Chen returned the bow.

"I am Luo Chen. Greetings, Fellow Daoist."

Limbwood Sage smiled. "I know. I've known you for a very long time. In the past, I disagreed with Senior Brother's plans for you, so when he brought in cultivators from the Luo Tian Sect, I intentionally sheltered your disciples. But unfortunately, it still came to this."

Was he trying to claim credit?

Wanting Luo Chen to help gather his remnant soul out of consideration for his past care of the Luo Tian Sect?

As Luo Chen frowned, he found that wasn't the case.

Limbwood Sage sighed emotionally, looking at the Five Elements Divine Sect's mountain gate beneath the endless rain, and even further, the entire Five Elements Domain.

Beside him, a female cultivator's remnant soul was conversing with Divine Fire, who was slumped on the ground.

"Junior Sister Qingchi, it's destroyed, all destroyed!"

"Senior Brother, you did your best. We all saw it."

"But it's all gone—Emei, Yao Hong, even your disciple Ding Baifan, the entire sect is gone!"

Saying this, the dignified Sect Master of the Divine Sect actually began to sob.

Limbwood Sage watched all this, and ultimately, his expression was one of loss.

"The Divine Sect rose because of Senior Brother, and fell because of Senior Brother. In the end, it was all empty."

He no longer spoke with Luo Chen, but instead beckoned to the female cultivator and another somewhat dazed male cultivator.

"Junior Sister Qingchi, Junior Brother Heiyan, let's go!"

Amidst the wind and rain, three remnant souls scattered with the wind.

They should have passed away long ago. It was due to Divine Origin Sage's methods that they were forcibly retained within his body.

When Divine Origin attempted to have his Nascent Soul accommodate heaven and earth origin qi, forcibly transforming and ascending, they too were assimilated by heaven and earth.

Souls scattering and consciousness fading was merely a matter of sequence.

Luo Chen stood still in the void, not activating his dharma power barrier, letting the spiritual qi raindrops fall upon his body.

At this moment, his dharma power was completely depleted, his Nascent Soul somewhat withered, but his heart was unprecedentedly open and at ease.

"The main spirit veins of the Five Elements Domain have been destroyed by me and Divine Origin. Fourth-order spirit veins can no longer be born here. But after this rain of spiritual qi falls, in the future, spirit veins will be everywhere. Mountains, rivers, and lands will all be suitable for cultivation."

"Major powers won't care for it, but small sects and even solitary cultivators will surely seek it out."

"This domain may become a holy land for wandering cultivators!"

Murmuring to himself, Luo Chen descended from the void and arrived before the Divine Fire Sect Master.

The other was oblivious, still immersed in grief.

Luo Chen pressed his lips together, pointed a finger, and struck the old man's forehead.

*Pfft!*

A bloody hole appeared. Divine Fire's eyes went lifeless as he slowly fell.

This was the Sage cultivator Luo Chen had killed most easily.

But he felt no joy, only a sense of having helped the other find release.

A wisp of flame burst forth, burning the corpse of this fellow fire-path cultivator to ashes. Luo Chen lingered no longer, leaping up into the nine heavens.

...

Inside the Frost Eagle Flying Shuttle, Elder Fu and Canglong looked at Luo Chen's return with a hint of awe.

Even though the dharma power on his body was nearly exhausted, the strength contained within his calm still inspired genuine reverence from the depths of their hearts.

This was Luo Chen of the Alchemist Sect!

In the silence, Luo Chen slowly spoke.

"The show is over. Let us part ways for now!"

"Canglong, take those young ones and return to the Luo Tian Sect on the Frost Eagle Flying Shuttle. Little Bai will personally receive you, riding the Mystic Turtle."

Canglong looked at Luo Chen with concern. "You're not in good condition right now. Should you recover a bit first?"

Luo Chen waved his hand. "No need. Black King is with me; petty villains are nothing to fear."

Then, he looked at Elder Fu.

The other smiled bitterly. "I will return to the Divine Talisman Sect and quietly await my end. But... after this parting, who knows when we'll meet again. Luo Chen, can we cultivators truly reincarnate and cultivate anew?"

"I don't know." Luo Chen shook his head, but hesitated, "It should... be possible, right?"

He wasn't sure, but Elder Fu seemed to gain some comfort from it.

Divine Origin Sage's act of forcibly charging the Ascendant realm even at death had stirred something in him.

If not for the limitation of his lifespan, he might have rekindled some desire to cultivate.

Now, he could only place his hope in the next life.

At the moment of parting, the atmosphere was tinged with melancholy.

Luo Chen comforted him, "If there truly is a chance for reincarnation and renewed cultivation, Elder Fu, remember to come and seek refuge with my Luo Tian Sect! I believe that before long, my Luo Tian Sect can openly open its mountain gates and stand tall in the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world!"

"I believe that day will come too!" Elder Fu didn't believe in the Luo Tian Sect, but he believed in Luo Chen. However, he then changed his tone, "But when that time comes, this old man won't go to the Luo Tian Sect. I'll return to the Divine Talisman Sect. I'm afraid I'll disappoint you."

Luo Chen was startled, then burst into laughter.

"I won't forget that inconvenient request of yours, old man!"

Because of this laughter, the melancholy atmosphere of parting was somewhat dispelled.

A moment later, Luo Chen left the Frost Eagle Flying Shuttle. A black light shot up from below, carrying him southward.

Elder Fu went east, returning to the Divine Talisman Sect, where he sat in meditation and passed away three days later. The entire sect wept in mourning.

The Frost Eagle Flying Shuttle traveled west all the way, entering the Nether Origin and returning to the blessed land.
