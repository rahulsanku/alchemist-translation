# Chapter 872: Ultimate Techniques Clash, Grudges Washed Away with a Smile?

The world did not know why Luo Chen was pressing so relentlessly.

Nor did they understand why Divine Origin, with the overall situation already decided, would still engage in such a seemingly pointless action.

Only the two men standing in the void above Tai Lake Domain knew in their hearts what this single-strike battle truly meant.

In his pursuit of Ascension, Sage Divine Origin had once coveted Luo Chen's Five Spirit Root constitution, attempting to reverse-refine the legendary Primal Embryo Dao Body of the immortals!

This was not merely a fantasy; Divine Origin had acted upon it.

For centuries, the Five Elements Divine Sect had recruited several mortals with Five Spirit Root constitutions into the sect, pouring vast resources into them. Yet the best result was merely stepping into the Adept realm.

A mere Adept-stage Five Spirit Root was utterly insufficient to meet the basic conditions for reverse-refining the Primal Embryo Dao Body.

Under these circumstances, Luo Chen, who relied not on a great sect's resources but solely on his own efforts to reach the Adept stage, naturally fell under Sage Divine Origin's watchful eye.

Thus, at Luo Chen's Core Formation Ceremony, the Five Elements Divine Sect sent Jin Buque to congratulate him.

And in the subsequent exchanges, they continuously showed goodwill toward Luo Chen, offering various forms of assistance.

Even the Dragon-Slaying Art, which Luo Chen relied upon as a sharp weapon when facing Han Zhan's body-seizing crisis, originated from the Five Elements Divine Sect!

Whether Luo Chen had already discerned such scheming, Sage Divine Origin was uncertain. But from the hostility Luo Chen occasionally displayed toward him, it seemed the other party had already guessed through some channel.

Especially later, when Divine Origin's series of actions targeting the Luo Tian Sect were too obvious.

With Luo Chen's wisdom, how could he not be on guard?

Now, facing Luo Chen's icy-cold gaze, Sage Divine Origin suddenly sighed and transmitted his voice with divine sense:

"Alchemy Sect, you advanced to Sage through fire techniques, and have now joined the Nether Origin Holy Land. I naturally will no longer have any designs on you."

"Let the past be the past!"

"Let this single-strike battle be a case of 'no fight, no acquaintance.' From now on, you will gain the friendship of a Grand Cultivator."

Luo Chen was stunned. He had never expected the other party to voluntarily yield at such a moment.

Was it because his four spirit roots had receded, revealing his Fire Spirit Body, which the other had detected? Or was it the deterrence of the Nether Origin Holy Land?

Or perhaps, as the other said, a combination of both?

But the matter of the other party coveting his fleshly body—even if he had gained much profit from it—could not conceal the immense malice beneath that goodwill!

Luo Chen's eyes narrowed slightly as he slowly extended his right hand, forming a hand seal, then brought his fingers together like a sword.

"No fight, no acquaintance?"

"Fine!"

"But first, we fight!"

His white Daoist robe moved without wind, the hem fluttering endlessly.

As Luo Chen formed the seal, surging mana fluctuations poured out without concealment, their intensity rivaling the full-force strike of Nine Spirits Primordial Lord from earlier!

This sight made Nine Spirits Primordial Lord, who had just defeated Peng Xiang, involuntarily narrow his eyes.

Mana profundity had always been his proudest capital. Even when Peng Xiang praised him as the number one beneath the Grand Cultivators, and he humbly demurred, he merely thought Luo Chen might have mastered some formidable technique. But he had never doubted that his own mana surpassed Luo Chen's.

Yet Luo Chen's display now undoubtedly declared everything.

Mana at Sage Layer Four, comparable to Sage Layer Six?

No!

Nine Spirits Primordial Lord's mana was benchmarked against ordinary Grand Cultivators!

Didn't this mean that Luo Chen, at merely Sage Layer Four, already possessed mana no weaker than a Sage Layer Seven Grand Cultivator?

"How did he cultivate to achieve this?"

No one answered this question. Only Luo Chen knew how he had obtained his mana.

Before advancing to Sage, his Five Spirit Root constitution was like the vastest earth—raising his realm was exceedingly difficult, but the amount of mana he could bear was also several times that of his peers.

And with each major realm advancement, the transformation of mana increased severalfold.

Coupled with Luo Chen's concurrent body refinement, his meridians were tough, his apertures all open, and his dantian qi sea was like a boundless ocean.

Step by step, he had built the profound mana that allowed him to look down upon his peers!

Even later, when his constitution changed, four spirit roots receded, and the fire spirit root alone manifested, the terrifying foundation had already been laid, enabling his many feats of overcoming the strong with the weak and fighting above his level.

At this moment, Luo Chen's mana was enough to rival a Sage Layer Seven, his soul was approaching that level, and coupled with a terrifying physical body, he was almost indistinguishable from an ordinary Grand Cultivator.

But!

"Not enough!"

Luo Chen stared fixedly at Sage Divine Origin.

The other was no ordinary Grand Cultivator; he was also a terrifying existence possessing Five Spirit Roots!

Few knew this intelligence.

But Luo Chen had once heard from Nether Spring of Green Parasol Mountain that there was a Five Spirit Root cultivator in the Eastern Wilderness who had reached the Sage realm. Though he had stopped at Sage Layer Seven without breaking through, he indeed possessed the Five Spirit Root constitution.

Now, having had varying degrees of contact and understanding with most of the Eastern Wilderness's Sage cultivators, Luo Chen could match the description to only one person—Sage Divine Origin!

In other words, Sage Divine Origin's mana absolutely far exceeded that of ordinary Grand Cultivators!

His own mana could only be said to preliminarily match this realm, far from surpassing Divine Origin.

Therefore, for this strike to achieve its effect...

Luo Chen gritted his teeth and, risking great danger, began to draw upon the origin qi of heaven and earth with a divine ability!

The next moment, wind and clouds changed color, heaven and earth trembled!

The chaotic spiritual energy of Tai Lake Domain, under Luo Chen's divine sense coverage, seemed to find a backbone. Vast amounts of chaotic, violent spiritual energy surged toward him.

Accompanying this were various anomalies.

Hard mountain rocks collapsed and crumbled into sand.

Grasses and trees swayed, their green fading to reveal withered yellow.

The already barren water veins—those from which even Nine Spirits Primordial Lord could not draw living water—were forcibly squeezed dry, bubbling forth with springs of clear water.

In the murky yellow sky, only Luo Chen stood amidst a patch of lush green, filled with vibrant life.

Peng Xiang of White Deer Cave watched this scene, his mouth involuntarily hanging open.

His Deer-Chasing Art, reputed to be the closest to a divine ability, could drive chaotic spiritual energy. But compared to Luo Chen's display now, it was like a minor sorcery before a great one.

"Divine ability!"

Nine Spirits Primordial Lord clenched his fists tightly, constantly measuring the gap between himself and Luo Chen.

Under this continuously gathering strike, he could not block it!

But...

"In a life-and-death battle, once I unfold my Great Nine Heavens River, I would never give him the chance to calmly execute this technique."

Lingfengzi exclaimed inwardly, "It's this technique again! Back then, the Seven-Rhino Great Demon Emperor was defeated and retreated under this technique!"

His gaze swept over the others. Deng Taiyue looked confused, as if pondering something. Fenghua Fairy's beautiful face was pale, her delicate body trembling slightly. Elder Fu's expression was dim, filled with envy. Xianhe Sage was extremely shocked, as if he had never imagined Luo Chen possessed such power. And Lin Qingxuan, also hailed as a prodigy of his generation, was biting his lip tightly. Though his face showed unwillingness to admit defeat, it was more filled with a deep sense of frustration.

Lingfengzi withdrew his gaze and cast it toward the old man shrouded in a black robe.

"Can he, hailed as the number one cultivator of the Extreme East, receive this strike?"

Amidst the surging wind and clouds, Sage Divine Origin's black robe rustled loudly.

Facing Luo Chen's continuously gathering strike, he gave a bitter smile.

"Alchemy Sect, you have harbored resentment for a long time! Very well, I hope that after this strike, you and I can let bygones be bygones with a smile!"

The black robe trembled. A withered right hand slowly extended, drawing a circle in the void.

A large portion of the origin qi of heaven and earth that had been rushing toward Luo Chen seemed to have part of its control seized, converging toward the circle.

With the replenishment of vast origin qi, the circle gradually took on a cyan hue.

Then, the withered palm pushed outward, and the circle slowly flew out.

"Go!"

Luo Chen, sensing the anomaly, ceased his gathering. He too pointed out with a single finger.

The originally verdant spiritual light, after being pointed out, suddenly transformed into a grim, deathly white.

After pointing, he visibly breathed a sigh of relief.

Clearly, this forcible manipulation of the origin qi of heaven and earth placed great strain on him at this moment.

In the void, the deathly white finger pointed toward the cyan circle.

Upon first contact, a piercing grinding sound arose.

Crack, crack, crack...

Fortunately, the range of their battle was too far from the observing cultivators. Otherwise, anyone with slightly weaker souls would have fainted upon hearing this sound.

But Luo Chen and Divine Origin both possessed powerful souls and were naturally unaffected by such noise.

Both stared fixedly at the collision of their respective ultimate techniques.

That finger continuously penetrated, continuously breaking through.

That circle continuously shrank, continuously constricting.

Suddenly!

*Chi!*

A light sound, and mana dissipated.

The cyan circle abruptly shattered, and even the deathly white finger collapsed in the void.

Gusts of wind surged, layer upon layer, pushing outward like waves.

Luo Chen's expression changed slightly. This was the backlash of the origin qi of heaven and earth after the divine ability failed.

He waved his sleeves repeatedly, severing the impact of various spiritual energies, while simultaneously closing his Purple Mansion tightly, cutting off the faint connection between his Sage and heaven and earth.

At the same time, he looked up.

In the distance, Sage Divine Origin stood motionless, letting the vast origin qi brush past his face.

Seeing this, Luo Chen's pupils contracted slightly.

He knew very well that Sage Divine Origin was definitely not at the Ascendant realm and could not be unaffected by the origin qi backlash.

But this indifferent demeanor?

Hoo...

In his line of sight, the gusting wind lifted a corner of the hat.

Beneath the black hat was a withered, shriveled, wood-like aged cheek. From a distance, it looked no different from an ordinary mortal on the verge of death.

"This is the person who once sought to harm me?"

As Luo Chen was stunned, the impact of the origin qi of heaven and earth gradually calmed. Sage Divine Origin pulled his hat down, covering his aged features.

A hoarse voice slowly transmitted.

"Truly, the younger generation is formidable. The title of number one cultivator of the Extreme East will belong to Alchemy Sect in a hundred years."

Luo Chen put away his stunned expression and smiled faintly. "I dare not accept. Fellow Daoist struck later but arrived first, blocking this sect master's most proud technique in my life. Your mana is profound, your techniques exquisite—few in the world can match!"

This mutual flattery was not confined to divine sense transmission but spread clearly through the air.

The originally tense atmosphere, where everyone was holding their breath, suddenly loosened like a drawn bowstring.

Sage Divine Origin laughed. "Alchemy Sect, let bygones be bygones from today. Let us part here, how about it?"

Luo Chen fell into silence.

Regarding past matters, although the other had harbored intentions to harm him, in the end, he had gained much profit. Even the Luo Tian Sect had been preserved thanks to the protection of the Five Elements Divine Sect.

Now that they had fought—though not a life-and-death battle—the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger at full power was already his proudest killing technique aside from unleashing the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire. To entangle further would only be overestimating his abilities.

In the future, he would go far away to the Nether Origin. The Luo Tian Sect would remain in Motian Domain. There was no need to create a great enemy beside them.

Perhaps letting bygones be bygones was the best outcome.

Luo Chen was not a narrow-minded person. Turning an enemy into a friend was impossible, but forcibly making an enemy was also not his style.

Thus.

Luo Chen made a Daoist salute. "Fellow Daoist, have a good journey!"

Sage Divine Origin returned the salute. "May we meet again by fate another day!"

With that, Sage Divine Origin drifted back to the Five Elements Divine Sect's five-colored treasure ship.

Once aboard, ignoring the wary eyes of Divine Fire Sage, he waved his hand.

"Let's go!"

...

The Five Elements Divine Sect departed, taking their allied forces to the nearest domain to the Five Elements Domain—the Ethereal Domain.

They would temporarily station there, using it as a base to reclaim their former territory.

But the closer to the Extreme East Six Domains, the more demon beasts would be entrenched. The Five Elements Divine Sect would have their hands full in the coming days.

As for Motian Cliff, with their Supreme Elder's physical body destroyed and only a Sage remaining, whether they could even carve out their own spiritual land in the short term was another matter.

Luo Chen had no time to think about others' affairs now.

The Tianhe Lingxu Ship continued forward, circling above Motian Cliff.

The treasure ship had not yet descended, but beams of light were already shooting out in all directions.

Led by Wang Yuan, all Luo Tian Sect cultivators descended from the air, landing and charging into the thousands of miles surrounding Motian Cliff, slaughtering the remaining low-level demon beasts within.

Other sects that had come to assist also sent their own strong ones to help clear the demon beasts.

As for those forces that planned to settle in Motian Domain or do business there in the future, they were not idle either.

Starting from the junction of Motian and Tai Lake Domains, they spread out like a great net, advancing layer by layer, determined to sweep away all demonic filth.

Boom!

The great ship slowly descended onto a plain.

The Sage domain was released, and even the earth-attribute demon beasts lurking underground could not withstand the terrifying pressure, fleeing in panic in all directions.

Luo Chen ignored them. His subordinates would go and slaughter them one by one.

His spiritual eyes gazed into the distance at the nearby Motian Cliff, a look of emotion on his face.

From now on, the Luo Tian Sect would no longer be rootless drifters.

This territory was carefully chosen by him—relatively the largest area, with relatively better spiritual veins, and with few remaining demon beasts that would cause much loss to the Luo Tian Sect.

Perhaps he had introduced too many small forces, but the Luo Tian Sect's current number of cultivators was also not large.

Let those small forces fight and scramble. As long as the Luo Tian Sect cultivated internal strength peacefully and developed steadily, it would not take long for the name of 'superior sect' to become worthy of its reputation.

Then, following the precedent of the Medicine King Sect, dominating an entire domain would not be impossible.

Fenghua Fairy was the first to congratulate. "Congratulations, Fellow Daoist, on securing Motian!"

Luo Chen waved his hand modestly. "The demon beasts have not yet been pacified. Congratulations are premature."

At these words, everyone burst into laughter.

Earlier, to prepare for the Biyou Mountain peace talks between the human and demon races, the Ancient Demon Emperor Tian had specially ceded the middle nine domains. Even if some demon beasts had not had time to evacuate, they were at most insignificant low-level beasts.

As for Demon Emperor-level strong ones, there were none at all.

Unlike the Extreme East Six Domains like the Five Elements Domain, which not only harbored a large number of low-level demon beasts but also many fourth-order Demon Emperors lingering reluctantly. Reclaiming them would take considerable effort.

As for Motian Domain, with so many cultivators cooperating now, it would be completely pacified in at most a month or two.

Tsk, completing a reclamation war in a month or two—this was a first in the history of Eastern Wilderness cultivation!

Deng Taiyue laughed heartily. "Why still call it Motian Domain? From now on, it should be called Luo Tian Domain!"

Xianhe Sage nodded repeatedly. "Exactly, exactly. Not just the domain's name, but even this four-order mountain range stretching thousands of miles before us should be renamed."

Since ancient times, the names of great domains followed those set by the Sage-level superior sects.

Calling it Luo Tian Domain was entirely appropriate.

Luo Chen was also in a good mood, but when he saw the highest peak, the one piercing into the clouds, he paused.

"Other things can change, but keep the name Motian Cliff."

*Three myriad li of river flow east to the sea, five thousand ren of peaks scrape the sky.*

This name was indeed excellent. No need to change it.

No one refuted Luo Chen's words.

Luo Chen turned around and looked at Nine Spirits Primordial Lord and Elder Fu, who wore hopeful expressions. He understood.

"After the demon beasts here are pacified, I will help you two establish your mountain gates."

Nine Spirits Primordial Lord cupped his hands. "Alchemy Sect is indeed a man of his word!"

Elder Fu said, "No rush, no rush," but his face was full of relief.

At this moment, few low-level cultivators remained on the great ship. Even the disciples of the Heavenly Capital Sect had dispersed to survey the distribution of spiritual veins in this domain.

After all, a great calamity had befallen this place, and the spiritual veins within had changed to varying degrees.

The disciples of the Heavenly Capital Sect were skilled in dragon-seeking and point-acupoint techniques, as well as mountain-moving and vein-constructing methods. After they finished their survey, they would provide a plan for constructing the most suitable spiritual land for the Luo Tian Sect's mountain gate.

These Sage cultivators would also have to contribute. They might even need to relocate some remnant spiritual mountains from the destroyed domains like Tai Lake and Nine Spirits to fill the gaps.

Thus, though the overall situation was decided, there were still many trivial matters.

Of course, these matters did not require Luo Chen's personal attention. He only needed to sit in the center, chatting with the Sage cultivators about interesting events in the Eastern Wilderness and exchanging cultivation insights.

It was under these circumstances that an offhand remark from Elder Fu made Luo Chen freeze.

"Fellow Daoist Divine Origin has indeed aged. Not only has his temperament become more eccentric, but even his appearance has become unfamiliar. He doesn't look like himself anymore, but rather like another fellow Daoist."

Temperament changes were understandable.

But for a cultivator, how could their appearance, as the years passed, give a familiar person a sense of strangeness?

And, not looking like himself, but like another—what was that about?

"Like whom?"
