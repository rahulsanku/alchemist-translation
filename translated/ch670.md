# Chapter 670: One Step Ahead, Shark Emperor Liujun

Han Zhan's words were like a sharp blow to the head.

Luo Chen, whose heart had been full of heat, seemed at this moment to be drenched by cold water from head to toe, trembling uncontrollably.

"It seems you have only now finally realized it."

"How could it be like this?"

"It should have been like this from the start. I had planned to wait until you failed to devour the fourth-tier Burning Heaven Fire [TERM?] before comforting you. I did not expect that you would actually succeed. Seeing that you still wanted to imitate what you did before and indulge the path of devouring, I had no choice but to remind you."

Luo Chen glanced toward Vermilion Bird Mountain [TERM?], then found a place where spiritual qi was poor and sat down cross-legged.

He calmed his mind and inspected himself inwardly.

Within his qi sea, his lifebound Golden Core and second Origin Pill were still spinning, especially the former, which was wrapped by the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Apart from these, a wisp of azure flame leapt with lively vigor. Because the flame stretched outward, it had the illusory shape of a small tree and looked extremely spirited.

Luo Chen truly sensed a faint will inside it!

In other words, his origin true fire had already gained spirit!

This was an enormous joyful event, yet at this moment it was like a sharp sword hanging above him, like steel nails piercing flesh, leaving Luo Chen sitting on pins and needles.

Han Zhan's voice drifted over.

"The qi sea is where the Golden Core resides. In the future, it will evolve into the purple mansion, the dwelling place of the Nascent Soul. Yet your origin true fire gained spirit one step earlier and has begun changing the qi sea, making it more suitable for its own dwelling. Then in the future, where should your Nascent Soul place itself?"

"One mountain cannot hold two masters."

"And this is only the distant worry. The immediate worry has already appeared. As the Withering-Bloom True Fire gains spirit, it controls power even stronger than your main body. If it rebels, how will you resist?"

Seizing the guest's seat and becoming host!

These four words leapt into Luo Chen's mind.

Cold sweat ran down him. He had never expected that beneath a great opportunity lay such a hidden danger.

He said hoarsely, "Then why did I not discover it earlier? I even felt that I still had strength to spare."

"That powerful body of yours gave you an illusion!"

In an instant, Luo Chen's clear mind understood the reason.

People had the three treasures of essence, qi, and spirit. When condensed into one, they became the three flowers gathering at the crown, the body of the Nascent Soul.

Now this Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit had occupied the qi sea. It could be compared to the weakest Nascent Soul.

Luo Chen's "essence," meaning his physical body, was enough to carry the existence of the Withering-Bloom True Fire.

But his "spirit," meaning his divine soul, might not be able to suppress the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit!

If he continued stubbornly, devouring more sourceless flames and strengthening the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit, it would become a situation where the subject was strong and the ruler weak, where the lower overturned the higher.

The best result would certainly be what Han Zhan said: "He would no longer be Luo Chen, but the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit."

There was even a possibility that he would be assimilated by Blazing Refinement Prison and become one of the countless fire spirits inside.

After understanding all this, Luo Chen gave a bitter smile.

"Truly, success because of the body, failure because of the body. It nearly led me into eternal ruin."

Han Zhan spoke to comfort him.

"In truth, you cannot be blamed. Even the master of this place probably did not expect that a Golden Core cultivator could rely on a powerful body, a special cultivation method, and a high-tier true fire to do what only Nascent Soul Sages could do."

"Only Nascent Soul Sages can do this?"

"Yes. A Sage's Nascent Soul is enough to suppress a fire spirit and even use the chance to glimpse fire-type law power. But your divine soul, relatively speaking, is too weak."

Luo Chen fell silent.

His divine soul foundation had always been one of his proudest strengths, once even surpassing the strength of his flesh.

This was related to his early cultivation of corresponding soul-tempering methods, his use of Tongyou Pills, and his passage through several heart-demon confusions.

But after advancing to the Golden Core stage, his divine soul foundation no longer had external help. It could only naturally grow through realm advancement and the passage of time.

Even though his foundation was strong and each change exceeded that of ordinary people, his current Golden Core Stage Six soul foundation strength was only barely equivalent to someone at Golden Core Stage Eight.

This was also why, though he held the illusion killing move Mirror Flower Water Moon, he rarely used it on great cultivators.

It was not that it had no effect, but that it was extremely easy to suffer backlash!

In battle, if the divine soul suffered backlash, the consequences were simply immeasurable.

Luo Chen could not afford it!

It sounded somewhat ridiculous. He clearly had divine soul strength far beyond his own realm, yet he underestimated himself like this.

But from Luo Chen's perspective, it was very reasonable. After his body reached Archaic Tier Four, the enemies he measured himself against were Nascent Soul Sages.

Compared with a Nascent Soul Sage, he indeed had an enormous gap that was hard to make up.

Especially since the Rotten Axe-Handle Black Chess Pieces had already been destroyed.

Seeing Luo Chen silent, Han Zhan continued, "Of course, not just any Nascent Soul Sage can do what you did in Blazing Refinement Prison. A powerful origin true fire cannot be carried by an ordinary body, and one also needs the corresponding method to devour sourceless flames. That Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture of yours..."

"Enough."

Suddenly, Luo Chen rose leisurely.

"The matter of devouring sourceless flames ends here!"

Luo Chen looked around, his tone carrying faint regret.

Such a place of opportunity, yet he could not take another step. It was truly a pity.

Han Zhan smiled.

"If you can understand, that is naturally for the best. It saves this old man from watching with my own eyes as you are devoured by a fire spirit and a hundred years of bitter cultivation is destroyed in one day."

Luo Chen tugged at the corner of his mouth.

"Oh, right!" Han Zhan suddenly spoke again. "Pay attention to your state of mind!"

"What is it now?" Luo Chen frowned slightly.

Han Zhan said heavily, "Have you truly not noticed that ever since stepping into Blazing Refinement Prison and absorbing the first sourceless flame, you have not been as calm and composed as before?"

Luo Chen was stunned.

Thinking carefully, it did seem to be true.

Originally, his only goal in entering Demon-Fall Land was the Five Elements Lotus Platform.

But after absorbing the first sourceless flame, he had instead stopped caring about the Five Elements Lotus Platform close at hand and turned to collecting other sourceless flames on a large scale.

Not only that, he had also become increasingly domineering toward Tai Sui and Moya Cave Master.

Even when Han Zhan reminded him earlier, he had felt full of impatience, and much resistance had shown in his words.

In an instant, Luo Chen's whole body shook.

"The fire spirit changed my state of mind?"

"Not changed. Influenced!" Han Zhan corrected.

Luo Chen took a deep breath and already understood what the other party meant.

The path of cultivation was vast and long. Many wondrous methods, pills, and secret arts could influence and even alter cultivators.

For example, when he had taken True Flame Pills on a large scale to cultivate, medicinal toxins remained and caused his skin color to change.

Or earlier, when refining the Withering-Bloom True Fire had caused his vitality to fade and his hair to turn snow-white.

This time, the continuous strengthening of the Withering-Bloom True Fire had influenced his normally calm and indifferent way of handling matters.

"Cultivators of fire arts are often like this: irritable, fierce, domineering, and unwilling to brook argument. Your way of acting these years has already been moving in this direction. I had no intention of saying much. It was natural evolution."

"But this time is very different!"

"You are in Demon-Fall Land. One careless move and you may perish. If your state of mind changes greatly and you rush for quick gains, not only will you fail to display twelve parts of your strength, but you will also easily drag both you and me into danger."

These words came gently, like spring rain falling on Luo Chen's heart, making his mind grow calmer and calmer.

Luo Chen slowly exhaled turbid air and once again rode clouds and mist into the sky.

Only when he moved again did he softly say, "Thank you, Senior."

Han Zhan smiled faintly and said no more.

...

They had arrived!

Luo Chen's steps stopped at the foot of Vermilion Bird Mountain.

Tai Sui and Moya Cave Master quietly appeared, standing to his sides.

"Why not go directly up the mountain?"

As he spoke, his gaze fell on a mountain peak. There was a figure sitting cross-legged there, his body swaying as if about to fall.

"Dao Lan? Is it because of him?"

Tai Sui shook his head.

"Not only him. There are also two great demon kings and another unknown existence."

Luo Chen raised a brow and said in a low voice, "Wait a moment. Let me see what they are doing."

Tai Sui and Moya Cave Master looked at Luo Chen in surprise.

Even the two of them, with higher realms, could not detect what the other party was doing. Could Luo Chen, only at Golden Core Stage Six, do it?

Divine sense transmission.

"Senior, I must trouble you."

"A small matter."

Han Zhan smiled slightly, and his divine sense swept out.

The divine sense of a Nascent Soul Sage, and one proficient in soul-refining methods at that, passed through the area like rain moistening things silently. Dao Lan and the other two did not notice at all.

Han Zhan's divine sense swept through the air once, then followed the veins of the formation the three were forming. Along the place where mana gathered, it began tracing upstream.

Gradually...

Within a magma lake deep inside Vermilion Bird Mountain, Han Zhan saw a figure sitting quietly on a lotus platform, his aura rising and falling.

At that very moment, that figure's eyelids twitched, as if about to open.

Han Zhan hurriedly withdrew his divine sense.

Vast divine sense surged back like a tide.

Without thinking, Han Zhan shouted in a low voice, "Luo Chen, enter Vermilion Bird Mountain immediately. Someone has taken the first step!"

With just that sentence, Luo Chen's expression changed greatly.

At this moment, he could no longer care about the calm conduct he had spoken of earlier. When urgency was needed, he still had to be urgent!

He abruptly raised his head and said, "Someone has taken the first step and is trying to refine the Five Elements Lotus Platform. Attack!"

Tai Sui and the others froze. Before they could ask how Luo Chen had found the specific situation, they saw him already charging straight into the sky.

Not only that, Luo Chen's transmission immediately followed.

"One man, two demons. Each of us deals with one."

"Tai Sui, you deal with the Sea King Squid. Cave Master, go block Dao Lan. Leave that archaic beast Sea-Containing Golden Toad to me!"

Tai Sui's eyes lit. Sea King Squid?

That suited his wishes perfectly!

Moya Cave Master's expression hesitated. He was not Dao Lan's opponent!

But Luo Chen had taken the most powerful archaic beast, the Sea-Swallowing Golden Toad. How could he shrink back?

"Do not worry. They have maintained the formation for many days. Their mana is nearly exhausted!"

After Luo Chen reminded him, he rushed toward the mountain peak where the Sea-Swallowing Golden Toad was located.

Just as he said, the three on the mountain peaks were in extremely poor condition.

Even while sitting, they were swaying, like candles in the wind.

One could imagine how much strength they could display now.

While in midair, two swishing sounds came from Luo Chen's back as Broken Moon Wings appeared.

Whoosh!

With several flashes, he arrived before the Sea-Swallowing Golden Toad.

The one-zhang golden toad croaked lowly, seeming to threaten Luo Chen.

However, Luo Chen ignored it completely. He directly activated Voidburst Step, crossing past its position in one step.

The Sea-Swallowing Golden Toad separated from the formation and was about to rise into the sky to intercept Luo Chen.

But in the next instant...

Boom!

Boom!

Two enormous figures blocked its path.

One was a black-scaled jiao-python, coiling its body and raising its head, spitting a snake tongue, its vertical pupils cruel.

The other was a green-feathered fighting gull, trembling its wings and holding a huge plantain fan. With several waves, it broke open the fire qi in this place and fixed the battlefield.

Heavenjade said coldly, "Stop here. Your opponents are us!"

"Croak!"

The Sea-Swallowing Golden Toad did not understand what they were saying. It only knew that it could not allow anyone to disturb its master's great matter. It had to stop the man flying into Vermilion Bird Mountain's depths.

But with two demons blocking its road, how could it move so easily?

A great battle thus broke out in the environment least suitable for all three demons!

On the other two mountain peaks...

Dao Lan slowly rose and looked at Moya Cave Master, whose face was solemn.

"Cave Master, long time no see."

"Yes. How did you fall to such a state?"

Dao Lan revealed a bitter smile.

"If one often stands by the river, how can one's shoes not get wet? I merely hunted geese all day, only to have my eyes pecked by a goose."

Moya Cave Master shook his head. His gaze fell on the huge broad saber in Dao Lan's hand.

"A pity for this Golden Suanni Saber. It was the attack treasure this old man was most proud of in the first half of his life, yet now its pearl is covered in dust."

Dao Lan's lifebound magic treasure, the Golden Suanni Saber, had been custom-forged for him by Moya Cave Master using a demonic saber-refining method passed down from the Yuan Demon Sect.

When the saber had first been completed, it had only been low grade, but its foundation was extremely thick!

Because it suited its master perfectly, and after years of killing demons to temper it, it had already advanced into the upper-grade ranks. It could absolutely be counted as a famous demonic saber in the current Northern Sea cultivation world.

Listening to the words "pearl covered in dust," shame appeared on Dao Lan's face.

But soon, he said heavily, "Now we each serve our own master. What Dao Lan does is not from my own will. Cave Master, forgive me."

The broad saber rose high, and baleful qi surged.

"Be careful. This next saber will be very strong."

Moya Cave Master took a deep breath. Many treasure lights flashed at his side.

He could see that Dao Lan's mana was insufficient and his state was extremely poor. He intended to pour everything into one saber.

And that saber would surely shake stone and startle heaven.

If he could not receive it, he would die.

If he received it, everything could still be discussed!

On another peak of Vermilion Bird Mountain...

Tai Sui walked toward the Sea King Squid, which was slowly rising, with a smiling expression. He seemed not to care at all about those huge tentacles.

Part of his gaze, however, followed the direction Luo Chen had gone.

"If I sensed correctly, that hidden existence inside Vermilion Bird Mountain should be a Sage-level expert. Can Devil Lord Azure Yang deal with him?"

Dense worry filled his heart.

But until Luo Chen left, Tai Sui had not spoken to stop him.

Because he knew very clearly that Luo Chen was determined to obtain the Five Elements Lotus Platform. How could he allow another to seize the first step?

...

Whoosh!

The escape light suddenly stopped and descended above the magma lake.

"Inside?"

Luo Chen murmured. From the wooden tablet at his chest came an accurate response.

In the next instant, Luo Chen slowly reached out, and a giant claw that propped up the sky broke through the air and rumbled downward.

"Come out!"

The Cloud-Seizing Divine Claw plunged straight into the lake. Wherever it passed, heat waves surged and firelight parted.

Only when it reached a thousand zhang deep did a blue light curtain slowly appear, incomparably tough as it blocked this giant claw.

Separated by a thousand zhang, two gazes clashed head-on.

"It is you!"

"Shark Emperor Liujun!"

*(End of Chapter)*
