# Chapter 697: His Legacy

Mountains had collapsed, and rivers had changed course.

Across the earth stretched a field of broken walls and ruins. One sky-supporting stone pillar stood abruptly upright, its complex, exquisite patterns seeming to speak of this land's former brilliance.

Three figures swept across the sky.

They were three cultivators wearing the same standardized disciple robes, their realms no higher than Initiate.

But the black horses beneath them had fierce auras, and at a glance they were clearly not easy to provoke.

The three seemed long accustomed to the scene below and did not pause in the slightest.

Before long, the three rode their black horses to a towering mountain peak.

Indistinctly, one could see buildings of varying height scattered throughout the mountain range, as well as the dense heaven-and-earth spirit qi in the surroundings.

Seeing this, the leader among the three snorted coldly.

"A little sect moved here from some remote region actually deserves to possess such a fine spirit-vein land in our Five Elements Domain. I truly do not know what the people above are thinking."

Hearing this, the people beside him echoed one after another.

However, amid their agreement, there were also differing opinions.

"Although it is a declining little sect, it does have its unique points. The Luo Tian Sect's Great Elder, Battle Demon Wang Yuan, has truly outstanding body-refinement arts!"

"Indeed! I heard that back on the Eastern Wilderness demon-chaos battlefield, there were twenty-seven famous great sects on the front lines. When the demon-chaos war erupted, apart from Sage upper sects such as our Five Elements Divine Sect, most were buried in the first wave of the beast tide. Only a few Golden Core great sects escaped. The Luo Tian Sect was one of them, and Battle Demon Wang Yuan was the foremost contributor to preserving the sect back then! They say that battle shattered heaven and earth, cut off rivers, and amid corpse mountains and blood seas, who knows how many profound experts fell there..."

Listening to his junior brothers speak of the past, Ran Feng, a late-stage Initiate, narrowed his eyes slightly.

Battle Demon Wang Yuan was indeed quite a figure.

Even among his sect elders, he was someone who had to be treated with respect.

But the Luo Tian Sect had only this one person.

The rest were not worth fearing!

"Enter the mountain. Once we deliver the order, our mission is complete."

As the words fell, he directly drove his black horse straight into the Luo Tian Sect.

The Luo Tian Sect disciples on watch had discovered the abrupt arrival of the three long ago.

Seeing their actions, they hurried forward to welcome them.

"Visitors, please dismount. Kindly announce yourselves..."

Before the words were finished, Ran Feng directly raised his hand and threw out a token. Without caring for the other party's reaction, he charged into the mountain.

"They are people from the Five Elements Divine Sect!"

"That is a Jiafeng Beast. The one who came is Ran Feng, famous among the Five Elements Divine Sect's Initiate disciples!"

The Luo Tian Sect disciples' expressions changed slightly, and they hurried after them.

Even if they were people from a Sage upper sect, they could not be so discourteous!

Seven or eight disciples chased as quickly as they could, and finally stopped on a mountain path halfway up the slope.

Because someone had blocked Ran Feng's group of three.

It was a powerful middle-aged man with metal wings spread behind his back, holding an enormous broadsword.

On the ground, a long ravine lay between the two sides like a heavenly moat.

Clang!

The broadsword struck the ground, and the powerful man said with a baleful expression, "This is the Luo Tian Sect! It is not a place for you to run wild. Even when Superior Linmu of your noble sect comes personally, he observes proper courtesy. You lot are far too presumptuous."

Ran Feng's contemptuous expression withdrew, and he looked at the other man with a somewhat solemn gaze.

"Who are you?"

The powerful middle-aged man said coldly, "I do not change my name when I walk, nor alter my surname when I sit. I am Zeng Yilong!"

When this name emerged, Ran Feng frowned slightly. In his memory, there was no such person.

But the methods by which the other party had just forced back their three Jiafeng Beasts and forcibly intercepted them with matchless force absolutely should not belong to some nameless nobody.

His junior brother behind him had traveled outside for years and knew the figures of the cultivation world well. He immediately explained for his senior brother, who rarely left the sect, "His Dao title is Baleful Dragonzi. He is Battle Demon Wang Yuan's sole personal disciple, extremely strong, and is also called the strongest beneath Golden Core in the Luo Tian Sect."

When the three words Baleful Dragonzi emerged, Ran Feng raised a brow.

"Oh, so you are the battle maniac Senior Sister Qi often mentions!"

"Fellow Daoist Qi?" Hearing a familiar name, Zeng Yilong's expression eased slightly, and he nodded. "If there is no mistake, then the person she mentions should be me."

"I heard that in the battle of the border city, you cooperated with Senior Sister Qi to slay a third-tier Fire-Eyed Savage Ox King, thereby securing Senior Sister Qi's place among our sect's Nine Dao Seeds. Seeing you now, you do have some bearing..."

In this exchange, the atmosphere between the two sides eased slightly.

Zeng Yilong gave the Luo Tian Sect disciples who had rushed over a look. They immediately understood and circled around both sides toward the sect's important grounds.

Only the two sides remained in place, speaking in that strange atmosphere.

Before long, Zeng Yilong's expression moved slightly, and he opened the road.

"Please. Our sect master is waiting for you!"

Ran Feng nodded. Before leaving, he looked seriously at Zeng Yilong.

He had known of a figure called Baleful Dragonzi, but he had not known the other party came from the Luo Tian Sect.

From the look of it now, this Luo Tian Sect was not entirely useless!

After dismounting from the Jiafeng Beast, Ran Feng walked neither quickly nor slowly, his previous contempt also withdrawn.

His gaze swept across the surrounding environment from time to time.

"This formation... has some merits. Although it cannot compare with our Divine Sect's grand formation, if I had barged in without regard just now, I would have lost a layer of skin even if I did not die."

"Is that rear mountain the place where the Luo Tian Sect's experts enter seclusion?"

"With my divine sense, I can actually feel some suppression from so far away... No wonder the elders told me not to look down on the people of the world. These cultivators who fought their way out of the Eastern Wilderness demon-chaos war truly are not comparable to those senior and junior brothers in the sect who only know how to talk big."

As thoughts churned within him, Ran Feng gained a little more respect for the Luo Tian Sect, this small sect moved over from a remote place, but there was also more pity in his eyes.

Once he delivered the order from above, perhaps before long this Luo Tian Sect would decline.

Before long, he saw a graceful woman's figure standing before a newly built great hall.

Seeing Ran Feng, the woman gave a gentle salute.

"Yao Mingyue, sect master of the Luo Tian Sect, respectfully welcomes the Divine Sect's envoy."

Compared with the forceful Baleful Dragonzi, this Luo Tian Sect sect master's attitude was much humbler.

After the previous encounter, Ran Feng did not take advantage and push further.

He took back from her hand the token he had thrown out earlier to prove his identity.

"I am Ran Feng, disciple of Superior Flamecloud of Divine Fire Peak. My grandmaster is Divine Fire Sage!"

Divine Fire Peak?

Hearing the other party's origin, Yao Mingyue's heart could not help jumping.

Within the Five Elements Divine Sect, Famous Sword Peak and Supreme Wood Peak were the friendliest to them. Back then, this place of settlement had been granted to them under Famous Sword Peak's authority.

The least friendly was clearly Divine Fire Peak.

Ordinarily, it was always people from Famous Sword Peak and Supreme Wood Peak who contacted them.

This time, the person coming to deliver a message had changed to someone from Divine Fire Peak. Could something be about to happen?

...

In a cave dwelling on the rear mountain.

Yao Mingyue stood respectfully before a red-robed young matron who looked even younger than she did.

"Master, this is what the Five Elements Divine Sect means."

The red-robed young matron turned around, her expression heavy.

"They want our Luo Tian Sect to take the lead, join seven Golden Core sects, and reclaim Three Roads River?"

Yao Mingyue bit her lip tightly. "A great number of second-tier demon beasts are entrenched at Three Roads River, and even third-tier Demon Kings number more than ten. Most importantly, several nearby immortal cities have fallen. Once the battle begins, the Demon Kings stationed in those immortal cities will pour out in full force, and we will be caught in a pincer from both sides. It is nearly the same as sending us to our deaths!"

Indeed!

The Eastern Wilderness cultivation world was no longer in its former outward-expanding situation.

From the Million Mountains, countless demon beasts surged out, constantly attacking human territories.

Over the past several decades, more than a dozen great domains had already fallen.

Even when the Holy Land Deep Abyss Sect came forward to organize manpower and once reclaimed several great domains, what greeted them was the next, even fiercer beast tide.

It was said that even several battles between Sage-stage experts had erupted.

The red-robed young matron fell silent.

After a long while, she asked softly, "Were you able to find out whether this is the Five Elements Divine Sect's meaning, or an edict from the Holy Land?"

Yao Mingyue's tone hesitated. "The Holy Land's edicts have always concerned only the broad direction. Specific manpower deployment is handled by Sage upper sects like the Five Elements Divine Sect. This time, although Ran Feng still claimed it was the Holy Land's arrangement, in this disciple's view..."

She did not finish.

But the meaning was already very clear.

The red-robed young matron sighed. "It seems the Divine Sect can no longer tolerate us occupying this excellent third-tier spirit vein."

Shaking her head, she asked about another matter.

"Has any news come from Fenghua Palace, from Lingjun?"

Hearing that name, Yao Mingyue fell silent.

Qu Lingjun, the Luo Tian Sect's chief alchemist, had entered Fenghua Palace and since then sent no news.

Had he not said he would rely on alchemy to establish himself in Fenghua Palace, then seek a territory in the rear of the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world where the Luo Tian Sect could rest and recover?

After a long while, she ultimately could not help saying, "Master, I fear Qu Lingjun has already forgotten..."

"Shut your mouth!"

The red-robed young matron's expression sharpened.

"He is your senior brother! He is the Supreme Elder's disciple! How dare you speak recklessly of him?"

Yao Mingyue lowered her head, but a trace of resentment still remained in her eyes.

Over these several decades, the Luo Tian Sect had swayed in wind and rain. Everyone had worked with one heart, dying and surviving again, only then keeping the mountain gate from falling.

There was no need to mention guest elders and temporarily recruited wandering cultivators; fleeing at critical moments was common for them.

But Qu Lingjun's departure, in the eyes of sect members who did not know the truth, was the greatest blow.

She herself had given up cultivation and spent all her energy handling mundane affairs and interpersonal dealings, only thus preventing the Luo Tian Sect from being rejected by the local forces of the Five Elements Divine Sect.

Who could understand the bitterness and effort within?

Why should she respect Qu Lingjun, who had not sent a single letter for twenty years?

"Sigh... Mingyue, withdraw for now! Regarding the Three Roads River battle, I will discuss it with the elders. When the time comes, the rear will still depend on you to manage the overall situation."

Yao Mingyue murmured assent.

Before leaving, she carefully raised her head and looked at the red-robed young matron with expectation.

"Master, when will you be able to form your core?"

The red-robed young matron was full of confidence. "At most thirty years. You need not worry. Our sect now has five Golden Core elders, and your Martial Uncle Gu has already reached Initiate Perfected Completion. Her core formation is close at hand. Once she and I form cores one after another, the Luo Tian Sect will have seven Golden Core cultivators. With such strength, the Eastern Wilderness will ultimately have a place for us."

Receiving this assurance, Yao Mingyue's mood finally eased somewhat.

Even her departing steps no longer seemed so heavy.

Only, she did not see the red-robed young matron's eyelids droop.

"Core formation..."

A faint sigh sounded in the cave dwelling.

Accompanying it was another mocking voice.

"Sima Huiniang, why did I never know you had such confidence in core formation? You are only barely at Initiate Stage Nine right now!"

A young matron in a white palace dress strode in openly.

Seeing the newcomer, Sima Huiniang's face sank.

"Gu Caiyi, you finally reached Initiate Perfected Completion with great difficulty. Instead of entering seclusion to form your core, why are you running around? The core-formation resources the sect squeezed out for you through gritted teeth are not to be wasted like this."

"Hmph!"

Gu Caiyi snorted coldly. "If I did not come out, would I only learn you had died after you went to the Three Roads River battlefield?"

Sima Huiniang pursed her lips and gently shook her head. "This matter is still uncertain. It depends on how Wang Yuan, Chu Kui, and the others decide."

The Luo Tian Sect's military affairs had always been decided by Wang Yuan and Chu Kui.

The former charged into battle, while the latter commanded from the center of the battlefield.

As for the other three Golden Core elders, they were guest elders recruited gradually over these years.

And the Luo Tian Alliance of the past had already become a name without substance on the day of the demon chaos.

Gu Caiyi said in a low voice, "Three years ago, in the battle of Shifang Immortal City, Wang Yuan suffered no light injury. This time, I fear we..."

"What are you trying to say?" Sima Huiniang looked over with a frown.

"What I mean is that the Five Elements Divine Domain is not a place to stay long. The Five Elements Divine Sect's attitude toward us is also extremely ambiguous. I fear they were not so kind in taking us in, and now they even want us to go die. So, should we consider your younger brother Sima Wenjie's plan?"

"You mean merging into Motian Cliff?" Sima Huiniang spoke that plan aloud, then decisively refused. "Impossible! The Luo Tian Sect is his foundation. Even if I die, I will never agree to merging the sect."

The Motian Cliff of which the two spoke was another rather famous Sage upper sect in the Eastern Wilderness.

Back then, it had also participated in the opening war of the Howling Moon Mountain Range.

In that battle, several Sage upper sects had joined hands.

But the final result was that the Falling Cloud Sect was destroyed, the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect was scattered by demon beasts, and even the nearby Medicine King Sect was forced to move away from its original great domain.

Only the Five Elements Divine Sect, Fenghua Palace, Motian Cliff, and other upper sects located farther away preserved their strength.

Oh!

There was also Tianfan City, the main participant in that opening war. Now, it too existed in name only.

The sect remained, but those in charge were already strong experts from the Deep Abyss Sect acting on its behalf.

After that war, the Sage upper sects were affected, but the greatest impact was actually on Golden Core sects like the Luo Tian Sect.

Once-famous Golden Core great sects of the Jade Cauldron Domain, such as Green Pill Valley and Icecastle, had long since scattered like wind and cloud.

Even if the sect elites survived, they either became wandering cultivators or were absorbed by Sage upper sects.

Among these forces, very few were like the Luo Tian Sect, running millions of li yet still preserving their sect framework.

From this, one could also see how elite the power the Luo Tian Sect retained was.

Therefore, Sage upper sects like Motian Cliff had developed the thought of absorbing the Luo Tian Sect and repeatedly sent people to make contact in secret.

Seeing Sima Huiniang so resolute, Gu Caiyi's eyes reddened.

"Do you think I do not take his foundation to heart?"

"But if all the people are gone, what use is a bare name called Luo Tian Sect?"

"There is no news from Lingjun, and the Five Elements Divine Sect's attitude is ambiguous. If this continues, after one great battle after another grinds us down, how many people of our Luo Tian Sect can survive?"

"Most importantly, is he truly still alive?"

Sima Huiniang abruptly took a step forward and stared fixedly at Gu Caiyi.

"He is alive!"

Gu Caiyi gritted her teeth. "But all the Golden Core cultivators who went to the Great Snowy Mountains died! This is information later disclosed by every sect. How can you be so sure he is still alive?"

Sima Huiniang pointed a finger at her own chest. "He truly is still alive!"

"Alive in your heart?" Gu Caiyi looked at Sima Huiniang with pity. "I am willing to believe he is still alive too, but the fact is that sixty years have passed without any news!"

Sima Huiniang's tone softened slightly, and she shook her head.

"I once told you that at my Initiate ceremony, Chu Kui gave me a pair of Spirit-Rhinoceros Eggs. One male and one female, implanted into me and him and nourished with heart blood. Mine is still alive, which means he is still alive, and may even be living very well."

"Stop mentioning those Spirit-Rhinoceros Eggs!"

Unexpectedly, at the mention of this thing, Gu Caiyi lost control in rare fashion.

Her face flushed red, and her tone was almost a hoarse shout.

Sima Huiniang shook her head and did not argue.

She knew the other woman was jealous.

She had been jealous all these years.

Jealous that Sima had become Luo Chen's person one step ahead of her.

Jealous that Sima, more than anyone, firmly believed Luo Chen was still alive.

Jealous that Sima and Luo Chen had the so-called "heart's spirit-rhinoceros" bond of those egg insects.

As for Gu Caiyi, amid year after year of holding on, she had gradually begun to doubt whether Luo Chen was still alive.

So much so that even that love had gradually become indistinct.

Whenever she thought of her own wavering, she would be tormented by endless guilt.

So-called love was the thing least able to withstand the test of time and distance.

"Enough!"

"You should go back and enter seclusion properly. Form your Golden Core as soon as possible!"

"The Luo Tian Sect is ultimately too weak, unable to bear the storms of the cultivation world. As for me..."

Sima Huiniang grew somewhat dim.

Even though she had handed the sect's mundane affairs to her disciple Yao Mingyue and focused wholeheartedly on cultivation, the gap in aptitude and the lack of resources made her increasingly uneasy about core formation.

Especially!

She did not have much lifespan left.

She had already been older than Luo Chen, Gu Caiyi, and the others, and had only barely completed Foundation Establishment before sixty.

Now, only a few decades remained before the Initiate lifespan limit of two hundred and fifty years.

To go from newly entering Initiate Stage Nine to Initiate Perfected Completion, and then cross the final step to form a Golden Core, the time required in between was simply not something she possessed.

As for the thirty years she had just mentioned to Yao Mingyue, hehe...

She laughed self-mockingly, already somewhat giving up.

Perhaps this battle of Three Roads River would be the last strength she contributed to the Luo Tian Sect.

Gu Caiyi rose and walked toward the cave dwelling's exit.

Her back looked somewhat lost and soulless.

It was at this moment!

Behind her, a suddenly terrifying aura spread out.

Gu Caiyi whipped her head around!

In her sight, Sima Huiniang's clothes were disheveled, her hair hanging loose.

Her two hands clutched her chest tightly, her face incomparably pale, her eyes filled even more with disbelief.

As if the sky had collapsed.

Such loss of composure was something Sima Huiniang had never shown even when she learned that all cultivators at the Great Snowy Mountains had fallen into the Demon Emperor's hands.

Could it be...

Gu Caiyi felt as if she had fallen into an ice cavern, and said hoarsely:

"Huiniang, what happened to him?"

*(End of Chapter)*
