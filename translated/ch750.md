# Chapter 750: A Thousand-Year Arrangement Reaches Its Endgame

Small ones are called clams; great ones are called mirages.

They swallow clouds and exhale mist, raise wind and summon rain.

When gathered, they are high as mountains; when dispersed, they turn into sea currents.

Between formless gathering and dispersal, they contain Sumeru and mustard seed within their bodies, forming a world of their own.

This beast is called the Mirage Dragon!

Rumor said that in the ancient desolate era, many desolate beasts sprawled across Central Continent, and among them, the Great Desolate Mirage Dragon[TERM?] was the most famous.

It had no fixed form or appearance, gathering and dispersing unpredictably.

At times it transformed into beasts and birds to appear in the world; at times it was like cloud and mist, covering Central Continent.

Because of its existence, the Tianyuan Dao Sect suffered bitterly when opening Central Continent and paid countless blood and lives.

And now, it had been dead for several thousand years, yet once again opened its mouth.

Gazing at the deep great mouth opened beside the Passing River, everyone who had originally been immersed in the Alchemy Saint's magnificent ascension was instantly drawn away.

Noisy discussion sounded at once.

"So the entrance to Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven is right beneath Heaven-Earth Peak!"

"That makes sense. How could a top-level great desolate beast like this be erased so easily? According to the Mirage Dragon's traits, one careless moment could allow it to revive from death. Only a blessed treasure mountain rarely seen in the world like Heaven-Earth Peak could suppress it life after life, preventing it from ever turning over!"

"In other words, the Tianyuan Dao Sect disciples will go inside later for that competition held once every five hundred years?"

"It should be so."

"Actually, I am very curious why the qualification for Sages to enter the grotto-heaven must be limited to those who formed their Nascent Soul within a hundred years. If there are truly good things inside, would it not be better for older-generation Sages with longer cultivation and greater strength to enter and obtain them?"

"Who knows? In any case, the Tianyuan Dao Sect says it is to give the younger generation opportunities and give the sect's future more hope."

"What a pity. We Star Gate Sages are all old and declining, with no chance to glimpse the grotto-heaven's true face."

"Who says there is no chance? Did that one not become the Fu family's Dao protector?"

Nearby Star Gate guest elders' discussions continually entered Luo Chen's ears.

Luo Chen's expression shifted slightly.

Especially since he could sense those people's gazes faintly falling again and again on the light sphere in his hand.

Heavenly Secret Pill?

Luo Chen chuckled and quietly put it into his pouch.

He raised his head and looked toward the Aristocratic Clans opposite.

As if their hearts were linked, Fu Chaosheng nodded to him.

But in the instant before that nod, Luo Chen clearly saw a trace of loss flash through the other party's eyes as well.

"Is he also thinking about the Heavenly Secret Pill in my hand?"

Luo Chen's expression did not change, and he then stepped out from the crowd.

He was not the only one moving this way.

One figure after another appeared under ten thousand expectant gazes.

There were thirteen at the front. These were the most outstanding generation of the Tianyuan Dao Sect in the past hundred years!

Behind each of the thirteen stood a Sage with different bearing and style. These were their Dao protectors.

Luo Chen sized them up one by one.

Among the three disciples of the Great Houses, behind Gu Yuan stood a man in gold robes named Gu Chen[TERM?], a loyal servant specially cultivated for him by the Ancient Gu Great House and given the Gu surname.

Eminent Daoyin's younger brother Gu Daosu stood there in silence. Beside him was a beautiful young matron. If Luo Chen remembered correctly, that woman also had the surname Gu and was of Gu Daosu's clan. She had married out in her early years, then returned to the Gu family after her husband's death, unexpectedly becoming Gu Daosu's Dao protector.

Shi Ju...

Luo Chen frowned. He remembered that this person had not prepared to bring a Dao protector.

But now, Qi Xiongcheng, whom he had met once, stood behind Shi Ju with hunched back.

When Luo Chen looked over, the other party calmly met his gaze.

Luo Chen pressed his lips together and continued moving his gaze.

Xiang Qi, genius of the Xiang family, first among the Thirty-Six Aristocratic Clans, had Wrathful Vajra as his Dao protector, from a West Desert Buddhist school.

Yu Xu's Dao protector was a Sage likewise shrouded in a black robe, whose title was Sage Heavenly Mountain. Rumor said he was the final cult master of a declining great teaching in the Southern Frontier.

Lin Bufan's Dao protector was named Lin Jingtang.

...

Sage Dragon Abyss Liu Yuan's Dao protector was Mingyan.

When Mingyan walked out from behind the Tianyuan Sect Master and stood behind Liu Yuan, everyone was present.

Luo Chen withdrew his gaze, matching these people one by one with the descriptions in the intelligence. His heart gradually settled.

As for the other three hundred or so Initiate and Adept disciples, he did not pay much attention to them.

The two sides were simply not on the same track.

Everyone stood together quietly, as if waiting for something.

They did not wait long.

Tianyuan Sect Master Mo Shouzhuo slowly lifted the finger that had been pressing on the go board.

With a turn of his wrist, white star-like specks appeared in the air. Looking carefully, they were actually chess pieces condensed from pure magic power.

With a flick of his finger, all the chess pieces fell before each person.

"These are illusion chess pieces[TERM?] formed by combining my magic power with Mirage Dragon power. One for each person."

"They can allow you all to move normally within Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven."

"Even if you fall inside, you may use this illusion chess piece to die and revive in the outside world."

"But remember, the chance comes only once. Once revived, you lose the qualification to enter the grotto-heaven again. Moreover, though the illusion chess piece can preserve your lives, after revival, you will fall into an extremely long weakened state, and it may even harm your cultivation."

"If you truly encounter something you cannot resist, I suggest actively activating the illusion chess piece and leaving the grotto-heaven. Do not fall into an irreversible state."

The Tianyuan Sect Master's vicissitude-filled voice fell like spring wind and fine rain into every person's ears.

Luo Chen accepted the white chess piece.

To the touch, it felt incomparably soft and lacked solid substance.

But he knew that this was the guarantee Fu Chaosheng had once spoken of, the guarantee that this trip would not carry mortal danger!

Something combining the power of an Ascendant great power and Mirage Dragon power?

It was probably more than that.

On it, Luo Chen vaguely sensed even more complicated things.

For example, the spiritual impulse that had gathered from all directions before, though only a tiny thread.

"The Mirage Dragon has awakened. Go in and seek your own opportunities!"

After the Tianyuan Sect Master closed the go board and handed it to Chess Saint Ke Lianshan, he spoke his final sentence.

Luo Chen and the others no longer hesitated. They turned into escaping light and shot down the mountain, charging without looking back into that deep mouth.

After they left, everyone on the cloud-sea plaza had different thoughts.

Some faces held expectation, while others remained unmoved.

Some walked around, while others continued sitting in place.

The opening of Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven would last anywhere from several months to more than a year. It was difficult to estimate accurately, depending on how long the last cultivator remained inside.

Not everyone was willing to wait that long to see the result.

Especially the Star Gate Sages and outside-continent observers.

The young man from the Nether Origin Sect was the first to propose taking his leave.

The Tianyuan Sect Master did not keep him and let him go.

The mysterious black-robed Southern Frontier cultivator did the same, only glancing curiously a few more times at the deep mouth breathing clouds and mist before leaving.

Several bald monks from the West Desert, however, ignored the strange gazes of others and sought out the Tianyuan Sect Master with bitter faces.

"The West Desert demons and wasteland forces have joined hands and are stirring restlessly. Yet the sacred land Hanging Temple has closed its mountain gate and ignores worldly affairs. We lack an Ascendant great power to hold the line. We ask your noble sect to lend aid!"

Because of these words, a smile appeared on Mo Shouzhuo's calm, aged face.

"No hurry. Wait until my sect's centennial competition ends. Then we will discuss dispatching troops."

The Tianyuan Dao Sect held the reins of the world!

Among the five great sacred lands, it had always possessed the deepest strength.

After more than three thousand years of rest and recovery, fully controlling a continent's essence and resources, its current strength was even more impossible to estimate.

From the fact that, under circumstances where no one in the Mountains and Seas Realm had ascended for several hundred years, a mere outsider like the Alchemy Saint could cultivate to ascension, one could see a clue.

Now, the outside continents were all turbulent and unstable, swaying in wind and rain.

It was precisely the moment for the Tianyuan Dao Sect to act.

Mo Shouzhuo nodded to the Chess Saint behind him. The latter nodded slightly and poured magic power into the go board.

A strange space immediately appeared.

In the next moment, figure after figure appeared within it one after another.

Aristocratic Clans and humble families, patriarchs of Great Houses.

The nineteen elders of Star Gate, and candidate elder Hui Yuanhong, anxious and uneasy, were also among them.

Not only that, one Tianyuan Dao Sect high-level figure after another appeared at the first instant.

Everyone raised their heads to look.

At the highest point of that space, five mysterious and noble figures were faintly visible. Mo Shouzhuo stood in the center.

He spoke in a clear voice, brimming with confidence and vigor, completely lacking his previous aged vicissitudes.

"A thousand-year arrangement has reached its endgame. Are you all prepared?"

...

Muddled and dazed, not knowing where he was going.

Luo Chen woke amid waves of stabbing pain in his brain.

What entered his eyes were birdsong and flower fragrance, clear mountains and beautiful waters. It was nothing like the imagined barren mountains and bad rivers, bone-scraping astral winds, and five turbidities everywhere.

"This is Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven?"

"Dao Brother, you are awake!"

Luo Chen subconsciously looked beside him. Fu Qinglan stood nearby, watching him with concern.

Seeing her state, Luo Chen could not help frowning.

It was not because of her concern, but because of her condition.

Even he had fallen briefly unconscious the moment he entered Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, and his divine soul still ached. Her cultivation was not even as good as his, so why did she look as if nothing had happened?

Perhaps seeing his confusion, Fu Qinglan hurriedly explained, "Every cultivator who enters Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven will be washed by the Mirage Dragon's remnant consciousness. If not for the illusion chess piece resisting it, they would sink into boundless illusions. I am different. In the sect, I experienced cleansing by Mirage Dragon qi in advance, so I was prepared."

Luo Chen stood from the bluestone, displeased.

"Why did you not say so earlier?"

He had indeed encountered many illusions before, leaving him dazed and muddled.

If not for the many similar things he had experienced in past cultivation, he might have slept even longer.

Fu Qinglan smiled slightly. "This kind of thing is not good to say early. Once one is on guard, one subconsciously resists those illusions, and instead sinks inside them longer. On the contrary, with the illusion chess piece made by the Sect Master helping, you wake faster."

Luo Chen subconsciously raised his hand. That soft white chess piece appeared before his eyes.

Indeed!

What she said was not false. Earlier, in those illusions, though he had somewhat lost control of himself, this chess piece had constantly emitted gentle light, protecting him all the way.

That scene was even somewhat similar to his experience in Leaving Forgetting Heaven within Demon-Fall Land.

Only, at that time, the Rotten Axe-Handle black stone he had refined had shattered soundlessly, completely inferior to this white chess piece in effect.

"Hm?"

Luo Chen exclaimed softly. He discovered a black speck on the chess piece.

Seeing this, Fu Qinglan could not help freezing as well.

"Within Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, countless illusion beasts are born. Each is wondrous and extraordinary, with boundless power. If one is tainted too deeply by them, the illusion chess piece protecting us will slowly turn black. When it completely changes from white to black, it represents that the time we can remain in the grotto-heaven has reached its limit. At that time, we must activate this chess piece and teleport outside. Otherwise, we can easily be assimilated into illusion beasts and trapped in the grotto-heaven for life."

"Dao Brother, you have already been corroded!"

Luo Chen silently looked at the white chess piece in his hand. The black speck occupied only about one or two percent.

It did not matter.

Now that he had "free time," the doubts from the outside world instead filled his mind.

Why had Chu Yan, the Alchemy Saint, given one of the five pills to him before leaving?

Was it merely because they were both alchemists?

Probably not. Across the vast Tianyuan Dao Sect, there were definitely many powerful alchemists!

Also, the Heavenly Secret Pill's refining method and pill arts, though only casually performed by the Alchemy Saint, had felt rather familiar to him.

"It was the fifth-tier Law-Derivation Pill!"

Luo Chen finally remembered completely.

When he refined the fifth-tier Law-Derivation Pill in the Pill Realm of Demon-Fall Land, his methods had been extremely similar to the methods displayed by the Alchemy Saint.

Combined with the pill's name, Heavenly Secret... Law-Derivation...

Luo Chen fell into thought.

But this thought did not continue for long, because there were even more doubts.

He had seen very clearly that the Alchemy Saint's ascension had absolutely not been normal!

The Star Gate above the ninth heaven had led somewhere that was definitely not the legendary immortal realm!

Where had she gone?

And why had she given up this realm's enormous spiritual impulse, allowing the Tianyuan Sect Master to intercept it?

Where did those intercepted spiritual impulses go?

Question after question filled Luo Chen's mind like countless layers of mist, leaving him unable to find the way through.

Seeing Luo Chen remain silent, Fu Qinglan thought he was worried about being corroded by Mirage Dragon qi.

"Dao Brother need not worry. Later, when we hunt illusion beasts, all illusion cores[TERM?] obtained will be absorbed by me. You only need to be a little careful, and you will not continue to be corroded."

"In any case, we Tianyuan disciples generally reach the limit before you Dao protectors."

"When the time comes, we will leave together."

Luo Chen glanced at her and nodded.

Hunting illusion beasts was the content of the competition.

Simple and singular.

Below the Sage realm, among low-level disciples, the more illusion beasts one killed and the more illusion cores one obtained, the higher the ranking, and the more sect rewards one would gain after leaving.

But among Sage true disciples, victory and defeat were not decided by the number of illusion cores.

One could even say there was no ranking of victory and defeat.

The true result depended on who could advance one step farther within Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven!

These Sage true disciples such as Fu Qinglan would refine and absorb the illusion cores hunted by their Dao protectors and use Mirage Dragon power to comprehend the true intent of laws.

Once they gained something, they would become so-called Ascendant seeds.

This was the true victory!

And what Dao protectors like Luo Chen had to do was, first, help hunt illusion beasts, and second, protect them while they refined and absorbed, preventing disturbance from illusion beasts and other competitors.

That was all!

"Let us go. I also want to see what an illusion beast is."

Luo Chen spoke calmly.

*(End of Chapter)*
