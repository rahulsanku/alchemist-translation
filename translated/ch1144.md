# Chapter 1144: Martial Combat Decides the Master

"Has everyone arrived?"

On the high platform at the center of the martial arena, a white-robed man lounged lazily in his chair, his gaze fixed solely on the teacup before him, utterly indifferent to the sea of people below and the lively spectacle. It was as if nothing could rouse his interest.

Two figures walked up from left and right, each handing over a name placard, then speaking in unison.

"Reporting to the Special Envoy, these are our side's combatants."

The Carefree Special Envoy raised his eyes, sweeping his gaze over the two men who shared similar features but possessed vastly different temperaments.

Xiao Feng and Xiao Yun, the two brothers.

What a pity. The two who carried the bloodline of that martial-obsessed City Lord Xiao had none of their father's love for martial arts, instead immersing themselves in the struggle for power and profit.

The Carefree Special Envoy reached out a hand. "Present them!"

As the name placards entered his hand, the Carefree Special Envoy had intended to give them only a cursory glance, but his gaze paused at the very end.

"A Blood River realm martial artist? Xiao Feng, are you certain?"

As soon as these words were spoken, the first to react was Xiao Yun, the one competing with Xiao Feng for the position of city lord.

Surprise flickered in his eyes as he looked at his elder brother in astonishment.

Yet Xiao Feng's expression remained unchanged. He merely nodded calmly. "Our side's fifth combatant is Luo Chen."

The Carefree Special Envoy was slightly surprised, but thinking that the number of experts on the Lost Immortal City side was limited, he simply assumed Xiao Feng had no capable generals at hand and had hastily included such a weakling. He dismissed it as nothing serious.

Of course, judging by the combat order on the list, the Carefree Special Envoy believed Xiao Feng was confident enough to win in the first four matches, without even needing the fifth combatant.

After all, among the first four, there were two long-established Furnace Realm experts, and the third match, the most crucial, was anchored by Tie Xiong, whose cultivation had reached the Leakless Realm. Even Xiao Sanniang, who appeared the weakest, had once been the old city lord's personal attendant and was bound to possess some formidable killing moves.

However, after comparing Xiao Feng's lineup with Xiao Yun's deployment on the other side, a meaningful smile spread across the Carefree Special Envoy's face.

One side sought a swift victory, aiming to secure the win within the first four matches.

The other side, however, was employing a horse-racing strategy. The strength distribution in the early matches was extremely obvious and highly targeted. This was a deliberate attempt to drag the focus of this martial contest into the later two matches!

With this thought, the Carefree Special Envoy actually became somewhat interested.

"Go, post the lists and announce the start of the martial combat!"

The Carefree Special Envoy waved his hand, and his attendant respectfully took the two lists and walked toward the martial arena.

Meanwhile, Xiao Feng and Xiao Yun, after exchanging confident glances, took their seats in the high-platform viewing area.

...

The vast martial arena was as noisy as a vegetable market.

When the Carefree City martial artists posted the list for this city-lord-deciding martial combat, a brief silence fell.

Everyone stared at the combat list with intense curiosity.

Even those with poor eyesight learned the general details through others' retellings.

"First match: Granny Yu versus Wood Wolf Divine Lord!"

"Second match: Red Flame versus Silver-Handed Yama!"

"Third match: Tie Xiong versus Helian Shanque!"

"Fourth match: Xiao Sanniang versus Hua Guzi!"

"Fifth match: Luo Chen versus Iron Statue!"

When the crowd learned of this predetermined combat order, the momentary silence vanished, replaced by an uproar far greater than before. The entire martial arena was like a pot of boiling water, with countless discussions surging within.

And the first topic of discussion was none other than the two combatants of the first match.

"Wood Wolf Divine Lord? Could it be the one who once roamed the Great Destruction Borderlands? The one who killed without mercy, came and went like the wind, was wanted by the Nine Cities Alliance, yet still lived well?"

"My heavens, when did this one join Lost Immortal City and become a guest elder under Second Young Master Xiao?"

"This first match... I fear Granny Yu is in danger!"

"Hard to say. Granny Yu has been famous for two hundred years. Even the Great Chief of the Meteor Bandits fell to her. Her combat experience is vast; she may not necessarily be weaker than Wood Wolf Divine Lord!"

On the main viewing platform, Xiao Feng's expression shifted slightly upon seeing the opponent's combat order.

The appearance of Wood Wolf Divine Lord, in particular, made his heart skip a beat.

He coldly looked at Xiao Yun. "Second Brother, I wasn't aware that you would harbor a wanted criminal of the Nine Cities Alliance."

Xiao Yun's expression was smug. "What do you mean, wanted? Don't you know that the Nine Cities Alliance lifted the bounty on Wood Wolf Divine Lord half a year ago?"

Xiao Feng's heart sank, and his mind instantly connected the dots regarding the composition of the Nine Cities Alliance.

The so-called Nine Cities Alliance was an alliance of nine major cities on the outermost edge of the Great Destruction Borderlands.

Unlike Lost Immortal City, which was located in a prosperous region, those borderlands were extremely dangerous, with scarce resources and constant beast attacks on the towns.

And the dominant forces within that alliance were none other than the two major cities of Xilong and Mofeng.

If there was any force that could make the Nine Cities Alliance lift the bounty on Wood Wolf Divine Lord, it had to be one of these two cities.

He then thought of the Meteor Bandits, who had frequently targeted Lost Immortal City's merchant caravans in recent years. Their Great Chief, Tong Huaijin, and Third Chief both hailed from Xilong City and Mofeng City.

So, whether it was the Meteor Bandits or Wood Wolf Divine Lord, they were all clear evidence of the Nine Cities Alliance meddling in the succession of Lost Immortal City's city lord.

All of it was aimed at him, Xiao Feng!

"Big Brother!"

Xiao Yun's voice came again, making Xiao Feng look at him coldly.

"Don't say Second Brother caught you off guard. Didn't you also not send the expected General Chu, but instead put forward an unexpected new guest elder, Luo Chen?"

Though he said this, the sneer hanging at the corner of Xiao Yun's mouth was all too obvious.

Clearly, he didn't think much of this new guest elder.

Xiao Feng snorted coldly, no longer arguing with him. His gaze fell upon the other person Luo Chen was about to face.

Meanwhile, the discussions in the arena grew even more intense.

"Iron Statue is Tie Xiong's junior brother. Rumor has it their strength and cultivation are about the same. Why aren't they matched against each other?"

"Helian Shanque is strong, but his foundation is ultimately inferior to Iron Statue's, isn't it? Can he defeat Tie Xiong?"

"And who is this Luo Chen? A newly joined guest elder of the city lord's mansion?"

"Old Monster Red Flame and Silver-Handed Yama started out around the same time, with similar records. It's hard to say who will win."

"Hua Guzi? Could it be that expert who once admired the old city lord but was never requited?"

...

On the left viewing platform, Luo Chen sat calmly in his chair, his mind clear, taking in all the surrounding circumstances.

The discussions, the changes in expression, and even the movements of the several martial artists beside him were all clearly visible.

General Chu, clad in heavy mysterious armor, looked at the posted combat list, his already ruddy, date-like face turning even darker.

"This is going to be difficult. Second Young Master clearly knows our situation very well. He came prepared!"

Luo Chen looked at him with curiosity. "General Chu, what do you mean by that?"

General Chu said worriedly, "Earlier, when we learned about Second Young Master's combatants, Wood Wolf Divine Lord wasn't among them. Although the others were strong, they were roughly on par with our side. And no matter who went up, Uncle Tie was guaranteed to win this match, even against Iron Statue. But Wood Wolf Divine Lord's addition has broken that balance... Most importantly, Second Young Master deliberately disrupted the combat order, sending the weakest, Helian Shanque, to face Uncle Tie. Instead, he pushed his junior brother Iron Statue, who is supposedly on par with Uncle Tie in strength, to the very end. This is clearly a horse-racing strategy, aiming to avoid our strongest point and take down the weakest..."

Speaking up to this point, he felt Luo Chen's gaze and instantly realized he had misspoken. He quickly changed the subject.

"With such a targeted arrangement, even if I went up, it would be hard to deal with. I'm afraid that the missing Xiao Zirui has leaked all our information to Second Young Master!"

Luo Chen was not angered by General Chu's slip of the tongue. After all, he had never displayed his strength in front of them. In outsiders' eyes, he was indeed just a Blood River realm martial artist, far inferior to a Furnace Realm expert who had undergone a qualitative transformation.

Instead, he curiously looked at Tie Xiong, who was stationed here. "Daoist friend, what do you think of this battle?"

In terms of strength, even if Tie Xiong was a Leakless Realm existence, he was not even one percent of Luo Chen's peak. The title "Daoist friend" was hardly fitting.

But considering his current cultivation level, Tie Xiong was far ahead of Luo Chen in the martial path, so the address was barely acceptable.

Tie Xiong said in a deep voice, "Since the Eldest Young Master has entrusted us with this task, we simply need to go up and fight to the best of our ability."

Just then, a grand drum sound echoed from the martial arena.

"First match begins! Please have both combatants take the stage!"

On the viewing platform, Luo Chen and the others' gazes fell simultaneously on the old woman who had been silent all along.

Granny Yu took a deep breath, and her walking stick struck the ground with a clang.

"This old woman would like to see if the name Wood Wolf Divine Lord lives up to its reputation!"

With that, she walked out of the viewing platform.

At the same time, a green light leaped out from the other viewing platform.

As the green light dissipated, a thin man with sharp, ruthless eyes was revealed under countless gazes.

Gasps of surprise rang out across the arena.

"Is this the legendary Wood Wolf Divine Lord?"

Luo Chen observed him with slight curiosity, a faint red light flickering in his eyes, his expression calm and composed.

On the martial arena, the battle had already begun.

The fights of Furnace Realm experts were no longer limited to mere physical combat between martial artists.

Apart from weapons, their attacks frequently involved bursts of powerful astral qi!

It was a force with a shorter transmission range than spiritual power, but with far greater attack intensity.

Even though Granny Yu and Wood Wolf Divine Lord were only in the initial probing stage, the astral qi colliding between them would occasionally explode with terrifying sounds, like metal striking metal, or thunder and lightning, deafening to the ears.

It was fortunate that their combat was confined to the martial arena, which was only ten zhang in length and width, and reinforced by a special formation. Otherwise, the mere shockwaves and noise would have been enough to destroy a large portion of the audience.

"Daoist Luo, you don't seem worried at all about Granny Yu's victory or defeat?"

Tie Xiong's voice came from beside him, drawing the attention of Red Flame and Xiao Sanniang, who had not yet taken the stage.

Luo Chen's gaze remained fixed on the arena, but he replied casually, "What is there to worry about? If you can win three of the first four matches, or lose three, I won't need to take the stage at all. So, victory or defeat isn't that important to me."

Tie Xiong frowned. "This battle is ultimately about the Eldest Young Master's position as city lord. If the first four matches end in a tie, you will certainly have to fight. And your opponent, Iron Statue, is my former junior brother. He is not weak—in fact, he is extremely strong! If I hadn't had a fortunate breakthrough, even at the same cultivation level, it would be difficult for me to defeat him."

Regarding Tie Xiong's breakthrough to the Leakless Realm, Xiao Feng had already informed everyone during a private gathering two days earlier.

Luo Chen was not surprised. Instead, he asked, "Daoist friend, are you giving me advice?"

Tie Xiong nodded. "If you don't mind, Tie is willing to tell you about some of my junior brother's formidable techniques and the approximate weak points in his cultivation art."

As he said this, he had already begun to divulge information about Iron Statue.

"My junior brother practices a body-tempering method, paired with a suit of Iron Statue battle armor. He is said to have the courage of ten thousand men. His heavy hammer is even more..."

But as he spoke, he stopped.

Because Luo Chen let out a light laugh, showing a hint of dismissiveness.

Tie Xiong recalled the sense of threat he had once felt from Luo Chen and gave an awkward smile. "My apologies, I was presumptuous."

For a top-tier martial artist, revealing an opponent's information in advance like this was a slight to Luo Chen's strength.

Moreover, he hadn't fought Iron Statue in a long time. If the latter had compensated for his weaknesses and vulnerabilities, this information might actually harm Luo Chen.

Luo Chen waved his hand. "Thank you for your kindness, Daoist friend. I only need you to tell me one thing."

"Please speak!"

"Can you, as you are now, defeat Iron Statue?"

"With my golden body complete and my entire being leakless, he cannot sense my aura, but I can use aura-sensing methods to find his flaws. I can defeat him easily within ten moves!"

"Ten moves? That's enough." Luo Chen smiled calmly.

This calmness was so infectious that even Red Flame and Xiao Sanniang, who were listening nearby, subconsciously chose to believe Luo Chen.

But then, they looked at each other in surprise, finding it hard to believe that Luo Chen's strength had reached such a level.

That was Iron Statue!

Just then, Luo Chen's words interrupted their thoughts.

"The battle is about to be decided."

"So fast?"

Startled, they exclaimed and instinctively looked at the martial arena.

At some point, surging astral qi had filled the entire arena. Granny Yu's hunched figure stood in the center, while all around her were blurry afterimages. A green light flickered frequently, making it impossible to find her true body.

Such speed was astonishing!

Unmatched speed—this was the foundation of Wood Wolf Divine Lord's ability to roam freely through the Nine Cities Alliance!

Throughout this process, Wood Wolf Divine Lord would occasionally send out crescent-shaped astral qi attacks. Even though Granny Yu danced her walking stick so fast that not even water could penetrate, her movements were severely restricted, making her little more than a punching bag.

This scene was hard to believe.

That was the long-famous Granny Yu! When had she ever been so disheveled?

Even Red Flame and Xiao Sanniang, watching from below, had grim expressions. Even if they were in her place, facing such speed, they would probably have little chance to counterattack!

If this continued, Granny Yu's defeat was almost inevitable.

To break the deadlock, she would have to take a risky gamble.

No wonder Luo Chen had suddenly said the battle was about to be decided.

Sure enough!

The next moment, Granny Yu let out a sharp screech. Ignoring the crescent-shaped astral qi attacks around her, she slammed her walking stick heavily into the ground.

Boom!

A stream of pure astral qi erupted from her aging body, surging in all directions. Countless crescent-shaped astral qi shattered instantly.

Not only that, but the remaining afterimages also shattered one after another.

In an instant, Wood Wolf Divine Lord's true form was revealed before Granny Yu's eyes.

She released her walking stick and raised both hands.

Swish! Swish! Swish!

The sound of piercing air was incessant, followed by the sound of sharp objects stabbing into flesh.

Visibly, one embroidery needle after another pierced into Wood Wolf Divine Lord's body. Behind each needle trailed a blood-red thread, dripping with crimson.

"Come here!"

Granny Yu's ten fingers danced, and she suddenly pulled with both hands. Wood Wolf Divine Lord, like a puppet, flew toward her.

Seeing this, the crowd showed expressions of delight.

Astral qi transformed into threads, sealing the heart and locking the meridians!

With such a brilliant technique, no matter how powerful a Furnace Realm expert was, their astral qi flow would be obstructed, leaving them powerless to counterattack and forced to surrender.

However, a red light flickered in Luo Chen's eyes, and he let out a soft sigh.

Beside him, General Chu was puzzled, but the next moment, his eyes widened.

Crack!

Bang!

On the martial arena, two figures crossed paths, accompanied by a thunderous roar.

The walking stick had already broken into two pieces, and Granny Yu's figure, like a torn cloth sack, was sent flying several zhang away, crashing helplessly to the ground.

Only Wood Wolf Divine Lord stood on the obsidian platform, his sleeves hanging low, his expression cold.

Granny Yu, who had already secured victory, had fallen to such a state in the blink of an eye. Even standing up was an immense struggle.

Red Flame shot to his feet, his face full of disbelief.

"How is that possible? That move of Granny Yu's has never failed. Even the Meteor Bandits' Great Chief, Tong Huaijin, was defeated by it. Wood Wolf Divine Lord took the full brunt of it, yet he's completely unharmed and even counterattacked with full force?"

Tie Xiong first glanced at Luo Chen, a look of admiration in his eyes, then sighed. "Granny Yu's move has probably been seen through already, which is why it failed. Look, what is that on Wood Wolf Divine Lord's body?"

Everyone looked, and then they were horrified.

Wood Wolf Divine Lord's clothes had been torn, but the exposed skin looked like pieces of wood, hard and indestructible.

Those sharp embroidery needles had never actually pierced this wooden skin, let alone sealed his heart and locked his meridians.

It seemed that Wood Wolf Divine Lord being pulled over by Granny Yu earlier was merely him going along with the flow.

On the main viewing platform, Xiao Feng clenched his fist and slammed it heavily onto the table, laughing in anger.

"Excellent! Excellent! Granny Yu's move was once praised by Father himself, yet it was broken so easily. It seems that the Meteor Bandits' Great Chief attacking the merchant caravan Granny Yu was leading back then was done on your orders, all to test the strengths and weaknesses of this move, preparing for today's battle!"

Xiao Yun smiled smugly, not admitting it, but instead saying, "Big Brother, you're losing your composure."

Xiao Feng shot him a fierce glare, then immediately sent someone to protect Granny Yu. Suppressing the anger in his heart, he sat back down.

After this battle, Granny Yu was severely injured. Even if she recovered, she would likely be of little use.

He asked coldly, "It seems you are fully prepared for this battle. I suppose you also learned about Red Flame's information from Xiao Zirui!"

Xiao Yun spread his hands. "Not just Red Flame."

Xiao Feng said no more. He merely looked coldly at the two people who were now ascending the stage in the martial arena below.

The Carefree Special Envoy, on the other hand, didn't seem to care much about this. Instead, he watched the two brothers with great interest.

It seemed that the scheming between the two of them was far more interesting than the battle below.

Dong!

Another drum sound announced the official start of the second match.

Red Flame versus Silver-Handed Yama!
