# Chapter 1086: The Ninth Soul, Jambu Mountain

Within the vast sea of consciousness, as Luo Chen's resounding voice rang out, it stirred up towering waves and surging billows.

Amidst the rolling golden waves, a black light sphere curled tightly into itself, struggling to maintain its form and not be swallowed by the great tide.

Seeing the other party's stubbornness, Luo Chen was not angered. He only said calmly:

"Back in the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, when my life soul took over everything about you, when the seal on the Nether Water Spring Eye was undone, you should have understood: I am you, and you are me."

"Now that the life soul is complete, you too should return."

"Resistance is useless. Although you used a demonic secret art to create a soul shell, you know very well that with my ability, it would only take a little time to grind that soul shell away."

Faced with Luo Chen's words, the black light sphere trembled slightly, as if shivering.

He understood and could hear that Luo Chen was telling the truth, with no deception whatsoever.

Because the two were originally one; lies and truth could not be hidden from oneself.

But he was unwilling!

"Why should I?"

"Mm?" Luo Chen's voice carried a heavy note of questioning.

The black light sphere trembled violently, emitting a sharp and intense voice.

"Why should I return to you?"

"Just because you want to achieve the Ascendant realm?"

"I, Zhou Fuyou, learned to read as a weak child, entered the Dao at six, and had already established my foundation by twelve. While mediocre people were still pursuing Foundation Establishment, I had already formed my core, and at a speed unimaginable to the world, formed my Nascent Soul at a hundred years old!"

"With my talent, why should I return to you, and not have me take the lead?"

Faced with this interrogation, sentence after sentence of accusation, Luo Chen remained extremely calm.

He could hear the anger and even fear beneath the other's surface unwillingness.

Luo Chen only said faintly: "It is simply because everything you have was created by me."

"You're lying to me. Everything I have was earned through bitter cultivation, obtained by giving up all pleasure and rest, by meditating and cultivating in every cold and lonely night."

Luo Chen did not explain. He moved his divine sense slightly.

Within the sea of consciousness, the golden waves rippled gently, and a scene suddenly took shape.

It was a room in the White Deer Marsh on the former Tiannan Continent.

On the bed, a visibly pregnant young woman slept soundly.

By the bedside, an old man and a young man spoke in low voices.

Finally, the old man withdrew outside, while the black-robed youth stepped forward, inscribing a formation, striking into the spring eye, and planting a soul seed.

Watching this scene, the black light sphere trembled even more violently.

He recognized it. That was the old ancestor, that was his mother, and the words exchanged between them were clearly audible.

Especially a certain part of the content!

"Not just a pair of twins, but a dragon and phoenix pair."

"A boy and a girl, one yin and one yang, perfectly complementary."

"This technique is called the Life-Offering Art!"

"And the child whose innate qi was drained?"

"She will become a cripple or an ordinary person, unable to cultivate for her entire life."

"Forget it. I won't let her nourish my avatar for nothing. I will give her another path. Although that path will be very difficult, it is still a path."

...

Everything, everything had been arranged for him by others.

The world-rare Profound Nether Dao Body originated from that spring eye.

The water lotus seed bestowed by the old ancestor came from the black-robed youth.

Even his strong physique from childhood and his effortless entry into the Dao were all due to devouring the foundation that should have belonged to his little sister!

No wonder his sister, born of the same mother, equally intelligent and clever, had such poor aptitude, not even possessing a spirit root.

So it was he who had harmed her.

No!

Not him, it was the man before him!

Zhou Fuyou cried out bitterly: "Why me? Why did you choose me?"

Luo Chen said indifferently: "If not you, it would have been someone else from the Zhou family. For me to achieve the Ascendant Dao, and for Zhou Yunshen to preserve the clan, something always had to be paid, someone always had to be sacrificed. If others could be sacrificed, why can't you?"

"Then you should have asked me first!" Zhou Fuyou said hatefully.

Luo Chen's clearly visible main soul twitched the corner of its mouth. Those in power never needed to listen to the voices of ants.

But this time, he was willing to give the other party a chance.

"The Zhou family is already under my protection. Are you willing to open your soul shell and ensure their safety for a thousand years?"

"I'm not..." The answer that slipped out surprised even Zhou Fuyou himself.

At this moment, he actually felt a trace of shame.

The promise he had once made to the old ancestor had become so insignificant before his own life.

"Heh."

Luo Chen let out a light laugh, its meaning unclear.

To Zhou Fuyou's ears, it was so ironic.

So, knowing the answer, why bother asking?

"Forget it. This soul shell of yours should be a technique derived from cultivating the Primordial Demon Sect's *Dew Cicada Art*, carrying the meaning of the cicada shedding its shell. It seems you had an early warning; otherwise, an ordinary person would not waste effort cultivating a soul shell. But unfortunately, with my soul cultivation comparable to a great Ascendant power, such tricks are ultimately insufficient."

Verbal persuasion was useless and ineffective, and Luo Chen had already grown somewhat impatient.

But this impatience was built upon a hundred years of forbearance.

To seize the life soul, he could scheme for a hundred years and endure for a hundred years. Why force the other party to open the soul shell willingly? He could just spend a little more time forcibly grinding it away and devouring it.

Luo Chen could do it!

The golden waves rippled, rolling in thickly, enveloping the black light sphere.

The eight divided souls simultaneously shot out a beam of light, striking the black light sphere.

A feeling of imminent doom arose in Zhou Fuyou's heart.

This time, he knew he was truly about to die.

Zhou Fuyou's emotions churned wildly—fear, anger, unwillingness, resentment... until finally, acceptance of reality.

"Stop. I am willing to open the soul shell and merge with you, to save you time, but you must promise me three conditions!"

Whew!

The wind suddenly calmed, the waves abruptly stilled.

Luo Chen stopped his actions.

This divided soul was not as important to him as the previously incomplete life soul; it would not affect his breakthrough to the Ascendant realm.

But this divided soul was different from the other life souls.

It carried the comprehension of the Water Origin and was the vessel of the Profound Nether Dao Body.

In the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, Luo Chen had detonated the Nether Water Spring Eye. If he wanted to condense the Nether Water Spring Eye again in the future and control the Nether Abyss, he had to reclaim this divided soul.

Therefore, he was willing to spend a great deal of time absorbing and merging with Zhou Fuyou.

But if he could save some time, he would also be happy.

There were still many things to do—healing injuries, alchemy, cultivation, breaking through realms—all of which required time.

"Three is too many."

"Two!"

"Are you bargaining with me?"

"Just two. For you, they are mere trifles. The first condition: fulfill the agreement between you and the old ancestor, protect the Zhou family, and you must ensure that the Zhou family produces one more Sage!"

Luo Chen was silent.

After the subconscious selfishness came consideration and then self-sacrifice. Was this Zhou Fuyou's final choice?

"This point, you don't need to say it. I will do it."

Zhou Fuyou breathed a sigh of relief, then said eagerly: "The second condition: let my little sister, Zhou Qingyu, truly embark on the path of cultivation!"

"Didn't I leave her a Spirit Root Pill?"

"Not enough, far from enough. An artificial spirit root can only last forty years. After forty years, although Foundation Establishment can extend cultivation, the efficiency is too low to achieve the next realm. Back then, you used my little sister's foundation to nurture my aptitude. This time, you should repay her! Luo Chen, you must promise me!"

Luo Chen quietly gazed at that black light sphere.

For a long moment.

He nodded gently.

"I grant you this!"

When these two words were uttered, the black light sphere paused, then slowly cracked open a slit.

Within the light sphere, Zhou Fuyou's soul body was exposed before Luo Chen.

The soul body had no tears, but its expression was sorrowful.

"Come..."

Luo Chen beckoned lightly, and the soul body flew slowly toward his main soul, eventually merging with it.

The eight surrounding divided souls also merged into the main soul.

After a long time, Luo Chen's massive main soul, like a giant, suddenly trembled.

Swoosh! Swoosh! Swoosh!

One by one, the divided souls manifested again.

But unlike the previous eight divided souls, this time there was an additional one.

Nine divided souls were the mark of having cultivated the *Soul-Splitting Art* to the minor accomplishment stage.

But because of his previous missing life soul, even though Luo Chen had cultivated the *Soul-Splitting Art* left by Han Zhan to great perfection, he had never been able to condense the ninth soul.

He could only focus his energy on nurturing each divided soul.

Especially under the nourishment of large quantities of pills and soul stones, the eight divided souls all possessed terrifying foundations, each far exceeding that of a great perfection Sage.

Now, with his soul complete, he had finally used Zhou Fuyou's soul body to condense the ninth soul!

That ninth soul sat quietly behind Luo Chen's main soul, its expression serene, silently cultivating the *Soul-Splitting Art* technique. All memories related to Zhou Fuyou had been erased, leaving only Luo Chen's consciousness as the master.

But for some reason, beneath the ninth soul's serene expression, there was a faint trace of sorrow.

"Next, it's time to focus entirely on healing."

...

Time passed leisurely, years flowed by swiftly.

In the blink of an eye, twenty years had passed.

A bald monk, quietly cultivating in a secluded spot within the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land, suddenly heard something.

He opened his eyes and rose, walking out of his cave dwelling.

A white-robed Daoist stood there quietly, gazing at the somewhat empty scenery of the blessed land in the distance.

"Master!"

"Kongchan, come with me to the Northern Sea."

Kongchan nodded, then asked with concern: "Master, have your injuries healed?"

"A great power's single divine technique is not so easily healed, but it no longer affects my movements." Luo Chen drifted up and flew out of the blessed land.

His gaze was distant, occasionally sweeping over the scenery along the way.

The Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land was now much quieter than before.

A large number of low-level disciples had been moved out under the leadership of Sect Master Li Yingzhang.

The current Upper Nether City had been transformed into the temporary mountain gate of the Luo Tian Sect, convenient for disciples to move about and for the Luo Tian Sect to recruit new talents with good aptitude.

Those who remained in the blessed land for secluded cultivation were instead those with profound realms.

Kongchan followed behind, full of doubts, wondering why Luo Chen was going to the Northern Sea at this time.

Leaving the blessed land, the two roamed through the Nether Abyss.

The once-feared Nether Water could not erode them in the slightest under the protective barrier Luo Chen casually cast.

Having grasped the Water Origin, and with the ninth soul's experience of using the Profound Nether Dao Body to control the Nether Water Spring Eye, the Nether Water could no longer hinder Luo Chen.

As they passed an undersea mountain, Kongchan suddenly exclaimed in surprise.

"That mountain has such rich spiritual energy!"

Luo Chen smiled slightly. "You have good eyesight. That is Jambu Mountain, once the cultivation dojo of the Nether Origin Sect's Ascendant ancestor."

Kongchan's eyes widened. "Could it be a fifth-grade spirit vein?"

Luo Chen nodded.

Jambu Mountain was indeed the main vein of a fifth-grade spirit vein. Even after falling from the sky into the abyss, its essence remained unchanged; otherwise, it could not have condensed such a creation treasure as the Nether Water Spring Eye.

This was also one of his backup locations for achieving Ascension.

The reason it was a backup was that Luo Chen had not yet made up his mind.

Before his fall, the Nether Origin Sect's Heavenly Abyss Ancestor had tried to die together with Rosycloud, consuming a great deal of Jambu Mountain's spiritual mechanism.

Even though nearly two hundred years had passed, its recovery was extremely slow.

This would provide too little assistance for breaking through to the Ascendant realm.

At this moment, ghostly qi permeated Jambu Mountain, and a graceful figure materialized.

"Master!"

It was Ghost Emperor Xiaobai.

Luo Chen waved his hand. "No need for formalities. I'm just passing by for a look."

Xiaobai was curious, her gaze sweeping over Kongchan. "Master, are you going on a long journey?"

Luo Chen said, "Mm, I'm going to the Northern Sea. Guard Jambu Mountain well and don't let any little ghosts barge in and disrupt the arrangements on the mountain."

Xiaobai naturally had no objection, and she was happy to cultivate on Jambu Mountain.

However, she was quite curious about the purpose of Luo Chen's trip.

"What is Master going to the Northern Sea for?"

This was also something Kongchan was curious about.

At this juncture, shouldn't healing and cultivation be the most important things for Luo Chen?

Luo Chen smiled without answering. After greeting them, he drifted away leisurely.

...

Twenty years was but a snap of the fingers in the countless years of the cultivation world.

But these twenty years had brought earth-shattering changes to the power structure of the Mountains and Seas Realm!

The strongest human in the Eastern Wilderness, Luo Chen, had led a force to attack the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, using a shocking divine technique to forcibly slay the sect's only great power.

Afterward, the Northern Sea great power Li Canghai had taken the sect's ancestral land, the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range, while two great demon powers had also plundered and seized a large amount of resources.

The Central Continent, once divided into three parts, had seen the Eastern Primordial Continent submerged by a world-destroying tsunami, no longer suitable for low-level cultivators to train. The Northern Departure Continent, being too close to the Northern Sea, had suffered numerous attacks from demon beasts and was therefore fraught with danger.

Under such circumstances, the disciples of the once-first sacred land had lost their pride and were forced to crowd into the narrow Tiannan Continent.

Especially since, when the tree fell, the monkeys scattered. Some families of Star Gate guest elders who had never fully integrated into the Dao Sect took the opportunity to break away.

It was both taking advantage of the situation and being pushed out by the remaining great clans of the Dao Sect.

For a time, many low-level cultivators were displaced, living in uncertainty.

Northern Sea.

On an unnamed small island.

A streak of escape light flew unsteadily and landed on the island.

As soon as she landed, a mouthful of stagnant blood sprayed out.

The young woman wiped the blood from the corner of her mouth, looked around warily, then quietly slipped into the dense forest.

Entering the forest, she twisted and turned, eventually sneaking into a cold and damp underground cave.

Only then did the young woman's face show a hint of relaxation.

She hurriedly took out a jade bottle from her storage bag, poured a few pills into her mouth, and then sat down to recover.

In this manner, by the time night fell, her continuously declining aura finally stabilized.

"Hoo..."

Exhaling a breath of turbid qi, the young woman felt relieved. A flick of her hand produced a demon core brimming with demonic qi.

"A Crybaby Beast inner core. Using the secret technique from the *Dew Cicada Art*, I can form a false core!"

Looking at the demon core, a brilliant smile appeared on the young woman's face.

But after a moment, that smile turned bitter.

A false core was the most despised method of forming a core among cultivators.

Even if one formed a golden core, they would be trapped at the early Adept stage for life.

If she were still cultivating in the Tiannan Dao Palace, how could she have come to this?

But she couldn't go back!

According to the Dao Sect elders who had survived the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, her elder brother had defected at the critical moment.

Not only had he killed many fellow disciples, but he had also ruined the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's grand plan. He was now the one the Dao Sect most wanted to kill!

Even the family she belonged to had been implicated.

The clansmen left in the Dao Palace had all died tragically.

The hundreds of thousands of mortals in the White Deer Marsh had also been slaughtered in one fell swoop, leaving no bones behind.

If she hadn't been out on a mission with her fellow disciples at the time, and had had a bad premonition and made thorough preparations, she would have met a miserable end as well.

She had escaped, but she couldn't go back.

Now, in this state, it didn't seem much different from misery.

"Forget it. My aptitude is inherently poor. I could never form a core normally in my lifetime. If I can condense a false core, at least I'll have a sliver of self-preservation in this Northern Sea, where demon beasts run rampant."

"And..."

A glint of determination appeared in the woman's eyes.

"Big brother must still be alive. I have to find him!"

With this determination, under the protection of her formation, the woman slowly fell asleep.

She needed to heal her injuries and ensure her condition was perfect, so she would have a chance to break through to the late Initiate stage and then attempt to condense a false core.

The resources her brother had left her before leaving the Tiannan Dao Palace were already running low.

This time, she could only succeed, not fail!

...

Bang! Bang! Bang!

Three blossoms of blood exploded in the air.

Kongchan didn't even glance at them as he walked over to Luo Chen.

"The three enemies chasing that little girl have been dealt with. That little girl is too inexperienced. Leaving such obvious traces, she would definitely have been caught up to."

Luo Chen was silent, only staring fixedly at that small island.

His gaze seemed to pierce through the dense forest, penetrate the underground cave, and even cross time, returning to a hundred years ago, to the little girl who had followed behind Zhou Fuyou.

Having been so well taken care of by Zhou Fuyou since childhood, how could she have rich experience in fighting others?

Kongchan was extremely curious.

"Master, could this trip be for that little girl?"

From what he could see, that Zhou Qingyu had extremely poor aptitude. He didn't know how she had managed to cultivate to the mid Initiate stage.

Had Luo Chen traveled thousands of miles to the Northern Sea just to find her?

The night wind blew. Luo Chen took a step forward.

In a trance, he had already entered the cold and damp underground cave.
