# Chapter 616: Preaching on the Sea, Refining the Six Armors

"The Northern Sea has many demons. When I was young, I also went out to sea with elders to kill demons, but this time truly was the greatest spectacle I have ever seen in my life."

"Yes. I could hardly believe that so many demon beasts were arrayed inside the ruined city of Taohai. If not for the complicated environment of the Liuyang Sea Region and the grand formation blocking them, just those demon beasts charging into the human heartland would bring countless disasters."

"I also do not know why those beasts are holding position inside the ruined city of Taohai. They clearly charged out of Arctic Yemo Heaven, yet they do not leave that place. It is truly strange."

"I do not know what they want to do, but Fellow Daoist Li's sword-light division art back then was truly stunning."

On the flying boat deck, more than a dozen Foundation Establishment cultivators gathered in twos and threes.

As they tasted tea and drank wine, taking in the scenery along the way, they discussed the earlier battle at the first defensive line in the Liuyang Sea Region.

The atmosphere was relaxed and leisurely, not at all like they had just experienced a life-and-death slaughter.

But clearly, after experiencing battle through blood and fire, every seemingly relaxed face had gained a trace of maturity.

At this moment, they gathered together. On the surface, they were praising each other. In truth, they were also exchanging battle insights and making up for their flaws and shortcomings.

Amid the crowd, young Wang An was praised a great deal, but his own face carried a bitter smile.

"You only saw the glory of my Qishan Art suppressing three second-tier demon beasts, but you do not know that this one spell almost emptied all my spiritual power. If Senior Brother had not protected me from the side at the time, then with my spiritual power depleted, I would have been left for the demons to slaughter."

Someone nearby was surprised. "Does the Qishan Art consume that much spiritual power?"

Wang An said in distress, "I did not notice when I practiced before. The Qishan Art is one of Langya Mountain's most famous third-tier spells, and its suppressive ability is outstanding. I practiced it for years and treated it as a hidden trump card, but I did not expect it to have such a flaw in battle."

The same spell, released by different people, naturally had different power.

Likewise, in different situations, changes could also occur during spell execution.

When Wang An practiced normally, he would often stop at a touch and would not affect his own cultivation.

But during battle, there were too many demon beasts. To ease his senior brother's pressure, he had driven an excessive amount of spiritual power all at once.

By the time he noticed, it was already too late to stop. He only felt the spiritual power in his body pour out like a tide, almost beyond his control.

In front of everyone, Wang An demonstrated the Qishan Art on the spot.

Above the deck, an illusory mountain condensed from spiritual power immediately appeared.

Although it carried suppressive intent, it certainly could not simultaneously suppress three demon beasts of the same tier.

Wang An said with a distressed face, "This is the Qishan Art in its normal state. If I want to reach the state from the battlefield back then, it causes an irreversible consequence of spiritual power leaking outward. That consequence leaves me with no ability to protect myself. If I cannot solve this problem, I truly will not dare use this hidden trump card again in the future."

Seeing this, although the others did not understand the specific cultivation method of the Qishan Art, they each gave their own guesses and insights, hoping to help Wang An.

After that battle, their relationships had become much more harmonious. After all, they were battle comrades.

Perhaps because of this fate, after a hundred or so years, when each controlled one side, these connections would become external support.

But clearly, other people's insights were like scratching an itch through a boot and could not solve Wang An's current difficulty.

If only a sect elder who had cultivated the Qishan Art were here. They could point him toward clarity.

But unfortunately, Langya Mountain had already joined the Cang Hai Alliance and was deeply trapped in the righteous-demonic war. What Golden Core elder would be here to resolve Wang An's confusion?

Just as Wang An grew disappointed and prepared to stop this topic and join another discussion, a cold, stern voice came from the doorway of the largest hall at the center of the flying boat.

"Your technique is wrong."

Wang An froze and subconsciously turned his head.

The others did the same, all focusing their gazes on the red-robed Daoist.

It was Devil Lord Azure Yang.

Under everyone's gaze, Luo Chen walked over step by step.

Glancing at Wang An, he slowly spoke.

"Although I do not know the specific cultivation steps of this spell, it is clearly rooted in the idea of using four taels to move a thousand catties. With a small amount of your own mana, you exert several times your own strength to carry out powerful suppression. Is that right?"

Wang An was incomparably stunned. "Senior, that is indeed so."

"Then your technique is indeed wrong." Luo Chen smiled with confidence. "Your spiritual power output is too forceful. It lacks finesse and the intention of circling back. This sort of method, using four taels to move a thousand catties, requires softness overcoming hardness. Why not try, when casting, to first let the spiritual power rushing out from your qi sea circulate through your meridians several times, and only then pour it out with your spiritual seals."

Wang An was half-believing and half-doubting. Would that not cause excessive spiritual power to block his meridians, damaging them?

Also, if he truly did that, would the casting time not slow down significantly?

But under everyone's expectant gazes, and under Luo Chen's encouraging eyes, he tentatively formed spiritual seals again and mobilized the spiritual power in his qi sea.

This process was somewhat slow, a full twice as slow as before, but he did indeed follow Luo Chen's guidance.

The instant the spell took form.

Boom!

Suddenly, a tremendous gravity came from above, pressing down so hard that even the rapidly flying boat slowed.

"This!"

Wang An's eyes went wide, his face filled with disbelief.

Beside him, his senior brother from Langya Mountain blurted out subconsciously, "It is like this. Junior Brother, on the battlefield back then, your Qishan Art had power almost the same as it does now."

After praising, he curiously asked Wang An.

"Junior Brother, how much spiritual power did you mobilize this time?"

Wang An swallowed. "Less than one third of my total."

The moment these words came out, everyone was in an uproar.

Previously, a spell that required him to mobilize all his spiritual power could now be cast with only one third.

Perhaps the upper limit of its power had not increased, but clearly, it could leave Wang An with enough spiritual power that he would not become exhausted after using this move, lacking even the spiritual power to protect himself.

And this had only come from a few words of guidance from Devil Lord Azure Yang.

In his wild joy, Wang An immediately asked more questions, the very ones he had thought of earlier.

Regarding this, Luo Chen seemed to have expected it already.

"This sort of third-tier spell is not something Foundation Establishment true cultivators like you should be able to master in the first place. If you want to obtain the corresponding power, you must pay a certain price. Damage to the meridians from overexpansion by excessive spiritual power is one such price."

"As for the slow casting time you mentioned, that is not a problem with the technique I taught. It is merely that you are not familiar enough yet."

Wang An instantly understood.

This art had indeed not been taught to him by a sect elder, but something he had secretly learned after Foundation Establishment.

He only half-understood its taboos.

Now that he had Luo Chen's guidance, he vaguely felt as if things had opened before him.

No wonder, when he had previously used the Qishan Art, the spiritual power inside him had fallen out of control.

So-called using four taels to move a thousand catties was one thing, but if the thousand catties of strength he held completely overwhelmed the spiritual power inside his body, then guest and host would be reversed.

At that time, it was no longer the person casting the spell, but the spell dragging the person forward in return.

While things opened before him, Wang An also felt troubled.

It seemed he could not use this move in solo battle in the future. He needed companions to hold the front so he would have enough time to release it.

Suddenly, Luo Chen smiled faintly.

"In this one's view, if this art is slightly improved and used in a suitable battle environment, it is not unusable."

"Try first spreading your spiritual power outward, attaching it to nearby mountain stones and earth. Finally, coordinate it with the spiritual power in your body and cast the Qishan Art again. The effect may be better."

Wang An was half-believing and half-doubting.

At just this moment, the flying boat slowed and passed over a stretch of islands.

It was as if an experimental environment had been prepared for him.

Wang An still had quite a bit of spiritual power in his body, so he immediately cast the Qishan Art according to Luo Chen's improved method.

Under everyone's gaze, the illusory spiritual-power mountain appeared again.

This time, countless stones and soil on the ground, already attached with Wang An's spiritual power, filled into it continuously from below.

That suppressive force raised its upper limit bit by bit. Later, even a late Foundation Establishment great cultivator among them felt his movement slow and his body no longer obey him.

Luo Chen watched this scene, his eyes showing the look of "as expected."

Although he did not have the specific cultivation method of this Qishan Art, the path it followed was almost of one lineage with the Earth Mountain Art and Mountain Collapse Art he had learned before.

Only, the Earth Mountain Art placed more emphasis on suppression, while the Mountain Collapse Art was a merged earth, water, and fire spell that focused more on fire eruption.

Under such circumstances, the Qishan Art could naturally absorb the advantages Luo Chen had gained when cultivating those two spells years ago.

Borrowing external objects to increase spell power.

Wang An's face was red, looking so excited he could hardly control himself.

He wanted to ask more, but the others had already squeezed him away from his spot.

Everyone could see that Devil Lord Azure Yang was not as domineering and bloodthirsty as the rumors claimed. Moreover, he was in a good mood today and intended to guide them.

With such a good opportunity, why not ask a few questions?

"Senior Daoist Azure Yang, I want to know why, when I fought demon beasts, the flying sword I controlled always deviated. My spiritual sense had clearly locked onto the target area."

"Demonstrate."

Hearing this, the cultivator immediately controlled his flying sword and demonstrated.

After watching, Luo Chen first nodded, then shook his head.

"Your foundation is indeed not bad. Your spiritual power is pure and deep, but there is deviation in your basic Guiding Art, and it still remains at the stage of spiritual power control. For cultivators like us, after Foundation Establishment, when controlling external objects, spiritual sense becomes the first method. I can tell you are a bitter cultivator, but the methods that protect your Dao still need proper study."

"So that is how it is. Many thanks to Senior for resolving my confusion."

"My turn, my turn. Senior Daoist Azure Yang, I previously faced a demon beast, but under its attacks, even when I erupted with extreme speed, I still could not avoid its strikes. If not for the protective measure bestowed by a sect elder, I fear I would already have died. Could there be a problem with my movement secret art?"

"What demon beast was it?"

"Three eyes and four legs, like a beast yet also like a fish. Right, on its feet, there were broad and thick webs."

"Then this was not your problem. That was a Three-Eyed Blue-Eyed Beast, born skilled in controlling seawater. Once adult, it is a third-tier Demon King. Even Golden Core cultivators like us should avoid it as much as possible when encountering it on the sea. Your luck was good. What you met should have been an underage Three-Eyed Blue-Eyed Beast. Otherwise, even with a protective measure bestowed by an elder, you would not have withstood its attack."

...

In the corner, Sang Jiugong watched this scene with a smile.

But as he watched, his expression grew serious.

The questions these Foundation Establishment cultivators asked were not especially difficult, but the fields involved were entirely different.

There were questions about spells, cultivation, battle techniques, demon beast attack methods, movement arts, and even magic instruments and magic treasures.

With such complexity, even Sang Jiugong, who had lived over four hundred years, might not be able to answer them all one by one.

Yet Daoist Azure Yang answered all of them easily.

Even when it was something clearly obscure at first glance and not what Daoist Azure Yang was skilled in, the other man could still ask carefully, then give advice from a high vantage point.

The cultivators he guided all gained something after testing it, and almost none returned disappointed.

"I used to think Daoist Azure Yang merely had profound cultivation and excellent combat methods. Later, after coming into contact with him, I discovered this person was also very experienced in mind, schemes, and speech. I did not expect that in knowledge, he would also be this widely learned."

"If not for the fact that he previously revealed his age was not great, I would almost think he was one of those old monsters who has cultivated for four or five hundred years."

So-called Dao, methods, mind, and speech could all be trained, and they varied from person to person.

But knowledge alone required vast accumulation. Not the slightest shortcut could be taken.

The breadth Luo Chen now displayed proved that he was not the sort of bitter cultivator who built carts behind closed doors and knew only quiet cultivation.

Such a person usually would not encounter any difficulties. Once he did encounter something, it would certainly be a thorny matter.

In order to befriend him, Sang Jiugong had deliberately promised a favor. He did not know whether that was right or wrong.

For a moment, a faint trace of regret appeared in Sang Jiugong's heart.

From ahead came a cough.

"It is only half a day's journey to Dragon Abyss Immortal City. After returning, this one will enter seclusion to cultivate, so there is no need for you to visit."

"However, if you have needs for cultivation resources, you may go to Azure Yang Hall in Dragon Abyss City and take a look. That is a small business of mine. Occasionally, I also place some little things I refine in my leisure there. If you like them, you may buy one or two to play with."

After speaking, Luo Chen ended this preaching on the sea and walked toward Sang Jiugong.

Sang Jiugong squeezed out a smile and addressed the crowd.

"Everyone, after arriving at Dragon Abyss Immortal City, there is no need to hurry away. Soon there will be a Dragon Abyss Auction. You may go and broaden your horizons. Perhaps the Dragon Abyss Auction cannot compare to Divine Origin City's, but among wandering cultivators, fine resources often appear as well. Opening your eyes and increasing your knowledge is always good."

After the two of them sang in harmony, they returned together to the inner hall, and the noise outside no longer entered their ears.

As soon as they entered, Sang Jiugong sighed deeply.

"Fellow Daoist's performance earlier almost made me think I had seen a transmission elder of some Nascent Soul upper sect. With a casual sentence, you enlightened people as if pouring wisdom over their heads. With slight guidance, you resolved others' confusion. Truly... learned and widely knowledgeable."

He held back for a long time, and in the end only came up with the four words "learned and widely knowledgeable."

Regarding this, Luo Chen pursed the corners of his mouth and smiled without speaking.

This sort of thing was something he had truly done often in the past. Whether friends and family at his side, or low-level cultivators of the Luo Tian Sect, he had never been stingy with guidance.

He had even, on the day he successfully formed his core, transmitted a poem containing a hidden Core Formation method to countless wandering cultivators in Tianlan Immortal City.

From this point, Luo Chen had never been a stingy person.

Seeing Luo Chen say nothing, Sang Jiugong gritted his teeth and said, "Daoist Azure Yang, if you have time, you can visit my Earth Mulberry Sect and incidentally give a few pointers to my useless disciples."

He felt the favor he had promised earlier was too small. If he had to expend great effort later to repay it, he would lose too much.

He might as well smash the cracked pot and owe more small favors. With small things accumulated, repaying them later would no longer be a loss.

Luo Chen shook his head. "Forget it. After returning, I truly need to enter seclusion for a while. I have no time to go out."

Sang Jiugong froze. "I thought your earlier words were only an excuse. So they were true."

Luo Chen gave a sound of acknowledgement.

Since he had discovered the wondrous use of the Primordial Unity Cauldron, he could not possibly leave it empty.

Especially the Demon Emperor shed husk in his hand. It had already been set aside for long enough.

It was also time for him to speed up the schedule and put the matter of refining a defensive magic treasure on the agenda as soon as possible.

...

Half a day later, the flying boat landed inside Dragon Abyss Immortal City.

The Foundation Establishment cultivators moved freely, while Luo Chen and Sang Jiugong first went to the Demon Hunter Office to submit their missions.

After saying farewell to each other, Luo Chen did not linger at all. He rushed back to Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake at lightning speed.

After returning, he summoned Heavenjade and Zhang Jiadi.

"Four matters."

"First, take this defensive magic treasure I refined and enter it into the Dragon Abyss Auction under the name Daoist Azure Yang."

"Second, these are talismans personally drawn by Lord Lushan. Put all of them in Azure Yang Hall for sale, and inform the wandering cultivators that talismans of different grades will be sold every year in the future."

"Third, privately issue missions to the wandering cultivators who trade with you, searching for the two medicinal materials Purple Monkey Flower and Five Elements Lotus Platform for me. If there are no materials, news is acceptable as well."

"Finally, take this list of mineral materials and purchase them for me with all your strength. Heavenjade, come to me to receive the spirit stones needed. If your connections are insufficient, go find the Angler at the Demon Hunter Office. He will open convenient doors for me."

Heavenjade and Zhang Jiadi carefully memorized these four matters, not daring to miss the slightest detail.

Their lord had gone out for a year, and the moment he returned, he hurriedly issued missions. Clearly, every one was very important to him. They could not delay at all.

After the two left.

Luo Chen stepped into the Star-Dipper Hall he had not returned to for a long time and took an iron slip from his storage ring.

His gaze fell on the most core formation.

The Yin-Yang Six Armor Formation.

This formation was a fourth-tier composite formation, formed from the third-tier Yin-Yang Array and six sets of third-tier defensive armor arrays.

The Dark Blue Mysterious Armor Array, Bright Yellow Boulder Armor Array, Yimu Light Armor Array, Geng Metal Heavy Armor Array, Blazing Sun Prison Armor Array, and Spirit-Channeling Cloud Armor Array.

Next, he had to master these six armor arrays and use each one as the core to refine one third-tier lower-grade defensive magic treasure.

Finally, he would merge the Six Armors with the Yin-Yang Array and refine the Demon Emperor shed husk.

Walking forward steadily, step by step like this, he would surely refine a Dao-protecting treasure that belonged to him, Luo Chen.

"It is somewhat troublesome, but I hope it will be worth it in the end."

Luo Chen murmured, his gaze as firm as ever.

*(End of Chapter)*
