# Chapter 1131: A Thousand Li in a Day, Gathering Qi and Embracing the Pill

The gifts that Supervisor Yan had sent were, strictly speaking, not the remuneration from that young city lord of Yixian City. They were merely his personal meeting gift.

Not particularly valuable, yet they could solve Luo Chen's most pressing needs.

At first glance, they appeared to be nothing more than grain, flour, oil, and rice. But every single item was something Luo Chen urgently required at this moment.

Having once been a mighty Ascendant cultivator, he had long ago reached the state of fasting, able to sustain himself by absorbing the origin qi of heaven and earth from the outside world without needing to take in food. But this Great Destruction Realm was different—utterly devoid of spiritual energy. Luo Chen could not absorb energy from outside.

Previously, he had managed to maintain his daily activities by breaking down and absorbing the source blood within his body, and the consumption had not been too great. But if he added the source blood lost to healing his injuries, then that source blood became water without a source—every drop spent was one less drop.

It was precisely for this reason that Luo Chen had wanted to leave this village early on.

Now that Supervisor Yan had delivered a large batch of meat and fine rice, although the energy contained within was not much, it could at least barely supplement his needs.

Moreover, that Supervisor Yan was a clever fellow. Among the meeting gifts he had sent, he had even included an oil lamp.

"What kind of lamp is this?" Jing Lan asked curiously, looking at the broken oil lamp before her.

Luo Chen explained calmly, "A Truth-Revealing Green Lamp."

Clearly, Supervisor Yan had indeed investigated Luo Chen's background thoroughly, even looking into his actions that day at Qingshan Town in great detail.

He had probably learned at that Myriad Manifestations Tower that Luo Chen was interested in this lamp, so he had specially bought it and given it to Luo Chen.

This lamp originated from the Shadow-Reflecting Sect in the Southern Frontier of the Mountains and Seas Realm.

Originally, it had been driven by gu insects, but after Luo Chen modified it, it could be powered by either mana or source power. Its function was not complicated—it could illuminate transformed objects, break illusions, and reveal the true forms of illusory things.

Now, without mana to drive it, and with the lamp itself heavily damaged, it was actually of no use to Luo Chen anymore.

He told Jing Lan to dispose of the thing however she pleased, and then focused entirely on his cultivation.

Jing Lan, however, was quite interested in this ancient-looking broken lamp. She added some lamp oil and placed it properly on the table inside the house.

It was now deep into the night.

Jing Lan lay on the table, gazing at the oil lamp inside the room and the tall figure sitting cross-legged behind the lamplight. Her eyes were filled with a sense of security.

If only it could continue like this forever.

But being able to leave this place was also something she looked forward to.

"Four more months!"

……

Supervisor Yan's return date was set for the end of the year, when the tobacco workshop's business concluded. That was still a full four months away.

For ordinary people, this was an agonizing wait.

But for Luo Chen, who was focused on cultivation, this time was still not enough.

In orthodox body refinement, the second realm's training primarily focused on comprehending the changes in qi and blood.

In the past, due to various reasons—talent, time, future prospects, and so on—Luo Chen had not delved deeply into that path. Now that he was taking it up again, he suddenly realized that this path was actually not that difficult for him.

Perhaps it was because he had read widely, or because he had trained in many ancient martial techniques, or because he had once possessed an Archaic fifth-order physical body. In any case, he was already at a level of high insight in this path. Coupled with the fact that body refinement paths often shared common principles, once he started, his progress was swift.

Among the changes in qi and blood, the concept of Ming Jin was achieved in an instant.

The subsequent An Jin took him only two or three days of practice.

Hua Jin was the most difficult of the four forces to comprehend. It emphasized smooth operation, internal circulation of potential, and the union of qi and force, spreading all energy throughout every part of the body. This way, whether attacking or defending, one could act freely, and force could manifest at will.

This stage took Luo Chen a bit longer. He needed a full half month to spread force throughout his entire body.

Of course, if any orthodox body refiners saw that Luo Chen had comprehended three of the forces in the changes of qi and blood in less than a month, breaking through three minor realms in succession, they would probably have their eyes pop out of their sockets.

But for Luo Chen, this was truly nothing to be proud of.

His foundation in body refinement was simply too solid!

If one were to carefully divide the Blood River Realm, there were actually nine complete minor realms. Back then, Wang Yuan had explained them to Luo Chen: [Tempering the Organs], [Changing the Sinews], [Washing the Marrow], [Opening the Meridians], [Aperture Opening], [Ming Jin], [An Jin], [Hua Jin], and [Dan Jin].

Among these nine minor realms, Luo Chen had already completed the first five steps—Changing the Sinews, Washing the Marrow, Tempering the Organs, Opening the Meridians, and opening all the major and minor apertures of the body—several hundred years ago.

Now, he was merely perfecting the unfinished work from a position of high insight.

After comprehending Hua Jin, Luo Chen had to consider the last of the four forces—Ming, An, Hua, and Dan—Dan Jin!

In a quiet spot behind the mountain.

Luo Chen sat cross-legged under a large tree, a contemplative look in his eyes.

Hua Jin was the most difficult stage in the changes of qi and blood, difficult because of its complexity—after all, one had to spread force throughout every corner of the body.

But Dan Jin was the most dangerous stage in the changes of qi and blood. Back then, Wang Yuan had been stuck at this minor realm for a long time.

The reason it was called dangerous was that one had to fuse all the force scattered throughout the body into a single point!

This point, in martial arts cultivation, was called the "Dan"!

It was similar to the golden core in qi cultivation, yet different.

It emphasized not only the fusion of force but also the internal convergence of will and spirit.

The process of fusing force was extremely painful. One needed a strong martial will to endure the pain while controlling the fusion of forces.

This fused Dan was like the cosmic egg at the dawn of chaos, evolving heaven and earth. The more concentrated, the smaller, the more internalized it was, the more powerful the force that would erupt in battle. It would even affect the cultivation of the next realm.

In terms of willpower, Luo Chen had no concerns at all.

With his Dao heart as strong as it was and his will as firm as it was, a little pain was nothing to worry about.

What he was thinking about now was how to avoid damaging his body during the process of embracing the Dan.

This current body, though seemingly fine, was actually like a crumbling building. Before it was fully restored, it could not afford the slightest mistake.

"Perhaps I can try it bit by bit, familiarize myself with the steps of embracing the Dan, and only take that step when I am absolutely certain," Luo Chen murmured to himself.

Compared to qi cultivation, martial arts cultivation placed greater emphasis on day-after-day practice.

And when it came to proficiency, his attribute panel could still be opened.

In other words, he could completely rely on the visualized progress bar to decide when to embrace the Dan and when to take that step!

Moreover, this process did not rely solely on bitter cultivation. He could also gain insight by practicing martial techniques derived from force, thereby increasing his proficiency.

"Let me think... what martial techniques did Wang Yuan record in his *Divine Astral Fighting Qi*? The Blood God Tribulation Finger, which focuses on Ming Jin; the Immortal Returns to Penglai, which uses An Jin to kill unexpectedly; and the most vicious of all, the Blood-Corroding Demon Finger..."

……

The Great Destruction Realm still had seasonal changes.

However, these changes were relatively vague compared to the Mountains and Seas Realm.

Because of the three suns hanging high in the sky, the temperature difference between day and night was extreme, and the seasonal changes were polarized.

Spring and autumn were extremely short, while winter and summer were harsh.

The entire summer lasted from April until around September or October before it finally ended.

But the end of the scorching summer did not mean the arrival of a cool autumn. On the contrary, the days ahead were even harder for ordinary people.

Because this period was the peak season for two of the three great natural disasters: Heavenly Wind and Ghost Rain.

Ordinary people simply dared not stay outside for too long. Some daily part-time jobs could only be done at home.

For example, the job of tanning Thousand-Fold Rope that Jing Lan had previously wanted to take on could be done at home without going to the tobacco workshop.

Of course, since Jing Lan was going to leave Qingshan Town with Luo Chen, she naturally didn't need to do this anymore.

With time on her hands, she simply stayed home sewing clothes, preparing for the journey ahead.

"At the end of the year, heavy snow falls, and it's especially cold. If you don't go out, it's fine. But if you're going on a long journey, you'll need a thick coat to keep warm."

Jing Lan looked at the simple linen clothes Luo Chen was wearing and spoke as she sewed.

Luo Chen was about to say it wasn't necessary, but seeing the earnest look in the woman's eyes, he didn't refuse.

"Oh, by the way, there's likely to be ghost rain tonight. Among the gifts Supervisor Yan sent, there's a complete Dreamstone. Take one out and light it!" Jing Lan reminded him.

Luo Chen hummed in acknowledgment and went to fetch a Dreamstone.

But before he could light it, his scalp tingled, and he subconsciously looked toward the door.

In the dim sky, a flash of white light flickered faintly, followed by a thunderous roar that shook the ears.

*Boom!*

Jing Lan, on the other hand, didn't feel much. She just smiled and looked outside.

"See, I told you it was going to rain."

Outside, the sound of large raindrops hitting the ground began, and waves of cold spread out.

Luo Chen walked to the doorway and looked at the white light that seemed to tear the sky apart on the horizon. His expression was grave.

Was this the Soul-Shaking Thunderclap that Supervisor Yan had mentioned?

In the fire pit, firewood was piled up, and the burning Dreamstone emitted wisps of green smoke.

After dinner, Jing Lan went to sleep on her own, while Luo Chen continued his cultivation.

He had become quite proficient in gathering his will, embracing the Dan, and comprehending Dan Jin. Perhaps it wouldn't be long before he succeeded.

This way, he could use his qi and blood energy more precisely in the future, rather than the crude and simple use of source power.

Outside, the rain was torrential. Inside, Luo Chen calmed his mind and sat cross-legged in meditation.

Streams of force energy flowed continuously through the meridians he had opened in his body. Force energy also pulsed in and out of his apertures. Amidst a sea of chaos, it seemed as if a cosmic egg was being born.

Suddenly!

Luo Chen's brow furrowed slightly. He subconsciously opened his eyes and looked toward the wooden door.

In the unseen realm, it felt as if some ghostly thing was secretly spying on him.
