# Chapter 816: Divine Fire's Might, Combat Positioning

On a tall building.

Two figures stood leaning against the railing, gazing into the distance at the massive green eagle carcass being paraded through the streets. Both wore grim expressions.

These were none other than Jin Ling and Divine Fire.

Suddenly, Jin Ling slammed his palm hard against the railing.

"I thought before that his magical power was extraordinary and his foundation deep. Now it seems I still underestimated him. Such methods of suppressing and slaying a peer—this is no ordinary feat!"

He judged himself to be among the outstanding figures among Sages, yet even he would find it difficult to defeat a peer in a short time.

Let alone annihilating both body and soul, not even allowing the opponent's Nascent Soul to escape.

Taking a deep breath, Jin Ling said in a low voice, "Junior Brother Divine Fire, can you tell what methods he used?"

"It should be that strange flame that instantly refined Old Ghost Motian's true artifact on the dueling platform back then."

Divine Fire Sage answered thus, but there was also some doubt in his eyes.

Jin Ling Sage frowned and directly voiced that doubt: "What strange fire can instantly refine Motian Cliff, and even make it difficult for a powerful demon emperor like Frost Eagle, who possesses both ice and wind attributes, to resist?"

Divine Fire Sage couldn't help but smile bitterly.

He himself was considered a master of fire control.

Not only did he primarily cultivate a fire-attribute technique, but he had also refined a fourth-rank strange fire within his body, which was why his peers flattered him with the Dao name "Divine Fire."

Yet compared to Luo Chen's achievements, the honorific title "Divine Fire Sage" clearly suited the other party more.

"Rumor has it that the Jade Cauldron Domain once had many strange fires. Though their ranks were not high, their varieties were numerous. Luo Chen comes from the Jade Cauldron Domain; perhaps he refined some kind of flame and carefully nurtured it to an extremely formidable level."

"In my view, that flame of his is white in color and cold in nature, with a strong corrosive effect on magical treasures and true artifacts. It may have been specially refined using some heretical method."

"To counter such a flame, one could use water and fire to overcome each other, or suppress it with grand and righteous hao qi. The best method would be the power of thunder and lightning, which restrains all evil."

Hearing this answer, Jin Ling subconsciously shook his head.

Water and fire overcoming each other—it was mutual. Unless one had an exceptionally powerful water-attribute treasure, it couldn't suppress Luo Chen's fire.

And grand and righteous power?

Luo Chen's magical power showed no trace of evil; it was more righteous than anyone's. Could hao qi truly target him?

Thunder and lightning power...

Such power was not something ordinary people could master.

Even some cultivators with thunder-attribute spirit roots dared not attempt to absorb thunder and lightning power into their bodies.

"In the end, we let him grow too strong!"

Jin Ling sighed and turned to leave.

Divine Fire Sage caught up, troubled. "Senior Brother Shenyuan told us to keep an eye on him, and if there's an opportunity, capture him secretly. Now that he's so formidable, where's the chance? Senior Brother Jin Ling, what should we do?"

"What should we do?" Jin Ling paused slightly, a flash of resentment crossing his face. "Let Senior Brother Shenyuan worry about that himself!"

When he said this, there was quite a bit of resentment in his tone.

Divine Fire Sage watched his retreating figure, somewhat puzzled about where this resentment came from.

Senior Brother Shenyuan had been extremely good to them. Though he appeared to be a senior brother, he had actually played more of a master's role over the years.

That was why everyone in the Five Elements Divine Sect respected him so much.

But recently, Senior Brother Jin Ling's attitude toward Senior Brother Shenyuan had grown increasingly cold, even to the point of little interaction.

What had gone wrong?

...

"The crisis at the West City has been resolved."

In a peculiar space, Tian Duzi slowly opened his eyes.

Above his head were the stars of the Zhou Tian, dazzling and brilliant.

Beneath his feet, water gurgled. Black Netherwater flowed calmly, like an inverted mirror.

He sat cross-legged within it, looking at the man who had stepped into this space.

Ling Tian City Lord raised an eyebrow. "That fast?"

Tian Duzi nodded slightly. "It was somewhat unexpected, but over there, a demon emperor fell swiftly, causing the demon beast army's morale to collapse. They eventually retreated in panic."

"Who killed it?"

"It should be Luo Chen of the Pill Sect." Tian Duzi said slowly.

It was a speculative tone, but the meaning in his words was certain.

"Him, huh..." Ling Tian City Lord stroked his chin.

Tian Duzi asked curiously, "Why don't you seem surprised when you hear it was the Pill Sect who killed it? Do you have that much faith in this man's abilities?"

Ling Tian City Lord laughed freely. "It's not that I trust him lightly, but this man has a glorious record of achievements. He cannot be underestimated in the slightest. For him to achieve such a bountiful result now is only natural."

"Oh?" Tian Duzi became interested. "I'm unaware of what glorious achievements a Pill Dao grandmaster skilled in alchemy could have in combat."

But clearly, Ling Tian City Lord didn't want to dwell on this matter.

He put away his smile, his expression turning gloomy.

"I was thinking of dragging this battle out a bit longer, making the cultivators in the city hurt more, so that when I explained the pros and cons, they would be willing to take the initiative to strike. But now that it's ended hastily, it's hard to bring up that plan."

Tian Duzi knew exactly what that plan was.

He said helplessly, "There's no need for such painful sacrifices. Everyone understands reason. Just speak directly. If you feel the pressure isn't enough, then lead the team yourself. They'll have to give you some face."

"Lead the team myself?" Ling Tian City Lord frowned.

"Yes. If you take the lead yourself, who would dare to refuse? Besides, I'm guarding Ling Tian Pass now. Not even a fly can get in. What else do you have to worry about?"

Tian Duzi said this.

Ling Tian City Lord fell into deep thought.

Suddenly.

"Could it be that cultivators from the Eastern Wilderness can enter this bloody battlefield, but your noble Nether Origin Sect cultivators truly don't want to get involved at all?"

Ling Tian City Lord looked up sharply, staring at the old man's half-smiling gaze.

Their eyes met, and in the end, it was Tian Duzi who couldn't withstand the pressure and shifted his gaze away.

But Ling Tian City Lord didn't pursue that piercing remark either.

"You're right. I should set an example."

Having said that, he turned and left.

Alone in the enclosed space, Tian Duzi let out a heavy sigh.

At least, from his probing here, it seemed the Nether Origin Sect hadn't truly intended to drag all the Eastern Wilderness sects into the water.

But he wasn't sure. Ye Ling Tian was a relatively fair person; he might not necessarily represent the Nether Origin Sect's true thoughts.

"Better to inform the fellow Daoists from other sects first!"

...

The siege of the West City was broken early.

But the other three battlefields weren't so easy.

The offensive and defensive battles lasted over a month. Occasionally, Sages from within the city would personally intervene to intercept those fourth-rank demon emperors.

Various intelligence reports kept coming from the front lines.

But at least there were no more shocking achievements like Luo Chen slaying a demon emperor in formation.

Without absolute power or numerical advantage, battles at the Sage level were often a matter of pulling their punches.

There might be victories or defeats, but life-and-death outcomes were rare.

And precisely because of this, Luo Chen's achievement of slaying a demon emperor in formation appeared even more shocking.

If Luo Chen's reputation as the Pill Sect weren't already deeply ingrained, perhaps in the constant retelling, he would have gained several more Dao names like "Demon-Slaying Sage" or "White Flame Divine Lord."

Luo Chen didn't particularly care about any of this.

A reputation earned through battle might be solid, but it wasn't of much benefit to others. The title "Pill Sect" seemed more amiable.

As for the Frost Eagle's corpse, Luo Chen ordered half of its flesh to be taken and sent to Bai Li Qingchuan.

Fourth-rank demon emperor flesh was definitely a great supplement for Wang Yuan.

It might even help him reach the Great Perfection of the Furnace Realm.

The remaining half, as previously agreed, would be handed over to the Luo Tian Treasure Pavilion to sell to the cultivators of Ling Tian Pass.

Even just half of it could exchange for spirit stones worth ten million.

Moreover, the high-level resources involved—feathers, bones, essence blood, and internal organ essence—weren't even included.

If this business was done well, it would be enough to make Luo Tian Sect instantly wealthy.

Luo Chen also ordered a pot of meat soup to be boiled for himself, but after eating it, the gains were negligible.

"Although it's a fourth-rank demon emperor, these demon beasts have converted to the human cultivation method after all. Their essence is concentrated in their Nascent Souls. In my view, their physiques can't compare to fourth-rank archaic beasts."

"The energy contained within is too meager for my fourth-rank mid-stage archaic physique."

Luo Chen glanced at the panel showing his body refinement progress. Compared to before, it had only increased by two or three points.

And those two or three points weren't even brought by the demon emperor's flesh.

They came from his practice of the Moon Step, which strengthened his legs.

The technique *Wind, Moon, Thunder, Cloud—Stepping on the Immortal Path* itself had body refinement effects, but only targeted the legs.

In Luo Chen's view, even if Wang Yuan completely perfected the four killing moves and he learned them all, it would at most increase his combat methods.

It wouldn't bring much improvement to his body refinement realm.

To continue improving his physical body, he would have to cultivate a more profound body refinement technique.

At the very least, it would have to surpass the *Myriad Path Convergence* that Luo Chen had created himself.

"The path of body refinement has declined since ancient times. Being able to rely on my own efforts to reach the fourth-rank mid-stage is already my limit."

"To reach the fifth-rank, where one can see spirit without breaking and shatter the void—too difficult! Too difficult!"

Luo Chen sighed.

He had been clearer than anyone about the shortcomings of body refinement from the very beginning.

The four words "unclear path ahead" were no empty talk.

That was why, after encountering the bottleneck of the fourth-rank mid-stage archaic stage, he hadn't spent great effort to continue delving into this path.

Instead, he had placed all his focus on the path of qi cultivation.

As things stood now, his decision was correct.

His profound realm, nearing the fourth layer of the Sage stage, along with numerous powerful methods, were gradually surpassing his former trump card—his physical body.

A flick of his hand.

A diamond-shaped shuttle appeared in his hand.

This was the only legacy left behind after Frost Eagle Demon Emperor's death.

He hadn't found a storage pouch on the corpse, nor any alternative storage space like Black King's stomach pouch—only this shuttle.

After several days of study, Luo Chen had gained some understanding of this flying shuttle.

Its level: true artifact grade.

Its material: the bone of some unknown creature, with a faint remnant of source blood aura. It was probably the bone of some archaic beast.

As for the shuttle's effect, Luo Chen had already understood it clearly during the battle.

It excelled at concealment, could break through space, and had an instinctive ability to find flaws in large-scale area spells like the Azure Yang Great Handprint.

It seemed that when Frost Eagle refined this treasure, it was specifically designed to counter human cultivators' spells.

He had to admit, the effect was somewhat unexpected.

The Azure Yang Great Handprint, after Luo Chen's continuous optimization and the addition of formation insights, the Spirit-Enslaving Seal, and other elements, was already extraordinary.

Yet it was still broken by this treasure.

But unfortunately, Luo Chen's methods weren't limited to just one Azure Yang Great Handprint.

In battle, after realizing that large-scale spells were ineffective against Frost Eagle Demon Emperor, Luo Chen had immediately changed his strategy.

Instead of overwhelming with profound magical power, he switched to using his profound divine sense to ambush.

As they passed each other, he imperceptibly attached a wisp of Withering-Bloom Divine Fire to the opponent.

Then, silently, he deployed his lifebound treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, into the void.

In the frontal battle, he mainly focused on dodging.

The final result was that, amid Frost Eagle Demon Emperor's shock, Luo Chen struck with a sudden killing blow.

He probably died without even understanding what had happened!

"This treasure's function probably isn't limited to combat. Otherwise, he wouldn't have chosen to use it to carry his Nascent Soul when fleeing, instead of using Nascent Soul teleportation."

"No rush!"

"After I spend some time removing the marks left on it, I'll be able to see its true form!"

Luo Chen smiled slightly and put away the flying shuttle.

Then he opened his mouth and spat.

The gates of his Purple Mansion swung wide open, and a spark of fire flew out from his mouth.

It landed on the ground, and in the blink of an eye, transformed into a small tree of green and white.

At its core, a point of golden light was tightly wrapped.

The moment the fire tree appeared, the grand formation shielding the cave dwelling began to melt rapidly.

Layer after layer of light screens continuously retreated.

Luo Chen frowned and shouted in a low voice, "Restrain yourself a bit!"

The fire tree shook its branches and leaves, seeming somewhat aggrieved, but it actively controlled its power.

Rings of light confined themselves to a range of about a zhang around the fire tree.

Looking at this fire tree, Luo Chen showed a satisfied expression.

The Withering-Bloom True Flame itself had the function of refining flesh and blood and nourishing vitality.

After absorbing the Red Lotus Earth Fire, the fourth-rank Netherfire, and many unknown demonic flames, another attribute had gradually emerged.

Until the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, when this fire reached the fifth-rank, that attribute had fully equaled the original one.

Luo Chen hadn't understood the terror of this other attribute before.

He had only used its properties to help himself refine impurities in his magical power.

But after instantly refining Motian Cliff and battling Frost Eagle Demon Emperor, he had fully understood.

It wasn't a low-level effect of filth or corrosion, but an overbearing might that forcibly destroyed the vitality of enemies.

The vitality of a true artifact originated from the naturally formed array patterns that could communicate with heaven and earth's power. But once contaminated by the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire, those array patterns would rapidly disintegrate, revealing flaws and becoming porous. That was how Motian Cliff had disappeared in an instant.

The vitality of living beings originated from their flesh and blood bodies. Once contaminated by the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire, not only would it rapidly extinguish, but that vitality would even fuel the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire's power, making it more severe and harder to extinguish.

In Luo Chen's view, with his current fifth-rank Withering-Bloom Divine Fire, as long as it landed on an enemy unexpectedly, early-stage Sage cultivators would die on contact.

Even mid-stage cultivators, without a method to counter it, would find it difficult to escape.

"If I disregard everything and use all my magical power and even my source power to drive it, I believe even late-stage Great Sages would have trouble enduring the fully flourishing Withering-Bloom Divine Fire!"

"But that would leave me in an absolute weakened state, putting me at a disadvantage."

After some thought, Luo Chen ultimately decided that unless absolutely necessary, he would not use this fire in front of others.

This would be his greatest trump card. Either he wouldn't use it, or when he did, it would achieve absolute results!

Besides, Luo Chen had many methods. It wasn't as if he was at his wit's end without the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire.

He had a body of peak-level spells, and a self-created divine ability that had defeated all the geniuses of the Dao Sect.

The Yuantu Sword was sharp and unmatched, the Primordial Unity Cauldron suppressed all things.

His fourth-rank archaic physique, combined with a body of martial techniques, made him a living ancient archaic beast.

Looking at the overall picture, Luo Chen had fully understood his combat positioning.

He could crush and sweep through those of the same realm!

For mid-stage Sage cultivators one minor realm higher, if he put in a bit more effort, he could also defeat them. Only when facing sixth-layer Sage cultivators did he need to be somewhat careful.

As for late-stage Great Sages...

Luo Chen licked his lips, caution flashing in his eyes.

"After all, I've never personally fought one. It's hard to estimate the gap between us."

"But I believe self-preservation won't be a problem!"

...

The demon beasts' siege of Ling Tian Pass had effectively already failed after the West City's encirclement was broken.

After two months of fierce fighting on the other three fronts, they ultimately retreated in defeat.

With this retreat, the cultivators of Ling Tian Pass gained a chance to catch their breath.

Everyone seized the time to rest and heal, or prepare various methods, ready for the next great battle.

But this was only for low-level cultivators.

On this day, Luo Chen received an order to go to the City Lord's Mansion for a meeting.

He assumed it was just a routine meeting and went gladly.

But upon arriving, looking at the gathering of Sages, including some unfamiliar faces he hadn't seen before, he couldn't help but sigh inwardly.

The battle that Ling Fengzi had worried about had finally come!

He looked up toward the upper seats, where three people sat in the places of honor.

With just one glance, his gaze fell upon the cultivator shrouded entirely in a black robe.

Under the black hood, the other party's deep eyes stared at him without concealment.

It was him, Five Elements Shenyuan!
