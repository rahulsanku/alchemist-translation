# Chapter 674: Hundred Crow Ghost Forest, Reaching Wood Heaven Plain

Beside the great river.

The Daoist sat in lotus posture upon the lotus platform, a treasured cauldron hanging above his head.

Some hesitation showed on his face.

His gaze fell on the pure white surface at the center of the lotus platform. Five lotus seeds the size of fists were embedded there, radiating pure five-element spiritual qi.

For a lotus platform to grow lotus seeds was only natural.

And the lotus seeds condensed by this Five Elements Lotus Platform, which was only one step away from the fifth tier, would naturally not be poor in quality.

If used as medicine, they could serve as main materials for fourth-tier pills.

If used for artifact-forging, the disposable magic treasures made from them might not have killing power inferior to the Thunder Immortal Pearls [TERM?] that had flowed out from White Night's cave abode on Mount Accumulated Thunder.

Luo Chen did not have the corresponding methods to use them.

After all, what was needed to refine the Nascent Soul Pill was only the lotus platform itself.

At this moment, Luo Chen experimentally placed his hand on a red bead. It was the fire lotus seed.

According to Han Zhan, the Five Elements Lotus Platform was an auxiliary cultivation treasure that only Sage Sages could use. It could use its trait of swallowing heaven and earth spiritual qi and automatically filtering impurities to accelerate cultivation.

Even in the Eastern Wilderness, this object was rarely circulated and was controlled by the Nether Abyss Sect [TERM?].

Only occasionally would a small amount flow out.

It was said that the reason Divine Origin Sage [TERM?] of the Five Elements Divine Sect could cultivate all five elements together and step into the late Sage stage was because he obtained the help of one Five Elements Lotus Platform.

Most likely, the lotus platform Divine Origin Sage obtained was inferior to the one before Luo Chen.

That topic was a little far.

Returning to the main matter.

Although this object was for Sages, it was not absolutely useless in the hands of Golden Core cultivators!

If one used a Golden Core to replace a lotus seed and supplemented it with a mind-confusing art, the spirit of the lotus platform would treat the cultivator's Golden Core as a lotus seed nurtured by itself.

In this way, it could also assist cultivation.

And during this process, Luo Chen could also conveniently "tame" the lotus platform's spirit, making it stop resisting and struggling, so that he could easily take it away from Blazing Refinement Prison.

"Let me try it for the moment!"

Luo Chen applied slight force. Amid the Five Elements Lotus Platform's angry struggle, he forcibly pried out the fire lotus seed.

Then he opened his mouth and spat. A white pill-like bead spun out.

It was not his lifebound Golden Core, but the second Origin Pill whose mana was exhausted!

Although he trusted Han Zhan, Luo Chen still did not quite dare use his lifebound Golden Core to experiment. What if something went wrong?

After all, Han Zhan himself had never cultivated with the help of a Five Elements Lotus Platform. The small trick he gave Luo Chen had also been learned from hearsay while exchanging with fellow Daoists.

The second Origin Pill was fine.

If it truly did not work, Luo Chen still had strength left and could pull it out.

The Origin Pill entered the lotus platform, like one of its lotus seeds.

When Luo Chen circulated the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture, large amounts of heaven and earth spiritual qi surged over from all directions, from the magma river beneath his feet, from Blazing Refinement Prison, which was full of dense fire spiritual qi.

If it had been ordinary times, Luo Chen would have had to capture and refine such violent spiritual qi carefully.

But now, he omitted that step.

In the next moment, the heaven-reaching spiritual qi poured directly into the Five Elements Lotus Platform.

Receiving this batch of spiritual qi, the Five Elements Lotus Platform, which had still been struggling, became slightly calmer.

It was as if someone was "feeding" it.

Luo Chen held his breath and concentrated, carefully sensing the changes within.

After a while, he let out a turbid breath in relief.

"It works, as expected. Han Zhan did not deceive me!"

In his perception, the violent heaven and earth spiritual qi poured into the entire lotus platform. Impurities began to be filtered, while the essence flowed through the meridians of the lotus platform and moved separately toward the five lotus seeds.

Because this was Blazing Refinement Prison, most of the spiritual qi was fire attribute. Thus, streams of fire spiritual qi surged toward the position where the fire lotus seed had originally been.

Under this support, the second Origin Pill seemed like a starving beast. It refused nothing, opened its mouth wide, and madly devoured.

After one great circulation cycle, when he looked again at the second Origin Pill, the spiritual qi contained inside it had already reached one or two percent.

The progress was pleasing!

While Luo Chen rested, Han Zhan specially reminded him.

"Remember to refine this batch of spiritual qi in the Origin Pill with divine sense later and thoroughly transform it into your own mana. After all, you are only at the Golden Core stage and do not have a Nascent Soul to control the power of heaven and earth spiritual qi. Strictly speaking, the spiritual qi you absorbed has not yet been imprinted by you."

"Mm."

Luo Chen nodded lightly.

Afterward, after a brief adjustment, Luo Chen once again began circulating his cultivation method.

...

After a great battle, there naturally had to be recuperation.

Luo Chen cultivated beside the magma river, where spiritual qi was abundant. Moya Cave Master did the same. He found a quiet place and entered seclusion to heal.

Tai Sui, however, was strange.

Originally, his condition had been rather poor. But after his battle with the Sea King Squid, not only had his body changed from a thin bamboo pole back into an inflated fatty, his mental condition had also improved greatly.

He did not enter seclusion to cultivate. Instead, he wandered through Blazing Refinement Prison.

None of the three urged the journey onward.

Time passed slowly like this.

During this process, Luo Chen released Heavenjade and let her try searching nearby for some fire-attribute resources.

She was to explore only safe places.

As for dangerous places, she should avoid provoking them as much as possible.

When another day of cultivation ended, Luo Chen felt his divine soul somewhat fatigued.

This kind of day-and-night circulation of one's lifebound cultivation method was usually done only during deep seclusion. It placed great pressure on a cultivator's divine soul.

Even he had to stop and rest from time to time.

He took out a Fasting Pill from his storage ring and swallowed it. While his body felt slightly full, his attention fell on the rolling magma river beneath his feet.

There was a red fish swimming nearby.

Splash!

Where Luo Chen's gaze reached, the red fish lively leapt from the river surface, splashing layers of red waves.

It was that fourth-tier fire spirit.

Perhaps because Luo Chen had saved the silver-white fire python, this red fish, also a fire spirit, had greatly increased its friendliness toward him. Recently, it swam near him from time to time, also absorbing some pure fire spiritual qi leaking from the Five Elements Lotus Platform.

Looking at the red fish, Luo Chen's heart stirred.

A wisp of azure flame appeared at his fingertip.

He had refined the Withering-Bloom True Fire for several decades, almost a hundred years.

It had absorbed large amounts of demon beast vitality, and its foundation could be called deep.

This time, after coming to Blazing Refinement Prison, it had devoured seven low-tier sourceless flames and one fourth-tier Burning Heaven Fire. Logically speaking, its might should have become even greater.

But because the devouring had been hurried, he had not yet had time to fully control its power. This caused its performance in the battle with Shark Emperor Liujun to be rather ordinary.

"It should not be like this."

Murmuring, Luo Chen sank into thought.

He began reflecting on the battle with Shark Emperor Liujun.

Under circumstances where the other party was injured and lacked home-field advantage, Luo Chen had still fallen into disadvantage in a frontal battle. He had even been forced to do something like a lizard severing its tail.

Such a result was unacceptable to him.

What was the reason?

...

Half a month later.

Luo Chen still sat withered beside the great river.

Within the magma river beneath his feet, an azure fire tree stretched its branches, allowing the red fish fire spirit to roll up stream after stream of magma and pour it over those branches.

The azure fire tree did not avoid it in the slightest. Instead, it felt incomparably comfortable.

From time to time, it even split off strands of azure flame to provoke the red fish, making the red fish produce strange laughter.

With such a close relationship, if Luo Chen took the chance to devour and refine it, the red fish would probably still be confused and ignorant.

Luo Chen naturally would not do such a thing.

At present, the number and quality of sourceless flames devoured by the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit had already reached the limit. Taking another step would no longer be something he could control.

While controlling the Withering-Bloom True Fire to play and wrestle with the red fish, Luo Chen chatted with Han Zhan through divine sense.

What they discussed was clearly the matter that had troubled Luo Chen for most of this half month.

"Your spells were still exquisite, and your magic treasure control was also appropriate. As for your grasp of battle opportunities and use of the battlefield, there was even less to criticize..."

Han Zhan carefully analyzed matters for Luo Chen.

In the end, he reached a conclusion.

"In that battle, you made no great mistake."

Seeing worry still hanging on Luo Chen's face, Han Zhan said helplessly, "Why demand too much from yourself? Being able to repel a Sage Sage would shake the entire cultivation world if word spread. With you behaving like this, how are those old Golden Core cultivators supposed to face themselves?"

Luo Chen forced a smile, his tone low.

"I only feel that I could have done better in that battle, but I do not know where the problem was."

"Perhaps there was no problem in the first place?" Han Zhan objected seriously. "That Liujun is no weakling. He is an existence who survived the demon-devil war. Even when this old man's fleshly body was still present, if I faced him, it would still be hard to say who would live or die. If it were above the sea, I would certainly lose!"

These words were comfort, but also fact.

Liujun was a generation's Shark Emperor. The methods he was best at all involved controlling water qi.

Back when Blood Wanderer, a Sage said to be comparable to late-stage great cultivators though only at mid-stage Sage, chased him in the Fallen Sea, Liujun had still escaped.

From this, one could see his abilities.

Luo Chen tugged at the corner of his mouth and only said, "Senior is too modest." After that, he gave no reply.

Suddenly...

Luo Chen raised his head. Escape light was flying over.

It was Heavenjade.

Behind Heavenjade was a black-robed cultivator with a powerful aura.

Swish!

Wind howled as Heavenjade landed.

"Master..."

Luo Chen raised his hand, interrupting Heavenjade's words, then looked coldly at the newcomer.

That black-robed cultivator sensed Luo Chen's existence and stopped. Then his eyes fell on the five-colored lotus platform beneath Luo Chen.

He opened his mouth, wild joy appearing on his face.

"A supreme-grade Five Elements Lotus Platform!"

"This treasure, this old man wants it!"

After saying this, he kicked the flying sword beneath his feet, releasing a chilling killing intent as he charged toward Luo Chen.

There was almost no unnecessary talk. Cultivators seizing treasure were just this simple and direct.

Luo Chen had thought that he might encounter others in Demon-Fall Land, but he had not expected that whether it was Adept Red Corpse and his group earlier or this Golden Core Stage Eight black-robed cultivator, they would be this pure.

When seizing another person's object, they were not even willing to say a false "this treasure is fated with me."

It could only be said that Demon-Fall Land's isolation from inside and outside magnified the ugliness of human nature. Whether human or demon, none bothered pretending.

Including Luo Chen himself!

Facing the flying sword that came shrieking toward him, Luo Chen's expression did not change. He merely stretched out his left hand.

Seeing a Golden Core Stage Six cultivator behave this way, the black-robed cultivator laughed coldly.

"Seeking death. My Tai Xuan Severing-Vein Sword [TERM?] was refined from Severing Heaven Balefulness [TERM?] and large quantities of Geng Metal. It is incomparably sharp, and ordinary defensive magic treasures find it hard to resist. You, a junior, are actually so arrogant as to receive it barehanded... receive it..."

A stunned expression appeared on his face.

Because that left hand, white as jade, lightly and easily pinched the sword tip.

No matter how the black-robed cultivator drove his mana, the so-called Tai Xuan Severing-Vein Sword remained motionless under terrifying strength.

"How is this possible!"

He roared madly in his heart, his face full of disbelief.

Then he saw the red-robed Daoist form seals with his right hand, finally extending his fingers into a palm and pushing horizontally.

An enormous azure palm seal rumbled toward him.

The black-robed cultivator no longer cared about recalling his lifebound magic treasure and hurriedly used defensive arts. Yet under that azure palm seal, all defenses were equal to nothing.

Even when he took out a low-grade defensive magic treasure at the end, a wisp of azure flame attached itself to it. Under his wide-eyed gaze, that defensive magic treasure's spiritual light dimmed, as if it had lost its vitality.

Boom!

One palm pushed across eight hundred zhang.

The black-robed cultivator flew through the air like a torn sack within the violent mana.

Heavenjade seized the chance and leapt up. The plantain fan in her hand waved wildly, scattering several strands of astral wind that directly killed him.

When the storm calmed, Heavenjade carried a storage bag and returned beneath Luo Chen's seat.

"Reporting to Master. This person coveted a piece of fourth-tier Amber Lava [TERM?] I dug out and chased me along the way..."

Heavenjade reported what had happened, but as she spoke, she discovered that Luo Chen seemed somewhat distracted.

Clang!

The Tai Xuan Severing-Vein Sword fell to the ground.

Luo Chen's eyes stared at his left and right hands, revealing a thoughtful expression.

"Yes. My spells are exquisite, and my magic treasure control is unmatched, but those are qi cultivator methods."

"Yet now, what I am most powerful in is clearly body refinement."

"In the past, I treated body refinement as a hidden killing move and habitually used spells and treasures against ordinary enemies. Even against Shark Emperor Liujun, I continued this habit."

"Clearly, this habit now has flaws."

He slowly lowered his right hand, leaving only his left hand held before him.

When fighting peak experts, life and death lay in a single line. The Dao of balance had no merit.

Even if his spells were more exquisite, they still could not cross the gap between Golden Core and Sage.

If he wanted to break the situation, only this powerful body comparable to an archaic beast could do it!

"Next time, if I meet a Sage-level enemy again, perhaps I should change my fighting style."

His left palm slowly clenched, forming a fist the size of a sandbag. Muscles tightened, and his knuckles looked ferocious.

...

On a mountain peak.

Moya Cave Master, face pale, watched this scene with his heart and courage trembling.

"He has become stronger again!"

A great Golden Core cultivator who clearly did not look easy to provoke had not even survived one move under Luo Chen. What level of ability was this?

Beside him, Tai Sui smiled with joy.

"It is good if he is stronger! Good if he is stronger!"

Facing Moya Cave Master's surprise, he explained somewhat unnaturally, "I mean, Demon-Fall Land is full of danger. The stronger he is, the safer we are, no?"

Moya Cave Master accepted this explanation.

Indeed, from what they had seen so far, Demon-Fall Land was extremely dangerous.

Not only did outside cultivators fight and seize from one another, but there were also powerful local existences naturally born here.

Those fourth-tier strange beasts, fire spirits, and even regional overlords were absolutely not things Golden Core cultivators like them could resist.

If they wanted gains, they needed a powerful fighter comparable to a Sage, like Devil Lord Azure Yang, standing at the front.

Tai Sui looked at the sky and said, "How is your condition now? Can we set off?"

Moya Cave Master coughed twice and slowly nodded.

"Good. I will go ask Devil Lord Azure Yang and decide a time to leave Blazing Refinement Prison."

Tai Sui leisurely descended the mountain.

He did not want to stay in Blazing Refinement Prison for even one more day.

...

One month later.

Within a primeval ancient forest radiating dense vitality, three figures appeared and disappeared faintly.

A fat cultivator walked at the front. Behind him were an old man and a young man.

The three walked neither fast nor slow, stepping through the forest one deep step and one shallow step at a time.

Suddenly...

A thick branch from an ancient tree dozens of zhang tall slapped horizontally toward them.

"Hmph!"

The fat cultivator snorted coldly. That branch stopped before the three of them.

Moya Cave Master looked at this scene in surprise. Finally, his gaze settled on the huge face formed on the trunk of the ancient tree.

"Wood spirit?"

That ancient tree spirit looked at the three in confusion. Its branches and leaves rustled. Finally, it bent its branch slightly toward the fat cultivator, as if saluting.

"It seems you still remember me!"

Tai Sui smiled slightly and casually waved a hand.

In an instant, the gloomy ancient trees ahead all trembled. Branches and vines spread apart, as if making way.

Tai Sui turned back.

"This Hundred Crow Ghost Forest [TERM?] contains countless wood spirits and Duskvine Crows [TERM?]. It is truly the most dangerous land in Five Elements Heaven. Within it even dwell a Woodman King [TERM?] and a Duskvine Crow Ancestor [TERM?], both at the fifth tier. Their danger is not inferior at all to the Iron-Eating Beast of the Thousand-Ren Mountains, Heavenloam of the Flowing Sand Sea, or the Vermilion Bird Fire Spirit of Blazing Refinement Prison."

"With them holding this place, even Sage experts do not dare cross Hundred Crow Ghost Forest lightly."

Moya Cave Master was horrified.

"This place actually has two powerful existences comparable to the fifth tier?"

"Of course. After all, the existence of Hundred Crow Ghost Forest is for the sake of guarding Wood Heaven Plain!"

Tai Sui pointed with his right hand. Across the distance, a towering plain entered the eyes of the three.

"That is Wood Heaven Plain, and also Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's former spirit herb garden."

At this point, he paused and revealed a proud expression.

"Also, you misunderstood one thing. It is not two. In truth, it is three."

He pointed at himself.

"Once, I was also one of the three giants of Hundred Crow Ghost Forest!"

"This time, with me leading the way, you are blessed."

After saying this, Tai Sui laughed loudly. His laughter spread through the gloomy ancient forest, causing trees to tremble and Duskvine Crows to rise one after another.

He strode forward. Wherever he passed, withered vines and ancient trees all made way.

The three advanced as if on a smooth road.

Luo Chen calmly watched this scene. Finally, his gaze turned toward the towering plain surrounded by endless ghost forest.

There was the legendary Wood Heaven Plain!

It was also the place countless Northern Sea cultivators entered the Fallen Sea and rushed toward, wave after wave.

*(End of Chapter)*
