# Chapter 1137: The City Lord's Night Banquet

The magnificent City Lord's Mansion was ablaze with lights tonight!

Fine wines and delicacies flowed from the back kitchen like a never-ending stream, filling over a hundred banquet tables.

All of this was to celebrate the successful return of three merchant caravans.

In previous years, this would not have happened, but this year was somewhat special.

The City Lord's Mansion had dispatched a total of five merchant caravans, yet two had been lost, and even among the remaining three, some had suffered heavy casualties.

Logically speaking, with such losses, they ought to have lowered their profile and licked their wounds. However, that young deputy city lord, Xiao Feng, had instead made a grand display with a lavish banquet to reward the meritorious ones who had protected the caravans to the death.

"In times of crisis, not losing momentum—this deputy city lord has a good grasp of strategy."

Luo Chen sat in the main hall, watching the lively scene outside with a calm expression.

He had once governed a behemoth like the Luo Tian Sect, so he naturally understood that the more difficult the times, the less one should reveal one's weakness to the outside world. Otherwise, enemies might swarm in all at once.

However, judging by the arrangement of seats in this main hall, he was not the true guest of honor at tonight's banquet.

Figures filed in one after another from outside.

There was an old woman leaning on a cane, a red-faced burly man, and a relatively young-looking man.

When these three entered and saw Luo Chen, surprise immediately appeared on their faces.

"At tonight's banquet, there's actually someone who isn't a Third Realm Body Cultivator sitting with us?"

The speaker was the young man with an arrogant expression.

Luo Chen acted as if he hadn't heard, continuing to sit quietly in his chair, pouring himself drinks.

Seeing this, the young man's face showed anger, as if greatly displeased by Luo Chen's haughty attitude.

The contrast between their reactions was immediately apparent.

The old woman and the burly man inwardly shook their heads.

"Xiao Zirui is still too young!"

"He must have been hit hard, seeing disdain in everyone he meets. But this stranger is indeed a bit too proud."

Of course, they did not voice their thoughts.

After the three newcomers took their seats, they began conversing among themselves.

"Redfire, you returned quite quickly this time, far ahead of us."

"Just lucky to be close to the main city. The bandits who attacked were nothing formidable. But Granny Yu, I heard you displayed great power and severely wounded that Meteor Bandit leader Tong Huai? That's truly remarkable. That Tong Huai's horizontal training allowed him to roam freely across the battlefield, yet he suffered a great loss at your hands."

"You flatter me. Battlefield skirmishes and frontal combat are two different things. Even so, I only managed to barely defeat Tong Huai by a single move through cleverness. There was no great display of power."

"Brother Xiao, why brood? It's just some goods. You and the deputy city lord are cousins; surely he won't blame you."

"Yes, with the city lord's position still undecided, the deputy city lord needs people he can rely on. As long as you preserve yourself, you'll still have a place to prove your worth."

"Thank you both for the comfort. I won't dwell on it. This time, I was simply careless. The Meteor Bandit's second-in-command was supposed to be only at the Blood River Realm peak, but I didn't expect him to be hiding his true strength. Caught off guard, I fell for his trick. If I ever meet him again, I'll grind his bones to dust!"

"Haha..."

Luo Chen observed coldly, quickly learning the general situation of these three through their conversation.

The old woman leaning on a cane was called Granny Yu, the red-faced burly man was addressed as Redfire, and the young man was a descendant of the original city lord's lineage.

They had all been sent by the deputy city lord to oversee various regions, handling the City Lord's Mansion's industries and security matters.

Returning at year's end, like Steward Yan, they had also encountered the sudden attack of the Meteor Bandits.

As for the results, among those now sitting at this table, aside from Xiao Zirui, the other two had naturally succeeded in bringing their caravans back safely.

As for why Xiao Zirui, despite his failure, could still sit here—it was simply relying on his family connection.

Luo Chen paid no attention to these matters. In fact, he was very familiar with this process.

Next, the deputy city lord would make his appearance, followed by the distribution of rewards according to merit.

He himself could then legitimately enter the other's field of vision and temporarily settle down in this Lost Immortal City.

Luo Chen was already accustomed to being subordinate to others. One's status should match one's strength. Although he didn't consider these people worth his notice, he didn't mind lying low for a while until he understood the true situation of this world.

The next moment, footsteps came from outside.

Everyone rose and looked toward the door. Several figures made their way through the crowd from the banquet outside the main hall.

Wherever they passed, voices of "Deputy City Lord" rang out in flattery.

Luo Chen looked up. At the forefront was a handsome man in white robes holding a folding fan. To his left was an old man with a sinister expression, and to his right was a beautiful woman in palace attire. Behind them followed a group of elderly attendants.

Steward Yan was among them.

At this moment, he was crossing through the crowd, making eye signals to Luo Chen, who was still seated.

Luo Chen pretended not to see, maintaining his calm expression.

The group reached the main hall's entrance. The other old attendants withdrew, leaving only the front three to enter.

"Greetings, Deputy City Lord!"

Everyone in the hall paid their respects.

Xiao Feng clasped his hands with his folding fan, "Xiao Feng offers his greetings. I must thank you all for safely escorting the merchant caravans, ensuring that Lost Immortal City's future business will not be affected."

Upon hearing this, Granny Yu and Redfire both waved their hands, "It was our duty."

Luo Chen, however, remained seated and properly received the greeting. It was what he deserved.

Seeing this, Xiao Zirui frowned, "How dare you!"

But Xiao Feng extended his folding fan to block Xiao Zirui, smiling as he looked at Luo Chen: "This senior also contributed greatly, single-handedly slaying dozens of Meteor Bandits and protecting the caravan's safety. Xiao Feng is truly grateful."

Everyone looked at Luo Chen in surprise, then showed expressions of understanding.

So that's why Luo Chen could sit so openly in this main hall—the merit he had earned was the same as theirs!

But then, someone caught two words in Xiao Feng's speech and their expression changed slightly.

Granny Yu tested the waters: "Senior?"

Luo Chen didn't look old. In fact, if one ignored his unique ethereal and otherworldly aura, his appearance could be said to be the youngest among everyone present.

Anyone who saw him would take him for a young man in his twenties.

Neither his realm nor his status should warrant the title "Senior" from Xiao Feng, the nominal deputy city lord.

There was only one possibility!

Redfire blurted out, looking at Luo Chen with astonishment, "Could it be that you're a cultivator who came in from the outside world?"

Luo Chen didn't answer, instead looking at Xiao Feng.

"You've done quite a bit of investigating. Steward Yan told you, didn't he?"

Xiao Feng clasped his hands in salute, "Please forgive me, Senior. These are troubled times, and I must be cautious."

Luo Chen hummed in acknowledgment, then helped himself to a cup of wine, displaying an air of natural ease and grace.

Seeing this, those at the table who had previously thought him arrogant now felt he embodied the bearing of an immortal.

Cultivators who managed to fall into the Great Destruction Realm were all those who had lived for hundreds or thousands of years, some even being ancient Daoist giants who had witnessed the passage of eons.

Such beings, even if trapped in shallow waters, were not to be humiliated by ordinary mortals.

As for Xiao Zirui, after his initial shock, he still showed disdain.

So what if he was a cultivator?

Once in the Great Destruction Realm, they were no different from ordinary people. Moreover, due to their Daoist cultivation, when they switched to martial arts, they could often only stagnate at the third realm of body refinement. And the sudden drop in power often made such people lose sight of themselves, frequently ending in death and destruction.

Thus, why bother showing them superficial respect?

The banquet proceeded with cups clinking and toasts exchanged. Xiao Feng was a skilled socializer, maintaining an excellent atmosphere.

Until he brought up a matter.

"At the beginning of spring, a special envoy from Carefree City will arrive. If he brings my father's decree, then all is well. If not, there will surely be another martial contest to determine the city lord's succession. At that time, I hope you will all lend me your strength!"

Instantly, the hall fell silent.
