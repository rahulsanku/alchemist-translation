# Chapter 973: Soul Stones Everywhere, Soul Splitting Perfected

"What is this?"

A handful of crystalline grit lay in his thick palm, sifting through his fingers with the breeze, until only a single grain remained at his fingertips.

Luo Chen stared fixedly at that one grain, as if his entire mind had sunk into it.

A look of dawning recognition crossed his face, yet also a trace of disbelief.

His gaze wandered—from the knife-carved, axe-hewn Severed Soul Cliff to the boundless Yellow Spring Sea, finally settling on the black, jewel-like beach beneath his feet.

"If this really is that legendary thing... isn't there too much of it?"

Luo Chen couldn't help swallowing. Even his normally placid mind rippled with waves of emotion.

To avoid misidentification, he performed several tests.

He infused it with mana—it remained untouched by spiritual power.

He dripped his essence blood onto it—it shone as brilliantly as before.

He wrapped it in divine sense—the black grain burst into dark light, flew from his fingertip, and circled him like a bee.

Finally, Luo Chen cautiously refined it with his true flame. The black grain slowly melted, releasing wisps of green smoke. He gave a slight sniff, and just that one breath made his soul unconsciously float, even forgetting himself.

Luo Chen didn't restrain his mind. He grinned broadly, appearing somewhat unhinged.

He had confirmed it. This was exactly what he had suspected—a Soul Stone!

"The Devil Lord Heaven-Refining did not deceive me. Such extraordinary treasures truly exist in this world!"

No wonder Luo Chen was so excited. Treasures like "Soul Stones" were exceedingly rare in the world.

Their effects were also heaven-defying. They could be directly absorbed to strengthen one's soul foundation!

A single such object far surpassed a thousand secret soul-tempering techniques or ten thousand soul-nourishing pills.

Back then, the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining had encountered great fortune, obtaining a fist-sized Soul Stone by chance. After absorbing it, his soul had reached great perfection.

Later, obsessed with finding more, he had researched and developed the Great Soul Pill formula after failing to obtain another.

Even the main ingredient of the Great Soul Pill—soul essence—was merely a substitute for Soul Stones in his conception.

Both were essentially condensed essence of living souls.

But the difference was immense!

Soul essence was artificially extracted, limited both by the soul-refiner's technique and the strength of the raw material. As a result, it often retained the lingering will of countless living beings before death.

Using it to refine pills came with many constraints. The residual will was often difficult to completely remove. When taking a Great Soul Pill, one had to expend great effort to suppress and purify those wills, making it time-consuming and laborious.

Even Luo Chen, with his formidable soul foundation, had needed one or two months to digest a single Great Soul Pill in the past.

Moreover, according to Buddhist and Daoist theory, borrowing the souls of living beings to cultivate, while seemingly fast, actually carried great hidden dangers.

The deeper one's cultivation, the more karmic debt one accrued. When breaking through bottlenecks or major realms, one was extremely prone to qi deviation.

But Soul Stones were different!

This substance seemed to have been filtered countless times, having shed all residual will, leaving only the purest, most condensed soul essence.

Cultivators could either use them as ingredients for pills or directly absorb and refine them to strengthen their soul foundation.

The difference between the two was like a chasm.

Furthermore, according to the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining—who was also a master of artifact forging—if one used a large quantity of Soul Stones to refine a magical treasure, it would undoubtedly become a top-grade soul-attack treasure!

In this world, anyone who recognized such a treasure would never let a single Soul Stone slip by.

His heart pounding, Luo Chen felt his mouth go dry.

The Soul Stone in his hand was only the size of a grain of sand, far smaller than the fist-sized one the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining had obtained.

But...

Looking out, black grains covered the entire beach!

The quantity was self-evident.

"If I absorb and refine all the Soul Stones here, how far will my soul foundation expand?"

"At the very least, can my Sage Nascent Soul take a step forward and reach the Primordial Spirit level?"

His throat bobbed. Luo Chen took a deep breath and circulated the *Fine Dust Primordial Art*, striving to stabilize his mind.

In an instant, he returned to that calm, well-like state of mind.

Looking around, Luo Chen pondered carefully.

This place was eerie!

A vast number of Soul Stones lay scattered on the beach, yet when the Yellow Spring Sea's waves swept over them, they were not carried away. Instead, they accumulated more and more.

And he had already figured out the tidal patterns of the Yellow Spring Sea over these past months.

Unlike ordinary tides, the Yellow Spring Sea was extremely violent during the day and calm at night.

If he wanted to collect the treasure, he should avoid the daytime, lest he be swept in again.

Luo Chen dared not gamble on whether he could survive if he entered the Yellow Spring Sea a second time.

He even suspected that these Soul Stones were what the Yellow Spring Sea waves had vomited onto this beach after swallowing countless living beings.

If he wasn't careful, he might become just another grain of sand in this vast ocean.

"Let me first test whether the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining exaggerated the effects of Soul Stones."

Having thought of it, he acted. Standing on higher ground, he swept out his divine sense.

Unlike ordinary materials, mana and source power had no effect on Soul Stones. Only soul power could absorb and control them.

It was daytime now, and the Yellow Spring Sea's waves were still churning. He could only use his divine sense to grab a handful.

A handful of Soul Stones wasn't large—easily grabbed by hand—but using divine sense to seize them felt strangely heavy.

Fortunately, Luo Chen's soul was powerful, and he managed to obtain a fair amount.

As soon as he had them, he couldn't wait to return to his cave dwelling on the northern side.

Glancing at the slumbering Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit in his Purple Mansion, Luo Chen chose to mobilize the Nirvana Sacred Flame.

A golden bird flew from his mouth, circling a grain-sized Soul Stone and spitting golden flames. Wisps of green smoke rose.

Luo Chen closed his eyes and slowly absorbed the green smoke.

Unlike his earlier tentative taste, this time he didn't let a single wisp escape.

Two strands of green smoke, like dragons and serpents, followed his nostrils and slowly disappeared into his body.

And in his brain's sea of consciousness!

Golden waves rippled. Four figures hung high above.

As the green smoke entered, all four figures opened their eyes simultaneously!

...

That night, Luo Chen's emotions surged once more.

The Devil Lord Heaven-Refining had indeed not exaggerated the effects of Soul Stones. They were even more miraculous than recorded in the alchemy texts.

A single Soul Stone was enough to rival a Great Soul Pill refined from a large quantity of precious materials!

Moreover, in terms of refinement and absorption, it was far simpler and more convenient.

"Whether I can reach the Primordial Spirit realm, I don't know. But as long as I have enough time and enough Soul Stones, I can definitely cultivate the *Soul-Splitting Art* to great perfection!"

"By then, with nine souls united, who below the Ascendant realm could have a stronger soul than mine? Even Ascendant powerhouses might not necessarily..."

Suppressing the restlessness in his heart, Luo Chen began to formulate his cultivation plan for the coming period.

This island had no spirit veins whatsoever, making qi cultivation impossible.

The pills he had brought with him had also been consumed over the years through daily use and two severe injuries.

Therefore, he would set aside qi cultivation for now.

Having made this decision, Luo Chen unconsciously breathed a sigh of relief.

These past months, suffering from an inability to cultivate, his heart had felt empty. Now, at least he had something else to fill the void.

As for body cultivation, he would continue.

He still had a small jar of the poisonous wine, *Zhenling Millet Wine*. This wine was extremely toxic, and even the Hundred Poison King Gu could only digest a small portion each time.

As long as he persistently cultivated the *Hundred Poisons Body-Tempering Art*, over time, he might be able to step into the true fifth-rank desolate beast level.

Of course, body cultivation required maintaining a balance.

There were almost no materials on the island. If something went wrong with his body, there would be no way to repair it.

Therefore, the focus would be on cultivating the *Soul-Splitting Art*!

This incomplete technique, derived from Han Zhan's memories, had long reached the master level in terms of proficiency under Luo Chen's many years of practice.

Even if Han Zhan were resurrected, he might not surpass Luo Chen's attainment in this technique.

But attainment was one thing; power was another.

Because of the Eastern Wilderness war and the Southern Frontier expedition, the *Soul-Splitting Art* had remained at the three-soul level, long unable to split off a fourth soul.

This was the flaw.

Back then, Han Zhan, who had just entered the Sage realm, already possessed nine split souls, though none were particularly strong.

But having more split souls meant more derived techniques.

Whether in attack, defense, or life-saving ability, it would be greatly enhanced.

Even the methods Luo Chen had once envisioned—like external avatars or a second Sage Nascent Soul—all depended on having a sufficient number of split souls.

Now that he had this opportunity, how could he not seize it?

"My soul foundation was already strong enough to split a third soul when I was at the fourth layer of the Sage realm, but I delayed until the fifth layer."

"Now that I'm at the sixth layer, let alone a fourth soul, I can even attempt a fifth."

"The goal of nine souls is within reach!"

"At that time, with nine souls perfected and my soul expanded to its limit, I might be able to contend with Ascendant beings. And as for Ascendant great powers..."

Luo Chen recalled a certain rumor.

The Soul-Refining Ancestor of the Primordial Demon Sect had personally entered Severed Soul Cliff, reaped a great harvest, and successfully left this place.

Why not the Blood Sea Ancestor?

Why not the Primordial Demon Ancestor?

Why was it specifically the Soul-Refining Ancestor, renowned for his powerful soul?

From this point, did it mean that the key to leaving this place lay in having a sufficiently powerful soul?

If so, then if he didn't want to end his days here, mastering the *Soul-Splitting Art* was absolutely necessary!

Without hesitation.

That night, just as before, Luo Chen began to split his fourth soul.

In the silent darkness, without the sound of wind or waves, only Luo Chen's suppressed, agonized voice drifted faintly through the air.

...

Under the night sky.

A figure carrying a green lamp from the Illumination Shadow Sect walked along the soft beach.

A gold-red light emanated from his eyes. He would walk, stop, and from time to time bend down to pick up the grit beneath his feet.

When the sky began to lighten and the sound of waves came from afar, the man in hemp clothes stopped what he was doing, turned, and walked toward a nearby towering mountain.

But unlike previous years, this time he didn't go directly up the mountain. Instead, he stopped at its base, watching the fierce waves crash against the steep cliff.

In his sight, the cliff stood unmoving, yet faintly, grains of sand could be seen falling from the black rock face.

As the tide receded, those black grains spread across the beach.

The man's expression was serious, his eyes filled with obvious shock.

He had previously thought that the large number of Soul Stones on the beach had been swept in by the Yellow Spring Sea. But a discovery a few days ago had overturned that guess.

Now, upon careful observation, it was indeed different!

"Could it be... that all the Soul Stones originate from this single cliff face?"

Luo Chen couldn't help but look up at the black rock wall. Though it showed no signs of life, it gave him a chilling sensation.

If this were true, didn't it mean that this entire cliff was one giant Soul Stone?

A fist-sized Soul Stone had once made the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining unable to forget it.

If this Severed Soul Cliff, thousands of feet high, was a single Soul Stone, what kind of fortune would that be?

After refining and absorbing it, how far would his soul grow?

A great fortune lay before him, yet Luo Chen dared not feel the slightest greed—only great terror.

Suppressing his covetous thoughts, he trudged alone back to his cave dwelling.

Casually placing the Soul Stones he had collected that night into a jade tray, Luo Chen first sat in meditation to regulate his breathing.

When his mind was calm, he examined himself.

In his sea of consciousness, six figures sat in a circle, surrounding a tall figure at the center.

Now, the *Soul-Splitting Art* had reached the level of six split souls!

And unlike before, each of these split souls was no weaker than Luo Chen's main soul when he had first arrived at Severed Soul Cliff.

That was terrifying.

Luo Chen's main soul back then had already been comparable to a normal late-stage Sage Grand Cultivator.

Now, with six split souls all at that level, it was hard to imagine how strong his main soul had become.

"No wonder I've been cultivating persistently for five years!"

Luo Chen sighed. His tone carried little self-satisfaction, instead tinged with melancholy and desolation.

Because even having reached this point, he still had no confidence to explore the Yellow Spring Sea or leave this place.

"Could it be that I truly need to condense a Primordial Spirit, with a soul comparable to an Ascendant great power, before I can leave here?"

He sighed again.

Luo Chen shook his head, dispelling the oppressive emotions in his heart. Years of bitter cultivation had kept him from going mad from loneliness and isolation in a short time.

"Tonight, it's time to split the seventh soul."

Luo Chen's gaze fell on the tray of collected Soul Stones. He skillfully began to circulate the *Soul-Splitting Art*.

His body trembled slightly, cold sweat beading on his forehead.

No matter how many times he split his main soul, the pain could never be gotten used to.

All he could do was endure!

When the morning sun rose and the evening sun set, when another lonely day passed and silence returned...

Luo Chen's eyes snapped open. He gasped for breath.

The Nirvana Sacred Flame appeared once more, burning the Soul Stones and releasing green smoke.

Luo Chen devoured it greedily, and only then did the weakness inside him gradually fade.

And in his sea of consciousness, another illusory figure had appeared.

The green smoke drifted, splitting in two. The thicker part entered the main soul, while the thinner part was absorbed by the newborn illusory split soul.

After absorbing the green smoke, both figures stabilized.

In just five years, Luo Chen had split seven souls—all thanks to the Soul Stones!

No elixir or miraculous medicine in the world could compare to even a fraction of a Soul Stone.

This pure soul essence allowed Luo Chen to recover quickly and then grow rapidly.

"Once the seventh soul has grown, it will be time to split the eighth and ninth souls."

"At that time, with nine souls united and re-entering the Sage realm, can I achieve a Primordial Spirit?"

Luo Chen didn't know how other Sages gradually transformed their Sage Nascent Souls into Primordial Spirits. He only knew that he was taking great strides, wanting to achieve it in one fell swoop!

And he firmly believed that the method to leave Severed Soul Cliff lay within this very process!

(Chapter End)
