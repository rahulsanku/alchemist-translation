# Chapter 537: True Flame Pill at Perfected Completion, a Moluo Stream Expert Appears

The reason Han Zhan had been forced to awaken from his recuperation was not merely that his Yin Pole Puppet could no longer hold on.

Another important reason was that Luo Chen's sacrificial refinement of the Primordial Unity Cauldron had deepened over these years.

From the initial tempering of impurities, to gradually controlling the formations upon it, and even delving into the depths of the magic treasure.

As a result, the spatial restriction Azure Frost had set down back then was also affected.

Repeated fluctuations completely deprived Han Zhan of a quiet environment in which to recuperate.

For him, it was a bad thing.

For Luo Chen, however, it was a thoroughly good thing.

This represented that Luo Chen was already not far from perfectly controlling the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Luo Chen also took the chance to ask Han Zhan about the backup measures Heaven Smelter had left on the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Regarding this, Han Zhan's answer somewhat surprised Luo Chen.

"They were merely temporary methods. There is no need to worry too much."

Amid his astonishment, Luo Chen thought it over carefully and gradually understood.

Heaven Smelter's artifact-refining methods were indeed superb.

But people like Azure Frost and Nether Spring were not fools either.

If overly obvious countermeasures had been left on the Primordial Unity Cauldron, they would certainly have seen through them.

Only those temporary methods that were not too obvious and whose power was relatively weak had some small possibility of deceiving the heavens and crossing the sea.

This also explained why Heaven Smelter, after seizing the Primordial Unity Cauldron in the dungeon of Cangwu Mountain's Truth Crossing Hall, had been unable to freely drive it in his battle against the Thunder Prison Divine Peng.

He had merely left backup measures in a few key places.

And these backup measures had directly dissipated with Heaven Smelter's fall.

"No wonder my sacrificial refinement has become smoother and more obedient these past few years!"

After his realization, Luo Chen could not help laughing self-mockingly.

He had truly been bitten once by a snake and feared the well rope for ten years!

In his leisure time, he also asked Han Zhan about some past matters from Cangwu Mountain, such as how he and Heaven Smelter had conspired to break open the dungeon restrictions.

Han Zhan told him everything he knew, laying it all out one by one.

"Those demon cultivators knew the facts but not the reasons. Even if that Demon Emperor Azure Luan, relying on racial talent, touched the power of spatial laws at the Sage stage, using it to imprison dead objects was still far too crude."

"Heaven Smelter was a famous formation grandmaster. Apart from the dao of puppets, I also cultivated formations concurrently. Otherwise, I would not have been able to control the Yin-Yang Five Elements Sword Formation."

"Relying on spatial restriction methods within formation fundamentals, and after testing and studying them many times, we slowly glimpsed the flaws within..."

"If one really speaks of it, the one who benefited in the end was still you!"

How had Luo Chen benefited?

Naturally, through his lifebound magic treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron!

After Heaven Smelter and Han Zhan achieved some small success in their study of spatial restrictions, they asked Azure Frost to personally act and perfect Luo Chen's lifebound magic treasure in order to verify their ideas.

After the verification was complete, Luo Chen's magic treasure gained the might to cross space, seize, and suppress.

It even naturally developed a mustard-seed space within it, capable of storing living creatures and absolutely not inferior to those finely made spirit beast bags.

Toward this, Han Zhan was also rather envious.

He said bluntly that if Luo Chen carefully nurtured and refined it, this Primordial Unity Cauldron would sooner or later advance into the ranks of true artifacts.

When they spoke of true artifacts, Luo Chen asked a few more questions about how such weapons differed from magic treasures.

Han Zhan's answer was that true artifacts were the artifacts of Perfected Beings. They fused with the mind and spirit of Sage cultivators and possessed an extremely strong amplifying effect when domains unfolded.

The so-called Sage domain was to absorb and control spirit qi of a certain attribute corresponding to the cultivator within heaven and earth.

But a cultivator's strength was limited. In reality, they could not control much. With a true artifact, this effect could be expanded.

Han Zhan himself had formed his Nascent Soul for a hundred years and still did not have a true artifact.

Luo Chen found that rather strange. Back then, in White Night's cave abode on Mount Accumulated Thunder, had there not been rumors that several true artifacts had appeared?

"Those were all relics of mighty figures from the Nether Origin Sect. How could they be things we could touch? After Nether Origin Sect experts arrived, they took all of them back. Otherwise, if this old man had possessed a true artifact, how would I have been so passive when fighting Wolf Emperor Aoxiao?"

Through this matter, Luo Chen also learned a piece of common knowledge.

That was, although it was difficult for the demon race to assume human form, once they did so, they could easily use certain parts of their own bodies, or companion objects, to refine lifebound true artifacts belonging solely to themselves.

This was also why, under the same realm, low-level demon beasts were completely no match for human cultivators, but after reaching high realms, there was almost no difference between the two. One could even say that because of certain special racial talents, high-level demon beasts could press human cultivators down and beat them.

Just like Aoxiao against Han Zhan, and just like Azure Frost suppressing three late-stage Sage great cultivators by herself.

The days slowly passed within this watery rhythm of life.

Luo Chen did not hurry to demand the things he wanted from Han Zhan.

First, his current focus was on refining large batches of True Flame Pills.

Second, Han Zhan was also anxious to study the way for himself and the Soul-Refining Banner to coexist, wanting to have a safe place of rest.

However, even if Luo Chen did not actively ask, Han Zhan, who was well versed in human relations, naturally would not be stingy.

Once, when Luo Chen was gulping down blood food, Han Zhan voluntarily handed over the Black Emperor Paste formula that Luo Chen had yearned for over many years.

In his words, this was the first step in sincere cooperation between the two of them.

Luo Chen gave him the Soul-Refining Banner, and he returned the Black Emperor Paste formula as a gift.

With a good beginning, perhaps they could get along a little better over the next several hundred years.

On this day.

Inside the pill chamber.

Luo Chen focused intently on the hazy gray great cauldron while azure flames continuously roasted it.

Under his control, the medicinal liquid inside the cauldron flowed ceaselessly like magma.

This was already his fifth year in the Northern Sea!

For the third-tier pill True Flame Pill, under several years of refinement, his proficiency had grown higher and higher, long since reaching grandmaster level.

Not only was the pill success rate controlled above fifty percent, he had also long ceased being limited to one portion of raw materials per furnace.

Now, under ordinary circumstances, he could begin refining ten portions of raw materials in one furnace.

If he went all out, refining one hundred portions in one furnace was not impossible!

It was just that the latter required a more focused state, as well as a longer time, making it very hard to disengage if something happened.

"These problems are actually not problems."

"So long as my True Flame Pill reaches Perfected Completion, they will be easily solved."

"And the day of Perfected Completion is today!"

Luo Chen's expression remained focused, but his manipulation of source true fire became several degrees more delicate.

Speaking of it, refining one hundred portions of raw materials in a single furnace was a feat Luo Chen had never imagined in the past.

Ordinary pill-refining vessels were simply insufficient to support such a refinement method.

Only the Primordial Unity Cauldron could!

Through repeated exploration and testing, Luo Chen had gradually touched the limits of this treasure.

In fact, in his view, this did not even seem to be the Primordial Unity Cauldron's limit.

In Han Zhan's words, "A supreme treasure of the alchemy dao like yours is an existence worthy of being placed inside a top great sect as a sect-suppressing treasure."

What was a sect-suppressing treasure of the alchemy dao?

It meant that if it did not move, so be it, but once activated, the entire sect's strength would be mobilized to refine pills!

Beneath raging flames, countless strange flowers and exotic herbs, hundreds and thousands of medicinal materials, would be thrown inside. Only such a thing could be called a sect-suppressing treasure of the alchemy dao!

Luo Chen also had plans.

Once he pushed his True Flame Pill proficiency to Perfected Completion, he would exert his full strength and attempt a hundred-pills simultaneous refinement!

It would also let him see whether he possessed the breadth of an alchemy grandmaster!

Just as his mind was focused, he suddenly frowned.

The motions of his hands did not stop. He turned his head slightly, and a voice transmission passed through the pill chamber's isolating formation to a side hall on Invitation Moon Island shrouded in black mist.

"Senior Han, a guest has arrived. Please receive him for me."

Inside the side hall, the black mist quaked.

Vaguely, one could see over a hundred powerful Ghost Generals, and even a Ghost King, all bowing their heads and kneeling before a three-inch illusory little figure.

Han Zhan quietly spread his divine sense and also discovered the newcomer.

First, he praised Luo Chen's caution, then said, "I can barely control this Soul-Refining Banner now, but the newcomer is at Core Formation Stage Six and is rather strong. Relying only on this third-tier Ghost King, I fear he cannot be stopped."

From afar, Luo Chen's voice drifted over.

"No matter. Delay him for a while. Wait until I free my hands."

"Mm. As long as you know what you are doing."

Han Zhan smiled slightly and formed a spirit seal with his hand.

Inside the hall, over a hundred second-tier Ghost Generals surged out together.

As for Han Zhan himself, he slowly closed his eyes.

The next moment!

The third-tier Ghost King prostrate on the ground abruptly opened its eyes, brimming with intelligence.

It was clearly the puppet art of the Falling Cloud Sect!

A faintly human expression appeared on the Ghost King's face.

"Let this old man see what the combat methods of Northern Sea cultivators are like."

With a murmur, black mist swept out, and the third-tier Ghost King had already vanished from the side hall.

And among the vast Swallow Islands, a great battle had already erupted without anyone asking for the reason.

...

Time rewound to fifteen minutes earlier.

On the ten-thousand-li ocean,

a Daoist came treading over the waves. As he passed a small island, he suddenly stopped.

"Come out, junior!"

As soon as the words fell, a frightened voice came from the island.

"Senior has arrived, and this lowly one failed to welcome you from afar. I truly deserve death."

A sturdy black-faced man flew out from the island and landed on the sea surface with slight nervousness.

Wu Qi looked at the black-faced man and nodded faintly.

"Foundation Establishment Perfected Completion..."

His gaze also swept across the stretch of reefs around the small island.

"An early third-tier Black-Scale Jiaolong."

"You are Kun Jiao, gang leader of the Swallow Islands' Jiaolong Gang!"

Kun Jiao's heart chilled. His spirit beast hidden beneath the reefs had actually been seen through at a glance.

He hurriedly nodded. "That is indeed this one. Senior has keen eyes."

Wu Qi shook his head and laughed softly. "What keen eyes are there to speak of? You were the one who took the initiative to contact my Moluo Stream and lure me here. If we did not know your background, would that not make my Moluo Stream's intelligence ability seem lacking?"

Hearing the three words "Moluo Stream," a deep look of fear appeared on Kun Jiao's face.

This was one of the top great powers in the northern human territory of the Demon Sea!

Within it, Foundation Establishment cultivators were countless, Core Formation Seniors were numerous, and even several Sage Perfected Beings, dragons whose heads were seen but not their tails, existed.

Not only that, under the great momentum of the Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance sweeping across the Northern Sea and seeking to become the new Holy Land, Moluo Stream was one of the few regional powers that dared contend with it.

He had drawn an expert of this faction here, and he did not know whether it was right or wrong.

"That Yan Nantian of Laboring Swallow Island really could run. To think he ran to such a remote corner." Wu Qi looked into the distance, at that stretch of islands shaped vaguely like a sea swallow. "Is he still hiding there?"

Kun Jiao awkwardly said, "Yan Nantian is already dead."

"Mm?"

Wu Qi frowned. He had not expected this situation.

It was rare for him to take on a Moluo Stream mission, yet before he even acted, the mission target was already dead.

This was truly somewhat unexpected.

He looked Kun Jiao up and down twice, then glanced at the trembling Black-Scale Jiaolong beneath the reefs of the nearby island and revealed a disdainful expression.

"With the strength of you and that third-tier Black-Scale Jiaolong, I fear you could not kill Yan Nantian. Speak. How did he die?"

Kun Jiao's spirits rose. He immediately narrated Yan Nantian's battle with Luo Chen in detail.

"Demon Lord Qingyang?"

As he muttered to himself, Wu Qi's face revealed a half-believing, half-doubting expression.

Before the Primordial Demon Sect was destroyed, there had indeed been some unworthy Foundation Establishment disciples who liked artificially creating Qingyang Demon Flame.

However, this kind of demon flame had huge flaws.

Few Primordial Demon Sect disciples treated it as a main method, and fewer still cultivated it to the Core Formation stage.

Now that a Core Formation cultivator skilled in using Qingyang Demon Flame had appeared in the Swallow Islands, if one said he was a Primordial Demon Sect expert, Wu Qi truly did not quite believe it.

The Primordial Demon Sect's high-level cultivators were not so shortsighted!

Could it be an impostor?

While he was puzzled, Kun Jiao steadied his mind and embellished as he recounted the events that had happened in the Swallow Islands over the past few years.

"Occupying the third-tier spirit vein on Invitation Moon Island. That is not strange."

"Taking out a middle-grade Core Formation secret art... He has some foundation. Even my Wu clan has only one low-grade Core Formation secret art. Such generosity does have a bit of the bearing of a true disciple of a great sect."

"Disregarding everything and enslaving the great clans to collect medicinal materials for him, tormenting the Swallow Islands into exhaustion in a short four or five years. Heh, this really does have some style of a demonic sect expert."

"Wait. A third-tier alchemist?"

"Early Core Formation realm?"

Wu Qi pursed his lips, his eyes continuously turning. An eager intent surged in his heart.

Kun Jiao hurriedly confirmed these two pieces of information.

"He is indeed early Core Formation. Also, the medicine masters and alchemists sent over by the various great clans have all confirmed that Demon Lord Qingyang can indeed refine third-tier pills. Moreover, he can also casually produce some low-tier pills with extremely high grades!"

After saying this, he looked nervously at Wu Qi.

He very much wanted the other party to act and kill that Demon Lord Qingyang!

If Qingyang were killed, Wu Qi would certainly not care about a small place like the Swallow Islands.

In that case, he could return to the Swallow Islands, rely on his Foundation Establishment Perfected Completion realm and an early third-tier Black-Scale Jiaolong to occupy the Swallow Islands and become the lord of Swallow!

As a gang leader, he had spent these years fleeing outside in sorry straits.

He had finally waited until Yan Nantian died, only to have to fear a new Demon Lord.

He, Kun Jiao, had truly had enough!

Wu Qi of Moluo Stream knew his thoughts perfectly well.

Being used by a lower-realm cultivator would normally displease him. He might even turn around and kill the other party to make an example of him.

But an early Core Formation alchemist... no, the other party very clearly could refine multiple types of pills, and his attainments were extremely high. He could already be called a master-level existence of his generation.

If such an existence were controlled in his hands,

then his Wu clan would surely rise.

And he, Wu Qi, could also occupy a place in Moluo Stream and become part of the first rank below the Perfected Beings!

This job, he would do it!

Glancing at Kun Jiao, who was staring at him eagerly, Wu Qi coldly said, "Follow me over. When the time comes, I will act against Demon Lord Qingyang. You help me suppress the others. Do not let those ants disturb me."

Kun Jiao was stunned. He had to act too?

But when he thought that after success he could monopolize the Swallow Islands, that slight hesitation was thrown to the back of his mind.

"I respectfully obey Senior's command!"

"Come!"

Wu Qi's expression turned solemn. With a sweep of his great sleeve, a towering wave surged up from the blue sea, carrying his body forward in a rolling rush.

Where he passed, the waves towered into the sky and water exploded everywhere.

From afar, it looked as if a tsunami capable of swallowing everything was taking shape, pressing toward the Swallow Islands in a heaven-covering, earth-shrouding tide.

(End of chapter)
