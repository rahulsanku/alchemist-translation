# Chapter 1079: A Just Cause Gains Much Support, The Battle of Heaven and Earth

"Luo Chen…"

"Master…"

"Father King…"

Within the Immortal City of Zhenhai, on the Tiannan Continent.

In a silent room, murmuring voices could be heard from time to time.

The voice muttered, sometimes filled with longing, sometimes brimming with resentment.

Looking closely, the one making the sounds was a middle-aged man.

His features were strong, his countenance dignified, as if born with immense nobility.

A golden robe further accentuated his kingly aura.

He sat there, no one knew for how long, or perhaps only a short time. He sat as steady as a mountain, without the slightest restlessness, or perhaps he had long grown accustomed to such days.

In his hand, he rubbed an ornate and grand crown, a look of deep attachment in his eyes.

This crown was called the Pingshan Crown!

It had been worn by his father king in life. After his father king fell, it passed through many hands before finally ending up in the possession of his master, Luo Chen.

And now, his master had bestowed the Pingshan Crown upon him.

In a sense, it was also returning the crown to its rightful owner.

Suddenly!

The golden-robed man seemed to sense something and slowly raised his head.

He looked outside the room, then silently and solemnly placed the crown upon his head, before rising to his full height.

Not far away, inside a great hall.

*Hum!*

A dull humming sound arose, and the cultivators nearby all turned their heads.

When eight figures successively appeared on the teleportation platform, the group of cultivators guarding the teleportation hall erupted in commotion.

"That's Fairy Qinglan!"

The first to be recognized was Fu Qinglan, who had once visited Zhenhai Immortal City.

Following closely behind, most of the others were also identified.

"The Tiannan Dao Palace, the Abbot of Heavenly Origin Temple!"

"Palace Master of Ziwei Hall!"

"Palace Master of Changsheng Hall!"

"Palace Master of Zhongxing Hall!"

"And Diviner Bu…"

Faced with the shock of the cultivators below, Zhan Bingtao swept his gaze over them, and instantly the entire hall fell silent.

Everyone dared not even breathe loudly, not understanding why this group of people, who held the pinnacle of power and strength on the Tiannan Continent, had all come here.

The five Sages of the Dao Palace, plus the three Nascent Soul cultivators, Diviner Bu, Sage Hu, and Sage Zhai.

What kind of situation warranted mobilizing so many experts?

*Whoosh!*

A gust of wind stirred, and a figure entered the hall.

"Have all of you arrived?"

Fairy Qinglan looked at the newcomer; it was Sage Wen, who guarded Zhenhai City.

"Almost everyone is here. There are a few more, but the teleportation arrays on the Tiannan Continent can currently only support the simultaneous teleportation of eight or nine of us. What about your side?"

Sage Wen said seriously, "I have been keeping a close watch on the Dongyuan Continent. It is indeed engulfed in fierce battle. A large number of Nascent Soul cultivators from other continents have invaded. If not for the Abbot's orders, I would have gone to support them long ago. I just don't know where those people came from."

Zhan Bingtao laughed angrily, "Just clowns from the Eastern Wilderness! Once we go to support, we will surely bury them all. Is the temporary teleportation array you were told to prepare ready?"

From the Eastern Wilderness?

Sage Wen swallowed his saliva. According to legend, the Rosycloud Lord had originally come from Green Parasol Mountain in the Eastern Wilderness.

He dared not think back to that devastating scene from back then.

Steadying his mind, Sage Wen quickly replied, "It has been set up. By gathering the power of the Ninefold Zhenhai Locks, it can help us instantly cross the ocean and teleport to the Dongyuan Continent."

"Then let's begin!"

"You just teleported from the Dao Palace, don't you need to adjust your breathing first?"

"Will the enemy give us time to adjust our breathing?" Zhan Bingtao retorted, his face even angrier. "Activate the cross-sea teleportation array immediately!"

Sage Wen quickly nodded, then dispersed the crowd of low-level cultivators, took out a compass, and placed it on the original teleportation array.

As the compass began to operate, a suction force emerged.

In the distance, the location of the Ninefold Zhenhai Locks trembled in unison. Strands of accumulated power were drawn out, beginning to converge towards the teleportation hall.

Zhan Bingtao and the other eight suppressed their impatient feelings, silently waiting for Sage Wen to act.

Ever since the collapse of the Central Continent back then, most of the teleportation arrays that had been spread across it were destroyed. Although teleportation points were rebuilt in various places afterwards, there were only a few that could truly support ultra-long-distance teleportation.

And the location of Zhenhai City was the closest teleportation point to the Dongyuan Continent.

Amidst this unspeakable waiting, the atmosphere gradually grew tense.

Suddenly!

Zhan Bingtao raised his head sharply and bellowed.

"Enemy attack!"

Without waiting for the others to react, he shot into the sky, his fierce battle intent exploding forth.

However!

*Clang!*

A dazzling, heaven-shaking blade of light descended from the sky, directly cutting Zhan Bingtao back to his original spot.

The powerful residual wave destroyed the teleportation hall in an instant.

Sage Wen, who had been setting up the teleportation array and channeling the power of the Ninefold Locks, was the first to suffer the backlash. He cried out in pain and fell to a distant spot.

The others ignored him for a moment, first looking at Zhan Bingtao who had been cut back.

"I am unharmed!"

From the dust and smoke came Zhan Bingtao's vigorous voice.

Then, everyone looked up at the enemy.

Through the broken dome of the hall, a golden figure was reflected. He wore an imperial crown, held a golden long saber, and was filled with an unmatched, sharp aura.

It was as if he were the embodiment of sharpness, the existence that could cut through everything.

"Nascent Soul Peak!"

"Gold Origin!"

"Not a human, a demon beast!"

"Such a powerful existence, why have I never heard of it?"

Although the newcomer was strong, there was only one of him.

Therefore, the Tiannan Dao Palace side was not particularly afraid.

Fu Qinglan swept her divine sense over the opponent, and a deep look of confusion appeared in her beautiful eyes.

"This demon's aura is so complex, and it feels too familiar to me… it's Master's aura. Why does it have Master's aura, and also another aura…"

From the dust and smoke, Zhan Bingtao walked out step by step, holding a long halberd.

"Who are you? Why are you attacking us? Don't you know that we are cultivators of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect?"

Faced with all these questions, the golden-robed man slowly raised his long saber.

"I am Di Tu, under the command of Dan Zong Luo Chen. None of you shall go to the Dongyuan Continent today!"

Fu Qinglan's pupils contracted sharply, and she subconsciously looked at Zhan Bingtao.

He took a deep breath. "No need for words. Fight!"

Fu Qinglan bit her lip, sword in hand, and charged into the sky with Zhan Bingtao.

Facing the siege of eight people, Di Tu's expression did not change.

"He said that if I complete this task perfectly, he will grant me freedom. My little sister is still waiting for me, my people are also waiting for me. You shall not cross this line today!"

Murmuring to himself, Di Tu held his long saber and slashed down towards a distant spot.

This slash was not aimed at any of the eight, but at Sage Wen, who was adjusting his breathing and unable to react in time.

Facing the siege of eight, Di Tu was so unexpected, killing the unprepared Sage Wen with a single slash. This scene truly stunned everyone.

Then came gnashing of teeth and extreme fury.

With Sage Wen dead, who among those present could quickly repair the teleportation array?

Zhan Bingtao roared, "Kill him!"

Di Tu silently raised his saber.

"Come!"

...

At another location on the Dongyuan Continent, facing the Libei Continent.

A bird was circling in the sky.

Suddenly, the bird's body stiffened, and a look of terror appeared in its eyes.

Fifteen streaks of light flew out from the Libei Continent, heading towards the Dongyuan direction.

Among them, three revealed incredibly powerful auras, clearly being great cultivators of the late Nascent Soul stage!

"Just as Master predicted. Once Dongyuan is attacked, the Libei Continent will inevitably send strong reinforcements."

"The North Sea Demon Race didn't fully delay the Libei cultivators."

"No, I must go and inform the others!"

Bai Ou fluttered her wings, intending to return.

However, halfway there, she saw two figures approaching her, one in front and one behind.

"Daoist Tianxuan, are the reinforcements from the Dao Sect here?"

Tianxuan transformed her form, revealing her tall figure, and looked at the newcomers with surprise and joy.

"Sage Taiyue!"

Then, she glanced warily at the one behind. "They have indeed arrived, and in great numbers, a full fifteen people."

Deng Taiyue was thoughtful. "Fifteen people?"

Behind him, Shi Gandang said in a muffled voice, "Master, the enemy's numbers are a bit high."

Deng Taiyue shook his head. "Although it's a bit more than expected, since I promised Luo Chen, I can't just turn around and leave."

He raised his hand, and Shi Gandang immediately understood what he meant.

A yellow earthy light transformed, like mud, covering Deng Taiyue's body, forming an incredibly hard suit of armor.

Not only that, but it also condensed a broad, heavy sword.

Seeing this, Tianxuan was stunned for a moment.

That Shi Gandang was actually a rock that had become a spirit?

At this moment, Deng Taiyue's late Nascent Soul cultivation was fully revealed, and the armor and sword in his hand moved as he willed.

However, Tianxuan still said worriedly, "Among those fifteen, there are three great cultivators of the same level as you!"

Deng Taiyue smiled slightly. "Although I am not as formidable as Daoist Luo Chen, who single-handedly fought the thirty-six eminent monks of the Hanging Temple, temporarily blocking a dozen or so people should not be a problem. Besides, I am not fighting alone."

"Shi Gandang?" Tianxuan asked doubtfully.

But Deng Taiyue shook his head, his gaze fixed on a spot not far away.

On the still relatively calm sea surface, turbid waves suddenly rolled.

A figure walked out from the sea. He had a bald head, a white beard, and a prayer bead necklace around his neck.

He put his palms together and nodded at Deng Taiyue.

"Kongchan has arrived as promised. I hope I am not too late."

Deng Taiyue nodded back in acknowledgment.

One man and one demon. Killing fifteen Dao Sect reinforcements might be beyond their power, but temporarily blocking them should be enough.

"Daoist Tianxuan, go and deliver the message. Leave this place to us."

Tianxuan was still a bit worried, but with Luo Chen's orders ahead of her, she dared not delay any further and hurriedly left.

After she left, Deng Taiyue and Kongchan stood still, waiting for the enemy's arrival.

Before the great battle, there was still a moment of leisure.

The two chatted casually.

"Daoist, you come from the North Sea? A demon cultivator at the ninth level of Nascent Soul is truly rare!"

"Although Sage Taiyue's realm is slightly inferior, this mana imbued with the Earth Origin is truly enviable."

"What do you think of this upcoming battle?"

"What else can I think? This humble monk has no choice but to grit his teeth and go through with it. But how dare Sage Taiyue stand alone as a gatekeeper? What if I had broken my promise? What would you have done then?"

"Haha, I made a promise first. Even if it's a mountain of blades or a sea of fire, I have to walk this path!"

As the two were conversing, the fifteen streaks of light gradually came into view.

On the vast sea, two figures stood in the path of the fifteen.

Without a word, the great battle was about to begin!

Just then, from the nine heavens above, a streak of blood-red light suddenly descended from the sky, crashing heavily into the midst of the fifteen.

Amidst a chorus of exclamations, the blood light suddenly spread, transforming into a sea of blood. Black demonic energy coalesced into thousands of figures.

Kongchan's pupils contracted, and he exclaimed, "It's him!"

Deng Taiyue frowned. "Who is that person?"

Kongchan hesitated, unsure how to introduce him.

Was it the Mad Wanderer? Or Layman Fengling? Or a demon?

In the end, he only introduced him as, "A friend of Luo Chen's."

Deng Taiyue was enlightened, then laughed heartily. "As they say, a just cause gains much support, an unjust one gains little. With so many friends helping Luo Chen, how can his great undertaking fail? Let's go too. We can't let this Daoist friend fight alone!"

"Indeed!"

...

On the Dongyuan Continent, the war was raging like a wildfire.

At the two unnoticed locations of Tiannan and Libei, there were also perilous interception battles.

But the battlefield that truly determined the outcome was only one!

That was the Heaven-Earth Island!

Especially after Luo Chen saw through Lian Cheng's true plan, he understood that whether the Central Continent was reunited or the formations in various places were destroyed, none of it mattered.

Those were all smokescreens on the surface, things done as a matter of convenience.

The core of the Heaven's Dome Land-Gathering Formation was within the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, on the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard controlled by Chess Saint Ke Lianshan. Because of the separation of the two realms, outsiders could not interfere with Ke Lianshan's actions at all.

And the core of the God-Descending Formation was on Heaven-Earth Peak, in Lian Cheng's hands.

What Luo Chen had to do first was to stop the God-Descending Formation from proceeding, to stop that terrifyingly murderous True Lord from the upper realm from descending into the Mountains and Seas Realm!

He did not blame Li Canghai for fleeing before the battle.

Everyone had their own philosophy of life. Perhaps Li Canghai's ability to cultivate to the Ascendant realm relied on this cautiousness of seeing the situation turn bad and immediately retreating.

But Luo Chen could not!

One could be cautious in ordinary times, but at a critical moment, one must have the courage to take a gamble.

"Luo Chen, go!"

Li Canghai's final reminder still echoed in his ears.

Luo Chen pressed forward undauntedly.

"I can't leave!"

Wreathed in terrifying blue-white flames, Luo Chen followed the gap in the Severing Heaven-Earth Passage Grand Formation that Li Canghai had previously broken open, and charged into Heaven-Earth Peak.

The moment he entered, he felt his breath catch.

The feeling of being closely connected to heaven and earth, which he had possessed ever since comprehending the true meaning of the laws, was stripped away at this moment.

He could not even control or manipulate the origin qi of heaven and earth.

"I must destroy this formation!"

Luo Chen took a deep breath, then opened his mouth and spat.

The Withering-Bloom True Flame took root on the ground.

A tree of fire sprouted, took root, and in an instant grew into a towering giant tree.

The once weak Withering-Bloom Fire, after centuries of Luo Chen's painstaking nurturing and refinement, had finally grown into a towering tree.

At this moment, countless branches, like coiled dragons, climbed and spread along the yellow light barrier.

He was going to burn the Severing Heaven-Earth Passage Grand Formation to the ground!

Not enough!

Still not enough!

The God-Descending Formation was still being maintained. Lian Cheng's figure was faintly visible within it. The cloud giant enveloped and protected him, like a moat that was difficult to cross even a single step.

Luo Chen looked up at the mountain peak, at the pillar of light connecting to the cosmic starry sea.

He took a step, and began to run.

The Severing Heaven-Earth Passage Grand Formation had not yet been completely incinerated. Being within Heaven-Earth Peak, he could not control the origin qi of heaven and earth.

But this formation had been damaged by the Rosycloud Lord before, and its power was incomplete.

It could once suppress all living beings within it, but now it was difficult to achieve its full effect.

And he, Luo Chen, aside from his abundant mana and ability to control the origin qi of heaven and earth, also possessed a physical body unparalleled in the world!

As Luo Chen ran, his body began to swell and grow.

By the time he climbed to the mountain peak, he was nearly a thousand feet tall.

Wielding his massive hand, the giant punched towards the God-Descending Formation.

At the mountain peak.

Lian Cheng watched this scene, his previously contemptuous expression long gone.

He could not move easily at the moment, but to be safe, he had already made preparations.

The surrounding clouds surged, and the pure white cloud giant slapped down with its palm.

At this moment, the collision of fist and palm became the only sight between heaven and earth.

*Boom!*

A thunderous sound echoed in all directions.

The vast residual waves of the collision shook the entire island, stirred the boundless sea of clouds, and even the starlight of the universe seemed to dim.

At the foot of the mountain.

The giant, smashed into the dust, knelt on one knee, and raised his head once more.

Within his red eyes and golden pupils, a ferocious gaze was fixed firmly on the mountain top.

"ROAR!!!"

A roar like a wild beast burst from his mouth, and the giant began to leap and run once more.

Not only that, but his body also began to undergo strange changes.

Black armor flowed like mercury, covering his entire body.

His original two hands became six arms.

Two additional heads grew from his shoulders.

Watching this, Lian Cheng's heart trembled slightly.

"Three heads and six arms, a Buddhist divine ability!"

"Since you seek death, then I shall grant it to you!"

"Go!"

With his light shout, the massive giant that had been formed earlier began to struggle within the sea of clouds. Its body grew larger, its hands grew longer, and then, pushing off the sea of clouds, it grew legs and feet.

With a sudden leap, the cloud giant jumped out of the sea of clouds.

Then, descending from the sky, it clenched its fist and smashed it down towards the black-armored giant.

In an instant, the two giants collided like falling stars striking the continent.
