# Chapter 904: Refining the Spark, Smoke and Clouds Turn to Rain

The moment the token entered his hand, a surge of intent burst forth, attempting to rush into Luo Chen's sea of consciousness.

Luo Chen's mind turned, and he instinctively unfolded the Nether Prison Soul Art, blocking that surge of intent. But upon a brief perception, he sensed that this intent, containing vast information, bore no malice, so he released his blockade.

The intent entered his sea of consciousness and dissipated in an instant, leaving behind only that immense information.

Luo Chen rapidly digested it.

On the other side, Master Xuanyan naturally detected this scene and could not help but sigh inwardly.

"This Luo Chen's soul foundation is incredibly profound!"

That surge of intent had been left behind by Chu Yan.

Although Chu Yan had not yet reached the Ascendant stage when she left the Nether Origin back then, she was still a genuine late-stage Sage grand cultivator. A strand of divine-sense intent left by her, taken off guard, would be difficult for ordinary Sages to withstand. That Luo Chen had inadvertently displayed a soul foundation comparable to a grand cultivator was truly astonishing.

When Luo Chen opened his eyes, Master Xuanyan took the initiative to ask, "How is it?"

Savoring that information, Luo Chen began to slowly form hand seals with his fingers, wave after wave of vast mana surging forth from his meridians. The burden of this was so great that even Luo Chen found it somewhat difficult to bear.

The barrier of his Purple Mansion had long since slowly opened, and the little Sage infant opened its mouth, inhaling and exhaling vast amounts of pure mana. With this motion, the very heavens and earth seemed to be pried open. Furthermore, some inexplicable existence was attracted by it.

Luo Chen said with difficulty, "Is it like this?"

Master Xuanyan stared blankly at this scene, finding it hard to believe. He had imagined that Luo Chen might spend a certain amount of time learning that alchemy art and unlocking the token. But he had never imagined that Luo Chen could grasp it instantly, even executing it with proper form. What kind of talent was this, what kind of brilliance!

After a long while, Master Xuanyan said heavily, "It is exactly so!"

As if receiving approval, Luo Chen suddenly relaxed, withdrew his mana, and felt an immense relief. He looked down at the token in his hand, his face full of amazement.

The Alchemy Saint had left an alchemy art on the surface of the token.

Yes, this alchemy art was the key to unlocking the token.

Only by learning this alchemy art and successfully executing it could one unlock the token.

It sounded simple, something any alchemist could get started with. But in reality, the difficulty was something only a grandmaster like Master Xuanyan would know. This was because this alchemy art, called *Refining the Heavenly Spark*, could only be learned by alchemists who had cultivated the true intent of the laws. Even alchemists who possessed the true intent of the laws might not be able to learn it as quickly as Luo Chen, who had grasped it almost instantly.

The secret lay in another unique experience of Luo Chen's. Let us not dwell on that for now. Luo Chen looked at Master Xuanyan, his face showing sympathy.

"From the looks of it, sir, you must have forcibly executed this art, which is why you have ended up in your current state, correct?"

Master Xuanyan looked across the stream and nodded dejectedly.

"Yes, I was already in a state of returning radiance. With my understanding of the alchemy dao, I learned this art. But when I tried to execute it, even disregarding the danger of being assimilated by heaven and earth, I still could not pry open the origin qi of heaven and earth, let alone find that faint, elusive spark."

The alchemy art *Refining the Heavenly Spark* had only one use.

That was to pry open the origin qi of heaven and earth, capture the spark, and integrate it into the pill. In this way, the pill would possess spirit, and its grade would naturally be one level higher than ordinary pills. This also explained why the first- to fourth-grade pills in the Alchemy Saint Hall trial were so full of spirit—because, under Chu Yan's methods, they had all been imbued with an imperceptible trace of the spark.

But to execute this art normally, only those who had comprehended the true intent of the laws could do so without a threshold.

Thus, this was why Master Xuanyan had said earlier that only Luo Chen could unlock this token. Because Luo Chen was a special existence who had comprehended the true intent of the laws ahead of time, at the Sage stage! Moreover, he was also an alchemy grandmaster!

Nevertheless, Master Xuanyan still did not understand.

"Have you studied a similar alchemy art before? How could you grasp it instantly, without even a learning process?"

"Perhaps this art is fated for me! I once inadvertently performed a similar act." Luo Chen said with a slight smile, but then exclaimed in admiration, "But at that time, I only knew the how, not the why. I could not compare to the Alchemy Saint, who understood the principle and even created a specific alchemy art from it."

Master Xuanyan was stunned. An inadvertent similar act in the past?

Was it cultivation?

Or alchemy?

If it was the former, it was just like many cultivation geniuses under heaven who, when comprehending the true intent of the laws, stumbled upon it by chance. He would not care about it at all. But if it was the latter, what kind of pill required Luo Chen to refine the spark into it?

Master Xuanyan was a very straightforward, simple man.

He did not hold back and asked directly.

To others, this might seem very rude, but to Luo Chen, who was a fellow traveler on the same path, he could understand the other's thirst for knowledge.

However, it involved that past event, and Luo Chen fell silent.

Because of this silence, Master Xuanyan knew he had been presumptuous.

He instinctively wanted to apologize, but as the words reached his lips, he suddenly understood. If it were a matter of cultivation, there was nothing that could not be said. Only if it was as he had guessed—that Luo Chen had refined some extraordinary pill—would he be so hesitant.

For a moment, Master Xuanyan's heart began to pound.

"I only want to see it... No! Even a description would suffice."

"I am willing to exchange the *Xuanyan Alchemy Book* I have left behind for your answer!"

Luo Chen waved his hand. "That is too precious. In truth, the favor of gifting me this token is enough to warrant that answer. However..."

Luo Chen looked up at the sky. This blessed land should be sealed, with no one able to spy. After all, the token had not yet been unlocked, meaning the door was still closed. And the old man across from him, his life force was rapidly dissipating, an uncontrollable decline. He probably did not have much time left before he sat in meditation and passed away.

A man about to die...

Luo Chen took a deep breath, retrieved a jade box from his storage pouch, and slowly pushed it toward Master Xuanyan.

The old man took the jade box, held his breath, and carefully opened it.

A brilliant golden light met his eyes.

In an instant, the old man was too excited to contain himself.

"A fifth-grade divine pill!"

He looked up at Luo Chen across from him in disbelief. "You refined this?"

Luo Chen nodded. "Yes, I refined it when I was at the Adept stage. Its name is Law-Derivation Pill. It took three years."

"Impossible, absolutely impossible!" Master Xuanyan cried out as if stimulated, repeatedly shouting impossible. "How could an Adept possibly refine such a pill? Even Chu Yan only managed to refine a fifth-grade divine pill by luck at the Sage stage, earning her the title of Alchemy Saint. How could you possibly..."

Luo Chen did not argue or explain.

He just quietly watched the old man across from him, acting like a child throwing a tantrum.

After a long time, the other party finally calmed down.

On his deathly pale face, an unnatural flush remained from the intense emotions of a moment ago.

"A fifth-grade divine pill, containing blood threads... was a blood-refining method used? No, it seems to be some kind of power similar to mana."

"Its name is Law-Derivation Pill. Could it have an auxiliary effect on cultivation?"

"Just smelling the pill fragrance gives one a feeling of clarity of mind and liveliness of soul. But it contains a vast, even terrifying power. I'm afraid it is not something a Sage cultivator can withstand. You had best not take this pill at this stage. Only after becoming an Ascendant might you be able to endure that terrifying medicinal power."

"It's a pity that my lifespan is nearly exhausted. Otherwise, with the aid of this pill, I might have had a chance to fight for Ascension."

With a series of murmurs, Master Xuanyan reluctantly closed the jade box and returned it to Luo Chen.

Along with it was a thick bamboo slip.

Luo Chen first carefully put away the Law-Derivation Divine Pill, then looked at the bamboo slip floating before him.

"Sir, I am unworthy of such a gift."

Master Xuanyan waved his hand. "It is not the *Xuanyan Alchemy Book*, merely a derivation of an incomplete formula. Take a look and you will understand."

Luo Chen curiously opened the bamboo slip, and a large number of words gave him a familiar feeling.

In an instant, he understood what pill's incomplete formula it was.

*The Awakening Pill!*

For a moment, Luo Chen was overjoyed.

Master Xuanyan seemed lost, still emotionally unsettled by the impact of the Law-Derivation Divine Pill from earlier.

At the final barrier, he had failed due to his limited realm.

Now it seemed that Luo Chen's impending success had its reasons.

It was not just about his cultivation realm, but also his alchemy realm.

This young man was not merely a grandmaster.

He was clearly an alchemy sage on par with Chu Yan!

"Indeed, I guessed you would be interested in this incomplete formula."

"A fellow Daoist entrusted me to refine this pill, but after years of research, I have made little progress, mainly because too much is missing." Luo Chen said while flipping through the bamboo slip, conversing with the other. Suddenly: "Eh? Why are the styles of medicinal ingredients so different, even conflicting?"

Master Xuanyan laughed. "That is because the derivation routes recorded on this bamboo slip are not just mine alone. Back then, Chu Yan also deduced this famous ancient Awakening Pill. Unfortunately, no complete pill has been preserved, otherwise she would surely have been able to reverse-engineer the complete formula."

Luo Chen was surprised. So this bamboo slip was a joint work of the two.

That made sense. He had heard people say that many were interested in the Awakening Pill, and even the Alchemy Saint had once publicly collected incomplete versions of this ancient formula.

"Do not hold out too much hope for this bamboo slip. At most, it has been deduced to about seventy or eighty percent, and it might even be in the wrong direction. It can only serve as a reference for you." Master Xuanyan said thus.

Luo Chen was not at all disappointed. Even as a reference, it was far more convenient than his current blind groping in the dark.

The problem was that the incomplete formulas for the Awakening Pill that Deng Taiyue had given him were a jumbled mess, completely unsystematic. Otherwise, an ordinary fourth-grade formula would have been completed by him long ago. Why had it been delayed for decades with little progress?

Seventy or eighty percent?

As long as one or two routes were correct, Luo Chen was confident he could complete the entire formula!

Luo Chen accepted the bamboo slip and smiled. "Although I feel unworthy, this bamboo slip is indeed of great use to me. I will accept it."

Compared to a lifetime's worth of alchemy dao inheritance, the value of an incomplete fourth-grade pill formula was much lower.

Luo Chen was modest in his words, but he did not feel much psychological burden.

Master Xuanyan did not care about such an incomplete formula either. He simply sat down on the grass.

He was tired.

"Begin!"

"I want to see what Chu Yan has left inside the token. An alchemy art alone would not have caused her to go to such trouble."

"While I still have a breath left."

Luo Chen looked at the weary old man and nodded heavily.

More than four years had passed since he entered the Alchemy Saint Hall, and his son was still waiting for him outside.

Unlocking the Alchemy Saint token as soon as possible and completing the trial was something he was eager to do.

A wind suddenly rose.

Luo Chen's robes fluttered as he slowly floated into the air.

As before, his hands trembled as he methodically formed hand seals, mana surging from his thick meridians toward his fingertips.

The barrier of his Purple Mansion opened again, the Sage infant opened its eyes, inhaling and exhaling mana!

It was still that difficult.

Luo Chen's mind stirred, and a vast source power surged like a tide from his limbs and bones.

Sure enough, with the addition of this source power, executing the *Refining the Heavenly Spark* art became much easier.

"Just as I thought!"

Luo Chen confirmed his conjecture.

Back when he was refining the Law-Derivation Divine Pill in the Pill Realm, he had failed many times.

The last time, he had burned his boats, using source power to mobilize the spiritual energy of heaven and earth, and before exhausting all his strength, he had successfully refined the Law-Derivation Divine Pill.

That alchemy session had been like a dream to him!

Even after succeeding, he did not know why.

His alchemist rank had not even been promoted to fifth-grade because he had refined a fifth-grade divine pill.

Clearly, the system also believed that he knew the how but not the why, and thus judged him to still be a fourth-grade alchemist.

But as Luo Chen progressed step by step, in one cultivation session after another, he had vaguely guessed at the reason for his success back then.

It was only now, upon coming into contact with the *Refining the Heavenly Spark* art, that Luo Chen fully understood!

So, he had once, in the Pill Realm, drawn the spark into his pill!

The desolate beasts were native creatures of the Mountains and Seas Realm, one could call them the darlings of heaven's dao. The source power within desolate beasts, though seemingly on the same level as a cultivator's mana or a body-tempering cultivator's astral qi, was a type of energy more compatible with this realm. It was also easier to attract the spark!

Back then, although Luo Chen's immortal dao realm was Adept, his physical body had long been cultivated to the fourth grade, almost indistinguishable from a desolate beast. By using source power to mobilize the spiritual energy of heaven and earth, he had inadvertently attracted the so-called spark.

At the time, it was unintentional. Later, Luo Chen discovered that it was not a coincidence!

Now, when he activated his source power, forming the image of crimson eyes and golden pupils, he could glimpse the flow of the spark—this was evidence!

But being able to see it was one thing; how to attract and even capture the spark was another.

The alchemy art left by the Alchemy Saint, Chu Yan, was the tool for attracting the spark!

In mid-air.

Luo Chen's robes fluttered, his crimson eyes and golden pupils bright and sharp.

As he formed seals and recited incantations, he searched for the location of the spark.

And this search required no effort at all—it was right there, all around him!

"A coincidence?"

"No!"

"It must be the spark attracted by my earlier cultivation!"

The spark was difficult to find. Even Ascendant great powers could only rely on the vast spiritual energy of a fifth-grade spiritual land to automatically attract the spark to assist their cultivation. It was absolutely not something Luo Chen could easily summon by executing a single alchemy art. Especially since he was not very proficient at it.

But this space was a sealed blessed land.

Before it was opened, the ancestor of the Nether Origin Sect had used means to draw the spark into it.

Furthermore, Luo Chen had once again comprehended the true intent of Withering-Bloom earlier. In that state, the spark had automatically come to him.

If Luo Chen had not been preoccupied with refining the Two Modes Pill, but had instead turned to cultivation, his mana would have greatly increased.

Nevertheless, those sparks had not yet departed.

Since he could see them now, he might as well save some effort and capture them directly!

Luo Chen did not hesitate. With a wave of his hand, those sparse sparks sank into the token.

The next moment!

The white jade token suddenly emitted a hazy glow.

In the sky, a lifelike, indistinct phantom appeared.

The phantom was hazy, but its features were crystal clear.

Eyebrows like a painting, eyes like autumn water.

The bridge of the nose was straight, yet not lacking in softness, as if it were a work of art meticulously carved by the most outstanding sculptor. The lips parted slightly, like cherry blossoms just beginning to bloom. The contours of the cheeks were slightly round, giving a sense of fragility while also possessing a touch of loveliness and liveliness.

A pair of bright eyes stared downward.

She seemed to be looking at Luo Chen.

But upon closer inspection, she was staring at some void.

She casually waved her hand, and in the air appeared a pill cauldron formed purely from condensed mana.

Then, she threw one material after another into it.

At the same time, many other illusions appeared elsewhere.

Just like before, it was also the phantom of this woman, also refining pills.

Only the materials thrown in were bizarre and varied, never the same.

Melting metals and stones into liquid, absorbing grasses and woods into medicine, clouds and smoke swirling, wind and thunder following, and even a small space collapsing and disintegrating under her actions.

By the stream.

Master Xuanyan watched this scene thoughtfully.

Condensing qi into a cauldron, refining the myriad things of heaven and earth into pills—was this what she had left behind?

The law insights of the alchemy dao!

Just by observing from the sidelines, he was already greatly benefited. Luo Chen, who was truly receiving the infusion, must have gained even more.

If Luo Chen could rely on this opportunity to comprehend the true intent of the alchemy dao's laws, he could then use it to advance to Ascendant in the future.

Within the entire Alchemy Saint Blessed Land, a sea of spiritual energy surged toward Luo Chen.

...

Just as the phantom appeared in the blessed land, the vast clouds and mist that had shrouded the Alchemy Saint Hall for hundreds of years began to churn violently.

A large amount of clouds and mist began to dissipate, turning into fine droplets of rain, falling to the earth.

Outside the hall, Chen Mozi, Xinyi Sheng, Lu Chun, and others all sensed something.

Chu Yu seemed to want to speak, her face showing both unwillingness and a hint of relief.

The ancestor's inheritance had been obtained by another—it was her failure, but she no longer had to be shrouded in pressure day and night.

The City Lord of Lingtian hurried over, both surprised and delighted.

"Within the Alchemy Saint Hall, there are still two people. Who has passed all the trials and obtained the Alchemy Saint's inheritance?"

"Luo Chen?"

"Or Master Xuanyan?"

Within the Nether Origin Sect, countless gazes were cast toward the Alchemy Saint Hall.

One transmission after another spread outward from this central point in all directions.

Everyone knew that the position of master of the Nether Origin Sect's Alchemy Saint Hall, vacant for hundreds of years, had finally found its owner.

But who the new master was remained unknown.

Clouds and mist turning to rain—this celestial phenomenon seemed to trigger a chain reaction.

The Nether Origin Holy Land, located in the Eastern Wilderness yet independent of it, was eight or nine times the size of the Jade Cauldron Domain. Under the chain reaction, dark clouds gathered, thunder and lightning flashed.

Drops of rain suddenly fell, startling countless specks of dust.

Only one place was unaffected by this.

Mount Jambu, suspended in the nine heavens!

Within the Heavenly Nether Palace, the three ancestors opened their eyes simultaneously.

Ancestor Imperial Nether sensed slightly and detected the faint traces of aura leaking from the surging clouds and mist.

"It's Luo Chen!"

(The End)
