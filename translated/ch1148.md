# Chapter 1148: Decadent Immortal, Corroding Immortal, Luo Chen Breaks Through

Late at night.

Jing Lan, who had been busy all day managing the opening of the new shop, returned to the residence under the escort of guards.

Upon hearing that the Wuyou Envoy had come calling during the day, she was somewhat surprised, but since Luo Chen hadn't sent anyone to notify her, she assumed it was nothing major.

As she entered the main hall, Jing Lan's steps paused slightly, her gaze falling upon three large chests in the hall.

"What are these?" Jing Lan asked in confusion.

The steward, Uncle Zhong, explained, "Gifts sent by City Lord Xiao. The master hasn't come out of seclusion to inspect them yet, so they haven't been put into storage."

Jing Lan frowned slightly. Having several chests sitting in the main hall wasn't ideal, and these trivial matters shouldn't disturb Luo Chen.

Rubbing her brow, ignoring the fatigue of the day, she began inspecting the gifts from the City Lord's residence.

When the first chest was opened, gold and silver filled her eyes, dazzling her mind.

Jing Lan roughly understood what these gifts were.

This was City Lord Xiao's reward for Luo Chen's help in fighting and winning the fifth battle, even more generous than previously promised.

The gold, silver, and jewels in this single chest alone would be enough for an ordinary person in the city to live comfortably for a lifetime.

The second chest felt somewhat familiar to Jing Lan, as it contained mostly medicinal herbs.

However, in terms of age and quality, they were far superior to what was sold openly at Renji Hall. She imagined that for martial artists, the medicinal properties would also be better.

"This is quite a nice gift. Brother Luo should like it."

Jing Lan smiled, but when she opened the third chest, her expression froze.

...

In the cold midnight.

The door to the cultivation room slowly pushed open, and a wave of heat surged out first.

Although Luo Chen was drenched in sweat, his expression was calm as usual.

Leaving the cultivation room, Luo Chen went to the bathing chamber.

There, a large white jade pool sat quietly. Outside the bath, servants had been constantly adding firewood to maintain the water temperature, ready for Luo Chen to wash up at any time.

Though not as convenient and quick as the Cleansing Art, in terms of luxury and enjoyment, it was more than adequate.

Soaking in the bath, Luo Chen closed his eyes, pondering matters of cultivation.

Light footsteps sounded behind him.

Without getting up, Luo Chen knew who it was.

"Brother Luo, your clothes."

"Mm." Luo Chen divided his attention, thinking about cultivation matters while responding to Jing Lan.

Through the hazy mist, Jing Lan could vaguely see the man's thick, mountain-like broad shoulders. Her face flushed slightly, but her tone remained normal.

"City Lord Xiao Feng sent gifts this afternoon, three chests in total. Among them, gold and silver..."

Luo Chen listened casually, not paying much attention.

These mundane things were of little use to him, aside from allowing a more comfortable lifestyle.

As for those medicinal herbs, in his eyes they were utterly ordinary, hardly worth a glance.

But when Jing Lan mentioned the gift in the third chest, Luo Chen's eyes opened slightly.

"Decadent Immortal Smoke?"

Jing Lan pursed her lips and said, "Yes, an entire chest of Decadent Immortal Smoke, all top-grade. Even in the Green Mountain Town where I used to live, only a small amount of top-grade could be produced in a year. That chest would require the entire year's supply from four or five towns as large as Green Mountain Town!"

The corners of Luo Chen's mouth lifted slightly. "It seems Xiao Feng is quite generous!"

But Jing Lan spoke with concern, "Brother Luo, can you please not use that Decadent Immortal Smoke?"

"Oh?"

Luo Chen was somewhat surprised, then seemed to recall the Wuyou Envoy's warning before leaving during the day, and his expression gradually turned serious.

"Xiao Lan, how much do you know about Decadent Immortal Smoke?"

Jing Lan's expression was tense as she spoke rapidly, "It's not a good thing. Although Green Mountain Town's main export was Decadent Immortal Smoke, ordinary people simply couldn't use it. It's said that this smoke is highly addictive and can cause mental confusion. Especially the top-grade Decadent Immortal Smoke—if an ordinary person consumes even a little, they die instantly! Brother Luo, you absolutely must not touch this stuff!"

Addictive?

Mental confusion?

Poison?

Luo Chen's brow furrowed slightly, and he had already stepped out of the bath.

Casually grabbing a piece of clothing and draping it over himself, he walked toward the main hall.

Under the dim lamplight.

A chest was opened, revealing boxes filled with a special fragrance.

One box in Luo Chen's hand had already been unsealed, revealing pale golden tobacco shreds.

Beside him, Jing Lan was extremely tense, afraid that Luo Chen would consume it.

"You may withdraw for now."

"Brother Luo."

"Withdraw!"

Jing Lan's expression was worried, but she could only leave unwillingly, her eyes filled with concern as she waited in the courtyard.

After she left, Luo Chen took out a strand of tobacco, lit it with a lamp flame, and wisps of sandalwood incense rose.

He was not afraid of this thing being poisonous!

In his early years, when he cultivated the *Hundred Poisons Body-Tempering Art*, his physical body had gained the characteristic of being immune to a hundred poisons, unless it was something stronger than the poisonous wine of Poisonous Spirit Millet.

But the moment the scent of Decadent Immortal Smoke was inhaled into his body, Luo Chen's brow twitched involuntarily.

"How strange!"

Luo Chen opened his mouth and exhaled, and all the absorbed fragrance of Decadent Immortal Smoke was expelled.

With a sweep of his sleeve, the thick smoke inside the room was swept away by a gust of wind, dissipating into the night sky.

Under the lamplight, Luo Chen gazed deeply at the chest of Decadent Immortal Smoke before him.

"This thing doesn't target the physical body, but the soul—especially those who have cultivated divine sense!"

Just at that moment, his sealed soul had stirred slightly, as if craving this substance.

Decadent Immortal...

This stuff seemed to be specifically for cultivators.

With Luo Chen's knowledge in medicine, pharmacy, and alchemy, just that slight reaction was enough for him to see through the characteristics of this smoke.

Hallucinogenic, debilitating, addictive, and even causing mental confusion...

For cultivators, such a thing was the most supreme poison.

If taken long-term, at the very least, cultivation would stagnate. In severe cases, it could even lead to constant heart tribulations and demonic obstacles—commonly known as qi deviation.

Luo Chen pursed his lips, his gaze flickering uncertainly.

Xiao Feng sending him such poison—what ulterior motives!

"He's probably afraid I'll seize his city lord position and is trying to corrupt me with this."

Luo Chen chuckled lightly and casually closed the chest lid.

Hearing the sound, Jing Lan hurried in again, her eyes constantly examining Luo Chen. Seeing that he showed no signs of that obsessed, manic expression, she breathed a sigh of relief.

"Brother Luo, I'll have the City Lord's residence stop sending Decadent Immortal Smoke from now on."

"No!"

Luo Chen raised his hand, cutting off Jing Lan's thought, and instead curled his lips slightly.

"Let them send it. Not only send it, but one chest every year, and it must be top-grade! This was the reward Xiao Feng promised me earlier!"

"But..."

"This thing is of great use to me. Don't worry too much. Just seal it properly and don't let anyone else come into contact with it."

Hearing the word "seal," Jing Lan, who had been somewhat worried, finally relaxed. As long as Brother Luo didn't consume it, that was fine.

"I'm going into seclusion for a while. If there's nothing major, don't disturb me."

"Alright."

"Oh, and buy me a few smoking pipes. Do it conspicuously, buy good ones. If we're putting on a show for others, we might as well do it properly."

...

Time passed slowly.

Half a year had passed since the new master of Yixian City was established.

After taking office, Xiao Feng first consolidated power, eliminated dissidents, then diligently managed internal affairs. Externally, he struck hard, sending experts led by Tie Xiong to extensively clear out bandit troubles in the surrounding areas.

In just half a year, Yixian City, which had been somewhat chaotic, once again showed signs of prosperity.

But no matter how busy he was during the day, Xiao Feng always found time to pay attention to the Luo residence.

Under the night sky.

Xiao Feng stood on the highest tower of the City Lord's residence, looking down from afar at the mansion three li away.

"Half a year, never leaving his house. Is he truly immersed in cultivation, indifferent to any power or status?"

Xiao Feng seemed to be muttering to himself, or perhaps questioning the middle-aged man behind him.

Over these six months, most of his guest elders, whether original or newly recruited, had been dispatched to other towns, each governing a region. The rest mostly held positions within the city or started businesses.

Only Luo Chen, this guest elder, had made no moves from start to finish.

He appeared to have no desires, unmoved by anything.

Tie Xiong sighed, "Perhaps that's really the case! After all, before coming to the Great Destruction Realm, he was a cultivator. Legend has it that cultivators pursue the Great Dao of longevity in the river of time, indifferent to all external things, including power, status, and beauty."

Xiao Feng frowned deeply, "How can a person truly have no desires? If he doesn't move, it's probably because he has greater ambitions! You must know, the Wuyou Token is in his hands. With that token, even forcibly seizing Yixian City is not impossible."

Tie Xiong shook his head, "What does ambition matter? Didn't you say he was very satisfied with that batch of Decadent Immortal Smoke? According to information we gathered from Wuyou City, this substance most easily corrupts cultivators. How many with wills as firm as rock have become neither human nor ghost after years of smoking it? In time, Luo Chen, who once amazed Yixian City, will likely become like them."

Xiao Feng's brow relaxed slightly.

Sending Decadent Immortal Smoke to Luo Chen was his proudest move.

Even if it meant less profit, as long as it reduced Luo Chen's threat, it was enough.

But ultimately, pinning hopes on an imagined enemy's self-destruction—to truly stand firm, he had to rely on his own strength.

"Uncle Tie, have you made up for your deficiencies? Is your realm complete?"

Tie Xiong showed a regretful expression upon hearing this.

"Breaking through to the Fourth Realm with a secret technique, the golden body inevitably has flaws. It can't be repaired in a year or two."

Saying this, Tie Xiong's loving gaze fell on Xiao Feng.

"City Lord, your talent itself is not bad. You cultivated to the Furnace Realm at a young age. Why not focus your energy on cultivation? In time, you might be able to restore the might of the old City Lord."

Xiao Feng curled his lips, not responding.

He deeply understood the difficulty of martial arts cultivation.

Even with talent, one had to undergo countless tempering and refinement, the pain of which no one wanted to endure forever.

Ruling Yixian City alone at the Furnace Realm, enjoying wealth, honor, power, and status—that was enough.

If he wanted to go further, he didn't know how much more he would have to pay.

Most importantly, even if he reached that step, what then?

Wouldn't he still have to obediently go to Wuyou City at the summons of a Wuyou Token, unable to return?

Thinking of the Wuyou Token, Xiao Feng grew even more irritable.

Luo Chen, holding the Wuyou Token, had nominal authority to replace him at any time. And with the reputation of being Yixian City's number one expert, Luo Chen's status was already on par with his.

If the other party truly had the Leakless Realm, going directly to Wuyou City would be fine.

But unfortunately, Luo Chen's realm was only the Blood River Realm. According to normal cultivation speed calculations, he would need at least two to three hundred years before he had a chance to break through to the Leakless Realm.

To endure two to three hundred years with someone on equal footing—Xiao Feng couldn't accept that!

After all, even a Furnace Realm expert could only live two to three hundred years.

Tie Xiong vaguely sensed Xiao Feng's mindset, and his original intention to advise could only turn into a light sigh.

Just then.

The wind suddenly stopped.

Xiao Feng didn't notice, but Tie Xiong abruptly raised his head, his gaze fixed firmly in the distance.

Under the deep night, the quietly standing Luo residence seemed to come alive.

At that moment, it was as if a long-slumbering ancient giant beast had ceased its slumber and opened its eyes to the world.
