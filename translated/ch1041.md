# Chapter 1041: Overwhelming Five Sages, Four Halls Vie for the Disciple

"The grandfather and grandson of the Zhou clan have come to pay respects at the mountain!"

Zhou Yunshen's calm voice, amplified by his dharma power, rolled forth like tidal waves.

The entire vast Tinnan Dao Palace could hear it with perfect clarity.

One after another, powerful auras surged into the sky, with five among them drawing the most attention.

Five beams of escaping light shot upward, all turning to look in this direction.

Zhou Youyou stood behind Zhou Yunshen, gazing at the five figures—one woman and four men. Naturally, the sole woman was the first to catch Zhou Youyou's attention.

A long robe of silk and gauze, mist and rosy clouds flowing upon it.

A slanted hairpin embedded in her tresses, a walking sway gently trembling.

Ink-black hair set off snow-white skin, and her entire temperament was as aloof as a snow lotus from the Tianshan Mountains.

Most striking were her distinct features. Individually, none were perfect, yet combined, they created a breathtaking beauty.

"According to rumors, the current Dao Palace is divided into one temple and four halls. The master of Houtu Hall is a female cultivator—that must be her! Fairy Qinglan!"

Fu Qinglan's attention had originally been on Zhou Yunshen, like everyone else.

But her keen divine sense detected someone's covert scrutiny, and she cast a sidelong glance at the young man.

With just one look, a strange sense of familiarity and closeness stirred in her heart.

Yet the next moment, that inexplicable feeling vanished.

The woman furrowed her brows imperceptibly.

A hearty laugh rang out from beside her.

"So it's Fellow Daoist Zhou. Forty years unseen, I trust you've been well?"

"Elder Hui, ah no, I should now call you Hall Master Hui." Zhou Yunshen smiled, then sighed. "You should know my situation best. How could I possibly be well?"

Hui Yuanhong laughed heartily. "You still look hale and hearty, Fellow Daoist. Why be so self-pitying?"

Seeing the other four's confusion, he sent a simple divine-sense transmission.

"His lifespan is short; he's nearing his end."

The others immediately showed looks of understanding.

If anyone present knew Zhou Yunshen best among the five, it was Hui Yuanhong, a former elder of Star Gate.

After all, back then, Zhou Yunshen had merely been an ordinary guest elder within Star Gate.

Given his strength, he could have become a higher-ranking offering elder, but his short time in Star Gate and his meager remaining lifespan had kept him as a mere guest elder.

During their brief exchange, more and more gazes within the Dao Palace turned toward the sky above.

Zhan Bingtao, the temple master of Heavenly Origin Temple, stepped forward.

He spoke in a deep voice. "Fellow Daoist, what brings you here?"

Zhou Yunshen put away his smile and glanced at Zhou Youyou beside him.

"Naturally, I am sending my Zhou clan's prodigy to cultivate in the Dao Palace."

At this, the five Sages all fixed their gazes on Zhou Youyou.

The young man was exceedingly handsome, with a gentle, yin-tinged temperament. His slender frame seemed to contain boundless energy.

Facing the stares of five Sages, he stood there quietly, neither humble nor arrogant, his bearing unchanged.

This alone—a composure unshaken under heavy pressure—was worthy of praise.

Moreover, they all recalled the news brought back from Jade Balance Court nine years ago.

The Zhou clan of White Egret Marsh had produced a youth of astonishing talent, one whose aptitude could rival that of the Shi clan's prodigy, Shi Ju!

Instantly, every gaze grew fervent.

Zhan Bingtao exhaled a turbid breath and, for the first time, revealed a smile. "Fellow Daoist is willing to send your clan's qilin son to Tinnan Dao Palace. Your intent is commendable, your aspiration laudable. The grand display earlier led us to misunderstand."

Yet Zhou Yunshen shook his head. "Not so fast."

Everyone was taken aback.

Zhou Yunshen spread his wide sleeves, and his red coral-white robe billowed without wind. A vast Sage domain, centered on him, slowly expanded, pressing down on the five.

"Before that, I need to know whether you all truly have the ability to guide my Zhou clan's qilin son. Otherwise, it would be better to keep him by my side, where I can teach him myself."

Faced with such blatant provocation, Zhan Bingtao made no effort to hide his anger.

This was the pride of one who had once belonged to the strongest holy land under heaven!

He disdained to conceal his emotions, and he was angered by Zhou Yunshen's challenge.

In days past, when facing recruitment by Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, let alone challenge or doubt, kneeling in gratitude was common. How could anyone be allowed to be so insolent?

"Hall Master Hui!"

Hui Yuanhong nodded lightly and likewise released his own Sage domain.

He was one of only two Grand Cultivators among the Dao Palace's five Sages.

The moment his late-stage Sage domain emerged, its momentum alone was in no way inferior to Zhou Yunshen's.

Yet upon the first contact, Hui Yuanhong's expression changed drastically.

He felt his control over the surrounding heaven and earth spiritual qi being greatly suppressed, retreating step by step at a visible pace, utterly unable to withstand Zhou Yunshen's domain pressure.

"You broke through to late-stage Sage?"

A pleasant, heavenly voice rang out simultaneously, tinged with surprise.

"Law true intent, origin power!"

Zhou Yunshen looked at Fu Qinglan, his eyes showing approval.

"Rumor has it that Fairy Qinglan, under the protection of Wilderness Wanderer, comprehended law true intent within Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven and even won first place in the five-hundred-year grand competition. As it appears now, whether your ability is good or not is hard to say, but this insight and discernment are certainly genuine!"

As he spoke, his previously unremarkable Sage domain suddenly erupted with the sound of surging tidal waves.

Vast spiritual qi swept in, giving birth to clusters of brilliant coral, crowding together and spreading endlessly, almost blotting out the sky.

Domain imaging was not an especially exquisite technique.

But to achieve this was rare under heaven.

Moreover, unlike ordinary domains, Zhou Yunshen's domain, now elevated to another level, faintly resonated with this very heaven and earth, causing everyone to tremble.

Under the pressure, Hui Yuanhong could only compress his domain tightly around himself to remain unaffected.

The other two hall masters fared worse, their faces showing shock.

Fu Qinglan sighed softly. Without any visible movement, a trace of green light unfolded, shielding the coral formed from spiritual qi.

"Hmph!"

At that moment, a cold snort broke the deadlock.

Zhan Bingtao stepped forward, his majestic aura coalescing into a sharp war halberd that pierced the vast ocean and shattered the boundless coral.

The war halberd advanced like a splitting bamboo, straight ahead.

The coral rose and fell, vanishing and regenerating, continuously grinding against the halberd.

To outsiders, this strange spectacle seemed dazzling, but in truth, it was the purest collision of domains!

When the war halberd reached three feet before Zhou Yunshen, the latter's face finally showed gravity.

He swept his wide sleeve, and the war halberd dissipated into smoke.

On the opposite side, Zhan Bingtao also appropriately withdrew the step he had taken.

The two stared at each other in silence, then finally exchanged smiles.

"As expected of the clan leader of the Zhan family, the strongest combat power among the great houses. Rumor has it that you once withstood a strike from the ascended great power, Divine Lord Youli, and survived. As it appears now, there is indeed something worthy of praise."

"You flatter me. That was merely a non-discriminatory attack from Divine Lord Youli; it can hardly be called withstanding. But as for your ability, your realm... what a pity."

Zhou Yunshen was helpless. He knew what the other meant by "pity."

Through personally witnessing Zhou Youyou's growth, he had accumulated experience and finally comprehended the water origin. Yet his lifespan ultimately prevented him from advancing further.

"With Fellow Daoist Zhan guarding the Tinnan Continent, I can rest assured."

Zhan Bingtao was unconcerned. His gaze once again fell on Zhou Youyou.

"So now, does Fellow Daoist Zhou believe that Tinnan Dao Palace is still qualified to teach your Zhou clan's qilin son?"

Zhou Yunshen smiled and nodded. "Naturally, it is sufficient."

The vast domain dissipated like smoke.

Everyone breathed a sigh of relief.

Zhan Bingtao extended his hand in a gesture of invitation. "Please!"

The grandfather and grandson of the Zhou clan did not decline. Under the welcome of the five Sages, they drifted into Tinnan Dao Palace.

Behind them followed countless gazes.

Each gaze held a different meaning, and most fell upon Zhou Youyou.

The old man who had overwhelmed four Sages and contended with the temple master had not come to flaunt his power. His purpose was clear to all—to elevate Zhou Youyou!

Thus, Zhou Youyou's future cultivation within the Dao Palace would surely be smooth sailing, with everything he desired within reach.

...

Within Tinnan Dao Palace, Heavenly Origin Temple.

Six Sages sat together, with Zhou Youyou attending behind his grandfather.

He spoke not a word, quietly listening to the six chat idly.

This was not boring for him, for these six Sage cultivators did not exchange cultivation insights. Instead, they spoke mostly of matters on the Tinnan Continent, as well as the continents of Libei and Dongyuan.

Or rather, matters of "Central Continent"!

"That battle back then was truly brutal. The vast Central Continent was shattered into its current state. Even Sage-level experts like us were powerless in battles of that caliber. Fewer than two or three out of ten survived."

"Indeed. If I hadn't been on the Northern Sea battlefield, I likely wouldn't have escaped that calamity."

"I am the same. Because I was on the Eastern Wilderness battlefield, I was fortunate to keep my life."

"You all fared well. The sect master Lian and I were in the Southern Frontier, and the dangers we faced were no less than the Central Continent catastrophe. That Divine Lord Youli, who had shown no sign of his power for over a thousand years, turned out to be unfathomably profound. With a single strike, he resolved the Life and Death Gate crisis, and I was severely wounded, spending a full forty years recuperating before recovering."

"None of you were in Central Continent, so you never faced the might of the Demon Phoenix directly. But I saw it. That scene is unforgettable for life! Accompanied by four archaic beasts and commanding nine ghost gods, it landed on Central Continent. Wherever it passed, mountains crumbled and earth split, cultivators fell like rain. The previous sect master, the great Mo, joined with our Star Gate's nineteen elders to forcibly activate the Star Gate, seeking aid from the upper realm. Yet no matter how many Ascendant-level avatars descended, none could halt the Demon Phoenix's advance. Until the Severing Heaven-Earth Passage formation was broken, and Heaven-Earth Peak collapsed in ruins!"

...

Zhou Youyou stood behind, his heart surging with excitement, barely able to contain himself.

In White Egret Marsh, surrounded by Zhou clan members, his status was as lofty as his grandfather's. His talent was enough to look down on all his peers. Especially after effortlessly killing his seventh uncle across realms, a sense of arrogance had arisen within him.

But after witnessing his grandfather casually annihilate Cold River Valley and Zhan Bingtao withstand his grandfather's Sage domain with profound cultivation, he understood that there were always those stronger, and heavens beyond heavens.

Yet this was still not the end!

Beyond the Tinnan Continent, there were ascended great powers who could rout a million-strong army with a wave of their hand.

On the boundless Northern Sea, there were formation grandmasters who could refine mountains and seas, acting with impunity.

Deep within the Eastern Wilderness mountains, there was the supreme beast, Rosycloud, who withstood the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's attacks and forcibly seized the Star Gate, one of the sect's three treasures.

These legendary feats, narrated by the accessible seniors before him, seemed to unfold right before his eyes.

All of Zhou Youyou's arrogance had vanished, but another kind of pride arose within him.

With his talent, one day, he too could achieve such things!

Zhan Bingtao's voice came down from above, clear and equally filled with pride.

"Although Li Canghai's formation dao is divine and unfathomable, he is restrained by two ancient demons of the Northern Sea. Though Divine Lord Youli is powerful, he has already ascended, and the Life and Death Gate is but a skeleton in the tomb. The brutal Demon Phoenix, with its heaven-reaching methods, is unmatched under heaven. Yet after participating in several great battles and facing the self-sacrifice of our Heavenly Origin Five Elders, it was finally severely wounded, no longer able to covet Central Continent."

"Now, the Eastern Wilderness has no holy land, the Southern Frontier has no great power, the Western Desert Buddhist sect has closed its doors, and the Northern Sea is a heap of loose sand. Though our Heavenly Origin Dao Sect has suffered heavy losses, it remains the strongest holy land in the Mountains and Seas Realm!"

"With our profound foundation and our generation's utmost efforts, we will eventually restore the old mountains and rivers and revive the Dao Sect's glory!"

"When that time comes, we shall carry forward the will of our predecessors, unify the Mountains and Seas, and trample Green Parasol Mountain flat!"

As his words fell, they struck the ground with force, echoing continuously within the temple.

Among the four hall masters, three immediately echoed in unison.

"Restore the old mountains and rivers, revive the Dao Sect's glory!"

"Unify the Mountains and Seas, trample Green Parasol Mountain flat!"

Hui Yuanhong, hall master of All Stars Hall, was a step behind but quickly added, "This hall master shall strive alongside you!"

Zhou Yunshen stroked his beard. "A pity. I have the heart but not the strength. I fear I will not live to see that day."

Everyone knew his lifespan situation and understood he spoke the truth.

Zhan Bingtao laughed heartily. "Though Fellow Daoist Zhou will not see it, your descendant possesses unparalleled talent. He will surely surpass his predecessor. When that time comes, he will be a banner figure among the younger generation of our Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, leading the Zhou clan and the Heavenly Origin youth forward bravely and invincibly!"

Everyone's gaze fell upon Zhou Youyou behind him.

Zhou Yunshen beckoned, and Zhou Youyou stepped forward at the right moment.

"Just in time, while I am still here, let us determine his master-disciple lineage today!"

Silence fell over the gathering.

Zhou Yunshen nodded to Zhou Youyou.

The young man no longer hesitated and immediately released his dharma power fluctuations.

In that moment, all were astonished.

"Adept?"

"No, it's Initiate!"

"So fast?"

Zhou Yunshen stroked his beard and said, "As you can see, Youyou's progress is extremely rapid. He can completely skip the foundational learning stage of Seven Stars Court and enter the four halls directly for cultivation. I do not wish for him to waste time, hence this visit today. I wonder, which of you would be willing to accept him as a disciple?"

The current Heavenly Origin Dao Sect was far from its peak.

With the loss of a great number of talents, and even the twelve great houses reduced to nine, the sect was desperately seeking talent.

Relying solely on the traditional clan system could not rapidly cultivate many strong individuals.

Thus, the once-declining master-disciple system had naturally risen.

The establishment of Tinnan Dao Palace and the rule of the four hall masters selecting the best for discipleship were specially formulated by the current Heavenly Origin Dao Sect to quickly recover its vitality.

Regardless of clan distinctions, unbound by past traditions, concentrating resources and pouring them into cultivation!

This system was implemented not only on the Tinnan Continent but also in Libei and Dongyuan.

If anyone had still harbored reservations about the new rules set by the sect's ancestral home at Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range, after Zhou Youyou displayed a cultivation level completely incongruent with his age, the four hall masters within the temple cast all restraint aside.

Such heaven-defying talent must be taken under one's wing!

Hui Yuanhong spoke first.

"My All Stars Hall is willing to accept Zhou Youyou as a disciple!"

The other three halls were not to be outdone, each expressing their willingness.

Seeing this, Zhou Yunshen smiled with satisfaction.

"You all wish to take Youyou as a disciple, but there is only one of him!"

That was the problem.

Zhan Bingtao raised an eyebrow. "Zhou Youyou, what do you think?"

He was handing the decision to this twenty-one-year-old youth?

Zhou Youyou said calmly, "I do not know the cultivation directions, teaching philosophies, or individual abilities of these four seniors. I am unclear as to who would suit me, so I wish to consider further."

Consider further?

Everyone was taken aback, then looked at the young man with amusement.

He was setting conditions!

Faced with the favor of four Sages, he still dared to set conditions.

Yet strangely, no one was angered. Instead, they were even more satisfied with Zhou Youyou.

Cultivation required not only talent and aptitude but also the mindset of a strong individual.

From his bearing and demeanor at their first meeting to his current boldness in making demands of Sages, Zhou Youyou's mindset was indeed not lacking.

Hui Yuanhong spoke again. "I am the master of All Stars Hall, primarily cultivating the earth element. It may seem incompatible with you, but All Stars Hall inherited most of the essence of the former Star Gate. Within it are countless classics, covering the five continents of the world. As long as you enter All Stars Hall, there will always be something suitable for you!"

Then another quickly followed. "I am Long Ting, master of Longevity Hall, supreme elder of the Long clan. If you are willing to worship me as your master, the great dao of longevity is within reach. I can even pass on my Long clan's True Dragon Divine Art to you."

Zhang Bairen, in his purple robe, coughed lightly. "Though the four halls are nominally equal, Ziwei Hall is ultimately ranked first. I hold the most orthodox cultivation methods of Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. As a disciple of the Dao Sect, you should rightfully enter Ziwei Hall."

Fairy Qinglan, who had spoken little earlier, now smoothed the strands of hair by her cheek and said calmly, "I am the first-place winner of the five-hundred-year grand competition, a disciple of the great power Chunyuan, and I comprehended law true intent early on. If your goal is not merely the Sage realm, then you should choose me."

After the four had laid out their conditions, before Zhou Youyou could choose, they began attacking each other.

"Inheriting most of Star Gate's essence? Fellow Daoist Hui, you are too confident. I know you were the last to join the elder council, inheriting the position left after Alchemy Saint ascended. How much do you truly know and grasp of Star Gate's legacy?"

"The True Dragon Divine Art is but a hollow shell, nothing more. It lacks the substance of a divine ability, and its threshold is extremely high."

"The orthodox methods of Heavenly Origin Dao Sect? Which of our four halls lacks them?"

"Fairy Qinglan is too arrogant. You yourself are still at mid-stage Sage. How can you speak of the path to Ascendant?"

...

Amid the chaos, Zhan Bingtao coughed heavily.

"How disgraceful!"

The hall fell silent instantly.

Then Zhan Bingtao looked at Zhou Youyou.

"The four halls of the Dao Palace are no ordinary places. With your talent, guidance from any one hall will allow you to smoothly form your core and condense your nascent soul. As for Ascendant, that will require even more effort from you."

"How will you choose? Give your answer!"

(End of this chapter)
