# Chapter 1175: An Ordinary Punch, A Mad Challenge

Creating one's own divine ability was something Luo Chen had done before.

And not just once.

First came the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger, then the Starfire Burning the World.

More recently, there was also the Heaven-Earth Inverse Reincarnation.

But upon careful consideration, each situation was completely different.

The Withering-Bloom Dao Finger had come naturally when he comprehended the true meaning of a law.

Starfire Burning the World was inspired by a meteor falling from the sky when he was traveling through the untamed wilderness of the Mountains and Seas Realm.

As for Heaven-Earth Inverse Reincarnation, it was derived bit by bit from the foundation of Xiao Lin's forbidden technique fragments, and its power mainly lay in the strength of the astral qi.

What he needed to do now, however, was to create a martial divine ability of his own from nothing, without any enlightenment, comprehension, or sudden epiphany.

The difficulty of this matter was far beyond what ordinary people could imagine.

Nor was it something that could be achieved through mere summarization, refinement, or innovation.

As Luo Chen's realm improved, he had been constantly thinking about how to create it.

Even though another five years had passed and his realm had already climbed to the peak of the mid-Leakless stage, just one step away from the late-stage Grandmaster realm, he still had no clue.

This had even caused him to halt his progress toward the Fifth Ring and Fourth Ring.

Xiao Lin, who had figured out Luo Chen's cultivation and rest schedule, often came to discuss matters. Seeing him in such a troubled state, he couldn't offer any good advice either.

"Your foundation is too deep. Your qi and blood are unprecedented—practically inexhaustible. If you want to pour it all out in a single move as you envision, how could that be easy?"

Indeed!

That was the crux of the problem.

Luo Chen's foundation was simply too strong!

His physical body, at the fifth-rank true spirit level, was enough to bear anything.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron continuously transformed chaotic energy day and night, resulting in a massive accumulation of qi and blood power within his body.

The Blood Refining Divine Astral Qi he had condensed was even more overbearing and unparalleled.

The advantage was that when facing enemies, he could crush them head-on with majestic, domineering force.

Even the simplest techniques became incomparably powerful in his hands.

Take the Flower-Folding Finger technique, for example. Xiao Lin knew it, and Luo Chen knew it too.

When Xiao Lin used it, he could weaken the enemy's strongest attack inch by inch, like a traveler plucking a flower, greatly reducing its power. It was an excellent method for blunting an enemy's edge.

But in Luo Chen's hands, it wasn't flower-plucking at all—it was flower-destroying. A single finger strike didn't weaken the enemy's attack; it directly annihilated and dismantled it.

These were all his advantages.

One could imagine how terrifying the power would be if he could concentrate all that fearsome force together and then unleash it in an instant!

That wouldn't be a full-power strike.

It would be a devastating strike.

One that destroyed the enemy, and also destroyed himself.

Because after that move, he would have no strength left, and he himself would enter the weakest state he had ever experienced.

It could be said that this was the most extreme technique!

But just as Luo Chen had said to Gu Xun, "If not extreme, how can one cross the Guixu?"

Only by surpassing one's own extremes could one leave the Guixu Land, which was heading toward destruction and extremes.

In Luo Chen's view, even all the top martial artists in the entire Guixu Land were pursuing extremes.

The Door of Destruction was so dangerous, yet it was said that someone had still forcibly broken through it.

A Great Vehicle Heavenly Venerable, the pinnacle of ordinary cultivators, could even hold a place in the Immortal Realm. Yet someone had still thought of a mad method to slay a Great Vehicle Heavenly Venerable, attempting to use the complete laws condensed by the Venerable to escape the Guixu.

Even further away, in the Corpse Immortal Domain and the Sea of Stars, people were persistently searching for a way to leave.

When Luo Chen entered the Destruction Nine Rings to cultivate, he was already destined to walk the path of extremes.

Originally, he had planned to consider it when he reached the fifth realm of body refinement, the Martial Immortal.

Now, he was merely taking that step ahead of time.

But how exactly should he do it?

A thousand tangled threads were like a complex ball of yarn, with no end to be found.

Luo Chen said bitterly to Xiao Lin, "Truly, the foundation that makes me also breaks me. Even if I actively collapse the qi and blood furnace and open all seven hundred and twenty acupoints, I still can't unleash all my power at once. After all, no matter how well-polished the acupoints are, they are still just insignificant external channels on the human body. It's not like I can self-destruct my physical body!"

"Remove all restrictions and self-destruct your physical body? That's too dangerous. It's a technique of mutual destruction, jade and stone burning together." Xiao Lin disagreed. That wasn't extreme; that was self-destruction.

However, he noticed that Luo Chen, after saying those words, had inexplicably fallen into deep thought.

Xiao Lin swallowed his saliva. "You're not really thinking about that, are you?"

Luo Chen waved his hand, explaining that he didn't have such self-destructive tendencies, but he was still deep in thought.

From his mouth came a low murmur.

"Acupoints..."

Clearly, during their conversation, Luo Chen seemed to have found some kind of inspiration.

Xiao Lin sat quietly across from him, no longer disturbing him.

The fragrance of tea wafted between them, and the atmosphere was quite harmonious.

It was as if Luo Chen had never been the murderer of his son!

Perhaps, for people at their level, for those who had abandoned everything to enter the Destruction Nine Rings, blood ties and familial affection had long faded to the extreme.

The pursuit of martial arts, the longing for a broader world, the freedom to escape the cage of the Guixu—that was the meaning of life.

Time passed, who knew how long.

Suddenly!

Xiao Lin seemed to sense something. He instinctively raised his palm, forming a blade with his fingers, and placed it before his face.

Layers of qi membranes appeared before him, and within those membranes, golden scales were densely packed, shining brightly.

This technique was called the Golden Scale Robe. It was inspired by his discussions with Luo Chen about the Blood Cloth Robe, and he had created this defensive move based on his own situation.

Even Luo Chen had greatly praised its defensive power.

The moment the Golden Scale Robe unfolded, Luo Chen threw an ordinary punch. It seemed slow but was as fast as lightning, arriving in an instant.

*Thump!*

A dull sound rang out, and Xiao Lin was forced back dozens of zhang from his seat.

This result didn't surprise Xiao Lin.

Because Luo Chen was too strong. When they had tested techniques before, he was often forced to retreat.

Especially as Luo Chen's realm grew higher and higher, he couldn't withstand Luo Chen's attacks anymore. Only in discussions of martial arts concepts could they still find common ground.

But what surprised Xiao Lin was that in that move just now, Luo Chen hadn't used any technique at all.

Yes, no technique at all!

Nor did he use his own terrifying physical strength.

It was merely a direct output of astral qi.

Xiao Lin looked up and saw that Luo Chen's right arm was a bloody mess!

This scene was unbelievable to Xiao Lin.

He had long experienced Luo Chen's inhuman physical qualities. Ordinary attacks couldn't possibly injure his body, let alone with the Blood Cloth Robe always covering the outside.

Yet with just a simple punch, Luo Chen had caused his own arm to explode to such an extent.

He cautiously approached.

"What exactly did you do?"

"Not enough, still not enough." Luo Chen muttered to himself, then looked down at his arm. There was still residual astral qi on it. "No, still wrong!"

Xiao Lin took a deep breath and then growled, "Luo Chen!"

Luo Chen, as if struck by lightning, woke up from his dazed, contemplative state.

He looked at Xiao Lin and forced a smile. "Sorry."

"I'm fine." Xiao Lin shook his head, then frowned. "But you're not!"

As he spoke, his gaze fell on Luo Chen's bloody arm.

Luo Chen smiled. "Don't worry."

Then, in the next moment, under Xiao Lin's shocked gaze, the wound on Luo Chen's arm healed in an instant, as if nothing had happened.

Only the torn sleeve showed that the scene just now was not an illusion.

"What is this?" Xiao Lin asked in astonishment.

Luo Chen casually explained, "A characteristic of my physical body. You can think of it as a talent similar to the Indestructible Body, an innate divine ability possessed by some demon beasts."

"Demon beasts?" Xiao Lin was stunned.

Luo Chen suddenly realized that Xiao Lin, who had grown up in the Guixu, didn't know about demon beasts, nor did he know what the Indestructible Body was.

Xiao Lin frowned. "Are you trying to rely on this physical characteristic of yours to create the technique you envision?"

Luo Chen directly shook his head, denying his guess.

The physical characteristic he possessed originated from the source power that came with his first promotion to the fourth-rank Archaic stage.

It seemed similar to the Indestructible Body of the demon race, but there was still a fundamental difference.

He could only achieve flesh regeneration and limb reattachment while maintaining the existence of his main body.

Even though cultivating the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture and undergoing the physical nirvana of the Nine Tribulations and Three Nirvanas had greatly enhanced this characteristic, it still hadn't reached the level of blood-drop rebirth.

Moreover, because he had dispersed his source blood into his astral qi, this characteristic had been weakened even further.

So how could he consider self-destructing his physical body to unleash a brilliant strike?

Xiao Lin was puzzled. "Then how did you throw that punch just now?"

Luo Chen raised his hand, made a fist, and then gently punched.

The speed was slow, allowing Xiao Lin to see clearly.

At that moment, Xiao Lin clearly observed that dozens of acupoints on Luo Chen's arm seemed to come alive, all outputting astral qi as Luo Chen threw the punch.

But because it was slowed down, he noticed that the frequencies were somewhat different, not reaching a strict synchronization.

"Is that all?" Xiao Lin was baffled.

If that was the case, why would it unleash greater power?

But this time, Luo Chen's explanation left him bewildered.

"Of course it's not just that, because at that moment, it wasn't one me throwing the punch, but dozens of mes."

"Uh..." Xiao Lin was at a loss.

Luo Chen smiled, then continued to ponder.

A thought had flashed through his mind earlier.

Relying on himself alone, he couldn't instantly output all the power in his body.

But what if there were more of him?

Perhaps that sounded a bit mysterious.

But to explain it simply—what about divided souls?

Earlier, to avoid attracting the Soul-Shaking Thunder Sound in the Guixu with his massive divine soul, Luo Chen had split his main soul, cutting out one relatively weak divided soul after another, and placed them in his acupoints, wrapped in astral qi.

This way, the target wasn't so obvious.

And just now, Luo Chen had thought of using the divided souls in his acupoints to simultaneously drive the Blood Refining Divine Astral Qi and throw a single punch.

To avoid any unforeseen accidents, he only used the acupoints on one arm.

The result was both surprising and disappointing.

What surprised him was that an ordinary punch had indeed far exceeded its previous power, proving that this path was feasible.

But what disappointed him was that, without the main soul being able to mobilize them, the divided souls couldn't synchronize, causing the sequence of the punch to be off, and the power hadn't been maximized.

And most importantly, the divided souls were too sluggish in driving the Blood Refining Divine Astral Qi!

So Luo Chen said:

Not enough, still not enough.

No, still wrong!

Xiao Lin observed Luo Chen's expression and clearly understood that Luo Chen himself was still in a state of confusion, so he didn't ask any more questions.

After agreeing on the next meeting time, he took the initiative to take his leave.

After Xiao Lin left, Luo Chen continued to ponder bitterly.

Day after day, year after year.

Time seemed to lose its meaning in the Destruction Nine Rings, which were like an endless universe.

Or rather, for the martial artists of the Guixu, time and space seemed so meaningless.

Even if they lived long and saw far, they would still turn to dust with the arrival of the Great Destruction Era.

Five years later.

Xiao Lin came to visit Luo Chen as agreed.

But what awaited him was only a note pressed under a stone table.

[Brother Xiao: Luo has been pondering the method of martial divine abilities and has some ideas. However, the Sixth Ring cannot meet my requirements, so I have left early to go to the Fifth Ring and Fourth Ring. Next, I will challenge various experts to hone my martial intent and refine myself. I am sorry for not being able to bid you farewell. But Luo is merely going ahead. With Brother Xiao's talent, you will surely catch up. I hope that one day, I can drink with you in the Upper Three Rings — Luo Chen]

"The Upper Three Rings, huh. That would be the Martial Immortal realm."

Xiao Lin sighed. He wasn't angry at Luo Chen for leaving without saying goodbye; instead, he felt inspired.

In the decades of discussion with Luo Chen, he had also gained a great deal.

After all, Luo Chen had integrated the strengths of many schools, had insightful views on physical cultivation, and had brought some martial concepts from the outside world.

Colliding with each other, Xiao Lin naturally had his own gains.

Not to mention anything else, just his own realm had gradually climbed to the peak of the mid-Leakless stage, and he was only some water-grinding effort away from the late-stage Grandmaster realm.

"When I cultivate to the late Leakless stage, I will charge into the Fifth Ring. I won't keep you waiting too long."

Xiao Lin muttered to himself, his expression firm and confident.

...

In the same year that Luo Chen left the Sixth Ring, a late-stage Leakless Grandmaster fell from the Fifth Ring into the Sixth Ring.

No one dared to touch the sore spot of that unlucky Grandmaster and ask what had happened.

But Xiao Lin knew that it was a poor soul who had been defeated by Luo Chen and forced to give up his cultivation stone.

There were thirty-six cultivation stones in the Sixth Ring, eighteen in the Fifth Ring, and only nine in the Fourth Ring.

When Luo Chen went up, he would naturally squeeze someone out.

Xiao Lin was also looking forward to the day he would go up.

Ten years later, he got his wish.

As a late-stage Leakless Grandmaster, he broke into the Fifth Ring and even challenged another Grandmaster.

After a hard-fought battle, as a newly promoted Martial Grandmaster, he successfully established a foothold.

Then he began to inquire about Luo Chen.

But the news he received shocked him.

Luo Chen had already left!

In fact, his stay in the Fifth Ring was extremely short—less than half a year.

During those six months, he challenged one Grandmaster every ten days.

Completely ignoring the unspoken rules, ignoring the fatigue brought by the rotation of battles, he challenged everyone with an almost mad frenzy.

In battle, he never used his terrifying physical body, never used the Heaven-Earth Inverse Reincarnation that could kill nine Leakless martial artists in one move, and didn't even reveal any ordinary martial techniques.

In every fight, he only used the purest astral qi to face his enemies.

Every exchange was just a punch, a punch, and another punch!

Yet, even so, all eighteen Grandmasters of the Fifth Ring were defeated by him.

Half a year later, Luo Chen charged into the last ring of the Middle Three Rings, the Fourth Ring.

That was the place in the Destruction Nine Rings, apart from the Upper Three Rings where the Martial Immortals resided, where only the strongest martial artists were worthy of staying for a long time.

The Leakless martial artists there were all old monsters who had survived for hundreds of years and were nearing the end of their lifespans.

Whether in the purity of their astral qi or their martial techniques, they had all been polished to the limit.

They could be called the strongest group below the Martial Immortal realm, and it was said that if they joined forces, they could slaughter all the martial artists of the Lower Three Rings.

But Luo Chen still charged in without hesitation.

It had been nine and a half years since Xiao Lin entered.

"I can't catch up at all!"

Xiao Lin sighed with emotion, but he felt no frustration. He was still firm and unwavering.
