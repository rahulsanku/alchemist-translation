# Chapter 643: Taiping Fortress, the Sea-Overturning Demon Jiao

As the carriage of Hundred Craft Mountain's mountain lords through the generations, the Sixfold Imperial Flying Cloud Carriage was not merely luxurious and grand on the surface.

It also possessed many functions of its own.

Flight was the most basic and also the most outstanding function.

It also had merits in both offense and defense, though it required a large number of people to operate before its might could be displayed. This point was rather awkward for Luo Chen.

However, another "monitoring" function on this magnificent cloud carriage was very suitable at this moment.

Relying on the water mirror and jade qing, Luo Chen truly learned quite a few secrets from the three people during the journey.

They all came from Central Continent.

Although they called one another senior and junior siblings, they did not share the same teacher. Rather, they came from three clans.

The Liu, Wu, and Guan clans.

According to them, Dragon Abyss Sage, the newly risen figure of the Myriad Immortals Assembly, was not some rootless wandering cultivator at all. He came from the Central Continent Liu clan to which Changfengzi belonged.

The reason the three had traveled thousands of li from Central Continent to the Northern Sea was to imitate Dragon Abyss Sage, obtain nine-star demon hunter qualifications in the Myriad Immortals Assembly, and thereby gain resources for Sage formation.

Apart from Changfengzi, who had some worries, the other two were full of confidence, as if nine-star demon hunter status were already within easy reach.

Their words were filled with arrogance, as though the Northern Sea were some poor backwater.

Luo Chen did not know where their confidence came from. Central Continent might be blessed with outstanding people and rich spiritual lands, but the Northern Sea was also famous for abundant resources and the constant emergence of powerful experts. Where did their sense of superiority come from?

Regarding this, Luo Chen paid no further attention.

For the remaining travel time, he devoted himself completely to adjusting his own condition.

Although this trip was only to test his sword, and the enemies he had to face were merely demon beasts, he could not be careless.

Any enemy should be faced seriously.

Otherwise, it was very easy for one's boat to capsize in a gutter.

Moreover, these demon beasts were no ignorant fools. They were all demon race powerhouses who had participated in the battle that destroyed the Ascendant Holy Land, the Primordial Demon Sect, and survived.

The cloud carriage traveled onward, passing through the Ring Ocean sea region and the Qu Ocean sea region before finally arriving at the first defensive line, the Flow Ocean sea region.

Before a vast stone fortress, the Sixfold Imperial Flying Cloud Carriage slowly descended.

Through the water mirror, Luo Chen looked at the surging crowds outside the fortress and sensed a solemn yet restless atmosphere.

As soon as they landed, a Foundation Establishment cultivator came forward to greet them.

"Junior Zeng Xiong greets the four Seniors!"

Luo Chen put away the cloud carriage and swept his sleeve forward.

"Take us to meet Fellow Daoist Shentu, the person in charge here."

As he spoke, he displayed his brand-new five-star demon hunter waist token.

When Changfengzi and the other two saw the star grade of that token, their expressions differed.

Changfengzi was somewhat puzzled. He had thought this Daoist Azure Yang, who possessed a Dao title, was very formidable. Why was his demon hunter star grade only five?

Wu Tao and Guan Xiaoping looked at each other, then curled their lips slightly.

In their view, within the Myriad Immortals Assembly, a cultivator's strength was linked to demon hunter star grade.

Those below seven stars were nothing special, merely ordinary wandering cultivators.

Previously, Senior Brother Changfengzi had told them to show Luo Chen some respect. Now it seemed entirely unnecessary.

After Zeng Xiong inspected Luo Chen's waist token, his gaze fell on the three behind him.

"To prevent demon race enemies skilled in transformation arts from infiltrating Taiping Fortress, all newcomers must display their identity tokens. I ask the three Seniors for understanding."

Luo Chen turned around and looked at the three, his eyes signaling them to cooperate.

Changfengzi gave a hum, displayed his waist pendant, and injected mana to activate it, proving that he was its owner.

Three white stars flickered faintly.

Wu Tao and Guan Xiaoping did the same, though their expressions were somewhat unnatural when their three stars flashed.

Perhaps in their eyes, such a low star grade was rather embarrassing.

Especially under Luo Chen's gaze.

Luo Chen did not feel much. This was only because the Myriad Immortals Assembly had changed its system. Otherwise, anyone who joined the Demon Hunter Office would have to slowly rise from one star.

Three stars was actually quite good.

Seeing this, the guard cultivator Zeng Xiong's eyes could not help lighting up.

With four more Golden Core Adepts arriving at once, the pressure of clearing the Sunken Sea demon beasts would certainly be greatly reduced.

"Four Seniors, this way please!"

Luo Chen nodded and strode forward.

The three behind him followed closely, their gazes taking in the scenery of the fortress.

Luo Chen did the same, occasionally asking Zeng Xiong about Taiping Fortress.

"I see this fortress is stained with blood and filled with baleful qi. Why is its name so peaceful?"

"The two words 'Taiping' were chosen by Mad Wanderer of our Myriad Immortals Assembly during one of his clear-minded periods. They express the wish that this place hold fast against demon race invasion and preserve peace for the human race behind it."

"How many people are usually stationed here?"

"Junior has not understood the exact details, but ordinarily there are at least around three thousand stationed here. Recently Blood Wanderer issued orders, so the number of cultivators has multiplied severalfold. More than ten thousand cultivators have come and gone. However, most have gone to the Sunken Sea, leaving not many here."

"The two defensive lines behind have three Golden Core cultivators stationed long term. What about here?"

"There are also three, but Lord Dao Lan is an exception. He borrows the demon race to sharpen his blade and is stationed here year-round, so counting him, there are four."

"How many Golden Core cultivators have come here recently?"

"The exact number is uncertain. Some Seniors completed sufficient demon beast hunting missions and returned early. But as far as I know, there should be around forty or fifty."

"That many? That is half the Golden Core cultivators of the Myriad Immortals Assembly."

"It cannot quite be said that way. The number of Golden Core Seniors fully from our Myriad Immortals Assembly is actually not that high. Some were sent by the three families of Wuzhong Valley."

Luo Chen nodded thoughtfully.

The Myriad Immortals Assembly had nearly one hundred Golden Core cultivators, including many false-core cultivators, but this did not count the experts of Wuzhong Valley, the Tianqu Chen clan, and the Coral Sea Zhou clan.

The Golden Core experts of those three families ordinarily cultivated in their own territories. Only in wartime would they coordinate with the Myriad Immortals Assembly.

When they arrived below the fortress gate, Luo Chen raised his head and looked at the blood-colored fortress that seemed cast from steel. His gaze landed on the square openings in its middle section.

"Hm? Why are there holes in the middle of the city wall?"

It was no wonder Luo Chen was curious. In his life, he had seen many immortal cities. For the sake of formation integrity, apart from the necessary four city gates, they were basically solid walls throughout, with few openings.

With Taiping Fortress doing this, was it not afraid that flaws would appear in the formation and be forcefully broken through?

Zeng Xiong immediately explained, "These holes are reserved for Heavenly Origin Spirit Energy Cannons. Once the number of demon beasts becomes too great and threatens the fortress, the spirit energy cannons will be activated to carry out large-scale covering attacks."

"Heavenly Origin Spirit Energy Cannons?" Luo Chen was surprised. He had heard of spirit energy cannons, back when Tianfan City brought them to the Tiangu Plain battlefield. But why did the spirit energy cannons here at Taiping Fortress have a name, and such a sensitive one at that?

Zeng Xiong's answer confirmed Luo Chen's guess.

"These Heavenly Origin Spirit Energy Cannons were purchased in bulk by our Myriad Immortals Assembly from the Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance. It is said they are powerful artifacts researched by Central Continent's Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. Seventy years ago, when the beast tide was at its worst, they were once used. That time, a thousand cannons fired together, and their might was almost no less than a Sage's full-strength attack. Truly terrifying!"

As Zeng Xiong spoke, he praised them repeatedly.

Luo Chen also clicked his tongue in admiration, but when his peripheral vision caught the three behind him looking as if they shared in the glory, his brow rose.

With that manner, could they be from the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect?

Or perhaps related to it?

Luo Chen's guess did not last long. Very soon, they entered Taiping Fortress and met the person in charge here.

Shentu, eighth level of Golden Core, one of the few seven-star demon hunters in the Myriad Immortals Assembly.

He wore casual clothes, his face faintly golden, which echoed Luo Chen's red face in an interesting way.

When he saw Luo Chen, Shentu was very enthusiastic.

Luo Chen naturally did not put on the airs of an artifact-forging master and conversed warmly with him.

After exchanging pleasantries, he explained his purpose.

"You want to go to the Sunken Sea?" Shentu nodded knowingly. "The fellow Daoists who have come here recently are basically all going there. It is simple enough for you to go, Fellow Daoist. However, there is one point I must explain in advance."

"Mm. Fellow Daoist Shen, please speak."

"It is actually the old rule from the early days of joining the Demon Hunter Office. Demon beasts killed on the battlefield, unless kept for personal use, must be sold to the Demon Hunter Office if they are to be sold."

Luo Chen was not surprised. "Fine."

Shentu looked at the other three. "And you? Are you also going to the Sunken Sea battlefield?"

Shentu and Luo Chen had been chatting quite pleasantly earlier, while he had been somewhat cold toward Changfengzi and the other two. This had already made the two younger cultivators dissatisfied.

Wu Tao snorted. "Naturally. Why else would we travel thousands of li to this place?"

Guan Xiaoping also said impatiently, "We have met the people, and our identities have been verified. If there is nothing else, we will go directly."

Changfengzi's lips moved, as if he wanted to say something, but in the end he only nodded.

Shentu's expression remained normal. He did not grow angry because of the dissatisfaction in their tone. He merely beckoned Zeng Xiong outside, gave a few instructions, then had Zeng Xiong lead the three to the Sunken Sea battlefield.

With the three gone, the hall became quiet.

But the atmosphere of Luo Chen and Shentu's conversation remained as warm as before.

"This is a bottle of pills I refined in my spare time. The quality is not bad. As a small gift for our first meeting, I will give it to Fellow Daoist."

Shentu was somewhat surprised, as if he had not expected Master Daoist Azure Yang, rumored to be aloof and indifferent to worldly affairs, to be so "worldly."

He accepted the jade bottle and smelled the dense pill fragrance within. His eyes widened slightly.

"Third-tier middle-grade pills! I did not expect Master Daoist Azure Yang, famous in the Myriad Immortals Assembly for artifact forging, to also be a formidable alchemist."

Luo Chen smiled faintly. "Alchemy and artifact forging do share some common ground. I only do it occasionally and do not deserve Fellow Daoist's praise."

Shentu put away the medicine bottle, his expression becoming serious.

"I will not accept Fellow Daoist's gift for nothing. If you have any needs, speak directly."

A straightforward person.

Luo Chen did not waste words.

"The war at the Sunken Sea has continued for two or three years and consumed large amounts of manpower and material resources from the Myriad Immortals Assembly, yet the demon beasts inside remain impossible to clear out. I need to know the reason."

"In addition, Luo also wants to know the distribution of demon beasts there, as well as the methods of the powerful Demon Kings."

Hearing this, Shentu frowned, then slowly nodded.

"That matter is simple enough."

The next day, Luo Chen, having rested and adjusted, drove his escape light alone toward the northwest.

In his mind echoed the intelligence Shentu had given him.

"The demon-sealing grand formation of the Arctic Yemo Heaven originally had two layers. One layer was arranged by the Primordial Demon Sect's Soul Refining lineage Ascendant ancestor before his death, and the other was later arranged by the Northern Sea's newly advanced great power, Li Canghai. The former restricts fourth-tier and higher experts from escaping, and it continues to function even now. But the latter was mostly broken thirty years ago."

"Therefore, over these thirty years, more and more low-rank demon beasts have escaped."

"At first, we were extremely worried after they escaped. But later we observed that they were stockpiling within the ruined city of Taohai and did not assault the Three Oceans defensive line. Only recently did we discover that they were not waiting passively, but had transferred to the Sunken Sea."

"Demon beasts are constantly escaping from the Arctic Yemo Heaven. This is the main reason the Sunken Sea war cannot end."

After Luo Chen learned this information, he did not doubt its truth.

As early as when he had performed garrison duties at the Qu Ocean defensive line, he had heard related news.

Now, putting the pieces together, the beginning and end matched.

The only thing that made Luo Chen doubtful was how many demon beasts had actually been sealed inside the Arctic Yemo Heaven.

More than a hundred years ago, the demon race had claimed to gather an army of hundreds of millions to attack the Primordial Demon Sect.

That battle directly destroyed the Primordial Demon Sect.

The Primordial Demon Sect had three great Ascendant ancestors. Apart from Blood Sea Ancestor, who was not in the sect at the time, Soul Refining Ancestor turned his remnant body into a formation, combined with the geographical advantage, and isolated the inside from the outside. As for Primordial Demon Ancestor, his life or death was unknown.

Apart from the Ascendant great powers, the other disciples of the Primordial Demon Sect all died within the sect, except for a small number who were outside.

The battle sounded terrifying, and the demon race had achieved great results.

But the Primordial Demon Sect was no soft persimmon. As a top sect famous for the demonic path, its final counterattack before death must have erupted with earthshaking power.

After that battle, how many of the demon race's army of hundreds of millions could have survived?

If the number was not large, then even if the two polar sealing formations were broken after a thousand or ten thousand years, the demon race experts who escaped should not have much impact on the human race.

If that was the case, Luo Chen could continue cultivating peacefully in the Myriad Immortals Assembly.

But if the Primordial Demon Sect had not taken many demon beasts down with it before its destruction, then Luo Chen would have to consider finding another place to cultivate.

"It is all too distant. Perhaps I am worrying over nothing."

Luo Chen shook his head, and his escape light stopped atop a solitary peak.

Looking out, the vast sea seemed to have been drawn through with a long, winding line.

On the two sides of the long line, the separation was clear.

One side was azure blue. The other was black and turbid.

"There. Is that the Sunken Sea?"

Luo Chen looked at the black, turbid sea surface and took a deep breath. Then he took a beast-skin map from his storage ring.

It was a simple distribution map of the Sunken Sea demon race forces that Shentu had sent someone to deliver before Luo Chen left.

Dense star points covered it, and several dozen places were specially marked with red dots.

The red dots all marked powerful third-tier Demon Kings.

Luo Chen sent his divine sense into it, and corresponding information quickly entered his mind.

Suddenly, Luo Chen's divine sense stopped on one red dot.

Sea-Overturning Demon Jiao, late third-tier Great Demon King. Skilled in controlling water. The single horn on its head can release demonic art attacks. It has extremely strong restraint against fire-attribute cultivators. If encountered, unless one has a late Golden Core cultivation base, flight is recommended.

"It will be this one!"

Luo Chen's eyes lit slightly, and strands of battle intent spread from his body.

This would be a fine opponent for testing his sword.

*(End of Chapter)*
