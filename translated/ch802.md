# Chapter 802: Icecastle's Jueqing Pays Homage to the True Man

Luo Chen's arrival had eased much of the tense war-preparation atmosphere within Lingtian Pass.

At this time, all resources were severely lacking. Pills—those resources that could rapidly enhance a cultivator's cultivation—were especially in high demand.

The Eastern Wilderness had once had the Medicine King Sect, but it had been destroyed.

High-grade alchemists had either been secretly absorbed by major sects or were too preoccupied with their own survival, leaving a vacuum in the market.

Luo Chen's appearance had indeed filled that vacuum.

This was the main reason he had been able to rise to fame in such a short time.

Therefore, Lingtian Pass treated Luo Chen with exceptional favor.

Among the five Sages, Qin Baixiang of the Myriad Beast Sect had been stationed at the very front, facing the extreme east—the most likely direction from which the demon beast army would attack.

Xiao Shiniang and Sage Xianhe were positioned on the left and right, a thousand li apart, guarding the central direction.

Luo Chen's patrol area had been arranged in the relatively rearward western side, where the pressure he would face would only come from scattered, idle demon tribes.

Lingfengzi, renowned for his escape speed, served as a mobile unit, on standby at all times.

Rumor had it that he was occasionally sent out to scout the front lines.

That was roughly the arrangement.

On that very day, Luo Chen first went to the cave dwelling that the Lingtian City Lord had arranged for him.

"So this is where I'll be living for the next three years!"

Luo Chen withdrew from the cultivation chamber, feeling quite emotional.

It had been a while since he had enjoyed a fourth-order spirit vein cultivation environment.

Within Lingtian Pass, such cave dwellings were not lacking.

Legend had it that during its construction, a group of Sage-level experts had moved mountains and transported seas, forcibly relocating them from some fallen domains.

If the battle truly entered a protracted phase, the cave dwellings of Lingtian Pass could support the rest and recuperation of the Sage-level experts, as well as their need to restore their dharma power.

"As long as Fellow Daoist is satisfied, that's good. Within the city, this cave dwelling is second only to the City Lord's residence," Lingfengzi said.

Luo Chen blinked.

No wonder!

He had felt that the spiritual qi within the cave dwelling was exceptionally abundant. Neither Longyuan Island, where he had previously lived, nor the Jia-78 cave dwelling where he had briefly stayed at Heaven-Earth Peak, could compare.

It seemed the Lingtian City Lord had put considerable thought into his arrangements.

Seeing Luo Chen's satisfaction, Lingfengzi could no longer hold back.

"I've heard that Fellow Daoist Jiuling has already obtained the pill he desired. It seems the rumors are true—your alchemy skill truly possesses heaven-reaching ability!"

Luo Chen smiled freely. "Flattery, flattery. At a time like this, there's no need to beat around the bush. If Fellow Daoist wishes to have a pill refined, feel free to speak your mind."

Lingfengzi's expression lit up with joy. Without any hesitation, he produced a pill formula.

Unlike Jiuling Yuanjun, who had hesitated, he directly handed it to Luo Chen.

"This pill involves one of my life-saving techniques. If Fellow Daoist can refine it for me, I would be eternally grateful."

Luo Chen waved his hand, not making any grand claims. Instead, he lowered his head and carefully examined the formula.

Lingfengzi dared not disturb him and quietly waited for a response.

This careful examination of pill formulas had become second nature to Luo Chen.

After about an hour, he had a clear picture in his mind.

"I can refine this pill."

The moment the words left his mouth, Lingfengzi's eyes lit up.

"However, you will need to provide the materials yourself."

Lingfengzi nodded repeatedly. "That goes without saying. For Fellow Daoist to be willing to refine pills for me is already an act of kindness. How could I let you bear the cost?"

Luo Chen said with a smile, "And the price?"

As before, Lingfengzi was once again troubled.

He understood very well that in these times, for someone to be willing to help refine pills was already very generous.

But to spend a great deal of energy and a long time on alchemy was also a very extravagant matter for Sage-level cultivators who valued every second.

Compensation was necessary!

But he was poor!

He clearly remembered the three conditions Luo Chen had proposed during his Sage Ascension Ceremony.

But whether it was Scarlet Firmament Qi or various pill formulas, he had none to offer.

He did have some body-tempering techniques, but their grades were not high, and he feared Luo Chen would look down on them.

Gritting his teeth, Lingfengzi braced himself and said, "I can offer three hundred high-grade spirit stones."

Three hundred high-grade spirit stones?

That was only three million low-grade spirit stones. In the current situation, high-grade spirit stones had strategic resource value, so it was acceptable.

But it wasn't enough!

Luo Chen's expression remained unmoved.

Lingfengzi continued, "I also have a few ancient body-tempering techniques, around the third order."

Third-order body-tempering techniques—too low.

Luo Chen still looked at him with a smile.

Lingfengzi thought for a moment, then gritted his teeth again. "I can also give Fellow Daoist a fourth-order Nether Serpent's main sinew. This beast was extremely powerful. It took several fellow daoists and me working together to barely kill it. Its main sinew is an excellent material for artifact forging. If Fellow Daoist obtains it..."

Luo Chen sighed. "Lingfengzi, you know very well that these things are of little use to me. On the contrary, if I have to spend a year or half a year refining pills for you, the loss to me would be incalculable. Do you think these conditions can buy a Sage's precious time for a year?"

Lingfengzi's expression froze.

What he had offered, if one were to ask whether it could move a Sage, the answer was actually yes.

But that would only be if the other party had a need for these things.

And that was for a full year's engagement!

Seeing his difficulty, Luo Chen suddenly spoke. "However, I have always been kind to others and cannot bear to see fellow daoists in distress. If you can agree to one condition of mine, then even if I have to spend a year or half a year refining pills for you, it would not be impossible."

"What condition?" Lingfengzi blurted out.

Luo Chen smiled slightly. "Very simple. In the future, if there is an opportunity, you will make a move for me once. Or, if you see my disciples in trouble, you will take the initiative to look after them a little. Rest assured, the scope of action will not be a death sentence, nor will it put you in a difficult position."

Lingfengzi's expression shifted from initial difficulty and hesitation to blossoming joy, all within a single sentence from Luo Chen.

He clasped his hands together and bowed. "Thank you, Fellow Daoist, for your kindness!"

This was genuine gratitude toward Luo Chen.

Making a move for someone was not uncommon among Sage True Men.

Moreover, Luo Chen had specifically stated that it would not be anything too dangerous. For him, it would likely be just a matter of adding icing on the cake when the time came.

The Alchemy Sect was truly benevolent!

Jiuling Yuanjun had not been wrong. Luo Chen indeed had the demeanor of a gentleman.

After leaving behind the corresponding raw materials and the trivial compensation mentioned earlier, Lingfengzi happily departed.

Luo Chen first put away the raw materials, then counted the compensation the other party had given.

Three hundred high-grade spirit stones—for a Sage True Man, it was truly a bit shabby.

But these were not ordinary times. High-grade spirit stones had too many uses, and their consumption had naturally increased greatly.

Perhaps this was the last batch of spirit stones the other party had on hand.

Luo Chen carefully stored them away. He was also short on spirit stones right now!

"Maybe I could sell a batch of low-grade pills to earn some spirit stones? And incidentally, increase the goodwill of those low-grade cultivators toward me?"

"But I would need to study how to sell them. If it's an auction format where the highest bidder wins, others won't be grateful to me—they'll only think they spent a lot and got what they deserved."

Luo Chen pondered carefully, and many ideas sprang to mind.

His hands, however, moved quickly. With one mind for two purposes, he checked the other items.

The main sinew of the Nether Serpent!

Rich in blood qi, tough in texture—it was indeed a rare fourth-order material.

"The core material for Caiyi's lifebound artifact—now I have it!"

Then there were a few third-order body-tempering techniques. Luo Chen glanced at them and instead became interested.

"All of them focus on speed. No wonder the Piaomiao Sect specifically collected these things—their sect is famous for its escape speed."

"Although the grades are not high, they are all complete, with nothing missing."

"I'll have someone take these books back and give them to Wang Yuan for study. Perhaps he can complete the last two forms of *Wind Moon Thunder Cloud Steps to Immortality*."

After a thorough inspection, Luo Chen was quite satisfied.

Although the overall value of these things was not too high, far inferior to the large amount of Scarlet Firmament Qi that Jiuling Yuanjun had given, or the pill formulas and talisman skins that Fu Lao had provided, they were all useful to him.

Moreover, he had obtained a promise of action from the other party.

This transaction—he, Luo Chen, had not lost out.

...

After a brief rest, Luo Chen began his formal patrol duties the next day.

In truth, for Sage True Men, many things did not require their personal involvement.

The Luo Tian Sect cultivators he had brought, together with those originally forced to stay in the city, gathered under the leadership of the Luo Tian cultivators and began deploying defenses on the western side.

Inspecting the city's formations, patrolling the city walls, forming small teams to patrol within a hundred li radius of Lingtian Pass—that was roughly it.

For the Sage True Men, there was actually only one task.

From time to time, release their divine sense to probe in a single direction, to prevent top-tier demon experts from forcefully breaking through Lingtian Pass.

Once detected, they would notify the city. As long as Lingtian Pass's grand formation was activated, even a late-fourth-order transformed demon could not break in.

Regarding this, Luo Chen had a concern.

At the City Lord's Mansion, he had directly asked.

"There is a Demon Emperor named Azure Frost, whose true form is the divine bird Azure Luan, and who controls spatial laws. If she descends, can Lingtian Pass withstand her?"

This question received an affirmative answer from the Lingtian City Lord.

"I'm just afraid she won't come!"

With that, Luo Chen's worry subsided.

It was because Azure Frost Demon Emperor's battle record was too terrifying—instantly defeating five Sages, fighting three Grand Cultivators to a standstill, and even holding out for a moment under a Ascendant-level expert.

If such an existence had no countermeasure, then Lingtian Pass, hailed as the eternal fortress of the Eastern Wilderness, would be nothing but a fragile piece of white paper.

Of course, he was also curious about what method could counter Azure Frost Demon Emperor.

Unfortunately, the Lingtian City Lord kept it very secret, saying it would not be easily used until the True Man was seen. Luo Chen had to suppress his curiosity.

...

After a sweep of his divine sense, Luo Chen steadied himself.

Having familiarized himself with the patrol duties, he could now perceive the trouble within them.

Releasing one's divine sense from time to time was not a great drain on a Sage True Man, but it was very easy to be distracted.

Whether cultivating or doing other things, it was very inconvenient.

"However, this is not a problem for me."

With a slight smile, Luo Chen took out two puppets from his storage ring.

It was the Black Ape and the Red Dog!

Two divided souls entered the two puppets.

Luo Chen's main body issued the command, and the Black Ape flew out, heading toward the city wall of Lingtian Pass.

The divine sense of a divided soul was slightly weaker, but it still had the strength of a normal early-stage Sage. Patrolling day and night would actually be more efficient.

As for the remaining Red Dog puppet, Luo Chen did not let it idle either. He directly handed Lingfengzi's pill formula to the Red Dog.

Although this pill formula was also fourth-order, its refinement difficulty was not high.

Far inferior to the Nascent Soul Pill and the Ninefold Separation Pill, and even slightly inferior to the Transformation Pill.

In Luo Chen's estimation, its refinement difficulty was probably a bit higher than the fourth-order Black Emperor Ointment, similar to the Purple Qi Poison-Resisting Pill.

The reason Lingfengzi had not been able to refine it before was probably because he was short on funds and no one was willing to refine it for him.

With this level of difficulty, Luo Chen did not even need to consume system achievement points.

He would first let the divided soul study it carefully, familiarize itself with it, and then the main body would directly take over the refinement.

Thus, the main body was free.

But when was Luo Chen ever idle?

A wisp of sword qi suddenly emerged from his fingertip.

"I have already refined seven strands of Scarlet Firmament Sword Qi. Three years should be enough for me to refine another three strands."

Once I reach ten strands, that will be the minor success stage of the sword canon. For my proficiency panel, that will also allow me to break through a small level. Perhaps I will see additional changes.

Luo Chen was lost in thought.

At the same time, the main sinew of the Nether Serpent appeared in his hand.

"Caiyi wants a flying sleeve, or a ribbon, or a long whip as her lifebound artifact. This material is very suitable, and its attributes are fine."

"The only thing is, I lack the forging blueprint for this type of artifact."

"I need to go around and see if others have the corresponding blueprints. If I can obtain one, I can take the time in these three years to forge Caiyi's lifebound artifact—that would also be a good thing."

In just a short moment, Luo Chen had already planned out his three-year schedule at Lingtian Pass.

It had to be said, the *Soul-Splitting Art* that Han Zhan had left behind had greatly helped him.

Without the divided souls he had cut out, how could he now handle so many matters at once while still remaining at ease?

He would probably have to be like ordinary Sage cultivators, focusing all his energy on a single path of cultivation.

...

Just as the countless cultivators of Lingtian Pass had thought, ever since the Alchemy Sect arrived in the city, he had been like a divine dragon that showed its head but not its tail.

That one meeting upon entering the city had become the only opportunity to see him.

But that did not mean the Alchemy Sect had no presence!

A rumor spread that the young leader of the Luo Tian Sect, Baleful Dragonzi, held a large quantity of pills in his hands!

These pills were all personally refined by the Alchemy Sect, Luo Chen, and given to Baleful Dragonzi.

They included various third-order pills from Qi Refinement to Adept stages, with a wide range of uses—cultivation-type, auxiliary-type, and even the most scarce realm-breaking pills.

As long as Baleful Dragonzi agreed, one could exchange for the pills they needed from him at a relatively fair price.

But if he did not agree, no matter how high the price offered, one could not exchange for a single pill!

For a time, during their breaks, many cultivators would specifically run to the western district to seek an audience with Baleful Dragonzi.

Those without connections even began to befriend the other Luo Tian Sect cultivators, trying to get an introduction to contact Baleful Dragonzi.

As a result, the few dozen Luo Tian Sect cultivators gradually received different treatment within Lingtian Pass.

When they went out, they were surrounded by friends.

When they patrolled, other cultivator teams would often maintain close contact with them, keeping them from falling into extremely dangerous situations.

It might sound a bit exaggerated.

But the fact was, even if one did not seek pills, no one wanted to offend the Luo Tian Sect cultivators.

Getting familiar, building a good relationship—that was something everyone would do.

And this situation was exactly what Luo Chen wanted to see.

At first, he had thought of using a large batch of low-grade pills to strengthen his reputation and status among the scattered cultivators.

But after careful consideration, he realized that such a thing would not actually benefit him much.

The help those low-grade cultivators could provide him was too small.

On the contrary, if he could rely on his reputation and the advantage of pills to allow his disciples to build a wide network of connections, the returns would be even greater.

This situation, following precedent, was probably similar to how cultivators from the Azure Pill Valley and the Medicine King Sect would often receive courteous treatment whenever they went out.

Because they had the backing of their sect, the backing of Luo Chen, they were respected and treated with courtesy by everyone.

After delegating this matter, Luo Chen did not pay much attention to it.

He had handed the matter over to Zeng Yilong anyway, and also to fulfill Wang Yuan's teaching intentions, letting Zeng Yilong learn to discern good and evil in people's hearts through extensive social interactions, so he would not be deceived by a woman like he had been at the Battle Platform.

This day.

Luo Chen had just returned from the City Lord's Mansion, his expression relaxed.

The Lingtian City Lord was indeed a good man!

When he had "casually" mentioned that he had a Nether Serpent's main sinew but no corresponding method to use it, the other party had generously given him a forging blueprint.

It recorded a method for forging an artifact called the *Nether Serpent Whip*.

The main materials required were the main sinew of a jiao or serpent-type demon beast, and the Nether Water unique to the Nether Origin Sect.

Once this treasure was truly forged, not only would its power be vast, capable of corroding an enemy's artifact, but it could even briefly transform into the original form of the sinew's owner before death—truly wondrous.

For this, the Lingtian City Lord had even conveniently given Luo Chen three drops of Nether Water, which he could dilute and use.

"No wonder the Lingtian City Lord has such an excellent reputation—he is truly this generous!"

"Compared to him, I am sometimes a bit petty."

Luo Chen sighed with emotion as he eagerly wished to study the blueprint for the *Nether Serpent Whip*.

But just as he returned to his cave dwelling, he found the white-haired Xu Xiaoliu standing outside, looking as if he had been waiting for a long time.

"Supreme Elder, someone wants to see you."

"For pills?"

Luo Chen asked habitually. Recently, many people had wanted him to refine pills, including disciples sent by some Sage-level forces to make contact and probe.

However, most of them sought out Chu Kui and Zeng Yilong.

It was rare for someone to go through Xu Xiaoliu's connections.

Rare things were bound to be unusual.

Sure enough!

Xu Xiaoliu said in a low voice, "It's not for pills. They want to ask you to save someone."

Save someone?

Luo Chen's footsteps halted.

A moment later.

Luo Chen stood with his hands behind his back by the window, quietly waiting for the visitor.

Footsteps came from outside the mansion.

In his divine sense, it was a familiar face.

Still that cold and distant demeanor, but the former arrogance was long gone.

The footsteps gradually slowed, until Xu Xiaoliu respectfully pushed open the door for the visitor.

The figure hesitated at the doorway for a moment, then took a deep breath and strode in.

When she saw Luo Chen's back, the visitor's pupils contracted.

Then, biting her lip, she lifted her skirt and knelt on the ground.

"Icecastle's Jueqing pays homage to True Man Luo!"
