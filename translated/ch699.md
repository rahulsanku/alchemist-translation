# Chapter 699: Fierce Battle with Feiyun, Overlord Unarmoring

"I actually survived?"

Old Daoist Jiyuan, who in the past had possessed the bearing of an immortal like an old sage from storybook legends, was now in ragged clothes, hair and beard disheveled, looking utterly wretched.

But he had indeed survived!

That great black cauldron looked anything but ordinary.

At the very least, it had been a powerful existence at the level of a spirit treasure.

The self-detonation of such a treasure was simply not a might Sage cultivators could resist.

Yet Old Daoist Jiyuan had indeed survived.

It could even be said that not only he, but all the other Sage Sages on the cloud-sea plaza had survived.

Just as he was rejoicing in luck, a murmur sounded by his ear.

"Of course you lot were fortunate. It was only because we blocked the might of the Heaven-Refining Cauldron's self-detonation for you!"

Old Daoist Jiyuan froze, then saw a woman descend gracefully from the air, watching him with a smile that was not quite a smile.

A nightmare!

Old Daoist Jiyuan trembled all over. "Moon Wanderer, you..."

"There is no need to beg for mercy. I have heard those words from Penglai disciples far too many times." Moon Wanderer's smile remained, but the killing intent in her eyes grew increasingly intense.

At this moment, Fairy Jadecold moved over and blocked Moon Wanderer's path.

Seeing this woman, Moon Wanderer's smile gradually faded.

"Little sister, what are you doing? I am avenging you!"

Fairy Jadecold bit her lip and remained unmoved.

Moon Wanderer's expression gradually darkened. "Have you forgotten how Penglai Immortal Sect abandoned you and me back then, and even caused you to become the furnace cauldron of a Primordial Demon Sect elder?"

"Elder Sister, stop speaking. That person treated me well, and he helped me achieve the Sage realm. Besides, he is already dead." Fairy Jadecold's face was somewhat pale, and the body blocking Moon Wanderer was actually trembling subconsciously.

Moon Wanderer's face grew even gloomier. "That person is dead, and the Primordial Demon Sect is destroyed, but Penglai Immortal Sect still exists! I swore an oath that I would slaughter every remnant spawn of the Primordial Demon Sect and every Penglai disciple!"

Fairy Jadecold bit her lip and slowly raised the scroll in her hand.

"My apologies. Before departure, we made a Dao oath. I cannot abandon Senior Brother Jiyuan."

"Good!"

Moon Wanderer's expression sharpened. An ink-wash scroll floated up behind her, and on it there seemed to be beasts roaring and birds circling.

"Since you are so stubborn, I will kill Jiyuan first, then bring you back and discipline you properly!"

...

High in the sky.

Luo Chen looked at the quiet gray light and found that its aura seemed somewhat dimmer.

"The Heaven-Refining Cauldron's artifact spirit discovered that I carry Rosycloud's cultivation method, so it defied the Devil Lord's command and did not hesitate to self-detonate the Heaven-Refining Cauldron in order to destroy this gray light?"

He pondered.

His gaze swept the surroundings, and he nodded slightly.

The Heaven-Refining Cauldron had been a heaven-reaching spirit treasure. Even ruined beyond repair, the might of its self-detonation was absolutely not something ordinary people could withstand.

The reason the explosion had not completely destroyed Bright Radiance Heaven was clearly that other "people" had acted.

The ink diagram on Moon Wanderer should be the spirit treasure Myriad Beast Diagram.

In addition, the Light Palace and Dark Palace, still shining, also had powerful auras gradually lying dormant.

If his prediction was not wrong, they should also have spirit treasures suppressing them.

Perhaps they were even the Black Devil Jar and True Yang Crown the Heaven-Refining Cauldron's artifact spirit had mentioned before, powerful existences likewise at the rank of heaven-reaching spirit treasures.

It was precisely these existences containing artifact spirits acting at the same time that absorbed most of the Heaven-Refining Cauldron's self-detonation.

Only, because of that, all those troublesome people also survived.

For example, the one before him!

Wind and cloud rolled as a human figure leapt above the cloud sea, staring at Luo Chen like a tiger eyeing prey.

"Junior, move aside!"

Luo Chen laughed.

"You clearly know the power of my punch was no weaker than a Sage's strike, yet you still call me junior. Fellow Daoist, are you not overestimating yourself a little?"

Feiyunzi raised a brow, and his gaze toward Luo Chen turned wary.

But under the instinctive temptation of the faintly visible gray light behind Luo Chen, he could not care about that.

Because!

In that broken round mirror within the Artifact Realm's Weapon Mountain, there had vaguely been a scene of this gray light's birth.

It was something personally evolved by Devil Lord Heaven-Refining!

If he could obtain it, perhaps it would be the greatest opportunity of this journey!

"Golden Core junior, I will say this one last time. Move aside!" Feiyunzi shouted coldly, a cun of sword light appearing and disappearing at his fingertip, its sharp might cutting wind and splitting cloud.

Luo Chen was about to speak when he was interrupted by a shrill voice.

"Devil Lord Azure Yang, give me your life!"

Looking over, he saw an old woman transformed into human form rushing toward him, her wrinkled face filled with resentment.

"So it is Cape Heaven Matron!"

Luo Chen laughed.

Seeing this, Feiyunzi frowned.

Under the pincer attack of two Sage-level existences, this man could still laugh?

What exactly did he rely on?

Then another person's action explained everything.

A black-robed figure blocked Cape Heaven Matron.

"So you have a helper. No wonder you are so fearless." Feiyunzi suddenly understood.

Luo Chen glanced at Han Zhan. Although he did not know where Han Zhan had obtained a Sage-stage puppet to temporarily serve as a fleshly body, at this moment it had indeed helped him greatly.

Afterward, he withdrew his attention and focused entirely on the man before him.

"Name?"

"A dying man has no need to know my surname or given name!"

Before the words had fallen, Feiyunzi struck first.

The sword light in his hand suddenly erupted, releasing countless streams of sword qi.

The myriad sword qi was formless and traceless, visible only when it tore through wind and cloud.

Beneath this awe-inspiring sword momentum, Luo Chen was unafraid and pressed forward.

He slapped out with one palm, and the Azure Yang Great Handprint appeared once more in the mortal world!

The vast handprint, supported by mana, rumbled forward.

But after this palm shot out, Luo Chen frowned slightly.

His mana... seemed to have vanished by ninety percent out of thin air?

Could it be that too much mana had been consumed during fleshly nirvana?

At this moment, inside his body, the second Origin Pill had long since vanished, destroyed when the Nine-Refined Beacon Fire detonated it.

Only his lifebound Golden Core still hung within his remade qi sea.

He had not noticed it earlier, but now he discovered that his mana was far inferior to its peak state.

So much so that the Azure Yang Great Handprint possessed only its form and not its might.

The instant it collided with the formless sword qi, it shattered apart.

Hiss! Hiss! Hiss!

In an instant, terrifying sword qi descended upon Luo Chen.

In such a perilous situation, Luo Chen remained calm. The Xuanyan Armor unfurled, layers of qi armor spreading outward from within.

However, visible to the naked eye, these qi armors instantly collapsed before that formless sword qi.

"Sword qi specialized in breaking protective qi barriers and defensive types? Could this man be a pure sword cultivator?"

Thoughts flickered through Luo Chen's mind, and he formed a seal with one hand.

In an instant!

His body began to swell and expand wildly.

Boundless qi and blood washed through him. His bones cracked explosively, and frightening muscles like ancient gnarled roots coiled and spread until they wrapped his entire body.

Countless thick layers like horn covered skin as clear as jade.

His hands and feet enlarged in proportion, their palm lines and blue veins resembling those of an ancient giant.

As his body expanded, the black cuirass also extended wildly, enveloping Luo Chen completely within.

Whoosh!

A pair of crimson wings spread from his back, covering the sky and blotting out the sun.

In only one breath, a nine-zhang-tall giant stood above the cloud sea, treading clouds like an ancient divine being.

Those formless sword qi fell like a rainstorm.

But the result was merely ripples blossoming across his terrifying physique.

They could not stir a wave!

This strange phenomenon naturally drew gazes from all around.

Han Zhan, shrouded beneath the black robe, was inwardly startled. "Luo Chen's body-refinement art seems to have changed again in ways I do not know, and it has become powerful to this extent."

Blood Wanderer, who was chasing down Blood Nightmare Moluo, paused slightly and narrowed his eyes toward Luo Chen.

"I did not know that Azure Yang brat had such a fleshly body. It is very suitable for refining into a Blood God Child!"

Moon Wanderer, who was overpowering Fairy Jadecold and Old Daoist Jiyuan one against two, could not help glancing sideways as well.

In her peach-blossom eyes, rich interest appeared.

"This child came out of the Pill Palace, so he should have obtained Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's alchemy inheritance. But the Heaven-Refining Cauldron, noble as a heaven-reaching spirit treasure, preferred self-detonation rather than recognizing the inheritor as its master like the Myriad Beast Diagram. Something unexpected must have happened there."

"Still, an existence who obtained a Void Refinement True Lord's alchemy inheritance, if he can be used by me, may become my greatest aid in the future road to Ascendant."

"I must quickly resolve Old Daoist Jiyuan, then take this child before Blood Wanderer can."

With her exquisite mind, she naturally noticed Blood Wanderer's fixed gaze on Luo Chen.

It was said that three of Blood Wanderer's four great Blood God Children had been destroyed, leaving only one. He was probably thinking of refining Luo Chen into a Blood God Child.

Such an outstanding talent would truly be a pity if he became that muddled, beast-like thing.

Apart from these three, the others had no leisure to pay attention to Luo Chen.

The reason was simple: they were basically all being suppressed by these three, so where would they have the energy to pay attention to Luo Chen?

Feiyunzi, however, was the exception.

"So this is your confidence?"

Feiyunzi's expression changed slightly. Then he opened his mouth and spat, and a platinum sword pellet spun out from within.

A sword cultivator!

No one had ever imagined that the lord of Feiyun Ravine, famous in the Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance for artifact-forging, was also an orthodox sword cultivator!

Perhaps only this could explain why he dared personally enter the Sunken Sea without a helper of the same tier.

Because sword cultivators were the very symbol of strength!

The sword pellet appeared, and sword threads stretched out.

Feiyunzi flicked his finger!

The sword pellet pierced through the air, explosions ringing wherever it passed.

Not only that, the seals at his fingertips continued to change.

Whoosh! Whoosh! Whoosh!

When the sword pellet reached Luo Chen, its trajectory instantly became strange, suddenly left, suddenly right, suddenly up, suddenly down.

With every turn, streams of sword qi shot out.

The sword qi struck the nine-zhang giant's body, actually leaving pit after pit in the black cuirass.

The giant seemed unwilling to passively take the beating and began charging toward Feiyunzi.

"Want to fight up close?"

"Hmph!"

Feiyunzi sneered and transformed into sword light, shooting into the sky, his speed in no way inferior to the nine-zhang giant's stride.

Sword cultivators had always been famed for swift escape speed!

While keeping several li of distance from Luo Chen, he controlled the sword pellet to erupt with sword qi.

One wound after another appeared on Luo Chen's body.

Even with his powerful physique resisting them, these wounds were insignificant, but small wounds accumulated, and there would always be a moment when the great mansion collapsed.

Yet Feiyunzi was still dissatisfied.

Suddenly!

His figure halted, standing in midair as his hands formed seals.

"Sword array, rise!"

Clang! Clang! Clang!

Terrifying sword cries immediately resounded through heaven and earth.

The sword qi that had erupted before, which had already dispersed, suddenly reappeared in the void like lines, crossing vertically and horizontally to connect into a net.

Without anyone noticing, he had actually laid down a great sword-qi array!

"Sword array, open!"

Feiyunzi's hand seals paused, then his hands pulled horizontally through the void.

In the distance, a great net of sword qi unfurled in response, wrapping toward the nine-zhang giant from every direction.

"If you kept a small human form, my Heavenly Cloud Sword Net would require more effort to trap you. But at this moment, your enormous size is simply throwing yourself into the net."

Amid repeated cold laughter, a sword net covered Luo Chen.

The interwoven sword qi seemed to possess the might to cut through everything.

Luo Chen did not dare block it with his body and hurriedly retreated.

But behind him, another sword net appeared.

At this moment, above and below, it was as though all was net!

Hiss...

The sword nets crossed through.

With only one crossing, countless cracks appeared across Luo Chen's upper-grade defensive magic treasure, the Xuanchen Armor.

When the second round of sword nets appeared again, Luo Chen's expression grew solemn, and a wisp of azure flame erupted from his body.

Looking carefully, strands of golden light seemed to exist within that azure flame.

Under this azure flame, the second round of sword nets invisibly evaporated.

Seeing this, Feiyunzi's face grew slightly solemn.

"Fourth-tier true fire?"

He murmured, but he did not seem surprised.

For someone who could pass the Pill Realm inheritance, it would be strange if he did not possess a suitable source true fire.

And he had one too!

"Phew..."

He lightly exhaled, and a wisp of golden flame slowly flew out.

It was precisely White-Gold Xin Fire, gathered from the essence of metal and iron, best at melting gold and forging iron!

After many years of artifact-forging, Feiyunzi's use of source true fire had already reached a profound level.

He did not crudely use the White-Gold Xin Fire to attack the enemy. Instead, he attached it to the sword pellet and empowered the sword net.

As expected!

Sword nets filled with platinum-colored flames once more covered Luo Chen, and this time, the sword nets did not directly melt.

Although their cutting speed became much slower, they truly limited the other party's high-speed movement.

Feiyunzi struck while the iron was hot, his hands constantly forming seals as more and more sword nets appeared.

Later.

Within the sword array, the giant's figure became blurry and uncertain. Only azure-gold flames and platinum sword nets rose against each other.

"He is already a turtle in a jar. Now let me cut him down with one sword!"

Feiyunzi perceived the gap as the enemy's aura shifted and grasped empty air.

A long sword suddenly manifested.

Afterward, he transformed into light and escaped, entering the array himself and cutting down toward Luo Chen's head!

This sword was certain to succeed!

From far away came two fierce shouts.

"You must not!"

"Spare his life!"

Feiyunzi ignored them completely.

He only knew that he had failed the Artifact Realm's trial and had not obtained the Artifact Palace inheritance.

And the Golden Core junior before him had committed the crime of possessing a treasure, luckily taking the Pill Palace inheritance.

For Feiyunzi to seize it in his place was only heaven's law and earth's principle.

Whoosh!

The sword light was cold, cutting all things.

But just when Feiyunzi was certain of victory, he sensed that this sword seemed to have sunk into a muddy swamp.

Though it still continued forward, its speed grew slower and slower.

Spiritual light burst from his eyes, and amid the crisscrossing sword qi, he glimpsed the truth.

One hand was gripping the giant sword transformed from the sword pellet's true body.

"A mantis trying to stop a cart, overestimating yourself!"

Feiyunzi shouted explosively, and boundless mana poured out.

This time, the sharp sword broke through all obstruction like a knife cutting tofu, splitting the giant's arm in two and continuing toward the other party's enormous body.

Seeing this, Feiyunzi should have been delighted.

However.

The strange scene that entered his eyes caused his expression to change drastically.

The arm sliced into two halves sprouted countless bits of flesh and blood like living snakes and pythons, climbing back along the sword body toward his true body.

With bared fangs and brandished claws, they looked as if they would devour everything.

"Not good!"

Feiyunzi's mind shook greatly, and he tried to pull back his sword and retreat.

But coming had been easy; leaving was not so simple.

Within the sword array, Luo Chen's cold laughter boomed.

"Stay!"

Suddenly, a gigantic claw grabbed down from the cloud's edge.

Feiyunzi could not evade and was directly struck by the claw.

Luo Chen frowned. The other party had been hit by his long-hidden Cloud-Seizing Divine Claw, yet his momentum had not fallen.

"Does he have some powerful defensive magic treasure?"

He murmured, then looked at the sword nets binding him.

He stretched out a leg and stomped!

Boom!

The void exploded, and his body shot out like lightning.

When he appeared again, blood covered his body, but the positions of offense and defense had already reversed!

A strange power spread over him. His originally severed left arm began to recover continuously, and countless fine wounds across his body also healed rapidly.

Luo Chen paid no attention to this and looked with cold severity at Feiyunzi, who was frantically retreating.

As the twin wings on his back shook, he raced like mad thunder, the atmosphere sounding with repeated explosions.

Thunder Kick!

The giant foot kicked down like a thunderstorm.

Before the tiny Feiyunzi, layers of sword net unfolded, but in haste they could not withstand this kick.

Boom!

With only one kick, he fell onto the white jade plaza of the cloud sea.

Great clouds of mist surged and scattered in every direction.

Luo Chen kicked again!

The giant descended from the sky.

Boom!

Feiyunzi narrowly avoided this kick, and looking at the shocking giant footprint, he fled backward without regard for anything else. Not only that, while fleeing, he took out treasured magic treasures of his own.

But after losing the initiative, how could Luo Chen allow him to open the distance again?

One should pursue a cornered enemy with the courage that remains!

Broken Moon Wings supported Ninety Thousand Li, continuously erupting with extreme speed.

Moreover, while flying, he constantly launched attacks with both palms.

Mountain-Breaking Form!

One punch smashed apart a flying trident.

Mountain-Colliding Form!

He directly smashed a suddenly transformed enormous mountain peak into powder.

Amid drifting smoke and dust, Luo Chen stepped with Voidburst Step again and faced Feiyunzi's eyes directly.

"Where are you running?"

"You truly think you have me cornered?"

Feiyunzi's face was savage. His hands locked tightly together, forming a lotus seal, and an illusory golden bell appeared faintly above his head.

Luo Chen pressed forward.

"Is it this thing that saved your life?"

With a fierce shout, Luo Chen slapped one palm onto the golden bell's illusory image.

The powerful backlash nearly broke his bones and split his arm.

A true-artifact-level defensive heavy treasure!

Luo Chen's face turned ruthless. Giving the other party no chance to open distance again, he ran around the golden bell, afterimages appearing one after another.

Between his hands, attacks issued without end.

Sometimes he hooked with his fingers, sometimes he struck with his palm. Fist and claw alternated; one slap, one hook, countless exquisite body arts rose and fell in an instant.

With these movements, source power surged through his entire body and sank completely into the golden bell, staining it blood-red.

Suddenly!

Luo Chen's two palms slapped onto the golden bell's illusory image, and his ten fingers sank into the illusory golden bell as if entering wet brick and mud.

Overlord Removes Armor!

"Open for me!!!"

Under Feiyunzi's disbelieving gaze, the indestructible golden bell image cracked apart like a dream.

And the lotus seal he held also suddenly loosened, his mind instantly suffering backlash.

Pfft!

Spitting a mouthful of fresh blood, Feiyunzi looked as if struck by lightning and nearly fainted.

But he knew that at the juncture of life and death, he could not allow the slightest carelessness.

Seeing the giant palm grasp toward his body, Feiyunzi gritted his teeth and made a decision contrary to his own heart.

Whoosh!

Luo Chen's palm paused as he saw a streak of golden light fly out between his fingers.

Lowering his eyes, he saw that only a fleshly body remained in his palm.

As for that golden light...

A three-cun little man held a sword pellet in his mouth and hugged an ancient golden bell covered in cracks, glaring at him from the air with a face full of resentment.

"If not for resisting that storm, which damaged my true-artifact golden bell, how could you have broken my defense?"

Luo Chen grinned.

"With the effort you spend throwing out harsh words, you do not actually think you are safe right now, do you?"

As he spoke, a great cauldron rose from above his head.

Five-colored brilliance circulated without cease!

*(End of Chapter)*
