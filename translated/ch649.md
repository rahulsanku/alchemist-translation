# Chapter 649: Seven-Star Demon Hunter Luo Chen

Blood Wanderer was the first to leave, followed by Dragon Abyss True Being.

Although the latter used the name of Dragon Abyss Immortal City as his Dao title, to be honest, he rarely managed the city's ordinary affairs.

Even when it came to major matters of the Myriad Immortals Assembly, he rarely interfered.

By contrast, Moon Wanderer often inquired into many affairs of the Myriad Immortals Assembly. External contacts, and even the internal Demon Hunter Office, were all controlled by her.

After the two Sages left, Moon Wanderer leaned lazily against the back of her chair, her cool gaze sizing up the four people below.

Suddenly.

"Liu Ziweng, I hear you have been quite close to Blood Wanderer recently."

Plop.

What Luo Chen had not expected was that Angler immediately knelt on the ground, without the slightest bearing or integrity of a late Golden Core cultivator.

He trembled as he lay there. "Sage, you misunderstand. This little one only did missions for Blood Sage and received some rewards. This matter was not only done by this little one. Many people in the Myriad Immortals Assembly also..."

Luo Chen looked at this scene in astonishment, but gradually, from the expressions of the other two who seemed long accustomed to it, he understood.

Just as rumors said, although Blood Wanderer had founded the Myriad Immortals Assembly, he did not manage affairs and lived no differently from a wandering cultivator.

To him, the Myriad Immortals Assembly was only a tool. He did not need the people below to answer to him, and he did not need to answer to anyone.

The true person in charge of the Myriad Immortals Assembly was instead Moon Wanderer.

In daily management, most of the backbone strength in the Myriad Immortals Assembly leaned toward Moon Wanderer.

Among them were the three great eight-star demon hunters of the Demon Hunter Office.

Previously, Angler had shown signs of turning his elbow outward. At this moment, he naturally had to be beaten back into line.

After understanding all this, Luo Chen looked at Angler with a trace of pity.

Wandering cultivators were not so free either.

Or rather, all who desired something would be restrained by others.

"Enough. Chattering on and on, how annoying."

Once Moon Wanderer spoke, Angler immediately shut up and nimbly stood, though his back remained hunched. His aged face was actually drenched in cold sweat.

Watching this scene, Luo Chen roughly grasped some of this woman's temperament.

Her word was final. The people below had best not ramble in front of her, and should also not take certain matters too far.

After beating Angler back into line, Moon Wanderer's gaze drifted across the other three before finally landing on the youngest red-robed man.

"Raise your head."

Luo Chen's expression was calm as he raised his head and looked directly at the incomparably beautiful, graceful female cultivator above.

Within his body, the Microdust Origin Art continuously operated, doing its utmost to prevent him from being affected by the other party's invisible charm power.

He openly sized her up, and she naturally saw through him completely as well.

"A fine appearance, but a pity about all that filthy blood qi. It is exactly the same as Pang Bujue."

After commenting, Moon Wanderer went straight to the main topic.

"What star demon hunter are you now?"

"Five-star."

"Only five-star?" Moon Wanderer frowned, her beautiful eyes sweeping over Luo Chen's body. "With your ability, that should not be."

Purple Dowager spoke at the right time. "Reporting to Sage, Daoist Azure Yang has hunted many demon beasts on the Sunken Sea battlefield over the past year. The Great Demon King corpses he handed in alone numbered three. With his merits, he can already advance to six-star."

"Oh..."

A surprised sound came from Moon Wanderer's mouth, the final tone drawn out as her gaze once again flowed across Luo Chen.

"At only sixth-level Golden Core, you can casually slaughter Great Demon Kings. Are you that formidable?"

Luo Chen's mind tightened slightly. "It was only luck. Sage overpraises me."

"Hehe."

The woman laughed softly. "Quite modest... Little Zi, later, raise Daoist Azure Yang's star grade to seven-star."

"Ah!" Purple Dowager was startled, but quickly reacted. "As Sage commands."

Luo Chen was also very surprised. "Many thanks for Sage's favor, but Luo cannot accept reward without merit. How can I skip star grades for nothing and enjoy the Assembly's resources?"

Moon Wanderer continued smiling, which looked extremely out of place on her cool face.

"How can this be called accepting reward without merit? Your first merit is killing tens of thousands of demon beasts. Your second merit is bringing back intelligence that a Demon Emperor escaped Nightmara Heaven. Based on these two points, a mere seven-star rank rightly belongs to you."

At this point, her smile deepened, like a delicate flower blooming.

"Cultivate diligently. Although the Myriad Immortals Assembly is a wandering cultivator alliance, it has quite a few resources, and seven Sages oversee it. It is no weaker than any current Sage upper sect."

"If one day you advance to nine-star demon hunter, you can also receive the Sages' support in Sage-formation resources."

"Just like Dragon Abyss!"

Listening to these words, Luo Chen's eyes shone, and his body trembled slightly, as if he were too excited to restrain himself.

He bent and bowed, then shouted respectfully,

"Thank you, Sage, for your cultivation. Luo Hai will not fail Sage's expectations!"

Under the moonlight, a man and woman walked side by side.

The plump young matron in a purple dress smiled as she congratulated the red-robed man beside her.

"There are not too few seven-star and above demon hunters in the Myriad Immortals Assembly, perhaps seven or eight, but all of them are late Golden Core cultivators. Someone as young as Fellow Daoist, advancing to seven-star at the sixth level of Golden Core, is unique. Especially since you have entered the Sage's sight and gained her favor, Daoist Azure Yang, your future truly has no limit."

Luo Chen accepted her congratulations with considerable excitement, repeatedly speaking words praising Moon Wanderer.

What profound cultivation, no lower than Blood Wanderer. What peerless beauty, worthy of being the Northern Sea's number one beauty. What uniquely discerning eyes, seeing with a glance that he was an uncarved jade.

Words of this sort poured out without cost, showing his excitement.

At the end, he saluted. "Regarding my star grade advancement, I will trouble Fellow Daoist."

Purple Dowager waved her hand. "It is only a procedure. Tomorrow I will have someone send your new identity waist token to Peng Lake."

"There is one more matter I must trouble Fellow Daoist with."

"Hm?"

"When Blood Sage left, he ordered you to bring people to the Sunken Sea battlefield to clear out the demon race. You all know my situation. I spent nearly a year there and experienced countless battles. Not only am I physically tired and mentally exhausted, I also have many small injuries. Therefore, I hope that when you choose people later, you can skip me and give me some time to recover."

Hearing this, Purple Dowager could not help smiling faintly.

"I thought it was something major. This is only a small matter. I will mention it to Angler and the others. Fellow Daoist, return and rest well."

"In that case, many thanks."

Luo Chen cupped his hands, then took leave of her and hurried toward the eight hundred li of Peng Lake in the night.

After he left, Purple Dowager suddenly turned back to the Demon Hunter Office.

In a hall full of bamboo slips and records, she bowed behind Moon Wanderer.

"How was he? How did he react?"

"Just as before, incomparably excited. For wandering cultivators like him, advancing to the Sage stage is the greatest temptation, especially when Daoist Azure Yang is still so young."

"Hehe, with Dragon Abyss's precedent before him, who would not be tempted?"

"Right, he said he is tired and injured and does not want to go to the Sunken Sea battlefield again."

"Hm?"

Moon Wanderer's finger stopped on a bamboo slip, and doubt seemed to appear on her face.

"That should not be. Logically speaking, after being exceptionally promoted to seven-star demon hunter, he should seize the opportunity to carry out missions, consolidate his reputation, and work hard toward nine-star. Why stop?"

Purple Dowager hesitated. "Perhaps he truly is injured and tired?"

Moon Wanderer slowly shook her head, her long eyelashes narrowing faintly.

"This seat underestimated this person's composure."

Purple Dowager seemed to understand and suggested, "Should we forcibly conscript him, using Blood Wanderer's name?"

Moon Wanderer thought for a moment, then continued flipping through the important intelligence the Myriad Immortals Assembly had collected over recent years.

"Let him go for now. I just gave him a sweet date. If I make him risk his life before he has even swallowed it, others will look down on this seat."

Peng Island.

Star Dou Hall bloomed with light once again.

After bathing and changing clothes, Luo Chen sat within, refreshed. He exhaled a turbid breath as if a heavy burden had been lifted.

The Microdust Origin Art circulated through one great heavenly cycle. With his mind clear, he looked back on the brief meeting with the Sages and finally relaxed.

"There should have been no major mistakes. I said what should be said, and did not say what should not be said."

The excitement on his face had long since vanished.

In its place was calm indifference.

Moon Wanderer's act might tempt other Golden Core cultivators, but in his eyes, it was incomparably familiar.

It was nothing but the methods of a superior controlling subordinates.

Draw a great cake and urge others to risk their lives.

Moon Wanderer was still fairly proper. Before the great cake was fulfilled, she first gave him a little sweetness, exceptionally raising his demon hunter star grade to seven-star.

To obtain the status of seven-star demon hunter at the sixth level of Golden Core was also a first in the Myriad Immortals Assembly.

But who was Luo Chen?

He was also someone who had taken a wandering cultivator force composed of only a few late Qi Refinement cultivators and led it all the way to the level of a major Golden Core sect. He had also once used the great cake of "Foundation Establishment" to make hundreds and thousands of people run and risk their lives for him.

He was long familiar with power games and controlling subordinates.

Most of his previous excitement had merely been acted.

As for why it was only most, it was because a small portion was genuine joy.

Seven-star demon hunter meant he could enjoy a seventy-percent discount in many of his transactions within the Myriad Immortals Assembly.

This included the rent for Peng Lake spiritual land.

The only thing that made Luo Chen somewhat uneasy was probably that final matter.

He did not know whether Moon Wanderer would question Purple Dowager and use this to see through his true thoughts.

But even if he had to do it again, Luo Chen would still act this way.

A Sage's favor was not necessarily a good thing for him. If he truly fully relied on the other party, many matters would be beyond his control.

Moreover, his sword-testing journey was already over. He did not want to engage in meaningless battles.

After all, he was not a battle fanatic like Dao Lan.

Oh, Dao Lan had also capsized. Walk along the riverbank often enough, and how could your shoes not get wet? Who knew whether he was still alive now?

Luo Chen's plan was simple. Before the ancient cultivator ruins of the Sunken Sea opened, he would seize the time to settle himself further. If he could smoothly break through to Ancient Wilderness Fourth Tier, that would be best.

"Time. I need enough time!"

Luo Chen's eyes were bright, and in his heart, he had already begun planning his next cultivation schedule.

He had to cross the final step of body refinement at all costs and add another trump card for the Sunken Sea trip.

A trump card that could serve as a king-bomb.

*(End of Chapter)*
