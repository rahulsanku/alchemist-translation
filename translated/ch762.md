# Chapter 762: The Sword Shatters Purple Yang, Suppressing All Heroes by Force

The blood sword shot into the sky, sword light spraying like rain.

The chaotic spiritual qi of heaven and earth all around seemed to have found its backbone, and all of it rushed toward that sky-flooding sword light.

Within the sword light like a tornado, a white figure flickered in and out of sight, its true appearance impossible to see clearly.

But that earlier laugh, contemptuous and disdainful, was immediately recognized.

"Wilderness Wanderer!"

Yu Xu cried out softly, her face deathly pale.

Whoosh!

A figure appeared beside her. Without another word, he grabbed her arm. It was Sage Heavenly Mountain.

He wasted no breath and spat out only one word.

"Go!"

A small beast like a pangolin flew out from his body. In the indistinct moment, a great mouth opened, swallowed him and Yu Xu inside, then closed. With a turn, it instantly burrowed into the earth.

Elsewhere.

Because he had privately collected illusion cores earlier, Shi Ju, who had been faintly surrounded at the center by the crowd, did not take advantage of the gap that had appeared in the encirclement to leave. Instead, he raised his eyes with great interest and looked over.

What entered his sight was all piercing sword light, but in his pupils there was only the illusory figure flickering within that majestic sword light.

"So that is Wilderness Wanderer?"

Qi Xiongcheng clamped the great gourd under his arm. His face was grave as he nodded, and his tone held even a trace of fear.

"He has grown stronger again!"

Shi Ju shook his head instead. "It is not that he has grown stronger. This person has been accumulating power for a long time, and only by fully unleashing all his magic power can he create such overwhelming might. But once this move passes, he will certainly fall into a state of emptied qi and exhausted strength. At that time, it will be the best chance for me to take him down and seize the Heavenly Secret Pill!"

Qi Xiongcheng started, then looked carefully. It was indeed so!

In truth, everyone present also discovered this point.

Such an enormous disturbance was absolutely not something a Sage could casually create.

The other party had poured out all his magic power. Clearly, he wanted to settle everything in one stroke.

Aside from Yu Xu and Sage Heavenly Mountain, who had witnessed Luo Chen's power before, no one present wanted to flee.

In everyone's heart, the three words "Heavenly Secret Pill" surfaced.

That was a pill personally refined by the Alchemy Saint. Whether it was taken by a true Sage disciple of this world or brought back to offer to their own ancestor, it would be greatly beneficial.

They only needed to withstand this one blow. Afterward, Luo Chen, whose magic power would be depleted, would be at their mercy!

Especially!

The one who hated Luo Chen most at this moment was not them, but Gu Daosu, who had been plotted against, ambushed, and had his state of sudden enlightenment broken.

The Wuwang Treasure Mirror in his hand was growing brighter and brighter, enough to show the anger in his heart at this moment.

He would stand at the very front!

No one advanced and tried to interrupt Luo Chen.

Because no one knew when the other party would release this sword.

At such a distance, if they went halfway and he suddenly chopped down on their heads, or if he concentrated the attack on the bird that stuck its head out first, that would be far too poor a trade.

Everyone was waiting.

Waiting for that sword to fall.

Clearly, Luo Chen would not make them wait long.

Because the illusion beasts that had survived all around, under the earlier slaughter and the terrifying sword light at this moment, had already begun to scatter and flee in every direction.

These were all his prey. How could he bear to let all of them run?

Only a long cry could be heard from the air.

"Slash!"

The next instant!

Majestic sword light crashed down.

Between heaven and earth, everything was blood-red!

Everyone acted at once, resisting the might of this sword.

Gu Daosu held the treasure mirror in one hand, illuminating heaven and earth, and a pillar of light as thick as a water bucket erupted, facing that sword head-on.

Gu Chen leaped forward and landed in front of Gu Yuan. He raised both hands toward the sky, and his golden robe swelled madly, rolling heaven and earth backward from behind him to become a golden curtain of light blocking before the two of them.

Shi Ju snorted coldly, folded his fan shut, and slapped one palm against the treasure gourd under Qi Xiongcheng's arm. The gourd trembled, spitting out a sheet of dazzling golden radiance that covered the two of them.

The others also displayed their respective abilities.

The Wang clan elder tapped his bamboo staff repeatedly, and a bamboo forest rose from the ground.

Sage Dragon Abyss and Mingyan exchanged a look. Their twin swords joined as one, using offense in place of defense and rushing upstream.

Facing all this, Luo Chen turned a blind eye.

His hands came together, fingers like a sword, controlling the Yuantu blood sword as it descended openly and without restraint.

The first to bear the brunt was the light shot out from that treasure mirror.

Hiss!

With only a single contact, the treasure mirror light that had previously overturned a white eagle comparable to a mid-stage Sage was abruptly cut in two.

The remaining force of the sword light did not cease and continued falling.

Seeing this scene, everyone froze.

Was this sword truly so powerful?

But they were given almost no time to react.

Hiss! Hiss! Hiss!

The piercing sword light swept across a hundred li like a knife cutting tofu.

The sword qi released by the joined twin swords was like the difference between fireflies and the bright moon beneath this blood-colored sword light, shattering in an instant.

When Sage Dragon Abyss saw this, his face changed drastically. Without a second thought, he wrapped an arm around the waist of the woman beside him and pushed one palm outward, pouring out his vast magic power in full.

Boom!

He spat blood, his hair coming loose, and fell toward the earth in a wretched state while holding Mingyan.

Elsewhere.

The green bamboo forest was cut cleanly in half at the waist.

The verdant bamboo staff in the Wang clan elder's hand actually cracked with a snap, a long fissure appearing down its middle.

Before he had time to feel pain over this magic treasure he had painstakingly refined, his attention was drawn by a scream behind him.

He turned and looked, his eyes widening, almost splitting with rage.

Without the bamboo forest's cover, the Young Master was submerged in the blood sword, his face full of despair.

The Wang clan elder only had time to shout, "Activate the illusion chess piece and leave the grotto-heaven!"

Even in agony, that youth remained calm and activated the illusion chess piece.

Black and white light flowed outward, wrapping around him and turning into a vortex as he was about to break through the realm and depart.

But just as he was about to leave, a streak of blood-colored sword light actually chased in after him.

Boom!

Gu Chen staggered backward, his face flushed scarlet.

Yet just as he retreated before Gu Yuan, he gritted his teeth and spat out a mouthful of blood essence.

The golden curtain of light before him, riddled with holes, immediately closed as good as new and covered the two of them inside.

When Gu Yuan saw this scene, even his originally calm expression could not help changing.

"Gu Chen, how could this be?"

He understood better than anyone the power of Gu Chen's golden robe.

It was called the Golden Iron Robe, and it had been forged in its entirety from a piece of high-grade meteorite ore bestowed by the clan. It could merge a cultivator's magic power with the strength of blood and qi.

In battle, its sleeves struck like iron maces.

In defense, its golden curtain was like a wall of bronze and iron.

At the moment it had first been forged, it had already been a rare high-grade magic treasure.

After Gu Chen painstakingly refined it for several hundred years, when he advanced to the Sage stage, the clan's experts had even helped him catalyze it in one breath to the level of a true artifact.

Especially when paired with Gu Chen's dual cultivation of law and body, its might was extraordinary!

How could merely resisting a streak of sword light bring it to this?

Gu Chen's face was grave, and his arms trembled faintly.

"That sword is no ordinary thing!"

That sword?

Gu Yuan was stunned.

Above the shattered Three-Rainbow Falls, Gu Daosu's expression changed slightly. The treasure mirror in his hand had actually been shaken free by the terrifying force.

On his handsome, picturesque face, a trace of blood had even been carved by the piercing sword qi.

Within his eyes, the majestic sword light was still descending without end.

"What magic treasure is this?"

"Su'er, do not lose focus!"

A cry of alarm came from behind him. At some point, Madam Xu had already stood up. She bit open her finger and smeared fresh blood across the bronze mirror.

Seeing this scene, Gu Daosu's expression changed drastically.

"Aunt, you!"

"I used that secret art to raise my realm. From here on, I am already of no use to you and will only become a burden. At this moment, let me use this last measure of strength to perish together with that wretch!"

The Wuwang Treasure Mirror rose again, and mirror light reappeared.

A dazzling pillar of light burst out from the mountain, shooting straight toward the source of that sword light.

Gu Daosu watched this scene. His mouth opened, but he said nothing more.

To perish together was likely impossible.

How terrifying and unrivaled was that sword!

If they could block it, that would already be no easy thing. How could they speak of mutual destruction?

But in the next moment, delight appeared in his eyes.

Within his field of vision, that blood-colored sword light actually swerved away from the treasure mirror's light and fell toward Shi Ju.

In this way, with nothing left to block the treasure mirror's light, it could just happen to attack Luo Chen, who was in the sky forming hand seals and driving the flying sword.

"Hm?"

Shi Ju looked at the descending sword light in confusion.

"It doesn't go after Gu Daosu, but comes for me instead? However, if you want to scheme against me, you are gravely mistaken."

He slapped the Purple Yang Gourd again. Blazing fire spat out, actually forming a golden crow in the air.

The golden crow circled him once, raised its head, and pounced toward the brilliant sword light.

The moment the two touched, a scene like accumulated snow melting appeared.

Great quantities of blood-colored sword light vanished into the air.

Seeing this, Shi Ju smiled coldly.

"Blood-colored and filthy. One look tells me it is a treasure of the demonic path. This gourd of mine has accumulated quite a bit of Solar True Flame, and it counters these crooked ways best."

Hiss, hiss, hiss...

When the golden crow scattered, the originally majestic sword light had little left.

A savage-looking blood-colored longsword, containing terrifying killing intent, revealed its true body.

"Out of tricks already? Is this the limit of your skill?" Shi Ju licked his lips, greed showing in his eyes as he reached out toward that blood sword.

With his vision, he could naturally see how extraordinary this sword was.

Even if it was not a spirit treasure, its killing power was absolutely no lower than those spirit treasures that were not attack-oriented.

If he could capture it, he might gain another arm of strength.

Great amounts of golden flame began to spread over his extended hand. This was the confidence that let him dare seize a bare blade with his empty hand!

Just then.

"Go!"

A light shout sounded by his ear, and that blood sword suddenly turned, bypassing his flaming hand with exquisite precision.

Afterward, before everyone's eyes, the sword slashed toward that huge gourd.

Qi Xiongcheng's expression changed slightly, and he subconsciously reached out to block it.

Crack!

His right arm was severed through the middle.

The blood sword did not pause at all and landed on the huge gourd with one strike.

Under Shi Ju's stunned gaze and Qi Xiongcheng's caught-off-guard stare, the Purple Yang Gourd seemed to suffer a heavy blow.

Cracks spread continuously like the pattern on a turtle shell.

Until...

Bang!

The gourd abruptly shattered, and great masses of white mist instantly surged out. Within it, illusion cores spun madly one after another, fleeing in every direction.

"You bastard!"

Shi Ju could no longer keep calm and immediately cursed aloud.

These were all the illusion cores he and Qi Xiongcheng had spent twenty years collecting!

Without the Purple Yang Gourd sealing them, what were they supposed to do now?

Qi Xiongcheng was frantically using methods to catch back the fleeing illusion cores.

But since they had broken free of their cage, how could those illusion cores enter captivity again?

Borrowing the boundless cloud and mist, the illusion cores dragged long tails like meteors and flew in every direction.

"Freeze!"

Sage Dragon Abyss mustered his remaining strength, fixed one illusion core in place, and grasped it in his hand.

"Go!"

Mingyan looked furiously toward Luo Chen in the distance. "He has already exhausted his qi and strength. Are we really going to let Wilderness Wanderer go?"

Sage Dragon Abyss said urgently, "A Northern Sea rogue cultivator has many methods. How could he be so easy to kill? I am heavily injured now and no longer have the strength to fight. Leaving is the best plan!"

Mingyan could feel her companion's aura continually decline, and no longer dared delay.

Only before leaving, she still shot Luo Chen a hateful look.

It was precisely this look that made her lovely face lose all color.

The treasure mirror beam that had pierced through the air had clearly stabbed through Luo Chen's body, but in a daze, Luo Chen's figure appeared again in the void.

The treasure mirror light instead landed on a great mountain behind him.

Silently and soundlessly, the great mountain turned to powder and rose behind Luo Chen.

Mingyan's red lips parted wide in disbelief.

It missed?

"How could it miss?"

Gu Daosu looked at this scene, his eyes full of disbelief.

The Wuwang Treasure Mirror had the marvelous function of locking onto enemies with divine sense. Perhaps its strength could prove insufficient, but the attacks it released very rarely missed.

"Perhaps my consciousness was unclear, and my divine sense failed to keep him locked."

Madam Xu sighed, holding the treasure mirror in both hands, her face ashen.

"Su'er, from here on, you can only rely on yourself. Your aunt cannot hold on any longer."

As she spoke, the illusion chess piece flew out, turning into black and white light that enveloped her, and she slowly vanished.

Before leaving, she transmitted a warning. "Without me protecting you, this place cannot be stayed in for long. Although that Wilderness Wanderer set up a trap to harm you, that sword was aimed at Shi Ju. What he covets is clearly the illusion cores and spiritual resonance. After I leave, do not seek trouble with him. Find a quiet place, use the illusion cores I left behind, and try again to comprehend the true meaning of the laws. You are only a step from crossing the threshold. There is no need to quarrel with them out of pride..."

Gu Daosu watched the vortex disappear in the air, his expression extremely ugly.

His gaze fell toward the white mist beyond the mountain, and toward the man who still stood in the void, white robes drifting.

Even if he was unwilling, he could only follow his elder's words and quietly retreat.

Just after he left, two explosive shouts rang out from below the mountain.

"Wilderness Wanderer!"

"I want you dead!"

The Wang clan elder shot into the sky with bloodshot eyes, while Shi Ju burst out of the mist with a face full of anger.

Not only that, the two Gu family members also stepped out one step at a time from the white mist filling the sky.

All of them stared at Luo Chen, every strand of their divine sense locking onto him.

There was even a giant eagle that struggled to flap its wings up from the mountain hollow and fly toward Luo Chen.

That earlier sword had covered such a vast area that it had not targeted only these Sage cultivators.

Those illusion beasts had also been targets!

Aside from the white eagle and a small number of somewhat stronger illusion beasts, all the other illusion beasts had had their forms shattered.

This was also why, after the sword light swept through, the area for several hundred li around them was still shrouded in boundless white mist.

On a mountain peak.

Two figures slowly appeared.

Yu Xu's little face was deathly pale, full of lingering fear.

Where exactly had Fu Qinglan found this Dao protector? The might of a single sword was actually so terrifying!

It was only that the sword had not locked onto any one person alone, and its power had been scattered considerably.

Otherwise, among everyone present, not a single one would have been able to withstand it alone.

Even so, it had still heavily injured one person and eliminated one true Sage disciple from an aristocratic clan from the grotto-heaven.

If she knew that Madam Xu also could not hold on and had been forced to leave, the fear in her heart would likely be even greater.

But even as it was, her heart pounded wildly, and fear lingered.

"Fellow Daoist Heavenly Mountain, fortunately your judgment was excellent and you brought me out."

Sage Heavenly Mountain looked at the withered Pangolin Gu in his hand and shook his head.

"With the lesson before me, how could I repeat the same mistake?"

He remembered very clearly!

Back when he and Wrathful Vajra had joined hands to besiege Luo Chen, apart from a defensive magic treasure, the other party had not used any other magic treasure at all.

This time, the other party had driven his flying sword so solemnly, and had even accumulated power for so long. It was easy to imagine just how terrifying the might of that sword would be.

At the very least, it was above that Fire Phoenix and Flame Dragon!

Only, after seeing the result of this sword suppressing all the Sages, he discovered that he had still underestimated this person.

"Fellow Daoist, then should we take advantage of Wilderness Wanderer's lack of magic power and go besiege him?"

"Lack of magic power?"

Sage Heavenly Mountain laughed despite himself and raised a hand to point.

Yu Xu looked in the direction he indicated and saw the mist filling the sky, like a qi dragon, continuously gathering toward the great lake beneath Luo Chen's feet.

"How does that look like someone lacking magic power and unable to fight again?"

Yu Xu froze.

By her ear came Sage Heavenly Mountain's sigh.

"Moreover, do not forget that he still has a terrifying physique capable of tearing Wrathful Vajra apart by hand. Even at his worst, before he dies, he is enough to drag others down with him. We had best not involve ourselves in these muddy waters."

...

"Ghosts and Gods Give Way truly is useful. It throws divine sense into chaos and avoids locking, especially when facing these long-range attacks."

Luo Chen smiled faintly and reached out to grasp.

Whoosh!

A smear of bloodlight tore through the clouds and mist and fell into his hand.

Holding the longsword, Luo Chen looked toward the enemies approaching from several directions.

The Wang clan elder, who had lost the true disciple he was protecting, had bloodshot eyes and wished he could hack him into ten thousand pieces.

Shi Ju, a prodigy of a Great House whose gourd had been broken and whose decades of bitter labor had been lost in a single morning.

The Gu family's Dao protector, in complete condition and watching like a tiger, wore a golden robe, his fighting intent blazing.

There was also an incomparably furious illusion beast white eagle that cried incessantly, its powerful aura drawing sidelong glances.

Luo Chen shook his head.

"If this were an ordinary time, Luo would not mind exchanging a few pointers with all of you, but right now, there is no need."

He beckoned with his hand.

The great lake beneath his feet rumbled loudly.

Boundless lake water parted, and a giant cauldron broke out of the lake. Great quantities of cloud and mist were surging into the giant cauldron.

Inside the cauldron, more than ten illusion cores could be faintly seen struggling madly, but they were being forcibly suppressed by the treasure cauldron driven by an illusory soul.

"That should be about enough."

Luo Chen murmured. The treasure cauldron instantly shrank, and the divided soul returned to his sea of consciousness.

Without looking at those enemies who had sensed something wrong and were charging over frantically, he tossed out a formation disk.

Nine heavens and ten earths, transposition in an instant!

Hazy azure light shot out, and Luo Chen stepped into it.

Boom!

In only one breath, the azure light shot into the sky.

Overwhelming golden flames swept over, and the terrifying high temperature instantly evaporated the lake water below.

However, there was already no trace of Luo Chen in the surroundings.

*(End of Chapter)*
