# Chapter 978: Fate Soul Defective, Fierce Battle with Azure Frost

Humans are born with souls and spirits.

The soul governs the spirit; the spirit governs the flesh.

The soul is divided into three—Heaven, Earth, and Human attributes. Among these, the human soul is also known as the fate soul in common cultivation knowledge.

The Heaven and Earth souls remain outside the body year-round, while the fate soul dwells alone within.

The process of a qi cultivator's training is one of continuous transformation and growth of the divine soul. Only when the three souls of Heaven, Earth, and Human are unified into one can it be called the primordial spirit, reaching the state where the body may perish but the self does not die.

Before that point, once a cultivator dies, their soul and spirit will naturally scatter. Even if they have a Nascent Soul as a secondary parasitic body, it merely delays this process.

The reason why low-realm cultivators who transform into ghost cultivators often have clouded intellect is precisely because their fate soul has been destroyed, leaving only the external Heaven and Earth souls to form a simple shell. Only through subsequent cultivation can those ghost cultivators gradually recover a sliver of fate soul, thereby possessing their own consciousness.

It can be said that among the three souls, the fate soul is the paramount importance for a qi cultivator, never to be lost.

When Mo Shouzhuo abruptly uttered the words, "Luo Chen, do you still want your fate soul?" everyone present understood the implications contained within.

And the one who reacted fastest was not Luo Chen, far away in the Yellow Spring Sea, but Rosycloud, who was right beside him!

Her expression darkened, and without hesitation, she struck first.

Target: Mo Shouzhuo!

A palm shot out, the Nirvana Sacred Flame moving with it.

Faced with this killing move, Mo Shouzhuo's eyelids twitched as he hastily retreated.

Behind him, Zhan Buxiu, Liancheng, and the others tacitly surged forward, blocking his path as they each employed their own methods to counter Rosycloud's attack.

*Boom!*

With just one exchange, the hazy spiritual light constructing the great formation began to shake.

Following that came Rosycloud's even more ferocious attacks, surging like tidal waves.

Meanwhile, in the distance, Luo Chen emerged from the Yellow Spring Sea, his entire body bathed in a Withering-Bloom Fire Robe.

He looked darkly toward the distance.

"Patriarch Mo, please explain yourself!"

With his fellow sect members temporarily bearing the pressure for him, Mo Shouzhuo became much more composed. He said calmly, "Seeing that your cultivation is nearly approaching late-stage Sage, you must have also been about to discover that your fate soul is defective, right?"

Luo Chen seemed to sense something was coming. His body, which he usually controlled with perfect ease, began trembling uncontrollably.

The ninth soul had been unable to be split off for a long time. Even though his main soul had already expanded to its limit, he still dared not forcibly split the ninth soul. His intuition told him that once he took that step, everything would be irreversible.

He had long since cultivated the *Soul-Splitting Art* to the perfect level. Over the years on Soul Island, he had even reached great perfection, far surpassing Han Zhan back then. Yet he often felt a sense of loss, as if something was missing, inferior to Han Zhan's minor achievement back in the day. He had thought it was due to the incomplete cultivation method, but now it seemed that was not the reason at all.

Moreover!

Having split off eight divided souls, his divine soul foundation had long surpassed any late-stage Sage Grand Cultivator in the world. In fact, judging by the nearly tenfold increase in his divine sense coverage range, he was infinitely close to an Ascendant-level great power.

Under such circumstances, his inability to condense a true primordial spirit was truly baffling.

And all these baffling things seemed to find their answer in the face of Mo Shouzhuo's words.

Luo Chen walked out of the Yellow Spring Sea step by step, his gaze fixed firmly on Mo Shouzhuo.

"When?"

"At the time you joined the Star Gate, at the moment you entered the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven." Mo Shouzhuo said frankly.

*Chi, chi, chi!*

The azure Withering-Bloom Fire Robe, without any wind, suddenly expanded, transforming into wisps of flame that enveloped Luo Chen, obscuring the changing expression on his face.

Only his murmuring voice could be heard from within.

"No wonder..."

No wonder that the moment he entered the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven back then, his divine soul had convulsed, falling into a brief coma.

Yet Fu Qinglan, whom he had been protecting, with a divine soul foundation and cultivation realm inferior to his, seemed completely unaffected.

At that time, Fu Qinglan had explained that every cultivator entering the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven for the first time would be washed over by the residual consciousness of the Mirage Dragon, thus falling into a coma. Only by relying on the "pass" at that time—the illusion chess pieces Mo Shouzhuo had distributed to everyone in advance—could one escape the illusion constructed by the Mirage Dragon's consciousness.

As for Fu Qinglan and the other Dao Sect cultivators, they were different. They had undergone a baptism of Mirage Dragon Qi within the sect beforehand, so they did not fall into a coma.

Thinking about it now, there were many flaws in that explanation!

A baptism of Mirage Dragon Qi was not a baptism of Mirage Dragon consciousness.

No matter how familiar one was with it, how could one be completely unaffected?

At the time, his attention had been focused on the Heavenly Secret Pill that Alchemy Saint Chu Yan had given him before ascending, and he had not believed that the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect would harm them, their Dao protectors. So he had not given it much thought.

But now, connecting the dots, he had finally arrived at the answer.

At the moment he entered the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, he had not only been washed over by the residual consciousness of the Mirage Dragon but had also had a portion of his fate soul intercepted by Heavenly Origin Sect Leader Mo Shouzhuo, using the illusion chess piece!

And this was the true culprit that had caused his divine soul convulsion and the current defect in his fate soul!

"Despicable!"

"It's not despicable. You agreed to join the Star Gate, which meant your life and death were destined to be on Heaven-Earth Peak. That intercepted portion of your fate soul would have been returned at any time, not affecting your breakthrough to the Ascendant realm. It was you! You, Luo Chen, who first betrayed the Star Gate, betrayed the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!"

"Enough talk. What must I do to get back that wisp of my fate soul?"

Faced with this question, Mo Shouzhuo had no time to answer.

Because...

*Bang!*

A violent explosion sounded, followed by Liancheng tumbling like a rolling gourd.

Spitting out a mouthful of blood, Liancheng's expression was panicked.

"Senior Brother, help quickly!"

Could the four of them not even suppress Rosycloud?

His conversation with Luo Chen had lasted only a few breaths, but in that short time, Rosycloud had gone berserk. Even within the great formation, even while facing four Ascendants simultaneously, she still dominated, unstoppable.

Mo Shouzhuo slowly extended his hand, blocking an attack from Rosycloud that was pursuing Liancheng, while simultaneously issuing an order to Luo Chen.

"Kill Azure Frost, and your fate soul will be returned! If not, you will return to the Yellow Spring right now!"

"Fine!"

Without any hesitation, a sudden explosion resounded within the vast Yellow Spring Sea.

Amidst falling sparks, Luo Chen's figure had already vanished.

When he reappeared, he was beside the woman in azure robes.

A punch was thrown!

*Smack!*

Azure Frost calmly caught Luo Chen's thick fist, shaking her head coldly.

"That fate soul of yours may not necessarily be in his hands right now."

"I dare not gamble!" Luo Chen's expression was fierce.

Azure Frost withdrew her wrist, then released it, sending Luo Chen flying back with overwhelming force.

As her azure hair fluttered, her eyes were calm.

"But you are no match for me!"

Luo Chen grinned. "Tying you up, keeping you from influencing their battle—I can always manage that."

Even as he spoke, his attacks did not cease.

Stepping with Moon Step, his figure flickered as he began kicking out afterimages around Azure Frost.

Azure Frost frowned. She could only set aside her intention to go help Rosycloud and instead focus on dealing with Luo Chen.

And as soon as they engaged, she noticed some changes in him compared to before.

Faster!

Stronger!

Tougher!

As they exchanged kicks in midair, the collisions actually brought her a slight sensation of pain.

This was a pain she had never felt in all her years of body refinement—pain inflicted by another.

*Smack! Smack! Smack!*

Another exchange of palms forced them both back.

Azure Frost said in surprise, "You've gotten stronger again?"

Luo Chen let his hands hang down, wisps of red-black color beginning to spread at his fingertips. He said calmly, "People change. Not just you, but me as well."

Azure Frost nodded slightly. That was not wrong.

She was now only one step away from the Ascendant realm.

And Luo Chen's transformation over the years was also clearly visible.

The first time they fought, he had no power to resist, silently taken in by her Sleeve Universe.

The second time, he had the strength to resist, even using his Withering-Bloom Divine Fire to injure her at one point, but in the end, it was just a desperate struggle, and she captured him.

And this third time!

"You are worthy of my full strength."

Luo Chen smiled slightly. "I also want to verify the gains from my cultivation over the years."

As the words fell, they both struck simultaneously.

The void exploded with a bang, leaving only afterimages in place. When they reappeared, their figures had already converged.

From the moment they set foot on Soul Severing Cliff, both Luo Chen and Azure Frost had realized that spells and techniques were not very effective here, subject to great suppression.

They were not Ascendant existences like Rosycloud and the Five Elders of Heavenly Origin, who could resist that suppression.

Therefore, close combat was the best method of fighting.

This understanding had become their current unspoken agreement.

However, this meant that while the battle might not be as spectacular as the other battlefield, its ferocity and danger were ten or even a hundred times greater.

*Bang!*

With a dull thud, Luo Chen, without his Mystic Dust Armor protecting him, had his chest caved in, his back bulging outward.

He reached back and clawed down from her slender, tough arm.

*Shua!*

Five red-black claw marks were clearly visible, along with a faint, lingering stench of rot around the nose.

Azure Frost's expression changed slightly. She shook her arm.

The claw marks vanished, but the stench still lingered.

Potent poison!

"Poison arts? Is this the reason your body refinement has progressed so quickly?"

Even as she spoke, she closed in, fingers pressed together like a sword, stabbing directly at Luo Chen's vital points.

Luo Chen's qi and blood surged. As his source power circulated, his chest healed. Facing the stabbing finger-sword, he used Moon Step to retreat repeatedly, then turned and slammed his elbow into her.

Mountain Lean Style!

*Pfft!*

An extreme sensation of pain came from his shoulder. Looking closely, a bloody hole was shocking to behold.

And Azure Frost was not faring well either. She was sent flying backward, her knuckles slightly bent.

"For this battle, you have no idea how many times I have simulated it in my mind!"

Ignoring the bloody hole on his arm, Luo Chen pressed his advantage, unleashing the Devil Lord's Seven Scattered Hands and the Wind, Moon, Thunder, Cloud, and Stepping on the Immortal Path in succession, the transitions seamless and flawless.

Azure Frost countered each move, using fists, palms, fingers, claws, and leg kicks in rapid succession, matching him blow for blow without falling behind in the slightest.

The two figures, on the outskirts of Soul Island, sometimes collided, sometimes separated.

As they appeared and disappeared unpredictably, blood rained down.

When their palm winds clashed, the waves surged.

Yet, despite the ferocity of this close-quarters slaughter, the protagonists of this scene were still not them.

The great formation that almost enveloped the entire Soul Island had been pushed to its limit.

Within it, six figures rose and fell.

Powerful magical treasures crisscrossed like shooting stars.

From time to time, an aura of utter destruction would spread, causing Soul Island to tremble with thunderous roars.

This battle, involving the lives of eight people, would ultimately have to determine life and death.

Luo Chen had no time to worry about the other side. He only knew that his fate soul was in the hands of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, and that his life at this moment was held in the balance of each collision.

The Huntian Staff was in his hand, now long, now short, stirring up great waves of turbid water.

And Azure Frost, at some point, had put on a pair of silver-white gloves. Even when the heavy, mountain-like Huntian Staff came crashing down, she fearlessly met it head-on.

Moreover, those gloves were made of some unknown material of extremely high grade.

When she grabbed the Huntian Staff, she actually managed to pinch off a handful of pale stone powder!

That was the Huntian Staff, which even the Yuantu Sword could only leave a white mark on!

At that moment, Luo Chen's pupils contracted sharply.

He dared not hold back any longer. The Life-Seizing Golden Feather swayed at his side, sometimes stabbing, sometimes whipping, not allowing Azure Frost to get too close to his body.

If the Golden Feather Sage of the Five Elements Divine Sect were still alive and saw this, he would probably be cursing furiously.

The razor-sharp Life-Seizing Golden Feather was meant to be used in conjunction with spells and techniques, yet Luo Chen was using it as a close-combat weapon.

But Luo Chen had no choice. Once Azure Frost's hand touched him, not only would his bones break, but his flesh would also be torn open without fail.

After another short-range clash, Luo Chen lunged forward with a thrust.

The Huntian Staff suddenly expanded and lengthened, directly pushing Azure Frost back dozens of feet.

Then, a fierce glint flashed in Luo Chen's eyes.

"Go!"

The Withering-Bloom Divine Fire, along the Huntian Staff, spewed out like a fire serpent.

But just as it was about to reach Azure Frost, her figure vanished eerily.

"A spatial technique?" Luo Chen was startled. He suddenly remembered that on this Soul Island, while other laws might be suppressed, unable to draw upon the origin qi of heaven and earth, Azure Frost would not be.

Because the law she had comprehended was the true meaning of Space!

Time and space are omnipresent, omnipresent!

From the void, a voice of approval came, like the praise between worthy opponents.

"I acknowledge your progress over the years!"

The next moment, ripples spread through the void, and a silver-white palm emerged, pressing toward Luo Chen's back.

Just as it was about to touch, mana, devoid of any light, erupted, wanting to destroy everything.

*Boom!*

The palm force erupted, and the bubble shattered.

Azure Frost was stunned. On her beautiful face was an expression of disbelief.

This sure-kill strike had actually missed.

The answer to her was a blood-red sword tip.

Luo Chen turned back with a single sword stroke, Plucking Stars and Seizing the Moon, stabbing straight at her heart.

Azure Frost's heart alarm bells rang loudly. Her left hand twisted eerily, appearing in front of her chest with uncanny speed, blocking her flat chest.

*Clang!*

The sword tip met the glove, producing a deafening screech, so sharp it almost burst one's eardrums.

Luo Chen gritted his teeth, his expression fierce. With one hand gripping the sword and the other pushing the hilt, he pressed forward.

"Break!"

The razor-sharp Yuantu Sword actually advanced three inches, forcibly breaking through the silver-white glove.

*Shua!*

Azure Frost floated backward, her azure robe blooming like a flower, blood suddenly appearing.

Where her chest and abdomen had been flat, the faint outline of towering peaks now appeared.

She slowly raised her hand, looked at it, then raised her head thoughtfully.

"Good sword!"

"But the best thing was the illusion art that confused my divine sense!"

However, in the face of this praise, there was not much pride on Luo Chen's face.

Soul Art—Ghosts and Gods Give Way!

This was a technique he had created by observing the illusion of the desolate beast Ghost Immortal Tower. Against those with a weaker divine soul foundation than his, it was virtually invincible.

Especially now that his divine soul foundation had expanded to its limit, there were almost no Sage-level cultivators who could match him.

Since the battle began, he had suppressed the urge to use this soul art.

All of it was to create a single, sure-kill opportunity.

Yet, even though Azure Frost had fallen for it, he still had not succeeded in killing her, only inflicting a minor injury.

"I have simulated the scene of our rematch countless times, repeatedly overestimating you. But now that this dream has come true, I find that I still underestimated you by far."

Luo Chen held the blood-red longsword, the Life-Seizing Golden Feather swaying above his head. He stood in the void, his figure flickering, now real, now illusory, his true form unseen.

Even his voice seemed to come from all directions, making it impossible to locate his true body.

Azure Frost looked at him, her expression also becoming severe.

She was hailed by the outside world as the number one under the Ascendant realm!

And her past achievements were worthy of that reputation.

If not for a mistake in her cultivation, even the great power Chunyuan of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect might not have been able to capture her so easily.

But now, she had been injured by someone of the same realm. If not for her quick reaction, she would have ended up with a pierced heart.

She could not afford to be careless!

Neither spoke further. They each unleashed their full power and charged at each other.

When Azure Frost was halfway through her charge, her figure vanished again.

Ripples spread through the void. When she reappeared, she was at Luo Chen's side.

Miraculously, the Yuantu Sword was perfectly positioned at her swan-like neck.

As if she had actively run into it.

*Chi...*

A blood mark appeared.

And Luo Chen was not faring well either. A heavy palm force, ignoring the confusing effect of Ghosts and Gods Give Way, struck him from a distance on his waist and ribs.

*Bang!*

Luo Chen fell from the air, landing in the turbulent Yellow Spring Sea. With one hand on his sword and the other pressing his waist, his expression was dark.

"Why could she ignore Ghosts and Gods Give Way?"

On the other side, Azure Frost wiped her neck with her fingertip. The blood was as delicate as a flower. On her beautiful face was also a hint of disbelief.

"Why could he detect my Void Concealment Divine Art?"

In the murky sea, their gazes met across the distance, both filled with gravity.

*Smack!*

A palm struck the sea surface, and Luo Chen leaped up again.

Azure Frost was fearless, once again breaking through the void.

Mana surged, source power intensified. The two figures tumbled up and down, constantly colliding. Injuries began to appear uncontrollably on both of them.

This battle, far less spectacular than the one above Soul Severing Cliff, gradually escalated to extremes.

*Rumble!*

The waves rolled in, growing more and more turbulent, washing over the two tiny figures like two swaying seabirds.

And Soul Severing Cliff, as if carved by knives and axes, stood silently in the distance, witnessing the most peak group of people in this realm heading toward their mutual destruction.

(Chapter End)
