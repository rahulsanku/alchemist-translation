# Chapter 941: Reflection Entering the Spirit, Anomaly in Heaven and Earth

The various powers of the Mountains and Seas Realm's Five Provinces of the Immortal Cultivation World each had their own characteristics.

The Eastern Wilderness had many sects, the Western Desert was filled with temples, the Northern Sea was dominated by various large islands, and although the Central Continent was nominally under the unified rule of the Tianyuan Dao Sect, it was essentially divided into countless large and small cultivation clans.

As for the many powers of the Southern Frontier, they were mostly based on cults.

For example, the Sage Heavenly Mountain that Luo Chen had known before came from the Heavenly Mountain Cult.

And now, this Reflection Teaching was the same.

Based on Luo Chen's brief contact with the Southern Frontier cultivation world, he vaguely understood the reason behind this.

The key point still lay in that single word: "Gu"!

Gu insects were quite mysterious. For unfamiliar gu insects, ordinary people simply did not dare to use them as cultivation aids.

Only with trust from the bottom of one's heart would one dare to accept a gu into their body.

Thus, the major and minor powers of the Southern Frontier, based on various strange gu insects, developed doctrines, guided disciples, and gradually formed all kinds of cults.

The Reflection Teaching was one such example.

In a hall, two large red lanterns hung high before the door, and the shadows of trees swayed outside.

Inside the hall, Luo Chen was playing with the "Shadow Gu" that he had casually asked the Lantern Master for earlier out of curiosity.

"I see!"

"By recording the general scene through the gu insect's five senses, and then the cultivator extracting its memories, one can achieve the level of capturing images and sounds."

"Quite ingenious."

Luo Chen's face showed an expression of admiration, but it was merely admiration.

Ingenious enough, but lacking in grandeur.

For cultivators, it was merely a way to pass the time when bored.

Just as he was about to put away the Shadow Gu, he suddenly had a second thought.

The Lantern Master had only given him the most basic first-order Shadow Gu. If he had a higher-order Shadow Gu, would it have other miraculous uses?

What if what was recorded was not just light, shadow, sound, and color, but other, more special things?

"If I have the time, I could inquire about it."

Luo Chen put away the Shadow Gu and prepared to rest.

He had no real friendship with that Lantern Master; after a brief banquet, they had parted ways.

By now, Cave Lord Minglei was probably deep in conversation with the other party!

"Friends who stand by and do nothing in times of great calamity like this—if it were me, I probably wouldn't be able to exchange more than two sentences. It's hard on Minglei."

Luo Chen chuckled lightly and went to the cultivation chamber himself.

Sitting cross-legged on a cushion, he assumed his posture, and the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture* immediately began to circulate.

Since restarting his cultivation of this lifebound technique, Luo Chen's cultivation speed could be described as advancing a thousand li in a single day.

His surge from the fifth layer of the Sage realm to the sixth layer was certainly aided by his comprehension of the origin of fire, but it was also inseparable from the help of this foremost fire-attribute technique in the world.

Especially since he possessed a Fire Spirit Body, his efficiency in absorbing the world's spiritual qi far exceeded that of ordinary people.

At this moment, a vast amount of spiritual qi converged within the cave dwelling.

Without Luo Chen having to exert much effort, he could easily refine and absorb it.

Within his Purple Mansion, the small Nascent Soul figure had already developed considerably compared to its initial infant state.

From the contours of its facial features, it faintly showed signs of a youthful appearance.

It was said that a cultivator's primordial spirit was almost identical to their own demeanor, temperament, and even appearance.

If one's Nascent Soul could reach that level, it could be said to be no different from an Ascendant!

At this moment, Luo Chen's small Nascent Soul figure, amidst continuous cultivation, revealed a peaceful expression, and was even extremely lively, with an impulse to leave the Purple Mansion and roam the vast heavens and earth.

Lively...

When the small Nascent Soul figure touched the thick barrier of the Purple Mansion, Luo Chen was suddenly jolted awake.

"Strange!"

"Why is my Nascent Soul so active?"

What was called a Nascent Soul was the fusion of the three treasures: essence, qi, and spirit.

When a Sage cultivator was cultivating, most of their soul was integrated into it, with the cultivator's own will taking the lead.

Under normal circumstances, cultivating step by step would not make it so active.

Otherwise, during long-term cultivation, no matter how deep the cultivator's soul foundation was, they would still feel tired.

It was like how even the smartest person would get tired if they kept using their brain to think about problems.

But now, without Luo Chen's deliberate guidance, the Nascent Soul was so active—it was truly abnormal.

If not for the fact that his Purple Mansion barrier was thick enough, the Nascent Soul would probably have wanted to break free and embrace this world.

"How could this be?"

Just as Luo Chen was about to think deeper, some commotion came from outside the hall.

He frowned and walked out of the cultivation chamber.

"Come in!"

Cave Lord Minglei, who had been waiting under the lamp for a short while, walked in, carrying a faint smell of alcohol.

"Sorry to disturb Brother Qi's cultivation."

Luo Chen waved his hand. "No matter. Did you have a good talk with that Lantern Master?"

Cave Lord Minglei smiled self-deprecatingly. "What good talk could there be? Just going through the motions."

Having said that, he sighed, a look of melancholy in his eyes.

"I thought that by working hard from being an unknown rogue cultivator to reach the Sage realm, anyone would have to look at me with respect. But only upon reaching this realm did I realize it's not that simple at all."

"Those of the same realm still look down on me. My background is shallow, my foundation insufficient, and everyone wants to take a squeeze at me."

"Feng Buyan was like that, which is why he chose to make a move and force me. The Lantern Master was also like that, which is why he stood by and watched, unwilling to offend the Feng family for my sake."

"Thinking about it carefully, back when I happened to be lucky enough to participate in that Sage gathering, and some of the resources I wanted to buy, others wouldn't sell to me—could there also be this reason?"

Luo Chen's brows were tightly furrowed. In the middle of the night, he came to talk about this?

He said bluntly, "How important are the gazes of others? This cultivation world, when it comes down to it, is nothing more than the survival of the fittest, where strength reigns supreme. If you display enough formidable power, who would dare to ignore you?"

Cave Lord Minglei smiled bitterly. "What you say is indeed the truth, but improving one's strength is not as simple as drinking water or eating. Away from the Wind-Thunder Gorge, I am nothing more than a very ordinary early-stage Sage."

This was indeed the truth.

Because the Wind-Thunder Gorge was perpetually accumulated with a large amount of lightning and thunder, it provided an excellent natural advantage for cultivators who practiced this path during battle.

But once he left, Cave Lord Minglei's strength would be greatly diminished.

Luo Chen thought for a moment and suggested, "If strength cannot be improved quickly, reputation can. If you can establish a power, over time, you will become a lord of a domain, and others will no longer overlook you."

This was the path Luo Chen had once walked.

Although it was somewhat troublesome, it was indeed effective.

Cave Lord Minglei nodded in agreement. "On this trip, I actually have this intention as well. But establishing a power requires many things. For example, inherited techniques, core gu insects, and even methods for making spirit stones, and so on."

"As for techniques, that's fine; I have my own set. The core gu insects are more troublesome, especially the gu insects that assist disciples in their cultivation. Most of them are closely guarded secrets of various families."

"The reason I endured the nausea and talked with the Lantern Master earlier was also to see if I could buy some low-level Reflection Spirit Gu from his Reflection Teaching. As for going to Mi Jie Mountain, it's because the Life and Death Gate is now closed, and it's rumored that the Fierce Corpse Stiff Alliance has also mastered the method for making spirit stones. I plan to obtain it from them."

Listening to these rambling words, Luo Chen felt a sense of familiarity.

It was as if when he had established the Luo Tian Sect back then, it was the same.

Encountering suitable techniques, he collected them.

Encountering suitable resources, even if they were in the distant Northern Sea, he would bring them back.

It was through this bit-by-bit, penny-pinching process that he had accumulated such a vast family fortune for the Luo Tian Sect.

He no longer felt as impatient as before and patiently listened to the other party continue his rambling, occasionally chiming in.

"Reflection Spirit Gu? What kind of gu insect is that?"

"This is one of the Reflection Teaching's most core gu insects. After being refined into the body, it can completely replicate the internal situation of a cultivator during their cultivation. After a cultivator finishes a session of cultivation, they can use the Reflection Spirit Gu to review the various details of the previous cultivation, thereby identifying and filling gaps. With the assistance of this gu, low-level cultivators can lay a very solid foundation. Even for high-level cultivators like us, if we have a high-level Reflection Spirit Gu to assist us, we can save a lot of trouble."

"Will the Reflection Teaching sell it?"

"They definitely won't sell the high-level ones, but as for the low-level ones, as far as I know, the Lantern Master has privately sold quite a few to some major powers, specifically to maintain interpersonal relationships."

"Did you succeed?"

"The Lantern Master hasn't agreed yet, but because he feels guilty for not helping me earlier, I estimate that after a day or two of pressing him, he will sell me some low-level ones."

"Then I wish Cave Lord success! If you can gather rogue cultivators at the Wind-Thunder Gorge and establish a power, that would be a wonderful thing."

"Thank you for your kind words, Brother Qi!" Cave Lord Minglei smiled, his gloomy mood much improved.

For rogue cultivators like them who had drifted for too long, being able to establish a power and become a sovereign ruler was indeed a wonderful thing.

If they could pass down their own inheritance, and have people still singing the praises of the founder's name a thousand years later, that would be even better.

Luo Chen smiled. "In my opinion, the rogue cultivators of the Wind-Thunder Gorge will not refuse you. By then, you can take on a few personal disciples... that Cheng Bai is quite good!"

When Cheng Bai was mentioned, Cave Lord Minglei's expression turned unnatural for a moment.

He actively changed the subject, looking around and showing an expression of surprise.

"It seems the Lantern Master hasn't treated us poorly. He's arranged a pretty good residence for you too!"

It was indeed a good residence!

The spiritual qi in this hall was quite rich, reaching the fourth-order level.

By the standards of guest reception, it was considered very high.

"This is all thanks to you..."

Luo Chen smiled and nodded, but as he spoke, his voice trailed off, and ripples stirred in his heart.

Cave Lord Minglei waved his hand. "What thanks do I deserve? It's because of the impressive aura you displayed earlier that he thought highly of us. Well, it's late at night, so Brother Qi, please rest early! We might have to stay here for another two or three days, so please bear with it."

Luo Chen showed no intention of keeping him and let him leave.

The moment the hall door closed, Luo Chen drew his fingers across his eyes.

Open!

Golden pupils flickered, and bloodshot veins spread across the whites of his eyes.

Crimson Eyes, Golden Pupils—once again appeared on Luo Chen's face.

He stared fixedly at the great hall, especially at the trajectory of spiritual qi flow in the cultivation chamber.

Gradually, Luo Chen found the clue.

"This is nothing more than the most ordinary fourth-order spirit vein. Why is the spiritual qi so rich?"

"Furthermore, there is even some pure origin qi of heaven and earth floating around."

"Could it be that my Nascent Soul was so lively earlier because of this external environment?"

At the thought of this, Luo Chen's face instantly darkened.

A Nascent Soul being too lively was not a good thing.

At this stage, the Nascent Soul was still far from reaching the powerful level of a primordial spirit.

It was like the difference between an infant and an adult.

Being too active would instead harm the soul!

Moreover, if it truly broke free from the Purple Mansion and roamed freely between heaven and earth, it would gradually become lost within it, and the consequence would be those four words—**Assimilation by Heaven and Earth**!

These four words sounded light and carried no weight.

But after personally witnessing the scene of Sage Shen Yuan's fall, Luo Chen had been avoiding it at all costs.

In that state of losing self-awareness, no matter how profound one's dharma power or how strong one's physical body, it was insignificant.

A gust of wind, a rainstorm, and everything would return to nature.

Luo Chen absolutely did not want to reach that point.

"Did the Lantern Master harm me?"

"Or does he himself not know?"

In a state of shock and uncertainty, Luo Chen returned to the cultivation chamber.

First, he adjusted his mindset, and then he began to act.

With a flick of his sleeve, several formation flags fell into the corners of the cultivation chamber.

From his sea of consciousness, three divided souls emerged, transforming into three streaks of dark light that circled around his body.

With a shake of his hand, a mottled stone mirror appeared on it.

It was the Dust Fate Immortal Mirror that Luo Chen had painstakingly refined!

Speaking of which, since this treasure was refined, Luo Chen himself had not used it much, mostly using it on Canglong.

But he was already thoroughly familiar with the mirror's wondrous uses.

Without needing instructions, the three divided souls, connected by thought, immediately began to move.

Each emitted dark light that was injected into the treasure mirror, remaining dormant and suspended above his head.

Only at this point did Luo Chen consider himself fully prepared.

He took a deep breath and once again began to circulate the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture*.

As he breathed and his divine sense flowed, a vast amount of the world's spiritual qi surged into his body.

It flowed through his extraordinary meridians and eight extraordinary channels, back and forth through his various acupoints and spiritual apertures, gradually being refined into dharma power.

Finally, it entered his Purple Mansion and merged into the small Nascent Soul figure.

Time passed, bit by bit.

One day, two days, three days...

Cave Lord Minglei's delay at the Reflection Teaching was longer than previously expected.

And Luo Chen's seclusion, while not within reason, was actually anticipated by some.

Under the night sky, lantern shadows flickered, and figures moved indistinctly.

One by one, figures gradually gathered outside the hall where Luo Chen was staying.

Luo Chen ignored all of this.

He only focused on his own world. The growth of his dharma power was astonishingly fast!

Even if it still couldn't compare to the efficiency of cultivating in that fourth-order top-grade cave dwelling on Green Parasol Mountain, it far exceeded any ordinary spirit vein.

But his eyes were quite dazed and chaotic.

The fluctuations of his dharma power were also uncontrollably, slowly emanating outward.

If not for the formation flags he had prepared earlier to conceal it, once this vast, ocean-like dharma power fluctuation spread out, the entire Reflection Teaching would be alarmed.

Yet even so, a suppressed, powerful aura brought immense pressure to people under the night sky.

It was as if an ancient giant beast was crouching inside the hall!

Suddenly!

The mottled stone mirror suspended above his head cast down a large swath of light.

"Wake up!"

"Wake up!"

"Wake up now!"

The three divided souls shouted in unison, their soul sounds echoing, stirring the Nascent Soul.

In an instant, the confusion in Luo Chen's eyes vanished completely, and he woke up from his dazed state.

He instinctively pressed down on his lower abdomen, a lingering fear still on his face.

"Wrong!"

"This place has an anomaly in heaven and earth!"

"The concentration of origin qi of heaven and earth is too high, almost taking on the form of an ocean of origin qi."

"Being in such a place, although the efficiency of cultivation is extremely high, if one is not careful, they will sink into slumber and then gradually be assimilated by heaven and earth."

"How could this be?"
