# Chapter 1173: Everyone Wants Him Dead, The Martial Madman's Challenge

"Did you say that man's name is Luo Chen?"

In the lonely void, atop a cultivation stone, Dugu Cheng repeatedly questioned Cui Zhuo, who had come to visit him in distress.

His expression was filled with disbelief.

Cui Zhuo nodded and carefully described Luo Chen's appearance.

Seeing the look on Dugu Cheng's face, he realized that the newcomer was no unknown figure, and he himself had indeed felt that the name Luo Chen sounded familiar.

Suddenly!

Cui Zhuo exclaimed in surprise, "Could he be that martial artist suspected of being an immortal alchemist who entered in this batch?"

Dugu Cheng slowly nodded. "You guessed right. It's him."

Cui Zhuo clicked his tongue in wonder. "So it's him. I once wanted to visit and make his acquaintance, but unfortunately, I had no Void Sprout Crystals to gain entry. Upon first meeting, although he acted somewhat domineering, his bearing and demeanor were not bad. Worthy of someone who once sought immortality and pursued the Dao."

Dugu Cheng did not think the same way. Instead, his expression gradually grew solemn.

"Before he even entered the Nine Rings of Ruin, he was already highly regarded and listed as a Human Immortal Seed. I used to think it was an exaggeration, but now, having entered for only ten years, he has already broken into the Middle Three Rings. This shows his boundless potential."

Cui Zhuo was puzzled. "Is there a problem?"

Dugu Cheng countered, "With such a prodigy, do you think he would be content to cultivate peacefully on your original cultivation stone?"

Cui Zhuo was taken aback, then realized that Luo Chen could never be the type to stay put.

Once he achieved some minor success, he would likely frequently change cultivation stones.

This would bring unpredictable turmoil to the Middle Three Rings cultivation zone, where the power structure was already stable.

Especially since the man acted domineeringly. He had spared Cui Zhuo's life perhaps because Cui had been polite enough initially, but how many other Leakless Realm experts had such a good temper?

If they clashed in anger, who knew how many would fall.

As he pondered, Dugu Cheng suddenly spoke. "Brother Cui, this man has broken the rules. He is someone everyone wants dead. Let me gather the alliance members and reclaim your cultivation stone!"

Cui Zhuo was startled, then quickly waved his hand.

"No need. He did spare my life after all. Besides, I know my talent is limited. A good cultivation stone should belong to the virtuous. At worst, I'll retreat to the next ring and wait ten or twenty years. Isn't Old Li's lifespan almost up? I'll go to his place then."

But Dugu Cheng disagreed. "Since the rules are set, as beneficiaries, we should all work together to uphold them. Otherwise, if others encounter a similar situation in the future, how will we judge?"

Cui Zhuo opened his mouth, still hesitant.

He was not a combative person.

Even when Luo Chen acted domineeringly, he had initially tried to negotiate politely.

Now that Luo Chen hadn't killed him, they could barely call it even. Why stir up more trouble?

Hesitating, Cui Zhuo muttered, "But he is a Human Immortal Seed. If such a genius meets an untimely end, will Carefree Realm Master blame us?"

"Heh, a genius who dies midway is still a genius?" Dugu Cheng sneered, then changed his tone. "But what you say is not without reason. Luo Chen broke the rules, but he also showed restraint. If we oppress him too much, we'll appear petty. However, if he reveals a murderous nature, then we can act in self-defense. When that time comes, even if Carefree Realm Master blames us, we will have our say!"

Cui Zhuo was confused.

From Luo Chen's earlier behavior, he didn't seem like a bloodthirsty man. How could they make him reveal a murderous nature?

Dugu Cheng raised his eyes, looking into the distance.

"That man who, in the past hundred years, was the only one to enter the Middle Three Rings from the Lower Three Rings and establish a foothold—he has a mortal, irreconcilable enmity with Luo Chen!"

"Let him be the spearhead for this battle!"

"If he wins, good. If he loses and dies, then we can act with just cause!"

---

After seizing Cui Zhuo's cultivation stone, Luo Chen did not waste any time. Instead, he focused on stabilizing his realm.

Thus, after more than half a year, he finally stood firmly at the mid-Leakless stage.

Having reached this point, the self-created *God-Devil Formless* technique gradually revealed its wonders.

A layer of astral qi, like a blood membrane, clung to Luo Chen's physical body, swirling without dispersing.

Even under the impact of chaotic energy, it could regenerate endlessly.

Especially as Luo Chen's mind moved, this blood membrane could transform into clothing or armor, providing constant protection.

Compared to the astral qi shields of ordinary Leakless Realm martial artists, this blood membrane was far more powerful and agile.

As long as Luo Chen's blood qi was not exhausted, his apertures unbroken, and a thread of life remained, the blood membrane could persist indefinitely.

For such a near-martial divine ability, Luo Chen named it—**Blood Cloth Armor**!

In leisure, it could serve as clothing to conceal his true form. In battle, it could block the enemy's qi sense detection while withstanding sufficient attacks. Moreover, the upper limit of Blood Cloth Armor, in Luo Chen's conception, had a complete upgrade path.

That path was—**Death Qi**!

Over the past few centuries, he had killed many, accumulating a great deal of baleful qi. But to prevent cultivators from identifying him through it, he had actively used the Dragon-Slaying Art, fused with the Cleansing Art, to remove it all.

Now that he had entered the Great Ruin Realm and switched to orthodox martial arts, he found it regrettable.

The baleful qi clinging to him from the dead was actually excellent cultivation material.

But from now on, he would not waste it.

If Luo Chen killed powerful enemies again, he could convert the baleful resentment clinging to him into death qi, thereby protecting himself.

The more people who died by his hands, the stronger Blood Cloth Armor would become.

At that time, combined with his already indestructible physical body, Luo Chen would become an unassailable fortress under heaven!

At this point!

Luo Chen's cultivation in orthodox martial arts could truly be said to have entered the hall.

Internally, he had the Blood Cloth Armor, a growable means of defense.

Externally, he had a killing move comparable to a great divine ability—Heaven-Earth Reversal.

In addition, the Devil Lord's Seven Scattering Hands had long been mastered, Wang Yuan's martial techniques came at his fingertips, and newly learned moves like the Great Luo Five Dragon Hands and Flower-Picking Finger could serve as supplements.

To say he had entered the hall was by no means an exaggeration.

Even by Luo Chen's own standards, without using his physical body, his combat power was by no means inferior to his former self at the peak of mid-Sage stage.

And if he used pure physical strength, even if an Ascendant great power or a Martial Human Immortal stood before him, he would have the strength to fight!

Long periods of cultivation inevitably brought fatigue.

After ascending a step, Luo Chen would also relax a little.

Today, after finishing his cultivation, Luo Chen took a break and observed the situation of the Vacuum Gu.

In size, there was no change.

This was the result of consuming a large number of Spatial Prism Apertures and two Void Sprout Crystals.

The Void Sprout Crystal stone box given by Fang Zhibai had also been mostly eaten, with only a small amount remaining.

Logically, after eating so much, its size should have changed. However, the Vacuum Gu seemed to have a universe inside it, and no matter how much it consumed, it could not grow larger.

Instead, the wrinkles on its milky-white skin surface became more detailed and numerous.

How much more food it would need to break through to the fifth-rank Divine Gu was unknown.

No wonder the Southern Frontier Luo Sect, despite its deep foundation, could only remain at the level of a great sect, unable to ascend to the holy land standard of Life and Death Gate.

The resources needed to cultivate a Vacuum Gu to the fifth rank could not be described merely as "vast."

Luo Chen was also helpless about this.

Resources like Void Sprout Crystals, which involved breaking through the seventh realm of body refinement and were also related to avoiding the Return to Ruin, were simply beyond the grasp of ordinary fourth-rank martial artists.

Presumably, they were mostly in the hands of Human Immortals like Gu Xun.

For them, the attraction of fourth-rank Meridian-Repairing Pills was ultimately too low.

In fact, even Human Immortals like Gu Xun only had a small portion of Void Sprout Crystals. The real bulk was with Carefree Realm Master.

This could be seen from the fact that he had sent Immortal Origin Stones, stored in a stone box cast from a large piece of Void Sprout Crystal.

As he observed, Luo Chen's heart suddenly stirred.

His peripheral vision gradually focused. In the distance, a figure was approaching at lightning speed.

No!

It wasn't just one person!

Luo Chen infused blood qi into his eyes, his Crimson Eyes Golden Pupils seeing further.

Behind that person, there were twenty or thirty more following in a line. Their breathing qi crisscrossed, their momentum vast as if a thousand troops were marching.

"Just for seizing a cultivation stone, has almost the entire Sixth Ring been alarmed?"

Luo Chen put away the Vacuum Gu and slowly stood up.

Facing a great enemy, his expression remained calm.

*Boom!*

The leading figure suddenly stopped.

A hundred zhang apart, he stared directly at the man in white.

"Are you Luo Chen?"

Luo Chen examined the newcomer. Hemp-clothed, barefoot, with a gaunt face, but exuding a soaring spirit.

Vaguely, he gave Luo Chen a familiar feeling.

"An ascetic demeanor, full of battle intent. You are... Xiao Lin!"

Xiao Lin was not surprised that Luo Chen recognized him. Many portraits of him remained in his hometown.

"The one who killed my son and seized my Relic Immortal City—was it you?"

Luo Chen smiled slightly. "Your son deserved to die. As for your Relic Immortal City, it still bears the surname Xiao. What will you do about it today?"

Relic Immortal City still bore the surname Xiao?

Xiao Lin had not expected this. After all, he had not gone out during this hundred-year period, having been cultivating in the Sixth Ring.

All information about the outside world had been told to him by Dugu Cheng.

But just from the discrepancy in information, Xiao Lin realized there was discord.

Facing the candid Luo Chen, the hatred in Xiao Lin's eyes dissipated somewhat, but his battle intent burned even stronger.

"Words are useless. Xiao Lin requests your guidance!"

As he spoke, he leaped forward, charging straight at Luo Chen.

Before the fight, Luo Chen glanced into the distance. Figures stood in the void, watching coldly.

"It seems today cannot end peacefully!"

Luo Chen smiled faintly and threw a punch.

The Blood-Refined Divine Astral Qi was overbearing and unmatched, like a mountain-shattering, peak-crushing force surging forth.

Xiao Lin did not dodge. He threw a punch as well.

Under their clash, his fist force receded inch by inch.

Xiao Lin's expression changed slightly, but his eyes sparkled even more. He then threw another punch.

This punch contained seven layers of force, stacking like waves, canceling out Luo Chen's mountain-shattering punch inch by inch.

But one punch was followed by a claw descending from the sky.

Facing the sudden Cloud-Seizing Divine Claw, Xiao Lin changed his fighting style. His entire body became ethereal and unpredictable, his extremely fast form turning into layers of ghostly afterimages, pouncing on Luo Chen from all directions.

The Cloud-Seizing Divine Claw missed, surprising Luo Chen.

But he was not flustered. Instead, his eyes held a hint of amusement as he pointed out.

*Xiu!*

*Xiu!*

*Xiu!*

One Blood God Tribulation Finger after another shot out, breaking the illusory afterimages one by one.

When the last finger was about to land on Xiao Lin's true body, Xiao Lin also extended a finger.

The two finger forces touched. Xiao Lin's whole body shook, his face flushing red.

And Luo Chen's finger force dissipated strangely, its remaining power exhausted.

"Flower-Picking Finger?"

Luo Chen murmured, his gaze suddenly sharp, focusing on the empty air.

Then, he gathered qi around his finger, as if picking a flower, and pointed out inexplicably.

*Puff!*

A sharp sound exploded in the void.

Xiao Lin was stunned. He had just secretly released a killing move he had created over the past hundred years, called the Formless Void Finger.

It could turn his astral qi into formless, invisible energy, striking through the void, making it hard to guard against.

Unexpectedly, Luo Chen had still caught it and blocked it.

Most importantly, the technique Luo Chen used to intercept his finger was exactly the same as the Flower-Picking Finger Xiao Lin had used to neutralize Luo Chen's Blood God Tribulation Finger.

"You also know Flower-Picking Finger?!"

Xiao Lin stopped and asked directly.

Luo Chen smiled slightly. "I have to thank Brother Xiao for leaving those martial arts manuals in Relic Immortal City."

Xiao Lin frowned. If that was the case, then using other old martial arts would hardly harm Luo Chen.

And the agile guerrilla tactics he had changed to earlier were also exposed under that strange pair of eyes.

Thus, he had to return to a head-on confrontation, and he had to use martial arts that Luo Chen did not understand.

"Just this one move!"

Xiao Lin took a deep breath and pointed at his lower abdomen.

The next moment, the blood qi furnace in his body reversed its operation. All his apertures opened wide, spewing out powerful astral qi.

Then, he brought his hands together and slowly pushed forward.

The surrounding astral qi turned into rings, one after another, surging toward Luo Chen.

Like an immeasurable ocean, each wave stronger than the last.

"This move was created by me after a hundred years of bitter cultivation in the Nine Rings of Ruin, inspired by the surging chaotic energy. Fellow Daoist, be careful!"

Seeing this, Luo Chen's eyes lit up.

The opponent's starting stance was clearly the *Heaven-Earth Reversal* he was familiar with.

This incomplete forbidden art had actually been perfected, allowing him to multiply his combat power several times. And judging by his confident expression, the side effects had also been resolved.

As for the move he was now executing, it was unfamiliar yet vaguely reminiscent.

"Inspired by the surging chaotic energy?"

"Interesting!"

Luo Chen praised, then casually summoned his sword.

The Yuantu Sword fell into his hand.

In an instant, boundless sword qi burst forth.

It turned into a vast net, ever-changing and unpredictable.

Seeing this, someone in the distance changed color dramatically.

That was a killing move he had only used once in person—**Sword Net**!

But upon closer inspection, it was completely different.

The flow of sword qi was more complex, and in the subtle places, strands of sword silk flew.

Sword qi turning into silk—this was an extremely advanced sword technique.

Compared to sword qi taking form, one excelled in the macro, the other in the micro.

The former was easy to learn but hard to master, while the latter could not even be entered without a certain level of swordsmanship.

At this moment, the vast sword net enveloped one wave after another, seemingly trying to kill them within the net.

But sword qi was finite, while the waves were inexhaustible.

Gradually, the vast sword net was breached.

Seeing this, Luo Chen was not surprised.

Using Blood-Refined Divine Astral Qi to drive the *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon* was ultimately not orthodox enough, and his conversion time was insufficient.

But through the probing of countless sword silks, he had already seen through the flaw in this move.

The Yuantu Sword suddenly turned, raised high.

Countless sword qi returned like tired birds, transforming into the purest Blood-Refined Divine Astral Qi, coiling around the sword tip, instantly forming a giant sword reaching the sky.

The next moment.

Luo Chen casually swung down.

*Boom!*

One sword cut through a thousand waves!

If it came to a head-on clash, what did Luo Chen fear?

Xiao Lin was struck heavily, flying backward.

When he steadied himself, looking at his trembling hands, there was a sword scar cutting through the apertures in his palms.

"Defeated!"
