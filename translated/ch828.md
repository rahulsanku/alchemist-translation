# Chapter 828: Jade Emperor Clear Pupil, Solitary Swan of the Sword Realm

Several days later.

Inside the pill chamber, a mass of golden liquid emanated a hazy aura from within the Primordial Unity Cauldron, which was gradually losing its heat.

The white-robed Daoist formed a hand seal. The golden liquid, resembling molten glass, slowly rose into the air before falling like raindrops, accurately entering each prepared jade bottle.

The Red Dog Puppet held a tray bearing five jade bottles.

Luo Chen waved his hand, and one of them flew before him.

He first sniffed it, then took out a drop and ground it between his fingers for a long while. He even lightly tasted it with his tongue—and a crisp sensation came from his mouth?

Luo Chen couldn't help but show a look of wonder.

"Is this the Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid?"

For him, the difficulty of refining this spiritual medicine was actually quite ordinary; it just required switching to water-based alchemy methods. His previous repeated refusals of Pavilion Master Li had only been due to time constraints.

In truth, when he had gone into seclusion five years ago, the Red Dog Puppet had already thoroughly studied this pill formula, which was why he could accomplish it in one go today.

"Taking the eye pupils of several demon beasts with excellent eyesight as the main ingredients—clearly this is to supplement form with form. But after using this thing, can it truly cause one's eyes to undergo a transformation?"

Luo Chen felt an urge to try.

Ever since the Qi Refinement stage, when he had rooted the Spirit Eye Art in his eyes, he had never stopped cultivating this pair of eyes.

With the Moistening Bright Pearl Liquid, the Clarity Liquid... under persistent cultivation, his eyes had indeed improved.

Even during the Adept stage, he had integrated illusion techniques like Mirror Flower Water Moon into them, allowing for instant casting!

And having a good pair of eyes naturally provided an undeniable boost to combat strength.

Seeing through disguises, anticipating the enemy's moves.

Especially when combined with divine sense, unless one's cultivation level far exceeded Luo Chen's, it was often difficult to perfectly conceal oneself before him.

Not to mention anything else, in that battle against the Boundless Light Demon Emperor a few years ago, his ability to quickly find the opponent's true body in the darkness was largely thanks to this pair of eyes.

The reason he had agreed to refine the Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid for Pavilion Master Li was also partly because he intended to use it himself.

"I'd like to see what effect it has!"

Luo Chen smacked his lips. After confirming the medicinal liquid was harmless, he carefully raised the jade bottle, tilting the mouth downward.

Just like when he had put in eye drops back in his school days, a drop of golden liquid resembling glass slowly fell, landing in his right eye.

Plop!

Luo Chen closed his right eye, carefully feeling that strange sensation.

The first experience upon entry was a kind of granular feeling?

It was clearly a liquid, yet it gave one the feeling of small pebbles, small pieces of glass.

He couldn't help but slightly furrow his right brow.

Then, a cool sensation spread.

That coolness continuously seeped in, reaching his eyeballs, his pupils, and even his soul... Vaguely, it seemed something was indeed slowly undergoing a change.

...

**Danluo Mountain.**

The original name of this mountain was no longer known; all that was known was that the city's Sages had joined forces to transport it from another great domain using great means.

Because Alchemy Sage Luo Chen had lived on this mountain for nearly ten years, it had gradually acquired the name "Danluo."

At this moment, at the foot of the mountain, the Heartsevered Fairy had received instructions and was waiting quietly.

Suddenly seeing a streak of light flying in the distance, she immediately went to meet it.

"Junior Heartsevered greets you. Are you Sage Li in person?"

The light dissipated, revealing the true appearance of Wan Jian Pavilion's Pavilion Master Li.

He smiled slightly. "Indeed, it is I. I've kept you waiting, little friend."

Heartsevered Fairy shook her head. "The Alchemy Sage has been in seclusion researching something recently, so he specially ordered me to welcome Pavilion Master Li in advance, lest you be neglected. May I ask who these two are..."

Heartsevered Fairy looked at the other two figures, but her words gradually faltered as the man and woman revealed their true appearances.

The man was dressed in a blue robe, his figure solitary and gaunt, his brows like sharp swords, his eyes like stars.

The woman was in a red dress, charming and beautiful yet not lacking in ferocity.

She recognized these two!

Pavilion Master Li smiled slightly and introduced them. "The current sect master of the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect, Lin Qingxuan, and the Enforcement Elder, Hong Ling. Both hail from the former Jade Cauldron Domain. Little friend Heartsevered should not be unfamiliar with them, right?"

The two looked at Heartsevered Fairy and nodded in greeting.

How could Heartsevered Fairy not recognize them? They had been true disciples of the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect, the former Nascent Soul superior sect of the Jade Cauldron Domain. Lin Qingxuan, even at the Initiate stage, had been cultivated as an Adept Dao seed.

His later achievements had also lived up to the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect's investment. At a young age, he had advanced to the Sage realm amidst slaughter and battle.

If not for Luo Chen's meteoric rise, the Solitary Swan of the Sword Realm, Lin Qingxuan, should have been the most dazzling figure among the younger generation in the past hundred years!

Although it was a reunion of "old acquaintances," Heartsevered Fairy had no intention of reminiscing.

After a simple greeting, she asked, "They also wish to see the Alchemy Sage?"

At this question, Lin Qingxuan and Hong Ling both showed slight discomfort on their faces.

Pavilion Master Li, however, chuckled and said, "Yes, I have already informed Fellow Daoist Luo of this matter in advance. You need not worry."

Since that was the case, Heartsevered Fairy no longer obstructed them. She opened the formation of Danluo Mountain and led the three upward until they reached a hall halfway up the mountain.

"Please wait a moment, three. Allow me to inform him."

After Heartsevered Fairy left, Hong Ling couldn't help but say, "The Alchemy Sage, quite the airs he puts on!"

"Junior Sister, watch your words!" Lin Qingxuan said in a low voice.

Hong Ling pouted but also showed a look of defeat.

Thinking back to Luo Chen, he had formed his core even later than she had. After all these years, she was still stuck at the mid-Adept stage, while he had long stepped into the Sage realm.

She had heard of the Alchemy Sage's great reputation before, but it had always been like looking at flowers through a mist, without deep feeling.

But just now, seeing Heartsevered Fairy—once a famously aloof beauty in the Jade Cauldron Domain—now reduced to Luo Chen's subordinate, serving him like a gatekeeper...

One must know that Luo Chen had once relied on Icecastle's protection to survive.

Now, the roles of master and servant were reversed. Truly a lamentable change, truly... one could empathize!

Pavilion Master Li watched the senior and junior siblings and couldn't help but smile.

"In chaotic times, heroes emerge in abundance. You are like this, Qingxuan, and Luo Chen is also like this. He has simply walked ahead of you."

"A temporary retreat can lead to a broader world."

"If you can clearly find your own position, your future holds great promise. Once the demon chaos is pacified, you can emulate the Jade Cauldron Sage of old, open up a domain, and rebuild your sect."

Lin Qingxuan listened to this earnest instruction, his expression calm, without any shame at being lectured by a fellow realm existence.

There was a reason for this.

The world only knew that he had advanced to the Sage realm amidst slaughter, but how could they know what kind of heaven-collapsing desperate situation it was? How many miles of bloody path could a Adept-stage cultivator carve out?

His current achievements were inseparable from the covert support of Wan Jian Pavilion.

Thus, even when exchanging with fellow Daoists, he would show respect to Pavilion Master Li in front of outsiders.

Even if today, the other party had brought him here to apologize!

...

"They've come to apologize for what happened before."

Luo Chen stood before a large iron anvil, using a heavy hammer to strike a red-hot piece of jade.

His movements were fierce, raising high and striking hard, yet utterly silent, without any great commotion. Thus, his voice could be clearly heard by the woman behind him.

The woman looked at the man's back, naked from the waist up, revealing terrifyingly knotted muscles. She couldn't help but secretly marvel.

Whether it was because of Luo Chen's powerful physique, which exuded a dense aura of danger, or because of what he had just said.

Lin Qingxuan was now, after all, the master of a sect and a Sage who had made a name for himself.

And he had taken the initiative to come and apologize to Luo Chen?

Clang!

The heavy hammer was casually thrown onto the rack. Luo Chen paid no attention to the scorching temperature and picked up the jade, which had half-faded from red to reveal its seven-colored nature.

He nodded slightly.

This piece of Seven Soul Jade had been forged perfectly and could be used to refine the Heart-Inquiring Mirror!

Then, he formed a hand seal, and an iron box opened, emitting a chilling cold.

After storing the Seven Soul Jade in the cold box, Luo Chen finally breathed a sigh of relief.

Without any visible movement from him, the lower part of his pants automatically extended upward, transforming into a loose white robe that completely covered his back muscles, which were like the roots of a great tree.

"Let's go. I'll also go see them and find out if their sincerity is sufficient."

As he turned around, Heartsevered Fairy was momentarily stunned.

Because Luo Chen's right eye was unusual!

She lowered her head, not daring to look further, and followed behind Luo Chen as he left this artifact-refining hall.

"What are they apologizing for? Was it because Hong Ling participated in the Luo Tian internal strife, the Ten Jue Battle, before?"

"Mm."

Luo Chen walked ahead, his wide sleeves fluttering, his expression calm.

"At that time, Wan Jian Pavilion was supporting the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect in their attempt to encroach upon the White Crane Domain. This behavior was no different from the Five Elements Divine Sect and Motian Cliff trying to encroach upon Fenghua Domain. So, they were naturally allied. But with the demon beasts at the city gates, those actions unfriendly to unity naturally had to stop."

"Once they stopped, the fragile alliance essentially ceased to exist. And because of my rise, they had to make a choice."

"The Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid was merely a trigger. Even without this matter, they would have taken the initiative to negotiate peace with me."

Heartsevered Fairy savored this carefully and found it to be true.

But she also clearly understood that the biggest factor in the shift of this matter was still Luo Chen's powerful rise!

She had heard that at the Shouyang Dao Discussion, he was sought after by many peers of the same realm.

Even great cultivators like Tian Duzi and Ling Tian City Lord had taken the initiative to show goodwill.

How could a mere rising star like Lin Qingxuan continue to maintain an aloof posture?

However, thinking that the once lofty disciples of the Sword Sect in the Jade Cauldron Domain now had to come and apologize to Luo Chen, who had once been like an ant to them, a sense of absurdity involuntarily rose in the woman's heart.

But in an instant, she couldn't help but bitterly laugh.

Was there any absurdity more absurd than the transformation of the relationship between herself and Luo Chen?

...

"Greetings, Supreme Elder!"

"Greetings, Supreme Elder!"

Some Luo Tian Sect disciples were permanently stationed on Danluo Mountain. As Luo Chen arrived at the mountainside, he was greeted by a flurry of salutations.

Luo Chen nodded gently in acknowledgment, then stepped into the hall where the guests were waiting.

"Fellow Daoist Li, Fellow Daoist Lin, sorry to keep you waiting!"

Pavilion Master Li stood up from his chair and smiled. "No matter, it was only a moment..."

"Eh!"

An exclamation came from the side, from Lin Qingxuan.

And the reason for his surprise, Pavilion Master Li also noticed.

Luo Chen's right eye!

Bright and lustrous like jade, with specks of fluorescence.

As his gaze shifted, it was like golden amber!

Lin Qingxuan opened his mouth, revealing an incredulous expression. "You truly succeeded?"

"If I hadn't successfully refined the Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid, why would I have notified you to come?" Luo Chen said with a hearty laugh. After taking his seat, he asked in return, "Fellow Daoist Lin, judging by your reaction, it seems you have seen the effects after using the Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid?"

Lin Qingxuan's surprise gradually faded, and he nodded. "Indeed, I have seen it. After all, my sect had been researching this pill formula for a long time. Once, we refined it to near perfection and had the senior apprentice brother use it. Unfortunately, after the senior apprentice brother fell, we discovered that the medicinal liquid was still not perfect; there were flaws within it. So for many years, my sect has not dared to refine it again, for fear of harming more disciples."

The existence that Lin Qingxuan would call "senior apprentice brother" was no ordinary person.

The first thing that came to Luo Chen's mind was the genius who had once topped the Jade Cauldron Domain's Prodigy Rankings as the number one Adept—Ren Pingsheng!

He had also heard Han Zhan talk about the details of the Falling Cloud Sect's ambush on Ren Pingsheng back then.

To help Han Zhan cultivate the formidable technique of the Yin-Yang Five Elements Sword Formation, the Falling Cloud Sect had paid a huge price to secretly ambush Ren Pingsheng.

That battle had resulted in many casualties, and they had only succeeded by luck.

The reason it was called luck was that the Falling Cloud Sect elders had used several Adept-stage puppets to shift forms and shadows, deceiving Ren Pingsheng's eyes, and thus captured him.

Now it seemed that Ren Pingsheng's failure to see through the deception was due to the weakness of his eyes!

"Although we dared not refine it again after that, research into the pill formula never stopped. My sect's chief alchemist finally concluded that the perfect Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid should appear bright and lustrous like jade, the color of golden amber. Only after complete absorption can it nurture a divine pupil."

Lin Qingxuan spoke slowly, his gaze fixed solemnly on Luo Chen's right eye.

"Just like you now!"

Luo Chen laughed heartily. "Then I have stolen the research achievements of your sect's predecessors!"

Lin Qingxuan sighed. "I dare not claim that. It is truly due to the Alchemy Sage's superb skill that you have achieved this."

Pavilion Master Li, who had been silent for a long time, finally couldn't hold back.

"Fellow Daoist Luo, since you have succeeded, why not discuss the transaction? The content we discussed earlier..."

"Not so fast!"

Luo Chen waved his sleeve, looking at Lin Qingxuan and Hong Ling with a half-smile.

"That transaction is between the Luo Tian Sect and Wan Jian Pavilion. These two are not included in the cooperation. Why have they come here today?"

Everyone knew what was going on. Did he have to expose it?

Plop!

The red-dressed woman gritted her teeth and knelt on the ground.

"Interfering in the Luo Tian Sect's internal strife back then was my fault, Hong Ling's. I ask the senior to be magnanimous and not hold it against a junior!"

Lin Qingxuan sighed, took out an object, and sent it floating before Luo Chen with his magical power.

"I have heard that Brother Luo is also interested in the Dao of the sword. Lin has specially prepared a copy of my sword cultivation insights as a token of apology."

Luo Chen took the thin booklet with a smile. As soon as it entered his hand, he felt a sharp sensation.

And the handwriting on it was unconventional, filled with a spirit of solitary courage.

The writing was like the man!

"Did Fellow Daoist Lin write this himself?"

Lin Qingxuan smiled self-deprecatingly. "A small trick. I hope Brother Luo will not disdain it."

How could Luo Chen disdain it? Although he already possessed a fifth-order sword canon, all his cultivation had been self-explored.

Lin Qingxuan was different. He had been trained as an orthodox sword cultivator by the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect since childhood, and in his day, he had been hailed as the best Initiate in the Jade Cauldron Domain.

This showed how solid his foundation was.

Obtaining his cultivation insights would be a tremendous supplement for Luo Chen.

Seeing Luo Chen's satisfied expression, Pavilion Master Li couldn't help but breathe a sigh of relief.

"Fellow Daoist Luo, can we discuss the transaction now?"

Luo Chen placed the booklet aside. Amidst the three's astonishment, a wisp of crimson sword qi slowly emerged from his fingertip.

"We can discuss the transaction, and the grievances between my Luo Tian Sect and the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect can also be wiped clean. But before that, this sect master has a presumptuous request."

"This sect master has not been practicing the sword dao for long. I feel I have achieved some small success, but I have rarely had the chance to test my skills against others. Thus, I do not know how far I am from a true sword cultivator."

"Today, by coincidence, I would like to ask the Solitary Swan of the Sword Realm to grant me some guidance!"

Unfortunately, BLG lost.

But life must go on. Battling life is far harder than battling in a game, so the novel must still be updated.

Goodnight, everyone.

(Chapter End)
