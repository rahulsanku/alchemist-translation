# Chapter 1042: A Prodigy Arrives, Taking Bluejade as Master

“I choose…”

In the solemn Daoist temple, it was utterly silent, with only the young man’s voice ringing out clearly.

Under everyone’s concerned gazes, he seemed to give his answer without much deliberation.

“Houtu Hall!”

In that instant, all eyes shifted to Bluejade Fairy.

Zhan Bingtao’s brow furrowed slightly, but it smoothed out in a flash, and he was even quite pleased.

It was Hui Yuanhong, also a Great Cultivator, who showed some displeasure. “Youngster, you’d better think this through. The Houtu Hall Master may be beautiful, but her years of cultivation are the shortest among us.”

“Hmph!” Bluejade Fairy gave a light snort, clearly dissatisfied with the old fellow bringing up appearances.

Zhang Bairren took over the conversation: “Hall Master Hui speaks truly. Bluejade Fairy’s cultivation time is ultimately too short. Most of her journey has been spent receiving instruction from others—how could she possibly teach you with meticulous care?”

Bluejade Fairy knew she had to defend herself now, or else Zhou Fuyou might actually be swayed.

“Precisely because my cultivation years are shorter than yours, I have an advantage in this regard. You all formed your cores and condensed your Nascent Souls hundreds of years ago. Even if your theories are rich, your memories of cultivation at those corresponding stages must be rather fuzzy. How could they be as fresh as mine?”

“That’s a flawed argument. When we teach disciples, we take a broad view and go into fine detail—how could memory affect that?” Long Ting of the Longevity Hall first refuted Bluejade Fairy, then eagerly said, “Zhou Fuyou, do you know that Bluejade Fairy’s main cultivation is of the Wood element? But from the mana fluctuations you displayed earlier, they were full of warm, moist water vapor—clearly of the Water element. That suits me perfectly!”

Bluejade Fairy’s expression darkened. “If that’s Hall Master Long’s reasoning, then why bother with selection and apprenticeship in our four halls? We might as well just divide our disciples by the Five Elements!”

Long Ting fell silent, unable to respond.

Just then, Zhan Bingtao, seated above, finally spoke.

“Zhou Fuyou, are you certain of your decision?”

Zhou Fuyou nodded firmly. “This junior wishes to join Houtu Hall and take Bluejade Fairy as my master!”

Hui Yuanhong was still dissatisfied, staring hard at him. “Give me a reason.”

“Enough!” Zhan Bingtao cut off his questioning and rapped the table. “Since the decision is made, from now on Zhou Fuyou is a member of Houtu Hall. There’s no need for him to go to the Seven Stars Court to study basic cultivation knowledge.”

Hui Yuanhong had no choice but to give up.

He couldn’t understand why Zhou Fuyou would choose Bluejade Fairy—the youngest of the four hall masters, with the lowest cultivation, the weakest influence, and even a cultivation direction completely different from his own—as his teacher.

Was it just because Bluejade Fairy had comprehended a sliver of true law and had hope of becoming an Ascendant? That ethereal reason?

Zhou Fuyou was like this, and before him, other outstanding youngsters had been the same.

If ordinary disciples had any choice in the matter of apprenticeship, Houtu Hall would probably be overflowing by now.

But they never considered that since Bluejade Fairy had such great potential, she would naturally focus on her own cultivation. Where would she find the time to frequently instruct low-level disciples?

Hugging a thigh was not as good as becoming a thigh yourself.

These youngsters truly had short-sighted vision!

And Zhan Bingtao was letting them have their way.

What he didn’t know was that Zhan Bingtao had frowned at first for the same reason.

But in just a moment, he had figured it out.

Zhou Fuyou was different from other youngsters. His talent was too terrifying; his future performance would at least be on the level of Shi Ju.

A figure like that, placed in any hall, would inevitably rise with overwhelming force.

Among the four halls, the Starry Hall was mainly composed of remnant guest elders from the Star Gate. Although there was talk of accepting them into the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, no one wanted an external faction to grow too large.

And compared to the well-preserved Zhang and Long families, the Fu family—to which Fu Qinglan belonged—had shallow foundations. Even if they gained a good seedling like Zhou Fuyou, their influence on the Tiannan Continent would never surpass the two great houses, the Zhan and the Mo.

Balance—that was what Zhan Bingtao was considering.

As for whether Fu Qinglan actually had the ability to train Zhou Fuyou?

That question didn’t worry Zhan Bingtao at all!

Once Zhou Fuyou had laid a solid foundation, he could personally step in to guide him, or even, in his capacity as the overseer of the Tiannan Continent, order the other three hall masters to instruct Zhou Fuyou together.

As long as the boy performed well at the sect’s ancestral court in the future, everyone would still benefit!

“Fellow Daoists, I’ve traveled a long way and am a bit tired. Besides, there are a few descendants of my Zhou clan inside the Dao Palace whom I’d like to see. How about we call it a day?” Zhou Yunshen said with a smile.

Zhan Bingtao nodded. “Very well. Junior Sister Bluejade, please arrange accommodations for Fellow Daoist Zhou and his party. Don’t be remiss.”

Bluejade Fairy rose gracefully. “Yes!”

…

Bluejade Fairy led the two out of the Heavenly Origin Temple and headed west, soon entering a stretch of rolling mountains.

Within, countless pavilions and towers stood in rows, with palaces laid out like fish scales.

Although most of the buildings were newly constructed, they still possessed the grandeur and solemnity of an immortal sacred land.

Especially the great hall at the mountain’s summit—wreathed in clouds and mist, with vapors rising and swirling. On closer inspection, it was a manifestation of vast amounts of spiritual qi converging to an extreme point.

Zhou Yunshen gazed at this scene. With nearly a thousand years of cultivation behind him, he couldn’t help but show a trace of envy.

“We’ve arrived. Below is Houtu Hall!”

Bluejade Fairy spoke gently.

And below, several figures had already flown up, in a welcoming posture.

“Master!”

“Master, you’re back!”

Bluejade Fairy smiled slightly. “I’ve not only returned, but I’ve also brought you a new junior brother.”

In an instant, eight pairs of unfamiliar eyes fell upon Zhou Fuyou.

Zhou Fuyou stepped forward at the right moment. “My name is Zhou Fuyou. Greetings to all senior brothers and sisters!”

The group was quite surprised.

In truth, although Zhou Fuyou himself had not yet arrived, his name had become well-known in the Tiannan Dao Palace in recent years.

Everyone knew that a peerless genius was about to join the Tiannan Dao Palace.

Not only did he possess a heavenly spirit root, but he also had the most compatible Innate Dao Body!

Such talent was the rarest of the rare.

The last example was Shi Ju.

At the height of Shi Ju’s fame, even the Ascendant ancestors had looked upon him with great favor.

Now, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect only had one Ascendant ancestor left, who had no time to instruct juniors. But they never expected that this famous genius would be taken under their master’s wing in Houtu Hall.

For a moment, everyone was curious and excited as they introduced themselves.

Eight people. Zhou Fuyou committed all their names to memory, especially the senior sister, Shi Miaoyin, because her temperament was somewhat unique. Gentle and virtuous, her gaze even carried a hint of “maternal kindness,” much like how his mother used to look at him.

Zhou Yunshen sighed with emotion beside him. “As expected of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. This Tiannan Dao Palace on this continent has only been established for a few decades, yet Houtu Hall alone has trained eight Adepts.”

Bluejade Fairy shook her head with a smile. “Not entirely trained by me. For instance, Miaoyin and four others came with their own skills. The remaining three were mostly meticulously cultivated by Sage clans since childhood, then sent to the Tiannan Dao Palace.”

After a brief explanation, Bluejade Fairy began making arrangements.

“Miaoyin, take Fuyou to the Mystic Grade, Class A, Cave Dwelling Number One!”

At these words, everyone showed expressions of surprise.

But no one objected. Shi Miaoyin stepped forward. “Junior Brother, come with me!”

Zhou Fuyou glanced at Zhou Yunshen. The old man nodded to him, and then Zhou Fuyou followed Shi Miaoyin as they flew downward.

…

“The Tiannan Dao Palace was constructed by formation grandmasters invited from the sect’s ancestral court. Using great power and means, they gathered the spiritual qi of the earth’s veins.”

“Within the Dao Palace, tens of thousands of cave dwellings have been opened. They are classified by the four grades: Heaven, Earth, Mystic, and Yellow. The letters A, B, C, and D represent the four levels: top-grade, upper-grade, middle-grade, and lower-grade.”

“Junior Brother is truly valued by Master. As soon as you arrive, you’re assigned the best second-order cave dwelling.”

Mystic Grade, Class A, Number One—that was second-order top-grade, only one step away from a third-order cave dwelling.

It was a cultivation treasure land that most Initiate cultivators could never access in their entire lives.

Shi Miaoyin led Zhou Fuyou through a quiet, sparsely populated palace, explaining in detail.

“Though called a cave dwelling, it’s actually a palace. Here, besides the main hall, there are two side halls, one bedchamber, and two mu of spirit fields.”

“The main hall is for you to receive guests and hold banquets. Of the two side halls, one is connected to earthly fire below, suitable for artifact refining and alchemy—learning the myriad arts of cultivation. The other you can arrange as you please, whether as a study or a treasure vault. Don’t waste the spirit fields—they’ve been cultivated with excellent spirit soil. Even casually planting some medicinal herbs can bring extra income.”

“The bedchamber connects to your true cultivation chamber. The spiritual qi inside is extremely abundant. If your cultivation level isn’t sufficient, I wouldn’t recommend entering it to cultivate. Even the small amount of qi that leaks out is enough for a normal Initiate cultivator to train.”

Her meticulous introduction covered every aspect.

Zhou Fuyou followed closely, listening attentively.

When Shi Miaoyin turned around and asked if there was anything he didn’t understand, Zhou Fuyou bowed respectfully.

“Thank you, Senior Sister Miaoyin!”

Such politeness caught Shi Miaoyin a bit off guard.

“Junior Brother, there’s no need for such formality.”

Zhou Fuyou’s expression was calm. He walked out of the bedchamber and looked at this quiet, beautiful place.

In the future, this would be his cultivation abode.

Shi Miaoyin stood a step behind him, watching the effeminate young man in white robes with lifelike red coral patterns, and asked gently, “Junior Brother, this cave dwelling doesn’t have a name yet. You can give it one.”

“A name?”

Zhou Fuyou thought for a moment, then said casually, “Let’s call it Dawn and Dusk Palace!”

“‘Hearing the Dao in the morning, dying in the evening is acceptable’?” Shi Miaoyin’s beautiful eyes lit up. “I didn’t expect Junior Brother’s resolve for the Dao to be so firm.”

Zhou Fuyou didn’t refute her. “That’s one way to understand it. But it could also mean that I cultivate here in the morning, and by evening I’ll move to the next, better cave dwelling. With my talent, this is merely a temporary stop.”

A second-order top-grade cave dwelling was far from enough to satisfy him.

Once he formed his core and condensed his Nascent Soul, third-order and fourth-order cave dwellings would be his true home.

Shi Miaoyin was speechless for a moment. She felt that this junior brother, whom she had met for the first time, though quiet and reserved, displayed a self-confidence far stronger than ordinary people.

Perhaps that was what matched his astonishing talent!

…

Late at night.

Zhou Fuyou was organizing the daily necessities that Shi Miaoyin had specially sent over.

There was nothing particularly rare—all common items, though those issued by the Tiannan Dao Palace were of better quality.

As for things related to cultivation, he would have to wait until he personally met his master, Bluejade Fairy, for her to decide.

The night breeze blew, moonlight spilled through the window, and Zhou Fuyou stopped what he was doing.

“Ancestor, you’ve arrived.”

Zhou Yunshen laughed heartily. “Sharp spiritual sense—you detected my presence right away.”

Zhou Fuyou was helpless. This was merely a deliberate flaw his ancestor had left.

On the journey here, the old ancestor had often played this little game with him, to train his spiritual sense’s alertness.

Zhou Yunshen casually sat down beside the bedchamber, a satisfied expression on his face.

“As expected of the Tiannan Dao Palace, and as expected of Bluejade Fairy, who has hopes of becoming an Ascendant. The spiritual vein in this area is the best among the four halls. Especially in Houtu Hall itself, the spiritual qi is nearly approaching fifth-order. Such foundations are rare in this world!”

Zhou Fuyou stood to the side and asked curiously, “I’ve read many classics since childhood and know that Central Continent was once extremely rich in spiritual qi. But legend has it that there were only two fifth-order spiritual veins: one in the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountains and one at Heaven-Earth Peak. So why does the Tiannan Dao Palace also have a fifth-order spiritual vein? Could it be that after Central Continent collapsed, these other continents gained from the misfortune?”

Zhou Yunshen shook his head. “Gaining from misfortune isn’t quite right. It’s just that after Heaven-Earth Peak collapsed, the various spiritual veins it had gathered scattered across the four continents.”

Zhou Fuyou was greatly shocked. “Just one Heaven-Earth Peak could split into so many fifth-order spiritual veins—the Tiannan Dao Palace, the Libei Dao Palace, and even the Western Continent, which has already sunk beneath the ocean?”

Zhou Yunshen said matter-of-factly, “Otherwise, why would Heaven-Earth Peak be the headquarters of the Star Gate, able to accommodate so many Sage cultivators from the five continents of the Mountains and Seas Realm? Otherwise, how could Heaven-Earth Peak withstand the descent of so many great incarnations during the Central Continent Catastrophe back then? To achieve all this, Heaven-Earth Peak had to possess spiritual vein foundations beyond ordinary estimation!”

After a moment of reflection, Zhou Yunshen changed the subject.

“I’ve already seen a few of the Zhou clan’s younger members. I’ll be leaving early tomorrow morning.”

Zhou Fuyou asked curiously, “Where is Ancestor going next?”

Zhou Yunshen didn’t answer directly. Instead, he said, “Before I go, there are some things I need to give you.”

He took off a ring from his wrinkled finger.

It was a storage ring, with a storage space a hundred or a thousand times greater than an ordinary storage pouch. But what truly made it precious was the blood-red coral pattern embroidered on it.

Wearing it meant being the clan leader of the Zhou clan!

Anyone, including the family head Zhou Junxiao, would have to obey its commands.

“From now on, the Zhou clan will listen to you.”

Zhou Fuyou was truly caught off guard, completely unable to react.

Before he could respond, Zhou Yunshen continued, “Inside are the experiential insights from my lifetime of cultivation. Additionally, there are ten top-grade spirit stones of various attributes, and a total of ten million ordinary spirit stones.”

“Finally, considering that you’re about to form your core, I’ve also prepared a pile of top-grade materials for you. These materials can be used to refine a suitable lifebound natal artifact in the future!”

Zhou Fuyou said dazedly, “Ancestor, I am unworthy of such a gift!”

But Zhou Yunshen had already placed the ring on his finger, with an unquestionable finality.

The movement was light, yet it felt infinitely heavy.

Only Zhou Yunshen himself knew that what was inside this ring was not only his “wager” but also the gift Luo Chen had left behind at White Egret Marsh all those years ago.

Once the coral ring was fitted, Zhou Yunshen patted Zhou Fuyou on the shoulder.

“Treasure the path ahead, and walk it well.”

“Go to sleep!”

Zhou Fuyou stroked the ring, still warm from the old man’s fingers, and felt an inexplicable heaviness.

That night, he did not cultivate.

When the first light of dawn began to rise, Zhou Fuyou slowly opened his eyes.

That kind and gentle old man had already vanished.

Forlornly, he pushed open the door of the bedchamber. In the early morning dew, a graceful figure came into view, as if she had been standing there for a long time.

“Disciple greets Master!”

Zhou Fuyou bowed deeply.
