# Chapter 914: Crossing the Forbidden Land, Secrets of Space

"The ghostly creatures in the Nether Abyss are growing more numerous!"

This was Luo Chen's first discovery after leaving the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land.

He had thought that after Rosycloud's sweep, the powerful ghostly beings in the abyss would have been wounded, killed, or gone into hiding, and wouldn't be so many.

But the reality was far from it!

Large numbers of low-rank ghostly creatures roamed everywhere, occasionally brushing past Luo Chen, who had concealed his aura.

There were even Ghost Kings comparable to Adepts appearing frequently.

After seeing these new ghostly creatures a few times, Luo Chen understood their origins.

"Humans!"

"From the Nether Origin Sect!"

Yes, that Ascendant-level battle that had spread across the Nether Origin Sect's sacred ground had affected all Nether Origin Sect disciples.

Many cultivators, in their confusion and ignorance, had been swept up by the aftershocks of the great powers' attacks and instantly reduced to ashes.

But because Rosycloud and the others' attacks weren't primarily aimed at them, quite a few souls had managed to survive by chance.

Now, two years had passed, and those confused souls were gradually awakening, becoming part of the endless ghostly creatures of the Nether Abyss.

"I wonder if those Nether Origin Sect Sages managed to preserve their souls?"

Luo Chen was even more curious about this.

There were three different thresholds for cultivators to transform into ghost cultivators, related to their cultivation realm before death.

The first was the Adept stage. At this level, cultivators could project their divine sense. Once they fell, after passing through the initial confused stage, they would gradually recover their memories from life. For example, Pang Renxiong of the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect back then was such a case.

If one was below the Adept stage, it was very difficult to transform into a proper ghost cultivator.

They couldn't maintain their memories and consciousness, mostly becoming dull and chaotic beings.

Like Bai Meiling—if she hadn't met Luo Chen, she might never have been able to walk the proper path of ghost cultivation.

The second threshold was the Sage stage.

At this level, a cultivator's soul could reside within their Nascent Soul. Even if their physical body collapsed, their soul had a place to dwell.

Relatively speaking, their transformation into ghost cultivators was much simpler. They didn't even need to pass through the initial confused stage and could maintain clear consciousness.

Moreover, they even had the ability to seize the bodies of living people and resume their cultivation path.

Han Zhan from the past was the best example.

Luo Chen wasn't sure if there were any ghostly creatures transformed from Nether Origin Sect Sages' souls in the Nether Abyss now, but he thought that even if there were, they wouldn't be many.

Given Rosycloud's ruthless methods, any Sage who dared to provoke her would inevitably meet their end, with both body and soul destroyed.

As for the third threshold?

Luo Chen wasn't very clear about it either, having only seen some descriptions in ancient texts.

Some powerful beings, even after their bodies and souls were destroyed, could still have their souls exist eternally.

At that point, they had two choices.

One was to switch to the ghostly path. The other was to maintain a sliver of spiritual consciousness and re-enter the cycle of reincarnation, being reborn through a womb.

Especially the latter—once successful, they would carry past-life wisdom. After awakening, relying on their previous life's experience and knowledge, their cultivation speed would be extremely fast.

With these random thoughts, Luo Chen continued forward.

After traveling a great distance from the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land, he stopped and fixed his golden pupils on the space above.

"Time to go up and take a look!"

Without hesitation, he shot up like an arrow leaving the bowstring, breaking through the water's surface.

Golden flames suppressed the abyss, preventing anything from escaping.

This had also been one of the main reasons Luo Chen had been trapped earlier.

But this time!

*Chi!*

A wisp of the same golden flame emerged from within Luo Chen's body, circled once, and then enveloped his entire body.

Then... *Shua!*

A fish leaping into the blue sky, reappearing in the mortal world.

Luo Chen looked down below, and with just one glance, he couldn't help but draw a sharp breath.

As far as the eye could see, stretching a thousand li, even ten thousand li, were countless golden flames burning slowly.

*Gulp.*

Luo Chen swallowed his saliva, a sense of disbelief rising in his heart.

"How deep is Rosycloud's magical power, to be able to unleash the Nirvana Sacred Flame over such a vast range, suppressing the Nether Abyss with the strength of one person!"

He reckoned that even if he achieved the Ascendant stage in the future and unleashed the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame, he would at most cover ten thousand li.

Let alone this majestic scene that stretched beyond the horizon.

As for now?

Even if Luo Chen exhausted all his strength, even if it meant his Nascent Soul collapsing, his Withering-Bloom Divine Flame would probably only cover a few hundred li.

Shaking his head, Luo Chen stopped thinking about others, found a direction, and flew off quickly.

At the gap in the water surface where he had emerged.

A group of ghostly creatures gradually gathered. Hesitant and curious, one Adept-level Ghost King finally couldn't resist.

It quietly swam upward, and when it reached the gap, it leaped with all its might.

Then came a scream that made all the ghostly creatures underwater tremble from the depths of their souls.

Golden flames spread, and the gap closed.

That so-called Ghost King had already turned into a wisp of green smoke, dissipating into nothingness.

...

Two days later.

Luo Chen stopped in midair, his expression extremely ugly.

He didn't dare move, as if someone had cast a immobilization spell on him. He stood there quietly in the air.

On his left arm, now bare, there was a thin red line.

As for his snow-white sleeve, it had completely disappeared.

This robe was the Snowfall Frost Garment bestowed upon him by the Nether Origin Sect.

Although it was only given to alchemists to wear, protecting them from fire poison during long periods of alchemy, its grade was not low at all—it was at the true artifact level!

Yet, without encountering any enemy, the sleeve was gone.

Not only that, even Luo Chen's extremely powerful arm had been cleanly severed at the root.

If Luo Chen hadn't reacted quickly enough, stopping his forward momentum and using his magical power to retrieve the severed arm, it wouldn't have been just a matter of a broken sleeve and hand.

Origin power flowed, the red line disappeared, and his left hand recovered as before.

Luo Chen's gaze was fixed firmly on the void. There, several black lines flickered faintly, like a giant beast opening its mouth wide, waiting for prey to walk right in.

"Space fissures!"

Luo Chen gritted his teeth, spitting out these four words like soybeans.

He was no stranger to this peculiar phenomenon.

When Rosycloud struck down Yanfu Mountain with one blow, the terrifying power had shaken the void, revealing strands of space fissures.

But he hadn't expected that even after the battle had ended, space fissures still existed here.

Moreover, there were quite a few of them, and they were unstable, appearing and disappearing at times, making them hard to guard against.

Were they left behind by Rosycloud?

Or had the fierce battle between Ascendant-level great powers caused this realm to be unable to bear the strain?

Luo Chen didn't know.

He only knew that the path above the abyss was not easy to travel.

It might even be more dangerous than below the abyss.

"And!"

Luo Chen frowned as he looked toward the high sky.

Dark clouds gathered there, with lightning bolts flashing from time to time.

"Why hasn't the thundercloud dispersed for so long?"

This time, he managed to discern some clues.

Luo Chen murmured, "A formation?"

Not just a formation, but a formation that had gone out of control!

Luo Chen recalled something.

Ye Lingtian had once said that the Nether Origin Sect's protective sect formation incorporated the methods of successive generations of ancestors.

Among them was a divine ability from the great power Bai Ye, called the Nine Heavens Universal Great Thunder Art.

Thunder Daozi had even relied on the accumulation of this art to once break through to Rosycloud's level.

Now that the Nether Origin Sect was destroyed, the sect-protecting grand formation should have vanished along with it.

But based on Luo Chen's formation knowledge, this formation was not established with people as its foundation, but with the earth as its base.

The Nether Abyss still existed, so the formation base hadn't collapsed.

It was just that without human control, the remnants of the formation had lost control, unrestrainedly drawing upon the power of thunder between heaven and earth.

"Another Accumulated Thunder Nine Mountains!"

Luo Chen sighed. Seeing that the space rift had dissipated quite a bit, he continued flying.

But compared to before, his speed was much, much slower.

It was barely better than an Initiate cultivator's speed.

He truly didn't dare to touch those space fissures again.

If he reacted too slowly, it wouldn't just be a matter of losing a hand.

If his entire body were drawn in, even with his desolate beast-level self-healing ability, he would likely be ground to dust in an instant, never to recover.

In this situation, even his powerful divine sense was useless.

Space fissures were not living beings; they had no aura, no soul fluctuations, and weren't even solid like mountains, rocks, earth, or wood.

What could a person perceive in the void?

Fortunately, Luo Chen had a pair of Crimson-Golden Eyes!

As long as he looked carefully enough, there wasn't much danger.

And as he observed the appearance of space fissures day after day, Luo Chen gradually noticed some patterns.

"If we compare this realm to a large house, then space cracks are caused by the instability of the walls, roof, foundation, and tiles."

"Their appearance is actually not without warning."

"Often, before they form, there is an extremely faint spatial fluctuation, which then triggers a chain reaction, causing them to take shape."

Upon discovering this, Luo Chen felt inexplicably delighted!

Because there was once a powerful enemy that had left him helpless, and that enemy was extremely proficient in this kind of escape art.

Borrowing the power of space, appearing and disappearing unpredictably, impossible to track, hard to find.

Whenever encountering that one, all enemies could only passively take a beating. Yet that one's attacks were simple and unadorned, yet carried an irresistible power.

"If I become familiar with this kind of spatial fluctuation, will I no longer be so passive the next time I encounter Azure Frost?"

The relationship between Green Parasol Mountain and Luo Chen had eased considerably, no longer so irreconcilable.

There was no deep hatred between Azure Frost and him.

But Luo Chen really disliked that feeling of powerlessness, of being completely suppressed by someone.

Rosycloud was too far from him; there wasn't even room to treat her as a hypothetical enemy.

But Azure Frost was at least in the same Sage stage as him.

Luo Chen felt that even if he encountered any same-level strong opponent, he would have the strength to fight. Yet in front of Azure Frost, even if he self-detonated his Nascent Soul, he might not be able to leave a mark on her.

How could he be willing to accept that?

When another space rift formed, Luo Chen stopped.

He was still on guard, but now he added more observation.

When his eyes felt dry, he even took the initiative to drip two drops of Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid into them.

"People walk through heaven and earth; fish swim in water."

"Fish are unaware of it; people also take it for granted."

"But when water moves, there are waves. When people move, there should also be a sense of spatial change."

"It's just that because we are too weak and cannot comprehend the true meaning of spatial laws, we cannot feel that kind of spatial change."

"Everyone is like this, and I am too. But observing now, this kind of change seems to have some connection with the Space Seal among the seven basic formation seals."

The seven basic formation prohibitions—Mountain, Water, Wind, Cloud, Obscurity, Brightness, and Space.

Mountain and Water prohibitions, being physical, were the easiest to begin with.

Wind and Cloud prohibitions were unpredictable, but at least they could be felt and observed, so they weren't too difficult.

Obscurity and Brightness restrictions were more in the realm of the soul. Luo Chen's best skill in his early years was the obscuring restriction.

Only the space restriction was the most difficult, hard for ordinary people to learn.

It was only after Luo Chen became a fourth-rank formation master and created that "Nine Heavens Ten Earths Grand Transposition Formation" that he gained some experience.

Now, using the space prohibition as a stepping stone to understand the birth, appearance, existence, and eventual disappearance of these space fissures, he seemed to have found some clues.

Just as Luo Chen was concentrating on his research.

Suddenly!

A flash of white light.

"Not good!"

Luo Chen had left a sliver of his divided soul to monitor the outside world, so he naturally noticed something was wrong.

A thunderbolt fell from the nine heavens!

Under normal circumstances, he could easily fly away to dodge it.

But now, his surroundings were filled with newly formed space fissures. How could he recklessly charge through them?

He had no choice but to take the hit head-on!

Luo Chen's expression remained calm as he cast a spell toward the sky.

Sevenfold Blazing Heaven Rings!

Ring after ring of fire appeared, only to instantly dissipate under the thunderbolt's impact.

Luo Chen wasn't surprised. The upper limit of this defensive technique was about this much.

To withstand this thunderbolt drawn by the Nine Heavens Universal Great Thunder Art divine ability, he would need to put in more effort.

Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain!

A phoenix phantom rose from behind him and collided with the thunderbolt.

All of this, though complicated to describe, happened in the blink of an eye.

After two consecutive moves, the thunderbolt slowly dissipated.

Only then did the deafening thunderclap explode in his ears.

Luo Chen's entire body trembled, with strands of electric light still flickering over him.

Clearly, this falling thunder from the sky was not so easy to receive.

And this was without anyone controlling it.

It was hard to imagine, if the Nether Origin Sect were at its peak and someone tried to forcefully break through the sect-protecting grand formation, with an Ascendant great power personally activating the divine abilities within it, what kind of destructive power it would have.

Probably even Rosycloud would find it difficult to cross the Nether Abyss under that pressure!

As his magical power circulated, frost and snow spread from the Snowfall Frost Garment on his body, forcing out the residual lightning power.

Just as Luo Chen breathed a sigh of relief, his pupils suddenly contracted.

Because of the commotion just now, the surrounding heaven and earth spiritual energy had become even more chaotic.

The batch of space fissures that had just formed seemed to be affected. Through a chain reaction, they visibly grew inch by inch.

In particular, a rift above him had already reached seven or eight zhang in length and was spreading toward Luo Chen.

Without a second thought, Luo Chen's body dropped.

With the force of a thousand jun, he plunged beneath the sea surface.

*Plop!*

As the waves churned, Luo Chen's figure disappeared from this realm.

The surrounding space cracks spread like a spider's web, lingering for a long time even after Luo Chen had left.

Such a scene would make even Sage cultivators want to avoid it at all costs.

The Nether Abyss had once been a forbidden land, but now, after the Ascendant-level battle, it had become a place of certain death!

A place where no living being could survive!

But Luo Chen had no time to observe all this.

Because at this moment, he was holding his breath, staring into the dark depths.

A pair of eyes, as large as lanterns, glowed with blood-red light, fixedly staring at him!

(End of this chapter)
