# Chapter 976: Whitebone Boat, Great Power Comes from the East

Luo Chen had been planning his departure from Soul Island for a long time.

Unlike conventional sea voyages, he could not seek calm weather; instead, he had to do the opposite.

Beneath the calm surface of the Yellow Spring Sea, undercurrents surged, and the suppression of the soul was extremely powerful. With his divine sense unable to spread far, it was easy to fall into danger.

On the contrary, during times of great wind and waves, the undercurrents had already been triggered. Although it appeared more dangerous, the danger was only on the surface.

Under such circumstances, as long as one was cautious enough and dealt with each move as it came, there would always be a chance to leave.

These days, Luo Chen had been refining the Myriad Formation Gu left behind by Granny Redleaf.

He had to admit that the Southern Frontier Gu cultivator lineage, while perhaps slightly weaker in frontal combat, had a very commendable approach to using Gu insects to assist in cultivation and battle.

Take this Formation Gu, for example!

Ordinary formation masters could naturally use treasures like formation flags and formation plates to assist in setting up formations, but both the effect and speed depended on the formation master themselves.

But with a Formation Gu, it was completely different.

Such living Gu insects themselves contained a large number of basic restrictions. The formation master only needed to rearrange and recombine them on this basis to quickly obtain the formation they desired.

Even if there were deficiencies, the formation master could add additional restrictions to piece together a new formation.

This kind of auxiliary effect was one plus one greater than two.

Especially for accomplished formation masters like Luo Chen and Granny Redleaf, it was like adding wings to a tiger.

Refining the Myriad Formation Gu was also Luo Chen's way of keeping a backup plan.

If he couldn't hold on in the Yellow Spring Sea, at least he could use the defensive formations prepared by the Myriad Formation Gu to buy himself a moment to catch his breath.

Originally, Luo Chen had always been prepared for this—that was Black King!

Whether it was for cultivation, travel, or even battle, the reason Luo Chen chose to keep Black King by his side was so that he could suddenly appear and buy him a chance to breathe.

But the Yellow Spring Sea waves had come so abruptly that the two were forced apart.

He could only seek another way out now.

The Myriad Formation Gu was a good choice.

"I wonder how Black King is doing now?"

In the early morning, Luo Chen put on his half-damaged Mystic Dust Armor and stood silently on the shore.

Thinking of Black King, a rare trace of worry rose in his heart.

The Southern Ocean was mysterious and dangerous. Without his protection, although Black King was strong, it would be difficult for him to move even an inch.

Especially since the Yellow Spring Sea had destroyed all life wherever it passed. Could Black King have escaped this undeserved calamity?

In his sea of consciousness, there was a drop of Black King's soul blood.

Under normal circumstances, he could use this to sense Black King's life or death.

But on this Soul Island, he could feel nothing. After all, even his own divine sense could not spread far, so how could he expect a drop of soul blood to respond?

Only by leaving the Yellow Spring Sea could he sense Black King's condition again!

And the day to leave was today!

Luo Chen's mind stirred, and the next moment, his body suddenly changed.

*Chi!*

Cyan flames flickered out, transforming into a thin, gauzy robe that enveloped his entire body.

It was the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire!

But it was different from before.

At this moment, the intensity of the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire had not actually increased much, but the utilization of its details far surpassed the past.

At least, Luo Chen had never been able to achieve such delicate changes before.

The fire robe was light and transparent.

But it covered his entire body, leaving no gaps.

This was the benefit of a powerful soul foundation—the ability to control fifth-grade divine fire with meticulous precision.

In this way, although the power did not increase, the consumption of mana was greatly reduced.

And this was also the confidence Luo Chen had to forcefully cross the Yellow Spring Sea!

Since the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire could protect him safely to Soul Island on the way here, it should be the same on the way back.

*Crash, crash...*

The waves in the Yellow Spring Sea had been quite turbulent recently, surging day and night without rest.

Luo Chen carefully stepped into the turbid waves.

As soon as he entered, the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire on his body crackled—the fire robe he had manifested was constantly being consumed.

But with Luo Chen's mana supply, the fire robe was also being replenished at all times.

"Even if I have to collapse a body of Sage-level mana, I will get out!"

Luo Chen's heart was set, and his figure resolutely disappeared into the vast yellow sea.

...

*Crash!*

Another huge wave crashed against the Soul-Breaking Cliff.

When the spray receded, Luo Chen's figure reappeared on the black sand beach.

It had been less than the time it takes to drink a cup of tea since he set out.

Had he failed?

But there was no disappointment on Luo Chen's face; instead, he looked quite surprised.

He left the black sand beach and stood on a nearby reef, gazing into the distance, as if waiting for something.

This wait was not long.

After about the time it takes to drink a cup of tea, a huge object appeared in his field of vision.

A flash of bright red rose and fell in the yellow sea.

A gleam of light burst from Luo Chen's eyes, and he blurted out, "It really is it—the Ghost Immortal Tower!"

When another huge wave crashed in, a loud impact sound rang out from Soul Island.

*Boom!*

As the tide receded, the massive body of the Ghost Immortal Tower was revealed.

At a single glance, there were layers of tower phantoms and swirling, dense ghostly aura.

In a trance, it seemed as if immortals were roaming, with jade towers and pavilions, the cold of the heights unbearable.

But with a low shout from Luo Chen, all the illusions dissipated.

"As expected of the Desolate Beast Ghost Immortal Tower. Even in death, the mind-confusing illusions still exist at all times."

Luo Chen praised it, but was not alarmed.

With his current soul foundation, he could remain completely unaffected by such passive illusions.

With the illusions gone, Luo Chen could finally see the true form of this desolate beast clearly.

Even lying down, it was dozens of *zhang* tall!

And its legs, like elephant feet, stretched for hundreds of *zhang*, reaching straight into the Yellow Spring Sea.

His gaze moved upward, and huge tentacles spread out from its body.

Luo Chen knew that these tentacles were from the famous Northern Sea demon beast, the Great King Squid.

The Ghost Immortal Tower fed on Great King Squids. After eating, it would deliberately leave these tentacles, and over time, similar tentacles would even grow on its body.

But now, the once-feared tentacles were in tatters, most of them severed, a pitiful sight.

Seeing this, Luo Chen's heart trembled.

When he had seen the Ghost Immortal Tower back then, it was only at the fourth-grade late stage.

But it was said that during the Northern Sea human-demon war, the Ghost Immortal Tower had advanced to the fifth grade, comparable to a great Ascendant power.

A fourth-grade Ghost Immortal Tower was already terrifying enough. How powerful must a fifth-grade one be?

Yet such an existence had fallen to this state. It was hard to imagine how brutal that battle in Central Continent had been not long ago.

And what kind of existence could have beaten the Ghost Immortal Tower to death alive!

Swallowing his saliva, Luo Chen's gaze fell on the Ghost Immortal Tower's head.

On its tumor-like head were two empty, hollow pits.

Those were the eye sockets, where the parasitic demon beast Blood Lantern had once dwelled.

But now, there were only sunken eye sockets, with no light to be seen.

"A fifth-grade desolate beast, fallen to such a state?"

In his lament, Luo Chen cautiously approached the Ghost Immortal Tower.

This was the reason he had temporarily given up on leaving!

The corpse of a desolate beast was a rare material in the Mountains and Seas Realm.

No matter what it was used for, it would certainly be of great use.

Especially since this corpse had drifted across the Yellow Spring Sea. If he took its skin, flesh, and bones to make a dharma boat, he could save a lot of effort and leave this sea area more easily.

But just as Luo Chen was about to get close, the Ghost Immortal Tower's corpse, which should have been lifeless, suddenly erupted with a red glow.

Luo Chen did not hesitate; he retreated.

Not only that, but the Myriad Formation Gu hidden on his body flickered with spiritual light, and layers of formations that concealed his aura enveloped him.

Only after retreating to a certain distance did Luo Chen stop.

Now, as he looked, the massive corpse began to tremble strangely.

A large lump bulged on the tumor-like head, growing higher and higher.

Suddenly!

*Pfft...*

The blood lump burst, and a thing like a doll crawled out from inside.

This doll had short limbs, an extremely thin neck, and only a large head that was particularly prominent.

It sat blankly on the Ghost Immortal Tower's head, dazed for a moment as if it hadn't realized what had happened. Finally, its gaze fell on the beach.

It opened its mouth, and a large amount of black sand flew toward it.

"Gaba gaba."

"Gaba gaba."

The sound of chewing came from its mouth, and its originally wilted aura gradually began to strengthen.

This scene made Luo Chen extremely surprised.

"Is this the true body of the Ghost Immortal Tower?"

"It can directly chew soul stones raw!"

"And it seems to be familiar with this place, knowing the effects of soul stones very well. It started devouring them as soon as it came out."

Thinking of this, Luo Chen couldn't help but hide his aura even deeper.

No other reason!

The soul stones on that beach were already few and far between.

Most of them had been collected by him in advance.

What remained now was negligible.

The big-headed doll-like Ghost Immortal Tower seemed to realize this as well. After eating soul stones for a while and finding no more, it actually started wailing loudly.

It was as if it were crying.

It struggled to jump down from the corpse, trying to find more soul stones.

Suddenly!

It raised its huge head and looked into the distance.

On the Yellow Spring Sea, the wind and waves were still raging, with nothing special about it.

But its big, black eyes actually showed a hint of fear.

Then, as if it had made up its mind, it turned without hesitation and charged toward the Soul-Breaking Cliff.

Watching this from afar, Luo Chen's eyelids couldn't help but twitch violently.

An unexpected scene appeared.

That big-headed doll, after crashing headfirst into the cliff wall of the Soul-Breaking Cliff, actually merged directly into it, disappearing completely.

In an instant, there was no more life here.

Watching this scene, Luo Chen took a deep breath.

Some guesses surfaced in his mind.

Could it be that the birthplace of the Desolate Beast Ghost Immortal Tower is this Soul-Breaking Cliff? And even the Soul-Breaking Cliff didn't hesitate to cross oceans and travel millions of miles straight to Central Continent, all because of the Ghost Immortal Tower?

Luo Chen didn't have much confidence in this guess; it was merely inferred from the fact that it could seamlessly merge into the Soul-Breaking Cliff.

What truly put him on guard now was the reason it had hidden.

What was on the Yellow Spring Sea that made the Ghost Immortal Tower so afraid?

Luo Chen began to retreat.

He retreated cautiously, heading toward a hidden place he had discovered over the years.

But his eyes were fixed on the surface of the Yellow Spring Sea, trying to see something.

In a trance, it seemed as if a bone boat was crossing the sea.

...

*Swoosh!*

The whitebone dharma boat pushed through layers of turbid waves, coming from the east.

Two graceful figures stood at the bow, their skirts fluttering, gazing into the distance.

The one at the lead was dressed as a married woman, with a serene appearance and a gentle temperament.

Looking at the tall, solitary peak in the distance, she sighed emotionally, "Is that the legendary Soul-Breaking Cliff?"

The cold woman behind her said flatly, "It is also the burial ground you have chosen for yourselves."

The beautiful woman sighed softly. "If the main sect hadn't ordered it, who would want to fight to the death with your sister? Besides, even if the fish dies, the net may not break. Your sister is severely injured; she may not be our match."

The cold woman said nothing more.

The beautiful woman smiled helplessly.

"Azure Frost, if you are willing to surrender to my Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, after this battle, I can spare your life. If you can guard Central Continent..."

When she mentioned Central Continent, her tone paused, and a look of sorrow spread in her eyes.

She paused for a moment, then continued, "If you can also guard the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect for five hundred years, the main sect of the Mystic Realm in the future can even guide you to ascend to the upper realm. I, Chunyuan, can guarantee it for you!"

Azure Frost sneered and did not answer.

Seeing this, Chunyuan did not press further, only sighing at the depth of sisterly love.

Then, she focused all her attention on the distant Soul-Breaking Cliff, her expression quite serious.

Whether this place was the best ambush site as her sect master brother had said, or the burial ground for the five of them as Azure Frost had claimed, would depend on the outcome.

Suddenly!

Her gaze froze, landing on a collapsed tall tower.

"The Ghost Immortal Tower?"

"I didn't expect the escaped Ghost Immortal Tower to have drifted here."

The whitebone dharma boat immediately sped up, pushing through the turbid waves, and soon arrived at that barren beach.

As soon as she set foot on the island, Chunyuan released her divine sense.

But unexpectedly, the range of her divine sense was only about a hundred *li*, unable to cover the entire island.

"It is indeed true that the Soul-Breaking Cliff has the power to suppress the soul."

Muttering to herself, she walked straight to the massive corpse of the Ghost Immortal Tower.

After a quick inspection, she was completely reassured.

The desolate beast Ghost Immortal Tower was dead, leaving only its corpse, buried on Soul Island.

She turned around and looked at the tall woman who was a head taller than her, smiling slightly.

"Rosycloud led four wild beasts and nine Ghost Gods to ambush our sect. The result is that she is severely injured, the Ghost Gods are all dead, and of the four wild beasts, one is dead, one is injured, one has fled, and even this Ghost Immortal Tower died here."

"The foundation of my Dao Sect is far beyond what you can imagine."

"Now, do you still think your sister can save you?"

Azure Frost still did not answer, but her eyes were clearly much dimmer.

She had not personally participated in that battle, but had been in the Eastern Wilderness, tying down part of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's forces.

But judging by the result, it seemed her sister had still underestimated the foundation of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, resulting in mutual destruction.

If the Five Elders of Heavenly Origin succeeded in using her as bait to set a trap for Rosycloud...

Azure Frost dared not imagine the consequences.

Seeing her like this, Chunyuan's heavy mood lightened slightly.

Mana surged out, bringing Azure Frost up to the ridge above.

She would go ahead and choose a battlefield for her fellow disciples in advance.

And in a hidden place at the bottom of the cliff.

Luo Chen, who was doing his utmost to conceal his aura, was now filled with shock.

The other party's divine sense had not swept over him, but his eyesight was still good enough. By interpreting the beautiful woman's lip movements, he had roughly learned of the major events happening in the outside world.

But none of that mattered!

What mattered was, how had the Soul-Breaking Cliff become a battlefield for Ascendant-level powers?

How was he supposed to escape now?
