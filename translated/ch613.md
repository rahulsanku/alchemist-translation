# Chapter 613: Natal Magic Treasure, First Display of Wonder

"Congratulations, Master! Felicitations, Master!"

Inside the cave dwelling, Black King flattered him with heartfelt sincerity.

What answered him was one sentence.

"Do not make noise. I am thinking."

Black King flicked his snake tongue, his face gloomy, and slunk off to one side. Only his vertical pupils still looked now and then toward Luo Chen, whose brows were tightly furrowed.

It had clearly succeeded. Why was he still unhappy?

Yes, it had clearly succeeded.

"It should not be..."

Luo Chen looked at the armor plate in his hand in shock and uncertainty, then changed his mana and activated it.

In an instant, the blue armor plate transformed into a ray of light and enveloped Luo Chen's body, its patterns ancient and plain, like flowing water.

From the top of his head to the soles of his feet, across his limbs and torso, even over the most vulnerable parts, his eyes, a layer of qi barrier appeared.

He summoned the Flowing Fire Hammer and smashed it against himself.

Boom!

A muffled sound rang out. Luo Chen did not move at all. Although a powerful shock force struck his organs, under Luo Chen's own formidable physique, it had almost no effect.

His gaze fell on Black King.

"Release a water arrow to attack me."

Black King twisted awkwardly. "Master, that is not too good, is it? How would this little one dare offend a superior from below? That would be great disrespect..."

"Too much nonsense. If I tell you to release it, release it. Control the attack power to around early third-tier."

As Luo Chen's tone grew heavier, Black King no longer dared delay. He immediately gathered demon power and spat out a black water arrow.

The water arrow came fiercely, demonic qi cold and sharp. A Foundation Establishment cultivator would die on contact, and even an early Golden Core cultivator would have to defend carefully.

Yet when this water arrow flew over, Luo Chen neither dodged nor evaded. He merely activated the Dark Blue Mysterious Armor to its limit.

Hiss.

A light sound emerged. The deep blue radiance shook a few times, then returned to calm.

"It indeed carries the characteristics of that defensive magic treasure method. Apart from the armor body itself, it also generates qi armor. It can defend against physical attacks from magic treasure bodies and also withstand energy attacks from cultivator mana or demon beast demon power."

"In other words, I truly succeeded in one try."

After verifying it both ways, the shock in Luo Chen's heart surged even more fiercely.

"It should not be!"

He had always been accustomed to failure. So-called success also came only after repeated familiarity, arriving naturally when the conditions were right.

But this time, it had been so easy.

Luo Chen understood his own artifact-forging art very clearly.

Even if he had not heard the Moya Cave Master's assessment of him, if he did hear it, he would only agree.

At present, Luo Chen's artifact-forging skill still remained only at the level of refining magic instruments.

His success rate was highest with lower and mid-grade instruments. With upper-grade magic instruments, after failing a few times, he could occasionally succeed.

Only magic treasures were entirely beyond him.

Even when he had previously tried and failed to refine magic treasures, obtaining several supreme-grade magic instruments, that was because the raw materials were excellent and preserved part of their power.

For this, Luo Chen had lost many spirit stones and had no choice but to risk offending the Myriad Immortals Assembly's local artifact-forgers by selling some magic instruments to recover costs.

At that time, Luo Chen had known that if he wanted to achieve something on the path of artifact-forging, the road would be long and difficult.

He had also analyzed why this was so.

There were several reasons.

First, the time he had immersed himself in artifact-forging was too short. Second, he lacked instruction from a famous teacher. Although Duan Feng had laid a foundation for him early on, Duan Feng's own artifact-forging level had not reached the grand hall.

Finally, it had to do with his artifact-forging style.

When other artifact-forgers forged artifacts, earth fire, furnaces, and cauldrons were all indispensable. Formation masters and fire workers worked day and night without pause, and from time to time they used various secret arts and secret medicines to properly adjust the refining state of the weapon.

But he himself was very simple. What he relied on was merely the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

As for the flame most needed in artifact-forging, he also used mana to drive the Withering-Bloom True Flame, for convenience and ease.

The more he understood himself, the lower Luo Chen placed his expectations for refining the Demon Emperor shed husk.

He did not at all hope to reach the level of an artifact-forging master before refining the Demon Emperor shed husk. Instead, he planned to begin once the six sets of mysterious armor reached the supreme-grade magic instrument level.

But now, an accident had appeared.

The first time he worked on one of the six sets of mysterious armor, the Dark Blue Mysterious Armor, he had succeeded directly.

"Could I be an unprecedented artifact-forging genius? So-called magic treasures, easily made with a flick of the hand?"

Luo Chen muttered, then immediately threw out this possibility.

If he were truly a genius, he would not have failed the previous few attempts to refine offensive lower-grade magic treasures.

He glanced at the system panel.

Second-Tier Artifact-Forger.

Sure enough, his artifact-forging level still remained at the second tier and had not reached the level needed to forge lower-grade magic treasures.

The system judged it the same way.

Yet now he had truly refined a lower-grade magic treasure, and the most precious kind among magic treasures: a defensive magic treasure, which almost never circulated on the market.

The success was real, but his artifact-forger rank did not rise.

This meant that, in the system's judgment, his artifact-forging level had not yet reached that tier.

"Why would this happen?"

Luo Chen sank into thought.

Inside the cave dwelling, an indescribable silence descended.

Even restless Black King did not dare breathe loudly for fear of disturbing Luo Chen's thinking.

After a long while.

Luo Chen slowly raised his head, his gaze landing on the great cauldron standing inside the cave.

"Could it be because of it?"

After thinking it over and over, the only variable seemed to have appeared in the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Luo Chen remembered very clearly that the previous few attempts to refine lower-grade magic treasures had all used conventional forging methods. Processing mineral materials, fusing and smelting them, shaping them with mana or molds, and finally engraving formations. His failures had mostly occurred in the fusion stage and the formation-engraving stage.

But this time, forging the Dark Blue Mysterious Armor had followed a somewhat different process.

Because Huangfu Song came to provoke him, Luo Chen had been forced to interrupt the magic treasure forging and leave the raw materials idle inside the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

The later fusion and smelting, and even the adaptation stage after engraving the formation, had all been carried out by placing it inside the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Especially during this half year on Quyang Island, he had been absorbed in using soul essence to cultivate the Dragon-Slaying Art. Without noticing, he had nurtured the Dark Blue Mysterious Armor's embryo inside the Primordial Unity Cauldron for half a year.

Half believing and half doubting, Luo Chen flew into the air.

Under Black King's wide-eyed stare, he flew directly into the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

One had to know, the fierce flames beneath the cauldron had not yet gone out.

"Is Master going to stew himself in the great cauldron?"

Inside the cauldron.

Luo Chen extended a hand, ignoring the scorching heat, and touched the solid cauldron wall.

To the touch, it seemed metal and yet not metal, while also possessing the warmth and softness of plants and trees. If one sensed carefully, there was even wind and thunder power stirring without rest.

All along, he had kept this natal magic treasure inside his qi sea to nurture. Even when he occasionally observed it, he mostly swept it with divine sense.

This time, he changed perspective.

From outside the body, he sensed it by touching it with his flesh.

The moment he made contact, a feeling of being connected by blood came over him.

A natal magic treasure was something cultivated with both life and fate, so such a feeling was normal.

As he continued sensing it, Luo Chen faintly gained some understanding.

Since the day this cauldron had been forged, he had already nurtured it for nearly thirty years.

When it had first formed, although it had an upper-grade rank, that had been because of its materials. Its own power had not been particularly outstanding.

Under constant nurturing, the impurities inside the cauldron grew fewer and fewer, and its various wonders gradually began to manifest.

During previous battles, nine crimson chains often appeared around Luo Chen. That was one of this cauldron's great uses. Those chains had an extremely powerful binding effect. With the Primordial Unity Cauldron as the center, they also carried a faint automatic enemy-seeking effect.

This was also one of the reasons Luo Chen had instantly discovered the white-robed cultivator Yan Zhixian's sneak attack during the demon hunter assessment mission.

In addition, the Primordial Unity Cauldron's suppressive effect had also begun to bloom.

Back then, under Luo Chen's full control, the Primordial Unity Cauldron had forcefully suppressed Golden Core Stage Seven Qian Ting. Later, it had also suppressed a Golden Core Ghost King comparable to late Golden Core. These were all clear proof.

But clearly, the effects of this natal magic treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, were not limited to this.

It itself had five great functions.

Suppression, confinement, locking, nurturing, and refining.

As described above, only suppression and locking had begun to show their effects.

In ordinary times, Black King lived inside it. That was the wonder of confinement, because an internal space had formed within.

But as one of Green Pill Valley's three sect-suppressing cauldrons, the Primordial Unity Cauldron's most important functions were clearly the latter two.

Nurturing and refining.

Luo Chen had used it for alchemy over the years and found it extremely convenient. Occasionally, when refining pills, it would also break through the grade and produce pills of better quality.

But he had truly never tested the effect of nurturing.

If he had to look for the reason this magic treasure had been refined successfully in one try, the greatest possibility was that the Primordial Unity Cauldron's nurturing function had taken effect.

"But why did I never discover before that this function was powerful to this degree?"

According to Luo Chen's estimate, tempering the Primordial Unity Cauldron's impurities would take at least several hundred years before full success could be achieved.

Only at that time would this treasure be completely rounded and unified. After all, when it was first refined, it had swallowed far too many raw materials that had not been processed beforehand.

Suddenly.

Luo Chen opened his mouth and spat out a small basin, which landed in his hand.

"It is because of this Dragon-Nurturing Basin!"

He spoke the conclusion decisively.

The Dragon-Nurturing Basin obtained from Qian Ting primarily had the effect of nurturing magic treasures.

Over the past five or six years, he had been using this treasure inside his qi sea to nurture the Primordial Unity Cauldron, the Shattered Moon Wings, and other treasures.

Unknowingly, the Shattered Moon Wings, which had accumulated many years of foundation already, had advanced to mid-grade magic treasure.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron seemed unchanged, but in truth, under accelerated nurturing, it had quietly improved as well.

And its greatest improvement was precisely the nurturing power that came from the same source as the Dragon-Nurturing Basin.

"If that is the case, then it explains why I could refine the Dark Blue Mysterious Armor, this lower-grade magic treasure, while the system still judges that I have not reached third-tier artifact-forger."

"Because that was not refined by me at all. It was nurtured by the Primordial Unity Cauldron for me."

After thinking this through, Luo Chen's mind instantly opened.

All confusion was swept clean.

Many stray thoughts even surfaced in his heart.

"If it can nurture the Dark Blue Mysterious Armor, that means it can also nurture the other five mysterious armors."

"It might even be possible for the Demon Emperor shed husk."

"No, not only that. I can fully borrow the Primordial Unity Cauldron to refine other magic treasures on a large scale. Even if the system does not count it as my artifact-forging level, if I succeed often enough, I will always find the trick to success."

Failure was the mother of success.

But those who succeeded would always succeed.

The two were not contradictory, and the latter could bring Luo Chen unexpected gains.

Leaving aside other things, take defensive magic treasures.

Their value far exceeded ordinary offensive magic treasures.

An ordinary lower-grade offensive magic treasure was priced within one hundred thousand. Even now, when war flared everywhere across the Northern Sea and weapon prices rose with the tide, the premium only just pushed the price above one hundred thousand.

But defensive magic treasures were naturally one or two times more expensive than offensive magic treasures of the same tier.

Under the current situation in the Northern Sea, a defensive magic treasure of decent quality could easily sell for three or four hundred thousand.

One could even say there was demand but no supply.

If placed in a large auction, and if someone found it pleasing to the eye or suitable to their spirit root attribute, it was not impossible for them to empty their fortune for it.

As thoughts rose one after another, Luo Chen vaguely saw golden light filling the sky. That was an endless source of wealth.

With a leap, Luo Chen jumped out of the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Looking at the great cauldron beneath him, his expression was incomparably excited.

"This cauldron is truly like adding wings to a tiger for me."

In the corner, Black King instantly straightened his neck and shouted, "Congratulations, Master! Felicitations, Master!"

"Haha, you wicked beast, you have learned how to read expressions."

One had to say that this time, Black King's flattery had finally found the right moment.

In his joy, Luo Chen casually tossed out the blue armor plate on his body, sending it flying toward Black King.

Black King's eyes widened. Could it be...

He had already opened his great mouth. "Many thanks, Master, for the rewa..."

Suddenly, the armor plate stopped in the air.

Luo Chen smiled and recalled it.

"Although this object is not compatible with my mana, it is still a lower-grade defensive magic treasure, priceless in value. If I take it out and sell it, it can also establish the name of Daoist Azure Yang, artifact-forging master. Giving it to you would truly be somewhat wasteful."

Black King's wild joy seemed as if it had been doused with a basin of cold water, instantly wilting.

Luo Chen smiled and shook his head.

"Fine. Considering that you watch my house and courtyard, tend the fire and watch the furnace for me, even if you have no merit, you have still worked hard. Quyang Island has many complete water-attribute demon beast corpses. In a few days, I will open the furnace and refine a batch of supreme-grade Imperial Moonlight Nectar for you."

Hearing this, Black King perked up again.

Supreme-grade Imperial Moonlight Nectar.

He had not drunk that in quite a few years.

That thing did not look high in grade, but when accumulated little by little, it continuously unearthed the power deep in his bloodline.

The reason Black King had been able to advance step by step from a Black-Scale Jiaolong with inferior bloodline to where he was now, even surpassing the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull that had once been more powerful than him, lay in the fact that the amount of Imperial Moonlight Nectar he had taken far exceeded Heavenjade's.

Compared with external objects, he valued the improvement of his own body more.

"Go out first. I need to sort through my next cultivation plan."

After Black King happily left, Luo Chen put away the various treasures and let his thoughts enter a calm state.

Now that another function of the Primordial Unity Cauldron had been developed, he had to consider how to maximize the use of this function.

This was not useless work.

It could broaden his sources of wealth, improve his artifact-forging art, and ultimately feed back into his own cultivation and strength.

Therefore, it had to be carefully sorted through. There could be no carelessness.

...

Three months later.

On the seaside beach, a female Daoist and a red-robed Daoist walked side by side.

The elegant ink-white bamboo robe trailed behind her, yet not the slightest sand or dust stained it.

The female Daoist's gaze fell on the enormous figure in the ocean, swallowing and exhaling vast spiritual qi and stirring the seawater into surging waves.

From her mouth came a voice filled with both admiration and envy.

"Daoist Azure Yang, your spirit pet is truly formidable. From what I see of his realm, he is even above you and me, and he even gives me the feeling that his potential is endless. Is this truly the most ordinary Black-Scale Giant Python?"

Luo Chen laughed freely. "My luck is somewhat good, I suppose. Black King's origin was not much, but he wins by cultivating diligently. Thus he achieved this sight of python transforming into jiaolong, swallowing and exhaling the sea."

When he said this, his gaze also occasionally landed on the part of Black King's body exposed above the water.

Unlike the pitch-black appearance from their first meeting, the scales on his body had become increasingly deep and dark. On his white belly were rings of patterns that seemed like branches and vines, and also like mountain rock textures.

Especially the top of his head.

The original single horn remained savage, while beside it, a fleshy lump had risen, as if a second dragon horn were about to be born.

A majestic presence appeared and disappeared.

The only unexpected point was that after many years of cultivation, he had not developed so-called four limbs and dragon claws.

According to Black King's explanation, it was due both to the influence of his own bloodline and the changes brought by devouring the Cliff Serpent corpse.

The savage beast Cliff Serpent had no feet to begin with.

Just as Luo Chen was thinking, Lord Lushan suddenly said from beside him, "Only three months remain before your term ends, yes?"

The corners of Luo Chen's mouth lifted. "Yes. In three months, I can return to the Fulong Mountain Range. Then I will have another five years of leisure."

Lord Lushan blinked. "Fellow Daoist is not at all worried about Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake's expensive rent? You do not think of raising your star rank to obtain more discount benefits?"

"Many thanks for Lord Lushan's concern." Luo Chen smiled and waved a hand, then said with some confidence, "I have my ways of making money. A little rent is not a problem."

Regarding this, Lord Lushan sighed repeatedly.

"An existence like you, powerful in strength, with a fearsome name abroad, Golden Core servants, a Demon King spirit pet, and mastery over the art of making wealth, truly is an oddity among us wandering cultivators. I imagine that even the supreme elders of those Golden Core great sects might be comparable to you, but when it comes to freedom, even they cannot match your ease."

Luo Chen raised a brow, withdrawing his gaze and placing it on the other party's elegant face.

"Fellow Daoist, if you have something to say, speak directly."

Having her thoughts seen through, Lord Lushan's expression became somewhat unnatural. After coughing lightly, she said:

"This humble Daoist has a business deal she would like to discuss with you."

*(End of Chapter)*
