# Chapter 843: The Alchemist’s Wisdom, Post-Battle Gains, That Corpse

The rumor that Divine Transformation great powers could not make a move—if Luo Chen had heard it back when his cultivation was still low, he might have believed it.

But after the battle at Green Parasol Mountain, he no longer believed that rumor at all.

Back then, with Azure Frost Demon Emperor seated at Green Parasol Mountain, even three late-stage Sage grandmasters arriving in person could do nothing about it.

It was only because that great power from the Nether Origin Sect personally made a move that Green Parasol Mountain was forced to flee into the void.

This single example was enough to prove that Ascendant great powers *could* make a move.

And such examples were actually not few!

How had the Primordial Demon Sect of the Northern Sea been destroyed?

Those two Primordial Demon Sect ancestors were also Ascendant great powers. If not for opponents of the same level, who could have killed them?

The formation great power Li Canghai had also, in full view of the public, reached out and taken the Ice Pole Sect’s spiritual mountain to refine into a spirit treasure.

Cases like these all proved that the rumor was mistaken.

Of course, perhaps it wasn’t mistaken, but rather *incomplete*!

Ever since he had grasped the true meaning of Withering-Bloom, Luo Chen had faintly sensed some inexplicable resonance between himself and this heaven and earth.

Very subtle, but it did exist!

At first, Luo Chen thought it was the effect of heaven and earth assimilating his Nascent Soul, along with his unstable realm. So during that period, he dared not cultivate too much and focused solely on consolidating his realm.

Later, once his realm was completely stable, he discovered that this sense of resonance still remained.

Only then did Luo Chen realize that it was absolutely not heaven-and-earth assimilation. Rather, he had faintly acquired some kind of authority, similar to that of heaven and earth, which was why he had that seemingly-real-yet-not feeling.

Law True Meaning!

The reason lay precisely in Law True Meaning.

Luo Chen speculated that once a cultivator comprehended some kind of Law True Meaning, they essentially grasped a portion of the world’s authority.

In such a situation, even if they hadn’t been assimilated by this heaven and earth, they were almost indistinguishable from it.

And Sage cultivators who could comprehend Law True Meaning were ultimately few—even the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect could only produce two or three such people every five hundred years.

Those who truly grasped this profound truth appeared among the group of cultivators at the Ascendant level!

They were akin to heaven and earth. Their dharma power was finite, but as long as they possessed a primordial spirit and had comprehended Law True Meaning, they could draw upon the origin qi of heaven and earth at any time to fight, making their energy inexhaustible.

“Great power figures,” “great divine ability practitioners”—these were the best descriptions for them!

Once such beings engaged in unrestrained great battles among themselves, the destructive force they could unleash was utterly terrifying!

It would never be limited to a single mountain or region, a thousand or a hundred li, like Sage cultivators.

The most likely outcome would be terrifying scenes like continents sinking, mountains and seas overturning!

However, heaven and earth had their own spirit. How could they sit idly by while parasites within their own body destroyed it at will?

Therefore, there *must* be restrictions on Ascendant great powers in certain aspects.

This was the source of the rumor that Ascendant great powers could not easily make a move.

This conclusion was a guess Luo Chen, with his extensive experience, had made based on his own specific circumstances.

Some parts might be incorrect, but it was enough to explain why, during the racial war, powerful beings with decisive military force like the Ancient Demon Emperor and the Nether Origin great power did not directly intervene, instead letting their subordinates kill each other and indirectly compete for supremacy.

It wasn’t that they didn’t want to fight directly, but that restrictions existed in certain places, preventing them from acting easily.

But if they were truly pushed to the brink, what did it matter if they tore those restrictions apart?

So, in the current situation, had the Eastern Wilderness demon race pushed the Nether Origin Sect to that brink?

Luo Chen judged in his heart, and the answer he arrived at was: seemingly not urgent, but in truth, they were already anxious.

What did “seemingly not urgent” mean?

The Nether Origin Sect’s own strength had hardly been affected at all. They still sat high above the clouds.

The way they leisurely issued orders showed no sign of urgency.

But!

Earlier, eight great commanders had simultaneously struck, commanding billions of demon beasts to invade the thirty-six domains, even pushing all the way to the rear areas. Some places could already be described as a scene of utter devastation.

This intensity of war had already exceeded the Nether Origin Sect’s expectations.

If this continued, the entire Eastern Wilderness would be beaten to pieces. If a few more places like the Spirit-Severing Domain appeared, then even if the Nether Origin Sect unified the Eastern Wilderness in the future, all they would get would be a pile of mess.

Therefore, they too could no longer sit still.

This was evident from the Nether Origin Sect sending out six late-stage Sage grandmasters and nearly forty Sage cultivators in one go.

“So, as long as we kill the Ancient Demon Emperor, the leader of the Eastern Wilderness demon race, we can completely dismantle the current demon army!”

Facing Luo Chen’s speculation, Fairy Fenghua did not agree.

“How could an Ascendant existence be so easy to kill?”

Luo Chen gave a wry smile. “Even if we can’t kill him, as long as we force the Ancient Demon Emperor to make the first move, wouldn’t the Nether Origin great power gain the upper hand in the situation? Then, whether to fight or negotiate, the initiative would be in their hands. Sometimes, the name of righteousness is very important!”

Fairy Fenghua frowned. The name of righteousness?

Luo Chen clasped his hands behind his back, pacing along the quiet paths of Danluo Mountain, and laid out his views on this war in detail.

“After the ancient era, the human and demon races concluded a ceasefire treaty, forbidding large-scale racial wars. Only small-scale battles like pioneering wars were allowed, and they were often limited to the scale of a single sect or faction.”

“But in the pioneering war at Howling Moon Mountain Range, the human side first violated the agreement. Han Zhan invited four experts from other sects to besiege and kill the Wolf Emperor Aoxiao.”

“Not only that, the expanding Joyous Union Sect also got involved, trying to open two domains simultaneously with the Falling Cloud Sect.”

“These were all betrayals of the original ceasefire treaty!”

“Most importantly, the Nether Origin great power appeared at the feathered race’s sacred ground, Green Parasol Mountain, and personally made a move!”

“It was precisely because they held the name of righteousness that the Ancient Demon Emperor had reason to ignite the fury in the hearts of the demon race’s strongmen, and smoothly unified the demon race of the Million Great Mountains. Under his call, countless strongmen rushed to his cause and were driven by him, including top experts like Wolf Lord Aokuang, who had once contended with the Ancient Demon Emperor for supremacy—they could not defy him.”

“These are all the benefits brought by the name of righteousness!”

“And the result is obvious. Of the thirty-six domains, fully fifteen were lost. Even the twenty-one domains in the rear suffered heavy losses. Fenghua Palace was breached, Seven Mounds Mountain was destroyed, Dieyun Sect was knocked into the dust, and even the mighty Tian Du Sect visibly declined.”

“This is what we have seen with our own eyes. Other places can only be more tragic than this. Void Emperor Sect, Taishan Sect, Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion, Hundred Beasts Sect, Mu Manor…”

“If we compare this war to a game of chess…”

Speaking up to here, Luo Chen inexplicably thought of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

But only for a moment.

He shook his head and continued speaking eloquently.

“We, the countless living beings, are the chess pieces. The Ascendant existences of the Eastern Wilderness’s human and demon races are the chess players.”

“The outcome of the chess pieces’ game represents the victory or defeat of this war.”

“But if the chess players refuse to play anymore and personally make a move, preparing to erase some chess pieces, that means they want to overturn the board. The other side will naturally no longer follow the rules.”

“Tell me, can the Ancient Demon Emperor alone defeat the three ancestors on the Nether Origin Sect’s side?”

Fairy Fenghua frowned. “The Eastern Wilderness demon race is rumored to also have three ancient demons. It’s not necessarily that they fear the Nether Origin Sect’s three ancestors.”

Luo Chen chuckled.

He had heard of the Eastern Wilderness’s three ancient demons.

The most prominent was undoubtedly Pingshan Lord Di Tian.

The other two were the master of Green Parasol Mountain, Rosycloud, and a mysterious existence named Qinghe Xian.

It was precisely with the support of these two existences, plus favorable timing and terrain, that Di Tian dared to launch the war.

But from the current situation, the support of one of them seemed not to have been fully implemented.

Rosycloud!

Thinking of this great power, whose name he had heard countless times, Luo Chen’s smile turned somewhat forced.

But the heaviness in his heart did not affect his judgment of the situation.

“Nine great commanders, eight have already made their move.”

“Only the Azure Frost Demon Emperor, representing the Green Parasol Mountain lineage, has taken no substantial action aside from rescuing the Thunder Prison Divine Peng earlier.”

“Is she truly fully supporting the Ancient Demon Emperor?”

Faced with this counter-question, Fairy Fenghua fell into hesitation.

“Do you mean that the demon race is not as monolithic internally as it appears on the surface?”

Luo Chen continued his eloquent analysis. “At the very least, the support from Rosycloud’s side for this war is insufficient. Otherwise, if the Azure Frost Demon Emperor under her command joined the war, who could stop her? The famous Thunder Daozi? Tian Duzi, who wields the Twelve Capitals Heavenly Fiend Banner? Or those bitter cultivators within the Nether Origin Sect?”

“And that Qinghe Xian, who has cultivated alone in the Million Great Mountains for thousands of years with little renown, may not truly be so enthusiastic about vying for territory.”

“So, in my view, Di Tian has been playing the name of righteousness from beginning to end, using the accumulated foundation of the Million Great Mountains over thousands of years to take a risky gamble. Once he can no longer sit still and personally makes a move, this war will begin to head toward its end.”

When a chess player enters the game himself, either he wins by half a move against heaven, or he withers and dies on the chessboard.

Luo Chen was not optimistic about Di Tian, just as he had never been optimistic about this human-demon war from the start.

The victors would not be their small sects and factions, nor would they be those demon beast tribes. It would only be the Nether Origin Sacred Land!

The only unexpected factor might be Rosycloud, this behind-the-scenes hand who may have orchestrated the destruction of the Northern Sea Primordial Demon Sect.

After listening to Luo Chen’s entire analysis, Fairy Fenghua stood frozen on the spot.

After a long while, she looked at Luo Chen’s profile with a complex expression and spoke in a low voice.

“I do not know what the future will bring, Brother Luo, but this lofty discourse of yours is not something ordinary people could produce.”

“Fenghua has been taught today!”

A person’s form of address often reveals their attitude toward that person.

At first, she called Luo Chen “Fellow Daoist Luo,” which was polite.

Later, she called him “Alchemist,” respecting his alchemy abilities, with an intent to please.

Now, like Nine Spirit Lord and others, she called Luo Chen “Brother Luo”—this was actively drawing closer, wanting to build a relationship.

And this process of relationship transformation had lasted a long time, only truly winning her over today with Luo Chen’s words.

Luo Chen smiled, feeling quite gratified inside.

In truth, it wasn’t that people like Fairy Fenghua were “stupid.” Rather, they had been in the game from the start, their thinking constrained.

Luo Chen was also in the game now, but at the very beginning, he had been outside it!

Especially his travels through the Northern Sea and Central Continent allowed him to have a comprehensive view of this largest war in the Eastern Wilderness since ancient times, enabling him to reach so many judgments.

But he was also very clear that he was not an Ascendant great power. He could not truly stand at that height to survey the overall situation.

His thoughts and speculations might contain errors.

“Great Palace Master, you flatter me!”

Fairy Fenghua smoothed her hair, disheveled from days of travel, and spoke with a desolate tone.

“My junior sister is dead. Fenghua Palace has only me, a single Sage cultivator. There is no distinction between Great Palace Master and Second Palace Master anymore. Brother Luo, just call me Fenghua directly, as you did just now.”

Luo Chen opened his mouth. Her expression was so similar to his own from a few days ago.

He could only comfort her as he had comforted Tian Duzi earlier. “Fenghua Palace’s foundation is still there. There are also excellent talents like Fengyue and Xialan. If they are well nurtured, producing another one or two Sage cultivators is not impossible.”

“Them…” Fairy Fenghua shook her head and said offhandedly, “If they were truly excellent, they wouldn’t still be stuck at late-stage Adept after three or four hundred years, with the path to forming a Nascent Soul utterly distant. If we’re talking about promising juniors, the one with hope for Sagehood would have to be that Meng Qin’er, who was lured away to the Luo Tian Sect by your good disciple.”

As she spoke, she looked at Luo Chen with a meaningful smile.

Luo Chen coughed dryly twice.

“Those two children are deeply in love. Let’s not separate them! We’ll see in the future, we’ll see!”

He laughed it off and brushed the matter aside.

But in his heart, he had already taken note of Meng Qin’er.

This woman had excellent aptitude. She had advanced to the Adept stage in less than a hundred years, and now, at a young age, she had already reached mid-stage Adept cultivation.

Although the process had been aided by Qu Lingjun’s pills, being able to digest large amounts of pills without being affected by pill poison was also a talent!

If she could be completely retained in the Luo Tian Sect, she would be a great help in the future!

After Fairy Fenghua left, Luo Chen muttered to himself.

“Speaking of which, I should go see my unworthy disciple.”

…

The reconstruction of Lingtian Pass was the top priority at present.

The Nether Origin Sect provided materials and resources, while the various sects and factions provided manpower. The entire majestic city was bustling with activity.

Any cultivator with even a modicum of talent was given important tasks.

Luo Chen even happened to see, during one inspection, an Adept cultivator from the Ailao Mountain of the Jade Cauldron Domain from years past.

Unfortunately, they weren’t acquaintances. He didn’t even greet the man, only secretly watching as the other was excitedly selected by a Nether Origin Sect Sage.

Luo Chen felt quite emotional about this.

In the past, the six great Adept-level sects of the Jade Cauldron Domain had seemed so unattainable in his eyes.

But times had changed. The Flame Alliance had been destroyed by him, the Falling Cloud Sect was fractured, the Icecastle existed in name only, the Hundred Flowers Palace was long gone, and now it seemed that Ailao Mountain, famous for its spiritual architecture, was left with only a few stragglers.

“What about the Azure Pill Valley?”

Luo Chen inadvertently thought of that small sect with which he had considerable history.

The last sect master should be Tao Wan, whom he had expended great effort to push into that position!

He wondered if any disciples of the Azure Pill Valley still survived, and whether the sect’s inheritance had been carried on.

With these random thoughts, Luo Chen went to the city lord’s mansion and requested leave from the Lingtian City Lord.

“No problem. The last great battle at Lingtian Pass cost us heavy losses, but the demon side also suffered badly. There won’t be any new conflicts in the short term. If you have personal matters, go ahead and handle them first!”

The Lingtian City Lord was as lenient as ever.

He also casually asked about Luo Chen’s injuries.

What injuries did Luo Chen have? It was merely an excuse to decline others’ invitations.

Having obtained some free time, he left Lingtian Pass and quietly returned to the Luo Tian Sect.

…

“Initiate stage, eighth layer?”

Luo Chen stood in a quiet courtyard, his eyes gazing faintly at a mountain wall, as if through the thick rock he could see the middle-aged man sitting cross-legged in meditation inside.

Meng Qin’er, dressed as a young matron, stood respectfully behind him.

Hearing his murmur, she boldly defended her husband. “Lingjun has worked very hard. In just over ten years, he has crossed two minor realms.”

Indeed, when Luo Chen first returned to the Eastern Wilderness, Qu Lingjun’s cultivation was roughly at the late sixth layer of Initiate stage.

Now, over ten years later, ignoring all worldly affairs, setting aside all attachments, and focusing entirely on cultivation, he had finally entered the late Initiate stage and barely broken through to the eighth layer.

Hearing the worry in the woman’s voice, Luo Chen smiled and waved his hand.

“Don’t worry. I’m not blaming him; I’m gratified. That he can understand my intentions and focus on cultivation shows that his Dao heart has not yet been completely obscured.”

Meng Qin’er couldn’t help but breathe a sigh of relief, but soon held her breath again.

“Unfortunately…”

Luo Chen sighed. “In the end, it’s still too slow!”

Qu Lingjun had experienced few battles in his life and carried no major injuries, so he should be able to reach his theoretical lifespan limit.

But no matter how long an Initiate cultivator could live, it was only about two hundred fifty to sixty years!

He had only twenty or thirty years left.

With the aptitude of four spirit roots, to go from the eighth layer of Initiate stage to the ninth layer, and even to the Great Perfection state, and then attempt to break through to the Adept stage—the difficulty was unimaginable.

One had to know that even Luo Chen had spent a full thirty years crossing that final step!

He had five spirit roots; Qu Lingjun had four. Both were practically crippled in terms of aptitude.

Meng Qin’er bit her lip and said, “No matter how slow, he still has to take the gamble! My husband said he doesn’t seek the Great Perfection realm. When the time comes, even if he’s only at the ninth layer of Initiate stage, before his lifespan runs out, he will still attempt to break through to the Adept stage!”

“Admirable Dao heart!” Luo Chen praised, then said proudly, “But how could my disciple be so shabby?”

Then, amid Meng Qin’er’s delight, he handed over a storage pouch.

“Inside are supplementary resources I prepared for Lingjun!”

“There’s the third-rank Miao Shen Ye from the Misty Sect. Burn it in a furnace and absorb it; it can keep the spirit platform clear and the mind relaxed.”

“There’s an ice silkworm meditation mat from the Icecastle, at the dharma treasure level. Sitting on it, the external spiritual qi can be ice-refined, becoming less violent and easier to absorb.”

“That ointment is a specialty of the Taishan Sect. When cultivating, remember to have him apply it all over his body first. Its effects are miraculous…”

“The Netherpassage Pills should be useless to him by now, but I’ve already refined the third-rank spiritual medicine Tranquil Soul Powder from the Medicine King Sect. Taking it is even more effective than the Netherpassage Pills.”

“Finally, these jade bottles contain the Starfield Pills I refined! They contain the five elements’ spiritual qi and are suitable for cultivators of any attribute. They are a great tonic for Lingjun. Considering the difference in realms, I’ve deliberately adjusted the medicinal strength. Half a pill per month, held in the mouth to refine, absorbed slowly!”

As Meng Qin’er listened to Luo Chen’s introduction, her hands trembled slightly.

This entire set of supplementary cultivation resources was precious even for Adept cultivators!

But Luo Chen had generously given them out, and it even seemed as if he had prepared them in advance.

For a moment, the words her husband had said to her kept surfacing in her mind.

“The master has always treated him generously!”

It was true!

But the strange thing was, since he cared so much for Qu Lingjun, why hadn’t Luo Chen bestowed these things earlier?

She dared not ask this question.

She only bowed deeply to Luo Chen. “Disciple thanks the master, on behalf of Lingjun, for your great kindness and virtue!”

Luo Chen smiled and gently raised his hand.

“No need for this.”

Then, he suddenly took out another storage pouch.

“You have stayed by Lingjun’s side every step of the way all these years. You have worked hard. Here are some resources I had left over from my Adept stage. If you don’t mind, take them!”

Meng Qin’er was stunned.

Who was Luo Chen?

A Sage cultivator, an Alchemist!

The resources he had used during his Adept stage were things any Adept cultivator would dream of obtaining.

How could she mind?

Sure enough, after Luo Chen left, she carefully took out a jade bottle.

With just one glance, her eyes lit up.

A third-rank top-grade pill!

Holding the jade bottle, the young matron murmured:

“Is the master… loving the house and its crow?”

…

Leaving Qu Lingjun’s cultivation site, Luo Chen did not directly return to the mountain-top palace. Instead, he first went to Little Stone Flat.

This was originally Sima Huiniang’s cultivation ground. After she went to Lingtian Pass, she had ceded this spiritually rich place to her disciple, Yao Mingyue.

After all, she was the second-generation sect master in the Luo Tian Sect’s history. Since Luo Chen had returned, he naturally would not neglect her.

After a sweep of his divine sense, Luo Chen revealed a satisfied expression.

Yao Mingyue’s aptitude was not bad. In her early years, she had only been burdened by worldly affairs.

In these recent years of leisure, her realm had steadily advanced. She was now just one step away from the Great Perfection of the Initiate stage.

What she lacked to condense a golden core was merely time and patience, and mastering the Luo Tian Sect’s core-formation secret art, the *Fine Dust Primordial Art*.

“I hope everything goes smoothly for her!”

Luo Chen only offered a blessing. He did not leave behind many cultivation resources as he had for Qu Lingjun.

What should have been given to Yao Mingyue, the sect had long since provided.

Including the third-rank Condensed Liquid Pill needed for core formation.

After all, she was a meritorious minister. The sect would not treat her poorly.

The reason Qu Lingjun received different treatment was deliberate.

His only formally accepted disciple had never been enthusiastic about cultivation due to his poor aptitude. After years of wear and tear, his Dao-seeking heart was even less firm.

Luo Chen’s earlier deliberate coldness was also meant to temper the other’s character.

Now that Qu Lingjun had awakened, Luo Chen naturally would not be stingy with rewards.

With a Sage cultivator’s full support, a mere Initiate cultivator seeking to form a core—as long as his luck wasn’t too bad—would succeed nine times out of ten.

“The departures of Fu Jiusheng and Chu Kui were a not-insignificant blow to the Luo Tian Sect.”

“If Lingjun and Mingyue can fill the gap, it will also boost the sect’s morale a little.”

Luo Chen sighed with emotion, then leisurely ascended the mountain.

Arriving at the long-quiet mountain-top palace, he cast a Cleansing Art on the residence, which was already cleaned daily, making it sparkle anew.

Then, he slapped the armrest of his chair.

“Come out!”

As if sensing something, the earth trembled slightly.

It caused quite a few Luo Tian Sect cultivators to look down at the ground, but without much surprise.

After the earlier demon emperor invasion battle, they had also learned that their own sect actually had a mountain-protecting spirit beast.

And unlike other sects, their mountain-protecting spirit beast was extremely powerful. According to the sect master, its strength was in no way inferior to a Sage cultivator!

This faint earthquake was most likely their “Black King” turning over in his sleep.

They were used to it!

But unlike what they thought, this time it wasn’t Black King turning over, but waking up.

A figure emerged from the ground and respectfully bowed to Luo Chen.

“Master, what does Little Black have to do?”

Luo Chen smiled faintly. “Spit out what you swallowed earlier!”

“Yes!”

As the words fell, a bulge rose in Black King’s abdomen, moving upward continuously. Then, amidst a sloshing of mucus, items were softly spat out.

What met the eye in the great hall were neatly arranged old jars containing various thousand-year vintage wines.

And a corpse that had long since lost all signs of life.
