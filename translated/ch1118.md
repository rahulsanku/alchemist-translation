# Chapter 1118: Nether Abyss Divine Ability, Opening Heaven and Earth

Half a year later.

Outside the Heavenly Nether Palace, Tian Xingzi was carefully deciphering the formation, compass in hand.

His eyes were slightly narrowed, his expression solemn. Every single hand seal he cast was done with extreme caution.

This was not his fault. After all, this was the long-term dwelling cave of an Ascendant-level great power. Even with the sect's protection, the formations laid down would not be too complex. But even simple formations often contained unfathomably powerful and unpredictable might.

If he made a mistake, even a small one, it would invite backlash.

Although he was someone whose lifespan was nearly exhausted, he still did not wish to die on the spot. At the very least, he wanted a perfect ending, passing away peacefully after a full life.

Just as he was carefully exploring, a rumbling vibration suddenly came from outside.

The sound was faint at first, but then it rapidly swelled and surged layer upon layer, like dominoes falling in a chain.

This also caused the entire Mount Yanfu to tremble.

Fortunately, Deng Taiyue acted in time, using great means to forcibly stabilize Mount Yanfu, preventing it from spreading elsewhere.

Tian Xingzi instantly realized what was happening, and a look of surprise appeared on his face.

"Luo Chen was actually this fast? He broke through the Heavenly Water Palace's formation in only half a year?"

...

At the Heavenly Water Palace, Luo Chen looked at the slowly opening main gate and a smile appeared on his face.

In terms of formation mastery, let alone the fifth rank, he was still far behind even Tian Xingzi.

If one were to use a more refined classification, his current formation mastery lay somewhere between a Grandmaster and a Patriarch.

He had mastered various high and low-level restrictions and formations, and could set them up effortlessly. In terms of speed, he even surpassed formation Patriarchs.

But what defined a Patriarch was someone who could absorb all rivers and establish their own school, possessing a unique understanding that set them apart.

Luo Chen could not yet create his own formations. He could only improve some ancient formations, achieving a degree of innovation.

The reason he had completed in only half a year what Tian Xingzi had estimated would take three to five years to break the formation came down to one point: strength!

He possessed the great power of the Ascendant realm!

When testing the formation's reactions, he did not need to be as cautious as Tian Xingzi. Even if the formation fought back fiercely, he could suppress it with even greater force.

Coupled with his Crimson-Golden Eyes, which could see through illusions and reality, Luo Chen's formation-breaking was like a hot knife through butter.

Now that the great formation was broken, the Heavenly Water Palace was no longer defended against him!

Luo Chen strolled in leisurely, advancing with calm composure.

What met his eyes was a main hall that was completely different from the Heavenly Nether Palace's splendor. Instead, it had a rather deep and old-fashioned style.

There was nothing interesting here, so Luo Chen went straight to the rear hall.

The first thing that attracted him was the rich spiritual energy that had leaked out after the formation was broken.

Opening a hall, Luo Chen only needed a brief sense to nod in satisfaction.

"Since the fall of Yanfu over two hundred years ago, the scattered earth veins have re-condensed, and the fifth-rank spirit vein has recovered by more than half. The spiritual energy here is abundant. If someone wanted to break through to the Ascendant realm here, they could give it a try."

In the quiet hall were exquisite incense burners, jade meditation cushions, bells, and other auxiliary cultivation items.

None of them were ordinary!

But Luo Chen only glanced at them before leaving.

The next location was his main target for breaking the formation.

A study!

Just like Luo Chen, who in the early days carried all his possessions on him, as the Luo Tian Sect gradually flourished, Luo Chen would also leave part of his wealth within the sect.

This included, but was not limited to, various cultivation resources, his personal cultivation techniques, insights gained, and various cultivation experiences.

This study was clearly the place where Black Marsh Ancestor had lived for many years.

Luo Chen stood inside, his divine sense spreading out like a spider's web, falling upon one tome after another.

These tomes were either paper, beast hide, bamboo slips, or mostly jade slips.

Very few of them had restrictions left on them.

After all, who would set up layers of protection in their own most private space?

Luo Chen's divine sense swept over them, and then his heart stirred. He lightly reached out and beckoned.

A piece of beast hide, a jade slip, and a thickly rolled bamboo slip flew towards him.

Spreading open the beast hide, a look of realization appeared on Luo Chen's face.

This recorded a fifth-rank pill formula called the *Yao Shen Dan* (Dazzling Spirit Pill), with an extremely high refining difficulty. Regardless of its effects, just in terms of refining difficulty, it surpassed even the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's *Gui Yuan Dan* (Return Origin Pill) formula among fifth-rank pills.

The reason Luo Chen was suddenly enlightened was that the main ingredient of this pill formula was none other than the Black Obsidian Lotus Pod that had once been raised in the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land!

"As I thought back then, the Black Obsidian Lotus Pod was indeed prepared for Ascendant-level great powers, and specifically used to nourish the primordial spirit. In terms of effects, it has some similarities with the Soul-Shaking Pill I refined using the Black Obsidian Lotus Pod."

Luo Chen smiled and put away the pill formula.

If in the future his soul stones were exhausted and he encountered another Black Obsidian Lotus Pod, he could perhaps use this method to refine a similar pill.

His gaze fell upon the jade slip, and Luo Chen's expression became somewhat cautious.

This was one of the rare items in the study that had both a mana and a soul imprint attached to it.

It seemed that even for Black Marsh Ancestor, this was a rather precious item.

Now that Black Marsh Ancestor was dead, the soul imprint he left behind was extremely faint. Moreover, even at his peak, he might not have been able to compare to Luo Chen's current divine sense strength.

Luo Chen's divine sense swept over it once, and the soul imprint was broken.

What remained was to convert the mana containing the Water Origin and break the mana imprint bit by bit.

After a moment, the jade slip lightly trembled.

*Hum...*

A brilliant light burst forth, and the true form of the jade slip appeared before Luo Chen's eyes.

One character after another floated up like tadpoles or swimming fish.

Luo Chen looked up and smiled.

*The Myriad Swamps True Scripture*

"As expected, it's that old fellow's main cultivation technique. Its rank is not low, reaching all the way to the Body Integration stage!"

"It contains the cultivation method for the Ascendant realm, which is largely the same as what Li Canghai explained, focusing on the step of condensing the Dharma Aspect. But other aspects are more comprehensive, whether it be refining magical power, or nurturing divine thought, and..."

When he saw what came after, Luo Chen's smile grew even brighter.

This jade slip actually recorded many secret arts!

Water-element spells from the first to the fourth rank, with a very wide coverage.

The Water Concealment Art was also among them.

There were also various methods for refining matching water-element magic treasures, allowing cultivators to refine their own water-element lifebound magic treasures according to their own cultivation circumstances.

If it were only that, it would be one thing.

But this jade slip also recorded a divine ability art!

"*Thousand Rivers Guide*, that's an interesting name."

"It uses the cultivator's own mana as a guide to draw upon the origin qi of heaven and earth, transforming it into flowing rivers. An enemy trapped within it would be unable to control themselves, as if stuck in a quagmire, and their mana would be continuously scoured away. And most importantly, the person executing this divine ability would receive a great boost, the degree of which depends on their own foundation. If one could truly achieve the power of containing a thousand rivers, then every move they make would possess immense might."

"Thinking about it this way, when Black Marsh first set foot on Green Parasol Mountain back then, the scene of rain falling from the sky was clearly him using this divine ability."

Luo Chen read with great interest, and even combined his own experiences from back then to analyze the strengths and weaknesses of this divine ability.

Moreover, he hypothesized how he would compare to Black Marsh Ancestor if he were to unleash this divine ability.

The answer was self-evident. In terms of his own Dao foundation, he was far superior to Black Marsh Ancestor. The number of great rivers he could accommodate would naturally be greater!

If combined with the growth-type divine ability of the Qi Sea Floating Heaven, perhaps the power would be even greater!

"This trip was not in vain!"

Luo Chen praised, and contentedly put away the jade slip.

He would naturally make a copy later and leave it for the Luo Tian Sect.

After all, this was a complete set of sixth-rank techniques, from the Qi Refinement stage all the way to the Body Integration stage. This one book alone could serve as a sect's inheritance.

As he put away the jade slip, a doubt suddenly arose in Luo Chen's heart.

Or rather, it was a doubt that had always existed.

"The *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture*, as a top-tier technique that can reach the Great Vehicle stage, even if for humans it can only be cultivated to the Body Integration stage, it doesn't record a single secret art or divine ability."

"Is it that there were none to begin with, or did Rosycloud deliberately withhold them and not give them to me?"

Most complete techniques would have matching spells, divine abilities, realm-breaking secret arts, and even magic treasure refining methods.

But the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture*, which Luo Chen currently cultivates as his main technique, was extremely simplified. The only set of secret arts was the "Nine Tribulations, Three Nirvanas" method.

Thinking of how Rosycloud had never harbored any malice towards him, Luo Chen subconsciously thought in a positive direction.

Perhaps the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture* originally had matching secret arts and divine abilities, but they were suitable for the demon race, so she removed them and did not give them to him.

After putting away the *Myriad Swamps True Scripture*, Luo Chen turned his attention to the last item, the thick roll of bamboo slips.

This time, there was no obstruction. He easily unrolled it.

The handwriting on it was sometimes light, sometimes heavy, sometimes scribbled, sometimes clear. It was evident that Black Marsh's state of mind was different when he left these words.

And the content matched this situation.

Joy when cultivation was successful, confusion and bewilderment when stuck, sudden clarity after communicating with others...

These various contents formed this thick roll of bamboo slips.

Clearly, this was a record of Black Marsh Ancestor's cultivation experiences during his lifetime, containing many insights and realizations.

Once Luo Chen started reading, he became immersed.

Although their main cultivation directions were different, using the experiences of someone at the same realm as a reference still had its merits.

Especially after the Ascendant realm, Black Marsh Ancestor had left more notes, often filled with confusion, speculation, and deduction.

It was a full three days later before Luo Chen carefully put away the bamboo slip.

A complete set of cultivation insights from an Ascendant-level great power. The value of this bamboo slip was in no way inferior to any fifth-rank technique!

At this point, Luo Chen looked around the room and had no more regrets.

There was still a large collection of books in the study, but none could compare to these three gains. Of course, as the collection of a sect's founding ancestor, they were certainly not ordinary. After making copies to leave for the sect, Luo Chen would naturally read them in his spare time.

For now, he had to go help Tian Xingzi break the restrictions of the Heavenly Abyss Palace.

...

Another half a year passed.

With a *hum*, the restriction of the Heavenly Abyss Palace was also easily broken under the combined efforts of Luo Chen and Tian Xingzi.

Its inner treasures were countless, and the harvest even surpassed that of the Heavenly Water Palace.

After all, Heavenly Abyss Ancestor had attained the Dao before Black Marsh, so the wealth he left behind was naturally more abundant.

Luo Chen took it all, reserving it as the foundation for the future inheritance of the Luo Tian Sect.

After completing this matter, Tian Xingzi chose to take his leave and depart.

On this trip, although he had contributed, he had also gained a lot.

Luo Chen had copied all the formation-related tomes of the Nether Origin Sect and gifted them to him.

Tian Xingzi left in a hurry, perhaps also wanting to organize these gains and the insights from a lifetime of cultivation before his great limit arrived, to then bequeath them to his sect.

Deng Taiyue stayed behind. Under the title of guest elder of the Luo Tian Sect, he took up residence in the Heavenly Water Palace, occupying a fifth-rank cave dwelling.

He had been comprehending the true meaning of the laws for a long time, and had been stuck at the late Sage stage for two hundred years. He had long wanted to break through to the Ascendant realm.

But he still had to wait a while.

Luo Chen was also waiting.

Waiting for the first Heart-Linking Fruit to mature, waiting for the Luo Tian Sect disciples to finish laying down the basic formations he needed, waiting for the Nether Water Spring to condense once more.

...

Time passed bit by bit.

Days, months, years. Ten years of frost and snow passed in the blink of an eye.

From the perspective of a high-level cultivator's lifespan, these ten years were almost negligible.

But in those ten years, under the assignment of Sect Leader Luo Lingxi, the disciples of the Luo Tian Sect had already set foot on all the former sites of the Nether Origin Sect.

During this time, the Black Turtle Dharma Protector controlled the Nether Water.

Xiaobai, known within the sect as the "White Emperor," commanded a large number of little ghosts to explore various places, facilitating the work of Luo Tian's disciples.

Over the ten years, specially refined formation flags, formation plates, and a large number of spirit stones were embedded in the old site of the Nether Origin Sect.

The manpower and material resources expended nearly exhausted the savings the Luo Tian Sect had accumulated over the years.

Such a labor-intensive and costly undertaking would have inevitably led to widespread complaint in the past.

But if this order was given by the Supreme Elder, there would be no doubt.

He was like a mythical existence in the sect's history!

All his orders were blindly followed by the entire Luo Tian Sect.

Moreover, in the entire Eastern Wilderness cultivation world, Luo Chen's word was law, and few dared to resist.

The high-ranking cultivators of the sect who were cultivating in seclusion within the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land also left in the ninth year, all heading to Shangyou City.

Thus, within the entire Nether Abyss, aside from some ghost cultivators hiding far away, only Luo Chen and Deng Taiyue remained on Mount Yanfu.

Ten years later.

Within the Luo Tian Sect in Shangyou City, Luo Lingxi was convening a meeting of the sect's high-ranking members to discuss a major matter.

Nowadays, no one would treat him as a mere young lad.

Whether it was his powerful background, his status as the fourth-generation Sect Leader, or his powerful Sage realm cultivation, Luo Lingxi had already become a pivotal figure in the Eastern Wilderness.

Looking at the hall filled with high-ranking cultivators, Luo Lingxi was full of vigor and ambition.

If Mother were still here to see this scene, she would probably be proud of him!

Since the three Adepts in the Blessed Land had broken through to the Sage realm at nearly the same time back then, the Luo Tian Sect had not truly hit a bottleneck.

Subsequently, more people had gradually formed their sages, increasing the sect's high-end combat power.

Even on the War God Peak, where Wang Yuan was, aside from Baleful Dragonzi advancing to the fourth rank of body refinement, there was also a reclusive body refinement genius who broke through to the fourth rank.

This was truly a sign of great prosperity!

The only thing restricting the Luo Tian Sect now was probably the sect's territory.

Relying solely on Shangyou City and the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land was no longer enough to support the cultivation needs of so many experts.

The sect's territory had to expand, and the Luo Tian Sect's influence had to spread outwards!

This was the major matter Luo Lingxi was preparing to discuss.

Just as he was about to speak, his expression suddenly changed, and he subconsciously looked up towards the outside.

Many others had the same reaction, mostly the top-tier Sages of the sect.

People like Lingfengzi and Canglong, who both had mid-Sage strength.

"Is this..."

"Could it be?"

Soon, other newly promoted Sages in the hall also reacted, subconsciously looking outside.

Amidst the commotion, Luo Lingxi pressed down with his hand in the air.

"Silence!"

Then, he led the crowd out of the hall, looking from afar towards the location of the Nether Abyss.

There, ominous clouds gathered, and the sky was dark and gloomy.

The Nether Abyss boiled, its waves raging like anger.

In the void, thunder and lightning crackled incessantly, causing everyone's hearts and gallbladders to tremble violently.

This scene made everyone restless.

Luo Lingxi took a deep breath and murmured, "Father, are you about to begin?"

Then, he gathered his qi in his dantian and sent out a vast transmission that spread for thousands of miles.

"All disciples, do not go out! Stay away from the Nether Abyss!"

"Activate the sect-protecting grand formation! Spare no expense!"

"Invite the Four Great Spirit Lords to guard the banks of the Nether Abyss!"

One command after another was issued. The previously peaceful Luo Tian Sect immediately became busy.

But inside the Nether Abyss, it was a different scene.

Deng Taiyue stood on Mount Yanfu, looking at Luo Chen with an extremely solemn expression.

"Are you really going to do this?"

Luo Chen's expression was calm, as if he was not worried at all. He simply said flatly, "The arrow has been nocked; it must be fired."

Deng Taiyue was somewhat worried. "Although I have not yet broken through to the Ascendant realm, following your guidance, I can now perceive the origin qi of heaven and earth. If you truly do such a heaven-defying thing, I'm afraid this heaven and earth will no longer tolerate you."

Luo Chen smiled faintly, turned around, and walked towards the Heavenly Nether Palace.

His voice came leisurely from ahead.

"I, Luo, have long intended to ascend. Before I leave, it is only right for me to do a trivial thing for the Luo Tian Sect and for my old friends and descendants."

Deng Taiyue took a deep breath. Such a matter, which could be called opening heaven and earth, was hardly trivial!

The gates of the Heavenly Nether Palace slammed shut.

The next moment, an aura of profound mystery began to madly spread from within, gradually covering the entire Nether Abyss!

(End of this chapter)
