# Chapter 917: Core Formation Ceremony, Luo Chen Appears

Three days later.

A man of ordinary appearance with dark skin walked out of Lingtian Pass amid the surging crowd.

His footsteps paused for a moment at the boundary marker engraved with the words [Ling Tian].

"Though Ye Lingtian is dead, at least he left some traces in this world."

After murmuring to himself, the man headed toward the nearest Spirit Severed Domain.

The current Spirit Severed Domain was so depleted of spiritual qi that it was no longer suitable for cultivators to reside in.

Thus, some of the nearby major powers had relocated large numbers of mortals here to thrive and multiply.

Every ten or hundred years, these powers would regularly hold Ascension Ceremonies here, recruiting youths who possessed spirit roots.

It was a way of not wasting this once-precious land.

The man's figure descended into a certain canyon.

The canyon was deep and long, stretching for a hundred li.

Within, miasma filled the air, and wild beasts roared incessantly, making it seem like a forbidden zone for living beings.

This place was now called "Fallen Immortal Valley." Legend had it that decades ago, a powerful immortal had fallen here.

Before his death, he had split open mountains and continents with a single saber strike, forming this gorge of breathtaking, uncanny craftsmanship.

And Luo Chen knew that the person involved was the former master of the Spirit Severed Domain, the Sect Master of the Overlord Saber Sect.

He stood in Fallen Immortal Valley for a while, his eyes glancing back with a hint of a smile, then his figure flickered and vanished in the blink of an eye.

A moment later, three figures shrouded in black robes appeared where he had disappeared.

They looked at each other, somewhat incredulous.

"The fat sheep ran away?"

"How is that possible? Wasn't he marked with butterfly-attracting powder?"

"To be able to detect the butterfly-attracting powder and vanish from under the noses of us three Core Formation cultivators, this person is probably not as simple as he appears on the surface."

"What do we do now?"

"He was extremely generous, casually spending high-grade magic treasures and rare materials. He must have reaped a great harvest over at the Nether Abyss. I'm not willing to let him get away."

"Forget it. At least it was a fair trade, and this man possesses formidable skills. Let's stop here!"

The three black shadows conversed for a moment before reluctantly leaving Fallen Immortal Valley.

After they departed, high in the nine heavens, Luo Chen slowly withdrew his gaze.

"Sure enough, a big forest has all kinds of birds."

"Back when Lingtian Pass was under military control, although it wasn't very free, there were never these kinds of troubles in trade."

Shaking his head, Luo Chen couldn't be bothered to exterminate these juniors.

Black market transactions carried risks, as everyone knew.

This was one of those situations—someone sells something, then wants to snatch it back along with the person, engaging in business with no capital.

He had already gained enough; making a move would only invite unnecessary trouble.

Yes.

During these three days at Lingtian Pass, Luo Chen had collected a large amount of soul essence from the black market by spending lavishly.

The quantity amounted to at least three bottles of ten-million-person soul essence.

And that was in just three days.

If Luo Chen had enough time, he could have collected even more soul essence!

It might sound somewhat exaggerated.

But upon careful thought, it was only natural.

The method for refining soul essence had originally spread outward from Lingtian Pass as the center.

Later, during the human-demon war, how many millions of demon beasts and cultivators had died?

Through the covert collection of interested parties, and even the refining by extreme demonic cultivators, large quantities of soul essence had circulated in the black market within just a few decades.

Luo Chen had once personally witnessed a cultivator collecting souls on the battlefield to refine soul essence.

It was easy to imagine that once someone figured out how to use soul essence, its value would increase further.

Some powerful individuals lacking resources would inevitably take risks to refine soul essence for exorbitant profits.

"I wonder if I, the originator, will face retribution in the future?"

Luo Chen chuckled lightly and headed toward the Luo Tian Domain.

He had also gathered enough intelligence on the Luo Tian Sect.

Overall, during the eight years he had been away, the Luo Tian Sect had made no major moves. They had followed his instructions before his departure and had not aggressively recruited new disciples like other Nascent Soul sects after the war.

Similarly, no major disasters had occurred.

They had merely acted as intermediaries, mediating a few conflicts between Core Formation powers within the Luo Tian Domain.

That was enough.

...

A few days later, Luo Chen's figure appeared on Motian Cliff.

His divine sense swept out, probing several locations.

Immediately, it drew startled and uncertain responses.

"Who dares to spy on me!" Lingfengzi abruptly woke from his seclusion.

"The master's divine sense?" Bai Meiling was both surprised and delighted, instantly recognizing Luo Chen.

"Husband!" Gu Caiyi, who was cultivating on Motian Cliff, was the first to rush out of her seclusion chamber, and then she saw the familiar white-robed figure come into view.

Luo Chen smiled and embraced her.

Gu Caiyi's voice choked, "Why didn't you let us know you were coming back?"

"Isn't this me letting you know?" Luo Chen smiled, then frowned. "Where is everyone else?"

Gu Caiyi wiped the corner of her eyes and quickly said, "Huiniang heard that something major happened over at the Nether Origin Sect two years ago and rushed over with people. I don't know if she's arrived yet. Baleful Dragonzi and Qin Yuanjiang personally escorted her, along with her disciple Yao Mingyue."

Luo Chen had guessed this might happen and wasn't surprised.

What surprised him was that he hadn't detected Wang Yuan.

Just then, a figure flew over from afar, moving with fierce gale winds.

Only when he was visible to the naked eye did Luo Chen notice the newcomer.

Luo Chen was extremely surprised.

When the man landed, he asked, "You've stepped into the fourth realm of body cultivation and cultivated the Leakless Golden Body?"

For a fourth-realm body cultivator, the changes were not just a significant increase in lifespan.

The main thing was the ability to seal all qi and blood flow in the body, maintaining a peak state until the approach of death.

Unlike cultivators, who focused on refining qi and spirit, the aging of the physical body was uncontrollable.

It was precisely because Luo Chen had swept through with his vast divine sense but hadn't detected Wang Yuan's presence that he guessed the other party had cultivated the Leakless Golden Body.

Wang Yuan nodded, his expression calm, but a hint of relief in his eyes.

"I knew you wouldn't fall so easily."

Luo Chen laughed heartily. "You have quite a bit of confidence in me!"

Wang Yuan sighed helplessly. "There's no choice even if I didn't have confidence. I heard that this battle at the Nether Origin Sect involved Ascendant-level powerhouses. No matter how we maneuvered, it would have been futile."

Luo Chen found that quite reasonable.

Before an Ascendant powerhouse, everything is but an ant.

Sima Huiniang's anxious departure was merely out of concern for him and her son. Luo Chen could understand that.

As they conversed, two others quietly arrived.

Lingfengzi looked incredulous. "You're still alive?"

Bai Meiling, however, excitedly came to Luo Chen's side. "Master!"

Luo Chen nodded, acknowledging them, and then waited for the last person to arrive.

Li Yingzhang's figure appeared on Motian Cliff only after a long while.

When he saw Luo Chen again, his face was equally filled with excitement.

"Yingzhang pays respects to the Supreme Elder!"

"No need for formalities." Luo Chen waved his hand and headed toward the mountain palace. "Let's sit down and talk. There's something I need your agreement on."

The others snapped out of their joy at Luo Chen's survival and curiously followed him into the palace.

Soon after.

Li Yingzhang's reluctant voice came from inside.

"We have to leave the Luo Tian Domain we fought so hard to take?"

...

The commotion of Luo Chen's return was not significant.

Within the entire Luo Tian Sect, only his close Dao companions and those at the Nascent Soul stage like Lingfengzi knew.

Li Yingzhang was a special case due to his status as the Luo Tian Sect's Sect Master.

Apart from them, none of the five hundred or so disciples of the Luo Tian Sect knew that their Supreme Elder was not only alive but had returned to the sect.

Let alone outsiders who might be keeping an eye on things.

They were still immersed in the immense shock of the Nether Origin Sect's destruction.

In just three years, the specific news had only gradually spread to these remote areas.

In their discussions, the various powers, large and small, within the Luo Tian Domain inevitably brought up the Alchemy Lord Luo Chen, who had made a grand departure from the Luo Tian Sect to the Nether Origin Sect years ago.

Since the Nether Origin Sect was destroyed, the Alchemy Lord Luo Chen was probably gone too.

With that, the Luo Tian Sect had lost its greatest backing. Did that mean they had an opportunity?

After some discussion, they despairingly realized there was no opportunity at all.

The Luo Tian Sect still had the Nascent Soul cultivator Lingfengzi sitting in charge.

Although the War Devil Wang Yuan had stayed behind closed doors these years, he had a terrifying record of battling Nascent Soul experts.

Especially since there was a Ghost Howl Peak within the Luo Tian Sect where a Ghost Emperor occasionally appeared.

Three Nascent Soul-level combat forces were enough to suppress any petty villains in the domain.

Even the Lin and Gu families, who had been stationed in the two neighboring domains to monitor the Luo Tian Sect, had dared not make a move in these three years.

Thus, one could see the depth of the foundation Luo Chen had left behind.

In this situation, a minor stir gradually spread.

Qu Lingjun, the only personal disciple of the Alchemy Lord Luo Chen, had successfully broken through to the Core Formation stage before his great limit arrived!

As if to dispel the sorrowful atmosphere of the Supreme Elder's supposed fall, or perhaps to stabilize morale, the Luo Tian Sect decided to hold a Core Formation Ceremony.

However, this ceremony was only for internal disciples and did not invite outsiders to observe.

For a time, Luo Tian Sect cultivators scattered abroad received the notice, stopped their tasks, and hurried back to the sect.

...

Half a year later.

On Motian Cliff, clouds surged like an ocean.

A hundred-zhang green jade Dao platform rose from the ground, with sixty-four dragon-coiling pillars standing in midair.

Green curtains cascaded from the horizon like waterfalls, and large banners embroidered with talisman patterns flapped noisily.

All this grand spectacle was prepared for one person.

Yet as Qu Lingjun walked among it, his face showed no excitement, only deep dejection.

Meng Qiner held his arm and tried to console him. "Husband, today is your happy day. Why be like this?"

Qu Lingjun sighed. "Achieving Core Formation and not failing my master's expectations—that is indeed a great joy for me. But with my master's soul returning to the Yellow Springs, how can I be happy?"

Then he pointed at the red silk and colorful ribbons around him, his face filled with anger.

"It should have been all white mourning cloth. Why this celebration?"

Meng Qiner explained in a low voice. "These were all arrangements by Sect Master Li. He wanted to use your Core Formation Ceremony to dispel the sorrowful atmosphere and stabilize morale within the sect. After all, you are the Supreme Elder's only personal disciple."

Qu Lingjun opened his mouth. He could understand the Sect Master's good intentions, but it was hard for him to accept hearing the tragic news of his master's death right after emerging from seclusion.

On his most glorious day, the person he most wanted to see was that man who was like a teacher and a father.

But now, he was supposed to use his own glory to cover up that man's influence within the sect?

Luo Tian Sect cultivators walked up the mountain and saw Qu Lingjun on the square, respectfully greeting him one after another.

"Greetings, Uncle-Master Qu!"

"Greetings, Senior Brother Qu!"

Cultivators addressed each other by seniority based on cultivation realm, but when realms were similar, the order of entry into the sect determined seniority.

Qu Lingjun was absolutely a founding-level figure of the Luo Tian Sect. Anyone who saw him had to address him with respect.

Meng Qiner squeezed Qu Lingjun's thick palm. "Cheer up, husband. You'll have to take the stage later to give a lecture on your Core Formation insights."

Qu Lingjun sighed. "What's there to say? I only advanced by piling up resources..."

As the words reached his lips, he seemed to remember something.

He would not allow his master's existence to be forgotten, especially not covered up by his own success.

Since that was the case, he would take this day to expound on the insights and experiences his master had shared when he formed his core back then!

...

When the sandalwood incense curled upward and all the disciples had arrived.

Lingfengzi led the disciples of Misty Veil Peak into the venue. Wang Yuan, along with Baleful Dragonzi who had just rushed back, took their seats in the elder section. Even Li Yingzhang was accompanied by Qin Yuanjiang, Yao Mingyue, and other Core Formation cultivators who had been absent for two years. With that, this internal Core Formation Ceremony was declared to have begun.

Qu Lingjun floated onto the stage and looked around.

His heart was filled with a myriad of thoughts.

Although the sect hadn't recruited many new people in recent years, they had still added some hands, totaling about five hundred people.

It was roughly the same scale as when they had left Dahe Market back then.

Unfortunately, the most familiar face was missing.

Even his two master's wives hadn't shown themselves.

Steeling his mind, Qu Lingjun recalled the scene of his master emerging from seclusion after forming his core, and slowly began to speak:

"The profound origins of the Great Dao, the hidden secrets of the high and true—how can the worldly know them?

The primordial one qi, the clear and turbid naturally separate.

If one does not know the reversal of Kan and Li, who can distinguish the sinking and floating of metal and wood..."

Inside the palace, Luo Chen had just calmed down Sima Huiniang, who had rushed back via teleportation formation. Hearing the chanting from outside, he was momentarily stunned.

"This kid..."

Sima Huiniang wiped away the tears at the corners of her eyes and choked, "Isn't that the Core Formation poem you chanted when you emerged from seclusion back then?"

Luo Chen nodded. The more he listened, the more certain he was.

That poem had been composed on a whim, based on his experiences before and after Core Formation. After it spread, it had indeed had a considerable impact.

Many low-level cultivators in Tianlan who had been struggling without a method for Core Formation later relied on some understanding of that poem to gradually find their own path to Core Formation.

Later, when his reputation as the Alchemy Lord had grown so grand, this poem had contributed a little.

Now, Qu Lingjun had copied it verbatim without changing a single word.

He didn't know if it was laziness or intentional.

"Husband, Lingxi..."

"He's still alive. You'll see him soon."

"As long as he's alive, that's good." Sima Huiniang murmured.

"Also, I've found a clue about his cultivation."

Sima Huiniang was stunned, and light burst from her eyes again.

But Luo Chen didn't say more. He simply handed her a small booklet that recorded the cultivation method for Devil Heart Gu cultivators that Gu Xian Fengjiang had mentioned back then.

"I should make my appearance."

Luo Chen walked out, with Gu Caiyi and Sima Huiniang following closely behind, as if afraid he would disappear again.

On the square.

The Luo Tian Sect disciples looked puzzled.

Why was Qu Lingjun's discourse on Core Formation the same as what the Supreme Elder had spread back then?

Wasn't this old hat?

Was this senior of the sect being so secretive and stingy with his own Core Formation insights?

Sensing the dissatisfaction below, Qu Lingjun paused slightly on the green jade high platform.

"What I have just said is the Core Formation Dao of our Luo Tian Supreme Elder."

"You may have already known it, but you must never underestimate this widely circulated Core Formation treatise. The more you study it, the greater the harvest."

"I hope that after returning, you will pay more attention to the many treasures left behind by the Supreme Elder."

"Of course, I also have some insights of my own. Though they may not compare to the Supreme Elder's, I am willing to share them with you all."

With that, he briefly shared his own experiences and insights before and after Core Formation.

The preparations he had made, the difficulties he had encountered, how he had broken through bottlenecks, and how he had quickly stabilized his realm.

His words, perhaps not as profound or complex as Luo Chen's back then, were more detailed and carried a unique personal style.

The square gradually grew quiet. Whether high-level cultivators or low-level disciples, everyone listened attentively.

When Qu Lingjun finished speaking, a sudden sound of applause and admiration rang out.

"Well said!"

"Worthy of being my disciple!"

"I reward you with the true artifact Snowfall Frost Robe, capable of warding off fire poison."

"I reward you with a Fortune Cauldron, once used by an Alchemy Saint, capable of greatly improving pill quality."

Under Qu Lingjun's incredulous gaze, amid the commotion of the entire crowd rising to their feet, Luo Chen slowly appeared.

Facing a pair of shocked eyes, Luo Chen smiled slightly.

"I'm back!"

(End of chapter)
