# Chapter 805: One Move, Three Gains; Lingering Fragrance of Dahe

The Ghost God Heart-Inquiring Mirror!

Without a doubt, this was the most extraordinary magic treasure Luo Chen had ever possessed.

At first, he had only thought this treasure had a bewitching effect. But after personally experiencing it, he discovered its heaven-defying power.

It actually had the miraculous ability to assist Initiate cultivators in simulating Adept formation!

Because of this treasure, Luo Chen had vaguely glimpsed the future path of development, understood the general process of cultivation at the Initiate stage, and even before forming his core, he had relied on this treasure to forcefully simulate the results of several core-formation methods. Finally, he created the *Fine Dust Primordial Art* on his own and broke through to the Adept stage in one fell swoop!

Such a rare treasure might not greatly increase a cultivator's personal combat power, but it held extremely significant meaning for the rise and fall of a sect.

Unfortunately, this treasure was already incomplete, and under excessive use, it was completely destroyed.

In the hundred-plus years that followed, Luo Chen would occasionally miss this treasure.

When his nominal disciple in the Northern Sea, Xu Muxian, once wavered in his Dao heart and could not see the path ahead, Luo Chen had thought that if he still had this treasure, he might have been able to let the other see what he truly desired in his heart.

Now that Luo Chen was once again thinking of the Ghost God Heart-Inquiring Mirror, he had his own reasons.

If he had this treasure, combined with the large number of Condensed Liquid Pills and the *Fine Dust Primordial Art* in his hands, he could surely make Adepts emerge from the Luo Tian Sect in droves!

If he had this treasure, he might be able to use the heaven-defying effect of "drawing the soul into illusion, the ghost god inquiring the heart" to allow Canglong to actively cultivate the *Soul-Splitting Art* without needing to unseal himself. Combined with the Great Soul Pill, this could allow the other party to recover from their soul injuries, lay a foundation for their soul, and charge toward the Sage stage.

These two reasons—one to ensure the Luo Tian Sect's inheritance never breaks, and the other to save a fallen old friend.

But Luo Chen had a third thought.

The Ghost God Heart-Inquiring Mirror actually had a partial effect of tempering the soul.

The reason he had been able to possess such a vast soul foundation after forming his core was partly due to this factor.

Since cultivating the *Soul-Splitting Art*, Luo Chen had only mastered the method of splitting, but had no method of nurturing. He could only rely on the Great Soul Pill to forcibly raise the realm of his divided souls.

But such improvement was extremely slow.

For this reason, he had once used the thunderous power in the Primordial Unity Cauldron to temper his soul.

However, that thunderous power had been absorbed by the Primordial Unity Cauldron when he crossed the Sage heavenly tribulation, and its nature was extremely domineering.

Every attempt Luo Chen made required extreme caution and was accompanied by excruciating pain. Often, he would dare to temper himself only after a long interval.

He didn't even dare to use his fragile divided souls for tempering, only allowing his main soul to undergo it.

If he could refine the Ghost God Heart-Inquiring Mirror, supplemented by the Great Soul Pill, he might be able to reproduce the wonder of Han Zhan's incredibly fast cultivation of nine divided souls in a hundred years!

Taking a shortcut was never better than this.

As for how to re-refine the Ghost God Heart-Inquiring Mirror?

Luo Chen's gaze fell upon the illusion core in his hand.

"Back in the Central Continent, I once suspected that Ghost God Valley was a casual move by the Tianyuan Dao Sect in the Eastern Wilderness, with the Rotten Axe-Handle black chess pieces passed down from that lineage as evidence."

"And the Ghost God Heart-Inquiring Mirror's function of interrogating a cultivator's heart and simulating core formation is almost identical in subtlety to a fourth-grade illusion core emitting spiritual mechanisms and assisting Sage cultivators in comprehending the true meaning of laws!"

"Without a doubt, such a rare treasure could not be refined from ordinary materials. Only the illusion cores of the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven possess such miraculous properties!"

Luo Chen was not delusional.

In the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, when he first obtained a low-grade illusion core, he had asked Fu Qinglan about it.

She had also said that low-grade illusion cores were useless to Sage cultivators, merely serving as credentials for testing Adept disciples. The low-grade illusion cores they hunted would basically be traded away after leaving the grotto-heaven, made into various treasures related to illusionary realms.

Clearly, the Ghost God Heart-Inquiring Mirror was one of the derivative products, and at the same time, one of the most outstanding works!

It could serve as a sect's inheritance treasure!

However, the Heart-Inquiring Mirror of Ghost God Valley back then was of a slightly lower grade.

It could only take effect on Initiate cultivators, and its power was difficult to control, requiring treasures like the Rotten Axe-Handle black chess pieces for protection.

"If I use this mid-fourth-grade illusion core as the main material, the Heart-Inquiring Mirror I refine will certainly be of no low grade."

"At the very least, it can assist Adept cultivators."

"At that time, the Adept-level souls I have split off can use it to temper their souls and accelerate their growth!"

The more Luo Chen thought about it, the more excited he became.

The regret he felt over the final failure of the long whip magic treasure also dissipated completely.

As long as he could refine the Ghost God Heart-Inquiring Mirror, he could achieve one move with three gains. Even if it cost him enough time and energy, it would be worth it!

But clearly, although he had the direction, he still needed to take it step by step to achieve his goal.

Whether it was the auxiliary materials, the illusionary formation, or even the most core refining blueprint, he had none of them. This matter would have to be carefully considered.

"How should I take the first step?"

Holding the misty, ethereal illusion core, Luo Chen fell into deep thought.

A tangled mess wrapped around his mind, thousands of threads with no way to start.

Suddenly!

Luo Chen found a thread's end.

Pursing his lips, Luo Chen put away the Primordial Unity Cauldron and the long whip, then walked out of his cave dwelling.

With a thought from his divine sense, he sent a transmission.

Not long after, a figure who had been guarding the cave dwelling rushed over in a hurry.

It was Xu Xiao Liu.

Now advanced in age and nearing the limit of his lifespan, he should have been enjoying his retirement in the Luo Tian Sect. But this time, he had volunteered to follow Luo Chen to Lingtian Pass.

In his words, he had been afraid of death his whole life, and waiting for death at home was even more terrifying. It was better to follow the Supreme Elder and serve with all his might, making the most of his remaining years.

As for whether his true purpose was to enjoy the admiring and fawning gazes of others in the twilight of his life, or to gain some favor and goodwill for his daughter in front of Luo Chen, no one could say.

In any case, he intended to give his all until his dying breath.

"Supreme Elder, what are your orders?"

Luo Chen smiled slightly and asked, "Have there been many visiting cards this past year?"

Xu Xiao Liu answered without hesitation: "Many! I screened them. Among them, there is one from your acquaintance, Pure Crane, and three from Sage-level forces, each from a different sect. Additionally, there are countless Adept cultivators. With your permission, I have read their visiting cards; most want to ask you to refine pills."

"None from Lingfengzi?" Luo Chen was somewhat surprised.

More than a year had passed, and the other party was actually so patient.

Xu Xiao Liu shook his head.

Luo Chen didn't press further, instead asking about another matter.

"Has the Tianyuan Merchant Alliance's shop in Lingtian Pass opened yet?"

Xu Xiao Liu hesitated at this question.

He said with confusion: "Half a year ago, I heard that the Tianyuan Merchant Alliance had chosen a location in Lingtian Pass and was preparing to build a shop. But for some unknown reason, not long after construction began, the Lingtian City Lord unilaterally ordered it to stop. That alliance's grand elder is still going back and forth to the city lord's mansion, seemingly trying to push the matter forward."

"Oh?" Luo Chen blinked, quite curious.

But clearly, with Xu Xiao Liu's level, he wouldn't know the secrets behind this and couldn't provide an answer.

Luo Chen took the four Sage-level visiting cards but didn't even ask about the Adept-level ones.

His energy was limited now; he couldn't afford to bestow favors on Adept-level cultivators.

With the four visiting cards in hand, Luo Chen dismissed Xu Xiao Liu.

He first opened Pure Crane's visiting card.

It was good news!

The fourth-grade Crane Emperor of the White Crane Sect had successfully transformed after taking Luo Chen's Transformation Pill!

The other party was grateful and wanted to meet Luo Chen in person.

But because it was inconvenient for him to come to Lingtian Pass himself, he had asked Pure Crane to notify Luo Chen and invite him to the White Crane Sect as a guest.

"An invitation from the Crane Emperor—I dare not refuse. Unfortunately, now is not the time."

Luo Chen's three-year patrol mission period had not yet ended, so it wasn't suitable for him to leave Lingtian Pass casually.

Back when Nine Spirit Lord had come to ask him to refine pills, they had come and gone in a hurry, precisely because they dared not delay this patrol mission.

Then, Luo Chen opened the other three visiting cards.

They were all from Sage-level sects, also seeking to ask him to refine pills.

The grades ranged from third to fourth.

Two of them were from sects behind the five regions of Lingtian, having heard of his reputation and come seeking him out.

As for the remaining one, it surprised him slightly.

"The Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion belongs to orthodox sword cultivators, who cultivate without relying on external objects. Could they also covet the benefits of pills?"

Muttering to himself, Luo Chen did not reply to these visiting cards.

Instead, he tidied himself up a bit and then openly left his residence.

Heading straight for the city lord's mansion!

...

At dusk.

A slightly disappointed figure walked out of the city lord's mansion.

Gu Zuokai was quite frustrated. Once again, he had returned empty-handed.

Suddenly, a light laugh came to his ears.

"Elder Gu, you said you would come find me when you had time. Nearly two years have passed, and I haven't seen you visit!"

Gu Zuokai was slightly startled. He turned his head and saw a white-clad figure standing under the shade of a tree, looking at him with a smile.

It was Luo Chen!

Gu Zuokai's heart stirred. Although he hadn't released his divine sense out of respect for the Lingtian City Lord, he shouldn't have failed to notice a fellow cultivator of the same rank approaching so closely.

But Luo Chen had appeared nearby without a sound.

"Compared to our meeting in Dongyang Domain three years ago, his Dao has advanced again. His mana fluctuations are so well concealed!"

Sighing inwardly, his face showed the joy of reuniting with an old friend.

He walked toward Luo Chen.

"Fellow Daoist Luo, why are you here?"

Luo Chen smiled and said, "In name, you and I both belong to the Tianyuan Dao Sect. Now that you've come to the Eastern Wilderness, I can barely be considered a host. I had planned to entertain you well when you visited, but I waited in vain, so I had to come and welcome you myself."

Gu Zuokai said helplessly, "You are now honored as an Alchemy Sage, your doorstep crowded with visitors. How could I bear to disturb you?"

"Haha, all empty fame!" Luo Chen extended his hand in a gesture of invitation. "Let's find another place to talk. Today, I'll be the host. Please!"

Gu Zuokai naturally wouldn't refuse. He also wanted to relax a bit.

Under the setting sun, the two figures gradually walked away.

Inside the city lord's mansion, Ye Lingtian slowly withdrew his divine sense.

"Both belonging to the Tianyuan Dao Sect?"

He curled his lips, a hint of mockery in his expression.

...

As a city in a state of war, Lingtian Pass was not entirely cold and forbidding. There were also places for diversion.

After all, a group of fleeing cultivators had been forcibly detained at the front lines and organized into the Death-Defying Army, which had placed immense psychological pressure on them.

Therefore, there were quite a few places within the city for cultivators to relax.

The combat arena was one such place.

Wine shops and tea houses were also common.

And there was another establishment called "Heavenly Fragrance Pavilion," whose purpose was an open secret among cultivators.

This place was open to both men and women!

Inside, there were not only all kinds of beautiful female cultivators but also a large number of handsome male cultivators.

They were not only skilled at flattery and pleasing others but also proficient in dual cultivation techniques.

As long as one could pay the price, they could enjoy a night of pleasure.

And Luo Chen was quite familiar with such an establishment.

Standing by a window on the fourth floor, Luo Chen looked down at the courtyard below, filled with singing, dancing, and flickering shadows. He felt a surge of emotion.

"In my youth, I couldn't afford to visit places of pleasure. I never thought I would make up for that regret at this time."

Yes, this Heavenly Fragrance Pavilion was the same famous Heavenly Fragrance Pavilion from Dahe Market back then.

The same name, the same origin!

They all came from the Joyous Union Sect!

Ever since the Azure Frost Demon Emperor had charged into the human heartland and forcefully killed the remaining Sage cultivators of the Joyous Union Sect, this once-thriving great power, which had almost established branches, had instantly collapsed.

Many Joyous Union Sect cultivators had fled in all directions.

Some had become bandits and villains, like the Joyous Union Sect Adept that the Heartless Fairy had encountered—one who had tried to take advantage of the chaos, capture her out of lust, and use her as a dual cultivation cauldron.

Others had changed their appearance, gone into hiding, and mingled among the common folk, living low-key lives without calling themselves remnants of the Joyous Union.

As for the largest group of Joyous Union Sect cultivators, they had followed the tide and fled to Lingtian Pass, where they huddled together for warmth and still gathered as a group.

However, they no longer dared to walk the world under the name of Joyous Union. Instead, they borrowed the name of the old Heavenly Fragrance Pavilion.

Now, this Heavenly Fragrance Pavilion was their headquarters.

*Thump! Thump!*

A knock came at the door.

Luo Chen turned around and saw a procession of beautiful female cultivators filing in.

Today, to entertain two Sage cultivators, the master of Heavenly Fragrance Pavilion had spared no effort, gathering all the female cultivators of the highest quality.

Even she herself and several Adept elders were willing to offer themselves as companions.

"Two True Persons, these are the finest female cultivators of our Heavenly Fragrance Pavilion. Please select one or two to attend to you. Later, this humble one will arrange singing and dancing for your entertainment."

Luo Chen said casually, "Brother Gu, you go first?"

Gu Zuokai was drinking his wine in melancholy. He waved his hand, indicating that Luo Chen should arrange it.

Luo Chen smiled. Although it was his first time visiting such a place as a guest, he was not shy.

His gaze swept over the crowd. Each female cultivator raised her head and puffed out her chest, each with different charms, yet all with myriad postures, living up to their reputation as beauties.

He spotted the most dazzling one among them and pointed.

"You, go to Brother Gu's side and attend to him well!"

That woman naturally had no objection. She moved with light, graceful steps and walked over joyfully.

If she could win the favor of True Person Gu, she might have a chance to escape this nightmare of a life, and even get far away from the demonic calamity and beastly chaos. How could she not attend to him well?

"You too!"

Luo Chen pointed to another woman. Although her appearance was not as stunning as the first, she had a voluptuous figure and a different charm, one of the most outstanding among the group.

From this, one could see how much he valued Gu Zuokai.

Then, he waved his hand.

"All of you, leave!"

The master of Heavenly Fragrance Pavilion was stunned. "True Person, what about you?"

Luo Chen was about to say he didn't need anyone, but his gaze inadvertently fell upon a female cultivator in the second row who had her head lowered.

All the women were thrusting out their chests and raising their hips, displaying their exquisite figures to seek the True Person's attention. But this woman had been avoiding his gaze.

Strange?

Luo Chen beckoned. "You, come here!"

That female cultivator trembled all over and walked out from the crowd.

Luo Chen frowned. "Lift your head!"

The master of Heavenly Fragrance Pavilion also sensed something was wrong and scolded, "Yu Xiang, why are you so shy in front of a True Person?"

A name that was vaguely familiar from memory, combined with that fair, jade-like face that held a hint of resemblance, immediately clicked into place.

The female cultivator bowed gracefully to Luo Chen, her voice trembling slightly: "Yu Xiang pays respects to True Person Luo!"

It was indeed Yu Xiang, the inner disciple of the Joyous Union Sect from the Heavenly Fragrance Pavilion in Dahe Market back then.

Luo Chen's expression remained calm. "You stay. Just pour wine for me."

Then, ignoring her thoughts, he sat down.

He raised his cup to Gu Zuokai across from him.

"Brother Gu, cheers!"

(End of this chapter)
