# Chapter 549: This Job Has to Be Done! (Includes Attribute Inventory)

In the dark and mysterious depths of the ocean,

the water flowed steadily and regularly, while schools of fish drifted freely in sheets.

Occasionally, a ray of sunlight pierced through layer after layer of water and shone down, but after glimpsing a smear of bright red, it vanished.

The quiet coral trench, within several thousand li around it, was enough to be called a forbidden zone for living beings.

Any creature with some intelligence would subconsciously avoid this place.

Because beneath the vivid exterior of those clustered masses of blood coral were piles of white bones.

A layer of red light spread along the rock wall, isolating the water current.

Deep within the rock wall was a simple cave residence.

Inside the cave residence, a white-robed Daoist in loose clothing sat quietly.

At first glance, he made no sound and had no presence, as if he were dead.

But every thirty-six breaths, his lips would open and close slightly, exhaling a long and vigorous breath.

Unmoving as a mountain, lofty as a pine, he actually gave people the illusion of an ancient Black Tortoise alive in the world.

Suddenly, the white-robed Daoist slowly opened his eyes.

His calm eyes examined himself, and he nodded in satisfaction.

"Three months of seclusion to polish my realm has finally worn away that restless fire."

As his thoughts moved, the aura on his body suddenly released and climbed section by section, like a volcano long accumulating power and about to erupt. Its momentum was overbearing, hard for ordinary Core Formation cultivators to match. That terrifying spiritual pressure could perhaps only be compared to great cultivators.

Suddenly, the aura withdrew like a receding tide, all of it restrained into his body. He seemed like dead wood and a mute sparrow, showing not the slightest fluctuation.

Looking at him now, he was almost no different from a mortal.

Between this release and withdrawal, it represented that Luo Chen's realm had stabilized, and he had thoroughly become a mid-stage cultivator at Core Formation Stage Four!

Only, although he was Core Formation Stage Four, Luo Chen had ample confidence that he could fight a late Core Formation cultivator!

His eyelids lifted slightly, staring into the void.

A transparent light screen slowly unfurled like a curtain.

Looking at the attribute panel, Luo Chen's heart filled with a sense of harvest.

A full twelve years had passed since he escaped Cangwu Mountain.

Twelve years was enough to let an infant who had just fallen crying to the ground grow into a youth who understood human affairs.

And for him, it had also brought earth-shaking changes!

First was the terrifying change in lifespan!

It had directly gone from 446 to 480, violently increasing by a full thirty-six years.

Among these changes, aside from the fixed increase of twenty years from Core Formation Stage Three to Core Formation Stage Four, the extra sixteen years, according to Luo Chen's judgment, should have come from that grand feat back when he was in the Xintian Sea Region, using azure flame to refine over ten thousand Blue-Ringed Sea Serpents in one stroke.

Over ten thousand first-tier sea serpents, after he absorbed their vast vitality, had brought sixteen years of lifespan.

This could not be called anything but a pleasant surprise!

Because of this pleasant surprise, Luo Chen had at one point felt the impulse to hunt demon beasts on a large scale. Even coming to the Xuanyan Sea Region to hunt demon crabs had been driven partly by this desire.

However, under this desire, he still did his utmost to restrain the greed in his heart.

Some things, if pushed too far, became worse than not enough.

What did a higher lifespan limit matter?

If realm did not rise, in the end it was still moon in water, flower in mirror.

On the contrary, if he engaged in great slaughter, it would instead easily attract fatal disaster.

He had only hunted demon crabs for five years and had already drawn the Demon Spider Crab race's siege. If he truly acted without restraint, who knew whether the next one to come knocking would be some Demon Emperor!

Putting aside changes in qi refinement realm, another pleasant surprise was the progress in body refinement.

However, because he had watched his physique grow at every moment over these five years, Luo Chen was not that excited now.

Archaic Tier Three, progress had reached sixty-seven percent.

This also meant he had reached the late-stage realm.

Archaic beasts did not transform into human form and purely walked the path of strength, excelling in physique.

A late third-tier Archaic beast physique, placed in the current Mountains and Seas Realm, was enough to crush the vast majority of third-tier demon beasts.

Perhaps it could even contend with some fourth-tier demon beasts that did not particularly emphasize body refinement.

"This is the result of fleeing far to the Xuanyan Sea Region and bitterly cultivating for five years!"

A faint smile hung on Luo Chen's mouth.

In five years, from just entering mid stage to stepping into late stage in one stroke, it had brought him powerful combat strength.

However, aside from joy, Luo Chen also had a faint worry.

"This physique of mine has higher and higher requirements for external objects. Now, third-tier Black Emperor Paste can still take effect, but it no longer has the efficiency of advancing by leaps and bounds as before."

"If I want to maintain high-speed growth during increasingly difficult late-stage cultivation, I fear I must consider fourth-tier Black Emperor Paste."

"But fourth-tier Black Emperor Paste..."

Luo Chen found this troublesome.

The main material for fourth-tier Black Emperor Paste was third-tier demon beasts!

Demon beasts capable of cultivating to the third tier were basically local heroes, enough to be called demon kings, and absolutely not weaklings.

Especially the demon crab races suitable for refining Black Emperor Paste. They lived in groups.

After so many years, he had only killed three Demon Kings three months ago and gathered two complete sets of crab shells.

Oh!

The third-tier Demon Spider Crab that had been captured alive was already gone.

Its crab shell had been refined into medicine.

Its crab meat had been divided and eaten by Luo Chen and Black King.

Its pair of giant claws and eight crab legs had been carefully collected as artifact-refining materials, to be handled after leaving the Xuanyan Sea Region.

The demon core was the same.

As for the other scraps, Luo Chen had bestowed them all upon Black King, letting him devour and refine them.

Finally, there was the demon soul!

Luo Chen personally drew it into the Soul-Refining Banner, where it became the Myriad Souls Banner's third Ghost King-level main soul.

Under Han Zhan's control, it should be able to erupt with quite decent power.

One could say that this existence, whom Luo Chen had still called "Fellow Daoist" verbally before, had been skinned, deboned, and used to the utmost.

Returning to the main topic.

Thinking of refining large amounts of fourth-tier Black Emperor Paste made Luo Chen somewhat troubled.

One could even say that it might not merely be fourth-tier Black Emperor Paste. Under the current situation where the grass had been stirred and the snake startled, hunting low-tier demon crabs and refining third-tier Black Emperor Paste would likely be difficult too.

"Take it one step at a time first!"

"No matter how much I plan, it cannot keep up with changes."

Luo Chen shook his head, his gaze sliding downward.

The two main lifebound cultivation arts, one progressing slowly and the other completely perfected, naturally did not need much mention.

The Myriad Beast Sutra, used to enslave demon beasts, had reached the Perfect level. This was an unexpected joy.

It was probably related to him frequently driving Black King and Heavenly Pivot.

After long-term command, Luo Chen had gained some understanding of how to enslave demon beasts.

Therefore, it was only natural that the Myriad Beast Sutra, only at second-tier level, had grown so quickly in proficiency.

"Speaking of which, this world has a famous Myriad Beast Sect. Although it is only a Sage upper sect, its ancestors are suspected to have produced an Ascendant mighty figure. Unfortunately, this sect is not in the Eastern Wasteland, nor in the Northern Sea, but in that Southern Frontier that makes people turn pale when mentioned."

After muttering this, Luo Chen's gaze continued moving.

The renamed Innate Primordial Great Handprint had reached Perfected Completion ten years ago.

The defensive spell he created, Sevenfold Blazing Heaven Rings, because it was self-created, progressed quickly and had already reached grandmaster level.

The Cloud-Seizing Divine Claw he had cultivated for many years had reached Perfected Completion, as had Blazing Fire Miasma.

His self-created Voidburst Step had likewise progressed quickly, reaching the Perfect level and standing only one step away from Perfected Completion.

These were all gratifying changes in Luo Chen's combat strength.

This also meant his "hardware" had obtained an absolute increase.

Aside from this was his "software."

At present, Luo Chen concurrently held three great skills, which could naturally also be viewed as three professions.

The one with the highest achievement was impressively the alchemist profession.

A full fourth-tier level!

Alchemy arts of every element came readily to his hand. Whether in knowledge or practical operation, he could be called outstanding.

Calling him a present-generation alchemy master, even grandmaster, would not be excessive in the slightest!

The third-tier True Flame Pill he personally refined had reached Perfected Completion, adding bricks and tiles to his cultivation.

The first-tier Condensed Water Pill originating from the Swallow Islands allowed Luo Chen, after personally refining it, to gain direct experience of the Northern Sea cultivation world's alchemy.

He refined it to Perfected Completion in one breath, while also integrating the Northern Sea alchemy style within it into his own alchemy system.

If, in the future, he could understand more of the Northern Sea's high-level alchemy, perhaps Luo Chen's alchemy could climb another level.

Third-tier physician?

Luo Chen shook his head. Perhaps this path ended here.

After cultivating only the fire element, he lacked the other four elements of mana among the Five Elements. Even though he could temporarily convert them, in actual use, it was rather inconvenient.

And medical arts often involved every aspect.

What they required was comprehensiveness, not specialization.

If he were a cultivator mainly cultivating water or wood, that would be fine, since they were more inclusive.

Unfortunately, the fire element leaned toward tyranny, making it very difficult for him to concurrently cultivate medical arts of other attributes.

The physician path could be seen to its end at a glance, but on the formation master side, his progress was advancing a thousand li in a day!

After Min Longyu and Fu Jiusheng laid Luo Chen's foundation,

now he had Han Zhan, this fourth-tier formation master, personally guiding him.

Luo Chen's study of basic formations of every element advanced extremely quickly!

The Five Elements basic formations were complete, and the spirit-gathering and qi-gathering formations were also complete.

He was also mostly proficient in some second-tier formations.

Especially the Nine-Turn Flame Dragon Grand Formation he favored. Under day after day of copying, it had reached grandmaster level.

If he had sufficient materials to lay it out, he believed its power might even be three parts greater than the Nine-Turn Flame Dragon Grand Formation Min Longyu had arranged back then!

Now he had also obtained a third-tier formation plate.

For Luo Chen, this truly counted as falling asleep and meeting a pillow, allowing him to use all he had studied over many years.

However, that formation plate mainly contained water-type formations, and still required adjustment and modification.

Regarding this, Luo Chen had already asked Han Zhan to help adjust the formation plate. After some time, there might be results.

After taking inventory, Luo Chen could not help feeling proud and satisfied.

Finally, his gaze landed on the Achievement Points column.

[151]

The highest value in history!

Twelve years ago, it had only barely broken one hundred.

Yet in a mere twelve years, it had soared by fifty achievement points.

"As expected, a cultivator's secluded quiet cultivation will always be the best cultivation method above battle."

"If it were the me of the past, rushing around and toiling, forget twelve years, even another ten or twenty years would not have produced this many achievement points."

That dazzling number made Luo Chen feel not only proud, but also full of security.

There was even a little expectation.

"As for the Nascent Soul Pill formula, I have only the medicinal material list, not the corresponding proportions, refinement method, details, taboos, and so on."

"If I want to deduce the true pill formula in the future, I will inevitably have to spend an enormous number of achievement points."

"Crossing an entire major tier, the required achievement points will be many. But no matter how many, it should not exceed one hundred points. My one hundred fifty-one points should be enough no matter what, right?"

As he muttered to himself, Luo Chen grew even more confident in his future cultivation path.

The path of cultivation had many difficulties and dangers.

Even without meeting powerful enemies, there were still limitations from the resources he himself needed.

But under his efforts, those so-called limitations were also solved one by one.

Cultivation arts, pills, spirit lands, magic treasures...

"It is nothing more than earth covering water when it comes, and generals blocking soldiers when they arrive!"

Putting away the attribute panel, Luo Chen laughed freely, stood, and left the seclusion cave residence.

...

"This is the intelligence sent back from Heavenly Pivot?"

In the outer hall, Luo Chen held the wooden board Black King handed over. On it was intelligence written personally by Heavenly Pivot.

The handwriting was crooked and twisted, but one could vaguely see a few traces of elegance.

Heavenly Pivot was female!

Of course, this was not the important point. The important point was the intelligence.

"The five great royal demon crab races, Feiyun Ravine cultivators, the Ring-Headed Turtle race... these forces have gathered at Xuanyan Island. What are they trying to do?"

As he muttered, Luo Chen faintly sensed a pressure like mountain rain about to arrive.

According to what was written, these forces could be said to have come out in full strength.

In the sea regions near Xuanyan Island, they had clashed several times and fought violently.

Even the Feiyun Ravine cultivators had once been accidentally dragged in, and one Core Formation cultivator had even died!

If there were nothing attracting them, Luo Chen absolutely would not believe it.

"Could it be the fourth-tier spirit vein land?" Black King carefully said.

Luo Chen raised his brow!

He had long known that the Xuanyan Sea Region had a fourth-tier spirit vein.

It was on Xuanyan Island.

Because of this spirit vein land, countless demon races had also been attracted to occupy and reside there.

Because of this, when he had chosen his base back then, he had deliberately stayed far away from Xuanyan Island.

If they were fighting over the spirit land, that was not impossible.

But!

What were human cultivators involving themselves in it for?

At present, the Northern Sea cultivation world had more than enough for self-defense, but could not advance.

Forget opening wars, just going to sea to hunt demon beasts was full of dangers.

Feiyun Ravine had a mere seven Core Formation cultivators. Were they mad to involve themselves in a spirit land dispute?

Luo Chen shook his head, eliminating this possibility.

Perhaps there was some precious treasure or opportunity inside that had caused the demon races of the Xuanyan Sea Region to fight so fiercely!

Wait!

Luo Chen looked at the wooden board again. On it were written the numbers of fallen third-tier demon kings from the five great royal demon crab races.

The more he looked, the more his heart pounded.

He had just been worrying earlier about how to refine large amounts of fourth-tier Black Emperor Paste.

If he could participate in this, fish in troubled waters, and get a few third-tier demon crabs, would this problem not naturally be solved?

Third-tier demon crabs were enormous. The Black Emperor Paste refined from one could equal a hundred portions from second-tier ones.

If he got seven or eight, no, perhaps five or six,

it would be enough for his late Archaic cultivation needs!

The moment he thought of this, Luo Chen could no longer hold back.

"This job has to be done!"
