# Chapter 865: Mountains and Seas Leveled, Forbidden Land Appears, Dust Finally Settles

A figure drifted like a willow catkin through the sea of clouds, flying slowly but steadily toward the direction of Azure Sky Domain.

His pale golden eyes pierced through layer upon layer of mist, occasionally glancing downward.

The scene before him was shocking.

The embankments of the water lakes had collapsed. Large numbers of fish and shrimp lay exposed on the dry riverbeds, their bodies twitching feebly.

Bubbles emerged from their mouths as they tried to moisten themselves, but it was a drop in the bucket.

"The intricate underground water system of Taihu Domain has collapsed," Luo Chen murmured. "A vast amount of lake water has poured toward lower elevations. Without basic material support, the spirit veins of this domain are as good as destroyed."

After only a brief observation, Luo Chen had roughly understood the situation in Taihu Domain.

He did not stop his steps but continued advancing along the path he had come.

Because he had deliberately slowed his pace, it took him a full three days to return to Nine Spirits Domain.

Before he had even crossed the boundary marker, Luo Chen's footsteps halted.

The sound of lapping waves reached his ears from a great distance.

A strange expression appeared on Luo Chen's face.

Nine Spirits Domain, with its many mountains and steep terrain, stood at a high altitude, earning it the name "Connecting to the Nine Heavens."

Yet this sound of lapping waves signified a tremendous change.

*Swish!*

Luo Chen leaped into the air and landed on a broken cliff face.

What met his eyes made him draw in a sharp breath.

"Has all the water from Taihu Domain flowed into Nine Spirits Domain?"

The vast expanse of water stretched endlessly, as if connected to the horizon.

The lake waters surged violently, wave after wave crashing against the shore, producing thunderous roars.

The precarious cliff where he stood occasionally shed gravel blocks under the impact of the lake water.

Gazing into the distance, sunlight spilled across the lake's surface, glittering and sparkling. The dazzling light was like countless gems shining, nearly blinding the eyes.

Occasionally, sharp peaks rose above the water like small islands, offering brief resting places for birds.

But upon closer inspection, those were no islands—they were clearly the high mountain peaks that had once stood in Nine Spirits Domain.

This scene, both unfamiliar and familiar, made Luo Chen feel as though he had returned to the Northern Sea.

"The land has sunk, mountains have collapsed, lake water has poured in, creating a seamless union of sea and sky!"

As he murmured, Luo Chen felt a deep reverence for the destructive power of Ascendants.

They had recklessly drawn upon the origin qi of heaven and earth, causing an imbalance in spiritual energy and the collapse of earth veins, forcibly altering the natural environment.

Taihu Domain had become a land of drought.

In just a few days, a great lake had been born in Nine Spirits Domain. No, it could no longer be called a lake—its vast area of tens of thousands of li could fully be called a sea!

And what of Azure Sky Domain, the main battlefield?

Luo Chen took a deep breath and shot forward.

The powerful air current parted the calm sea surface, creating a white wave that rolled forward.

One day later.

*Chi!*

Luo Chen, who had been moving at extreme speed, suddenly stopped.

He reached out in surprise and touched his cheek.

There was a warm, moist sensation.

He brought his fingers before his eyes—a smear of bright red was clearly visible.

As if realizing something, Luo Chen extended his hand and reached forward.

A gust of wind blew past, and a faint wound immediately appeared on Luo Chen's palm.

Because of his battle with the Hundred-Legged Great Demon Emperor, the Mystic Dust Armor that had been constantly attached to his body had been severely damaged, so he had removed it.

But the defensive strength of Luo Chen's physical body was by no means inferior to the true artifact-level Mystic Dust Armor!

How could a mere wisp of wind break through his body's surface defense?

What kind of change had Azure Sky Domain undergone?

Luo Chen's mind tightened. Source power spread throughout his body, forming a tough blood membrane.

Not only that, but he also formed hand seals and cast spells, adding several protective shields over himself.

He advanced cautiously.

One step, two steps, ten zhang, a hundred zhang. After penetrating a thousand li deep, Luo Chen helplessly stopped.

All his magical power shields had already collapsed.

The blood membrane covering his entire body had been destroyed and regenerated time and again under the repeated cutting of the astral wind.

Although Luo Chen could still provide vast amounts of source power to sustain this protective blood membrane, the rate of consumption had become unbearable.

But through this brief contact, Luo Chen had vaguely realized what changes had occurred in Azure Sky Domain.

This place was filled with the true intent of the metal-attribute laws!

Everything within it, even the flying sand, rolling stones, clear wind, and white clouds, was filled with terrifying sharpness and cutting power.

For ordinary cultivators, let alone a thousand li, it would be difficult to enter even a hundred li.

If he recklessly pushed his source power to the limit, and with the Mystic Dust Armor at full strength, he might be able to rush ten thousand li deep, but it would still be extremely dangerous.

Luo Chen was not unfamiliar with such a special environment.

In fact, he was very familiar with it!

In the Demon-Fall Land of the Fallen Sea, there were three heavens: Leaving Forgetting Heaven, Five Elements Heaven, and Bright Radiance Heaven.

And that Five Elements Heaven was divided into five domains, each containing the ubiquitous true intent of a single-attribute Five Elements law!

However, compared to the orderly, man-made arrangement of Five Elements Heaven, the law intent in Azure Sky Domain was chaotic and filled with hostility toward all living beings.

Using Five Elements Heaven as a comparison was somewhat inappropriate.

After all, that was personally arranged by Devil Lord Heaven-Refining.

The current situation in Azure Sky Domain was more similar to another secret realm Luo Chen had experienced.

Bai Ye's Cave Mansion!

In the Jade Cauldron Domain, beneath Accumulated Thunder Nine Mountains, a great power had fallen, and their palace had become a relic.

It contained terrifying true intent of thunder!

In fact, Accumulated Thunder Nine Mountains had formed bit by bit precisely because of the thunder true intent that had spread from the relic.

Luo Chen had heard the general story from some cultivators who had entered Bai Ye's Cave Mansion back then. After a thousand years, the true intent had not ceased, and the deeper one went, the more dangerous it became, like a thunder pool.

Wasn't the current situation in Azure Sky Domain extremely similar to Bai Ye's Cave Mansion?

The golden wind astral energy was everywhere on the outside.

The deeper one went, the more frequently law intent appeared.

In other words—had the Ancient Demon Emperor Ditian already fallen?

The law intent he had painstakingly cultivated, now unconstrained by his physical body, had filled the heavens and earth.

The Ancient Demon Emperor Ditian, whose original form was a White Tiger, whose laws were primarily metal—this perfectly matched the current situation.

Through the changes in the environment, Luo Chen had guessed the outcome of the generation-old ancient demon.

The sacred land had just been established, the demon court had not yet been erected. He fell at Azure Wandering, his fate ended at Nether Origin. In the end, it was all a grand ambition come to nothing!

He felt both a sigh of regret for the fall of Pingshan Lord and shock at the ruthlessness of the Nether Origin Sect. When they did not move, they were still; when they moved, they quelled the demon rebellion; when they moved again, they destroyed the ancient demon.

This Eastern Wilderness was ultimately going to completely change its surname to Nether Origin.

Luo Chen abandoned the thought of continuing forward.

With the methods of three Ascendants, having killed Ditian, they would certainly not leave any good things behind for others to pick up.

The environment here was so chaotic and disordered; one careless mistake could mean death within. Why take the risk?

Perhaps after eight hundred or a thousand years, under the infiltration of metal-attribute law true intent, Azure Sky Domain would naturally generate some precious resources like Five Elements Heaven and Bai Ye's Cave Mansion. But that was not something Luo Chen could covet now.

Just as Luo Chen was about to turn and leave, his steps paused for a moment.

He turned back, looking uncertainly into the depths of Azure Sky Domain.

There, there was a faint connection with him!

"Could it be... Ditu!"

Luo Chen had once collected the other's soul blood and placed a slave seal on him.

Even though the slave seal had been removed and the soul blood returned, that had only happened a few days ago, so Luo Chen could very accurately identify the other's aura.

"How could Ditu still be alive!"

Luo Chen swallowed his saliva and took a step forward.

But in the end, he withdrew his foot.

His pale golden eyes took a deep look into the azure depths, then he turned and left!

After he left, the golden wind still howled in the depths of Azure Sky Domain, the astral energy scraping bones.

A golden tiger, covered in wounds and several hundred zhang in size, curled up in a hollow, its breath faint and intermittent.

Suddenly, a golden wind blew toward it, but upon touching its body, it became gentle and brushed past.

The golden tiger's body trembled, its eyelids tightly shut.

Vaguely, turbid tears flowed down...

---

The peace talks between the two races at Azure Wandering Mountain had changed!

This matter was reported to everyone on the same day by Tandouzi, who had fled back to Lingtian Pass.

Everyone understood what this meant.

If the Nether Origin Sect won, the Eastern Wilderness human race would once again take the dominant position.

If they fought to a draw—that was a situation no one wanted to see.

Because if that happened, this great war between humans and demons would inevitably drag on. Apart from the Nether Origin Sect, no other faction could bear such losses.

If the Nether Origin Sect lost...

This last possibility was rejected by everyone.

Three ancestors had made their move, plus thirty-six Sage cultivators from the Nether Origin Sect forming a grand formation—with such a lineup, aside from the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, who in the world could defeat them?

Thus, everyone was hoping for the first possibility to become reality.

Of course, during this process, everyone was not without action.

Those with slightly weaker strength were retreating desperately to the rear, to avoid being caught in the battle of the great powers.

Those with slightly stronger strength were wandering at extremely far distances, peeping at the details of the battle.

When the great battle had lasted three days and three nights, everything settled.

Between heaven and earth, the turbulent origin qi gradually calmed. Sage cultivators could tentatively spread their domains and control the free spiritual energy.

In the nine heavens, there were no more shining stars, no more rumbling thunder—that meant the other battlefield had also produced a result.

After the battle ended, the three ancestors of the Nether Origin Sect did not show themselves but went straight back to the Nether Abyss.

Not only that, but after the group of Sage cultivators from the Nether Origin Sect returned, they did not stay long before hurriedly going back to their mountain gate.

Only the Lingtian City Lord, who looked greatly weakened, stayed behind, still managing the affairs of Lingtian Pass.

Some busybodies went to the battlefield to observe, trying to gain something.

But they gained nothing!

All they saw was shocking, heart-trembling scenes.

Azure Sky Domain had become a forbidden land, with no living beings allowed entry!

The earth veins of Fire Ridge had collapsed in chaos, magma erupted, turning into a vast world of flames. Unless one was a fire-attribute cultivator, it was impossible to move even an inch within, completely unsuitable for cultivation.

Nine Spirits Domain no longer had high mountains, only a boundless sea. When Nine Spirits Lord himself arrived at the seaside, he was silent before his homeland, merely giving this sea a name—**Pingshan Sea**.

It carried the meaning of leveled mountains and lakes filling the land, as well as a memorial symbol for the fall of the generation-old ancient demon, Pingshan Lord.

Taihu Domain had lost a great deal of water and soil, its spiritual energy thin, showing signs of future persistent drought.

Just one battle of Ascendants, and one that had been artificially limited in scope, had destroyed four entire domains!

This showed the might of Ascendants!

As for Overlord Saber Domain, which had originally been turned into a spirit-absent land by a group of Sage cultivators, it had suffered the least impact in this great battle.

Many cultivators used this relatively calm place of Overlord Saber Domain to travel back and forth, gathering information.

Lingfengzi was one of them.

Luo Chen had disappeared!

As the newly promoted Sage elder of the Luo Tian Sect, he had to personally come forward to find Luo Chen.

He first went to Fire Ridge, the land of flame mountains, because Luo Chen primarily cultivated fire, so Lingfengzi thought Luo Chen might be hiding there.

But after a thorough search with no results, he had to detour to Overlord Saber Domain, trying to go to the rear.

Just as Lingfengzi was at a loss, a voice transmission reached his ears.

Lingfengzi was startled, then overjoyed. He flew toward an empty valley.

Luo Chen, dressed in a white robe, had been waiting for some time.

"Senior Brother, you're still alive indeed!"

Luo Chen smiled faintly and asked about the recent situation of the Luo Tian Sect.

Lingfengzi truthfully informed him, "The Luo Tian Sect is fine. We are still residing in the Fenghua Palace."

Luo Chen nodded. "That's good."

Lingfengzi was somewhat curious. "What exactly happened that day, to provoke the Ascendants to personally make a move, and even involve all the Sage cultivators of the Nether Origin Sect?"

Luo Chen waved his hand and did not tell him the specifics.

After all, the three ancestors of the Nether Origin Sect had taken advantage of the peace talks to launch a sudden, ruthless attack. Spreading that would not be glorious.

Knowing too much would be harmful rather than beneficial for those with slightly weaker strength.

He curiously asked about another matter.

"How were the casualties among the Nether Origin Sect's Sage cultivators?"

Mentioning this, Lingfengzi became eager to talk.

"Very heavy!"

"During the great battle at Lingtian Pass back then, the Nether Origin Sect only lost five Sage cultivators. But this time, they lost a full twelve Sage cultivators!"

"Not only that, but of the six late-stage Sage Grand Cultivators, only two have shown their faces—the Lingtian City Lord and Chongxuan Sage."

"But the Lingtian City Lord was greatly weakened and has been refusing visitors since returning. The situation of Chongxuan Sage is also not good; he went to Shouyang Mountain to recuperate."

"I heard from Tandouzi that another, Hanyang Sage, had even his dharma body shattered, with only his Nascent Soul surviving."

"As for Leidaozi, there's no sign of him. I don't know his situation."

Hearing these pieces of news, Luo Chen fell into deep thought.

Thirty-six Sage cultivators had formed a grand formation to contend with an Ascendant. Initially, they could suppress him, but in the end, they suffered heavy casualties.

Even a strong cultivator like the Lingtian City Lord had been greatly weakened. The other Grand Cultivators were probably not much better off.

Indeed, the price of fighting across a major realm under such suppression was not something ordinary forces could bear.

He wondered if Qinghe Immortal had escaped.

Luo Chen asked about Qinghe Immortal's fate, but unfortunately, Lingfengzi was not clear on that.

"Never mind. Let's go back first!" Luo Chen said.

As for his killing of the Hundred-Legged Great Demon Emperor, Luo Chen did not inform the other party.

His reputation was already sufficient, and his future direction was already set. He no longer needed such matters to enhance his fame.

Moreover, Hundred-Legged was ultimately a Great Demon Emperor. If word spread that he had died at Luo Chen's hands, it would be too shocking.

Great Demon Emperor Grand Cultivators, when Ascendants did not make a move, were already the most peak existences in this realm, extremely difficult to kill.

The Mad Wanderer, foremost of the Three Great Wanderers of the Northern Sea, was still alive and well in the Northern Sea cultivation world, even though he was mad.

The Eastern Wilderness great war had been fierce, yet after over a hundred years, not a single Great Demon Emperor Grand Cultivator had fallen.

Even the Seven Rhinoceros Commander, after successive battles and being severely wounded multiple times, was still alive.

While this was partly because cultivators of the same rank did not go for the kill, it also showed the strength of Great Demon Emperor Grand Cultivators.

If Luo Chen quietly killed a Great Demon Emperor, even the weakest of the nine commanders, if word reached the ears of cultivators of the same rank, it would instead make others more wary of him.

On the way back, Luo Chen casually asked about the situations of Tandouzi and Divine Origin Sage.

Lingfengzi also gave him the answers one by one.

Tandouzi was fine, but one of his twelve Dutian God-Slaughter Banners had been destroyed, and he was now frantically collecting materials to try to repair it.

Divine Origin Sage was the same as before, elusive and unpredictable.

Lingfengzi had only learned from low-level cultivators of the Five Elements Divine Sect that their Supreme Elder was safe and sound.

"They're all old monsters with countless life-saving methods!"

"If Hundred-Legged hadn't taken the initiative to come after me, if my combat methods weren't varied, if Xiaoling hadn't used a soul shock to make him pause for a moment, he probably could have escaped alive."

Luo Chen sighed with such emotion.

And before they knew it, they had arrived outside Lingtian Pass.

"Should we go in?" Lingfengzi asked.

Luo Chen hesitated. He wasn't very keen on entering Lingtian Pass right now.

But just as he was about to detour back to Fenghua Palace, a weak voice reached his ears.

"Since Fellow Daoist has returned, why not come to my residence for a chat?"
