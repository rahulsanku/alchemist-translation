# Chapter 1083: Thousand Skies Converge, Reversing the Slaying of a Great Power

The complete Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard was the most important of the Three Treasures of the Dao Sect.

It was not a supreme offensive treasure, nor was its defensive power anything remarkable.

Its main function was to suppress the qi fortune of an entire continent and absorb external spiritual energy.

It was precisely because of the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard that the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, after thousands of years, could become the foremost holy land of Ascendants in the Mountains and Seas Realm!

But to say it truly had no defensive capability would be nonsense.

Even Rosycloud had to shatter the entire Central Continent before she could heavily damage the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard—this alone showed it had some defensive merit.

With only this clone of Luo Chen's now, he truly could not break through it.

Especially after the previous great battle. Although he had strolled leisurely, wantonly slaughtering many Sages, this fleshly body, which had not been overly tempered, was ultimately overwhelmed after absorbing a massive amount of power from the Nether Water Spring.

That sword strike from Mingyan—had he not wanted to block it?

No!

It was simply that Luo Chen's body at that time could no longer bear any more power from the spring.

If he used more, it would collapse.

Therefore, Luo Chen relied on a soul art from his destiny soul's memories—Ghosts and Gods Give Way—to barely dodge Mingyan's killing blow.

Now, facing the complete Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard, facing Ke Lianshan who was burning himself in a final radiance, Luo Chen's expression was calm, his state of mind open and clear.

For certain things, one must ultimately sacrifice some things.

The destiny soul was something he must obtain, and this clone was merely a tool, even if it had been fused with and carried the Nether Water Spring.

"Then let it be discarded!"

Murmuring, Luo Chen floated up, his fingers continuously tapping points on his body.

Vaguely, the sound of shackles being unlocked echoed in his ears.

Deep within his sea of consciousness, a restless, despairing, and unwilling voice cried out.

"Don't!"

Luo Chen ignored it, his final finger heavily pressing on his own glabella.

"Jade Shattering Seal, open!"

The gloomy voice slowly echoed within the grotto-heaven.

In the distance, Mingyan watched the bizarre scene in terror.

Luo Chen's fleshly body seemed to melt, turning into streams of black liquid, drifting eerily in the void.

Then, a spring, like an eye, suddenly appeared.

From the spring flowed a great amount of liquid, deep and dark as ink.

That was... Nether Water!

A vast amount of Nether Water surged forth, rushing towards Ke Lianshan.

Drops converged into streams, streams into rivers, rivers into an abyss.

Countless Nether Water formed a mighty river, madly scouring the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard.

One had guarded the Nether Origin Sect for thousands of years, restraining billions of demons and ghosts.

The other had suppressed the qi fortune of the Central Continent, earning the Dao Sect its foremost reputation.

In this secluded grotto-heaven, unknown to outsiders, a pure collision unfolded.

The two seemed evenly matched?

Until the spring visibly swelled and expanded wildly, a destructive aura instantly filling the entire Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven.

Only one sound was heard in the void.

"Explode!"

The next moment!

That Nether Water Spring, which had once nurtured the Nether Abyss, exploded with a roar.

*Boom!*

An extremely unmatched force, like a heavy hammer, smashed onto the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard.

*Crack!*

Cracks appeared once again on the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard.

Ke Lianshan's eyes widened in disbelief as he watched this scene, and then endless Nether Water washed over him.

The other party was actually willing to self-destruct such a precious treasure, not even caring about his own fleshly body?

There was no time for too many thoughts in his heart. He knew he had ultimately failed.

After detonating the Nether Water Spring, a streak of light, using the powerful recoil force, shot towards the vortex leading to the outside world.

Ke Lianshan watched all this, extremely unwilling to face the fact that boundless Nether Water was about to drown him.

"Failed?"

"No!"

"As long as the person is still here, there is always hope."

Ke Lianshan murmured, waving his hand.

The Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard protecting him transformed into a light shield, falling over several places on the wasteland.

Then, he calmly faced the torrent of Nether Water.

Mingyan knelt on the ground, leaning on her sword, protected by the light shield of the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard beside her, but her eyes were only on the figure of the old man submerged in the Nether Water.

Her eyes blurred with tears, she only wailed.

"Senior Brother..."

Ke Lianshan's figure ultimately disappeared. The vast Nether Water rolled forward, flooding the great wasteland, roaring towards the distance.

The entire Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven fell into the Netherworld!

...

"Monster, you cannot stop this Lord!"

Within the pillar of light connecting to the cosmic star sea, a roar filled with boundless killing intent echoed.

A phantom figure stood at the edge of the sky, step by step walking towards the Mountains and Seas Realm.

Before him was a strange space.

This space existed neither in the Mountains and Seas Realm nor in a minor realm, seemingly forming its own world within the cosmos.

It was precisely because of this space that White Tiger True Lord was separated from descending into this realm.

After White Tiger True Lord roared, a sharp blade light slashed from his hand.

That strange space suddenly shattered!

White Tiger True Lord took another step closer!

Facing all this, Azure Frost's expression was calm, silent, only gazing at the void, frequently forming hand seals.

One by one, white flower buds emerged from the void's origin qi, then bloomed.

Strange spaces, like great mountains and natural moats, once again blocked the path of White Tiger True Lord's advance.

A Flower Blooms a World, Empty Peaks a Thousand Cliffs!

This was a divine ability she had obtained upon advancing to Ascendant, after completely refining the Accumulated Void Flower.

As long as her dharma power was not exhausted, she could manipulate the world's origin qi to form new small worlds, binding enemies and even all attacks within them, wearing them away into nothingness.

White Tiger True Lord was very strong, unexpectedly strong. Those small worlds could not trap him.

But the other party was crossing realms, relying only on his own dharma power.

Meanwhile, Azure Frost could harness the vast origin qi of the Mountains and Seas Realm. With this ebb and flow, she could barely hold him back for a time.

In the void, countless worlds were annihilated and generated, endlessly.

In this repeated attrition, White Tiger True Lord finally lost his patience.

"Beast, you seek death!"

He no longer restrained himself, joining his palms like a blade and slashing distantly towards Azure Frost's position.

What a stunning slash that was; there was nothing else in his gaze.

What a domineering slash that was; countless spaces under that slash were like silk paper, shattering piece by piece.

*Chi!*

Azure Frost grunted, a faint red line appearing on her body.

Then, her entire body split in two along that line, her upper half sliding diagonally downwards.

But the next moment!

Blood qi surged on Azure Frost's body, stopping this slide, and her fleshly body re-coalesced.

Indestructible Body, one of the powerful talents of the demon race.

She coldly looked at that figure.

"You cannot cross over!"

White Tiger True Lord grew even more furious, roaring without cease.

If the distance were not so great, only the killing intent of this divine ability of his could cross realms, how could he have let her calmly re-coalesce her body?

"Old man, I must descend into this realm today!"

White Tiger True Lord roared, expending vast dharma power, trying to forcibly squeeze into this world.

Azure Frost did not dare to be careless. With a clear, piercing cry, she manifested her thousand-zhang true body.

A majestic Azure Luan, wielding flowing-light wings, danced and circled continuously within the pillar of light.

That divine ability of deriving spaces to isolate worlds was continuously released as her wings trembled.

Until!

*Boom!*

A heaven-shaking sound came from below the mountain.

The origin qi vortex caused by Ke Lianshan's Ascendant transformation shrank sharply.

The portal connecting the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven and Heaven-Earth Peak also disappeared in an instant.

In a chain reaction, this pillar of light also began to decay wildly.

White Tiger True Lord sensed something was wrong, roaring incessantly.

"These wastes, wastes!"

But the deafening roars gradually faded, as if separated by two worlds, only scratching the surface.

Azure Frost's form flickered, transforming into human shape, stumbling as she landed on the peak of Heaven-Earth Peak.

She subconsciously looked down the mountain.

"That is..."

In the rapidly shrinking origin qi vortex, a streak of dark light escaped in the nick of time. The familiar aura, the connection of life and death, made Azure Frost recognize the essence of the dark light at a glance.

"Luo Chen!"

After the dark light escaped, without any hesitation, it disappeared into the void.

Azure Frost looked into the distance. On the boundless ocean, a figure was rapidly rushing towards Heaven-Earth Peak.

However, the moment the dark light appeared, that figure suddenly froze in place.

He stiffly turned around, looking towards the previous battlefield, forcing out two words from his mouth: "Luo Chen!"

Divine sense was reborn!

Sword qi reappeared!

A vast divine sense locked tightly onto Liancheng, and then a terrifying sword intent broke through space, enveloping Liancheng.

In his stunned pupils, a beautiful scene was reflected.

Under the night sky, the sword technique soared into the sky, sword qi spraying across the ocean.

Boundless Scarlet Firmament sword qi coalesced into one, flowing in parallel, illuminating the darkness, like a vast, surging river of sword qi scouring all things in the world.

It was precisely Thousand Skies Converge, Qi Rushing the Starry River!

There was no time to react, no space to maneuver. Liancheng's eyes were wide with fury, his hand instinctively reaching forward.

A large umbrella unfurled before him in an instant.

The handle was like bone, the canopy like skin. Countless ferocious skulls loomed faintly on the umbrella, an oppressive aura spreading out.

One of the Dao Sect's three great spirit treasures—the Burial Bone Umbrella!

"Go!"

Countless skulls roared out, fearlessly charging towards the vast river of sword qi.

*Boom!*

The contact between the two was only an instant.

A sky-shaking roar shook the ocean.

The river of sword qi and the army of skulls began a frenzied clash, then annihilated each other.

Until!

*Whoosh!*

A streak of blood light shot out from the river of sword qi, touching the canopy of the Burial Bone Umbrella.

Liancheng stared fixedly at that streak of blood light, recognizing it at a glance as Luo Chen's personal sword, named Yuantu.

The madly spinning sword tip and the unmoving bone umbrella clashed head-on. The piercing screech nearly scattered one's soul.

The next moment!

*Boom!*

The void exploded, flames flickered.

A figure suddenly appeared before Liancheng. He grasped the sword hilt with one hand and pushed heavily forward.

*Rip...*

The sharpest sword tip in the world pierced the tough bone umbrella, advancing all the way, and finally, in Liancheng's frantic gaze, stabbed deeply into his exposed primordial spirit.

Liancheng couldn't believe it, grabbing the scorching hot sword body with one hand, his lips moving.

"How... is it... possible..."

Nothing was impossible.

When the destiny soul returned and the main soul was complete, Luo Chen's primordial spirit had no flaws left. That vast divine sense had already completely locked onto the opponent.

The collision of the final move was the key to turning defeat into victory, and also the fatal blow that broke the camel's back.

Luo Chen held the sword hilt with one hand and pushed horizontally with the other.

The sharp Yuantu sword, with irresistible force, cut through Liancheng's primordial spirit. Then, a blood-colored halo spread, just like when he killed Black Marsh Ancestor back then, completely absorbing the opponent's primordial spirit into the sword.

After doing all this, Luo Chen withdrew his sword, staggering back a few steps in the void.

The pierced bone umbrella fell towards the sea below.

A pure white, slender hand suddenly appeared, catching the bone umbrella, then flew to Luo Chen's side, opening it like a flower bud, while supporting Luo Chen's arm.

Coincidentally, as the great battle ended, the surging origin qi slowly dissipated, and a torrential downpour poured down.

In the rain curtain, Luo Chen gasped for breath, looking at the woman beside him, whose brows were like paintings, her expression like frost.

"I thought you really wouldn't come."

"Your matters are your matters. My task was to prevent the True Lord from the minor realm from descending. That's all."

The woman's face was pale, and even as she spoke, she still had a distant, cold, and indifferent look.

Luo Chen grinned.

This grin seemed to pull at his injuries. The palm print on his chest, clear in every detail, was shocking.

Azure Frost frowned, "Why take such a risk?"

Luo Chen coughed and laughed, "It wasn't really a risk. When the origin qi vortex appeared in the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, what Liancheng sensed was that the Chess Saint was about to break through to the Ascendant stage, while what I sensed was that my destiny soul was already free. Since he wanted to change the battlefield, I might as well go along with it, creating an opportunity for my clone's actions."

Azure Frost shook her head, "I'm asking why you had to clash head-on with him. You and he are ultimately a full major realm apart."

Luo Chen smiled freely, "Some things are known to be impossible, but one must still do them. Isn't it the same for you, forcibly stopping a powerful being a full major realm above you from descending? Aren't you afraid of death?"

Azure Frost fell silent.

Luo Chen said no more, his gaze looking into the distance.

On the Eastern Origin Continent, streaks of escaping light were pulling out.

Compared to the initial number entering, the count had decreased, showing this battle was not easy.

But what Luo Chen was concerned about was not just this.

His divine sense stirred slightly, and Luo Chen raised an eyebrow.

"Li Canghai indeed didn't leave!"

Azure Frost glanced in that direction, a trace of disgust on her face.

Above the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range, a figure suddenly appeared, holding a large seal, facing the mountain range.

A vast suction force, amidst the despair of many Dao Sect disciples, uprooted the massive Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range, pulling it continuously towards the large seal.

No wonder Azure Frost was disgusted.

At the beginning of the great battle, Li Canghai was the first to flee.

After the great battle, he returned to seize the spoils.

Such conduct was naturally hard to look upon.

Suddenly!

Luo Chen released the hand Azure Frost was using to support him, holding his long sword, and looked in one direction.

"Two fellow daoists, why hide your heads and tails? Why not show yourselves!"

Azure Frost looked in Luo Chen's direction in surprise, seeing ripples in the void, and two figures appeared one after the other.

One was majestic like an emperor, the other ethereal as if flying.

"To slay an Ascendant great power at the Sage realm, Alchemy Master Luo Chen is truly astonishing!"

"This praise, I, Luo, deserve." Luo Chen's expression was calm, looking at the two. "May I ask how to address you?"

The majestic one said solemnly, "Sea Emperor Whale!"

The other laughed, "The world calls me Great Sage Beili!"

Luo Chen did not seem surprised. He nodded, then asked, "Choosing to appear after the great battle, I wonder what intentions the two fellow daoists have?"

With this question asked bluntly, the atmosphere immediately became tense.
