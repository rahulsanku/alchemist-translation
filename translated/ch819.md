# Chapter 819: Sweeping Ten Thousand Miles Unopposed, One More Soul Beneath the Sword

Azure Yang Great Handprint!

This spell, for all its many flaws—such as insufficiently concentrated power and vulnerability to water- and earth-attributed treasures—was nonetheless Luo Chen's favorite technique.

In his hands, it could be cast instantaneously. Every hand seal, every incantation, every surge of mana output had become so familiar that he could not be more practiced.

Thus, whenever he encountered an unfamiliar battle, he habitually opened with a Great Handprint to test his opponent's mettle.

Because it was merely a probing move, the spell's full power had rarely been displayed.

Until now. This time, with ample preparation and no interference, Luo Chen had completed his first fully charged strike since reaching the Sage realm!

Even during the mere process of gathering power in the heavens above, its might had already reached the level of a full-force blow from a mid-Sage cultivator.

That was why it had drawn Golden Feather's sidelong glance, and why the Seven Rhinoceros Commander had been both startled and enraged.

Now!

Luo Chen released the fully charged Azure Yang Great Handprint. Its effect of enslaving the free-floating spiritual energy of heaven and earth for his own use was now fully on display.

Descending from the nine heavens, it entered the vast domain.

Wherever it passed, spiritual energy merged into it.

The cyan handprint swept forward relentlessly. Only the sound of golden drums thundering, deafening to the ears, could be heard. As far as the eye could see, mountain ranges collapsed and monstrous waves crashed against the shores.

Within the palm print, surging mana and spiritual energy combined, no longer bound by Luo Chen's control. Like a runaway steed or a burst dam, it surged forward with overwhelming momentum, showing no sign of stopping.

Nothing could stand in its way. Nothing could block its path.

The battle formation where the two great demon emperors had surrounded Golden Feather now stood in the path of this giant handprint's charge. But as the great handprint roared past, they were forced to yield.

They followed the handprint eastward in shock.

Along the way, great mountains turned to dust, great rivers were severed midstream, and countless demon beast armies blocking the path exploded into clouds of blood mist with a single *bang*.

*Rumble!!!*

The rumbling sound echoed for an unknown duration.

The cyan handprint had charged for who knows how many tens of thousands of li.

Its vast momentum was like a natural disaster born of heaven and earth itself, showing almost no sign of stopping.

Until a blood-colored light curtain spread out.

It was the All-Heaven Twelve Fiend God Grand Formation that the Emerald Sky City Lord had previously set up!

Once this formation was deployed, aside from the Seven Rhinoceros Commander—a great demon emperor comparable to a late-Sage cultivator—who had forcibly broken out, no one else had yet managed to escape.

In everyone's line of sight, they saw the thousand-zhang-tall handprint, as high as the heavens themselves, slam heavily into the blood-colored light curtain.

*Hum…*

A strange sound rang out. The light curtain trembled without cease.

It seemed even the grand formation could barely withstand the force of that palm strike.

*Whoosh! Whoosh! Whoosh!*

Three of the nearby All-Heaven formation flags suddenly unfurled.

With the added force, the Azure Yang Great Handprint could no longer sustain itself.

With a single explosive sound, the great handprint collapsed and shattered!

Countless foreign objects swept along—mountain rocks, river water, trees, demon beasts—now fell like a curtain of rain.

It was precisely through the force of this collapse that residual palm power managed to forcibly breach the formation's light curtain and fly out.

*Boom!*

Beyond the range visible to the naked eye, having already crossed the boundary of Overlord Blade Domain, it entered the neighboring Azure Sky Domain.

A great mountain that had stood for tens of millions of years met the remaining palm force head-on.

Five enormous finger marks were deeply imprinted on the mountain wall.

Within Overlord Blade Domain.

Above the massive canyon that had once been carved out by a single sword strike, Luo Chen stood on a mountain rock, letting the fierce wind whip his robes noisily.

His previously calm expression had now turned subtle.

A fully charged Azure Yang Great Handprint—one that no one had forcibly interrupted—could actually increase in power to such an extent!

The might of that single palm strike had, in the end, temporarily broken through the All-Heaven Twelve Fiend God Grand Formation.

This meant that, at that moment, the Azure Yang Great Handprint had reached a level comparable to a full-force blow from a late-Sage great cultivator!

This was an unexpected delight.

Even Luo Chen had not anticipated it.

Fusion spells were powerful—he had known that since his lower cultivation levels—but to reach such a shocking degree was truly extraordinary.

Of course, Luo Chen had also discovered the spell's flaw.

After unrestrainedly enslaving a vast amount of heaven and earth's spiritual energy, the proportion of his own mana within the spell grew smaller and smaller. In the end, even he, the caster, could no longer control it.

He could only let it surge forward.

"Although this spell's power is great, I must not become greedy for it in the future. After all, it has too many flaws."

"But for the present moment, establishing authority and intimidation is more than enough!"

He gazed across thousands of li. Wherever the handprint had passed, it had forcibly carved a massive trench into the earth—a hundred zhang deep, several hundred zhang wide, and of unknown length. Within it were countless pools of blood, all from demon beasts.

Now, as the blood flowed sluggishly, it seemed to converge into a river of blood.

With this single move, he had slaughtered over a million low-rank demon beasts!

He then swept his gaze around. The battles between Sage-level powerhouses had all instinctively moved away from him. Presumably, they were wary of the might of that palm strike and did not wish to be casually caught in its range.

"So, who is my opponent?"

Luo Chen's sharp gaze swept across each battle group one by one.

The one with the most participants was undoubtedly Iron View Daoist's location.

He stood atop a massive daoist temple made of profound iron, wielding an iron ruler as he fought fiercely against a bare-armed giant.

On the ground, countless demon beasts—too numerous to count—surged toward him from all directions.

Within the daoist temple, two stone statues had somehow transformed into guardian spirit beasts.

A turtle and a serpent firmly protected the area where Iron View Daoist stood.

"It looks intense, but Iron View Daoist appears completely at ease. It shouldn't be a problem."

Next was the most dazzling and eye-catching battle group.

In the sky, strips of nine-colored light bands had formed a massive river, isolating the inside from the outside.

Within it, thunder and lightning crackled without cease, and roars could be heard from time to time.

Even a pair of sky-obscuring black wings tried to spread out, only to be pulled back into the river by the nine-colored light bands.

Luo Chen saw it clearly. That nine-colored light river was nothing but the purest manifestation of mana!

"I never imagined that the great technique cultivated by Nine Spirits Primordial Lord could possess such overwhelming might. Even the Thunder Prison Roc, who once fought the old thief Divine Origin to a standstill, is trapped within and has fallen into a disadvantage."

"No wonder he was so desperate for that Ninefold Separation Pill. Such a great technique—though perhaps inferior to divine abilities in subtlety—already has an unmatched sense of power when it comes to might!"

"I wonder if, after I cultivate a hundred strands of Scarlet Firmament Sword Qi, I will have such momentum?"

Murmuring to himself, Luo Chen looked toward the most dangerous battle group.

Unlike Iron View Daoist's ease and Nine Spirits Primordial Lord's overwhelming advantage, this one was deadly with every move and perilous at every turn.

Golden Feather True Person, one against two!

The legendary three fundamental golden feathers had already drawn two.

Facing the pincer attack of two great demon emperors, Golden Feather True Person wielded the feathers in his hands, and ten thousand golden lights made it impossible for anyone to look directly at him.

The two great demon emperors fought with reckless abandon, forcing Golden Feather True Person into a difficult position. Even a metal-attributed shield he used for defense had been dented.

"Looking at the battle situation, I should go help him."

"But…"

Luo Chen's gaze swept across the other two battlefields.

Sect Master Li, with his single sword, was pursuing a fourth-rank initial-stage demon emperor.

Elder Fu sat cross-legged in the void, casting talisman after talisman, but pain was already showing on his face.

Faintly, the sound of cicadas could be heard, vexing the heart.

"Compared to Golden Feather, it would be better to help Elder Fu!"

Just as Luo Chen's thoughts turned and he was about to make his move, he suddenly looked down at his feet.

*Sssss…*

The originally hard mountain rock was melting in a bizarre manner.

A foul, rancid smell assailed his nostrils.

"Poison!"

Suddenly, the sky darkened, and night fell.

Luo Chen lost his footing and fell into the great canyon.

This scene was remarkably similar to the methods of a certain type of demon beast he had heard of.

A No-Light Beast?

Luo Chen smiled faintly. His right hand trembled, and the blood sword fell into his grasp.

"I heard that Fellow Daoist survived a strike from the Emerald Sky City Lord's Four Directions Mystic Sword, which shows your strength is no small matter. Now you seek me out—it seems you are quite confident!"

As he spoke, he spread his mana shield.

The Mystic Dust Armor moved with his intent, covering his entire body, leaving only a pair of eyes gleaming with spiritual light visible.

*Rustle, rustle, rustle…*

In the darkness, faint sounds arose.

Then came a *puff, puff* sound.

Luo Chen's mana shield seemed to be attacked by something, instantly riddled with holes.

Luo Chen looked closely. They were weapons like flying needles.

And each one glowed with an eerie blue-black color, clearly coated with some potent toxin.

Without a second thought, he placed a Purple Qi Poison-Resisting Pill in his mouth, just in case.

The flying needles pierced through his mana shield and struck Luo Chen's body.

Then came a clattering sound.

A true artifact-grade protective armor was not so easily breached!

From the darkness came a soft sound of surprise.

Then, a gentle female voice rang out.

"I have heard of the Alchemy Sect's reputation, young lady. After witnessing the might of that palm strike earlier, No-Light was filled with fear. With my abilities, let alone killing you, I doubt I could last even thirty or fifty exchanges in a direct battle."

Luo Chen laughed heartily. "If that's the case, why expend all your mana to trap me here?"

No-Light sighed faintly. "I am merely doing my best. In today's battle, you clearly came prepared. We are already trapped like turtles in a jar. Looking at your cultivation levels, each of you is clearly a cut above us. It is obvious you intend to kill us all. No-Light is merely putting up a desperate struggle!"

Luo Chen, holding the blood sword, began to fly rapidly through the rain of flying needles that continuously fell from the darkness.

But no matter how he flew, he remained shrouded in darkness.

This phenomenon was too bizarre.

He did not know when the other party had set up this technique, causing Luo Chen to fall into the trap without realizing it.

But on the surface, Luo Chen showed no panic, continuing to converse with the other party.

"Fellow Daoist No-Light faces a death tribulation. Why not surrender on the spot? This sect has a love for life. If you are willing to submit, I can accept you as my mount. In the future, when I ascend to Ascendant, you will not be left behind."

"This…"

In the darkness, No-Light fell into hesitation and delay.

Even the attack of the flying needles slowed considerably.

"No." Luo Chen shook his head, raising the blood sword slightly. "You are stalling for time!"

"Heh, the Alchemy Sect is indeed no ordinary mortal. You saw through No-Light's little trick in an instant."

No-Light's soft laughter rang out. Though not loud, it caused the black curtain to tremble continuously.

"This perilous situation today is not unbreakable!"

"How do you break it?"

"If the Seven Rhinoceros Commander kills the Emerald Sky City Lord, it can be broken. The grand formation suppressing Overlord Blade Domain cannot last forever. Once it fails, we can escape far away—that is another way to break this situation. Finally, do you truly believe that the Yellow Barbarian Tribe will sit idly by while we are annihilated?"

"So, in the end, your life and death are entrusted to others?"

"Hmph! Do you truly think this emperor fears you?"

The black curtain suddenly stopped trembling, and a sharp, urgent voice rang out.

A sense of danger arose spontaneously in Luo Chen's heart.

Without thinking, he pushed out a palm.

Another Azure Yang Great Handprint!

Seeing this handprint, No-Light in the shadows could not help but shrink her pupils. But under her own attack, the handprint was instantly broken.

So it was just a showpiece?

Then, how would this famous Alchemy Sect resist the full-force blow she had secretly prepared earlier?

*Ssshh!*

The Azure Yang Great Handprint was broken.

The Sevenfold Blazing Heaven Rings were broken again!

Even the external qi armor of the Mystic Dust Armor did not hold for a moment!

With his naked eye, Luo Chen finally saw the object of the sneak attack.

It was a curved, sharp stinger, much like a scorpion's tail.

This stinger radiated a chilling light, dazzling even in the darkness. It was now no more than three feet from Luo Chen's body.

Luo Chen's eyes narrowed. In No-Light's incredulous gaze, he suddenly reached out and grabbed.

*Slap!*

The stinger was caught in his palm, wrapped in the Mystic Dust Armor. The true artifact-grade armor began to melt rapidly.

At that very moment, Luo Chen's eyes snapped wide open. Spiritual light burst from his pupils like two beams of light, sweeping through the endless darkness.

"Got you!"

With a single stride, Luo Chen moved!

*Boom!*

The void exploded. He flashed to a spot of nothingness, and the blood sword in his hand slashed down.

*Riiip…*

The dark curtain was torn apart with a ripping sound.

A delicate figure flew backward, crashing among the jagged mountain rocks. There, she transformed into her ferocious true body and attempted to flee in panic.

Decisive!

Looking at the bizarre demon beast—its upper body like a bat, its lower body like a scorpion—Luo Chen grinned fiercely.

"You're not getting away!"

He stamped his foot. In the canyon, a small cauldron spun rapidly, beginning to expand continuously.

You, No-Light Beast, could stall for time and secretly gather a full-force blow, hoping to achieve a decisive victory.

But he, Luo Chen, was no fledgling just starting out. He had long since quietly released the Primordial Unity Cauldron, covering this entire battlefield!

Nine crimson chains shot up from the canyon, binding toward the No-Light Beast's true body.

The ferocious demon beast charged left and right, trying to escape the chains' capture.

Within the canyon, another force of suppression, sharing the same origin, slowed her movements to a crawl.

*Whoosh! Whoosh! Whoosh…*

Nine times in succession. The chains formed by the Primordial Unity Cauldron bound her tightly. Then, with mana added, they dragged her back toward Luo Chen.

The No-Light Beast looked at the white-robed Daoist flying toward her through the air, blood sword in hand, her eyes filled with terror.

"Fellow Daoist, I am willing to surrender…"

"Too late!"

With a flash, the blood sword left his hand and stabbed straight into the center of the No-Light Beast's chest.

In an instant, her Purple Mansion was broken, and her Sage was firmly pinned by the great sword.

The No-Light Beast's eyes filled with venom. "If I had not been severely wounded in the battle at Emerald Sky Pass, how could you have so easily found my true body? Since you refuse to accept my surrender, then die with me!"

Her abdomen swelled, and her Sage began to emit intense spiritual light.

"Trying to self-detonate your Sage?"

Luo Chen smiled contemptuously. His right hand formed a spirit seal.

The crimson chains originally binding the No-Light Beast's true body instantly followed the gap in her Purple Mansion pierced by the Yuantu Sword, rushing inside and directly stabbing into the Sage.

Thus, the surging mana, like a serpent whose seven inches had been clamped, could no longer be released.

Only at this moment did the demon beast completely lose the ability to resist.

If this scene were to spread, it would surely cause an uproar.

Between Sage-level powerhouses, determining victory was easy. Even deciding life and death was somewhat possible. But to be able to capture a peer powerhouse alive like Luo Chen had done was simply too shocking.

Luo Chen flew over, looking at the terrified No-Light Beast, and nodded slightly.

Such a demon beast might be mediocre in direct combat, but as an auxiliary, capable of spreading a black curtain in battle to confuse the enemy's vision, it would be extremely useful.

If it weren't for his eyes—nourished since childhood with secret medicines, and having studied a great deal of illusion and soul arts—an ordinary person would not have been able to see through her concealment technique.

"Now, are you willing to submit to this sect?"

The No-Light Beast looked utterly despairing. She glanced up at the nine heavens and closed her eyes helplessly.

"I am willing… *Ahhh!*"

Before the words "I am willing" were fully spoken, a piercing scream rang out.

Luo Chen's expression changed drastically. He instantly realized what was happening and moved to recall the blood-colored great sword stuck in her chest.

But a will of resistance transmitted from the sword body.

Luo Chen hesitated.

It was the will of the Yuantu Sword Spirit sealed in the third layer.

Given the degree to which he had refined this sword, if he wished, he could indeed forcibly recall the Yuantu Sword.

But between subduing a demon beast and gaining the recognition of the Yuantu Sword Spirit, Luo Chen ultimately chose the latter.

"Forget it!"

Luo Chen sighed. The No-Light Beast's sharp, piercing cries gradually weakened in his ears. He watched helplessly as her Sage, and even all her blood, were completely devoured by the Yuantu Sword.

When the No-Light Beast's lifeless body fell into the canyon,

the Yuantu Sword, having drunk its fill of demon blood, shot into the sky, flew before Luo Chen, and trembled incessantly.

This scene reminded him of a memory deep in his mind.

Back then, in the ruins of Coldlight Island, Blood Nightmare Moluo had used this sword to wound his sturdy flesh, drawing quite a bit of blood.

At that time, Blood Nightmare Moluo had said, "You really like his blood!"

Luo Chen had only taken it as a casual remark made during battle, using frightening words to break the enemy's will to fight.

Now, it seemed this sword really did enjoy drinking blood?

A sudden thought struck Luo Chen:

"All along, I have been unable to refine the sword spirit. Even persisting in cultivating the *Thousand Skies Sword Canon* has only deepened my connection with the sword spirit slightly… If I use my own essence blood to feed the sword spirit, would it be willing to serve me completely?"

As if sensing Luo Chen's thought, the Yuantu Sword trembled even more violently. The sword tip deliberately aimed at Luo Chen's heart.

The grooves on the sword body flickered with an eerie blood light.

Looking at the sword tip pointed at him, a flash of anger crossed Luo Chen's face.

"Bastard!"

The thought of feeding the sword spirit with his essence blood was immediately dismissed.

A cultivator's essence blood was the crystallization of their entire cultivation.

Even though Luo Chen also cultivated the archaic body-refining path, possessing several times the essence blood of an ordinary cultivator, it was not something he could casually feed to a sword spirit.

Especially in the current situation—how could he possibly regress instead of advance?

Gripping the sword hilt, Luo Chen rose from the depths of the canyon. His gaze swept across the various battle groups locked in fierce combat, and the countless demon beast armies.

"Although I don't know why the Yuantu Sword Spirit is so bloodthirsty, at least I have found a general direction for refining this sword spirit."

"My essence blood is precious and cannot be easily sacrificed."

"But what about these beasts?"

A surging killing intent, centered on Luo Chen, began to spread in all directions across the battlefield!
