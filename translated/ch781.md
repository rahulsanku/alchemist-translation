# Chapter 781: Their Desire for My Body Never Dies!

Outside the hall, the sunlight blazed fiercely. Inside, the figures were sparse.

But though the number of people was small, the auras they subtly emanated were like vast oceans, making it difficult for anyone to breathe.

Luo Chen swept his gaze over them only once, then revealed a smile and strode boldly inside.

He felt nothing in the face of that surging pressure.

Because he, too, was a Sage now—no longer the junior who had to tread carefully and fear saying the wrong word in front of Nascent Soul cultivators.

“Fellow Daoist Luo Chen, please take a seat!”

The City Lord of Lingtian pointed with his hand, making an inviting gesture.

And the position he indicated was none other than the left-hand seat of honor, the first seat below his own.

Such high regard drew sidelong glances.

But no one questioned it. The way Luo Chen had instantly refined Old Monster Motian’s lifebound true artifact earlier was more than enough to earn him such a seat.

Luo Chen sat down without any hesitation.

The atmosphere in the hall eased slightly after he took his seat, and with a hearty laugh from the Lingtian City Lord, it relaxed even further.

“Fellow Daoist Luo, you have just arrived… no, returned to the Eastern Wilderness not long ago, so you must be unfamiliar with the people and affairs here. If you don’t mind, this City Lord would be happy to introduce you!”

Luo Chen had already learned from Sage Xianhe that the Lingtian City Lord was a chivalrous figure with a warm temperament and a wide circle of friends.

It was precisely because of this personality that the Nether Origin Sect had appointed him as the City Lord of Lingtian Pass, placing him in command of the assembled heroes.

Since the host was willing to make introductions for him, Luo Chen naturally would not refuse.

“I’ll trouble the City Lord, then.”

“Haha!”

The Lingtian City Lord laughed heartily, then pointed his right hand at an elderly man in his eighties or nineties.

“This is Elder Fu, the founding ancestor of the Divine Talisman Sect. You should have heard of his great name.”

*The former master of the Divine Talisman Domain, one of the Extreme Six Domains?*

Luo Chen clasped his fists. “Elder Fu’s name was already thundering in my ears back when I was still a wandering cultivator.”

Elder Fu had been staring at Luo Chen with a hint of curiosity. Hearing Luo Chen’s flattery, he couldn’t help but shake his head with a light laugh.

“I’m merely a few hundred years older. But you, Sage Luo… your fame spread barely a century ago, and you were only at the Initiate stage back then!”

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow. “Elder Fu has heard my story?”

“The number one Initiate of the Jade Cauldron Domain—I’ve heard a little. Now that I see you in person, you surpass your reputation. To reach the Sage realm at the age of two hundred—your genius is rarely seen in this world!”

At these words, the faces of everyone in the hall changed.

Gazes couldn’t help but fix on Luo Chen’s incredibly youthful appearance.

Yes!

Though Luo Chen’s eyes were deep and his bearing weathered, just like theirs, his excessively young face and sharp, aggressive aura still set him apart from everyone present.

Such a demeanor could only belong to the legendary peerless prodigies!

Luo Chen accepted everyone’s scrutiny openly and generously.

Ever since he had decided to return to the Eastern Wilderness, he had prepared himself on all fronts.

He could have used an aged appearance to conceal certain things.

But there was no need!

If anyone with a discerning mind dug into it, they would always find out how many years it had taken him to achieve the Great Dao of Sagehood. Trying to cover it up would only make him seem petty.

On the contrary, the more outstanding he appeared, the more it would make certain lurking villains think twice before acting.

“It’s just that I survived a great calamity and was blessed with extraordinary fortune afterward!”

Luo Chen said this, both as a modest remark and as a subtle hint at something.

Sure enough!

Old Monster Motian, his face pale, couldn’t hold back and asked eagerly, “How did you survive Green Parasol Mountain and escape from it?”

Luo Chen glanced at him but offered no explanation.

Everyone assumed he was still angry about Motian’s earlier underhanded dealings against Wang Yuan, and thus unwilling to answer.

The Lingtian City Lord took the opportunity to introduce the others.

“The Great Palace Master of Fenghua Palace, Fairy Fenghua!”

Fairy Fenghua’s original name was no longer known to the world. Ever since she became the supreme elder of Fenghua Palace, she had inherited the Dao name “Fenghua.”

As she was about to say something under Luo Chen’s gaze, she saw him stand up first and bow to her solemnly.

“Sage Luo, what is this…”

“Luo thanks the Fairy for looking after the cultivators of the Luo Tian Sect while I was away.”

Grievances were grievances, kindness was kindness—Luo Chen had always kept them clear.

Even under the watchful eyes of the crowd, he did not feel belittled by offering thanks and a bow.

The others also noticed that beneath his sharp, ruthless exterior, Luo Chen was not without a sense of propriety.

Seeing this, the Lingtian City Lord was even more pleased.

“At Lingtian Pass, we normally have five Sages stationed here. When an enemy attack comes, we can summon fellow Daoists from the five domains, sometimes reaching over a dozen. Unfortunately, many of our comrades are not here today, or I would have introduced them all to you.”

“This one here, like you, Fellow Daoist Luo, is a rare cultivation prodigy from the Eastern Wilderness. He formed his Sage Embryo before the age of three hundred and has set foot on the supreme path of the sword.”

Just as the Lingtian City Lord pointed to a gloomy-looking middle-aged man who seemed like a dust-covered sword, Luo Chen smiled.

Even though his bearing had changed greatly, his features still held a hint of familiarity.

“Fellow Daoist Lin, it’s been a while!”

Lin Qingxuan—the former number one Initiate of the Jade Cauldron Domain, who had long held the top spot on the Jade Cauldron Prodigy List for the Initiate stage.

Luo Chen had only gotten to know him during the Expansion War, after he himself had formed his Adept core.

But after Luo Chen left for the Great Snowy Mountains, the two had never seen each other again.

A century later, the Jade Cauldron Domain had fallen, and the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect had splintered.

Lin Qingxuan had followed the surviving disciples, drifting from place to place, struggling to survive.

Perhaps having witnessed the constant loss of those around him, his sword heart had become even purer, and his progress in the sword dao had been swift.

By now, after countless battles against both the demon race and humans, he had earned the title “Lone Swan of the Sword Realm”!

Facing Luo Chen’s greeting, his expression was complex, tinged with curiosity.

“Fellow Daoist Luo, a century apart, and you have truly made people see you in a new light!”

Luo Chen smiled. “The name ‘Lone Swan of the Sword Realm’ reached my ears as soon as I arrived in the White Crane Domain. You’re not far behind, Fellow Daoist Lin.”

*The White Crane Domain!*

Lin Qingxuan’s expression shifted slightly. He took a deep breath and stared straight at Luo Chen.

“Regarding the battle at the Great Snowy Mountains back then, what really happened? I ask you to enlighten me!”

His sword sect’s only Sage—Sage Jade Cauldron—had been chased eighty thousand li from the Great Snowy Mountains by the Demon Emperor Azure Frost, and finally slain at the foot of Mount Accumulated Thunder.

It was from that moment that the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect began its irreversible decline.

Faced with this question, Luo Chen smoothed his robes and sat down directly.

“The events of that year are like a tangled mess—impossible to sort out or explain clearly. Though I have some knowledge, limited by my cultivation level at the time, what I saw and understood was only the tip of the iceberg. How could I possibly enlighten you?”

Lin Qingxuan frowned deeply. He could hear the dismissiveness in Luo Chen’s words.

*Did he really not know?*

*Or was it because he had sent his disciple Hong Ling to participate in the internal strife of the Luo Tian Sect, and Luo Chen was displeased, deliberately holding back?*

But now, whether Luo Chen spoke or not was not entirely up to him.

He looked at the Lingtian City Lord.

The Lingtian City Lord, however, seemed not to notice his gaze and instead asked Luo Chen, “The remaining two fellow Daoists—I assume no introduction is needed?”

The remaining two were Sage Divine Fire and Old Monster Motian.

Luo Chen nodded.

He didn’t need an introduction, but it was clear those two would not let the matter rest easily.

Old Monster Motian said hatefully, “Today, Luo Chen broke the rules by force, storming the Combat Arena. Not only did he ruin the Ten Duel, but he also killed Yin Sixiang and destroyed my lifebound true artifact, which I had spent centuries refining. I ask the Lingtian City Lord to judge this matter and demand an explanation from Luo Chen!”

“I haven’t held you accountable for stirring up discord and disrupting my sect, yet you have the audacity to accuse me first.” Luo Chen’s eyes were sharp with killing intent as he stared at the old monster. “Besides, it was you who broke the rules first!”

Old Monster Motian slammed the table directly. “Full of sophistry and glib talk! Your Luo Tian disciples were deeply favored by the Five Elements Divine Sect, sheltered by them for thirty years. Yet later, they repaid kindness with ingratitude and defected to Fenghua Palace. No matter where you take this matter, you can’t justify it!”

Luo Chen retorted without backing down, “And what does that have to do with your Motian Cliff?”

“You!”

Old Monster Motian pointed at Luo Chen with his right hand, trembling almost uncontrollably. His lips quivered, and his beard shook.

His eyes reddened as he looked at the red-robed old man beside him.

“Divine Fire, say something!”

Such a loss of composure was unbecoming of a Sage!

But everyone thought of his destroyed lifebound true artifact and the damage to his soul from the backlash, so they couldn’t bring themselves to scorn him.

Moreover, this small gathering at the City Lord’s mansion today—aside from Elder Fu, the Great Palace Master, and others who wanted to take the opportunity to get to know Luo Chen—had the main purpose of resolving the “Ten Duel” incident.

Faced with his ally’s angry words, Sage Divine Fire, who had earlier been furious over the death of his proud disciple, was now eerily calm.

“Sixiang’s defeat was due to his bad luck.”

“The Luo Tian Sect’s betrayal was just every man for himself in the face of disaster.”

“But all of this has left our Divine Sect’s face utterly lost. I am powerless to decide this grievance, but our sect’s founding ancestor will surely seek out Fellow Daoist Luo in person to demand an explanation!”

When Sage Divine Fire finished speaking, Old Monster Motian listened with growing disbelief.

But when the last sentence was uttered, a look of wild joy appeared on his face.

The others also became solemn.

The Divine Origin Ancestor of the Five Elements Divine Sect—that was a late-stage Sage!

If he personally stepped forward, no matter how arrogantly Luo Chen had behaved earlier, in the face of such a vast gap in cultivation level, he would be nothing more than a clown jumping about.

In response,

Luo Chen stood up with a loud laugh.

“Haha! I’ll be waiting for your founding ancestor to come find me! I have matters to attend to now, so I won’t waste time chatting with you all.”

With that, he made a slight bow to the Lingtian City Lord, Elder Fu, and the others, then strode toward the door.

Throughout this process, no one stopped him.

The Lingtian City Lord, in particular, watched him with an appreciative look.

Just as the man reached the doorway, he turned back.

“Once I’ve sorted out the trivial matters within my sect, I will host a banquet. I hope you will all do me the honor of coming for a cup of wine.”

*Creak…*

The doors opened, and sunlight streamed in once more.

The man returned to the broad daylight, walking away openly and honestly.

Only the people in the hall were left exchanging glances.

The Lingtian City Lord, a smile on his face, replied,

“We will certainly attend!”

---

At the western city gate of Lingtian Pass, a cloud carriage floated up into the sky after passing inspection.

“Long Yu, I’ll leave the steering to you.”

“Mm-hmm! Most of the formations on this cloud carriage are ones I’ve never seen before. They don’t seem to be in the Eastern Wilderness style!”

Min Longyu was delighted by the sight and couldn’t resist starting to explore the unfamiliar formations on the Sixfold Imperial Flying Cloud Carriage.

Though he was even more thrilled by Luo Chen’s return, he understood that now was not the time for him to catch up, so he had to divert his attention.

Before leaving, Luo Chen suddenly called out to him.

“You…”

He pointed at his own head.

Min Longyu subconsciously touched his hair—a head of silver-white, dry and withered.

For a cultivator who hadn’t reached old age, this was extremely rare.

Min Longyu wasn’t very old—just over two hundred and sixty, at the early Adept stage.

What’s more, the Luo Tian Sect had always had a tradition of refining Appearance-Fixing Pills, so he shouldn’t have looked so aged.

Min Longyu smiled freely. “I got too absorbed in studying formations and ended up like this by accident. It’s nothing serious.”

With that, he walked off on his own.

Watching his back, Luo Chen opened his mouth as if to speak, then stopped.

*How much mental energy must one expend to turn a cultivator who has taken an Appearance-Fixing Pill white-haired and haggard-looking?*

He sighed, walked to the railing, and gazed at the scenery rushing past below, his mind churning with thoughts.

*“According to Zhaowu of the White Crane Sect, Lin Qingxuan, with the help of the Ten Thousand Sword Pavilion, is trying to usurp the White Crane Domain. But limited by the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect’s own weak strength, and the Ten Thousand Sword Pavilion’s inability to send people openly, he has allied with the Five Elements Divine Sect, who were also part of the Extreme Six Domains back then. In exchange, he sent sword sect cultivators to participate in the Ten Duel.”*

*“The Lone Swan of the Sword Realm is not the main enemy!”*

*“Old Monster Motian’s true artifact was destroyed, so he will surely hold a grudge against me. But he was already an open enemy. Since I’ve offended him, I might as well offend him thoroughly. I don’t regret using the Withering-Bloom True Flame to destroy his artifact. In fact, he should be glad I didn’t kill him outright!”*

*“The Five Elements Divine Sect was willing to shelter the Luo Tian Sect for decades?”*

*“And just now, Divine Fire deliberately didn’t pursue my arrogant behavior—he must have an ulterior motive.”*

*“Combined with what Deputy Hall Master Youquan of the Truth Crossing Hall said back then at Green Parasol Mountain—after she soul-searched Jin Buque, she learned that the old monster of the Five Elements Divine Sect was trying to use my Five-Spirit Root constitution to reverse-refine a Primal Embryo Dao Body, in order to advance to the Ascendant stage.”*

*“Now it seems… their desire for my body never dies!”*

Luo Chen’s eyes narrowed slightly.

If there were two things he worried about most upon returning to the Eastern Wilderness, they were:

First, the pursuit of the Eastern Wilderness’s holy land, the Nether Origin Sect.

Second, that undying old monster of the Five Elements Divine Sect at the late Sage stage.

For the former, he was already prepared, waiting at any time for the holy land’s envoy to arrive.

As for the latter…

He had coveted Luo Chen back then, later formed a three-man team of late-stage Sages to attack Green Parasol Mountain, and even after Luo Chen disappeared, he kept the Luo Tian Sect under his control, waiting for Luo Chen to come to him willingly.

Now, Sage Divine Fire’s ambiguous attitude said it all.

*“Perhaps I am still no match for a Great Cultivator at this moment. But as long as my preparations are thorough, he won’t dare to make a move against me openly.”*

*“And if he comes in secret… then I’d like to see whether the Divine Sect’s secret arts are more formidable, or my Withering-Bloom Divine Fire!”*

Suddenly, Luo Chen’s narrowed eyes relaxed, and a hint of tenderness appeared in them.

A graceful figure walked out from inside the carriage.

“Won’t you see him?”

“No. Take him back and let Huiniang handle it.”

“Huiniang, she…” The woman sighed softly and leaned gently against Luo Chen’s shoulder. “Have you been well these past hundred years?”

Luo Chen rubbed the woman’s head, the faint fragrance of her hair drifting to his nose.

“Now that I’m back, everything is fine.”

The cloud carriage rode the clouds and crossed the mist, gliding over thousands of mountains and rivers.

Two figures stood upon it, leaning on each other, as close and intimate as they had been all those years ago.

(End of chapter)
