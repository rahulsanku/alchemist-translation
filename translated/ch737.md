# Chapter 737: Ghosts and Gods Give Way, Blood Dyes Central Continent

Hum...

Along with the rolling waves, the dense spiritual light aboard the Sword-Halberd gradually went out.

The giant ship docked at a harbor.

One cultivator after another walked down from the ship in orderly fashion, and under the guidance of Tianyuan Merchant Alliance cultivators, they successively boarded a large island.

After half a year, Sun-Moon Island had arrived.

This island was still within the Northern Sea's range, but it was already extremely far from the human heartland.

Human figures moved across the island, shouts rang out loudly, and as people came and went, all kinds of large materials unsuitable for storage bags were moved into warehouses prepared in advance.

Clearly, Sun-Moon Island was a transfer center of the Tianyuan Merchant Alliance in the Northern Sea, located between the Northern Sea and Central Continent.

A Sage of the Tianyuan Dao Sect was stationed on the island year-round.

Yes, of the Dao Sect, not of the merchant alliance.

The two seemed to be divided very clearly!

At this moment, that rather young-looking Dao Sect Sage was standing on the ship with Fu Chaosheng, watching the weaving crowd while chatting.

"Recommending seven Sages to join Star Gate in one go. Senior Brother Fu, your merit this time is considerable!"

"Not at all. Of these seven, only two were recommended by me. The remaining five were recommended by other merchant alliance elders."

"In the end, it is still the merit of the Tianyuan Merchant Alliance's Northern Sea branch. Senior Brother is too modest." The young man smiled, then his expression turned solemn. "Senior Brother Fu, are you certain you want to use this island's teleportation formation? By comparison, the teleportation formation in Divine Origin City should be more suitable!"

Fu Chaosheng shook his head. "Divine Origin City's teleportation formation is good, capable of cross-continent teleportation. But the spirit stone consumption is enormous, and its recovery time is extremely long. Without my knowledge, Elder Shen activated it early and already sent someone away."

"Who?" The young man was surprised. "Who has such qualifications that Senior Brother Shen would use his authority to forcibly activate the cross-continent teleportation?"

"That one from the Liu family," Fu Chaosheng said faintly.

The young man suddenly understood. "So it was Junior Brother Liu Yuan. I hear he traveled ten thousand li, gave up Central Continent's excellent cultivation environment, and deliberately went to the Northern Sea to temper himself, then succeeded in one stroke and broke through to the Sage stage. Looking at it now, his performance after breaking through should be quite impressive, for Senior Brother Shen to make an exception for him."

When he spoke to this point, he suddenly realized something.

"The Shen family has no suitable clansman in this generation to participate in the five-hundred-year grand competition. Could Senior Brother Shen have taken a liking to Junior Brother Liu Yuan?"

Fu Chaosheng smiled with some effort and did not say much.

Although they were both in the Northern Sea, he had spent his years walking the southeastern region and had not interacted much with Liu Yuan.

Shen Wandou was different. He had always been stationed in Divine Origin City and was very close to Liu Yuan, who was tempering himself in the Myriad Immortals Assembly. The two had interacted privately with extreme frequency.

Without anyone realizing it, Shen Wandou had already drawn the lesser-house genius Liu Yuan to his side.

Not only had he placed heavy stakes on Liu Yuan and gifted all kinds of resources, forcibly activating the cross-continent teleportation formation as an exception was enough to make anyone feel grateful.

Especially before Fu Chaosheng returned, Shen Wandou had once probed Luo Chen's ability. This was clearly also testing the foundation of Liu Yuan's future opponent.

The young man glanced at Fu Chaosheng and roughly understood why the other party had such an expression.

The Fu family's favored daughter Fu Qinglan was said to have already signed up for this five-hundred-year grand competition, intending to glimpse the direction of the Great Dao inside Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven.

In that case, Liu Yuan was bound to become Fu Qinglan's opponent.

Compared to Liu Yuan, who had formed his Nascent Soul long ago and was not far from the hundred-year limit, Fu Qinglan, who had formed her Nascent Soul only a few years ago, was far too lacking.

Especially the two people's battle experience against enemies. That difference was even...

The young man smiled in tacit understanding and did not continue on this topic.

He patted the railing on the ship and smiled. "The teleportation formation on Sun-Moon Island is usually used to transport dead resources and is not very suitable for transporting people. Therefore, anyone who wants to enter the formation must have a certain realm before they can withstand that enormous spatial pressure. Senior Brother Fu should understand this point."

Fu Chaosheng made a sound of acknowledgment and handed over a list.

The young man accepted it and examined it carefully.

There were not many people on the list, only a dozen or so, and most were Adepts and Sages.

But when he reached the end, he could not help frowning.

"Why are there two demon beasts in here?"

"And this Initiate junior. How can she withstand the enormous pressure of the teleportation formation?"

"They are all under this Wilderness Wanderer Luo Chen. Who is he?"

Faced with this string of questions, Fu Chaosheng did not panic and instead smiled. "Wilderness Wanderer is a newly risen expert of the Northern Sea. Although he is a rogue cultivator, his abilities are absolutely no weaker than those of us holy land disciples. As it happens, the teleportation formation needs time to prepare. I will introduce him to you."

The young man's brow lifted. He carefully studied Fu Chaosheng, and in the end, he did not refuse.

However, as the two waited at the bow for half a day and still did not see Luo Chen appear, the atmosphere grew awkward.

"Hongzhang, go see what is happening on Wilderness Wanderer's side," Fu Chaosheng transmitted.

Fu Hongzhang, who was standing by, did not hesitate at all and hurried toward Kunxiao Palace.

Before long, he rushed back.

"Family head, according to Sage Luo's maidservant, her master is at a critical moment in studying a secret art, so he still needs a few days before leaving seclusion."

Fu Chaosheng froze.

Since that farewell at Blue Tide Palace, Luo Chen seemed to have stayed inside Kunxiao Palace for more than a month, right?

What kind of secret art needed this amount of time?

It was not long, which suggested the secret art's might was not great.

But it was not short either, and for him to make Fu Chaosheng wait for it, it likely had some separate wonder.

However, the stronger Luo Chen was, the happier Fu Chaosheng would be. How could he forcibly disturb him?

Turning around, he smiled apologetically. "Junior Brother Yu..."

The young man waved his hand generously. "If we do not meet, then we do not meet. There is no rush for the moment. However, when the time comes, he will still need to explain that Initiate cultivator's matter. Otherwise, if an accident occurs during teleportation, I will not be responsible."

Fu Chaosheng hurriedly nodded.

Only after the young man left did Fu Chaosheng show a regretful expression.

If he could use this opportunity to introduce Luo Chen to this person, Luo Chen's future inside Star Gate would likely be much smoother.

Now that Luo Chen was in seclusion, after he came out, the teleportation formation would probably already be prepared. At that time, there would not be much time for the two to become acquainted.

...

Inside the sealed chamber on Kunxiao Palace's fifth floor.

Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes, several traces of thought in them.

"So that is how it is."

The power of Ghost Immortal Tower's roar was a desolate beast innate divine ability, not something he could learn after one hurried glance.

However, the other method Ghost Immortal Tower displayed when he charged into Ice Soul Spirit Mountain earlier gave Luo Chen great inspiration.

Those Ghost Immortal phantasms circling beside him could confuse a Sage cultivator's divine sense, thereby causing an enemy's attack to deviate.

Such a method was absolutely a rare defensive soul art!

It was a powerful method not even Han Zhan had mastered.

Luo Chen had been greatly amazed at the time, and after carefully pondering it later, he discovered that he seemed to already possess the basic conditions needed to learn this method.

Desolate beast methods were not exquisite. On the contrary, they were very crude. It was only because of racial differences that human research was obstructed.

If it were an ordinary Sage, they would surely be utterly confused and have no way to begin, but Luo Chen found the thread.

Ghost Immortal Tower used desolate beast source power as a medium, combining the terrifying baleful aura across his body with his divine soul, thereby constructing the illusion of Ghost Immortals circling him.

And in these three aspects, Luo Chen possessed foundations in all of them.

Especially!

The illusion art Mirror Flower Water Moon, which he had learned years ago, had long since reached Great Completion.

Later, borrowing a trace of ill fortune from Ghost Immortal Tower within the soul-nourishing wood, he had created the second layer of illusion, Ghost Immortal Tower Illusion [TERM?], on his own.

Relying on this move, he had once made a late-stage Adept great cultivator retreat in the face of difficulty when he himself was only mid-stage Adept.

However, in the end, the difference in divine-soul essence caused this move to fall short when facing Sage cultivators, so Luo Chen rarely used it.

But now!

If Luo Chen wanted to imitate Ghost Immortal Tower's defensive soul art, it became simple.

On the foundation of the illusion art Ghost Immortal Tower Illusion, he would continuously explore and optimize, stimulate the enormous baleful aura accumulated on his body over the years, then use source power as the medium to weave together his sea of consciousness and divine soul...

"Compared to Ghost Immortal Tower, this move of mine may be somewhat weaker in might, but it is more exquisite. Given time, it may surpass the original and exceed him!"

As he murmured, Luo Chen's eyes suddenly opened wide.

At once, within three feet of his body, misty qi abruptly rose.

Rosy light spread, and vapor steamed upward.

Chanted curses and furious shouts sounded without end.

In the blur, faces, some young and some old, appeared one after another. There were men and women, and even fierce-faced phantoms of all kinds of demon beasts struggling and tumbling within.

Although it did not have the majestic sight of Ghost Immortals circling him, it had a horror and strangeness in its exquisite subtleties.

Luo Chen closed his eyes.

His divine soul withdrew, source power returned, and all the illusions instantly vanished as if nothing had happened.

But on the attribute panel, a brand-new entry had already appeared.

[Ghosts and Gods Give Way [TERM?] Beginner 1/100]

It was categorized under the soul arts section.

The instant he saw the entry appear, Luo Chen felt as though a heavy burden had been lifted.

This move was born from Mirror Flower Water Moon and could be viewed as Mirror Flower Water Moon's third layer of illusion.

But since it could be listed separately, that meant it had already far surpassed the third-tier illusion art Mirror Flower Water Moon.

In terms of grade alone, it should at least be above the fourth tier.

Moreover, self-created cultivation arts of this kind often had enormous potential and still had room to be excavated and optimized.

"As it happens, the divided soul I cultivated cannot undergo too much tempering with tribulation lightning's power. In my spare time, I will use the divided soul's strength to practice this art and accelerate its proficiency increase!"

With a faint smile, Luo Chen even arranged the follow-up cultivation plan.

However, for the moment, there was another important matter.

Luo Chen washed briefly, then left the sealed chamber refreshed and clean, summoned his subordinates, and stepped onto Sun-Moon Island.

...

"Wilderness Wanderer, are you certain you can protect your subordinates?"

Before an ancient hall protected by layers of formations, the Tianyuan Dao Sect's young man looked at the equally young Luo Chen and tried to confirm once more.

Luo Chen glanced at the nervous Zhang Jingqing and smiled faintly.

She subconsciously relaxed somewhat.

Afterward, Luo Chen calmly looked toward the young man. "Fellow Daoist Yu [TERM?], please rest assured. If the others can protect their own cultivators, then Luo can naturally protect Qingqing and the others as well."

Yu Ziyou [TERM?] frowned. "The people brought by the other two Sage fellow Daoists are Adept cultivators. There is still a great difference between them and this junior."

Seeing Luo Chen persist, he said no more.

In any case, he had already warned him. If something truly happened, it had nothing to do with Yu Ziyou.

His gaze swept over the fifteen people present: three Sages, each bringing four clansmen or subordinates.

Luo Chen's side was especially peculiar.

Aside from the low-realm Zhang Jingqing, there were actually two demon beasts.

Heavenjade, who had transformed before reaching the fourth tier.

Black King, who had already reached the fourth tier but had not transformed.

The latter especially clearly revealed a desolate beast aura!

This not only surprised Yu Ziyou, but also greatly shocked the other two Sages who had traveled with Luo Chen for half a year.

No one had known that the early-stage Sage Wilderness Wanderer Luo Chen actually had a fourth-tier desolate beast as a spirit pet, and had brought it along the whole way without letting anyone discover it.

If not for the fact that everyone had to report the condition of all living beings when riding the teleportation formation, they probably would still not have discovered it.

"Adjust your conditions first. The teleportation formation will activate soon."

Yu Ziyou said this, then hurried into the great hall.

Luo Chen stood outside and did not put on any aloof airs. Instead, he began conversing briefly with the two traveling Sages.

One was titled Winter Mountain Hermit [TERM?], and the other was named Lu Qiuzhen [TERM?].

Both of them were Sage cultivators who had been famous in the Northern Sea for many years.

The latter especially, Fu Chaosheng had instructed Luo Chen to befriend, because Lu Qiuzhen had also taken Fu Chaosheng's route and was going to join Star Gate.

After chatting for a while, the three suddenly fell silent.

The tightly shut palace doors slowly opened a crack.

Yu Ziyou's voice came from inside.

"Everyone, come in!"

The three exchanged glances, and Lu Qiuzhen said, "Wilderness Wanderer, you first."

Luo Chen smiled freely and did not act coy, bringing Black King and the others into the palace.

The moment he entered, he saw Fu Chaosheng standing together with Yu Ziyou, smiling as he looked at them.

"Let us go home together!"

Luo Chen froze, then lightly nodded.

It was going home.

Only, Fu Chaosheng was returning to his home in Central Continent, while Luo Chen was borrowing Central Continent's road to return to his home in the Eastern Wilderness.

He hoped the road would be smooth.

One incense stick later.

Boom!

An enormous pillar of light rose into the sky from the central hall of Sun-Moon Island, illuminating the surrounding hundred li.

Zhou Yunshen, who had brought his clansmen aboard another ship, gazed distantly at that pillar of light and softly sighed.

"Fellow Daoist Luo has gone one step ahead."

This sigh contained complicated emotions.

Those who did not know might think Luo Chen had died first.

In truth, it was Zhou Yunshen who had to bring his clansmen over mountains and seas, braving the danger of death, and travel by water toward Central Continent.

He only hoped there would be a day when he and Luo Chen met again.

It was not because of affection, but because being able to meet again meant he was still alive.

"This old man will arrive after you."

Zhou Yunshen withdrew his gaze and began exchanging divine-sense voice transmissions with the other three Sages.

On this trip, they not only had to bring their clansmen and disciples to Central Continent, but also had to escort a batch of Tianyuan Merchant Alliance goods.

In a certain sense, before they had even joined Star Gate, they had already begun working for the Tianyuan Dao Sect.

...

Star Island [TERM?].

One of the few large islands separated from the Central Continent mainland.

The island had a fourth-tier spirit vein and was planted with large quantities of medicinal herbs, truly a blessed land.

However, the island's true use was not cultivating spirit herbs, but the teleportation formation at the island's center.

It connected to the Northern Sea and led to the Southern Frontier, serving as a north-south hub.

If the teleportation formation were not too important, this place would certainly be incomparably bustling because of cultivators coming and going, not this quiet and secluded.

But this quiet scenery was suddenly broken on a certain day.

Inside the underground palace wrapped by a grand formation, enormous tremors sounded, and one Tianyuan Dao Sect cultivator after another rushed over from every direction.

They stared in shock toward the underground palace.

That place was the center of the teleportation formation. Ordinary outside-continent people who teleported over would all be restricted within the underground palace by the formation and could not easily leave the island.

Such careful arrangements were also to guard against accidents.

But today, this commotion was truly somewhat beyond expectation.

"How many people did the Northern Sea side teleport over? The spirit stone consumption is actually this enormous?"

"The upper limit for a single teleportation is at most a hundred people. But this commotion is far more than a hundred people, is it not?"

"Hiss, look. What is that?"

Blood!

Stream after stream of foul-smelling blood spread out from the underground palace, seeping into the ground and dyeing the earth a hazy red.

The Tianyuan cultivators looked at one another, and a thought appeared in all their minds.

Could this batch of people have all died during the teleportation process?

If that was true, then this matter would become enormous. A teleportation formation that had not had an accident in thousands of years had suddenly killed so many people.

Inside the underground palace.

The three great Sages stared in shock at Luo Chen and at the enormous jiaolong lying beside him.

Its opened mouth was constantly vomiting blood.

The surging quantity of blood flooded the underground palace, as though forming a river of blood.

Facing this, Luo Chen smiled bitterly in helplessness.

"My apologies. This was my oversight."

*(End of Chapter)*
