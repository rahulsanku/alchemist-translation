# Chapter 1080: A Dragon Appears in the Clouds, One Sword Severs the Land

*Boom!*

A muffled, oppressive sound transmitted through his limbs and bones to his eardrums.

The cloud giant that had previously appeared illusory was now, for some unknown reason, incredibly solid and heavy. Upon contact with Luo Chen's three-headed, six-armed golden body, it was a true, solid clash.

The two colossal figures tumbled down along Heaven-Earth Peak, crushing countless ancient trees and shattering hard mountain rocks. When they reached the mountain's base, a deafening tremor rang out.

Void waves exploded, dust billowed from the earth!

Amidst the surging, raging dust, Luo Chen slowly stood up, staring fixedly at the cloud giant opposite him.

Without a word!

Ultimate techniques were brought to hand!

His hands grasped the void, stretching and extending to both sides. The Heaven-Equal Staff, thick as a heavenly pillar, materialized in an instant.

Gripping the grey stone staff, Luo Chen mustered a thousand jun of strength, leaped up, and smashed down.

*Boom!*

The cloud giant raised its hands to parry, actually managing to withstand this blow.

But Luo Chen had more than one pair of hands, and his attacks would not be limited to a single move.

Two streaks of golden light, like snakes spitting their tongues, circled around from left and right. As they neared the cloud giant, they suddenly became incredibly straight.

*Sss!*

*Sss!*

The cloud giant looked down. Two golden feathers had pierced straight through its heart and lower abdomen.

Luo Chen grinned ferociously. Surge of immense power from his two hands, and the two life-seizing golden feathers immediately began to churn violently inside the giant's body.

"Break!"

With a low shout, golden light blazed!

As Luo Chen withdrew the life-seizing golden feathers, a massive hole, through which the sky's light could be seen, suddenly tore open in the cloud giant's broad chest.

"With mere tricks like this, you dare to contend with this sect's unmatched physical combat?"

As Luo Chen muttered, his gaze once again turned upward.

The God-Descending Formation was still ongoing. The killing intent from the upper realm was becoming increasingly clear. Vaguely, within the pillar of light, ripples of void could be seen undulating, as if opening a door to another world.

Suddenly!

Luo Chen's heart rang with alarm. He turned his head to see the cloud giant, whose vital points he had just pierced, throwing a punch at him.

*Boom!*

Luo Chen did not dodge or evade, taking the blow head-on with his physical body.

The black armor on his body flowed like liquid mercury, dissipating the boundless force. He only let out a muffled grunt.

This Mystic Dust Armor, which had been reforged with four supreme-grade lotus seeds and the pure source blood of a Xuanyan Turtle, possessed unparalleled defensive power. Though he didn't know how it compared to a spirit treasure-grade defensive artifact, it was not something an existence like this could easily break.

Luo Chen's entire body slid backward several li, dissipating that immense force.

He looked again at the cloud giant. Its performance was now completely different from before.

The hole torn open by the life-seizing golden feathers was rapidly closing, as if it were immortal and indestructible.

Meanwhile, on its body, clouds and mist swirled. A pure white python and a fanged giant serpent were born with hissing cries, clinging to the cloud giant like ribbons.

"Troublesome!"

Luo Chen took a deep breath and charged forward once more.

The Heaven-Equal Staff swept horizontally, meeting force with force.

The golden feathers danced, soft yet possessing an indestructible, razor-sharp power.

The cloud giant also showed no sign of retreat. It swung its hands, forming fists to meet the Heaven-Equal Staff head-on. The pure white python and fanged giant serpent on its body shrieked, twisting their forms to entangle and clash with the two life-seizing golden feathers, not falling behind in the slightest.

Just as the cloud giant parried Luo Chen's grey stone staff once more, Luo Chen's head suddenly turned.

His original white-faced human visage transformed into another, wrathful Yaksha face.

The hideous Yaksha face opened its massive mouth and let out a sky-shaking roar.

"*Roooar!*"

The surging sound waves transformed into unparalleled airwaves, like a continuous tidal wave.

The cloud giant was caught off guard. The cloud qi on its body began to dissipate under this impact, most of its form becoming illusory.

Seizing this opportunity, Luo Chen's Heaven-Equal Staff smashed down heavily!

*Thump!*

The cloud giant's head was smashed straight down from its shoulders, sinking into its chest.

Its entire body was deeply embedded in the hard earth.

Having reached this point, surely it couldn't recover, could it?

Luo Chen let out a rough breath and slowly withdrew his stone staff.

On Heaven-Earth Peak.

Lian Cheng watched this scene, a hint of contempt in his eyes.

Even if the opponent displayed formidable combat power, he was ultimately just an uncultured wilderness rogue cultivator. How could he know the mysteries of immortal Dao techniques?

With one hand holding a seal towards the sky, and the other forming a hand seal towards the ground, he slightly moved the hand seal in his left hand. The nine heavens' sea of clouds churned, and in an instant, a massive amount of cloud qi was infused into the giant.

Ignoring the changes on the giant, Lian Cheng continued to maintain the hand seal of the God-Descending Formation.

At the base of Heaven-Earth Peak.

Luo Chen watched in shock as the cloud giant, on its headless shoulders, grew another head.

And the head that had been smashed in appeared again on its chest.

Not only that, but cloud qi also rose on its back. Under his divine sense observation, an extra face had grown there.

Was this some kind of alternative three-headed form?

After the initial shock, Luo Chen instantly calmed down.

Thinking back to the python and evil jiao that had grown on the cloud giant after his sneak attack with the life-seizing golden feathers...

"This giant has a strong ability to learn and imitate, but this ability doesn't originate from it... it originates from Lian Cheng! From that sea of clouds!"

Luo Chen slowly retreated, his Yaksha wrath-face eyes watching the cloud giant, but his white-faced head's gaze was fixed on the sky above.

Sure enough, the sea of clouds that had existed for who knows how long was churning and rolling, sending down strands of cloud qi into the giant's body.

"Sea of clouds... phantom form... giant."

Muttering to himself, Luo Chen suddenly realized why he had felt a sense of déjà vu about this technique earlier!

Back then, when the Southern Frontier's Youli Divine Lord ascended, he had revealed a sky-supporting, earth-standing phantom form within the sea of clouds!

That was when his realm was about to enter the Refining Void stage, a phantom form condensed with his mana containing the true meaning of laws. In the classics, it could also be called a Dharma Form!

Given Lian Cheng's realm, it was absolutely impossible for him to reach that level.

Furthermore, even Youli Divine Lord's Dharma Form was relatively illusory, far inferior to the phoenix Dharma Form that Rosycloud had condensed during the Nether Abyss battle.

But Lian Cheng was not foolish. On the contrary, for someone who could advance to Ascendant, he could be called a heaven-defying genius.

He had once been injured by Youli Divine Lord.

Yet he had also comprehended some of the opponent's techniques. Thus, through imitation, he had used a vast amount of cloud qi and his own mana to condense the cloud giant.

As long as that sea of clouds was not exhausted, even if Luo Chen tore the cloud giant before him into pieces, it would quickly recover.

Furthermore, during each exchange, the cloud giant could imitate and learn Luo Chen's techniques, making corresponding responses.

"Do you truly think Luo has no intelligence?"

Luo Chen let out a grim laugh. Instead of retreating, he charged straight at the cloud giant.

The creature also roared and pounced at Luo Chen, its massive roar carrying rolling sound waves, clearly imitating Luo Chen's earlier Yaksha roar.

Ignoring this lowly imitation, Luo Chen did not dodge or evade, relying on his physical body and soul foundation to withstand it.

Then, his two hands reached for the opponent. The cloud giant did the same.

Four hands locked together in a death grip, engaging in pure strength contest.

A fierce wind struck from the side. The pure white python and fanged giant serpent both bit at Luo Chen.

Luo Chen extended his other two hands, grabbing each serpent by the neck, preventing them from struggling.

Then!

*Whoosh! Whoosh! Whoosh!*

One after another, crimson chains burst forth from Luo Chen's body, like arrows shot in a volley, piercing into the cloud giant's body.

Each chain carried an extremely powerful suppressing force, preventing the enemy from mobilizing power for a short time.

Finally, Luo Chen's remaining two hands, each the size of a millstone, slapped the opponent continuously.

With each slap, a portion of source blood was injected.

Every attack carried the power of a sealing restriction.

After eighteen slaps, Luo Chen struck out with both palms simultaneously.

"Open!"

Heaven and earth paused for a moment, followed by the sound of shattering and splitting, echoing through the void.

*Crack!*

The massive cloud giant suddenly split into countless fragments.

Seeing this, Lian Cheng was unconcerned. He once again guided cloud qi down.

But this time, no matter how much cloud qi was injected into those fragments, it could not make them coalesce and reform.

Vaguely, a layer of red light was blocking the fusion of the cloud qi.

"A blood-sealing technique?"

Lian Cheng's brow furrowed slightly, as if he hadn't expected the opponent to possess such exquisite skill.

But what he hadn't expected was far from over.

Using the Overlord Removes Armor technique, combined with source blood seals, Luo Chen had completely sealed the cloud giant, preventing it from recovering at will.

Now, there was only one target left!

The nearly thousand-zhang tall giant's body slightly crouched, its feet firmly planted on the ground, then it suddenly looked up!

*Thump!*

Its legs pushed off with force, and its entire body shot up like an arrow from a string.

Lian Cheng shouted lightly: "Go!"

Within the boundless sea of clouds, dragon claws appeared.

A cloud jiao dragon bared its fangs and brandished its claws, then came two or three, then more and more, until there were thousands upon thousands!

Staring at the colossal giant charging up from below, all the cloud dragons descended simultaneously.

Facing this scene, Luo Chen showed no fear.

Chains surged from his body, one crimson chain after another shooting forward.

*Sss! Sss! Sss!*

Every cloud dragon that tried to tear Luo Chen apart was shattered into the purest cloud qi by the crimson chains transformed from the Primordial Unity Cauldron, then returned to that sea of clouds.

But in an instant, even more cloud dragons would condense within that sea of clouds.

It seemed just like the cloud giant before—as long as the sea of clouds was not exhausted, countless monsters would be born from it.

Luo Chen's expression turned fierce.

His hands formed a seal, and he let out a low shout.

"Come forth!"

The next moment!

Behind him, a golden, radiant great bird shrieked as it flew out.

Its speed was extremely fast, as if it could tear through the void.

Its power was even greater, golden flames following it like ribbons.

In an instant, the golden firebird burrowed into the sea of clouds.

Thousands, tens of thousands of cloud dragons roared and surrounded it, but the golden firebird merely trembled its wings, scattering trails of golden flames, burning all the cloud dragons to ashes.

"*Caw!*"

A clear, piercing cry echoed through the universe.

Surging waves of fire swept across the four seas and eight wastelands. The sea of clouds that had shrouded Heaven-Earth Peak for countless years collapsed in an instant.

Countless cloud clusters shattered, and a torrential downpour fell.

The golden firebird stretched its wings wantonly in the pouring rain, the scorching power on its body burning all the rain curtains back into nothingness.

Until the sky was clear and bright, the azure sky washed clean.

*Thump!*

Luo Chen finally set foot on the mountain peak!

And opposite him, within the God-Descending Formation, Lian Cheng could no longer sit still.

His fingers trembled slightly, and a hint of fear appeared in his eyes as he looked at the golden firebird, which then turned into boundless anger.

"Nirvana Sacred Flame!"

"You are one of the Demon Phoenix's people!"

As he spoke, his divine sense bloomed, a majestic pressure as heavy as a mountain bearing down on Luo Chen.

Luo Chen's steps paused slightly, then he also released his divine sense, reinforcing his own Sage domain.

And at the moment the sea of clouds dissipated, the Heaven-Earth Severance Grand Formation that had previously shrouded Heaven-Earth Peak collapsed instantly!

A green-white fire tree, along with the golden firebird, returned together to Luo Chen's body.

Seeing this, all traces of contempt vanished from Lian Cheng's eyes, and the disdain on his face was gone.

He had thought that this brat mastering a single fifth-grade green-white flame was nothing special, far inferior to the Solar True Flame mastered by his sect's junior, Shi Ju.

But when the Nirvana Sacred Flame appeared, he knew he could no longer judge Luo Chen by any common standard.

Especially since the opponent had also released his divine sense, reinforcing his Sage domain to contend with his divine domain, evenly matched.

This was an existence that had condensed a primordial spirit ahead of time, comprehended the true meaning of laws, and mastered two fifth-grade divine flames. He was almost no different from a great Ascendant power.

Perhaps the only difference was the missing life soul.

Bearing his pressure, Luo Chen walked forward step by step. In his hand, a longsword had appeared at some unknown time.

This sword was ancient, with chilling grooves.

Its entire body was suffused with a bloody halo, as if it had absorbed the blood of billions of people.

He looked at the sky-piercing pillar of light. After the sea of clouds had dissipated, starlight filled the pillar, and a phantom figure flickered within, as if it could descend into this world at any moment.

"Ascendant Lord of the Upper Realm, you cannot descend!"

Luo Chen did not argue whether he was one of Rosycloud's people. He only spoke cold words.

Then, he swung his sword!

Lian Cheng waited with a cold smile.

This God-Descending Formation had been fully activated. With Heaven-Earth Peak as the Dao beacon, a fifth-grade spirit vein as the energy source, and an entire continent's spiritual mechanism as the guide, the intensity of the formed light pillar was nearly equivalent to the complete Central Continent!

How could Luo Chen possibly sever it with a single sword strike?

A vast sword light descended, striking the light pillar.

*Clang!*

A terrifying sonic boom shook the entire Heaven-Earth Peak.

Even Lian Cheng's eyes showed a hint of disbelief.

From within the sky-piercing light pillar came an angry roar.

"Brat, how dare you!"

"What would I not dare!"

The sword light rose again, the sword technique was unleashed again. Boundless sky qi converged, forming the most biting sharpness, the most domineering and unparalleled torrent of sword qi.

It seemed that under this sword, anything that dared to block it would be destroyed.

"Stop him!"

The roar came again from within the swaying light pillar.

Lian Cheng could no longer sit idly by. He rose to his full height, mana surging within his body, drawing upon boundless heaven and earth origin qi, then struck a palm towards the void.

"Horizon Neighbor!"

The void's origin qi became chaotic. Luo Chen's body moved slightly, and even the Heaven-Earth Peak in his eyes seemed to become distant and unreachable.

But the sword technique had already been unleashed and could not be withdrawn. He could only follow his instincts and bring the sword down.

*Boom!*

The vast sword qi poured down like a flood.

One sword fell, severing the land!

Heaven-Earth Island, vast as a continent, was cut in two like tofu under this single sword strike.

The residual power of the sword qi torrent was unending, rolling forward. The entire ocean was split in half, towering waves reaching thousands, tens of thousands of zhang high, before crashing down onto the sea surface. Countless living beings died in the aftermath of this single sword strike.

Blood began to stain the ocean, dust and smoke shrouding the shattered Heaven-Earth Island.

Lian Cheng watched this scene with lingering fear, inwardly exclaiming his luck.

If he hadn't used his divine ability technique to forcibly teleport Luo Chen away, this sword strike would have severely damaged the God-Descending Formation, or even directly severed the teleportation channel.

This sword technique was comparable to a divine ability, and it also carried a hint of familiarity.

This sword was incredibly sharp, as if it could cut through all things. It was definitely no ordinary true artifact; it was likely already at the spirit treasure grade.

Luo Chen slowly raised his longsword, the tip pointing diagonally at Lian Cheng.

"This sword is named Yuantu. Today, it will slaughter all the great powers of Heavenly Origin!"
