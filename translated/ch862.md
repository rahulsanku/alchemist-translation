# Chapter 862: Spirit Treasure Covers the Domain, Hundred-Legged Demon Emperor (Asking for Monthly Votes)

Two Ascendants, no!

It was five Ascendant-level great powers fighting!

On the Nether Origin Sect's side, aside from Black Marsh Ancestor, the other two ancestors had also arrived in full force.

One had borrowed the body of Emperor Slaughter, the other had lodged the soul of High Ghost.

Under the sudden assault, coordinated with Black Marsh Ancestor, they had surrounded the Ancient Demon Emperor Heaven in an instant.

And on the Ancient Demon Emperor Heaven's side, he was not alone either. He had long since secretly invited Azure Grain Immortal, an Ascendant-level powerhouse who had emerged from the Million Mountains.

The moment both sides made their move, a vast sea of origin qi from heaven and earth was mobilized.

The ten-thousand-li surroundings of Azure Wandering Mountain were immediately sealed off.

The turbulent origin qi turned this space into something like a quagmire, making it difficult for those below the Ascendant stage to even take a step.

Sounds like clashing swords and sabers rang out, spreading across millions of li.

"Nether Prison, Heaven Abyss, Black Marsh! I never thought that to deal with me, the Nether Origin Sect's great powers would dispatch their entire force. You truly think highly of this seat!"

"Lord Peace Mountain, since you have entered the Netherworld Yellow Spring Diagram, don't think of leaving." It was Black Marsh Ancestor's voice.

"And don't expect Azure Grain Immortal to come down and help you. She attained the Dao not long ago; she can barely protect herself."

"Submit and be captured. Three against one, you cannot escape the path of falling!"

The latter two voices, amplified by vast dharma power, turned into rolling sound waves sweeping across thousands and tens of thousands of li. All who heard them felt their minds shaken.

As for the enemy's persuasion to surrender, the Ancient Demon Emperor Heaven paid it no heed at all.

His reply to everyone was only one sentence.

"Then let's fight!"

A pitch-black scroll, under the support of endless origin qi, began to crazily expand and grow larger.

A hundred zhang, a thousand zhang, ten thousand zhang...

A hundred li, a thousand li, ten thousand li...

Vast like a heavenly canopy, it obscured the azure sky.

Wherever it passed, mountains, rocks, trees, birds, and beasts were all absorbed into it.

Within the scroll, three figures stood on three sides, forming a triangular formation, completely surrounding a huge mass of platinum-white light.

And above the black canopy.

In the nine heavens, a mass of cyan light shone brilliantly.

What greeted it was a violent thunder pool and dazzling stars.

The world had no idea what had happened in that short time.

Only the group of Demon Emperors and Sages who had personally participated in the Green Wandering negotiations knew the details of the sudden change.

But at this moment, no one paid attention to any of that.

Everyone was using all their strength to flee outward, afraid of being swallowed by that Netherworld Yellow Spring Diagram.

Heavenly Capital Master, having received Luo Chen's warning, was the first to run.

Three baleful, unmatched great flags wrapped around him tightly, and he crazily fled outward like an arrow.

When the stars shone in the nine heavens above, he subconsciously glanced up.

With just one look, his eyes nearly split with rage!

That was one of his Heavenly Capital Sect's three fifth-rank formations, the Circumambient Star Dipper Grand Formation!

To resist the demon race's invasion, he had deployed this formation at Lingtian Pass, specifically to limit powerful demon emperors from launching sneak attacks from the nine heavens.

He never expected that this formation had been secretly learned by Nether Origin Sect cultivators.

Not only had they learned it, but they had also made some covert changes.

But no matter how they changed it, this formation was ultimately derived from the Circumambient Star Dipper Grand Formation, so he instantly recognized its operating principles.

Those shining stars were clearly a group of Sage cultivators!

A rough count showed there were thirty-six stars.

This meant that in the nine heavens above, there were thirty-six Sages.

And from what Heavenly Capital Master knew, among them must be great cultivators like Lingtian City Lord and Chongxuan Sage, late-stage Sages.

Six great cultivators!

And among these six, the leader was clearly Thunder Daozi, who had comprehended the true meaning of the law.

"Using the name of the two-race great war to steal my sect's fifth-rank formation, then using the Circumambient Star Dipper Grand Formation to channel boundless power onto Thunder Daozi, using the true meaning of the thunder law to restrain that Azure Grain Immortal."

"Good, good, good!"

"What a fine Nether Origin Holy Land!"

Heavenly Capital Master's aged face was full of indignation, but his feet moved no slower.

But he wasn't slow; someone else was faster!

Swish!

Amidst the violent origin qi turbulence, a black shadow seemed unaffected, overtaking him from behind and instantly surpassing him.

"It's Divine Origin!" Heavenly Capital Master recognized the other's back, marveling inwardly, "Both being Sage cultivators, why is he not suppressed by the heaven and earth origin qi?"

The other party naturally wouldn't stop to answer him. His back disappeared in an instant amidst clumps of black qi.

Heavenly Capital Master also didn't dare linger in Azure Sky Domain.

Now, in this great domain, because of that Netherworld Yellow Spring Diagram, the origin qi of heaven and earth was being crazily drained.

The violent environment made it difficult even for Sage cultivators to discern direction. They only knew to stay far away from the storm's center.

Heavenly Capital Master's mind moved, and two more baleful, fierce great flags appeared, wrapping his entire body.

"First, leave this place!"

Just as he was about to disregard his dharma power and flee Azure Sky Domain, he subconsciously looked in another direction.

Chi...

In the void, flames suddenly appeared.

A young figure walked out from the flames.

The young man glanced at Heavenly Capital Master, nodded slightly in greeting, then formed hand seals and took a step.

With one step, the flames flickered and vanished again.

And his figure had already disappeared.

"What a brilliant fire escape art! I never expected the Pill Sect to have such a technique!"

Heavenly Capital Master sighed in admiration, then, disregarding others, his figure vanished like an arrow into the vast origin qi.

...

Chi!

Flames flickered, white robes fluttered.

He landed on a mountain peak.

As soon as Luo Chen stopped, he picked up his gourd and took a gulp of Golden Wind and Jade Dew Wine, quickly replenishing his dharma power.

This self-created fire escape art was powerful, but its consumption of dharma power was even greater than fourth-rank large-scale spells like Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain and Flame Dragon Descends.

Even with his profound dharma power, he couldn't sustain it for too long.

Moreover, in the current environment, he had to retain some dharma power just in case. So, after learning from his previous lesson, he never stopped drinking the Golden Wind and Jade Dew Wine.

The strong wine entered his throat, turning into violent spiritual qi, converging into his Purple Mansion.

While recovering his dharma power, Luo Chen looked around.

Winding, undulating mountain ranges, peaks that pierced the clouds...

"This is Nine Spirit Domain!"

Luo Chen hadn't expected that, in that environment where direction was hard to discern, he had originally been flying toward Lingtian Pass but had ended up in Nine Spirit Domain.

With a sweep of his divine sense, there were no powerful beings within several thousand li.

Only then did Luo Chen relax, turning his head to look back in the direction of Azure Sky Domain.

His gaze involuntarily fell upon that sky-covering black canopy.

"The Netherworld Yellow Spring Diagram, one of the Nether Origin Sect's two spirit treasures!"

"To deal with the Ancient Demon Emperor Heaven, Black Marsh has even sacrificed this treasure. If we add the two ancestors Nether Prison and Heaven Abyss... Heaven is likely in grave danger."

Luo Chen sighed, then shifted his gaze to the nine heavens above.

Those thirty-six shining stars were too conspicuous.

Endless thunder light, like a heavenly tribulation, fiercely suppressed a tenacious mass of cyan light.

Luo Chen's brow furrowed.

Ascendant existences, considered the pinnacle of this realm, were already completely beyond what Sage cultivators could compare to.

Could thirty Sages, plus six great cultivators, suppress an Ascendant great power?

Wasn't that a bit too easy?

Luo Chen wanted to observe further, but a terrifying trembling sound suddenly reached his ears.

Rumble...

Looking down, sand and gravel rolled beneath his feet, and green stones shook.

"Not good!"

Without a second thought, Luo Chen stamped his foot and shot up into the sky.

The next moment, the mountain peak he had been standing on shattered with a bang.

Endless smoke and dust rose, and a large amount of spiritual qi erupted from the underground spirit veins, surging toward Azure Sky Domain.

Luo Chen's eyes narrowed, and his throat bobbed.

"So this is the might of an Ascendant?"

"When they go all out, the range of origin qi they draw upon is no longer limited to a single city or domain. Even the neighboring Nine Spirit Domain has been affected."

"And this is because the Netherworld Yellow Spring Diagram has confined the battlefield. If Heaven were unrestricted and they fought a mobile battle, the world would surely be devastated wherever they passed!"

At this moment, Luo Chen finally understood why Ascendant great powers were not allowed to act lightly.

Even though he had speculated about this before, only now, having truly experienced it, did the realization sink deep into his bones.

"This place cannot be lingered in!"

Without much thought, Luo Chen unhesitatingly activated Ninety Thousand Li, flying toward the nearby Taihu Domain.

That Netherworld Yellow Spring Diagram had already enveloped Azure Sky Domain!

And the nearby Fire Ridge, Overlord Blade Domain, and Nine Spirit Domain were all within its range of influence.

If they continued fighting, the area affected would likely be even larger.

Taihu, Firework Ancient Isle, Misty, Motian Cliff, and even the Lingtian Five Domains...

Luo Chen didn't know the limit of the Nether Origin Sect's three ancestors' ability to absorb the origin qi of heaven and earth when working together, but he knew the farther he was from that storm's center, the better!

After he left.

Nine Spirit Domain, once home to the Nine Spirit Sect, began to change, little by little but inexorably.

Countless mountains collapsed, thousand-year-old ancient trees groaned and split.

Rivers changed course, surging into Azure Sky.

The earth cracked open, and crustal magma erupted and burst forth.

All of this was like the end of the world!

...

Luo Chen's self-created fire escape art combined the fused spirit burst of the metal escape art with the principle of cloud-pattern gathering and dispersing formlessness from the Stack Cloud Sect, coupled with his powerful physique. Once activated, its momentum was immense.

With this escape art, it was enough to become a life-saving trump card.

Unfortunately, this art was in its early stages of creation, consuming a huge amount of dharma power, and still needed refinement.

But even before mastering this art, Luo Chen's flying speed was already outstanding among his peers.

Thus, disregarding dharma power and flying at full speed, Luo Chen took only half a day to traverse Nine Spirit Domain and reach Taihu Domain.

Hum...

The tips of his toes lightly touched the lake's surface, and ripples slowly spread outward.

This showed how calm the water was.

Luo Chen took a large gulp of Golden Wind and Jade Dew Wine into his mouth, then heavily exhaled a breath of wine vapor.

"Taihu Domain is still relatively calm. The influence of the Ascendant great powers drawing on the origin qi of heaven and earth hasn't spread here yet. I can stop for a while."

Luo Chen released his divine sense, and after a moment, fixed a direction.

He traveled forward, skirting around scattered lakes.

Finally, he arrived at a place where countless palaces stood tall.

This was the former Taihu Palace, once a great Sage sect!

But compared to its peak, only a few traces of Taihu Palace could be seen from these broken walls and ruins.

Truly a pity.

Luo Chen sighed, stepped forward, and entered a palace that was relatively well-preserved but stank to high heaven.

A great demon had once lived here!

Luo Chen quickly deduced the reason for the stench.

He didn't mind, waved his hand, and cast a Cleansing Art. The air in the place immediately became fresh.

Then, he sat down cross-legged and quickly began to recover his dharma power.

Golden Wind and Jade Dew Wine was good, but its pure spiritual qi hadn't been imbued with his soul's aura. If he drank too much without refining it, it could easily destabilize his Sage.

Just as Luo Chen was calmly sitting and refining the spiritual qi, deep within the great lake in front of Taihu Palace, a pair of lantern-sized eyes slowly opened.

As if sensing a familiar scent, it looked around in confusion, then finally raised its head to look upward.

...

Crash!

A great lake collapsed.

A vast amount of lake water turned into a surging torrent, rolling toward Azure Sky Domain.

A figure in a yellow-green floral robe, with both hands tucked inside, swept over the torrent without pausing.

His speed was so fast, and the expression on his face so tense, as if something soul-seizing and life-taking was chasing him from behind.

The floral-robed man muttered to himself as he flew.

"Before, I followed Arrogant and refused to submit to Heaven. Now it seems I was terribly wrong."

"Ascendant existences are truly this terrifying!"

"If Heaven dies, then so be it. If he still lives, this emperor will never oppose him again."

The battle in Azure Sky Domain had already lasted a day and a night.

The origin qi of heaven and earth being drawn upon could no longer be sustained by just the nearby domains alone.

Even Taihu Domain, nearly a hundred thousand li away, was being affected, showing the ferocity of the battle.

The floral-robed man didn't dare stop. He urged his dharma power, wanting to go to a safer rear area.

His figure was like an arrow, never ceasing.

Wherever he passed, lake light flickered, appearing and disappearing in an instant.

Suddenly!

The floral-robed figure stopped, his gaze falling on a relatively well-preserved palace.

At that moment, a white-robed man walked out from inside the palace.

His calm eyes met the other's surprised and delighted gaze.

"Hundred Legs, you shouldn't have stopped."

Hundred Legs Commander laughed heartily, "I was just wondering why the spiritual qi here was so calm compared to other places. So it was you cultivating here! Pill Sect, come with me for a trip!"

As he spoke, his right hand, hidden in his robe, suddenly extended and grabbed toward the palace below.

As he reached out, that arm suddenly grew larger and thicker, turning into a sky-supporting giant hand, pouncing toward Luo Chen.

A fierce light flashed in Luo Chen's eyes.

Come with him for a trip?

Everyone wanted him to come with them for a trip!

Even this beast dared to speak such arrogant words!

Black Marsh of the Nether Origin Sect had a high cultivation base and tempted him with great profit, so he could disrespect him. But how dare you spout such nonsense?

The white-robed Daoist flicked his fingertip.

The next moment, a sea of sword qi surged into the sky.

Shua! Shua! Shua!

The sword qi giant hand collided instantly.

Under the sharp cutting, it finally faded away silently.

Hundred Legs slowly withdrew his right hand. The sleeve was completely shredded, and his arm bore deep sword marks.

Staring at these marks, Hundred Legs raised an eyebrow.

"So this is your reliance?"

He grinned fiercely, showing no fear at all, and charged toward Luo Chen.

"I want to see just how powerful that sword formation Arrogant was so fixated on really is!"

With this wild laughter, vast demonic qi erupted violently.

Luo Chen extended both hands, and even more sword qi rushed out from beneath Taihu Palace.

Not only that, but his Sage domain was also about to unfold.

He wanted to recreate the Triple Prison!

But just as Luo Chen tried to manipulate the heaven and earth spiritual qi, his expression changed slightly.

Boom!

Luo Chen flew backward, his form ethereal like a roc flapping its wings.

And the majestic palace he had been occupying had turned into a pile of dust.

Luo Chen waved his hand, and five sword pellets spun and flew back into his hand.

"The origin qi here is turbulent. The fire-attribute spiritual qi is completely beyond my control. I can't use the Triple Prison anymore."

A giant hand flew out from the endless dust.

Luo Chen didn't dodge. His right hand chopped down.

Blood light flashed, and the giant hand was severed in midair.

Amidst the broken walls and ruins, Hundred Legs waved his hand, dispersing the smoke and dust, revealing a ferocious face.

"This sword formation is nothing special. It can be broken with a wave of the hand."

"You do have some skill. To avoid any more delays, I won't waste time sparring with you!"

As he spoke, both hands reached out from inside the floral robe, peeling the yellow-green robe open and letting it hang at his waist.

What was revealed was a dense mass of hands and feet, covering his entire body.

Demonic wind swept, demonic qi surged.

His body began to continuously expand and grow larger.

Pairs of thick, sturdy hands and feet supported his massive frame, making him appear both ferocious and terrifying.

Not only that, but weapons also emerged from within his flesh and blood, spitting out into those palms.

Sabers, spears, clubs, staves, swords, halberds, great axes.

Looking from afar, a huge centipede, seven or eight hundred zhang long, holding over a hundred dharma treasures, stood across the palace ruins.

Luo Chen held the Yuantu Blood Sword in his hand, his expression solemn as he watched this scene.

Casually, he caught a glimpse of another black shadow slowly swimming beneath the choppy waters of Taihu Lake.
