# Chapter 773: Sunrise in the East, Star Gate Luo Chen

Upon the vast, boundless sea, a figure descended like a falling meteor, plummeting straight downward.

Upon reaching the water's surface, he abruptly halted, sending up layers of splashing waves.

"This realm still needs some polishing. Such crude control over my mana is hardly my usual style."

The Daoist muttered to himself, oriented his bearings, and without hesitation headed east.

Before long, a magnificent large carriage came into view.

With a few quick bounds, Luo Chen landed lightly on the carriage deck.

"True Person?"

Standing on the flying boat, Zhang Jingqing was the first to spot Luo Chen's figure, but not entirely certain, he tentatively greeted him.

Luo Chen nodded. As he passed by, he smiled warmly and said, "Your cultivation has improved. It seems you haven't been slacking off lately!"

Zhang Jingqing's face lit up at the praise, but he humbly replied, "All thanks to Uncle Long's efforts."

The "Uncle Long" he referred to was none other than Black King.

And his words were not without reason.

When Black King practiced his breathing and cultivation normally, he would attract a vast amount of heaven and earth spiritual energy to converge in one area, creating a spiritual energy concentration no less than that of a third-grade lower cave dwelling.

This was also why Luo Chen, despite finding Black King's habit of clinging to him annoying, had never refused him.

That fellow, ever since devouring the soul of the desolate beast Stone Dragon, had become practically a spirit-gathering device!

He could attract a stream of ambient spiritual energy at any time, anywhere.

However, it was clear that Black King could not fully utilize his talent. He could not spontaneously condense a fourth-grade or higher spiritual energy environment like the desolate beast Stone Dragon could.

He still needed earthly qi and spiritual veins to assist!

If he could arrange a high-quality spiritual vein for Black King, this talent could be fully displayed!

Luo Chen continued forward. Hearing the commotion, Tianxuan and Sang Jinghe also emerged.

Seeing it was Luo Chen, they couldn't help but breathe a sigh of relief.

"Master, you're back!"

"Greetings, True Person!"

Luo Chen waved his hand. Seeing everyone safe and sound, he felt somewhat reassured.

"Has anything happened these past six months?"

Tianxuan shook her head. "Nothing major. We encountered some cultivators along the way, but with the Star Gate guest elder identity token you left behind, they didn't cause us much trouble."

"Mm, good. Continue onward!"

After giving this instruction, Luo Chen hurried inside the carriage.

Black King, who had been curled up on the floor in a light doze, sensed Luo Chen's presence and instantly woke up.

"Go back to sleep."

Leaving these words, Luo Chen paid Black King no further attention.

The latter, however, was a bit thick-skinned. Shrinking his size, he slithered over. Though he dared not climb onto Luo Chen's arm, he prostrated himself at his feet.

Like this, he seemed to sleep more soundly!

But less than two months later, Black King suddenly jolted awake from his dreams, his fur standing on end.

He opened his eyes and looked up.

Luo Chen, clad in white robes, sat cross-legged on the soft couch. The blood sword, brimming with killing intent, lay horizontally across his knees.

Threads of crimson sword qi, like serpent tongues spitting venom, flickered and pulsed on the blood sword.

It was that sword qi that made him feel a stabbing pain!

Black King swallowed his saliva, his heart filled with uncertainty. "Is Master cultivating some powerful secret art again?"

In all his years following Luo Chen, he had seen him practice new secret arts countless times.

Many times, he had even been used as a test subject.

But this time, Black King prayed inwardly that he wouldn't be chosen to test moves again.

That sword was already terrifying enough. Now, who knew what kind of ghostly thing had been attached to it? Just being near it made his whole body ache. If it actually landed on him, at best he'd be cut and bruised; at worst, he might have to rely on his Undying Body talent.

To Black King's relief, Luo Chen didn't even look at him. He was entirely focused on the blood sword before his knees.

Luo Chen let out a relieved sigh.

"Finally, I've entered the gate!"

He had never imagined that entering the gate of this sword scripture would be so difficult.

He had long cultivated *Settling Wind and Waves*, possessing a wisdom heart capable of severing all thoughts with a single sword stroke. He had even manifested sword light within the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven.

Logically, with this foundation, entering the gate of the *Thousand Clouds Myriad Transformations Sword Scripture* should not have been too hard.

But unexpectedly, when he tried to refine the Red Cloud Qi and integrate it into the Yuantu Sword, an accident occurred.

The nameless sword spirit, long dormant within the sword, instinctively resisted the Red Cloud Qi.

This made things quite difficult.

To enter the gate of the *Thousand Clouds Myriad Transformations Sword Scripture*, one must find a vessel and refine the Cloud Qi, whether it be a sword pellet or a flying sword.

Luo Chen had set his sights on the Yuantu Sword, but it was exceptionally resistant!

Left with no choice, Luo Chen stopped insisting on entering the gate on his own. Instead, he used the achievement points he had accumulated over the years to forcibly enter the gate.

Having thus entered the gate of the sword scripture, he realized his mistake.

This sword already possessed a sword spirit, yet he had no sword intent to communicate with it. Forcibly refining the Red Cloud Qi into it would damage the spirit's intelligence.

This was why entering the gate had been so difficult before.

Now that he understood the reason, solving it was not hard.

Since he could not break through the third seal of the Yuantu Sword and refine that sword spirit, he simply set up three more layers of restrictions outside the third seal!

One was a Blood Origin Restriction, one was a Mana Restriction, and finally a Soul Restriction.

With these three layers of insurance and three layers of isolation, the sword spirit finally stopped resisting the Red Cloud Qi.

Only then did Luo Chen methodically incorporate a strand of Red Cloud Qi into the sword body.

Now, staring at the blood sword with its flickering sword qi, Luo Chen fell into deep thought.

"Red Cloud Sword Qi must be nurtured day and night to achieve perfect control."

"According to the sword scripture, nurturing it within the Purple Mansion is best!"

"But this sword already has a spirit, and that sword spirit is not as submissive to me as the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit. If I were to put it inside my Purple Mansion, it would be too dangerous if something went wrong."

After deep contemplation, Luo Chen suddenly formed a sword gesture with the fingers of his left hand and brought it down on the bare, smooth skin of his right forearm.

With a heavy slash!

*Chi...*

His skin, tough as iron, was parted like solidified fat.

With a thought, the blood sword instantly shrank, transforming into a beam of blood light that submerged into his arm.

The point of entry was precisely along the line connecting the three acupoints: Heavenly Scholar, Earthly Scholar, and Human Scholar.

Luo Chen calmly smoothed his right arm. The flesh immediately closed up, smooth as before, with only a faint bloodline flickering in and out of sight beneath the fair, delicate skin.

"By supplying it with mana from these three acupoints, I can achieve the effect of nurturing it day and night. When facing an enemy, drawing the sword will also be more convenient."

Having arranged the Yuantu Sword's resting place, Luo Chen took another strand of Red Cloud Qi from the Primordial Unity Cauldron and began to slowly refine it. Once it was refined to a suitable degree, he would integrate it into the Yuantu Sword.

...

Another month passed.

In the hazy early morning.

The Sixfold Imperial Flying Cloud Carriage came to a stop at the edge of a sea region where blue and black waters intermingled.

"Come up!"

"Oh."

Black King happily climbed onto Luo Chen's left arm, transforming into a dragon pattern coiled around the Mystic Dust Armor.

He had no intention of going near the right side. That sword was something he greatly disliked!

After settling Black King, Luo Chen walked out of the room. Tianxuan and the others had been waiting for some time.

Luo Chen unhurriedly walked to the edge of the deck and looked down at the sea surface below.

The clear water was normal seawater, while the black water was the legendary Nether Water.

It was said that this Nether Water was drawn from the Nether Abyss that protected the Nether Origin Sect. If a cultivator so much as touched it, their dharma robes and magic treasures would be defiled. Even protective qi shields would inexplicably dissipate. Moreover, Nether Water was like a clinging bone; once contaminated, it could never be fully removed.

"It looks somewhat similar to the seawater of the Fallen Sea, but its properties are completely different. One is primarily defiling, the other corrosive. The Nether Origin Sect has arranged it along the coast, so neither sea beasts nor cultivators from other continents can easily sneak in from beneath the sea surface."

Just then, a group of cultivators flew over from the shore on a flying boat.

A black flag was planted on that flying boat.

Wherever it passed, the flag shone with brilliant light, and the Nether Water scattered in all directions.

Soon, they arrived near the carriage.

"Who goes there?"

Sang Jinghe, standing behind Luo Chen, quickly stepped forward and presented their travel documents.

The young cultivator leading the squad took the jade slip and swept it with his divine sense.

"People from the Tianyuan Merchant Alliance?"

"Yes, we are merchant alliance cultivators, heading to the Eastern Wilderness to trade." Sang Jinghe replied.

The young cultivator frowned slightly. "Merchant alliance cultivators? Which family from Central Continent? Or are you Tianyuan disciples?"

Sang Jinghe was stumped.

The inspection was this strict?

Luo Chen smiled faintly. "We are neither from a Central Continent family nor Tianyuan disciples. We are Star Gate guest elders."

The young cultivator had long noticed that the true master of this carriage was this white-robed Daoist, who stood there like a sharp sword piercing the heavens, his aura so sharp it was hard to look directly at.

Now that the other party spoke, he couldn't help but narrow his eyes.

"Star Gate guest elder? Someone from Heaven-Earth Peak?"

"Yes. My name is Luo Chen."

As he spoke, an identity token flew out slowly.

The young cultivator carefully received it. It was indeed one of the identity markers that the sect had instructed them to remember.

With that, the realm of the white-robed man before him was self-evident.

"My apologies for my negligence, Senior."

This Adept cultivator's expression turned respectful. He waved the flag on his boat, and the Nether Water in the sea parted, forming a path.

Luo Chen nodded. Without any visible action, the carriage beneath him slowly flew into the opening.

At that moment, he distinctly felt a pressure being parted along with the Nether Water.

Had they also arranged a suppression-type formation here?

As Luo Chen pondered, the carriage passed by the Nether Abyss flying boat.

The earlier cultivator flew over.

"Junior Chi Zhong, greetings, True Person Luo!"

Chi Zhong? Mature and steady?

A good name.

Luo Chen nodded and asked, "Are you a disciple of the Nether Origin Sect?"

Chi Zhong replied with a hint of pride. "The True Person has keen eyes. I am indeed an inner disciple of the Nether Origin Sect, currently on patrol duty here."

It wasn't that Luo Chen had exceptional insight, but that he had seen too many disciples of sacred lands and was accustomed to the pride that unconsciously leaked from them.

Whether it was the remnants of the Primordial Demon Sect in the Northern Sea or the true disciples of the Tianyuan Dao Sect, they were all the same.

Moreover, that pride was far different from that of ordinary Sage-level superior sect disciples. It was a true arrogance that looked down on everything and feared nothing!

The respect Chi Zhong showed Luo Chen now was merely superficial.

But Luo Chen didn't mind. Instead, he asked about the others.

"Those people shouldn't be from your Nether Origin Sect, right?"

"Indeed, they are not. They are disciples of the East Sun Sect."

"The East Sun Sect, is it?"

Luo Chen murmured, a thoughtful expression on his face.

The Eastern Wilderness of old was said to have thirty-six domains, each with a Sage-level superior sect!

Each sect governed one domain, and each domain was named after the sect.

Luo Chen was most familiar with the six easternmost domains near the Million Great Mountains, but he had also heard that in the far west of the Eastern Wilderness, there was an extremely powerful sect called the East Sun Sect.

Within it, there were no fewer than five Sage True Persons!

It was a powerful superior sect that could rival the Ice Pole Sect, which Luo Chen had glimpsed in the Northern Sea.

Logically, the sun rises in the east, so a sect in the west should not be named thus.

But when viewed from outside the Eastern Wilderness, the great sun appeared to rise from the sky above the East Sun Sect.

Thus, geographically, the East Sun Sect served as the gateway to the Eastern Wilderness, overlooking Central Continent and radiating its influence to the Southern Frontier and Northern Sea.

And there was a reason why the Nether Origin Sect, a sacred land of the Ascendant stage, would entrust such a gateway to the East Sun Sect for management.

That was because the founding ancestor of the East Sun Sect was a blood brother of an Ascendant-level great power from the Nether Origin Sect. Later, the East Sun Sect's rise to prominence was largely supported by the Nether Origin Sect.

Now, it made sense that Chi Zhong, a Nether Origin Sect disciple, could command these East Sun Sect disciples on patrol. Among their peers, they were considered fellow disciples.

Understanding the reasoning, Luo Chen didn't ask further. Instead, he inquired about another matter of concern.

"Where is the Tianyuan Merchant Alliance's headquarters in the East Sun Domain located?"

Chi Zhong answered without hesitation. "In the Divine Origin Immortal City."

"Heh."

Luo Chen couldn't help but laugh. Divine Origin City again?

But soon, his smile faded.

Compared to the Tianyuan Merchant Alliance in the Northern Sea, which had established its own city, opened a port, and traded freely in all directions, here in the Eastern Wilderness, it was confined within the East Sun Domain, under the surveillance of the East Sun Sect.

From this alone, one could see the vast difference in control between the Primordial Demon Sect over the Northern Sea and the Nether Origin Sect over the Eastern Wilderness.

And that raised a question.

The Primordial Demon Sect's control over the Northern Sea was insufficient, so when facing the Northern Sea Demon Alliance's army, they had to rely solely on their sect's strength to resist the Northern Sea horde. Their defeat was understandable.

But the Nether Origin Sect's control over the Eastern Wilderness was so strong—second only to the Tianyuan Dao Sect, which completely dominated an entire continent—why, then, when facing the century-long assault of the Eastern Wilderness demon clan, had they been so slow to make a forceful response?

"Perhaps, the Eastern Wilderness I am returning to is a quagmire of trouble!"

Luo Chen sighed inwardly.

The flying boat beneath him did not stop, rapidly approaching the East Sun Port.

As he looked up, the thin mist and thick clouds suddenly parted, and a brilliant sun leaped onto the horizon.

This was a good omen!

The sun rising in the east was far better than the blood staining the stars when he had first set foot in Central Continent!

...

"Master, I feel a bit uncomfortable?"

As they stepped onto the port, Tianxuan suddenly transmitted her voice.

Luo Chen instantly found the source of Tianxuan's discomfort.

It was a bright mirror hanging high on the port's archway.

A demon-revealing mirror?

He hadn't expected the Eastern Wilderness to be so vigilant against demons. The earlier interrogation and the Nether Water barrier were one thing, but they had even set up a magic treasure at the port's entrance.

Luo Chen's expression remained calm as he reassured her. "Don't worry. With this mirror's power, it cannot see through your true form."

Tianxuan felt somewhat reassured. She controlled her mana to circulate around her demon core, trying her best not to let any leak out.

In truth, Luo Chen thought it wouldn't matter even if some did leak out.

Tianxuan had taken human form for a hundred years. Her mana was grand and upright, completely different from the sinister, demonic aura of monsters.

If it were a True Spirit Treasure Mirror like Fu Chaosheng's, it might be able to see through Tianxuan's true form. But a mere magic-treasure-level demon-revealing mirror, with no powerful figure presiding over it, could never see through Tianxuan's true body.

And even if it did, it wouldn't matter. In this day and age, which powerful cultivator didn't have a mount for transportation?

As the Sixfold Imperial Flying Cloud Carriage entered the port,

Sure enough!

The high-hanging demon-revealing mirror did nothing.

Seeing this, Chi Zhong and the others following behind also completely let down their guard.

"True Person, I still have duties to attend to, so I won't see you off. The location of Divine Origin Immortal City is marked on this jade slip. You can find your way there on your own."

As he spoke, Chi Zhong handed over two jade slips.

One was a map recording the location of Divine Origin Immortal City; the other was a travel document that had already been verified.

Luo Chen took them without examining them closely. He smiled at Chi Zhong.

"Thank you, young friend. May we meet again if fate allows."

The Sixfold Imperial Flying Cloud Carriage slowly rose, passed over the gradually bustling East Sun Port, and flew deep into the East Sun Domain.

After he left, the junior disciple beside Chi Zhong sighed in admiration. "That True Person's aura was so sharp, I couldn't even look directly at him from a distance. And you, Senior Brother Chi, could chat and laugh with him. Such a state of mind—truly worthy of a sacred land disciple!"

Chi Zhong, flattered by the praise, smiled wryly and humbly said, "It has nothing to do with me. I suspect this senior was cultivating some extraordinary secret art or method. His temperament was actually quite easy to get along with. He wasn't as frightening as you all made him out to be."

Then, he waved his hand.

"You all continue your patrol. I need to go report to Uncle Lu that a Sage True Person has arrived from Central Continent. By the way, his name was Luo Chen, right?"

"Yes, he called himself Luo Chen, and his identity was a Star Gate guest elder!"

(End of Chapter)
