# Chapter 1023: Now, You Ought to Give This Seat an Answer

"Wilderness Wanderer is the Alchemy Sect, and the Alchemy Sect is Wilderness Wanderer."

Luo Chen chuckled as he lifted his black hood and casually sat down on a chair next to the old man.

Zhou Yunshen sighed with emotion. "A lone wanderer who came from the Eastern Wilderness—it turns out you had already revealed your origins from the very beginning. I suppose that so-called Luo Hai was also just a pseudonym of yours!"

Luo Chen poured himself a cup of tea and took a sip, smiling. "A single speck of dust falling into the Northern Sea—calling himself Luo Hai isn't too far off."

With just a few words of exchange, Luo Chen had already explained away the disguises of those years past.

Zhou Yunshen watched the other man's familiar, at-ease manner, the wrinkles on his face twitching slightly.

"Rumor has it that the Alchemy Sect possesses extraordinary abilities—slaughtering countless demons upon his first return to the Eastern Wilderness, and decades ago even matching the Demon Emperor Azure Frost, who was hailed as the strongest under the Ascendant stage. I wonder if that's true or false?"

"That news should have been released by the Dao Sect's Ancestor—how could it be false?" Luo Chen wore an ambiguous smile and countered, "Or could it be that you, old friend, are still half-doubting?"

Zhou Yunshen said calmly, "Rumors are often exaggerated. This old man is nearly a thousand years old, yet my cultivation is only at the sixth layer of the Sage stage. Fellow Daoist Luo is far younger than me and attained the Dao after me, yet you've advanced so rapidly—it all seems rather dreamlike. I still remember when you left the Central Continent, your cultivation was merely at the early Sage stage..."

The words "early stage" stretched long in the old man's mouth, and what followed could no longer be sustained.

Only because an abyss-like, ocean-deep aura had begun to rise and spread faintly within the hall. Caught within it, one felt fear and sinking despair.

That aura originated from Luo Chen.

Looking closely, it was as if a majestic, blazing sun had risen, causing the old man's divine soul to tremble and his mind to quake with terror.

A dry, hoarse voice uncontrollably burst from Zhou Yunshen's lips, his murky, aged eyes filled with shock.

"Yin soul crossing into yang, Sage transforming to Ascendant—have you already reached the Ascendant stage?!"

*Shwoosh!*

All the aura, every strange phenomenon, vanished in an instant, as if nothing had ever happened.

Only Luo Chen, clad in black robes, sat there, unhurriedly savoring his spirit tea.

With the interference gone, Zhou Yunshen finally regained his normal judgment and stared intently at Luo Chen. "Not yet Ascendant, but only a single step away."

Luo Chen laughed. "Old friend still has such keen eyesight and broad knowledge. You can even see through Luo's state—gold on the outside, strong on the surface but weak within."

"This is hardly 'strong on the surface but weak within.' For a moment just now, I felt as if I were seeing the Northern Sea's great power, Li Canghai." Zhou Yunshen had abandoned any improper thoughts, and even his most basic probing intentions had vanished like smoke. He asked directly, "Fellow Daoist has quietly returned to the Central Continent—may I ask what business you have?"

Luo Chen caught the mention of a major figure in his words and raised an eyebrow. "Can't I come see an old friend without having business? After all, we were both guest elders of the Star Gate."

Zhou Yunshen shook his head. "The Star Gate is no more, Heaven-Earth Peak is broken—how can there still be talk of Star Gate guest elders?"

"But those days when we fought together against the demon race and crossed the vast oceans—Luo still remembers them clearly!" Luo Chen said with a sigh, adding, "I still recall the Coral Sea, with guests filling the seats, celebrating the eight-hundred-year birthday of the Zhou clan's ancestor—how joyous that was."

Back when Zhou Yunshen celebrated his eight-hundredth birthday, Luo Chen had personally attended, in his capacity as the Peng Lake Master of the Myriad Immortals Assembly.

Though their interactions had been few, their connections ran deep.

Even Luo Chen's former secular agent, Zhang Jiadi, had married a Zhou clan woman, establishing a cooperative relationship with the Coral Sea Zhou clan.

One could say that Luo Chen and Zhou Yunshen had already cooperated long ago.

Perhaps it was because of his advanced age, or perhaps it was the earlier conversation with his clan's juniors—now that Luo Chen had brought up the past, Zhou Yunshen couldn't help but sink into reminiscence.

But that reminiscence was quickly cast aside.

"The Coral Sea... is no more."

Luo Chen asked curiously, "Did Fellow Daoist Zhou go see it himself?"

Zhou Yunshen sighed. "A hundred years ago, when the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect marched on the Northern Sea, this old man was conscripted because of his Northern Sea origins. Naturally, I found time to go back and see the Zhou clan's birthplace. But alas, things had changed, and I could no longer see any familiar sights."

Luo Chen said thoughtfully, "The Coral Sea isn't in the core area of the Northern Sea cultivation world. For you to be able to return for a visit, it seems the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's conquest of the Northern Sea was very fast!"

Zhou Yunshen glanced at Luo Chen but did not refute his conclusion.

"Fellow Daoist Luo sees the fine details. The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect indeed nearly completely occupied the Northern Sea cultivation world."

Luo Chen was surprised. "That fast?"

He had personally experienced the vastness of the Northern Sea.

Perhaps it didn't have the continuous landmasses of the Eastern Wilderness or Western Desert, but with its countless scattered islands, even without counting the remote corners, its total area exceeded both the Eastern Wilderness and Western Desert cultivation worlds.

And it was precisely this vast area that yielded such abundant resources, unmatched in the Mountains and Seas Realm.

The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect had crossed the sea to the Northern Sea, with such a short time and without fighting on home ground—yet they had nearly conquered the entire Northern Sea?

Were the native cultivators of the Northern Sea so useless, or was the demon army so easily defeated?

Thinking of the Northern Sea great power Zhou Yunshen had mentioned earlier, Luo Chen probed, "Did Fellow Daoist personally meet the great power Li Canghai?"

Zhou Yunshen hummed in acknowledgment. "When we first arrived in the Northern Sea, the Dao Sect's War Elder brought us all to meet Great Power Li. It was precisely because of his cooperation that the Cang Hai Alliance didn't put up stubborn resistance, allowing the Dao Sect's army to conquer the Northern Sea and drive out the demons much faster."

Having said this, he couldn't help but size up Luo Chen again.

The moment the other had unfolded his primordial spirit earlier, it was almost comparable to the soul foundation that War Elder had displayed when he suppressed Great Power Li.

Of course, it was also possible that his own cultivation wasn't high enough to see through the true nature of Ascendant great powers.

But he couldn't see through Luo Chen's strength either!

In the great hall, after some idle chat and reminiscing about old times, the slightly unfamiliar atmosphere between the two had warmed up.

Zhou Yunshen hesitated for a long while before finally asking directly.

"Fellow Daoist Luo, there's no need to keep beating around the bush with this old man. If you have something to say, just say it plainly!"

Luo Chen laughed heartily and put down his teacup.

"My matter is actually quite simple. Fellow Daoist and I were both guest elders of the Star Gate—you should know about the matter of the life soul, right?"

Zhou Yunshen suddenly understood. Connecting it to the cultivation realm Luo Chen had just revealed, he grasped Luo Chen's purpose.

The other party probably wanted to retrieve his life soul and formally advance to the Ascendant stage.

But then, a thick doubt surged into his heart.

"Fellow Daoist Luo is a guest elder of the Star Gate and once helped the Five Elders of Heavenly Origin deal with the demon phoenix sisters at Soul-Breaking Cliff. You could directly apply to Ancestor Liancheng to retrieve your life soul! With your current strength, if the Dao Sect gave you their full support—no, even if they just let you meditate in seclusion in the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range for a few decades, the Ascendant stage would come naturally. Especially now that the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect is desperately short of Ascendant great powers, Ancestor Liancheng shouldn't refuse."

What people call advancing to the Ascendant stage—the world sees it as incredibly difficult, but in truth, there is only one obstacle: condensing the primordial spirit!

Many late-stage Sage grand cultivators spend their entire lives unable to condense a primordial spirit.

They can only hope for the spiritual opportunities contained within fifth-rank spirit veins, naturally absorbing them to forcibly fuse a primordial spirit, then connect with the great momentum of heaven and earth, draw vast amounts of origin qi from the ocean of origin qi, and finally consolidate the primordial spirit.

Reaching this point, one becomes what is called an Ascendant great power!

But for someone like Luo Chen, who had already condensed his primordial spirit in advance—a peerless genius—advancing to the Ascendant stage only required one final step.

This was no empty talk. The monk Xuan Du, who had also condensed his primordial spirit early, was exactly like this. His breakthrough process had almost no obstacles, completely smooth and natural.

Luo Chen should have been the same.

But his life soul was incomplete, so his primordial spirit couldn't persist for long. The final step, which should have been a mere formality, he instead dared not take.

Zhou Yunshen, old and experienced, well-traveled and knowledgeable, gave the most reasonable advice.

However!

Under his gaze, Luo Chen shook his head gravely.

"I am different from you."

Zhou Yunshen didn't understand.

Luo Chen said slowly, "You all joined the Star Gate normally and were formal guest elders. The collection of life souls also had corresponding rules and restrictions. At the appropriate time, you could apply to retrieve your life souls and advance to the Ascendant stage. But I, because my conditions weren't met, was only an external affairs guest elder."

Zhou Yunshen asked doubtfully, "If you weren't a formal guest elder, then your life soul shouldn't be in the hands of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect either, right?"

A bitter expression appeared on Luo Chen's face, and within that bitterness was even more resentment.

"Fellow Daoist Zhou, do you still remember when I escorted the Fu family Sage into the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven to participate in the grand competition and seek opportunities?"

Zhou Yunshen nodded, but then his expression changed drastically.

"Could it be... that when you entered the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, a Dao Sect great power forcibly extracted your life soul?"

Luo Chen sighed. "Luo said earlier that Fellow Daoist has keen eyesight, and it seems that's indeed true—you saw through the trick at a glance."

Though it was a compliment, to Zhou Yunshen's ears, it sounded like a death warrant that chilled him to the bone.

If the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect had treated Luo Chen like this, how could they possibly return his life soul generously, let alone help Luo Chen advance to the Ascendant stage?

It was likely that during the battle at Soul-Breaking Cliff, Luo Chen's choice to help the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect also had hidden circumstances.

And under these circumstances, Luo Chen, who had reached a bottleneck, had quietly returned to the Central Continent. His purpose went without saying.

He hadn't even notified the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, instead coming to the declining Zhou clan.

What he intended was obvious!

Zhou Yunshen didn't believe Luo Chen didn't want to break through to the Ascendant stage. Then there was only one method—retrieving his life soul by any means necessary!

And the Zhou clan was one of the means in his "any means"!

The moment he thought of the Zhou clan getting involved in a conflict between Luo Chen and the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, Zhou Yunshen's scalp went numb.

"Fellow Daoist Luo, you seem to be going too far?"

Luo Chen acted as if he hadn't heard, saying calmly, "It's very simple! Now that the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's strength has greatly diminished, with internal power struggles and endless external troubles, this is precisely when they most need to replenish their living forces."

"They've taken out thousands of years of the Dao Sect's resources to vigorously cultivate a new generation of experts, and even relaxed restrictions on Star Gate guest elders, allowing you to fully integrate into the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect."

"I don't need to appear personally. Just one clone, borrowing your Zhou clan's name, can enter the ranks of the Dao Sect's core true disciples."

"You don't need to worry about the Dao Sect's experts discovering any clues. At that time, I will completely sever the connection between the clone and my main body, handling all loose ends. With my attainments in the divine soul, as long as no great powers emerge in this world, no one can see through its true nature. Even Ascendant great powers, in a hurry, may not be able to see through the clone's essence."

"When my clone is qualified to have its life soul extracted, relying on the same-source connection of the divine soul, I can guide that portion of life soul that was taken back to return."

"This process can't be called completely unnoticed, but under the current situation, it has a very high success rate."

"Fellow Daoist Zhou..."

Zhou Yunshen's eyes were wide with fury, his entire body radiating fluctuating spiritual power as he growled, "Stop talking!"

Luo Chen raised his head, his calm, even dangerous gaze fixed on the old man with his hair and beard bristling.

"I've already finished explaining my plan."

Zhou Yunshen ground his teeth, his face flushed red. "Then should this old man accept it? Should I cooperate? If I don't accept, don't cooperate, what will you do?"

"Nothing much." Luo Chen shook his head, his tone as calm as before, without any fluctuation.

Zhou Yunshen was stunned, not understanding why Luo Chen was so composed.

This matter concerned his Ascendant Dao path! He had even explained his plan in such detail. If he refused, the other man wasn't angry, wasn't furious?

Luo Chen stood up and walked step by step to the entrance of the hall.

His deep eyes seemed to take in the vast White Heron Marsh, seeing all the Zhou clan disciples.

With his back to Zhou Yunshen, Luo Chen said softly, "If old friend doesn't agree, Luo will just find another family. I can act in secret without informing anyone. Perhaps the process will be more troublesome, but in the end, all roads lead to the same destination. But I am giving old friend an opportunity—an opportunity for the Zhou clan to rise, or rather, a way out."

Zhou Yunshen laughed in fury. "An opportunity to offend the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, or a dead end that brings destruction to the clan?"

Luo Chen murmured, "Old friend, your great limit is approaching, isn't it?"

There was no response from behind.

Luo Chen, as if unaware, gazed at the White Heron Marsh filled with countless miasmas and said to himself, "If you go, can the Zhou clan keep this White Heron Marsh? If you go, with the strength of those three Adept cultivators, will they ever have the chance to touch a fourth-rank spirit vein and break through to the Sage stage? Among this generation of clansmen, is there anyone with outstanding talent and lofty character who can lead the clan to restore the glory of the Coral Sea Zhou clan after your passing?"

Behind him, the ancient, withered old man's body went limp, his thin frame nearly collapsing into the wide chair.

He stared at the tall man at the entrance. In the light tinged with a little dust, the man's silhouette was both evil and holy.

Luo Chen turned around. Though he was at the lower end of the hall, his perspective looking up at Zhou Yunshen was like looking down from above.

He spoke from on high: "If you accept, the Zhou clan will produce a peerless genius, and countless clansmen will benefit. If you cooperate, there will be fewer variables, and the Zhou clan will be safer. Even if things don't work out, with my strength that roams the five continents and four seas, it will be enough to lead the Zhou clan to retreat to the Eastern Wilderness and preserve them. You don't need to worry—the Dao Sect's great powers absolutely dare not set foot in the Eastern Wilderness!"

"Now, you ought to give this seat an answer."

Facing Luo Chen's all-devouring eyes, Zhou Yunshen slowly closed his own.

"I... agree."

Those three words seemed to drain all of Zhou Yunshen's strength.

Luo Chen smiled faintly, a smile filled with the power to control everything.

"Trust me, you won't regret this decision."

(Chapter end)
