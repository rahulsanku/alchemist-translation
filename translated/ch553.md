# Chapter 553: Not a Bone Left, a Hidden Mastermind

What could a lone remnant soul of a cultivator accomplish in the physical world?

A Qi Refinement fool — ignorant and without wisdom — might, if it clung to the mortal realm, do nothing more than haunt troubled dreams.

A true Initiate cultivator, even stripped of clear reasoning, might preserve the instinct of cultivation, accumulate yin qi, and become a Ghost General.

Only an Adept who had condensed the Golden Core — who possessed a divine sense, however fragmented — could find a path back to the Great Dao as a ghost cultivator, even from nothing more than a single remnant soul.

And yet even those who made that journey required a long time and favorable circumstances before a ghost king born of an Adept could return to the right path.

A Sage Sage was another matter entirely.

Even if their Five Qi dispersed, their Three Flowers atop the head were extinguished, and their Nascent Soul shattered, the lone remnant soul remaining still possessed the essence of a Nascent Soul.

With that remnant, a Sage could reach into the physical world and execute all manner of powerful techniques.

It fell short of their living strength, of course — but against an ordinary Adept cultivator, the advantage remained overwhelming.

And right now —

With Han Zhan's help, Luo Chen's Water Mirror technique stabilized in an instant.

Even as demon qi surged overhead and spiritual power churned in chaos across the battlefield, the mirror displayed a seven-hundred-li panorama of Xuanyan Island in clear and vivid detail.

The images were small, hard to make out with the naked eye.

But this was Luo Chen's own spell, his divine sense woven through it — he could focus his attention on whichever portions of the battlefield interested him.

Soon, the clashes between third-tier demon kings were being magnified one by one.

"The Ring-Headed Turtle clan truly runs deep. Even after years of war, their stronghold still holds over thirty third-tier beings."

Luo Chen watched with something between surprise and genuine awe.

They were turtles — and turtles were famous for their longevity. Centuries of quiet accumulation had produced a frightening foundation.

A strength like this, placed among human cultivation sects, would mark them as the most elite Adept-level power. Even some recently-elevated Sage upper sects, minus their newly-achieved Sage Sages, might not compare favorably.

Consider the Falling Cloud Sect.

At its peak, they had barely over ten Adept cultivators.

That a figure like Han Zhan — a genuine Sage Sage — had ever emerged from their ranks was the product of countless generations of unbroken sacrifice and effort.

*I wonder what the Falling Cloud Sect looks like now, after Han Zhan's "death"?*

He shook the thought away and returned his focus to the mirror.

His prey was in there somewhere.

As he observed, he formed a rough picture of both sides' overall strength.

On the whole, the demon crabs held an absolute advantage.

However deep the Ring-Headed Turtles' foundations ran, the five royal crab clans fielded a crushing numerical superiority at the high levels.

On the battlefield now, even without the five patriarchs committing personally, the crab clans' gathered elites were pressing the Ring-Headed Turtle third-tier demon kings hard from every side.

Mostly through sheer numbers.

Beyond that, the count of third-tier late-stage great demon kings among the five royal clans exceeded what Luo Chen had expected.

The Overlord Crab clan had four great demon kings. The Red Rock Crabs and Demon Spider Crabs each had three.

The Nine-Clawed Poison King Crabs and Azure Emperor Crabs were considerably weaker — between them only two great demon kings. Subtract the patriarchs, who held back, and each clan had only one free-acting great demon king to deploy.

Yet even so, the sheer concentration of great demon kings in the Xuanyan Sea Region left Luo Chen quietly staggered.

If each crab clan were compared to a human Adept-level great sect, the distribution of powerhouses would be genuinely overwhelming by human cultivation standards.

Take the Jade Cauldron Domain for example: the Nine Adepts of the Ice Fortress had only a single great cultivator among them — Great Master Canglang.

The Falling Cloud Sect had only Great Master Cheng Yan. The Aihao Mountains and the Hundred Blossoms Palace had none at all.

Even Green Pill Valley, which had the most Adepts of any sect — counting carefully, only the Grand Elder Azure Pill and the Dragon's Head Peak master at Battle Hall held late-stage Adept status.

"No wonder the Northern Sea still earns the name Demon Sea even when the Primordial Demon Sect once held sway — a realm where man and demon lived side by side."

"Looking at it now, a single Xuanyan Sea Region alone hides this many dragons and tigers."

"Across the vast Northern Sea, across its countless sea regions — who knows how many supreme great demons lie dormant? How could human cultivators ever truly conquer all that territory? Now that the Primordial Demon Sect is destroyed, once the demon races have a free hand, their counteroffensive will set this realm ablaze again."

He set aside his contemplations and went back to scanning the mirror for a worthy target.

Because the demon crabs were pressing the Ring-Headed Turtles so relentlessly, the overall situation, chaotic as it was, moved within a rough order — and that, ironically, worked against Luo Chen.

But he hadn't given up.

*Cornered against death, no one will accept slow defeat. They'll go down swinging.*

Luo Chen had been in enough great battles over the years.

He knew exactly how desperate an enemy could become when pushed to the brink.

And this was a war of extinction.

Not even a stick of incense's worth of time passed before the first Ring-Headed Turtle third-tier demon king fell.

But Luo Chen still didn't move.

He was waiting for a signal.

Then — suddenly —

His divine sense locked onto one spot in the mirror. The image began expanding. The demon qi fluctuations within it grew wilder, wilder — and touched something suicidal.

In the image.

Under the coordinated assault of three demon kings, a Ring-Headed Turtle the size of a palace hall suddenly blazed red in both eyes.

Its overwhelming demon qi folded inward, all of it drawn back into the body.

That vast frame began to expand.

Then —

*BOOM.*

Blood-light spread outward like a wave. A violent detonation shook Xuanyan Island.

The three demon kings attacking it screamed and reeled backward.

Everyone watching knew what had just happened.

A third-tier demon king had chosen to self-destruct and drag its enemies into the abyss.

In the swamp, Luo Chen felt a surge of respect for the demon king's resolve — even as he steadied the rippling mirror with his mana.

His signal had come.

"Senior Han, I'll need you to watch the mirror for me from here and transmit instructions via divine sense as I move."

Luo Chen pressed one hand across the mirror's surface, fixing the nearby battlefield terrain in memory.

From the Myriad Souls Banner drifted a figure three inches tall — a translucent, ghostly elder.

Han Zhan.

What Luo Chen intended was to move personally through the battlefield and hunt demon kings, while Han Zhan monitored the situation from here and kept him updated in real time.

With that kind of inside-outside cooperation, he could pick chestnuts out of the fire.

Han Zhan looked at Luo Chen with resignation. "Go on. I'll let you know if anything comes up."

Luo Chen gave a quiet smile, slipped back inside the second-tier demon crab puppet, and drove it silently out of the swamp.

...

Once the signal was given, others followed.

To protect their home. To defend the land they lived on. With no ground left to yield.

The Ring-Headed Turtles' most powerful fighters cast aside every last reservation.

When a third-tier early-stage Ring-Headed Turtle was surrounded by two powerful enemies, gravely wounded with no chance of turning the tide, it chose to take them with it.

"Come to hell with me!"

With one roar, its demon core contracted and released. The demon power built across a lifetime ignited in a thunderous detonation.

The shock wave of that self-destruction swept across a hundred li in an instant.

Demon beasts of all tiers caught in its radius — whether from the five royal crab clans or their own Ring-Headed Turtle kin — had no chance of survival.

The two Azure Emperor Crab demon kings at the heart of the exchange, even as they retreated with frantic speed, were still swallowed by the enormous demon qi shockwave.

No hesitation.

Both demon kings deployed their techniques simultaneously. One flipped its shell like a shield; the other spat qi to generate a bubble, encasing itself within.

Endless waves of force rolled and churned.

Blood mist and smoke spread across the battlefield, turning that stretch of ground into something indistinguishable from hell.

Through the haze, a third-tier Azure Emperor Crab tumbled and rolled across the ground.

Her bubble burst, re-formed, burst again.

Over and over she spat new bubbles, maintaining her outer defense.

Seeing the worst of the self-detonation's shockwave finally pass, she let out a quiet breath of relief.

*Why do these creatures have to be so extreme? Wouldn't it be better to surrender and become a vassal?*

*In the early days, we demon crabs were vassals to the Ring-Headed Turtle clan ourselves.*

*Dead now. I hope Fifth Brother's side held up. Let him at least come through with his life.*

Even as thoughts churned in her mind, the bubble before her burst again.

Without thinking, her demon power surged and she opened her jaws — ready to spit another defensive bubble.

But something unexpected intervened.

A hand, five fingers curled like talons, reached into the half-formed bubble.

One sharp claw drove into her soft underbelly.

When it withdrew, a brilliantly blue demon core sat in the palm.

It had happened too fast for her to react.

But she would never misidentify that demon core.

That was the crystallization of several hundred years of bitter cultivation.

A silhouette materialized before her eyes, and she opened her mouth to call for help — but met his gaze, and felt her mind cloud.

The moon reflected in the sea, inverted. Flowers bloomed and wilted in the heart.

By the time clarity returned, body and head were parted, and her demon core was sealed.

One clean strike. Luo Chen blinked, swept his wide sleeve, and the enormous crab carcass vanished instantly.

He himself sank underground, diving into the hardened shell of the Primordial Unity Cauldron — Concealment Veil Formation activated fully to mask his presence.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron, a high-grade magic treasure in classification — yet forged of material that was incomparably hard.

As both Heaven Smelter and Han Zhan had once described it: fully tempered, it functioned as half a true artifact.

Though it possessed no offensive or defensive power itself, its raw material quality made it serviceable as a defensive treasure by nature alone.

With it, Luo Chen could endure a demon king's self-detonation head-on.

The shockwave outside gradually subsided.

A figure shot skyward, its aura dim and hollow.

It rose into the air and called out repeatedly, but no answer came.

It circled and circled, sweeping every inch of ground and sky, finding nothing.

Finally, it departed that stretch of battlefield in exhaustion.

For a clan's future, some losses were unavoidable — yet when this sort of loss touched one's own little sister, it was no easier to accept.

Inside the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Luo Chen monitored the situation outside. Once the heavily wounded demon king had withdrawn, he let out a breath.

He hadn't expected both Azure Emperor Crab demon kings to be capable enough to survive a peer-level self-detonation at the same time.

He had anticipated the other one surviving — that one had clearly been no weakling.

But his own target had survived too.

Thinking back on the bubbles she had spat, that defensive demon art was genuinely unusual.

Pity that, with his ambush added to the equation, it had not been enough.

Cloud-Seizing Divine Claw and Mirror Flower Water Moon released in tandem — even a complete and uninjured third-tier late-stage great demon king would not have been beyond Luo Chen's reach, let alone a third-tier early-stage demon king already clinging to life through the self-detonation's aftermath.

Her fall had been inevitable.

"The first one."

Luo Chen murmured.

Then his expression shifted — a divine sense transmission entered his mind.

"Fifty li to the southeast?"

"Close."

In the enormous crater blasted out by the detonation, earth churned and tumbled.

A demon crab burrowed up from underground.

It glanced around to orient itself, then spread its eight legs and moved off at speed.

Under normal circumstances, such motion would attract attention.

But in the current battlefield, that pace barely stood out at all.

Shortly, the demon crab arrived at a new stretch of fighting.

Skirting a few clusters of battle, Luo Chen crouched behind a massive boulder and looked skyward.

Above, four demon kings were locked in fierce combat.

Three against one — the scales tipping steadily.

"Up in the air, is it?"

"This time it's a bit more troublesome."

...

The sun rose and set, the stars wheeled overhead.

The decisive battle on Xuanyan Island, driven by the demon crabs' relentless advance, had taken on an unstoppable momentum.

Victory was now only a matter of time.

Yet the advance had come at a steep price for the five royal crab clans.

Out on the open sea, five-color shells drifted in the water.

The five patriarchs waited with expectant eyes.

Demon crabs moved in and out, reporting the state of the battlefield.

"Patriarch, the Ring-Headed Turtles have gone mad."

"When offered terms of surrender, they refused to the death. Some have begun widespread self-detonation, inflicting enormous casualties on our forces."

"We haven't counted precisely, but the casualties..."

As they listened to the reports, the five patriarchs' expressions grew grave.

Jin Hun, at their head, worked to stay calm behind his serious expression.

"No matter. This was within expectations. If the positions were reversed, we would do much the same. Continue the advance — we must push into the island's center. Qihuan and Hailo have yet to make their move."

Qihuan. Hailo. The Ring-Headed Turtle clan's two supreme powerhouses.

Both cultivators of immense longevity and strength — each capable of fighting any of the five individually and coming out ahead.

It was precisely why the five patriarchs, as the highest combat power available, had deliberately held back from the island.

The messenger below answered quickly.

But before departing, he hesitated and added: "Reporting to the patriarch — Lord Golden Armor has noticed something wrong."

"What's wrong?" Jin Hun frowned.

"Our third-tier clansmen's losses have exceeded projections. Beyond that — after they fall, their bodies can't be found. This strange occurrence has happened more than once."

Jin Hun's eyes narrowed, his expression uncertain.

*The bodies are disappearing again?*

His gaze swept across the other four patriarchs.

"And on your sides?"

"The same."

The other four answered.

One or two might have been explainable — but over this past year, nearly ten third-tier demon crab kings had vanished without a trace.

The leadership could hardly be indifferent to something this strange.

Zhu Xiang of the Demon Spider Crabs suddenly spoke: "Intelligence from yesterday: a human cultivator appeared on Xuanyan Island. He was identified as Gu Shaoshang — the one who left earlier."

"He came back?"

"Returned after leaving — what is he after?"

"He probably came back long before this." Zhu Xiang said slowly. "The disappearing bodies aren't only on our side. A number of the Ring-Headed Turtles' fallen warriors are missing too."

When put that way, the otherwise disconnected oddities began to connect.

Gu Shaoshang returning and quietly collecting Ring-Headed Turtle corpses from the battlefield — that was entirely plausible.

And picking up some third-tier demon crab bodies while he was at it would seem quite natural by human cultivator logic.

But for the five royal demon crab clans, that was not something they could accept.

Red Flame's temper ignited. "Humans — treacherous and vile, exactly as expected!"

"Is he still on the island?" Nine Claw demanded furiously. "If so, I'd have no objection to going after him personally."

Zhu Xiang shook his head and shared the intelligence: Gu Shaoshang had already left Xuanyan Island.

"Good that he left — otherwise if I'd caught him..."

"Wait."

Jin Hun straightened suddenly.

"If he's already gone — then why are corpses still vanishing from the Xuanyan Island battlefield?"

The demons stared at each other.

A suspicion surfaced in every mind.

*Could there be another mastermind working in the shadows?*

Even as they turned the thought over, Zhu Xiang's expression shifted suddenly.

A moment later, almost in the same instant, the other four's expressions changed too.

"Hailo has finally made his move."

*(End of Chapter)*
