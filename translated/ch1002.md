# Chapter 1002: Luo Tian Grand Elder, Overwhelming the Ghosts

Unlike the Withering-Bloom True Intent, the Fire Origin was merely the most fundamental law intent within the laws of fire.

Therefore, ever since comprehending the Fire Origin, Luo Chen had never grasped any related powerful divine abilities.

But that did not mean the Fire Origin provided no enhancement to his strength!

His mana became increasingly overbearing and vigorous, at one point clashing on equal footing with Azure Frost's mana, which contained the true intent of spatial laws.

His control over all kinds of flames also became increasingly effortless. The fact that he could survive in the Yellow Spring Sea and massively drive the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame was clear proof.

And there was another point—the enhancement effect of the Fire Origin on ordinary spells!

Back when he had first comprehended the Dao, Luo Chen had discovered this special characteristic.

The ordinary fire spells he now released received tremendous enhancement, their power effectively doubled.

In particular, spells like Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain and Flame Dragon Descends—both fourth-rank fire spells he had cultivated to the Great Perfection level—when augmented with the Fire Origin, their power was infinitely approaching that of divine abilities.

Thus, a single spell had swept across the Six Ghost Emperors!

However, that earlier Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain, after charging up Yama Floating Mountain, had failed to kill the last Ghost Emperor and was instead broken by someone.

Even a greatly weakened Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain should not have been so easily broken.

Therefore, that Great Ghost Emperor was no ordinary being!

"It's a Wang Ghost!" A startled cry came from behind.

Luo Chen followed the sound and saw Bai Meiling's face filled with shock.

Understanding his look, she immediately explained, "Among us ghost cultivators, there are those of extreme power, known as the Chi, Mei, Wang, and Liang. They are innate ghost bodies cultivated postnatally. Such existences are no longer afraid of ordinary supreme yang and supreme steel power; they can be said to be no different from normal cultivators with physical bodies. Hence, their fame spreads far and wide, even reaching the mortal world with related legends. And that person just now was clearly the Wang Ghost among the Chi, Mei, Wang, and Liang!"

Luo Chen asked curiously, "How do you distinguish the Chi, Mei, Wang, and Liang?"

"The Chi Ghost has a human face and beast body. The Mei Ghost takes the form of tree spirits, plants, and grasses. The Wang Ghost is the essence of dark water. The Liang Ghost resembles a young child." Bai Meiling briefly explained, then added, "That person earlier was surrounded by powerful water vapor, his body condensed from Dark Water True Essence. The filth of Dark Water is such that even great cultivators like you, Master, would avoid it at all costs. Yet he could use it to condense a body—he must be the Wang Ghost among the Chi, Mei, Wang, and Liang!"

With this explanation, everyone was no longer confused.

Luo Chen also showed a thoughtful expression, understanding what means the other had used to break his Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain.

If it was a ghost body condensed from Dark Water True Essence, with water and fire opposing each other, and the fire spell weakened, it could indeed be calmly resolved.

"Master, such an existence will be hard to deal with! Moreover, I can sense that there are no small number of powerful Ghost Emperors up there. If there are more existences comparable to the Wang Ghost among them, with our current strength..." Bai Meiling said worriedly.

She didn't finish her sentence, but the fear in her tone was clear to all.

For a moment, everyone looked at Luo Chen.

Deng Taiyue hesitated for a moment, then still said, "Yama Floating Mountain is right here, it won't run away. Why don't we go back first and discuss this at length? At worst, we can go to the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world and invite a few friends to come back and seize the treasure."

This was a safe approach, and everyone quite agreed.

However!

Luo Chen laughed.

He stepped forward, walked to the tiger's head, and gazed into the distance above.

"Everyone has suffered considerable losses along this journey, so if a battle breaks out later, there will likely be casualties. You all don't need to make a move. Brother Deng, you are still in good condition; just protect my disciples a little. As for the rest... leave it to me!"

Deng Taiyue was stunned.

The others were also dumbfounded, almost thinking they had misheard.

There weren't just one or two Ghost Emperors up there—the number was at least twenty, or even over thirty.

The vast Nether Abyss had probably gathered half of all fourth-rank Ghost Emperors here.

And Luo Chen wanted to face them alone?

Wasn't that too arrogant?

Deng Taiyue swallowed his saliva. He had witnessed Azure Frost's methods. That one, known as the number one under the Ascendant level, had indeed displayed strength worthy of that reputation.

But even if Azure Frost were present, she might not come out unscathed.

Even if the rumors that Luo Chen and Azure Frost had fought to a standstill were true, he couldn't surpass her by much. What gave him such confidence?

"Xiao Bai, give me the Soul-Refining Banner!"

Luo Chen reached out his hand, and the banner that had absorbed a hundred thousand souls and already advanced to the Fierce Soul Banner level fell into his hands.

He flicked his fingers several times, striking a few seals onto the banner.

In this way, without destroying Bai Meiling's imprint, Luo Chen, as the earliest owner of the soul banner, could also use it.

"Follow me up!"

As Luo Chen spoke, he broke through the clouds and stepped onto the white jade square.

Everyone sat on the White Tiger Puppet and closely followed, flying up as well.

*Thump!*

The four feet landed, producing a heavy sound.

The first thing to enter everyone's sight was naturally the three majestic and ancient palaces.

Heaven Nether, Heaven Abyss, Heaven Marsh—these were the three great Yama Floating Heavenly Palaces.

And at the Heaven Nether Palace, where the former Nether Origin Sect Master, Yu Ming Ancestor, had once resided, a large number of ghost shadows were gathered.

Vague and indistinct, there were almost no less than thirty of them.

At this moment, all the ghostly beings cast their gloomy gazes over.

A pressure that was almost tangible surged toward them.

The weaker ones, like Min Longyu, a Core Formation cultivator, had almost no resistance and collapsed onto the puppet's back with a *plop*.

"Brother Deng?"

Luo Chen called from ahead.

Deng Taiyue snapped out of his daze and immediately extended the Xuan Huang Great Seal, transforming it into a light curtain that covered everyone.

"Apologies, I was careless!"

Deng Taiyue took a deep breath, his heart filled with shock.

Those ghostly beings were truly targeting Luo Chen, who walked alone at the front.

The gazes cast toward them were merely a fraction.

Yet just that fraction of attention had caused them to lose their composure. He himself was still somewhat alright, but Ling Fengzi and the others were even more pathetic, their faces pale and eyes vacant.

As for Canglong Fairy, whom he had thought was the weakest, she quickly woke up after a momentary daze.

"Her soul is not weak!"

That was Deng Taiyue's assessment of Canglong.

But!

Compared to Luo Chen, who was bearing the brunt at the front, Canglong's soul foundation was completely inadequate.

"A brief glance at the foot of the mountain earlier made me feel that Luo Chen's soul foundation was extremely deep. Now looking again, it's indeed true. Even deeper than I imagined—vast as the sea!"

"Could it be that he has already condensed his primordial spirit?"

A thought suddenly emerged in Deng Taiyue's mind.

But in an instant, he shook his head and dismissed it.

The primordial spirit—divine sense transforms into thoughts, one thought being a myriad of divine sense components, extremely condensed, extremely terrifying.

Above, it can control billions of heaven and earth origin qi; below, it can reach the human heart and discern truth from falsehood.

Luo Chen's soul was strong, but it had not yet reached that level.

Yet precisely because of this, Deng Taiyue was even more shocked by the depth of Luo Chen's soul foundation.

Just then.

The ghost emperors gathered near the Heaven Nether Palace stirred.

Several attacks were suddenly launched.

Some transformed into dense ghostly hands, some unfolded layers of dark clouds, and there were also shrill sonic waves surging like tidal waves.

But facing these attacks, the man in feathered robes stepped with mysterious footwork, dodging one after another, truly not a single leaf touching his body.

Occasionally, one would hit, but when it landed, it still missed, as if there was some deviation.

Even the shrill sonic waves, upon reaching him, seemed like mud cows entering the sea or a gentle breeze brushing his face, causing him no effect.

Such a peculiar scene caused an uproar among the ghost cultivators.

Watching Luo Chen walk step by step without any sign of stopping, a series of questions were thrown out.

"Who are you?"

"This is a place my Nether ghost cultivators have set their sights on first. Human cultivator, retreat quickly!"

"Don't come any closer, or don't blame us for being rude!"

Luo Chen looked up. Although he was looking up, for some reason, it gave the feeling of looking down on all the ghost cultivators.

"I am the former master of the Nether Origin Pill Saint Hall, now the Grand Elder of the Luo Tian Sect, Luo Chen! This Yama Floating Mountain should rightfully belong to me. You all have broken into my home, yet you come to interrogate me?"

A disciple of the Nether Origin Sect?

The master of the Pill Saint Hall?

The ghost cultivators looked at each other, mostly disbelieving.

But among them, a few powerful ghost emperors showed half-believing expressions.

They had once devoured some newly transformed ghosts of dead Nether Origin Sages and extracted some memory fragments from them.

It seemed there was indeed such a person.

But now, with heavy treasures before them, how could they willingly give them up?

"Kill him!"

No one knew who said it first, but then came a chorus of agreement.

Several figures charged down from above, straight at Luo Chen.

This was the simplest and most direct attack method of ghost cultivators.

Using the ghost body to pounce and kill could extract the souls from living beings.

Facing such an attack, Luo Chen's steps did not stop, and he did not even use spells or magic treasures, letting them rush to close range.

Such arrogant behavior made the ghost cultivators sneer coldly.

But the next moment, a scene that shocked all the ghosts appeared.

Those few figures, upon reaching Luo Chen, actually froze in place, not moving at all.

Then they saw him unfold a black banner, casually wield it, and directly collect the five ghost emperors inside.

"This..."

"How is it possible?"

"It's an illusion technique!"

A deep voice sounded from within, revealing the essence of Luo Chen's method in one sentence.

Now the ghosts looked and suddenly discovered Luo Chen's eyes, which were emitting dazzling golden light.

Just a single glance from afar made their minds waver, almost uncontrollable, and boundless illusions suddenly arose.

They quickly lowered their heads, calmed their hearts, and gradually broke free from those hallucinations.

"Do not look into his eyes! The illusion technique driven by those eyes is beyond your ability to resist!"

Again, that deep voice reminded them.

On the square, Luo Chen, who had just collected five ghost emperors, turned his gaze toward the ghosts.

None dared to meet his eyes!

And deep within the crowd, a pair of weathered, murky eyes were quietly watching him.

When their gazes met, Mirror Flower Water Moon unfolded again!

That pair of weathered, murky eyes suddenly became dull, but in a moment, they cleared again.

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow, seeing through the old ghost emperor's entire being, and gradually understood in his heart.

"One body, two souls—Mother-Child Ghost Fiend. No wonder you're not affected by my illusion technique."

At the same time, another voice sounded beside the old woman.

"Real Person Luo, your soul is the strongest I have ever seen. But to drive such an illusion technique, the consumption of mana and soul must be extremely high. Before your mana runs out, you may not necessarily be able to kill all of us."

Luo Chen followed the sound and saw a delicate, slender figure hiding under a red paper umbrella.

"What is your name?"

"Phantom Nether Maiden."

The red paper umbrella lifted slightly, and a pair of autumn-water eyes met Luo Chen's gaze for a moment, then shyly lowered their eyelids, lowering the paper fan.

Although it was only a fleeting glance, the breathtaking beauty revealed in that instant was enough to make one unable to extricate themselves.

Luo Chen was unaffected—mere pink skeletons, where was the stunning beauty?

His gaze swept over the Mother-Child Ghost Fiend, the Phantom Nether Maiden, and two other figures hidden deep in the crowd whose auras rivaled great cultivators, and he already had a count in his heart.

Luo Chen asked loudly, "What do you want?"

The Phantom Nether Maiden said coquettishly, "Simple! The Nether Origin is destroyed; your identity doesn't work here. We are all the same. Why not wait quietly for the Heaven Nether Palace to open, and when the Dark Water Spring Eye is completely condensed, everyone can rely on their own abilities. How about that?"

Faced with this proposal, Luo Chen dismissed it with contempt.

"The same? What makes you think you can sit on equal footing with this sect?"

"Real Person Luo, you are too arrogant!" the Mother-Child Ghost Fiend said heavily.

"Arrogant, and even more confident!"

Luo Chen spoke arrogantly, his feathered robe sleeves moving without wind, rustling loudly.

A vast aura, unrestrained, surged into the sky.

Not only that, but a Sage domain spread out from him, rolling forward. The Sen Luo Fire Domain reappeared in the world.

He actually intended to use his own strength to fight dozens of same-rank ghost emperors alone!

Such a arrogant posture enraged the ghosts.

One by one, ghost domains—the Sage domains unique to ghost cultivators—were simultaneously released.

This kind of domain field could only be possessed by ghost cultivators at the Ghost King level.

But to truly master it, one had to be a fourth-rank Ghost Emperor to wield it as easily as an arm.

A large number of ghost domains emerged, wanting to resist the Sen Luo Fire Prison, but because they were too close, they conflicted with each other, actually weakening their power by three parts.

Not only did they fail to break through the Sen Luo Fire Domain, but they instead felt immense pressure.

Taking this opportunity, Luo Chen directly stepped forward.

*Boom!*

The void exploded with a roar.

Luo Chen, like a tiger entering a flock of sheep, slapped out with a large palm, true flames following.

Strands of cyan flames coiled around every move and stance. Any ghost emperor that was even slightly touched let out screams.

"Careful!"

"This flame is strange! It contains terrifying vitality that will consume our ghost qi."

"Spread out quickly!"

The group of ghost emperors quickly scattered, simultaneously pulling away from Luo Chen.

But this scattering fell right into Luo Chen's plan.

Both hands simultaneously formed seals, mana flowing like water.

The Flame Dragon and Fire Phoenix appeared almost at the same time, sweeping the surroundings with overwhelming force.

The ghost emperors all made moves to resist, but with little effect.

Luo Chen snorted lightly, not surprised by this scene.

Although the number of ghost emperors here was roughly equivalent to the monks sent by the Hanging Temple, they were nothing but a heap of loose sand.

Moreover, they had been suppressed by the Nether Abyss for a thousand years, and most of their protective magic treasures had been corrupted by the Dark Water.

The only thing worth mentioning was their purest soul foundation.

But his own soul was more than ten times that of a great cultivator in quantity. One-on-one, he was not afraid at all.

Even one against many, he had a tremendous advantage.

When a sky-covering red umbrella descended from above, Luo Chen backhandedly scattered a sword.

*Rip!*

A huge crack split open in the middle of the red umbrella.

The Phantom Nether Maiden's eyes were filled with shock as she repeatedly retreated.

There was also a soul sound like weeping and complaining attacking Luo Chen's sea of consciousness, but it was blocked outside, unable to disturb his mind at all. The Mother-Child Ghost Fiend instead suffered the backlash of the Prison Soul Art, and its form showed signs of collapse.

From left and right, ghost palms descended and chains came lassoing.

Luo Chen did not dodge or evade. His whole body erupted with sword qi, transforming into a sword qi storm that dissolved the two powerful attacks into nothing.

Holding the Yuantu Sword, he looked contemptuously at the group of ghost emperors.

"A rabble of motley crew, how dare you block the chariot with your mantis arms?"

In an instant, everyone was shocked by Luo Chen.

Just then!

*Creak! Creak! Creak!*

Everyone suddenly turned their heads. The door of the Heaven Nether Palace, which had been sealed for decades, was slowly opening.

When it had just revealed a crack, a dark light rushed in before everyone else.

The Phantom Nether Maiden recognized that fellow and screamed sharply, "Wang Ghost Emperor!"

It was the great ghost emperor who had fought from a distance with Luo Chen and the others earlier.

(End of Chapter)
