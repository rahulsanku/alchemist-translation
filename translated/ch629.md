# Chapter 629: In This Life, One Must Always Take One Risk

"The ancient cultivator's exact origin is unknown, but one point is widely accepted: his sitting-death time was roughly a thousand years ago."

"Why is there this time point? Could Fellow Daoist Luo perhaps not have heard the saying that the Sunken Sea is born once every thousand years?"

"Although the Sunken Sea was also very dangerous in the past, that danger came only from the sea region's own geographical environment. It was far from the current intensity of being called the place where all things sink and demon spirits cannot enter. The change within originated from the methods the ancient cultivator arranged before sitting in death, causing the Sunken Sea to undergo an earth-shaking transformation."

"The ruin's opening time is not fixed, but over many years, under the observation of Primordial Demon Sect powerhouses, its patterns were gradually found."

"Before the ruin opens, celestial phenomena will be born over the Sunken Sea region. Only when the ruin completely closes will the celestial phenomena disappear. This process can last as short as one or two years, and as long as forty or fifty years."

"According to Primordial Demon Sect cultivators' deduction, that ruin should be the ancient cultivator's portable traveling palace, arranged with a spirit-absorbing grand formation. When it opens by itself without anyone controlling it, the powerful spirit-absorbing disturbance creates obvious celestial phenomena."

"You ask what kind of traveling palace, what kind of grand formation can create such a disturbance? Haha, Ding does not know either. But many people speculate that the ruin's owner must have been a great formation-path power in life. One piece of evidence is that after the Canghai Alliance's great power, surnamed Li, came out of the ruin alive, he advanced to Ascendant through formation-path laws."

"According to Primordial Demon Sect cultivators' calculations, the next opening of the ruin should be soon. The specific time still depends on when the celestial phenomena are born."

"However, putting aside the situation inside the ruin for now, we first have to consider how to cross the Sunken Sea. On this point, we must mention the legend that the Sunken Sea is known as the place of return-to-void."

...

Inside Star Dou Hall.

Luo Chen sat cross-legged with eyes closed, his mind constantly savoring the conversation among the three in Moya Cave.

Most of it had been said by Ding Yi.

At the beginning, Luo Chen did not quite believe its truthfulness. After all, Ding Yi's every sentence was "Primordial Demon Sect research," "Primordial Demon Sect deduction," "Primordial Demon Sect powerhouses"... Ding Yi was not a member of the Primordial Demon Sect, so how could he know so much information?

Only after Ding Yi took out a Golden Core to dispel his misgivings did he believe.

That was the Golden Core of a Primordial Demon Sect Golden Core cultivator!

After the Primordial Demon Sect was destroyed, Ding Yi captured him and used secret methods to ask out the information he needed. Ding Yi had even personally made a trip to the Sunken Sea to investigate on site, only then confirming the truthfulness of the intelligence.

According to Ding Yi, that piece of soul-nourishing wood tainted by foul qi, as well as his body of fat, were both thanks to that trip to the Sunken Sea.

Since the intelligence was true and reliable, Luo Chen grew increasingly interested in the ancient cultivator ruin.

But before that, just as Ding Yi had said, he had to consider how to safely cross the Sunken Sea.

As for the method?

Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes. His fingers brushed across his clothing, and a strange smile appeared.

"Is there truly such a coincidence in the world?"

The Sunken Sea was now known as the place where all things sank and demon spirits could not enter.

But this was after its thousand-year birth.

Before a thousand years ago, the Sunken Sea was in truth the place of return-to-void.

Of course, the so-called "return-to-void" was the place in myths and legends where all things returned and transformed into nothingness. A small Sunken Sea did not deserve such a name.

The reason it was known as a place of return-to-void was actually because of a homophone.

"Return-to-void" and "turtle ruins"!

In the Mountains and Seas Realm, during the distant ancient wilderness, archaic beasts ran rampant and great demons appeared everywhere. Among them, the black turtle race were the longest-lived existences.

Once their lifespans ended, these beings would actively seek out a safe place as their burial ground.

The Sunken Sea was the most famous black turtle burial ground.

To prevent other existences from targeting their remains, the black turtle race arranged many methods, making it so those without black turtle bloodline could not step into the depths of the Sunken Sea.

Therefore, to step into the depths of the Sunken Sea, one had to falsely refine black turtle bloodline into one's body, or seek corresponding secret treasures.

Ding Yi claimed that he had already refined a secret treasure possessing black turtle bloodline, and this time he would surely be able to enter the depths of the Sunken Sea.

The Moya Cave Master was proficient in artifact-forging and had a wide network of friends, so naturally there was no issue there.

On Luo Chen's side, by coincidence, he had just refined a genuine Xuanchen Armor that possessed black turtle bloodline!

"No, perhaps it is not a coincidence!"

Suddenly, Luo Chen remembered some people and matters.

In the Xuanyan Sea Region, apart from him, who had gone to hunt demon crabs, there had also been a group of demon hunters.

And what those people had wanted was not demon crabs, but the Ring-Headed Turtle race that possessed black turtle bloodline.

The Sage force Feiyun Ravine, and the Golden Core cultivator Gu Shaoshang!

Using Ding Yi's words, among the Northern Sea cultivation world, there were no small number of existences plotting after the ancient cultivator ruin in the Sunken Sea.

Especially with the two examples of the Canghai Alliance great power surnamed Li and Fairy Jadecold of Jadecold City before them, more and more people were beginning to stir.

Feiyun Ravine itself was one of the many allied forces within the Canghai Alliance.

That rumored Feiyunzi, faced with the living example of the great power surnamed Li, how could he not be moved?

He had sent his own son and daughter along with a group of disciples to hunt Ring-Headed Turtles. Eight or nine times out of ten, it was also for black turtle bloodline to refine corresponding treasures.

Once this was linked together, everything instantly became clear.

Only, thinking of competing on the same stage as Sage Sages made Luo Chen somewhat uneasy.

Without a relationship of interest, Sage Sages might pride themselves on their bearing and not deal with Golden Core juniors like them. But once they entered the secret realm, everyone would be an opponent, and a relationship of interest would already exist.

Perhaps some Sage would want to clear the field, first exterminating their group of Golden Core juniors peeking at treasures.

"According to Ding Yi, that ancient cultivator traveling palace covers an extremely large area. The entrances according to the formation-eye distribution of the spirit-absorbing formation are not in the same place, so the chance of encountering one another is not large. Moreover, what Sages seek is not the same as what we seek. Unless we encounter someone bloodthirsty, things are still within the controllable range. What needs to be considered most is instead the threat from other Golden Core cultivators."

"At that time, I will bring Black King and Heavenjade as well. In this way, sea, land, and sky will all be protected."

"And Han Zhan!"

Thinking of Han Zhan, Luo Chen gained another measure of confidence.

In their recent meetings, he remembered vividly the changes to Han Zhan's Nascent Soul.

After obtaining the soul-nourishing wood, the other party's strength was rapidly recovering.

Unlike other Sage cultivators who relied on external objects, what Han Zhan was most proficient in were divine soul secret arts and puppets controlled by divine sense.

As long as his Nascent Soul recovered to a normal state, his strength could recover at least seventy to eighty percent.

Because of the restraint of the Great Dao oath, at the critical moment of life and death, Han Zhan would naturally protect him a little.

In this way, even against a Sage Sage, Luo Chen would have some power to protect himself.

After thinking repeatedly, Luo Chen's heart settled slightly.

But only slightly.

This time would be the first time in his life entering a secret realm for exploration, and also one of his rare "risk-taking moves." How could he feel ninety percent certain?

But when it involved the opportunity to break through to Sage, taking risks was unavoidable!

"People! Sometimes, in this life, one must always take one risk."

Luo Chen sighed, then rose and left Star Dou Hall, heading toward the Pill-Artifact Hall.

He did not need to refine a black turtle treasure; he already had one.

As for this extra time, it was better to handle the Purple Monkey Flower matter first!

For the three great main ingredients of the Nascent Soul Pill, not one could be missing.

Although the Five Elements Lotus Platform was precious, the Purple Monkey Flower also could not be taken lightly.

Very soon.

Inside the Pill-Artifact Hall, Luo Chen busied himself around a flower planted in an earthen basin.

Taking material, extracting fragrance, analyzing components, studying medicinal properties.

...

One month later.

Heavenjade personally arrived at Cailian Island.

Zhang Jiadi and his wife personally received her.

They were puzzled inwardly. They had clearly only just settled last year's surplus not long ago, and this year's matters had also been arranged. Why had Fairy Heavenjade come again?

But as Fairy Heavenjade stated her purpose, they understood why.

"Infant Face?"

"That is a core item of my Coral Sea Zhou clan. It is rarely sold outside. Senior, are you not overestimating my Zhou Shanshan's face?"

Because she had her maiden family as a backer, Zhou Shanshan had quite some confidence and was not as submissive before Fairy Heavenjade as Zhang Jiadi.

Heavenjade did not care about her attitude and merely stated the conditions.

"Rarely sold outside means there are still occasional external trades."

"My master will not take it for nothing. Apart from the corresponding spirit stones, he also promises to tailor-make one defensive magic treasure for a Coral Sea Golden Core cultivator!"

"This condition should be acceptable, right?"

Zhou Shanshan's eyes could not help brightening.

A tailor-made defensive magic treasure was much more suited to the cultivator themselves than one bought from the market.

The Coral Sea Zhou clan had quite many Golden Core cultivators, even surpassing the Tianqu Chen clan. Only in combat methods were they slightly weaker.

If a Zhou clan prodigy could obtain a tailor-made defensive magic treasure, they would surely be able to suppress the Zhou clan's younger generation in later exchanges.

In ordinary times, Daoist Azure Yang would not agree to requests to tailor-make magic treasures.

The outside world only rumored that his skill was not refined. Looking at it now, how was it that he could not? It was clearly that he did not want to!

"Senior Heavenjade, please wait for a while. I will send your conditions back to the Coral Sea. After the family elders decide, I will give you a reply."

"Mm. As soon as possible!"

Heavenjade left.

When she came to Cailian Island again half a month later, Zhou Shanshan took the initiative to offer up three Infant Face plants with cupped hands.

...

"No wonder Purple Monkey Flower can be used in medicine. This medicinal property that disturbs the divine soul is truly tyrannical beyond compare."

"If it is handled properly through alchemy, preserving the core that guides the divine soul, then the Nascent Soul Pill refined with it will surely achieve twice the result with half the effort during Sage formation."

"However, if I want to seek a corresponding method to restrain its poison, relying only on alchemy cannot accomplish it."

Inside the Pill-Artifact Hall, Luo Chen refined poison-avoidance pills while pondering methods to restrain Purple Monkey Flower's poisonous qi.

The poison-avoidance pill formula in his hands named Poison-Resisting Pill was not some rubbish item.

It was an inheritance from the storage belt of Qian Ting, the head of Cold Light Island's Qian family.

Its grade was as high as fourth-tier!

This was also the reason Luo Chen, after refining it, had felt comfortable letting Black King go to Purple Spirit Island alone.

Yet unexpectedly, the fourth-tier Poison-Resisting Pill could not withstand the Purple Monkey Flower's invasion of the soul.

Therefore, Luo Chen intended to make slight improvements on the foundation of the Poison-Resisting Pill.

The Poison-Resisting Pill could not withstand Purple Monkey Flower's soul poison, but it was not wholly ineffective. Black King had only been too deeply infected after staying on Purple Spirit Island too long.

The Coral Sea Zhou clan's specialty, the third-tier coral Infant Face, emitted bursts of infant crying when swaying. This sound could make cultivators irritable and confused.

But if one endured through it, it greatly benefited the cultivator's will and soul.

Luo Chen planned to use it as a main ingredient to replace one of the Poison-Resisting Pill's main ingredients, strengthening its defensive effect on the divine soul.

If it had been in the past, Luo Chen would absolutely not have had the ability to improve a fourth-tier pill.

But after he refined a fourth-tier Transformation Pill on Cangwu Mountain and advanced to fourth-tier alchemist, his alchemy attainments had become increasingly exquisite.

And pill formulas of the poison-avoiding and detoxifying type were also comparatively targeted. Studying them was not as difficult as orthodox cultivation-type pills.

The more universal something was, the harder it was to improve. The more niche something was, once the main difficulty was overcome, it instead became simpler.

Therefore, Luo Chen had this confidence.

After the Zhou clan delivered the Infant Face, Luo Chen immediately entered research without stopping.

...

Half a year later.

On a small island eight thousand li away from the Fulong Mountain Range.

Luo Chen's feet landed solidly upon it.

Looking at the surrounding environment, lush with greenery, green leaves, and purple fruits, he could not help drawing in a deep breath.

"So this is Earth Mulberry Island? Hearing its name truly cannot compare to seeing it. It is genuinely a good place!"

Beside him, Sang Jiugong's spirits lifted, and he revealed a modest expression.

"Fellow Daoist overpraises it. It is merely a small island with a third-tier lower-grade spirit vein."

"That makes Fellow Daoist's ability in spirit plant cultivation even more evident!" Luo Chen blinked. Before Sang Jiugong, he plucked a black-purple mulberry and swallowed it.

As the juice burst in his mouth, satisfaction also appeared in his eyes.

"It is indeed a good fruit, no inferior to those spirit fruits of rumor."

Sang Jiugong was delighted inwardly. "Fellow Daoist also thinks so! This is the Ink Immortal Mulberry I deliberately cultivated, hybridized from the Ink Blood Mulberry and Heavenly Immortal Mulberry. The fruit it bears is not only rich in spiritual qi, it also provides tremendous assistance in strengthening the meridians."

Luo Chen sensed the changes in his body and smiled without speaking.

It was somewhat exaggerated!

Sang Jiugong's expression changed, revealing a bitter look.

"Unfortunately, this Ink Immortal Mulberry of mine is a new variety and has little reputation, so it cannot sell at a high price."

"It is only that outsiders do not recognize the goods. Elder Sang need not brood over it." Luo Chen took out a handkerchief and wiped the black-purple juice from the corner of his mouth.

Sang Jiugong shook his head. "Reputation is only one aspect. Mainly, there are no good channels for sale. I heard Fellow Daoist's Azure Yang Treasure Pavilion is now an extremely famous large shop in Dragon Abyss Immortal City. If my Earth Mulberry Island's Ink Immortal Mulberries could be placed on Azure Yang Treasure Pavilion's counters..."

Luo Chen smiled as he listened to the other party complain, watching the other party perform.

Until Sang Jiugong stopped speaking.

"Fellow Daoist's visit to my Earth Mulberry Island presumably is not only to sightsee the scenery of mulberry groves and spirit plants everywhere, right?"

Luo Chen smiled faintly.

"Ink Immortal Mulberries can be placed on Azure Yang Treasure Pavilion's counters. However, Fellow Daoist, do you still remember the favor you owed me back then?"

Sang Jiugong's expression turned solemn!

Devil Lord Azure Yang, Luo Hai, was already incomparably formidable, and also bore the reputation of an artifact-forging master outside. Even under such circumstances, he was still using Azure Yang Treasure Pavilion's help selling Ink Immortal Mulberries plus that verbal promise from back then as conditions to ask him to do something.

What sort of important matter would that be, and what sort of difficult matter would it be?

"Fellow Daoist, my Ink Immortal Mulberries are not actually completely without sales channels..."

"I once heard Fellow Daoist boast that you have quite some insights into spirit plant cultivation, especially excelling at hybridizing new varieties and accelerating maturation methods. I wonder, regarding the ancient poison flower Purple Monkey Flower, do you have corresponding understanding?"

This time, Luo Chen directly interrupted him and stated his true purpose.

*(End of Chapter)*
