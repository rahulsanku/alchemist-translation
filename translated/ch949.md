# Chapter 949: Ancient Secret Realm, Dao Sect Visits the Mountain

"According to Purple Robed Marquis, it was you who requested to purchase my *Hundred Poisons Body-Tempering Art*?"

Luo Chen did not hesitate and immediately replied:

"Indeed. I, Qi, have always favored body refinement. Whenever I come across related techniques, I never let them pass. I just wonder, Heavenly Master, what price do you set for your technique?"

In response, Li Tianshi waved his hand.

"The price is not important. Before discussing the price, you should first take a look at the general idea."

As he spoke, he nodded to Purple Robed Marquis beside him.

The latter flipped his hand, and a jade slip appeared before him.

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow, took it, and directly injected his divine sense into it, scanning through.

The *Hundred Poisons Body-Tempering Art* was precisely the high-grade body refinement technique that Ming Lei Cave Master had mentioned earlier.

However, unlike back then when the other party had wanted to meet but couldn't, Luo Chen had obtained the opportunity for a brief reading on his very first request.

From this, it was evident that the disparity in their cultivation and strength had earned him different treatment.

Luo Chen was delighted inwardly.

Yet, after browsing through the small portion that had no restrictions, his heart gradually sank.

"This technique is really somewhat..."

He didn't know how to put it. Hesitating, he saw the amused look in Li Tianshi's eyes.

With a thought, Luo Chen tightened his grip on the jade slip.

"I'll take it!"

Li Tianshi was startled, and even Purple Robed Marquis beside him was greatly surprised.

Purple Robed Marquis advised, "This body refinement art is no ordinary technique. It requires taking a king gu into the body, refining a hundred poisons, and day and night eroding the flesh, blood, and bones. The pain goes without saying. But those of us who have achieved the Sage realm can more or less endure pain. What is truly dangerous is that, given your current body refinement level, for it to have an effect on your physical body, the poisons refined must be extremely potent exotic toxins. One mistake, and it's eternal damnation..."

"Fellow Daoist, no need to persuade further!" Luo Chen waved his hand. "Though I, Qi, have considerable attainments in the path of body refinement, the deeper I cultivate, the more obstacles I encounter. Therefore, I greatly need relevant techniques for reference, to break through bottlenecks. Since such a marvelous art exists, how could I shrink back at the sight of difficulty?"

Purple Robed Marquis opened his mouth, but in the end could only sigh with some admiration and cease his dissuasion.

He too had read the complete text of the *Hundred Poisons Body-Tempering Art* and knew how great its power was upon great accomplishment.

A phrase like "a hundred poisons cannot harm, water and fire find no purchase" was actually an understatement.

If not for the numerous taboos and the extreme difficulty of cultivation, he would have long chosen to practice this art as a supplementary technique.

Now that the other party was eager to try, further dissuasion would only make him the villain.

Luo Chen turned his attention to the true owner of this technique. "Heavenly Master Li, please name your price! Anything on my person that can be offered, I am willing to trade."

Li Tianshi asked with a smile, "In your view, what is this technique worth?"

Luo Chen frowned slightly.

He recalled the small portion he had seen earlier and said hesitantly, "This technique is actually not too exquisite. It is merely a method of transporting toxins to temper the flesh, blood, and bones. What is truly valuable is instead the accompanying hundred poisons king gu."

"Exactly!"

Li Tianshi nodded with a smile.

"This technique was created by me. It is indeed not very profound. Because the very purpose of creating this technique was to match the hundred poisons king gu I refined back then. Rather than saying you are buying a technique from me, it's more accurate to say you are buying the hundred poisons king gu. And this gu insect is a masterpiece of mine. Its value..."

Li Tianshi sized up Luo Chen from head to toe, until even Luo Chen felt uncomfortable, then suddenly named his price.

"If Fellow Daoist is willing to do me a favor once, I will agree to gift this gu and its accompanying technique to you, free of charge."

"Oh?"

Luo Chen's face showed confusion.

Given Li Tianshi's status in the Southern Frontier, it was comparable to Luo Chen's level in the Eastern Wilderness.

With a single call, countless experts would be willing to work for him.

Just like back when Luo Chen, in the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world, initiated a war of expansion under the name of the Pill Sect, countless major and minor forces and Sage experts had gathered in response.

Why would he need Luo Chen's help?

Moreover, with Li Tianshi himself being a late-stage Sage Grand Cultivator, his personal strength was already considerable. Why would he need the assistance of a "stranger" expert like Luo Chen?

This "free of charge" was probably the greatest cost!

Luo Chen said coldly, "I ask you to speak plainly. What exactly is this matter that requires Qi's intervention?"

Li Tianshi did not beat around the bush and frankly spoke of the matter from beginning to end.

Ever since embarking on the path of a gu refiner, his dream had been to refine a divine gu that surpassed the mortal!

A fifth-grade divine gu!

However, as everyone knew, in the history of the Southern Frontier, there had only been three fifth-grade divine gu, and never a fourth.

Even those three fifth-grade divine gu had, with the passage of time, gradually become incomplete.

The Life-and-Death Gu had been destroyed, evolving into many low-grade gu such as the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu, Devil Heart Gu, and Food-Eating Gu.

The Tribulation Gu had disappeared, rumored to have attached itself to a top expert and left the Southern Frontier.

Only the Fortune Gu was still preserved within the Life and Death Gate.

After carefully studying various gu and collecting many clues, Li Tianshi had gradually discovered a remnant ancient secret realm.

Within it, there was a great possibility that the original gu formula of the Life-and-Death Gu was preserved!

But that secret realm was extremely dangerous, where even Sage cultivators treaded as if on thin ice.

To be thorough, Li Tianshi planned to organize a team to explore it.

As for the composition of the exploration team, he had to ensure there were no gaps.

Experts in various fields had already been recruited.

Only in the area of body refinement was there a deficiency.

Originally, he had placed his hopes on Purple Robed Marquis, but...

Purple Robed Marquis smiled freely. "Though I consider my body refinement attainments not insignificant, Heavenly Master Li still had some doubts. That's why he brought out the *Hundred Poisons Body-Tempering Art*, trying to lure out some hidden body refinement experts in the Southern Frontier cultivation world."

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow. "So Qi is the big fish that was hooked?"

Purple Robed Marquis looked at Li Tianshi, who did not avoid the topic.

"To be able to suppress the Demon Buddha Ning Yuzhuo with body refinement techniques, and to leave two Sage cultivators of the Shadow Image Sect dead and wounded—such attainments, among contemporary body refiners of the Southern Frontier, I fear none surpass you."

"Heavenly Master's intelligence sources are truly impressive!"

Luo Chen praised with a hint of sarcasm, then fell into silence.

Whenever it came to exploring secret realms, he always kept his distance.

If not for forming his Sage Soul, he wouldn't have gone to the Demon-Fall Land either.

Now that the other party was inviting him to explore some ancient secret realm, how could he not be cautious?

Li Tianshi and Purple Robed Marquis exchanged a glance, somewhat surprised.

The tyrant cultivator Qi Tian was rumored to have a bold and unrestrained style, yet now he was being rather cautious.

"Right. If he weren't cautious, how could he have come this far."

Li Tianshi thought to himself, then tempted him with benefits.

"The cultivation of the *Hundred Poisons Body-Tempering Art* requires not only the hundred poisons king gu as the core but also various powerful toxins as consumables."

"Though Fellow Daoist has the Wind Thunder Gorge as a backing, it mostly consists of unaccomplished rogue cultivators who can hardly collect toxin resources for you."

"If Fellow Daoist is willing to help, I can mobilize the power of the Fierce Peak Corpse Alliance to purchase corresponding toxins for you on a large scale, and even some peculiar poison gu can be sought for you."

This was indeed no empty talk.

From Luo Chen's brief glance earlier, he had seen the various toxins recommended in the jade slip.

Their grades ranged from low to high, from first-grade toxins to fourth-grade toxins, listing thousands of varieties.

Perhaps the prices weren't too high, but relying on himself alone, it would be difficult to collect them all.

Luo Chen asked in a low voice, "Heavenly Master Li, you can mobilize the manpower and resources of the Fierce Peak Corpse Alliance?"

Li Tianshi smiled slightly. "Fellow Daoist Qi, do not underestimate my influence within the Corpse Alliance!"

"In that case..." Luo Chen made up his mind and directly made a lion's mouth request. "Qi also wishes to join the Fierce Peak Corpse Alliance. I ask you to put in a good word for me."

"That is a trivial matter."

"Additionally, I wish to use the Corpse Alliance's intelligence channels to inquire about a hidden expert who disappeared in the Southern Frontier many years ago."

"Easy to say. Just give the name. With the Corpse Alliance's intricate internal structure, we can always find some clues or traces for you."

"Devil Heart Gu Emperor!"

The moment these four words appeared, Luo Chen stared fixedly at the two before him.

Li Tianshi's face darkened with some anger, while Purple Robed Marquis's expression shifted slightly, showing disgust.

Luo Chen asked tentatively, "I wonder if you two have any understanding of the Devil Heart Gu Emperor?"

Purple Robed Marquis snorted coldly. "A freeloader."

Li Tianshi's anger gradually faded, leaving only a trace of sigh.

"A perfectly good Devil Heart Gu, reduced to a tool for plundering cultivation. What a waste. Since you seek this person, I don't mind asking around for you. But you must be mentally prepared. This person's whereabouts are extremely secretive, and he hasn't shown his face for many years. It may very well come to nothing!"

"Agreed is enough." Luo Chen did not insist. He had been in the Southern Frontier cultivation world for a short time and was in no hurry.

Li Tianshi nodded. "Then what about the matter I mentioned?"

Luo Chen smiled slightly. "Qi agrees. When the time comes, just inform me, and I can set off at any time."

"No rush, no rush. Before the Fierce Peak Corpse Alliance attacks the Life and Death Gate, we still have enough time to prepare." Li Tianshi breathed a sigh of relief, then waved his hand and struck several hand seals. The many restrictions on the jade slip in Luo Chen's hand were instantly undone, allowing him to read the full text now. "The corresponding hundred poisons king gu will be sent to you as soon as possible, and some basic toxins can also be gifted free of charge."

Luo Chen was somewhat surprised by this.

"The deed hasn't even been done, and the reward is already given?"

Li Tianshi laughed heartily, exuding confidence.

"In this Southern Frontier, no one can take advantage of me for nothing. What harm is there in giving you the reward first?"

Luo Chen's mind stirred, and he cupped his hands. "Then many thanks!"

This scene was quite similar to when he had refined pills for Ling Fengzi back then.

He wasn't afraid that the other party would take the pills and run.

As for the result, hadn't Ling Fengzi eventually joined the Luo Tian Sect?

"Purple Robed Marquis, I still have some trivial matters to attend to. I'll trouble you to entertain Fellow Daoist Qi for me."

"Heavenly Master, go ahead. Leave Fellow Daoist Qi to me."

After Li Tianshi hurriedly left, Purple Robed Marquis winked at Luo Chen, looking eager to try.

Luo Chen sighed helplessly. "There's no rush for sparring. I still want to look around at this ten-day exchange meeting."

"Haha, fair enough." Purple Robed Marquis laughed heartily and led Luo Chen back into the great hall.

...

This exchange meeting, involving most of the Sage cultivators of the Southern Frontier, was quite unsettled from start to finish.

The strong giving lectures was merely a formality.

Apart from Li Tianshi at the beginning who offered some genuine insights, the rest was mostly old hat.

This was only natural. They weren't close friends, so who would be willing to give away useful cultivation insights for free?

The focus of the exchange meeting was on two points.

Exchanging resources and joining the Fierce Peak Corpse Alliance!

Especially the latter. In the following days, one after another, ten more Sage cultivators joined the Fierce Peak Corpse Alliance.

Only Luo Sect Left Envoy Bodhi, Ming Lei Cave Master of Wind Thunder Gorge, and others hesitated and did not state their position.

Late one night.

Luo Chen was in the fourth-grade cave dwelling arranged for him by the Fierce Peak Corpse Alliance, inventorying the resources he had traded for during the day.

"Erosion Gold Vine, Immortal-Corrupting Miasma, Nether Dew..."

Bottles and jars were laid out before him, all covered with sealing restrictions, looking formidable.

All were highly toxic substances!

For instance, the Immortal-Corrupting Miasma was a highly toxic miasma extracted from the Misty Wilderness. If it spread even a little, cultivators below the Adept realm would die upon contact.

Another example was Nether Dew, rumored to be refined from the filthy places deep within earth veins where spiritual energy was stagnant. A single drop would cause an Adept cultivator's mana to stagnate, turning them into livestock on the chopping block, at the mercy of others.

These were relatively rare items. Even if their grades weren't high, they rarely circulated on the market.

If not for Luo Chen bringing out a large batch of medicinal herbs collected from the Dan Saint's Blessed Land and the Five Elements Divine Sect, it would have been difficult to trade for them.

Even so, Luo Chen did not immediately begin cultivating the *Hundred Poisons Body-Tempering Art*.

Because this body refinement art progressed from low to high, step by step.

He still lacked some of the most basic toxins as cultivation consumables.

It was laughable. At his level, the circles he moved in were too high-end, making it hard to quickly gather those most basic resources.

"It seems I'll have to rely on Li Tianshi's side."

After a round of inventorying, Luo Chen categorized and stored these items according to grade and toxicity.

There was movement outside the cave dwelling.

With a thought, Luo Chen released the cave's formation.

Ming Lei Cave Master walked in.

He looked around and clicked his tongue in admiration. "Others joining the Fierce Peak Corpse Alliance have to pass the assessment of Alliance Leader Songyang. Your treatment is different, Brother Qi. Not only did you get in directly, but you were also assigned such a good cave."

"Is it that good?" Luo Chen asked casually.

"At least better than my Wind Thunder Gorge," Ming Lei Cave Master said.

Luo Chen said indifferently, "Given the current cultivation environment in the Southern Frontier, the better the cave, the greater the harm to us, isn't that so?"

Ming Lei Cave Master was taken aback. That did make sense.

He waved his hand and got to the point.

"I'll be leaving Mi Jie Mountain soon."

Luo Chen asked curiously, "Tomorrow is the last day of the exchange meeting. From what Heavenly Master Li hinted, there will be an important figure appearing. Don't you want to see?"

"No matter how important, what does it have to do with me?" Ming Lei Cave Master asked back, then lowered his voice. "Better to leave early, to avoid Ning Yuzhuo causing trouble."

Luo Chen understood.

"Then I wish you a smooth journey, Fellow Daoist!"

Ming Lei Cave Master smiled slightly. He too had not come in vain.

Not only had he met more top experts of the Southern Frontier, but he had also exchanged for many useful resources.

After returning, he would reorganize Wind Thunder Gorge, establish his own power, and could henceforth be considered a lord of his own domain.

But before leaving, he still had some advice for Luo Chen.

"Be careful of Burial Life Elder!"

"Oh?" Luo Chen showed a puzzled expression.

Ming Lei Cave Master transmitted his voice. "This person's whereabouts are mysterious, and his style is ruthless. There's a high chance he is the Devil Heart Gu Emperor you seek. If you don't come into contact with him, so be it. But if you do, be extremely cautious."

Luo Chen's lips tightened slightly. The intelligence on Burial Life Elder surfaced in his mind one by one.

He came from Burial Life Valley, calling himself the Valley Master.

But unlike normal forces, Burial Life Valley did not have many disciples.

If he truly was the Devil Heart Gu Emperor, it did fit some of the rumored modus operandi.

"This is all I could find out. There may be errors. Brother Qi, you should look at it dialectically and not take my word entirely. That's all I have to say. Farewell!"

With that, Ming Lei Cave Master no longer lingered and drifted away from Mi Jie Mountain.

In the night, Luo Chen watched his departing back, murmuring the name of Burial Life Elder.

"Could he be the one I'm looking for?"

The next day.

Luo Chen saw Burial Life Elder, who had been absent for a long time, in the summit great hall of Mi Jie Mountain.

Four Grand Cultivators sat at the head, all on high alert.

When Luo Chen entered the hall, Songyang Zhenren glanced at him, his expression neither sad nor happy.

Li Tianshi smiled in acknowledgment, everything unspoken.

As for Burial Life Elder, his appearance was ordinary, showing no particular traits.

Faced with Luo Chen's gaze, he merely returned a glance without any expression.

Luo Chen sat down beside Purple Robed Marquis, feeling somewhat contemplative.

Even Ming Lei Cave Master could connect the Devil Heart Gu Emperor to Burial Life Elder. Why hadn't Purple Robed Marquis and Li Tianshi mentioned this possibility to him?

And with such a lineup today, were they welcoming someone?

The important figure Li Tianshi had hinted at earlier?

Just then!

At the entrance, a powerful and imposing aura burst in without any concealment.

"Zhan Bingtao of the Tianyuan Dao Sect comes to visit the mountain!"

(End of chapter)
