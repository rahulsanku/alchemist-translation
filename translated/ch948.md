# Chapter 948: Li Tian Lectures, Hundred Poisons Tempering the Body

On the tranquil mountain peak, wind and clouds suddenly stirred.

Thousands of ancient seal characters rose from the void, drifting upward to weave a golden net amidst the clouds.

One after another, beams of escaping light landed on the square outside the grand hall atop the mountain.

Luo Chen was among them.

After exchanging a glance with Thunderclap Cave Master, he turned his attention to his surroundings.

At a glance, he already had a clear picture.

"Including us newcomers, there are a full forty Sages here!"

This was no small number. To put it in perspective, at its peak—excluding the holy land's experts—the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world only boasted about seventy or eighty Adept-level cultivators.

Now, this Fierce Mountain Corpse Alliance occupied roughly half of that number!

In comparison, even the largest cultivator alliance Luo Chen had ever seen, the Cang Hai Righteous Alliance, fell short.

"I wonder what methods Alliance Master Songyang used to gather all these people together?"

While Luo Chen pondered this, exclamations of surprise rang out from time to time in the hall.

"Fellow Daoist Xie, why are you here at Mijie Mountain too?"

"The Sword Vault Pavilion Master—the world thought he was cultivating his nature, keeping his sword sheathed, but no one expected he had already joined the Fierce Mountain Corpse Alliance."

"Brother Guan, long time no see!"

This behemoth known as the Fierce Mountain Corpse Alliance, even with only its surface exposed, was merely the tip of the iceberg.

Only now did the newcomers realize just how many powerful figures from the Southern Frontier had secretly joined the Corpse Alliance over the past two hundred years, unnoticed by all.

Luo Chen didn't recognize many people, so he wasn't deeply affected.

But comparing it to the alliances he had seen before, he understood its weight all too well.

"The Fierce Mountain Corpse Alliance is determined to oppose the Life and Death Gate!"

As he sighed, Luo Chen suddenly sensed gazes fixed upon him.

More than one!

He calmly swept his gaze across them, offering no avoidance.

There was Ning Yuzhuo, calm yet carrying resentment; the Purple-Robed Marquis, eager and restless; and Zhenguan Daoist, smiling and nodding in greeting.

As for the Luo Sect's Left Envoy, Bei Puti, he looked at Luo Chen with a hint of belated confusion, then nodded amicably when their eyes met.

Luo Chen paid him no heed, his attention instantly sharpening.

Because the doors of the ancient palace atop the mountain were slowly opening.

Three figures gradually appeared before the crowd.

Two men and one woman, all exuding formidable auras.

The leader wore a purple Daoist robe, his face imposing and majestic. A pair of sword-like brows seemed capable of cutting through the morning mist, causing everyone who met his gaze to tremble inwardly.

The other man had crane-white hair but a youthful face, his gaze kind, with long snow-white eyebrows hanging down on either side, giving an immediate impression of great virtue and respect.

The female cultivator, in comparison, was not so well-preserved. Her hunched figure, layers of wrinkles, and a bright red robe only made her seem out of place.

"Sage Songyang has come out!"

At the sight of the three, a faint stir rippled through the square.

Thunderclap Cave Master leaned close to Luo Chen and introduced them uncertainly: "According to rumors, the leader is Sage Songyang. That crane-haired, youthful-faced old Daoist is the renowned Li Tian Master, and the other female cultivator is Matron Redleaf."

Luo Chen nodded and asked, "Isn't there supposed to be an extremely powerful Great Cultivator within the Corpse Alliance?"

"You mean Elder Zangsheng?" Thunderclap Cave Master reacted, then replied cautiously, "That one has a very peculiar temperament and rarely shows himself. If you join the Corpse Alliance, it's best to have as little dealings with him as possible."

"Why?" Luo Chen asked.

But before Thunderclap Cave Master could answer, Sage Songyang spoke from above.

"Esteemed Sages, this is the first public appearance of our Fierce Mountain Corpse Alliance. I hope everyone will gain something from this exchange meeting."

"I'll keep it brief. I'll only state three rules!"

"First: during this exchange meeting, all transactions are based on mutual willingness. No one may use their cultivation level to pressure others, nor rely on martial force to force a sale."

"Second: the exchange meeting will last ten days. After ten days, those who have not joined the Corpse Alliance must leave of their own accord and not linger."

"Finally…"

Sage Songyang's majestic gaze swept across the crowd, and his words turned cold:

"Within Mijie Mountain, violence is forbidden. Violators will be killed without mercy!"

When the words "killed without mercy" were uttered, a powerful aura erupted from Sage Songyang as the center.

A Sage domain, as heavy as a mountain, instantly pressed down upon everyone's heads.

Those with weaker mana could barely breathe.

Even Luo Chen felt a noticeable sluggishness in his movements.

His heart tightened. From this small sign, he gained a clear judgment of Sage Songyang's strength.

"A genuine Great Cultivator!"

"Judging by appearances alone, his might is no less than that of Moon Wanderer, Heavenly Capital Master, and the like."

"If he fully unleashes his domain, he would likely be among the top figures even among late-stage Sages."

Above.

The crane-haired, youthful-faced old Daoist witnessed this scene and said gently with a smile, "Fellow Daoist Songyang, we are all fellow cultivators of the Southern Frontier. Why be so harsh?"

Sage Songyang pursed his lips. Seeing that everyone below was duly intimidated, he nodded slightly.

The vast Sage domain suddenly retracted.

He clasped his hands in a salute. "Gentlemen, please!"

With this gesture, the atmosphere in the hall immediately became more lively.

Figures filed in one after another, entering the grand hall.

As soon as they entered, attendants greeted them and arranged seats accordingly.

The same happened for Luo Chen.

The attendant whispered beside him, explaining the schedule for the next ten days.

"Each day, a high-ranking Sage of the Corpse Alliance will give a public lecture. If you have any insights, Senior, you may also go up and share your cultivation experiences."

"In the side halls, there are private rooms arranged. If Senior wishes to converse privately with others or trade certain resources, you may inform us at any time, and we will arrange a suitable room."

"For other needs, Senior, you may also tell this humble one, and I will do my utmost to satisfy them."

Luo Chen looked at the beautiful attendant and nodded slightly. Then he took a seat in a prominent position in the central area of the hall.

As soon as he sat down, he drew many gazes.

Although this was an exchange meeting, the seating arrangement clearly reflected each person's strength and status.

Luo Chen, a cultivator who had only recently risen to fame, being placed in such a conspicuous seat showed how highly the Fierce Mountain Corpse Alliance regarded him.

Even Bei Puti was only treated on par with him.

And this was despite Bei Puti not only possessing great personal strength but also bearing the identity of the Luo Sect's Left Envoy.

Behind him stood a great sect that had been passed down for thousands of years in the Southern Frontier. Luo Chen, relying solely on his personal strength, had achieved such status.

Who would dare underestimate him?

As soon as he sat down, Bei Puti couldn't help but strike up a conversation.

"Fellow Daoist Qi, earlier you almost mistook me, saying I bore some resemblance to someone you knew. May I ask who that person is and where they are?"

Only now did he react?

Or had he investigated Luo Chen behind his back before daring to inquire?

Luo Chen said calmly, "That person's true name is unknown. Fellow cultivators of the Northern Sea only call him Old Ghost Nanqi. The mana aura he exudes is indeed somewhat similar to yours, Fellow Daoist Bei."

"Old Ghost Nanqi…" Bei Puti murmured the name, his eyes growing brighter and brighter. "So he fled to the Northern Sea?"

He wanted to ask more, but Luo Chen had already begun conversing with the Purple-Robed Marquis beside him.

Seeing this, he did not disturb them, merely thinking that the Purple-Robed Marquis was rather impolite.

The Purple-Robed Marquis was indeed somewhat impolite.

As soon as he engaged Luo Chen, he eagerly asked, "Qi Tian, I hear you primarily cultivate the body-tempering path. Coincidentally, I have also dabbled in some body-tempering methods. How about we spar a little when we have time, to verify each other's insights?"

Luo Chen twitched the corner of his mouth. "Alliance Master Songyang just said that violence is forbidden at Mijie Mountain. What, do you intend to defy the Alliance Master's orders?"

The Purple-Robed Marquis waved his hand. "No harm done. A full-blown fight is naturally out of the question. But if we merely have a light spar in my cave dwelling, who can say anything about it?"

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow, then agreed without hesitation.

"I would also like to see the attainments of Southern Frontier cultivators in the body-tempering path!"

The Purple-Robed Marquis was overjoyed. "Then you've come to the right person. The body-tempering path has long declined in the Southern Frontier. If not for the sake of my cultivation technique, I wouldn't have taken it up as a side practice. But even as a side practice, I am confident I am no less than an orthodox body-tempering cultivator."

In a few brief exchanges, the two had already exchanged communication talismans.

Luo Chen's message gu was the most common type—a messenger pigeon.

He had only obtained and refined it before leaving Wind Thunder Gorge, specifically asking for it from Thunderclap Cave Master.

Communication among Southern Frontier cultivators was not accustomed to using sound transmission jade slips, and flying sword messages were limited by distance.

In comparison, various message gu insects, which possessed a certain degree of intelligence and could travel long distances, had become popular.

The messenger pigeon was the most common and the most widespread.

Beyond that, there were some more unique, exclusive letter gu.

For instance, the one the Purple-Robed Marquis exchanged with Luo Chen was actually a purple blood mosquito, dotted with mysterious specks.

The Purple-Robed Marquis hadn't expected Luo Chen to be so straightforward, agreeing to the spar so quickly.

But soon, he understood the reason for the other's forthrightness.

"You want that high-grade body-tempering technique I didn't manage to trade last time?"

As he said this, he subconsciously glanced toward Thunderclap Cave Master, who was in a remote corner.

At that last private gathering, that fellow had indeed been present, brought in by Lantern Bearer.

Then, frowning, he said, "That high-grade body-tempering technique is extremely difficult to cultivate and carries great risks. I wouldn't recommend you take it up, Fellow Daoist."

"Oh?" Luo Chen smiled. "You were selling it, yet now you advise against it?"

The Purple-Robed Marquis shrugged. "It's not mine. I was just helping someone dispose of it, exchanging it for some usable resources. If you really want it, we can talk privately when we have time."

"Good!"

Luo Chen agreed decisively.

And the lectures in the hall officially began.

The first to give a lecture was not the leader of the Fierce Mountain Corpse Alliance, but Master Li Tian of the Moon Worship Sect!

He was the foremost Gu master in the Southern Frontier over the past five hundred years.

Even before the Life and Death Gate had closed its mountain gates, they often invited him to exchange insights on the path of Gu refinement.

In the Southern Frontier cultivation world, he was truly a figure of great virtue and high prestige.

Countless powerful cultivators prided themselves on possessing Gu insects personally refined by him.

Now that he was personally giving a lecture, everyone set aside their distractions and listened with rapt attention.

Even Luo Chen listened earnestly, holding to the idea that stones from other mountains could polish jade.

At the head of the hall, the crane-haired, youthful-faced, kind-eyed Li Tian Master sat cross-legged on a cushion. Before him, on a low table, wisps of sandalwood incense rose, making him seem like an immortal expounding the Dao.

Clear sounds issued from his mouth, carrying a subtle effect that refreshed the mind and brought sudden enlightenment.

"Our cultivation is nothing more than refining qi to transform the spirit, and refining the spirit to return to emptiness."

"Yet the Great Dao shares the same origin, though each path diverges."

"For example, here in the Southern Frontier, we have blazed a unique trail, using Gu insects as aids, forging a path of Gu cultivation distinct from the other four continents."

"All of you have cultivated to the Sage realm, so you must have considerable research into gu insects. But in my observation, many of you often only seek the power of gu insects, without understanding their true essence. Therefore, you often know the 'what' but not the 'why.' Your cultivation is hasty and superficial. Though your realm advances quickly, you do not grasp the true principles."

"This is one of the reasons why so few prodigies among the Southern Frontier Sages can comprehend the true meaning of laws at the Sage stage!"

As these words unfolded, the cultivators in the hall, already focused, became even more attentive.

Even Luo Chen couldn't help but nod inwardly.

In his view, although Southern Frontier cultivators were very mysterious and their methods rather peculiar, when it came to actual combat strength, they were not necessarily superior to their counterparts from the other four continents at the same level.

In fact, throughout his journey, he had never encountered anyone who had comprehended the true meaning of laws in advance.

From this alone, he knew that the Southern Frontier cultivators' method of using Gu insects as auxiliary cultivation tools was a shortcut, but one that made it difficult to reach the Ascendant path.

He didn't believe the Southern Frontier lacked brilliant and exceptionally talented individuals, yet the result was so stark.

Even the Life and Death Gate, with its single Ascendant great power, could not compare to the Northern Sea's Primordial Demon Sect, which had three ancestors coexisting, or Li Canghai standing alone. The Nether Origin Sect also had three Ascendant ancestors. And the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect alone possessed six Ascendant great powers.

The only problem might lie in the use of Gu insects as auxiliary cultivation tools.

And now, the most powerful Gu master in the Southern Frontier, Li Tian Master, had laid this issue out on the table.

Did he have a solution?

Under everyone's expectant gaze, Li Tian Master spoke eloquently.

"Gu insects are auxiliary tools for our cultivation, a means to reach the Great Dao. But how can the true Great Dao be comprehended by relying on tools?"

"After many years of refining, nurturing, and using Gu insects, I have gradually discovered that even Gu insects themselves are products of the Great Dao's natural creation."

"To merely regard them as tools is to abandon the Great Dao itself."

"Therefore, in my view, only by truly understanding the nature of one's own Gu insects and making them dual cultivate with one's own life can one approach the Great Dao and comprehend the legendary true meaning of laws."

"As for how to understand, integrate, and even cultivate in dual cultivation without distinction between self and Gu, I have some modest insights. I might as well share them with everyone for reference."

…

Inside a side hall.

Luo Chen waited quietly.

When half a stick of incense had burned, two sets of footsteps sounded outside the hall.

He immediately rose and clasped his hands in a salute to the Purple-Robed Marquis and Li Tian Master as they pushed open the door and entered.

"Qi Tian pays respects to Li Tian Master!"

The kind-eyed Li Tian Master smiled gently and teased, "My name is Li Tian (Leaving Heaven), yet yours is Qi Tian (Equal to Heaven). Isn't that going against the grain?"

Luo Chen quickly replied, "It's merely a name. What taboo could there be?"

"Haha!"

Li Tian Master laughed heartily, then his expression turned serious.

"I hear from the Purple-Robed Marquis that you wish to purchase my 'Hundred Poisons Body-Tempering Art'?"

(End of this chapter)
