# Chapter 694: Nine-Refined Beacon Fire, Nirvana Sacred Fire

The sudden voice, and that familiar cadence, instantly made Luo Chen's wildly joyous mood fall back.

He had originally thought that the voice in the five Pill Realm trials had been a remnant left by the formation arranger. But now, someone had once more emitted this voice, clearly showing that it had indeed come from a real existing person!

He looked around warily and blurted out, "Who?"

The hall was empty and silent.

In the end, Luo Chen's attention settled inside the black cauldron.

"Is it you?"

"It is I."

The weak voice sounded from within the black cauldron again. It seemed to muster its remaining strength and spoke with difficulty: "Inheritor, tell me your name."

Luo Chen hesitated for a moment, then sincerely gave his true name.

"Luo Chen!"

When he said this, he had already understood the other party's identity.

Clearly, this was the so-called artifact spirit!

In legend, only spirit treasures could give birth to true artifact spirits.

Such existences possessed the power to destroy heaven and earth and overturn rivers and seas. They were absolutely not comparable to pseudo-spirits of enchanted magic treasures.

It was precisely because he thought this through that Luo Chen became even more certain that the black cauldron before him was a true spirit treasure!

After learning Luo Chen's name, the black cauldron's artifact spirit fell silent for a while, then slowly asked,

"Are you prepared to truly accept Master's inheritance?"

Luo Chen was stunned. After passing the assessment, had he not already accepted the other party's inheritance?

Seventy-two alchemy books and thirty-six pill formulas.

There were even the three great pill formulas, Spirit-Stabilizing [TERM?], Void-Filling [TERM?], and Dao Union [TERM?], that led straight to the Great Dao.

Could it be...

Luo Chen tentatively asked, "There is still another inheritance?"

His gaze wandered over the black cauldron.

As expected.

The black cauldron's artifact spirit slowly said, "The heavy inheritance treasure is I, this Heaven-Refining Cauldron [TERM?], as well as the things within the cauldron. If you are ready, I will use my remaining power to help you accept the true inheritance."

Heaven-Refining Cauldron?

The things within the cauldron?

In an instant, Luo Chen's heart grew fiery.

Without hesitation, he asked, "What does being ready mean?"

"Agree to two conditions."

"What conditions?"

"When your cultivation succeeds in the future, look after Master's clansmen, and complete a promise Master owed before his death. Personally refine pills for a certain existence."

Look after a clan?

Refine pills once?

Luo Chen's brows furrowed tightly.

Looking after Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's clansmen was not unexpected. After the other party had fallen, he must have still had people he cared about.

But refining pills for someone was somewhat surprising.

With Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's Dao of Pills ability, if he wanted to refine pills for someone, he could simply do it himself. Why would he need an inheritor to do it in his place?

Most importantly...

"What counts as cultivation succeeding?"

"By your own heart."

Facing this answer, Luo Chen was speechless.

Whether it meant knowingly doing what could not be done, or acting within one's power, or having strength to spare, each situation corresponded to completely different realm and strength.

Yet the black cauldron's artifact spirit used only four words to throw the difficult problem onto Luo Chen.

By his own heart?

He took a deep breath and asked seriously, "Where are the True Lord's clansmen now? What is their situation? Do they have internal troubles or external threats?"

Facing this series of questions, the black cauldron's artifact spirit weakly replied, "I do not know."

Luo Chen frowned and asked again, "Is that promise compulsory? And who must I refine pills for? How should I contact the other party, or rather, how will the other party believe that I am the True Lord's alchemy inheritor?"

This time, the black cauldron's artifact spirit gave an answer.

"That promise was made by Master before his death. He originally intended to complete it after advancing to the Dao Integration stage, but there was no longer time. The person he promised was the Jade Pool Merchant Woman [TERM?]. When you meet in the future, the other party will naturally understand."

Jade Pool Merchant Woman... Luo Chen memorized this name.

Afterward, Luo Chen asked again.

"If I do not agree to these two conditions, can I accept the true inheritance spirit treasure?"

The black cauldron's artifact spirit said, "No."

Luo Chen's brow twitched slightly. "Then what about the alchemy books and pill formulas I obtained earlier, as well as the series of pills I refined in the Pill Realm?"

The black cauldron's artifact spirit said, "They can be taken away. Those are blessings belonging to those who pass the assessment, and have always been so."

Luo Chen thought for a moment, then suddenly asked another question: "Do those who pass the assessments in other inheritance lands also have to face these two conditions?"

The black cauldron's artifact spirit said, "I do not know."

Luo Chen seemed thoughtful.

They likely did not. At the very least, when it came to refining pills for someone, the other inheritors did not satisfy the condition.

Then what about looking after Heaven-Refining's clansmen?

Why was this condition placed here in the alchemy inheritance? Was there something special about this inheritance?

As he thought, the artifact spirit did not urge him and simply waited quietly for his consideration.

After a long time, Luo Chen raised his head and threw out another question.

"What exactly is inside the Heaven-Refining Cauldron?"

This time, unlike before, the black cauldron's artifact spirit gave a different answer.

"Only after you accept the inheritance will I tell you what is inside."

It knew, but it would not say.

Was that so?

Luo Chen murmured, and then his expression gradually grew firm.

Something Devil Lord Heaven-Refining had left inside must be incomparably precious.

Even without those things, the Heaven-Refining Cauldron, a spirit treasure that had given birth to an artifact spirit, was enough to make him take the risk.

Moreover!

He had laboriously passed the five trials of the Pill Realm. These things should have belonged to him in the first place!

Luo Chen no longer hesitated. Before the black cauldron's artifact spirit, he agreed to those two conditions.

At this point, the scene inside the hall abruptly changed.

A golden light screen slowly rose from within the cauldron. One character after another, like tadpoles, swam across it.

Luo Chen looked at those characters he did not recognize at all, his face blank.

At the same time, the black cauldron's artifact spirit's voice came leisurely.

"In Master's life, he refined a total of thirteen spirit treasures. Each contained power that could penetrate heaven and earth."

"Of the thirteen spirit treasures, one was gifted to the Jade Pool Merchant Woman, one fell into the hands of Tianyuan Great Sovereign [TERM?], and one remained within our clan. Of the remaining ten, five were destroyed by Rosycloud, leaving only five within Bright Radiance Heaven: Heaven-Refining Cauldron, Black Devil Jar [TERM?], True Yang Crown [TERM?], Spirit-Dotting Brush [TERM?], and Myriad Beast Diagram [TERM?]."

"Among these five spirit treasures, only the Heaven-Refining Cauldron, Black Devil Jar, and True Yang Crown stand above the rest. They are heaven-reaching spirit treasures beyond ordinary spirit treasures, and also the essence of Master's lifetime cultivation."

"For low-tier cultivators such as you, if you wish to use treasures of this level as you please, you must first cultivate the Treasure-Commanding Seal [TERM?]."

"Ordinarily, you would need several decades or a hundred years to completely master the Treasure-Commanding Seal. But this time, with the assistance of my main body's artifact spirit, this time can be greatly shortened. At least recognizing you as master will not be a problem."

"As for afterward, I am powerless. That can only depend on your fortune..."

As it spoke, the golden tadpoles on the light screen swam toward Luo Chen.

The tadpole-like characters sank one by one into Luo Chen's mind.

Even though he did not understand the characters, he could become familiar with their meaning.

"Gather your mind into one point. Do not have distracting thoughts!"

While helping Luo Chen cultivate the Treasure-Commanding Seal, the black cauldron's artifact spirit reminded him, clearly sensing the shock in Luo Chen's mind at this moment.

It only thought Luo Chen was shocked by Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's ability and the high grade of the spirit treasures, so it specially instructed him.

It did not know that at this moment, stormy waves had risen in Luo Chen's heart.

Because in those words, one name had been mentioned.

Rosycloud!

If he remembered correctly, in Green Parasol Mountain, the Holy Land of the feather race in the Eastern Wilderness, there stood three halls: Crossing Truth, Daytime Void, and Profound Passage!

Crossing Truth had no master, and Nether Spring temporarily served as vice hall master.

Daytime Void had a master, namely Azure Frost Demon Emperor.

As for the master of Profound Passage Hall, that was the most mysterious existence.

Even when the Ascendant mighty figure of the Deep Abyss Sect [TERM?] forcefully attacked Green Parasol Mountain, that person had not appeared.

Luo Chen did not know this existence's personal name, but he knew their Dao title, and it was precisely Rosycloud!

Was it the same name, a coincidence, or were they the same person?

But Rosycloud of the Mountains and Seas Realm was at most Ascendant realm. How could she have fought Devil Lord Heaven-Refining?

One had to know that in Tai Sui's mouth, Devil Lord Heaven-Refining had been praised as the strongest Void Refining cultivator from ancient times to the present!

During nearly twenty years in Demon-Fall Land, Luo Chen had also glimpsed a corner of Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's strength through countless details. That was an unimaginable power.

And such an existence had fought Rosycloud?

Rosycloud had even destroyed five of Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's spirit treasures!

Luo Chen was unwilling to believe this conjecture.

That would mean the shattering of Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's image of power, and also the expansion of Rosycloud's shadow in his heart.

Countless distracting thoughts arrived in a rush.

If the black cauldron's artifact spirit had not scolded him again and again, Luo Chen would almost have wanted to ask for the truth.

But with a great matter before him, Luo Chen ultimately endured these emotions, forcibly calmed down, and silently memorized the so-called Treasure-Commanding Seal.

Later, with the help of the black cauldron's artifact spirit, he even struck seal after seal toward the Heaven-Refining Cauldron.

When seal after seal sank into the black cauldron, Luo Chen's mind seemed to be drawn inside as well.

In a daze,

He saw the things Devil Lord Heaven-Refining had left inside.

It was a gray light floating in the depths of the great cauldron. He did not know its might or ability. Inexplicably, Luo Chen felt as if his mind would be devoured and refined by it.

Aside from that, two other objects coiled in the very center of the black cauldron, standing opposite each other like rival courts.

They were two clusters of flame.

One was nine-colored, and the other golden-yellow!

Beneath these two flames, the Withering-Bloom True Fire Luo Chen had once been so proud of seemed like an ant, trembling. This was not a gap in power, but suppression from essential grade and status.

Especially the latter, which gave Luo Chen a slight sense of familiarity.

As if he had seen it somewhere before?

"Are these three things what the True Lord left me?"

The world called the artifact spirit's master Devil Lord Heaven-Refining. That was a Dao title.

But Luo Chen and Tai Sui did not dare directly use this "honored title," often referring to him as True Lord instead.

Just as Golden Core cultivators were honored as Superiors, Sages as Perfected Beings, Void Refining cultivators in ancient books were often honored as "True Lords."

The artifact spirit quickly answered his question.

"Yes. These are the true inheritance obtained after passing the alchemy assessment."

"The two clusters of flame are respectively one strand of the source true fire left by Master, named Nine-Refined Beacon Fire [TERM?], and Rosycloud's source true fire, named Nirvana Sacred Fire [TERM?]."

"In that battle years ago, Master intercepted a strand of Rosycloud's source true fire and trapped it inside my body, using Nine-Refined Beacon Fire to wear it away."

"After so many years, with Master's death, Nine-Refined Beacon Fire no longer has will, and Nirvana Sacred Fire is also ownerless. Only some faint instincts remain in both."

"You should first refine the Heaven-Refining Cauldron. When you fully control it in the future, you can use the Heaven-Refining Cauldron to try subduing these two high-tier flames and let yourself walk farther on the Dao of Pills."

Luo Chen pursed his lips tightly.

No change appeared on his face.

When he heard the name "Nirvana Sacred Fire," a ripple passed through his thoughts, but he quickly suppressed it.

Perhaps he had been shocked too many times before, and now he was no longer surprised.

Or rather, he had thoroughly understood that Devil Lord Heaven-Refining and Rosycloud of Green Parasol Mountain truly had an enormous connection.

Because he had truly seen Nirvana Sacred Fire on Green Parasol Mountain!

Although that Nirvana Sacred Fire had been stronger in might than the one here, in terms of quality, it was one in heaven and one on earth.

The one here was purer and deeper!

Suddenly, Luo Chen seemed to remember something and asked, "Then what about you? You keep saying 'in the future.' In the future, will you not help me?"

Once the Heaven-Refining Cauldron recognized him as master, its artifact spirit should also be under Luo Chen's control.

Recalling the artifact spirit's incomparably weak voice from before, a bad premonition surfaced in Luo Chen's mind.

As expected, bad news arrived.

The artifact spirit said gloomily, "Your spiritual sense wanders inside the Heaven-Refining Cauldron. Can you not see those wounds?"

Luo Chen opened his mouth. He had naturally seen them, but subconsciously chose to ignore them.

"For more than a thousand years, Nine-Refined Beacon Fire and Nirvana Sacred Fire have battled without cease inside me. Their power has been wildly worn away, and how could my main body not also be riddled with holes? Especially because most of my power has been used to seal that gray light, leaving me even more powerless to stop all this."

"After you complete one cycle of the Treasure-Commanding Seal, I will disperse my own spirituality and use the portion of refining Dao law principles I have comprehended to perfect the seal Master left on that gray light."

"Afterward, you can only rely on yourself."

"Do not worry about the Heaven-Refining Cauldron. Its grade remains. Although it is riddled with holes, if nurtured for a thousand or a hundred years, it may have the chance to give birth to an artifact spirit again and return to a heaven-reaching spirit treasure."

When it spoke these words, its tone contained unconcealable sorrow.

After a thousand or a hundred years, would the newly born artifact spirit of the Heaven-Refining Cauldron still be it?

Most likely, everything would have changed.

"What is that gray light?" Luo Chen asked in a deep voice. He had glanced at it just now and had not dared to look longer at all.

"Do not ask, and do not think about it. When you reach Void Refining, you will naturally understand everything."

The artifact spirit left him this sentence.

Afterward, its voice gradually weakened.

It seemed that it had already walked toward its own end.

The black cauldron standing silently in the great hall trembled faintly, as if carrying some emotion of reluctance and unwillingness.

Luo Chen pursed his lips tightly, understanding that the other party had already sacrificed itself and was now entering that gray light.

He formed spirit seals, continuously striking the Treasure-Commanding Seal toward the Heaven-Refining Cauldron.

With the experience of the artifact spirit previously guiding him through the Heaven-Refining Cauldron, the first step of refining and making it recognize a master proceeded incomparably smoothly.

If nothing unexpected happened, it would not take long before he obtained this so-called heaven-reaching spirit treasure that surpassed ordinary spirit treasures!

Even in an incomplete state, it would surely be one of the top heavy treasures in the Mountains and Seas Realm!

Time slowly flowed.

Unknowingly, more than a month passed.

Luo Chen had struck the Treasure-Commanding Seal toward the Heaven-Refining Cauldron at least a hundred times, faintly seeming about to fully control it.

On this day,

Luo Chen stopped and began meditating to circulate his cultivation and recover mana.

Suddenly, his brows moved, and he subconsciously looked toward the black cauldron.

A wisp of golden light poked its head out from within the cauldron.

"Nirvana Sacred Fire?"

Luo Chen froze.

The next moment, his expression changed greatly!

That golden flame charged straight toward him in a daze!

How could Luo Chen dare let this thing touch him?

The terror in his memory had never been forgotten.

Subconsciously, he raised a mana shield, then prepared to perform other movements.

But the golden flame's speed was astonishingly fast. In the blink of an eye, it flew before him, even ignoring the mana shield on Luo Chen's body and directly sinking into him.

Luo Chen froze.

Why was there a feeling of a wanderer returning home?

Subconsciously, he thought of what he had just been doing.

In order to keep his mana sufficient, he had circulated his lifebound cultivation method... the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture and Nirvana Sacred Fire?

Before this thought fully surfaced, an extremely shrill scream suddenly erupted in the great hall.

With that scream, a cluster of nine-colored flame rose from the damaged Heaven-Refining Cauldron.

It locked onto Luo Chen.

Or rather, it locked onto the Nirvana Sacred Fire inside Luo Chen!

Then the nine-colored flame left the cauldron.

"You're coming too!"

Luo Chen was horrified.

Even though he retreated madly, he could not avoid it in time. He could only watch helplessly as the nine-colored flame drilled into his body.

A thought surfaced in Luo Chen's mind.

"The battle between Nine-Refined and Nirvana has not ended?"

Just as he thought, the instant Nine-Refined Beacon Fire entered Luo Chen's body, it was sensed by Nirvana Sacred Fire. The latter directly mobilized Luo Chen's mana to strengthen itself, and even the Withering-Bloom True Fire fused into the Nirvana Sacred Fire with soundless lament, making its might even more blazing.

A battle moved from first contact straight into a white-hot stage.

The battlefield expanded without pause.

Flesh and blood, bones, five viscera, meridians, apertures, and even the qi sea!

In an instant, two violent forces exploded rumblingly inside Luo Chen's body.

Flame after flame drilled out from his pores, turning him into a man of fire.

An extreme aura of destruction appeared on Luo Chen.

Pain, distortion, anger... all kinds of expressions surfaced on Luo Chen's face.

"They are using my body as a battlefield!"

*(End of Chapter)*
