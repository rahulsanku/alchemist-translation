# Chapter 1140: The End of an Era

In the secret chamber.

Luo Chen's wrists turned over, his palms held together in a hollow shape, his legs crossed in meditation, and waves of hot steam rose from his entire body.

His figure appeared lean, but the exposed flesh was knotted with muscles. Merely sitting there, he resembled a towering mountain.

Within that mountain, there was an extremely suppressed aura, as if it could burst forth at any moment.

The whole person looked like a volcano on the verge of eruption!

And inside his body, beneath the tightly closed Purple Mansion, a large number of bright red blood beads had accumulated at some unknown time.

These blood beads appeared to be blood, but upon closer inspection, they were actually thousands upon thousands of strands of qi force, interwoven and fused together, their power already compressed to the extreme.

If an ordinary martial artist saw this scene, they would surely be dumbfounded.

Because each blood bead was condensed from a massive amount of pure Dan force, enough to form a Dan core and condense into Gang force on its own, evolving into a bodily furnace!

Yet at this moment, Luo Chen already had a full thirty-six blood beads inside him, but he still had not chosen to take that step.

If he truly used this as his foundation to take the third step of body refinement, the terror of it would be unimaginable.

But Luo Chen was still not satisfied!

Thirty-six corresponded to the number of the heavenly cycle, but it was not Luo Chen's limit.

He still had a large amount of source blood that had not been transformed.

Since he had decided to switch to the path of qi and blood, he was determined to lay the most perfect foundation.

"Transforming thirty-six Dan seeds in half a year—the progress is gratifying. But thirty-six Dan seeds already form a system of their own. If I want to condense new Dan seeds, it might disrupt the original system. My physical body is strong enough, but if the cycle of Dan seeds inside me breaks, there will inevitably be backlash."

"I need to find a good method to resolve this hidden danger."

As this thought arose, Luo Chen had already awakened from his cultivation state.

Blinking his eyes, he calmly rose and left the cultivation chamber.

Entering the inner room, Jing Lan had already prepared a medicinal bath. Luo Chen directly immersed himself in it.

The hot water contained a large amount of boiled medicinal liquid, which was now seeping into Luo Chen's body through the pores of his skin, repairing the many hidden injuries he had sustained during his previous drift here.

After a long while, when the water in the wooden tub had become clear, Luo Chen finally ended his medicinal bath.

The subsequent matters were left to the servants to handle. Luo Chen casually draped a loose outer robe over himself and went to the study.

Pushing open the window, the snow-covered scenery outside came into view, refreshing his mind and spirit.

Luo Chen exhaled a mouthful of turbid qi and casually opened a book.

He had already read most of the books borrowed from the city lord's mansion, and the intelligence on the Three Great Calamities that he wanted to understand was already firmly memorized.

The so-called Three Great Calamities were peculiar natural phenomena summarized by the powerful martial artists of this world.

The Heavenly Wind seemed to only affect ordinary people, but in reality, it was also very dangerous for martial artists—only the danger hung over their heads.

Whenever the Heavenly Wind blew, extremely violent astral winds would sweep through the high altitudes. That kind of astral wind could even tear space, causing terrifying spatial cracks. Even the Leakless Golden Body of a fourth-stage body refiner could not withstand it!

As for the harm of Ghost Rain, Luo Chen had long understood it.

The bitter cold was secondary; the most dangerous thing was the Ghost Nightmare that accompanied the Ghost Rain.

Even martial artists with wills as tough as iron could lose themselves under the erosion of the Ghost Nightmare.

As for the final Calamity—the Annihilation Mist?

The records in the classics were not very detailed, but the few words made Luo Chen's entire body feel cold.

**[When the great mist spreads, it annihilates everything!]**

The Annihilation Mist appeared once every ten thousand years, and each time it was inevitably accompanied by a large number of Chaos Beasts. Wherever it passed, it devoured all living things, and its duration was uncertain.

Every twelve times—that is, roughly one era—a larger-scale Annihilation Mist would appear, one capable of destroying everything!

Whenever the eras changed, the Great Annihilation Era would arrive.

The name of this world, the Great Annihilation Realm, was derived from this.

When Luo Chen learned this news, even his calm, well-like Dao heart trembled slightly.

He did not know what power the Annihilation Mist possessed, but whether it was the Chaos Beasts described in the classics as capable of devouring all living things, or the world-destroying Great Annihilation Era, it was enough to make one understand the terror of this calamity.

Luo Chen wanted to learn more information, but unfortunately, the records on this point were too scarce.

This might be related to the fact that the people of this world had too short a lifespan!

From another classic, Luo Chen roughly understood the lifespan situation of body-refining cultivators in this world.

Although the path of body refinement in this world was relatively well-preserved, and one could even cultivate to the high levels of the fifth or sixth stage of body refinement, the increase in lifespan was far inferior to that of the Mountains and Seas Realm.

In the first and second stages of body refinement, lifespan almost did not increase significantly.

Only upon reaching the third stage of body refinement, the Furnace Realm, would lifespan barely increase to around three hundred years—far less than the five-hundred-year limit of the Mountains and Seas Realm.

According to legend, even the Leakless Realm only had a lifespan of eight hundred years!

Moreover, very few people could actually live to that age, because the path of body refinement, whether in cultivation or combat, focused heavily on the physical body. A single careless mistake could damage the physical body's origin, leading to a reduction in lifespan.

This was completely different from qi-refining immortality. Although qi-refining immortality also relied on the physical body, once one reached a high realm, the origin was focused on mana and divine soul. Even if the physical body was injured, it would not greatly affect the lifespan limit.

From this point, the reason why the records on the Annihilation Mist calamity were so few could also be explained.

The strong lived too short a time, and with the Great Annihilation Era that destroyed everything, it naturally led to a break in history.

Luo Chen even privately believed that the intelligence he had obtained was probably half true and half false, with many omissions.

At the very least, when did the last Annihilation Mist appear?

And which of the twelve calamities within an era did it belong to?

Luo Chen had not found this information.

Of course, after the initial disturbance of his Dao heart, Luo Chen quickly calmed down about these things.

He would prepare for a rainy day, but worrying about imaginary disasters was never his nature.

Even if he truly encountered the once-in-an-era Great Annihilation Era, he would definitely find a way to survive!

And strength was the bargaining chip to deal with everything!

Luo Chen calmly opened the *Casual Writings of a Fallen Immortal*. Originally, he had only intended to pass the time, but as he read, his attention deepened.

"A fellow traveler?!"

Luo Chen's eyes shimmered with unusual light, his attention highly focused.

This transcribed *Casual Writings of a Fallen Immortal* actually recorded the life experience of a person named **Xiao Lan**.

Like Luo Chen, this person was a qi-refining cultivator!

He came from an ancient cultivation world called the **Heavenly River**, and his realm before death had reached the unimaginable **Void Refinement** stage.

That was the Void Refinement stage, where one condensed a Dharma Aspect and refined the spirit to return to the void!

Unexpectedly, even such a powerful existence had unfortunately fallen into this world because of a battle.

And what surprised Luo Chen the most was that even someone as strong as Xiao Lan, after healing his injuries and restoring his Void Refinement stage cultivation, still could not successfully leave the Great Annihilation Realm.

At the extreme heights of the nine heavens, there were terrifying astral winds and explosive thunder, enough to tear apart any physical body and destroy any divine soul. There were even chaotic, broken laws of the Dao filling the space. Even when Xiao Lan unfolded his Dharma Aspect and displayed his divine powers, he only ended up heavily injured and falling again.

Later, Xiao Lan tried several more times, only giving up after complete failure.

He began to settle down in the Great Annihilation Realm, establishing the Fallen Immortal City, propagating his bloodline... until his lifespan was nearly exhausted. Still unwilling to accept his fate, Xiao Lan chose to abandon everything and leave quietly, only to seek a sliver of hope to leave the Great Annihilation Realm.

In this *Casual Writings of a Fallen Immortal*, there were also some cultivation insights from high-level qi-refining stages, as if the author, in his boredom and bitterness, had recorded his conjectures and verifications from his past. These included how to condense a Dharma Aspect.

But unfortunately, these cultivation insights, which would be extremely precious in the cultivation world, were completely worthless in the Great Annihilation Realm, which had no spiritual qi. As a result, they had been placed by the Xiao family's descendants in the most inconspicuous first floor of the scripture pavilion.

"A thousand years have passed. I wonder if this senior found a way out?"

If he got out, that would be fine. As long as he could return to the Void Refinement stage, with a lifespan of five thousand years, he could still try to advance further.

If he failed, then he had probably already vanished into smoke and dust!

Luo Chen, while recording these cultivation insights word by word, also imagined the ending of Senior Xiao Lan. His heart was filled with sympathy and emotion for a fellow sufferer in a strange land.

As for these cultivation insights, they were actually useless to Luo Chen at present, like chicken ribs.

But he always held a hope of leaving this world. If he really did get out, these cultivation insights would come in handy.

While recording, Luo Chen could not help but think that there must be more qi-refining cultivators like Xiao Lan and himself in the Great Annihilation Realm. Among them, there were inevitably those who had become disheartened. If he had the chance, perhaps he could obtain some advanced cultivation techniques from them?

But then, Luo Chen could not help but laugh at himself in self-mockery.

Whether he could leave was still an open question. What was the point of going to great lengths to collect these things?

The nine-heaven astral winds, the soul-shaking thunder sounds, and the mysterious, broken laws of the Dao recorded by Xiao Lan were absolutely not something he could face now.

Luo Chen even suspected that even if he relied on the path of body refinement to cultivate to the **Seeing the Divine** realm, where one could break the void, would he be able to break free from the shackles of this world?

After all, this world had no spiritual qi, no spiritual opportunity, and even the world's consciousness and heavenly laws were incomplete!

Outside the study, hurried footsteps sounded.

Soon, there was a knock on the door.

"Brother Luo, the young city lord has come to visit!"

Luo Chen's brush stopped. His brows furrowed slightly, but as if he thought of something, they relaxed in an instant.

"Invite him in!"

Jing Lan hurried away, and soon two sets of footsteps sounded outside the door.

The wooden door was pushed open, and Xiao Feng's figure, holding a folding fan, appeared before Luo Chen.

Before the other party could speak, Luo Chen took the initiative to ask, "Is it about the martial duel to determine the city lord?"

Xiao Feng was startled. "Senior, how did you know it was this matter?"

Luo Chen stood up, looking at the white snow outside the window, and said calmly, "Spring is about to begin. At this critical juncture, the only thing that could make you come to me is this matter."

Xiao Feng took a deep breath, clasped his hands, and bowed deeply to Luo Chen's back. "I ask Senior to lend me a hand!"

(End of this chapter)
