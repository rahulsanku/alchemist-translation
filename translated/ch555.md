# Chapter 555: Fierce Battle Against a Peak Great Demon King!

"So that's a third-tier late-stage great demon king."

Racing through the air, Luo Chen's expression was grave.

Below him, the fires of battle had thinned to scattered embers — he had drifted to the outer edges of the island's rear, well past the main battlefield.

His speed hadn't dropped, but he had opened considerable distance from the pursuing enemies. He had a moment to turn over the recent fights in his mind.

After careful reflection, he had a clear picture of where he stood.

With mana fully unleashed and a sword strike: he could kill a third-tier early-stage demon beast outright.

With his best techniques: a third-tier mid-stage demon beast couldn't withstand him.

But against a third-tier late-stage great demon king, his current toolkit would be strained.

That Nine-Clawed Poison King Crab great demon king had been waiting at leisure, yet he had still needed to deploy both his Blazing Fire Miasma — cultivated over years — and all three Soul-Breaking Nails.

Both cards played, and the Blazing Fire Miasma had been more than half consumed by the demon king's venom. Of the three Soul-Breaking Nails, the Armor-Breaking Nail and the Qi-Breaking Nail were gone; only the Soul-Breaking Nail itself remained.

And even then — he had only barely come out slightly ahead.

In a true fight to the death, he would have needed to pull out considerably more.

On top of that, given the Nine-Clawed Poison King Crab clan's corrosive venom, neither his Mystic Flame Sword nor the Primordial Unity Cauldron were well-suited to engage them. His honest odds of winning: forty to sixty against him.

To win with no suspense at all — perhaps only deploying the Heavenly Roc true body could manage it.

"Great demon king..."

Luo Chen murmured, and let himself feel a quiet awe.

At that level, a demon beast began to approach the standard of same-realm human cultivators. Even if not quite equal, their racial talents narrowed the gap to almost nothing.

His current strength — without burning everything — was genuinely taxed when fighting above his level.

If anyone outside could hear this reasoning, they would likely stare in disbelief.

At a mere Adept Stage Four, he had broken through the interception of demon king after demon king in succession, had even faced a great demon king head-on and briefly come out ahead.

And *still* not satisfied. Truly infuriating.

After that analysis, Luo Chen arrived at his conclusion.

In humanoid form, at Adept Stage Four, his mana reserves, divine soul foundation, and full toolkit were no longer inferior to a third-tier great demon king.

Translating that to human cultivator terms matched exactly what he had already intuited before.

Against a late-stage Adept great cultivator, he now possessed sufficient ability to preserve himself.

And with preparation, he might even contest an Adept Stage Seven great cultivator.

Further, with the full Archaic Tier Three physique activated, the scales would tip further in his favor.

Just as these thoughts settled —

His expression shifted suddenly.

His enhanced eyes caught it: a colossal figure rising from the sea, clutching the still form of a massive Ring-Headed Turtle.

The moment it breached the surface, it raised the Ring-Headed Turtle's corpse with both claws and hurled it directly at him.

Like a mountain being thrown.

The strike came without warning.

Worse — a vast pressure of demon soul aura swept across the area, closing off any path of avoidance.

Luo Chen's expression changed sharply. He began to retreat, his right hand driving the Mystic Flame Sword through arcane traceries in the air.

Left hand forming seal after seal.

Then — one last decisive thrust, the blade aimed at the soaring Ring-Headed Turtle body.

A short shout.

"Collapse!"

A torrent of mana poured from the Mystic Flame Sword and erupted across the sky in a brilliant blaze.

*BOOM!*

High above, that Ring-Headed Turtle body — mountain-sized — shattered and came apart.

Blood scattered like liquid rock. Bone and flesh cracked and exploded.

The already fractured shell, struck by that mana force, split into hundreds of razor-edged shards flying outward in every direction.

Mountain Collapse completed, and the sword-light shrieked upward —

*Hiss!*

Straight into the charging enemy.

*Clang!*

At the last possible instant, the sword struck what felt like an indestructible steel plate.

Luo Chen looked close, and drew a sharp breath.

In the sky above hung a behemoth the size of an island.

Its two great claws shone like gold, sharp beyond reckoning.

The Mystic Flame Sword's full sword-light, everything it had — clashed against those closed claws and could not advance a single inch.

Luo Chen's mind reached for the sword, trying to recall it.

No matter how he tried, the Mystic Flame Sword would not move.

His heart began to sink.

Looking at the enormous figure, a sense of recognition surfaced.

"It's him."

Something familiar stirred at the back of his mind.

Unless his eyes were wrong — this was the Overlord Crab that had once hunted Black King.

Back then, that creature had never revealed its true body. It had appeared unremarkable, and Luo Chen had chosen not to start trouble, thinking to let it go.

Now, fully revealed, it spanned seventy zhang in every direction, hanging across the sky like a city in the clouds.

Its aura billowed out, terrifying in its weight.

Third-tier late stage — almost at Perfected Completion.

Luo Chen swallowed. The gravity in his eyes was absolute.

This was already in the same class as the five great royal crab patriarchs.

He had just kicked a steel plate.

Even as he assessed the creature, Golden Claw fixed him with eyes full of savage hunger.

"You're the mastermind behind the Heavenly Pivot Island incident?"

Facing a demon beast, Luo Chen saw no point in pretense.

And besides — the thing had charged at him the moment it appeared, without a word. Words wouldn't resolve this.

"What if I am?"

Golden Claw let out a savage grin. Its massive golden body resonated with a wave of cracking bone sounds.

"Then you stay."

As the words landed, the great claws clenched.

*Crack.*

Under Luo Chen's gaze, the Mystic Flame Sword — trembling, straining against that grip — snapped and crumbled, scattering into iron fragments that fell to earth.

"Hmph."

A low, suppressed grunt escaped Luo Chen's lips. A thread of blood crept from the corner of his mouth.

Unlike the Soul-Breaking Nails, which he had never deeply refined into himself, the Mystic Flame Sword had accompanied him since the Initiate stage. More than a hundred years of refinement and bonding.

After forming the Golden Core especially, it had been his most trusted treasure — and however modest its quality, he had poured enormous effort into nurturing and refining it.

Of all his possessions, the soul-connection ran deep. He ranked it in his top three.

Second only to the Rotten Axe-Handle Black Chess Pieces — and even stronger than his life-bound treasure in terms of soul-bond.

Now that bond was severed. Through their connection, the shock landed like a strike to his divine soul itself.

As if someone had torn a piece of flesh directly from his body.

He had always known the sword would eventually reach the end of its usefulness. Its quality was mediocre — it could no longer fully bear the overwhelming, pure mana of a mid-stage Adept. That day had always been coming.

But he had never imagined the Mystic Flame Sword would end this way — crushed bodily by an enemy's claws.

Those golden claws.

Luo Chen drew a slow breath. His entire body's blood and qi surged. Azure flame began to coil.

The blood at the corner of his mouth evaporated in the heat.

A fierce, surging pressure began to build and expand from him.

"What gives you the right to think you can keep me?"

Feeling that wholly different pressure, Golden Claw blinked in surprise.

*Demon qi?*

No — human blood and qi. But saturated with enormous quantities of demon qi.

And that demon qi gave it a strange, uncertain feeling. It felt almost like... the same kind?

The thought ignited Golden Claw's fury.

"How dare you — using our clanspeople to temper your body! I'll tear you apart!"

Even as it spoke, its massive form launched forward.

The charge struck like a great beast hitting a mountain — absolute and unstoppable.

Facing that impact, Luo Chen had not a trace of fear. He charged straight back.

As he drove forward, his body began to change.

Endless blood and qi crashed through every muscle and bone from top to bottom.

Crystal-smooth skin cracked and reknit, forming layers like armor — like beast-hide.

White scales spread across the surface, their color deepening, turning a burning, blazing red — like feathers of fire.

Both hands became talons that could cleave through anything. Both eyes widened to the size of a bull's, blazing with razor-sharp spiritual light; along the outer edges of each eye, sheets of keratin membrane began to form.

*Whoosh.*

The Broken Moon Wings snapped open without warning, hanging from the sky, vast enough to block out the sun.

Azure Withering-Bloom True Balefulness surged like a tide, sweeping across the whole body — a ring of azure radiance wrapping him completely.

In that moment, a fire roc came into being.

This was Luo Chen's first deployment of the Heavenly Roc true body in actual combat — achieved only after advancing to Archaic Tier Three late stage.

And its first opponent: a near-peak Overlord Crab at the very summit of the third tier.

Azure flame churned and rolled, pouring into the sky.

One fierce shout split the air.

"Come!"

In an instant, the transformation was complete.

Two colossal figures, propelled by an absolute, no-retreat momentum, crashed into each other.

*BOOM!*

The force of the impact converted into a shockwave that swept in every direction.

A low hill nearby was silently demolished by that pressure.

The earth trembled, as if a dragon had turned in its sleep below.

*A contest of force.*

It began on first contact.

And ended just as quickly.

*Thud!*

One strike — the massive crab was hurled to the earth, blasting a crater into the ground.

The fire roc was flung back several hundred zhang, wildly beating its wings in midair to stabilize itself.

"Again!"

From the earth, Golden Claw tore free and shot skyward.

Luo Chen's expression turned sharp. He dove.

*Crash!*

One short exchange — then separation.

Then again.

One, two, three times...

After the ninth round, two enormous figures faced each other across the air, both breathing hard.

Both bore heavy wounds.

Luo Chen looked at himself. Countless wounds, large and small, covered every inch — flesh turned back, brutal and raw.

All of them inflicted by Golden Claw's pair of great claws and eight crab legs in those close-range exchanges.

He looked across at Golden Claw. The great crab had not fared well either.

Across its golden shell, wound after wound.

The hidden strike technique of the Broken Moon Wings — fire-feather blades, which he had never before released, only because years of careful refinement had finally made them ready — had come into use for the first time here. Against the Overlord Crab's shell, they had left cuts like small blades in their wake.

Besides the fire-feather strikes, the Overlord Crab's golden shell was also pitted and cratered — as though eaten by some corrosive fluid. Those were the remnants of his Blazing Fire Miasma, the vicious balefulness spreading across its body.

And among all the wounds, one stood out above the rest: a five-fingered claw print, the marks punching clean through a thin section of shell and reaching down into bone.

The Cloud-Seizing Divine Claw, deployed at Perfected Mastery under the Heavenly Roc true body.

The most savage flesh-to-flesh exchange — and the most dangerous wound-trading of all.

But clearly, Luo Chen's physical defenses had not yet reached their peak, and so the injuries he bore were the most direct and severe.

Golden Claw, by contrast, had been refining its shell and claws in the direction of a magic treasure — a form that combined offense and defense as one.

"I got careless."

Luo Chen caught his breath, with a faint self-reproach.

He rarely deployed the Heavenly Roc true body against enemies. He had felt long before that there was room for improvement — and the fight confirmed it.

But mid-battle was no time for adjustments.

This body of his was the product of years of bitter cultivation. Its potential was enormous.

Even without fully unlocking it, he had spent years pondering what fighting methods would pair with it. A thought formed.

A small cauldron flew from his mouth.

In the wind, it expanded instantly — a terrifying great cauldron fifty zhang in size.

Luo Chen caught one of the cauldron's legs in one hand. A pity about the Mystic Flame Sword — otherwise one hand holding the sword, one arm swinging the cauldron would have been exactly the fighting form he'd imagined.

Still. As it was, it was more than enough.

He fixed Golden Claw with a ferocious look.

"This time, I will not hold back."

The words barely finished — he took one step into the void.

A sharp crack rang through empty space.

His enormous body vanished, reappearing directly above Golden Claw.

The great cauldron in hand, he brought it crashing down.

Golden Claw's eyes blazed with fury. One great claw swept upward to meet it.

One strike.

*Crack!*

The claw tore free from its body — falling like a meteorite, slamming into the earth.

And Luo Chen was sent flying back several dozen zhang, a clear white void-mark sliced through the air in his wake.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron itself was flung from his grip.

Pure rebound force — neither came away unscathed.

And worse — Luo Chen's wounds exploded in fresh blood, as if rivers were flowing from him.

"I can't keep fighting."

As he forcibly recalled the Primordial Unity Cauldron, the thought settled in his mind.

Another exchange would end in mutual destruction.

But leaving — after several rounds of combat, he had confirmed it was possible.

With the Heavenly Roc true body active, his Nine Thousand Li movement art had broken through another ceiling.

An Overlord Crab clan member like Golden Claw — whose kind were, by nature, "slow-moving" — simply could not hold him.

Before going, though, Luo Chen had one thing left to do.

"What is your name?"

Golden Claw glared at him with raging hatred. One remaining claw raised horizontal. "I am Golden Claw, the Overlord Clan's mightiest warrior. I don't kill nameless enemies. Tell me your name."

Luo Chen nodded. "Good. Golden Claw. I, Azure Yang, will remember you."

With that, his wings beat once and he shot toward the open sea beyond Xuanyan Island.

Golden Claw stared after him, stunned for a moment —

Then erupted in fury.

*At this point in the fight, and it's just going to run?!*

He had never suffered such a disgrace. Even one of his claws was severed.

He had already steeled himself for a battle to the death with this cultivator named Azure Yang — and the man had simply left.

"Coward! You don't deserve to know my name — GET BACK HERE!"

Screaming in rage, he gave chase.

But just as he was about to fly clear of Xuanyan Island, he stopped.

He snapped around to face the island's center.

A powerful aura had erupted — filling all four directions.

His expression changed.

"That's the aura of the Xuanyan Demon Emperor. Could this be Qihuan and Hailo finally making their move?"

And at that moment, five enormous demon clouds rose from the sea's surface and descended on Xuanyan Island.

A rumbling sound rolled across the world.

"All demon kings of the five clans — join us and kill Hailo. Hold nothing back!"

Golden Claw heard those words and hesitated.

In the end, he looked once more with blazing hatred at the direction Luo Chen had fled — then turned and flew toward Xuanyan Island's center.

In this battle, personal gain was secondary.

The clan's interest came first.

*(End of Chapter)*
