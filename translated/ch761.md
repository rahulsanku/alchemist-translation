# Chapter 761: Fire Burns Three-Rainbow Falls, a Sword Rises from Cloud-Dream Marsh

Hiss, hiss, hiss...

Beside the great lake, a giant python flicked its long tongue and rustled in circles along the shore.

At any ordinary time, such behavior would have been nothing.

But just now, every illusion beast was charging toward Three-Rainbow Falls, while this giant python illusion beast did not follow the tide. That made it truly strange.

All at once!

The giant python shuddered from head to tail, its eyes going vacant, and slowly swayed its enormous body away from the lakeside.

Only after it had gone several li did the confusion in its eyes gradually fade.

It looked back at the great lake in puzzlement, but very soon its attention was caught by the charge of its own kind, and it too joined that raging tide of giant beasts.

Above the great lake, turbid waves rolled, as though stirred up by the tremors all around.

Beneath the great lake, Luo Chen let out a breath of relief.

"Strange. I clearly withdrew the formation yesterday, so why are there still so many illusion beasts being drawn here? Their numbers haven't lessened at all."

"Could it be that so many illusion beasts were entrenched across these tens of thousands of li at the same time?"

He could not make sense of it no matter how he thought.

Although this trap had been laid in haste, he had still put considerable thought into it.

To avoid being discovered by Gu Daosu's Dao protector, Madam Xu, he had already withdrawn the formation when there were about seven or eight illusion beasts.

He had also pushed his concealment art to the utmost!

Although the present situation was somewhat out of control, there were still gains to be had.

Luo Chen lifted his head and looked toward the sky, but the lake water was turbid, and nothing could be seen.

"Too blurry!"

Luo Chen murmured, mobilizing a stream of pure magic power and slowly beginning to transform it.

Abruptly, he lightly tapped a finger against the rippling water before him.

"Cloud-Chess Water Mirror, reflect heaven and earth!"

Hum...

Ripples visible to the naked eye slowly spread through the water.

The clouds in the sky seemed to open cracks as well, and strands of heavenly light projected down from the cloud layers onto the vast lake surface.

In an instant, this nameless great lake became gorgeous beyond description!

This spell was not profound.

But in terms of subtlety, it was rare in the world!

It was something Luo Chen had formed by combining the low-grade Water Mirror with the cloud patterns of formation fundamentals, with a faint trace of inspiration gained from the Peach Garden chess formation.

At this moment, the clouds in the sky were like chess pieces dotted above the battlefield, gathering every scene together and imprinting them upon this great lake's water mirror.

Scene after scene from the outside world also fell vividly into Luo Chen's bright, lively eyes.

There was a beautiful woman furious with rage, driving a jade hairpin to hang high overhead.

There were gathered Sages holding their forces still, yet watching like tigers eyeing prey.

There were more than a dozen illusion beasts, led by a huge white eagle, launching a charge toward Three-Rainbow Falls at the same time.

In the far distance, rolling smoke and dust could faintly be seen, as though still more formidable illusion beasts were coming.

"This little entertainment has gotten rather big."

Luo Chen pursed his lips, and not only was there no worry in his eyes, there was instead an inexplicable excitement.

The greater the risk, the greater the profit.

Especially since he was not the one standing in the open, but the oriole waiting behind!

Smack!

Luo Chen slapped the small gray cauldron before him with one palm.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron instantly transformed, changing from about a zhang in size into a hundred-zhang giant, crouching at the bottom of the great lake.

The enormous mouth of the cauldron opened toward the outside world like a beast's blood-filled maw.

Luo Chen closed his eyes, then opened them again!

Whoosh!

A divided soul identical to his main soul flew out from his sea of consciousness.

Without the cover of the sea of consciousness, and without a Sage to bear it, the divided soul looked extremely uncomfortable the moment it emerged, even in the water.

But it did not hesitate at all and directly drilled into the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

"With a divided soul driving my lifebound magic treasure at full power, its might should be enough."

"But if I'm exposed, relying on only these will still be lacking."

As Luo Chen's thoughts turned, he swept his right hand forward.

Clang!

The blood sword silently appeared. Luo Chen gripped it in one hand, then thrust it into the thick mud beside him.

"Next, all that remains is to wait for the fine show to begin!"

Luo Chen tilted his neck slightly and focused on the many scenes reflected upon the lake surface.

...

Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh!

In the sky, the jade hairpin transformed into countless streaks of light and shadow, then suddenly condensed, all of them shooting into a giant ape that had climbed onto Three-Rainbow Falls.

Boom!

In a single instant, the giant ape, which had climbed the sheer cliff as though it were level ground, was shattered apart, turning into boundless white mist.

Madam Xu had no time to rejoice that her strike had succeeded. Her whole body shot into the sky like an arrow leaving the string, her gaze fixed only on that white eagle.

The water sleeves at her wrists flowed and turned, becoming as hard as steel in the blink of an eye.

Both sleeves shook at once, and the water sleeves shot out like long spears.

The white eagle turned nimbly, actually dodging the two water sleeves by the barest margin.

But just as it tried to break free, those two water sleeves instantly softened and wrapped around its body.

"Get away from me!"

Madam Xu's willow brows stood on end, and the surging magic power of a mid-stage Sage pulled at the white eagle, trying to shake it apart.

Yet in the next moment, her expression changed again.

The white eagle spread both wings with all its strength, and with tremendous force, it forcibly burst open the water sleeves, then charged resolutely toward Madam Xu.

An embroidered handkerchief appeared before the woman, its light and shadow hazy.

Boom!

With only one blow, the white eagle was sent flying back several hundred zhang.

Madam Xu, meanwhile, was smashed straight into Three-Rainbow Falls like a meteor.

Great clouds of smoke and dust spread, and within the mountain, rings of golden light also diffused outward.

"Is that the Mountain-Suppressing Balance-Guarding Formation?" Gu Yuan asked.

Gu Chen nodded. "Young Master has good eyes. That is indeed one of our sect's most famous fourth-tier mountain-protecting grand formations."

Gu Yuan clicked his tongue in praise. "I never expected that for this grotto-heaven competition, the Gu Great House would not only recall Madam Xu and have Senior Brother Daoyin obtain the Rotten Axe-Handle Black Chess Pieces, but even set up a mountain-protecting grand formation. With such heavy capital spent, no wonder Gu Daosu walked ahead of us."

Gu Chen's expression was cold. "Without anyone presiding over it, this formation will not last long, especially since this world's spirit veins are insubstantial and there is no earth qi to support it. The foundation of the grand formation is unstable to begin with. With that white eagle comparable to a mid-stage Sage at the side, Madam Xu will likely be unable to protect Gu Daosu."

"Unable to protect him is good!" Gu Yuan said with a light laugh.

Next, it would depend on whether Madam Xu collapsed first, or that grand formation collapsed first.

Whichever it was, it would mean Gu Daosu no longer had any protective power, and that state of sudden enlightenment would also be broken.

When a formation on the level of a mountain-protecting grand formation appeared, the many cultivators watching from the scene cried out in alarm.

Such a grand formation was extremely difficult to set up!

And it consumed an enormous amount of materials.

For the Gu Great House to use it in a single competition trial was truly somewhat extravagant.

But this was the foundation of the Twelve Great Houses. For a possible Ascendant seedling, even if they had to pay ten times or twenty times more, they would do so gladly.

Mingyan watched this scene, and apart from envy, she was also somewhat worried.

"Senior Brother, if even such a grand formation cannot protect Gu Daosu, then when our time comes..."

Before she finished, Sage Dragon Abyss raised a hand and cut her off.

"With me here, I am enough to protect you. And if you succeed and comprehend origin power, then whether inside this grotto-heaven or in the outside world, you will be able to move unhindered. At that time, if you protect me, nothing will happen either."

Mingyan nodded.

Only, when her gaze turned toward those charging giant beasts, she seemed somewhat eager to act.

"Senior Brother, so many illusion beasts have gathered here. Should we..."

"Wait a little longer. Let's see how the others move!"

Sage Dragon Abyss spoke calmly, his eyes unconsciously sweeping across the surroundings.

There were people in the forest, one old and one young. They should be from the Bochuan Wang clan.

In a small mountain several li away, everything seemed perfectly ordinary, but a thread of aura had accidentally leaked earlier. If he remembered correctly, it should be Yu Xu of the Yu family. Since she had appeared, her Dao protector was certainly following by her side as well, and Southern Frontier gu cultivators were not easy to deal with.

If it were only these people, that would be one thing.

But Gu Yuan and Shi Ju of the Three Great Houses were both watching like tigers from the side. If he wanted to make a move and seize benefits, it would be no different from snatching chestnuts from the fire.

He looked toward the clouds. Shi Ju and Qi Xiongcheng, who carried a purple-gold gourd on his back, were standing there.

When he looked over, the other party also glanced his way.

Yet for some reason, Sage Dragon Abyss felt as though a third person were watching him.

"Could there be someone else above the clouds?"

Just as he was wondering, Madam Xu, who had previously been struck into the mountain, appeared again.

Golden formation light slowly lifted her up.

Her hair was disheveled, and her face was fierce.

An eagle cry rang out in the air, as if in provocation.

But Madam Xu paid no heed to the illusion beast's provocation. Instead, she spread out her divine sense and sent a transmission.

"Fellow disciples, will you still not act and aid me?"

No one answered.

She gritted her teeth and offered terms.

"I will fully activate the Mountain-Suppressing Balance-Guarding Formation and slaughter every illusion beast here. Of the illusion cores obtained, I will take not a single one and will let all of you divide them as you please!"

"Not only that, I also have seven extra illusion cores on me, originally kept as backup for Su'er. If you help me resolve this predicament, these seven illusion cores will also be given to you!"

At last, she said in a deep voice, "Those who act will gain the friendship of my Gu Great House!"

With that, she no longer tried to persuade them, but devoted herself wholly to presiding over the grand formation.

Ring after ring of golden light spread out from Three-Rainbow Falls.

Every illusion beast that entered within fifty li seemed to have a mountain over its head, its movements slowed severalfold.

Not only that, stone cones condensed one after another in the air and stabbed toward those illusion beasts.

Across the earth, tremors burst out one after another.

She was actually using her own strength alone to drive the grand formation into attacking every illusion beast!

Everyone knew this was something that could not be sustained.

Just as they expected.

Madam Xu's face grew paler and paler, and her mid-stage Sage aura also continued to recede as she presided over the formation.

"In the end, she is not a formation grandmaster. Relying on brute cultivation to preside over a grand formation is far too taxing."

In the forest, the elder among the old and young pair made this assessment.

The youth beside him watched for a while, then suddenly said, "Clan Elder, make your move!"

The elder glanced at the youth. "For what?"

The youth smiled faintly. "Illusion cores, the Gu Great House's friendship, and besides, Senior Brother Gu's relationship with me in the sect can be considered decent. There is no harm in helping."

"Very well. Then I will do as you say."

The elder answered, stepped out of the forest, flicked his wrist, and a verdant bamboo staff extended, striking toward an illusion beast.

His sudden move seemed to break the balance.

Before long, the others also began to act and lend aid.

With the grand formation assisting them, these Sage cultivators were like fish in water inside it, and slaughtering fourth-tier illusion beasts became far easier than usual.

Even Shi Ju and the other man no longer stood high in the clouds, but entered the formation to help.

Only, unlike the others, among these two it was actually Shi Ju who acted, while Qi Xiongcheng assisted at the side.

Shi Ju's performance when he attacked was no weaker than those Dao protectors invited by the great families. In some respects, he even surpassed them.

As a folding fan opened and closed, scorching and tyrannical flame currents swept across the four fields.

In an instant, large numbers of illusion beasts were shattered apart, turning into boundless white mist that scattered into the air.

Illusion cores raced through the mist one after another at incredible speed, and vast amounts of cloud and fog continuously gathered toward them.

Qi Xiongcheng firmly remembered Shi Ju's instructions, driving the Purple Yang Gourd to absorb the cloud and fog and capture those illusion cores as much as possible.

Divide them equally?

Impossible!

Since Shi Ju had acted, he could not possibly divide them equally with the others.

On the contrary, the others were making wedding clothes for him, and especially with that mountain-protecting grand formation assisting him, he hunted illusion beasts as easily as a fish in water.

"Old ghost, how many illusion cores have you collected?"

Shi Ju asked while fighting the illusion beasts.

"One so far."

Shi Ju frowned. "Why so slow? In this short time, at least five illusion beasts have been killed, and you have only collected one illusion core?"

Qi Xiongcheng had bitterness he could not speak. For some reason, another force was drawing away that cloud and fog, making it extremely difficult for him to collect the illusion cores.

He only assumed the illusion beasts were too strong, and that he had spent too little time refining the Purple Yang Gourd.

Shi Ju suppressed the irritation in his heart. "Do your best to collect the illusion cores. Tell me once you reach five."

With five illusion cores, this trip would not have been in vain.

But would five illusion cores be enough for the Young Master?

Qi Xiongcheng was puzzled, but the movements of his hands did not pause in the slightest.

The people present also gradually sensed something strange.

The illusion beasts slain by everyone began to disappear before their illusion cores had been collected.

If it had only been one or two, that would have been fine, but in a short span, several illusion beasts had neither condensed bodies nor left behind illusion cores.

Finally, someone saw Qi Xiongcheng, who had not joined the battle and had instead been driving the Purple Yang Gourd the entire time.

"What are you doing?" Gu Chen asked coldly.

Qi Xiongcheng kept his mouth shut and said nothing.

Shi Ju, however, withdrew from the fight. Qi Xiongcheng nodded at him, which meant he had already collected at least five illusion cores.

In that case, there was no longer any need to risk life and death for others.

"When my Shi Great House handles matters, I need not explain myself to you!"

Afterward, paying no heed to Gu Chen's ashen face, Shi Ju raised his folding fan and brought it down with force toward Three-Rainbow Falls, which was close at hand!

He laughed wildly and said:

"Gu Daosu, you should come out and see this scenery too!"

Flame clouds that blotted the sky, accompanied by fierce winds, surged upstream.

Wherever they passed, the golden formation light vanished inch by inch.

Any mountain-protecting grand formation was extremely difficult to break from the outside, but if one attacked from within...

When Madam Xu, who was using all her strength to suppress the white eagle, saw this scene, her eyes nearly split with rage.

"No!"

In the distance.

Gu Yuan, who had not joined the battle, nodded slightly.

"As expected, Shi Ju has never been someone who follows rules. How could he be so kind as to help Gu Daosu? This is fine as well. I can harvest illusion cores, Gu Daosu cannot continue maintaining his state of sudden enlightenment, and this enmity will not even fall on me."

Rumble...

A deafening sound rang out. The once incomparably beautiful Three-Rainbow Falls instantly stopped flowing, and great quantities of rock tumbled down.

The entire great mountain began to tremble.

Moreover, without the formation's assistance, Madam Xu, whose realm had long since declined, could no longer hold back that white eagle.

The other party transformed into a streak of light and shot straight toward Three-Rainbow Falls.

Madam Xu's eyes were filled with despair, but her body resolutely and decisively blocked the direct path of the charge.

One person and one beast collided in an instant, then plunged into the great mountain without the slightest delay.

Smoke and dust rose at once, and waves rolled through the earth.

Everyone immediately withdrew, leaping into the high sky to watch this scene.

"Can Madam Xu survive?"

"Likely not. She had already used a forbidden method to forcefully raise her realm, and then Shi Ju broke the grand formation, causing a backlash against her body. With this white eagle's collision on top of that, she is probably doomed."

"What about Gu Daosu?"

"With such a huge disturbance, his state of sudden enlightenment definitely can't continue."

Just as everyone was discussing it heatedly.

Whoosh!

A dazzling pillar of light shot into the sky.

Along with it came a great eagle hurled out through the air.

The great eagle flipped several times in midair before finally steadying itself, then glared viciously toward the former site of Three-Rainbow Falls.

Within the broken waterfall, a young man with picturesque brows and eyes stood at the moss-covered cave entrance, holding a bronze mirror. Behind him, Madam Xu sat collapsed on the ground. Though her aura was withered, she was still alive after all.

At the sight of this, everyone was shocked!

Gu Daosu had awakened!

And he had even personally acted, forcibly repelling that great eagle.

Could it be that he had already succeeded in comprehending origin power?

"Ha ha, he failed in the end! What he used is the Gu Great House's Wuwang Treasure Mirror. It isn't his own power at all."

Shi Ju laughed wildly, immensely proud of his own success.

Everyone looked at that bronze mirror one after another, revealing looks of realization.

The Gu Great House's Wuwang Treasure Mirror had a rather great reputation. Its formidable aspect lay in its ability to store a cultivator's all-out strike, and it was even enough to store several such strikes.

Clearly, that earlier attack was not something Gu Daosu could do, but the work of the Wuwang Treasure Mirror.

Within the shattered great mountain.

Madam Xu asked weakly, "Did you fail?"

Gu Daosu sighed. "I was a little short. Only a little."

Only a little?

While Madam Xu felt regret, thick fury rose in her eyes.

"It's all that fellow Shi Ju's fault!"

If Shi Ju had not turned on them and broken the Mountain-Suppressing Balance-Guarding Formation, then even if she had to stake this life of hers, she absolutely could have bought Gu Daosu enough time for sudden enlightenment.

Everything was his fault!

After they got out, their Gu Great House would definitely make the Shi Great House give them an explanation!

However!

"It is not his fault."

Amid Madam Xu's shock, Gu Daosu raised the bronze mirror high, shining it toward heaven and earth from afar.

As the mirror illuminated heaven and earth, he spoke slowly in a low voice.

"During my sudden enlightenment, I sensed something strange in this region. Someone harbored wicked intentions and used a formation to disturb my spiritual resonance. That person is the chief culprit. Shi Ju and the others are only chess pieces inside the game, manipulated by another."

"Found you!"

Abruptly!

The cloud layers scattered, and the heavenly waves rippled.

The Wuwang Treasure Mirror emitted a sharp azure light, stopping over a great lake in the far distance.

Gu Daosu said in a clear voice:

"Fellow Daoist, have you watched enough?"

Everyone followed the sound and looked over.

The great lake's waters were utterly still, but clouds and mist gathered around it, as dreamlike as a vast marsh, exceedingly ethereal.

If not for the treasure mirror's light shining upon it, no one would have been able to clearly see that calm water surface, which was like a mirror.

And those wisps of cloud and mist being continuously sucked into the lake were precisely the illusion beasts whose forms had been shattered apart.

When they looked over, they happened to see an illusion core being silently drawn into the great lake.

This scene needed no explanation. Everyone understood the reason behind it.

Leaving aside what thoughts arose in their hearts.

Between heaven and earth, only a soft laugh could be heard.

Then, reflected in every pair of eyes, there appeared a smear of blood-red.

A sword light soared into the sky from the marsh that was like cloud, like dream!

*(End of Chapter)*
