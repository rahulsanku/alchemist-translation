# Chapter 1112: Grotto-Heaven in Hand, the Barren Wasteland

With a splash!

The turbid waves parted, and a white-robed Daoist descended from the sky, standing in the black water.

His hands shimmered with fluorescence as he bent down, reaching toward the peculiar existence that radiated light from the massive pit left after Heaven-Earth Peak had been removed.

Yet this grab was like trying to catch the moon's reflection in the water—he grasped nothing but air.

"An illusion?"

Luo Chen was astonished.

With his level of divine sense, illusions that could deceive him were exceedingly rare in this world.

Luo Chen vaguely guessed at something, and an expression of great interest appeared on his face.

Without a second thought, he poured two completely different powers—spiritual power and source power—into his eyes. When he opened them, his crimson eyes and golden pupils manifested once more!

Something that could deceive divine sense might not necessarily be able to deceive these eyes.

These were spiritual eyes capable of breaking illusions, breaking through emptiness, and even tearing through space itself. No demon or monster could hide before them.

As expected!

After Luo Chen opened his Crimson Eye Golden Pupils, he finally detected the location of the light point.

It was an existence like a pearl.

Silently buried beneath Heaven-Earth Peak.

When his divine sense swept over it, it felt like a small world.

Layers of white mist shrouded it, and occasionally black water seeped out.

In an instant, a name surfaced in his mind.

"Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven!"

No wonder this thing could deceive his divine sense.

The Mirage Dragon itself was a top-tier great desolate beast, capable of conjuring sun, moon, and stars, forming its own grotto-heaven paradise. After its death, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect had transformed its body into the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, but it had retained its illusory properties. Because of this characteristic, it had become the most suitable secret realm for Heavenly Origin Dao Sect disciples to train in.

But looking at it now, perhaps the rumors were mistaken.

"It's not a corpse, but its inner core!"

Luo Chen took a deep breath, a hint of excitement flickering in his eyes.

Demon beasts also had inner cores, but this was mostly limited to lower ranks.

Once they reached the fourth-rank Transformation Stage, they would shatter their cores and form nascent souls like human cultivators, embarking on the path of demon cultivation.

But desolate beasts remained consistent from beginning to end, rarely transforming.

Therefore, their inner cores were truly the essence of their entire being.

Luo Chen hadn't expected that the so-called Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven was essentially just the Mirage Dragon's inner core.

"The wonders of heaven and earth are truly too marvelous. To us, it's merely a desolate beast's inner core, but to the creatures living inside it, what difference is there from a planetary world?"

"According to the memories of my divided soul, the Ancient Gu Great House, one of the Twelve Great Houses, used the time flow difference within the grotto-heaven to cultivate ten-thousand-year spiritual medicines inside."

"If I gain control of this grotto-heaven, can I follow suit?"

Luo Chen had never considered using the time flow difference of the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven for cultivation—that method was too dangerous.

The Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven itself was not suitable for cultivator training. Its spiritual veins were scattered, and its spirit apertures were unformed. The reason Ke Lianshan and the Dao Sect disciples had been able to cultivate inside it last time was because the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard had absorbed spiritual energy and stabilized the grotto-heaven's spiritual veins.

Now that the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard was destroyed, the grotto-heaven's spiritual veins would inevitably be disrupted again.

But unformed spiritual veins, though unsuitable for cultivation, had little impact on the growth of herbs and spiritual plants.

As long as the overall environment remained, the medicinal power would gradually accumulate as the years passed.

Moreover, his current body refinement path had reached a bottleneck. Relying solely on body refinement techniques would be difficult to break through. Only the Demon-Devil Heart-Linking Tree might be able to help him.

This Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven might allow the Demon-Devil Heart-Linking Tree to bear a fruit.

Of course, according to the research results left by Sang Jinghe before his death, cultivating the Demon-Devil Heart-Linking Tree also required the heart blood of ancient demons, ancient devils, or high-level great desolate beasts—an indispensable condition.

But for Luo Chen, this wasn't difficult, as long as he was willing!

"First, I'll take this treasure!"

Luo Chen hesitated no longer. He reached out again, grasping toward the pearl in the water.

This grab felt like lifting an entire world.

Large clusters of mist dispersed, and the deep, dark underworld water gurgled and flowed, until finally, Luo Chen raised his hand with difficulty.

He looked at it—a sight that seized the heart.

A pearl in his palm—the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven!

Luo Chen probed it with his divine sense, and a vast scene came into view, but the grotto-heaven's area seemed to have shrunk many times compared to his memory.

"Was it because of the influence of the last great battle?"

When Luo Chen came back to his senses, his expression was somewhat uncertain.

"Not only has the area shrunk, but the time flow also seems to have slowed down considerably... Could it be that this secret realm is dying?"

The death of a secret realm sounded somewhat horrifying, but it was the most common situation in the cultivation world.

Most secret realms were left behind by predecessors or formed from special spatial structures.

But if the foundations of a secret realm—such as formations, treasures, or even spiritual veins and space—gradually collapsed and decayed, the entire secret realm would become unstable. Eventually, it would either perish or merge with reality, becoming part of this world.

Of course, in that case, the secret realm's special nature would naturally cease to exist.

"There should still be time. If I can extract the underworld water that permeates the entire grotto-heaven and supplement it with restrictive formations, I might be able to delay this process."

"For now, this secret realm still has great use for me!"

Luo Chen exhaled a turbid breath and tucked the Mirage Dragon inner core into his bosom.

This thing itself formed a grotto-heaven, so it couldn't be placed into a storage ring or the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Otherwise, spatial repulsion would cause one side to be torn apart or compressed into collapse.

Putting away the Mirage Dragon inner core, Luo Chen gazed into the distance, silently contemplating.

Because of this unexpected gain, his ascension plan had temporarily changed. Time might be running short.

"I'll have to make a trip to the Barren Wasteland!"

...

Eastern Wilderness, Alchemy Saint Blessed Land.

Within a great black lake.

Waves rolled, and a massive creature gradually emerged.

It silently looked toward the lakeshore, where a woman with a fiery figure and a long, swan-like neck was waiting quietly.

Seeing the giant turtle emerge, the woman spoke.

"Xuanming, the master commands that we rush to Green Parasol Mountain immediately!"

The great turtle Xuanming snorted, spraying water columns from its nostrils, nodded silently, and slowly crawled out of the Nether Lake on four legs as thick as heavenly pillars.

Tianxuan smiled slightly. "Wait at the entrance of the blessed land. I'll notify the other two."

Tianxuan hurried off without stopping, flying toward a deep canyon within the blessed land.

Soon, when she came out, Black King followed, shaking his head.

"Tianxuan, what great matter is the master planning this time, that he needs all of us to move out together?"

"The master commands; just follow orders. Why ask so many questions?"

"Tch, you little girl, always acting high and mighty. If this turns out to be a wasted trip, don't blame me for giving you a beating."

"With your pathetically slow flying speed, catch me first!"

The two bickered as usual, but their movements were not slow.

When they arrived at the entrance of the blessed land, besides the great turtle Xuanming, the demon monk Kongchan was already waiting.

And there was another person who somewhat surprised Tianxuan.

"Mistress, why are you here?"

Gu Caiyi, dressed in magnificent palace attire, asked calmly, "Can't I come?"

Tianxuan quickly waved her hand. "That's not what I meant, maidservant. It's just that this journey is dangerous, and the destination is quite sensitive, so the master only called the four of us."

Gu Caiyi looked around at the four. Black King and Xuanming were desolate beasts; Kongchan and Tianxuan were demon beasts.

Clearly, the place they were going was not suitable for human cultivators.

And for Tianxuan, a Sage-level being, to say it was dangerous showed that this journey was no easy task.

Gu Caiyi sighed. "I want to see him."

Tianxuan opened her mouth, then finally nodded.

"Then let's set off together!"

As for what came after, let the master worry about it. Why should she trouble herself?

Four beasts and one human left the blessed land together.

They crossed the Nether Abyss, arrived at the Luo Tian Sect's mountain gate, and then took a teleportation formation.

When they reappeared, they were in a land of birdsong and floral fragrance.

Gu Caiyi emerged from the underground teleportation hall, looking at the circling birds and the lush, verdant Green Parasol Mountain, her eyes filled with intense curiosity.

"Is this where my husband ascended to Ascendant—the legendary feather tribe's sacred land, Green Parasol Mountain?"

"Exactly!"

Tianxuan nodded, her eyes full of reverence.

News of Luo Chen's ascension to Ascendant had gradually spread in recent years.

Whether it was the wolf tribe of the Million Great Mountains suffering heavy losses, or Luo Chen's appearances in the Northern Sea and Southern Frontier, it had greatly influenced the changes in the power structure of the various major forces in the Mountains and Seas Realm.

As the sect Luo Chen had founded, the Luo Tian Sect's information on these matters, though delayed, was not lacking.

Especially with witnesses like Tianxuan and Kongchan.

From afar, an ethereal voice sounded.

"Is this the master's wife?"

Gu Caiyi turned to look and saw a woman with picturesque features and an extraordinary temperament walking toward them with a smile.

"This is...?" Gu Caiyi asked curiously.

Tianxuan quickly explained. "The current hall master of Daytime Void Hall, Fairy Huamei, and also the master's disciple."

Gu Caiyi raised an eyebrow. "Disciple? Like Xu Muxian?"

Huamei smiled slightly. "The master's wife jokes. Huamei is only the master's nominal disciple."

Xu Muxian had once been only a nominal disciple too.

Gu Caiyi thought this.

Then she looked around, vaguely seeing the magnificent palaces on two distant mountain peaks.

The highest one was Tongxuan Hall, once inhabited by Rosycloud.

Opposite Daytime Void Hall should be Truth Crossing Hall, the place her husband Luo Chen had governed.

Following her gaze, Fairy Huamei explained softly, "The master has now stepped down from the position of Truth Crossing Hall master, passing it to Pure Crane. Daytime Void Hall has been given to me. As for the master, he is temporarily residing in Tongxuan Hall."

Gu Caiyi was slightly bewildered, not expecting that Luo Chen had already arranged everything concerning Green Parasol Mountain.

Her heart was filled only with relief.

Fortunately, she had come along this time. Otherwise, given her husband's style, once everything was properly arranged, after returning to the Luo Tian Sect, he would immediately begin preparations for ascension.

Then the time she could spend with him would be pitifully short.

"Everyone, please follow me. The master has been waiting in Tongxuan Hall for some time."

Without hesitation, the group followed Fairy Huamei, flying toward Tongxuan Hall.

At the great entrance, a burly man with a blade of grass in his mouth was guarding, looking utterly bored.

Seeing the group, he merely nodded casually and let them pass.

Gu Caiyi glanced at him, and her heart tightened.

A late-stage Sage great demon emperor!

Her husband's status among the demon race was higher than she had imagined, to have a great demon emperor as a gatekeeper.

Inside the hall.

Pure Crane was reporting to Luo Chen on the current situation of the demon race in the Million Great Mountains.

"On the tiger tribe's side, Splendid Immortal is supporting the Emperor's daughter, wanting to reorganize the demon court. But because of Emperor Slaughter's influence, the various tribes can't cause much trouble."

"As for the wolf tribe, with Aokuang's fall, they are fragmented and currently engaged in internal strife."

"The feather tribe's status is now the highest in the Million Great Mountains. With the three hall masters re-established by you, the power structure is already clear and distinct. There is no dissent within the feather tribe. Although Thunder Prison was unwilling to take the position of Tongxuan Hall master, the title of Feather Tribe Grand Commander you bestowed is enough for him to suppress the petty villains."

Now, Green Parasol Mountain had its three halls re-established.

Pure Crane governed Truth Crossing Hall, Fairy Huamei governed Daytime Void Hall, and the position of Tongxuan Hall master remained vacant.

No one dared to replace the position Rosycloud had once held.

Luo Chen had originally intended Thunder Prison Roc, but the other party also dared not overstep, so he had given him another title—Feather Tribe Grand Commander.

Thus, the power structure of the feather tribe's sacred land, Green Parasol Mountain, was no longer chaotic but became clear and distinct.

It could be said that Luo Chen had handled all the "affairs after his death" within his ability, in an orderly manner.

Additionally, he had recorded Xu Muxian's name in Truth Crossing Hall.

This allowed him free access to Green Parasol Mountain while not being under Truth Crossing Hall's jurisdiction.

The purpose of this arrangement was naturally only one—to make it convenient for Xu Muxian to occasionally return to the Northern Sea and look after the Swallow Islands.

Pure Crane, who was reporting, suddenly fell silent because the Luo Chen above had turned his gaze toward the hall's entrance.

Several figures walked in one after another.

Pure Crane looked them over. There were familiar faces and strangers.

But it was the stranger who drew her attention the most.

Because as Luo Chen beckoned, she naturally walked up and sat beside him.

"Why have you come?"

"I heard that my husband is about to journey to the Barren Wasteland. Your concubine has been in bitter cultivation at the blessed land for a long time and also wants to go out and see the world. Will I hold my husband back?"

"Haha, aren't you asking knowingly? But since you're here, there's no reason to send you back. Don't worry, with my abilities, I can protect you."

Gu Caiyi's heart was delighted. She knew that occasionally acting a little spoiled would not bother Luo Chen.

Especially now that Luo Chen had the ability to take care of everything!

After chatting for a bit, Luo Chen turned his gaze to the turtle that had shrunk its size and entered the hall together.

"Xuanming, I have a task for you."

Xuanming stepped forward.

What came into view was an inner core like a pearl, appearing before it.

"Enter this place, use your innate talent to sort out the underworld water inside, and clear a clean space."

Xuanming did not question it. Under Luo Chen's spell, it entered the Mirage Dragon inner core.

The Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven was now incomplete, so opening it was not difficult.

Especially since Luo Chen had mastered the *Heavenly Secrets* alchemy art, which could intercept spiritual energy. Being on Green Parasol Mountain, there was naturally plenty of spiritual energy for him to open the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven.

Then it was Tianxuan's turn.

"Have you brought the Demon-Devil Heart-Linking Tree?"

Tianxuan replied, "Yes, it's planted inside the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion."

"Excellent!"

Luo Chen slowly rose, his eyes filled with confidence.

"Then let's set off. Our destination—the Barren Wasteland!"

Pure Crane and Fairy Huamei bowed respectfully. "Wishing you a smooth journey, Lord/Master!"
