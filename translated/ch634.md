# Chapter 634: Heaven-Earth Grand Formation, Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft

A streak of escape light traveled from west to east, piercing through the air.

Everywhere it passed, wind and clouds surged, leaving traces in the void.

When it passed a tall mountain wreathed in clouds and mist, it suddenly vanished.

Three days later, the escape light appeared again, but it was gray and difficult to detect, flying slowly while hugging the sea surface.

"No one followed."

Luo Chen's divine sense spread in all directions. After confirming that Angler and the others had not complicated matters by tracking him, the great stone in his heart fell.

This task had been single-handedly facilitated by Angler, whose aim was Blood Wanderer's Sage formation insights. The place Luo Chen was about to go to was likewise for Sage formation resources.

There was no difference between the two, and neither wanted changes to arise near the elbow and armpit.

He put away the Broken Moon Wings and formed a spirit seal. The Concealment Array engraved on his body in the past fully activated.

In an instant, Luo Chen's aura was almost hidden to the extreme.

Even though low-level demon beasts passed through the sea region beneath his feet, they did not discover the slightest trace of him.

In this way, his speed toward Purple Spirit Island would perhaps be much slower, but secrecy and safety were undoubtedly raised to the maximum.

Only now did Luo Chen have time to review the battle at Withered Wood Ridge.

In truth, there was not much to review. In that battle, among the five great Golden Core cultivators, apart from Huangfu Song, who had relatively little battle experience, the others had not used any true trump cards, including Luo Chen.

Under such circumstances, no matter how resplendent Star Hook Envoy's star radiance art was, or how domineering Dao Lan's Golden Suanni Saber was, none of it shook Luo Chen's mind.

What he could not let go of was that Sage battle he had observed at close range!

The battle between the Blood God Child and the Vine King had beaten the entire Withered Wood Ridge to pieces, even overturning the earth veins.

Especially the final scene where all vitality in Withered Wood Ridge vanished. That was what Luo Chen remembered most deeply.

Thinking back now, all the Blood God Child's previous methods had clearly been constantly weakening the Vine King's living strength, forcing it to absorb back all the vitality scattered throughout Withered Wood Ridge so it could be taken down in one stroke.

And under such circumstances, the battle aftermath scattering out while the two fought had still made the five great Golden Core cultivators tread as if on thin ice.

From this, one could see Sage power!

Yet this was still not true Sage methods.

One side was a grass-and-wood spirit, the other an external avatar. Neither was the most orthodox Sage Sage.

But precisely because they were imperfect, Luo Chen instead developed even greater awe for the abilities of Sage Sages.

"Even a Sage-stage avatar and spirit are this formidable. Then in the Sunken Sea ancient cultivator ruin I will go to later, if I encounter a Sage running rampant, how should I conduct myself?"

Luo Chen asked his own heart, but he had not the slightest confidence!

Even though Ding Yi had explained before that the entrances were different, and what Sage Sages plotted for was different from what Golden Core cultivators like them plotted for, meaning the two sides would not necessarily directly collide, Luo Chen did not dare fully believe it now.

What if!

What if some big figure wanted to clear the field and casually wipe them out? Could he protect himself?

Could Han Zhan, who had recovered seventy to eighty percent of his strength, truly protect him?

One had to know that even Han Zhan at full strength had actually been only someone newly entered into Sage.

And the Two Modes Five Elements Sword Array Han Zhan had relied on most back then, as well as that Sage-stage puppet, were both already gone or destroyed.

Could one incomplete Nascent Soul truly protect Luo Chen's safety?

Perhaps with full effort, he could preserve Luo Chen's life. But would Han Zhan truly risk everything for him?

Because of the Withered Wood Ridge battle, Luo Chen had gained a clearer and more direct understanding of the strength of Sage Sages. At this moment, the red-faced man on the road had tightly locked brows, and his eyes were filled with worry.

"Previously, perhaps because I refined a supreme treasure and saw many years of effort succeed in one day, my head was muddled from satisfaction. Ding Yi tempted me with the medicinal material I valued and then bewildered me with flowery words."

"Now, thinking carefully, perhaps I should not take the risk, or should not take it so early?"

Luo Chen examined himself. He was now only a little over one hundred years old, still far from the great Golden Core limit. He was nowhere near the point of risking his life in a secret realm.

That Five Elements Lotus Platform, if he obtained it, it was his fortune; if he lost it, it was his fate. Why rush for one moment?

At worst, if he truly could not find it later, then he could go risk danger in the Sunken Sea.

Presumably by that time, Luo Chen's Dao progress would certainly have reached a new realm, and going to the Sunken Sea would be even safer.

Ding Yi had also said that the ancient cultivator ruin in the Sunken Sea did not open only once. Although the time was not fixed, it had opened rather frequently over the past thousand years. He could completely wait until he was fully prepared before going.

"Only, in that case, things will be somewhat hard to explain to Ding Yi."

Luo Chen's thoughts were somewhat chaotic at this moment, but faintly, he seemed to have already made a decision.

In any case, first he should handle the matter in front of him well!

"With Black King leading the way, Lord Lushan and the others should be about to arrive at Purple Spirit Island. I also need to quicken my pace somewhat."

Luo Chen murmured, and his escape speed increased slightly by thirty percent.

...

Two months later.

On the boundless great sea, a sudden rushing wind struck the face, raising clusters of waves.

Suddenly.

The strong wind eased slightly. A human figure flew slowly, holding a brand-new map and identifying the direction.

"There are three places where Black King detoured. One is Withered Wood Ridge, one is a strait with surging hidden currents, and another is a giant island with dense ancient woods."

"According to him, either there are existences on them that make him feel fear from the bottom of his heart, or there are troublesome and formidable Demon Kings."

"I have already gone around all these places. Next, the road ahead should be smooth."

Luo Chen exhaled turbid air, and the tense mood he had carried for many days relaxed a little.

He put away the map and let his gaze fall on the several faintly visible small islands ahead.

There were not many, only seven.

Their area was not large. Each one was roughly the same size as Dahe Market's inner city back then.

They connected to one another like a ladle.

"After crossing Seven Stars Island, it should be the Purple Spirit Sea Region covered in purple mist."

Luo Chen smiled and stepped over the waves.

He did not choose to fly over, but chose to walk.

This was only because Black King had said before that these seven small islands aligned like seven stars formed a natural maze grand formation due to their special terrain.

If one flew high in the air, it was extremely easy to lose direction.

By contrast, if one kept both feet on solid ground and passed through each island one by one, only spending a few days, one could reach the Purple Spirit Island sea region.

It was precisely because of the Seven Stars Island's guard that Purple Spirit Island, covered in countless Purple Monkey Flowers, had not been discovered by high-realm humans and demon beasts over countless years.

Only some low-level demon beasts and humans, ignorant and muddled, who crossed the seven-star blockade by chance, would reach Purple Spirit Island, wreathed in purple qi poison fog.

The reason that wandering cultivator back then could discover it was also because his realm was low, allowing him to luckily pass through.

This principle was actually easy to understand.

High-realm cultivators would fly if they could, run if they could not fly, and walk only if they could not run. Who would be willing to slowly walk for two or three days?

Luo Chen did not mind.

Even if he could not fly high in the air, once Free and Unfettered Roaming unfolded, his speed was not necessarily much slower than Foundation Establishment cultivators, or even some newly entered Golden Core figures.

After entering the first of the Seven Stars islands, Luo Chen's spiritual eyes opened wide.

While flying, he observed this so-called grand formation naturally formed by heaven and earth.

Every formation master, when initially studying formations, would be told by teachers or classics that the essence of so-called formations was the evolution of heaven and earth's principles.

They intercepted the momentum of mountains and rivers, the shapes of wind rolling and clouds moving, and the meaning of the vast galaxy, thereby deducing corresponding formations that humans could arrange.

And this was Luo Chen's first time seeing a purely natural formation.

"A maze formation; is it because the seven islands are extremely similar?"

"But this kind of formation should not be an obstruction for Golden Core cultivators who possess divine sense."

Golden Core cultivators' divine sense spread extremely far.

Using it as a thread, choosing a specific reference point, there should be no issue of direction shifting at all.

The more Luo Chen walked, the more puzzled his heart became.

Suddenly, his steps stopped. He stood motionless in place.

Closing his eyes, only after a long while did he slowly open them.

"I am moving?"

"No! It is the land beneath my feet that is moving!"

With a thought, his vast divine sense spread out completely, directly covering this not-large first island in its entirety.

Under his careful observation, this island was indeed slowly moving at a speed difficult for the naked eye to perceive.

After detecting this point, Luo Chen's heart was slightly startled.

If the island was moving, then the fixed reference point the cultivator chose would actually also move.

Even if the movement speed was slow, the cultivator's own speed was too fast, causing an error of a hair's breadth to become a difference of a thousand li.

This became the so-called maze!

In this process, the longer the time spent, the farther one would deviate from the destination.

What about high in the air?

Luo Chen raised his head and looked. Amid rolling and unfurling clouds, those white clouds seemed also to be shifting stars and turning constellations!

After observing this phenomenon, Luo Chen could not help being amazed inwardly.

"This Seven Stars Island can not only move itself like a living thing, even the surrounding clouds, water flows, and even the wind direction change along with the islands' movement."

"Is this the uncanny workmanship of nature?"

While amazed, Luo Chen shook his hand, and a sheet of white silk paper appeared before him.

As he flew, he used brush and ink to draw strange lines on the silk paper.

This kind of natural maze formation generated by heaven and earth was truly marvelous.

He wanted to record it. After returning, in his spare time, he could deduce it, and perhaps arrange a similar formation.

If he then had corresponding geographical advantage or tools, reproducing Seven Stars Island's illusionary maze power might not be impossible.

At that time, arranging it at a cave residence, traveling palace, or even hidden cultivation place would be a proper good formation.

It had to be said that in the years Luo Chen focused on artifact-forging, his formation attainments had also become increasingly exquisite.

Now, upon finding a quarry worth hunting, he had already developed the thought of imitating ancient formation great powers, deducing formations from heaven and earth principles, and had put it into practice.

Time slowly passed.

By nightfall, Luo Chen had already stepped onto the second island.

On the white silk paper before him, dense lines had already been left behind. Although he had not glimpsed the whole picture, it already possessed a few parts of Seven Stars Island maze grand formation's essence.

However, the gloomy look on Luo Chen's face grew heavier and heavier.

The brushstrokes falling on the silk paper also became fewer and fewer.

"This is not right!"

"According to my understanding, this earth qi circulation and water-vein transformation should accord with the image of the Kun trigram. The virtuous generate earth's Dao, bearing all things with great thickness, moving without rest and advancing without limit. It should circulate smoothly, so why does it crack apart layer by layer halfway through, as if there is an intent of reversal?"

As doubts arose one after another, his swift flight abruptly stopped.

Luo Chen stood in the center of an empty forest. Brilliant light shot from his eyes as he looked toward the dark forest ahead, which was pitch-black like a demon beast's bloody maw.

He grabbed the silk paper and murmured, "This natural formation generated by heaven and earth seems to have been modified by someone."

As he spoke, he withdrew without hesitation.

Wings grew from his back, his speed like thunder and lightning!

At this moment, the lush forest suddenly came alive.

Huge trees blocked his retreat route one after another.

In the empty place, there seemed to be a regretful murmur. "A pity. You stopped at the second island."

Luo Chen's expression turned cold. Without any visible movement, his two wings trembled, and flying blades cut through the air one after another.

Moonbreaking Slash. No matter what thousand-year ancient trees they were, they were immediately cut in half at the waist.

In an instant, moonlight fell and his vision became open.

On a mountain peak in the distance, under the moonlight, a human figure was coldly watching him.

"Devil Lord Azure Yang, you finally came!"

Whoosh!

With a tremble of his wings, Luo Chen slowly flew into the air, level with the green, lean old man on the small mountain.

With only one look, though he had seen him only once, Luo Chen quickly recognized this person's origin.

"Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft!"

"I did not expect you to still remember this old defeated opponent." Liang Baizao laughed bitterly.

"Defeated opponent? Heh."

The corner of Luo Chen's mouth tugged. In the eyes of the world back then, Luo Chen had struck the mountain lord of Hundred Craft Mountain to the horizon in one blow. But only he himself knew that after that strike, his qi was nearly exhausted and his strength almost empty. If not for Han Zhan and Heavenjade taking him away, he would probably have been captured without resistance on Cold Light Island long ago.

Of course, at such a moment before battle, he also could not possibly explain these things and weaken his own momentum for nothing.

He looked around, not discovering any helpers from the other party. While his heart relaxed slightly, he recalled the other party's first words after appearing.

Luo Chen's eyes narrowed slightly.

"The news of Purple Monkey Flower was deliberately released to me by you?"

Liang Baizao admitted frankly, "That is indeed so."

Luo Chen frowned. "But I checked repeatedly before and after. That Foundation Establishment wandering cultivator did not seem to be faking."

"He was naturally real. It was only that I gave him the opportunity."

"That is not right either. I checked that miscellaneous book. Whether the age of the paper or the brush and ink marks, all were over a hundred years old."

"This old man knows you have the reputation of an artifact-forging master in the Myriad Immortals Assembly, but my Liang Baizao's artifact-forging skill is above yours. A mere technique to fake antiquity is nothing to me."

Looking at the old man's confident, fluent speech, Luo Chen fell silent.

On a skill path where he had some small accomplishment, he had been dug a pit. It was laughable that he had previously been somewhat pleased with himself.

Only.

"Why go to such lengths?"

Luo Chen looked at the old man's increasingly flourishing momentum.

"I killed two Golden Core cultivators of your Hundred Craft Mountain, but that was after you surrounded and killed me first. You and I originally had no grudges or enmity. Why painstakingly scheme for years to seek revenge against me?"

At this point, he sighed.

"It has been fully twenty-odd years, has it not? Your Dao progress has not increased, and has even slightly regressed. Tell me, old man, what are you pursuing?"

"Do you think this old man wanted to?"

A low roar came from the green, lean old man's mouth, his white teeth bared and sinister, his expression rather hideous.

"If not for Blood Nightmare Moluo forcibly ordering me, do you think this old man would willingly waste twenty years, set down my ruined sect, halt my already difficult cultivation, and bitterly wait for you in the Myriad Immortals Assembly for so many years?"

"Especially when you also hid like a turtle inside the Fulong Mountain Range and almost never took a step out, leaving me no way to act. I could only use news of Purple Monkey Flower to slowly fish you, this little turtle, out."

"A Sage's order is as heavy as mountains. I could not defy it in the slightest!"

Luo Chen frowned. It was Blood Nightmare Moluo's order?

He thought of the final battle before leaving Cold Light Island.

Under He Yuan, who had been possessed by Blood Nightmare Moluo, he and Han Zhan had joined forces and struck the other party's divine soul.

He had not expected that even someone as honored as a Sage Sage would hold such a grudge against a junior like him!

The hideousness on Liang Baizao's face gradually faded. "Fortunately, after tonight, I can end these drifting twenty years. Devil Lord Azure Yang, I give you two roads. First, let me lay restrictions on you, obediently follow me back, and bear thorns to plead guilty before Blood Nightmare Moluo. This way, you can avoid the result of death and Dao extinction. Second, die on Seven Stars Island, and I will bring your corpse back."

Luo Chen stared at him, and mana fluctuations on his body also rippled out like the tide.

"You truly think you have me in your grasp?"

"Looks like you choose the second road." The old man raised both hands, and behind him appeared streaks of treasure light. Looking carefully, they were all swords!

Luo Chen's expression tightened, and his figure shot straight into the sky.

"If I want to leave, everywhere is a road!"

His speed was extremely fast, running like startling thunder.

With such speed, crossing a thousand li in the blink of an eye seemed not out of the question.

Yet Liang Baizao had no intention of stopping him at all.

He merely fiddled with the seven treasure swords behind him, sword light swimming beside him like fish.

"After this battle, I wonder how many can be preserved."

He murmured once, then raised his head toward the sky again.

Just as he expected, the red-robed Daoist's speed became slower and slower, until finally he seemed to crawl like a turtle.

"Do not waste effort. Back then, you displayed all your brilliance in the great battle on Cold Light Island. Do you think this old man made no preparations in advance? This battle is not something you can avoid."

At the extreme height of the sky, Luo Chen's expression was gloomy.

He stopped his meaningless struggle and began slowly descending.

As he descended, that terrifying suppressive power also slowly weakened.

He raised his head and looked.

Beneath the deep night, a towering mountain faintly appeared and vanished at the horizon. Streaks of flowing light scattered, like chains, connecting with the seven islands beneath his feet.

*(End of Chapter)*
