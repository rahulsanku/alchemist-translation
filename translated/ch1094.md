# Chapter 1094: The Arrogant Wanderer, the Heavenly Wolf Alliance

Eastern Wilderness, the Million Mountains!

Countless demon beasts bred and thrived within, and over thousands of years, a tripartite balance had gradually formed.

The Tiger, Wolf, and Feather clans dominated the mountain ranges.

As Di Tian advanced to the Ancient Demon realm and Rosycloud displayed her unparalleled divine abilities, the once-peerless Wolf clan gradually declined, even once submitting to the command of the Ancient Demon Di Tian.

But there is no eternal kingship in this world.

Di Tian fell, Rosycloud ascended, and the once-powerful Tiger Demon clan and Feather clan instead declined.

Even though Green Parasol Mountain still had a Truth Crossing Hall Master who was hailed as the number one under the Ascendants, that person was a great human cultivator, naturally opposed to the demon race.

Thus, in a situation without a leader, the various great demon clan powerhouses of the Eastern Wilderness were all gravitating towards the Wolf clan.

On this day.

Atop Heavenly Wolf Mountain!

Rolling demonic aura, howling wolves.

Streams of fleeing light flew in from all directions, landing within the Heavenly Wolf Altar.

Han Shan Que arrived a little late. He himself didn't get along well with the Wolf clan, but this situation was truly somewhat special.

Before he even entered the Heavenly Wolf Altar, he heard fierce arguments coming from inside.

"That's the Truth Crossing Hall Master, an existence as renowned as the Daytime Void Hall Master, and he even received the recognition of the Primordial Sovereign!"

"So what? Luo Chen is ultimately human. Do you want him to be forever above us?"

"Yes, he is human, but he is also a peak human existence who personally slew a great power of the Dao Sect!"

"Heh, you should all have been on the East Yuan Continent for that battle. Did any of you personally witness him slaying a god?"

"This..."

"Don't forget, Daytime Void Hall Master Azure Frost also made a move in that battle. No matter how strong Luo Chen is, he is still just a Sage cultivator. How could he possibly slay an Ascendant great power? I admit, it's possible he contributed, but the greater likelihood is that he acted as support. The true god-slayer was Azure Frost. Don't rush to refute me, Old White Brow. Calm down and think about it. Even Sage-level demon cultivators like us are extremely hard to kill, let alone the life-preserving methods of an Ascendant great power. Only Hall Master Azure Frost, who controls the true essence of spatial laws, could completely eliminate an existence of the same rank. Luo Chen... he cannot do it!"

Han Shan Que stepped into the hall, just in time to see the White Brow Immortal Ape arguing fiercely with a middle-aged man with a fierce expression.

The other Sage-level demon cultivators remained silent, not participating, but each had a contemplative look on their faces.

Han Shan Que recognized that middle-aged man.

Ao Jun!

The second strongest of the Wolf clan, second only to Heavenly Wolf Ao Kuang. In recent years, he had advanced to the late Sage stage, a rare Great Demon Emperor.

Han Shan Que said gruffly, "We are all fellow cultivators. Why argue so fiercely?"

Ao Jun snorted coldly, "I cannot stand to see others boost another's prestige and destroy our own morale."

White Brow Immortal Ape sighed deeply, "It's not that I am timid, old man, but the Truth Crossing Hall Master truly possesses unparalleled abilities. Regardless of whether he personally slew an Ascendant great power, his past achievements cannot be faked. The title of Number One Under the Ascendants is no empty boast."

Ao Jun disagreed, "That title of his was only gained by fighting Azure Frost and then bullying the Western Desert bald donkeys. If we calculate it that way, our ancestor could also be called the Number One. It's just that our ancestor has never cared about such empty fame."

Everyone exchanged glances but couldn't refute him.

Because Heavenly Wolf Ao Kuang had indeed fought Azure Frost back in the day, and even though Azure Frost had comprehended the true essence of spatial laws, he was at a disadvantage in that battle.

Some even speculated that if it weren't for the concerns of Rosycloud Primordial Sovereign, Ao Kuang might have killed him back then.

White Brow Immortal Ape pursed his lips and looked at Han Shan Que.

"Daoist friend, we were both in the Truth Crossing Hall back then. Tell us, what is Luo Chen's ability?"

Han Shan Que instinctively wanted to deny Luo Chen's strength, but when the words reached his lips, that scene from back then involuntarily surfaced in his mind.

Suppressing a horde of demons with the power of one man!

Such ability...

"Enough!" Ao Jun shouted, glaring at White Brow Immortal Ape. "Old White Brow, if you don't want to participate in this great matter, you can leave. Why are you here sowing discord among us?"

Being shouted at like this, White Brow Immortal Ape was also displeased.

He had been invited to discuss a great matter, yet he was being refuted at every turn. Even a clay figurine has a temper.

But in the end, he didn't retort angrily.

Because three figures stepped into the Heavenly Wolf Altar from the rear hall.

One was shrouded in a black robe, a graceful figure faintly visible.

One had a ferocious face, as if about to devour someone, the lone Great Demon Emperor Bi Zheng.

And in front of the two was the master of this place.

The Wolf clan's common lord—Ao Kuang!

"Ancestor!"

"Lord Ao Kuang!"

"Daoist friend Ao, you've arrived."

Greetings rang out one after another, each face bearing a hint of respect, showing Ao Kuang's esteemed position among the demon race.

A kind smile appeared on the old man's face, which naturally commanded respect without anger.

He waved his hand, "No need for such formalities."

Then, Ao Kuang looked at Ao Jun.

"Daoist friend White Brow is merely worried that we are acting rashly, like throwing an egg against a rock. Why be so angry?"

Ao Jun frowned, "But..."

Ao Kuang smiled and sat down at the head.

The black-robed figure and Bi Zheng stood on his left and right.

"In fact, Daoist friend White Brow is not wrong. Luo Chen's abilities have long been renowned across the Mountains and Seas Realm. Moreover, his reputation is not an empty name blown up among human cultivators, but earned through battle after battle. Setting aside everything else, just the title of Demon-Slaying Sage, I'm sure you've all heard of it!"

The demons' expressions changed slightly.

They were demon cultivators. The name 'Demon-Slaying' was the most ironic title for them.

Ao Kuang was not embarrassed, and continued, "As for the title of Number One Under the Ascendants, I have no intention of contending with him for it. Even if we were to face each other, the odds of victory or defeat would probably be three to seven."

"I am three, he is seven!"

The demons were stunned, seemingly not expecting Ancestor Ao Kuang to so frankly admit his disadvantage.

Han Shan Que said gruffly, "If that's the case, Daoist friend, how dare you actively provoke the Truth Crossing Hall Master?"

Ao Kuang glanced at him, his expression calm as he pointed to the black-robed figure beside him.

"Daoist friend, you speak!"

The figure stepped forward, pushed back the hood, revealing a face of pure beauty, like a cold moon fairy. A single glance inspired thoughts of not daring to profane.

The woman bowed gracefully, and a seductive voice came from her mouth.

"I am Moon Wanderer. I was once a close attendant of Rosycloud Primordial Sovereign, and now I temporarily oversee the Profound Xuan Hall."

Faced with this seductive voice, the demon powerhouses actually felt a ripple of infatuation in their hearts.

Some even eagerly wanted to tear open that black robe and get a look at the fiery figure beneath.

Ao Kuang frowned and snorted coldly.

A cold snort, like thunder in the ears.

The demon cultivators suddenly woke up, shocked by the woman's seductive voice, and then reacted.

"From Green Parasol Mountain?"

"Also a human cultivator!"

"Why is she here, could it be..."

"Everyone!" Moon Wanderer shouted, interrupting their train of thought towards bad places, and then said seriously, "The past is the past, the present is the present. Luo Chen is currently in seclusion, charging towards the Ascendant realm. This is his weakest moment, and also our best opportunity."

Ao Kuang took over, saying solemnly, "That's right. According to my clan's scouts, Green Parasol Mountain has activated its mountain-protecting grand formation, strictly forbidding entry and exit. Everything is a sign of Luo Chen preparing for his Ascension. In this state, I am confident I can fight him. At that time, you only need to act to restrain one or two of those loyal to Luo Chen."

Everyone looked at each other, both shocked at the speed of Luo Chen's breakthrough to Ascendant, and feeling that this was indeed an unprecedented good opportunity.

When a cultivator breaks through a realm, it is often their weakest moment.

This is true for any realm, and they are most vulnerable to outside disturbance.

Once interrupted, at best they suffer severe injuries and a regression in cultivation; at worst, they die on the spot, falling into eternal damnation!

Someone questioned: "He is the strongest human. Why didn't he charge for Ascension in the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world, but chose Green Parasol Mountain instead?"

Ao Kuang answered calmly: "Two hundred years ago, Rosycloud Primordial Sovereign killed her way into the Nether Abyss and destroyed the Nether Origin Sect. Yama Floating Mountain fell and has not reappeared since. That was the only fifth-order spirit vein in the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world. And for a Sage cultivator to break through to Ascendant, they absolutely cannot lack a fifth-order spirit vein. Therefore, Luo Chen had no choice but to rely on the geographical advantage of Green Parasol Mountain to charge towards a higher realm. And this Green Parasol Mountain was originally our territory!"

Word by word, sentence by sentence, it was both an explanation and an incitement.

"Luo Chen is now far from his sect, without any powerful protectors, and in the midst of breaking through a realm. This is his weakest moment."

"I know that many of you here are on the Truth Crossing Hall's demon cultivator roster, and your concerns are understandable. So, at that time, you won't have to face Luo Chen. I will personally make a move."

With such a guarantee, the people in the hall were clearly already somewhat tempted.

But it wasn't enough!

Ao Kuang took a deep breath. "This time, I am acting against Luo Chen. For the public good, I do not wish to see a human powerhouse suppress us demon race, especially one who once made a name for himself by slaying demons. For personal reasons, he and I have some minor grudges, and I have been stuck at the peak of the Sage stage for a long time, urgently needing the Green Parasol Mountain spirit vein. Therefore, regardless of whether you are willing to help, I will inevitably march on Green Parasol Mountain!"

These words were spoken with such sincerity.

Given Ao Kuang's reputation in the Million Mountains, the demons were willing to believe he was speaking from the heart.

How could a demon race spirit vein be usurped by a mere human cultivator?

And if he truly succeeded, would the Truth Crossing Hall Master once again oppress the Eastern Wilderness demon race for centuries?

"Whether you are willing to help or not, I hope you will give me an answer today. If anyone is unwilling, after swearing a Great Dao oath not to reveal today's secrets, you may leave on your own. I will never pursue the matter in the future."

Ao Kuang spoke his final words, then closed his eyes and began to rest.

Within the Heavenly Wolf Altar, chaos erupted for a time.

There were those who agreed with Ao Kuang's words, not wanting Luo Chen to sit high above the demon race, and those who feared that if the great matter failed, they would face Luo Chen's reckoning, the demon cultivators of the Truth Crossing Hall.

Amidst the chaos, the black-robed female cultivator, Moon Wanderer, suddenly spoke.

"In the battle on the East Yuan Continent, you all shed blood and sweat for Luo Chen. I wonder if you received any rewards afterwards?"

The demons were silent.

In that battle, they were merely following orders.

They were both subdued by Luo Chen and under the terrifying pressure of the then Green Parasol Mountain master, Rosycloud Primordial Sovereign, so they had no choice but to participate.

As for rewards afterwards, naturally, there were none.

Moon Wanderer smiled. "Perhaps you don't know, but the Eastern Wilderness human powerhouses who participated in that battle all received a Tí Hú Pill afterwards!"

The demons were bewildered.

Tí Hú Pill, what was that?

Moon Wanderer didn't see the desired effect, felt a bit stifled, and silently cursed them as a bunch of country bumpkins.

Then, she carefully explained the effects of the so-called Tí Hú Pill.

When they learned of its heaven-defying effects, their eyes all lit up.

"Such a divine pill, can he really refine it?"

"He is an Alchemy Saint!"

"Why give it only to humans, and not to us? Such favoritism!"

"He is ultimately a human cultivator. How could he treat everyone equally? I can't understand why Rosycloud Primordial Sovereign insisted on giving him the position of Truth Crossing Hall Master."

"Kill him, and seize the Tí Hú Pill!"

"It's not that easy. Even if Lord Ao Kuang kills Luo Chen, a powerhouse of his level only needs a single thought before dying to destroy his storage magic treasure, leaving us empty-handed."

"No, no, no, don't forget, he founded a large sect. Such a pill, and even the pill formula, how could he not leave them within his sect?"

"Then kill him, and then march on the Eastern Wilderness, seize the Tí Hú, and prove our path to the Ancient Demon realm!"

...

Amidst the surging emotions, Ao Kuang and Moon Wanderer exchanged a knowing smile.

Hearts were gathered, the great matter could be accomplished.

After the meeting.

At the peak of Heavenly Wolf Mountain, Ao Kuang personally saw off Moon Wanderer.

No, to be precise, it was a clone of Moon Wanderer.

Her true body remained in the Profound Xuan Hall of Green Parasol Mountain, not stepping out the door.

At their parting, the two were still plotting the great deed to come.

"It cannot be too early, nor too late! We must strike fiercely at the moment he is on the verge of success. Otherwise, Luo Chen at his peak is not an existence you or I can match!"

"Are you praising him too highly?"

"This is not praise, but fear. If you knew that over two hundred years ago, he was just an Adept cultivator, an ant who could only flee for his life under my hands, you would not underestimate him like this."

"I naturally will not underestimate him. After all, he is an existence once as renowned as Azure Frost. I am just wondering, you are so meticulously plotting against him, even roping in us demon powerhouses, is it only because you had a grudge with him in the past?"

"Heh, that's not convenient to elaborate on. Anyway, afterwards, Green Parasol Mountain is yours, I return to the Northern Sea. The Tí Hú Pills are also yours, I don't need that thing. But his life-bound magic treasure, whether damaged or not, must be given to me!"

"A life-bound magic treasure?"

"Farewell. When the time comes, I will notify you."

Ao Kuang watched Moon Wanderer leave, his eyes narrowing slightly as he gazed at her graceful figure.

...

A day in the mountains, a thousand years in the world.

When a cultivator goes into seclusion for bitter cultivation, the passage of time always exceeds imagination.

Especially for high-level cultivators.

Often, a single deep-level seclusion could be the entire life of a mortal.

Luo Chen was not quite like that, after all, his accumulation during the Sage stage was already sufficiently abundant.

Not to mention the day-and-night polishing of his mana to consolidate his realm, just the comprehension of three types of true essence of laws had made his foundation reach a level of robustness unprecedented in millennia.

Fifty years!

Since Luo Chen came to Green Parasol Mountain for seclusion, fifty years had quietly passed.

For fifty years, the grand formation enveloping Green Parasol Mountain had never been removed, isolating the inside from the outside, blocking all prying gazes.

Until...

Boom!

With a heaven-shaking roar, vast red spiritual energy violently erupted, sweeping across the ten-thousand-li range around Green Parasol Mountain like a tidal wave.

The Sage-level demon cultivators guarding the mountain all opened their eyes, looking at the sky with joy.

This day had finally come!
