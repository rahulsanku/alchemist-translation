# Chapter 1151: Kneeling in Submission, Power Bestowed

Of the five great experts, one had fallen.

He died so silently, like an ant crushed beneath someone's foot.

But Xiao Zirui was no mere ant. He was a genius among the younger generation of the Xiao clan, second only to the brothers Xiao Feng and Xiao Yun.

Otherwise, Xiao Feng would never have taken him back after his betrayal, setting aside past grievances.

Yet such a genius—Luo Chen had killed him with a flick of his finger, without even blinking.

If that was the case, what weight did the others hold in Luo Chen's heart?

They were merely at the Furnace Realm.

They didn't even share the blood relation that Xiao Zirui and Xiao Feng had.

If Xiao Feng still refused to bow his head in submission, who would be next for Luo Chen to kill and establish his authority?

For a moment, the few kneeling on the ground were filled with terror.

Luo Chen seemed oblivious, the astral threads at his fingertips still locked onto the heart meridians of the four great experts, while his qi sense was firmly fixed on the distant ruins—Tie Xiong was still alive.

As for Xiao Feng, he merely watched him with a calm gaze.

Yet to Xiao Feng, that calm gaze was something he dared not meet.

As if one glance would reveal mountains of corpses and seas of blood.

He had understood before that Luo Chen was no easy person to deal with, but he had never imagined the man would turn hostile so decisively.

If he continued to prevaricate, to hesitate, to delay...

*Thump!*

Xiao Feng bent his knee and knelt on the ground.

Xiao Sanniang, standing nearby, was stunned, unable to believe it, but she did not speak. The situation forced them to yield; bowing their heads was only natural.

Xiao Feng knelt, head bowed.

"Xiao Feng is willing to serve you, my lord!"

"Heh."

Luo Chen let out a soft laugh. The astral threads that had been gripping the heart meridians of the others slowly dissipated, and his laughter grew louder.

The sound spread outward, stirring the lake waters into churning waves, echoing again and again in everyone's hearts.

Even without the life-threatening astral threads restraining them, under that laughter, none of the experts could muster any thought of resistance.

Since Luo Chen had arrived at Relic Immortal City, his only two displays of power had left an indelible impression of overwhelming strength on their minds.

Who would dare to shake a mighty tree?

The laughter gradually subsided. Luo Chen clasped his hands behind his back and paced over to Xiao Feng's side.

"This Relic Immortal City will still be under your command. Don't mismanage it."

Xiao Feng, who had been anxious, was taken aback by these words.

Luo Chen had no intention of taking his position as city lord?

"But from now on, if I need anything, you will spare no effort to obtain it for me. There must be no negligence."

Having kept his position as city lord, Xiao Feng was inexplicably overjoyed. He hurriedly replied, "Whatever you command, my lord!"

"If you had been this sensible earlier, today's events wouldn't have been necessary." Luo Chen snorted, then continued, "Alright, I have a list here. Use the power of Relic Immortal City to find the items on it for me. Also, the top-grade goods from the Mi Immortal Smoke—stop trading them externally and keep them all for me. Finally, I didn't find Tie Xiong's secret technique for breaking through to the Leakless Realm in the library, nor did I find your father's cultivation insights on breaking through to that realm. I assume they're with you. Hand them all over!"

One condition after another—no, they were commands!

These commands, which would have sounded excessive in the past, no longer seemed so hard to accept when spoken by Luo Chen. Xiao Feng agreed to them all.

Luo Chen shook his head with a smile, looking down at Xiao Feng from above.

"I hope you can continue to be this obedient from now on."

With that, he walked away calmly.

After he left, the previously noisy Wave-Watching Pavilion fell into utter silence, almost eerie.

Xiao Feng stood up, facing the gazes of Tie Futu, Chu Zhaonan, and the others, watching Xiao Sanniang support the severely injured Tie Xiong out of the ruins. His expression turned awkward.

But in the end, his gaze fell on the corpse on the ground.

Only helplessness remained on his face.

The others tacitly lowered their heads and said nothing.

The enemy was too strong. What could they do?

...

Under the night sky.

Luo Chen walked alone along the long street, a smile never leaving his lips.

He was in a very good mood, not just because he had suppressed Xiao Feng and the others, gaining a group of tools to serve him.

In truth, ever since arriving at Relic Immortal City, Luo Chen had already harbored similar thoughts.

To cultivate quickly—whether in qi cultivation or body refinement—relying solely on individual effort was absolutely impossible.

Especially at the lower stages, which only valued the accumulation of resources!

Back in the Mountains and Seas Realm, whether during the Po Shan Gang period, the Luo Tian Sect period, or the Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake period, Luo Chen had never lacked people to gather resources for him.

A proven path to success, he would certainly replicate it.

But this time, he didn't want to establish his own faction.

That was too troublesome, and Luo Chen considered himself a man with principles and limits. If he truly built such an organization, he would have to take responsibility for it to some extent.

Therefore, this time, his approach was more decisive. He simply used force to suppress them, not caring whether they were sincerely convinced.

The reason he hadn't acted earlier, instead lying low for a short time, was that he didn't know enough about this world.

But after gaining some understanding of the high-end combat power of this world, as well as the Great Destruction Realm and the Seventy-Two Cities, he no longer had any concerns.

Hence, today's sudden strike.

What truly put Luo Chen in a good mood was his performance in that battle.

Defeating Tie Xiong was nothing special—just like when he fought the martial champion, it relied purely on physical strength.

But the move that suppressed Chu Zhaonan and the other five demonstrated exquisite technique.

The perfected Immortal Returns to Penglai, multiple finger forces bursting forth, with spiral techniques added inside, could forcibly break through astral qi shields.

Not only that, but from his cursory reading of *Thousand Astral Hundred Turns*, Luo Chen had instantly comprehended a method similar to Granny Yu's astral qi turning into threads, and put it into practice.

All of this was a testament to Luo Chen's superb comprehension and his battle-hardened combat skills.

At the same time, from these exquisite martial techniques, he glimpsed the profound depth of the path of martial body refinement!

If he continued down this road, his future achievements might not be inferior to those of qi cultivation!

When that time came, leaving this place would no longer be a fantasy.

"Next, I just wait for Xiao Feng to deliver the resources I need, one by one."

Thinking this, Luo Chen had already arrived at the Luo residence.

Jing Lan stood at the door, smiling warmly.

"Brother Luo, you're back!"

"Mm. Why are you so early today? Isn't the Benevolence Hall busy?"

"The Benevolence Hall has always been an established name. Once I got familiar with the operations, it naturally wasn't as busy as when it first opened. Brother Luo, are you hungry? I learned some new dishes and have already prepared them."

"Alright, let's give them a try!"

...

The next day, Xiao Feng sent over a portion of the items Luo Chen had demanded.

A secret technique for breaking through realms called *Hundred Flaw Art*, and a cultivation manual personally written by Xiao Feng's father.

Luo Chen first read the cultivation manual.

As a famous martial fanatic of the Seventy-Two Cities, Xiao Feng's father had deep research into the martial path.

This manual not only recorded knowledge related to breaking through realms but also contained complete cultivation insights for the Furnace Realm.

Luo Chen spent a day carefully studying it, while also cross-referencing and comparing it with Wang Yuan's records in *Divine Astral Fighting Qi*.

Gradually, he formed a complete impression of Furnace Realm cultivation.

The Furnace Realm, the third realm of body refinement, seemed to correspond to the Adept stage of qi cultivation. However, aside from the accumulation of energy, the two were fundamentally different.

Adept stage cultivation focused on the external!

This sounded counterintuitive—the most important aspect of the Adept stage was clearly the fusion of essence, qi, and spirit into one, which was internal cultivation!

But Luo Chen, having experienced the complete qi cultivation system, would not view it so one-sidedly.

Qi cultivation and spirit refinement emphasized the path of the soul.

At the Initiate stage, the soul was released externally, forming spiritual sense. At the Adept stage, divine sense was formed, searching heaven and earth, revealing everything—these were all changes brought about by a powerful soul.

And in Adept stage cultivation, there was a very important step: refining one's lifebound artifact.

In that process, if conditions allowed, any Adept would try to incorporate some thunder-attribute materials into their lifebound artifact.

For example, the Thousand-Year Thunder Essence that Luo Chen had once valued most!

This kind of thing not only improved the quality of the lifebound artifact but also, through daily nurturing, allowed the originally yin-attributed divine sense to be gradually tainted by the supremely yang thunder, becoming more yang-attributed.

Hence, there was the concept of the yin soul transforming into the yang soul at the Ascendant stage.

In comparison, Furnace Realm cultivation was rather extremely internal!

Using the astral qi produced by the qualitative change of blood qi, one continuously tempered the acupoints opened during the Blood River Realm, allowing them to open and close freely.

Once all the acupoints in the body were fully tempered, one could completely seal their own essence qi, preventing any leakage to the outside.

Therefore, after the Furnace Realm was perfected, the next realm was aptly named the Leakless Realm.

After understanding the difference between these two paths, Luo Chen's thoughts began to diverge.

Qi cultivation... body refinement...

The path of qi cultivation, starting from refining spiritual qi into the body and then cultivating step by step, was essentially the process of cultivators exploring heaven and earth.

In this process, the higher the realm, the less important the physical body became.

As long as the soul remained, one could coexist with heaven and earth.

Using dharma power to drive the origin qi of heaven and earth, comprehending the true meaning of laws, and acting on behalf of heaven to exercise the authority of the Dao.

But the path of body refinement, starting from the Furnace Realm, took a completely opposite direction.

Turning the body into a furnace, devouring everything from heaven and earth, and attributing all power to oneself.

In this process, whatever was absorbed, not even a fraction was willing to be returned to heaven and earth.

When reaching the extreme, the martial artist's physical body itself would become a heaven and earth, and the martial artist's will would be the so-called Heavenly Dao!

"Comparing the two, qi cultivation and immortal cultivation follow heaven, wielding the creation of the Dao. Martial body refinement, however, goes against heaven, attributing all power to oneself."

"For heaven and earth, the latter is extremely harmful. No wonder the orthodox path of body refinement gradually declined."

"But why, in this Great Destruction Realm with its scarce resources, has martial body refinement become the mainstream?"

"Is it because of the lack of spiritual qi, or is this heaven and earth itself special?"

Luo Chen had already put down the books.

But the confusion on his face grew deeper.

The more principles he understood, the more he couldn't see the essence of the world and the universe.

Luo Chen didn't know if this situation would occur in others, but he knew that this kind of "confusion" could exist, but it must not persist forever, or it would eventually affect his Dao heart.

The answer to everything would ultimately fall on practice!

Luo Chen adjusted his mood and then studied the *Hundred Flaw Art*.

This was the secret technique Tie Xiong had relied on to break through to the Leakless Realm.

But after a cursory reading, he lost interest.

To break through from the Furnace Realm to the Leakless Realm required two conditions.

One was to accumulate enough energy, i.e., astral qi.

The other was to temper all the major acupoints in the body, achieving a state where they could open and close freely.

But while it sounded simple, astral qi was violent and fierce, and acupoints were much more fragile than the thick meridians, especially some key acupoints involving the head, heart, and even the lower body.

A single mistake could lead to the loss of all previous progress, or even death.

The function of the *Hundred Flaw Art* was to forcibly seal some acupoints that were difficult to temper, thereby achieving a different kind of "leaklessness."

However, in this way, those forcibly sealed acupoints could only be closed, not opened.

When astral qi flowed, it would be blocked and difficult to advance. In the eyes of experts of the same level, this so-called leaklessness would have flaws.

Luo Chen's realm hadn't reached the Leakless Realm, but his insight, even his qi sense detection method, could see through those flaws.

In the battle at Wave-Watching Pavilion, Tie Xiong couldn't withstand his full-force strike, partly because his Leakless Golden Body had flaws.

After understanding all this, Luo Chen naturally looked down on this secret technique.

He would rather focus his energy on high-level martial techniques like *Flower-Folding Finger* and *Heaven-Earth Inverse Wheel*.

Moreover, that book *Thousand Astral Hundred Turns* recorded various subtle changes and uses of astral qi, which was also very helpful for the subsequent tempering of acupoints. Even after reading it once, Luo Chen felt it necessary to study it again.

Apart from these texts.

What Xiao Feng sent also included a large amount of resources Luo Chen needed for martial training.

Food!

Yes, a massive amount of food resources.

Various meats rich in surging blood qi, as well as Dragon Tooth Rice that only Furnace Realm experts could enjoy, and so on.

These things were the best backup resources for martial artists who couldn't refine the spiritual qi of heaven and earth.

Luo Chen wasn't worried about not being able to digest them.

Not to mention his strong physical body, the blood qi furnace he had just condensed was enough to refine all of this.

Xiao Feng wasn't stingy. The first batch of resources he sent was of great value, far exceeding the hundredfold or tenfold of Luo Chen's previous guest elder offerings!

Presumably, for him, it was also a huge loss.

Luo Chen didn't care what Xiao Feng thought. He simply enjoyed it freely, refining more blood qi and converting it into more astral qi.

Half a month later.

Xiao Feng sent the second batch of resources Luo Chen had demanded.

They were large jars!

Sealed tightly, as if they contained something dangerous.

In fact, to mortals, even to low-level martial artists, these things were indeed very dangerous.

In front of Chu Zhaonan, Luo Chen opened a large jar.

Instantly, a fierce, murderous aura rushed toward him.

"Careful!"

Chu Zhaonan warned.

But Luo Chen paid it no mind. With a flick of his finger, he traced a circle, and that fierce aura obediently coiled around his finger.

This scene stunned Chu Zhaonan.

Luo Chen smiled faintly, his gaze falling on the familiar black aura at his fingertip.

He was no stranger to this stuff.

"Balefulness!"
