# Chapter 1020: Western Desert Old Tales, Great House Sage

"Regarding that one from Dongyang Sect, Brother Deng, how much do you know about him?"

"Not much, but in my view, that one is far inferior to you, Brother Luo, in every way. There's no need to worry too much."

"Oh?" Luo Chen blinked and smiled slightly. "Brother Deng has such confidence in me?"

Deng Taiyue sighed with emotion. "Not to mention the time you suppressed that Netherworld ghost cultivator right before my eyes, just the widely circulated battle at the Western Desert Pure Land in recent years is enough to fill me with confidence in you."

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow. He hadn't expected news of his deeds in the Western Desert to have spread all the way to the Eastern Wilderness.

But thinking about it, the timing was about right.

Especially that battle—his achievements were quite remarkable, worthy of spreading to other continents.

Seeing that Luo Chen neither refuted nor defended himself, Deng Taiyue understood that the rumors were not false, and his admiration only grew.

"Fighting alone against thirty-six Hanging Temple monks without defeat, then joining forces with that demoness Azure Frost to instantly break the Arhat Formation—Brother Luo, your abilities, no matter how highly I've estimated them, still exceed my imagination!"

Luo Chen laughed heartily. "The world tends to exaggerate. How would they know that under the siege of those thirty-six eminent monks, I was barely holding on?"

Deng Taiyue knew he was being modest and didn't expose him. Instead, he asked curiously, "According to what Heavenly Origin Dao Sect put out, you and that demoness Azure Frost were once locked in a life-and-death struggle. Why did you join hands to face the world together in the Western Desert?"

"Gratitude and enmity, love and hate—they shift unpredictably. In just a few words, I can hardly explain it all!" Luo Chen laughed it off, glossing over the matter, and changed the subject to something he cared more about. "From the Western Desert, have only these messages come through?"

Deng Taiyue's expression turned serious. "There is indeed another major matter!"

Luo Chen grew interested, adopting an attentive posture.

And what Deng Taiyue said next was exactly what Luo Chen wanted to know.

The Hanging Temple had once again closed its gates!

But this time, it wasn't voluntary—it was forced, sealed up by someone else!

According to reports, after that demoness Moluo defeated the Hanging Temple's most elite thirty-six eminent monks in the Pure Land, Great Dharma Master Xuan Du descended upon the temple beyond the Green Mist Screen.

At the cost of his own Ascendant-level cultivation, he activated the Glazed Pure Land, manifesting boundless Buddha realms, attempting to merge it with the Green Mist Screen.

During this process, Hanging Temple's Arhat Mahakala struck out several times, directly beating Great Dharma Master Xuan Du to death. Yet even that couldn't stop the fusion of the Glazed Pure Land and the Green Mist Screen.

A spatial spirit treasure merging with a natural defensive barrier that was already unparalleled—its power was simply beyond imagination.

Amid Mahakala's unwilling roar, the Hanging Temple was completely sealed.

This news sounded simple, but if one had actually been there, they could imagine how brutal that battle must have been.

After hearing this, Luo Chen couldn't help but sigh deeply.

"To think that Fellow Daoist Xuan Du truly sacrificed himself for the sake of all living beings."

If it were him, having barely achieved the Ascendant realm, where would he find the resolve to die so calmly?

Recalling his conversation with Xuan Du before leaving, Luo Chen thought that if he had the time, he really ought to burn a stick of incense for him.

Deng Taiyue continued, "Mahakala was sealed, but the Hanging Temple's lineage hasn't been severed. Before his death, Great Dharma Master Xuan Du left behind sixteen eminent monks, each with the strength of a Sage. They still carry the name of the Hanging Temple, spreading the Buddhist path in the Western Desert."

At this point, Deng Taiyue looked at Luo Chen.

"I hear those sixteen are part of the thirty-six eminent monks who were fortunate enough to survive under your hand."

Luo Chen smiled and shook his head, steering the conversation back.

"The Western Desert is too far from us. Let's talk about that one from Dongyang Sect for now."

Deng Taiyue grunted in acknowledgment and began to speak based on his understanding of the Dongyang Sect Grand Elder: "This man is a very traditional Dongyang Sect cultivator. He showed talent from a young age, achieved mastery in his studies, and after advancing to the Sage realm, he followed his predecessors to study at the Nether Origin Sect. What sets him apart from other Dongyang Sect cultivators is that back then, he truly submitted to the Nether Origin Sect—compliant in every way."

"During his time in power at Dongyang Sect, not only did the sect guard the Eastern Wilderness gateway for the Nether Origin Sect, but even Dongyang Sect disciples could be commanded and deployed at will by Nether Origin Sect cultivators."

"To put it nicely, they were like one family. To put it bluntly, he was a dog."

Luo Chen recalled some of his own firsthand experiences from back then.

He had indeed seen Nether Origin Sect disciples commanding Dongyang Sect disciples—a situation rarely seen even between friendly sects.

Let alone between sects; even within a single sect, there were different factions.

Such "harmony" showed just how low Dongyang Sect had bowed.

So what had the Dongyang Sect Grand Elder gained in exchange for selling out to the Nether Origin Sect like this?

"He is the youngest Grand Cultivator in Dongyang Sect's history. When he stepped into the late Sage realm, he was barely five hundred years old! At that age, most people might still be seeking various opportunities to break through to the Sage realm." Deng Taiyue sighed, then instinctively glanced at Luo Chen. A wild guess surged into his mind. "Brother Luo, you should already be at late Sage, right?"

Luo Chen smiled and said frankly, "Naturally, I am."

Deng Taiyue breathed a sigh of relief. If Luo Chen weren't at late Sage, he would have doubted life itself. After all, Luo Chen's illustrious achievements in the Western Desert would be far too terrifying if he were only at mid-Sage.

Of course, he didn't know that Luo Chen had indeed only been at Sage sixth layer back then.

But even so, as he relaxed, he couldn't help but sigh repeatedly.

"I speak of how the Dongyang Sect Grand Elder is a heaven-sent genius, but compared to you, who are so astonishingly brilliant, he still falls far short! You're only a little over three hundred years old, aren't you?"

Luo Chen smiled without speaking.

Deng Taiyue shook his head and continued introducing the Dongyang Sect Grand Elder.

"He advanced to late Sage very early. Logically, during the human-demon war, he should have come out to help. But for the entire two hundred years of that war, he never personally made a move. It's said that he took the opportunity to achieve perfect merit and became the only Sage Grand Perfection in the Eastern Wilderness!"

Sage Grand Perfection?

Luo Chen's lips curled slightly, showing a hint of interest.

He asked casually, "Does the Dongyang Sect Grand Elder have any famous battle achievements?"

Deng Taiyue was taken aback. "That stumps me."

Luo Chen frowned. "Then are there any records of him making a move that many witnessed?"

Deng Taiyue thought for a moment. "There is one, but it wasn't a life-and-death struggle. Not long ago, during that auction I hosted, Nine Spirit Primogenitor, who had just reached Grand Cultivator realm, was in high spirits and exchanged a move with the Dongyang Grand Elder."

"The result?" Luo Chen showed curiosity.

"Nine Spirit Primogenitor lost, slightly weaker by a hair," Deng Taiyue replied.

Luo Chen wasn't very satisfied with this answer. Too little information.

"But later, when we gathered privately, Nine Spirit Primogenitor seemed dissatisfied with the outcome of that duel. He said outright that if it had been a life-and-death struggle, the outcome would have been uncertain."

With this supplementary description, Luo Chen had a rough idea.

At least when he had sparred with Nine Spirit Primogenitor, who had just reached Grand Cultivator, the latter had lost with genuine conviction.

But the Dongyang Grand Elder had defeated Nine Spirit Primogenitor in one move, yet the other wasn't convinced—that meant the gap between them wasn't that large.

"It seems this man is skilled at cultivation but not proficient in combat," Luo Chen made a rough judgment.

Deng Taiyue asked curiously, "Do you want to eliminate him?"

Luo Chen countered, "Didn't you say he wouldn't be tempted by the Enlightenment Pill to become my ally?"

Deng Taiyue nodded. "Indeed. After the Nether Origin Sect was destroyed, he surrendered directly to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. Even now, with the main force of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect having left the Eastern Wilderness, he still collects tributes from various Eastern Wilderness sects on behalf of the Dao Sect. There are rumors that he will follow the old Dao Sect Star Gate system and join the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. In return, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect has promised to help him achieve the Ascendant realm. So your Enlightenment Pill will have a hard time moving him."

The Enlightenment Pill only gave one a chance to comprehend the true meaning of laws early and accelerate cultivation.

Although comprehending the true meaning of laws greatly helped in future advancement to the Ascendant realm, what was truly needed to reach Ascendant was not the true meaning of laws, nor even the primordial spirit, but a fifth-order spirit land!

And such territories were only held by the various holy lands of the Mountains and Seas Realm.

If Luo Chen could one day reopen the Jambu Mountain buried in the Nether Abyss, he too would have a chance to possess a fifth-order spirit land.

In fact, this was precisely why Deng Taiyue was so accommodating to him now—because Deng Taiyue had truly seen Jambu Mountain and knew that Luo Chen had collected the Netherwater Spring Eye, already possessing the basic conditions to reopen Jambu Mountain.

If he one day reached the final step, he too would desperately need a fifth-order spirit land.

At that time, the three promises Luo Chen had made today would be very precious.

By the same logic, he could well understand why the Dongyang Grand Elder would absolutely never accept any conditions from Luo Chen.

The temptation of achieving Ascendant was enough to override everything!

Deng Taiyue hesitated for a moment, then still advised, "But if you eliminate him now, wouldn't that alert the enemy? If the Dao Sect becomes wary, we truly won't have any chance to turn things around."

Luo Chen smiled and said, "You're right. I don't plan to make a move against him now. But I always need to meet him face to face. After all, rather than waiting for them to come to me, I'd rather go see the situation myself."

"Is that wise?" Deng Taiyue was worried.

Luo Chen brushed past him and patted his shoulder.

"Don't worry about these things. For now, make good use of the Enlightenment Pill and the pill-taking experience I gave you to cultivate well! Make sure you comprehend the true meaning of the laws!"

Luo Chen put his hood back on, took a step into the void, and disappeared into the night.

Only after he left did that boulder transform back into its beast form under the moonlight.

It said in a low voice, "Sage Taiyue, that man must never be made an enemy!"

Deng Taiyue said helplessly, "I naturally know how formidable he is. Before I comprehend the true meaning of the laws, I absolutely cannot be his opponent."

Shi Gandang growled, "No, even if you make a breakthrough, don't try to provoke him."

Deng Taiyue frowned slightly, looking at his stone spirit in confusion.

Shi Gandang took a deep breath, his eyes filled with deep fear.

"The feeling he gives me is no weaker than an Ascendant great power."

Deng Taiyue was stunned. Was it really that exaggerated?

...

"Master, that stone spirit was quite interesting. Why didn't you take it in?"

Bai Meiling piloted the Frost Eagle Shuttle, asking curiously about that fellow named Shi Gandang from earlier.

Luo Chen said helplessly, "That's someone's mountain-protecting stone spirit. How could I take what others treasure?"

Bai Meiling let out an "oh," then said, "But it seemed very afraid of you. You just casually sent out a strand of magic power, and it reverted to its original form."

Luo Chen said calmly, "It should naturally fear me! Among sentient beings, there are also distinctions of strength. My Purple Mansion contains two great fire spirits, both fifth-order existences comparable to Ascendants. I merely leaked a trace of my aura, enough to make Shi Gandang feel terror."

Bai Meiling suddenly understood.

She had also witnessed the Nirvana Fire Spirit not long ago—a powerful existence like a true divine beast phoenix!

"Master, where are we going next? Directly out to sea, or to Dongyang Sect?"

"To Dongyang Sect, and also out to sea, since it's on the way," Luo Chen said calmly, showing no concern about entering the Eastern Wilderness's most supreme Sage sect.

Bai Meiling giggled and accelerated the Frost Eagle Shuttle.

...

A few days later.

Inside Dongyang Sect, on a long stone staircase, Luo Chen walked calmly upward.

Bai Meiling's surprised voice came from beside him.

"Master, Dongyang Sect is so lively!"

Luo Chen looked up at the sky and couldn't help but sigh. Yes, it had been a long time since he'd seen such a lively sect.

Between heaven and earth, streaks of light were like threads, and sword-flying cultivators were everywhere.

Among the forests and mountains, figures flickered.

Even on this stone staircase leading to the peak where guest elders resided, disciples came and went.

This was truly an anomaly among the various sects that had suffered talent depletion after two hundred years of human-demon war.

But considering that Dongyang Sect, aside from dispatching Shouyang Mountain Hermit and some low-level disciples, had hardly participated in the front-line battles over these two hundred years, it made perfect sense.

Especially since after the human-demon war ended, Dongyang Sect had been collecting tributes from various Eastern Wilderness sects on behalf of the Dao Sect—the amount of oil they could skim from that was unimaginable.

Under these circumstances, Dongyang Sect could truly be called the number one sect of the Eastern Wilderness!

Unfortunately, such a Dongyang Sect had no right to independence.

Luo Chen advanced step by step, and the disciples around him seemed unable to see him.

Before long, he arrived at a courtyard.

Inside, two people were conversing.

"I wonder when we can return to the Central Continent."

"What's so good about going back? Now the sect is fragmented, and the various great families are fighting for power. The ancestor's mind isn't on governing the sect. If we go back, we'll be dragged into the vortex of internal strife. Isn't it better to be here, collecting tribute resources from the sects below while cultivating in peace?"

"That's not entirely true. Didn't the ancestor issue an order to vigorously cultivate a new generation of disciples? I was thinking that if I were home, I could add my newly adult grandson to the list. Maybe he'd have a chance to become a true disciple someday."

"Don't think about it. The conditions for this cultivation plan are extremely harsh. How many spirit roots does your grandson have?"

"True enough. Forget it. Let's just cultivate peacefully here in the Eastern Wilderness and aim to reach mid-Sage before going back. That way, we'll have a stronger voice in the clan."

"Cultivation alone won't cut it. The ancestor sent word that we need to watch out for Luo Chen."

"This Luo Chen..."

The conversation in the courtyard continued, covering many topics, not limited to one continent or region, or cultivation techniques.

The two Sages chatted freely, speaking of whatever came to mind.

It wasn't until evening that one of them took his leave.

After he left, the remaining cultivator was about to use his magic power to close the courtyard door when he saw a figure shrouded in a black robe walk in.

"You are?"

This cultivator was just about to show displeasure at the uninvited guest when he saw the person remove his hood, revealing a pair of eyes flickering with strange colors.

Beyond those eyes, he could see nothing, remember nothing of the visitor's face.

He only heard a voice coming through the haze.

"Name?"

He murmured in reply, "Shi Chuanting."

The corner of the black-robed man's mouth lifted. "Lucky me. A Sage from the Twelve Great Houses, after all."

(Chapter End)
