# Chapter 1039: Main Soul Returns to Its Place, Refining the Treasure in the Azure Void

Above the White Egret Marsh.

A massive bird spread its wings, circling endlessly through the void.

Its eyes blazed with sharp light as it gazed down upon this territory that had once belonged to it. There was a human-like expression in those pupils.

When it saw that the rolling black qi currents—which had made even it feel a tremor of fear—had dissipated, the great white egret flapped its wings and descended to the deepest part of the White Egret Marsh.

Pushing through layers of miasma, threading through dense curtains of water, it entered a cave dwelling.

Inside, a colorful python lay coiled, sleeping soundly.

The python's body was enormous, yet not bloated. On the contrary, it was slender and long, giving off a certain seductive allure.

If a knowledgeable cultivator were present, they would have instantly recognized the python's origin.

It was a Nine Nether Charm Python!

When the White Egret Demon King entered, the Nine Nether Charm Python seemed to sense something and opened a pair of clear eyes.

Their gazes met. The White Egret Demon King lowered its head respectfully.

The Nine Nether Charm Python's body trembled, and then it transformed into a young woman.

After scanning the White Egret Demon King's memories, a flicker of unusual light shone in the young woman's eyes.

"Only twenty years old, yet already at the mid-Initiate stage. Such talent is truly baffling!"

"If it were some demonic quick-cultivation method, that would be one thing. But his mana is abundant, his foundation solid, without the slightest hint of instability. To easily slay an early-Adept cultivator while at mid-Initiate—even my master couldn't have done that back then."

"Truly, one can only say he is a clone that my master raised with great effort and care!"

After a moment of reflection, the woman hesitated slightly.

She was hesitating whether to report Zhou Fuyou's rapid progress to her master—especially the astonishing solidity of his foundation.

But after thinking it over, she ultimately dismissed the idea.

Her master should already be very familiar with Zhou Fuyou's cultivation level.

As for other aspects, she doubted her master would care.

"Besides, there are only two uses left on that voice transmission jade slip. I can't waste them on such trivial matters."

Muttering to herself, the woman looked at the bowing White Egret Demon King.

"The Zhou family's development of the White Egret Marsh has already begun. You and your tribe cannot continue to live in peace. Make your plans early. Choose whether to die on this ancestral land, or follow my advice and go to the Eastern Wilderness, to join the Luo Tian Sect."

"I can't guarantee anything else, but helping you transform ahead of time is a trivial matter for my master."

The White Egret Demon King seemed to have already made up its mind. It prostrated itself on the ground and spoke in a melodious voice.

"Little demon will follow your instructions, milady!"

A brilliant smile spread across the young woman's face. "You've made the right decision. Don't think that just because the Zhou family has ignored you all these years, they've forgotten about you. They simply haven't had the time to deal with you yet. Not to mention anything else, if the Zhou family's old ancestor were willing, you wouldn't be able to resist. In the next couple of days, take my token and lead your tribe to the Eastern Wilderness. Someone will be there to receive you."

The White Egret Demon King nodded heavily, anticipation shining in its eyes.

According to this woman's description, the sect called Luo Tian Sect had a miraculous pill that allowed demon beasts to transform ahead of time at the third rank.

If that were true, then it might have hope of reaching the fourth-rank Demon Emperor realm!

Such temptation was enough to make it risk leaving its homeland and submitting to servitude.

...

The indigenous inhabitants that had occupied the White Egret Marsh for centuries began a quiet migration, unnoticed by anyone.

This should have been a major event that alarmed the Zhou family.

But the sudden, violent death of Sixth Elder Zhou Haiye had captured everyone's attention!

Even the plans to clear out the demon beasts and develop the White Egret Marsh had to be interrupted.

Of course, once they came to their senses, they could easily take over the now-empty White Egret Marsh. That would be a fortunate turn of events.

But for now, everyone's greatest concern was still Zhou Haiye's sudden fall.

The answer the family head had given was hardly convincing!

Several Initiate true cultivators had been circling outside at the time, and none of them had sensed any demon king's demonic qi. No one was willing to believe that Zhou Haiye had died at the hands of a demon beast.

Moreover, he was a dignified Adept superior. Even if he couldn't defeat a demon king, he wouldn't have fallen so easily.

After all, the family head, Zhou Junxiao, had been outside overseeing the situation, ready to intervene at any moment.

Therefore, all the Initiate true cultivators, along with Fourth Elder Zhou Xiaocheng—who had been in seclusion—and Third Elder Zhou Shuzhen—who had been handling matters outside—all rushed to the meeting hall, hoping to get a true answer from the family head.

But Zhou Junxiao did not show himself directly.

After dropping a single word—"Wait"—he hurried to the Coral Hall where the old ancestor resided.

It was half a day later before Zhou Junxiao walked out of the hall, his expression dazed.

"Was it a traitor?"

Just as the clan members were unwilling to believe his perfunctory explanation, he too was unwilling to believe Zhou Fuyou's explanation.

But with the old ancestor showing favoritism, he couldn't very well pressure Zhou Fuyou, the family's future hope.

Therefore, as soon as he left the Coral Hall, he went straight to Zhou Haiye's residence and began an inspection.

Not only that, but he recklessly used violent means to forcibly open the storage pouch left on Zhou Haiye's corpse.

In the end, when he saw certain letters and a large number of cultivation resources that bore the distinctive characteristics of the Yin family—resources that did not belong to the Zhou family—he had no choice but to accept the truth of what Zhou Fuyou had said.

"Zhou Haiye was a traitor. Zhou Fuyou killed him to cleanse the family!"

"But... the old ancestor isn't dead yet. Why couldn't he wait?"

...

In the deep, secluded Coral Hall, a weathered voice echoed slowly.

"The great tree hasn't fallen yet, but the monkeys beneath are already looking for new mountains to climb. A Sage family, yet its cohesion can't even match that of some large sects."

Inside the hall, Zhou Fuyou stood calmly, his white robe with red coral patterns spotless.

He spoke: "Old ancestor need not grieve. Some people are merely blinded. Since our Zhou family has survived the demon beasts of the Northern Sea and the great calamity of the Central Continent, what are these storms compared to that?"

Above, Zhou Yunshen shook his head. "But this old man's lifespan is indeed running out. At most, I have ten years left."

Zhou Fuyou, who had remained calm throughout—who had not even lost his composure when killing Zhou Haiye—was now shocked.

"Ten years?"

Zhou Yunshen said, "Yes. Ten years at most. If not for a friend who extended my life back then, I wouldn't even have these ten years."

Zhou Fuyou opened his mouth, not knowing what to say.

But Zhou Yunshen was very calm. He smiled and said, "If I go, the Zhou family will no longer have a Sage to guard it. Fuyou, tell me, what should be done then?"

Without hesitation, Zhou Fuyou replied, "The Grand Elder is in seclusion, striving to break through to the Sage realm. Once he succeeds, he will surely protect the Zhou family for another five hundred years!"

"He won't make it." Zhou Yunshen seemed to have seen through everything long ago. He sighed. "Cang'er's innate talent was among the worst of the second-generation clan members. Those with better talent either died in the endless demon beast attacks of the Coral Sea back then, or perished in the Central Continent calamity. He was purely built up by the clan's resources. The Sage realm, for him, seems like a single step away, but I fear he will exhaust all his strength and still not cross that threshold."

Zhou Fuyou had never imagined that the old ancestor, who usually seemed to have everything under control, could be so pessimistic.

He thought for a moment, then a resolute expression appeared on his face. "Old ancestor need not worry. There are multiple Adept superiors in the clan to guard it for now. Outside the clan, a group of young talents have joined the Tinnan Dao Palace. Given time, they will become the backbone of the family. And above all... there is me!"

Zhou Yunshen's cloudy gaze fell upon the young man, and he was silent for a long time.

Facing that look, Zhou Fuyou felt a strange familiarity.

Over the years, he had occasionally come to the Coral Hall to seek the old ancestor's guidance on advanced cultivation knowledge, especially the cultivation of the spell *Qi Sea Floating Heaven*.

The old ancestor had been almost completely open with him, holding nothing back.

He had even given Zhou Fuyou his famous early-life magic treasure, the *Life-Severing Ruler*.

But rarely had he seen the old ancestor look at him with such an expression.

Affection, cherishing, relief, gratification—it was the look of an elder toward a junior. Yet within it, there was also an indescribable, unfathomable sadness.

When had he seen this look before?

Zhou Fuyou recalled the first time he had met the old ancestor. Yes, that time!

And in that look, there had also been a trace of indifference.

Now, the indifference had faded, but it only made Zhou Fuyou more puzzled.

A vague sigh reached his ears.

"If only it were true."

Zhou Fuyou was confused. "Old ancestor, what did you say?"

*Hoo...*

The old man exhaled a breath of turbid qi and forced a smile.

"Nothing. I was just reminded, when you mentioned the Tinnan Dao Palace, that it's time for you to go there."

Zhou Fuyou was caught off guard.

"But..."

"The Zhou family's inheritance is too narrow. The longer you stay in the clan, the more you waste your talent. The Tinnan Dao Palace is a branch of the former strongest holy land of the Mountains and Seas Realm, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. Its inheritance and cultivation resources far surpass those of our little Zhou family. I'm sure they are eager to see you. Coincidentally, I have been feeling restless from sitting too long. This time, I will personally escort you to the Tinnan Dao Palace!"

Zhou Fuyou clenched his fists. "Fuyou does not wish to leave yet!"

Zhou Yunshen seemed to have seen through him already. He smiled. "Don't worry about your little sister. Since I gave her the Spirit Root Pill back then, I naturally won't mistreat her. After you leave, the clan will treat her as a core member. I will have Third Elder Zhou Shuzhen personally instruct her. All resources will be doubled. She will definitely succeed in building her foundation. Are you reassured now?"

Zhou Fuyou was still not reassured. Or rather, the reason he insisted on staying in the clan was precisely to protect his little sister.

Faced with this stubbornness, Zhou Yunshen patiently reasoned with him.

"She is no longer a child. She has come of age. She can cultivate now. You cannot protect her forever."

"No, I can!"

"But if you keep delaying your arrival at the Tinnan Dao Palace, they will surely blame the Zhou family, and even implicate your sister. Do you want that to happen?"

"This..."

"Go to the Dao Palace and cultivate well. Once you have a firm foothold, you can bring her there for better guidance. If your cultivation reaches great heights, and you become a major figure in the Tinnan Dao Palace, or even the entire Dao Sect, won't you be able to protect your sister even better?"

This time, Zhou Fuyou wavered.

Zhou Yunshen did not continue to persuade. He believed this intelligent child would be able to figure it out.

The White Egret Marsh might be vast, but it was only three thousand li.

And the entire cultivation world was far more than three thousand li.

"Go back and think it over carefully. This may be this old man's last long journey."

...

In the azure void, a deathly silence shrouded everything.

Suddenly!

A roar, muffled to the extreme, erupted, carrying an unyielding, majestic will that heavily crushed the void.

"ROAR!"

A colossal figure broke free from all restraints, standing proudly in the boundless starry sky.

It was clad in cyan scales, its body mottled. Nine enormous sword scars ran through its entire form, like traces left by the passage of time.

On its massive head, there were no majestic horns, yet it seemed capable of piercing the heavens.

Such a terrifying, thousand-zhang body was supported by only a single, thick leg.

That single leg was as heavy as a heavenly pillar, not only bearing the terrifying body but also suppressing the starry sky beneath its feet, the boundless azure void.

The moment it appeared, a fierce wind arose around it. A host of powerful vengeful spirits and fierce ghosts rushed at it from all directions, howling.

Among them were mother-and-child pairs linked together, flickering phantoms, roaring blood demons, and three-legged crows spouting layers of ghost fire.

If these resentful spirits and fierce ghosts were placed in the cultivation world, every single one of them would be a calamity capable of overturning rivers and seas.

Yet before this creature, they were like gadflies and mosquitoes, utterly beneath its notice.

Its eyes, like stars, were fixed solely on the black-robed Daoist in the distance, whose robes were flapping in the wind. Its gaze burned with boundless fury.

"ROAR!"

The furious roar, carrying matchless power, surged toward the soul of every living creature.

But when it reached the black-robed Daoist, it was like a gentle breeze brushing his face, merely stirring the strands of hair at his temples.

Luo Chen stood atop the Primordial Unity Cauldron, holding the Soul-Refining Banner, his expression solemn.

"Kui Ox, stop struggling! This banner is your soul's true home!"

In response, the Kui Ox roared even more fiercely.

Luo Chen was not surprised.

After twenty years of effort, consuming vast amounts of mana, and refining it day and night with the Nirvana Sacred Flame and the Primordial Unity Cauldron, he had finally reached this day.

The Kui Ox, now seemingly invincible, as if it could swallow the sun and moon, was merely a final flicker of light, a last unwilling struggle.

Without a physical body, the only thing the Kui Ox could rely on was its soul attacks.

But Luo Chen's soul foundation far surpassed that of this Kui Ox ghost god. Especially with the enhancement of the *Prison Suppression* soul art, the Kui Ox ghost god could not even break through his defenses.

Rage!

Struggle!

In the end, submit to me!

Luo Chen divided his mind, shaking the great banner until heaven and earth trembled, and unfurling the great cauldron until even the stars seemed suppressed.

Amidst this constant suppression and pulling, the Kui Ox ghost god, fighting for its life, could only roar unwillingly as it was slowly pulled into the Soul-Refining Banner.

The main soul had returned to its place!

The remaining ghosts also howled as they entered the Soul-Refining Banner.

Just as Luo Chen was feeling complacent about having completely subdued a fifth-rank ghost god, a faint cracking sound reached his ears.

*Crack!*

Luo Chen lowered his head, his gaze falling upon the banner in his hand.

On the banner's surface, a million ghostly shadows overlapped, with the Kui Ox roaring.

But on the flagpole, cracks were visibly appearing one after another.

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow and immediately understood why.

"This Soul-Refining Banner, after all, cannot bear the weight of a fifth-rank ghost god!"

This banner had originally been obtained from a Northern Sea Adept cultivator. Back then, it was merely the most ordinary Myriad Souls Banner.

Later, through Luo Chen and Xiao Bai's continuous refinement, it had gradually grown into a Hundred Thousand Fierce Souls Banner, and then a Million Baleful Souls Banner.

Although Luo Chen had often added materials to strengthen the banner during the process, bearing a fifth-rank ghost god was still too much for it.

Fortunately, Luo Chen's knowledge of artifact refinement was considerable, and he quickly thought of a solution.

With a flick of his wrist, another large banner appeared before him.

It was white, peaceful, and serene—the Soul-Nourishing Banner.

It had come from the Blood Nightmare Moluo of the Primordial Demon Sect, and was of genuine true artifact quality.

If he refines it into the Soul-Refining Banner, he could further enhance the banner's strength.

However, it would still not be enough to bear the Kui Ox's power.

Luo Chen did not stop. Beams of light shot out from his storage ring. Looking closely, they were clearly many rare and precious metal, stone, jade, and wood materials that were hard to come by even in the cultivation world.

With a sweep of his hand, he threw both soul banners and all the materials into the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Opening his mouth, he spat out a cyan flame and began the smelting.

The Daoist sat cross-legged in the void, his true flame blazing as he continuously refined the treasure.

This stretch of azure void seemed to have disturbed heaven and earth with the brief but intense battle that had just occurred.

A meteorite floating in the cosmos was attracted and flew toward this spot.

The Daoist glanced at it, golden light flickering in his eyes. He reached out with a large hand across the void.

A giant hand materialized in the void, grabbing the meteorite firmly, and then threw it into the gray great cauldron as well.

Time passed, bit by bit.

About half a year later, the Daoist extinguished his flame and withdrew his hand.

A pitch-black, profound banner floated down before him.

There was still a long crack on it, but satisfaction had already appeared in the Daoist's eyes.

"It should be enough to support one condensation of the Kui Ox's divine body!"

(End of this chapter)
