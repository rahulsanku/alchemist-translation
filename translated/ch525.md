# Chapter 525: The Demon Sect Has Fallen, Order Collapses

---

On the blood-reeking deck, no one dared even breathe too loudly.

Because of Luo Chen's silence, the great ship had become deathly quiet.

*Demon Sea!*

The moment he heard those three words, Luo Chen immediately thought of the Northern Sea cultivation world, one of the Five Continents of the Mountains and Seas Realm.

For no other reason than this: the Northern Sea was also known as the Demon Sea.

And the origin of that name lay in the fact that the Holy Land governing the Northern Sea cultivation world was a thoroughgoing demon-path sect.

During the years he had spent in quiet cultivation on Tianlan Peak, Luo Chen had read a great many books.

Most of them had been cultivation methods and secret manuals, useful for increasing his strength.

After that came biographies of notable figures connected to the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world. After all, if one lived on that continent, one had to go out sooner or later. At low realms it was one thing, but once one reached Adept, it was naturally impossible to keep cowering inside a single domain forever. If he went out and accidentally provoked someone he should not provoke, that would be a disaster from nowhere.

Beyond those, he had also read no small number of travelogues, regional records, and accounts of strange customs throughout the world.

Among those classics that recorded local customs and landscapes, there had been systematic overviews of the Mountains and Seas Realm itself.

Remote places, dangerous regions, forbidden lands, and dead zones were not described in much detail. But the Five Continents were absolutely the priority of priorities.

Especially the hegemon-level existences that ruled the Five Continents.

The Nether Origin Sect of the Eastern Wilderness, the Life and Death Gate of the Southern Frontier, Hanging Temple of the Western Desert, the Primordial Demon Sect of the Northern Sea, and the Tianyuan Dao Sect of Central Continent.

Of the Five Continents, Central Continent had already been completely controlled by the Tianyuan Dao Sect. No natural calamities or man-made disasters remained there, nor demons, monsters, ghosts, or fiends. It was the one true human cultivation Holy Land beneath heaven.

Rumor had it that since the ancient age three thousand years ago, every person in the Mountains and Seas Realm who had ascended had come from Central Continent.

And precisely because of that, every year, countless peerless geniuses from the four outer continents journeyed to Central Continent, seeking that chance at ascension.

As for the other four continents?

The Eastern Wilderness had the million barbarian mountains watching like a tiger from beyond the border. Even after the ancient war and the current era's three thousand years of expansion, the human cultivators had only occupied the thirty-six domains. Who knew how many heavenly materials, earthly treasures, and supreme-grade spirit veins were still hidden in the depths of the wilds?

That was also why the Eastern Wilderness hegemon, the Nether Origin Sect, was extremely willing to support the large and small sects beneath it.

Whenever a new Sage upper sect was born, it did its best to mediate between that new upper sect and the established local sects, stopping their internal conflict and redirecting their contradictions outward instead. Time and again, it launched expansion wars against the million mountains.

If one ignored the petty scheming and intrigue within the Eastern Wilderness cultivator community itself and looked only from a grand perspective, the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world could still be considered united in purpose, outward-facing on major matters.

By comparison, the Southern Frontier was extremely mysterious.

That Life and Death Gate was proficient in all manner of gu arts.

The path of gu involved external things, and was not too unusual by itself.

But the Life and Death Gate had combined gu with cultivation methods, deriving an entire path of gu cultivation.

Such gu were called spirit gu.

Spirit gu were strange and secretive beyond measure. In battle, they were impossible to guard against.

Even if a cultivator was ten thousand parts vigilant, a single lapse could easily see them fall victim.

Thus, even powerful experts with great curiosity generally had no desire to travel there unless they were native cultivators of the Southern Frontier.

For that reason, among the Five Continents of the Mountains and Seas Realm, news from the Southern Frontier was the scarcest. As time passed, ordinary cultivators spoke of it with increasing caution, and it became ever more mysterious.

Outsiders found it difficult to judge Southern Frontier cultivators. The only warning given was: make little contact.

The Western Desert was another matter entirely.

Hanging Temple. The name alone told one it was a Buddhist Holy Land.

Most of those within were monks.

However, unlike ordinary Buddhist forces that relied on vast numbers of people, Hanging Temple itself did not have many monks at all. It was said that year-round, it maintained only one hundred and eight eminent monks.

But if one underestimated Hanging Temple because of that, one would be gravely mistaken.

Although Hanging Temple itself had few monks, that did not mean its strength was weak. The entire Western Desert was filled with Buddhist sects.

And every one of those Buddhist sects had countless ties to Hanging Temple.

It could even be said that virtually every powerful Buddhist cultivator in the Mountains and Seas Realm had some connection to Hanging Temple.

Buddhism did not flourish in the Eastern Wilderness, yet even there stood the Tianji Chan Sect.

That late-stage Sage monk, Huiping, was rumored to have once been one of Hanging Temple's one hundred and eight monks.

Lastly came the Northern Sea cultivation world.

The Northern Sea was boundless. Its resources were so abundant they crowned the entire Mountains and Seas Realm. Even the Eastern Wilderness's million mountains, famed as a place of matchless natural treasures, could not compare to the Northern Sea.

The world had only ever heard of Northern Sea resources flowing into the other four continents. No one had heard of the Northern Sea actively requesting cultivation resources from those other four continents.

When Luo Chen was young, one of the main ingredients he often used to refine the All-Wonders Pill, the Jade Yao Pillar, had come from the Northern Sea.

The two regions were separated by hundreds of millions of li, yet it could still be transported to the Eastern Wilderness. That alone showed how abundant the Northern Sea's resources were.

And beneath those plentiful resources lay countless dangers and crises.

Because within the boundless ocean there were equally boundless demon beasts and sea monsters.

The truly powerful among them could contend with Ascendants.

Such a territory was not something an ordinary sect could suppress.

Fortunately, the force stationed in the Northern Sea was no righteous sect that believed in kindness toward others.

It was the Primordial Demon Sect, hailed as the foremost demon sect beneath heaven.

Within it were countless demonic arts and evil techniques. Its disciples were all ruthless, cruel, and cold-blooded, while those in the upper ranks were tyrannical beyond compare.

The entire sect believed in the law of the jungle: the weak were prey to the strong.

That was true not only externally, but internally as well. Even terrifying "blood trials" were often held within the sect.

Thus, any existence able to distinguish themselves within the Primordial Demon Sect was without exception a fiendish devil or evil lord.

Because of this, in the Northern Sea, countless great demons roamed the waters, while great devils likewise ran rampant. Over time, the other four continents gave the Northern Sea its unique alias: the Demon Sea.

*"I, Luo Chen, a perfectly decent and kindhearted man, have actually fallen into such a dangerous and wicked place?"*

Although he had already made more than enough mental preparations, Luo Chen still felt somewhat dazed.

While he sank into thought, the hearts of the black-faced man and the others kept dropping lower.

He had merely heard the names Swallow Islands and Northern Sea. Why had this Adept cultivator reacted like this?

Thinking of the man's earlier display of divine might, when he had burned more than ten thousand sea serpents to death in one stroke, and especially that strange azure-red flame, one thought abruptly surfaced in the black-faced man's mind.

Could this person be a remnant of the Primordial Demon Sect?

In the end, he could not hold himself back.

"Senior, although our Swallow Islands are under the jurisdiction of the Cang Hai Alliance, that has only been true for the past thirty years. The people here still turn their hearts toward the Primordial Demon Holy Sect!"

The remark came out of nowhere.

Luo Chen frowned, drew back his thoughts, and looked at him coldly. "What is your name?"

Beneath that indifferent gaze, the black-faced man's heart trembled.

Asking his name...

"I... I..."

"Hm?" Luo Chen stared at him, a faint dangerous flavor in his expression. "I saved your entire ship, yet you refuse even to give your name? Do you truly think I am easy to deal with?"

Thud.

The black-faced man dropped to his knees at once, terrified, and began knocking his head against the deck.

"Senior, calm your anger! Senior, calm your anger. This junior is Cheng Haichang. My lowly name is not worth dirtying Senior's ears. I did not say it earlier only because I feared it would sully your hearing!"

Luo Chen shook his head, a sense of absurdity rising in his heart.

In his view, this Cheng Haichang had the respectable cultivation of Initiate Stage Three. Placed in Dahe Market, he could have ruled one side. Even in Tianlan, he would count as the leader of a small force.

When meeting an Adept cultivator, perhaps he could not be neither servile nor overbearing, but there was no need to abase himself to this degree.

It was only a few questions. Why act so lowly?

Vaguely, Luo Chen understood something.

Perhaps this was the atmosphere that had formed in the Northern Sea cultivation world after centuries and millennia beneath the rule of a demon-path Holy Sect.

Absolute obedience from those below toward those above.

After all, demon-path experts truly were the sort to draw blades at the slightest disagreement.

Some of the more debased among them did not even need to speak. If something displeased their eye, and if the cultivation gap was great enough, they would simply seize it by force.

After all, the term "robber cultivator" had originally come from the Northern Sea cultivation world.

But if absolute obedience was the rule, then why had he not directly given his name earlier?

Luo Chen did not pursue that question.

Right now, he only needed to understand enough information. He could distinguish and judge the specifics for himself. The deeper he asked, the easier it would be to expose his identity as an outsider.

Unless he killed everyone aboard afterward.

But Luo Chen was not truly some great devil. How could he behave so devoid of humanity?

"Cheng Haichang, is it? Raise your head and look at me."

Cheng Haichang stopped kowtowing. His forehead was covered in red marks, proof of how heavily he had struck the deck earlier; even the body of an Initiate cultivator who had cleansed marrow and remolded tendons bore slight injuries.

He looked at Luo Chen in fear, and what entered his eyes was no longer a sheet of coldness, but a pair of kindly, gentle eyes.

The feeling was like seeing the old clan chief who had personally instructed him in his lessons years ago.

Before him, he had not the slightest secret. Nor did he need to have the slightest secret.

After all, this was a relative who had treated him like a beloved grandson.

"Next, I ask. You answer."

"Mm. Haichang will speak everything he knows and hold nothing back!"

Luo Chen's eyes were deep and dim, his voice clear and cold. "What force do you belong to?"

"The Cheng clan of Black Swan Island. We are one of the thirty-six islands under the Swallow Islands, and we are ruled by the newly risen Cang Hai Alliance."

"Do you have a detailed map on you?"

"Yes."

"Give it to me."

Obediently, Cheng Haichang took out a well-preserved map and presented it respectfully to Luo Chen.

When Luo Chen took it, it felt slick and smooth, almost like fish skin.

He unfolded the map and examined the densely marked terrain. Following Cheng Haichang's guidance, his gaze landed on an area in the lower right corner.

There, an extremely tiny sea swallow seemed about to spread its wings and fly.

A small black dot was marked at the most remote part of the sea swallow's wing. That was where the Cheng clan of Black Swan Island had its clan territory.

From that point, expanding outward and then looking back, the Swallow Islands' area appeared quite large.

Yet across the entire map, there were countless carefully marked places even larger than the Swallow Islands.

The world said the Northern Sea was boundless and vast without end.

Now, even a map obtained from an Initiate cultivator verified that point.

At least in Luo Chen's eyes, even if the Eastern Wilderness's million mountains were fully explored one day, they would likely still not compare to the breadth of the Northern Sea.

Because on the edges of the map, many unknown sea regions were still marked.

As he asked questions, Luo Chen compared them against the map.

"How many cultivators does the Cheng clan have at each realm?"

"What is the overall level of cultivation across the Swallow Islands? Are there Adept cultivators holding them down?"

"What level of force is this Cang Hai Alliance? Why have I never heard of it before?"

Cheng Haichang answered each question one by one. He truly did speak everything he knew, and in exhaustive detail.

And when he blurted out the words "Cang Hai Alliance, Northern Sea Holy Land," Luo Chen's pupils could not help contracting.

"What kind of joke is this? Isn't the Northern Sea Holy Land the Primordial Demon Sect?"

Cheng Haichang said sincerely, "A hundred years ago, chaos erupted in the Northern Sea. The Primordial Demon Sect's mountain gate, Arctic Yemo Heaven, was besieged by hundreds of millions of sea demons. Several ancient demons at the Ascendant level personally took action and completely broke it."

"Nonsense!" Luo Chen still found it unbelievable. "That Arctic Yemo Heaven was known as the most impregnable forbidden land beneath heaven. It had the Fallen Sea, which descends once every thousand years, at its back, and was guarded on both sides by primordial magnetic auroras. How could it have ended up besieged?"

"This junior does not know. I only know that battle overturned heaven and earth and made the ocean boil. There was even an Ascendant existence who fell within it."

Luo Chen's mind shook violently.

An Ascendant existence fell?

That was practically the greatest joke beneath heaven.

Ascendant experts could directly ascend. They were existences known as reserve immortals.

Even they could fall?

He forced down the shock in his heart and asked, "Then what is this Cang Hai Alliance?"

Without hesitation, Cheng Haichang said, "The Cang Hai Alliance is a force personally established after the Northern Sea chaos by a mighty expert surnamed Li. It is said that although this person came from the Primordial Demon Sect in his early years, he is upright and proper, unlike demonic fiends and evil Daoists. Unwilling to see the Northern Sea human race become food for the sea demons, he established an alliance, asking only that the human race connect islands and form arrays to protect themselves. The Cang Hai Alliance is also known as the Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance."

"Cang Hai Alliance? Connect islands and form arrays?"

"Yes. That mighty expert differs from the powerful figures of the Primordial Demon Sect and is extremely proficient in the path of formations. So long as one acknowledges oneself as part of the Cang Hai Alliance, he will selflessly pass down miraculous great formations that link island chains together, using them to prevent sea demons from coming ashore."

Luo Chen listened until his head began to ache.

So much information had shattered his fixed impression of the Northern Sea in an instant.

The new state of the Northern Sea gave him a thousand thoughts, and he did not know where to begin sorting them.

He only knew that after the original ruler had fallen, the order that had never been particularly good under the demon sect's rule had once teetered on the brink of collapse.

Fortunately, another mighty human expert had personally taken action, holding up the collapsing sky with one hand and establishing a new order.

Though that order seemed precarious as well.

Still, even the most fragile order was order. Luo Chen was exceptionally adaptable. Order meant laws could be relied upon. As long as he adapted to it, he could move through it like a fish in water.

If this were truly a place with no order at all, then he would have no choice but to consider finding some remote place, cultivating bitterly in seclusion, and grinding his realm forward one day at a time.

Strife was not what Luo Chen desired.

Rubbing his forehead, Luo Chen closed his eyes for an instant.

Then he waved a hand and walked toward the depths of the ship cabin.

"Return. Take me to Black Swan Island."

On the deck, Cheng Haichang, who had been kneeling earlier, suddenly trembled as though waking from some daze.

He turned and looked. Several of his companions were still staring blankly.

At that moment, horror rose in his heart.

*"As expected of a demon's methods. He casually uses soul-bewitching illusions to confuse people!"*

Shaken, he hurriedly cast a Clear Heart Art to awaken the companions who had been affected.

A moment later, everyone on deck looked terrified beyond measure.

The oldest among them, Cheng Ji, went to control the demon-hunting vessel and return, but before leaving, he still could not help transmitting with spiritual sense.

"Haichang, bringing such a devil back to the clan... will it cause some great trouble?"

"Whether it will cause great trouble, I don't know," Cheng Haichang said irritably, waving a hand. "But if we don't set sail now, we die. Besides, my Black Swan Cheng clan is merely a trifling Initiate clan. That Senior should not take any interest in us."

Cheng Ji asked again, "He personally asked your name just now, and he is proficient in soul-bewitching arts. Do you think he might be skilled in the Primordial Demon Sect's curse-killing arts?"

In an instant, Cheng Haichang's face went snow-white.

He swallowed. "He probably isn't. If he wanted to kill me, why use a curse-killing art with such a terrible price? A wave of his hand would be enough."

That made sense.

Cheng Ji nodded. Looking at the corpses scattered across the cabin and deck, sorrow showed in his eyes.

"I'll go steer the ship. You clean up here."

Although he grieved, he also looked as though he had long since seen such things too many times.

After he left, Cheng Haichang also fell silent.

Under the gazes of the remaining juniors, he suddenly took out that long white banner from his storage bag, his face turning fierce.

"So many souls cannot be wasted. At the very least, these people and demons should die for a purpose."

As he spoke, the long banner unfurled completely.

Streams of ghostly qi surged out from within.

On the deck that had been covered in blood moments ago, wails immediately rose. Remnant souls and broken spirits, some belonging to sea serpents, some to mortals, and even some to Cheng clan cultivators, were all drawn into the white banner.

As those souls poured in, the spiritual light on the white banner grew several shades denser.

Judging by its grade, it had already reached the supreme-grade level, only one step away from becoming a treasure.

...

Inside the cabin, Luo Chen silently watched the water mirror before him. Cheng Haichang's actions were all laid bare before his eyes. Even the brief spiritual-sense conversation between him and the old man had nowhere to hide beneath Luo Chen's powerful divine sense.

Moments ago, the Initiate cultivator had been timid and submissive, without the slightest bottom line. In the blink of an eye, he ignored the bonds of clan kinship and collected his clansmen's souls into that soul banner.

Such conduct truly displayed the words "fickle and changeable" to the fullest.

"Is this the survival path of Northern Sea cultivators?"

Luo Chen murmured. Seeing one leaf and knowing autumn, glimpsing the large through the small, this single person had already allowed him to sense a little of the atmosphere in the Northern Sea cultivation world.

He also understood now why, when he had asked the other party's name earlier, the man had been so terrified, to the point of risking Luo Chen's anger rather than daring to speak directly.

"A curse-killing art?"

"It seems the Primordial Demon Sect does indeed possess a strange spell called the Seven Nets Curse of Fate."

"Its cultivation is complex, its price enormous, and one must know the enemy's true name. But the power of the art is likewise extraordinary. Even without seeing the target, even separated by ten thousand li, one word can decide life and death."

"In that case, if I wish to move through the Northern Sea in the future, I will need to carefully consider my name and background."

Murmuring to himself, Luo Chen did not dispel the Water Mirror Art.

Because of the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture, his mana had fully converted into fire-attribute mana, making it ineffective for casting spells of the other elements.

But low-tier spells like the Water Mirror Art were still no issue.

He had cultivated this spell to Perfected Mastery long ago. Moreover, Mirror Flower Water Moon was rooted in his eyes, so it was not affected in the slightest.

Especially here, deep within the great sea, where dense water-element spirit qi abounded.

He only needed to circulate his cultivation method slightly, condense the Water Mirror Art before the spirit qi's attribute converted, and he could maintain it for a long time.

Some other low-tier spells could also be forced in this way if the environment was suitable and his proficiency high enough.

But as for exactly how far he could take that, Luo Chen would still need to find time to experiment properly.

Otherwise, in future battles, if he subconsciously used a spell he had once excelled in only for it to fail, the joke would be far too big.

As thoughts flowed through his mind, the Water Mirror Art monitored every corner of the ship. Luo Chen closed his eyes, divine sense spreading throughout his body.

It was time to take stock of his gains.

Just now, above the sea, he had burned and refined over ten thousand first-tier sea serpents with his azure flame. The life force he had absorbed could be called immense beyond measure.

The quality was insufficient, but the quantity was truly terrifying.

With only a slight perception, Luo Chen could not help grinning.

"This Northern Sea, called the Demon Sea, may be the land of opportunity meant for me!"

*(End of Chapter)*
