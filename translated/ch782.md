# Chapter 782: Sacred Land Message, Hundred Li Green Stream

Within Lingtian Pass.

Old Monster Motian and Divine Fire Sage had a fierce argument, eventually parting on bad terms.

As Divine Fire Sage's most beloved grand-disciple, Ran Feng curiously asked, "Grandmaster, why didn't you make a move against Luo Chen?"

Having already reached the Adept realm and spent his early years dealing extensively with the Luo Tian Sect, he was somewhat familiar with many secrets. He knew the inside story of the Divine Sect's Supreme Elder's covetousness toward Luo Chen.

Logically speaking, this was a good opportunity.

Using the combined strength of the two superior sects to challenge Luo Chen, requesting the Lingtian City Lord to punish him, and then they could take the opportunity to...

"What meaning would there be in making a move?" Divine Fire Sage gazed in the direction Old Monster Motian had departed, his eyes narrowing slightly as he asked casually.

"Uh..." Ran Feng couldn't articulate a reason.

Divine Fire Sage continued, "Setting aside whether the Lingtian City Lord would punish Luo Chen, the mere fact that he now holds the status of a Sage means any punishment would not be severe. At most, he'd be fined some spirit stones or assigned some menial tasks—such penalties are utterly useless to us. Moreover..."

He paused, his tone turning grim, "The Supreme Elder wants Luo Chen intact. Rather than alerting the snake by striking the grass, it's better to first report this news to the Supreme Elder. He will handle it himself."

Ran Feng suddenly understood, his expression showing the wisdom of his ancestor.

Divine Fire Sage forced a smile, inwardly weighing the gains and losses.

Yin Sixiang was dead; he would certainly be held accountable. But given the critical current situation, the sect wouldn't do anything severe to an Ascendant-level combatant like him.

Especially since he had also brought back news of Luo Chen—the Supreme Elder might even reward him for that.

"His realm... roughly between Sage second layer... and third layer?"

Divine Fire Sage was somewhat uncertain. Luo Chen's displayed realm during battle fluctuated unpredictably, making it hard to pin down.

But regardless, at most he was merely an early-stage Sage.

The only thing worthy of caution was probably that white flame of his.

It had refined Old Monster Motian's lifebound true artifact in an instant—truly powerful and bizarre.

This intelligence also needed to be reported.

"The Great Palace Master of Fenghua Palace might be his potential ally, and also that Lingtian City Lord..."

As he pondered his assessment of Luo Chen and considered the Lingtian City Lord's attitude toward him, Divine Fire Sage hesitated.

It was simply too friendly!

Although the outside world always rumored that this man was keen on making friends, that was only with those whose backgrounds were relatively well-known.

As for this suddenly emerged Luo Chen—his temperament, his conduct, his exceptional qualities—all were still completely unknown. How could the Lingtian City Lord favor him so much?

It was precisely because of the Lingtian City Lord's attitude that Divine Fire Sage had chosen to restrain himself and not act at the City Lord's mansion back then.

"Could it be that he isn't even curious about how Luo Chen escaped from Green Parasol Mountain back then?"

...

Within the City Lord's mansion.

Before Ye Lingtian lay a thick stack of bamboo slips, his head buried among them.

These bamboo slips were not old; instead, they emitted a faint, refreshing fragrance of bamboo and ink, as if freshly made.

And the content recorded on them was also somewhat scattered.

"Luo Chen, aged two hundred thirty-one, originated from Dahe Market in the Jade Cauldron Domain of the Eastern Wilderness, rose to prominence in Tianlan Immortal City of the Jade Cauldron Domain..."

"Possesses five spirit roots, talent extremely poor, with no sect guidance or family support."

"During the Qi Refinement period, he displayed considerable alchemy talent and, among the rogue cultivators, earned the Dao name 'Alchemy Dust'."

Seeing this, the Lingtian City Lord paused slightly.

He had a Dao name even during the Qi Refinement period?

"Interesting!"

He continued flipping through the bamboo slips, from Luo Chen's Qi Refinement period to his Initiate period, and then to his Adept period.

The locations also progressed from the obscure Dahe Market to Tianlan Immortal City, step by step, until the time of the Falling Cloud Sect's expansion war.

The Great Snowy Mountains battle marked the failure of the expansion war.

The Green Parasol Mountain battle opened a new round of all-out war between the human and demon races in the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world after ancient times.

At this point, the intelligence on Luo Chen was interrupted.

The Lingtian City Lord picked up another bamboo slip—one that was relatively older among the many new ones.

"This was personally written by Junior Brother Chongming after he returned from the Central Continent!"

Recognizing the handwriting, the Lingtian City Lord's expression turned slightly serious.

He unrolled the bamboo slip. The content recorded on it was still related to Luo Chen, but unlike the rogue cultivator struggling in the mire before, Luo Chen at this time was already a different person entirely.

Wilderness Wanderer!

Gained the Dao in the Northern Sea, made his name in the Central Continent.

In the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven Grand Competition, he single-handedly turned the tide, pushing Fu Qinglan, whose aptitude was not originally outstanding, to the top spot.

The battle situation within the grotto-heaven was unknown; the only thing worth noting was that Luo Chen was favored by Alchemy Saint Chu Yan!

When the Alchemy Saint ascended, he bestowed a Heavenly Secret Pill!

It was precisely because of this that Chongming noticed this person and went to the trouble of having the Nether Origin Sect collect intelligence related to him.

"Why did Senior Sister Chu favor him?"

The Lingtian City Lord paused in his movements, falling into deep thought.

Some might find it strange that he called Chongming "Junior Brother," Chongming called Alchemy Saint Chu Yan "Uncle," yet he and Chu Yan addressed each other as "Senior Sister" and "Junior Brother."

This seniority was chaotic.

But in fact, it wasn't chaotic. Chongming was a genius of the new generation of the Nether Origin Sect, not from the same era as Alchemy Saint Chu Yan, Ye Lingtian, and others.

Thus, even though he had reached the Sage realm and even represented the Nether Origin Sect on a mission to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, he still respectfully called Chu Yan "Uncle."

And Ye Lingtian continued using the form of address from when they were all in the same sect.

The Lingtian City Lord pondered for a long while. "Could it be because of Luo Chen's uniquely gifted alchemy talent that Senior Sister Chu favored him?"

"That must be it!"

"Luo Chen's natural talent is not good, yet his cultivation has never stagnated. His realm advances by leaps and bounds, forming his core in barely a hundred years—no less than a heavenly spirit root cultivator. Without sect guidance, the only explanation is that he took the shortcut of the alchemy path."

"In his hundred-year journey through the Northern Sea, he leaped from early Adept to Sage! Such progress may involve fortuitous encounters, but it absolutely cannot be separated from the aid of alchemy."

Sure enough!

When Ye Lingtian opened the last bamboo slip, it confirmed his thoughts.

"Star Gate guest elder Luo Chen can casually produce fourth-grade mid-tier cultivation pills; suspected of being able to refine fourth-grade high-tier pills."

"Suspected to be an Alchemy Grandmaster!"

Before he could rejoice at his own speculation, Ye Lingtian took out a jade slip from his storage bag.

"Keep an eye on Luo Chen. This child is connected to the old affairs of Green Parasol Mountain. But do not use force; treat him with courtesy!"

Two simple sentences, yet they contained immense divine sense, instinctively making one want to submit.

Clearly, the one who left this message on the jade slip was a Ascendant-level great power above the Sage realm!

It was precisely because of this jade slip that the Lingtian City Lord had been so warm toward Luo Chen.

And this jade slip had arrived in the Lingtian City Lord's hands even before Luo Chen's return, reaching him first.

Caressing the smooth jade slip, the Lingtian City Lord couldn't help but furrow his brow.

"Since the Ancestor is so concerned about Luo Chen, why not just capture him and bring him back to the Nether Origin Sect for interrogation?"

"Cherishing talent, taking a fancy to his alchemy skills, wanting to nurture the next Alchemy Saint for the Nether Origin Sect?"

"Shouldn't that call for even quicker action?"

"Is it because the Ancestor can't spare the effort, or because he's wary of the identity of the Star Gate guest elder?"

After some thought, the Lingtian City Lord was utterly perplexed.

He organized all the bamboo slips, then suddenly let out a laugh.

"Why bother with all this? Such an interesting character—dealing with him is far better than dealing with these lifeless peers of the Eastern Wilderness!"

...

Unlike the many small forces in Lingtian Pass that had no home to return to, the Luo Tian Sect, though not a Sage-level superior sect, still had its own separate encampment.

It was a piece of land specially arranged for them by Fenghua Palace.

Not large, but not small either.

A hundred li of territory, with mountains and water, and even exotic flowers and rare herbs.

Of course, this arrangement was not free. In that spirit land called "Hundred Li Green Stream," most of what was planted were medicinal herbs. The Luo Tian Sect's task while residing here was to help Fenghua Palace tend to these herbs and deliver them on schedule.

The Luo Tian Sect had this capability—after all, in their early years, they had made their living by refining pills and growing medicine.

On the cloud carriage, Qu Lingjun knelt before Luo Chen, his head pressed to the ground, silent for a long time.

After Luo Chen finished listening to his account of the general situation, he too fell silent.

After a long while, he said softly, "So you're saying that after I left, you abandoned your wife and children, turned your back on the sect, and threw yourself into another's tutelage?"

Each word was like a heart-piercing accusation, driving straight into a man's heart and lungs.

Qu Lingjun's whole body trembled, and he bowed his head heavily.

"It is all true. Lingjun is unfilial, disgracing Luo Tian. I beg Master to grant me death!"

Gu Caiyi, standing nearby, quickly spoke up: "Husband, you must not!"

Luo Chen looked over.

Gu Caiyi sighed. "This matter is not Lingjun's fault. Back then, on the Luo Tian Sect's road of flight, people's hearts were scattered. It happened that Fengyue Fairy of Fenghua Palace passed by with her people, and she proposed to take Lingjun away. Although Lingjun insisted on leaving with Fengyue Fairy at the time, we later learned that he only wanted to find a way out for us."

"Over the years, Lingjun has secretly funded the Luo Tian Sect with large amounts of cultivation resources, especially pills. That my Luo Tian Sect, despite a hundred years of hardship, has produced many talents—Lingjun's contribution is great."

"Moreover, he truly secured this Hundred Li Green Stream spirit land for us, so that we wouldn't have to live under someone else's roof."

Although still under the control of a Sage-level superior sect, unlike in the Five Elements Domain, here in the Fenghua Domain, the Luo Tian Sect was self-reliant, with a certain degree of independence.

Even if they had to flee again in the future, they wouldn't end up in the passive situation of being coerced by gratitude like they had been with the Five Elements Divine Sect.

Faced with Gu Caiyi's plea for leniency, Luo Chen looked at Qu Lingjun again.

"Is what your master's wife said true?"

Qu Lingjun choked up. "It is true. But it is also true that Lingjun disrupted the sect's morale, causing many private criticisms and even some to leave the Luo Tian Sect. Master..."

"Your matter... we'll discuss it later!"

Luo Chen waved his hand, and Qu Lingjun was lifted to his feet by a surge of spiritual power.

Then, Luo Chen rose and walked outside.

"Hundred Li Green Stream, we've arrived."

...

The Luo Tian Sect did not have the ultra-long-distance communication capabilities of the Nether Origin Sect, so Luo Chen's return was still unknown to those who had remained at the sect headquarters.

On this day.

Above Hundred Li Green Stream, a magnificent cloud carriage sped in from afar.

When it reached the sect's territory, its speed immediately slowed.

One by one, low-level cultivators busy in the medicine fields looked up, curiously watching the slowly flying cloud carriage.

Three plain and three ornate, cloud patterns everywhere.

Black, green, and white long sails on one side; purple, gold, and red long sails on the other, mirroring each other.

On the carriage roof, Ruyi Dripping Pearl boards were everywhere, flowing tassels and flying sleeves hung down in strands, and a noble five-colored canopy stood tall.

The spacious carriage was also paved with white jade, gleaming with golden light.

Even the six wheels below were quite extraordinary, with eighteen spokes crisscrossing, all coated with gold dust.

Moreover, as it flew, the shadows of three flood dragons rode clouds and mist, displaying even greater divine might.

"Which great figure's travel carriage is this, so magnificent?"

"Fenghua Palace cultivators love luxury—could it be someone from the superior sect?"

"It could also be from the Five Elements Divine Sect!"

"The fourth day of the Ten Decisions Competition is almost over. If someone comes now, could it be to announce the results?"

When this last speculation appeared, everyone's hearts tightened.

Some with fiercer tempers even took out their magical artifacts, ready for battle.

At that moment, a figure flew out from the cloud carriage, shooting like an arrow toward the only green mountain in Hundred Li Green Stream.

Someone crossed paths with him.

"Yilong, what's happening?"

Zeng Yilong's speed didn't slow at all; he only replied, "Uncle Li, the Supreme Elder has returned!"

The middle-aged cultivator was stunned, his eyes full of disbelief.

But soon, a look of ecstasy appeared on his face. Ignoring the emptiness of his spiritual power, he rushed toward the cloud carriage.

When he reached it, he hesitated slightly.

Fu Jiusheng, who was on the outer side, smiled at him and was about to say something when he heard a gentle laugh.

"Is that Yingzhang?"

A familiar voice!

Li Yingzhang subconsciously swallowed his saliva, then straightened his clothes and boarded the cloud carriage.

"Li Yingzhang, respectfully welcomes the Supreme Elder's return!"

Dong!

Dong!

Dong!

The sky was clear and bright, and the bells chimed melodiously.

Outside a cave dwelling on the Great Green Mountain.

A woman with a flushed face excitedly pounded on the cave door.

"Master, he's back!"

Inside the cave dwelling, a woman wearing a gauze veil suddenly opened her eyes.

Like a shooting star, she rushed to the front of the door.

But just as she was about to open the cave dwelling, her footsteps involuntarily stopped.

"Really?"

"It's definitely true. Zeng Yilong told me himself. He even rang the great bell. Now all the disciples are being gathered to welcome him. Master, you..."

"Mingyue, you go ahead first. I won't go."

"Uh... Master, you are his Dao partner, how can you not go?"

The woman raised her hand—a black gauze covered her arm—then subconsciously touched her veil-shrouded cheek, shaking her head dejectedly.

"It's not convenient for me. You go first. You are now the sect master of the Luo Tian Sect; you must be present. Don't be late."

Yao Mingyue was puzzled, but hearing the increasingly urgent bell, she eventually left.

After she left, the woman let out a faint sigh and murmured:

"It's good that you're back. It's good that you're back."

Under the sound of the bell, figures gathered like flowing streams, converging from all directions of Hundred Li Green Stream.

Yao Mingyue put on her most solemn clothes and stood at the front of the crowd.

Under the bewildered gazes of three hundred disciples, she bowed in welcome.

"Yao Mingyue, respectfully welcomes the Supreme Elder's return!"

At these words, the crowd erupted in commotion.

At that moment, an overwhelming pressure slowly spread out.

Vast but not sharp, grand yet gentle as a spring breeze.

It was like the might of heaven, yet more like basking in a warm wind.

It felt as if a benevolent elder was watching over them.

Everyone realized what was happening. They craned their necks, widened their eyes, and stared fixedly at the cloud carriage radiating brilliant light.

One by one, cultivators walked out from within, lining up in two rows, standing respectfully at attention.

Among them were the highly respected Fu Jiusheng, Min Longyu, who had grown gray hair for the sect, and Tian Kuizi Chu Kui, who had achieved numerous battle merits.

The rising star Yang Ping stood among them.

Li Yingzhang, who had recently gained great fame, had also boarded the cloud carriage at some point and stood respectfully to one side.

Then, they saw a tall, straight figure, like an unsheathed sharp sword, slowly walk out.

No one noticed Gu Caiyi and Qu Lingjun standing on either side of him; everyone's mind was fixed on that pair of eyes that seemed to rotate like the sun, moon, and stars.

Portraits of Luo Chen had always been enshrined in the Ancestral Hall.

Every person who entered the sect remembered that face.

Even though a hundred years had passed, it was still etched in their hearts.

It was him!

It really was him!

Yao Mingyue spoke again.

"All cultivators of Luo Tian, respectfully welcome the Supreme Elder's return!"

This time, everyone responded in unison. Even though there were only a hundred voices, they rolled like thunder.

Luo Chen watched this scene and nodded slightly.

With just one sentence—"I have returned"—all voices ceased.

In his heart, however, he was quite emotional. The Luo Tian Sect, which at its peak had thousands of disciples, now only had these three hundred followers left.

His departure had indeed been ill-timed!

Suddenly.

His gaze sharpened.

"Where is Huiniang?"
