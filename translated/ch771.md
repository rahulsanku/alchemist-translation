# Chapter 771: Wasteland Blood Nurtures the Tree, Nine Heavens Harvest Qi

"Honored Sage, I have found a method to cultivate the Demon-Heart Tree!"

Upon hearing this, Luo Chen was momentarily stunned. He subconsciously asked, "What did you say?"

"Demon..."

"Wait!"

Luo Chen interrupted Sang Jinghe's words, glancing at Fu Hongzhang, who was currently exchanging resources with Heavenjade. The man's face showed a look of bewilderment.

Seeing Luo Chen look over, he quickly lowered his head.

"Come out with me!"

Luo Chen rose unhurriedly, paced outside the house, and then released a soundproof barrier.

Sang Jinghe, knowing he had spoken carelessly in front of others, said fearfully, "Please forgive me, Honored Sage. I was momentarily careless..."

"It's fine."

Luo Chen waved his hand. He could understand the other's indiscretion—mere excessive excitement.

Moreover, although the Demon-Heart Tree was precious, it was a newly hybridized variety. Throughout the history of the Mountains and Seas Realm, there were no corresponding records. Even if a Fu family cultivator heard its name, it didn't matter.

What was unexpected was that Sang Jinghe had actually researched and found a method to cultivate this tree.

The Demon-Heart Tree had been created by the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining using the True Scale Tree as its foundation, infused with the hearts of a fifth-rank ancient demon and a fifth-rank ancient devil.

It resembled a spirit tree but was not one; it seemed like a demon but was not.

From the moment of successful hybridization, it had been fifth-rank.

However, because its creation defied heaven's harmony, it was extremely difficult to awaken its spirit. According to what the Jinsui had said, unless it underwent a lightning tribulation and endured heaven and earth's wrath, it could not become sentient and bear fruit.

As for the fruit it would bear, based on the Jinsui's speculation, it might be greatly beneficial for body refinement!

Unfortunately, after Luo Chen obtained it, let alone letting it mature and bear fruit for body refinement, he had no idea how to even continue cultivating it.

He never expected that Sang Jinghe, merely a Golden Core cultivator, had actually thought of a method.

"Tell me in detail."

Sang Jinghe's spirits lifted, and he immediately began speaking confidently.

"According to what you said, Honored Sage, this tree was hybridized from demon and devil hearts. Therefore, its cultivation method requires demonic qi and devilish qi. Demonic qi is easy to find—just have a great demon exhale and inhale mana day and night. But devilish qi is extremely rare in the Mountains and Seas Realm. I've heard that only the direct lineage of the Primordial Demon Sect's Primordial Demon bloodline can learn it."

"Therefore, orthodox cultivation methods won't work."

"I tried several traditional cultivation methods, but the effects..."

He spoke very meticulously, from the initial research experiments to later theoretical deductions, almost narrating decades of research results in detail.

Luo Chen did not urge him but listened very attentively.

He had some knowledge of the dao of spirit plant cultivation, but he still couldn't compare to technical specialists like Sang Jinghe who were dedicated to this path.

Moreover, the Demon-Heart Tree was related to his body refinement path, so he couldn't afford to be careless.

Ever since reaching the mid-stage of the fourth-rank Archaic realm, his body refinement cultivation had almost stagnated.

Conventional methods like the *Myriad Path Convergence*, and even true fire tempering the body, had become useless.

Regarding external auxiliary methods, although he had several body refinement pill formulas, some left by the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining, they were demon race body refinement pill formulas and not suitable for him.

Additionally, after forming his Nascent Soul, he had invested too much energy into the qi refinement path, causing his once-strongest trump card to be forced into stagnation.

Even though he had made considerable progress in physical techniques, it was ultimately developing inward rather than climbing upward.

If the Demon-Heart Tree could bear fruit, perhaps he could use it to advance another step!

"On Demon Sea's Longyuan Island, I saw you use the blood pool method to reconstruct Lord Black King's body, Honored Sage. This inspired me, and I imitated this method to construct a demon blood spirit pool."

"But this method ultimately had flaws. The Heart Tree was completely uninterested in the blood of those low-grade demon beasts."

"Later, I changed my approach. Although the Heart Tree incorporates the hearts of ancient demons and ancient devils, its foundation is still the True Scale Tree. According to ancient records, the growth of the True Scale Tree requires large amounts of powdered fish-dragon scales. So I thought of imitating ancient methods."

"But in the end, I found this method also wouldn't work."

"After arriving in Central Continent, I helped Fu family cultivators deal with spirit plant problems. To thank me, they let me borrow many texts. Among them, I discovered many records about wasteland beasts!"

"Yes, wasteland beasts!"

Mentioning this, Sang Jinghe's eyes lit up.

"Central Continent cultivators' understanding and utilization of wasteland beasts is unmatched in the entire Mountains and Seas Realm. Among these records, there were mentions of using wasteland beast materials to cultivate spirit plants!"

"In their view, wasteland beasts are a very unique type of lifeform. They don't pursue transformation, don't seek sentience, merely relying on instinct to inhale and exhale heaven and earth's spiritual qi, yet they can cultivate step by step to fourth-rank, fifth-rank, and even ascend! This is truly a creature of heaven and earth's creation, worthy of being called this realm's favored child."

"I thought at the time, if we use wasteland beast blood as the main ingredient, supplemented by your blood pool method, Honored Sage, and add large amounts of fish-dragon type scales and armor, could we replace demonic and devilish qi to stimulate the Demon-Heart Tree's growth?"

Luo Chen looked at him until he finished speaking in one breath.

Then...

"Is this your method?"

Sang Jinghe was stunned.

Under Luo Chen's gaze, his face slowly flushed red.

He mumbled with his lips, "Although it's still just a conjecture, in my view, Jinghe, this is already the most feasible path."

"Mm, you've worked hard. It sounds like it has some feasibility. I'll find time to try it."

Luo Chen patted Sang Jinghe's shoulder as a reward and also gave him a few bottles of pills he had refined in his spare time.

"Go back and pack up first. We'll be leaving soon."

"Mm, mm."

Sang Jinghe received the pills and, because his idea had been affirmed by Luo Chen, left very happily.

After he left, Luo Chen couldn't help shaking his head.

The other's conjecture sounded quite good, but to actually implement it would be too difficult.

First of all, where would one find wasteland beast blood!

Although the Mountains and Seas Realm was vast, after the ancient great war, wasteland beasts had gradually become extinct. Either they were killed like the Mirage Dragon, or they hid deep in the wilderness.

Occasionally appearing ones were mostly of low rank.

For example, the Sea-Containing Golden Toad he had seen once in the Northern Sea was merely third-rank.

Given the Demon-Heart Tree's rank, the blood of a third-rank wasteland beast probably wouldn't work at all.

Rustle, rustle...

An undisguised crawling sound came from beside his ear.

Luo Chen looked down. A small black dragon had crawled to his side.

He reached out, and the small dragon coiled around his right arm.

"Master, you're back!"

Black King greeted enthusiastically but received no response.

Instead, a thoughtful gaze was fixed on him.

For some reason, that gaze made him feel a chill run through his body.

"Master, what are you going to do? Little Black hasn't caused any trouble this whole year!"

Luo Chen withdrew his gaze and hummed in acknowledgment.

But in his mind, he was thinking: the uniqueness of wasteland beasts lies in their production of source power, and their essence blood also evolves into source blood. If they can cultivate step by step to fifth-rank, they will instinctively master the corresponding origin power.

That's why, in the Mountains and Seas Realm, wasteland beasts are called heaven and earth's favored children.

Black King had now switched to the wasteland beast path and was fourth-rank.

Could his blood be used to nourish the Demon-Heart Tree?

Additionally, he himself cultivated both dharma and body, his physique almost indistinguishable from a wasteland beast. Could his own essence blood also work?

But this thought passed in an instant.

Large amounts of essence blood would have too great an impact on his and Black King's strength. It wasn't worth it.

If he could settle down peacefully in the future, he would consider this matter then!

Letting Black King coil around his arm hidden under his sleeve, Luo Chen returned inside.

The resource exchange that had taken considerable time was almost complete.

Heavenjade reported a number that made Luo Chen's eyelids twitch, but he could only grit his teeth and take out that batch of spirit stones.

Thus, the savings on his person were reduced to only thirty million spirit stones.

Although this matched the wealth of an ordinary Nascent Soul cultivator, he was no longer as glorious as when he had carried three hundred million spirit stones.

Finally.

Luo Chen intended to set off immediately.

But Fu Hongzhang stopped him and handed over another item.

"What is this?"

Luo Chen took the beast-skin book and looked down.

*Netherpassage Collection*

"This is a relatively obscure chess manual that my young lady specially ordered to be sent. It records many special chess game fragment patterns. The young lady said that if you can thoroughly read this book, your chess skill will surely improve greatly."

So it was just a chess manual!

Luo Chen casually put it into his storage ring. "Thank Qinglan for her kindness. If there's an opportunity in the future, I will definitely play a satisfying game with her."

After speaking, he led his people onto the carriage and flew out of Wangwu Mountain.

Unlike the lively arrival, the departure was somewhat quiet.

But Luo Chen's heart was rarely burning with more passion.

He was going back to the Eastern Wilderness!

...

Tianxing Harbor.

This was Luo Chen's first destination.

It was the largest coastal port in eastern Central Continent.

To go to the Eastern Wilderness, one had to depart from this harbor.

This time, Luo Chen did not choose to take a teleportation formation. Instead, he planned to obtain a travel permit at Tianxing Harbor and then pilot his own flying boat back to the Eastern Wilderness.

The reason for this was, firstly, that Black King still had many dragon earthworm remnants on him. With his current wealth, he couldn't afford the large amount of spirit stones required for the teleportation formation.

Moreover, between the Eastern Wilderness and Central Continent, there weren't as many natural barriers and demon beasts as on the road to the Northern Sea. The only obstacle was probably the Dark Water separating the two continents.

But as long as he obtained a travel permit jointly issued by the Eastern Wilderness and Central Continent, he could smoothly pass through the Dark Water checkpoint.

However, not long after leaving Wangwu Mountain, the Sixfold Imperial Flying Cloud Carriage stopped.

He had run into an old friend.

Looking at the weather-beaten Zhou Yunshen, Luo Chen was somewhat surprised.

"Didn't you need at least four or five more years to reach Central Continent?"

The former ancestor of the Coral Sea Zhou clan now looked completely relaxed.

He replied, "We encountered Dao Sect experts clearing natural barrier checkpoints on the road. When they returned to Central Continent, they conveniently brought us along."

"So that's how it is!" Luo Chen nodded.

Through intelligence from Elder Seven Lamps before leaving, he had learned much about the current situation in Central Continent.

The road to the Northern Sea had been completely opened!

Next, the Dao Sect's elite would take teleportation formations to the Northern Sea first, while the main army of cultivators would cross the sea in batches to reach the Northern Sea cultivation world.

The great war was about to begin!

Going to the Eastern Wilderness first now was actually somewhat disadvantageous.

If he had followed the Heavenly Origin army to the Northern Sea first, he could have seized the initiative and made contributions.

But Luo Chen had no hesitation. He still decided to return first.

Conquering an entire continent was never that simple. Especially now that the demon forces in the Northern Sea were gradually unifying, and the elite of the Demon Alliance had experienced the battle against the Primordial Demon Sect—they were no pushovers.

This war might drag on for a long time.

He would still have plenty of opportunities later!

Zhou Yunshen was very curious. "Are you also going to Heaven-Earth Peak?"

Luo Chen shook his head. "I just came back from there. Now I'm going to the Eastern Wilderness."

"Eastern Wilderness?" Zhou Yunshen was confused. Calculating the time, Luo Chen had only been in Central Continent for two or three years. Was he leaving already?

Luo Chen smiled and said, "This journey is long and distant. I don't know when we'll meet again. Fellow Daoist Zhou, until we meet again."

He didn't talk long. He returned to his carriage, and then it passed by Zhou Yunshen's flying boat.

Zhou Yunshen returned to his flying boat, still somewhat bewildered.

He had just arrived, and now he was leaving?

A Northern Sea Nascent Soul who had come with him asked about it, and Zhou Yunshen didn't begrudge telling him the situation.

The man casually said, "Maybe he couldn't make it in Central Continent!"

"How could that be? The Wilderness Wanderer is young and accomplished. Where would he not have a place to stand?" Zhou Yunshen didn't believe it.

The other person chuckled. "Not necessarily. Central Continent is based on family inheritance and is the most exclusionary. The recruitment conditions for guest elders at Star Gate on Heaven-Earth Peak are extremely strict regarding age. It's very possible he really couldn't make it here."

Zhou Yunshen was speechless.

He had to admit, what the other said did have some truth.

But not long after, when they took a large city teleportation formation to Heaven-Earth Peak and heard about Luo Chen's deeds in the Grotto-Heaven Grand Competition, they couldn't help looking at each other in dismay.

Someone who could be valued by Star Gate elders and sought after by various major families—how could he not be able to make it!

On the contrary, they were the ones who felt somewhat awkward.

"Return to the Northern Sea?"

"Yes, my sect's main army has already advanced into the Northern Sea. You all came from over there, so you must understand the Northern Sea situation better. Of course, it's up to you. My sect doesn't absolutely need you. The Northern Sea also has the Penglai Immortal Sect as a guide. But if you want your juniors to have a better life in Central Continent in the future, it's best to do something for the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. Consider it!"

Facing the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect expert's gentle persuasion, Zhou Yunshen and the others hesitated, uncertain.

...

In the bustling and lively Tianxing Harbor.

A magnificent carriage slowly rose, hovered in the air for a moment, and then headed east.

On the Flying Cloud Carriage.

Luo Chen held a jade slip and studied it with considerable interest for a while.

This was the travel permit he had obtained after presenting his Star Gate guest elder identity token.

It was said that when approaching the Eastern Wilderness continent, there would be a sea area covered by Dark Water, blocking attacks from sea demons.

That sea area also had experts from the Nether Origin Sect stationed there year-round.

Only by presenting this travel permit could one set foot on Eastern Wilderness territory.

"Such strictness—is it to keep outsiders out, or to keep insiders in?"

Luo Chen thought of the matter of Alchemy Saint Chu Yan leaving the Nether Origin Sect to join the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, and couldn't help speculating endlessly.

But soon, he lost interest in the travel permit and took out another jade slip.

This journey was long. Even with Heavenjade and Sang Jinghe piloting the flying boat day and night, it would take a year or two.

This time couldn't be wasted.

Earlier, Fu Chaosheng had given him a set of formation books and a sword canon.

Studying formations was too troublesome and required extensive accumulated knowledge.

But the sword canon, Luo Chen was very interested in!

The Yuantu sword was powerful, but it required too much mana. He wasn't very proficient in the sword dao, and when controlling the flying sword, he often wasted too much mana, and its speed was average.

This sword was very difficult to control!

Its previous owner, Blood Nightmare Moluo, couldn't fully utilize the sword's sharpness. The two times Luo Chen had activated it, either he had prepared for a long time and accumulated momentum, or he had secretly ambushed successfully. Neither could be considered perfect utilization.

If he could make up for this shortcoming, he might be able to maximize the sword's power.

His divine sense probed into the jade slip, and immediately sword intent pierced his soul, myriad scenes bursting forth.

*Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon*

It was said to be unrestricted by sword pellets or flying swords—both could be cultivated—and it was even as high as fifth-rank!

[Those who cultivate sword pellets: hold the sword heart, comprehend sword intent, until reaching the supreme sword dao...]

[Those who control flying swords: ascend the nine heavens, harvest spirit essence, nurture boundless sword qi, achieve the thousand-skies panorama...]

Studying the sword canon again, Luo Chen immersed himself in it.

A long time later, he put down the jade slip and looked up.

His gaze seemed to pierce through the canopy of heaven, seeing the nine heavens above.

"Go there to harvest spirit essence?"

The term "spirit essence" was somewhat obscure.

But in reality, Luo Chen was not unfamiliar with it.

That which blooms but bears no fruit is called essence; that in which the purest essence is concentrated is also called essence.

Simply put, the essence of a certain substance can be called essence.

For example, the blossoms of plants and trees!

And spirit essence generally refers to the essence of spiritual substances.

For instance, the thunder essence Luo Chen had obtained before was the concentrated essence of thunder-attribute substances.

Another example was the Green Duckweed Gentle Wind he had seen, which was also called wind essence.

And the frequently seen Profound Origin Heavy Water, Ren Water, Heavenly Unity True Water, etc., were all water-attribute essences.

In short, spirit essences were all rare items. When cultivators wanted to refine magical treasures or cultivate powerful secret arts, they often sought high-grade spirit essences.

According to the *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon*, if he, as a sword cultivator who controlled flying swords, wanted to cultivate this sword canon, he needed to ascend the nine heavens, harvest the corresponding nine heavens spirit essence, and use it to cultivate sword qi.

"The threshold is a bit high!"

Luo Chen murmured to himself, but he was not discouraged. Instead, he was eager to try.

Since he possessed a peerless sword weapon, how could he use it clumsily?

Now that he even had the specific method for wielding it, he should cultivate it properly.

Compared to the illusory and intangible Great Five Elements Sword Formation, the Yuantu blood sword already in his hand was undoubtedly more reliable!
