# Chapter 793: Practicing Swordsmanship, Crafting Talismans, Guiding Juniors, and Refining Another Nascent Soul Pill

Three months later!

The quietest area of Daqing Mountain was now a scene of bustling activity.

"Qi Yi, add the Ice Soul Marrow!"

"Yes!"

Qi Yi leaped up, holding a jade bottle, its mouth facing downward toward the grey great cauldron.

As he activated his spiritual power, wisps of white smoke billowed forth.

Within that white smoke, the Ice Soul Marrow fell into the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Qi Yi silently calculated the amount in his heart…

"Enough!"

Luo Chen reminded him from the side. Qi Yi also felt the amount was just right and quickly withdrew his hand.

"Adding Cold Marrow as an ingredient will drastically alter the temperature inside the alchemy cauldron. If we want the medicinal power to continue rising and fusing, we must increase the flame. Step back for now, lest my true flame injure you."

After Luo Chen's instruction, the Withering-Bloom True Flame suddenly erupted.

Qi Yi cautiously retreated to the rear, watching Luo Chen's every move with eager anticipation.

Clearly, Luo Chen also had the intention of guiding him, occasionally speaking to explain certain details.

"When refining high-grade pills, the requirements in every aspect are extremely stringent. The alchemy vessel, the strength of the alchemy flame… even the cultivator's personal mana is closely related. Back when I refined the Nascent Soul Pill, my realm was only late Adept, so it took an extremely long time. I had to slowly simmer it out with painstaking effort. Now that my realm is higher, I can save a considerable amount of time."

"With your alchemy skill, your foundation is actually quite solid. The main reasons you can't stably refine third-grade pills are two-fold. First, you haven't refined a strange fire. Second, your spiritual power is insufficient. The former is easy to solve—just use precious spiritual wood as a substitute, supplemented by flame-control and fire-gathering formations. For the latter, you need to pay attention and temper your own spiritual power."

"Don't think tempering spiritual power is unimportant. It also concerns your future Adept Dao!"

"In recent years, I have been tempering my mana and gained some insights. I've written a book called the *Spirit-Tempering Art*. After we finish refining the Nascent Soul Pill, you can take it and study it."

Qi Yi listened attentively, both admiring Luo Chen for having refined a fourth-grade realm-breaking pill back when he was only a late Adept, and also identifying some of his own shortcomings through Luo Chen's words.

When he heard that Luo Chen was going to pass on a secret art for tempering spiritual power, he was overjoyed.

After first thanking Luo Chen, Qi Yi asked curiously, "We failed the previous few times. Can we succeed this time?"

Luo Chen kept his attention fixed on the situation inside the Primordial Unity Cauldron, his face flushed red by the surging Withering-Bloom True Flame.

Facing Qi Yi's question, his expression was calm.

"Regardless of success or failure, we must give our all every single time!"

"If one day you think your alchemy skill is good enough and you grow careless when refining pills, then the burnt, fishy stench of failure will be your mockery."

"Only by being fully focused every time will the final fragrance of the pill not betray all the sweat and effort."

"Watch closely! Next comes the most important stage—adding the three main ingredients. I won't explain further. How much you can learn depends on your comprehension."

With that, Luo Chen tossed out a fourth-grade Purple Monkey Flower.

His ten fingers moved like fluttering butterflies as he rapidly began forming hand seals.

As his mana surged, streams of alchemy techniques poured from his hands, continuously decomposing and melting the Purple Monkey Flower suspended in the air.

A patch of purple mist appeared, dreamlike and illusory, gradually sinking into the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Qi Yi held his breath, not daring to exhale loudly as he watched this scene, as if trying to etch every single step Luo Chen took deep into his memory.

Several days later.

*Dong!*

Luo Chen landed gracefully, a hint of satisfaction in his eyes.

Qi Yi, who had held on for several days, now had bloodshot eyes. "Supreme Elder, have we succeeded?"

Looking up at the grey cauldron, Luo Chen smiled slightly.

"This batch of medicinal herbs given by the Divine Talisman Sect has been stored for too long; the medicinal power is somewhat sparse. Next, we need to simmer it with the alchemy flame for a few more days to concentrate the power before we can open the cauldron. But based on my judgment, this time, nine times out of ten, we will form the pill!"

Upon hearing this, Qi Yi couldn't help but let out a long sigh of relief.

Having endured for so long, his appearance had become quite haggard.

Refining high-grade pills, even just learning and assisting Luo Chen, was far too difficult.

However, through this process, he had indeed learned a great deal. Once he digested it all later, his alchemy skill would surely see a qualitative improvement!

"You're tired too. Go and rest for a couple of days!"

Qi Yi subconsciously nodded, but then had another thought.

"I'll stay and watch the fire!"

Luo Chen chuckled.

"No need. Someone else will take care of watching the fire."

As he spoke, crisp footsteps sounded from a corner.

Qi Yi looked up. The red dog puppet, which he had previously only thought was used for odd jobs and transporting herbs, silently walked over to the bottom of the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Light shone brightly from its body, and then each of its hands spat out a wisp of Withering-Bloom True Flame.

Supplemented by the pre-prepared firewood, the flame beneath the Primordial Unity Cauldron was maintained at a certain level.

Qi Yi was astonished. This puppet could even help watch the fire?

"With Red Dog here, there's no need to worry about changes in the alchemy flame," Luo Chen said with a smile, his eyes full of confidence.

The Red Dog puppet contained his strongest first divided soul. What Luo Chen knew, Red Dog also knew. Combined with the Withering-Bloom True Flame, this place was almost equivalent to him personally watching the fire. Nothing would go wrong.

Then, Luo Chen took out a green-skinned gourd from his storage ring and handed it to Qi Yi.

"This is Soul-Calming Powder, a third-grade soul-nourishing spiritual medicine. Taken when a cultivator is extremely fatigued, it can not only restore the divine soul but also slightly strengthen your soul. It was originally a core pill of the Medicine King Sect. Elder Fu gave me the recipe, and I hastily refined some. Take it, consume it, and rest!"

Qi Yi curiously took the gourd. He had no idea when Luo Chen had refined this spiritual medicine.

Could it have been during the stage of preparing the herbs?

But Soul-Calming Powder was a famous third-grade wonder drug. Had the Supreme Elder's alchemy skill already reached such a level that he could casually refine third-grade pills?

"Thank you for the gift, Supreme Elder!"

After respectfully expressing his gratitude, Qi Yi left the alchemy chamber.

After he left, Luo Chen watched the Primordial Unity Cauldron for a while longer. Once he confirmed that the Red Dog puppet could handle the situation, he left the chamber and returned to his own bedroom.

He first glanced at his storage ring.

The raw materials Elder Fu had given him still had a full thirteen portions remaining!

However, deducting the other Five Elements Lotus Platform he had secretly kept for himself, there were actually only three portions of raw materials that could be used to refine the Nascent Soul Pill.

"I'll keep them. It won't hurt to refine them in my spare time."

Luo Chen wasn't particularly concerned about the Nascent Soul Pill. He already had two in stock!

They were the high-quality ones he hadn't used up back on Longyuan Island in the Northern Sea, which he had nurtured to the fourth-grade mid-tier level using the Dragon-Raising Basin!

If this attempt succeeded again, based on past experience, he could probably produce around ten more pills.

Elder Fu's minimum requirement was two pills, with no maximum stated—everyone understood the unspoken rule.

At that time, Luo Chen could be generous and give the other party three to five pills, leaving plenty for himself.

These "conscientiously" skimmed pills would all become the future foundation of the Luo Tian Sect!

Fourth-grade Nascent Soul Pill!

He also had a batch of dozens of third-grade Condensation Pills from the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's inheritance, refined on Longyuan Island, which were helpful for forming an Adept core!

Plus the Imperial Nectar he already possessed, which was helpful for Initiation!

And the complete set of Spirit-Root Pill recipes he had comprehended back in the Pill Realm, which could give mortals without spirit roots the qualification for thirty years of Qi Refinement cultivation!

In this way, Luo Chen had collected all the major realm-breaking pills for the human race, from Qi Refinement to Sage.

If paired with corresponding cultivation pills for each stage, the overall path of cultivation would be even smoother.

He had also more or less collected all the auxiliary pill recipes for the path of soul refinement.

Netherpassage Pills, Soul-Calming Powder, Great Soul Pills!

If he could obtain the Medicine King Sect's first-grade Spirit-Calming Incense Pill recipe, or the method for refining the Blazing Spirit Incense created by the former Fragrant Master of the Hundred Flowers Palace, it would be the icing on the cake.

"With these sets of systematic pill recipes, supplemented by spirit plant cultivation methods, the large amount of spiritual soil in the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion, and the numerous dragon earthworms on Black King, I could completely establish a Dao of Alchemy sect comparable to the Medicine King Sect."

"For body cultivation, Wang Yuan and I are here, along with outstanding juniors like Zeng Yilong. We can fully revive the glory of the ancient body cultivators."

"If Qin Yuanjiang is clever enough to master the Dao of Puppets, combined with my soul-refining pills and soul-refining techniques, he might even have a chance to replicate the path of the Falling Cloud Sect."

"I have also gained some insights into the Dao of Artifact Refining. Before his death, Duan Feng left a decent foundation in artifact refining for the Luo Tian Sect. If…"

One thought after another emerged, each painting a beautiful future.

Luo Chen even felt like not returning to the Central Continent to be a guest elder of the Star Gate.

Why be subservient to others when he could dominate a region and leave behind his own legacy?

But soon, Luo Chen suppressed these thoughts.

To achieve the ideas above, he needed too many things.

A resource-rich, vast domain with at least a fourth-grade or higher spirit vein!

A large number of excellent disciples and followers!

And a sufficiently stable situation!

These things were all unattainable for Luo Chen at present.

Instead of fantasizing, it was better to focus on improving his own strength step by step.

Fame and reputation were soft power.

Realm, mana, divine abilities, and techniques—these were the hard power that would allow him to roam freely and stand firm in this chaotic world.

After a short rest, Luo Chen began his sword path cultivation.

He had personally collected seven strands of Crimson Cloud Qi from the azure sky, and Elder Fu had given him another three.

If he could refine them all, he could step into the first stage of the *Thousand Clouds Myriad Transformations Sword Scripture*, giving him another method to deal with enemies.

He could not afford to slack off!

Luo Chen took out a strand of Crimson Cloud Qi, opened his mouth, and inhaled it, storing it in his chest and abdomen.

The Yuantu Sword lay horizontally across his knees. As Luo Chen breathed in a rhythmic pattern, it began to resonate with the Crimson Cloud Qi.

Amidst this resonance, his entire being became increasingly sharp and dazzling, making it hard for others to look directly at him.

…

"Uncle-Master, this is the third batch of talisman skins."

In a large hall, Zeng Yilong led a group of people in hurriedly.

He first respectfully bowed to the tall black ape, then signaled the disciples behind him to put down the talisman skins.

A dull thud sounded, accompanied by a fishy smell.

The Black Ape Luo Chen walked over to the pile of beast skins, which was as tall as a man, and carefully inspected them.

One of the disciples behind him introduced, "This batch of talisman skins was all made by Divine Talisman Sect cultivators from various demon beast hides. The grade is around second-grade, with varying attributes. As per your request, we tried to select fire-attributed ones, but quite a few of other attributes were mixed in."

The Black Ape Luo Chen nodded.

This was one of the only drawbacks of using beast skins to make talisman skins—the attributes were limited by the original beast's body.

But for him, it wasn't a problem.

His main body could convert mana attributes and was extremely proficient in various Five Elements spells. He could definitely put all these talisman skins to use.

This Black Ape puppet clone of his only needed to delve into the study of talisman crafting techniques and master the theory. When the time came, his main body could handle the actual operation.

After a thorough inspection, Luo Chen straightened up with satisfaction.

"You've worked hard making these trips. Go to Yang Pingdu to collect your compensation."

The Merit Hall of the Luo Tian Sect had long since changed hands.

It was no longer Sima Wenjie of the old days, but Yang Pingdu, who had joined the Luo Tian Sect midway.

He wasn't a strong cultivator trained by the Luo Tian Sect itself; he had brought his skills when he joined. Without any backing within the sect, he tried his best to be impartial.

Even if he offended everyone, it could be seen as offending no one.

He was the most suitable person to lead the Merit Hall.

Just as Zeng Yilong was about to lead the people away, Luo Chen suddenly called out to the cultivator who had just introduced the talisman skins for him.

"You, stay!"

Everyone was a little surprised, but didn't dare ask any questions.

After everyone else had left, Luo Chen looked at the young man before him, whose features seemed somewhat familiar.

"You're Qu Jiu, right!"

Qu Jiu was caught off guard. He hadn't expected the Supreme Elder to know him.

Strictly speaking, the Supreme Elder was not only their sect's founding ancestor but also his grandmaster.

Could it be that he was being kept behind because of his father's matter?

Unexpectedly, Luo Chen started talking about something else.

"You're not interested in the Dao of Alchemy, but you knew all about these talisman skins just now. It seems you have a good understanding of the Dao of Talismans!"

Qu Jiu was startled, then said a little awkwardly, "Because of my mother, I was quite curious about the Dao of Talismans, so I learned a bit."

"Oh? Mi Li?"

"Yes, before my mother passed away, she left me a safety talisman. Later, during a battle with demon beasts, that safety talisman saved my life. When I asked the senior members of the sect, I found out it was a third-grade defensive talisman she had commissioned from the Divine Talisman Sect's strong cultivator, Night Elder, through her connections."

Qu Jiu spoke of the past with a look of remembrance, an unconscious smile spreading across his face.

Then, he returned to the main topic.

"From then on, I became interested in talismans. It's a convenient and powerful art, and the key is that it can protect loved ones even when I'm not around."

The Black Ape Luo Chen stiffly nodded and said slowly, "The Dao of Talismans is indeed like that. Although it can't directly improve a cultivator's strength, it can help us in many ways."

This understanding was also what Luo Chen had realized during his recent days of studying talismans.

Talismans weren't just for battle; they could be used in many aspects.

Most importantly, climbing the realms of cultivation was extremely difficult. Once a cultivator encountered a battle and expended a large amount of mana, recovery would be very slow.

If the mana not usually needed could be infused into talismans to replace consumption during battle, it could indirectly help the cultivator's cultivation.

Of course, being overly obsessed with talisman crafting was not advisable.

Looking at the handsome Qu Jiu, he felt a strange closeness to him for some reason.

After a moment of thought, the Black Ape Luo Chen said in a low voice, "From now on, you will follow me and learn talisman crafting!"

Qu Jiu was stunned, not quite reacting.

"Supreme Elder, I…"

"Just call me Grandmaster."

A hard palm patted Qu Jiu's shoulder, and the Black Ape Luo Chen said so.

His current talisman crafting level, according to the system's evaluation, had just barely stepped into the third grade. It wasn't particularly impressive.

But it was more than enough to teach Qu Jiu.

Now that he had a large amount of talisman skins to accumulate proficiency, it was easy to imagine how fast Luo Chen's talisman crafting skills would improve in the coming days.

If Qu Jiu followed him to learn and received his guidance, it would surely be better than him trying to figure things out on his own.

"Let me first test your foundation in talisman crafting, and then…"

Just as the Black Ape Luo Chen was saying this, his voice suddenly caught.

He looked up, gazing into the distance towards where his main body was.

"Success, as expected?"

"Then Elder Fu should be coming soon!"

Once Elder Fu arrived, the news of successfully refining a fourth-grade pill would surely not be hidden from others.

This would confirm that what Luo Chen said at his Sage Establishment Ceremony was not an exaggeration.

He truly possessed the grandmaster-level skill of refining a hundred pills, mastering everything!

Then, those strong cultivators who had been holding back but had a strong need for pills would surely be unable to sit still.

"Main body's peaceful days are about to end."

"I hope the new visitors can bring some different pill recipes!"
