# Chapter 1036: Perfect Sword Qi, Perfect Foundation Establishment

Cold, silent.

Such was the vast blue void.

Only the occasional flocks of sky insects that flew past in groups, their screeching adding a splash of color to this monotonous place.

But in truth, high up in this firmament, a black-clad figure sat cross-legged, surrounded by a storm of countless crimson sword qi that danced and whirled without rest.

If one looked closely, they would notice that those sword qi faintly displayed three colors: pale red, deep purple, and dark crimson.

The three types of sword qi had originally been clearly distinct, but under the black-robed Daoist's continuous tempering with his mana, they were gradually reduced to a single color.

Crimson red!

Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes, gazing at the crimson sword qi around him that resembled the evening glow, a satisfied look appearing in his eyes.

This *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon* was truly troublesome to cultivate. Not only was the required sky qi expensive and scarce, but the refinement process was extremely difficult.

At the entry stage, it took over half a year to refine a single strand.

He had spent a full fifty or sixty years just to raise his proficiency by one level.

To reach the level of "Thousand Skies Converging," the difficulty was unimaginable.

But as Luo Chen gradually mastered this sword canon, his refinement speed unexpectedly underwent a leap forward.

This time, leaving the Eastern Wilderness and coming to the "Central Continent," he had spent a total of fourteen years, finally elevating it from Mastery to the Perfect level.

And upon reaching the Perfect level, Luo Chen discovered new changes.

"The Crimson Sky Qi, Purple Sky Qi, and Crimson Cloud Qi, which were not originally harmonious, have perfectly fused together, becoming Crimson Sky Sword Qi that best suits my Fire Spirit Body. The increase in power from the change in overall quantity was within expectations, but the power of a single strand of sword qi has directly doubled!"

"This is the change brought about by purity!"

"Not only that, but the refinement speed has been further improved. From now on, refining a strand of sword qi will only take about twenty days."

"That is to say, if I want to raise my proficiency from Perfect to Master-level, provided I have enough sky qi, it will only take about eleven years."

After a simple calculation, Luo Chen's spirits lifted greatly.

He didn't know if others who cultivated this sword canon experienced the same situation, but for him, it was a tremendous advantage.

Ordinary Crimson Sky Sword Qi was already considered a formidable combat method at the Sage stage.

Now, with Perfect-level Crimson Sky Sword Qi, its power had doubled. In terms of sheer lethality, it was almost no weaker than the Grand Five Elements Sword Formation that Luo Chen had painstakingly refined earlier.

What about Master-level Crimson Sky Sword Qi?

And what about after cultivating this fifth-grade sword canon to the Great Perfection level?

Abandoning everything, alone in this desolate and lifeless place, tempering his techniques while enduring unspeakable loneliness and solitude, Luo Chen hoped the final result would not disappoint him.

As for whether the final result would meet Luo Chen's expectations, he didn't know yet. But during these ten-odd years of austere cultivation, Luo Chen had gained some additional benefits.

The first was that the mana instability brought about by his suddenly surging realm had gradually settled down during the continuous tempering of sword qi.

He could now control the mana within his body as easily as moving his fingers, without worrying about the hidden dangers of an unstable foundation.

The second…

Luo Chen glanced at his Purple Mansion, and correspondingly, the Sage within his Purple Mansion also opened its eyes.

Clad in Mystic Dust Armor, it held an ancient blood-colored sword in both hands.

This sword was far more spirited than before.

If one looked closely, they would see that the blood-red color on it was flowing like blood itself.

Especially the blood groove on the sword's body, which was as crystalline and translucent as the blood vessels of a human body.

This pseudo-spirit treasure, after successively absorbing the blood of two Ascendant-level experts and countless living beings, and after Luo Chen's unrestrained sacrifice and refinement, was finally about to take that final step and transform into a true spirit treasure!

Luo Chen slowly withdrew his divine sense.

There was no rush yet.

Based on his knowledge of artifact refinement, the Yuantu Sword's ascension to a spirit treasure was very different from that of ordinary true artifacts.

The sword itself possessed a "sword spirit," and another was about to be born. Which would be the master and which the servant, or whether they would merge into one, was still unknown.

This required Luo Chen's constant attention, nurturing and refining it bit by bit, ensuring that all his efforts wouldn't be wasted at the final moment.

Finally, Luo Chen had one more gain.

His mana slowly spread out, flowing gently through the blue void.

This heaven and earth seemed to respond, surging from all directions with vast amounts of origin qi, surrounding Luo Chen as if, whenever he wished, he could command this origin qi.

Not only that, if Luo Chen could condense his primordial spirit, he could replenish it at any time, quickly converting it into mana.

This scene was a hallmark ability of Ascendant-level experts!

Spirit fused with the vast heaven and earth, controlling boundless origin qi!

That was why Ascendant-level experts usually refrained from acting, but when they did, the slightest movement could shatter mountains and split the earth, and the most severe could burn seas and sink continents.

Moreover, they were not restricted by the environment.

Places like the blue void, commonly considered spirit-dead zones, could still sustain them because there was an endless supply of origin qi even in the void.

Suddenly!

Just as Luo Chen attracted a massive amount of origin qi, the long-dormant Primordial Unity Cauldron abruptly trembled.

A series of violent beast roars erupted from within.

Luo Chen's brow twitched.

"You sensed that I've advanced again, so you're getting anxious?"

The Primordial Unity Cauldron trembled wildly, and a phantom shadow floated out from within, expanding as if trying to break free from the cauldron.

That incomplete Kui Niu ghost spirit had indeed sensed Luo Chen's change.

It could no longer sit idly by.

If it continued, it would be like a frog being slowly boiled in warm water; sooner or later, Luo Chen would completely refine its self-will.

So, it began to resist.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron was Luo Chen's lifebound treasure, always connected to his mind. Luo Chen had long seen through the Kui Niu ghost spirit's secret accumulation of power, intending to break free from suppression.

Now that the other party wanted to "fight to the death," Luo Chen was not afraid.

Better to strike while the iron is hot; he might as well finish it all in one go!

That Million-Soul Baleful Banner had been without a main soul for a long time; it could hardly wait.

But just as Luo Chen was about to make his move, a mysterious and profound sensation came from within his divine soul.

"Foundation Establishment?"

Luo Chen blurted out.

A gleam of sharp light shone in his eyes.

According to his perception, that clone of his had actually broken through to the Initiate stage.

Calculating the time, he should only be twelve years old!

"Entering the Dao at six, establishing a foundation at twelve… tsk tsk, with such a cultivation speed, it's no wonder I spent so much effort on him!"

Though surprised, it was more of a pleasant surprise.

Luo Chen was not afraid that the clone's cultivation would be too fast and cause any accidents. True geniuses should surpass the imagination of ordinary people.

After all, that body had absorbed the potential of a twin sister, been nurtured from a young age by an extreme-grade water lotus seed, and fused with a spring that carried the power of fundamental laws.

This configuration had already surpassed Luo Chen's Fire Spirit Body, and was even more excellent than the Shi Ju he had seen back then.

No matter what cultivation miracle occurred with the clone, it would not be an exaggeration!

"Keep up the good work!"

"One day, you will meet me and merge into one."

Murmuring, a strange smile appeared on Luo Chen's face.

Opposite him, the Kui Niu phantom on the Primordial Unity Cauldron had grown to its limit, roaring up at the sky in the vast blue void.

Luo Chen's smile faded. He pointed a finger at his brow and chanted a Sanskrit sound.

"Svāhā!"

Golden light burst forth from between his brows, then enveloped his entire body.

A primordial spirit suddenly opened wide, wrapping Luo Chen's physical body within it, and reached out two large hands toward the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

*Boom!*

Golden flames erupted!

*Boom!*

Azure fire spread!

At this moment, Luo Chen's primordial spirit held Nirvana in its left hand and Withering-Bloom in its right, launching the final tempering assault on the Kui Niu ghost spirit!

…

At the same time.

On another continent, within the vast White Egret Marsh.

Inside a waterside pavilion, a delicate-looking youth opened his eyes in a daze.

"Advancing to the Initiate stage… was it this simple?"

Before him lay an unrolled, yellowed scroll. It was the core method for breaking through to Foundation Establishment of the Zhou clan. Other disciples could only be taught orally, and even if there were books, they were mostly copies. But what he had before him was the original.

This was a manifestation of the privilege granted to him by the Zhou clan's ancestor.

Apart from the Foundation Establishment method, there was also a colorful coral, a specially prepared spiritual liquid, and three Foundation Establishment Pills of excellent quality.

These were all the thorough preparations Zhou Fuyou had made for breaking through to the Initiate stage.

However, none of them were used!

He had only subconsciously tried, and then succeeded, so smoothly that it was almost unbelievable.

If outsiders knew this, they would surely be astonished.

Even with a heavenly spirit root, it shouldn't be this smooth.

Even if Luo Chen knew about this, he might feel a hint of envy. After all, when Luo Chen had broken through to the Initiate stage back then, it was incredibly arduous, a life-and-death struggle, and he had only succeeded by a stroke of luck.

But Zhou Fuyou had crossed that step so effortlessly.

With a thought!

A strange power emerged from the youth's mind, spreading outward from the Drinking Green Waterside Pavilion as its center.

One li, two li, three li… By the time it reached three li, it had already surpassed the normal range for a newly advanced Initiate-stage cultivator, but Zhou Fuyou still did not stop this delicate perception, continuing to spread outward.

Four li, five li… It wasn't until he reached a range of ten li that he began to feel a hint of fatigue.

But within the fatigue was more surprise.

He knew that this perception was a sign of the divine soul manifesting after a cultivator advanced to the Initiate stage, called "spiritual sense."

An ordinary newly advanced Initiate-stage cultivator could only cover one or two li, but he had expanded it to a full ten li!

This showed how deep Zhou Fuyou's divine soul foundation was.

"Is it because of my constitution?"

"But divine soul foundation has little to do with constitution; it's more about what one is born with, plus natural growth over time. Why does my divine soul foundation not match what is recorded in the scriptures?"

Zhou Fuyou was deeply puzzled.

In these six years of cultivation, he had not received much guidance from his elders. He had mostly relied on his own knowledge, exploring step by step.

But the achievements he had made contradicted many things in the scriptures.

Zhou Fuyou didn't know where the problem lay, but his tired divine soul urged him to withdraw his spiritual sense.

During the withdrawal process, he also perceived the scenes of many living beings.

The neighbors nearby were keeping their distance from the Drinking Green Waterside Pavilion, as if afraid to disturb him.

Granny Lin was directing a few sturdy maids to clean the courtyard. As if remembering something, she hurried toward the kitchen, but her advanced age had already shown signs of frailty.

In a quiet training room, his younger sister Zhou Qingyu was closing her eyes, trying her best to sense the spiritual energy, but there was no fluctuation of spiritual power on her body. After taking those three pills, was it still so difficult for his sister to cultivate?

When all his spiritual sense was withdrawn, the youth felt the changes within his body.

The boundless spiritual energy, like clouds, was gathering.

Drops of extremely pure spiritual liquid fell from the clouds like a light drizzle.

Later, it became a torrential downpour, falling endlessly.

This scene once again overturned the records in the cultivation scriptures.

Ordinary people who established their foundation extracted spiritual liquid drop by drop, but he was like a heavy rain, inexhaustible.

Such abundant spiritual power even violated the characteristic of the *Dew Cicada Art*, which was known for having little mana.

In Zhou Fuyou's view, even cultivators who practiced the Zhou clan's ancestral technique, or the three high-grade water-attributed techniques of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, probably couldn't compare to him.

Amidst his astonishment, Zhou Fuyou's hands did not stop moving.

He formed a hand seal, beginning to mobilize the vast spiritual liquid, using the method of the *Dew Cicada Art* to reconstruct his dantian.

No one knew how much time passed.

Zhou Fuyou looked inward at himself, his mind shaken.

The qi sea constructed from his dantian was not a puddle, not a pond, not even a great river or ocean. It was more like a bottomless abyss.

No matter how torrential the rain of spiritual liquid, this qi sea could still contain it.

"Marvelous!"

"Cultivation is truly marvelous!"

Murmuring, Zhou Fuyou opened his eyes.

Dressed in red coral and white robes, he carefully put away all the Foundation Establishment materials. These could all be used by his sister later.

He himself took out a large number of spell technique scriptures that Mo Wanting had given him from his storage pouch.

A long time ago, Zhou Fuyou had discovered that he possessed a terrifying talent for spell techniques.

He could understand them at a glance, and master them after one study!

After a little grasp, he could even achieve the level of instantaneous casting!

Such talent, combined with his abundant mana, would be a waste not to learn spells.

Healing Art, Water Bullet Art, Water Arrow Art, Water Prison Art…

These first-grade water-attributed spells, Zhou Fuyou skimmed over them without a second thought. He had already learned them privately, mastering them all without exception.

Water Avoidance Art, Walking on Waves, Water Dragon Whirlwind, Glazed Heavenly Canopy…

Just by reading the introductions, Zhou Fuyou knew the functions of these spells.

Escape techniques, large-scale area attacks, single-point attacks, defense.

Learn them all, he could!

But just this was still not enough for Zhou Fuyou.

His spiritual sense spread out, beginning to scan several other spells stored in jade slips.

*Mist Shrouding the Jade Tower*, *Nine-Bend Heavenly River*, *Spirit Communication and Form Retention*…

"Third-grade?"

"And there are even fourth-grade water-attributed spells!"

"That Dean Yuheng, Mo Wanting, is really generous!"

Zhou Fuyou felt a stirring in his heart. With his talent, could he learn these third-grade water-attributed spells?

Hesitating, he subconsciously opened a third-grade spell that was marked as not to be cultivated before the Adept stage.

Its name—*Mirror Flower Water Moon*!

In the depths of his being, he felt that this spell would be very suitable for him.

(End of Chapter)
