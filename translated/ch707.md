# Chapter 707: Build the Blood Pool, Break Through Stage Nine, Myriad Paths End, Dao Body Hidden

What kind of condition was Black King in now?

After several months of Luo Chen's careful examination, he already had some understanding.

His flesh body was extremely severely injured, almost to the stage where it could no longer be reshaped.

His demon core was shattered, and the demon power he had bitterly cultivated for several hundred years had either dispersed outward or merged into his broken bones.

His soul was near extinguished, like a tiny lamp flame, swaying on the verge of collapse.

In anyone's eyes, injuries like these were no different from a state near death.

In fact, it was normal for such wounds to exist.

The explosive might of that heaven-reaching spirit treasure [TERM?], even after being reduced layer upon layer by the Pill Palace grand formation, was not something Black King could withstand head-on even as a mere aftershock.

If not for his solid foundation, his soaking in the Spirit-Molding Sacred Spring [TERM?], and Luo Chen protecting him somewhat after completing his physical nirvana, Black King would likely have fallen inside the Pill Palace on the spot.

After being heavily injured, Black King instinctively used the Cliff Serpent method and fell into a hibernating state.

His soul, which could extinguish at any time, was thus preserved.

But that was all.

If he wanted to recover, relying on himself alone was absolutely impossible.

Under such circumstances, even after Luo Chen used all kinds of medical arts, he had only repaired a tiny bit of Black King's bodily injuries.

One could even say that in Heavenjade's eyes, even if Luo Chen saved Black King, the other party would be no different from a cripple.

For no other reason!

His demon core was already shattered, and there was no longer any possibility of continuing on the great road.

But if one said not to save him, how could Heavenjade bear it? The two demons had entered Luo Chen's service one after the other. Though their usual relationship was not harmonious, they were still friends after all.

Luo Chen had many considerations on his side as well.

Black King was a decent helper to him, and previously he had invested a large number of resources into cultivating Black King.

To abandon him now would truly be a pity.

Especially the loyalty Black King had shown at that final moment...

"First build the blood pool!"

As Luo Chen murmured, he had already made his decision.

If it had been the past him, he would have had no method to save Black King. The overall injuries were far too severe.

But after experiencing the Pill Palace inheritance, he had searched the classics throughout these three months and truly found a method.

The Pill Palace inheritance, aside from the Heaven-Refining Cauldron [TERM?], still had seventy-two volumes of pill books, thirty-six pill formulas, and also three wondrous sealed pill formulas: Stabilizing Spirit [TERM?], Void Surveying [TERM?], and Dao Union [TERM?].

Among these, the seventy-two volumes of pill books and pill formulas had no restrictions at all.

After Luo Chen browsed them quickly, he gained a vague concept.

These pill books and pill formulas covered the three great races of human, demon, and devil!

There were naturally subdivisions among them, but more of the differences lay in grade.

Taking the twelve pill formulas related to the human race as an example, they ranged from the Qi Refinement stage to the Sage stage, with each realm divided into three types. The Spirit Root Pill [TERM?] formula from the Pill Realm [TERM?] trial was also among them, only far more complete.

And the three pill formulas for each realm had different uses.

Realm-breaking pills, cultivation pills, and the remaining type varied in function, covering assistance, healing, life preservation, barrier breaking, mind clearing, spirit stabilizing, and many other aspects, even poisonous pills.

The human race was like this, and the twelve pill formulas of the demon race were the same.

Among them, one fourth-tier blood pill method had the effects of reshaping the flesh body, healing wounds, and changing blood.

Only, if he wanted to refine this pill, the first thing he had to do was construct a blood pool!

As for how to construct it, Luo Chen already had a rough idea in mind.

...

Three days later.

Sang Jinghe [TERM?] looked with a face full of pain at the enormous pit, ten zhang long and wide.

This had been one of the few fourth-tier spirit soil regions on Dragon Abyss Island [TERM?]!

He had even been preparing to cut it apart and plan it into spirit fields, specially for planting rare medicinal grasses.

But now, Daoist Azure Yang had actually used true fire to refine it alive, supplemented by other ores, and built it into a glass-like pool.

What was this pool meant to be used for?

Luo Chen quietly looked at the pool beneath his feet, then suddenly spoke:

"Jinghe [TERM?], were you not troubled by those omnipresent Dragon Earthworms [TERM?] interfering with your transplanting of Dragon Saliva Grass [TERM?]?"

Sang Jinghe [TERM?] froze, then subconsciously nodded.

Luo Chen smiled faintly. "Then this is perfect. Today, I will solve this trouble for you."

Sang Jinghe [TERM?] was surprised. Had Senior Luo already thought of how to arrange a shielding formation?

However, in the next moment, the other man's action made his heart chill.

He saw Luo Chen no longer suppress his aura, instead releasing it all.

An aura like a primordial savage beast spread out in an instant, rushing in every direction.

In the blink of an eye, it had already taken on the posture of enveloping nearly half the island.

Not only that, Luo Chen stomped one foot!

Boom!

Surges of enormous force transmitted layer upon layer into the depths of the earth vein.

As if in an earthquake, Dragon Abyss Island [TERM?] began trembling.

Under the coordination of this aura and vibration, the countless Dragon Earthworms [TERM?] hidden on Dragon Abyss Island [TERM?] felt as if the end of the world had arrived. They could no longer hide within the soil and rock layers, and crawled out from the earth one after another.

Rustle, rustle...

Rustle, rustle...

Everywhere the eye could see, gray long worms wandered over the earth in panic and confusion.

The sight made one physically uncomfortable, nearly sick!

Yet Luo Chen's expression was incomparably calm.

He raised both hands!

A blue-gold point of light slowly appeared behind him, flames swaying and branches stretching.

In an instant, an azure fire tree manifested, floating in midair.

Dreamlike and illusory, shining with splendid brilliance.

This azure fire tree kept its might restrained. No high heat could be seen, nor was there any fierce, violent fire.

Yet the instant it appeared, countless Dragon Earthworms [TERM?] all became restless and boiling, subconsciously fleeing toward the outside of Dragon Abyss Island [TERM?].

That was fear born from the soul's instinct!

However, it was already too late.

With a thought from Luo Chen, the fire tree flew into the high sky as if sentient. Then its branches stretched, and clusters of sparks flew out like willow catkins scattering through the air.

Of the countless Dragon Earthworms [TERM?], all that were touched stood frozen on the spot.

Seeing this, Luo Chen revealed a satisfied smile.

Without him noticing, the Withering-Bloom True Flame had already grown powerful to this degree.

Strange fire taking form, giving birth to true spirit!

If Luo Chen did not deliberately suppress it, the spirituality of this flame could in an instant become like those source-less fire spirits in Blazing Purgatory [TERM?], filled with autonomous will.

But right now, Luo Chen did not have that intention.

He himself had not advanced to the Sage realm. If his own true flame gained a free will, it would instead easily become the guest overwhelming the host.

It could only be left waiting for now!

Waiting until he broke through to the Sage realm!

At that time, once he achieved the Sage realm, the restrictions on this ball of true flame in his hand could be loosened somewhat. Combined with its own terrifying foundation, it would certainly become a great killing move in Luo Chen's hands!

But for the moment, the might of the Withering-Bloom True Flame was already enough to threaten the Sage stage, instantly suppressing these hundreds of thousands of low-tier Dragon Earthworms [TERM?].

"Heavenjade, Jinghe [TERM?], go bring back all the Dragon Earthworms [TERM?] suppressed by my true flame."

"As you command!"

"Yes!"

The two of them flew out without hesitation, roaming across Dragon Abyss Island [TERM?] and bringing back one living Dragon Earthworm [TERM?] after another.

They had only been suppressed by sparks of the Withering-Bloom True Flame, but they had not died.

From this, one could also see how exquisite Luo Chen's fire-control technique had become, reaching the level of release and recall as easily as thought, fine down to the smallest detail!

At first, they still did not understand Luo Chen's plan.

But when they saw Luo Chen kill one Dragon Earthworm [TERM?] after another and pour that turbid blood into the glass pool, they vaguely understood something.

Hundreds of thousands of Dragon Earthworms [TERM?], even if placed there for people to kill, would exhaust one half to death.

But under the combined effort of three people, in no more than several days, they were all exterminated.

A stinking blood pool appeared before the three.

"This is truly too wasteful. If these Dragon Earthworms [TERM?] were used properly, they would be enough to make a sect prosper." Sang Jinghe [TERM?] was incomparably regretful. He had not expected Luo Chen's method of solving the Dragon Earthworm [TERM?] problem to be not a formation, but solving the source of the problem itself.

Heavenjade, however, had no particular thoughts. "Dragon Abyss Island [TERM?] is very large. The low-tier Dragon Earthworms [TERM?] hidden within it number in the tens of millions. These are only a few hundred thousand."

Luo Chen said nothing, tossing all kinds of assorted medicinal materials inside.

Afterward, in front of the two of them, he also threw Black King's sleeping true body inside.

The turbid blood bubbled and gurgled, completely submerging Black King.

Heavenjade could not help asking, "Master, can Black King recover just by soaking like this?"

"It is not enough!"

Luo Chen shook his head.

"Black King's injuries are too severe. These things alone are still insufficient to cure him. Next, when you have spare time, capture more second-tier Dragon Earthworms [TERM?], draw their blood, and place it in the blood pool. In addition, after your injuries recover, sweep the surroundings. If you encounter third-tier Demon Kings, kill them and bring them back as well."

Heavenjade did not refuse, only worrying that her own strength was insufficient.

She was now at mid-stage Adept realm. Relying on that upper-grade magic treasure, the Nine Winds Plantain Fan, she dared fight a Great Demon King, but if she wanted to kill it and bring back the corpse, that would be somewhat difficult.

Luo Chen had already anticipated this.

"Take this fire with you!"

Luo Chen pointed, and three azure fire seeds detached from the fire tree, slowly sinking into Heavenjade's brow.

"When you encounter someone you cannot defeat by strength, activate this fire."

"All right. You may go for now!"

After speaking, Luo Chen dismissed those at his side.

After the two left, Luo Chen sat cross-legged before the glass blood pool, his expression resolute.

He lightly bit the tip of his tongue.

A drop of essence blood from the tongue slowly flew out.

Drip!

The crimson blood drop fell into the blood pool.

In an instant, it was like boiling oil entering water. A series of crackling explosions rang from the quiet blood pool.

Black King's body appeared and disappeared within those explosions.

When this rejection reaction gradually faded, the original turbid color formed by large amounts of Dragon Earthworm [TERM?] blood mixing together became incomparably bright red.

"For a mere spirit pet, you are quite willing to expend your vitality."

At some unknown time, a person had quietly stood beside Luo Chen.

This person's face was pale, seeming dead yet alive, and he bore several traces of Han Zhan's original appearance.

"Why have you come?"

Han Zhan chuckled softly. "You made such a large disturbance earlier. I had to come."

Luo Chen slowly stood and looked toward "Han Zhan."

"How are you adapting to this body?"

Han Zhan stretched his muscles and bones, then smacked his lips.

"It is still early. Feiyunzi's realm was different from mine, and his spirit root attributes are also different. Especially after many years of cultivation, the direction of the meridians in his body and the number of apertures opened are vastly different as well. Right now, my Nascent Soul is merely occupying the Purple Mansion [TERM?], and it is still far from perfect possession. By my estimate, it will take at least ten years or so before I can perfectly adapt to this body and display the strength of my peak."

As he spoke, he waved a hand.

"Let us not speak of me first. You made such a great move, building this Four-Spirit Blood Pool [TERM?]. Can it truly save Black King?"

Luo Chen said in a low voice, "We take one step and see one step. First solve the injuries to his flesh body, then solve the other problems. As for the divine soul aspect, I may have to trouble Senior when the time comes."

"I have no objections." Han Zhan tried to stroke the beard that did not exist, froze for a moment, then lowered his hand behind his back. "As for you, next you should be preparing to break through Adept Stage Nine, then assault the Sage realm, correct?"

"Precisely."

"Since that is so, before this you squeezed out heart blood, and now you expend vitality again, squeezing out a drop of tongue-tip blood. Are you truly not afraid of shaking your foundation?"

Regarding this, Luo Chen smiled faintly.

"Senior need not worry about that. Since Luo dares do this, naturally I have the confidence that it will not delay my cultivation."

Luo Chen truly did have this confidence!

A person's essence blood originated from a mixture of mana and fleshly blood, and was also mixed with dense vitality within.

Ordinary people absolutely did not dare consume essence blood recklessly.

But Luo Chen had no such worry.

After he devoured Tai Sui's true body, the vitality inside it could be called terrifying beyond compare. Even after Luo Chen used it to complete his physical nirvana, he still had not completely digested it.

Now, wasting a mere few drops of essence blood truly did not affect him too much aside from slightly damaging his mana.

Moreover, this Four-Spirit Blood Pool [TERM?] genuinely required powerful blood as its main material.

Looking across the resources in his hands, only the essence blood produced by Luo Chen's archaic-beast-comparable flesh body could be called powerful.

"Fine. As long as you know what you are doing." Han Zhan shrugged somewhat unnaturally and walked down the mountain.

Looking at his back, Luo Chen frowned.

The other man had first stopped abruptly while trying to stroke his beard, and then shrugged unnaturally. It could be seen that his control over the body was not very smooth, but was possessing a flesh body this difficult?

One had to know that Feiyunzi's Nascent Soul had already been crushed by him.

It was only an empty flesh body. With Han Zhan's ability, he still could not control it naturally after several months?

Luo Chen thought for a while, then finally activated the formation, concealing the blood pool, and returned alone to the thatched hut in the abyss.

Sensing the pure spirit qi purified by the formation, Luo Chen revealed a satisfied smile.

This was where the main vein of the fourth-tier spirit vein was located!

The spirit qi within was incomparably abundant.

At least by Luo Chen's experience, it was an utterly standard lower-grade fourth-tier spirit vein.

Logically speaking, Adept cultivators absolutely did not dare cultivate in such a dense environment.

It was like humans needing water. A suitable amount of water quenched thirst, and abundant water resources let people swim freely, but if under the crushing pressure of the deep sea, let alone swimming, they could not even open their mouths.

But all these rules were broken by Luo Chen.

A flesh body comparable to an archaic beast!

A fire spirit body that, after reshaping, could freely contain fire-attribute spirit qi!

Added to a Grandmaster-level Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture!

With these three foundations, Luo Chen completely dared, while in the Adept realm, to sit for a long time on a fourth-tier spirit vein and cultivate.

One could even say that because of the support of these many conditions, Luo Chen's cultivation speed had also reached its fastest in history!

According to his estimate, it was at least more than three times faster than before he left Peng Lake!

Such an earth-shaking change was hard for outsiders to imagine.

But compared to everything Luo Chen had paid to reach this cultivation speed, it was only natural.

Whether an archaic beast flesh body, or a fire spirit body, or even the Grandmaster-level Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture, who in this world could achieve even one or two of them?

Let alone a fourth-tier spirit vein, which was absolutely not something an Adept cultivator could have the fortune to monopolize.

In Han Zhan's view, since Luo Chen now possessed such excellent internal and external environments, he naturally ought to advance bravely and focus on cultivation in order to try assaulting the Sage realm.

But Luo Chen did not think so!

"Inside the Fallen Demon Land [TERM?], I soaked in that... Spirit-Molding Sacred Spring [TERM?], and my realm skyrocketed in one breath from Adept Stage Six to Adept Stage Eight. At first glance, my foundation remains solid and shows no feeling of emptiness. But in my own heart, I feel as if I am sitting in a tower in the air, up and down, uneasy and unsettled."

Luo Chen had already learned the name of that wondrous spring water from the pill books left by Devil Lord Heaven-Refining.

As for its many wondrous effects, he was naturally amazed.

If Devil Lord Heaven-Refining had not left behind no pill formula for refining this spirit spring, Luo Chen would even have wanted, if he had the chance in the future, to reproduce this spring water.

That was a digression.

Returning to the main point.

The explosive rise in realm left Luo Chen feeling unsteady.

This had nothing to do with the realm itself, but was a sense of disparity in his state of mind.

Therefore, even with such an excellent cultivation environment, Luo Chen still restrained his desire to advance bravely.

"Let me temper myself a little more!"

Luo Chen murmured, circulated his lifebound cultivation method, and entered deep cultivation.

...

The tempering he spoke of was absolutely not as simple as merely sitting in meditation.

One point within it was refining the purity of his mana!

The second Origin Core [TERM?] had shattered.

But Luo Chen had long since grown used to living with double mana.

Because of his five-spirit-root physique, his own mana had already been several times that of others at the same realm. With the support of two Golden Cores, the gap had been over tenfold!

This had also been one of the sources of Luo Chen's confidence in the past, letting him casually release large-area destructive spells, kill enemies above his realm, and even challenge Sage Sages.

But now, without the second Origin Core [TERM?], Luo Chen only felt at a loss.

But people, after losing something, eventually had to get used to it.

Since he could not think of methods in terms of quantity, then he would make an issue of quality!

In earlier years, for the sake of cultivating two cores together, his mana looked purer than that of ordinary Adept cultivators, but in truth it had not reached his personal extreme.

Now, he would temper and refine it bit by bit, first purifying it to the utmost possible state!

A red Golden Core hovered in his qi sea.

Below, the Withering-Bloom Fire Tree [TERM?] stretched its branches vigorously, slowly scorching the Golden Core.

Occasionally, tiny traces of "impurities" were expelled. Those were mana of other attributes.

Under this scorching, the Golden Core became more and more crimson, like a vermilion fruit!

One could even say that the five-colored Golden Core in essence was gradually being concealed.

...

Half a year later.

Within the abyss, a powerful aura flashed and vanished.

Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes.

In his eyes, there was both surprise and helplessness.

"Adept Stage Nine..."

He had wanted to properly polish his foundation, but the excellent internal and external cultivation environment had, in the end, allowed him to advance to Adept Stage Nine without realizing it.

This was not what he wished, but now that the matter had arrived, he could only accept it.

It could not be that his realm had risen and he still put on a dead man's face, could it?

Luo Chen sighed and summoned the attribute panel.

[Lifespan: 186/900]

[Spirit Roots: Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, Earth]

[Constitution: Fire Spirit Body (Five-Spirit Dao Body Hidden)]

[Realm: Adept Stage Nine 1/100, Archaic Tier Four 35/100]

[Cultivation Methods: Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture Grandmaster 650/1000, Myriad Path Convergence Great Completion, Myriad Beast Scripture [TERM?] Grandmaster 510/1000]

On the rippling attribute panel, entries appeared and disappeared one by one.

Luo Chen did not look at the other attributes, only focusing on the first five major entries.

The explosive increase in lifespan was within expectations.

Not only had he broken through three realms in succession, his flesh body had also, after nirvana, once crossed into the middle-period level of Archaic Tier Four.

Gaining an additional hundred years of lifespan was perfectly normal.

Luo Chen even had a feeling that if he completely absorbed the power of Tai Sui within his body, his lifespan limit would surge again!

After one glance, Luo Chen paid it no further attention.

As far as the present was concerned, he no longer had lifespan pressure. More than nine hundred years old was already long-lived. Some Sage Sages who had suffered serious injuries had only this much lifespan. Luo Chen was only at the Adept stage, so what was there to rush about?

Luo Chen's attention mainly focused on that new entry that had appeared.

[Constitution: Fire Spirit Body (Five-Spirit Dao Body Hidden)]

He could understand the Fire Spirit Body. It was the constitution transformed after nirvana to adapt to the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture.

But what was the meaning of that "(Five-Spirit Dao Body Hidden)"?

"Could it be that my Five-Spirit Dao Body has not disappeared? It is merely concealed by the Fire Spirit Body, to the point that it cannot manifest?"

Luo Chen was quite puzzled.

He tried circulating the Yi Wood Medicine King Scripture [TERM?] he had cultivated in his early years, but after only one small circulation, he felt his mana grow sluggish and his meridians faintly ache.

Clearly, this was not a manifestation the Five-Spirit Dao Body should have.

According to records in ancient texts, the Five-Spirit Dao Body could cultivate all arts together, so how could it have this feeling of sluggishness?

But on the panel, it was indeed displayed this way.

After painstakingly thinking for a long time, and experimenting for half a day, he ultimately obtained no result.

Unwilling though Luo Chen was, he finally gave up.

His gaze moved downward, sweeping past the changes in realm, and Luo Chen began paying attention to the changes in his cultivation methods.

First was the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture!

From first entering Grandmaster level in Blazing Purgatory [TERM?] to the current progress bar of 650, it had surged by a full one hundred and fifty proficiency points!

The changes within...

"They should come from that physical nirvana!"

One transformation brought not only changes to the body itself, but also transformation in the cultivation method.

Luo Chen could feel that when he now circulated his lifebound cultivation method, his efficiency in absorbing heaven and earth spirit qi was far greater than before.

Not only that, when outputting mana and casting spells, he was also one or two fractions faster than before.

Do not underestimate this small progress.

One had to know that most of Luo Chen's spells had already been cultivated to Great Completion.

Some had gone even further. On the foundation of Great Completion, Luo Chen had continuously optimized and studied them until they had already reached a state where nothing could be added.

On this foundation, they could still improve. How could Luo Chen not be surprised?

"It seems this current Fire Spirit Body is even more suited to cultivating the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture, and subsequent proficiency increases should also speed up greatly."

"Perhaps I have a chance to cultivate it to Great Completion before the Sage realm!"

Clearly, after the surge in lifespan, the realm increase, and even the fact that the Five-Spirit Dao Body still existed, this was another piece of good news.

Luo Chen smiled, then inexplicably sighed.

"Sigh..."

His gaze stopped on Myriad Path Convergence.

This self-created cultivation method, which had once gathered the strengths of many schools, had ultimately reached its limit.

He had felt this very early on.

His body cultivation progress had grown slower and slower, to the point that he continuously sought external methods and even exchanged many ancient body cultivation arts from the Myriad Immortals Assembly's Demon Hunting Office.

Relying on these patches and repairs, he had finally cultivated to Archaic Tier Four.

But it stopped there.

After advancing to Archaic Tier Four at Peng Lake, he had very clearly sensed that his body cultivation had reached its limit!

This limit came from Myriad Path Convergence, and also from himself!

If one traced it to the root, it came from the body cultivation method Heavenly Roc Transformation!

In earlier years, Luo Chen and Wang Yuan had bought the Heavenly Roc Transformation from Dahe Market's black market.

That was a beast hide, along with the unexpected joy of the Imperial Moonlight Nectar formula.

But at that time, the Heavenly Roc Transformation itself was an incomplete third-tier cultivation method. To cultivate it, one needed to contain the essence blood of roc-type demon beasts within the body.

Wang Yuan did not take that path, only absorbing some of its strengths to supplement himself.

Using this as a blueprint, Luo Chen, inside the cave abode of the Ascendant mighty one White Night, borrowed the system to complete it and, in a moment of crisis, created Myriad Path Convergence.

Over the years, Luo Chen, broad in learning and knowledge, had pushed this body cultivation method bit by bit to the fourth-tier level.

Then, it had reached its limit.

The Heavenly Roc Transformation's foundation was too thin, and it was incomplete to begin with.

The roc-bird essence blood Luo Chen had absorbed into his body back then had also come only from a second-tier Blazing Fire Cloud Peng [TERM?].

With these two factors stacked together, that they could aid him in cultivating to Archaic Tier Four was already incomparably fortunate.

Without Tai Sui's true body, plus that wisp of Nirvana Sacred Flame's assistance, Luo Chen would have had absolutely no possibility of possessing middle-period Archaic Tier Four cultivation.

While sighing, Luo Chen gradually organized his emotions.

"For now, let it be this way!"

"The body cultivation path has an unclear future and many branches. It can serve as martial strength assurance, but it is difficult to place hopes of longevity on it."

"Giving it up is naturally impossible, but in the future I must seek higher-grade arts, either switching cultivation or using them as reference. Creating another method myself is not impossible either."

"But right now, the urgent matter remains cultivation in qi refinement."

Since he had already reached Adept Stage Nine, he had no choice but to consider Nascent Soul formation.

However, before that, Luo Chen had another small matter to handle.

That was to take inventory of the gains from the Fallen Demon Land [TERM?] journey.

He could not have gone through life and death once, yet not know in his heart what he had gained and what he had lost.

With a thought, he raised a hand.

One streak of flowing light after another floated in midair within the wooden hut.

Looking at those treasures, Luo Chen revealed a knowing smile.

"This trip was not wasted after all."
