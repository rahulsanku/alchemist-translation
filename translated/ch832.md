# Chapter 832: Thunder Tribulation Birth, Dustbound Immortal Mirror

The better the quality of a magic treasure, the longer it required to be nurtured and refined.

Such was the case with the Mystic Dust Armor, and likewise with the Nether Serpent Whip.

Only the Primordial Unity Cauldron, because it had incorporated a drop of Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's blood, had emerged early, lacking the fully mature nurturing process.

This was an advantage, but the disadvantages were naturally quite obvious.

Luo Chen had subsequently spent a full hundred years in the Northern Sea just to completely refine the impurities contained within it.

Every function had to be developed on his own, bit by bit—quite troublesome.

The Heart-Inquiring Mirror was different. It was meant to be refined and then used immediately, so naturally he could not be impatient. Thus, after Luo Chen had finished crafting the embryo, he had nurtured it for a full two more years.

Now, everything was ready!

Sitting cross-legged before the Primordial Unity Cauldron, Luo Chen rhythmically sent out streams of mana, preparing to retrieve the treasure.

The Withering-Bloom True Flame gradually extinguished, and the treasure light within the cauldron grew increasingly blazing.

After persistently applying Jade Emperor Clarity Eye Fluid for three years, Luo Chen's eyes had gradually developed a certain divine peculiarity.

It deviated somewhat from the eye evolution direction he most desired in his heart, but the new eyes were ultimately not bad either.

Building upon the original ability to see through reality and illusion, they had been continuously optimized, gradually gaining a "phantom-breaking" attribute.

This was precisely why the Panther-Leopard Demon Emperor, who excelled at the art of splitting and transformation, had been so constrained and unable to fully leverage his greatest advantage.

Seeing through reality and illusion, not deceived by transformations!

It was perfectly suited for the current situation.

At this moment, the treasure light within the cauldron blazed intensely, giving rise to all sorts of strange and varied illusions.

A white eagle soared through the sky, hunting leisurely amidst a vast horde of illusion beasts.

A cultivator wielded a precious treasure, constantly pressing forward to kill, with dangers emerging one after another.

A man stood atop a cauldron, holding a sword, forming hand seals and Daoist gestures, his might pressing down upon all living beings.

Finally, amidst the vast misty clouds, one could see a huge, blurry creature inhaling and exhaling the Great Desolation, evolving sun, moon, and stars.

Among these many strange phenomena, if one were to sink into even a single one, they might miss the optimal time to retrieve the treasure.

But Luo Chen's eyes blazed brightly, completely unmoved.

Instead, a feeling of sudden clarity arose in his heart.

"These illusions must be the past-life memories of the white eagle, the owner of this illusion core. That man standing on the cauldron holding a sword—isn't that me?"

"As for that huge, blurry creature... it must be the Mirage Dragon's original body that evolved into the white eagle."

Having even traced back the memories of the Mirage Dragon's original body, Luo Chen's heart grew even more delighted.

The quality of this Heart-Inquiring Mirror was definitely not low—it should be extremely high!

This was only natural, after all. Luo Chen's refining skills were now incomparable to what they had been a hundred years ago, and the materials used far surpassed those of the Mystic Dust Armor.

"High-grade, it must be high-grade!"

Just as Luo Chen was anticipating and about to form the treasure-retrieving seal, a sudden change occurred!

Crackle!

Within the Primordial Unity Cauldron, dark clouds suddenly gathered, and wisps of black electric arcs diffused from within the thick cloud layer.

This cloud mass was not large, just barely enveloping the treasure-light-radiating Heart-Inquiring Mirror.

Those dangerous black electric arcs continuously struck against the treasure light, causing the Heart-Inquiring Mirror to wobble precariously.

Seeing this, Luo Chen's expression changed, and he instantly thought of something.

Tribulation Lightning Power!

Back when he had formed his Nascent Soul, the final round of tribulation lightning he had drawn out—also the strongest round!

He remembered that the color of that round of tribulation lightning was no longer the ordinary white, but had turned black, and had even taken on bestial forms, extremely dangerous.

At that time, Luo Chen had calculated that even if he endured it, he would be severely injured, let alone having to deal with Han Zhan's subsequent attempt to seize his body.

Fortunately, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, after advancing to a true artifact at that time, had protected him on its own, forcibly devouring the black tribulation lightning.

Over the years, Luo Chen had always treated this tribulation lightning power as a hidden card, to be used for attack.

Occasionally, he had whimsically thought of using it to temper his divine soul, but had abandoned the idea due to its extreme danger.

He never expected that while refining a magic treasure at this moment, it would actually stir up this tribulation lightning power.

"It must be that the Heart-Inquiring Mirror's grade upon completion was too high, attracting the tribulation lightning power."

As this understanding dawned on Luo Chen, he was about to manipulate the Primordial Unity Cauldron to withdraw this black lightning.

He had the authority to do so!

The Primordial Unity Cauldron was under his control, and the tribulation lightning power it had devoured was naturally also under his control.

But just as Luo Chen was about to withdraw the tribulation lightning power, he suddenly thought of something, and his movements hesitated.

How high did a magic treasure's grade need to be to attract tribulation lightning power?

Compared to cultivators, it was only when achieving the Nascent Soul stage that a heavenly tribulation descended!

And for magic treasures, it was only when advancing to a true artifact that they needed to use heavenly thunder to refine their formations, evolving their own "meridians."

The scene before him now—how similar was it to when the Primordial Unity Cauldron and the Mystic Dust Armor had advanced to true artifacts?

In an instant, Luo Chen recalled the records in Feiyunzi's classics regarding the refinement of true artifacts.

It was not that every true artifact was advanced together with the cultivator when they broke through to the Nascent Soul stage.

That would be too demanding on the treasure's quality.

After all, not every Golden Core Great Circle cultivator could possess a top-grade natal magic treasure that had been tempered to the extreme.

Some newly advanced Sages might not even have a true artifact to use for a short time.

In reality, most of the time, Nascent Soul cultivators would, after advancing in realm, slowly nurture and refine their natal magic treasures, elevating them to the perfect level, and then ask a refiner to summon heavenly thunder, allowing the treasure to receive the baptism of heavenly thunder and undergo the final transformation.

The demon race's side was slightly different. Those Demon Emperors would often, at the third rank, instinctively select the most excellent part of their demonic body, focusing on tempering it with mana. Then, by means of the transformation tribulation lightning, they would obtain their own true artifact.

However, this kind of demon-refined true artifact often had similar quality, but its power was not pure enough.

The Glowless Beast's scorpion tail needle was a product of this kind. It had the level of a true artifact, but aside from sharpness, it had no additional attached means.

The potent poison was all added by its stomach sac.

Getting off track, back to the main topic.

At this moment, the tribulation lightning had appeared, surrounding the Heart-Inquiring Mirror, almost identical to the method of post-naturally refining a true artifact recorded in Feiyunzi's classics.

"So, if it survives this ordeal, I might obtain a true artifact?"

Thinking of this possibility, Luo Chen's heart immediately began to pound wildly.

Licking his dry lips, Luo Chen's eyes blazed with spiritual light, golden and intense, staring fixedly at the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

"Should I... gamble on it?"

"At worst, I can withdraw the tribulation lightning power at the critical moment. Even if it fails, I'll still get a defective Heart-Inquiring Mirror, enough to treat Canglong."

Once this thought emerged, it could no longer be suppressed.

Especially since Luo Chen could feel that he could withdraw the tribulation lightning power at any time, there was no longer any concern.

The final hand seal of the treasure-retrieving technique was delayed again and again, allowing the lightning and thunder to rage within the cauldron, the treasure light to tremble.

Time passed bit by bit.

Even though Luo Chen was not inside it, cold sweat seeped from his forehead.

This was even more nerve-wracking than when he himself had crossed the tribulation.

Watching the treasure light grow weaker and weaker, until the battered and broken body of the Heart-Inquiring Mirror was revealed, Luo Chen even felt a belated sense of regret in his heart.

Suddenly!

The tribulation lightning vanished abruptly, and a mottled, gray stone mirror stumbled and flew out from the mouth of the cauldron.

At the same time, a large amount of spiritual energy within the cave began to automatically surge towards the gray stone mirror.

Luo Chen finally breathed a sigh of relief. Meridians had been born, breathing and inhaling—this was already a sign of a true artifact.

Looking at the surrounding spiritual energy, Luo Chen formed a hand seal, loosening the formation of the cultivation chamber. Instantly, more of the previously trapped, dense spiritual energy surged out, frantically devoured by the stone mirror.

The stone mirror, covered in cracks, under the continuous devouring of spiritual energy, although still mottled, the cracks ultimately disappeared.

One day later.

Luo Chen reached out, and the still-warm gray stone mirror fell into his hand.

Caressing the mottled mirror surface, a feeling of lingering fear arose in Luo Chen.

"If the refining materials hadn't been excellent enough, it would have failed at the last moment."

"I even thought that if this succeeded, I would use the tribulation lightning within the Primordial Unity Cauldron to temper my other magic treasures in the future. Now it seems, that was simply reckless audacity!"

"That black tribulation lightning—even I didn't dare to withstand it head-on when I was advancing to the Nascent Soul stage. How could ordinary magic treasures dare to take such a risk?"

Luo Chen had already come to his senses. The reason the Heart-Inquiring Mirror had barely succeeded was that the main material was the illusion core of a fourth-rank mid-stage illusion beast, itself comparable to the mid-Nascent Soul stage.

Ordinary magic treasures simply did not have this kind of foundation!

The lingering fear gradually dissipated, replaced by ecstasy.

He had refined a true artifact!

Even if it relied on top-grade raw materials and the coincidentally gathered tribulation lightning power, he had still produced a genuine true artifact.

Such an experience was something many high-rank refiners could only hope to encounter, but rarely did.

He couldn't even wait to test the functions of this gray stone mirror; Luo Chen quickly opened his attribute panel and took a look.

Sure enough!

The [Third-Rank Refiner] entry was flickering continuously, faintly visible, gradually transforming into [Fourth-Rank Refiner].

Luo Chen couldn't help but grin, laughing out loud.

---

Amidst ice and snow, blood stained the peach blossoms, a spirit fox fell beneath a sword.

Tossing and turning, beasts roared, lost and uncertain where to go.

Blood rain and gory wind, nowhere to escape, only using one's body as a blade to kill out a bright and clear world.

As sandalwood incense curled upwards, Luo Chen held the stone mirror, injecting mana into it, and layers of illusions were stimulated.

Lifelike, almost impossible to distinguish truth from falsehood.

Opposite him sat several people.

There was the Heartless Fairy, there was Gu Caiyi, and even Wang Yuan.

But at this moment, aside from Wang Yuan, whose face showed a struggling expression, the others were deeply trapped within, unable to extricate themselves.

Luo Chen even felt that if he increased his mana, integrating his own divine sense into it to guide, even Wang Yuan, whose will was unyielding and unmoved by external things, would be unable to resist this mirror.

"It truly has not disappointed me!"

After murmuring this, Luo Chen slowly withdrew his mana.

The several Luo Tian Sect cultivators who had been invited to test the treasure also opened their eyes one by one.

The first to open his eyes was Wang Yuan. His whole body's astral energy vibrated, and his thick brows furrowed tightly.

"Everything just now... was an illusion?"

"Mm."

"Then you saw it all?"

Luo Chen nodded slightly, not deceiving him.

Wang Yuan fell into silence.

The second to wake was the Heartless Fairy. Unbeknownst to her, tears had already fallen from the corners of her eyes.

Just now, she had returned to the softest and most painful scene in her heart. If Luo Chen had not awakened her, she would likely have been forever trapped in that heart-wrenching, bone-chilling cold.

Luo Chen's gentle voice came to her ears.

"If I guide it, the illusion will be indistinguishable from reality. With this mirror, I can revive Canglong's consciousness without awakening her physical body."

The Heartless Fairy nodded lightly. "To save Senior Sister, you have gone to great trouble, Sage."

The last to wake was Gu Caiyi. The moment she saw Luo Chen, her tense expression instantly relaxed.

Ignoring the gazes of others, she even burrowed into Luo Chen's embrace.

"Can you not leave anymore in the future?"

Luo Chen gently stroked the woman's black hair. "It is your husband's fault, I should not have made you experience these things again."

Then, like taking out candy, he produced four jade bottles, stuffing two of them into Gu Caiyi's hands.

"This is Soul-Calming Powder, a third-rank soul-nourishing sacred medicine from the former Medicine King Sect, best used when fatigued."

"You all should take some too!"

The remaining two bottles were distributed one each to Wang Yuan and the Heartless Fairy.

Although there was a hint of favoritism, no one questioned it.

The Heartless Fairy, whose emotions had risen and fallen greatly, instead felt a trace of envy towards Luo Chen's open and generous partiality.

She steadied herself. "Does this treasure have a name?"

"Of course. Its name is Ghost God..." As the words reached his lips, Luo Chen couldn't help but chuckle softly.

In the past, one could say "ask not about the mortal world, but about ghosts and gods." But this mirror was refined by him, and also controlled by him.

So, why ask ghosts and gods? Only ask about the past!

"Dustbound!"

"Thank the dustbound, return to the immortal court."

"Joy and pleasure passed, pain endured, should thank the dustbound, can return to the immortal court. Rejoice in tonight's awakening from the dream, teaching you all to wander freely and choose your path. Do not cling to the lost path, do not cling to the lost path, return early to the Great Dao!"

Luo Chen held the mirror in one hand, stroked the woman's hair with the other, chanting and singing.

Gu Caiyi looked up, infatuated, gazing at Luo Chen, murmuring under her breath.

"The Dustbound Immortal Mirror?"

---

Now that the Dustbound Immortal Mirror had been refined, the matter of treating Canglong was placed on the agenda.

Wang Yuan and the others did not linger. That day, after taking the Soul-Calming Powder in Luo Chen's cave, they took their leave.

However, before leaving, Wang Yuan once again reminded Luo Chen of the recent changes in the situation.

The beast horde's attacks were growing fiercer and fiercer, and even the Four Great Commanders were attempting to make a move, probing some weak points in the formations of Lingtian Pass.

The casualties among the cultivators within the city, under the frenzied assault of the sea of beasts, could no longer be controlled.

Not to mention others, among those Luo Chen was familiar with, Elder Fu had nearly died at the hands of a fourth-rank mid-stage Demon Emperor. If he had not activated a costly substitute talisman at the critical moment, he would not have been able to escape back to Lingtian Pass.

Lingfengzi's combat power was slightly weaker, but his life-preservation abilities were strong. However, the disciples of his Misty Sect had suffered heavy casualties. It was said that now, less than one in ten remained, and the sect's numbers had sharply decreased to below a thousand, almost no different from an Nascent Soul force like the Luo Tian Sect that had risen later. In terms of the foundation of mid-level pillars like Wang Yuan, they were even somewhat inferior.

Even Nine Spirits Primordial Lord was having a hard time now, because a Great Commander had set its sights on him!

"We must resolve personal matters as quickly as possible and focus all our energy on battle. Otherwise, one wrong step and we will regret it endlessly!"

Wang Yuan gave this advice before leaving.

Luo Chen took it to heart. Treating Canglong was his last personal matter.

---

In the mouths of outsiders, Luo Chen was eager to help the public, promoted the younger generation, and had a gentlemanly demeanor.

In the eyes of those close to him, Luo Chen was benevolent and kind, like a towering tree, sheltering them from wind and rain.

Only Luo Chen himself knew that for the sake of longevity, everything could be cast aside.

At his core, he was an extremely selfish person.

But before crossing his bottom line, he could distinguish between kindness and grievance, and keep his promises.

Standing inside the ice room, Luo Chen moved aside the Icecastle, which had been repaired to about seventy or eighty percent over the years, revealing the ice coffin containing the sleeping beauty.

Looking at the woman's tightly furrowed brows, Luo Chen sighed softly.

This woman had shown him kindness. By reason and sentiment, he should repay her a little.

The Dustbound Immortal Mirror floated out, hovering over the ice coffin.

His ten fingers flew, forming spiritual seals in rapid succession.

"The great dream not yet ended, the soul awakens first; as if in illusion, yet also in the heart."

"Senior Sister Canglong, enter the dream now!"

White mist emanated from the mottled stone mirror, seeping into the ice coffin.

The woman's deeply locked brows relaxed slightly, and beneath her crystal-clear eyelids, her pupils moved, as if she were dreaming of something.

(Chapter End)
