# Chapter 1147: The First Man of Lost Immortal City

On the busiest street of Lost Immortal City, the day was exceptionally lively. A massive crowd had gathered before a brand-new shop.

A plaque hung above the shop, still covered by a red silk cloth.

Under everyone's watchful eyes, a portly middle-aged man chuckled and clasped his hands in greeting to a beautiful woman beside him.

"Lady Jing, you do the honors of opening!"

Jing Lan smiled faintly. "Shopkeeper Xue, how could I monopolize such an auspicious occasion? Why don't we do it together?"

Xue pondered for a moment, then nodded firmly. "Together it is!"

Then, standing left and right, each holding a silk ribbon handed up by a shop assistant, they pulled the red cloth apart under the gaze of the crowd.

*Whoosh!*

The red silk fell, and the three gilded characters—**Benevolence Hall**—on the plaque were suddenly revealed to everyone's eyes.

At the same time, the crackling sound of firecrackers erupted.

The onlookers cheered, and the festive clamor spread outward.

Standing on the steps, Shopkeeper Xue loudly announced the day's discount promotions for the new branch. He also specially introduced two combined medicinal formulas for body-tempering martial artists—half price for anyone who bought them as a set today.

The crowd's hearts stirred at this, and they surged into Benevolence Hall one after another.

As each person passed by Xue and Jing Lan, they enthusiastically wished the pair prosperity and flourishing business.

Such a lively scene drew attention even from a li or two away.

Fellow merchants gathered across the street, watching with eyes full of envy and jealousy.

"What's Xue Renji so smug about? Isn't it just opening a branch?"

"Yeah, and I heard this Benevolence Hall branch doesn't even make money. Half the profits go to that Luo Estate. Even daily management and personnel decisions are at the command of that Lady Jing. Only the drudgery of stocking goods belongs to him. Such a thankless task—what's he after?"

"What's he after?"

Someone let out a cold laugh, sweeping his gaze over the group with undisguised mockery.

"Why sour grapes, everyone? Who doesn't know that Xue Renji is deliberately currying favor with that one great offering elder? Now he's just sharing the profits of a new shop with that one's woman. But the benefits Xue Renji gains are far from comparable to a single branch's profits. Just watch—before long, this old brand Benevolence Hall will have branches all over the city, and its influence will even spread to other major cities!"

At these words, the gathered merchants all changed color.

But they couldn't refute a single point.

All because Benevolence Hall had latched onto that one's thigh early on!

Half a month ago, at the martial arena.

City Lord's guest elder Luo Chen, under the gaze of ten thousand spectators, had defeated Iron Vajra in a single move!

That matter had shocked the world, leaving people incredulous and unwilling to accept it.

But the fact had truly happened. Iron Vajra, who bore a great reputation and was said to be able to protect himself even against a Leakless Realm expert, hadn't even lasted a single move.

This was something even his senior brother, Iron Xiong, would find difficult to achieve.

Yet Luo Chen had done it, and he had done it with such ease, showing no sign of strain.

That day, the entire city was in an uproar. Everyone sang Luo Chen's praises.

That battle had not only secured Xiao Feng's position as city lord but had also firmly established Luo Chen as the number one expert of Lost Immortal City!

The subsequent effects were unending. The fact that Benevolence Hall, under Luo Chen's protection, had opened a new branch in just half a month was proof enough.

It was said that another of the three shops that had first invested in Luo Chen—the Thousand Gold Pavilion—was also showing signs of expansion.

After all, Luo Chen's reputation was now too great. These minor forces under his protection could naturally borrow his momentum to develop.

Of course, borrowing his name came at a price.

Aside from daily tributes, they also handed over considerable profits to Luo Chen.

This Benevolence Hall branch was exactly like that—not only handing over half the profits, but even entrusting the entire shop to Luo Chen's close associate, Jing Lan, to manage.

Standing at the entrance.

Jing Lan watched the endless stream of customers pouring into the shop, her expression somewhat dazed.

She knew very well that her current power and status all came from Luo Chen.

Who could have imagined that a village girl would rise to become such an important figure in Lost Immortal City, respected wherever she went, and even owning her own shop?

The dazed look flickered and vanished.

Jing Lan took a deep breath, silently making up her mind.

Although she couldn't practice martial arts, she would still do what she could—like managing this shop well for Brother Luo. And as for the trivial matters he usually didn't pay attention to, she would also do her best to handle them, ensuring that such trifles never disturbed Brother Luo's cultivation.

Just as she made this resolution, her gaze suddenly froze.

From that tall building not far away, figures filed out one by one, standing in a solemn row.

"It's the special envoy's carriage from Carefree City!"

Shopkeeper Xue walked out, watching the scene curiously. "Are they leaving?"

Jing Lan also watched with curiosity, but gradually she noticed something amiss.

The carriage team, radiating powerful auras, did not head out of the city. Instead, they turned and went deeper into the city.

Were they going to bid farewell to the new city lord before departing?

...

At the City Lord's Mansion.

Xiao Feng, who had been immersed in consolidating his power for the past half month, immediately set aside everything when he heard that the Carefree carriage team was heading his way.

He led a group of household servants out of the mansion.

Xiao Feng was now riding high on success.

Ever since he won the martial contest to determine the city lord that day, he had received the official appointment from the Carefree special envoy, formally becoming the city lord of Lost Immortal City.

Not only that, but he had seized the opportunity to make lavish promises to the guest elders on his second brother Xiao Yun's side, poaching them away.

Many of the city's forces that had originally supported Xiao Yun had also defected to him.

It could be said that given enough time, he would completely control Lost Immortal City, making this prosperous city hear only his voice!

Xiao Feng's ambitions ran deep. He not only wanted to fully control Lost Immortal City but would also organize forces to sweep away the bandits in the surrounding areas, expanding the city's sphere of influence. Finally, he would settle accounts with those other major cities that had secretly supported Xiao Yun in trying to get their hands on Lost Immortal City!

Of course, these were matters for the future. For now, he had to entertain the Carefree special envoy properly.

Xiao Feng stood at the main gate, full of anticipation.

But after waiting for a long time, the carriage team still hadn't arrived.

Three li shouldn't have taken that long.

"Uncle Iron!"

Iron Xiong nodded slightly and went ahead.

A moment later, he returned to the city lord's mansion gate with a somewhat complicated expression.

"The Carefree special envoy has gone to visit Luo Chen."

Xiao Feng was stunned. The special envoy, on the verge of departure, wasn't bidding farewell to him, the master of Lost Immortal City, but instead going to visit Luo Chen. What did that mean?

Xiao Feng's expression gradually darkened.

It seemed that in this vast Lost Immortal City, there would not be only his voice in the future.

...

Inside the Luo Estate.

Luo Chen toyed with a token, a thoughtful expression on his face.

"I've heard that this Carefree Token can only be possessed by the city lords of the seventy-two cities or by Leakless Realm experts. Special Envoy Fang, what do you mean by giving it to me?"

Fang Zhibai smiled freely. "In the Great Cataclysm Domain, the strong are revered. That Xiao Feng bears the name of city lord but lacks the strength of a city lord-level expert. Naturally, he doesn't deserve this Carefree Token. You, Brother Luo, on the other hand, possess astonishing strength. Even if you don't achieve a Leakless Golden Body in the future, taking the position of Lost Immortal City lord would be but a casual act. This Carefree Token rightfully belongs to you."

"Heh."

Luo Chen chuckled lightly and placed the token on the table.

"I have no interest in the position of city lord."

Fang Zhibai raised an eyebrow but quickly realized that the man seated above was not one to indulge in worldly pleasures. Instead, he cared more about improving his personal strength.

He put away his smile and spoke solemnly. "Brother Luo, please keep this token safe. In another ninety years, news will come from Carefree City. At that time, you can go to Carefree City and contend for a stroke of fortune!"

Carefree City?

Fortune?

Luo Chen's heart stirred. "Is it like the matter of the previous Lost Immortal City lord?"

Fang Zhibai nodded. "Correct. This grand event occurs once every hundred years, and the next one is said to be the most magnificent in a millennium. I hope Brother Luo will seize the opportunity."

Hearing this, Luo Chen's expression turned contemplative. He was clearly intrigued.

But since Fang Zhibai showed no intention of revealing the details, he didn't press further.

Whatever it was, he would find out when he went there himself.

"Very well. I will definitely go to Carefree City when the time comes!"

Hearing this reply, Fang Zhibai finally breathed a sigh of relief.

"With Brother Luo's word, my trip here hasn't been in vain."

He stood up, looking as if he was about to take his leave.

But just as he was about to depart, Fang Zhibai suddenly asked, "Brother Luo, you are a cultivator from beyond the heavens, aren't you?"

The question came abruptly, but Luo Chen nodded very naturally.

Such an open and candid response caught Fang Zhibai somewhat off guard.

He shook his head, abandoning the probing words he had prepared, and instead offered a piece of advice.

"If you can avoid it, it's best not to touch that Mi Xian Smoke."

With that, he clasped his hands in a salute and left the Luo Estate.

Luo Chen saw him to the gate, pondering the envoy's final warning, when he suddenly realized something.

At that moment, he glanced to the side. On the other side, Xiao Feng, guarded by Iron Xiong and others, was watching from afar.

Luo Chen's expression was completely composed. He nodded slightly, then turned and entered his estate.

This demeanor made Xiao Feng's expression instantly darken.

A so-called guest elder, yet he showed none of the humble posture of one living under another's roof.

He acted as he pleased, standing aloof and independent!
