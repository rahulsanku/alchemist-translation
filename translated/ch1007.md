# Chapter 1007: The Dao Sect's Great Enemy, Full-Scale Preparations

The Tihu Pill!

A fourth-grade pill researched by Qi Refinement cultivators in ancient times, with miraculous effects that could help Sage cultivators comprehend heavenly secrets and grasp the true meaning of laws in advance.

It was known as a pseudo-fifth-grade divine pill!

For Luo Chen, now a Grandmaster of the Alchemy Dao, refining such a fourth-grade pill was not particularly difficult.

The reason he had never refined it over the years was not deliberate delay, but rather that the real problem lay in the pill formula being too incomplete.

Even with the various fragmentary formulas painstakingly collected by Deng Taiyue, the degree of completion was only sixty to seventy percent.

In such a state, hasty refinement would have a one hundred percent failure rate!

But things took a turn after Luo Chen joined the Nether Origin Sect back then.

As the only Ascendant sacred ground in the Eastern Wilderness in ancient times, it was naturally impossible for them to remain indifferent to the famous pseudo-fifth-grade divine pill from antiquity.

Alchemy Saint Chu Yan and Master Xuanyou had both painstakingly researched the Tihu Pill through collecting fragmentary formulas.

Although they ultimately failed to refine it, they had deduced the formula to about seventy or eighty percent completion.

And this research result eventually fell into Luo Chen's hands!

That was still during the Alchemy Saint Hall trial, when Master Xuanyou handed it to Luo Chen before his passing.

Now that Deng Taiyue had come to him, he might as well take care of this matter.

But before that, Luo Chen still had one thing he needed to carefully explain to Deng Taiyue.

"Brother Deng, I have great confidence in refining the Tihu Pill."

Upon hearing this, Deng Taiyue's face lit up with joy.

"But!"

At the word "but," Deng Taiyue's expression paused. "Brother Luo, if you have something to say, speak frankly. As long as Deng can do it, I will spare no effort!"

Luo Chen said solemnly, "I will have a great enemy in the future, one that concerns my path to Ascension and the continuation of the Luo Tian Sect. Therefore, before that day comes, I must use all my time to cultivate and prepare for battle. Refining pills for you now will inevitably take a very long time, and it might even cause me to fail at the critical moment due to some minor deficiency. So, can you understand my current predicament?"

Deng Taiyue was stunned. He had personally witnessed Luo Chen's capabilities.

His strength was enough to dominate the world!

With such power, for Luo Chen to utter the words "great enemy," what kind of existence would that enemy be?

He tentatively asked, "Demon Emperor Azure Frost?"

Luo Chen shook his head. "The world says Azure Frost and I are irreconcilable, but it's just a temporary misunderstanding."

Deng Taiyue asked again, "The Eastern Wilderness Demon Race?"

Luo Chen waved his hand. "Although the demon race has been fickle, I already have a solution. I'm just waiting for the right opportunity."

"Then this..." Deng Taiyue fell into confusion. These were the only enemies he could think of.

Luo Chen didn't keep him in suspense and personally revealed the possible future enemy.

"The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!"

Deng Taiyue was dumbfounded.

He even failed to react for a moment, repeating subconsciously, "The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect?"

"Yes!"

Luo Chen turned around and slowly paced atop the crimson alchemy mountain.

The moonlight, drawn by the formation, spilled over him, bringing a somewhat unreal illusion, as if he were a mere phantom.

"Regardless of grievances and enmities, I will eventually have a confrontation with the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. When that time comes, a great battle will be unavoidable. Therefore, I not only need to improve my own strength but also recruit like-minded allies. So, when the time comes, will you help me?"

Deng Taiyue said dazedly, "But that's the overlord of the Mountains and Seas Realm, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!"

Luo Chen recalled the intelligence given to him by Nether Spring and slowly said, "The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect was once the overlord, but now the Dao Sect has been shattered along with the collapse of the Central Continent. The remaining power is not as terrifying as you imagine."

Deng Taiyue swallowed his saliva, a hint of fear in his eyes. "How do we deal with an Ascendant great power?"

Luo Chen countered, "What do you think of my strength?"

"Invincible under the Ascendant realm!" Deng Taiyue said without thinking, but then he looked incredulously at Luo Chen's back. "You want to personally deal with an Ascendant great power? But..."

Luo Chen smiled and sighed, "Naturally, I am no match for a great power now. But give me another hundred or two hundred years, and there will always be a one or two percent chance. For the path of longevity, that one or two percent chance is enough for me to risk my life for."

Deng Taiyue's lips moved, but he could not speak.

He could not imagine how a Sage cultivator could contend with the great divine abilities and powers of an Ascendant cultivator.

Luo Chen turned around and said calmly, "Of course, if things go smoothly, I might not have to face the only remaining Ascendant great power of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. I'm just preparing for the worst. If that day really comes, would Brother Deng be willing to stand on my side?"

Deng Taiyue fell into hesitation.

At this moment, Luo Chen was clearly "demanding repayment for a favor," using the yet-unrefined Tihu Pill to ask for his commitment to act.

And the target of that action was such a behemoth.

Any other Sage cultivator would have refused without hesitation.

But Deng Taiyue truly hesitated.

After hundreds of years of arduous cultivation, before the within-reach Tihu Pill, under the temptation of comprehending the true meaning of laws, he fell into a hesitation he had never experienced in his life.

Luo Chen did not urge him, quietly waiting for the result.

This place was the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land, and whether to stay or leave was all within his thoughts.

If Deng Taiyue disagreed, Luo Chen might not kill him, but he would not let him leave before the matter was accomplished.

Perhaps demanding repayment for a favor was somewhat despicable, but compared to his future path to Ascension, Luo Chen had to strengthen every bit of power on his side.

He was now the master of the Truth Crossing Hall on Green Parasol Mountain, able to mobilize a large number of top-tier demon race experts.

If he could also gain the help of the top human existences in the Eastern Wilderness, then even against the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, he would have a fighting chance.

Deng Taiyue was not the most top-tier existence now.

But if Luo Chen could really refine the Tihu Pill for him, with the other's comprehension, he would surely cross the mid-Sage stage and become a late-stage Sage Grand Cultivator.

How could Luo Chen miss out on such combat power?

After a long time, Deng Taiyue finally let out a bitter laugh.

"Luo Chen, why must you be so frank? Back then, I already promised to work for you. You could have refined the Tihu Pill first and then ordered me to help. Now that you're being so straightforward, you've put me in a difficult position."

Luo Chen smiled but said nothing.

How could an order back then compare to sincere help?

If the other party showed up but didn't exert effort, he would be in danger.

Instead, by stating the danger now, he could let the other party think it over carefully and prepare for battle.

He just quietly looked at the somewhat unreal moon in the night sky, thinking about when he could stand openly on the ground and admire the true bright moon.

The night wind blew, and Deng Taiyue finally made his decision.

"I will act!"

"If you give me the Tihu Pill and I attain the true meaning of laws, I will definitely go all out for you once in the future. If I break this oath, may heavenly thunder strike me, heart demons harass me, and the Great Dao be impassable!"

Although there was a premise, he had ultimately made a Great Dao oath.

This showed his sincerity!

Luo Chen let out a long breath of turbid qi, turned around, grasped the other's hands, and said warmly:

"In that case, why don't you do two small things for me now?"

Deng Taiyue was stunned, then couldn't help but smile bitterly. "Luo Chen, you really push your advantage and take everything you can!"

Luo Chen laughed heartily. "We are now allies. It's only right for us to work for each other."

Deng Taiyue shook his head with a wry smile but did not refuse.

...

The next day, after leaving behind all the medicinal herbs he had collected over the years, Deng Taiyue boarded the Nether Crossing Ship and left the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land.

When he left, he took with him all the Nascent Soul Pills treasured by the Luo Tian Sect over the years!

A total of ten pills, most of which were refined by Luo Chen himself in his early years, with a small portion collected by the Luo Tian Sect from the nearby Nether Origin Sect ruins.

Although their effects might differ, they were all excellent pills that could help people condense a Sage.

Next, he would use these Nascent Soul Pills as a gimmick to hold a large auction in the Eastern Wilderness and collect as much firmament qi as possible for Luo Chen.

These were the two things Luo Chen had entrusted to him.

And Luo Chen was not idle either. That very day, he summoned all the cultivators of the Luo Tian Sect one after another on the alchemy mountain.

The first person, surprising many, was Sang Jinghe, who had followed Luo Chen from the Northern Sea to the Eastern Wilderness!

Luo Chen handed over all the source blood of the Golden Dragon Lion he had collected from the Yin-Yang Remnant Realm to him.

There was only one goal: to let Sang Jinghe verify his early conjecture—using desolate beast source blood to cultivate that Demon-Devil Heart-Linking Tree.

If it could bear real fruit, it might allow Luo Chen's body refinement realm to explode with improvement in the short term.

The premise was that the Jisui from back then had not lied to him.

The second person was Bai Meiling.

Luo Chen handed over the now-empty Soul-Refining Banner to her.

The original souls inside had all been transferred by Luo Chen to another Soul-Nourishing Banner.

Luo Chen's instruction to Bai Meiling was for her to carry the Soul-Refining Banner, ride the Mystic Turtle, and sweep through the Nether Abyss.

Collect all ghostly beings below the fourth-rank Ghost Emperor that she encountered.

The more, the better. If she could reach a million, that would be ideal.

The Soul-Refining Banner from the Primordial Demon Sect also had a complete advancement path.

From the ordinary Myriad Souls Banner, to the Hundred-Thousand Fierce Souls Banner, and then to the Million Baleful Souls Banner.

These corresponded to the Adept, Sage, and Ascendant realms respectively.

Rumor had it that above the Baleful Souls Banner, there were also the Fierce Souls Banner and the Venerable Souls Banner, corresponding to the Body Integration and Great Vehicle stages.

But obviously, in this Mountains and Seas Realm, such existences were impossible.

Unless Luo Chen went to collect that Body Integration stage Great Desolate from the Soul-Severing Cliff.

"Relying on your soul body advantage, Little Bai, you can move freely in the Nether Abyss. With the Mystic Turtle's help, you can come and go as you please. Wait a few days for me, and the Mystic Turtle will carry you into the Nether Abyss to do this. When you come back, bring back the two fellow daoists I left outside."

Bai Meiling did not refuse and immediately went down to prepare.

Then it was the third person Luo Chen summoned—Li Yingzhang.

To him, Luo Chen gave only one requirement.

Spare no expense, spare no resources, to improve the strength of the Luo Tian Sect's cultivators.

If there were any needs for pills, bring them up as soon as possible. He would refine them all before going into seclusion.

Li Yingzhang was no ordinary man. He sensed that under such a tense situation, something major might happen.

But Luo Chen didn't say, and he didn't ask.

Just before leaving, Li Yingzhang curiously asked, "Where is Black King? If he could help, improving the spirit land's spiritual qi might be twice the result with half the effort!"

To this, Luo Chen just waved his hand and said Black King would not be returning for now.

After Li Yingzhang left, the Alchemy Dust Hall on the alchemy mountain fell into brief silence.

"Black King?"

Luo Chen tapped the armrest of his chair, a puzzled expression appearing on his face.

In his perception, Black King was not dead.

But the other party had not returned to the Luo Tian Sect.

He wanted to search, but the distance between them was too great, so he didn't even have a direction to guide him.

"Where has that fool gone?"

Knowing Black King wasn't dead, Luo Chen was temporarily relieved. The urgent task was to prepare for battle on all fronts.

As for how to retrieve his incomplete destiny soul, Luo Chen had roughly formed a plan when he left Green Parasol Mountain.

If things went smoothly, he might not even need to go to war with the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

But if things didn't go smoothly, he would have to prepare for war with the former number one overlord of the Mountains and Seas Realm!

He didn't want to go that far.

But if it was truly unavoidable, then Luo Chen wouldn't mind having a confrontation with that Liancheng Ancestor who had survived the Soul-Severing Cliff battle!

Footsteps sounded outside the hall, numerous, over a hundred!

Soon, familiar faces came into Luo Chen's view.

"Disciple Qu Lingjun leads all third-rank and second-rank alchemists of the Grand Alchemy Academy to pay respects to Master!"

Qu Lingjun immediately knelt, extremely respectful.

Behind him, over a hundred cultivators also knelt in unison to pay their respects, their expressions, besides reverence, filled with undisguised fanatical worship in their eyes.

"Greetings to the Supreme Elder!"

Looking at this batch of cultivators from the Luo Tian Sect's Grand Alchemy Academy, a satisfied smile appeared on Luo Chen's face.

Dean Qu Lingjun, a third-rank alchemist!

Vice Dean Qi Yi, along with three Grand Alchemy Academy elders, were all third-rank alchemists at the Adept realm.

The remaining hundred disciples were only at the Initiate realm, but their alchemy skills had reached the second rank.

This was the current foundation of the Luo Tian Sect!

It could also be said to be the most outstanding academy among the various major organizations within the Luo Tian Sect.

What they inherited was Luo Chen's most proud alchemy lineage.

Among them, Qu Lingjun and Qi Yi had even been personally taught by him.

Perhaps compared to the Qingdan Valley back then, they were one less third-rank alchemist, but these five third-rank alchemists' alchemy skills absolutely surpassed those few elders of the Qingdan Valley back then.

And the number of second-rank alchemists far exceeded the thirty-plus that the Qingdan Valley had scraped together.

With the help of this batch of alchemists, his efficiency in deducing the Tihu Pill would be greatly improved.

He had already collected most of the fragmentary formula for the Tihu Pill.

With the pioneering work of Alchemy Saint Chu Yan and Master Xuanyou.

Moreover, he vaguely felt that the Heavenly Secret Pill that Alchemy Saint Chu Yan had given him back then also had a homologous relationship with the Tihu Pill.

Next, he only needed to have the Grand Alchemy Academy alchemists help him experiment with various medicinal herb properties, ratios, and even heat levels, etc. Through the process of elimination, he could find the most suitable combination of medicinal herbs for the Tihu Pill.

Then, through the attribute panel, he could confirm whether it could be refined.

Thus, everything would fall into place naturally.

Looking down at the crowd, Luo Chen slowly spoke: "Do you know why I have called you all here?"

The hundred people in the hall had already been informed of the general situation by Qu Lingjun, and they answered without hesitation.

"Disciple is willing to assist the Supreme Elder in alchemy!"

Luo Chen laughed heartily. "Good, then let's begin!"

The Daoist in feathered robes rose to his full height, waved his hand, and streaks of light flew toward the crowd.
