# Chapter 671: Fierce Battle with a Sage, Mountains Collapse and Earth Splits

Skin like congealed fat, eyes like stars.

His handsome face seemed like that of an immortal exiled to the mortal world, enough to move the heart at a glance.

Most striking of all was that iconic blue hair.

Even fourth-tier transformation could not change that hair color. It belonged to the emperor of the sharkfolk race, Liujun!

Luo Chen spoke the other party's name and origin, drawing surprise from Liujun.

"Junior, you actually recognize me?"

"How could I not? You fled in defeat at the Fallen Sea, a defeated subordinate of Blood Wanderer of my Myriad Immortals Assembly. Luo would not dare mistake you in the slightest."

"You!"

Anger flashed across Liujun's face, yet he made no movement.

While Luo Chen was puzzled, his gaze fell on the Five Elements Lotus Platform beneath Liujun.

It was precisely his goal for this trip!

Moreover, its quality was far higher than the one he had seen in Azure Pill Valley [TERM?] back then. The aura upon it was already infinitely close to the fifth tier!

"Something is wrong, Luo Chen!"

Han Zhan's voice entered his ear, carrying a trace of urgency.

"He is drawing on the lotus platform's power to recover from his injuries."

Luo Chen raised his brow. No wonder the other party still sat steady as Mount Tai after such verbal provocation.

However!

If Luo Chen let him absorb the power of the Five Elements Lotus Platform, how would he use it to refine a Nascent Soul Pill in the future?

"If you will not come out, then I will go in!"

Luo Chen gave a low shout. His body shot like lightning straight into the depths of the magma lake.

This was precisely entering the tiger's den to seize the tiger cub.

As he charged, the seven flying swords beside him suddenly merged into one and became the vanguard.

The long sword was empty and sharp, its baleful qi oppressive.

In the blink of an eye, it arrived before Liujun.

The other party merely flicked a finger lightly.

Clang!

The Seven Baleful Sword [TERM?] seemed to suffer a heavy blow. The merged great sword immediately broke apart and became seven streams of light, scattering involuntarily into the depths of the magma lake.

Seeing this, Luo Chen was not surprised.

This set of flying sword magic treasures from Mountain Lord Hundred Craft was certainly powerful, but he had not refined them much. He purely relied on the Soul Heaven Sword Case [TERM?] to control them indirectly.

Let alone a Seven Baleful Sword Formation [TERM?], even some ordinary wondrous uses were difficult to activate. He had only grasped the Great Sword Art [TERM?].

He still had follow-up moves!

An azure handprint rose from his palm, yet he held it back and did not release it.

As he rushed forward at great speed, the surrounding magma seemed drawn by it and gathered one after another. The azure handprint became larger and larger.

When he reached a hundred zhang away...

"Go!"

With a low roar, the Azure Yang Great Handprint pressed down.

Seeing this palm, Liujun frowned slightly. His left hand drew a circle, then slowly pushed out.

Buzz...

A layer of blue light slowly spread outward.

When the halo and the great handprint touched, silent annihilation occurred before their eyes.

Seeing this, Liujun looked at Luo Chen with some seriousness.

"A fire spell at the peak of attainment, even capable of drawing fire qi for its own use. I underestimated you."

"In ordinary times, I could take you as a slave."

"But now, I must heal. I have no mood to play with you."

The murmuring voice sounded like moving song as it drifted out faintly.

These simple, direct words fell into Luo Chen's ears, yet he felt they were graceful and pleasant beyond compare.

Like intimate whispers, like a spring sobbing softly.

"He is healing. He truly has injuries."

"They should be injuries left by Blood Wanderer back then. Blood Wanderer is extremely strong. Though he is only mid-stage Sage, he is said to be no weaker than late-stage great cultivators."

"Shark Emperor Liujun is only early-stage Sage. How could he be Blood Wanderer's opponent?"

"In other words, right now is my best chance to defeat him..."

Because of those words, thoughts rose and fell without stop in Luo Chen's mind. It was as though he already saw the smooth battle that would follow.

At that very moment, a sensation like a needle prick pierced his sea of consciousness and divine soul.

"Wake up!"

Suddenly, Luo Chen's distracting thoughts vanished. Amid the sharp pain, his heart was horrified.

The sharkfolk song was like dream and illusion!

If Han Zhan had not forcibly awakened him, perhaps the other party's few simple words would have delayed his movements and left him at another's mercy.

As expected!

When Shark Emperor Liujun's tone sharply changed afterward, it was like a silver bottle shattering and blades clashing.

A blue light was racing straight toward Luo Chen.

At such a perilous instant, there was no time for Luo Chen to react.

That light reached his body instantly!

Pfft!

Hiss!

Clang!

Three different sounds rang out one after another.

Luo Chen slowly raised his head. In his hand, he gripped a water-type flying sword like a swimming fish. It should be among upper-grade magic treasures.

It was because of this flying sword that two layers of his extreme defense had been broken in succession.

Shark Emperor Liujun was somewhat stunned. Looking at Luo Chen's red Daoist robe gradually transforming into black armor, he instantly understood.

"As expected of a human cultivator. Your external objects are excellent. One suit of armor actually has inner and outer qi armor, delaying for a moment and saving your little life."

Luo Chen's expression was ruthless.

"Do not think of using charm sound again to disturb my mind!"

Beside his ears, pure mana had already formed two barriers, isolating all outside sounds.

Shark Emperor Liujun smiled slightly and flicked his finger again.

Instantly, the flying sword Luo Chen held began spinning wildly, attempting to forcefully break through his defense.

Hiss!

Hiss!

Hiss!

Sharp buzzing rang without stop, like an electric drill.

Luo Chen's expression turned fierce.

"Get lost!"

One hand gripped the sword, and one fist smashed sideways.

Clang!

The flying sword in his hand visibly bent.

Only bent. It did not shatter.

Liujun beckoned.

Whoosh!

The flying sword forcefully broke free of Luo Chen's restraint and returned to his side. After mana replenished it, the sword instantly straightened again, just as before.

This transformation showed the quality of the sword's material.

Even Luo Chen's brute strength could not destroy it by force.

Shark Emperor Liujun also put away his earlier contempt.

He remembered this person. His escape speed was extremely fast. Back at the Three Pillar Gorge [TERM?] in the Fallen Sea, he had run early, avoiding the fate of being enslaved like Dao Lan.

Now, the other party's spells and body had also shown attainments far beyond Golden Core cultivators.

Thus, he could not be treated as an ordinary Golden Core cultivator.

Taking a deep breath, Liujun directly used the simplest and most effective method Sage Perfected Beings used against lower-ranked cultivators.

Boom!

A powerful suppressive field spread from him as center with a crash.

In an instant, it covered Luo Chen.

It was a Sage domain!

"I do not know why you seek death, but I remember that you once killed one of my clansmen, and cruelly burned him to ash."

"Today, I will use a person's own Dao to return it to that person."

As he murmured, he leisurely spat out a ball of mana.

That mana flew into the air and became a blue flame.

It was not sourceless fire, but the most orthodox Sage true fire!

The blue flame slowly drifted over, as if not caring at all whether Luo Chen would run.

Under normal circumstances, this was how it should be.

After all, what Golden Core cultivator could resist the domain suppression of a Sage Perfected Being?

But Luo Chen was no ordinary Golden Core!

Boom!

"All-Encompassing Fire Prison, open!"

Vast crimson mana erupted with a crash under the amplification of the lifebound magic treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

It was like a volcanic eruption, instantly breaking through the Sage domain's suppression.

Azure flame rose and wrapped Luo Chen's whole body.

Like a god-demon of purgatory, he stood amid the magma.

He raised his head toward the sky. The corner of his mouth curled slightly. Shark Emperor Liujun's domain suppression was truly somewhat weak!

Either his own realm was too weak, or he was restricted by this environment.

Within Blazing Refinement Prison, fire-attribute spiritual qi was everywhere. The other four types of heaven and earth spiritual qi were pitifully scarce.

Especially water-attribute spiritual qi, which restrained and was restrained by fire. It was almost nonexistent.

Under such circumstances, Liujun, a sharkfolk Sage born in the sea and raised in the sea, probably could not display even half of his strength.

Therefore!

"This place is my home ground!"

Luo Chen suddenly lowered his head, stretched out his right hand openly, and punched.

The blue flame drifting toward him was directly shattered.

After shattering the other party's Sage true fire, Luo Chen's body moved greatly. He became a streak of firelight and began running madly through the magma lake.

With the Five Elements Lotus Platform as the center, Luo Chen's afterimages grew more and more numerous.

Azure great handprints struck out from within the magma lake one after another.

Boom!

Boom!

Boom!

All of this sounded lengthy, but from Luo Chen breaking the domain suppression, to dispersing the true fire with one blow, then counterattacking violently, it all occurred in a single instant.

Facing the boiling magma lake and the countless Azure Yang Great Handprints cast by those afterimages, Liujun's expression finally changed completely.

"He can actually ignore my Sage domain!"

There was no time to think about what method the other party had used.

Countless great handprints blasted toward him, and he could only continuously use demon arts to defend.

But while activating demon arts, he sensed something wrong.

"My mana... This brat is actually using a quasi-domain art to provoke my mana into boiling!"

"Hmph!"

With a cold snort, anger appeared on the handsome man's face. He opened his mouth and spat out a round pearl.

Light spread from it, about to isolate inside and outside.

At that moment!

Clang!

A sword cry sounded!

In the next moment, seven swords rang together, freely forming a formation!

Seven streams of baleful qi became a sword-blade storm that directly knocked the pearl into the air.

It was a sword formation!

Shark Emperor Liujun's expression changed greatly.

"Despicable!"

Without hesitation, his mind moved.

The pearl suddenly shattered.

An extreme freezing power spread outward.

Wherever it passed, boiling magma became glacier, and the high temperature that had covered this place for countless years began dropping rapidly.

Liujun took a deep breath. His divine sense swept out, about to find Luo Chen's true body.

Suddenly, the sound of ice cracking rang out.

Within his star-like eyes was reflected a nine-zhang giant, wings trembling as it charged from beneath the ice surface into the sky.

The giant hand clenched, and an iron fist shattered the air.

Mountain-Breaking Form!

This time, Liujun could no longer remain seated.

He rose from the Five Elements Lotus Platform and punched with his right hand. As he swung, mottled scales appeared on his arm.

The two collided.

Bang!

The great force rebounded, and both figures flew backward uncontrollably.

The Five Elements Lotus Platform slowly sank amid shattered ice and boiling magma.

Seeing this, Shark Emperor Liujun grew anxious.

With a shake of his wrist, he cast out a flying sleeve, intending to wrap the Five Elements Lotus Platform.

But just as it was about to wrap around it, the lotus platform, quite spiritual, turned in the air and suddenly fled into the depths of the lake.

"Damn it!"

Shark Emperor Liujun was furious. He had no time to chase the Five Elements Lotus Platform that he had found with such difficulty. His gaze landed on Luo Chen.

With one look, he revealed uncertainty.

"Are you human, or an archaic beast?"

Luo Chen, who had already sealed both ears, could not hear what he said.

Even if he could understand the meaning through the movement of Liujun's mouth, Luo Chen did not want to answer.

Silent, he slapped the ice layer beneath his feet.

Great strength surged, the ice accelerated its collapse, and sealed magma surged out once more.

The power of home ground was still present!

Afterward, Luo Chen stepped forward.

The void exploded!

The two were already extremely close. Once Voidburst Step activated, he was face to face in the blink of an eye.

A claw swept across the air.

The blue sword swung again!

Clang!

The Cloud-Grasping Divine Claw [TERM?] grabbed again!

Clang!

In a short time, Luo Chen displayed the Devil Lord's Seven Free Hands [TERM?] to the fullest.

Extremely high movement speed, enormous brute force, refined body arts, and occasional instant Azure Yang Great Handprints.

With this combination, in only several dozen exchanges, Shark Emperor Liujun was beaten into constant defense and fell into disadvantage.

When another clash rang out, that blue flying sword of quite good quality turned into fragments before Shark Emperor Liujun's eyes and was buried in the lake.

"No. I cannot keep fighting him here!"

"My domain is suppressed by Blazing Refinement Prison. Every portion of mana I use is one portion less, and my body is inferior to his."

"Especially that hidden person, who makes me feel like a thorn in my back!"

Taking a deep breath, Shark Emperor Liujun took Luo Chen's strike head-on with a swing of his sleeve, then borrowed the force to retreat, rushing straight into the sky.

Seeing this, Luo Chen's killing intent surged.

He glanced down at the magma lake, then without hesitation charged into the sky, pursuing close behind.

Just as he rushed out of the magma lake and arrived in the sky, Luo Chen's pupils shrank.

In his sight, Shark Emperor Liujun had not fled.

He stood in the high sky, holding an oil lamp.

A blue oil lamp!

A drop of liquid, like blood and also like grease, flowed from Shark Emperor Liujun's fingertip and fell onto the oil lamp.

"Did you truly think I am completely restrained by you here?"

"I am an existence who survived the war between demons and devils. How could I fear a mere conflict between water and fire?"

The earlier suppression and wretchedness made Shark Emperor Liujun laugh in anger.

Hiss!

On the oil lamp, a dot of blue flame suddenly appeared.

He blew gently, and that blue flame drifted slowly toward Luo Chen.

Danger!

An extreme danger signal wildly surged through Luo Chen's mind.

Without thinking, he fled into the distance.

However, although the flame seemed slow, its escape speed was extremely fast.

Even with Luo Chen's Archaic Tier Four true body and the Great Perfection Nine Thousand Li movement art, he somehow could not shake off that strange flame.

When Luo Chen was forced to stop atop a high mountain, the flame appeared before him.

His expression turned fierce. He stretched out his left hand and grasped toward the flame.

His palm, wrapped in Withering-Bloom True Fire, seized the blue flame in a posture unafraid of death.

Not far away, Shark Emperor Liujun, who had chased after him, saw this scene and revealed a cold smile.

"You do not know life from death."

In the next moment, before Shark Emperor Liujun's eyes, that giant's left arm directly touched the flame.

One azure and one blue, two lights fought one another, neither yielding an inch!

Shark Emperor Liujun was surprised again. This brat actually controlled such a wondrous flame?

However, it was still not enough.

As expected.

Azure and blue fought, and the deciding point lay in the "battlefield."

The former had greater momentum, while the latter's light was weak.

Yet after the blue light touched flesh, it suddenly rose section by section and suppressed the azure flame in return.

"In the Northern Sea there are sharkfolk, beautiful and skilled in song. Divine gauze comes from their hands, their tear pearls shine like the moon. Sharkfolk blood can prolong life, and their oil can burn for ten thousand years..."

Ever since he had killed a late third-tier sharkfolk in the Fallen Sea and encountered Shark Emperor Liujun's emergence, Luo Chen had investigated much information about this race.

At this moment, that information leapt into his mind.

He looked at his left hand. The surging vitality of his physical body was instead being used by the blue flame. He fell silent.

Clearly, this was a strange flame produced by a Sage Perfected Being using his own oil as lamp oil to ignite an oil lamp comparable to a true artifact.

This method was not something Luo Chen's Withering-Bloom True Fire, which he had not yet fully mastered, could resist.

If this continued, even with his body comparable to a fourth-tier archaic beast, he might be burned away completely.

Unfortunately, there was not enough time. Otherwise, he could have used the Dragon-Slaying Art [TERM?] to cut it off.

When a decision had to be made, it must be made. Otherwise, disorder would turn back upon him.

Luo Chen said nothing. His right hand formed fingers like a sword, and a blood light swept out.

Crack!

His left hand fell at the sound.

Shark Emperor Liujun, who had already come within ten zhang, could not help freezing at this scene.

He had thought the other party would rely on that wondrous azure fire to hold on a little longer.

He had not expected his nature to be so decisive!

At that moment, Luo Chen raised his head. A pair of cold eyes stared fixedly at him.

Shark Emperor Liujun frowned.

"What other methods do you have? My race's true artifact activates a demon flame that specifically restrains archaic beasts with surging vitality. Now your body is restricted by me, and you no longer have the convenience of home ground..."

Luo Chen smiled darkly. Then his right hand formed a one-handed seal, and he struck out with one palm.

It did not strike Shark Emperor Liujun.

It landed on the mountain beneath him.

"Mountain Collapse!"

The low roar spread through the wilderness.

In the next moment, under Shark Emperor Liujun's puzzled gaze, the great mountain split apart.

The powerful explosive aftermath forced both of them to fly backward.

"Trying to run?"

Within the explosion's aftermath, Liujun's expression was cold. This junior had forced him to such a point. There was no way he would let him escape.

He stepped out, about to pursue Luo Chen.

At that moment...

From within the broken mountain came a sharp hiss.

A silver giant python rushed out furiously.

With one glance, Shark Emperor Liujun recognized the silver giant python's essence.

"Fire spirit!"

Luo Chen smiled coldly and opened his mouth to spit out azure flame, which became a fire tree in the void.

"Go. Kill him!"

The fire tree swayed and rushed toward Shark Emperor Liujun.

And that silver giant python seemed to sense movement from its own kind, also fixing its target on Shark Emperor Liujun.

Seeing two fire spirits, each at least fourth tier, charge toward him, Shark Emperor Liujun panicked!

Ruthlessness appeared in Luo Chen's eyes. He swept his gaze around, killing intent filling his heart.

His body moved, and he flew toward another erupting volcano.

"Not enough!"

"Still not enough!"

"I want him dead inside Blazing Refinement Prison!"

*(End of Chapter)*
