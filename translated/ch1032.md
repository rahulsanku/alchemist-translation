# Chapter 1032: Family Warmth, Spirit Root Testing Ceremony

In the morning hours.

After practicing a set of body-strengthening fist techniques, Zhou Fuyou briefly washed up and then headed toward the Zhou clan's council hall.

That was the most central location, second only to the Coral Hall where the ancestor was in seclusion, so walking there took considerable time.

Zhou Fuyou was in no hurry. Following the memories he once had, he walked at a steady, unhurried pace.

Along the way, he encountered many Zhou clan members, and from time to time, people would look at him with curious gazes.

Clearly, the events from yesterday at the training grounds and in the evening had already spread.

Zhou Fuyou's expression was calm. He pretended not to notice any of the stares, and whenever he met an elder he knew, he respectfully greeted them, not lacking in propriety.

After about half an hour, the solemn and majestic assembly hall came into view.

Zhou Fuyou approached, but before he could walk inside, someone stopped him.

He looked up at the somewhat aged man and hesitated. "Uncle, Second Grandfather called for me."

The man blocking him was Zhou Junxiao's eldest son, of the same generation as Zhou Fuyou's father. Since he was also older, Zhou Fuyou called him Uncle.

But if anyone put "Uncle" and "Second Grandfather" together, they might easily mistake the relationship.

Such was the way of cultivators. Those at lower realms often had white hair and aged appearances, while some accomplished experts had mastered the art of preserving their youth, looking quite young.

Zhou Fuyou had heard that this was related to the age at which a cultivator established their foundation. The earlier one built their foundation, the easier it was to maintain the appearance of that time.

Though they would still age over the years and as their blood and qi declined, they were still much better off than others.

Uncle made a shushing gesture and said softly, "They're discussing matters inside. Wait a moment."

Zhou Fuyou nodded and waited quietly outside.

But the voices from inside the hall clearly reached his ears.

"Patriarch, Yuhao was beaten by Zhou Fuyou. Is there really no punishment at all?"

"It was a conflict of youthful pride between children. What punishment do you want? Were you never beaten when you were young, or have you never beaten someone your own age?"

"But he was too ruthless! Half of my Yuhao's face was smashed, three teeth knocked out, and most of the rest are loose. Is this still a game between children? They are at least of the same clan, relatives who should support each other in the future. This cannot be indulged!"

"They were born in the same year, started training on the same day. As far as I know, Yuhao also had a group of friends helping him. Being beaten like that only means he was outmatched. If you hold a grudge, you can tell your Yuhao to train hard and get his revenge later. Our Zhou clan does not encourage internal strife, but we absolutely do not prohibit competition."

"Patriarch..."

"Enough. Your own father hasn't spoken, so why are you making a fuss here? Xiaocheng, what do you think?"

"I'll follow Second Brother's arrangements."

"Hmm. Let this matter rest here. But we do need to assign Qi Refinement cultivators to keep an eye on the training grounds. The ones teaching techniques are mortal Xiantian martial artists; they dare not interfere in conflicts between clan children. To prevent excessive situations in the future, I will send Qi Refinement cultivators to watch nearby."

Hearing this discussion, Zhou Fuyou immediately understood it was about what happened yesterday.

Aside from Second Grandfather, the people discussing it should also include Thirteenth Grandfather, who was also a Golden Core Superior, and his son.

The hall fell silent.

Zhou Fuyou looked up at Uncle, who smiled gently and shook his head.

Was there still something to discuss?

The next moment, voices rose again inside the hall.

"Patriarch, regarding yesterday's incident, my Yuqi came back and explained the situation in detail. The matter was initiated by Zhou Yuhao, but Fuyou's temperament is extreme, and he did strike too hard. In the past, Fuyou had a gentle personality, refined and courteous; he wouldn't have done such a thing. In my opinion, his change in character is probably because of the unfortunate fall of Baihan and his wife half a year ago. The shock, combined with a lack of guidance, has turned him this way."

Outside the hall, Zhou Fuyou unconsciously pressed his lips together.

He recognized the owner of this voice.

Seventh Uncle—Zhou Haiye!

Second Grandfather's voice rang out.

"Xiao Qi, what are you trying to say?"

"Having Qi Refinement cultivators watch over them is ultimately treating the symptoms, not the root cause. Xiao Qi has an idea. Patriarch, see if it works."

"Speak."

"Our family has always had a good relationship with Baihan's family, and the children often visit each other. How about I adopt Fuyou and his sister? I can regularly teach him right from wrong, and prevent him from causing greater trouble in the future."

There was a moment of silence in the council hall.

After a long while, Second Grandfather said in a deep voice, "That won't be necessary."

Zhou Haiye seemed somewhat surprised. He hadn't expected his seemingly surefire request to be rejected.

He couldn't help but ask, "Fuyou and his sister are still so young. How can they manage without someone to care for them?"

"Who said no one is caring for them? Baihan and his wife died carrying out a mission for the clan. Our Zhou clan naturally takes care of the orphans! I have already decided to adopt Fuyou... and his sister. You don't need to worry about these matters."

The Patriarch's own line, personally adopting Zhou Fuyou and his sister?

When this news came out, Zhou Haiye was dumbfounded.

Even Zhou Yuhao's father, who had been indignant earlier, suddenly understood why the Patriarch had been so determined to protect that boy.

"Finally, I want to say one more thing. Keep your own children in line, especially their mouths! If they can cultivate when they grow up, they will eventually walk the path of the cultivation world. Strength is not everything. Many times, disaster comes from the mouth. Do I really need to teach you this?"

"Alright, dismissed!"

A few voices came from inside the hall, followed by the sound of chairs moving.

A group of people walked out of the council hall but stopped at the entrance.

A boy with a gentle, delicate face and a slender figure stood there, looking at them calmly.

Facing the gaze of the crowd, he was neither humble nor arrogant, his emotions unreadable.

Zhou Xiaocheng, one of the Zhou clan's only five Golden Core Superiors, raised an eyebrow, glanced back into the hall, and then smiled at Zhou Fuyou.

"You must be Fuyou?"

Zhou Fuyou bowed slightly. "Greetings, Grandfather Cheng."

"Good!" Zhou Xiaocheng stroked his beard. "An extraordinary temperament, a promising appearance. You will surely be an outstanding figure of our Zhou clan in the future. My little grandson Yuhao was a bit reckless with his words before. You've already taught him a lesson. From now on, you are still friends, still brothers. If he doesn't change his bad habits, you can continue to teach him a lesson for me, Grandfather. How about that?"

Zhou Fuyou was slightly surprised. He hesitated and said, "Fuyou will remember Grandfather Cheng's words. We are still friends, still brothers."

Zhou Xiaocheng laughed heartily and walked away with his hands behind his back.

Behind him, Zhou Yuhao's father was a bit slow to react. He looked at Zhou Fuyou, then blankly followed.

Seventh Uncle Zhou Haiye, however, approached Zhou Fuyou with a somewhat ingratiating manner.

"Fuyou, you haven't come to our house to play in a long time."

Zhou Fuyou's expression was cold. "My studies have been too demanding, and I have to take care of my little sister. I haven't been going out much lately. Seventh Uncle, Second Grandfather still has matters to discuss with me. Let's talk another time."

With that, he walked alone into the hall.

Seventh Uncle stood at the entrance, stunned.

Uncle patted him on the shoulder. "Haiye, don't worry about little Fuyou. He should have felt your goodwill."

With that, Uncle also entered the hall.

Had he really felt it?

Zhou Haiye forced a smile, but a hint of danger flickered in his narrowed eyes.

Inside the hall.

Seeing Second Grandfather's sturdy back again, Zhou Fuyou bowed respectfully.

"You must have heard all of that, right?"

"Yes."

"Any thoughts?"

"Fuyou will follow Second Grandfather's arrangements. But that home still has traces of Father and Mother. My sister and I don't want to move for now."

"That's fine. I will send people to check on you regularly. If you need help with anything, you can find your Uncle, or come directly to me. Take this token. If you encounter danger, just crush it."

Zhou Fuyou respectfully accepted the token. He could faintly sense a vast power contained within it.

After thanking Second Grandfather, Zhou Fuyou prepared to leave.

But just before he went out the door, Second Grandfather suddenly called out to him.

"This is at home. You don't have to act so mature and keep your face stern all day. You can smile more, just like other children your age."

The boy's body stiffened. He didn't turn around, but an inexplicable emotion surged in his heart.

After his parents left, he had taken care of his sister alone for so long. No elder by blood had ever shown him concern or care. Living in the vast Zhou clan, there had been no warmth at all.

Until this moment.

His voice choked as he said, "Fuyou will remember what Second Grandfather said."

"Go on."

Zhou Junxiao waved his hand, watching the boy walk away with a slightly lighter step. He couldn't help but shake his head slightly.

Though precocious, having learned much from books, knowing how to hide his emotions and disguise himself, he was still just a child after all.

He looked at his son.

"From now on, look after the two of them. Distribute their resources on time. Also, open an extra..."

Zhou Junxiao seemed to think of something and quickly changed his words.

"Open two extra portions of body-nourishing medicinal herbs and deliver them privately!"

...

At the Drink Green Waterside Pavilion.

Zhou Fuyou silently reflected on the day's events.

There was the relief of Zhou Yuhao's family extending an olive branch to him, and also the faint disgust at Seventh Uncle's request to adopt him and his sister.

He didn't believe that the tonics sent by Seventh Uncle's family back then were an innocent mistake, nor did he believe today's adoption offer came from the heart.

It was simple logic: if they truly had such intentions, why hadn't they brought it up half a year ago?

And the reason Zhou Fuyou was unwilling to go live at Second Grandfather's house was also simple.

Second Grandfather's house also had many people. It would be hard to avoid conflicts from daily interactions. He wasn't sure that if he or his sister offended someone from the Patriarch's line, they would still receive Second Grandfather's protection.

It was better to live alone at the Drink Green Waterside Pavilion.

And he needed to cultivate!

In his view, starting cultivation at too young an age would easily invite interference from elders, which was not conducive to quickly mastering powerful strength.

As for how powerful his current strength was?

Zhou Fuyou brought his fingers together like a sword and pointed at the pond in front of the pavilion.

Whoosh!

A streak of deep blue light flashed, and a century-old bluestone in the pond silently crumbled into powder.

A first-rank spell, Water Arrow Art!

Such power was absolutely not something a Qi Refinement initial-stage cultivator could achieve.

And it was also instant-cast!

For other elemental spells, Zhou Fuyou could not achieve this level of proficiency. Only with water-element spells did he feel an instinctive ease, as if they were an extension of his will.

As if, as long as he willed it, he could do it!

Unfortunately, the spell manuals left in his father's study were too few, and water-element spells were only a handful.

But Zhou Fuyou was in no hurry now.

With Second Grandfather's protection, he didn't need to take risks or rush.

He was still young, not yet of age.

Once his spirit root was truly tested, the clan would issue a cultivation method of the corresponding attribute, and he could also enter the clan's Scripture Pavilion to choose his preferred spells.

That would be the time when he truly revealed his talent.

The ripples on the pond gradually calmed. The boy looked at his own reflection in the water.

Recalling what Second Grandfather had said to him when he left the council hall, the boy unconsciously curled the corners of his mouth, trying to squeeze out a smile.

But the smiling face reflected in the pond water looked more like crying.

The boy opened his mouth, but in the end, pressed his lips tightly together.

...

Six years later.

The Zhou clan of Egret Marsh.

On this day, crowds bustled and banners fluttered.

After another six years of recuperation and growth, the Zhou clan's momentum was more magnificent than ever.

In six years, clan members had gradually built their foundations, and even one more Golden Core cultivator had emerged.

Such development was a miracle, not just near Egret Marsh, but across the entire Tiannan Continent.

Not long ago, the Zhou clan had just produced two new Golden Cores, thanks to the personal guidance of the ancestor himself.

But this time, the one who had advanced to Golden Core, Zhou Haiye, had reached the Golden Core stage entirely through his own independent cultivation.

Moreover, he was a third-generation clan member, not one of those second-generation members with deep foundations.

Many said that Egret Marsh was a blessed land, and would be the place where the Zhou clan would rise to prominence, comparable to their former clan grounds at Coral Sea in the Northern Sea.

But today's excitement was not because of Zhou Haiye's achievement. It was because it was the day for testing the clan members' spirit roots once again.

Testing spirit roots normally didn't require a specially arranged day.

When the children reached the right age, the clan elders could test them at any time.

But this time, the situation was special.

Thirteen years ago, the ancestor had issued an order encouraging clan members to have children. From Foundation Establishment true cultivators down to mortals without spirit roots, everyone was to have as many children as possible.

As a result, twelve years ago, the Zhou clan had a huge batch of children.

The thirty-plus children who usually trained at the clan's training grounds were only a part. In Coral Town, where the mortals were settled, there were several dozen more.

Today, they were all gathered together for convenient testing.

Not only that, it was said that the Qingtong Lin clan and the Hanjiang Valley Yin clan, who were on good terms with the Zhou clan, would also send strong cultivators to observe, to see the quality of the next generation of Zhou clan members.

That was why the Zhou clan was making such a grand production of the Spirit Root Testing Ceremony.

Choosing an auspicious day, selecting a spacious location, setting up corresponding formation treasures, and even specially arranging seats for the observers.

At the Drink Green Waterside Pavilion.

Two figures walked out, followed by a white-haired old woman who waved her hand shakily.

"Young Master, Young Miss, come back late!"

The slender, graceful Zhou Qingyu said with a smile, "Grandma, other people hope their children come home early. Why do you want us to come back late?"

The boy beside her said calmly, "Because only those who test positive for spirit roots will be kept by the clan. Those without spirit roots will be sent home early. Grandma wants us to come back late, naturally because she hopes we both have spirit roots."

"Hmph, I actually know that, Brother!" Zhou Qingyu wrinkled her little nose and said.

The one she called brother was Zhou Fuyou.

Now twelve years old, Zhou Fuyou was still slender, but he had grown to about 1.7 meters, almost indistinguishable from an adult. His sister Zhou Qingyu stood beside him, a full head shorter.

His fair face was exceptionally handsome, even giving a feeling of "striking beauty."

His gentle, delicate temperament only added to this sense of beauty.

He looked calmly at the girl before him. Though he didn't smile, the corners of his mouth were slightly raised.

After six years of nourishing with various medicinal herbs, his sister was finally not so frail.

Combined with daily training, her condition of qi deficiency and physical weakness had greatly improved.

Compared to ordinary people, there was no trace left of the "sickly child" she had been in her childhood.

"Mao Mao, let's go! We need to arrive at the Spirit Root Testing Ceremony site before the important guests, so as not to be discourteous."

"Alright, I know. And, Brother, don't call me Mao Mao anymore."

"Okay, Mao Mao."

"Ah, bad brother! That nickname is so ugly! Some people even call us the Caterpillar Siblings. You're not allowed to call me that anymore."

"Who said that?" The boy's voice turned cold.

"Don't be angry. They were just joking. And Zhou Yuhao helped me teach them a lesson, so you don't need to get involved."

Under the light of the morning sun, the two figures walked unhurriedly toward the location of the Spirit Root Testing Ceremony.

Along the way, laughter like silver bells occasionally scattered in the air.

(Chapter End)
