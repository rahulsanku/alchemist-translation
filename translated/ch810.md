# Chapter 810: A Single Sword Slays the Demon, So It Was Him

"Under Lord Pingshan's command, Seven Rhino comes to knock at the pass!"

The wild laughter rumbled and rolled across the entirety of Lingtian Pass.

Countless cultivators in the city raised their heads to gaze at that enormous multicolored rhinoceros, their eyes filled with utter despair.

The defensive light curtain enveloping the great city let out an extremely harsh grating sound as he descended continuously.

Some of the weaker cultivators fainted from the mere influence of his voice alone.

Such momentum, such savagery—truly terrifying!

However!

A cheerful laugh rang out, instantly smoothing away the fear that the beast's roar had brought to people's hearts, filling countless hearts with a sense of security.

"Fellow Daoist Seven Rhino, such rudeness is hardly the way of a guest!"

It was the voice of the Lingtian Pass City Lord.

Accompanying this voice was a sudden change in the grand formation of Lingtian Pass.

The originally unchanging light curtain abruptly came alive, like a water curtain covering the sky.

The seven-colored rhinoceros forcibly pressed downward. The water curtain merely sank, but the deeper it sank, the tougher it became. Light curtains from all directions surged over like waves, instead attempting to wrap around his massive body.

*Only by not contending can no one in the world contend with you!*

The Seven Rhino Commander, who had originally wanted to shatter the Lingtian Pass grand formation with lightning speed, only now understood his own ignorance.

How could the protective formation of such a mighty city be broken by him alone?

At least four more of his caliber, or even more, would be needed to forcibly break the formation with brute strength.

"Today, a direct assault won't work."

In an instant, the Seven Rhino Commander made his decision.

His four hooves stamped in succession as he flew upward.

The surrounding currents, like chains, covered his body, trying to drag him down.

He was not afraid at all. The seven-colored patterns on his skin seemed to come alive, constantly shedding force.

But even so, his escape speed was far slower than his divine descent earlier.

Clearly, the Lingtian Pass City Lord would not just watch him leave.

"Sword, rise!"

The cheerful voice sounded like a decree.

From the vast water curtain, from the four directions of southeast, northwest, and northeast, four ancient-style swords simultaneously emerged.

Water droplets fell with a patter, and the swords gradually began to shine.

"Go!"

In the unseen, one sword suddenly broke free of the light curtain, expanding to a thousand feet in size.

The giant sword tilted toward the sky, and ten thousand miles of dark clouds instantly collapsed, turning into gray cloud clusters that fled in all directions like gray dandelions.

The sword tip slanted, then slashed downward.

*Boom!*

Only a single great sound was heard, and heaven and earth shook.

A seven-colored radiance burst forth violently, spreading across the entire sky.

Before the wind and waves could settle, a miserable howl rang out.

"Flee!"

A tiny black dot, barely visible to the eye, shot out from the center of the storm, fleeing in utter disgrace toward the far east.

The Lingtian Pass City Lord, clad in a green robe, stood above the City Lord's Mansion, watching this scene with regret.

He was ultimately not proficient in formations and could not simultaneously control four profound swords, thus failing to achieve complete success.

However!

"You may escape, but the others cannot!"

He formed a sword gesture with his fingers and traced lightly in the air with one hand.

That giant sword suddenly split apart, turning into four smaller swords that flew toward the four cardinal directions.

Midway, the four swords split again, unexpectedly producing three more.

Seven flying swords in total, each locking onto one of the invading demon emperors.

*Shiiing! Shiiing! Shiiing!*

The sound of piercing air was unending.

Shichamo, who was fighting Qin Baixiang, showed terror in his eyes. Completely disregarding Qin Baixiang's attack, his whole body shook.

*Whoosh!*

A streak of spiritual light shot out from his body, teleporting a thousand miles away in the blink of an eye.

And the next moment, that small sword pierced through his body.

Qin Baixiang snorted coldly. "Abandoning your body to flee—you got off easy."

At the same time.

In the northern district, the two demon emperors besieging Xiao Shiniang felt the fear of death approaching as two flying swords came at them.

Without a second thought, the two demons revealed their true forms in the vast mist.

One had white feathers and a golden crown, with two feet and two wings, resembling a giant rooster.

The other had cracks all over its body, numbering in the thousands, with an extremely bizarre appearance.

Xiao Shiniang withdrew, and upon seeing the two demon emperors revealing their true forms, she raised an eyebrow.

"Golden-Crowned White Pheasant!"

"Thousand-Land Howling Insect!"

After revealing their true forms, the two demon emperors each responded differently.

One shrieked and charged into the sky; the other frantically tried to burrow into the earth.

The one charging upward was too slow—a single sword strike took its head.

The one burrowing into the ground was blocked by the formation—a single sword pierced its body. After a brief stalemate, a thousand sword lights burst out from its cracks.

Both ended with their souls utterly destroyed, their nascent souls gone!

Only the vast mana from their shattered nascent souls formed a violent spiritual storm, sweeping across the sky above Lingtian Pass.

The formation's water curtain flowed, absorbing these spiritual storms in a few breaths.

Everything seemed as if nothing had happened.

Seeing this, Xiao Shiniang's eyes filled with fear, and she swallowed deeply.

"Is this the formation that the Nether Origin Sect set up back then?"

In her shock, she spread her divine sense toward the southern district.

There, the night was dark and lightless, with faint green fireflies dotting the scene.

After the sword light flashed, the night suddenly dispersed, and the green fireflies fell.

"Did one escape?"

Xiao Shiniang murmured. In her sight, a deep black light fled Lingtian Pass without regard for anything, countless drops of blood spilling from its body.

Then, she shifted her gaze.

Crossing a vast thousand miles, at the western edge of the city, thunder and lightning flashed, revealing a powerful momentum.

A sword light was fiercely battling a divine black roc that had unfurled sky-covering wings.

With just one glance, Xiao Shiniang's pupils contracted.

"Was the opponent that Luo Chen of the Pill Sect faced earlier actually this strong?"

...

West of Lingtian Pass.

When that flying sword came, Luo Chen knew there was nothing left for him to do.

But he still did not retreat. Instead, he stood in the air, locking onto the Thunder Prison Roc with his divine sense.

"If I want to leave, none of you can stop me!"

The Thunder Prison Roc shouted fiercely. His three-zhang human body suddenly shook, and amidst endless thunder light, he revealed his enormous true form.

Facing the flying sword that had split off from the protective formation, he was not afraid at all. He reached out a claw and grasped it.

*Crackle! Pop!*

Countless arcs of electricity moved with his body.

The flying sword swayed continuously, cutting down large swaths of electric light.

After several exchanges, the sword light became dim and dull, no longer a threat to him.

Seeing this, the Thunder Prison Roc laughed wildly.

But the laughter abruptly stopped when he saw one of the remaining three suspended black swords slowly rise!

He took a deep look at Luo Chen, then without hesitation charged toward the nine heavens above.

After he left, the turbulent Lingtian Pass seemed to fall silent in an instant.

The Lingtian Pass City Lord showed slight regret.

A thunder-attribute demon beast, incredibly fast, and clearly also skilled in body refinement.

If only Junior Brother Zhang were here.

All thunder cultivators in the world would pale nine parts in comparison before him!

He exhaled a mouthful of turbid qi, manipulated the formation, and the remaining three profound swords quietly sank back into it.

Outside the city, countless beasts, like a tidal wave, were rolling in.

He chuckled lightly, instantly understanding the Seven Rhino Commander's plan.

Eight demon emperors, forcibly breaking the Lingtian Pass protective formation.

Then, with the demon army's charge, they wanted to directly crush Lingtian Pass.

He had to say, it was too simple, too reckless, and too hasty.

"Cultivators in the city, go out and kill the demons!"

With one command, the eastern city gate swung open, and countless cultivators flew out like a flood, charging toward the beasts covering the mountains and plains.

...

At the western city.

Luo Chen slowly descended from the sky onto the city wall, a thoughtful look on his face.

"Did the Thunder Prison Roc recognize me?"

"Maybe he recognized me, maybe not. But that's not important. What matters is that in this largest human-demon war in the Eastern Wilderness since ancient times, Green Parasol Mountain has joined in!"

He remembered very clearly that when the Thunder Prison Roc announced himself, he said, "Under the command of the Green Parasol Mountain Yuanjun!"

Clearly, the word "Yuanjun" was not a Daoist title given out of flattery like Nine Spirit Yuanjun, but rather that Rosycloud who was suspected of fighting the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining a thousand years ago!

If even she was involved, could the Nether Origin Sect really achieve its goal of unifying the Eastern Wilderness?

Luo Chen couldn't be sure.

He also didn't know what injuries Rosycloud had sustained in that life-and-death battle that caused even the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining to fall.

Had she recovered after a thousand years?

"In any case, such a peerless powerhouse probably wouldn't care about a nobody like me!"

Luo Chen exhaled a mouthful of turbid qi and swept his gaze around.

Zeng Yilong and Yang Pingdu were both leading Luo Tian Sect cultivators to guard around him.

"It's fine. Everyone, disperse!"

Zeng Yilong asked with some concern, "Uncle-Master, are you injured?"

Although their realms were insufficient, they could still see the big picture clearly.

The Lingtian Pass City Lord had retreated the seven-colored rhinoceros with one sword.

And the sword light that had split off from that sword was ground down by the Thunder Prison Roc with vast mana.

Such an enemy had been faced alone by Luo Chen.

That was enough to make them worry.

To this, Luo Chen waved his hand.

"No harm. That demon was quite courteous. When fighting me, he didn't go for the kill. I am completely unharmed."

Hearing this, everyone took it as Luo Chen being modest, but their hearts were calmed nonetheless.

Many people had stereotypes about alchemists.

They felt that although their realms were high, their combat skills were not particularly strong among their peers.

Luo Chen had made his name as the Pill Sect, so others naturally had this stereotype about him.

If he were still using Daoist titles like Fire Spirit Lord, Azure Yang Demon Lord, or even Wilderness Wanderer, low-level cultivators probably wouldn't think that way.

Yang Pingdu tentatively asked, "The Lingtian Pass City Lord just ordered that a large number of demon beasts are attacking from the eastern district, commanding the city's cultivators to go out and kill them. Should we go?"

Luo Chen twitched the corner of his mouth. "No need. Just guard this small patch of the western side."

With that, he drifted away.

...

Inside a cave in the Fire Ridge.

The Seven Rhino Commander sat cross-legged, both hands pressed against a woman's back.

He exhaled and shouted, "Go!"

The black-clothed woman was struck as if by a heavy blow, her whole body shaking.

Under the impact of vast mana, a wisp of sword qi sprayed out from within her.

The other woman, dressed in dark gold attire, saw this and her pupils contracted. She hurriedly dodged the wisp of sword qi, letting it strike the ground and sink deep into the rock layer.

*Pfft!*

The black-clothed woman spat out a mouthful of blood, but her complexion visibly improved.

"Wuguang, are you alright?" Anming asked with concern.

Wuguang wiped the blood from her mouth and said with relief, "I'm fine. I saved my life thanks to my clan's innate demon art. It's just a pity about Youying."

Anming sighed. "Not just Youying. Chenguan and Luhou didn't survive either. If I hadn't seen the situation was wrong and retreated immediately, I probably wouldn't have fared much better. Shichamo is strong enough, right? Even he lost his physical body. I saw him cut off his tail like a gecko, abandoning his body to flee."

Wuguang's eyes held fear. "Is the human formation truly so invincible?"

Unlike many more active demon emperors, Wuguang and Youying had always dwelled deep in the Million Great Mountains. If not for this summons by Lord Ditian, they might never have dealt with human cultivators in their lives.

They had only heard that human cultivators mastered the hundred arts of cultivation and countless techniques.

But that was limited to those killing arts. They had never imagined that a formation built around a city could have such terrifying power.

Anming said nothing, his gaze subtly glancing at the Seven Rhino Commander.

On the horn atop his head, there was clearly a deep sword scar.

It had almost cut that horn off!

And Seven Rhino had withstood a complete sword strike!

Seeming to sense his subordinate's gaze, the Seven Rhino Commander, who was regulating his breath, slowly opened his eyes.

His face was iron-blue, his tone heavy.

"It's not your fault. This battle was my recklessness."

Wuguang, who had just been saved by Seven Rhino, couldn't help but say, "Commander..."

Seven Rhino interrupted her and looked at Anming.

"Where is Shichamo?"

Anming replied, "He didn't come back with me. He just said that the price for helping you this time was too great, and the promise he owed you is now void."

Hearing this, the Seven Rhino Commander showed no reaction.

It was normal for the other party not to return. After all, he had lost his physical body, leaving only a nascent soul. If he appeared before them, he would be at their mercy.

As for the other promise the other party still owed him...

"Void it is!"

The Seven Rhino Commander sighed faintly.

After this battle, of the ten demon emperors under his command, three were dead and one was injured.

Although six remained, those six were not very obedient to his orders. Thus, among the nine commanders, he instantly fell to the bottom.

"I was too eager for merit."

"The formation at Lingtian Pass requires at least four commander-level demon emperors to withstand. To break it by force, at least five or more are needed."

"If I want to avoid paying a price myself, I can only use a slow, grinding approach to exhaust its power bit by bit."

"The first merit for breaking the city won't go to me, but it won't go to any of them either!"

After thinking it over, he instructed, "Anming, go to the front line and bring back the low-level demons that charged Lingtian Pass... Given the Lingtian Pass City Lord's methods, there probably aren't many left. Bring back as many as you can!"

His tone had dropped considerably by the end.

Anming accepted the order and was about to leave.

"Watch your own safety!"

Anming paused in her steps. "Thank you for your concern, Commander!"

She set off again.

After she left, a flash of lightning and thunder suddenly appeared in the cave.

"Seven Rhino, how did you end up so miserable?"

A flash of anger crossed the Seven Rhino Commander's face. "If you had faced that sword directly, you probably wouldn't have survived! And if you hadn't given enough pressure at the western city, how could the Lingtian Pass City Lord have calmly activated the formation and killed three demon emperors under my command!"

Speaking of this, the sharp qi on his horn came faintly again, making him even angrier.

"I thought we had a good relationship. Back then, when you sought my clan's Thunder Marsh method, I lent it to you for a look. Today, entrusted by me, why didn't you do your best?"

The Thunder Prison Roc's massive body hunched, disliking the crampedness of the cave.

Faced with his friend's interrogation, he frowned but knew he hadn't done well.

He could only defend himself with one sentence. "The one guarding the western city is strange."

"Strange? What strangeness?"

"I don't know."

"You!" The Seven Rhino Commander pointed angrily at the Thunder Prison Roc.

The Thunder Prison Roc said gruffly, "I owe you one this time. I'll repay it later! But for now, I have important business to report back to Azure Frost Hall Master, so I came specifically to take my leave."

The Seven Rhino Commander felt a surge of irritation and waved his hand.

"Go!"

The Thunder Prison Roc said nothing more, hurriedly left the cave, and shot into the sky.

When he reached the sky, he looked down into the distance.

On the plains outside Lingtian Pass, there was a bloody wind and rain—a one-sided massacre.

East of the Fire Ridge, rolling demon qi came from the east, with a golden-yellow banner standing in the middle.

"Yellow Barbarian is here too! Things are probably going to be even harder for Seven Rhino now."

A trace of guilt rose in his heart, but the image of that white-robed figure kept flashing through his mind.

Suddenly!

The Thunder Prison Roc's swift flight came to an abrupt halt.

That calm, indifferent, unflappable face overlapped with another face in his memory—one that was timid, fawning, and humble.

"So it was him!"

In the clear sky, thunder suddenly crashed.
