# Chapter 1073: The Clone Forms a Nascent Soul, the Spring Eye Emerges

The path to forming a Nascent Soul is nothing more than the unification of the Three Treasures and the convergence of the Five Qi.

The so-called Three Treasures are essence, qi, and spirit!

They correspond respectively to the fleshly body's blood essence, dharma power and spiritual qi, and the soul and spirit.

One takes the essential marrow of these Three Treasures, fuses them into one, and forms a spiritual embryo with the golden core. Once the embryo stirs, one can shatter the core and form the Nascent Soul, setting foot on the Great Dao of the Sage stage!

Long before participating in the Sexagenary Grand Competition, Zhou Fuyou had already thoroughly learned the relevant knowledge about breaking through to the Sage stage from his master, Fairy Bluejade.

Especially following the guidance of his master and the old ancestor, Zhou Fuyou had very early on begun to consciously coordinate the Three Treasures of essence, qi, and spirit.

When he was at the ninth layer of the Adept stage, the spiritual embryo within his body had already taken on a proper form.

Now, having come to the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, as the days of quiet cultivation passed one by one, his realm was also climbing upward, step by step, with solidity.

In his spare time, he would look inward at his own body.

Within his qi sea, his dharma power was vast as an ocean, with two spirit pearls, one large and one small.

The small one was his lifebound dharma treasure, the Sea-Settling Pearl, which had the reputation of calming the oceans and suppressing the heavens. What was rare and valuable was that this pearl, at the moment of its formation, had already undergone refinement by thunder tribulation, reaching the grade of a true artifact.

At this moment, the compact Sea-Settling Pearl was spinning nimbly around the larger spirit pearl, appearing very affectionate.

Because that larger spirit pearl was Zhou Fuyou's golden core!

This golden core was different from an ordinary person's. Its entire body was pitch black, containing not a single impurity, as if it could absorb all light.

And because of that profound blackness, it seemed as if it could create something from nothing, giving birth to the myriad things of the universe.

When this golden core could no longer absorb any more dharma power, an inexplicable sensation emerged from within Zhou Fuyou's consciousness.

*Thump!*

The embryo stirred!

The spiritual embryo trembled, shattering the core to form the Nascent Soul—this was the symbolic opportunity for forming a Nascent Soul.

For a normal person, at this time, they would suppress the urge to break through based on the experiences of predecessors, instead stabilizing their realm. Then, step by step, they would familiarize themselves with this feeling, and through the assistance of a large amount of Nascent Soul-forming resources, strive to break through to the Sage stage in one go.

At the very least, they would first prepare the means to survive the Sage tribulation lightning.

But Zhou Fuyou cast aside all the experiences of his predecessors.

He had an intuition: the Dao is naturally formed, so why must one restrain oneself?

Everything, follow one's own heart!

With a thought, the blood qi of his entire body surged violently towards the golden core within him. His originally thin and gaunt body became even more withered.

Vast dharma power, centered on Zhou Fuyou's body, spread outward in ring after ring.

From the initial state of ripples, to the surging waves and tides that followed, the momentum grew greater and greater.

Amidst this continuous fluctuation, the entire heaven and earth seemed to synchronize with him to the same frequency and rhythm.

*Boom!*

Within the Three Cascading Waterfall, that fourth-order spirit vein suddenly erupted with a massive amount of spiritual qi, sweeping across a radius of a hundred li.

The flying waterfall, like a bolt of white silk, rolled back a thousand zhang, transforming into a silver-white giant dragon.

Atop the giant dragon's head, the figure of the withered young man had already sat down cross-legged, riding the waves into the sky, straight up to the nine heavens.

*Crackle!*

In the void, without anyone knowing when, dark clouds had already gathered densely. Thin, silver-white electric serpents shuttled back and forth within them, an utterly terrifying sight.

Such a peculiar scene—any cultivator could react and realize that this was the heavenly tribulation of a golden core cultivator breaking through to the Sage stage.

But anyone would be extremely shocked at how easily Zhou Fuyou had formed a spiritual embryo and triggered the lightning tribulation.

From beginning to end, he had not relied on any external objects.

Whether it was the Nascent Soul-forming pills his master had prepared for him, or the Nascent Soul-forming resource rewards obtained from being the top scorer in the Sexagenary Grand Competition, Zhou Fuyou had not used any of them.

It was simply that he felt it was time, and then it was time.

The lightning tribulation, in an instant, was fully brewed.

Unlike ordinary cultivators undergoing tribulation, this tribulation had no probing actions whatsoever.

The moment Zhou Fuyou appeared, a bolt of lightning as thick as a water bucket crashed down!

Zhou Fuyou's expression was calm. He casually raised a hand.

The thousand-zhang waterfall beneath his feet transformed into a water dragon, roaring as it charged towards the lightning.

*Boom!*

An ear-splitting sound rang out. Zhou Fuyou was unmoved, merely raising an eyebrow.

"Is the lightning tribulation only this powerful?"

From the Dao Sect's scriptures, he knew that a cultivator undergoes a total of only three heavenly tribulations in their lifetime, with three rounds each time, totaling nine rounds. Hence, it was also called the Three-Nine Heavenly Tribulation.

But this first heavenly tribulation, in Zhou Fuyou's view, was completely unworthy of fear.

"Perhaps the later ones will be stronger. If there is a manifested lightning beast as the old ancestor mentioned, that would be even more wonderful."

Thinking of this, Zhou Fuyou not only felt no fear but even showed an expression of anticipation.

He did not have to wait long. The second round of heavenly tribulation arrived as scheduled.

This time, five bolts of lightning struck down.

Five thunderbolts crashing down from above, heaven and earth facing utter destruction.

Zhou Fuyou remained calm. He simply brought his hands together in a circle, and a clear water mirror emerged.

The water mirror was laid flat, and his hands slowly pushed upward.

The five bolts of lightning smashed squarely onto the azure water mirror, splashing up layers of spray.

*Crack, crack...*

The sounds of shattering came one after another.

When the azure water mirror shattered, the five bolts of lightning also lost their momentum at the same time. Zhou Fuyou merely waved his sleeve, dispersing the fine arcs of electricity.

Having so easily weathered two rounds of lightning tribulation in succession, even heaven and earth seemed enraged.

From within the dense dark clouds came a suppressed, restless, muffled roar.

This time, Zhou Fuyou finally became serious.

Within his seriousness, there was also a hint of anticipation.

When four beastly feet, like heavenly pillars, emerged from the thick dark clouds, Zhou Fuyou's hanging heart finally settled.

The manifested lightning beast had arrived!

Legend had it that only those with exceptionally good aptitude and extremely deep foundations would attract the terrifying manifested lightning beast during their Sage tribulation.

The lethality of such an existence was extremely terrifying, far exceeding the level that an early-stage Sage could handle.

It was a power meant to annihilate the tribulation-crosser!

But if this power was absorbed and utilized by the tribulation-crosser, its upper limit was also far higher than ordinary killing techniques.

For this point, the old ancestor had long been prepared.

And Zhou Fuyou had an additional auxiliary method, determined to turn the lightning tribulation to his own use.

His entire body shook. Strands of black qi emerged from his body, and surging dark water flowed within them.

The art of Qi Sea Floating Heaven appeared once more, forming another black canopy centered around him.

It shone in mutual radiance with the thick lightning clouds.

When the lightning beast, resembling a turtle or tortoise, revealed its form within the lightning clouds, Zhou Fuyou let out a low shout.

"Come!"

The manifested lightning turtle moved its four feet, roaring as it charged towards Zhou Fuyou. It did not dodge or evade the grim canopy formed by the Qi Sea Floating Heaven, plunging straight into it.

Wherever it passed, this representative killing technique of Zhou Fuyou's began to retreat step by step, seemingly completely unable to resist.

At that moment!

Zhou Fuyou opened his mouth and spat out a pearl. It flew out, spinning nimbly.

Treasure light flickered. The young man pointed a finger.

"Still!"

A terrifying power of suppression poured out, causing even the lightning turtle formed by the heavenly tribulation to freeze for an instant.

This instant of opportunity was exactly what Zhou Fuyou needed!

He formed hand seals with both hands. Within the immeasurable qi sea, the purest dark water transformed into numerous flood dragons, pouncing on the lightning turtle. Like chains, they tightly wrapped and bound its four feet, torso, and even its head.

A large amount of qi currents surged, highly spiritual as they drilled into the lightning turtle's body, which was entirely composed of lightning.

The lightning turtle was enraged, struggling to move forward, but with the qi currents disintegrating its body from within, the dark water flood dragons binding it from without, and Zhou Fuyou continuously urging the Sea-Settling Pearl, it could not advance an inch.

Even more, its body began to continuously disintegrate, transforming into the most primitive and pure power of lightning tribulation.

"As long as I integrate this lightning tribulation power into the Qi Sea Floating Heaven, this killing technique will truly have the prototype of a divine ability!"

"The old ancestor's vision will be perfected by me!"

"From now on, at the Sage realm, I will have the astonishing means of 'my heart is the heart of heaven, driving thunder and commanding lightning'!"

Zhou Fuyou clenched his teeth, unconcerned about the consumption of his dharma power, continuously disintegrating and absorbing the power of the manifested lightning turtle.

The dense dark clouds rolled wildly, seemingly enraged. However, the three rounds of heavenly tribulation had already passed. It was powerless, and could only watch helplessly as the manifested lightning turtle gradually dissipated.

"Gather!"

With only a light shout, the strands of qi currents crackling with arcs of electricity retracted back into Zhou Fuyou's body.

He looked up. The dense dark clouds had gradually dispersed, replaced by a rain of spiritual qi falling like a torrential downpour.

And on the ground, golden lotuses formed from condensed spiritual qi also broke through the earth, converging towards Zhou Fuyou.

Golden lotuses surging from the earth, heavenly nectar pouring down to crown the head!

Zhou Fuyou smiled slightly and formed a Daoist hand seal.

The golden core within his body collapsed and shattered.

He let out a muffled groan, and then, within the golden core, a small figure appeared.

This small figure was initially no bigger than a thumb, but after swallowing the golden core, it began to grow robustly.

Seeming to feel that the food was still insufficient, the small figure flickered, emerged from Zhou Fuyou's body, and looked at the outside world with pleasant surprise.

Those spiritual qi raindrops and spiritual qi golden lotuses were the best food.

The small figure flew onto a golden lotus and sat down cross-legged, letting the massive amount of spiritual qi raindrops fall upon its body.

A feeling of joy and pleasure arose spontaneously. There was also a fleeting illusion, as if returning to the mother's womb.

In this daze, the Nascent Soul small figure seemed to remember something.

It reached out a hand. A stream of light flew out from the storage ring on the physical body and was swallowed by it in one gulp.

It was precisely the fourth-order illusion core obtained from killing the fourth-order flood dragon illusion beast earlier, which contained a thread of spiritual opportunity.

The time of forming a Nascent Soul was the most dangerous, because externally there was the threat of heavenly tribulation, and internally there was the simultaneous pressure of condensing the Three Treasures of essence, qi, and spirit.

But what the world did not know was that the time of forming a Nascent Soul was also the closest a cultivator would ever be to heaven and earth in their entire life.

Having weathered the lightning tribulation, the barrier between heaven and earth was temporarily gone. And because the Purple Mansion barrier had not yet formed, at this moment, the Nascent Soul and heaven and earth were almost completely fused together.

Back when Luo Chen formed his Nascent Soul, he had a similar feeling. But he knew the phenomenon without knowing the reason, and subconsciously missed it.

In fact, even if Luo Chen had realized this point, there was nothing he could do, because he was still missing one thing.

Spiritual opportunity!

And Zhou Fuyou happened to have spiritual opportunity in his hand.

Although it was not much, only a single thread.

But it was already enough!

The illusion core entered his body. The Nascent Soul small figure slowly closed its eyes, letting this heaven and earth wrap around it.

Vaguely, he sensed the operating laws of the world—a web woven from interwoven strands of principles. Above the principles, there were also Dao rules governing everything.

The Dao rules were too vast, like a heavy hammer that a child could not lift. But the principles were like balls of thread, which a child could seemingly pull out a strand from while playing.

"Which strand to pull from?"

Countless threads crisscrossed and intertwined. He could not find the end of the thread, and had no idea where to start.

Time passed, unknown for how long...

Suddenly!

The Nascent Soul small figure abruptly opened its eyes. Its gaze was burning, and even somewhat terrified, as it stared at its own physical body.

There, there was a clear, even complete thread!

He, Zhou Fuyou, had been carrying a heavy treasure all along without knowing it?

...

At the center of the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven.

On a vast plain, dozens of figures were each sitting cross-legged in mysterious positions.

Beneath them were crisscrossing lines of light patterns.

If one looked down from a high altitude, it was clearly the layout of a chessboard.

And these people each occupied a node of the chessboard, each roughly controlling one-tenth of the board's range.

At the very center, an old man with graying temples, aged to the extreme, sat like withered wood. The position he occupied was the *Heavenly Origin*—the central point of the chessboard.

Suddenly!

The old man's heavy eyelids blinked. The dormant dharma power on his body stirred slightly.

Sensing his abnormality, someone asked with concern, "Senior Brother Ke, what's wrong?"

The old man slowly opened his eyes, gazing into the distance.

"Among the disciples who entered the grotto-heaven for cultivation this time, are there any with exceptionally outstanding aptitude?"

"Reporting to Senior Brother Ke, the ten who entered the grotto-heaven are all outstanding talents. Especially Zhou Fuyou, who possesses the Profound Dark Dao Body. He can be called the one who stands out from the rest!"

The old man seemed to be thinking, murmuring to himself, "Then it should be him."

The others did not understand.

But soon, following the old man's light, gratifying remark—"He has formed his Nascent Soul"—everyone suddenly understood.

Then, sounds of praise came one after another, unceasing.

Everyone knew of Zhou Fuyou's talent and aptitude, but having entered the grotto-heaven for only a little over thirty years, he had already formed his Nascent Soul.

If placed in the outside world, he could even be hailed as forming a Nascent Soul in a hundred years!

Such a terrifying cultivation speed, looking across the Mountains and Seas Realm for millions of years, was probably unprecedented.

"Junior Brother Li, please invite him over! Although this spiritual treasure, the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard, has long acknowledged me as its master, I am ultimately not an Ascendant great power. When the time comes to fully activate it, I must rely on all of your strength. Since Zhou Fuyou has formed his Nascent Soul, he can also serve as a reserve force, lending a little assistance when you all cannot hold on."

The Sage cultivator addressed by the old man as Junior Brother Li was precisely that Uncle Li who had been present when they entered the grotto-heaven.

He came from the Li family, one of the Twelve Great Houses. His realm was not considered high, around the third layer of the Sage stage. But he had a wide circle of acquaintances and excellent interpersonal relationships. Whether with superiors or subordinates, most people knew him.

Having received the order, Uncle Li did not decline. He mounted his flying light and headed in the direction the old man had indicated.

In fact, the distance between the two sides was not too far, after all, the Three Cascading Waterfall itself was considered an inner area of the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven.

When Uncle Li arrived, the Three Cascading Waterfall had already returned to its usual calm, with only a continuous, drizzling rain falling.

Uncle Li surveyed the surroundings. He did not see any of the devastation that usually followed a cultivator's tribulation-crossing. He felt that this junior was indeed extraordinary in strength, having easily weathered the tribulation alone.

It was the old burn marks and sword scars on the exposed mountain rocks that made Uncle Li sigh with emotion.

"These should be the traces left behind when the Wilderness Wanderer Luo Chen single-handedly contended with Shi Ju, Gu Yuan, Gu Daosu, and others during the five-hundred-year grotto-heaven grand competition back then. I never thought that after so many years, they still haven't been worn away by time."

Amidst his sighs, Uncle Li had already detected the traces of the formation Zhou Fuyou had set up.

He walked forward politely, opened his mouth to call out, but stopped just as the words reached his lips.

Zhou Fuyou had just formed his Nascent Soul. He should be in urgent need of consolidating his realm. A rash interruption might affect him.

Senior Brother Ke was not in a hurry either. It would be fine to simply leave a letter to inform him.

Taking out a sound transmission talisman, Uncle Li recorded a divine sense message, then sent it in. After circling briefly, it leisurely returned.

And within the cave dwelling inside the Three Cascading Waterfall, Zhou Fuyou was still sitting cross-legged on his meditation cushion.

Strangely, his Nascent Soul was manifested outside, its gaze fixed intently on his own physical body.

"Why is there a spring eye sealed within my physical body? And a spring eye containing the origin of water!"

"Is this the origin of my Profound Dark Dao Body constitution?"

"Who did this?"

"Father, mother... or the old ancestor?"

Zhou Fuyou could not understand. And at the moment his Nascent Soul returned to his physical body, the seal on that spring eye collapsed like a bursting dam, unraveling at a visible speed.

In that instant, boundless, vast power filled Zhou Fuyou's body.

He looked at his own hand. When he gently clenched it, it was as if he held rivers, lakes, and seas in his grasp.

He raised his head to look at the dome of the cave dwelling. His gaze seemed to pierce through the stone walls, seeing the drizzling curtain of rain.

"Disperse!"

He murmured under his breath. The dense rain clouds in the sky scattered in an instant.

Such a means of grasping the changes in the great momentum of heaven and earth was no longer something an ordinary Sage could master.

Zhou Fuyou fell into silence.

(Chapter End)
