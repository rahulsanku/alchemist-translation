# Chapter 1171: Backhanded Slaughter, Breaking Through in Ten Years

No one had expected this!

Today's excitement was about to become even more exciting.

Luo Chen's interference was unexpected. Purple Haze City Lord's concession was within reason. And after exchanging for the cultivation stone, Luo Chen's attitude remained arrogant. Facing the "senior" Qu Laoguai, who had long since entered the Nine Rings of Rupture for cultivation, he directly issued a challenge.

This series of changes was full of drama.

Qu Laoguai looked at the emboldened Luo Chen, feeling somewhat uneasy.

His eyes rolled, and he suddenly said, "Luo Chen, this old man entered the Nine Rings of Rupture for cultivation two hundred years ago, so I can be considered your senior. If we truly fight, word will spread that I'm bullying the younger generation."

"Senior?" Luo Chen smiled mockingly.

Qu Laoguai pretended not to hear the contempt in Luo Chen's words and continued, "How about this: give me one of the Meridian-Repairing Pills you traded to Purple Haze City Lord, and this cultivation stone is yours. Allow you to cultivate for ten years, and I'll come back to challenge you next time. That way, it won't be said that I'm bullying the younger."

Hearing these words, everyone exchanged bewildered glances.

He clearly wanted the cultivation stone desperately, yet he was concerned about his reputation and willing to concede?

Even if the Meridian-Repairing Pill was extremely precious, now that things had come to this point and both sides had been forced into a corner, how could there be any room for retreat!

But for those truly familiar with Qu Laoguai's way of doing things, this might be his true nature.

On the surface, he was arrogant and aggressive, but in reality, his actions were extremely prudent.

He never stuck his neck out for things he wasn't sure about.

Even for things he was confident about, if he could resolve them with words or achieve his goals through interest exchange, he would rather not make a move.

Just like earlier!

Facing Purple Haze City Lord, who had been knocked down from the Third Ring, he had a considerable advantage.

But before the battle, he still used various words to shake Purple Haze City Lord's will, wanting to subdue the enemy without fighting.

If Luo Chen hadn't suddenly jumped out, it would be hard not to suspect that Qu Laoguai might have already prepared some resources to exchange for Purple Haze City Lord's cultivation stone through resource trading.

This current speech was nothing more than advancing as a means of retreat.

Without understanding Luo Chen's background, he would rather retreat temporarily and make a comeback ten years later.

If it were someone else, they would probably agree.

But he had encountered Luo Chen!

"Hahaha..."

Clear laughter spread through the void, the undisguised arrogance highlighting Luo Chen's contempt for Qu Laoguai.

Under this laughter, Qu Laoguai's expression gradually turned ugly.

"Luo Chen, what do you mean?"

The laughter abruptly stopped.

Luo Chen stared coldly at the other party and said in a frigid tone, "What do you think you are, to deserve a pill from me?"

"You!"

Qu Laoguai was furious and was about to say something more, but Luo Chen had already grown tired of bickering with him.

Luo Chen raised his sleeve, and the true qi within him surged like clouds.

The next moment!

A sky-covering giant claw abruptly emerged from the true qi clouds, reaching straight for Qu Laoguai.

Its momentum was fierce, its speed swift.

Qu Laoguai instinctively struck out with a palm, his fingers splitting into five, which suddenly transformed into five snow-white giant pythons—his signature move of true qi materialization.

The five giant pythons flicked their tongues, winding upward, pouncing on the sky-covering giant claw from various angles.

They wanted to entangle, wanted to block.

Between their fanged jaws, a foul wind gusted, as if trying to corrode and devour the giant claw.

However!

At the moment of their collision, a scene that left everyone dumbfounded appeared.

The five giant pythons, without distinction of order, were torn apart alive by the giant claw at almost the same instant.

The remaining force of the claw, undiminished, continued to grab toward Qu Laoguai.

"What kind of technique is this?"

Qu Laoguai's heart was filled with shock and anger, but his footwork was not slow as he hurriedly retreated.

During his escape, he continuously unleashed killing moves, trying to neutralize this giant claw.

But whether it was his signature killing moves or his trump cards, they were all so fragile before that giant claw.

Or rather, was it not a difference in technique?

The onlookers had already vaguely sensed the crux.

Luo Chen's Cloud-Seizing Divine Claw had no fancy movements, nor any mysterious and profound changes. It was purely that the true qi condensing the giant claw was too overbearing.

The difference in essence created a battle scene like a knife cutting through tofu.

Qu Laoguai fled frantically, but the Cloud-Seizing Divine Claw moved faster and faster, grabbing at him from time to time in the air.

He was truly panicked.

"Luo Chen, you and I have no enmity, why must you be so ruthless?"

"Brother Luo, let's stop here!"

"Senior, spare my life!"

The last sentence was uttered after the Cloud-Seizing Divine Claw had caught him.

At this moment, Qu Laoguai no longer cared about the impact of the chaotic energy nearby and actively opened all his acupoints.

The furnace of qi and blood within him spun wildly, and streams of his lifebound true qi sprayed from his acupoints, forming an indestructible true qi shield.

However!

In the distance, Luo Chen only gave him a cold glance and suddenly closed his right hand.

Boom!

An explosion resounded all around.

It also shook the hearts of every martial artist watching.

Those who heard it couldn't help but tremble, looking with awe at the white-clothed man who hadn't moved a single step from beginning to end.

The Cloud-Seizing Divine Claw slowly withdrew, surrounded by some blood mist that had burst out when Qu Laoguai fell. The claw also held some miscellaneous items, finally hovering above Luo Chen's head.

The corners of Luo Chen's mouth lifted slightly, and he looked at everyone with a half-smile.

"Everyone, I have defeated the challenger. Does that mean no one will come to cause trouble for ten years?"

Everyone fell silent in fear.

Qu Laoguai, a martial artist at the middle stage of the Leakless Realm, had been effortlessly killed by Luo Chen like pinching an ant. Who would dare to cause him trouble?

Luo Chen asked again, "Your silence means you agree. But I find the ten-year interval too short, so I hope that even after ten years, no one will disturb me until I leave the Seventh Ring."

Purple Haze City Lord suddenly spoke up: "Brother Luo is absolutely right. Everyone came here to cultivate. Fighting and killing really spoils the mood."

Luo Chen gave her an approving look.

The others also came to their senses. This Luo Chen was a real killer.

Ordinary challenges, while not necessarily to the death, were too ruthless if they ended in slaughter.

Once provoked, such an existence would likely lead to a situation of no end until death.

Especially since the other party possessed the Meridian-Repairing Pill that everyone wanted. It was better not to offend him, just in case he was in a good mood and casually gave one away like before.

After realizing this, the originally oppressive atmosphere suddenly became lively.

"That Qu Laoguai really had no eyes, daring to offend Brother Luo."

"Yes, such a person who takes advantage of others' misfortune is naturally despised by us, and we won't imitate his actions."

"Brother Luo, just cultivate well without worrying about anything else."

Amidst the laughter, Luo Chen pressed his hand down in the air, and the atmosphere quieted down again.

Looking around at everyone, he said warmly:

"I know you all want the Meridian-Repairing Pill. The exchange is very simple. As long as you can produce Void Sprout Crystals, I can give you one. And not just the Meridian-Repairing Pill..."

At these words, everyone's eyes lit up.

"Alright, that's enough for today!"

Waving his hand, the crowd began to disperse in groups.

As for what they would discuss about Luo Chen after leaving, that was no longer his concern.

Only Purple Haze City Lord remained where she was.

"What, can't bear to part with this cultivation stone of yours?" Luo Chen asked with a half-smile.

Purple Haze City Lord quickly shook her head, "Since we've already exchanged, how would I dare to reap the benefits? I stayed only to inform Brother Luo of some information regarding this cultivation stone."

"Oh?" Luo Chen was slightly curious.

Purple Haze City Lord didn't play coy. She landed directly on the stone and then shot out a stream of true qi.

The next moment, the stone membrane covering the meteorite swelled up, enveloping an area of about ten feet.

"If Brother Luo feels tired from cultivation, you can drive this stone membrane to resist the erosion of chaotic energy and take a short rest."

Luo Chen looked at the stone membrane above his head, not paying much attention to it.

This protective shield, which was quite useful for ordinary martial artists, was practically useless to him. He even felt the chaotic energy concentration was insufficient.

But upon second thought, he nodded lightly.

"Thank you!"

Purple Haze City Lord wanted to say more to ingratiate herself, but seeing Luo Chen's expression of keeping people at a distance, she didn't stay long.

After saying her goodbyes, she drifted away.

Judging by her direction, she was probably heading for the cultivation stone left behind by Qu Laoguai.

After she left.

Luo Chen gazed at the stone membrane above his head, and the Blood-Refining True Qi on his body flowed out, replacing the true qi left by Purple Haze City Lord.

Under his control, the stone membrane quickly shrank until it was three feet in diameter.

Then Luo Chen took out the Vacuum Gu and the two-joint-sized Void Sprout Crystal he had obtained from the trading meeting.

These items were all placed within the stone membrane.

"When I was cultivating before, chaotic energy continuously surrounded me on all sides. It had a great impact on the Vacuum Gu, which couldn't be stored in the storage ring. For this reason, I had to pause its feeding."

"With this stone membrane as a shield, I can continue feeding it."

"How convenient!"

Smiling, Luo Chen began to inspect the belongings left by Qu Laoguai.

A technique manual called *Five Dragons Flourishing Spirit* was probably his main cultivation method. Its grade wasn't high, but the parts involving techniques were quite exquisite.

However, Qu Laoguai hadn't cultivated it well. His true qi materialization could only reach the level of five giant pythons, far from the might of five dragons at full mastery.

Luo Chen put it away. He could study it in his spare time, at least as a supplement to his basic attack methods.

There were also large quantities of aged medicinal herbs and gold beads.

Luo Chen didn't care about these things.

Instead, he felt a slight regret in his heart.

The Ninefold Immortal Tower was located below the Gate of Rupture, drawing the chaotic energy emitted by the Gate of Rupture to supply martial artists' cultivation.

During this process, special resources from other worlds were sometimes swept in by the chaotic energy.

For example, Immortal Origin Stones, and Void Sprout Crystals.

Martial artists who cultivated in the Nine Rings of Rupture for a long time would occasionally pick up some drifting rare treasures.

Even if they didn't recognize them, they would keep them as collectibles.

But unfortunately, the hundred-year period had just passed, and many martial artists had gone out to exchange those rare treasures for the cultivation resources they needed.

His gains from killing this enemy were not great!

Luo Chen's hand moved slightly, and the storage ring on his index finger approached these miscellaneous items, absorbing them all.

If this scene fell into the eyes of a cultivator in the Great Destruction Realm, they would surely exclaim in surprise.

Storage rings required magical power and even divine sense to activate.

But just now, Luo Chen had clearly not used any magical power, let alone divine sense.

In fact, the storage ring Luo Chen was currently using had long been modified by him in terms of its item-collecting logic.

As a formation master and also a skilled artifact refiner, when his divine sense revived, Luo Chen had already actively removed all the divine sense restrictions and magical power restrictions on the storage ring, instead modifying the storage ring's underlying logic with the simplest "blood drop recognition" method used in the cultivation world.

So now he could use qi and blood power to activate the storage ring.

Of course, these details only made his life more convenient, nothing worth mentioning.

After calming down, Luo Chen reviewed his earlier actions.

Although he appeared extremely domineering in front of others, it was essentially a well-thought-out move by Luo Chen.

First, he used the Meridian-Repairing Pill to exchange for Purple Haze City Lord's cultivation stone, thus bypassing the unspoken rules of Qu Laoguai challenging Purple Haze and avoiding the risk of forcefully seizing the cultivation stone in violation of the rules.

Then, with thunderous means, he killed Qu Laoguai to intimidate the others—killing the chicken to scare the monkeys.

Finally, he used words and inducements to appease everyone.

With these three consecutive moves, his occupation of this cultivation stone became perfectly justified.

And from now on, it was unlikely that anyone would dare to lightly offend him.

"Now, let's begin cultivation!"

Murmuring to himself, Luo Chen sat cross-legged, extending all six arms, assuming the cultivation posture of *God-Devil Formless*.

The acupoints on his body, under his control, opened one after another.

When the ninetieth acupoint opened, a feeling of fullness arose spontaneously.

Luo Chen knew this was the limit point of his control over internal and external balance.

From this, he could also judge the difference between the Seventh Ring and the Ninth Ring.

In the Ninth Ring, all seven hundred and twenty acupoints had to be opened before this feeling of fullness appeared, and the nearby chaotic energy often only lasted a few days.

Now in the Seventh Ring, this feeling appeared after opening only ninety acupoints.

In other words, between the Seventh Ring and the Ninth Ring, the concentration of chaotic energy was at least eight times different!

This way, he didn't have to worry about insufficient external energy during cultivation.

And as his realm improved and he gradually adapted, he would open more and more acupoints, and his realm advancement would inevitably become faster and faster.

The decision to skip the Eighth Ring and come directly to the Seventh Ring seemed to have been the right one.

...

Cultivation knows no years; time flies in the blink of an eye.

Unknowingly, nine years passed in an instant.

On this day.

In the pitch-black void, thunder suddenly exploded.

On a cultivation stone, Luo Chen sat cross-legged like a pine tree. The Blood-Refining True Qi around him fluctuated unsteadily, even forcibly pushing away the chaotic energy.

And on his back, silver-white electric light flickered, as if a thunder dragon was coiled there.

As Luo Chen moved his qi and blood, the Blood-Refining True Qi gradually retracted into his body, and the acupoints around him also closed.

Swish!

Luo Chen suddenly opened his eyes, feeling as if he had broken through some bottleneck, but everything seemed effortless, as if it had come naturally.

He opened his attribute panel and looked at the realm column.

**[Leakless Realm 34/100]**

The corners of Luo Chen's mouth lifted slightly. This counted as breaking through to the middle stage of the Leakless Realm.

There had been no difficulty in the process.

The speed was fast, the efficiency high. Even though he had expected it, he was still overjoyed.

Ordinary martial artists would take a hundred years or even longer to break through a small realm, but he had only used ten years.

These ten years also included the process of familiarization and adaptation.

Especially since Luo Chen had spent time cultivating while also optimizing the *God-Devil Formless* technique he had created himself.

If he truly let loose and cultivated, the speed of breaking through realms would only be faster!

Fortunately, the optimization of *God-Devil Formless* had reached perfection, and he could truly let loose and cultivate from now on.

"It's time to change to another cultivation environment."

"But before that, I need to find someone to ask about the situation in the Middle Three Rings, so I don't go in blind and know nothing."

"I'll ask Purple Haze!"

Luo Chen chuckled, stood up, and vanished into the void.
