# Chapter 1018: The Plan to Reclaim the Life Soul, The Million-Soul Baleful Banner

Within the Scripture Transmission Hall.

Calm conversation sounded from time to time.

"Did you stir up some big trouble out there?"

"Brother Wang, why would you say that? What do you think of my current strength?"

"Unfathomably deep, vast as the sea. At the very least, I am no match for you."

"Haha, right? With my current strength, what kind of enemy could be considered a big trouble?"

"But the urgency you've shown, and the almost reckless way you've been improving your strength—it's almost exactly the same as the preparations you made back then before attacking the Flame Alliance."

"The Flame Alliance? What a distant name that is!"

Before the desk, Luo Chen murmured to himself, his right hand pausing the talisman brush he had been drawing with.

On the jade slip, a fourth-grade body-tempering technique called the *Hundred Poisons Body-Tempering Art* had been left behind. But unlike those that required the assistance of venomous gu insects, this body-tempering technique was a simplified version Luo Chen had derived; it only required the corresponding toxins to cultivate.

The work he was doing now was precisely to leave behind, one by one, the high-grade techniques he had acquired in recent years before he departed.

The *Hundred Poisons Body-Tempering Art* was just one of them. Similar techniques like *Three Heads and Six Arms*, *Moonlight Heart-Cleansing Mantra*, and various strange arts and methods obtained from the Southern Frontier were all being transcribed.

Not only that, but Luo Chen had also transcribed a copy of his cultivation insights from recent years regarding the *Soul-Splitting Art*.

All these things would be left in the Scripture Pavilion for future Luo Tian Sect disciples to exchange and cultivate.

He picked up the transcribed body-tempering technique, stood up, and walked towards a nearby bookshelf, placing it in an empty spot.

Wang Yuan, following him as he moved, said, "Yes. Back then, the Flame Alliance was one of the Seven Sects of the Jade Cauldron, a genuine Adept-level great sect. Our Luo Tian Association couldn't even be considered a first-rate force, and we couldn't even scrape together a single Adept. And in that situation, you actually had the wild idea of dealing with them. Through alliances and personal strength, you ultimately annihilated the Flame Alliance, which had been passed down for four hundred years, in a life-or-death battle."

Luo Chen put down the jade slip and casually picked up a yellowed, incomplete jade slip from the shelf beside him.

He swept his divine sense over it, and five large characters imprinted themselves in his mind—*Nether Sea Return to Origin Qi*.

This was a high-grade technique from the Nether Origin Sect. How high it was, no one knew, only that it had already been damaged when Luo Tian Sect disciples excavated it from a ruin, leaving only this part behind.

It was barely around the third to fourth grade.

Dividing his attention, Luo Chen smiled. "In that battle, wasn't it you, Brother Wang, and Chu Kui who protected me?"

Wang Yuan nodded. "Yes. It's precisely because I witnessed your madness back then that I know the seemingly calm emotions you show now might be hiding a madness even greater than that of those days!"

Luo Chen's hand paused in its movement, a strange expression flickering in his eyes.

Back then, the gap between the Flame Alliance and the Luo Tian Association was two whole tiers—the difference between an Adept-level great sect and a second-rate force.

Now, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect he was about to face seemed only a hair's breadth away from the Luo Tian Sect, but the gap was like heaven and earth.

Could his madness still prevail this time?

"Who is the enemy?" Wang Yuan stood opposite Luo Chen, frowning. "Is it the Million Great Mountains? An ancient sect? Or a Ascendant sacred ground?"

When he said the last part, there was even a hint of gritting teeth in his tone.

Luo Chen chuckled and placed the *Nether Sea Return to Origin Qi* back on the shelf.

This was an ingenious high-grade technique, extremely mysterious, claiming to be able to borrow the qi of heaven and earth!

However, the entry threshold was high; the cultivator needed to abandon their own foundation to cultivate it. The stronger the foundation, the stronger the qi of heaven and earth that could be borrowed after retraining, and it also contained a secret art for converting the borrowed power into one's own mana.

Unfortunately, the latter part was missing.

The technique was ingenious, but Luo Chen didn't care about it.

Who would be willing to discard their entire life's bitter cultivation to practice a technique that required borrowing power?

It was the same with Luo Chen's journey to where he was now; before the path of Ascension, he would never give up.

Luo Chen's expression turned serious as he stared at Wang Yuan. "Brother Wang, you want to help me?"

Wang Yuan countered, "Do you look down on my meager strength?"

Luo Chen shook his head. How could he despise such things? An extra bit of strength was always good.

But thinking about how hard-won the Luo Tian Sect's survival was, he had never intended to involve them.

Now that Wang Yuan was pressing him, he no longer hid anything.

"It's the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!"

Wang Yuan's pupils constricted, and he momentarily lost his voice.

Luo Chen walked between the bookshelves, looking at the dazzling array of techniques, secret arts, and various inheritances accumulated by the Luo Tian Sect over three hundred years, and let out a faint sigh.

"Don't worry too much. If things go smoothly, there might not even be a fight. After all, the current Heavenly Origin Dao Sect is also beset with internal and external troubles. They might not be too concerned about a small fry like me."

Behind him, Wang Yuan replied heavily, "If that day truly comes, count me in!"

Luo Chen looked back. Sunlight streamed through the window, casting its rays upon Wang Yuan, making his figure seem even more imposing.

Brother Wang was still Brother Wang. After all these years, he hadn't seemed to change much.

Luo Chen smiled, then turned and walked out of the Scripture Pavilion.

As he passed the entrance, a gray-robed sweeping Daoist bowed his head to him in salute.

"Farewell, Supreme Elder."

"Mm."

Luo Chen acknowledged, then soared into the air. When he reached a high altitude, he glanced down again.

That gray-robed Daoist had not a trace of mana fluctuation on him; he was merely a mortal. How could he be allowed to sweep the Luo Tian Sect's most important Scripture Pavilion?

He wondered how Wang Yuan, the Scripture Transmission Elder, had arranged this.

Luo Chen muttered to himself, then returned to the Luo Tian Grand Hall.

Since the Alchemy Mountain had been destroyed by a heavenly calamity, he was temporarily residing in the Luo Tian Grand Hall.

Before leaving, he needed to refine a batch of magical artifacts capable of traversing the Nether Abyss. Conveniently, Luo Tian Sect disciples could deliver the corresponding materials to the hall.

During this process, Luo Chen did not call for any help but relied entirely on himself.

Even with the formation's shielding, dense water vapor would occasionally diffuse outwards.

Within the Luo Tian Grand Hall, streams of Netherwater flowed from the high-hanging spring eye, which Luo Chen then refined into small black boats.

Luo Chen couldn't even be bothered to name this batch of magical artifacts, simply adopting the name "Nether Crossing Boat" from the one the Luo Tian Sect had previously obtained.

The function was similar anyway, and in terms of effectiveness, the ones he refined far surpassed the original Nether Crossing Boat, being even more mana-efficient.

With these Nether Crossing Boats, Luo Tian Sect disciples could freely enter and exit the Nether Abyss in the future.

Bai Meiling had swept clean the surrounding low-level ghost cultivators, so Luo Tian disciples no longer had to worry about being disturbed by them.

Hao Ranzi remained in the Upper Wasteland Abandoned City, establishing an outpost for the Luo Tian Sect in the outside world, ensuring the sect wouldn't be completely in the dark.

As long as it was managed well in the future, and the Luo Tian Sect digested the legacy of the Nether Origin Sect over the next few centuries, the sect was bound to flourish.

Luo Chen had no more worries.

His gaze now fell upon that spring eye.

"I originally planned to use the remaining Water Lotus Seed to refine a clone, sneak it into the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, and reclaim my missing life soul. But the Water Lotus Seed's foundation is ultimately insufficient; the clone's strength wouldn't improve quickly in a short time, and it wouldn't gain the Dao Sect's attention. If I refine this spring eye into the clone, would it create a clone with talent comparable to a monstrous prodigy?"

Luo Chen murmured to himself, his eyes sparkling with the brilliance of this sudden inspiration.

The plan to reclaim his life soul was not yet complete; Luo Chen only had a vague idea.

According to the intelligence from Nether Spring, the Central Continent was split into five, forming five landmasses. The Heaven-Earth Peak, broken by Rosycloud, occupied one landmass alone.

The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect occupied the remaining four landmasses.

Back then, the top-tier Great Desolate Soul-Calling Desolation had driven the Yellow Spring Sea westward, crashing into and sinking the Western Continent in the process. All living beings were swept into the Yellow Spring Sea, with no survivors.

Thus, only the three landmasses of the East, South, and North remained, inhabited by Heavenly Origin Dao Sect cultivators.

The Dao Sect's mountain gate, the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range, was located on the Eastern Continent. It was conceivable that the sect's Ascendant powerhouses, upon their return, would also cultivate there.

What Luo Chen needed to do was avoid the Eastern Continent, use an incarnation to enter the Dao Sect's branches on the other two continents, and cultivate to the Sage stage at an extremely fast speed.

At that point, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect would surely follow tradition and collect his life soul.

Taking that opportunity, he could use the connection between souls to find his missing part, then escape from the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

The entire process might not necessarily involve a direct confrontation with the Dao Sect's great powers.

But if things didn't go smoothly...

"Then I'll have to fight it out and forcibly reclaim my life soul!"

Luo Chen's eyes narrowed, a very dangerous glint flickering within them.

He knew exactly where his missing life soul was!

Back then, when Mo Shouzhuo took his life soul while he was inside the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, he had used an "Illusion Chess Piece."

And that Illusion Chess Piece was clearly a derivative method of the spirit treasure, the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard.

Considering the various powers of the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard—guarding the soul, gathering spiritual mechanisms, and suppressing an entire continent—it must also be a treasure for collecting souls.

Furthermore, according to intelligence from Green Parasol Mountain, during that great battle, the Central Continent collapsed, spiritual mechanisms scattered everywhere, and the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard also suffered backlash.

In this situation, seizing the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard through war would also be a way to retrieve his life soul.

But unless it was a critical moment, he was unwilling to do that.

Because once he did, it would be a clear declaration of war against the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. The Dao Sect's great powers were not fools; they would either destroy the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard or find a way to locate his life soul and crush it directly. Luo Chen would most likely end up with nothing.

Therefore, it was best to do everything quietly!

...

Half a month later.

At the entrance to the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land, figures stood in a forest, the atmosphere solemn.

Luo Chen's gaze swept over everyone one by one.

The steady and unchanging Wang Yuan, the flickering-eyed Ling Fengzi, the radiant-complexioned Jadewater Dragon, the respectful and adoring Li Yingzhang...

Luo Chen said nothing. Instead, in front of everyone, he called over Sima Huiniang and the other two women.

"I'm leaving. Why isn't Lingxi here to see me off?"

Sima Huiniang's expression was originally worried, but upon hearing Luo Chen's question, she quickly explained, "Lingxi has already begun his seclusion to prepare for breaking through to the Initiate stage. He also wants to improve his realm so he can come and help you sooner!"

Luo Chen was a little dazed. His son was about to become an Initiate too?

He should be able to succeed, right? His spiritual root talent wasn't bad, he had famous teachers guiding him, and the Luo Tian Sect had opened its resource reserves to him.

Even a pig should be able to successfully become an Initiate!

If he failed, Luo Chen wouldn't care much; he would just consider his previous efforts a waste.

Luo Chen's gaze fell upon Gu Caiyi. He gently rubbed her soft hair with pity.

"I know you've always been strong-willed, but when it comes to cultivation, you should take it slower. Don't be so anxious."

The mana fluctuation on Gu Caiyi's body was very obvious at this moment—a sign of the eighth layer of the Adept stage.

How long had it been since she had just entered the late Adept stage?

Less than thirty years in total, and now she was suddenly at the eighth layer of Adept. It was truly astonishing.

Gu Caiyi wanted to explain something, but her gaze was drawn to a box Luo Chen had taken out.

"This is a Nascent Soul Pill I specially left for you. But I've placed a seal on it that can only be opened in a hundred years. During this time, you need to sort out your mana and adjust the state of your essence, energy, and spirit."

"Husband..." Gu Caiyi's voice choked. Only then did she realize that Luo Chen had never neglected her. He had prepared a Nascent Soul Pill for her alone, something not even Sima Huiniang had.

Luo Chen smiled, his gaze meeting Canglong Fairy's.

Although she was still worried, a smile had forced its way onto her face.

"Go ahead without worry. I'll help keep an eye on things at home."

The words she spoke were calm and composed, generous and natural.

Sima Huiniang's expression was a little strange, as if she had thought of something, but what she said was, "My son and I will wait for you to return."

Luo Chen waved his hand and drifted out of the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land.

Behind him were a group of Luo Tian Sect disciples with varied expressions.

Ahead of him was Bai Meiling, who had hurriedly returned on her Mysterious Turtle after receiving his summons.

"Master, are you leaving again?"

"Mm." Luo Chen nodded, then extended his hand. "Show me the results of your twenty years of work."

Yes, twenty years had passed in the blink of an eye since Luo Chen returned to the Luo Tian Sect.

During that time, he had condensed his primordial spirit, refined the spring eye, comprehended the origin of water, and even undergone a soul nirvana. These many matters had filled those twenty years.

If he had to talk about rest and relaxation, perhaps those few days of intimacy with Canglong Fairy could be considered relaxation!

And what Bai Meiling had done in those twenty years was only one thing.

A pitch-black Soul-Refining Banner fell into Luo Chen's hands. The moment he held it, an inexplicable sense of heaviness arose.

"Master, Xiao Bai has lived up to your expectations. I have traveled across most of the Nether Abyss, gathering a full million low-level ghost souls. Please inspect them!"

Luo Chen's eyes lit up, and he immediately sank his divine sense into the Soul-Refining Banner.

Countless mournful wails and piercing cries came through.

Seeming to sense the entry of a living soul, countless fierce ghosts surged towards Luo Chen in unison.

But before they could get close, they were frozen in place by a natural pressure.

In an instant, ten thousand ghosts fell silent!

Bai Meiling watched this scene, exclaiming in admiration, "As expected of Master. With a single thought, you can suppress a million ghost souls. Lately, I've felt like I'm about to lose control of this Myriad Souls Banner."

"You've worked hard." Luo Chen took out a wooden box and handed it to Bai Meiling.

Inside was a piece of soul stone, one he had brought out from the Soul-Severing Cliff back then.

This item was also a great tonic for ghost cultivators!

After telling Bai Meiling how to refine and absorb it, Luo Chen turned his attention back to the Soul-Refining Banner in his hand.

"Myriad Souls Banner?"

"A million ghost souls already form the foundation of a Baleful Soul Banner. It's only missing a main soul capable of devouring spirits and ghosts."

He took out his own Soul-Nourishing Banner and threw the dozens of Ghost Kings inside into the Soul-Refining Banner.

After a brief sense, Luo Chen couldn't help but shake his head.

Not enough!

Using these dozens of Ghost Kings to fuse into a single Ghost Spirit comparable to an Ascendant was still not enough.

As he was pondering, Bai Meiling had already sidled up to him.

"Master, can you take me with you on this trip?"
