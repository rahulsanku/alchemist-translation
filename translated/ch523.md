# Chapter 523: Bird into the Azure Sky, Fish into the Great Sea

---

A low groan broke the silence.

How long had passed, he couldn't say. The sound came from a young man whose robes hung in tatters, his flesh crosshatched with deep, cracked wounds.

In the dim and lightless space, Luo Chen forced himself upright.

His head swam with a dull, persistent ache. His eyes fell first to the two objects clutched in his hands.

One was a three-inch wooden figurine, jet-black and still. The other was a cyan feather, withered and dull, its luster long since gone.

Looking at them, the memories of the teleportation came flooding back.

The moment the spatial array had activated, a crushing pressure had descended from every direction, squeezing in from all sides at once. Even with his mastery of body tempering—even with the powerful physique of a Third-Grade Primordial Body—he had felt his flesh on the verge of collapse beneath that terrifying force.

Fortunately, the cyan feather had blazed with azure light at the last possible instant, wrapping both him and Han Zhan in a protective cocoon.

Then the azure light flashed—and merged into the array.

In that single breath, Luo Chen had witnessed the turning of stars and the shifting of heavens, glimpsed the four seas and five continents, and beheld countless strange lands across the Shan Hai Realm.

And in that moment, he had truly grasped the sheer scale of the teleportation array carved into Cang Wu Mountain.

It was a supreme-grade array whose reach extended into nearly every major region of the Shan Hai Realm—far beyond anything Luo Chen had ever understood from reading about ordinary teleportation arrays in the archives.

*An array like this should not exist within the Shan Hai Realm.*

And because of it, his dread of Qi Xia Yuan Jun deepened—that ancient demon who had never once shown her face, yet commanded the Eastern Wasteland's sole fifth-grade holy ground by herself alone.

A single remnant technique she had left behind was enough to render every Nascent Soul cultivator powerless to stop it. Even a Divine Transformation great master would be forced to retreat three li.

And the teleportation array she had laid down was grander still.

*"Truly a remarkable figure. One wonders where she came from."*

Without quite meaning to, Luo Chen found himself thinking of the rumors he had occasionally overheard during his time on Cang Wu Mountain.

According to those tales, Qi Xia Yuan Jun had risen to prominence a thousand years ago. At the time, she had obtained the phoenix inheritance of the upper realm from within the Lian Xia Mountain Range—which had not yet been renamed the Jade Cauldron Domain.

From that day forward, she had taken "Qi Xia" as her Daoist title.

*Qi Xia. Qi Xia.* The name meant "dwelling upon the Lian Xia"—roosted within that range.

Later, however, the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect—backed by the Ming Yuan Sect of the Eastern Wasteland's holy grounds—had launched a war to seize the Lian Xia Mountain Range. Qi Xia Yuan Jun had no desire to clash with the Ming Yuan Sect, and she was seeking a spirit vein better suited to her level of cultivation, so she withdrew of her own accord and retreated deep into the Eastern Wasteland.

That had left He Qing Zi alone to guard the Lian Xia Mountain Range.

As for how He Qing Zi later came to be subdued by—or rather, came to take a liking to—the then-Golden Core cultivator Yun He Shang Ren, that was another story entirely.

*"So the legend I kept hearing back in Da He Fang—about a divine phoenix crashing into the Lian Xia Mountain Range—is seven or eight parts credible after all."*

*"Otherwise, how could this realm have produced such a powerful demon cultivator?"*

His head still thick with fog, stray thoughts drifting in and out.

Then Luo Chen snapped abruptly awake.

*Now was not the time for idle wandering.*

He tightened his grip.

*Tch—*

The cyan feather in his right hand crumbled under the slightest pressure, dissolving into a drift of ash.

With a light toss, it scattered and settled across the ground.

*"The power held within it has fully dissipated."*

This feather had only ever been a temporary token from You Quan, issued for entering and leaving the dungeon.

In truth, the fact that it had been able to serve as a key to briefly activate the teleportation array had already been an unexpected boon.

Now, it had no further use.

Luo Chen drew a slow breath. He fished the last remaining bottle of peak-grade Myriad Stars Pills from his storage ring, swallowed them without ceremony, and—without waiting for them to refine—pushed himself to his feet.

He turned. The Xuan Huo Sword materialized in his grasp.

Without hesitation, he brought it down.

The elaborate teleportation array cracked—one corner shattered in an instant.

He carefully pried the stone fragment free and sealed it into his storage ring.

With the array damaged, any pursuers from Cang Wu Mountain should be unable to follow.

*"But while the enemies from Cang Wu Mountain can't cross over, I still need to be on guard against powerful foes that might come from the White Bone Dao Palace."*

Luo Chen pressed his lips together, moistening their dryness, and slipped silently toward the exit.

He could not afford to wait here until he had fully recovered. He had to flee before the people of the White Bone Dao Palace had time to react.

He crept forward one careful step at a time. Black lines traced across every patch of exposed skin—the mark of his Concealment Formation driven to its absolute limit.

With a perfected formation like this active, even a Nascent Soul cultivator, without deliberately searching, would fail to detect his aura.

One step. Two steps...

Luo Chen sniffed the air.

A salty, damp scent reached him.

*The den of a demon serpent swarm should smell of blood and rot. Why this salty wetness?*

While the question still turned in his mind, he had already walked clear of the cave entirely.

He pushed aside the curtain of hanging vines and emerged onto a mountain slope—and stood there, jaw slack, staring.

Sea and sky, one seamless expanse. Ten thousand li of blue ocean, waves rolling without end. Streaks of rosy light pierced down through the clouds at the horizon, casting the far distance in shimmering gold.

The sun was sinking into the west. The hour when weary birds return to their nests.

Luo Chen swallowed. A sudden, absurd feeling rose in his chest.

*"When did the Great Snow Mountain ever look like this?"*

The unfamiliar surroundings were difficult to accept. But drawing on his extraordinary adaptability, he forced himself to acknowledge the reality of his situation.

This was not the Great Snow Mountain.

Nor was it anywhere he had ever been.

And from the terrain alone, he could glean nothing.

Still—judging by the descriptions of the Shan Hai Realm he had read in various texts, the number of places boasting an open ocean of this scale was limited.

The Eastern Underworld Abyss. The South Ocean. The North Sea.

The Eastern Underworld Abyss was said to be perpetually shrouded in thick underworld qi and ghost mist—a place of total darkness. So this was not that.

Which left only the South Ocean and the North Sea.

*The South Ocean, full of strange gu. The North Sea, crawling with demons.* Neither was a gentle place.

Luo Chen's expression shifted. He stepped back quietly, his sleeve sweeping through the air, erasing the traces of his passage from the cave mouth.

Once he had retreated back inside the cave where the array was carved, he sank into a long silence.

He could say with certainty now: something had gone badly wrong during the teleportation.

Han Zhan had said their destination was the Great Snow Mountain. How had they ended up beside a boundless ocean?

And that tremendous surge of power Han Zhan had held in reserve—it had clearly never been meant for him. It was intended for the White Bone Serpent of the White Bone Dao Palace at the Great Snow Mountain.

What neither of them had anticipated was that even a sliver of Nirvana Sacred Flame, diminished ten-thousand-fold, had not only consumed Han Zhan's entire reserve of contingency power—it had gravely wounded him. Even now, he had yet to regain consciousness.

Luo Chen's eyes drifted cold and intent to the charred black wooden figurine.

A flicker of killing intent stirred in his heart.

He could feel Han Zhan's weakness with perfect clarity—a weakness so profound that even a Foundation Establishment cultivator could end him right now.

A wisp of divine soul, shrunken and curled within the wooden figurine, with no physical body to nourish his sea of consciousness. His injuries were recovering with excruciating slowness.

*Should he kill him?*

The dark impulse took shape.

He had witnessed the fearsome power of Nascent Soul cultivators far too many times. Even the weakest, at the initial stage, was beyond what any Golden Core cultivator could resist.

And even if Han Zhan had been reduced to a single wisp of soul, completely unthreatening to behold—his greatest expertise had always been divine soul attacks and puppet manipulation.

Who could say whether he was capable of launching some strange assault with nothing more than a fragment of his soul?

Leaving a threat like this at his side was not the act of a wise man.

*Kill him. That is the only sound path.*

Luo Chen's wrist turned. A tendril of cyan flame coiled across his palm. One strike was all it would take—Han Zhan's wisp of soul would vanish utterly from the world.

Whether he would fall back into the cycle of reincarnation, return to the underworld, or lose all chance of rebirth entirely—that was beyond Luo Chen's ability to foresee.

The cold, burning light edged closer to the black wooden figurine.

But in the end, Luo Chen waved his hand and dispersed the Withering-Bloom True Flame.

*"Forget it. Let it go."*

He thought of the kindness the Falling Cloud Sect had shown him along the way.

The deal Tian Xing Zi had struck with him during the battle of Fen Xiang Valley. The moment Falling Cloud Sect's Golden Core elder Qu Xi Bai had opened the Immortal City's sealing formation to help him advance. The time beneath Qian Ye Peak, when Han Zhan had intervened to aid him while he was under the corruption of the Gold Wolf King's demon qi.

In each of those events, he, Luo Chen, had rendered something in return. But on the whole, both sides had cooperated in good faith.

And above all else—

The memory of the teleportation array's activation. Han Zhan, already critically wounded by the Nirvana Sacred Flame, must have known full well that even if the teleportation succeeded, he would be utterly defenseless—at anyone's mercy.

And yet, even so, he had placed the Azure Luan Feather in Luo Chen's hands.

*"Don't let me down."*

Han Zhan's low, hoarse words still echoed in his ears.

Luo Chen shook his head.

*"If someone treats me with sincerity, I owe them the same in return."*

The impulse to kill Han Zhan faded.

But Luo Chen did not leave things undone.

With a thought, he produced an object from his storage ring.

The Primordial Cauldron.

Looking at the great cauldron, his eyes held a mix of fondness and wariness.

*Whose natal treasure refuses to obey its own master?*

But Tian Ye Zi had already fallen. No one in the world should be capable of controlling this treasure now—not even Han Zhan or Qing Shuang, both of whom had participated in its forging. They could not reach that level of control.

After all, Tian Ye Zi had been an artifact-forging grandmaster. Luo Chen was this treasure's rightful master. The others had merely contributed a formation or two.

They were incapable of reversing the relationship between guest and host.

*"The Primordial Cauldron's innate functions include suppression, imprisonment, and binding—perfectly suited for this purpose."*

Luo Chen channeled a thread of Dharma power. From the gray cauldron, chains extended one after another.

Unlike the azure chains Tian Ye Zi had once summoned, the chains Luo Chen called forth were red—wind-attribute Dharma power rendered visible, not fire.

The chains coiled slowly around the black wooden figurine, then drew it gently into the cauldron.

With that done, inside and outside were completely sealed. Han Zhan could neither spy on Luo Chen nor attempt any hidden assault.

Even if he woke, he would have no choice but to sit quietly inside until Luo Chen chose to open the cauldron.

*"Once he's awake, I'll have a talk with him. See what he has to say."*

*"If it comes to it, we go our separate ways and part on good terms."*

*"At least that's a clean ending."*

He muttered the words to himself, took one last look around, then found a spot and sat cross-legged, activating his natal cultivation method.

He was not in a stable state. He had broken through to the third layer of Golden Core only recently, then gone straight to find Tian Ye Zi—the minor realm had never been properly consolidated.

The long-distance spatial teleportation had drained a tremendous amount of Dharma power on top of that.

In this condition, wandering outside would be unwise.

Even if the spirit qi here was thin, it was still better than nothing.

The dim cave gradually settled into quiet. Only the ragged cultivator's steady breathing remained, cycling the sparse heaven-and-earth spirit qi of this place.

——

Half a month later.

Luo Chen emerged slowly from his seclusion.

He first glanced at the Primordial Cauldron—silent, still. Han Zhan had not yet woken.

Then, naturally and without thinking, he opened his attribute panel.

A translucent panel rippled open like water, visible to Luo Chen alone. His gaze sharpened.

Line after vivid line, each entry distinct and real. Ripples spread across the still lake of Luo Chen's heart.

*"A hundred and twenty-four years old at Golden Core Third Layer—by the Eastern Wasteland's standards, I'd count among the finest geniuses."*

He couldn't help but sigh.

A cultivation speed like this would leave even the direct disciples of upper-tier sects in the dust. Take Yu Jiao Long of the Meng Quan Cave—a Heaven Spirit Root cultivator—and even she would fall marginally short.

In truth, Luo Chen hadn't always moved this fast.

After forming his Golden Core, he had spent a full ten years just barely consolidating his realm and reaching the first layer. And the reason the following fourteen years—which should have been even more arduous and drawn-out—had seen him break through two minor realms in succession came down to one thing above all else: he had solved the problem of external resources.

Whether it was the rising proficiency of his natal cultivation method, or his acquisition of a third-grade spirit vein that could sustain long-term cultivation—both had been immeasurable blessings.

And above all else—

He had refined enormous quantities of high-grade and peak-grade Myriad Stars Pills to supplement his cultivation.

*Peak-grade third-grade pills.*

Who else in all the Eastern Wasteland's cultivation world could afford such extravagance? Luo Chen even suspected that Golden Core cultivators within the Ming Yuan Sect couldn't enjoy this kind of treatment.

High-grade pills were still considered normal. But peak-grade pills were entirely cost-inefficient—trading resources for time at a terrible rate of exchange.

Luo Chen could afford to be this reckless. A great sect could never justify such lavish investment in their own disciples.

Beyond all that, his self-created *Dust Mote Origin Art* had made its own indispensable contribution. From the day he first created it, he had been cultivating it for over thirty years. By now, it had barely reached Grand Perfection.

Had he still been within the Luo Tian Sect, he would likely have already transcribed a simplified version and graded it at mid-grade or higher. Cultivators who used it to supplement their Core Formation would succeed at a rate exceeding most other high-grade Core Formation secret arts.

*"And in the end, what allowed me to cross from Golden Core Second Layer to Third Layer in such a short time was the peak-grade third-grade spirit vein of Cang Wu Mountain—and my conversion to the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Sutra."*

He exhaled slowly, his emotions slow to settle.

Everything before that last point had come from his own effort—something he accepted with a clear conscience, even a quiet pride.

But the rest of it? That was fortune.

A third-grade peak spirit vein. Who else could ever have access to such a thing?

The *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Sutra*, hailed as the progenitor of all fire arts—even Nascent Soul upper sects likely didn't possess it. Even Divine Transformation holy grounds across the Five Continents might not have it.

*"Between life and death lies great terror—but also great fortune."*

*"I fell into the hands of demon cultivators on Cang Wu Mountain. I had resigned myself to being a caged bird for the rest of my days, a tool in someone else's hands. Yet I came away with more than I lost. Every sip and every peck is set by heaven. Cause and effect, in endless cycle. It is nothing less than this."*

Luo Chen pressed his lips together, his heart full of something he couldn't quite name.

Those three years on Cang Wu Mountain—looking back now, they still felt unreal.

Not that he had been crushed or humiliated. Not that he had been kept locked away. If anything, he had gained more than he lost.

He had bantered with Nascent Soul demon cultivators. He had witnessed a Divine Transformation great master strike with his own hand.

Of all the Golden Core cultivators in the world, how many could claim experiences as strange as his?

The deeper he followed those thoughts, the further they led.

But Luo Chen had long since learned to cut stray thoughts short before they wandered too far. He reined in his mind and continued to scan the attribute panel.

The growth on his body cultivation side was modest. Over these years he had advanced only five points of progress.

If his body tempering path had a named realm to speak of, he would say he had reached the final stage of early-period—ten points away from stepping into the middle period.

And even that modest progress had only come because *Ten Thousand Dao Confluence* had gained another hundred-odd points of proficiency.

That the flesh was slow to advance, Luo Chen understood perfectly.

First, his cultivation method was self-created, and despite several rounds of streamlining, remained too disparate in nature.

Second, once he left Tian Lan Immortal City, he no longer had Bai Mei Ling regularly preparing spirit cuisine for him—cutting off the simplest and most direct supplementary path.

Third, he had no pill formulas designed to assist body tempering.

Of course, if Luo Chen lifted his self-imposed restraints and began killing living creatures in great numbers—refining their life force with his Source True Fire—he could likely accelerate his body's progress.

But doing that openly in his old environment would have drawn far too much attention.

While his thoughts lingered here, Luo Chen's eyes drifted again to the Primordial Cauldron, almost without thinking.

An idea surfaced, vague and formless.

*"No rush for now. Wait until he wakes up, then we'll talk."*

He muttered the words under his breath and let his gaze slide further down the panel.

On the technique side, the fourth-grade Dragon Severing Art had reached the Proficient tier. Worth cultivating further going forward—it might yet yield surprising results.

The third-grade Primordial Qi Great Seal had achieved Grandmaster rank. Grand Perfection was not far off.

This technique was remarkably versatile—all five elemental Dharma powers could drive it, each with its own distinct effect.

Its versatility, however, was certain to diminish going forward.

Now that he had switched to the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Sutra*, all Dharma power within his Golden Core was of the fire attribute. The other four elements would gradually be converted over time.

Once that happened, the Primordial Qi Great Seal would be limited to its fire-attribute form.

*"There's always a trade. A single-element Great Seal might simply be stronger for it."*

Under the third-grade tier, he also had Mirage of Flower and Moon, the Dust Mote Origin Art, and the Life-Sever Finger.

The first two, after years of diligent cultivation, had both reached Grand Perfection—they would bring him greater aid in the days ahead.

As for the Life-Sever Finger—that strange technique obtained from Li Jin Huang—Luo Chen had barely cultivated it over all these years.

He had tested it a few times on demon beasts, but the lifespan extracted never lingered long before dissipating.

Meanwhile, his own lifespan continued to flow away uninterrupted.

It neither raised his lifespan ceiling nor substituted for his natural drain. The technique was woefully impractical.

Perhaps it had been perfectly suited to Li Jin Huang's circumstances at the time—and only then.

Once he finished reviewing his techniques, the remaining entries needed little comment.

Alchemy had gained one new technique—the Crane-Burning Hand, exchanged from Mu Yun Yan of the Falling Cloud Sect. Still at Novice rank, it was suited for handling fire-attribute medicinal herbs, particularly when refining Foundation Nascent Pills.

He had reached Novice rank in the fourth-grade Shape-Shifting Pill, which had elevated his profession from 3rd-Grade Alchemist to 4th-Grade.

Myriad Stars Pill and Imperial Flow Elixir—both Grand Perfection.

On the formation arts front, he remained at first-grade.

But every first-grade foundational formation in his arsenal had been cultivated to Grandmaster or Grand Perfection.

That had been accomplished during the Howling Moon War, when he spent his idle days beneath Qian Ye Peak painstakingly copying and studying them. His original intention had been to build a foundation for refining a natal treasure.

As it turned out, Tian Ye Zi had "helped" him with that—sparing him the trouble.

And last—the Achievement Points.

So many techniques, pills, and formations reaching Grand Perfection had generated dozens upon dozens of points.

Even after spending ten of them mid-way through to reach Novice rank in the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Sutra*, he still held a staggering total of 101 points.

The most he had ever held at once, by far.

With these Achievement Points, Luo Chen felt a bedrock of security beneath his feet.

Give him any fourth-grade technique, cultivation method, or pill formula right now—he could bring it to Novice rank on the spot, without the slightest difficulty.

When he finished reviewing the panel, Luo Chen sat quietly for a long while.

*What next?*

He didn't know whether this place was the South Ocean near the Southern Frontier, or the North Sea they said was thick with demons. But either way, both were vast distances from the Eastern Wasteland.

From his home, the Luo Tian Sect—countless hundreds of millions of li.

In the short term, there was no going back.

And even if he did return, he would walk straight into the war between the Eastern Wasteland's demon tribes and the Ming Yuan holy ground.

At the Golden Core level, in the surging waves of a conflict on that scale, he wouldn't even be capable of protecting himself.

Especially with the stain of having refined pills for the demon tribe hanging over him. If that became known, it would drag his companions and disciples down with him.

*"Hah..."*

A long breath escaped him. The exhilaration that had been building in his chest sank and went quiet.

Far from home. Far from those he cared about. Stranded in a place he didn't recognize. The feeling was almost like transmigrating all over again—that same adrift bewilderment.

The sect he had painstakingly built. The legacy he had left behind. His companion, his disciples, his friends, his subordinates. The third-grade spirit vein he had claimed with his own hands.

All of it—gone.

*"No."*

*"They're not dead."*

*"As long as I'm still alive, I'll find a way back. One day."*

Luo Chen rallied himself.

So what if the third-grade spirit vein was gone? The Shan Hai Realm was vast. Were high-grade spirit veins found only in the Eastern Wasteland? Could he not find one here?

With a Golden Core cultivation, as long as he avoided the misfortune of drawing a Nascent Soul cultivator's attention again—where could he not live comfortably?

And besides—he had his exceptional alchemy to fall back on.

The priority right now was to understand his surroundings, then plan for the long term and continue on the path of cultivation.

With his thoughts settled at last, Luo Chen moved to close the attribute panel.

But before he did, an idea stirred.

As his realm grew higher, his horizons broader, his lifespan longer, and his mastery of various arts deeper—the panel would only grow more complex. Scrolling through it was already becoming cumbersome.

So, with a single thought, the attribute panel rippled gently—and reorganized itself into three chapters.

The first held basic attributes: cultivation method, techniques, and so on.

The second covered everything alchemy-related.

The third held formation arts.

If he learned other disciplines in the future, he could simply open a new chapter for them then.

With everything in order, Luo Chen rose to his feet, unhurried.

He stowed the Primordial Cauldron into his storage ring, then left the quiet cave.

Outside, he glanced once at the vine-draped cave mouth.

The spirit vein here was of extremely low grade—barely at the edge of second-grade, and sealed beneath a formation that masked it entirely from the outside world. Luo Chen's half-month of seclusion had nearly drained every wisp of spirit qi from it; the grade had already slipped to low first-grade.

He had nearly severed the spirit vein at its root.

And the demon cultivators of Cang Wu Mountain had set up no concealing formations here—only the natural labyrinth of trees, vines, and stone that formed an organic maze, meant to deter low-grade demon beasts from stumbling in by accident.

*"No need to add anything more."*

A faint smile crossed Luo Chen's lips. Spiritual light blazed in his eyes, and in a single glance he saw through the scenery beyond the mountain.

Tracing the gaps in the natural maze, he stepped through one footfall at a time—and at last leapt free, landing atop the boundless ocean.

Waves surged and hissed against the shore.

The morning sun was just rising, and the sea stretched to the horizon without end.

Luo Chen looked north, south, east, and west. Then he produced a high-grade flying boat from his storage ring, set it upon the surface of the water, and channeled Dharma power into it. The vessel set off, bearing him southeast by instinct.

Cold sea wind lifted his long black hair.

His robes snapped and billowed. Luo Chen gazed at the unknown horizon ahead, his eyes bright and clear.

*"On Cang Wu Mountain, I was a bird in a cage—a fish caught in a net. But from here, I am a bird into the azure sky, a fish into the great sea. No chains. No more."*

Out on the vast ocean, a single small boat carried the young man in black robes away with the wind.

*(End of Chapter)*
