# Chapter 530: The Demon Lord First Shows His Might

---

Blazing Fire Miasma had originally come from a consumable spell attached to the incomplete body cultivation method, Heavenly Roc Transformation[TERM?].

It simulated the fierce, baleful might innate to fire-type demon rocs.

Luo Chen had studied this spell very early on, adding large quantities of earthfire baleful qi[TERM?] and cultivating it to minor success.

This spell was quite practical, yet also rather awkward.

Its practicality lay in the fact that once released in battle, it could create a combat environment extremely suited to his own style.

And because of the powerful baleful qi contained within, it had a strong defiling effect on an enemy's treasures, divine soul, and body.

Its awkward point was that in the early years, this spell had to be used while transforming into the true body of a heavenly roc.

Otherwise, given the structure of a human body, absorbing too much baleful qi would instead backlash against him.

Not only that, the spell's proficiency was extremely difficult to raise. It was not limited only by the number of times it was released, but also by the grade of the baleful qi.

The baleful qi Luo Chen collected in his early years was mostly first-tier or second-tier earthfire baleful qi. It could hardly be called high quality.

Even when he later fused in third-tier earthfire baleful qi, in the end that was still the most common ordinary baleful qi.

Luo Chen had also expended great effort to have the Luo Tian Sect collect rare baleful qi, but unfortunately there had been very little. They only brought back large quantities of third-tier earthfire baleful qi, enriching this method.

These were the reasons for its awkwardness.

However.

As time passed, these problems had gradually been solved.

First was the problem of releasing Blazing Fire Miasma in human form. After he refined his life-bound treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, he could use the cauldron to nurture Blazing Fire Miasma.

Under constant communication with his mana, his body had gradually become able to withstand the baleful qi impact within the miasma.

Second was the problem of high-tier baleful qi.

In this regard, he had obtained a gourd of Withering-Bloom True Balefulness[TERM?] from the Yan Alliance. This was a rare baleful qi in no way inferior to True Solar Balefulness[TERM?].

And it shared the same source as his source true fire, the azure flame.

In this way, his adaptation to and control over Blazing Fire Miasma rose another level.

Lastly, he had now switched to cultivating the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture.

His entire body of tyrannical yet pure fire-attribute mana allowed him to be completely like a fish in water within Blazing Fire Miasma.

It could be said that the current Blazing Fire Miasma fully counted as one of Luo Chen's signature methods.

Using it to deal with Yan Nantian's Soul-Refining Banner should not be much of a problem.

As for the question of whether it was immoral for Luo Chen to attack Yan Nantian, despite Yan Nantian having no enmity with him, never offending him, and even having no connection with him whatsoever?

Luo Chen had no moral burden in that regard.

Cultivating the Great Dao had always been like this.

When robber cultivators blocked the road and tried to kill him, there had been no talk of hatred or grievance.

When Mi Shuhua[TERM?] forcefully brought him into the Broken Mountain Gang[TERM?], could Luo Chen have said "no"?

It was precisely through seeing and hearing such things that Luo Chen had gradually grown accustomed to this style.

If others do not offend me, I do not offend them?

What an outdated thought.

Back when he attacked Little Huan Mountain, Duan Qiankun[TERM?] had not yet truly acted against him.

When he first arrived in Tianlan, in order to promote his own Jade Marrow Pills[TERM?], he used every kind of method, forcing other pill factions into unspeakable misery.

Before that, had the Zheng clan of Rolling Dragon Ridge[TERM?], Danyuan Sect[TERM?], or the Li clan of Qinhua River[TERM?] ever offended Luo Chen?

In business, killing was most bloodless of all.

Reduced pill profits meant fewer spirit stones, fewer cultivation resources, and slower cultivation. That was hatred that obstructed one's Dao.

Therefore, afterward came incidents like the Li clan of Qinhua River being forced to switch sides through marriage, and Danyuan Sect repeatedly wavering.

Luo Chen's style had been established long ago.

Vaguely, it leaned somewhat toward the Primordial Demon Sect's survival of the fittest and belief in the law of the jungle.

Perhaps that was why Cheng Haichang and Cheng Dou had subconsciously thought of him as a remnant of the Primordial Demon Sect when they first met him.

His aura truly was somewhat similar.

Some might also wonder why Luo Chen, with such a style, nevertheless had a fairly good reputation.

This had to mention the one tenderness Luo Chen possessed: his conciliatory methods toward his own people.

Gentle internally, fierce externally, and unwilling to bully ordinary cultivators who posed no threat.

Such a style was actually already rare in the current cultivation world.

Only this time, Luo Chen was being more naked and more blatant.

He was no good person, and Yan Nantian was no virtuous sort either. With Cheng Dou threading the needle between them, the timing was just right.

...

Half a month later.

On Invitation Moon Island.

Black fog shrouded the place. The old beauty of a lake heart inviting the bright moon, with three shadows formed by man and reflection, was gone.

The island was not large. Even including the freshwater lake attached to it, it was only ten li across.

Yet precisely because of this small area, it emphasized that this was the essence within the essence of the thirty-six Swallow Islands.

Most of the thirty-six islands had only first-tier or second-tier spirit veins.

Only this little Invitation Moon Island possessed a small third-tier spirit vein all by itself.

In earlier years, although this island had been controlled by a clan with three late-stage Initiate great cultivators, that third-tier spirit vein had in fact been shared by the entire Swallow Islands.

Any clan's cultivator who reached Initiate Perfected Completion could, according to agreement, go to the third-tier spirit vein to attempt Core Formation and charge into Adept.

But this benefit had already existed in name only since a hundred years ago.

Ever since Yan Nantian, Senior Nantian, occupied Invitation Moon Island, the Initiate Perfected Completion cultivators of the thirty-six islands no longer had a quiet and safe place to form their Core.

Core Formation?

If you were forming your Core, where did that leave Senior Nantian?

It was precisely for this reason that even though the Swallow Islands had the island-linking formation method bestowed by the Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance and could defend against demon beasts, making it a safe cultivation land, Initiate experts still occasionally left for other places and never returned.

There was no hope of advancement.

As for the thoughts of those beneath him, Yan Nantian, nominal lord of Swallow, never cared.

So long as his basic foundation did not move, he could continue cultivating here.

And his basic foundation was naturally the Swallow Islands' large population and this third-tier spirit land, Invitation Moon Island.

At this moment, inside a quiet room on the island.

A withered, yellow-faced middle-aged man sat cross-legged, madly devouring the heaven-and-earth spirit qi around him.

Within his qi sea, a blue Golden Core spun rapidly, swallowing mouthful after mouthful of initially refined spiritual power.

A powerful aura was suppressed inside his body, faintly about to rise.

"A hundred years. I healed my injuries and cultivated bitterly. Now I, Yan, am finally about to advance to Adept Stage Four and reach the middle stage."

"At that time, unless the Moluo Stream[TERM?] sends a late-stage Adept great cultivator, no one will be able to restrain me."

"I can finally leave this remote place, return to Laboring Swallow Island, and restore my Yan clan."

As thoughts flowed, the aura accumulated inside his body grew stronger and stronger.

His realm, solid and true, was slowly rising as naturally as water flowing into a channel.

Just then.

A warning omen exploded in his heart.

Yan Nantian grunted, and the heaven-and-earth spirit qi surging in from outside abruptly stagnated.

"The Myriad Souls Grand Formation[TERM?] is under attack?"

"Could the Moluo Stream's experts have finally come?"

"Flee, or fight?"

"No. Years of accumulation are gathered in this one moment. I absolutely cannot let all previous efforts come to nothing."

"The Myriad Souls Grand Formation can still block them for a while. There is plenty of time."

His thoughts raced. The withered yellow-faced man slapped his storage bag, sending a pill flying out. He opened his mouth and swallowed it directly.

The next moment, his Golden Core spun even faster, and the previously sluggish heaven-and-earth spirit qi once again surrounded him madly, pouring into his body like young swallows returning to their nest.

...

Outside the small lake of Invitation Moon Island.

A figure stood beside the lake.

Beside him stood a round gray cauldron. Cluster after cluster of firelight surged from the great cauldron and spread toward the ten-li calm lake.

As if stimulated, the thick black fog shrouding the ten-li lake immediately began to churn.

Hisses, roars, and miserable howls rang into the clouds.

Its aura was so great that even the dark night clouds were scattered, revealing a full moon.

Clean moonlight spilled down, wishing to cover the earth, yet beneath that firelight and black fog, it could not advance an inch.

Within the black fog, layers of ghostly shadows madly spat ghost qi to scatter the incoming baleful qi.

Watching this, Luo Chen's heart settled slightly.

As expected, Blazing Fire Miasma was quite effective against this great formation formed from a Soul-Refining Banner.

Fire balefulness, though a filthy thing, also inherited a masculine and scorching nature. It was precisely the counter to the path of yin ghosts.

Next, he only needed to wait for Blazing Fire Miasma to fully release and fill this ten-li lake. Then he could fight freely.

"But even at such a time, Yan Nantian still has not appeared?"

Luo Chen frowned, not knowing why the other party refused to show himself.

His hands, however, did not slow in the slightest.

Mana surged. Blazing Fire Miasma released fully.

Within the dense firelight, a smear of gray-white appeared.

That was the deathly yet flourishing baleful qi left behind after the azure flame devoured great quantities of life force. Its name was Withering-Bloom True Balefulness.

The last time it appeared was during the defense battle of Danxia Mountain[TERM?], when it destroyed the Seven-Colored Danxia Grand Formation[TERM?] that Min Longyu[TERM?] had painstakingly arranged.

This baleful qi possessed the strongest corrosive nature.

...

As Luo Chen acted, the ghostly wails and wolfish howls on Invitation Moon Island reached their peak, spreading over hundreds and thousands of li.

The large and small islands around it could hear them clearly.

The mortals and Qi Refinement cultivators on the islands were all terrified.

The Initiate true cultivators of the islands, uncertain and alarmed, quietly approached Invitation Moon Island.

But when they reached a range of one hundred li, seven figures shot up from below and stopped them.

Everyone looked by moonlight and saw a white-robed man standing with a smile.

"Everyone, this is as close as you may come to observe the battle."

"It's Clan Head Cheng of Black Swan Island!"

A cry of surprise came from the crowd.

After that, questions were transmitted one after another toward Cheng Dou.

"Clan Head Cheng, what is happening up ahead?"

"Has Senior Nantian suffered cultivation deviation? Or has a great enemy come to his door?"

"Cheng Dou, what is your intention? Are you rebelling against Senior Nantian?"

Questions and rebukes came rushing at him.

Especially the last.

Cheng Dou's face chilled. "Yan Nantian has acted against the Dao and seized the Swallow Islands for a hundred years, accumulating crimes beyond count. Everyone, could it be that your hearts still incline toward this person?"

Such righteous words?

Everyone looked at each other.

One older and steadier cultivator quietly asked, "Fellow Daoist Cheng, speak honestly. Did you invite an expert from the Moluo Stream?"

Cheng Dou raised a brow. "It seems Fellow Daoist Xu[TERM?] also knows that the Moluo Stream has been hunting Yan Nantian."

The cultivator surnamed Xu nodded. "It would be one thing if we did not know before, but the Moluo Stream's reputation has risen greatly in recent years. They have allied with major forces to compete with the Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance for spirit islands and territory, contending for hegemony over the Demon Domain. How could we not understand them?"

But what did that have to do with a mere Adept like Yan Nantian?

Cheng Dou chuckled. As expected, there were many in the Swallow Islands dissatisfied with Yan Nantian.

There were also many who had secretly investigated Yan Nantian's origins and learned of the existence of his enemies.

Since that was the case, many things would be easier.

"That Qingyang... Demon Lord is not an expert of the Moluo Stream. But in terms of origin, perhaps he and the Moluo Stream's founder come from the same lineage."

Demon Lord Qingyang.

Hearing this unfamiliar name, everyone froze and showed blank expressions.

They had never heard of him.

But hearing that he might come from the same lineage as the Moluo Stream's founder made them understand.

Rumor had it that the Moluo Stream's founder was a Sage elder of the Primordial Demon Sect who had been left outside.

When the Primordial Demon Sect was destroyed, he had luckily survived outside.

After the demon race occupied Arctic Yemo Heaven, he gathered the Primordial Demon Sect cultivators from its external businesses and created the Moluo Stream.

If Demon Lord Qingyang came from the same lineage, then he too was a remnant branch of the Primordial Demon Sect.

Their origins were deeply connected. It was only natural for him to act on behalf of a fellow sect member and clean up Yan Nantian.

After learning this, even Initiate true cultivators who had intended to help Yan Nantian could not help retreating inwardly.

Just then, a blue watery light appeared in Cheng Dou's hand.

The light slowly rose. As he swept his fingers left and right, a huge water mirror appeared in the void.

He lifted both hands.

"This is a Water Mirror Art left by that Senior. It can help us observe the battlefield details."

"Everyone, let us witness the Demon Lord's methods together."

The water mirror continued expanding. In the blink of an eye, it formed a light screen several zhang across. The image within was vivid and lifelike, as though right before their eyes.

Such attainment drew cries of surprise.

In the Northern Sea cultivation world, most cultivators practiced water-element methods.

But those who could cast the most basic Water Mirror Art to this extent were few and far between.

That Senior had to be a water-path expert.

For a time, everyone grew excited and widened their eyes, preparing to properly watch this battle.

After all, this was a battle at the Adept level. In ordinary times, how could they observe one in such detail?

If they could comprehend even a little of the surface, whether for cultivation or battle, they would benefit endlessly.

Cheng Dou cared nothing for their shock.

Or rather, he had already been shocked at the beginning. What he disdained now was their shallow experience.

Demon Lord Qingyang allowing them to watch this Adept battle for free was not actually free.

After they witnessed the Demon Lord's methods, the Cheng clan could borrow the Demon Lord's might and smoothly integrate the large and small forces of the Swallow Islands.

Every gift had its price secretly marked long ago.

The Cheng clan had paid everything. Later, they could borrow the tiger's might and recover even more.

Suddenly, the cultivator surnamed Xu from earlier cried out in alarm.

"That divine soul aura... isn't that Fellow Daoist Peng Yu[TERM?]?"

As soon as he said this, Cheng Dou's heart trembled. He hurriedly turned back to the water mirror.

Within the mirror, amidst black fog blasted apart by endless firelight, dozens of figures leapt out.

The leader was shockingly an old man with crane hair and a youthful face. Even in ghost form, his bearing from life was not concealed in the slightest.

With the endless black fog supporting him, he seemed even more imposing.

Peng Yu. A hundred years ago, he had been the grand elder of the number one clan in the Swallow Islands.

He had once attempted Core Formation. Although he failed, he had luckily condensed a false core.

This person had been extremely strong and proficient in water spells, known as Boundless Ocean[TERM?].

Yet a hundred years ago, when Yan Nantian entered and ruled the Swallow Islands, Peng Yu had regretfully fallen.

No one expected him to appear in this form.

"He was refined alive into the Soul-Refining Banner by Yan Nantian!"

"He enjoyed supreme honor in life, but after death he still has no freedom. Fellow Daoist Peng truly is pitiful."

Listening to the others' sighs, Cheng Dou's throat grew dry.

Peng Yu had been far stronger than him, and could count as his senior.

In life, he had even reached the false core realm.

Yet he ended up like this.

What if Demon Lord Qingyang later imitated Yan Nantian?

For a time, regret actually rose in Cheng Dou's heart.

The others did not know of his deal with Luo Chen, and their gazes remained fixed on the water mirror.

After Peng Yu's resentful soul appeared, dozens more figures appeared one after another.

The cultivators present, after recognizing several familiar figures among them, cried out in shock again.

The three great cultivators of the Peng clan were all among them.

There were also nine ordinary Initiate true cultivators of the Peng clan.

The entire Peng clan had been refined into the soul banner.

Afterward, several more people were gradually recognized among the ghostly crowd.

They were all cultivators from several great clans of the Swallow Islands that had been destroyed. In life, everyone present had interacted with them.

For a time, everyone felt the sorrow of foxes when the rabbit dies.

"Senior Nantian is too brutal. Even if these people defied him, killing is no more than the head touching the ground. Why do something so absolute?"

"Indeed. He did not even spare their remnant souls and broken spirits, leaving several fellow Daoists unable to reincarnate and cultivate anew."

"The world declines, and morality collapses. When the Primordial Demon Sect still existed, even though demon-path experts ran rampant, at least there was restraint. Now that the Primordial Demon Sect is gone, the devils have no one to restrain them. Even with the Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance going against the current to rebuild order, these devils below have ultimately lost all restraint and directly refine human souls into banners."

"One, two... hiss, eight late-stage Initiate great cultivator souls!"

"Yan Nantian is far too ruthless. I do not even recognize several of the others."

As Blazing Fire Miasma erupted vigorously within the image, constantly sweeping through the black fog, the true face of the Myriad Souls Grand Formation gradually appeared before everyone.

The more they looked, the more shocked they became.

Eight late-stage Initiate great cultivator split souls were already horrifying, as though great cultivators cost nothing.

And there were also more than forty other Initiate souls.

As for the remaining Qi Refinement souls, they could still be explained, because most were sea demon souls. But that terrifying number was densely packed, almost impossible to count. At minimum, there were already more than ten thousand souls.

The cultivator surnamed Xu drew a deep breath. "With so many souls, only the legendary Myriad Souls Banner[TERM?] can contain them. Moreover, the number of Initiate cultivators is far too high. If an Adept main soul is added, then even among the many long-famous Myriad Souls Banners, this banner would absolutely be a fine product."

"Fellow Daoist Xu truly has deep experience. Yan Nantian's Myriad Souls Banner[TERM?] really does contain an Adept main soul." Although Cheng Dou's mouth tasted bitter, his face maintained an expression as though everything was within expectation.

After he said this, everyone present cried out in shock.

That Yan Nantian had such a heavy killing nature.

He had actually forcibly refined a Myriad Souls Banner with an Adept main soul, and of such high quality at that.

Only, in that case, did the so-called Demon Lord Qingyang truly have confidence in taking him down?

...

Beside the lake.

Under the bright moonlight, the young man's brows had nearly furrowed into the character for river.

Driven by him, Blazing Fire Miasma advanced unstoppably, sweeping in every direction. This so-called Myriad Souls Grand Formation, without anyone presiding over it, could hardly block him at all.

Especially wherever Withering-Bloom True Balefulness passed, large numbers of low-tier sea demon souls directly scattered into oblivion.

Even Initiate souls could not block it in the slightest and collapsed with a sweep. Although they could reform, their fierce might was clearly far less than before.

Deep within the formation, the roars grew increasingly violent.

It seemed the Adept soul stationed at the center was finally about to lose patience.

"A Ghost King existence[TERM?]. With my current strength, I need not fear it at all."

"But why has Yan Nantian still not appeared even now?"

Luo Chen formed a spirit seal, and spiritual light shot from his eyes.

His spirit eyes seemed able to peer into the Nine Nether, piercing through endless black fog and reaching Invitation Moon Island at the center of the lake.

In an instant.

A vast, surging aura rose straight up, faintly seeming about to shed its shackles.

Luo Chen's expression changed slightly.

"What courage. Breaking through on the battlefield? Do you truly think I am just decoration?"

Before the words fell, he leapt up, shooting straight into the night sky.

The bright moon shone behind him. Spiritual light empowered his eyes.

Drawing breath. Raising his hand.

A heaven-overturning palm thunderously pressed down.

On one side of the formation, a black light shot into the sky, seeking to block this palm.

Luo Chen glared at it.

"Move!"

Mirror Flower Water Moon activated abruptly. The black light's eyes went blank, and as its body froze, the vast palm print had already pressed down like a blazing sun.

It was none other than the Qingyang Great Handprint.

If he did not act, so be it. Once he acted, it was his strongest move without reservation.

One palm fell, and the entire ten-li calm lake exploded.

The little island at the heart of the lake, nourished by spirit qi for eight hundred years, was forcibly blasted a thousand zhang into the depths of the earth.

An aura like surging waves shot up from deep underground.

Boundless killing intent, accompanied by a heart-rending roar, rushed straight at Luo Chen.

"Despicable villain! I will kill you!"

Two enormous blue dragons coiled around a withered figure, spiraling out from the exploding curtain of water.

Seeing this scene, Luo Chen grinned savagely and reached out. The Mystic Flame Sword fell into his hand.

"You came at just the right time."

*(End of Chapter)*
