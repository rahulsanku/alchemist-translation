# Chapter 794: Nine Spirits Lord Seeks the Alchemy Sect

Within the cave dwelling, Luo Chen sat cross-legged on the lotus platform. A sliver of sharp, unmatched crimson sword qi sprayed from his breath as he inhaled and exhaled, continuously pouring it onto the Yuan Tu sword resting across his knees.

The *Thousand Clouds Myriad Transformations Sword Scripture* claimed not to be rigidly bound to the distinction between sword pellets and flying swords, but in truth, its cultivation method evolved from orthodox sword cultivator techniques.

Nurture a sharp sword qi within, breathe it out to soar proudly for thousands of miles.

This breath of qi originally required a sword pellet as its vessel, only then could it undergo a thousand transformations, the sword rising and ten thousand calamities following.

After altering the cultivation method, it could be attached to a flying sword, achieving a similar effect.

Moreover, in Luo Chen's view, this sword scripture was less a sword cultivator method and more akin to a one-time sword dao divine ability.

It would be consumed; each sliver of sword qi used was one less, but it could be continuously replenished.

It was not as profound as the divine ability of the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger, which was comprehended through enlightenment, but it had the advantage of being cultivable step by step according to the method.

Gather the spiritual essence of the nine heavens, refine ten thousand slivers of sword qi, then release them all at once—an overwhelmingly majestic and unmatched force.

*Hoo...*

Luo Chen exhaled a long breath of crimson qi, which, like a red serpent, completely submerged into the Yuan Tu sword.

He slowly opened his eyes, revealing a satisfied expression.

After several months, he had finally refined another sliver of Crimson Cloud Sword Qi!

If he added the two slivers refined during the year of travel before, that made three in total.

Calculating the time, the satisfaction on Luo Chen's face faded considerably.

Three slivers of Crimson Cloud Sword Qi had taken a year and a half!

Ten slivers would take five years?

To reach the thousand-cloud count recorded in the sword scripture... five hundred years?

"No wonder this sword scripture is ranked at the fifth order, exclusive to Ascendants. To master it, even without any consumption, would take five hundred years!"

"Which ordinary Sage has such a lifespan to waste on a one-time divine ability?"

Luo Chen murmured and shook his head, but he was not particularly despairing.

According to the sword scripture, ten slivers of Crimson Cloud Sword Qi could already dominate the Adept stage, and a hundred slivers would become a decent technique in the hands of a Sage.

He did not pursue the thousand-cloud count, only sought a hundred slivers of sword qi, an extra means of self-protection.

A hundred slivers, just fifty years!

"Wait?"

As if remembering something, Luo Chen's heart stirred.

A panel of light spread before his eyes.

It was the familiar attribute panel!

His gaze immediately locked onto the technique column.

**[Thousand Clouds Myriad Transformations Sword Scripture: Beginner 30/100]**

Seeing this, Luo Chen was completely stunned.

Three slivers had increased the proficiency by a full thirty points!

That meant refining one sliver of Crimson Cloud Sword Qi represented ten proficiency points, and ten slivers would raise it by one level?

But that wasn't the important thing!

What mattered was that techniques on the attribute panel often produced some peculiar changes after raising their proficiency level.

And most of these were related to the upper limits of the technique's trend or its spells.

The *Thousand Clouds Myriad Transformations Sword Scripture* emphasized quantity but limited speed. If proficiency could be raised, was it possible to break through the speed limit and greatly increase the refinement speed?

"If that's the case, perhaps I won't need five hundred years to master this sword scripture."

At this thought, Luo Chen's heart couldn't help but pound.

According to the scripture, when this method was mastered, a thousand clouds would merge, their qi surging to the stars—a formidable divine ability rarely obtained even by Ascendant powerhouses.

Even in the hands of a Sage, if used unexpectedly, it could threaten an Ascendant great power.

The reason Fu Chaosheng agreed to Fu Qinglan's request and gave this powerful sword scripture to Luo Chen was precisely because mastering it was too time-consuming, so he didn't care.

But if Luo Chen could shorten the time...

*Hiss...*

Luo Chen took a deep breath, suppressing the excitement in his heart.

He caressed the Yuan Tu sword on his knee with deep affection.

"Whether it can succeed depends on the changes after the first stage. I must not be greedy and rush ahead."

"For now, I must proceed step by step according to the previously planned schedule."

"Elder Fu should be arriving soon!"

---

Two days later!

A mighty procession of people entered Fenghua Domain.

This sight immediately alarmed the major and minor powers within Fenghua Domain.

Especially those Adept sects, who began to panic.

They feared this group was coming for the territory of Fenghua Domain, which would mean war with Fenghua Palace.

Regardless of who won or lost, their small forces would suffer.

But unexpectedly, this team, led by a Sage and accompanied by a full fifteen Adepts, did not head towards Fenghua Palace at all.

The direction they were advancing...

"It's Boli Qingchuan!"

The Valley Master of Liegu Valley, who had once been fortunate enough to attend Luo Chen's Sage Ascension Ceremony, stood atop a mountain peak, carefully observing before reaching this conclusion.

A nearby sect leader from a friendly Adept sect was puzzled.

"Boli Qingchuan? The Luo Tian Sect? So many people making a grand show of force to go to the Luo Tian Sect. What are they doing? Could it be a vendetta?"

The Liegu Valley Master shook his head. "Currently, the Luo Tian Sect's Grand Supreme is at the height of his power. Rumors say he is on good terms with Fenghua Palace and the White Crane Sect. Who would be so blind as to cause trouble for them on Fenghua Palace's turf?"

After thinking it over, he instructed, "They're not coming for us. Send the disciples and sect members back first. Let's follow and see what's happening."

"The Valley Master is right. The situation is changing rapidly. If we can get firsthand information, we can adapt better."

Soon, the leaders of the two Adept forces dismissed their people and joined forces to follow behind that massive procession.

Along the way, many forces received the news and followed as well.

Even Motian Cliff, entrenched on the outskirts of Fenghua Domain, was alarmed and sent people to inquire about the situation.

When they learned the group was heading for Boli Qingchuan, the Motian Cliff cultivators hurried back to report to their ancestor.

---

"It's the Divine Talisman Sect!"

Hidden in the void, the Motian Old Ghost gazed from afar at the team that had stopped by the banks of the Blackwater River, standing in strict formation. He recognized their origins at a glance.

But he was very puzzled. Why would Elder Fu bring so many Adepts to the Luo Tian Sect?

"Could it be..."

Thinking of a certain possibility, the Motian Old Ghost's expression uncontrollably changed.

He gritted his teeth but did not act rashly, waiting quietly instead.

After about half a day, he saw Elder Fu, that old fellow, fly out from Daqing Mountain, his face stern.

Yet within that stern expression, his eyes were full of joy!

Just then, Elder Fu looked up in his direction.

"This old thing is so sharp! He can actually see through my concealment technique!"

The Motian Old Ghost's face turned ugly, not because Elder Fu had discovered him, but because of what the other party had done.

That Luo Chen, after spending most of a year, had actually refined the pills the Divine Talisman Sect needed!

And it was highly likely a fourth-order realm-breaking pill that aided Sage formation!

Otherwise, Elder Fu wouldn't have made such a grand show, bringing almost all the sect's elite to the Luo Tian Sect.

He was afraid of an accident on the way back with the pills!

Fifteen Adepts, if coordinated properly and supplemented with other means, were no less formidable than any early-stage Sage.

Especially since these people were all from the same sect.

In his sight, the Divine Talisman Sect cultivators did not hesitate. After Elder Fu returned, they immediately left Boli Qingchuan without stopping.

The Motian Old Ghost hesitated for a long time but ultimately did not follow.

Without his true artifact, Motian Cliff, to aid him, Elder Fu alone was more than he could handle, let alone the fifteen Adepts.

He stopped in place, unable to help but look towards that Daqing Mountain.

"According to rumors, Luo Chen boasts of having the skill of an Alchemy Grandmaster. Now it seems it's no empty boast."

"In the future, there will surely be many fellow Daoists coming to him to refine pills. But if that's the case, the enmity of him destroying my true artifact..."

Thinking of the future, the Motian Old Ghost's face grew even uglier.

*Zheng!*

Suddenly, a sword cry exploded in the void.

A crimson sword qi shot up from Daqing Mountain!

Its sharp intent shot straight towards the Motian Old Ghost's hiding place.

Startled, he quickly retreated and fled.

"Did Elder Fu notify him, or did that brat Luo Chen discover me himself?"

---

Within Daqing Mountain, Luo Chen pressed his left hand against his right forearm. The faint blood-colored sword shadow gradually calmed down.

Three slivers of Crimson Cloud Sword Qi were far from reaching the point where quantity caused a qualitative change.

The credit for startling the Motian Old Ghost with a single sword strike belonged to the Yuan Tu sword itself.

Caressing the spot on his right forearm where the Yuan Tu resided, Luo Chen fell into thought.

"The Yuan Tu sword is already unmatched in sharpness, one of the finest weapons under heaven, second only to true sword dao spirit treasures."

"Using this sword as a vessel to cultivate Crimson Cloud Sword Qi, its power might be far greater than the effects recorded in the sword scripture."

"I hadn't noticed this before."

After this thought passed, Luo Chen left the cave dwelling and headed for the hall where the Black Ape Puppet was stationed.

As soon as he arrived, the Black Ape Puppet, which was instructing Qu Jiu on drawing talismans using demon beast talisman skins, suddenly stiffened.

"Grandmaster?"

Qu Jiu was startled.

Then he saw a faint light fly out from the Black Ape's head and sink into the crown of the white-robed Daoist who had just entered.

Seeing that young yet awe-inspiring face, Qu Jiu understood everything in an instant.

He bowed respectfully. "Qu Jiu pays respects to Grandmaster!"

Luo Chen waved his hand. "No need for formalities."

Then he walked to a desk.

After a moment of stillness, he suddenly moved.

He picked up the brush and dipped it in ink!

On a dark red talisman skin, he quickly traced talisman patterns. His mana poured out from the tip of the brush, strand by strand embedding itself into the talisman skin.

Qu Jiu didn't even dare to breathe as he watched this scene.

The Black Ape Puppet itself had no mana, and the high-grade spirit stones embedded in its body were mostly needed for daily movement.

Therefore, during this time, it had only been studying theory, without any practical work.

Most of the time, it was instructing Qu Jiu in talisman-making, while also verifying certain ideas.

Now that the main body had arrived, there was no need for further talk.

In just the time it takes to burn an incense stick, Luo Chen suddenly withdrew his hand.

He reached out with his hand, and the already formed talisman on the desk flew up gracefully.

"Little Jiu, you evaluate this. What level would you say this Blazing Sun Talisman is at?"

Qu Jiu swallowed his saliva and looked carefully.

Over the past few months, his foundation in the talisman dao had been firmly laid by Luo Chen.

Just as he had taught Qu Lingjun alchemy back then, Luo Chen's teaching model initially focused on instilling knowledge, with practical work coming later.

Qu Jiu might still be relatively average in talisman-making ability now, but in terms of talisman knowledge, he was already quite accomplished.

After carefully observing for a long while, he finally said cautiously, "From what I can see, Grandmaster, this Blazing Sun Talisman of yours has power penetrating three-tenths of the way through. The talisman patterns seem naturally formed, and the mana contained within has almost reached the limit of this talisman skin. If judged by grade, it should be at the second-order upper grade. If judged by power, it has the might of a full-force strike from a late-stage Initiate great cultivator!"

Hearing this answer, Luo Chen couldn't help but smile.

"Very accurate judgment! Not bad!"

On the attribute panel, the proficiency of the Blazing Sun Talisman had just reached the Grandmaster level.

The reason it hadn't reached Great Perfection was that the talisman skin he had just used had limited him.

"Little Jiu, go pick out a few more second-order fire-attribute talisman skins for me. While I have the time, I'll draw a few more Blazing Sun Talismans."

"Yes!"

Qu Jiu hurried off to select suitable talisman skins.

Luo Chen stood with his hands behind his back in front of the desk, a smile on his face.

The news that he had refined the Sage Ascension Pill for the Divine Talisman Sect should spread throughout Fenghua Domain, and even the Five Domains of Lingtian, within a short time.

Next, there would definitely be people who couldn't resist coming to his door, begging him to refine pills, constantly disturbing his quiet cultivation.

At such a time, it wouldn't be suitable to continue refining Crimson Cloud Sword Qi.

No matter. He would use this interval to improve his talisman-making skills!

His third-order talisman master status was somewhat inflated, solely because his spell mastery was too high, giving him a high starting point.

If he wanted to solidify this skill, he would have to do as he did when practicing the alchemy and formation dao—learn a large number of various basics.

This was not difficult for him!

Luo Chen could effortlessly produce various basic spells.

Now, he had plenty of talisman skins, and a talisman brush that was a magic tool level, the Green Mane Brush.

Even the most lacking talisman ink and cinnabar, he had obtained a batch from Elder Fu during the exchange of the Sage Ascension Pill, in return for some insights and experiences gained from taking that pill.

The Divine Talisman Sect was a large and wealthy sect, and they had no shortage of these small items.

On the contrary, insights helpful for forming a core were priceless and not to be traded for gold. This could also be considered a small, covert compensation to the Divine Talisman Sect after Luo Chen had skimmed off the Sage Ascension Pill.

Luo Chen offered a plum, and Elder Fu naturally would not be stingy, sending back a large quantity of talisman ink and cinnabar.

Thus, Luo Chen could let loose and engage in large-scale talisman-making.

"Every one of the hundred arts of immortality is closely related to cultivation."

"Although my ambition does not lie in the talisman dao, if I can cultivate it on the side, it might help me at some point in the future. Just as the alchemy dao improves my cultivation, the formation dao aids my artifact refining, and even helps me complete the killing move of the Encompassing Fire Prison."

"If I can cultivate all the arts, I can draw on their strengths to attack jade with other stones, and face any obstacle without hindrance!"

"In the past, I didn't have the conditions. Now, with a long lifespan, profound cultivation, and the cheat of the proficiency panel, it's time to pick up the various skills I've neglected, one by one."

As he was thinking, he heard the sound of Qu Jiu hurrying back.

Luo Chen smiled slightly and released a divided soul into the Black Ape Puppet.

"Grind the ink for me!"

"Little Jiu, lay out the talisman skins for me!"

"Today, I will indulge in drawing talismans to my heart's content!"

---

Just as the Motian Old Ghost had thought, and as Luo Chen had predicted, after the Divine Talisman Sect made a grand show of force, going to and from Boli Qingchuan, many onlookers understood the truth.

Luo Chen, the Grand Supreme Elder of the Luo Tian Sect, had truly refined the fourth-order pill the Divine Talisman Sect desired!

Thus, his self-proclaimed ability as an Alchemy Grandmaster was no longer in any doubt.

The name of Alchemy Sect Luo Chen instantly resounded throughout Fenghua Domain.

And with an unstoppable momentum, it began to spread to Lingtianguan and the Five Domains of Lingtian.

Those of low cultivation admired, looked up to, and revered him greatly.

Those with profound Dao attainment, like Sages, began to stir restlessly.

And one of those who had attended Luo Chen's Sage Ascension Ceremony could no longer hold back.

He first went to the Divine Talisman Sect's encampment in the Hundred Beasts Domain, trying to visit Elder Fu and inquire about the inside story.

But the Divine Talisman Sect had closed its mountain gates, not allowing anyone to disturb.

This person immediately understood that the rumors were not false.

Elder Fu had obtained a fourth-order realm-breaking pill that aided Sage formation, and without a moment's delay, had a certain cultivator in the Divine Talisman Sect who had been stuck at the Adept stage for a long time take it, beginning a forced breakthrough to the Sage stage amidst this turbulent and chaotic era.

He hesitated no longer and headed straight for Fenghua.

On this day.

Outside Boli Qingchuan, a middle-aged man dressed in nine-colored splendid robes, with a dignified and stern bearing, actively and deliberately lowered his dharma cloud.

Amidst the curiosity of the Luo Tian Sect cultivators and the covert observation of fellow Daoists following in secret, he flicked his wide sleeve.

Separated by a hundred li, he spoke in a clear voice.

"Nine Spirits Lord seeks the Alchemy Sect!"
