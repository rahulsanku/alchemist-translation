# Chapter 938: All of You, Come at Once!

On the plain.

The white-robed monk held a dharma blade in one hand, while the other formed a palm seal.

The dharma blade was pitch-black, emitting rolling black qi.

The palm seal was solemn, from which the phantom of a golden bell originated.

Two extreme auras converged upon a single person, yet were incomparably harmonious.

Facing Luo Chen, who stood high above, his expression was calm as he spoke lightly: "This humble monk's dharma name is Jue Ning. Of course, benefactor may also call me by my secular name before I left home—Ning Yuzhuo!"

When the name "Ning Yuzhuo" appeared, Luo Chen felt a jarring sensation of illusion blending with reality, of memory colliding with the present.

When he had traveled the Northern Sea with Han Zhan, the latter had mentioned once seeing a Northern Sea demon cultivator in the Eastern Wilderness who wielded pure demonic qi.

In Canglong's dream, he had seen that demon cultivator. However, that man hadn't been arrogant for long before being personally suppressed by the eminent monk Master Huiping.

His name had also been Ning Yuzhuo!

Now, looking at the man before him, whether in bearing and demeanor or in the magical power he displayed, everything matched those fragmentary impressions.

It was indeed the same person!

Luo Chen smiled freely. "Excellent. I do not slay nameless men. Since you have a name and a surname, then receive another strike from me!"

These words were spoken with extreme arrogance.

What did it mean that having a name and surname meant one had to receive another strike?

Yet Luo Chen's actions were even more overbearing than outsiders perceived.

He stepped through the void as if climbing a heavenly ladder, each step giving birth to a lotus.

When he reached a great height, he suddenly kicked out.

His entire body shot forth like an arrow from a string, heading straight for Ning Yuzhuo. Wherever he passed, wind and thunder roared.

His momentum surpassed even that of Thunderclap Cave Master drawing down heavenly thunder.

Thunder Kick!

Facing this kick, Ning Yuzhuo remained calm as the hand seal he formed with his left hand bloomed once more.

A great golden bell emerged from his body, enveloping him.

All of this, though lengthy to describe, happened in the instant Luo Chen finished speaking.

Their contact occurred in but a single moment.

*Dong!*

A heavy sound rang out. Ning Yuzhuo stood rooted to the spot, unmoving.

Luo Chen's kick was blocked outside the golden bell.

Yet, centered beneath Ning Yuzhuo's feet, the earth began to tremble violently.

Waves of soil, like ripples, spread outward.

Ten li, twenty li, and even a hundred li away, they still did not cease.

Ning Yuzhuo gave a muffled grunt. With immense effort, he dragged his long blade and slowly swung it down at Luo Chen.

"Benefactor, the sea of suffering has no shore; turn back and reach the other shore!"

His expression was as kind as a buddha's, yet he swung his blade like one chopping wood.

This strike was utterly unremarkable, far less imposing than his previous attacks.

However, Luo Chen, at the center of the attack, brightened.

Within the blade light, there were whispers of demons and chants of buddhas. A series of mind-confounding sounds continuously entered his sea of consciousness.

Had it been an ordinary person, their mind would surely have been lost, unable to withstand this strike.

But Luo Chen, whose soul was constantly defended by the Soul-Imprisoning Art, remained unmoved. He only laughed loudly. "If you wish to help others leave the sea of suffering, why do you raise your blade against them?"

Ning Yuzhuo, who had been perfectly calm, finally showed a hint of surprise on his face at Luo Chen's unperturbed actions.

He seemed not to have expected that the other party would be unaffected by his demonic sounds and buddhist chants.

But since he had already struck, how could he stop halfway?

Ning Yuzhuo murmured, "The world is deluded, sinking in the sea of suffering, always unwilling to leave. Then this humble monk can only cut away their sins for them."

As he spoke, the blade light changed once more!

Gone was the patience to guide people toward goodness. Instead, it became wildly ferocious, as if a great demon had descended upon the world.

Its power was far greater than before, yet Luo Chen felt the danger had diminished, making it easier to deal with.

He stepped forward, both palms striking out simultaneously.

With pure physical strength, he shattered the blade light into pieces.

The demonic qi was violent, but source power was no less overbearing!

His vast palm force was unending, surging toward Ning Yuzhuo.

Even though the golden bell phantom reappeared, this time, under Luo Chen's enhanced source power assault, it could no longer maintain its immovable stance.

*Boom!*

Amidst a heaven-shaking sound, Ning Yuzhuo was pushed backward as if by an invisible force.

Only after retreating several hundred zhang did he finally stop, his expression stiff.

Luo Chen straightened up and withdrew his stance, one hand behind his back. He clicked his tongue. "If you're going to enter the demonic path, then enter it. If you're going to cultivate buddhahood, then cultivate it. Fellow Daoist Ning, mixing them together like this is truly neither fish nor fowl!"

As his words fell.

Ning Yuzhuo, as if unable to suppress the injuries within his body any longer, flashed an unnatural flush across his face, and blood slowly seeped from the corner of his mouth.

The look he gave Luo Chen was no longer as peaceful as before, faintly revealing a trace of confusion.

Luo Chen paid him no heed.

Under the gaze of the crowd, he spoke loudly to Feng Buyan and the others in Wind Thunder Gorge. "A mere three men, and you think you can strike at my brother Ming Lei's cultivation ground? Why don't you all come at once? I, Qi Tian, am enough to handle you all!"

His clear, light shout carried matchless confidence.

But no one doubted whether he was being arrogant.

From suddenly striking out and heavily wounding Shepherd Daoist, to exiting the valley and forcing back Ning Yuzhuo head-on, in just a few moves, he had displayed incomparably powerful strength.

Even Thunderclap Cave Master, who had previously harbored some doubts about Luo Chen, now felt his heart tremble.

Could it be that the other party hadn't been boasting earlier?

That he could fight anyone below the Ascendant stage?

He couldn't see through the white-robed monk Ning Yuzhuo, but he knew of Shepherd Daoist. He was the temple master of Green Sheep Temple, having achieved the Sage stage three hundred years ago. His cultivation was at the early Sage stage's peak, only a step away from breaking through to mid-Sage.

A figure of such caliber couldn't withstand Qi Tian's bare palms, demonstrating a vast gap in strength.

Now, with such a declaration, how would the enemy respond?

Thunderclap Cave Master ceased his attack on Feng Buyan, clearing a path.

Feng Buyan's expression flickered with uncertainty. One moment he looked at the resentful Shepherd Daoist, the next at the demonic-qi-flaring Ning Yuzhuo.

Suddenly, a shout rang out.

"If you will not fight, nor retreat, then why do you linger!"

The voice was like a thunderbolt, shaking the heartstrings.

Among the tens of thousands of Feng clan disciples, at this shout, some fell over, others were dizzy and disoriented.

Feng Buyan's entire body trembled. "Enough!"

Shepherd Daoist couldn't believe it. "Brother Feng, he is but one man. If the three of us attack together, we might yet fight!"

Ning Yuzhuo was silent. His surging demonic qi concealed his white robes as he slowly raised his long blade.

Feng Buyan sighed. "The matter cannot be accomplished. Withdraw the troops!"

Having said this, he directly left Wind Thunder Gorge.

Shepherd Daoist clutched his broken wooden staff, unwilling to accept it.

Yet, facing Luo Chen's gaze, he involuntarily averted his eyes, not daring to meet them.

Ning Yuzhuo's buddhist light reappeared, suppressing much of his demonic qi. That blade also hesitated before being sheathed.

At the entrance of Wind Thunder Gorge, Thunderclap Cave Master took the initiative to come to Luo Chen's side, a naturally ingratiating smile on his face.

"Brother Qi, thank you!"

Luo Chen waved his hand. "Don't thank me yet. Driving them back this time is merely a temporary measure. If, after I leave, they become covetous again, how will you deal with it?"

Thunderclap Cave Master was stunned.

Then, he quickly realized the importance of this moment.

"We should sign a treaty, establish a non-aggression pact. Additionally, Wind Thunder Gorge must nominally belong to... to us!"

Luo Chen smiled ambiguously. "Us?"

Thunderclap Cave Master said matter-of-factly, "Yes, Brother Qi is one of the thirty-six cave masters of Wind Thunder Gorge. Naturally, this place has a share for you."

Luo Chen pointed upward. "For territorial divisions involving Sage domains, don't we need to notify the sacred land, the Life and Death Gate?"

"In the past, yes. But now..." Thunderclap Cave Master shook his head. "Though I don't know what exactly happened to the Life and Death Gate, they have enough troubles of their own and won't bother with such trivial matters."

Luo Chen grew curious, a strong interest stirring within him. What exactly had happened to the sacred land, the Life and Death Gate?

But his expression remained calm, betraying nothing.

"In that case, Fellow Daoist, go ahead and negotiate!"

Thunderclap Cave Master cupped his hands, then departed joyfully.

In the distance, the Feng clan army, which had come with high hopes only to return in defeat, appeared quite dejected.

No one had expected such a grand expedition to end so anticlimactically.

Low-level cultivators couldn't clearly see which way the balance of battle had tilted.

They only knew that after a brief exchange, their own supreme elder had backed down.

This was truly demoralizing.

Feng Buyan could naturally feel the resentment below, but he found it difficult to explain.

Luo Chen's strength had been utterly unexpected.

In that brief exchange, the two experts he had invited each suffered varying degrees of injury.

At his advanced age, if he were to truly fight to the death, whether he could win was one matter. Leaving behind even a minor injury might mean he could no longer protect the Feng clan.

"Wind Thunder Gorge probably isn't that special. It's just that Ming Lei, having recently advanced to the Sage stage, was less affected by it. There's no need to stake the clan's future on this."

"Overbearing Cultivator Qi Tian..."

Feng Buyan muttered this name, his heart full of doubt.

There was no such figure in the Southern Frontier!

Could he also be an expert from another continent, like Ning Yuzhuo?

As he speculated, he couldn't help but look toward Ning Yuzhuo's room. From time to time, large amounts of buddhist and demonic qi would surge out, making it difficult for living beings to approach.

"He came to the Southern Frontier only a little over a decade ago, and his cultivation progress has been advancing by leaps and bounds, like flowers on brocade."

"But judging from today, it seems he too has begun to undergo changes."

"Should I..."

Just as this thought arose in his mind, a clan member reported from outside.

"Supreme Elder, a message gu is floating outside."

"Who sent it?"

"Thunderclap Cave Master!"

Feng Buyan frowned. He beckoned to the outside, and the next moment, a swallow-like bird landed in his hand.

After briefly scanning the information within, he sneered.

"This fellow certainly knows how to exploit a crisis!"

After sneering, he intended to reject the negotiation. But then his hand suddenly stopped.

"If I could borrow the strength of those two, even if Ning Yuzhuo is strange, I should be able to take him down easily, right?"

...

"Senior Qi, this is your cave dwelling."

A Golden Core cultivator respectfully introduced a half-new, half-old cave dwelling to Luo Chen.

With his eyesight, Luo Chen could naturally tell that someone had lived here before and that it had only recently been vacated.

In terms of spiritual qi concentration, it was already top-grade for the third rank, but had not yet reached the threshold of the fourth rank.

Thunderclap Cave Master had arranged this for him, not out of neglect, but genuinely fulfilling his earlier words.

Luo Chen was now one of the thirty-six cave masters of Wind Thunder Gorge!

As a guest, he could temporarily stay in Thunderclap Cave Master's cave dwelling.

But since he had become an owner, he should have his own cave dwelling.

"Senior, are you satisfied?" the Golden Core cultivator asked cautiously, looking at Luo Chen with awe.

The earlier battle, though brief, had left a deep impression.

Sages were already beings they had to look up to. And Luo Chen, who could easily defeat Sages, was an unattainable peak-level expert!

Luo Chen nodded. He wasn't particularly satisfied, but neither was he dissatisfied.

It was merely a temporary lodging.

He casually asked, "Where is Cheng Bai?"

The Golden Core cultivator was startled, then answered with a somewhat unnatural expression. "Cheng Bai has gone to the Misty Wilderness to seek treasures."

"He just came out of there, and he's going back in?" Luo Chen hadn't expected this.

"Senior, if you have any matters to attend to, you can entrust them to this junior as well."

"I do have some trivial matters. Go and collect some body-tempering techniques for me. No restrictions on rank, no taboos. As long as they involve body tempering, gather them all for me."

Luo Chen didn't mention payment, but the other party respectfully accepted the task.

Helping Thunderclap Cave Master secure Wind Thunder Gorge was already the greatest reward.

Such trivial matters as collecting techniques were nothing.

After the junior left, Luo Chen finally calmed down.

Gone was the arrogance and overbearingness he had shown to outsiders. His expression was now calm and collected.

"The strength of Sage cultivators in the Southern Frontier is weaker than I imagined!"

A doubt arose within him.

The strength he had displayed earlier was merely that of an ordinary fourth-rank mid-stage body cultivator.

In terms of immortal cultivation realm, that was equivalent to mid-Sage.

As for the two enemies he had faced, Ning Yuzhuo was a special case. Like himself, he came from another continent and had performed decently, barely able to confront Luo Chen head-on.

But Shepherd Daoist, at the third layer of the Sage stage, was utterly clumsy.

His natal magic treasure was not wielded smoothly, and his magical power circulation was miserly, giving a distinct feeling of being... constrained!

"Yes, constrained!"

"Even Feng Buyan's subsequent voluntary retreat seemed to be out of concern for a real life-or-death battle?"

"I had already prepared to unleash all my true artifacts, even transform into my true form, and suppress the three of them with thunderous momentum. But none of it was needed."

"What exactly are they worried about?"

Luo Chen was puzzled.

Unable to find an answer through contemplation, he decided to wait for Thunderclap Cave Master to return and inquire about the situation.

With time on his hands, he thought back to Ning Yuzhuo, whom he had fought earlier.

Truly worthy of being a true disciple of the Northern Sea Primordial Demon Sect, his talent was exceptional!

Suppressed by the Ultimate Zen Sect for centuries, upon breaking free, he had not only broken through to the Sage stage but also cultivated buddhist techniques.

"Buddha and demon cultivated together. I look forward to having a normal conversation with him next time."

A look of anticipation appeared in Luo Chen's eyes. It was said that the body-tempering path of the Western Desert buddhists was the only complete one remaining in the Mountains and Seas Realm.

He wondered if Ning Yuzhuo had also cultivated techniques in this area.

Time passed quickly. After only one cycle of the sun setting and the moon rising, Thunderclap Cave Master came calling.

"It's settled!"

Thunderclap Cave Master's face was filled with barely suppressed joy.

Luo Chen smiled slightly. "That smoothly?"

Thunderclap Cave Master laughed heartily. "With you as a deterrent behind the scenes, that old fellow Feng Buyan naturally spoke kindly."

The older one gets in the martial world, the more timid one becomes.

This saying also held true in the cultivation world.

Luo Chen's earlier display of killing intent was extremely rare. Feng Buyan was also afraid that if words failed, they would attack the Pine Wave Mountain Range.

After laughing, Thunderclap Cave Master's expression turned slightly serious.

"Although Feng Buyan acknowledges that Wind Thunder Gorge belongs to me from now on, and is even willing to have mutual exchanges and cooperation, he has proposed one condition. In a situation that does not involve mortal danger, he hopes that the two of us will make a move for him once."

Luo Chen refused this condition without a second thought.

Thunderclap Cave Master was not surprised by this reaction. "I guessed you would react this way and have already told him that it might not be possible. I don't think he will insist."

Then, Thunderclap Cave Master casually said, "Feng Buyan is quite curious about your origins. Although the Southern Frontier is vast, a body cultivator of your profound skill cannot be an unknown. He asked me to inquire about your background."

After this question was asked, the cave dwelling fell into silence.

The smile on Thunderclap Cave Master's face gradually faded. He looked somewhat unnaturally at Luo Chen.

He saw the other party looking at him with a faint smile, a thought-provoking expression on his face.

"Brother Ming Lei, have you ever heard a saying? He who sees too clearly invites disaster; he who knows too much courts calamity."
