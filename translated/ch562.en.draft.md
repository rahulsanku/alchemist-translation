# Chapter 562 — Esteemed Master, Do You Think I Can Form a Core?

As Luo Chen returned, Invitation Moon Island, which had been quiet for a long time, became lively once again.

Alchemists and medicine apprentices moved about the island one after another, handling all sorts of miscellaneous medicinal ingredients for Luo Chen.

That enormous pill cauldron also appeared on the small island from time to time.

Only this time, few cultivators dared to come and watch.

Within the ten-li flat lake, a vicious black jiaolong lay dormant. From time to time, vast demonic qi leaked uncontrollably from its body.

Without Luo Chen’s protection, ordinary Qi Refinement and Foundation Establishment cultivators simply could not withstand that demonic qi.

Others only assumed the black jiaolong was guarding the island and forbidding outsiders from approaching to pry.

Only Luo Chen knew that after Black King devoured the cliff serpent that had failed its tribulation, he had begun properly refining the essence within it.

Because all his attention was focused on refining it, he had fallen into a state of “false sleep,” and thus could not control the leakage of his own power.

It could be imagined that once Black King awakened, his strength would surely surge by a large margin. Perhaps even his own foundation and aptitude would improve further.

The only question was whether the Black-Scale Python race had cliff-serpent blood in its ancestry, and whether he could properly assimilate it.

The busy alchemy work on the island affected many people, and many benefited from it.

Xu Muxian was one of them.

Twelve years ago, he had merely been a collateral clan member, one who received no attention from anyone.

Twelve years later, he possessed a Qi Refinement Stage Seven cultivation base and had formed his own small family through marriage with the Cheng clan, currently the number-one clan of the Swallow Islands.

Whenever he walked across the various islands, anyone who saw him treated him with utmost respect. Even Foundation Establishment true cultivators greeted him with smiling faces.

The reason for all of this was merely the identity hanging above his head: “disciple of the Demon Lord.”

But only Xu Muxian himself clearly understood that this so-called Demon Lord’s disciple was only “registered” in name.

Demon Lord Qingyang’s guidance toward him had remained limited to a suitable amount of resources and the occasional pointer when Xu Muxian helped with alchemy.

As for true master-disciple inherited cultivation methods and secret arts, there had been none at all!

Because of this, Xu Muxian’s feelings were rather complicated.

He sincerely wished to become the other party’s true successor, but it was obvious Luo Chen had no such intention.

This Core Formation superior was broad-minded by temperament, yet he clearly had no intention of treating the Swallow Islands as his main base and operating them long-term. Otherwise, he would not have turned a blind eye to certain petty thoughts among those below him.

Inside a side hall, Xu Muxian finished counting the pills. He placed pill after pill, each exuding sharp cold qi, onto a cold-jade tray, then held it and walked toward the main hall.

His gaze fell on the cold-jade tray. Looking at those pills, worship appeared in Xu Muxian’s eyes.

Even if he could not become the Demon Lord’s disciple, that did not affect the reverence he felt toward Luo Chen from the bottom of his heart!

Jade Dew Pills were second-tier pills of extremely high refinement difficulty.

With his current alchemy skill, he simply could not imagine how they could be refined.

Yet under Demon Lord Qingyang’s hands, the other party could casually refine large batches of high-grade and even top-grade Jade Dew Pills!

Such attainment was simply inconceivable.

Especially since this was far from the Demon Lord’s limit.

The same was true of third-tier pills!

“In this lifetime, can I reach even half of Esteemed Master’s level?”

As he murmured, the man, who was already nearing middle age, stepped into the quiet Qingyang Great Hall.

The instant he entered, a deep roar thundered into his ears like rolling thunder!

“Roar…”
