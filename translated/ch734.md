# Chapter 734: Yuantu's Might, Withering-Bloom Mutation, Ice Pole Sect's Calamity, Ghost Immortal Tower Reappears

An immortal family's giant ship was not merely large.

Its internal facilities were complex, its buildings lofty, far exceeding ordinary imagination.

It was like certain luxury cruise ships before Luo Chen transmigrated. Ordinary people simply had no idea how extravagant things aboard could be. Commercial streets, grand theaters, rock-climbing halls, even golf courses: such things were all available.

Compared with such mundane objects, the Sword-Halberd surpassed them rather than fell short!

The great ship's main body was made from the corpse of a fourth-tier Sword-Halberd Whale three hundred zhang long, and on that foundation it had been extended and expanded. The entire Sword-Halberd was five hundred zhang in length.

Pavilions, terraces, towers, gardens, and waterside halls were everywhere upon it.

Majestic palaces of varying height stood row upon row like dragonfish scales.

All kinds of shops and all sorts of cultivator leisure venues were present.

One could even see handsome half-demon, half-human mermaids appearing and disappearing aboard the Sword-Halberd.

So grand and vast, it could be called an immortal city upon the sea!

Accommodating a few thousand people did not make it seem crowded in the slightest.

Its main function was not carrying people, but loading and transporting some bulk Northern Sea resources unsuitable for storage magic treasures.

In the center of the Sword-Halberd, inside a luxurious palace, Luo Chen withdrew his gaze and slowly nodded.

"Elder has been thoughtful."

Fu Chaosheng tucked both hands into his sleeves and said with a chuckling smile, "After I return this time, I will no longer serve as merchant alliance elder. In the future, we should still address each other as fellow Daoists!"

Luo Chen blinked in puzzlement.

Fu Chaosheng explained, "The term for merchant alliance elders like us is two hundred years, and the post is often held by Sage cultivators with no hope of Ascendant advancement and considerable age. This old man does not have many days left in this life. After I return, I will naturally live out my remaining years, teach my descendants, and no longer come out to hold office."

So that was how it was. Luo Chen instantly understood. He remembered the other party vaguely mentioning this before.

Fu Chaosheng looked around this great hall named Kunxiao Palace [TERM?], his face showing emotion.

"Since you are satisfied, this old man can be at ease. The time on the return trip is not exactly long, but it is not short either. If you can use this place to increase your strength by even a fraction, that is one more assurance inside Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven."

Kunxiao was the original name of that Sword-Halberd Whale while it had been alive.

A great hall named after it was inevitably the most essential place on the Sword-Halberd.

How was the word "essential" embodied?

Naturally not in luxurious buildings or hundreds of servants, but in the abundant spiritual qi, gathered yet unreleased, vigorous beyond measure!

In Luo Chen's perception, although the spiritual qi here was not as clear and bright as in a natural cave abode, it still possessed fourth-tier quality, in no way inferior to the cave abode on Little Qiong Mountain.

According to Fu Chaosheng's introduction, a large spirit-gathering formation had been arranged beneath Kunxiao Palace, constantly absorbing free spiritual qi from heaven and earth into it.

Not only that, he had also embedded five supreme-grade fire-attribute spirit stones into the spirit-gathering formation, making it even more compatible with Luo Chen's primary cultivation art.

Such an arrangement could not be called anything but a grand expenditure.

How could Luo Chen not be satisfied?

That was why he had said the other party had been thoughtful.

Fu Chaosheng led Luo Chen through Kunxiao Palace, viewing it briefly like flowers from horseback.

At times, he introduced the situation inside the palace; at times, he chatted about trivial matters.

"I slighted Fellow Daoist some time ago. It was truly this old man's failure as host."

"There are too many matters here in the Northern Sea. Although my term has ended this time, extricating myself so quickly is, in truth, not entirely according to rules. I can only spend more time handling the loose ends everywhere properly, lest I draw the displeasure of my successor."

"This is how we people of Central Continent conduct ourselves. Although there is a Tianyuan Dao Sect over our heads, in essence, families are everywhere, advancing side by side. The Fu family has declined for a long time, so this old man naturally will not make enemies on many sides for the clan."

"You should have heard of Star Gate from Shen Wandou, and thus should know Star Gate is jointly controlled by the Twelve Great Houses. These Twelve Great Houses are all top clans that once produced Ascendant great powers. Not only do they control Star Gate, they also possess enormous influence inside our sect. Beneath the Great Houses are the Thirty-Six Aristocratic Clans and countless lesser houses. How are lesser houses and aristocratic clans distinguished? That is not difficult. Listen as I explain in detail..."

"As for the Northern Sea? It is just like this. Between righteous and demonic paths, people's hearts are ghost domains. Being able to form a superficial peace and cooperation is already not easy. Although our sect indeed exerted effort in this matter, it also depends on that Great Power Li's stance."

"His attitude is somewhat ambiguous. Although he formed an alliance and bestowed island-protection formations, he does not seem to care much about either the righteous-demonic war or the Demon Alliance invasion..."

"On this trip, we first go to Sun-Moon Island [TERM?]. After we arrive there, the passengers will split up. At that time, we will take a teleportation formation directly to Central Continent territory, saving several years of effort."

"There is no need to worry about safety on the road. The Dao Sect flag hanging on the Sword-Halberd is enough to repel any petty people's covetousness."

"Let us stop here today. This old man still has quite a few matters, and those people must be growing anxious."

It could be seen that Fu Chaosheng truly was extremely patient in his treatment of Luo Chen.

After boarding, although he clearly had many matters to handle, he still found time to accompany Luo Chen for a while.

Not only that, he also transferred Fu Hongzhang, an Adept cultivator, to Kunxiao Palace to listen to Luo Chen's commands at all times.

Luo Chen knew who the "those people" he mentioned were. They were none other than the other six Northern Sea Sages who had boarded with him.

Their party of seven had not all gone to Central Continent through Fu Chaosheng's connections as Luo Chen had.

Now that they shared the same ship, it was necessary to make simple acquaintance.

Luo Chen walked alone up the tower. By the time he reached the fifth floor, he was already at the highest point of the Sword-Halberd.

This was also where the cultivation chamber was located.

Turbid qi sank and clear qi rose. At the highest point, spiritual qi was the clearest and also most suitable for absorption and breathing exercises.

Luo Chen did not enter the cultivation chamber directly. Instead, he arrived at the corridor, hands clasped behind his back, overlooking the vast Sword-Halberd.

The cultivators who had just boarded moved densely like ants, heading to palace halls and ship cabins under the guidance of low-level Tianyuan Dao Sect cultivators.

Those people did not notice Luo Chen, but two or three Sages instinctively cast their gazes over.

Luo Chen's expression was calm, and he nodded in acknowledgment.

Afterward, he spread out his divine sense until it reached a thousand li around them.

"As expected, they have all followed."

Luo Chen pursed his lips. In his divine-sense perception, an enormous number of demon beasts, counted in the tens of thousands, trailed behind the Sword-Halberd.

They had no desire to attack at all. Rather, they seemed to be monitoring it.

Because his divine sense actively probed outward, the top experts among them also sensed it and collided over without the slightest courtesy.

The corners of Luo Chen's mouth lifted slightly, and his divine sense withdrew.

"So cautious. What are they afraid of?"

Luo Chen muttered to himself, then returned to the cultivation chamber.

He did not need to worry about Heavenjade and the others; Fu Hongzhang would naturally arrange their lodgings.

And Luo Chen's present task was only one thing.

Refine the Yuantu blood sword!

Even if he could not break the third layer of seal and fully master this sword, he had to familiarize himself with it as much as possible and bring out the power it ought to possess.

...

Several months had unknowingly passed since the Sword-Halberd left Divine Origin City.

Although this ship was large, its speed was not slow in the slightest.

After such a long time, they still had not left the Northern Sea region, which showed how vast the Northern Sea was.

Along their route, human presence was rare, while demonic miasma hung thick and gloomy!

Clearly, the Northern Sea Demon Alliance was nibbling away at human territory bit by bit from the outside inward.

That was why the farther out they traveled, the more desolate it became, and the more rampant the demon beasts grew.

What was strange was that wherever the Sword-Halberd passed, not a single insignificant demon beast collided with the ship.

Low-level cultivators clicked their tongues in wonder at this situation, while high-level cultivators at the Sage level understood that it was caused by the demon-beast force following behind them.

They truly seemed to be escorting the Sword-Halberd away, preventing any demon beast from approaching the ship.

Regarding this situation, the Sages born in the Northern Sea felt only incomparable sorrow, yet there was nothing they could do.

If not for this, why would they have left their homeland?

These high-level cultivators closed one eye and opened the other, treating it as though they had seen nothing, and shrank into their own worlds.

Almost no one cultivated in seclusion. The journey was short, and not every palace on the Sword-Halberd had a fourth-tier cultivation chamber like Kunxiao Palace. Therefore, the Sages aboard the ship often held small gatherings every three to five days and larger gatherings every half month, exchanging cultivation insights with one another.

They also wanted to meet Luo Chen, this "Wilderness Wanderer" whom Shen Wandou had visited on his own initiative, whom Fu Chaosheng treated with first-class guest honors, and whom sixth-level Sage Zhou Yunshen praised repeatedly.

But every invitation they sent basically sank like stones into the sea.

Gradually, people no longer paid attention to Luo Chen and only treated him as a rogue cultivator of solitary temperament.

After all, rogue cultivators cultivated in lonely places, so their temperaments should also be lonely, especially when the other party's Dao title even contained the word "Wilderness."

Then suddenly, one day!

Above the Sword-Halberd, wild winds rose, and blood stench surged into the sky.

With a sword cry, spiritual qi between heaven and earth converged.

All cultivators raised their heads to look. Zhou Yunshen and the other Sage cultivators, who were currently gathered together, also walked out of the palace one after another and watched this phenomenon with curiosity.

In the sky, blood clouds gathered, their color gradually transforming until they formed brilliant clouds like the burning glow of sunset.

This scene came quickly and left quickly.

In only an instant, it vanished.

The Sages' expressions varied.

"What true artifact is that? When activated, it can actually mobilize so much spiritual qi of heaven and earth?"

"Blood stench rising to the sky, blood clouds gathering. Although it later transformed into fire clouds, such signs are clearly the style of a blood-path magic treasure."

Among the crowd, Zhou Yunshen's expression changed slightly. He took a deep breath and said, "Back when the Myriad Immortals Assembly's Blood Wanderer revealed the life-bound true artifact he had sacrificially refined for centuries, there was also such a phenomenon!"

Everyone was startled. "Fellow Daoist Zhou, could you be speaking of that Divine Astral Blood Blade?"

Zhou Yunshen nodded.

The others were even more shocked.

The scale of spiritual qi of heaven and earth a true artifact could mobilize was often related to the cultivator's own realm and how long it had been sacrificially refined.

The Divine Astral Blood Blade was fiercely infamous. It had been refined for several centuries, and no one knew how many souls had fallen beneath its blade.

This Wilderness Wanderer was only a mere first-level Sage, yet the true artifact he refined already possessed such might?

Fu Chaosheng had also accepted the invitation to attend the Sages' gathering today. At this moment, listening to everyone's discussion, he could not help stroking his beard.

The stronger Luo Chen was, the happier he was!

Although that sword-path true artifact belonged to the blood path and somewhat diverged from the righteous path, a weapon's nature did not have much to do with a cultivator's disposition, so long as Luo Chen did not create slaughter karma recklessly like Blood Wanderer.

With Luo Chen possessing such a powerful treasured sword, his granddaughter would also gain more assistance in the coming five-hundred-year grand competition.

"As expected, he gives me more and more surprises."

"This old man truly has eyes like torches, able to detect this child's extraordinariness at a single glance among a crowd of mediocre people!"

...

Inside the sealed chamber on Kunxiao Palace's fifth floor, a three-foot-seven-inch longsword flew up and down, left and right around the white-robed Daoist like a swimming fish.

Luo Chen beckoned with his hand, and the Yuantu Sword settled horizontally before him.

The joy in his eyes was not concealed in the slightest.

After undergoing the deep-planted soul seal, sacrificial refinement with magic power, and connection to the spiritual qi of heaven and earth, this sword's might had already revealed the tip of the iceberg.

It was no exaggeration to say that this sword's sharpness was the strongest Luo Chen had ever seen.

It was in no way inferior to the Divine Astral Blood Blade he had seen years ago on Withered Wood Ridge!

"No wonder the mad Blood Nightmare Moluo broke through both my Sevenfold Blazing Heaven Rings and Mystic Dust Armor's defenses with a single sword strike back then, even injuring this fourth-tier Archaic true body of mine."

"And this is only the tip of the iceberg of this sword!"

"If I can undo the third layer of seal..."

Luo Chen licked his lips, his heart somewhat restless.

But in the end, he suppressed that greed.

How formidable had the Blood Sea Ancestor been, yet he had still perished beneath this sword. Even if there were reasons related to that dark world, it still showed Yuantu's ferocity.

Being able to grasp several fractions of this sword's might right now was already a heaven-flooding fortune. How could he ask for more?

If he truly opened the third layer of seal and neither he nor the Primordial Unity Cauldron could suppress it, then the joke would be enormous.

"It is already enough for now. I cannot be greedy for more."

Luo Chen took a deep breath and grasped the sword hilt with one hand.

Flesh spread out, gradually wrapping around the Yuantu Sword, and bit by bit, the Yuantu Sword vanished into his right hand.

He did not choose to store this sword in his Purple Mansion.

In the end, it was an object of unclear origin, unlike the Primordial Unity Cauldron or Mystic Dust Armor, magic treasures he had personally created and whose roots he knew. If it went berserk inside the Purple Mansion, the one injured would be himself.

Storing it with the power of the fleshly body would also allow him to draw the sword instantly at a critical moment.

That was enough.

After refining the Yuantu Sword, Luo Chen's time suddenly freed up.

With a thought, several voice-transmission talismans flew in from outside. His magic power activated them, and they burned without wind.

All of them were invitations to Sages' gatherings.

Luo Chen curled his lip and ignored them.

Those Northern Sea Sages were all rather old, had accomplished Dao attainments, and did not care about the short cultivation time during this journey.

But he was different. He could not waste even one minute or one second.

Time still had to be used where it mattered most.

"Next, I must consider cultivation."

Luo Chen's eyes flashed, his face showing that he had a plan well in hand.

After forming his Nascent Soul, aside from the Great Soul Pill, he had not taken many pills.

Even though the Hongyuan Pill had already entered the door and could be refined at will, he had not used this pill much to assist cultivation.

It was not that he feared pill poison corroding the Purple Mansion barrier.

The Purple Mansion evolved from supreme-grade five-phase spirit roots was incomparably vast. Viewed through a cultivator's divine-sense microperception, it seemed to contain a small world within.

The thickness of its barrier was simply not something mere pill poison could corrode away.

The reason he had not taken too many was because Luo Chen was thinking of the alchemy taboo of "drug resistance."

Now that the conditions were present, he naturally had to eat and drink the good things. Why wrong himself?

Therefore, Luo Chen's plan was to first raise the Hongyuan Pill's proficiency and refine large quantities of middle-grade, even high-grade quality pills.

Taking them this way would both increase his cultivation speed and greatly delay drug resistance to the pills.

When he met Fu Chaosheng again this time, the other party had, as expected, delivered the second batch of resources into his hands.

Compared to the first time, the second batch was even larger. Luo Chen paid ninety million spirit stones in one breath, three times the first transaction!

Among them, the medicinal herbs for the Hongyuan Pill occupied more than half.

After receiving this batch of herbs, Luo Chen had Heavenjade and the other two process them in advance for him. With Fu Hongzhang sending people to assist, Luo Chen did not spend much energy at all.

Now that he had freed up time, it was time to grind that proficiency up in earnest.

Huff...

He opened his mouth and exhaled lightly. The Primordial Unity Cauldron spun down before him.

"Grow!"

At the word grow, the Primordial Unity Cauldron expanded to the proper size.

Afterward, with a thought from Luo Chen, a wisp of azure-gold flame emerged from his fingertip.

Looking at this flame, Luo Chen's gaze paused.

The gold was growing richer and richer!

Ever since absorbing the netherfire inside Old Monster Netherfire's body at Molan Court, the Withering-Bloom True Flame seemed to have undergone another evolution.

Only, this evolution was very slow, unlike its rapid leaps in Blazing Refinement Prison.

But even the slowest evolution, accumulated over time, had produced obvious changes.

This change was embodied in its color.

"The Withering-Bloom True Flame was originally azure. That trace of gold came from the Nirvana Sacred Flame, with the primary and secondary clearly distinguished."

"Now its color is transforming toward gold. Could it be that my Withering-Bloom True Flame is approaching the Nirvana Sacred Flame bit by bit?"

Luo Chen was somewhat puzzled and did not really understand what level of transformation his source true flame, which accompanied him day and night, was undergoing.

Unfortunately, there was no evenly matched battle to let him verify the Withering-Bloom True Flame's current might.

"For now, I will borrow pill refinement to explore this flame's condition bit by bit!"

Luo Chen murmured, and one medicinal herb after another was cast into the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

...

The Sword-Halberd continued sailing, drawing closer and closer to Sun-Moon Island.

Everyone's hearts also grew increasingly expectant.

That island, like Divine Origin City, completely belonged to the Tianyuan Merchant Alliance.

And once they arrived there, it seemed that Central Continent would be only one teleportation away.

Of course, the scenery along the way also grew increasingly broken and ruined.

Scenes of demon beasts wreaking havoc were seen again and again.

Clearly, this war between humans and demons in the Northern Sea had spread wider and wider.

Even some demon races that had not joined the Demon Alliance were, under the general trend, taking the initiative to join in and begin counterattacking human territory.

After all, since ancient times, the Mountains and Seas Realm had belonged to desolate beasts. Demon beasts originated from desolate beasts, so the Northern Sea's large and small islands naturally belonged to demon beasts.

Aboard the Sword-Halberd, another gathering of Sages was taking place.

Fu Chaosheng mentioned the arrangements to come.

"Next, so long as we pass the Ice Pole Sect's Blue Ice Sea Region [TERM?], we can proceed without obstruction and reach Sun-Moon Island directly."

The Blue Ice Sea Region's geographical environment was special. The entire sea region was covered beneath solid ice.

This place was also counted among the Northern Sea's first-class spirit lands.

It contained a large fourth-tier spirit vein.

The Ice Pole Sect [TERM?] was established atop the Blue Ice Sea Region.

When the Ice Pole Sect was mentioned, everyone present became interested in conversation.

"This counts as one of the Northern Sea's very top Sage upper sects. Even during the righteous-demonic war, they could remain outside the matter."

"Indeed. Their five great Sages are all renowned. I once met Superior Bluejade [TERM?], and her bearing and grace were absolutely first-rate in the present age."

"Speaking of which, their ability to remain outside the matter is not only because the sect is powerful, but also because their relationship with the Cang Hai Alliance is considerable. It is said that Grand Elder Daoist Cold Pole [TERM?] once traveled the Mountains and Seas Realm together with Great Power Li before he advanced to Ascendant, and their private friendship is extremely good."

"That rumor is true. The Cang Hai Alliance's island-linking formation method was first openly used by the Ice Pole Sect, the first non-Cang Hai Alliance force to do so."

"When we pass by later, should we go pay a visit?"

"Forget it. The Ice Pole Sect is rather exclusionary. Even when the Primordial Demon Sect took the initiative to recruit them back then, they refused."

"They have such confidence? That was an Ascendant holy land. How did they dare?"

"Hehe, this old man knows a thing or two about this matter. Back then, the Mountains and Seas Realm was opened up by all the sects together. There were Ascendant holy lands and Sage upper sects, and everyone contributed. The Ice Pole Sect was also one of them. On the alliance covenant, it was clearly marked that holy lands could not use force against these sects that had rendered merit. In truth, it is not only the Ice Pole Sect. Within the five continents, aside from Central Continent, there are several other sects of this kind."

The last speaker was Fu Chaosheng.

He also explained why the Ice Pole Sect's status was so transcendent among the many Sage forces.

Suddenly!

Everyone present changed expression one after another.

Without thinking, each person rushed out of the palace, sending divine sense toward the blue, icy sea region not far away.

The scene they perceived made them pale with shock.

"The demon race coalition will not even let the Ice Pole Sect go?"

"What a great formation. So many demon beasts!"

"That is... Ghost Immortal Tower!"

*(End of Chapter)*
