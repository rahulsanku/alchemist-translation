# Chapter 857: What Is a Divine Ability? A Great Power Summons

In the cultivation chamber.

Threads of spiritual energy converged, forming tiny vortices that swirled around the tips of Luo Chen's fingers.

This scene appeared normal.

But in truth, it was completely different from Luo Chen's usual execution of the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger!

For these spiritual energies were not merely the fire-attribute spiritual energy he controlled, but also a vast amount of other attributes.

The five phases, yin and yang divisions, wind and thunder forces…

Every single thing, though minuscule in total quantity, encompassed all phenomena, leaving nothing uncovered.

And for this energy that encompassed all attributes of spiritual energy, the cultivation world had a collective name for it—origin qi!

Luo Chen had never imagined that while in a state of depleted mana, executing the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger would allow him to perceive origin qi, draw upon origin qi, and even manipulate the origin qi of heaven and earth!

At that moment, shock had filled his heart.

A divine ability could actually leverage origin qi?

Because of this shock, he had not hurried to recover his mana afterward, maintaining that state of depletion.

Thus, in that state, together with Black King, he had simultaneously sensed the origin qi turbulence over at Lingtian Pass and deduced that it was an Ascendant great power making a move.

Vaguely, he seemed to have made a new discovery about divine abilities, which stood above mere spells.

Now, he wanted to give it a try!

Luo Chen took a deep breath and continued drawing upon the origin qi of heaven and earth.

The vortices at his fingertips grew more numerous!

Spiritual energy of all attributes converged unceasingly, following the true intent of withering and blooming, swelling and growing.

The principle behind this technique Luo Chen had created himself was actually not complicated.

He extracted all kinds of vitality from heaven and earth, continuously boosting and climbing, amplifying the power to a peak limit several times or even dozens of times his own mana output—this was called Bloom.

After striking, when it landed on an enemy, the attributes would suddenly and extremely reverse, causing the enemy's physical body to age and their mana to recede—this was called Wither.

If the enemy already had injuries during this process, they would worsen sharply, rebounding upon themselves.

Thus, he had simply named it the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger at the time.

At this moment.

The massive spiritual energy vortex had almost completely enveloped Luo Chen, his face becoming indistinct.

And in this process, Luo Chen's main soul constantly watched the changes in the Sage within his Purple Mansion.

The Purple Mansion barrier was wide open. The originally withered Sage gradually revealed a look of confusion.

The outer world of heaven and earth and the inner world of the Purple Mansion blended like water and milk. The tiny infant seemed to have returned to a womb filled with amniotic fluid.

Luo Chen's half-closed eyes slowly relaxed, a look of comfort appearing in them.

Suddenly!

"Open!"

Within his sea of consciousness, Luo Chen's strongest divided soul formed a hand seal and gave a low shout.

The Aperture-Thatching Art struck directly onto the main soul.

In an instant, the blockage was cleared, and Luo Chen woke as if from a great dream.

As if shocked by electricity, Luo Chen abruptly released the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger tightly held in his right hand.

"Huff, huff, huff…"

The white-robed Daoist collapsed onto the meditation cushion, drenched in sweat, his eyes filled with lingering fear.

A thought emerged, and he softly uttered a single word.

"Assimilation!"

Just now, with the Purple Mansion barrier wide open, the inner and outer heavens and earth had connected. His Purple Mansion had become almost indistinguishable from the outside world, and a vast amount of origin qi had tried to merge into his Sage.

This scene was exactly the same as the "Heaven-Earth Assimilation" recorded in countless ancient texts!

Swallowing his saliva, Luo Chen slowly raised his right hand.

Fear, dread, panic… one after another appeared on his face, until finally, it gradually turned into a thoughtful expression.

"A cultivator refines qi, from the inside out. Starting from refining essence into qi, the spiritual power liquefies, becomes extremely pure, then condenses into a golden core, fusing with divine sense to form mana. Then the core shatters to form the Sage, using the Sage to grasp the corresponding attribute of heaven-earth spiritual energy, gaining a certain degree of control, forming one's own domain. When the Sage grows into a vast primordial spirit, one is no longer limited to the corresponding attribute of heaven-earth spiritual energy but can leverage the collective of all spiritual energies—that is, the origin qi of heaven and earth—to fight!"

"Divine ability, divine ability, what is a divine ability?"

"Using the primordial spirit to sense and communicate with the origin qi of heaven and earth, and then manipulate it."

"Thus, the art of divine ability is a spell used by Ascendant great powers, leveraging the primordial spirit to move the origin qi of heaven and earth."

"Although such spells are powerful, they have an extremely high threshold for cultivators, the first being a powerful primordial spirit."

"When my mana was depleted, my Sage withered. At that moment, executing a divine ability was no different from a child swinging a sledgehammer—it would only injure oneself!"

After some thought, Luo Chen no longer attempted such risky actions.

Although he had discovered that the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger executed by leveraging the origin qi of heaven and earth was far more powerful than using a single attribute of spiritual energy, in that state, his Sage was easily assimilated by heaven and earth, causing him to lose his self-will.

This move was far too dangerous.

For Sage cultivators, divine abilities could only be tasted lightly, never delved into too deeply!

At this point, Luo Chen suddenly thought of the Tianyuan Dao Sect.

If the intelligence was correct, Fu Qinglan, Shi Ju, and Gu Daosu were the final winners of the Grotto-Heaven Grand Competition and were each granted three great divine abilities.

Was the Tianyuan Dao Sect not afraid that their disciples would sink into divine abilities and be assimilated by heaven and earth?

There was no answer, and Luo Chen did not dwell on it further.

He readjusted his posture, returning to a cross-legged sitting position.

He first swallowed a top-grade Hongyuan Pill, then took a sip of Golden Wind Jade Dew wine, circulating the *Withering-Bloom Dao Scripture* to quickly recover his mana.

Leaving the Luo Tian Sect and fleeing far to the Dieyun Domain was not Luo Chen's wish.

But he had no choice.

When Di Tu had led his men to kill at Baili Qingchuan, Luo Chen had fallen into a dilemma.

Killing was not an option—the other party's father was an Ascendant powerhouse.

Releasing was even less of an option—in the midst of a great battle, who would dare let a Demon Emperor go free?

And after subduing him, advancing or retreating was equally impossible.

Killing him would still offend the Ancient Demon Di Tian; releasing him would inevitably invite criticism from the Eastern Wilderness cultivators. The prestige he had built over many years might crumble overnight. Moreover, Di Tu did not seem like a good sort; he would likely seek revenge, becoming a thorn in Luo Chen's back.

But keeping him by his side was a constant slap in the face of the Ancient Demon Di Tian.

Although Di Tu guessed that Di Tian would not come to save him, what if?

Whether for familial affection or for the sake of his own face, what if?

After much deliberation, Luo Chen could find no way to break the deadlock.

Thus, he left the Luo Tian Sect and left Di Tu at Daqing Mountain.

In this way, even if the Ancient Demon Di Tian came personally, or sent someone, it would not affect the Luo Tian Sect cultivators.

His main body was far from Di Tu, leaving only a puppet clone there.

If the Ancient Demon Di Tian came in person, he would simply release Di Tu and self-destruct the puppet clone, thus giving an explanation.

That puppet clone was not as simple as it appeared.

When Luo Chen had refined it, he had combined Han Zhan's methods with ancient puppet arts, even refining his own flesh and blood into it.

The appearance could change, the blood and qi were consistent, and the soul bore his aura.

Even the realm was mid-stage Sage!

It was a good hand in battle, and even better as a substitute for death.

Of course, Luo Chen did not wish to go that far. After all, it was a divided soul and puppet he had cultivated with great effort.

"Let's wait until the human and demon races of the Eastern Wilderness reach a conclusion!"

Murmuring, Luo Chen entered a deep state of cultivation.

Vast amounts of spiritual energy surged, swallowed into his body, continuously refined and purified, turning into pure and overbearing mana.

…

"Greetings, Uncle-Master Kang!"

"Good morning, Uncle-Master Kang!"

Kang Ledao walked through Liuyun Mountain, occasionally greeted by disciples sweeping the grounds.

He responded to them all with a cheerful smile.

But after leaving those disciples, the smile on Kang Ledao's face turned bitter, and his red, drinker's nose wrinkled upward from time to time.

How had the once-prosperous Dieyun Sect fallen to such a state!

The vast mountain gate now had only him, a golden core cultivator with his lifespan nearly exhausted, sitting in charge.

"I wonder how many of the junior brothers conscripted to resist the demon race at Lingtian Pass will return?"

With a heavy heart, Kang Ledao finally stopped outside a mist-shrouded cave dwelling.

Inside was the cave dwelling of their Supreme Elder, Sage Liuyun!

But unfortunately, protected by this maze formation, even their Dieyun Sect's golden core cultivators could not enter.

"I hope to break this formation soon, obtain the Supreme Elder's legacy, and revive our Dieyun Sect. If there are resources for forming a Sage, that would be even better."

Harboring hope, Kang Ledao stepped into the mist formation.

A day later.

A figure returned to the same spot, dazed.

"Failed again?"

Kang Ledao's face was pale, his head drooping as he walked toward his own cave dwelling.

He was already the most proficient formation master in cloud restrictions among the Dieyun Sect's golden core cultivators.

Yet, before the formation left by mid-stage Sage Liuyun, he remained powerless.

"Next time, I will definitely break the formation and enter the cave dwelling!"

But inside the cave dwelling at that moment.

A figure stood quietly before a bookshelf, reading a tome in silence.

Naturally, the white-robed Daoist had sensed the disturbance in the formation outside.

That old man had come no fewer than thirty times in just one year.

Each attempt to break the formation took anywhere from a day to over ten days.

Every time, he returned empty-handed.

"The cloud pattern restrictions of the Dieyun Sect are truly wondrous!"

Luo Chen put down the book.

This scripture pavilion was the most important and valuable place in Sage Liuyun's cave dwelling.

While recovering his mana, he occasionally came here to "borrow" some reading.

He did not care much for those techniques and secret arts.

They did not suit him, and learning them would be useless.

But books on formations and restrictions, Luo Chen read with great interest.

In the dao of formations, Luo Chen had already advanced to a fourth-rank formation master!

In the cultivation world, that was as rare as phoenix feathers and qilin horns.

And regarding the seven fundamental restrictions of formations, he was even more proficient.

Yet, in these texts, he discovered that his grasp of the fundamentals was still incomplete.

Especially the cloud pattern restrictions!

Clouds have form and appearance, yet they gather and disperse unpredictably.

But the Dieyun Sect could turn the void into substance and substance back into void, allowing cloud pattern restrictions to permeate formations everywhere.

Through continuous reading, Luo Chen's understanding of cloud pattern restrictions deepened.

His formation attainments improved rapidly.

Additionally, an idea was becoming clearer and clearer.

A book suddenly appeared in Luo Chen's hand.

It was not taken from the bookshelf but from his storage ring.

It was the *Golden Escape Art*, originally Jin Ling's legacy.

"Previously, I tried to use the Golden Escape Art to create my own Fire Escape Art, but I could never grasp the method, only absorbing the concept of fusing spirit and explosive burst from the Golden Escape Art."

"Looking at it now, the Fire Escape Art is actually more compatible with the concept of cloud pattern restrictions."

"Clouds have form and appearance, gathering and dispersing unpredictably. Fire is the same."

"If I combine the strengths of both, could the Fire Escape Art I create surpass the perfected Ninety Thousand Li?"

Luo Chen sank into contemplation.

…

The startling wind swept past the white sun, and the scenery flowed westward.

Unknowingly, three years had passed since Luo Chen arrived at the Dieyun Sect.

In the cold, lonely cave dwelling.

Black King, who had been sleeping coiled on Luo Chen's shoulder for a long time, was suddenly startled awake.

He lay on the ground, his eyes still drowsy, looking at the white-robed Daoist.

"Master, has something happened at Daqing Mountain?"

Luo Chen smiled and shook his head. "Three years, safe and sound."

"Oh."

Black King mumbled drowsily, then asked in confusion, "Then why did you wake me? Is there something?"

"Nothing. I just saw you sleeping soundly and wanted to wake you up."

"Uh."

While Black King was stunned, a jar of wine landed in front of him.

"Have a drink with me!"

Luo Chen smiled faintly, raising the gourd in his hand.

Black King did not understand. For no reason, why did the master want him to drink with him? Was he in a bad mood?

But after opening the clay seal and smelling the aroma, Black King's eyes lit up.

It was Sweet Bitter Wine!

Among the batch of spiritual wines they had obtained back then, this was his favorite.

And the master said "have a drink," but gave him an entire jar!

Well, with his size, one jar was indeed just one cup.

Licking his lips, Black King prepared to drink heartily.

Just as he was about to drink, a sudden inspiration struck, and he said, "May the master enjoy boundless immortal blessings and live as long as heaven!"

Luo Chen was startled for a moment, then laughed heartily.

"Cheers!"

They clinked their vessels across the air, and a mouthful of bitter wine entered his throat.

Instantly, both Luo Chen and Black King wrinkled their faces.

But in a flash, an endless sweetness returned, filling their taste buds.

"Burp!"

Black King let out a wine burp, a look of enjoyment on his face.

A whole jar of Sweet Bitter Wine like this would let him savor the aftertaste for a long, long time.

"Go back to sleep. I'll call you if something comes up," Luo Chen said.

After Black King obediently returned to his left arm and continued sleeping, a hint of emotion appeared on Luo Chen's satisfied face.

This cup of wine had a certain significance.

Because today was a very important, yet not so important, day.

He was three hundred years old!

For cultivators of a certain realm, especially Sage cultivators, major birthdays were often counted in centuries.

The birthday feast held by the Zhou clan ancestor Zhou Yunshen of the Northern Sea back then had been his eight-hundredth birthday.

But now, Luo Chen sat in this cold, lonely cultivation chamber, without a beautiful woman by his side, without friends to congratulate him, only the naive Black King to share a drink with him.

Of course, even if it were not for avoiding the Ancient Demon Di Tian, he probably would not have held a so-called birthday feast outside.

Because in the knowledge of those familiar with him, he was far from three hundred years old now.

At most, he was around two hundred and fifty or sixty, roughly the lifespan limit of an Initiate cultivator.

The reason for this difference in perception was that Luo Chen had experienced an extra fifty years in the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven.

"That little Black's blessing was quite fitting for the occasion."

Luo Chen chuckled lightly, then glanced at his attribute panel.

As he had thought, the lifespan column indeed read three hundred.

And the realm he cared about had unknowingly reached [Sage Fourth Layer 10/100].

Faced with this result, Luo Chen was both satisfied and not entirely satisfied.

In the twenty years since returning to the Eastern Wilderness, after several great battles, his realm had not regressed but had instead advanced. This was certainly a cause for celebration in anyone's eyes.

Even Luo Chen felt quite proud.

But there was also a fly in the ointment.

With Luo Chen's uniquely gifted talent, supplemented by top-grade fourth-rank pills, his realm had only increased by ten progress points.

According to his estimation, he would need at least another forty to fifty years to reach Sage Fifth Layer!

For other cultivators, this would already be considered extremely fast.

But for Luo Chen, it was not fast enough, far from fast enough!

Mid-stage Sage had three minor levels, and late-stage had three more.

Even with simple multiplication, it would take three to five hundred years to reach Sage Great Perfection.

This was completely inconsistent with the expectations Luo Chen had when he formed his Sage.

A powerful soul, a terrifying physique, a thick Purple Mansion barrier, and never-lacking auxiliary cultivation resources—his cultivation configuration definitely surpassed ninety-nine percent of Sage cultivators in the world.

Where was the problem?

Was the spiritual land lacking?

Was his self-created *Withering-Bloom Dao Scripture* insufficient?

Or was he disturbed by worldly affairs?

There was no answer; perhaps every one of them was the answer!

Luo Chen sighed. Once things settled down, he would solve them one by one!

He also took a look at his body refinement realm.

At this glance, Luo Chen broke into a smile.

His body refinement realm had been forcibly halted after reaching the mid-stage of Archaic Fourth Layer, unable to advance further.

But unexpectedly, with that large batch of thousand-year Five Temple Immortal Brew, the stagnant body refinement progress had slowly begun to improve.

In just a few years, it had already increased to [Archaic Fourth Layer 50/100].

Although it was only five progress points, it was already a tremendous surprise.

At this moment, Luo Chen's mana was fully restored, his Sage was plump, source power spread throughout his body, and his five organs beat vigorously.

Compared to three years ago, Luo Chen had made considerable progress!

In particular, he had already created his own Fire Escape Art!

From now on, he had another means of protecting the Dao.

Just as Luo Chen was feeling joyful, his expression suddenly turned serious.

With a flip of his hand, a jade token appeared in his palm.

He injected mana into it, and the jade token slowly melted, forming a jade wall standing before him.

Sentences slowly emerged on the jade wall.

[The great battle at Lingtian Pass: The Ancient Demon Di Tian briefly clashed with the Nether Spring Black Marsh Ancestor, neither gaining the upper hand. After setting a three-year agreement, he withdrew calmly.]

[The vast beast tide subsequently receded, leaving nine domains, including Jueling and Bikong, as buffer zones between the human and demon races.]

[Three years, safe and sound.]

[During this time, some people occasionally tried to visit you, including cultivators from the Nether Origin Sect. We all turned them away on the pretext that you were in seclusion recovering from injuries after the great battle.]

[A few days ago, the City Lord of Lingtian personally came to Fenghua Palace, asking me to inform you to go to Lingtian Pass to pay respects to the Black Marsh Ancestor.]

[Return quickly!]

Crack, crack…

The jade wall shattered inch by inch, and the message gradually dissipated.

A precious Ten-Thousand-Li Sound Transmission Jade Token was thus wasted.

Luo Chen felt no heartache at all, only solemnity on his face.

"They want me to go pay respects to the Black Marsh Ancestor?"
