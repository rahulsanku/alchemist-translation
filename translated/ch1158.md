# Chapter 1158: Moving Mountains, Arranging Formations, Sealing the Cave in an Instant

Six hundred years of cultivation life had not been merely bitter cultivation of qi-refining methods.

Luo Chen had dabbled in all the hundred arts of immortality.

Among them, alchemy was his strongest, having already reached the level of a fifth-rank alchemist.

His other skills had also mostly reached the hall of mastery.

Take the dao of formations, for instance. Back in the Mountains and Seas Realm, Luo Chen could already be called a formation grandmaster.

When he ascended and departed, he had even left behind several grand formations for the Luo Tian Sect, to safeguard their strength.

This foundation was displayed on his attribute panel. After a large number of skills were sealed along with the disappearance of his mana, only two skills remained showing as usable.

One was the medical arts, and the other was the dao of formations!

The former was easy to understand, but the latter was somewhat strange.

Luo Chen had privately pondered why this situation had occurred, and had arrived at an answer that was relatively convincing to himself.

The dao of formations, strictly speaking, was not entirely reliant on mana and divine sense.

From the very birth of this skill...

No!

It should be said that the dao of formations had always existed. There was no such thing as birth or extinction.

It was the rotation of the sun, moon, and stars. It was the flow of mountains, rivers, and streams. It was the accompaniment of wind, rain, thunder, and lightning. It was the swaying of forest trees, flowers, and leaves.

The reason it had formed a separate skill was merely that intelligent people had summarized and categorized it, then presented it to the world in a more accessible way.

For convenience, this process had used mana and divine sense as tools to manifest formations.

But without these tools, formations still existed.

Mortals could pile up earth to form formations—the strange and bizarre arts of the Eight Gates were everywhere.

Martial artists could arrange formations with blood and qi—the martial arena in the Immortal Remnant City was a clear example.

Therefore, if Luo Chen used the most primitive methods to arrange formations, he could still reproduce the wondrous abilities of the dao of formations!

Moving mountains and creating lakes, dredging baleful qi.

Changing the terrain's pattern, guiding the flow of feng shui.

This was what Luo Chen was doing now, and what he would do next.

Since arriving at Red Leaf Ridge, Luo Chen had been observing on the spot.

The hundred Mysterious Armor soldiers he had brought were sent out by him, responsible for evacuating the surrounding populace.

Time passed, bit by bit.

Three months later, Luo Chen looked at the simple compass in his hand and the map spread across the desk that covered a radius of a hundred li. He already had an air of confidence.

Outside the camp tent, Chu Zhaonan rode up on horseback.

"Milord, the populace within a hundred li has been completely evacuated and relocated to other towns."

Luo Chen did not lift his head. "You work quickly, I see."

Chu Zhaonan cupped his hands. "Your subordinate dare not take the credit. In truth, because of Red Leaf Ridge, there were not many people nearby to begin with. The few small villages that existed were also simple to arrange."

Finally, Chu Zhaonan added, "According to the other squad's three months of uninterrupted investigation, the surrounding mountains and underground rivers have also been thoroughly explored."

Hearing this, Luo Chen slowly raised his head.

He put down the compass in his hand and took a deep breath. "In that case, let's begin!"

Soon, one command after another issued from Luo Chen's mouth.

A deputy captain of the Mysterious Armor soldiers on this trip took a letter personally written by Luo Chen and returned to the Immortal Remnant City to mobilize the massive amount of materials Luo Chen needed for his formation.

Chu Zhaonan led people to guide the water veins, gathering them toward Red Leaf Ridge.

As for Luo Chen?

The next day, he appeared before a towering mountain, a full three hundred zhang in height.

Gazing at this mountain, a rare look of nostalgia appeared in Luo Chen's eyes.

"It's been a long time since I last moved a mountain. The last time was before my ascension, when I lifted the Jambu Tree."

Exhaling a mouthful of turbid qi, Luo Chen's hands formed a Buddhist mudra, full of meditative meaning.

The next moment.

Luo Chen's figure grew with the wind, transforming in an instant into a giant a thousand zhang tall!

His muscles rolled and surged like earth dragons. His legs, thick as heavenly pillars, sank deep into the earth.

His black hair danced in the wind like giant pythons, while beneath his robust body, six arms silently hung, a terrifying sight.

Although he had not manifested the Buddhist divine ability of three heads and six arms, he was not far from his peak form.

After revealing his thousand-zhang true body, Luo Chen did not act immediately. Instead, he carefully sensed his own state.

Inside his body, streams of Blood-Refined Divine Astral Qi, accompanying the rotation of the qi and blood furnace, surged unimpeded through his enormous fleshly body, like a rushing river.

The broken, dead Purple Mansion hung above the furnace, its vitality somehow unknown.

But even in this state, the thick barrier of the Purple Mansion still left any Sage cultivator far behind.

This was the terrifying foundation formed when a five-spirit root cultivator formed their Sage core.

It was also the reason why, even after Luo Chen's primordial spirit had collapsed, his Purple Mansion had not shattered.

And inside his head, which was like a sun, a feeling of being tightly bound, magnified a thousandfold, lingered in Luo Chen's consciousness.

The sea of consciousness, where his soul resided, was wrapped and restricted by one golden line after another, to the point where his divine sense could not extend outward.

This was the divine sense sealing technique cast by Rosycloud!

Even seeing it for the first time, this seal gave Luo Chen a familiar feeling.

It was the same origin power as the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture*, which he had bitterly cultivated for hundreds of years!

"If I ever return to the immortal path one day, the first thing I will do is change my main cultivation technique."

Luo Chen thought this, then slightly moved his limbs, his attention falling on the three-hundred-zhang "small mountain" below the clouds.

His knees bent slightly. His six arms reached out in unison.

Some grabbed, some embraced, some lifted, some pulled.

"Rise!"

Accompanied by a heavy exhalation and opening of his voice, the great mountain was actually pulled out from its original location with a rumbling sound.

At the same time, ten li away, Chu Zhaonan, who had been dredging the water veins, was staring at this place in shock, together with his Mysterious Armor soldiers.

The terror of the giant made them want to turn and run.

And in the movements of the giant, who was like an ancient human ancestor carrying a mountain on a trek, they could not help but want to kneel and prostrate themselves in worship.

Chu Zhaonan inwardly screamed, a look of near madness on his face.

"What kind of person is Lord Luo Chen?"

"What kind of existence did City Lord Xiao and the others think they were provoking back then?"

...

While Luo Chen was carrying out his earth-shaking, heaven-altering great movements on his side, the Immortal Remnant City had also fallen into endless busyness with the return of that squad leader.

A list personally written by Luo Chen had set the entire Immortal Remnant City running like a machine.

For the timber, ores, and beast blood written on the list, as long as the Immortal Remnant City had them, Jing Lan spared no expense to gather them.

If rare items were owned by wealthy families, handing them over was one thing; if they refused, the consequence was the destruction of their family and household.

As for specialty products from other city districts, Jing Lan sent Xiang Fei, either exchanging benefits or seizing them by force.

And for the "human-shaped materials" specifically named on the list, Jing Lan also began to prepare them, waiting only for the day to send them all over.

Time passed quickly in this busyness.

Days, months, and even years.

Ten years later, the great movements of the Immortal Remnant City had deeply affected all the nearby large city districts of the same level.

This was not only the seven large cities that had previously offended Luo Chen, but also more than a dozen other large cities that had business dealings with the Immortal Remnant City.

It was even said that even the Carefree City had reacted.

Fortunately, after ten years, the Immortal Remnant City's movements had paused, as if nothing had ever happened.

The Carefree City also did not send anyone to inquire.

But for Chu Zhaonan and the others, facing the familiar yet unfamiliar terrain standing before them, how could they pretend nothing had happened?

Red Leaf Ridge was still Red Leaf Ridge.

But where was even half the original appearance of Red Leaf Ridge to be seen in the emerald lake, the canals where blood qi and baleful qi flowed simultaneously, and the five surrounding peaks that blotted out the sky?

Such a great divine ability to alter heaven and earth was something he had never seen in his life, at most only heard from some storytellers.

But Luo Chen had truly demonstrated it before his very eyes.

"Before Milord came to the Great Destruction Realm, he must have been a great existence who could pluck stars and seize the moon!"

Amidst such speculation, a solitary figure walked out from the mountain path leading to Red Leaf Ridge.

He casually waved his hand, and the surrounding rocks flew up as if controlled, then neatly blocked the only mountain path that could enter or leave Red Leaf Ridge.

"Milord, is it finished?"

When Luo Chen approached, Chu Zhaonan asked cautiously.

"Finished?"

Luo Chen smiled and shook his head. With such primitive methods of arranging formations, it was not so easy to finish.

He had only laid down the prototypes of three grand formations inside.

These three grand formations were the Blood Baleful Demon Cloud Formation of the Primordial Demon Sect, the Dream Bubble Illusion Formation learned from the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's book *Immortal Secrets of the Stone Chamber*, and a self-created Five Ghosts Soul-Locking Formation based on his own situation.

Next, he had to wait for them to grow naturally.

Relying on the power of time, they would slowly accumulate and take shape. Only then would there be a chance to achieve Luo Chen's plan to unlock his soul seal.

"Let's go. The next time I come, I don't know when it will be."

Luo Chen waved his hand and stepped onto the carriage that had already been prepared.

Chu Zhaonan was worried that someone might come to destroy the arrangements here and planned to leave people to guard them, but Luo Chen refused.

The feng shui pattern here had already changed. It was an out-and-out great ominous land!

Ordinary martial artists were simply not suited to guard this place.

Moreover, he had left blood qi restrictions at several key nodes. If anyone dared to destroy the arrangements here, Luo Chen could sense it quickly.

Most importantly, any martial artist with some knowledge would not dare to recklessly enter such a great ominous place.

As for ordinary people?

Luo Chen smiled and closed the carriage curtain.

In front, the Mysterious Armor soldiers led the way. Behind were the craftsmen who had been sent here over the years. A procession of over a thousand people marched grandly toward the Immortal Remnant City.

Behind the procession was Red Leaf Valley, surrounded by five peaks, with the emerald lake reflecting the sky.

In the valley, a Dream Illusion Tree covered in scarlet leaves was swaying its branches, as if it had come alive.

...

Another year was drawing to a close.

In the deep mansion, the fragrance of food wafted, and the air was drunk with wine.

To celebrate Luo Chen's safe return, Jing Lan had personally cooked a table of fine dishes.

Those seated at the table, besides Luo Chen, were only Xiang Fei.

But compared to Luo Chen and Xiang Fei, who looked young but were actually old monsters of considerable age, Jing Lan, who was originally the youngest, instead appeared increasingly aged.

She was already over fifty years old.

In this harsh environment of the Great Destruction Realm, this was already a high age among mortals.

"These years have been hard on you."

Luo Chen raised his glass in a toast, a rare display of genuine emotion.

Having held the position of city lord for many years, Jing Lan had long lost her restraint, but under Luo Chen's initiative to raise his glass in toast, she still felt somewhat unaccustomed.

"Brother Luo, these are all things I should do. There's no need for this."

Luo Chen sighed softly and still drank the wine in his cup.

Seeing this, Jing Lan had no choice but to drink Luo Chen's toast.

She was somewhat puzzled as to why Luo Chen had sighed. Had this trip not gone smoothly?

Luo Chen, however, did not explain much.

This soft sigh was not for himself, but for Jing Lan.

Even though he had pushed blood through her palace, nourished her with qi and blood, and supplemented her with precious medicinal herbs, Jing Lan's physical condition was still irreversibly declining.

This was not only the growth trend of a mortal, but also due to her special Moonstone Body.

At most, in one or two decades, at most in four or five decades, Jing Lan might reach the end of her lifespan.

Luo Chen had seen many fellow cultivators sit in meditation and pass away at the end of their lifespans. Most accepted it with a peaceful mindset. Some even used the situation of others whose insufficient cultivation levels led to death from lifespan exhaustion to encourage themselves to cultivate harder.

But he had never seen a mortal close to him pass away from the end of their lifespan.

Luo Chen did not know what kind of mood he would be in when that day came.

He stood up and walked to the window, looking at the goose-feather snow falling in the courtyard.

Jing Lan watched his back, somehow feeling the illusion that he might ride the wind and leave at any moment.

The woman bit her lip and suddenly gathered her courage to ask, "Brother Luo, is there a place you want to go?"

Luo Chen, with his back to her, smiled. "Does the Carefree City count?"

Jing Lan shook her head. "That place, Brother Luo will definitely go, but it probably doesn't count as a 'want.' In my opinion, the place Brother Luo wants to go must be completely different from every city in the Great Destruction Realm."

Luo Chen fell silent.

Where was the place he wanted to go?

At first, it should have been that dreamlike Immortal Realm!

But if Rosycloud had not lied to him, the Immortal Realm had collapsed, the immortal path was severed, and this dream was already an illusion.

Even if he left the Great Destruction Realm, left the Guixu, where would he go?

Somehow, a name surged into his heart.

The Xuan Realm!

There, Chu Yan, who had given him the grace of a single pill, should be.

There, the demon phoenix Rosycloud, who had caused him to fall to this state, was also there.

There, there were cultivation techniques and resources to reach the Great Vehicle stage, as well as channels to understand the secrets of the Immortal Realm.

If he could leave this world that would eventually perish, then he would definitely make a trip to the Xuan Realm!

Having figured this out, the confusion in Luo Chen's eyes vanished.

He turned around and sat back down at the table.

He raised his glass in invitation, toasting the New Year together!

The next day.

In the newly rebuilt Tide-Watching Pavilion, Xiang Fei meticulously explained to Luo Chen the crucial points of breaking through from the Furnace Realm to the Leakless Realm.

"The human body has thirty-six major acupoints, countless minor ones. The points involved in cultivation total seven hundred and twenty."

"During the Blood River Realm, there is a minor realm called the Aperture-Opening Realm, but the requirements at that time were not high. Opening the thirty-six major acupoints was the main goal."

"But the Furnace Realm is different. You need to use your own astral qi to open all the major and minor acupoints throughout your body. And you must carefully polish them, achieving the state of opening and closing at will. This step is not only to prepare for breaking through to the Leakless Realm, but also to prepare for cultivation at higher realms after the Leakless Realm."

"And using overbearing astral qi to polish fragile acupoints is a very dangerous thing. Watch me demonstrate personally."

Accompanied by Xiang Fei's explanation, Luo Chen gained a more vivid understanding of the final step of cultivation in the Furnace Realm.

This was truly a matter of water grinding, with no room for shortcuts.

Anyone who tried to take shortcuts would end up badly.

For example, Tie Xiong had taken a shortcut to break through to the Leakless Realm, but the result was that when facing an opponent of the same level, he had no power to fight back.

For Luo Chen, the situation seemed even better than imagined.

The reason was that back when he was at the Initiate stage, he had already expended a great price to fully open all seven hundred and twenty major and minor acupoints.

At that time, he had used earthly baleful qi and lightning power to forcefully open many acupoints, and he had suffered enough for it.

Now, he could enjoy some of the dividends.

When Xiang Fei used herself as an example to demonstrate the action of polishing acupoints with astral qi, Luo Chen pondered for a moment, then mobilized his Blood-Refined Divine Astral Qi and charged straight into a major acupoint.

"Is it like this?"

In just a moment, a layer of qi membrane formed over that major acupoint.

Although thin, it was clearly visible, and it had sealed off the leakage of Luo Chen's qi.

Xiang Fei was stunned.

She remembered that when she had first attempted this step, she had been extremely careful, not daring to be the least bit negligent, and had suffered greatly.

But she had not expected Luo Chen to succeed in an instant.

Were there really people in this world who were born with knowledge?

(Chapter End)
