# Chapter 965: Without a Heart, Can One Live?

"Roar!"

An ear-splitting sound came from beyond the heavens.

It echoed through Yin-Yang Tomb, seizing the hearts and minds of all present.

Amidst the lightning and thunder, in almost an instant, it broke through the broken formations outside the tomb and descended into the main burial chamber.

Li Tianshi suddenly looked up, a sharp light flashing in his eyes.

He raised his hand casually.

A cerulean light screen rose slowly around him, centered on his position.

This light screen flowed like a gentle stream, continuous and unbroken. As it flew upward, it suddenly became extremely tough, blocking the ferocious dragon head outside.

A harsh scraping sound rang out, revealing the stalemate between the two.

Standing beneath the light screen, Li Tianshi clicked his tongue in admiration. "This strike has been building for a long time—it was no hasty move. Luo Chen, you planned this well in advance!"

In the distance, Luo Chen's brows furrowed.

He recognized it. This cerulean light screen was the same technique that had previously blocked the Lion Prime's attack on the Purple-Robed Marquis and the other man.

He remembered the Lion Prime calling it a "Glazed Gu."

A gu insect specifically used for defense?

A fierce look flashed across Luo Chen's face. "Without a plan, how would I dare travel with an old fox like you?"

As he spoke, the hand seal at his fingertips suddenly changed.

"Explode!"

The flame dragon roared, then crashed violently against the light screen. The vast spiritual energy that formed its body began to disintegrate in an instant.

An extreme force was suddenly unleashed.

Crack!

Under Li Tianshi's stunned gaze, cracks appeared in the cerulean light screen.

Through his spiritual connection, he sensed that the Glazed Gu that formed this technique had been killed outright by the terrifying force.

"Have I still underestimated him?"

Facing Luo Chen's exquisite Dao techniques and his decisive, swift adaptability in battle, a deep wariness arose in Li Tianshi's heart.

But on the surface, he showed nothing.

Looking at Luo Chen charging toward him from afar, and the threat of rippling spatial distortions all around, he smiled slightly.

"You people are too annoying. This old man is busy opening the coffin—you should play with my little ones first!"

He scattered something casually. Ten golden beans, shining like soybeans, fell to the ground.

A cry of alarm came from the void.

"They're Bean Soldier Gu! Luo Chen, be careful!"

He didn't need the warning. Luo Chen had already noticed and had even struck first.

Opening his mouth, he spat out three wisps of azure-white true flame.

In an instant, they burned away three of the golden beans—those with the strongest aura, the fullest shape, and the richest color.

But the remaining seven were too late.

Shua! Shua! Shua!

In the spacious hall, cracking sounds came one after another.

It was as if beans were exploding inside an extremely hot cauldron.

The next moment, seven figures appeared before their eyes.

Each was shrouded in a black robe, each exuding the powerful aura of the Sage realm.

As soon as they appeared, three rushed toward Luo Chen and Meng Shentong, while one remained guarding Li Tianshi, even having the spare strength to press down the triple blood shackles that suppressed the Flying Nightwalker.

Facing the three Sage cultivators charging at him, Luo Chen neither dodged nor evaded. He simply threw a punch.

"Even the Purple-Robed Marquis and his two companions couldn't stop me. You think this petty trick can?"

Bang!

With one punch, qi surged in all directions.

The entire space seemed to compress.

The black robes were blown away, revealing the rigid, stiff, and now-deforming faces of cultivators within.

Luo Chen's body moved like drifting catkins, twisting and turning in the air as his iron fists struck repeatedly.

Bang!

Bang!

Bang!

After three consecutive punches, Luo Chen's figure suddenly halted.

And the three Sage cultivators charging at him exploded on the spot, their bodies and bones utterly destroyed.

They hadn't even had time to unleash any of their techniques—a testament to the ferocity of Luo Chen's assault.

But fast as he was, Meng Shentong was no slower.

He directly used his void-concealment technique, bypassing the three enemies and quietly approaching Li Tianshi.

Just as he was about to strike, his figure flickering in and out of visibility, Li Tianshi's mocking voice rang out.

"After all, that's not the true Vacuum Gu passed down through the Luo Sect's millennia of heritage. The one you cultivated later has too many flaws!"

The black-robed cultivator guarding Li Tianshi crouched slightly, his hands forming an embracing gesture.

A vortex of airflow suddenly expanded.

Spinning rapidly, the vortex grew wildly.

A violent suction force instantly forced Meng Shentong's figure into visibility, even dragging him step by step closer.

As the fierce wind howled, it lifted the hood of the black robe, revealing the true face beneath.

Seeing that dark, sinister countenance, Meng Shentong's heart trembled violently, and he blurted out,

"Devil Heart Gu Emperor!"

Wrong!

It wasn't the Devil Heart Gu Emperor—it was the older generation's Devil Heart Gu Emperor.

Only, Li Tianshi had refined its physical body into a Bean Soldier Gu.

Ignoring Meng Shentong, who was struggling against the vortex, Li Tianshi cast a wary glance at Luo Chen, then turned back and began forming hand seals toward the black coffin.

In the distance.

Luo Chen's advancing footsteps suddenly stopped.

Because three more Sage cultivators had surrounded him.

He didn't know where Li Tianshi had obtained so many Sage cultivators' bodies as materials for refining gu insects.

If he hadn't acted decisively earlier, destroying the three Bean Soldier Gu that matched the old Devil Heart Gu Emperor's aura, the situation would be even worse now.

But even so, under the successive obstructions, Li Tianshi had already gained enough time to open the black coffin.

Luo Chen glanced at Meng Shentong, who was locked in a stalemate with the airflow vortex, and made a decision in an instant.

Instead of advancing, he retreated, his figure flashing to a corner of the wall.

His hands turned azure-black as he grabbed fiercely.

"Ah…"

A miserable scream suddenly rang out.

It was the old woman, Granny Hongye, who had been pinned to the ground by a sword.

"Bear with it!"

"I'll pull the sword out for you and give you a chance for revenge."

Without waiting for Granny Hongye's consent, Luo Chen gripped the sword hilt, ignoring the corrosive corpse poison that made green smoke hiss from his palm.

With a low shout,

"Come out!"

He pulled hard, yanking the sword out in one motion and tossing it into a corner.

Then, with a flick of his finger, several pills flew into the old woman's wounds.

The pills entered her body, were refined in an instant, and spread through her limbs and bones.

Though there wasn't time to expel the corpse poison, it was enough to temporarily suppress it.

Granny Hongye struggled to her feet, looking at Luo Chen with gratitude.

His expression was cold and stern; he simply pointed toward Li Tianshi.

Granny Hongye's face twisted with hatred. "The grudge of being stabbed in the back, I will never forget. That black coffin—don't think you can open it so easily!"

Her words were filled with bitter resentment.

How many years had it been? As the lofty supreme elder of the Hongye Sect, she had never suffered such humiliation and pain.

Especially since this humiliation came from her old friend of many years, Li Tianshi!

The next moment.

Granny Hongye, enduring the agony of the poison, manipulated the tortoiseshell compass in front of her. Streams of light flew out from the Myriad Formation Gu.

These streams of light didn't fly toward the black coffin. Instead, they sank into every direction—into the walls, the floor, and deep into the earth of Yin-Yang Tomb.

"Is she trying to draw on the residual formation power here?"

Luo Chen glanced thoughtfully at the gu insect that looked like a tortoiseshell, then turned around calmly.

The three black-robed Sage cultivators formed from Bean Soldier Gu finally arrived, each offering their treasures and casting spells at Luo Chen.

In response, Luo Chen simply took a step.

All the attacks, just as they were about to hit him, deviated by a few inches.

Walking as if strolling through a garden, Luo Chen brushed past them.

Sharp sword qi flickered at his fingertips. Wherever it passed, the bodies of the three Sage cultivators cracked like spiderwebs.

Inside, there were no Sages formed from the convergence of essence, qi, and spirit.

"Empty Sage aura, but no true Sage substance. Li Tianshi, your methods are far too weak."

As he mocked, Luo Chen opened his mouth and spat.

Whoosh!

A sword pellet shot through the air, flying straight at Li Tianshi's back.

Unexpectedly, Li Tianshi didn't even block this attack from behind. He let the sword pellet hit him.

Hum…

A strange sound rang out. The next moment, something astonishing happened.

That platinum sword pellet, upon touching Li Tianshi's body, vanished eerily.

Like a clay ox sinking into the sea, it left no trace.

No matter how Luo Chen urged his divine sense, he couldn't perceive the existence of the sword pellet.

"A spatial treasure, or some bizarre divine ability?"

Luo Chen frowned. From a distance, he once again unleashed the Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain technique.

At the same time, Meng Shentong, having paid a heavy price, finally broke free from the airflow vortex's restraint.

As soon as he charged out, he raised his hand.

A shimmering long blade appeared in his hand, and he slashed horizontally downward.

The Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain and the void slash fell simultaneously.

The bizarre scene appeared again!

Both powerful attacks, just as they were about to touch Li Tianshi, vanished abruptly.

As if swallowed whole by some giant beast opening its maw.

The disturbance dissipated without cause.

Li Tianshi didn't even turn his head. His eyes were fixed intently on the black coffin.

With a low shout,

"Open!"

The next moment, the black coffin trembled violently. The lid shifted, and a crack gradually appeared.

A corner of the coffin's interior came into view.

It was a face—half yin, half yang; half black, half white!

Just then, a sharp, aged voice rang through the hall.

"Don't even think about it!"

One chain after another materialized from the tomb walls, flying like swallows returning to their nest, landing on the black coffin.

The lid, which had just been pried open a crack, snapped shut instantly.

Seeing this, a trace of anger finally appeared on Li Tianshi's calm face.

"You deserve to die!"

In his furious roar, one gu insect after another flew out from his body. In the blink of an eye, there were hundreds, thousands—countless.

With him at the center, the countless gu insects formed a pavilion-like structure.

This small pavilion began to expand outward in all directions with an unstoppable momentum.

Seeing this, Meng Shentong's expression changed drastically.

"It's a Gu House!"

He didn't hesitate. He shot directly toward the passage entrance.

But a despairing sight appeared—the passage entrance began to squirm and contract, leaving no chance to escape.

When she heard the name "Gu House," even Granny Hongye's face showed a flicker of panic and fear. Her control over the formation gu insects faltered for a moment.

Li Tianshi seized this opportunity. A flash of illusory fire passed over his hands as he swept them across the densely packed chains on the coffin lid.

"Open again!"

With a thunderous roar, the entire lid of the black coffin was pushed open.

Inside, a young man in a black-and-white Daoist robe appeared before everyone's eyes.

In his hands, he held a jade bowl.

Inside the bowl, two black-and-white fish swam slowly.

Seeing this, Li Tianshi reached out and grabbed.

Just then, sparks flickered beside him.

"Luo Chen!"

Li Tianshi's eyes nearly split with rage. The old-generation Devil Heart Gu Emperor instantly blocked behind him.

But Luo Chen, who had expended great magical power to use the Fire Concealment Art to appear abruptly at close range, paid no attention. His right hand reached straight out.

One hand pierced through the old Devil Heart Gu Emperor's body. His arm stretched unnaturally, wreathed in golden flames, and plunged into Li Tianshi's back.

"Your Devil Heart Gu, I'll take it!"

Luo Chen laughed lightly, grabbed Li Tianshi's heart, and yanked it back abruptly!

Illusory flames chased after him.

But in the face of the golden flames, they couldn't stop him for even a moment.

Hu…

He exhaled a turbid breath. Bursting to a corner, Luo Chen looked down at the object in his hand, a joyful light flickering in his eyes.

Thump! Thump! Thump!

A vividly colored heart, even separated from the body, even with its blood vessels severed, still beat vigorously.

Its abundant vitality was plain to see.

"Is this the Devil Heart Gu?"

Not far away, Li Tianshi, with a hole through his chest and back, slowly turned around. In his hand, he held a jade bowl.

He pushed aside the old Devil Heart Gu Emperor's broken body, letting it fall to the ground. His eyes were fixed venomously on Luo Chen.

"How did you know my Devil Heart Gu dwells in my heart?"

Luo Chen weighed the heart in his hand. Its beating grew even more violent.

Under Li Tianshi's narrowed gaze, he chuckled lightly. "That wasn't hard to guess. Since it's called the Devil Heart Gu, it naturally resides in the heart."

"Because of the name?" Li Tianshi didn't believe Luo Chen would risk such a bold grab based on mere speculation.

Luo Chen shrugged, a hint of flippancy in his tone. "In the Xiongyue Hall, I listened carefully to your lecture, Elder."

"To enter the Dao through gu, one must not treat them as mere auxiliary cultivation tools. One must understand their principles, comprehend their nature, and achieve dual cultivation of nature and life, so that one may advance without hindrance."

"That's what you said, and that's what I heard. I imagine you practice what you preach."

"Especially since the *Hundred Poisons Body-Tempering Art* you taught me also requires placing the king gu in the human stomach."

"Following the same logic, I was very willing to believe that your Devil Heart Gu was raised right on your devil heart!"

As he finished speaking, he flicked his wrist, and the living heart vanished instantly.

"Now, can you tell me—without a heart, can one live?"

Li Tianshi's body trembled slightly. He stared fixedly at Luo Chen.

He now understood why, when he had offered to give Luo Chen the Devil Heart Gu in exchange for killing Meng Shentong, Luo Chen had refused without hesitation.

From the very beginning, Luo Chen had guessed that the Devil Heart Gu had fused with his heart.

How could a person give away his own heart?

He was not yet an Ascendant cultivator. Without a heart, his physical body would wither, his Sage would have no place to dwell, and the Great Dao would be hopeless.

So that condition had never been valid from the start.

And now, this little thief dared to ask him if one could live without a heart?

"One can live. Of course, one can live!"

Li Tianshi viciously tossed the jade bowl in his hand into the air. A swarm of gu insects flew up, plucked the two fish from the bowl, and merged them into the Gu House.

"After I kill you all, I will still live!"

The next moment, the Gu House—Wind-Wave Pavilion—fully expanded, enveloping the entire Yin-Yang Tomb.

Not only that, but Li Tianshi raised his hand, and the Corpse Rot Sword flew into his grasp.

On the ground, the triple blood shackles, now empty, rose up and locked around his hollow chest.

Moreover, a wisp of illusory flame blazed up from his body.

Li Tianshi soared into the air, sword in hand, body wreathed in flames, looking down at the three people on the ground, each with different expressions.

"Now, it's your turn to bear this old man's wrath!"

(Chapter End)
