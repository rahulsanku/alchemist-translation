# Chapter 731: A Homeless Dog, Luo Chen's Grudge

A group of people had arrived in Divine Origin City.

This was not strange in itself. Although Divine Origin City was faintly surrounded by the demon-beast coalition, the name of Central Continent's Tianyuan Dao Sect pressed down on it, and over all these years, the demon beasts had never truly attacked the city. For this reason, cultivators had always been coming and going.

Only, this newly arrived group drew attention from every side the moment they entered the city.

Among them were cultivators of every realm, most at the Initiate and Adept stages. They were relatively young, and there were both men and women.

The one thing they shared was the same clan emblem on their clothing, a pattern of coral flowers.

Their bodies reeked of blood. Their expressions were numb, yet also carried a trace of the relief that came after surviving calamity.

In the dark, strands of divine sense exchanged words.

"These are cultivators of the Coral Sea Zhou clan [TERM?], are they not?"

"Judging by that distinctive clan badge, there should be no mistake. I did not expect that of the Myriad Immortals Assembly's three great Sage clans, the Coral Sea Zhou clan would be the one to hold out until the end."

"The Heavenly Channel Chen clan [TERM?] had the Desperate Peril Channel [TERM?] as a natural moat, and Endless Valley [TERM?] was known as a place from which no myriad demons returned. Yet both were broken by the Demon Alliance [TERM?] army within a few short years. Instead, the utterly ordinary Coral Sea Zhou clan, with no natural defenses to rely on, actually held on for a full twenty years. Tsk, tsk..."

"Being able to hold on for so long is indeed praiseworthy, but the Zhou clan once had more than a hundred million clansmen and over ten thousand cultivators. Now, I fear only these few people are left."

"Fellow Daoist Li, shall we find an opportunity to visit the Zhou clan ancestor together? In the past, we thought that since he had passed eight hundred years of age and was trapped at the fourth level of the Sage stage, he was nothing more than dry bones in the grave. Now it seems he could lead his clan cultivators out of a heavy encirclement. He truly hid his depths!"

"Good!"

The survivor party made up of several dozen Zhou clan members was currently undergoing inspection by Divine Origin City's cultivators to prevent transformation-stage Sage demon cultivators from slipping into the city.

And they discovered that ever since entering the city, their clan ancestor had vanished.

For a time, each of their hearts grew uneasy.

Their ancestor was their heaven!

Now that heaven was gone, everyone panicked.

In the end, an older, steadier person stepped forward on his own initiative to calm the crowd, saying the ancestor had gone to meet a friend and prepare a place to settle the clan cultivators.

After this consolation, everyone finally settled down.

Only, under the appraising gazes of Divine Origin City's many unfamiliar cultivators, the Zhou clan cultivators still could not help lowering their heads, like homeless dogs.

...

Below Little Qiong Mountain, a long-bearded elder in green robes hesitated for a time. In the end, he still lifted his feet and stepped into the mountain.

As he entered, the clouds and mist opened of their own accord, yielding a path.

This represented that the master of this place already knew of his arrival.

Seeing this scene, the green-robed elder, for some reason, instead felt his heart settle.

He walked forward all the way, and in the end, halfway up the mountain, inside a cool pavilion, he saw a young man with a smile on his face.

The young man wore white robes, a jade belt with gold-red cloud patterns wrapped around his waist, and his pitch-black hair fell loose behind him, gathered only by a single wooden hairpin.

When the young man saw him, he chuckled and shook his head.

"Fellow Daoist followed my spirit pet all the way here and came uninvited. That is somewhat impolite."

The green-robed elder hesitated, looking at the man's face, which was vastly different from the one in his memory. For a moment, he could not confirm it.

He probed, "Devil Lord Azure Yang, Luo Hai [TERM?]?"

The corners of Luo Chen's mouth lifted slightly. "This humble one is Luo Chen, styled Wilderness Wanderer."

Luo Hai, Luo Chen?

Devil Lord, Wanderer?

The green-robed elder shook his head. "I do not know who you truly are. Perhaps even Wilderness Wanderer is merely one of your aliases. But that black jiaolong, I do recognize. It is the spirit pet of Peng Lake's master in the Myriad Immortals Assembly, Devil Lord Azure Yang."

Luo Chen asked in reply, "Could Devil Lord Azure Yang's spirit pet have such ability?"

The green-robed elder fell somewhat silent, but very quickly, he said firmly, "If Devil Lord Azure Yang can become Wilderness Wanderer, then it is only natural that his spirit pet could make such progress."

Luo Chen was speechless.

Fine. Since coming out of seclusion, the false identity he had prepared had been exposed one after another before Fu Chaosheng and the Zhou clan ancestor, but neither exposure was because his disguise was inadequate. Rather, it was because the two great spirit pets under his command had each revealed him.

Looking at it this way, among high-level cultivators, perfectly hiding oneself was no longer limited to name, appearance, aura, and divine sense.

Entirely different divine sense and magic power, two completely different sets of signature methods, even one's travel mount, spirit pets, and servants: all these facets had to be considered.

Since that was so, there was no need to conceal it.

Luo Chen smiled faintly, cupped his hands, and bowed. "This humble one is Luo Chen. Greetings, Fellow Daoist Zhou."

The green-robed elder released a breath and returned the salute. "Coral Sea... Zhou Yunshen [TERM?]. Greetings, Fellow Daoist Luo."

He had already concluded that the other party was Devil Lord Azure Yang, whose name had once resounded through the Myriad Immortals Assembly. Since Luo Chen was being so solemn, "Luo Chen" was probably his true name.

Only...

Zhou Yunshen sensed the faintly discernible fluctuations of magic power on the other party, as well as that unrestrained divine sense. His feelings grew complicated, and his tone was filled with endless emotion.

"When this old man celebrated his eight-hundredth birthday, Fellow Daoist was only at the Adept stage. In a mere few decades, you have actually broken through to the Sage stage. It is truly astonishing!"

Luo Chen smiled. "Is Fellow Daoist not also hiding his depths? The world only thought you were at the fourth level of the Sage stage and already advanced in years, yet who knew you had long since reached the sixth level of the Sage stage, only one step away from the realm of a seventh-level great cultivator?"

During his days in the Myriad Immortals Assembly, Luo Chen had not spent all his time cultivating behind closed doors.

Not only had he made wide connections within the Myriad Immortals Assembly, he had also maintained contact with the three external forces that formed the Myriad Immortals Assembly.

There was no need to say much about Endless Valley.

As for the Coral Sea Zhou clan, they were the side with which he had the deepest connection.

Not only had a Zhou clan woman married Zhang Jiadi, who ran errands for him, Luo Chen had once attended the eight-hundredth birthday of Zhou Yunshen, the Zhou clan ancestor, and had even received a personal audience from him at the time.

Especially later, when Luo Chen needed to refine the fourth-tier Poison-Resisting Pill for the trip to Purple Spirit Island, the Zhou clan ancestor had opened the door for convenience and ordered people to send over the related spirit herbs he needed.

These minor details looked like nothing in ordinary times, but once the two reached equal realms, they became what could be called a connection.

At this moment, they flattered each other a little; a flowered sedan had to be carried by people on both sides.

Only, on Zhou Yunshen's aged face, there was now only a bitter smile.

"What of the sixth level of the Sage stage? No matter how deeply one hides, before absolute power, it merely lets one hold on a little longer."

At this point, he suddenly bowed to Luo Chen again.

"This time, I must thank Fellow Daoist for dispatching your spirit pet and resolving the Zhou clan's trapped situation."

Luo Chen raised his brow. "Black King?"

Zhou Yunshen straightened, then nodded with lingering fear. "Indeed! At that time, the Coral Sea had been besieged for twenty years and had already reached the end of its road. If Black King had not sent tens of thousands of dragon earthworms [TERM?] to disrupt the situation, this old man likely would not have been able to bring out the clan's final bloodline. Perhaps, when the true desperate moment arrived, I could only have left on my own and become a solitary man."

The situation had actually reached such a point?

Luo Chen was somewhat surprised.

Recently, he had been in seclusion and quiet cultivation the whole time, knowing very little of outside intelligence. He had not expected even the Coral Sea to have already "fallen."

Seeing his surprise, Zhou Yunshen guessed that the other party had probably been occupied these years with breaking through his realm and knew nothing of outside affairs, so he simply explained the Myriad Immortals Assembly's situation in brief.

After the Demon Alliance escaped from Arctic Yemo Heaven, its first target was the Myriad Immortals Assembly.

The Three Oceans defensive line, maintained for two hundred years, was broken in several days.

The Demon Alliance began invading the Myriad Immortals Assembly's headquarters, the Fulong Mountain Range, on a grand scale.

With Mad Wanderer and Blood Wanderer absent, and Sage Dragon Abyss missing, the Chen clan ancestor, Endless Valley's Spirit-Control Sage, and Zhou Yunshen himself went to the Myriad Immortals Assembly to help Moon Wanderer resist the demon race army.

But who would have known that Moon Wanderer would withdraw and leave at the critical moment, causing the Myriad Immortals Assembly's countless rogue cultivators to be left without a leader.

The three great Sages naturally could not defend to the death under those circumstances, so they each retreated.

After that, the Heavenly Channel Chen clan was broken, and the Chen clan ancestor fled.

Endless Valley, known as a place no myriad demons returned from, repelled three waves of demon-beast assaults in succession, but on the fourth time, it drew over a powerful Demon Emperor. Not only was Endless Valley destroyed, even the famous Spirit-Control Sage fell on the spot.

Last of all was the Coral Sea Zhou clan.

"In truth, the demon-beast armies attacking us were only a small portion of the Demon Alliance's strength. The main force all went south to assault the Cang Hai Alliance's territory. Otherwise, my Zhou clan could not have held out for so long."

Zhou Yunshen's expression was bitter and miserable, showing how difficult these years had been.

Especially when Spirit-Control Sage, with whom he was on good terms, had relied on Endless Valley's terrain to defend to the death, yet also been defeated by that very terrain. Not even his Nascent Soul escaped.

Luo Chen was secretly startled. Sage cultivators were in fact extremely difficult to kill.

Even if the physical body was destroyed, the Nascent Soul could use its instinct to merge into heaven and earth and perform the method of void teleportation.

Unless the enemy possessed some wondrous magic treasure like the Primordial Unity Cauldron, capable of seizing a cultivator's Nascent Soul.

He pursed his lips and suddenly asked, "And before that?"

Zhou Yunshen did not understand.

Luo Chen repeated, "Before the Demon Alliance, after Moon Wanderer returned, what changes occurred within the Myriad Immortals Assembly? Especially... things related to me."

Hearing this, Zhou Yunshen's wrinkled face showed a trace of unnaturalness.

Seeing Luo Chen insist, he finally sighed and said, "At the time, rumors were flying everywhere outside, all saying Moon Wanderer had obtained enormous benefits in the Fallen Sea. The rumors also mentioned how imposing you were, and how you killed Feiyunzi across realms. I do not know the true situation. But after Moon Wanderer returned, she began sweeping away forces connected to you within the Myriad Immortals Assembly on a large scale."

"As far as I know, Peng Lake was taken back, and the Zhang family of Cailian Island was exterminated. Only Zhou Shanshan, my junior of who knows how many generations removed, survived, and that was because Moon Wanderer gave me face. Mm, Moon Wanderer hid even deeper than I did. In truth, she had long since reached late-stage Sage. She also knew I possessed sixth-level Sage cultivation, and only then did she give me that thin bit of face."

"That little Azure Yang shop your subordinate once opened in Longyuan Immortal City is gone. Not only that, rogue cultivators connected to you were also taken away. I hear they suffered no small amount of abuse."

"The rogue cultivator who was interrogated seemed to be called Duan Li? Later, he seems to have died under the demon-beast assault. As for the specifics, this old man does not know."

"The Earth Mulberry Sect [TERM?] was wiped out entirely. That part was rather regrettable. Among rogue cultivators, anyone skilled in spirit-plant cultivation is a talent!"

Luo Chen listened quietly, but at some unknown point, his originally calm and breezy face had already turned iron-blue.

Standing to the side, Zhou Yunshen grew uncomfortable as he spoke. Clearly, his own realm was much higher than the other party's, yet in this moment, it was as though he were facing a volcano that had been building for a long time.

That deep killing intent was scorching as flame and cold as ice, making a man restless in both sitting and standing.

Was this the baleful aura of someone who had killed Sage cultivators while still at the Adept stage?

In his own life, Zhou Yunshen had never accomplished the feat of killing a Sage of the same realm.

Zhou Yunshen shook his head and forced himself to say, "Actually, there is some good news. Moon Wanderer wanted to capture an Adept rogue cultivator called Lord Lushan [TERM?], but she could not find any trace of him. Also, the Zhang family was not completely exterminated. One surviving female cultivator hid in a cavern deep beneath Peng Lake and fortunately lived. Later, my descendant brought her back to the Zhou clan."

In the face of this tiny piece of good news, Luo Chen's iron-blue expression did not change in the slightest.

Only after listening did he coldly spit out three words.

"Moon Wanderer!"

At this moment, heaven-filling killing intent was no longer concealed. Invisible flames spread from Luo Chen's body, burning the void until it twisted.

But this killing intent came quickly and vanished quickly, just like that strange flame flashing once before disappearing.

Luo Chen took a deep breath and forced out a smile.

"Thank you for telling me these things."

"Compared to your saving my Zhou clan, it is truly insignificant." At this point, Zhou Yunshen hesitated slightly. "Black King took that Zhang family orphan girl away from among my clan members. I wonder how that woman will be arranged in the future?"

Zhang Jingqing?

Without thinking, Luo Chen said, "Thank you for caring for her all these years. In any case, her father once served me for many years. In the future, naturally I will look after her."

Zhou Yunshen looked as though he had expected this long ago, and then a storage bag appeared in his hand.

"Since that is so, I will have to trouble you to pass these things to her. Consider it compensation for these years."

Compensation?

Luo Chen frowned.

Zhou Yunshen did not seem to want to say more. Using the excuse that his clansmen had just arrived in Divine Origin City and had not yet settled down, he took his leave.

Luo Chen did not force him to stay. He only arranged a time to chat again when they were free.

After Zhou Yunshen left, Luo Chen stood quietly in the mountain pavilion for a very long time before returning to the cave abode.

The moment he returned, he saw a beautiful woman standing there gracefully, her eyes full of worry as she looked at Black King curled up on the ground.

Luo Chen glanced at him, then joined two fingers and swept them out. A strand of azure-gold flame suddenly appeared, enveloping Black King's body.

In an instant, the azure-gold flame tangled together with the deep-blue flames on Black King's back.

"Was it Shark Emperor Liujun [TERM?]?"

Black King bared his teeth. "It was him. Of the three Demon Emperors besieging the Zhou clan, one was that Shark Emperor Liujun we met before. In the sea, I naturally am not afraid of him! But the flames released from that oil lamp of his are too great a threat to my desolate beast body."

Luo Chen understood.

He had witnessed the might of Shark Emperor Liujun's oil lamp.

Even Luo Chen back then, with a fourth-tier Archaic fleshly body, could not withstand that deep-blue flame by force. He could only cut off one arm himself and still needed the Withering-Bloom True Flame to isolate the blue flame's corrosion.

Black King's current physique was similar to Luo Chen's back then. Even though he had an innate undying body, he had no true flame to assist him, so naturally he was not Shark Emperor Liujun's match.

Rumor said that in early years, sharkfolk [TERM?] had been servants of desolate beasts, and only later had they risen up and become masters.

The blue flame lit with sharkfolk oil was specially used to restrain desolate beast bodies.

Now, directly wiping away this residual wisp of blue flame on Black King would be somewhat wasteful.

With a thought from Luo Chen, the Withering-Bloom True Flame wrapped around all the deep-blue flame and returned into his Purple Mansion.

Afterward, he looked toward that woman whose appearance felt somewhat familiar.

The woman no longer had the tender clarity of youth. Her expression carried several traces of hardship, and though she had originally only resembled her mother in appearance, now she resembled her in spirit even more.

They were both people of bitter fate.

Under Luo Chen's gaze, the woman became rather constrained.

She hesitated, then finally gave a graceful bow.

"Qingqing greets Sage."

"From now on, follow me."

Luo Chen said this gently, and the woman opposite him visibly relaxed.

...

"It is actually this generous?"

Luo Chen looked at the storage bag Zhou Yunshen had given Zhang Jingqing. The resources inside were absolutely not something an early-stage Initiate cultivator could match; they were comparable to the wealth of an ordinary Adept cultivator.

Black King lay beside Luo Chen and gave a disdainful sound.

"Qingqing was treated harshly in the Zhou clan. Especially after the demon-beast army surrounded the Coral Sea, she, a mere Initiate cultivator, was still sent onto the battlefield. These things are nothing more than compensation."

Luo Chen shook his head. "If she had truly been treated harshly, when more than ten thousand Zhou clan cultivators died in battle, there would be no reason for Qingqing to have survived."

Black King froze. That did indeed seem to be the case.

Luo Chen handed the checked storage bag to Black King, telling him to pass it to Zhang Jingqing when he had time, then asked about the details of that battle.

"Although the Zhou clan had no natural stronghold to rely on, they operated the Coral Sea until it was like a wall of bronze and iron. The Bloodsucking Coral [TERM?] filling the entire sea region caused endless suffering for the demon beasts."

"According to what Master would say, I imagine it was formed into an array. That was how they could hold on for so long."

"The reason I could charge in and break the demon beasts' formation was because the batch of dragon earthworms I brought out from Longyuan Island [TERM?] back then rendered great service."

"That old fellow Zhou Yunshen really is formidable. After he decided to abandon the Coral Sea, he stopped suppressing his realm and fully displayed his ability, suppressing two Sage Demon Emperors by himself. But I could sense that he truly is very old. Acting with such intensity was also a great burden to him."

After seriously understanding the situation of the Coral Sea battle, Luo Chen understood more about the Zhou clan ancestor, Zhou Yunshen.

This person's temperament was forbearing. Unless he had no other choice, he would never act lightly.

But once he made a decision, he was also extremely decisive.

And the other party's strength absolutely could not be underestimated.

But now, the other party had become a "homeless dog." Where should he go from here?

He had not fled to the Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance, the Northern Sea's greatest power at present, but had instead come to Divine Origin City. His intention hardly needed to be stated.

Did he also want to go to Central Continent?

Luo Chen's eyes narrowed slightly.

His divine sense stirred, and he sent out a voice transmission. Before long, Sang Jinghe walked in from outside.

The other man's eyes were red-rimmed, so he had likely already learned the situation of the Earth Mulberry Sect from Zhang Jingqing.

"Sage, what are your orders?"

"Go ask Fu Hongzhang when their family head will return to Divine Origin City."

Sang Jinghe froze. Asking when Fu Chaosheng would return carried an obvious meaning: Luo Chen wanted to know when they could set out for Central Continent.

But once they left the Northern Sea, then the grudge of the Earth Mulberry Sect, the grudge of the Zhang family of Cailian Island, and the grudges of the Sage's friends...

Luo Chen seemed to guess what Sang Jinghe was thinking, but he gave no explanation.

Only after the other party left in low spirits did Luo Chen softly sigh.

The Northern Sea today was full of vicious war and peril, and who knew how many people were searching for a road of retreat.

Revenge?

Putting aside whether he was currently Moon Wanderer's match, he did not even have the slightest clue where she was.

Some things were simply this helpless.

However!

Though this realm was vast, that was only in relation to low-level cultivators.

When his Dao attainment was complete one day, even if he had to search the heavens and scour the earth, he would ultimately find that woman and have words with her!

*(End of Chapter)*
