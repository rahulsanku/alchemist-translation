# Chapter 1174: The Great Mill Grinds the World; Going Too Far Is as Bad as Falling Short

"I have lost!"

Xiao Lin found it somewhat unbelievable.

From the very first exchange, he had actually already discovered that Luo Chen's raw strength was above his own, which was why he had proactively changed his fighting style.

Yet, before Luo Chen's sharp eyes, no matter how ingenious or adaptable he was, he had nowhere to hide.

In particular, the other party was extremely familiar with his martial arts system.

Forced into a corner, he once again chose to clash head-on.

To that end, he even executed *Heaven-Earth Reverse Cycle*, multiplying his own strength several times over.

In that state, even if a late-stage Leakless Realm martial grandmaster stood before him, he was confident he could contend for a while.

But in the end, he still failed.

Defeated by this rising star who had only entered the Nine Rings of Ruin for a little over a decade.

Defeated by the most fundamental disparity in cultivation!

Hard to accept, impossible to believe, deeply ashamed...

All kinds of emotions surged in his heart.

But in the end, the fighting spirit in his eyes, like a spark, reignited once more.

"In this battle, I, Xiao, concede defeat. The matter of Relic Immortal City is hereby settled. But in the future, I, Xiao, will still come to seek instruction!"

Xiao Lin cupped his hands and withdrew without hesitation.

Luo Chen watched the other man leave with a smile, a rare look of appreciation on his face.

Everyone said Xiao Lin was a true martial arts fanatic, and now it seemed the reputation was well deserved.

Not only did he innovate in martial arts creation, but he also did not lose heart in the face of defeat.

He did not pursue Xiao Lin. Or rather, from the moment that sword strike had stopped at the critical point, only breaking the other's technique, he had intended to let him go.

It was very clear!

In this battle, Xiao Lin was merely a pawn pushed forward by someone else.

The true mastermind behind the scenes...

Luo Chen's gaze fell upon the group of martial artists watching from afar.

"Everyone, would you also care to offer some guidance?"

---

In the distance.

Dugu Cheng and the others exchanged glances, never expecting things to develop this way.

The scenarios they had imagined were either Luo Chen losing to the martial arts fanatic Xiao Lin, or Luo Chen killing him ruthlessly, confirming his reputation for bloodlust.

But in the end, none of these conjectures came to pass!

Now, how were they supposed to handle themselves?

The martial artists of the Vulture Alliance all fixed their gazes on Dugu Cheng.

Meanwhile, the other Sixth Ring martial artists who had come to watch the excitement also observed them with great interest.

"Brother Dugu, how about we just let it go?" Cui Zhuo whispered.

But Dugu Cheng's expression hardened. He stepped forward and shouted at Luo Chen from afar:

"Luo Chen, you forcibly seized a fellow cultivator's cultivation stone and broke the rules. Do you understand that?"

Luo Chen's expression was calm, unperturbed. "So what if I did?"

Dugu Cheng sneered. "In that case, the rules naturally cannot protect you. I will not bully you with numbers. As long as you are willing to hand over a large quantity of miraculous pills and medicines to compensate Cui Zhuo, this matter will be settled."

Miraculous pills and medicines?

So that was what this was about!

Behind him, Cui Zhuo suddenly understood.

Dugu Cheng's forceful intervention had never been for his sake from the start. It was merely a pretext to make a move against Luo Chen.

Cui Zhuo felt stifled inside. He subconsciously glanced at Xiao Lin, who had not yet gone far, and felt a sense of shared misfortune.

Would Luo Chen really submit?

The answer was naturally no.

Luo Chen chuckled lightly. "What if I say no?"

Dugu Cheng's brow furrowed deeply. "There are seventeen of us from the Vulture Alliance present. No matter how strong you are, can you possibly defeat all of us?"

The smile on Luo Chen's face grew even more radiant. As he stretched his body, a thunderous crackling of bones and joints rang out.

"I would like to give it a try!"

As soon as these words were spoken, the situation suddenly froze.

Even those watching the excitement could not help but marvel that there was such an arrogant person in the world.

Having only recently entered the mid-stage of the Leakless Realm, he dared to face seventeen experts of the same rank alone.

What choice would the Vulture Alliance make now?

In this predicament where they were riding a tiger and could not dismount, Dugu Cheng's gaze swept over the members of the alliance.

Those who met his eyes were either burning with fervor or evasive, but in the end, they all became resolute.

The tales of the miraculous pills and medicines on Luo Chen's person had long since spread throughout the alliance. Now, with a numerical advantage and no risk involved, who would not want a share of the spoils?

Even Cui Zhuo, who had been hesitant, finally nodded.

Although he had been used as a pawn in this battle, it was ultimately fought in his name. How could he flee at the last moment?

"Let me, Kang, test your mettle first!"

A figure stepped out from the crowd, his body wreathed in thick astral qi, and charged at Luo Chen.

But Luo Chen waved his right hand, and five blood dragons roared forth, forcing him back on the spot.

Flicking his sleeve, Luo Chen contemptuously swept his gaze over the seventeen Leakless Realm martial artists.

"One person is not enough. Come at me all at once!"

He did not want to waste time, and his enemies were naturally happy to oblige.

The next moment, seventeen figures struck out simultaneously, attacking Luo Chen.

Amidst their attacks, they also moved swiftly, closing in on Luo Chen.

Facing the overwhelming barrage of attacks, Luo Chen showed no fear. He laughed heartily and let the many attacks land on his body.

A layer of blood-colored film emerged on his body—it was the Blood Cloth Armor!

The seventeen attacks landed on his body. The Blood Cloth Armor trembled violently, but with the support of his nearly boundless and mighty internal astral qi, it still deflected them all one by one.

Meanwhile, waves of vibration came from the surrounding void.

From several hundred zhang away, Xiao Lin carefully observed this battle.

"An upgraded version of the astral qi shield? The ultimate martial art of deflecting force and transferring power, where not even a feather can be added, not even a fly can land?"

"Quite ingenious!"

"But with so many enemies, how many rounds can this deflection and transfer of force last?"

"Or perhaps, he intends to borrow force to strike back, thereby turning one against many?"

Just as Xiao Lin was pondering how he would deal with such a situation, his gaze, which had been fixed on Luo Chen, suddenly changed.

Because, facing the onslaught of the crowd, Luo Chen formed a hand seal.

"That starting gesture... *Heaven-Earth Reverse Cycle*!"

Xiao Lin was astonished.

He recognized this technique. It originated from the incomplete forbidden art scroll he had left behind in the Relic Immortal City's scripture pavilion back then.

But unlike his own choice of amplifying his own physical constitution, Luo Chen's completion of the incomplete forbidden art had taken a completely different extreme!

The next moment!

*Boom! Boom! Boom!*

*Boom! Boom! Boom!*

Two enormous millstones emerged above Luo Chen's head.

The millstones were blood-red, rotating in opposite directions.

Torrents of scarlet astral qi poured down, like blood seeping from living beings being ground to death.

It bore an almost apocalyptic omen!

A terrifying suction force, centered on the World-Destroying Great Mill, frantically spread in all directions.

Even those standing extremely far away felt as if they were about to be pulled in and ground to death alive.

Xiao Lin retreated hastily in alarm.

It was not just him; the other onlookers also changed color and frantically retreated.

But the members of the Vulture Alliance, who were at the center of the battlefield, did not have this opportunity.

Or rather, from the moment they chose to surround Luo Chen, the outcome they had chosen was death!

Shouts of surprise and screams came one after another.

Streams of astral qi were thrown at Luo Chen, attempting to interrupt the operation of the two great millstones.

Xiao Lin's eyes were sharp. As someone who had also studied the incomplete forbidden art scroll of *Heaven-Earth Reverse Cycle*, he vaguely sensed the flaw.

Attacking the caster himself was ineffective!

All attacks would dissipate into nothingness as the World-Destroying Great Mill turned.

Only by attacking the manifested, solid millstones formed from astral qi was there a glimmer of hope.

"Can they perceive it?"

There are fools in the world, but there is no shortage of clever people.

Especially those who had cultivated to this level; they were all people of wisdom and perseverance.

As attacks on Luo Chen proved futile, and as some died miserably within the blood-colored great mill, Dugu Cheng had already realized the crux of the problem.

"Everyone, attack the millstone above our heads with me! Otherwise, we will all die!"

Amidst his roar, he had already taken the lead. His body surged with yin-cold astral qi, which he blasted towards the blood-colored great mill.

The others followed suit, locking their attacks onto the constantly rotating millstone.

Luo Chen watched coldly from the sidelines.

So what if they had seen through the flaw?

There had never been a perfect technique in this world, no divine ability without a flaw.

But under the crushing force of a true great momentum, an individual's counterattack was nothing but the light of a firefly.

This technique, in its incomplete scroll form, could at most double the caster's strength.

After Xiao Lin completed it, it could multiply one's own physical constitution several times over.

But in Luo Chen's hands, it possessed completely different power and variations.

Back when it was driven by the Nine Calamities True Astral Qi and the Unforgiving Heavenly Astral Qi, *Heaven-Earth Reverse Cycle* could already unleash power ten times greater than his normal physical attacks.

And now, with the Blood-Refining Divine Astral Qi, which fused the two astral qi types, its power was even greater!

It could even be said to have grown exponentially.

At this moment, the power of this technique was roughly a hundred times that of his normal martial attacks.

Let alone these seventeen people, even if there were several dozen more, they would be nothing but mantises trying to stop a chariot.

"Turn!"

With a sharp shout, his hands suddenly twisted.

In an instant, heaven and earth were overturned, and the universe trembled.

The terrifying grinding force turned all attacks into nothingness.

More than a dozen figures were continuously pulled into the great mill amidst their terror, then turned into blood mist.

Just as they were about to be annihilated one by one...

Suddenly!

"Daoist friend, that is quite enough."

A cyan finger pierced through the void, pointing at the center of the great mill.

The moment they clashed, a violent tremor erupted.

"Hmm?"

The person who wanted to stop this seemed not to have expected the technique to be so overbearing that even he was shaken.

However, as the cyan finger slowly descended upon the great mill, the reversing force that had almost ground the universe flat finally began to slow down.

Seeing this, Luo Chen immediately withdrew his hands.

The blood-colored great mill in the void also dissipated instantly.

He looked up into the heights.

A familiar figure in a blue robe was looking at him helplessly.

The eight surviving martial artists of the Vulture Alliance, upon seeing the old man, immediately began to "wail and plead for mercy."

"Senior, save us!"

"Senior, save me!"

The onlookers in the distance also bowed respectfully.

"Senior Gu!"

"Greetings, Senior Gu!"

Luo Chen was slightly displeased and asked directly, "Daoist Gu Xun, what is the meaning of this?"

The newcomer was none other than the Human Immortal, Gu Xun!

He looked rather helpless. "These martial artists are not easy to cultivate. Killing one or two is fine, but killing too many in a short time might invite questions from the Carefree Realm Lord."

Luo Chen sneered. "A group of Leakless Realm martial artists can catch the Realm Lord's eye?"

Gu Xun shrugged. "Are you not also in the Leakless Realm now?"

Luo Chen was taken aback.

His gaze swept over the crowd, especially the eight survivors, among whom Cui Zhuo was included.

These people did not dare to meet Luo Chen's eyes at all. As his gaze turned towards them, they all flinched away, filled with lingering fear and terror.

A rabble that had been scared out of their wits!

Since the battle had already been disrupted, Luo Chen could not be bothered to stir up his killing intent again.

He formed a hand seal, as if drawing water, and the blood mist floating in the void condensed onto his body.

The originally scarlet Blood Cloth Armor became even more eerie.

Such a nonchalant action drew sidelong glances once again.

Gu Xun frowned slightly. "Daoist Luo, that technique from before was too overbearing, and this current one is too sinister. Be careful not to go too far!"

Luo Chen retorted, "And if I don't go too far, how can I reach the void?"

Going too far is as bad as falling short; going too far is the way to reach it!

Gu Xun was momentarily speechless.

But he quickly realized that this might be precisely why the Carefree Realm Lord thought so highly of Luo Chen, and had even specifically told him to secretly look after Luo Chen.

Unexpectedly, Luo Chen's strength had now soared to the point of touching his own level, rendering his protection unnecessary.

In fact, he could vaguely sense a threat from Luo Chen's unmoving physical body.

"Forget it, forget it. Do as you please!"

As he was about to leave, Gu Xun's gaze swept over the others.

"Conduct yourselves well in the future. If Daoist Luo truly decides to pursue the matter, I will be powerless to help."

With those words, Gu Xun vanished into the boundless void.

The crowd had no time to see him off before they had to face Luo Chen's sweeping gaze once more.

Amidst the commotion, they all respectfully took their leave.

Even the eight who had narrowly escaped death hurriedly retreated in panic.

The Sixth Ring, which had been so boisterous moments before, became eerily quiet in the blink of an eye, as if nothing had ever happened.

But the fall of nine Leakless Realm martial artists still left a bloody scent in the air.

The Vulture Alliance was as good as dissolved.

The nine cultivation stones that had been vacated would likely be filled soon by long-standing martial artists from the Seventh Ring.

Luo Chen paid it no further mind.

This battle today was merely an opportunity for him to stretch his limbs amidst his austere cultivation.

After resting for three days, Luo Chen once again entered a state of cultivation.

---

The overall cultivation of the Leakless Realm in martial arts was monotonous and dull.

It mainly involved absorbing chaotic energy and converting it into one's own natal astral qi.

The internal manifestation was probably that the qi and blood furnace would become increasingly powerful.

Luo Chen's self-created *God-Devil Formlessness* added an extra step to this foundation.

That was to stockpile Blood-Refining Divine Astral Qi in the seven hundred and twenty acupoints, nurturing the martial gods.

In his conception, the martial gods nurtured in this way, aside from lacking a physical body, would be almost identical to his original self in every other respect.

This required an unimaginable amount of energy as supply.

But only martial gods nurtured in this way would possess earth-shattering power.

The earlier execution of *Heaven-Earth Reverse Cycle*, which had even caused the Human Immortal Gu Xun to be shaken, was already a clear indication of this.

However, this cultivation method was only suitable for Luo Chen. No one else could even think of it.

First, the sheer terrifying energy supply and conversion were not something ordinary martial artists could achieve. Only Luo Chen, who possessed the Primordial Unity Cauldron, could proceed step by step.

Second, regarding the acupoints themselves, ordinary martial artists could not possess the kind of acupoints that had been tempered to the extreme like his.

A physical body comparable to a true spirit was the very foundation for bearing all these martial arts concepts!

Finally, one also needed a vast soul foundation to be able to carry it all out simultaneously.

Unbeknownst to Luo Chen, another twenty years had slipped by since his battle that destroyed the Vulture Alliance back then.

He paid no attention whatsoever to the extent of the aftershocks from that battle.

What did interest him during his leisure time, however, was the frequent challenges from Xiao Lin.

With the absolute gap in strength, so-called challenges hardly amounted to any real fighting.

Instead, they involved more discussions and exchanges of martial arts concepts.

For instance, Luo Chen's improved Great Luo Five Dragon Hands was, in Xiao Lin's view, ingenious but scattered in power. When facing an existence that could truly threaten Luo Chen, this technique was merely flashy.

Luo Chen naturally disagreed, as he had created this technique precisely for the variation of astral qi.

However, the point Xiao Lin raised about the power being scattered did serve as a reminder.

*Heaven-Earth Reverse Cycle* was powerful, but it was too slow to activate, and the enemy could find flaws and counterattack in the middle of it.

Could he perhaps create another ultimate killing move that could unleash all his power in a single strike?

Similar to the sword dao divine ability of the *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon*—the Thousand Skies Converging Stream!

Perhaps that kind of killing move could be considered a true martial divine ability? (End of this chapter)
