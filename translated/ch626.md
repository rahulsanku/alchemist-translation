# Chapter 626: Heavenjade, I Want You to Aid My Cultivation!

Blood Wanderer!

Luo Chen was no stranger to this name.

Rather, it should be said that the entire Northern Sea cultivation world was familiar with this name.

There were many legends about him. Born a heavenly prodigy, he once fell into the dust, then finally rose powerfully again.

He had a grudge with the Primordial Demon Sect, had once been a robber cultivator, possessed an extreme and domineering personality, acted violently, and had even committed the crazed deed of living-refining a Sage force.

Among the many legends, truth and falsehood were difficult to distinguish.

But at least one thing could serve as evidence for his domineering behavior.

That was Longyuan Immortal City's current ruined state!

After the Primordial Demon Sect was destroyed, Blood Wanderer was the first to sound the prelude to hunting down Primordial Demon remnants. He once personally launched a fierce attack on Longyuan Immortal City, which at that time had still been a super immortal city.

One had to know that Longyuan Immortal City back then was the first immortal city before the Primordial Demon Sect's gate.

Its formations were complete and its defenses fully equipped.

There was even a Sage Perfected Being of the Primordial Demon Sect personally holding the city!

Under such circumstances, Blood Wanderer still attacked Longyuan without regard for anything, beating the originally prosperous and powerful Longyuan, which had even surpassed Divine Origin City, into pieces until it almost became ruins.

Even though more than a hundred years had passed, and despite the constant rebuilding by Myriad Immortals Assembly cultivators, the current Longyuan still gave people a shabby, broken-down feeling.

Though such a city did indeed fit the style of a place occupied by wandering cultivators.

Over these years, among the Myriad Immortals Assembly's Three Great Wanderers, only Moon Wanderer occasionally appeared to oversee the overall situation. The other two Great Wanderers showed only the head of the dragon but never the tail.

Mad Wanderer was still understandable. Rumors outside said he had gone mad, and his actions had no order. Whether the Myriad Immortals Assembly had him or not made little difference.

It was said the last time he appeared was on the battlefield where Moluo Stream attacked the Canghai Alliance's key island in its hinterland.

But for Blood Wanderer to be this mysterious was somewhat unreasonable.

Yet unexpectedly, he had suddenly returned.

It truly surprised Luo Chen somewhat.

"Has he done anything after returning?"

Heavenjade nodded without hesitation. "He really has. The moment Blood Wanderer returned, he issued a major task through the Demon Hunter Office."

"A major task?"

"Yes. It contains many smaller tasks. Overall, it is to collect related resources for him."

"What are they specifically? Tell me."

"Ranked first is the fifth-tier spirit plant Withered Blood Vine, preferably over a thousand years old! After that are the inner core of the fourth-tier demon beast Soul-Seizing Ghost Crow, Gold-Weaving Cloud Silkworm Thread, Ten-Thousand-Li Everbright Smoke..."

Perhaps guessing that Luo Chen would be interested in this matter, Heavenjade had memorized the task contents firmly in advance, with almost no errors or omissions.

From fifth-tier Withered Blood Vine to second-tier Manjusaka, she listed them one by one.

Luo Chen listened seriously, and his mind began guessing what these things could do when combined.

In truth, he did not think for long before his experience gave him the first intuition.

"Is he going to forge an artifact?"

Heavenjade looked at Luo Chen in surprise, then said, "Just as Master predicted, many people outside also think Blood Wanderer intends to refine some secret treasure of great power. Not long ago, the Moya Cave Master personally went to the Demon Hunter Office and offered up a large batch of materials, receiving Blood Wanderer's praise."

Luo Chen frowned. "Only praise?"

Heavenjade shook her head. "Not quite. This issued task can accumulate merits and raise one's demon hunter star rank. By using this treasure offering, the Moya Cave Master advanced from five-star demon hunter to six-star without expending the slightest effort."

Five-star to six-star?

Luo Chen curled his lip, unimpressed.

For someone as wealthy as the Moya Cave Master, a mere one or two star ranks did not count for much at all.

Only seven stars and above had some meaning to them.

However, the Moya Cave Master's enthusiasm probably also carried the purpose of pleasing Blood Wanderer.

As Luo Chen thought, Heavenjade abruptly told him something.

"This task, apart from demon hunter merit points, also has one additional reward from Blood Wanderer: a set of Sage formation insights. As long as someone brings him Withered Blood Vine, they can obtain this reward."

This time, Luo Chen was startled.

"Sage formation insights! Wouldn't the Myriad Immortals Assembly's hundred-plus Golden Core wandering cultivators go mad for this?"

"Just as Master sees, the Golden Core cultivators inside the Myriad Immortals Assembly have indeed begun going mad."

The Myriad Immortals Assembly's Golden Core wandering cultivators numbered over a hundred.

Even if one excluded those false-core cultivators who could no longer advance, there were still several dozen proper ones.

Among them, if one carefully counted the formidable figures with hope of advancing to Sage, they numbered both hands.

The first to mention would naturally be those three great eight-star demon hunters!

"Angler and the others?"

"Angler set out long ago. As for the other two, there has been no movement so far."

Luo Chen was somewhat stunned, but soon reacted.

Among the three great eight-star demon hunters, Purple Dowager came from the former Great Zhou dynasty. Presumably, she did not lack Sage formation insights.

As for Ghost Spirit Child, though he looked young, in truth he was the oldest of the three, with only a few years left before his great limit.

Over these years, Ghost Spirit Child had paid a great price to rent the Hundred Ghosts Cavern, spreading branches and leaves in order to continue his bloodline and Dao inheritance.

Therefore, under this seemingly frenzied tide, among the three, Angler was instead the most proactive.

Only, fifth-tier Withered Blood Vine was probably not so easy to find.

Even if it were found, it would be difficult to harvest.

For a spirit plant like this, after accumulating years, it also had a chance to awaken intelligence and become one of those so-called "spirits."

Perhaps a fifth-tier spirit plant could not compare to a corresponding Ascendant powerhouse, but it would still possess a certain number of Sage methods.

Angler was only Golden Core Stage Nine. How would he bring Blood Wanderer a fifth-tier Withered Blood Vine?

"Master, will you accept this task? Your current star rank is very low. Perhaps you could soar several demon hunter star ranks at once," Heavenjade asked.

Luo Chen shook his head. He had no such plan for now.

Leaving aside whether it was dangerous, the matter of the Purple Monkey Flower alone was enough for him to set everything else aside and handle with full effort.

Next, he asked whether Blood Wanderer had made any other moves. After learning that the other party remained reclusive and rarely emerged, Luo Chen placed his focus on Black King.

"It has been nearly a year now. Has Black King still not sent back news?"

Heavenjade was also very helpless. She had received no news at all.

She even revealed a poor expression. "Master, do you think Black King might already have..."

"That is not the case. The mind-link between him and me still exists." Luo Chen waved his hand and dispelled her worry.

After pondering for a long while, he suppressed the urgency in his heart. "Perhaps he only encountered some troublesome matter on the way back."

He could only comfort himself like this.

On the map in the miscellaneous book, the recorded distance was indeed somewhat far.

But with Black King's escape speed, moving unhindered through the sea, one year should have been enough for a round trip no matter what.

For him not to have returned after so long could only be explained by some matter delaying him.

Next, Luo Chen asked Heavenjade about the internal situation in Peng Lake.

This time, he did receive some good news.

The Zhang family, this vassal, had managed Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake prosperously over these years. Black Golden Dragon Carp, coral fruit, and some aquatic shellfish had already begun maturing and being sold.

Because they now had the relationships with Wuzhong Valley and the Zhou clan, the sales route problem they had worried about most was also resolved.

Last year, Heavenjade went over and settled accounts with them once, receiving seventy thousand spirit stones into the accounts.

In addition, from Azure Yang Treasure Pavilion, Luo Chen also received two hundred thousand spirit stones.

It did not seem like much, but this was only one year's income.

What Luo Chen had paid was merely his reputation and the territory of Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake. In all other areas, he had made no investment.

"Nine hundred thousand a year. It is neither too much nor too little, but it can also greatly relieve the pressure of my rent for Peng Lake."

Luo Chen smiled faintly, his mood rather comfortable.

It had to be said, holding a piece of territory in hand was equivalent to possessing a natural source of wealth.

Back then, the Luo Tian Assembly had only had a small Danxia Peak, and after exhausting itself for a year, its income had been only thirty or forty thousand spirit stones.

The contrast between the two was truly too great.

And one could imagine that as the Zhang family continued diligently working it, the income Peng Lake could produce would become more and more abundant.

Because the Zhang family itself was also developing vigorously, and outside it had technical support from the Sage force, the Coral Sea Zhou clan. If it still could not rise like this, then it would truly be beyond help.

On Luo Chen's side, with Xuanchen Armor successfully refined, his subsequent investment in artifact-forging would also become smaller and smaller.

At most, he would complete one or two law device or magic treasure forging tasks each year, giving Wuzhong Valley and the others an explanation.

Overall, it was opening the source and reducing expenditure!

Luo Chen's spirit stone pouch, which had been shriveled for a long time, would slowly swell again if nothing unexpected happened.

"When a house leaks, rain falls all night. Good things always come in pairs!"

"Next, I only need to wait for news from Black King's side."

Luo Chen smiled with pursed lips. Taking advantage of his good mood, he asked about Heavenjade's cultivation situation.

The latter was naturally overjoyed and hurriedly brought up the many problems she had encountered in cultivation one by one, seeking Luo Chen's guidance.

...

While waiting for Black King to return, Luo Chen was not doing nothing.

Previously, to stabilize the formation on Xuanchen Armor, his cultivation had almost stagnated for a year.

Now that there were no miscellaneous matters beside him, he immediately began cultivating madly with absolute focus.

Immortal cultivation emphasized slow grinding effort. Luo Chen could not rush it even if he wanted to.

But on the side of body cultivation, Luo Chen had something to say!

He once again fused something new into Myriad Path Convergence, namely the fusion of artifact-forging concepts.

He treated the body as a magic treasure to be forged.

Previously, because he had to forge artifacts, he used only internal refinement methods when body-tempering. But in truth, the perfected Myriad Path Convergence also had external refinement arts.

On a small island sealed by formations.

The sound of metal clashing constantly rang out.

Looking carefully, one saw a black hammer using the bare-bodied Luo Chen as its center, constantly flying up and down.

Every time it flew, it smashed down on Luo Chen with a crash.

Every time it struck, Luo Chen's body gave off a surging vibration.

From time to time, azure flames would even leap from the surface of his body.

Using the Flowing Fire Hammer Method to operate the Hundred-Tempering Art, he had to temper Luo Chen's physique from top to bottom to the extreme, forming that so-called meaning of being forged by a thousand hammer blows and a hundred refinements.

If it were an ordinary person, under such smashing blows, their five viscera would have shifted and their six bowels would have been injured long ago.

Yet Luo Chen clenched his teeth and forcibly endured it.

In fact, because he was controlling the Flowing Fire Hammer to attack himself, he still had to overcome the body's instinctive thought of "stopping self-harm" at every moment.

Fortunately, his will was tough, and he was not greatly affected.

"Strengthen it with qi and blood, burn it with inner fire, and temper it further with external force!"

"This is still far from enough!"

After half an hour of tempering ended.

Heavenjade manifested her true body and looked at Luo Chen with some nervousness.

"Master, do you really want to do this?"

"I told you to do it, so do it. What are you hesitating for?"

Heavenjade hesitated no longer. Her two wings beat, releasing the instinctive attack method she had used most proficiently in her early years.

A cyclone containing countless wind blades formed in the blink of an eye.

Afterward, it slowly swallowed Luo Chen within it.

Low, muffled roars sounded from within from time to time.

Heavenjade's expression was incomparably tense. She had already driven this demon art to its limit. If it were any Golden Core cultivator, even a late-stage great cultivator, without any defenses, they would suffer heavy injury.

Yet Master insisted on hardily enduring the wind blade cutting and cyclone tearing inside her cyclone.

If the other party did not call for her to stop, she did not dare withdraw her hand.

From this, one could see how terrifying Master's physique was. It had long since far surpassed human cultivators. In her view, even some third-tier Demon Kings famed for tyrannical physiques could not compare to Master.

Perhaps only the most orthodox archaic beasts of the same tier could match him slightly.

The time it took for one stick of incense to burn passed so slowly.

Only after a tired "stop" came from inside did Heavenjade stop this demon art as if released from a heavy burden.

At this moment, one man and one woman both had pale faces.

The former had been "beaten," while the latter had consumed too much mana.

A jade bottle flew before Heavenjade.

"Inside are one hundred Starry Pills. They can both replenish mana and aid cultivation. Take them!"

Heavenjade accepted them with incomparable joy.

She knew how powerful human pills were. Cultivation speed with pills assisting and without pills assisting were completely different matters.

This batch of Starry Pills from Master should have been refined in the early years before leaving the Swallow Islands. Later, he had not consumed them much or sold them, so now she benefited.

However...

Looking at Luo Chen, covered in wounds and bloodstains, Heavenjade still could not help asking, "Master, are you all right?"

Luo Chen grinned. "How could I be all right?"

Heavenjade's heart tightened. Could it be that she had used too much force before?

"But it is a good thing. Do not worry."

After saying this, Luo Chen took out a jar of fourth-tier Black Emperor Ointment from his storage ring and evenly smeared it over his body.

The intense pain he had once felt had, after enduring it again and again, turned into a tingling itch.

Feeling the medicinal power seep strand by strand into his physique, Luo Chen's mouth split open involuntarily.

"As expected, after extreme tempering, taking Black Emperor Ointment instead makes the medicinal power absorb faster!"

"This ointment is what the Falling Cloud Sect used to strengthen puppet bodies. Puppets are dead objects, and their absorption efficiency is far too slow and low. How could it compare to my active absorption?"

Sensing this change, Luo Chen grew more and more confident in pushing his body cultivation progress to Archaic Tier Four.

"However, this self-harm-style tempering should not be done too often. I still have to handle the frequency in between properly."

Luo Chen put on Xuanchen Armor and walked toward the outside of the island.

He instructed Heavenjade behind him, "In the future, once every ten days. Arrange your time properly. Also, how has the Nine Winds Plantain Fan I purchased for you back then been nourished?"

Heavenjade said in astonishment, "Could it be that the power of my Wind Cyclone Heavenly Blade Art is still not enough?"

"It is enough now, but in the future, that may not be certain. At that time, perhaps I will need you to use the Nine Winds Plantain Fan to aid my cultivation."

Heavenjade swallowed and looked at Luo Chen with even greater reverence.

The demon art was not enough, and it had actually already reached the point where a magic treasure had to be used?

One had to know that the Nine Winds Plantain Fan was an upper-grade magic treasure. She had nourished it for twenty years, and even now could only grasp sixty percent of its power.

If she accidentally injured Master at that time, what should she do!

"No. Recently, I must spend more mana nourishing the treasure fan and grasp it more as I wish."

...

During the days waiting for Black King's return.

Luo Chen's life once again became regular.

Qi cultivation, body cultivation, studying alchemy books and classics, occasionally personally refining a furnace of pills.

As for the disturbances outside, he ignored them all.

He seemed very much like a carefree wandering cultivator roaming with idle clouds and wild cranes.

Occasionally, he would also make a trip to Longyuan Immortal City, strolling through the major shops and hunting for treasures.

He also often went to the Demon Hunter Office, using his star-ranked demon hunter authority to actively inquire about intelligence on the current Northern Sea cultivation world.

He was even not limited only to the Northern Sea. The Eastern Wilderness was also one of his main focuses.

Not to mention, intermittently, he truly did learn a little news of the Eastern Wilderness.

News that could spread from the Eastern Wilderness to the Northern Sea certainly was not a small matter. It was absolutely a heaven-piercing great event capable of startling the Mountains and Seas Realm, just as the destruction of the Primordial Demon Sect had once been transmitted to the Eastern Wilderness.

What Luo Chen learned was that after the failure of the opening war in the Howling Moon Mountain Range back then, the demon beasts of the Million Mountains stirred, and a violent beast tide rushed out from the depths of the great mountains, attacking the human hinterland of the Eastern Wilderness.

This beast tide was extremely fierce!

Of the dozen-plus cultivation domains bordering the Million Mountains, more than half fell.

Only when the Eastern Wilderness Holy Land, the Nether Origin Sect, personally acted and called upon all cultivators of the Eastern Wilderness's thirty-six domains to act together did they forcibly drive that beast tide back out.

But this was only the first Eastern Wilderness beast tide.

Ten years ago, the second beast tide erupted again!

This time, the demon beasts rushing out from the Eastern Wilderness's Million Mountains no longer attacked in a covering sweep like before. Instead, they aimed at five cultivation domains and attacked them forcefully.

Even though the Nether Origin Sect again organized manpower, they only repelled two areas.

In three other places, demon beasts forcefully occupied the land and began a tug-of-war with the Eastern Wilderness human cultivators.

The related intelligence stopped there.

As for which cultivation domains were involved, and what their exact names were, Luo Chen did not learn.

The Howling Moon Mountain Range and Jade Cauldron Domain he most wanted to know about were utterly insignificant in this vast Mountains and Seas Realm, not even worth a stroke of ink.

Even so, Luo Chen felt a sense of urgency in his heart.

In the Northern Sea, he was merely an outsider.

The Eastern Wilderness was his "home."

There were his Dao companion, his friends, and also his disciples and subordinates.

When one lived long, one had attachments.

Luo Chen's cultivation years were short. This was his advantage, and also his disadvantage.

The disadvantage was that time had not been able to sever those attachments!

And he himself was also unwilling to let go.

"Under such chaos, the Eastern Wilderness should no longer have anyone paying attention to the matter of Cangwu Mountain back then. Perhaps I can return safe and sound."

Inside Star Dou Hall, after finishing meditation, Luo Chen developed a lucky thought.

But this thought came quickly and left quickly.

A mere Golden Core cultivator might dominate a single place, but within a continent where Sage cultivators emerged in numbers, he did not count for much.

If someone truly investigated seriously, Luo Chen would be unable to bear the consequences.

Only by advancing to the Sage stage could he occupy a place in this world.

As for higher Ascendant cultivators?

On this point, Luo Chen was not so worried. Ascendant cultivators generally did not personally act.

Among the two times he knew of Ascendant cultivators acting, one was the Nether Origin Sect powerhouse attacking Cangwu Mountain, and the other was the Northern Sea demon race attacking the Primordial Demon Sect.

These were all disputes between Holy Lands.

Luo Chen ended cultivation and walked out of Star Dou Hall.

Today, he needed to refine a batch of True Flame Pills. His stock was almost exhausted and needed replenishing.

Just then, Heavenjade hurriedly walked in from outside.

Seeing her so hurried, Luo Chen was somewhat surprised, then his face turned solemn.

"Has news come back from Black King?"

"No. A Golden Core cultivator has specially come from Moya Mountain to visit Master. He calls himself your old friend."

"Old friend? Moya Mountain... the Moya Cave Master?"

"Not him either. He calls himself Ding Yi."

Luo Chen raised a brow.

In his mind appeared the image of a man with a large belly but thin cheeks.

Connecting it with Moya Mountain.

In an instant, Luo Chen recalled the words the Moya Cave Master had spoken back then.

So it was him?

"Invite him in. I would like to see what important matter he has, after dragging things out for so long before coming to see me."

*(End of Chapter)*
