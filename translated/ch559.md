# Chapter 559: Loose Ends, An Ambush Set to Kill

Beneath the shadowed sea surface, a dim light moved — appearing slow but actually fast, darting in flight.

The surging undercurrents and ocean streams seemed to have no effect on their owner, and in fact helped accelerate the pace.

Every so often, the light paused and tilted upward toward the sky.

Above, the sky appeared empty — but under that gaze, the shapes of one person and one bird were perfectly visible.

Even with the Fighting Gull's tremendous speed, this pale light could stop and start and still keep pace.

To any discerning human cultivator who happened to witness this, the technique in use would have been immediately recognizable: the Five-Phase Escape Arts — specifically, the Water Concealment Art.

The Water Concealment Art, in terms of difficulty, ranked second among the Five-Phase Escape Arts, just below the Earth Concealment Art.

With the right manual, even an Initiate cultivator could learn it.

Some particularly gifted Qi Refinement cultivators had even managed to grasp it.

But like the Earth Concealment Art, it was easy to learn and extremely difficult to master.

In terms of escape speed, it lagged behind the Metal Concealment Art. In burst capability, it fell short of the Fire Concealment Art. In stealth, it couldn't match the Wood Concealment Art.

But clearly, the one currently deploying it had cultivated it to a state of effortless mastery — true Perfection.

And practiced over the endless open sea, it was like a fish in water. The speed was remarkable.

Even Tian Xuan, a species celebrated for aerial velocity, could not shake this pursuer.

Watching the Fighting Gull's form from below, the light's owner turned things over in its mind.

*If I haven't found the wrong target, this person must be the mastermind behind the Heavenly Pivot Island incident.*

*Not only running wild in the Xuanyan Sea Region, but bold enough to participate in the Xuanyan Island battle itself.*

*If I kill them and take their possessions — I might be able to use those resources to break through to the transformation stage and cultivate the Great Dao in human form.*

*Heh. The Demon Spider Crabs want to capture this person and reclaim the Demon Emperor's relic too. Without asking this Yuan Yin's opinion first.*

Time wore on. After a full day and night, the Fighting Gull seemed to tire; its pace slowed.

Beneath the sea, Yuan Yin held its breath and reduced speed as well, maintaining a distance of ten li between itself and the gull.

Ten li was the minimum safe tracking distance.

Any closer, and even the Hidden Dust Sand clan's natural concealment gifts might slip — especially before a Golden Core cultivator, a race renowned for its powerful divine soul.

Watching the slowly-beating wings of the Fighting Gull, and the tiny silhouette standing motionless on its back, Yuan Yin shivered with quiet excitement.

*Once.*

*Only one chance.*

*This person is only mid-stage Adept — yet they roamed the Xuanyan Sea Region freely and enslaved two third-tier demon kings. Their methods must be formidable.*

*And my clan, the Hidden Dust Sand, has nothing but our concealment gift. Even at Perfected Completion of the third tier, the only techniques I've mastered are the Water Concealment Art and the Ten-Thousand Pearl Blossom. Once those are broken, I am no match.*

*Therefore — one chance. Strike in the moment of inattention. Kill in one blow.*

Repeated deliberation made Yuan Yin — a rare Perfected Completion specimen among the Hidden Dust Sand — keenly aware of the enormous risk in what it was about to do.

It had already mentally prepared for the possibility of a single missed strike followed by fleeing into the endless ocean.

It watched the Adept cultivator's fluctuating aura. Even at a distance, the signs of unhealed wounds were obvious.

Out here on the Demon Sea, far from safe land, traveling in this condition was inadvisable.

So — just wait for the person to stop and recover from their wounds. Slip inside quietly. Kill in a single strike.

The Xuanyan Demon Emperor's relic. A human Adept cultivator it could parasitize.

Both possibilities set Yuan Yin's heart blazing.

One day. Two days...

Yuan Yin's mood sank steadily.

The person showed no sign of stopping to heal. By all appearances, they were simply flying straight back to human cultivation territory.

If that happened, there would be no opportunity to strike.

*Am I truly going to watch this opportunity walk away?*

Yuan Yin grew almost frustrated enough to wish a powerful demon king would materialize on the path ahead and intercept the target.

But not too powerful — killing the person outright would ruin everything.

The contradictory wishes kept rising and falling in its imagination.

Then —

On the fourth day, in a stretch of sea known as the Sheluo Sea Region, the Fighting Gull began slowly descending from the sky.

Seeing this, Yuan Yin's pulse surged.

*They've finally stopped.*

Whether from four days of continuous travel exhausting the Fighting Gull, or because the Adept's wounds could no longer be suppressed — either way, they had come down on a small island that faintly radiated sparse spiritual qi.

*My chance is here.*

Yuan Yin's excitement crested. It moved carefully toward the island.

Rather than landing directly, it gradually spread its faint glow and reached out to the Hidden Dust Sand in the surrounding waters.

From the deep sea, other pale lights pulsed in response.

Lower-tier Hidden Dust Sand, responding to its call, slowly rose to the shallows and drifted toward the island to observe the Adept cultivator's movements.

By the time the moon reached its midpoint in the sky, Yuan Yin had made up its mind.

*This moment. Don't miss it.*

The pale glow rose from the sea surface, drifting like a firefly toward the nameless island.

Just as it drew near the island's edge, its pace froze.

*Something's wrong.*

*Something feels wrong. But I can't say what.*

Yuan Yin couldn't identify it — just an instinct. It lingered outside the island, uncertain.

After a long hesitation, it decided to hold back.

*I've already marked this person's aura. I can keep tracking, find out where they make their real base, and make my move then.*

It trusted this feeling deeply.

That feeling had guided it through countless disasters over centuries of cultivation.

From the lowest, weakest Hidden Dust Sand — prey for any human cultivator or demon beast — it had climbed, step by painstaking step, to Perfected Completion of the third tier.

Dying here for this scheme would not be worth it.

Its form began to quietly withdraw.

Then —

A clear, unhurried voice carried from the island.

"A friend comes from afar — what joy. Fellow daoist, since you've come this far, why not show yourself?"

By moonlight, a figure appeared — broad white daoist robes, long hair hanging loose except for a single wooden hairpin. He seemed to walk on moonlight, eyes cool and sharp as a bottomless winter pool, watching from several li away with a steady cold gaze.

Yuan Yin's heart hammered. A sense of extreme danger rushed over it.

No time for conversation — it dropped straight into the sea.

The moment it touched water, it tried to activate the Water Concealment Art.

But in the next instant, a stream of flowing light poured out across the sea surface like a waterfall, spreading in all directions.

In the north, south, east, and west, four pillars of light shot skyward.

Through the glow, shapes could be made out: a jiaolong at one point, a gull at another, a shadow at the third, each standing at a cardinal direction, each bathed in a pillar of light. The fourth pillar was in the hands of the figure on the island.

With four-point cooperation, the stretch of sea instantly hardened — impenetrable as solid ground.

No matter how Yuan Yin poured in its power, it could not break into the escape speed of the Water Concealment Art.

In other words — the Water Concealment Art had been sealed.

*What kind of formation is this? Why does it feel like the entire sea has been locked?*

Yuan Yin's soul nearly fled its body. Its greatest trump card, the Water Concealment Art it had relied on for centuries in the Xuanyan Sea Region — useless. The despair it felt was deeper than if the sky had caved in.

But at the edge of life and death, even despair couldn't stop it from fighting.

Without thinking, it shot upward.

The sea was closed. Then it would take to the sky.

But the figure on the island gave the compass plate in his hand a slight adjustment, and four more pillars of light flared outward — blocking every angle of escape from above.

Yuan Yin's movement locked. A garbled semblance of human speech emerged.

"Fellow daoist — let mercy guide you. I've done you no harm. Consider this a chance encounter, and let me go?"

Luo Chen's lip curled slightly. Contempt colored his expression.

"You've been tailing me for days, and then lurking around the place I stopped. What — did you need to actually *do* something to me before I could have a word with you?"

Yuan Yin's heart sank.

It still didn't know when it had been exposed — but today would not end well.

*Then —*

Its resolve hardened. A surging aura erupted outward.

It resembled demon qi, and blood-and-flesh qi, but somewhere underneath ran a quiet, clear otherworldly purity.

Feeling that aura, Luo Chen's expression shifted slightly.

This presence was nearly on par with the five royal demon crab patriarchs.

A Perfected Completion third-tier demon beast?

"I am Yuan Yin of the Aurora Clan. Do you truly wish for a battle to the death?"

*The Aurora Clan.*

At those words, Luo Chen's entire attention tightened.

That was one of the most powerful demon beast lineages in the endless Northern Sea.

Born from the sea, dwelling in the misty reaches of sky, feeding on the colored lights of the aurora.

Rumor held that the Arctic Yemo Heaven's elemental magnetic aurora grounds gathered vast numbers of aurora beasts.

Ordinary cultivators didn't dare provoke them.

Looking at Yuan Yin's body — that round, constantly glowing form — it did resemble the descriptions of the aurora beast.

This was a species that could stand against human cultivators at the same realm.

In a true fight to the death — the odds of winning were unclear. But if things escalated and the powerful demons of the Sheluo Sea Region were drawn in, that would be an even worse situation.

Luo Chen's expression shifted back and forth. He pressed the compass plate.

Immediately, the pillars of light in the sky began to fade.

Yuan Yin's heart eased — but it kept its voice forceful.

"Clever kid. I have other business. I won't hold this against you."

With those words, it shot directly skyward.

But before it cleared the formation —

An enormous blazing sun detonated directly above it.

*When had that been placed—*

Yuan Yin's mind went cold with shock. No choice but to drop back down.

And from the sea below, several water-dragons shot straight upward.

Trapped between above and below — nowhere to go.

Yuan Yin let out a furious shout. "*Scatter!*"

In an instant, thousands upon thousands of points of light exploded outward from its body.

The blazing sun's detonation was met and shattered. The water-dragons in the sea broke apart.

And those dense points of light turned on the four formation-anchors simultaneously.

The jiaolong opened its vast maw and swallowed every incoming light-point in its range whole, giving a grunt with each one it consumed.

The Fighting Gull beat its wings furiously, whipping up spiraling windblasts to deflect the rain of light-points. Even so, a number struck its body, punching bloody holes through the feathers.

On the island, Luo Chen's expression was grave.

One hand holding the compass plate.

The other hand traced a circle, then slowly pushed forward.

In the night sky, ring after ring of azure-red circles appeared.

Each ring independent, yet linked end to end. Five in total.

Azure flame coiled across them, a sight that made the heart go cold.

The Sevenfold Blazing Heaven Rings — Luo Chen's self-created fire-element defensive technique.

Cultivated to Grandmaster level. And yet, until this moment, never before deployed in actual combat.

The rings met the densest cluster of light-points head-on.

*Hiss, hiss, hiss...*

The first ring shattered in an instant.

The light-points drove through with unstoppable force, and the second ring's fire-shield collapsed as well.

But at the third ring, surging mana pulsed — and besides the fire-shield, a flight of small red fire-moths came pouring out.

The moths flew straight into the mass of light-points.

Both sides made contact: the moths winked out, only to immediately re-form and fly again.

Like moths to a flame — wave after wave, tireless.

The relentless blocking gradually wore down the light-points' momentum by more than half.

The third ring was ultimately broken — but when the storm reached the fourth ring, it had largely spent itself.

Azure flame blazed outward. The Withering-Bloom quality kindled.

Under that azure fire, the light-points lost their brilliance of their own accord, revealing themselves as dull, gray stone pearls.

Luo Chen's vision was exceptionally sharp. He saw their nature in one glance.

"Pearls?"

Even as surprise crossed his face, Yuan Yin had recovered its breath and saw what seemed like an opening to escape.

No more trying sea or sky — it charged straight toward the formation-anchor with the weakest aura.

Clearly, that was just a third-tier Ghost King soul.

And the Hidden Dust Sand had no fear of ghost-kind whatsoever.

But as it closed in, the Ghost King curled back its lips in a taunting smile.

"Not an aurora beast at all — just a Hidden Dust Sand pearl."

With those unhurried words, the wind died.

In the night sky, within the Sea-Sealing Eight Trigrams Formation, a pearl the size of a fist had shed its light — hanging motionless in the air.

*Drip.*

A water-drop fell.

Luo Chen walked across the sea surface and came to stand before the great pearl.

He looked at it with something between puzzlement and fascination.

"So this is a Hidden Dust Sand?"

The Ghost King spoke in Han Zhan's voice: "An aurora beast curls into a ball but spreads open like a butterfly — it's nothing like this round, plump pearl. Hidden Dust Sand is also known as the pearl demon or sea demon seed, which perfectly matches this form."

Luo Chen nodded — and then frowned with a new question.

"Tradition says Hidden Dust Sand have negligible combat power — essentially none. But what this creature just unleashed wasn't inferior to a third-tier late-stage demon king."

Han Zhan chuckled. "True enough — but exceptional individuals always exist. And besides: this creature showed third-tier Perfected Completion, and the Ten-Thousand Pearl Blossom it deployed was below half the strength of any of the five crab patriarchs. Doesn't that actually fit the descriptions we've heard?"

Luo Chen thought about it and nodded.

Even extraordinarily gifted, the Hidden Dust Sand's last-ditch counterattack had gone as far as it could.

It hadn't even broken through the fifth ring of the Grandmaster-level Sevenfold Blazing Heaven Rings.

He had been startled earlier — unnecessarily so.

"When you put it that way, it makes sense."

"No wonder I kept feeling someone watching me these past few days, yet couldn't locate the source no matter how I looked."

"Hidden Dust Sand, from the moment they gain intelligence, are naturally inclined to conceal their own aura. They slowly absorb power from their host as parasites, growing steadily until they emerge into cultivation on their own."

"This one reached Perfected Completion of the third tier. Its concealment ability must surpass even my Concealment Veil Formation at full activation."

From the day Luo Chen had refined his spirit eyes, his vision had been exceptional — and he had become acutely sensitive to being watched.

Two days ago, he had sensed something wrong but couldn't find the source — so he had asked Han Zhan to investigate for him.

A small thing like that was no trouble to refuse.

With his Sage-level divine soul as his foundation, Han Zhan's deliberate search found the enemy quickly.

Then tonight they had descended on this island, deploying the Sealing Divinity Compass to arrange the Sea-Sealing Eight Trigrams Formation.

Luo Chen couldn't set up a formation using his own eight limbs as anchor points the way the Demon Spider Crabs had — but he could use Black King, Tian Xuan, and Han Zhan as three of the four points with himself as the fourth.

Especially with Han Zhan as a formation master assisting, the Sea-Sealing Eight Trigrams Formation's power was no inferior to that Demon Spider Crab's arrangement.

And so, the Hidden Dust Sand that had walked straight into the ambush without knowing it had no choice but to be taken.

Luo Chen shook his head. "I can't believe there's still nuisance like this, when I was almost done here."

Han Zhan laughed. "What did you expect? Why do you think human cultivators prefer to found sects and clans, gathering together in sacred lands? Out in the wild, spirit lands abound — but most great demons are instinctively drawn to them. The conflicts never stop."

Luo Chen nodded in quiet acknowledgment.

Wild cultivation had a certain romantic appeal to it. But without absolute strength, "peace and quiet" was simply never part of the deal.

It was a direct contradiction of the surface ideal of "serene cultivation" that cultivators claimed to pursue.

Just then, the faint sound of conch shells reached them from the surrounding waters.

This place could no longer be stayed in.

Luo Chen drew a slow breath, recalled the wounded Black King and Tian Xuan, called up his mana cloud, and flew into the night sky.

*(End of Chapter)*
