# Chapter 703: Master of the Sky-Splitting Stream, Descendant of Lord Sang

Counting carefully, the righteous-demonic war in the Northern Sea had already lasted nearly a full century, from small-scale friction to all-out war.

Especially after total war began, the two sides had tugged back and forth for fifty years.

At first, the two great immortal and demonic forces had suddenly risen in attack against the Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance [TERM?], occupying an absolute advantage. Later, after seizing half of the Northern Sea human cultivation world's territory, the righteous and demonic sides lowered their banners and ceased hostilities, each standing equal to the other.

At that time, it could barely be counted as a rare period of calm in the Northern Sea cultivation world after the Primordial Demon Sect's destruction.

The immortal-demonic allied army occupied half of the Northern Sea human domain. The Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance [TERM?] occupied two-thirds of the remaining half. Although its territory was inferior to that of the immortal and demonic side, it was the inner-sea essence land that Northern Sea cultivators had managed for several thousand years.

The remaining scattered corners were divided up by the Myriad Immortals Assembly, the Tianyuan Merchant Alliance, and some remote minor forces.

At that time, order could be considered stable. Even if there were minor frictions, they remained confined to the righteous and demonic sides.

Immortal-path exchanges and commercial dealings had once recovered.

Even in Luo Chen's early years traveling to the far north and southeast, as long as he did not deliberately stir up trouble, no one would target him.

Unfortunately.

This calm had been completely broken several months ago.

As Fallen Sea's strange phenomenon of the golden sky and black sea disappeared, Penglai Immortal Sect [TERM?] inexplicably began reducing its forces and recalling large numbers of sect members and disciples stationed outside.

With that, the balance between the righteous and demonic sides was broken.

The Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance [TERM?] cast aside its dispirited state of being suppressed for fifty years and took the initiative to attack, recovering on a large scale the immortal islands and sea regions Moluo Stream had occupied in earlier years.

The blazing intensity of the war caused those minor forces who were still muddled and had not yet sensed the shifting winds to be dragged in for no reason.

The Sui clan fleet forcing a turn beside the burning city gate could already be considered an extremely wise choice by the cultivator leading them.

But Luo Chen going straight into the center of the war flames seemed rather reckless.

A late-stage Adept Great Cultivator could look down on a region in ordinary times, and with a little restraint was still enough to range across the Northern Sea.

But before a battlefield of tens of thousands of cultivators at every turn, he truly could not count for much.

Driving the wooden kite, Luo Chen stepped into the war flames, a mana shield covering his body as he charged forward without scruple.

Suddenly, a flying sword emitting powerful mana stabbed through the air from ahead.

Luo Chen's expression did not change. He stretched out his right hand, his five fingers splayed wide.

Seeing this scene, the Cang Hai Alliance Adept cultivator who had sent out the flying sword sneered. There was actually someone in the world so arrogant, trying to catch a flying sword bare-handed.

Did he not know that magic treasures could be released and recalled as easily as thought? How could one seize a white blade empty-handed so easily?

Even if the other party had some reliance, as long as the sword circled around that grab, then slashed diagonally, it could cut off one of his arms, and afterward...

His thought stirred, controlling the flying sword to skirt around Luo Chen's face.

But in the next instant!

The other man's large hand shifted several cun through empty air, forcibly blocking in front of the sharp flying sword.

Then his five fingers grabbed down!

The flying sword seemed sentient, its hard blade abruptly becoming soft and spreading out from between the other man's fingertips like a spirit serpent.

Luo Chen's expression remained calm. His right hand twisted, wringing the soft sword into a coil of rope and crossing it over his arm. Then he shook it lightly.

Crack, crack, crack...

The sharp flying sword turned into countless fragments.

With his lifebound magic treasure destroyed, the Cang Hai Alliance cultivator suffered a heavy blow and spat out a mouthful of blood on the spot.

Luo Chen did not even look at him, casually flinging the flying sword fragments in another direction.

There, a thick little mountain was rumbling as it flew over.

Facing those fragments, the mountain had no fear whatsoever.

However!

Luo Chen's seemingly casual fling contained exquisite technique in the details, and he also tried injecting origin power into it.

Densely packed fragments, wreathed in blood light, plunged straight into the mountain.

Whoosh! Whoosh! Whoosh!

In no more than a breath, the fragments pierced straight through the mountain like blades cutting tofu, attacking the Moluo Stream cultivator who had released that magic treasure.

It was a group of cultivators, seven or eight people clustered together.

After piercing through the mountain magic treasure, the remaining force of those fragments did not diminish. When they reached those cultivators' side, they directly exploded.

Answering the explosions was a chorus of panicked cries and wails.

Luo Chen did not even look at the result of the battle. Standing on the wooden kite, he sped through this battlefield.

The righteous and demonic sides, who had been fighting in full swing moments before, actually came to a stop because he had thrust himself into the fray.

On this vast battlefield, it seemed extremely strange.

On the Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance's [TERM?] side, Shang Junshu [TERM?] hurriedly flew over and looked at the pale middle-aged cultivator who was vomiting blood. He was the one who had released that flying sword just now.

"Second Uncle, how are you?"

"I am fine, but my lifebound flying sword has been destroyed and my mind wounded. I am not very suited to continue fighting." The middle-aged cultivator wiped away the blood at the corner of his mouth and looked in terror toward the black-clothed cultivator. "He is a Sage old monster!"

Shattering a magic treasure bare-handed, heavily injuring seven or eight Moluo Stream Adept cultivators with one strike. Aside from a Sage cultivator, he could not think of anyone who could accomplish such a thing.

Shang Junshu [TERM?] looked in surprise at the black-clothed cultivator's departing back. That person's body was clearly emitting the realm aura of an Adept!

Although his mana was domineering and pure, far surpassing theirs, no matter what, he could not be called a Sage old monster.

Only, after looking for a while longer, Shang Junshu [TERM?] revealed a puzzled expression.

Why did this person give him the faintest sense of familiarity?

It was as if he had seen him somewhere before.

Under Shang Junshu's [TERM?] protection, the middle-aged cultivator withdrew from the front line.

"Young Master, why have you come? Shouldn't you be in the rear, leading our Four Seas Merchant Association [TERM?] manpower in organizing logistics?"

The young man called him Second Uncle, but he could not disregard clan etiquette.

As Shang Junshu [TERM?] looked suspiciously at Luo Chen's departing back, he said, "Father came, and I came with him. Later, we may need to speak with the Master of the Sky-Splitting Stream [TERM?]."

Shang Junshu's [TERM?] father was precisely Four Seas True Being [TERM?], founder of the Four Seas Merchant Association [TERM?].

The middle-aged cultivator said in pleasant surprise, "The True Being actually came personally to the front line?"

"Mm. There are some secrets involved that are not suitable to tell you. But as long as we take this battle, the sea region ahead may become our Four Seas Merchant Association's [TERM?] main base from now on."

As Shang Junshu [TERM?] spoke absently, he searched in his mind for where that familiar feeling from the black-clothed cultivator came from.

Gradually, a crimson face began to overlap with that black-clothed cultivator.

"Is it him?"

"Has he truly reached such a point?"

Before the recent righteous-demonic war, the hottest news was undoubtedly that the Myriad Immortals Assembly's powerhouses had become the greatest winners of Fallen Sea's secret realm.

And among those rumors, there was one that Shang Junshu [TERM?] cared about most.

Devil Lord Azure Yang, the rising star of the Myriad Immortals Assembly, had committed the lower against the higher and slain Feiyunzi, True Being of Feiyun Ravine [TERM?]!

As for this Devil Lord Azure Yang, he had dealt with him in earlier years.

At that time, it had been at the auction in Jadecold City [TERM?] on Cold Light Island [TERM?].

It was also at that time that Devil Lord Azure Yang had first made his name in the Northern Sea cultivation world.

Only fifty short years had passed, yet the nameless minor figure who had fled under the pursuit of a group of Adept cultivators back then had now become an existence beyond his reach.

A Northern Sea legend, a Devil Lord of his generation!

...

Luo Chen's gaze paused for an instant.

But the wooden kite beneath him neither retreated nor avoided, continuing straight ahead.

A sinister voice rang out from the battlefield.

"You wounded the cultivators under my command and still think you can leave so easily?"

A vast power descended from the sky like shackles.

It was a Sage domain!

Along with the domain came a dark, heavy giant mana palm.

Luo Chen gave a cold snort. Within that Sage domain, blood light spread over his body, and he was not slowed in the slightest. Like a fish in water, he passed freely through it.

Afterward, he slowly clenched his fist and drew back his elbow, then punched out.

His right hand stretched like soft clay, spreading with blood light as it met that giant mana palm head-on.

Boom!

With a single strike, a large hole burst open in the giant mana palm.

Luo Chen drove the wooden kite through that opening, and at the same time released a cold, fierce voice.

"Master of the Sky-Splitting Stream [TERM?], you should think about how to deal with the Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance's [TERM?] Sage Sages instead!"

A man in black-gold ritual robes hidden in a concealed place saw this scene and was first somewhat surprised.

This Adept junior could actually ignore his Sage domain.

He even possessed tremendous power, enough to forcibly receive a casual strike from him.

What kind of energy was that strange blood light?

Afterward, he frowned. The Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance's [TERM?] Sage Sages?

It was nothing more than Old Ghost Nanqi [TERM?]. The two of them had been deadlocked in this region for ten years already. What was there to deal with?

But in the next instant, his face changed instantly, like clear weather turning to black clouds.

Three powerful auras not at all inferior to his rose from the rear of the Righteous Dao Alliance [TERM?].

"Old Ghost Nanqi [TERM?]! Shang Sihai [TERM?]! Madam Mi [TERM?]!"

"How could they appear at the same time?"

...

"Your use of that power seems to be growing more and more practiced!"

Above an endless, wave-tossed sea, the wooden kite sped by.

Luo Chen cast a Dragon-Slaying Art, removing the mana aura the Master of the Sky-Splitting Stream [TERM?] had left on him just now. That man was rather insidious; under the test of a casual move, he had still left a hidden hand.

He did not refute Han Zhan's words, only gave a soft grunt.

After body cultivation reached Archaic Tier Four, a blood-colored power would be generated by squeezing the flesh. Luo Chen had named it origin power.

He had possessed this power for twenty years, but at first he had not found a way to use it.

He had merely gained a crude understanding of a few superficial tricks, such as rebirth of severed limbs and controlling bodily transformations.

But with battle after battle in the Fallen Demon Land [TERM?], especially after the physical nirvana in Bright Radiance Heaven [TERM?], he had already completely confirmed that this power was not at all weaker than a Sage Sage's mana, and had also understood several ways to use it.

For example, just now, without his second Origin Core [TERM?] and unable to release the Forest of All Things Fire Prison [TERM?], he had directly ignored the suppression of the Master of the Sky-Splitting Stream's [TERM?] Sage domain, then counterattacked with ease.

In the past, he had heard that in ancient times, archaic beasts were the masters of the Mountains and Seas Realm, and the human race could not be counted as heaven and earth's favored children.

Looking at it now, the rumors were not false.

Fourth-tier archaic beasts might not be able to control the spirit qi of heaven and earth, but with the support of origin power, they were likewise not suppressed by the spirit qi of heaven and earth.

How should this feeling be described...

It was like the relationship between fish and the sea.

Humans might be clever, able to understand the principle that water can carry a boat and also overturn it, and could control seawater for their own use.

But fish were creatures born in the sea. How could they drown in the water?

Luo Chen had previously been blocked by a leaf before his eyes.

He had even painstakingly researched the Forest of All Things Fire Prison [TERM?], trying to resist the domain suppression of Sage cultivators.

But then again, if he had not gone through the Forest of All Things Fire Prison [TERM?], he would not have so easily understood origin power's similar marvel.

Luo Chen did not want to discuss this topic, and instead began praising something else.

"Senior, this wooden kite puppet of yours may not be gorgeous, but it is truly the fastest flying treasure I have ever seen."

Han Zhan chuckled. "Naturally. This was personally refined by Devil Lord Heaven-Refining in his early years. Although its grade is not high, its value is in no way inferior to that spirit wood puppet I obtained."

That spirit wood puppet was fourth-tier, an existence corresponding to the Sage stage!

This wooden kite was actually that impressive?

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow but did not say anything more.

According to his memories, he flew at extreme speed toward Purple Spirit Island [TERM?].

The reason he had not hesitated to cross the righteous-demonic battlefield was precisely to save time. If Moon Wanderer arrived at Purple Spirit Island [TERM?] one step ahead of him, the result would be sitting by the stump waiting for the hare.

And he was that hare.

...

"Purple Spirit Island [TERM?], Purple Monkey Flowers?"

Within the Myriad Immortals Assembly.

Moon Wanderer slowly withdrew her hand.

Opposite her, Duan Li, who had once possessed a warm and gentle bearing, now looked vacant, his aura withered.

Purple Dowager said cautiously, "Angler's statement can also verify the result from Duan Li's side. Devil Lord Azure Yang indeed disappeared for one or two years without cause after the Withered Wood Ridge [TERM?] mission. According to the timing, it matches completely. However, why would Devil Lord Azure Yang want a poisonous flower like the Purple Monkey Flower?"

For what?

Moon Wanderer did not know.

But she knew that since the other party had spent so much effort clearing out demon beasts, setting up an island-encircling formation, and painstakingly cultivating Purple Monkey Flowers, he must have had a plan.

And he would surely not easily abandon the things there.

She waved her sleeve and turned to walk out of the dungeon.

Purple Dowager followed behind her. "Sage, how should we handle Duan Li?"

"At least he is a mid-stage Adept cultivator, and his foundation can be considered solid. Keep him! I am currently short of manpower. Although I searched his soul, he still has the strength for one battle."

"Then what about those disciples and sect members of Tumulus Mulberry Sect [TERM?]?"

"Kill them!" Moon Wanderer said indifferently.

Purple Dowager gave an acknowledgment, then asked again, "And the Zhang family of Cailian Island?"

Moon Wanderer waved a hand. "Kill them too. But keep that Zhou woman alive. Count it as giving the Zhou family of Coral Sea [TERM?] some face."

Purple Dowager's expression chilled, and she respectfully lowered her head in acknowledgment.

In her heart, however, she thought that the Sage truly disliked Devil Lord Azure Yang to an extreme degree! Anyone connected to him was basically met with one word: kill.

Thinking it over carefully, it was indeed impossible for her to like him.

Purple Dowager did not know what had happened inside Bright Radiance Heaven [TERM?], but Devil Lord Azure Yang had indeed forcibly seized the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion [TERM?] from beneath her and Angler's hands.

That treasure was something Moon Wanderer had specifically named as desired.

The way Moon Wanderer was watching Devil Lord Azure Yang so closely now was likely also because she intended to seize back the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion [TERM?].

This was good as well!

Azure Yang had not returned, and the Zhang family would be slaughtered. That hard-managed eight hundred li of Peng Lake would become an "ownerless thing," and she could pluck the peach.

...

The wooden kite's speed was incomparably fast.

Back then, when Luo Chen had traveled from Withered Wood Ridge [TERM?] to Purple Spirit Island [TERM?], it had taken him a full half year. This time, he arrived in less than a month.

Of course, this was partly because he trusted in his own abilities, charging straight through without taking any detours, but it also could not be separated from the wooden kite's merit.

Looking at the empty sea region, spirit light bloomed in Luo Chen's eyes, and in the next moment, an island shrouded in faint spiritual light entered his view.

It was precisely the concealment formation he had left behind years ago!

Without Seven Stars Island [TERM?] to shield it, Purple Spirit Island [TERM?] was no longer so hidden.

If not for the island-protecting grand formation he had left behind, who knew how many cultivators and demon beasts it would have provoked.

Luo Chen hid outside and quietly waited for a while.

Not until Han Zhan's voice came did he move.

"Apart from one late-stage Initiate cultivator, there is no one else on the island. It looks like Moon Wanderer has not yet come to catch you."

Luo Chen let out a breath.

That was good!

As for that Initiate cultivator, he was not surprised.

Back then, Sang Jiugong had said that during the cultivation of the Purple Monkey Flowers, someone dedicated would be needed to protect and tend them. The cultivator on the island should be a Tumulus Mulberry Sect [TERM?] disciple sent by him.

With a slight smile, Luo Chen no longer concealed his aura and went straight onto Purple Spirit Island [TERM?].

...

The simple cave dwelling Luo Chen had once opened was now arranged with care.

Moonlight Grass [TERM?], which calmed the mind and soothed the spirit, and Pale Spirit Flowers [TERM?], which tempered the spirit and gathered qi, were planted in the corners of the cave dwelling.

From this arrangement, it could be seen that the cultivator living here had the intention of residing long term.

Luo Chen looked at the honest-looking middle-aged man before him, who seemed even older than he was, and nodded in satisfaction.

"Jinghe [TERM?], these years have been hard on you."

Sang Jinghe [TERM?] stood respectfully opposite him, his face filled with flattered surprise. He had never expected that before the Purple Monkey Flowers matured, Senior Daoist Azure Yang would arrive ahead of schedule.

And he had given high praise to his work.

"It was not hard. This was all what I should do. Before Third Grandfather sat in meditation and passed away, he specially instructed me that I must carefully tend the Purple Monkey Flowers here. This junior has not dared slack for even a single day."

Luo Chen smiled, and there was a trace of regret in that smile.

Sang Jiugong had died in meditation.

Ten years after Luo Chen left the Myriad Immortals Assembly, Sang Jiugong had exhausted his lifespan and passed away.

For an Adept cultivator, this counted as a decent ending.

Only, being unable to see him one last time before he sat in meditation was truly somewhat regrettable.

He still remembered the last time they met, on that Tumulus Mulberry Island [TERM?] full of spirit mulberry trees, where the other man had warmly entertained him and traveled with him to admire the scenery of Tumulus Mulberry Island [TERM?].

This Sang Jinghe [TERM?] was also the junior Sang Jiugong had specially introduced to him at that time.

He was not of Sang Jiugong's direct bloodline, and their relationship was relatively distant.

But he was indeed the only relative of solitary Sang Jiugong apart from his disciples and sect members.

Not only did they possess a slight blood relation, he had also inherited Sang Jiugong's spirit plant arts.

It could be considered that there was someone to carry on his legacy.

Sang Jiugong had arranged for him to come here, far from Tumulus Mulberry Sect [TERM?], and he had lived here for almost thirty years now, had he not?

To other cultivators, this could be counted as exile.

But in Luo Chen's eyes, it was Sang Jiugong's earnest care for his junior before passing away.

Not only had Sang Jinghe [TERM?] been kept far from the disputes of the cultivation world, but he could also use the merit of cultivating Purple Monkey Flowers to win Luo Chen's favor.

Since an old friend had passed, Luo Chen was not stingy about looking after his descendant.

After all, this was also one of the agreements the two of them had made back then.

Thus, after Luo Chen gave a few simple words of concern, he went straight to the main topic.

"I want to take away all the Purple Monkey Flowers on Purple Spirit Island [TERM?] now!"

"Ah!" Sang Jinghe [TERM?] froze. "But those poisonous flowers have not matured yet! Although I used the ripening spirit liquid Third Grandfather gave me, counting everything fully, they still need at least ten more years."

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow. Ten years faster than the original estimate of fifty years?

He did not investigate the changes in detail and continued, "After transplanting the spirit medicines here, I will not return to the Myriad Immortals Assembly. Instead, I plan to go elsewhere. Are you willing to leave together with me?"

Sang Jinghe [TERM?] was stunned again.

Tumulus Mulberry Sect [TERM?] was still over at the Myriad Immortals Assembly.

According to Third Grandfather's last words, as long as he completed this mission, he could return and inherit the position of Tumulus Mulberry Sect's [TERM?] sect master.

Now, Superior Azure Yang actually wanted to take him along?

For a time, he hesitated.

Luo Chen did not urge him. Instead, he left the cave dwelling and came before the sinkhole where the Purple Monkey Flowers were cultivated.

Whoosh...

A breeze blew past, and an octagonal pavilion slowly appeared, hovering above the sinkhole.
