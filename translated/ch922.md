# Chapter 922: Netherwater Spring Eye, Lord of the Abyss, Spell Perfection, Spirit Opportunity Appears

The Yama Mountain of old had hung high above the nine heavens, with few able to glimpse its full form.

Now that the immortal mountain had fallen, Luo Chen could finally see its true appearance.

At first glance, it was not particularly majestic, not comparable to the Heaven-Earth Peak of Central Continent that connected heaven and earth.

In terms of precipitous strangeness, it was not like Motian Cliff's abrupt towering either.

But this mountain sat so quietly within the abyss, yet it brought a sense of immense weight, like a giant beast crouching in slumber.

Moreover, Yama Mountain was not at all defiled by the Netherwater. A great deal of heaven and earth's origin qi was still gathering from all directions, being devoured by the light of those formations.

Truly a wondrous mountain!

At this moment, at the periphery of Yama Mountain, sounds of shouting came forth.

A vast amount of ghost qi howled back and forth, sweeping the surroundings, as if heaven and earth had darkened and a hundred ghosts were marching by night.

"Where did this junior come from, daring to contend with me, Yan Qianji, for the opportunity!"

"Learning knows no precedence; attainment makes the master. We are both at the fourth-rank Ghost Emperor realm—why rely on age to act superior?"

"I have walked the path of ghost cultivation for three thousand years. At that time, you were still unaware of where you were reincarnating. How dare you compare yourself to me?"

"What a fine 'three thousand years'! I suspect those three thousand years you sealed your soul and locked your spirit, sleeping like an old turtle!"

The old man's hoarse tone and the graceful woman's voice rose and fell, not merely simple conversation.

Every word and syllable that bloomed forth carried a vast amount of soul-seizing sound waves, the most dangerous directly attacking the enemy's soul origin.

Ghost cultivators had already lost their physical bodies and had no sea of consciousness to shield the outside world.

Their strongest asset was their soul, but their greatest weakness was also that very soul, unprotected by a sea of consciousness!

Under the continuous assault, Bai Meiling, whose foundation was slightly weaker, gradually showed signs of being overwhelmed.

The large banner waving in her hand also seemed to be on the verge of folding.

"Offer up your life!"

With a furious roar, Yan Qianji fully displayed his massive ghost body, looking down and striking out with a palm.

Bai Meiling's expression changed drastically. Without hesitation, she manifested her true form, turning into a hundred-zhang ghost python, and rolled up her soul banner, preparing to leave.

But the ghost body blotted out the sky, and thick black fog shrouded all directions. No matter how she charged left and right, she could not escape. It seemed she had no choice but to receive this palm strike.

"I should have heeded Sect Master Li's words!"

A trace of regret flashed across Bai Meiling's face, but she had already made a decision.

She could die here, but the few disciples she had brought out must be sent back unharmed!

"Heavenjade, I leave it to you!"

After sending a divine sense transmission, she threw outward. The Soul-Refining Banner turned into an arrow from a bowstring, charging out of the black fog.

Bai Meiling turned back, sacrificing herself as she rushed toward that enormous palm print.

Just then, a spark blossomed abruptly, turning into a soaring fire phoenix that met the ghost palm before Bai Meiling could.

*Boom!*

In the violent impact, Bai Meiling stopped in shock and joy.

Watching the soaring fire phoenix not only break the ghost palm but also have ample afterpower, sweeping away the thick ghost fog all around in a prairie-fire momentum, revealing a clear blue sky.

A large area of ghost fog, as if struck heavily, began to shrink continuously, finally revealing Yan Qianji's true form.

It was actually a creature covered in a thick shell, like a turtle.

Only its head and tail were connected to the shell formed by ghost qi, more resembling creatures like softshell turtles or giant turtles.

His killing move broken, Yan Qianji was shocked and furious.

"Who?"

Outside the mountain, in the Netherwater, a figure stepped on the shimmering waves, entering the waterless Yama Mountain, exactly as Bai Meiling had hoped.

A black-robed man with black hair, eyes like a cold pool abyss, pupils like sun, moon, and stars, making one unable to look away.

It was Luo Chen!

He spoke calmly: "Fellow Daoist, since you claim to be a senior, why bully the weak?"

Yan Qianji snorted coldly, "The Nether Origin Sect suppressed me for several thousand years. Before their destruction, that brat Tian Yuan even urged us to be their borrowed knives. Now this Yama Mountain is merely a slight compensation. This little bitch doesn't know her place and tried to snatch my opportunity. Tell me, should I strike or not?"

Bai Meiling said anxiously, "Master, we arrived first. The broken formations at the periphery were also cracked by Min Longyu and Heavenjade. This old thing has no shame, not only following us in but also biting back!"

Master?

Yan Qianji frowned, his powerful divine sense sweeping over Luo Chen repeatedly.

Bai Meiling might not be that formidable in his eyes, but she was still a fourth-rank ghost soul venerable—and she had taken someone as master?

Then this person must be even stronger than imagined!

But no matter how his divine sense probed, he could not detect any trace of Luo Chen.

This situation could only mean one thing: this black-robed man's soul foundation was even stronger than his own!

After hearing Bai Meiling's words, Luo Chen's expression did not change. He turned to look at Yan Qianji.

"Fellow Daoist, everything must follow a principle of first come, first served. What do you think?"

Yan Qianji sneered, "Words are useless. In the end, we must settle this with strength."

Luo Chen nodded, quite agreeing: "Well said. So-called opportunities are for those fated to obtain them. And as for fate... Little Bai, the Soul-Refining Banner is not used the way you were using it. Watch closely!"

Before his words faded, the banner in Luo Chen's hand suddenly unfurled its surface.

One fierce ghost after another roared out from within.

Qi Refinement stage, Initiate stage, Adept stage... and at the end, even a deer-headed, human-bodied Ghost Emperor-level existence flew out.

Facing these ghostly beings, Yan Qianji sneered, completely unconcerned.

Ghosts refined by the Soul-Refining Banner had clouded consciousness and unclear minds. He had no fear of them!

But the next moment!

Luo Chen formed a hand seal and shouted softly: "Merge!"

Then, before Yan Qianji's disbelieving eyes, those ghosts, strong and weak alike, actually merged together, forming a towering fierce ghost a thousand zhang tall, charging at him.

"Different ghosts, with huge gaps in realm—why can they merge together?"

No one gave him the answer, and he could not think deeper.

Because at this moment, the fierce ghost's erupted power was enough to rival a late fourth-stage might!

If it were only this, Yan Qianji could still hold on for a while.

But with Luo Chen watching menacingly from the side, how could he dare to fight with full concentration?

He let out a furious roar, rolling sound waves spreading in all directions, and charged toward the merged fierce ghost.

"Master, be careful! This person's soul attack techniques are extremely powerful. Do not let his soul sound disturb your ears." Bai Meiling hastily reminded.

Bai Meiling had once used soul attack techniques to ambush the Hundred-Legged Great Demon Emperor. She had considerable skill in this path.

For her to be so wary showed the power of Yan Qianji's soul sound.

But Luo Chen did not avoid it. He let the rolling soul sound crash against his body, his form standing unmoving.

A pair of golden pupils calmly stared at Yan Qianji.

*Boom!*

Two fierce ghosts collided violently.

In an instant, the powerful fierce ghost Luo Chen had merged collapsed.

"Still a bit lacking in skill?"

Luo Chen murmured to himself. This soul-merging method originated from what he had obtained in the Northern Sea.

It was originally passed down by disciples of the Primordial Demon Sect's soul-refining lineage. After Luo Chen and Duan Li killed them, they collected it into the Myriad Immortals Assembly.

Later, Luo Chen exchanged for it and practiced it a little.

In his many years of experience, this technique had been used very few times.

Because at that time, the Soul-Refining Banner did not have a powerful main soul. Even the Ghost King merged from it was only at late Adept stage.

It was only after the battle with Bai Li Qingchuan that Luo Chen used this banner to collect the soul of one of the three Lu brothers, finally giving him the conditions to truly execute the soul-merging method.

Now it seemed, its power was indeed impressive.

The only thing lacking in skill was probably that Luo Chen was not yet proficient in using it!

But that was enough!

Under the most direct collision, the fierce ghost king collapsed, and Yan Qianji could not withstand the impact of countless evil ghost thoughts either. His entire massive ghost body began to shatter, turning into wisps of mist dispersing outward.

Bai Meiling realized something and said, "Master, he is feigning defeat to escape!"

Luo Chen had naturally already noticed this. He pressed down with a palm toward a certain void.

The Azure Yang Great Handprint was unleashed instantly, also carrying wisps of Withering-Bloom True Flame.

*Dong!*

A figure staggered into view from a certain void.

On its back was a deep palm print, almost dispersing its condensed ghost body.

It glanced fearfully at Luo Chen, then without hesitation rushed out of Yama Mountain, disappearing into the vast Nether Abyss.

"Master, should we pursue?" Bai Meiling asked.

Luo Chen waved his hand, "Let him go. The Nether Abyss is ultimately not suitable for my combat. Facing an enemy head-on is fine, but pursuing an enemy makes it easier to fall into traps."

Do not pursue a desperate foe. Luo Chen also had no mortal enmity with the other party, so he was too lazy to waste energy.

He shook the Soul-Refining Banner, and several figures fell out in a row.

As soon as they appeared, they respectfully bowed to Luo Chen.

"Master!"

"Supreme Elder!"

"Supreme Elder!"

Heavenjade, Min Longyu, and a few familiar Initiate-stage disciples.

Luo Chen looked at the group and said solemnly, "You have quite the nerve!"

Bai Meiling took the initiative to take responsibility, "Master, it was my idea. Do not blame them."

"Oh?" Luo Chen stared at Bai Meiling.

She felt a pressure as heavy as a mountain and instinctively knelt, saying fearfully, "Within the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land, the vitality is too strong. To me, it is like a cage. But in the Nether Abyss, death qi, ghost qi, and nether qi are everywhere, most suitable for my cultivation. So I could not resist and violated Sect Master Li's orders, sneaking out. Bringing Min Longyu and the others was also thinking they could help me set up formations, to avoid being discovered by other powerful existences and bringing disaster to Luo Tian Sect."

Luo Chen nodded inwardly. The current Nether Abyss was indeed very suitable for Bai Meiling's cultivation.

He had only thought about how to settle Luo Tian Sect before, neglecting the cultivation needs of some special existences within the sect.

But on the surface, he still showed nothing and asked, "Then how do you explain Yama Mountain?"

Min Longyu took over the explanation: "I discovered it. The broken formations of this mountain can block the defilement of Netherwater. It did not look like an ordinary place, so I urged Elder Bai to enter and explore. But the mountain's formations were too powerful. Even though the peripheral formations had been mostly broken by others, some powerful internal formations were still operating, so we were trapped at the foot of the mountain."

Luo Chen shook his head, looking up at the towering mountain above.

"You have no idea what kind of place this is. To barge in so recklessly is truly audacious!"

Then he explained the mountain's origin to everyone.

It came from deep within the earth's veins, originated from the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land, and had hung above the Nether Origin Sacred Land for three thousand years.

It was the sacred mountain of the Nether Origin Sacred Land!

Three great ancestors had once dwelled and cultivated there, and all who came and went were Sages.

One could say that the entire essence of the Nether Origin Sect was concentrated within this single mountain.

Relying on just a few Adept and Initiate juniors, and Bai Meiling, a Ghost Emperor at the Sage stage, to explore this mountain was simply seeking death!

Even Luo Chen dared not go deep inside.

After hearing this, Heavenjade suddenly spoke: "Master, since this mountain is so important, if our Luo Tian Sect obtains it, would we not be able to harvest a large amount of the Nether Origin Sect's inheritance?"

Luo Chen's gaze flickered, and he took a deep look at Heavenjade.

"What you say has some merit, but now is not the time."

With that, he swept his sleeve, surging mana enveloping the few people as he leisurely left the mountain.

Not long after he left, about half an hour later.

Several figures appeared outside Yama Mountain.

Each was ghostly and eerie, with violent soul fluctuations.

"So that's why Yan Qianji was fighting with someone—he discovered this mountain!"

"Is the Netherwater Spring Eye of the Nether Abyss inside Yama Mountain?"

"If the rumors are not false, it should be!"

"The reason the Nether Origin Sect was able to suppress the Nether Abyss for three thousand years was by relying on the Netherwater Spring Eye. The three great ancestors tried to suppress and refine Qinghe Immortal also by relying on the Netherwater Spring Eye. If we obtain this item, we can become the Lord of the Nether Abyss!"

"If not now, when!"

"Still, be careful. Since someone could beat that old monster Yan Qianji into severe injury and fleeing, they are likely an old monster hidden in the Nether Abyss for thousands of years."

"Careful? How many years have we been lying low? Are we going to keep shrinking?"

"Right. Even that big softshell turtle Yan Qianji has stuck his head out. There's no reason for us to be more cowardly than him."

"Let's go!"

After a discussion, the several figures charged in without hesitation.

Not only them, but as time passed, more and more ghostly beings gathered outside Yama Mountain. Almost without much hesitation, they all surged inside.

Only, most who entered did not come out.

A rumor slowly spread among the vast ghost cultivator community of the Nether Abyss.

Yama Mountain appears, the Netherwater Spring Eye emerges.

If one can control the Netherwater Spring Eye, they can become the Lord of the Nether Abyss, replacing the Nether Origin Sect and mastering this vast forbidden land!

...

Returning to the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land, Luo Chen handed over Bai Meiling and the other violators of orders to Li Yingzhang.

Those who should be punished were punished, those who should be confined were confined.

However, Luo Chen still left a way out.

"The Blessed Land is good, but bitter cultivation behind closed doors is always like building a cart behind closed doors."

"The ruins of the Nether Origin Sacred Land hide a large amount of remaining resources from the Ascendant Sacred Land. If we can obtain ten or twenty percent of it, our Luo Tian Sect will have hope for the future."

"I will leave behind a method to drive the Mystic Turtle, along with a map of the Nether Origin Sacred Land. From now on, you will regularly send people to explore the ruins around the Blessed Land and dig up resources useful to us."

"Every time you go out, have Bai Meiling lead the team, and also take the opportunity to train the sect's disciples."

"Finally, find a suitable cultivation place for Bai Meiling outside. It can serve as a place for her to cultivate, and also as a second stronghold for our Luo Tian Sect in the Nether Abyss."

Although Li Yingzhang was worried, he also felt Luo Chen's words were reasonable and respectfully accepted the order.

The Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land was good, but in the end, it only had one exit.

Once someone blocked the gate, the several hundred cultivators of Luo Tian Sect would become turtles in a jar.

If they could develop a second, or even a third stronghold, they could advance and retreat with flexibility.

As for Yama Mountain?

He and Luo Chen discussed it for a while, both believing it was not something Luo Tian Sect could explore now.

Better to let those ghost cultivators explore first and test the waters.

When something truly came of it, Luo Tian Sect could join in later.

...

After handling these trivial matters, Luo Chen returned to his previous daily routine.

He consumed the Five Temple Immortal Brew to temper his five organs, insisted on dripping two drops of Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid every day to enhance his Crimson Eye Golden Pupil.

Cultivation was not neglected either. Morning and evening lessons each once, his mana steadily improving.

And the talisman-making plan that had been forced to stop earlier was restarted after harvesting a large number of high-grade talisman skins from Hanyang Mountain.

As days passed, the fourth-rank talismans Luo Chen produced increased more and more.

Correspondingly, on the attribute panel, the proficiency of Luo Chen's high-grade fire spells, which had stagnated for many years, began to climb rapidly.

Three years later!

On this day, in the Dan Mountain cave dwelling.

Luo Chen was carefully drawing the spell *Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain* onto a talisman skin.

His movements were extremely gentle, his divine sense absolutely focused.

The normally domineering fire-attribute mana was now as obedient as a child, letting him inject it bit by bit into the talisman patterns.

When the last stroke fell, Luo Chen let out a sigh of relief.

He raised the talisman, which contained immense power, his eyes full of joy.

Another success!

Not only that, the proficiency of Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain on the panel, after being stuck for over a hundred years, had finally and arduously risen to the Great Perfection level.

Sure enough, his early idea was correct!

Making talismans required high attainment in spells.

Conversely, mass-producing talismans could also promote the improvement of a cultivator's spell proficiency.

Complementing each other, even without deliberately releasing spells to train, one could gradually push Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain to Great Perfection.

After all, a fully concentrated drawing of a fire spell talisman was more difficult than simply releasing a spell. Such progress was only natural.

"Next, I need to draw heaven and earth's origin qi into the talisman as a supplement."

Fourth-rank talismans were already different from ordinary talismans.

They contained not only the cultivator's own mana but also some external spiritual qi.

This way, when someone actively activated the talisman, it could reproduce the power of a Sage drawing on heaven and earth's origin qi for their own use.

Luo Chen smiled slightly, spread his divine sense, released his domain, and began drawing a large amount of fire-attribute spiritual qi into the talisman.

This process required caution, lest the guest overpower the host and ruin the stable talisman that had been painstakingly made.

Luo Chen was not afraid, as he was already familiar with this process.

Only this time, it lasted only a moment before Luo Chen felt something special.

"Why does it feel much easier to draw in external spiritual qi than before?"

As Luo Chen wondered, a sudden inspiration struck.

He poured mana into his eyes, source power flowing upward.

Instantly, bloodshot eyes filled his vision, golden pupils flickering.

His blood-red eyes and golden pupils stared straight into the void.

In the vast currents of qi, there were traces of a special existence that cultivators could hardly detect.

"That is... spirit opportunity!"

Luo Chen suddenly realized what it was. He quickly sat cross-legged on the ground and began to circulate his lifebound technique, actively drawing spirit opportunity into his body.

Within his sea of consciousness, the golden ocean also began to boil.

His vast soul completely merged into the Purple Mansion Sage embryo's body, beginning to inhale and exhale heaven and earth's origin qi.
