# Chapter 1182: The Cauldron Becomes a Dao Artifact, Journeying to the Ruins

**[Water Origin 9/100]**

Staring at this entry, Luo Chen was momentarily stunned.

Over five hundred years of immortal cultivation, three hundred years of body refinement, and at nearly nine hundred years of age, he had mastered countless divine techniques and myriad cultivation arts.

The once sparse attribute panel was now densely packed with entries, brilliant as a starry sky.

So much so that some of his past achievements were buried among countless entries, utterly inconspicuous.

Even though he had repeatedly compressed and hidden unimportant entries, a great many still remained.

For instance, having comprehended four of the Five Elements laws, Luo Chen, for the sake of convenience, had compiled the three origins other than [Withering-Bloom True Intent] directly together, collectively referring to them as [Water-Fire-Earth].

The reason for this arrangement was not merely convenience; the comprehension progress of those three origins was all at the starting point, with no differentiating factors.

But at some unknown time... perhaps while Luo Chen was immersed in cultivation, the entry for Water Origin had been singled out.

There was only one possibility for such a situation.

Its progress had diverged from the other entries.

And indeed!

Luo Chen swept his gaze over it. The two major origins of Fire and Earth were still at the starting point of **[1/100]** .

Only Water Origin had gained an additional eight points of progress.

Amidst the surprise, Luo Chen even felt a chill of alarm.

He had not engaged in any cultivation related to law comprehension within the Great Destruction Realm. There wasn't even any spiritual energy, so how could his law comprehension have deepened?

Furthermore, Luo Chen was certain that before entering the Ninefold Immortal Tower, before cultivating in the Destruction Nine Rings, he had checked his attribute panel, and it had not been like this.

"Laws... Destruction Nine Rings!"

Suddenly.

Luo Chen recalled something.

In the void, Luo Chen halted his return to Lisheng Island and immediately sat cross-legged in the Chaotic Sea.

He poured his entire mind and spirit into his body, even awakening a part of his long-dormant main soul.

All his attention converged upon the Primordial Unity Cauldron, which was operating autonomously.

Upon careful observation, Luo Chen was startled to realize that the cauldron's surface was covered in a faint layer of blue light. These blue lights originated from the paintings on the inner and outer walls of the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

On the murals, mountains and rivers leaned upon each other, birds circled, and beasts roamed everywhere.

Each one was lifelike, but ultimately, they were not living things.

Only those streams, rivers, and seas were incredibly lively, as if they were real.

Seeing this, Luo Chen's entire body trembled.

Through the heart-mind connection with his lifebound magic treasure, he instantly recognized what this signified.

"Water Origin has reached completion!"

"No!"

"It should be phrased differently. The Primordial Unity Cauldron has gathered the Water Origin. It possesses a complete law origin!"

In the Ruins, the Dao was incomplete, and laws were shattered.

Therefore, aside from the cultivation methods for tempering the physical body itself, no other cultivation systems could continue.

Luo Chen had known this for a long time.

In fact, when he first entered the Destruction Nine Rings, he had discovered that while the Primordial Unity Cauldron absorbed chaotic energy, it also absorbed some of the imperceptible law fragments within it.

But those fragments were too small and too few. After the initial surprise, Luo Chen had not paid them much attention.

Until over a hundred years later...

At some point, the Primordial Unity Cauldron had silently gathered the law fragments of Water Origin, constructing a complete origin law.

"If that's really the case, then these extra points of Water Origin on my own panel are the cauldron's feedback after it had its fill?"

Luo Chen made this conjecture.

But this conjecture only made him more astonished.

Long ago, back in the Mountains and Seas Realm, right after he first comprehended a law true intent, he had discussed with others at the Shouyang Discourse, asking how to improve the comprehension of law true intent.

The answers he received back then were few.

However, over the subsequent centuries, Luo Chen had gradually grasped the relevant cultivation common sense.

Within the universe, there were three thousand Great Daos and countless laws.

Great Daos encompassed laws, and many homologous laws coalesced into a Great Dao.

For instance, the Withering-Bloom True Intent that Luo Chen had first comprehended was merely one type of law true intent contained within the Wood Great Dao.

To truly master the Wood Great Dao, one would inevitably need to comprehend Wood Origin and many other law true intents related to the Wood Dao.

Among the many law true intents, origin power was particularly unique.

It was not obtained through chance or insight but required daily accumulation and gradual saving through cultivation.

Moreover, this cultivation process had been summarized and systematized by generations of sages, forming a complete path.

Ascendant was the starting point, but that did not mean that upon reaching the Ascendant stage, a cultivator could directly cultivate origin power. They could only use this key to grasp the origin qi of heaven and earth.

Ascendants cultivated the primordial spirit, and Void Refiners condensed the Law Manifestation. These two realms were merely laying the foundation for cultivating origin power.

Only by achieving Void Refinement and Dao Union, refining the false into the true, and merging the Law Manifestation with the primordial spirit, could one be considered to have laid a solid foundation for cultivating origin power.

In a sense, the three realms of Ascendant, Void Refinement, and even Body Integration could all be seen as the "Initiate stage" on the path of cultivation.

After Body Integration, cultivators no longer feared getting lost in the ocean of origin qi, nor did they fear the danger of assimilation from long-term activation of origin power. They could openly extract law origin from heaven and earth.

Once this was achieved, they reached the Great Vehicle stage.

At this point, a cultivator's lifespan would reach an entire epoch, enough to share the heavens' longevity, and they were thus revered as a Great Vehicle Heavenly Sovereign!

This was precisely where Luo Chen was astonished.

Setting aside whether he could currently cultivate qi and refine energy, his previous realm was merely the Ascendant stage. He was far from extracting heaven and earth's origin; he hadn't even condensed a Law Manifestation.

Yet now, he had begun absorbing origin power.

This was equivalent to cultivating across two major realms!

And it wasn't even him doing the cultivation; it was the Primordial Unity Cauldron helping him "grind levels"!

Luo Chen did not know if there were any risks involved. He only knew that this completely defied all common sense.

If this were spoken of, countless races and mighty experts would absolutely refuse to believe it.

And the source of all these bizarre changes was none other than his lifebound magic treasure, which had absorbed that wisp of Order Light—the Primordial Unity Cauldron!

Luo Chen gazed at the small grey cauldron. Three legs, two ears, a round belly.

What grade should be used to classify the current Primordial Unity Cauldron's level?

A true artifact was naturally impossible.

A spirit treasure... a spirit treasure was merely an enlarged version of a true artifact that had developed intelligence and greatly increased its killing power. It absolutely could not reach this level.

What about a heaven-reaching spirit treasure?

Luo Chen pondered for a moment, then slightly shook his head.

He had seen a heaven-reaching spirit treasure. The Heaven-Refining Cauldron left behind by Devil Lord Heaven-Refining was of that grade.

Such treasures, under the constant nurturing of their masters, gradually became imbued with law power. Though called "heaven-reaching," they were still far from the point of containing a complete law within.

An immortal artifact?

Luo Chen was currently inside an immortal artifact.

The Ninefold Immortal Tower!

If it were before, he might have still viewed immortal artifacts through a filter.

But now it seemed, immortal artifacts were merely weapons made of better materials and powered by higher-grade energy sources.

Inexplicably, a term surfaced in Luo Chen's mind.

"A Dao artifact?"

Legends said that when heaven and earth first opened and chaos evolved, some innate treasures would be born within the great thousand worlds.

The powerful ones inherently carried law and Dao rhyme, becoming Dao artifacts on their own.

The weaker ones would also possess corresponding characteristics. If added during the refinement of a magic treasure, their effects would be endless.

Luo Chen had once obtained a weak innate treasure.

Its name was Xirang, extracted from the Five Elements Pagoda, the magic treasure of the Five Elements Divine Sect Ancestor. It was a very small amount, yet it had greatly increased the quality of the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Looking at it now, the current Primordial Unity Cauldron did indeed somewhat match the description of a legendary Dao artifact.

Having a complete law origin within itself—was that not carrying law and Dao rhyme?

Furthermore!

Luo Chen opened his eyes, his gaze sweeping across his surroundings.

The surging chaotic energy had almost formed an ocean.

Could this be considered a certain state of "heaven and earth first opening, chaos evolving"?

It was under these circumstances that the Primordial Unity Cauldron had condensed a complete Water Origin!

Therefore, it was very likely a Dao artifact, or at least had the prototype of one!

At this thought, Luo Chen's heart could no longer remain calm.

An ordinary spirit treasure was already the dream of any cultivator.

The emergence of a heaven-reaching spirit treasure would inevitably stir up a bloody storm of unknown magnitude.

If it were an immortal artifact, an entire realm would probably fight to the death over it.

Yet he possessed a Dao artifact!

"Such a heavy treasure must never be known to outsiders, or it will surely invite immense trouble."

Amidst the surging emotions, it took him a long time to calm down.

His gaze swept around, and then he hurried back to Lisheng Island.

...

In the following days, Luo Chen devoted all his attention to cultivation.

Though called cultivation, it also involved observing the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

With each session of cultivation, with each operation of the Primordial Unity Cauldron, as vast amounts of chaotic energy were transformed, the cauldron continued to absorb those unknown, imperceptible law fragments.

Whether the speed was fast or slow, Luo Chen could not know.

But he could make a simple inference through the changes in the entries on his attribute panel.

The first to be completed was Water Origin, so it had fed back water-attribute origin power. However, the speed was somewhat slow. Over twenty years had passed, and it had only increased from **[9/100]** to **[10/100]** .

Next was Earth Origin, because on Luo Chen's panel, the entry for Earth Origin had also been singled out and had risen to **[2/100]** .

Finally, there was Fire Origin. This was the major Dao direction Luo Chen had primarily cultivated over his centuries of cultivation.

Yet unexpectedly, it was the last to receive the cauldron's feedback.

As for the Wood Withering-Bloom that Luo Chen had comprehended earliest, there was no movement at all.

Luo Chen did not know if it was because the Primordial Unity Cauldron had not gathered this type of law true intent, or if higher-order law true intents related to comprehension would not be fed back. In any case, the progress bar remained completely still.

After long-term observation, while Luo Chen was pleased, he had lost his initial ecstasy and shock.

Because the speed was too slow!

Over twenty years, only one or two points were fed back. Even if he were given another two or three hundred years, it would at most amount to ten or twenty points.

It was far from reaching the level of a complete law needed to escape the Ruins.

This tremendous opportunity seemed to have only been tasted superficially.

Luo Chen was somewhat unwilling, but he could only accept it.

Because according to what Carefree Realm Lord had said, they only had two hundred years left.

"I can't just sit and wait any longer!"

Law origin was difficult to complete, and while his martial realm seemed to advance by leaps and bounds, it had fallen into the bottleneck of the late Human Immortal stage.

To leave this cage, he had to find another way.

A object materialized in Luo Chen's hand.

It was the map left behind by Carefree Realm Lord before his departure. Both he and Gu Xun had copied a copy from Fang Qingxue.

"Ancient God's Dao Field?"

Luo Chen murmured to himself.

...

On Sigu Island.

Gu Xun hosted a banquet, inviting Luo Chen and Fang Qingxue.

Amidst the warm wine and lively conversation, they chatted about various unrelated topics.

Most of it was Luo Chen speaking.

The content was his experiences back in the Mountains and Seas Realm, and the sights of the Xuan Realm he had learned from others or ancient texts.

Gu and Fang listened, their eyes showing longing.

"After reaching the Human Immortal realm, I once traveled through the Ruins."

"I have seen falling stars, observed the Bone Mountain, and also visited a few small human settlements, such as the Canopy Pagoda Forest and the Weeping Abyss. These various places, though seemingly vast, are actually cramped. Compared to the boundless universe you speak of, Fellow Daoist Luo, this Ruins is truly too narrow."

Luo Chen had long known that the Ruins contained more human settlements than just the seventy-two cities of the Great Destruction Realm.

Beyond the Great Destruction Realm, there were the Corpse Remnant Immortal Domain, the Sea of Stars, as well as some smaller settlements, or mysterious and eerie ruins.

The Ancient God's Dao Field that Carefree Realm Lord had gone to explore this time was one of them.

These humans gathered together not only for mutual warmth and protection but also, like Carefree Realm Lord, to search for a way out of the Ruins.

The Great Destruction Realm had the Destruction Gate, containing a one-in-a-billion chance of reaching the outside world.

In the Sea of Stars, countless broken stars filled the sky like an ocean. Legend said that when all the stars fell, guided by the starlight, one could leave the Ruins.

The Canopy Pagoda Forest stretched straight to the heavens, but its path was blocked by endless thunder.

The Weeping Abyss extended deep underground, and to this day, no one had explored its end.

...

In all these places, there was hope of leaving, and equally, martial experts held sway.

The powerful were not weaker than Carefree Realm Lord.

The weakest were at least at the Human Immortal level of martial arts.

Therefore, to travel the Ruins, one needed at least the strength of the Human Immortal realm.

"The century mark will arrive once more. I also wish to emulate Fellow Daoist Gu and travel outside for a while."

Amidst the casual conversation, Luo Chen proposed this plan.

Gu Xun and Fang Qingxue were immediately caught off guard.

Luo Chen told them frankly, "My cultivation has reached a bottleneck. Perhaps if I bitterly cultivate in the Destruction Nine Rings for another one or two hundred years, I might break through. But by then, I'm afraid there will be no chance to leave. So this journey is inevitable."

Gu Xun and Fang Qingxue looked at each other, somewhat at a loss for words, even finding it a little amusing.

The breakthrough Luo Chen spoke of was likely the next major realm.

Spending only this much time to break through was truly rare.

But the other party was still unsatisfied.

However, dissatisfaction was understandable.

Time waited for no one!

Even if he managed to break through to the sixth realm of body refinement, the Destruction Realm, what then?

Wasn't Carefree Realm Lord at that very realm? Yet he was still forced to abandon the Destruction Gate he had guarded and seek opportunities elsewhere.

"Will you come back?" Fang Qingxue asked softly.

Luo Chen nodded heavily. "If I do not fall outside and do not find a way out, I will naturally return."

"Then if you leave, who will maintain the Ninefold Immortal Tower? Or, as Carefree Realm Lord had previously planned, will you simply close it?" Gu Xun followed up.

Luo Chen smiled slightly. "You need not worry about this matter. I have a way to keep the Ninefold Immortal Tower running even after I leave. You may continue to cultivate in the Destruction Nine Rings."

Gu Xun and Fang Qingxue exchanged a glance, relieved.

"That is excellent!"

"Give me a few more decades, and I can break through to the late Human Immortal stage."

Then the two raised their cups.

"We wish you a smooth journey, Fellow Daoist / Brother Luo!"

Luo Chen raised his cup and drank with them, toasting the century of shared cultivation.

Setting down the cup, Luo Chen lifted his head, looking towards the heights.

"Before I leave, I would like to go and see that gate."
