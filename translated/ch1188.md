# Chapter 1188: Three Styles United, Corpse Immortal’s Finger

*“All living things decay, only bones endure forever.*
*Corpse wax for sacrifice, qi and blood as burning fuel.*
*Flesh and blood as offerings, skeletons as steps.*
*All things return to nothingness, immortal bones bear eternal witness.”*

On an endless expanse of land, a long procession of people slowly advanced forward.

They wore garments of every color, had different appearances and attire, yet all knelt and kowtowed in perfect unison, chanting the same words of praise.

The procession stretched beyond the horizon, but in someone’s eyes, its endpoint could be seen.

At the head of the human flow stood a towering white bone palace!

It rose at the end of a sea of bones and a thousand mountains, shrouded in a thin mist of pitch-black phosphorescent light.

The main material constructing the palace was not ordinary stone or giant timber, but countless bones—some ghastly white, others pitch black.

The black-and-white color scheme was far too conspicuous on this land, and combined with the majestic, towering architecture, it resembled a slumbering giant beast standing upon the earth.

Luo Chen gazed into the distance with a solemn expression, while Yuan Qingzang stared in shock at the long procession below.

“Those are the people of Chonghai Tower!”

“What are the warriors of Xueyan Gorge doing here?”

“Eh, that old woman in the magnificent red dress—could she be an elder of the Red Lotus Tribe!”

……

As the master of the Azure Dome Pagoda Forest, Yuan Qingzang might not know every detail of the Guixu, but he was somewhat familiar with the nearby powers, large and small.

From mere physical characteristics or distinctive attire, he could quickly distinguish the origins of these people within the procession.

Hearing names like Chonghai Tower and Red Lotus Tribe, Luo Chen matched them one by one from his memories.

When Gu Xun had traveled through Guixu back then, he hadn’t wandered aimlessly.

Instead, he had deliberately avoided dangerous areas like the Corpse Remnant Immortal Domain and the Heavenly Wind Tribe, choosing to visit places that weren’t particularly powerful and were relatively friendly to humans.

Among them were Chonghai Tower, Xueyan Gorge, and the Red Lotus Tribe.

The leaders of these factions were mostly at the Human Immortal realm, perhaps not even as strong as the Azure Dome Pagoda Forest in terms of power.

But still, they counted as regional powers!

How could they tolerate their subjects coming to the Corpse Remnant Immortal Domain? And chanting slogans that seemed like faith-based sacrificial worship?

Most importantly, Yuan Qingzang had said earlier that the vast Corpse Remnant Immortal Domain had only one permanent resident—the one called “Corpse”!

Had something changed here?

Luo Chen’s mind stirred. In the instant before Yuan Qingzang could speak, he had already descended into the procession.

His great hand reached out, five dragons materializing, and forcibly pulled out an old woman who was in the midst of kowtowing.

“Luo Chen, what are you doing?”

“Nothing much, just asking about the situation.”

Luo Chen smiled, his gaze falling on the old woman.

Her skin was like withered wood, her eyes like flickering flames, her life force rapidly draining away.

But judging by her features, she must have been a beauty in her youth.

Especially the dim red lotus pattern between her brows, which seemed even more eerie.

According to what Yuan Qingzang had said earlier, this person was likely an elder of the Red Lotus Tribe, at roughly the Vajra realm.

Someone like this was suitable for questioning.

“What is your name?”

The old woman’s eyes were vacant.

“What are you doing here?”

The old woman remained silent.

Luo Chen’s brow furrowed slightly. He glanced into the distance, then withdrew his gaze.

His eyes gradually grew deep, and he softly uttered a single word.

“Zha!”

This was a word-soul he quite liked—the first sound in Daoist mythology that awakened all spirits at the dawn of the universe. It had a peculiar effect when used to break through mind-shaking secret arts.

Especially since, when Luo Chen spoke the soft sound, it was accompanied by rumbling thunder.

That was the Soul-Shaking Thunder Sound!

As expected!

When Luo Chen uttered that single word, it fell upon the old woman’s ears like a great bell or massive drum. Her originally confused eyes gradually gained a trace of clarity.

But within that trace of clarity was an irrepressible struggle and sinking.

Luo Chen knew the opportunity was fleeting, so he immediately used qi-tone resonance and divine-intent bewitchment techniques to induce her to speak the truth.

“Name?”

“Red Leaf.”

“What are you doing here?”

“Worshipping the holy hall, offering incense to the Corpse Immortal.”

“Corpse Immortal? Could it be the one called Corpse?”

“He is no longer an ordinary corpse, but a Corpse Immortal who has attained the Dao. All living beings should revere him and offer their incense.”

“Is Corpse still within the holy hall?”

“He is both present and not present. The Corpse Immortal is eternal, existing in infinite time and space.”

……

Hearing these words, Luo Chen’s brow furrowed deeply.

Beside him, Yuan Qingzang was utterly horrified.

What “attained the Dao and become an immortal”?

What “both present and not present”?

And what was this talk of “eternal life”?

Yuan Qingzang recalled Corpse’s fearsome reputation from the past and subconsciously said, “Luo Chen, how about we leave?”

Luo Chen glanced at him without responding, intending to continue questioning Red Leaf.

But at that moment, the struggle in her eyes grew increasingly intense.

It was as if another powerful will was interfering with Luo Chen’s sound-wave soul-capturing technique.

Just as she was about to sink into fanatical confusion, a rare moment of clarity appeared in Red Leaf’s eyes.

She pointed at her own brow.

A dim red lotus flower floated out.

“Senior, please inform the Red Lotus Ancestor that the Red Lotus Tribe has been destroyed by Corpse. Avenge us!”

As the words fell, that flash of clarity suddenly dissipated, replaced by the original fanatical determination.

She didn’t even look at Luo Chen, returning to the procession and continuing her pilgrimage toward the distant white bone holy hall.

A dim red lotus hung before him.

In silence, Luo Chen reached out and let it fall into his palm.

Seeing this, Yuan Qingzang on the side couldn’t help but sigh.

“Why invite these troubles?”

“If it were your three-thousand-pagoda subjects facing this, would you still call it trouble?”

“So you intend to see this through to the end?”

“I’m not one to meddle in others’ affairs. But…”

Luo Chen clenched his fist, a hint of interest appearing in his eyes.

“But I’m very curious about what exactly happened in the Corpse Remnant Immortal Domain. Not only did Corpse go to such lengths to destroy and enslave the subjects after the tribal leaders left, but he also brought them here for this religious-like pilgrimage?”

“You mean the leaders of the Red Lotus Tribe and other factions left long ago, and Corpse merely took advantage of the opportunity?”

Luo Chen couldn’t be bothered to answer that question.

Red Leaf’s earlier message for him to relay already proved that the Red Lotus Ancestor was no longer within the tribe—perhaps he had also gone to the Ancient God Dao Field.

While Yuan Qingzang was still stunned, he saw Luo Chen heading toward the direction of the endless procession’s pilgrimage.

He quickly followed, still voicing his concerns.

Saying things like how formidable Corpse was, and how they shouldn’t provoke such trouble—the same old refrain.

But Luo Chen ignored him entirely.

He now had a general concept of his own strength.

He was practically invincible at the Human Immortal realm!

Against someone like Yuan Qingzang, who had touched upon the Destruction realm with his Azure Dome Pagoda Forest, Luo Chen was confident he could win at the cost of minor injuries.

Only against beings like Wuyou Domain Master and Corpse—those who had cultivated to the Destruction realm and could be called regional kings—was he unclear about the gap.

But Luo Chen was also confident that, even if he couldn’t win, he could protect himself by using his martial arts divine abilities.

This trip was about touring Guixu. Since he’d encountered something interesting, he naturally wanted to get to the bottom of it.

And besides!

What if there were opportunities similar to the Chaotic Thunder Pool or the Yuan Magnetic Force again?

Seeing Luo Chen persist in his course, the chattering Yuan Qingzang could only stop trying to dissuade him, merely preparing himself to flee at any moment.

When the white bone holy hall appeared in his line of sight, confusion appeared on his face for the first time.

“When did the Corpse Remnant Immortal Domain get such a palace?”

Luo Chen advanced while activating his Crimson Pupil Dual Eyes and simultaneously spreading his astral qi, using his martial arts divine intent to perceive everything around him.

Gradually, he detected the differences in this realm through his vision.

And the source of these differences lay in the increasingly numerous crowds nearby.

*“All living things decay, only bones endure forever.*
*Corpse wax for sacrifice, qi and blood as burning fuel.*
…”

Faintly, it seemed as though translucent forces were emanating from the chanting of these crowds, drawn by some attraction, and finally converging upon that white bone holy hall.

Luo Chen was certain he had never seen this kind of power before.

But the feeling… it didn’t seem unfamiliar.

“It’s like I’ve encountered it somewhere before?”

Just as Luo Chen was puzzled!

Yuan Qingzang’s footsteps beside him stopped.

Luo Chen instinctively turned his head. “What’s wrong?”

Yuan Qingzang swallowed his saliva, pointing downward.

Luo Chen looked down in confusion, only to find that at some point, the grand chanting—as grand as a Buddhist invocation—had ceased.

Hundreds of thousands, perhaps millions, of devotees were staring at Luo Chen and his companion with fanatical eyes, which then gradually turned icy cold.

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow, instinctively glancing at the white bone holy hall.

“No matter!”

Yuan Qingzang’s face tightened under the gaze of millions.

“Are you sure it’s fine?”

Luo Chen smiled, a hint of disdain appearing on his face.

Although there were many powerful beings in Guixu, it was ultimately a cage.

Aside from those like Wuyou Domain Master, Mu Bugui, and himself who had unfortunately fallen in from the outside world, the battles the natives experienced were mostly limited to individual conflicts.

Even when there were large-scale scenes, they were mostly tribal wars.

But Luo Chen was different!

Throughout his journey, he had walked over mountains of corpses and seas of blood.

Whether it was the great war between the human and demon races in the Eastern Wilderness and Northern Sea, or the continent-level battle between the Eastern Wilderness and Central Continent, the scale had been immense.

Now, with not even a single person capable of facing him head-on, how could he be intimidated?

“If they dare to block my path, I won’t mind helping them find release.”

Luo Chen spoke calmly, his steps never faltering as he drew closer to the white bone holy hall.

And as he approached, the devotees’ gazes grew colder and colder, until finally, all were fixed upon him.

“Kill!”

A bone-chilling voice sounded from somewhere unknown.

But it drew thousands of responses.

“Kill!”

“Kill!”

“Kill!”

The killing intent was overwhelming, directed at one man!

Countless warriors shot into the air, charging at Luo Chen like moths to a flame.

Faced with this scene, Luo Chen’s expression didn’t change in the slightest. He merely uttered two words.

“Courting death!”

The next moment, his entire body shook. Dense thunderlight burst forth, spreading across the wilderness, covering ten thousand li in all directions.

Then astral qi flowed, lightning surged like waves, continuous and unending.

All the devotees were drowned in the thunderlight and electric currents.

Finally, Luo Chen’s hands clenched into fists, countless electric currents erupting from between his fingers.

As if responding, within the ten-thousand-li range, all the thunderlight and electric currents suddenly exploded.

*Crackle!*

In the crisp sound of thunder, all the warriors fell like rain.

Fresh white bones were added to the earth.

One move annihilated a million people!

The thick stench of blood rose to the heavens. Luo Chen stood amidst it all, like a god or a demon.

Yuan Qingzang, who had fled far away, watched this scene, his eyes filled with indescribable shock.

Not because of Luo Chen’s massacre.

But because in that brief move just now, every technique was familiar to him.

First was the Thunderbolt Wilds—the method of spreading thunderous astral qi.

Then came the Raging Thunder Torrent—the exquisite technique of building momentum.

Finally, the Deluge Extinction—the ultimate killing move that annihilated everything with absolute force.

And Luo Chen had completely fused these three techniques together, creating a martial arts divine ability that was both familiar and strange to him.

“Could this be what a true martial arts genius looks like?”

As for Luo Chen stealing and learning his clan’s martial arts, Yuan Qingzang couldn’t muster any resentment at this moment—only boundless admiration.

Just as the rain of blood was falling.

A vast will rose from within the white bone holy hall.

Yuan Qingzang shouted, “Be careful!”

Luo Chen, with his back to him, squinted at the white bone holy hall, his expression shifting slightly.

Then his hands suddenly turned, forming a positive-negative posture.

Two enormous millstones rose rumblingly above his head.

At the same time!

Above the white bone holy hall, that vast will emanated a deathly stillness, beginning to spread wildly.

A hundred li, a thousand li, ten thousand li… its end was unknown.

It seemed as though the entire Corpse Remnant Immortal Domain was completely enveloped by this will.

Streams of pitch-black power converged, finally forming a gigantic finger suspended in the sky.

The lines of the finger’s joints were vividly clear, the bluish-black nails hideous and terrifying.

“Little junior, will you not retreat?”

An icy voice issued from the white bone holy hall.

But Luo Chen revealed a rare ferocious smile.

“Bluffing!”

As the words fell, his hands suddenly spread apart.

The great mill officially began to turn!

The vast sea of blood, the endless mountains of bones—all were drawn in by the extreme pulling force, completely absorbed.

A fierce light flashed between Luo Chen’s brows. He felt this move still wasn’t extreme enough.

His mind stirred!

Another force flowed out from within his body, adding to the World-Annihilating Mill.

Yuan Qingzang, who had already fled far away, caught a glimpse of this and blurted out, “Yuan Magnetic Divine Power!”

Yes.

Luo Chen had a sudden inspiration, boldly incorporating the Yuan Magnetic Force he had only recently obtained into the *Heaven-Earth Reversal* technique!

The divine ability, already amplified a hundredfold, was now even more formidable.

Under the divine power’s enhancement, even that white bone holy hall began to stir restlessly.

Only then could that vast will no longer tolerate Luo Chen’s provocation. It pointed down at the World-Annihilating Mill.

Ultimate techniques clashed, the impact was earth-shattering.

Mountains and rivers changed color, heaven and earth lost all light!

In the rumbling roar, surging air waves rolled back to the horizon, endless layers of clouds swept clean.

The already fragile space was densely covered with hideous, centipede-like cracks.

In such an apocalyptic scene, even Yuan Qingzang, who had already fled thousands of li away, couldn’t help himself. He could only open his astral qi shield to protect himself as best he could.

Wave after wave of air struck him, battering him like duckweed in a flood.

Groaning in misery, a fierce look flashed across Yuan Qingzang’s face.

Did he really have to use *that* move?

Suddenly!

No one knew how long had passed, or if it was only an instant.

The terrifying air waves outside abruptly vanished.

Yuan Qingzang was stunned. He flew out in confusion.

Looking around now, the previous scene of a sea of bones and a thousand mountains was gone.

The earth seemed to have been plowed, covered in crisscrossing ravines.

The sky was even more terrifying—streaks of spatial cracks stretched and twisted, making one’s scalp tingle.

Yuan Qingzang hesitated for a moment, then finally gritted his teeth and headed back to the previous location.

The return was many times slower. After dozens of breaths, he could finally see the scene at the center of the battlefield from afar.

An image of blood and bone intertwined, like hell itself.

And in the midst of that hell, Luo Chen stood in his white robe, spotless.

He was standing on the last step of the white bone staircase, walking toward the holy hall’s gate, which resembled a bloody maw.
