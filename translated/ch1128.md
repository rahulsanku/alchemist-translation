# Chapter 1128: The Tobacco Workshop Manager

The green lamp was broken and worn, its flame faint and flickering.

When the shopkeeper, who had initially paid little attention to Luo Chen, saw this scene, he casually asked:

"Want to buy it?"

Luo Chen avoided answering and instead pressed for the lamp's origin.

The fat shopkeeper frowned and replied perfunctorily, "Where else could it come from? We acquired it! The Myriad Manifestations Tower spans all the towns and cities of the Great Destruction Realm. As long as something is even slightly special, we take it in. This lamp isn't worth much. The previous shopkeeper didn't take it when he left office, so it was left behind."

There wasn't much information in this explanation.

And as the fat shopkeeper said, this lamp was indeed not a valuable item.

But to Luo Chen, it held extraordinary significance!

Because this lamp was once his magic tool, named the **Broken Form Green Lamp**!

Yes, it was merely a magic tool, one Luo Chen had obtained when he casually destroyed the Shadow Image Sect during his trip to the Southern Frontier.

Later, on Mi Jie Mountain, in order to cultivate the spatial restriction among the seven basic prohibitions, Luo Chen had spent day and night observing the spatial nodes on Mi Jie Mountain. During that process, he had conducted many experiments. One experiment he had attempted several times.

That was to place some items marked with imprint clues inside a storage bag, then forcibly shatter the storage bag. Through the destruction of this spatial treasure, he could observe the most minute spatial fluctuations.

During the experiments, one phenomenon had greatly piqued Luo Chen's curiosity.

That was, as the storage bag shattered, some of the treasures inside would fall into the real world, while another portion would mysteriously vanish without a trace.

Even though Luo Chen had once placed divine sense, spiritual power, and even blood essence marks on those items, he could not track their whereabouts.

The Broken Form Green Lamp was one of the experimental subjects that had disappeared!

What Luo Chen had never expected was that this item would actually appear in the small town of Qingshan.

Even though the divine sense and spiritual power marks on it had been completely erased, a small amount of blood essence mark still remained. That was why Luo Chen could easily activate it.

"So, are you buying it or not?" The fat shopkeeper grew impatient and urged him.

Luo Chen stared at the green lamp for a long time, then finally put it down.

"If you're not buying, stop asking questions and wasting time. Go, go, go..."

Unfazed by this rude attitude, Luo Chen pondered and left the Myriad Manifestations Tower.

After he left, the fat shopkeeper began to tidy up the items Luo Chen had touched. When he felt the slightly warm green lamp, he suddenly remembered something, and a look of shock and uncertainty appeared on his face.

"How could he activate this thing?"

...

The Qingshan Tobacco Workshop was built within Qingshan Town and covered a large area.

It contained many buildings of various sizes and a great quantity of stacked raw materials.

This tobacco workshop integrated raw material processing, rough processing, and fine processing. Every year, a large amount of Decadent Immortal Tobacco was produced here, then transported to the main city by specialized merchant caravans.

Under a large tree at the main entrance, Luo Chen stood in the shade, gently stroking his chin in contemplation.

He could be completely certain that the green lamp was his experimental magic tool from back then, but why it had appeared here baffled him.

The Broken Form Green Lamp itself was not valuable, so he did not intend to buy it.

But Luo Chen vaguely sensed that the reason the Broken Form Green Lamp had appeared here might be closely related to how he had ended up in the Great Destruction Realm. Perhaps it was even the same answer!

"Why is that?"

"Clearly, it was a storage bag shattered in the Mountains and Seas Realm, yet some treasures always disappeared. Did all those vanished treasures come to the Great Destruction Realm?"

"And what exactly is this Great Destruction Realm?"

As he pondered, faint noises of commotion came from inside the tobacco workshop.

Luo Chen frowned and looked over.

...

"I never thought Xiao Lan was so pretty!"

"It's almost noon, so hot. Why go back? Why not come to Second Master's place for a meal?"

"What are you hiding from..."

Lai Er looked at the evasive woman before him, a lascivious gleam shining in his eyes.

He had never imagined that this ordinary female worker named Jing Lan in the tobacco workshop actually had such a beautiful face!

If he hadn't taken the time to visit the workshop today, he would have missed out.

Usually, she was always covered in dust and grime, and because the tobacco-making process had a pungent smell, coupled with Jing Lan's usual introverted and withdrawn nature, he had hardly paid her any attention.

Today, although she still wasn't dressed up, just wiping the tobacco ash off her face and lightly penciling her brows and moistening her lips gave her a fresh, lotus-like beauty emerging from clear water.

Such beauty was like a celestial being among the nearby villages.

"Why not follow Second Master? You'll have plenty to eat and warm clothes to wear, and you won't have to do these hard jobs every day. Look at these little hands, they've become so rough..."

As he spoke, he had already reached out his hand.

Jing Lan's face was full of panic, and tears welled up in her eyes.

Everyone in Qingshan Town knew Lai Er's reputation.

Relying on his position as a manager in the tobacco workshop, he had been throwing his weight around for years and already had five or six concubines at home.

But those women didn't have an easy life!

After Lai Er tired of them, they still had to do all kinds of heavy labor, and the money they earned had to support Lai Er in turn.

The hardship was even greater than that of ordinary farmers.

Today, accompanying Luo Chen out, she had inexplicably dressed up a little, only to be targeted by this scoundrel Lai Er.

From now on, there would be no peace while working in the tobacco workshop.

Seeing Lai Er reach out, she slapped his hand away and then ran out in panic.

Lai Er naturally wouldn't let the beauty he had finally discovered get away. He was about to chase after her when a tall figure blocked his way.

"Who are you?"

Luo Chen didn't look at him, but instead looked at Jing Lan.

Jing Lan's face showed relief, then she bit her lip and shook her head.

Seeing this exchange of glances, Lai Er's anger flared.

"This is the tobacco workshop, a restricted area. Who let you, an outsider, in? Looking for a beating!"

With a furious shout, Lai Er threw a punch at Luo Chen.

*Smack!*

A fist the size of a sandbag was caught in the palm of a hand.

Luo Chen paid it no mind, instead raising his head to look at a small building in the distance.

A gaze full of doubt met his.

"Ah, it hurts, it hurts..."

Lai Er's miserable wails sounded in his ears. Luo Chen snorted lightly and casually let go, while several invisible streams of energy burrowed into him.

Lai Er looked at him in terror, huge beads of sweat forming on his forehead. His arm hung limply at his side.

"You..."

"Let's go!"

Luo Chen turned and stepped out of the tobacco workshop, with Jing Lan following closely behind.

Lai Er could only watch, unable to muster any thought of revenge, his heart filled only with fear.

Outside the tobacco workshop.

Jing Lan suddenly said, "That man has nothing to do with me."

Luo Chen casually acknowledged her.

Jing Lan bit her lip. "He's a minor manager in the workshop, responsible for processing the Decadent Tree leaves, and also doubles as a guard. I'm afraid there will be repercussions later..."

The woman's words were cut off by Luo Chen.

"Don't worry."

Just four words, yet they brought Jing Lan an immense sense of security, as if whatever he said would come true.

She put on her hat, lowered her head, and followed behind Luo Chen as they gradually left Qingshan Town.

Inside the tobacco workshop.

An old man with a somewhat sinister expression stood upstairs, still staring in the direction Luo Chen and Jing Lan had left.

He beckoned, and soon someone hurried upstairs.

"Go and investigate that man!"

...

After the trip to Qingshan Town, Luo Chen and Jing Lan's lives remained unchanged.

It was still the same as before: waking up early to farm, resting at noon, and doing some simple farm work in the cool of the evening.

Although Luo Chen's body was still thin, his condition improved day by day.

Perhaps because they had just harvested new rice, Jing Lan had been cooking quite well lately, at least not mainly thin porridge like at the beginning.

Because of the improved diet, coupled with deliberate grooming, Jing Lan's complexion became more and more rosy, and her temperament had also changed considerably compared to before.

In the village, everyone said it was because she now had a man.

But in reality, that man didn't linger on her deliberately made-up face for even half a moment. Instead, he often stared blankly at the vast sky, lost in thought.

Jing Lan didn't know why, and could only nurse her secret sorrow.

One day, in the evening, Jing Lan, who was sewing new clothes inside the house, vaguely heard footsteps outside.

She hurried out, and was stunned at the first glance.

"Manager Yan, why are you here?"

Yan, the manager, with his usually sinister face, today appeared somewhat amiable.

"Xiao Lan, where is your man?"

Jing Lan hesitated for a moment, then said, "He went to the back mountain to fetch water."

The well the village used for drinking water was at the back mountain, which faced away from the sun. These days, it had been Luo Chen who went to fetch the water.

Hearing this, Manager Yan was somewhat surprised.

A person of that caliber, still doing such things?

"Then let's wait a while!"

Manager Yan waved his hand, and the group of servants who had followed him put down the things they had carried over, then went outside to wait.

Jing Lan was puzzled and asked worriedly, "Manager Yan, you're not here about Lai Er, are you?"

Manager Yan smiled slightly. "Don't worry. Lai Er died of a sudden illness a few days ago."

"Ah!" Jing Lan was stunned.

Just then, a figure walked over from the back mountain, two water buckets balanced on his shoulders, steady and without spilling a single drop.

When he returned home, he didn't seem surprised by Manager Yan's arrival. He calmly poured the water into the household vat.

Jing Lan followed behind him, wanting to speak but hesitating.

"Stay inside."

Luo Chen casually instructed, then walked out. He only glanced at the patiently waiting Manager Yan.

"You came later than I expected."
