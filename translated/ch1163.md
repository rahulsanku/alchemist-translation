# Chapter 1163: Dao Heart Shattered, Ignorance Poses as Bliss

Carefree City!

Foremost among the seventy-two cities, it lay at the very heart of the Great Cataclysm Realm.

From the very first moment he set foot inside, Luo Chen sensed a difference from the other districts.

It wasn't just the city walls, which lacked any defensive fortifications. It was also the relaxed expressions on the faces of the passersby—a stark contrast to the struggle of everyone else surviving in this world.

The word "carefree" seemed to settle upon every single person.

After entering the city, Luo Chen walked on foot, using his own two eyes to personally experience the customs and atmosphere of Carefree City.

When a man with flowing hair and beard, dressed in unrestrained green robes, passed by with his arm around a strikingly beautiful woman, Luo Chen's gaze followed him. A flicker of surprise crossed his face.

"Senior, what is it?"

Xiangfei was puzzled, then followed Luo Chen's gaze. Understanding dawned on her.

"That should be a cultivator."

With Xiangfei's confirmation, Luo Chen was certain his perception hadn't been mistaken.

That man's blood and qi were abundant enough, but there was no sign of any tempering or refinement.

And through qi sense, Luo Chen could clearly detect the "Adept core" within the man's qi sea!

It was rather interesting, actually. When cultivators couldn't use their divine sense to conceal themselves, martial artists could rely on qi-sensing methods to clearly detect another's cultivation level.

But one thing surprised Luo Chen.

"Without sensing his qi, how did you recognize him as a cultivator at a single glance?"

Xiangfei replied matter-of-factly, "In Carefree City, it's usually only cultivators who act like this. To put it nicely, they're unrestrained and free-spirited. To put it bluntly, they've let themselves go, wallowing in self-indulgence."

Luo Chen couldn't disagree with Xiangfei's description.

In his centuries of cultivation, he had indeed seen some cultivators who acted differently, unconcerned with others' gazes.

But someone parading down the main street with an ordinary woman in his arms like that? That was truly rare.

Only those low-level cultivators who knew they had no hope of breaking through, who had given up on cultivation and begun to enjoy life, would behave this way.

And Luo Chen had only ever seen such people occasionally in market towns during his own low-level days.

"Letting oneself go" was a rather apt description.

"This person…" Luo Chen had initially wanted to make their acquaintance, but after his gaze swept over another person staggering and drinking wine on a different street, he changed his words.

"Are there many such people in Carefree City?"

Luo Chen's tone was somewhat low, carrying a hint of sympathy for one's own kind.

Xiangfei was startled for a moment, then said seriously, "Not many, but there is indeed a group of them. Most live in Pingyang Lane, and their haunts are places like the Fallen Immortal Pavilion and the Drunken Immortal Residence."

"Are they all cultivators? Why are they like this?" Luo Chen murmured to himself.

Xiangfei explained, "Anyone with discerning eyes can tell at a glance that those who act like this are cultivators. Only they would behave this way. According to some seniors, these cultivators ended up in the Great Cataclysm Realm. The environmental restrictions prevent them from using their divine abilities and techniques, and the lack of spiritual qi robs them of any chance to cultivate normally. Over time, their mindsets changed. And because of their long lifespans and the abundant wealth they brought with them, they gradually began to indulge in pleasure and debauchery."

Speaking up to here, Xiangfei sighed.

"That soul-attacking technique I mentioned before—I traded a top-grade Decadent Immortal Smoke for it from a down-and-out high-level cultivator outside the Fallen Immortal Pavilion."

Even though Luo Chen had heard Xiangfei mention this before, he hadn't fully grasped the weight of it.

After all, no matter how expensive a batch of Decadent Immortal Smoke was, it was nothing compared to a secret technique capable of attacking the soul.

The fact that the Mountains and Seas Realm's cultivation system was so well-developed, yet soul-attacking secret techniques were still extremely rare, showed just how scarce and valuable such methods were.

So, just how down-and-out did a cultivator have to be to casually sell off such a trump card technique to a stranger on the street?

It wasn't until now, seeing these indulgent cultivators with his own eyes, that Luo Chen truly understood.

Their Dao hearts had shattered!

"Senior, let's go! I've already arranged lodgings on Yuelong Lane. There's a residence there that the martial artists from the Three Xiang Cities used to stay in when they visited Carefree City."

"Mm."

Arriving at Carefree City was already half a year later.

A month longer than planned, because that great fog had forced Luo Chen and his group to take a detour.

Along the way to Yuelong Lane, Luo Chen kept observing the city's customs, while Xiangfei supplemented his observations with explanations, occasionally bringing up some of Carefree City's systems.

This city was located at the very center of the Great Cataclysm Realm, making it the safest place.

Ordinary martial artists simply couldn't settle here.

Those who could permanently reside in Carefree City generally fell into three categories.

First, the city's natives, like Fang Zhibai. Among these natives, the Fang family, to which Fang Zhibai belonged, was the most noble, because the Carefree Realm Lord was the Fang family's founding ancestor!

Second, there were the top-tier martial artists invited by Carefree City every hundred years, along with their servants and families. However, high-level martial artists weren't commonly seen among these, as they spent most of their time cultivating on the Ninefold Tower.

The third type were the cultivators Luo Chen had just seen!

"Top-tier martial artists who are invited can settle in Carefree City for free, and the same goes for those cultivators. Although the two groups have some interaction, it's not deep. You can tell just from their residences being on the north and south ends of the city."

"And there's a deep prejudice between them!"

"Cultivators look down on martial artists from the bottom of their hearts. Martial artists look down on cultivators because they can crush them in terms of pure strength."

"If not for the Carefree Realm Lord's absolute authority keeping the peace, the two sides would have clashed long ago."

"But this situation has become less of a problem as the number of cultivators dwindles."

"Senior, we're here!"

In front of an old residence, Xiangfei stopped her explanation.

Someone was already at the door to greet them, showing great respect to Xiangfei, while looking at Luo Chen with a mix of curiosity and awe.

Luo Chen's expression was somber. As he entered, he asked a question.

"When can I go to the Cataclysm Nine Rings?"

Xiangfei dismissed the servants and explained, "The Cataclysm Nine Rings are connected to the Ninefold Tower. The tower opens once every hundred years, and it's always easier to get in than to come out. Within a century, aside from severely injured returnees like myself and Lord Moya, very few people willingly come out. Now, there are still sixteen years until the next centennial opening. Even if you have the Carefree Token personally issued by the Carefree Envoy, you can't enter."

"Sixteen years."

Luo Chen silently repeated the number.

Calculating from the time Xiao Lin, the old city lord of Relic Immortal City, had left, it was indeed still about a decade or so off.

Fine, then!

He still had matters to attend to, so there was no rush to enter.

Once inside the residence, Luo Chen naturally took the main room with the best environment and the most space.

He didn't need to worry about other trivial matters. After settling in, he took out a large number of books and papers.

In the study.

Luo Chen looked at the papers covered in dense writing, his eyes full of confidence.

Since he planned to develop well on the martial path, summarizing and creating a set of techniques suited to his physique was essential.

He had done this before, creating a technique like *Myriad Path Convergence*.

However, *Myriad Path Convergence* had significant limitations. It focused heavily on tempering the physical body but was very mediocre in cultivating blood and qi.

Even later, when he supplemented it with profound methods like the *Hundred Poisons Body-Tempering Art* and *Three Heads Six Arms*, he hadn't broken free of this constraint.

This time, he intended to use *Divine Astral Fighting Qi* and *Demon Manifestation Art* as a foundation, integrating everything he had learned, centuries of accumulated knowledge, plus the martial arts knowledge of the Great Cataclysm Realm, to create a new divine martial art.

He had already started working on it during the journey.

Although the workload was enormous, he was quite confident.

And he was also very sure of the upper limit of this new technique.

He planned to invest all the achievement points he had hoarded on the attribute panel into it, to deduce as high-level a technique as possible.

For now, what he needed to do was, first, consolidate his Leakless Realm cultivation, and second, summarize the strengths and weaknesses of various schools of body refinement, so he could take the essence and discard the dross.

However!

After sitting in the study all afternoon, Luo Chen's mind couldn't settle.

Why was that?

As evening fell, Luo Chen chuckled softly and rose calmly.

Xiangfei happened to come over, asking what Luo Chen wanted for dinner, saying she would cook personally.

"I'm going out. Just handle dinner as you see fit."

Leaving behind this one sentence, Luo Chen left the residence alone.

Xiangfei knew very well that the only friend Luo Chen had in Carefree City was the Carefree Envoy, Fang Zhibai. But since he had just arrived and hadn't even sent a visiting card, he shouldn't be paying a direct visit.

So, where was he going so late?

...

It wasn't yet deep into the night, and Carefree City itself had no curfew.

Even though Luo Chen's face was unfamiliar, the deliberately revealed astral qi aura was enough to mark him as a top-tier martial artist.

Naturally, no one took the initiative to question him.

Leaving the residence, following Xiangfei's earlier directions, Luo Chen's figure appeared in a wide alley lined with civilian homes.

This place was called Pingyang Lane, the settlement area for most cultivators.

He walked along, spreading his qi sense to perceive his surroundings.

Suddenly.

A strange expression crossed Luo Chen's face, and his steps stopped outside a quiet, secluded little building.

He hesitated for a moment, then took the initiative to knock.

After a moment, the door swung open, and a young figure poked his head out to look.

A flicker of surprise passed through his eyes. When he sensed the astral qi Luo Chen was actively emitting, a look of undisguised disgust appeared on his face.

"A martial artist?"

Luo Chen's expression was calm. He simply said, "Daoist friend, care for a chat?"

As he spoke, he revealed his hand, on which a ring was worn.

The young man looked at it, then froze.

"A storage ring? You're also a cultivator?"

"Mm."

"But as far as I know, among the cultivators in Carefree City now, there has never been one as powerful a martial artist as you."

As he spoke, the young man had already opened the door wide, making a gesture of invitation.

Clearly, the shared identity of being a cultivator had dispelled the other's disgust and wariness.

Luo Chen walked in and stopped at a pavilion.

Without paying attention to the confusion on the other's face, he first introduced himself.

"My name is Luo Chen. I was... also a cultivator before, having cultivated to the Ascendant stage."

The young man was startled, then eagerly introduced himself, "This junior is Chi Mo, at the Adept Great Perfection stage."

The two looked at each other. Though their realms had once been different, they now shared the same rueful sigh.

Luo Chen let out a long breath and said unhurriedly, "I could sense your cultivation level. It was precisely because I sensed you trying to cultivate as I passed by outside that I took the liberty of visiting."

Chi Mo smiled bitterly. "Is cultivating qi-refinement methods here so conspicuous?"

Luo Chen nodded. "Were you using a high-grade spirit stone to cultivate?"

At this point, faced with this situation, there was no need to hide anything.

Chi Mo sat down on a chair, his expression pained. "That's right, junior. I stumbled into a certain outer realm battlefield. Although I chanced upon a great opportunity, I was unfortunately swept here. These high-grade spirit stones were part of that opportunity."

Facing Luo Chen, for some reason, Chi Mo's words suddenly flowed freely.

As if guided, or perhaps because his heart was full of pent-up frustration he had long wanted to vent, Chi Mo recounted his origins one by one.

He came from a cultivation nation called the Great Yu Dynasty.

Resources there were scarce, and advancement was difficult.

But the geographical environment was quite special. Near the Great Yu Dynasty were scattered independent spatial nodes, rumored to be ancient battlefields left over from the ancient war between humans and demons.

Later, after exploration and development by high-level cultivators, they gradually formed special locations known as "outer realm battlefields."

Any cultivator who aspired to longevity would, after achieving some success in their cultivation, go and explore these outer realm battlefields, hoping to gain a great opportunity to reach a higher realm.

Chi Mo was such a person.

And he had indeed succeeded. He had obtained a legacy and inheritance from the meditation site of an ancient cultivator.

But as he was leaving, he was unfortunately caught in a spatial rift and inexplicably ended up in the Great Cataclysm Realm.

Naturally, he was at a loss in this situation.

After living in Carefree City for a while and witnessing the depravity of the cultivators around him, he couldn't agree with it.

Especially his Adept Great Perfection cultivation level, which gave him an intense obsession and urge to break through to the Sage stage, which was so close yet so far.

Therefore, even knowing that the Great Cataclysm Realm was unsuitable for cultivators, he still tried his best to cultivate.

And he hoped that after reaching the Sage stage, he could try to leave the Great Cataclysm Realm.

Only...

The efficiency was too low!

The spiritual qi from a single high-grade spirit stone, when released here, was less effective than an ordinary low-grade spirit stone back in the Great Yu Dynasty.

As soon as it was released, most of the qi would dissipate.

And he couldn't fully release his divine sense, lest it attract the Soul-Shaking Thunder Sound and damage his soul origin.

The conversation unconsciously stretched into the deep of the night.

"Sorry, I've made a fool of myself before you, Senior." Chi Mo said, as if relieved.

Luo Chen waved his hand. "With your talent of forming your core at a hundred and twenty, and your Dao heart that refuses to give up even in adversity, if you were outside, breaking through to the Sage stage would surely be a matter of course."

Chi Mo sighed. "Yes, I've worked so hard, and my preparations were thorough. Now, others tell me that breakthrough is hopeless. I either have to scatter my Dao fruit and switch to the martial path, or wallow in self-indulgence, using the long lifespan I earned through bitter cultivation to enjoy life. Neither option can I accept."

Luo Chen asked, "Do you know that even if you break through to the Sage stage, you still can't leave the Great Cataclysm Realm?"

"I know that, of course. But I always want to try. And even if the Sage stage isn't enough, what about the Ascendant stage? The Void Refinement..." As he spoke, Chi Mo suddenly stopped, because the senior before him had once been at the Ascendant stage in the immortal path! An expression of despair crept onto his face. "Could it be that even someone as strong as you, Senior, can't leave?"

Luo Chen stood up and patted him on the shoulder.

"If you can, it's better to scatter your Adept core, convert it into blood and qi, and try the martial path!"

With that, Luo Chen left.

Behind him, Chi Mo's expression shifted between light and dark, still showing signs of struggle.

...

Leaving Pingyang Lane, where the cultivators lived, Luo Chen turned towards Carefree City's bustling main streets.

At the place called the Fallen Immortal Pavilion—truly a "Fallen Immortal" pavilion—the scent of Decadent Immortal Smoke was intoxicating. Cultivators who had once achieved success in the immortal path reveled in the smoke, enjoying the comfort of their souls, immersed in one illusion after another.

The Drunken Immortal Residence was brightly lit all night long. Fine wines and delicacies, once mere pastimes, had now become the best way for cultivators to pass the time.

And pawnshops lined the long streets.

Whenever a cultivator took out medicinal herbs or pills to pawn, someone would naturally offer up large amounts of gold and silver.

Various immortal techniques and methods, once closely guarded secrets, could no longer fetch high prices.

Through Xiangfei's introduction and Chi Mo's confessions, Luo Chen had come to understand that this vast Carefree City had a complete industrial chain, slowly squeezing out the wealth of these cultivators.

Of course, there were also those with firm Dao hearts, like Chi Mo, who still tried to cultivate, but they struggled immensely.

He had heard that there were also those, like himself, who had switched to the martial path, but their progress was slow, and very few could break into the Cataclysm Nine Rings.

At the break of dawn.

Luo Chen returned to Yuelong Lane in the morning dew.

Thinking about the different groups of people in the two lanes, Luo Chen's heart was filled with complex emotions.

Some were unfortunate, like a tiger brought low, while others tried to leap over the dragon's gate. Everyone struggled to survive in this place of return to nothingness.

Carefree?

It was nothing but ignorance!

And what about himself?

In the small alley, Xiangfei stood leaning against the doorframe. Seeing Luo Chen, she waved from afar.

"Senior, you're back!"
