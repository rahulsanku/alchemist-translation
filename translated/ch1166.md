# Chapter 1166: Human Immortal Gu Xun, Spirit Pill Reappears

Nine Smoke Tower.

One of the most important external enterprises of the Fang clan, the premier family of Carefree City.

Its main business was collecting rare treasures, secret arts, and cultivation techniques.

Occasionally, it also hosted high-standard trading conferences.

In recent days, the number of guests entering and leaving Nine Smoke Tower had increased more than tenfold.

This left the steward in charge grinning from ear to ear.

And everyone was well aware of the reason for this change.

A century had passed, and the Ninefold Tower had opened!

A large number of high-level martial artists who had been cultivating bitterly within took this opportunity to come out, either to rest, recuperate, or purchase resources to prepare for their next entry into the Ninefold Immortal Tower for cultivation.

These high-level martial artists who came out also brought back some resources completely different from those of the Great Destruction Realm.

Among them were some ordinary resources they didn't care for. For convenience, they basically handed them over to Nine Smoke Tower for processing.

That was why so many ordinary guests were coming and going from Nine Smoke Tower.

Steward Fang tapped his abacus on the counter, unable to hide the joy on his face. The daily earnings recently were enough to match a month or even several months in the past.

Once this year passed, he could submit a perfect report to the clan and then retire happily.

"Steward, a distinguished guest has arrived. You may need to receive them personally."

A maidservant came to report, interrupting Steward Fang's fantasies.

He frowned slightly. "What distinguished guest requires me to personally receive them? If it's those newly arrived Unblemished realm martial artists who want to participate in the trading conference, can't you just lead them up directly?"

The maidservant said cautiously, "This one is different."

"What's so different? I'd like to see for myself."

Though Steward Fang said this, his expression had already turned serious. Before going out, he even tidied his clothes.

When he saw the Carefree Token that Xiangfei presented, the disdain in his heart vanished completely.

"This is the highest-level golden token within the clan!"

Luo Chen didn't know that Carefree Tokens had grades, but since the grade was higher, that was naturally better.

"I heard that Nine Smoke Tower regularly holds trading conferences among high-level martial artists throughout the year. May I use this token to enter?"

Steward Fang respectfully returned the golden Carefree Token.

"Senior naturally has the qualifications. This way, please!"

Luo Chen exchanged a smile with Xiangfei and followed Steward Fang toward the trading conference venue.

Unexpectedly, the venue wasn't upstairs but went downward instead, growing more spacious as they walked.

"Have you been here before?"

As they walked, Luo Chen sent a sound transmission.

Xiangfei sighed. "I've heard of it, but never came. The specifications of this level of trading conference are too high. One needs to reach a certain spending threshold at Nine Smoke Tower to be actively invited. Back then, to save cultivation resources, I naturally wouldn't waste money like that. But precisely because of insufficient preparation, I didn't understand the Destruction Nine Rings well enough and ended up returning severely injured."

Luo Chen toyed with the golden Carefree Token in his hand, developing some goodwill toward the Carefree Realm Lord he had never met.

The distribution of Carefree Tokens was specifically handled by special envoys like Fang Zhibai.

But the establishment of standards was certainly set by the Carefree Realm Lord.

He had opened wide the door of convenience for martial artists with higher aptitude.

This made Luo Chen even more inclined to believe what Fang Zhibai had said—that the Carefree Realm Lord wanted to discuss the Great Dao with him.

The Guixu was too strange, and the future of eventual destruction was utterly despairing.

Under such circumstances, unscrupulous infighting was nothing but a tremendous internal drain.

Only a strong-willed figure with sufficient breadth of mind, like the Carefree Realm Lord, could gather the geniuses and experts of the seventy-two cities together, to progress together and jointly study how to leave Guixu.

A bronze door stood before them.

Before pushing it open, Steward Fang turned back and bowed respectfully.

"Senior, your seat is arranged on the upper level. Do you need a mask to conceal your face?"

"No need."

"Then please enter!"

The door swung open, and Luo Chen stepped inside.

Instantly, countless gazes shot over, landing squarely on Luo Chen.

These looks carried curious appraisal, unfamiliar wariness, and suspicious speculation.

All of this seemed to weigh a thousand jun.

Luo Chen paid them no heed and walked along the passage upward.

Xiangfei, following behind him, was somewhat uncomfortable.

"Oh? City Lord Sanxiang, you've returned?"

A surprised voice rang out.

Xiangfei turned to look and greeted, "City Lord Zixia, long time no see."

This was not a place for catching up. After greeting, Xiangfei quickly followed Luo Chen to the upper level and chose one of the few seats there.

City Lord Zixia was a rather beautiful female martial artist, dressed in purple attire, elegant and graceful.

Her gaze followed Xiangfei, and gradually, shock appeared on her face.

Or rather, she had been shocked from the very beginning.

Shocked that Xiangfei, decades ago, had been forced to withdraw from the Ninefold Immortal Tower because she couldn't endure the cultivation environment within the Destruction Nine Rings.

Such a withdrawal meant regression, and also meant she was not far from death.

Yet unexpectedly, upon emerging from seclusion this time, she could see Xiangfei return to Carefree City.

But what shocked City Lord Zixia the most was where the two of them sat.

Above her, at the highest point of the circular hall!

There, only two kinds of people could sit.

One kind were those who cultivated in the Upper Three Rings.

The other kind were those favored by the Carefree Realm Lord, with future prospects of entering the Upper Three Rings for cultivation.

To put it more intuitively, there were currently only four people cultivating in the Upper Three Rings.

Aside from the Carefree Realm Lord himself, the remaining three were all Martial Dao Human Immortals who had broken through the Fifth Realm.

In other words, the young man Xiangfei was following was favored by the Carefree Realm Lord, with a future chance to step into the Human Immortal realm?

In truth, this shock wasn't limited to City Lord Zixia's face.

Any martial artist who saw Luo Chen sitting in the upper seats could hardly hide their shock, and they couldn't help whispering among themselves.

"Who is that person?"

"So young, yet with such cultivation—could he be a rising star from the Fang clan?"

"Doesn't seem like it. Didn't you see the Sanxiang City Lord Xiangfei accompanying him?"

Unfortunately, many didn't recognize Luo Chen, couldn't even make any connection.

These people had been bitterly cultivating in the Destruction Nine Rings for a hundred years and had only just come out. How could they know what had happened in the outside world, or which rising stars had appeared?

But!

A newly arrived Unblemished martial artist who had been invited this year looked half-doubtfully at Xiangfei's respectful posture and vaguely guessed Luo Chen's identity.

He explained to his elder.

Then, this information spread quietly without anyone noticing.

"The hidden master of Relic Immortal City, who at the Furnace realm killed five city lords including Xilong and Mofeng, and enslaved the Sanxiang City Lord for decades."

"Over the decades, Relic Immortal City has conducted foreign trade with extremely tough methods, devouring massive resources to supply his cultivation."

"Now, this person has cultivated to the Unblemished realm."

"His name is Luo Chen!"

When this news spread and was confirmed, everyone's occasional stolen glances at Luo Chen changed.

No wonder it was said that the number of martial artists qualified to enter the Ninefold Immortal Tower this session was nearly one-fifth less than previous sessions!

If Luo Chen had occupied the resources of over a dozen cities, affecting the normal cultivation of rising talents, then that made sense.

And because of Luo Chen's formidable battle record, it was enough to convince people he was qualified to sit there.

At the Furnace realm, he could fight above his level, and against six opponents at that!

Such a heaven-defying figure would naturally be entrusted with high hopes by the Carefree Realm Lord.

Although Luo Chen couldn't hear the discussions below, he could infer a thing or two from observing their expressions.

They were nothing more than discussing his past deeds!

Since he hadn't concealed his face, he had long been prepared for this.

Moreover, acting this way had its benefits.

When he actually entered the Ninefold Immortal Tower and wanted to seize good cultivation stones in the Destruction Nine Rings, it would save him some unnecessary trouble.

Suddenly!

A stir arose within the venue.

Luo Chen followed the sound and saw an old man in green robes walking in through the bronze door.

Immediately after, voices rose one after another, greeting him.

"Senior Gu, long time no see."

"Senior Gu, hello!"

"Senior Gu, you're still as impressive as ever!"

Luo Chen stared intently at the man, vaguely able to see some chaotic aura lingering on him.

"Who is he?"

Xiangfei hesitated and said, "He should be Senior Gu Xun, one of the three Martial Dao Human Immortals. He's also the most special among those three seniors. He doesn't always cultivate in the Upper Three Rings; occasionally he enters the lower six rings, so many people recognize him."

"Is that so…"

Luo Chen murmured to himself, then somewhat impolitely dispersed his aura and actively probed.

He wanted to know, what exactly was a Martial Dao Human Immortal!

The moment their auras connected, Luo Chen's entire body trembled.

At that instant, he seemed to see a black hole, devouring all perception from the inside out.

That black hole was constantly expanding, merely standing there gave one the feeling it would burst the entire venue space.

This was the Martial Dao Human Immortal, who was incompatible with heaven and earth, and thus needed to shatter the void and depart?

Luo Chen steadied his mind and forcibly withdrew his aura perception.

"Oh?"

Gu Xun seemed to sense something and looked up.

When he saw Luo Chen's position, he couldn't help but reveal a hint of a smile.

Ignoring the gazes of others around him, he came to the seat adjacent to Luo Chen.

"I am Gu Xun. May I ask your esteemed name, Daoist?"

Daoist?

This form of address, it had been a long time since anyone had used it with him.

Harboring certain hopes, Luo Chen saluted and said, "Luo Chen, greetings to you, Daoist!"

Gu Xun smiled, seeming to understand the hope in Luo Chen's tone. "Unfortunately, I am not an immortal cultivator. I'm afraid I've disappointed Fellow Daoist Luo."

Luo Chen was indeed disappointed.

After seeing the degeneration of many immortal cultivators in Carefree City, experiencing Chi Mo's fall, and being unable to meet the Carefree Realm Lord in the short term, he urgently wanted to find a like-minded person.

Especially a former immortal cultivator who had cultivated to a high martial realm.

But Gu Xun was not.

However, in an instant, Luo Chen put away this disappointment.

For an immortal cultivator to switch to martial cultivation was easy at the start, often guaranteeing at least the Blood River realm. But to cultivate to a higher realm—how could that be easy?

After all, these were two paths that went in completely opposite directions!

Luo Chen and Gu Xun did not talk much, because the trading conference began.

The person hosting this trading conference was also an old acquaintance of his—Fang Zhibai!

To be honest, when he saw Luo Chen and Gu Xun sitting next to each other, Fang Zhibai was somewhat surprised.

He had come to host this trading conference, first to represent the Fang clan and keep order, and second to pave the way for his own upcoming entry into the Ninefold Immortal Tower.

By appearing so openly, everyone would recognize him, at least know his face.

Then, in the Destruction Nine Rings, he could save himself some trouble.

And for Luo Chen to be so proactive, without even concealing his face while sitting in that high seat, was somewhat unusual.

Wasn't he afraid of incurring everyone's jealousy?

Not everyone would respect and fear someone with outstanding potential and talent.

On the contrary, the Great Destruction Realm had no shortage of martial maniacs and martial lunatics. After learning of Luo Chen's potential and possible future, someone might actively seek trouble with him to prove their own Dao!

"Forget it!"

Fang Zhibai muttered to himself, then said loudly:

"It seems almost everyone has arrived. I declare the trading conference officially begins. Let me, Fang Zhibai, represent the Fang clan and cast a brick to attract jade!"

"This is a Blood Dragon Ginseng, containing abundant blood qi, enough for a Furnace realm martial artist to break through to the Unblemished realm. Most importantly, the ginseng qi within the Blood Dragon Ginseng is quite gentle. After slight transformation, it can slowly nourish damaged meridians, having miraculous effects for Unblemished realm martial artists."

"The starting price is thirty thousand golden beads."

As he spoke, he took out a blood-red ginseng that resembled a coiled dragon.

As soon as this item appeared, it immediately drew a series of sharp breaths.

Breaking through to the Unblemished realm was of little use to the experts present. Moreover, once they decided to enter the Ninefold Immortal Tower, they often had thin ties with their blood descendants, so naturally they wouldn't consider their juniors' advancement.

But nourishing meridians—that made many people's eyes red.

The cultivation environment of the Destruction Nine Rings was too harsh. Most martial artists who cultivated inside carried hidden injuries.

Especially the meridians that blood qi must pass through!

Even though thirty thousand golden beads was a high price, after Fang Zhibai finished speaking, a steady stream of bids came in.

In the end, this Blood Dragon Ginseng was purchased by City Lord Zixia for one hundred and twenty thousand golden beads.

Four times the starting price!

This was definitely a premium!

Luo Chen watched thoughtfully.

Over the years, although he had never personally handled financial matters, leaving everything to Jing Lan and Xiangfei, he had a simple understanding of the currency system and purchasing power in the Great Destruction Realm.

The highest-level currency was a special and scarce golden ore, often hidden in the depths of earth veins in the shape of round beads.

A thousand golden beads could purchase enough resources for a Blood River realm martial artist to cultivate normally to the Furnace realm.

And ten thousand golden beads was a huge sum. Even Furnace realm martial artists rarely had so much cash on hand.

A starting auction price of thirty thousand was very high.

But for Unblemished realm martial artists, it wasn't much.

What Fang Zhibai said about casting a brick to attract jade wasn't false.

But a fourfold premium was still somewhat exaggerated.

From this transaction, what Luo Chen truly saw was the scarcity and longing of martial artists for medicinal herbs that could repair meridians.

If that was the case, then what he planned to do next would be much simpler.

However, Luo Chen was in no hurry. He watched a few more trades.

Although this trading conference started with Fang Zhibai auctioning the Blood Dragon Ginseng, the subsequent segment truly proceeded in the form of trades.

The resources privately hoarded by those martial artists didn't require ordinary gold or silver; they needed resources of equivalent specifications.

Especially those martial artists who had just come out of the Ninefold Immortal Tower. Occasionally, they would take out all sorts of strange things, sometimes unable to even explain their effects and functions themselves.

Luo Chen watched, gradually forming an understanding.

"Fellow Daoist Luo, you only watch and don't make a move?"

Beside him, Gu Xun suddenly asked.

Luo Chen smiled lightly. "Then let's do it now!"

As his words fell, he flicked his sleeve, and a pill the size of a dragon's eye flew out.

Above the pill, several golden cloud patterns coiled around it, looking extraordinary.

But overall, there was no leakage of aura at all, clearly indicating the medicinal power was condensed to the extreme.

"One fourth-grade upper-rank Meridian-Repairing Pill, capable of completely repairing damaged meridians in Unblemished realm martial artists."

The clear voice had barely fallen when the venue fell into a momentary silence.

Then came a rumbling vibration!
