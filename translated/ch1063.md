# Chapter 1063: The Alchemist Sect’s Grand Gesture, the Awakening Giant Beast

Little White and Black King had been traveling since leaving Green Parasol Mountain, crossing mountains and ridges, traversing wilderness and forests.

Without using any teleportation formations, it took them a full six months to return to the human-demon boundary line.

This boundary line was, in truth, rather vague.

At its center lay the ancient Gushan Mountain Range, the bridgehead from which humanity had once marched into the Million Mountains. To the south was the Aoxiao Mountain Range, once conquered but later retaken. To the west was the Jade Cauldron Domain.

The Jade Cauldron Domain, in particular, now encompassed an area far beyond its original scope.

It had become a ghost realm!

Within it were countless vengeful spirits and fierce ghosts, and a ferocious fourth-rank Ghost Emperor held court.

It was precisely because of this fourth-rank Ghost Emperor that the place had become a natural dividing line. Neither humans nor demons would easily cross it.

Little White paused at the outskirts of the Gushan Mountain Range, gazing down at the ruins below, sighing with emotion.

"What's wrong?" Black King asked, puzzled.

Little White said with feeling, "See that river?"

Black King snorted hot air from his nostrils. "I'm not blind. Of course I see that big river."

"The ruins beside it are Dahe Market. It's where my master and I cultivated in our humble beginnings."

Black King was stunned. Ever since he had refined the remnant soul of the desolate beast Stone Dragon two hundred years ago, his perception of spiritual qi and spiritual veins had become extremely sharp. Looking now, he was somewhat astonished.

"The spiritual qi is so thin here, and you say this is where your master got his start?"

Little White nodded slightly, a somewhat wistful smile on her face.

Back then, although her cultivation was low and her strength weak, she had been carefree and without worries.

Now, she had reached the Ghost Emperor realm with great power, but she was no longer as simple as she once was.

And she didn't know if this body condensed from ghostly qi would ever have the chance to attain the Dao and ascend.

"Let's go. First to the Joyous Union... oh, it should be called the Nine Spirit Domain now."

The Nine Spirit Domain was the territory of the Nine Spirit Sect.

In truth, when the Nine Spirit Sect's mountain gate was first established, the first choice had been the Medicine King Domain.

But a world-shaking battle had turned the Medicine King Domain upside down, shattering its spiritual veins. Even if they could slowly nurture them back over time, for the sake of the sect's disciples, Nine Spirit Primogenitor had moved the sect to what was once the Joyous Union Domain.

At least the spiritual veins of the Joyous Union Domain were relatively well-preserved.

Little White and Black King traveled in stealth, heading straight for the Nine Spirit Domain.

When they passed through the Jade Cauldron Ghost Realm, they used ghostly methods and easily crossed it.

Before long, they arrived at the Nine Spirit Sect's mountain gate.

At the gate, the two hesitated.

"Should we sneak in, or directly announce ourselves?"

No wonder they were uncertain. One was a ghost, the other a demon. Directly announcing themselves could easily cause a commotion.

But sneaking in would lack propriety.

Little White blinked. "Master has widely distributed discussion invitations, covering all the Nascent Soul upper sects of the Eastern Wilderness, seeking a grand spectacle. Since that's the case, let's just announce ourselves directly!"

She tapped Black King's head with her lotus foot.

"You've got a loud voice. You do it!"

Black King snorted, then slowly opened his mouth. A thunderous roar exploded forth.

"Under the Alchemist Sect, Black King comes to pay respects!"

The rumbling sound spread across the mountains, startling birds and beasts into trembling.

One after another, streaks of light flew up from within the Nine Spirit Sect, filled with killing intent.

Looking at those murderous cultivators, Black King was dumbfounded.

They had come for a friendly visit. Why were they being treated like this?

Two vast beams of light flew out from within the Nine Spirit Sect, landing simultaneously at the mountain gate.

A man and a woman. The man was mature and steady, the woman young and beautiful.

Both were early Nascent Soul cultivators!

As soon as they landed, their gazes fell upon the ghost and the demon, then gradually focused on Little White.

"You claim to be under the Alchemist Sect?"

"I am Little White, a ghost servant of the Alchemist Sect. This fool is called Black King, my master's spiritual pet." Little White smiled apologetically. "Black King's wild nature is hard to change. His commotion was a bit excessive just now. Please forgive us, fellow Daoists. May I ask how you two are called?"

The man and woman exchanged glances, quite shocked.

In their perception, both Little White and Black King exuded extremely powerful auras.

Even some mid-Nascent Soul cultivators would appear slightly inferior.

Such powerful beings were merely the Alchemist Sect's maidservant and spiritual pet?

The woman, who was slightly higher in cultivation, said seriously, "I am named Changxi."

The other middle-aged man, who seemed to have recently formed his Nascent Soul, also gave his name, Meng Ye.

"Since your master is the Alchemist Sect, and he is an old friend of our sect's Supreme Elder, please enter!" Changxi gestured invitingly, and even had her junior brother Meng Ye go ahead to inform the Supreme Elder, Nine Spirit Primogenitor.

Black King shrank his body, lying on Little White's shoulder, following Changxi as they slowly flew toward the highest mountain within the Nine Spirit Sect's mountain gate.

Along the way, they said nothing, letting Little White handle all interactions.

Little White spread her divine sense, briefly observing the situation in the Nine Spirit Sect, then couldn't help but praise.

"The Nine Spirit Sect was once in decline, but I didn't expect that in just over a hundred years, it has flourished again. Even two more Nascent Soul cultivators have appeared."

Changxi smiled. "I was personally taught by my senior brother. As for Junior Brother Meng Ye, he received your master's favor and managed to form his Nascent Soul by luck."

"Oh?" Little White was somewhat surprised, then seemed to think of something. "Nascent Soul Pills?"

Changxi nodded. "Yes. Back then, we purchased a Nascent Soul Pill personally refined by the Alchemist Sect from Grand Adept Taiyue of Mount Tai Sect, and that allowed us to nurture Junior Brother Meng Ye. Speaking of which, that batch of Nascent Soul Pills the Alchemist Sect produced had an exceptionally excellent effect. Throughout the Eastern Wilderness, human sects gained seven new Nascent Soul cultivators one after another. Everyone has benefited from the Alchemist Sect's kindness!"

Little White knew that batch of Nascent Soul Pills numbered around ten, but she hadn't expected seven people to successfully break through using them.

She was astonished. "Such a high success rate?"

Changxi smoothed her hair and praised, "Indeed, such a success rate shocked many people. Some even questioned it. But in our occasional gatherings of Nascent Soul cultivators, we confirmed that those seven had all succeeded by taking Nascent Soul Pills refined by the Alchemist Sect. The Alchemist Sect's alchemy truly stands unrivaled in the world!"

Hearing others praise her master, Little White couldn't help but smile, feeling pleased, as if sharing in the glory.

In truth, although Luo Chen's alchemy was superb, that batch of Nascent Soul Pills he had produced were mostly fourth-rank lower grade. Moreover, besides those he had refined himself, some were collected from the ruins of the Nether Origin Sect.

The reason for such a high success rate was partly the pills' effectiveness, but also because the Golden Core cultivators preparing to form their Nascent Souls had deep foundations. After all, at their age, those Golden Core cultivators preparing for Nascent Soul were the elite of the elite who had survived the two-hundred-year human-demon war. With the full support of their sects, how could they not soar?

As the two conversed, they arrived at Nine Yuan Peak.

Meng Ye, whom they had seen earlier, was now standing respectfully behind a man in ornate robes with a domineering aura.

It was Nine Spirit Primogenitor!

He looked at Little White, and at the black dragon on her shoulder who had shrunk his size.

"Black King, long time no see."

Black King chuckled. "Nine Spirit, I didn't expect you to still remember me!"

Nine Spirit Primogenitor shook his head with a light laugh. Although he and Black King had little interaction, he had frequently visited Bai Li Qingchuan back then and had seen him.

Then Nine Spirit Primogenitor's gaze fell on Little White's face.

"What business does your master have? Why doesn't he come himself, but sends you instead?"

Little White spoke without hesitation, taking things out as she talked.

"Master has important matters and cannot come. But before long, you should be able to meet."

A large black urn was produced, along with an invitation.

Nine Spirit Primogenitor casually handed the black urn to Changxi and took the invitation.

"Shouyang Mountain Discussion?"

Nine Spirit Primogenitor raised an eyebrow, carefully reading the invitation's contents. When he saw the true information, his breathing became uncontrollably rapid for a moment.

A surprised cry came from beside him.

Nine Spirit Primogenitor suppressed the shock in his heart. "What's wrong?"

Changxi pointed at the black urn.

This large urn was the Nine Spirit Sect's inherited true artifact, specially used to collect the Nine Heavens Xiao Qi, assisting the sect's Nascent Soul cultivators in cultivating powerful killing techniques.

A hundred years ago, Luo Chen had borrowed it.

As a result, Meng Ye, who had newly advanced to Nascent Soul in the past century, had no chance to cultivate the sect's signature killing move.

Now Luo Chen had returned it, which was only right.

But what surprised Changxi was that it was filled with Xiao Qi. A rough count put it at over a thousand strands.

Although it lacked the three types—Red Firmament Qi, Purple Firmament Qi, and Crimson Firmament Qi—the quantity of the other six types was still astonishing.

Over a thousand strands—this was an amount that an ordinary Nascent Soul cultivator could hardly gather in a lifetime.

And its value was immeasurable!

Nine Spirit Primogenitor was naturally also shocked by Luo Chen's grand gesture.

Taking a deep breath, he sighed, "This is too precious. Brother Luo is too generous."

Little White said with neither humility nor arrogance, "This is my master's token of appreciation for borrowing your sect's true artifact to cultivate his sword technique. Please do not refuse. Also, regarding the Shouyang Mountain Discussion Conference six years from now, we hope you will attend."

Nine Spirit Primogenitor nodded solemnly. "Reply to your master: I will definitely not be absent!"

Little White smiled. The first step of their mission was successfully completed.

She and Black King declined Nine Spirit Primogenitor's invitation to stay and hurriedly left Nine Yuan Peak.

Nine Spirit Primogenitor watched their retreating figures, his ears still seeming to catch their bickering—something about not making such a big commotion next time they visited.

He tightly gripped the invitation, his expression somewhat dazed.

Beside him, Meng Ye thought the Supreme Elder was simply shocked by the Alchemist Sect Luo Chen's grand gesture. But Changxi, who knew Nine Spirit Primogenitor better—having been both his student and junior sister—understood it couldn't be that simple.

"Senior Brother, what's wrong?"

Nine Spirit Primogenitor said nothing, simply handing the invitation to the two of them.

Changxi and Meng Ye exchanged glances, then looked at the invitation's contents together.

With just one look, an even more shocked voice than when they had seen the thousand strands of Nine Heavens Xiao Qi burst forth.

"An opportunity for Ascension?!"

Nine Spirit Primogenitor exhaled a turbid breath. "The Alchemist Sect's Luo Chen is always so unexpected, always full of surprises!"

...

The majestic Dao palace stood with its skeletal frame exposed.

Among the stark white bones, snakes and pythons slithered, reeking of filth.

Luo Chen, having changed back into his black Dao robe, teleported from Green Parasol Mountain and stepped out of the Whitebone Dao Palace. As far as the eye could see, the mountains and fields were clad in silver, all covered under a blanket of snow.

He stamped his foot on the thick frozen ground, raising an eyebrow slightly.

This fourth-rank spiritual vein occupied by the Whitebone Mystic Serpent was truly extraordinary!

No wonder Canglong had chosen this place to undergo his tribulation when he first attempted to break through to the Nascent Soul stage.

Thinking of those past events, Luo Chen felt a momentary daze.

The Great Snowy Mountains!

Back then, he had fought for his life here and obtained the opportunity to form his Golden Core.

Now, as he was about to leave this place, he was heading toward his next battle, an opportunity to break through to Ascension.

"I hope this journey goes smoothly!"

Murmuring to himself, Luo Chen summoned a patch of white clouds. With a sprinkle of his magic power, it turned into a red cloud, and he stepped onto it as if walking on level ground.

The red cloud drifted, gradually moving away from the Great Snowy Mountains.

This journey was neither far nor long. There were still several years, so Luo Chen was in no hurry. He didn't use the mana-draining Voidburst Fire Escape, only traveling at normal flying speed.

Leaving the Great Snowy Mountains, the adjacent area was the Jade Cauldron Domain.

This place had long become a ghost realm.

Luo Chen's appearance was completely undisguised.

The vast ghost realm instantly boiled. A Ghost Emperor suddenly awakened, leading thousands of Ghost Kings and Ghost Generals surging toward Luo Chen.

Luo Chen did not retreat or evade in the slightest.

Since leaving the Great Snowy Mountains, the path ahead of him contained only friends and enemies.

Friends would help him continue forward, and as for enemies blocking his way, he would suppress and tear them apart without mercy.

Facing the thousands of vengeful spirits and fierce ghosts, Luo Chen merely cast a glance, and it intimidated them all.

Then he took out the Soul-Refining Banner and collected them all, including that Ghost Emperor.

The Jade Cauldron Ghost Realm, which had straddled the human-demon boundary line and caused chaos for three hundred years, was cleared in an instant.

Even if a few scattered first-rank little ghosts remained, they would never amount to anything in the future.

Luo Chen did not linger. Crossing the Jade Cauldron Domain, he headed west.

Passing through Tianfan, Five Elements, and Divine Talisman—three of what were once the Extreme East Six Domains—he spread his divine sense, briefly observing the situation in various major domains of the Eastern Wilderness.

These three domains, once prosperous and flourishing, now had not a single Nascent Soul upper sect remaining. They had all become gathering places for small powers and rogue cultivators.

From this, it was clear that the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world had indeed declined greatly.

But after crossing the Extreme East Six Domains, the situation improved considerably.

Within Firework Ancient Isle, although there was no Nascent Soul sect, when Luo Chen's divine sense swept over it, he was startled to discover a fourth-rank spiritual vein where an unfamiliar Nascent Soul cultivator was quietly cultivating in seclusion.

There was no sect insignia, and the magical power aura he emitted didn't resemble any Eastern Wilderness school Luo Chen knew.

Curious, Luo Chen took a closer look at the man. He wore a simple blue robe, his expression was calm, and his only distinctive feature might be his pair of frost-and-snow-like eyebrows.

"A rogue cultivator?"

Luo Chen marveled. He hadn't expected that the Eastern Wilderness of today could produce a Nascent Soul rogue cultivator.

After his admiration, he also found it reasonable.

A two-hundred-year human-demon war had seen too many powerful beings fall, countless great sects vanish like smoke. It was brutal, but a vast amount of resources had been freed up.

And among rogue cultivators, there had always been those with unyielding will.

Seizing the opportunity and rising with the tide was only natural.

Luo Chen did not stay long, leaving hastily.

Not long after he left, the man hidden in the ancient isle shook his frost-and-snow eyebrows and slowly opened his eyes.

His gaze was filled with utter shock.

"Since I managed to form my Nascent Soul by luck, my divine sense has far surpassed those of the same rank. Even if I can't compare to late Nascent Soul grand cultivators, it's enough to conceal myself and avoid detection by outsiders."

"But how could the Eastern Wilderness have such an existence? He secretly observed me for a long time, and I had no feeling at all."

"If it weren't for my innate perception of danger, he might have watched me for ages without my noticing."

"Who exactly is that person?"

...

Leaving Firework Ancient Isle, the next area was what was once Motian Cliff, later called the Luo Tian Domain when occupied by the Luo Tian Sect.

But now, it was said to have been renamed.

Called the Qingdan Domain!

Luo Chen paused for a long time before leisurely departing.

The reason for his pause was only one.

On Motian Cliff, the female cultivator occupying the fourth-rank spiritual vein for cultivation was an old acquaintance of his.

Tao Wan!

The former Qingdan Valley had been one of the top Golden Core great sects in the Jade Cauldron Domain, once even attempting to nurture a fourth-rank spiritual vein and refine fourth-rank Nascent Soul Pills to cultivate a Nascent Soul cultivator.

But its subsequent development had been a steady decline.

The Jade Cauldron Domain was destroyed, Qingdan Valley became displaced. Under the persistent efforts of Sect Leader Tao Wan, they barely survived the human-demon war and eventually settled in the Luo Tian Domain.

This was not without the protection of the Luo Tian Sect.

After all, Luo Chen and Tao Wan had once shared a friendship from their old days.

But Tao Wan's Nascent Soul formation was somewhat unexpected to Luo Chen.

After observing carefully for a while, Luo Chen suddenly understood.

"She succeeded in forming her Nascent Soul in recent years by taking the Nascent Soul Pill I refined!"

Tao Wan had not formed her Nascent Soul long ago and was still in a state of consolidating her cultivation, so from time to time, she emitted mixed and unstable magical power fluctuations.

Within them was the aura of Luo Chen's refined Nascent Soul Pill.

Realizing this, Luo Chen couldn't help but smile lightly.

He had obtained the Nascent Soul Pill formula from Qingdan Valley, refined the Nascentous Soul Pill, and successfully broken through to the Nascent Soul stage himself.

That later batch of Nascent Soul Pills had used the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's formula.

Now Tao Wan had relied on the Nascent Soul Pill he refined to successfully break through. Such a cycle of cause and effect could be considered a coincidence of fate.

Luo Chen did not visit her, nor did he stay long.

Before long, they would meet anyway.

And now, he had only one destination!

Crossing Tai Lake, passing through Pingshan Sea, Luo Chen's feet finally stepped onto the soil of the Bikong Domain, a place where living beings found it difficult to approach.

Here, golden light filled the air, and the sounds of slaughter echoed faintly.

Chaotic origin qi of heaven and earth scoured the bodies of any living thing daring to approach.

Luo Chen felt nothing, advancing step by step.

Compared to his first visit here, when he had hesitated and retreated, and his second, when he had advanced with difficulty, this third time he traveled unimpeded all the way to his destination.

A giant beast lay within the earth.

Its fur stood erect like swords and blades. Its sleeping roar was like thunder.

As Luo Chen approached, the giant tiger's eyelids trembled continuously, as if it might wake at any moment.

Luo Chen smiled slightly, slowly extending his right hand toward the giant tiger.

"It's time to wake up, Emperor Slayer!"

(Chapter End)
