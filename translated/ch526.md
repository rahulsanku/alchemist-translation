# Chapter 526: I Am Azure Yang

---

Within his qi sea, true fire blazed.

Azure-red light nearly filled the entire qi sea.

In this state, it seemed full of vigorous life, as though all things were racing to flourish.

His Golden Core and that pair of fire-attribute Broken Moon Wings were like fish in water within this environment of abundant vitality.

The Broken Moon Wings transformed into flowing moons, constantly flying and circling.

The crimson Golden Core spun faster and faster of its own accord, greedily trying to absorb the immense life force contained within the azure flame.

The azure flame seemed almost spiritual. It was not particularly willing to share the life force refined from burning more than ten thousand sea serpents with the Golden Core and Broken Moon Wings.

But since they were within it, some of that life force was inevitably divided away.

Of course, the vast majority remained with the azure flame itself.

Vaguely, it seemed to be transforming in some direction.

Luo Chen closed his eyes and sensed the changes within his qi sea. That faint unwillingness from the azure flame was caught by him in an instant.

"This is... spirituality?"

Surprise appeared on Luo Chen's face.

The world possessed strange fires. Most were born in extraordinary and dangerous places, and once they took shape, they could exist apart from their source.

For that reason, the world's strange fires were often called sourceless fires.

When a cultivator refined one into true fire, the sourceless fire gained a vessel and a root. Thus, it became source true fire.

But sourceless fires were as numerous as the stars, and they were not all powerful or unique.

They were merely a different sort of cultivator, like plants becoming spirits or stubborn stones giving birth to souls.

At the beginning, they basically possessed no intelligence and acted purely on instinct.

Moreover, such beings were unlike demon beasts evolved from wild animals. Demon beasts could give birth to intelligence at low realms and actively seek transformation.

According to legend, for a sourceless fire to develop intelligence, it had to advance at least to the fifth tier before a tiny bit of spirituality could be born.

Luo Chen's azure flame had come from the Yan Alliance.

At first, it had only been third tier.

For three hundred years, the Yan Alliance had nourished it with the Incense Burning Spirit-Seizing Formation, which had been modified from the Heavenly Craft Spirit-Seizing Formation. From time to time, they tossed in all manner of wood and demon beast blood. Only after long nurturing had its grade been pushed to the fourth tier.

And that was basically the limit of this flame.

Its potential went only that far.

But after Luo Chen refined it, he discovered that it had unknowingly inherited the habit of incense burning and spirit-seizing.

Thus, after wantonly burning and refining living creatures, it could actively plunder the life force contained within those beings.

Most of that life force was absorbed by the azure flame itself. A smaller portion blessed Luo Chen's physical body, even increasing his lifespan.

Even so, the flame's upper limit had still seemed obvious at a glance.

At most, it would reach the extreme of the fourth tier. It could not break through to the fifth.

But now, it faintly possessed a trace of spirituality. That was no small matter.

Spirituality was the mark of a fifth-tier sourceless fire.

In other words, the azure flame now had hope of advancing to the fifth tier.

"Could it be that quantitative change produced qualitative change?"

"But while ten thousand sea serpents sound like many, can they really compare to several third-tier demon beasts?"

"Unless... it was the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture!"

The more Luo Chen cultivated this method hailed as the progenitor of all fire arts, the more profound and mysterious he found it.

Even if it was only the Adept section. Even if it came with no attached spells or secret arts.

Merely its miracle of changing spirit root attributes, covering all five elements with one element, was enough to benefit him without end.

Now it seemed this cultivation method still held many, many things worth exploring.

At present, his proficiency in the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture had only barely reached the Skilled level. Once it later reached Perfect, Grandmaster, or even Perfected Mastery, perhaps new changes would naturally arise.

After inspecting the azure flame, Luo Chen examined his own physique.

Even though it was only a portion of the life force shared by the flame, after his flesh absorbed it, warmth flowed through him from head to toe.

Luo Chen even felt that the upper limit of his lifespan would rise again as his body transformed.

And that number might be quite large.

"More than ten thousand sea serpents. I wonder how many years of lifespan ceiling they can provide me."

"If it is a great deal, does that mean I can hunt demon beasts on a large scale, refine them, obtain lifespan, and thereby achieve a different form of longevity?"

When this thought appeared, Luo Chen actually grew excited for a time.

Only after a long while did he finally suppress that excitement.

Such longevity was ultimately not the proper path.

Perhaps it could be attempted, but he absolutely could not rely on it.

What he wanted was not merely longevity, but longevity without death.

Lifespan could bring longevity, but avoiding death required sufficient means to protect his Dao.

And powerful Dao-protecting methods necessarily relied on a cultivation realm that continued to transform.

Thus, cultivation remained the priority of priorities. He must never attend to one thing and lose sight of another.

After carefully examining himself, Luo Chen sent out a voice transmission.

Before long, Cheng Haichang entered the cabin.

"Senior, do you have instructions?"

This time, Luo Chen did not use Mirror Flower Water Moon. He simply chatted with the man in a comparatively calm manner.

"Earlier, I observed that under the sea serpent swarm's assault, all of you remained standing. This ship is quite unusual."

Cheng Haichang nodded. "My lord's eyes are extraordinary. This demon-hunting ship is indeed impressive. My Cheng clan bought it at great cost. It can sail across distant seas and hunt sea fish and demon beasts far from islands where humans gather."

"Demon-hunting ship?" Luo Chen raised a brow, his gaze falling to the ship beneath his feet.

With his experience, he could naturally tell the quality of this vessel. It was at treasure level.

Such a large ship had to be incomparably expensive.

Through the other man's explanation, Luo Chen also came to understand where this type of ship came from.

It was a special treasure refined by artifact-forging grandmasters of the Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance, adapted to the conditions of the Northern Sea.

It could travel through sea and sky, and was especially fast upon the sea.

The entire vessel was refined from the bones of dragon whales from the depths of the ocean, making it particularly compatible with the water element.

Not only was it fast, its carrying capacity was also quite excellent.

Any cultivation clan on the Northern Sea islands with even a little strength would spend heavily to purchase one and work the sea with it as a supplementary source of wealth.

"That white banner is a Soul-Refining Banner, is it not?"

Luo Chen abruptly brought up the weapon Cheng Haichang had used earlier.

Cheng Haichang lowered his head and said nervously, "It is indeed a Soul-Refining Banner from the soul-refining lineage of the Primordial Demon Sect. However, this junior has not actively violated the prohibition by absorbing cultivators' souls. In ordinary times, I have always used demon beast souls to replenish it."

"Oh?"

Luo Chen raised a brow. He had seen very clearly earlier that the man had absorbed the remnant souls of those who died aboard the demon-hunting ship.

Did that not count as violating the prohibition?

Under Cheng Haichang's explanation, he soon learned about the former Primordial Demon Prohibition.

Soul-Refining Banners could be used.

But the souls within should preferably be demon beast souls, which were countless in the great sea.

The souls of human cultivators could not be forcibly seized.

If anyone harbored wicked intentions and hunted cultivators on a large scale to refine a Soul-Refining Banner, the Primordial Demon Sect would issue a warrant and hunt them down.

Of course, with the Primordial Demon Sect destroyed, this so-called prohibition basically existed in name only.

Someone like Cheng Haichang was skirting the line, using the souls of his own fallen clansmen to supplement the banner.

"And that formation?"

"Does Senior mean the formation on the demon-hunting ship? That is the Sea Soul Formation. It draws on the power of the great sea and the souls of countless sea demons to form an array. It grows stronger the longer it fights and is basically standard equipment for all large demon-hunting ships."

"Interesting. Do you have the related formation diagram?"

"Ah, no. This was specially researched by the Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance using methods from the Primordial Demon Sect's soul-refining lineage. How could a small clan like ours have the chance to obtain the original formation diagram?"

"Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance? What relationship does it have with Central Continent's Holy Land, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect?"

"Senior is wise. The Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance is an enterprise of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. It is responsible for collecting resources in the Northern Sea and selling them to the other four continents, while also transferring resources from the other four continents to the Northern Sea."

Luo Chen nodded. Business, then. Buying low and selling high. Nothing more.

"The Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance is very strong. It has Sage true persons stationed here year-round. It also supports countless master-level figures in every profession: alchemy, formations, artifact forging, talismans. It is proficient in all of them. Rumor has it they can even refine demonic artifacts."

"Earlier, Senior seemed interested in those spirit-bonded weapons. In truth, those are also one of the Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance's works."

"These artifacts are not activated with spirit qi, but with a martial artist's inner qi."

"In this way, the countless mortals of the Northern Sea have a place to use their martial ability. So long as they practice martial arts properly, they can borrow spirit-bonded weapons to unleash strength no weaker than early Qi Refinement, or even middle Qi Refinement."

"A demon-hunting ship this large cannot possibly be staffed entirely by cultivators. Generally speaking, the great clans bring large numbers of mortal martial artists aboard, having them help catch fish, hunt demons, process prey, and even assist in battle."

The more Luo Chen listened, the brighter his eyes became.

In the Eastern Wilderness, mortals were not worth mentioning, almost like livestock kept in enclosures. He had never expected them to possess such status in the Northern Sea cultivation world.

For mortals to unleash strength at the middle Qi Refinement level was indeed somewhat inconceivable.

He asked Cheng Haichang for three spirit-bonded weapons, then dismissed him.

Afterward, Luo Chen began studying the things.

...

The enormous demon-hunting ship raised its sails high, riding wind and breaking waves as it advanced.

It drew closer and closer to its destination.

Two days later, Luo Chen put down the saber, sword, and trident in his hands.

A thoughtful look appeared on his face.

"Strange. Marvelous!"

"Within a tiny spirit-bonded weapon, there is actually a mystery of transforming qi and blood into spiritual power. Is this not the early qi refiners' refining essence into qi?"

"Only, the Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance changed it to a method using formations and spirit stones."

"Unfortunately, the qi-to-spirit conversion formations on them all have secrecy measures. I cannot forcibly open them for study."

"If such exquisite methods were popularized throughout the entire Mountains and Seas Realm, the human race's strength would undergo heaven-shaking changes. After all, mortals reproduce far faster than cultivators who cherish their cultivation. If someone at Wang Yuan's level as an innate grandmaster in the past activated the best spirit-bonded weapon, he might be able to threaten late Qi Refinement."

"But why has the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect only distributed this method to the Northern Sea?"

Putting away the three spirit-bonded weapons, Luo Chen sighed.

Right now, he knew what it was, but not why. His curiosity had truly been aroused.

But clearly, the grand Heavenly Origin Dao Sect would not explain itself to a mere Adept like him.

Besides, there was no more time.

They had arrived.

As the anchor was thrown down, the enormous demon-hunting ship swayed slightly and came to rest beside a massive dock.

Luo Chen walked out of the cabin and looked at the bustling, prosperous dock. A strange light flashed in his eyes.

So many ships.

So many people.

There were seven or eight demon-hunting ships of the Cheng clan's scale gathered here, and more than a dozen smaller ones.

According to Cheng Haichang's earlier explanation, those smaller demon-hunting ships could only work in nearby waters.

Only large demon-hunting ships like these dared sail beyond a range of ten thousand li.

And those people!

Aside from the Qi Refinement cultivators maintaining order, ninety-nine percent of them were mortals.

These mortals moved with vigorous force. Their bare arms and legs were knotted with muscle, and rich qi and blood power was stored within their bodies.

Judged by Wang Yuan's standards from back then, these mortals working on the dock were at least second-rate martial artists, if not first-rate.

Some had even touched the threshold of innate master.

What drew Luo Chen's attention most was that these mortal martial artists did not have the numb expressions of people deadened by life.

What they had was intense drive, as though they were full of hope for living.

This feeling did not resemble a world ruled by a so-called demon sect at all. Instead, it seemed like some righteous world blessed with flourishing fortune, clear governance, and harmonious people.

While he observed, the other people at the dock also noticed the Cheng clan's arriving demon-hunting ship.

Seeing the sparse clusters of people aboard, as well as the thick smell of blood on the vessel, everyone understood what had happened.

The Cheng clan's distant-sea hunt had met disaster.

Discussion rose in waves, and sympathetic gazes poured toward the Cheng clan.

Cheng Haichang's expression was somewhat ugly as he walked to Luo Chen's side.

"Senior, our Black Swan Island is located in the northwestern corner of the Swallow Islands. There is still some distance from here, but from this point on, we must travel on our own."

"Mm." Luo Chen nodded.

"Senior, please wait a moment while I make arrangements."

"Do as you please."

Luo Chen continued observing the lively scene on the dock. The Concealment Veil Formation was fully activated, revealing only the spiritual power fluctuation of a mid-stage Initiate.

However, his divine sense had already spread out silently.

"There are two early-stage Initiate cultivators stationed at the dock."

"The nearest island should belong to the Jiaolong Gang Cheng Haichang mentioned earlier, which specializes in dockside business. I didn't expect the Jiaolong Gang to have a late-stage Initiate great cultivator inside."

Luo Chen slowly withdrew his divine sense and looked toward the central region of the Swallow Islands.

Supposedly, there was an itinerant Adept cultivator there.

Taking advantage of the chaos after the Primordial Demon Sect's destruction, he had personally acted to exterminate the strongest Initiate clan in the Swallow Islands and seize the land of their ancestral third-tier spirit vein.

He used that place as his cultivation center and ordered those below to offer tribute to him.

Thirty years ago, this person voluntarily joined the Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance, becoming nominally part of the Cang Hai Alliance and thereby obtaining the method of linking islands into arrays that the Cang Hai Alliance selflessly passed down.

In this way, the Swallow Islands became even more like an iron plate.

These thirty years, that person must have lived quite comfortably indeed.

"May the originator of this practice have no descendants."

The corners of Luo Chen's mouth lifted slightly. He also lacked a third-tier spirit vein suitable for cultivation.

If the other party could do the first day, then naturally he could do the fifteenth.

But for now, there was no rush. He first had to probe that person's foundation.

"Senior, I have made the arrangements. This way, please."

"Mm."

Luo Chen followed Cheng Haichang and flew northwest.

The two moved very quickly. In less than half a day, they arrived at a comparatively remote corner.

Standing in the sky, Luo Chen looked down.

Black Swan Island was long and narrow overall, shaped like a crescent moon. A large stretch of beach lay in the middle.

At one end stood a mountain five hundred zhang high. It was not particularly tall, but it was extremely steep, with sheer cliffs blocking threats from the great sea.

Small lakes dotted the island.

Within those lakes and along the beach, large numbers of black swans walked about.

He knew this was the Cheng clan's main industry: breeding first-tier spirit beasts, black swans.

As his divine sense swept over the island, he also gained a rough understanding of the Cheng clan cultivators' overall level.

One great cultivator at Initiate Stage Nine.

None at middle-stage Initiate.

There were four at early-stage Initiate.

If Cheng Haichang and Cheng Ji, who had been active aboard the demon-hunting ship, were added, then there were six early-stage Initiates.

"One late-stage, six early-stage. Such strength is already impressive among Initiate forces. The Cheng clan's ability to occupy a place in the Swallow Islands, even rank among the top five, truly has its reasons."

While Luo Chen observed the situation on Black Swan Island, Cheng Haichang had already hurried into the clan's important grounds.

The Cheng clan's Initiate cultivators seemed to have received notice long ago. Although some were curious along the way, none disturbed Cheng Haichang.

Before long, Cheng Haichang arrived in front of a small lake like a round mirror.

A young man dressed in white stood there, appearing to have waited for some time.

Seeing Cheng Haichang arrive alone, the white-robed man frowned.

"Where is the Adept Senior you mentioned in the transmission talisman?"

"Reporting to the clan head, that Senior is still waiting outside."

Cheng Dou was stunned. "So polite? Didn't you say he was a demon-path expert?"

"Clan head, be careful with your words!"

Cheng Haichang's expression changed slightly.

Cheng Dou waved a hand. "No matter. Around Little Mirror Lake, I set up a sound-isolation formation in advance. Perhaps it cannot withstand an Adept Senior's divine sense, but it can still give warning. As long as he eavesdrops, the formation will shatter. Look, the formation is still perfectly intact, which shows that this powerful figure still observes etiquette."

These words dispelled Cheng Haichang's concerns.

He let out a breath. "That Senior does seem quite principled, but in actual style, he is nothing like the righteous path."

"How so?" Clan head Cheng Dou showed an expression of sincere attention.

The earlier transmission talisman had contained too little information.

He needed to know more about this mysterious expert, so that he could make arrangements for receiving him next. He even had to consider whether to report the matter to the lord at the very center of the Swallow Islands.

Next, Cheng Haichang recounted everything that had happened in the Xingtian Sea.

From the major points to the minor details, almost nothing was omitted.

"A flame suspected to be Azure Yang Demon Flame."

"Soul-bewitching and heart-confusing arts."

"Extreme concern for intelligence about the Primordial Demon Sect."

"According to what you say, this person is indeed very likely to be a Primordial Demon Sect disciple who traveled outside in his early years. He has not returned to the Northern Sea for many years and actually does not even know the Primordial Demon Sect was destroyed."

"Of course, we cannot rule out that he is an expert from another continent who crossed the vast ocean to travel in our Northern Sea."

Based on the information, Cheng Dou made his analysis.

Then he asked in detail about Luo Chen's preferences, temperament, and so on.

Cheng Haichang answered according to his own judgment, though he could provide little information in that regard.

After a long while, the hesitation in Cheng Dou's eyes vanished, replaced by firmness.

"According to what you say, this person acts with extreme tyranny, but at the same time deliberately gathers intelligence from all sides. Clearly, he is a ruthless figure who plans before acting."

"Especially after reaching the Swallow Islands, he did not go visit Senior Nantian and instead chose to come here first. That supports this view even more."

"No matter what, we must receive him properly later."

"Send people to prepare the best spirit meal. Take out one jar of the Huan Orange Wine that has been sealed for two hundred years."

Cheng Haichang froze. "But that Huan Orange Wine is the spirit wine that will assist clan head when you break through to Adept and form your Core. Even when Senior Nantian occupied the Swallow Islands back then, we did not send him a jar. We only sent over one gourd."

Cheng Dou shook his head. "For my own Core Formation, I will not need that much. You are all still early-stage Initiates, far from Core Formation. Taking out one jar is nothing. The most important thing is that we must receive this person well."

"Why? Perhaps he is merely a passing traveler temporarily lodging on Black Swan Island."

Cheng Dou smiled faintly. "The way he asked about information on the Swallow Islands does not seem like someone merely passing through in haste. Oh, have Seventh Sister prepare. When the time comes, she will accompany me."

Cheng Haichang's expression changed slightly.

"Clan head, Little Seven has only just reached Initiate!"

"If I tell you to go, you go!" Cheng Dou's smile vanished in an instant, and he glared fiercely at him.

Cheng Haichang clenched his fists, but after a moment, he understood.

The clan head must have plans. For this, he would spare no cost. How could the clan drag him down?

Cheng Haichang withdrew and began arranging every matter of hospitality along the way.

Cheng Dou smoothed the corners of his robe, then personally left the island to welcome the guest.

...

Outside the island, Luo Chen's expression shifted slightly.

The conversation between Cheng Haichang and clan head Cheng Dou had fallen into his ears word for word.

His divine sense was already incomparably powerful. In his early years, he had also cultivated the Sound-Isolation Art to Perfected Mastery, and in recent years had become proficient in the obscuring restriction among the seven basic restrictions.

With several factors combined, a mere sound-isolation formation could not block him at all.

Without anyone knowing, he had listened in on their conversation.

At this moment, a thoughtful look appeared in Luo Chen's eyes.

"No wonder Cheng Haichang immediately proclaimed loyalty to the deceased Primordial Demon Sect when he saw me. He mistook me for a remnant of the Primordial Demon Sect."

"Azure Yang Demon Flame?"

A ball of flame appeared in his palm, and the corners of Luo Chen's mouth lifted slightly.

To be fair, his azure flame truly did rather resemble Azure Yang Demon Flame.

In his early years, he had obtained a cultivation method that recorded Azure Yang Demon Flame.

This type of flame was not naturally born, but man-made.

It required gathering great quantities of living beings' essence blood and refining it with demon-path secret arts. It was the most heinous, evil, and strange sort of thing.

In battle, it possessed an extremely powerful ability to defile cultivators' artifacts and treasures.

However, with such man-made flames, every bit used was one bit lost, and it had to be replenished after battle. It could not compare to even a single hair of Luo Chen's azure flame.

"Since that is the case, I may as well push the boat with the current."

Luo Chen murmured. In his eyes, Cheng Dou's figure had already appeared, dressed in fluttering white with wide sleeves spread.

From far away, Cheng Dou quickened his steps and half-jogged over.

"Senior's honored self has descended upon us. This junior failed to welcome you from afar. Please forgive me!"

"No matter. I do not care for such details."

Luo Chen smiled faintly. Wrapped in close-fitting black clothes, his figure appeared even taller, a full head above Cheng Dou.

His gaze swept once behind the man. There were no other Cheng clan cultivators.

It seemed Cheng Dou had come out alone to welcome him because he wanted to keep news of Luo Chen's arrival circulating only within a certain range.

After bowing respectfully, Cheng Dou carefully raised his head.

"This junior dares ask Senior's honored name."

The corners of Luo Chen's mouth lifted. In a clear voice, he said:

"I am Azure Yang."

*(End of Chapter)*
