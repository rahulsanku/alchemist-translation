# Chapter 1000: Peak Luo Tian, Since We're Here

*Wum...*

A strange, low sound rang out, and faint ripples began to spread gently.

At the very center where the ripples emanated, an entrance gradually revealed itself.

The ghostly creatures lurking nearby immediately detected it and surged in from all directions.

At the entrance, a Core Formation cultivator was about to strike, but Min Longyu quietly stopped him.

"Hold!"

Under the puzzled gazes of the others, he explained, "No need to worry. These low-level ghostly creatures are the little ghosts under Elder Bai's command, specifically lured here to guard the entrance to the blessed land. They are friends, not foes!"

The crowd suddenly understood.

Looking closely, indeed, after the low-level ghostly creatures approached, they showed no hostility. Instead, they wandered around, concealing their auras.

This way, other ghostly creatures elsewhere would not so easily detect the opening of the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land.

Min Longyu gazed into the distance. A compass in his hand emitted a trembling sound.

Amidst the violent vibrations, the needle finally stopped, pointing east.

"Two elders, they are still within Mount Yanfu."

Lingfengzi and Canglong exchanged a glance, then the former unhesitatingly released a small black boat.

The boat entered the water and rapidly expanded, in the blink of an eye becoming large enough to carry about ten people.

This hastily assembled support team from the Luo Tian Sect totaled only seven people, so there was more than enough room for everyone to sit together.

Surprisingly, this small black boat could travel freely through the Netherwater. The hazy light radiating from its hull was actually able to resist the filth of the Netherwater.

Canglong watched from the side, clicking her tongue in wonder. "I experienced the power of the Netherwater a bit when I returned back then. Without the aid of a Mystic Turtle, crossing the Nether Abyss would be utterly impossible. I never thought there would be such a rare treasure that could achieve the same effect."

Lingfengzi smiled. "This treasure was obtained by Elder Bai some years ago while searching for the Nether Origin Sect's legacy within the Nether Abyss. It was presumably originally used for crossing the Nether Abyss. However, this Nether-Crossing Boat requires a tremendous amount of mana. Even with my cultivation, I cannot rely on it to cross the entire Nether Abyss."

"I see!" Canglong nodded, then swept her gaze into the profound depths.

The vast Nether Abyss, for tens of thousands of years, had not only suppressed countless yin souls and fierce ghosts but had also once buried the Nether Origin Sect.

That was a Divine Transformation sacred ground!

No matter how filthy the Netherwater was, there were always some strange and rare things that could be preserved.

If the Luo Tian Sect could gather all such treasures, the area where they were most lacking—their foundation—could be greatly supplemented.

But the Netherwater was filthy. Even with the Mystic Turtle and the Nether-Crossing Boat, it would not help Luo Tian Sect disciples come and go freely to explore the Nether Origin Sect's ruins.

"If we can truly seize the Netherwater Spring this time, then none of these will be problems!"

Whoever obtains the Netherwater Spring controls the Nether Abyss!

No one knew if this rumor was true or false.

But with the precedent of the Nether Origin Sect, even if the Luo Tian Sect had to exhaust all its strength, they would still give it a try.

"Set out!"

Although the Nether-Crossing Boat was small, it was extremely fast.

At Lingfengzi's command, it turned into a streak of light, like a water arrow shooting through the ocean of the abyss.

When they completely left the area of the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land, little ghosts gradually became attracted, swarming around them like moths to a flame.

Lingfengzi was piloting the boat and could not attend to other matters.

Canglong did not act either, merely watching coldly from the sidelines.

Min Longyu, however, had already taken the initiative, directing Gu Caiyi and the other three to deal with these little ghosts.

Although the environment of the Nether Abyss was not suitable for normal human cultivators to fight in, the four of them were, after all, late Core Formation Grand Cultivators. Ordinary little ghosts were nothing to fear.

With each of them employing their own methods, the Nether-Crossing Boat advanced unimpeded.

After about the time it takes an incense stick to burn, the compass in Min Longyu's hand suddenly began to tremble violently.

His expression changed drastically, and he whispered, "A fourth-rank Ghost King, comparable to a Nascent Soul cultivator, is approaching. Elder Canglong..."

Ahead, Canglong slightly raised her hand, calm and composed, with a hint of casual ease.

"Continue forward. I'll handle everything!"

Soon, a massive ghostly mist charged from east to west.

Wherever it passed, it swept up everything.

Large and small yin souls and fierce ghosts were all drawn into it. Its might was truly terrifying.

However, if one observed closely, they would notice that this mass of ghostly mist carried a hint of fleeing panic.

It had clearly also discovered the group on the Nether-Crossing Boat.

After an initial moment of shock, it let out a sinister laugh.

"Hehehe... Such fresh blood food! Perfect for replenishing this old man!"

Amidst the vicious laughter, the ghostly mist swelled by another three parts, blotting out the sky as it descended upon the Nether-Crossing Boat.

Fairy Canglong, dressed in white, stood at the bow of the boat, holding an elegant long sword inlaid with precious gems, as clear as autumn water.

She calmly watched the ghostly mist descend, then struck out with her sword.

A waterfall-like sword light instantly poured forth.

Seeing this, the fourth-rank Ghost King was not afraid.

"My fierce ghost body can gather and disperse without form. What can such a weak sword light do to me?"

But the next moment!

A cold, stern voice rang out.

"Ten Directions, All Freeze!"

Where the waterfall-like sword light passed, large swaths of Netherwater froze into blocks of ice, and a vast amount of ghostly mist was also frozen within.

The fourth-rank Ghost King's mind was thrown into chaos. Mustering its remaining strength, it forcibly broke through the ice and then fled without looking back into the depths of the Nether Abyss.

The crisis was instantly resolved!

Cheers erupted from the Nether-Crossing Boat.

Lingfengzi, piloting the treasure boat, could not help but praise, "Daoist Canglong, your method of striking the insubstantial with the substantial is truly exquisite!"

However, Canglong Fairy did not fully accept this praise.

She twirled her sword, looking at the Canghai Zhenlong Sword in her hand, a thoughtful expression on her face.

"That Ghost King was already heavily injured. It seemed to have overwhelming ghostly aura, but in reality, it was just a paper tiger, outwardly strong but inwardly weak. If it had been at its peak, I would never have been able to force it back so easily. This is evident from the fact that even trapped by my Profound Ice Qi, it could still forcibly break free."

Lingfengzi was startled. "That thing was injured?"

Canglong nodded emphatically. "Yes, and very badly. Otherwise, it wouldn't have been so easily suppressed by my Profound Ice Qi!"

Lingfengzi was puzzled. "Who injured it? Could it be..."

The two exchanged a glance. The mood that had lifted from repelling a powerful enemy began to grow heavy again.

Such a powerful Ghost King—why would it be so heavily injured for no reason?

There must have been an even more extreme battle that caused it to end up in such a state.

If it was Wang Yuan and Bai Meiling, what was their situation now after a battle of that level?

"We can't just wait and see anymore!"

Canglong muttered to herself, then, in front of everyone, took out the storage pouch that Li Yingzhang had given her before they left.

With a slap of her jade hand, she struck several spell seals into it.

The next moment!

A powerful aura instantly spread uncontrollably.

Everyone was startled.

Lingfengzi, in particular, felt it deeply. His expression changed drastically.

"Late Nascent Soul!"

"When did the Luo Tian Sect have such a trump card?"

Canglong did not explain. Instead, she issued an order.

"Speed up! We'll charge straight into Mount Yanfu. I'll clear the way!"

As she spoke, several top-grade spirit stones fell into her hand. Everything was ready to go.

...

On the western side of Mount Yanfu, there was an empty area.

The Ghost King from earlier had fled from here.

As time passed since its departure, countless low-level ghostly creatures gathered from all directions, trying to enter Mount Yanfu.

Suddenly!

A group of yin souls and resentful ghosts turned their heads in unison, looking behind them into the abyss.

What greeted them was a blazing white beam of light, breaking through everything, sweeping across the universe.

*Chi!*

This blazing white beam came extremely fast, and its power was also extremely fierce. Not only did it instantly destroy all yin souls and fierce ghosts, but its residual power did not cease, charging straight into Mount Yanfu.

On the Nether-Crossing Boat, Lingfengzi watched this scene, his eyelids twitching wildly.

A giant, behemoth-like white tiger was slowly closing its mouth. Stiff, clicking sounds came from within its maw.

And the top-grade spirit stone on its forehead had lost a small portion of its spiritual power due to this single strike.

Not only that, but the mana fluctuations on Fairy Canglong, who was controlling the White Tiger Puppet, also showed a momentary decline.

There was also a hint of shock in Canglong's eyes.

Was this the killer move that Luo Chen had left for the Luo Tian Sect before he left?

A puppet comparable to a late Nascent Soul cultivator!

Where had he obtained it?

Moreover, the method he left for controlling the puppet gave Fairy Canglong a very familiar feeling, as if it originated from the Falling Cloud Sect lineage!

Could this puppet also be related to her old friend Han Zhan?

After witnessing the power of the puppet's single strike, Canglong's thoughts raced through her mind. Then she pointed her long sword forward.

"Enter the mountain!"

The Nether-Crossing Boat turned into a streak of light and charged straight into Mount Yanfu.

But soon, its forward momentum came to an abrupt halt.

...

*Boom!*

A terrifying white beam of light broke through the mountain, crashing into the great seal blocking its path, causing it to tremble incessantly.

"A powerful enemy is coming!"

Deng Taiyue withdrew his Xuanhuang Great Seal with one hand, a grave expression on his face. He reminded the others without turning his head.

But unexpectedly, Luo Chen's voice behind him was as calm as before.

"It's fine. It's my people."

Deng Taiyue was stunned. The power of that attack just now was almost comparable to a full strike from an ordinary Grand Cultivator.

Did his Luo Tian Sect still have such a hidden expert?

The next moment, he saw a giant white tiger carrying seven figures charging violently up the mountain from outside.

Deng Taiyue's eyes suddenly widened.

A late Nascent Soul puppet?

The moment the White Tiger Puppet set foot on this ground, its form suddenly halted, leaving two deep pits in the earth.

The hearts of the several figures standing on it, which had been hanging in suspense, instantly relaxed, turning into surprise and joy.

Everyone flew down from it in unison.

"Master!"

"It's the Supreme Elder!"

"The Supreme Elder has returned!"

"Brother Luo Chen, it's been a while!"

Luo Chen nodded. "Lingfengzi, it's been a few years. Your cultivation has improved considerably!"

Lingfengzi suppressed the shock in his heart and modestly waved his hand. "The blessed land has dense spiritual energy. Such progress is nothing."

Although he was modest, the slight upturn of his mouth clearly revealed the pride in his heart.

He had been stuck at the first level of Nascent Soul for three hundred years, partly due to talent issues and partly held back by the human-demon war.

But since entering the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land, after decades of secluded cultivation, his hardships had finally paid off. He had broken through to the fourth level of Nascent Soul, stepping into the mid-stage.

This Great Dao still had a glimmer of hope!

Luo Chen smiled, his gaze falling on the gentle woman in the white dress.

Canglong spoke first. "Are your matters concluded?"

"Long finished. If not for some unexpected events, I would have returned many years ago." Luo Chen explained briefly, then looked Canglong Fairy up and down, staring until she became a little embarrassed. After a long moment, Luo Chen said thoughtfully, "Your realm is not yet stable. Why did you come out to fight?"

Canglong smoothed a strand of hair from her cheek and said frankly, "This battle concerns the rise and fall of the future Luo Tian Sect. I naturally had to contribute my share."

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow, his gaze sweeping over Canglong, Lingfengzi, and the excited cultivators behind them, pausing for a moment on the silent Gu Caiyi.

During the time he was away, the sect cohesion of the Luo Tian Sect had clearly improved significantly.

Li Yingzhang was doing a good job as Sect Master!

"Master, that Netherwater Spring..."

Tianxuan couldn't help but speak, but Luo Chen raised his hand to stop him.

"I already know the specifics from Xiao Bai."

Upon hearing this, everyone felt at ease.

The Supreme Elder was still so calm; it seemed everything was still under his control.

Luo Chen turned around, pointed at Deng Taiyue, and introduced him solemnly.

"True Man Taiyue, Sect Master of the Taishan Sect. You should all have heard of his reputation. He is also a good friend of mine. He has come this time to lend a hand to our Luo Tian Sect."

"Greetings, True Man Taiyue!"

The group of Luo Tian Sect cultivators all bowed and greeted him, their etiquette impeccable.

Lingfengzi, who already knew him, also called out a greeting.

Canglong, however, solemnly announced her own name, using the prefix of Luo Tian Sect Elder.

Only then did True Man Taiyue snap out of the shock brought by the White Tiger Puppet. After returning the greetings of the Luo Tian Sect juniors, he could not help but sigh again.

"The world only knows Brother Luo's reputation as an Alchemy Sect Master and his power in slaying demons. They would never imagine that he also possesses such astonishing methods in managing a sect."

This sigh came from the bottom of his heart.

The Luo Tian Sect was, at best, an ordinary sect established only three hundred years ago, with no foundation to speak of.

Yet, in this hasty meeting, they had three Nascent Soul True Men, one Body Refining powerhouse, and a fourth-rank Ghost King.

Oh, and he had almost forgotten the docile desolate beast Mystic Turtle and the two newly advanced Nascent Souls left behind at the abandoned city of Shangyou!

With such a lineup, even placed in the heyday of the Eastern Wilderness, it would be comparable to top-tier upper sects like the Five Elements Divine Sect or the Heavenly Capital Sect.

If some, like Lingfengzi, were outsiders recruited rather than personally trained disciples, then the late Core Formation juniors like Gu Caiyi, Tianxuan, and Ye Suifeng were enough to show that the Luo Tian Sect's development had not suffered a gap.

In comparison, his own Taishan Sect, which he had neglected to manage while focusing on cultivation over the years, was far, far too inferior.

And this gap, in his opinion, was not due to the talent or aptitude of the disciples, but to the difference between him and Luo Chen as leaders.

He was indeed inferior to Luo Chen!

Faced with this praise, Luo Chen smiled faintly, neither taking credit nor deflecting it.

He simply turned his head to look up at Mount Yanfu.

On the mountainside and even the summit, ghostly aura surged to the heavens, and yin winds howled.

The sounds of countless ghosts wailing could be heard clearly even from a hundred li away.

This battle concerned the rise and fall of the Luo Tian Sect, comparable to the Danxia Defense Battle and the Bai Li Qingchuan Battle.

But the opponents they were about to face far surpassed any before.

Yet, in Luo Chen's eyes, there was not a trace of fear.

"Since we're here, let's go up and take a look!"
