# Chapter 612: Arctic Demon-Sealing Formation, Dark Blue Mysterious Armor Emerges

Luo Chen saw very clearly that the clothes of the Foundation Establishment true cultivators waiting outside the hall all bore badges and marks to some degree.

Some were clan emblems, and some were sect symbols.

Although three Nascent Soul Sages within the Myriad Immortals Assembly also brought their own forces, the emblems of those three forces were not any of these.

Wu Ruofu carefully explained from the side, "These people should be prodigies from Divine Origin City. Through Elder Sang's connections, they have come to the Three Oceans defensive line for tempering."

"Prodigies?"

Luo Chen raised a brow.

His sharp gaze swept across them.

Sure enough, most of these Foundation Establishment cultivators looked young, and the older ones were only middle-aged in appearance.

Even though cultivators could use spiritual qi in daily cultivation to beautify and preserve their looks, that consumed spiritual power, and those who aspired to longevity generally would not do such things.

If an entire group looked this way, it could only mean they truly were young.

Wu Ruofu nodded. "Yes. The current righteous-demonic war grows more chaotic by the day. Some forces forced to participate in the war, fearing that their entire army may be wiped out, send some promising seedlings from their sects to neutral forces."

"After all, eggs cannot be placed in the same basket. Divine Origin City is the Tianyuan Merchant Alliance's headquarters and has the background of the Central Continent's Tianyuan Dao Sect behind it. It is truly the safest place in the current Northern Sea."

"Over these years, more and more young cultivators with excellent aptitude have appeared in Divine Origin City. At first, when this junior saw so many beings of the same realm as myself who were decades or even a century younger, I felt rather ashamed. But after seeing it enough, I became numb."

Toward the end, his tone carried a trace of feeling.

However, he received no response from Luo Chen.

This was because Luo Chen's gaze had landed on one person.

Wang An, a Foundation Establishment disciple of Langya Mountain.

When Luo Chen had traveled from the southeast to Giant Whale Harbor, he had taken Langya Mountain's fleet, and Wang An had been the one to bring him aboard.

Unexpectedly, this person had also come to the Three Oceans defensive line.

This man was not old. Back then, he had been only in his thirties. Even now, he was at most a little over forty.

Yet his realm had already advanced to mid Foundation Establishment.

One could only say he truly deserved to be one of the talented people Langya Mountain had carefully selected. Without the disturbance of mundane affairs, his cultivation progress advanced a thousand li a day.

Only...

'I vaguely remember that the elders of Langya Mountain instructed them to cultivate properly in Divine Origin City, serving as the sect's living strength while quietly waiting for the righteous-demonic war to end. Now, instead of staying properly in Divine Origin City, they have run to the Three Oceans defensive line for so-called tempering. I do not know whether they trust too much in their methods or simply do not know death.'

Luo Chen muttered inwardly for a few moments, then entered Quyang Hall without hesitation.

Behind him, gazes followed one after another.

After he entered, faint discussion rose like the buzzing of insects.

Most of it was about Luo Chen being one of Quyang Island's three Golden Core garrison cultivators. Their words did not dare show the slightest disrespect.

Amid the crowd, Wang An, dressed in white, looked doubtfully at Luo Chen's back. Born with sharp spiritual perception, he somehow sensed a trace of familiarity.

It was as if he had seen an old acquaintance.

But inside the Myriad Immortals Assembly, how could there be an old acquaintance of his?

"Junior Brother Wang, what is it?"

A senior brother from Langya Mountain patted his shoulder.

Wang An did not speak.

The senior brother smiled. "Do not worry. Quyang belongs to the second defensive line and is not dangerous."

Wang An returned to himself and asked doubtfully, "Was it not said that we were going to the first defensive line in the Liuyang Sea Region to hunt demon beasts? I heard things have not been peaceful there."

The senior brother shrugged. "If it were peaceful, how would we temper ourselves? These years, we have only cultivated bitterly, and our cultivation has greatly advanced. But only power we can actually bring to bear belongs to our strength. Building carts behind closed doors absolutely will not do. Only such dangerous places can polish our methods."

At this point, he blinked.

"Junior Brother, you do not want us, whom the sect spent such a great price to cultivate, to be a group of empty showpieces, do you?"

Fighting demon beasts could temper their combat methods without needing to worry about the deceitful schemes of human cultivators.

Put another way, it allowed them to safely improve combat experience without facing excessive danger.

This had always been a tradition of the Northern Sea cultivation world when sect and clan disciples achieved a little success in their realms.

Only, in the past, they went in groups to the outer seas to hunt demon beasts, while now they had changed it to coming to the Myriad Immortals Assembly.

Wang An's heart was still not very calm. He did not know whether it was because of Luo Chen's back earlier, or the unease deep in his own heart.

"The orders from Langya Mountain were not like this. How will we explain ourselves later?"

Seeing his junior brother nag like this, the senior brother also grew somewhat annoyed.

"We spent a heavy sum to use Elder Sang's channel, so he will naturally protect us somewhat at critical moments. Besides, this time, it is not only our Langya Mountain cultivators who have come to the Three Oceans defensive line. There are others too. Do you see them worrying?"

Wang An opened his mouth. His gaze swept past and saw that most eyes were expectant, their faces flushed with excitement.

Every person was eager to try.

...

Inside the hall.

A woman's voice, carrying thin anger, lightly echoed around them.

"These people are not cultivators of my Myriad Immortals Assembly. How can they be casually brought into Quyang Island, this central hub? Sang Jiugong, I need you to give me an explanation."

"Why must Lord Lushan be angry? It is not as if cultivators from other forces have never moved around the Three Oceans defensive line before."

"That was during wartime. The outside cultivators belonged to reinforcements from various sides. How can that be compared with now? Furthermore, the central hubs of the three great defensive lines have always forbidden cultivators of other forces from setting foot within. Sang Jiugong, you joined the Myriad Immortals Assembly even earlier than I did. Do not tell me you do not know this."

"Lord Lushan, must you truly be so serious?"

"I am the master of this place for ten years. If anything goes even slightly wrong on Quyang Island, it has little to do with you, but I will bear primary responsibility. Tell me, how can I not be serious?"

When Luo Chen entered the hall, this was the argument he heard.

He had not expected that a mere group of outside cultivators would cause two Golden Core cultivators to flare up so fiercely.

At the same time, from the two people's conversation, he vaguely sensed that Sang Jiugong had likely used the convenience of his position to skim some profit.

Those young prodigies from various sects who had run to Divine Origin City from every direction to avoid disaster might not have especially high realms, but they were certainly wealthy.

Sang Jiugong had probably made a great windfall from this.

Otherwise, there would be no need to butt heads like this with Lord Lushan, who was at Golden Core Stage Five.

Seeing the atmosphere between the two sink to freezing point, Sang Jiugong's expression extremely ashen, he suddenly looked toward Luo Chen.

On that ashen face, he forcefully squeezed out a smile.

"This must be Daoist Azure Yang, master of Peng Lake. This old man, Sang Jiugong, offers greetings."

One did not strike a smiling face.

Luo Chen returned the salute. "That is indeed me. Fellow Daoist Sang is too courteous."

Lord Lushan, however, showed surprise from the side.

She had long known the name Daoist Azure Yang, a fairly well-known artifact-forger in the Myriad Immortals Assembly. Five years ago, he rented Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake, once stirring up quite a bit of discussion.

Outsiders understood him as wealthy, with a deep realm, and faintly overlapping with the rumored identity of Devil Lord Azure Yang.

This was also why, when she met him, she had taken the initiative to propose talisman business.

She had even wanted to build a good relationship with him afterward and do more talisman business.

But for Sang Jiugong to be this polite was somewhat beyond expectation.

One had to know, although Sang Jiugong's realm was not especially high, only Golden Core Stage Four, he was extremely old and had abundant combat experience. Within the Myriad Immortals Assembly, he was also a person of some stature.

When meeting other Golden Core cultivators, he usually relied on his seniority.

Yet before this Daoist Azure Yang, he was actually this "humble"?

Sang Jiugong smiled faintly. "How could this be too courteous? Fellow Daoist made Huangfu Song retreat from difficulty on Peng Lake. For such ability, no amount of courtesy would be excessive."

Made Huangfu Song retreat from difficulty?

What matter was this?

Lord Lushan frowned slightly. She roughly understood that, by taking office early, she had probably missed some news from the Fulong Mountain Range.

As for Huangfu Song, he was a Golden Core Stage Seven great cultivator.

Daoist Azure Yang could actually make the other man retreat from difficulty?

Seeing the two chat more and more happily, Lord Lushan coughed lightly twice. After they stopped talking, she spoke coldly.

"Let us first discuss how to handle this batch of cultivators brought by Sang Jiugong. Their backgrounds are complicated, and most come from other Nascent Soul forces. If they remain on Quyang Island, they will ultimately be a hidden danger."

Sang Jiugong's wrinkled face was now squeezed together.

"I brought them here. It is impossible for me to let them slink back in disgrace."

"You!"

Sang Jiugong turned to Luo Chen. "Daoist Azure Yang, you are a reasonable person. Why not comment on who is right and who is wrong?"

Luo Chen felt helpless. How was he supposed to comment?

No matter what he said, he would offend one side. He was not a fool.

After thinking for a moment, Luo Chen calmly said, "Ultimately, this matter is only a problem of lacking a proper name and justification. Who is right and who is wrong is not important. Solving the problem is what matters. In my view, simply grant them temporary identities as wandering cultivators of the Myriad Immortals Assembly."

"This... there is no precedent!" Lord Lushan hesitated slightly, but still rejected Luo Chen's suggestion.

Luo Chen smiled. "It is merely the identity of ordinary Myriad Immortals Assembly wandering cultivators. They could apply for that themselves, and it is not the star-ranked demon hunter identity we register at the Demon Hunter Office. Also, their purpose is actually to go to the first defensive line to hunt demon beasts and temper themselves. Would it not be enough to urge them to go to the first defensive line sooner?"

This was a suggestion for her to throw the trouble away.

Lord Lushan's elegant brows furrowed slightly as she considered this suggestion.

Seeing her loosen somewhat, Sang Jiugong struck while the iron was hot. "Daoist Azure Yang is absolutely right. Especially since I hear more and more demon beasts are appearing at the first defensive line, and they are short-handed there. With this batch of Foundation Establishment true cultivators joining, the pressure on my Myriad Immortals Assembly can also be eased. With such a perfect solution, Lord Lushan, why not consider it?"

Lord Lushan thought for a long while, then finally nodded slowly.

"Three days."

"At most three days. They must leave Quyang Island."

"Elder Sang, I hope you do not make this humble Daoist's position difficult."

Huu...

Sang Jiugong let out a breath. First he made a promise, then thanked Luo Chen and left the hall ahead of them.

After he left, Lord Lushan sighed audibly.

"This Sang Jiugong schemes and scrambles, running between the Myriad Immortals Assembly and Divine Origin City all day long. How does he have the drifting-clouds-and-wild-cranes bearing of cultivators like us?"

Luo Chen chuckled. "Free as drifting clouds and wild cranes though we may be, are we not also bound by missions, forced to sit on Quyang Island for years?"

"It is not the same."

Lord Lushan shook her head. "Sang Jiugong is old, and his hope of breaking through realms is gone. He wants to leave behind a legacy before he sits in death. He also worries that the force he establishes will be coveted by others, so he runs everywhere, building relationships."

"So that is how it is..."

Luo Chen fell into thought.

In the few short years since coming to the Myriad Immortals Assembly, he had already seen three wandering cultivators who wanted to establish forces.

But each one had a different purpose.

One was Zhang Jiadi and his wife. They established a family force to protect their child, Zhang Jingqing.

One was Huangfu Song, who wanted to rebuild the Great Zhou imperial dynasty, only to kick the iron plate that was Luo Chen at the very beginning of his enterprise.

As for Sang Jiugong, Luo Chen had thought this sort of scheming was greed for money and profit, but unexpectedly, he wanted to leave behind his own inheritance so that someone would carry on after him.

Looking at it this way, among the countless wandering cultivators, there were not many who truly wished to drift for a lifetime as wandering cultivators.

This female Daoist before him, however, was a genuine wandering cultivator, alone and without any followers or dependents.

"Now that you have met Sang Jiugong, there are no other matters here. Daoist Azure Yang, you may do as you wish."

"Mm."

Luo Chen gave a sound of acknowledgement and was about to leave when he suddenly remembered something.

"Earlier, Sang Jiugong said the battle at the first defensive line is tight. What is happening there?"

Lord Lushan smiled bitterly. "What else could it be? The seal at Arctic Yemo Heaven has loosened again."

Luo Chen's expression changed instantly.

He remembered that rumor said Arctic Yemo Heaven sealed hundreds of millions of demon races, among which there were no lack of fourth-tier Demon Emperors at the Nascent Soul level, and he had even heard that there were fifth-tier ancient demons at the Ascendant level inside.

If all of them charged out...

Then he would truly have to consider whether to pick up and run.

Seeing this, Lord Lushan hurriedly comforted him.

"Do not worry. This sort of situation occurs once every ten years. Even if powerful demon beasts run out, they are only some formidable third-tier demon beasts. Fourth-tier and fifth-tier ones cannot come out yet."

"Yet?" Luo Chen could not help worrying. "What about later? The Myriad Immortals Assembly has a tiger sleeping beside its couch. If Arctic Yemo Heaven's seal is fully broken and Ascendant ancient demons run out, will you and I not become fish on the chopping block, left for demons to slaughter as they please?"

Lord Lushan shook her head. "There is no need to worry about that either. The sealing grand formation there was personally arranged by the ancestor of the Primordial Demon Sect's soul-refining lineage before his death. It combined heavenly timing, earthly advantage, and the Primordial Demon Sect's ten-thousand-year foundation. How could it be so easily broken? Especially over a hundred years ago, when the mighty expert surnamed Li of the Cang Hai Alliance personally went outside Yemo Heaven and arranged another grand formation himself. Under this double guarantee, it will be safe for at least a thousand years. As for a thousand years later, you and I will either have become Nascent Soul Sages and roam freely across the Mountains and Seas Realm, or we will have turned into a handful of yellow earth. Why care if the flood rises to the sky?"

Luo Chen opened his mouth, but in the end could only smile bitterly.

It seemed that was truly so.

He had also heard that the Ascendant mighty expert of the Cang Hai Alliance had become divine through the Dao of formations, with formation attainments that shook heaven and earth.

The island-linking formation method he had casually transmitted had spared countless immortal islands in the Northern Sea from the worry of demon beast attacks from the sea.

If he had gone outside the Primordial Demon Sect ruins and personally arranged a sealing formation, then even ancient demons of the same Ascendant level would probably find it very difficult to break through.

The reason low-level demon beasts below fourth-tier occasionally broke through the seal now was probably intentional on the part of those two mighty experts.

For that sort of sealing grand formation, the higher the realm and the stronger the divine soul, the greater the restraint.

Conversely, the lower the realm of the demon beast, the weaker the restraint it received.

This bit of reasoning was something Luo Chen had inferred after studying formations over the years and gaining some modest understanding.

The sealing grand formation of Arctic Yemo Heaven was like a giant fishing net. Small fish could escape through the mesh, while big fish struggled in every way and could not get out.

Of course, what the specific situation was, Luo Chen had no way to know.

In any case, if the sky fell, taller people would hold it up.

Did they not see that the several Nascent Soul Sages of the Myriad Immortals Assembly were not panicking at all?

After taking leave of Lord Lushan, Luo Chen did not return directly to his cave dwelling. Instead, he first went to the seaside.

Under the reverent gazes of the patrolling cultivators, he released Black King.

"This is my spirit pet. I have already notified Lord Lushan and Elder Sang. When you see him in the future, do not panic."

Seeing Black King bare fangs and brandish claws in the air, his savage appearance fully revealed.

Luo Chen had no choice but to add one more sentence.

"Do not be afraid. He does not bite people."

Black King's aura froze, then he slipped into the vast ocean in disgrace.

Whether it was the Primordial Unity Cauldron's space or Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake, neither was as comfortable as this boundless blue sea.

"Do not play too wildly. Remember the time and come back to help me watch the furnace fire."

After giving this instruction, Luo Chen returned to his mountain cave dwelling under the reverent gazes of the low-level cultivators.

Three days later.

Sang Jiugong personally led the batch of prodigies who had registered temporary identities as Myriad Immortals Assembly wandering cultivators to the first defensive line.

Regarding this, Luo Chen expressed no further opinion.

He simply devoted himself to the daily life of artifact-forging and practicing arts.

...

Half a year later.

Outside the cave dwelling, Black King, with his body shrunk, controlled two flying knives, one black and one white, chopping hard wood in a casual, intermittent rhythm.

Luo Chen had miscalculated.

He had not expected his artifact-forging art to progress so quickly, exhausting all the consumable materials he had brought in a short half year.

This was also related to his long immersion in cultivating the Dragon-Slaying Art, which made him unable to replenish the Withering-Bloom True Flame in time, forcing him to use spirit wood to keep the furnace fire vigorous.

Thus, the later wood was collected for him from various places by Quyang Island's logistics unit.

These were all raw logs and had not been processed in advance.

After Luo Chen received them, he simply handed them to Black King to handle.

Only, if Qian Ting were here and saw the two flying knives he had once wielded across Cold Light Island being used by a wicked beast to chop firewood, he would probably be angered back to life even if already dead.

Under the nourishment of the Dragon-Nurturing Basin, the two flying knives had long since both reached the mid-grade magic treasure level.

One was named Yin Slaughter, and the other Yang Severance, names that sounded full of baleful force.

After Luo Chen bestowed them upon Black King, he also taught him the human race's consecration method. The other party refined them day and night, and over the years, he had learned to control them with some style, the blades moving with fierce wind.

Clack.

The firewood fell to the ground, but Black King paid it no attention. He put away the two flying knives and slipped into the cave dwelling.

"Master, you are awake!"

On the stone bed, Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes.

With just one glance, Black King's whole body went cold.

It was as if, in that instant, a long blade shining with cold light had slashed toward his divine soul.

Seeing his reaction, Luo Chen was extremely satisfied.

Half a year had passed, and the soul essence in that little flag had been completely consumed.

He had also finally mastered the technique of using the divine soul to execute the Dragon-Slaying Art.

From frightening Wu Ruofu at first to making even mid third-tier Black King feel cold all over now, one could see that his mastery of this art had reached its limit.

The true situation was that within his sea of consciousness, once the Dragon-Slaying Art emerged, it tolerated no foreign thing. Outside the sea of consciousness, within one zhang of his body, it could cut another person's divine sense.

But it was only one zhang.

Any farther, and the whip could not reach.

Even so, it allowed Luo Chen to see the formidable nature of fourth-tier spells.

"I have mastered two fourth-tier spells in total. There is no need to say more about the Dragon-Slaying Art. At Minor Completion, it could remove foreign mana, and at Great Completion, it can even cut away divine soul contamination. As for the Yuan Dan Art, it directly doubled my mana out of thin air, and its potential is boundless."

"I wonder what wonders other fourth-tier spells possess?"

Regarding the path of spells, Luo Chen's talent was beyond question.

But over these years, although he had cultivated all sorts of third-tier spells basically to Great Perfection, he had very few fourth-tier spells.

The main reason was that high-level spells of this kind had basically stopped circulating on the market. They were all secret transmissions of Nascent Soul upper sects.

Even with the system in hand, allowing Luo Chen to forcefully enter the threshold, a clever wife still could not cook without rice.

Even the Demon Hunter Office had only put out one fourth-tier spell for wandering cultivators to exchange for over the past hundred years, and once it was exchanged for, they would not allow others to exchange for it again.

"What a pity."

Luo Chen sighed and rose, arriving before the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

"Did you add firewood on time these days?"

Black King lay at his feet and said respectfully, "Never slackened. The fire has also always remained vigorous."

Luo Chen gave a sound of acknowledgement. Then his hands formed seals, and a stream of incomparably pure azure flame emerged from his mouth.

With this azure flame joining in, the Primordial Unity Cauldron grew even redder, and faint ding-dong sounds came from within.

Half a year had passed. Whether this Dark Blue Mysterious Armor succeeded or failed, there should be a result now.

As his mana continued being consumed, the strange sounds inside the Primordial Unity Cauldron grew louder and louder. At first listen, it was as if an ocean were stirring up wind and waves inside.

Luo Chen's expression was calm, his eyes carrying a trace of anticipation.

Seizing a certain moment, he suddenly slapped the cauldron body with one palm.

Clang!

"If you do not emerge now, when will you?"

With a stern shout, the sealing qi barrier above the cauldron mouth shattered, and a thick rush of water vapor swept into his face.

Within the misty vapor, the illusory outline of a deep blue suit of armor appeared and disappeared.

Seeing this, Luo Chen let out a soft sound of surprise.

"Hm? It actually succeeded?"

With an outstretched hand, the illusory outline dispersed, and a palm-sized thin armor plate fell into his hand.

Without a doubt, this was the lower-grade water-type defensive magic treasure he had named the Dark Blue Mysterious Armor.

But looking at this object, Luo Chen's thick brows unconsciously furrowed.

"Why is it like this?"

*(End of Chapter)*
