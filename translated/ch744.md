# Chapter 744: Creating a Dao Scripture; Divine Seal Locks the Nascent Soul

Beneath Heaven-Earth Peak, the Passing River surged and rolled.

Like time itself, flowing quietly day and night without pause.

Before anyone knew it, half a year had passed.

At cave abode Jia Seventy-Eight, Fu Qinglan looked worriedly at the tall parasol tree. Even heavy white snow could not hide its lush green color.

What truly drew her gaze, however, was the white-robed man seated at the treetop.

"Grandfather, is he really all right?"

Fu Chaosheng stood beside her, equally worried.

In the past, though he was quite old, he had maintained himself extremely well. His complexion was ruddy, and wrinkles were not obvious.

But in a short half year, his whole person seemed to have aged by several decades at once, showing the decline of a mortal.

"This is a state of sudden comprehension. For cultivators, it is something encountered by chance and not sought. Once they encounter it, they often prolong the time of comprehension as much as possible. But of all times, it had to happen now!"

This timing was far too sensitive!

The Alchemy Saint's ascension was imminent, and Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven was about to open. If Luo Chen could not wake from sudden comprehension, he would delay great matters.

Fu Qinglan was somewhat envious. "Sudden comprehension? Is his talent truly, as Grandfather said, not inferior to a sacred-land prodigy?"

Fu Chaosheng smiled bitterly and nodded.

He did not doubt Luo Chen's talent in the slightest.

At mid-stage Adept, he had comprehended a method of forming a Nascent Soul without a teacher.

Later, as a rogue cultivator, he formed a Nascent Soul.

Especially at Molan Court in the Northern Sea, Fu Chaosheng had personally seen Luo Chen's spirit merge with heaven and earth and enter a brief state of sudden comprehension.

That time had been extremely brief.

But this time, it had continued for a full half year!

"His early years of wandering without stability made it difficult for him to settle down and cultivate quietly. Now that he finally obtained a place for quiet cultivation, his accumulated foundation burst forth at once."

"But sudden comprehension can be good or bad. I fear only that he has taken a wrong path!"

Fu Chaosheng's worry was not unreasonable.

Because in the eyes of the grandfather and granddaughter, Luo Chen's current state was not rising proudly upward at all, but endlessly falling!

His fusion with heaven and earth was faint and intermittent.

His magic power no longer brimmed with scorching vitality, but had become dead silent and low.

Even his seemingly incomparably young face had begun to develop wrinkles, changing toward an elderly state.

The black hair that had originally hung loose behind him was now a sheet of gray-white.

"Was I too impatient?"

Fu Chaosheng questioned himself, and a measure of self-reproach appeared.

"If I had not thought to make him a little stronger and given him that old-version Taihe Pill. If I had not given him all the fire-attribute cultivation arts I had collected in one breath. If I had waited until the matter was done before giving him that bottle of Flameflow Marrow..."

Fu Chaosheng did not know where the flaw had appeared.

But he knew that Luo Chen's current changes were surely connected to him.

This forced him to doubt himself.

"Was I too bold, or too eager for quick success?"

"Or have I truly grown too old to distinguish..."

Just as Fu Chaosheng's heart wavered and he questioned himself again and again, a soft cry suddenly came by his ear.

"Grandfather, he seems to be waking?"

Yes, he was waking!

Suddenly, vast amounts of spiritual qi surged madly toward Luo Chen.

On the parasol tree, that dead silent body was like a vortex, wildly devouring this spiritual qi. For a time, it actually formed a small spiritual qi storm.

Within the storm, Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes.

When his gaze swept over the two of them, those deep black eyes inexplicably held a few traces of vicissitudes.

"Huu..."

He breathed out a mouthful of turbid qi, and the spiritual qi storm stopped abruptly.

Luo Chen rose to his full height and floated down.

As his gray-white hair drifted, the lush green tree behind him inexplicably decayed, turning into ash across the ground.

Seeing this, Fu Chaosheng could not wait to speak. "Luo Chen, are you all right?"

"What could be wrong with me? It was only..." Luo Chen smiled and began to speak, but when his voice emerged, his brows could not help furrowing.

From the disbelief on the two people's faces across from him, he also realized the hoarseness of his voice just now.

"Cough, cough!"

After coughing softly twice, Luo Chen spoke again.

"It was only a nap."

This time, his voice was as always, low and gentle, yet firm and powerful.

Logically, such a voice would inspire great confidence, but the Fu grandfather and granddaughter became even more worried.

Changing one's tone was simply too easy for a cultivator.

This change in Luo Chen seemed more like a cover-up.

Especially since his magic power fluctuations were nearly nonexistent. Standing there, he was like a Qi Refinement cultivator.

No!

Like an aged Qi Refinement cultivator!

"How could this happen?" Fu Chaosheng murmured.

Luo Chen currently had little mind to explain. "I need to rest, and also organize and summarize this deep cultivation session. I will not entertain you two. If you need to discuss matters with me, three to five days from now would be best."

This was seeing them out.

Fu Chaosheng did not pester him and turned to leave.

Fu Qinglan, however, kept looking back at the man standing between mountain and abyss, letting clear wind blow through his gray-white hair.

"Grandfather, I do not know why, but the current Wilderness Wanderer Luo Chen feels even more dangerous to me than before."

Fu Chaosheng, still immersed in self-reproach, worry, and confusion, froze when he heard this.

"Truly?"

Fu Qinglan nodded slightly. The movement was small, yet exceedingly solemn!

...

After standing in the mountain wind for a while, Luo Chen's dazed spirit from half a year of sudden comprehension cleared somewhat.

He returned to the cave abode, bathed and changed clothes, burned incense, and sat down.

His gray-white hair was only tied behind him with a wooden hairpin. He did not form any hand seals; his mind sank into the Purple Mansion at once.

The first scene that entered his eyes was the wrinkled Nascent Soul sitting beside a faint flame.

The former Nascent Soul had skin white as snow and a delicate body.

How could it compare to the current Nascent Soul, covered in wrinkles, exactly like a true newborn infant?

If someone who did not understand the inside story saw this, they would think Luo Chen had suffered cultivation deviation and that his Dao attainment had greatly regressed.

But when Luo Chen saw this scene, he smiled from the bottom of his heart.

"Though I do not know whether this cultivation method applies to others, it suits me best."

"In the end, I comprehended it!"

He opened the attribute panel.

The original cultivation art entry, "Great Perfection Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture," had now changed.

Replacing it was the name Luo Chen had newly given it: "Withering-Bloom Dao Scripture[TERM?], Beginner 50/100."

Naming it a Dao Scripture was somewhat exaggerated, but it carried Luo Chen's hopes: with this true scripture, he would seek the Great Dao!

Especially since Luo Chen had indeed found the direction of the Great Dao in that half year of sudden comprehension.

This self-created cultivation art took the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture as its vigorous foundation, fused in the essence of the Flame Sovereign True Scripture and Eastern Jia Li Fire Scripture regarding meridian and aperture circulation routes, and also borrowed the Purple Yang Divine Art's trait of precisely absorbing fire-attribute spiritual qi between heaven and earth.

Not only that, Luo Chen also fused in the Fine Dust Primordial Art he had created years ago, making up for cultivation of the mental state.

He even absorbed some of the Soul-Splitting Art's experience in cultivating the divine soul.

Overall, the Withering-Bloom Dao Scripture was rather mixed and somewhat rough, but it inherited the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture's comprehensiveness. From inside to outside, from qi to spirit, it considered every aspect.

In terms of cultivation speed, its beginner level was comparable to before. And because it considered too much, circulating it was more troublesome and more tiring; on the surface, it was even a little slower.

But Luo Chen was confident that as proficiency increased, these flaws would be perfected one by one, and cultivation speed would far surpass the original!

There was one more point!

When Luo Chen created the cultivation art, because he borrowed the principle of the Withering-Bloom True Flame's changes, he comprehended a spell.

Its name was Withering-Bloom Divine Seal[TERM?]!

Calling it a spell was not quite accurate. This art leaned more toward an obscuring restriction among restrictions, and faintly had the prototype of a formation.

If it truly had to be described, this art was somewhat like the innate demonic art of a powerful demon beast, or the legendary art of divine abilities.

The focus of this art was neither attack nor defense.

Its function was to assist Luo Chen's cultivation!

By means of the Withering-Bloom True Flame, it sealed the magic power inside the Nascent Soul, preventing it from leaking out at ordinary times.

Not only that, within the seal, the Withering-Bloom True Flame would also constantly temper the impurities in the magic power within the Nascent Soul's body.

It could be said that Luo Chen's current physical changes were all caused by this art.

At ordinary times, the Withering-Bloom Divine Seal would seal away most of his magic power, causing him to remain in a half-cultivating state even when he did not circulate his art.

If he encountered battle and released this art, Luo Chen would not only return to peak condition, but temporarily obtain even stronger magic power.

In Luo Chen's estimate, the longer the Withering-Bloom Divine Seal remained sealed, the stronger the eruption after releasing it would be.

On the surface, this art had outstanding strengths and also enormous flaws.

After all, no Sage would be willing to remain in a half-weakened state all the time. If an enemy were encountered in haste and there was no time to release the seal, the consequences would be unthinkable.

But for Luo Chen, this flaw was roughly equal to nothing.

Because apart from his enormous magic power, he also had an even higher body-refining realm!

There was no counterattack faster than the body's instinct.

The benefits of half a year of sudden comprehension seemed not to end there!

"How did my realm improve so quickly?"

Looking at the realm column, where the progress bar had already approached the second level of Sage and reached ninety, Luo Chen was secretly shocked.

He remembered that during sudden comprehension, he had clearly not cultivated with much attention.

Most of the time, he had been pondering how to create a cultivation art more suitable for himself.

"Could it be caused by that state?"

Luo Chen remembered that when he had comprehended the method of forming a Nascent Soul during the Adept stage, he had also drawn down the infusion of heaven and earth's spiritual qi.

Both were sudden comprehension. Perhaps...

"No, this time I did not draw much infusion of heaven and earth's spiritual qi, though I did always feel the outside spiritual qi gently merging into me."

Luo Chen could not understand how outside spiritual qi had actively merged into his body without his circulating a cultivation art.

After carefully thinking for half a day, he still had no answer.

Luo Chen could only give up.

Or rather, he was already used to it.

The path of cultivation was like this. Many things, at one's current realm, were understood in what they were but not why they were.

Often, only after reaching a higher realm and looking back would one suddenly understand that things had been like this and that back then.

At that time, cultivators moved by feeling would stand at a higher angle to write books and expound doctrines, creating cultivation arts for later generations to practice.

Later generations cultivated step by step according to the arts. Even if they did not understand, they would still gradually reach the height of their predecessors.

Broadly speaking, this was so-called inheritance!

After Luo Chen accepted the current situation, he quickly decided on his next cultivation plan.

That was: not cultivating!

His realm had improved too quickly. Even though the foundation seemed very solid, his heart still felt somewhat uneasy.

Regarding the cultivation art, Luo Chen also felt it could be adjusted a little more.

Therefore, there was no hurry to improve. Stabilizing his realm came first.

...

With no hurry to cultivate, Luo Chen's days suddenly became leisurely.

After meeting the Fu grandfather and granddaughter and explaining that his strength had not been affected, they still had not believed him, so Luo Chen revealed a small move.

One signature Azure Yang Great Handprint remained incomparably powerful, barely allowing them to set their minds at ease.

Luo Chen was not eager to prove himself. In any case, he knew where he stood.

Apart from adjusting his cultivation art, he placed the extra time into alchemy.

Whether Hongyuan Pill or Great Soul Pill, their proficiencies had previously been stuck at the Perfect level.

The reason for being stuck had nothing to do with Luo Chen's alchemy. It was purely because he did not have enough medicinal materials.

After Fu Chaosheng delivered a large amount of resources last time, Luo Chen could open the furnace again and raise proficiency.

He calculated the time very well.

By the time he raised proficiency to the Grandmaster level, his realm would also be almost stabilized.

When he resumed cultivation then, he would have high-grade Hongyuan Pills and high-grade Great Soul Pills as support, and his realm could again soar rapidly.

Everything was orderly, relaxed, and beautiful!

Perhaps because she was not at ease with Luo Chen's condition, while Luo Chen refined pills behind closed doors, Fu Qinglan visited cave abode Seventy-Eight more and more frequently.

The time she stayed also grew longer and longer.

The way the two interacted gradually changed from initial unfamiliarity to increasing familiarity.

Luo Chen did not reject this way of interacting. He knew the Tianyuan Dao Sect's Tianyuan Grand Competition was by no means as simple as imagined.

According to historical records, the trip to Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven had never had a cultivator die, but if one wanted abundant returns, trust between participant and Dao protector was indispensable.

Luo Chen was also using this opportunity to cultivate mutual understanding with the other party.

As for how to cultivate it?

By playing go!

Inside the cave abode, between the inches of the go board, the man moved with great difficulty while the woman was calm and unhurried.

"I push!"

"I press! No, you should extend here. If you do not extend, how will you kill your way out?"

"You are pressing too hard. I am surrounded on all sides, and every side is an ambush."

"Then cross over. Did I not teach you the board-crossing method? That copy of Spring Mountain Hall Game Records also records seven detailed solutions for this situation."

"Naturally, I remember them clearly. But very obviously, you have buried another follow-up move here, right?"

"You actually saw it."

The woman smiled slightly and placed a stone, instantly forming a dragon-slaying momentum.

Luo Chen sighed helplessly, tossed down the stone in his hand, and frankly declared defeat.

He had discovered that this woman might lack the bearing of a Sage in handling people and affairs, but that was only due to lack of tempering.

On the go path, however, she was rather confident, with the killing bearing of someone who could decide life and death.

If this bearing could be transformed into her way of acting, she would not be merely someone who talked strategy on paper.

While Fu Qinglan counted the stones, she asked in a seemingly casual manner, "Regarding those twelve opponents, has Dao Brother studied them?"

Luo Chen smiled slightly and said with confidence, "I have roughly read all the intelligence and made analyses."

"Lin Bufan's Dao protector Lin Jingtang[TERM?] was born a servant of the Lin family. He did not enter the Tianyuan Dao Sect and has long traveled the outside continents. He formed his Nascent Soul at four hundred seventy-eight, has ordinary aptitude, outstanding battle ability, and possesses a true artifact called the Fright Bell..."

"Yu Xu's Dao protector is the master of a declining great teaching in the Southern Frontier, the Heavenly Mountain Cult, who uses the teaching as his title and is called Sage Heavenly Mountain[TERM?]. He has only recently formed his Nascent Soul, so logically he is not worth fearing. But to cultivate to Sage in a place like the Southern Frontier, he must have something unique. I will be careful of the dao of gu insects."

"Great House disciple Gu Yuan[TERM?]..."

At this name, Luo Chen paused, seeming to remember some familiar place in his memory.

Fu Qinglan waited for what came next.

Luo Chen smiled softly and continued in detail.

"His Dao protector is Mou Jianxiong of the Mou family among the Thirty-Six Aristocratic Clans. This person cannot be underestimated. Though born from an Aristocratic Clan, he received the Ancient Gu Great House's teaching from childhood. However, this person excels in water arts. I am confident that by the principle of water and fire restraining each other, I can suppress him instead."

"The Dao protector of the Shi family participant has somewhat unclear intelligence..."

Luo Chen spoke methodically, his words brimming with confidence.

As Fu Qinglan listened, her heart relaxed.

The other party was not as indifferent as he appeared on the surface. In truth, he had already memorized all opponents as much as possible.

At the end.

Luo Chen said, "Of course, these are only intelligence on paper. The specific situation must still be analyzed specifically. If I could see these people in person, or if there were recorded images of them acting, I would have even more confidence."

Fu Qinglan nodded in agreement.

"That is true. After all, intelligence may also have been deliberately spread by them to mislead others."

Suddenly, her words turned.

"Right now, there is an opportunity for you to see these people."

"Oh?" Luo Chen raised a brow, interested.

Since arriving in Central Continent, he truly had not interacted much with the Sages here.

Even among the outside-continent cultivators on Heaven-Earth Peak, apart from that late-stage Sage elder, he had barely interacted with anyone else.

If there was now an opportunity to get to know one or two of them, he did not mind.

"Tomorrow, the Peach Garden[TERM?] will open, and Eminent Daoyin will challenge Chess Saint Ke Lianshan. At that time, the four directions will surely stir, and gathered heroes will assemble. Dao Brother might as well come with me and take the chance to glimpse the depths of those opponents."

The Peach Garden was Chess Saint Ke Lianshan's cultivation ground.

Compared with those opponents, Luo Chen was more interested in this legendary Chess Saint.

An existence described as a saint must surely have reached the peak of a certain path, just like Chu Yan, the Alchemy Saint.

Especially since that Chess Saint was also a late-stage Sage great cultivator!

To be honest, after coming to Central Continent, Luo Chen had found it rather hard to adjust.

In the Eastern Wilderness and Northern Sea, Sages were rarely seen. In Central Continent, they seemed to be everywhere.

When going out, one might raise or lower one's head and encounter a Sage.

This made the pride he had finally obtained after reaching this realm vanish completely.

Of course, he also knew this was not the normal situation.

Central Continent was originally the place where the essence of the Mountains and Seas Realm gathered.

After ancient times, it was also the only peaceful land that had not suffered demon-beast threats.

With several thousand years of accumulation, such foundation was indeed normal.

"I have been cooped up here for too long as well. Then let us make the trip!"

Luo Chen stood and stretched lazily as he spoke.

"Good. I will make arrangements today and request an invitation for Dao Brother!"

Fu Qinglan happily left Luo Chen's cave abode.

Looking at her cheerful appearance, Luo Chen could not help shaking his head. He did not know whether she was happy that he had taken her matter seriously, or happy that she would witness a grand go event.

*(End of Chapter)*
