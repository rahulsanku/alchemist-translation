# Chapter 1050: Meeting Luo Chen Again, A God Appears on the Icy Sea

A streak of fire flashed across the sky.

The next moment!

*Thump!*

On a nameless little island, a black-robed cultivator stood silently, the ground beneath his feet as undisturbed as before.

To come to such a sudden halt at such tremendous speed without causing any damage to the surroundings—this demonstrated the man's ultimate mastery over both his spiritual power and physical body.

This person was none other than Luo Chen.

He had already left the Western Continent in order to capture the *xiao* qi, and was not far from the Tiannan Continent. After receiving Xiao Bai's transmission, he had unleashed his full Voidburst Fire Escape. In just two or three short days, he had traversed the continent and arrived at this sea region.

During these days of travel, Luo Chen had gained a new understanding of this escape technique, which combined his Voidburst Step and Fire Escape Art.

In terms of pure speed alone, it was likely unmatched in the world.

Its only flaw was probably that it could not be used consecutively; it required several breaths as a buffer. It could not achieve the seamless, frequent void-shifting of Azure Frost's Void Escape Divine Art.

"Still, it's enough. The buffer gap, supplemented by Moon Step, won't leave too big a flaw."

Luo Chen chuckled lightly, his gaze sweeping the surroundings.

According to Xiao Bai's transmission, the two had agreed to meet near this area.

He had arrived now, but where was Xiao Bai?

As Luo Chen's gaze turned behind him, it suddenly froze.

A woman in a green dress came into view, her eyes shimmering with a hint of surprise.

"It really is you!"

Luo Chen was momentarily silent, but then he broke into a carefree smile, cupping his hands in a Daoist salute. "Fellow Daoist Fu, you've been well, I trust!"

Azure Frost Fairy looked at this polite greeting, and her originally joyful mood became somewhat complicated.

"I have been well, naturally. But you, Fellow Daoist Luo, have had a very winding path. Ever since you left the Central Continent, news of you has come one after another. You made a name for yourself in the Eastern Wilderness under the banner of the Pill Sect, fought alongside the demoness Azure Frost against the heroes of the Western Desert, and I've heard even the Southern Frontier has seen your shadow. If we count your experiences in the Northern Sea and Central Continent, one could say you've shaken the Five Continents, the one and only in the Mountains and Seas Realm."

"Haha, just a bit of wind and frost, nothing so tortuous about it."

Luo Chen's expression was cheerful, as if he cared nothing for the life-and-death crises he had encountered.

But the more he acted this way, the more complicated Azure Frost Fairy's mood became.

"Did he not even notice the change in how I addressed him?"

The woman's heart felt a little sour. She remembered how her attitude towards him had undergone a constant process of change, evident in her forms of address. From the initial polite 'Fellow Daoist Luo,' to 'Brother Luo,' and she had even called him 'Big Brother Luo' at one point. But now, having reverted to the initial, polite form of address, the other party showed no change in expression at all, completely indifferent.

It was very clear that even the experience of sharing hardships with her in the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven was, in his heart, merely another trial of wind and frost.

Since that was the case, Azure Frost Fairy no longer dwelled on the past. Her expression suddenly turned serious.

"That black dragon that attacked Zhenhai City—it's your tamed beast, isn't it!"

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow, then nodded slightly.

Receiving the affirmative answer, Azure Frost Fairy bit her red lip. "Forty years ago, was it you who did the deed at the God-Burial Valley?"

Luo Chen was stunned for a moment. He had thought his subduing of the Kui Niu Ghost God at the God-Burial Valley was quite secret, and hadn't expected Fu Qinglan to recognize him.

But Luo Chen disdained to pretend otherwise and nodded again, frankly.

Faced with his lack of denial or explanation, Azure Frost Fairy's face finally showed anger.

"First, you broke the ghost god's seal, and then you ordered your tamed beast, the black dragon, to charge Zhenhai City. Luo Chen, what exactly are you trying to do to my Heavenly Origin Dao Sect?"

Luo Chen finally spoke.

His originally carefree face now showed an anger equal to Azure Frost Fairy's.

"What can a man with a broken life soul like me possibly do to your esteemed sect?"

This counter-question left Azure Frost Fairy stunned.

She was not an ignorant fool. Ever since she had become a disciple under the great power Chun Yuan, she had learned the details of the Grotto-Heaven Grand Competition.

At that time, all the Dao protectors who entered the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, especially those from outside continents, had a sliver of their life soul intercepted by the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard. It was done silently, leaving no trace, and would not have much impact on the Dao protectors at the time. But that missing wisp of life soul would affect a cultivator's breakthrough to the Ascendant realm! This was a precautionary measure taken by the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect to prevent the opportunities within the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven from falling into the hands of others.

But the Fu family had not known about this before, and had even concealed the fact that the time flow in the grotto-heaven was different, which would greatly consume the Dao protectors' lifespans.

These two matters were enough to fill Luo Chen with anger.

Azure Frost Fairy opened her mouth, wanting to explain, but the anger on Luo Chen's face across from her came and went quickly, and even his gaze shifted to the side.

*Whoosh!*

A spray of foam burst open, and a dim light flew out, manifesting into the figure of an enchanting woman.

"Master, Black King seems to have been controlled by someone. He doesn't remember you or me at all. I tried to call out to him with a transmission, but he ignored me completely, just burying his head and fleeing north."

Luo Chen took a deep breath. "He won't escape!"

Having said this, he turned and left, as if he had no desire to say anything more to Fu Qinglan.

Xiao Bai curiously glanced at Azure Frost Fairy before quickly following behind Luo Chen.

Behind them, Azure Frost Fairy, on the other hand, became somewhat anxious.

"Luo Chen, listen to me. As long as you are willing to return to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect with me. After sixty years, I will definitely help you retrieve your life soul!"

The black-robed man's heart paused for a moment, but his steps did not stop.

"Believe me, I am a true disciple of the Dao Sect and the master of the Houtu Hall. I have a say in the sect."

The black-robed man gave a light snort but still walked forward through the air.

"You have to tell me, did my grandfather leave any last words before he fell!"

Fu Chaosheng had fallen in the Nether Origin Sect. He had gone there specifically for Luo Chen, and the only person he might have seen before his fall would have been Luo Chen.

This time, Luo Chen's steps did stop, but he did not turn around. Only a faint voice drifted over.

"He was very proud of you."

"Is that all?"

There was no response. Only a dull, heavy boom was heard from the spot, and with a flicker of sparks, Luo Chen had already disappeared from where he stood. When he reappeared, he was already dozens of *li* away.

Such an astonishing escape technique would have surely drawn a gasp of surprise from Azure Frost Fairy in the past.

But at this moment, the woman in green just stood blankly on the small island, letting the sea breeze blow her black hair, her expression one of melancholy.

...

"Master, that woman has a deep connection with you!"

In the howling wind, Xiao Bai's voice came from beside his ear, sounding a bit indistinct.

Luo Chen's expression was calm, but ripples stirred in his heart.

His connection with Fu Qinglan was more than just deep?

Because of her, he had come to the Central Continent, entered the Star Gate, gained the favor of the Alchemy Saint who bestowed upon him the Heavenly Secret Pill, thus laying the foundation for his later comprehension of the Withering-Bloom True Intent. Even the *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon* he was now painstakingly cultivating was a gift from that woman to compensate for the lifespan he had lost.

But it was also because of her that he had fallen into the most awkward predicament, forever unable to take that final step before the Ascendant realm.

The gratitude and resentment between them were too complicated to explain in a few words.

This meeting should be the last one between friends. The next time they met, it would likely be a life-and-death struggle.

His earlier distant behavior was also intentional on Luo Chen's part, to avoid being soft-hearted when the time came to strike.

"Forget it!"

Luo Chen sighed, pulled himself together, and asked, "Where is Black King?"

Xiao Bai quickly replied, "He was probably injured by that woman just now, and now he's fleeing for his life towards the Northern Sea. I left a soul-guide on him. Based on the connection, he's not far from us. With your master's miraculous escape technique, we can catch up in at most half an incense stick's time."

Having said this, Xiao Bai's face showed a puzzled expression.

"Master, Black King has your slave mark on him. You should still have his soul blood. How come you can't sense his location?"

Luo Chen's face turned cold. "Didn't you just say it? He's suspected of being controlled by someone. That person behind the scenes even covered up the slave mark I placed on him. How could I possibly sense him?"

Xiao Bai was visibly shocked.

She knew better than anyone how strong her master's soul foundation and his attainments in soul prohibitions were.

Even if the slave seal he had placed on Black King back then was only at the early Sage stage, being able to cover up the slave seal on Black King meant that the soul foundation of the person behind this was definitely not weak.

That person behind the scenes was probably a bit terrifying!

Luo Chen's expression was grim as he led Xiao Bai, continuously using his Voidburst Fire Escape.

On the vast, boundless sea, only sparks flickered as they headed north.

Just as Xiao Bai had estimated, after half an incense stick's time, Luo Chen's heart stirred slightly, and he revealed himself from the void.

His gaze fell upon the sea, where he could faintly see a black shadow fleeing at a leisurely pace.

"Master, Black King is down there!"

"I know."

"Uh... Master, aren't you going to drag him out?"

Luo Chen snorted coldly. "Why drag him out now? Since we've found him, bringing him back under my command is a trivial matter. Right now, I'm more curious about who it is that could cover up my slave mark, forcibly control Black King, and even have the memories related to us blocked by the other party."

Xiao Bai's eyes lit up. "Master, you mean...?"

"Follow him!"

Luo Chen slowed his escape speed, trailing far behind Black King, even concealing his own aura.

His eyes narrowed as he looked at the black shadow deep in the sea, calculating some thoughts in his mind.

He not only wanted to see who had controlled Black King, but also wanted to know where that person got the audacity to order Black King and a group of demon beasts to attack the Tiannan Continent.

Although the Tiannan Continent was the weakest continent after the collapse of the Central Continent, it was still the territory of the Dao Sect.

In the current era, after two of the five sacred lands had been destroyed, one sealed, and one closed, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect was still the strongest major power in the Mountains and Seas Realm.

Moreover, with the profound foundation passed down from ancient times, even Ascendant great powers would not dare to provoke it easily.

And now, not only the Tiannan Continent, but also the Libei and Dongyuan Continents had been reporting successive disturbances by demon beasts. It was very clear that someone was deliberately targeting the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

"Going north... the Northern Sea... Could it be the two ancient demons rumored to be in the Northern Sea?"

At this thought, Luo Chen's fingers unconsciously stroked his sleeve.

The Million Souls Banner was within!

...

Time passed quickly.

Luo Chen followed Black King's trail, spending three months crossing the Tiannan and Libei Continents, traveling day and night without rest.

Such a vast distance made Luo Chen's guess in his heart increasingly close to the truth.

If he went further, it would be the Northern Sea cultivation world.

"Master, isn't that Black King guy tired? He's injured, and he's been traveling for so long without stopping."

"You underestimate him. With his desolate beast body combined with his indestructible physique, plus the large number of demon beasts he's been devouring along the way, his injuries have long since recovered."

As Black King's former master, Luo Chen knew that fool's background inside and out.

Although Fu Qinglan had progressed rapidly, advancing to the mid-Sage stage in just a hundred years since their parting, Black King was no pushover either. Back then, Luo Chen had invested who knows how many precious resources into Black King, laying a foundation for him that far surpassed any demon beast.

He had never lacked the Imperial Nectar that opened his spiritual wisdom and consolidated his foundation. Various flesh and blood resources were also supplied at any time. Coupled with devouring the remnant soul of the Stone Dragon and advancing to the fourth-rank desolate beast, Black King's foundation was extremely deep.

It was just that the guy was too lazy, always wanting to sleep in the past. Otherwise, his current achievements would not be merely the mid-fourth rank.

The injuries from Fu Qinglan's palm strike were enough for Black King to recover from in three months.

But he still hadn't stopped, clearly because he was scared and didn't want to fight anymore, only wanting to return to his current "master's" side.

It seemed that this new master could give him a great sense of security!

Just as Luo Chen was thinking this, Xiao Bai's puzzled voice came from beside him.

"Why is there so much fog?"

Luo Chen's heart skipped a beat, and he grabbed Xiao Bai.

"Attach yourself to my body. Don't get separated."

Then, Luo Chen bit his fingertip and smeared a drop of vital blood over his eyes.

The next moment, blood vessels spread, golden light blazed in his eyes, and his Crimson-Eyed Golden Pupils reappeared.

Luo Chen looked out. On the vast sea, the vast, misty fog faintly revealed some special patterns.

"What a brilliant formation!"

Hearing this, Xiao Bai, who was attached to Luo Chen's back, couldn't help but feel a tightening in her heart.

For a formation grandmaster like her master to praise it so highly showed how dangerous this place was.

"Who in the world could set up such a formation?"

Luo Chen pursed his lips, directly overturning his previous guess, and another guess emerged in his mind.

"Could it be that one?"

He took a step forward, confidently and calmly appearing in another place where the fog was thick.

As soon as he landed, without stopping, Luo Chen stepped into the void again, appearing in another place.

And so it went, Luo Chen's figure flickering continuously.

Until a patch of cold, frosty mist appeared, Luo Chen pointed his fingers like a sword and stabbed forward.

*Puff!*

It was as if a bubble had burst. Heaven and earth suddenly overturned, and the surrounding fog rolled away.

In the blink of an eye, Luo Chen appeared on a sheet of ice.

Looking around, he felt a sense of familiarity.

A scene of fierce battle emerged in his memory.

"This place is... the Ice Pole Sect?"

The moment this thought appeared, Luo Chen's eyes suddenly looked into the distance.

In the distance, where the ice stretched, a scholar with a weathered, refined temperament was holding a fishing rod, leisurely fishing in the icy sea.

As if feeling Luo Chen's gaze, the weathered scholar curiously turned his head.

"Oh? Someone actually managed to break through my Mist-Hiding Spirit-Bewildering Formation?"

In his surprise, he met Luo Chen's eyes.

"Young friend, those eyes of yours are quite formidable!"

Luo Chen took a deep breath, neither humble nor arrogant as he said, "This junior, Luo Chen, pays respects to Senior Li!"

"Luo Chen?!"

The weathered scholar muttered this name, and the fishing rod in his hand trembled.

In an instant.

The ice surface shattered, and a huge black dragon soared into the air, crashing down onto the ice.

Luo Chen's gaze fell upon the black dragon, and he said calmly, "That is me. And this dragon is also my spiritual pet!"

As soon as these words were spoken, the weathered scholar's eyes instantly narrowed.
