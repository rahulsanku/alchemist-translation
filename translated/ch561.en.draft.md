# Chapter 561 — A Common Man Is Innocent; Possessing Jade Is His Crime

Blood Nightmare Moluo!

The master of Moluo Stream, one of the Northern Sea’s top demonic-path powers!

Although that was only in name.

Many high-level cultivators knew that Moluo Stream was an alliance formed by twelve Sage-level cultivators. Strictly speaking, it could not be considered an inherited sect.

Most of those within it were early-stage Sages, but it also had a late-stage Sage great cultivator holding down the fort.

The reason the faction had been named after Blood Nightmare Moluo, and why he was publicly placed at its head, was merely because they were borrowing his reputation as a member of the Primordial Demon Sect.

With that title, Moluo Stream, a rising power that had emerged over the past century, could justifiably participate in the Northern Sea cultivation world’s struggles over various resources.

After all, before the Primordial Demon Sect’s destruction, the entire human cultivation world of the Northern Sea had been under its jurisdiction.

Of course, if ordinary people thought Blood Nightmare Moluo was merely a puppet, they would be badly mistaken.

Which of those who could reach the Sage realm was not an outstanding genius? Which of them did not possess a firm Dao heart, having endured countless hardships before finally reaching that step?

At the very least, Luo Chen would not look down on Blood Nightmare Moluo in the slightest because of those rumors.

Luo Chen had always treated Sage-level cultivators with reverence.

That was because, throughout his journey, none of the Sage-level cultivators he had known had been easy to deal with.

Even the five Sages who had seemed unable to withstand a single blow beneath the hands of the top demon emperor Azure Frost had each possessed their own hidden supports.

Pure Crane needed no further mention.

As for Cloud Crane, the fact that he had subdued Pure Crane while only at the Core Formation stage, regardless of whatever hidden circumstances there might have been, was enough to prove his ability. Moreover, he had even been permitted by the Joyous Union Sect to leave the sect and establish his own power.

Jade Tripod was a sword dao expert. With only an early Sage cultivation base, he had faced Azure Frost Demon Emperor and forced the other party to chase him tens of thousands of li before finally barely managing to kill him.

Heaven Smelter was a technical specialist, but his battle prowess could not be underestimated at all. Even without any magical treasures, under an absolute disadvantage, he had clashed head-on with the Thunder Prison Divine Peng and had nearly carved out a bloody path of survival.

Lastly, there was Han Zhan.

Among the five Sages involved in the Howling Moon Mountain Range development war, he could be considered the one with the lowest realm and the shortest cultivation time.

Yet in several battles, all manner of methods had emerged from him one after another. He was absolutely not someone an ordinary Core Formation cultivator could look down upon.

Aside from raw strength, the schemes and calculations of these Sages were also worthy of their long cultivation lives, each measured in centuries.

During his time on Green Parasol Mountain, Luo Chen had deeply experienced the calculations of Heaven Smelter and Han Zhan.

One of them had used Luo Chen’s lifebound magical treasure as the focus of his plan to seize a slim chance of survival. Although he had ultimately failed, it was still enough to inspire awe.

The other was old and cunning. His method of feigning death to extend his life had escaped even the perception of the Thunder Prison Divine Peng, who was a minor realm higher than him, and he had concealed his strength several times.

It could be said that among the five Sages back then, aside from Pure Crane, who had directly surrendered, Han Zhan, supposedly the weakest Sage, had instead become the only one to leave Green Parasol Mountain and preserve his life!

Even now, Luo Chen still maintained some wariness toward him.

And all of this was merely Luo Chen’s surface-level understanding of them.

How they calculated in private, and what their true styles of action were like, he did not know at all.

After experiencing such matters, how could Luo Chen dare, like ignorant low-level cultivators, to recklessly speculate about experts a major realm above him?

Thus, after learning that Blood Nightmare Moluo had personally come to visit him, Luo Chen was extremely shocked.

He immediately pressed Cheng Haixin for the details of what had happened.

However, the woman said helplessly that Blood Nightmare Moluo had arrived abruptly. Since Luo Chen had not been present, he had left just as decisively.

“Perhaps True Person Blood Nightmare was delighted to encounter a surviving fellow sect member, and that was why he came personally,” Cheng Haixin said softly and cautiously while looking at Luo Chen.

Fellow sect member?

Luo Chen frowned. Could it be because of the Primordial Demon Sect cultivator identity he had deliberately disguised himself with?

If that was truly the case, it barely made sense.

After Wu Qi left back then, Luo Chen had specially sent people to collect information about Blood Nightmare Moluo’s deeds.

As an Ascendant holy land, the Primordial Demon Sect possessed an ancient and far-reaching inheritance, and internally, it was divided into three lineages.

They were the Soul Refining lineage, the Blood God lineage, and the Primordial Demon lineage.

Blood Nightmare Moluo was an outstanding figure of the Blood God lineage. With the rank of elder, he had governed a region outside the sect, and because of that, he had escaped the calamity of the Primordial Demon Sect’s destruction.

This person was gentle by temperament and treated his subordinates extremely well.

After the Primordial Demon Sect’s destruction, he also made great efforts to gather the sect members who had fled and were wandering outside, attempting to restore the sect’s glory.

Over the years, many fugitive Primordial Demon Sect disciples had voluntarily placed themselves under his command.

Precisely because of this, among the twelve stream masters of Moluo Stream, the strength of his faction was not weak at all. It was even second only to that late-stage Sage great cultivator behind the scenes.

In the eyes of those with insight, the claim that he was a puppet was nothing more than rumor.

Back when Luo Chen stood before Wu Qi, he had been at the early Core Formation stage, and the battle power he displayed was not the slightest bit inferior to Wu Qi’s. It was very much in line with the level of a direct disciple from an Ascendant holy land.

Combined with the Soul-Refining Banner, it was not surprising that the other party had concluded he was a disciple of the Primordial Demon Sect’s Soul Refining lineage.

Of course, Luo Chen had never verbally acknowledged this identity!

“That is one aspect. Another likely reason is that he has taken interest in my identity as an alchemy master!”

When Wu Qi had come to the island, he had smelled the residual pill fragrance left behind from Luo Chen refining top-grade Trueflame Pills.

An existence capable of refining top-grade third-tier pills would be one of the most outstanding talents not merely in the Northern Sea, but even across all five continents of the Mountain and Sea Realm.

Hadn’t Wu Qi said that among the twelve stream masters, one Sage who specialized in alchemy had only been lucky enough to refine a high-grade third-tier pill?

In that case, even if Luo Chen himself was not a member of the Primordial Demon Sect, he would still be worth Blood Nightmare Moluo making this trip personally.

“Perhaps he did more than merely make one trip.”

With a thought, a token fell into Luo Chen’s hand.

It was the Blood Nightmare Token!

His divine sense covered it, and he sensed a smear of blood-red light flowing within it, as if alive.

Han Zhan’s divine-sense voice transmission entered his ears.

“Someone indeed activated this token before. However, before we set out, I used a secret technique to conceal it, so the other party was unable to locate you through the token.”

Luo Chen nodded slightly.

That was good news.

At the very least, before he met Blood Nightmare Moluo, he would have enough buffer time.

“Are you going to meet him?” Han Zhan asked.

When Cheng Haixin had described Blood Nightmare Moluo’s visit earlier, she had mentioned the message he left behind: he wanted to meet Luo Chen in Feileng City.

Counting the days, it happened to be the ten-year period Wu Qi had mentioned.

Luo Chen thought for a while, then replied in a somewhat low voice, “I’ll see how things are.”

Han Zhan chuckled. “You brat, you really aren’t afraid of dealing with higher-realm cultivators.”

The corner of Luo Chen’s mouth twitched. He truly was not particularly afraid.

Along his journey, he had never lacked existences whose realms were higher than his own.

Although interacting with them was somewhat oppressive, as long as he grasped the proper measure between them, not only would he suffer no loss, he would instead gain enormously.

As long as he did not directly anger the other party, a technical talent like him would be welcomed wherever he went.

Or rather, many technical talents skilled in alchemy and talisman-making were naturally supposed to rely on great powers.

By contrast, his previous behavior of venturing alone overseas to hunt demons was the unusual path.

Seeing Luo Chen’s indifferent appearance, Han Zhan suddenly reminded him, “Demonic-path experts are different from the righteous-path cultivators of our Eastern Wilderness. Their temperaments are volatile, and their styles of action are also strange and difficult to predict. If the other party means you harm, I may not necessarily be able to protect you.”

Luo Chen was startled.

The meaning behind Han Zhan’s words seemed to be that he did not want Luo Chen to make contact with Blood Nightmare Moluo!

Luo Chen was somewhat puzzled and merely assumed the other party was worried about him.

However, from those words, Luo Chen also tasted another piece of information.

Han Zhan truly did have some trump cards, enough for him to casually say something like “protect you.”

Not necessarily—that meant there was at least some possibility!

The corner of Luo Chen’s mouth rose slightly. He did not insist on getting to the bottom of it. Instead, he withdrew his attention and began asking Cheng Haixin about the other matters that had occurred in the Swallow Islands while he was away.

Under Cheng Haixin’s detailed explanation, the specifics slowly unfolded.

As for collecting medicinal ingredients, the work had continued all along.

However, it was very obvious that the longer Luo Chen had been gone, the fewer medicinal ingredients had been collected.

This meant the other clans had become somewhat negligent.

This was within expectations, and Luo Chen was not surprised.

After all, a single Core Formation secret method was far from enough to make every cultivator bankrupt themselves over it.

Especially for some smaller clans, which only had one or two early- or mid-stage Foundation Establishment cultivators within them, they had not yet reached the point of losing their heads over Core Formation.

Cheng Haixin’s words were somewhat severe.

Judging from the implication in her tone, it was probably related to the Cheng clan’s status being threatened, which was why she was deliberately targeting the other clans to a certain degree.

Seeing that Luo Chen did not care much, Cheng Haixin’s heart dimmed, and the despondency on her face deepened.

“My eldest brother failed his Core Formation breakthrough.”

Luo Chen was taken aback. That ambitious man had failed Core Formation?

This was somewhat unexpected.

What was unexpected was not the failure itself, but that the other party had truly been so impatient to attempt Core Formation.

Calculating the time, Cheng Dou had only obtained the Qingyang Pill Canon a few years ago. At most, he could have cultivated it to minor achievement, yet he had rushed to form his core in such haste. Failure had been obvious.

After all, the Core Formation threshold was rather difficult to cross.

Among the Foundation Establishment peers Luo Chen had once known, whether it was the loose cultivator Chu Kui, who had planned for many years, or the true sect disciple Tantai Jin, both had fallen short at the Core Formation pass. Because of this, Chu Kui’s realm had regressed, while Tantai Jin had perished in death seclusion.

Cheng Dou’s foundation was not even comparable to Chu Kui or Tantai, so his failure was only natural.

Luo Chen merely said softly, “My condolences.”

Cheng Haixin tugged at the corner of her mouth and squeezed out a bitter smile.

“Life and death are determined by fate. Perhaps my elder brother was destined to have no connection with the Great Dao. Others cannot be blamed for it.”

When a person died, their Dao vanished. No matter how great their ambition or how many schemes they had, all of it became the past.

Luo Chen murmured in acknowledgment and casually asked who was currently in charge of the Cheng clan on Black Swan Island. The answer he received was Cheng Haixin.

However, the specific mundane affairs were being handled by the elder Cheng Ji. Cheng Haixin merely bore the position of clan head.

“In this way, it is also more convenient for this concubine to serve Your Excellency,” Cheng Haixin said with her head lowered, expressing her loyalty.

Luo Chen looked her over with faint amusement. In this atmosphere, the woman’s tall figure actually seemed rather pitiful and delicate.

Was this what happened after taking charge of a household and learning how expensive firewood, rice, oil, and salt truly were?

So now, in both words and actions, she had become even more respectful than before?

She even knew to deliberately curry favor with him and say pleasing words.

Luo Chen chuckled softly and waved his hand.

“You may withdraw first. I’ve hurried back over a long journey and need to rest for a while.”

Cheng Haixin raised her head, appearing as though she wanted to speak but stopped herself. In the end, she lowered her head and withdrew from the great hall.

After she left, Han Zhan’s voice sounded within the hall.

“You’ve always been generous. Since she already sold misery to you, won’t you give her some benefits?”

“Heh. Have I given too few benefits?”

“That’s true. You allowed her to advance to Foundation Establishment Stage Five in just a little over ten years and even bestowed a suitable water-element magical treasure upon her. Even a direct disciple of a great sect would receive treatment no better than that.”

“Exactly. I’ve already given enough benefits. If I give more, she herself won’t be able to swallow them. A common man is innocent; possessing jade is his crime. If she has too many benefits, she may instead invite calamity.”

At this point, the voice in the hall paused.

Only after a long while did a faint sigh drift out.

“After all, this region has too few resources. In the end, I must leave.”

…

After returning to the Swallow Islands, Luo Chen rested for a full half month before he finally adjusted himself.

During this half month, he first slept for two days, replenishing his mental energy.

After that, he began treating his injuries!

The sudden battle with Golden Claw had only inflicted flesh wounds, but throughout the long journey afterward, he had had no time to properly deal with them. He had relied entirely on his powerful recovery ability to heal automatically.

Now that he had free time, Luo Chen used both medical techniques and miraculous pills. In barely half a month, he had recovered completely.

Such speed left Han Zhan extremely surprised.

What Han Zhan did not know was that Luo Chen’s physical body was not merely as tyrannical as it appeared on the surface. Internally, because the Withering Glory True Fire had refined many demon beasts, it possessed vast vitality.

Even if he had not yet reached the point of rebirth from a drop of blood or regrowing severed limbs, he already far surpassed ordinary body refiners.

The injuries caused by a top third-tier great demon king were nothing more than this.

After his wounds recovered, Luo Chen also began normal cultivation.

Carefully counting, it had only been about a year since he advanced to Core Formation Stage Four and stepped into the middle stage.

However, during this year, because he had been collecting third-tier demon crab kings, he had not cultivated seriously. At the time, the situation had been complicated, and there had not been a suitable third-tier spirit land.

Now that things were quiet, how could he allow his cultivation to sit idle?

Inside the cultivation chamber.

Dressed in a loose white Daoist robe, Luo Chen sat in the lotus position, five hearts facing the heavens.

His two hands formed opposing spirit seals, and his lips opened and closed.

Surging streams of spiritual energy entered his body from the outside in. Under the operation of the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture, they transformed into absolutely pure fire-element magic power.

Within his qi sea, his crimson golden core spun rapidly.

A small cauldron sat beneath it, unmoving as a mountain.

A feathered wing, like a bird, flew freely and joyfully within his qi sea. Because Luo Chen’s magic power was so abundant, after more than twenty years of refinement, the Moonbreaker Wing had long since shed its impurities. Not only had its grade stabilized at low grade, there were even faint signs that it might rise in grade.

If it could truly advance, that would be another piece of good news for Luo Chen.

It meant his flight speed would increase greatly.

Originally, when flying at full strength, even late third-tier great demon kings were not his match. If it improved again, even late Core Formation human cultivators would not be able to eat his dust.

Of course, all of this was merely a side path.

The most crucial thing was still his own cultivation.

“Huu…”

A white qi dragon emerged from Luo Chen’s mouth. He withdrew his spirit seals and slowly opened his eyes.

There was a trace of dissatisfaction on his face.

For someone who had enjoyed a top-grade third-tier spirit vein before, this barely low-grade third-tier spirit vein on Invitation Moon Island was truly somewhat trash.

The density of spiritual energy that others considered surging only made him feel as though he were having difficulty breathing!

For cultivators, cultivation emphasized wealth, companions, methods, and land.

He possessed a top-tier cultivation method, had the good teacher Han Zhan at his side, and also had the large batches of high-grade Trueflame Pills he had refined. It could be said that his cultivation conditions were almost maxed out.

However, “almost” meant they were not truly complete.

The deficiency lay in “land.”

What kind of immortal did a cultivator cultivate into?

If one broke the character apart, it was merely a person and a mountain.

The mountain was the spirit land.

Yet among the four essentials, he happened to lack the most important spirit land.

It had to be said that this was truly a great regret.

“Under my current conditions, it will take me at least twenty years to cultivate to the fifth stage,” Luo Chen murmured.

Twenty years, for the Luo Chen who had just formed his core back then, would already have been an unimaginable speed.

One had to know that he had spent ten years merely consolidating his realm.

But now, Luo Chen only felt dissatisfied.

The Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture allowed him to specialize in the fire dao, granting him a cultivation speed comparable to a heavenly spirit root.

But cultivators with heavenly spirit roots were said to reach Foundation Establishment in ten years, Core Formation in a hundred years, and Sage in two hundred years.

If he had to spend twenty years at Core Formation Stage Four alone, then how many years would the increasingly difficult minor realms afterward take?

Thirty? Forty? Fifty?

Or even a hundred years?

If every minor realm required a great amount of time, then would all his efforts not have been wasted?

Even if he could cultivate to Core Formation Perfected Completion before his lifespan limit arrived, what about forming a nascent soul?

Luo Chen had always been used to preparing for a rainy day. Whatever he did, he would think ahead in advance.

When he had been at mid Foundation Establishment, he had already begun planning for Core Formation resources.

He did not want to wait until Core Formation Perfected Completion before bitterly searching all over the world for a method to form a nascent soul.

Therefore, there were some matters he had to do in advance.

And to do those things, he naturally had to squeeze out more time beyond cultivation.

“Leaving the Swallow Islands is already inevitable.”

“It isn’t only for a better third-tier spirit land. There are pills as well! I’ve long been able to refine top-grade Trueflame Pills, yet I can still only consume high-grade Trueflame Pills because this place’s impoverished resource environment has restricted me.”

The Swallow Islands were a peaceful cultivation environment, but they very clearly did not meet Luo Chen’s needs.

Even the Luo Tian Sect spirit land he had personally built back in the Howling Moon Mountain Range had a spiritual energy environment close to mid-grade third-tier, let alone the mere Swallow Islands.

However, before leaving, there were still preparations he had to make.

For an alchemist, the preparations for a long journey were naturally pills.

Especially since next, there was a high possibility he would interact with Core Formation cultivators in his identity as an alchemy master. It was necessary to prepare some pills in advance that could display his alchemy skills.

Thus, after Luo Chen came out of seclusion, Invitation Moon Island, which had been calm for a long while, once again became busy with pill refining.

This time, the pills Luo Chen refined were no longer limited to Trueflame Pills alone.

Emperor’s Flow Nectar, Jade Dew Pills, Netherpass Pills, Black Emperor Paste… and Star Pills!

Even if he had to exhaust all of Swallow’s strength, Luo Chen would refine large batches of various pills to solidify his identity as a top alchemy master!
