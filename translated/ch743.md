# Chapter 743: Sudden Comprehension of Withering-Bloom; Spiritual Impulse Descends

Between clear wind and white clouds, two figures drifted past.

From Fu Qinglan's mouth, Luo Chen came to understand Heaven-Earth Peak's personnel structure more and more.

As a treasure land with a fifth-tier spirit vein, the spiritual qi on Heaven-Earth Peak was exceedingly abundant.

But to prevent unnecessary waste, the mountain only opened cave abodes for Sages and Ascendant great powers. Even when Adept cultivators appeared here now and then, they merely served as attendants.

As for outside-continent cultivators who joined Star Gate with disciples and dependents, their disciples and followers were arranged elsewhere.

It could be said that as long as one cultivated long term on Heaven-Earth Peak, one truly came and went with no Initiates in sight, and all who laughed and spoke were Sages!

The composition of the Sages on the mountain was also rather complicated. Though outside-continent cultivators were the majority, some elder Sages from great clans also left their clans after growing old and devoted themselves to Heaven-Earth Peak.

The representatives of this group were precisely the fourteen Heaven-Earth Peak elders sent by the Twelve Great Houses and Thirty-Six Aristocratic Clans.

After saying all this, Fu Qinglan could not help sighing. "This single mountain alone contains nearly half of Central Continent's essence!"

Luo Chen did not refute her. Perhaps to outsiders, this mountain seemed heavy with twilight decay, but the people on it were indeed all capable figures.

If he connected it with what Fu Chaosheng had once said and thought carefully, it became even more frightening.

Was this spirit mountain supporting those aged Sages, or were those Sages feeding back into this mountain as they approached the end of their lives?

He had no intention of continuing that thought. His steps stopped before cave abode Jia Seventy-Eight.

He took out the guest elder jade token bestowed by the Star Gate elder and activated it with magic power.

In an instant, white clouds withdrew, and deep within the mist, a corner of a wondrous peak revealed itself.

"It is a quiet and secluded place."

Luo Chen chuckled softly.

Fu Qinglan, however, frowned slightly. "The spiritual qi concentration here seems a little low?"

Luo Chen smiled and strode inside, apparently already very satisfied.

He knew that perhaps she would think he had never seen the world and was content with this ordinary fourth-tier spirit vein.

But he understood even more clearly that he was only a passing guest in Central Continent. Even the Star Gate guest elder title was merely a layer of gold over him.

So long as it did not affect the essence, what difference was there between this gilding being a true guest elder or a temporary guest elder?

As for spiritual qi concentration?

One or two years would not affect much.

...

Cave abode Jia Seventy-Eight had not been occupied for many years, but that did not mean dust covered the floor or spiderwebs hung in the shadows.

With formations running year-round, it remained clean and refreshing.

Luo Chen walked around once and was rather satisfied.

There was only one shortcoming: Tianxuan Jing and the others had been left at the Fu family clan land, so there was no one usable at his side. Many things might have to be done personally.

Seeing no dissatisfaction from Luo Chen, Fu Qinglan could not say much. After he stopped, she took several jade slips from her storage brocade pouch.

"These are?"

Luo Chen accepted the jade slips and casually asked while browsing through them.

"It was not easy for my grandfather to come to Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain, so he needs to visit some former fellow disciples. He said these things are what you need, so he entrusted me to bring them over on the way."

They were indeed things Luo Chen needed.

Five fire-attribute cultivation arts: two fourth-tier and three third-tier.

Another jade slip contained the Eastern Wilderness intelligence Luo Chen had privately requested.

At the end, Fu Qinglan hesitated, then took a small booklet from her robes.

"This is the currently collected intelligence on the newly advanced Sage cultivators participating in the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven competition, as well as information on their Dao protectors. Though it is not yet complete, it should be of great help to us."

Luo Chen glanced at it and casually put it into his storage bag.

Seeing this, Fu Qinglan pressed her moist red lips together.

So casual?

Was the other party confident enough not to care about those opponents, or did he simply not take this matter seriously at all?

She had originally intended to talk properly with him, but when the words reached her lips, Fu Qinglan subconsciously said, "I heard Dao Brother Luo has not been in the Sage realm for long, yet your magic power is incomparably vigorous. Little Sister would like to experience it a little. May I?"

Luo Chen raised his head and looked at her in surprise.

"Did they not say you people may not recklessly fight before entering Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven?"

Fu Qinglan persisted. "Simply experiencing it a little should not matter."

Luo Chen smiled softly. "Since that is so, Luo has no objection."

As soon as the words fell, wind and cloud trembled.

A domineering, matchless magic power spread outward with him at the center, connecting with the wandering spiritual qi of heaven and earth. In an instant, a Sage domain formed.

At this moment, it was as if a volcano had erupted!

Fu Qinglan's small face turned deathly pale.

She intended to release her own Sage domain, but under the other party's suppression, even the step of connecting with heaven and earth's spiritual qi became extremely difficult.

"The gap is actually this enormous?"

In her heart, the woman could not believe it.

...

"Mountains and Seas calendar, year 3505: a beast tide emerged from the Million Mountains and struck the Eastern Wilderness human race's thirty-six domains..."

"Mountains and Seas calendar, year 3531: the Eastern Wilderness sacred land Nether Origin Sect organized the great sects' experts to counterattack the demon race and recover large swaths of lost land."

"The ancient Demon Emperor Tian appeared frequently. Though he did not personally act, he placed tremendous pressure on Sages, causing the front-line battle situation to repeatedly collapse."

"The tug-of-war continued for a hundred years. Forty percent of the Eastern Wilderness cultivation domains became scorched earth."

"Mountains and Seas calendar, year 3566: Green Parasol Mountain's three halls, sacred land of the long-vanished Feather Race, reappeared in the mortal world. Demon Emperor Azure Frost killed her way alone into the human hinterlands, seeking to avenge the past, destroying Tianji Chan Sect and the Joyous Union mountain gate. Because the two sects' grand elders had fallen decades earlier beneath Green Parasol Mountain, no one could stop Demon Emperor Azure Frost. Two great Sage upper sects were destroyed in an instant."

"The Eastern Wilderness sacred land Nether Origin Sect was furious and personally acted, dispatching large numbers of Nether Origin disciples to slaughter demons and exterminate beasts, counterattacking the Eastern Wilderness Million Mountains! Zhang Che, Daoist Thunder, revealed superb thunder-Dao cultivation at their head, suspected to have inherited the mighty White Night Daoist Tradition, with hope of becoming an Ascendant..."

Inside the cave abode, Luo Chen slowly withdrew his divine sense from the jade slip, his expression complicated.

In a short hundred years, the Eastern Wilderness had already become scorched earth among men.

Though he had long been mentally prepared, seeing definite intelligence still shook his mind.

Under those circumstances, had the insignificant Luo Tian Sect truly survived?

He did not know, but the emotions in his heart already made him impatient to return to that homeland.

Taking a deep breath, the Fine Dust Primordial Art circulated once within his body, calming Luo Chen's turbulent mood by several degrees.

Now, as he carefully savored the intelligence, he found one suspicious point.

The Feather Race sacred land Green Parasol Mountain had returned roughly forty years ago.

At that time, he had just reached the ninth level of Adept on Demon Sea's Longyuan Island, while outside, the seal on Arctic Yemo Heaven had been broken and vast numbers of demons had escaped.

These things seemed entirely unrelated, but if he connected them with the repaired teleportation formation in Xingtian Sea that linked the Eastern Wilderness and Northern Sea, it became somewhat coincidental.

Green Parasol Mountain must have had something to rely on, or it would not have dared return before the world.

Demon Emperor Azure Frost also had something to rely on, or she would not have dared kill her way into the human hinterlands.

And that greatest reliance must be the fellow Ascendant existence, Rosycloud!

"If Rosycloud returned at that time, then before that, where was she?"

"Could she have been in the Primordial Demon Sect mountain gate, inside Arctic Yemo Heaven?"

Luo Chen swallowed, suddenly struck by this wild thought.

"Forget it. These matters are still too far from me. I should first handle the matters at hand."

Luo Chen shook his head, unwilling and afraid to think about those towering beings standing at the peak of this realm.

Instead, he placed his attention on matters related to his own cultivation.

He firmly believed that as long as he was strong enough, many matters that seemed troublesome would be solved with ease.

The spiritual qi concentration of cave abode Jia Seventy-Eight was fairly ordinary, far inferior to the Fu family's Wangwu Mountain and Longyuan Island's Earth Abyss. It was only comparable to Little Qiong Mountain in Divine Origin City.

Since that was so, there was no need to hurry into arduous seclusion.

Now Fu Qinglan had delivered five fire-attribute cultivation arts, making the cultivation arts in his hands suddenly more ample.

This was an excellent opportunity to study cultivation arts and create a Sage-stage art suitable for himself!

Luo Chen hesitated no longer and took out the first copied jade slip.

Eastern Jia Li Fire Scripture[TERM?].

...

The path of cultivation was nothing more than refining qi and breathing it in and out.

Because of the structure of the human body and changes in nature, it was subdivided into yin-yang and the five elements, the twelve heavenly stems, the four images and eight trigrams, and so on.

Most cultivators did not understand the fine principles within, but for Luo Chen, who also cultivated the path of formations, understanding them was not difficult.

In his view, this Eastern Jia Li Fire Scripture was clearly a rather delicate fourth-tier cultivation art.

The spiritual qi it absorbed was unlike other ordinary fire-attribute arts, which were comprehensive. Instead, it used a partial aspect to represent the whole, focusing on absorbing the relatively refined parts of fire-attribute spiritual qi between heaven and earth.

For Luo Chen, this was somewhat useless.

The Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture, worthy of being the ancestor of fire arts, followed the domineering principle of majestic openness and ruling over all flames beneath heaven. It did not need to use a partial aspect to encompass the whole.

However, some of this cultivation art's details regarding meridian adjustment were very worth borrowing and absorbing.

After obtaining the Fire Spirit Body, Luo Chen had not developed this physique much.

Now, by means of the Eastern Jia Li Fire Scripture, he gained some alternative ideas.

Afterward, Luo Chen began comprehending another fourth-tier fire-attribute cultivation art, the Purple Yang Divine Art[TERM?].

This cultivation art had strict requirements regarding cultivation time and location.

Unlike Luo Chen's habit of cultivating deep at night, the best time to practice the Purple Yang Divine Art was at the first rising of the sun, when the morning light first shone, to absorb the first strand of solar true fire between heaven and earth.

At this time, the aura of solar true fire was the purest, yet also the weakest, making it suitable for Sages to refine.

If this art reached great accomplishment, it could greatly increase the power of Nascent Soul true fire, and it naturally restrained filthy and evil things.

On this day.

Luo Chen sat upright atop an ancient parasol tree. Beneath him was a pitch-black abyss, with dark wind blowing.

His eyes were brilliant as he stared directly into the black night.

When the night gradually brightened and a smear of fish-belly white appeared at the edge of the sky, the magic power in his body naturally began to circulate.

Between breaths, rhythm rose and fell, as though echoing heaven and earth.

Suddenly.

Whoosh!

A round ball like an egg yolk appeared in his eyes.

At the horizon, the rising sun emerged.

Luo Chen's breathing rhythm also reached synchronization in this moment.

As the rising sun continually climbed, large quantities of faint yet pure fire-attribute spiritual qi began gathering toward him.

"So that is how it is..."

When Luo Chen ended his cultivation of the Purple Yang Divine Art, a new understanding appeared in his heart.

If the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture focused on increasing the quantity of magic power, then the Eastern Jia Li Fire Scripture leaned toward internal adjustments of the self, making magic power circulate more smoothly, while the Purple Yang Divine Art focused on the quality of magic power.

After long-term cultivation, one's magic power would be incomparably pure, and even one's Nascent Soul true fire would possess a few traits of solar true fire.

As for the Flame Sovereign True Scripture[TERM?] he had studied before, it had its own ingenious design regarding apertures and the Purple Mansion.

"If I take the Heavenly Phoenix as the foundation and borrow and absorb the strengths of these three arts, can I continue a comprehensive cultivation on the path of fire arts?"

Luo Chen's eyes shone, his heart full of greed.

He had the confidence to do so.

The Fire Spirit Body gave him the foundation to cultivate all kinds of fire-attribute arts.

The proficiency panel allowed him to study, fuse, and even create cultivation arts, raising the upper limit.

As long as he grasped the measure properly...

At this moment, Luo Chen's heart suddenly stirred.

His divine sense looked inward. Within his Purple Mansion, the Withering-Bloom True Flame that was slowly withering seemed, after many days of his cultivation, to be withering faster and faster.

After absorbing that Withered-Source Fire Marrow, the Withering-Bloom True Flame had nearly gone out. This had not been Luo Chen exaggerating to Fu Chaosheng, but fact.

Logically, this situation should not have happened.

But Luo Chen had deduced the reason it had occurred.

The Withering-Bloom True Flame did not only have its flourishing side full of life. It also had another side of dead silence and withering!

Only, for many years, under Luo Chen's stimulation, it had slowly improved, constantly strengthening its flourishing vitality.

This strengthening actually had a limit.

Even fusing with a strand of Nirvana Sacred Flame origin could not conceal that limit.

But at Molan Court, after absorbing that seemingly ordinary fourth-tier netherfire, the Withering-Bloom True Flame found its direction.

And because of the guidance of the Withered-Source Fire Marrow, its limited intelligence made up its mind to walk in that direction.

That was "when flourishing reaches its peak, decline begins; withering and blooming cycle again and again!"

Without doubt, this was the true essence of the Withering-Bloom True Flame.

In Luo Chen's expectations, as long as it fully walked through the process of flourishing to decline and withering and blooming cycling once, the Withering-Bloom True Flame might undergo a complete transformation.

From nothing to something, until fourth tier, then withering into dead silence and returning again to nothing.

When it ignited once more...

"It is just that this time is far faster than I expected. How can this be?"

Luo Chen was somewhat puzzled.

In his expectation, this process should have lasted at least several years, perhaps even more than ten.

But now, less than a year had passed, right?

"Could it be caused by my cultivating other fire-attribute arts?"

Luo Chen had always considered himself widely experienced, but at this moment he could not grasp it at all.

However, from the accelerated withering of the Withering-Bloom True Flame, he vaguely gained some inspiration.

"The cycling of withering and blooming inherently contains the meaning of nirvana. If I fuse this change into my cultivation art as well, would it not perfectly fit the principle of the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture?"

As soon as he thought of this, Luo Chen could not help becoming eager.

He had once borrowed fire to temper the body.

Now, borrowing fire to refine qi did not seem impossible either.

For a time, Luo Chen fell into long contemplation. The magic power on his body began to circulate of its own accord, and the spiritual qi outside continually surged toward him, then was continually breathed out.

Clear wind and white clouds coiled around him. Wind, rain, thunder, and lightning mingled. Even when the Fu grandfather and granddaughter came to visit, he seemed not to notice.

If this state were explained in cultivation terminology, it would be two words: sudden comprehension!

Within a quiet cave abode on Heaven-Earth Peak.

A frail woman opened her eyes in puzzlement.

With a thought, she instantly found the source of the change.

Looking at the young man sitting cross-legged at the treetop while his aura continually changed, surprise appeared in her eyes.

Only after examining him carefully for a long while did she come back to herself.

"Dongfang Bai's worry was not without reason."

"If such a heaven-bestowed genius enters Star Gate, he will harm the interests of Central Continent's prodigies. Has it not happened already?"

"My primordial spirit resonates with heaven and earth, drawing in an enormous spiritual impulse. One part aids my ascension, one part is absorbed by Central Continent's land. This was something the Tianyuan Dao Sect liked to see. Unexpectedly, now some of it has been shared by an outsider."

"Since that is so, I will lend you a hand."

As she murmured to herself, her primordial spirit spread outward, controlling those spiritual impulses that should have merged into Central Continent's land and drawing out a portion, guiding it toward that young man.

Not only that, faint magic power emerged from her fingertips and drifted there, concealing the four directions.

Across the vast Heaven-Earth Peak, besides her, no other local mountain cultivator discovered that young man's unusual phenomenon.

After doing all this, a complicated expression appeared on the woman's face.

"As fellow alchemists, I hope you are not bound by shackles, and that your immortal path is free."

Everything happened in secret. No one knew, no one discovered it, and even Luo Chen, the person involved, remained entirely unaware.

*(End of Chapter)*
