# Chapter 990: Sword Qi Like a Rainbow, the Grand Array Tests the Gold

Counting from the moment Luo Chen and Azure Frost stepped into the Glazed Pure Land, three or four months had actually passed without anyone noticing.

Over such a long period, the major powers of the Mònan Royal Court had basically all received the news.

Even some more distant places, such as the Extreme East Boundless Sea that Luo Chen and the others had passed through earlier, had more or less learned of the situation here.

Therefore, the number of cultivators gathered at the entrance to the Glazed Pure Land was also increasing.

Although they had not assembled together, if one looked out, the scene outside the entrance was already a sea of people.

Among them were not a few old monsters at the Sage stage hidden from the Mònan Royal Court and the Extreme East Boundless Sea.

These old monsters did not directly enter the Glazed Pure Land but instead lingered outside, watching the fire from across the shore, their intentions unknown.

Suddenly!

Among the crowd, someone murmured softly.

"They're here!"

As the crowd stirred, a vast beam of light could be seen tearing through the sky. Where it passed, wind and clouds churned, heaven and earth changed color, leaving behind a long, empty trail that lingered for a long time.

The light scattered, and dozens of figures suddenly came into everyone's view.

When they saw these dozens of people, the crowd burst into an uproar.

All of them were Sage-level experts!

Even some of the old Sage monsters in the crowd gasped, blurting out.

"The eight hall heads of the Hanging Temple have all come!"

Looking out, among the solemn and dignified Hanging Temple monks, eight figures possessed auras as vast as the sea and abundant Buddha power.

The Arhat Hall, the Mind Meditation Hall, the Medicine King Court, the Precepts Court... even the heads of the Prajna Hall and the Dharma Hall stood among them.

Perhaps some did not know what the heads of these two halls signified, but as long as one mentioned that the current master of the Hanging Temple, Mahakala, was once the head of the Prajna Hall, the meaning became clear.

Their simultaneous appearance was enough to show the importance they placed on the manifestation of the Glazed Pure Land.

After all, not long ago, when the Ancient Red Sand Scorpion attacked the Hanging Temple, the Hanging Temple had not treated it with such a solemn formation.

Now, with the thirty-six strongest monks among the one hundred and eight eminent monks, and even the eight hall heads going out together, who in the entire Western Desert could stop them?

The clamoring crowd and the restless atmosphere did not affect the solemn mood of the Hanging Temple monks in the slightest.

After glancing around, their gazes all fell upon the entrance, where light swirled as if opening a door in the void.

A white-faced monk in a brocade kasaya pointed at the center of his brow.

As light swirled, the center of his brow slowly split open, revealing a third eye.

It was the divine power of the Buddhist sect—the Heavenly Eye!

With a single glance, the white-faced monk revealed a shocked expression.

"Spiritual energy is circulating, Buddha rhythm is rising!"

Beside him, an old monk with a broad face and large ears, his kasaya half-open, did not seem surprised.

"As the Arhat says, Senior Brother Xuan Du is indeed using the Glazed Pure Land to gather spiritual energy, intending to step into the Arhat realm."

The white-faced monk took a deep breath. "We cannot let him do that. If he does, the Glazed Pure Land will be unusable for at least a hundred years, and Mahakala will be unable to personally take action to sweep away the demonic aura and enlighten the people."

The old monk beside him also nodded. "Correct. If he wants to advance, he can return to the Hanging Temple. The power of this Glazed Pure Land cannot be wasted like this!"

The two exchanged a glance, then silently walked into the entrance.

The others followed without hesitation, filing in one after another.

In an instant, the thirty-six eminent monks of the Hanging Temple had vanished, as if they had never been there.

But the eyes of tens of thousands of cultivators would not deceive them.

After they left, the old Sage monsters among the crowd began to communicate with each other.

"Should we go in?"

"With such a lineup, it's likely to be a fierce battle. When the city gate catches fire, it's hard to avoid disaster spreading to the innocent. I won't be going."

"Hmph, Fellow Daoist Fish, why are you so timid? The Hanging Temple has sent so many experts; reclaiming the Glazed Pure Land is just a matter of course. We're just watching the battle, what's there to fear?"

"Fellow Daoist Stone is right. If the situation gets a little chaotic, we might even gain some benefits. Besides, Brahma Heavenly Son and Xiao Yan went in earlier, and nothing happened to them, right?"

"You go if you want. I'm not getting involved in this muddy water."

After some discussion, the hidden old Sage monsters gradually split into two camps.

One camp no longer concealed their tracks and stepped into the Glazed Pure Land.

The other camp remained hidden among the crowd, even moving a little further away. However, they still spread their divine sense as far as possible, constantly monitoring the situation near the Glazed Pure Land.

Clearly, once a real opportunity arose, they would not hesitate to rush in and claim a share of the spoils.

After all, the Glazed Pure Land was known as the number one treasure of the Buddhist sect!

Regardless of how many resources were hidden inside, just this spirit treasure alone was enough to make people covet it.

While waiting, suddenly all the old Sage monsters focused their divine sense on a group of monks in simple robes.

It was the Tuluo monks, who adhered to the philosophy of ascetic cultivation!

At this moment, the Tuluo monks' expressions changed drastically, and an atmosphere of worry and panic instantly spread.

There was only one reason for such a change.

"The head of the Tuluo sect has fallen!"

The old Sage monsters who had not entered were all stunned.

How long had the Hanging Temple's eminent monks been inside, and how had they already implicated a powerful ascetic cultivator?

...

Inside the Glazed Pure Land.

Time went back a little.

The moment the thirty-six eminent monks of the Hanging Temple entered, they saw the moonlit Buddhist temple and the primordial spirit suspended high in the void.

Absorbing spiritual energy, relying on it to roam the ocean of origin qi—this was a sign of advancing to the Ascendant stage.

The white-faced monk's pupils contracted, and he immediately shouted, "Amitabha! Senior Brother Xuan Du, how can you violate the precepts and use the power of the Pure Land for your own selfish desires?"

The Buddha's voice echoed, rushing straight towards the Moonlight Glazed Hall.

However, before it could affect Master Xuan Du, it was blocked.

"Everyone, coming uninvited makes you unwelcome guests. Shouting so loudly is even more impolite!"

A light laugh spread across the land, instantly suppressing the white-faced monk's Buddha's voice.

The white-faced monk's expression changed slightly, and he finally shifted his attention to the pavilion outside the main hall.

A man in a golden robe and a white-robed Daoist.

He naturally recognized the man in the golden robe—it was Brahma Heavenly Son, who had almost replaced him as the head of the Prajna Hall. The other person was the powerful existence who had just spoken and suppressed his Buddha's voice.

Based on the Hanging Temple's intelligence, the white-faced monk instantly matched the names to the faces.

"The strongman from the Eastern Wilderness, Alchemist Luo Chen?"

Luo Chen looked at them from afar, a slight smile on his face. "It seems my reputation is really quite loud. Even the Hanging Temple, which has been secluded for years, knows of my origins."

The white-faced monk shook his head. "Benefactor, you were once known as a benevolent and righteous gentleman. Why do you now aid the wicked? First, you helped the great demon Azure Frost escape, and now you want to meddle in the internal disputes of our Buddhist sect?"

Luo Chen clasped his hands behind his back and walked out of the pavilion, displaying the demeanor and style of a grandmaster.

"I am entrusted by someone, so I have no choice!"

"Alone, you cannot stop what we are about to do." The white-faced monk was unperturbed, turning his gaze to the man in the golden robe. "Benefactor Lian Sheng, do you intend to stop us or help us?"

Lian Sheng?

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow.

So this was Brahma Heavenly Son's real name.

Behind him, the questioned number one expert of Mònan, Brahma Heavenly Son, revealed a helpless expression.

"This is your Buddhist sect's own affair. I will neither help nor stop you."

Having said that, he cupped his hands towards Luo Chen.

"Apologies, Fellow Daoist!"

Luo Chen hummed in acknowledgment without turning his head.

"Go on! With me here, they shall not cross the thunderous pond even one step!"

As he spoke, his right hand hung low, and a blood-colored long sword slowly materialized.

Brahma Heavenly Son cast a wary glance at that long sword and slowly retreated.

In the distance, the white-faced monk couldn't help but purse his lips.

"A demon sword!"

"Benefactor, you have truly fallen deeply. You practice evil arts and wield a bloody demon sword. In time, you will surely bring disaster to the world!"

"Since that is the case, then we shall invite you back to the Hanging Temple as well!"

Having said that, he waved his hand, and the monks behind him immediately flew towards Luo Chen.

Not only that, but their individual flight trajectories also became mysterious.

Luo Chen's previously relaxed expression immediately tightened. Under his Crimson-Eyed Golden Pupils, he suddenly noticed a great change in the flow of spiritual energy between heaven and earth.

A reminder came to his ears.

"This is the Hanging Temple's most famous Arhat Grand Formation. Fellow Daoist, be careful!"

It was Brahma Heavenly Son.

Although he did not say much, just uttering the four words "Arhat Grand Formation" was enough.

The name of this formation was not particularly stunning; even in the mundane world, formations with this name were common.

But its power was worlds apart.

This formation was based on nine people, with the false twelve earthly branches as its core. It contained the changes of yin and yang and encompassed the five elements. Once formed, it was perfectly smooth and had almost no flaws.

In its changes, its attacks were wonderfully unpredictable.

In its expansion and contraction, the formation could be large or small.

Moreover, the more people, the stronger its power.

An ordinary eighteen-Arhat formation formed by Adept-stage monks was enough to threaten Sage cultivators.

If Sage cultivators were used as the foundation, the power was unimaginable.

It was rumored that the reason the Hanging Temple had always maintained the seats of one hundred and eight eminent monks was to try and assemble the lineup of a one hundred and eight Arhat formation.

If the Arhat formation was composed of one hundred and eight Sage monks, it would be like a peak Arhat descending into the world, unstoppable in this realm!

Of course, the only sect in the entire Mountains and Seas Realm that had such a foundation was the former Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

With the current foundation of the Hanging Temple, they were far from being able to assemble a hundred and eight Sages to form the Arhat formation.

Even the number seventy-two was quite a stretch.

This time, in order to reclaim the Glazed Pure Land, they had sent the most suitable thirty-six eminent monks, all at the Sage stage!

Especially the leading eight hall heads, who were all existences comparable to Grand Cultivators.

The thirty-six Arhat formation formed with them as the core might not be able to dominate the world, but it was already enough to threaten Ascendant existences.

Luo Chen had profound knowledge of formations and extensive experience. From the moment Brahma Heavenly Son told him the name of the formation, all the relevant information had surfaced in his mind.

His brows were deeply furrowed.

If it were one-on-one combat against the thirty-six eminent monks, including the eight hall heads, he was full of confidence.

If they formed an ordinary formation, he was also confident he could break out of it, with the odds of victory being three to seven.

Three for them, seven for him!

But if it was the Hanging Temple's most famous Arhat formation, Luo Chen's confidence would be greatly diminished.

Thirty-six eminent monks... that number was really not very auspicious.

In the past, the Nether Origin Sect had used thirty-six Sage cultivators as a foundation, including six Grand Cultivators like the Lord of Lingtian City, to form the Zhou Tian Star Formation and forcibly subdue Qinghe Immortal.

Was he now a match for the intelligent Ascendant Qinghe Immortal?

Luo Chen was on high alert!

Just as his mana was circulating, his divine sense stirred slightly. He turned sideways and slashed down with his sword.

Shua!

The sword light suddenly bloomed, and a scream came from behind.

"Ah!"

It was the head of the Tuluo sect!

Before he could even make a move, merely revealing a hint of hostility and restless intent, he had suffered a thunderous blow, unable even to resist.

On the spot, only a deep gully and a body split in two remained.

Xiao Yan and Dapin stood dumbfounded on either side, their eyes full of terror.

This sword...

"Courting death!"

Luo Chen snorted coldly. With a great enemy before him, how could he tolerate a petty thief lurking nearby?

From the Moonlight Glazed Hall behind him came a sigh.

In the end, he had failed to save the life of the Tuluo sect head.

Luo Chen still did not look back, only saying coldly, "A bodhisattva's heart must also have thunderous means. If you want to save all living beings, compassion alone is not enough. Master Xuan Du, in this battle, I cannot be as merciful as before."

As he spoke, he slowly rose into the air. The long hair bound by the Ping Shan Crown fluttered behind him in the windless air, making him look like a murderous star descending into the world.

After killing one person first, the fear of the Arhat formation in his heart instantly dissipated, replaced only by an unparalleled killing intent, wild and exposed!

Faced with Luo Chen's violent act, the Hanging Temple monks were even more enraged.

"Such a demon! Cannot be saved, cannot be forgiven!"

Luo Chen was not afraid. Being called a demon was not the first time; he had once had the Dao name Azure Yang Demon Lord.

His answer to the Hanging Temple monks was only a single sword strike!

The Yuantu Sword, which was infinitely close to the grade of a spirit treasure, had long been ready. It drew upon the spiritual energy of heaven and earth and slashed out a shocking rainbow that tore through the air.

He wanted to take advantage of the Arhat formation not yet being fully formed to first cut down a few weaklings.

Under his Crimson-Eyed Golden Pupils, nothing could hide.

A Sage monk with the weakest mana fluctuation bore the brunt of the attack!

Faced with this sword strike, the Sage monk's eyes showed shock, but no fear. He simply pushed his palm forward flatly.

The rainbow of sword qi and the ordinary palm strike collided in an instant.

The bloody demonic qi was fierce and sharp, seemingly able to pierce anything.

And the Buddha's palm was balanced and peaceful, as unmoving as a mountain.

Upon collision.

Boom!

The rainbow of sword qi slowly dissipated.

The bodies of the thirty-six eminent monks trembled slightly, but they were largely unharmed.

The white-faced monk, the head of the Prajna Hall, curled his lips slightly.

"You do have some insight. You wanted to kill one person first to prevent the formation from taking shape. Unfortunately, we were already prepared. The thirty-six Arhat formation is ready at all times. Demon, surrender!"

As he spoke, thirty-four figures scattered, forming a large net that enveloped Luo Chen.

Only the head of the Prajna Hall and the head of the Dharma Hall remained in the rear.

The other six hall heads had completely merged into the formation.

Luo Chen had nowhere to dodge, nowhere to retreat.

Because the Moonlight Glazed Hall was right behind him.

He also did not want to retreat!

With his current strength, he could go anywhere in the world. But if he wanted to retrieve his life soul from the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, he would inevitably have to face an Ascendant great power.

Was he currently a match for an Ascendant great power?

Luo Chen did not know, but this thirty-six Arhat formation, which was comparable to an Ascendant great power, was an excellent touchstone.

"Then let me see what you are capable of!"

With a loud laugh, Luo Chen calmly entered the formation.

(End of this chapter)
