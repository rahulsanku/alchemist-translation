# Chapter 628: Black King Returns, First Visit to Moya

Beside Peng Island.

The Daoist stood with his hands behind his back, quietly looking at the water surface where undercurrents surged.

Suddenly.

Splash, splash!

The lake water seemed pushed apart by two hands, and a huge one-horned dragon head slowly rose from the water. After it fully emerged, that jiaolong-python dragon head alone was far taller than the white-robed Daoist.

Though in a posture of looking down, the jiaolong's eyes were filled with respect.

"Master, Black King has returned."

Luo Chen quietly looked at him. His divine sense naturally spread out and swept across Black King's whole body.

His aura was somewhat dispirited, but no injuries were visible. This was exhaustion caused by long-distance travel.

Since there were no injuries, why had he been delayed so long?

Suddenly.

Luo Chen's extremely invasive divine sense landed on the jiaolong-python dragon head.

"Your divine soul was injured?"

Black King drooped his head and seemed somewhat listless, exhaling thick dragon breath. "Master sees clearly. Black King was indeed struck by soul poison before."

"Soul poison? Was it the poisonous qi released by the Purple Monkey Flower?"

"Mm, that is indeed so."

After obtaining this answer, Luo Chen instantly became delighted instead of worried.

Soul poison capable of injuring Black King's divine soul had to be released by at least a fourth-tier Purple Monkey Flower.

And for Luo Chen to refine the Nascent Soul Pill, the standard for the main ingredient Purple Monkey Flower was at least fourth-tier.

On that Purple Spirit Island, there truly was the thing he needed!

As expected, in Black King's following report, he pointed out that Purple Spirit Island had fourth-tier Purple Monkey Flowers.

"You have worked hard."

Luo Chen gave a word of consolation, then asked about the specific details, as well as the reason the other party had returned so late.

"The route on that map was roughly correct."

"However, perhaps because too many years have passed, some spiritless islands transformed into flourishing spirit lands over the long passage of time, attracting some powerful demon beasts to occupy them. On the way there, I accidentally trespassed into the territories of several Demon Kings and was forced into battle."

"After arriving at the destination, I swallowed the poison-resisting pill Master refined, and using the qi-concealing method, quietly went onto Purple Spirit Island to search for Purple Monkey Flowers. But in truth, there was no need to search much at all, because the island was covered everywhere in Purple Monkey Flowers!"

"Yes, everywhere! As if that island were the kingdom of Purple Monkey Flowers."

"The only problem was that most of these Purple Monkey Flowers covering the island were low-tier. Little Black thought that Master wanted fourth-tier Purple Monkey Flowers, so I secretly began searching."

"Purple Spirit Island is not safe. On it are many demon beasts bewitched by the Purple Monkey Flowers, including no lack of powerful third-tier Demon Kings. Especially the Purple Qi Poison Monkeys!"

Classics recorded that wherever Purple Qi Poison Monkeys gathered, Purple Monkey Flowers would surely be found, but in truth cause and effect had been reversed.

It should be that there were Purple Monkey Flowers first, and only then Purple Qi Poison Monkeys, because the latter were manipulated by Purple Monkey Flowers year-round. Their growth habits changed, and they gradually evolved into a special demon beast race.

"These Purple Qi Poison Monkeys have an extremely strong sense of territory toward outsiders, but internally they like gathering in groups. I deliberately avoided them and did not want to fight. But in the end, when I discovered the fourth-tier Purple Monkey Flower, I was forced to face a large group of third-tier Monkey Kings."

"These third-tier Purple Qi Poison Monkeys are not actually very strong, and their attack methods are terribly lacking. If I had enough time, Little Black would surely be able to deal with them one by one."

"But it was time that harmed me!"

"The longer one stays on that island, the more one is tainted by the purple qi poison miasma. Unknowingly, even my demon soul was affected. Even the poison-resisting pill Master bestowed had less than one-tenth its effect."

"Seeing more and more Purple Qi Poison Monkeys coming to surround me, and worrying that a great battle would destroy Purple Spirit Island, I had no choice but to flee in a sorry state."

"After escaping Purple Spirit Island, my mind was muddled and my strength greatly reduced. To avoid encountering danger on the return journey, I found a place to recover from my injuries. In addition, I had to circle around the territories of those Demon Kings I passed on the way back. In the end, I only returned to Peng Lake today, nearly half a year later than the set time."

After Black King slowly explained everything in detail, Luo Chen gradually formed a rough outline in his heart of the route to Purple Spirit Island and the specific situation on the island.

At the end.

He deliberately asked one more question.

"Along the way, were there human cultivators?"

"No." Black King shook his head, raising wave upon wave.

Looking at the ripples slowly spreading from within to without, Luo Chen's mood finally calmed.

Overall, Black King's solo operation this time was successful.

He had both ascertained the route and found the fourth-tier Purple Monkey Flower Luo Chen needed. Although the number of fourth-tier Purple Monkey Flowers was not large, the potential was excellent. There was an entire island of Purple Monkey Flowers!

In addition, Black King's trip had also eliminated the possibility that someone had deliberately used news of Purple Monkey Flower to lure Luo Chen out.

This point was what Luo Chen cared about more.

Especially after Ding Yi had just used news of the Five Elements Lotus Platform as leverage to invite him to the Sunken Sea, Luo Chen became even more cautious about Purple Spirit Island.

After all, in the early years, Azure Yang Hall had issued the tasks for "Purple Monkey Flower and Five Elements Lotus Platform" to the outside world. This would fall into the eyes of many people with ulterior motives, so Luo Chen had no choice but to be cautious.

Ding Yi was one. Who knew whether the Purple Monkey Flower side might be another?

Looking at it now, everything was worry-free.

Heavenjade, standing nearby, was somewhat worried.

"Master, according to what Black King said, fourth-tier Purple Monkey Flowers can already affect creatures at the third-tier Demon King level. Then for Golden Core cultivators, would they not also be very dangerous?"

Luo Chen nodded.

"In addition, the island has a group of powerful beings bewitched by Purple Monkey Flowers. If we want to forcefully occupy this island, battle will inevitably erupt. But once battle breaks out, it will also cause a huge effect on the island's terrain, environment, and the spirit plants themselves, right?"

Luo Chen nodded again, but his face clearly did not contain much worry.

Heavenjade did not understand.

Luo Chen smiled freely. "These problems are not problems. They can simply be solved one by one."

Yes. To Luo Chen, so long as there was no external human factor interfering, other problems were not major problems.

"Ugh!"

In front of Luo Chen and Heavenjade, Black King retched and immediately spat out an object wrapped in black liquid.

Heavenjade frowned. "Did I not teach you how to use storage bags?"

Black King chuckled. "Habit."

Afterward, as the black liquid dripped away from that object, its true appearance was revealed. Purple light stained it, the flower stem was like muscular blood vessels, and clusters of blossoms piled layer upon layer. Taken as a whole, it looked like a "brain flower" that had been ripped out alive.

It was impressively a Purple Monkey Flower exactly like the classics recorded!

At the plant's roots, there was also a clump of fresh red mud.

With a wave of his hand, Luo Chen summoned this Purple Monkey Flower before him. He widened both eyes and counted the purple halos on the flower clusters one by one.

One, two, three!

"A third-tier Purple Monkey Flower. Good! With this living plant, I have even more confidence in conquering Purple Spirit Island."

Luo Chen withdrew his gaze and revealed a warm smile to Black King.

"You were thoughtful. Go back and recuperate first!"

"Master, I am fine. After leaving Purple Spirit Island, the soul poison's effect grew smaller and smaller. I was already mostly recovered on the return journey. When Master goes to Purple Spirit Island, you must take me with you. I absolutely must teach that group of monkeys a lesson."

After saying this, Black King turned, shook his head and swayed his tail, and submerged into the lake water.

By the lakeside, Luo Chen held the Purple Monkey Flower in his hand, looking at the lake surface as its ripples gradually stilled, and fell into thought.

Only, from his mouth, murmurs occasionally emerged.

Purple Spirit Island... Ding Yi... Five Elements Lotus Platform... Sunken Sea...

...

Moya Mountain.

This was Luo Chen's first time setting foot on this spirit mountain famous in the Myriad Immortals Assembly.

Of course, he knew very clearly that what was famous was not Moya Mountain, but the artifact-forging master, the Moya Cave Master.

Led by a Moya Mountain cultivator, Luo Chen climbed the mountain without hurry.

Along the way, his divine sense spread out from time to time, falling on the buildings throughout the mountain.

Dense fire qi and passionate sounds of hammering iron returned to Luo Chen's sea of consciousness under divine sense perception.

"This Moya Mountain is worthy of being one of the rare places in the Fulong Mountain Range where earthfire gathers in clusters! It truly is an immortal blessed land extremely suited to artifact-forging and alchemy!"

Luo Chen sighed with feeling.

Hearing this, the Foundation Establishment cultivator beside him proudly said, "Superior overpraises us. However, our Moya Mountain's artifact-forging ability is indeed famed far and wide. Countless wandering cultivators of the entire Myriad Immortals Assembly rely on us to repair law devices and magic treasures for them. In their words, if not for the Cave Master not wishing to establish a sect or faction, Moya Mountain could completely become a top-tier artifact-forging great sect!"

A top-tier artifact-forging great sect?

Surpassing small forces like the Duan family and Refining Edge, comparable to existences like the Yan Alliance and Hundred Craft Mountain?

Luo Chen did not comment.

However, he also did not deny the earlier statement that "the wandering cultivators of the entire Myriad Immortals Assembly rely on Moya Mountain."

Because the Myriad Immortals Assembly faced the threat of the Three Oceans defensive line and had to fight demon beasts year-round, the wear and tear of law devices and magic treasures had always been a bottomless pit.

Although law devices and magic treasures were all said to be "used for a hundred years, repaired for a hundred years, then patched and mended for another hundred years," passed down generation after generation like family heirlooms.

But those were extremely high-quality treasures!

How could ordinary cultivators each have such treasures?

Under frequent battle, the wear and tear on law devices and magic treasures was enormous.

For example, Luo Chen. During Qi Refinement, frequent battles had ruined a Cyan Musk Gathering Jiaolong Flag he liked very much. During Foundation Establishment, things were still fine; battle intensity had not been that high, and only because of overuse did he ruin a pair of Fiery Cloud Wings. But after Golden Core, in the battle on Xuanyan Island, first under the hands of a Nine-Clawed Poison King Crab, two of the three Soul-Breaking Nails, the Qi-Breaking Nail and Armor-Breaking Nail, were destroyed, leaving only a Soul-Breaking Nail preserved. Later, in his battle against Golden Armor Demon King, his most handy Mystic Flame Sword was shattered.

Even someone as strong as Luo Chen had suffered such misery in terms of weapon loss.

Other wandering cultivators would fare even worse.

Under these circumstances, Moya Mountain, this artifact-forging holy land, could naturally earn wealth until its basin and bowl were full.

Through Demon Hunter Office commission tasks, Moya Mountain cultivators would regularly repair law devices and magic treasures for Myriad Immortals Assembly cultivators.

This was their main source of income!

It was also one of the reasons why the Moya Cave Master could still restrain his temper despite Luo Chen's sudden rise.

How many defensive magic treasures could Luo Chen produce in a year?

His Peng Lake also did not have a large batch of artifact-forging talents he could command, so naturally it could not seize the meat in the Moya Cave Master's bowl.

Under the guide cultivator's introduction, Luo Chen learned more about Moya Mountain's situation.

Roughly eight hundred wandering cultivators depended on the Moya Cave Master for their livelihood.

Internally, they processed mineral materials for the Moya Cave Master and assisted him in artifact-forging.

Externally, they had their own shop in Dragon Abyss Immortal City, accepting artifact-forging tasks from wandering cultivators.

They also had the Demon Hunter Office's regular large orders.

Accumulating year after year, these eight hundred wandering cultivators almost completely followed the Moya Cave Master's lead. Among them were quite a few late Foundation Establishment great cultivators who had received the Moya Cave Master's guidance and grasped complete artifact-forging arts.

Calling them personal disciples would not be excessive.

"If I were still in the Eastern Wilderness, the Luo Tian Sect would presumably be like this too!"

"No, it should be even better!"

"After all, a sect management model has stronger cohesion. And inside the Luo Tian Sect, apart from me, there are also capable people. I wonder whether Wang Yuan succeeded in breaking through to the Furnace Realm, whether Chu Kui advanced to Golden Core. Counting the time, with the large batch of Jade Dew Pills I left behind, Huiniang and Caiyi should already have reached late Foundation Establishment. Have they ever worried about Core Formation? Presumably not. I also left them Fine Dust Primordial Art and Nether Origin Pills..."

Because of the grand sight of wandering cultivators gathering on Moya Mountain, Luo Chen saw things and thought of people, and waves arose in his heart.

But in a flash, he suppressed the distracting thoughts.

Only because before Moya Cave, two figures were looking at him with smiles.

"Fellow Daoist, we have waited for you for a long time!" Ding Yi called out loudly.

The Moya Cave Master, with whom Luo Chen had previously had slight friction, also cupped his hands in greeting at this moment.

Luo Chen swept his sleeve, smiled, and cupped his hands in return.

Afterward, amid the three men's cheerful conversation, they entered Moya Cave.

*(End of Chapter)*
