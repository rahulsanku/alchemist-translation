# Chapter 1142: The Carefree Envoy

The spring chill lingered, the cold still seeping into one's bones.

But within the city, the accumulated snow had gradually begun to melt. Occasionally, birds would fly in, their chirping bringing a touch of spring's vitality.

The long-silent Ruins Immortal City was slowly beginning to stir with life.

It was during this season of all things reviving that a caravan arrived at Ruins Immortal City.

The caravan was enormous, consisting of over a hundred people, all of them martial arts masters of formidable strength that drew every eye.

As they entered the city, their arrival was grand and imposing. All the major figures of Ruins Immortal City had gathered to welcome them. Naturally, this included the two strongest contenders for the current position of city lord, Xiao Feng and Xiao Yun.

With such a grand display, everyone knew—the Carefree City's special envoy had arrived.

That very day, at the city's largest restaurant, the Ascension Pavilion, a grand banquet was laid out to entertain the Carefree Envoy.

In attendance, besides representatives from all the major powers of Ruins Immortal City, were all the top-tier martial arts masters who had been invited.

Such a scale was truly astonishing.

...

"It's like a superior officer inspecting a subordinate unit."

Late at night, Luo Chen emerged from his meditation chamber after finishing his cultivation practice and said exactly that.

"Indeed! Brother Luo, you didn't see it. Everyone lined up one by one to greet that Carefree Envoy. It was just like when our village went to the town to pay grain taxes after the autumn harvest every year."

Jing Lan looked tired, but her eyes burned with excitement. Today, she had attended the banquet on Luo Chen's behalf.

It had only been about three months since Jing Lan followed Luo Chen to Ruins Immortal City, but her transformation had been earth-shattering.

Dressed in gorgeous gowns, adorned with jewelry and precious ornaments, though she still seemed a bit awkward, the rustic air of a country girl was gradually fading away.

This was probably what they meant by "one's dwelling changes one's qi, and nurture changes one's body"!

Luo Chen hardly ever managed affairs. All matters inside and outside the residence were handled by Jing Lan together with the butler, Uncle Zhong. As for matters outside the residence, aside from those involving Young City Lord Xiao Feng, Jing Lan was basically the one to deal with them.

Some might think that since Luo Chen had just arrived and had shallow roots in Ruins Immortal City, no one would seek him out. That would be a grave mistake.

Not only were there people, there were many!

The most frequent visitors were Supervisor Yan Song and his family. Since Luo Chen had been introduced by Supervisor Yan, the two families naturally had the closest relationship.

Next came the numerous small powers within the city—shop owners, masters of pleasure establishments, and even leaders of minor gangs.

Finally, there were martial arts masters who had achieved some realm but had yet to make a name for themselves.

These people came to visit him voluntarily, their goals simple—fame or profit!

The fact that Luo Chen had become a guest elder under Xiao Feng with his Second Realm Body Refining cultivation seemed ordinary on the surface, but the news had long since spread throughout Ruins Immortal City.

Especially during this cold winter season, when many city residents were idle at home, they loved discussing such gossip.

Learning that Luo Chen had become Xiao Feng's guest elder, those small powers who couldn't reach the young city lord but had some family wealth took the initiative to attach themselves to him, seeking protection or backing.

Additionally, because his realm appeared relatively low, far inferior to those Furnace Realm masters, some martial artists who were also at the Second Realm of Body Refining frequently came calling, trying to increase their own fame by sparring with Luo Chen. If they were lucky, they might even catch Xiao Feng's eye. If Xiao Feng officially became the city lord of Ruins Immortal City this year, they would become guest elders of the city lord's residence. That was far more convenient than challenging those Furnace Realm experts.

Unfortunately, to their disappointment, Luo Chen, this new guest elder, completely ignored all challengers who came to his door.

As for those who came bearing gifts of profit, Luo Chen basically handed them all over to Jing Lan to handle.

Only when Jing Lan encountered something she couldn't manage would Luo Chen occasionally convey his intentions.

In the end, most were rejected, and only a small portion were accepted.

Thus, in just three months, Luo Chen had become the patron elder of two medicine shops and one tavern. He did nothing at all and could enjoy generous tributes, requiring only the use of his name as Luo Chen, guest elder of the city lord's residence.

Throughout this process, Jing Lan's skill in dealing with people had gradually improved, giving her a completely different bearing and charm from the country girl she had been a few months ago.

Today, Luo Chen was at a critical moment in condensing the first dan seed of his Third Cycle Great Circulation, so naturally he couldn't attend the banquet at Ascension Pavilion. He had sent Jing Lan to represent him and then report the general situation back to him.

Now, hearing Jing Lan's account, Luo Chen couldn't help but chuckle.

"That's the Carefree Envoy, not someone comparable to the town chief of Green Mountain Town."

Jing Lan couldn't help but sigh with emotion as well. "Yes, even President Wan, whom Boss Zhou usually treats with such respect, was utterly humble before that Carefree Envoy, bowing and scraping at every turn. And those guest elders who share the same status as Brother Luo—they all put away their arrogant airs and obediently submitted to the Carefree Envoy's inspection. It seems that even if Xiao Feng truly becomes the city lord of Ruins Immortal City in the future, he'll still have to treat the Carefree Envoy with the utmost courtesy!"

The Boss Zhou Jing Lan mentioned was the owner of a medicine shop currently under Luo Chen's protection. As for President Wan, he was the president of the Wanji Medicine Association, holding the power to set medicinal herb prices and allocate supplies for Ruins Immortal City and several nearby large cities.

Such a top-tier figure was already comparable to a city lord, yet he still had to ingratiate himself with the Carefree Envoy—this showed just how high the envoy's status was!

"Carefree City..."

Luo Chen murmured the name, his eyes narrowing slightly.

Throughout the entire Great Cataclysm Realm, there were countless martial artists and endless experts, but most of them relied on the great cities for their livelihood.

And all of the Seventy-Two Cities served Carefree City as their master.

The master of Carefree City was, in truth, the common master of the Seventy-Two Cities. The entire Great Cataclysm Realm revered him as sovereign, and thus he was also called the Carefree Realm Lord!

Every year, a massive amount of resources was transported to Carefree City. Countless experts flocked there by reputation. Moreover, every hundred years, the Carefree Realm Lord would specially invite those city lord-level experts who had reached the Fourth Realm of Leakless Golden Body to Carefree City to discuss martial arts together.

Ten years ago, Xiao Feng's father, the city lord of Ruins Immortal City, had been invited just like that, and he still hadn't returned to this day.

Now it seemed that this invitation was practically compulsory—not something one could simply decline.

And today, the Carefree Envoy had specifically requested that all Furnace Realm masters in the city attend the evening banquet, which felt more like a "baseline assessment."

Was he observing in advance which experts had a chance to break through to the Fourth Realm of Body Refining, preparing for the next centennial invitation?

What kind of opportunity did Carefree City possess that made so many martial arts experts flock to it?

And what was the situation that made the Carefree Realm Lord invite all the top experts every hundred years?

Even without attending the banquet, combining what he had learned from reading various texts, Luo Chen had already analyzed many things and developed numerous speculations.

Jing Lan watched the contemplative Luo Chen, the excitement from attending the grand feast gradually fading from her eyes, replaced by unease.

"Brother Luo, are you going to Carefree City?"

Luo Chen was startled and answered with a question instead. "Why do you ask?"

Jing Lan said softly, "I heard them say that when other martial arts masters come to Ruins Immortal City and become guest elders, all they think about is how to gain the young city lord's favor and how to reap benefits. But you never seem to do anything like that. Even the patron elder positions at those three shops you accepted seem like you just wanted to give me something to do. I feel... you won't stay in Ruins Immortal City forever."

Because you seek nothing, so you won't stay long?

Luo Chen smiled faintly and didn't deny it.

"I'll probably go, eventually."

Jing Lan's face showed obvious dejection.

"But it's still early."

Luo Chen's next words made the woman's mood improve slightly, but only slightly. In the end, Luo Chen was going to leave.

And judging by his current arrangements for himself, the next time he left, he probably wouldn't take her with him.

"Go and rest. You're tired," Luo Chen said.

Jing Lan nodded. Before leaving, she reminded Luo Chen to attend Xiao Feng's private banquet tomorrow.

After she left, Luo Chen shook his head with a light sigh.

How could he not understand a woman's heart? But in the end, immortals and mortals were different. He had never been moved. What he was doing now was merely repaying her with a lifetime of wealth and honor for saving his life.

As for going to Carefree City?

That was indeed still early. At least by mortal standards of time, that might as well be a lifetime away.

"Xiao Feng is hosting a banquet?"

Luo Chen recalled what Jing Lan had just mentioned and curled his lips.

Though it was a bit troublesome, he still planned to go.

The first dan seed of the Third Cycle Great Circulation had already been condensed. The subsequent cultivation would proceed naturally; there was no rush.

Xiao Feng had treated him with considerable respect, so in their usual interactions, Luo Chen could afford to give him some face.

Meanwhile.

In the Celestial Room Number One of Ascension Pavilion, the largest restaurant in Ruins Immortal City.

A young man in a white robe, noble in bearing, stood leaning against the railing, hands clasped behind his back, gazing at the moon.

Footsteps sounded behind him, followed by a respectful voice.

"Lord Envoy, the list you requested has arrived."

The Carefree Envoy casually beckoned, and the list fell into his hands. As he flipped through the pages, names came into view, gradually matching the martial arts masters who had attended today's gathering.

This list recorded not only those people's cultivation realms but also their backgrounds and origins, their likely primary cultivation techniques, and a large number of past battle records.

People like Granny Yu and Old Monster Red Flame under Xiao Feng were all on it.

However, after flipping through it all, the Carefree Envoy closed the book with some boredom.

"They're all a bunch of mediocrities!"

If these words were to spread, countless people would be shocked. After all, everyone on this list was a powerful Furnace Realm existence!

Yet in the Carefree Envoy's mouth, they only merited the evaluation of "mediocre."

Only the black-clad man behind the envoy nodded.

"You're right, my lord. There are few outstanding ones among them. Even the two Xiao brothers, though they might be hiding their true strength, are only at the initial Furnace Realm. In a hundred years, they'll struggle to achieve a Golden Body."

But then, the black-clad man changed his tone.

"However, this trip hasn't been entirely without gain. At least that old fellow named Tie Xiong has a cultivation that seems to be at the Leakless Realm. He might catch the Realm Lord's eye."

The Carefree Envoy frowned slightly. "Tie Xiong, huh..."

He murmured the name but quickly shook his head firmly.

"This person probably won't do. His qi still leaks out, which shows that even if he managed to break through to the Leakless Realm by luck, his Golden Body hasn't been perfectly forged. He likely used a secret technique to force the breakthrough. The Realm Lord wouldn't look favorably on such a shortcut-taker. I'm afraid that if Tie Xiong approaches that door, the leaking qi will resonate with the universe and tear him to shreds."

The black-clad man behind him was startled. His strength wasn't as great as the envoy's, so he hadn't seen things so clearly.

Hearing this analysis now, he could only feel helpless.

"Then this trip will probably be for nothing."

The Carefree Envoy patted the railing. "Forget it. Good seedlings aren't so easy to find. In another hundred or two hundred years, this Ruins Immortal City might produce another expert like Xiao Feng's father. Go and inventory the goods. After the martial competition is over, we'll return to Carefree City!"

"Yes, my lord!"

After the black-clad man left, the Carefree Envoy closed the window, looking thoroughly bored.

This trip to Ruins Immortal City seemed to be nothing more than a routine errand. There were no surprises to be found.

(End of chapter)
