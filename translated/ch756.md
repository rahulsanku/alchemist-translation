# Chapter 756: Fourth-Realm Body Cultivation Is Only This Much, Southern Frontier Gu Cultivator Hiding Head and Tail

What the sudden arrival of the two Dao protectors meant was clearer to Fu Qinglan than anyone.

She spread her divine sense and searched the surroundings.

Very soon, two figures appeared within her perception.

"They really came."

Whoosh!

Whoosh!

Two figures came from far to near and appeared within sight.

Before they had even arrived, a sweet, cloying female voice came over.

"Sage, where are those two illusion beasts?"

The black-robed cultivator who was locking onto Luo Chen with divine sense now said in a hoarse voice, "They have already been killed, and the illusion cores have fallen into this person's hands."

The woman froze, then cried out involuntarily, "That fast? How long has it been? Those were two fourth-tier illusion beasts!"

The black-robed cultivator had not yet had time to answer.

Fu Qinglan had already spoken. "Little Xu!"

Yu Xu had naturally recognized her when Fu Qinglan released her divine sense. At this moment, she nodded, shock still in her eyes.

They had discovered those two illusion beasts not long ago and had pursued them all the way here, so not much time had passed at all.

Yet now there was no trace of the illusion beasts!

"Two fourth-tier illusion beasts were truly killed by him alone in such a short time?"

Sage Heavenly Mountain, shrouded in the black robe, said faintly, "He borrowed this place's special terrain and arranged a grand formation targeting illusion beasts in advance. It is not strange that the illusion beasts were defeated in an instant once they entered the net."

His tone was calm, but the wariness within it was not concealed at all.

Yu Xu frowned and looked around. There were indeed traces of a formation's existence.

But the most important thing remained that person!

Fu Qinglan's Dao protector, Wilderness Wanderer!

At this moment, Fu Qinglan was already looking anxiously toward the other person. He wore white finery, dotted with golden cloud patterns, his face like crowned jade, and his pride pressing upon others.

"Junior Brother Xiang Qi."

This noble person was precisely Xiang Qi, the Xiang family genius from the first of Central Continent's Thirty-Six Aristocratic Clans!

Xiang Qi glanced at her, merely nodded in acknowledgment, then asked the great monk, "Master, do you have confidence in defeating this person?"

"Amitabha!"

Wrathful Vajra chanted a Buddhist name in a rough voice, then said solemnly:

"Defeating him will not be difficult, but if I wish to keep this benefactor here, I fear my strength may fall short."

Xiang Qi could not help raising an eyebrow.

His gaze continuously moved over that middle-aged man on the plain, whose hair was gray-white and whose air carried the weariness of one who had seen through worldly affairs.

Northern Sea rogue cultivator, Wilderness Wanderer!

Among the thirteen Dao protectors, he was the most mysterious, even more mysterious than Sage Heavenly Mountain of the Southern Frontier.

After all, Sage Heavenly Mountain's origins could be traced, while this person's rise had been far too brief, making it impossible to gather relevant intelligence.

Now that even the powerful Wrathful Vajra had no confidence in keeping him, his strength was clearly extraordinary.

Defeating him had no meaning!

Leaving him was also irrelevant.

What Xiang Qi wanted was to capture him!

"Yu Xu, how about our two sides join hands and capture Wilderness Wanderer?"

Yu Xu seemed to have expected this already. Her gaze swept over Fu Qinglan, then she laughed softly. "Two illusion cores, one for each person?"

"No!"

The corner of Xiang Qi's mouth lifted. "They both belong to you."

Yu Xu froze.

"The illusion cores go to you, but the Heavenly Secret Pill on his body must belong to me!" Xiang Qi said this.

So that was it!

Yu Xu suddenly understood. Xiang Qi had set his sights on the Heavenly Secret Pill on Wilderness Wanderer!

Outsiders did not know this pill, but the Yu family was an alchemy family and understood a great deal.

It was a fourth-tier pill the Alchemy Saint had improved after studying an incomparably precious incomplete formula of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, and it had extremely wondrous effects for Sages.

It was said that after those with outstanding talent consumed it, there was a ten-percent chance of comprehending law true intent.

Even some Ascendant ancestors longed for one but could not obtain it.

Over all these years, the Alchemy Saint had only perfected the formula. The number of times she had refined it could be counted on one hand.

The reason, supposedly, was the lack of a certain main material.

Many people did not know what that main material was.

But when the Alchemy Saint ascended, the Yu family found out!

It was shockingly spiritual impulse!

That sort of ethereal, unfathomable thing, hard to find in the world, could actually be refined into medicine. It was truly appalling to hear.

But to replicate such an achievement was harder than hard.

Spiritual impulse existed everywhere between heaven and earth, in every place, yet truly finding it was extremely difficult.

Often, only when a cultivator reached sudden enlightenment or ascended would it gather in large quantities.

Even inside Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, spiritual impulse was extremely rare. It was still something the Heavenly Origin Sect Master had intercepted in large quantities from the outside world, then had special creatures like illusion beasts absorb it and solidify it inside illusion cores. Only in this way could true disciples like them simply absorb tiny threads and wisps.

Ordinary alchemists like them had no ability at all to use it as medicine.

Now, Xiang Qi's idea was clearly to use the Heavenly Secret Pill as a substitute for illusion cores in cultivation.

After all, the Heavenly Secret Pill personally refined by the Alchemy Saint absolutely contained spiritual impulse several times, or even several dozen times, greater than that of an ordinary fourth-tier illusion core.

If Dao Sect true disciples like them obtained it, they would completely have the chance...

"Hm? Yu Xu, how are you considering it?"

Yu Xu was somewhat hesitant.

Watching from the side, Fu Qinglan was burning with anxiety. Seeing Yu Xu hesitate, she could not help letting out a breath of relief.

"Little Xu, you and I are the closest of friends. How can we face one another with weapons?"

Unexpectedly, Yu Xu shook her head decisively.

"Before saying such words, why did you not think about Little Lin's relationship with you? Was his relationship with you worse than ours?"

She knows we seized Lin Bufan's illusion core back then?

Fu Qinglan was left speechless.

Yu Xu ignored Fu Qinglan no further. What she was truly hesitating over was whether something as good as the Heavenly Secret Pill should really be given to Xiang Qi.

As for so-called deep sisterly affection, before the struggle for the Great Dao, she had never hesitated!

She could not help raising her head toward the black-robed cultivator.

"Sage Heavenly Mountain, in your view, what should be done?"

Sage Heavenly Mountain's voice was hoarse and unhurried. "Everything is for Fellow Daoist to decide."

Yu Xu hesitated no longer. Under Xiang Qi's gaze, she slowly nodded.

"Then let us join hands!"

Before her words had fallen, a scream sounded from the plain.

"Luo Chen, run!"

On the plain, Luo Chen had been watching those people speak back and forth with calm eyes all along.

Hearing Fu Qinglan's scream, he could not help grinning.

The other party was not coming for the illusion cores, but for that Heavenly Secret Pill of his.

They truly had a good plan!

He said loudly, "There is no need to run. Luo also wishes to experience the methods of a West Desert eminent monk and a Southern Frontier gu cultivator."

As he spoke, he stamped a foot.

On the three great mountains, formation light immediately rose one after another.

He would borrow this formation to fight these two.

Unexpectedly, after the other side decided to join hands, they had already tacitly devised a combined-attack method.

"I shall go first!"

The coarse voice suddenly exploded!

Like an enraged lion's roar, vast sound waves attacked Luo Chen.

Because the three mountains surrounded the area, these sound waves continuously echoed within, becoming rolling thunder.

A sound-wave spell?

Luo Chen frowned and temporarily loosened his connection to the grand formation, while drawing a deep breath.

His abdomen swelled like a ball.

The next moment!

"Roar!"

A strange sound, seeming beast yet not beast, human yet not human, erupted from his mouth.

It collided madly with those surging sound waves.

Luo Chen had seen many sound-wave spells, especially those performed with instruments such as qin, flutes, and drums.

He had even once obtained a sound-wave cultivation art called Immortal by the River Melody from an Eastern Wilderness Initiate rogue cultivator named Xiao Wanderer.

In order to learn the divine-soul attack attached to that cultivation art, he had once taken time to study it, but because he was not proficient in musical instruments, he had not delved very deeply into it.

However, when it came to sound-wave arts, he still understood some of their key tricks.

Now, with a desolate-beast physique and dense source power, he roared out according to the unique vocal rhythm of Immortal by the River Melody.

Rumble!

Rumble!

Above the plain, the lion's roar was like thunder, while the human cry was like a tide!

Under repeated impacts, large amounts of sounds like space breaking and barriers shattering exploded from nowhere.

It was at this moment that Luo Chen raised an eyebrow.

Under his divine sense perception, Wrathful Vajra had already moved.

His entire body was suffused with a red-gold color, transforming into a streak of golden light as he rushed toward the nearest great mountain.

With the momentum of one who would not turn back until he hit the southern wall, he charged inside.

His speed was extremely fast!

In the blink of an eye, he had pierced out from the other side of the great mountain.

His momentum did not stop!

At extremely high speed, he attacked the next mountain.

When he appeared again, his position had already exchanged with where he had begun.

Behind him, the three great mountains still stood.

Wrathful Vajra paused, pressed his palms together, then abruptly clapped them and exhaled with a shout.

"Break!"

The next moment, under everyone's shocked gazes, the three great mountains that had stood in Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven for several thousand years collapsed in an instant.

And the formation Luo Chen had set up, because the earth veins collapsed and the terrain changed, no longer had anything to anchor it.

A small cauldron spun down from the air.

While Luo Chen reached out to catch it, he swept his sleeve and scattered the remaining noise.

"You used the Buddhist Lion's Roar to seize the initiative, then used a body technique similar to the Mountain-Colliding Form to break the mountain range. In one smooth movement, you shattered my geographical advantage in an instant."

"Master, fine methods indeed!"

Luo Chen sighed in admiration, stepping out from the large amount of smoke and dust.

His body was unstained by dust, his hair gray-white, his eyes deep and dark. He naturally possessed the bearing of a grandmaster standing like a deep pool and towering mountain.

"Amitabha!"

Wrathful Vajra chanted a Buddhist name, then said solemnly, "Although Benefactor's sound-wave art is not as exquisite as my Lion's Wrath Thunder Roar, its fierce power is not inferior at all."

The true contact between the two of them had actually occurred in that sound-wave collision.

On the surface, Luo Chen had fallen into the disadvantage, but sound-wave arts were not his specialty to begin with, and with only a hurried roar, he had blocked the other party's prepared strike.

This alone showed the difference!

"Next, Benefactor must be careful!"

Wrathful Vajra raised both hands sideways. Surging momentum rose and fell like tides, and fierce astral winds gathered around him.

A monk's staff slowly appeared and fell into his hand.

In the sky, Sage Heavenly Mountain opened his mouth and spat, sending a small sword flying out. It circled beside him like a swimming fish.

Just as the two were about to act.

"Master, I want that small cauldron as well!"

Wrathful Vajra's momentum could not help stalling.

Xiang Qi was truly too greedy!

Who could not see that the gray small cauldron possessed wondrous power?

It could be said that Luo Chen's ability to easily subdue illusion beasts relied half on his own ability and half on the power of that small cauldron.

This Xiang Qi wanted both the Heavenly Secret Pill and the gray small cauldron, making later illusion-beast hunting easier!

So be it. Since he had become the man's Dao protector, he naturally had to satisfy the other party's requirements in the grotto-heaven as much as possible.

While his thoughts turned, he suddenly heard an explosive boom.

In his naturally dignified eyes, a weathered yet handsome face was reflected.

"How is his speed this fast?"

Shoulder lowered, elbow angled, pressing forward without fear!

It was precisely the Mountain-Colliding Form of the Extreme Mountain Six Forms!

This was answering the other with his own method!

Luo Chen slammed forward.

Boom!

On the earth, a golden figure was sent flying backward like an iron ball several li away. Even though he tried to stabilize himself in midair, he still staggered.

"Master, you were distracted."

Luo Chen laughed softly, lifted his foot, and stomped down again.

Voidburst Step activated once more!

In the void, afterimages flickered continuously, and when he appeared again, he was right several zhang above Wrathful Vajra's body.

He could even clearly see the shock and anger on the other party's face.

"I hear Buddhist schools excel at body refinement. Perfect. I want to test the methods of the fourth realm of body cultivation. Master should not disappoint me."

Luo Chen's body abruptly pressed downward.

Breaking through layers of astral wind, he actually intended to use his body as a great mountain and forcibly suppress the other party.

Wrathful Vajra slapped the ground with one hand and stabbed upward with the staff in his right.

Thud!

A strange sound rang out instantly.

Luo Chen's toe touched the monk's staff and was just about to exert force when a warning of "Careful!" came by his ear.

In truth, there was no need at all for Fu Qinglan to warn him. Luo Chen had long been prepared.

Without turning his head, he grabbed backward. His five fingers flexed and spread.

The Cloud-Seizing Divine Claw came casually to hand, grabbing toward the small flying sword breaking through the air.

However, that small sword was rather nimble and actually threaded through the gaps between his fingers without being slowed at all.

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow. "What a slippery flying sword, and what strange magic power."

As he sighed aloud, his hands formed seals, and layer after layer of fire rings erupted from around him.

The small sword continued advancing, but its power grew weaker and weaker.

Ding!

Like the bite of a mosquito, the sword tip stabbed Luo Chen's chest.

Luo Chen lowered his head for a look and grabbed toward it.

The small sword suddenly withdrew and retreated.

High in the sky, Sage Heavenly Mountain, who was forming seals to control the flying sword, warned in a low voice, "Master, be careful. This person's protective magic treasure should be at the true artifact tier."

"No matter. Watch me break it!"

After gaining Sage Heavenly Mountain's support, Wrathful Vajra finally obtained a chance to breathe.

He no longer resisted Luo Chen's enormous bodily suppressive force, instead borrowing its strength to fall into the ground. Like a knife cutting tofu, he easily merged into the earth.

Whoosh!

A streak of golden light flew out from the ground a hundred zhang away.

Afterward, large amounts of astral qi enveloped his body, and he wielded the nine-ringed monk's staff, smashing through the air.

Luo Chen nodded slightly in his heart.

Earth-Running Golden Light?

A fusion movement technique of the metal and earth attributes?

It was not inferior to his self-created Voidburst Step.

But if they were truly going to meet force with force?

Luo Chen smiled ferociously, his fleshly palm fearlessly grabbing toward the monk's staff.

Clang!

Clang!

Clang!

The sound of metal striking metal rang continuously from the collisions between the two.

Golden astral qi and blood-colored source power interwove with each other.

Such pure close combat was truly rare in today's cultivation world.

It had less elegant detachment and more bloody boldness.

In a short time, dozens upon hundreds of fierce exchanges occurred.

The eyelids of those watching jumped wildly.

Xiang Qi could no longer hold back and shouted in a low voice, "Yu Xu!"

Yu Xu understood what he meant and nodded toward Sage Heavenly Mountain.

The other party no longer watched from the side. Instead, he activated the flying sword again, transforming it into streaks of sword light that killed toward Luo Chen from every angle.

It seemed as though he was using his full strength.

Seeing this, Xiang Qi finally breathed a sigh of relief.

"That Wilderness Wanderer is truly rather difficult to deal with. Two great Sages joining hands, one fighting at close range and one attacking from afar, yet he can still both attack and defend. How did such a talent end up lowering himself to a last-rate clan like the Fu family?"

On Luo Chen's side, however, he did not think this way.

Wrathful Vajra was undoubtedly using his full strength, continuously displaying all sorts of exquisite body techniques and matching him blow for blow.

But Sage Heavenly Mountain was clearly slacking!

From their first contact, he should have known that his flying sword could not break the Mystic Dust Armor's defenses, even if he drove more magic power into it.

Yet he still pretended to exert his full strength in this way.

He could deceive inexperienced young geniuses like Xiang Qi and Yu Xu, but for Luo Chen, there was no pressure at all. The only one he had to face was Wrathful Vajra.

"Golden Astral Slash!"

A roar came by his ear. The bald monk held his staff high, using the staff as a saber, and with berserk astral qi amplifying it, abruptly slashed down.

Luo Chen's expression turned slightly solemn, and he condensed source power, punching out.

Boom!

This time, it was his turn to fly backward.

But as he flew backward, both hands continuously pointed with all ten fingers, sending out blood-colored source power like needles to block the other party's pursuit.

When Luo Chen landed, the flying sword attacked again.

Luo Chen did not fear its edge and flicked it away with one finger.

With this, the battlefield fell into a solemn stillness, as if it had returned to the earlier state of well water not offending river water.

Everyone held their breath. Even the proud and talkative Xiang Qi no longer opened his mouth.

Earlier, it had been because he opened his mouth that Wrathful Vajra's accumulating momentum was disrupted, leading to Luo Chen pressing the other party down and attacking.

Fu Qinglan also no longer warned Luo Chen. She already understood that it was not her place to warn Luo Chen, whose battle experience was so rich.

The only thing she did not understand was that Luo Chen clearly had profound spell methods, yet when facing enemies in battle, he had not used them at all.

Was he unable to, or unwilling?

On the earth, Luo Chen brushed his chest. Dust fell rustling from his Daoist robe, yet he did not seem at all disheveled.

His gaze swept continuously over Wrathful Vajra and Sage Heavenly Mountain.

Afterward, he slowly opened his mouth.

"A fourth-realm body cultivator seems only this much. I cannot see at all the might of the Leakless Golden Body recorded in ancient texts!"

Wrathful Vajra glared back at him!

He had indeed not cultivated the Leakless realm. This could be seen from the astral qi leaking outward when he fought.

In body cultivation's four realms, the first realm was Vajra, the second Blood River, the third Furnace, and the fourth Leakless.

Upon reaching the fourth realm, one's vast astral qi was hidden within, locking the essence qi of the entire body so that nothing leaked out. In battle, there would be no grand, majestic scene. Instead, one pursued concentrating power at a single point, making it difficult for enemies to withstand.

The reason he had still reached the fourth realm of body cultivation despite not cultivating the Leakless Golden Body was related to his excessive pursuit of the first realm's limit when he first touched the path of body cultivation.

He had possessed the Great Vajra physique very early on!

The title Wrathful Vajra largely came from his Great Vajra physique.

But success was due to the Great Vajra physique, and failure was also due to the Great Vajra physique.

By forcefully advancing to the fourth realm of body cultivation, he unexpectedly could not achieve a fully leakless body and lock away his own essence qi.

The reason he had agreed to the Xiang family's invitation this time and traveled thousands of li to Central Continent to participate in the Heavenly Origin Grand Competition was also because he wanted to obtain a Daoist body-cultivation art from the Xiang family.

By fusing Buddhist and Daoist methods this way, he could make up for the gaps in his golden body.

Unexpectedly, this Wilderness Wanderer's eyes were so sharp and his knowledge so broad that, after several hundred brief exchanges, Luo Chen had seen the clues.

Seeing the other party glare angrily at him, Luo Chen shook his head, then looked toward the black-robed cultivator standing high in the sky.

"Rumor says Southern Frontier cultivators are skilled at using gu. Not only do they cultivate with gu, they often borrow gu insects in battle as well. Yet I have not seen Fellow Daoist display any similar methods. Hiding your head and tail like this leaves Luo rather disappointed."

Sage Heavenly Mountain's voice was hoarse. "I think it would be better if you did not see them."

Luo Chen blinked. "Very well. Then I will not see them."

The moment his words fell, his Sage domain unfolded with a rumble!

Dense, dry-hot spiritual qi pressed down on everyone until they almost could not breathe.

With his strength alone, he pressured the entire field!

Borrowing this opportunity, he rose into the air and shot straight into the high sky.

In his vision, Wrathful Vajra's astral qi surged into the sky as he forcibly broke free of the domain's suppression, transforming into a streak of golden light and rushing toward him.

At the same time, Sage Heavenly Mountain released his Sage domain, lightly drew a fingertip, and the small sword vanished into the void. Its sword track could not be seen, while its sword momentum seeped deeper, as if it were everywhere.

"Let us end this."

Luo Chen murmured, and behind him, a pair of wings unfolded with a boom.

An enormous fire phoenix shot into the sky, wreathed in azure flame tail-wings as it swept across the eight wilds.

It was precisely the grandmaster-level fourth-tier fire spell, Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain!

*(End of Chapter)*
