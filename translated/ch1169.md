# Chapter 1169: Light of Order, Fragments of Law

Whenever a cultivator with the means refines their life-bound treasure, they will pursue comprehensiveness to the greatest extent possible.

Luo Chen was no exception.

From the moment the Primordial Unity Cauldron was born, it possessed both offensive and defensive capabilities, while simultaneously integrating the five major functions of suppression, entrapment, nurturing, and refinement.

Offense and defense went without saying. The reason these two aspects had not shown their power before was that Luo Chen himself had better offensive and defensive methods.

As for the entrapment function, it had repeatedly displayed its might during Luo Chen's Adept and Sage periods, helping him survive many dangerous battles.

The suppression ability had not been particularly impressive before.

However, before ascending, in order to enhance the Primordial Unity Cauldron's foundation, Luo Chen had directly refined three fifth-grade spirit mountains into it.

It was simply that he had not encountered a suitable opponent; otherwise, Luo Chen would have very much wanted to test just how powerful the suppression ability would be after unleashing the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

After all, those three spirit mountains were Heaven-Earth Peak, Mijie Mountain, and the Yuan Magnetic Divine Mountain!

Among these functions, the last one—nurturing and refinement—had always remained obscure and unremarkable.

But in reality, this function had played an extremely important role throughout Luo Chen's centuries of cultivation.

Whether it was alchemy or artifact forging, or nurturing treasures and refining medicinal ingredients, none of it was done without relying on the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Through day-after-day use, Luo Chen naturally came to understand that this nurturing and refinement function was also effective for refining foreign energies.

Therefore, he had tried using the Primordial Unity Cauldron to refine chaotic energy.

And the effect was indeed present!

For the same portion of chaotic energy, because his qi-blood furnace was powerful enough, Luo Chen could convert more qi-blood than others.

While others could convert less than one percent, he could achieve one or two percent.

After adding the Primordial Unity Cauldron, this efficiency doubled, allowing him to convert around two to three percent.

The remaining energy was difficult to refine and could only be expelled through the apertures as impurities.

But!

What had just happened was absolutely not the case!

A surge of chaotic energy, refined through the Primordial Unity Cauldron, had achieved one hundred percent conversion!

This was an increase of a hundredfold.

Throughout the process, no impure energy was wasted; it was all converted into the qi-blood power that Luo Chen currently needed most.

If such a thing were spoken of, it would drive any martial artist mad.

One must know that even with Luo Chen's knowledge, he could not fully distinguish the compositional structure within chaotic energy.

From it, he could see various attributes of spiritual qi, as well as yin nether balefulness, deathly still ghost qi, overbearing demon qi, and so on. There were even some things resembling the immortal qi within immortal essence stones, along with some indescribable and unknowable substances.

Those things, even he found difficult to refine completely.

But the Primordial Unity Cauldron had truly accomplished it.

And the reason for this change... the gray light!

Back during the expedition to the Demon-Fall Land, Luo Chen had gained many harvests, among which were quite a few things that shocked the world.

For instance, the alchemy inheritance of Devil Lord Heaven-Refining, the Five-Spirit Dao Body, and even the recipe for the Dao Union Pill that Rosycloud herself had personally requested.

But compared to these things, the most mysterious was undoubtedly the wisp of gray light that had escaped after the Heaven-Refining Cauldron self-destructed.

Several hundred years had passed, yet even with Luo Chen's knowledge, he could not discern what kind of existence that gray light truly was.

Whenever he tried to investigate it ordinarily, he could not find its trace either.

He only knew that it had always resided within the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

He had never expected that at this time, in this place, under these circumstances, it would trigger a reaction from the gray light.

Luo Chen suppressed the agitation in his heart, striving for absolute calm.

He paid no attention even to the refined energy liquid, focusing his entire attention on his life-bound treasure.

The gray light and the gray small cauldron, the same color, stood side by side yet distinctly separate.

During the process of refining chaotic energy, they gradually merged into one, becoming indistinguishable from each other.

And as the gray light diffused and permeated, the chaotic energy that had been drawn in earlier became no longer violent, but docile and obedient. The complex energy composition also separated in an orderly fashion.

Then, under the operation of the Primordial Unity Cauldron, it was continuously refined one by one.

The entire process was filled with a sense of "order."

"Order?"

This thought arose spontaneously in Luo Chen's mind.

But what followed was a sense of absurdity.

The entire Great Destruction Realm, even the entire Guixu, was said to have shattered the Great Dao, with incomplete laws, being a place of disorder.

Under such circumstances, to speak of order?

How could Luo Chen not find it absurd?

"My observation is still not detailed enough!"

Luo Chen vaguely felt that this might be the greatest opportunity in his pursuit of immortality. He absolutely could not, as before, muddle through things he did not understand.

For this reason, even taking some risks was extremely necessary.

Having made up his mind, Luo Chen carefully released a strand of divine thought from his main soul, which had already weakened to its limit within his sea of consciousness.

Throughout the process, all the apertures in his body were tightly closed, not leaking a single bit.

Then, this strand of divine thought silently seeped into the Primordial Unity Cauldron like spring rain.

As a cultivator's life-bound treasure, although Luo Chen could barely activate it with qi-blood power, divine thought remained the best method of activation.

Now, with the familiar divine thought appearing, the changes in the Primordial Unity Cauldron became clearer in his perception.

He could feel that, due to the addition of qi-blood nurturing, those blood-vessel-like meridians on the Primordial Unity Cauldron truly existed. And through day-after-day refinement, they tightly connected all the materials, including the three divine mountains.

He could even feel that the Primordial Unity Cauldron had been faintly emitting a message of "hunger," caused by being deprived of dharma power nurturing for too long.

But what truly shook his mind was that during the refinement of chaotic energy, the converted energy went to him, while some other faint existences, both familiar and unfamiliar, were absorbed by the Primordial Unity Cauldron itself.

After absorbing those things, the Primordial Unity Cauldron began another transformation, as if it were a metamorphosis in the essence of life.

What exactly was it absorbing?

Luo Chen pondered deeply through that feeling of both familiarity and unfamiliarity.

Suddenly!

"Law!"

Boom!

His mind shook like a great bell, and Luo Chen was forced out of his state of extreme calm observation.

His divine thought also quietly withdrew back into his sea of consciousness.

Opening his eyes, Luo Chen's expression was extremely complicated.

There was both shock and confusion, as well as fear of the unknown.

He was certain he had not seen wrongly!

Although the quantity was extremely scarce, negligible even, the Primordial Unity Cauldron was indeed absorbing something akin to "law fragments" from within the chaotic energy.

That very thing, he himself had once comprehended, and had even seen its concrete manifestation from the ocean of origin qi when he advanced to the Ascendant realm.

Therefore, just now, Luo Chen had felt both familiar and unfamiliar.

What was familiar was that within the faint "laws," there were some that he understood—the five elements laws.

What was unfamiliar was that the other parts were laws he himself had never comprehended.

Luo Chen once again recalled something that City Lord Mofeng had mentioned before.

The reason cultivators could not cultivate within the Destruction Nine Rings was not only due to the physical body but also due to conflicts at the soul level.

Because chaotic energy might contain "law fragments," which were extremely dangerous for high-level cultivators who had comprehended the true meaning of laws.

Once they inexplicably absorbed law fragments they had not mastered, their souls would instantly perish.

At the time, Luo Chen had been half-convinced.

Now, with mutual corroboration, Luo Chen believed that the rumor City Lord Mofeng had heard was actually true.

Because he had truly seen concrete law fragments!

And his life-bound treasure, under the blessing of the gray light, had absorbed them!

"Light of Order," Luo Chen tentatively called that mysterious gray light.

He did not know where Devil Lord Heaven-Refining had obtained this thing, but the Light of Order had now merged uniquely with his life-bound treasure, aiding his cultivation as if with divine help, and was even driving the Primordial Unity Cauldron to absorb law fragments, furthering its transformation.

Because of this wisp of light, Luo Chen saw, for the first time, hope of leaving Guixu.

His complex gaze gazing into the dark and profound void, Luo Chen's emotions surged as he murmured to himself.

"One day, I will unravel the secret of the Light of Order."

In the short term, Luo Chen would no longer mobilize his divine thought; that was too dangerous.

Moreover, in his view, being able to observe the surface was already fortunate, relying on the fact that the Primordial Unity Cauldron, as his life-bound treasure, was cultivated in tandem with his life. To see more through this was not something he could accomplish at his current realm.

But in any case, it was enough that it benefited him!

It took a long time for Luo Chen to slowly calm his emotions.

Then he turned his thoughts back to the beginning.

He activated his qi-blood furnace, converting those pure energies one by one into vast qi-blood, which then became Blood-Refined Divine Astral.

These astral energies were transported to his apertures, wrapping around those fragile divided souls.

Following the heart method of *God-Devil Formless*, he used soul power to influence the astral energy, gradually making it spiritual.

In this way, the astral energy and divided souls formed a mutually beneficial pattern within the apertures.

One wrapped and protected, the other influenced and bestowed spirituality.

Luo Chen's realm also steadily and rapidly increased in this process.

Several days later.

Luo Chen had a sudden inspiration.

For ordinary martial artists, the cultivation mode within the Destruction Nine Rings relied almost entirely on the qi-blood furnace. To avoid bursting themselves, they needed to absorb a portion of chaotic energy and then close their apertures. Only after completely refining it would they open them again.

This cycle repeated, forming a periodic loop.

But he was different now.

He was a special existence.

There was no need to use the qi-blood furnace to refine chaotic energy at all.

Because this task had already been taken over by the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

In that case, why not loosen some restraints?

With this thought, Luo Chen felt an urge to try.

"Let's give it a try!"

He attempted to open all his apertures, allowing chaotic energy to enter his body while simultaneously driving both the Primordial Unity Cauldron and the qi-blood furnace to operate with divided attention.

The former refined the chaotic energy, the latter converted it into qi-blood.

After one incense stick's time.

Luo Chen grunted and immediately closed all his apertures.

Blood trickled from the corners of his mouth, a sign of internal organ damage.

It was indeed too much chaotic energy rushing in.

Luo Chen took out a large number of pills for repairing internal organs and swallowed them in one gulp.

While refining the medicinal power and repairing his organs, Luo Chen's eyes sparkled with light.

Although it had been very risky just now and he had suffered some backlash, he had verified that this path was feasible.

"It's just a matter of controlling the amount of chaotic energy that rushes in."

"This is not difficult."

"Simply by controlling the number of open and closed apertures, I can achieve the desired effect."

The human body was like a large water tank, while the external chaotic energy was like rivers, lakes, and seas.

If one tried to draw in the water of rivers, lakes, and seas all at once, the human body's tank would naturally not be able to bear it and would burst.

But since the tank itself was sealed, as long as one opened fewer water inlets, the amount of water entering could be relatively reduced.

Then, the Primordial Unity Cauldron would quickly refine the introduced chaotic energy.

This process required consideration of balance.

But as long as balance was achieved, Luo Chen could save the time of periodic cycles and remain in a constant state of cultivation.

"Let's take it step by step!"

Luo Chen first opened one aperture, guiding chaotic energy into his body while rapidly refining it.

This continued for several days without any problems.

Reassured, Luo Chen began to increase the load, opening a second, third, and even more apertures.

And his martial realm began to skyrocket at an inconceivable speed.

...

One year later.

With a rumbling sound, the Ninefold Immortal Tower officially closed.

Those martial artists who wanted to enter and cultivate would have to wait another hundred years.

Yu Huai was quite lucky.

On the last day, he had mastered the process of refining chaotic energy and planned to formally enter the Ninefold Immortal Tower.

His subsequent plan was also simple.

Step by step!

First, figure out the limit of chaotic energy he could absorb at one time, then refine it in small, multiple batches.

Steady and stable, avoiding all danger.

After a hundred years, his strength would surely increase greatly!

However, the urgent matter was still to find a cultivation stone as a place to stand.

"Although I've heard there are many cultivation stones inside the tower, I've entered too late. The good spots are probably already taken by others."

"Those people entered before me, so naturally they are stronger than me. Robbing them is certainly impossible."

"If I can find a small foothold, that would be good enough."

Yu Huai flew through the dark, deep void, constantly observing his surroundings, trying to find a suitable cultivation stone.

He hadn't flown for long.

Suddenly!

Yu Huai's figure paused, his gaze falling not far away.

On a two-foot-long, two-foot-wide meteorite, a white-robed Daoist sat cross-legged.

That person, Yu Huai recognized.

It was Luo Chen!

The legendary genius with the bearing of an immortal, and the first among this batch of newly advanced Leakless Realm martial artists to enter the Destruction Nine Rings.

"This person is not to be provoked."

Yu Huai planned to take a detour.

But unexpectedly, the white-robed Daoist opened his eyes and looked at him.

Yu Huai hastily waved his hand: "Brother Luo, don't misunderstand. I'm just passing through."

During cultivation, the most taboo thing was being disturbed by others.

In this environment with no one monitoring, martial artists either lived alone in seclusion or cultivated in groups, all to ensure they were not affected, or even ambushed.

However, Luo Chen stood up casually.

"You just entered, right? Do you have a cultivation stone to settle on?"

Yu Huai hesitated for a moment, then shook his head.

Luo Chen smiled slightly. "Then this place is yours."

With that, Luo Chen flicked his sleeve and vanished into the profound void.

Yu Huai stood there dumbfounded, somewhat incredulous.

This cultivation stone had a decent area, and there were few people nearby to disturb it. It could be considered a good cultivation environment.

And Luo Chen just gave it to him?

Hesitating, Yu Huai ultimately stood on that meteorite.

After a brief adjustment, he tentatively began to cultivate.

But a strange feeling arose.

"How can the chaotic energy around here be so thin?"

"That Luo Chen could actually endure this kind of cultivation environment?"

"Never mind!"

"Let's first secure a foothold. It's also a good chance to test my limits."

Yu Huai made up his mind and settled down here.

But a few days later, he suddenly woke from his cultivation state.

A surge of qi seemed to rush in from all around!

Only at this moment did he suddenly realize that this place was no different from anywhere else.

But why had it been like that before?

A thought that he did not want to dwell on, yet could not help but think, surfaced in his mind.

"Could it be that all the chaotic energy here was absorbed clean by Luo Chen?"
