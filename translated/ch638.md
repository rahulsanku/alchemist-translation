# Chapter 638: You Have No Choice; I Will Let You Die with Some Dignity

Luo Chen's bewilderment came quickly and went quickly.

This was related to his personality.

Pragmatic!

Even if the road was long, as long as he walked forward one solid step at a time, there would eventually be a day he arrived.

During the process of advancing, he would inevitably encounter many problems.

And these problems were like scenery. They could be remembered in the heart, but there was no need to linger and forget to return.

Unless these problems completely blocked his path forward, he rarely became trapped in a dead end.

Beside the cold pool-abyss, apart from the major problem of "the higher the realm, the more flaws," Luo Chen had indeed found three problems limiting his current combat strength.

In truth, for ordinary Golden Core cultivators, these problems were not troubling things at all.

Because no one would every day treat great cultivators, or even Sage Perfected Beings one realm higher, as imagined enemies.

Moreover, Luo Chen's own ability was already enough to crush early Golden Core, mid Golden Core, and even not fall into any disadvantage when facing late Golden Core great cultivators.

He merely wanted to go "one step further."

According to Luo Chen's thoughts, if he could solve the three current problems, perhaps he could truly run rampant among those of the same realm!

Even if he encountered an ambush like the Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft's again, he would not be so battered.

"Spells, magic treasures, developing the physique..."

As Luo Chen murmured, his mind seemed like a ball of tangled thread, with nowhere to begin.

His gaze subconsciously fell at his feet, on the formation flag he had planted into the solid ground.

A thread seemed to be pulled from the tangled ball.

...

On Purple Spirit Island, beside where the fourth-tier Purple Monkey Flower once grew on the cliff face, the Purple Monkey Flower no longer swayed in the wind. Instead, there was a simple cave residence.

In the end, it was a place capable of supporting the growth of a fourth-tier spirit plant. The spiritual qi quality here barely reached the level of newly entered third-tier.

Unlike human cultivators and demon beasts, the growing place of spirit plants had requirements for spiritual qi concentration, but not that high.

Before awakening intelligence, spirit plants all swallowed the spiritual qi of heaven and earth unconsciously. Some spirit plants did not rely on spiritual qi to grow at all.

They valued more the essential transformation brought by long years and time.

Of course, this growth mode also caused spirit plants, even after giving birth to spirit intelligence and possessing realms as high as fourth-tier or even fifth-tier, to have extremely low battle strength.

The Withered Blood Vine was like this. Purple Monkey Flowers were also like this.

But in any case, the spirit vein here could support Luo Chen's daily cultivation for a short time.

Inside the cave residence.

Two objects were placed before Luo Chen.

One was a dark green finger ring, the other a jade box.

The ring was the Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft's spatial storage ring. Apart from the treasures left behind on Purple Spirit Island during the previous battle, the Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft's lifetime accumulation should be inside.

It could not be ruled out that this person had traveled light and left most of his accumulation at the sect, Hundred Craft Mountain.

Luo Chen carefully observed it. The "password" on the dark green ring was rather complicated.

Not only were there ordinary divine sense restrictions and mana imprints, it had even been bound by blood recognition.

For it to have such specifications showed that this ring itself was a rather good magic treasure.

The latter two were both easy to remove. Even the blood recognition could be easily removed as long as the Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft completely died.

Only the divine sense restrictions were rather troublesome.

The Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft was not only a formidable artifact-forging master, but also a formation master with no shallow attainments.

This could be seen from how he had arranged that formation specifically targeting Luo Chen's powerful physique on Seven Stars Island according to local conditions.

Therefore, the divine sense restrictions on his own storage ring were arranged rather intricately.

With Luo Chen's divine soul foundation and his attainments in restrictions, to remove them, according to his estimate, would take at least three or four years.

As for that jade box...

Luo Chen's eyes flashed. He struck a stream of mana onto the jade box.

Immediately, the dense restrictions sealing the jade box broke layer by layer like falling dominoes.

Spiritual light suddenly bloomed, and a dim Golden Core entered his eyes.

"Fellow Daoist, long time no see."

The Golden Core spun up, then after wobbling, finally fell back into the jade box.

During this process, Luo Chen looked on coldly and felt somewhat thoughtful.

Before cultivators advanced to Sage, their divine souls lived in the sea of consciousness. Even when divine sense was released outward, it was only a small branch and minor detail.

Once the physical body was destroyed, the divine soul had nothing to rely on.

Unless it thoroughly transformed into a ghost cultivator or resentful soul, it would be completely erased if it encountered blazing sun exposure or attacks from other firm and fierce things.

However, this restriction was not entirely without solution.

The Golden Core stage was such a transitional stage.

The Golden Core was condensed from pure mana.

And mana was formed from the pure spiritual power the cultivator refined, after being tainted and fused with the divine soul.

From this point, a cultivator's Golden Core already possessed a slight divine soul attribute.

After a Golden Core cultivator's physical body collapsed, the remaining Golden Core could serve as a boat crossing the sea, carrying the cultivator's divine soul.

However, this boat could not compare to a Nascent Soul. It was truly too small, unable to cross the bitter sea, and the period it could carry the soul was short beyond short.

In addition, without later mana replenishment, the Golden Core's original mana would also dissipate day by day, and the divine soul relying on it would ultimately have to face the blazing daylight.

"I wonder, if I reached such a state, to what extent could my Golden Core protect my remnant soul?"

The thought flashed and vanished, and Luo Chen again looked at that dim Golden Core.

"Mountain Lord, why go to such pains?"

A faint divine sense voice transmission sounded dimly from within the Golden Core.

"Twenty years of pursuit, yet today I have become a prisoner. Indeed, why did I do it?"

Luo Chen shook his head. "The wrong lies neither with you, nor with me. It was merely a Perfected Being's order, and you had no choice."

The Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft froze. While feeling sorrowful, he also sensed something wrong.

Saying Blood Nightmare Moluo had pressured him was no problem.

But saying he had no fault was somewhat against conscience.

Back then, on Cold Light Island, he had personally taken part in surrounding and killing Luo Chen.

If not for that incident, Blood Nightmare Moluo would not have found him.

Cause and effect ultimately lay on his side.

But why did Luo Chen take the initiative to absolve him?

His sight involuntarily fell on that ring: his storage ring.

Immediately, the Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft gave a mocking laugh.

"You are thinking of my inheritance!"

Luo Chen calmly said, "We are both artifact-forgers. You presumably sensed a little of my restriction attainments from this jade box's seal. To undo the restrictions on your storage ring, how much time do you think I would spend?"

"Seven or eight years? Three or five years?"

In any case, the other party simply did not need to beg anyone for this cooked duck.

The Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft hesitated briefly, then asked, "Then what do you want? My artifact-forging art?"

"Luo's aspiration is not here."

Neither for the inheritance, nor the artifact-forging art? Then what did Devil Lord Azure Yang want?

The Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft completely sank into confusion.

However, this confusion did not last long.

With a prisoner, Luo Chen had no need to speak in circles. The earlier conversation was only to calm the other party's emotions.

Seeing that there was already a gentle chatting atmosphere, he went straight to the point.

"When you set the ambush against me before, what formation did you use?"

"Northern Dipper Seven Peaks Array."

"Northern Dipper Seven Peaks Array?"

"Mm. This formation was improved by this old man from the Northern Dipper Demon-Sealing Array. Demon beasts have enormous bodies, and many are incomparably agile. The Northern Dipper Demon-Sealing Array is a fourth-tier formation specifically used by the Northern Sea cultivation world to suppress powerful demon beasts. As for powerful body cultivators, their physical tempering is comparable to demon beasts. After improving it and combining it with my lifebound magic treasure, it can produce very good targeting."

When he spoke to here, the Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft was rather self-satisfied.

But that complacency vanished in a flash.

In the end, even with the first-move advantage, he had still lost to Luo Chen. What was there to be proud of?

He looked at Luo Chen in confusion. "Why are you asking this? Are you afraid of encountering similar formations in the future and want to prepare countermeasures in advance?"

Luo Chen shook his head. "Not quite. After all, existences like you, who spent twenty years hunting me, are few and far between. Although I cannot say I am so wise as to seem demonic, I am also not a foolish person. Being ambushed is enough once. In the future, naturally I will not again step into another's formation ambush."

Faced with these words, the Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft fell silent.

He did not doubt the other party's confidence, or rather, he did not deny his own patience.

How many fools like him existed in the world?

After half a moment of silence, he spoke again.

"Then what do you want to know?"

Luo Chen spoke frankly. "I want to know how you improved this formation, and how you raised its power to that degree."

So he was interested in this aspect!

Understanding this, the Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft chose to bargain.

"Why should I tell you?"

Luo Chen said coldly, "You have no choice."

"I can choose death!"

"Your life and death are held in my hand. Do you think you can choose? I fear you do not know, but I also understand some human puppet methods. In addition, I know a Golden Core cultivator of the Heavenly Demon Star Sect. I hear that sect is best at using demon beast inner cores and cultivator Golden Cores to refine secret treasures. Presumably, a cultivator Golden Core with a complete soul can certainly be refined into something powerful."

"You!!!"

The divine soul shook, and the divine sense voice was full of anger.

But after the anger, the Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft quickly calmed down again. "Even if I tell you, what benefit is there? Will you let me go?"

"Go? Where can you go?" Luo Chen's tone was calm. "In this boundless sea, human traces are rare. Even if you want to seize a body, a clever wife cannot cook without rice. Moreover, you and I have a life-and-death grudge. It is impossible for me to let you go."

The Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft laughed from fury. "Then what do you want?"

"Tell me the answer, and I will let you die with some dignity." Luo Chen's expression was indifferent. The words from his mouth were calm, yet carried quite some weight. Hearing them, others would seem to have no doubt he was lying.

The Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft fell silent again.

Luo Chen also said nothing more. He made no extra oath or guarantee, which would have been gilding the lily.

At this moment, he was the one in control.

The Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft's divine sense kept looking at him. Only after a long while did a faint sound come from the cave residence.

"I can tell you."

"In that case, good!"

...

On a small island fifty li from Purple Spirit Island.

Black King lay there, looking at an earthen pit, unwillingness in his eyes.

"Master, are we really wasting it? This is the Golden Core of a Golden Core Stage Eight cultivator. Even if it does not have much remaining mana, if you let me eat it, perhaps I could directly step into the third-tier late period."

Luo Chen was too lazy to look at him. After digging the earthen pit, he took out a piece of rather good stone from the Accumulated Thunder Treasure Ring and was about to carve words on it.

However, just as he was about to write, a voice came out.

"No need. This old man has wronged Hundred Craft Mountain in this life, wronged Fellow Daoist, and wronged himself. What face do I have to erect a monument and leave words?"

Luo Chen hummed and took the stone back.

"That is fine. It saves leaving a tombstone, only for someone in the future to dig up the grave and think it is a predecessor's opportunity."

"Heh, Fellow Daoist is truly humorous." The Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft laughed once, but the laugh was somewhat bitter.

Looking at that pit, the Golden Core seemed to tremble.

He had bitterly cultivated for four hundred years, and once had glorious displays. Was this his destination?

The sea wind blew, salty, damp, and hot.

Seeming to guess what the Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft was thinking, Luo Chen softly consoled, "Rumor says reincarnation exists in the world. After a thousand years, Fellow Daoist may be able to step onto the cultivation path again."

"Reincarnation?" The Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft murmured. "Is it at the blue sky and yellow springs, or in the Netherworld underworld, or perhaps in the return-to-void of all things?"

Luo Chen had no answer. He had not reached that realm, so how could he know the truth of legends?

The Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft sighed. "Take out my storage ring!"

Luo Chen was surprised for a moment, then took out the dark green ring from his robes.

The dim Golden Core trembled for an instant, and its few remaining traces of mana landed on the ring. Spiritual light shone greatly, then finally returned to extinction.

Looking at it now, the divine sense restrictions and mana imprints on it were completely gone. Even the fused essence blood had been forced out.

At this moment, this ring was ownerless!

"Mountain Lord, what is this?"

"Consider it extra compensation after you kept your promise! Though it was originally your battle spoils anyway, this old man merely saves you several years of effort."

The Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft smiled, then mobilized mana and took out a clean bright-yellow Daoist robe, a flying sword law device only of upper-grade, and a jade seal engraved with the words Liang Clan Hundred Craft.

Under Luo Chen and Black King's gazes, the Daoist robe, flying sword, and jade seal all fell into the earthen pit.

"The rest of the things will be yours in the future, Fellow Daoist."

The Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft sighed faintly, and the Golden Core flew down into the earthen pit.

Afterward, mana reversed, and the Golden Core collapsed layer by layer. A wisp of remnant soul appeared in the world.

Under the warm sunlight, the remnant soul gradually scattered.

Luo Chen silently watched everything until his divine sense swept over and there was no longer any trace of the Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft's aura. Then his sleeve waved, and soil completely covered the earthen pit.

He turned and left.

At that newly covered yellow soil, no one would imagine that this was the cenotaph of a sect master, a Golden Core Stage Eight great cultivator.

Black King swam his enormous body, closely following behind.

"Master, this old fellow ambushed you. Why still let him dissolve himself, giving him a chance to reincarnate?"

Luo Chen did not answer. His steps only grew faster and faster.

Before long, he returned to Purple Spirit Island.

After returning to the cave residence, he took out the jade slip analyzing the Northern Dipper Seven Peaks Array that the Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft had left him before dying.

Looking at the words on it, Luo Chen sank into thought.

"Powerful lifebound magic treasures can amplify mana, and powerful formations can both amplify oneself and restrict others."

"If the two are combined, then paired with a decent energy source, can it perfect my original conception of a Sage domain?"

He opened his mouth and spat, and three streams of light suddenly flew out.

Looking carefully, they were clearly two cores and one cauldron.

Golden Core, Origin Pill, Primordial Unity Cauldron!

*(End of Chapter)*
