# Chapter 881: Divine Fire Extinguishes the Demon, Divine Origin Retreats in Shock

Heavenly tribulation!

According to legend, a cultivator must undergo three heavenly tribulations in their lifetime.

Each tribulation consists of three rounds, making nine rounds in total, hence it is also called the Three-Nine Heavenly Tribulation.

For cultivators, every heavenly tribulation is the most perilous checkpoint on the path of cultivation.

Survive it, and the sky is the limit—one's entire realm and strength undergo earth-shattering changes!

But if one fails to cross it, they sink like a fish into the abyss, unable to turn things around.

For demon beasts, the terror of heavenly tribulation is even more dreadful.

Human cultivators often master various means, cleverly using the hundred arts of immortal cultivation to strengthen themselves, facing heavenly tribulation with relative composure.

But demon beasts, before they have transformed, due to their late awakening of spiritual intelligence and the limitations of their beastly bodies, can only endure heavenly tribulation through brute force.

Thus, even after many fourth-rank demon emperors have achieved their status for years, they still harbor a bone-deep fear of heavenly tribulation.

At this moment, facing the heavenly tribulation that Luo Chen had suddenly summoned, both Huang Man and Bi Zheng were stunned.

How could a mere living being use personal power to summon the punitive force of heaven and earth?

Though hesitant in their hearts, their hands moved again.

They each struck out at the Primordial Unity Cauldron, which was spinning in midair.

Neither was foolish; they quickly discerned that Luo Chen's method of summoning heavenly tribulation likely relied on that gray cauldron. Thus, this attack served both to test the power of the tribulation and to deal with the cauldron itself.

*Crackle!*

*Crackle!*

Both attacks were intercepted mid-flight by black arcs of lightning.

But after only a brief stalemate, the two attacks broke through the thunderous power and struck the Primordial Unity Cauldron itself.

*Thump! Thump!*

Under the assault, the cauldron wobbled again.

Even though this treasure possessed a terrifying foundation, it was still somewhat inadequate before the attacks of two great demon emperors.

In fact, tiny cracks began to appear on the cauldron's patterned walls.

*Boom!*

With a thunderous roar, the Primordial Unity Cauldron trembled violently, and from its mouth crawled out a strange beast resembling a deer.

In the great river.

Luo Chen lay on his back, gazing at the deer-shaped thunder beast, a grin spreading across his face.

This thunder beast originated from the final round of tribulation he had summoned back when he formed his Nascent Soul.

It had been suppressed within the Primordial Unity Cauldron for so many years.

He had also explored methods of using the power of tribulation lightning—to temper his divine soul, but it was too powerful; his own body could not withstand it.

Using it to refine treasures, he had obtained the true artifact, the Dustbound Immortal Mirror. But when he tried to replicate the feat, other treasures lacked the foundation of the Dustbound Immortal Mirror and could not bear the power of tribulation lightning either.

Today, he would release it to slay his great enemies!

"However, it's still not quite enough. I need to supplement it!"

Luo Chen took a deep breath, his single palm striking the surging river in reverse.

Using the rebounding force, he shot into the air once more, gripping the blood sword as he charged toward Bi Zheng.

The two great demon emperors, who had just tested the tribulation, breathed a sigh of relief after witnessing the power of the thunder.

"This heavenly tribulation is strong, but it cannot threaten us directly."

"All smoke and mirrors. Kill Luo Chen first!"

The two demons exchanged glances, temporarily deciding to ignore the tribulation hanging high in the sky.

After all, having transformed for so many years, how could they fear mere heavenly thunder summoned by a Golden Core cultivator undergoing tribulation?

Facing Luo Chen charging at him again, Bi Zheng grinned ferociously.

"Treating me as a soft persimmon, trying to break through on my side?"

In his sneer, the black curved blade transformed into countless forms once more, like a myriad of crescent moons spreading across several li of land.

Luo Chen slashed out streaks of sword light horizontally, cleaving apart those curved blades, but his momentum was already weakening. By the time he reached within a few zhang of Bi Zheng, he had no strength left to continue.

He struck out with a palm!

Bi Zheng returned the gesture with a palm of his own.

*Boom!*

Under the mutual force, Bi Zheng lightly floated backward.

Luo Chen wanted to press the advantage, but the scattered blade lights converged again.

Watching this scene, Bi Zheng shook his somewhat numb left hand, marveling inwardly at Luo Chen's immense strength and finding this man indeed troublesome to deal with.

Frowning, he looked at Huang Man.

"Why have you stopped, Fellow Daoist?"

It turned out that just when they had agreed to act together, Huang Man had flown halfway and then suddenly halted.

Huang Man's expression was now one of uncertainty as he pointed upward.

Bi Zheng looked up and couldn't help but change expression.

After the deer-shaped thunder beast appeared, perhaps wary of the two of them, it did not attack directly. Instead, it began to run across the sky.

With each of its runs and leaps, the dark clouds gathered deeper, and the arcs of lightning within flashed more frequently.

The power of this heavenly tribulation was continuously escalating!

The two demon emperors exchanged glances, reaching an understanding.

"Destroy the tribulation first!"

"Don't let it grow any stronger!"

This time, the two demons did not test but unleashed their ultimate techniques.

Huang Man placed his stone rod horizontally between his arms, pressed his palms together, then slowly opened them, revealing a square opening.

"Go!"

The next moment, a thick yellow beam of light shot into the thunderclouds.

Bi Zheng flew several li away, temporarily distancing himself from Luo Chen, then lightly spat out a wisp of black light toward the thunderclouds.

Facing the oncoming yellow beam and black light, the thunder beast clearly knew it could no longer continue growing.

It let out a roar, stamping its four hooves as it charged down.

Countless arcs of electricity, like silk ribbons, followed closely behind.

The three collided.

*Boom!*

After a terrifyingly loud explosion, the entire world darkened for a moment.

Countless silver-white lightning bolts writhed within like silver serpents dancing, a sight of utter horror.

Bi Zheng waved his hand, recalling the black light—it was clearly a sharp scale.

There were faint traces of residual lightning on it.

Bi Zheng frowned. This treasure was a material from a desolate beast's body he had accidentally obtained in the wilderness, and he hadn't been refining it for long.

"Fortunately, it wasn't damaged. Now that the tribulation is broken, next..."

His gaze fell upon Luo Chen, who was surrounded by his own natal true artifact.

Just then!

Luo Chen also looked over.

Holding the blood sword, his expression calm, his left hand hanging low, concealed within his sleeve.

"Now..."

"Withering-Bloom!"

The soft sound rang out as Luo Chen pointed a finger at him from afar.

In that instant, Bi Zheng felt his soul nearly flee in terror as he frantically retreated.

But no matter where he fled, that hazy green light followed him, as if he could not escape this world and thus could not avoid this strike.

"It's a divine ability!"

Bi Zheng roared inwardly, his eyes nearly splitting with rage!

Only a great divine ability capable of manipulating the origin qi of heaven and earth could produce such a feeling.

With no time to flee further, Bi Zheng abruptly stopped and pointed at his own body.

His surging mana erupted like a violent tide, enveloping his entire body in protection.

The hazy green light finally touched his body.

In an instant, it transformed into a pale white light.

This pale white light began to continuously erode Bi Zheng's protective mana, step by step seeping into his body.

Gradually, even Bi Zheng's jet-black hair began to turn gray.

In the distance, after releasing this divine ability, Luo Chen felt it was still insufficient. He raised the Yuantu Blood Sword high toward Bi Zheng.

Unlike before, this time, streaks of crimson sword qi began to circle around the Yuantu Blood Sword.

Scarlet Firmament Sword Qi!

There weren't many strands of this sword qi—barely a dozen or so.

But under the empowerment of the Yuantu Sword, it gave a feeling that nothing could withstand it.

"Slash!"

Luo Chen shouted softly, and the Yuantu Sword slashed out.

The Scarlet Firmament Sword Qi he had painstakingly cultivated for years was all unleashed in this moment.

*Chi!*

In an instant, a gap appeared in Bi Zheng's protective mana, and even a faint sword mark emerged on his shoulder.

Though it was only a trace, the power of the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger was still at work, following the gap to burrow into the sword wound.

Bi Zheng's expression completely changed!

He took a deep look at Luo Chen, recalled his black curved blade, and shot away without looking back.

Faced with this scene, Luo Chen had no time to rejoice.

Because Huang Man's attack had already arrived.

He had no means of defense and could only watch helplessly as the terrifying stone rod smashed down.

Black King roared as he rushed to intercept, but with a single blow, his body was smashed in half, blood mist spraying everywhere.

Luo Chen's face was splattered with blood droplets.

He simply raised his hand to block!

A flash of blue light flickered and vanished, and Luo Chen emerged unscathed.

Huang Man was stunned for a moment, then flew into a rage!

"It's that old bastard Heize's technique!"

Luo Chen sighed. The blue water droplet pattern on his brow could no longer be concealed and began to flicker.

With his current strength, he could perhaps face a great cultivator head-on.

Against a great demon emperor with relatively limited means, he even had the advantage of versatility.

But the sheer, straightforward suppression of power from a great demon emperor was something no amount of techniques could avoid.

If that blow had truly landed, he would have been severely wounded.

Forced to do so, he had no choice but to activate the protective divine ability left on his body by Ancestor Heize—Heavenly One Water.

But he dared not use this technique too much.

Not because of a limit on uses, but because he felt that the more he used it, the more influence Ancestor Heize would have over him.

However, for the moment, it was enough!

"Bi Zheng's intent to kill me was not firm; he was merely giving an explanation to Ao Kuang."

"I used him as a breakthrough point, using the heavenly tribulation as a feint to buy time to execute the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger and release the Scarlet Firmament Sword Qi, finally forcing him away."

"Now, I only need to deal with Huang Man alone."

Luo Chen reached out, and the dimly glowing Primordial Unity Cauldron flew back.

Black King, having reassembled his body, crawled fearfully onto Luo Chen's shoulder.

In the distance, the Whitebone Mountain still stood tall, its bones rising and falling within, while the flow of the nine-colored light river grew slower and slower.

Luo Chen gripped his sword hilt tightly, looking at Huang Man.

"You and I have no mortal enmity. Why must you push so hard?"

Huang Man laughed in fury, "When you enslaved my brother, did you ever consider this?"

Luo Chen shook his head, "He came to provoke me first. Not killing him was already an act of mercy on my part."

Huang Man glared viciously at Luo Chen, "Not only have you been invited to join the Nether Origin Sect, but you even have a protective divine ability personally bestowed by Heize. It seems the Nether Origin Sect truly values you! In that case, I must kill you all the more!"

Enough!

In the end, it would come down to life or death!

Luo Chen ceased his verbal disputes, gripping the Yuantu Sword as he charged into battle once more.

On Huang Man's side, without the heavenly tribulation to hinder him and no need to coordinate with Bi Zheng, he was actually more relaxed.

His stone rod treasure, made of some unknown material, was incredibly hard. Even the sharp and unmatched Yuantu Sword could only leave faint sword marks on it, unable to cut through in one stroke.

The battle between the two instantly entered a white-hot phase.

Treasures clashed, spells and demon arts were wielded freely.

The two figures fought from the sky to the ground, from the ground into the great river, and then suddenly shot back into the high heavens.

The vast Medicine King Domain had now become their battlefield entirely.

Mountains collapsed, great rivers were severed—a terrifying scene like the arrival of doomsday.

*Roar!*

With a thunderous roar, a massive tiger suddenly descended among the mountains, charging on all fours toward Luo Chen.

*Roar!*

Luo Chen was not to be outdone. He also enlarged his body, transforming into a giant five hundred zhang tall, firmly catching the pouncing tiger's claws.

The immense force pushed his body backward continuously.

Wherever he passed, mountains crumbled like tofu, smashed into powder.

Seeing his master at a disadvantage, Black King no longer stood idly by.

He slithered along Luo Chen's fleshly body, biting down on Huang Man's original form's arm, clamping it tightly.

Under the three-way struggle, Luo Chen's side ultimately had the slight advantage.

He raised his foot and kicked, transforming into countless afterimages that finally converged into one kick.

*Boom!*

Huang Man's massive body staggered backward as if struck heavily.

After he retreated, Luo Chen couldn't help but gasp for breath.

Such intense combat was an unimaginable drain on his mana and source power.

Especially—

He felt a prickling gaze, greedily fixed on him.

"Who is it?"

"Who is still watching from the shadows?"

With no time to think further, Huang Man charged again.

Not only that, but a rod swept across the sky, smashing down from above.

Luo Chen raised his sword to block the stone rod horizontally, but his massive body could not evade Huang Man's charge and was pushed back again.

"Today, I will surely kill you!"

Having gained the upper hand, Huang Man roared to the heavens.

In his bloody maw, a dense yellow light began to gather, and a sphere quickly materialized.

Luo Chen was startled. This move was remarkably similar to the one Huang Man had used to blast the heavenly tribulation earlier!

Even more so, fueled by rage, its power had multiplied.

In his current state, he feared he could not withstand it.

"You leave me no choice!"

Luo Chen roared, both intending to slay his powerful enemy and to intimidate the hidden observer.

Without any action, he simply opened his mouth and spat out.

A wisp of blue-white flame slowly flew out.

The Withering-Bloom Divine Fire, appearing in the world once more!

The fire spirit seemed to have been suppressed for a long time. Upon emerging, it became extremely active.

The Golden Wind and Jade Dew that Luo Chen had swallowed in one gulp, which had originally transformed into vast spiritual energy, was instantly drained dry by the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire.

If Luo Chen had not tightly sealed his Purple Mansion barrier, even the pure mana of his condensed Nascent Soul would have been drawn away.

In fact, this was precisely why Luo Chen, despite mastering this fifth-rank divine fire—a great killing weapon—had rarely used it in battle over the years.

This fire was his origin true flame, and when activated, it had to be fueled by mana.

And a single full activation consumed an unimaginable amount of mana.

At the very least, it would deplete his mana; at worst, it could cause his Nascent Soul to collapse.

But now, he could no longer afford to be stingy!

His natal treasure was severely damaged, and he had already broken the taboo of not drawing upon the origin qi of heaven and earth to execute the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger. Why should he still begrudge this?

Deprived of mana support, the Withering-Bloom fire spirit still felt insufficient.

It stretched out its branches, revealing its true form as a fire tree, attracting vast amounts of origin qi from heaven and earth to replenish itself.

Facing Huang Man's incoming strike, the Withering-Bloom fire spirit flashed with white light.

*Chi...*

The terrifying sphere instantly dissipated.

Huang Man was stunned, seemingly in disbelief.

But when a burning sensation spread across his body, he suddenly sensed something amiss.

*Boom!*

The divine fire blazed fiercely, incinerating the void.

A blue-white fire tree took root on Huang Man's massive, several-hundred-zhang body, beginning to burn fiercely.

A miserable scream spread far and wide. Countless living beings across the vast Medicine King Domain seemed able to hear it.

A Nascent Soul emerged from the yellow tiger's body, attempting to flee in panic.

But the fire tree's branches merely coiled around it, burning it to ashes, turning it into pure spiritual energy that made the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire even more vigorous.

...

In the void.

Divine Origin watched this scene, his eyes filled with horror.

His lips trembled as he muttered to himself.

"It's that fire, that very fire. Back then, Old Ghost Motian's Motian Cliff was instantly refined by that fire!"

Divine Origin Sage was both shocked and delighted at this moment.

Shocked that Luo Chen still possessed such a terrifying killing move, delighted that Luo Chen's physical body was even stronger than he had imagined.

"Such a body is more than enough to bear my entire Dao!"

"Especially since he was originally also a Five Spirit Root body, making him even more compatible with my Great Dao."

"Practicing the Primal Embryo Dao Body in reverse was ultimately just a fantasy. But if I obtain this body, the Ascendant realm will no longer be a vain hope!"

Murmuring to himself, Divine Origin Sage had already begun to move.

Now was the moment when Luo Chen was at his weakest!

His treasure was broken, his mana nearly exhausted, and even his powerful body showed signs of fatigue.

Even though Ancestor Heize of the Nether Origin Sect had placed a protective divine ability on him, Divine Origin Sage's connection with heaven and earth had grown too close over the years, giving him some confidence in breaking that protective divine ability.

"Opportunity!"

Divine Origin Sage shot downward without hesitation.

On the earth below, Luo Chen suddenly looked up at the falling meteor.

"Here it comes!"

Suddenly, the meteor was struck heavily, halting midair.

After a momentary stalemate, it flew back at a speed far greater than before.

This sudden turn of events caught Luo Chen off guard.

In his hesitation, Luo Chen's pale golden eyes swept across the wilderness.

In a pit of jagged bones, Nine Spirits lay weakly on the ground, his aura listless.

The great demon emperor Ox Ghost knelt in the distance, leaning on his bone staff, panting heavily.

Yet, facing the severely wounded Nine Spirits, Ox Ghost showed no intention of attacking.

Only because, before Nine Spirits, stood a tall, slender woman who bent down and plucked the small white flower from his body.

"Pity, it's not yet ripe."

The woman sighed and tucked the small white flower into her sleeve.

Strangely enough, the Accumulated Void Flower, which could not be stored in a storage ring, was effortlessly put away by her.

But Luo Chen was no stranger to such means.

Sleeve Universe, Azure Frost of Green Parasol!

The demoness paid no heed to the severely wounded Nine Spirits, nor did she glance at Ox Ghost, who was slowly retreating step by step. She didn't even spare a moment's attention for Divine Origin Sage, who had appeared with such momentum and fled in panic.

Instead, she directly met Luo Chen's eyes.

Their gazes locked.

The several-hundred-zhang giant involuntarily took a step back.
