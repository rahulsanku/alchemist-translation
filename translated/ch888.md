# Chapter 888: Object-Self Mystic Union, Ultimate Nirvana, Threefold Nirvana Luo Chen, Golden Flame Crosses Truth

Sunset Red Plain.

This was a vast plain terrain in the Moya Domain, a full thousand *li* in circumference, with almost no tall mountains and few ravines.

In theory, it was an excellent place suitable for cultivation.

Yet in the thousand-plus-year history of the Moya Domain, no faction of any consequence had ever established a foothold here.

The sole reason was that the spiritual veins here were scattered and chaotic, utterly lacking any system, and simply could not support even a moderately sized force to station itself.

All that existed were merely scattered rogue cultivators and a few small families struggling to survive here.

But ever since the Luo Tian Sect took over the Moya Domain, everything had changed.

The once desolate Sunset Red Plain had suddenly become bustling with activity.

People flowed like a woven tapestry, carriages and horses moved like dragons, and every day a great number of flying lights came and went.

All these changes originated from the plan to build an immortal city here!

The Luo Tian Superior Sect took the lead, and the thirteen Adept-level great sects jointly provided manpower and resources. It was even said that forces from outside the domain had invested funds, wanting to claim a share.

The current busy scene was precisely the result of this.

Building an immortal city was not a day's work. Surveying feng shui trends, gathering spiritual veins, arranging formations, selecting suitable immortal construction talents to plan the city's layout...

But no matter how slow, with the collective participation of cultivators who possessed individual great power, the progress speed far exceeded ordinary imagination.

From determining the site selection to implementing the project and starting construction, in just two or three years, the most important city walls had already taken shape.

Next, as long as the main roads were opened up and the city layout was planned according to the spiritual veins guided by the Adept cultivators, it would be almost done.

At this juncture, the cultivators involved were all fervently enthusiastic, looking forward to the day when this "Red Plain Immortal City" would be completed.

It was imagined that it would surely be extremely lively then.

Rogue cultivators would come to seek refuge, merchants and travelers from all directions would gather, and various resources from all over the world would flow in and out...

But beneath this lively scene, the Luo Tian Sect strongman in charge of supervision, Baleful Dragonzi, seemed somewhat distracted, as if he didn't care at all about this great joyous event that everyone was anticipating.

He leaned against a large tree outside the city, chewing on a wild grass stem in his mouth, his gaze cast into the distance.

It seemed he was looking at the majestic immortal city about to be completed, and also seemed to be looking at the hazy setting sun about to fall over the city walls. His eyes were slightly dazed.

A beam of escaping light flew across the sky, circled once, and then flew back.

The light descended, and a disciple put away his flying sword, walking over cheerfully while shouting:

"Uncle-Master, today the Rock Firmament Gang Leader is hosting a banquet for all parties, you..."

However, before he could finish his words, he was interrupted quite impatiently by Baleful Dragonzi.

"Go away, I'm not in the mood."

The disciple was stunned, put away his smile, and cautiously walked over to squat down beside him.

"What's wrong, Uncle-Master?"

Baleful Dragonzi did not answer, but the wild grass in his mouth was chewed until green juice came out.

The disciple tentatively asked: "Is it because of the Supreme Elder?"

The man's mouth suddenly stopped moving. He glared fiercely at the disciple, startling the other into sitting down on the ground with a plop.

Seeing this, Baleful Dragonzi knew that he had scared the other. Over the years of campaigning in all directions, he had accumulated a dense baleful aura on his body, which was not something this junior who had just reached Initiate could withstand.

He spat out the wild grass, restrained his baleful aura, stood up, and casually pulled the other up as well.

Then, a long sigh escaped him.

Looking at the Red Plain Immortal City, which still only had its walls, Baleful Dragonzi murmured: "They only think about how to divide the immortal city's profits in the future, but they forget who the greatest contributor to conquering the Luo Tian Domain was."

"Everyone says, 'When drinking water, don't forget who dug the well.' But in just three short years, who still cares about Uncle-Master Luo?"

The disciple scratched his head. "It might be because there were rumors a long time ago that the Supreme Elder would leave the Luo Tian Sect. Although the destinations were different, these people had long been mentally prepared, so they don't care now!"

"Destinations different?"

Baleful Dragonzi let out a sarcastic laugh. One was climbing high to enter a Holy Land, the other was unknown whether alive or dead. Could this be called 'destinations different'?

But he would not argue with a junior.

Because everyone's perception of Luo Chen was different.

They only saw him as a Sage, lofty and high above, rarely seen, someone to be admired from afar but not approached closely. But for himself, he was the third closest person after his mother and his master.

Even after his mother passed away and his master was strict and harsh, Luo Chen was more like a "kind mother" to him.

A long time ago, when he had just been accepted as a disciple by his master and had to undergo brutal body tempering for Initiation. He was still young then and often couldn't hold on. It was Uncle-Master Luo Chen who comforted him with kind words and encouraged him with the glorious deeds of his father's high strength during the Qi Refinement stage in his early years.

Although he later learned that his father wasn't actually that powerful, and had been quite dissolute before forming a Dao companion with his mother.

During the Roaring Moon Expansion War, Uncle-Master Luo Chen often kept him by his side, giving him constant instructions, and even controlled the enemy's strength specifically to let him practice.

Not to mention that whenever there was something good, Uncle-Master Luo Chen always gave it to him first.

Even compared to other outstanding figures of his generation within the sect, Uncle-Master Luo Chen still put him first.

Such a benevolent elder had just disappeared without a clear reason.

And it was different from the last time.

That time, at least Sect Master Sima had sworn with certainty that he was still alive.

But this time...

"Forget it, you go attend the banquet in my place! If the Rock Firmament Gang has any extra requests, don't agree, don't refuse directly either, just stall them."

The disciple was stunned for a moment, then nodded and left.

After he was gone, Baleful Dragonzi squatted down again and reached for a wild grass that was growing well.

"Three years..."

"Hmm?"

What his hand touched was smooth and hard.

Baleful Dragonzi's fingers trembled, and he abruptly withdrew them, his eyes staring at the ground.

A dragon horn was slowly emerging from the soil, like a bamboo shoot.

Then a pair of angry pupils appeared.

"Little one, who told you to touch my dragon horn?"

Baleful Dragonzi opened his mouth wide, looking incredulous. "Black... Black..."

"Call me Lord Black King!"

Lord Black King snorted, blasting out a puff of hot air.

"Lord Black King, you're still alive!" Baleful Dragonzi exclaimed, his expression shifting from shock to joy. He seemed to realize something.

Although within the Luo Tian Sect, Lord Black King and Elder Heavenjade both addressed Uncle-Master Luo Chen as their master.

In reality, they all knew that Elder Heavenjade was not strictly Uncle-Master Luo Chen's spirit pet, because Uncle-Master Luo Chen had not collected her soul blood, let alone placed a slave seal on her.

But Lord Black King was a genuine spirit pet.

If the master fell, he would inevitably suffer the consequences!

Now that Lord Black King had appeared, didn't that mean...

"Keep your voice down!"

Lord Black King opened his mouth and spat out a stream of source power, forming a soundproof barrier.

Such a simple spell, he had long learned from his master.

Then, his lips moved, and he carefully conveyed the message to be relayed to Baleful Dragonzi.

Finally, Lord Black King said with utmost solemnity: "Remember, the news that the master is still alive can only be reported to your master. Don't even tell the master's two Dao companions or his disciples!"

Baleful Dragonzi was puzzled and wanted to press for more.

But Lord Black King said impatiently: "Don't ask why. Your master will naturally understand."

Baleful Dragonzi nodded. As long as Uncle-Master was still alive, anything was fine.

"I'm leaving!"

"Lord Black King, aren't you returning to the Luo Tian Sect?"

Lord Black King paused, then sneered: "How can I go back? As soon as I show my face, it will expose the master's situation."

Baleful Dragonzi fell into thoughtful silence. Indeed, that made sense.

But where exactly was Uncle-Master now, and why was he so unwilling to expose his whereabouts?

And why not tell the news that he was still alive to his two closest Dao companions?

These questions, the departed Lord Black King would not answer him.

And not long after, within his master's heavily guarded cave dwelling, upon learning of this matter, Wang Yuan did not directly tell him all the answers either.

But what was visibly apparent was that the always composed Master Wang Yuan had gained a bit more calmness.

"Go inform Sect Master Li to suspend the Ascension Gathering two years from now. My Luo Tian Sect will not recruit new disciples for a short time."

Baleful Dragonzi asked curiously: "What about Elder Lingfengzi? I heard he recalled quite a few disciples of the Misty Void Sect who were scattered outside, and he even has ideas about expanding the Misty Void Hall."

Wang Yuan's face darkened. "It doesn't matter. I will negotiate with him myself. But for now, the sect has no leader, making it easiest for spies from other forces to infiltrate. All matters will wait until he returns."

Baleful Dragonzi immediately understood.

Of course, he still didn't understand why this news wasn't told to Uncle-Masters Sima Huiniang and Gu Caiyi.

Wang Yuan thought for a moment, then said in a low voice: "Perhaps it's because Sima Huiniang just had a child recently, and her emotional control isn't as good as before, making it easy to leak secrets. As for Gu Caiyi... Hah, maybe he's still thinking about fairness!"

...

Wang Yuan's guess was correct. Luo Chen did have such concerns.

Furthermore, the matter of his disappearance was neither big nor small.

As long as he eventually returned, it would be fine.

But the destination of his disappearance was a big problem.

If someone knew he had returned to Green Parasol Mountain, during this period when the Eastern Wilderness human-demon war had just ended, it could easily stir up certain storms.

Especially regarding the Nether Origin Sect, given Black Marsh Ancestor's style of conduct, he might do something.

Back then during the great battle, Nine Spirits Primordial Lord and the others only knew that he had escaped. No one had personally witnessed him and Azure Frost leaving "peacefully."

With his brilliant achievement of killing Huang Man on the spot, when he returned and said he had only been severely injured and found a place to recuperate for many years, it would naturally fool everyone.

It was better not to mention the Green Parasol Mountain trip if possible.

As for why he only notified Wang Yuan?

Luo Chen was still worried that his disappearance would cause the hard-won stability of the Luo Tian Sect to fall into chaos again.

Wang Yuan was the person he trusted most, with enough seniority. The only thing originally lacking, the realm aspect, had also been smoothed over by his strength after the battle with the Moya Old Ghost.

As long as Wang Yuan had confidence in his heart, he could handle all the big and small matters of the Luo Tian Sect.

After sending Lord Black King back to deliver the message, Luo Chen had been trying to switch back to his main cultivation technique.

The self-created *Withering-Bloom Dao Scripture* was definitely not bad.

Even if placed outside, it could serve as the foundational inheritance of an Sage-level superior sect.

But this technique was too mixed, and was created with the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire as the leading element. The more he cultivated, the more it caused Luo Chen to be too deeply influenced by the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire.

Previously, when he was assimilated by heaven and earth and gave birth to a heart demon, he only thought it was the aftereffect of using the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger divine ability.

Now it seemed, wasn't it also the covert influence of the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire?

After all, this kind of heavenly strange fire was already no different from those fifth-rank sentient spirits, capable of communicating with the origin qi of heaven and earth.

Now, switching back to cultivate the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture* could change this situation.

This set of techniques was the most supreme fire-attribute technique, capable of commanding all fires under heaven!

Of course, for a Sage cultivator, switching one's lifebound cultivation technique twice in succession was a major taboo in cultivation.

But for Luo Chen, the impact wasn't that great.

Because before the first switch to the *Withering-Bloom Dao Scripture*, he had originally cultivated the Adept chapter of the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture*. Now, it was merely renewing a past connection.

But that the impact wasn't great didn't mean the switch would go smoothly!

In the final analysis, this set of techniques was the true inheritance scripture of the Phoenix clan.

He, Luo Chen, was not a Phoenix body after all, and was severely stuck during the process of switching.

He even tried to forcefully enter the gate using system achievement points, but couldn't succeed.

Because the complete version of this technique given to him by Rosycloud was the full version!

No one had thoughtfully divided it into a Sage chapter, an Ascendant chapter, and so on for him.

To forcefully enter the gate, he would need to invest achievement points spanning four or five major realms above his own.

System entry had always been ten times the cost for one realm higher, which meant he would need at least one hundred thousand or even a million achievement points. How could Luo Chen have such savings?

Therefore, he could only rely on his own understanding to slowly ponder and learn.

In this process, a vast amount of heaven and earth spiritual qi was needed as support; otherwise, it would be very difficult to continue advancing.

This was also why Luo Chen chose to stay on Green Parasol Mountain.

This cave dwelling in the Truth Crossing Hall was truly the finest spiritual land he had ever seen in his life.

The density of spiritual qi was rarely seen in the world.

Perhaps the only thing that could surpass it were the legendary fifth-rank spiritual lands.

In Luo Chen's repeated attempts, paying no heed to cost, he was getting closer and closer to entering the gate.

But the closer he got, the more he found an invisible barrier firmly blocking him.

For this reason, even if he didn't hesitate to reshape his meridians and convert his acupoints, he still couldn't cross that barrier.

"Could it be that Rosycloud deceived me?"

In the cave dwelling, Luo Chen's expression was uncertain.

He began to suspect whether Rosycloud had used a technique he simply couldn't cultivate to trick him out of the Dao Union Pill formula.

But it was completely unnecessary!

In front of a powerhouse of that level, Luo Chen was at her mercy. Why would she bother with deception?

Luo Chen's attention turned towards his sea of consciousness.

"Could it be that I must complete the Nine Tribulations Threefold Nirvana of the Adept chapter to fully enter the gate of the true scripture?"

Nine Tribulations Threefold Nirvana. After the dharma power nirvana came the physical body nirvana, and then the soul nirvana.

The dharma power nirvana completely transformed his entire dharma power towards the fire attribute.

The physical body nirvana created a Fire Spirit Body.

If the soul nirvana succeeded, according to the scripture's records, he would possess a "Fire Soul."

In this way, his own essence, energy, and spirit would all become pure and unadulterated. Therefore, in the future, he could command all fires and directly reach the Great Vehicle stage.

But the matter of nirvana was not that simple!

The first dharma power nirvana should have been extremely perilous. Luo Chen had crossed it by taking a shortcut.

That time, he had used system achievement points to forcefully enter the gate. It was equivalent to buying a ticket after getting on the bus; there was no threshold issue.

The second physical body nirvana... its danger was hard to describe. If Luo Chen hadn't possessed a desolate beast body beyond ordinary people during the Adept stage, even containing the innate talent of flesh regeneration and limb regrowth, he would have undoubtedly died.

This third soul nirvana would likely be even more dangerous than the first two.

"To place oneself in a position of certain death and then survive—is that nirvana?"

"Or is it to stay far away from inverted dream thoughts, and then one can achieve ultimate nirvana?"

"Or is it to exist in the wonderful harmony, the essence of which lies in the mysterious unity. Then, objects are not different from me, and I am not different from objects. Object and self mysteriously unite, returning to the infinite—is this nirvana?"

Luo Chen couldn't figure it out. These understandings even leaned more towards Buddhist theories.

Although his knowledge was vast and he could be considered broadly learned, being quite proficient in the demon, dao, and devil paths, he had very little understanding of the Buddhist path.

But a living person couldn't be suffocated to death!

Luo Chen had always been flexible in his thinking.

Soul nirvana, was it?

He wasn't afraid to give it a try.

With a thought, his main soul slowly emerged from his sea of consciousness, distancing itself from his main body.

And within his sea of consciousness, the strongest first divided soul took the dominant position.

He had to give it a try!

If successful, he would usher in an earth-shattering transformation.

Even if he failed, at worst he would lose a divided soul.

Following the oral formula of the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture*, Luo Chen began to mobilize his Sage dharma power to spread throughout his body, while in his sea of consciousness, a large amount of scattered golden soul power began to burn, enveloping the first divided soul.

Whether nirvana would be achieved or not rested on this single move!

Just as he initiated the final nirvana of the Nine Tribulations Threefold Nirvana, an invisible ripple began to spread from the peak of the Truth Crossing Hall.

Birds were not startled; mortals found it hard to know.

Only within the Universal Mystery Hall, a woman in a red dress who was fiddling with a small white flower raised an eyebrow.

"Sister, what's wrong?" Azure Frost sensed the anomaly but didn't know where it came from. She thought her sister was displeased because the Accumulated Void Flower hadn't matured.

Rosycloud waved her hand. "It has nothing to do with you. Although the Accumulated Void Flower hasn't matured yet, you can take it and place it inside your body to cultivate, nourishing it with the true intent of spatial laws. When this flower matures, that will be the day you achieve Ascendant. But you must be careful: during the stage of nurturing this flower, your Leakless Body will have flaws. It's best not to fight with anyone."

Azure Frost nodded. She had similar thoughts but hadn't dared to put them into action yet.

Now, with her sister's endorsement, she could let loose and act.

But why had her sister's expression changed just now?

Rosycloud paced to the outside of the hall, her gaze looking down below.

"What a decisive fellow..."

A look of approval appeared on her face, but before she could finish her words, she frowned.

"Using the divided soul method for nirvana—what kind of 'placing oneself in a position of certain death and then surviving' is that? This guy, is he too used to taking shortcuts?"

After saying this, she flicked her sleeve in displeasure and returned to the hall.

Earlier, she had thought that if the other party could successfully switch techniques, she might consider leaving the position of Truth Crossing Hall Master to him.

But this thought was now completely dismissed.

Because Luo Chen was bound to fail!

Was such a person worthy of receiving the alchemy inheritance of Devil Lord Heaven-Refining?

However, when the ninth day arrived, the great doors of the Universal Mystery Hall suddenly burst open.

Rosycloud, in her red dress, looked with a surprised expression towards the Truth Crossing Hall.

There, a phantom golden flame was rising slowly!
