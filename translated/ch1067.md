# Chapter 1067: The True Ones Arrive, A Treaty of Cessation

The people from Splendid Cloud Palace were the second group to arrive at Shouyang Hall.

Compared to the top-tier Nascent Soul upper sects, their numbers were somewhat small—only two.

They were the Great Palace Master, Splendid Cloud Fairy, and the newly ascended Nascent Soul, Wind Moon Fairy.

As soon as they entered the hall, they heard the sound of hearty laughter coming from within.

"Nine Spirits, I heard you lost to Deng Taiyue. That surprised me a little."

"Hmph! It was merely a clash of Sage domains. I don't know how he cultivated, but even though he only just stepped into the late Nascent Soul stage, his domain is absurdly strong. However, if we were to fight to the death, it's still uncertain who would prevail between Deng Taiyue and me!"

"I don't doubt that. Nine Spirits' killing arts far surpass what his surface-level cultivation suggests."

"What about Deng Taiyue? He arrived at Lingtian Pass even before I did. Why isn't he here?"

This question, for now, had no answer, because the two True Ones from Splendid Cloud Palace had already entered the hall.

Splendid Cloud Fairy greeted familiarly, "Alchemy Sect, it's been a long time."

"Splendid Cloud, it has been a long time indeed. Your grace is even more radiant than before," Luo Chen replied leisurely, then his gaze fell upon the other woman beside her. "Fellow Daoist Wind Moon, congratulations on your breakthrough to the Nascent Soul stage."

Wind Moon's expression was somewhat complex as she looked up. Luo Chen was seated above, his wide black Daoist robe accentuating the bearing of a grandmaster, as steady as a mountain and deep as an abyss.

Behind Luo Chen sat two exceptionally beautiful women in silence.

On the left and right were the radiant and dazzling Gu Caiyi, and the noble and pure Canglong Fairy, like a snow lotus from the Heavenly Mountains.

They were also two Nascent Soul True Ones!

To be dressed in full regalia and seated openly behind Luo Chen on such an occasion left their identities beyond doubt.

Wind Moon gracefully bowed. "I owe thanks to you, Brother Dao, for the grace of your pill that allowed me to break through to the Nascent Soul stage."

Back then, one of that batch of Nascent Soul Pills had indeed fallen into the hands of Splendid Cloud Palace.

Now it seemed that Wind Moon had been the one to enjoy that pill.

Luo Chen smiled, unconcerned, and pointed a finger.

"Please, take your seats!"

Today's Shouyang Hall had been expanded, and its interior arrangement was different from the usual casual style.

Tables and cushions were arranged in neat rows, with fruits and refreshments exuding an enticing fragrance.

Rising sandalwood smoke drifted, calming the mind and pleasing the spirit.

Little Bai led a group of beautiful female ghosts through the hall, placing wine on the tables.

Splendid Cloud Fairy and Wind Moon glanced at Little Bai, inwardly sighing with emotion. Even the task of arranging the venue was being handled by a Nascent Soul ghost cultivator. The Luo Tian Sect was truly formidable in strength these days!

They sat down beside the Nine Spirits Sect and briefly got to know Changxi and Meng Ye, whom Nine Spirits Primordial Lord had brought this time.

Luo Chen did not spend too long catching up with his two old friends, because more and more cultivators were entering Shouyang Hall one after another.

Among them, many were old acquaintances of his.

For example, the three Nascent Soul True Ones from the White Crane Sect—among them, Leisure Crane True One and Crane Emperor were already acquainted, while the newly ascended Nascent Soul, Bai Yunfei, like Wind Moon, had succeeded thanks to that batch of Nascent Soul Pills Luo Chen had previously released.

Only one person came from the Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion: Lin Qingxuan.

Logically speaking, with the combined heritage of the Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion and the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect—two Nascent Soul upper sects—they shouldn't have been so shabby. Moreover, the Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion had also bought one of Luo Chen's Nascent Soul Pills from Deng Taiyue.

Yet it had failed.

Luo Chen didn't know if it was because the pill's efficacy was insufficient, or if that cultivator lacked the opportunity, or if Lin Qingxuan had deliberately suppressed them.

Luo Chen uncharitably considered the last possibility.

Lin Qingxuan was no longer the arrogant youth he once was. He had become mature and steady. After greeting Luo Chen, he went to the back and found a table to sit at by himself.

Heavenly Capital Master of the Heavenly Capital Sect also arrived, bringing a full three Nascent Soul True Ones!

The foundation of the former top formation sect of the Eastern Wilderness was not to be underestimated. Even after the great human-demon war ended, the Heavenly Capital Sect had only two Nascent Souls left, including Heavenly Capital Master. But in the hundred-plus years since the war, two more Nascent Souls had gradually emerged.

As soon as the Heavenly Capital Sect arrived, Luo Chen immediately had Little Bai arrange the best seats for them, directly opposite the Nine Spirits Sect.

No one questioned this arrangement.

It was not only due to the strength of the Heavenly Capital Sect and the respect for Heavenly Capital Master and Nine Spirits Primordial Lord as fellow late-stage Nascent Soul grand cultivators, but also because of the deep friendship these two had with Luo Chen.

The strong from the various sects who arrived later were seated in descending order.

The Hundred Beasts Sect, the Void Emperor Sect, the Seven Hollows Mountain, the Stacked Cloud Sect, the Green Pill Valley, and even the lone wandering cultivator Bo Hanjun all had a place.

Among them were the resurgent Void Emperor Sect and the seasoned Seven Hollows Mountain.

Many had thought the Stacked Cloud Sect would decline, since during the demon war, the Demon Emperor's elite forces had struck their rear, wiping out all three of their Nascent Soul True Ones in a single battle. Yet, unexpectedly, the Stacked Cloud Sect had quietly cultivated another Nascent Soul expert.

As for Tao Wan of the Green Pill Valley and the wandering cultivator Bo Hanjun, although they attracted some attention as new forces and new faces, they were ultimately still unknown figures.

What truly caused a stir was the appearance of the White Deer Cave.

A full six Nascent Soul True Ones!

Among them was True One Peng Xiang, a newly ascended grand cultivator!

With such a lineup and such formidable strength, even at the peak of the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world, the White Deer Cave would have ranked among the most top-tier Nascent Soul upper sects!

When he saw Luo Chen again, True One Peng Xiang acted very freely and easily.

He showed not a trace of embarrassment over having once helped the Five Elements Divine Sect oppose the Luo Tian Sect. Times had changed, and there had never been any deep, irreconcilable enmity, so why hold a grudge?

Moreover, because of Luo Chen's Nascent Soul Pills, the White Deer Cave had gained a new Nascent Soul in Kongtongzi. Thus, True One Peng Xiang took the initiative to show goodwill.

Luo Chen smiled and let bygones be bygones.

Outside the hall, two more announcements rang out, one old and one young.

"Eastern Sun Sect, Grand Elder Eastern Sun True One!"

"Heavenly Yuan Dao Sect, Shi Chuanting, Lian Buwei!"

As the voices faded, three figures walked in together.

Everyone's gaze shifted to them, especially when looking at the two walking shoulder to shoulder with Eastern Sun Grand Elder. Discomfort was written on every face.

Back when the Heavenly Yuan Dao Sect had taken over the Eastern Wilderness, though the time was short—only about thirty years—they had done little good!

They occupied spirit lands, plundered resources, and when facing the resurgence of the demon army, they often made the disciples of the local Eastern Wilderness sects bear the brunt of the fighting.

The grievances they had caused were remembered by all the sects. They were usually left unspoken only out of caution.

But now that they were actually seen, who could feel comfortable?

The arrival of the Eastern Sun Sect was expected.

But the presence of representatives from the Heavenly Yuan Dao Sect at the Shouyang Dao Discussion was hard to accept.

The Heavenly Yuan Dao Sect was not a sect of the Eastern Wilderness!

And this time, when Alchemy Sect restarted the Shouyang Dao Discussion, the invitation was limited to the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world.

They had come uninvited!

Everyone instinctively turned their gaze back to Luo Chen above, wondering how he would handle this.

Unexpectedly, Luo Chen's expression was calm. He simply waved his hand. "Three fellow Daoists, please take your seats!"

But the three did not sit down immediately. Instead, they looked around the hall and shook their heads.

"Luo Chen, Lian and Brother Shi represent the Heavenly Yuan Dao Sect. How could we sit at the back?" Lian Buwei stepped forward, his attitude even more arrogant than Eastern Sun Grand Elder's. He snorted, "Don't forget, you were once a guest elder of our Heavenly Yuan Dao Sect's Star Gate!"

Luo Chen's eyes narrowed, a dangerous glint flashing as he looked at the two.

With the four great Demon Emperors he had specially stationed at Shouyang Mountain, the deterrent effect was already very clear.

All the guests who had come today were polite and courteous.

Even the White Deer Cave, with its six Nascent Soul True Ones appearing together, had shown Luo Chen respect.

How could these two from the Heavenly Yuan Dao Sect compare?

"Eastern Sun Grand Elder, are these the people you brought?"

Under Luo Chen's gaze, Eastern Sun Grand Elder, himself a grand cultivator, felt a surge of fear rise in his heart for some reason.

His expression did not show it, but he had already blocked Lian Buwei and Shi Chuanting.

"Two fellow Daoists, we arrived late. Let's not argue about seats."

With that, he took the initiative to walk toward a position at the back of the hall.

Eastern Sun Grand Elder nodded slightly to the woman beside him, then flicked his Daoist robe and sat down calmly.

Lian Buwei and Shi Chuanting frowned, but feeling the heavy atmosphere in the hall, they had no choice but to sit down at the back.

The place they sat was near the outer edge of the hall, where only two weaker sects were seated.

They were the Stacked Cloud Sect and the Green Pill Valley.

Tao Wan was beside them. Because of the tense atmosphere earlier, she felt somewhat uncomfortable and pursed her lips tightly.

When everyone was seated, Wang Yuan hurried in and placed the scroll containing everyone's signatures in front of Luo Chen.

Luo Chen picked up the scroll and scanned it carefully.

There were twenty-seven people in total. Subtracting the two from the Heavenly Yuan Dao Sect, that left twenty-five.

Concerning the opportunity for Ascension, none of the Nascent Soul experts of the Eastern Wilderness wanted to miss it. This was probably the peak strength of the current Eastern Wilderness human cultivation world!

Oh, and the Eastern Sun Sect had only sent its Grand Elder.

Based on Luo Chen's personal investigation when he had entered the Eastern Sun Sect back then, there should still be four Nascent Soul True Ones there.

Twenty-nine... even adding the Luo Tian Sect's seven human Nascent Souls, that was only thirty-six people.

Putting down the scroll, Luo Chen let out a soft sigh.

"This gathering today may be a reunion of old friends, or it may add new companions on the path of the Great Dao. As such a grand event, this sect master should be joyful. Yet, looking at this list, the number is less than half of what the Eastern Wilderness had at its peak. Too many fellow Daoists fell in that calamity."

His melancholy sigh echoed softly through the hall.

Everyone in the hall was downcast. Among those who had fallen were their friends, their fellow disciples.

Even some of the newly ascended Nascent Souls sighed deeply.

Achieving the Nascent Soul stage, with a lifespan of thousands of years, was already considered no different from an immortal in the eyes of mortals.

Yet a single calamity had cut short so many people's paths to longevity.

The world only saw the splendor and grandeur of today's Shouyang Dao Discussion. How could they know that at its peak, even without counting the Holy Land of the Nether Origin Sect, the Eastern Wilderness had fifty or sixty Nascent Soul True Ones?

Compared to back then, it was indeed a decline.

"Why must Alchemy Sect be so melancholic?"

A hearty, bold voice rang out from the hall. Nine Spirits Primordial Lord spoke out.

"The dead are gone. Chasing after them is useless. We who remain should press forward with determination and climb the peaks. Moreover, there are new fellow Daoists like Fellow Daoist Gu of your sect, Changxi and Meng Ye of my sect, and Kongtongzi of the White Deer Cave. As long as the path of immortality is not cut off, companions will always emerge."

At these words, a chorus of agreement arose.

That great human-demon war, which had spanned two hundred years, had indeed brought immense disaster to the Eastern Wilderness. But after the war ended, the benefits gained by the survivors were also unprecedented.

Thus, amidst the sorrow, people were more grateful.

Surviving a great disaster brings good fortune!

Wasn't today's Shouyang Dao Discussion the greatest blessing?

Someone couldn't hold back and asked directly.

"Alchemy Sect, have you truly refined the Enlightenment Pill?" Medicine Master from the Seven Hollows Mountain was the first to eagerly ask the question everyone cared about.

Luo Chen had not ascended—that was the truth everyone could now see.

But his invitation had indeed mentioned the words "opportunity for Ascension."

So, without having ascended, how could one grasp the opportunity for Ascension?

The answer, in fact, many had already guessed.

The Enlightenment Pill!

An ancient divine pill that could help one enter a state of sudden enlightenment and comprehend the true meaning of the laws.

If one could truly comprehend the true meaning of the laws at the Nascent Soul stage, that would indeed be grasping the opportunity for Ascension. Advancing to the Ascension stage in the future would be a natural progression.

As for the Enlightenment Pill, Medicine Master was the most knowledgeable among everyone.

Back then, Deng Taiyue had first placed his hopes on him. Unfortunately, Medicine Master's alchemy skills were insufficient; he couldn't complete the pill formula, let alone refine it.

Later, Deng Taiyue had placed his hopes on Luo Chen.

It was said that Deng Taiyue had gone into seclusion for a hundred years. When he emerged, his cultivation had broken through to the late Nascent Soul stage, and his strength had skyrocketed. There was a high probability that he had taken the Enlightenment Pill.

As for whether he had comprehended the true meaning of the laws, the world still didn't know.

But Medicine Master would not let this opportunity pass.

Even though he had a poor relationship with Luo Chen in the past, he had come today, risking the humiliation of being rebuffed by Luo Chen, just for a glimpse of that ancient divine pill!

Faced with Medicine Master's eagerness, Luo Chen waved his hand.

"Let's not talk about the pill for now. This sect master has another important matter."

Everyone was taken aback. What important matter could compare to the opportunity for Ascension?

A flash of spiritual light appeared in Luo Chen's hand, and a scroll materialized in the hall.

He pressed down on the scroll and spoke loudly. "The demon calamity has plagued the Eastern Wilderness for two hundred years, claiming the lives of countless cultivators. Even if you all have the chance to break through to Ascension, if the demon race were to return in full force, how could a single intact egg remain under an overturned nest?"

At these words, everyone's expressions changed drastically.

"Originally, the two races were to sign a treaty of cessation at Azure Sky Mountain back then. But because the Nether Origin Sect went back on their word, the cessation failed."

"In the battle at Azure Sky Mountain, the Ancient Demon Emperor Tian fell, the Demon Court collapsed, and we humans seemed to have won that war. Yet what awaited us was an even more frenzied counterattack!"

"The great demon power, Rosycloud, personally emerged from seclusion and buried the Nether Origin Sect. From then on, the Eastern Wilderness had no more human Holy Land."

"Later, when the demon race raised their armies a second time to threaten the Eastern Wilderness, we were reduced to relying on the outer-continent Holy Land, the Heavenly Yuan Dao Sect. How pitiful was that!"

Luo Chen spoke eloquently, recounting the past.

He spoke of events from years gone by, but touched upon painful experiences that no one wanted to revisit.

"Today's Eastern Wilderness has withered upper sects, a hundred things waiting to be revived, and is at its most vulnerable. It can no longer afford the cost of another war with the demon race."

"With this in mind, this sect master believes that a cessation of hostilities is imperative!"

Everyone knew that although the Heavenly Yuan Dao Sect and the Eastern Wilderness demon race had both withdrawn, the war was not yet over.

If a treaty of cessation could truly be reached, then the Eastern Wilderness humans would have a real chance to recuperate and recover.

But without a Holy Land of Ascension in the Eastern Wilderness, who was qualified to discuss cessation?

That scroll slowly unfurled before everyone's eyes.

Each person's gaze fell upon it, unable to look away for a long time.

"This sect master personally entered Green Parasol Mountain and obtained the approval of Rosycloud. The other party proactively extended goodwill, allowing the human and demon races to conclude a treaty of cessation and renew a thousand years of peace."

"Rosycloud has already left her name. What say you all?"

Luo Chen spoke slowly, his gaze sweeping across every face.

He saw eagerness, he saw relief. There might still be hatred, but in the face of survival and cultivation, it was negligible.

Conclude a treaty of cessation, renew a thousand years of peace.

A thousand years was enough time for them to develop.

As for what came after a thousand years?

Either the strong present would pass away, and they wouldn't care about the flood after their deaths. Or they would break through their shackles, achieve Ascension, and take control of their own fate and that of humanity.

Time was the most precious thing everyone in the Eastern Wilderness needed most right now.

However, a discordant voice suddenly exploded.

"How dare you!"

"Luo Chen, you actually dare to collude with the demon race!"

Luo Chen's face instantly darkened, his eyes fixed on those two from the Heavenly Yuan Dao Sect.

Was it the prestige of being the number one Holy Land of the Mountains and Seas Realm that gave them the courage to be so presumptuous before him, time and again?
