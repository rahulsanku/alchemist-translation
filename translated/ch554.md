# Chapter 554: Exposed and Pursued, A Breakneck Escape

"I wonder how much more I can take in this time."

Beside the fallen body of a Ring-Headed Turtle, Luo Chen's demon crab puppet lay motionless, feigning the aftermath of mutual destruction.

His full attention was fixed on the chaotic battlefield ahead.

Three against one — no different from before. Three Demon Spider Crabs were pressing down on a single third-tier Ring-Headed Turtle.

The only difference was that this Ring-Headed Turtle's defenses were remarkably sturdy. It had held out under the three-demon assault for a full day and night.

Han Zhan had sent him this way — otherwise Luo Chen might have found a different target.

*It's gravely wounded. Even if it survives, there's no road forward. And with its entire clan on the edge of extinction... will it have the resolve to self-destruct?*

He murmured to himself.

Not every Ring-Headed Turtle demon king chose self-detonation when cornered.

That took absolute, unwavering resolve.

Just in the past day and night, by swooping in on battle-ravaged demon kings who had wounded each other to the brink, Luo Chen had taken five demon crab kings.

A remarkable haul, all things considered.

In the entire year before, sneaking and stealing, he had accumulated only seven.

One day's work against a full year — the gain was extraordinary.

With twelve third-tier demon crabs taken, Luo Chen was honestly ready to withdraw.

Twelve specimens for Black Emperor Paste refinement — the resulting quantity would be staggering.

Together with whatever he could purchase through the market later, he would have more than enough to support the Archaic Tier Three late-period tempering all the way to the fourth tier.

Of course.

An opportunity in front of him was an opportunity not to waste. Who turned down extra cultivation resources?

Watching the battle ahead grow increasingly grim and hopeless, Luo Chen felt a flicker of something — not quite sympathy, but recognition.

Chances like this — fishing in troubled waters on a battlefield of this scale — came perhaps once in a lifetime.

It was different from fighting cultivators.

On a cultivator battlefield, the same game would attract notice after just a few rounds.

On a demon beast battlefield, the sheer mass of low-tier creatures actually gave Luo Chen cover.

Add to that the demons' comparatively weaker divine sense, his own powerful concealment methods, and Han Zhan's support as a Sage Sage — that combination was what made him so effective here.

Even as these thoughts ran through his mind, the battle ahead began to reach its conclusion.

The moment he sensed the battered, barely-alive Ring-Headed Turtle suddenly surge with an explosive final burst of demon qi, Luo Chen's attention snapped to full alert.

*It's coming.*

The demon crab puppet burrowed downward, sinking into the ground and sliding into the Primordial Unity Cauldron prepared below.

The same operation as before: use the life-bound treasure to tank the first wave of the self-detonation, then surface in the chaos and ambush a demon crab.

A direct kill was ideal — that saved even more work.

Just as Luo Chen settled inside the cauldron's interior space, the battle outside reached its end.

That vast demon qi surged from the Ring-Headed Turtle demon king in a last blazing flare of life.

What came next should have been a world-shaking detonation.

Luo Chen waited in the cauldron, anticipating exactly that.

But after several breaths, the only thing that reached him was a single pained cry — no earthshaking explosion followed.

"Strange?"

Inside the cauldron, Luo Chen frowned.

By instinct, his divine sense extended outward, peering toward the center of the battlefield.

What he saw: the Ring-Headed Turtle's neck had been severed clean. It lay dead, seven orifices bleeding.

Its massive body was bound in dense white spider silk — not a single tremor remaining.

The final death-strike had been snuffed out before it could form.

And the moment Luo Chen's divine sense touched the scene, the three Demon Spider Crabs swung around as one to look directly at where he was.

*Not good — I've been found.*

Even as the thought surfaced —

"Get out here!"

The three demons roared and struck together.

Three orbs of condensed demon qi came crashing down in a thunderous volley.

Working in perfect concert, they drove their combined force straight into the earth.

*Clang.*

A sharp metallic ring rang out.

Dirt and soil exploded in every direction. Demon qi tore across the ground, and a huge detonation erupted.

From within that rolling blast, a small cauldron came flying out, radiating spiritual light — and a figure emerged with it, his expression dark.

"Damn it."

Worthy of the Demon Spider Crab clan's reputation for the sharpest perception among the five races. Three working together had actually detected Luo Chen's divine sense.

Muttering a curse, he snatched the Primordial Unity Cauldron and bolted without a second thought.

Being detected wasn't new.

*Walk along the riverbank often enough, and eventually your shoes get wet.*

But normally it was a single demon king that noticed him. If he struck fast enough, he could silence the target before word spread.

This time, three were present at once.

So Luo Chen ran. Without hesitation.

The three demons stared after him for a half-beat, stunned.

"A human cultivator?"

"An Adept?"

Before the disbelief could fully spread, the oldest of the three Demon Spider Crab demon kings snapped alert.

"This person must be the hidden mastermind the patriarch mentioned. Lurking here and watching us fight — nine chances out of ten he was planning an ambush."

That hit the other two like cold water.

"Don't let him escape!"

"After him!"

"Send word to the patriarch — and notify the other four clans' fighters on the battlefield to close in and kill this man!"

In those few seconds, the three demons reached their decision.

Three auras of demon qi shot skyward as they gave chase.

...

*Damn it — that Ring-Headed Turtle demon king was about to self-destruct. Why did it stop?*

*Luo Chen, self-detonation is not a sure thing. There are many techniques in this world capable of suppressing it. Given that the Ring-Headed Turtles have been self-detonating in numbers and the five royal clans have been suffering heavy losses, each side has already prepared countermeasures. The Demon Spider Crab clan is not renowned for battle strength, but their perception and their sealing and suppression abilities are uniquely their own. Three of them together can suppress a same-tier demon without much difficulty.*

*Fine. I'd been running too smoothly. I underestimated these creatures.*

*What's your plan now?*

*What else? Run. Xuanyan Island is no place for me anymore.*

Divine sense transmissions passed in fractions of an instant.

A brief exchange with Han Zhan, and the strategy was set.

He told Han Zhan to scan ahead and identify the best escape route. Luo Chen, meanwhile, flooded his mana and drove his speed to its limit.

Beyond that — a radiance bloomed at his back. A pair of wings spread wide.

In an instant, his already formidable escape speed exploded several times over.

Within a few wing-beats, the three pursuing Demon Spider Crabs were left far behind.

A streak of red light tore across the battlefield, churning the air in its wake.

The spectacle attracted attention.

The three Demon Spider Crabs watching it happen could only stare.

"That speed!"

"He's too fast. What did the patriarch say? The other clans need time to deploy — he might actually escape."

Infuriated but still running, the three demons refused to stop, dogging Luo Chen's trail.

Others on the battlefield had noticed too — not just low-tier demon beasts.

Several third-tier demon kings who had finished their own battles saw it.

Whatever the reason, this was an internal demon clan war in the Xuanyan Sea Region. No human cultivator had any business meddling.

That was understood by all.

It was why Gu Shaoshang had been driven off on his first day.

Now that Luo Chen was exposed, the five royal clans' fighters began moving to cut him off.

...

"Out of the way!"

A third-tier Azure Emperor Crab lunged in to block his path. Luo Chen didn't slow, didn't hesitate — he drew his sword and slashed.

A vast blade of sword-light swept down.

Unmatched mana behind it, and in a single exchange, the third-tier early-stage Azure Emperor Crab had half its body severed.

Luo Chen didn't even glance back. He surged onward, faster, harder.

Behind him, the Azure Emperor Crab — badly wounded, half its form gone — was frantically pulling its halves back together.

Watching Luo Chen's retreating figure, raw shock flickered in its eyes.

*That one sword stroke... how?*

*What realm is this person?*

"Another third-tier demon blocking ahead — mid-stage."

Han Zhan's divine sense transmission reached him. Luo Chen's expression stayed level.

He gathered his mana. His right hand curled through a forming seal. A technique wound to a peak.

Soon a massive demon crab peeled away from the battlefield and charged toward him — its shell bright red, as if freshly boiled. Unmistakably a Red Rock Crab.

Before the beast could close the distance, Luo Chen drove forward with a single palm.

An enormous azure handprint slammed down from above.

*BOOM.*

The sound shook the world. The demon king felt his soul rattle at the technique's pressure before it even reached him. He threw up his defenses in desperation —

And was pushed back several hundred zhang regardless, step by helpless step.

Without checking the result, Luo Chen's wings beat once, and his speed in humanoid form hit its absolute limit.

"Three Demon Spider Crab demon kings ahead — low-stage only, but their formation positioning is unusual. Watch yourself."

Han Zhan's transmission carried an edge of caution.

Luo Chen's expression shifted slightly.

His gaze found them: three Demon Spider Crabs striped with white markings, positioned squarely across his most direct escape route.

This was the shortest path off the island. The best line.

He couldn't afford to go around.

Seeing Luo Chen approaching, the three Demon Spider Crabs moved without hesitation — opening their maws and disgorging curtain after curtain of spider silk.

Each braced all eight legs against the ground, forming a stable three-point configuration.

In an instant, an immense sky-spanning net covered several li in every direction.

Against any ordinary Adept cultivator, that web would have been inescapable — even a breakthrough attempt would have forced a fatal pause.

But Luo Chen hadn't even thought of slowing down.

A thought — and his sword ignited in layers of azure flame, sheathing the Mystic Flame Sword entirely.

The fire roared.

Then —

Spiritual light blazed in his eyes. He scanned the net and found its single weakest point.

"Go!"

A quiet command. The Mystic Flame Sword launched outward, driving straight for the center of the web.

*Hiss.*

The blade flashed across the distance and struck the silk.

Demon qi and mana collided — it should have been a stalemate. But in the spread of the Withering-Bloom True Balefulness, the web tore as easily as a paper window.

The Mystic Flame Sword punched a hole through the net.

In the same instant, Luo Chen drove straight through the breach.

On the ground, the three Demon Spider Crab demon kings exchanged looks, not quite believing what they had seen.

Their kind loved forming three-unit squads to compensate for any individual's weaknesses.

Working together, three of them could advance and retreat as one, and the sealing and suppression demon arts they performed in formation had never failed against any foe.

Even the Ring-Headed Turtles' self-detonations, which had threatened the entire royal alliance, held little fear for them.

Yet just now — that carefully prepared net, torn just like that?

"That fire..."

Unease flickered in all three pairs of eyes.

Luo Chen, having cleared the obstacle, paid no attention to what they were thinking.

The battlefield was saturated with peer-level opponents.

He couldn't stop.

If he did, even with the Ring-Headed Turtles as a distraction, he would eventually be swallowed by the ocean of demon beasts.

And no amount of self-confidence would save him from that.

His only path was this: one continuous motion, a thousand li of distance, off Xuanyan Island.

"You've nearly left the range of the Water Mirror. My remnant soul's divine sense can scout maybe a hundred li ahead for you at most."

"Multiple demon kings are converging behind you, but they can't form a true encirclement at their speed. Only one threat remains ahead: a great demon king at third-tier late stage. Break through without stopping for more than ten breaths."

Han Zhan's voice arrived through divine sense.

Every fiber of Luo Chen's awareness pulled taut.

*Still one more blocking me.*

*And not a common one — a great demon king at late stage.*

"Which clan?" He asked in a low voice.

"Nine-Clawed Poison King Crab."

Luo Chen's expression flickered. This was the clan he least wanted to encounter.

In all these years, Black King and Heavenjade had never once caught a Nine-Clawed Poison King Crab.

Because this crab's venom — deeply ingrained through its entire body — could corrode mana itself. One touch, and the Golden Core could become contaminated.

Even some Sage cultivators refused to engage them directly.

At the thought of a Nine-Clawed Poison King Crab great demon king standing between him and the water, Luo Chen felt his chest tighten.

*Go around?*

No.

Demon kings were converging from all sides. Going around was not an option.

*Then break through.*

He clenched his jaw. His Broken Moon Wings beat with furious intensity.

Wind howled around him as his speed surged.

Soon, a vast, jet-black demon crab came into view atop what appeared to be a small mountain — head tilted back, watching him approach.

Nothing else was nearby.

Luo Chen looked again.

That wasn't a mountain under its claws.

It was a mountain of Ring-Headed Turtle corpses, stacked upon each other.

The demon fixed him with a savage grin, then opened its maw and spat.

Black liquid surged outward, hitting the air with a stench that turned the stomach.

Touching the atmosphere, the black liquid transformed — a spreading cloud of black fog blanketed the surrounding sky in every direction, sealing off the world.

The Nine-Clawed Poison King Crab clan's signature technique: centuries of refined personal venom, transformed into a lethal mist.

Clearly, accounting for Luo Chen's speed, ordinary attacks would never catch him — so it had shifted to the maximum-coverage poison fog instead.

Touch a single wisp, and the consequences would be severe.

And beyond that —

After releasing the fog, the great demon king rose into the air. Its hideous maw split open, skin and flesh peeling back — and a curved black hook floated out from within.

It had refined its own ninth claw into something like a human cultivator's life-bound magic treasure.

A black hook. A poison hook.

Braced and ready, the demon spoke in human tongue.

"Human. Why not stay a while?"

In response.

Luo Chen said nothing. His speed didn't drop by a hair.

His wings beat and he drove straight into the rolling poison fog.

The great demon king let out a sneer.

"Blind panic?"

But in the next moment, the sneer died on its lips.

From deep within the dense fog came a chorus of sharp hissing sounds.

Like oil hitting a blazing wok.

Peering closer, a cloud of fire-red surged outward through the fog — the Blazing Fire Miasma spreading in every direction, pushing the poisonous mist back layer by layer.

The cloud was miasma-like, and balefulness-like, and something wholly its own.

Where the balefulness met the fog, both consumed each other steadily.

Watching its centuries of refined personal venom being eaten away at this rate, the great demon king's expression darkened with genuine pain.

It could wait no longer — it hurled the black poison hook forward.

Shrieking through the air, the hook drove into the fog.

And at that same moment, Luo Chen burst through, Blazing Fire Miasma boiling around him.

Facing the incoming black hook, he snapped three fingers in rapid succession — three streaks of black light shot out, one after another, each a hair's breadth behind the last.

*Ding!*

*Ding!*

*Ding!*

In rapid succession, the three black lights met the poison hook in brief, sharp exchanges.

First strike: the hook powered through, scattering the first black light's radiance entirely.

Second strike: the hook shuddered and locked with the second black light for just an instant before pushing forward again — but its surging demon qi had already dissipated by more than half.

Third strike: the hook simply froze in midair.

And the great demon king controlling its life-bound treasure froze too, its body locked in place for a single breath.

That one breath was all Luo Chen needed.

He shot past, leaving the demon king behind.

One glance back at the grotesque crab, and Han Zhan's voice came through.

"Past here, I can't help you. You'll have to judge the road yourself. Don't worry about the swamp — I'll bring Black King out through the deep sea underwater."

Luo Chen acknowledged with a hum, and reached backward with one hand.

One of the three black lights came streaking back to him.

Of the other two: one had gone silent entirely. The other rose unsteadily, wavering — trying to follow.

But the great demon king, recovered now, drove its poison hook down and knocked the wobbling light from the air.

It looked close.

A black nail.

The great demon king watched Luo Chen's receding figure, expression dark.

*Fine technique.*

Three nails: one attacking the hook's body directly, one scattering the demon qi wreathing it, one severing for just a moment the connection between it and its master.

And before that — the miasma-balefulness cloud.

Two killing techniques prepared in advance — both broken in an instant, one after another.

The combat instincts of this human cultivator — to read a live battle and make such perfect judgments on the fly — were genuinely beyond belief.

*So this is a human cultivator.*

Word was that Gu Shaoshang had clashed briefly with several third-tier demon crabs and shown considerable skill — enough to earn the five clans' acknowledgment.

People like this came loaded with trump cards, and they weren't remotely comparable to demon kin with only one or two signature techniques.

Perhaps only reaching the fourth tier — changing one's nature, refining the flesh, taking human form — would make it possible to match them.

The transformation stage.

The great demon king's gaze fell to the Xuanyan Island beneath it.

Once this island was taken, it would become the grounds for that advancement.

While those thoughts flickered through its mind, figures converged from all sides.

Mostly Nine-Clawed Poison King Crabs — but even more from the other four clans.

They looked at Luo Chen's retreating form and couldn't help asking:

"Demon King Jiunan, why did you let that Adept go?"

"That person is almost certainly the mastermind behind the Heavenly Pivot Island incident. Our clans have suffered serious losses because of them over these years. We cannot let them escape alive."

"Could it be that your Nine-Clawed Poison King Crab clan has taken no losses, and you're willing to just stand by and watch them walk away?"

Accusation after accusation.

Jiunan was unmoved. From its elevated position, one gaze swept across every demon king below.

One look — cold, cruel, absolute — and the assembled demons shuddered, heads instinctively dipping.

"Hmph."

A cold snort. Jiunan's voice came slow and quiet.

"I didn't let them go. That person had too many tricks. They used a secret treasure to briefly sever my bond to my life-bound treasure. One moment's disadvantage, and they had their opening."

"As for why I didn't pursue —"

Jiunan's lips parted in a cruel smile.

"Don't worry. Golden Claw has been chasing targets that fled the island. He's been fighting out at sea. Calculate the time — that battle should already be over by now. If that human returns by the same route, they'll run straight into him."

"Heh. Give it a while — and you'll be seeing that human's corpse."

*(End of Chapter)*
