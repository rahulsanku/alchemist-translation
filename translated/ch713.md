# Chapter 713: Han Zhan's Legacy, New Life Amid Ruin

Splash, splash...

The seawater that had poured backward into Dragon Abyss Island [TERM?] came fiercely and withdrew just as quickly.

Along one ravine after another, it swept mountain stones, forests, and trees back into the calm sea.

From time to time, tidewater gently washed the beach, and when it withdrew again, fine soft sand spread across the flat tidal flats like a delicate velvet carpet.

It seemed everything had become peaceful.

But if one's gaze moved from outside to inside, one could see that Dragon Abyss Island [TERM?], once like a paradise beyond the world, had now become a field of ruins.

The already low mountains had collapsed and broken.

Ancient trees hundreds and thousands of years old burned with roaring flames. Even after the waves retreated, they had not been extinguished, and faint crackling arcs of lightning could be seen remaining on the ancient trees.

The spirit fields Sang Jinghe [TERM?] had carefully arranged earlier were even more broken at the ridges, their medicinal grasses flattened.

Especially looking down from high above, centered on Mount Rosy Hill [TERM?], the thousand-li area of Dragon Abyss Island [TERM?] was full of jagged rocks and crisscrossing ravines.

One ugly gigantic trench after another, like earthworms, densely spread across the land.

Countless strands of spirit qi broke through cracks in the stone, uncontrollably escaping from the underground abyss.

The culprits behind all this, apart from the heavenly punishment lightning tribulation Luo Chen had drawn, were those two gigantic beings.

The towering white tiger, blade-wings slanting upward and four feet spread apart, stood dead still on the earth, without any aura.

A hundred-zhang-long black jiaolong lay prone on the ground, breathing heavily.

Split skin and torn flesh were insufficient to describe his wretchedness. Being cut through at the waist was the present state.

Even the spiral patterns on the single horn atop his head were now dim and lightless.

"Huu... It stopped moving?"

Black King lay panting on the earth, his bloodshot eyes making his vision blurred.

Just then, a teasing voice entered his ears.

"Black King, how did you end up so sorry-looking?"

The voice carried teasing and bits of mockery, yet falling into Black King's ears, it was so warm and intimate, like heavenly music.

He struggled to lift his dragon head and looked toward the sky.

The enormous giant body had already vanished. Only a small black dot fell from the nine heavens, gradually revealing a clear face.

It was Luo Chen!

"Master..."

This cry of Master contained endless vicissitude, joy, worship, guilt, and self-reproach beyond words.

Only the connection in his bloodline made him believe that the person before him was Master Luo Chen, and not old thief Han Zhan!

Luo Chen looked at Black King's current condition and could not help nodding.

Although he was miserable, he had at least temporarily withstood the white tiger puppet while Luo Chen and Han Zhan fought with their divine souls.

From this, it could be seen that after Black King advanced to fourth tier, his strength had indeed improved enormously.

"Need help?"

Black King hurriedly shook his head. "No, no. Little Black's injuries are nothing."

He gritted his teeth, and the two halves of his body cut apart by the white tiger puppet actually reconnected automatically, while the torn surface of his body also recovered rapidly.

Such rapid fleshly self-healing speed... Luo Chen's eyes focused.

Undying Body [TERM?]!

This was a constitution only some legendary archaic beasts could possess.

It did not rely on any power, but purely on the attraction of the body's own fleshly tissues.

Although Luo Chen could also reconnect severed limbs and regenerate flesh and blood, he relied on origin power to support it.

Just now, under Luo Chen's divine sense, he had seen everything clearly. Black King had not even used origin power, yet the severed jiaolong body had naturally reconnected like that.

Was it his own innate talent? Or the foundation after soaking in the Spirit-Molding Sacred Spring [TERM?]? Or perhaps... the drop of tongue-tip essence blood of mine that contained both origin power and Tai Sui vitality?

The greater possibility was that many factors had created this constitution together.

No wonder he, with ability just entering fourth tier, could hold back the white tiger puppet for so long.

"Master, are you all right?"

As Black King recovered his body, he carefully showed concern for Luo Chen.

In his words, there was still some self-reproach.

He had actually fallen for old thief Han Zhan's scheme. When Master formed his Nascent Soul, not only had he failed to protect him, he had instead wanted to imprison Master's Nascent Soul.

Luo Chen glared at him irritably. "There are plenty of matters. Roll back and properly heal your injuries!"

After being scolded, Black King not only did not tremble in panic, but instead let out a breath and chuckled.

While the two conversed, Heavenjade had already brought the dazed Sang Jinghe [TERM?] over to join them.

At first sight of Luo Chen, hesitation appeared on Heavenjade's face.

"Master?"

"Mm."

Luo Chen softly acknowledged, then said, "Next, Black King will heal. You will reorganize Dragon Abyss Island [TERM?] and clean up the aftermath. What happened to Sang Jinghe [TERM?]?"

"He..."

Heavenjade had just opened her mouth when Sang Jinghe's [TERM?] wailing could be heard.

"Destroyed, all destroyed."

"The spirit fields, the medicinal grasses, and the blood pool I studied to cultivate the Demon-Beast Heartlink Tree [TERM?] are all destroyed."

Luo Chen frowned. What kind of behavior was this, crying and sobbing?

He flicked his sleeve, and mana swept over. Sang Jinghe [TERM?] immediately came awake.

He shivered, looking at Luo Chen with uncertainty.

Luo Chen did not say much. "Handle the island's affairs as you see fit. I need to enter seclusion for a while. Do not disturb me."

After speaking, he withdrew and left.

When he passed that enormous, dead-still puppet, Luo Chen paused, then said without turning his head:

"Do not touch this fellow either. Let it stay here. I will deal with it later."

After he left, one human and two demons looked at one another.

Heavenjade was still somewhat unable to confirm. "That was Master, right?"

Black King snorted. "Of course that was Master. Could it be that without a slave seal, you cannot recognize Master?"

A slave seal was a type of contract formed between cultivators and spirit pets, nearly between a restriction and a spell.

At first, it was generated with assistance from the Spirit-Enslaving Seal [TERM?] and pills, and later, the connection gradually deepened until divine souls were linked.

The slave seals on the two of them had already been removed in Bright Radiance Heaven's [TERM?] Pill Palace because of Luo Chen's physical nirvana.

That was why Heavenjade had been unable to recognize Luo Chen for a time.

But through that familiar tone, manner of speech, and way of doing things, she also came awake. Senior Han... no, old thief Han, who had harbored evil intentions, was not like this.

Sang Jinghe [TERM?], whose mind had been thrown into chaos because the painstaking effort of many years had been lost in a single morning, had not spoken since Luo Chen awakened him.

Only after the two great demons finished speaking did he cautiously ask, "Did Superior... succeed in forming his Nascent Soul?"

Black King and Heavenjade looked at each other, then nodded in tacit understanding.

"From now on, you must call him Sage!"

...

Within the earth abyss.

Several li away from Luo Chen's previous cultivation place, Luo Chen sat cross-legged inside a newly opened cave.

That previous place, because of his Nascent Soul condensation, had drawn away large amounts of spirit qi from the earth vein, causing its spirit qi concentration to fall below fourth tier.

Although it was slowly recovering, during the recovery period, the spirit qi was restless and wild, making it not very suitable for cultivation.

Therefore, Luo Chen changed to another cultivation place.

This crude cave, not very spacious, still had a fourth-tier spirit vein's spirit qi concentration, and it was relatively peaceful.

Luo Chen had no time to arrange it carefully. He immediately circulated the Fine Dust Primordial Art, clearing his mind, stabilizing his spirit, and gathering stray thoughts.

Half a day later.

Only then did he slowly recover.

The previously restless emotions and mixed thoughts had finally calmed.

"For the outside matters, a few instructions are enough. Next is handling the matters of this body."

After murmuring, Luo Chen first examined his flesh body.

Under that lightning tribulation, not only had it suffered no real damage, it had advanced again because he had borrowed heavenly thunder to refine his body.

The increase was not large, only five points of progress, reaching [Archaic Tier Four 40/100].

Regarding this, Luo Chen was already very satisfied.

The higher the body cultivation realm, the slower progress became, especially when Myriad Path Convergence could no longer provide more assistance.

This time, in only one day's effort, it had jumped five points of progress, which was already an unexpected joy.

"This is probably the final afterglow of Myriad Path Convergence."

Luo Chen shook his head. There was no long hair fluttering, only threads of cold wind.

He subconsciously touched his bald head and laughed despite himself.

To say his flesh body had no damage, his fine hair had at least been burned dry and gone under the five thunderbolts striking the crown.

However, that did not matter.

Hair was only hair. Along this road, it had long since endured endless torment.

There had been times when it had all fallen out, times when it had turned white, and cycles of withered and flourishing hair had been even more frequent.

If he wanted it, by mobilizing origin power, he could grow it back in an instant.

But that would be somewhat wasteful.

It would be better to cultivate in seclusion for a year or two, and it would naturally grow back.

Luo Chen took a deep breath and no longer paid attention to his flesh body. Instead, his mind turned inward, mobilizing faint divine sense to inspect his Purple Mansion [TERM?].

The qi sea that had once been like a sea of fire had vanished.

It had transformed into thick barriers, forming something like a cave dwelling within his body.

According to the records in classics, plus what Han Zhan had described...

Thinking of Han Zhan, Luo Chen's emotions paused, then he compared his current state to his memories.

After cultivators condense the Nascent Soul, their three treasures temporarily unite, forming the so-called state of three flowers gathering at the crown [TERM?].

In this state, the divine soul, which once feared blazing sun and astral wind, could leave through the Nascent Soul, achieving the initial level of yin soul transforming into yang spirit.

This was only the most preliminary stage. To truly transform into a yang spirit, fearing no harsh natural environment outside, one had to wait until the Ascendant realm.

That was a digression.

Returning to the Sage realm.

In this realm, a cultivator's Nascent Soul could roam heaven and earth, observing drifting spirit qi, the natural evolution of things, and even the veins of the Great Dao! Thus, it was called seeing the true heaven and earth, which was also the origin of the term "Sage."

And during Nascent Soul formation, Luo Chen had already briefly experienced this state of observing heaven and earth.

The spirit merging with heaven and earth!

However, no matter how one spoke of the Nascent Soul blending with heaven and earth like water and milk, the Nascent Soul was ultimately the condensation of a cultivator's own great power. If it left the flesh body for too long, it would instead be assimilated by heaven and earth, erasing its own will.

That experience was something every cultivator longed for, yet also feared.

Therefore, during the long process of cultivation, to prevent the Nascent Soul from being assimilated by heaven and earth, the cultivation result of the qi sea giving birth to the Purple Mansion [TERM?] appeared.

The vast qi sea transformed into thick Purple Mansion [TERM?] barriers, isolating the Nascent Soul from assimilation by heaven and earth. Only during battle would the Nascent Soul briefly come into contact with heaven and earth.

This was also why after Han Zhan's flesh body had been destroyed in the past, he did not dare act lightly. Most of the time, he curled up inside Luo Chen's Primordial Unity Cauldron, Soul-Refining Banner, and even the soul-nourishing wood plaque, not hesitating to halt cultivation for this.

One could say the Purple Mansion [TERM?] was both a restriction on the cultivator and a protection for the Nascent Soul.

Now, under Luo Chen's observation, the thickness of his Purple Mansion [TERM?] barrier was difficult to measure.

As for the internal space of the Purple Mansion [TERM?], it was even broader, absolutely not a mere three-cun place.

"It should be formed from my foundation."

After understanding this, Luo Chen focused on the situation within the Purple Mansion [TERM?].

Large amounts of pure mana formed misty qi and were bound within the Purple Mansion [TERM?].

Within the mist, a fair, tender infant identical to himself sat cross-legged.

It was called an infant, but nose was nose, eyes were eyes, and hands, feet, and torso all had clear outlines, completely a scaled-down version of Luo Chen's body.

This work of creation was truly breathtaking.

The infant sat there quietly, making no movements. The surrounding misty aura gathered toward him, but was blocked outside.

Looking at that misty qi, Luo Chen's mood became heavy.

This energy was absolutely not his own, nor was it bestowed by heaven and earth after he passed the lightning tribulation.

"It is the pure mana Han Zhan refined throughout his life!"

As Luo Chen inspected the Purple Mansion [TERM?], a blue-gold light jumped about with joy.

A jumping flame!

Luo Chen's divine soul expressed approval, mobilizing mana to gently stroke that flame.

It was as if saying, "I know, I know, this is your contribution."

Receiving praise, the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit [TERM?] grew even more excited, lightly shaking its branches and rolling a wisp of misty qi over to Luo Chen's Nascent Soul.

Taking credit again?

At first Luo Chen paid it little mind, still thinking about how to expel the mana left behind after Han Zhan's Nascent Soul had been refined from his Purple Mansion [TERM?]. He did not want another person's mana to corrode his Nascent Soul.

But upon examining it closely, he was shocked.

"This..."

Luo Chen's Nascent Soul trembled lightly and subconsciously drew that wisp of misty qi from the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit's [TERM?] hand.

Wood-attribute mana, pure mana, without any mark left by the cultivator before death. Not even the slightest divine soul aura remained!

"Did you refine this as well?"

Luo Chen looked at the Withering-Bloom True Flame. The other party proudly shook its branches.

Luo Chen fell silent.

When he crossed tribulation, aside from his flesh body and the Mystic Dust Armor [TERM?], he actually had many other methods he could use.

For example, the Withering-Bloom True Flame, which had advanced to fourth tier for many years and was not far from fifth tier!

But at that time, he had forcibly suppressed that impulse, in order to preserve the Withering-Bloom True Flame's power and guard against Han Zhan possibly rising in sudden attack.

The final result had been correct.

It was precisely because the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit [TERM?] acted that Han Zhan's Nascent Soul had been burned away, thoroughly severing the retreat for his divine soul.

Looking at it now, he discovered there had been an extra gain.

The Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit [TERM?] had not crossed tribulation, but it had also enjoyed the misty qi poured down by the heavenly ladle during the Purple Mansion's [TERM?] evolution, which greatly increased its spirituality.

Not only that, it seemed to have used this fortune to completely absorb that wisp of Nirvana Sacred Flame it had devoured inside the Pill Palace.

That was why, without Luo Chen controlling it, it could refine away the remaining marks in Han Zhan's Nascent Soul on its own.

"In this case, I have benefited. As long as I absorb all this pure mana without any marks or aura, it will be enough to save me decades of work consolidating my realm!"

The infant beamed and cheered.

Han Zhan had previously said he was making wedding clothes for him. Looking at it now, he had instead left Luo Chen an incomparably rich legacy!

Although this mana was wood-attribute, converting it would not be difficult for Luo Chen.

Especially among the mutual generation of the five elements, wood gave birth to fire.

Back when he switched cultivation methods in the early Adept stage, he had converted from wood attribute to fire attribute.

This kind of thing was familiar work!

However, before converting the mana and consolidating his realm, there were still some matters.

Luo Chen suppressed his impulse and observed the magic treasures floating within the Purple Mansion [TERM?].

The Mystic Dust Armor [TERM?] was still tattered, but within the ruin, there was a sense of new life!

After absorbing the thunder power from the heavenly tribulation, it was enough to open the formations artificially carved into it during refinement. During its slow restoration and evolution later, these man-made formations would merge more thoroughly with those materials.

It would be as if they became the meridians and blood vessels inside the magic treasure.

With this, the magic treasure could advance to a true artifact and, like a Sage Sage, communicate with the spirit qi of heaven and earth instead of only relying on its own power.

Communicating with heaven and earth spirit qi, borrowing heaven and earth spirit qi, no longer relying only on a cultivator's own mana: this was the difference between a magic treasure and a true artifact.

Not much, and not great, but the difference was heaven and earth.

The Moonbreaker Wings [TERM?] still danced within the Purple Mansion [TERM?] space. They too had endured the lightning tribulation's bombardment.

Unfortunately, their foundation was insufficient. According to Luo Chen's observation, even if they completely absorbed the remaining thunder power on them, they could only reach upper-grade at most, far from the degree of refining the formations and opening meridians.

After only one look, Luo Chen paid them no further attention.

What he cared about most was actually only one treasure.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron!

With a thought from Luo Chen, the gray little cauldron standing in the Purple Mansion [TERM?] trembled slightly.

Only this slight tremble made Luo Chen feel relieved and joyful.

"Finally!"

"I can activate my lifebound magic treasure again!"
