# Chapter 906: Black Water Deep Pool, Honored Guest Arrives (Happy New Year, Everyone!)

Two streaks of escaping light shot across the sky like flying stars.

Piercing through the vast layers of clouds, they finally beheld the towering mountain suspended in midair.

Vaguely visible were the protruding eaves of three palace halls.

This was Yanfu Mountain!

Luo Chen knew that the three halls were respectively the Heavenly Nether Palace, the Heavenly Abyss Palace, and the Heavenly Marsh Palace, corresponding to the three Nascent Soul Ancestors of the Nether Origin Sect.

Yu Ming, Tian Yuan, Hei Ze!

The two of them advanced together. There seemed to be formations blocking their path, but with Tan Sheng in the lead, none of those formations truly revealed their power.

Suddenly!

"We're here!"

Tan Sheng landed on the ground in a spacious, empty square.

Without turning his head, he said, "Pavilion Master Dan Sheng, please wait a moment. I will go and see the Ancestor."

Luo Chen clasped his hands and smiled. "No trouble at all. Thank you for your efforts, Fellow Daoist Tan."

Only then did Tan Sheng turn around and carefully appraise Luo Chen. The haughty expression on his face had faded considerably.

He explained, "It will be quick!"

Then, Tan Sheng ascended the long staircase, heading toward the Heavenly Nether Palace.

Luo Chen smiled. Although this person's attitude had been less than ideal from the start, there was no real malice.

He supposed it was merely the natural, inherent pride of a sacred land's disciple.

Especially since the other party was a personal disciple of Ancestor Yu Ming himself—such pride was only to be expected.

But a smiling face invites no blows. In the future... no, even now, he could practically be considered the foremost figure beneath the three Ancestors of the Nether Origin Sect. There was no need to puff himself up and maintain that kind of pride.

He had graciously offered a way out with kind words. As long as the other party wasn't too foolish, they should take the hint and come down from their high horse.

Watching the other ascend the steps of the Heavenly Nether Palace, Luo Chen began to survey Yanfu Mountain beneath his feet.

Those three majestic, imposing palaces didn't particularly interest him.

Cultivators, especially those from major sects, always liked to put up a grand front. Weren't the important halls of his own Luo Tian Sect also built like immortal paradises?

What he was focusing on now was this mountain!

His golden pupils shifted, and Luo Chen's brow twitched slightly.

"Not being within this mountain, one cannot know its true face. Now that I see it, I understand its divine subtlety!"

He had seen floating buildings before.

Back then, the Azure Pill Valley had possessed such a Cloud Summit Heavenly Palace.

At first, he thought it was magnificent. Only later did he learn that it was merely a special arrangement by the Azure Pill Valley for their True Disciple Grand Competition.

Maintaining the Cloud Summit Heavenly Palace required a massive amount of spirit stones. It was only brought out to show off during the True Disciple Grand Competition or when the Sect Leader traveled.

But Yanfu Mountain was completely different!

Under Luo Chen's dharma-eye scrutiny, Yanfu Mountain itself was inhaling and exhaling a vast amount of heaven and earth spiritual energy.

Not only that, but this spiritual energy rolled downward, enveloping the entire Nether Origin Sect's territory of hundreds of thousands of li, like grace bestowed upon all living things.

Furthermore, strands of formation spiritual light flickered in various places, causing the earth's qi to circulate without pause.

Luo Chen withdrew his gaze, a thoughtful expression on his face.

"So this is the main vein of that fifth-order spirit vein of the Nether Abyss!"

Legend had it that Yanfu Mountain was originally deep underground.

In ancient times, it was excavated by powerful cultivators of the Nether Origin Sect and then propelled into the sky with great means.

Now, it seemed this was also a method of nurturing a spirit vein.

Suspended in the nine heavens, receiving starlight, with earth qi circulating, grace bestowed upon all living things.

Moreover, through constant refinement, Yanfu Mountain itself had already become a powerful dharma artifact, only one step away from the mountain gaining sentience.

This was a quasi-spirit treasure!

It wasn't merely about blessing the Nether Abyss. It also suppressed the earth qi of this region, attracting various spiritual opportunities to converge here.

If it continued to grow, it might one day achieve the level of a sect-suppressing spirit treasure, comparable to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard.

At that point, it would no longer suppress just one region, but an entire continent!

Although it was still far from that now, even so, Yanfu Mountain's foundation was already thick enough.

If someone were to forcibly drive this thing to attack an enemy...

"Tsk tsk!"

Luo Chen gave a soft exclamation of admiration. He dared not imagine that scene.

In any case, with his current formidable physique, he absolutely could not withstand it.

Just as Luo Chen was marveling, a figure hurriedly descended the distant staircase.

Tan Sheng quickly arrived at Luo Chen's side. "Go. The Ancestor has freed up some time."

"Fellow Daoist, you could have just called out from up there. Why go to all this trouble?" Luo Chen smiled.

Tan Sheng waved his hand. "The Heavenly Nether Palace is currently under a state of heightened alert. Ordinary cultivators are not allowed to approach. Even I, as the Ancestor's disciple, am no exception. You may go up on your own."

Luo Chen clasped his hands in thanks. "In that case, I shall take my leave!"

Having said this, he smoothed his robes and gracefully stepped onto the staircase.

The moment his foot touched the step, his figure paused slightly.

But it was only a brief pause. After that, he proceeded normally, and his speed wasn't even greatly affected.

Seeing this, Tan Sheng's eyes widened from behind.

"Is he not affected by the overflowing pressure?"

Just one step!

Luo Chen felt something was wrong.

Waves of pressure were occasionally spilling out from the palace above.

Although it wasn't strong and wasn't deliberately directed at him, that power was still uncomfortable to bear.

"Is it the pressure of the Nether Abyss Ancestor?"

"No!"

"How could a mighty Ascendant cultivator fail to control their own pressure?"

Suddenly, the source power within his body circulated, and that pressure dissipated like a gentle breeze against his face, having little further effect.

A thought arose in Luo Chen's mind.

"Could this pressure be emanating from that suppressed Qinghe Immortal?"

He stopped thinking about it and, step by step, ascended the densely packed staircase, walking both quickly and steadily.

From a high vantage point, Luo Chen, looking like an ant on that long heavenly staircase, appeared so insignificant, as if he would never reach the end.

But the moment he set foot on this staircase, there was no turning back.

No one knew how many storms and obstacles Luo Chen had weathered along this journey, how many times he had risen early and toiled late, racking his brains, nor how he had once been young and brash, filled with soaring ambition.

Until this very moment, he stood before the gates of that palace.

Luo Chen looked back at the path he had traveled, a nameless melancholy in his heart.

Perhaps there wouldn't be so many storms in the future.

With the shelter of this great tree that was the Nether Origin Sect, and with the guidance of Rosycloud on the right path, the only tiger blocking his way was the advancement to Ascendant.

Finally, he could relax a little.

He shook his head, composed himself, and straightened his robes.

"Before the struggle ends, I still have to meet those three!"

He slowly pushed open the door, and three divine senses instantly fell upon him.

Luo Chen bowed deeply. "This junior, Luo Chen, pays respects to the three Ancestors!"

...

"What is your relationship with Rosycloud?"

The moment he entered the hall, the azure-robed elder seated in the center suddenly posed this question.

It was so sharp, so unprepared for.

Luo Chen calmly replied, "I have no relationship with her!"

This answer was not satisfactory.

Ancestor Yu Ming's gaze burned as he stared fixedly at Luo Chen, as if trying to see through him completely.

Luo Chen hesitated for a moment, then, as if confessing, lowered his voice and said, "Just as the three Ancestors value my alchemy, Rosycloud also valued it. She invited me to Green Parasol Mountain to refine a few Transformation Pills."

"Transformation Pills?" Ancestor Yu Ming was startled.

Luo Chen hummed in acknowledgment and explained, "In fact, back when this junior was still at the Adept stage, I was captured by demon cultivators from Green Parasol Mountain after the Falling Cloud Sect's expansion war failed and was taken back there. To survive, this junior was forced to refine pills for them. Unfortunately, my skills were lacking at the time, and the pills I refined were of poor quality, causing one of the major figures to fail in their transformation. Hence, there was a second trip to Green Parasol Mountain."

Ancestor Hei Ze beside him looked somewhat skeptical.

But then, he recalled something.

"When this old man went to probe Green Parasol Mountain back then, I did indeed see a woman with a crow's head and a human body. It was obvious she was a failed product of transformation."

Luo Chen explained softly, "That woman is Nether Spring, the vice-pavilion master of the Truth Crossing Hall, one of the three halls of Green Parasol Mountain."

A dignified vice-pavilion master being a failed product of transformation was something shameful to speak of.

If he was taken back because of such a matter, it seemed understandable?

Moreover, for the demon race, the importance of Transformation Pills far exceeded that of ordinary fourth-order pills.

"You truly know how to refine Transformation Pills?"

Luo Chen said nothing, but simply produced three Transformation Pills he had once refined in the Demon-Fall Land. They were also among the few he had left in stock.

After all, the materials required by the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's Transformation Pill formula were lacking in the Mountains and Seas Realm.

Of course, with Luo Chen's current Grandmaster-level alchemy realm, he could completely adapt the formula using local materials and refine pills with effects no less than the original version.

The three Ancestors sat in a triangle, facing each other. Before them was a pool of black spring water, bubbling and gurgling.

They waved their hands, summoned the Transformation Pills, and carefully examined them for a while. They could indeed detect the subtlety within.

Yet, even so, Ancestor Hei Ze remained somewhat skeptical.

"You are human, they are demon race. They invited you over, and then let you return?"

At this moment, Luo Chen appeared somewhat hesitant.

Ancestor Hei Ze's eyes flickered with cold light. "Now that you have entered the Nether Origin Sect and the position of Pavilion Master Dan Sheng has been granted to you, we are one family from now on. If you still have something to hide, just as we let you ascend to the nine heavens today, we can send you down to the underworld tomorrow!"

Ancestor Tian Yuan laughed. "Junior Brother, you exaggerate."

"Hmph!" Ancestor Hei Ze snorted coldly, still staring fixedly at Luo Chen.

In truth, it wasn't just him. Ancestor Yu Ming was the same, and even Ancestor Tian Yuan, though wearing a smile, was also staring intently at Luo Chen.

Under the scrutiny of three Ascendant-level great powers, who would dare lie to their faces?

Luo Chen took a deep breath and said in a trembling voice, "The reason they were willing to let me return was not only because I resolved the transformation aftermath of the demoness Nether Spring, but also because my fire-attribute cultivation was exceptionally outstanding. Rosycloud is a fellow practitioner of the Dao. She took pity on my difficult cultivation path and thus did not deliberately make things difficult for me. She even offered some guidance."

In Luo Chen's account, Rosycloud was suddenly transformed into a virtuous and respected senior.

And she even offered guidance!

But what he said was the truth, even if only part of it!

"Exceptionally outstanding fire-attribute cultivation?" Ancestor Tian Yuan became interested. "Is it your origin true flame?"

Luo Chen nodded and, without holding back, immediately focused his mind.

Boom!

A mass of cyan-white flame materialized around him.

In the great hall, it transformed into a small tree of cyan and white, its branches swaying.

The expressions of the three Ancestors shifted slightly.

"A fifth-order divine flame!"

"And it's two opposing true flames!"

"Truly outstanding!"

Amidst their exclamations, they could clearly sense a hint of danger.

If Luo Chen's realm were equal to theirs, and he could control the heaven and earth origin qi, then activating this flame would make it a deadly trump card.

But as things stood, it was like a child wielding a great sword—unable to harm others and likely to injure itself.

Gurgle, gurgle!

In the center of the hall, that pool of dark, cold water churned with violent waves.

Luo Chen subconsciously looked over.

Ancestor Hei Ze gave a light snort. "Put it away. Stop showing off."

With that single snort, the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit was struck as if by a heavy blow, and it swiftly retreated back into Luo Chen's body.

Luo Chen, the master, turned pale, also realizing what had happened.

This Ancestor Hei Ze had already comprehended the true meaning of the water-element law!

Water and fire counteract each other. One could even say that the water element naturally restrains the fire element.

Unless a cultivator of fire-attribute techniques was naturally far stronger than a water-attribute cultivator, they could form a reverse suppression.

But clearly, Luo Chen was far, far too inferior to Ancestor Hei Ze at this moment.

He cast another discreet glance at that pool of black water, then stood respectfully to the side, speaking with a slightly aggrieved tone:

"Actually, this junior is already used to this kind of thing."

"When I was young, as a rogue cultivator, I merely showed a hint of alchemy talent, and I was noticed by an Initiate-stage expert who forcibly recruited me under his command."

As he said this, he paused, then continued:

"After reaching the Initiate stage, it was also by relying on my alchemy skills that I was able to befriend a sect and gain a foothold."

"I just never expected that even after finally becoming a Sage, I still couldn't escape this fate."

"I suppose there is always someone better, and heavens beyond heavens. This junior still has many shortcomings and cannot control my own destiny!"

This speech was filled with grievance and helplessness.

Even the three Ancestors felt a little awkward.

Their recruitment of Luo Chen into the Nether Origin Sect was, in essence, no different from that Initiate-stage cultivator forcibly recruiting him.

But they knew that everything Luo Chen said was true.

The intelligence on Luo Chen was hardly a secret.

As long as one was willing to collect it, not to mention the Nether Origin Sect, even other ordinary Sage-level sects could thoroughly investigate Luo Chen's entire rise to prominence.

Ancestor Tian Yuan comforted him, "The tree that stands out in the forest is the first to be felled by the wind. An innocent man may cherish a jewel only to become a criminal. Possessing alchemy skills that surpass the common knowledge of the world will naturally invite the covetousness of others. Now that you have joined our Nether Origin Sect, you need not worry any longer."

Luo Chen's eyes lit up. "Is that true?"

Ancestor Tian Yuan laughed heartily. "How could it be false? From now on, you can freely study the Dao of alchemy, just like Chen Mozi and Xin Yisheng. You can even leave behind your own legacy, like Chu Yan and Master Xuanyan. If you wish to break through to the Ascendant stage in the future, we will certainly lend you a hand!"

Luo Chen said in surprise, "Then I thank the Ancestor!"

Ancestor Hei Ze suddenly spoke up. "Before that, hand over the key to the Dan Sheng Blessed Land."

"Is it that space?" Luo Chen asked.

Ancestor Tian Yuan nodded. "Yes. That space was the source land left behind after Yanfu Mountain ascended into the sky. It itself possesses a fifth-order foundation. Originally, it was given to Chu Yan, both for her cultivation and in the hope that she would slowly nurture it to the fifth order, giving our Nether Origin Sect an additional fifth-order spirit vein. Unfortunately, before she left, she locked the door. We couldn't force it open and sever the connection between the blessed land and the outside world. Now that you have taken back the blessed land, that place will be yours to use in the future, as your cultivation dojo. But the key..."

Before the other could finish, Luo Chen proactively took out the Dan Sheng Token.

The divine sense left on it had long been exhausted.

Whether it was the alchemy technique "Heaven-Refining Secrets" or Chu Yan's alchemy insights, they had all dissipated into nothingness along with those illusions.

Seeing Luo Chen so understanding and cooperative, the three Ancestors said no more.

Moreover, this also demonstrated Luo Chen's attitude.

Handing over the key to his future cultivation dojo so openly—how could he be any more forthright?

The token first fell into Ancestor Yu Ming's hands.

He toyed with it for a moment, then nodded.

Through this token, and by extension Yanfu Mountain, he could naturally sense the existence of the Dan Sheng Blessed Land.

As for the medicinal herbs inside, someone would be sent to inventory them later.

For now, the interrogation of Luo Chen was over.

"You may go back!"

Luo Chen, as if relieved of a great burden, respectfully took his leave.

It was only after leaving the Heavenly Nether Palace that Luo Chen realized his back was soaked with cold sweat.

For someone with his accomplished body refinement, this was a very remarkable thing.

It showed just how great the pressure of facing three great powers was.

He slowly descended the staircase, replaying his earlier responses in his mind, confirming that there were no problems.

His greatest worry—refining pills for the demon race—had been spoken of openly, and the other party hadn't minded at all.

This was the influence brought by his Grandmaster-level alchemy.

Others wouldn't care about what you had done in the past; they would only look at what you could do for them in the future.

If he were still at the Adept stage, with only an ordinary Master-level skill, the Nether Origin Sect wouldn't have cared. They could have taken his life or granted it with a single word.

There were far too many existences like that within a sacred land.

Halfway down, Luo Chen was surprised to see Tan Sheng hurrying upward.

They passed each other. Before either could say anything, Tan Sheng merely nodded slightly and continued upward.

...

Inside the Heavenly Nether Palace.

After Luo Chen's departure, there was a moment of silence.

Ancestor Tian Yuan broke the silence first, asking, "Why didn't you lift the 'Heaven Born Water' restriction for Luo Chen?"

Ancestor Hei Ze retorted, "And why didn't you give him the formula for the Liyuan Pill?"

The Liyuan Pill was the main pill used by the three of them for their cultivation. Since Chu Yan's departure, no one within the Nether Origin Sect had been able to refine it.

Master Xuanyan had succeeded once or twice by chance, but it was only by chance. He couldn't produce it consistently, and it consumed a large amount of precious medicinal herbs.

Hundreds of years had passed, and the Liyuan Pills left by Chu Yan had long been exhausted.

They had originally hoped for Luo Chen, but at their first meeting, they hadn't even mentioned it.

"It concerns our very lives and safety. How could we be so rash?" Ancestor Tian Yuan sighed and looked at their senior brother, the current master of the Nether Abyss, Ancestor Yu Ming. "What do you think, Senior Brother?"

Ancestor Yu Ming took out the token again, his eyes narrowing. "He didn't lie, but he may not have told the whole truth either."

Ancestor Hei Ze snorted lightly. "Although he disguised it well, a dignified Sage acting so pathetic was a performance far too clumsy."

Sometimes, acting too well made it seem less real.

Ancestor Yu Ming nodded. "It doesn't matter. The man is here, the blessed land is open. From now on, he'll be under our noses. Time will reveal his true nature. Junior Brother, I'll give you this key. Don't you have some things inside?"

Ancestor Hei Ze took the token without ceremony. He did indeed have a few medicinal herbs planted inside.

They had been entrusted to Chu Yan for cultivation back then. Now, hundreds of years later, it was about time for them to mature.

Afterward, Ancestor Yu Ming turned his attention to the pool of water before him.

When Luo Chen had released his Withering-Bloom True Flame earlier, this pool had inexplicably become agitated.

Had it sensed a powerful external force and wanted to break its seal?

The other two also withdrew their attention.

"We're only two or three years away from success. We absolutely cannot afford any mistakes during this period."

"I noticed something was wrong earlier and immediately had Luo Chen withdraw that fifth-order divine flame. At this critical juncture, not a single error can be tolerated. Otherwise, not only will all our previous efforts be wasted, but if Qinghe Immortal breaks free, it will also cause us three immense backlash!"

Just as the three were conversing, Tan Sheng requested an audience.

...

Descending the long staircase, Luo Chen finally breathed a sigh of relief.

The rest would be much simpler.

He was, in fact, a very patient person. As long as he could survive and was given a certain amount of dignity, he could overlook many details.

At the very least, the three Ancestors had shown him enough respect in front of others.

They had given him status and bestowed gifts.

Even if they asked him to refine some pills in the future, he wouldn't mind.

For now, it was best to return to the Dan Sheng Hall. It would be more comfortable there.

But his mind still occasionally wandered back to that pool of black water that had been agitated by his presence.

"What exactly is inside it?"

Just as Luo Chen was about to leave Yanfu Mountain, a voice transmission suddenly entered his ear.

"An honored guest has arrived. It concerns you!"
