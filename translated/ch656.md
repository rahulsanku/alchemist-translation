# Chapter 656: Demon-Fall Land, Void Refining True Lord

Listening to Cave Master Moyun's words, Luo Chen recalled the hurried glimpse from earlier. There was indeed no difference.

That monster had stood beside the vortex leading to the spirit medicine garden like a red tower.

Because the black turbid seawater blurred vision, ordinary people truly would not have noticed it was actually a desolate beast.

However!

"Such a famous desolate beast, yet why have I never seen corresponding records in the classics, while Cave Master knows it in such detail?" Luo Chen stared at Cave Master Moyun, his gaze burning.

Cave Master Moyun helplessly shifted his gaze to Ding Yi, who was sitting on the ground panting.

"You will have to ask Fellow Daoist Ding about that. Everything I know came from him."

"Oh?"

Ding Yi looked over irritably. "Why do you think I know? Have you forgotten the soul-nourishing wood you bought back then?"

Soul-nourishing wood? Ghost Immortal Tower!

In an instant, Luo Chen connected the two.

That piece of soul-nourishing wood he had bought for Han Zhan had contained extremely terrifying foul qi. Luo Chen had spent enormous effort, even cultivating a fourth-tier spell to Great Perfection, before barely removing it.

At the time, he had been guessing what the owner of that foul qi was.

Now, when he compared it, even though decades had passed, that familiar feeling surged up like a tide.

That foul qi was clearly the aura emitted from the desolate beast Ghost Immortal Tower.

"Huff..."

Ding Yi caught his breath, and the fat on his body swelled out again as if inflated.

Disgust appeared on his face, but he did not show it too much, as if he were already used to it.

Luo Chen looked a few more times.

Just now, the other party had borrowed the fat on his body to deploy a blood-transformation escape art. Its speed was extremely fast, almost comparable to Luo Chen's great accomplished escape speed in a short time.

Yet strangely, there seemed to be no price.

It truly was astonishing.

But at this moment, what he cared about more was still the terrifying desolate beast, the Ghost Immortal Tower.

Ding Yi did not keep them in suspense and explained it to him.

"This beast is born feeding on the various demons in the sea. It especially likes turtles and Great King Squids. Every time it devours a Great King Squid, it preserves the tentacles and wraps them around itself like hair. I do not know whether this is to show off trophies or if they have some other use."

"Blood Lanterns are very powerful, but they especially like living on the Ghost Immortal Tower, serving as its eyes. We only saw two Blood Lanterns just now, but that was only the tip of the iceberg. On that enormous body several hundred zhang tall, who knows how many low-rank Blood Lanterns also live."

"This beast has slaughtered too many living beings. Over time, countless foul qi gathers around it, and it naturally produces illusions, causing those who see its true body to sink instantly."

"The reason you cannot see records of this desolate beast in the classics is because for thousands of years, Primordial Demon Sect cultivators constantly wanted to subdue it, once forcing it into the depths of the demon sea."

"Even if human cultivators who occasionally ventured deep into the demon sea saw it, they would be affected by the dense foul qi on its body, fall into illusion, and become its food."

"Therefore, over the long years, apart from the Primordial Demon Sect, few in the Northern Sea cultivation world recorded intelligence on the desolate beast Ghost Immortal Tower."

"Only after I was injured by that strand of foul qi on the soul-nourishing wood, while searching everywhere to treat my soul injury, did I finally learn the related intelligence from Primordial Demon Sect remnants."

Luo Chen listened with his mind shaken.

To cultivate the All-Encompassing Fire Prison, he had personally slain a late third-tier Blood Lantern beside the Sunken Sea, so he naturally knew how powerful that demon was.

Yet that sort was merely a parasitic beast on the desolate beast Ghost Immortal Tower?

As for the Great King Squid, that was even more powerful, above demons like the Sea-Overturning Demon Jiao and Golden-Horned Silver Rhinoceros.

Several hundred years ago, one had appeared in the Coral Sea and once slaughtered countless low-rank cultivators of the Coral Sea Zhou clan. In the end, the Zhou clan ancestor had personally acted and slain it.

Even setting aside hundreds of years ago, just three years ago, Luo Chen had seen one in the Sunken Sea.

It had forced Dao Lan to flee into the air above the Sunken Sea, withstanding the pressure of the Void Burning Wind to avoid its attacks.

Such an existence was also food for the Ghost Immortal Tower.

When he heard that even the Ascendant Holy Land Primordial Demon Sect had repeatedly tried and failed to subdue this beast, Luo Chen grew even more awed.

"I wonder what tier this beast is."

Ding Yi answered, "Desolate beast tiers have never been easy to judge. In terms of body size, large ones rampage without restraint, yet tiny ones can also swallow and spit oceans. They also have no concept of demon cores or Nascent Souls. If cultivators want to judge a desolate beast's strength, they can only rely on predecessors' experience and personal testing. As for this Ghost Immortal Tower, comparing its strength to us human cultivators, it is at least at the late Sage stage. In fact, judging from its record of repeatedly escaping from Primordial Demon Sect experts, I fear it is already infinitely close to fifth-tier Ascendant."

As he spoke toward the end, lingering fear clearly appeared in his eyes.

The dread in his gaze toward Luo Chen also grew heavier.

He remembered very clearly that under the Ghost Immortal Tower's foul qi contamination, Luo Chen had obviously been struck by illusion.

But in just an instant, the other party had woken and been the first to flee.

While Luo Chen marveled at the Ghost Immortal Tower's realm, he also sensed Ding Yi's dread.

He did not explain anything.

Such an illusion would make any Golden Core cultivator sink with one glance.

But he was different. He was already proficient in the illusion art Mirror Flower Water Moon, and using Ghost Immortal Tower foul qi as a template, he had deduced Mirror Flower Water Moon's second layer, Ghost Immortal Tower.

It could be said that he already had a certain resistance.

Added to Han Zhan's soul roar at the critical moment, he naturally escaped easily.

After understanding the Ghost Immortal Tower's situation, Luo Chen turned his attention to the nearby environment.

Or rather, from the very beginning after entering this vortex passage, they had been carefully observing the surroundings.

This was a narrow corridor.

Deep, with no bottom visible.

Large amounts of Void Burning Wind gushed from within and then surged outward through the vortex above their heads.

If they wanted to enter the true ruins, they could only go against the corridor.

Luo Chen naturally did not fear it. With the Black Dust Armor, he could ignore the Void Burning Wind.

But Ding Yi and the others were in a bad spot.

The Treasure Basin Fish had been taken, and the backup Origin-Returning Pearl had turned to powder.

This corridor, continuously spewing Void Burning Wind, had become a heavenly moat.

"What do you plan to do next?"

Cave Master Moyun frowned. "I have materials on me and can refine a new Origin-Returning Pearl on the spot, but it will take at least half a month."

"No, that is too long!" Ding Yi decisively refused this suggestion and looked at Luo Chen. "Daoist Azure Yang, that armor of yours previously covered that black jiao. I imagine protecting the two of us should be no problem, right?"

Luo Chen smiled.

Earlier, Ding Yi had directly called him Devil Lord. Now he respectfully called him Daoist Azure Yang again?

"Protecting you is not a problem. But the question is, why should I do that?"

As he spoke, he moved slightly, approaching the corridor entrance.

Once he made this movement, the expressions of the two opposite him immediately changed.

"Devil Lord Azure Yang, what do you mean? Are you trying to use this chance to extort us?"

"Daoist Azure Yang, we are in the same boat, grasshoppers on one rope. Relying on your strength alone, you cannot obtain what you want in those ruins."

Luo Chen disagreed and smiled calmly.

"Correct. We are on one boat. But activating this magic treasure also consumes a great amount of my mana. I remember very clearly that when you boarded the Treasure Basin Fish earlier, you did not think of bringing me."

Ding Yi was somewhat displeased. "Do you think we deliberately targeted you?"

Luo Chen shrugged. "Who knows?"

"You!" Ding Yi was so angry that he extended a finger, his motion pointing at Luo Chen trembling slightly.

Cave Master Moyun steadied himself. "Daoist Azure Yang, your mana is deep. We can all see that. It is only a short stretch of road. Why be so stingy?"

"Having deep mana is not a reason for you to take advantage."

"Then what do you want? Magic treasures? Pills to recover qi..."

"I want none of those."

Luo Chen put away his smile and said seriously, "I want the exact location of the medicine garden where the Five Elements Lotus Platform is, as well as all intelligence related to the ruins!"

Cave Master Moyun suddenly understood and looked at Ding Yi.

Ding Yi looked relieved. "So it was these things. Why make everyone's relationship so tense?"

Luo Chen stared at him. "Then can you speak?"

He knew very well that his three-person team could not be considered harmonious, nor did they have any method to restrain one another.

The situation regarding the Sunken Sea ruins was entirely in Ding Yi's hands.

If he did not understand as much as possible, then even with overwhelming martial force, he would still be restrained by others.

Therefore, even under cooperation, he did this kind of shameless extortion.

Ding Yi thought for a moment and said seriously, "I truly do not know the specific location of the Five Elements Lotus Platform."

Luo Chen frowned. "Impossible. You clearly said before that the vortex led to the ruins' spirit medicine garden."

"So, only that vortex!" Ding Yi vowed. "Only that vortex stably leads to the spirit medicine garden region. But that place is occupied by the Ghost Immortal Tower. If we do not enter from there, I am also at a loss."

"How can this be?" Luo Chen was half-believing and half-doubtful.

Ding Yi looked sincere. "That is the situation. I cannot possibly deceive you."

"Then how can you be certain that vortex passage is correct?"

Luo Chen was determined to get to the bottom of it.

Under his questioning, even though Ding Yi was unwilling, he could only tell the truth.

"Do you remember the matter of Tai Sui I told you about? This flesh on my body was influenced by Tai Sui and cannot be removed. The remnant thoughts on it guide me toward its main body. That vortex is where the remnant thoughts' perception is strongest, which is why I am so certain."

Luo Chen glanced at the fat swelling on the other's body and finally chose to believe.

However, the other question had not yet been answered.

"Intelligence related to the ruins?"

Ding Yi smiled bitterly and asked a question in return.

"What kind of cultivator's ruins could continue to be explored by the Ascendant Holy Land Primordial Demon Sect for a thousand years without being exhausted?"

Cave Master Moyun clearly had not understood much of the inside story before. Hearing this, he and Luo Chen looked at each other.

Indeed.

What kind of existence, after dying, could still block the exploration of a sect with three great Ascendant ancestors and many Sages?

A Sage? An Ascendant ancestor could easily push through.

An Ascendant great power? While alive, perhaps they were evenly matched, but how could methods left behind after death resist the living?

Faintly, a possibility appeared in their hearts.

The wooden plaque on Luo Chen's chest trembled slightly.

An aged voice came beside Luo Chen's ear.

"As expected, just as this old man guessed, these ruins were left by a True Lord."

"True Lord?" Luo Chen blurted out.

Cave Master Moyun also spoke without thinking, "A Void Refining powerhouse?"

Ding Yi slowly nodded. "Correct. According to Primordial Demon Sect records, these ruins are indeed the traveling palace of a Void Refining True Lord. Moreover, that True Lord may have been the strongest Void Refining existence in all history. Because he fell here, this place is also called the Demon-Fall Land!"

*(End of Chapter)*
