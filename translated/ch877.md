# Chapter 877: A Tiger Will Eat Its Own Cub, a Single Thought Makes All the Difference

Whoosh...

The mountain wind gently brushed past, sweeping over Motian Cliff but not entering.

Birds flapped their wings, only to be blocked and fall from the sky.

All of this was because of the sudden appearance of a formation light screen, cutting off everything outside.

This commotion was detected by quite a few Adept cultivators within Luo Tian Sect.

At first, they were surprised, but after discovering the direction from which the formation had been activated and calculating the time slightly, everyone immediately understood.

Carrying an inexplicable excitement and anticipation, figures flew one after another toward Motian Cliff.

Even if they couldn't enter, as long as they could catch a glimpse of the "young master" after his birth, that would be wonderful.

Gu Caiyi, Li Yingzhang, Min Longyu, Zeng Yilong, Qin Yuanjiang, Yang Pingdu, Meng Qin'er, and even the Heartless Fairy had come, bringing Shi Lan with her.

In the shadows, a dark figure flashed past.

Bai Meiling landed on a mountain peak, gazing into the distance at the towering Motian Cliff that pierced the clouds, her eyes filled with anticipation.

"The master will have offspring!"

...

On Motian Cliff, within the palace halfway up the mountain.

Several figures were busily moving back and forth.

At the head was an old woman with graying hair. Her cultivation was merely at the late Initiate stage, but in terms of age, she had long surpassed the Initiate lifespan limit—she was already two hundred and seventy years old!

Her name was Su'e, and she was the senior disciple of Sun Sifang, the medical sage who had joined Luo Tian Sect back in the day.

Although she couldn't break through to the Adept stage due to insufficient talent, she was quite proficient in the art of nourishing life and had stubbornly lived to the age of two hundred and seventy.

Two years ago, feeling that her end was near, she had already begun making arrangements for her final affairs.

But upon hearing that Luo Tian Sect was about to wage a war of expansion to carve out a foundation for the sect, she gritted her teeth and used her medical arts to stimulate her vitality, holding on until Luo Tian Sect established its dominion over the great region.

At that point, even if her lifespan ended and she sat in meditation to pass away, she could smile with contentment in the underworld.

Unexpectedly, she then heard that the founding sect leader, Sima Huiniang, was pregnant!

This was the child of the Supreme Elder!

If she could personally deliver the baby before her own passing, then her life would have come full circle, beginning and ending well.

For this reason, Su'e sealed her meridians and locked her channels, dispersing all the spiritual power in her dantian to nourish her aging flesh, enduring pain day after day, persisting until today.

"Tormenting myself like this, unwilling to let go—I'm afraid I'll end up in hell after I die," Su'e thought to herself, a hint of self-mockery in her heart.

Most cultivators, upon sensing their lifespan was nearly exhausted and their end was near, would often accept reality.

For someone like her, who tortured herself through various means just to cling to life, it was a very cruel thing—not only for herself but also for the friends and family around her.

But when Su'e saw Sima Huiniang's pained cries, she felt it was all worth it.

"How could childbirth be this difficult for a female cultivator?"

"If an ordinary person were to deliver the baby, even if successful, the mother would suffer severe damage to her origin qi, and her cultivation realm would drop by more than half."

"Thankfully, I am still here!"

Su'e could not bear to watch Sima Huiniang, who had shown her kindness, suffer like this, nor could she allow the future hope of the sect to die in the womb.

Silver needles rose and fell, connecting the major meridians.

Spiritual power flowed, consolidating the qi sea.

Supplemented by finger techniques, she stimulated the major acupoints all over Sima Huiniang's body.

At the same time, she kept soothing the woman with her words, telling her to relax.

Time passed bit by bit, from midnight to early morning.

The originally confident Su'e gradually began to feel a sense of panic rising in her heart.

Why was this little one so difficult to bring out?

With her medical skills, could she not even help Sima Huiniang have a smooth delivery?

Watching the woman, whose face had turned pale and whose pupils were dilating, Su'e took a deep breath. "Natural delivery is too difficult. Let's do a C-section!"

The disciples beside her had already sensed this possibility and began preparing the corresponding tools.

Just then, a figure appeared beside her.

"No C-section. It must be a natural delivery!"

Su'e was stunned for a moment, then looked up at the tall, young man.

"But—"

Luo Chen waved his hand behind him without turning his head. "All of you, leave!"

The low-level disciples from Su'e's lineage bowed to Luo Chen and respectfully withdrew.

Watching this scene, Su'e was somewhat puzzled.

Then she saw the Supreme Elder slowly extend his hand, stroking Sima Huiniang's belly, and softly calling out.

"Huiniang... Huiniang..."

Gradually, Sima Huiniang, whose pupils had been dilating, seemed to wake up. Light returned to her eyes, and strength returned to her body.

Su'e instinctively covered her mouth.

If it were only that, Su'e wouldn't have lost her composure so much.

What shocked her was that where Luo Chen's palm was pressed, patterns were spreading.

Lines of blood, like earthworms, converged from all over Sima Huiniang's body, flowing toward the concentrated area of her lower abdomen.

The blood flower was about to bloom; the fruit was about to fall.

At that moment, Luo Chen turned his head and coldly looked at Su'e.

"What are you waiting for?"

Su'e's entire body trembled, and she lowered her head to begin busying herself.

Strangely enough, the delivery process, which had made no progress before, became much smoother after Luo Chen intervened.

She saw the feet!

"No wonder it was so difficult before—the feet came out first!" Su'e suddenly understood.

Then came the small body, covered in slippery fluids.

Throughout this process, Sima Huiniang's cries grew louder and louder, as if they could pierce the sky.

"It hurts!!!"

When Sima Huiniang suddenly let out a loud shout, everything fell silent.

Su'e cut the umbilical cord, washed the baby with warm water, and with trembling hands, delivered the child to Luo Chen.

"Con... congratulations, Supreme Elder. It's a... boy."

A white swaddling cloth, suffused with a red glow, dazzling and mesmerizing.

Such an omen would involuntarily make one think of some great reincarnation or sacred legend.

But that red light was so sinister, as if it could erode a person's mind.

In the swaddling cloth, the infant's eyelids were tightly shut, his mouth tightly closed, silent like a stillborn.

Luo Chen took the swaddling cloth. His first glance was not at the child, but at the old woman.

"Su'e, how old are you this year?"

Su'e hunched her back. "Disciple is two hundred and seventy-one years old this year."

With a single glance, Luo Chen saw through the pain she was enduring. He nodded. "You've had a hard time. Go now, and I hope that in your next life, you can still be a disciple of my Luo Tian Sect."

Luo Chen pointed out a finger, and a wisp of green light entered Su'e's body.

The physical pain that had tormented her for two years instantly dissipated upon the green light entering her body.

Su'e felt her entire body relax, as if she had returned to her maiden days, full of vitality.

If she were to pass away in this state, perhaps her journey on the Yellow Springs Road would be much smoother?

Su'e thought so.

In her ears came Luo Chen's calm words.

"Today's matter must not be spoken of to anyone."

Su'e prostrated herself, performing a grand kneeling bow to Luo Chen.

Then she rose and walked out of the hall.

When a female disciple asked if everything had gone smoothly, she smiled without answering.

When Gu Caiyi, Li Yingzhang, and other Adept cultivators waiting outside eagerly asked whether it was a boy or a girl, she only said that the specific details would be announced later by the Supreme Elder.

Then, accompanied by her disciples, she returned to her cave dwelling.

Thinking of the young master's round face and the ominous red glow he was born with, she sighed.

The final affairs that needed to be arranged had already been taken care of two years ago.

As for now?

Su'e sat cross-legged, her aged hands forming a hand seal, quietly waiting for death to arrive.

The formation on Motian Cliff had still not been lifted.

As they waited, the excited and expectant feelings of Gu Caiyi and the others began to change, and many bad guesses surged into their hearts.

Could something have gone wrong?

This delivery had already lasted from midnight until daytime.

For powerful Adept cultivators, this was far too inconceivable.

Back in the day, Yao Mingyue's parents were only Qi Refinement cultivators, yet in the midst of a sudden battle at Xieyue Valley, they had given birth to Yao Mingyue in just a few breaths.

Sima Huiniang's cultivation was not low—why was it taking so long?

Coupled with Su'e's evasive words, they couldn't help but let their imaginations run wild.

But the great formation blocked them, and even Min Longyu was helpless.

It was only at this moment that Min Longyu suddenly realized that the Supreme Elder's formation mastery had already reached such a profound level that even he could not catch up.

Inside the bedchamber.

Luo Chen quietly looked at the little person in the swaddling cloth, his expression neither sad nor happy.

Unlike most newborns, who are wrinkled and ugly, the one in his arms was like a freshly steamed bun—white, smooth, with a round little face.

It seemed well-nourished, fully developed in the womb.

Under his gaze, the sinister red light seemed very afraid of Luo Chen, continuously retracting and sinking into the little person's body.

"Afraid of me?"

"That's right. You not only came out of Huiniang's body, but also from mine."

"Now that the host is so weak, you really should be afraid of me."

Luo Chen slowly raised his right hand, reaching toward the child's face, his expression as cold as steel.

This child was indeed his.

But from the very beginning, he had never intended to let him live.

He was merely a transitional vessel, allowing Sima Huiniang to shed the burden of the Devil Heart Gu.

And even if he lived, what then?

The Devil Heart Gu was completely bound to him. He would not be able to cultivate in the future, and would even become extremely weak as the Devil Heart Gu devoured his blood and qi.

Even with a mortal body, it would be difficult to live past a hundred years.

Rather than a life of suffering, it would be better to end it early.

As for killing his own son—with a will as firm as Luo Chen's, he could do it!

Just as Luo Chen's hand touched the baby's smooth skin, a weak voice came from beside him.

"Husband, our child..."

"Waa!"

A loud cry suddenly rang through the empty bedchamber.

The originally cold bedchamber, because of that cry, gained a bit of warmth.

Luo Chen's hand stopped.

His steel-like face showed a flicker of emotion at that moment, and a trace of hesitation appeared in his eyes.

"Husband?"

Sima Huiniang, as if suffering from a serious illness, struggled to lift her neck and looked toward Luo Chen.

In her line of sight, she only saw Luo Chen stroking the baby in the swaddling cloth.

Because of that cry, she became somewhat anxious.

"The child! The child!"

Luo Chen hesitated.

In more than three hundred years of cultivation, some said he had changed, some said he hadn't.

He knew that his external style of conduct had become increasingly decisive and ruthless, his mind calculating and cunning as a fox, but toward those close to him, he remained as he always had.

Even toward old acquaintances, he could extend kindness—how much more so toward his own child?

He looked down again. The little one had somehow opened his eyes, black, shiny eyes that seemed unreal, staring at him, still crying loudly.

"Husband, I want to see our child!"

The woman's voice by his ear grew more and more anxious.

Luo Chen pursed his lips, gritted his teeth, his jaw muscles bulging hard.

"Alas..."

An inexplicable sigh, as if a burden had been lifted.

Luo Chen finally placed the baby on the bed, lifted Sima Huiniang's soft, powerless arm, and tucked the child into her embrace.

At that moment, Sima Huiniang's brows curved, and she revealed a smile that came from the bottom of her heart.

"It's a son!"

Luo Chen nodded. "Yes, it's a son."

He, Luo Chen, had lived two lives and finally had a son of his own.

A feeling of blood connection finally crossed the inexplicable obsession he had held earlier and reached his heart.

He was nothing but an ignorant infant!

Even if he carried the demon gu in his body, even if he couldn't cultivate, even if he only had a hundred years to live—he was a Sage, an Alchemy Saint!

With his protection, even a mortal could live a brilliant life.

How could he, because of future suffering, erase all his possibilities before he had even opened his eyes?

Luo Chen touched his face. Unbeknownst to him, fine cold sweat had broken out.

A single thought's difference—was this a heart demon delusion?

A sense of fear arose in Luo Chen's heart.

He dared not think about what would have happened if he had truly struck the fatal blow earlier.

Would it be Sima Huiniang's lifelong hatred of him? Or the chaotic power struggles in Luo Tian Sect's future? Or the guilt that would haunt him in his midnight dreams?

If, one day, when he broke through to the Ascendant realm, the child's vengeful spirit came to claim his life, would that become the final straw that broke him?

In the end, he had awakened.

At this moment, Luo Chen felt that his thoughts had become much smoother and more active.

Sima Huiniang happily played with the child in her arms. She looked at the pensive Luo Chen.

"Husband, earlier I asked you what to name the child, and you said you didn't know the gender so you couldn't choose. Now you know—do you have any ideas?"

Luo Chen smiled bitterly. He had been trapped in a demonic delusion earlier and hadn't even thought about this.

Thinking of a name for a dead child—wasn't that just asking for trouble?

But now that he had awakened, he was somewhat embarrassed.

Facing Sima Huiniang's expectant gaze, inspiration struck, and the words tumbled out.

"Let's call him Lingxi!"

Sima Huiniang was startled, then pursed her lips and smiled.

What a good name!

Back then, wasn't it because they had planted the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu that she had been able to hold on until Luo Chen's return?

Luo Chen looked at the mother and son with pity, sighing inwardly.

He only hoped that this child would have a heart that understood, and would not be born with demonic delusions!

"Rest for a while. I'll go inform the others and arrange for disciples to take care of you."

"Mm, go ahead, husband!" Sima Huiniang responded sweetly, then lowered her head to look at the baby who had stopped crying. "Lingxi, Lingxi, from now on, you're called Luo Lingxi!"

Luo Chen did not linger and hurriedly left.

As if he dared not face the mother and son.

Arriving outside the hall, with a single wave of his hand, the great formation that had covered the sky and sun dissipated in an instant.

Figures, as if receiving a signal, flew in one after another.

Facing a multitude of expectant eyes, Luo Chen smiled slightly.

"Mother and child are both safe."

In an instant, cheers erupted from the mouths of these Adept cultivators who had weathered countless storms.

Luo Chen shook his head with a smile and was about to arrange for people to take care of the mother and son.

But just as he was about to move, his brow furrowed.

He took out a jade slip from his bosom. Spiritual light flickered on it incessantly, as if extremely urgent.
