# Chapter 878: Fifth-Order Divine Flower, a World Blooms from One Flower (Double Monthly Tickets Requested!)

"Nine Spirits is in danger, come quickly!"

*Crack!*

Luo Chen crushed the specially-made sound transmission jade pendant in one grip, his eyes narrowing instantly.

He did not doubt the authenticity of this transmission.

Only because this type of sound transmission jade pendant was his unique magical artifact, usually only given to those he trusted.

The last one had been given to Splendid Cloud Fairy.

This time, Lingfengzi and Wang Yuan had gone to battle, and he had given one to each of them as well.

The one who went to help Nine Spirits Primordial Lord was Lingfengzi, and this transmission was sent by him.

What shocked Luo Chen was, what kind of enemy could be so formidable that even someone as powerful as Nine Spirits Primordial Lord had fallen into a dangerous situation?

"Could it be the Bull Demon?"

The difficulty of opening up the Medicine King Domain wasn't actually that great for the Nine Spirits Sect.

Initially, only three demon emperors had remained behind to guard it.

But during the year-long process of opening up, those three demon emperors, sensing they couldn't withstand Nine Spirits Primordial Lord, had invited two more demon emperors.

These two were precisely the strong ones under the command of the Bull Demon Great Demon Emperor.

Now, they had beaten the small ones, and the big ones had come?

To rescue or not?

Luo Chen only pondered for a moment before making his decision.

Rescue!

This was his promise to Nine Spirits Primordial Lord and Elder Fu; he could not break his oath and go back on his word.

Moreover, when the Luo Tian Sect was being established, Nine Spirits Primordial Lord had also lent a hand, helping him defeat the powerful enemy, Daoist Peng Xiang.

However, an existence that could force even Nine Spirits Primordial Lord into a bitter struggle, even if it wasn't the Bull Demon Great Demon Emperor, was definitely no easy opponent.

Therefore, he had to make proper preparations before setting out.

With a thought, two streaks of dim light drifted down from above Motian Cliff.

The two divided souls returned to their positions, the three souls merging into one!

He stomped his foot, and an invisible wave transmitted deep into the earth, reaching the slumbering Black King.

Black King slowly opened his eyes and, without hesitation, swam towards Luo Chen.

During the waiting interval, Luo Chen reached out with his hand.

A small cauldron spun out from the belly of Motian Cliff.

It was his lifebound magic treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron!

Earlier, he had used this very item to set up a grand formation, blocking external detection, leaving even Min Longyu helpless against the mountain-protecting formation.

Finally, Luo Chen used divine sense to summon two people.

The first to arrive was Bai Meiling, who had already returned. However, because of her Ghost Emperor body, her yin qi was too strong, so she hadn't dared to enter the mountain to visit the weakened Sima Huiniang and her child. Now, she came at the first call.

"Master, what are your orders?"

"I am going out for a while. You will guard Motian Cliff for me, and guard Huiniang and her child well."

Bai Meiling was startled, but she accepted the task without any question. Back then, her master had brought her out of the Dahe Ghost Domain. She had never been able to repay her master over the years. Now, guarding the young master was only her duty!

Li Yingzhang arrived shortly after, a genuine smile still on his face, seemingly because he had seen Little Lingxi.

In recent days, because Luo Chen was about to leave, the issue of power succession in the Luo Tian Sect had brought him considerable suspicion.

But he had never harbored such thoughts; it was a completely unwarranted calamity!

He didn't have many thoughts about Luo Chen's summons.

But upon hearing that Luo Chen was going out, he couldn't help showing a puzzled expression.

"The young master has just been born, and you, Supreme Elder, are leaving now?"

Luo Chen said calmly, "I promised someone something; I have to go. I'll trouble you to manage the sect's affairs."

Li Yingzhang nodded repeatedly, "This is my duty."

Luo Chen looked back at the sleeping palace behind him, "As for Huiniang..."

Before Luo Chen could say more, Li Yingzhang unhesitatingly declared, "Rest assured, everything will prioritize the young master and his mother. I will arrange for disciples to take care of them. The physicians from Su'e's lineage will also be in attendance."

With that, Luo Chen felt at ease.

However, after thinking for a moment, he still instructed, "If any petty villains come to cause trouble, you can go and invite the nearby Lin Yonghua and Sage Gu Hong. You can also send someone to invite those from Lingtian Pass. They... will give me this face."

Li Yingzhang noted these instructions, but felt somewhat puzzled.

Given the Luo Tian Sect's current status, who would dare to come and cause trouble?

Luo Chen also knew this reasoning; he was just being cautious just in case.

The earth beneath his feet trembled, and a black shadow broke through the soil, appearing at Luo Chen's feet.

Luo Chen extended his hand, and the black shadow climbed up along it, disappearing into Luo Chen's left arm.

Before leaving, Luo Chen took one last look back at that sleeping palace, feeling as if a tether had been formed in his heart.

The dust that had once drifted in all directions, because of that tiny little one, had finally settled, and had even grown roots.

The memories of his past life were becoming increasingly blurred.

The experiences of this life, however, were growing heavier.

*Hoo...*

Luo Chen opened his mouth and exhaled. Amidst Bai Meiling's terrified soul, a small flame, green and white, slowly flew out and landed deep within Motian Cliff.

"Let's go!"

Luo Chen murmured softly. Under the respectful farewells of Bai Meiling and Li Yingzhang, he shot into the sky, transforming into a streak of red light that vanished into the distance.

...

Amidst the vast clouds.

Luo Chen's figure suddenly stopped, and a shuttle appeared in his hand.

It was the Frost Eagle Flying Shuttle!

He infused it with his magic power, and the shuttle enlarged to about one *zhang* in size.

Luo Chen stepped inside and released Black King.

"You control it!"

Black King didn't question it. This kind of thing was common when they had traveled in the Northern Sea before. Either Tianxuan piloted the flying boat, or he did.

Times like this usually meant Luo Chen had his own matters to attend to.

Seeing that Black King had taken over, Luo Chen finally had a moment to spare.

Sitting cross-legged in the flying shuttle, Luo Chen recalled the events at Motian Cliff.

After the initial confusion, he was now certain that his inhuman, even cold-blooded thought of "killing his son" had not come from his heart.

It was more like a sudden, fleeting notion, which, given the seemingly reasonable external conditions, had gradually developed into a level of inner demon and obsession.

If he had truly done it back then, this matter would definitely become the greatest flaw in his heart on his future path of cultivation!

"So, what gave rise to this inner demon?"

Luo Chen fell into deep thought.

...

This journey to the Medicine King Domain spanned several regions, a long and distant road.

Fortunately, the Frost Eagle Flying Shuttle was a rare flying true artifact, and with Black King driving it with all his might, its speed was very fast. The estimated arrival time was about three days.

In three days, could Nine Spirits Primordial Lord hold on?

Luo Chen had no time to think about that. He could only take things one step at a time regarding the situation over there.

As for himself, he was gradually figuring out the general reason for the sudden appearance of the inner demon.

"The Withering-Bloom Dao Finger!"

A day later, Luo Chen opened his eyes.

A lingering trace of fear still remained on his face.

Throughout his life, he had always been susceptible to inner demon invasions, even falling into obsession.

This had happened during the Qi Refinement and Initiate stages.

However, this situation had basically stopped occurring after he condensed his divine sense and stepped into the Adept stage.

Especially after entering the Sage realm, the thick Purple Mansion barrier protected his Sage Embryo, and his soul had become increasingly powerful. Ordinary external demons found it hard to taint him.

But an accident had occurred!

Relying on the transformation of his Withering-Bloom Divine Fire, Luo Chen had comprehended the surface-level essence of the Withering-Bloom True Intent, thereby creating the great divine ability, the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger.

At first, when he used this technique, he mostly relied on his own magic power.

But in his battle with the Seven Rhinoceros Great Demon Emperor, he had comprehended the trick of communicating with the origin qi of heaven and earth, allowing him to achieve the means of an Ascendant-level powerhouse, drawing upon the origin qi of heaven and earth for his own use.

In his one-move battle with Divine Origin Sage, he had used this divine ability in this way, condensing a powerful finger amidst the chaotic spiritual energy of Taihu Domain, thus fighting Divine Origin Sage to a standstill.

However, this move was not perfect.

When performing this technique, the Purple Mansion barrier opened wide, and the Sage Embryo used the Withering-Bloom True Intent to pry open the origin qi of heaven and earth.

During this process, the Sage Embryo, without the protection of the Purple Mansion barrier, was too closely connected to heaven and earth.

Although the time was short, and it wouldn't be assimilated by heaven and earth, there were still consequences.

That inner demon thought of "killing his son" had arisen under these circumstances, without any abrupt transition.

"The Dao of Heaven and Earth conforms to nature."

"In my previous actions, I found a solution, using the bloodline descendant of Huiniang and myself to resolve the calamity of the Devil Heart Gu. This was also a natural and logical course of action."

"However, in this process, I completely failed to consider familial affection—that unique emotion born from the instinct of living beings to reproduce."

"It wasn't until Lingxi's cry that I suddenly woke up!"

Luo Chen let out a faint sigh, lowering his head to silently gaze at his own finger.

This Withering-Bloom Dao Finger would probably have to be used more sparingly in the future.

Otherwise, at his current Sage realm, if he recklessly drew upon the origin qi of heaven and earth, even if he could hold fast to himself and not be assimilated by heaven and earth, other calamities would inevitably follow one after another.

Thinking of this, Luo Chen felt another doubt arise.

"Then why doesn't Divine Origin suffer from such effects?"

Divine Origin Sage could control the origin qi of heaven and earth!

This was by no means an unfounded claim.

Back during the Ascendant battle at Biyou Mountain, the many Sages had scattered in all directions.

In that process, the various Sage powerhouses had each displayed their abilities, some using magic treasures and true artifacts, some employing powerful escape techniques. Only Divine Origin Sage had calmly withdrawn from that battlefield right under the noses of Luo Chen and Tainduzi.

Furthermore, he hadn't been affected by the chaotic origin qi or the loss of direction.

In the Taihu Domain battle, Luo Chen had relied on the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger to draw upon the origin qi of heaven and earth, while the cyan circle spell that Divine Origin Sage had cast had clearly also drawn upon the origin qi of heaven and earth.

Thinking back to when Divine Origin Sage first appeared at Daqing Mountain in Baili Qingchuan...

Luo Chen became even more certain of his guess.

Suddenly!

Luo Chen shook his head, "No, perhaps he has already been greatly affected!"

According to worldly rumors, after the battle at Green Parasol Mountain, Divine Origin Sage's temperament had changed drastically.

But a defeat in battle would at most make one feel discouraged; how could it lead to a complete change in temperament?

The Azure Frost Demon Emperor had no personal or emotional conflict with Divine Origin Sage.

Furthermore, the three late-stage Sages deciding to join forces against the Azure Frost Demon Emperor also indicated that Divine Origin, Joyous Union, and the Monk Huiping had known from the start that they might not be Azure Frost's match. This also showed that there was no huge gap caused by a desire to win or lose.

The only reason for Divine Origin Sage's drastic change in temperament should be this matter of contacting the origin qi of heaven and earth.

"In a normal fight, I am no match for him. But if he truly is also suffering from the erosion of the origin qi of heaven and earth, perhaps I can change my approach to deal with him?"

Luo Chen thought this way.

But the thought flashed by, and he couldn't help but chuckle.

The other party had already reconciled with him; why was he still subconsciously thinking about how to deal with others?

Luo Chen calmly stood up and took over control of the Frost Eagle Flying Shuttle from Black King.

After all, the other was a desolate beast, not like him who possessed great magic power; he could make the flying shuttle go faster.

Under Luo Chen's control, the flying shuttle lowered its altitude and dove beneath the clouds.

The scene below also swept past his eyes at great speed, like a film playing in reverse.

There were demon beasts migrating, and cultivators flying through the sky and diving into the earth.

On a battlefield after some great battle, Luo Chen even saw someone holding a black banner, walking through it furtively.

Seeing this, Luo Chen took a few more glances, and an understanding dawned on him.

They were collecting the souls of living beings!

And it wasn't the conventional way of collecting souls to refine soul-path magic treasures.

The unique technique was clearly the method for refining soul essence that Luo Chen had secretly leaked back in Lingtian Pass!

Thinking of the small amounts of soul essence that had occasionally appeared on the black market in Lingtian Pass over the years, the corners of Luo Chen's mouth lifted slightly.

The seeds he had personally sown back then had now bloomed into flowers through the hands of others.

In the future, he could harvest the fruits and obtain a large amount of soul essence.

"After this matter is concluded, I should also consider splitting a third divided soul to continue strengthening my soul foundation."

Luo Chen thought this as the time to reach the Medicine King Domain drew nearer and nearer.

One day, two days.

When the third day arrived, the Frost Eagle Flying Shuttle stopped atop a broken mountain peak.

Luo Chen flicked his sleeve, put away the Frost Eagle Flying Shuttle, and waited quietly, his faintly golden eyes fixed firmly on the distant place.

After a moment, a fierce wind swept past.

*Whoosh!*

The wind stopped, and a person appeared!

Lingfengzi said with a face full of pleasant surprise, "Senior Brother, you came so quickly!"

Luo Chen withdrew his gaze and landed it on him.

His aura was a bit low, but there were no obvious injuries on him. As expected, his cheap junior brother's life-preserving abilities were top-notch!

He chuckled lightly, "Is coming quickly not a good thing?"

Lingfengzi quickly shook his head, "It's naturally excellent. If you hadn't come, Nine Spirits Primordial Lord might have been refined alive."

The words "refined alive" were terrifying to say.

In fact, in Luo Chen's eyes, that was indeed the case.

Three thousand *li* away, the light was hazy, the sky illusory. A grand formation, like an inverted porcelain bowl, covered the universe, making it hard for living beings to approach.

And within it, a nine-colored great river was rolling and flowing, resisting the terrifying refinement of that grand formation.

With just one glance, Luo Chen could tell that the person inside was Nine Spirits Primordial Lord.

And the one presiding over the formation...

"Give me the details, briefly."

"Mm!"

Lingfengzi nodded heavily and spoke quickly, "Within the Medicine King Domain, there is a hidden, opened-up space, a forbidden area left behind before the Medicine King Sect was destroyed. It nurtures a fourth-order miraculous flower, only one step away from the fifth order. The three demon emperors lingered there, unwilling to leave, precisely to guard this flower until it matured."

"When we arrived, sensing they were no match, they deliberately leaked the information to the Bull Demon Commander lineage."

"Half a year ago, two fourth-order mid-stage powerhouses under the Bull Demon came to reinforce, but they still couldn't withstand Nine Spirits Primordial Lord, who forcefully broke into the forbidden space of the Medicine King Sect."

"A few days ago, that small forbidden space couldn't withstand the power of the Sage battle and began to slowly shatter. Nine Spirits Primordial Lord, having seized the miraculous flower, fought his way out."

"But unexpectedly, the Bull Demon Great Demon Emperor had already arrived in secret, joining forces with the four surviving demon emperors to set up a grand formation, intending to forcibly refine Nine Spirits Primordial Lord!"

This account wasn't long, but the information contained within was considerable.

Luo Chen immediately seized upon the key phrase.

"A fourth-order miraculous flower?"

"Exactly. I don't know the specifics, but I heard one of the demon emperors call its name: the Accumulated Void Flower!"

Luo Chen's eyes suddenly lit up.

The Accumulated Void Flower was a famous fifth-order divine flower in the Mountains and Seas Realm from ancient times.

Legend had it that this flower could only grow in a chaotic and disordered environment, feeding on the origin qi within the void turbulence.

Once it grew to the fifth order, it would have two uses.

One was "a world blooms from one flower." With this flower, one could support a small world. It was the most coveted treasure for top-tier sects seeking to pass down their legacy for ten thousand years.

The other was also "a world blooms from one flower," but its meaning differed from the literal one. It meant that a cultivator, after consuming it, could open a second Purple Mansion within their body, and even nurture a second Sage Embryo from nothing.

This Sage Embryo, born from the Accumulated Void Purple Mansion, would not affect the growth of the natal Sage Embryo. Moreover, during cultivation, it would not be eroded by the origin qi of heaven and earth, and there was no worry about the second Sage Embryo being assimilated by heaven and earth.

"No wonder the Medicine King Sect kept this flower in a secret space, and an unstable, broken small space at that. Once it matured, the Medicine King Sect would have a truly eternal foundation."

"No wonder Nine Spirits Primordial Lord took such a great risk to forcibly seize this flower. With his abundant magic power, if he obtained this flower and opened a second Purple Mansion, he would be able to generate a second Sage Embryo extremely quickly. In the future, when he attempts to break through to the Ascendant realm, his chances of success would increase by at least twenty to thirty percent."

"No wonder the Bull Demon Great Demon Emperor came back, braving the pressure of the human counterattack, to seize this flower..."

Three consecutive "no wonders" showed Luo Chen's astonishment at this flower.

Even ordinary herbs and grasses, upon advancing to the fifth order, would possess miraculous and unpredictable uses.

Let alone a heavenly material and earthly treasure as magical as the Accumulated Void Flower!

Listening to Luo Chen's muttering, Lingfengzi asked cautiously, "Senior Brother, have you taken a liking to it?"

Luo Chen laughed heartily, "It would be a lie to say I'm not tempted. But how can a gentleman take what another desires? This was seized by Fellow Daoist Nine Spirits; it naturally belongs to him. My coming today is merely to keep my promise and fulfill our agreement."

Hearing this, Lingfengzi couldn't help but regard Luo Chen even more highly.

A heavy treasure was right before him, yet he was not swayed by it, still remembering his promise to a friend.

Truly worthy of the name Alchemy Saint!

"So, what should we do now? That Bull Demon Commander, together with the four demon emperors, has set up a formation that is not easy to break. Fellow Daoists Qin Baixiang, Xianhe, and I are all at our wit's end."

Luo Chen smiled confidently, walking forward slowly, while his hands began to form different hand seals.

"The demon race is nothing but fur-clad, horned, wet-born, egg-laying creatures."

"How dare they prattle on about formations containing the principles of heaven and earth?"

"Watch me break it with sheer force!"
