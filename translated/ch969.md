# Chapter 969: Ocean of Origin Qi, Divine Lord Youli

One hand scooping the moon, heaven and earth overturned.

The entire Yin-Yang Realm seemed to have been turned upside down.

Countless living beings and dead things on the earth rushed toward that bright moon, like fireflies chasing the moon.

The immense suction force attached itself to Luo Chen's body, causing him to fly involuntarily toward the moon.

Luo Chen erupted with both his dharma power and source power simultaneously, trying to stabilize his body, but the effect was minimal.

His dharma power was almost completely exhausted, aside from the basic dharma power that constituted his Nascent Soul.

The consumption of source power did not appear obvious, but in reality, it was no less than the consumption of dharma power.

Whether it was pushing open that Death Gate or engaging in close combat with Li Tianshi, both had required source power and had consumed a tremendous amount.

Especially the act of being pierced through half his body by the Netherworld Earth Dragon and then instantly recovering—that had cost him an incalculable amount of source power.

Flesh and blood regeneration was not omnipotent; it required time.

Accelerating regeneration came at the cost of exhausting source power.

Therefore, even though Luo Chen was doing his utmost at this moment, he still found it difficult to control his trajectory as he flew toward that high-hanging bright moon.

Luo Chen's expression shifted constantly, his thoughts racing.

"Senior Brother?"

"Why did that moon-scooping person call the Netherworld Earth Dragon 'Senior Brother'? Could it be that the consciousness of the great power Yin Yangzi, who had passed away in meditation, still remains inside?"

"The great power Yin Yangzi was a major figure from over two thousand years ago. The lifespan limit of an ordinary Ascendant is only two thousand years. Therefore, the moon-scooping person cannot be someone who existed after Yin Yangzi. Then who could it be, spanning such a long period of two thousand years?"

A forbidden existence automatically surfaced in Luo Chen's mind.

He hesitated and looked downward.

That enormous Netherworld Earth Dragon was still struggling, its four feet nailed firmly to the earth like spikes, unmoving.

It seemed very unwilling to be scooped into that moon treasure.

Luo Chen did not know whether it could resist successfully, but he knew that if he stayed, he would surely die.

Since that was the case...

Luo Chen pursed his lips, released his source power and dharma power, and no longer resisted the immense suction force. His entire person floated ethereally toward the moon, as if ascending to immortality.

On the earth below, the Netherworld Earth Dragon was still struggling, still putting up a stubborn resistance.

However, the suction force emitted by the moon treasure grew even more terrifying. Even the thick earth itself was flying toward the moon, layer by layer, piece by piece.

How much more so for the Netherworld Earth Dragon, which used the earth as its foundation?

A streak of light slowly appeared on the back of the Netherworld Earth Dragon.

Its face was ferocious, wings grew from its back, and its entire body was cyan-black.

It was the Flying Heavenly Nightwalker that had disappeared earlier!

However, unlike the cunning gaze of the Flying Heavenly Nightwalker before, this flying corpse's eyes now clearly held a trace of clarity.

He knelt on one knee on the back of the Netherworld Earth Dragon and emitted a strange and contradictory voice.

"Since you still call me Senior Brother, why must Junior Brother be so ruthless and drive me to extinction?"

The voice was rough and bold, yet carried a sharp and piercing tone, as if a man and woman were resonating together.

Under this voice, the wind and waves seemed to pause for a moment.

From within the bright moon, the voice came again, but this time it carried a hint of regretful emotional fluctuation.

"Calling you Senior Brother is merely respecting the grace of teaching the art back then. A mere evil thought, and you still vainly hope to cling to life in this world?"

As these words were spoken, the Flying Heavenly Nightwalker's expression changed drastically, and he instinctively tried to dive back into the Netherworld Earth Dragon's body.

However, a ray of moonlight slowly descended, enveloping him.

Within the moonlight, green smoke permeated.

No sound was transmitted, and the Flying Heavenly Nightwalker inexplicably vanished from between heaven and earth.

High in the sky, Luo Chen watched this scene, his pupils contracting.

The Flying Heavenly Nightwalker was by no means weak. Even if Luo Chen was not afraid of him, dealing with him would require considerable effort.

Especially, from the exchange of questions and answers between the two, it seemed that an evil thought of a great power was residing on this flying corpse.

Its capability could no longer be measured by an ordinary late-stage Sage.

From the fact that it had settled the wind and waves with a single word, one could see the evidence.

It was likely infinitely close to the Ascendant level.

Under such circumstances, facing this moonlight radiance, it had no power to resist at all.

Then what level of strength did this moon-scooping person possess?

Luo Chen found it difficult to speculate.

His departing gaze fell upon that Netherworld Earth Dragon.

What about it?

Within his field of vision, he suddenly saw a thick yellow light surge into the sky, shaking the bright moon.

The Netherworld Earth Dragon had self-destructed!

But what self-destructed was only its thousand-zhang physical body.

From the corner of his eye, Luo Chen saw a tiny figure, borrowing the power of the self-destructing body, cross the Death Gate and escape from the Netherworld.

"Was that the Netherworld Earth Dragon I saw at the very beginning?"

With this thought, Luo Chen's consciousness paused, and his entire person fell into a daze.

...

"Kill!"

"Kill, kill, kill!"

"Destroy the Holy Land, sever the shackles, kill!"

The shouts of killing were deafening, the smell of blood filled the nose and lungs.

Figures covered the mountains and fields, escape lights rose and fell, thousands of flying treasures danced—it was shocking to the mind and spirit.

War was the only melody at this moment!

The allied forces of the Fierce Peak Stiff Alliance and the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, after a hundred years of preparation, had finally struck like thunder and killed their way into the Southern Frontier Holy Land, the Life and Death Gate.

The battle had entered a white-hot stage from the very beginning.

The Life and Death Gate, which had closed its mountain gates for several hundred years, was not as low and dispirited as the world had imagined. Instead, at the start of the battle, it erupted with powerful strength.

Especially by relying on the geographical advantages of the sect, which had been managed for several thousand years, these Life and Death Gate cultivators dealt a heavy blow to the allied forces from the very beginning.

They almost drove them out of the mountain gates.

This dangerous situation was only alleviated after the top experts of the allied forces at the Sage realm all personally took action.

On the Fierce Peak Stiff Alliance side, there were over thirty Sages.

On the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect side, there were over twenty Sages.

Moreover, those who participated in this battle were all outstanding individuals among them.

After they personally intervened, the situation was immediately stabilized, and they led the army of cultivators to advance rapidly.

The strong of the Life and Death Gate naturally refused to sit idly and wait for death. Streaks of flying light flew out from various places, entangling those Sage-level experts.

The most intense killing, the most brutal fighting, appeared among these peak existences below the Ascendant level.

"Boom!"

After an abrupt clash, the Songyang Alliance Leader fell heavily to the ground.

His face flushed with surging qi and blood, and dharma power surged within his body.

He fixed his gaze and looked at the rather familiar face, blurting out, "So it is you! Zhu Shanzi, you are still alive!"

"My good friend, so you still remember me!" Zhu Shanzi slowly recalled his flying wheel, looking at the Songyang Alliance Leader with an unfriendly gaze, and his tone suddenly changed. "Since that is the case, why have you led your troops to kill into my Life and Death Gate?"

The Songyang Alliance Leader's face alternated between pale and red, but it instantly returned to normal.

He retorted, "You should ask your sect why it has closed its mountain gates tightly."

Zhu Shanzi frowned. "It concerns the sect's secret secrets, and I cannot disclose them."

The Songyang Alliance Leader slowly flew up into the sky, his gaze sweeping over the countless cultivators below, like ants and moths, and he grinned.

"Precisely because you said nothing, we came in ourselves to seek an explanation."

"Ourselves?" Zhu Shanzi said with sarcasm. "Since when did the Central Continent Dao Sect count as one of us?"

Before Songyangzi could answer, he roared angrily, "You are inviting a wolf into the house!"

The Songyang Alliance Leader said calmly, "A fierce tiger is already in the house, so why fear inviting another evil wolf?"

The friends who had once talked about everything now found that they had nothing in common, and half a sentence was too much.

Since they could not reach an agreement, then let it be war!

However, before the Songyang Alliance Leader could make a move, a vast pillar of light broke through the air from behind him, charging straight at Zhu Shanzi.

Zhu Shanzi's expression changed slightly. With their divine senses locked onto each other, a powerful enemy had appeared again. How could he block it?

A strong voice rose from behind him.

"Senior Brother, I will help you!"

A figure flashed past Zhu Shanzi and charged straight at the incoming enemy.

However, as fast as he went, he came back just as fast.

Boom!

Zhu Shanzi's junior brother was directly blasted into a mountain belly by that vast pillar of light.

Zhan Bingtao strode forward, his gaze arrogant, a look of disdain on his lips.

"Is this the strength of a Sage from the Southern Frontier Holy Land?"

After speaking, he did not even look at Zhu Shanzi, but only looked at the Songyang Alliance Leader.

"These people have been at peace for too long; their claws and teeth have dulled. Fellow Daoist Songyang, why can you not take him down yet?"

The Songyang Alliance Leader sighed. "I suddenly saw an old friend, so I showed some mercy."

Zhan Bingtao raised an eyebrow. "Is that so? Then I will leave this person for you to deal with yourself. I am going to the next battlefield."

With that, he was about to fly toward another intense battle group.

Opposite him, Zhu Shanzi's face was ashen. This person's strength was beyond expectation.

Even among the great cultivators, he was a top-tier peak powerhouse.

If he allowed the other party to rampage across the battlefield, the already weak Life and Death Gate cultivators would likely suffer a tremendous blow.

He instinctively wanted to block Zhan Bingtao, but the Songyang Alliance Leader stood in his way.

"My good friend, we have been apart for three hundred years. Can you let me see your Life and Death Wheel once more?"

Zhu Shanzi took a deep breath, withdrawing his gaze from Zhan Bingtao.

"Fine, since you have made a request, I will grant your wish. I also want to know just what level your Fierce Peak Seal has reached."

As he spoke, he held a white flying wheel in his hand, and another black flying wheel slowly rose behind him.

On the opposite side, the Songyang Alliance Leader smiled slightly and pointed a finger at the center of his brow.

In an instant, his dharma power surged, several times stronger than before.

"You may not know, but with the help of the Fierce Peak Stiff Alliance, my Fierce Peak Seal has long been perfected. You are no match for me!"

Zhu Shanzi's expression changed drastically.

...

The battle within the Life and Death Gate was in full swing.

However, not every place was covered by the flames of war.

At the extreme peak of the sea of clouds, the sound of heavy panting suddenly arose.

"Huff..."

Luo Chen suddenly woke from his daze and instinctively released his divine sense.

But in fact, he did not need to use his divine sense to explore at all. A figure in a blue robe was already reflected in his eyes.

Without thinking, he bowed respectfully.

"This junior pays respects to Divine Lord Youli!"

The old man in the blue robe smiled slightly. "Do you know me, old man?"

Luo Chen said respectfully, "One who calls Yin Yangzi 'Senior Brother' and can perform such a great divine ability as scooping the moon from within a realm—in the Southern Frontier, there is only Divine Lord alone!"

Divine Lord Youli laughed. "Since you know it is me, and you all broke into the Yin-Yang Remnant Realm arranged by my Life and Death Gate, why are you not the least bit afraid that I will hold you accountable?"

Luo Chen slowly straightened his waist and said calmly, "Fear is useless. With your methods, if you wanted to hold us accountable, we would have no reason to live. But now, I am still standing here safe and sound, which means you have no intention of killing me."

"As expected of Luo Chen of the Alchemy Sect, clever and quick-witted." Divine Lord Youli stroked his beard, looking at Luo Chen with a hint of appreciation in his eyes.

"You flatter me, Senior. May I ask why you have brought me here? Do you have any guidance?"

"Guidance is not the word. It is merely to retrieve an object."

As he spoke, Divine Lord Youli patted the small gray cauldron in front of him.

Luo Chen instinctively touched his own chest, his expression changing slightly.

He looked at his natal treasure, a thought flashing through his mind, and he hesitated. "Could it be...?"

Divine Lord Youli nodded. "Exactly, the Calamity Gu!"

After speaking, he flicked his finger, and the small gray cauldron emitted a sound.

The sound was deep, its echoes reverberating endlessly.

Divine Lord Youli raised an eyebrow. "This treasure has a good foundation!"

Luo Chen's heart tightened. That was his natal treasure, and now it was in the hands of another, but he dared not do anything.

Within his field of vision, amidst the ringing of the cauldron, the Primordial Unity Cauldron began to emit light.

Red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet—seven colors, brilliant and beautiful.

A gray butterfly seemed to come to life, fluttering gracefully within the rosy clouds.

The old man's gentle voice slowly reached Luo Chen's ears.

"Do you know why my Life and Death Gate closed its mountain gates and did not set foot in the mortal world?"

"This junior does not know."

"Pay attention to the surrounding environment, and you will have a rough idea."

"Eh, this concentration of heaven and earth origin qi?"

With a single perception, Luo Chen's expression changed drastically.

The concentration of heaven and earth origin qi in the vicinity was so high that it was like bathing in an ocean of origin qi.

But this was by no means a common phenomenon!

That ocean of origin qi was a place that could only be explored when comprehending the true meaning of laws, the origin place of a world.

Yet here, at the extreme peak of the sea of clouds, it was like another ocean of origin qi.

Being deep within it naturally had many benefits, but the drawbacks were predictable.

With the slightest inattention, one would be assimilated by heaven and earth.

In fact, it was not a matter of attention. Even if one was on guard day and night, one would likely sink into it.

"Senior, this..."

Divine Lord Youli did not directly answer Luo Chen, but instead praised him again.

"To be able to comprehend the true meaning of two laws in advance at the Sage realm, even if placed in the Xuan Realm, you would be considered a top-tier cultivation genius."

"The Xuan Realm? What kind of place is that?" Luo Chen had a vague guess in his heart, but still could not help asking.

Divine Lord Youli pointed upward.

"The Upper Realm?" Luo Chen asked.

Divine Lord Youli laughed. "You can think of it as the Upper Realm, or you can think of it as another Mountains and Seas Realm. The difference between the two is merely one being large and one small, one ancient and one young. That is all. There is no substantial difference."

Luo Chen frowned, pondering carefully.

A genius like himself could be considered unique in the Mountains and Seas Realm.

But in the other's mouth, although there was no lack of praise, it was merely a first-rate figure.

Clearly, the Xuan Realm was the larger realm, and also the more ancient world.

The Mountains and Seas Realm, by comparison, was much smaller.

As for young?

Luo Chen shook his head inwardly and turned to another matter.

"This junior has a doubt. I wonder if Senior can resolve it?"

"You want to ask whether the five Holy Lands of the Mountains and Seas Realm come from the Xuan Realm, right?"

Luo Chen nodded.

The old man in the blue robe also nodded slightly, acknowledging this guess.

But afterwards, without waiting for Luo Chen to press further, he turned the topic back to the very beginning.

"The heaven and earth origin qi here is abnormal, almost like the descent of the ocean of origin qi. Do you know why?"

Luo Chen did not understand.

Divine Lord Youli slowly raised the Primordial Unity Cauldron. That gray butterfly was still dancing within the seven-colored rosy clouds, as if it would fly out in the next second.

Staring at this butterfly, Divine Lord Youli's gaze was complex.

"It is precisely because of it!"

Luo Chen blurted out, "The disaster brought by the Calamity Gu?"

"It was caused by it, but not brought by it. Being able to cultivate by borrowing the ocean of origin qi is a fortune that many Ascendant cultivators dream of but cannot obtain. How can it be called a disaster?" Divine Lord Youli murmured, his expression slightly pained. "But many times, when fortune is too heavy, blessings turn into calamities."

As he spoke, he reached out and beckoned.

From the sea of clouds formed by the massive amount of heaven and earth origin qi, a red light flew over.

Looking closely, it was actually a red butterfly.

(End of chapter)
