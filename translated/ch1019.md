# Chapter 1019: Spirit Stone Gansui, Three Promises

*Crack!*

*Crackle!*

Thunder and lightning crashed and boomed without cease.

Flashing bolts of lightning illuminated the vast ocean.

Within the pitch-black Nether Abyss, a large turtle pushed through the waves, swimming forward at a pace that seemed slow yet was actually swift.

Luo Chen sat cross-legged on the turtle's back, frowning as he looked at a rolled-up bamboo slip that had been organized.

It was something Sang Jinghe had handed over.

It recorded the situation of feeding the Demon-Devil Heart-Linking Tree with Golden Dragon Lion blood, along with a large amount of Sang Jinghe's analysis and research.

"Jinghe's initial speculation was correct. Archaic beast essence blood does indeed help the Demon-Devil Heart-Linking Tree grow."

"But to condense a fruit, it's still far from enough."

"Or rather, the blood of a low-grade Golden Dragon Lion isn't enough!"

Luo Chen held the bamboo slip and murmured softly. According to Sang Jinghe's analysis, the rank of the Demon-Devil Heart-Linking Tree was too high. Although it had no spiritual intelligence, it sprouted at the fourth rank and matured at the fifth rank.

Such a high-grade spiritual plant naturally required extremely high-grade resources for cultivation.

Archaic beast blood alone wasn't enough; it needed high-grade Archaic beast blood!

And even that was taking a shortcut. Sang Jinghe believed the correct cultivation method should use the heart blood of ancient demons and ancient devils, which might lead to faster growth.

After all, its name was the "Demon-Devil Heart-Linking Tree."

"Forget it. I won't be using this thing anytime soon anyway." Luo Chen put away the bamboo slip and stopped thinking about this spiritual plant obtained from the Demon-Fall Land.

Whether it was high-grade Archaic beasts or ancient demons and devils, none of them were things he could consider right now.

Since he had time, it would be better to cultivate another divine ability to have at his side.

Thinking of divine abilities, Luo Chen's gaze fell upon this Nether Abyss.

The Mysterious Turtle beneath his feet was still silently moving forward, while Bai Meiling happily appeared and disappeared around them.

Because Luo Chen had agreed to bring Bai Meiling along on this trip, she was very happy. She hadn't traveled outside for a long time.

Considering that Little Bai had worked hard to collect low-grade ghosts for him over twenty years, Luo Chen naturally agreed to bring her along as a reward.

"Master, the Nirvana Sacred Flame above the Nether Abyss has almost completely disappeared!" Bai Meiling cheered from a distance. Without the suppression of the Nirvana Sacred Flame, they, as yin souls, could move freely within the Nether Abyss.

It could be said that the reason Bai Meiling had been able to collect so many low-grade souls in just twenty years was also related to the sudden disappearance of the Nirvana Sacred Flame.

However, Luo Chen showed no surprise on his face regarding this matter.

He pursed his lips and moved his mind.

The next moment!

Bai Meiling watched in terror as a fire phoenix flew out from Luo Chen's body, circled once above the pitch-black abyss surface, sweeping away layers of ghostly qi, and then slowly flew back into Luo Chen's body.

That wasn't a spell!

It was a genuine sentient being!

She asked in a trembling, fearful voice, "Master, could it be that you have collected those Nirvana Sacred Flames?"

Luo Chen shook his head, but then slightly nodded after shaking it, indirectly admitting to the matter.

The Nirvana Sacred Flame in the Nether Abyss had already been greatly worn down over time by the continuous flow of Nether Water.

Last time, Luo Chen had triggered a heavenly change, attracting a large amount of Nirvana Sacred Flame inside.

He had absorbed a portion with the Blood Jade Three Flame Fan, while the remaining portion had rushed into the Primordial Unity Cauldron during his soul's nirvana.

At that time, Luo Chen had lost consciousness and didn't know what happened afterward.

He only knew that after waking up, the Nirvana Fire Spirit in his Purple Mansion had expanded dozens of times, becoming an existence on par with the peak Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit.

Coupled with Luo Chen having completed his soul's nirvana, the Nirvana Sacred Flame could now truly be considered a fifth-rank divine flame!

"But is this good or bad?"

Luo Chen subconsciously pressed his chest, his expression uncertain.

The original Nirvana Fire Spirit originated from a remnant fire spirit he had obtained from the Heaven-Refining Cauldron, whose consciousness had been completely obliterated.

So at that time, Luo Chen could use it without any scruples, boldly refining it into his own true flame.

Even if it was weak, even if it wasn't as powerful as the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame.

But now, the Nirvana Fire Spirit had absorbed a large amount of the Nirvana Sacred Flame left behind by Rosycloud. Even though Luo Chen had checked and confirmed that this fire spirit still belonged to him, what if Rosycloud harbored ill intentions? With her level of fire mastery as an ancestor-level figure...

Luo Chen didn't dare to think about what the outcome would be if it really came to that.

But soon, Luo Chen had a plan in mind!

In the upcoming actions, the Nirvana Fire Spirit, which had grown to the fifth rank, was a major asset, no less than the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame. He couldn't deal with it for now.

But after this matter was resolved, he would have to start dealing with the Nirvana Fire Spirit.

"I wonder how badly Rosycloud is injured, and whether she still has the strength to make a move?"

Suddenly, this thought popped into Luo Chen's mind.

...

"Xuan Ming, go back!"

A delicate young girl crouched by the shore, making a goodbye gesture to the large turtle that rose and fell in the water.

The large turtle sprayed a water spout, shook its head, and sank into the Nether Abyss, gradually fading away.

Luo Chen asked, "You call it Xuan Ming?"

Bai Meiling stood up and said proudly, "Yes, you never gave it a name. Over these twenty years, it has been carrying me around swimming. So I simply took Xuan from Mysterious Turtle and Ming from Nether Water, giving it the name Xuan Ming. It really likes this name too; every time I call it, it responds."

Luo Chen looked at the shadow of the Mysterious Turtle reluctantly drifting away and couldn't help but smile.

"Xuan Ming, that's a good name."

After praising it, Luo Chen waved his hand and a flying shuttle landed in front of him.

It was the Frost Eagle Flying Shuttle!

This shuttle had an extremely fast flying speed and was very stealthy, perfectly suited for Luo Chen's current trip.

"Let's go. Before leaving the Eastern Wilderness, I still need to see an old friend."

Bai Meiling boarded the flying shuttle and asked curiously, "Aren't we going to take a look at Shangyou Immortal City? With the support of Sect Leader Li, Haoranzi has managed that abandoned city quite well. He has gathered a fourth-rank spirit vein, and it's gradually showing signs of prosperity."

"No need to look. That place holds no meaning for me." Luo Chen casually cast a stream of mana, and the flying shuttle slowly rose into the sky. "But on the way, you can tell me about the situation in Shangyou Immortal City."

"Yes, Master."

The Frost Eagle Flying Shuttle dove into the clouds, turning into a streak of black light, and vanished over the horizon in an instant.

...

**Dapan Peak!**

One of the eighteen inner sect peaks of Mount Tai Sect.

It wasn't as towering and steep as Aolai Peak, nor as scenic and beautiful as Little Bamboo Mountain, but its steady and imposing character was unmatched among all the peaks of Mount Tai Sect.

Under the night sky, Dapan Peak resembled a slumbering giant beast, overlooking the entire world.

Suddenly!

Dapan Peak trembled for an instant, and a large amount of mountain rocks rose and fell, as if the giant beast had truly awakened.

A sound of surprise came from within.

"Stone spirit?"

The next moment, within the cave dwelling of Dapan Peak, a voice filled with shock and anger rang out.

"Which fellow daoist has come uninvited?"

Streams of light shot up into the sky, a truly captivating sight under the night sky.

The surrounding disciples seemed to sense something and flew over, but were soon ordered back by Deng Taiyue.

The trembling of Dapan Peak gradually ceased.

Deng Taiyue looked at the newcomer with shock and uncertainty.

Opposite him stood a tall man, draped in a black robe with a hood covering most of his face, only a pair of eyes blazing with golden light, hot as fire.

Such eyes, Deng Taiyue had only ever seen on one person.

It was Luo Chen!

Luo Chen took off his hood, first nodded, then looked at the towering black stone standing at the top of Dapan Peak.

Under his Crimson Eye Golden Gaze, that black stone was transforming into a strange beast, resembling a wolf and a tiger, staring at him warily.

"I thought I had hidden my aura perfectly, but I didn't expect to be discovered by this thing as soon as I set foot on this mountain. A naturally sentient stone?"

Deng Taiyue's expression was somewhat complicated. Luo Chen's claim of hiding his aura perfectly was not boasting; it was the truth.

Because he, the master of Mount Tai Sect, hadn't even sensed Luo Chen's arrival on his own turf.

Even the surrounding formations had no reaction at all!

If it weren't for the protective mountain stone spirit of Dapan Peak discovering the intruder, Luo Chen could have entered his cave dwelling without him even knowing.

This was too shocking!

Even the previous Luo Chen, as powerful as he was, couldn't have achieved such a feat.

There was only one explanation: he had grown even stronger!

Deng Taiyue took a deep breath, shook off the unease in his heart, and introduced, "Its name is Gansui. It is indeed a rare sentient spirit stone. Moreover, it was condensed by Dapan Peak three hundred years ago. So the moment you set foot on this mountain, it could sense you."

Luo Chen suddenly understood. No wonder this stone spirit could detect him; after all, by stepping onto the mountain, he was walking on its very body.

Suddenly, Luo Chen caught one of the key words.

"Three hundred years ago?" Luo Chen looked uncertainly at Deng Taiyue. "I recall Brother Deng once mentioned that you had an accidental moment of enlightenment, almost comprehending the true meaning of the law. Wasn't that over three hundred years ago?"

Deng Taiyue smiled bitterly. "Brother Luo is indeed sharp; you saw through the key point immediately. Yes, that time I happened to witness Gansui's sentience and was moved by it, entering a state of enlightenment. I almost comprehended the true meaning of the law."

Luo Chen showed an expression of "just as I thought." He waved his hand and cast a stream of mana. The Gansui, which had been staring at him warily, immediately turned back into a black stone that had weathered wind and rain for ages.

This small display of skill was enough to show the vast gap between them.

Seeing this, Deng Taiyue couldn't help but narrow his eyes.

Luo Chen walked over to the stone, stroked it, and shook his head. "It's a pity. This stone's foundation is too shallow. It only managed to gain sentience by absorbing a large amount of earth qi and the essence of the sun and moon. If its foundation were stronger, it would have triggered the origin of earth during its sentience, and Brother Deng wouldn't have failed."

Deng Taiyue looked bewildered.

Luo Chen smiled and didn't explain further.

In essence, Deng Taiyue's so-called enlightenment back then was the same principle as his own comprehension of the true meaning of the law by observing the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit in the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven.

Both were shortcuts, observing a "divine object gaining sentience" to comprehend the corresponding true meaning of the law.

It was just that he was lucky; the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit was of a high enough grade, so the shortcut worked.

Deng Taiyue, on the other hand, was a bit unlucky and had been troubled by this for three hundred years, unable to make any progress.

But now, there was a new solution to all this.

*Thump!*

A jade bottle was placed on top of the black stone.

"The thing you want."

With just one glance, Deng Taiyue's gaze fell on the jade bottle and couldn't move away.

He couldn't help but swallow his saliva and asked, "Has the Initiation Pill been refined?"

Luo Chen smiled. "Living up to expectations! Combining the accumulated knowledge of the Nether Origin Sect and some of my own understanding, I managed to refine it. Moreover, I tried it once myself and gained something from it."

As he spoke, Luo Chen released the mana he had already converted within his body.

The moment this portion of mana appeared, it seemed to resonate with heaven and earth.

*Plop!*

A drop of rain fell.

It landed on the black stone, and Gansui let out a muffled roar of pain.

Deng Taiyue's eyes widened, staring at this scene in disbelief.

"You've comprehended another new true meaning of the law?"

Luo Chen moved his mind, and the vast mana instantly retracted. He had forgotten that drawing in the origin qi of heaven and earth would cause some damage to this Gansui.

Faced with Deng Taiyue's shock, Luo Chen made no attempt to hide it and nodded heavily.

"Yes. By borrowing the power of the Initiation Pill and combining it with the Nether Water Spring Eye, I have comprehended the origin of water. My strength has greatly increased, even more than before!"

Deng Taiyue was both shocked by Luo Chen's transformation and grew increasingly fervent about that bottle of Initiation Pills.

Without hesitation, he took out a storage pouch.

"This is the Xiao Qi obtained from trading those Nascent Soul Pills, all meeting your requirements."

"Also, I have sounded out Nine Spirit Primordial Lord, Tian Duzi, Fenghua Fairy, and others. If you need helpers, they are willing to help you. However, I did not say that the enemy would be the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. I was afraid that if I said it, they would all back out."

Luo Chen nodded, reached out, and took the storage pouch.

With a sweep of his divine sense, he showed a satisfied expression.

There was indeed a considerable amount of Xiao Qi inside, very suitable for him to cultivate the *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon*.

As for Deng Taiyue's concerns, he didn't mind.

If the enemy was too powerful, friends would be unwilling to draw their swords to help. That was only human nature.

But if he showed enough strength to contend with the enemy and offered sufficient rewards, they would be willing to lend a hand under the guise of friendship.

"What do you think? If I offer the Initiation Pill as a reward, would they be willing to help me?"

Deng Taiyue was stunned. He subconsciously looked at the jade bottle.

Luo Chen shook his head with a smile. "Don't worry. This bottle is all yours. There are three Initiation Pills inside. One person can only use one in their lifetime, regardless of success or failure. The extras can be kept as a foundation for your sect's heritage."

Deng Taiyue breathed a sigh of relief and carefully retrieved the bottle.

But then, a subtle expression appeared on his face, and he didn't answer Luo Chen's question.

Luo Chen seemed to understand what he was thinking. "Do you feel it's unfair? That you painstakingly pursued something for hundreds of years, and only after finally obtaining it, others can get it so easily?"

Deng Taiyue didn't want to admit it, but under Luo Chen's gaze, he reluctantly nodded.

Luo Chen had considered this possibility, so he generously said, "You are different from them. I will record a copy of my experience and insights from taking the Initiation Pill for you. Not only that, but from now on, the Luo Tian Sect and Mount Tai Sect will be eternal allies. If you comprehend the true meaning of the law and wish to advance to the Ascendant realm, I can even help you find a suitable fifth-rank spirit vein."

With these three promises laid out, the resentment in Deng Taiyue's heart finally faded.

In truth, he shouldn't have had these feelings in the first place.

Who was the person in front of him?

He was the one who, it was said, could slaughter all the Sages under heaven when joining forces with the Demon Emperor Azure Frost!

What right did he have to bargain?

But the fact that Luo Chen was willing to say this was indeed giving him face.

Deng Taiyue hesitated for a moment, then replied, "If the Initiation Pill is offered as a reward, I believe most people will be tempted. However, this excludes that one from the Dongyang Sect!"

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow, and information about that person instantly flooded his mind.

The former head vassal of the Nether Origin Sect.

The current lackey of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect in the Eastern Wilderness.

The Grand Elder of the Dongyang Sect!

(End of chapter)
