# Chapter 646: Worthy of the Name Devil Lord, What a Demon-Headed Manner!

Since joining the Myriad Immortals Assembly, Luo Chen had experienced very few missions.

Especially the most common demon beast hunting missions. He had not done even one.

Therefore, he was not very clear about the transaction details involved.

At this moment in Taiping Fortress, several Foundation Establishment True Cultivators spent half a day appraising the demon beasts he had taken out one by one. When they encountered something they did not understand, they sweated profusely while repeatedly taking out transmission artifacts to communicate with another more skilled appraiser who was not present.

In the end, they finally reached the relevant result.

The four demon beast corpses, if sold to the Demon Hunter Office, could obtain around two million six hundred thousand spirit stones.

At the same time, the converted merits would be enough for Luo Chen to advance from a five-star demon hunter to six-star, with plenty to spare.

This result was not beyond Luo Chen's expectations.

After all, they were four late third-tier Great Demon Kings comparable to late cultivators.

For an ordinary person, being able to hunt one would be enough to boast about for a lifetime, let alone Luo Chen killing four at once.

The only thing that surprised Luo Chen was the spirit stone price for the Demon King corpses.

Along his path of cultivation, he had experienced many demon beast corpse transactions, so he was very sensitive to various prices.

An ordinary first-tier demon beast was only worth around a thousand low-grade spirit stones.

A second-tier one was around ten thousand spirit stones, and the corpse had to be relatively complete.

Third-tier was expensive, easily reaching several hundred thousand, and the prices were divided by level.

Early-stage Demon Kings had one price, middle-stage Demon Kings another, and late-stage Demon Kings were several times or even dozens of times higher.

Of course, this was the former market for demon beast corpse transactions in the Eastern Wasteland.

In the Northern Sea, because the sea was vast and all kinds of demon beasts emerged endlessly, the demon beast market was relatively low.

But no matter how low it was, these were four late third-tier Great Demon Kings.

Forget two million. Even selling them together for seven or eight million would be reasonable.

"Two million? Are you joking with this seat?" Luo Chen asked in a deep voice.

Under the pressure of his restrained but unreleased aura, several Foundation Establishment True Cultivators trembled and cast pleading gazes toward the silent Dao Lan.

Dao Lan was helpless.

Facing Luo Chen's gaze, he sighed. "When the Demon Hunter Office purchases demon beast materials, the internal price is usually half the outside world's. Has Fellow Daoist not understood this over the years?"

Luo Chen snorted coldly, quite displeased.

Inwardly, however, he came to a realization. No wonder Zhang Jiadi and his wife back then had risked being punished by the Demon Hunter Office to sell their hunted demon beasts to Giant Whale Port.

This internal price was pressed too harshly.

How was this a fifty-percent cut? It was clearly sixty-forty, or even seventy-thirty.

Seeing Luo Chen displeased, Dao Lan still patiently explained a few more sentences because he had a request to make.

"Although the price is low, a portion of it can actually be considered converted into the merits needed to raise a demon hunter's star grade."

"Moreover, the portion lost can also be made up through the discounts granted by demon hunter star grade when exchanging for internal resources at the Demon Hunter Office."

"It is not low. The price has been like this for many years."

"Besides, Fellow Daoist, these demon beast corpses of yours have many missing parts. They cannot reach the optimal transaction price to begin with."

"If one day Fellow Daoist raises your star grade to nine-star demon hunter, you can even obtain the Myriad Immortals Assembly's resource support for Sage formation. Compared to that, this little loss is nothing at all."

Listening to this seemingly rough battle fanatic comfort him with the tone of a household accountant fussing over every coin, Luo Chen found it rather amusing inwardly.

"Enough, enough. Then two million it is. This seat cannot be bothered arguing with you."

This amount of spirit stones would be an enormous sum in the eyes of ordinary Golden Core cultivators. It would be enough to rent an ordinary third-tier spiritual land and cultivate for a long time.

But in Luo Chen's eyes, it was not worth mentioning.

Putting aside the increasingly high output of the Peng Lake spiritual land over these years, just from Mountain Lord Hundred Craft's storage bag earlier, he had obtained a full thirty-plus million spirit stones.

That was a truly enormous sum.

Hundred Craft Mountain was a great artifact-forging sect, rich enough to drip oil.

Clearly, Mountain Lord Hundred Craft had carried most of his personal wealth with him on this trip. In the end, it had benefited Luo Chen.

His earlier displeasure was not because he cared about the price, but merely because he did not want to suffer a loss.

Now that the rules were like this, he could not be bothered to argue.

Dao Lan, however, was somewhat surprised. "Wasn't it two million six hundred thousand?"

Luo Chen snorted. "The other corpses can be sold to the Demon Hunter Office, but I will keep the Sea-Overturning Demon Jiao myself."

As he spoke, he waved his hand and directly placed the dried Sea-Overturning Demon Jiao corpse into the Thunder Accumulation Treasure Ring.

The other appraisers looked at one another and ultimately did not dare say a single word.

Dao Lan wanted to remind Luo Chen that if he took it away himself, it was best used personally and should not circulate outside the Myriad Immortals Assembly, or he could easily be punished by the Demon Hunter Office.

But when the words reached his lips, he swallowed them back.

Luo Chen had already agreed to sell him the sawteeth from the Scorchflame Sawtooth Shark, and at an extremely low price. Since Dao Lan had gained a great advantage, how could he offend the other party now?

Moreover, the Myriad Immortals Assembly managed low-rank cultivators strictly, but for high-star demon hunters like them, its restraining power was not so strong.

Let him do as he wished.

After handling this matter, Luo Chen had no more reason to linger. He said farewell to Dao Lan and returned to his cave residence.

After resting for two days, he once again rushed toward the Sunken Sea.

After he left, the name Devil Lord Azure Yang began spreading through Taiping Fortress.

This time, the discussion among wandering cultivators was several times more heated than when Dao Lan killed the Golden-Horned Silver Rhinoceros.

Dao Lan's strength had long since become familiar to the wandering cultivators in the fortress. Even killing the Golden-Horned Silver Rhinoceros was only adding flowers to brocade.

But Devil Lord Azure Yang, this Golden Core Adept famous in the Myriad Immortals Assembly for artifact forging, had achieved such glorious battle results on his first appearance on the battlefield. How could people not discuss him with relish?

The only thing that made everyone itch with curiosity was that in all the discussions, the true methods by which Devil Lord Azure Yang defeated his enemies almost never appeared.

Everyone only knew he had this strength, but did not know what magic treasure or secret art he relied on.

On the lake of a small island, sorrowful clouds and thick mist gathered, and ghost qi filled the air.

Luo Chen hung high in the sky.

The dignified great sun shone dazzlingly above, but its radiance could not pierce the ghost mist below.

Within Luo Chen's body, it was also as if there were a great sun endlessly radiating light outward.

His lifebound Golden Core spun constantly.

His lifebound magic treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, shone brilliantly, scattering his surging mana outward through special formation patterns and forming the so-called All-Encompassing Fire Prison in the void.

Below him, more than one thousand low-rank demon beasts wailed and cried.

A middle third-tier Demon King unwillingly raised its head to the sky and roared at the red-robed Daoist. It had just sacrificed an ill-suited magic treasure flying sword when the sword swayed and fell to the ground, as if completely uncontrolled.

Under the suppression of the All-Encompassing Fire Prison, powerful Great Demon Kings might be able to resist somewhat.

But these low-rank demon beasts could only be slaughtered.

Even a middle third-tier Demon King could not freely mobilize demonic power and could not even control a magic treasure.

It wanted to use its body to charge into the red-robed Daoist, but from the dense ghost qi all around, Ghost Kings flew out from time to time and drilled into its body, striking it until it could not even control its own flesh.

Under these circumstances, Luo Chen could activate his origin true fire without pressure and forcibly refine these still-living demon beasts.

Burning, refining, devouring vitality, and feeding it back to himself.

"The Myriad Immortals Assembly's purchase price for demon beasts is far too low. Rather than sell to them, I may as well use them to benefit myself."

As Luo Chen controlled the All-Encompassing Fire Prison to suppress the demon beast group, he used origin true fire to burn-refine vitality and even had enough spare attention to think about other things.

When the fourth-tier Withering Glory True Fire burned everything away and landed on the demon beast that seemed both deer and not deer, sheep and not sheep, several Ghost Kings hurriedly fled out and flew to the side to await orders.

Around a dozen breaths later, Luo Chen slowly withdrew his origin true fire.

The All-Encompassing Fire Prison that suppressed everything also abruptly scattered.

Luo Chen floated in midair, eyes closed, feeling the changes in his body.

"Over one thousand low-rank demon beasts plus one third-tier Demon King. The converted vitality is less than half of that jiaoren's."

"But mosquito legs are still meat. Accumulated little by little, it will always produce a qualitative change."

On the far shore of the Sunken Sea, Great Demon Kings were hard to find, but low-rank demon beasts were everywhere.

If they were killed cleanly, there were still endless streams from the Arctic Yemo Heaven.

Luo Chen believed that if he spent more effort, he would always gain something, and this process was completely not dangerous to him.

The only problem was probably that Luo Chen discovered the vitality the Withering Glory True Fire could feed back was becoming less and less.

Regarding this point, Luo Chen was quite puzzled.

Was the demon beast rank insufficient, or had the carrying limit of his body reached its extreme?

Or was the Withering Glory True Fire limiting him?

Shaking his head, Luo Chen opened his eyes.

He exhaled a long breath of turbid air and beckoned with his hand.

Whoosh!

A black banner fell into his hand. At the same time, the Ghost Generals and Ghost Kings flying throughout the sky, along with the dense ghost qi, all drilled into the Myriad Souls Banner.

Only then did the blazing sunlight outside, along with many burning gazes, fall on Luo Chen.

"It is Devil Lord Azure Yang! He came out!"

"Show some respect. You should call him Adept, or Senior Daoist Azure Yang!"

"What do you know? Some big figures like this kind of style."

"Never mind that. I wonder what the Devil Lord's battle results are?"

As Luo Chen appeared, those burning gazes finally fell to the lakebed below, which had been burned completely dry and now showed only a parched riverbed.

No demon beast corpses were visible, only thick bone ash.

Everyone looked at one another as an unbelievable guess appeared in their minds.

Could it be...

"Impossible. The demon beasts occupying this island are skilled in formation-linked attacks. Adept Guo and the other four joined forces and still failed to take this island. The Devil Lord killed them all by himself?"

"What is impossible about it? Adept Guo and the others are not orthodox Golden Core cultivators to begin with. They are false-core cultivators who advanced by refining demon cores. Demon beast groups they cannot kill, could Lord Devil Lord not kill?"

"But this... no corpses remain. No corpses remain!"

Amid the astonished cries, Luo Chen seemed not to notice.

His gaze fell on the five Golden Core cultivators at the front of the crowd.

When his gaze landed on them, the five involuntarily cupped their fists in salute.

"Guo Fengdao greets Lord Devil Lord!"

Luo Chen had no intention of remembering their names. They were merely a few false-core cultivators. But at the moment, he had something to ask.

According to the previous intelligence, this demon beast group had not been on the island. In other words, there had been a deviation in the intelligence.

Next, he still needed to hunt other low-rank demon beasts. To prevent similar situations from occurring, it was better to ask these people clearly.

As soon as he spoke, Guo Fengdao and the others immediately explained the recent intelligence on demon beast gatherings one by one.

"Frogmouth Great Whirlpool... Bluegreen Plain... Xu Flow Sea... Three Pillars Gorge..."

One place name after another was firmly remembered.

These places were all gathering areas for large numbers of low-rank demon beasts, and they differed greatly from the intelligence on the force distribution map that Taiping Fortress's Shentu had given him.

After all, demon beasts did not always occupy fixed locations.

Especially with immortal cultivators hunting them.

They would also change places.

After Luo Chen noted these locations, he nodded in thanks to the few people and hurriedly flew away.

After he left, Guo Fengdao and the others stood together, looking at one another for a long time without saying a word.

As Golden Core cultivators, even if they were false-core cultivators, they were indeed powerful experts who possessed mana.

Therefore, before Luo Chen, they could more directly sense the other party's strength.

Even though the other party had restrained much of his spiritual pressure, those deep, sea-like mana fluctuations still left them unable to withstand him.

After a long time, Guo Fengdao finally spoke.

"If he wanted to kill us, perhaps he would not need three moves."

Over the next several months, Luo Chen frequently appeared on the human-demon battlefield of the Sunken Sea.

Wherever he went, he first unfolded the Myriad Souls Banner to block sight inside and outside.

Then he used the All-Encompassing Fire Prison to forcefully suppress low-rank demon beasts, then used origin true fire to forcibly burn-refine them and extract vitality.

When he encountered somewhat powerful third-tier Demon Kings, at most he used a few Azure Yang Great Handprint. If he found it troublesome, a glare of Mirror Flower Water Moon could also easily take them down.

After he left, it often meant that hundreds, thousands, or even tens of thousands of low-rank demon beasts had disappeared.

No corpses remained.

Such domineering and frequent actions made the Myriad Immortals Assembly wandering cultivators on the battlefield go from shocked and horrified to numb and accustomed.

The name Devil Lord Azure Yang also surpassed the former respectful title of Daoist Azure Yang with each retelling.

Luo Chen could naturally sense the increasingly thick awe in other people's gazes toward him.

But he ignored it all and continued doing as he pleased.

There were Golden Core garrison cultivators from Taiping Fortress who came with Shentu's orders to ask how he handled the demon beast corpses he killed.

He brushed them off with the explanation that he had crushed their bones and scattered their ashes.

With Devil Lord Azure Yang's prestige growing thicker by the day, Shentu could do nothing to him and could only let him be.

Overall, Luo Chen's large-scale slaughter was a good thing for the first defensive line.

Large numbers of low-rank demon beasts being slaughtered meant Taiping Fortress's pressure was constantly reduced. Who could criticize anything?

On this day, a streak of escape light landed in a swift sea region.

Luo Chen raised his head and looked. Three sky-supporting giant peaks stood above the sea.

Three surging currents continuously converged in the gorge, yet remained clearly separate.

Three Pillars Gorge.

A dangerous place linking the Sunken Sea, the Flow Ocean sea region, and the Arctic Sea.

According to the intelligence, this place was occupied by a massive demon beast group numbering no fewer than thirty thousand.

Among them, there were at least five third-tier Demon Kings alone.

There was even a Great Demon King lurking in the waters on the side of Three Pillars Gorge facing the Sunken Sea.

Luo Chen's target was not that Great Demon King, but the thirty thousand low-rank demon beasts.

"If I can devour the vitality of this demon beast group, my merit will be complete."

Luo Chen murmured to himself and was about to step into the Three Pillars Gorge region.

But as he was about to move, he stopped. A large man carrying a saber was coming over the waves.

It was Dao Lan.

"It has been a long time, Fellow Daoist Dao Lan."

Dao Lan's expression became slightly tense. When he saw Luo Chen's blood-red face, his eyelids could not help twitching wildly.

The long saber in his hand trembled uncontrollably, emitting waves of bloodthirsty desire.

'How is this possible?'

'He has acted so frequently. Logically speaking, he should have consumed enormous amounts of mana. Why does this person feel stronger and stronger to me?'

'What is with that boiling blood qi and vigorous vitality that he cannot suppress?'

'My Golden Suanni Saber grows more excited seeing him than seeing a Great Demon King!'

Dao Lan roared repeatedly in his heart, but on the surface he still maintained restraint.

"Devil Lord, it has been a long time. Why have you also come to Three Pillars Gorge?"

Luo Chen smiled faintly. Instead of answering, he asked, "And you?"

Dao Lan frowned. "Naturally, I came for that Great King Squid!"

Luo Chen gave an oh, then smiled freely.

"In that case, I will leave that troublesome fellow to you. I will handle the rest."

So arrogant?

Dao Lan was surprised for a moment, then reminded him, "There are long-term wandering cultivators doing demon hunting missions here. When Fellow Daoist acts, it would be best to pay some attention."

"Haha, people like us still care about such things?"

Luo Chen laughed loudly, leapt forward, and plunged straight into the towering gorge.

Dao Lan stood behind, looking at that broad-robed, large-sleeved back like blazing fire, and murmured,

"Worthy of the name Devil Lord. What a demon-headed manner!"

*(End of Chapter)*
