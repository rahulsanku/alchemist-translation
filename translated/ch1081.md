# Chapter 1081: Divine Powers Clash, Luan Cries Through the Long Night

"This sword is named Yuantu, and today it shall slaughter all Heavenly Origin great cultivators!"

The black-robed Daoist's clear and penetrating voice slowly spread outward.

A flash of anger crossed Liancheng's face. "Arrogant nonsense!"

He struck out with his palm, and the origin qi of heaven and earth, as if under his control, formed a massive hand that slammed toward Luo Chen.

Luo Chen showed no weakness. He formed a hand seal and pointed.

A fire phoenix spread its wings, roaring proudly as it charged forward.

The great hand and the fire phoenix collided in an instant, shaking the void.

These two moves, though appearing grand and imposing, were in truth merely the most basic techniques of both sides.

"Indeed, you have comprehended the true meaning of the laws, allowing you to control the origin qi of heaven and earth across realms." Gazing at the turbulent scene of origin qi in the heavens, Liancheng raised an eyebrow, then abruptly changed his tone: "However, you are ultimately not an Ascendant great cultivator. The amount of origin qi you can control is only this much."

Even as he spoke to demoralize Luo Chen, Liancheng continuously struck out with both hands. More and more origin qi of heaven and earth empowered his palm techniques, like a Thousand-Armed Buddha ceaselessly slapping toward Luo Chen.

Faced with this attack, Luo Chen's expression remained calm. He employed the same tactic, only now his hand seals were cast with lightning speed.

The fire phoenix spread its wings; the flame dragon roared. One spell after another poured forth from his hands as if mana cost meant nothing.

Once each spell was unleashed, it would draw upon the origin qi of heaven and earth, amplifying its power.

This differed from how an exceptional Sage, after comprehending the true meaning of the laws, would infuse their spells with origin power. Luo Chen had also released the barrier between himself and heaven and earth, allowing even more origin qi to merge into his techniques.

Thus, even ordinary fourth-tier spells, in Luo Chen's hands at this moment, possessed immense and overwhelming might.

What's more!

All these spells of his were limit-breaking, perfected fourth-tier spells.

Every single move was in no way inferior to the normal attacks of an Ascendant great cultivator.

Most importantly, Luo Chen had not comprehended merely one true meaning of the laws!

If he had only comprehended one, he might indeed be outmatched by an Ascendant in the amount of origin qi he could control. But Luo Chen had comprehended three. Therefore, even when fighting across realms, he was in no way inferior to Liancheng.

For a time, above Heaven-Earth Island, great hands appeared frequently, dragons and phoenixes tumbled through the air, and a fierce collision unfolded.

The origin qi of heaven and earth churned violently; wind and clouds rolled and howled without cease.

Suddenly!

Liancheng retreated, casting a sidelong glance toward a certain location.

There, what had previously been a tiny vortex was now continuously expanding. A vigorous and flourishing aura was rising fiercely from within.

That aura gave one a feeling as if it could control all things in heaven and earth.

Liancheng's heart could not help but rejoice.

"Ke Lianshan has taken that final step! He is about to become an Ascendant!"

With this, the God-Descending Formation would have a new master. No one could stop the descent of White Tiger True Lord.

On his side, they now had an additional Ascendant. On Luo Chen's side, however, Li Canghai had fled the battlefield.

"Luo Chen, your death is truly at hand!"

Liancheng laughed aloud, making no attempt to conceal his joy.

Yet, for some reason, apart from the initial shock and surprise on Luo Chen's face, he seemed to catch a glimpse of unexpected delight.

Though he did not understand why, Liancheng already knew what he had to do next.

Ke Lianshan, breaking through to the Ascendant stage within the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, would not be disturbed by the outside world. His safety was sufficiently guaranteed.

But the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven was, after all, only a small grotto-heaven world, formed from the remains of a powerful being after its fall.

Being inside it, one could not break through the shackles of a living being's upper limit.

This was also why some families of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect cultivated spiritual medicines within it. Yet those spiritual medicines, after growing to the fourth or fifth tier, could not properly gain sentience.

Spiritual intelligence was the upper limit of plants and herbs.

The soul was the shackle of the human race!

Therefore, Ke Lianshan had to borrow the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard to open an internal and external passage, drawing in the origin qi of the outside world.

But now, Heaven-Earth Island had become the battlefield between himself and Luo Chen. The origin qi of this region's void was in violent turmoil, which would greatly affect the other party's time to break through to Ascendant.

"Whether for the smooth operation of the God-Descending Formation or to allow Ke Lianshan to break through to Ascendant, I must lure Luo Chen away, or rather... slay him!"

Liancheng's heart hardened. He interlocked his hands, forming a vertical "mouth" shape. His fingertips extended outward, and four fingers lengthened, adding four more corners outside the "mouth." In an instant, it formed a "well" character.

Though this "well" character was small, it enveloped the distant Luo Chen within it.

For a moment, the originally turbulent origin qi of heaven and earth suddenly fell silent.

Luo Chen's heart stirred. It was that familiar feeling again.

Just now, when he had wanted to slash and sever the starlight pillar of the God-Descending Formation, he had been affected by this very feeling, causing his sword strike to go astray.

Was it the earth-attribute divine power that Liancheng had mastered?

Luo Chen mobilized the power of the fire origin, wanting to reclaim dominance over the origin qi of heaven and earth, but with little effect.

However, the Withering-Bloom True Flame within his body stirred eagerly.

*Can you do it?*

Luo Chen understood. As fellow true meanings of the laws, the Withering-Bloom True Intent, being wood-attributed, did indeed subtly restrain the other party's divine power.

But Luo Chen did not resist this move. He merely sneered as he looked at Liancheng.

The next moment.

Space and time shifted!

The two figures abruptly appeared above the vast, boundless sea.

In the distance was the unreachable Heaven-Earth Island, like a tiny black dot.

Behind them was the Eastern Origin Continent, close at hand. Under the night sky, flames shot into the sky, and smoke rose from all directions.

Luo Chen gripped his long sword and sneered, "You moved the battlefield here. Aren't you afraid that the residual waves of our battle will destroy your sect's ancestral court?"

Then, without waiting for Liancheng to reply, he muttered to himself, "That's right! From the moment you used the pretext of condensing the Central Continent while actually absorbing the spiritual mechanisms of the four continents, you stopped caring about the survival of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. Otherwise, why would you ignore the group of experts I led as they charged into the Eastern Origin Continent?"

Liancheng's expression darkened.

Clearly, Luo Chen's words had torn away the hypocrisy on his surface.

He had indeed thought this way.

The reason was also simple. Back then, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect of the Mountains and Seas Realm had risen from nothing. In the future, even if it was destroyed, it could be rebuilt.

As long as he himself still existed, as long as White Tiger True Lord could descend into this realm and truly obliterate the great enemy, the Demon Phoenix Rosycloud!

"I have no more patience for you. Accept your fate!"

Liancheng let out a low shout. He raised his hand and flung it, and a yellow sand bag flew up from behind him.

Strands of golden-yellow fine sand spewed forth from within, and a fierce wind arose with it.

In an instant, sand and stones flew, and heaven and earth became a blur of dim yellow.

Luo Chen took a deep breath. The long sword in his hand continuously scattered sword light.

But the previously sharp and unmatched sword light—enough to sever the heavenly light pillar, enough to cut through the continent of Heaven-Earth Island with one strike—sank into the flying sand like a mud oxen entering the sea, completely losing its force.

Not only that, but every grain of flying sand that struck his body consumed a large amount of his protective qi shield.

Even the newly refined Mystic Dust Armor, under this relentless scouring, began to waver and tremble, revealing cracks.

"A spirit treasure?"

"No, not a spirit treasure."

"It's a magic treasure combined with special materials and restrictions, forming a unique space."

"To break this thing..."

Luo Chen's hand, which had been tightly gripping the Yuantu sword, loosened slightly.

That one move could not be used as easily as before. He had not yet mastered it sufficiently.

But aside from that move, he was not without other means.

A flash before his eyes, and a crystalline coral jade fan fell into his hand.

It was the pseudo-spirit treasure Blood Jade Three-Flame Fan bestowed by Rosycloud!

Within it, a large amount of residual Nirvana Sacred Flame from the Nether Abyss had already been absorbed. Once released, its power would be considerable.

But that was far from enough!

Luo Chen's mind stirred, and the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire appeared once more!

"Go in!"

The Withering-Bloom Divine Fire seemed to sense something. It was reluctant, but in the end, it dove into the Blood Jade Three-Flame Fan.

Not only that, but Luo Chen's expression turned ruthless. A wisp of deep blue flame appeared at his fingertip.

After appearing, this deep blue flame transformed into a small blue fish.

It was the Shark Emperor True Flame obtained from the Arctic Yemo Heaven!

Originally, this object had a mixed will. Even after being nurtured by the sharkfolk race for countless years, it had been unable to break through to the fifth tier.

But under Luo Chen's powerful fire control ability and meticulous purification, the Shark Emperor True Flame had become extremely pure. After decades, it was only one step away from advancing to the fifth tier.

But now, there was no time left to let it continue growing.

"You go in too!"

The blue fish fire spirit let out a mournful cry and, without looking back, charged into the jade fan.

Three powerful flames converged together, fusing into an extremely destructive force. Even Luo Chen could not control it.

Let alone this pseudo-spirit treasure?

A scorching power began to boil. A destructive aura began to spread.

Liancheng, who was manipulating the yellow sand bag, sensed this scene and could not help but feel terrified. What exactly was the other party doing?

The next moment!

Amidst the hazy flying sand, the black-robed Daoist lightly waved his jade fan.

*Boom!*

Three-colored light and flame poured forth.

Transforming into an overwhelming, earth-shattering force, it swept in all directions.

Flying sand and stones were all turned to ashes.

The yellow sand bag burst apart abruptly.

Before Liancheng could react, his magic treasure was broken, and his mind was backlashed. In a moment of daze, he saw the three-colored light and flame rushing toward him like a torrent of fire.

He raised both hands in front of his face. A jade pendant transformed into an unbreakable city wall.

However!

The moment they made contact.

*Boom!*

The three-colored light and flame passed through in an instant.

The terrifyingly high temperature, in a single breath, incinerated his physical body into charcoal.

A black figure followed closely behind. He pointed his fingers like a sword and thrust out.

"Withering-Bloom!"

The Withering-Bloom Dao Finger landed from afar on the charcoal-like flesh. A gray-white light enveloped the entire body.

That charcoal body began to visibly shatter, like a failed porcelain doll.

*Crack!*

Liancheng's physical body suddenly broke apart. Around his massive primordial spirit, a weak blue fish was biting, and a resilient, unyielding green-white small tree was climbing and spreading its branches.

But Liancheng's primordial spirit paid them no heed. It struck out with a palm toward the front.

"Overturn the Earth!"

A divine power, another divine power!

After mastering the divine power of *Horizon Neighbor* for shifting through the void, Liancheng had also mastered a highly lethal divine power.

Such was the foundation of a great cultivator of the Dao Sect!

With just this one palm, it heavily stamped onto Luo Chen's chest as he pursued closely behind.

In his pursuit of a one-hit kill, Luo Chen had consecutively swung the Blood Jade Three-Flame Fan and released the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger, leaving his defenses wide open and unable to respond to this move.

He only felt heaven and earth change, boundless origin qi pressing down mightily.

Unable to dodge, he could only endure!

The Mystic Dust Armor spread over his entire body. His physical form suddenly expanded a thousandfold. Furthermore, his lifebound magic treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, moved with his will, spreading a five-colored halo to envelop him.

All of this happened in an instant.

Then came...

*Boom!*

The black-robed Daoist, like a falling meteor from the heavens, plummeted heavily into the boundless ocean.

The power of Liancheng's palm did not stop there. Using his pure primordial spirit to connect with heaven and earth and unleash this move had already reached his limit.

After the palm landed, not only did it strike down Luo Chen, but its residual might also forcibly pressed a terrifying, horrifying five-fingered palm print into the great sea.

Like a chain reaction, boundless giant waves surged up, crashing toward the surroundings.

The Eastern Origin Continent, being too close, bore the brunt!

The wildly surging giant waves directly rushed into the inland.

A terrifying tsunami swept across the entire Eastern Origin Continent. Countless living beings, caught off guard, were swept into it.

Liancheng could not care about this. His primordial spirit manifested externally, panting heavily, a look of lingering fear on his face.

His divine soul shook, scattering the chaotic golden flames.

Although the Nirvana Sacred Flame was powerful, this portion of it was not nurtured by Luo Chen himself. It was gathered from the remnants left by Rosycloud in the Nether Abyss. After over a hundred years, it had lost much of its spirituality, so dispersing it was relatively simple.

Then, he grabbed with both hands.

The Withering-Bloom Fire Tree was quite clever. It had realized what would happen long before entering the Blood Jade Three-Flame Fan. Therefore, it immediately fell into the sea, dodging this grab.

But the blue fish fire spirit was not so lucky.

It was directly caught in Liancheng's hand.

"I never imagined I would be pushed to such a state by an Initiate cultivator!"

Liancheng's heart still trembled with fear. Then, he fiercely squeezed the blue fish fire spirit.

*Pop!*

Like a bubble bursting, the blue fish fire spirit dissipated into the void amidst a mournful cry, leaving only faint ripples of firelight.

The massive primordial spirit stood between heaven and earth, but it did not appear imposing. Instead, a large amount of gray-white light continuously spread and eroded over its body.

With this erosion, the originally youthful appearance of the primordial spirit's face constantly shifted between youth and old age, an eerie sight.

The mana on Liancheng's body continuously tried to stop the erosion of the gray-white light, but this power was like a clinging bone, extremely difficult to remove quickly.

"A wood-attribute divine power that most restrains the earth origin?"

"If I had known this, I should not have been careless and only used the yellow sand bag to deal with him. I should have directly used the Burial Bone Umbrella."

"Fortunately, he is ultimately not an Ascendant. Having taken my Overturn the Earth Divine Palm head-on, even gods and immortals would find it hard to save him!"

His divine sense searched the heavens and scoured the earth, plunging into the ocean, but could no longer find any trace of Luo Chen's aura.

Letting out a rough breath, Liancheng finally felt relieved.

Although his physical body was destroyed and his primordial spirit was injured, for an Ascendant cultivator, as long as the primordial spirit remained, a mere fleshly shell was nothing. With some effort and materials afterward, he could condense a new one.

The great enemy was eliminated. Next...

Liancheng turned to look toward Heaven-Earth Island. With just one glance, he was filled with disbelief!

At the entrance to the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, the origin qi vortex that had been forming and condensing had, for some reason, begun to violently destabilize.

This meant that Ke Lianshan's advancement to Ascendant was about to fail!

This was impossible!

Having come this far, with Ke Lianshan's thousand years of accumulation, the countless Ascendant experiences of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, and the assistance of the spirit treasure Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard, how could he fail at the moment of success?

If he failed, and without himself presiding over the God-Descending Formation, then the descent of White Tiger True Lord...

The heavenly light pillar connecting to the cosmos was trembling violently. A large amount of light began to dim, as if everything was about to be declared a failure.

At that moment.

A pair of white-golden hands reached out from within the passage, firmly grasping this world.

A Boundless Realm True Lord, wanting to forcibly descend into this realm!

Seeing this scene, Liancheng's hanging heart fell back into place.

"As expected of White Tiger True Lord! How could he miss this opportunity? Once he truly descends into this realm, the severely injured Rosycloud will have no way out but death. Moreover, with his help, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect can unify the Mountains and Seas Realm!"

Just as Liancheng was relaxing and indulging in his thoughts, a clear, piercing cry suddenly echoed between heaven and earth.

A Luan's cry split the void, tearing through the long night.

A beam of cyan light broke through the air, leaped over Heaven-Earth Island, and charged without hesitation into the light pillar of the God-Descending Formation.

"Wretch, how dare you!"
