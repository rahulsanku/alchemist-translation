# Chapter 1009: One Hundred Thousand Ghost Cultivators Aid Cultivation, Moonlight Clears the Heart to Condense the Primordial Spirit

That the Dan Saint's Blessed Land contained spiritual opportunity was no secret to Luo Chen.

Back when the Dan Saint Hall had opened, and Luo Chen had cultivated the *Heaven-Refining Art* within the blessed land, he had personally verified this fact.

Perhaps the quantity could not compare to the vast heavens and earth outside, but it was more than enough for refining pills.

At this moment, as Luo Chen executed the *Heaven-Refining Art*, the Dan Saint's Blessed Land seemed to tremble, and a wisp of hazy spiritual opportunity drifted over faintly.

With his Scarlet Eyes and Golden Pupils, which had grown increasingly bright from cultivation, Luo Chen could see it with perfect clarity.

"It is said that Ascendant cultivators must cultivate in places abundant with spiritual qi, seeking spiritual opportunity, comprehending deeper layers of law true intent, and even constructing their Law Manifestations to roam the ocean of origin qi. If they were to master a wondrous alchemy art like the *Heaven-Refining Art*, could they perhaps be free from the constraints of fifth-rank spiritual vein locations, able to cultivate anytime and anywhere?"

This thought flashed by in an instant, and Luo Chen focused his attention on the alchemy work at hand.

That wisp of spiritual opportunity silently submerged into the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

With the infusion of this wisp of spiritual opportunity, the medicinal liquid that had been reacting violently began to coalesce rapidly.

Luo Chen split his attention, keeping an eye on the formation of the *Enlightenment Pill* while carefully observing any changes in the Primordial Unity Cauldron itself.

The formation of the *Enlightenment Pill* was proceeding very smoothly, as if the medicinal liquid naturally resonated with the spiritual opportunity, and the speed had even increased.

As for the Primordial Unity Cauldron... everything was normal!

Luo Chen frowned. Though puzzled in his heart, he did not let down his guard.

But even after three full days had passed, and a rich pill fragrance had begun to emanate from within the cauldron, he still detected nothing unusual.

However, the disciples watching below, upon smelling the pill fragrance that had diffused out, all held their breath in anticipation.

Luo Chen did not keep them waiting long.

When he sensed the opportune moment, he used his unique pill-collecting technique to finish the process.

Streams of light flew out from within the cauldron, one after another.

One pill, two pills, three pills... by the fifth pill, the cauldron was finally empty.

Qu Lingjun, holding a jade plate below, heard a series of *ding-dong* sounds from within the plate. Looking down, he saw five round, golden-yellow pills resting quietly inside.

"It succeeded?"

He murmured in disbelief, "On the very first try, it was a success?"

This miracle instantly ignited the atmosphere at the scene.

Many found it hard to believe. Even though they had contributed to completing the pill formula for the *Enlightenment Pill*, they still could not believe it had succeeded on the first attempt.

Some even thought that even if the Dan Saint Chu Yan of yesteryear were alive, he could not have succeeded so easily!

Yet the Supreme Elder had refined five pills so calmly and naturally, as if it were the most ordinary thing in the world.

Luo Chen cast a *Cleansing Art*, simply washing the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Facing the fervent gazes of the crowd, he shook his head with a light smile.

"I was somewhat fortunate. To succeed on the first try."

"However, although the pills are formed, their medicinal power is not yet stable. To reach a state where they can be consumed, they must be nurtured in a place abundant with spiritual qi for at least a few years, so that the medicinal power can be internalized."

Even though he said this, it could not shake the admiration of the assembled disciples.

Luo Chen paid it no mind, instead beginning to prepare for the second batch of *Enlightenment Pills*.

With the Luo Tian Sect's foundation, plus all the years Deng Taiyue had spent gathering materials, the stock of medicinal herbs for the *Enlightenment Pill* was quite substantial.

It would be a waste to leave them sitting idle, so he might as well strike while the iron was hot and refine them all in one go!

Unfortunately, the subsequent refinings met with failure one after another.

The second time, everything went smoothly at first, but when it came time to absorb the spiritual opportunity, the medicinal liquid could not coalesce. He could only watch helplessly as the liquid turned into dregs under the scorching true fire.

The third and fourth times were the same.

Three consecutive failures, each occurring at the exact same point. Even with Luo Chen's iron will, he could not help but feel frustrated.

The disciples, however, had no complaints. What they saw was the Supreme Elder's terrifyingly stable alchemy technique, with every step executed to perfection.

Even if the final result was unsuccessful, it was merely a matter of lacking a bit of luck.

And refining pills itself required a measure of luck, let alone the famous, long-lost *Enlightenment Pill*.

Luo Chen had no time to pay attention to their thoughts, immersing himself entirely in each subsequent refining session.

Gradually, Luo Chen began to understand.

The problem might not lie with the pill formula or the medicinal liquid, but with the *Heaven-Refining Art* itself.

The process of drawing in spiritual opportunity required circulating the true intent of the laws, allowing the mind to merge with heaven and earth. A portion of his attention had been diverted to observing the Primordial Unity Cauldron, causing him to frequently miss the optimal moment.

As for the success of the first attempt... it was truly a stroke of luck!

Therefore, Luo Chen stopped observing the Primordial Unity Cauldron and focused entirely on the alchemy.

Although the fifth attempt also failed, out of the next seven refinings, he succeeded twice!

When Qu Lingjun excitedly presented the jade plate, Luo Chen's gaze fell upon the fifteen golden pills within, and he felt a moment of daze.

All the medicinal herbs had supported a total of twelve refining sessions.

And the number of successes was fully three!

This success rate had already completely surpassed the proficiency of an entry-level master.

In other words, he had broken the normal pattern of the proficiency panel.

This kind of situation had occurred before, but at most, it meant one extra success.

This time, however, there were three full successes!

"Is it a qualitative change brought about by my Grandmaster-level alchemy, or a boosting effect from the Primordial Unity Cauldron?"

Luo Chen stroked one of the golden pills. He could not yet judge its grade, but all fifteen were at least at the fourth rank.

He exhaled a breath of turbid qi. "Take them to Divine Elegance Peak for nurturing! Min Longyu has already set up the best spirit-gathering formation there. He will personally look after this batch of *Enlightenment Pills*."

"Yes, Master!"

Qu Lingjun nodded respectfully, then led the mentally exhausted disciples away one by one.

The Dan Mountain, which had been bustling for over a year, finally settled into tranquility.

Luo Chen's expression remained calm, but the doubt in his heart lingered. He stared silently at the Primordial Unity Cauldron, which he had not yet put away.

"Senior Brother Luo Chen, your alchemy this time was a great success. Why do you still seem somewhat gloomy?"

A graceful, gentle voice came from beside him. Luo Chen did not turn around, but he had long since noticed Canglong.

He pursed his lips and reached out to retrieve the Primordial Unity Cauldron, which was still radiating residual warmth.

"It's just some doubts regarding my cultivation. Nothing to be gloomy about."

Turning around, Luo Chen looked at Canglong with curiosity. "Junior Sister, is there something you need?"

This time, it was Canglong's turn to hesitate.

Luo Chen looked at her with confusion. The woman opened her mouth slightly, then closed it again. She repeated this several times but still did not speak.

This kind of indecisive behavior was not like the Canglong Fairy he knew.

He could only smile and say, "If there's anything you need help with, just say it directly. If you don't speak now, who knows how long it will be before you see me next time."

Canglong was startled. "You only just returned. Are you leaving again?"

Luo Chen flicked his sleeves, as if brushing away the smoke and fire accumulated from days of alchemy.

He walked towards the Dan Chen Hall, speaking as he went. "I will leave, but not right now. Next, I will go into a deep seclusion for a period of time. All the major and minor affairs within the sect have already been arranged."

Having said this, he blinked. "Junior Sister, are you truly not going to say anything?"

Canglong bit her pretty little lip.

Luo Chen smiled. "Then I'll see you next time!"

With these words, Luo Chen stepped into the quiet Dan Chen Hall.

With a single wave of his sleeve, the two thick doors slowly closed.

Canglong watched his gradually disappearing back. Somehow, her shoulders drooped, and she seemed to let out a sigh of relief.

...

Back inside the Dan Chen Hall, Luo Chen did not immediately begin his secluded cultivation. Instead, he first had a good, long sleep.

This sleep lasted a full three days and three nights.

When he woke up, he felt refreshed and full of energy.

The exhaustion brought on by the recent alchemy was completely swept away.

Inside the great hall.

Luo Chen sat cross-legged on a cushion and sent a stream of mana into the hall's central hub.

The next moment...

*Weng!*

With a dull sound, the slightly dim hall instantly became radiant.

Lines of formation patterns flickered, nodes began to spew spiritual qi, and the spiritual qi of the entire Dan Mountain began to converge on the Dan Chen Hall. Even several nearby large spiritual veins began to supply spiritual qi to this location.

This arrangement had been personally set up by Min Longyu and his team during the decade or so Luo Chen had been away, all to provide Luo Chen with the best possible cultivation environment.

Luo Chen slightly sensed the concentration of spiritual qi, and a look of satisfaction appeared on his face.

Although it was far inferior to the fourth-rank top-grade cave dwelling on Green Parasol Mountain, it was still at the fourth-rank mid-grade level.

"But still, it's not enough!"

With a thought, Luo Chen took out the Ten-Thousand-Formation Gu and set up another powerful spirit-gathering formation.

With two grand formations working together, the speed and range of qi extraction increased greatly.

With Dan Mountain at the center, the spiritual veins within a radius of ten thousand li, as if responding to a summons, all began to supply vast amounts of spiritual qi to this hall.

"With this concentration, it's comparable to a fourth-rank top-grade cave dwelling!"

After doing all this, Luo Chen took out the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

*Clang!*

His gaze fell upon the grey cauldron, and a contemplative look appeared on his face.

"Should I refine the Netherwater Spring Eye now?"

Hesitating for a moment, Luo Chen decided to give it a try first.

He mobilized his mana, and a vigorous blaze of flame enveloped the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Inside the cauldron's space, the quiet spring eye seemed to have encountered its natural enemy, beginning to tremble violently.

Refining a spring eye did not mean wearing it down to nothing. It meant wearing down the nascent will within it, then replacing it with one's own divine sense.

Only then could one completely master the Netherwater Spring Eye.

The Withering-Bloom Divine Flame was merely an external threat. The true focus of the refinement was on the soul!

Without hesitation, Luo Chen's eight divided souls, working in concert with his main soul, simultaneously exerted force, unleashing their divine sense and charging into the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

In an instant, Luo Chen's expression froze.

Time passed quickly.

After about the time it takes to burn an incense stick, a flush suddenly appeared on Luo Chen's stiff face.

*Pfft!*

He spat out a mouthful of blood. The flames blazed fiercely, but Luo Chen's eyes snapped open.

Wiping the blood from the corner of his mouth, Luo Chen's expression was somewhat gloomy.

Failure!

It was within reason, yet also unexpected.

With the Primordial Unity Cauldron suppressing it, the Netherwater Spring Eye could not escape.

But the moment he tried to wear down its will, it would launch a massive counterattack. That will even tried to defile and assimilate his own soul.

Luo Chen, unwilling to accept defeat, clashed head-on with his vast soul foundation, but he was always a step behind.

"Is this the reason the Nether Ghost Emperor failed?"

If the soul's strength was insufficient, no matter how great the quantity, one could not wear down a will that seemed born of heaven and earth.

Luo Chen fell back into thought.

After a long time, the gloom on his face gradually faded.

He took a top-grade *Great Soul Pill* that he had kept in the Luo Tian Sect, restoring his soul foundation. His expression gradually improved.

"Since my strength is insufficient, I'll strengthen it further!"

"If divine sense won't do, then I'll use divine thought. If the Sage infant won't do, then I'll use the primordial spirit!"

"After all, I already have a cultivation plan for this!"

As Luo Chen murmured to himself, he began to recite a Buddhist sutra.

It was the *Moonlight Clears the Heart Mantra* that Master Xuandu of the Pure Glass Pure Land had taught him. This mantra could help him wash his soul and forcibly condense his primordial spirit.

As he chanted, the eight divided souls in his sea of consciousness began to merge with his main soul. In an instant, they formed a towering golden giant, as if it were splitting heaven and earth.

This was Luo Chen's complete main soul!

If this Sage infant could fully merge with this soul, it would form the primordial spirit that countless Sage cultivators dreamed of, leaving them only one step away from the Ascendant realm!

With a thought, the barrier of his Purple Mansion opened wide, and his Sage infant emerged, flying out from the crown of his head.

The infant Sage floated above Luo Chen's head, its large eyes curiously observing this world.

Countless golden lights diffused from Luo Chen's sea of consciousness, beginning to envelop the Sage infant, attempting a fusion.

But as the forced fusion progressed, the infant Sage's expression grew more and more pained.

"Pain?"

"Is the Sage infant not strong enough to bear the soul? Or are there too many distracting thoughts in the soul, preventing absolute fusion?"

Thoughts flickered through Luo Chen's mind, and then he made a decision.

With a wave of his hand, a stream of light flew out and hovered above the Sage infant.

It was the *Mortal Fate Immortal Mirror* he had painstakingly refined back then.

When he had originally refined this item, besides treating Canglong, Luo Chen had also intended to use it to temper his soul.

However, later on, with the help of the *Great Soul Pill* and the large amount of soul stones he had obtained from the Soul-Breaking Cliff, he had long since had no need for the *Mortal Fate Immortal Mirror's* meager tempering effects.

But now, the *Mortal Fate Immortal Mirror* finally had its use!

Rays of light sprinkled down. The infant Sage seemed to fall into a dream, and even the pain of the soul fusion seemed to lessen.

Amidst the pervading golden light, the fusion of the two accelerated.

However, a few days later, Luo Chen let out a muffled groan and was forced to forcibly interrupt the fusion process.

With a wave of his hand, the treasure mirror fell into his palm. Luo Chen shook his head.

"The illusions brought by a magic treasure are, after all, just illusions. To achieve fusion, I must rely on the *Moonlight Clears the Heart Mantra*."

Yes, he had been cultivating the *Moonlight Clears the Heart Mantra* for too short a time, and his attainment was insufficient. That was why he could not fuse and form his primordial spirit.

"In that case..."

A glint of thought appeared in Luo Chen's eyes, and a fierce look emerged.

If he were to face an Ascendant in the future, he had to ensure he had no weaknesses.

Whether it was divine abilities, magic treasures, his physical body, or his primordial spirit.

He had to master the *Moonlight Clears the Heart Mantra* in a short period of time, reaching a level where he could condense his primordial spirit.

Therefore, he had to take a shortcut!

And Luo Chen had already arranged this shortcut for himself.

The Sage infant returned to his mansion, and the soul returned to its place.

The treasure mirror hung above the hall, and the true words of the Buddha flowed from his mouth.

A large banner unfurled with a *boom*, centered on Luo Chen.

In an instant, gusts of dark wind arose, and countless ghostly wails and howls assailed the Dan Chen Hall, even passing through the hall to affect the entire Dan Mountain.

Luo Chen looked at the ghostly cries and soul-shaking sounds rushing towards him in the dark wind, a look of utter determination on his face.

This *Soul-Nourishing Banner* held all the ghostly beings that had been transferred from the *Soul-Refining Banner*.

Among them were no fewer than dozens of Ghost Emperor-level existences!

Although they were suppressed by the banner, unable to escape or use various techniques, their normal instinctive soul attacks were unaffected.

Now that he was inside the *Soul-Nourishing Banner*, their resentment at being imprisoned had a target to vent upon.

For anyone with a slightly weaker soul, they would likely have their soul scattered and their spirit destroyed under the first wave of impact.

But Luo Chen stood as immovable as a mountain.

His eight divided souls reappeared, the *Prison Suppression Soul Art* protected his sea of consciousness, and the *Mortal Fate Immortal Mirror* unfolded layers of illusion formations, concealing his true body.

Luo Chen took a deep breath and declared boldly.

"All of you, assist me in my cultivation!"

At these arrogant words, all the ghostly beings became enraged, unleashing even stronger soul attacks.

This confrontation was no longer a contest of spells or magic treasures, but the purest and most dangerous battle of souls.

A crushing pressure rolled in like a tidal wave, but Luo Chen remained unmoved. However, the mantra he chanted grew faster and faster.

He calmed the heart-monkey, tethered the mind-horse, and under the moonlight that cleared his heart, he finally reached a state of no-thought.

And on the attribute panel, the proficiency of the *Moonlight Clears the Heart Mantra* began to skyrocket at a visible rate.

(End of this chapter)
