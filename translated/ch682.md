# Chapter 682: Bright Radiance Heaven, the Inheritance Land

Massive spiritual qi poured backward, forming a heaven-filling spiritual qi tornado.

A figure floated within it, sometimes rising, sometimes sinking, sometimes high, sometimes low, as helpless as duckweed.

His body and soul, comparable to an archaic beast, allowed him to withstand the terrifying impact of spiritual qi with difficulty, preventing him from being torn apart at the first moment.

But he could find neither the road ahead nor the road back.

At this moment, a stream of pure magic power faintly spread out from the wooden tablet at his chest, merging into the spiritual qi tornado like ripples.

It was a Sage domain!

With the domain added to his body, the spiritual qi around him remained violent, but there was already a calm place amid the storm.

Luo Chen's body suddenly paused and stabilized. A thoughtful look appeared in his eyes.

"So that is how it is."

If one wanted to enter the inheritance land through this spiritual qi tornado, there was a trick to it. One had to possess a method similar to a Sage domain to follow the current downward. Only then would one avoid being torn to pieces by the violent scouring of spiritual qi.

Thinking to this point, he had Han Zhan withdraw the Sage domain and unfolded his own All-Encompassing Fire Prison.

As expected, after the All-Encompassing Fire Prison appeared, he could likewise move freely within the spiritual qi tornado.

However!

Because his All-Encompassing Fire Prison relied on his own power rather than borrowing the spiritual qi of heaven and earth outside, the price of moving freely was the rapid consumption of his own magic power.

"If other Golden Core cultivators want to enter Bright Radiance Heaven, they either need to possess a pseudo-domain method like mine, or they must rely on the protection of a Sage Sage, or else, like Moya Cave Master, refine a targeted magic treasure in advance."

After understanding this point, Luo Chen no longer forced it.

"Senior, you should do it."

"Mm."

The All-Encompassing Fire Prison withdrew, and Han Zhan's Sage domain unfolded once more.

Within the faint green light, Luo Chen steadily descended.

After an unknown amount of time, Luo Chen's feet touched the ground. He had reached the deepest part of the spiritual qi tornado.

Where he landed was a stone platform.

"A teleportation formation?"

The thought had just emerged when the formation activated on its own.

Luo Chen's body stood like a mountain, unmoving. He only felt light and shadow flowing around him, while his body seemed to be on the verge of being torn apart.

When that feeling dissipated, he had already arrived in an unknown place.

An ancient stone platform hung alone in the night sky.

Beneath his feet, all was dim and dark, with no bottom in sight.

Outside the stone platform, an unnamed spotless wind blew without pause.

Everywhere he looked was lonely and desolate. Only fourteen light sources, like stars, scattered radiance.

No!

Rather than fourteen, it would be better to say thirteen. One of them was so dim that it gave the feeling that the road ahead had already been cut off.

Just as Luo Chen was observing his surroundings, the wooden tablet at his chest trembled slightly and detached on its own.

"Senior?"

The soul-nourishing wood tablet hovered in the air, emitting a dim light.

The next moment, a human figure emerged. It was another refined corpse made by Adept Red Corpse.

There had been two in total. One had melted inside Vermilion Bird Mountain, leaving only this one.

Han Zhan's soul-nourishing wood tablet landed on the refined corpse. After a short moment, the corpse, which had originally been deathly still, showed a feeling of agility.

Its eyelids trembled. It slowly opened its eyes, revealing an aged, weathered meaning.

"It has been so long, so very long, since I felt my feet touching solid ground."

Han Zhan possessed the refined corpse and stamped his feet, rather cheerful and delighted.

He looked at Luo Chen. "Give me the Seven Baleful Swords and the Myriad Souls Banner. Without handy tools on me, if we truly encounter battle, I can at least help you a little."

Luo Chen gave a sound of assent. He took down the Soul Heaven Sword Case containing the Seven Baleful Swords from his back, then handed the Myriad Souls Banner to him as well.

With these two magic treasures assisting him, Han Zhan finally had some fighting strength.

While Han Zhan familiarized himself with the refined corpse body, Luo Chen observed the surroundings.

At last, he slowly raised his head and looked toward the enormous stone wall surrounding the platform.

At the very front, three words were written.

Night Bright Radiance.

This was the origin of the name Bright Radiance Heaven, the third heaven of Demon-Fall Land.

Luo Chen did not understand its meaning. His gaze slowly moved back, and a small poem entered his eyes.

Nineteen paths of heaven and earth, speech turns even and strange.

Human hearts have no countable place; even masters sometimes lose.

The shape of power flies far as meteors; the sound dries, and falling geese come late.

Before the window, one game is only begun, and the cold sun again hangs west.

"This is a poem about Go," Han Zhan said slowly after walking over and glancing at it.

Luo Chen naturally understood as well, pondering its meaning.

Its general idea was simple. It used chess as a metaphor for people, speaking of the world's rough roads and the unfathomable human heart. Circumstances changed constantly; with the slightest inattention, a lifetime might already have been wasted.

The poetic meaning was not difficult. What was strange was why this small poem had been placed at the entrance to Bright Radiance Heaven.

On this, Han Zhan could not give a better answer either.

"We should look at the path ahead, lest Tai Sui catch up in a while."

"Does he dare enter?" Luo Chen asked.

Han Zhan chuckled. "I fear he does dare. Did you not hear what he said? Before leaving Demon-Fall Land, he cannot merge with his main body. The shackles that caused this should be some method Devil Lord Heaven-Refining placed upon his body. If he acts contrary to that and maintains the separation between soul and body, he likely will not be restricted."

Luo Chen opened his mouth, but in the end had no retort.

His gaze landed on the fourteen star points emitting light, and he slowly said,

"These should be the exits."

Han Zhan stepped forward. "At the same time, they are also the entrances to the inheritance lands!"

Luo Chen stepped forward and stood shoulder to shoulder with him, glancing at him.

"You want to obtain an inheritance?"

"If you did not want to, why would you enter?" Han Zhan asked in return.

Luo Chen shrugged. "I cannot go back. Falling Demon Abyss is blocked by Tai Sui's avatars. Even if I break out, Yue Canglan has Fallen Abyss Dragon Sect, and Hundred Crow Ghost Forest has two great fifth-tier enlightened spirits blocking the road..."

"Heh."

Han Zhan chuckled and did not expose Luo Chen's true inner thoughts.

He walked around the fourteen points of light, speaking as he walked. "Tai Sui once said that Devil Lord Heaven-Refining was the most formidable Void Refining True Lord he knew, and had once displayed more than ten kinds of law power."

"Leaving aside whether those words were exaggerated, judging only from the arrangement of Five Elements Heaven, this man's ability was indeed world-shaking, astonishing throughout ancient and modern times."

"And the inheritance left by such an existence must have its purpose. Its contents are also absolutely extraordinary!"

"If we can obtain even one or two parts of it, we will benefit for life, and perhaps even have hope of advancing to Ascendant!"

When he reached this point, his steps stopped beside the dimmest star point.

"Here, the starlight is dim, and the path ahead is cut off. This means someone has walked it, and the inheritance has also been taken. Luo Chen, who do you think obtained this inheritance?"

Luo Chen frowned. He subconsciously wanted to say Yuan Demon Sect, but the words stopped at his lips.

In his mind, a name subconsciously surfaced.

The only present human Ascendant power of the Northern Sea, Li Canghai!

According to legend, he had only luckily broken through to Ascendant after walking out of the Sunken Sea.

There were also rumors that he had obtained a formation inheritance in the ruins of the Sunken Sea.

Looking back on their journey through Demon-Fall Land, every place was indeed filled with formations, all of them exquisite beyond compare.

If Li Canghai truly obtained Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's core formation inheritance, then advancing to Ascendant seemed only natural.

"Li Canghai, alliance leader of Cang Hai Alliance."

He spoke the name.

Han Zhan smiled and nodded. "The same as I thought."

Then his smile faded, and he said solemnly, "Since he could do it, why can we not?"

As he spoke, his steps moved again, finally stopping before an entrance where starlight flickered.

A powerful attractive force rose naturally in his heart.

It was as though this place was the one most suitable for him to enter.

The only thing that made him hesitate was probably whether a refined corpse body could allow him to obtain the true inheritance.

If he failed, would he fall inside?

Hesitation, uncertainty, and eagerness stirred together.

While he lingered, Luo Chen also began to walk.

Thirteen paths. Which one should he take?

Just as Han Zhan thought, how could he not covet these inheritances deep in his heart?

He, Luo Chen, was a wandering cultivator without a master.

Everything he had was gained through his own effort.

But the things circulating on the market of the cultivation world were ultimately not truly impressive goods.

Across his entire body and possessions, the truly formidable things amounted to only two or three.

The Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture was the most important. It had improved his cultivation speed and came from Green Parasol Mountain. It was very likely the cultivation method of Rosycloud.

His life-bound magic treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, came from Green Pill Valley, but its upper limit had been forged jointly for him by Heaven Smelter, Han Zhan, and Azure Frost, three great Sages.

Even the Mystic Dust Armor and the second Origin Pill came from Penglai Immortal Sect and Falling Cloud Sect.

It could be said that the truly formidable things were all in the hands of great sects, great powers, and great figures.

And now, a generation's Void Refining True Lord was far "greater" than all people and all powers in the Mountains and Seas Realm.

If he could obtain even one or two parts, perhaps it would be exactly as Han Zhan said, something he could "benefit from for life."

Then which path should he choose?

Luo Chen walked slowly, sensing the connection between himself and those star points.

One, two, three!

Among the fourteen star points, three emitted an attractive force that moved his heart.

But one place could not be chosen.

That was the road that likely represented the formation inheritance.

Only two places remained.

"What do these two places represent?"

Just as Luo Chen hesitated, the stone platform behind him trembled slightly.

A figure slowly appeared.

A fat body, a thin face, and eyes filled with monstrous fury.

It was Tai Sui!

"Luo Chen, I will go one step ahead."

Han Zhan's voice transmission arrived. Then he made his decision and stepped into the starlight.

The reason he was so decisive was simple.

Tai Sui emitted a terrifying aura that completely surpassed early-stage Sages.

Such an existence was no longer something he and Luo Chen could fight even if they joined hands.

Luo Chen's expression changed slightly. Finally, at the starry entrance with the strongest attraction to him, he made his decision.

He nodded to Tai Sui, then stepped inside.

At this moment, the formation gradually faded, and Tai Sui strode forward.

But on the lone hanging stone platform, there was already no figure of the old acquaintance.

Tai Sui clenched his teeth, his eyes blood-red.

He stepped out and arrived before the starry entrance Luo Chen had entered. Just as he was about to chase directly inside, his foot stopped in midair.

"What Master left behind is an opportunity for others, but how is it not one for me as well?"

"The inheritance lands lead to the same destination by different paths. As long as I get out quickly enough, I will always meet that kid again... He is called Luo Chen, not Luo Hai?"

"And if I want to get out faster, I must choose the path most suited to myself."

After murmuring a few words, Tai Sui withdrew his foot and began wandering among the thirteen glowing entrances.

...

In a dim world, all kinds of ominous auras spread.

Some beings, human or demon, like devils and ghosts, wandered within like phantoms and evil spirits.

The earth was covered with scars, as if it were an ancient battlefield that had experienced countless great wars.

A blood-robed Daoist walked through it. Around him, over a hundred human figures circled and flew, sometimes fleeing far, sometimes returning.

Suddenly.

The blood-robed Daoist's sinister eyes paused, and he gazed toward one direction.

With a thought, the hundred-plus avatars all returned together and merged into his body. Then he leapt forward.

Several days later, a young man stood beside a blood sea shore and nodded to him, then also merged into his body.

If Luo Chen were here, he would certainly recognize at a glance that the young man was He Yuan, the Yuan Demon Sect direct disciple he had met twice in the past.

And this blood-robed Daoist was clearly Blood Nightmare Moluo.

He stood quietly before a vast blood sea, a look of emotion on his face.

"Ancestor, if you had not died, how could my Yuan Demon Sect have perished at the hands of the demon race?"

Then he shook his head again.

"If Yuan Demon Sect had not been destroyed, how could I violate the first-generation ancestor's rules and complete what I desire, attempting to unite the three lineages and achieve the True Yuan Demon Body?"

As he murmured, he was about to step into the blood sea.

At this moment, a soul-seizing sword cry rang out.

Within the blood sea, waves surged like dragons. A streak of blood light shot out and transformed into an ancient battle sword.

The battle sword hung high, its tip slanting downward and pointing at Blood Nightmare Moluo from afar.

Blood Nightmare Moluo frowned slightly. "Why block me? You clearly guided me here."

The blood-colored battle sword said nothing. It simply hung there, its edge chilling.

Blood Nightmare Moluo drew a deep breath. Dense blood light slowly rose from him.

"I am the twenty-seventh-generation disciple of Yuan Demon Sect. I bear the Blood Sea inheritance and have come only to collect the power left by Blood Sea Ancestor, to help me advance toward the Ascendant realm."

"If I become an Ascendant, I will surely restore the Demon Sect."

"If you will not help me, so be it. How dare you block me?"

That dense blood light rolled upward, about to contaminate the ancient battle sword.

As though sensing that this blood light filled with filthy power was not easy to provoke, the ancient battle sword gave a mournful cry and flashed away.

Seeing that he had forced back this so-called God-Slaying Blood Sword, Blood Nightmare Moluo finally relaxed slightly.

This dark world was far too dangerous, filled with countless demonic things he had only read about in sect records.

Even Blood Sea Ancestor had fallen in this world!

Therefore, he did not dare think at all about the dark world's inheritance.

His purpose in coming here was simple. He wanted to absorb the power left behind by Blood Sea Ancestor and take another step on the road of uniting the three lineages.

Of course, if he could comprehend a little of the blood Dao law, he would not mind using it to advance to Ascendant.

"I am only at Sage Stage Three now. It is still too early to speak of the Ascendant realm. I should first absorb the ancestor's remaining power here!"

After muttering to himself, Blood Nightmare Moluo slapped his body several times.

Then over a hundred figures escaped from him one after another.

"Blood God Children, go!"

At his command, the hundred-plus figures all sank into the blood sea.

After cultivating and absorbing for several days, Blood Nightmare Moluo confirmed that there were no problems. Only then did he carefully step into the blood sea.

The moment he entered, he began circulating his cultivation method.

In an instant, his body began to melt and merge with the blood sea.

At the same time, on another battlefield in this world,

Four figures stood upon the broken earth.

Three people were motionless. Only a tall, powerful, domineering man squatted on the ground, looking at a damaged corpse.

"The demonic things here are too fierce. I deployed all four Sage-stage Blood God Children together with my main body, and only then did I forcibly kill it. For this, one Blood God Child was destroyed, leaving only three."

He slowly stood, his full beard unable to hide the anger on his face.

"Where exactly is Blood Sea Ancestor's place of seated death?"

"If I cannot find his seated-death place, I cannot advance to late-stage Sage at all, much less speak of the great Dao of Ascendant!"

Looking around, he reluctantly made a decision.

Relying on himself alone, it would be difficult to find Blood Sea Ancestor's seated-death place. Perhaps he had to divide his strength.

With a thought, the three Blood God Children beside him fled in three different directions.

As for he himself, he silently sank beneath the surface of the earth.

...

Within a world of green waters and verdant mountains, full of thriving vitality,

A figure suddenly staggered out of the air.

He fell sideways, smashing a great cloud of dust from the ground.

When the dust dispersed, Luo Chen's slightly pale face appeared.

He looked around, and finally his gaze focused on a tall gray-white stone stele.

Upon it were two words.

Pill Realm.

*(End of Chapter)*
