# Chapter 607: I Don't Want to Negotiate

"Do you truly wish to die?"

The drifting voice — at once above the clouds and within the great marshland below — was deep and sourceless.

But the killing intent woven into it sank into the marrow like cold iron.

All seven Golden Core cultivators froze in the same instant.

Even Huangfu Song, the highest in cultivation among them, felt his eyes flicker uncontrollably at that towering, murderous weight.

*They say this Azure Yang distinguished himself in the battle that ignited the great righteous-demonic war. Countless trump cards, an overbearing style, essentially unmatched among his peers. I thought the rumors were exaggerated — but this...*

*Seven of us combined may well be walking into a repeat of Cold Light Island.*

"Fellow Daoist, do not let another man's power intimidate you. In the end he is alone — we have seven Golden Core cultivators on our side."

"Is it truly safe to say that?"

The Myriad Immortals Assembly, as a wandering cultivator organization, had little interest in managing its members' lives. Even fighting within the Fulong Mountain Range was largely left to itself — the Nascent Soul Sages above rarely intervened in what they regarded as the squabbles of insects.

But if things escalated too far and disrupted the already scattered spirit land situation of the Assembly — that was a different matter.

Even if no Nascent Soul Sage stepped in personally, the Demon Hunter Office would have questions to answer.

Watching his subordinates losing their nerve one by one, Huangfu Song understood: whatever happened here, it was largely his own problem to solve.

*These people came for profit. The force I've assembled has no cohesion.*

*Very well. If this battle helps them find some confidence, they'll be easier to direct afterward.*

Huangfu Song drew a slow breath and said: "Lord Gui — I'll ask for your help."

He was sending his two fully loyal old servants to hold the front line.

Lord Gui was not surprised. At a glance from the Night Elder, he stepped forward and flew toward a spot in the air.

The Night Elder had, in the brief time available, deduced one of the formation nodes — and whoever had spoken from concealment was most likely lurking there.

"Coward — hiding your head while showing your tail. Bold words from behind a veil."

Lord Gui's sharp voice rang out. He struck out a palm through the air.

Before the palm force had even reached his face, the piercing keen of it had already filled the space.

The empty air rippled with aftershocks.

Then a gale swept out.

*Boom!*

The two forces met — shattering fragments of white cloud, leaving a jagged tear across the sky.

A graceful figure emerged from concealment, her brow sharp, her expression proud and unbroken.

She let out a shout — and between her hands, a plantain fan appeared.

It grew from small to large until she gripped it two-handed by the handle, then fanned it out in one great sweep.

The gale surged again, several times greater than before.

"Upper-grade law device!"

At a single glance, Lord Gui had read the plantain fan's tier — far above any ordinary law device.

Not giving it a second thought, he dropped all restraint.

His right wrist flicked — countless white beams shot outward, burying into the howling wind to anchor themselves against it.

A brief standoff — and the beams revealed their true form.

Glistening white, crystalline threads.

All of them gathered together formed a horsetail silk whisk.

The woman saw this and narrowed her eyes. She swept the plantain fan again.

Multiple hurricane bursts tore from every direction, encircling Lord Gui.

Beyond that, she herself became a swift arrow and shot straight toward him.

Lord Gui bared a cold smile.

With a flick of the wrist, all the threads retracted — coiling back into the soft whisk.

The whisk swept wide — streams of white light flying out to meet the hurricanes — while Lord Gui himself, whisk stiffened now to rigid hardness like a long sword, rushed straight for the woman.

Not far away.

Huangfu Song watched this, first with a frown, then with dawning understanding.

"That's Heavenjade — the woman who has been running errands on Devil Lord Azure Yang's behalf all these years. I assumed she was only an ordinary attendant. It seems she also has some cultivation."

"Master, do not worry. Lord Gui's law device may be outmatched, but his own cultivation stands at mid Golden Core — he has the higher realm. He may find himself at a disadvantage for a hundred exchanges or so, but afterward he will gain the upper hand." The Night Elder's assessment was calm.

Huangfu Song nodded, and shifted his focus away from that exchange.

"This Azure Yang is hiding and dragging things out — the longer it goes, the more it wears down our side's morale. And it looks poorly to observers. Night Elder — I need you to force him out."

The Night Elder understood without further prompting.

"Very well. Let them see what I can do."

The black-robed elder stepped out from the group.

Under the gathered gaze of all present, he walked through empty air in measured steps, moving through a sequence traced according to the eight trigrams, covering several hundred paces through the void.

Beyond that, one hand held a star plate while the other moved through spiritual seals — scattered pinpoints of starlight drifting outward as he went, as if tracing out a road of silver-grey light.

The road grew brighter and brighter, pointing toward a single direction.

The Night Elder stopped and turned back to Huangfu Song.

"Master — found him."

Breaking the formation outright, in this short a time, was beyond him.

But locating an enemy who was hiding within a formation — for that he had some modest ability.

Before Huangfu Song could respond, he caught the look in the man's eyes.

Shock.

"Bad."

The Night Elder's heart dropped. Without even turning around, he slapped the star plate in his hand.

Rings of silver radiance exploded outward, enveloping him entirely.

The next moment —

A colossal single-horned dragon head materialized.

In full view of everyone, a gaping maw opened wide.

From that bloody opening, a nauseating wind poured out — and a terrifying devouring force erupted in every direction.

Countless silver beams, under that pull, surged toward the jaws like a tide rushing a shore.

The sight was as if a great dragon were swallowing a river of stars.

...

On Cailian Island.

The little girl had just climbed down from Zhang Jiadi's arms when she suddenly lifted a hand and pointed at the sky.

"Papa, look — I told you there was a dragon in the lake. I wasn't lying!"

Zhang Jiadi stared at the scene, dazed. He had always believed her.

What he had not known was just how powerful that black flood dragon was — capable with a single effort of leaving a mid-stage Golden Core cultivator completely helpless.

The scale of it, from what he could see, suggested the Night Elder was barely managing to hold on, with no hand free to mount a counterattack.

But this was not the moment for dwelling on that.

He gestured for a servant to take Qingqing away, then issued his orders.

"Everyone — move out!"

The others were stunned. In a battle of this scale, was there any role for them?

But under Zhang Jiadi's iron command, the Zhang family cultivators had no choice but to board a large boat and set off toward the deep water.

...

The battle above Peng Lake grew fiercer.

The immense surges of mana made it difficult for Qi Refinement and Foundation Establishment cultivators to see the fight clearly.

But some Golden Core cultivators were doing their best to capture every detail with whatever arts they had.

High in the sky, above the clouds, two figures sat.

One had grey-white hair and a gourd in hand, drinking at a leisurely pace — his expression carrying the amusement of someone watching exactly the spectacle they'd expected.

The other had a graceful, full-figured frame, her purple dao robes gathered around a supple form. The beauty of her features, still striking despite her years, now carried a note of helplessness.

"Angler — if you hadn't made that private decision to rent Peng Lake to this Devil Lord Azure Yang, none of this would have happened."

The Angler let out a chuckle. "Private decision — what private decision? These spirit lands and cave dwellings don't belong to us. If someone can pay the rent, they can rent. That's always been the rule."

The Purple Dowager shook her head. "You know that's not what I mean."

There was a certain kind of unspoken understanding between colleagues that needed no spelling out.

The Angler pursed his lips. "And I don't know what you're worrying about. You were part of the Great Zhou, yes — but obviously only as a puppet figurehead. Now that the Great Zhou is gone, you've been freed. Why entangle yourself in this again, just to prop up that boy Huangfu Song?"

The way he spoke of Huangfu Song — a fellow mid-to-late Golden Core cultivator — made it sound as if the man were barely worth mentioning.

The Purple Dowager let out a sigh. "Songer's character may not be ideal, but his talent is exceptional. We have this connection — if I can help him, I should."

"Whatever your reasons — now that you and I are two of the three chiefs of the Demon Hunter Office, we're no longer purely independent wandering cultivators. The fighting below us — stay out of it."

The Angler's expression grew serious as he delivered this.

The Purple Dowager let out a cold snort. "And you think that Devil Lord Azure Yang can truly hold off Songer and his group?"

"He has the title of Devil Lord, however much the rumors may have exaggerated it — he's bound to have some real substance behind the name." The Angler turned over his brief exchanges with Luo Chen in his mind, and felt quietly settled. He added, with faint dismissal, "And Huangfu Song's Golden Core cultivators — excluding those two old servants — are not a real force."

Just as the Purple Dowager was about to reply, her expression shifted.

A dragon's cry shook the heavens.

She and the Angler looked up — and saw the enormous black silhouette, half of its body emerging from the lake's surface.

"A black scaled serpent?"

"No — it's already become a flood dragon." Before the Angler even finished speaking, he noticed something off.

The black-scaled flood dragon's underbelly bore ring after ring of mysterious markings — like trailing vines, or like swirling silk.

These markings appeared only, if legend was to be believed, on primordial beasts of the Cliff Serpent lineage.

Whatever the exact lineage, this flood dragon's appearance had overturned every prior calculation.

The Angler's eyes lit up with a startled admiration. "This Azure Yang is no wandering cultivator 'alone with no backing' at all. He has a Golden Core-tier female cultivator as his attendant and a demon king spiritual beast comparable to Golden Core Stage Six as his companion. A setup like this — even a main-line disciple of an upper sect might not match it. Perhaps only a true disciple from one of those transcendent holy lands, traveling abroad, could have this kind of backing."

The Purple Dowager's expression had become somewhat strained.

A disciple from a transcendent holy land?

She had met such people.

Without relying on the holy land's name, that kind of cultivator could still attract a circle of subordinates through sheer personal magnetism — but getting Golden Core cultivators to take the role of servants? That remained very difficult.

Only those among the main-line disciples who were themselves truly outstanding — cultivators on whom their elders had invested heavily in resources: law devices, pills, spiritual beast companions, everything — could manage that.

Looking at this moment, this Devil Lord Azure Yang and those great main-line disciples from transcendent holy lands were, in every meaningful sense, equivalent.

She couldn't help asking: "Angler — are you certain the screening done when this person joined the Assembly had no gaps? You're sure he truly isn't a remnant member of the Primordial Demon Sect?"

The Angler rolled his eyes.

"Do you really think the detection law devices gifted by the Three Great Wanderers are decorative?"

The unspoken meaning: this person was definitively not a member of the Primordial Demon Sect.

And yet — a wandering cultivator who had subjugated a demon king of higher realm than himself... that was, by any measure, extraordinary.

Especially considering he was a wandering cultivator.

...

If both eight-star demon hunters were this shaken, it was hardly surprising that the man at the center of it all — Huangfu Song — was shaken too.

Watching the Night Elder growing more desperate by the moment under that terrible devouring force, Huangfu Song called out sharply to the four cultivators behind him.

"Still not moving to help?"

The four hesitated.

At that exact moment, a streak of light descended onto the battlefield.

"Gentlemen — does ganging up on one person not strike you as somewhat dishonorable?"

Huangfu Song's brow creased at the arrival.

"Who are you?"

"I am Duan Li. A good friend of Azure Yang's." Duan Li's expression was somewhat tense, but his bearing held steady.

Luo Chen had once helped him choose a good cave dwelling.

Returning the favor meant standing here today to speak on his behalf.

Especially given how much power Luo Chen had been displaying — it gave Duan Li a good deal more confidence to stand up.

Perhaps he couldn't save anyone in the snow — but adding flowers when already blooming, he could manage.

With Duan Li's arrival, the four Golden Core cultivators recognized him.

"It's the one from Huanrui Island's Duan family."

"I've heard he carries a highly potent flower basket law device — not something an ordinary instrument can match."

"If he deploys it, the four of us at early Golden Core wouldn't easily take him down in short order."

Hearing the whispers of his subordinates, Huangfu Song's irritation deepened.

A pack of fence-sitters.

No matter — he had no time to guard against the hidden Azure Yang anymore.

First: rescue the Night Elder.

The Night Elder's real value was not in direct combat. As a formation master, his strength lay in prepared engagements. A talent like that was prized by every major power — and wasting him in a head-on fight was the last thing Huangfu Song intended.

Huangfu Song's expression turned sharp.

"Out of the way."

With those words, two fingers pressed together like a sword — and a streak of purple sword qi shot outward.

It came fast. Terrifyingly fast. Almost impossible to react to in time.

Duan Li's condition had not returned to its peak over these years, and his mana responded sluggishly.

For a moment, real fear filled his eyes — and with it, a flicker of regret.

No one had invited him here. He had stepped forward on his own. Had that been too rash?

If he died here, what would become of his family's vendetta?

In the span of a single heartbeat, the purple sword qi closed to within three zhang of Duan Li.

Then — from empty air, rings of fire appeared in rapid succession.

The sword qi sliced through them like tearing silk, cutting straight through ring after ring.

But its speed finally fell away.

Duan Li, given that sliver of reaction time, managed to bring his defenses to bear — and barely stopped the weakened strike.

"You treat my friend this way — courtesy demands a response. Take one of my moves."

In empty air, an enormous azure palm emerged without warning.

Blotting out the sky, it pressed down with surging force.

Wherever it passed, azure flame coiled — and the very space around it seemed to twist and distort.

Huangfu Song's face went pale. He threw out defensive spell after defensive spell — each one collapsing under the weight of that palm.

In the end, a glow of light blazed from his body and consumed the attack entirely.

*The Azure Yang Great Handprint.*

The legendary signature strike of Devil Lord Azure Yang.

Under this technique, those of lower cultivation than its wielder faced death or grievous injury.

Even a same-stage opponent would struggle to withstand it.

Huangfu Song, for all his Golden Core Stage Seven power, had barely survived — and the final burst of protective radiance had clearly not been his own technique. It bore all the signs of something a senior had given him to carry.

"Everyone, stand down."

A faint voice drifted out.

In an instant, the gale died. The devouring force receded.

Black King slipped back beneath the surface of the lake; Heavenjade spun in a flash of escaping light and landed beside Duan Li.

And before all of them, a red-robed Daoist stood tall — eyes half-narrowed, gaze resting quietly on the figures ahead.

Huangfu Song's expression shifted through several shades. He swept a look across the people behind him.

Lord Gui, shaken and furious. The Night Elder, still catching his breath from a near thing. The four attached Golden Core cultivators — who had gone through the motions without contributing.

At last, he steadied himself and looked at the red-robed Daoist.

"Devil Lord Azure Yang — I think we can negotiate."

Luo Chen's expression was cold. "Now you want to negotiate?"

"You gave me no chance — I left a calling card."

"Not responding to your calling card was my way of saying I don't want to negotiate."

"You —"

Huangfu Song's anger flared, his finger rising to point.

Luo Chen's eyes narrowed slightly. The spiritual pressure at his side began to spread outward — slowly at first, then rising like a tide, growing stronger and stronger.

"And what of it?"

"Do you truly want to make a fight of this?"

"Have you prepared yourself to struggle with me to the death?"

*(End of Chapter)*
