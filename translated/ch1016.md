# Chapter 1016: Exquisite Furnace Cauldron, Sage Ninth Layer

"Senior Brother, did you hear all that just now?"

"Apologies, I didn't mean to eavesdrop. I had just awoken and subconsciously spread out my divine sense. If Junior Sister is unwilling, then I won't force the matter."

If it had been before, perhaps Luo Chen truly wouldn't have had such thoughts.

But now that he had spoken thus, how could he not be tempted?

Canglong shot Luo Chen a glare, then became generous and open. "If Senior Brother needs it, this Junior Sister is willing from the heart!"

Luo Chen laughed heartily. With a mere wave of his hand, the woman was pulled into his broad embrace, seated upon his knee.

The scorching aura of masculinity rushed toward her. Canglong, who had been composed just moments ago, now had a face flushed crimson.

She hadn't expected it to be so fast, so direct.

Even though she had mentally prepared herself, she still felt dazed and disoriented.

Canglong subconsciously changed the subject: "Senior Brother, what cultivation level are you at now?"

Luo Chen answered without hesitation: "Sage Ninth Layer."

"Ah!" Canglong exclaimed in surprise. Although she usually couldn't accurately gauge his realm due to his immense magical power, Sage Ninth Layer was far too high!

How many years had he been cultivating?

"What's wrong?" Luo Chen asked, looking down.

Canglong hesitated. "The gap between our realms is too great. I'm afraid the pure yin power I've accumulated won't be able to help you ascend to the next level!"

Unexpectedly, Luo Chen spoke with sincere earnestness: "With centuries of accumulation, how could I, as your elder brother, forcibly seize and plunder? What I wish to borrow is precisely your Seven-Orifice Exquisite Body!"

Canglong was stunned.

The Seven-Orifice Exquisite Body possessed many extraordinary qualities.

It could make a cultivator naturally intelligent, clever, and resourceful.

It could also open an additional heart orifice outside the qi sea, allowing for the accumulation of vast amounts of magical power. The reason Canglong had been able to directly charge into the Sage stage after breaking her seal back then was precisely due to the massive magical power accumulated in her heart orifice.

Finally, there was the matter of a woman's primordial yin, something many coveted!

This substance was extremely important for female cultivators in their early stages of cultivation, even more precious than a male cultivator's primordial yang.

And Canglong, possessing the Seven-Orifice Exquisite Body and an extreme-grade ice spirit root, had preserved her chastity for centuries. The primordial yin power she had accumulated during this time was a great tonic for any male cultivator!

By using this power to harmonize yin and yang, breaking through a minor realm would be effortless.

But what Luo Chen wanted was not that power at all.

For him now, the vast origin qi of heaven and earth surging within his body was already immense. He had no need to covet Canglong's power.

What he wanted, as he had said, was precisely this Seven-Orifice Exquisite Body, this additionally opened heart orifice!

This heart orifice could not only bear Canglong's own magical power but could also bear a male cultivator's power during dual cultivation. When yin and yang harmonized, it would perform a function similar to filtering and refining, purifying and refining the male's magical power.

In fact, this was precisely why Canglong, despite possessing such an excellent constitution, had remained unmarried all those years.

That heart orifice was far too helpful to male cultivators.

If the male party acted wantonly?

If Canglong lost control of herself?

Then she would degenerate into what was rumored to be a "furnace cauldron."

A tool for others to plunder and harvest.

Especially now that she had advanced to the Sage realm, that heart orifice had grown even larger, making her even more coveted!

Now that Luo Chen had spoken bluntly, Canglong was immediately placed in a difficult position.

Contributing her pure yin power, she could grit her teeth and do it.

But if she lent her body to another for cultivation, and if that person had malicious intentions...

"I'll say it again: if Junior Sister is unwilling, I won't force the matter. It'll just take a bit more time." Luo Chen said with a sincere expression.

Canglong was silent for a long time. Finally, she bit her red lips and gently nodded.

After all, this "other person" was not just anyone—it was Luo Chen!

She was willing to trust Luo Chen.

Seeing this, Luo Chen's face lit up with joy. He immediately lowered his head and kissed her.

The matter of resolving the hidden danger in his magical power could not be delayed!

...

Luo Chen's awakening was undoubtedly a storm within the Luo Tian Sect.

Swoosh! Swoosh! Swoosh!

Figures streaked through the air, all landing by the shores of Ming Lake.

Before everyone could even exchange words, they were startled by the sudden change in Ming Lake.

Clatter!

Raging waves suddenly erupted, layer upon layer of giant billows surging outward.

This was Ming water, possessing the powerful effect of corrupting a cultivator's magical power.

If it were calm, Luo Tian Sect cultivators could still cross using the Nether Crossing Boat or the Mystic Turtle. But in such a turbulent state, no one could approach.

Everyone looked at each other in bewilderment, not understanding what was happening.

"Didn't the Supreme Elder awaken? Why is it like this?" Li Yingzhang asked, uncertain.

"These signs suggest unstable magical power, a massive leakage. Forcing our way in now would be equivalent to contending with Senior Brother Luo Chen's immense magical power. We cannot do it." Lingfengzi said, his eyes full of wariness.

Wang Yuan observed carefully for a long time, then showed a thoughtful expression.

Someone beside him asked, and he rubbed his chin as he said, "I don't yet know Luo Chen's current situation, but judging from the leaked aura, he's still alive."

Wasn't that obvious?

With such a commotion, who didn't know he was still alive?

But "Luo Chen is alive" was itself good news. Everyone's expressions relaxed slightly.

"Then what should we do next?" someone asked.

Li Yingzhang answered without hesitation: "Since the Supreme Elder is fine, don't disturb him!"

Everyone hesitated, but Li Yingzhang showed strong leadership. Even though he was only at the Initiate stage, his status as the Luo Tian Sect's Sect Master gave him the authority to make this decision.

Everyone had no more questions, but Jueqing Fairy looked anxious.

"My Senior Sister is still inside!"

...

Inside the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

The atmosphere was hazy and seductive.

Skin as smooth as milk, earlobes as crystalline as fresh snow, those confused eyes, peaks like the Heavenly Mountains.

All of this painted a picture of worldly indulgence.

But Luo Chen, immersed within it, remained clear-minded and unmoved.

From his meridians and acupoints to his Purple Mansion and Sage, vast amounts of mixed magical power, under his difficult mobilization, entered Canglong's heart orifice. Then, Luo Chen used his terrifying soul foundation to rapidly refine it.

The heart orifice's own filtering and screening function also provided great assistance.

Throughout this process, Luo Chen only took the magical power he needed.

The large amount of remaining origin qi of heaven and earth was actively expelled by Luo Chen to the outside world, thus causing the violent shaking of Ming Lake.

"The Seven-Orifice Exquisite Body is truly an excellent furnace cauldron!"

Luo Chen sighed, but he did not harbor evil intentions. Instead, he constantly protected Canglong's heart orifice.

Not only that, he even released a mass of pure yang magical power to merge with the pure yin power Canglong had maintained for centuries.

He would not take back this power; it would be considered Canglong's opportunity for helping him.

Moreover, as Luo Chen gradually regained control of his body and his internal magical power was sorted out in an orderly manner, he began to regret the portion of origin qi he had abandoned.

"Although I cannot absorb it, for Canglong, isn't it another opportunity?"

With this thought, Luo Chen had another idea.

He began to cast a series of forbidden seals, sealing off that portion of origin qi and leaving it within Canglong's heart orifice.

This power was too vast; if it got out of control, even Luo Chen would suffer.

Therefore, Luo Chen spent great effort converting a portion of magical power containing the water origin, spreading it around the heart orifice. In a critical moment, it could save Canglong's life.

In the future, if Canglong gradually refined it using the methods Luo Chen had passed down, her cultivation progress would surely advance by leaps and bounds!

After all, the power was already within her; why seek it externally?

Time passed little by little.

When several days had passed, Canglong, who had been sitting in Luo Chen's embrace, finally awakened.

"Mm..."

A soft murmur met Luo Chen's eyes, which held a hint of a smile.

Canglong avoided that scorching gaze and said softly, "Is it over?"

"My situation has stabilized; it can be considered over. However..."

"However what?" Canglong asked, puzzled.

But as the man's heavy body pressed down on her, she immediately understood everything.

This time, it had nothing to do with cultivation—only romance.

...

The Primordial Unity Cauldron, expanded to a full ten zhang, sat at the bottom of Ming Lake.

Luo Chen walked quietly along the cauldron's rim, as if walking along a city wall.

The surrounding Ming water rushed toward him, but when it reached his side, it became exceptionally gentle, turning into ribbons of water that coiled around Luo Chen.

This was a change brought about by refining the Ming water spring.

But at this moment, Luo Chen did not dwell on this matter. Instead, he focused all his attention on the attribute panel.

[Lifespan: 350/1300]
[Spirit Root: Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, Earth]
[Constitution: Fire Spirit Body (Five-Spirit Dao Body Hidden)]
[Realm: Sage Ninth Layer 1/100, Archaic Fourth Stage 75/100]
[Laws: Withering-Bloom True Intent·Wood 12/100, Fire Origin 1/100, Water Origin 1/100]
[Techniques: Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture Perfect 301/500, Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon Proficient 180/200, Soul-Splitting Art Grandmaster 900/1000, Hundred Poisons Body-Tempering Art Grandmaster 510/1000, Myriad Beast Sutra Great Perfection, Myriad Path Convergence Great Perfection]
[Spells: Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain Great Perfection, Flame Dragon Descends Great Perfection, Withering-Bloom Dao Finger (Divine Ability)]
[Body Arts: Three Heads Six Arms (Divine Ability), Wind Thunder Moon Steps on the Immortal Path Grandmaster 750/1000, Devil Lord's Seven Scattered Hands Great Perfection (Voidburst Step, Overlord Removes Armor, Cloud-Seizing Divine Claw, Extreme Mountain Four Forms)]
[Soul Arts: Town Prison Grandmaster 666/1000, Moonlight Pure Heart Mantra Grandmaster 501/1000, Mirror Flower Water Moon Great Perfection, Spirit-Intimidating Art Great Perfection, Spirit-Bewildering Art Great Perfection, Ghosts and Gods Give Way Great Perfection, Aperture-Thatching Art Great Perfection]
[Achievement Points: 160]

This panel data was only part of the whole. The parts involving various skills like alchemy, artifact refinement, and talisman making had been hidden by Luo Chen.

For him now, no matter how proficient in the hundred arts of cultivation, they could not change the situation.

Only his own power could solve everything.

The first thing Luo Chen noticed was the sharp reduction in lifespan.

A full two hundred years less!

And it was a reduction in the upper limit of his lifespan.

Luo Chen pondered and roughly understood why it had decreased so much.

His lifespan far exceeded that of ordinary Sages because he also cultivated body refinement. But what built him up also tore him down. When his physique suffered severe damage, even if he later recovered completely, his lifespan limit would be reduced.

This was the same principle as an ordinary person suffering a major injury and living many fewer years.

And the injuries Luo Chen had sustained in this series of battles were far more than just ordinary severe wounds!

The battle with the three Great Demon Kings, the battle with the freed Nether Earth Dragon, and the nearly life-and-death battle with Azure Frost.

Each time, none had failed to injure Luo Chen's physical body.

Especially the last two times, Luo Chen had consumed a great deal of his own lifespan just to use source power to restore his nearly broken body.

"No wonder the path of body refinement gradually declined after ancient times."

Luo Chen sighed and stopped lamenting. He had enough lifespan to withstand the waste.

What he was most concerned about now was the constitution entry.

[Constitution: Fire Spirit Body (Five-Spirit Dao Body Hidden)]

It was still the Fire Spirit Body, but those hidden words "(Five-Spirit Dao Body)" were flickering continuously, their radiance even stronger than before.

Seeing this, Luo Chen's spirits were greatly lifted!

Sure enough!

His speculation back then was correct.

As he comprehended more and more laws, especially law intents of different attributes, this once-hidden [Five-Spirit Dao Body] would eventually fully recover.

Although Luo Chen enjoyed the extremely fast speed of cultivating a single-attribute technique, he also missed the vast magical power that the five spirit roots had provided him.

He didn't know if, after advancing to Ascendant with the Fire Spirit Body, his magical power would have corresponding multiplicative changes. If he still had the five spirit roots, he wouldn't need to worry.

Then Luo Chen turned his attention to the realm entry.

After dual cultivating with Canglong, his magical power had finally stabilized.

Although it wasn't completely perfected, he could slowly refine it over time on his own.

"Sage Ninth Layer?"

Luo Chen murmured, a sense of disbelief washing over him.

That day's heavenly bestowal, the qi infusion, had far exceeded any previous comprehension of law intents. It had directly elevated him by two minor realms, leaving him only one step away from perfect completion.

"What exactly was the power born from the mutual clash of those two origins, fire and water? And why did it vanish in an instant?"

Luo Chen knew the culprit that had pushed him to the brink of life and death, but he did not know its essence.

This feeling of knowing the "what" but not the "why" was truly frustrating.

However!

Misfortune is where fortune depends; fortune is where misfortune hides.

The sudden surge in realm could save Luo Chen decades or even centuries of bitter cultivation, but to fully master this power was also a test for him.

His gaze fell upon the three laws.

Staring at these entries, Luo Chen fell silent.

After a long while, he said softly, "Wood's withering and blooming has a divine ability. Even the body art has Three Heads Six Arms, a divine ability that can be gradually cultivated. But the two origins I comprehended on my own have no accompanying divine ability techniques."

"If I were a direct descendant of a holy land, how could I be in such a dire situation?"

He knew that back then, when Fu Qinglan and others had comprehended law intents, they had been granted corresponding great divine abilities by the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

But he still had to rely on illusory luck to comprehend them on his own. Truly disheartening!

Sighing, Luo Chen closed the attribute panel.

He now had a concrete understanding of his current situation.

But it wasn't enough!

Luo Chen silently recited the *Moonlight Pure Heart Mantra*.

Soon, his Sage and soul merged together, forming a primordial spirit.

Luo Chen did not release it; he merely quietly observed the situation within his body, calculating the duration of the primordial spirit's existence.

The limit back then was the time it took for one incense stick to burn.

But as Luo Chen waited, the time had already exceeded one incense stick.

One incense stick could be ten minutes, a quarter-hour was fifteen, one joss stick was about half an hour.

And as Luo Chen waited quietly, after about half a shichen, or one hour, his primordial spirit finally transmitted a feeling of imminent collapse.

"Under normal conditions, one hour?"

Luo Chen murmured to himself.

Could this duration support him in battle with an Ascendant great power?

And if he were in combat, would the primordial spirit's existence time shrink further?

If he recovered his complete destiny soul, could his primordial spirit further strengthen, even enduring forever in the world?

Many questions surfaced in his mind. Luo Chen withdrew from the primordial spirit fusion state, glanced at the outside world, and then walked toward the space inside the cauldron.

On a bed, Canglong slept peacefully.

Her skin, originally as white as the first snow, was now flushed red like a cooked lobster.

There was naturally no more filth on Canglong's body; Luo Chen had cleaned her before leaving.

Looking at the woman's quiet sleeping form, Luo Chen said to himself:

"All matters are settled. Next, I will journey far to the Central Continent. But before that, I must give you a status."

The cauldron was quiet. Whether Canglong had heard these words was unknown. She merely turned over naturally, revealing a beautiful curve of her waist and hips.

(Chapter End)
