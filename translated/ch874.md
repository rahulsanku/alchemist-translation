# Chapter 874: Luo Chen, Do You Know What It Means to Betray the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect?

Can a low-level cultivator reason with a Sage?

The answer is no!

Especially when Luo Chen was now the most celebrated Sage in the entire Eastern Wilderness.

Countless experts curried favor with him; a single call from him would rally a multitude.

The Nether Origin Holy Land actively recruited him, an Ascendant personally invited him, and he was set to take charge of the Pill Saint Hall in the future.

Even a renowned grand cultivator like Sage Shenyuan dared not act arrogantly before Luo Chen, instead speaking with kind words and consultation.

Under these circumstances, even though many small forces participating in the war to open the Moya Domain knew full well that the Luo Tian Sect's mediation of the sect war harbored ulterior motives, they could only grit their teeth and accept the Luo Tian Sect's mediation.

First, they dared not resist the Luo Tian Sect.

Second, it was also because, after drifting for so long, their sects had declined to their lowest point, and each urgently needed a territory to rest and recuperate.

Therefore, without significant personnel losses, within the current Luo Tian Domain, a scene emerged where two factions coexisted on an ordinary third-order spirit vein, and even three Core Formation powers would simultaneously occupy a large third-order spirit vein.

For the future development of these sects, this outcome was practically no different from drinking poison to quench thirst.

But on the surface, the Luo Tian Sect had indeed not caused anyone losses; every family had a place to live, and every person had immortals to cultivate.

As for the future, they couldn't care too much about that.

After all, even Luo Chen, when his cultivation was low, had to take things one step at a time. How much more so for these ordinary masses?

Within the ten-thousand-li territory demarcated by the Luo Tian Sect,

large-scale transformation work was underway.

Led by a group of formation experts from the Tian Du Sect, with assistance from the Luo Tian Sect and even cultivators from the other six major Sage sects who had lent their aid, this relatively intact spiritual land was undergoing a special transformation.

Mountains were moved, veins were filled, ancient trees were transplanted.

Dragon veins were sought, acupoints were located, and caves of various ranks were divided.

If they encountered something tricky, even Luo Chen and the other Sages had to get busy.

They went to the Nine Spirits Domain to move mountains, dug deep into the earth of the Tai Lake Domain to excavate remnant water veins, and traveled thousands of li to the Fire Ridge, using great means to pry loose a Flame Mountain and transport it over, compensating for the Moya Domain's lack of high-quality earth fire.

Several pieces of top-grade spirit stones that Luo Chen had treasured for a long time were even embedded into the fourth-order main vein, all to re-energize this spirit vein that had been abandoned for a hundred years.

Under this day-by-day transformation, the Luo Tian Sect's ten-thousand-li territory constantly changed its appearance, and its foundation continuously increased.

...

On this day.

On a mountain near Moya Cliff, slightly shorter but no less majestic and imposing, a solemn and majestic palace was finally completed.

Luo Chen stood on the newly laid white jade square, looking at the palace plaque covered by red cloth.

"Yingzhang, go ahead!"

Li Yingzhang was startled for a moment, then respectfully bowed to Luo Chen before gracefully flying over.

Under the expectant gazes of the crowd, he lifted the white cloth, and the three characters [Luo Tian Hall] slowly emerged.

Seeing this, the nearby Sage Fenghua and other Sages all offered congratulations to Luo Chen.

Lin Qingxuan was also among them, but his emotions were very complex.

Before he was even born, when the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect had conquered the Jade Cauldron Domain, there must have been a scene like this too!

But compared to the narrow Jade Cauldron Domain, this Moya Domain was far, far better.

Not only was its territory vast, but it also had numerous spirit veins and countless resources.

Although the number of forces under its command was large, none of them were formidable enough to pose a threat of the servant overpowering the master to the Luo Tian Sect.

In terms of future development, the Luo Tian Sect was bound to have boundless prospects!

And what about himself? What about the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect?

Although they had cleverly taken over the Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion, avoiding the bloody war of conquest, facing the profound foundation of the Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion, he would have his hands full in the future.

Luo Chen stood on the square, gazing at the resplendent Luo Tian Hall. Around him were banners bearing the sect's emblem, "Rosy Dawn Cauldron Ascends Sun Pill," and Luo Tian Sect cultivators stood under the banners, chests puffed out, heads held high, filled with joy.

Under the gaze of countless eyes, Luo Chen walked step by step to the front of the hall.

Li Yingzhang stepped aside, retreating behind Luo Chen.

Everyone's attention was completely focused on that man.

A white robe unstained by dust, a youthful face like a young boy, but his eyes, weathered by countless vicissitudes, were enough to tell the difficult journey this man had traveled.

Under everyone's expectant gaze, Luo Chen slowly spoke.

"My Luo Tian Sect began by the banks of the Great River, originally a mere speck of duckweed."

"Through countless fellow disciples sacrificing their lives, advancing wave after wave, cutting through thorns and thistles, slaying demons and vanquishing monsters, enduring two hundred years of wind and rain, we have finally become the Sage sect we are today, and finally established our mountain gate here."

"I, Luo Chen, declare the Luo Tian Sage Sect's mountain gate established here, building a foundation for ten thousand generations, with a heritage that will never be extinguished!"

After this impassioned speech, cheers erupted to the skies.

All the Luo Tian Sect cultivators could not suppress the excitement in their hearts and cheered enthusiastically.

Some emotional cultivators even felt their noses sting and tears fell.

Luo Chen's heart was also filled with emotion. The once weak and insignificant Luo Tian Society had finally, step by step, arrived here.

He patted Li Yingzhang on the shoulder. "I'll trouble you to entertain the cultivators from the other Sage sects. They've contributed a lot recently."

Li Yingzhang nodded repeatedly. These cultivators from the Sage sects would become the Luo Tian Sect's connections in the future.

When the Luo Tian Sect's disciples later completed their training and traveled outside, everyone would have to look out for each other.

Under such circumstances, he naturally wouldn't be negligent.

Today, good wine and fine food were a must. Even if it meant emptying the Luo Tian Sect's meager coffers, they had to put on a proper show.

Luo Chen, leading Sima Huiniang and Gu Caiyi, stood at the entrance, inviting the attending Sages inside.

"Fellow Daoists, please come in!"

The others smiled and entered the hall, finding a table laden with spirit wine and delicacies, and sat down in the back.

Ling Fengzi and Wang Yuan didn't need Luo Chen's invitation; they had already entered the hall on their own.

Luo Chen adjusted his robe and, leading his two Core Formation Dao companions, Huiniang and Caiyi, took the seat of honor.

Then came the clinking of glasses and a lively scene.

During this time, Luo Chen specifically asked the Nine Spirits Sect and the Divine Talisman Sect about their next plans.

The Divine Talisman Sect naturally wanted to return to their old territory, but the Nine Spirits Sect was in a more troublesome situation.

The great domain they wanted to open was the territory once occupied by the Medicine King Sect.

Not only was it infested with a large number of demon beasts, but the key issue was that the Nine Spirits Sect was very unfamiliar with the geography there and was still investigating the situation.

Gu Caiyi was a bit curious and asked, "Why not choose the vacant Misty Domain or Firework Ancient Isle? They're closer to our Luo Tian Sect, and we could be neighbors in the future. Could it be that you find these two places too small?"

Among those present, Gu Caiyi had the lowest cultivation level. This inquiry seemed a bit rash, but no one would blame her.

It was only because she sat beside Luo Chen, as his Dao companion!

Nine Spirits Primogenitor shook his head. "Size doesn't really matter. Having a place to settle down is good enough for now. The problem is, these two places already have owners."

Gu Caiyi was stunned. How could these two places have owners?

The demon race had ceded them, the Misty Sect had been absorbed into the Luo Tian Sect, and Firework Ancient Isle had already collapsed with Xiao Shiniang's fall.

While she was puzzled, Ling Fengzi explained, "These two places have already been claimed by someone. Although the strength of those two families isn't great, no one dares to provoke them!"

"Which two families?"

Just as Gu Caiyi was curious, a voice announcing gifts rang out from outside the door.

"Lin Yonghua and Gu Hong of the Nether Origin Sect, come to congratulate the Luo Tian Sect on establishing its great domain!"

Luo Chen's eyelids drooped, and he said softly, "Here they come!"

Led by Li Yingzhang, two figures entered the hall.

All the Sages present rose to greet the two who had entered.

"Sage Lin!"

"Sage Gu!"

Luo Chen forced a smile. "You two are also busy people, yet you took time out of your day to congratulate our sect on its initial establishment. Our sect is truly grateful. Yingzhang, make arrangements."

Li Yingzhang immediately took out two prayer mats and also had others send over spirit wine, delicacies, and other fine foods of the same standard as the other Sages.

The two unfamiliar Sages took their seats, without any sign of displeasure at having to wait.

Lin Yonghua looked at Luo Chen. "Coming unannounced today to offer our congratulations has troubled the Pill Sect."

Gu Hong said in a gruff voice, "The Pill Sect need not be so polite. My fellow disciple and I come today not only on behalf of the Nether Origin Sect but also on behalf of the Lin and Gu families. From now on, we will be neighbors."

Luo Chen nodded. "Indeed, we will be neighbors from now on. We should visit each other more often."

As he said this, there was a smile on his face, but a chill spread through his body.

Lin Yonghua and Gu Hong were both Sages from the Nether Origin Sect!

They had broken the old tradition of Nether Origin cultivators cultivating within the Holy Land, left the Nether Origin Holy Land, established their own families, and settled in Misty and Firework Ancient Isle.

This move not only confirmed Luo Chen's earlier speculation about the Nether Origin Sect—emulating the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's strategy of covering an entire continent with its influence—but also subtly surrounded the Luo Tian Sect, directly foiling the Luo Tian Sect's plan to stay away from the Nether Origin Holy Land.

This Holy Land really kept a tight watch on him!

Luo Chen sighed inwardly, but on the surface, he continued to converse warmly with them.

The two spoke very nicely, saying things like Luo Chen would soon join the Nether Origin Sect, they were all fellow disciples, and the Lin, Gu, and Luo Tian families were so close with such deep connections that they should naturally watch each other's backs.

They also mentioned that the three high-grade alchemists within the sect were very much looking forward to Luo Chen's arrival, hoping he would come over soon to discuss the Dao of alchemy together.

Finally, as they were about to leave, Gu Hong boisterously offered a gesture of goodwill.

"That Moya Old Ghost and the Five Elements Divine Sect have always been at odds with you. Not long ago, they wanted to use my territory in the Misty Domain as a base to attack the Five Elements Domain, but I sternly refused them!"

"Offending the Pill Sect means offending me, Gu Hong!"

These words were a great show of face. After all, Moya Old Ghost was not worth worrying about, but Sage Shenyuan was a proper grand cultivator. For Luo Chen's sake, Gu Hong being so tough made anyone think he was fully protecting Luo Chen.

But in Luo Chen's eyes, it wasn't like that at all.

What fellow disciples, what three high-grade alchemists eagerly awaiting, what a grand cultivator's face being less important than the Pill Sect's face... all of it seemed to be urging Luo Chen to hurry up and go to the Nether Origin Holy Land.

Was it that urgent?

The banquet ended noisily and happily.

In the dead of night, Luo Chen extricated himself from the soft warmth and fragrant jade, casually threw on a thin outer robe, and stepped onto the cold mountain path.

He walked unhurriedly upward, with peaks upon peaks coming into view in the distance.

Beneath his feet was Moya Cliff. Ahead was Luo Tian Peak, where Sect Master Li Yingzhang resided. To the left and right were peaks bestowed upon Ling Fengzi and Wang Yuan respectively.

One was renamed Misty Peak, the other Divine Battle Peak.

Further ahead was the planned Danxia Peak, built to commemorate the Danxia Mountain where the Luo Tian Sect once resided.

In the future, it would also be the place specifically used to train new disciples recruited by the Luo Tian Sect.

As for other places, they were still being planned. They would be dealt with when the Luo Tian Sect's disciple numbers gradually increased.

"Everything is in order, everything is on the right track."

"Perhaps, it is also time for me to leave!"

Just as Luo Chen was murmuring to himself, he suddenly raised an eyebrow and slowly turned around.

The moonlight was obscured by dark clouds. In the vast darkness, a figure slowly emerged.

"Luo Chen, do you know what you will face for betraying my Heavenly Origin Dao Sect?"

Luo Chen pressed his lips together. "Fellow Daoist Gu, you know it was not my intention."

Under the night, the figure drew closer and closer. When it landed on Moya Cliff, it revealed itself to be none other than Gu Zuokai, the Grand Elder of the Eastern Wilderness Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance.

He looked calmly at Luo Chen, hearing the sincerity in the other's words.

In fact, from his observations, joining the Nether Origin Sect was indeed not Luo Chen's intention.

Previously, the Lingtian City Lord had repeatedly recruited him, but Luo Chen had refused every time.

It was only when the Ascendant, Nether Origin Ancestor, personally appeared that Luo Chen was forced to agree.

And what he had seen today—the two Sages Lin Yonghua and Gu Hong leading their families to settle in Misty and Firework Ancient Isle—subtly restrained the Luo Tian Sect.

Clearly, the Nether Origin Holy Land valued Luo Chen very much!

Luo Chen stared intently at Gu Zuokai and said solemnly, "I am merely an external affairs guest elder of the Star Gate, not even a formal guest elder. Isn't calling it betrayal an overstatement?"

"But you did enter the Star Gate, and you have a registered cave dwelling on Heaven-Earth Peak." Gu Zuokai said this.

Luo Chen shook his head. "Then let the Star Gate, let the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, go and reason with the Nether Origin Sect, go and negotiate with that Heize Ascendant! I, Luo Chen, have little influence, and with my disciples, old friends, Dao companions, and close ones being watched by the Nether Origin Sect, it is difficult for me to simply walk away."

Gu Zuokai sighed. The facts were indeed so.

In the end, it was because the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect had not valued Luo Chen enough in the beginning.

They had overlooked the immense potential alchemical ability hidden within him.

To the point where, not to mention being a Star Gate elder or offering elder, even the guest elder position they had given him was the most insignificant external affairs one.

Seeing him like this, Luo Chen breathed a sigh of relief.

"Fellow Daoist, coming alone today, surely you are not here to call me to account?"

Gu Zuokai waved his hand. "The might of the Pill Sect, the power of the Demon-Slaying Sage, is renowned throughout the Eastern Wilderness. Although I consider myself to have some strength, I would not dare to come looking for trouble alone."

With that, he took out a jade box, covered in restrictions.

"Here, Sage Heavenly Mountain's reply."

Luo Chen was stunned for a moment. He had come all this way just to deliver a letter?

But this reply was indeed something he had been waiting for for many years. To resolve the calamity of the gu insects on Huiniang's body, he could only hope for Sage Heavenly Mountain, who hailed from the Southern Frontier.

Seeing Luo Chen take it, Gu Zuokai prepared to take his leave.

Before leaving, he couldn't help but leave a few words.

"I am in charge of the Eastern Wilderness Merchant Alliance; the Star Gate is none of my business. Thinking of our good relationship, I want to remind you: anyone who enters the Star Gate either dies of old age on Heaven-Earth Peak or fights for the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. Your current actions are no different from betrayal. The Star Gate Elder Council will absolutely not sit idly by."

"In the future, be careful in everything!"

The dark clouds dispersed, and moonlight once again spilled onto Moya Cliff.

In Luo Chen's field of vision, Gu Zuokai's figure was gone. Only the red jade box in his hand emitted a faint warmth.

He clenched the jade box in his hand, his lips pressed tightly together.

But gradually, his hand relaxed, and the corners of his mouth lifted.

Back then, the Pill Saint had traveled far to the Central Continent and joined the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. Had the Nether Origin Sect not been enraged?

Now, he was merely the reverse of that situation.

Pill Saint Chu Yan had cultivated all the way to the Ascendant realm, and the Nether Origin Sect hadn't caused any trouble; they had even sent people to observe the ceremony.

So now?

"Since you have forcibly recruited me, then you should bear the pressure from the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect yourselves."
