# Chapter 811: Fame Spreads, Entrusting Affairs

The great battle outside the city lasted less than a single day.

The beast tide had come with overwhelming force, but the Death-Defying Army within Lingtian Pass had been equally vigilant and prepared.

Without a Demon Emperor-level commander directing them, Qin Baixiang held the eastern city wall, and the battle between his subordinate cultivators and the demon beasts was a one-sided slaughter.

If not for the later appearance of that six-winged golden cicada named Dark Cry, who led the remnants away, the direct-line troops of Commander Seven Rhino would have been completely annihilated outside Lingtian Pass.

"Great victory! Great victory!"

"The beast tide has retreated!"

Luo Chen walked along the broad streets of the city, the excited cheers of running cultivators filling his ears.

He watched this scene in silence, his steps finally stopping before a cluster of buildings covered in green tiles and red glazed glass.

He looked up at the plaque on the tall building before him.

**[Luotian Treasure Pavilion]**

"Guest, what do you—"

The Luotian Sect cultivator standing at the entrance, well-trained and enthusiastic, immediately came forward to greet him.

But upon seeing Luo Chen's face, he instantly reacted.

"Greetings, Supreme Elder!"

"Mm." Luo Chen nodded, asked nothing, and walked straight inside.

In his divine sense perception, Sima Huiniang was busy in the back courtyard.

As Luo Chen walked inward, he observed the empty treasure pavilion.

Empty now, but it would soon become bustling.

Hurried footsteps approached. Sima Huiniang, having received word, set aside her work. Her eyes lit up when she saw Luo Chen.

"Ruoqi, you may withdraw!"

The female cultivator following closely behind respectfully bowed to Luo Chen and obediently retreated.

Luo Chen watched the woman's retreating figure. She was one of the younger generation of Luotian Sect whom he knew relatively well.

She had distinguished herself back during the Expansion War, leaving an impression on him.

Over the years, though she had not yet advanced to the Adept stage, she had reached a respectable late Initiate stage.

She was a rare talent for Luotian Sect.

"You took Mei Ruoqi as your disciple?"

"With nothing to do, and you forbidding me from cultivating, I thought I'd take on a few disciples to train properly." Huiniang's brows curved gently as she smiled warmly. "Ruoqi is quite an outstanding junior in the sect. Bringing her to Luotian Treasure Pavilion is also to give her some experience and help her build connections."

Speaking thus, Sima Huiniang suddenly sighed.

"If Caiyi's disciple hadn't died, she would have been the most suitable person to manage this Luotian Treasure Pavilion."

Gu Caiyi had only ever taken one disciple in her life.

That was Yuan Xiaoyue!

A young girl Luo Chen had specially looked after in Dahe Market back in the day, who later formally became Gu Caiyi's disciple.

Back then, Luotian Sect wasn't yet Luotian Sect—it was the Luotian Society, and the entity handling external business was the Golden Hall.

Yuan Xiaoyue had been sensible enough to work in the Golden Hall from a young age, sharing Gu Caiyi's burdens.

In Luo Chen's memory, she had successfully established her foundation.

But when Luo Chen returned to the Eastern Wilderness, all he saw was a trivial entry in the *Luotian Records*—"Perished at Taihu Westward Flight."

Taihu was a rare watery region among the thirty-six domains of the Eastern Wilderness.

When the demon beast army poured out from the Million Mountains, this place was besieged by several water-attributed Demon Emperors. There was no escape above or below. Countless cultivators desperately fled outward, but few succeeded in escaping.

Taihu Domain's Nascent Soul superior sect, Taihu Palace, was destroyed in that battle.

Even the Nine Nether Charm Python sinew Luo Chen had used to refine his magical treasure was one of the spoils from that battle.

One could imagine how fierce a battle must have been to claim even a Nascent Soul Demon Emperor.

Yuan Xiaoyue had been sent there on business but couldn't retreat in time, ultimately perishing in Taihu Lake.

Thinking of old acquaintances, Luo Chen's expression darkened.

Seeing this, Sima Huiniang changed the subject. "I heard earlier that eight Demon Emperors attacked Lingtian Pass's four gates, two at each. Though you faced only one, they say he was second only to Commander Seven Rhino. Even the Lingtian City Lord activated the formation but couldn't keep him?"

"That is indeed true." Luo Chen nodded, then frowned slightly. "News travels this fast?"

Sima Huiniang studied Luo Chen as she replied, "Information flows quite freely in the city. There are almost no secrets about major movements. Husband, are you alright?"

Luo Chen knew Huiniang was worried about him and reassured her briefly.

But in his mind, he still felt something was off.

Yes, cultivators could transmit messages quickly.

But battles involving Nascent Soul cultivators weren't so easily discerned by lower-level cultivators.

To describe the Thunder Prison Roc's strength so clearly was no simple matter.

Who was secretly spreading this information?

"It's good that you're fine, it's good that you're fine. Though we now have a place to rely on, I've seen the brutality of demon beasts. Husband, you must be careful when fighting!"

"Mm."

Luo Chen shook his head, temporarily setting aside his doubts.

"I came today to give you a few instructions."

"Speak."

"After the great battle, there will be many demon beast corpses. We've accepted the Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance's commission to purchase them. But many in the city are in this business. If Luotian Treasure Pavilion is too greedy, it will offend those established Eastern Wilderness powers. Be mindful of your limits—don't take too large a share."

"Mm, I understand."

"Also, take some of the high-quality flesh from the purchased corpses and send it to Wang Yuan. He's one step away from the fourth body-refining realm, the Leakless Golden Body, and needs large amounts of high-quality blood food."

"I'll remember. I'll also prepare a portion for Little Dragon."

Luo Chen paused, then smiled slightly. Indeed, he should prepare some for Zeng Yilong as well.

After reaching the third realm in body refinement, there was a fundamental difference from qi-cultivation.

The third realm was called the Furnace Realm.

This furnace evolved from the qi sea.

It could not only store large amounts of astral qi but also madly refine the energy within flesh and blood for one's own use.

The main reason Wang Yuan had reached the peak of the third body-refinement realm in just a hundred years, even able to briefly contend with fourth-rank Demon Emperors and Nascent Soul cultivators' true artifacts, was that he had refined countless demon beast flesh and blood over that century.

And Zeng Yilong, currently at the early Furnace Realm, also greatly needed high-quality blood food.

"You two spoil him quite a bit," Luo Chen teased.

Sima Huiniang smoothed her hair and smiled. "Little Dragon grew up under the watch of us older generation. He's almost no different from our own child. It's only natural to treat him well."

The words "our own child" were emphasized heavily.

Luo Chen's expression stiffened slightly, but he didn't avoid the topic. Instead, he said softly, "The current situation isn't the right time."

Just then, the jade token hanging at his waist flashed with light.

"The City Lord has matters to discuss. Prepare a quiet room for me."

"Come to the back courtyard. My residence is the most secluded and secure."

Sima Huiniang led Luo Chen to a room in the back courtyard. After he entered, she personally closed the door behind him.

But as he went inside, her face carried a hint of resentment.

She wanted a child!

---

Inside the room.

Luo Chen poured his divine sense into the jade token specially made by Lingtian Pass for Nascent Soul cultivators. Instantly, his body was enveloped in dark light.

Within that dark light, figures began to emerge.

At the center was the Lingtian City Lord.

Then Xiao Shiniang.

Coughing sounds arose, and the soul projection of Xianhe Zhenren also appeared.

Luo Chen looked closely. Xianhe Zhenren's soul projection seemed somewhat ethereal—clearly injured.

"Fellow Daoist Xianhe?"

"Fellow Daoist Xianhe?"

Xianhe Zhenren smiled bitterly. "Among the two Demon Emperors who joined forces to attack me, one's true body was a Lightless Beast, skilled in soul-poison assassination. I was caught off guard by one move and suffered a loss. Fortunately, the City Lord rescued me in time."

Hearing this, Luo Chen's heart stirred.

Hearing this, Luo Chen's heart stirred.

The Lightless Beast, when its true body expanded, could turn day into night.

Within it, phantoms moved invisibly, harming people's souls. It was a great enemy of cultivators.

Xianhe Zhenren spoke lightly, but one could imagine how difficult his situation had been.

Xiao Shiniang also sighed. "My side wasn't much better. If I hadn't activated my lifebound true artifact in time, combining it with my Sage domain to release my Misty Cloud domain, that Thousand Land Howling Insect alone would have given me a hard time."

Speaking thus, she couldn't help but look at Luo Chen, clicking her tongue in wonder.

"Though Fellow Daoist faced only one enemy, that great roc seemed to possess the bloodline of the ancient desolate beast Thunder Roc, and its cultivation was profound. Yet Fellow Daoist Luo emerged unscathed. Truly, a true person doesn't reveal his capabilities!"

Luo Chen's expression was calm. "It wasn't my ability. The opponent simply didn't go all out."

Even so, Xiao Shiniang looked unconvinced.

She had heard that when the Alchemy Sect first returned to the Eastern Wilderness, Luo Chen had destroyed the Motian Old Ghost's lifebound true artifact in one blow.

Though the rumor was that the Motian Old Ghost had privately lent his true artifact to a junior Adept, resulting in delayed control and Luo Chen taking advantage, combined with yesterday's battle, Luo Chen's capabilities might be greatly underestimated.

Or rather, it wasn't that his abilities were underestimated, but that his alchemy achievements overshadowed his peerless martial arts.

Luo Chen didn't want to continue this topic. He looked at the Lingtian City Lord.

"Summoning us, City Lord, do you have urgent matters?"

The Lingtian jade token could use the formations within Lingtian Pass to connect the divine senses of the city's Nascent Soul cultivators and even project their images, facilitating rapid intelligence sharing.

But when activating this token, cultivators had to pour their divine sense into it, greatly reducing their defensive capabilities.

Without something important, this function was generally not used.

The Lingtian City Lord said calmly, "Everyone hasn't arrived yet. Wait a moment."

Luo Chen didn't press further and fell silent.

Time passed slowly. After dozens of breaths, a distorted phantom formed into Qin Baixiang's projection.

"Apologies. Cleaning up the battlefield took some time. I've kept you all waiting."

"No matter. We still wait for one more."

The Lingtian City Lord said calmly.

Everyone exchanged glances, but soon knew who they were waiting for.

In their waiting, a projection suddenly emerged from the center.

It was Lingfengzi!

As soon as he appeared, he spoke urgently. "I'll make it brief. I've scouted the nearby situation. Besides Commander Seven Rhino, the Huangman army has appeared, only half a day's journey from the front lines. Additionally, the severely wounded Seven Rhino has been reinforced by two Demon Emperors, suspected to be two of his ten Demon Emperors. They came alone, but one can imagine their demon beast armies are not far behind."

At these words, everyone present changed color.

Seven Rhino's strength had been fully displayed yesterday.

If not for the city-protecting formation, he alone could have posed a great threat to Lingtian Pass.

Now, another of the rumored nine commanders, Huangman, was also leading his army.

Lingtian City was in danger!

The Lingtian City Lord naturally sensed everyone's emotional changes.

He smiled lightly. "Rest assured. With me here, two commander-level Demon Emperors pose no real threat to Lingtian Pass. Moreover, I've already sent word to the rear. The Supreme Elder of Tiandu Sect will personally come to Lingtian Pass. He will personally preside over the Black Tortoise Four Directions Formation!"

Lingtian Pass had many formations—nine great formations, twenty-seven small ones. The city-protecting formation covering the entire city was the Black Tortoise Four Directions Formation passed down from the Nether Origin Sect.

This formation was ranked fifth rank!

It had unparalleled defensive power, with supplementary killing power. Even the Lingtian City Lord couldn't display one-tenth of its full might.

But the Tiandu Sect Supreme Elder was different.

Tiandu Sect itself was a famous formation grand sect in the Eastern Wilderness, extremely fond of studying formations, with great influence.

Even the Tianfan City of the far eastern six domains had originally come from Tiandu Domain, absorbing some formation essence before opening a domain in the far east and becoming a renowned artifact-forging grand sect.

The Tiandu Sect Supreme Elder was rumored to have already reached late Nascent Soul stage, with formation abilities unmatched in the Eastern Wilderness.

When he visited the Nether Origin Sect back then, even their formation experts held him in high regard.

If he personally presided over the Black Tortoise Four Directions Formation, Lingtian Pass would be completely stable!

Not to mention two commanders—even two more would be no worry!

Everyone's expressions calmed somewhat.

And the good news didn't stop there.

The Lingtian City Lord continued. "The distress signals we sent out have all received responses. The Nascent Soul cultivators of Lingtian's five domains are leading their forces in rapid reinforcement. They will arrive at Lingtian Pass soon."

"The Second Palace Lord of Fenghua Palace, Mu Mansion's Mu Zhenren, Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion's Pavilion Master Li, Five Elements Divine Sect's Jinling and Divine Fire Zhenren..."

One familiar name after another came from the Lingtian City Lord's mouth.

With each name, the hearts of the five present settled a little more.

By the end, counting carefully, there were ten people in total.

If added to the six Nascent Soul cultivators already stationed at Lingtian Pass, the overall number of top experts would not be weaker than the combined forces of Huangman and Seven Rhino.

And clearly, this was not the limit of Lingtian's five domains.

Strong experts like the Crane Emperor of White Crane Sect, the Great Palace Lord of Fenghua Palace, and the Supreme Elder of Five Elements Divine Sect had yet to move.

Even if it wasn't enough, the old sects of the sixteen rear domains would come to aid according to the agreement made when Lingtian Pass was built.

Seeing everyone relieved, the Lingtian City Lord changed his tone.

"Of course, before the reinforcements arrive, do not slack off."

"I hear that Huangman is a clansman of the ancient Demon Emperor Di Tian, with an extremely violent temperament. If he can't restrain himself and directly attacks Lingtian Pass, it will inevitably trigger a great battle. Everyone must be careful!"

Everyone's hearts tightened, and they acknowledged in unison.

"That's all for today!"

The Lingtian City Lord was about to dismiss the small meeting.

Lingfengzi suddenly spoke. "City Lord, can I return now?"

The Lingtian City Lord glanced at him. "Not yet. Gather more intelligence on the demon race. But also take care to preserve yourself. Don't be lost."

His voice still echoed, but his projection had gradually faded.

Lingfengzi smiled bitterly. "Fellow Daoists, things are unsettled on my end. I'll take my leave first."

Before leaving, he hesitated, then deeply bowed to everyone.

While Luo Chen and the others were surprised, he said softly, "If I don't return, I beg you four to look after the disciples of my Misty Sect. Lingfengzi is eternally grateful!"

With that, his projection suddenly dissipated.

The remaining few looked at each other but didn't exchange more words, hurriedly withdrawing their divine senses.

---

Inside the room, the jade token floating in midair fell with a clatter.

Luo Chen caught it, his expression somewhat heavy.

The Lingtian City Lord had spoken well, giving everyone hope.

But before the reinforcements from all sides arrived, Lingtian Pass ultimately only had them, a few Nascent Soul cultivators.

The darkness before dawn was the hardest to endure!

"Hopefully..."

Luo Chen shook his head, abandoning those ethereal thoughts.

He had hoped earlier that his three-year patrol duty wouldn't encounter the demon army's attack.

But in the end, he had still faced Commander Seven Rhino leading Demon Emperors in a strong assault on Lingtian Pass.

Ultimately, rather than relying on prayers, it was better to rely on oneself!

He rose to his full height and left the room.

Sima Huiniang was waiting in the courtyard. Seeing Luo Chen emerge, she quickly approached.

"Husband!"

"Send these two items to Xianhe Zhenren at the southern city. Say they are a small token of Luo's regard."

Two jade bottles—one contained a fourth-rank Purple Qi Poison-Resisting Pill personally refined by Luo Chen, the other contained a fourth-rank Fool's Truth Pill he had once given to Nine Spirit Primogenitor.

Perhaps these two pills couldn't specifically cure the Lightless Beast's poison, but they would help somewhat.

Sima Huiniang nodded, then took out a storage pouch and handed it to Luo Chen.

"This is?" Luo Chen asked curiously.

"It was sent by Elder Gu of the Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance. He said it contains something you need."

Luo Chen swept it with his divine sense and immediately understood.

It was the illusion core magical artifact he had wanted earlier.

Putting away the storage pouch, Luo Chen walked out.

Sima Huiniang followed behind, wanting to speak but hesitating.

Just as he was about to leave the back courtyard, Luo Chen suddenly turned around. Sima Huiniang, caught off guard, bumped into his chest.

The intense masculine scent filled her nostrils, and his breath brushed against her earlobe.

"I don't want our children to be born facing heavenly disasters and demonic calamities. Even less do I want to be powerless, ending up like Lingjun and Qu Jiu—father and son turning against each other."

"But I promise you, once things settle down, we will have children."

"More than one!"

With that, he gently kissed her forehead and strode away.

The woman stood in the courtyard, petals falling around her, a flush of red spreading across her cheeks.

(Chapter End)
