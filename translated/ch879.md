# Chapter 879: Three Commanders, Ambush Luo Chen

At the heart of the Medicine King Domain.

It had once been the mountain gate of the Medicine King Sect, a former hegemon of this region.

Yet after multiple great battles—demon calamities and human conflicts—it had long since fallen into ruin.

Now, with Sage-level experts contending for the Accumulated Void Flower, the area spanning thousands of li had been flattened, leaving no weak living creatures behind.

So-called expansion wars, when it came to matters concerning breakthroughs to the Ascendant stage, had become far less important.

Low-rank demon beasts and low-rank cultivators had each retreated, leaving the stage to those few contemporary powerhouses.

A grand formation, spreading ten li in every direction, covered everything within.

Inside, a nine-colored light river surged and rolled, but no matter how it charged left or crashed right, it could not break through the formation condensed from terrifying magical power.

Outside the formation.

Three Sage-level experts stood together, their expressions extremely grave.

The Crane Emperor, with his narrow cheeks and prominent cheekbones, spoke in a low voice: "This formation is merely linked together by pure magical power. It is not particularly exquisite, but its essence lies precisely in that—great skill seeming clumsy. Unless one has magical power surpassing the combined might of the five demon emperors within, it will be very difficult for us to break through from the outside."

Xianhe Sage trusted his companion's judgment greatly. Faced with this unsolvable situation, he could not help but sigh.

If it were five ordinary demon emperors, that would be one thing.

But within that grand formation, the one presiding over it was Commander Niugui, who had reached the Great Demon Emperor realm!

Which ordinary Sage could surpass him in magical power?

Even if a Grand Cultivator were present, they would have to consider the formation's backlash after Niugui gathered the magical power of the other four demon emperors.

Xianhe lamented, "Fellow Daoist Jiuling, why must you be so stubborn? That Accumulated Void Flower has not yet matured. What use is seizing it? You are only throwing your life away for nothing!"

Qin Baixiang clenched his fists. If his Dusk Monkey were still alive, he might have been able to try breaking this formation.

Unfortunately, the Dusk Monkey had fallen in the great human-demon war over a decade ago.

He had originally thought to assist Jiuling Yuanjun in this expansion war, using his opponent's hand to capture one or two demon beasts with good talent to bring back for cultivation. He never expected Jiuling Yuanjun himself to fall into such a trap.

"By the way, where is Lingfengzi?"

Hesitating at this question, Xianhe Sage said, "After coming out of the Accumulated Void Secret Realm, Lingfengzi contacted the Alchemy Sect and requested their aid. Now he has disappeared. Could it be..."

Before the words "could it be" had even landed, the three of them could not help but turn their heads in unison to look behind them.

Between heaven and earth, a sky-covering giant palm suddenly came pressing down.

Its color was deep azure, its five fingers thick and large.

The veins were vivid and lifelike, no different from an ordinary human hand.

To be able to execute a spell technique to such perfection showed the caster's extraordinary mastery!

Xianhe Sage blurted out, "The Alchemy Sect has come!"

This familiar aura of magical power, he could never mistake it!

Qin Baixiang gazed at the enormous palm print rolling in, recalling a rumor.

Back when Ling Tian City Lord had led six Sages in a surprise attack on the Overlord Blade Domain, Alchemy Sect Luo Chen had used a terrifying palm print as a vanguard.

Wherever it passed, none could stand against it!

Could this be that very spell technique?

Under his divine sense perception, he could not help but nod. This technique was indeed quite powerful; ordinary Sages would find it difficult to withstand head-on.

But in the next moment, he shook his head again.

"Not enough!"

The Crane Emperor of White Crane Sect also frowned. "Indeed, not enough. Although this technique is vigorous and powerful, it cannot be greater than the formation jointly set up by Niugui and the five demons."

Xianhe Sage hesitated. "I learned from Fenghua Fairy that the Alchemy Sect is also somewhat versed in formations. Perhaps he only wants to use this spell technique to test the depth of that five-demon formation?"

Just testing the waters?

The Crane Emperor and Qin Baixiang accepted this explanation.

However!

That enormous palm print traveled eastward. Wherever it passed, the vast spiritual energy rushed into it like moths to a flame, regardless of consequence. Its pressure grew heavier and heavier.

The distance it traveled went from ten li to a hundred li, and then from a hundred li to a thousand li.

Three thousand li in merely a few dozen breaths.

By the time the Azure Yang Great Handprint crossed above the heads of Xianhe and the other two Sages, the gusting wind whipped their hair and beards wildly. The terrifying pressure made the three of them change color involuntarily.

"This palm!"

"It is actually a technique of accumulating momentum, growing stronger and stronger!"

"Having built up such momentum for so long, it should have the force of a Grand Cultivator's strike!"

But could a Grand Cultivator's strike break a grand formation jointly set up by a Great Demon Emperor leading four ordinary demon emperors?

The three did not doubt the power of this technique, but they doubted the final result.

Within the grand formation, the five demon emperors presiding over it had naturally also detected the commotion outside.

Some changed color, some stirred restlessly.

The leader was a burly middle-aged man. He merely glanced up indifferently and said unconcernedly, "Do not panic. This strike cannot break the formation!"

At these words, everyone calmed down.

Niugui turned his attention back to the nine-colored light river within the formation, his brow furrowing once more.

"Integrating firmament qi into magical power—it actually has such power?"

That nine-colored light river was formed from the combination of pure magical power and firmament qi.

Its killing power was immense. It had once wiped out a fourth-stage mid-phase demon emperor under his command in a single encounter.

But looking at it now, this spell technique not only had great killing power, its defensive power was also not weak.

A great technique that combined offense and defense!

Niugui's heart stirred with desire. If he could refine Jiuling Yuanjun, he wanted not only the Accumulated Void Flower but also the cultivation method for this great technique.

However, Jiuling Yuanjun's magical power was indeed abundant, almost comparable to his own. To refine him would still take a day or two.

To avoid complications from a long delay, Niugui intended to persuade surrender.

"Fellow Daoist Jiuling, if you voluntarily hand over the Accumulated Void Flower and also impart the cultivation method of this nine-colored light river to me, then I can make the decision to let you live. Not only that, but I can also yield this Medicine King Domain to you, helping your Jiuling Sect take this vast territory without shedding a drop of blood!"

Within the nine-colored light river, Jiuling Yuanjun let out a cold laugh.

"Dream on! You'd better think about how to face the Alchemy Sect's technique!"

"The Alchemy Sect?" Niugui was startled. The one who had come was actually that Luo Chen of the Alchemy Sect, who had wounded Qixi?

In that moment of surprise, his expression suddenly changed—to one of shock and fury!

The Azure Yang Great Handprint descended!

A perfected technique, continuously optimized after reaching great completion.

Calmly and unhurriedly executed with hand seals.

Continuously building momentum over three thousand li of travel.

Above the grand formation, Luo Chen slowly pressed down his right palm.

That great handprint, formed from the fusion of vast magical power and spiritual energy, also slowly pressed down following his movement.

The thick light barrier that isolated the outside world let out a piercing, grating sound at this moment, as if it could not bear the strain.

But the light barrier was extremely tough. Even when pressed down a hundred zhang deep, it still did not shatter.

It seemed that it was still slightly lacking in power?

Luo Chen's expression remained unchanged. His pale golden eyes swept across the surroundings, taking in every detail of the grand formation.

His empty left hand formed a seal, casually resting on his right arm. This movement could even be called elegant!

The fingers of his left hand came together like a sword, sliding down along his right arm.

The Azure Yang Great Handprint gained renewed strength from behind, suddenly becoming agile, shifting slightly to one side.

"Break!"

The white-robed Daoist expelled his breath and let out a clear, resonant sound.

The grand formation's light barrier split open with a crack, tearing a long gash.

The terrifying palm print drove straight to the heart of the matter!

Boom!

Only a loud noise was heard, mixed within it a terrified scream.

"Ah..."

A cloud of blood mist exploded. It was the weakest of the fourth-stage demon emperors, who had alone borne the force of this palm strike.

With this demon's fall, the grand formation collapsed without being broken.

The vast spiritual energy within the Azure Yang Great Handprint, no longer constrained by Luo Chen, instantly erupted.

Heaven and earth trembled. Amidst the hazy smoke and dust, several streaks of escaping light shot out.

Among them, a nine-colored escape light rushed straight toward Luo Chen, with four demons chasing closely behind.

Qin Baixiang, Xianhe, and the others, who had been watching the battle, could no longer sit still. They all made their moves.

Spell techniques and magic treasures rained down like a storm.

Boom!

Bang!

Dong!

From the void came the sounds of fierce, short clashes.

When the smoke and dust cleared, Jiuling Yuanjun, his aura weakened, was protected in the middle by Lingfengzi and the other three.

At the very center of the battlefield, two figures stood facing each other.

Niugui rose to his feet. Even in his transformed human form, he was a full zhang tall.

He looked down at the handsome, young white-robed man before him, his expression somewhat grave.

"You are the Alchemy Sect's Luo Chen?"

Luo Chen withdrew his divine sense from Jiuling Yuanjun. The other party had merely overconsumed his magical power, not suffering any serious injury.

Now looking up at the towering Niugui, Luo Chen smiled.

"Since the demon race has already been defeated, why do you linger here reluctantly? This Medicine King Domain was originally human territory. Today, it should be returned."

A flash of anger crossed Niugui's face. "My race has never been defeated. It is merely that you humans are despicable, ambushing Lord Ditian during the peace talks at Green Floating Mountain. Moreover, this Medicine King Domain, and indeed the entire Eastern Wilderness, has been demon territory since ancient times. It is you who should yield!"

Luo Chen shook his head. Arguing about this was meaningless.

In the final analysis, the cultivation world was nothing more than the survival of the fittest!

His white dharma robe fluttered without wind. Luo Chen slowly rose into the air, his gaze cold and sharp. "So, do you still want to fight?"

Niugui showed no weakness, releasing the terrifying domain unique to a Grand Cultivator, pressing it toward Luo Chen.

"Have Jiuling Yuanjun hand over the Accumulated Void Flower!"

Behind Luo Chen, at Jiuling Yuanjun's waist, a small white flower was tucked.

It looked ordinary, but the power contained within it was unforgettable even at a single glance.

This was the ancient miraculous flower—Accumulated Void!

Because of its survival needs, it grew in fragmented spaces, absorbing the origin qi within the chaotic void currents as nourishment.

Therefore, sealed storage spaces simply could not contain this treasure.

That was why Jiuling Yuanjun had no choice but to tuck it at his waist.

Luo Chen did not look back. Facing the Great Demon Emperor's Sage domain, he remained unmoved, his cold gaze sweeping over Niugui and the other three demons.

"Now, with four of you and six of us, the situation has reversed. The advantage is mine. If we truly fight to the end, it will only be mutual destruction."

"But if you want to fight, this Sect will oblige!"

Niugui laughed in extreme anger. "Merely an Adept mid-phase, yet you dare speak such arrogant words. Today, I will teach you that before a Great Demon Emperor, numbers mean nothing!"

Before his words had even fallen, Luo Chen had already struck first.

"Kill!"

On the ground, a flash of light bloomed. Fierce sword qi burst forth from the earth, forming a vast sword formation that encircled Niugui.

Not only that, but a great cauldron also pressed down from high above.

Combined with Luo Chen's own Sage domain, his self-created Triple Prison appeared once more!

Terrifying sword qi cut through the surroundings.

Niugui changed color. When had this man silently laid down such a sword formation?

Could it have been when he broke the formation earlier?

He had no time to think further. With a furious roar—

"Kill!"

A white bone staff fell into his hand, sweeping across the incoming terrifying sword qi.

The other three demon emperors also made their moves.

But compared to Niugui, their situation was not so easy.

Lingfengzi and Qin Baixiang joined forces to take on one.

Xianhe Sage and the Crane Emperor joined forces to take on another.

The last one, at the fourth-stage mid-phase, was taken on by Jiuling Yuanjun, who still had the strength to fight.

Jiuling Yuanjun could no longer sustain the execution of the Great Nine Heavens Firmament River, but even his other methods were far from ordinary.

After encircling his enemy, his sharp eyes swept over the other three battlefields. A plan had already formed in his heart.

"Fellow Daoists, quickly finish off the enemies before you, then assist the Alchemy Sect!"

Even without his telling them, when Lingfengzi and the others saw Luo Chen personally charging into the sword formation holding a blood sword, they knew that the battle against Niugui was also a great pressure for Luo Chen.

For now, they could only first resolve their respective enemies before freeing their hands to aid Luo Chen.

The Medicine King Domain, which had suffered repeated devastation, welcomed another calamity today.

And compared to the past, today's battle was even more brutal.

The four battlefields began to spread outward continuously. Even the residual ripples of the battle were not something ordinary living beings could withstand.

If the great battle continued, regardless of who ultimately won or lost, the Medicine King Domain itself would be destroyed.

Perhaps it would become the next Overlord Blade Domain!

...

High above the nine heavens.

Two figures stood one behind the other within the clouds.

The leader was dressed in a black robe, his magical power fluctuations faintly visible.

Divine Fire Sage stood half a step behind, looking down with a fearful expression.

"I never thought Luo Chen would actually be this formidable. In a frontal battle, he can even take on an enraged Great Demon Emperor!"

The Divine Origin Sage, shrouded in his black robe, let out a low laugh. "Someone who can take one of my strikes—you didn't think it was just luck, did you?"

Divine Fire Sage swallowed his saliva.

He really had thought that before.

In fact, many people thought the same, believing that Luo Chen merely mastered a single powerful divine ability.

Aside from that divine ability, he was just a slightly more powerful Adept mid-phase cultivator. How could he compare to a Grand Cultivator?

But looking at it now, the result was completely different.

Simultaneously controlling five sword pellets to lay down a sword formation, driving a great cauldron, wielding a blood sword—just the depth of magical power and strength of soul foundation displayed in these actions alone was not something an ordinary Adept mid-phase cultivator could match.

Divine Fire Sage's lips trembled as he said worriedly, "Then at this rate, doesn't that mean Luo Chen and his group are sure to win?"

Divine Origin Sage shook his head. "We are both here. Where do you suppose Bi Zheng is?"

Divine Fire's eyes suddenly widened!

"Could Bi Zheng make a move against Luo Chen?"

"Lord Aokuang long ago gave the order to definitely kill Luo Chen, who has ties with Han Zhan, to avenge his descendant Aoxiao. Bi Zheng is the strongest expert who has followed Aokuang the longest. Before returning to the Million Great Mountains, he absolutely will not miss this opportunity."

Divine Origin Sage spoke calmly, his gaze falling on Luo Chen. The greed on his face could not help but appear once more.

"Moreover, in the great human-demon war, the one who lost the most is actually someone else!"

Divine Fire's whole body trembled. According to his senior brother's words, could there be others who would make a move?

But what did all this have to do with them?

Divine Origin Sage suddenly pulled down his hood, revealing a face familiar to Divine Fire.

But compared to that face from a year ago, this face now bore several characteristics of the old monster Motian.

Divine Fire lowered his head, not daring to look closely.

Divine Origin Sage let out a faint sigh. "You should have already guessed it back in Taihu Domain. Actually, Jin Ling discovered it earlier than you, but absorbing the Sages of Linmu and the others was a necessity for me. In the battle at Green Parasol Mountain, my physical body was destroyed by the Nirvana Sacred Flame, and my Sage was on the verge of perishing. Only by absorbing the Sage magical power of fellow disciples from the same sect could I barely sustain my life force."

Divine Fire, with his head lowered, bit his lips tightly, his eyes filled with terror, but he dared not utter a single word.

He was afraid!

Afraid that he would be the next one!

Divine Origin Sage's words continued.

"But after absorbing the Sages of so many fellow disciples from the same origin, I have become less and less like myself. Moreover, because of the mixed magical power, I have been forced onto the path of being assimilated by heaven and earth."

"Continuing like this, I will die!"

"Unless, before my Sage perishes, I can rush to the Ascendant stage in one go."

"But before forcibly breaking through to the Ascendant stage, I still need a physical body! A powerful physical body!"

"Luo Chen is my best target!"

"Although I do not know why his five spirit roots have disappeared, only revealing a Fire Spirit Body, among the present age, only his physical body can bear my vast and mixed magical power."

"Now, I am using the demon race's hand to deal with Luo Chen. Even if the Nether Origin Sect investigates afterward, their anger will not fall on me."

"As for the future?"

Divine Origin Sage suddenly stopped speaking, his gaze turning to a place not far away.

Two streaks of escaping light no longer concealed themselves. Their vast auras filled the wilds, surging downward toward the earth.

On the ground.

Within the sword formation, Luo Chen was locked in fierce battle with Niugui.

Relying on the Triple Prison, he was not suppressed by the other's domain.

Combined with the Primordial Unity Cauldron, the Yuantu Sword, and the Great Five Elements Sword Formation, he was temporarily fighting on equal terms with his opponent.

This confirmed his earlier speculation. Without using the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger, he truly had the strength to fight a Grand Cultivator.

But this state would not last long, given the terrifying drain on his magical power.

No matter!

As long as Jiuling Yuanjun and the others freed up their hands, his pressure would sharply decrease.

At that time...

Luo Chen suddenly retreated.

In his line of sight, Niugui ignored the sword qi slicing toward him, thrusting his white bone staff into the ground. His entire body raised his head and let out a long roar.

Visibly, his physical form began to change.

Sharp ox horns, a massive ox head, and jagged white bones growing wildly.

"Revealing your true form?"

Luo Chen frowned deeply. "Did I put that much pressure on him?"

But in the next moment, his expression changed.

From the earth, countless white bones rose up, forming a mountain of bone.

In an instant, they broke through his Great Five Elements Sword Formation and even enveloped it within.

Luo Chen continuously fled outward, and the white bones continuously grew, stabbing toward him from all directions.

Niugui moved his massive bone body, reaching out a hand to grab at him from afar.

Within the bone mountain, a giant bone hand burst forth from the earth, grasping at Luo Chen, who was darting and weaving through the air like a bird.

At the same time, from outside the bone mountain, two overwhelming attacks struck together!

The other three battlefields, locked in fierce combat, were forced to a halt by this abrupt change.

Jiuling Yuanjun, Lingfengzi, and the others stared in shock at this scene, their gazes fixed on the white figure rising and falling within the bone mountain.
