# Chapter 651: The Celestial Phenomenon Descends, Golden Sky and Black Sea

While Luo Chen secluded himself for bitter cultivation, the outside world surged with wind and clouds.

In the literal sense.

Above the Sunken Sea, the Void Burning Wind that had originally appeared irregularly began appearing in great quantities beyond control.

Even three years after the battle between Blood Wanderer and Jiaoren Emperor Flow Lord, the clouds above the Sunken Sea began to stop moving, gradually transforming into a flowing golden color.

Golden sky and black sea formed the Northern Sea's most magnificent sight.

Such a heavenly phenomenon naturally drew countless cultivators to the shore of the Sunken Sea.

But with the black turbid seawater that corroded everything and the Void Burning Wind that swept down myriad living beings blocking the way, all cultivators with intentions of entering to explore were kept outside.

Even those who could enter could at most reach the extreme edge of the outer region and advance no farther.

Under these circumstances, some who knew the inside story also began acting.

Ding Yi was one of them.

On Moyun Peak, Ding Yi paced back and forth inside a cave residence, anxiety showing on his face.

"Dao Lan has disappeared, causing my understanding of the situation there to grow even more blurred. What about your side?"

Cave Master Moyun, seated opposite him, said in a deep voice, "The intelligence I obtained through my contacts says that although the golden sky and black sea phenomenon has appeared above the Sunken Sea, no spiritual-qi backflow vortex has appeared yet. In other words, the ruins have not truly opened."

Golden sky and black sea, spiritual qi flowing backward. These were the complete heavenly signs of the ruins opening.

Ding Yi shook his head. "It may not be that they have not opened. Perhaps they have already opened, and your contacts simply have not discovered it yet. Perhaps someone has already gone in early and is wantonly exploring the ruins' treasures."

Cave Master Moyun frowned. "Then what should we do?"

Ding Yi suddenly stopped walking.

"I intend to explore the Sunken Sea. If I find a suitable vortex, I will enter ahead of time."

"But what about Devil Lord Azure Yang?"

Cave Master Moyun mentioned Luo Chen, making Ding Yi even more agitated.

"That fellow has been crazily purchasing medicinal materials these past years. He likely wants to refine pills and step into the late Golden Core stage before setting out, gaining a few more degrees of assurance."

"Refine pills?" Cave Master Moyun was startled.

"You probably do not know. He is also a formidable alchemist. Ordinary second- and third-tier pills are no problem for him."

This time, Cave Master Moyun was truly astonished.

Was there such a prodigy in the world?

So young, yet proficient in both alchemy and artifact forging, with considerable achievements in both?

"I will make a trip to Peng Lake." Ding Yi exhaled and descended the mountain before Cave Master Moyun could react.

However, when he arrived at the shore of Peng Lake, what faced him was layer upon layer of cloud and mist sealing the way.

Within that cloud and mist, terrifying killing intent spread.

One glance was enough to tell that several grand formations had been activated at the same time. One absolutely could not step inside lightly.

Ding Yi was helpless. He could only release a transmission talisman, then turn and leave.

He had already decided to first go to the Sunken Sea and examine the specific situation.

No matter how Devil Lord Azure Yang delayed, when the time came, Ding Yi would urge him to leave. If that truly did not work, he and Cave Master Moyun would join hands to break into Peng Lake and make Devil Lord Azure Yang give an explanation.

At Peng Lake, beneath Apricot Flower Peak, on a plain with a radius of more than ten li, an enormous figure moved freely back and forth. Sounds of breaking through the air exploded again and again like thunder.

Another tiny figure stood on Apricot Flower Peak, watching the scene.

Suddenly, the enormous figure paused and looked toward the summit.

"Senior, come!"

Han Zhan smiled faintly and pressed down with an empty hand.

Heaven and earth spiritual qi suddenly turned, and a vast mass of ownerless spiritual qi came under his control and pressed toward the nine-zhang giant.

Luo Chen's expression was cold. A domineering power suddenly emerged from his body and charged outward.

The All-Encompassing Fire Prison appeared again, forcefully resisting the Sage domain.

The domain power he had once feared like snakes and scorpions seemed to have become evenly matched after the All-Encompassing Fire Prison appeared.

"Not enough!"

Han Zhan gave a hum and waved his hand. The power of the domain immediately increased again.

Under this surging pressure of heaven and earth, an ordinary Golden Core cultivator would long since have fallen flat and been completely unable to mobilize mana.

But Luo Chen still stood in the air, seemingly not greatly affected.

It was not that there was no effect. His body began slowly sinking.

It could be seen that a full-power Sage domain was still effective against him.

But Luo Chen remained unpanicked. His hands formed a seal, and his body immediately changed, suppressing the void like a mountain.

It was the Mountain-Suppressing Form from the Extreme Mountain Six Forms.

Relying on the All-Encompassing Fire Prison and the Mountain-Suppressing Form, one internal and one external killing move, he finally resisted Han Zhan's Sage domain for a short time.

Seeing this, Han Zhan was rather surprised.

"Not bad. You really did explore it out, boy. With your own strength, you can resist the might of heaven and earth."

Luo Chen said slowly, "The so-called might of heaven and earth is merely a Sage borrowing a trace of heaven and earth's momentum. It cannot be considered the complete might of heaven and earth. With my inside and outside united, even if I cannot defeat it, I can still preserve a certain ability to move."

At this point, he slowly bent. His enormous body seemed to become a leopard about to hunt.

The next moment, he stomped.

The void exploded.

With Voidburst Step, he broke through a hundred zhang of space.

While in the air, Luo Chen leapt high, and his right foot kicked out like thunder.

Thunder Kick!

Boom!

An afterimage arrived abruptly, landing before Han Zhan.

An astonishing impact force also broke through the air.

Han Zhan smiled faintly and drifted back.

This world-shocking kick actually failed to cause him any injury.

Luo Chen withdrew his leg and frowned.

"The distance... is still too far?"

He did not continue pursuing Han Zhan. His gaze fell on Apricot Flower Peak below.

With one palm, he cut through the air. Vigorous blood qi rolled out, then he struck down with a palm.

With an earthshaking explosion, Apricot Flower Peak below immediately split into pieces, countless stones falling like rain.

With only physical power, one palm broke a mountain.

It seemed somewhat like the Mountain-Opening Monument-Breaking Palm he had learned in the past, but it was clearly a level more profound.

The Extreme Mountain Six Forms lacked the Mountain-Splitting Form and Extreme Mountain Form, leaving only Mountain-Suppressing Form, Mountain-Pulling Form, Mountain-Breaking Form, and Mountain-Colliding Form.

The one Luo Chen picked up fastest was precisely the Mountain-Breaking Form, which shared similarities with the Mountain-Opening Monument-Breaking Palm.

He had also gained some grasp of the other three killing moves over these years.

Amid the rain of shattered stones, Han Zhan leisurely flew over.

Although his core remained that soul-nourishing wooden plaque, the external Nascent Soul miniature had become quite solid, showing that he had recovered well over these years and now only lacked a fleshly body.

Looking at the shattered peak below, he shook his head.

"You really are aiming to fight Sages, boy."

First the All-Encompassing Fire Prison that resisted domains, then extreme physical killing moves. Clearly, Luo Chen intended to avoid the enemy's strengths and attack their weaknesses.

Even Sages did not necessarily all cultivate body refinement.

Luo Chen said in a deep voice, "Preparedness prevents trouble. I am simply making the worst plans for everything."

"But you must know that a Perfected Being's methods are extraordinary. Even if they cannot use the domain power most effective at suppressing Golden Core cultivators, their ordinary methods are still above yours. Especially since they are not dead, unmoving mountains standing there for you to hit."

Luo Chen frowned. "With my Ninety Thousand Li movement speed, plus Voidburst Step, Thunder Kick, and other extreme-speed movement arts, I still cannot get close?"

"Very close!" Han Zhan praised him, but his tone still carried regret. "Yet still lacking by a little."

Luo Chen opened his mouth and was speechless for a moment.

A difference of a hair's breadth was the gap between life and death.

"Unless this fleshly body of yours goes one step further!"

Luo Chen looked at Han Zhan in surprise.

The other party shrugged. "I can naturally see your rapid progress in the fleshly body. I fear some Sage-level demon cultivators' bodies are already inferior to yours. But if you want a qualitative change, you are still a step short."

Transformed demon cultivators still walked the path of core formation and Nascent Soul condensation. They did not rely entirely on the fleshly body.

Some extreme existences, after transforming, would even refine their own limbs into lifebound magic treasures.

"One step further?"

Luo Chen murmured inwardly. Soon. Very soon.

These years, he had crazily practiced physical killing moves. Relying on the system, his progress was rapid.

Even incomplete arts could be used to take the strong points and make up for weaknesses, forming alternative killing moves.

For example, the combination of Voidburst Step and Thunder Kick, or the advancement of Mountain-Opening Monument-Breaking Palm with Mountain-Breaking Form, and even the internal-external combination of the All-Encompassing Fire Prison and Mountain-Suppressing Form.

Not only that, he had also combined incomplete physical arts such as Five Monument Hands and Flowing Cloud Immortal-Seizing Finger to complete an inspiration, creating a new armor-breaking killing move.

That inspiration came from Purple Spirit Island, where, to vent his anger, he had forcefully killed an Abyss Crocodile.

At that time, he had forcibly peeled open the Abyss Crocodile's incomparably thick scales.

While practicing physical arts, Luo Chen had a sudden thought. Could this move be used against the defensive magic treasures of human cultivators?

After researching it over these years, he had achieved minor success.

He named it Overlord Removes Armor. It contained eight hand methods: finger, palm, fist, claw, flick, slap, clasp, and smash. In batches, he would inject vigorous blood qi into the enemy's defensive magic treasure, then instantly detonate it.

In this way, he could break the opponent's divine sense control.

It could be said that in just three years, Luo Chen's development of this fleshly body had far surpassed the previous hundred-plus years.

Such results would seem inconceivable to outsiders, but Luo Chen knew this was merely the natural result of accumulated foundation.

He had already possessed the base. He only needed to use a little thought and change his attack methods.

Using the martial arts novels he had read before as an example, it was probably like completing external moves after internal strength reached great success.

Of course, these were all minor details.

The most important thing was still his body-refinement progress.

"I will go back first. Remember to make the sword case, or else you can only look at the Seven Baleful Swords and not use them." Han Zhan gave a greeting and drifted back to his cave residence.

The sword case he mentioned was an unusual artifact he had told Luo Chen about not long ago.

With this sword case holding the Seven Baleful Swords, Luo Chen could attach divine sense to it and use them somewhat even without refining the swords.

The most important purpose was that when Han Zhan ordinarily did not use the Seven Baleful Swords, Luo Chen would carry them for him.

According to Han Zhan, a Nascent Soul body had no fleshly body to separate it and could not be tainted by too much baleful qi, so he needed such a convenient sword case.

Luo Chen did not mind.

With his current artifact-forging skill, refining magic treasures might take effort, but a mere unusual artifact would not take long.

Thinking about what Han Zhan had said, Luo Chen returned to Peng Island.

"One step further. It should not take too long now."

On the attribute panel, his body-refinement progress had already reached Ancient Wilderness Third Tier: 98/100.

There was now only a countable distance from breaking through to fourth tier.

From this, it could be seen that Luo Chen's decision to slaughter on a grand scale in the Sunken Sea and absorb massive vitality had been incomparably correct.

Under ordinary circumstances, how could body refinement be so fast?

Just refining fourth-tier Black Emperor Paste required the body to spend large amounts of time adapting.

Just as Luo Chen was estimating his advancement time, his gaze paused. He saw the transmission talisman outside Peng Island bumping around like a headless fly.

He reached out, and the transmission talisman ignited without wind.

After listening to Ding Yi's somewhat anxious divine sense message, Luo Chen frowned.

"Golden sky and black sea. The heavenly phenomenon has appeared?"

*(End of Chapter)*
