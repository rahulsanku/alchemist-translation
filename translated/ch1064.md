# Chapter 1064: Luo Tian Empties Its Nest, Immortal City Grand Gathering

Azure Sky Golden Domain, a desolate forbidden land.

A black-robed Daoist sat cross-legged before a golden tiger. His nascent soul had already condensed, and his divine sense slowly submerged into the golden giant tiger's sea of consciousness.

Throughout this process, the beast before him maintained an instinctive resistance, yet before Luo Chen's powerful divine sense, it had no power to resist.

Finally, Luo Chen once again touched the depths of Emperor Slaughter's soul.

There, a faint green mark flickered, appearing as if it might dissipate at any moment, yet it tenaciously persisted.

The moment he saw that green mark, Luo Chen couldn't help but praise.

"As expected of a mighty Ascendant cultivator. The enslavement seal left by Chunyuan has persisted for a hundred years and still remains. Truly stubborn!"

Emperor Slaughter, son of the ancient demon.

Back then, when peace talks between the human and demon races in the Azure Sky Domain failed, a great battle ensued, and the ancient demon Emperor Heaven fell.

At the moment of his fall, his vast mana swept the surroundings, destroying everything in the Azure Sky Domain.

Only his offspring was preserved.

Not only that, but Emperor Slaughter had slept here for over a hundred years, continuously absorbing the residual power of the ancient demon.

Unfortunately, the Azure Sky Domain was deep within the Eastern Wilderness human territory. How could they allow him to sleep soundly and absorb power at his leisure?

The mighty Chunyuan from the Central Continent had discovered this place.

Chunyuan could have killed him, but perhaps coveting a seed of an ancient demon, she did not strike him down. She merely left behind an enslavement seal, planning to take him under her wing once he awakened.

However, Chunyuan herself probably never expected to fall at Broken Soul Cliff.

According to Luo Chen's calculations, since Chunyuan was dead, the enslavement seal she had so carefully arranged would gradually dissipate over time.

But looking at it now, Chunyuan's mastery in this area was not something time could erase.

Or rather, not something a mere hundred years could accomplish.

After observing carefully for a long while, Luo Chen had a clear understanding.

"It is indeed an enslavement seal constructed from divine sense. Therefore, even if only a single thought remains, it still endures, fresh and undispersed."

"If I hadn't been vigilant back then and refrained from arrogantly trying to break this seal with powerful soul force, Emperor Slaughter might have perished from a single careless mistake."

"Not only that, but the seal also contains a strand of mana imbued with the origin of wood."

"Breaking a divine sense enslavement seal is not difficult for me now. As for this wood-origin mana..."

Luo Chen's eyes narrowed slightly as he pondered deeply.

About a few days later, Luo Chen's spirits lifted.

He carefully pressed his palm against Emperor Slaughter's head through the air, and a wisp of cyan-green mana slowly emerged.

Luo Chen's mana was fire-attributed, painstakingly refined over hundreds of years—domineering, fierce, and extremely destructive. This cyan-green mana, however, was incredibly gentle, like a spring breeze. The two seemed utterly incompatible, yet when Luo Chen displayed them now, there was no sense of incongruity.

It was merely a matter of converting mana attributes, which was not difficult for him.

What was difficult was that this strand of Luo Chen's mana did not contain the origin power of fire, but rather the true intent of Withering-Bloom.

The surroundings were filled with metal-attributed origin qi of heaven and earth. For Luo Chen to continuously unleash Withering-Bloom mana, he could only rely entirely on his own foundation.

"Little by little!"

"With my persistent erosion, it will take no more than a few years to completely wear away Chunyuan's enslavement seal."

"At that time, I will have a fourth-stage great demon emperor who masters the origin power of metal. This will undoubtedly be a great help for my assault on the Central Continent!"

...

Within the Azure Sky Golden Domain, time seemed to slow down, everything proceeding at a leisurely pace.

But outside, across the entire Eastern Wilderness, things were boiling over.

As Little White and Black King delivered invitation letters to various mountain gates, a piece of news spread like wildfire.

**[Alchemy Master Luo Chen, Shouyang Dao Discussion]**

In this news, both the title of Alchemy Master Luo Chen and the once-renowned Shouyang Dao Discussion from the human-demon war carried immense prestige.

Combined, they carried even more weight.

But the true reason the Eastern Wilderness was boiling over was just one thing!

**The opportunity for Ascension!**

It was said that Alchemy Master Luo Chen would, at the Shouyang Mountain Dao Discussion Assembly, exchange experiences on breaking through to the Ascension stage with all the Sages of the Eastern Wilderness.

This sounded somewhat laughable, after all, Luo Chen himself had not yet advanced to Ascension!

But do not forget the title of "Alchemy Master"!

If he had concocted some divine pill or miraculous medicine that could help one break through to the Ascension stage, that was also highly possible.

Especially a hundred years ago, when the Alchemy Master had made a grand gesture by producing ten Nascent Soul Pills at once, giving the major sects of the Eastern Wilderness, whose top-tier strength had been depleted after the war, a chance to catch their breath. This was clear proof.

The Alchemy Master had established a reputation for refining realm-breaking pills.

It could be said that whether it was the vast network of connections Luo Chen had built as a high-level alchemist, the grace of the Nascent Soul Pills a hundred years ago, or the yearning of cultivators for the opportunity of Ascension, countless Eastern Wilderness experts were flocking towards Lingtian Pass.

Shouyang Mountain was located within Lingtian Pass.

This was originally the core region of the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world!

Its area of influence alone covered five Sage-level sects, and it had shone brilliantly during the two-hundred-year human-demon war, acting like a massive reef that firmly blocked the demon tide beneath Lingtian Pass.

Even after the war ended, Lingtian Pass still existed and grew increasingly prosperous.

Its permanent population was over a million.

Remember, these were all bona fide cultivators, not mortals.

An ordinary immortal city might have tens of thousands, or even over a hundred thousand cultivators. But Lingtian Pass had millions of cultivators—this was already a super immortal city!

Looking across the entire Mountains and Seas Realm, no second comparable existence could be found.

And this number of cultivators was still increasing crazily as the date of the Shouyang Dao Discussion Assembly approached.

During the human-demon war, the city walls of Lingtian Pass had been mostly destroyed. After the war ended, those ancient walls were directly demolished.

Without the no-fly formation covering the entire city, cultivators came and went freely.

For a time, above and around Lingtian Pass, escape light flowed like a river, cultivators fell like rain, and the bustling scene was even more lively than the peak of the Eastern Wilderness before the human-demon war!

With more people came more conflicts.

Various vendetta killings, robberies, and disputes of pride played out daily in Lingtian Pass.

But this place was not entirely without order.

On the main bustling streets of the city, each one was guarded by experts from Adept-level great sects or even Sage-level sects, to prevent pointless conflicts from affecting their business.

Moreover, another behemoth stood above Lingtian Pass.

That was the **Lingtian Alliance Council**!

This was an alliance formed by five Sage-level sects bordering Lingtian Pass, led by Splendid Flower Palace, assisted by White Crane Gate, and including Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion, Hundred Beasts Sect, and the Eastern Sun Branch Sect. Together, they controlled a vast amount of interests in Lingtian Pass.

Facing the terrifying influx of people drawn by Luo Chen's revival of the Shouyang Dao Discussion Assembly, the Lingtian Alliance Council reached a consensus from the start: they vacated Shouyang Mountain, waiting for Luo Chen's arrival.

From this perspective, the title of "Alchemy Master" indeed carried significant weight.

Before he even arrived, merely by his power of summoning, the local cultivators had vacated the "venue."

Not only that, but the Alliance Council also dispatched a large number of cultivators to maintain order near Shouyang Mountain as much as possible.

Of course, the scope of this order maintenance was limited to cultivators below the Sage stage.

When it came to battles and contests between Sages, the Lingtian Alliance Council had little binding power.

The human-demon war had ended over a hundred years ago, and the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world had slowly recovered some of its vitality from the ruins. The number of experts was emerging in an endless stream.

At this time of convergence, some newly promoted Sages couldn't help but spar and exchange blows, making a name for themselves. This was very normal.

In fact, it wasn't just newly promoted Sages. Even some long-established old experts, after years of solitude, had secretly fought among themselves.

Naturally, some battle records were released by those with ulterior motives.

Among them, Taiyue Sage of Mount Tai Sect became the center of everyone's discussion!

Whether in the former Eastern Wilderness cultivation world or during the two-hundred-year human-demon war, Taiyue Sage was hardly considered a top-tier existence.

His mid-Sage cultivation level put him below the late-stage great Sages above, and he also found it difficult to have absolute suppression power against the early-stage Sages below.

But after the human-demon war ended, he went into seclusion for a full hundred years.

As soon as he emerged, it was for the Alchemy Master's revival of the Shouyang Dao Discussion Assembly.

Then he made successive moves, challenging several long-famous great Sages.

Heavenly Capital Sage of Heavenly Capital Sect!

Peng Xiang Sage of White Deer Cave!

Nine Spirits Primordial Lord of Nine Spirits Sect!

These three—one was a grandmaster of formation dao, one was the supreme elder of White Deer Cave—both were long-established.

And although Nine Spirits Primordial Lord had only broken through to the great Sage realm for a little over a hundred years, even before his breakthrough, he had a reputation for possessing mana as abundant as a great Sage. In particular, Nine Spirits Primordial Lord had once mastered a great killing move comparable to a divine ability, which once dominated the human-demon battlefield. Now, having stepped into the late Sage stage for over a hundred years, his strength must have risen to another level.

It could be said that none of these three were easy opponents!

Yet Taiyue Sage's sparring records against these three were a terrifying two wins and one draw!

Even if he had also broken through to the great Sage realm, he shouldn't have such an overwhelming advantage, right?

This rumor led many to question its accuracy.

But those three great Sages, standing at the pinnacle of the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world, did not come forward to refute it.

This made people think.

Had Taiyue Sage truly soared, not only breaking through in realm but also explosively increasing his strength to the point of suppressing those of the same rank?

Soon after, another piece of inside information circulated within the Sage circle.

**[Taiyue Sage received the help of the Alchemy Master, not only breaking through to the late Sage stage but also comprehending the true intent of the laws. If no accident occurs in the future, Ascension is already a foregone conclusion.]**

This news once again shocked the Sage circle.

Especially combined with the four words "opportunity for Ascension" written on the Alchemy Master's invitation, it made people restless.

Who didn't know that Taiyue Sage had once been trapped at the sixth layer of the Sage stage for a long time, and was a single-element heavenly spirit root cultivator who cultivated the slowest at the Sage stage? If he could reach this point, if one obtained the help of the Alchemy Master, could they go even further?

For a time, many people sought an audience with Taiyue Sage, hoping to first learn something about the Alchemy Master.

But Taiyue Sage refused to see anyone, only saying, "Wait!"

Wait for what?

Everyone knew in their hearts—naturally, they were waiting for the formal convening of the Shouyang Dao Discussion Assembly, waiting for the appearance of Alchemy Master Luo Chen.

However, as the time drew near, Alchemy Master Luo Chen was still nowhere to be seen.

But one day, a special group arrived at Lingtian Pass.

The special nature of this group lay in two points.

First, their strength was formidable, far surpassing that of the major sects. Even White Deer Cave and Eastern Sun Sect, which had preserved their strength relatively well during the human-demon war, were not much stronger than them.

A full six Sages led the group, and one of them was the battle-crazed demon who had once crossed realms to defeat a Sage!

Second, their attire was uniform, all wearing dharma robes embroidered with the emblem of a cauldron supporting the sun.

And this sect emblem of a cauldron supporting the sun belonged to only one sect in the entire Eastern Wilderness.

That was **Luo Tian Sect**!

Thus, the world knew the origin of this special group.

The Luo Tian Sect founded by Alchemy Master Luo Chen!

This also shocked countless cultivators even more!

Who didn't know that back then, apart from Luo Chen, the Luo Tian Sect had only a few cats and dogs, unable to produce even a second Sage.

Unexpectedly, in less than four hundred years since its founding, it had produced six, no, counting the battle king Wang Yuan, a full seven Sage-level combat forces.

With such foundation, the world no longer doubted the abilities of the Alchemy Master.

A powerful alchemist could indeed flourish a sect.

Let alone one hailed as the foremost in alchemy since the Alchemy Saint, Alchemy Master Luo Chen?

The experts of Luo Tian Sect had emptied their nest. The first thing they did upon arriving at Lingtian Pass was to take over Shouyang Mountain from the Lingtian Alliance Council, then begin arranging the Dao Discussion Assembly venue and entertaining the many experts who had traveled from afar.

After everything was prepared, everyone was waiting for that point in time.

Seven years after the invitations were sent out, the seventh day of the seventh month!

Whether this time had any special significance, the world did not know. But the date set by the Alchemy Master for the Shouyang Dao Discussion Assembly was this day.

...

*Swish!*

A flash of cold light streaked across the sky.

A man with white eyebrows, ordinary in appearance but with an extraordinary temperament, pierced through clouds and mist, crossing mountains and seas.

On his shoulder lay a centipede with six wings, occasionally spitting out cold air.

The man looked down at the centipede and muttered to himself, "I managed to break through to the Sage stage thanks to pills, but you're stuck at the fourth stage. I have already felt the power of the Alchemy Master. If I can get his help at this Shouyang Dao Discussion Assembly, perhaps you can also take that final step."

Just as the man was muttering, the wind and clouds ahead suddenly changed color!

*Boom!*

A thunderous roar echoed between heaven and earth.

Then, countless golden wind astral winds erupted, completely blocking this stretch of the sky.

The man's expression changed drastically as he hurriedly stopped.

He was not unfamiliar with the terrain ahead. It was known as the Azure Sky Forbidden Domain, paired with the nearby Spirit Severance Domain.

One prevented living beings from entering, the other was unsuitable for cultivators to train in. Both were products of the human-demon war back then.

Logically, people had grown accustomed to these existences.

But why was there such an abrupt change?

His eyes stared fixedly ahead.

Within the golden wind astral winds, a vortex spontaneously formed, spinning continuously in the sky.

Like a funnel, countless golden winds flowed down into it and then disappeared.

And the Azure Sky Forbidden Domain, which blocked all living beings from entering, also began to rapidly disappear, countless golden winds vanishing strangely.

At the same time, an immensely powerful aura crazily spread in all directions.

The white-browed man's eyes widened. "A great Sage?"

"No!"

"It's a great demon emperor!"

Terrifying demonic qi, uncontrollably climbing step by step, shot up into the sky, tearing a huge hole in the clouds for ten thousand li, revealing the clear blue sky.

The white-browed man's heart and soul were about to split. How could such a terrifying great demon emperor be lurking in the heart of human territory?

Just as he was about to flee!

Suddenly!

"It's not time yet. Stop!"

A calm voice rang out. The demonic qi that had been climbing step by step suddenly halted, then rolled back.

The white-browed man looked up and saw, among the mountains, a huge golden tiger prostrate on the ground, respectfully lying there.

And before it stood a black-robed Daoist, his hand pressing on its head.

Under his hand, the golden giant tiger was as docile as a house cat.

Was this the great demon emperor that had just caused heaven and earth to change color?

Who was that black-robed Daoist?

The white-browed man swallowed his saliva and turned to leave.

But unexpectedly, a voice leisurely fell into his ears.

"Meeting is fate. Fellow Daoist, please stay."

The white-browed man took a deep breath, forcefully turned around, and although he was looking down from a height, he cupped his hands and bowed to the black-robed Daoist.

"Junior Bo Hanjun. May I ask how to address Senior?"

"Senior?" The black-robed Daoist's lips curled slightly as he said slowly, "Luo Chen. You may also call me Alchemy Master."

(Chapter End)
