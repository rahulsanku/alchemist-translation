# Chapter 890: The Sect's Future, the Youth on the Black Tiger

"Is that... Five Elements Mountain?"

In a forest that blotted out the sky and sun, a white-robed cultivator stood on a tree branch, gazing through the gaps in the leaves at the five great mountains in the distance, and... the divine mountain that pierced the clouds at the very center.

With Luo Chen's experience, even from hundreds of li away, he could discern the feng shui trends and the secrets of earth-qi circulation.

Five Elements Mountain, as the name suggested, was divided according to the five phases.

Surrounding the Divine Origin Mountain, they were both sheltered by it and supplied it.

Under the circulation of the five qi, they provided a vast amount of heaven and earth spiritual qi for Divine Origin Mountain.

Speaking of it, this method of nurturing a spirit land was somewhat similar to the Five Peaks Valley-Canopy Art of Azure Pill Valley, except the configuration here was far, far superior.

On his left shoulder, Black King's muffled voice came out: "The Five Elements Divine Sect returned to their old site seven years ago, reopened their mountain gate, and vigorously recruited disciples and followers. Although they haven't yet recovered to their peak state, they are gradually regaining their vitality. I heard that the sect master, Divine Fire Sage, selected ten Adept-stage disciples for focused cultivation, intending to nurture several Sage-stage cultivators for the sect within a hundred years. Not only that..."

Listening to the intelligence Black King had gathered over the years, Luo Chen could feel how much effort Divine Fire had poured into the sect amidst the ruins awaiting reconstruction.

If it were before, he might have sighed at the correctness of each of the other's measures.

But at this moment, there was only a cold sneer.

"Heh."

Having laughed, Luo Chen turned and left.

Black King was somewhat surprised. "Master, aren't you going to find that old bastard and settle the score?"

Some things, though unspoken, Black King understood, and Luo Chen knew them clearly in his heart.

The three great Demon Emperors suddenly joining forces to attack Luo Chen back then was by no means without reason.

Although each had their own stated motives—for the Accumulated Empty Flower, to vent anger for Aokuang, to avenge their father and brother...

How could these reasons have so coincidentally led to a cooperative alliance?

Huang Man had long since fled deep into the Million Great Mountains; how had he received advance news to ambush Luo Chen?

Bi Zheng was trapped in the Five Elements Domain, delayed by the Five Elements Divine Sect; how had he broken free from his restraints and traveled ten thousand li to attack?

There must have been someone running around, connecting them all!

And that person's identity had already been exposed the moment the heavenly light scattered and the shadow emerged.

Divine Origin Sage!

Luo Chen would not mistake it. Even though his attention had been drawn to Azure Frost at the time, the Divine Origin Sage, who had appeared in haste and fled in panic, was still fresh in his memory.

Now, with the enemy before him, he turned and left. How could Black King not be curious?

After all, this master of his had never been a magnanimous person.

In the forest, a strong wind blew, stirring up waves of pine trees.

The white-robed cultivator's unbound black hair fluttered in the wind, a cold and fierce smile hanging at the corner of his mouth.

"Don't worry. That day will come!"

Having said this, he turned without lingering and left.

For Luo Chen as he was now, his fear and caution toward Divine Origin Sage had grown smaller and smaller.

Perhaps before, he could only barely exchange a few moves.

But after re-cultivating his lifebound technique and reaching Sage Fifth Layer, relying on various means, he was confident he could fight the other to a standstill.

However, to completely annihilate him, Luo Chen still lacked some certainty.

Divine Origin Sage was not like Huang Man and the other great Demon Emperors. This man was a long-established human Sage, with profound and unfathomable Dao cultivation.

The key point was that cultivators of the Five Elements Divine Sect were already famous throughout the Eastern Wilderness for their multitude of techniques.

Five-element spells came at their fingertips, and numerous treasures were at their command.

Even Azure Frost had failed to take him down back then. Even Joyous Union Ancestor and Monk Huiping had died on Green Parasol Mountain, with only Divine Origin surviving—this alone was enough to show how formidable his methods were.

If Luo Chen did not act, fine. But if he did, it must be with the force of a thunderbolt to slay him, leaving no future trouble behind.

Now was indeed not the time to make a move.

Still, stirring up some trouble was not difficult.

...

Within Divine Origin Mountain.

Sect Master Divine Fire Sage led ten Adept-stage cultivators into the Supreme Elder's cave dwelling.

"Senior Brother, these are the ten disciples I have carefully selected. Each has extraordinary aptitude and abundant dharma power."

"More importantly, they have all experienced the human-demon war and possess unyielding hearts!"

"If we invest some resources to nurture them, they will surely have hope of reaching the Sage realm!"

The three essentials of immortal cultivation: heart, aptitude, and fortune.

For ordinary people, one always had to be prioritized.

But for disciples of a sect with conditions, naturally all three were required, none dispensable.

At this moment, Divine Fire Sage had brought people to ask the Supreme Elder for fortune.

Divine Origin's deep gaze swept over the ten disciples.

Some were old, some young, some male, some female. Observing their essence, qi, and spirit, they were indeed different from ordinary Adept-stage cultivators.

With this as a foundation, charging toward the Sage stage held great promise.

He took out a storage pouch and casually handed it to Divine Fire.

"Take it!"

Divine Fire's face lit up with joy as he quickly took the storage pouch.

However, when his divine sense probed inside, he couldn't help but frown.

"Senior Brother, isn't this a bit too little? Not to mention the Five Elements Divine Pill, but the most important thing for breaking through to the Sage stage, the spiritual marrow—why is there only one portion per person?"

These cultivators from Sage-stage upper sects did not rely solely on so-called Nascent Soul Pills for their breakthrough methods.

They often developed various special Sage-forming auxiliary items based on their own sect's cultivation characteristics.

Such as the famous Haoyi Three Yi Qi of Dongyang Sect, or the spiritual wine Infant Green of Dieyun Sect... Although their effects might not be as simple and direct as the Nascent Soul Pill, when combined with each sect's techniques, they were not far behind.

For the Five Elements Divine Sect, what was relatively common yet somewhat scarce was the Five Elements Spiritual Marrow.

It was an essence extracted from numerous heaven-and-earth treasures containing a single attribute. Through the sect's secret method of conversion, it had an excellent auxiliary effect on cultivators practicing the sect's techniques when breaking through to the Sage stage.

This was also why the Five Elements Divine Sect, despite having been established for a shorter time than many ancient sects of the Eastern Wilderness, had produced several Sage-stage cultivators in the same generation.

At its peak, it even boasted the magnificent lineup of Five Peaks Sages plus one Great Cultivator!

But the secret method for converting spiritual marrow had always been in the hands of Supreme Elder Divine Origin Sage; those below only provided the materials.

This was also why Divine Fire, who had temporarily taken over the position of sect master amidst the turmoil, still had to come specially to beg Divine Origin in order to cultivate disciples.

He lacked spiritual marrow!

Dissatisfied with his junior brother, Divine Origin snorted coldly.

"Five Elements Spiritual Marrow is of great use to my cultivation. Over the past hundred years, I have consumed a considerable amount. To have these ten portions, are you still not satisfied?"

A single cold snort silenced everyone present.

Some, under the pressure, nearly fell to their knees.

Divine Fire gritted his teeth and insisted, "Senior Brother, it really isn't enough!"

"You..."

Divine Origin was about to reprimand him—had this junior brother of his rebelled?

But suddenly, he felt a palpitation!

He froze, and his vast divine sense abruptly spread out.

Ten li, a hundred li, a thousand li... at its limit, it covered nearly ten thousand li in radius, shrouding a small half of the vast Five Elements Domain.

Yet, he found nothing.

His divine sense slowly withdrew, and Divine Origin was puzzled about where the palpitation had come from.

His gaze fell upon his still-insistent junior brother Divine Fire, and suddenly he sneered inwardly.

Could it be that because he had explained his own situation to him that day, this junior brother had developed some improper thoughts?

He thought that he, Divine Origin, could not break through to the Ascendant stage, that he was already heading toward his end, and so had come to demand the Five Elements Spiritual Marrow that was of great use to his cultivation, in order to nurture the sect's future?

But what did the sect's future have to do with him, Divine Origin?

At this moment, only breaking through to the Ascendant stage could turn things around.

Moreover, for the sake of the divine sect, he had already given enough in this life. Now that he was old, did he still have to give?

These people would never know satisfaction!

The corners of Divine Origin Sage's mouth slowly lifted, and his vast, ocean-like dharma power fluctuations gently rippled outward.

An immense pressure enveloped the cave dwelling.

Everyone within it instantly felt as if a great mountain was pressing down on them, as if the heavens were collapsing, and they could barely maintain themselves.

Divine Fire's expression changed, and he even sensed a hint of killing intent.

With a thud, before the others could kneel, he was the first to prostrate himself before Divine Origin.

"Senior Brother!"

"We don't want it anymore!"

"We don't want it anymore!"

Divine Origin looked down at him, and his terrifying aura suddenly withdrew.

"You want it, so how could I not give it?"

"I was just testing their heart to see if it was as good as you said. It seems now that they are indeed all dragons and phoenixes among men, far better than you."

"Here, these are forty portions of spiritual marrow. Added to the previous ten, each person can equally share five portions."

"One portion of spiritual marrow can already shorten ten years of effort; five portions should be more than enough. I also have a secret technique I've researched here. When taken in conjunction, it can allow the three treasures of essence, qi, and spirit to reach a perfect state of balance. Take it as well!"

As he spoke, two more storage pouches flew before Divine Fire.

His face pale, Divine Fire cautiously raised his head, first glanced at his senior brother, and only then dared to take the storage pouches.

"Senior Brother, I..."

"I am tired. You may all withdraw!"

Divine Fire nodded, and carrying the precious resources he had begged for, he led the ten disciples out of the cave dwelling.

Only after descending Divine Origin Mountain did he suddenly realize that his back was soaked with cold sweat.

He turned back to look at the ten disciples.

Divine Fire was silent for a long while, then smiled bitterly.

"Just now, I made a fool of myself before you."

One of the female disciples stepped forward and said, "The sect master's grace is profound. Yaohong will remember it in her heart!"

Since losing to Baleful Dragonzi on the dueling platform, Qi Emei had returned and devoted herself to bitter cultivation. Now she had reached Adept Sixth Layer and was also one of the ten Sage seeds selected this time.

She spoke bluntly, "The Supreme Elder's divine might is immeasurable. The sect master lost face for our sakes; how could it be a joke?"

The others also spoke up one after another, not finding Divine Fire's kneeling and begging for mercy to be at all shameful.

They all knew what was good for them.

Divine Fire smiled with relief, scanned the three storage pouches, evenly divided the various resources into ten portions, and handed them to everyone.

When it came to the final secret technique meant to be used in conjunction with the spiritual marrow, he hesitated.

His senior brother, Divine Origin Sage, was a genius of unparalleled talent. Not only was his cultivation realm high, but he was also skilled in writing and establishing doctrines.

Among the vast sea of techniques and spells of various ranks in the Five Elements Divine Sect, more than half were created by Senior Brother Divine Origin in his early years.

But this secret technique—he had never heard of it, never seen it.

When had it been researched?

If it had been created long ago, why had it not been brought out for the disciples to learn?

If it had been self-created over the past hundred years, it was normal not to take it out, but had Senior Brother had the leisure for such things during this century?

For a moment, he fell into deep thought.

One male disciple asked, "Sect Master, that secret technique?"

He snapped back to reality. "Wait a moment. Let me go back and study it first, then copy ten copies and give them to you all. No need to rush."

Everyone understood and did not urge him further.

But regarding the Supreme Elder's latest self-created secret technique, especially one related to forming a Sage embryo, they were all very expectant.

The hundred-and-fifty-year human-demon war had shown them their own weakness.

The lofty Adept-stage superiors of the past were nothing more than mantises trying to stop a chariot before the surging tide of beasts.

Only by achieving the Sage stage could they control their own destiny!

That secret technique was the hope that could change their fate in a short time!

...

"Master, aren't you going to take a look at the Joyous Union Domain?"

"That place?"

"Yes. That battle of ours crippled half of the Medicine King Domain, making it unsuitable for Sage-stage upper sects to operate in the short term. So Nine Spirit Primogenitor led the Nine Spirit Sect to the Joyous Union Domain. But they moved a bit slowly. The demon beasts entrenched in the Joyous Union Domain seemed to have received some news and withdrew en masse. Many rogue cultivators and small forces in the Eastern Wilderness got wind of it and rushed in ahead, gambling on the risk. By the time Nine Spirit Primogenitor brought people over, although they had still occupied the fourth-rank spiritual vein, they couldn't directly expel people from other places."

That battle back then had indeed been brutal.

The overall loss to the Medicine King Domain was not very great, after all, the battlefield had been centered on Luo Chen personally.

But the battlefield had started right on the former mountain gate of the Medicine King Sect.

That was where the main vein of the fourth-rank spiritual vein was located.

Under the chaotic battle of three great Demon Emperors, plus Luo Chen and Nine Spirit Primogenitor—five great powers comparable to Great Cultivators—even the most stable fourth-rank spiritual vein could not withstand such an impact.

Perhaps the Medicine King Domain would become a holy land for rogue cultivators in the future, but it was indeed no longer suitable for a Sage-stage upper sect to station itself.

After hearing this situation, Luo Chen pondered for a moment, then slowly shook his head.

"Forget it!"

"In that battle, Nine Spirit wouldn't have fared much better than me. I could recover quickly, but he might not be able to."

"Going to see him now would only show me his weakness and interrupt his healing. It wouldn't mean much."

"Let's meet again at our peak!"

The deeper one's Dao cultivation, the stronger one's dharma body, the slower the recovery after severe injury.

Not everyone could possess a desolate beast's body like Luo Chen's after nirvana transformation.

Moreover, after returning this time, news would soon reach the Nether Origin Sect. He wouldn't even be able to stay long in his own home, so why bother saying goodbye to a friend?

Luo Chen subconsciously reached up to touch the hidden water droplet pattern between his eyebrows, and with a surge of dharma power, he disappeared into the vast sea of clouds.

...

On the Motian Cliff.

Halfway up the mountain.

A graceful figure sat on the jade steps before a palace, her expression mournful, her eyes vacant, lost in thought.

Her gaze drifted aimlessly into the distance, where a large black tiger was climbing and leaping nimbly among the mountains.

On its back sat two people, their laughter and chatter constantly drifting out.

Anyone who heard it could sense the joy within.

After a moment, the black tiger leaped with all its might, landing on the jade square. Under the control of a thin youth, it charged toward the young woman.

When it drew near, the youth suddenly pulled on the reins, and the great tiger came to an abrupt halt.

"Mother! I can control this big tiger now!"

The youth turned over and jumped down, his movements reckless, startling the woman back to her senses.

"Lingxi, what are you doing!"

Unexpectedly, a wing extended from the side of the great tiger, supporting the youth as he slid down smoothly.

Only then did Qin Yuanjiang, who was still on the black tiger's back, say with a smile, "Aunt Sima, no need to worry. I have specially modified this puppet. Even though Lingxi has no spiritual power, he can still control it."

A mortal without spiritual power, yet able to control a graded puppet—such a thing, if spoken of, would shock many.

But in Qin Yuanjiang's mouth, it was as ordinary and trivial as could be, far less important than the youth's momentary excitement.

Sima Huiniang opened her mouth, then said softly, "Actually, the sect's construction needs you more now. There's no need to waste your effort on unnecessary things."

Qin Yuanjiang looked down at the youth, a hint of emotion in his expression.

"It's not unnecessary. It's just that I remember when I was young, my father also bought me small puppets because I liked playing with them, which moved him to send me to the Falling Cloud Sect to cultivate."

"Lingxi isn't very suited for orthodox cultivation methods, so I thought perhaps the path of puppetry could be a choice for him to enter the immortal path."

"And this child, Lingxi..."

When he reached the end, his expression carried a hint of melancholy.

If his pregnant wife had not died in that internal strife of the Falling Cloud Sect, he too would have had a child.

Alas...

"I'll be going back now. If anything comes up, Aunt, just call for me."

Seeing that the day was growing late, Qin Yuanjiang took the initiative to bid farewell.

Sima Huiniang naturally could not detain him. She saw him off a few steps and then let him go.

Turning back, she looked at her son, who was still excitedly fiddling with the black tiger, and couldn't help but sigh.

Hearing the sigh, Luo Lingxi ran over curiously and took his mother's hand.

"Mother, I heard them say that when I turn twelve, I can start cultivating. I'll be able to fly through the sky and burrow into the earth, just like them, and be capable of anything. Is that true?"

Sima Huiniang's expression suddenly changed. "Who told you that? Who are 'them'?"

Luo Lingxi's mouth puckered, but he didn't cry, though he also didn't dare to ask further.

Whenever matters of cultivation came up, his mother always reacted this way.

Seeing her son's aggrieved look, Sima Huiniang lovingly picked up his thin little body.

"Lingxi, how about we don't cultivate, okay?"

Luo Lingxi tilted his head and said stubbornly, "No!"

"You!"

Sima Huiniang was furious.

Luo Lingxi suddenly turned his head and glared at her. "Are you going to hit me again? I heard them say that my father is very powerful, the most powerful person in this world. If you hit me, when Father comes back, I will definitely tell him, and he will definitely beat you up."

As he spoke, the little person had already broken free from the woman's hand and ran toward the palace.

As he ran, he shouted loudly.

"Sister Mingyue! Mother is going to hit me again!"

Sima Huiniang clenched her fists, then slowly released them.

His father was indeed a very powerful person.

If he found out that his son was being bullied, he probably would beat her up!

But cultivation...

The woman shook her head and sat down dejectedly on the steps again.

Lingxi must not cultivate. Absolutely not.

At least, until his father returned, she absolutely did not dare to let him cultivate.

Otherwise, the consequences would be unpredictable.

"Husband, if I had known back then that you had that intention, I would never have wanted this child."

"To bring him into the world is only to make him suffer. It would be better for me to bear it alone."

Sima Huiniang was both angry and heartbroken, but she only muttered the word "husband" more and more frequently.

When will you come back?

Under the moonlight, a figure came stepping on the moon.

The woman stared blankly at the white-robed figure, as if in a dream.
