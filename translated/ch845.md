# Chapter 845: Dan Sect Restarts Shouyang Dao Discussion

Deep within the earth vein of Daqing Mountain.

Luo Chen stood in an underground cavern, a faint hint of joy on his face.

Black King hadn't deceived him—a spirit acupoint had indeed been born here!

As luck would have it, when Splendid Splendor Palace was breached, the formation that had confined the spirit vein developed a gap. Though not enough to cause spiritual energy to leak, it had indeed lost some of its binding power.

Combined with Black King's spirit-gathering talent, at this earth vein node, he had forcibly converged a fourth-order spirit acupoint.

If nurtured well from now on, supplemented with relevant spirit-gathering formations, perhaps a hundred years later, a hundred li of green river would actually evolve into a fourth-order spirit vein!

If this had been before, Luo Chen would have been ecstatic.

But now, he was only happy for a moment.

"A hundred li of green river is ultimately too small."

"Far from enough to establish a sect, it's not even sufficient to support my own cultivation. Even with an additional fourth-order spirit acupoint, it's the same."

The smile on Luo Chen's face gradually faded.

He glanced at the puppet standing behind him, now completely shrouded under a black robe.

"Golden Feather..." Luo Chen shook his head. The name was grating to call. "From now on, I'll call you Jin San!"

Having settled on the name, with a thought, Jin San leaped into the spirit acupoint and sat down cross-legged.

A large amount of spiritual energy was attracted and began converging toward his body.

Puppets must be refined, but they must also be nurtured!

That was how the puppet made from Mi Shuhua's flesh had been.

Tian Xingzi had found a second-order spirit vein and nurtured it for many years before a junior came to collect it.

Luo Chen's puppet had a richer foundation, used more precious materials, and especially since Luo Chen had fused his own flesh and blood into it, combining the Falling Cloud Sect's methods with ancient puppet arts in his own hands, the nurturing conditions required were even more stringent.

This fourth-order spirit acupoint had come at just the right time!

"Nurture it well, and deepen your understanding of the puppet arts while you're at it!"

Having given his instructions, Luo Chen walked out.

Next, he had to return to Lingtian Pass.

...

Swish!

A flying shuttle broke through the clouds, speeding along.

The white-robed Daoist sat within it, continuously forming hand seals toward a ring floating in midair.

Suddenly, he swept his hand across it, and the ring burst with golden light before vanishing without a trace.

"Finally unlocked!"

This ring was none other than Jin Ling's storage ring!

If it had been before, unlocking a storage ring from a cultivator of the same rank would have taken Luo Chen anywhere from half a year to several years.

But as his formation arts advanced to the fourth order and his soul foundation grew stronger, breaking through the remaining restrictions had become much easier.

This time, it had only taken a month or so to smoothly unlock the restrictions Jin Ling had left behind.

With a thought, his divine sense probed inside.

After a long while, Luo Chen slowly withdrew his divine sense, a few booklets now in his hand.

*Life-Seizing Three Golden Feathers*
*Five Elements Divine Pill*
*Golden Escape Art*
...

Jin Ling's legacy was more "meager" than expected.

Whether spirit stones or pills, there were very few.

This actually fit the current situation of most cultivators who had fought through over a hundred years of war—directly usable cultivation resources were becoming increasingly scarce.

What he had collected the most of were various ores.

That batch of legacy was the most valuable.

If Luo Chen needed to refine any magic treasures in the future, those ores would come in handy.

For now, what interested him were these cultivation technique manuals from the Five Elements Divine Sect.

"There's even a pill formula?"

Luo Chen picked up the Five Elements Divine Pill formula and began flipping through it.

He was well aware of this famous pill. When he was forming his core, Jin Buque had sent him one.

He had taken it!

Also following the principle of balancing the five elements, the Five Elements Divine Pill was far, far superior to the Starfield Pill.

But as his knowledge grew, though Luo Chen still praised the Five Elements Pill, he felt that calling it a "Divine Pill" was somewhat exaggerated.

Yet now, as he flipped through the pill formula, a look of surprise appeared on his face.

"It's a fragmentary formula?"

He had never imagined that the most renowned pill of the Divine Sect, the Five Elements Divine Pill, was merely derived from a fragmentary formula.

This clearly copied formula recorded the general situation. A complete Five Elements Divine Pill, when refined, should be at the fifth order.

It did not disappoint the name "Divine Pill"!

The reason the Five Elements Divine Sect couldn't refine it was simply that too much related content was missing.

Only the relevant pharmacology and a few material names remained.

If divided by completeness, it was probably only ten or twenty percent, even more incomplete than the Tihu Pill formula.

"This is missing far too much. Even I couldn't supplement it to completion."

After only a cursory glance, Luo Chen abandoned the idea of completing the formula.

Too many specific material names were missing. At best, he could build a framework based on pharmacology, but he couldn't control the specific medicinal properties.

He would probably invest centuries and still see no results.

Putting away the Five Elements Divine Pill formula, Luo Chen's gaze swept over the other booklets.

Some unimportant ones he skipped directly—they could serve as low-level inheritance for the Luo Tian Sect in the future.

Finally, his attention fell on the two highest-grade texts.

*Life-Seizing Three Golden Feathers* was clearly Jin Ling's main cultivation method, and also the origin of his Dao name.

After a rough flip-through, Luo Chen understood that this was a very complete fourth-order gold-element cultivation method.

It not only recorded the corresponding cultivation techniques but also included three fourth-order gold-element spells and the refining method for his lifebound golden feathers.

[Take the tail feather of a golden pheasant, supplement with a large amount of Geng metal... If Nine Yang Geng Metal is available, that is best!]

"Golden pheasant tail feather?" Luo Chen's eyes narrowed slightly. He knew this demon beast.

The Splendid Immortal he had encountered before was rumored to have a golden pheasant as its original form.

This type of demon beast was unremarkable in every aspect except for its beautiful appearance and splendid feathers.

For Splendid Immortal to achieve the Great Demon Emperor realm as a golden pheasant was truly no easy feat.

Yet he hadn't expected that their tail feathers, after special refinement, could possess such powerful killing might.

Geng metal?

Luo Chen recalled that he still had a piece of top-grade Nine Yang Geng Metal lying idle.

"If I use my Nine Yang Geng Metal, combined with Splendid Immortal's tail feather, to refine the Life-Seizing Golden Feather, the power would probably be even greater!"

Luo Chen chuckled and put down the technique.

But then he picked it up again and examined it for a moment.

The Five Elements Divine Sect, as the name suggested, walked the path of the five elements. At its peak, the sect not only had the Supreme Elder, Divine Origin Sage, but also five peak elders. It was the most powerful among the newly risen Sage sects of the Eastern Wilderness.

Even compared to Dongyang Sect and Tiandu Sect, it was in no way inferior.

Luo Chen had never seen the cultivation methods of Divine Fire Sage or Luxuriant Wood Sage, but from Jin Ling's technique, it was clearly an extreme single-attribute path.

Thus, not only was cultivation speed very fast, but combat power was also formidable.

Like Luo Chen himself, he had later understood this principle and focused all his energy on the fire element.

Then the question arose.

Just how strong was Divine Origin, that old thief who cultivated all five elements?

Though he had lost to Azure Frost, he was still the only survivor among the three great cultivators who had escaped Mount Green Parasol alive.

And with such strength, he dared not set foot on Daqing Mountain just because Luo Chen and Nine Spirits Lord had joined forces?

Holding the booklet, Luo Chen's eyes narrowed slightly.

"It seems the injuries he suffered at Mount Green Parasol are heavier than I imagined!"

Murmuring to himself, Luo Chen casually picked up the remaining text, *Golden Escape Art*.

...

As soon as he returned to Lingtian Pass, Luo Chen sensed the unusual atmosphere.

The Sage cultivators in the city were not as idle as usual, but were quite active, patrolling various places.

As if on guard against something?

Luo Chen immediately sought out Lin Qingxuan, who was closest, to inquire about the situation.

"What's happened?"

Lin Qingxuan shook his head. "I don't know. Half a month ago, Thunder Daozi from Nether Origin Sect arrived. After that, the Lingtian City Lord ordered us to be on alert and patrol everywhere."

At the mention of "Thunder Daozi," Luo Chen's expression changed slightly.

How had he returned?

And with such an attitude the moment he came back—had he really captured that Gao Chang?

If so, why not bring him back to Nether Origin Sect, or kill him directly? What did bringing him to Lingtian Pass mean?

Countless doubts arose.

Lin Qingxuan didn't understand why he was so alarmed, and instead asked about another matter.

"Hall Master Li is dead. What about the Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid..."

Luo Chen came back to his senses and forced a smile. "The transaction was originally led by him and me, but you were also involved. Now that he's dead, do you still want to continue the transaction?"

Lin Qingxuan's face lit up with joy. "Is the Dan Sect still willing to continue the transaction?"

Luo Chen calmed his emotions, becoming composed. "You and I have some friendship, and the pill formula came from the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect. If you want to continue, this sect naturally won't refuse. However, the medicinal ingredients originally provided by the Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion will now be your responsibility."

"That's not a problem!" Lin Qingxuan answered without hesitation.

Luo Chen nodded. "As for the distribution plan, given Hall Master Li's unfortunate fall, it must be renegotiated."

Lin Qingxuan also nodded. "Lin knows when to advance and retreat. How about we split it fifty-fifty, as you originally proposed?"

The original transaction details had started with Luo Chen proposing a fifty-fifty split, with him taking half.

Later, Luo Chen had conceded, changing it to forty-sixty, with him taking forty, and Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion and Jade Cauldron Sword Sect taking sixty. How Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion and Jade Cauldron Sword Sect divided it was their own business.

Now, Lin Qingxuan felt the power dynamic was unequal, so he directly relented.

However!

"Eighty-twenty. I take eighty, you take twenty!" Luo Chen said calmly.

Lin Qingxuan's pupils contracted, almost in disbelief.

"If you agree, we continue cooperating. If not, this sect doesn't mind spending a bit more effort sending people to collect those low-level materials."

Under the calm words was an undisguised forcefulness.

Lin Qingxuan subconsciously clenched his fist, his nails almost digging into his flesh.

But soon, he slackened his grip in defeat, even forcing a smile onto his face.

"Eighty-twenty it is!"

Luo Chen chuckled lightly. "You know when to let go. Don't worry, you won't lose out. This sect's alchemy skills are growing daily, and the Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid I refine will only get better."

Lin Qingxuan smiled stiffly.

"This sect plans to find time to invite fellow Daoists to gather at Shouyang Mountain. Fellow Daoist Lin can come and show your support."

Leaving these words behind, Luo Chen drifted away.

Only Lin Qingxuan remained where he was, his forced smile fading, his face full of dejection.

The gap between him and Luo Chen was growing wider and wider.

Not just in strength, but also in status.

Especially with Hall Master Li gone, he didn't even have the qualifications to bargain with Luo Chen.

But he couldn't give up the Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid!

Having experienced the effects of that spiritual liquid, he knew it was the only thing that could increase his strength the fastest in a short time.

Currently, only Luo Chen could refine it.

And he had only been getting twenty percent before anyway. Keeping it the same now wasn't bad.

It was just... there was still a little unwillingness!

...

Luo Chen didn't care what the other party was thinking.

For him, this was actually a soft-hearted move.

Without Hall Master Li, he didn't have to continue the cooperative transaction.

But considering the persona he had built before, turning his back so ruthlessly would invite gossip. Plus, with the Luo Tian Sect's current shortage of personnel, collecting those materials would be stretched thin.

Thus, he chose to continue the transaction with Lin Qingxuan.

What he cared about most now was the situation with Nether Origin Sect's Thunder Daozi.

As soon as he returned to Danluo Mountain, Luo Chen personally began writing invitations.

[Recalling the past, the Shouyang Dao Discussion, cups clinking, all guests joyful. But after Lingtian was breached, we were separated in all directions, fate keeping us apart. I still remember the Shouyang Mountain Lord's words before leaving, wanting to gather again at Shouyang after the battle...]

He flourished his brush, ink drying as words appeared.

Signing his name, he handed it to a disciple.

"Send this to the Great Palace Lord of Splendid Splendor Palace!"

Then, he wrote again.

"This one to Nine Spirits Lord."

"This one to..."

In a short time, over a dozen invitations were sent out from Luo Chen's hand.

Among them were those with whom he had good relations, as well as some casual acquaintances.

By the end, Luo Chen even sent invitations to Sage cultivators he had only heard of but never met.

In the dead of night, Luo Chen sat alone, a contemplative look on his face.

"After a great battle, one should lie low."

"But if my earlier speculation is true, the situation is about to reach its peak, and even Ascendant great powers might make a move."

"Under such circumstances, Nether Origin Sect is still being evasive—their intentions are despicable!"

"Only by uniting can we force them to show their hand."

"The Shouyang Dao Discussion is their territory. Now that I'm restarting it in my name, they can't possibly remain indifferent, can they?"

Having thought through all this, Luo Chen's heart was still uneasy.

Using his own name to initiate the Shouyang Dao Discussion wasn't just to force Nether Origin Sect to reveal the truth, but also to see what their attitude toward him was.

Over the years, the residences of other Sage cultivators had constantly changed.

Only he, favored by the Lingtian City Lord, lived alone on the spiritually abundant Danluo Mountain.

This alone was a clue!

As long as he clarified Nether Origin Sect's attitude toward him, Luo Chen could act with more direction in the future.

"I hope they don't disappoint me!"

Murmuring, Luo Chen once again picked up the *Golden Escape Art*.

The five-element escape arts were definitely the most exquisite among the five-element spells, covering everything from the second to the fourth order.

After so many years, Luo Chen had never mastered any type of escape art.

Though partly because his own speed was extremely fast and he didn't need such arts, it was also due to a lack of opportunity.

Now, with this *Golden Escape Art*, using another's stone to polish his jade, he vaguely felt some inspiration.

...

At the same time, the invitation Luo Chen had sent to the newly arrived Sage from Dongyang Sect was placed on the city lord's desk.

"Fellow Daoist Jiao, you may take your leave first!"

Dongyang Sect's Elder Jiao cupped his hands and respectfully withdrew.

The Lingtian City Lord stared at the invitation in his hand, lost in thought.

Footsteps sounded outside the hall. Without looking up, he said, "Junior Brother Zhang, you've arrived!"

A figure moved, and a handsome, upright silhouette appeared before the Lingtian City Lord, casually picking up the invitation.

"Heh. We've just issued the order to patrol Lingtian and put the entire city on alert, and that Luo Chen privately invites fellow Daoists to gather. He truly acts with impunity!"

The Lingtian City Lord looked up at his junior brother, who was growing stronger by the day, even arousing his own wariness.

"Dan Sect Luo Chen is a talent personally requested by the sect. He naturally has the right to act with impunity."

Zhang Che raised an eyebrow and directly pocketed the invitation.

"Perfect. Then I, Thunder Daozi, will attend the gathering!"

(Chapter End)
