# Chapter 1143: Terrifying Foundations

Yixian City, early March.

The snow had melted, the grass was growing, and orioles were flying.

Crowds of people converged from streets in all directions toward the city center, like a hundred rivers flowing into the sea.

Such a grand occasion—let alone in Yixian City, even in the other seventy-two cities—was rarely seen even once in a hundred years.

Because today was the time of "Martial Combat Determines the Master"!

The city lords of the seventy-two cities were almost always martial artists of formidable strength.

Such beings had profound cultivation realms and lifespans far exceeding ordinary mortals, often spanning centuries at a minimum. Thus, for mortals, a single lifetime might not be enough to witness a change of city lord and the establishment of a new master.

But this time, it was happening in Yixian City!

In truth, ever since the previous city lord had left ten years ago, everyone had sensed this day would come. They just hadn't expected the old city lord to truly leave no decree, allowing matters to develop to the harsh point where the city lord's position would be decided through combat.

Among the flowing crowds, those dressed in luxurious attire led the way, with no one daring to contend with them for precedence.

These people were the top figures within the city. Some owned vast industries within the city, while others had expanded their businesses to other cities—powerful individuals who dominated far and wide, each typically having one or two Furnace Realm experts under their command.

Behind them came the local wealthy of Yixian City. Their strength was inferior to the former, and they couldn't afford to hire Furnace Realm experts to personally sit in their establishments. They could only spend a great price to invite a Furnace Realm expert as a nominal offering elder, providing some protection.

The owners of Benevolence Hall and Thousand Gold Pavilion were among them. They let their own stewards and guards protect them while they walked and chatted.

"Shopkeeper Xue, you sent a message earlier saying that this time, for the Martial Combat Determines the Master, the offering elder we hired would personally take action. Is that true?" Shopkeeper Shen of Thousand Gold Pavilion asked loudly, a thick anticipation shining in his eyes.

Shopkeeper Xue of Benevolence Hall pricked up his ears and caught Shopkeeper Shen's voice amidst the noisy crowd. He laughed heartily. "No mistake. This news came from the Luo residence. I privately asked Madam Jing, and although she didn't explicitly confirm it, she didn't deny it either."

Shopkeeper Shen's eyes lit up. "If that's really the case, we might borrow his reputation to intimidate the petty villains!"

Shopkeeper Xue beside him nodded repeatedly, his gaze sweeping across the surroundings from time to time, his heart filled with excitement.

In the past, only those large shops with many branches could invite experts at the level of city lord's mansion guest elders as their own offering elders. For people like them, who had some strength in Yixian City but whose influence could never spread outward, the reason was precisely the lack of high-level martial artists to sit in.

This time, they were lucky enough to have connected with Luo Chen's line.

Although many people looked down on Luo Chen upon hearing that his realm was only Blood River Realm, even mocking the three establishments for spending a great price to hire a mere second-stage body-tempering martial artist, they had no choice. They were forced to gamble on this hand.

In their view, martial realm meant nothing, but the title of city lord's mansion guest elder was solid and real.

No matter how high one's realm, strength ultimately determined status. The title of city lord's mansion guest elder was absolute recognition of Luo Chen's strength!

"Of course, all of this still depends on that one taking action today—and winning!"

"Indeed, indeed!"

"Shopkeeper Yang of Half Moon Tower has also come. Let's join him!"

Half Moon Tower, like Benevolence Hall and Thousand Gold Pavilion, was one of the forces that had spent a great price to hire Luo Chen as a nominal elder. When the three parties met, they gathered together without prior arrangement, preparing to seriously observe today's martial grand gathering.

...

The Martial Arena!

This world was dominated by body-tempering martial artists, and martial combat contests occurred frequently.

Thus, venues like the Martial Arena were common, found in all cities large and small.

Especially the Martial Arenas of the seventy-two main cities—all were built at great expense by Carefree City.

Legend had it that once a Martial Arena of this level was fully activated, it could even withstand the intensity of combat between Leakless Realm experts.

Of course, under normal circumstances, it never reached that level. Generally, only first- and second-stage body-tempering martial artists used the Martial Arena. Even Furnace Realm experts rarely stepped onto the platform.

This was easy to understand. Only low-level martial artists still pursued fame, naturally requiring many public battles to draw the world's attention.

But Furnace Realm experts had no such need.

At that level, numerous forces would actively recruit them, providing cultivation resources.

They only needed to train hard on ordinary days, study martial techniques and killing moves, and display their might when trouble arose.

But today was different. Yixian City's Martial Arena, with the permission of the Carefree Envoy, had been fully activated, providing a perfect combat environment for all the experts participating in the Martial Combat Determines the Master.

Gazing at the Martial Arena stained with dark blood, Luo Chen slowly withdrew his gaze.

The floor was paved with lapis lazuli, capable of withstanding pure substantial attacks, supplemented by obsidian platforms that absorbed the impact of blood qi power. On the Martial Arena, there were also array patterns traced with the blood of some special beast.

Now, under deliberate activation, a transparent yet tough invisible qi barrier had formed, giving a feeling of being unshakable!

"Carefree City, which built this Martial Arena, clearly possesses extremely advanced refining inheritance. Only by perfectly combining the dao of formations and casting materials from the path of immortal cultivation with the blood qi transformations of body-tempering could they forge a Martial Arena capable of withstanding such powerful attacks."

"Although the Martial Arena here in Yixian City is merely an imitation, the casting skill revealed in just this glimpse is already nearly approaching the Dao!"

"Carefree City truly makes me increasingly curious!"

Luo Chen's eyes narrowed slightly as he murmured inwardly.

He was proficient in a hundred arts, already at a master level in casting, formations, and even material identification. Naturally, he could perceive the foundations of Carefree City through these details that ordinary people would overlook.

Only a formation dao grandmaster-level existence could easily construct a Martial Arena capable of withstanding Leakless Realm experts!

If one also considered the many Leakless Realm experts who gathered at Carefree City every hundred years, then Carefree City's foundations were simply unfathomable!

It wasn't that Luo Chen was letting his imagination run wild. The fourth stage of body-tempering, the Leakless Realm, was comparable to the Nascent Soul stage of the qi refinement path.

Before the Extinction Mist spread again, how many Leakless Realm experts had Carefree City accumulated over an era?

I'm afraid the number of Nascent Soul cultivators in any Ascendant sacred land within the Mountains and Seas Realm would be hard-pressed to compare!

While Luo Chen was observing the Martial Arena, many others were secretly sizing him up.

People like Granny Yu, Redfire, even Xiao Sanniang and the Mysterious Armor soldiers.

No one had expected that after Xiao Zirui disappeared, Xiao Feng would arrange for Luo Chen to be among the five candidates for the Martial Combat Determines the Master. Even though Xiao Feng had already informed the others at a private gathering beforehand, it still felt surreal now that the day had actually arrived.

After all, Luo Chen's externally displayed realm was only Blood River Realm. Although his blood qi was powerful, he had ultimately not condensed indestructible astral qi!

According to the intelligence they had gathered, Second Young Master Xiao Yun's side this time had dispatched genuine Furnace Realm experts.

Could Luo Chen withstand them?

"Hahaha..."

A hearty laugh came from behind. Everyone turned to look, seeing Xiao Feng, personally guarded by Tie Xiong, stepping into the venue.

His face was full of high spirits, his eyes like torches sweeping over the crowd. Then he closed his folding fan and clasped his hands in a bow.

"This battle, I entrust to you all!"
