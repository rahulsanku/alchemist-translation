# Chapter 721: Molan Court, the War-Ceasing Assembly! Succeed by the Purple Mansion, Fail by the Purple Mansion?

Sage cultivators were rare to begin with.

Anyone who achieved such a realm would be someone who overawed a region and governed an entire domain.

Rogue Sage cultivators were even rarer than phoenix feathers and qilin horns.

Even though the Northern Sea was famed for its rich resources, with powerful rogue cultivators emerging one after another, the rogue cultivators who could reach the Sage realm numbered only the Three Great Wanderers.

And in truth, the Three Great Wanderers all had backgrounds that could be traced.

Moon Wanderer had come from the Penglai Immortal Sect in her early years, then later betrayed the sect and became a regional hegemon in her own right.

Blood Wanderer went without saying. His deeds were known to everyone in the Northern Sea cultivation world.

Mad Wanderer seemed somewhat mysterious, but the name Layman Fengling was still something the older generation of Sage cultivators with some years behind them knew a little about.

By comparison, this Wilderness Wanderer sitting opposite Sage Langya at this moment had rather thought-provoking origins.

His extremely youthful appearance showed that his years of cultivation could not be long; by Sage Langya's judgment, he was at most two or three hundred years old.

His divine sense was powerful. Though he had just entered the Sage realm, it was not inferior to Sage Langya's, and was even slightly stronger.

One had to know that Mei Yiqi had formed his Nascent Soul quite some years ago.

Yet this man also paid such attention to Blood Nightmare Moluo, an elder of the Primordial Demon Sect.

Two or three hundred years old, powerful soul, inexplicable rise, attention on the demonic sect, and now inquiring about soul essence, a material that had once been supplied specifically to Primordial Demon Sect experts...

Sage Langya narrowed his eyes slightly. "Could Wanderer be a survivor of the Primordial Demon Sect's soul-refining lineage?"

Only the demonic sect's soul-refining lineage knew how to use the incomparably special material known as soul essence!

Luo Chen's expression was calm as he asked in return, "What if I am? What if I am not?"

Sage Langya was startled, then shook his head.

"If you are, it is no great harm. With the demon calamity ravaging the world today, human cultivators wish to stand united, so remnants of the demonic sect can naturally be tolerated. If you are not, then of course that is even better."

"Moreover, this poor Daoist has no particular connection to the Primordial Demon Sect. Whatever Wilderness Wanderer's origins may be, they have nothing to do with me. Let us simply count today as a chance meeting on the water, a friendship over one cup of tea."

The meaning in these words was that if Luo Chen truly was a remnant of the demonic sect, he, Mei Yiqi of Langya, would not stir up trouble. At most, they would be chance acquaintances.

Luo Chen listened quietly.

Suddenly, his brows stirred and he burst into loud laughter.

"Fellow Daoist, there are countless heroes beneath heaven. It is not only the sacred lands that can produce geniuses. Though Luo may not be a genius, I am no skulking coward either. Look carefully, then. Am I a member of the demonic sect?"

As he spoke, Luo Chen no longer concealed the fluctuations of his magic power at all.

A surge of upright, majestic, and incomparably blazing magic power roared out!

Its momentum was surging and high-spirited. As his aura breathed in and out, it was like volcanic lava erupting.

In Sage Langya's eyes, the man opposite him was clearly sitting calmly, yet seemed like a leaping flame, stirring unease in the heart.

Especially the purity of that magic power. It did not look like someone newly entered into the Sage realm at all, but rather like someone whose realm had long since stabilized.

The blazing restlessness of his magic power that Sage Langya had assumed before was not caused by an unstable realm at all, but was an innate change brought about by the attributes of the other man's cultivation art.

Wonder appeared in Sage Langya's eyes. He took a deep breath and said, "Such upright and majestic magic-power aura is domineering as well, yet it is entirely different from the dark, oppressive dominance of the demonic sect. It is more like an inborn tyranny that overawes all things. From this, it seems Fellow Daoist is indeed not a remnant of the demonic sect."

Luo Chen smiled faintly, and his magic power withdrew as soon as it had appeared.

As his robes settled, he said leisurely, "Then can Fellow Daoist now tell me about Moluo Stream's soul essence?"

"This poor Daoist will certainly treat you with sincerity!"

Sage Langya smiled faintly and filled another cup of tea.

...

The conversation between Sage Langya and Wilderness Wanderer lasted an entire afternoon.

From probing each other's foundations at the beginning and discussing major affairs of the cultivation world, they later, at Luo Chen's initiative, exchanged cultivation insights regarding the Sage stage.

Although experience in this area was precious beyond measure, and both naturally held some things back, simple insights could still be shared.

On this point, Luo Chen gained a great advantage.

After all, he had not formed his Nascent Soul long ago, so what cultivation experience could he really speak of?

However, he was not purely taking advantage, and instead proactively and sincerely shared some of his experience and insights regarding condensing the Nascent Soul.

Sage Langya was not alone in the world. Under him were disciples and family, among whom there was no lack of Adepts.

Though he had formed his Nascent Soul, his own experience was suitable only for a small number of people.

With Luo Chen's supplement, it would be much more convenient for him to teach disciples in the future. Perhaps Langya Mountain might even produce one more Sage because of Luo Chen's experience and insights.

After Luo Chen left, an Adept cultivator of Langya Mountain entered Langxuan Hall.

"Master, is everything all right? I saw the hall formation fluctuating just now and almost thought you had started fighting."

Sage Langya waved a hand. "It is fine. We were merely exchanging cultivation insights and occasionally testing methods."

The disciple came to a sudden understanding, then asked curiously, "I see their carriage is trailing behind us. Are they going to Molan Court with us?"

"Mm. For high-level cultivators like us, this War-Ceasing Assembly is a rare grand gathering seen only once in two hundred years. Wilderness Wanderer is a rogue cultivator, and what he lacks most are exchanges with Fellow Daoists and resource trades. Naturally, he does not wish to miss it."

At this point, hesitation entered Sage Langya's tone.

Stroking his beard, he still felt uncertain.

Even if this man was not a member of the demonic sect, how could such a grand bearing and such majestic, domineering magic power be cultivated from the crude shallow arts of a rogue cultivator?

Especially when Wilderness Wanderer was still so young!

...

On the Sixfold Imperial Flying Cloud Carriage.

Luo Chen digested the information he had just obtained.

Regarding cultivation insights for the Sage stage, after comparing them with what Han Zhan had said in earlier years, he confirmed that Sage Langya had not fooled him.

However, one point had been mentioned that somewhat surprised Luo Chen.

That was the relationship between the Purple Mansion, the Nascent Soul, and realm breakthroughs.

The Nascent Soul was like a newborn infant. Though it contained the cultivator's entire vast power and was incomparably mighty, it was also extremely "fragile"!

This fragility was in comparison to heaven and earth.

The Nascent Soul longed to mingle with heaven and earth, yet also dreaded merging with heaven and earth too deeply.

If a cultivator became too immersed in cultivation, and the Nascent Soul interacted too deeply with heaven and earth, it would very easily be assimilated by heaven and earth and completely lose itself.

Therefore, there was the Purple Mansion, evolved from the qi sea, to protect the Nascent Soul and prevent it from being exposed nakedly outside and assimilated by heaven and earth.

Because of this, the thicker the barrier, the more at ease a cultivator could be while cultivating, and the faster their cultivation speed would be.

For a vivid example, it was like flowers in a greenhouse.

With no wind or rain beating them, and with suitable soil as well, of course they would grow bright and beautiful.

But Luo Chen understood very clearly what flowers in a greenhouse meant: they could not withstand hardship.

Translated into cultivation, the meaning was that the thicker the Purple Mansion barrier, the harder it would be to break through to the Ascendant realm in the future.

Because at that time, if a cultivator wished to advance to Ascendant, they had to comprehend the true meaning of the laws between heaven and earth, merge it into the Nascent Soul, and transform it into the primordial spirit. If the Purple Mansion barrier was too thick, it would inevitably obstruct the cultivator from comprehending the ultimate principles of heaven and earth.

This cultivation experience was something Luo Chen was learning for the first time.

Han Zhan had never mentioned it before.

Sage Langya was also quite troubled, because his Purple Mansion barrier was too thin. When he cultivated deeply, he was very afraid of becoming lost in heaven and earth. Each time he cultivated, he had no choice but to be cautious as though treading on thin ice, which had led to him forming his Nascent Soul more than two hundred years ago, yet now possessing only the second level of the Sage realm.

He had spoken of this matter because he wanted to seek Luo Chen's solution or suggestions.

But what could Luo Chen have? He could only offer a few words of encouragement and blessing.

Returning to himself, Luo Chen reflected inwardly.

"Is my Purple Mansion barrier thick or thin?"

This question soon obtained an answer.

Because when Han Zhan had once tried to seize his body, upon first entering his Purple Mansion, he had been extremely shocked.

It seemed even Han Zhan, who had watched Luo Chen cultivate step by step as someone at his side, had never expected Luo Chen's Purple Mansion to be so majestic, as if it truly were a small world inside his body!

"At that time, we had already completely torn away all pretense. His astonishment could not have been false. Therefore, my Purple Mansion must be rare in this age, and even Han Zhan, that genius unseen in Falling Cloud Sect for several hundred years, was far inferior."

"In that case, if I want to break through to the Ascendant realm in the future, I fear..."

"Forget it. That is far too distant, far too distant. At the very least, having enough Purple Mansion foundation is an enormous good thing for me right now. It means that when I cultivate, I do not need to worry too much about being corroded by heaven and earth and losing myself."

Luo Chen chuckled softly and temporarily pressed the matter down.

Afterward, he thought back to what Sage Langya had said regarding soul essence.

Moluo Stream was still producing soul essence!

Even though the demonic sect had been destroyed and demand for soul essence no longer existed, over these two hundred years, a small number of Stream Lords within Moluo Stream had still continued producing it.

Everyone knew the strength of the three Primordial Demon lineages.

In the past, the one that made people turn pale at the mere mention of it was the Blood Sea lineage, because this lineage placed greatest emphasis on slaughter, and its conduct was arrogant and without restraint. Blood Wanderer had merely obtained a fragmentary cultivation art from the Blood Sea lineage, yet he had achieved the resounding fame of one of the Northern Sea's Three Great Wanderers. From this, one could see a hint of it.

But after the demon war, the reputation of the soul-refining lineage completely surpassed that of the Blood Sea lineage.

Even when the demonic sect was destroyed, the Ascendant ancestor of the soul-refining lineage could still lay down the Billion-Soul Demon-Locking Grand Array, forcibly locking the demon coalition inside Yemo Heaven.

If Ghost Immortal Tower had not coordinated from inside and outside, perhaps the Northern Sea could still have remained safe beneath that formation seal for several hundred years.

And apart from its cultivation-art inheritance, the strength of the soul-refining lineage had surely owed a great deal to the resource known as soul essence.

That was why those Stream Lords of Moluo Stream had persisted in producing soul essence.

And how had this matter been discovered?

The answer was simple. During the righteous-demonic war, someone discovered that Moluo Stream had dispatched specialists to gather the souls of battlefield cultivators.

Nor had anyone seen them use those souls to make Soul-Refining Banners, so what their purpose was became self-evident.

"The Primordial Demon Sect only passed down the method for producing soul essence, but did not teach them how to use it."

"In two hundred years, the Stream Lords of Moluo Stream do not seem to have figured out the specific method of use either."

"At this War-Ceasing Assembly, perhaps someone will bring some out to trade. I will need to make a trip there."

"In addition, if I want to return to the Eastern Wilderness, this assembly is also an opportunity. Perhaps I will have the chance to meet an intermediary."

As Luo Chen considered everything carefully, he occasionally raised his head and saw only a hazy starry night outside, foretelling that heavy rain might descend before long.

...

The outbreak of war was always sudden.

The ending of war, however, was often incomparably complicated.

Contact between two sides required continuous negotiation and constant bargaining, from cautious probing to confirming a shared understanding.

During this, there also had to be an intermediary of sufficient weight to mediate between them, preventing the talks from collapsing.

Even with external enemies watching from all sides, peace could not be achieved overnight when internal interests and demands differed.

Inside Molan Court, the righteous-demonic War-Ceasing Assembly had already continued for several days.

More and more cultivators had come here, yet a final result had still not been reached.

Everyone only knew that the one standing out for Moluo Stream was that late-stage Sage First Stream Lord.

This person was not only first in realm and first in strength within Moluo Stream, even his surname was "First" [TERM?]!

As for the Cang Hai Alliance's side, that was somewhat intriguing.

It was quite normal that the Ascendant great power Li Canghai did not appear. What realm and status did he possess?

But even the two late-stage Sage great cultivators had not come out, and that was rather disrespectful.

The negotiation representative they sent was named Mu Lan Gaoxiong!

This name sounded somewhat familiar. It was precisely that mid-stage Sage powerhouse who had been ambushed and surrounded by Blood Nightmare Moluo during the Coldlight Island incident years ago.

He had fortunately escaped that encirclement. Though he had not died, his vitality had been badly damaged.

It was said that even after he later healed his injuries, his realm could no longer advance for the rest of his life.

This made his temper increasingly violent. In the following hundred-year righteous-demonic war, his methods were incomparably ruthless. Of Moluo Stream's Twelve Stream Lords, two Stream Lords had fallen or been wounded at his hands.

"This poor Daoist cannot understand why Great Power Li sent him to negotiate. Does he not want to bring about peace quickly?"

In the torrential rain of Molan Court, atop a tall building, Sage Langya looked at the streaks of escaping light coming and going through the rain curtain, his heart full of confusion.

Luo Chen, dressed in white, rested a hand on the railing and chuckled. "If the matter can be negotiated even with Mu Lan Gaoxiong sent, does that not show the benefits Moluo Stream is yielding are enormously, enormously great, so great that even the aggrieved party, Mu Lan Gaoxiong, is willing to agree to stop the war?"

Sage Langya froze, then laughed despite himself.

That did seem to be the logic.

If Great Power Li Canghai had the mind, he could personally destroy these clowns of Moluo Stream at any time.

There might be reasons why he did not do so, but that did not mean Moluo Stream could act wantonly without cause. Their posture had to be low enough.

Mu Lan Gaoxiong's arrival was meant to press down a head that had already lowered far enough even lower!

"What Luo is curious about is this: the one that gave Moluo Stream the courage to start the war was the Penglai Immortal Sect, yet at this negotiation, I have not seen the shadow of any Penglai cultivator!"

Unexpectedly, Sage Langya chuckled.

"Fellow Daoist, that is where you do not know. Who says there is no shadow of them? The intermediary responsible for mediation is one of the four great elders of the Northern Sea Tianyuan Merchant Alliance. Does his stance not represent the Penglai Immortal Sect?"

Luo Chen was stunned, then immediately revealed a knowing smile.

Sure enough, his guess from back then had been right.

Behind the Penglai Immortal Sect truly was Tianyuan Merchant... no, the Tianyuan Dao Sect!

Only, to be both contestant and referee, their strength was astonishingly excessive.

In the rain curtain below, several streaks of escaping light stopped at this moment.

Sounds of argument could be heard indistinctly.

Those were Adept cultivators from Moluo Stream and the Cang Hai Alliance clashing below.

After a hundred years of friction followed by a hundred years of war, the hatred and grudges between them had long since become impossible to count.

At this moment, enemies meeting made their eyes burn especially red.

Even with orders from above, no one could control another person's mouth when it came to harsh words.

"If you want war, then war it is. My Blackwing Island [TERM?] has never feared you!"

"Haha, never feared us? These thirty years, we beat you so badly you could not lift your heads or strike back, and even Moluo Stream founder Blood Nightmare Moluo's lair was yielded up for peace talks. Yet your mouth is still as stubborn as a boiled duck's."

"You!"

"What about me? I remember perfectly clearly how you led your people to raid Heavenstar Sea back then. Twenty years ago, when you counterattacked Feiyun Ravine, you brutally slaughtered three thousand cultivators from top to bottom. Even my younger sister who had married into Feiyun Ravine died at Old Ghost Kang's [TERM?] hands. Where is he? Why does he not dare come to Molan Court today? I, Bi Dan, will settle this account with him, and with your Blackwing Island [TERM?]!"

...

The harsh words between Adept cultivators were not empty bluster hiding weakness, but cold and cutting killing intent.

On the tall building, the two Sage cultivators listened quietly, and neither spoke.

Only after a long while did Sage Langya sigh softly.

"My ninth disciple was not an unmatched genius, but he did have some ability. Unfortunately, he died at the hands of a Penglai Immortal Sect Adept named Yu Gao. Now Penglai has fled, and this poor Daoist no longer even has a chance to seek an explanation."

Luo Chen's expression turned strange.

Sage Langya smiled bitterly. "It seems I have made Wanderer laugh. The Penglai Immortal Sect is deep in strength, with more than one late-stage Sage great cultivator. This old man is indeed somewhat overestimating himself."

Luo Chen hurriedly put away his strange expression.

"Sage need not belittle yourself. What sect in this world can stand forever without falling? Even something as powerful as the Primordial Demon Sect became yesterday's wilted flower. The Penglai Immortal Sect may not shine for much longer either. As the saying goes, a gentleman's revenge is never too late after ten years. We Sage cultivators can wait a few hundred years for the time being. There will always be an opportunity."

After receiving comfort, Sage Langya seemed to feel somewhat better.

He no longer wished to watch the tense friction below that could not actually turn into a fight, and turned back toward his room.

Before leaving, he asked once.

"There is a small gathering between Sage cultivators this afternoon. Do you wish to come? Though Langya lacks talent, I can still introduce you on your behalf."

"In that case, I would be discourteous to refuse."

Luo Chen nodded and smiled.

After he left, Luo Chen's gaze burned like a torch as he fixed his eyes on the Adept cultivator in the rain curtain who was shouting for blood debts to be paid in blood.

A strand of divine sense transmitted outward.

Sang Jinghe, who was resting in his room, froze for a moment, then quietly stepped into the rain curtain.

*(End of Chapter)*
