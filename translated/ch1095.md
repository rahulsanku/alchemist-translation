# Chapter 1095: Heavenly Wolf Howls at the Sun, Fire Phoenix Burns the Moon

*Boom!*

Terrifying spiritual qi erupted like a tidal wave, sweeping across ten thousand miles in all directions.

A hundred beasts fled in panic; birds circled overhead.

And at the very center of that spiritual qi explosion, the towering, majestic Green Parasol Mountain seemed to have come alive.

Layer upon layer of millions of great mountains, beneath Green Parasol, all shrank by three parts.

A suppressed yet grand aura brewed within Green Parasol Mountain, as if ready at any moment to take that final step.

Everyone held their breath, afraid to disturb this scene.

But there were exceptions.

Ten thousand miles away, on an unremarkable small mountain, two figures stood side by side.

On one face, an uncontrollable expression of shock appeared.

"He actually chose to undergo his Ascension at Green Parasol Mountain!"

The sinister man beside him, clad in a blood-colored dharma robe, also bore a look of surprise, but was more curious. "I heard that the Heavenly Wolf Altar has been preparing to deal with Luo Chen. Fellow Daoist Whitebone, as the gatekeeper of Green Parasol Mountain, you would be of greatest help to them. Surely they must have invited you? I wonder why you didn't join in?"

Whitebone Nether Serpent snorted coldly. "Didn't you say it yourself? I am the gatekeeper of Green Parasol Mountain!"

As a gatekeeper, one must be the most loyal. Even if the master changed, that great gate remained. How could one so easily switch allegiances?

Blood Vulture Daoist chuckled. "If that's the case, then why didn't you go to help the Truth Crossing Hall Master?"

Whitebone Nether Serpent frowned slightly, a knot still seemingly in his heart.

He didn't respond directly, but said, "The mountain-protecting grand formation of Green Parasol Mountain was personally laid down by Rosycloud in recent years to heal her injuries. Even though she has already ascended, the remnants of that formation are no trifling matter to break. There's no need for me there."

Then, his tone shifted abruptly.

"Blood Vulture, why didn't you join in and claim a share of the spoils?"

At this, Blood Vulture Daoist put away his playful smile, his expression turning grave.

"Spoils?"

"That's Truth Crossing Hall Master Luo Chen!"

A single, simple sentence, yet it contained immense fear and reverence.

The terrifying scene of Luo Chen single-handedly suppressing the group of demons within the Truth Crossing Hall still lingered in his mind from time to time.

He didn't know how the others dared to betray Green Parasol Mountain, but he, Blood Vulture, absolutely did not dare!

The Blood Vulture clan was born to feed on carrion.

They had a natural instinct for judging a person's life or death, strength or weakness.

He didn't believe that Luo Chen, the Truth Crossing Hall Master who had survived even the Heaven-Earth War, would fall on what should be his greatest day.

Of course!

If he did fail, he wouldn't feel any regret either.

Just like a blood vulture feeding on carrion, even if all the good stuff was snatched up by the wolf clan and other powerful demons, he could always find some scraps in the corners.

"Let's just watch. That old monster Aoxiao hasn't made a move in many years. Even during the most intense period of the Eastern Wilderness human-demon war, he held back, only to preserve his strength. I want to know, just how far has he come now, to dare face the Truth Crossing Hall Master directly?"

Blood Vulture Daoist looked towards Green Parasol Mountain with a hint of curiosity.

He didn't know where Aoxiao and the others were hiding, but as long as they made a move, it would definitely be on Green Parasol Mountain.

Green Parasol Mountain was the greatest stage right now. A good show was about to begin. It just depended on who would pull back the curtain.

...

In a hidden location.

Over a dozen figures stood in silence.

When the spiritual qi tide erupted from Green Parasol Mountain, everyone's expression changed.

"Has he started?"

"Are we still going to wait?"

"Lord Aoxiao, when do we strike?"

Amidst the clamor, a cold shout rang out.

"Quiet!"

Aoxiao's gaze swept over the crowd, finally settling on Green Parasol Mountain in the distance.

"Wait! It's not the best time yet. We must wait!"

His Heavenly Wolf clan was no small tribe among the Eastern Wilderness races. Not only were they powerful, but they also had a long history, with an ancient demon wolf who had ascended among their ancestors.

Therefore, they had corresponding records of the process of a living being's Ascension.

At this moment, Luo Chen hadn't even manifested his Nascent Soul. This couldn't be considered Ascension at all.

Only when Luo Chen's Nascent Soul left his body, enveloping his soul, immersing itself in the ocean of origin qi, and forming a primordial spirit through external pressure, would be the best time to strike.

Of course!

He, Aoxiao, was not ignorant. From Luo Chen's participation in the Ascension War, it was highly likely that he had already condensed his primordial spirit in advance.

Therefore, that step of condensing the primordial spirit would be shortened to almost nothing.

Then his opportunity would be when Luo Chen was immersed in the ocean of origin qi, absorbing it and comprehending the true meaning of the laws.

During that process, the cultivator would be completely defenseless!

The best opportunity would be at that moment!

Waiting was agonizing.

The aura within Green Parasol Mountain grew stronger and stronger, putting increasing pressure on everyone.

Especially for Han Shan Que, White-Browed Immortal Ape, and even Bi Zheng, who had all faced Luo Chen's terror directly in the Truth Crossing Hall. At this moment, the rising aura irresistibly brought back memories of their humiliating past.

Along with anger, fear arose.

Some people were even subconsciously having second thoughts.

"Maybe..."

"It's started!"

A sharp howl shattered the second thoughts of some.

Aoxiao's expression turned cold and fierce. He waved his hand.

"Attack!"

With the order, everyone swarmed out.

The artifacts concealing their forms were withdrawn. Over a dozen figures shot out like arrows from a string, rushing towards Green Parasol Mountain.

Along the way, everyone instinctively looked up at the mountain peak.

A colossal, illusory giant, over a thousand *zhang* tall, was slowly rising from the Truth Crossing Hall. A terrifying pressure was uncontrollably released, inspiring awe.

Its entire body was crimson red, flames blazing high.

Suspended in the void, it was like a blazing sun!

Aoxiao's eyes widened.

Even though he had anticipated that Luo Chen might have condensed his primordial spirit in advance, he never imagined it would be this massive.

"Did those cultivators who have already ascended have this level of soul foundation in their early stages?"

This thought uncontrollably arose.

But Aoxiao's arrow had left the string; there was no turning back.

A tough light screen blocked their path into Green Parasol Mountain.

It was the mountain-protecting grand formation of Green Parasol Mountain!

Without hesitation, everyone struck out, unleashing their full power.

Demon qi great hands, sharp claws, a heaven-shaking staff...

Each brilliant attack was unleashed simultaneously. Such a level of power was enough to destroy any Nascent Soul Supreme Sect.

Even the recent Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain had been left in a mess under such a chaotic battle.

However!

All the attacks landed on the formation's light screen, only stirring up tiny ripples.

Everyone was momentarily stunned.

Aoxiao was not surprised. "This is the mountain-protecting grand formation that Rosycloud laid down during her years of recuperation. It cannot be broken by anyone below the Ascendant realm. Even an Ascendant would need to expend considerable effort to shake it."

"Ancestor, what should we do then?" Ao Jun, also a Grand Cultivator, asked with some anxiety.

The others felt the same.

If they couldn't break this mountain-protecting grand formation, they could only watch helplessly as Luo Chen underwent his Ascension, step by step towards the Great Power realm.

By then, once Luo Chen had free hands, he could deal with them with a casual wave.

In fact, they wouldn't even have to wait for Luo Chen to act. Feeling the commotion of their attack on the formation, figures were already flying out from Green Parasol Mountain, charging towards them.

The number wasn't large, but none of them were weak.

The late Nascent Soul Thunder Prison Roc, the mid Nascent Soul Pure Crane, and a massive black dragon stretching six to seven hundred *zhang* when fully extended.

The auras of the remaining few were also quite impressive, all at the Nascent Soul stage.

Perhaps these people were still no match for Aoxiao's side, but they could certainly delay them for a while.

That short time could make all the difference.

Faced with this predicament.

Aoxiao took a deep breath and looked towards another main peak of Green Parasol Mountain.

"Moon Wanderer! If you don't act now, when will you!"

His vast voice spread across the peaks, echoing without end.

The response was a seductive, charming laugh.

"Why are you in such a hurry, Fellow Daoist? I will open the formation now."

This familiar voice?

Within Green Parasol Mountain, Pure Crane suddenly turned his head, looking towards the direction of the Tongxuan Hall.

The Hall Master was right; that woman had never been content.

The next moment.

A strange tremor came from the direction of Tongxuan Hall.

As this tremor expanded, the formation light screen covering the entire Green Parasol Mountain began to tremble at the same frequency.

Suddenly!

A gap, just large enough for one person to pass through, opened!

"Everyone, please enter!"

"No one is allowed in!" A thunderous roar sounded instantly.

Thunder Prison Roc blocked the gap alone, charging towards it.

However, a figure strode in domineeringly, waving a sleeve and slapping out.

*Bang!*

With just one exchange, Thunder Prison Roc was sent flying back, crashing into the mountain wall.

His powerful physique meant Thunder Prison Roc wasn't seriously injured, but his eyes still showed disbelief.

When he was at mid Nascent Soul, he dared to clash with the late Nascent Soul Divine Origin Sage.

Now that he had reached the late stage, he was a true Great Demon Emperor!

In his opinion, even if he couldn't compare to Azure Frost and Luo Chen back then, he was definitely among the best in the Nascent Soul realm.

This had been proven during the attack on the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

He was almost invincible!

But now?

He looked into the distance.

Inside and outside the light screen, an old man flicked his sleeve, standing alone, blocking the path of all the demon cultivators of Green Parasol Mountain.

Behind him, over a dozen figures were filing in.

When the last person entered, Moon Wanderer seemed unable to continue holding the formation open, and the light screen suddenly closed again, as before.

Aoxiao looked back, a thoughtful expression on his face, which then turned ruthless.

The retreat was cut off. They had to risk everything to survive.

To gain enough benefits, they had to bear enough risk.

"Aoxiao!!!"

Thunder Prison Roc transformed into a bolt of thunder, roaring forward again.

But this time, Aoxiao didn't make a move.

"Leave these people to you."

Behind him, Bi Zheng and Ao Jun exchanged a glance, then struck out simultaneously, meeting the furious Thunder Prison Roc.

The others no longer held back. According to the agreement, one or two at a time, they engaged the demon cultivators of Green Parasol Mountain.

On the surface, they immediately gained the upper hand.

Aoxiao didn't care about this. All he needed was an opportunity—an opportunity to interrupt Luo Chen's Ascension.

At that moment, under the backlash of heaven and earth, Luo Chen would die from the failed breakthrough without even needing a direct battle.

Aoxiao's mind stirred. The cyan spiritual qi around him slowly spread out.

Countless strands of cyan spiritual qi formed into wind vortices.

Then, he took a step!

*Hum!*

When he reappeared, he was already high in the sky.

Suddenly!

A formation diagram materialized beneath Luo Chen's primordial spirit.

Five streams of light shot out in all directions, forming a grand formation.

Countless sword qi erupted instantly.

Seeing this, Aoxiao couldn't help but blurt out.

"The Great Five Elements Sword Formation!"

"Back then, that brat Han Zhan used this sword formation to kill my most promising descendant. I can't believe you still dare to use this formation against me."

"If you were personally presiding over this sword formation, I might be a bit wary. But just relying on a formation diagram?"

"Hmph!"

Amidst the cold snort, countless wind vortices left his body.

The rolling sword qi came, but was continuously absorbed by the wind vortices. Everything seemed futile.

However, Luo Chen's pre-arranged measures to deal with the inconvenience of acting during his Ascension were not so simple.

The sword qi dissipated, but sword light rose again!

Countless sword lights converged, forming a five-colored giant dragon, opening its mouth as if to swallow.

Aoxiao's expression remained unchanged. A golden, dazzling claw appeared in his hand.

The Heavenly Wolf Claw!

A treasure left behind by that Heavenly Wolf who had ascended from the wolf clan, refined from one of its original claws.

After thousands of years of refinement, even though it had yet to give birth to an artifact spirit, it already possessed the power of a spirit treasure!

With one claw strike, five black lines appeared in the void.

Those were spatial cracks!

Though extremely faint and fleeting.

In an instant, the five-colored giant dragon was dismantled.

"After the sword qi storm came the sword light transformation. After the sword light transformation, what other changes do you have?"

The response was countless flying threads, descending like a heavenly net.

The same technique of turning sword qi into threads, in Gu Caiyi's hands and in Luo Chen's hands, produced completely different changes.

Those flying threads were as if they had intelligence, as if they had consciousness. Even though the Heavenly Wolf Claw kept cutting them, they were still endless, as if immortal and indestructible.

In the blink of an eye, they formed a heavenly net, firmly trapping Aoxiao.

...

Tongxuan Hall.

Moon Wanderer watched this scene, her expression grave, cursing inwardly.

"The Wolf Clan's Sovereign, contending with Pingshan Jun, suppressing Azure Frost Demon Emperor? All fake!"

"A mere sword formation has you stuck in place."

"In the end, I have to make a move myself!"

She looked at the void. The massive, cross-legged primordial spirit had its eyes tightly shut, seemingly at a critical juncture.

Around that primordial spirit, dense origin qi of heaven and earth was frantically gathering.

At this moment, the sky seemed to descend, so close to the earth.

But Moon Wanderer knew these were all illusions.

It was the phenomenon of Luo Chen's massive primordial spirit, guided by spiritual opportunity, entering the ocean of origin qi during his Ascension.

She couldn't see the spiritual opportunity, but she knew that if she could disturb the ocean of origin qi in this area of heaven and earth at this moment, she could also affect Luo Chen.

And how could she disturb it?

Ascendant Great Powers had many methods, but for Nascent Soul cultivators, there was often only one.

That was to use dharma power infused with the true meaning of laws to actively manipulate a portion of the origin qi of heaven and earth.

As for the true meaning of laws, she, who had passed through the Heaven-Refining inheritance, also had them!

Of course, for a Nascent Soul cultivator, this kind of action was often very risky. A slight carelessness could lead to assimilation by heaven and earth.

"It's a pity that the Myriad Beast Diagram was borrowed by Rosycloud and self-destructed. Otherwise, why would I have to take such a risk?"

Moon Wanderer murmured, seeming to hold great hatred and resentment towards Rosycloud.

But her movements were not slow.

Chanting incantations and forming hand seals, her dharma power surged out, beginning to actively connect with heaven and earth.

Her vast dharma power formed an ocean.

Within that ocean, a bright moon slowly emerged.

As one of the Three Great Wanderers of the Northern Sea, Moon Wanderer naturally had her own brilliance.

This technique was a divine ability she had created herself after comprehending the true meaning of laws over two hundred years—Rising Moon over the Sea!

This bright moon grew larger and larger, and the surrounding origin qi of heaven and earth began to be pulled, showing signs of surging towards her.

Just then!

"*Caw!*"

A clear, resonant phoenix cry echoed through Green Parasol Mountain.

Hearing this sound, Moon Wanderer was struck as if by lightning. She instinctively looked towards Luo Chen.

A fire phoenix was spreading its wings, sweeping towards her.

"Rosycloud!!!"

In an instant, her mind was lost, and the bright white moon showed signs of collapsing.

"No, the demon phoenix has already ascended. This is definitely not her doing!"

Moon Wanderer forcibly steadied her mind, but a strong sense of unease still surged within her.

The fire phoenix pierced through clouds and mist, charging forward, diving straight into the bright moon.

*Crack...*

A clear sound of breaking came.

Like a porcelain plate shattering, or the crackling of burning wood.

The bright white moon was burned to ashes in an instant.

Her divine ability was broken, her dharma power backlashed. Moon Wanderer spat out a mouthful of blood.

She stared in terror at the phoenix that had emerged from the flames of the moon, finally understanding what it was.

Nirvana Sacred Flame!

Luo Chen had actually cultivated the Nirvana Sacred Flame, and had nurtured it to the fifth rank, giving birth to a fire spirit!

With just this one thing, she was no match for it.

"Flee!"

A simple thought filled every corner of the woman's body at this moment.

Without hesitation, she flew out of Tongxuan Hall, fleeing towards the outside of Green Parasol Mountain.

Behind her, the fire phoenix, full of intelligence, pursued relentlessly.

When the fire phoenix and Moon Wanderer had both left, the endlessly operating Great Five Elements Sword Formation suddenly had a momentary pause.

The next moment!

"*Awoo!*"

A heart-wrenching, utterly desolate wolf howl suddenly rang out.

Amidst the stirring wind and clouds, a massive silver wolf phantom broke free from the sword formation, moving its limbs, charging towards Luo Chen's primordial spirit, which was like a blazing sun.

Heavenly Wolf Howls at the Sun!

No one had expected that Aoxiao had also condensed a primordial spirit.

If he truly collided with Luo Chen's primordial spirit, the consequences would be unimaginable!

However, the response to him was not from Luo Chen, but a sword cry filled with murderous intent.

"*Zheng!*"

A flash of blood light burst out from Luo Chen's body.

In an instant, it pierced through the silver wolf's primordial spirit. The terrifying force forcibly dragged the massive silver wolf backward.

In his eyes was Luo Chen's primordial spirit, still sitting high in the nine heavens, getting farther and farther away from him.

On his body was the blood-colored sword light, eager to devour, trapping his primordial spirit so it could neither escape nor disperse.

Aoxiao knew he had failed.

*Boom!*

The massive primordial spirit was firmly nailed by the Yuantu Sword into the cliff wall of Green Parasol Mountain.

He wasn't dead yet, but with this spirit treasure flying sword suppressing his primordial spirit, he could only watch helplessly as Luo Chen took the final step of his Ascension.

And from beginning to end, Luo Chen had never truly made a move.

(End of chapter)
