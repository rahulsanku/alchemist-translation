# Chapter 608: A Fine Show? A Farce!

A struggle to the death.

Four simple words. Yet in truth, unless driven to the final extremity, how many people would choose to go that far?

When high disciples of sects or cultivators from clans traveled abroad, their elders would bestow all manner of protective arts upon them, precisely to keep promising seedlings from being forced to that step.

By comparison, wandering cultivators cherished their lives even more.

Because sect and clan cultivators had great trees to shelter beneath, while everything a wandering cultivator possessed had been gained only after bitter labor and several times the effort.

Once a life-and-death struggle began, the loser would see all prior effort come to nothing, every bit of labor washed away by the current, and might even end up making another man's wedding clothes.

As a descendant of the Great Zhou royal clan, Huangfu Song had lived in comfort since childhood.

Even after calamity struck, even after he fled to the Northern Sea and became a wandering cultivator, he still had the support of people like Lord Gui and the Night Elder. He even had the protection of a late Golden Core great cultivator like the Purple Dowager.

To make him stake his life merely for Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake? He was not willing.

Luo Chen was the exact opposite.

In his youth, even for a handful of cultivation resources, he had to plan with meticulous care, squeezing out spirit stones one by one from between his teeth.

In every conflict, unless it erupted in haste, he made every effort to plan ahead.

Yet even so, he rarely grew careless. More often than not, the moment he acted, he used all his strength.

Just like the battle of Little Huan Mountain years ago, when he destroyed the Duan family. Hidden behind the curtain, his first move had been a Great Perfection Fireball Art accumulated to its absolute peak.

Or like the recent battle on Cold Light Island. Facing a siege by several enemies, and with formations already laid in advance, he had activated the Heavenly Roc Transformation without the slightest hesitation, revealing his strongest power. He had never once considered saving those methods for the end.

A so-called trump card was only a trump card after it had been used.

If you died, then those trump cards belonged to someone else.

Hearing Luo Chen's threatening words and feeling the spiritual pressure rising from him stage by stage, Huangfu Song likewise released the vast spiritual pressure of his own Golden Core Stage Seven cultivation.

He suppressed the anger in his heart as much as he could and advised, "Daoist Azure Yang, why must you and I make such a great clash of arms? I sent in a calling card because I wished to settle this dispute peacefully."

Luo Chen sneered. "Then when I did not reply, when you were refused at the door, why did you insist on forcing your way into my Dao field?"

Huangfu Song choked for a moment, then said, "Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake is truly of great use to me. You are different. Alone, with no... with only a few followers and spirit pets, would changing to another third-tier low-grade spirit land not be enough?"

"Heh. Your Golden Core Stage Seven cultivation has been fully revealed. I imagine a low-grade spirit land like this is not of much help to your cultivation. Why must you insist on it?"

Question answered by question. Each one a challenge.

Luo Chen's attitude was far too unyielding.

At the same time, Huangfu Song was inwardly astonished.

He had already unfolded his own spiritual pressure to its limit. Logically speaking, against cultivators below Golden Core Stage Seven, it should have suppressed them in momentum.

Yet Daoist Azure Yang's Golden Core Stage Five spiritual pressure was contending with his own without falling the slightest bit behind.

In fact, as it climbed higher and higher, it even seemed ready to counterattack.

Was the other man truly only at Golden Core Stage Five?

Steadying himself, Huangfu Song had already made every effort to remain calm, but his next words still carried a touch of impatience.

"What exactly will it take for you to give up Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake?"

Negotiating terms?

Mockery appeared on Luo Chen's face. Then he slowly shook his head and spat out two words.

"I won't."

The moment those words fell, the spiritual pressure he released reached its zenith.

In the void, two surges of mana collided against each other.

The result was that Huangfu Song's entire body shuddered, and his figure staggered for an instant.

Looking at Luo Chen, however, he stood as steady as Mount Tai, unmoving.

Seeing this, the six Golden Core cultivators behind Huangfu Song all changed expression in shock.

They understood what it meant with absolute clarity.

In purity and depth, Luo Chen's mana was still above Huangfu Song's Golden Core Stage Seven mana. Even if it was only higher by a thread, it meant he had surpassed him.

At this moment, Huangfu Song's expression was exceedingly ugly.

Since Luo Chen had appeared, a single palm had forced him to use a protective measure. In their verbal exchange, Luo Chen had refused to yield even an inch. Now, even in a contest of spiritual pressure, Huangfu Song had fallen behind.

All of this had completely exceeded his expectations.

Even if he was confident that, relying on his own trump cards, he could defeat the other man in a direct battle, the rumored methods of Devil Lord Azure Yang were also incomparably sharp. If they truly fought to the point of using trump cards, the gains would not make up for the losses.

But now, beneath the gaze of ten thousand eyes, he, Huangfu Song, was already riding a tiger and unable to dismount.

Perhaps that move would be suitable?

Head lowered, right hand behind his back, he quietly pinched a spiritual seal.

Suddenly, Huangfu Song shouted.

"Devil Lord Azure Yang, all under heaven is the king's land. Why cling so desperately to a mere Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake?"

Luo Chen frowned and replied, "Peng Lake may not be large, but I have managed it for years. If I were to give it up, how could it be so easy... hm?"

As he spoke, Luo Chen's expression abruptly changed.

A layer of black radiance flashed across his body and vanished.

Within him, the Rotten Axe-Handle Black Chess Pieces were trembling faintly, as if they had come under attack.

"A soul-intimidating art that bewilders the mind? Or one of the rumored word-spirit curse-killing arts held by the Primordial Demon Sect?"

As Luo Chen weighed this in his mind, Huangfu Song stared fixedly at him, unwilling to miss even the slightest shift in his expression.

When Luo Chen said those words, the great stone in Huangfu Song's heart had nearly dropped.

So-called "not so easy" meant there were terms to discuss.

As expected, the royal secret art he had learned was effective. Amid words spoken without anyone noticing, it could shake an enemy's will.

But the next instant, when Luo Chen let out that soft sound of surprise, Huangfu Song sensed something wrong.

"So this Daoist Azure Yang's mana is no weaker than mine. Fine. But even his divine soul foundation is this powerful? He is not affected by my Imperial Voice, and he reacted so quickly."

"No. I need to say a few more lines, break down his will. Whether we negotiate or fight afterward, the initiative will be on my side."

Just as Huangfu Song opened his mouth, intending to say something more, his body suddenly stiffened.

He met Luo Chen's eyes.

Deep as an abyss. Cold as ice.

In a daze, within his sea of consciousness, illusions seemed to manifest.

"Illusion art!"

A wisp of purple light emerged from his chest, blocking Luo Chen's gaze.

Huangfu Song loosened slightly inside. Fortunately, he carried a magic treasure left behind by his ancestor, one capable of resisting illusion arts below the Nascent Soul level.

This sort of superficial divine soul attack, before that magic treasure, was nothing more than a petty trick to deceive the eyes.

However, the illusions within his sea of consciousness abruptly changed.

It was as if jade towers and jeweled halls hung high in the sky, immortal qi drifting through them, white clouds stretching into the distance.

Heavenly maidens in gauze robes and flying sleeves sang softly and danced gracefully.

Immortals with feather fans, tall crowns, and broad sashes came and went, laughing and conversing.

But after these heavenly maidens and immortals, fairy-like as they seemed, flew out from the layered towers, they transformed into resentful ghosts and wraiths that lunged toward him.

Looking carefully, how were those jade towers and jeweled halls any immortal sanctuary? They were clearly ghost towers and spirit houses.

Hiss...

The purple light blazed brightly and poured into his sea of consciousness, madly dispersing the illusions.

Huangfu Song shuddered violently and woke from the illusion.

Within that illusion, it felt as if a long time had passed. In reality, it had been only one meeting of gazes, a single instant.

Cold sweat seeped from his back, and Huangfu Song instinctively avoided Luo Chen's eyes.

Beside his ear came Luo Chen's calm voice.

"Fellow Daoist Huangfu, please leave."

Huangfu Song opened his mouth, but for a long while no words came out.

Clearly, in that instant, his Soul-Shaking Imperial Voice had failed to intimidate the other man. Instead, the other man's illusion art had stirred waves in his heart and nearly made him lose control of his mind.

If not for the measure left behind before his Nascent Soul ancestor's fall, in that single instant he likely would have been killed by Luo Chen a thousand times over.

A trace of fear arose of its own accord.

He feared the lingering ghostly shadows that would not fade from memory, and he feared Devil Lord Azure Yang's endless succession of methods.

Instinctively, he turned around.

"Let's go."

"Master, what are you saying?"

Huangfu Song froze. Facing the Night Elder's stunned question, he instinctively shook his head.

"No, I..."

"Fellow Daoist Huangfu, the weather is poor today, so I will not see you off. Please leave."

This time, Luo Chen's voice was colder still.

It plainly carried the sign of restraint pressed to its limit, the sense that if they did not leave, he would strike.

Like a volcano on the verge of eruption.

An ugly smile appeared on Huangfu Song's face. "Forgive the disturbance."

After that, ignoring the displeasure of the Night Elder and Lord Gui, he led the four Golden Core cultivators under his command away.

As they left, he kept explaining himself to the two old ministers.

Things like how their own side was not of one mind, how Devil Lord Azure Yang was troublesome, how Peng Lake's spirit land was only so-so, and how there were even better places in the Fulong Mountain Range.

Outsiders did not know about the collision between him and Luo Chen in that brief span of time, and half-believing, half-doubting, they accepted his words.

But the two old ministers, who understood this young master's temperament deeply, could not stop sighing inwardly.

Sometimes, taking one step back did not mean open seas and skies. It meant falling into a doom beyond redemption.

Master, do you truly understand how serious this matter is?

When they reached the lakeshore, Zhang Jiadi led his people in returning the cloud ship and the hundred-plus Foundation Establishment cultivators suppressed by the formation, all intact and unharmed. Only then did Huangfu Song's group leave Peng Lake in disgrace.

Seeing this, the onlookers felt unbearably stifled.

What about the great battle they had been promised?

What about a real fight, a struggle to the death?

The fine show had only just begun, and it ended before it had even formally taken the stage?

As for Luo Chen, the person at the center of it all, he merely watched Huangfu Song and the others leave with cold eyes.

After they had gone far away, Heavenjade could not help asking aloud.

"Master, these enemies were so discourteous. Are you truly letting them go?"

The real meaning in her words was that Luo Chen had always been decisive in killing. How could he tolerate this kind of provocation?

It did not match her impression of Luo Chen.

In response, Luo Chen merely lifted his gaze slightly.

"Cultivating in the Myriad Immortals Assembly, there are some people's face that still must be given."

The direction he looked toward held only drifting white clouds and no visible trace of anyone, yet it seemed as if he were meeting someone's eyes.

Heavenjade thought for a moment, and soon understood the person Luo Chen was wary of.

She also understood that Luo Chen, who had seemed so incomparably forceful today, had in truth been restraining himself the entire time. Otherwise, with his usual style, how could he have tolerated such provocation from the other side?

Heavenjade was still somewhat worried.

On the surface, her master had forced Huangfu Song and the others to retreat under the eyes of the crowd, increasing his prestige further.

But an enemy at the late Golden Core stage, once offended, and then simply allowed to leave like this...

"What if they retaliate later? That Huangfu Song is still a Golden Core Stage Seven great cultivator!"

"Hiss..."

The sneer was undisguised.

Even Duan Li, who had been silent at the side, found it hard to understand. Since when were great cultivators so unworthy of regard?

Luo Chen waved a hand. "No matter. Huangfu Song has the title of great cultivator in name only. In truth, he is nothing but an embroidered pillow."

An embroidered pillow?

Duan Li's face filled with confusion. The other man's realm was not fake, and that small display of purple sword qi had nearly taken his life.

All of that fully lived up to his late Golden Core realm. How was he an embroidered pillow?

Facing the two people's confusion, Luo Chen sighed.

"Although I have met this man only once, he is far too shallow. One glance is enough to see through his essence."

"Fierce in bearing but timid in courage, fond of schemes but lacking decision, cherishing his own body when doing great things, and forgetting his life at the sight of small profit. How could such a person become a grave danger to me?"

Regardless of how the two understood those words, Luo Chen did not feel there would be much trouble.

They were both seizing spirit land, and what he had done in the Swallow Islands was the best possible comparison to today's matter.

For the third-tier low-grade spirit vein on Invitation Moon Island, he planned before moving, gave Yan Nantian no chance at all, went straight to his door, and struck without explanation.

One had to know that at the time, Luo Chen's foundation had not yet stabilized, and even his realm was not necessarily higher than Yan Nantian's.

But Huangfu Song?

His own strength, as well as the number of powerful cultivators under him, plainly far exceeded Luo Chen's side.

Yet he still insisted on playing at courtesy first and force afterward, using reason to persuade and profit to tempt.

Then, when he could not outargue or overpower Luo Chen, he even resorted to petty tricks, hoping to claw back a round.

If Luo Chen had not feared the eight-star demon hunter behind him, he would have killed him on the spot in the great marsh of Peng Lake.

Nascent Soul trump card or no Nascent Soul trump card.

Inside his Peng Lake Dao field, there truly was a Sage Perfected Being!

After these years nourished by the soul-nourishing wood, Han Zhan's soul body had grown increasingly stable. His strength, Luo Chen imagined, had also recovered by thirty percent.

If truly pushed, Han Zhan would also act on his behalf.

"Enough."

Luo Chen turned and looked at Duan Li.

"Thank you for your righteous aid today, Fellow Daoist. I will remember this favor."

Duan Li hurriedly waved his hands. "It hardly counts as a favor. On the contrary, I brought you trouble."

He had more or less understood.

With Daoist Azure Yang's methods, those enemies had never been in the other man's eyes at all.

By abruptly stepping in to help, he had instead forced Daoist Azure Yang to act personally to extricate him, and might even have disrupted some other arrangement of his.

Luo Chen did not mind. "I accept the intention behind it. But now is not the right time for idle conversation. I still have a guest to receive. Let us chat again in a couple of days."

Duan Li froze.

Another guest had come to the door?

His gaze swept in every direction. Was it these wandering cultivators who wished to visit after hearing of his reputation?

"Angler."

The divine sense transmission entered his ear, and he abruptly understood.

"Heavenjade, see Fellow Daoist Duan Li off for me."

"Yes, Master!"

After Heavenjade had seen the guest away, Luo Chen drifted back to Peng Island.

Before long, a streak of escaping light descended beside him.

"Today, I really did watch a fine show!" the Angler said with a laugh.

Luo Chen shook his head. "Nothing but a farce."

The Angler's mouth twitched. A farce?

Not necessarily.

"I never expected the legendary Devil Lord Azure Yang to be skilled in artifact-forging as well!"

Luo Chen was not surprised that the Angler had called out the alias he used while moving through the Northern Sea. He had never hidden it in the first place. He had even made a great show of spreading it.

After this matter, those petty people who coveted Peng Lake's spirit land would presumably abandon their thoughts.

At worst, they would go inquire about Devil Lord Azure Yang's record in the battle of Cold Light Island.

That was a terrifying existence who had killed several peers, and under the joint attack of two late Golden Core great cultivators, had managed to kill one and force one back.

Even within the Myriad Immortals Assembly, few could be compared to someone like that.

Aside from the three eight-star demon hunters, probably only the dozen or so seven-star demon hunters could match him a little.

"A trifling little artifact-forging art, nothing more than something Luo does in his leisure to pass the time."

Luo Chen first offered a modest sentence, then asked with deliberate implication, "How did that one react?"

The Angler knew who he was asking about and answered honestly.

"Dissatisfied, and satisfied."

"Hm?" Luo Chen frowned.

The Angler laughed. "Relax. What the Purple Dowager was dissatisfied with was Huangfu Song's temperament. What she was satisfied with was your handling of the matter. You need not worry too much. Although the Purple Dowager and Huangfu Song have an old connection, after so many years as wandering cultivators, the affection has long since faded. Right now, her entire mind is fixed on reaching Golden Core Perfected Completion and breaking through to the Nascent Soul stage. How would she be willing to make a great clash of arms for an old acquaintance's junior?"

This short speech contained quite a lot of information.

Especially the final part, which settled Luo Chen's heart.

When cultivators reached the great late stage of each realm, they generally disliked large conflicts and wished only to cultivate quietly with one mind.

Unless they were truly forced.

Like Luo Chen himself. In his early years, after reaching the late Foundation Establishment stage, aside from taking revenge and gathering resources, he had basically cultivated in seclusion on Tianlan Peak until he advanced to the Golden Core stage and only then traveled abroad.

Compared with advancing from Foundation Establishment to Golden Core, breaking through from Golden Core to Nascent Soul was obviously even more delicate.

Such existences would not act lightly.

"Looking at it this way, Luo did handle today's matter with the proper measure." Luo Chen gave a soft laugh. Relieved, he cupped his hands toward the Angler. "Of course, I must also thank you for your assistance, my lord."

The Angler waved a hand. "Calling me lord is too distant. We can address each other as fellow Daoists."

What was a fellow Daoist?

Only those walking the same Dao could be friends.

The Angler's implication was clear, and his purpose went without saying: Luo Chen's guidance on the path of body tempering.

Luo Chen understood at once and extended a hand in invitation.

"Earlier we said we should set a time and place to exchange a few things. Why choose another day when today will do?"

The Angler broke into a broad smile. "I could ask for nothing better!"

The two looked at each other and smiled, then stepped into the palace halls of Peng Island.

*(End of Chapter)*
