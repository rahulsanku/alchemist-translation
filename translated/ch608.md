# Chapter 608 – A Fine Show? A Farce!

A life-and-death struggle.

The four words sounded simple, but in reality, how many people would truly choose that path before reaching the point of absolute desperation?

Whether they were elite disciples of sects or cultivators from great clans, elders would always bestow all manner of defensive treasures and techniques before sending juniors out to travel the world. The purpose was simple: to prevent promising seedlings from ever being forced into such a situation.

Compared to them, rogue cultivators cherished their lives even more.

After all, sect cultivators and clan cultivators had mighty trees sheltering them. Rogue cultivators, on the other hand, had earned everything they possessed through painstaking effort and countless times the hardship.

Once a battle to the death began, the loser would lose everything. All their years of labor would vanish into smoke, perhaps even becoming another man’s spoils.

Huangfu Song was descended from the Great Zhou imperial clan and had enjoyed luxury since childhood.

Even after the upheaval that forced him into exile in the North Sea and reduced him to a rogue cultivator, he still had supporters like Elder Gui and Elder Ye. He even enjoyed the protection of a late-stage Golden Core powerhouse like the Purple Queen.

For him to risk his life over the Eight Hundred Li Penghu? He simply could not bear it.

Luo Chen was the exact opposite.

In his youth, he had pinched every spirit stone for the sake of a little cultivation resource.

Every battle he fought, unless it erupted suddenly, was meticulously planned in advance.

Even so, he rarely held anything back once he moved. He struck with everything he had.

Just like the battle at Little Huan Mountain years ago, when he destroyed the Duan Clan from the shadows with a fully perfected Great Fireball Technique.

Or the recent battle at Coldlight Island—facing multiple enemies at once, he had activated the Heavenly Roc Transformation without hesitation and displayed his full strength immediately.

A trump card only counted once it was used.

If you died before using it, then those trump cards simply became someone else’s.
