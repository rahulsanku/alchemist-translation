# Chapter 1109: Grand Empyrean Sleeve, Ascending from the Vast Sea

On Sea-Watching Cliff.

The white-robed Daoist stood facing the wind, his robes rustling loudly.

His eyes narrowed slightly as he gazed at the distant ocean, slowly raising his sleeve.

The wide, voluminous sleeve swelled at this moment, especially the gold-rimmed, silver-patterned cuff, which opened like a black hole.

An infinite suction force arose spontaneously.

The entire sky, the boundless ocean, seemed to be enveloped within it.

“Gather!”

With a low shout, his spiritual power circulated.

The next moment, countless living creatures leaped out of the ocean, flying toward the great sleeve.

There were fish, shrimp, crabs, clams, demon beasts, and sea tribes—all creatures bewildered and unaware. Once inside, they only felt darkness, stumbling about in a daze.

At this point, the swollen robe gradually quieted down.

But Luo Chen fell into deep thought.

Not far away, a tall, long-legged woman in blue watched this scene, her eyes sparkling brightly.

She slowly approached, a smile in her eyes. “Congratulations, husband, on mastering this great divine ability!”

Luo Chen snapped out of his contemplation. Seeing Cheng Haixin, he revealed a sincere smile.

“What great divine ability is this? It’s merely using the Void Gu, spatial restrictions, and this specially made sleeve to barely perform a Sleeve Containing Heaven and Earth. Compared to Azure Frost, who casually waved her hand and effortlessly collected a large number of Adept and Sage cultivators without a trace of worldly aura, I’m still far, far behind.”

Cheng Haixin smiled. “Legend has it that the enchantress Azure Frost’s true body is the divine bird Azure Luan, possessing spatial talent. Having reached the Sage realm, she even comprehended the true meaning of spatial laws ahead of time. Husband is not proficient in this path, yet in only three years, you can perform a similar technique—how can that not be considered a success?”

Luo Chen chuckled wryly upon hearing this.

Similar, after all, was only similar.

The Sleeve Containing Heaven and Earth he now performed, compared to Azure Frost’s, was ultimately similar in form, not in spirit.

It could be seen as an improved version, or a weakened one.

Its only advantage was that it was easier for humans to cultivate, and it could be renamed and passed down.

Luo Chen flicked his sleeve. Light flickered on the gold-rimmed, silver-patterned fabric, and the countless living creatures that had been silently collected earlier were thrown out again.

Like rain, they fell into the sea.

As if they had been dreaming, they were completely bewildered.

“Let it be called Grand Empyrean Sleeve!”

Luo Chen thought to himself, then looked at Cheng Haixin.

“Do you want to learn this technique?”

Cheng Haixin was startled. “Can I, your wife?”

“Haha! Since ancient times, creating divine abilities has been extremely difficult, often requiring centuries of accumulation to create a single usable technique. But for later learners, it’s merely following a map to find the treasure, comprehending its essence. Especially with the creator present to personally teach, learning only depends on whether you want to or not.”

Luo Chen laughed heartily, his face full of confidence.

Azure Frost’s Sleeve Containing Heaven and Earth required possessing the true meaning of spatial laws, but his improved Grand Empyrean Sleeve did not have such a high threshold.

Although others did not have such marvelous gu insects as the Void Gu, by using specially made dharma robes and sleeves, and learning the corresponding hundreds of spatial restrictions, they could still perform this technique with some skill.

In the two paths of artifact refinement and formation arrangement, Luo Chen still had some confidence.

Cheng Haixin’s eyes lit up. How could she not want to learn such an elegant and extraordinary technique?

“Please teach me, husband!”

“No rush. First, learn these spatial restrictions, and I will tailor-make a dharma robe for you.”

“Mm, alright.”

…

For three years, Luo Chen stayed on the Swallow Islands.

After Cheng Haixin’s condition improved slightly and her realm stabilized a little, she emerged from seclusion to meet him.

Exchanging heartfelt feelings was only natural.

On ordinary days, Luo Chen accompanied Cheng Haixin in eating, living, and sleeping together, touring the sea and admiring the moon.

Sometimes they went far away, covering hundreds of thousands of li in a single day. Luo Chen also personally took Cheng Haixin to visit nearby Sages.

The sweetness of that time seemed to make up for the hundreds of years the woman had spent waiting alone.

Luo Chen did this intentionally.

Among the women he had been involved with in his life, whether Huiniang, Caiyi, or Canglong, he felt he had fulfilled his responsibilities without much deficiency.

Only Cheng Haixin—a woman’s silent waiting was always moving.

Therefore, before his ascension, Luo Chen deliberately took a few years to accompany her.

In this process, it was not just about eating, drinking, and having fun.

Luo Chen also broke down the cultivation of the Sage realm, teaching it to Cheng Haixin from a high-level perspective.

This included suitable magical treasures, killing moves, and even divine abilities with a lower entry threshold!

The improved *Grand Empyrean Sleeve* and the scalable *Qi Sea Floating Heaven*—Luo Chen taught them all without reservation.

In the Pill Hall.

Blazing flames raged, heat waves rolled.

Luo Chen stood within, forming hand seals one after another, solely to refine a new dharma robe for Cheng Haixin.

The materials used were, of course, the best.

Spatial Prism Apertures containing spatial power, tough and unyielding Ice Silkworm Silk, rare water-attributed demon cores…

One by one, the materials were thrown in, all merging into the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

But although he was refining the dharma robe, a considerable part of Luo Chen’s attention was focused on the Primordial Unity Cauldron itself.

“Three years!”

“That Magnetic Mountain still hasn’t been completely refined, and I can’t even see any possibility of it merging in.”

“Where exactly is the problem?”

Luo Chen was quite puzzled.

According to Li Canghai’s method, even if he couldn’t extract the mountain soul of the Magnetic Mountain to condense it into an artifact spirit, it should still be able to integrate well.

But after three years, progress was slow.

His Primordial Unity Cauldron was still extremely heavy. Forget using it against Ascendant peers; even if he released it in ordinary combat, it would probably be too slow.

This was not Luo Chen’s original intention.

In his imagination, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, having absorbed the Magnetic Mountain, should retain its original agility while greatly increasing its suppression power.

But being this clumsy, how could it be used to restrict peers of the same level? It would probably be a burden instead.

“Agility…”

Luo Chen pondered silently, vaguely having some clues in his mind.

“No rush. Let’s take it step by step!”

Luo Chen turned his attention back and focused on refining the dharma robe for Cheng Haixin.

It took half a year, and finally, he refined a dharma robe that possessed defense, clarity of mind, and the ability to gather and collect all things!

When the dharma robe was first completed, with the help of a pre-arranged formation, it attracted naturally formed heavenly thunder.

After undergoing the baptism of the thunder tribulation, it finally reached the true artifact level.

From this, it could be seen that Luo Chen’s attainments in artifact refinement had unknowingly reached the fourth-rank master level.

With the dharma robe’s assistance, Cheng Haixin found it much easier to learn the *Grand Empyrean Sleeve*.

While instructing her, Luo Chen also occasionally took Cheng Haixin out to visit nearby Sages.

For example, Mei Yiqi of Langya Mountain was an old acquaintance from before.

There was also a place called Emerald Sea, not far from the former Coldlight Island, where Luo Chen took Cheng Haixin to visit a female cultivator.

During this process, Luo Chen occasionally spread his divine sense to search the surroundings.

But unfortunately, he found nothing!

Cheng Haixin occasionally noticed and asked what he was looking for. Luo Chen smiled and explained that it was just an old enemy.

Indeed, an enemy—his name was Blood Nightmare Moluo!

Back when Luo Chen had just formed his Sage core, he had encountered this person at the Coldlight Island ruins. A great battle ensued, and Luo Chen seized his Yuantu sword.

As for Blood Nightmare Moluo, under the pursuit of Blood Wanderer, he had fled in panic.

Luo Chen was somewhat wary of this person.

Not of Blood Nightmare Moluo himself, but of the large amount of residual power from Blood Sea Ancestor that he had absorbed from the Yuantu sword.

If the other party was still alive, was it Blood Nightmare Moluo, or Blood Sea Ancestor?

But according to rumors, Blood Nightmare Moluo and Blood Wanderer had both perished together!

Whether this rumor was true or false, Luo Chen indeed had not found any trace of either of them.

Another two years passed.

Xu Muxian returned.

He had previously borrowed the Great Sage Feng Zhang’s defensive treasure, the Phoenix Nest, and after the matter was resolved, he went specifically to return it.

Unexpectedly, that trip took five years.

“Some unforeseen circumstances occurred in the middle, so it was delayed for a long time.”

Xu Muxian sighed, recounting the story of what had happened.

A large number of sea tribes had begun to inexplicably retreat, leaving the Northern Sea human cultivation world.

Thus, the remaining demon beasts seemed isolated and weak.

Human experts began to reclaim their lost territories, and battles were naturally unavoidable.

Old Monster Feng Zhang, as one of the top experts among humans, naturally led by example, initiating attacks on the remaining demon beasts.

Xu Muxian was also invited to join.

He could not refuse this matter, so he participated in several great battles.

Luo Chen’s heart stirred upon hearing this.

The sea tribes retreating—could it be the Sea Emperor Whale’s order?

He was quite sensible. Luo Chen had been thinking that after his lifebound treasure was complete, before his ascension, he would go find him to test his skills.

With such an action, Luo Chen found it hard to make a move.

“Also, there’s a piece of gossip.” Xu Muxian hesitated for a moment, then finally couldn’t help but say it. “According to rumors, Heaven’s End has been sealed for a long time, with no sign of the great expert. Some with keen divine sense detected some heavenly changes a few years ago, and some suspect that the great expert Li Canghai has already ascended.”

Luo Chen was stunned.

Li Canghai ascended?

He had no expectation of this at all.

But thinking carefully, this seemed to be Li Canghai’s consistent style.

When he broke through to the Ascendant realm, no one knew.

When he ascended to the upper realm, he also left without a sound.

It was just unknown whether he went to the Immortal Realm or the Mystic Realm.

It should be the Immortal Realm, after all, going to the Mystic Realm required a special passage and coordinate guidance.

Ascending to the Immortal Realm, however, relied on the local world’s generous bestowal of spiritual opportunities, as well as the connection between the great thousand world and the Immortal Realm, with the cultivator ascending by soaring in a mysterious trance.

“But in the end, it’s just a rumor. I need to go see for myself. Muxian, you stay and guard the Swallows. After I return, come with me back to the Luo Tian Sect in the Eastern Wilderness. Then, you will truly become my disciple!”

Xu Muxian couldn’t help but show excitement. “Alright!”

…

At Heaven’s End.

A figure suddenly appeared, silently watching for a long time, then finally turned and left.

Inside, there was no longer any trace of Li Canghai.

However, on this land, a rather powerful killing formation had been left behind, facing outward but not inward.

That should be the only goodwill Li Canghai had left for the Northern Sea cultivators.

If a critical moment came, once this killing formation was activated, all those below the Ascendant realm would be turned into blood water, and even Ascendant great experts could be held off for several days.

“Leaving so freely, truly unexpected!”

Luo Chen sighed with emotion, but he also understood Li Canghai’s actions.

Having already refined the spirit treasure Mountain-Seal Seal, this world was no longer his homeland but a shackle and cage. What was there to be nostalgic about?

Then when should he, himself, ascend?

Luo Chen returned to the Swallow Islands in silence and informed everyone to prepare to return to the Eastern Wilderness with him.

But unexpectedly, Cheng Haixin refused this time.

“Why?” Luo Chen asked. “With my current abilities, and the Luo Tian Sect’s position in the Eastern Wilderness, it is enough to shelter your future cultivation.”

Cheng Haixin smoothed her hair and smiled. “With husband’s abilities and personality, I’m afraid that imitating the great expert Li Canghai’s ascension will happen soon!”

Luo Chen was silent. This woman was intelligent and perceptive. After discovering his Ascendant realm and connecting it to Li Canghai’s matter, she had already understood everything.

These few years of wholehearted guidance and tender intimacy were both compensation and preparation for his ascension.

He, Luo Chen, was not Li Canghai, not so free and easy. Before ascending, he always had to settle his loved ones and companions.

Cheng Haixin sighed. “Even if I follow husband back to the Luo Tian Sect, in the end, it will be another parting, a parting where we will never see each other again. Even if I stay in the Luo Tian Sect, your rumored dao companions might not make things difficult for me, but the Luo Tian Sect holds no affection for me. And here, these Swallow Islands…”

The woman’s gaze looked down at the Swallow Islands, which seemed about to spread their wings and soar, her eyes filled with deep reluctance.

She was born here, grew up here, broke through her realm here, had her clan and friends here, and countless scattered cultivators admired and worshipped her.

How could she just leave?

“Let Xu Muxian return with you! Now, his dao heart is firm, and he has inherited your alchemy arts. He has hope of advancing to the Ascendant realm in the future. The Luo Tian Sect is another opportunity for him.”

“As for me?”

Cheng Haixin smiled slightly. “Perhaps husband will achieve the realm of a Grand Empyrean Dao Ancestor in the future, possessing the ability to cross time and space. Then, with these small but conspicuous Swallow Islands, perhaps husband can easily find me.”

Luo Chen was speechless for a moment, only able to hold the woman tightly in his arms.

…

“Master, is Mistress really not coming back to the Eastern Wilderness with us?”

Above the Xingtian Sea, Xu Muxian couldn’t help but ask.

Luo Chen’s expression was calm. “She has made her decision, and I naturally won’t force her. But don’t worry, I’ve taught her everything she needs, she’s not lacking in protective means, and I’ve smoothed over relations in the vicinity. If you have the heart in the future, you can come back and visit often.”

Xu Muxian nodded heavily. “Disciple will, of course!”

It was still that nameless small island, still that teleportation formation.

When the teleportation light flashed and vanished, the woman watching from afar in the distance had a hint of regret on her face.

But this time, there was also a bit of satisfaction within the regret.

She gently touched her lower abdomen, a soft radiance radiating from her face.

“The Eastern Wilderness is too unfamiliar and complicated for us. Staying in the Northern Sea, perhaps you can grow better, and maybe one day you’ll have a chance to find him.”

“What do you think, child?”

…

Green Parasol Mountain in the Eastern Wilderness.

The teleportation tremor flashed and vanished.

When Luo Chen emerged with Kongchan and Xu Muxian, Pure Crane and Heavenjade were already bowing in wait.

“Greetings, Hall Master!”

“Welcome back, Master!”

Xu Muxian, who was beside them, couldn’t help but be slightly shaken.

In his divine sense perception, these two women were both at the Sage realm, and outside, there were many powerful auras.

Could all these people be under his master’s command?

“No need for formalities. I won’t be staying long; you may disperse.” Luo Chen waved his hand, then instructed, “Kongchan, take Muxian back to the Luo Tian Sect first!”

“Yes, Master.”

Xu Muxian asked doubtfully, “Master, aren’t you coming back?”

Luo Chen shook his head. “Your master has some small matters to attend to outside.”

For Luo Chen, they were small matters, but for the place he was about to go, it would be a world-shaking event.

(End of chapter)
