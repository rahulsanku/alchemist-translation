# Chapter 723: Spending One Hundred Million, Netherfire Extends an Invitation

"Fellow Daoist Luo, are you all right?"

The blocked rain curtain fell once more.

The scorching heat still lingering on the red-jade plaza seemed to speak of the previous confrontation without smoke or flame.

Sage Langya looked at Luo Chen with some concern, unconcealable shock in his eyes.

He truly had never expected that this rogue cultivator he had happened to meet on the road would possess such ability.

In a pure collision of Sage domains, Luo Chen had suppressed the third-level Sage Split Heaven Stream Lord with absolute advantage.

The so-called Sage domain was nothing more than using the resonance between the Nascent Soul and heaven and earth to control the external spiritual qi of heaven and earth corresponding to one's own attribute.

The stronger the Nascent Soul, the stronger the spiritual qi of heaven and earth it could control.

Thus, the height of one's realm became the sole standard for measuring the strength of a domain.

For countless years, this standard had always been regarded as a rule as firm as jade.

But just now, Luo Chen's performance had directly shattered that standard. With the realm of a first-level Sage, he had overwhelmed Split Heaven Stream Lord in a domain contest, and had even forced the other party to take out a true artifact.

If such a thing were spoken aloud, perhaps no one would believe it!

Yet it had truly happened right before his eyes.

Thinking of this, Sage Langya felt somewhat embarrassed inwardly as well.

Earlier, he had still felt that because his realm was one layer higher than the other man's, even if his divine sense was inferior to Luo Chen's, he should have confidence in surpassing him.

But now it seemed that whether divine sense or magic power, he was weaker by quite a lot.

If the other party possessed a suitable magic treasure, then if they truly fought...

"How can I have such thoughts!"

"The better he performs, the steadier my position in the Cang Hai Alliance becomes, and no one will be able to look down on me. After all, I am the one who introduced him here as a friend."

Leaving aside the thoughts churning in Sage Langya's heart.

Luo Chen brushed non-existent dust from his clothes, smiled, said he was fine, and then stepped toward Return Origin Palace.

Along the way, there was no divine sense probing him.

But the moment he stepped into the palace, several gazes all looked over in unison.

Smiling faintly, Luo Chen performed a Daoist salute, his bearing calm. "Rogue cultivator Luo Chen greets all Fellow Daoists."

Everyone fell silent, speechless for a time.

A middle-aged man with somewhat familiar features sat in the upper seat and was just about to speak when the elder seated beside him spoke first.

"Wilderness Wanderer, please take a seat!"

Shang Sihai froze, then frowned at Netherfire Stream Lord.

Not only him, even Split Heaven Stream Lord, who had entered the hall in advance, raised his head in astonishment and looked toward Netherfire.

No one had expected that after Luo Chen displayed his strength, the first person to accept him would actually be Netherfire, not Shang Sihai.

Luo Chen was somewhat surprised, but he did not show anything. He openly found a chair and sat down.

Sage Langya took a seat beside him.

As he sat, he transmitted with divine sense, introducing everyone in the hall.

"Of the two above, the one on the left is Netherfire Stream Lord, and the one on the right is Shang Sihai."

Luo Chen looked over. Netherfire Stream Lord was half-lying and half-sitting sickly against his chair, his face wax-yellow, his entire aura locked down tightly so that not the slightest depth could be seen.

Shang Sihai appeared comparatively young, but his features gave Luo Chen a sense of familiarity.

Very quickly, Luo Chen found where this familiarity came from.

At the Jadecold City auction back then, Four Seas Merchant Alliance's young master Shang Junshu had once competed with him for the soul-nourishing wood.

According to rumor, Shang Junshu's father was the famous Four Seas Sage, Shang Sihai.

Since they were father and son, it was no wonder there was a sense of familiarity.

"Below Netherfire, the first person on the left is the woman named Du Feiyan. In Moluo Stream, she is called Mistress Smokecloud."

"The second needs no explanation. Split Heaven Stream Lord. His true name cannot be verified. It is said that only Blood Nightmare Moluo knows his real name."

"The third is Calamity Star Stream Lord..."

Matching the introductions from the road with the names called out now, Luo Chen gradually linked these people to the rumors and deeds associated with them.

The concrete images of one Northern Sea great figure after another gradually finished sketching themselves in his mind.

Overall, because most of the Sage cultivators on Moluo Stream's side had once been dependents of the Primordial Demon Sect, whether in Daoist titles, cultivation arts, or deeds, they leaned more toward the demonic path.

As for the Cang Hai Alliance's side, it was quite odd.

There was Shang Sihai, who had made his fortune as a merchant and excelled at smooth dealings in all directions.

There was Madam Mi, with a pair of peach-blossom eyes and quite the air of a female cultivator from a Joyous Union Sect.

There was also someone like Old Ghost Nanqi, whose origins were secret and whose conduct was moody and unpredictable.

Inside the spacious hall, there were no attendants serving them, only a dozen or so Sage cultivators.

Yet because of these dozen people and the collision of their auras, the hall seemed dull, oppressive, and unbearably crowded.

Above, Netherfire Stream Lord and Shang Sihai looked at one another. The latter nodded and lightly tapped the table.

Everyone's gazes gathered, and he opened his mouth with a beaming smile:

"After a hundred years of chaotic war, now that weapons are being laid down, this is a happy matter for people like us who seek truth and pursue Dao."

"But outside, the Demon Alliance invades, while inside, friction continues without end. This is still not a peaceful age."

"Therefore, there is today's matter: we gather privately to build relations, resolve misunderstandings, exchange the Great Dao, and trade resources."

At this point, he paused, his expression becoming slightly solemn.

"The Northern Sea is not small, but neither is it large. I imagine none of you wish to find enemies everywhere whenever you go out in the future, and have to be cautious in everything you do?"

Even if the people present did not quite agree inwardly, they still cooperated and nodded.

Seeing this, Shang Sihai smiled faintly.

Once there was a shared understanding, matters became easier.

"Then I will be the first to cast out a brick to draw jade!"

"I have a good friend named Lord Yingyuan, whose Daoist title is Baleful Dragonzi. Under him, he originally had a sea region where he spread his Daoist tradition, but because of demon-beast invasion, his sect was destroyed. He is currently staying temporarily at a branch of my Four Seas Merchant Alliance. In order to rebuild his sect, he requires a large quantity of spirit stones."

"Thus, he has taken out a batch of fourth-tier spirit grasses and entrusted me to trade them."

As he spoke, he flipped his wrist, revealing a palm-sized gray cloth bag.

When everyone had heard "fourth-tier spirit grasses" earlier, they had already become attentive; now, seeing this gray cloth bag, they no longer had any doubts.

"That is a heavy treasure of the Four Seas Merchant Alliance. It is called the Sumeru Bag. It contains space within and is best at transporting large shipments of goods. Even living things such as demon beasts and spirit herbs can be contained for a short time. It is truly a famous spatial magic treasure of the Northern Sea."

Sage Langya's voice sounded by Luo Chen's ear.

Luo Chen could not help but look at that gray cloth bag a few more times, interest showing in his eyes.

He wondered how this cloth bag compared with his Penglai Octagonal Pavilion.

But thinking about it, a function so miscellaneous won in comprehensiveness but lost in specialization. In terms of storing spirit herbs, it was probably far inferior to the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion!

The next moment, Luo Chen's attention was diverted.

"Fourth-tier Hong Spirit Grass. It contains hongyuan qi, said to be a kind of energy closest to the origin qi of heaven and earth. Its adaptability is extremely strong, and most high-level pills in the world can use it as medicine. In rumor, even Ascendant great powers use this grass for alchemy."

"There are one thousand two hundred stalks in total. They are Lord Yingyuan's accumulation over many years, and they have not yet been harvested, so their medicinal strength is abundant!"

"One stalk for one hundred thousand lower-grade spirit stones. Fellow Daoists who are interested may state your numbers."

There was no talk of auctioning them to the highest bidder.

Instead, he directly set the price at one hundred thousand lower-grade spirit stones per stalk.

This was already an absolute premium. A fourth-tier spirit grass valued at the same level as a mid-grade magic treasure could not reasonably go any higher.

For a time, everyone in the hall grew restless.

Very quickly, there were responses.

"Give me ten stalks."

"I want fifty stalks. Recently, I have been studying an incomplete pill formula and have never found a suitable substitute for the main ingredient. Hong Spirit Grass may be a decent replacement."

"Forget it. This old man does not understand alchemy, so I will not participate."

"I want one hundred stalks!"

The moment these words emerged, everyone turned their eyes to the woman in a seven-colored cloud dress.

It was Mistress Smokecloud, Du Feiyan, one of the few Stream Lords in Moluo Stream proficient in alchemy.

Her ability to stand firm within Moluo Stream was greatly related to her outstanding alchemy.

Shang Sihai watched this scene with satisfaction. The reason the first item taken out for trade was Hong Spirit Grass was mainly because he did not want the mood to go cold.

Trades between high-level cultivators were mostly barter.

He was settling with spirit stones, so most people could afford to buy some.

Only, compared to the quantity of one thousand two hundred stalks, this transaction volume of one or two hundred stalks truly seemed somewhat insufficient.

His friend was urgently short on spirit stones, and Shang Sihai himself had no time to put this batch of medicinal herbs into the merchant alliance and sell them slowly.

"I slightly overestimated their wealth."

Shang Sihai felt somewhat regretful, thinking that this batch of Hong Spirit Grass would probably have to be promoted to those few high-level alchemists of the Northern Sea.

Just after most people had stopped reacting, a voice that Shang Sihai had not expected rang out.

"Since everyone has no further interest, then give all the remaining Hong Spirit Grass to this wanderer!"

Shang Sihai froze and stared at the incomparably young white-robed man.

"Wilderness Wanderer, are those words true?"

Luo Chen smiled faintly. "With so many Fellow Daoists bearing witness, how could they be false?"

At this moment, everyone held their breath and looked at Luo Chen with uncertainty and surprise.

There were a full thousand stalks of Hong Spirit Grass remaining!

At one hundred thousand per stalk, that was over one hundred million spirit stones!

Everyone present had accumulated wealth for many years and considered themselves fairly well-off, but if any of them were asked to take out so many spirit stones in one breath, it would absolutely injure their foundations.

There were even Sage cultivators like Old Ghost Nanqi, who had not obtained territory for long and had not managed it much; they might not even be able to take out so many spirit stones at all.

Shang Sihai took a deep breath and cupped his hands toward Luo Chen. "In that case, on behalf of my friend, I thank Fellow Daoist for your generous act."

Afterward, he brought Luo Chen to a side hall and took out all one thousand stalks of Hong Spirit Grass.

Looking at those white spirit grasses on the ground, still planted in shallow soil and resembling young grain seedlings, Shang Sihai explained, "The reason this batch of Hong Spirit Grass is priced high is that the roots have not been severed. If you have a suitable cultivation method, you may even be able to induce a batch of seedlings."

Luo Chen nodded. With his understanding of spirit-plant cultivation, he could naturally see this clue.

Without another word, Luo Chen immediately took one hundred million spirit stones from his storage ring, placed them in a storage bag, and gave it to Shang Sihai.

"Wilderness Wanderer, do you have a suitable magic treasure to contain this batch of medicinal herbs? I have several spatial magic treasures here. Although their quality cannot compare to the Sumeru Bag, they can be used temporarily."

Luo Chen smiled faintly. "There is no need to trouble Fellow Daoist with that."

Shang Sihai raised his brows, but said no more and left the side hall.

After he left, Luo Chen stared at this batch of Hong Spirit Grass and revealed a brilliant smile.

He truly had never expected that he had come for soul essence, yet had obtained Hong Spirit Grass.

This spirit grass was precisely one of the optional main ingredients for a fourth-tier pill used by Sage cultivators to refine qi, among the thirty-six pill formulas of Devil Lord Heaven-Refining!

Using it as medicine, one could refine a fourth-tier pill called the Hongyuan Pill, which aided cultivation.

Originally, Luo Chen had not set his mind on this area.

With his current physique and spirit root, even without taking pills, he was completely confident that he could cultivate to late-stage Sage within two or three hundred years.

At most, he was somewhat lacking in cultivation arts.

It could be said that Luo Chen's current aptitude absolutely counted as genius!

However, if he had cultivation-type pills as supplement, then naturally that would be adding good upon good!

"Truly, it came without effort at all!"

Luo Chen took out a formation disk, laid down a concealment formation, and then used the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion to collect all of these medicinal herbs.

Having left the side hall, Shang Sihai's steps slowed.

When the formation emerged, he was slightly surprised.

Then he saw Luo Chen appear in his line of sight.

At this moment, certain suspicions in Shang Sihai's heart were thoroughly confirmed.

"Fellow Daoist, let us return together!"

...

Inside the palace, the trades between cultivators were still continuing.

However, gazes fell on Sage Langya from time to time.

There were even divine senses quietly transmitting questions to Sage Langya, asking about Luo Chen's background.

But Sage Langya's face was full of bitter smiles, and to every question he knew nothing.

That was right. Someone newly entered into the Sage realm did not fear the famous Split Heaven Stream Lord, and now took out more than one hundred million spirit stones in one breath.

You call him a rogue cultivator?

Who would believe it?

No one would believe it!

Especially when Shang Sihai returned and discovered that the other party even had a spatial magic treasure to hold medicinal herbs, he became even more convinced that this man was not a rogue cultivator, but an expert from some powerful force traveling outside under an alias.

Because of this, during the following trade segment, everyone paid special attention to Luo Chen's movements.

But this time, they were somewhat disappointed.

No matter what rare and unique treasures they took out afterward, Luo Chen remained unmoved, not the slightest bit interested.

High-level medicinal herbs, ores, the corpses, bones, and even inner cores of powerful demon beasts, and even damaged true artifacts: he had no desire to bid on any of them.

One had to know that although true artifacts were standard magic treasures for Sage cultivators, not every Sage possessed one.

When undergoing tribulation, one could borrow the power of tribulation lightning to advance a high-grade magic treasure into a true artifact.

But that required time, and required powerful strength!

Not everyone had the strength to advance their magic treasure's grade while undergoing tribulation.

Many Sage cultivators' true artifacts were slowly nurtured afterward over long years of cultivation, using the Nascent Soul's power to connect with heaven and earth.

Wilderness Wanderer was not even interested in true artifacts, so he probably already had a suitable true artifact of his own.

This further confirmed their guess that Luo Chen's background was great.

Luo Chen sat there in a perfectly composed manner, eyes watching his nose, nose watching his mouth, mouth watching his heart.

He truly could not raise much interest in these trade resources.

In the trip to Demon-Fall Land, he had harvested too many high-level resources, whether medicinal herbs or ores.

In terms of weapons, he lacked them.

But what he lacked was not damaged items like these, nor items whose attributes did not match his own.

He was only waiting for one thing now.

That was soul essence!

Sage Langya had not lied to him. Someone truly did bring out soul essence to trade.

But the quantity was extremely small and could not satisfy his needs at all.

By contrast, Old Ghost Nanqi had been buying it the whole time, which drew Luo Chen's particular attention.

Could it be that he also had a method to use soul essence?

Just as the first segment was about to end, Netherfire Stream Lord, who had not spoken for a long time, finally slowly straightened his back.

"This old man has a large quantity of soul essence for sale."

A glazed treasure bottle appeared in his hand.

At this moment, Old Ghost Nanqi stood up directly.

"Fellow Daoist, trade it to me!"

Netherfire Stream Lord glanced at him. "Do you know what I want?"

Old Ghost Nanqi chuckled. "It is nothing more than resources that can resolve the injuries on your body. I have a third-tier Fire-Devouring Gu [TERM?] that can devour source-less fire. If paired with Fellow Daoist Split Heaven's Nine Nether Immaculate Wind [TERM?], it will surely cure you like medicine meeting the illness. What do you think?"

Netherfire Stream Lord's gaze fell on Split Heaven Stream Lord.

The other party snorted softly, but did not deny it.

"It seems you truly did inquire into this. However..."

The moment the word "however" came out, Old Ghost Nanqi's expression changed instantly.

He had spent so much effort contacting Split Heaven Stream Lord precisely to trade for the large quantity of soul essence Netherfire Stream Lord had accumulated over several hundred years. Was there going to be an accident now?

Netherfire Stream Lord's gaze landed on Luo Chen. "Wilderness Wanderer, would it be convenient to have a private chat with this old man?"

Surprise appeared on Luo Chen's face.

Just now, he had still been thinking about how he would have to bleed heavily to compete with Old Ghost Nanqi for the soul essence, but unexpectedly, Netherfire Stream Lord had taken the initiative to seek him out.

Since that was the case!

"Naturally convenient!"

Netherfire Stream Lord slowly rose, holding the glazed treasure bottle, and walked outside step by step.

Wherever he passed, scorched footprints were left on the ground.

Luo Chen's gaze fell on those footprints, thoughtful.

Inside the great hall, Old Ghost Nanqi's face was gloomy. He had never expected that the matter he had planned would be interrupted by someone cutting in sideways.

Split Heaven Stream Lord watched this scene, originally unable to make sense of it, but as he pondered, a guess surfaced in his mind.

Because this guess was very close to the truth, he became even more astonished.

"Old Monster Netherfire is actually this bold, willing to believe an outsider of unclear background rather than me?"

"Moreover, how can he be so certain that Wilderness Wanderer possesses such ability?"

*(End of Chapter)*
