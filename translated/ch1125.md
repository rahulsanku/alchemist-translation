# Chapter 1125: The City of Fallen Immortals

Crackle!

The sound of burning wood in the fire pit was crisp and clear.

Luo Chen sat by the bedside, watching the woman busily washing vegetables and cooking. On the chopping board, there was even a small piece of dark red meat.

"Wait a moment for me. It'll be ready to eat soon."

"Why so lavish today?"

"I thought that having plain congee and pickled vegetables every day wouldn't be good for your health, so I bought a piece of meat from the town to nourish you." The woman spoke matter-of-factly, then hesitated for a moment before lowering her voice. "Besides, today is my *she shui* day."

*She shui*?

Luo Chen was momentarily stunned, then quickly realized—this meant it was the woman's birthday.

On such an occasion, one ought to give a gift to celebrate. But Luo Chen looked around himself and found nothing else to offer. He could only smile awkwardly.

"Sorry, I didn't prepare a gift. I can only wish you eternal youth and a long life of a hundred years."

Jing Lan smoothed her hair and smiled radiantly. "Thank you. That one sentence is enough. It's always been just me alone, but this year feels a little different."

Luo Chen opened his mouth, wanting to ask about her family, but the words died on his lips. Since she had said it that way, it was clear the past was not something pleasant to bring up.

The meal was soon ready. Luo Chen didn't eat much, only taking a light taste of the meat.

Jing Lan seemed a little nervous, explaining that she rarely cooked meat dishes and might not have prepared it well.

Luo Chen simply said he wasn't fond of meat, which eased the woman's embarrassment.

After dinner, Jing Lan went to the corner of the room to lay out bedding.

But this time, Luo Chen walked over.

The woman's face flushed slightly. "What are you doing?"

Luo Chen calmly pointed at the bed, which was simply cobbled together from wood and stone. "You sleep up there. Leave this spot to me."

"But your body…"

"It's fine."

Luo Chen's tone was calm, but his attitude was firm.

Jing Lan wanted to persuade him, but under Luo Chen's imposing demeanor—one that commanded respect without anger—she ultimately went to the bed that was rightfully hers.

That night, there was still no fire lit to accompany their sleep.

Or rather, as long as the ghost rain wasn't falling, there seemed to be no need to burn that dreamstone for light.

Jing Lan lay in bed, tossing and turning, unable to fall asleep.

From time to time, she would turn over and look at the thin man sitting cross-legged on the straw mat across from the fire pit.

In the darkness, Jing Lan finally couldn't help but ask, "Are you an immortal from the outside?"

Luo Chen opened his eyes, quietly gazing at her. After a long pause, he replied, "No."

Having failed at ascension, how could he call himself an immortal?

At least, that was what Luo Chen believed.

"Oh…"

The woman's voice carried a hint of disappointment.

But Luo Chen's next words sparked her interest in conversation.

"Do immortals often appear in the Great Cataclysm Realm?"

Jing Lan perked up and spoke, "Not often, but there are many legends like that. Especially when the Three Great Calamities occur, immortals from outside occasionally stumble into the Great Cataclysm Realm by accident. Even the City of Fallen Immortals is said to have been built by immortals from beyond."

"The City of Fallen Immortals?" In the darkness, Luo Chen savored the name.

Jing Lan replied, "I've heard that this place of ours—you can enter, but you can't leave. Even immortals, if they accidentally come in, can only be trapped here for life. That's why the legend of immortals building a city to live in arose. Half a year ago, when I saw you in the river, you were shrouded in a hazy aura. I thought you were an immortal, so I saved you."

Luo Chen's brow furrowed more tightly. He said, "I am not an immortal, but you could consider me someone who cultivates in pursuit of immortality."

"A cultivator?" Jing Lan was curious.

Luo Chen was equally curious. "Are there cultivators in this place too?"

Jing Lan hesitated before answering, "There are some who call themselves cultivators, but they have nothing to do with the ethereal, immortal-like figures of legend. For instance, the new Supervisor Yan at our tobacco workshop is said to be a cultivator."

Luo Chen blinked, silently committing the name "Supervisor Yan" to memory.

He didn't press for more. Given Jing Lan's situation, she couldn't know much. A general outline was enough.

"Sleep early!"

"Mm."

The woman responded and soon fell into a deep sleep. Clearly, her day's labor had not been easy.

Luo Chen remained seated cross-legged on the straw mat. After reviewing the events of the past, he focused his attention on himself.

The meridians in his body were still blocked, and more than half of them were ruptured!

Under such conditions, let alone fighting, even simple cultivation was extremely difficult.

Being able to get up and walk was already the limit!

The most urgent task was to repair his body and clear his meridians.

But after several attempts, Luo Chen discovered a terrifying truth.

Green Mountain Town had no spiritual qi!

This was an extremely inconceivable thing.

Even back when he was in the most barren places of the Mountains and Seas Realm, there was still faint spiritual qi available for absorption—just not enough to support realm advancement.

But here, Luo Chen could not sense even a trace of spiritual qi.

What was more, the spiritual qi that should have naturally clung to his body from years of cultivation seemed to have dissipated at some unknown time.

Luo Chen didn't know if this was a problem unique to Green Mountain Town or a common condition throughout the entire Great Cataclysm Realm.

Without spiritual qi, he couldn't cast spells. Worse, he couldn't even open his storage ring to retrieve the spirit stones and pills inside.

This wasn't just due to the lack of spiritual qi and mana—it was also because his divine soul had been sealed, preventing him from mobilizing his divine sense.

After the initial panic, he now had to settle for the next best option and seek other methods to restore his condition.

Origin Blood!

He still had pure Origin Blood within him—condensed from countless amounts of blood essence, no less valuable than the refined blood essence painstakingly cultivated by cultivators.

If he was willing to part with some, he could disperse it and then use healing spells to drive it, perhaps as a substitute for mana.

However, the same healing spell, when powered by mana versus blood essence, would likely have greatly diminished effects.

Mana was gentle, flowing and retracting as the heart willed. Blood essence was yang, fierce, and violent. For healing spells, blood essence was not as compatible as mana.

But at least, it was a path!

Luo Chen was a grandmaster of spells, having cultivated countless techniques and even created many of his own.

Now, with deliberate effort, he was gradually finding some leads.

A drop of Origin Blood dispersed, turning into violent blood essence that surged toward his limbs and bones. Yet, because of the blocked and ruptured meridians, it became stagnant between his organs.

Luo Chen let out a muffled groan, but his hands moved without pause, following the most basic *Healing Art* to guide the circulation.

This art was fundamental, requiring neither divine sense nor spiritual awareness. Even ordinary Qi Refinement cultivators could use it. It was perfectly suited for Luo Chen in his current state, with his divine soul sealed.

He mobilized strands of violent blood essence, over and over again, to heal the external wounds on his body.

Those wounds were the most gruesome and terrifying. Even Luo Chen couldn't imagine what kind of attack he had endured to damage his fleshly body—which was comparable to that of a Great Desolate being—to such an extent.

He clearly remembered that during his battle with Rosycloud, she hadn't targeted his physical body.

Had it been caused by Azure Frost casting him into the spatial turbulence?

Thinking of Azure Frost, Luo Chen's emotions were complicated.

That battle had come abruptly.

Without Azure Frost, Luo Chen had been prepared to burn his True Spirit Origin Blood, break through Rosycloud's seal, and even self-destruct his primordial spirit.

If it had come to that, he would have died, but Rosycloud would not have fared well either. Not to mention whether she would have been severely injured, at the very least, she wouldn't have been able to obtain Luo Chen's vast mana and spiritual essence.

Because of Azure Frost, Luo Chen's desperate counterattack had been thwarted.

But because of Azure Frost's banishment, he had been given a sliver of a chance at life—even if it meant ending up here.

He could sense that the spatial banishment had been deliberately done by Azure Frost, giving him a way out.

Thus, his feelings toward Azure Frost were very complicated.

"If one day I can leave this place and find Rosycloud to take my revenge, as long as you don't stand in my way, I will let bygones be bygones. But if you still oppose me…"

In the darkness, Luo Chen's half-open, half-closed eyes revealed a piercing, cold light that sent chills down one's spine.

"Mm…"

A murmuring sound rose in the silent night. The woman on the opposite bed turned over.

Luo Chen glanced at her, withdrew his thoughts, closed his eyes, and focused all his attention on repairing his physical body.

(Chapter End)
