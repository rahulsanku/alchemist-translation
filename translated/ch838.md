# Chapter 838: The Might of a Grand Cultivator, Instant Kill! (Seeking Monthly Tickets)

On this day, before the boundary stele of the two domains, Heaven Capital and Heaven's Pole, three figures stood apart.

They gazed at the battlefield ahead, where spiritual qi boiled and light and shadow flickered.

Each wore a grave expression, their bodies on full alert.

"As expected, Heaven Capital Sect has used its millennia of accumulated foundation to hold back the invading Demon Emperor. The battle still rages!"

"Should we enter the formation?"

"We haven't seen any Nether Origin Sect disciples yet. Entering the formation now would be unwise."

"Do you still remember what Heaven Capitalzi asked of us before we set out?"

The last sentence came from Luo Chen.

This remark made Jin Ling, who had been the most confident at the start, raise his eyebrows.

Luo Chen did not hesitate. He walked at the front, stepping into the Heaven Capital Domain where spiritual qi boiled.

"Since I agreed to Heaven Capitalzi, I won't stand by and watch them die. I must do my utmost."

Feng Hua took a deep breath. The peace she had regained over the past few days was now replaced by renewed ferocity.

"The enmity of my junior sister—I must avenge it!"

As she spoke, a dark wind swirled around her fingertips. With a mournful whoosh, the dark wind transformed into a taut, straight sword.

The longsword was pale green. The woman was like the wind, drifting gracefully into the domain.

Jin Ling, who was last, grinned wryly and let out a self-deprecating laugh. A golden feather appeared in his hand.

"Fine then. I want to see what kind of ghostly, sinister thing it was that once intimidated my senior brother and made him dare not act rashly!"

A dharma power shield enveloped his entire body, and Jin Ling shot forward like an arrow from a string, charging into the Heaven Capital Domain.

...

"How infuriating! If the twelve Heaven Capital God-Slaughter Banners were still here, how could we let such a monstrous fiend run rampant?"

"Junior brother, stop sighing. The Heaven Capital God-Slaughter Banners require immense power—only a grand cultivator can activate them. Even if Senior Brother Heaven Capitalzi hadn't taken them, we couldn't have used them."

"Then what do we do now? Ordinary killing formations can't do anything to that female demon."

"For now, the only plan is to contract our defenses, abandon the outer areas, and gather all the domain's spiritual qi to defend the mountain gate!"

"Senior Brother Heaven Authority is absolutely right!"

On Heaven Pillar Mountain, four Daoists sat cross-legged in a circle, each with a solemn expression.

Once the plan was decided, they did not hesitate.

They formed hand seals and began to cast spells. The spiritual veins beneath them trembled incessantly.

Throughout the entire Heaven Capital Domain, vast amounts of spiritual qi began to converge along the spiritual veins, surging toward the direction of Heaven Capital Sect's mountain gate.

The speed was so great that the earth veins shook, as if an earth dragon were rolling beneath them.

Above the ground, the wind and clouds shifted dramatically. Massive amounts of spiritual qi gathered into mist, rolling and churning like a tidal wave reversing course.

Such methods were far beyond what ordinary Sage cultivators could perform.

Only Heaven Capital Sect, with its millennia of management, could so freely mobilize the spiritual qi of an entire domain.

As vast amounts of spiritual qi were drawn away, the various formations spread throughout Heaven Capital Domain, deprived of their main energy source, began to collapse one after another.

Just as one formation was being retracted, Heaven Authorityzi, the leader of the four, suddenly let out a soft exclamation.

"There are unfamiliar Sage-level experts within the formation."

One of his junior brothers showed a shocked expression. "Could there be more than seven Demon Emperors?"

Heaven Authorityzi shook his head. "No, they are human cultivators. Don't panic, junior brothers. Let me contact them carefully!"

As he spoke, he formed hand seals, his lips moving as if he were conversing with someone across the void.

A moment later, a look of surprise and joy appeared on his face.

"They are fellow Daoists who came to support us from Lingtian Pass!"

"The one contacting me is Luo Chen of the Alchemy Sect."

"He asked us to tell them the number of enemies, their positions, and the techniques they have displayed. Also, to leave some formations to conceal their movements."

"Heaven Capital Sect is saved!"

...

Inside a remaining illusion formation, Luo Chen sat with his eyes slightly closed. A man and a woman guarded him on either side.

Suddenly!

*Shua!*

Luo Chen's eyes snapped open. His pale golden pupils fixed directly on a certain location.

"Did you make contact?" Feng Hua asked urgently.

Luo Chen nodded. "I've made contact. Fellow Daoist Heaven Authority of Heaven Capital Sect has given me a general picture of the situation. Next—"

Beside him, Jin Ling blinked, a deep look in his eyes.

Earlier, in the Dieyun Domain, Luo Chen had already demonstrated a superb understanding of formations.

Now, right before their eyes, he had reversed the principles of formations to converse across the void with a Sage of Heaven Capital Sect.

Such mastery of formations was far more than just "a little understanding."

An Sage-level cultivator with extremely strong combat power, who used his peerless alchemy skills to build a vast network of connections, and now revealed formation attainments that could rival the array masters of Heaven Capital Sect.

"He has fully come into his own!"

"And such a person is the enemy my senior brother has always wanted to deal with."

"If my senior brother succeeds, all is well. But if he fails, he will surely suffer backlash, and it will be a great calamity for my Five Elements Divine Sect!"

Thinking this, Jin Ling's lowered eyelids grew even darker.

Feng Hua saw him with his head down and a bad expression, and assumed it was because of the enemy intelligence from Heaven Capital Sect.

The great commander, Splendid Immortal, a late fourth-stage great demon emperor!

The other six unfamiliar demon emperors—three were at the mid fourth-stage, and the strength they had displayed while fighting their way toward Heaven Pillar Mountain was no less than that of ordinary mid-stage Sage cultivators.

The remaining three had not yet made a move, so their cultivation levels were unclear. However, these three had been constantly probing for formation weaknesses, leading the demon emperors forward quickly. Clearly, these three were the formation experts Luo Chen had mentioned earlier.

"Very troublesome!" Feng Hua murmured.

Luo Chen, however, appeared confident. "Heaven Capital Sect is no ordinary sect. They have managed this domain for thousands of years. They won't be broken so easily. If we coordinate well with them, we have a chance to win!"

Jin Ling raised his eyelids. "How do we coordinate?"

Luo Chen smiled confidently. "If you don't mind, how about following my command?"

Jin Ling and Feng Hua exchanged a glance, then nodded.

"Let's hear it!"

...

The great battle in the Heaven Capital Domain had been raging for three days.

From the very beginning, it had been a one-sided affair.

Seven demon emperors rampaged unimpeded, charging straight toward Heaven Capital Sect's mountain gate, centered on Heaven Pillar Mountain.

The various formations along the way had never stopped activating.

Even knowing it was like a mantis trying to stop a chariot, the cultivators of Heaven Capital Sect did their utmost to activate the formations, doing everything they could to block the seven demon emperors.

This was a matter of life and death. The four great elders had already given the order: fight to the death, never retreat!

In fact, their efforts had indeed delayed the demon emperors' advance to some extent.

Each formation was led by Adept-level cultivators, backed by the full spiritual qi of the domain. The killing power they unleashed was no less than that of ordinary Sage cultivators.

Even though the seven demon emperors had three brothers skilled in breaking formations, breaking formations still consumed dharma power.

Under such a war of attrition, the dharma power of the other four, led by Splendid Immortal, was being continuously drained.

By the fourth day, the seven demon emperors finally saw the towering Heaven Pillar Mountain.

The beautiful woman in the splendid palace dress looked at the pillar, and at the same time felt the surrounding spiritual qi beginning to converge toward Heaven Capital Sect. She understood everything.

"They intend to hold out to the death!"

Behind her, a man in white with a sinister smile sneered, "Turtles in a jar. How far can they hold out?"

Splendid Immortal smiled slightly, then looked at the three demon emperors whose appearances and clothing were extremely similar—so much so that it was impossible to tell them apart.

"Deer Longevity, can you break the mountain-protecting grand formation of Heaven Capital Sect?"

One of the three, Deer Longevity, stepped forward. He gazed into the distance at the layered light barrier, which rose and fell like waves, yet also resembled a primeval forest. A look of gravity appeared on his face.

"The formation dao practiced by us three brothers originates from the formation texts passed down by Lord Emperor Heaven. We understand the movement of celestial phenomena and the flow of earthly qi, making us most adept at countering these large-scale formations built upon mountains."

"But Heaven Capital Sect is different. As the most outstanding formation grand sect among the human cultivation world, they have long since developed their own school."

"Especially this mountain-protecting grand formation that guards their sect. It not only combines celestial phenomena and earthly qi but also incorporates the principles of yin-yang circulation and the five elements and eight trigrams."

"Breaking it will not be easy!"

Another demon emperor, a burly man in yellow, spoke in a simple, honest tone. "If it were easy, why would they need us to join forces? Just tell us—can you break it or not?"

Deer Longevity's face showed difficulty.

It was Splendid Immortal who spoke up to relieve his embarrassment.

"You don't need to break it completely. Just find the weaknesses as you did before, and leave the rest to me."

With this promise, Deer Longevity visibly relaxed.

"With such a large formation, it must be interlocking, allowing no room for error. Next, you attack the formation, and we three brothers will have the opportunity to find the formation nodes. Once we remove them one by one, the chain reaction will inevitably expose the formation's flaws!"

"Good!"

Splendid Immortal praised him, then swept her gaze over the other three demon emperors.

"From now on, all of you will follow the commands of the three Deer brothers!"

The other three demon emperors snorted but did not object.

Then, under the command of the three Deer brothers, the three mid fourth-stage demon emperors began to attack one by one, testing the formation's reactions.

Splendid Immortal did not act for the time being.

...

*Boom!*

A tremendous roar was heard, followed by the heavy sound of running footsteps rumbling closer.

Within the light barrier, the man in white with a sinister expression was forced to retreat, pursued by a giant blazing with flames.

Deer Longevity's voice suddenly rang out.

"This is a fire formation. There must be wood and earth formations on the left and right."

"Yellow Wind Guest, attack the upper part and draw out the water formation!"

"When water and fire clash, internal friction will arise within the grand formation!"

The burly man in yellow did not hesitate. He shot into the sky and unleashed a rolling yellow wind.

As if stimulated, a blue water serpent immediately emerged from the grand formation, shaking its head and tail.

The burly man in yellow did not linger. He also retreated backward.

The blue water serpent chased after him, and together with the flame giant, one above and one below, their powerful auras influenced each other, clearly weakening them.

At that moment, a golden giant sword, a thousand zhang long, shot into the sky.

Deer Longevity's voice rang out again.

"Thousand Cords, use softness to overcome hardness. Counter it!"

Among the three demon emperors, one in black who had not yet acted blinked his eyes and charged directly at the golden giant sword.

At the same time, the man in white and Yellow Wind Guest both turned back, entangling the flame giant and the blue water serpent.

The shimmering light barrier of the grand formation began to tremble violently.

A diagram of the eight trigrams slowly emerged above Heaven Pillar Mountain.

Seeing this, Deer Longevity took a deep breath.

"Great Commander, it's up to you now. Once we break this core formation diagram, the remaining formations will be nothing to fear."

Splendid Immortal nodded. With a light step, she entered the formation.

As she entered, a vast and surging demonic qi erupted, and the eight trigrams diagram began to tremble wildly.

Inside Heaven Pillar Mountain.

The four Sage cultivators were drenched in sweat, their expressions terrified.

Heaven Capital Sect had three supreme formations!

They were the All-Encompassing Star Dipper Grand Formation, which sealed the nine heavens; the Heaven Capital Twelve God-Slaughter Formation, which was supreme in killing; and the Five Elements Eight Trigrams Formation, which was extremely versatile in both offense and defense.

Each formation had extremely strict requirements for deployment.

The All-Encompassing Star Dipper Grand Formation required more than ten Sage cultivators to act simultaneously.

The Heaven Capital Twelve God-Slaughter Formation required twelve true-artifact-level Heaven Capital God-Slaughter Banners as its core, plus a grand cultivator to preside over it.

As for the Five Elements Eight Trigrams Formation, which was said to be extremely versatile, it naturally required consideration of all aspects during the deployment stage.

At this moment, within Heaven Capital Sect's mountain gate, over ten thousand low-level cultivators were simultaneously seated at various formation nodes, fully controlling the grand formation's operation.

Among them, the number of Adept-level cultivators had already exceeded a hundred!

It was by relying on these low-level cultivators that they could transform the five elements and stubbornly withstand the attacks of three mid fourth-stage demon emperors.

With the support of the domain's earthly vein spiritual qi, they could hold out for a short time.

According to their plan, the four of them, focusing on two tasks at once, could unfold the eight trigrams diagram and suppress the remaining four demon emperors.

But none of them expected that Splendid Immortal alone, by entering the formation, could withstand everything.

Moreover, the boundless and unmatched demonic power within the eight trigrams formation showed that this woman still had strength to spare and was not being suppressed.

"Is the gap between a grand cultivator and us really that great?"

A Heaven Capital Sect elder spoke with a bitter expression.

Heaven Authorityzi's expression was grave. "When Ascendant-level great powers cannot act lightly, a late-stage Sage grand cultivator is the pinnacle of this world. How can they be underestimated?"

The power of a grand cultivator had nearly exhausted the limits of human capability.

Only a grand cultivator of the same level could be their match.

No amount of praise in this world could fully describe the capabilities of a grand cultivator.

A simple example: how does one describe the strength of a Sage cultivator?

Just give them the title "comparable to a grand cultivator," and that says it all!

The Blood Wanderer of the Northern Sea was like this. Yin Wanderer before he stepped into the late Sage stage was like this. Nine Spirits Lord of the Eastern Wilderness was also like this.

And this was only "comparable." If they truly faced a late-stage Sage grand cultivator, they could often only barely protect themselves.

The Blood Wanderer barely escaped with his life under the pursuit of a Primordial Demon Sect grand cultivator. Nine Spirits Lord was defeated by the Demon Emperor Azure Frost. This shows how formidable a true grand cultivator is.

Now that Splendid Immortal was unleashing her full power, even with the formation's amplification—which could multiply their dharma power several times or even dozens of times—the four Sages of Heaven Capital Sect could not help but feel a sense of hopelessness.

As the strongest and highest-ranking among the four, Heaven Authorityzi knew that he could not let this feeling continue to spread.

Steeling himself, he shouted in a low voice, "Open the Purple Mansion wide! Let the Sages connect with heaven and earth! Push the eight trigrams formation to its limit! Use all our strength to hold Splendid Immortal back, and create opportunities for the Alchemy Sect to break them one by one!"

"Will they really come?" someone asked hesitantly.

"If they don't, we die anyway!" Heaven Authorityzi glared at him.

The three junior brothers naturally understood this reasoning and no longer hesitated.

The barriers of their Purple Mansions suddenly parted, as if opening the gates of a cave dwelling.

One by one, their Sages, as tender and white as infants, emerged from the crowns of their heads, maintaining the same posture as their physical bodies, forming seals and chanting incantations.

In an instant, dharma power surged forth!

Not only that, but the spiritual qi of heaven and earth that had been summoned was also forcibly controlled and integrated into the eight trigrams diagram.

That eight trigrams diagram immediately began to spin rapidly, evolving a grayish mist that completely enveloped Splendid Immortal's graceful figure.

After doing all this, Heaven Authorityzi murmured to himself, "Alchemy Sect, do not disappoint us!"

...

"Great Commander!"

Almost the moment Splendid Immortal was engulfed by the eight trigrams formation diagram, the six demon emperors outside sensed it.

Yellow Wind Guest even intended to abandon his opponent and go to her aid.

But Deer Longevity's voice came quickly before he could withdraw: "This is the perfect opportunity to break the formation. Do not let go."

While dealing with the blue water serpent's attack, Yellow Wind Guest angrily retorted, "You three brothers talk without feeling the pain. If something happens to Commander Splendid Immortal, even if we conquer the Eastern Wilderness later, you'll only get a corner of it."

Deer Longevity laughed. "No need to worry. With Splendid Immortal's strength, that formation can only trap her, not kill her. And it won't trap her for long. Besides, it's time for us three brothers to act!"

With that, he looked at his two brothers.

After a tacit exchange of glances, a treasure light appeared in each of their hands.

They were a compass, a bronze mirror, and a banner.

"Let's enter the formation!"

Deer Longevity said this and was about to take the lead.

But the next moment, a premonition struck him, and he felt a chill of terror.

He suddenly turned his head. In an empty space, three streaks of light suddenly erupted.

"Not good! There are enemies!"

The three streaks of light shot into the sky, their auras completely unconcealed. The fluctuations of their vast dharma power were all at the mid-Sage level.

Where the streaks of light passed, the void exploded and the earth trembled.

The three Deer brothers instinctively deployed their defenses.

However, the three streaks of light were not aimed at them at all. They charged directly into the formation, rushing toward the man in white.

The man in white sensed all this. Panic appeared on his sinister face.

But the flame giant before him showed no intention of letting him go. Instead, it erupted with flames recklessly, trying to entangle him tightly.

It was at this moment.

*Cry!*

A phoenix cry resounded through the sky.

A huge fire phoenix spread its brilliant wings and came roaring in.

*Hu...*

It was as if someone had exhaled, blowing out a mouthful of clear wind.

Then a gale suddenly appeared, attaching itself to the fire phoenix, making it fly faster and its flames burn brighter.

The wind fueled the fire, and the fire borrowed the wind's power.

That fire phoenix, which had already been a hundred zhang long, instantly swelled to a thousand zhang. Even its eyes seemed to become more lively.

With a single dive, it pounced on the man in white with lightning speed.

*Boom!*

In the great explosion, a pained roar was heard.

The fire phoenix and the flame giant, one in front and one behind, exploded simultaneously, engulfing the white-clothed demon emperor in endless destruction.

"Ahhh!!!"

The pained roar of a wild beast spread across the land after the explosion subsided.

Then, streaks of white light broke free from the mountain-protecting grand formation, flying recklessly in all directions.

"Northwest! The weakest one!"

A cold, cruel voice suddenly rang out.

Then, a golden feather stabbed out sharply.

*Pfft!*

It was like a bubble being popped. Then, terrifying dharma power erupted uncontrollably.

Jin Ling stood within it, clad in a golden robe. He let the aftermath of the shattered demon emperor's Sage crash against his dharma power shield, making his robe rustle.

He looked behind him.

A man and a woman revealed themselves. It was Luo Chen and Feng Hua.

Seeking monthly tickets, begging on my knees!
(Chapter End)
