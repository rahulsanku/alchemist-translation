# Chapter 910: Ancestor Goes to Death, Eternally Sinking into the Nether Abyss

*Thump!*

A heavy sound rang out.

In response, a massive figure collapsed beyond the overlapping valleys.

The strange bird that had been shrouded in countless gales shrieked sharply, then reluctantly dispersed, leaving behind only the true body of Nether Abyss Ancestor.

Nether Abyss Ancestor struggled with difficulty, attempting to stand.

But whether it was his physical body or his divine soul, both had now weakened to the extreme.

Especially that once heaven-defying powerful primordial spirit—after excessively channeling the origin qi of heaven and earth, it had instead suffered backlash.

At this moment, an incalculable amount of origin qi of heaven and earth surged toward him, continuously battering his vast primordial spirit.

The gale howled in its death throes!

Yet it also dissipated unceasingly!

Nether Abyss Ancestor had no recourse left. He could only watch helplessly as his primordial spirit swelled and expanded, step by step heading toward the outcome of being assimilated by heaven and earth, returning to nothingness.

But compared to the sound of footsteps approaching from afar, death itself no longer seemed so terrifying.

"Of the three great powers of the Nether Abyss Sect, one fled without a fight, another skulked in the shadows, and only you still have some backbone."

Nether Abyss Ancestor smiled bitterly. He was the master of the Nether Abyss Sect; of course he would fight to the death!

Within his line of sight.

A figure in red robes walked slowly forward. The surrounding gales only served to manifest her unmatched might.

Likewise, an incalculable amount of origin qi of heaven and earth battered that figure, but upon reaching a few *zhang* before her, it could advance no further.

Throughout the Mountains and Seas Realm, rumors circulated that Ascendant-level great powers could not act lightly.

This was actually not a baseless rumor!

Such beings had grasped the true meaning of laws and could wield the power of the world's origin as a means of combat, nearly indistinguishable from the Heavenly Dao itself.

But an individual's power was insignificant.

If they overexerted themselves, they would suffer the world's backlash.

Precisely because this world that had nurtured countless living beings was itself not that vast—how could it allow anyone to squander everything that belonged to it without restraint?

If an Ascendant great power was still in good condition, using themselves as leverage and relying on the fulcrum of law's true meaning to pry open even greater amounts of origin qi of heaven and earth, they could briefly suppress that backlash.

But clearly, after the great battle, Nether Abyss Ancestor could no longer suppress the backlash.

His leverage was insufficient, so naturally the other end would pry him up.

At this moment, Nether Abyss Ancestor stared incredulously at the red-robed woman approaching slowly, enduring the impact of origin qi of heaven and earth against his primordial spirit as he said hoarsely, "The power you've displayed is no different from a late-stage Ascendant. Why are you completely unaffected by the qi backlash and assimilation by heaven and earth?"

Rosycloud stopped in her tracks, a full *li* away from Nether Abyss Ancestor lying on the ground.

The corners of her lips curled into a beautiful arc as she chuckled lightly. "Naturally, because I possess the strength to suppress even a late-stage Ascendant who would counterattack."

Nether Abyss Ancestor opened his mouth, instinctively wanting to say that was impossible.

This realm could indeed produce late-stage Ascendant cultivators—in fact, that was the optimal realm for ascending.

But the every move of such an existence was accompanied by a sea of origin qi of heaven and earth. How could they act as recklessly as Rosycloud?

And she said she had the strength to suppress a late-stage Ascendant?

That would mean... Void Refinement?

"No!"

Nether Abyss Ancestor crawled with difficulty to a bare stone, leaning his body against it, shaking his head in despair.

"I don't believe it!"

"Though your strength is formidable, the realm you currently display is truly only equivalent to ours."

This time, Rosycloud did not answer his words.

Instead, she cast her beautiful eyes toward one of the valleys among the overlapping mountain ranges.

"Little one, come out!"

A figure walked out slowly.

He looked calmly at Rosycloud, then his attention fell upon the disheveled Nether Abyss Ancestor, whose aura was frantically expanding.

Though covered in injuries after the great battle, his aura was swelling wildly—such a violation of common sense, in the eyes of us cultivators, naturally revealed what was happening.

"Feng Jiang, why did you come out?" Nether Abyss Ancestor asked with a tone of frustration.

Feng Jiang's expression carried deep sorrow as he respectfully bowed.

"The holy land is devastated, the sect is lost—how could a disciple stand idly by?"

Nether Abyss Ancestor said despairingly, "But you are no match for her."

"Junior Brother Leidaozi was no match for her either, yet didn't he draw his sword without hesitation?" Feng Jiang countered, then slowly straightened up, facing the red-robed woman not far away under unimaginable pressure. "At the very least, I won't let her leave this place unscathed!"

"Such big talk!"

Rosycloud sneered, then pointed a finger at him through the air.

*Pfft!*

A bloody hole suddenly appeared in the center of Feng Jiang's brow.

His sea of consciousness shattered, his divine soul scattered.

A Valley Immortal had fallen in an instant.

Yet at this moment, Feng Jiang's face instead showed an expression of relief.

As if he had been trapped here for far too long and had finally obtained release.

Rosycloud frowned, sensing something was wrong.

Her divine sense swept over, and she suddenly looked down at her own hand.

A wisp of gray qi, as Feng Jiang collapsed with a crash, coiled around Rosycloud.

"This is... tribulation qi?"

Suddenly!

Rosycloud's willow-leaf brows rose sharply as she blurted out, "Valley Immortal Feng Jiang? The tribulation gu—one of the three great fifth-rank immortal gu of the Southern Frontier—is in your hands!"

Unfortunately, Feng Jiang could no longer answer her.

And what answered her was the earth heaving, as massive ravines spread in all directions centered on Rosycloud.

Boundless earthfire erupted violently, and boiling magma shot into the sky.

The Earth Calamity had appeared!

Nether Abyss Ancestor watched sorrowfully as his disciple, who had departed before him, fell. This had once been the one he favored most among the younger generation, far surpassing Leidaozi, Ye Lingtian, Tan Sheng, and others.

Yet now he had fallen like this.

"If I had taken back the Alchemy Saint's blessed land earlier, with its protection, perhaps you would not have suffered the daily torment of that tribulation gu, and might even have had hope of advancing to Ascendant."

"Never mind. Now you have finally obtained release."

"Then let this old man use his last strength to aid you, to make your departure more glorious."

As he murmured to himself, his primordial spirit, vast enough to span a thousand *zhang*, began to burn fiercely.

Carrying the dense gray qi emanating from Feng Jiang's corpse, it shot straight into the nine heavens.

The Earth Calamity had already appeared—where was the Heavenly Tribulation?

It was as if heaven and earth were answering him.

The next moment!

*Boom!*

A thunderclap suddenly exploded across the sky.

It was as if Leidaozi, who had once controlled a pool of lightning, had been resurrected, but the momentum was a thousand times greater than his!

The arrogance on Rosycloud's face finally diminished by three parts.

She looked up at the sky. Dark, oppressive calamity clouds were gathering at an unimaginable speed.

Within them, white light flickered, lightning coiled like serpents and dragons—a sight like the destruction of the world itself.

"What a fine junior!"

"Before dying, he actually attempted to break through to the Ascendant realm, thereby triggering the tribulation gu, with the dying Nether Abyss Ancestor fanning the flames."

"I killed you, and instead drew the wrath of heaven and earth upon myself."

"But do you think this mere Heavenly Tribulation and Earth Calamity can keep me here?"

Rosycloud sneered, transforming into a soaring pillar of fire that shot straight toward the sky.

She would shatter this Heavenly Tribulation before it could fully take shape!

This action seemed to trigger a chain reaction.

From the earth, giant hands of yellow earth reached for her, torrents of magma surged from all directions to attack her, violent spiritual qi erupted from the earth's veins, and an unimaginable gravitational force pressed down upon her.

In the nine heavens above, the dark calamity clouds gathered faster and faster, containing countless thunderbolts and lightning strikes.

This was a Heavenly Tribulation beyond the imagination of any Sage cultivator!

Even more, vague forms of strange beasts began to appear within.

There were white electric serpents, purple thunder dragons, and even black elk.

Rosycloud's expression grew increasingly grave. The power of this Heavenly Tribulation seemed to be growing stronger in response to her own strength.

If she were at her peak, she would naturally not fear it.

But being within the Mountains and Seas Realm, no matter how strong she was, how could she overpower this world itself?

She even felt that the Heavenly Dao of this world harbored the intention to erase her—Valley Immortal Feng Jiang's action was merely going along with the current.

"This world cannot trap me!"

"Let alone mere Heavenly Tribulation and Earth Calamity!"

Rosycloud shouted sharply, waving her hand to strike down.

The myriad strange forms of the Earth Calamity were suppressed in an instant.

Boundless golden flames erupted from her body, sweeping toward the thunderclouds.

She was confident she could suppress this Earth Calamity and destroy this Heavenly Tribulation.

However!

"Fifty thousand *li* of Nether Abyss land, over three thousand years of holy land history—all reduced to ashes because of you alone. How could I let you walk away?"

"Demon Phoenix, sink into oblivion here with us!"

"Eternally sinking into the Nether Abyss!"

A figure appeared beneath the endless calamity clouds, looking down at the soaring fire phoenix.

He murmured softly, ancient and obscure secret incantations spilling from his mouth—words that did not sound like any common language of this realm.

But Rosycloud understood them instantly, and her expression changed dramatically.

"You dare!"

Heaven Abyss Ancestor, who had been missing for so long, paid no heed. He only continuously expended his dharma power, forming hand seals one after another.

Under his ministrations, it seemed as if many things were being torn open.

"Open the Jade-Shattering Seal, enter the Nether Abyss."

The Nether Abyss, which had surrounded the holy land for thousands of years, surged violently, rising continuously from its banks.

"Heaven and man in chaos, the mind's eye opens!"

A pair of aged eyes, identical to Heaven Abyss Ancestor's, appeared within the calamity clouds behind him. At this moment, he replaced the Heavenly Will with his own heart, fixing his gaze firmly on Rosycloud.

"No successors remain, the water barrier opens!"

The fifty-thousand-*li* Nether Abyss land had long been lost to the flames of war. Under the sweeping devastation of vast origin qi, survivors were few and far between. The great Nether Abyss Sect was virtually indistinguishable from being completely destroyed. To say there were no successors was entirely accurate.

Since that was the case, why leave such a precious land for others?

The invisible barriers that had sealed the vast Nether Abyss suddenly opened at this moment. The surging Nether Abyss waters rose continuously, the water level already countless *zhang* higher than the Nether Abyss continent.

By this point, Heaven Abyss Ancestor was already quite strained, nearly unable to continue.

Rosycloud could not simply watch him perform this suicidal forbidden art. She took a deep breath and emitted a piercing sound toward Heaven Abyss Ancestor.

"Caw!"

The phoenix cry resounded through the nine heavens, spreading across the entire universe.

Heaven Abyss Ancestor's open mouth suddenly froze, and his eyes went blank as he fell into the Nether Abyss waters.

Rosycloud breathed a sigh of relief. She had interrupted him after all.

With her knowledge, she could naturally tell that this was some kind of forbidden art to unseal something.

And what seal was there in the Nether Abyss Sect to be undone?

Only the Nether Abyss itself—more ancient than the Nether Abyss Sect, flowing through the Mountains and Seas Realm for millions of years!

Within it were not only the souls of demon beasts and desolate beasts that the Nether Abyss Sect had suppressed during ancient battles, but also powerful evil spirits and ghostly beings that had naturally been born from the dense yin qi since time immemorial.

Once the seal was opened, the consequences would be incalculable.

Yet the next moment, the murmuring began again.

Rosycloud's expression froze. She abruptly turned her head to see, within the surging Nether Abyss waters, countless avatars of Heaven Abyss Ancestor rising and falling.

The three great ancestors of the Nether Abyss Sect each possessed great divine abilities.

One excelled at controlling the wind, with few equals in direct combat.

One commanded rain and dew, unmatched in trapping enemies and killing.

And Heaven Abyss Ancestor was most proficient in the dao of avatars. In terms of survival ability, he was the foremost of the Nether Abyss Sect.

It could be said that if he had wanted to leave today, even with Rosycloud in pursuit, it would likely have been extremely difficult to completely annihilate him.

But if he did not want to leave, many things became different.

This time, all of Heaven Abyss Ancestor's avatars, scattered across various locations, simultaneously activated the incantation. Their tone was urgent, as if afraid they would not last until that moment.

"Order is lost above and below, the soul lock opens!"

"The great momentum collapses, the demon chains open!"

"Yin and Yang part ways, the ghost gate opens! Open! Open! Open!"

When the final three consecutive cries of "Open!" burst forth, Rosycloud felt as if something in the world had been snapped.

Within the boundless Nether Abyss waters, countless howls suddenly arose.

One after another, powerful ghosts seemed to return from the Netherworld, rejoicing without end.

Standing atop the waves, they unfurled their massive ghostly bodies.

They scrambled to devour Heaven Abyss Ancestor's avatars, who could no longer resist. Their already powerful auras became even more terrifying.

Among them, those comparable to Ascendant great powers numbered eight or nine.

Those below the Ascendant level were simply uncountable!

Facing the vicious ghosts devouring his body, Heaven Abyss Ancestor's expression was calm as he faced death without fear.

This was the price of opening the Nether Abyss and releasing the evil ghosts that the Eastern Wilderness's number one forbidden land had suppressed for millions of years.

But he had no regrets!

It was his avatar that had invited the wolf into the house, leading the great enemy into the sect's heartland. Naturally, he should pay the price.

Likewise, Rosycloud had destroyed his sect, so she too should pay the price.

And if he could keep her here, with the foundation the Nether Abyss Sect had left outside, there would still be a chance to rebuild the Ascendant holy land.

"Junior Brother Heize should have hidden inside the Alchemy Saint's blessed land, able to escape this calamity."

"Grandnephew Chongming is traveling abroad. Having taken the Heavenly Secret Pill, he has already comprehended the true meaning of laws and has great hope of advancing to Ascendant in the future."

"And there's Shangyou Immortal City, and Dongyang Sect, and..."

His subsequent thoughts could no longer remain clear, because too many ghosts were devouring his avatars one by one.

Some ghosts at the Sage level, after devouring his avatars, were able to briefly advance to the Ascendant realm.

Though only for an instant, it should be enough to cause trouble for Rosycloud.

"Keep her here!"

This was Heaven Abyss Ancestor's final thought.

When his consciousness completely dissipated, all the evil ghosts turned their gazes in unison toward that incomparably beautiful, voluptuous red-robed figure that made one's mouth water.

"Ooooh..."

Amid ghostly wails and wolf howls, billions of ghosts, carrying the endless Nether Abyss waters, drowned the Nether Abyss holy land—and also drowned Rosycloud.

...

Within the surging Nether Abyss ocean.

Birds could not approach, living beings did not exist.

Suddenly!

*Chi!*

A wisp of flame ignited within the Nether Abyss waters.

An evil ghost pounced, but the flame turned around and devoured it instead.

Then, with an even more frenzied and determined attitude, it spread in all directions.

In just a few short breaths, the hundreds of thousands of *li* of Nether Abyss were completely ignited by golden flames, boiling without cease.

The resentful souls and fierce ghosts within howled without end, emitting agonized sounds.

A clear note pierced through the layers of Nether Abyss waters, resounding across the sky.

"Heaven Abyss, you cannot trap me!"

*Splash!*

Waves rose and fell, golden flames parted.

A figure sat atop a treasure diagram painted with countless beasts, charging straight out of the Nether Abyss.

Her face was pale, her eyes filled with ruthlessness.

*Crack!*

The moment she appeared, a bolt of lightning struck down from the sky, and electric serpents and thunder dragons roared toward her.

"Annoying thing!"

Rosycloud showed anger for the first time, but this time she no longer attempted to destroy the Heavenly Tribulation.

Because during the time she had been trapped in the Nether Abyss, the Heavenly Tribulation had already brewed to its peak.

This was a Heavenly Tribulation capable of erasing any powerful existence in the Mountains and Seas Realm.

To oppose it was to oppose all of heaven and earth!

Rosycloud glanced at a certain place within the Nether Abyss, then rose without any lingering attachment.

Thunderlight struck her body, and a shadow of a beast on the treasure diagram beneath her vanished.

If she could not destroy the tribulation, nor endure it, then she would substitute for it!

Fortunately, she had obtained the spirit treasure Myriad Beast Diagram from Moon Wanderer, prepared for emergencies. It contained countless powerful beast souls, enough to bear many strikes of the Heavenly Tribulation for her.

Such a Heavenly Tribulation would never last long; it would eventually dissipate.

Rosycloud departed, leaving behind only the surging Nether Abyss where no living being could enter, and the blazing Nirvana Sacred Flame.

Even the calamity clouds that had gathered endless thunder followed her departure.

Black and gold became the only two colors of this place.

And the direction Rosycloud had looked at in her final glance before leaving was precisely the Alchemy Saint's blessed land!

Within it, Heize Ancestor struggled to his feet, trembling as he walked toward Luo Chen, who was protected by a ring of golden fire.

"Nirvana Sacred Flame!"

"It's you!"

"You invited the wolf into the house!"
