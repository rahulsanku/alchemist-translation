# Chapter 1012: The Mastermind Behind the Scenes, Sudden Change in the Blessed Land

Eastern Wilderness!

Mountains and Seas Calendar, year 3667!

Although beast tides still erupted from time to time in various places, the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world had ultimately settled down.

That calamity which had lasted two hundred years seemed to have gradually faded into the distance. Yet the sight of nine out of ten houses standing empty, of devastation everywhere, still reminded Eastern Wilderness cultivators that the demon race within the million great mountains could still charge out at any moment.

Of course, such matters were for the big shots to consider.

For low-level cultivators, this era was undoubtedly the best of times!

The once lofty great sects had either been destroyed or had perished. Even those that survived did not possess half the control they had at their peak.

The vast thirty-six domains had more than half left vacant. The ubiquitous battlefield ruins and fallen cultivator sites everywhere contained opportunities for achieving immortality and attaining the Dao.

Thus, while the major sects licked their wounds and recuperated, some smaller factions began to occupy mountains and establish themselves as kings, rising rapidly.

Even the weakest rogue cultivators of the past seized this opportunity, desperately climbing upward.

In the old days, a rogue cultivator achieving Initiation was considered a fine tale.

But over the past two hundred years, Adept-level rogue cultivators had appeared time and again.

There were even rumors that several powerful Adept rogue cultivators had already touched the threshold of forming a Nascent Soul, needing only that final step.

And the opportunity to cross that final step had arrived this year!

Within a misty swamp in the Firework Ancient Domain.

Treasure light flickered, magical power crisscrossed, and figures filled the area.

A fierce battle was underway.

After a long while, as a patch of swamp exploded and chunks of flesh rained down, a man with skin as pale as frost walked out from within.

Hanging from his body were seven storage pouches!

All seven storage pouches were stained with blood—the legacy left by several Adept clan cultivators who had tried to ambush and kill him.

In the end, it had all benefited him instead.

With a wave of his hand, a six-winged centipede crawled out from the mud, listlessly burrowing into his sleeve.

After doing all this, the man couldn't help but breathe a sigh of relief.

He glanced around, a fierce glint flashing in his eyes, but ultimately shook his head and suppressed the killing intent in his heart.

His aura rapidly concealed itself as he fled into the distance.

Not long after he left, sounds of breaking air came from nearby.

Soon, several Adept cultivators bearing family emblems appeared in the swamp.

Looking at the gruesome battlefield, each of their faces was dark and gloomy.

"That Bo Hanjun escaped!"

"Seven Adept masters surrounded him, including Old Ghost Li, a peak late-stage Adept expert. How could it fail?"

"As expected of the rogue cultivator Bo Hanjun, rumored to have mastered the strengths of many schools! The traces of battle here show that he clearly set up a formation in advance, buried a large number of talismans, and had that six-winged centipede suspected of having Demon Emperor bloodline. Only then could he single-handedly fight seven Adepts!"

"Forget it. We cannot seize the upper-sect inheritance on him. Moreover, this man is extremely clear about gratitude and grudges. He will likely come seeking revenge. Let us each return to our own homes!"

After some discussion, the group reached a consensus and planned to leave.

After all, Bo Hanjun's reputation for "repaying both the smallest kindness and the slightest grievance with overflowing abundance" was too well-known.

If he happened to target their families for revenge, it would be troublesome.

But just as they were about to depart, an old man who had been lingering nearby grew increasingly grim-faced.

Those beside him thought he was angry about his own elder being killed and tried to console him.

However, he revealed a piece of news instead.

"The Shouyang invitation was on my clan elder. Now it has vanished without a trace, taken by that Bo Hanjun!"

Everyone was stunned at first, but quickly reacted.

"Shouyang invitation?"

"You mean the invitation to the auction at Shouyang Mountain in Lingtian Pass?"

"That's the top auction in the Eastern Wilderness since the end of the human-demon war! Presided over by Sage Taiyue of Mount Tai Sect. It's said that only Nascent Soul upper sects and some powerful Adept clans can obtain invitations."

"Your Zhang family, counting the guest elders you've hired, only has three Adept cultivators. How could you possibly qualify for an invitation?"

Facing the chorus of doubts, the Zhang family patriarch clenched his fist. "My Zhang family has some connection with an executive elder of Mount Tai Sect, so we obtained one invitation. We originally planned to use this opportunity to go to the Shouyang auction and gather some cultivation resources."

A woman beside him sneered, "I'm afraid it's not just simple cultivation resources, is it?"

"Madam Liu, what do you mean by that?"

"According to what this humble wife knows, a large number of Nascent Soul formation resources will appear at this auction. Moreover, it's said there will be Nascent Soul Pills that require no threshold and ignore cultivation technique restrictions. Fellow Daoist Zhang's target is probably those Nascent Soul Pills!"

The Zhang family patriarch took a deep breath. "Yes, you are correct."

His gaze flickered as he swept over the crowd. "Bo Hanjun's strength far exceeds our imagination. This old man alone pursuing him will likely not succeed. All of you are masters of your own domains. If you continue to pursue him with me, we can retrieve the invitation. Then we can pool our resources to attend the Shouyang auction. If we are fortunate enough to obtain a Nascent Soul Pill, whoever among us achieves Sage status can join forces to establish an upper sect, dominate one domain, and allow our seven families to cultivate together."

Faced with this proposal, everyone began to stir with interest.

Deep in the swamp, a frost-white man sitting cross-legged looked at one of the storage pouches.

"Is the invitation inside?"

...

Nine Spirit Domain.

A woman dressed in gorgeous robes, with jade ornaments tinkling, urgently ascended Nine Yuan Peak holding an invitation.

The master within the peak had already anticipated her arrival, and the mountain-protecting formation slowly opened a path.

Soon, the woman had arrived at the cave dwelling.

Nine Spirit Lord opened his eyes. "Junior Sister Changxi, what is the urgency?"

Changxi handed over the object in her hand.

"An invitation?" Nine Spirit Lord glanced at it and muttered, "And it's sent out by that Deng Taiyue."

Changxi nodded and explained, "Sage Taiyue will hold an auction at Lingtian Pass, which has been in preparation for several years. This auction involves all the remaining Nascent Soul upper sects in the Eastern Wilderness, as well as over a hundred powerful Adept-level forces."

Hearing this, Nine Spirit Lord raised an eyebrow.

"Quite a grand scale. What gives him the confidence to hold an auction of this magnitude?"

"Senior Brother will understand once you see the invitation!"

Nine Spirit Lord opened the invitation and scanned it quickly.

The various resources listed at the beginning, though precious and rare, were nothing special to him. But when he reached the end, Nine Spirit Lord's gaze stopped.

"Ten Nascent Soul Pills!"

"Where did he get so many Nascent Soul Pills? And how could he bear to sell them to others? What is his purpose in using this as the core to hold this auction?"

Many questions, but the answer was already on the tip of Nine Spirit Lord's tongue.

"Alchemy Sect's Luo Chen!"

Changxi was startled. "Senior Brother means that this auction held by Sage Taiyue is actually being orchestrated from behind the scenes by Sage Luo?"

Nine Spirit Lord nodded. "That should be the case. As far as I know, the only person in the Eastern Wilderness who can still refine Nascent Soul Pills is Luo Chen. He once refined this type of pill for the Divine Talisman Sect. Moreover, Luo Chen has a good relationship with Deng Taiyue, who has been running around collecting medicinal ingredients for him in recent years. Having the boldness to take out ten Nascent Soul Pills at once—in the vast Eastern Wilderness, only Luo Chen could do that."

Changxi asked curiously, "Then what is Sage Luo's purpose?"

"Yes, what is his purpose?" Nine Spirit Lord's eyes narrowed slightly.

After a long while, he put away the invitation.

"I'll make the trip myself! Both to buy a Nascent Soul Pill for the sect and to give Luo Chen face."

In this day and age, the various Nascent Soul upper sects were not "wealthy."

This "wealth" did not refer to spirit stone resources, but to the top-tier combat power of Sage-level cultivators.

In the two hundred years of the human-demon war, too many Sages had fallen.

Even though they had used their own secret techniques to rapidly cultivate some in the years after the war, it was still a drop in the bucket.

The Nascent Soul Pills Luo Chen refined were orthodox breakthrough-type pills, not restricted by any sect's cultivation techniques.

If they could nurture one more Sage for their own sect, that would be an excellent thing.

...

At this moment, within Mount Tai Sect.

Deng Taiyue looked at the disciple who had come to report and asked, "Seven years have passed. Have all the invitations been delivered?"

The steady and prudent disciple nodded respectfully. "They have all been sent out. Even the farthest Nine Spirit Sect and East Sun Sect have received them."

Hearing this answer, visible relief appeared on Deng Taiyue's face.

The two tasks Luo Chen had entrusted him with were one, helping him collect Nine Firmament Qi, and two, selling the Nascent Soul Pills.

And in his view, these two matters could be accomplished together through a single super auction.

The disciple below couldn't help but ask, "Master, why sell so many Nascent Soul Pills? Our Mount Tai Sect currently only has two Sages, you and Elder Gong. Why not keep them for our own use?"

To this, Deng Taiyue did not explain. He simply waved his hand.

The disciple withdrew with a heart full of confusion, leaving only Deng Taiyue alone in the cave dwelling.

He let out a faint sigh. He had already guessed Luo Chen's intentions.

Once this batch of Nascent Soul Pills was sold, they would inevitably be consumed by the most talented disciples of each sect.

And these disciples had basically all experienced the two hundred years of the human-demon war, making them the cream of the crop.

One could say that their probability of breaking through to the Sage stage was already extremely high. With the assistance of Luo Chen's Nascent Soul Pills, their breakthrough was practically guaranteed.

In this way, the weakened Eastern Wilderness cultivation world would receive a tremendous boost.

Moreover, no one was a fool. They would inevitably guess that this batch of Nascent Soul Pills came from Luo Chen.

"Selling precious Nascent Soul Pills selflessly to everyone, spreading benevolence and righteousness, promoting the name of the Alchemy Sect. He is making full preparations for that war that might break out!"

It was conceivable that when Luo Chen raised his arm and called out, there would be some who responded to him.

But Deng Taiyue still wasn't optimistic.

After all, the opponent was the former overlord of the Mountains and Seas Realm, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, which had an Ascendant-level great power sitting in charge!

Under such a threat, those truly willing to follow him would likely be few and far between.

Just as Deng Taiyue was sighing with emotion, the disciple who had just withdrawn suddenly returned.

Deng Taiyue looked displeased at the newcomer.

"Is there something else?"

The disciple's face was filled with shock. "Master, a message has come from the Western Desert, related to your good friend, Sage Luo Chen."

"Oh?"

Deng Taiyue became interested.

"Tell me."

...

The outside world was in turmoil, but the blessed land remained peaceful.

Since Luo Chen's primordial spirit had left his body and suppressed the alchemy mountain, the Luo Tian Sect had passed three more years without incident.

In these three years, under Li Yingzhang's diligent governance, talented people had emerged in abundance within the sect.

The number of Adept cultivators alone had broken through to twenty.

Not only that, but under his leadership, they had also established a foothold in the outside world.

That was the abandoned city of Shangyou!

Haoranzi had not returned but had stayed in the abandoned city of Shangyou.

He felt that his trip to Green Parasol Mountain had severely damaged his foundation, and with his advanced age, he had no hope of reaching Ascendant in this lifetime. Thus, he had taken the idea of leaving behind a legacy.

After communicating with him, Li Yingzhang suggested that he follow the example of Lingfengzi and establish a separate Haoran lineage within the Luo Tian Sect.

However, before that, he needed to cooperate with the Luo Tian Sect on some matters.

Take over the Shangyou City, which possessed a fourth-order spirit vein. Using Haoranzi's reputation as a Sage, establish a Sage-level force to absorb disciples with spiritual roots from the outside world.

After careful review, they would be secretly transported into the Alchemy Saint Blessed Land.

In this way, the problem of the Luo Tian Sect's sparse disciple numbers would be temporarily solved.

In truth, the number of Luo Tian Sect members had not been small over the past few decades. After reproduction, there were over a thousand people.

And the vast majority were elites.

Twenty Adepts, over a hundred Initiate cultivators, and several hundred Qi Refinement cultivators.

Moreover, there were seven Sages sitting in charge!

Such strength was enough to rival the most powerful Nascent Soul upper sects at any stage in Eastern Wilderness history.

Of course, Li Yingzhang did not only manage the sect. He also devoted part of his energy to teaching his only formal disciple, Luo Lingxi.

Under his guidance, Luo Lingxi had rebuilt his foundation through Qi Refinement.

In just three years, he had already reached the middle stage of Qi Refinement.

This progress had surpassed the vast majority of people, showing that he had not betrayed Luo Chen's trust.

But Li Yingzhang could also see the dissatisfaction in Luo Lingxi's eyes.

Therefore, he would occasionally take the time to instruct him.

"You are very anxious?"

In the Luo Tian Grand Hall, Li Yingzhang questioned his disciple.

Luo Lingxi, having had his thoughts seen through, was not embarrassed. He said seriously, "I am already forty-two years old. There are only eighteen years left until the most suitable age of sixty for Initiation. How can I not be anxious?"

Li Yingzhang countered, "Do you know how many years it took the Supreme Elder to achieve Initiation back then?"

"Father?" Luo Lingxi hesitated. "Father started his Dao at sixteen and achieved Initiation at thirty-two. That should have taken sixteen years."

Li Yingzhang stroked his beard and smiled. "Yes, the Supreme Elder's aptitude is not as good as yours, and his cultivation resources are even less than yours. He could achieve Initiation in sixteen years. You still have eighteen years. Why be anxious?"

"But..." Luo Lingxi wanted to say, that was his father! But the unwillingness in his heart prevented him from saying those words.

Li Yingzhang's expression turned serious. "Haste makes waste. If you want to achieve Initiation, laying a solid foundation is the most important thing. That is also why the Supreme Elder entrusted you to me. Back then, to break through to the Initiation stage, I spent a full nine years fluctuating at the Great Perfection of Qi Refinement. This shows the importance of a foundation."

"And your father—don't look at how his advancement speed is rare in the world. His foundation is equally rare. At least, I have never seen anyone whose magical power could surpass his at the same realm."

"If you only want to quickly achieve Initiation, form a Core, or even reach the Sage stage, none of that is actually difficult. After all, you are the only son of the Supreme Elder, and you have the full support of the Luo Tian Sect. A massive tilt of resources, a sea of pills supplied—everything is within easy reach."

"But such a realm would ultimately be just a hollow shell, unable to withstand a single blow."

"Is that what you want?"

This time, Luo Lingxi was speechless.

He wanted to pursue his father, to surpass his father. But if it was merely chasing after realms, it would be meaningless.

The achievements his father had made were considered incredible by anyone.

Combat power unmatched at the same level, alchemy skills of miraculous proficiency, mastery of techniques from a hundred schools—all of it rested on that indomitable will.

If he was so impatient, even if he achieved Initiation and formed a Core, what would it amount to?

He would still be just an ordinary cultivator among the masses.

After a long while.

Luo Lingxi respectfully bowed to Li Yingzhang.

"Lingxi thanks Master for the wake-up call. From now on, I will tread carefully and treat the cultivation of every realm with seriousness."

Li Yingzhang revealed a gratified smile.

This should be exactly what the Supreme Elder wanted to see!

Luo Lingxi was different from ordinary cultivators. He carried a Devil Heart Gu within him. Even though Luo Chen had sealed it, it was always a hidden danger.

Therefore, Luo Lingxi needed to temper his mind, carefully polish his foundation, and not pursue quick methods.

Otherwise, when his realm was higher, it would be hard to avoid the thought of unsealing it and taking the demonic path of rapid advancement.

When it came to tempering the mind, there were only a few people in the entire Luo Tian Sect most suitable to teach Luo Lingxi.

Luo Chen, Wang Yuan, and himself—who had cultivated the *Ninefold Return to Origin*, stubbornly approaching the Initiation stage nine times only to re-refine his spiritual power.

That was why the Supreme Elder had entrusted his son to him.

"One cannot simply cultivate all the time. Tomorrow, after you finish your morning lessons, you will accompany me on an inspection of the sect."

"Disciple obeys!"

Li Yingzhang nodded and motioned for Luo Lingxi to withdraw.

But at that moment, a look of shock appeared on his face, and he rushed straight out of the Luo Tian Grand Hall.

Luo Lingxi was stunned for a moment, but quickly sensed the unusual commotion and hurriedly followed.

Once outside the hall, the two of them looked up at the sky.

They saw a vast tornado descending from the void, connecting heaven and earth, a magnificent sight.

Within the tornado, dark waters surged, golden flames burned, and an extremely destructive power loomed faintly. The entire Alchemy Saint Blessed Land began to tremble precariously under this tornado.

Such a horrifying scene inspired fear in all who saw it.

Li Yingzhang followed the direction where the tornado was descending and blurted out instinctively, "It's the location of the Alchemy Mountain!"

Luo Lingxi's heart tightened. "Father!"
