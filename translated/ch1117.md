# Chapter 1117: Four Heart-Unity Fruits, Yama Rising Again

A dim sky, a desolate land.

No longer the once picturesque scenery, the strange and magnificent beauty—only the ravaged aftermath of defilement remained.

A black ape, the height of an ordinary person, watched this scene with an expression neither sad nor joyful, but a sigh escaped its mouth.

"The Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven won't hold out much longer."

This black ape was a puppet controlled by Luo Chen's divine sense, its strength merely at the Sage stage.

The current Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, after enduring the self-detonation shockwaves of the Netherwater Spring Eye and the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard, could no longer bear Luo Chen's true body entering.

The vast dharma power contained within him, no matter how he concealed it, would inevitably burst and shatter this small world the moment he stepped inside.

Therefore, Luo Chen could only choose to use his divine sense to control a puppet and enter the grotto-heaven.

As soon as the black ape puppet entered, it determined its direction and headed towards a remote area.

About half a day later, it arrived at its destination.

It was precisely the valley that the Gu family had built in the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, specifically used for cultivating spiritual herbs.

However, unlike the lush greenery and fragrant spiritual herbs of the past, the valley was now desolate, with only one thing remaining.

A small tree!

The tree was not large, about a zhang in height, and its trunk was only as thick as a bowl's mouth.

But its branches were gnarled, thick and powerful, giving a sense of indestructibility.

Especially the veins on the leaves, which were like human blood vessels, crystal clear and shimmering with an eerie color.

The black ape puppet carefully examined this small tree, occasionally stepping on the ground beneath it.

The Demon-Devil Heart-Linking Tree, obtained over three hundred years ago, was taken from the Wood Heaven Plain within the Demon-Fall Land.

After acquiring it, it had never bloomed or borne fruit, like a chicken rib—useless yet hard to discard.

But research on this tree had never stopped.

It wasn't until Sang Jinghe failed in his breakthrough and sat in meditation before his passing that he left behind his lifelong research findings, pointing out a set of practical methods.

There were no ancient demons in this realm, and ancient demon beasts were also hard to kill. Using traditional demon hearts for cultivation was extremely difficult. Therefore, Sang Jinghe researched using desolate beast hearts for cultivation.

Paired with various secret arts, perhaps one day the dream of bearing fruit could be realized.

"It is indeed feasible, but Jinghe just couldn't live to see that day."

The black ape puppet stopped under the tree, looking up. Vaguely, several branches had sprouted flower buds.

Counting carefully, there were four in total.

They perfectly matched the four fifth-rank desolate beasts he had slain during his ten-year journey through the barbaric lands.

Cabinet Fox, Chaotic Void Stone Monkey, Nine Ultimacy Sparrow, Green Pearl Phoenix.

After these four desolate beasts died, most of their materials were bestowed by Luo Chen to Gu Caiyi and the four guardian spirit beasts. The flesh and blood vitality was supplied to the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame, and their most important hearts were all buried beneath the Demon-Devil Heart-Linking Tree.

Now, the four hearts were about to bear four Heart-Unity Fruits!

Once the fruits ripened, Luo Chen would take and refine them, perhaps breaking through the limits of his physical body and achieving a fifth-rank Archaic True Body!

With the time flow difference in the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, that day should not be far off.

The black ape puppet carefully inspected everything, then took out a large amount of high-grade spiritual soil and specially formulated ripening medicinal liquid, meticulously tending to this small tree.

...

"Is this the Luo Tian Sect?"

Outside Shangyou City, Qi Ying looked at the Luo Tian Sect's current mountain gate. Though his face remained expressionless, there was inevitably a hint of disappointment in his heart.

In his mind, with the strength of Senior Luo Chen, his sect's mountain gate would surely have the grandeur of a majestic sacred land.

But looking at it now, it was indeed somewhat prosperous, but merely slightly better than a Sage-level superior sect.

It was far from being called a continental sacred land!

Beside him, Gu Caiyi was full of pride.

"Yes, this is the Luo Tian Sect's temporary mountain gate! My husband founded the Luo Tian Sect less than five hundred years ago. From a mere Qi Refinement-level force back then, bit by bit, he developed and expanded it until now, gradually taking on the aura of a sacred land. Such an achievement is unprecedented in the Mountains and Seas Realm!"

Hearing this, Qi Ying's eyes couldn't help but show a flicker of surprise.

He was a solitary person, or rather, had a very focused temperament.

Apart from what interested him, he basically never paid attention to external matters.

This was also why Master Xuanyan had no disciples his entire life but made an exception in his later years to take Qi Ying in.

He had never imagined that the Luo Tian Sect, five hundred years ago, was still just a Qi Refinement-level force.

Thinking of it this way, looking at the mountain gate's atmosphere again, he felt there was something uniquely different about it.

Full of vigor, all phenomena unfolding.

Every disciple he saw wore the same heartfelt pride as Gu Caiyi.

The so-called sect cohesion had reached its peak. The only thing lacking was the sedimentation of time, allowing this cohesive power to be passed down forever.

"Fellow Daoist Qi Ying, my husband intends to recruit you and has specially established the position of Pill Court Guest Elder. What do you think?" Gu Caiyi warmly invited.

Qi Ying hesitated. "I am merely passionate about the Dao of alchemy and am not interested in power or status. Moreover, I hate trouble in life and often go out to search for medicinal herbs. I'm afraid this Guest Elder position..."

Gu Caiyi waved her hand magnanimously. "You need not worry. As our sect's first Sage-level Guest Elder, you won't have to trouble yourself with anything. Just focus on what you want to do. On the contrary, if you have any needs, you can dispatch sect disciples to handle them for you. If you need any resources, the Luo Tian Sect will supply them."

Saying this, Gu Caiyi winked.

"Fellow Daoist Qi, although there are many loose cultivators in the current cultivation world, you should have some understanding of how difficult life is for them, right?"

Qi Ying opened his mouth, a helpless bitter smile appearing on his face.

In the past, with Master Xuanyan's care and the Nether Origin Sect disciples running errands for him, he hadn't felt it deeply.

But the hardships and troubles he had encountered in recent years were truly beyond words, even causing his cultivation realm to be delayed.

If the Luo Tian Sect's Guest Elder position was truly that relaxed, then he could consider it.

Gu Caiyi suddenly said, "I think you might not know yet. The Pill Court of our sect is not without talent. It is presided over by my husband's two direct disciples, who have inherited his alchemy skills. You might be able to discuss and exchange ideas with them."

Upon hearing this, Qi Ying's eyes lit up.

The halo surrounding Luo Chen was not only composed of his brilliant battle achievements.

Before that, the title "Alchemy Saint" was the honor that made Luo Chen famous throughout the entire Eastern Wilderness.

There were even rumors that Luo Chen's current alchemy skills were probably no less than those of the former Nether Origin Sect's Alchemy Saint, Chu Yan!

As his direct disciples, their alchemy skills were certainly not ordinary.

"If that's the case, then I am somewhat tempted."

Having said this, Qi Ying suddenly became curious.

"By the way, where is Senior Luo? Since returning from the Western Desert, I haven't seen him. I was hoping to ask him for some guidance on alchemy matters."

Gu Caiyi hesitated at this question.

But in the end, she said frankly, "My husband has been handling a major matter recently. I believe you will be able to see him soon."

Qi Ying was quite curious. For a mighty Ascendant cultivator to call something a major matter, he wondered how important it could be.

...

The Nether Abyss, dark currents lurking.

A towering mountain was deeply embedded in the abyss's bottom, never seeing the light of day.

Four figures, one in front and three behind, slowly climbed along the mountain's base.

"Master, regarding the restrictions on Yama Mountain, I have broken through all those at the mountain's base over the years. But the three palaces on the mountainside and the mountain peak, I am at my wit's end. Occasionally, I send some little ghosts to explore inside, but most never return. It's truly troublesome."

Luo Chen, walking in front, didn't turn his head. "The mountainside mostly consists of cultivation caves left behind by the former Nether Origin Sect's Sages. With your formation attainments, naturally, their cave restrictions would be troublesome. But it doesn't matter. These restrictions won't affect my next actions."

Arriving at a certain cave on the mountainside, the white-robed Daoist casually waved his hand.

The restrictions inside instantly crumbled.

In an instant, a large amount of spiritual energy, accumulated for hundreds of years, surged out.

Seeing this move, Little Bai cheered, "As expected of Master!"

And the middle-aged man in plain black robes couldn't help but feel a myriad of emotions.

Everyone in the world knew Luo Chen as the Alchemy Saint, but they didn't know that his attainments in formations also reached a master's level of understanding.

Indeed, in this world, incompetent cultivators could do nothing, while capable ones could do everything.

As for the other old man, covered in wrinkles and full of a dying aura, he didn't seem to care much.

The four continued their journey and finally stopped at the square on the peak of Yama Mountain.

Before them stood three majestic palaces.

"What's truly important are the restrictions of these three palaces!"

The Heavenly Nether Palace, the Heavenly Abyss Palace, and the Heavenly Water Palace.

They corresponded to the three ancestors of the former Nether Origin Sect: Imperial Nether, Heavenly Abyss, and Black Marsh.

Luo Chen turned around, looked at the old man, and said in a gentle tone, "Isn't that right, Heavenly Capital Master?"

"What Senior says is true. If we want the Nether Abyss to sink and Yama to rise again, we must first break the restrictions of the three palaces and take control of the entire Yama Mountain."

The old man was none other than the supreme elder of the Eastern Wilderness's great formation sect, Heavenly Capital Sect—Heavenly Capital Master!

He was already advanced in age, and having been severely injured during the human-demon war back then, even with life-extending methods barely keeping him alive until now, he had no strength left to break through to the Ascendant realm.

After receiving the Initiation Pill gifted by Luo Chen, he only handed it over to a promising junior brother to take.

As for himself, at the invitation of Luo Chen, with his lifespan nearly exhausted, he came to Yama Mountain to help Luo Chen break the formation restrictions.

Heavenly Capital Master carefully observed the surroundings and suddenly exclaimed in surprise, "Has the restriction of the Heavenly Nether Palace been broken already?"

"As expected of you!" Luo Chen praised, then said, "Because the Heavenly Nether Palace condensed the Netherwater Spring Eye, its restriction power was the weakest. It was forcibly broken by a group of Nether Origin Ghost Generals two hundred years ago."

Heavenly Capital Master stroked his beard. "That makes things easier. The restrictions of the three palaces are interconnected. With one palace broken, the power of the remaining two will be reduced by a third. Originally, I estimated it would take at least ten years, but now it seems that in just three to five years, Yama Mountain can see the light of day again."

"Excellent!"

Luo Chen smiled, then turned to look at the other middle-aged man in plain black robes.

"Deng Taiyue, when the restrictions of the three palaces are completely broken, it will inevitably have external effects. At that time, I will trouble you to use your methods to stabilize Yama Mountain."

Deng Taiyue replied without hesitation, "This is my duty, Senior. Please rest assured!"

An outsider might wonder why Deng Taiyue, a supreme elder of Mount Tai Sect, would say something like "this is my duty."

But Luo Chen understood perfectly.

He had promised back then that if Deng Taiyue could help him succeed and comprehend the true meaning of a law, then after the matter was accomplished, he would help him find a fifth-rank spiritual vein to break through to the Ascendant realm.

And this Yama Mountain was the foundation of Luo Chen's promise.

As long as the Nether Abyss sank and Yama rose again, the Luo Tian Sect would control a fifth-rank spiritual vein!

Deng Taiyue breaking through to the Ascendant realm would be a mere side benefit.

Everything was ready. The work of breaking the formations was about to begin.

But before that, Luo Chen unexpectedly entered the Heavenly Nether Palace.

"What is he doing?" Heavenly Capital Master was puzzled.

The three waited quietly outside the palace.

After a long time, the Heavenly Nether Palace trembled slightly.

Seeing this, Heavenly Capital Master narrowed his eyes.

A formation?

"Did he set up a formation to attract water vapor?"

Deng Taiyue beside him first nodded, then looked around in surprise.

"Not just water vapor. It seems a large amount of water origin power is spreading from within the Heavenly Nether Palace. Is he attracting similar origin power from the Nether Abyss?"

The two exchanged glances, vaguely guessing.

Luo Chen was using the power of Yama Mountain, combined with his own comprehension of water origin, to forcibly condense a Netherwater Spring Eye!

Was this something human power could achieve?

Inside the Heavenly Nether Palace.

Luo Chen was fully focused, staring at the pool of black water in the hall.

The so-called Netherwater Spring Eye, in his view, was just a place similar to an earth-qi spiritual cave.

Such existences were not something human power could create; they often required the power of heaven and earth, slowly nurtured over time.

But it had been two hundred years since the last Netherwater Spring Eye was condensed.

Having been vacant for so long, it was time for a new one to form.

Luo Chen had experience controlling the Netherwater Spring Eye, and he himself had comprehended water origin. What he needed to do now was to use the former formation of the Heavenly Nether Palace on Yama Mountain to attract that mysterious power to this place.

He had to ensure that the newly condensed Netherwater Spring Eye would not be born elsewhere in the Nether Abyss, but right here in the Heavenly Nether Palace.

Only then could the Luo Tian Sect truly control this ancient, eternal abyss!

When everything was ready, Luo Chen exited the Heavenly Nether Palace and looked at the three.

"Let's begin!"

Next, each of the three had their tasks.

Little Bai left first, descending Yama Mountain and heading towards the Luo Tian Sect.

Her task was simple, yet the most tedious: the entire Luo Tian Sect, on the former grounds of the Nether Origin Sect, would set up the corresponding formations.

This matter could not be done by her alone; it required Sect Master Luo Lingxi to lead the entire sect in carrying it out.

Deng Taiyue remained in place, his dharma power slowly spreading, the power containing earth origin gradually enveloping the entire Yama Mountain.

And Heavenly Capital Master rushed towards the Heavenly Abyss Palace.

His task was the same as Luo Chen's: to break the restrictions of the remaining two palaces!

Standing before the Heavenly Water Palace, Luo Chen's expression rarely showed a hint of melancholy.

This was not only one of the three supreme symbols of power and status in the Nether Origin Sect, but also the cave dwelling of the Black Marsh Ancestor, who had died under his sword.

He had once submitted to him, then personally killed him, and now he was about to break everything and inherit the other's legacy.

The impermanence of worldly affairs—how could it not evoke sighs?

"I wonder what that old fellow left behind in his cave?"

Luo Chen chuckled softly and began to study the defensive formation of the entire palace, preparing to break it.
