# Chapter 1038: Past Events, Reversing the Kill of a Golden Core

*Hum!*

A strange sound suddenly rang out.

Then came a blur of five colors, rising and swirling in dense clouds.

That effeminate, handsome young man seemed to transform into a bright moon, reflected in the beautiful woman's heart, with boundless illusions rising one after another.

In an instant, Seventh Aunt was deeply trapped in the illusion, utterly powerless to resist!

This was Zhou Fuyou's first time using the illusion technique Mirror Flower Water Moon. Although he felt he had mastered it completely, he was also afraid of accidents, so he immediately began inquiring about past events.

"Fourteen years ago, what exactly was that tonic?"

Seventh Aunt, dazed and confused, answered involuntarily: "It was called Spirit-Strengthening Decoction, but in reality, a medicinal herb called Ghost Spur Seedling was added. After repeated consumption, the user would only feel strong in body and abundant in spirit, but the Ghost Spur Seedling would gradually take effect, damaging the user's spiritual foundation and affecting future cultivation."

Ghost Spur Seedling!

The moment he heard the name of this medicinal herb, Zhou Fuyou's expression changed.

He had once learned about this herb from a Daoist scripture. It was simply not something normal people could consume.

Descriptions of Ghost Spur Seedling's uses often referred to refining pills that could briefly stimulate a person's potential, greatly invigorating their spirit. When facing a life-or-death crisis or an enemy too powerful to confront, taking a pill refined from Ghost Spur Seedling could multiply one's explosive strength.

This kind of medicinal herb, normal cultivators wouldn't even take it, let alone Zhou Fuyou, who was only six years old at the time!

Zhou Fuyou was furious, his tone icy cold: "Why?"

Seventh Aunt did not answer, because the question lacked specific direction.

Zhou Fuyou took a deep breath, suppressing the anger in his heart, and slowly asked: "Our two families have always been on good terms. Not to mention that your son and I had a decent relationship, but even the bond of life-and-death between my father and Seventh Uncle shouldn't have led you to harm me like this. Why did you do it?"

Seventh Aunt's gaze was hazy, and she murmured: "Because you were too outstanding. At such a young age, you displayed extraordinary intelligence. Once you could cultivate in the future, you would definitely achieve great things."

"Just because of that?" Zhou Fuyou asked incredulously.

Seventh Aunt continued: "Also because your father was quite capable and valued by the clan leader. The marriage alliance was also placed at the forefront back then. Your mother was from the main branch of the Green Parasol Forest Lin clan!"

Zhou Fuyou didn't believe it. "No matter how capable my father was, our two families were on good terms. The more powerful he became, the more the clan benefited. Could you have been jealous of him because of that?"

Seventh Aunt murmured: "There was jealousy, but it wasn't enough for us to make a move against him. It was a request from my maternal family's side."

"Make a move?" Zhou Fuyou was puzzled, then seemed to realize something, and his expression completely changed.

Seventh Aunt nodded dazedly: "Yes, during the sea-patrol mission, your parents didn't die naturally. They were lured by Hai Ye into a death trap, leaving no remains behind."

*Chi chi chi...*

Threads of spiritual power shot out from Zhou Fuyou's body, striking the surrounding swamp and splashing up countless clumps of mud.

His face was filled with killing intent.

He had never imagined that even his parents' deaths had such hidden secrets behind them.

At this moment, he could barely restrain himself from killing the woman before him, whose face was like a peach blossom but whose heart was as venomous as a scorpion.

But he forcibly suppressed it, though the tone of his voice grew even colder.

"The Yin family, why did they target my parents like this?"

"Because Zhou Yunshen's time is running out."

"The Ancestor?!"

Zhou Fuyou blurted out, his mind wavering under the successive shocks.

Even the high-level illusion technique Mirror Flower Water Moon he was casting showed signs of instability.

Seventh Aunt's face showed a struggle, but under the suppression of the illusion, she was forced to answer her nephew's questions one by one.

"The world knows that Zhou Yunshen doesn't have much time left and could pass away at any moment. But the Zhou clan occupies the best fourth-rank spirit land within ten thousand li, namely White Egret Marsh. Once Zhou Yunshen passes away and the Zhou clan loses its Sage, they will certainly be unable to hold this place. Therefore, the two allied forces, the Green Parasol Forest Lin clan and my Cold River Valley Yin clan, have been secretly stirring all along."

"Your father was too capable. He would likely have succeeded to the position of clan leader or elder in the future. At that time, because of your mother's connections, the Green Parasol Forest Lin clan would have been the ones to benefit."

"Therefore, my Yin clan could not allow your father to exist. Drugging you was just a convenient matter."

"Not only that, but we also secretly provided Hai Ye with a large amount of resources, helping him forcibly form his core and occupy a high position in the Zhou clan."

"Once the Zhou clan's Ancestor passes away, Hai Ye will cooperate with my Cold River Valley Yin clan, working from within and without, seizing the nest and occupying the cultivation treasure land of White Egret Marsh."

So, it was all for the sake of profit?

Zhou Fuyou's face was filled with sorrow. His parents' murder and his sister's accidental injury were nothing more than the Cold River Valley Yin clan's coveting of the White Egret Marsh that the Zhou clan occupied.

Presumably, dealing with him was just a convenient way to undermine his father's prestige back then.

Suppressing his anger, he asked a somewhat furious question.

"Seventh Uncle is a member of the Zhou clan! How could he betray his own family?"

Seventh Aunt said very naturally: "He was not valued within the clan. Moreover, once the Ancestor was gone, the Zhou clan would also fall. Under these circumstances, my Yin clan extended an olive branch, helped him form his core, assisted him in seizing power, and allowed our son Zhou Yuqi to audit classes at the Southern Heaven Dao Palace. Even if the Zhou clan were to disappear in the future, he would still have a place. Hai Ye's choice was the wisest one."

Zhou Fuyou still refused to accept it: "Even if the Ancestor passes away, the Zhou clan still has Grand Elder Zhou Cangyi to attempt the Sage stage, still has five or six Golden Core superiors, and dozens of Initiate true cultivators. The Cold River Valley Yin clan cannot swallow the vast White Egret Marsh!"

"The Zhou clan is not a true Daoist sect aristocratic family. On the Southern Heaven Continent, there are many humble families and aristocratic clans passed down from Daoist sects. Once the great tree falls, the monkeys scatter. At that time, many families will swarm in, and the Zhou clan won't be able to hold out. And my Cold River Valley Yin clan only needs to cooperate with Hai Ye to obtain the fattest piece of the pie."

Having heard this, Zhou Fuyou had no heart to ask further.

He had only one last question.

"Does Zhou Yuqi know about all this?"

His childhood companion, his only "friend"—did he know about these things?

Seventh Aunt's face showed a gentle expression. "Yuqi knows nothing."

*Drip!*

A drop of water fell into the calm puddle in the swamp.

Ripples spread out, and the mirror-like surface, like a bright moon, shattered in an instant.

Seventh Aunt blinked blankly, looking at the opposite side.

What met her eyes was a whistling water arrow.

*Pfft!*

A blood-red plum blossom bloomed on the woman's forehead.

She fell into the filthy swamp.

Suddenly!

A faint light flew up from her corpse, and an angry voice came from within.

"Xixue!"

So Seventh Aunt's name was Yin Xixue?

Zhou Fuyou sneered, his spiritual sense touching that faint light.

"Seventh Uncle, Fuyou awaits you!"

The faint light shattered in an instant. Zhou Fuyou's expression did not change. A blue jade ruler appeared in his hand.

He reached out and tossed it, and the jade ruler sank into the thick mud.

Then he sat down calmly.

Although his expression was calm, the killing intent in his heart was rising wildly.

The enmity of one's parents is irreconcilable.

This matter, he would settle personally!

And he believed that Zhou Haiye would not bring people, nor would he dare to bring people.

This created a space for him to personally slay his enemy.

Would that Zhou Haiye come?

*Whoosh!*

A sharp whistle broke through the air.

Crimson magical power parted the layers of poisonous miasma, revealing the figure of a middle-aged man.

Zhou Fuyou slowly stood up.

"Seventh Uncle, you came after all."

Zhou Haiye's gaze fell on the corpse on the ground, his face ashen. "You little bastard, how dare you?"

Zhou Fuyou let out a light laugh. "How dare I not? You dared to make a move against my parents, to poison me when I was only six years old. Why wouldn't I dare?"

Zhou Haiye's expression changed drastically. "How many secrets did she reveal before she died?"

"All of them!" Zhou Fuyou pointed a finger.

*Bang!*

The beautiful woman's corpse on the ground instantly exploded into a shower of blood.

Such a cruel and tragic scene enraged Zhou Haiye.

"You're courting death!"

"Do you dare to kill me?" Zhou Fuyou sneered. "I am someone the Ancestor favors, a genius designated by the Southern Heaven Dao Palace. Do you dare to make a move?"

Zhou Haiye hesitated.

Zhou Fuyou shook his head. "No, you must make a move. If you don't kill me, the clan won't forgive you. Zhou Yuqi won't have an easy time. And in the future, there will be no place for you on the Southern Heaven Continent."

"Yes, I must kill you. If I kill you, I can say it was the White Egret Demon King in the swamp. No one can blame me."

Zhou Haiye murmured as the spiritual pressure unique to the Golden Core stage crashed down.

Zhou Fuyou did not dodge or avoid. Spiritual power flowed throughout his body.

*Boom!*

In an instant, dense streams of qi burst out from within him, meeting the Golden Core spiritual pressure head-on.

Not only that, but those black streams of qi, centered on him, surged and rolled in all directions.

In a short time, they had enveloped an area of ten li.

Faced with this scene, Zhou Haiye found it incomprehensible.

But soon, he realized something.

"Initiate stage? When did you advance to Initiate?"

"When I killed your wife, you should have realized that I had at least Initiate-stage strength."

Zhou Fuyou said calmly, his figure already stepping back and disappearing into the thick black streams of qi.

Zhou Haiye felt that many things had slipped beyond his control, but his Golden Core realm still maintained his last shred of rationality.

"You're only twenty years old. Even if you've reached Initiate, it's just a quick method, a waste of your exceptional talent. Moreover, you've never fought anyone before. You have no combat experience. What makes you think you can provoke me?"

This time, Zhou Fuyou did not answer.

He concealed himself within the vast black streams of qi, pouring out a vast amount of spiritual power like a tidal wave, churning up countless portions of the White Egret Poison Swamp.

His figure stepped on the waves, drifting and unpredictable.

One spiritual incantation after another was cast from his hands.

"Using the Ancestor's self-created technique to kill you can be considered cleaning house."

Zhou Fuyou murmured to himself, pushing both hands forward!

A wall of qi pressed down towards the distant Zhou Haiye.

The other party flipped his hand and broke it.

Then, three more thick qi walls pressed in from the other three directions.

Not only that, but a startling dragon roar roared from the sky. A blue flood dragon shook its head and tail as it pounced.

Countless water arrows, from all directions, from the sky and the ground, shot in densely.

Once he made his move, Zhou Fuyou held nothing back.

And Zhou Haiye's shock grew greater and greater.

Flicking his sleeve to break the qi wall, his magic treasure flying sword cut the blue flood dragon in two. A shield floated around him, blocking the countless water arrows outside.

"How can he have such abundant spiritual power, as if it's inexhaustible?"

"How can he cast spells so quickly, with no gaps in between?"

"No!"

*Chi...*

Amidst the rolling black streams of qi, Zhou Haiye's top-grade defensive magic tool was constantly being corroded. Even the Golden Core protective barrier he had cast couldn't withstand that corrosive power.

It was miasma!

Zhou Haiye suddenly realized. These black streams of qi, fused with spiritual power, were clearly the highly toxic miasma that had existed in White Egret Marsh for countless years.

Connecting this to the gradual decrease of miasma in White Egret Marsh over the years, an unbelievable thought formed in his mind.

"Zhou Fuyou actually refined the vast miasma of White Egret Marsh—the miasma that even Golden Core cultivators don't want to touch—into his own body?"

If Zhou Fuyou knew his belated realization, he might have praised his experience.

Over the years, Zhou Fuyou had indeed secluded himself in White Egret Marsh, refining countless miasmas into the growth-type technique *Qi Sea Floating Heaven*.

This was a cultivation method left on a jade slip by the Ancestor Zhou Yunshen.

The technique *Qi Sea Floating Heaven* was based on the qi sea, containing the qi of the great thousand, achieving boundless power.

As for this so-called "qi of the great thousand," the Ancestor had listed many examples.

Miasma, baleful qi, poisonous qi, and even extremely yin and cold and other powerful forces.

As long as the qi sea itself was sufficient, as long as the cultivator could withstand it, they could continuously absorb it!

Coincidentally, Zhou Fuyou had a qi sea like a bottomless abyss, and he also discovered that his constitution was not afraid of such highly toxic miasma.

Thus, in just three years, he had already cultivated *Qi Sea Floating Heaven* to a considerable level.

Killing Seventh Uncle Zhou Haiye, he had great confidence.

It wasn't just Zhou Fuyou's strong confidence in his own strength, but also because Zhou Haiye's Golden Core realm was achieved through shortcuts. Moreover, after reaching the Golden Core stage, he had indulged in power struggles and hadn't spent much time consolidating his own realm.

"Today, I will not only avenge my grievances but also clean house!"

Zhou Fuyou gritted his teeth. The spiritual power in his body, along his wide meridians, burst forth completely.

The black streams of qi surged even more fiercely, surrounding Zhou Haiye in the center. No matter how he charged left and right, it was useless.

Moreover, a large number of spells attacked from all directions, from the sky and the ground, from all kinds of unexpected angles.

This method of fighting had nothing to do with combat experience anymore.

It was pure squandering!

Squandering spiritual power, saturation strikes with spells.

Under the constant corrosion, in less than half a quarter of an hour, the protective shield around Zhou Haiye shattered.

At that moment.

A gleam of blue light rose from the sky, transforming into over a hundred shadows, all striking at Zhou Haiye.

"The Ancestor even gave you his famous magic treasure, the Life-Severing Ruler!"

Amidst the roar came a scream.

Then, Zhou Haiye took out a talisman and directly crushed it.

*Boom!*

A blazing sun exploded within the terrifying black streams of qi.

Seizing this opportunity, Zhou Haiye staggered and shot up into the sky, trying to escape.

However, the surrounding streams of qi transformed into ferocious evil flood dragons, biting him and dragging him down again.

...

At the periphery of White Egret Marsh.

Zhou Junxiao, who was sitting in the center overseeing the overall situation, suddenly felt uneasy. He looked up into the distance.

What met his eyes was rolling black qi filling the entire sky.

Vaguely, a figure rushed out of the black curtain, only to be pulled back by evil flood dragons.

That was Zhou Haiye!

His expression changed drastically. He left a "Hold the line" to his clansmen and then charged into White Egret Marsh.

Soon, Zhou Junxiao arrived deep in the poisonous swamp.

Around him, many Zhou clan Initiate cultivators were flying over. They were all clansmen participating in this campaign to clear out the demon beasts of White Egret Marsh.

Zhou Junxiao shouted loudly: "Everyone, do not approach. These black qi are highly corrosive. Your defenses cannot withstand them."

After saying this, he charged in.

As he advanced, his magical power protective barrier came into contact with the black qi, constantly being corroded.

Zhou Junxiao's expression was solemn. He did not dare to stop and quickly flew towards the center.

He could not let Zhou Haiye die here. After all, the Zhou clan itself did not have many Golden Core cultivators and could not afford such a loss.

However, when he stepped into the center of the battlefield, all he saw was a single, broken corpse.

It was Zhou Haiye!

His face was blurred, his body tattered.

Black streams of qi were constantly drilling in and out of the wounds on the corpse, full of spiritual nature.

Seeing this, Zhou Junxiao's eyes nearly split with rage. He roared in a low voice: "Who is it? Get out here, old man!"

*Whoosh! Whoosh!*

The sound of qi currents whistling came. Zhou Junxiao looked up.

He saw a very handsome man in white robes, holding a blue jade ruler, slowly descending from the pitch-black sky.

Seeing the newcomer, Zhou Junxiao was instead stunned.

"Zhou Fuyou?"

Zhou Fuyou calmly put away the blue jade ruler. "Fuyou greets the Clan Leader."

Zhou Junxiao looked around in disbelief, finally fixing his gaze on Zhou Fuyou.

"Did you do this?"

"Yes, I killed Zhou Haiye."

"How dare you? He was a Golden Core superior of the Zhou clan, and also your Seventh Uncle!"

Zhou Fuyou shook his head. "The details are difficult to explain in a short time."

Zhou Junxiao took a deep breath, suppressing the anger that was rising again.

"No, I need you to give me an explanation!"

The surrounding black streams of qi, like fledglings returning to the nest, continuously returned to Zhou Fuyou's body.

That lean, not-so-strong body was like a whirlpool.

Zhou Junxiao watched as those strange streams of qi, which could threaten even him, were being absorbed like this, his eyelids twitching wildly.

This junior was only twenty years old!

How had he cultivated such a terrifying killing move?

Could this be what an unprecedented genius was like?

Zhou Fuyou curled the corner of his mouth. "Clan Leader, you'd better think about how to explain Zhou Haiye's death to the others. As for the real reason, I will wait for you in the Coral Hall, and you will receive a satisfactory answer."

With that, Zhou Fuyou silently sank down and disappeared into the White Egret Poison Swamp.

Zhou Junxiao couldn't believe that the person who had just acted like that was Zhou Fuyou.

So willful, so proud and indifferent.

He had killed an elder of the clan, yet before him, the Clan Leader, he was so arrogant!

Anger burned in his heart, but thinking of Zhou Fuyou's talent and the Ancestor's protection, he opened his mouth and finally lowered his head helplessly.

Around him, streaks of escaping light, as the black qi dissipated, began to approach this side.

Upon seeing the broken corpse on the ground, they all exclaimed in shock.

Zhou Junxiao sighed and gave a reason that was hard to accept but had to be accepted.

"Sixth Elder Zhou Haiye died at the hands of a hidden demon king in White Egret Marsh."

(End of this chapter)
