# Chapter 1008: Reforging the Mystic Dust Armor, Refining the Initiation Pill

On Alchemy Mountain, a scene of bustling activity unfolded.

This mountain had once been touched by a wisp of Rosycloud's Nirvana Sacred Flame, and thus contained intense fire energy. Even the spiritual vein's attribute had completely shifted to the fire element. For ordinary cultivators, it was not suitable for cultivation, but for alchemists, it was a natural treasure land.

Arranging formations, constructing alchemy chambers.

Channeling earthfire, engaging in large-scale pill refinement.

This was the main theme of Alchemy Mountain these days.

The several jade slips Luo Chen had scattered that day contained multiple directions for his research on the Initiation Pill.

He had handed them to Qu Lingjun and the other four, having them carry out corresponding experimental work according to the paths he had mapped out.

Selecting medicinal herbs, sorting and processing them by category, experimenting with ratios, controlling the heat...

One task after another proceeded methodically under the concerted efforts of all Luo Tian Sect alchemists above the second rank.

Luo Chen, for his part, was not idle either.

He had left a divided-soul puppet stationed in the Alchemy Dust Hall to oversee the overall situation. Meanwhile, his true body had left Alchemy Mountain and, accompanied by Bai Meiling, arrived at a large lake within the Alchemy Saint's Grotto-Heaven.

Gazing at the vast, misty lake, Luo Chen narrowed his eyes.

With a thought, he immediately received a response.

"Moo..."

Accompanied by a dull sound, the calm lake stirred as the enormous form of the Mystic Turtle slowly surfaced.

Their eyes met, and the atmosphere grew somewhat oppressive.

But under Luo Chen's gaze, the Mystic Turtle ultimately prostrated itself.

It raised its head and, somewhat reluctantly, opened its mouth.

The surrounding water rushed toward it in unison, and an intense red light flared across its body.

Gradually, a crystalline blood bead condensed in its mouth.

With the formation of this blood bead, the Mystic Turtle's aura continuously weakened.

Seeing this, Bai Meiling immediately realized what was happening.

The Mystic Turtle had forcibly expelled a large amount of its essence blood to condense this blood bead.

This would be a severe blow to the vitality of this desolate beast Mystic Turtle.

Bai Meiling couldn't help but wonder—her master was by no means an evil person. How could he treat the always loyal and honest Mystic Turtle like this?

Luo Chen did not explain. He simply reached out and beckoned, and the bead containing a vast amount of the Mystic Turtle's origin blood fell into his hand.

Then, Luo Chen scattered a large handful of pills, which flew into the Mystic Turtle's mouth.

With these pills to replenish it, the decline in the Mystic Turtle's aura was finally halted.

Luo Chen nodded. "I have noted your sacrifice. If I attain the Dao one day, I will surely repay you abundantly. For now, go with Little Bai to collect souls!"

The Mystic Turtle responded with a low grunt, then carried Bai Meiling away from the Alchemy Saint's Grotto-Heaven.

Watching the retreating figures of the beast and the ghost, Luo Chen pressed his lips together and said nothing.

The blood bead in his hand transmitted a continuous, warm sensation.

Considering the battles that might lie ahead, no amount of preparation was too much.

And when it came to his own defense, he naturally could not neglect it.

The Mystic Dust Armor, which he had painstakingly refined back then, had gradually become unable to keep up with his strength as his cultivation level rose.

Moreover, during his journey to the Southern Frontier, it had suffered severe damage.

One could say that if he had been protected by the Mystic Dust Armor at the time, his physical body would not have sustained such severe injuries in his battle with Azure Frost.

Now that he had returned to the Luo Tian Sect, Luo Chen naturally intended to gather various materials to repair the Mystic Dust Armor.

But merely repairing it was not enough!

When the Mystic Dust Armor was originally refined, the main material was a portion of the Xuanyan Turtle Emperor's shed shell. For Adept cultivators, it was considered top-grade. For Sage cultivators, it was still decent.

But for someone at his current level, it was far too inadequate.

Therefore, Luo Chen had taken the desolate beast Mystic Turtle's essence blood precisely to raise the Mystic Dust Armor's upper limit!

Even if this upper limit could not reach the level of a spirit treasure, it had to be sufficient to support his normal combat after transforming into the Three-Headed Demon Body.

...

In a side hall on Alchemy Mountain.

Luo Chen sat cross-legged, casting one hand seal after another at a damaged black suit of armor suspended in midair.

The precious refining materials that had already been melted around him continuously merged into the armor, guided by these hand seals.

As spiritual light flickered, the armor slowly stretched out, gradually restoring itself to its original form.

Even at this point, Luo Chen did not stop. He struck the Mystic Turtle's condensed origin blood bead into the armor.

Instantly, the hall blazed with brilliant light!

"Still not enough!"

Luo Chen shook his hand, and five lotus seeds appeared.

These were precisely the Five Elements Lotus Seeds he had taken from that top-grade Five Elements Lotus Platform back then!

That Five Elements Lotus Platform had been on the verge of gaining sentience and entering the fifth rank. The Five Elements Lotus Seeds condensed from its body were the ultimate quintessence of the Five Elements.

Unfortunately, such treasures could not be used for alchemy, leaving Luo Chen with a precious treasure but no way to use it.

But now, he had found an excellent application.

Luo Chen pinched one between his fingers, and the heaviest Earth Lotus Seed drifted gracefully toward the Mystic Dust Armor.

Thus, one of the five was gone.

Luo Chen thought for a moment, then gritted his teeth and hesitated no longer. The remaining four seeds flew toward the Mystic Dust Armor one after another.

The Gold Lotus Seed, the Wood Lotus Seed, and even the Fire Lotus Seed merged into the Mystic Dust Armor in succession.

Only when it came to the last Water Lotus Seed did Luo Chen stop his hand.

His gaze fell upon the Primordial Unity Cauldron beneath the Mystic Dust Armor, a contemplative look in his eyes.

"This should be enough. This Mystic Turtle leans toward the water attribute, and the origin blood bead it condensed is the same. Paired with four lotus seeds, a complete Five Elements cycle can already be achieved. This Water Lotus Seed is unnecessary."

"And later, when I reclaim my life soul, I might be able to use this item. Let me keep it for a while longer!"

Murmuring to himself, Luo Chen carefully put away the Water Lotus Seed.

Then, he expelled his true flame and slowly began to smelt the Mystic Dust Armor.

He intended to refine all four lotus seeds and the Mystic Turtle's origin blood bead into the Mystic Dust Armor. This would not only greatly increase its defensive power but also raise its upper limit once more.

If Luo Chen did not die, there might even be a chance for this treasure to advance to the spirit treasure level in the future.

The refining in the side hall proceeded slowly, while the research progress on the Initiation Pill on Alchemy Mountain also advanced rapidly with the passage of time.

One day, two days... one month, two months...

During this period, Luo Chen's true body never participated. It was entirely the divided-soul puppet that oversaw the overall situation.

It was not until a year later that Luo Chen leisurely appeared.

Seeing Luo Chen, Qu Lingjun's weary face lit up with excitement. He quickly approached to report the specific situation.

"Master, we have currently eliminated the three hypotheses you proposed, but seven remain."

"During this time, your divided-soul puppet also personally experimented with those seven pill formulas, but all of them failed."

Speaking up to this point, Qu Lingjun's face showed confusion.

"It really shouldn't be like this. We all believed those seven pill formulas were the seven most suitable paths, especially three of them, which were nearly perfect. Even a complete Initiation Pill formula, I imagine, would be no more than this. But the repeated failures have greatly dampened the disciples' confidence."

Luo Chen nodded and reached out his hand. "I know all of this. Leave the rest to me!"

Qu Lingjun hastily took seven jade slips from his storage pouch, each recording a method for refining the Initiation Pill.

As the jade slips entered his hand, Luo Chen fell silent.

Qu Lingjun was puzzled but could only wait quietly by the side.

After about the time it takes to drink a cup of tea, Luo Chen spoke.

He picked up one jade slip and spread it open before Qu Lingjun. "Begin preparing a large quantity of the relevant medicinal materials according to this method. Additionally, requisition ten liang of Starfall Sand and one Bodhi Wood from Sect Leader Li. Process them using orthodox methods."

Qu Lingjun was stunned, then realized that this might be the true refining method for the Initiation Pill that his master had determined.

But he was still full of confusion.

"This method also failed before. It didn't even produce a pill-forming phenomenon."

Luo Chen smiled. "Failure is a common occurrence. For example, if I gave you a correct Nascent Soul Pill formula, could you succeed in refining it on your first try? A failed refinement does not necessarily mean there is a problem with the pill formula."

Qu Lingjun asked again, "Then why add Starfall Sand and Bodhi Wood?"

Luo Chen patiently explained, "Initiation—it takes the Buddhist meaning of 'initiation pouring from above.' The Initiation Pill is a pill that aids enlightenment. Our divine sense is like the sands of the Ganges, difficult to condense. Therefore, unconventional medicinal materials like Starfall Sand and Bodhi Wood are needed as auxiliary agents. This way, the medicinal properties of the Initiation Pill will be better."

Qu Lingjun blinked. "Master is so certain?"

"Just do as I say."

Faced with his disciple's rare questioning, Luo Chen was not angered.

The moment he had touched the seven jade slips, he had already used his attribute panel to confirm their authenticity.

Under the year-long efforts of his divided-soul puppet and all the outstanding alchemists of the Luo Tian Sect, the complete Initiation Pill formula had finally been deduced.

This also fulfilled the pursuit of generations of Eastern Wilderness alchemists.

The reason he had spent an additional half a cup of tea's time was that, drawing on his insight as a Grandmaster of the Alchemy Dao, he had added a few more medicinal ingredients to ensure that the Initiation Pill produced would have even better effects.

And this had also been recognized by the system.

Creating something from nothing might be difficult, but adding embellishments to perfection was merely a casual act for Luo Chen at his current level.

Qu Lingjun hesitated no longer. He took his leave and departed.

Next, he would lead the disciples in helping Luo Chen prepare a large quantity of the basic medicinal materials needed for refining the Initiation Pill. The time saved in this process was also extremely precious to Luo Chen.

As for Luo Chen, he once again fell into contemplation.

His divine sense spread, and he looked inward.

Within his Purple Mansion, the Sage wore pitch-black armor and held a gray small cauldron in his arms.

At this moment, all his attention was focused on that gray small cauldron.

The Netherwater Spring Eye was inside the Primordial Unity Cauldron!

And a year ago, when he had used the Primordial Unity Cauldron to collect the Netherwater Spring Eye on Jambu Mountain, an anomaly had occurred.

At that time, after the Netherwater Spring Eye was taken in, it still resisted violently, requiring Luo Chen to expend a great deal of mana to suppress it.

But strangely, this violent resistance disappeared after just a short while.

Luo Chen did not believe that his Primordial Unity Cauldron was so powerful that it could instantly suppress the Netherwater Spring Eye.

Yet that was exactly what had happened!

Over the past year, Luo Chen had never actively explored the silent Netherwater Spring Eye. Instead, he had been constantly thinking.

Gradually, among his past experiences, he found a similar situation.

Back when he had extracted the Tribulation Gu from his son Luo Lingxi's body, the Primordial Unity Cauldron had also easily suppressed it.

And whether it was the Tribulation Gu or the Netherwater Spring Eye, both far surpassed ordinary treasures of the cultivation world.

Each contained the true essence of laws and could be regarded as fifth-rank treasures.

Why was the Primordial Unity Cauldron able to suppress them even more easily than it suppressed ordinary Sage cultivators?

In the end, Luo Chen directed his suspicion toward that wisp of gray light he had collected in the Demon-Fall Land back then.

"What exactly is that thing?"

"Devil Lord Heaven-Refining had stored it with great care inside the Heaven-Refining Cauldron. It cannot possibly be an ordinary object."

"For so many years, it has never revealed its power. Even my divine sense cannot capture the slightest trace of it."

"But ever since that gray light merged in, whether I am refining artifacts or alchemy, I feel as if I have divine assistance."

"Could it be a treasure related to the Dao of Refinement?"

Lost in thought, Luo Chen felt a restless urge stirring within him.

The Netherwater Spring Eye was not merely a source of water origin; it also carried an extremely powerful corrupting force!

This was because it was condensed from the purest Netherwater.

Even the Nether Ghost King, whose body was formed from Netherwater essence, had been corrupted by it. Instead of refining it, he had been assimilated.

Even the Nether Origin Sect, after obtaining this item back then, had no one who could completely refine it. They could only bind it within the Heavenly Nether Palace.

With Luo Chen's current strength, he did not dare to forcibly refine this treasure, let alone entertain the delusion of controlling the Nether Abyss.

But if that wisp of gray light absorbed by the Primordial Unity Cauldron truly had such unique abilities in this regard, then Luo Chen could try to forcibly refine the Netherwater Spring Eye.

If successful, it might provide unexpected help for his subsequent plans.

"I'll use the refinement of the Initiation Pill to test it out first!"

Luo Chen made up his mind. For this alchemy session, he would spare no effort and concentrate fully.

Not only would he focus on refining the Initiation Pill, but he would also pay attention to the Primordial Unity Cauldron's reaction.

...

Qu Lingjun moved quickly.

Or rather, the alchemists of the Luo Tian Sect were all extremely devoted to Luo Chen. They put all their strength into completing the tasks he had assigned.

Thus, in just over a month, all the necessary preparatory work was completed one after another.

And everything that followed was left entirely to Luo Chen.

This time, Luo Chen did not dismiss the others. Instead, he chose an open area on Alchemy Mountain and allowed all his disciples to observe his alchemy.

"This alchemy session will surely succeed. All of you, observe from the sidelines. How much you can learn depends entirely on your personal fortune!"

On a temporarily constructed high platform, Luo Chen spoke slowly.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron had already been summoned and set in place. Nearby were various medicinal herbs arranged in separate categories.

Upon hearing this, the hundred-plus disciples around immediately took action.

One by one, they took out jade bottles and dripped liquid into their eyes.

It was Clarity Liquid!

A product of the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect's research on the Imperial Jade Clarity Eye Liquid back then, which had even spawned a substitute, the Moistening Bright Pearl Eye Liquid.

Under Luo Chen's influence, all the alchemists of the Luo Tian Sect had also developed the habit of using this medicine to enhance their eyesight.

Now that they were about to observe the Supreme Elder's alchemy, each of them was giving it their all.

Not only were they enhancing their eyesight, but some were also burning incense to calm their minds, taking medicine to strengthen their bodies, all in pursuit of not missing a single detail.

Luo Chen smiled and, like a gentle rain nourishing all things, began the refinement of the Initiation Pill.

His movements were neither fast nor slow. He employed various alchemy techniques with ease while also displaying the complete hand seals as much as possible. Especially during the processing of various medicinal herbs, he would occasionally pause to explain.

Perhaps this would be his last time refining pills in public, so he might as well let the younger generation see it clearly!

As for how much they could comprehend, that was up to them.

Time passed bit by bit, and the progress of the Initiation Pill's refinement proceeded at a steady pace, without a single error or omission.

The alchemy techniques Luo Chen displayed left all the disciples in awe.

Steady, natural, as if heaven-made.

His various basic alchemy techniques were used with supernatural skill.

His handling of medicinal herbs, even with the simplest methods, was flawless.

And his control over the heat far exceeded everyone's understanding.

From time to time, people came from other places. They had heard that the Supreme Elder was personally conducting a public alchemy session and had come to observe.

Even if they were not alchemists, they wanted to witness the style of the Eastern Wilderness Alchemy Saint.

However, as the refinement progressed deeper and deeper, by the later stages, not to mention ordinary cultivators, even the second and third-rank alchemists could barely understand Luo Chen's techniques.

Qu Lingjun and Qi Yi could be considered the best among them, but even they wore bewildered expressions when Luo Chen executed a strange alchemy technique.

"What alchemy technique is that?"

"Why is it that, without affecting the heaven and earth spiritual energy, without affecting the cauldron, or even the true flame, the medicinal liquid is undergoing such violent changes?"

By this point, Luo Chen had no time to explain.

The Initiation Pill formula he had deduced, although complete and recognized by the system, was not necessarily the original formula.

This was a formula he had supplemented by combining the contributions of generations of alchemy masters with his own understanding.

Especially at the most crucial point, he had integrated the concept he had used when refining the fifth-rank divine pill, the Law-Derivation Pill.

The Law-Derivation Pill, the Heavenly Secret Pill, and the Initiation Pill could be said to share the same lineage. All of them assisted cultivators in entering a state of enlightenment to comprehend the laws of heaven and earth.

To reach that mysterious and profound state, in Luo Chen's view, the participation of one thing was absolutely indispensable.

Spiritual Opportunity!

Therefore, he executed a profound alchemy technique learned from the Alchemy Saint's inheritance—Refining Heavenly Opportunity!

Leveraging the origin qi of heaven and earth, capturing spiritual opportunity, and merging it into the pill!

Under Luo Chen's full exertion, wind and thunder howled in the sky. Faintly, some kind of inexplicable, hazy aura was being drawn out from the Alchemy Saint's Grotto-Heaven.

Luo Chen's eyes blazed with golden light, and a hint of joy appeared on his face.

"Indeed! Back when the three ancestors of the Nether Origin Sect opened the Alchemy Saint's Grotto-Heaven, they drew in some spiritual opportunity. Now, it is just right for my use!"
