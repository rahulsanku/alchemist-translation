# Chapter 787: Holy Land Certification, Luo Tian Upper Sect

At the bottom of Great Green Mountain, rock strata crisscrossed in all directions.

Moisture from the Blackwater River seeped through, forming droplets on the rock surfaces.

*Drip!*

*Drip!*

Sima Wenjie suddenly awoke from his dream, and everything he saw was still pitch black.

With his magic power sealed, even though his body was strong, he still felt extremely cold in this dark underground, amplified by the dejection in his heart.

He tightened his thin blanket and listened carefully.

Then, subconsciously, he walked toward the prison bars.

*Chi!*

A faint light suddenly flashed!

Sima Wenjie was forced back, and at the same time, a contemptuous voice came from outside.

"Stay put. You don't actually think you can escape the formation personally laid by Elder Min, do you?"

Faced with the disdain and contempt in those words, Sima Wenjie's heart stung, but he recognized the voice.

"Lou Ying? It's your turn to guard me today."

A light appeared, and the stooped figure of Lou Ying, holding a night pearl, slowly walked to the prison entrance. His shadow almost completely enveloped Sima Wenjie.

"Unexpected, you still remember me."

Sima Wenjie looked at that face, covered in wrinkles and bearing a hideous claw scar, and sighed: "How could I not recognize you? Back then, you were in charge of the Outer Sect, with the most disciples and the most affairs. The Merits Hall I managed dealt with you more than anyone else. Even if your face were completely destroyed, I could still recognize you."

Lou Ying touched his own face, weathered by the years, and let out a low laugh.

"Outer Sect?"

After so many years of fleeing, the Luo Tian Sect's Outer Sect had long since ceased to exist. Even if any Outer Sect disciples were still alive, they had basically been absorbed under the command of the various elders.

He, as one of the rare Initiate-level Outer Sect elders of the Luo Tian Sect, had instead become idle.

But the more idle a person became, the more they liked to reminisce about the past.

Thinking of how many Outer Sect disciples had died before his eyes, ultimately to preserve the Luo Tian Sect, while this man before him, holding a high position, had instead thought of handing the Luo Tian Sect over to outsiders...

Lou Ying held Sima Wenjie in particular contempt.

Faced with that gaze, as if looking at an animal, Sima Wenjie accepted it calmly.

He didn't think he had done anything wrong.

Nor did he want to argue.

Or rather, he didn't want to argue with ordinary people.

At this moment, he raised his head and looked far into the distance outside the dungeon.

"It seems... quite lively?"

"Naturally, it's lively. Today is the day of the Supreme Elder's belated Nascent Soul Ceremony. Sages from all five domains have been invited. There has never been a more lively occasion for my Luo Tian Sect!"

Lou Ying's voice grew louder, his expression proud as he spoke.

Even his stooped waist seemed to straighten by three points.

This was the pride of being associated with glory!

Sima Wenjie's expression changed slightly. "He just returned from outside, with no influence in the Eastern Wilderness. How could he possibly invite so many Sages? You must be exaggerating!"

Lou Ying cast a disdainful glance at him.

"You, a frog at the bottom of a well, how could you know the Supreme Elder's prestige?"

"Let me inform you: yesterday evening, Sage Xianhe of the White Crane Sect arrived early at Baili Qingchuan. Early this morning, before the ceremony even began, the Great Palace Master of Fenghua Palace arrived at Great Green Mountain. Just now, the Sect Master of the Myriad Beasts Sect and Sage Mu of the Mu Estate arrived together. That's only a part of it. More Sages are coming later, even the Lingtian City Lord is coming to offer congratulations!"

Hearing one name after another that usually thundered in his ears, Sima Wenjie's eyes went blank.

How could that man have such great influence!

He clearly hadn't been in the Eastern Wilderness, yet the moment he returned, so many fellow Daoist Sages eagerly flocked to him.

In a daze, the scenes of Luo Chen's Initiate Ceremony and Adept Ceremony flashed before his eyes. Each time grander than the last, each time more spectacular than the last. Now, it seemed there were over a dozen Sages coming to congratulate him.

Was such a feat something they, the mud-legged folks who had come out of Dahe Market, could have dared to imagine?

"Could it be... that Second Sister's persistence was right?"

For some reason, Sima Wenjie's resolve wavered for a moment.

...

"This is Sage Mu, the Supreme Elder of the Mu family, and also the master of the Mu Estate."

"Sage Mu!" Luo Chen made a Daoist salute and greeted him with a smiling face.

The man opposite him was richly dressed, with a calm demeanor. Looking at Luo Chen, he couldn't help but praise: "Truly as the rumors say, young and accomplished, with profound Dao cultivation! With such bearing and aura, even the strong of the Holy Lands would be no more than this!"

Indeed, although today's Luo Chen was the same person as usual, his bearing and aura had changed greatly.

His plain white robe had been replaced with a gold-trimmed, silver-patterned Cloud Yang long robe, still elegant but not lacking in nobility.

His long, jet-black hair, which had been left loose down his back for a hundred years in the Northern Sea, held only by a Daoist hairpin, had today been combed up by Gu Caiyi's skillful hands. The hair on both sides was combed back, and on top of his head sat a red-gold high crown, with a horizontal black jade hairpin piercing through it.

At a single glance, one could see that the man before them had strong, defined features and deep, sharp contours.

Nobility without losing dominance!

With his sharp edge exposed, he was not to be looked at directly!

This was the case of "clothes make the man." In the past, Luo Chen didn't care about his appearance or demeanor, nor did he pay much attention to ostentation when traveling. But today, everything had to change.

Adhering to the domineering style of Lingtian Pass, Luo Chen wanted people to know that his future conduct would be like this, and would always be like this!

After exchanging a few words with Sage Mu, Luo Chen, guided by Sage Xianhe, went to meet another Sage.

His sleeves were wide open, his chest covered in a patch of bristly hair, and his eyes were sharp, like those of a tiger or leopard.

"This is the Supreme Elder of the Myriad Beasts Sect, Qin Baiming!"

When introducing this person, Sage Xianhe's expression was not good.

Qin Baiming acted as if he hadn't seen it, and instead stared at Luo Chen with a chuckle.

"Not bad, you do have some style. I just wonder if what was stated on the invitation is true."

Luo Chen smiled slightly. "Fellow Daoist Qin, you may wait and see!"

He didn't communicate much with him, but under Sage Xianhe's guidance, he got to know one new friend after another, all completely unfamiliar names he had heard many times in his youth.

Sage Mu of the Mu Estate, Qin Baiming of the Myriad Beasts Sect.

Pavilion Master Li of the Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion.

If you added Sage Xianhe and Fenghua, then he had basically met all the native Sages of the front-line five domains.

As for the remaining Sages, they were even more interesting.

The previously acquainted Elder Fu of the Divine Talisman Sect.

Lin Qingxuan of the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect.

Divine Fire and Golden Feather of the Five Elements Divine Sect.

These were the survivors of the former Extreme East Six Domains—no, Seven Domains. Tianfan had been destroyed, Falling Cloud had been annihilated, the Medicine King Sect had vanished like smoke, and the Joyous Union Sect had been attacked directly at its core. Only these three families were left, barely surviving.

Then there were those Luo Chen was relatively unfamiliar with: Xiao Shiniang, the master of Firework Ancient Islet; Nine Spirits Primordial Lord, the Supreme Elder of the Nine Spirits Sect; and Lingfengzi, the Supreme Elder of the Misty Void Sect.

These three people, or rather these three families, had origins roughly similar to the Divine Talisman and Jade Cauldron Sects—they could all be classified as "homeless dogs."

Before the current five domains, twelve domains had been lost, and three were in a state of constant war.

Firework Ancient Islet, Nine Spirits, and Misty Void all belonged to former regional powers among the twelve lost domains.

Now, these three families had been assigned to three other domains among the five, living as dependents under others' roofs.

However, when Luo Chen met them, his attitude was as before, without any change.

To be honest, wasn't he also a homeless dog?

Sharing the same misfortune, everyone instead felt sympathetic and understood each other.

Xiao Shiniang was an impatient person. After getting acquainted, she directly asked about the matter on the invitation.

"Fellow Daoist Luo, you say you can refine a hundred kinds of pills and are proficient in all of them. Is that true?"

Indeed, that sentence was on the invitation.

Or rather, it was precisely because of that sentence that so many unfamiliar Sages had traveled thousands of miles to attend his Nascent Soul Ceremony.

As soon as this question was asked, all eyes in the Luo Tian Hall of Great Green Mountain turned to Luo Chen.

Most of them held looks of doubt.

Only Sage Xianhe stood behind Luo Chen, smiling without speaking.

Even the Great Palace Master of Fenghua Palace, though aware of Luo Chen's alchemy abilities, was still half-doubtful at this moment.

Refining a hundred kinds of pills, proficient in all of them!

That was quite a bold claim!

The hundred arts of immortality encompassed all aspects of a cultivator's details in the pursuit of the Great Dao.

Among the hundred arts, alchemy was paramount.

This was a cliché, and also a truth passed down by the vast number of lower-level cultivators.

But in reality, as one's personal realm rose, the auxiliary effect of pills became less and less obvious.

Especially when they reached the Sage realm, taking pills compared to long-term meditation and cultivation on a spirit vein was far less efficient.

Moreover, high-level pills suitable for Sage cultivators were rare, and the alchemists capable of refining them were even rarer.

Due to multiple factors, the higher the realm, the lower the status of alchemy!

However!

This situation only applied to mediocre individuals. If there was someone who could refine fourth-tier spirit pills, or even fifth-tier divine pills, they would still be noble and distinguished wherever they went.

Just like Alchemy Saint Chu Yan!

Furthermore, there was another point.

This situation also depended on the times.

In the current era of beast calamities, with no guarantee of safety from one day to the next, a group of homeless dogs didn't even have a true place to call their own, let alone high-level spirit veins with abundant spiritual energy. Constant fighting made quiet cultivation even more impossible.

Low-level cultivators could still rely on the terrifying resources unleashed during the beast calamity to advance their realms.

But for the strong at the Sage level, what resources could compare to stable, abundant high-level spirit veins?

In the hundred years of the Eastern Wilderness beast calamity, few of those present had enjoyed the experience of quiet, secluded cultivation.

Many of their realms had been forced to stagnate.

Therefore, in this period, under these circumstances, miraculous pills that didn't depend on spirit lands and could be taken anytime for cultivation had once again risen in status.

Why was Qu Lingjun valued by Fenghua Palace?

Why did the Motian Old Ghost covet the Luo Tian Sect?

Why had the Medicine King Sect, once so wealthy it dripped with oil, standing proudly in the Extreme East Six Domains and even famous among the thirty-six domains of the Eastern Wilderness, been destroyed in an instant under the beast tide?

All of this was because pills stirred people's hearts!

Therefore, when Luo Chen added the sentence "Refining a hundred kinds of pills, proficient in all of them" to the invitations he sent to various families, even Sages who had never known him were moved.

A simple sentence revealed immense confidence!

Someone with such attainments was no ordinary alchemist. They had to be an Alchemy Grandmaster... no, an Alchemy Grandmaster, or even an Alchemy Saint!

Especially since he had written that sentence on the invitation. The implication was that he was willing to help people refine miraculous pills!

The attainments of an Alchemy Grandmaster were admirable and worth currying favor with.

And the willingness to refine pills for others—that was the main factor that made the invitees come without hesitation!

Facing the eager gazes of the crowd, Luo Chen smiled slightly and was about to say something when his brow suddenly twitched.

A thin man in a golden robe inside the hall also suddenly spoke: "A distinguished guest has arrived!"

Luo Chen glanced at that person, then swung his sleeves and went out of the hall to welcome them.

Behind him, the thin man in the golden robe watched Luo Chen's back, his eyes narrowing slightly.

"Divine Fire, is Luo Chen's alchemy ability real or fake?"

Divine Fire Sage's divine sense transmission quickly entered his ear.

"It should be real. He was famous for his alchemy in the Jade Cauldron Domain since his youth, and even had a Dao name called 'Dan Chenzi.' Golden Feather, didn't we discuss this topic before we came? Why are you suddenly asking again?"

Golden Feather Sage's expression was calm, but there was a hint of gloom deep in his eyes.

"We both underestimated the influence an Alchemy Grandmaster has over Sage-level cultivators!"

Divine Fire was startled, then immediately understood.

Just now, the crowd's enthusiastic attitude toward Luo Chen, their words fawning and flattering, was hardly appropriate for meeting a strange fellow Daoist.

If Luo Chen wasn't bragging, but truly had the level of an Alchemy Grandmaster, then as long as he was willing to show goodwill to this world, this world would give him unimaginable returns!

Low-level cultivators would madly pursue him, spreading his name.

Sage cultivators would flatter and curry favor with him, and at the very least, wouldn't deliberately offend him. If there were those who received Luo Chen's favor, then once Luo Chen encountered trouble, with a single command, how many Sages would run errands and work for him?

Even the Holy Lands of Transformation, hanging over thousands of sects in the Mountains and Seas Realm, wouldn't mind casting their lofty gazes down to invite Luo Chen as an honored guest.

Just like the former Eastern Wilderness Alchemy Saint!

And Luo Chen was clearly doing exactly that now!

Proclaiming his own abilities, revealing his goodwill to refine pills for others, and elevating his own momentum.

Once this great momentum was established, what could the Five Elements Divine Sect use to deal with Luo Chen?

Just the group of Sage cultivators inside the Luo Tian Hall and the forces behind them, united, would be enough to give the Five Elements Divine Sect a hard time.

If they waited until Luo Chen's reputation spread, how many strong cultivators from the Eastern Wilderness would come to him, running errands for him? His Five Elements Divine Sect would probably...

"True or false, we have to cut off his momentum first."

Golden Feather said with narrowed eyes.

Outside the hall, the sound of announcing gifts was already loud.

The first sentence shocked everyone present.

"The Nether Origin Sect bestows upon the Luo Tian Sect the method for crafting spirit stones!"

It was not a gift or a present, but a condescending bestowal.

Logically, it wasn't suitable for today's atmosphere, but one had to look at the prefix.

The Eastern Wilderness Holy Land, the Nether Origin Sect!

And what did the method for crafting spirit stones mean? Everyone present knew it clearly.

This was a method that could only be obtained by forces that had been permitted by a Holy Land to wage expansion wars and establish a Sage Upper Sect.

Many of them had experienced or understood this scene, and they all understood its meaning.

Now, this small Luo Tian Sect, curled up in Baili Qingchuan, was already, in terms of specifications, a Sage Upper Sect on equal footing with them!

What gifts came after, they no longer paid attention to.

In their line of sight, there were only two figures walking into the hall together, chatting amiably.

Lingtian City Lord Ye Lingtian.

Luo Tian Supreme Elder Luo Chen.

"Sage Luo, congratulations, congratulations!"

"Although your sect is temporarily stranded in a shallow stream, your disciples are all spirited and outstanding. In time, you will surely carry forward and display the glory of an Upper Sect."

"Now, although the beasts are powerful, they are ultimately no match for the foundation of our human race. Later, when we counterattack and tread the path of expansion again, securing one or two caves and blessed lands for the Luo Tian Sect—wouldn't that be wonderful!"

Amidst a flurry of congratulations, Luo Chen smiled and cupped his hands in thanks.

After exchanging pleasantries with the Lingtian City Lord, he sat down in the main seat at the head of the hall.

Sweeping his gaze over everyone in the hall, Luo Chen was all smiles.

"Everyone is almost here. Let the feast begin!"
