# Chapter 633: Blood God Battles the Vine King, Fishing Beside the Abyss

Rustle, rustle!

Clear wind drifted, toes lightly touched.

With a floating motion, Luo Chen stood atop the crown of a tree.

The moment he saw Dao Lan retreat with saber in hand, he had also subconsciously moved away from that valley.

In the earlier portion of this battle, things were actually just as Angler had said before they set out. It was not particularly "dangerous."

Those countless Withered Blood Vines could be broken with a wave of the hand under the powerful late Golden Core battle strength they possessed.

Even the seemingly troublesome fourth-tier Withered Blood Vines could not unleash killing power that truly threatened them.

Of course, this was built on the premise that all five of them possessed great cultivator battle strength.

If it had been early or mid Golden Core cultivators, dealing with it would not have been so easy and freehand.

Sometimes, the difference of one realm was a heavenly chasm!

At this moment, even someone as strong as Dao Lan had actively retreated, which showed how formidable the enemy they were about to face would be.

Luo Chen was no fool. Naturally, at the first moment, he opened the distance.

Looking at the terrifying white vine spanning the void in the sky, whose length was unknown, Luo Chen could not help swallowing.

"So this is the fifth-tier Withered Blood Vine?"

Unlike the human and demon races, which could easily awaken intelligence and step onto the Great Dao of cultivation, for wood and stone things to awaken intelligence and cultivate as spirits was extremely difficult.

Not only were there innate restrictions from the main body, but also restrictions from a cruel living environment.

In any case, over all these years, Luo Chen had seen only one kind of pearl-turned-spirit like Hidden Dust Sand.

One could imagine how formidable a Withered Blood Vine that could birth intelligence and cultivate to a fifth-tier body under such harsh restrictions would be!

Even if it could not possess the methods of an Ascendant great power, it was at least comparable to a Sage-stage cultivator.

This was Luo Chen's conclusion about the terrifying white vine before him.

And before such a judgment, Luo Chen was very curious what sort of method Angler would use to deal with this Vine King.

Within his field of vision, beneath the white vine, Angler's aged body was like a candle in the wind, trembling without pause.

Yet he did not retreat in the slightest, and his eyes were filled with wild joy.

"It has indeed awakened intelligence and can break free from innate shackles!"

Amid his wild joy, he suddenly half-knelt on the ground and shouted loudly, "Please, Perfected Being, manifest in the world!"

In the next moment.

His body shook violently, and dense red light spread from him.

The white vine in the sky seemed to realize something. It fiercely smashed down a giant vine, like a huge long whip.

Before that terrifying power truly descended, the sturdy valley had already turned to powder amid rumbling tremors.

How could such an attack be something a Golden Core cultivator could receive head-on?

Angler, he!

Just as Luo Chen's heart tightened, he saw a figure emerge within the spreading smoke and dust.

That person held one hand high and actually supported that long whip in midair.

It was a man with a sinister face and long, narrow eyes.

He wore red robes, like blood and fire.

Thinking of what Angler had shouted before this person appeared, Luo Chen blurted out, "Blood Wanderer?"

"Not so!"

Star Hook Envoy's voice sounded by his ear. At some unknown time, he had already arrived on a low peak one hundred zhang away from Luo Chen.

Under Luo Chen's curious gaze, he said softly, "It is Blood Wanderer, but not the man himself. This is the Blood Sea avatar he refined. It can be called a Blood God Child."

Luo Chen was puzzled.

Under his observation, that person was clearly a genuine Sage Perfected Being. How was he still an avatar?

Star Hook Envoy seemed to sense Luo Chen's confusion. With his understanding of the Primordial Demon Sect as a former Heavenly Demon Star Sect disciple, he quickly told Luo Chen the specific information in brief.

"Blood Wanderer is a heaven-sent genius. With the Primordial Demon Sect's Blood Sea lineage cultivation method as the foundation, he cultivated the Blood God Scripture to the extreme, surpassing even the direct-line powerhouses of the demonic sect's Blood Sea lineage."

"This avatar is none other than the Primordial Demon Sect powerhouse who guarded Longyuan Immortal City one hundred years ago."

"After being killed by him, he was forcibly refined into a Blood God Child!"

"Fellow Daoist, if this sort of existence has no master controlling it, its nature is cruel and vicious. You and I should stay farther away."

Luo Chen had originally not understood why Star Hook Envoy explained things to him in such detail. It could not be because Luo Chen had praised him a few times before coming, right?

Only now did he understand. The other party was worried that the Blood God Child might lack control and endanger them.

However!

"Judging from the current situation, this Blood God Child clearly received Blood Wanderer's order and came to capture the Vine King. Naturally, it will not place its attention on us."

Star Hook Envoy shook his head. "Even so, a Sage battle affects an extremely wide area, easily reaching hundreds or thousands of li. You and I should still be careful."

Luo Chen hummed in agreement and immediately withdrew together with Star Hook Envoy.

In his field of vision, Dao Lan retreated even farther.

After finding a relatively safe place, Luo Chen looked back.

On Withered Wood Ridge, the battle between the Blood God Child and the Vine King had already reached a fiery stage.

Surging mana fluctuations roared and echoed through the mountain ridge.

When the blood robe rolled, vast swathes of dense blood light spilled out.

Once the white vine's main body was tainted by the blood light, it released a foul stench.

Yet clearly, the Withered Blood Vine King was not something to be trifled with either. Its tyrannical main body bombarded constantly, each strike shattering mountains and raising wind and thunder.

Under its tyrannical attacks, the Blood God Child said nothing. He looked slightly battered and was knocked into the dust from time to time.

Even though he could stand up again and again and resume fighting, the dense blood light on his body also became increasingly thin.

"Can he win?"

Luo Chen frowned tightly, realizing something was wrong.

After observing carefully, he finally discovered where that sense of wrongness came from.

This Blood God Child indeed had the battle strength of a Sage Perfected Being, but it seemed not to have the amplification of a Sage domain?

As it unleashed one move after another, the mana on its body also grew less and less.

By contrast, the Withered Blood Vine King, which had seemed powerless at the beginning, was fighting with increasing bravery.

As if its strength would never stop, and its pure spiritual qi could be spent freely without cost.

Star Hook Envoy landed beside Luo Chen, relatively close to him, though still keeping a distance of several zhang.

Seeing this scene, he sighed. "It is ultimately a Blood God Child refined from a dead person. Not only does it no longer have potential for growth, it also cannot display the Sage domain from when it was alive."

Luo Chen looked over curiously.

Star Hook Envoy explained, "The so-called Sage domain is essentially a response generated by the compatibility between the cultivator's Nascent Soul and heaven and earth. Once a cultivator dies, even if the Nascent Soul remains, that responding ability is severed. Blood Wanderer used secret methods to preserve this Primordial Demon Sect powerhouse's Nascent Soul and refine it into a Blood God Child, but it is still difficult to defy natural laws and reproduce its domain from life."

Luo Chen suddenly understood, then said, "In that case, will this Blood God Child not become increasingly exhausted?"

"It should be so." Star Hook Envoy nodded, gazing hundreds of li away toward the center of the battlefield where smoke and dust rose. "We will have to see whether Blood Wanderer gave this Blood God Child any other battle methods."

Luo Chen settled his heart. Spiritual light shone greatly between his eyes as he stared fixedly at that constantly moving battlefield.

Within his field of vision, that Blood God Child constantly cast spells.

Sometimes placing fingers together like a sword, sword light crisscrossing.

Sometimes clapping both hands together, sending blood handprints striking the huge white vine.

Occasionally, with a wave of his sleeves, large amounts of blood light spilled out.

After watching for a long time, Luo Chen suddenly discovered that the blood light should be a kind of high-level baleful qi, with extremely powerful might. Every time it struck the white vine, the white vine's aura weakened by one point.

But clearly, this kind of baleful qi lessened with use.

If it wanted to use this method to grind down the white vine's power, who knew how much blood-light baleful qi the Blood God Child would need to have stored?

Just as Star Hook Envoy said, if this Blood God Child did not have other formidable methods bestowed by Blood Wanderer, he feared this battle could not be won.

...

Within Withered Wood Ridge, the great battle had already lasted a full half hour.

The battlefield area was no longer limited only to the original valley.

One man and one vine constantly shifted the battlefield.

Everywhere they passed was devastated.

There were even earth veins pierced through, erupting with scorching mountain fire.

And Luo Chen and the others watching the battle were also forced to flee early again and again.

If the task were not yet over, they would likely have left this human hell long ago.

Even so, the increasingly heavy atmosphere made several people lose the heart to converse. They raised all their attention, guarding against the battle aftermath that might arrive.

Boom!

With a huge sound, four figures fled in all directions.

After the smoke and dust scattered, the several people looked at each other.

Their gazes fell on the lifeless white vine remnant on the ground, and cold sweat could not help rising on their backs.

"We underestimated this Blood God Child. Even without the Sage domain's amplification, its vicious might is still not something we can imagine." Star Hook Envoy swallowed, fear thick in his eyes.

The battle aftermath just now, despite being a hundred li away, had still exceeded the range they could bear.

If not for the four joining forces and each using their methods, someone among them would probably have been heavily injured by that move.

Dao Lan leaned on his huge broad saber, his gaze suddenly falling on Luo Chen.

Looking at the man whose robes fluttered and black hair drifted, he suddenly praised, "That treasure is not bad!"

Luo Chen smoothed the trembling qi armor and pursed the corners of his mouth.

Under that strike just now, he had first struck out a Great Azure Yang Handprint, then activated Xuanchen Armor's qi armor. Only like this had he safely escaped.

He had thought he did it secretly, but had not expected Dao Lan to discover it with such sharpness.

Huangfu Song, with a large seal above his head, was the most composed of the four. Under the purple light's cover, he was completely unharmed.

At this moment, he smiled and said, "Daoist Azure Yang is a renowned artifact-forging master in the Myriad Immortals Assembly. Fellow Daoist Dao Lan, have you not long sought a close-fitting defensive magic treasure without success? After returning, why not ask Daoist Azure Yang to refine one for you?"

Dao Lan seemed moved and looked toward Luo Chen in silence.

Luo Chen snorted coldly. "No matter how good my magic treasure is, it seems it cannot compare to Fellow Daoist's great seal!"

Instantly, the three people's gazes all looked toward the great seal above Huangfu Song's head.

Looking carefully, a dragon head stood proudly on it. When fully activated, the patterns became increasingly clear. Upon closer inspection, they were clearly miniature carvings of flowers, birds, insects, fish, mountains, rivers, seas, and more.

This was clearly a jade seal used by the emperor of a mortal dynasty!

Seeing the three watching, Huangfu Song felt rather uncomfortable.

He wanted to put away the jade seal, but this battlefield was rather dangerous. With his experience in reacting to battle, without this treasure's protection, he feared he would have difficulty escaping harm.

Previously, he had wanted to divert trouble east and have Dao Lan seek Luo Chen to refine a treasure, delaying the other party's cultivation.

He had not expected the other party to return his own method against him, making Dao Lan's attention fall on his body-protecting treasure.

He gave a dry cough and changed the topic. "Where is Angler?"

The other three were stunned and could not help becoming curious.

That was right!

Where was Angler?

From the moment the Sage battle erupted, Angler had vanished.

If one said he had died in the battle's aftermath, not one of the four would be willing to believe it.

Although Angler usually looked as if he were about to enter the grave, his true battle strength was certainly above everyone present.

Being one of the Myriad Immortals Assembly's three great eight-star demon hunters represented not only status, but strength!

Luo Chen was also rather puzzled. Where had Angler run off to?

As his gaze wandered, it suddenly paused.

"The Blood God Child is finally about to end this battle."

Everyone followed the sound and looked.

They saw a blood-red figure shoot straight into the nine heavens. When he reached the clouds, his hands pulled horizontally.

A resplendent, bright blade light immediately filled heaven and earth!

The instant this blade appeared, there seemed to be wind and thunder responding between heaven and earth, and amid the radiance, thousands of ghosts and wolves howled in sorrow at once.

"It is the Divine Astral Blood Blade!"

"That true artifact Blood Wanderer bitterly cultivated and refined for hundreds of years!"

"I did not expect that for this fifth-tier Withered Blood Vine, Blood Wanderer would actually even take out his lifebound true artifact."

Cries of shock came from Star Hook Envoy's mouth.

Everyone's expression changed.

The so-called true artifact was transformed from a magic treasure, and in essence was still a magic treasure.

However, this kind of magic treasure had undergone the baptism of lightning tribulation and shed its mortal nature. Every movement and every strike possessed tremendous might.

Luo Chen had seen many Sage Perfected Beings, but very few possessed lifebound true artifacts.

Heaven Smelter perhaps had one, but after being captured by Azure Frost, it had already been stripped away.

As for the others, they either did not have one at all, or were ordinarily simply unwilling to take it out for battle.

He had not expected that Blood Wanderer, for the sake of this fifth-tier Withered Blood Vine, would actually bear to take out even his lifebound true artifact, and even hand it to a Blood God Child to control.

Looking at the long, narrow Divine Astral Blood Blade in the sky, resembling a miao saber, Luo Chen's eyes stung, and he subconsciously looked at the broad saber in Dao Lan's hand.

On it, there also seemed to be sounds of ghosts crying and wolves howling.

Perhaps noticing Luo Chen's gaze, the always silent Dao Lan now had an abnormal flush on his face.

"This Divine Astral Blood Blade is indeed from the same lineage as my Golden Suanni Saber, following the path of absorbing living souls and blood baleful power to advance. I have refined it for many years and killed countless demons, but I have only used demon beast blood baleful power to nourish it to the upper-grade level. Compared to Blood Wanderer's saber, the gap is truly too vast."

Using living souls and blood baleful power to advance a magic treasure's grade was the most popular artifact-refining method in the Northern Sea cultivation world.

According to rumor, it came from the Primordial Demon Sect.

And this style was indeed full of demonic sect flavor.

Clearly, Blood Wanderer's lifebound true artifact had already reached an extremely high level.

It had even purified ordinary blood baleful power to the astral baleful level.

And now, what it meant for the Blood God Child to take out this blade went without saying.

As expected!

After the Divine Astral Blood Blade appeared, the Blood God Child changed from his previous battle style and became open and forceful.

As blade light swung, every strike inevitably severed a segment of the white vine's main body.

Within the ruined Withered Wood Ridge, strange, miserable howls sounded from time to time.

As time passed, those strange howls that penetrated the soul also became quieter and quieter.

Seeing this, Star Hook Envoy's expression relaxed.

"The Vine King cannot hold on. Our task should also be ending."

The others thought the same.

However, before they could express opinions.

The next moment.

The earth trembled!

As everyone looked surprised and doubtful, their gazes fell on the nearby trees.

They saw the green color constantly fade, becoming increasingly pale. In a brief moment, the trees transformed into sections of rotten wood.

Even thousand-year ancient trees, under that strange trembling, became increasingly withered.

As if something were drawing out their vitality?

And this strange phenomenon did not appear only nearby.

Looking out, the vast Withered Wood Ridge was entirely like this.

The mountains, originally still stacked with green, lost their color at a speed visible to the naked eye. In only several breaths, only a smear of pale white remained.

As Luo Chen was doubtful and uncertain, his spiritual eyes abruptly stopped on a mountain peak.

Atop that mountain peak, Angler, who had been gone for a long time, reappeared in the world.

He sat dryly on a rock, holding the black-gold fishing rod. The charred Heaven-Remnant Silk line hung down into the endless abyss beneath the mountain. The Sound-Capturing Bell on the fishing rod trembled wildly, as if some great thing had taken the bait.

And all the vitality fading around them seemed to be gathering in Angler's direction.

Angler's face was pale, but his eyes were filled with dense joy.

Even though blood overflowed from the corner of his mouth, he still gripped the fishing rod firmly.

Within the abyss, a terrifying strange sound suddenly emerged, and a blood-colored long whip came tearing through the air.

An earthen-yellow light bloomed from Angler, but it lasted only an instant before being broken.

Seeing terrifying power accumulate again beneath the abyss.

Fear appeared in Angler's eyes, and he roared at the sky.

"Perfected Being, save me!"

The Blood God Child, who was trembling against the white vine, shot into the sky.

One hand pointed toward Angler, and a blood light flew out.

Afterward, he reached from afar toward Luo Chen and the others' direction.

Luo Chen and the others were utterly unaware.

The next moment, they heard a scream.

"My jade seal!"

Within their vision, Huangfu Song, with the jade seal above his head, had eyes full of madness as he chased toward Angler's direction.

In the air, a purple brilliance, like a meteor, was forcibly controlled by the Blood God Child and fell above that abyss.

A blood whip struck out again from the abyss, but was resisted by the blood light the Blood God Child had launched.

When another strange sound was about to erupt from within the abyss, the jade seal had already floated above it.

Dignified and noble purple brilliance constantly poured down from the jade seal.

No matter how frequently the strange sounds came from inside, the blood whip could no longer be seen rising into the air.

At this moment, the Blood God Child no longer cared about the lingering white vine. His figure descended beside Angler.

The long saber in his right hand swung, continuously cutting down the white vines attacking from every direction.

His eyes were fully focused, staring at the abyss.

When Huangfu Song arrived like wind and lightning, as if he had gone mad, the Blood God Child merely glanced coldly at him. The latter seemed to suffer a heavy blow, and all madness in his eyes faded, leaving only despair.

Luo Chen, Star Hook Envoy, and Dao Lan looked at one another, seeming to realize something.

They no longer delayed and flew toward that mountain peak at a neither fast nor slow pace.

When they arrived, the Blood God Child did not even look at them and still stared at the abyss.

Angler was still fishing beside the abyss. Even though his aura had weakened to the extreme, the hand gripping the rod did not relax in the slightest.

Just as everyone quietly waited.

The Blood God Child suddenly acted, brushing toward the jade seal suppressing the abyss.

Huangfu Song impatiently took the jade seal back, but with only one look, his face turned pale.

Luo Chen and the others looked over curiously. They saw that all the purple light on the jade seal had already scattered, revealing the magic treasure's main body.

Looking at it now, where was the power that had previously resisted countless Withered Blood Vines and even feared no aftermath from a Sage battle?

There were even several cracks appearing on the jade seal.

Luo Chen felt thoughtful.

Perhaps what was formidable was not this jade seal magic treasure, but the Sage Perfected Being method left on it.

His artifact-forging skill had become increasingly exquisite over these years. With only one glance, he could clearly see that this treasure looked as if it had never been refined by anyone.

It was impossible that Huangfu Song did not want to refine it. The only reason was that the Perfected Being method left on it blocked his refinement.

In this way, the full might of that body-protecting method could erupt.

But at the same time, it was also easy for others to forcibly seize.

During the battle just now, the Blood God Child had used power one realm higher to forcibly take the jade seal away, and had consumed the Perfected Being method left on it completely.

Now, the magic treasure had been returned to Huangfu Song, but it was almost no different from being ruined.

Judging by its appearance, at most it was only at the upper-grade level. Because of its mixed functions, it was even far inferior to an upper-grade magic treasure with a single power.

"Angler gathered the four of us, and although each of us has a different emphasis and he considered things comprehensively, clearly the most important one was Huangfu Song."

"He both used his ancestral magic treasure to suppress the creature in the abyss and consumed one of the other party's trump cards."

These thoughts flashed through Luo Chen's heart. Looking at the dispirited Huangfu Song, his emotions became rather complicated.

Just as everyone waited.

The Blood God Child standing in front acted again.

He grabbed that charred line and lifted upward.

A trace of dissatisfaction flashed through Angler's eyes, but it disappeared in an instant.

In the next moment.

Whoosh!

Under the sunlight, a section of vine glittering with crystalline blood light appeared before everyone.

Looking carefully, its form was like the white vine before, but its lively vitality surpassed it by a hundredfold, a thousandfold!

Everyone suddenly understood.

This was the fifth-tier Withered Blood Vine's true main body!

And that white vine had merely been its external avatar.

No, that was not quite right. The difference in vitality between the two should not have been this great.

As Luo Chen was puzzled, his gaze swept across the mountains and ridges, and his heart paused.

Everything in sight was pale, withered like ash.

This scenery seemed to truly match the meaning of Withered Wood Ridge.

"Gentlemen, you have worked hard."

Just as Luo Chen was dazed, Angler's hoarse, dry voice sounded faintly.

Luo Chen turned back, only to see that the Blood God Child had vanished without a trace.

Angler stood from the rock. The moment he rose, the huge stone beneath him turned to powder.

The old man patted off the dust, revealing a relieved smile.

He swept his gaze over everyone one by one, then nodded slightly.

"The task has ended. After we return, this old man will fulfill the conditions I promised you one by one."

"Huangfu Song, I will also negotiate with the Tianqu Chen clan on behalf of the Myriad Immortals Assembly. They should no longer interfere with that vassal below, and you may act freely."

The latter sentence was merely added on.

Huangfu Song revealed a smile even uglier than crying. "Thank you, Angler, for granting this."

Angler chuckled, his gaze falling on the mountain ridge that was now a field of ruins. With a wave of his hand, he scattered.

Heaven-Remnant Silk spread out like a fishing net.

When it was retrieved, seven or eight huge white vines fell before them.

"Although these white vines are not the Withered Blood Vine's true main body, they are also shed husks it bitterly cultivated for several thousand years. The Perfected Being did not cut all of them apart, which has benefited us."

Angler praised once, then divided the white vines, two per person.

When they came before Luo Chen, however, there were three.

Luo Chen was surprised.

Angler blinked. "Fellow Daoist, forcing you to participate in this task this time, I hope I did not delay your matters."

Luo Chen was suddenly speechless. This Angler had previously shown a cold attitude of disregarding their friendship, yet now he suddenly changed.

Who knew whether it was because his mood was comfortable after the task was completed, or because he wanted to mend the relationship between himself and Luo Chen.

Perhaps both.

His gaze swept over the other three. Apart from Huangfu Song, none were dissatisfied.

Luo Chen understood that before setting out, Angler should have reached corresponding conditions with them all. Only his side lacked the proper compensation.

He no longer hesitated and immediately accepted these white vines generously.

The shed husk of a fifth-tier Withered Blood Vine, even without the main body's essence, could still be used as a fourth-tier material no matter what.

Overall, this trip had not counted as a loss.

After accepting the white vines, everyone had no more lingering thoughts. Angler summoned the flying boat and was about to return.

Luo Chen, however, did not board.

"Fellow Daoist, what is it?" Angler was puzzled.

Luo Chen cupped his hands. "It is rare to come to the Demon Sea. Returning so hastily would be rather wasteful. Luo plans to travel a bit and will return to the Myriad Immortals Assembly on his own later."

After saying this, he raised a magic cloud and leisurely left in the opposite direction.

On the flying boat, Angler frowned as he looked at the direction he left, pondering for a long while without saying anything.

He merely waved his sleeve and started the flying boat.

*(End of Chapter)*
