# Chapter 624: The So-Called Sage Domain, News of Purple Monkey Flower Arrives

Han Zhan was thinking of having Luo Chen explore the path for him, probing the power of the Two Modes Six Armors Array in advance.

He did not expect Luo Chen to instead seek him out first.

"Domain?"

Inside the cave residence, looking at Luo Chen, who had come humbly seeking instruction, Han Zhan was momentarily stunned.

"You are only at Golden Core now. Why are you already thinking about domains?"

Luo Chen smiled freely. "It is only curiosity. This junior was thinking that if I could grasp domain methods at Golden Core, perhaps it could become my greatest killing weapon."

Han Zhan frowned. "As I see it, your current Dao-protecting methods already far surpass ordinary Golden Core cultivators. Yet you are still unsatisfied and covet more. Are you not afraid of delaying your cultivation?"

Indeed, the kinds of methods Luo Chen currently grasped could be called numerous.

Spells, illusions, body arts; he had almost no shortcomings, and each one possessed vast power.

As for the magic treasures in his hands, aside from lacking a top-tier offensive treasure, he also possessed the upper-grade Primordial Unity Cauldron, which was comprehensive in function.

If that defensive treasure were refined, then among Golden Core cultivators, it could be said that Luo Chen could run rampant as he pleased.

Now, he still seemed unsatisfied and wanted to cross realms to learn even stronger methods. It was truly a little like abandoning the root to chase the branch.

One had to know that after his realm broke through, the powerful methods he now regarded as killing weapons might perhaps become useless.

To be this greedy for variety was especially unwise.

In response, Luo Chen shrugged.

"It is always good to understand a little first. Besides, I am not saying I absolutely must learn it. If I understand it, then should I encounter a Sage enemy in the future, at least I will have targeted countermeasures."

"Then you are overthinking things."

Han Zhan snorted lightly. "The so-called Sage domain is control over the spiritual qi of heaven and earth. It possesses absolute suppression over lower-level cultivators. Even if you know of it, you cannot specifically break it."

Luo Chen did not understand and asked again.

Seeing that he looked as if he would ask until the end, Han Zhan simply began explaining to him what a Sage domain was.

"Golden Core cultivators possess natural spiritual power suppression over Foundation Establishment cultivators because mana is a higher-level advancement of spiritual power."

"Once a cultivator advances to the Sage stage, the core shatters and the Nascent Soul forms. After passing lightning tribulation, that newly born Nascent Soul receives the baptism of heaven and earth's spiritual qi, and naturally becomes intimate with heaven and earth. Because of this, it possesses control over corresponding attribute spiritual qi within a certain region."

"Once this control is fully unleashed, it forms the so-called Sage domain."

"Within this domain, only the Sage cultivator's mana and the external spiritual qi of the same attribute exist. All other low-level cultivators will suffer the double suppression of both the Sage Perfected Being and heaven and earth. Life and death lie only in the Sage Perfected Being's thought."

"You do not have a Nascent Soul and cannot echo the external spiritual qi of the same attribute. How would you arrange a domain?"

So that was how it was!

Through Han Zhan's deep explanation, Luo Chen finally understood what a Sage domain was.

He also understood why only Perfected Beings could contend with Perfected Beings!

In the cultivation world, examples of crossing ranks to kill enemies were numerous, but most were cases of crossing minor realms. Those who could cross major realms to kill were few and far between.

And among those few and far between examples, there had never been a single case of a Golden Core crossing ranks to kill a Sage. Merely escaping with one's life was already enough to be passed down through the world.

A domain was an absolute suppression higher than spiritual pressure!

But Luo Chen still had doubts.

He brought up the phenomenon of attracting the spiritual qi of heaven and earth when he recently studied the water-fire Two Modes Array.

Han Zhan laughed despite himself. "Formidable formations can naturally control the spiritual qi of heaven and earth for the formation's use, because many formidable formations were comprehended by predecessors from the great momentum of heaven and earth. This has nothing to do with the person."

Luo Chen shook his head. "No. I once saw a cultivator from the soul-refinement lineage of the Primordial Demon Sect personally display a kind of domain. That person was only early Golden Core, yet by relying on this domain, he independently resisted me and another early Golden Core cultivator."

This time, Han Zhan was truly somewhat shocked.

He hurriedly asked in detail about the specific circumstances of that battle.

After Luo Chen explained carefully, he sank into thought.

After a long while, Han Zhan finally sighed in a low voice, "Perhaps this is the foundation of an Ascendant Holy Land. To think they can let a Golden Core cultivator grasp a pseudo-domain similar to a Sage domain."

"Can I do it?"

"You..."

Han Zhan hesitated somewhat, then analyzed carefully, "If it truly is as you said, then that remnant of the Primordial Demon Sect clearly relied on a formation, the Myriad Souls Banner, and a powerful Ghost King that seemed neither human nor demon to arrange that pseudo-domain."

"I also have the Myriad Souls Banner. The souls inside the banner are no fewer than that person's back then, and I have even learned that person's Soul-Fusion Secret Art!"

"But you do not have the corresponding formation!"

"What about the Two Modes Array?"

"Your Two Modes Array takes the meaning of water and fire, and requires Black King's assistance. Moreover, you obviously control it with great difficulty. My Two Modes Array back then took the meaning of life and death and relied on a Sage-stage puppet I had refined, so I could command it like my own arm."

"You mean I need to borrow power that completely belongs to myself?"

"Mm. According to my deduction, that strange Ghost King the Primordial Demon remnant fused with should have been the product of his split soul, so he could control the pseudo-domain."

"Split soul..."

This time, Luo Chen was truly stumped.

The art of splitting the soul was a true immortal method. Without a great sect and without being a Sage Perfected Being, one could not grasp it.

One could even say only powerhouses proficient in the divine soul path could split the soul.

Luo Chen prided himself on his powerful divine soul, but he still did not dare split his soul.

Thinking of splitting the soul, he subconsciously looked at Han Zhan.

Falling Cloud Sect, among the Three Arts and Two Techniques, the Soul-Splitting Art!

"Do not think of targeting me again. That is a core sect inheritance method, not comparable to Origin Pill Art or Bead-Locking Curtain. Do not even think about it!"

With only one look, Han Zhan understood what Luo Chen was plotting in his heart.

After spending so many years together, if this boy twisted his backside, Han Zhan knew what sort of fart he was about to release.

Therefore, at the first moment, he extinguished Luo Chen's thoughts.

Luo Chen curled his lip and did not entangle himself much in this matter.

After asking some further details about Sage domains, he returned in low spirits.

...

After returning from Han Zhan's place, although Luo Chen still occasionally thought about Sage domains and constantly flipped through classics, his mind gradually set the matter down in the end.

The urgent matter was still to thoroughly grasp the formations related to refining magic treasures.

Otherwise, after the treasure embryo finished nurturing and emerged, if the formation side lagged behind, that would be laughable.

Although a magic treasure embryo could be stored for a long time and refined further years later, Luo Chen clearly could not wait that long.

Or rather, he had already waited for this treasure for far, far too long!

After arranging the water-fire Two Modes Array together with Black King, Luo Chen's understanding of this formation path deepened further and further.

Clearly, he had already achieved the step Han Zhan had spoken of, comprehending the formation's true meaning.

His subsequent practice also became increasingly smooth.

Three years later.

Inside Star Dou Hall, Luo Chen looked at the system panel falling like a water curtain, and satisfaction appeared in his eyes.

[Two Modes Array Great Perfection, Six Armors Array Great Perfection]

The most central Two Modes Six Armors Array had all reached its peak.

Not only that, even the third-tier Ruyi Array commonly used in artifact-forging had reached Great Perfection after years of practice and use.

As for the other small formations, there was no need to mention them.

Although among these formations, most were used for artifact-forging and could not be used on the two ends of attack and defense, Luo Chen was still enough to be called a third-tier formation master.

"In this state, engraving the treasure embryo should be foolproof."

After sighing inwardly, Luo Chen rose and went to the Pill-Artifact Hall.

He carefully lifted the qi barrier and took out the treasure embryo enveloped in his mana.

At first glance, it was a mass of hazy spiritual light. Looking closely, it was a refined suit of armor-clothing.

Though refined, it did not lose its heavy solidity.

The essence of the black turtle had been preserved by Luo Chen.

The dense treasure qi, even before formations had been engraved, made one feel this was a resplendent treasure. It was difficult to imagine what it would be like after truly emerging into the world.

Holding the treasure embryo, Luo Chen's eyes were filled with incomparable anticipation.

"Begin!"

...

The northern district of Longyuan Immortal City had originally been the most desolate district within the immortal city, but over these years, its popularity had instead grown vigorous.

The main reason was probably the sudden rise of a building named Azure Yang Treasure Pavilion [TERM?]!

Rumor said this pavilion was the property of a Golden Core superior of profound strength and mastery in artifact-forging within the Myriad Immortals Assembly.

From time to time, he would place the results of his artifact-forging in the shop for sale.

Not only did wandering cultivators flock to it, even people from Divine Origin City, including Golden Core and Foundation Establishment cultivators, would occasionally come to hunt for treasures.

But very clearly, Azure Yang Treasure Pavilion's rapid development was not only because of this one reason.

The Coral Sea Zhou clan had exerted great effort in this matter!

Previously, when the Zhou clan, Chen clan, and Wuzhong Valley, three Sage forces, joined the Myriad Immortals Assembly, their purpose had been to huddle together for warmth. Yet under the requirements of the Three Great Wanderers, they were also forbidden from encroaching on the Myriad Immortals Assembly's interests.

After years of planning, Wuzhong Valley gradually broke into the Myriad Immortals Assembly's interior and obtained the important position of deputy city lord of Longyuan Immortal City.

The Zhou clan was unwilling to fall behind. By marriage alliance with a family founded by a Myriad Immortals Assembly wandering cultivator, and using Azure Yang Hall as a channel, they pushed their own goods into the Myriad Immortals Assembly's wandering cultivator groups.

Now, Azure Yang Treasure Pavilion was nominally Daoist Azure Yang's property, but the core was operated by people from the Zhang family and Zhou clan. The law devices and magic treasures refined by Daoist Azure Yang no longer occupied such a large share within it, and were merely a signboard.

Heavenjade had once reported this matter of occupying the nest to Luo Chen.

In response, Luo Chen had only smiled and let it pass.

Once the cake grew larger, there would always be people who came to divide it.

He did not care who the cake belonged to, as long as the spirit stones sent up each year became more and more numerous.

Moreover, as for the so-called Azure Yang Hall and Azure Yang Treasure Pavilion, he had not invested much into them to begin with.

On this day.

Zhou Shanshan, the Zhang family's matriarch, personally came to Azure Yang Treasure Pavilion to inspect her Zhang family property.

Everywhere she passed, servants and maids were fully respectful, and passing customers greeted her warmly.

In a daze, the influence Wen Xiu had left behind years ago had already vanished.

Zhou Shanshan was very satisfied with such a scene.

That Wen Xiu was merely a female cultivator from a fallen small clan. After Zhou Shanshan entered and took charge of the Cailian Island Zhang family, Wen Xiu had voluntarily yielded her position to the capable, which could be considered sensible.

If not for Wen Xiu taking a wandering cultivator named Lord Lushan as master and gaining that backer, Zhou Shanshan would long ago have reduced her daughter Zhang Jingqing's treatment in the Zhang family.

In any case, Zhang Jiadi, that father, would not fuss with her over such a small matter.

Sitting on the second floor, listening to the shopkeeper report recent profits to her, Zhou Shanshan was both happy and somewhat dissatisfied.

"The lion's share is taken by Azure Yang and the Zhou clan. When it comes to my Zhang family, what remains is merely a fingernail's worth. How unfair!"

Feeling gloomy, she pushed open the window.

By coincidence, she heard an argument below between a steward and a Foundation Establishment cultivator.

"It was agreed before that this information was worth one hundred thousand spirit stones. Why does it no longer count now?"

"Before was before, now is now. How can you compare the two?"

Zhou Shanshan frowned and summoned the shopkeeper.

"What are they arguing about?"

The shopkeeper respectfully said, "Please wait a moment, Madam. I will go ask."

"Mm, go!"

The shopkeeper hurried away and hurried back.

"Reporting to Madam, this wandering cultivator brought back news of a spirit medicine and wants to exchange it for one hundred thousand lower-grade spirit stones. But this task was issued more than ten years ago by Wen... by Madam Wen, and no one knows whether it still counts now."

"That woman issued it?" Zhou Shanshan frowned.

Subconsciously, she wanted to deny it.

But verbally, she still asked one more question.

"What spirit medicine?"

"A spirit medicine called Purple Monkey Flower. We do not know its exact use."

"Purple Monkey Flower?"

Zhou Shanshan blinked, recalling a medicinal material recorded in the Zhou clan's classics.

"Is that not a rather famous poison flower from ancient times? Could Wen Xiu be cultivating poison arts, or planning to use this poison flower to harm someone? Otherwise, why issue a task for it?"

The shopkeeper smiled bitterly. "I do not know either. This lowly one only transferred here from the Zhou clan in recent years."

Zhou Shanshan waved her hand.

"One hundred thousand spirit stones, do not give them! Send that person away."

The shopkeeper immediately accepted the order and left.

After he left, Zhou Shanshan curled her little mouth.

"These wandering cultivators are poor and crazy. They want to exchange a single piece of information for one hundred thousand spirit stones. Wishful thinking! That Wen Xiu also does not know how to manage a household. How could the Zhang family squander wealth like this?"

At nightfall.

Zhang Jiadi, who had just returned from drinking with a low-level alchemist with whom he cooperated closely, was called away by Azure Yang Treasure Pavilion's steward before he even entered the door.

...

"You go in first. This person will remain outside for now."

Zhang Jiadi straightened his clothes and stepped into the Pill-Artifact Hall with a trace of nervousness.

The moment he entered, a wave of hot fire qi struck him so strongly he could barely breathe.

He blinked hard before he could clearly see the white-robed Daoist sitting cross-legged beneath the giant cauldron.

"Zhang Jiadi greets my lord!"

"I hear you brought back news of Purple Monkey Flower, one of the three spirit medicines I require?" Luo Chen asked without turning his head.

Zhang Jiadi lowered his head. "The specific information is still with that wandering cultivator. I did indeed bring the person back, but the reward has not yet been paid."

"Has the information leaked?"

"No. After all, the spirit stones have not been given to him yet, and no one else knows."

"You did well."

As he spoke, a large pill bottle flew to Zhang Jiadi's side.

"This is second-tier upper-grade Thick Earth Pill [TERM?]. It suits your cultivation very well. Take it!"

Zhang Jiadi's eyes brightened. Wild with joy, he hurriedly accepted the large pill bottle, and with a slight perception, discovered there were fully one hundred pills inside.

If he used them sparingly, they would be enough for two or three years.

"You may go. Leave that person behind."

After Zhang Jiadi left, Heavenjade walked in first and told Luo Chen everything that had happened earlier at Azure Yang Treasure Pavilion.

That treasure pavilion steward was a wandering cultivator Wen Xiu had promoted in the early years.

After Wen Xiu left, he had been suppressed by the newly arrived Zhou clan members and could only handle matters involving wandering cultivators.

Wandering cultivators, after all, were both poor and full of problems, the most troublesome, and there was not much profit to be had.

However, this steward had secretly become Zhang Jiadi's confidant and monitored Azure Yang Treasure Pavilion for him.

Therefore, as soon as there was news, he immediately notified Zhang Jiadi.

After learning the inside story, Luo Chen's expression remained as still as an ancient well, though verbally he sighed twice.

"In the past, I only thought Zhang Jiadi was unfit for great use, merely someone who clung to power. Now it seems that as the people and matters he encounters become more and more high-level, he has indeed grown somewhat."

On the surface, the current Zhang family was managed in good order by Zhou Shanshan, and everything took her, the matriarch, as the center.

But in reality, the broad direction was still controlled by Zhang Jiadi.

Especially in matters involving Luo Chen, Zhang Jiadi still did not slack off in the slightest.

Very clearly, he still understood whose breath he relied on to survive.

"Call that person in!"

"Mm."

Heavenjade went out and soon brought in a young Foundation Establishment cultivator.

The moment he entered, the young cultivator bowed. "Wandering cultivator Xu Wang [TERM?] greets Azure Yang!"

Luo Chen had his back to him, but his spilling divine sense still took in all the reverence and fear on his face.

"A young man of promise. For a wandering cultivator to reach Foundation Establishment at your age, you are quite good!"

Receiving Luo Chen's praise, Xu Wang's face finally showed joy.

"Thank you for the praise, Superior. This junior only obtained a predecessor's opportunity and found a Foundation Establishment Pill in a ruin cave residence, luckily succeeding by chance."

"Ruin cave residence?"

"Mm. Inside it was the specific information on that Purple Monkey Flower. According to the rumor, deep in the Demon Sea, on an island named Purple Spirit Island [TERM?], a large number of Purple Qi Poison Monkeys [TERM?] reside. Wherever Purple Qi Poison Monkeys are found, Purple Monkey Flowers will surely grow."

"What was that opportunity of yours like?"

"One scroll of cultivation method, three bottles of precious pills, one magic treasure, and three miscellaneous books. The information on the Purple Monkey Flower came from one of those miscellaneous books, and it also included a map."

"Have you told anyone else about this matter?"

"No, apart from Steward Yao of Azure Yang Treasure Pavilion."

Question and answer continued without pause.

Later, Xu Wang's speech gradually became somewhat broken, and there seemed to be pain in his eyes.

Suddenly.

A clear spiritual qi fell over him, accompanied by a storage bag.

"Inside are one hundred thousand lower-grade spirit stones."

Xu Wang shook his head hard, waking from that hazy, uncontrollable feeling, and subconsciously reached out to receive the storage bag.

Yet halfway there, he encountered a resistance and found it difficult to advance even an inch.

"Superior?"

"Leave the miscellaneous book behind. In addition, swear a Dao oath. This matter must never be spread for your entire life."

A Great Dao oath was different from an ordinary promise. Unless one intended to completely tear off all pretense and disregard one's Dao path, generally no one dared violate it.

Especially a young and promising person had to weigh his own future even more.

Xu Wang swallowed. For the sake of these one hundred thousand lower-grade spirit stones, he swore a Great Dao oath.

Afterward, after handing over that miscellaneous book, he smoothly obtained the storage bag containing one hundred thousand lower-grade spirit stones.

"You may leave."

"Um, Superior, may I ask you to refine a..."

"Go back!"

Xu Wang trembled all over and no longer dared make further requests. Clutching the storage bag, he left Peng Lake under Heavenjade's lead.

After a long while.

Heavenjade returned again to the Pill-Artifact Hall.

The moment she entered, she saw a withered-yellow book automatically turning pages before Luo Chen.

"Master, is the news of Purple Monkey Flower true or false?"

"It should be true. From the moment Xu Wang entered, I used illusion arts to disturb his mind. Every question and answer came from his heart, with absolutely no intent to fake anything."

Luo Chen looked at the miscellaneous book and spoke without turning his head.

With his attainments in illusion arts, even if he could not be as domineering as demonic soul-searching arts, a mere cultivator newly entered into Foundation Establishment could still be handled however he liked.

It was only that during the stage of swearing a Great Dao oath, the cultivator himself had to be clear-minded. Otherwise, Luo Chen could even control and guide him into swearing many oaths.

Heavenjade hesitated. "Then this book?"

"It has some age to it and is not forged. I imagine it truly was written by someone who liked traveling through the Demon Sea."

Generally, those who accomplished something in cultivation liked to write books and establish theories.

It was not merely to show off, but also to sort through what they had gained in their own cultivation.

Luo Chen was also like this. Over these years, the alchemy insights, artifact-forging insights, and cultivation insights he had casually written already numbered more than both hands could count.

If his great limit drew near, perhaps he would also write one or two travelogues to commemorate the places he had visited and the scenery and interesting matters he had seen in this life.

Finally, Heavenjade asked curiously, "Then will Master go to that Purple Spirit Island?"

This time, Luo Chen shook his head slightly.

"In the short term, I cannot go out."

Heavenjade did not understand.

Luo Chen sighed. "Although I have successfully refined this Demon Emperor shed husk, the fit between the formation and it is not as smooth as I imagined. Therefore, I need to spend at least a year stabilizing its formation."

Hearing this answer, Heavenjade was rather surprised.

In Master's past artifact-forging, it seemed this sort of thing had never happened.

How could Luo Chen not think the same?

In truth, this time he had finally and thoroughly groped out the upper limit of the Primordial Unity Cauldron's enhancement to artifact-forging.

Lower-grade magic treasures had almost no obstruction.

Middle-grade magic treasures also had fifty-fifty odds.

But when it involved upper-grade magic treasures, the nurturing effect it produced became negligible.

Yet this Demon Emperor shed husk's own grade was too high, and the formation he wanted to arrange was also too powerful. Therefore, when the two fused, an extremely strong rejection reaction appeared.

If not for the fact that he had nourished it long enough, and that his formation attainments had reached their peak in advance, the engraving at that time would have failed at the last step.

But even though he had forcibly engraved the formation, he still needed to constantly comb through the formation patterns afterward.

He had already sat dryly in this Pill-Artifact Hall for a long time.

And one could imagine that next, he would still have to sit dryly for a long time before truly stabilizing the formation on the treasure embryo.

"Then... the Purple Monkey Flower?" Heavenjade asked. Luo Chen had once revealed to her the Purple Monkey Flower's use, which had a tremendous connection to Sage formation.

Such an important treasure; were they simply to wait?

Luo Chen pondered for a long time, thought flickering constantly in his eyes. Finally, he slowly spoke.

"Go call Black King here!"

*(End of Chapter)*
