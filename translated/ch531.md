# Chapter 531: Destroy Nantian, Take Swallow, All Cultivators Submit!

---

The Mystic Flame Sword entered his hand. As Luo Chen said, "You came at just the right time," the sword tip slanted upward.

A streak of flowing light appeared, then abruptly expanded into a blazing sun that descended over Yan Nantian's head at the critical instant.

Blazing Sun Art.

After that blazing sun smashed down, Luo Chen did not even look to see the result. His left hand rose upright, five fingers continuously bending to form seals, while the longsword in his right hand pointed and traced through the void. Incantations rolled from his mouth, profound sounds trembling.

When the Blazing Sun Art exploded, a battered figure finally entered Luo Chen's eyes.

Two blue water dragons, their bodies shrunk by thirty percent, separated from Yan Nantian on the left and right and charged toward Luo Chen.

Luo Chen paid no attention to the two water dragons. Two black lights flew from his body and likewise rushed toward them from left and right.

As for Luo Chen himself, he slowly pressed the Mystic Flame Sword downward.

"Mountain Collapse."

In the void, an illusory mountain formed of mana slowly pressed down.

Within his eyes, the withered, yellow-faced man had blood at the corners of his mouth and torn robes.

He stared at Luo Chen with eyes full of ferocity. Holding a long black banner, he shook it upward.

"Myriad Souls Formation, rise!"

The endless black fog that had previously been broken apart immediately let out tens of thousands of shrieks, rising from the ground and rushing toward the illusory mana mountain.

In an instant, boundless black fog transformed into a black dragon, roaring hideously.

Black dragon and fiery mountain collided in a flash.

The great mountain held for a breath, then abruptly collapsed.

Yan Nantian grinned savagely. "A petty trick, and you dare show it off."

Amid his sneer, the long banner shook even more madly. The black dragon condensed from fog roared to the heavens, intending to sweep away all mountain force in one stroke.

At this, Luo Chen gave a cold laugh.

A shallow-sighted fool.

The Mountain Collapse art was meant to collapse.

Only then would its power be revealed.

The next moment, from within the collapsing mana mountain, layers of flowing firelight surged forth like waves, like magma, rolling down and completely enveloping both the black dragon and Yan Nantian.

Not only that.

The Blazing Fire Miasma previously mixed into the Myriad Souls Grand Formation now erupted with all its baleful qi. Streams of filthy balefulness spread, destroying the black dragon's composition from within.

Especially wherever a wisp of gray-white light passed, the dragon body formed from black fog disintegrated inch by inch.

Yan Nantian finally sensed something wrong.

But within such a situation, he was already riding a tiger and could not dismount. He could only drive the Myriad Souls Banner with all his strength and endure this wave.

Splitting his focus, Luo Chen glanced to both sides.

Those two black lights and the blue water dragons had already determined victory after several collisions.

The Qi-Breaking Nail and Soul-Breaking Nail had each nailed into a dragon head, producing clanging impacts from within.

"This should be the Water-Parting Spike, one of Yan Nantian's three treasures. It indeed has the might to part water and transform into dragons."

After one look and seeing the two black nails were evenly matched, Luo Chen stopped paying attention.

His left hand abruptly fixed before his chest, no longer forming seals.

On the Mystic Flame Sword in his right hand, clusters of flowing light began to tremble one after another.

Every tremble represented a vast surge of mana flowing from Luo Chen's body.

With such massive mana stacked together, Luo Chen could faintly hear the body of this low-grade treasure flying sword, whose quality was not very good, letting out mournful cries.

"Nine suns is the limit. Any more, and this sword will be unable to withstand my tyrannical mana."

Luo Chen sighed inwardly, then tapped the sword tip through the night sky.

Every tap produced a faint flash of light.

Every tap pointed in a different direction.

Before long, Luo Chen stopped moving the Mystic Flame Sword, and the sword's mournful cries finally ceased.

At the same time, the battle below had only just ended.

At some point, Yan Nantian had been forced to the lowest point. His feet stood on empty air above the water, his beard and hair curled and charred as though roasted.

The robes on him were even more ragged, exposing most of his bare chest. Scars covered it, showing how many life-and-death battles he had experienced in his life.

His hand was trembling.

The long banner in his hand was no longer a profound black. The black fog shrouding it had thinned by more than half.

He looked at Luo Chen with alarm and uncertainty. "What spell was that?"

"That art is Mountain Collapse. Merely second-tier."

Facing his question, Luo Chen answered without hurry, as though everything remained under control.

Yan Nantian's eyes widened. "A mere second-tier spell? How could it break the Myriad Souls Formation of my Myriad Souls Banner?"

Luo Chen smiled faintly. "Spells are divided into higher and lower grades, but their power has always depended on the caster's own ability. Just like your Myriad Souls Banner. If it were in the hands of a true expert from the Primordial Demon Sect's soul-refining lineage, it should display several times more power than it does with you. Fellow Daoist, do you not understand such a simple principle?"

Yan Nantian's expression hardened.

He had to admit that Luo Chen's words were not false.

But all along his path, he had almost never had quiet time or space to cultivate spells. After finally obtaining a hundred years to breathe, he had focused on raising his realm and had not even been able to spare too much energy to refine his treasures.

He understood the principle, but what could he do about it now?

He pressed his lips tightly together. "One last question. What is your name?"

He did not ask Luo Chen's purpose, nor his origins, nor any grudge. He only wanted to know Luo Chen's name.

After asking, he did not wait for an answer.

The long banner in his hand rolled out, wrapping him layer upon layer. Then he opened his mouth and spat out a small blue sword.

The small sword emitted light, enveloping him together with it, then abruptly rushed toward Luo Chen.

Facing this scene, Luo Chen still wore a faint smile.

How could he fail to see that the other party had been recovering breath and sorting the meridians within his body just now, seeking to mobilize more mana from his Golden Core?

But he had still cooperated with the man's little act and chatted.

Because he too had been waiting.

"I am Azure Yang. Fellow Daoist, walk well on the road to Yellow Springs."

With a clear whistle, Luo Chen exhaled a long stream of white qi.

The next moment, nine faint lights in the night sky erupted at once, transforming into enormous fireballs like blazing suns.

In an instant, nine suns crossed the sky, surrounding the blue light.

"Explode."

Boom!

Clearly, it was the detonation of nine Blazing Sun Arts. Yet in this moment, only a single sound existed between heaven and earth.

In the past, Luo Chen could also casually cast Blazing Sun Art instantly, and could even release eight or nine in succession.

But when detonating them, he usually did so one after another in sequence.

This time, however, nine suns crossed the sky, and nine suns exploded together.

The power erupting in that instant actually surpassed many third-tier spells.

With the black fog gone, the ten-li calm lake no longer reflected pale moonlight. This time, it reflected a crimson great sun.

Terrifying mana fluctuations first contracted inward, then suddenly expanded outward.

Vast waves of air, like a tsunami, pushed in layer after layer toward every direction, their range constantly widening.

Ten li, twenty li, thirty li... judging by their momentum, they seemed ready to affect a thousand li.

The Swallow Islands cultivators watching the battle were shaken to the core. Some screamed and retreated madly.

They feared being harmed by the battle's aftermath.

Cheng Dou, responsible for maintaining the Water Mirror Art, also changed expression greatly, unsure whether to retreat or remain in place.

Cheng Haixin, Cheng Haichang, and the others who had already fled farther back watched the hesitating Cheng Dou and hurriedly shouted, "Big Brother, retreat!"

Hearing this voice, Cheng Dou, who had been hesitating, instead gritted his teeth and hardened his heart.

In this battle, Luo Chen had given him only this one task. How could he retreat just because he wished to?

His hands formed seals constantly, and light shields appeared one after another before him.

In only a few breaths, the terrifying air waves had already swept to his side.

However.

Just as they were about to strike Cheng Dou, the air waves stopped.

Only gust after gust of wild wind blew the hair tied behind the man's back into chaotic flight.

Cheng Dou's face was pale. With difficulty, he raised his head to look at the water mirror above.

Ripples spread across its surface.

Yet the image within remained vivid and lifelike.

At this, his heart held only shock. Devil Lord Azure Yang's spell control was terrifying.

Whether the explosive range of that fire spell or the sturdy Water Mirror Art he had left behind, both had undoubtedly reached the peak attainment of their respective spells.

"Huu..."

He let out a long breath and turned back to look at the cultivators, who were as startled as birds.

"Everyone, this distance is the best viewing distance chosen by Devil Lord Azure Yang. If you do not trust me, can it be that you cannot trust the Devil Lord?"

Everyone looked at one another, speechless for a time.

Just then, a young, delicate cry came from the crowd.

"Danger!"

Within the water mirror, beneath the nine-sun explosion that seemed capable of destroying all things, a smear of blue light broke free and shot straight toward Luo Chen.

...

Above the ten-li calm lake, Luo Chen raised a brow.

The Mystic Flame Sword behind him switched to his left hand. His right hand abruptly extended, fingers together like a sword, and at the critical instant, he caught that sword tip in midair.

Ding.

"A decent life-bound treasure flying sword. Unfortunately, it is already an arrow at the end of its flight."

As he murmured, the blue flying sword disintegrated inch by inch, turning into fragments that fell into the exposed mud at the bottom of the lake.

Luo Chen's gaze had already turned in another direction.

Under the night sky, a long banner with dim spiritual light wrapped around a Golden Core and fled madly in one direction.

Under that nine-sun explosion, even though Yan Nantian's methods were extraordinary, and even though he relied on the Myriad Souls Banner to protect himself, he still could not withstand that terrifying explosive power.

His physical body had been completely destroyed.

Only a Golden Core remained, clinging to life, trying to flee.

With the Northern Sea cultivation world's demonic style, even with only a Golden Core left, there were still many methods to make a comeback.

But how could Luo Chen do something like release a tiger back to the mountain?

His body shook violently, and a pair of wings suddenly appeared behind him.

The wings trembled. After several flickers, he was already several li away, directly in front of the Soul-Refining Banner.

Luo Chen's great hand clawed out.

Within the Myriad Souls Banner, a Ghost King roared and charged out, trying to block the grab.

But Luo Chen ignored it. His great hand abruptly transformed into a claw. It was the Cloud-Seizing Divine Claw.

Endless azure flames coiled around the giant claw, which forcibly pierced through the Ghost King's soul body and seized the Golden Core within the banner.

"Fellow Daoist, spare m..."

Crack.

The azure-blue Golden Core instantly split into pieces.

Dense mana poured in every direction.

The remnant soul and broken spirit fell into the Myriad Souls Banner, where the remaining resentful souls and fierce ghosts madly bit and devoured them.

Luo Chen raised a brow, grabbed the Myriad Souls Banner, and sent his divine sense inside.

"The host is dead, and ten thousand ghosts backlash."

"Good."

After saying good, Luo Chen held the Myriad Souls Banner and drifted lightly back to the original location of Invitation Moon Island in the ten-li lake.

He reached out, and two streaks of light immediately flew from the lake.

Looking closely, they were the Soul-Breaking and Qi-Breaking black nails, as well as a pair of Water-Parting Spikes swept up by the mana attached to the nails.

He first put away the black nails, then grabbed the pair of Water-Parting Spikes.

At this point, the great battle had finally ended.

Yet less than the time to drink a cup of tea had passed.

Luo Chen quietly hovered above the lake, as though waiting for something.

Before long, spiritual power fluctuations rose in every direction. Figures landed around him one after another.

The leader was Cheng Dou.

He first looked at the original environment of Invitation Moon Island.

The island was gone, sunk a thousand zhang into the lakebed.

The ten-li calm lake no longer had a clear surface capable of reflecting anything. Its pitted lakebed was exposed, and large swaths of spirit fish and spirit plants within had died.

In the sky, raindrops fell in pattering lines.

Looking at the devastation before him, Cheng Dou's heart trembled.

The battle of Adept cultivators already nearly possessed the might to sweep seas flat, level lakes, and cut rivers.

And this battle had been under that man's control from beginning to end, without letting its range expand.

If they had truly fought without restraint, the thirty-six Swallow Islands might not have been enough for them to toss around.

Swallowing, Cheng Dou bent deeply at the waist.

"Cheng Dou of Black Swan Island leads the cultivators of the thirty-six Swallow Islands in greeting Devil Lord Azure Yang."

As he bowed in salute, the remaining six Initiate cultivators of the Cheng clan also bent in unison.

"The Devil Lord is mighty!"

Luo Chen's expression did not change. His seemingly calm gaze slowly swept toward the other Initiate true cultivators.

By now, all Initiate cultivators of the Swallow Islands had arrived one after another, numbering around a hundred.

Under his sweeping gaze, everyone who met his eyes could not withstand that pressure and subconsciously lowered their brows and heads.

Every one of them had personally watched this battle through the water mirror.

They had seen how Luo Chen suppressed Senior Nantian from beginning to end in a short span of time, to the point that the other party did not even manage to let a wisp of remnant soul escape.

Such fierce might exceeded even Nantian of the past.

No one knew who called out, "The Devil Lord is mighty!"

After that, a chain reaction followed.

"The Devil Lord is mighty!"

"The Devil Lord is mighty!"

"The Devil Lord is mighty!"

Only a hundred voices, yet when gathered at this moment, they were like a great bell, ringing into the clouds.

The corners of Luo Chen's mouth slowly lifted.

"Everyone, in one month, we meet here again. I hope not one of you will be absent."

His clear voice spread across the wilds and entered everyone's heart.

All who heard it trembled inwardly.

When they raised their heads, Luo Chen's figure was already gone from the night sky.

Before Cheng Dou floated a jade slip emitting red light.

Cheng Dou drew a deep breath and reached out to grasp the jade slip. Streams of information flowed out.

This was a formation for gathering spirit qi and stabilizing veins.

He looked at the devastated, ruined Invitation Moon Island and understood Luo Chen's meaning.

"One month. Clean up the aftermath and gather the third-tier spirit vein here that was scattered by the battle."

"Fellow Daoist Xu, Brother Wang, Elder Fu, I fear I must trouble the three of you with this matter."

"As for everyone else, those with money contribute money; those with strength contribute strength."

Everyone looked at one another.

After a great battle, how could the third-tier spirit vein here, which had not been particularly high-grade to begin with, endure?

Naturally, it had already been scattered.

If they wanted to repair and gather the spirit vein anew, the price they had to pay would surely be enormous.

Especially with time so tight.

Naturally, everyone was reluctant.

But when they thought of Azure Yang's demonic might, who dared slack off?

Especially that sentence before he left: "In one month, we meet here again. Not one of you should be absent."

Seeing no one speak, Cheng Dou stepped forward and barked:

"Do you dare disobey?"

With that shout, it was as though Devil Lord Azure Yang stood present. Everyone trembled in unison.

The cultivator surnamed Xu who had spoken with Cheng Dou earlier shook his head and smiled bitterly.

"This old man naturally does not dare."

After he spoke, the "Brother Wang" and "Elder Fu" Cheng Dou had named could only sigh and accept this task.

The three of them were the most formidable formation masters among the thirty-six Swallow Islands.

The island-linking formation method usually passed down by the Cang Hai Alliance was mostly maintained by them.

Once these three yielded beneath Devil Lord Azure Yang's oppressive might, the others also lost any thought of resistance and all expressed willingness to help.

Seeing this, Cheng Dou's tense expression finally loosened.

The corners of his mouth lifted. At last, he could no longer hold back and revealed a bright, unrestrained smile.

This time, he had won the gamble.

From now on, so long as Devil Lord Azure Yang remained in the Swallow Islands for a day, his Cheng clan would be the number one cultivation clan in the archipelago.

Nothing was more fitting than borrowing the tiger's might.

As long as he later succeeded in Core Formation, then even after Devil Lord Azure Yang left, his Cheng clan could still flourish.

Sensing Yan Nantian's lingering aura in this place, he laughed even more wildly.

The sea could not be measured by the dou. How could he, Cheng Dou, spend his whole life beneath others?

Yan Nantian could not suppress him, and Devil Lord Azure Yang was only a transition. One day, he, Cheng Dou, would become the true lord of Swallow in name and reality.

That day would not be far.
