# Chapter 796: Knife-Cutting the Nascent Soul, Heavenjade Returns (October Guaranteed Monthly Ticket Request)

Watching that infant speaking human words, Luo Chen’s pupils contracted.

A cultivator’s Nascent Soul was something that returned from the acquired to the innate, the purest thing emerging from the mud. Thus, they were always plump and white, utterly adorable!

Even the Nascent Souls of the elderly and decrepit had a certain childlike charm.

But such a deformed and terrifying Nascent Soul truly made him unable to suppress a frown.

He knew this was caused by the opponent cultivating a special technique.

Moreover, it was the kind of extremely powerful special technique that would produce such changes.

Ordinary techniques could at most affect a cultivator’s physique and appearance.

Like him, Luo Chen, who had an epiphany on Heaven-Earth Peak back then and created the *Withering-Bloom Dao Scripture* on his own, causing his Nascent Soul to become aged—yet in reality, there was no internal change.

For someone like Nine Spirit Primordial Lord, covered in tumors all over his body, like a monster…

“Eh?”

“So that’s it!”

After carefully examining those nine tumors, Luo Chen finally understood the reason for the opponent’s mana being so vast and unusual compared to ordinary Nascent Soul cultivators.

Every single tumor was almost pure solidified mana!

The total amount of mana in the nine tumors even exceeded that of the Nascent Soul itself!

Seeing this, Luo Chen also understood why the opponent was so desperate for the Nine Separation Pill.

Just like someone doing weighted training to increase their strength limit, Nine Spirit Primordial Lord used the method of externally growing nine tumors to horizontally expand his mana limit, thus achieving a vast mana that looked down upon those of the same rank.

But those who do weighted training, once the weight they bear exceeds their limit, not only can they not improve themselves, but it becomes a burden, even backlashing against them.

At this moment, the mana contained in Nine Spirit Primordial Lord’s nine tumors had already exceeded that of the Nascent Soul itself!

If this continued, then his Nascent Soul would either be unable to bear the weight and collapse, or be assimilated into the tumors, leaving no future chance for Nascent Soul to Ascendant!

“If I really help him refine the Nine Separation Pill and separate the nine tumors. Because they share the same origin, these nine tumors will not only stop affecting him, but will instead become his nine reserves of mana.”

“If there are subsequent advancement methods, his upper limit would be even more immeasurable!”

“Once he achieves the late Nascent Soul stage, Nine Spirit Primordial Lord’s mana might instantly surpass those long-established Grand Cultivators!”

After understanding all this, Luo Chen couldn’t help but feel some curiosity about the techniques of that Nine Spirit Sect.

What kind of secret art could incorporate the extreme Nine Heavens Spiritual Essence into the body and transform it into an appendage attached to the Nascent Soul?

Nine Heavens Spiritual Essence was Xia Qi!

This substance was extremely overbearing. Even Luo Chen dared not let his Nascent Soul come into contact with it, and could only refine it before using his own extremely powerful Yuantu Sword to bear it.

No wonder Nine Spirit Primordial Lord understood Xia Qi so well—he himself used this kind of extreme material for cultivation!

But curiosity was merely curiosity.

Having been entrusted with a task, one must be faithful to it. Luo Chen could not go digging for the roots, otherwise, his reputation as an Alchemy Saint would probably not last a few days.

Clearing his throat, Luo Chen broke the silence.

“Extracting Nascent Soul essence blood may be somewhat painful. You’ll need to endure for a moment.”

Nine Spirit Primordial Lord said calmly, “Do as you must!”

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow and wasted no more time.

With a flick of his hand, a crystalline, almost transparent small knife appeared in his grasp.

It was that knife made entirely of Profound Heaven Crystal, the same one used every time he cut the Five Elements Lotus Platform before.

The moment this knife appeared, Nine Spirit Primordial Lord’s Nascent Soul instinctively became wary.

A cultivator’s Nascent Soul was the seat of their entire Dao fruit—how could they allow a blade to be laid upon it?

Luo Chen did not force the issue, but slowly extended the knife.

He wanted to use his reputation as an Alchemy Saint to build a wide network, and it was inevitable that he would come into contact with many people’s secrets.

For example, that Divine Transformation great demon of the White Crane Sect had failed its transformation.

For example, who was the cultivator of the Divine Talisman Sect about to make a push for the Nascent Soul realm, and even that Talisman Elder secretly possessed a Nascent Soul Pill recipe.

Another example was now, Nine Spirit Primordial Lord’s own predicament, and even his possible future achievements.

When his reputation grew even greater and more people came to him for pill refining, he might learn that someone had a hidden ailment, or that so-and-so would use pills to cultivate some secret art, and so on.

Under such circumstances, he had to be like a doctor—a “harmless” person.

I know your hidden ailments, and I also know others’ secrets, but my reputation is here. You can be unreserved with me, at least when seeking pills, you must be open and honest!

If I really took the opportunity to harm someone, then all the reputation I had painstakingly accumulated before would be completely wasted.

Even with the ability of an Alchemy Saint, no one would acknowledge it, and no one would approach me anymore.

Closer, closer!

When the Heavenly Crystal small knife was about to touch his body, the Nascent Soul’s little eyes bulged wide, but in the end, he made no move to resist.

He chose to trust Luo Chen.

Or rather, he chose to trust his own judgment—Luo Chen needed that reputation now!

Of course, it couldn’t be ruled out that, under internal and external troubles, Nine Spirit Primordial Lord was simply grasping at straws.

In any case, Luo Chen’s small knife ultimately fell upon one of the Nascent Soul’s tumors.

The Nascent Soul was the product of the unity of the three treasures: essence, qi, and spirit.

To outsiders, its hallmark was vast mana.

In reality, it also contained the cultivator’s essence blood and soul power.

Thus, the Nascent Soul was so peculiar, practically a cultivator’s second life.

What Luo Chen wanted to extract was the Nascent Soul’s essence blood, which essentially meant cutting its flesh and taking its Nascent Soul power.

For him, this was a first, but it was not unfamiliar.

Back then on Green Parasom Mountain, when he was refining Transformation Pills for the demon race, the most important main ingredient was Nascent Soul essence blood.

Only, back then someone else did it for him; now he was doing it himself.

*Chi!*

A flash of brightness, gone in an instant.

The Nascent Soul suddenly trembled, but made no sound.

Luo Chen couldn’t help but silently praise him. This Nine Spirit Primordial Lord truly possessed great perseverance. Cutting the Nascent Soul was such agony, yet he could endure it without making a sound.

Compared to himself, it was probably not far off.

Luo Chen was not one to belittle himself; he was quite confident in this regard.

Ever since the Qi Refinement stage, he had endured the pain of piercing acupoints, and later even forcibly endured the pain of physical nirvana.

So, it was merely a word of approval.

His knife moved like flying, and in the blink of an eye, he had taken the portion of essence blood recorded in the pill recipe.

Because of the speed, Nine Spirit Primordial Lord also suffered a bit less pain.

“Done!”

*Whoosh!*

Without any pause, the Nascent Soul dove back into the physical body. Nine Spirit Primordial Lord slowly opened his eyes, his pupils filled with exhaustion.

“The rest, I leave to you, Fellow Daoist.”

Luo Chen smiled slightly. “This pill recipe is extremely obscure, and you should be clear about the difficulty of refining it, so it may take some time.”

Nine Spirit Primordial Lord nodded his heavy head. “No matter. As long as it’s refined before the demon race breaks through the three front-line domains and reaches the Lingtian Pass, it won’t be too late.”

Luo Chen was somewhat surprised. “Lingtian Pass has deep moats and high walls, countless experts, and is supported by the established sixteen rear domains and the Ascendant sacred grounds. Such a majestic city is second to none in the world. Why are you, Fellow Daoist, so pessimistic?”

The reason Nine Spirit Primordial Lord would take the risk of trusting Luo Chen to refine the Nine Separation Pill, aside from the constraints of his own cultivation, was also the pressure from the demon race outside.

That is to say, in Nine Spirit Primordial Lord’s view, if the demon race really fought their way to Lingtian Pass, even his current strength would not be stable.

Thus, he had to forcibly advance further in a short time!

In response to Luo Chen’s doubt, Nine Spirit Primordial Lord said wearily, “You have never seen the demon army, and do not know the sharpness of their blade. I can only tell you that unless the Nether Origin Sect personally enters the fray, Lingtian Pass alone will absolutely not be able to stop the flood of demon beasts that has been building for three thousand years.”

Luo Chen opened his mouth, wanting to ask more.

But seeing the other’s utterly exhausted face, he could only forcibly suppress his curiosity.

Forcing out so much Nascent Soul essence blood in one go was a considerable loss of vitality for Nine Spirit Primordial Lord.

At this moment, the other only wanted to rest well.

With that in mind, Luo Chen asked no more, but instead took out a white-glowing pill.

“This is a fourth-grade Yu Zhen Pill, a specialty of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect in Central Continent. It has no great use, except that it can briefly suppress the pain of the Nascent Soul. I gift it to you, Fellow Daoist!”

Nine Spirit Primordial Lord looked at the white pill, his gaze slowly shifting to Luo Chen’s youthful face.

He took the pill and said in a low voice, “Thank you!”

Luo Chen smiled, then immediately arranged for someone to take Nine Spirit Primordial Lord to rest.

After the other left, Luo Chen put away his smile and looked at the bronze figure the other had left behind.

The pill recipe on its back was faintly visible, while the nine tumors on its front were shocking. Luo Chen fell into thought.

“Incorporate the Nine Heavens Spiritual Essence into the body to obtain supreme mana.”

“Then separate the nine essences to form nine spiritual infants.”

“Spiritual essence, spiritual infant?”

“What difference is there between this so-called spiritual infant and a second Nascent Soul?”

……

At his peak, Luo Chen had a net worth of several hundred million spirit stones, almost equivalent to the lifetime wealth of a somewhat poor Nascent Soul cultivator.

All that wealth had been exchanged for cultivation resources.

Naturally, it couldn’t all be techniques and medicinal herbs; it also included some auxiliary cultivation items useful for Nascent Soul cultivators.

The Yu Zhen Pill produced by the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect was one of them.

Just as Luo Chen had said, this pill had no great use, except for “pain relief”!

It was a pill specifically used by Heavenly Origin cultivators to temporarily sever the connection between the Nascent Soul and the divine sense when they went astray in their cultivation, to prevent qi deviation.

Giving one to Nine Spirit Primordial Lord did indeed help alleviate his condition considerably.

After resting for a few days at the Luo Tian Sect, he quietly left.

After all, Luo Chen had said that refining the Nine Separation Pill would take a long time, and the Nine Spirit Sect had not been destroyed, so he could not stay away for long.

Before leaving, Nine Spirit Primordial Lord specifically exchanged contact tokens with Luo Chen.

So that they could quickly contact each other when needed.

After he left, Luo Chen’s life returned to the calm and uneventful days of before.

Consolidating his realm, refining the Red Firmament Sword Qi.

However, he set aside a portion of his energy to seriously study the Nine Separation Pill recipe on the back of the bronze figure.

He planned to use the system to directly master this pill recipe, in order to stably refine the Nine Separation Pill and spread the fame of his Alchemy Saint name.

But before mastering it, doing the basic work of thoroughly studying the pill recipe was still necessary.

This was being responsible to Nine Spirit Primordial Lord, and also to himself.

Moreover, Luo Chen was very curious about the other’s mention of “nine spiritual infants.”

The Nine Separation Pill separated the nine Nascent Soul tumors, and then one cultivated nine spiritual infants?

Such a method was truly strange and bizarre.

Looking at the result, it was somewhat like a second Nascent Soul.

But based on Luo Chen’s experience from back when he used Hidden Dust Sand to cultivate the Yuan Dan Art, it was a completely different path.

One relied on external objects, the other sought from within oneself.

In particular, some of the medicinal herbs on the recipe were from the demon realm, which made him suspect that the Nine Separation Pill was a demonic pill, and that the Nine Spirits Entering Sage Method was a demonic technique.

For this reason, while studying the Nine Separation Pill recipe, Luo Chen would from time to time flip through the many alchemy books left by Devil Lord Heaven-Refining, cross-referencing and corroborating.

Half a year later.

After Luo Chen had refined the fourth strand of Red Firmament Sword Qi, he began to set about refining the Nine Separation Pill for Nine Spirit Primordial Lord.

As for the pill recipe, he felt he had grasped it to about eighty or ninety percent.

Even without relying on the system, if he fumbled through it himself, he had a chance of refining the Nine Separation Pill.

But obviously, the raw materials the other had provided could not withstand the consumption of Luo Chen’s experiments.

Therefore…

[Detected improved Nine Separation Pill recipe. Initiate mastery?]

“Master!”

With a thought, light flickered on the panel.

Under the fourth-grade pill column, the entry [Nine Separation Pill Mastery 1/100] slowly appeared.

At that moment, Luo Chen gained full confidence in his ability to successfully refine the Nine Separation Pill!

He wasted no more time and went straight to the pill chamber.

Soon after, Qi Yi also hurried over.

Luo Chen’s divine sense swept over him, and he couldn’t help but show a satisfied expression.

“In half a year’s time, your mana has become much purer. It seems you haven’t been slacking off during this period.”

Qi Yi was grateful and said respectfully, “All thanks to the Grand Supreme’s cultivation. That *Spirit Tempering Art* is truly extraordinary. Although my realm hasn’t improved much, I feel like a completely new person compared to half a year ago.”

Luo Chen waved his hand. “Cultivation is your own affair. There’s no need to thank me. I called you here this time to refine a new pill. You will assist me.”

A new pill?

Qi Yi’s eyes instantly lit up.

Even though high-grade pills were beyond his current ability to refine, just being on the sidelines to assist and observe was immensely beneficial.

Moreover, Luo Chen was not stingy with his guidance and teaching.

Soon, after Luo Chen told him his tasks, he began processing some of the minor medicinal herbs.

As the chief alchemist of the Luo Tian Sect’s Pill Court, Qi Yi still had a bit of a reputation outside.

But at this moment, he was focused on processing the trimmings, looking like a medicine boy. If word got out, it would probably shock everyone.

Qi Yi didn’t care about that at all. To him, being able to study under Luo Chen was already an immense stroke of luck.

Let alone being a medicine boy, even being a fire-watching boy, he would gladly accept it.

Luo Chen was very satisfied with this junior who had the mindset of an apprentice.

“His alchemy talent is inferior to Ling Jun’s, and even slightly worse than my nominal disciple Xu Muxian from the Northern Sea. But his strength lies in an unflagging thirst for knowledge and a willingness to put in the effort.”

“He is worth cultivating properly!”

“After refining the Nine Separation Pill, I’ll give him some resources and let him go into seclusion to cultivate. If he can achieve the Adept realm, he will have great potential in the future.”

For his junior disciples, Luo Chen did not value talent highly, but rather valued temperament.

The three elements of cultivation were talent and aptitude, mindset and character, and opportunity and fortune!

The former could not be sought, the latter was hard to encounter.

Only mindset and character were things a cultivator could control.

Someone with poor aptitude like himself could reach his current state and carve out a place in the vast Eastern Wilderness. Someone of humble origin like Wang Yuan could blaze a new trail and, in an era where body refinement was in decline, forcibly carve out the name of War Demon.

What did they rely on, if not that unyielding and indomitable temperament?

The more Luo Chen looked at Qi Yi, the more he liked him. Originally, he had only thought of letting him assist and learn a bit, in consideration of his years of service to the Luo Tian Sect.

But now, he had already formed the idea of properly cultivating him.

However, the immediate priority was still to refine the Nine Separation Pill.

Luo Chen put away his stray thoughts and focused all his attention on the preparatory work before the formal pill refining.

……

Time flowed slowly.

The Luo Tian Sect enjoyed peaceful days.

The Luo Tian Sect cultivators had gradually grown accustomed to the Grand Supreme Elder never leaving his residence.

Even if Luo Chen did not appear before them, it did not change his lofty status in the hearts of his disciples and followers.

Nowadays, no one came to oppress the Luo Tian Sect anymore. Except for the cultivators of Motian Cliff and the Five Elements Divine Sect, everyone who came was polite and courteous.

They also did not cause trouble, strictly following the orders of Sect Master Li Yingzhang, focusing their energy on cultivation.

Additionally, from time to time, they had to start learning to ride the spirit cranes sent by the White Crane Sect!

All Initiate true cultivators, including some more powerful late Qi Refinement cultivators, had to master the method of riding spirit cranes.

This was a strict order from the Sect Master!

On this day.

Several figures were hovering over the lower reaches of the Blackwater River, each riding a second-grade spirit crane.

They were all Initiate cultivators of the Luo Tian Sect.

At this moment, they were all nervously watching the one person and one crane on the Blackwater River.

“Do you think Senior Brother Zeng can subdue this spirit crane?”

“It’s very difficult! That mutant spirit crane is the most powerful one among the cranes sent by the White Crane Sect, at the third-grade Demon King level! The Sect Master, thinking of Senior Brother Zeng’s meritorious service in risking his life over the years, specially bestowed it upon him.”

“If he used the normal beast-taming method, he could enslave it. But unfortunately, Senior Brother Zeng attained the Dao through body refinement, and doesn’t have an Adept’s divine sense, so he can’t forcibly enslave a third-grade Demon King.”

“He also disdains to forcibly enslave it, I hear! If he can gain the spirit crane’s recognition through his own ability, then in battle, even without giving orders, the crane can cooperate with him tacitly. That’s the effect Senior Brother Zeng really wants!”

These Initiate cultivators all looked at Zeng Yilong with admiration.

Among the younger generation, Zeng Yilong was definitely the most outstanding person in the Luo Tian Sect!

Not only had he earned the Dao name Baleful Dragonzi, but he had personally defeated the Five Elements Divine Sect’s Adept Qi Emei!

Over the years, he had been very protective of his junior brothers and sisters. When leading them on missions, he often charged ahead, and when retreating, he frequently stayed behind alone.

Everyone hoped that Zeng Yilong could successfully tame this mutant spirit crane and add to his combat power!

Just as everyone was waiting expectantly, a loud shout suddenly came from the void.

“Wretched beast, will you not submit?!”

They saw Zeng Yilong’s powerful hands firmly pressing down on that golden-crowned spirit crane. Man and crane plummeted directly from the sky into the Blackwater River.

Waves rolled, the great river churned.

Everyone held their breath and watched this scene. To force a third-grade spirit crane to submit through pure strength without injuring it—the difficulty could be imagined.

Could Senior Brother Zeng succeed?

In everyone’s anticipation, the vast blood energy of the man and the rolling demonic qi of the beast within the Blackwater River clashed continuously.

About the time it takes to burn an incense stick.

A massive figure slowly surfaced.

It was that golden-crowned spirit crane!

But what about Senior Brother Zeng?

As everyone worried, Zeng Yilong suddenly emerged from the water and climbed onto the back of the golden-crowned spirit crane.

Panting heavily, he grinned.

“In the end, I won!”

Using pure physical strength to contend with a third-grade Demon King!

On the shore, several Initiate true cultivators of the Luo Tian Sect cheered in unison.

Suddenly.

The golden-crowned spirit crane let out a sharp cry, its white feathers standing on end and bristling, as if facing a great enemy.

Zeng Yilong frowned deeply. “Golden Fur, we had an agreement before. You’re not going to go back on your word, are you?”

The golden-crowned spirit crane, called Golden Fur, ignored him, its sharp eyes fixed on the distance.

Zeng Yilong instantly realized something. He stood up from its back and looked into the distance.

In his line of sight, a flying boat was approaching at low altitude.

On the flying boat, three figures stood facing the wind.

The one at the head had knitted brows and a haughty expression, her entire body radiating an aloof, keep-away feeling.

She was an extremely beautiful woman!

She looked down at the Blackwater River, her gaze sweeping over the crowd.

“What a fine golden-crowned white-feathered crane!”

Zeng Yilong frowned. In his perception, this woman was very strong!

Moreover, she looked very aggressive, as if ready to fight at any moment.

But this was the Luo Tian Sect!

Behind him were a group of junior brothers and sisters who admired and loved him!

The golden-crowned white-feathered crane was already exhausted, so he could not retreat.

Without hesitation, Zeng Yilong mustered his remaining strength, his astral qi beginning to spread, and with a *shing*, a pair of crescent-moon-like fiery red wings unfurled on his back.

“Ahead is the Luo Tian Sect. Fellow Daoist, please stop!”

Seeing the fiery red wings, the woman was momentarily stunned. Her aura exploded, full of hostility.

But the next moment, as if remembering something, she immediately withdrew her mana fluctuations and gave Zeng Yilong a graceful bow.

Amidst everyone’s bewilderment, she smiled slightly.

“Please inform the Grand Supreme Elder of your sect. Heavenjade has returned!”

Behind her, on the shoulder of another beautiful woman, a small black shadow suddenly popped out.

“Have we finally arrived?”
