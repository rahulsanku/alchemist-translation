# Chapter 1122: The Immortal Realm Collapses, the Deadly Tribulation Descends

Boundless cosmos, vast and empty void.

Amidst the twinkling stars, a stream of light flickered into being.

In one moment, it was right before the eyes; in the next, even its trail of light had vanished without a trace.

To the native beings of certain primitive planets, this wisp of light might have seemed as wondrous yet ordinary as a shooting star.

But for the stream of light itself, everything felt hazy and dreamlike.

Though the thunderous farewells still echoed faintly in his ears, Luo Chen knew he had long since left the Mountains and Seas Realm.

Sensing the surging spiritual mechanism beside him and the immense pulling force from a distant place, Luo Chen—whose heart was usually as calm as a still lake—could not help but feel a surge of emotion.

This was ascension!

With the aid of an entire realm's spiritual mechanism, he leaped across the infinite cosmos, heading straight for the most coveted cultivation holy land of all cultivators—the Immortal Realm!

The scattered records about ascension found in ancient texts now flooded Luo Chen's mind.

Within the boundless universe, there were countless great worlds, each with its own wonders, yet only the Immortal Realm stood at the center of the cosmos. All these great worlds were inexplicably connected to the Immortal Realm. Whenever a cultivator broke through the limits of their realm and was expelled from their great world, they would journey to the Immortal Realm.

Such an experience could never be achieved by merely traversing the void. Success was only possible through the lifting power of spiritual mechanism.

This was what was meant by "rising on rosy clouds in ascension"!

However, amidst his excitement, Luo Chen could not help but feel a trace of doubt.

According to the records of ascension he had gathered, when cultivators ascended, it was often instantaneous. The immense pressure from spatial travel would completely suppress their divine sense, leaving them dazed and confused. By the time they regained clarity, they would have mostly arrived in the Immortal Realm. How could he be like this—clear-headed, with leisure to gaze upon the mysteries of the infinite cosmos through layers of rosy light?

"The speed seems to be slowing down?"

...

Deep in the cosmos, a massive continent lay horizontally.

Above the continent, clouds and mists churned endlessly, yet they were bound to the land by some inexplicable force, unable to leak out even a trace.

Suddenly!

In a remote corner of the continent, a colossal pillar of light shot up into the sky.

But in just an instant, the pillar subsided and slowly dissipated.

Such an anomaly drew the attention of many powerful beings on the Cloud Sea Continent.

Before long, three figures broke through the air and landed on a desolate, altar-like platform.

Beneath the platform, stone pillars were mottled, and three large characters could faintly be seen carved on them—Ascension Platform!

The three figures exchanged glances, then turned their gazes to the center of the platform.

An old man, aged and weary, spoke in surprise: "This Ascension Platform has been abandoned for a long time. Why has it activated on its own today?"

He looked down and saw that although the spiritual light on the platform had dimmed, there were still some residual traces in the middle.

Circle after circle, a full four circles extended outward!

"Four turns!"

The exclamation slipped out.

The palace-robed beauty beside him clearly understood the significance of this number. Her eyes lit up, but then quickly dimmed, leaving only a hint of regret.

"Four turns on the Ascension Platform means this person used at least four types of law principles to pry open the spiritual mechanism of heaven and earth and lift themselves up in ascension. Perhaps their realm is low, but with such comprehension, if they had successfully ascended, they would surely have reached the True Immortal realm within ten thousand years at most. Now, their immortal fate is severed. What a pity."

The old man nodded, then shook his head slightly, as if he did not fully agree with the palace-robed beauty's words.

"Ten thousand years? Fellow Daoist Yu underestimates the potential of a four-turn Ascension Platform! For a cultivator from the lower realms, not a native of the Immortal Realm, to comprehend four types of law principles at the Ascendant stage—such talent and comprehension are rarely seen in the world. They must also possess great perseverance. At most five thousand years, and they would attain the Dao and become an immortal!"

The beauty surnamed Yu frowned. "Fellow Daoist Lü, are you exaggerating? I admit this person's comprehension is strong, but their cultivation is mixed and impure. How can they focus single-mindedly and advance with vigor? They will likely hinder themselves, with each aspect conflicting and restraining the other, thus impeding their cultivation. Ten thousand years is already the result of my careful calculation."

Just as the old man was about to speak again, the remaining middle-aged man, whose brows carried a hint of ferocity, snorted coldly, interrupting their argument.

"Enough! The three of us gathered here today to discuss countermeasures against the Southern Zhan Realm. Why waste time on such trivial matters? In the end, it's just someone with shallow fortune!"

With that, he turned and left.

The beauty surnamed Yu opened her rosy little mouth, but could only sigh and follow him away.

As for the old man, before leaving, he paused on the Ascension Platform, pondered for a long while, and then left with a thoughtful expression.

...

In the pitch-black cosmos, a stream of light flickered continuously.

But for some reason, this stream of light seemed quite lost. In the end, its speed gradually slowed, revealing a figure wrapped in rosy light.

"It stopped?!"

Luo Chen stared at this scene in shock, his eyes filled with disbelief.

How could an ascension on rosy clouds be interrupted?

But the immense pulling force from before had indeed disappeared.

In the desolate starry sky, only Luo Chen remained, with nothing above or below to rely on, nowhere to stand.

At this moment, Luo Chen stood still in the cosmos, his face filled with boundless confusion.

He did not understand what had happened. He could not comprehend why this situation had occurred.

But the facts were right before him.

The Immortal Realm was nowhere to be found!

He, Luo Chen, had failed in his ascension!

A sense of absurdity welled up within him.

Time passed, he knew not how long. In the depths of the cosmos, the very concept of time seemed to blur.

The confusion on Luo Chen's face gradually faded, leaving only a bitter smile.

Although he did not understand why this had happened, the reality was before him, and he had to accept it. Otherwise, the immense sense of disparity and the confusion about the future would completely devour his Dao heart in this boundless universe.

When it came to adaptability, Luo Chen had always been excellent.

He began to analyze the current situation.

"Although I don't know why the ascension failed, at least my life is safe. The urgent task now is to find a place to settle down and make further plans!"

An Ascendant cultivator could already traverse the cosmos and roam the void.

But the void of the universe was filled with countless chaotic currents of origin qi, which were not conducive to cultivation or prolonged stay.

Moreover, although the ascension had failed, the surging spiritual mechanism pried open by the four law principles when he left the Mountains and Seas Realm still lingered around him.

Although this power could not be used for its intended purpose, if Luo Chen utilized it well, he could still refine his cultivation.

There would be time ahead. He would eventually find the reason for today's mystery!

The confusion in Luo Chen's eyes completely dissipated, replaced by the same firmness as before!

He took a step and began to wander through the void by instinct.

He did not know how long passed—perhaps two or three days, perhaps eight or nine—when a figure in white robes appeared outside a yellow-brown planet.

This planet was not large. With a sweep of his divine sense, Luo Chen could cover it entirely.

A feeling of decay welled up within him.

In an instant, Luo Chen knew that this planet had reached its end. Its destruction was only a matter of time.

"Stars can fall, the immortal path can be severed, and even the boundless universe may have its day of destruction and rebirth. Can we cultivators truly achieve immortality?"

A thought unconsciously surfaced in his mind.

But quickly, Luo Chen suppressed it.

He knew that the unprecedented and bizarre experience he had just undergone had ultimately affected his Dao heart. Hence, at this moment, he could not help but feel a sense of sympathy for this dying planet, like the fox grieving for the hare.

But Luo Chen was even more aware that such thoughts must not be allowed to persist, or they would inevitably breed inner demons, leading to his death and the destruction of his Dao!

"Let me rest and recuperate here for a while, then make further plans."

Luo Chen pursed his lips, broke through the fierce astral wind, and charged into the yellow-brown planet.

Once inside, he casually cast out a few formation flags and sat down quietly.

There was no sign of any living creature on this planet, only vast stretches of mountains and ridges, so there was no need for excessive vigilance.

The urgent task now was not to seek the root cause or refine the spiritual mechanism, but to calm his mind and stabilize his Dao heart.

Otherwise, under the immense blow of a failed ascension, there was a great possibility that he would fall into qi deviation.

The earlier doubt about the path of cultivation was one of the signs!

Luo Chen dared not gamble!

...

A day later, Luo Chen, who was circulating his cultivation technique, suddenly opened his eyes and looked up at the sky.

In the hazy yellow sky, a resplendent gate was slowly opening.

This scene was like a dream, as if the gate to the Immortal Realm was opening for Luo Chen.

But Luo Chen reacted in an instant.

"Star Gate!"

At that very moment, two figures walked out of the Star Gate.

One wore a red dress and a graceful bearing, looking at Luo Chen with a half-smile.

The other wore plain green robes, her face, which should have been cold and aloof, now showing a hint of complex emotion.

Looking at the two familiar faces, Luo Chen's heart had a bad premonition.

He forced himself to stay calm, stood up, and cupped his hands. "Rosycloud, Fellow Daoist Azure Frost, it's been a hundred years. I hope you've been well!"

Azure Frost turned her face away, avoiding Luo Chen's gaze.

But Rosycloud let out a soft laugh.

"Life beyond the heavens has been quite bitter. How could I be well?"

Luo Chen's heart sank. "You were waiting for me?"

Rosycloud smiled faintly. "Quite perceptive. No wonder you are an unprecedented cultivation prodigy in the Mountains and Seas Realm."

Luo Chen's mood had now plummeted to the bottom.

The three of them, including Rosycloud, who should have ascended a hundred years ago, had waited for him outside the heavens for so long. If he thought this was a friendly gesture, he would be deceiving himself.

Now that they had appeared before him, it seemed the time for a showdown had come.

"Why?" Luo Chen asked.

Rosycloud did not answer. Her beautiful eyes merely fell upon him, upon the lingering, surging spiritual mechanism around him.

In this brief day's stay, a hint of green had even appeared beneath Luo Chen's feet.

Spiritual mechanism of heaven and earth, the creation of the Dao!

Luo Chen understood. "Just because of this spiritual mechanism?"

Rosycloud no longer concealed her intentions. Instead, she praised him. "I never expected you to comprehend four types of law principles and pry open such a vast amount of spiritual mechanism, leaping to a distance so far from the Mountains and Seas Realm. But this is a good thing for me! The more spiritual mechanism there is, the easier it will be to activate the Star Gate, and returning to the Mystic Realm will no longer be an empty dream."

With Luo Chen's intelligence, he understood everything from these few words.

To activate the Star Gate, aside from a massive amount of mana, what was most needed was the spiritual mechanism of heaven and earth.

Back when Luo Chen witnessed the ascension of Alchemy Saint Chu Yan, he had personally seen the Star Gate open. With his current knowledge, he naturally understood the principle.

But what he could not understand was why it had to be him. Why was she so certain she could find him?

As his thoughts raced, Luo Chen's gaze suddenly stopped on Azure Frost's averted face.

"Heh..."

A self-deprecating laugh rang out abruptly.

"So that's how it is!"

Luo Chen's face tightened. "I was wondering why you refused to dissolve the Life-and-Death Gu back then. It was for this moment."

Because of the Life-and-Death Gu, Luo Chen and Azure Frost were intimately connected. Even if they were separated by the ends of the earth, there was a faint perception.

With Rosycloud's methods enhancing this connection, it became the perfect tracking tool.

Especially since she possessed a spirit treasure like the Star Gate. With a coordinate to guide her, finding Luo Chen was a simple matter.

What Luo Chen found self-deprecating was that he had once used the Life-and-Death Gu to coerce Azure Frost into giving him a chance at life, and now it had turned around to harm him.

Azure Frost still did not look at him.

Luo Chen would not question her like a resentful woman. Instead, he stared fixedly at Rosycloud.

"Have you been scheming against me all along?"

Hearing this, Rosycloud laughed instead.

"Don't overestimate yourself. With your former strength, why would I have the leisure to scheme against you? It was merely a casual move, but I never expected it to become my lifeline."

As she spoke, Rosycloud's smile faded, and her face showed some anger.

"If I hadn't been so severely injured in the Mountains and Seas Realm, and if that small world's assimilation of me hadn't deepened, why would I have left in such a hurry? I was even reduced to bitterly waiting for you to ascend and leave the Mountains and Seas Realm, just to have this chance at life today. I must return to the Mystic Realm. Today, you will offer your spiritual mechanism to me!"

Luo Chen's expression was gloomy. He forced a smile and said, "This spiritual mechanism was gifted by the Heavenly Dao during ascension. It is intimately connected to the Dao of my cultivation and my very life. How do you intend to take it? Why not bring me along, and let me drive this spiritual mechanism..."

"Sorry, the Star Gate is too small. It cannot accommodate even one more person. I cannot take you. As for the method to take the spiritual mechanism?" Rosycloud flicked her fingertip, and a wisp of golden flame rose. "Your Dao is of the same origin as mine. Taking this spiritual mechanism that belongs to you is not difficult!"

The next moment, Luo Chen's expression changed drastically.

His entire mana suddenly boiled, and within his Purple Mansion, a golden fire bird fluttered about anxiously. His whole body felt as if it were immersed in a sea of fire.

Luo Chen was unwilling to sit idly and wait for death. Even if he could not mobilize his mana, he still had a trump card.

Boom!

With a thunderous roar, a thousand-zhang giant rose from the ground, standing tall amidst the yellow-brown mountains.

Rosycloud's expression remained calm. She simply formed a hand seal.

As if responding, golden flames burst out all over Luo Chen's body, beginning to burn his massive physique.

"The Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture!"

The giant roared out these five words, his voice filled with anger.

Rosycloud smiled. "You react quickly. That's right, it is the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture that I bestowed upon you. If you had not undergone the complete Nine Tribulations and Three Nirvanas, subduing you might have required some effort. But now, it is simple."

As she spoke, she opened her mouth and inhaled.

The surging spiritual mechanism lingering around Luo Chen rolled toward Rosycloud.

At the same time, Luo Chen's mana and vital blood began to burn.

She intended to refine Luo Chen, both his person and his spiritual mechanism, together!

However, this process was not as smooth as she had imagined.

During her absorption, she gradually sensed a struggle emerging from Luo Chen's side.

"True Spirit Source Blood?"

Rosycloud was startled, then exclaimed in surprise, "You have actually cultivated your physical body to the fifth rank, and you are even evolving toward a True Spirit?"

Luo Chen did not answer. He simply burned his source blood without regard for the cost.

In the years before his ascension from the Mountains and Seas Realm, he had already consumed a mature Demon-Devil Heart-Linking Fruit, pushing his body refinement progress to the Archaic fifth rank.

The advancement path of Archaic beasts was vastly different from that of ordinary demons. When they reached their peak, they would transform into True Spirits, naturally wielding the laws of the Dao, surpassing even human Great Vehicle cultivators.

Although he was still far from becoming a True Spirit, Luo Chen had already condensed True Spirit Source Blood.

By burning it without regard for depletion, he finally gained some power to resist.

Seizing this opportunity, Luo Chen forcibly suppressed the boiling fire-attribute mana within his body and began to convert it into other attributes.

Only by doing so could he break free from the constraints of the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture and then contend with Rosycloud for life and death.

Not far away, Rosycloud saw this and frowned deeply.

"Troublesome."

Then, she parted her red lips slightly.

"Azure Frost, why haven't you made your move?"

Azure Frost looked at the man standing tall between heaven and earth, being burned by golden flames. She subconsciously opened her mouth, but in the end, she could only sigh.

"Forgive me."

With a low apology, Azure Frost took a step and arrived beside Luo Chen.

A blood-colored sword tip suddenly stabbed out, but Azure Frost flicked it away with a finger.

A black umbrella opened, but Azure Frost did not dodge.

Then, a palm was heavily imprinted on Luo Chen's body.

In that moment, Luo Chen was struck as if by a heavy blow.

His eyes widened, and then a mouthful of blood gushed out like a waterfall.

Already constrained by Rosycloud, how could Luo Chen's hastily prepared defenses withstand the attack of Azure Frost, whose strength was comparable to his own?

With that palm strike, all his means of resistance instantly crumbled.

Even his powerful physical body seemed to be imprisoned by a spatial force. He could only watch helplessly as Rosycloud continuously refined the surging spiritual mechanism, and even the ocean-like mana within his body became nourishment for Rosycloud under the burning of the Nirvana Sacred Flame.

"Demon Phoenix!!!"

With these two words, there was also boundless humiliation, unwillingness, and despair.

Rosycloud watched all this coldly.

"Let me make it clear so you can die with understanding. The Immortal Realm has long since collapsed, and the immortal path has been severed. The eternal life you seek is nothing but an illusion. In the Mystic Realm, Great Vehicle Celestial Lords who die of old age are everywhere. Rather than bitter cultivation for millions of years only to end up with nothing, it is better to end it early."

As she spoke, the woman's sleeves billowed, and the mana and spiritual mechanism on Luo Chen's body poured out like floodwaters bursting through a dam.

Just then, she seemed to sense something and frowned slightly.

"Trying to self-destruct?"

The next moment, Azure Frost closed in, facing Luo Chen directly.

Her hands opened and closed, and a spatial rift suddenly appeared.

Azure Frost struck out with a palm, and Luo Chen's aura, ready to burst forth, suddenly stalled. Then, his mountain-like physical body was directly pushed into the rift.

Seeing this, Rosycloud lost her composure and flew into a rage.

"What are you doing!"

Azure Frost turned around and said in a low voice, "Sister, he was about to self-destruct. To avoid harming you, I had no choice but to banish him into the chaotic void."

"I asked you, what are you doing!" Rosycloud's expression twisted with fury.

Azure Frost instinctively stepped back, then bit her lip and forced herself to say, "It's enough. Sister Qinghe was devoured by you for her essence and qi. Luo Chen's mana and spiritual mechanism have all been refined by you. Why must you exterminate him completely?"

Rosycloud growled, "He still has something I need on him. Who told you to banish him?"

Azure Frost was stunned.

But the spatial rift she had opened earlier had already healed as before.

Even this planet, which was on the verge of destruction, had begun to collapse and disintegrate due to the earlier turbulence of origin qi.

Rosycloud glared fiercely at Azure Frost, then took a deep breath, flicked her sleeve, turned around, and stepped into the Star Gate.

Azure Frost was bewildered. What else did Luo Chen have that her sister needed?

But there was no time to ask now. She hurriedly entered the Star Gate.

When the resplendent Star Gate closed and disappeared,

In the deep cosmos, only a planet remained, collapsing and disintegrating, stirring up a silent storm.

Just returned to work, a bit rusty, please bear with me.

(Chapter End)
