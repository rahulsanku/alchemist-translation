# Chapter 886: You’ve Taken the Wrong Path

One old, one young.

The old one was Haoranzi, the young woman was Jadewater Dragon.

These were the two Nascent Soul cultivators that Huamei had told Luo Chen were currently being used as medicine men on Green Parasol Mountain.

Coincidentally, Luo Chen knew both of them.

Haoranzi in particular had a rather good relationship with him; back in the day, they had even shared a certain friendship that transcended age.

But Luo Chen was no longer so concerned about this matter now.

All because Nether Spring had sent him a message: Rosycloud had awakened and would summon him in three days.

An audience with a legendary great power—this was something Luo Chen had to handle with the utmost caution.

It was not that he was overly cautious; it was simply that beings of such realms could not be measured by ordinary standards.

In his life, Luo Chen had dealt with very few Ascendant great powers—just two or three.

One he had observed from afar: Li Canghai, the formation grandmaster of the Northern Sea, who cared nothing for the conflict between humans and demons and only sought to refine a spirit treasure by extracting the mountains and seas. Strictly speaking, this did not count as dealing with him.

But the other two had left a deep impression on Luo Chen.

Alchemy Saint Chu Yan, despite having no deep relationship with him, had gifted him a Heavenly Secret Pill upon ascending. One had to know that the other party had only refined five such pills in total. What reason did Luo Chen, with no close ties to him, have to receive one of them?

Unprovoked goodwill was hard to fathom, but since the other had already ascended and they would have no further entanglements, Luo Chen simply chalked it up to the man’s kindness.

As for Nether Spring Black Marsh, he had forcefully recruited Luo Chen upon their first meeting, even going so far as to somewhat underhandedly plant a divine ability on him under the guise of protection. Moreover, as far as Luo Chen knew, this was the same person who had personally set foot on Green Parasom Mountain back then, attempting to capture and kill Azure Frost.

This man was nothing like the imagined image of a great senior. He cared nothing for grace or style, only for results!

Azure Frost’s threat was too great, so Black Marsh could disregard decorum and bully the weak, trying to nip her in the bud.

Luo Chen’s alchemy was too superb, so Black Marsh would stop at nothing to recruit him into the Nether Origin Sect. Luo Chen had no doubt that if he had refused back then, the other party would have resorted to drastic measures.

Thus, among the three Ascendant great powers Luo Chen had observed, one paid no heed to worldly affairs and only cared about his own cultivation, one treated others extremely well and made one feel like a spring breeze, and one was pragmatic to the point of being unscrupulous.

And what about Rosycloud?

There were many, many rumors about her.

A great demonic power who had risen in the Eastern Wilderness over a thousand years, she had attained the Dao after Emperor Ping Shan Di Tian, yet held an extremely transcendent status, single-handedly ruling the sacred land of Green Parasol Mountain.

The destruction of the Primordial Demon Sect in the Northern Sea had been too swift, too bizarre, and the shadow of Rosycloud flickered within that incident.

Within the Demon-Fall Land, the spirit of the heaven-reaching spirit treasure Heaven-Refining Cauldron had claimed that Devil Lord Heaven-Refining had fought a death battle with Rosycloud and had even fallen in the Mountains and Seas Realm. Was that Rosycloud the same as this Rosycloud?

And then there was this great war between the human and demon races in the Eastern Wilderness. On the surface, it was a joint effort by the three ancient demon lords, but from beginning to end, Rosycloud had never been seen making a move.

Even the Green Parasol Mountain lineage, had it not been for the invitation of the Thunder Prison Divine Peng’s good friend, Commander Seven Rhinoceros, it could not be said to have truly participated in the two-race war.

One rumor after another made it impossible to discern Rosycloud’s style of conduct, let alone guess her temperament.

She was far too mysterious.

Luo Chen found it hard to imagine what the scene of their meeting would be like.

All he knew was that he had to adjust his state to the best possible, lest he lose his composure.

Just before stepping into the cultivation chamber, a thought suddenly struck Luo Chen.

“Awakened?”

“That means she didn’t see me before not because of her mood, but because she was asleep?”

“It couldn’t be that the Green Parasol Mountain lineage didn’t participate in the Eastern Wilderness human-demon war simply because the boss was sleeping, could it?”

…

Three days later.

A full month had passed since Luo Chen arrived at Green Parasol Mountain.

Aside from the earlier farce with Nether Spring and Thunder Prison, everything had been calm and peaceful.

So peaceful that one could almost believe this was a paradise, untouched by the chaos of the outside world.

Yet clearly, the starting point of the Eastern Wilderness war had been right here!

Hoo…

A gentle breeze blew, and the clouds guarding the cave dwelling dispersed. Luo Chen, dressed in white robes, stepped out.

Someone was already waiting outside.

It was an unfamiliar woman, with a well-proportioned figure, dark skin, and a small, round, rather cute face. Upon closer inspection, it seemed as if one’s attention would be sucked into her deep, profound eyes.

Though unfamiliar, her aura was unmistakable.

Luo Chen blinked. “Deputy Hall Master, your transformation is very successful. You accomplished it in just three days.”

“Hmph, I prepared for this day for who knows how long. Once your pill was delivered by Huamei, everything fell into place naturally.”

Nether Spring gave a light snort, her tone seemingly dismissive, but there was a hint of joy hidden within.

She stood before Luo Chen, scrutinizing him carefully, then showed a trace of surprise.

“I heard that when you first arrived, your mana was severely depleted, and your physical body had considerable injuries. In just one month, you’ve recovered completely?”

Luo Chen humbly replied, “Thanks to the abundant spiritual qi of this cave dwelling.”

Nether Spring shook her head. No matter how abundant the spiritual qi, it was still an external condition.

To recover so quickly, one had to look at one’s own foundation.

She circled around Luo Chen and suddenly said, “Have you completed the second nirvana among the Nine Tribulations, Three Nirvanas?”

Without waiting for Luo Chen to answer, she continued on her own, “Yes, it must be so. Only by completing the physical nirvana can one absorb fire-attribute spiritual qi from heaven and earth at the fastest speed. No longer a false heavenly spirit root, but a genuine fire-attribute heavenly spirit root, and even a Fire Spirit Body!”

“I was wondering earlier why your realm was advancing so quickly, whether you had taken some demonic shortcut.”

“Now, everything makes sense.”

Luo Chen’s expression shifted subtly.

The Nine Tribulations, Three Nirvanas were the three realms within his main cultivation technique, the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture*, during his Adept stage.

They were the Mana Nirvana, the Physical Nirvana, and the Soul Nirvana.

Completing each nirvana brought immense benefits to the cultivator.

He was not surprised that Nether Spring knew these things, as the technique was given to him by her in the first place, to enhance his fire control and refine the fourth-tier Transformation Pill.

But he was surprised that she could deduce from the single detail of his rapid mana recovery that he had completed the second nirvana.

Knowing about it was one thing, but being able to understand it so thoroughly without actually cultivating the technique made Luo Chen wary.

“No!” Nether Spring suddenly spoke, stopping right in front of Luo Chen, staring intently at his face.

Luo Chen didn’t see anything wrong and asked back, “What’s wrong?”

Nether Spring’s face showed a hint of confusion. “I don’t know what’s wrong either, but you shouldn’t be like this.”

Luo Chen was genuinely curious now.

Just as he was about to press further, his attention was suddenly interrupted.

Both of them looked up at the sky.

At the summit of Green Parasol Mountain stood an incomparably magnificent palace, named Profound Heaven.

The Profound Heaven Hall was tall and imposing, suppressing the Truth Crossing and Daytime Void Halls below it, one on each side.

But it gave the impression that it was about to take flight, as if it could soar away at any moment.

And today, the long-closed gates of Profound Heaven Hall were slowly opening.

Torrents of spiritual qi gushed out from within the gates.

An indescribable aura gradually spread, causing countless birds to flap their wings and soar, circling above Profound Heaven Hall.

Even some third-tier demon kings who had taken human form could not help but reveal their true bodies and fly around the hall.

In an instant, a wondrous scene of ten thousand birds paying homage and auspicious clouds gathering took shape.

Luo Chen was not of the feathered race and did not feel it deeply, but Nether Spring beside him was quite excited.

“The gates are open.”

“Follow me!”

Nether Spring shot into the sky, and Luo Chen followed closely behind.

As he drew closer to Profound Heaven Hall, Luo Chen felt his mana becoming much more lively. Even some unhealed hidden injuries in his meridians were quietly being cleared as his mana circulated automatically.

This time, Luo Chen was truly surprised.

He had just guessed that the ten thousand birds were paying homage because they sensed the aura released by Rosycloud.

That aura had a “moistening” effect on bird-type demon beasts, hence the commotion.

But he was not a bird; why was he also affected?

Suddenly, a possibility occurred to Luo Chen.

The techniques shared the same origin, so the mana was similar, creating a resonance between higher and lower.

Rosycloud also cultivated the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture*!

“We’re here!”

The man and woman stopped before the gates.

It was only then that the gates stopped opening.

A figure with a contradictory aura stood quietly at the entrance, withdrawing her hand and standing respectfully to one side.

Clearly, it was she who had personally pulled open the gates.

The moment he saw that person’s face, Luo Chen’s heart shook, and three words almost escaped his lips.

But that cold yet beautiful face had already lowered its gaze as Luo Chen’s eyes swept over.

The fairy-like woman said softly, “The Primordial Sovereign is waiting inside.”

Nether Spring waved her hand and eagerly walked inside.

Luo Chen lagged behind, still unable to resist turning back for a look.

Coincidentally, the woman looked up, and their eyes met for a fleeting moment, but then she lowered her head again.

It was that one glance that dispelled all of Luo Chen’s doubts.

“Why is Moon Wanderer in the Profound Heaven Hall of Green Parasol Mountain?”

He could not be mistaken!

In all the world, there were very few women with such a temperament, almost none like her.

Cold yet passionate, distant yet alluring.

As cold as a moon palace fairy, yet with a flicker of her gaze, she was seductive and charming, making any man in the world want to fall at her feet.

This was Moon Wanderer!

One of the three great wandering cultivators of the Northern Sea, the master of the Myriad Immortals Assembly.

And his, Luo Chen’s, enemy!

Luo Chen had imagined countless scenarios of their reunion: meeting again in the old haunts of the Northern Sea, a life-and-death battle, or even the bizarre scene of Moon Wanderer being recruited by the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect to join the Star Gate and meeting her again at Heaven-Earth Peak.

But the one thing Luo Chen had never imagined was that she would appear before him in such a form, on such an occasion.

A generation’s strong figure, reduced to a servant opening the main gate for others?

“You know the maidservant the Primordial Sovereign took in?”

“Maidservant?”

Luo Chen couldn’t hold it together; he couldn’t connect that word with Moon Wanderer.

Nether Spring said matter-of-factly, “Yeah, a maidservant the Primordial Sovereign picked up on a whim when she came back. She dances quite beautifully alone under the moon when the Primordial Sovereign is admiring it; it once made the Primordial Sovereign quite happy.”

A moon-viewing maidservant?

Luo Chen was even more bewildered.

What had happened after he left the Northern Sea back then?

But this bewilderment lasted only a moment. Luo Chen instantly realized that for someone as proud and aloof as Moon Wanderer, who had even left the Penglai Immortal Sect to oppose the Primordial Demon Sect, to be so “submissive,” Rosycloud was definitely not an easy person to deal with.

So, what attitude should he adopt when meeting her?

Grovel?

Neither humble nor arrogant?

Stand tall with pride?

All his anxiety vanished the moment he saw that flash of red above.

She was half-reclining on a sitting couch, her eyes drowsy, like a young wife just awakened from her boudoir.

Her phoenix crown was set aside, her black hair cascading like a waterfall.

Her red skirt was disheveled, and in a fleeting glance, one could catch a glimpse of snow-white, plump thighs.

And that face…

Even though Luo Chen had seen countless beautiful women in his life, he had never seen such a stunning beauty.

Whether it was Cheng Haixin’s delicate charm, Sima Huiniang’s gentle virtue, Fu Qinglan’s heavenly pride, Azure Frost’s cold aloofness, or even the seductive charm of the woman he had just passed by, none of them could compare to the woman on the couch.

Graceful, dignified, noble, and grand.

Her innate nobility was displayed to the fullest, so much so that even a simple gesture of brushing a strand of hair aside seemed incredibly noble.

For a being like this, a man’s thoughts could only be summed up in two words.

Either conquer!

Or submit!

But her overwhelmingly powerful strength, a realm that could look down upon an entire world, would instantly dispel the first thought.

“Luo Chen pays respects to Primordial Sovereign Rosycloud!”

In the great hall, Luo Chen bowed slightly, clasping his hands in salute.

His voice of greeting echoed through the empty hall.

There was no response.

Instead, the woman beckoned.

Then, Nether Spring transformed into a streak of black light, turning into a black crow. It flapped its wings joyfully, flew onto the woman’s shoulder, and gently pecked with its beak, tidying her disheveled hair.

Still no response.

Even though the other party did not emit any mana fluctuations, Luo Chen still felt a pressure like a mountain, far exceeding the pressure he had felt when meeting Ancestor Black Marsh.

Even Li Canghai’s great technique of overturning mountains and draining seas to collect the Ice Pole Sect could not compare to the oppressive silence of this hall at this moment.

It felt like an eternity had passed, yet also like only a single moment.

Only when a faint voice sounded did Luo Chen dare to raise his head.

“Is the Dao Union Pill on you?”

The moment he saw Moon Wanderer, Luo Chen knew that his experiences in the Demon-Fall Land were no longer a secret to Rosycloud.

He shook his head. “I do not have the Dao Union Pill.”

Rosycloud’s brow furrowed slightly. “There was no Dao Union Pill inside the Heaven-Refining Cauldron?”

After a moment’s thought, she found it reasonable.

“Although Night Bright Radiance had made some progress in researching the Dao Union Pill back then, he was still in the process of collecting medicinal ingredients. It’s indeed possible that he didn’t refine it.”

“Do you have the pill formula?”

Night Bright Radiance, Bright Radiance Heaven, Devil Lord Heaven-Refining… So this was the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining’s true name?

The thought flashed through Luo Chen’s mind. In response to the woman’s question, he unhesitatingly took out a jade box.

“Here.”

Swoosh!

The jade box instantly fell into the woman’s hands. Only then did she tremble slightly as she sat up.

Luo Chen kept his eyes straight ahead. Clearly, he had long been prepared for this.

Rosycloud toyed with the jade box, carefully examining the intricate restrictions.

“It is indeed his handiwork.”

“But at my current realm, I’m afraid I cannot open it.”

With a flick of her snow-white wrist, the jade box disappeared.

It was only then that Rosycloud looked directly at Luo Chen.

“You are sensible, not like that stubborn Moon girl.”

“Since you have brought me this treasure, I will grant you a great fortune as well!”

Before the words had even faded, before Luo Chen could react, he saw the woman flick her finger.

A streak of fire shot through the air and landed on him in an instant.

At that moment, Luo Chen’s expression changed drastically.

Could it be that this Rosycloud was also a great power who cared nothing for dignity?

“Primordial Sovereign, you…”

But Rosycloud’s next words silenced Luo Chen.

“You’ve taken the wrong path.”
