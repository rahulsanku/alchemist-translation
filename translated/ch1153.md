# Chapter 1153: The Returner, Huifeng Mountain

Late summer, early autumn. North of Yixian.

A caravan laden with numerous goods slowly made its way northward.

Inside a luxurious carriage, Xiao Feng nervously reported the general situation to Luo Chen.

"For this trip, we'll pass through seven major city districts including Xilong, Sanxiang, and Zijin, with the final destination being Mofeng City. The pace might be a bit slow, but after unloading goods at each district, we'll pick up speed. Moreover, three of those cities won't require your personal presence, which will save some time. Based on past experience, we should reach Mofeng City within three months at most. I hope you can bear with us, sir."

Luo Chen hummed in acknowledgment, showing neither approval nor disapproval.

For him, whether it was three months or half a year, it didn't really matter.

As long as the destination had the divine wind he needed, this trip would not be in vain.

However, there was one thing Luo Chen was curious about.

"Merchant affairs, I've heard, have always been handled by officials under Yixian City's command. Why did you need to come personally this time, and even bring... Tie Xiong?"

At the end of his words, Luo Chen's gaze seemed to pierce through the carriage wall, landing on the one-armed man sitting up front.

Xiao Feng hesitated for a moment, then finally told the truth.

"Among the seven cities, there are some variables, so I had to make this trip myself."

"Variables?"

"Yes, actually, it's about some strong individuals who went to Wuyou City twenty years ago and have now returned. They were once the power-holders. Once they return, the power structure of their respective city districts will inevitably change. Correspondingly, this change will affect their cooperative relationship with our Yixian City. As the master of Yixian City and a junior, I naturally have to go negotiate in person."

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow, not expecting this twist.

But then, he looked at Xiao Feng with a half-smile.

"Your predecessor as Yixian City's master, your father Xiao Lin, hasn't he returned?"

"I wish he would, but unfortunately..." Xiao Feng gave a bitter laugh, sighed, and explained: "Such returners have happened from time to time in the past. But those people often return because their aptitude was insufficient, they failed to break through in Wuyou City, and were severely injured. After coming back, they usually don't last long before passing away. Someone like my father, a martial arts fanatic—whether in aptitude or temperament—would never fall to such a fate. So unless I break through to the Leakless Realm and go to Yixian City myself, I'm afraid I'll never see my father again in this life."

Hearing this, Luo Chen showed a thoughtful expression.

Wuyou City!

The foremost of the seventy-two cities of the Great Cataclysm Realm, whose city lord is the so-called Wuyou Realm Lord.

It was rumored that there, martial artists of the Leakless Realm could find the great opportunity to break through to the fifth-rank Human Immortal Realm.

That was why, every hundred years, a large number of strong individuals would actively or passively surge toward Wuyou City.

But as for the matter of returners, Luo Chen was hearing about it for the first time.

Combined with what Xiao Feng said—that these people lacked aptitude, couldn't break through in Wuyou City, and when they returned, they often carried old injuries and would soon die in obscurity—if that were the case, it made sense that no corresponding records were left behind?

After all, it was ultimately a shameful matter.

Xiao Feng looked at the contemplative Luo Chen and cautiously probed: "Sir, with your abilities, even Wuyou City would be worth a try. All you lack is the Leakless Realm. May I ask what your current cultivation level is?"

Luo Chen glanced at him. "Want me to leave?"

Xiao Feng quickly waved his hands. "Sir, you misunderstand. I'm only concerned about your cultivation. If you have any needs in daily life, please don't hesitate to mention them! I'll take my leave now. If anything comes up, just call for me."

Luo Chen snorted. He had never been shy about asking.

It seemed that facing his year after year of excessive demands, the other party felt pressured and thus tested him this way.

But those resources had indeed provided great assistance to Luo Chen's cultivation.

After Xiao Feng left, Luo Chen examined his current realm through the panel.

[Furnace Realm 52/100]

If divided into minor stages, it would be around the middle stage.

This was merely the result of ten years' effort—progress far faster than ordinary people!

Of course, the further one went, the slower the cultivation speed became. Especially when it came to the barrier of tempering apertures, Luo Chen's progress had been slow until now.

That was why, upon hearing news of the divine wind, he had taken the initiative to make this trip.

Only higher-quality astral wind could more efficiently temper his apertures!

The journey was rather dull and not suitable for daily cultivation, so Luo Chen simply took out a book he had brought along and read it seriously.

It was precisely the forbidden technique that Xiao Sanniang had used back in the arena.

Luo Chen was quite interested in this move.

Back then, Xiao Sanniang had relied on this forbidden technique to briefly unleash power far beyond her own level, thereby crippling Hua Guzi.

Although Xiao Sanniang had paid a great price afterward, her performance at the time was indeed impressive.

Now that Luo Chen couldn't use many of his spells and divine abilities, even though his physique was unprecedented, he still felt unsettled at heart.

Especially since this world might very well have fifth-rank or even higher-level experts, he naturally wanted to master more killing techniques to protect himself.

This *Heaven-Earth Reverse Cycle* had decent performance, and it was merely an incomplete method.

If Luo Chen could complete it, he might be able to restore its peak power, or even surpass it.

And on this point, Luo Chen had great confidence!

He had once been a grandmaster-level existence in the art of spells.

One method mastered, ten thousand methods understood!

Facing martial techniques, he naturally had unique insights.

In the end, it was all about the utilization of energy.

Especially since he still had the panel in hand, with quite a few achievement points stored on it, enough for him to deduce and complete a forbidden technique.

...

The caravan moved slowly forward, never encountering any bandit attacks from start to finish.

This was related to the large number of people in the Yixian City caravan, and also greatly due to the personal presence of Tie Xiong, a great expert.

Even if they occasionally encountered some fierce wild beasts on the road, Chu Zhaonan would lead the Mysterious Armor Army to handle them personally.

When Luo Chen occasionally became interested, he studied those fierce beasts that frightened the mortals of the Great Cataclysm Realm, but he didn't detect anything unusual.

Although they were fiercer and larger than the mountain beasts of the Mountains and Seas Realm, they ultimately didn't qualify as demon beasts.

After a while, Luo Chen lost interest.

But when Chu Zhaonan mentioned that Mofeng City, located in a remote barbaric area, had some even more unique beasts, Luo Chen actually looked forward to it.

Along the way, as the massive caravan entered another city district, a split occurred in both personnel and goods.

Xiao Feng took Tie Xiong to visit Xilong City to call on the local power-holders.

At their first parting, Luo Chen casually asked, "Need me to wait for you?"

Xiao Feng refused with a normal expression. "I won't delay your time, sir. Head straight for Mofeng City. After we finish handling matters here, we'll travel light and fast to catch up."

Luo Chen didn't insist, nor did he mention visiting the returners of Xilong City.

The caravan continued on.

A few days later, Xiao Feng and Tie Xiong caught up with their people, then parted ways again at the next city district.

And so it went, parting and reuniting repeatedly.

The originally bloated caravan became simpler and simpler.

Three months later.

When they neared Mofeng City, only a few carts of goods remained.

Inside the carriage.

Luo Chen closed the book, a satisfied expression on his face.

Regarding the forbidden technique *Heaven-Earth Reverse Cycle*, he had finally completed it in his hands.

The path of blood and qi was ever-changing.

The method of advancement was to condense the core into astral wind, evolving the heaven-earth furnace.

It emphasized refining into oneness, finding patterns amidst myriad changes.

But *Heaven-Earth Reverse Cycle* was about breaking patterns, using reversal methods to operate astral wind, even driving the blood and qi furnace to rotate in reverse.

In this way, during battle, one's power could be doubled.

Rather than calling it a killing technique, it was more similar to the Withering-Bloom Divine Seal that Luo Chen had studied back then.

Only, one focused on temporary burst, the other on long-term energy storage.

The former harmed the body, the latter consumed time.

Luo Chen naturally looked down on such killing methods that depleted the body's origin. After his transformation and completion, this forbidden technique had undergone a completely different change.

Taking its strengths, discarding its dross.

And its power had not decreased but increased!

According to Luo Chen's estimation, if he executed *Heaven-Earth Reverse Cycle* as envisioned, its power should be three to five times greater.

Moreover, this upper limit would increase along with the energy he mastered.

Especially if he could truly fuse the divine wind and cultivate his own divine astral combat qi, then the power of this forbidden technique would surely be even higher!

"I wonder if it can reach the level of a divine ability?"

Just as Luo Chen was thinking this, a respectful voice came from outside the carriage.

"Sir, we've arrived at Mofeng City."

Luo Chen lifted the carriage curtain and looked outside.

Ancient city walls entered his view. Though not as tall as Yixian City's, the mottled claw-shaped marks and dark bloodstains on the long brick stones added a fierce and murderous aura.

Combined with Chu Zhaonan's introductions along the way, Luo Chen knew that the living environment of Mofeng City was far harsher than Yixian City's.

This could be seen from the faces of the passing commoners on the roadside.

Unlike the ordinary people of Yixian City, who, though living in hardship, still had hope on their faces, the faces of Mofeng City's residents were mostly numb from wind and frost, even cold and stern.

"Is it caused by the Cataclysm Mist among the three great calamities?"

Luo Chen thought this way.

According to Chu Zhaonan, unlike Yixian City, which only suffered the two calamities of Heavenly Wind and Ghost Rain, the more remote city districts among the seventy-two were more prone to encountering small-scale Cataclysm Mist.

And when the great mist struck, special beasts would appear within it.

They were called "Mist Beasts."

These Mist Beasts seemed born to slaughter living beings, spontaneously attacking any settlement of living creatures.

Mofeng City was the first to bear the brunt.

"If I have the chance, I'd like to see these so-called Mist Beasts."

Thinking this, Luo Chen casually asked, "Xiao Feng and the others haven't caught up yet?"

Chu Zhaonan could tell Luo Chen was in a good mood, so he smiled and replied, "The city lord was at Huangsha City last stop, which is quite far from here. I doubt he'll make it today. But arrangements have been made here. You can rest for a night, sir. After we notify Mofeng City, we can head to Huifeng Mountain Range tomorrow."

Luo Chen hummed. "Then let's enter the city first!"

The caravan started moving again, slowly entering the ancient Mofeng City.

Once inside the city, he could feel how different the local customs were from Yixian City.

But before Luo Chen could observe much, his expression shifted slightly, and he looked without any concealment toward a tall building in the distance.

"Sir, what's wrong?"

Chu Zhaonan, following at his side, asked curiously.

A red light flashed in Luo Chen's eyes, and the corners of his mouth lifted slightly.

"Nothing!"

But in his mind, an old, sickly face had already appeared.

"Is that one of the returners who failed to break through and came back severely injured?"

Meanwhile.

On a tall building in Mofeng City, an old man in wide robes changed expression slightly, and an involuntary shiver ran through his body.

"Does Yixian City have such an expert? Just one glance gave me the pressure of a Seven-Cycle martial artist from Wuyou City?"

...

Late at night.

Chu Zhaonan, tired from a day's work, was about to sleep when he sensed something unusual outside.

He opened the door and was startled.

"City Lord?"

Xiao Feng turned around with a smile. "General Chu, your strength has improved quite a bit. You can already detect my arrival."

Chu Zhaonan gave a simple, honest smile. "It's because you didn't conceal yourself much, City Lord."

As he spoke, the corner of his eye glanced behind Xiao Feng. Tie Xiong, in a gray robe, stood there with his hands down, not leaking any aura, as if on alert.

Such vigilance—who was Tie Xiong guarding against?

Before Chu Zhaonan could figure it out, Xiao Feng asked directly, "When you entered the city today, you didn't run into anyone from Mofeng City's city lord's mansion, did you?"

Chu Zhaonan shook his head. "Following your instructions, we didn't actively contact the local authorities."

"Good!"

Xiao Feng let out an obvious sigh of relief, then said, "I won't disturb your rest. Go to sleep! Tomorrow, you'll need to trouble yourself to take Luo Chen to Huifeng Mountain Range."

Having said that, Xiao Feng left with Tie Xiong.

Chu Zhaonan watched the direction they left, but instead of staying, he went straight out of the inn.

Somehow, facing Xiao Feng's actions, Chu Zhaonan felt a sense of familiarity.

He didn't dare think too much, pursed his lips, and closed the door.

...

Early the next morning.

Several fine horses left Mofeng City, carrying a few strong, sharp-eyed men northward.

Along the way, no soldiers stopped them.

Even when passing through some mining areas strictly controlled by Mofeng City's authorities, no one came out to block them.

In less than half a day, the group of martial artists rode into a wilderness.

"Sir, ahead is the Huifeng Mountain Range!"

Following the direction Chu Zhaonan pointed, Luo Chen looked into the distance. He saw a winding mountain range stretching for a hundred li, prostrate on the earth like a coiled dragon.

Where the scales stood were the undulating peaks.

Even from such a distance, Luo Chen could hear the fierce howling wind within.

Chu Zhaonan introduced, "The Huifeng Mountain Range is different from ordinary continuous mountain ranges. Its peaks twist and turn, looping back and forth, forming layer upon layer of mountains. It's precisely because of this special terrain that after the Heavenly Wind, one of the three great calamities, blows through each year, a portion remains, hidden within. Over time, no grass or trees grow inside, and all living things have perished. It can be said that this terrain is a human forbidden zone! The divine wind you seek might be inside."

In the Great Cataclysm Realm, there were three great calamities.

Heavenly Wind, Ghost Rain, and Cataclysm Mist!

The Heavenly Wind was the most common, blowing from time to time every year.

If it were just ordinary strong wind, it wouldn't deserve the name of calamity.

The reason the Heavenly Wind was listed among them was that this wind was "Astral Wind"!

It was said to pour down from the extreme heavens. Mortal bodies, once touched, would wither away, flesh and blood gone.

Even martial artists who had embarked on the path of blood and qi found it hard to withstand this wind.

Someone like Chu Zhaonan, who had cultivated astral wind, might be able to endure the Heavenly Wind for a moment, but facing the Huifeng Mountain Range, where the Heavenly Wind howled all day long, he absolutely dared not enter.

But Luo Chen was not afraid. Gazing into the distance, his face was full of eagerness.

"Unforgiving Heavenly Wind—it's said to be an ultimate existence formed when the Heavenly Wind accumulates for over a hundred years and cannot be extinguished! If I can refine it into my astral wind and merge it with my current Nine Tribulations True Astral Wind, I will surely forge a divine astral combat qi unique to me!" (Chapter End)
