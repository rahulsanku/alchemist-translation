# Chapter 966: Fierce Battle!

"Now, it's your turn!"

Li Tian Master's gaze swept across the three of them, his face filled with cruel ferocity, as if all his previous benevolence had been nothing but a disguise.

The longsword in his hand traced idly through the air, and from the calm void, three sword beams suddenly shot forth, heading straight for the trio.

One against three, and all of them were renowned great cultivators.

Such confidence he had!

Because Luo Chen had seized the Devil Heart Gu, he was closest to him, and thus bore the brunt of the attack.

Facing the incoming sword beam, he did not hesitate to bring down his sword finger in return.

A stream of crimson sword qi moved in response.

But an unexpected scene unfolded!

Luo Chen's crimson sword qi, the moment it left his body, vanished eerily.

Just like everyone else's attacks on Li Tian Master earlier, as if erased by something.

Caught off guard, Li Tian Master's sword strike had already reached him.

"Ha!"

With a low shout, Luo Chen crossed his arms in front of him, forcefully blocking the sword light.

*Sssss...*

A harsh scraping sound rang out. In the blink of an eye, his protective qi armor shattered.

The Mystic Dust Armor itself was slashed open with a large gash.

The sword light entered his body, and Luo Chen's flesh, as tough as a true artifact, was torn open under this strike.

Even a foul, rotting odor faintly emanated.

"Corpse Rot Poison!"

Luo Chen's expression shifted slightly. The force of the impact blasted him back over a hundred zhang, crashing into the wall near the main tomb.

His face slightly dark, he looked down at his arm.

How many years had it been?

Aside from Azure Frost, how long had it been since anyone had inflicted such direct damage on his physical body?

"Is this the method you said you had specially prepared for me?"

Rumbling sounds also came from nearby—the result of attacks on Meng Shentong and Old Matron Hongye.

Li Tian Master slowly flew over from the dust, looking down at Luo Chen from on high, his face full of contempt, his longsword pointing diagonally.

"So what if your flesh is strong? This Corpse Rot Poison of mine was taken from the barbarian Taiye Giants, nurtured for decades with secret methods, specifically to counter those with powerful bodies!"

Luo Chen lowered his head to look at his arms. The long, slanted sword wound was deep enough to show bone. The lingering Corpse Rot Poison clung like maggots to a bone, and neither his mana nor his source power could remove it.

It was only because his physical body had been tempered to the extreme, hindering further penetration of the toxin.

Otherwise, in the blink of an eye, this poison would have spread throughout his body like it had with Old Matron Hongye, endangering his Purple Mansion.

But if it were only this, it wouldn't have caused his expression to change.

What he truly feared was the peculiar situation where his own attacks had been nullified.

He let his arms hang down, and the Mystic Dust Armor automatically healed, covering them.

Without turning his head, he asked, "Fellow Daoist Meng, what exactly is a Gu House?"

Earlier, Meng Shentong had let out a cry of alarm and decisively tried to flee.

This showed he had some understanding of Li Tian Master's methods.

On another patch of ground, Meng Shentong was quite disheveled, a somewhat damaged shield held in front of him.

"A so-called Gu House is a special entity constructed from a massive number of Gu insects. You can think of it as a Gu Dao magical artifact."

"This method has been rumored in the Southern Frontier for a long time, but no one has ever been able to create one. That's because building a complete and stable Gu House requires a very deep and clear understanding of Gu insects. You also need to arrange them in order according to their habits, types, and functional characteristics."

"The greater the number, the more stable the Gu House. The higher the grade of the main Gu, the stronger the Gu House's power!"

"And each type of Gu House has very unique functions, often appearing miraculous! Because it is a miracle born from the combination of thousands of Gu insects!"

A miracle?

Luo Chen's eyes narrowed slightly. His Crimson-Eyed Golden Pupils had unknowingly activated, sweeping across the surroundings.

Vaguely, there were indeed many overlooked auras rising and falling, moving around them according to a specific rhythm.

Having been in the Southern Frontier for many days, he was no longer as unfamiliar with Gu insects as he had been at the start.

These things weren't actually that powerful. You could think of them as a different kind of demon beast, or as special magical tools or artifacts.

What was truly formidable were the people—cultivators who developed and exploited the functions of Gu insects to assist their cultivation and aid in battle, achieving twice the result with half the effort.

If you really wanted to compare, a fourth-order Gu insect, which many low-level cultivators sought but couldn't obtain, was at most on the level of a high-grade magical artifact or an ordinary true artifact.

But within this Gu House, the fourth-order Gu insects involved were far more than one or two!

The number of other mid-to-low-grade Gu insects was in the hundreds or thousands, countless!

"Li Tian Master, truly worthy of being the Southern Frontier's number one Gu Refiner!"

In the midst of his praise, Luo Chen's body trembled slightly. The slashed Mystic Dust Armor healed automatically, and his protective qi armor was generated anew.

Not only that, but the Ping Shan Crown on his head flashed with brilliance, and a curtain of earthy yellow light cascaded down, enveloping him.

Li Tian Master watched this scene without any obstruction, instead showing a hint of appreciation.

"I've heard that Dan Sect's Luo Chen has another Dao name, 'Demon-Slaying Sage,' highlighting his formidable combat power. Now it seems, this experience in facing enemies is indeed rare."

Luo Chen asked coldly, "So, the special function of your Gu House is to nullify our attacks?"

Li Tian Master did not deny it. He nodded and said, "Exactly so. This Wind-Wave Pavilion consumed the 3,270 types of Gu insects I painstakingly collected. It is the crystallization of all my wealth and effort. Any cultivator within it will have their attacks automatically dissipate, like wind and waves subsiding. But I, within it, am unaffected. Inside the Wind-Wave Pavilion, I am unimpeded!"

Upon hearing this, Meng Shentong's face instantly turned deathly pale.

Old Matron Hongye, who was struggling with the Corpse Rot Poison, looked as if she had lost her parents.

"Can it really nullify any attack?" Luo Chen asked.

Li Tian Master smiled. "You can try!"

Try, then!

Luo Chen was not polite at all. With his left hand forming a seal, a phoenix flew slowly. With his right hand forming a palm, he could split mountains and crack stones.

Between left and right, he was simultaneously using both spells and martial arts.

This sight secretly shook Li Tian Master's heart.

Of course, the result did not surprise him.

After the two attacks were launched, they vanished without a trace, disappearing into the void as if by magic.

"How is it?" he asked with a chuckle.

Luo Chen did not answer him, but fell into silence.

Seeing his silence, hearing Hongye's wails, and watching Meng Shentong retreat in panic, Li Tian Master laughed.

"So, you'd better just give up!"

Hong and Meng seemed somewhat tempted.

But Luo Chen, amidst his contemplation, suddenly spoke, "If it were truly that powerful, then why did you, who were so furious earlier, not press your advantage? Why did you suddenly stop after just one sword strike?"

"I..."

"Just bluster!" Luo Chen's voice suddenly rose. "What you call nullification is nothing but lies. In reality, it's just a massive number of Gu insects absorbing and digesting the attacks. If our attacks were strong enough, how much could your Wind-Wave Pavilion absorb?"

Li Tian Master's expression remained unchanged. "You can try again?"

Luo Chen never lacked a spirit of experimentation.

Sword qi pulsed at his fingertip, but did not leave his body. He turned into an arrow leaving the bowstring, charging straight at Li Tian Master.

Seeing this, Li Tian Master immediately understood that the other party had unknowingly seen through some of the flaws in his Wind-Wave Pavilion.

Attacks that didn't leave the body wouldn't be absorbed.

But what truly shocked him was...

"Why aren't you affected by the Corpse Rot Poison?"

*Shua!*

A sword slashed down. Li Tian Master sidestepped to dodge, his eyes full of astonishment.

It was Luo Chen's nature to dislike answering such questions that revealed his own secrets during a battle. Even when he spoke, it was often to attack the opponent's psyche.

But this battle wasn't his alone.

As sword qi pulsed and flowed from his hand, he spoke calmly.

"The reason you were furious but held back was that you were waiting for the Corpse Rot Poison to take effect on me and Hongye, and waiting for Meng Shentong to completely lose his will to fight."

"Three against one, in a frontal battle, what do we have to fear?"

As he spoke, Luo Chen stepped with the Moon Step, his body tracing a strange arc in the air. Sword qi broke through the air, followed closely by his palm.

Under the simultaneous assault of sword and palm, Li Tian Master was caught off guard and blocked with all his might.

*Boom!*

With the added force, he crashed to the ground like a meteor.

Smashing a deep pit at the location of the black coffin.

Luo Chen stood in the air, looking down, his black hair flying, his eyes deep and profound.

"Why are you two fellow Daoists afraid to fight?"

"Even if a magical artifact cannot leave the body, it can still expand and contract. It won't be affected by this useless Wind-Wave Pavilion."

At his reminder, Hong and Meng also reacted.

A figure suddenly shot out from the pit.

Three attacks also landed simultaneously.

There was a flying sleeve coiling, a great seal pressing down, and a shimmering long blade revealing its true form, breaking through the air.

Facing the three attacks, the figure moved with incredible speed, dodging the great seal, the flying sleeve unable to catch up, and then reversed course along the transparent, watery long blade.

In a daze, Meng Shentong seemed to see a four-armed monster with compound eyes, spinning rapidly.

His mind was greatly shaken. He activated the Vacuum Gu within his body, vanishing into the void.

But the monster was extremely fast. Its blade-like arm stabbed straight into the void, following the hilt of the blade and piercing into his body.

Three more blade-hands followed.

*Shua!*

With a four-handed tear, Meng Shentong's body was ripped apart like a tattered cloth sack.

A bewildered Nascent Soul figure emerged into the void.

He instinctively wanted to flee, but a terrifying suppressing force came from all directions, trapping him as if in a swamp, unable to move at all.

*Shua!*

A blade slashed down!

The Nascent Soul figure instantly collapsed. Before the vast mana that composed the Nascent Soul could explode, the countless Gu insects in the Wind-Wave Pavilion absorbed and devoured it.

All of this took a long time to describe, but in reality, it happened in an instant after the three of them attacked.

Only then did the figure slowly turn around, staring fixedly at Luo Chen.

Luo Chen's pupils contracted slightly, and he uttered four words, "Ghost-Crying Evil Mantis!"

Then, the sword qi at his fingertip faded, and a blood-red sword tip slowly revealed its solid form.

"Who would have thought that the famous Li Tian Master is also skilled in the Dao of Body Refinement?"

Li Tian Master's compound eyes, which seemed to be made of thousands of eyes, stared fixedly at Luo Chen, and he spoke hoarsely.

"I also never expected that you would cultivate the *Hundred Poisons Body Refinement Art* to its great completion stage in just one year, so that even my Corpse Rot Poison is useless against you."

"When I sneak-attacked you with a palm strike earlier, crossing the Death Gate, I felt some residual poison on my hand. I thought it was just a coincidence."

"Now it seems, no matter how highly I estimate you, I still underestimate you!"

*Zheng!*

The Yuantu Sword was firmly grasped in his hand.

Luo Chen's heart settled slightly. Looking at the other's form—green-skinned, with two legs and four arms—many thoughts flashed through his mind.

This physical transformation of the opponent's was similar to the *Heavenly Roc Transformation* he had cultivated in his early years, both being demon-like changes.

But the principles were vastly different. The opponent should have used some kind of transformation-type Gu insect to achieve this level through a clever trick.

The form he had taken was that of a desolate beast recorded in ancient texts, the Ghost-Crying Evil Mantis.

If this were outside, he wouldn't care.

But right now, the Yin-Yang Grave was shrouded by the Wind-Wave Pavilion, putting him in the opponent's home ground.

In a short time, the Gu House Wind-Wave Pavilion had not only shown its function of absorbing and digesting attacks but also, in a flash, had a mysterious suppressing force.

Thus, before breaking the Wind-Wave Pavilion, he would have to be a bit more careful.

The tip of the sword rose slightly. Luo Chen took a deep breath and said no more.

Source power exploded beneath his feet, and his figure vanished instantly.

When he reappeared, he was right in front of Li Tian Master.

A horizontal slash of the sword was met by a cyan, blade-like arm.

Luo Chen did not dodge or evade, slashing his longsword straight at it.

*Crack!*

With just one strike, the cyan arm was severed cleanly.

Li Tian Master's expression changed drastically. He had never imagined that his blade-arm, comparable to a true artifact, would be so fragile.

With a thought, the Wind-Wave Pavilion stirred up another wave.

An inexplicable suppressing force surged forth, and Luo Chen's movement stalled.

Li Tian Master dodged the Yuantu Sword and stabbed forward. The severed arm was replaced by a Corpse Rot Sword.

Luo Chen casually blocked it, then seized the gap to counterattack.

His reaction speed was incredibly fast, and his sword angles were so tricky they were impossible to defend against.

From just a few simple exchanges, one could see the richness of Luo Chen's combat experience and the exquisite nature of his combat techniques.

But every time he was about to gain the upper hand, the omnipresent Wind-Wave Pavilion would slow his movements, allowing Li Tian Master to regain the initiative.

*Clang! Clang! Clang!*

The continuous sound of sword and blade clashing erupted wildly within the Yin-Yang Grave.

The piercing, sharp ring was almost deafening.

Old Matron Hongye hid within her simple defensive formation, watching the scene with eyes that couldn't keep up, her expression one of utter shock.

This battle wasn't particularly loud.

It wasn't like Adept-level cultivators, who could shatter mountains and split the earth with a single move.

But the fierce killing intent in the fine details was enough to make one's hair stand on end and scalp tingle.

Was this a battle between Body Cultivators?

Luo Chen's level of skill was not surprising.

He had already left a deep impression when he traveled the Southern Frontier under the alias of the Tyrant Cultivator Qi Tian.

But Li Tian Master, a friend she had known for many years, had actually hidden such peerless ability—this was truly shocking.

In the midst of the repeated close-quarters clashes, Old Matron Hongye gradually came to understand.

Li Tian Master's body refinement was specifically to complement the Gu House Wind-Wave Pavilion!

With this Gu House, ordinary Qi Refinement powerhouses couldn't launch large-scale long-range attacks at all. He could completely rely on his physical strength for close-quarters combat.

And even if there was a Body Cultivator of the same rank, under the suppression of the Wind-Wave Pavilion, the rhythm of the battle was entirely in his hands.

If it weren't for Luo Chen's overly rich skills and experience, he would have been taken down long ago.

"So, when did he cultivate this powerful physical body?" Old Matron Hongye was utterly perplexed.

And Luo Chen, who had been fiercely battling Li Tian Master for hundreds of exchanges, gradually came to understand.

In such an intense battle, he was even beginning to feel his source power flow slow down, yet the opponent showed no signs of fatigue.

The reason for this was definitely not that the opponent's Body Refinement realm was above his.

"It's the Devil Heart Gu!"

"The Devil Heart Gu excels at refining qi from essence. In this process, there is always a surplus, so it provides the opponent with a vast amount of blood and qi easily."

"Combined with the aid of this Wind-Wave Pavilion, his strength is inexhaustible."

With this thought, Luo Chen's desire to break the Wind-Wave Pavilion grew even more urgent.

After another clash of sword and blade, Luo Chen did not retreat but advanced. He sidestepped and rammed into the opponent, using his physical body to withstand a slash from one of the opponent's blade-arms.

Mountain-Crushing Strike!

After the collision, Luo Chen used the force to retreat, holding his sword in one hand and forming a seal with the other.

A powerful aura began to radiate from within his body.

This aura spread with incredible speed, instantly filling the Yin-Yang Grave and even spreading towards the vast lands of the underworld.

Seeing this, Li Tian Master knew something was wrong. He opened his mouth and spat out a wisp of illusory flame.

Across from him, Luo Chen also opened his mouth and spat out a wisp of golden flame.

The two flames met in the air, intertwining and writhing. But after a moment, the golden flame began to gain the upper hand.

Li Tian Master was shocked. This Boundless Void Flame, which he had obtained by chance in his early years, was quite miraculous.

Ordinary true flames were no match for it at all.

But when it met Luo Chen, it was suppressed twice in a row.

Once was earlier, when the other party was seizing his heart, and he wanted to block it but couldn't.

That was why he didn't dare to release it during the fight just now.

The other time was now.

He never expected that in a direct confrontation, it would be even more useless.

Without a second thought, a fierce look flashed across his face.

The three layers of blood shackles that had been sealing the wound on his chest directly detached from his body and pressed down towards Luo Chen.

"Roar!"

A black shadow roared out from Luo Chen's body, facing the three layers of blood shackles head-on.

It was Black King!

With these two delays buying him enough time, Luo Chen finally had enough.

The hand seal in his hand reached its end.

In an instant, the entire underworld, spanning thousands of li, was affected.

One by one, the skeletons and zombies wandering under the blood moon had their death qi extracted, falling to the ground senselessly.

One by one, the yin veins and crypts in the earth boiled and churned, emitting a dead, silent, and gloomy aura.

A sea of death qi surged towards Luo Chen.

Finally, it condensed on his fingertip!

"This sect master wants to know, can your Gu House withstand this move?"

A fierce light flashed in Luo Chen's eyes, and he pointed out a finger.

"Withering-Bloom!"
