# Chapter 1124: The Great Cataclysm Realm, Three Heavenly Calamities

What met his eyes was a delicate, unadorned face—pure and refined—with an expression of surprise that could not hide the weariness beneath it.

Ever since she had returned, Luo Chen had pretended not to be awake, secretly observing her movements.

Such caution was not due to excessive wariness on Luo Chen's part; rather, his current state left no room for carelessness.

Fortunately, her behavior was ordinary, and her movements in caring for his food and drink seemed quite practiced. Luo Chen could naturally perceive such obvious goodwill, which was why he had chosen to wake up on his own.

"Luo Chen?"

The woman in the thornwood hairpin and plain cloth skirt murmured the name, then, as if recalling something, instinctively grabbed at her collar, where a hint of skin was exposed.

"You were awake earlier?"

Luo Chen lay on the bed, a nasal hum escaping him as he replied, "Mm."

"Then that means when I was bathing and changing clothes earlier..." As she spoke, the woman's cheeks flushed red, appearing especially radiant in the flickering firelight.

But upon closer inspection of Luo Chen's condition, she saw that his body had not moved at all from start to finish, so he likely could not have seen what had happened earlier. She naturally understood that she had been overthinking.

"My name is Jing Lan. Jing as in the thornwood, Lan as in the orchid."

"Mm."

Another simple response, as if to say he understood.

Jing Lan carefully studied the man's chiseled, handsome face and asked cautiously, "Are you a native of the Great Cataclysm Realm itself, or did you come from outside?"

The Great Cataclysm Realm?

Luo Chen's expression did not change, but inwardly he was quite surprised.

Though Jing Lan received no response, it seemed her guess had already been confirmed.

"You've just woken up and your energy is low. Eat your meal and rest early! I still have work tomorrow. We can talk more once you're feeling better."

As she spoke, Jing Lan brought the spoon filled with thin congee over to him again.

Luo Chen glanced at her and opened his mouth.

The warm congee flowed down his throat and into his stomach, bringing a trace of warmth to this bitterly cold night.

Because of Luo Chen's active cooperation, the feeding went much more smoothly than before. Jing Lan fed him while wiping his mouth with a handkerchief, and it was finished in no time.

Jing Lan covered Luo Chen with the blanket, then went to wash the bowls and chopsticks on her own.

After finishing all this, Jing Lan said "Sleep early," and then there was no more human sound in the room, only the crackling of burning firewood.

Perhaps because his stomach was full, Luo Chen's body had mustered a bit more strength.

He laboriously turned his face to the right, his peripheral vision sweeping over areas he hadn't seen before.

In the small room, an iron chain hung down, suspending a pot. Below it was a place resembling a fire pit.

At this moment, firewood and a few white stones were burning together slowly in the fire pit.

In the corner opposite the fire pit, the woman slept in her clothes, quickly falling into a deep slumber.

But even in her deep sleep, Jing Lan's brows were still furrowed, as if troubled by something.

Luo Chen withdrew his gaze, and a wave of drowsiness washed over him.

"This body of mine now..."

With this thought, Luo Chen also sank into deep sleep.

...

The sound of footsteps rang in his ears, followed by a clattering noise.

Luo Chen could feel the blanket covering him being lifted partially. He opened his eyes accordingly, meeting those clear eyes directly.

"You're awake?"

"Mm."

"There was ghost rain all night last night. I need to go check on the fields, or this year's harvest will suffer. In the afternoon, I have to go to the tobacco workshop in town and won't be back. It gets hot during the day, so don't cover yourself too tightly with the blanket. I'll be back in the evening..."

Jing Lan rambled on for a few sentences. Before leaving, she hesitated slightly, then placed a rice ball where Luo Chen could reach it by turning his head.

"If you're hungry, make do with this!"

Having said that, the woman put on a straw hat, grabbed her tools, and hurried out the door.

The last thing that lingered in Luo Chen's ears was the sound of her locking the door.

Luo Chen stared blankly at that rice ball, his thoughts somewhat complicated.

He could roughly tell that this rice ball should have been her lunch. If he hadn't woken up, she probably wouldn't have left it, but now she had left it for him.

Pulling his thoughts back, Luo Chen silently repeated the two words Jing Lan had said before leaving.

"Ghost rain?"

That bone-chilling night rain from last night was called ghost rain by the people here?

And that thunder that could shake one's divine sense was also completely different from any he had experienced before in the Mountains and Seas Realm.

Moreover, according to Jing Lan, this place was called the "Great Cataclysm Realm." What kind of place was that?

"Where exactly have I ended up?"

Too many questions surfaced in his mind.

But in the end, they were all suppressed by Luo Chen, one by one.

Now was not the time to consider these issues. Restoring his condition was the top priority.

As long as he could recover to a certain state, he would have enough strength to protect himself, and exploring this place would be much easier.

But saying it was simple was one thing; actually doing it was another. Luo Chen could only smile bitterly inwardly.

At this moment, his vast divine sense seemed to be sealed, completely unsummonable.

And his previously most proud, abundant spiritual power was nowhere to be found.

Most importantly, his primordial spirit had already collapsed!

The primordial spirit was composed of the nascent soul and the spirit soul. Logically, if the primordial spirit collapsed, even a Divine Transformation Ascendant would fall with it.

But that Rosycloud had used some special technique at the time to separate his nascent soul and spirit soul. Then she sealed his spirit soul, refined the nascent soul formed from his spiritual power, and devoured it all.

"What ruthless methods, what venomous heart!"

Hatred flickered in Luo Chen's eyes, but the despair from before was gone.

Even if his cultivation had fallen to that of a mortal, and he couldn't even mobilize his divine sense, his physical body still remained.

And he could feel that his Purple Mansion had not actually collapsed. Treasures like the Primordial Unity Cauldron, the Yuantu Sword, and the Burial Bone Umbrella were still hidden within.

Although he couldn't mobilize them at all, as long as they were still there, that was fine.

"Before ascending, I consumed the first mature Demon-Devil Heart-Linking Fruit, bringing this physical body to the fifth rank of the Archaic stage and condensing true spirit origin blood."

"In my battle with Rosycloud, I burned through all my true spirit origin blood, but a small amount of ordinary origin blood remains."

"Right now, my meridians are shattered, and my physical body is severely injured. But as long as I can mobilize a small amount of origin blood, I can begin preliminary cleansing and treatment."

"This is not difficult for me!"

Luo Chen closed his eyes, silently sensing his body's condition, searching for a method of recovery.

But saying it was simple was one thing; in practice, it was quite complicated.

With his divine sense unusable, mobilizing origin blood to cleanse his body became extremely difficult.

For this situation, he had to find another path—using his strong will to drive the origin blood.

This method was quite unfamiliar to Luo Chen.

Because it was the technique of orthodox body cultivators!

Body cultivators did not cultivate immortal arts, did not raise divine sense. Their entire body's power was condensed into one, relying entirely on strong will to drive it.

This was something Wang Yuan had taught him when he first introduced Luo Chen to the path of body cultivation.

But later, Luo Chen's body cultivation took another path—one that relied on resource accumulation—and with the convenience of his spiritual sense and divine sense, he rarely used this most instinctive method.

Now, he could only clumsily use his will to drive his body, letting the origin blood hidden in various parts of his body wash through his flesh and meridians.

Chi...

Under the blanket, blood blossoms bloomed—the result of wounds splitting open wherever the origin blood passed.

Luo Chen was oblivious to it, ignoring the pain, and continued to persist.

Occasionally, amidst the pain, he could feel a sense of heat.

Jing Lan's words before leaving echoed in Luo Chen's mind. The daytime really was quite hot.

...

"How can this be? It's bleeding again. Wasn't it already healed?"

A worried, delicate cry rang out, followed by the motion of lifting the blanket.

Luo Chen woke from his daze and saw Jing Lan, covered in dust, cleaning his body.

Luo Chen forced a weak grin but said nothing.

The first time he had forcibly repaired his body, he hadn't controlled the force well, resulting in extensive bleeding all over. Those cracked, ferocious wounds were indeed quite frightening to an ordinary person.

Jing Lan's cleaning movements were gentle, but her speed was very quick.

First, she washed, then wrapped him in clean linen cloth. But when it came time to change his clothes, a look of embarrassment clearly appeared on the woman's face.

Luo Chen shook his head slightly to indicate it was fine, and Jing Lan breathed a sigh of relief before covering him with the blanket.

For now, he wouldn't wear anything.

"Wait for me a moment. I'll make dinner right away."

After disposing of the blood-stained gauze, Jing Lan went to wash up on her own.

But this time, she set up a curtain across from the fire pit, blocking Luo Chen's view.

When the smoky aroma mixed with the scent of rice filled the room, the smell of blood in the small hut seemed to be covered up, becoming warm and cozy again.

Jing Lan wolfed down a few bites first, then picked up her bowl and came to Luo Chen's side.

It was still the same feeding motion as before, as if she had done it many times, extremely familiar.

Facing Luo Chen's gaze, she was no longer as shy as yesterday, but much more at ease.

While feeding him congee, she casually talked about what she had seen and heard during the day.

"The sunward rice in the fields is growing well. It wasn't affected by the ghost rain. This year's harvest should be good."

"The tobacco workshop has a new manager. I heard he was sent from Yixian City."

"That man has a bit of a temper. Third Master Pu was just old and a bit slow in his movements, but when the manager saw him during an inspection, he beat him until he was unconscious. A few of us brought him back in the evening."

"If Manager Gu were still here, it would be fine. Manager Gu was strict, but he had a soft heart behind his sharp tongue. He treated us quite well before, and the production of Mi Immortal Tobacco was very stable. But now with Manager Yan in charge, life will probably be harder from now on."

"Fortunately, the work at the tobacco workshop usually only lasts until the end of October. After that, the heavenly winds start blowing, and it's not suitable for drying tobacco anymore."

...

Jing Lan talked, and Luo Chen listened, until the bowl of thin congee was finished.

"Sleep early."

"Mm."

That night, the fire pit was not kept burning.

Probably because there was no ghost rain.

After dawn, Jing Lan got up early.

Before leaving, she thought about leaving the rice ball for Luo Chen like the day before, but this time Luo Chen refused.

Jing Lan didn't insist. If she didn't eat enough and lacked energy, she might make mistakes while working. If the new Manager Yan saw that, it would be troublesome.

After she left, Luo Chen, as before, began his actions again.

His physical body was too powerful, and his meridians were extremely broad.

In normal times, this naturally brought many benefits.

But at this moment of severe injury, clearing the broken and blocked meridians was much more troublesome.

"Slow and steady!"

Luo Chen sighed and said this to himself.

...

Day after day passed. Even though Luo Chen never left the house, he still gained some fragmented understanding of the Great Cataclysm Realm from Jing Lan's words.

This was a place rich in resources but filled with dangers everywhere.

The ruler was a powerful existence known as the Carefree Realm Lord.

The place where Luo Chen was located was just a remote mountain village called Qingshan Town within the Great Cataclysm Realm.

Being remote did not mean safety; on the contrary, it was fraught with danger.

There were three great heavenly calamities that appeared from time to time, threatening lives.

Heavenly Wind, Ghost Rain, and Cataclysm Mist.

According to Jing Lan, she had picked Luo Chen up from the fields after a Heavenly Wind had passed.

These three great calamities were completely different.

Where the Heavenly Wind passed, not a blade of grass grew.

When Ghost Rain fell, one would sleep and never wake.

When the Cataclysm Mist spread, everything would fall silent and perish.

Among them, Heavenly Wind was the most common, Ghost Rain the most bizarre, and Cataclysm Mist the most domineering and dangerous.

Fortunately, that legendary Cataclysm Mist had not appeared for many years.

As long as Jing Lan and the others followed the instructions of the Qingshan Town authorities and responded appropriately when the calamities appeared, they could always preserve their lives.

On ordinary days, they made a living by planting crops.

In their spare time, they would take on odd jobs at some shops in town to earn some resources.

The job Jing Lan currently had was processing something called Mi Immortal Tobacco at the Qingshan Tobacco Workshop. The pay wasn't generous, but it included some scraps of something called Dreamstone—a mineral material used to resist the erosion of Ghost Rain. Normally, buying it would cost a lot. Even if the Qingshan Tobacco Workshop only offered some scraps, many people were eager for this job.

This was all the information Luo Chen had obtained about the Great Cataclysm Realm.

After all, Jing Lan was just a mortal who had never left the Qingshan Town area in her entire life. She had only heard of Yixian City.

Limited information, but it couldn't stop Luo Chen from inferring more.

For example, regarding the Dreamstone—after Luo Chen could move his hands and feet, he asked Jing Lan for one to observe.

In fact, Luo Chen had seen this Dreamstone before. It was the white stone that burned with the firewood in the fire pit during the ghost rain night.

After observing it, Luo Chen discovered that once this stone burned, it emitted a special smell.

After living beings absorbed it, they would sleep quite soundly, not even dreaming.

It seemed to have the effect of calming the mind and settling the spirit!

This made Luo Chen curious. How could mere mind-calming effects resist one of the three great calamities, the Ghost Rain?

Or was it not resisting the erosion of Ghost Rain itself, but something brought by the Ghost Rain?

Of course, these were just studies Luo Chen did in his spare time.

His main energy was still focused on recovering from his injuries.

One month later.

The tightly closed wooden door was slowly pushed open.

A figure walked out from inside.

Soft light spilled onto the outside, and just this one glance made the owner of the sight freeze.

The mountains were verdant green, and the water fields wound their way.

Three setting suns gradually faded, their thick yellow light splashing onto his cheeks.

"No wonder it's so hot during the day."

The man murmured to himself and casually sat down on the threshold.

On the winding mountain path, a group of figures came up from below, laughing and chatting. Among them was a quiet, serene figure who did not join the others' conversation. But when a middle-aged aunt pointed at him, that figure let out an exclamation and then hurriedly ran up.

"You're up!"

Jing Lan panted heavily, her eyes full of surprise as she looked at the man sitting on the threshold.

"Yes, finally up."

Luo Chen nodded slightly, a rare relaxed and carefree smile appearing on his face.

This time lying down, who knew how many years had passed.

(Chapter End)
