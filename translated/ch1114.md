# Chapter 1114: Ancient Totem Method, Golden-Winged Great Peng

Broken walls and collapsed beams.

The outline of a city came into view.

Several figures flew over and walked among the ruins, each wearing expressions of intense curiosity.

"In the midst of the wilderness, there is actually an ancient city built by humans!"

"These architectural styles are completely different from the five continents of the current Mountains and Seas Realm. Could they be from the ancient era?"

"Oh? On these walls, there seem to be remnants of murals? Are they depicting some kind of demon beasts?"

"That's strange. Aside from the Myriad Beast Sect, human sect buildings usually only carve various auspicious beasts. Why are so many ferocious beasts painted here?"

"It should be the Loulan Ancient City."

"Loulan?"

The crowd was surprised and looked curiously at Luo Chen.

Luo Chen thought for a moment, then began to explain in detail: "The five-continent sacred lands of the Mountains and Seas Realm were not originally native sects of this realm. They came from the Outer Mystic Heaven. After arriving in this realm, they blazed a trail, hunting demons and clearing the wilderness, gradually opening up the five-continent cultivation world. But this world also had its own indigenous human race."

Regarding the matters of the five-continent sacred lands of the Mountains and Seas Realm, Luo Chen occasionally told the group about them during their wilderness travels.

He had the *Outer Mystic Secret Record* gifted by Nether Spirit Divine Lord, so he could speak on most matters with considerable authority.

Combining that with some current classic records, deducing ancient events was not too difficult.

Gu Caiyi asked curiously, "Husband, do you mean this Loulan Ancient City was built by the indigenous human race of the Mountains and Seas Realm?"

"Yes." Luo Chen's gaze swept over the buildings that had begun to sandify after enduring the wear of time. Faint remnants of murals depicting various birds and beasts were still visible. "The indigenous people of this realm were weak. They were either enslaved by intelligent wild beasts or used as food by powerful ones. Therefore, out of natural fear and longing, they worshipped the wild beasts—the former masters of this world—as totems for their faith. These remaining murals are the evidence."

Because of fear, they worshipped.

Because of strength, they yearned.

This principle was not difficult to understand.

Gu Caiyi, lost in thought, subconsciously asked, "But why did they build their settlements in a resource-poor place like the wilderness? Oh, looking at it now, it doesn't seem poor at all."

That last sentence made everyone else laugh.

People generally believed the wilderness was dangerous and extremely poor in resources.

But during these few years of travel, Luo Chen and the others had discovered that the wilderness was completely opposite to what people described. It was not poor in resources at all; instead, there were surprises everywhere.

Ores scarce in the outside world, spirit medicines that had grown for thousands or even tens of thousands of years, and many extinct primeval species.

Aside from hunting high-level wild beasts, Gu Caiyi, Heavenjade, and the others had casually collected medicinal herbs, mineral veins, and even top-grade spirit stones worth more than all the wealth they had accumulated in their lifetimes of cultivation.

This might be related to the fact that no human cultivators had set foot in the wilderness for thousands of years.

Some resources that were extremely useful to human cultivators were insignificant in the eyes of wild beasts, and thus they had been unknowingly preserved.

Of course, if anyone thought they could enter the wilderness now to harvest its abundant cultivation resources, that would be wishful thinking.

Without the protection of a powerful Ascendant like Luo Chen, even ordinary Sages would be in mortal danger if they entered.

Those top-tier great wild beasts would not care about the esteemed status of Sages in the outside world.

The topic had strayed a bit. Gu Caiyi asked in confusion, "The five continents of the Mountains and Seas are all places with abundant resources. Why didn't the indigenous human race build cities in the heartlands and use the rich resources to strengthen themselves?"

Luo Chen sighed helplessly at this question.

"The answer is hidden within your question. Sometimes, Caiyi, you shouldn't rely on me too much. Try using your own thinking instead of asking!"

Gu Caiyi was startled, then immediately understood Luo Chen's intention.

Getting too accustomed to something leads to dependence.

But if Luo Chen ascended and left, who could she depend on then?

Her heart couldn't help but feel a little gloomy.

But soon, Gu Caiyi pulled herself together. At least these ten years of wilderness travel were an unforgettable, beautiful memory for her.

She turned her thoughts back to the previous question and instantly understood why Luo Chen had sighed helplessly.

The answer really was hidden within the question.

Gu Caiyi blurted out:

"Because they were weak, they were forced to leave the heartlands of the five continents and build cities and nations in the then-barren wilderness."

Luo Chen nodded. "Yes. Similarly, because they were weak, the wild beasts were defeated and retreated from the five continents into the wilderness. Their retreat led to the prosperity of the human race. But the Loulan Ancient City in the wilderness was destroyed by the invasion of a large number of wild beasts."

The historical truth buried under the yellow sand was thus simply and directly unearthed.

As the group sighed with emotion, their movements were not slow.

Figures scattered, searching among the broken walls and collapsed beams.

Treasure hunting!

Even the weak human race of the ancient times might have had some unique aspects.

If they could find something, it might serve as a stone from another mountain to aid their own cultivation.

Luo Chen smiled, swept his divine sense, and then fixed his gaze on the central area of the Loulan Ancient City.

With a leap, Luo Chen landed there.

With a wave of his sleeve, the dust and smoke cleared.

A huge altar was completely revealed. On the altar were blurry patterns and marks. Although most were broken, the outline of a formation was still visible.

A giant pillar lay toppled on the altar.

Judging by the layout of the place, it should have stood in the very center of the altar.

With a thought from Luo Chen, the pillar rumbled as it stood upright, inserted into the central groove of the altar.

Looking at it now, the pillar was painted with countless birds and beasts, appearing blood-red overall.

"Is this a totem pillar?"

Luo Chen's cultivation time was not long—at least, compared to normal Ascendant powerhouses, he was definitely a young man.

But his knowledge was by no means weak.

Starting from the Qi Refinement stage, Luo Chen had loved reading books. Not just those related to cultivation, but also records of customs, strange tales, and secret histories.

What stood before him now was clearly the most important part of the cultivation system of the ancient ancestors.

A totem pillar!

The birds and beasts carved on it were the forms of those powerful wild beasts.

And this altar...

As Luo Chen pondered, he waved his hand and a stream of materials flew out of his storage ring.

He calmly refined the materials and infused them into the broken patterns.

Gradually, the altar began to shimmer with spiritual light.

In the distance, Gu Caiyi and the others noticed the anomaly and flew over.

When they arrived, they saw Luo Chen already sitting on top of the totem pillar.

"If I'm not mistaken, this altar is similar to the enlightenment halls for mortals and the spirit-root testing ceremonies for cultivators."

Luo Chen murmured, as if explaining to the others, or perhaps strengthening his own confidence.

"But unlike normal enlightenment and aptitude testing, because of this totem pillar, the children of the ancient ancestors could use their own blood to resonate with the wild beast blood inside the totem pillar, thereby mastering certain special wild beast abilities."

"Is this the cultivation path of the ancient ancestors?"

"Body refinement? Qi refinement? Or neither, just using the power of wild beasts to strengthen themselves?"

"And how was the blood of those powerful wild beasts inside the totem pillar collected?"

As he pondered and questioned, the spiritual light of the surrounding altar gradually shone upon Luo Chen.

The totem pillar beneath him also began to tremble.

The totems of various birds and beasts flickered one by one, until finally, only one remained: a crane-like wild beast with red markings, a green body, a white beak, and a single leg. It was lifelike, spreading its wings as if to fly.

Luo Chen could feel that after he had re-activated the altar, a faint but infinitely potential power within the totem pillar was trying to burrow into his body.

"Heavenfire Crane?"

"No!"

"Judging by how well it matches my Fire Spirit Body and the potential of this bloodline power, its rank must be above that of the Heavenfire Crane wild beast."

"Moreover, although it is fire-attributed, it is born from wood, which is extremely similar to the characteristics of the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame."

"It's the divine bird, Bi Fang!"

In immortal legends, there are many gods of fire.

Such as the Phoenix, the Golden Crow, the Vermilion Bird, and Bi Fang.

But each fire god has its own emphasis.

The Phoenix, although in control of the Nirvana Sacred Flame, is more revered as the king of all birds.

The Golden Crow is actually a symbol of the sun. Whether such a divine bird truly exists in the world is still unknown.

But the divine bird Bi Fang is recorded in the classics as being authentically born from the fire of bamboo and wood. That is why it gave Luo Chen a feeling so similar to the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame.

Luo Chen became interested. He actively released his own mana barrier and guided that wisp of aura into his body.

As soon as this wisp of aura entered, it tried to mobilize Luo Chen's blood.

But how could a mere wisp of aura mobilize Luo Chen's source blood, which was nearly comparable to the fifth rank?

Luo Chen deliberately mobilized a portion of his source blood and let the aura guide it.

Then, this wisp of aura, carrying the source blood, began to circulate throughout Luo Chen's body.

Time passed, bit by bit.

One day, two days...

Perhaps because Luo Chen's physical body was too powerful, the flow of blood was somewhat slow.

After a long time, Luo Chen vaguely perceived that this seemed to be a meridian circulation route, a cultivation method.

It was vastly different from traditional body refinement arts, closer to Luo Chen's self-created *Myriad Path Convergence* from his early years. Its aim was to temper the cultivator's physical body in the direction of a wild beast.

"A totem body refinement art?"

While Luo Chen was comprehending, Gu Caiyi and the others had already taken up positions around him, forbidding any living being from disturbing him.

Suddenly!

As night fell, Kongchan abruptly looked up.

"Be careful!"

The other three spirit beasts and Gu Caiyi were puzzled and subconsciously looked up.

Under the night sky, they saw a meteor shaped like a broom, dragging a long fiery tail, coming from beyond the heavens.

Gu Caiyi's expression changed drastically. "It's a falling star!"

Without a second thought, she was about to shoot into the sky.

"Husband is comprehending the ancient method. Don't let this celestial phenomenon disturb him."

The other four spirit beasts were about to rise together, but a voice interrupted their movements.

"Everyone, stop!"

The group looked at Luo Chen with joy.

His expression was somewhat solemn as he said, "A meteor of this size is not something you can confront head-on. Come closer to me."

During these years of wilderness travel, everyone had grown accustomed to following Luo Chen's arrangements. They flew up to the altar in unison.

Luo Chen sat atop the totem pillar, looking at this unexpected visitor from beyond the heavens with a grave expression.

First, he released an umbrella, holding it over everyone's heads.

Then, a long sword appeared in his hand—it was Yuantu!

With a thought, sword qi burst forth.

A magnificent river of stars composed of sword light surged upward, charging straight at the falling star.

A Thousand Skies Converge, Qi Rushing the Starry Han!

Boom!

In an instant, the collision erupted with infinite light and heat.

Terrifying destructive power exploded in a single moment.

The entire earth shook, as if it had been pressed down by several zhang.

Everyone looked up. Above the Burial Bone Umbrella, stars fell like rain, fire filled the air, as if it wanted to destroy this world.

Luo Chen also stared blankly at this scene, seeming to gain some insight in his heart.

For an Ascendant powerhouse, it was not difficult to use human power to resist celestial phenomena.

In fact, they themselves could create terrifying celestial and terrestrial changes.

But the destructive power of this huge falling star was still somewhat beyond Luo Chen's imagination.

His frontal strike of A Thousand Skies Converge had not even completely destroyed it.

With a loud crash, the remnants of the meteor crashed into the Loulan Ancient City.

The already sandified Loulan Ancient City, under this impact, instantly turned to dust.

This scene had a kind of beauty, as if history had completely perished.

Luo Chen, lost in thought, used his fingertip to draw over a wisp of starfire.

"Using one's own powerful strength to disrupt the normal operation of heaven and earth's origin qi, thereby unleashing destructive power. This grand technique of the natural universe is almost indistinguishable from a divine ability in its power. If I were to do it, how would I go about it?"

After the turmoil of heaven and earth had passed.

The night was hazy. The bone umbrella was put away.

Gu Caiyi and the others all leaped up, looking at Luo Chen with even greater reverence than before.

This power, which could almost destroy the world, had been cut down by Luo Chen with a single sword strike. Even though they had witnessed it many times during their wilderness travels, they were still awestruck.

"Madam, that falling star was quite large. Under the master's sword, there should still be remnants," Heavenjade reminded.

Gu Caiyi nodded. "Yes. Such extraterrestrial meteorites are top-grade materials for artifact refining. Let's go find them quickly."

Without a second thought, the few of them flew off in the direction where the falling star had descended.

Luo Chen remained seated on the totem pillar. His mind was no longer on the totem body refinement art of the Loulan Ancient City. That technique was only crude to him, not even comparable to *Myriad Path Convergence*. What filled his mind now was the scene of the falling star descending and the starfire burning the city.

If he could master such a method, it would surely be a great divine ability on par with the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger and A Thousand Skies Converge!

But how could he master it?

Luo Chen released his restrictions. His primordial spirit subconsciously emerged and began to gradually merge with this heaven and earth.

...

Deep within the wilderness, at the farthest reaches of the vast desert.

There was a great mountain flowing with golden sand. Inside the mountain was a cave called the Golden Sand Cave.

The cave was filled with the skeletal remains of humans and demons, a terrifying sight.

A huge divine bird sat at the top, looking down at the tiny human below.

From its mouth came a voice that was deafening, but which it considered gentle.

"Little friend Qi Ying, do you have any leads on the Relic Pill I ordered you to refine?"

The human, standing amidst the skeletal remains, showed neither humility nor arrogance.

"If it were just the Relic Pill passed down from the Buddhist lands of the Western Desert, I could refine it with ease. But the Relic Pill that the Great Peng King wants to refine is not so simple. It still lacks many main ingredients."

The Golden-Winged Divine Bird snorted coldly. "The most important main ingredient is the relic left behind by the death of a high monk from the Hanging Temple. What else could be missing? I heard that you are a disciple of a human alchemy master. With this most important ingredient in hand, yet you have made no progress. Are you trying to deceive me and deliberately delay?"

The cultivator named Qi Ying sighed inwardly.

He actually had some idea of how to refine the Relic Pill the other party wanted.

But he was afraid that the day he finished refining it would be the day he died.

Suddenly!

A violent tremor came from outside, shaking the Golden Sand Cave until the sea of bones surged and the golden sand rustled down.

The Golden-Winged Great Peng King was stunned for a moment, then seemed to think of something and immediately rushed out.

Qi Ying also followed without a second thought.

What he saw was a falling star with a fiery tail plummeting from the nine heavens in the distant sky, and a vast sword light surging upward against the current.

The collision between the two burst forth with endless light and heat, actually stinging his eyes.

He couldn't help but close his eyes, but his ears caught a voice filled with ecstasy.

"A falling star from the heavens must contain extraordinary iron within. Perhaps it can help me refine a spirit treasure. I am truly tired of the humiliation of being suppressed by human spirit treasures!"

"Qi Ying, wait here. Don't wander off. I'll go and come back."

"Remember, if I return and don't see you, don't blame me for being ruthless!"
