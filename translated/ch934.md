# Chapter 934: Earth Treasure Xirang, an Old Monster Emerges

"Hand it over!"

Luo Chen was not surprised by Black King's attempt to claim credit.

This fellow loved doing things like this.

Last time, when they were pursuing the Demon Emperor, everyone was busy exploring traces of the great demon, but Black King alone had sneaked into Dieyun Sect and swiped several dozen jars of fine wine for him.

And now, he was at it again.

However, considering the capabilities of the Divine Origin Sage, Black King hadn't dared go too far back then, merely moving some good things off Divine Origin Peak.

A mass of black light emerged from Black King's mouth, and Luo Chen's divine sense probed into it.

Books!

A vast number of books, densely packed, hundreds upon thousands of them.

Among them, many were ancient texts from bygone eras, but there was no shortage of newer-looking scriptures either.

Luo Chen casually picked one up and glanced through it, and before he knew it, his mind had sunk into its contents.

*Detailed Explanation of the Five Elements Spell Arts*

After a long while, he pulled his attention away from it, an expression of admiration appearing on his face.

"I knew Divine Origin had a deep understanding of the Five Elements spell arts, but I never imagined it would be this profound. For each type of spell, at every level, he has insights and theories. In actual combat, when they cooperate with each other, their power multiplies."

"And judging by this scripture, it's clear this was merely written during his early Sage stage. Truly a genius for the ages!"

As he praised, Luo Chen scanned through the names of the other texts at lightning speed.

*Supplement to the Yangyi Dao Method*
*Bright Void Sword Canon*
*Seven True Core Formation Arts*
*Five Elements Divine Pill*
...

There were so many texts that Luo Chen's eyes nearly glazed over.

Unlike most high-level cultivators who only collected high-level Dao methods, Divine Origin Sage's collection also included a large number of mid- and low-level techniques.

The *Seven True Core Formation Arts*, which recorded seven superior-grade core formation secret arts, was among them.

Not only that, but the *Dragon-Slaying Art* that Luo Chen had learned in his early years was also present!

Luo Chen briefly flipped through it and was astonished to discover that the wondrous soul-slashing effect he had achieved after cultivating it to Great Perfection had actually been predicted by Divine Origin.

However, Divine Origin had experimented many times back then, found it impossible to succeed, and merely made a prediction before ceasing to advance this art further.

He never expected that Luo Chen would instead confirm his conjecture.

Looking at the dazzling array of collected texts, Luo Chen's mood improved greatly.

If he could digest these scriptures, not only would his knowledge increase greatly, but he would also be able to reach a higher level in the Dao of spell arts!

Just think about it—Divine Origin Sage had never comprehended the true meaning of laws, yet he could unleash powerful spells that resembled divine abilities. This showed his level of attainment in this area.

For an ordinary person, obtaining these things might not be of much use.

But for Luo Chen, it was like finding a pillow just when he was feeling sleepy.

"Maybe I can even use this to create another fire-attribute divine ability suited to me?"

Luo Chen murmured, a glimmer of hope in his heart.

Unlike last time, when he had comprehended the true meaning of Withering-Bloom and then smoothly realized the divine ability Withering-Bloom Dao Finger, this time, after comprehending the fire origin within the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land, Luo Chen had not gained a corresponding divine ability.

In fact, this was the norm!

Throughout the ages, many had comprehended the true meaning of laws.

But divine abilities were often powerful means that experts gradually summarized and improved upon through extensive cultivation, slowly emerging from ordinary spells.

This was especially true for the most basic law true meanings like the Five Elements origin. They merely allowed cultivators to understand their fundamental principles. The corresponding divine abilities had to be developed by oneself or rely on guidance from predecessors.

Take that batch of Dao Sect prodigies who comprehended law true meanings in the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven back then—none of them had created any divine abilities on their own.

It was only later, relying on rewards from their sects, that they learned some great divine abilities left by predecessors.

As for whether those were suitable for them, Luo Chen had no way of knowing.

However, creating one's own divine ability was a time-consuming matter. Luo Chen was in no hurry for the moment.

He put away the vast collection of texts in one go, then patted Black King's thick dragon back.

"And the rest?"

Black King was startled, then chuckled slyly.

"I knew I couldn't hide it from my master's discerning eye."

As he spoke, another mass of black light emerged from his mouth.

But this time, the brilliant five-colored radiance was so intense that even Black King's source power could barely suppress it.

Luo Chen's gaze sharpened, and he uttered softly, "Five-Colored Pagoda!"

It was Divine Origin Sage's lifebound treasure!

In that great battle, Divine Origin had not used many treasures—only two, in fact.

One was a crystalline flying sword, and the other was this pagoda.

The former had been destroyed under the Grand Five Elements Sword Formation, while the latter had fought fiercely against Luo Chen's lifebound treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, for a long time before finally breaking free to cooperate with Divine Origin in executing that extremely powerful combined spell.

This showed how extraordinary this treasure was!

What caught Luo Chen's attention the most was that, like his Primordial Unity Cauldron, this treasure also possessed all five elements.

"Divine Origin and I are truly similar. Not only do we share the same five-spirit-root constitution, but even the lifebound treasures we chose are so alike."

Sighing with emotion, Luo Chen took the Five-Colored Pagoda and examined it carefully.

At this moment, because its master had fallen and their hearts and minds were connected, the pagoda had suffered severe damage.

A large number of array patterns had collapsed, and spiritual energy was constantly leaking out.

This was also the main reason Luo Chen had discovered that Black King had secretly stashed this thing away.

As he studied it, Luo Chen gradually discovered some of the pagoda's wondrous uses.

Similar to the Primordial Unity Cauldron, its main function was suppression!

From this, one could also see that Divine Origin's early cultivation direction was quite similar to Luo Chen's.

Five-spirit-root cultivators had abundant mana, but during cultivation, they could easily absorb impure spiritual energy.

Therefore, besides its killing function, a lifebound treasure had to also have the effect of suppressing mana agitation and refining mana impurities.

"If it were at its peak, this treasure's value would be no less than the Primordial Unity Cauldron. It's just a pity..."

Luo Chen shook his head and tossed the Five-Colored Pagoda away.

This thing was too severely damaged and was of little use to him.

Perhaps if it were repaired, it could regain a third of its peak power, but that would be too time-consuming and labor-intensive.

Luo Chen, as he was now, did not lack one or two such treasures.

Seeing this, Black King's eyes instantly lit up.

But before he could receive the pagoda, the thing spun around and fell back into Luo Chen's hands.

"Master?"

Luo Chen paid no attention to his confusion.

Because at this moment, all of Luo Chen's attention was focused on the Five-Colored Pagoda.

Not only was his divine sense scanning it and his mana pouring into it, but his fingertips were also striking out streams of hand seals that landed on the pagoda.

As the hand seals took effect, the incomplete arrays were drawn into motion, beginning to operate one after another.

Although it was quite unsmooth, Luo Chen didn't care.

What he cared about was something else!

A wisp of Withering-Bloom Fire emerged and landed on the pagoda, beginning to slowly burn it.

Due to the power of this fifth-rank divine flame, Black King dared not make any more noise and could only carry Luo Chen as he flew south at high speed.

After a long time, Luo Chen's slightly surprised muttering came from his back again.

"I almost missed it!"

"I never imagined Divine Origin would be willing to invest such a huge cost. When he refined his lifebound treasure, he actually added a small piece of Xirang!"

He was absolutely certain. Under various artifact-refining tests, he had indeed detected the presence of Xirang within the Five-Colored Pagoda.

Although it was very faint—estimated to be no more than a fingernail-sized piece in total—

But that was the legendary Xirang!

Legend had it that just a small piece of Xirang could block floods and fill seas!

Of course, the small piece inside the Five Elements Pagoda couldn't compare to the "small piece" in mythology.

But regardless, its essence remained unchanged.

It was, after all, Xirang!

It was the ancestor of many high-grade earth-attribute mineral materials like Su Ni and Yan Tu.

This kind of "earth treasure" was incredibly difficult to find, and Luo Chen understood this deeply.

Back in the Demon-Fall Land, the "Tianrang" he had seen was a half-finished product that the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining had attempted to create by reverse-refining Xirang, using a large amount of earth-attribute materials combined with the true meaning of the earth origin law.

From this, one could see that even a generation's Devil Lord could not find this substance. For Divine Origin Sage to be able to add this material to his lifebound treasure showed how deep his fortune was.

"I wonder where he got it from?"

The thought flashed by, but only briefly.

Right now, Luo Chen was only filled with excitement and had no desire to delve deeper.

Back when Heaven Smelter had refined the Primordial Unity Cauldron for him, he had said that if he could add a top-grade earth treasure like Xirang to the cauldron, it could become even more perfect!

Just how perfect it could become, Luo Chen didn't know, but he knew that at least the tragic situation where his lifebound treasure had developed cracks last time would not occur.

"It's my gain now, haha!"

Amidst his laughter, Luo Chen ignored the need to restore himself to peak condition. Using his recently recovered mana, he drove the Withering-Bloom True Flame to begin fiercely burning and refining the Five-Colored Pagoda.

He wanted to completely destroy all the arrays on the pagoda, discard the useless materials, and extract the raw materials he needed.

Xirang was the most important target!

But the other materials that could merge with Xirang were certainly not of low grade.

If they were suitable, Luo Chen would also preserve them.

Then, through artifact-refining techniques, he would integrate them into the Primordial Unity Cauldron, thereby enhancing the foundation of his lifebound treasure.

Black King could feel the movements on his back and naturally understood his master's intentions.

He couldn't help but feel secretly dejected.

That was a treasure he had stealthily picked up while cleaning the battlefield!

The faint aura of Xirang within it held a natural attraction for him, who had merged with the remnant soul of the desolate beast Stone Dragon.

"Oh well, it's just scrap metal anyway. Let master have his fun. I still have other treasures!"

Black King thought smugly, carrying Luo Chen as they flew farther and farther away, the Southern Frontier seemingly already in sight.

...

The name "Mountains and Seas Realm" had a long history.

It originated from the fact that this realm had countless mountains and numerous rivers and seas, hence the name Mountains and Seas Realm.

The realm was divided into five continents: East, South, West, North, and Central.

But this was not the entirety of the Mountains and Seas Realm. It was merely that a large number of humans inhabited these five landmasses, so the realm's geography was defined with them as the center.

Beyond the five continents lay the Million Great Mountains, the Endless Demon Sea, and the desolate, uninhabited wilderness.

Take the Southern Frontier, for example. Why was it considered so mysterious in the eyes of cultivators from other continents?

Not only because the cultivators of this continent had strange temperaments and were difficult to approach, but also because the Southern Frontier's geography had several natural barriers that blocked active exploration by low-level cultivators from outside.

For instance, the Southern Ocean contained countless gu insects, cutting off the connection between south and north.

And in the bordering regions of the southeast and southwest, there were dangerous places known as the "Mist Wilds," which were extremely difficult to cross.

The Southern Frontier had many mountains, was soaked with moisture, and filled with miasma. When these gathered among the mountain forests and wilderness, they formed vast amounts of mist.

These mists seemed only to block vision, but in reality, they contained a large amount of toxins, making them hard to guard against.

Low-level cultivators simply dared not venture deep into them, let alone cross through them.

But for native cultivators of the Southern Frontier, the Mist Wilds, which cultivators from other continents considered extremely dangerous, were natural treasure troves!

Where living beings found it hard to approach, heavenly materials and earthly treasures often abounded within!

Moreover, because no one disturbed them, the treasures inside grew more precious as the years accumulated.

Thus, over time, a profession emerged in the Southern Frontier's cultivation world—the Mountain Seeker!

As the name implied, they were people who sought treasures in the mountains.

They had similarities with the herb gatherers of the Eastern Wilderness and the fishermen of the Northern Sea.

All of them ventured into remote, uninhabited places to find treasures.

But unlike those who gathered herbs in the mountains or fished in the sea, the requirements for Mountain Seekers were higher.

They not only needed formidable combat power but also outstanding knowledge, lest they venture hard into the Mist Wilds only to miss treasures they didn't recognize.

Cheng Bai was one such Mountain Seeker.

His cultivation level was not high, only around mid-Initiate stage, but his background was considerable. He was subordinate to one of the thirty-six cave masters of Wind Thunder Gorge—the strongest among them, the Thunderclap Cave Master.

In the outside world, no one usually dared to provoke him.

But at this moment, he was extremely cautious.

Being in the Mist Wilds, one not only had to guard against the ubiquitous poisonous miasma and the elusive gu demons but also be especially wary of other Mountain Seekers.

Once you exposed your whereabouts or revealed a weakness, others wouldn't care what your background was. Killing for treasure was the most common thing.

With a poison-resisting pill in his mouth and a stealth robe on his body, Cheng Bai advanced rapidly toward his destination.

"According to the map I took from that Mountain Seeker I killed last time, not far ahead in the Poison Dragon Pool, there's a seven-zhang-long, tail-broken alligator dragon."

"Alligator dragons are common, but a tail-broken one is rare."

"Generally, when a tail-broken alligator dragon appears, it's because a Formless Gu larva has parasitized it, causing unbearable itching and pain, leading it to voluntarily break off its tail."

At the thought of the Formless Gu, even Cheng Bai's calm heart couldn't help but grow a little hot.

The Formless Gu was one of the high-grade gu insects.

It was a famous combat-type gu insect that could make a cultivator's form formless and their figure vanish, providing excellent assistance whether in battle or escape.

If he could capture one, even just a juvenile, once he sold it, he would have no worries about resources for cultivating to the Adept stage in the future.

As for the sales channel, he had already inquired about it before coming.

The Thunderclap Cave Master was cultivating a spell called the *Flash Thunder Method* and urgently needed a Formless Gu to assist him.

If he captured one, he could present it to his superior.

Given the Thunderclap Cave Master's well-known generous nature, at the very least, he would receive a large reward. At best, he might be taken in as a disciple, giving him hope of reaching the Adept stage in the future.

"I must succeed!"

Cheng Bai tightened his grip on the fork in his hand. This was a low-grade treasure he had specially borrowed from a trusted friend to capture the tail-broken alligator dragon.

However, as he carefully made his way to the Poison Dragon Pool, he did not see any trace of the tail-broken alligator dragon.

"Could it be asleep?"

"That shouldn't be!"

"It's spring right now. Whether it's hunting or mating and reproducing, this is their most active season."

Cheng Bai patiently inspected the area and finally found a patch of grass that had been crushed.

So it hadn't moved its nest!

It must have gone out hunting.

Cheng Bai licked his lips and began setting up a simple trapping formation around the Poison Dragon Pool.

He planned to capture the tail-broken alligator dragon in one fell swoop when it returned.

But after waiting a whole day and night, there was still no sign of it.

He had patience, but if his prey had gone out hunting and ended up dying instead, then he would have made a wasted trip.

Taking a deep breath, Cheng Bai first checked the formation he had set up, then followed the traces and began to track the tail-broken alligator dragon's movements.

Ten li, fifty li, a hundred li...

When it exceeded the alligator dragon's normal activity range, Cheng Bai's heart began to tighten.

What kind of prey would make a tail-broken alligator dragon leave its territory and chase that far?

Suddenly!

His pupils contracted, and his body trembled slightly.

In his line of sight, up ahead, a large number of low-level demon beasts were prostrate on the ground, trembling and unable to move, as if suppressed by something.

Among them, an alligator dragon without a tail was quite conspicuous.

That was the target of his deep foray into the Mist Wilds!

But that wasn't the main point. The main point was the reason so many demon beasts had gathered.

A nameless barren mountain stood amidst the group of demon beasts.

Above that barren mountain, dark clouds were gathering, with thunder and lightning flashing.

This celestial phenomenon didn't seem man-made, as if it were naturally occurring.

"Could it be that a rare treasure is emerging?"

Legend had it that when top-grade heavenly materials and earthly treasures emerged, they often attracted celestial phenomena.

Especially when high-grade spirits and monsters became sentient, they would trigger heavenly changes, similar to when great demons transformed or when experts underwent tribulation.

Moreover, unlike intelligent great demons, such spirits grew up in ignorance and had extremely poor ability to deal with tribulations.

Often, after successfully becoming sentient, they would fall into a state of extreme weakness.

If one could take advantage of their weakness and claim them, the gains would far exceed those from a tail-broken alligator dragon or a Formless Gu larva!

In an instant, Cheng Bai's heart burned with passion!

But the next moment, his expression changed drastically.

A small cauldron, uncontrollably, flew out from within the barren mountain, spun around in the dark clouds, then retracted its radiance and sank back into the mountain's belly.

Seeing this scene, Cheng Bai's heart turned ice-cold in an instant.

"No!"

"This isn't a rare treasure emerging. It's a powerful expert refining a top-grade treasure here!"

He didn't dare to covet it. Controlling his aura, he carefully began to retreat.

However, a rough voice came from within the barren mountain, shaking his qi and blood and rattling his soul.

"Young friend, since you've come, why rush to leave?"

The voice was like thunder. Where it passed, low-level demon beasts exploded, their skin splitting and flesh tearing, turning into clouds of blood mist.

Even the highly defensive tail-broken alligator dragon did not escape this fate.

Cheng Bai was greatly alarmed and quickly knelt to beg for mercy.

"Junior is a Mountain Seeker under the Thunderclap Cave Master. I had no intention of disturbing senior's secluded cultivation. I beg you to spare my insignificant life!"

"Oh?"

A faint note of confusion came from the rough voice.

"This old man has been out of the world for a long time. I don't recall the Southern Frontier having a character called the Thunderclap Cave Master."

As he spoke, an invisible force erupted without warning, pulling Cheng Bai from afar to the foot of the mountain.

Cheng Bai's face was filled with terror, but inwardly, he relaxed a little.

Although he didn't know where this old monster had come from, as long as there was room for negotiation!

The mountain rocks trembled, and a large gate slowly opened.

A figure walked out from within.

He wore a tall feathered crown on his head, a dazzling jade belt at his waist, and a suit of black armor that fit his body perfectly. His cloud-treading boots seemed to float in the clouds even though they stood on solid ground.

But it was his young, handsome face that made Cheng Bai's heart skip a beat.

Where had such a young old monster come from?

(End of Chapter)
