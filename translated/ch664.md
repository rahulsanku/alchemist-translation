# Chapter 664: I, Luo Chen, Have Never Forgotten This Grudge!

"Soul, return!"

The shout echoed again and again across the empty, boundless gobi. The brown-robed Daoist's throat seemed about to turn hoarse.

The urgent ringing of the bell spread through a thousand li like a sound that sought and seized souls.

When the heavy black clouds in the sky revealed a dragon head, bright light burst from the Daoist's eyes.

He gave a low roar.

"Raise the banner!"

His two companions did not hesitate. They poured mana into the white long banner before them.

In an instant, the banner snapped open and stirred wind and cloud.

The black clouds in the sky seemed to be tugged by it and began to sway.

As wind and cloud shifted, a blue light quietly retreated.

Before long, Heavenjade met Luo Chen and the other two, who were rushing over at high speed.

"Master!"

"You worked hard."

Luo Chen nodded and patted the spirit beast bag at his waist. Heavenjade obediently slipped inside.

Moya Cave Master and Tai Sui could not help glancing at that bag again.

To have a "transformed" demon cultivator run errands for him was far more convenient than ordinary third-tier spirit pets.

If nothing else, in terms of intelligence alone, the difference was like heaven and loam.

After putting Heavenjade away, Luo Chen rose again. Before long, he arrived a hundred li from that stretch of gobi.

The urgent and eerie bell sounds still entered their ears one after another.

Their eyes, however, quietly watched the scene ahead. All three held their breath and did not even dare to exhale loudly.

Divine sense transmissions echoed between them.

"What are they doing?" Tai Sui asked.

Although he knew many things about Demon-Fall Land, his knowledge was limited to this place. In the end, he had gained intelligence here, and his understanding of outside methods was confined to the memories of Ding Yi, the body's original owner.

"It seems to be... soul summoning?" Moya Cave Master watched for a long while. At last, his gaze settled on the white banner. "Building an altar of stone, guiding the road with a golden bell, reporting to gods and spirits through true words, and summoning the soul with a banner. This is the orthodox soul-summoning art of the Primordial Demon Sect from back then. It far surpasses the shallow soul-leading methods of clans."

Tai Sui was surprised.

"Cave Master, you understand it so well. Could it be that you are also proficient in this art?"

Moya Cave Master shook his head.

"Not that. Back when my artifact-forging path had gained some small reputation, I once helped a Yuan Demon Sect Initiate cultivator repair a Soul-Summoning Banner [TERM?]. At the time, that person and his junior brother fought enemies deep in the Demon Sea, but his junior brother unfortunately..."

Perhaps because the soul-summoning altar ahead was progressing slowly, Moya Cave Master simply began speaking of some past events.

Luo Chen did not interrupt him. Learning more about the other party's actions would provide some reference for whatever came next.

He was not completely ignorant about soul summoning either.

Back when he had been in Tianlan, there was a shop that specially sold something called soul-summoning incense.

However, once that incense was lit, it could only draw souls of blood relatives, and its effect was hard to predict. Whether it could truly summon back a clan member's soul was another matter entirely.

Unlike now...

Luo Chen's gaze fell on the dragon head in the clouds.

"That soul-summoning back then was only performed by a Yuan Demon Sect Initiate disciple, so its power was quite ordinary. How could it compare to now, where such a great commotion has been stirred?"

Moya Cave Master sighed and also turned his gaze toward the dragon-shaped soul that was constantly struggling and twisting within the black clouds.

"This soul is extremely powerful. Even though its will is scattered, the level of its soul alone gives me tremendous pressure. I wonder what realm it had while alive."

Tai Sui stared for a long while, and his brows gradually knitted together.

He searched his memories again and again until even his head began to hurt.

After several breaths, he suddenly spat out two words.

"Ascendant!"

Luo Chen was shocked. Moya Cave Master's pupils also widened.

"How is that possible? If it were an Ascendant expert, even if dead, how could its soul be forcibly summoned by three mere Golden Core cultivators?"

"Tai Sui, do not make frightening claims so casually. This soul is strong, but how could it be that terrifying?"

Tai Sui said uncertainly, "It should be correct. Though it now looks only at the Sage level, its essence is indeed that of an Ascendant soul. This feeling is like... the difference between my remnant thought and my main body."

Hearing this, Luo Chen and Moya Cave Master looked at one another.

Especially the latter. He had already instinctively begun retreating.

Tai Sui noticed this small movement and comforted him.

"Do not panic. This remnant soul has probably been dead for no less than a thousand or ten thousand years. It has long since lost consciousness. Even this soul body seems to have been forcibly condensed by some great power using the might of heaven and earth."

Seeing that the two did not believe him, he shook his head.

"I can sense the ancient aura on its body. It is absolutely not a creature of this age. According to my guess, it is either a loser from an ancient great war or a remote ancient existence. The rotten death intent is simply too obvious."

"However, such a thing should not be something ordinary Golden Core cultivators dare to provoke."

By the end, doubt also appeared on Tai Sui's face.

Moya Cave Master stopped retreating and looked curiously toward the three Golden Core cultivators.

"Who are they? Why would they build an altar and summon a soul here, as though they specifically came for this thing?"

While they were curious, Luo Chen spoke.

"They are people of Moluo Stream."

"Moluo Stream?"

"Hm? Devil Lord, you know them?"

Luo Chen's gaze was complicated as he looked at the brown-robed Daoist on the altar who was shaking the bell and shouting urgently.

Wu Qi!

Island Master of Shaman God Island.

At the same time, he was also an able subordinate under Blood Nightmare Moluo of Moluo Stream.

The last time Luo Chen had seen him was at the Jadecold City auction. At that time, Wu Qi had only been at Golden Core Stage Six.

Now that they met again, he had already crossed the middle Golden Core stage and reached the late Golden Core stage. His mana was vigorous, and he looked not far from Golden Core Stage Eight.

As for the two people beside him, Luo Chen had also met them once before.

They were Eagle Demon [TERM?] and the Hundred-Transformations Old Monster [TERM?].

Both were famous late Golden Core great cultivators in Moluo Stream!

After Luo Chen explained quietly, Tai Sui's expression changed greatly.

"If it is as you say, these three people respectively belong under Blood Nightmare Moluo, the First Stream Lord, and the Split Heaven Stream Lord. Does their arrival mean that the three great Stream Lords of Moluo Stream have also come to Demon-Fall Land?"

Moya Cave Master, however, suddenly understood.

"Then that explains it. Blood Nightmare Moluo is a Yuan Demon Sect Sage and possesses the most orthodox soul-summoning art. He then passed it to... No, wait. Is Blood Nightmare Moluo not a Sage of the Blood Sea lineage?"

The Yuan Demon Sect had three lineages that did not belong to one another. Their cultivation methods were also clearly distinct.

Strong figures of the Blood Sea lineage normally did not know the powerful moves of the Soul-Refining lineage.

And soul summoning, soul refinement, and such methods were clearly all from the Soul-Refining lineage.

Luo Chen's expression was heavy. He did not answer their question.

At this moment, he had only one thought in his heart.

Blood Nightmare Moluo had also come to Demon-Fall Land!

There was enmity between them.

First, Luo Chen had slighted the other party, refusing recruitment. Then he and Han Zhan had joined forces and used soul arts to attack He Yuan, the other party's avatar.

Blood Nightmare Moluo was not a broad-minded man. Later, he had directly ordered Mountain Lord Hundred Craft to spend decades hunting Luo Chen.

To a Sage, perhaps that was only a casual sentence.

But to Luo Chen, if his strength had not been sufficient, he would likely have fallen long ago in Mountain Lord Hundred Craft's ambush on Seven Stars Island.

Luo Chen usually never spoke of this grudge, but in his heart he remembered it with absolute clarity.

Now Wu Qi and the other two were casting arts here. Was Blood Nightmare Moluo also here?

Luo Chen looked across the four directions. He saw no human traces, only sand filling the sky and layers of black clouds.

"Senior, search for me."

"Mm."

Since Luo Chen asked, Han Zhan answered.

Even though this place greatly suppressed divine sense, Han Zhan specialized in soul-refining methods, and the distance his divine sense could reach still far exceeded Golden Core cultivators.

After a short search, Han Zhan's reply arrived.

"I see no other Sage experts, but..."

Luo Chen had just relaxed when his heart rose again.

"But what?"

"This place is about to change. Be careful."

"Hm?"

Before Luo Chen could speak, a deafening dragon roar came from the distance.

"Look!" Moya Cave Master cried in alarm.

Luo Chen looked over. In the sky, within the clouds, the dragon soul phantom had begun struggling madly.

On the earth, that white banner clearly showed signs of being unable to endure.

At that moment, Eagle Demon and the Hundred-Transformations Old Monster, who controlled the banner, calmly took out earth-yellow rhombus crystals from their storage bags.

They shattered the crystals and threw them into the white banner.

As large numbers of crystals were fed in, the trembling of the white banner gradually calmed.

Looking at those crystals, Moya Cave Master gave Luo Chen a sidelong glance that seemed both present and absent.

Tai Sui was more direct.

"Devil Lord, you were collecting these crystals everywhere earlier. Could it be that you also noticed something wrong with them?"

"Yes, you even specifically asked me whether the stone monsters that produced these crystals were spirits personally enlightened by Devil Lord Heaven-Refining."

"It seems now that you noticed the problem one step ahead of us!"

Luo Chen narrowed his eyes.

He had indeed noticed earlier that the stone monsters were different from those strange beasts.

Those strange beasts were incomplete, their soul bodies lacking.

The stone monsters were likewise incomplete.

But the most essential difference between the two was that the strange beasts were divided into different categories and different races.

The stone monsters, however, shared the same aura, as if they all came from one person.

Moreover, as Black King collected more and more crystals, Luo Chen could increasingly sense that those crystals showed signs of wanting to merge into one.

Now, from Wu Qi and the others throwing in large numbers of crystals, his guess was undoubtedly verified.

They had even gone further and directly found the main body of that aura.

The dragon soul in the black clouds!

At present, Luo Chen had no thought to be pleased that his guess had been proven right. Nor did he intend to answer Tai Sui's question. All his attention was fixed on the surroundings.

What exactly was the change Han Zhan had spoken of?

Sha, sha, sha...

Flying sand and rolling stones seemed as calm as before.

Yet beneath that calm, a trace of difference appeared.

The boundary between the gobi and the sand sea had already been completely buried by flowing sand.

Streams of quicksand, like waves, were spreading toward the gobi.

Their speed looked slow, yet was actually extremely fast.

Without deliberately looking for a reference point, it was almost impossible to notice their advance.

When the living beings here noticed, they discovered in shock that the gobi, originally ten thousand li long, had already been surrounded by the endless Flowing Sand Sea!

"Something is wrong!"

Wu Qi's soul-summoning chant suddenly stopped.

At that same moment, within the black clouds, another heaven-shaking dragon roar rang out.

"Roar!"

As the dragon roar erupted, the gobi began to tremble.

One beam of earth-yellow light after another tore from the dragon soul and poured into the earth.

Amid the trembling, the broken chunks of stone monsters that had been scattered across the ground began clicking and clacking together, reconstructing themselves into towering stone monsters.

They ignored Wu Qi and the other two and charged toward the flowing sand surrounding them on every side.

As they ran, towering yellow-sand giants emerged from within the Flowing Sand Sea.

In an instant, stone monsters and sand giants began fighting.

Between the two sides, there were no hoarse battle cries.

They simply collided, crude and direct.

In silence, large numbers of stone monsters began to crumble.

A smaller number of yellow-sand giants also collapsed, revealing their cores: tiny mud figures.

Without the support of the yellow-sand giants, these mud figures became unbearably fragile. If a stone monster grabbed one, it swallowed it in a single bite.

Those with better luck threw themselves back into the yellow sand and gathered endless sand to form new giants.

Within one stretch of flowing sand, a golden sphere rolled with the wind.

Inside the golden sphere, three figures wore terrified expressions.

If Tai Sui had not activated the yellow-skinned gourd in advance and released golden sand to wrap around the three of them, they might already have been buried beneath the sand filling the sky.

Yet under these rolling sand waves, even Tai Sui showed signs of being unable to continue.

"That is Heavenloam!"

Through the golden sand, Tai Sui saw one little mud figure appear and perish. He could not help crying out.

Immediately afterward, his expression changed greatly.

"No. Even Heavenloam has been alarmed. The Flowing Sand Sea has become dangerous. We must leave."

Moya Cave Master had still been sunk in the shock brought by the word "Heavenloam." Hearing Tai Sui's words, he quickly agreed.

"Then run!"

With his companion's agreement, Tai Sui hesitated no further. He controlled the golden sphere and flew outward.

This movement was very conspicuous. When they crossed above the gobi, they drew the attention of the three Golden Core cultivators below.

"There were actually others spying?" Eagle Demon said.

"The Flowing Sand Sea is so barren. I thought no one would be here. I did not expect others." The Hundred-Transformations Old Monster was also surprised.

Wu Qi glanced at the golden sphere made of golden sand, his expression heavy.

"Do not bother with others. We must hurry and subdue the stone dragon remnant soul, or there will be no way to answer to the Stream Lord!"

Eagle Demon withdrew his gaze and looked again toward the black clouds in the sky.

"But now the stone dragon remnant soul is instinctively resisting entry into the Soul-Summoning Banner, and a powerful enemy has attacked from outside..."

"This is good news!" Wu Qi's tone was firm. "The thing controlling the yellow sand is the stone dragon remnant soul's natural enemy. Now that the stone dragon remnant soul has divided its strength to resist that enemy, its main body has been greatly weakened. This is exactly our chance to subdue it."

"Put in more effort!"

Seeing Eagle Demon and the Hundred-Transformations Old Monster hesitate, Wu Qi's eyes turned cold.

"You were able to enter Demon-Fall Land and collect large amounts of rare resources all because of Lord Blood Nightmare's help. If you cannot complete this matter for him, even if Lord Blood Nightmare does not pursue responsibility, after we go out, without his protection, do you dare face your respective Stream Lords?"

Eagle Demon and the Hundred-Transformations Old Monster looked at each other.

The First Stream Lord and Split Heaven Stream Lord they had previously joined were no good-hearted people.

If Blood Nightmare Stream Lord did not protect them afterward, their fate could be imagined.

"Listen to me and complete the mission with all your strength! When the time comes and Lord Blood Nightmare bestows the Yuan Demon Sect's Sage-formation method, all of us will have hope for the Sage path. If we are lucky enough to succeed, we will be the thirteenth, fourteenth, and fifteenth Stream Lords after Moluo Stream's twelve Stream Lords. Why fear First and Split Heaven?"

Wu Qi shouted sternly.

Not only did he urge them with words, he also set an example.

He bit the tip of his tongue and spat a mouthful of essence blood onto the bell in his hand.

In an instant, the bell rang loudly. The dragon soul high in the sky seemed to suffer a heavy blow.

Seeing this, Eagle Demon and the Hundred-Transformations Old Monster no longer hesitated. Each forced out essence blood and increased the Soul-Summoning Banner's power.

A terrifying suction came from the Soul-Summoning Banner, and the dragon soul in the sky immediately swayed as if about to fall.

Without the dragon soul's support, the numbers of the stone monsters charging forward also began gradually decreasing.

Replacing them was rolling yellow sand, spreading over heaven and earth!

A rare ruthlessness appeared in Wu Qi's eyes.

He wanted to seize chestnuts from the fire and subdue the stone dragon remnant soul before the sand sea closed in completely.

...

Whoosh!

A streak of golden light broke through clouds and mist.

Its true body was a golden sphere, and the golden sphere was formed by golden sand continuously spewed from a gourd inside.

Within the golden sphere, all three breathed in relief.

"As expected, the thing controlling the yellow sand was not targeting us," Moya Cave Master said, letting out a breath.

Tai Sui looked puzzled.

"Strange. Heavenloam has slumbered for a thousand years. Even when Yuan Demon Sect Sages came here to explore, it avoided meeting them. Why would it take the initiative to appear this time, and with such great fanfare?"

A question came from beside his ear.

"That is Heavenloam?"

Tai Sui looked at Luo Chen and nodded slightly.

"Correct. That is indeed Heavenloam. I said before that the Flowing Sand Sea has a master, and its name is Heavenloam."

Luo Chen revealed a thoughtful expression.

Moya Cave Master was curious.

"What rank is Heavenloam? Compared with your main body, which of you is stronger?"

Hearing this question, displeasure appeared on Tai Sui's face.

"We are different from cultivators and demon beasts. We are awakened spirits. Even if our realm is equal to cultivators, our methods are lacking. Heavenloam and I are both fifth-tier existences. As for who is stronger or weaker, that cannot be discussed simply. Terrain matters more."

"In the Flowing Sand Sea, it is stronger. In my original territory, naturally I am superior."

"However, Heavenloam is a man-made thing, a hybrid, and it has innate defects. If my main body can escape, I have several methods to kill it!"

When he said this, he was full of confidence, as though he truly disliked Moya Cave Master comparing Heavenloam to him.

Especially since his main body was a wood-attribute spirit plant that innately restrained earth-type spirits.

"Forget it. Let us ignore it for now. After we obtain the Five Elements Lotus Platform for Azure Yang, we will go to Falling Demon Abyss [TERM?] and undo the restrictions on my main body."

After saying this, he increased his mana and prepared to take the two away from the Flowing Sand Sea.

But just then, Tai Sui looked at Luo Chen in confusion.

"Devil Lord Azure Yang, what are you doing?"

A large hand was pressed against the inner wall of the golden sphere.

Azure flame spat out and silently burned a hole through it.

Luo Chen gave a smile that did not reach his eyes.

"You go first. I will follow shortly."

With that, his body moved and flew straight out through the opening.

Behind him, Tai Sui and Moya Cave Master both froze.

The golden sand flowed back and drilled into the mouth of the gourd.

The two appeared beneath the clear sky and watched Luo Chen's back.

He was like an arrow leaving the string, incomparably resolute, charging straight into the rolling Flowing Sand Sea in a single breath.

*(End of Chapter)*
