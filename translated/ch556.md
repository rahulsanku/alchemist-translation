# Chapter 556: Mutual Destruction, One Palm Sinks the Sea

On Xuanyan Island, a world-shaking battle was unfolding.

Perhaps that was an exaggeration.

But for the two clans — crab and turtle — it was the most consequential battle imaginable.

One side fought for the chance to rise — to seize a land with a fourth-tier spirit vein. The other fought for its right to exist — to protect its home, yielding not a single inch of earth.

This battle had been inevitable from the moment the five royal demon crab clans began dismantling the first island of the Nine Palaces Eight Trigrams Formation. It had merely taken until now to arrive — and now it called on every last reserve the Ring-Headed Turtle clan possessed.

An ancient Ring-Headed Turtle emerged from the base of the great mountain at Xuanyan Island's center.

Its clouded eyes swept the four directions. A vast, commanding divine sense spread across the homeland where it had lived for over a thousand years, and a tide of grief rose in its heart.

His clan had never loved war.

Even when they had once produced a transformation-stage Demon Emperor, they had been content to live in quiet peace within the Xuanyan Sea Region.

Even the various demon beasts native to the region — so long as they didn't disturb Ring-Headed Turtle life, they were rarely forced into submission, at most made to serve as vassals.

But all that benevolence had, in the end, produced this result.

A field of corpses. Blood enough to float oars.

"There's still time, I suppose," he murmured.

He watched the surviving Ring-Headed Turtle clanspeople, then let his gaze drift toward the direction the human cultivator had fled.

That one had bought them a little more time.

But there was no more buying to be had.

He glanced once behind him at the great mountain, then turned his eyes toward the five streaks of escape-light flying in from beyond the island, and the demon clouds surging at him from every direction.

"Come then."

"Let me fight this last battle for my clan — and leave behind one final thread of hope for the Ring-Headed Turtle people of Xuanyan."

Even as he spoke, his body began to expand.

The smooth bony plates of his underbelly shone like mirrors. The rough shell of his back was cragged as cliff-face.

Ring after ring of markings covered every inch of him — like the growth rings of an ancient tree, each one a record of how much frost and change this life had weathered.

In a few moments, a great sea turtle spanning eighty or ninety zhang crouched across Xuanyan Island.

At first glance it looked like a small continent.

In that same instant, five devastating attacks struck from five directions.

Golden light. Seaweed-green ripples. Spreading venom. Spider silk tearing the sky. A torrent of fire crashing down from above.

*Boom! Boom! Boom!*

Ear-splitting explosions rang out across Xuanyan Island and beyond.

A vast cloud of smoke and dust rolled upward.

When the dust cleared, old turtle Hailo stood unmoved in the center of the battlefield, not so much as a tremor.

Suddenly —

That figure — vast as a small continent — began to slowly rise.

Four legs thick with heavy hide pressed against the earth, lifting its enormous frame upright.

A deep amber radiance draped itself across the whole body, framing an aura of mountain-stillness and bedrock resolve.

The moment that amber light was seen, every heart present fell heavy.

Especially the five patriarchs, whose expressions changed in an instant.

"The Demon Emperor's aura!"

"That's the aura of the Xuanyan Demon Emperor — can this mean the Xuanyan Armor has been on Hailo all along?"

"That's the only explanation. Otherwise, what kind of being would stand there and eat a combined strike from five of us without flinching?"

"We have a problem now. I never thought Hailo would dare desecrate the ancestor's relic by refining it into himself."

"What problem? It's a turtle shell. Break it."

The last voice was Jin Hun — normally the composed one.

With the prize this close, there was no room for hesitation.

And five against one — the advantage was theirs.

In terms of living strength, the Ring-Headed Turtle clan had all but collapsed. Countless third-tier demon kings had self-detonated, thinning the high-end combat pool drastically. Among the five royal clans, they could still field several dozen third-tier demon crab kings combined.

Today, if nothing else, they would grind this old turtle down.

For that future, the Overlord Crab clan was prepared to pay any price.

Before moving, Jin Hun looked across at the other four demons.

"At this point, I trust you all have the resolve to throw everything in."

Red Flame showed a savage grin and stepped forward.

Nine Claw opened its maw and slowly expelled a poison hook — a chill aura bleeding off it that could make anyone's skin crawl.

Lüxiu hesitated, wavering.

Zhu Xiang shook his head, looking at Jin Hun. "This battle will cost us. The Azure Emperor clan in particular has few great demon kings left — if we lose too many more, the clan's future strength..."

He didn't finish.

But everyone understood.

Jin Hun swept a hand through the air. "Whatever the cost — seize this land, and the five clans have a chance for an emperor to be born among us. Whoever succeeds will unite all five clans, dominate the Xuanyan Sea Region. When that day comes, we all rise together or fall together. What you're worrying about is far too small."

The ambition, laid bare without disguise.

Even though everyone had known it was there, the open declaration still set their blood moving.

A Crab Emperor. Five clans united.

Dominance over Xuanyan. A demon power to be reckoned with across the Northern Sea.

Only then would their clans have the chance to truly rise — free from the larger demons' oppression, free from fearing human cultivators hunting them at will.

For that future — yes, everything was worth throwing in.

Lüxiu, who had been wavering, suddenly steadied.

"Then — we fight."

"Move."

Without a moment's hesitation, all five patriarchs deployed their true forms, taking positions at five cardinal points, and began their assault on Hailo, the Ring-Headed Turtle clan's patriarch.

And Hailo, protected by the Demon Emperor's relic, showed not a trace of fear.

He met every attack without guile — taking wounds to deal wounds, spending a life to take a life.

One turtle. Five crabs. Six colossal beings, using the most direct fleshy combat and the most violent demon arts, crashing into each other without a single trace of finesse.

The scale of the fighting forced lower-tier demon beasts off Xuanyan Island entirely; only third-tier demon kings could remain on the fringes of the battlefield and watch.

The most savage battle in this whole war began to unfold on Xuanyan Island.

...

*Crack.*

Perhaps it was the violence of their struggle that disturbed the spiritual qi of heaven and earth.

Or perhaps the heavens themselves grieved the extinction of a clan.

As the great battle finally wound to a close, storm clouds gathered over the Xuanyan Sea Region, the atmosphere pressing down with suffocating weight.

Within those dark clouds, lightning serpents writhed. Thunder shook the ears.

Wind and rain bore down. Waves surged.

Xuanyan Island — pitted and cratered from the battle — was beginning to flood, vast quantities of seawater pouring in from every direction.

Mountains that had stood through the ages now lay toppled in every direction.

The five demon crab patriarchs stood scattered across the island, each gasping for breath.

Every one of them bore wounds.

The worst was Lüxiu — her body had been cleaved nearly in half.

Even Jin Hun and Red Flame, as formidable as they were, had claws and legs severed.

All five felt the quiet chill of what-might-have-been.

Hailo, protected by the Xuanyan Demon Emperor's relic, was more dangerous than any of them had feared.

Fortunately, his strength lay in defense, not offense.

If it had been otherwise, one or two among the five would not have survived this day.

Now their gazes moved — still filled with shock — to the center of the battlefield.

Old turtle Hailo lay motionless where he had fallen.

Looking closer: three of four legs severed. Shell torn open. The abdominal plates shattered, exposing vast swaths of flesh soaked in red.

That wretched sight made the few Ring-Headed Turtle clanspeople who still lived turn red-eyed, weeping without sound.

Patriarch Hailo had, after the third tier, walked the Black Tortoise path of ancestral return.

The way he lay now was no different from death.

"No different" — meaning he hadn't entirely fallen. Not yet.

Jin Hun spoke slowly: "Patriarch Hailo. Hand over the Xuanyan Armor willingly, and I will leave your body intact."

A low, labored breath came from Hailo.

With effort, he opened his eyes and scanned his surroundings.

He didn't look at Jin Hun first — he looked at the dozens of third-tier demon crab kings surrounding them on all sides, then let his gaze settle on the surviving clanspeople sheltering beneath the distant mountain.

His voice came quietly: "Hand it over? To whom? To you?"

Jin Hun's brow tightened. Almost instantly, he felt the other four's unfriendly stares.

*Old turtle. Death's door. And still sowing discord.*

He didn't panic.

"Hand it over. We'll decide who gets it ourselves."

Hailo pulled at the cracked corner of his mouth. "And the terms are just — leaving my body intact?"

Jin Hun said coldly: "Given that you're a senior to all of us here, this is already generous. If you still feel it isn't enough — I can promise that the surviving Ring-Headed Turtles will be allowed to leave the Xuanyan Sea Region and find another place to live."

At that, Lüxiu cried out sharply: "Absolutely not. Release a tiger, and it will return to haunt you."

Zhu Xiang of the Demon Spider Crabs said with a low voice: "Short-sighted. They're a pack of strays now — what is there to be afraid of?"

Nine Claw said darkly: "We've been neighbors for years. There's no need to wipe them out entirely."

Red Flame laughed out loud. "My clan has wandered deep-sea trenches and volcanic fields for years — I know of a fine place where the Ring-Headed Turtles could make a home."

Lüxiu snapped: "Easy for you to offer. My cultivation is ruined. If they ever came back with enough strength to strike, the Azure Emperor clan would be the first to suffer — and I will not agree to releasing them!"

*This female crab has lost her head.*

Get the Xuanyan Armor first. Everything after that could be settled however they pleased.

Red Flame's suggestion was also sound — a familiar territory where they could monitor the Ring-Headed Turtles. Even keeping them as servants or captives wasn't out of the question.

Jin Hun was about to speak — then his eyes fell on Lüxiu.

And in that instant he understood.

Lüxiu wasn't really arguing about the Ring-Headed Turtles. She was negotiating with *them*.

Even as that internal friction began to build —

Old turtle Hailo gave a quiet sigh.

"Enough."

"Three of my four legs are severed. What does 'intact body' even mean?"

"Besides — better to rely on yourself than beg of others. My clan's future had better stay in our own hands."

The five crabs' expressions shifted together.

*More resistance?*

Red Flame blazed with fury. "Old creature! On death's door and still can't read the situation?"

Nine Claw was puzzled. "The Xuanyan Armor has already been revealed. What more could you possibly have?"

The others couldn't fathom what foundation Hailo thought he had left.

As they puzzled over it, Hailo said softly: "The only third-tier Perfected Completion in my clan isn't just this old, worn-out body of mine."

*Qihuan.*

The name surfaced in every mind at once.

In that same instant, Zhu Xiang suddenly looked up.

Lightning serpents writhed through the clouds. Thunder roared.

Above Xuanyan Island, the spiritual qi was churning in turmoil.

Before, they had assumed it was the aftereffects of their battle.

Now, looking closely — it was entirely different.

This was not refined demon qi. This was pure natural spiritual qi of heaven and earth.

Drawn from ley lines underground, drawn from the heavens above.

That sensation — it felt like...

"Transforming false into true — ancestral return, transformation achieved!"

"No — Qihuan is trying to force the transformation tribulation here and now!"

Exposed though the plan was, Hailo felt no particular alarm.

Everything that could be prepared had been prepared. Every effort had been made. Even this old body of his was being laid down here.

It was time to witness whether this last thread of hope arrived.

The next moment —

At the highest peak of Xuanyan Island, a colossal serpentine shape burst from the peak and spiraled upward, driving toward the heavens.

Looking close — that was no serpent.

It was the neck of a great demon.

Among the Ring-Headed Turtles: patriarch Hailo had walked the Black Tortoise path of ancestral return. Great Elder Qihuan had walked the Cliff Serpent path.

The former was aged and declining — realm at Perfected Completion, but unable to take that final step.

The latter was from the same generation as the five patriarchs. And after years of cultivation, drawing on the resources left by the Xuanyan Demon Emperor, it was the colossal pressure of their clan's extinction that had finally driven him to take that last step.

The heavenly tribulation struck.

On the very first wave, limitless thunder fell directly onto Qihuan's neck.

To meet it, Qihuan reached up and summoned a massive turtle shell to hold over its head.

This was the Ring-Headed Turtle body Qihuan had slowly shed as it walked the Cliff Serpent path of ancestral return.

Under normal circumstances, a shed husk like this could be tempered by tribulation lightning into a life-bound magic treasure — or even a true artifact.

But —

One strike.

*Crack.*

The indestructible Ring-Headed Turtle husk shattered into a sky full of ash.

The Cliff Serpent Qihuan was left with flesh torn open, blood pouring everywhere, its aura diminished and collapsing.

Seeing this, the five patriarchs — who had been holding their breath — finally let it out.

"Insufficient foundation. Forcing the advancement will fail."

"Qihuan poses no threat."

High above.

Within that curtain of thunder clouds, waiting for the second wave, Qihuan let out a bitter laugh.

"Patriarch, it still isn't enough."

On the ruined battlefield below, Hailo's eyes dimmed.

Their ancestor had achieved transformation as a Black Tortoise. The resources left behind were aligned to the Black Tortoise path.

What could be given to Qihuan in that sense was almost nothing.

If Qihuan had walked the Black Tortoise path — at minimum, he could have borrowed the ancestor's relic to hold out a few more rounds of tribulation.

But as it was, even the first wave had barely been endured. With Qihuan's foundation, the rounds to follow would be unsurvivable.

"Then let us sacrifice ourselves together, and at least win our people one last thread of hope."

He murmured.

Even as he spoke, an amber radiance rose from within his body — condensing at last into one great turtle shell, sent flying toward the Ring-Headed Turtle survivors sheltering beneath the mountain.

Seeing this, Jin Hun cried out involuntarily:

"The Demon Emperor's relic — the Xuanyan Armor!"

As he spoke he was already moving, reaching out to seize it.

Red Flame, Nine Claw, and Zhu Xiang moved at the same instant.

Hailo's lip curled in a sneer.

"You had better worry about yourselves first."

The four demons didn't understand.

And in that same instant, the second wave of the heavenly tribulation was fully charged.

Qihuan let out a sharp cry — and rather than meeting the tribulation head-on, it drew a deep, vast breath downward.

Dozens of invisible auras were inhaled into its body.

In that moment, some undefined link connected all the third-tier powerhouses of the five great royal demon crab clans to Qihuan.

This was the Cliff Serpent clan's racial demon art — *Horizon Neighbor*.

Legend held that this technique could merge many powers into one, amplifying the user.

The Cliff Serpent had never been a particularly fearsome ancient demon. Yet it stood alongside the Black Tortoise, Celestial Wolf, and Thunder Roc as one of the great ancient demon bloodlines — because their royal line could absorb the strength of their kin and fuse it into a single body, pitting an entire clan's power against the greatest of archaic demons.

But this technique was absolutely forbidden during a tribulation.

Heavenly tribulation was the Great Dao's test of those who broke through to a new level. It tolerated no outside interference.

Fusing in such chaotic, mixed power would not only cause the tribulation's power to surge — it would expand the tribulation's target from one individual to every life connected to those fused forces.

Even at Qihuan's level, it could not achieve that degree of control.

But it could, and did, connect its aura to every single third-tier demon crab king on the entire Xuanyan Island battlefield.

Thus, the tribulation lightning would fall on all of them alike.

Qihuan would die. But none of the others would emerge unscathed.

The demon beasts on the battlefield had no idea what this technique meant.

But when bolts as thick as barrels passed through Qihuan's body and came falling down on every demon crab king across Xuanyan Island — every one of them panicked.

"How is this possible?"

"Heaven's mercy — I only just advanced to the third tier. I cannot resist heavenly tribulation!"

"Patriarch, save me!"

Screams erupted.

The desperate fled on escape-light, trying to get off Xuanyan Island.

The powerful, knowing there was no avoiding it, expended everything they had to resist.

All five patriarchs — though their hearts were screaming curses — had no attention to spare for anyone else, throwing up every defense they possessed to withstand the tribulation.

In an instant.

On Xuanyan Island, heavenly thunder descended. Silver light flooded the earth.

The rumbling shook the world to its foundations.

At that point, the tribulation's strikes were no longer selective — no longer targeting a single individual.

Anyone in range was caught.

The entire island was blanketed in destruction.

...

In the swamp.

Han Zhan, who had been about to depart, watched the entire battle unfold with his own eyes — and at the very moment the tribulation began to gather, abandoned all thought of leaving.

"A heavenly tribulation."

He had survived his own Sage tribulation. He had once stood guard as another's escort through theirs — his old friend Great Master Canglang.

His understanding of heavenly tribulations ran far deeper than most.

And the moment this one began to gather, he knew something was wrong.

But he was also puzzled.

"Is a demon beast's transformation tribulation this weak?"

"Only one day spent gathering."

"Or is it that the one attempting it simply doesn't have enough foundation?"

Out of curiosity, he had stayed.

And he witnessed what came next.

When he saw dozens of demon king auras connect and be drawn into the tribulation together, Han Zhan couldn't help but exhale in admiration.

"What a technique."

"Throughout this whole battle, the one attempting the tribulation was quietly gathering the auras of every great demon in range. And then used those connections — channeling heaven's lightning as the final weapon for mutual destruction."

"The technique is exquisite. And the will behind it even more so."

A different thought surfaced alongside the admiration.

He glanced at the trembling Black King lying flat on the ground.

"Black King. I have a task for you. Complete it properly, and your master will not be stingy with his rewards."

Black King raised its head. "What task?"

Han Zhan explained, one point at a time.

After hearing the content, Black King immediately shook its head, its eyes going wide with horror.

"I'll die."

"Don't you want supreme-grade Imperial Moonlight Nectar?" Han Zhan said with a mild smile. "Your Devouring demon art was unlocked from your muddled bloodline precisely because of Imperial Moonlight Nectar. Complete this task, and you'll have as much of it as you could want."

Black King was tempted — but thinking of the danger ahead, it wavered.

"Don't worry. With me and your master both here, you won't die. And there's an extra reward waiting when it's done."

"Master is here too?" Black King blinked.

Han Zhan smiled faintly. "You didn't really think he could miss an event this loud, did you?"

The moment it heard Luo Chen was nearby, Black King gritted its teeth.

"Alright. I'll do it."

...

When the one attempting the tribulation perished, the heavenly tribulation dissipated of its own accord.

The black clouds blanketing the world gradually thinned and dispersed. All that remained was a downpour of rain.

Beneath that enormous tribulation, Xuanyan Island — already torn apart by the great battle — sank several hundred zhang deeper. Vast quantities of seawater poured in, flooding the land.

The demon kings who survived on the island were all in wretched states, shaken and frightened.

But the tribulation had finally ended.

And that meant this war had reached its conclusion.

Qihuan had used his body to call down the lightning. The tribulation had failed.

Hailo had fought all five patriarchs alone, and beneath the tribulation's coverage, had also passed from this world.

Among the five patriarchs, whose auras had been most deeply linked to Qihuan's technique: Lüxiu had perished. Nine Claw had fallen.

The three survivors bore wounds without exception — their auras at their lowest ebb, their cultivation slashed by more than half. Even centuries of bitter effort might not be enough to return them to their former peak.

The surviving demon kings numbered barely a dozen.

The Ring-Headed Turtle clan's attempt at mutual destruction had, in a strict sense, failed.

Or rather — not entirely failed.

Zhu Xiang, eyes barely open, looked at the seawater flooding in from every direction, and at the scattered, shattered remnants of the five clans' forces.

The fourth-tier spirit vein's formation structure had been destroyed. By their demon race methods, it would take a thousand years to repair — if it could be repaired at all.

The five clans' strength was gutted. They might no longer have the power to repel the great demons of other sea regions coming to contest this land.

This was mutual destruction. A shared fall.

"Hailo. Qihuan. You two... how ruthless."

Even as Zhu Xiang was taking stock of the situation around him, Jin Hun let out a sharp shout from nearby.

"Golden Claw — take the Xuanyan Armor!"

From a nearby crater, a streak of golden light burst through the water, shooting straight toward the collapsed remains of the great mountain.

At the same moment, the Overlord Crab clan's other surviving great demon king — Golden Armor — together with five third-tier demon kings, all pushed through their wounds and followed close behind Golden Claw.

Seven demons moving at once, targeting the Xuanyan Demon Emperor's armor hovering above the surviving Ring-Headed Turtle clanspeople.

But before they could close in —

A terrifying devouring force erupted from underground.

Moving faster than all of them, it swallowed the amber radiance whole.

Golden Claw felt a familiar aura the very instant it registered.

"You dare, demon snake!"

Black King had no hesitations about daring. Having swallowed the Xuanyan Armor, it dove straight into the deep sea.

The seven Overlord Crab fighters were about to cut it off — then felt a chill run through their very souls. A heaven-shaking aura bore down on them from above.

All seven looked up.

There, standing in the sky, a figure like a great roc — one hand pressing down.

Overwhelming mana had gathered within it. Endless azure flame coiled across it. A colossal handprint hundreds of zhang in scale had been building its power — and now it came crashing down.

*BOOM.*

Xuanyan Island — already teetering on the edge of collapse — could not bear it.

The final straw had fallen.

The island began to sink, rolling and rumbling into the sea.

The seven demons caught in the center of the Azure Yang Great Handprint's range let out agonized screams.

A voice drifted down from above.

"Many thanks for the treasure, friends."

The voice stretched into the distance — and by the time its echo faded, the speaker was already somewhere on the far horizon.

*(End of Chapter)*
