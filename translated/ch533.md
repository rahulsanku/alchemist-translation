# Chapter 533: Blood Food

---

Time flowed like water, years rushing like a shuttle.

Half a year later.

Invitation Moon Island, surrounded by more than thirty islands large and small, welcomed a streak of escaping light.

After circling briefly in the sky, the light followed the guidance of a token and descended upon Invitation Moon Island.

When she landed, the tall, graceful figure could not help glancing at the stone stele standing on the island. A trace of desire appeared in her eyes.

But she knew deeply that this was not something she could covet, so she forcibly restrained that desire and stepped into the layers of white mist.

As she walked, the vast surrounding mist seemed to possess spirituality and parted of its own accord, opening a path one meter wide for her.

A short while later, Cheng Haixin arrived before a magnificent palace. Carved beams, painted rafters, flying eaves, and interlocking brackets made it quite exquisite.

But upon closer inspection, the palace still had many rough areas.

There was no help for it. Luo Chen had given them too little time before. Only one month. Even if the Swallow Islands cultivators wished to please him, they could not produce miracles.

Most of the materials for this palace had been taken directly from ready-made materials on other islands.

"Fortunately, Demon Lord Qingyang is magnanimous and does not care about these things."

She muttered inwardly, lifted the hem of her skirt, and stepped into the palace.

As though he had known of her arrival, she did not wait long before a figure appeared in Cheng Haixin's eyes.

Cheng Haixin respectfully presented two storage bags.

"Senior, these are the first batch of True Flame Pill medicinal materials collected for you by the various clans. According to your standards, there are seventy-eight sets in total."

Luo Chen glanced at the other storage bag in her hand, then accepted the one containing the medicinal materials.

His divine sense entered it. After sweeping through it, displeasure appeared on his face.

"Half a year, and you only purchased seventy-eight sets. You are not putting enough heart into this."

The woman trembled inwardly and hurried to explain. "Senior, please forgive us. It is truly because more than half of the materials you require are fire-attribute medicinal materials. The nearby islands' output cannot satisfy the demand at all. It was only because my elder brother unified the clans and formed a merchant caravan to travel to Feileng City, then contacted many parties, that we managed to gather them."

Luo Chen raised a brow. "Is it so troublesome? Is our Northern Sea not famed for its abundant resources?"

Cheng Haixin shook her head. "That statement is indeed true. The problem is that the Swallow Islands are too remote. To travel to the inner region and back would take several decades. Even Feileng City is already the nearest great immortal island city to the Swallow Islands."

The terrain was too remote?

Luo Chen's heart stirred slightly.

If that was the case, it was understandable.

It was like when he had been in Dahe Market back then. Although he had faced the million mountains with their countless resources, when it came to high-tier resources, they were still scarce there, far less convenient than those great domains deeper inland.

Seeing Luo Chen's expression ease slightly, Cheng Haixin let out a small breath.

Striking while the iron was hot, she gave him a piece of good news.

"The first several batches of medicinal materials may be slower and smaller, but once my Swallow Merchant Caravan gains a firm footing in prosperous areas like Feileng City and Mohua Island, collecting the True Flame Pill materials Senior requires will become much more convenient."

Once channels formed, supply would naturally stabilize.

Luo Chen had done business before, so he naturally understood this principle.

Since that was the case, he no longer blamed them.

He swept through the medicinal materials in the storage bag and casually asked, "How many spirit stones for one Dazzling Light Seed?"

"Fifteen spirit stones each, but they are not sold individually. Usually they are sold by the hundred, unless the quantity is large enough to leave odd numbers."

"What about Yama Flower?"

"That is a third-tier medicinal material and very expensive. One flower costs more than six hundred spirit stones. My elder brother spent a great deal of effort and promised many benefits before cutting the price down to exactly six hundred."

"What is the price of Purple Smoke Wax Torch?"

"One hundred and twenty low-grade spirit stones per box. It is a little expensive, but they have already been processed in advance."

One medicinal material after another was asked by Luo Chen, and Cheng Haixin answered them one by one.

Luo Chen was using the prices of some common medicinal materials to understand the Northern Sea cultivation world from the side.

Prices often represented the degree of development of a region.

Of course, that was not absolute.

Prices could also be affected by many factors: war, scarcity, the degree of regulation by powerful forces, and so on.

Even so, Luo Chen could still glimpse the whole through one corner.

From what he saw now, the raw material cost for one set of True Flame Pill ingredients was around three thousand spirit stones.

This price could not be called low.

As another third-tier pill, the Starfield Pill's cost had only barely reached two thousand.

And in the Jade Cauldron Domain, Luo Chen had estimated in the past that collecting one set of True Flame Pill raw materials would cost roughly two thousand three or four hundred.

Now the premium was fully six or seven hundred.

This showed that the Northern Sea cultivation world was either wealthier, or the situation was more tense, causing the prices of all kinds of resources to rise. Or perhaps both were true.

While Luo Chen used the small to infer the large and analyzed these things, Cheng Haixin also seized every opening to express the Cheng clan's contributions.

For example, Cheng Dou had personally unified the great clans of the Swallow Islands and established the Swallow Merchant Caravan solely to collect medicinal materials for Luo Chen.

He had bargained, asked favors, and so forth.

Oh, she also mentioned that of this first batch of seventy-eight material sets, the Cheng clan of Black Swan Island alone had contributed ten.

Luo Chen naturally understood their thoughts.

That Cheng Dou had put aside seclusion and cultivation to make such a large series of moves. Without doubt, he was trying to curry favor with Luo Chen.

His purpose was to exchange for the Core Formation secret art on the stone stele on Invitation Moon Island.

This person had complex thoughts. He had previously been eager to break through his realm, yet before the middle-grade Core Formation secret art, he forcefully steadied his heart.

It could be seen he also had brains.

Incidentally, he had also borrowed Luo Chen's name to borrow people and strength from over thirty clans across the archipelago, forming this so-called Swallow Merchant Caravan.

With such a caravan, who knew how much profit he would seize in the future?

It could even be said that even if Luo Chen was no longer here someday, as long as Cheng Dou managed the caravan well, the other clans would likely still acknowledge him.

Luo Chen was not unaware of all this, but he chose to turn a blind eye.

Let him do as he pleased.

In any case, so long as he did not neglect Luo Chen's due respect.

The more prosperous Cheng Dou made things, the greater the benefits Luo Chen would receive in the future.

Speaking of which, the reason the Swallow cultivators, who originally obeyed him in word but not in heart, were now so actively running about and collecting medicinal materials for him was all thanks to Luo Chen's clever move.

The Qingyang Alchemy Canon.

This middle-grade Core Formation secret art was actually a method he had simplified from the Fine Dust Primordial Art.

Within it, he removed the core of Locked Pearl Curtain and the core of the Clear Origin Wondrous Pill Method, but retained the essentials of a series of Core Formation secret arts such as Settling Wind and Waves and Dragon-Phoenix Tribulation.

He also made slight mention of the parts involving spiritual power transformation.

Only then did he obtain this Core Formation secret art rated "middle-grade" by the system.

After understanding the cultivation environment of the Swallow Islands, Luo Chen had gained a very clear recognition.

Their foundations were all solid. There were not many who took extreme, crooked paths.

With the Qingyang Alchemy Canon, their chance of Core Formation could be increased by a full twenty percent.

It was precisely because of this that those cultivators worked so hard to perform.

First came establishing might. Then came tempting them with profit.

A big stick in one hand, a sweet date in the other.

The many cultivators were naturally used by Luo Chen.

"Senior, this is a small token of this concubine's regard. I hope Senior will accept it."

The other storage bag was lifted before Luo Chen.

Luo Chen grew interested and accepted the storage bag.

"What is inside?"

Cheng Haixin said anxiously, "When we first met, I saw Senior take a few extra bites of a local specialty dish. Later, I heard Senior ask about spirit meals containing abundant qi and blood, so I kept it in mind. Inside is a two-hundred-year Dragon-Tiger Grouper. Its grade is not high, but its flesh is tender and its qi and blood vigorous. Ordinarily, it is also the blood food most coveted by mortal martial artists on the various islands. It is said that if they eat such blood food, even one small mouthful gives them a great chance of breaking through into the innate realm and becoming master-level martial artists."

Luo Chen raised a brow and summoned it with mana.

In an instant, an aroma that made one's fingers twitch spread through the hall.

A huge fish fully three meters long and one meter wide, like a giant door panel, appeared on a specially made serving plate.

It was covered with various seasonings and sauces, hot steam rolling from it.

At a glance, it had clearly been cooked not long ago and only then placed into the storage bag and brought here.

Without much hesitation, Luo Chen performed a simple inspection, then took out a small knife, sliced off a piece of fish, and placed it in his mouth.

He did not care about its texture or taste.

Once Myriad Path Convergence circulated, that mouthful of fish immediately melted and decomposed, transforming into strands of qi and blood that fused into his physique.

The amount was not much.

But the fish was large.

This Dragon-Tiger Grouper was indeed good.

If he consumed it long-term, it would certainly benefit his body cultivation realm.

Satisfaction appeared on his face as he looked at Cheng Haixin.

"You made this fish?"

Cheng Haixin was about to answer yes, but then twisted slightly and honestly said, "This concubine is not very proficient in cooking. I invited an old senior from another island to make this. However, I personally and carefully selected this fish from the Cheng clan's catch. If Senior is satisfied..."

"You did very well. From now on, every month, prepare enough spirit meals for me... no, according to your local term, blood food."

Luo Chen pointed at the steaming Dragon-Tiger Grouper. "Blood food like this, prepare at least ten portions for me every month."

Cheng Haixin had originally been full of joy, having received Demon Lord Qingyang's praise.

But when she heard ten portions a month, her exquisite little face immediately filled with worry.

"Senior, Dragon-Tiger Grouper of this quality is very difficult to catch. One has to go to the deep sea. But if the demon-hunting ships go that far, the danger greatly increases."

Luo Chen said without concern, "It does not have to be only Dragon-Tiger Grouper. Other blood foods rich in qi and blood will also do. The key is the culinary skill for preparing such blood food. When you have time, bring that old senior here. I will speak with him. Of course, this merit will be counted under your name."

Cheng Haixin could not help letting out a breath of relief.

Seeing that Luo Chen was in a good mood, she gathered her courage and carefully asked, "Senior, may I ask how much contribution we need to accumulate before we can exchange once for the Qingyang Alchemy Canon?"

Luo Chen stopped eating and looked at the woman.

Under his gaze, the courage she had finally gathered instantly receded, and her swan-like snow-white neck subconsciously lowered.

"This concubine overstepped."

As she felt disappointed and frightened, a voice entered her ears.

"When six thousand sets of True Flame Pill materials have been gathered, your Cheng clan may send one person to receive one initiation of the Qingyang Alchemy Canon beneath the stele."

"After the Cheng clan, the other clans will follow. But at that time, the required contribution will be calculated separately."

Cheng Haixin abruptly raised her head, filled with surprise and joy.

One set of True Flame Pill raw materials cost three thousand spirit stones. Six thousand sets were roughly eighteen million. Spread across over thirty clans, that came to a little over five hundred thousand low-grade spirit stones each.

In other words, Luo Chen had practically set a clear price.

Of course, ordinary small clans definitely could not produce that many spirit stones.

But the Cheng clan could not be counted as any small clan.

A small clan could not afford a large demon-hunting ship.

Moreover, what Demon Lord Qingyang wanted was not spirit stones, but medicinal materials in hand.

Even so, they had finally seen hope.

"Big Brother, Core Formation is within reach!"

The woman, full of joy, took leave of Luo Chen and rushed straight to Black Swan Island.

Looking at the woman's delighted departing figure, Luo Chen curled his lip almost imperceptibly.

Clan cultivators were just like this.

They received the clan's resources, yet they were also sacrificed for the clan at every moment.

This Cheng Haixin had previously been extremely unwilling when her elder brother Cheng Dou sent her to Luo Chen's bed, yet now she still worked herself to the bone trying to help the Cheng clan produce an Adept.

With bonds too deep, one was not free.

Luo Chen shook his head and stopped thinking about these matters. In any case, he had no intention of managing the Swallow Islands like the Luo Tian Sect.

He ate the cooked Dragon-Tiger Grouper in large mouthfuls.

The enormous first-tier demon beast vanished piece by piece into his stomach, yet his belly did not swell in the slightest.

This scene made it seem as though Luo Chen was the demon beast with endless space contained in his abdomen.

After the blood food entered his body, Luo Chen began circulating Myriad Path Convergence.

The azure flame instantly activated, rapidly refining the fish he had eaten and transforming it into streams of qi and blood that fused into his physique.

Sensing the growth of his physique, Luo Chen showed satisfaction.

He did not care about the ingredients or delicacy of the blood food. He only required that such blood food possess dense qi and blood. With Myriad Path Convergence present, he could refine and absorb all of it.

"Today's Dragon-Tiger Grouper counts as an unexpected delight."

Luo Chen smiled, then slowly walked into a side hall.

Wooden shelves had long since been arranged neatly inside.

Luo Chen took many medicinal materials from the storage bag and placed them on the shelves one by one.

"Next, I process these raw materials, then refine True Flame Pills as quickly as possible, accelerating again on top of my already extremely fast cultivation speed."

As he muttered to himself, excitement appeared on the man's face.

The spirit vein on Invitation Moon Island was not especially high-grade, only lower third-tier.

It was merely somewhat better than the man-made third-tier spirit vein of Tianlan.

Even so, Luo Chen's cultivation speed was already unbelievably fast.

The Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture gave him a cultivation speed comparable to a heavenly spirit root.

By his estimate, even with only a lower third-tier spirit vein, cultivating to Adept Stage Four would take at most ten-some years.

If assisted by True Flame Pills, this time could be shortened to under ten years.

At that time, with a middle-stage Adept realm paired with his full set of methods, so long as he did not provoke Sage cultivators, he would already be able to roam the Demon Sea freely.

For now, he still had to keep a low profile and develop for a while.

Luo Chen could endure loneliness. It was only a few years.

He opened his mouth and spat, and the Primordial Unity Cauldron spun out, landing in the alchemy room.

The previously round cauldron body had visibly shrunk by thirty percent. This was the result of Luo Chen's half year of refinement.

If this trend continued, in the near future, Luo Chen would be able to fully refine away its impurities and possess a true high-grade treasure.

"Everything will come slowly."

Luo Chen smiled faintly and familiarly took up one set of medicinal materials.

Just as in the past, he began the most basic work of processing medicinal materials. There was no impatience on his face, only greater focus.

*(End of Chapter)*
