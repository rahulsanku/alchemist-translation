# Chapter 639: Returning to the Myriad Immortals, Ascending Moya Again

On the boundless great sea.

White waves rolled, and a black figure swam swiftly through them.

Looking carefully, the swimming range was impressively a circle.

At the center of that circle, high in the air, a small black dot could faintly be seen sitting cross-legged in the void.

While driving away the nearby low-level sea demons, Black King also watched that small black dot.

"After Master secluded himself for one month, he left Purple Spirit Island and came here. He said he wanted to test a new spell, but there has been no movement all along. Who knows what he is doing?"

As he muttered inwardly, his expression suddenly moved, and he swam in a certain direction.

A moment later, Black King's enormous body blocked a strangely shaped seahorse.

Seeing Black King, the seahorse demon beast gurgled out a string of words. Black King's eyes widened.

"Speak human!"

The seahorse gurgled and babbled for a long while more.

Black King listened with considerable impatience. Demon qi surged from his body, raising rolling layers of waves that pushed outward.

"I do not care what you want to do. In any case, go around. Ahead is my territory!"

In the Mountains and Seas Realm, although there was a division between the human race and the demon race, in truth, this was only a one-sided division human cultivators made according to their own wishes.

Among the demon race, all sorts of races were strange and varied. They themselves were different, so how could they be lumped together?

Languages, written words, growth habits, and even mutual generation and restraint, natural enemies and dependents, all differed.

Black King had evolved from a Black-Scale Giant Python and did not communicate with this seahorse group. Without having studied it in advance, he simply did not know the other party's language.

Divine sense voice transmission might let them understand each other's meaning, but communication between demon beasts was much simpler and more direct.

Territorial awareness was exactly what Black King was revealing at this moment!

Facing Black King's anger, the seahorse froze, then also erupted with vast momentum.

Demon qi was like waves, rolling horizontally, actually not inferior to Black King in the slightest!

For a time, under the two confronting one another, a stalemate formed.

Just as both sides were locked in stalemate, the two demons' bodies suddenly trembled, and they raised their heads together to look at the sky.

A heavy pressure came from afar. Even after being weakened by a long distance, it still made their bodies sink.

Joy appeared in Black King's eyes. It was Master!

The seahorse demon beast seemed to sense something, glanced warily in that direction, and then swam away in another direction without hesitation.

"At least you know sense!"

Black King snorted and turned to swim toward Luo Chen's location.

Only, as his gaze swept around, he did not find Luo Chen's figure in the sky.

"Eh?"

Suddenly, Black King leapt out of the sea and quickly arrived at a place where waves rose and fell.

Within his vision, the white-robed man rose and fell in the seawater, his eyes filled with confusion.

"Master, what happened to you?"

Black King hurried forward and used his broad back to push the man into a seated position.

"Let us go back first!" Luo Chen said in a low voice while rubbing his forehead, his words carrying some exhaustion.

Black King gave a muddled hum and carried Luo Chen toward Purple Spirit Island.

Only, in his perception, Master now seemed unable to maintain that secret art for hiding cultivation, revealing a state of empty mana.

"What kind of spell is it that actually exhausted even Master's abundant mana?"

Luo Chen had no mood to explain to him at this moment.

Sitting on the jiaolong-python's back, he recovered mana while lowering his head to think bitterly over something. Occasionally, he touched his lower abdomen and muttered to himself.

"A person is not a heaven-earth spirit vein. Extraction should have limits. How should I control the degree within?"

"The Mountain Lord of Hundred Craft's formation followed the heavy meaning of earth attribute, so it could suppress me with force. But my mana attribute is violent and domineering fire attribute. How should I modify it?"

"In addition, the scope of this domain must be controlled properly. Otherwise, the first to be drained dry will instead be my Golden Core and Origin Pill."

"No. The two great energy sources cannot be placed in the same basket. One must be used for ordinary battle. In this aspect, I still need to study further."

...

When Black King carried Luo Chen back to Purple Spirit Island, they happened to encounter Sang Jiugong coming out of the sinkhole.

"Eh, Daoist Azure Yang, did you just go out?"

After the rest just now, Luo Chen had already recovered part of his mana and again activated the Concealment Array.

Therefore, in Sang Jiugong's eyes, apart from Luo Chen's face being slightly pale, he did not sense his state of empty mana.

Oh, his pale face could not be seen either.

As Luo Chen's Dao progress grew deeper day by day over these years, the skin color of his face had also become increasingly crimson.

If the white robe on his body were changed to a red robe, from far away, he would look like a burning flame. How could ordinary people detect changes in his expression from his crimson face?

Luo Chen hummed, his gaze falling on Sang Jiugong.

"Elder, what is this?"

Sang Jiugong clapped his hands and chuckled. "The Purple Monkey Flower transplanting work has completely ended. Next, we only need to observe the growth conditions of the first small cycle and make slight adjustments, then we can leave Purple Spirit Island."

Hearing this, Luo Chen's spirits lifted!

They had already spent more than half a year on Purple Spirit Island. Now, it had finally reached one stage.

Next, under Sang Jiugong's lead, he went to the sinkhole and carefully examined it.

During this, Sang Jiugong explained the key points of this ripening method to him.

Using the sinkhole's special geographical environment, it absorbed the spiritual qi of the entire Purple Spirit Island, and when supplemented with large quantities of demon beast flesh and blood, the sinkhole became a postnatal blessed land.

And within it, the most important thing was the method of "same kind consuming same kind."

Using high-tier Purple Monkey Flowers to devour large quantities of low-tier Purple Monkey Flowers, only then could several years, dozens of years, even a hundred years of effort be saved!

"Later, will a spirit planter need to watch over it specially?"

"That is not necessary. Purple Monkey Flowers are born and raised by heaven and earth. They are not that delicate."

Seeing Luo Chen worried, Sang Jiugong thought for a moment and said, "However, if you fear accidents, later I can arrange for a Foundation Establishment disciple to come to Purple Spirit Island every so often and take a look."

"That is not bad. But disciple?" Luo Chen looked at Sang Jiugong in puzzlement.

The other party smiled faintly, revealing an open-minded look.

"This old man is already at this age, with at most twenty or thirty years left to live. You cannot expect me to travel a thousand li every year to this Purple Spirit Island to farm, can you?"

Luo Chen was stunned.

He had previously heard others say Sang Jiugong was quite old, which was why the other party had established a sect and sought to pass down his skills.

But he had not expected the other party's great limit to already be near at hand.

Twenty or thirty years... that was merely the length of one deep seclusion for a Golden Core cultivator.

His lips moved, but for a long while, Luo Chen could say nothing.

Sang Jiugong crouched by the sinkhole, looking at the densely packed purple flowers crowded together in the dim light, his expression calm.

The agitation of chasing fame and profit from past days was gone, replaced by a few parts of ease and tranquility.

"No need to think of comforting me. Everyone has this experience. That long-life path is not something everyone can keep walking. In this old man's life, I have drifted, fallen into hardship, and also once been glorious. Thinking carefully, it seems tending these flowers and grasses that cannot speak is what makes me most comfortable. Fertilizing them, driving away pests, watching them grow strong, the satisfaction this brings is greater than raising my realm..."

Listening to the old man ramble on, Luo Chen stood there and pursed his lips tightly.

After a long while, when the old man paused to breathe, Luo Chen sighed. "I did not expect Elder Sang to be this open-minded. It seems I am the one attached to appearances."

Elder Sang stood and stretched his old waist.

"Bah, that is only now. Who knows, when the great limit truly arrives, whether I will weep bitterly and regret not working harder in cultivation in my early years."

Luo Chen was speechless.

Sang Jiugong looked toward Luo Chen and hesitated briefly. "Daoist Azure Yang, there is something I do not know whether I should say."

In ordinary times, for such matters, Luo Chen's answer would be that if one did not know, then do not say it.

But at this moment, holding the thought that a dying person's words were often kind, he still let the other party continue.

"My Earth Mulberry Sect has only two or three big and small cats. Its core direct disciple is only at Foundation Establishment now. In this vast cultivation world, it is like a firefly. Perhaps a single wave striking over will make it wither."

"This old man knows that my relationship with you cannot be called deep."

"But I hope that in the future, when Earth Mulberry Sect encounters difficulties, you can help them a little within your ability."

At the end, he looked sincerely at Luo Chen.

"This time, consider it Sang Jiugong truly owing you a favor. Only, I fear this favor can only be repaid in the next life."

Facing the old man's sincere, pleading gaze, Luo Chen was silent for a while, then finally nodded slightly.

"Then next life it is."

Immediately, Sang Jiugong laughed heartily.

The bright laughter spread over more than ten li, and even the purple flowers in the sinkhole swayed their bodies.

...

Three months later.

Two figures lingered in the sinkhole for a long time, finally leaving in satisfaction.

The black jiaolong-python's figure rose and fell in the seawater outside the island. The old man leapt onto him first.

The black jiaolong was somewhat dissatisfied, but said nothing.

The two men's gazes landed on the human figure coiled high in the sky.

They watched him cast spells and arrange formations.

When ten thousand rays of light rose from every direction, finally returning to calm, the place once filled with purple qi poison miasma suddenly vanished.

No, it had not vanished either.

Purple Spirit Island was still there!

But when viewed with a cultivator's divine sense, this region seemed empty.

If looked at directly with the eyes, it was merely an ordinary small island.

In the sky, Luo Chen looked with satisfaction at the formation enveloping Purple Spirit Island.

His formation attainments over these years had been fully displayed on this island.

Not only did it gather concealment functions such as hiding, confusing, and guiding astray, he had also used several lower-grade magic treasures as formation eyes within it to arrange several killing formations.

Even if low-level demon beasts forced their way onto the island, under the slaughter of those killing formations, they would not be able to harm the Purple Monkey Flower growing base, the sinkhole.

If there truly was something lacking?

It was probably puppets!

If some powerful third-tier puppets from the Falling Cloud Sect back then were placed on the island, this island's safety could rise another level.

Unfortunately, Luo Chen had had too many matters over these years and had to seize time to cultivate. Otherwise, with Han Zhan, such a master-level figure in the puppet path, how could he not fleece him a little?

Shaking his head, Luo Chen withdrew and floated down onto Black King's back.

"The matters here are temporarily finished."

"Let us go. Return to the Myriad Immortals Assembly!"

Black King responded, then plunged headfirst beneath the seawater. Two great cultivators stood on his back, spiritual light blooming to cover themselves, seemingly unaware of the seawater's pressure.

...

When they came, they had spent no small effort.

When they returned, it was much faster.

Firstly, because Black King had already traveled this route several times and knew it thoroughly.

Secondly, because Luo Chen and Sang Jiugong, two great powerhouses, held the line. Along the way, even if they encountered third-tier Demon Kings with extremely strong territorial awareness, once they released their auras, the other party could only obediently give way.

The only thing that delayed their journey was probably the righteous-demonic war that had lasted twenty or thirty years.

On the way back to the Myriad Immortals Assembly, they had to pass through several territories belonging to the Canghai Alliance.

Black King's enormous body naturally could not hide its movements from formidable figures while traveling.

Fortunately, Luo Chen and Sang Jiugong both had star-ranked demon hunter identities. After showing their waist tokens and stating their origins, the Canghai Alliance people chose to let them pass.

During this time, Luo Chen also took the initiative to ask about the current situation of the righteous-demonic war.

The news he learned was somewhat unexpected.

The joint effort of Moluo Stream and the Penglai Immortal Sect was becoming increasingly fierce, and they had even once suppressed the Canghai Alliance, despite its numbers and power.

This should not have been.

The Canghai Alliance did not merely have numbers and force; behind it stood an Ascendant great power!

If the demonic side had seized the initiative at the beginning, that could still be explained.

But during a prolonged war, why was the Canghai Righteous Dao Alliance still retreating again and again?

Regarding this, after Luo Chen pondered inwardly, he finally attributed the reason to the force behind the Penglai Immortal Sect.

Central Continent, Tianyuan Dao Sect!

That was one of the five strongest forces in the entire Mountains and Seas Realm. Even when the Primordial Demon Sect still existed, it was not at all inferior.

The number of top powerhouses within it was countless, and its great powers were crowned above the Five Continents!

Perhaps it was precisely because of the Tianyuan Dao Sect's secret support that the Canghai Alliance's great power surnamed Li feared harming the rat and smashing the vase, not daring to act, letting those below behave recklessly.

"However, this matter has nothing to do with me. I, Luo Chen, am now only a wandering cultivator. Why should I care if floods reach the heavens!"

After resolving the matter on Purple Spirit Island, Luo Chen's mood was rather good.

Unfortunately, this good mood quickly vanished after he returned to Peng Lake.

...

"Over the past year or so, there were indeed many people who came seeking Master. However, after Angler and the others returned and announced that the Withered Wood Ridge task was complete, those people no longer entangled matters. After all, they also all knew that Blood Wanderer's task was most important."

Luo Chen held a pile of visiting cards and listened to Heavenjade report the situation during his absence.

After learning that Angler received heavy reward from Blood Wanderer after completing the task, being bestowed Sage formation insights, his expression became slightly solemn.

On his side, there was also a benefit.

That was that his previous four-star demon hunter rank had been raised to five-star.

He only needed to make a trip to the Demon Hunter Office and file it, and he would be a proper five-star demon hunter.

A rise in star rank naturally had benefits. First, the mission cycle became longer and frequency shorter. Second, for all kinds of resource exchanges within the Myriad Immortals Assembly, he could obtain a fifty percent discount.

Including the rent for Peng Lake's spirit land!

"On the Zhou clan side, a genius Golden Core cultivator came seeking a treasure. Master had previously agreed to the other party, so I could not refuse." Heavenjade handed over a visiting card. "Master, when do you plan to receive him?"

Luo Chen thought for a moment. "Set it for tomorrow!"

Heavenjade nodded and stated the final matter.

"Ding Yi wants to see me?"

Luo Chen frowned, and his mind recalled the previous worries.

He somewhat did not want to go to the Sunken Sea anymore.

However, since Ding Yi wanted to see him, he naturally did not fear it.

Without waiting to rest, he personally ascended Moya Cave that same day and received the personal reception of Ding Yi and the Moya Cave Master.

Only, before he could state his purpose, Ding Yi directly spoke a piece of bad news.

"There has been a change at Arctic Yemo Heaven!"

*(End of Chapter)*
