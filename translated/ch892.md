# Chapter 892: This Journey to Nether Origin

Motian Cliff was cold and clear, with a rushing waterfall cascading down.

A figure in white robes stood by the mountain stream, nearly blending in with the white spray of water.

Luo Chen wore a contemplative expression; the commotion of the outside world did not disturb the calm within his heart in the slightest.

The three cultivators from the Nether Origin Sect had come to invite him—a matter of great significance.

Standing at his current vantage point, many hidden secrets had become accessible to him.

Among the three cultivators, Thunder Daozi was the strongest combatant of the Nether Origin Sect aside from their Ancestor. Lingtian City Lord was highly likely to be the next Sect Master candidate.

And that Chongming's status was even more exalted.

Young as he was, he had comprehended the true meaning of the Water Law on his own, achieving no less than the young prodigies of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. He was a surefire Ascendant seed.

Most importantly, behind Thunder Daozi stood Ancestor Yu Ming, behind Lingtian City Lord stood Ancestor Heavenly Origin, and behind Chongming stood the Black Marsh Ancestor whom Luo Chen knew well.

For these three to come see Luo Chen together demonstrated the Nether Origin Sect's regard for him.

But from another perspective, did it not also show their wariness of him?

"I fobbed them off with the excuse of hiding away to recover from injuries, but I'm afraid they won't believe it easily."

"Azure Frost is no ordinary person. Escaping from her grasp is truly beyond belief."

"Add to that Ancestor Heavenly Origin's incarnation making a move earlier... they might have already guessed that I went to Green Parasol Mountain!"

Luo Chen's brow furrowed slightly.

But then, he shook his head.

So what if they guessed!

In the end, they had no solid evidence, so he could still tell whatever story he pleased.

Unless the Nether Origin Sect was willing to give up a talent like him.

Clearly, they were reluctant to do so. Otherwise, during the conversation in the hall earlier, Lingtian City Lord and the other two wouldn't have steered the topic away from that aspect, not actively breaking that thin window of paper.

But this meant that once he went to the Nether Origin Sect, he would likely face considerable suspicion.

"Perfect. Let me see if the Eastern Wilderness's Ascendant Holy Land has the magnanimity to accommodate me!"

By now, Luo Chen was no longer worried about whether the matter of refining Transformation Pills for the demon race would be exposed.

He had fully demonstrated his value.

If the Nether Origin Sect couldn't even tolerate something like this, then they would truly be too petty.

But before that, he still needed to find himself some backup plans.

Footsteps sounded behind him.

Two sets.

"Husband!"

"Husband!"

Luo Chen turned around and saw Sima Huiniang, dressed as a matron, and the young, beautiful Gu Caiyi approaching together.

"Husband, are you leaving?" Gu Caiyi asked.

Luo Chen nodded. "The time has been set. Two months from now! They will come to pick me up in person."

"Two months?"

Gu Caiyi murmured the time, her face full of reluctance.

But Sima Huiniang asked urgently, "Does the Nether Origin Sect truly have a way to resolve the Devil Heart Gu?"

At this question, a strange look flickered across Luo Chen's face.

Earlier, Huiniang had gone down the mountain for only half a month when she suddenly heard of a method to resolve the Devil Heart Gu, and the source of that method was the Nether Origin Holy Land!

During the casual chat in the hall, Luo Chen had tentatively inquired about it from Lingtian City Lord.

The other party had spoken frankly, mentioning that there was indeed a Sage in the sect who had spent a long time in the Southern Frontier and had considerable research into the dao of gu insects.

He hadn't said for certain whether it could be resolved, only that there had been considerable research.

Luo Chen couldn't help but suspect that the news had been deliberately released by the Nether Origin Sect.

After all, Ancestor Heavenly Origin's incarnation and Lingtian City Lord had both seen his son; they might have noticed something.

First sending the White Rhinoceros Horn, and then deliberately releasing such news at this critical juncture—their aim was probably to use Luo Lingxi to restrain Luo Chen.

So, should he bring Luo Lingxi to the Nether Origin Sect?

Luo Chen's gaze fell on the flustered Sima Huiniang, and he gently nodded.

Her eyes lit up. "Husband, can you take us to the Nether Origin Sect as well?"

"Lingxi can go." Luo Chen's answer brought joy to Sima Huiniang's heart, but his next words made her expression visibly change. "You cannot."

"Why?"

"Not yet. Once I'm settled, it should be possible," Luo Chen said.

Sima Huiniang breathed a sigh of relief.

"During the time I take Lingxi away, Huiniang, you must cultivate diligently. You can no longer slack off like this. The stronger we become, the more our children will be protected. Whether the Nether Origin Sect can truly cure Lingxi is still uncertain. If your cultivation level rises, you might be able to solve his problems yourself without needing others."

Luo Chen's earnest advice made Sima Huiniang nod repeatedly.

Perhaps it was because hope had appeared, or perhaps because what Luo Chen said made sense, but this time she did not refuse.

Seeing this, Luo Chen smiled with relief.

Just as he had said, whether the Nether Origin Sect could solve Luo Lingxi's problem was still uncertain. But taking his son away from Huiniang's side would at least allow her to cultivate in peace.

Two months was enough time for mother and son to say their goodbyes.

And as for himself, it was time to attempt that idea.

...

Three figures left Motian Cliff, flying unhurriedly toward Lingtian Pass.

The discussion about Luo Chen had not ceased since they left.

"Senior Brother, is Luo Chen really that formidable?" Chongming asked curiously, a hint of disbelief in his eyes. "His cultivation level doesn't seem much higher than mine."

Lingtian City Lord hesitated. "It's hard to say, but this man has repeatedly shown abilities that command respect, indicating his rapid progress. Moreover, his battle record cannot be false."

Luo Chen's battle record was indeed too glorious!

Just the title of Demon-Slaying Sage alone represented the corpses of fourth-tier Demon Emperors.

Not to mention his terrifying feats—defeating the Seven Rhinoceros, pacifying Divine Origin, and annihilating Huang Man—which ordinary people could hardly imagine.

Chongming had been cultivating in seclusion at the Nether Origin Sect all these years and was now just one step away from the mid-Sage stage.

"He received the Heavenly Secret Pill gifted by Senior Sister Chu Yan. Perhaps, like me, he has comprehended the true meaning of a Law ahead of time, granting him the ability to fight above his level. But there is still a chasm between us and Grand Cultivators. What methods did he use to reach such a level?"

At this, Thunder Daozi sneered.

"What methods? It must be that mysterious origin true flame!"

Chongming looked over curiously.

Thunder Daozi explained, "I once sparred with him on Shouyang Mountain. At that time, I wasn't using my full strength, and he was holding back as well. But I have a very deep impression of his origin true flame. Its power far surpasses ordinary Law true meanings."

Chongming was surprised. "Has he mastered a fifth-tier divine flame?"

"That would be a question for Brother Ye." Thunder Daozi looked at Lingtian City Lord.

The other party nodded, his expression grave. "My master's incarnation and I once went to the Medicine King Domain battlefield. There were indeed traces of a fifth-tier divine flame there, and Huang Man died under its burning refinement. This is also the main reason the sect sent the three of us to invite Luo Chen."

Lingtian City Lord had a good relationship with Luo Chen, Thunder Daozi was powerful, and although Chongming's cultivation was not high, he had comprehended the true meaning of the Water Law, which had a certain restraining effect on the fire-attributed Luo Chen.

If negotiations truly broke down and a fight ensued, they would still have a sure victory.

As for why, under the circumstances where Luo Chen had already agreed to join the Nether Origin Sect, they still had to consider the possibility of "negotiations breaking down," one had to mention Luo Chen's relationship with Green Parasol Mountain.

Thunder Daozi had been briefed, and his eyes narrowed slightly now.

"He hasn't been telling the truth!"

Lingtian City Lord took a deep breath. "Ever since he returned to the Eastern Wilderness, he hasn't been telling the truth."

Chongming smiled. "It doesn't matter. Once he goes to the Nether Origin Holy Land and the Ancestors have time, he will tell the truth. And even if he has some connection with Green Parasol Mountain, it's fine. Our Holy Land has that much tolerance. Think of Senior Sister Chu Yan—she also came from our Nether Origin Sect, but at the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect..."

"Chongming!" Thunder Daozi glared at him.

Chongming shrugged, unconcerned.

Lingtian City Lord did not join the discussion this time, because their opinions didn't matter.

How to treat Luo Chen in the end would ultimately depend on the three Ancestors' intentions.

For now, their task was merely to invite Luo Chen back.

And so far, it had gone very smoothly.

...

At the summit of Motian Cliff.

In the place with the richest and purest spiritual energy, two figures sat facing each other.

One was Luo Chen in white, the other was Jin San in black.

That black-clad Jin San was the Sage puppet Luo Chen had refined from Jin Ling's corpse back then.

Over the years, it had been nurtured in the spirit cave and had reached a usable state.

At this moment, Luo Chen's whole body was trembling, the muscles of his face twitching continuously.

On his face, a golden light flickered faintly.

The golden light was not dense, and it even showed signs of dissipating at any moment.

But a surge of rich life force emanated from Luo Chen's Purple Mansion, and the golden light became tenacious.

Suddenly!

"Separate!"

Luo Chen let out a low shout, his entire body's blood energy retracting, his soul hiding away.

Immediately, a water droplet pattern appeared on his brow, trying to reflect his image.

As if sensing something, the puppet Jin San across from him instantly opened its eyes, its aura flowing, and a unique aura belonging to Luo Chen spread out.

Opening its mouth, it gently sucked, and the water droplet floated out, landing on Jin San's brow.

But after landing, the water droplet pattern flickered violently, showing signs of breaking free from the puppet and returning to Luo Chen's body.

Seeing this, Luo Chen was not alarmed. He formed a series of hand seals, and strands of his aura were infused into the puppet. The water droplet pattern gradually stabilized.

When the water droplet completely disappeared into the puppet's brow, Luo Chen finally breathed a sigh of relief, a smile appearing on his face.

"This molting technique derived from the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture* is truly effective."

So-called nirvana, when applied internally, is the ultimate goal of cultivation; when applied externally, it is a method of resurrection from the dead.

But which cultivator would willingly fall into a deadly situation?

Thus, the true scripture contained a molting technique born from the nirvana method.

It was by using this technique, with the help of the Nirvana Sacred Flame, that Luo Chen had gradually peeled away the Heavenly One Water Divine Ability that Black Marsh Ancestor had placed on him.

Peeling off this divine ability, which was ostensibly protection but actually surveillance, had been Luo Chen's long-standing intention.

But the methods he had previously conceived were too harsh and would easily cause him great damage to his origin qi.

In comparison, the loss from the molting technique was much smaller.

But saying it was easy was relative; the conditions were still harsh.

First, one needed a divine ability like the Nirvana Sacred Flame, whose rank was not lower than that of an Ascendant-level great power.

Second, one needed a clone, preferably closely related to the cultivator themselves.

Finally, the cultivator's soul had to be strong; otherwise, it would be difficult to peel off the divine ability.

These three conditions were hard for ordinary people to meet. Luo Chen only had the qualifications to implement them after returning to Motian Cliff.

Moreover, during this process, he had also borrowed the puppet death-substitution method used by Han Zhan back then, and only then succeeded.

"With this, I have no more worries."

"I can make this trip to the Nether Origin Holy Land!"

Luo Chen stood up calmly. The puppet across from him also walked toward him as they locked eyes, and with a flash of light, it merged into his body.

Faintly, the water droplet pattern reappeared on Luo Chen's brow.

Even if Black Marsh Ancestor were present, he would only feel that his divine ability was still on Luo Chen, never imagining that Luo Chen had already slipped out of his control.

But this was not enough.

Considering he would be bringing his son on this trip, Luo Chen had to take complete precautions.

*Dong!*

He stomped his foot, and an invisible wave transmitted deep into the earth.

Moments later, Black King emerged from the ground.

"You will come with me as well!"

Black King had already guessed where they were going, feeling both some unease and some anticipation.

Last time Luo Chen went to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, he had not brought Black King along.

But this time, going to the Nether Origin Sect, it would be traveling with him.

What exactly was the difference between a so-called Holy Land and an ordinary immortal sect?

...

Two months passed in the blink of an eye, and the day Luo Chen was to descend the mountain had arrived.

On the agreed-upon day, a magnificent flying boat arrived at the Luo Tian Domain.

On it, banners fluttered, and cultivators stood in formation.

Every cultivator's face bore an air of pride, and each one was at the Adept realm.

Such a lineup was not something ordinary Sage-level upper sects could produce.

Not to mention the famous Lingtian City Lord, who was also among them.

Many cultivators in the Luo Tian Domain who had received the news came to watch the excitement.

As for the Luo Tian Sect cultivators, they had long gathered, waiting at the foot of Motian Mountain.

When Luo Chen, holding little Luo Lingxi's hand, floated down from the mountain, hundreds of Luo Tian cultivators looked up at him with reverence, their eyes filled with both reluctance and pride.

For ordinary cultivators, being able to join a Holy Land was a great honor.

Even the Divine Origin Sect, Joyous Union Sect, and others from back then had once thought of going to the Nether Origin Holy Land for advanced studies, to break through to the Ascendant realm.

Luo Chen's departure this time might be fraught with hidden agendas, but in their eyes, he was soaring to a higher branch.

"City Lord, I trouble you," Luo Chen said to Lingtian City Lord, standing outside the flying boat.

Lingtian City Lord smiled. "It's no trouble. I was going back to report anyway. Picking you up this time is just along the way."

Luo Chen nodded, his gaze falling below.

Familiar faces, eyes filled with worry, reluctance, or blessings—all came into view.

On the path of cultivation, they had helped him greatly.

In the end, he had found them a place to cultivate and settle.

This trip to Nether Origin might mark the end of their bond.

No need for more tender farewells.

"Let's go!"

Luo Chen took his son's hand and stepped onto the magnificent flying boat.

With Lingtian City Lord's command, the flying boat's spiritual light blazed, slowly starting, and then, at a moderate but unwavering speed, flew toward Lingtian Pass.

Behind the flying boat came a chorus of orderly farewells.

"Farewell, Supreme Elder!"
