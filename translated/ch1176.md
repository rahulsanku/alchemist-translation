# Chapter 1176: Martial Dao Divine Intent, Carefree Will Depart (Asking for Monthly Tickets)

The fourth ring!

Chaotic energy, omnipresent, scoured like a storm at all times.

Ordinary martial artists could not even stand firm within it.

Even lifeless metal and mineral ores would be corroded to nothing by the chaotic energy.

Only nine cultivation stones hung suspended in the boundless void, as if they had existed since time immemorial.

Compared to the cultivation stones in the rings below, those in the fourth ring were far superior in length, width, and thickness. Any one of them taken out would measure ten zhang in circumference.

The stone membranes they extended were like a transparent sky canopy, shielding the outside world, allowing martial artists to settle down and cultivate in peace.

But today, the eighth cultivation stone was not so peaceful.

It was filled with shouts and roars of anger, lasting a full three days.

"Old man refuses to believe he can't beat you to death!"

An elder with white hair and beard glared with wide, furious eyes, hovering high in the air like a great sun.

Surging true qi burst violently from his body, his hands positioned one in front of the other in a cross shape.

Then, he madly struck palm after palm downward.

Thousands of palm shadows, splitting light and transforming, were impossible to defend against.

Below him, a young man in white robes had a calm expression, but a hint of madness lurked in his eyes.

Facing the cross-shaped palms attacking from all directions, he made no subtle movements, simply throwing one punch after another.

Most of the cross-shaped palms were shattered by him.

But some still landed on his body.

After all, compared to his ordinary punches, the cross-shaped palms the elder executed were far too exquisite, ever-changing and impossible to defend against.

However, the cross-shaped palms that landed on his body did not cause the white-robed man any serious injury.

Because a layer of blood-colored cloth robe enveloped him. Whenever a cross-shaped palm struck, the blood-colored cloth robe would tremble rhythmically, deflecting the terrifying palm force from various angles, or transferring it elsewhere nearby.

Clearly, this blood cloth robe was a special defensive martial art for deflecting and transferring force.

Seeing the white-robed man break out of the encirclement of fist shadows, the elder was furious.

"Again, again!"

It was as if he had faced this kind of ordinary punch countless times.

No matter what killing move he used—domineering, gentle, or insidious and treacherous—the other party always responded to change with constancy, using only that simple punch to break it.

But this time!

He would severely injure the other party!

The elder's anger grew stronger, like a volcano about to erupt, his facial features twisted to the extreme.

The next moment, his hands suddenly pulled apart. The thousands of cross-shaped palm shadows that had been thrown out abruptly merged.

A terrifying giant cross-shaped palm, wreathed in true solar true qi pushed to the extreme, pressed down like a great sun.

Extreme Solar Cross Palm!

Boom!

Boom!

Boom!

The clash between the two sides lasted only an instant, followed by a continuous series of deafening sounds.

After unleashing this move, the elder was inevitably exhausted of qi and strength, falling from midair and landing with difficulty on the ground.

But his eyes were still fixed on the battle group that was being forced backward by the explosive impact of true qi.

Even though the white-robed figure inside was being blasted backward by the Extreme Solar Cross Palm, he was still throwing one punch after another, as if he never knew fatigue.

The elder's originally confident mood gradually sank to the bottom as the rumbling within grew louder.

In his pupils, when the white-robed man pulled his right hand back to gather strength, then threw a heavy punch—

Boom!

The cross-shaped true sun finally shattered.

The white-robed man stepped forward, his gaze falling on the elder.

Thump!

The elder could no longer hold on, collapsing to the ground, his face full of despair.

He had never imagined that he, known for his profound true qi and exquisite techniques, would one day be beaten to the point of exhausting his skills and depleting his qi and strength.

Looking at the white-robed man approaching, the Extreme Solar Martial Ancestor let out a weak, hoarse voice.

"Luo Chen, is your true qi truly inexhaustible?"

Luo Chen exhaled a turbid breath but did not answer this meaningless question. Instead, he raised his hand and looked at his fist.

The blood-colored true qi on it flowed like liquid mercury, seemingly full of spirituality.

But it was only seemingly.

"Still a little short."

Luo Chen murmured to himself. After glancing at the collapsed elder, he did not kill him but turned and left.

When the Extreme Solar Martial Ancestor saw Luo Chen's gaze, he was stunned.

What kind of look was that?

It was a look he had never seen before, or rather, no one had ever looked at him with such an expression.

It was dissatisfaction, regret, and even contempt.

Before Luo Chen's retreating figure had disappeared from sight, the Extreme Solar Martial Ancestor struggled to his feet and shouted angrily.

"Luo Chen, what do you mean!"

"Come back!"

"Give me a month, no, half a month. Then we'll fight again!"

Unfortunately, Luo Chen did not look back, as if he hadn't heard, or had heard but chose to ignore it.

The Extreme Solar Martial Ancestor could no longer give him any pressure.

So, he needed to seek the next opponent.

A whetstone to sharpen his true qi!

...

Forty years after entering the Nine Rings of Annihilation, Luo Chen broke into the fifth ring.

In just half a year, he defeated eighteen late-stage Leakless Realm martial artists.

Then, without any pause, he came to the fourth ring.

In the first year, he decisively defeated the ninth Martial Ancestor, Mu Yun, and successfully established a foothold.

In the second year, he challenged the eighth Martial Ancestor, Lin Yuan, and defeated him with only three punches.

In the third year, he fought a fierce battle with the seventh-ranked martial grandmaster, Ice Heavenly Son, for seven days and seven nights, finally defeating him.

After this battle, Luo Chen fell silent for a time.

It seemed he had gained something from the fight and went into seclusion for seven years.

Seven years later, as soon as he emerged from seclusion, he sought out the most troublesome figure in the fourth ring—the Extreme Solar Martial Ancestor.

This man was ranked sixth in the fourth ring, so his strength was naturally not the strongest.

But his temper was extremely fiery, and he often left no room for mercy in battle.

Whether against his enemy or himself.

Even if he fought until his qi and strength were exhausted, he would not easily admit defeat.

For a dangerous cultivation environment like the Nine Rings of Annihilation, being exhausted of qi and strength was a very dangerous state.

Therefore, few people would provoke him.

But Luo Chen deliberately sought him out and fought for three days and three nights.

The gains and losses of the battle were hard to judge, but the final result was clear. The Extreme Solar Martial Ancestor had indeed drained all his true qi. Even in the end, he refused to admit defeat and wanted to fight Luo Chen again.

But Luo Chen had indeed lost interest in him.

In the boundless void, Luo Chen flew while operating the Primordial Unity Cauldron, absorbing chaotic energy to replenish himself.

Regarding the previous battle, he felt some regret.

The Extreme Solar Martial Ancestor was very strong. If it had been earlier, he would certainly have gained something.

But unfortunately, before him, he had already encountered that woman, Ice Heavenly Son.

What he could have gained from the Extreme Solar Martial Ancestor, he had already obtained from Ice Heavenly Son in advance, for which he had gone into seclusion for seven years to digest the harvest.

And what he had gained was called "Divine Intent."

This was not an unfamiliar term.

As early as when Luo Chen had embarked on the first realm of body refinement, Wang Yuan had described this aspect.

Martial artists refine the body, with intent preceding qi!

Do not seek a pure heart and few desires, only seek to follow one's heart's desires.

At that time, to temper Luo Chen's will, Wang Yuan had even taught him his own technique, *Ming Shen Po Sha* (Bright Spirit, Break Balefulness).

After fully cultivating this method, Luo Chen's willpower had an excellent foundation. Especially as he experienced more and more things, his will became increasingly tenacious.

This will also played a significant role in his later immortal cultivation career.

However, as martial arts cultivation reached deeper levels, the so-called "intent preceding qi" would also undergo corresponding changes.

Martial artists must imbue their will into their true qi, generating divine intent.

If successful, they could achieve the realm of Human Immortal.

This was also the reason why martial arts masters, who did not train their souls, could resist the soul attacks of high-level cultivators.

The soul was insufficient, but true qi infused with divine intent was enough to interfere with a cultivator's soul attacks.

Luo Chen had discovered this from his battle with that woman, Ice Heavenly Son.

Her true qi was far inferior to his, even less than one or two percent.

But that woman had fought him for seven days and seven nights.

What she relied on was the divine intent initially condensed within her true qi, which allowed her to repeatedly dismantle his attacks and break his fist force in normal combat.

And the divine intent of true qi was closely related to the martial artist's own nature.

Ice Heavenly Son was as cold as ice, and her temperament was also calm and ruthless to the extreme.

So no matter how fierce Luo Chen's attacks were, she could calmly deal with them, finding ways to counter them one by one.

Luo Chen was inspired by this. After seven years of seclusion, his true qi also gained a similar spirituality.

The Extreme Solar Martial Ancestor had also reached this realm, but contrary to Ice Heavenly Son, his true qi was like himself, constantly roaring in anger.

But this time, Luo Chen was not satisfied.

So-called calmness, so-called anger—in the end, they were only the most superficial manifestations of divine intent.

He needed to seek more!

He needed to use more intense battles to gradually hone his true qi into an instinctive will.

Luo Chen's eyes grew increasingly mad, his movements swift.

A few days later.

Above a cultivation stone shrouded in the aura of slaughter, a figure suddenly appeared.

White robes fluttering, true qi like a dragon.

"Martial Ancestor Po Jun, Luo Chen comes to request a bout!"

On the cultivation stone, a gray-haired elder coldly opened his eyes.

Without a word!

With a flick of his finger, two long swords from a wooden rack shot into the sky.

Seeing this, Luo Chen laughed heartily and threw a punch downward.

"Well met!"

...

The third ring.

Unlike the boundless void of the lower three rings and the raging storms of the middle three rings, in the upper three rings, chaotic energy had almost condensed into a sea.

Vigorous energy tides came and went, scouring all things.

Only three cultivation stones, like islands, stood tall in the chaotic sea.

These three cultivation stones were extremely far apart, not interfering with each other.

A streak of flying light suddenly appeared, swimming through the chaotic sea.

Passing by a cultivation stone covered in heavy snow, the streak of light stopped, revealing the appearance of the Human Immortal Gu Xun.

He looked in surprise at the island-like cultivation stone. Two figures on it also looked over at the same time.

"Old Man Mu, Little Sister Fang, how do you have the leisure to gather and drink together?"

As he spoke, he descended onto the island.

Entering the wind-and-snow pavilion, he poured himself a glass of wine without ceremony, drank it with relish, and then his eyes lit up.

"Little Sister Fang, your Fang family's Thousand Mountains Twilight Snow truly lives up to its reputation!"

Fang Qingxue covered her mouth with a smile, "Brother Gu is joking. Such mundane wine is naturally no match for the Amber Flowing Light in Fellow Daoist Mu's storage ring."

Gu Xun subconsciously looked at Mu Bugui.

"Old Man Mu, it's rare for us to gather today. How about..."

Mu Bugui snorted coldly, "That is the wine from my hometown. Every drop I drink is one less. You've already tasted it, so how can you still covet more?"

Gu Xun shrugged, somewhat regretful.

Fang Qingxue was a little curious, looking in the direction Gu Xun had come from, and took the initiative to ask, "Have you been down below again?"

Gu Xun nodded without denying it.

Mu Bugui frowned, "Fellow Daoist Gu, if we work hard to cultivate, there is still a chance. Why do you give up on yourself like this, only seeking those heretical methods?"

Gu Xun sighed, "Even the Realm Lord himself is trapped in the Guixu, what chance do we have? Besides, I am old, my talent inferior to Little Sister Fang's, and my lifespan shorter than yours. If I don't find some other methods, wouldn't that be sitting and waiting for death?"

Speaking of this, he looked at the two with confusion.

"Old Man Mu, you usually only focus on bitter cultivation. Why did you have time today to come find Little Sister Fang for a drink and chat?"

Unexpectedly, at these words, the relaxed expressions of the other two became serious again.

Just as Gu Xun had seen when he first arrived.

Mu Bugui said in a deep voice, "The Annihilation Mist is coming."

Gu Xun was startled, "The kind that comes once every ten thousand years?"

Mu Bugui nodded but then shook his head.

Gu Xun didn't understand, but then he realized a certain possibility.

His expression changed drastically, "Could it be the kind that comes with the cycle of an era? Where did this news come from? Is it reliable? And when exactly is the specific time?"

A barrage of questions poured out, showing his extreme loss of composure.

Mu Bugui said nothing, and Fang Qingxue sighed.

"It was my clan's ancestor who told me, and he plans to leave the Great Annihilation Realm to seek a way out of the Guixu. So I took the initiative to find Fellow Daoist Mu to inform him of this matter. I was planning to tell you when you returned as well."

Gu Xun murmured, "How could it be so soon, how could it be so soon?"

Mu Bugui said solemnly, "Although this old man has a long lifespan, compared to this heaven and earth, I'm afraid I won't be able to hold out much longer. Therefore, I also plan to leave with Carefree Realm Lord on this trip. Gu Xun, what about you?"

In a daze, Gu Xun looked up at Fang Qingxue.

"Little Sister Fang, what about you?"

Fang Qingxue's face showed an open-minded expression, "My ancestor has no feelings for this place, but I grew up in the Great Annihilation Realm. If I leave, the Fang clan will have no Martial Dao Human Immortal to guard it. Moreover, when the time of annihilation truly comes, I will choose to forcefully break through the Annihilation Gate."

"That spatial vortex?" Gu Xun exclaimed, "That's a path even the Realm Lord has given up on."

Fang Qingxue smiled lightly, "Compared to the unknown, that is always a path with a billion-to-one chance of success. Brother Gu, what about you?"

"What about me?"

Gu Xun fell into a daze.

Seeing his confusion, the elder and the younger did not urge him. Having known each other for a long time, everyone understood each other's nature and knew that Gu Xun would find it difficult to make a choice.

They just continued to discuss their plans.

When this hundred-year period ends and the Ninefold Immortal Tower opens, Carefree Realm Lord will leave, and Mu Bugui will follow.

That day is only sixty years away.

After Carefree Realm Lord leaves, the martial artists cultivating in the Nine Rings of Annihilation will probably be expelled as well.

Because the Nine Rings of Annihilation can only exist by relying on the immortal artifact, the Ninefold Immortal Tower.

Only Carefree Realm Lord can simply control the Ninefold Immortal Tower.

Fang Qingxue, who stays behind, cannot control the Ninefold Immortal Tower.

Without the Ninefold Immortal Tower, without the chaotic energy in the Nine Rings of Annihilation, later martial artists will naturally have no ladder for upward advancement.

Waiting for them, perhaps, is only the result of the Annihilation Mist devouring everything when the cycle of the era turns.

"The predecessors have no path, the successors have no ladder. This world is truly like a cage, so despairing!" Fang Qingxue murmured with a sigh.

Mu Bugui also nodded, "Yes, I heard that a good seedling has entered this time, with hope of breaking through to the Human Immortal realm. Moreover, he was once a cultivator like me and Carefree Realm Lord. I wonder if I'll have a chance to meet him before I leave."

Gu Xun looked up and subconsciously said, "Luo Chen? He should be almost there, right!"

Almost there?

Mu Bugui and Fang Qingxue were startled and couldn't help but look at each other.
