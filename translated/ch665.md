# Chapter 665: Azure Yang's Strike, Heavenloam's Fury

Amid the sand and dust that filled the sky, a figure streaked past like a meteor.

One moment, he was close at hand. In the next, he was already at the horizon.

His speed was even greater than the golden sphere's escape speed earlier.

Luo Chen wore red robes, his face fair as carved jade. At this moment, his expression was calm, but Han Zhan, hidden within the soul-nourishing wood, could sense the surging emotion beneath that calm face.

"Are you truly going to ruin Blood Nightmare Moluo's plan?"

"Why not?"

"Between you and him, it does not count as a life-and-death hatred. But if you truly do this, you will have made real enmity. In the future, there will not even be a chance to sit down and talk."

"Heh. He may not have life-and-death hatred with me, but I have irreconcilable hatred with him!"

Han Zhan fell silent.

To a Sage, a Golden Core cultivator would never be placed in his eyes. Even Mountain Lord Hundred Craft hunting Luo Chen was only a single sentence from Blood Nightmare Moluo.

Even if Mountain Lord Hundred Craft failed, Blood Nightmare Moluo probably would not care.

If he saw Luo Chen again one day, he might even appreciate how quickly Luo Chen had grown and again think of recruiting him.

But to Luo Chen himself, if not for the Mystic Dust Armor he had painstakingly refined, he might truly have died on Seven Stars Island.

Ordinarily, Luo Chen said nothing and never hung hatred on his lips.

But this matter, Luo Chen remembered with perfect clarity.

He had merely acted according to the idea that a gentleman's revenge could wait ten years, choosing the moment to strike.

"This time, I will collect a bit of interest in advance. When I one day advance to the Sage realm, I must have a proper discussion with Blood Nightmare."

The corner of Luo Chen's eye twitched, and a trace of viciousness appeared.

Seeing this, Han Zhan naturally no longer advised him. Instead, he changed the topic.

"Since you and Tai Sui are separated, give me those two refined corpses."

Luo Chen's body suddenly paused.

He immediately took out two tall corpses from his storage ring.

They were the two corpses Adept Red Corpse had refined earlier, both with late Golden Core cultivation.

At the time, he had specifically instructed Heavenjade to put them away carefully. Now, they were perfectly intact.

"Can they be used?"

Luo Chen asked while looking at the dark wooden tablet floating out.

Han Zhan gave a hum.

"There is no great difference between refined corpses and puppets. Give me a little time, and I will be able to control them freely. With avatars like these, I will also be able to move more freely later."

Luo Chen glanced at the two refined corpses.

"Then Senior can wait here. I will go and return quickly."

When his words fell, he transformed into a streak of red light and shot into the sky.

Seeing this, Han Zhan shook his head and sighed. Then he placed his attention on the two deathly corpses before him.

"Still hiding, are you..."

With a light laugh, a wisp of azure light swept across.

In the next moment, the same scream came from both refined corpses.

That tone was exactly the same as Adept Red Corpse's earlier voice!

"A crude soul-splitting method, and you dare show it off before me."

Han Zhan smiled faintly. Azure light spread, completely covering the two refined corpses within this sand-filled sky.

...

"There!"

Luo Chen murmured to himself and stopped behind a black cloud.

The Concealment Formation [TERM?] was activated with full force, allowing none of his aura to leak out.

Afterward, he wiped a finger across his eyes. Spiritual light shone clear and deep, and his gaze projected toward the earth below.

The fierce battlefield instantly appeared in his eyes.

The already conspicuous gobi was now completely surrounded by the Flowing Sand Sea.

The battle between yellow-sand giants and stone monsters had also become one-sided.

The dragon soul within the black clouds appeared increasingly weak.

It had to divide its strength to resist the attack from Heavenloam, master of the Flowing Sand Sea, while also resisting the Soul-Summoning Banner's pull. This left it, already far from its peak, even more exhausted.

Under that tug-of-war, the dragon soul seemed close to being captured.

The enormous dragon soul phantom had gradually been pulled out from the black clouds and was gathering toward the Soul-Summoning Banner.

Seeing this, Luo Chen's heart tightened slightly.

"Fortunately, I came in time."

He smiled fiercely and patted the spirit beast bag at his waist.

Black King and Heavenjade appeared, guarding him on both sides.

The two demons also activated concealment arts at once.

"Protect me and guard against petty threats!"

After giving that order, Luo Chen's expression turned solemn.

His hands floated before his chest and began rapidly forming seals.

Those familiar seals were clearly the opening motions of the Azure Yang Great Handprint!

Only, unlike his usual near-instant release of this move, this time he became precise and methodical.

As he formed seals, pure and overbearing mana surged from his qi sea like a tide. It flowed through his extraordinary meridians, passed along his apertures, mouth, and nose, and gathered before him.

Logically speaking, such a surging aura should have been discovered long ago.

Yet even while Luo Chen gathered power, the three great Golden Core cultivators below who were presiding over the soul-summoning art noticed nothing.

The reason lay in both terrain and people.

The terrain was Five Elements Heaven's unique suppression of divine sense.

The human factor was the full activation of Luo Chen's Concealment Formation, hiding the surging mana fluctuation.

Black King and Heavenjade stood nearby. Feeling the terrifying aura continuously gathering and rising layer by layer, both swallowed unconsciously.

The Azure Yang Great Handprint was Master's proudest fire spell!

It was also Devil Lord Azure Yang's most iconic method outside.

Over these years, through constant study, battle, and refinement, it had already been cultivated to the extreme.

Ordinarily, releasing it casually was enough to suppress those of the same rank. Even cultivators one or two minor realms above Luo Chen had to handle it carefully and did not dare receive it head-on.

Now, Master did not hesitate to expend mana and was beginning to gather power fiercely.

One could imagine what level of might this palm would reach once released.

Heavenjade felt that even with her speed, she could not escape the attack range.

Black King thought that even with his thick skin and sturdy flesh, he would probably be blasted until his skin split and his flesh tore, losing eighty or ninety percent of his life.

No!

Not merely eighty or ninety percent.

When strand after strand of azure flame drifted out and wrapped around Luo Chen's hands, Black King's eyes instantly widened.

"Master is truly going all out!"

For a time, he could not help looking sympathetically at the three people not far away.

To be ambushed by Master without knowing it. When the time came, they probably would not even know how they died.

Just then, Black King suddenly sensed a change.

Heavenjade did as well and immediately spoke.

"Master, they are going to run!"

Luo Chen's hand movements slowed slightly, but he still did not stop.

His gaze merely fixed tightly on the altar.

Under the crashing yellow sand, the formation covering the altar was on the verge of collapse.

While Wu Qi controlled the golden bell with all his strength, he released a shuttle-shaped magic treasure.

After the flying shuttle appeared, it grew with the wind, turning two zhang long and hovering nearby.

Clearly, they had already prepared to run the moment they collected the dragon soul.

"Master, should we act?" Black King could not help asking.

Luo Chen did not speak. He continued gathering mana bit by bit, stacking the Azure Yang Great Handprint's power.

The time had not come!

Patience. Patience!

On the gobi, on the little solid ground that remained, Moluo Stream's three great Golden Core cultivators held their breath and carried out the final step.

When the stone dragon remnant soul was completely pulled out of the black clouds, Wu Qi shouted sternly.

"If the remnant soul does not return now, when will it!"

He bit the tip of his tongue and spat a mouthful of blood onto the golden bell.

Instantly, the bell rang loudly.

The dragon soul, already tottering and weakened to the limit, lost its instinctive awareness and transformed into a stream of light flying toward the white Soul-Summoning Banner.

When this scene appeared, a shriek suddenly came from within the yellow sand.

"That is mine!"

In the next moment, a sky-supporting hand emerged from the sand sea and smashed down toward the altar.

Facing this scene, the pupils of Wu Qi and the others widened.

Boom!

With a loud crash, the altar suddenly shattered.

On the gobi, the last patch of hard ground was completely covered by yellow sand.

Amid the explosion, the white banner was suddenly grabbed by one hand.

"Go!"

With a low shout, three escape lights merged into one and entered the flying shuttle, breaking through the rolling yellow sand at extreme speed and rushing toward the sky.

At that same moment, as the dragon soul was subdued, the heavy black clouds in the sky were slowly dispersing.

Beneath a clear and fresh sky, red robes snapped in the wind.

The Daoist's gaze was cold, his black hair flying.

He raised his palm.

And struck downward!

A giant palm wrapped in raging azure flames came rumbling down like a meteor.

Its might was not inferior at all to that earlier strike from the yellow sand hand.

From within the flying shuttle came a hoarse scream.

In a daze, it seemed to be the single word "No."

But no one had the leisure to listen carefully to what it meant.

Only endless explosion sounds deafened the ears at that moment.

Boom!

A heaven-reaching blast shook a thousand li of flowing sand.

With a single strike, the earth sank flat and the void exploded in waves!

Circle after circle of fire billowed outward, even forcing the nearby Flowing Sand Sea to retreat.

Under such might, the meteor shuttle instantly collapsed.

Three human figures within the huge explosion were like ants facing the might of heaven.

At that moment, a trace of blood light flashed.

When it reappeared, it had already escaped the center of the explosion.

"Pfft!"

Wu Qi had just used blood escape when he involuntarily spat a mouthful of blood.

At this moment, he was incomparably wretched. His law robes were tattered, his body scorched black, and only his eyes radiated a fierce will to live.

Holding the Soul-Summoning Banner, he bit off the index finger of his left hand.

Dense blood light began appearing around him.

Just then, an explosion rang through the void.

Hiss...

Sword qi slashed down. Wu Qi watched with his own eyes as his left arm was severed at the shoulder. The white Soul-Summoning Banner moved farther and farther away from him, finally falling into another person's hand together with the severed arm.

"No! That is Blood Nightmare's... It is you!"

When he saw that familiar face, Wu Qi froze for an instant.

After advancing to Archaic Tier Four, Luo Chen had shed the redness on his face and regained his fair complexion. At this moment, his appearance was no different from when Wu Qi had first met him on Invitation Moon Island.

In addition, Wu Qi had thought constantly about Luo Chen over those years and had long since carved his appearance deeply into memory.

Thus, at this moment, with only one glance, he recognized the newcomer.

Only, there was no joy of reunion between old acquaintances now.

There was only terror.

Bang!

With a single palm, Luo Chen brushed past Wu Qi.

"Sorry. Our positions differ. A little acquaintance is not enough to let you live."

Behind him, a lifeless corpse fell toward the yellow sand. While still in midair, it broke into pieces, finally becoming a cloud of blood mist.

Ahead of him!

Luo Chen pursed his lips. One hand gripped the severed arm and the white Soul-Summoning Banner, while the other reached into the air and held seven flying swords rotating without pause, facing the endless sand sea directly.

Within the rolling sand sea, grains of sand stacked layer upon layer. In the blink of an eye, a monstrous wave leapt into the sky.

A huge face appeared faintly within the wave.

"Give me... Stone Dragon!"

Along with those words, a sky-supporting giant hand reached out. It looked slow, but was actually fast, grabbing toward him.

Luo Chen's expression did not change. He took a deep breath, and mana surged wildly through his right hand.

The flying swords before him suddenly became one.

High in the sky, they formed an unrivaled giant sword.

"Slash!"

The giant sword abruptly cut down.

Hiss...

Like a knife cutting tofu, the sky-supporting hand split in two.

Yet seeing this, Luo Chen was alarmed instead of happy.

Under the replenishment of the surrounding yellow sand, the two separated hands each formed into hands roughly the same as before. One came from the left and one from the right, grabbing toward Luo Chen.

"Go!"

Luo Chen had no thought of fighting. Broken Moon Wings spread behind him, and Nine Thousand Li [TERM?] activated at full force.

His whole body became like a streaking fire meteor, flying toward the horizon.

"Return! It! To! Me!"

A sky-shaking shriek sounded behind him.

That enormous sand wave chased his back, rolling after him.

Within the sand sea, many giant hands appeared and grabbed toward Luo Chen.

At this moment, the entire Flowing Sand Sea had come alive!

Whoosh!

Whoosh!

Whoosh!

Luo Chen's speed was extremely fast, avoiding the grasping yellow-sand hands again and again.

When he could not dodge in time, he stomped the void. When he reappeared, he was already a hundred zhang away.

Suddenly!

Ahead, a sheet of yellow sand transformed into a giant wall, rising from the ground and blocking his path.

Luo Chen's expression turned fierce. He turned his shoulder and charged straight ahead.

Within the monstrous sand wave behind him, the huge face saw this and revealed a cruel expression.

This was like throwing an egg at a rock.

But in the next instant, Luo Chen, with unstoppable momentum, resolutely smashed through the southern wall and escaped the encirclement.

"No!"

"Catch him!"

The huge face roared in fury, and the whole Flowing Sand Sea began boiling.

More and more giant hands rose from the ground.

Not only that, sand spears screamed as they stabbed toward Luo Chen.

Luo Chen ignored them all. Mystic Dust Armor protected him, and blood qi surged to cover his whole body. He was like a savage bull, prepared to smash through even a mountain if it stood before him.

Boom! Boom! Boom!

Amid endless rumbling, a red light crossed a thousand li, ten thousand li.

Only once did he fall into the sand sea, yet after a brief moment, he shot into the sky again, completely unaffected.

About half a day later, Luo Chen finally reached the edge of the sand sea. In his vision appeared Tai Sui and Moya Cave Master's anxious and worried faces.

"What did you do?"

"Heavenloam is furious! What did you do to provoke it?"

Facing Tai Sui's shout, Luo Chen did not even acknowledge him. Voidburst Step activated again, and he appeared before the two.

His steps did not stop. His voice was low.

"To Blazing Refinement Prison [TERM?]."

"Then hurry. Heavenloam is truly beside itself with anger. I have never seen it so angry."

Tai Sui subconsciously glanced at the white soul banner in Luo Chen's hand, then crossed the boundary between the Flowing Sand Sea and Blazing Refinement Prison.

Before Luo Chen stepped into that world of fire, Han Zhan's voice sounded beside his ear.

"Why go this far?"

"If it can ruin Blood Nightmare's affair, then it is necessary!"

He activated Withering-Bloom True Fire, burned away the last trace Wu Qi had left in the world, stored the Soul-Summoning Banner in his storage ring, and without hesitation stepped into the world of firelight.

Not long after they left, grains of sand wriggled layer by layer and finally piled up on the boundary between yellow and red.

A little mud figure walked out from within. Its face held indescribable anger and venom.

It waved its hand, and streams of blood light came surging over, all broken limbs and flesh.

Grains of sand attached to the shattered limbs. At last, they formed the body of a one-armed man.

The little mud figure pointed at the man's brow. Heavy yellow light appeared, but gradually, that yellow light dispersed.

The one-armed man before it also melted unwillingly like a mud figure kneaded from sludge.

"It ran. It completely ran!"

"The remnant soul I finally lured here back then was not only not eaten and used to complete my true body, but was instead allowed to escape."

"Ah!"

A furious shriek came from the little mud figure's mouth.

Behind it, the Flowing Sand Sea exploded again and again, raising towering sand waves as though answering its fury.

*(End of Chapter)*
