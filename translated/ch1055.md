# Chapter 1055: The Revived Corpse Giant, the Sinking Mad Wanderer

Inside the Primordial Magnetic Light Shuttle.

Three figures stood silently, each with a somewhat tense expression.

It was only after they had smoothly passed through the Primordial Magnetic Mountain Path that Kongchan and Liujun breathed a sigh of relief.

"As expected of the tool that the Primordial Demon Sect once used to enter and exit Yemo Heaven. Although this Primordial Magnetic Light Shuttle is very slow outside, within the Primordial Magnetic Aurora, it is invincible." Kongchan praised.

Liujun smiled and nodded. "Next is the Soul-Locking Grand Formation arranged by the Soul-Refining Ancestor. However, that formation was basically already broken back then when I destroyed the formation eye at the Three Pillars Gorge and drew in the power of the Fallen Sea."

Kongchan did not deny this. If it hadn't been for Shark Emperor Liujun's great merit back then, the North Sea demon horde would not have known how much longer they would have been trapped within Yemo Heaven.

Next, as long as they returned to Yemo Heaven and guided the power accumulated for a thousand years in the Fallen Sea back, they would have the opportunity to open the Demon-Fall Land early.

At that time, he would be able to find a method to break through to the Ascendant stage inside.

Thinking of this, he glanced imperceptibly at the two people beside him.

These two still had value for now, but once they entered the Demon-Fall Land... Eh?

Kongchan looked suspiciously at the fat man.

"Taisui, what's wrong?"

Taisui frowned. "Nothing, I just inexplicably feel a bit uneasy."

Liujun chuckled lightly. "Fellow Daoist Taisui, are you perhaps too nervous? We've already passed through the Arctic Demon-Sealing Formation and the Primordial Magnetic Mountain Path. The road ahead is smooth sailing."

Taisui shook his head and said in a deep voice, "I always have a feeling of my heart pounding, as if someone is watching us from the shadows."

Hearing this, the other two also became serious.

"Could it be the one who opened the formation earlier and sneaked in?"

"Possibly. Anyone daring to force their way through the Arctic Demon-Sealing Formation is no weakling, even if the sealing formation hasn't yet recovered to its peak state! The last one who dared to break this formation was that madman."

"As I see it, that person probably isn't as strong as the madman, otherwise why would they sneak in so furtively?"

"Regardless, we still need to be careful!"

After a brief exchange, the three perked up.

The Primordial Magnetic Light Shuttle traversed the wind and snow, passing through the Primordial Magnetic Mountain Path in a flash.

Not long after they left, a hazy shadow slowly followed behind. It was Xiaobai.

She cautiously hung far behind, her aura perfectly concealed. Thanks to the geographical advantage, she was not discovered by the three ahead.

Soon, she too passed through the Primordial Magnetic Mountain Path.

The moment she entered Yemo Heaven, a feeling of being restrained arose.

Xiaobai looked back. At the entrance, there were still traces of residual formation power.

"Is it the remnants of the Billion-Soul Demon-Locking Grand Array that the Soul-Refining Ancestor set up before his death? I wonder where all those souls went after the formation was broken?"

Xiaobai was thoughtful, but her steps did not stop. She truly stepped into the Arctic Yemo Heaven.

What met her eyes gave her a sense of disorientation.

Layers upon layers of glaciers stretched endlessly before her.

The bitter, freezing cold pierced even the soul to the bone.

In the distance, however, there were mountains that pierced the clear, cold sky. Standing on those mountains, it felt as if the sky was within reach.

This was the mountain gate of the Primordial Demon Sect—Arctic Yemo Heaven!

A look of shock appeared in Xiaobai's eyes, and then she stepped onto the glacier.

Beneath her feet were countless frozen corpses—some human, some demon beasts. Broken limbs and bones were beyond number. Some of the better-preserved bodies even still bore the terrified expressions of their owners before they fell.

"I wonder how brutal that battle back then was?"

Xiaobai murmured to herself and was about to move forward when a sudden, inexplicable sense of dread struck her heart.

She abruptly looked around, but saw nothing.

"What is it?"

All around was silent, save for the howling north wind and the rustling sound of ice shards.

Xiaobai took a deep breath and cast several hand seals in succession, further concealing her aura.

"For now, the priority is to complete my master's task!"

Xiaobai transformed into a wisp of green smoke, drifting across the mirror-like glacier.

After she left, beneath the glacier, a corpse suddenly opened its eyes!

Not only that, but more and more corpses, one after another, also opened their eyes. Such a horrifying scene was enough to send chills down one's spine!

...

The area of Arctic Yemo Heaven was not very large, at least much smaller than the Nether Origin Sect at its peak.

But if measured against the Eastern Wilderness's Jade Cauldron Domain, it was at least five times its size.

Such an area seemed somewhat unworthy of the status of an Ascendant sacred land.

But this was related to many factors.

First, the two natural barriers—the Fallen Sea and the Primordial Magnetic Mountain—limited Yemo Heaven's outward expansion. They served as both defense and restriction.

Second, it was related to the relatively small number of cultivators in the Primordial Demon Sect.

Demon sects encouraged competition, and internal struggles were often bloody and cruel, leading to a sparse population.

Thus, an area five times the size of the Jade Cauldron Domain was sufficient to support the sect's core powerhouses.

For Xiaobai, this was an advantage. She quickly caught up with Kongchan and the other two demons.

But she did not disturb them. She simply hung far behind, observing what they were going to do.

During this process, she paid special attention to that fat cultivator.

This was Luo Chen's instruction!

"Master said he felt a familiar and kind aura from him. Why don't I feel it?"

Xiaobai was full of doubts.

Soon, Xiaobai's steps came to a halt.

The reason was that the three ahead had also stopped.

And the reason they were forced to stop was only one!

In that boundless glacial wasteland, a massive lake stood in the center.

This lake was different from ordinary large lakes. The water displayed a distinct red and black color!

A putrid and bloody scent filled the entire Yemo Heaven.

"What kind of lake is this?" Xiaobai was horrified.

Meanwhile, by the lakeshore, Kongchan and the other two also wore grave expressions.

In that battle back then, Yemo Heaven was drenched in blood, with countless demon beasts and human cultivators' blood spilling onto the ground.

Three hundred years had passed, and not only had that blood not dried up, it had actually formed such a large lake.

Not only that, but another force had also merged into it.

Liujun took a deep breath and said solemnly, "That black lake water should be the result of the Fallen Sea's power flowing in and corroding everything!"

Kongchan looked at Taisui. "Daoist friend, you said earlier that you had a way to guide this power back to the Fallen Sea?"

But Taisui shook his head, looking confused.

"This power has already completely merged with Yemo Heaven. It can't be guided out."

Kongchan and Liujun were stunned.

"How can this be?"

Taisui smiled bitterly. "I don't know either. The power of the Fallen Sea is quite special, formed by Devil Lord Heaven-Refining using an unfathomable formation. In theory, no one in the world should be able to utilize this power, right?"

The three looked at each other, all momentarily lost.

If they couldn't retrieve the power of the Fallen Sea, then what was the point of all their hard work coming in here?

Suddenly!

"Look, what is that?"

The demon monk Kongchan pointed at the blood lake.

Everyone looked together and couldn't help but show shocked expressions.

They saw a tiny figure rising and falling, sinking within the blood lake.

It was a person!

With that person at the center, a tiny vortex seemed to have formed, absorbing both the red and black powers.

The bloody aura and the corrosive power of the Fallen Sea merged together, forming an extremely special force within that person's body.

"It's demonic qi! Pure demonic qi!" Taisui blurted out, his eyes gleaming. "That person is using this blood lake to cultivate a demon race technique!"

Kongchan was puzzled. "A demonic path technique?"

Taisui waved his hand and explained, "Not a demonic path technique, but a demon race technique! The demon race is a collective term for the many races living in the demon realm. The power they wield is demonic qi. This power is overbearing, rejecting everything, and is by no means weaker than a human cultivator's mana. The demonic path techniques you know are merely techniques created by clever humans imitating the demon race. Among the best of them, such as the Primordial Demon lineage of the Primordial Demon Sect, can cultivate pure demonic qi."

After explaining, Taisui let out a turbid breath.

"I wonder where this person got his opportunity, to actually reverse his human body and cultivate a demon body? Who is he?"

This last question was soon answered.

Shark Emperor Liujun, who had been silent earlier, carefully observed the person in the blood lake and softly uttered three words.

"Mad Wanderer!"

Taisui was puzzled. "Who is he?"

"He's the madman we were talking about earlier. He was once hailed as the foremost of the Three Great Wanderers of the Northern Sea. Even the Primordial Demon Sect at its peak had no way to deal with him."

With this explanation, Taisui suddenly understood.

He had also roamed the Northern Sea cultivation world and naturally knew of the famous Three Great Wanderers.

The Mad Wanderer, originally named Layman Fengling, was a descendant of the Fengling Temple in the Northern Sea.

Later, the Fengling Temple was destroyed and occupied by the Primordial Demon Sect, and Layman Fengling became the Wind Wanderer. Later, he entered the Fallen Sea once, and when he came out, he had gone mad. The Wind Wanderer was then called the Mad Wanderer.

During his occasional moments of clarity, he, along with the Moon Wanderer and the Blood Wanderer, founded the Myriad Immortals Assembly.

However, the Myriad Immortals Assembly was later effectively controlled by the Moon Wanderer, and the Mad Wanderer completely disappeared. No one expected him to be here.

Thinking that the Mad Wanderer had once entered the Fallen Sea, Taisui immediately connected the dots. He might have luckily obtained a demon race technique in the Demon-Fall Land, and even his madness might have been caused by forcibly cultivating a demon race technique with a human body.

"Using the flesh and blood of countless corpses to rebuild a physical body, and using the power of the Fallen Sea left by Devil Lord Heaven-Refining to channel the demonic qi, trying to forcibly cultivate a demon body. This Mad Wanderer is truly mad enough!"

Taisui praised, then had a sudden inspiration.

"Actually, there is a way to take the power of the Fallen Sea away!"

The other two looked over curiously.

Taisui pointed at the Mad Wanderer's location. "We just need to wait. Wait for him to absorb all the power in the blood lake, then take him back to the Fallen Sea. Isn't that enough?"

Hearing this, the two immediately understood.

If the power of the Fallen Sea had completely merged into Yemo Heaven, they really wouldn't have any way to deal with it.

But if this vast power was concentrated in the Mad Wanderer's hands, it would be much easier to handle.

As for whether it could succeed?

The demon monk Kongchan said he could give it a try.

"The Mad Wanderer is mentally confused and has been insane for many years. My Chanting Monk clan is best at soul-bewitching and mind-control techniques. Back when we attacked the Primordial Demon Sect, I wasn't afraid of even the late-stage Sage powerhouse of the Soul-Refining lineage. How could I fear the Mad Wanderer, a madman with a clouded mind?"

"Then we wait!"

"Wait for him to finish absorbing!"

The three made up their minds and began making corresponding arrangements along the lakeshore.

After all, although the demon monk Kongchan spoke lightly, if there were any accidents later, it would be troublesome.

Moreover, the process of setting up the formation would also allow them to investigate who exactly that person who had sneaked in earlier was.

"Take my Morning Bell and Evening Drum and place them on both sides of the blood lake!"

"Once he has finished absorbing the power of the Fallen Sea, I will activate these two true artifacts, amplify the Buddhist chanting sound, and cleanse his soul!"

The demon monk Kongchan instructed.

This was his most skilled method.

The Morning Bell and Evening Drum were true artifacts passed down from ancient times by the Chanting Monk clan, very suitable for their clan's attack methods.

With the enhancement of these two artifacts, the power of Kongchan's Buddhist chanting could be amplified several times, maximizing his strength. This was also the confidence he had back then to face the strongmen of the Primordial Demon Sect's Soul-Refining lineage head-on.

While setting up the formation, Kongchan also had a sudden thought about the Buddhist lands in the Western Desert.

He had heard that the sacred Hanging Temple over there had been sealed, and thus there was no Arhat comparable to an Ascendant to suppress the Western Desert.

With his strength, he could easily take the time to go to the Western Desert and collect some Buddhist scriptures there, which might allow him to advance further.

If that happened, his chances would be even greater when he later entered the Demon-Fall Land!

While thinking this, Kongchan slammed his staff heavily into the ice layer.

This would be the formation eye where he would later perform the Myriad Buddha Chanting Grand Formation!

However, after the staff smashed into the ice layer, Kongchan felt the soft touch beneath the ice, and his expression immediately changed.

"Careful!"

Liujun and Taisui in the distance heard the warning and looked over in confusion.

Then, a surging tremor suddenly erupted from deep underground.

The three of them simultaneously flew into the sky, overlooking the earth.

They saw the ice layer, stretching for ten thousand li, cracking at a visible speed.

Crack! Crack!

In the broken ice, corpses that should have long since died staggered to their feet.

These corpses included demon beasts and human cultivators of the Primordial Demon Sect, some intact, some mutilated, but all now awakened from their burial.

Such a sudden change shocked everyone.

Even a delicate cry of surprise came from within an iceberg.

Kongchan and the other two looked over and saw a wisp of green smoke drifting into the sky, transforming into a beautiful woman.

After the beautiful woman flew out, the iceberg she had been hiding in collapsed with a crash, and a huge frozen snow ape corpse walked out from inside.

"So there really was someone spying!" Taisui stared fixedly at Xiaobai, murderous intent boiling in his heart.

The three had now gathered together, and Liujun stopped him.

"Forget about that woman for now. The priority is to figure out why these dead beings have awakened. Among them, there are even the remains of my sharkfolk clansmen!"

The demon monk Kongchan's attention was all focused on the countless, uncountable corpses below.

After carefully sensing, his face showed horror.

"Complex souls, trapped in lifeless bodies, yet they all act in unison. These ghosts... are the lonely souls and wild ghosts that the Soul-Refining Ancestor imprisoned in the Billion-Soul Demon-Locking Grand Array!"

As if to confirm his words, the countless "corpse ghosts" below began to move.

They paid no attention to Kongchan and the other two, nor to Xiaobai. They frantically ran towards the blood lake in the center.

During their run, these corpse ghosts surged forward, one after another, and began to overlap.

As time passed, a giant, towering to the heavens, a thousand zhang tall, stood upon the broken glacial wasteland.

This giant's body was composed of countless corpses. At a glance, it was utterly horrifying.

Countless eyes emitted eerie green flames, all staring fixedly at that blood lake.

"Groooan..."

A sound like weeping and complaining, yet also like an angry roar, came from the corpse giant. Then, it shuffled its steps and stepped into the blood lake.

Its target: the sinking Mad Wanderer!

Kongchan, Liujun, and Taisui silently watched all this, realizing that their arrival had awakened these undead spirits and further provoked them to attack the Mad Wanderer.

"No, the Mad Wanderer absolutely cannot be lost. We need the power of the Fallen Sea!" Taisui made his judgment.

Shark Emperor Liujun felt somewhat powerless. The oppressive feeling from that corpse giant far exceeded that of any Sage-level powerhouse. "We can't stop it."

"No need to stop for now. The Mad Wanderer will handle it himself." Kongchan pointed at the blood lake, where bubbles were rising one after another. Clearly, the Mad Wanderer was not completely unconscious, especially when his life was threatened.

Liujun hesitated. "So we watch from the sidelines and let them fight?"

"No!" Taisui's face twisted with ferocity, and his gaze suddenly fell on the woman standing gracefully in the air near the distant, broken iceberg. "First, kill this spying bastard!"

Liujun and Kongchan exchanged a glance, then nodded in tacit agreement.

Clear the field first!

Without needing words, the three of them simultaneously charged towards the woman, whose face was full of shock.

As the woman retreated, she cried out urgently, "Master, save me!"

(End of this chapter)
