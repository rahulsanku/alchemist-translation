# Chapter 640: The Demon Race Gathers Troops, Tai Sui Upon the Body

There had been a change at Arctic Yemo Heaven!

Hearing this news suddenly, Luo Chen's expression changed slightly.

As the former mountain gate of an Ascendant Holy Land, that was a grotto-heaven blessed land ranked among the top in the entire Mountains and Seas Realm.

After the Northern Sea demon race destroyed the Primordial Demon Sect, this grotto-heaven blessed land became an outright dangerous land. Who knew how many top demon beasts were imprisoned within it.

It could be said that this was a powder keg, always in danger of exploding.

And once it exploded, the nearest Myriad Immortals Assembly would inevitably bear the brunt!

In an instant, Luo Chen thought of the impact on his cultivation if the demon beasts on Arctic Yemo Heaven came pouring out in full force.

However, hearing the worry in Ding Yi's tone, Luo Chen was somewhat surprised.

"Fellow Daoist is not someone who grieves for heaven and pities people, with the common people in your heart. Even if something goes wrong there, will there not be tall people to hold it up?"

Ding Yi snorted. "I am merely a wandering cultivator, free to come and go. Even if the entire Northern Sea is occupied by demon beasts, I can escape to another human continent. But do not forget Arctic Yemo Heaven's geographical position, and its effect on our next plan!"

The Sunken Sea lay behind Arctic Yemo Heaven.

This time, Luo Chen's heart abruptly lifted.

Ding Yi did not keep him in suspense and immediately explained the specific situation he knew, one by one.

"The Myriad Immortals Assembly has relied on the Three Oceans defensive line to resist demon beast incursions for more than a hundred years."

"Over a hundred years, some patterns in the demon beasts' attacks have already been understood by them. Roughly every ten-plus years, large groups of low-level demon beasts will escape from the seal at Arctic Yemo Heaven and assault the Three Oceans defensive line."

"But in the last thirty years, this pattern has failed!"

Luo Chen did not understand. Had it failed?

The last time he went to the Quyang defensive line for garrison duty, he had also heard that large groups of demon beasts had appeared around the first defensive line, the Liuyang Sea Region.

Seeing Luo Chen disbelieve him, Ding Yi solemnly asked in return, "Did you ever see those demon beasts charge through to the later two defensive lines?"

"No!"

Luo Chen involuntarily clenched his fist.

"That is right." Ding Yi sighed. "In the past, when demon beasts escaped confinement, most would assault our human territories. In severe cases, they even broke through all three defensive lines and endangered the human hinterland. But in the last thirty years, this has become few and far between."

For any sealing formation, as years passed and the things being suppressed resisted, its effect would only become weaker and weaker.

The demon beasts trapped in Arctic Yemo Heaven would only emerge in greater and greater numbers. There was no logic for fewer and fewer to emerge.

Luo Chen naturally understood the principle within and subconsciously asked, "Where did they all go?"

"The Sunken Sea!"

Ding Yi gave the answer without thinking.

"As far as I know, the low-level demon beasts that escaped from the seal over these thirty years no longer tried to assault human territory, but ran toward the vicinity of the Sunken Sea to patrol and guard. Daoist Azure Yang, do you know what this means?"

Luo Chen's voice was somewhat dry. "Could it be that these demon beasts have set their eyes on the ancient cultivator ruin inside the Sunken Sea?"

"Eight or nine times out of ten, that is exactly so." Ding Yi sighed, a trace of dread in his eyes.

Luo Chen found it very strange. "Why would a human ancient cultivator ruin hold such attraction to demon beasts?"

This question had some internal logic.

Even if demon beasts walked the transformation path, humans and demons ultimately walked different roads. Their cultivation principles and cultivation method paths were all different.

Ordinary ancient cultivator ruins would not interest demon beasts at all, unless the owner of that ancient cultivator ruin was not human!

Regarding this, Ding Yi also could not provide an answer.

The Primordial Demon Sect Golden Core disciple he had captured back then had not held a particularly high status inside the sect. He had not grasped some important secrets, and what he knew was rather limited.

He could only sigh. "Perhaps these demon races found some intelligence inside the Primordial Demon Sect, which is why they raised the Sunken Sea's importance to the highest level, even disregarding attacks on us humans."

After all, it was an ancient cultivator ruin that even the Primordial Demon Sect had been rather moved by, spending over a thousand years researching.

If anyone in this world understood that ruin the most, it was certainly the already-destroyed Primordial Demon Sect.

On Luo Chen's side, his mood was half good and half bad.

Learning that the demon race had stationed heavy troops in the Sunken Sea, he both felt heartache that he could not go to a secret realm, and felt fortunate that he did not have to risk himself.

Along with this, even his tone became relatively light.

"In that case, I fear we cannot go to that ruin, right?"

The Moya Cave Master, who had been silently drinking tea for a long time, looked strangely at Luo Chen.

"Why could we not go? They are only some low-level demon beasts stationed there. If we want to go, they cannot stop us."

"Uh..."

Ding Yi echoed the Moya Cave Master's words. "Go, we must certainly go. Over thirty years, the number of demon beasts that could struggle free from the seal is relatively limited. If we prepare well, forcefully breaking in is not that difficult. Moreover, I also have a route. By contrast, if we miss this chance, then after several hundred years, when the demon race completely controls the Sunken Sea, we will no longer have any chance to glimpse the secret realm!"

Luo Chen's thought of following the slope down was directly denied.

He also sensed something wrong in his heart.

These two were far too determined to obtain that ancient cultivator ruin!

"Even if you do not worry about the demon race army, do you not fear those Sage Sages?"

Ding Yi did not understand. "Did we not say before? What we seek is completely different from what Sage Sages want, so there will be no conflict. Moreover, the places where we enter are different, and the chance of encountering them is small..."

At this point, his eyes narrowed slightly.

"Daoist Azure Yang, could it be you do not want to go?"

Luo Chen's face was calm. "I have indeed retreated somewhat."

"You agreed to the two of us back then!"

"But now, the situation has changed!"

"A few variables are still within the controllable range."

"Are they truly controllable? The celestial phenomena of the ruin opening have not yet appeared. When they truly appear, who knows what scale the demon beasts guarding near the Sunken Sea will reach? We are only Golden Core cultivators. Flying through the sky and burrowing through the earth are no problem, but facing the elite soldiers and fierce generals capable of destroying an Ascendant Holy Land, who can ensure there will be no accidents? Fellow Daoist Ding, Cave Master, can you guarantee it?"

This time, Ding Yi and the Moya Cave Master both fell silent.

Luo Chen also said no more and merely drank the still-steaming spirit tea.

After a long while, Ding Yi finally said in a hoarse voice, "I do not want to miss this chance. Perhaps it is the only chance in this life."

The Moya Cave Master sighed. "This old man is the same."

Bang!

Luo Chen set down the teacup and looked at the two in confusion.

"What exactly is inside that makes you so persistent?"

Ding Yi and the Moya Cave Master exchanged looks and finally stated the reason.

"The owner of that ruin must have been a top artifact-forger before death. Many powerful magic treasures have flowed out from inside. True artifacts and spirit treasures are also repeatedly seen. This old man wants to obtain his inheritance," the Moya Cave Master said.

Luo Chen froze. He had originally wanted to refute why the other party could speak so certainly, but a streak of blood light appeared in his mind.

That was a blood sword!

It had appeared at the Jadecold auction on Cold Light Island, said to be a spirit treasure, and had slain a blood-path great power.

It was said that after that blood sword broke away from a Sage's control, it headed north and entered the Sunken Sea.

Could this sword have come from the ancient cultivator ruin in the Sunken Sea?

"And Fellow Daoist Ding, what about you?" Luo Chen suppressed the surging in his heart and looked toward Ding Yi, with his fat body and small face. "The Cave Master loves artifact-forging, so I can accept that he is obsessed with this path. But why are you so determined to obtain that ruin?"

Ding Yi gave a bitter laugh. "What determination to obtain?"

He patted his body of fat. "It is merely being forced by necessity."

Amid the bitter laugh, before Luo Chen, he actually cut a piece of fat from his belly.

"What are you..."

Luo Chen was puzzling over his action, but saw that the cut-off flesh was actually wriggling, still visibly alive!

This horrifying scene made Luo Chen's words stop abruptly.

"You see it, right?"

Ding Yi released mana and burned away that piece of flesh.

"Back when I first explored the Sunken Sea, apart from the soul-nourishing wood, I also obtained an incomplete Tai Sui. I thought I had gained a supreme treasure, but unexpectedly, the soul-nourishing wood had foul qi and the Tai Sui had remnant thoughts. This treasure burrowed into my body and lived symbiotically with me, like a maggot attached to bone, constantly eating away at my physique. You can imagine that if it is allowed to freely devour me, my body and soul will surely scatter, and I will no longer have a boat of cultivation."

"Over these years, I have constantly sought a method to resolve it."

"Your Dustfall Pill was indeed effective for me. It allowed me to transform Tai Sui power into mana for my use within a short time. But this ultimately treats the symptoms, not the root!"

"I need to enter that secret realm, find the Tai Sui main body, and completely resolve the hidden danger on me."

Luo Chen half believed and half doubted.

But when he saw new white flesh grow from the place where Ding Yi had cut away meat, he believed a few points more.

Such ability of flesh self-generation was absolutely not something ordinary people could grasp.

Even he, with his current Archaic Tier Three physique, could not reach this degree.

Only the legendary Tai Sui, with extremely powerful vitality and known as "clear as solid ice, long-lived and unaging", could possess such creation.

Now Ding Yi was possessed by incomplete Tai Sui. If he did not want to be eaten away and digested, only by finding the true main body could he thoroughly resolve this hidden danger.

Lifting his clothes to cover his body, Ding Yi looked again toward Luo Chen.

"Tai Sui is a legendary spirit medicine of such high grade that nothing in the Mountains and Seas Realm surpasses it. And the Five Elements Lotus Platform you want, as I learned from this incomplete Tai Sui's remnant thoughts, grows nearby."

"Daoist Azure Yang, if you miss this chance, you may regret it for a lifetime!"

The Moya Cave Master also persuaded from the side, "An existence capable of refining spirit treasures and possessing a treasure such as Tai Sui must have been a top powerhouse. Moreover, that ruin could withstand over a thousand years of exploration from the Primordial Demon Sect, showing how profound its owner's realm must have been. If we enter it, even if we cannot achieve our goal, as long as we gain a little, we will benefit endlessly. Daoist Azure Yang, what exactly are you hesitating over?"

At this moment, the red face of the white-robed Daoist shifted between cloudy and clear.

Finally.

"Allow me to think further."

Luo Chen left.

Ding Yi and the Moya Cave Master once again sent him to the outside of the cave residence.

Looking at his departing back, Ding Yi put away the previous gloom and bitterness, his narrow eye corners carrying a trace of sinister shade.

"The former Devil Lord Azure Yang was not like this. Cherishing his body when doing great things, he has become cowardly."

The Moya Cave Master stood beside him, but his eyes carried some envy. "Did you not discover that his realm is growing higher and higher? Even before the two of us, he does not fall into the slightest disadvantage."

"Hmph, so what?"

The Moya Cave Master shook his head. "You said that during the Cold Light Island battle, he was only Golden Core Stage Four. But according to Dao Lan, at Withered Wood Ridge, he displayed the realm of fully Golden Core Stage Six. Breaking through two realms in a short twenty years. What does this cultivation speed mean?"

Ding Yi's expression froze slightly.

The Moya Cave Master sighed. "This person's cultivation speed is fast, and adding his youthful appearance, one can see his true age must also not be great. Unlike us, who at most have one or two hundred years to live, he still has plenty of time to continue climbing the Sage Great Dao. Under such circumstances, how do you expect others to willingly risk themselves?"

The fat cultivator suddenly understood. Over these years, he had been obsessed with observing the Sunken Sea's changes, but had forgotten that those around him were not stagnating like him.

Especially since wandering cultivators' cultivation speed was not much to speak of, he had completely overlooked Luo Chen's progress.

Thinking of it now, an existence who possessed Golden Core Stage Six at such an age, even placed inside a great sect, would certainly be a prodigy.

How could risking one's life be easy?

...

"You, this is precisely the older one gets in the jianghu, the smaller one's courage becomes."

On a solitary island in Peng Lake, the three-inch little figure manifested his Nascent Soul true body and softly laughed at the white-robed man.

Luo Chen ignored the mocking meaning within and directly asked, "In your elder's view, should I go to this Sunken Sea or not?"

"Your matter should naturally be decided by yourself. Why ask this old man?" Han Zhan sneered. Seeing Luo Chen rarely reveal hesitation, he shook his head.

"This old man cannot give you advice. I can only use myself as an example and give you a ready-made case."

"Oh?"

"Tell me, if this old man had not gone to explore that ancient Sage cultivator's ruin back then, would there be the name Sage Han Zhan in this world?"

Han Zhan's opportunity to advance to Sage had originated from one secret realm exploration.

Because of this, he had once offended a Golden Core cultivator of Tianfan City and provoked a Tianfan City Sage Sage to demand an explanation.

He instead profited from disaster, obtaining Tianfan City's support. Not only did he acquire a fourth-tier spirit land to break through to the Sage stage, he was also able to push the Golden Core great sect, Falling Cloud Sect, to the level of a Sage upper sect.

Luo Chen felt thoughtful.

At the end, he tentatively asked:

"If I go, is Senior willing to come along and act on my behalf?"

Han Zhan's three-inch Nascent Soul little figure now had the corners of his mouth raised, looking at Luo Chen with a smile that was not quite a smile.

"Your hesitation was false; asking me was the truth, yes?"

Luo Chen scratched his head, showing the green awkwardness of a young lad, rather embarrassed.

Han Zhan laughed loudly. "Between you and me, why speak in circles and act so distant? That Sunken Sea, I have heard of it for a long time and have long wanted to make a trip."

This time, Luo Chen was truly somewhat surprised.

"You have also studied the Sunken Sea, elder?"

"It is not study." Han Zhan's tone sank slightly, and his gaze fell on the soul-nourishing wooden plaque that housed his Nascent Soul body. "There may be an opportunity there for me to borrow a body and be reborn."

Luo Chen was shocked.

His eyes observed carefully, and only now did he discover that Han Zhan's Nascent Soul was so condensed it looked like a true infant.

The illusory, weak state from back then was no longer visible!

Joy appeared on his face. "Senior, your strength has recovered?"

Han Zhan waved his hand. "It cannot be called complete recovery. After all, without a physical body to rely on, the mana I possess is spent bit by bit in battle. If you truly want me to protect you while exploring the ruin, then it is best to find me several handy magic treasures."

Handy magic treasures?

Luo Chen's mind moved, and with a sweep of his sleeve, several streaks of brilliance appeared before them.

Han Zhan focused and looked, his eyes could not help brightening.

"This is a whole set of magic treasure flying swords?"

"They are named Seven Baleful. They can be used individually, and can also form an array to attack. Even if they cannot compare to the Two Modes Five Elements Sword Array Senior used before, I imagine in your hands, they can still display no small power."

"Seven Baleful Swords..."

Han Zhan murmured once. As mana surged, the seven resplendent sword artifacts immediately floated upright in the air. The surrounding spiritual qi of heaven and earth surged into them, and they immediately gave off ringing sword cries.

That sharp intent, even with Xuanchen Armor covering Luo Chen, still felt chilling.
