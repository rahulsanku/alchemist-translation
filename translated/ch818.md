# Chapter 818: Formation Covers a Domain, Great Palm Fills the Sky

He still remembered that when he first returned to the Eastern Wilderness, Gu Zuokai had told Luo Chen there was no teleportation formation that went directly to Lingtian Pass.

Back then, he had been forced to borrow the teleportation formation of the Dongyang Sect and take a roundabout route to get here.

Now it seemed that such a formation did exist—it was simply not widely known.

Since he had arrived relatively early and still needed to wait for the others, Luo Chen decided to spread out his divine sense to study the teleportation formation while simultaneously chatting with those who had arrived first.

Speaking of the six-man strike team, aside from Jin Ling, with whom he had a poor relationship, two of the remaining four were on very good terms with him.

Even Pavilion Master Li of the Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion wanted to ask him for help refining pills.

Today, Pavilion Master Li had also arrived early. The moment he saw Luo Chen, his eyes lit up.

"Alchemy Sect, how is your research on the Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid pill formula coming along?"

"I've done some preliminary study. It's quite tricky."

"If even you say it's tricky, then it must be true. This pill formula is an ancient remnant formula that the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect obtained by chance. It uses a water-based refinement method, making it extremely difficult. For centuries, the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect has only managed to derive a few low-grade formulas from it. They didn't dare attempt to refine this medicine directly, much less let their disciples test it."

The Jade Cauldron Sword Sect did indeed possess a series of low-grade pill formulas beneficial to the eyes.

Formulas like the Moistening Bright Pearl Liquid, the Clear Passage Liquid, and so on.

As it turned out, these were all merely ultra-simplified versions of the ancient remnant formula, the Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid.

According to the descriptions, the complete Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid, after long-term application, would make a cultivator's eyes clear and unobstructed, immune to illusion techniques, and might even give birth to a subsidiary pupil technique.

Whether that last claim was true or false was unknown.

But its most basic function was precisely what sword cultivators craved most.

Sword cultivators possessed formidable offensive power but were weak in self-defense. If they fell under the influence of illusions or their attacks deviated, they would find themselves in extremely dangerous situations.

That was why the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect had been researching this type of pill formula for so long.

After the Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion obtained the completed formula from them, they had eagerly sought out Luo Chen.

"If Fellow Daoist can refine this spiritual liquid and supply it steadily to my Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion, Li is willing to pay a considerable price."

"The materials are hard to find," Luo Chen demurred.

"We can provide a portion on our end. And I've heard that Fellow Daoist has a good relationship with the Tianyuan Merchant Alliance. Their business covers all five continents; they should be able to supplement the missing materials! As for the required spirit stones, my Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion will cover them!"

Luo Chen was somewhat surprised. He hadn't expected the other party to be so persistent about this spiritual medicine.

He instinctively glanced at the other's eyes.

Only then did he notice that this Pavilion Master Li was actually a double-pupil cultivator!

Deep within his pupils, there was an extremely tiny second pupil!

Unless one was impolite enough to stare directly, or possessed powerful spiritual eyes like Luo Chen, this anomaly would likely go unnoticed.

So that was it. Pavilion Master Li wanted to use the Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid to develop the abilities of his double pupils!

He hesitated for a moment, then still declined: "Luo has been too busy lately..."

Seeing Luo Chen repeatedly refuse, Pavilion Master Li asked urgently, "Is it because Lin Qingxuan targeted your sect's disciples? If that's the reason, Li can act as an intermediary and have him apologize to you in person!"

That was quite an arrogant claim.

Lin Qingxuan was, after all, a rising star who had made a name for himself under the title "Lone Swan of the Sword Realm," and he was now the master of a sect.

Could he really be made to apologize just like that?

But seeing Pavilion Master Li's confident expression, it seemed he truly could pull it off.

Just as Luo Chen was pondering how to respond, clear footsteps came from outside the cave.

Lingtian City Lord and Tandouzi arrived together.

"Everyone is here. Perfect timing. There are a few things I need to remind you all about."

Tandouzi spoke with a smile.

After drawing everyone's attention, he pointed to the formation platform in the cave and said, "This formation has the effect of teleportation and translocation!"

Hearing this, Luo Chen inwardly nodded. It matched his own judgment.

He had even guessed that this was the formation Tandouzi had used to enter Lingtian Pass undetected back then.

"For this strike operation, the key lies in the element of surprise."

"Therefore, a direct charge would be too exposed. An unexpected arrival from nowhere is what will catch them off guard."

"In a moment, I will personally preside over the formation and teleport you all to the designated location. During the teleportation process, everyone must calm their minds and protect themselves with their dharma power. If any accident occurs, this old man will not be held responsible."

Everyone nodded in agreement.

But Luo Chen, with his foundation as a fourth-tier formation master, understood why he had given such a warning.

Teleporting multiple Nascent Soul cultivators at once—and even including great cultivators at the late Nascent Soul stage like Lingtian City Lord—placed immense pressure on the formation, consumed enormous spiritual power, and demanded extremely high standards from the presiding formation master.

If the distance weren't so short, the other party probably wouldn't have dared to attempt such a risky plan.

"Finally, this old man will say one more thing. The existence of this formation is the last trump card of Lingtian Pass. I hope you will not leak it to outsiders."

"If someone does leak it and is found out..."

Tandouzi narrowed his eyes as he looked at everyone.

Lingtian City Lord stepped forward and said in a grim tone, "My Nether Origin Sect will be the first to pursue the matter!"

Naturally, no one would court trouble at a time like this. Everyone agreed.

"Alright, time is about right. Everyone, get on the formation platform. We'll send you over first, then Fellow Daoist Shenyuan and the others will follow."

Tandouzi suddenly said with a smile.

Such a rapid change in expression—if it were someone of a lower cultivation level, they probably wouldn't be able to react in time and would be left feeling uneasy.

But Luo Chen and the others were used to it. Who among them hadn't had their moments of moodiness?

Led by Lingtian City Lord, the six Nascent Soul cultivators stepped onto the formation platform.

Then they watched as Tandouzi, below, held a compass in his hands, forming hand seals and chanting incantations.

One by one, the embedded spirit stones extracted vast amounts of spiritual energy, and the formation patterns lit up one after another.

Just before it was about to be fully activated, Pavilion Master Li's expression flickered slightly, and then he looked at Luo Chen with delight.

The other party nodded gently.

"We'll discuss the Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid in detail after we return!"

There was no time to reply. Spiritual light erupted violently!

Blazing radiance shot into the sky, but was forcibly suppressed by the formation within the cave, not a trace leaking out.

But when the light faded, the seven people on the formation platform had vanished.

Tandouzi let out a muffled grunt.

"Getting old. Just seven people and I can barely handle it."

"I wonder what kind of miraculous teleportation formation they have on Green Parasol Mountain, to be able to teleport the entire mountain away?"

As he muttered to himself, another group of people walked in.

It was the interception squad led by Shenyuan.

"The instructions have already been given. Go on up directly!"

Tandouzi waved his hand, his face showing no sign of fatigue, completely unlike the frail old man he had just claimed to be.

...

"This is... the Overlord Blade Domain!"

High in the blue sky, Lingtian City Lord held a black mirror, within which the landscape of the earth below was reflected.

Among it was a massive canyon, hundreds of miles long, that was shocking to behold!

"It seems this short-distance teleportation didn't go off course. We've arrived at the most suitable place."

Lingtian City Lord smiled slightly, put away the black mirror, and instead took out a blood-red great banner.

The moment this banner appeared, the six people beside him all turned to look, all sensing a terrifying killing intent emanating from it.

"This is..." Iron Temple Daoist was shocked. "Could it be the Dutian Twelve Divine Baleful Formation Banners of the Dutian Sect?"

"Yes!"

Lingtian City Lord said with emotion, "For this battle, Tandouzi has contributed greatly, even lending out his sect's founding treasure. Although I am not skilled in formations and cannot fully activate this formation, as long as I follow Fellow Daoist Tandouzi's method of arrangement, the entire Overlord Blade Domain will be covered. At that time, all living beings will be allowed in but not out!"

After speaking, he swept his gaze over everyone with a smile.

"Everyone, do not hold back in this battle!"

Then, with a flick of his hands, the twelve blood-red great banners unfurled simultaneously.

Streams of vast dharma power surged into them as he formed hand seals.

The remaining six looked at each other, the phrase "allowed in but not out" still echoing in their minds.

It seemed they would truly have to give their all in this battle.

"Perfect timing. Here in the high heavens, I can also test out that technique I've just mastered."

Nine Spirits Primogenitor suddenly said, then sat down cross-legged as if no one else was there.

As he breathed and circulated his energy, rays of light from the blue sky began to converge toward him.

As the light gathered, the aura of Nine Spirits Primogenitor also began to surge, and a faint sense of dread spread through everyone's hearts.

Talisman Elder sighed. "Oh well. If we don't strike, fine. But if we do, we'd better go for the kill. This old man should also prepare a bit in advance."

Three talismans flew out.

One turned into a dark light and seeped into his brow.

One was unremarkable and stuck to his body.

The last one, however, was held in reserve, flickering with intense spiritual light.

The remaining four looked at each other.

Clearly, this battle was no longer just a simple strike operation.

It should be called an annihilation battle!

From the moment the Dutian Sect's sect-suppressing killing formation appeared, everyone had deeply understood the meaning of Lingtian City Lord's earlier words: "Make them feel the pain!"

Luo Chen had his own insights.

Once, the Seven Rhinoceros Commander had led seven demon emperors in a surprise attack on Lingtian Pass from the blue sky.

Now, they were repaying the favor in kind.

It seemed that control of the air in the blue sky was quite important!

No wonder Tandouzi, as soon as he arrived at Lingtian Pass, had set up the Zhou Tian Star Dipper Grand Formation to protect it comprehensively.

Since everyone was beginning to gather their power, he might as well not be idle.

He also wanted to see what kind of might the Azure Yang Great Handprint, accumulated to its peak state, would possess.

"Three Fellow Daoists, Luo will begin as well!"

With that, he drifted downward, away from the others, to a patch of void below.

His Sage domain suddenly expanded, covering a range of a thousand miles!

Luo Chen sat cross-legged within it, using his nascent soul to draw upon the vast free spiritual energy of heaven and earth.

His two hands began to form hand seals, one move at a time.

His right hand curled into a fist, then slowly opened, transforming into a palm—this was the original seal of the Azure Yang Great Handprint, the Primordial Seal.

His left hand flexed and extended, three fingers long and two short—this was the Spirit-Enslaving Seal used to enslave beast spirits.

The two seals overlapped, and the complete version of the Azure Yang Great Handprint slowly materialized.

The surrounding spiritual energy of heaven and earth began to gather toward the handprint.

He drew in the spiritual energy as if no one else was there, using his immense divine soul power to forcibly suppress the eruption of the Azure Yang Great Handprint.

And in the blue sky above, aside from Pavilion Master Li, who remained motionless, the other two also made their moves.

Jin Ling took out a golden feather, infused it with dharma power, and began to gather his momentum.

Iron Temple Daoist held a mysterious iron temple in his palm, clutching an ancient iron ruler in his hand, lightly tapping the stele on the temple, as if performing some kind of awakening action.

...

Time seemed to have passed for a long time.

But in reality, it was only a hundred or so breaths.

Into everyone's ears came a single low shout.

"Go!"

Luo Chen opened his eyes and saw twelve streaks of light, like falling stars, descend from different directions.

Their speed was extremely fast!

The people below had almost no reaction.

Only a single explosive roar suddenly rang out.

"Which scoundrel dares!"

"Move!"

Lingtian City Lord issued the command and charged down first.

Then, the other five followed suit, charging down as well.

As they passed by Luo Chen's location, Jin Ling glanced sideways and couldn't help but shrink his pupils.

There was a palm print suspended before him.

The five fingers were of proportionate length, the palm lines were vivid and clear.

It was lifelike, almost indistinguishable from a normal hand.

But clearly, that was not Luo Chen's hand. It was a manifestation of dharma power.

"What kind of technique is that?"

He had no time to think. Under his full-speed burst, the mountains and rivers of the Overlord Blade Domain had already begun to appear faintly in his vision.

The twelve banners had transformed into twelve pillars of light that pierced the heavens, enveloping a region of tens of thousands of miles.

Baleful energy crisscrossed, and a bloody hue began to spread.

A man with a horn on his head, a sword scar on the horn, forcibly broke free from the formation's restraints and charged out.

Seeing the six figures, his expression changed drastically.

"You..."

"Fellow Daoist Seven Rhinoceros, your opponent is me!"

Before the words had even faded, Lingtian City Lord had already blocked his path.

Then, without looking back, Lingtian City Lord said, "All of you, go ahead and slaughter freely!"

Nine Spirits Primogenitor was the first to charge into the formation.

His momentum was so immense that even Seven Rhinoceros, after a single glance, couldn't help but have his eyelids twitch wildly.

With such a powerful enemy, none of the demon emperors under his command would be able to stop him!

Without a second thought, he roared, "Thunder Prison, you promised to make a move for me once. Stop this man immediately!"

Within the Overlord Blade Domain, lightning flickered.

A figure materialized, blocking Nine Spirits Primogenitor's path.

"Who comes?"

"Nine Spirits Primogenitor!"

Nine Spirits Primogenitor responded. Nine-colored radiance swirled around him, transforming into a river of light that cascaded down.

Swish!

With just one strike, Thunder Prison was on high alert.

Caught within the river of light, he directly erupted with layers of thunderous radiance.

A roar of fury came from within the lightning: "You dare call yourself a primogenitor?"

His reply was not Nine Spirits Primogenitor's words, but the great commotion all around.

Pavilion Master Li entered the formation, erupting with ten thousand sword auras.

Iron Temple Daoist landed, heading straight for the center of the Overlord Blade Domain. The mysterious iron temple stood tall amidst the ocean of demon beasts, beginning to grow with a rumbling sound.

Every single invading demon beast that was struck by the light of the iron ruler vanished, as if devoured by the temple.

Jin Ling, holding the golden feather, stood in the void. Two demon emperors blocked his path.

Talisman Elder, however, was forced to stop, cautiously surveying his surroundings. There was the chirping of cicadas, but its source was unknown.

Above the Overlord Blade Domain, Lingtian City Lord frowned slightly.

"An unexpected development. There's actually an extra demon emperor. Why is this Thunder Prison Roc still here?"

Across from him, Seven Rhinoceros Commander revealed a cold smile.

"Not counting you and me, in terms of high-end combat power, it's seven against five. The advantage is mine. Moreover, this place is my army's home ground. Millions of demon beasts can bury them just by piling up corpses. Ye Lingtian, you've miscalculated!"

Lingtian City Lord took a deep breath. "Regardless of the outcome within the formation, I'm taking your ox head!"

"Arrogant!" Seven Rhinoceros Commander cursed, then let out a terrifying roar toward the earth.

All the low-grade demon beasts that heard this voice charged toward the Nascent Soul cultivators who had entered the formation.

Just as he had said, they would bury them by piling up corpses.

But!

This time, the human side was not just five people.

Just as the two late Nascent Soul powerhouses clashed, Seven Rhinoceros Commander caught a glimpse of a streak of light from the corner of his eye.

One look was enough to fill him with shock and fury.

Descending from the heavens, a great palm filled the sky!

A white-clad figure, following the rumbling, advancing giant palm print, drifted gracefully into the great formation.
