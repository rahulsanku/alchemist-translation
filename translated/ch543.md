# Chapter 543: Black Emperor Paste Tempering the Body, Third-Tier Overlord Crab

Heavenly Pivot Island was the name Luo Chen gave this nameless small island, derived from the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull.

This minor matter was not worth mentioning. What deserved Luo Chen's attention instead was the spirit vein deep within the island.

Small as it was, its quality was rather excellent, barely stepping into the third tier.

No wonder that Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull had ignored the danger and forcibly settled down in this Xuanyan Sea Region.

Deep underground, the Mystic Flame Sword erupted with sword qi, continuously cutting.

After half a day, an underground rock cave two zhang tall and several zhang in length and width took shape.

Looking at this simple and crude cave residence, Luo Chen nodded in satisfaction.

However, if he wanted to live here long-term, this alone would not do.

Light flowed around his fingertip, and small flags flew out from his storage ring one after another, shooting toward every corner of the cave residence.

Finally, Luo Chen flipped his wrist, and a compass plate appeared in his hand.

After looking left and right, Luo Chen came to the center of the rock cave.

"The earth qi flows. This shall serve as the hub."

"The spirit-gathering formation should be established here."

With a faint smile, that compass plate slowly seeped into the depths underground.

At this point, everything was ready, lacking only the east wind.

Luo Chen then sent out over a dozen middle-grade spirit stones, embedding them into the bases of those small flags. His hands formed several spirit seals, and he shouted softly.

"Rise!"

In an instant, layers of spiritual light bloomed inside the rock cave, colorful and splendid.

As Luo Chen sent out his final spirit seal, under the formation's control, those splendid spiritual lights slowly vanished. Meanwhile, on Heavenly Pivot Island, threads of white mist quietly began to rise.

Because it was early morning, with sea fog and vapor spreading everywhere, this white mist did not attract much attention.

When the sun rose high, this white mist would also gradually fade, leaving only one or two threads.

Han Zhan, inside the Myriad Souls Banner, watched this scene without a word and felt his heart shaken once again.

"Can there truly be such a genius in this world?"

"Putting aside alchemy, these formations seem basic, but whenever each one reaches Luo Chen's hands, it can always display a different kind of power!"

No wonder he was so shocked.

The spirit-gathering formation was also a common formation that was not much divided by grade. Its specific grade always came from the formation materials.

The better the materials, and the more one was willing to spend spirit stones, the more spirit qi could naturally be gathered.

Yet this spirit-gathering formation Luo Chen casually arranged, in Han Zhan's eyes, was actually more ingenious and exquisite than his own.

Not only could it gather more enormous spirit qi inwardly, it could also change outwardly according to the local environment without showing the slightest abnormality.

In Han Zhan's view, with Luo Chen's talent in the formation dao, so long as he was willing to spend time, Han Zhan estimated he would have nothing left to teach him in at most ten years or so.

Inside the soul banner, Han Zhan's remnant soul, in the form of a three-inch little figure condensed with ghost qi, helplessly shook his head.

Such great talent becoming a wandering cultivator was truly a pity.

If he were inside a sect, whether in alchemy or formation arrangement, he would have great hope of becoming a grandmaster of his generation and could help a sect grow and flourish!

Luo Chen did not know the inner thoughts of his traveling companion.

He briefly sensed the spirit qi density inside the rock cave. It was still third-tier low-grade, but only one step away from third-tier middle-grade.

If he were willing to replace the middle-grade spirit stones with high-grade spirit stones, he could break this boundary.

But very clearly, there was no need.

He himself did not have many middle-grade spirit stones. Before setting out, he had counted his current wealth: thirty-five high-grade spirit stones, two thousand middle-grade spirit stones, and very few low-grade spirit stones.

Taken together, his wealth was only around five hundred thousand.

One could say this was Luo Chen's poorest period.

There was no helping it!

During the previous opening war, in order to cultivate the Snow Wolf Plain spirit land he had conquered, he had spent much of his savings.

After arriving in the Northern Sea, he had also basically only spent and not earned.

With these spirit stones, every piece used meant one piece fewer.

"I wonder after the battle of Tiangu Plain, whether my Luo Tian Sect's sect spirit land can still be preserved?"

Luo Chen sighed, and several familiar figures appeared in his mind.

He shook his head and took out item after item from his storage ring.

Wooden racks, medicinal materials, stone mortar, small grinder, hay cutter... finally, the Primordial Unity Cauldron landed on the ground with a clang.

Where the Primordial Unity Cauldron landed, he added several fire-avoidance bricks beneath it and, according to the several methods Fei Ming had transmitted to him, arranged a simple fire pit.

In this way, this cave residence finally had a little liveliness.

Arriving at a specially left wide stone bed, Luo Chen took out quilts and thin blankets.

"I will rest for one night first. Tomorrow, I will officially begin the great plan of hunting demons and harvesting materials!"

He had traveled for a month, searched for land for half a month, and fought a third-tier avian demon beast. His mind had been tense the entire time.

First rest properly. Sharpening the blade did not delay chopping firewood. Luo Chen still understood this bit of logic.

...

"So this is the famous Azure Emperor Crab?"

On a desolate island, Luo Chen stood by the shore and looked at the enormous crab the Black-Scale Jiaolong had knocked unconscious in the shallows and dragged up step by step.

It was a full two zhang in size, green all over, as if covered in moss.

It was clearly enormous, yet for some unknown reason, it gave people a soft, slender beauty.

Inside the Myriad Souls Banner, Han Zhan answered, "There are many demon crab races in the world, but only the Azure Emperor Crab race is famed throughout the world for soft beauty. Although by the aesthetic sense of us humans, I do not know where exactly it is beautiful."

Luo Chen smiled faintly. "Whether it is beautiful or not, I do not know, but a demon crab of this size is full of treasures. We cannot waste it."

Hearing this, Han Zhan froze, then also revealed a smile.

Indeed, they truly could not waste it!

Luo Chen took out the Primordial Unity Cauldron and slapped its body. Several fiery-red chains immediately surged out, binding that Azure Emperor Crab layer upon layer and pulling it into the cauldron's internal space.

After putting away the Primordial Unity Cauldron, he looked at the Black-Scale Jiaolong lying eagerly in the sea and instructed:

"Continue capturing nearby demon crabs. Second-tier ones are best. First-tier barely work too. As for species, anything aside from Nine-Clawed Poison King Crabs is fine."

"If you encounter a third-tier crab king, avoid it if you can. If there is danger, seek help from Heavenly Pivot."

As he spoke, he pointed above his head.

A handsome azure bird was circling and flying around this desolate island.

The Black-Scale Jiaolong looked at the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull and curled its lip.

It would never seek help from its natural enemy!

The previous battle on the island had pecked it painfully.

After giving all these instructions, Luo Chen hurriedly left the desolate island and quietly returned to Heavenly Pivot Island.

After entering the cave residence,

clang!

The Primordial Unity Cauldron landed, and a spiritual light spat out from it.

In an instant, a large green crab entered his eyes.

After being taken in and released like this, it had already been startled awake.

At this moment, it was blankly looking at the man and the banner before it.

"You go first, or I go first?"

"You first. Black Emperor Paste requires live refinement. I will take the remnant soul afterward."

"Fine. Help conceal things a little. Do not let the sound get out."

This second-tier Azure Emperor Crab could not understand what that human was saying. It only knew that perhaps something bad was about to happen.

Sure enough!

That human walked toward it holding a stone mortar.

In his other hand was an enormous brush.

The brush stirred in the stone mortar, becoming wrapped in white liquid, then under mana control, began smearing along the shell of this big crab.

It looked just like painting a wall.

After a short while, the green crab shell was entirely covered in white lacquer.

It was also at this moment that the Azure Emperor Crab felt a tingling, numbing itch.

That feeling was like pain issuing from the soul itself.

Its eight crab legs trembled continuously on the ground, constantly trying to scratch its own back.

Once, twice...

In the end, under extreme frenzy, it actually forcibly shed its own carapace.

Seeing this, Luo Chen calmly nodded.

"Flesh and blood shed by themselves, spirituality not lost."

"The overlord sheds armor, yet remaining might persists."

The key points of refining Black Emperor Paste surfaced in his mind one by one.

Luo Chen lifted his empty hand, and that enormous carapace floated gracefully into the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Glancing at the shell-less giant crab wailing painfully on the ground, Luo Chen casually said, "The rest is for Senior to take as you wish."

Han Zhan did not respond.

But from that soul banner, streams of black qi flew out and wrapped the Azure Emperor Crab layer upon layer.

Gradually, a complete demon beast soul was pulled out, merging into the Soul-Refining Banner amid its unwilling howls.

In this way, this Soul-Refining Banner gained another second-tier demon soul.

Given enough time, it might not be impossible to reproduce the might of the Myriad Souls Banner, or even climb another level!

After finishing this, Han Zhan placed his attention on Luo Chen, who was refining the pill.

Unknowingly, Luo Chen's motions had already gone more than halfway.

A strange smell filled the rock cave.

When he smelled this scent, Han Zhan could not help sighing.

"Worthy of being an alchemy master!"

"The first time refining third-tier Black Emperor Paste, and there is not the slightest difficulty. It is completed in one breath."

A second-tier main material refined into third-tier Black Emperor Paste.

Sure enough!

When Luo Chen took out a large jar and began guiding the medicinal liquid within the Primordial Unity Cauldron to flow out, those sticky strands of paste like dough were undoubtedly the Black Emperor Paste Han Zhan was most familiar with.

Judging from its quality, it was around third-tier low-grade.

It was also one of the pill products that Mu Yunyan, chief alchemist of the Falling Cloud Sect, was best at.

After completely collecting the Black Emperor Paste, Luo Chen also let out a breath of relief.

He quietly glanced at the attribute panel in his mind.

[Black Emperor Paste Beginner 12/100]

Beginner-level proficiency, yet it could succeed on the first attempt. This truly was a good start.

Next, he could properly test how much help this paste he personally refined could provide for his body tempering.

However, before that, there was still one small matter to handle.

He turned back and looked at that Azure Emperor Crab on the ground.

No shell, no soul.

The fleshly body that should have been full of vitality was now nothing more than a walking corpse.

"The demon core cannot be wasted."

A demon core the size of a dragon eye was taken out.

"As for the flesh..."

Luo Chen sighed. Without its carapace and without its soul, even if he refined this flesh with azure flame, he would not be able to absorb much vitality.

But it certainly could not be wasted.

So it could only be used for eating!

His gaze finally landed on the eight huge crab pincers, especially the two thickest giant claws.

For a moment, Luo Chen truly did not know how to handle these things.

"Forget it. Put them away first. Even if I sell them later, they are excellent artifact-refining materials. Plenty of people will spend a high price to buy them."

In a short half day, this Azure Emperor Crab had been dismantled, skinned, drained of blood and marrow, and used to the utmost.

Even the remaining viscera, intestines, stomach, gills, and heart could be handed later to the Black-Scale Jiaolong and the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull.

The former did not fear toxins and especially liked flesh.

The latter especially liked eating crab hearts, often catching demon crabs and abandoning all their bodily essence, eating only the crab heart.

If Luo Chen's handling of it were known by a king of the Azure Emperor Crab race, it would probably fly into a rage and wish to dismantle Luo Chen, strip his skin, and pull out his bones.

But very clearly, Luo Chen did not care.

He only cared now about exactly what level of amplification Black Emperor Paste could provide to body refinement.

Inside the rock cave isolated from inside and outside, Luo Chen removed all his clothing.

Using mana, he smeared the black, still-warm paste over every corner of his body, even those private parts he usually paid little attention to, lest he leave any weak point in his body.

Afterward, he circulated the Myriad Path Convergence, which was close to Perfected Completion.

Raging fire burned within his body, while the paste soaked him from outside.

A faint scorched smell drifted out, seemingly there and not there.

Several hours later,

Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes.

Looking at the rough skin on his body, he first frowned.

Afterward, he took out the Mystic Flame Sword and lightly chopped at his arm.

A blood mark appeared.

But when it touched his body, there was indeed a faint sound of metal and iron clashing.

"My current physique still cannot resist magic treasures with the fleshly body, but very clearly, it is evolving in that direction."

"And I am currently only at the mid Archaic Tier Three stage. There is great room for improvement."

"Black Emperor Paste has quite good medicinal power!"

"This step of mine was correct!"

After reaching this conclusion, Luo Chen first cast a Cleansing Art on himself, then put on his outer robe.

When he walked out of the partitioned rock cave, Han Zhan looked over.

"How is the effect?"

Luo Chen smiled faintly. "If we want to see results, there is a long future ahead. There is no hurry."

Han Zhan understood.

In other words, there was indeed an effect.

This also confirmed his conjecture.

Black Emperor Paste had originally been a top-grade spirit medicine for body tempering!

It was simply that the requirements for cultivators using it were too high. Without the condition of a powerful physique, and without the will to endure severe pain, one could not use Black Emperor Paste to temper the body at all.

Normally, Falling Cloud Sect cultivators mostly used it on puppets that had no sense of pain.

Even so, they still had to add various other miscellaneous materials to ensure rigidity and toughness.

Thinking to this point, Han Zhan hesitated, but still reminded him, "Luo Chen, excessive hardness breaks easily."

Luo Chen gave an mm.

He naturally knew the medicinal nature of Black Emperor Paste.

Taking demon beast carapaces as medicine to forge a body-tempering tyrant physique was most fierce, hard, and unmatched!

But its flaw was also insufficient flexibility.

For puppets, this was naturally easy to handle. Add some materials to supplement flexibility and it was enough.

But on a cultivator?

Luo Chen thought for a moment, then merely laughed freely.

The enormous vitality his azure flame refined and absorbed strengthened his physique's recovery ability.

In a certain sense, this could also be counted as adding flexibility.

"Let us proceed step by step!"

...

The days of seclusion on Heavenly Pivot Island continued in this calm, uneventful way.

Day by day, the various demon crabs the Black-Scale Jiaolong delivered to Luo Chen became increasingly complete in type.

He also saw the so-called five royal demon crab races of the Xuanyan Sea Region.

The Overlord Crab had the strongest combat power, its two claws possessing terrifying enormous strength that could destroy anything.

The Azure Emperor Crab was "extremely beautiful." When it appeared normally, other demon crabs often accompanied and pursued it.

Red Rock Crabs, by contrast, were rarely seen. They always appeared in deep-sea volcanic trenches, and when they occasionally came ashore, they mostly bathed in sunlight in groups. The Black-Scale Jiaolong did not dare provoke them at all.

The Demon Spider Crab was the most terrifying. It looked like a crab, but its body was covered in eyes, and it could spit silk.

At first glance, it was no different from a spider.

Once, the Black-Scale Jiaolong encountered a third-tier Demon Spider Crab and nearly failed to return. It was only when the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull flew down from the sky and carried it away that it barely preserved its life.

However, Demon Spider Crabs also had merits.

Their meat was delicious. After Luo Chen ate one first-tier specimen, he could not stop thinking about it, repeatedly instructing the Black-Scale Jiaolong that if it encountered low-tier Demon Spider Crabs, it absolutely must not let them go.

The Black-Scale Jiaolong felt very wronged!

On this day,

Luo Chen was cultivating inside the cave residence when he suddenly heard urgent shrieks from outside.

"What happened?"

Han Zhan, who was staying outside, responded.

"It is Heavenly Pivot. Judging by its tone, Black Scale has run into great trouble."

Luo Chen frowned, then put on his clothes and calmly walked out of the cave residence.

With a leap, he flew into the high sky.

The Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull used its elegant back to carry him, fixed on a direction, and flew over.

A mere thousand li, at the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull's speed, was reached in an instant.

From afar, Luo Chen saw the specific situation.

In the deep sea, the Black-Scale Jiaolong revealed its full thirty-zhang enormous jiaolong body, raising wind and waves, wild waves churning.

But at this moment, it looked extremely sorry.

Because a demon crab like a colossal monster was waving its giant claws and madly chasing it down.

Luo Chen's eyes lit up.

"Third-tier Overlord Crab!"

(End of chapter)
