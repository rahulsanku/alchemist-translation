# Chapter 1189: Whitebone Altar, Incense Divine Power

Above the bone steps were two massive lower jawbones covered in sharp fangs.

They were opened ferociously, one above the other, forming a deep passageway like a great mouth leading to the stomach of hell.

Luo Chen stood there quietly, his expression neither sad nor joyful.

Whoosh...

A fierce wind howled, blowing away the thick smell of blood.

Yuan Qingzang descended and landed before Luo Chen, his emotions extremely complex.

There was both worry and surprise, as well as shock and reverence, and even a trace of guilt.

The battle just now had reached a height that no martial arts human immortal could attain.

Luo Chen had faced it head-on without a scratch, but he, Yuan Qingzang, had fled in panic.

"Luo Chen, I'm sorry, I just now..."

"It's fine. I'm already grateful that you gave me a warning." Luo Chen raised his hand to interrupt him, but his gaze remained fixed on that bloody mouth gate.

Through the endless, deep passageway, it seemed as if he could see something.

Yuan Qingzang breathed a sigh of relief, then asked hesitantly, "You're going in?"

Luo Chen nodded. "Of course!"

Yuan Qingzang's eyes widened. "I admit you're powerful, comparable to destruction kings like Carefree Realm Master and Heavenly Array Star, but aren't you afraid of offending Corpse to death?"

Destruction king?

A martial arts king from before the Great Destruction Era?

That was quite a title.

Heh!

"Only if Corpse is really here!"

Luo Chen chuckled lightly and had already stepped into the deep, endless passageway.

Yuan Qingzang stood at the entrance, looking bewildered.

What did "Corpse is really here" mean?

Had Corpse already left the Corpse Remains Immortal Domain?

Then who had sent that Corpse Immortal Finger earlier?

Seeing Luo Chen's back growing farther and farther away, Yuan Qingzang couldn't suppress his curiosity and quickly followed.

...

In the dark passageway, lights flickered unsteadily, and from time to time, sounds of explosions and rumbling could be heard.

That was definitely not the sound of Yuan Qingzang walking.

With his martial arts skill, he wouldn't make such a noise that would be discovered.

It meant that Luo Chen, who was leading the way ahead, was experiencing one battle after another.

Yuan Qingzang looked at the ground and could vaguely see scattered skeletons, with traces of Luo Chen's residual astral qi on them.

"These are puppet bone guards. The defense is quite tight."

"But why doesn't Corpse make a move himself? Clearly, these things pose no threat to Luo Chen."

"Could it be that Corpse really isn't here anymore?"

"Then who was it that issued the rebuke earlier and is now driving these puppet bone guards to block Luo Chen?"

Amidst his confusion, Yuan Qingzang gradually caught up with Luo Chen's back.

Without greeting each other, they tacitly joined forces to deal with the puppet bone guards surging ahead.

Their steps never halted; they killed as many as came, and they could sense that they seemed to be spiraling upward.

In the blink of an eye, the time it takes to drink a cup of tea had passed.

The originally dark passageway gradually showed a trace of light.

Two figures emerged against the light, and what met their eyes was a huge altar supported by layers upon layers of twisted, spiraling white bone steps!

Around the edge of the altar, rows of sharp ribs stood upright like a crown of thorns.

In the center of the altar was a massive skull.

"Corpse?!"

Yuan Qingzang blurted out.

At the same time, the skull's eye sockets flickered with faint, cold flames, and a chilling voice sounded.

"Ignorant junior, how dare you intrude? Leave now, and it is not too late."

Within the chill was a faintly seductive tone.

Yuan Qingzang hesitated.

But Luo Chen suddenly reached out, and five dragons materialized, grabbing hold of the skull.

"Bluffing with false bravado. Come out, this seat commands you!"

The five dragons bit down simultaneously. The massive skull could not withstand the power of the Blood Refining Divine Astral Qi and shattered in an instant.

A milky-white light mass popped out, spinning around.

"This is..."

Luo Chen looked at the light mass, hesitating.

Then, he turned his gaze to the altar on the ground.

The patterns were intricate and mysterious.

Some characters were engraved around it. Just one glance seemed to contain the mighty power of heaven and earth.

"Immortal bones... bone demon... myriad people's faith... incense divine power..."

It was Gold Erosion Script!

Luo Chen recognized this script.

When Carefree Realm Master had left the Great Destruction Realm, during those three years, in order to help him master the Ninefold Immortal Tower, he had taught him some.

Although not much, it was enough for Luo Chen to barely recognize some things on the altar.

With Luo Chen's intelligence, even with only a small amount of information, by connecting the pieces, he could deduce the general picture.

Looking at the milky-white light mass restrained by the Great Luo Five Dragon Hand, Luo Chen murmured:

"You spread your teachings and guided these people to believe in you, providing you with incense divine power, trying to exist forever, immortal and undying?"

He also finally understood where that fleeting sense of familiarity had come from.

This kind of scene, actually, he had seen hundreds of years ago.

The Western Desert Buddhist Land!

Too many common folk and ascetic Buddhists believed in those Buddhas and Bodhisattvas from myths and legends.

They built temples and monasteries, spread their doctrines, passed them down through generations, with incense never ceasing!

But at that time, Luo Chen's cultivation level was insufficient, and his understanding of the origin of the universe was lacking, so he hadn't seen its essence.

But now, Luo Chen already understood that this was actually also a kind of great path of cultivation.

Especially during the two hundred years he had used chaotic power to cultivate in the Destruction Nine Rings, he had seen too many types of energy.

Therefore, he immediately recognized the essence of this milky-white light mass.

"Incense divine power?"

As Luo Chen murmured to himself, the light mass no longer held its previous lofty attitude but instead let out an anxious cry.

"Daoist friend, you and I have no grievances or enmity. Why must you be so ruthless? Why not let me go, and we each go our own way. Corpse will also owe you a favor."

Luo Chen was silent. The other party was telling the truth.

Seeing that Luo Chen seemed tempted, the light mass added fuel to the fire and continued: "I can see that your cultivation method also has the style of my demon race. Although I don't know which clan your inheritance comes from, my Bone Demon clan is considered a major clan in the demon realm. If we can both escape this great calamity, I can promise to guide you into the demon realm in the future."

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow. He hadn't expected that his *Divine Demon Formless*, created by borrowing from the *Demon Phase Art*, could be seen through, even though his Blood Refining Divine Astral Qi contained not a trace of demonic qi.

The light mass finally said: "To be honest with you, Daoist friend, although I am a will split off from Corpse, our connection has not been completely severed. If you kill me today, the main body will also know about it. I imagine you wouldn't want to offend a destruction king!"

The moment the words fell.

Luo Chen's expression suddenly changed. His right hand clenched in the air.

The five dragons bit down together, and the milky-white light mass shattered in an instant, like a dream bubble, ceasing to exist.

A wisp of black qi, carrying a crystalline rib, tried to escape.

But where could it flee to?

In the holy hall, thunder roared. The black qi suddenly halted as if struck by lightning. The five dragons roared together and tore the black qi into pieces.

Luo Chen reached out and beckoned. The crystalline rib, like jade, floated slowly into his hand.

Yuan Qingzang stood to the side, silent as a cicada in winter.

...

The epochs turn, and all things return to the void.

The closer the Great Destruction Era approached, the more frequent the phenomena in the Guixu became.

Sometimes mountains collapsed and the earth split; sometimes abyssal floods surged.

Heavenly winds never ceased; thunderclaps were constant.

Most terrifying of all were the spatial rifts, which frequently appeared in some unstable places.

Deep in the Guixu, amidst a dangerous region riddled with spatial rifts, there existed a certain "spatial crevice," existing in silence.

Spatial rifts and spatial crevices—though only one word apart—were completely different existences.

The former were formed by the rupture of space, constantly in a state of collapse and reorganization. Ordinary people simply couldn't survive inside.

A spatial crevice, however, was formed by the interaction of two different spaces, creating something like a "sandwich layer."

As long as the spaces forming the crevice were not destroyed, this spatial crevice would exist forever, very stable and hidden.

And this particular spatial crevice in the Guixu was exactly such an existence.

It had existed for tens of thousands of years, surviving several Destruction Mists without being destroyed.

Furthermore, a peerless powerhouse who had drifted into the Guixu had found this place, built a Dao field inside, and spent the rest of his life there.

Its name was the Ancient God Dao Field!

For tens of thousands of years, countless martial artists had explored this place.

They tried to find the reason for the Ancient God Dao Field's eternal existence, tried to find a passage out of the Guixu through this place.

But most had fallen within, and few had managed to leave with any gains.

Over time, the Ancient God Dao Field was also hailed as the number one dangerous place in the Guixu!

No one dared to set foot there!

However, in the last hundred years or so, more and more martial artists had broken into the spatial crevice and reached the Ancient God Dao Field.

On a square paved with white jade, over a hundred people sat cross-legged.

Although each one had their divine will concealed within, their auras were still astonishing.

Especially the four people sitting in the very center, who were even more eye-catching.

They sat there, not emitting astral qi, not revealing their divine will, yet they were like pillars holding up the sky, impossible to ignore.

These four people, along with the other hundred martial artists, formed a distinct pattern.

Suddenly!

A suppressed grunt echoed through the silent square.

Carefree Realm Master, in his moon-white brocade robe, slowly opened his eyes and looked at the black-robed old man across from him, who was as thin as a bamboo pole.

"Corpse, what's wrong?"

The black-robed old man's expression was somewhat ugly. He subconsciously reached out and rubbed his chest and abdomen.

Inside, a rib was missing.

But at this moment, an inexplicable stabbing pain came from there.

He understood why, but facing Carefree Realm Master's "concern," he just shook his head.

"Nothing."

Carefree Realm Master frowned.

To his left sat a middle-aged Daoist with a handsome appearance, bright eyes, and sword-like brows.

He wore a purple Ziwei Daoist robe adorned with stars, adding to his mysterious and noble aura.

The Daoist had also opened his eyes now, staring at the black-robed old man with a dignified gaze.

"Corpse, if there are any changes that we don't know about, I hope you will share them honestly and not keep them to yourself."

Corpse looked back coldly and said indifferently, "Heavenly Array Star, mind your own business!"

The purple-robed Daoist, called Heavenly Array Star, twitched the corner of his mouth and said with a smile that wasn't a smile, "It's not for my own sake, but because this operation is a last-ditch effort for everyone. I don't want your secrecy to harm us all. After all, you are the only survivor who experienced the Destruction Mist ten thousand years ago, entered the Ancient God Dao Field, and returned safely."

At this point, the only one of the four who hadn't moved, a gray-robed old man, also slowly opened his eyes.

He didn't join the conversation but instead looked up at the sky above the square.

In the highest heavens, clouds and mist drifted, and a corner of golden splendor could be vaguely seen.

Without opening his mouth, his abdomen rumbled, producing a terrifying sound like crying and wailing.

"How much longer until the Ancient God Dao Field is fully opened?"

This question touched everyone's hearts.

Not only Carefree Realm Master and Heavenly Array Star, but even the group of martial artists sitting cross-legged nearby all looked over.

With so many gazes converging, it was like needles pricking their backs, but Corpse remained indifferent.

Carefree Realm Master frowned and said, "Heaven Cry Old Monster is right. We've been sitting here for over a hundred years, releasing our martial arts divine will to strike the Dao field gate every so often, yet we haven't seen a single crack. Corpse, you should at least give us an explanation!"

Heavenly Array Star also stared at Corpse.

Under the gaze of the crowd, the atmosphere was oppressive.

Corpse finally spoke slowly.

"At least sixty years, at most a hundred. Before the Destruction Mist arrives, the Ancient God Dao Field will open."

After saying this, he closed his eyes again and fell silent.

But his hand still unconsciously rubbed his chest and abdomen.

...

"What is this?"

Yuan Qingzang came closer, curiously looking at the crystalline, jade-like bone.

"Corpse's rib?"

"Perhaps..."

Luo Chen responded, then slowly ran his hand over the jade bone. The restrictions on it began to disintegrate inch by inch.

Those restrictions seemed powerful, but they were merely strong in force; their construction was extremely crude.

Luo Chen's attainments in the Dao of formations could be considered quite accomplished. Coupled with his powerful astral qi like the Blood Refining Divine Astral Qi, cracking them was not difficult.

After a moment, the black qi entwining on the jade bone completely dissipated, revealing a hint of immortal essence.

Luo Chen pursed his lips and invaded it with his divine will.

Just for a moment, he blurted out.

"Oh!"

"What's wrong?"

Luo Chen smiled and took out a stone the size of a fist.

Yuan Qingzang glanced at it and suddenly understood: "So it's Void Sprout Crystal!"

Luo Chen asked back, "Brother Yuan also recognizes this item?"

Yuan Qingzang naturally replied, "Of course I do. After all, there were rumors before that Void Sprout Crystal had the hope of allowing people to survive the Great Destruction Era. But rumors are just rumors. Who could gather such a large quantity of Void Sprout Crystal? However... a fist-sized Void Sprout Crystal is indeed rare!"

It was indeed rare. The largest piece Luo Chen had obtained before was only this size.

And it had come from a gift from Carefree Realm Master.

But Yuan Qingzang was wrong about one thing.

It wasn't that no one could gather the massive quantity of Void Sprout Crystal needed to construct a space.

Right now, inside this rib, there was a veritable "ocean" of Void Sprout Crystal!

"Did Corpse use these Void Sprout Crystals as a final refuge for the will he split off?"

Luo Chen pondered.

Myriad people's faith, incense divine power.

Bone demon secret arts, Void Sprout hut.

That Corpse had left behind many contingency plans for his separated will!

Now, it had all fallen into Luo Chen's hands.

In the past two hundred years, the Void Gu had long since eaten all the Void Sprout Crystals and Spatial Prism Apertures he had collected. Due to food shortages, it had no choice but to fall into slumber again.

Now, with such a huge amount of Void Sprout Crystal appearing, it could finally have a full meal.

Perhaps it could even take the opportunity to break through to the fifth-rank divine gu level in one go!

Yuan Qingzang curiously peered at the bone. "Is there only Void Sprout Crystal?"

Luo Chen smiled and took out a black book scroll made of bone. "There's also a demon race cultivation technique. Do you want to see it?"

Yuan Qingzang showed little interest. Instead, he hesitated, looking at Luo Chen.

Seeing his hesitant expression, Luo Chen put away the black bone scroll and tucked the rib close to his body.

"Daoist friend, if you have something to say, feel free to speak frankly."

Yuan Qingzang started to act a bit awkward, but finally couldn't help asking, "Brother Luo, how exactly did you manage to combine three moves into one and unleash the Raging Flow Extinction Realm in an instant?"

"So that's what it is!"

Luo Chen smiled, then said generously.

"These techniques were originally learned from you. Sharing the insights and tips with you is no problem. However..."

Luo Chen's gaze swept around, scanning everything in the Whitebone Holy Hall. It lingered on the Whitebone Altar for a long time, as if he wanted to etch it deeply into his memory. Finally, he turned and left.

"Let's get out of here first."
