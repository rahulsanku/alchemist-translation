# Chapter 621: Pill-Artifact Hall, Treasure Embryo Complete, the Happy Trouble

The doors of the Pill-Artifact Hall, whose furnace fires had not been lit for a very long time, slowly opened amid a rumbling boom on this day.

Luo Chen walked in front, Heavenjade behind him, as they entered the "crowded" hall.

"Master, all the various materials you requested have already been prepared in advance. To guard against failure, I also had the Zhang family specially supplement them with some extra materials as backup."

"Mm."

Luo Chen gave a soft hum, his gaze falling on the dazzling array of materials inside the hall.

There were fully over ten thousand jin of copper essence and iron essence. These were the most commonly used first-tier basic materials in artifact-forging.

Second-tier flowing white silver-tin was liquid under ordinary conditions, so it had been stored in glass bottles, five bottles in all. Judging by the current market price, a single bottle cost seven hundred lower-grade spirit stones. However, this batch had been bought very early, back when the righteous-demonic war had only just begun, and each bottle had cost only five hundred and eighty stones, saving quite a bit.

Second-tier materials took up the majority. Luo Chen looked them over one by one; their appearance and texture were all quite good.

After that came the third-tier mineral materials he cared about more.

Greenwild Beltweed was not a mineral material, but a spirit plant. This material was produced only in the deep sea, and required specialists carrying specialized tools to dive down and harvest it, so its price was not low.

For a spirit plant to appear among a batch of mineral materials naturally had its reason: Greenwild Beltweed over a hundred years old possessed excellent ductility. When fused into a magic treasure, it could allow the finished treasure to perfectly fit a Ruyi Array.

The defensive magic treasure Luo Chen wanted to refine was in the form of armor-clothing, possessing both the weight and solidity of armor and the convenience of a magic robe.

Most importantly, apart from its core defensive effect, this magic treasure had to be able to grow and shrink at will and change with his body.

The reason he had this requirement lay in the fact that Luo Chen's peak combat strength was not his normal state, but after he transformed into the true body of the Heavenly Peng.

Yet once he transformed into the true body of the Heavenly Peng, though the increase in size naturally brought a rise in attack power, his openings also increased by a thousandfold, a hundredfold.

When he was in late Foundation Establishment, during the battle in the Great Snowy Mountains against the grand elder of the Lianyun Merchant Alliance.

His Heavenly Peng true body, while unprotected, had been wounded by a third-tier Myriad Swords Talisman. Although he had not suffered heavy injury, that blood-soaked body was enough to show that under such a transformation, Luo Chen was rather powerless in terms of defense.

Another example was the battle at the Mo clan fishery on Cold Light Island. The mountain lord of Hundred Craft Mountain had driven a treasure mountain crashing down from above, and Luo Chen had countered it with brute strength.

Was his fighting style domineering?

No. It was only because under the Heavenly Peng true body transformation, his openings were too large. That giant mountain simply could not be dodged from its attack range, so he had been forced into a head-on clash.

After that move, his qi was nearly exhausted and his strength almost empty. Even though he later fled ten thousand li away, he still had to spend time finding a hidden place to recover from his wounds.

To sum it up, in ordinary combat, Luo Chen possessed both offense and defense. Even if neither was excessively outstanding, he could still adapt nimbly and control his movements as he pleased.

But in his peak state, the Heavenly Peng true body brought him unmatched combat power, while his defense plummeted to the extreme, leaving him able only to endure with his tyrannical physique.

Therefore, the armor-clothing he would refine next had to satisfy the needs of both forms at once.

However, the Heavenly Peng true body was easily over ten zhang in size. Ordinary Ruyi Arrays could not extend to such a degree.

This Greenwild Beltweed, a specialty of the Northern Sea cultivation world, solved this problem very well.

At this moment, inside the Pill-Artifact Hall, those dried, withered-yellow Greenwild Beltweed strands were piled together into a heap fully one zhang high, like a small mound.

One could imagine what volume it would swell to later during artifact-casting after being soaked in specially prepared seawater.

If such an enormous quantity of Greenwild Beltweed were fused into the magic treasure, how astonishing would its ductility become!

"Seven jin of high-quality Reversion Mud, including what I bought from the Cold Light Island auction, as well as what came from the estate of the Wu family's second son of Refining Edge."

"Five pieces of fire-avoidance lava. A single piece is worth several thousand spirit stones!"

...

As Luo Chen checked them one by one, he was also constantly simulating the next artifact-casting process in his heart.

The third-tier materials were very important. They determined the lower limit of the magic treasure.

But very clearly, what determined a magic treasure's upper limit was the higher-grade materials.

Under Heavenjade's gaze, Luo Chen lightly flicked his fingers, and streaks of flowing light flew out from his storage ring.

"Mystic Heaven Crystal!"

"Three-Yin Thunder Pole Wood!"

"Colorless Sunken Sea Sand!"

Luo Chen muttered to himself. This batch of fourth-tier mineral materials had not been obtained through robbery or plunder, but purchased at great cost from the Feileng auction on Cold Light Island, spending his savings heavily.

To guard against failure, he had deliberately bought multiple portions.

From this, one could also see why high-level artifact-forgers could make money.

For high-level cultivators, the selling price of magic treasures was not actually high in itself, making it very difficult for artifact-forgers to earn a profit.

Only by saving on costs to the absolute extreme could they reap profits of severalfold, even several dozenfold.

As for defensive magic treasures, whose failure rate was even higher, ordinary high-level artifact-forgers almost never refined them in normal times. If they did, they only accepted custom orders.

Even for custom magic treasure orders, they would require the customer to bear the material costs while paying an additional fee.

"Master, what is this?"

When Luo Chen carefully placed three objects resembling white camellias on the desk, Heavenjade finally could not help asking.

She looked at the three flowers, especially at the silver-white arcs of lightning that leapt across their petals from time to time, and the corners of her eyes twitched wildly.

The tremor from the depths of her soul made her realize that this thing posed an innate threat to her.

Luo Chen did not even look at her. He merely said softly:

"Thousand-Year Thunder Essence."

Hearing this name, Heavenjade could not help drawing in a breath of cold air.

After transforming into human form for many years and reading many human classics in detail, she was no longer the little fledgling who knew nothing about the human cultivation world.

Thunder Essence was a top-grade fourth-tier mineral material!

Whenever cultivators refined lifebound magic treasures, they longed for a single flower and could not obtain one.

Yet here, Master had taken out three flowers all at once.

Moreover, they were Thousand-Year Thunder Essence!

Did that not mean they were comparable to fifth-tier mineral materials?

Luo Chen ignored her shock and merely silently calculated the amount of Thunder Essence he possessed.

Back then, inside the cave residence of the Ascendant powerhouse White Night, he had collected eighteen flowers of Thousand-Year Thunder Essence in all.

To refine the Primordial Unity Cauldron, he had used three flowers.

Afterward, he had separately given three flowers to Nether Spring, the deputy hall master of Crossing Truth Hall, and two flowers to Azure Frost, the hall master of Daytime Void Hall.

During the chaotic battle on Cangwu Mountain, to prevent the Thunder Prison Divine Peng from acting against him, he had given away another flower.

Thus, he had only nine flowers of Thousand-Year Thunder Essence remaining in his hands.

And this time, to refine the Demon Emperor shed husk, he planned to use at least one flower and at most three!

The reason he intended to fuse this item in was that Luo Chen held extremely high expectations for it.

Ordinary magic treasures, nourished by Golden Core cultivators, could at most rise by one or two grades; some could not even be raised in grade at all.

For example, his Broken Moon Wings, after years of nurturing, had now advanced to the middle-grade level.

And that soul-defense magic treasure, the Rotten Axe-Handle Black Chess Pieces, had been nourished for even longer, yet even now it remained at the lower-grade level, still far from rising in grade.

From this, one could see how difficult it was to raise a magic treasure's grade.

But there was one type of magic treasure that was different!

That was a magic treasure fused with Thunder Essence.

Such treasures endured blazing fire tempering when cast, and then lightning baptism when nourished. Over time, they became clear and flawless, able to rise together with the cultivator's realm.

This was also one of the reasons cultivators liked to fuse Thunder Essence into lifebound magic treasures.

The so-called lifebound magic treasure was an object of dual cultivation of life and destiny. It referred both to the simultaneous cultivation of spirit and body, and broadly to the simultaneous cultivation of host and magic treasure.

The magic treasure he was about to refine used the shed husk of a fourth-tier Demon Emperor as its most central main material, comparable to a Sage Perfected Being!

How could such a treasure be treated as an ordinary magic treasure?

If it succeeded, it would surely become the greatest reserve treasure in Luo Chen's hands after the Primordial Unity Cauldron, and it was destined to accompany him for a long, long time.

If it could not rise together with his realm and instead dragged him down in the future, that would be far too regrettable.

"Three flowers will be used on the Demon Emperor shed husk, leaving six. Those six cannot be wasted either. Once the Primordial Unity Cauldron has been nourished to its limit in the future, I will fuse them in and raise its upper limit again."

In a flash, Luo Chen had already made up his mind.

A magic treasure that had not been fused with Thunder Essence could not have Thunder Essence added into it afterward.

But once its essence already contained Thunder Essence, then the same kind could later be fused into it.

After everything was prepared.

Luo Chen glanced at Heavenjade.

The latter immediately understood his meaning.

"Master, rest assured. I will manage outside affairs for you, and this time I will not let anyone disturb you again."

After saying this, she bowed and left the Pill-Artifact Hall.

The heavy bronze doors closed amid rumbling sounds.

After she left, Luo Chen took out two objects in succession.

One was the Primordial Unity Cauldron, which fell to the ground with a clang.

The other was the black turtle shell, floating in midair.

This time, Luo Chen did not throw the turtle shell into the Primordial Unity Cauldron, but placed it in the air just like that.

"This material is the crystallization of a demon race powerhouse's entire body. It can no longer be measured by grade."

"When refining it, ordinary methods will not have much effect."

"Fortunately, based on that set of artifact-forging blueprints from the Penglai Immortal Sect, I have groped out a method of my own."

With a faint smile, he sat cross-legged on the ground.

His five fingers spread in the air, then closed together like a sword. Two wisps of azure flame shot out from his fingertips, heading straight toward the Demon Emperor shed husk.

Not only that, his lips parted slightly, and another wisp of incomparably pure azure flame slowly emerged, completely enveloping the Demon Emperor shed husk.

Inside his qi sea.

His origin true fire blazed fiercely!

The crimson Golden Core spun round and round. Wave after wave of purified and refined mana fused into it, then was drawn out again in the form of flame.

This first step was the most direct burning refinement!

Luo Chen's Withering-Bloom True Flame was as high as fourth-tier. It was qualified to temper this Demon Emperor shed husk.

Only, the time required might be a little long.

Next, under Luo Chen's unceasing burning refinement, the black turtle shell floating in midair went from initially unchanged, to slowly charred black, gradually beginning to soften.

Inside the hall, surging demon power even began to leak outward.

That was all demon power the old ancestor of the Ring-Headed Turtle race, Xuanyan Demon Emperor, had cultivated over many years. For him to reverse his bloodline from the most ordinary Ring-Headed Turtle and cultivate into the body of an archaic beast, the black turtle, one could see how powerful he had been.

Even if this was only part of the shed husk he had left behind, the demon power hidden inside was still vast beyond measure.

Now that it had been stimulated out, it was somewhat wasteful.

Luo Chen did not care. He continued refining the turtle shell without pause.

To avoid insufficient mana, he often kept a supreme-grade True Flame Pill in his mouth.

Perhaps consuming pills in this way, without carefully refining them, would leave some aftereffects, but he could not care about that now.

After preparing for so many years, now that he had reached the final step, how could he be timid and hesitant?

Luo Chen's current artifact-forging skill, on the attribute panel, was already that of a genuine third-tier artifact-forger!

The opportunity for advancement had come from the jade ruyi he had given Zhang Jiadi at the time of his wedding.

That had not been a battle trophy he had seized in the past, but something he had personally refined, and refined without relying on the Primordial Unity Cauldron's nurturing state!

Over these years, he had continuously refined the six sets of mysterious armor.

Through constant success, he gradually groped out certain tricks.

Ultimately, in the same year he refined the Blazing Sun Prison Armor, he experimentally refined an ordinary jade ruyi magic treasure and succeeded.

In Luo Chen's view, that jade ruyi was said to possess both offense and defense, but in truth it was rather ordinary.

It was better than his Mystic Flame Sword from back then, but inferior to the Tianyue Purple-Gold Wheel.

It belonged to a quality level that was better than those below it, yet insufficient compared with those above.

But none of that mattered. What mattered was that Luo Chen had truly stepped across the threshold of a third-tier artifact-forger and was qualified to refine magic treasures!

Time slowly passed.

Three months later.

Luo Chen, his expression exhausted, finally withdrew the Withering-Bloom True Flame.

With a beckoning gesture, the turtle shell in midair landed in his hand.

Ignoring that terrifying high temperature, Luo Chen carefully sensed the turtle shell's state.

It was still extremely hard, but very clearly, it had already reached a state where it could be refined.

"The softening work is more than half complete."

Yes, at this step, only half had been completed.

Luo Chen had no time to rest. He immediately placed Xuanyan Demon Emperor's turtle shell on a specially made stone platform.

He removed his white Daoist robe, revealing the knotted, tempered muscles of his upper body.

With a shake of his hand, the Flowing Fire Hammer fell into his grip.

Facing the turtle shell that had been burned red-hot, he smashed down with force.

Boom!

With only one hammer blow, a thunderous crash rang out.

The sound was so loud that it even traveled out of the Pill-Artifact Hall and spread more than ten li across Peng Lake.

On a solitary island.

A three-inch little figure, close to half-illusory and half-solid, wearing a small wooden plaque around his neck, was quietly cultivating.

Because of this sound, he slowly woke.

The little figure frowned, then made a finger calculation. A look of realization appeared in his eyes.

"That boy has finally begun."

"It has been hard on him, being so careful and cautious. Without borrowing another's hand, he actually spent years preparing before daring to start refining."

"However, a Demon Emperor shed husk cannot be refined by anyone short of a top artifact-forger. I fear he will be disappointed."

As he sighed, the little figure suddenly shook his head.

"Still, let me wish him success!"

When he again inclined his ear to listen, he could no longer hear the rumbling sound of heavy hammer strikes.

Inside the Pill-Artifact Hall.

Luo Chen returned from the four corners of the hall.

He had rearranged the sound-insulation grand formation inside the hall to ensure that the movement within would not leak out.

Returning once more to the iron platform, he looked at the Demon Emperor turtle shell and clicked his tongue.

"I did not expect there to still be residual Demon Emperor mana inside. It nearly rebounded my Flowing Fire Hammer right out of my hand."

"But this is good too. The deeper this treasure's foundation, the greater the benefit to me once it succeeds."

"At worst, it merely costs more time."

His right hand raised the iron hammer high. Following a mysterious trajectory, he smashed it down with a crash.

One hammer after another, from slow to fast.

Later, inside the Pill-Artifact Hall, fire was like shooting stars and the hammer like a whirlwind cloak. This was precisely the Flowing Fire Hammer Method, an artifact-forging art at Great Accomplishment!

It had an exceptional effect on forging overly hard raw materials.

This was also one of Hundred Craft Mountain's artifact-forging secret arts that only Golden Core cultivators could learn.

Tempering the turtle shell took Luo Chen fully another three months.

Even with his robust physique comparable to a Demon King, even to an ordinary transformation-stage Demon Emperor, he almost could not endure it.

After sending the turtle shell, tempered to its limit, into the Primordial Unity Cauldron, Luo Chen practically collapsed onto the floor like a dead dog, unable to recover his strength for an entire day.

Several days later.

After adjusting both his spirit and body to a state where he could move, Luo Chen continued the artifact-casting work.

The first step, processing the core raw material, had already been completed.

Next came fusing all sorts of auxiliary materials into it to refine the magic treasure embryo.

This process seemed simple, but in truth it was extremely complicated, and it was also the process that most tested an artifact-forger's accumulation.

It required understanding every kind of auxiliary material, and during processing, different techniques had to be used.

Basic iron essence and copper essence only needed melting and purification. More advanced flowing white silver-tin had to be soaked in demon beast blood. As for Greenwild Beltweed, it needed to be soaked in large quantities of specially processed seawater so it could recover its original state.

These were still relatively basic.

The more difficult ones were those several fourth-tier mineral materials.

Mystic Heaven Crystal only needed to be shattered and ground into powder, but every single grain of powder had to be kept at the same size. During this process, only divine sense could be used with perfect precision.

Three-Yin Thunder Pole Wood required the Five Thunder Transforming Pole Hand to strip away the thunder bark and take the wood core.

Colorless Sunken Sea Sand, in turn, required another method...

In his spare moments, Luo Chen could not help sighing with feeling. No wonder most artifact-forgers of any renown in the cultivation world joined clans or sects.

With material-processing methods this complicated, relying on one person to accumulate them was simply as difficult as ascending to heaven.

If specialized talents existed who could process every kind of material during artifact-forging, then the artifact-forging master only needed to gather them together and refine them.

Luo Chen, however, did not have that easy fortune.

After sighing, Luo Chen still had to continue.

During the process, he failed several times, but fortunately there were enough backup materials, so it did not affect the overall situation.

Half a year later, it was time to process the final material.

That was the Thousand-Year Thunder Essence.

At this step, Luo Chen instead let out a breath of relief.

With a casual toss, one Thunder Essence entered the Primordial Unity Cauldron and slowly fused together with the other processed materials.

Yes, it was that simple.

As if the great Dao were most simple, the higher-grade the material, the more plain and unadorned its processing became.

As for how Luo Chen knew how to process this thing?

Back when the Primordial Unity Cauldron had been refined on Cangwu Mountain, he had personally watched how Heaven Smelter used Thousand-Year Thunder Essence.

Moreover, Hundred Craft Mountain's classics also contained records on processing ordinary Thunder Essence.

Combining the two, they were largely the same with only minor differences. It could be seen that the method was universal.

After one Thousand-Year Thunder Essence went in, Luo Chen watched the reaction inside at every moment.

After sensing that everything was normal, he tentatively added the second flower.

It was still normal.

Then, the third flower entered the cauldron.

At this moment, restlessness came from within the cauldron.

"Looks like this is the limit. It is about the same as I predicted."

This turtle shell came from Xuanyan Demon Emperor.

That was a fourth-tier Demon Emperor who had passed through lightning tribulation!

As the hardest part of the other's body, the turtle shell's importance was second only to the demon core.

It must also have endured brutal bombardment during that lightning tribulation back then.

Such a material had the foundation to accommodate three Thousand-Year Thunder Essence flowers.

But any more would test not the material itself, but Luo Chen's artifact-forging skill.

Very clearly, the current Luo Chen was still unqualified.

At this step, Luo Chen could not help admiring the late Heaven Smelter.

He had been a true grandmaster-level artifact-forger!

Back then, when the other party refined the Primordial Unity Cauldron for him, he had been at ease from beginning to end and had never felt strained.

If not for that drop of strange blood, Luo Chen's lifebound magic treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, could almost be considered something Heaven Smelter had produced with a casual wave of his hand.

Amid this admiration, Luo Chen also became vigilant inwardly.

"Every skill is broad and profound."

"With the system in my hand, perhaps I can easily enter the threshold of any skill and achieve no small accomplishment. But if I want to reach the peak, the time and energy I must spend will be unimaginable."

"Once I become addicted to it, it will instead affect my cultivation state."

"This matter, remember it well! Remember it well!"

After reflecting on himself, Luo Chen used artifact-forging techniques to adjust the fusion progress of the various materials inside the cauldron. From time to time, he would add or remove some materials, making the proportions reach the most suitable state.

This adjustment took a dozen days.

After a dozen days, the material ratios inside the cauldron reached the amount Luo Chen felt was most suitable.

At this step, he could finally let out a breath.

Inside the Pill-Artifact Hall.

Luo Chen floated in the air, repeatedly striking art seals toward the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Layer after layer of thin qi barriers covered it, sealing it completely.

Afterward, he added several handfuls of third-tier firewood beneath the cauldron, and the furnace fire grew increasingly vigorous.

"The magic treasure embryo is already close to taking shape. It has taken more than a full year. If it were someone else, at this step they would have to begin preparing to engrave formations, but I am different."

With a faint smile, Luo Chen turned and walked out of the Pill-Artifact Hall.

This treasure embryo could just be nourished inside the Primordial Unity Cauldron!

The longer it was nourished, the better.

When the treasure embryo became perfect, that would be the time to engrave formations.

Perhaps it would not be long before he could harvest a body-protecting treasure that suited him to an extreme degree.

Luo Chen had even begun to feel a headache over what this magic treasure should be called.

That feeling was like parents who had just conceived a child, already beginning to flip through dictionaries and ask experts, racking their brains in every possible way to think of a pleasant name for the child.

It truly was a happy trouble!

*(End of Chapter)*
