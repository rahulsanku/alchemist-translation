# Chapter 1097: Morning in the Northern Sea, Evening at Green Parasol Mountain

【Lifespan: 540/2500】
【Spirit Roots: Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, Earth】
【Physique: Fire Spirit Body (Five-Spirit Dao Body — latent)】
【Realm: Early Ascendant 1/100, Archaic Fourth Stage 99/100】
【Laws: Wood — Withering-Bloom 35/100, Fire — Origin 1/100, Water — Origin 1/100, Earth — Origin 1/100】
【Cultivation Arts: Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture — Master 501/1000, Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon — Great Perfection, Soul-Splitting Art — Great Perfection, Hundred Poisons Body-Tempering Art — Great Perfection, Myriad Beast Sutra — Great Perfection, Myriad Path Convergence — Great Perfection】
……

In the quiet cave dwelling, the attribute panel cascaded down like a waterfall, spreading out before Luo Chen.

The sheer number of entries dazzled the eye.

Each entry represented the mental energy and effort Luo Chen had invested in a particular skill or cultivation art over the past five hundred years.

In the past, Luo Chen would have sighed with emotion.

But opening this attribute panel again after so many years, he remained very calm.

He merely treated it as a mirror that could intuitively reflect his actual situation.

Yes, merely a mirror. He was no longer the ignorant youth who would get excited and work even harder upon seeing a progress bar for some cultivation art increase.

That strong positive feedback had long since numbed him.

Not that it was useless, but rather that during these five hundred years of cultivation, Luo Chen had long since integrated qualities like perseverance and hard work into his very bones.

Therefore, even without this mirror, he would still act the same way.

His gaze swept across the entries. Most of them he actively ignored, only keeping the most crucial few in his heart.

First was lifespan, the thing that all those pursuing immortality cared about most.

With one glance, Luo Chen felt a slight relief.

"After advancing to Ascendant, the lifespan limit has indeed increased, nearly doubled."

The upper limit of two thousand five hundred years far surpassed that of a normal Ascendant cultivator, exceeding it by at least five hundred years.

And that extra five hundred years was solely due to his concurrent practice of body refinement.

In fact, if Luo Chen had suffered fewer injuries in battles over the years and had not over-exerted his physical strength, his lifespan limit might have been several hundred years longer.

Not to mention anything else, just that single palm strike of Liancheng's Earth-Overturning divine ability had taken Luo Chen a full thirty years to recuperate from. The damage to Luo Chen's physical foundation, and the indirect impact on his lifespan limit, was far more than thirty years.

"Five hundred and forty years old. Even deducting the fifty years in the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, it's still nearly five hundred years. How many of those who were my peers back then, who walked the same path, are still alive?"

Luo Chen sighed softly, his gaze falling on the spirit root and physique entries.

The essence of his five spirit roots had not been concealed.

The Fire Spirit Body still shone brilliantly!

But that latent Five-Spirit Dao Body entry could no longer be ignored. It flickered with a faint, indistinct light, as if it could be activated at any moment.

And this change originated from his comprehension of the Earth Origin!

A slight smile tugged at the corner of Luo Chen's mouth.

Indeed, his choice had been correct.

The Five-Spirit Dao Body was the ultimate evolution of the five spirit roots, in no way inferior to those famous sacred and divine physiques in history.

In the future, as long as he could comprehend the final Metal-attribute Law True Intent, he could fully activate the Five-Spirit Dao Body. This would not only elevate his cultivation speed to an unimaginable level but also greatly enhance his combat abilities.

Most importantly, after merging the five elements, he would no longer be subject to most attribute restrictions. Instead, he could turn around and restrict the existence of other attributes.

This was an extremely powerful supplement for Luo Chen, who had previously relied solely on fire-attribute techniques in battle.

However, this matter was not as simple as it seemed.

Even though he had mastered the refinement of the Law-Derivation Pill, the corresponding raw materials were extremely rare in this world. It was only thanks to the raw materials left behind by Devil Lord Heaven-Refining in the Pill Realm that he had managed to refine one by chance.

Moreover, there was a very realistic problem starkly laid out before Luo Chen!

That was, laws were difficult to comprehend and even harder to cultivate!

Not to mention the Earth Origin he had just comprehended, even the two origins of Water and Fire that he had comprehended on his own earlier had remained at the entry level after so many years.

This might be related to his previous Sage realm, or it might be related to his focus on cultivating the *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon*.

But the fact that the progress remained completely still showed that daily cultivation was utterly useless for comprehending Law True Intent.

On the other hand, that Withering-Bloom True Intent had unknowingly increased to [35/100].

This was somewhat unexpected!

Staring at these figures, Luo Chen fell into deep thought.

Why were the situations so completely different?

Clearly, he possessed a Fire Spirit Body. He had not primarily cultivated a wood-attribute cultivation art, nor had he deliberately sought to comprehend the Withering-Bloom True Intent. Yet its progress had indeed increased imperceptibly.

Could it be that Law True Intent and Law True Intent were different?

Or perhaps pure origin power and relatively higher-order true intent had different methods of cultivation?

Luo Chen had some vague ideas in his mind, but nothing was certain.

These ideas would need to be exchanged and mutually verified with fellow Daoists on the same path.

He actively shifted his attention, his gaze landing on the realm and cultivation art sections.

The early Ascendant realm was as expected.

Cultivation after Ascendant was not as finely detailed as before.

Because at this realm, and even the realms beyond, it was not easy to quantify.

How much dharma power one had, and the strength of the Law True Intent one grasped, all depended on the individual.

Therefore, predecessors only used the three stages of early, middle, and late to roughly divide the progress.

On the other hand, the progress in body refinement was somewhat gratifying.

[Archaic Fourth Stage 99/100]

Only one step away from breaking through to the fifth stage!

This was a pleasant surprise, both expected and unexpected.

It was expected because Luo Chen had first broken through to the fourth stage in body refinement, with his qi cultivation lagging behind, still at the Adept realm. Now that his qi cultivation had reached Ascendant, it was very reasonable for his body refinement to have gradually reached the peak of the fourth stage.

The unexpected part came from the *Hundred Poisons Body-Tempering Art*.

He had obtained this cultivation art two hundred years ago and had begun preliminary cultivation after refining the Hundred Poisons King Gu.

However, over these two hundred years, Luo Chen's focus had always been on the qi cultivation path. He had not spent much effort cultivating the *Hundred Poisons Body-Tempering Art*.

Yet it had still unknowingly reached the Great Perfection level, pushing his body refinement progress to the peak of the fourth stage.

The secret lay in the Hundred Poisons King Gu!

This gu would consume the most poisonous things in the world and then nourish the host, tempering the physique.

The stronger the toxicity of the food it consumed, the better the nourishing effect.

Over two hundred years, Luo Chen had occasionally drunk diluted Poisonous Spirit Millet wine. By now, all his stock was used up.

Thanks to the vast quantity of Poisonous Spirit Millet, the Hundred Poisons King Gu had, through its day-and-night nourishment, slowly pushed Luo Chen's extremely slow body refinement progress to the limit of the fourth stage.

"This cultivation art is difficult to start, has harsh cultivation conditions, and requires immense willpower to endure pain, but its effects are truly miraculous!"

"As long as one can endure, as long as conditions permit, it can be said that I don't move in body refinement, but the King Gu does it all automatically."

"That trade with Li Tian of the Southern Frontier was a huge gain for me!"

Luo Chen smiled slightly, very satisfied with this unexpected surprise.

With a thought, his internal organs trembled violently.

The next moment, a colorful gu insect landed in the center of his palm.

It was the Hundred Poisons King Gu.

After appearing, the gu insect's mouthparts opened and closed continuously, as if extremely hungry.

Gazing at this gu, Luo Chen's smile gradually faded, replaced by a look of regret.

The peak of the fourth stage in body refinement was the limit.

Not only Luo Chen's limit, but also the limit of this King Gu.

Luo Chen no longer had any poisonous substances that surpassed the Poisonous Spirit Millet, and the Hundred Poisons King Gu was only at the fourth stage, unable to help Luo Chen break through to the Archaic Fifth Stage.

With a sigh, Luo Chen performed a rudimentary gu insect hibernation technique, causing the Hundred Poisons King Gu to enter a dormant state.

Then he took out a jade box and sealed it inside.

This item was useless to him now, but it was still a King Gu with a matching cultivation art.

If Luo Chen also integrated the *Myriad Path Convergence* into it, it could become a foundational sect-protecting inheritance art for the Luo Tian Sect in the future.

If there were those with great perseverance and great fortune, they might also rely on this method to cultivate a powerful physical body not inferior to Luo Chen's.

After doing all this, Luo Chen glanced at the attribute panel again.

The *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture* was lacking, only at the Master level. This was related to the extremely high grade of this cultivation art.

The other cultivation arts were all perfected, including the *Soul-Splitting Art*!

In terms of spells, there was nothing much to say.

However, in terms of divine abilities, Luo Chen was somewhat lacking.

The number of divine abilities he grasped was too few.

He only had the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger.

Although Three Heads Six Arms and Thousand Skies Concurrent Flow also had the power of divine abilities, one was based on physical strength, while the other relied on external objects, namely the Nine Firmaments Qi.

But Luo Chen was not in a hurry.

He was still young, and he had plenty of time to create his own divine abilities.

Not to mention anything else, after fusing the ninth soul, the *Qi Sea Floating Heaven* recorded in its memories was also a scalable spell, with an upper limit reaching the divine ability level.

Luo Chen flicked through the attribute panel, scanning ten lines at a time.

After refining the fifth-stage Soul-Scouring Pill, his alchemy skill was firmly established at the fifth-stage alchemist level. Perhaps he could now be called an "Alchemy Saint."

Apart from alchemy, among the other three most popular skills in the cultivation world — formations, artifacts, and talismans — Luo Chen's attainments were also quite impressive, each having reached the fourth-stage master level.

As for medicine, puppetry, beast taming, gu driving, and so on, Luo Chen had also dabbled in each.

In the leisurely span of five hundred years, even setting aside cultivation, Luo Chen's achievements in various skills were already dazzling enough.

The light of the attribute panel gradually faded. Luo Chen sat cross-legged in the cave dwelling, beginning to plan his next moves.

He had already broken through to Ascendant.

The matter of ascending to the upper realm was now before him.

Luo Chen's plan was to do it as soon as possible!

But given the lifespan of an Ascendant great power, the concept of "as soon as possible" was certainly not the present moment.

At the very least, he still had to return to the Luo Tian Sect once, and there were some mundane matters to deal with.

Moreover, his body refinement progress being stuck at ninety-nine was also a thorn in his side.

To take this step, relying solely on internal cultivation would take who knows how many years of painstaking effort. Therefore, Luo Chen planned to use external force.

With a wave of his hand, a spiritual light flew out with a whir.

Eight flying corners spread out like swallows about to take flight.

It was the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion.

His divine sense entered it, and Luo Chen saw that demon-devourer heart-linking tree he had obtained three hundred years ago but had been cultivating for who knows how many years.

Tai Sui had once said that the fruit borne by the demon-devourer heart-linking tree was a great tonic for body refiners.

But the fruit on it had never been able to ripen and fall.

For this reason, Luo Chen had studied it for a long time but could not find the method. Later, he simply handed it all over to Sang Jinghe.

Before the other party failed to break through to Sage and fell to death, he left Luo Chen a jade slip recording the corresponding research results.

Before going into seclusion, Luo Chen had briefly read a thing or two.

Now, as the information flowed through his heart, Luo Chen showed a thoughtful expression.

"To catalyze this fruit, either follow Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's original cultivation concept, nourishing it with the hearts of great demons and great devils together, drawing on the power of heart blood to condense the heart-linking fruit. Or, adopt Sang Jinghe's experimental inference method, using pure wild beast hearts, supplemented by the cultivator's own essence blood for cultivation, which might also produce a different kind of heart-linking fruit."

"This world has great demons, but no great devils. I wonder if using only the heart blood of great demons can condense the heart-linking fruit?"

"As for desolate beast hearts, they need to be at least at the fifth stage to be used as nourishing consumables."

"Where should I go to find these fifth-stage wild beasts?"

"The Barren Wasteland?"

As his thoughts raced, Luo Chen's expression suddenly shifted.

He waved his hand to put away the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion, and a sound transmission jade token flew out of his storage ring.

[Master, I have discovered the trace of Black Devil!]

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow.

Black Devil — Gu Shaoshang!

Like him and Moon Wanderer, he was one of those who had obtained the inheritance of Devil Lord Heaven-Refining.

Most importantly, Luo Chen had a blood debt with him — the murder of his father!

"Very well, let's make a trip!"

Luo Chen muttered to himself, left the cave dwelling, and stepped onto the teleportation formation heading to the Northern Sea in the twilight.

The teleportation took but an instant.

When he opened his eyes, he had gone from the lush, towering Green Parasol Mountain to the vast, boundless Northern Sea.

There was indeed a certain immortal elegance in roaming heaven and earth, morning at the Northern Sea and evening at Green Parasol Mountain.

Luo Chen looked at the familiar scene before him, glanced in the direction of the Swallow Islands, and then unhesitatingly flew towards the far north.

With a single step, the origin qi of heaven and earth around him gathered, as if he had merged into the void itself.

No flames flickered, only ripples undulated.

When he appeared again, he was a thousand li away.

Such a movement speed was truly astonishing!

And this did not seem to be Luo Chen's full power.

The Voidburst Fire Escape, which had incorporated Azure Frost's spatial talent, now merged with heaven and earth, carrying origin qi, displaying a unique style.

As long as he wanted, as long as his body could withstand it, ten thousand li in a single step was but an instant.

Luo Chen showed a satisfied expression. This escape technique, even without considering its effect on others, was already superior to the *Horizon Neighbor* that Liancheng had once displayed.

And that was a divine ability!

"It seems that my foundation has erupted comprehensively after advancing to Ascendant. Even an ordinary escape technique is already close to the divine ability power of an ordinary Ascendant like Liancheng."

After a sigh of emotion, Luo Chen stepped forward again.

The void origin qi stirred slightly, and a figure shot across the sky like a meteor, sometimes appearing, sometimes disappearing, traversing the vast Northern Sea.

The world did not know that an Ascendant great power was speeding overhead.

But Luo Chen occasionally swept his divine sense across the Northern Sea, taking in most of the scenery below.

What he saw matched the intelligence he had received before.

The Northern Sea nowadays could be called a sea of chaos.

Humans and demons mixed, evil cultivators emerged in droves, far surpassing the situation of internal strife among the few human forces back then.

It seemed that Li Canghai, the only human great power in the Northern Sea, truly did not care about anything!

……

By the time dawn arrived, the morning light was faint.

On a pitch-black sea area, a figure suddenly appeared, standing silently in mid-air.

Soon after, an old man in monk's robes hurried over, his face filled with disbelief.

It had only been half a day since he sent the transmission.

And the master had already arrived?

Was the master originally near the Northern Sea, or...

Kongchan swallowed his saliva and asked cautiously, "Master, have you already..."

Luo Chen nodded slightly. "Yes, I have already become an Ascendant."

Kongchan's heart shook with shock, his mind surging, and his words became somewhat stammering. "Congratulations to master on becoming an Ascendant, may you live as long as heaven."

"There's no need for flattery." Luo Chen waved his hand and asked, "Where are those two people?"

Kongchan subconsciously looked towards the sea ahead.

Luo Chen looked as well. Under the night sky, this sea area still gave him a familiar feeling.

Long-buried memories surged into his heart in an instant.

"The Fallen Sea?"
