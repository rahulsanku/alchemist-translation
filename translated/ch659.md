# Chapter 659: Thousand-Ren Mountains, Nine Yang Golden Eagle

"Come out."

Luo Chen lightly patted the gray pouch at his waist.

In an instant, two streaks of light flashed out and knelt before him.

They were Black King and Heavenjade.

"Master!"

"Master!"

Luo Chen nodded and pointed outside. "Using this hall as the center, explore the land within a thousand li. If you encounter special circumstances, use these talismans to report to me at all times."

Two talismans, wrapped in mana, landed in the hands of the two demons.

They were the Ten-Thousand-Li Transmission Talismans Luo Chen had purchased from Lord Lu Mountain back then.

In the Mountains and Seas Realm, after the ancient human-demon war, some battlefields had even gone deep into the wilderness.

After the war ended, many ancient cultivators died in various places, and their corpses could not be recovered in time. Because their abilities differed, some powerful figures left inheritances before death, transforming into secret realms and cave residences.

Even after human cultivators established the Five Continents, they did not necessarily recover all these secret realms and cave residences.

This was why major sects today occasionally discovered ancient cultivator secret realms.

Within these secret realms, because of the methods ancient cultivators used before death and changes over time, the secret realms produced all kinds of mutations.

Some involved spiritual qi mutation. Some involved extreme evolution of living things inside. In severe cases, there were even differences in the speed of time, making even high-realm cultivators unwilling to enter lightly and forcing them to send low-rank disciples to explore.

Regarding this Demon-Fall Land, Luo Chen had already made guesses before.

The Sunken Sea guarding the secret realm outside suppressed cultivators' divine sense. The inner secret realm would probably be even more so.

Looking at it now, it was exactly as expected.

Luo Chen had earlier tried releasing his divine sense. It should have covered seven or eight hundred li, but in this illusion realm, the limit was less than several dozen li.

Several dozen li sounded like a very long distance, but for immortal cultivators with clear ears and sharp eyes, it was not as far as what the eyes could see.

Yet often, what the eyes saw could deceive.

Under such circumstances, the danger brought by the unknown became the greatest.

The Ten-Thousand-Li Transmission Talismans would be of great use now.

After accepting the talismans, Black King and Heavenjade looked at each other, then each chose a direction and flew away.

Luo Chen stood at the stone hall door and watched them leave. His gaze fell more on those peaks that looked like sharp swords.

But after looking too long, his eyes actually began to sting.

At this moment, the wooden plaque on his chest trembled slightly.

"Luo Chen, have you noticed that the heaven and earth spiritual qi here seems somewhat different?"

Han Zhan's divine sense transmission entered his ear.

Luo Chen was startled.

He tried circulating the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture and began capturing the wandering heaven and earth spiritual qi outside, but as soon as the method started circulating, he froze.

"So little!"

"Right? This old man also felt that the wood-attribute spiritual qi here is very scarce."

Han Zhan mainly cultivated wood-attribute methods and absorbed external spiritual qi primarily as wood-attribute spiritual qi.

Luo Chen's was fire-attribute.

For both of them to say it was little showed how scarce the external wood and fire attributes were. And clearly, these two were not the only ones lacking.

In Luo Chen's perception, apart from wood and fire, water and earth attributes were also extremely scarce.

The most abundant was metal-attribute spiritual qi.

After detecting this, Luo Chen's expression changed slightly.

This secret realm seemed to have developed the most common phenomenon recorded in those secret-realm exploration manuals: spiritual qi mutation.

Later generations did not know the principle behind this mutation. They only guessed it probably had something to do with the Dao cultivated by the owner during life.

Whichever attribute they leaned toward, after death, the spiritual qi of the place they left behind would gather that attribute in large amounts.

In this way, the secret realm environment could be preserved very well.

But for later explorers, it was extremely dangerous.

Because this meant that if a cultivator did not cultivate the same attribute as the secret realm's owner, their mana would decrease with every bit used.

Conversely, those who cultivated the same attribute would be like fish in water, growing braver as they fought.

Not only that, Luo Chen had an even greater hidden worry.

Looking at the boundless space ahead, he quietly asked Han Zhan a question.

"Are we truly still in the Sunken Sea, still inside the Mountains and Seas Realm?"

Han Zhan was silent for a long while before finally answering, "Perhaps we are still in the Mountains and Seas Realm, but I fear we are no longer in the Sunken Sea."

Receiving the answer to his guess, Luo Chen could not help inhaling sharply.

"What kind of divine ability did this place's owner possess to create such a spiritual land before death?"

"Have you forgotten Ding Yi's statement about how the Primordial Demon Sect evaluated the ruins' owner?"

"The strongest Void Refining True Lord from ancient times to now? But can a Void Refining existence truly achieve this?"

"Whether an existence of that realm can achieve it, this old man does not know. But I know that the basic principles of all things are roughly similar. If you regard this Demon-Fall Land as a giant storage bag, it can explain a thing or two."

A thing or two, and only a thing or two.

What kind of storage bag could contain a vast, boundless world and be full of life?

Luo Chen thought of many things, such as spatial restrictions, mustard seeds containing Mount Sumeru, and even spatial cracks, yet felt all of them were far from enough.

Because these things were more than sufficient for small objects, but in such a vast environment, they absolutely could not achieve such stability.

Suddenly, Luo Chen remembered a rumor about this place's owner.

His formation Dao attainments were miraculous.

Li Canghai, the Northern Sea's only human Ascendant great power today, had obtained an opportunity in this Demon-Fall Land and thereby advanced into a formation Dao great power.

That had been only a little fortune, yet it created an Ascendant great power.

It showed how powerful the original owner's formations were.

And formations were also one of the few Daos capable of encompassing many methods and transforming many attributes for one's own use.

Perhaps formations were the core of this space's construction.

Thinking of this, Luo Chen looked at the stone hall behind him.

Spiritual light bloomed in his eyes as he carefully observed the internal and external formation pattern circulation, increasingly confirming his guess.

This seemingly ordinary stone hall combined four great formations: spirit absorption, spirit gathering, spirit dispersal, and spirit transformation. They fused together yet did not conflict.

At least with Luo Chen's current formation attainments, he could not do this.

And this was only the most basic formation structure.

If there were several hundred similar stone halls in this space, perhaps the internal spiritual qi could be guaranteed endless, no different from the outside world.

While Luo Chen pondered and observed, the transmission talisman on his body moved.

It was from Heavenjade.

After Luo Chen listened, surprise appeared on his face.

But in the next instant, he buried that surprise deeply, because Ding Yi and the others had come out of the hall.

"Recovered?"

Cave Master Moyun nodded. "More or less. Fortunately, this old man had expected this and prepared many external objects."

Luo Chen observed him. The other's mana was steady and had recovered to peak condition, showing his words were true.

But the other person was not quite the same.

"Fellow Daoist Ding, you..."

Ding Yi waved his hand. "No matter. Only some mana consumption. It will not affect the following action."

Cave Master Moyun asked curiously, "Then where do we go next?"

This was also what Luo Chen wanted to ask.

He also had no clue about the spirit medicine garden's location.

Ding Yi took a deep breath and extended a finger, pointing into the distance.

"In that direction."

Luo Chen and Cave Master Moyun looked at each other, then spoke together.

"Then next, we ask Fellow Daoist Ding to lead the way."

Ding Yi's expression did not change. He opened his mouth and spat, and a gourd appeared.

His body moved, leaping onto the yellow-skinned gourd.

Cave Master Moyun did not visibly move, but the cloud boots under his feet generated wind, slowly lifting him.

This was the posture of traveling at full speed.

Luo Chen moved his shoulders and waist, and a pair of fiery red wings appeared behind him.

Cave Master Moyun glanced at them. "A fine magic treasure, and especially well nurtured. I fear it is already close to middle-grade."

Luo Chen smiled faintly and said nothing. After the three set out, he fell slightly behind.

His gaze toward Ding Yi also carried a little gloom.

His mana had not recovered, yet he was in such a hurry to set out. If he had no ghost in his heart, that would be strange.

In the sky, three streaks of escape light flew rapidly.

Very soon, they approached the first tall mountain.

As the distance grew closer, Luo Chen's expression became increasingly solemn.

"It should be here."

Just as he murmured inwardly, there seemed to be movement on the mountain.

The next moment, a patch of golden light flew out from the mountain. The buzzing sound made people irritated just hearing it.

The three instantly stopped.

Seeing that golden light draw closer and reveal obvious hostility, the three treated it like a great enemy.

As the distance shortened, Luo Chen and the others saw the specific appearance of the attacking creatures.

They were the size of watermelons.

Like cicadas, yet also like mosquitoes.

Their wings were thin as golden gauze, and their faces had long, sharp mouthparts.

"What are these things?" Cave Master Moyun's expression changed slightly.

"First-tier. They should not be much of a threat, but their numbers are somewhat high," Luo Chen said.

Ding Yi did not turn his head. He slapped the gourd beneath him, and a great cloud of golden sand flew out.

"Only some Golden Melon Mosquitoes. No need to be nervous. I will handle this one."

As he spoke, the golden sand filling the sky had already flown out, covering the dense Golden Melon Mosquitoes.

Golden sand and golden mosquitoes collided. Before the sharp mouthparts could show their power, they were submerged in the endless sea of sand.

Luo Chen and Cave Master Moyun watched this scene and did not act.

After several breaths, a click was heard.

Ding Yi capped the gourd stopper and revealed satisfaction.

"Continue forward."

Hesitation appeared on Cave Master Moyun's face. He held a tiny mosquito in his hand and seemed somewhat puzzled.

He looked again at Ding Yi's gourd, which seemed to have even brighter spiritual light.

Perhaps sensing Cave Master Moyun's gaze, Ding Yi explained, "These Golden Melon Mosquitoes are formed from five-element metal qi. If refined in large numbers, they can enhance the might of metal-attribute magic treasures."

Was that why you acted first?

Cave Master Moyun was half-believing and half-doubtful.

When they flew below the second tall mountain and a large swarm of ants surged over, Cave Master Moyun spoke first.

"This time, I will do it."

Luo Chen and Ding Yi did not refuse.

Then, under their gazes, they saw Cave Master Moyun flick his hand and release a large net, covering more than ten thousand ants completely.

The mesh of the net was clearly very large, but after these ants entered, they could not break free.

Cave Master Moyun urged his mana, and the ants inside immediately died soundlessly.

In the end, a large handful of black ash remained within the net.

Cave Master Moyun beckoned, and the black ash came to his side. Sweeping it with divine sense, the old man's face immediately revealed surprise.

"It is actually Grinding Iron Ash!"

Hearing this, Luo Chen could not help showing astonishment.

Grinding Iron Ash was a first-tier mineral material in the cultivation world. Although its grade was low, its price was high, mainly because its output was rare.

If this thing was added to artifacts or magic treasures, it could greatly improve weapon toughness, in other words, improve durability.

If his Profound Fire Sword back then had been mixed with large amounts of Grinding Iron Ash, it would not have shattered into pieces in battle.

Unexpectedly, after these ants died, they produced so much Grinding Iron Ash.

Cave Master Moyun stopped, looking at the tall mountain the ants had crawled out from, somewhat tempted.

Seeing this, Ding Yi could not help snorting coldly.

"Cave Master, every mountain in these Thousand-Ren Mountains contains a rare mineral deposit. Now it is only a first-tier mineral deposit and you already feel greed and stop moving. Do you still want the artifact-forging inheritance later?"

Cave Master Moyun's motivation for coming to explore the ruins together was that rumor said this place's owner possessed not only formation Dao ability, but also considerable artifact-forging skill.

Hearing this now, he could not help struggling inwardly.

Luo Chen, however, was somewhat surprised.

"Every tall mountain here contains a corresponding mineral deposit?"

Ding Yi nodded. Seeing Cave Master Moyun become even more tempted, he frowned.

"There are many mineral deposits, but every mountain has corresponding strange beasts guarding it. The higher the grade, the stronger the strange beasts. Later, there are even fourth-tier strange beasts. Are you truly prepared to face fourth-tier strange beasts?"

As soon as these words came out, the two fell silent.

Ding Yi sneered and stopped advising, once again activating his magic treasure and flying forward.

Luo Chen and Cave Master Moyun looked at each other. They could only suppress the urge to explore the mountains and follow him.

However, when they later encountered attacks from strange beasts, the two of them acted more and more frequently.

Every time they killed a type of strange beast, they could more or less harvest fine mineral deposits from them.

From first tier, to second tier, then to third tier.

Ding Yi did not compete with them for monsters. Instead, he was happy to see it and save his mana.

Time slowly passed.

Five days later, three figures fled frantically, their breathing somewhat hurried.

Beneath them were several hundred enormous Black Gold Vipers, chasing like a tide.

Ding Yi's expression was ugly. "Damn it. I told you not to provoke these vipers. Now look. They cannot be killed, yet they hold grudges and have forced us away from the closest route to the spirit medicine garden."

Cave Master Moyun's face showed shame.

He had not held back because these vipers contained extremely rare Kui Steel.

He had originally intended to kill only one, but had not expected it to mobilize the whole force.

Cave Master Moyun discovered that Luo Chen's figure had fallen far behind.

Seeing he was about to fall into the viper encirclement, Cave Master Moyun shouted.

"Devil Lord, careful!"

Luo Chen's face was pale, and he subconsciously looked back.

A bloody maw instantly opened and swallowed him.

This scene immediately stunned Cave Master Moyun.

Even Ding Yi had not expected this.

Devil Lord Azure Yang was actually this incapable?

Just as he was uncertain, that black viper that had swallowed Luo Chen had already submerged into the snake swarm.

Shriek!

High in the sky, a sharp bird cry rang out.

Ding Yi turned back and saw a handsome azure bird swooping toward them, and behind it, a golden divine eagle seemingly bathed in the sun stirred wind and clouds as it came.

Two birds, one before and one behind, headed straight for Ding Yi and Cave Master Moyun.

Seeing this, Ding Yi's face turned pale, despair appearing in his eyes.

Though despairing, he did not panic. Opening his mouth, he spat out a small sword that broke through the air toward the azure bird flying in front.

The azure bird's body swayed repeatedly, its speed extremely fast. With a rather familiar movement technique, it narrowly avoided the slash.

Although it avoided the attack, it had already fallen into the viper swarm below.

Then it disappeared instantly.

"Hm?" Ding Yi was startled.

At this moment, the viper swarm slowly retreated like a tide.

The golden divine eagle that had lost its pursuit target turned its furious gaze onto Ding Yi and Cave Master Moyun.

Gulp.

The sound of swallowed saliva came from the throats of the two Golden Core cultivators.

Cave Master Moyun said hoarsely, "A fourth-tier strange beast?"

Ding Yi vaguely sensed something was wrong, but with a great enemy before him, he had no room to think further.

"This is a Nine Yang Golden Eagle. It lives by absorbing solar fire qi and devouring geng-metal mineral deposits. It is fourth-tier, its attacks fierce and domineering, its offense incomparably sharp, and its speed especially fast!"

"Cave Master, do not hold back. In this battle, we must cross the difficulty together!"

Cave Master Moyun nodded solemnly.

Behind them, among the slowly retreating third-tier viper swarm, an enormous black python left the group and coiled around an iron tree, its cold, emotionless eyes staring at them from afar.

*(End of Chapter)*
