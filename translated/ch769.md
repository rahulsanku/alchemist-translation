# Chapter 769: Formation Book and Sword Canon, Returning to the Eastern Wilderness

"Fellow Daoist Fu, you hid things from me so bitterly."

The faint sentence seemed like accusation, complaint, and emotion all mixed together.

The other party had once said that the competition would at most continue for a year or half a year.

In truth, that was indeed so. Calculated according to the time of the Mountains and Seas Realm, only half a year had truly passed.

But for Luo Chen inside Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, a full fifty years had passed!

The lifespan that had slipped away was also a very real fifty years!

Fu Chaosheng wore an ashamed expression and bent at the waist, cupping his hands toward Luo Chen.

"My apologies. What Fu did this time was indeed improper."

Luo Chen slightly turned aside and actually did not accept this salute.

He paced slightly inside the residence. "You really need not do this, Elder. Now Qinglan has entered Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak, and in the future, she even has hopes of becoming an Ascendant. How could I accept such a great courtesy?"

Fu Chaosheng's expression turned somewhat awkward.

In this cooperation, he had failed to inform Luo Chen beforehand, while Luo Chen had still exerted himself fully. Comparing the two, the difference was obvious.

It was said that a gentleman could be deceived by using what was proper, but the present circumstances were somewhat different.

Luo Chen's performance in Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven had exceeded everyone's expectations.

Many hated him, but even more favored him!

Within the Tianyuan Dao Sect now, who knew how many families were waiting to recruit Luo Chen?

If Luo Chen truly leaned toward another family, then the Fu family would become a great joke.

However!

Compared to the other families, the Fu family also had an advantage.

That was that Fu Chaosheng understood Luo Chen's needs better!

Swish! Swish! Swish!

Three items appeared before Luo Chen.

A book, a slip, and a gourd.

"These are?" Luo Chen narrowed his eyes and looked at the three things.

Fu Chaosheng introduced them one by one.

"This book is called Immortal Secrets of the Stone Chamber. It was written by an elder of our sect from two thousand years ago. Within it are recorded more than a hundred formations, great and small. Their grades range from first tier to fourth tier, all fully included, and it even records an incomplete fifth-tier defensive formation. That predecessor had some connection with my Fu family in his youth, and before passing away, he engraved a copy and gifted it to my Fu family."

"This jade slip records a sword canon called Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon. Although it was not created by an expert of our sect, it was personally written by an Ascendant great power in ancient times, and it is not limited by the distinction between sword pellets and flying swords. If one can bring this sword canon to great success, it is enough to range unhindered among those of the same level. My granddaughter also cultivates this sword canon, but her killing nature is insufficient, so she can only reach minor success and cannot be said to sweep invincibly."

"As for this gourd, you should be very familiar with it. It contains the soul essence of tens of millions of people. However, I have left the refining method for soul essence on it, and it is the orthodox refining method of the Primordial Demon Sect!"

These words came out slowly and clearly.

After they fell, the residence was utterly silent.

Luo Chen finally turned around and looked seriously at Fu Chaosheng.

"Our cooperation has already ended. Although Luo suffered a hidden loss, the compensation you gave earlier was already entirely sufficient. There is no need for these things."

Yes, Luo Chen had indeed been angered earlier that the other party had not informed him beforehand of the time-flow difference.

But through his own efforts, he had used only fifty years to leave Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, and his loss was not large.

Especially after coming out, Fu Chaosheng had not burned the bridge after crossing the river and had still spoken for him.

At this point, his anger could already be considered gone.

His earlier posture was only to see the other party's true attitude.

If Fu Chaosheng had flicked his sleeves and left, then the friendship between the two sides would have ended there, and in the future Luo Chen would have no further contact with him.

If he continued apologizing, Luo Chen would also not persist, and would simply let the matter pass magnanimously.

But he had never expected the other party to offer such a great gift!

Fu Chaosheng smiled faintly. "These are not compensation, nor are they repayment. They are merely gifts from this old man inviting you to become an offering elder of my Fu family!"

The Fu family's offering elder?

Luo Chen raised a brow.

"Although Qinglan has comprehended the true meaning of the laws and entered Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak, the road to the Ascendant realm remains long. As for me, I have little lifespan left. A hundred years from now, I will be nothing more than a handful of yellow earth. At that time, such a large Fu family will have no other Sage to support it, which would be rather shabby."

Fu Chaosheng's face was ruddy, and he said warmly, "If Fellow Daoist is willing to become my Fu family's offering elder, the situation will presumably be very different. I hope Fellow Daoist will accept!"

Luo Chen looked thoughtful. The other party was making arrangements for what would come after his death.

That was why he had paid such a heavy gift.

His gaze swept over the three gifts. Aside from the gourd containing soul essence, on the other two gifts, he seemed to see Fu Qinglan's shadow.

A formation book, a sword canon.

The formation dao and the sword dao.

Among these two abilities, only Fu Qinglan, who had accompanied him for fifty years, knew his strengths and his shortcomings.

He was skilled in formations, but he did not have any formidable grand formation he could truly show. The defensive grand formations he had arranged inside the grotto-heaven could be broken by Qi Xiongcheng with one palm.

He possessed the flying sword Yuantu, comparable to a spirit treasure, but his use of it was very crude. He still relied on force to suppress others, and in terms of technique, he depended entirely on his own research. If he could study an orthodox sword dao classic, he would certainly be able to bring out the Yuantu sword's power to the greatest extent.

Luo Chen pursed his lips and accepted the three gifts.

"The two of you were thoughtful!"

The words "the two of you" showed that Luo Chen understood the origin of these gifts.

Fu Chaosheng laughed happily.

"Fellow Daoist Luo, you worked hard in the grotto-heaven. Rest well. This old man will not disturb you further."

"I will see you out."

Both men wore smiles as they left the cave residence.

Luo Chen sent him a very long way before returning alone.

Fu Chaosheng, meanwhile, flew neither quickly nor slowly, his face full of smiles. Presumably, this picture of cheerful conversation would be seen by many people!

At that time, no one would think of digging at his Fu family's wall again.

The only pity was that Qinglan had rejected the proposal to become dual-cultivation Dao companions with Luo Chen. Otherwise, they could have bound the other party even more deeply.

"But when I put forward that suggestion, that girl Qinglan was clearly somewhat moved. How did she refuse in the end?"

Fu Chaosheng was somewhat puzzled.

He could see that there was some affection for Luo Chen in Fu Qinglan's eyes.

Especially that public phrase, "Big Brother Luo." There was not the slightest falseness to it.

But after entering Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak, her attitude had undergone a great change.

...

Inside the cave residence.

Luo Chen took out the three items again and examined them carefully.

He especially lingered for a long time on the soul essence refining method engraved on the gourd.

"So this is how it is refined. This is rather too vicious and greatly harms the harmony of heaven."

After memorizing the refining method, Luo Chen sighed in this way.

He also understood why the Primordial Demon Sect did not refine soul essence itself, but instead distributed this task to other Northern Sea cultivators.

If soul essence were truly refined on such a great scale, not to mention Northern Sea cultivators growing resentful, even the sacred lands of the other four continents would likely not tolerate the Primordial Demon Sect.

Now that Fu Chaosheng had taught him to fish, he would have to use this method cautiously.

Putting away the three treasures, Luo Chen exhaled a turbid breath and continued what he had not finished earlier.

With a thought, the attribute panel hung before his eyes like a waterfall.

[Lifespan: 280/1500]

[Spirit Roots: Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, Earth]

[Constitution: Fire Spirit Body (Five-Spirit Dao Body hidden)]

[Realm: Third-Level Sage 79/100, Archaic Fourth Tier 40/100]

[Law: True Meaning of Withering-Bloom, Wood 1/100]

[Cultivation Methods: Withering-Bloom Dao Scripture proficient 135/200, Myriad Path Convergence great completion, Myriad Beast Sutra grandmaster 510/1000, Soul-Splitting Art mastery 201/300]

[Spells: Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain grandmaster 620/1000, Flame Dragon Descends grandmaster 501/1000, Withering-Bloom Dao Finger (divine ability)]

[Body Arts: Voidburst Step great completion, Ninety Thousand Li great completion, Devil Lord's Seven Scattered Hands grandmaster 875/1000]

[Soul Arts: Mirror Flower Water Moon great completion, Spirit-Intimidating Art great completion, Spirit-Bewildering Art great completion, Ghosts and Gods Give Way proficient 110/200, Aperture-Thatching Art perfect 385/500]

With only one glance, Luo Chen once again looked toward the new entry.

The wood-attribute true meaning of the laws, Withering-Bloom, was plainly displayed there, proving that Luo Chen had absolutely not been seeing things.

It was also precisely the appearance of this new entry that made the Five-Spirit Dao Body, hidden for so long, flicker with faint light.

And there was not only this one new entry.

In the spells column, there was now a [Withering-Bloom Dao Finger].

This was exactly the new art Luo Chen had created himself.

But beyond expectation, this art did not display a proficiency progress bar at all!

In Luo Chen's nearly three-hundred-year cultivation journey, this was still the first time.

Moreover, after the entry, there were the two words "divine ability."

"Could it be that the new art I comprehended is not an ordinary spell, but a divine ability art recorded in the classics?"

Luo Chen was somewhat hesitant.

If that was truly the case, then the lack of a progress bar could be understood.

Such divine abilities were never strengthened by mechanical, practiced use.

If one wanted to make them stronger, only by strengthening the core of the art, which was this piece of true meaning of the laws, could the art grow stronger with it.

But Luo Chen glanced at the progress bar of the true meaning of Withering-Bloom. It was only at entry level, and he immediately dismissed the idea of increasing the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger's power.

Too difficult!

Comprehending this true meaning had all been done by borrowing the power of the Withering-Bloom fire spirit.

As for how to cultivate the true meaning of the laws, he was even more lost in fog and had nowhere to begin.

In the end, he still had no sect inheritance!

If he could also enter Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak to cultivate and receive guidance from Ascendant great powers, he would not be so helpless.

With a sigh, Luo Chen returned his attention to the beginning of the panel.

His lifespan was indeed the same as it had been inside the grotto-heaven.

It was 280 years old, not 230 years old, with only half a year having passed outside.

This also meant that those fifty years inside Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven were real, not illusory.

Then Luo Chen's gaze fell on the realm column.

Compared to the true meaning of the laws, where he had nowhere to begin, the visible and tangible realm was still more reliable.

However, no matter how he looked at this third-level Sage realm, even close to fourth level, it felt unstable!

"My foundation has become unstable!"

As Luo Chen looked at the panel and sensed the large amount of insubstantial magic power inside his Purple Mansion, he came to this conclusion.

Even his Sage body, though it had grown somewhat, was not solid.

The cause of all this was the hasty absorption of a large amount of origin qi of heaven and earth.

In the past, every measure of Luo Chen's magic power had come through repeated tempering. One measure of his magic power could equal five or seven measures from cultivators of the same level.

That was why his spells could be so tyrannical that others could not match him.

But now, this insubstantial magic power had become a hidden danger.

"The focus of my next cultivation needs to change. Restart the Withering-Bloom Divine Seal, press down my realm, and temper my magic power at the same time!"

With a thought, Luo Chen put away the panel, and the small Sage figure inside the Purple Mansion began forming spiritual arts.

Restriction after restriction appeared, hovering outside.

As the small figure's fingers fastened, all the restrictions landed upon the Sage's body surface, then gradually disappeared within.

When the Withering-Bloom Divine Seal appeared again, Luo Chen's Sage also became wrinkled once more. Only this time, under his deliberate control, his outer appearance did not change.

And that vast untempered magic power, under Luo Chen's control, separated from the Sage and flew toward a small azure tree close at hand.

The small tree was no more than three inches tall, but its branches, leaves, and veins were lifelike.

It was precisely the Withering-Bloom True Flame!

Unlike the illusory fire tree of the past, this small tree was now incomparably solid, its whole form composed of vast origin qi and the fire spirit.

And its realm was also a genuine fifth tier!

Only, at this moment, the Withering-Bloom fire spirit was also restricted by the Withering-Bloom Divine Seal, its consciousness sealed away.

This was not Luo Chen's intention, but the Withering-Bloom fire spirit's request.

On Heaven-Earth Peak, there were existences that could threaten it!

Therefore, the Withering-Bloom fire spirit had asked Luo Chen to suppress it until they left Heaven-Earth Peak.

"Although I cannot use you to fight right now, borrowing you for cultivation is no problem."

Luo Chen smiled faintly and drove all the vast, impetuous magic power near the small azure tree.

The fire spirit seemed to sense it. As its branches and leaves unfurled, they drew in current after current of magic power.

And as it breathed in and out, it exhaled strands of even purer magic power, which Luo Chen's Sage absorbed.

Seeing this scene, Luo Chen's heart filled with delight.

He had not yet had a chance to test the combat power of the fifth-tier Withering-Bloom True Flame.

But in his cultivation, the other party could already provide him enormous assistance.

The magic power breathed in and out by the fire tree would become even purer, and the fire spirit would not erase Luo Chen's soul imprint on that magic power.

Not only that, Luo Chen could even feel that if he absorbed spiritual qi from the outside world, the fire tree could also help him purify and temper it.

These functions had not existed before.

Even when Luo Chen had occasionally used the Withering-Bloom True Flame in the past to temper magic power and magic treasures, not only did it consume time and effort, it also caused great loss. How could it be like now, when he did not need to worry at all, and the fire spirit could help him complete this step while controlling its own power, without diminishing Luo Chen's magic power?

In this way, Luo Chen could save a great deal of time.

And he would not waste this time for nothing.

The Immortal Secrets of the Stone Chamber and Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon sent by Fu Chaosheng became the targets of his study.

...

While Luo Chen tempered his magic power and practiced formations and the sword dao, the competition inside Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven continued.

Because of Elder Seven Lamps and Fu Chaosheng's previous actions, whether the cultivators of Heaven-Earth Peak or the great families, no one came to seek Luo Chen again.

Everyone knew the Heavenly Secret Pill was gone.

Luo Chen was also thoroughly bound to the Fu family.

But it was not that absolutely no one came to look for Luo Chen.

For example, one month later, Luo Chen received a special guest. It was Sage Heavenly Mountain, with whom he had once fought.

The other party had protected Yu Xu inside Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven for several decades, but ultimately failed.

The fault did not lie with Heavenly Mountain as Dao protector. It was simply that Yu Xu did not have that fortune. The black qi within her illusion chess piece filled up, forcing her to withdraw.

Later, Luo Chen found time to ask Fu Chaosheng. The other party said it was likely Yu Xu had been too eager for quick success, and when she absorbed the spiritual resonance inside the illusion cores, she also absorbed great amounts of mirage dragon qi, which caused her to be forced out of the grotto-heaven even before time had expired.

In short, Sage Heavenly Mountain had neither merit nor fault, ending this Dao-protecting journey.

After coming out, he visited Luo Chen.

There was no great enmity between the two, and in the future, they would also both cultivate on Heaven-Earth Peak. They would see each other often, so establishing good relations was correct.

This meeting lasted half a day.

From the other party, Luo Chen learned many anecdotes of the Southern Frontier, and he also told the other party some of what he had seen and heard in the Northern Sea.

With that, the meeting ended.

Yu Xu's elimination opened the prelude to the end of the competition.

Before long, Lin Bufan also activated the illusion chess piece and returned to the Mountains and Seas Realm.

What disappointed the Lin clan head was that he had not comprehended law power.

It had nothing to do with aptitude, nor with others.

It was only that when using illusion cores to cultivate, spiritual resonance leaked outward and drew the attacks of illusion beasts. Without Lin Jingtang protecting him at the side, to preserve his life, he was forced to activate the illusion chess piece and leave Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven.

After Lin Bufan came Gu Daosu.

To everyone's surprise, although likewise without anyone protecting him, Gu Daosu succeeded!

Regarding this, Fu Chaosheng's explanation to Luo Chen was that the other party had a Rotten Axe-Handle Black Chess Piece bestowed by Chess Saint Ke Lianshan, and that he had already been infinitely close to success himself, so after his second sudden enlightenment, he was able to comprehend origin power again.

In this way, there were only four true disciples left inside the grotto-heaven.

And the time remaining was only one month.

Inside the grotto-heaven, that was less than ten years.

Could Shi Ju and Gu Yuan, as well as Dragon Abyss and Mingyan, succeed?

Luo Chen no longer cared about any of this. He went to find Elder Seven Lamps.

After Luo Chen explained his purpose, Elder Seven Lamps was somewhat surprised.

"The Northern Sea war will begin as soon as the competition ends. You want to go to the Eastern Wilderness at this time?"

*(End of Chapter)*
