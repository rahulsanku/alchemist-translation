# Chapter 918: All Parties Converge, Divine Art Slays Enemies

The matter of the Luo Tian Sect holding a Core Formation Grand Ceremony for Qu Lingjun was not only circulating within the Luo Tian Domain.

This news had been received one after another by many forces that kept a close watch on the Luo Tian Sect's movements.

After all, Luo Chen had deliberately extended the time by half a year in order to recall the Luo Tian Sect cultivators scattered in various places.

Half a year, given the communication speed of high-ranking cultivators in the immortal cultivation world, was enough to inquire about the relevant situation.

In the Misty Void Domain, adjacent to the Luo Tian Domain, Gu Hong was whispering in a low voice with someone.

"Senior Brother Yonghua, is our sect truly gone?"

Lin Yonghua's expression was desolate, even tinged with despair.

"It is indeed gone. I personally went to the shore of the Nether Abyss, and what met my eyes was a scene of thunder and fire interweaving, with Netherwater churning violently."

"None of the senior brothers from Upper Nether Immortal City survived."

Gu Hong swallowed his saliva. "What about the East Sun Sect? Didn't you go ask about the situation there?"

Lin Yonghua shook his head. "How would I dare? Don't you know that the Tianyuan Dao Sect has already begun to set foot in the Eastern Wilderness? The East Sun Sect was the first to submit and surrender to them."

"How dare they!"

Gu Hong cursed loudly, stood up, and paced back and forth in the secret chamber.

"Think back, how many benefits did the East Sun Sect receive from our Nether Origin Sect over the years? Every year, they had disciple quotas to enter our Nether Origin Sect for exchange and study. Even the Grand Elder of the East Sun Sect was guided by Ancestor Black Marsh to break through to the late Sage stage. And now, they surrender without a fight and welcome the enemy Tianyuan Dao Sect into the Eastern Wilderness!"

Lin Yonghua's expression was dazed as he murmured, "When the tree falls, the monkeys scatter. It's a common occurrence."

"A common occurrence?"

Gu Hong suddenly turned his head, his face full of anger.

"If everyone is like this, then our Nether Origin Sect is truly finished."

Lin Yonghua looked up blankly, gazing at his hot-tempered junior brother.

"What do you want to do?"

"Do?" Gu Hong retorted. "Don't you want to rebuild the Nether Origin Sect and restore the glory of the Holy Land?"

Lin Yonghua pointed at him. "Rely on you?"

Then he pointed at himself. "Rely on me?"

Afterwards, he shook his head. "We are merely early-stage Sages. What ability do we have to recreate the grand spectacle of the Nether Origin Holy Land, where a hundred sects came to pay homage!"

The anger on Gu Hong's face was not dispelled by his self-deprecating words; instead, it grew even more intense, spittle flying as he spoke.

"Our Nether Origin Sect has a vast foundation. Even if the Holy Land is gone and Upper Nether City is destroyed, are there no living people left?"

"You are one, I am one, and there are Nether Origin disciples scattered everywhere managing the sect's mundane affairs."

"Not only that, but there are countless Sage-level sects in the Eastern Wilderness that have received benefits from our sect. As long as we raise a banner, people will come to pledge allegiance!"

Lin Yonghua listened to these words, and the dazed look on his face gradually disappeared.

But in the end, he still seemed somewhat disheartened.

"They are all mediocre people!"

Yes, in his view, all the people and forces his junior brother Gu Hong mentioned were merely mediocre.

If they were truly talented, how could they have been sent out by the sect to handle mundane affairs, wasting time that could be spent on cultivation?

It wasn't like everyone was like Senior Brother Ye Lingtian, who could directly preside over important matters like the great war between the two races as soon as he left the sect gate.

Relying on such people to accomplish anything was nothing but wishful thinking.

Gu Hong said angrily, "Senior Brother, how can you be so self-defeating? Are you going to learn from the East Sun Sect and establish your own mountain gate?"

Lin Yonghua was somewhat tempted.

But before he could express that thought, Gu Hong mentioned a name.

"Chongming!"

"Don't forget, Junior Brother Chongming is still alive!"

"He is the personal disciple of Ancestor Black Marsh, and he received the grace of the Alchemy Saint in the Central Continent. Now he has comprehended the true meaning of the laws and is traveling everywhere. One day, he may achieve the Ascendant realm and rebuild the Nether Abyss!"

Lin Yonghua's expression shook as he thought of that junior brother.

His talent was indeed extraordinary.

Lin Yonghua's lips trembled as he said, "But where is he now? We don't even know."

Seeing his senior brother relent, Gu Hong's anger subsided somewhat.

He said calmly, "That's why we need to raise a banner, to gather the old forces, to let the world know that our Nether Origin Sect has not completely fallen, and at the same time, to attract Junior Brother Chongming's attention."

Lin Yonghua looked up and asked, "What do you intend to do?"

The corners of Gu Hong's mouth curled into a ferocious smile.

"Senior Brother hasn't been home lately, so you might not know that our neighbor is about to hold a Core Formation Grand Ceremony for a disciple of the Alchemy Sect."

"The Holy Land has been destroyed, the Alchemy Sect has fallen. The whole world should be in mourning, yet they do not think of remembering the departed. Instead, they want to put on this kind of celebratory farce."

"Tell me, isn't that worthy of death?"

Lin Yonghua frowned. "You want to kill the chicken to scare the monkeys, deal with the Luo Tian Sect, and use that to re-establish the Nether Origin banner?"

"Exactly!" Gu Hong nodded without hesitation, and changed the subject. "Back then, the Ancestor ordered us to leave the Nether Origin Holy Land and come here to establish a family. The purpose was not to spread our branches, but to keep an eye on the Luo Tian Sect. Once the Alchemy Sect made any unusual moves at the Holy Land, we were to immediately take down the Luo Tian Sect to restrain the Alchemy Sect."

"Now that the Alchemy Sect has been buried along with the Nether Origin Sect, in my opinion, there is no need for this Luo Tian Sect to exist any longer!"

Lin Yonghua nodded. "Indeed, there is no need for it to exist."

The two senior and junior brothers looked at each other and finally reached an understanding.

From beginning to end, they had never cared about the Luo Tian Sect's reaction, nor had they considered whether they could take it down.

Even though the strength the Luo Tian Sect had displayed to the outside world was not weak at all, they never doubted their own power.

This was the pride of a Holy Land disciple!

As soon as they decided, they acted. They immediately gathered their clan members and disciples and marched north, intending to deliver a heavy gift to Qu Lingjun's Core Formation Grand Ceremony!

...

In the Five Elements Domain.

That peak, shrouded in thick clouds all day long, had become a forbidden area.

All disciples of the Five Elements Divine Sect were ordered by their Sect Master, Divine Fire Sage, not to approach Divine Origin Peak.

Everyone thought the Sect Master was afraid they would disturb the Supreme Elder's secluded cultivation.

Only Divine Fire Sage knew that this was not what he was worried about.

Even he himself rarely set foot on this mountain.

But this time, he had to make a personal trip.

In the unbearable wait, the formation on the mountain slowly opened, and he mustered up the courage to walk in.

"What is it?"

A hoarse voice, tinged with a sinister tone, sent chills down one's spine.

Divine Fire Sage lowered his head, not daring to look at his senior brother.

He told his senior brother everything that had happened over the years, especially the major event of the Nether Origin Holy Land being destroyed.

After hearing this, Divine Origin's mind was greatly shaken, and a complex and contradictory aura suddenly burst forth.

At that moment, Divine Fire's expression changed drastically. From within, he sensed many familiar auras.

There was Senior Brother Linmu's, Junior Sister Qingchi's... and even the aura of that old ghost Motian was present.

"What exactly is Senior Brother doing?"

After a long while, a sinister laugh came from the cave dwelling.

"It's good that it's gone!"

"It's good that it's gone!"

"If it weren't for Black Marsh ordering us to go up Green Parasol Mountain back then, how could I have ended up like this!"

"That Rosycloud Primordial merely left behind a single means back then, and it caused our side to suffer deaths and injuries. They should have known long ago that the other party would come to retaliate."

"The Nether Origin Sect has been too high and mighty in the Eastern Wilderness for too long. Their stubborn pride made them think no one would dare to provoke them."

"Unfortunately, I didn't get to achieve the Ascendant realm. Otherwise, I would have gone to have a word with them about how they have bullied me all these years!"

Divine Fire Sage lowered his head, not daring to utter a single word.

He knew the deep resentment hidden beneath his senior brother's surface submission to the Nether Origin Sect all these years.

A long time ago, the Nether Origin Sect had said they would accept his senior brother into the mountain, to cultivate on that Yanfu Mountain and strive for the Ascendant realm.

After all, with his senior brother's Five Spirit Root constitution, ordinary spirit veins simply could not support him in climbing to a higher realm.

Only Yanfu Mountain could!

But over the years, his senior brother had done so much for the Nether Origin Sect, even damaging his own Dao cultivation, yet the Nether Origin Sect never extended an olive branch.

In the end, they casually sent his senior brother back to his hometown.

The disdain and mockery in that act was something no Sage could tolerate.

"Cough, cough, cough..."

The loud laughter suddenly turned into coughing.

Divine Fire Sage quickly reminded him, "Senior Brother, please take care of your health."

Divine Origin squinted, a half-smile on his face. "So you still care about me?"

Divine Fire was startled and quickly changed the subject. "I heard that the Luo Tian Sect is about to hold a Core Formation Grand Ceremony for a disciple of the Alchemy Sect's Luo Chen. It's coming up soon. Should we..."

But unlike his previous attitude towards the Luo Tian Sect, this time Senior Brother Divine Origin seemed somewhat disinterested.

Forget it. Luo Chen is dead. Going to pinch those parasites clinging to him is meaningless.

Divine Fire hummed in acknowledgment, then respectfully bowed and prepared to take his leave.

But just as he reached the entrance of the cave dwelling, the gloomy voice came from behind him again.

"It's been almost nine years. Why hasn't a single one of those disciples achieved the Sage realm?"

Divine Fire's whole body trembled. He wanted to explain, but his senior brother seemed too lazy to listen, merely saying indifferently.

"Don't disappoint me!"

...

The news of Luo Chen's only personal disciple's Core Formation Grand Ceremony spread far and wide.

The friendly sects, the Divine Talisman Sect and the Nine Spirits Sect, had also received the news.

But they could not get away.

Because they were currently facing the immense pressure of the demon army massing outside the six easternmost domains, how could they personally visit?

And although it was not good to speak of people leaving and tea cooling, the news of the Nether Abyss's destruction and the Alchemy Sect's Luo Chen's accompanying death was ultimately known to all.

Under these circumstances, Elder Fu and Nine Spirits Primordial could only send a few disciples to deliver a generous gift, expressing their intentions.

The different reactions of various parties revealed the treacherous hearts of men and the myriad forms of life.

Only in the nine heavens above, a figure came chasing the wind and the moon, his face full of murderous intent, his body carrying a monstrous demonic wind.

"Luo Chen!"

"Even if you are dead, I will not forget the enmity of that year."

"On the day of the Luo Tian Sect's great celebration, I will wash your disciples and descendants in blood and sever your legacy!"

...

"So deserted?"

Gu Hong and Lin Yonghua appeared before the mountain gate of the Luo Tian Sect.

Behind them were the ten thousand cultivators gathered from the Gu and Lin families.

Among them were over ten clansmen and disciples at the Adept realm, as well as guest elders recruited over the years from other surnames.

Add to that the countless true cultivators at the Initiate realm, and one could see a glimpse of the Nether Origin Sect's former glory.

Just two families that had split off had such a solid foundation!

Perhaps it was precisely for this reason that the destruction of the Nether Origin Sect had shocked the Eastern Wilderness so deeply.

Gu Hong explained, "Senior Brother Lin may not know, but the Luo Tian Sect has not been like ordinary Sage sects in recent years, indiscriminately recruiting new disciples. They only added a few people on the original basis, and up to now, they have only five or six hundred disciples."

"Five or six hundred disciples? That's not like the style of a Sage sect sitting on the resources of an entire domain!" Lin Yonghua frowned, spreading his divine sense to search for enemies.

Gu Hong raised his hand and pointed, aiming directly at the direction of Motian Cliff.

"This time, the Luo Tian Sect did not invite outsiders to observe the Core Formation Grand Ceremony. It is merely an internal celebration."

"And the location of the celebration is precisely Motian Cliff, where Luo Chen once resided."

"We will strike straight at the heart!"

Lin Yonghua's expression turned serious. "If that's the case, then let's go!"

Ten thousand cultivators, vast and mighty, headed towards the towering Motian Cliff.

From the mountain gate to Motian Cliff, it was only a thousand li.

For ordinary Initiate cultivators, it might take some time.

But Gu Hong and Lin Yonghua could not wait. They left the main force and arrived at Motian Cliff one step ahead.

They wanted to control the situation and prepare for the first act of reviving the Nether Origin Sect: killing the chicken to scare the monkeys, beheading someone to raise the banner!

However!

Upon arriving at Motian Cliff, it was still deserted.

Only the Coiling Dragon Pillar stood suspended in the air, its green curtains falling like a waterfall.

The banner embroidered with talisman patterns fluttered noisily, sending lonely echoes through the empty spirit mountain.

The two landed on the square and looked at each other in bewilderment.

"Where are the people?"

"I don't know!"

Just then, a voice came from above, tinkling like large and small pearls falling onto a jade plate.

"Coming uninvited makes you unwelcome guests! May I ask the names of you two Nether Origin Dao friends?"

Gu and Lin looked up simultaneously towards the hundred-zhang-high platform.

A graceful figure sat cross-legged on it, looking at them with curiosity.

Beautiful yet unfamiliar, powerful and dangerous.

Especially the conspicuous black and white chess piece sect emblem embroidered on her moon-white cloud-patterned silk Luo skirt, which made their expressions change drastically.

"Tianyuan Dao Sect!"

"Sage True Disciple!"

Could it be a helper invited by the Luo Tian Sect?

Right!

Luo Chen had once been a guest elder of the Tianyuan Dao Sect. After his misfortune, it was only natural for the Luo Tian Sect to seek refuge with the Tianyuan Dao Sect.

The two exchanged a knowing glance and, without hesitation, offered their magical treasures to strike at the woman on the platform.

Seeing this, Fu Qinglan gently shook her head and lowered her head with a sigh.

"Truly unwelcome guests!"

Her right fingertip moved, forming a hand seal, and then, seemingly slow but actually fast, she struck out.

A huge cyan handprint slowly emerged from the horizon, then pressed down with a bang!

This handprint was so similar to Luo Chen's famous technique, the Azure Yang Great Handprint.

However, its power was a hundred or a thousand times different, like the gap between a firefly and the bright moon.

It was the divine art personally taught to Fu Qinglan by an Ascendant powerhouse at the Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak of Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain—the Great Heaven Creation Seal!

At this moment, the pure and ethereal spiritual energy within Motian Cliff all surged towards Fu Qinglan.

Not only that, but a vast amount of the origin qi of heaven and earth was also attracted and merged into the giant handprint.

The moment the handprint appeared, a feeling of despair arose in the hearts of Gu and Lin. However, their reactions differed: one, with a ferocious expression, prepared to fight to the death, while the other retreated, intending to flee.

Boom!

The handprint fell, and the white jade square collapsed and shattered, leaving only the green jade platform where Fu Qinglan sat intact.

Amidst the tumbling rocks and rubble, Gu Hong let out an unwilling roar, which then faded into nothingness.

A streak of spiritual light, however, escaped from the physical body and shot into the distance.

Fu Qinglan glanced at the tiny Sage infant, but did not pursue it. She merely gazed into the distance, a deep sense of loss in her eyes.

"You are still alive, aren't you? I can feel your aura."

"Not only are you alive, but you have also returned and taken away your disciples and old friends."

"But why won't you see me? Why won't you tell me about your experiences over the years, or whether my grandfather left any last words before his fall?"

Her murmured words drifted in the air, but were drowned out by the sound of tumbling rocks, known to no one.

But from the nine heavens above came the sound of violent collisions.

Large fireballs fell like meteors.

Suddenly.

Bang!

A figure fell from the nine heavens, a look of ruthlessness on his face.

He was about to charge up again, but Fu Qinglan had already called out to him.

"Don't chase him. That great demon emperor has already left."

Shi Ju suddenly turned his head and snarled, "Bitch, why didn't you come and help me? Are you so fixated on that Wilderness Wanderer?"

Fu Qinglan frowned, unwilling to argue with him. She turned a deaf ear to his filthy words.

She simply rose gracefully and walked towards the summit of Motian Cliff.

"The remnants of the Nether Origin Sect are trying to stage a comeback. Compared to the conflict between you and the Wilderness Wanderer, this matter is probably what the Ancestors care about more."

Shi Ju was taken aback, then hatefully glanced at the high sky, turned around, and charged towards the direction from which the Gu and Lin clan members were coming.

Since they were remnants, they should be swept into the garbage heap. Why should they be allowed to live!

Unable to find the Wilderness Wanderer's disciples and descendants, and unable to defeat that great demon emperor, taking out his anger on these fellows was also a good choice.

And in the nine heavens.

Bi Zheng's expression was one of shock and uncertainty, his face full of bewilderment.

"When did the human race have so many experts who comprehend the true meaning of the laws ahead of time at the Sage stage?"

"And every single one of them knows divine arts!"

"In this human-demon war, can we really win?"
