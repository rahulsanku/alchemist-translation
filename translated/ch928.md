# Chapter 928: Elder Fu Entrusts His Orphans, the Descendant of an Old Friend

"Yes, I've come."

In the hazy morning mist, a figure in white robes walked out like an immortal banished from heaven.

Still so young, still so unhurried and composed, and even...

"You're still so cautious, aren't you?"

Elder Fu smiled as he sensed Luo Chen's divine sense spreading in all directions.

After confirming that there were no other Sage cultivators within the Divine Talisman Sect, Luo Chen withdrew his divine sense and gave an apologetic smile. "My situation is a bit complicated. I hope you'll bear with me, Elder."

Elder Fu nodded. "I know. Guest elder of the Central Continent Star Gate, master of the Nether Origin Pill Hall."

The fact that Luo Chen had a connection with the Tianyuan Dao Sect was not a secret; many people knew about it.

After all, the Luotian Treasure Pavilion that the Luo Tian Sect had established back then was backed by the Tianyuan Merchant Alliance.

But that he was a guest elder of the Star Gate—very few knew of this, only a handful like the City Lord of Lingtian.

Hearing the other party speak thus, Luo Chen's brow furrowed slightly.

"Dao Sect cultivators have been here?"

Elder Fu did not hide it and said bluntly, "In the Eastern Wilderness today, which major power hasn't been visited by Tianyuan Dao Sect cultivators?"

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow and was about to press further when Elder Fu changed the subject.

"However, the one who came to visit me was a woman. Her name—Fu Qinglan."

"So it was her!"

Luo Chen's expression was somewhat complicated. He hadn't expected that woman to have come to the Eastern Wilderness as well.

She had probably come not just for him, but for her grandfather too!

Seeing that Luo Chen indeed knew her, Elder Fu clicked his tongue in admiration. "From what I know, that woman is a major figure within the Tianyuan Dao Sect, and her master is an Ascendant powerhouse. For such a stunning person to travel thousands of miles to find you—truly worthy of Alchemy Saint Luo Chen!"

Luo Chen smiled bitterly. "Don't mock me, Elder. If we actually meet, we might even come to blows."

Elder Fu frowned, puzzled. "That shouldn't be the case. I found her attitude quite acceptable; she merely wanted to meet you. And because of you, my Divine Talisman Sect has even received some protection."

Luo Chen suddenly asked, "Then this meeting you arranged—was it at her request?"

Elder Fu quickly waved his hand. "How could I possibly expose your whereabouts? That I've held on with my last breath, waiting for you to appear, is merely my own final wish."

As he said this, the aura of decline about him became increasingly impossible to conceal.

Elder Fu was already quite advanced in years, and he had frequently fought with others.

Especially during that great battle at Lingtian Pass, where he was severely wounded by the Demon Emperor. Even with Luo Chen's pills to help, it had inevitably affected his lifespan.

Now his great limit was approaching, only a matter of days away.

In the heavy atmosphere, Elder Fu managed a faint smile.

"You've come a long way and must be tired. Have a cup of wine!"

It was now the depths of winter. Outside, snow fell heavily, covering everything in silver.

Beautiful as it was, the deep chill was unavoidable.

More importantly, as a Sage-level sect, not having any formation barriers or barriers to alter the weather and temperature was enough to reveal the current predicament of the Divine Talisman Sect.

Luo Chen said nothing, drinking the spirit wine that Elder Fu had warmed.

When warmth spread through his belly, he asked, "You asked to meet me—surely it's not just to see an old friend one last time before you go?"

Elder Fu sighed. "The reason has to do with that matter I just mentioned."

That matter?

Luo Chen was puzzled, but quickly understood.

The Tianyuan Dao Sect cultivators visiting the various major sects of the Eastern Wilderness!

Seeing that Luo Chen had grasped it, Elder Fu nodded.

"The Nether Origin Sect was destroyed, and the Tianyuan Dao Sect took over the Eastern Wilderness. The first thing they did was subdue the Eastern Sun Sect, a former vassal of the Nether Origin Sect. The second was to hunt down the remnants of the Nether Origin Sect on a large scale, preventing this overlord that had dominated the Eastern Wilderness for three thousand years from rising again. People like Gu Hong and Lin Yonghua, whom you and I have seen, are either dead or have fled. And the third thing, ahem..."

Elder Fu coughed a couple of times, his haggard face flushing slightly red.

After catching his breath, he smiled bitterly. "Forgive me. This old bones of mine really can't hold up much longer."

Luo Chen expressed understanding, a look of sorrow in his eyes.

Elder Fu's current condition could not be cured by medicine or pills.

It was a case of accumulated ailments that were too severe to reverse, compounded by having reached the limit of his lifespan.

Even if he were given some longevity-extending spiritual medicines, it would only be a drop in the bucket, buying him a few more days or months at most.

And it was very likely that the old man had already tried many methods of extending his life; ordinary means would have little effect on him now.

Elder Fu exhaled a long breath of turbid qi and said slowly, "The third thing is this: because of the unusual movements of the demon race in the Million Mountains, they have amassed troops outside the six eastern domains. Since the Tianyuan Dao Sect wants to take over the Eastern Wilderness, they have to bear the responsibility of dealing with the demon beast army."

"They have the strength, but clearly, they don't have the will."

"They talk a good game, but in reality, they issue orders to our various sects, trying to make us cannon fodder to fight the demon beasts."

"That was the purpose of those Tianyuan Dao Sect cultivators who came to our doors earlier."

"My Divine Talisman Sect is slightly better off. Because of your connection, that Fairy Qinglan only asked us to produce more talismans, not to fight on the front lines. But..."

Elder Fu looked up at Luo Chen, his face full of bitterness.

"You know, once a war truly bogs down into a quagmire, who can stay out of it?"

Luo Chen agreed with this view.

Hadn't those ancient sects of the Eastern Wilderness thought the same way back then?

But when the demon beast army ran rampant, weren't the various sects that had stayed safely in the rear also affected?

"However, as for the current state of my Divine Talisman Sect, you've already swept it with your divine sense, Luo Chen. You probably have a general idea, right?"

Luo Chen said bluntly, "The number of disciples is quite large. A rough sweep shows over a hundred thousand. It seems you haven't been idle at all these past years."

At its weakest, the Divine Talisman Sect had only about ten thousand people left.

In just over a decade, the number had multiplied to such an extent, showing how hard Elder Fu had worked in his later years.

His current state was probably also closely related to mental and physical exhaustion.

"But among them, there are very few who are truly promising material!" Elder Fu murmured. "A long time ago, I started cultivating successors, but either they died or were injured, or I finally managed to train someone like Ye Hua, only for him to go to the Yellow Springs before me."

"Even if I've built up the foundation now, once I sit in meditation and pass away, what difference will it make if a great Sage-level sect has no Sage cultivator to guard it? It will exist in name only."

Luo Chen was puzzled. "Back then, I gave you a full three Nascent Soul Pills, didn't I?"

Elder Fu smiled bitterly. "They were all used. The remaining two were given to others, but they all failed. In the end, our foundation is too shallow."

Luo Chen said without hesitation, "If that's not enough, I still have some surplus here. I can give you another one or two."

"You're quite generous!" Elder Fu probably hadn't expected Luo Chen to be so generous, offering to give him more Nascent Soul Pills just like that. However, he waved his hand. "Forget it. My Divine Talisman Sect actually has its own methods for forming a Nascent Soul. The Nascent Soul Pill is only an effective supplement. The biggest problem now is that there are no suitable talents to cultivate!"

What use were pills?

Without suitable talents to cultivate, even with countless Nascent Soul Pills, one couldn't raise a single Sage cultivator.

Just think about how much effort and time Luo Chen had spent helping Cang Long overcome his tribulation to understand the difficulty.

Elder Fu had already given everything he had to cultivate a Sage like Ye Hua back then.

But the young Sage Ye Hua, due to insufficient strength, had gone before Elder Fu.

So now, the Divine Talisman Sect was about to face the awkward situation of having no Sage cultivator to guard it.

And for a Sage-level sect, once the top-tier strongmen are cut off, the consequences are unimaginable.

"Luo Chen, I have an improper request. I hope you will agree to it!" Elder Fu said with extreme solemnity.

Luo Chen vaguely guessed what it was and hesitated. "You want me to protect the Divine Talisman Sect after you're gone?"

Elder Fu nodded, then shook his head.

"At first, that was indeed my thought. But your situation is very complicated now; it's probably not convenient for you to show yourself. So I abandoned that idea. And what is 'protection,' after all? How long can it last? In the end, one must still rely on oneself."

"Therefore, I want to entrust some people to you."

"You don't need to worry about their future cultivation resources. From the Initiate stage to the Sage stage, I will arrange everything for them one by one."

"All you need to do is take them away, settle them in your Luo Tian Sect that has retreated far away, let them eat, live, and grow together with the Luo Tian cultivators."

"When their cultivation reaches a certain level, no matter what the Divine Talisman Sect has become by then, they can rely on those inheritances to rebuild the mountain gate."

This act of entrusting orphans was the ultimate form of that.

Luo Chen felt a sense of familiarity with this scene.

He looked at the old man. "Aren't you afraid I'll swallow up those resources, or forcibly keep those promising seedlings you've selected?"

Elder Fu looked at Luo Chen with a half-smile. "Would you?"

Luo Chen shook his head. He wouldn't.

Once he agreed to something, he usually did his best to do it.

Wasn't it the same with the Li family of Qinhua back then? Outsiders thought Luo Chen would strip the Li family clean, but in the end, Li Yingzhang became the sect master of the Luo Tian Sect.

Not only was the family continued, but his personal cultivation and status had surpassed the peak of the Li family's past glory.

Clearly, Elder Fu had inquired about the relevant situation.

And he believed he wouldn't be wrong about Luo Chen!

Luo Chen thought for a moment and nodded. "Since it's a request from a friend, and it's just a small effort for me, I can agree to this request. But I'll have to see the candidates myself, to make sure no one sneaks in..."

Before Luo Chen could finish, Elder Fu sent out a divine sense transmission.

Three figures walked in from outside the house.

"I know your concerns. That's why the three I've chosen are all disciples with clean backgrounds, raised in the Divine Talisman Sect since childhood. Apart from their aptitude, I've tested their character and temperament. They won't cause trouble for your Luo Tian Sect." Elder Fu explained carefully.

Luo Chen hummed in acknowledgment and looked at the three young people who had entered.

Two men and one woman, all at the early Initiate stage.

Their cultivation wasn't high, but since Elder Fu had chosen them as seeds for the Divine Talisman Sect, they must have exceptional qualities.

Elder Fu barked, "Why haven't you paid your respects to Senior Alchemy Saint?"

The three did not hesitate. They respectfully bowed to Luo Chen and reported their names.

Especially the young woman, who unexpectedly knelt down before Luo Chen and performed a grand bow.

"This junior, Chen Dongling, pays respects to Senior!"

Although it was a grand bow, it was also a breach of etiquette.

In a cultivator's life, one only bows to Heaven and Earth, and to one's parents and masters.

Although Luo Chen was a senior, he was ultimately not their master or relative, so such a grand bow was uncalled for.

Unless they abandoned their status as disciples of the Divine Talisman Sect and completely joined the Luo Tian Sect, only then would such a grand bow be appropriate.

Of course, if one's courage had been scared out of them and they groveled before the strong, that was also possible.

Elder Fu was quite displeased. "Dongling, why are you so rude?"

But Luo Chen raised his hand, interrupting Elder Fu's words.

His sharp eyes stared fixedly at Chen Dongling, at her somewhat familiar features.

He asked softly, "You look a bit like an old friend of mine."

Chen Dongling said without hesitation, "My late mother, Chen Shuyi, was from Dahe Market. When she was alive, she often mentioned that there was an older brother in the market who took good care of her. She rarely spoke his name, but the Dao name 'Danchenzi' was often brought up. If this junior is not mistaken, Senior Alchemy Saint should be that older brother my mother spoke of."

When these words came out, the two accompanying Initiate cultivators both looked sideways.

Even Elder Fu was somewhat surprised, not expecting this connection.

If that was really the case, then Chen Dongling's grand bow just now was not a breach of etiquette.

For a moment, everyone turned their gaze to Luo Chen, waiting for his reaction.

Under their watchful eyes, Luo Chen slowly nodded. "So you're little Shuyi's daughter. No wonder there's a familiar look in your brows and eyes."

Then he asked, "The last time I saw your mother was on Danxia Mountain. After that, we each went to join the Falling Cloud Sect's expansion war. I wonder how your mother fared afterward?"

Speaking of the past, a look of sadness appeared in Chen Dongling's eyes.

"The expansion war was still manageable. But later, when a large number of demon beasts rushed out from the Million Mountains, Mother and the others were trapped in the Shattered Moon Immortal City. Although they eventually broke out under the leadership of the sect's elders, she was severely injured. In the following decades, her injuries recurred repeatedly. After giving birth to me, her vitality was greatly damaged. Before reaching Lingtian Pass, she rode a crane to the immortals."

After hearing this account, even with Luo Chen's firm resolve, he couldn't help but feel a sense of daze.

The faces of old friends like Old Daoist Chen, Chen Shuyi, and Fu Xiuxiu kept surfacing in his mind.

Each had their own opportunities, each had their own endings, but in the end, those who could meet again were few.

In the heavy atmosphere, Luo Chen looked at Elder Fu.

"I've seen the three of them. When I leave, I will take them with me."

Elder Fu was overjoyed and urged the three, "Why haven't you thanked your Uncle Luo?"

Uncle Luo?

Luo Chen was stunned for a moment. Amidst the three's bows, he looked at Elder Fu.

Elder Fu said matter-of-factly, "As the last seedlings of the Divine Talisman Sect, I naturally took them as my disciples. You and I are friends, and your cultivation level is higher than mine. It's only natural for my disciples to call you 'Uncle,' isn't it?"

What a smooth operator!

Luo Chen smiled helplessly but wasn't angry.

A man on the verge of death—let him have his way!

The next moment, Elder Fu handed a storage bag to Luo Chen in front of the three.

Luo Chen frowned. "This is..."

"Don't misunderstand. I know your character is noble and you look down on such worldly things. But the resources inside are too precious for their current cultivation levels. I'm afraid that 'possessing a jade invites calamity.'"

"Therefore, I entrust them to you for safekeeping, to be distributed as you see fit."

"The scriptures inside contain nine-tenths of the essence of my Divine Talisman Sect's inheritance. I'm afraid that if they read them before their cultivation is sufficient, it will affect their future path. So I also hope that after you've studied them, you can guide them in their place."

The Divine Talisman Sect's inheritance!

Luo Chen was shocked. "These gifts are far too precious!"

Elder Fu said solemnly, "They are not gifts, but an old man's request. I hope you will grant it, friend!"

In the silence, Luo Chen accepted the storage bag.

"I may be somewhat busy in the future and cannot instruct them personally at all times. But the cultivation of your three disciples will not be delayed. I will have someone look after them."

Elder Fu did not insist that Luo Chen teach the three himself. "That is enough."

One should not put all one's eggs in one basket.

Elder Fu would live and die with the Divine Talisman Sect, even if he passed away from old age, he would die within the sect.

But considering the unpredictable future, sending out three seedlings and entrusting them to a friend was also necessary.

With the matter settled, there was nothing else to discuss.

Luo Chen prepared to leave with the three.

Elder Fu naturally would not detain him, but there was a hint of reluctance in his eyes.

Was it reluctance for Luo Chen, or for the three hopes of the Divine Talisman Sect's future, or for the beautiful scenery of this world? It was impossible to say.

Suddenly.

At the doorway, Luo Chen turned around, a smile on his face.

"I don't know if you prefer quiet or excitement, but I have a major event to attend to. Would you like to come and watch the show?"
