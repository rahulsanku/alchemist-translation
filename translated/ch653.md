# Chapter 653: Severed Limbs Rejoined, Flesh Reborn, Luo Chen the Human-Shaped Desolate Beast!

Inside Dragon Abyss Immortal City, in a cave residence where spiritual qi was dense enough to nearly accumulate into clouds, the handsome and unusually young Dragon Abyss True Being was kindly chatting with the three respectful cultivators below.

"You come from Central Continent, which is controlled by my Tianyuan Dao Sect. Everything there is orderly. If you want to break through realms, obtain more cultivation resources, or reach higher places, you must contend with people, with those ancient clans that have continued for who knows how many tens of thousands of years."

"But the Northern Sea is different. Here, you must not only contend with people, but also with demons!"

"Most high-rank demon beasts possess intelligence. Their trump cards are mysterious, their abilities outstanding, and they are no weaker than we humans. How can you be careless? The Sea-Containing Golden Toad last time counts as a small lesson. In the future, you must remember this. Remember it well!"

For a dignified Sage to be so kind and tirelessly instruct Golden Core cultivators was truly rare.

While Changfengzi and the others felt moved, they also felt lingering fear.

"Second Uncle's lesson is correct. We were indeed careless before."

Guan Xiaoping, who had always been spoiled and arrogant outside, had also become obedient at this moment.

"It was also my first time seeing a true desolate beast. The Sea-Containing Golden Toad was so powerful. Such a small thing could actually swallow and spit oceans. Even the three of us together found it hard to break free from its suppression."

Dragon Abyss True Being smiled. "It is normal that you do not understand desolate beasts. Central Continent, where you were born and raised, was already swept clean by the Dao Sect three thousand years ago. Forget desolate beasts. There are not even many wild demon beasts left inside."

Curiosity appeared on Guan Xiaoping's face. "Sage, why do I feel desolate beasts are even stronger than demon beasts? Are they not all the same?"

Dragon Abyss True Being's face became solemn as he revealed a secret.

"At the beginning, they were the same. But their instinctive choices made their later paths different."

"You should have studied the Dao Sect's textbooks."

"As written there, after ten thousand years of war, the human race carved out the Five Continents in this vast Mountains and Seas Realm, establishing the foundation of our race. That period of history is called the ancient human-demon war. From the signing of the human-demon armistice and the return of ten thousand demons to the wilds, more than three thousand years have passed. It can be said that from ancient times to the present, our human race has officially become the favored child of heaven and earth, the master of this realm."

"But before that, in remote antiquity, the true masters of the Mountains and Seas Realm were the desolate beasts spread throughout mountains, rivers, deserts, and oceans. They were the favored children of heaven and earth, the true masters of this realm."

"The demon beasts you meet now should actually be called demon cultivators. Like us, they walk the path of forming cores, condensing Nascent Souls, and becoming Ascendants. They have turned away from their original cultivation path."

"As for the current desolate beasts, they are either ancient survivors or those who follow the old methods. Their cultivation may be slow, but in the same realm, they are naturally more powerful than neither-here-nor-there demon cultivators."

Comparing this to memories of lessons they had studied before, Guan Xiaoping and the other two listened in a hazy half-understanding.

Amid seeming comprehension, Guan Xiaoping still did not fully understand.

Dragon Abyss True Being naturally would not explain too much. After they returned, if they thought more, they would understand.

Instead, he changed the topic and spoke of people.

"Demon cultivators and desolate beasts are certainly frightening, but in the current Northern Sea, what requires the most attention is still humans. With the Primordial Demon Sect destroyed and order gone, the already savage Northern Sea cultivators act with fewer restraints and increasingly resemble demonic cultivators. Among their formidable figures, their methods may not be refined enough, but their combat experience is absolutely no less than my Dao Sect's heavenly geniuses."

"For example, that Devil Lord Azure Yang. He saved you out of respect for me. But if it had been someone else with a vicious heart, forget saving you. Not kicking you while you were down would already be good."

"Think about it. With his methods, if he had secretly attacked while you were trapped by the Sea-Containing Golden Toad, even with life-preserving methods bestowed by elders, could you truly have survived?"

Changfengzi and the other two immediately looked somewhat unnatural.

Especially since their three life-preserving methods had already consumed one when facing Jiaoren Emperor Flow Lord.

Seeing the three reflect, Dragon Abyss True Being smiled.

"So, whether toward demons or humans, you must not underestimate the strong of the Northern Sea."

Changfengzi bowed. "Thank you for Second Uncle's teaching!"

The other two also bowed in thanks.

After straightening, Wu Tao impatiently asked, "Sage, are we truly not going to the Sunken Sea?"

"The Sunken Sea?"

The corner of Dragon Abyss True Being's mouth rose. He was about to say something when his ears suddenly trembled.

Boom!

A thunderclap seemed to explode outside.

He frowned. Why would there be thunder in a clear sky?

When his vast divine sense swept out, even leaving Dragon Abyss Immortal City and sweeping toward the Fulong Mountain Range, he detected nothing unusual.

Had his perception been wrong?

Shaking his head, Dragon Abyss True Being withdrew his thoughts.

"Do not set your sights on that Demon-Fall Land."

Wu Tao was somewhat unwilling, but he also knew his realm was insufficient and his ability lacking. He should not harbor such fantasies.

Changfengzi, however, was quite puzzled. "Second Uncle, since you are so powerful, why not explore the Sunken Sea? I hear there are countless treasures inside. Would that not benefit others?"

"If I do not go, it is because I disdain it," Dragon Abyss True Being said slowly. "As for who it will benefit? Hehe, as long as the Sunken Sea remains, it will benefit no one. When the time comes, our sect will naturally send the most suitable person inside."

The most suitable person?

The three below looked at one another, not knowing who he meant.

Within the eight hundred li of Peng Lake, Han Zhan left his island and hung high in the void.

His divine sense transmitted into the ears of the two demons.

"Heavenjade, go to Lotus-Picking Island and restrain the Zhang clan cultivators from going out."

"Black King, patrol outside Peng Island and guard your master."

After giving all instructions, Han Zhan stared brightly at Peng Island, at that great hall covered by massive spiritual qi.

His gaze occasionally lifted toward the sky blocked by the formation.

Time passed bit by bit.

Although dark clouds accumulated in the air, no silver light flickered. In fact, those dark clouds gradually changed colors: red, orange, yellow, green...

A thoughtful expression appeared on Han Zhan's face.

"So this is the so-called favored child of heaven and earth?"

"When advancing to fourth tier, no lightning tribulation appears. Instead, spiritual qi pours into the body, as if auspicious clouds congratulate him."

"This old man also never expected pure body refinement could reach this step. Luo Chen..."

As he murmured inwardly, Han Zhan's eyes grew increasingly bright, as if happy for him.

Inside the spacious Star Dou Hall, Luo Chen wore the Black Dust Armor, with the great cauldron suspended above his head, heavily guarded.

However, the expected calamity did not appear. What appeared was the unexpected pouring of spiritual qi into his body.

Inside the hall, bright pearls shone brilliantly, and formation flags snapped loudly.

Massive waves of heaven and earth spiritual qi surged endlessly into the hall.

Gathering all the spiritual qi of the eight hundred li of Peng Lake, in a short time, Star Dou Hall was temporarily transformed into an environment comparable to a fourth-tier spiritual land.

Under the watering of this dense spiritual qi, Luo Chen's body uncontrollably devoured the incoming spiritual qi crazily.

This was unrefined spiritual qi.

Luo Chen did not dare draw it into his qi sea and let his Golden Core devour it.

But clearly, the target of this spiritual qi was not the qi sea, but the pure fleshly body.

Countless streams of spiritual qi drilled into Luo Chen's muscles, bones, meridians, and even fused with his blood.

Faintly, one phrase appeared in Luo Chen's mind.

"Marrow cleansing and meridian washing."

Only, unlike Foundation Establishment, when a cultivator used spiritual qi refined by himself to transform his meridians and cleanse his marrow, this time heaven and earth assisted him in marrow cleansing and meridian washing.

Later, Luo Chen even found it somewhat difficult to endure the massive spiritual qi pouring into his body.

He gave a low roar.

His body began automatically swelling and growing larger.

One zhang, two zhang... until nine zhang.

His enormous body sat inside Star Dou Hall, even making the spacious hall seem crowded.

Once his body became larger, he could bear more spiritual qi.

Luo Chen also gradually found the sensation of control over his body.

Following the circulation route of Ten Thousand Daos Converge, he tried to control this heaven and earth spiritual qi and actively cleanse his fleshly body.

Not only that, his former fire peng form was also gradually changing.

Later, it had faintly become an enlarged human shape.

If Wang Yuan were here, he would feel it extremely familiar. This was clearly the same path as his Giant Spirit Transformation.

This was something Luo Chen had sensed while studying physical killing moves. The Heavenly Peng true body was too straightforward and not suitable for deploying those killing moves.

Therefore, he had been fine-tuning his transformed body shape, gradually making it closer to human form.

This somewhat verified what Wang Yuan had said back then: demon-like transformations were not suitable for humans.

During this process, Luo Chen gradually sensed that within his body, every place seemed to have a strange power spreading.

That power was neither mana nor demonic power.

It was somewhat like the Core Force Wang Yuan had refined after comprehending blood-qi changes, yet it was not as pure and appeared slightly mixed. But beyond that mixed quality, it seemed even deeper and easier to control.

Specifically, it seemed to have formed from the fusion of blood, marrow, and external spiritual qi.

This power filled his entire body, everywhere and all-reaching.

It even formed a blood-red membrane outside his body.

Around the time it took an incense stick to burn later, Luo Chen gave a muffled groan and completely regained control of his body.

At the same time, he sensed the swollen fullness within his body.

"Has merit reached completion?"

Luo Chen murmured and began slowly shrinking his body. The external blood-colored membrane seeped back into his flesh.

The surrounding spiritual qi also gradually retreated, returning to the eight hundred li of Peng Lake.

When everything stopped, Luo Chen sat cross-legged inside the hall, refreshed in mind and body.

Suddenly, he lifted the Black Dust Armor. With his right hand shaped like a blade, he fiercely chopped at his left wrist.

If others saw such self-mutilation, they would certainly cry out in alarm.

Luo Chen's expression was firm, as if he wanted to test something.

After his palm-blade fell, his left wrist, without any protection and even with force actively withdrawn, broke off with a sound.

Luo Chen grunted and stared fixedly at the bowl-like wound cut cleanly at the wrist.

There, the blood was crystalline, the bones like jade, and the flesh tendons thick.

After staring for a while, Luo Chen's thought moved, and that strange power spread throughout his body gathered toward the left wrist.

A numb, tingling, unbearably itchy and painful feeling arose.

Under his gaze, granulation bloomed on the left wrist and began growing along the original shape.

Luo Chen's eyes suddenly widened.

"Flesh rebirth!"

After verifying his guess, Luo Chen controlled that power to retreat, then retrieved the left hand floating in the air and aligned it with his wrist.

How could newly grown flesh compare to a body tempered a thousand times?

Without using any medical arts, after a layer of blood-colored membrane spread over it, his left hand recovered as before, without leaving even the slightest scar.

Luo Chen swallowed.

"Severed limb rejoining!"

After seeing these two scenes in succession, Luo Chen fell silent.

Clearly, his body had undergone a fundamental change.

It was as if the essence of his life had transformed from its source.

That power different from demonic power, mana, vigor, or astral qi was very small, but possessed wondrous effects.

At this moment, rather than calling him a human, it was better to call him a desolate beast with a human-like form.

A fourth-tier desolate beast.

As for the power he had grasped, he would temporarily call it "origin power."

This power granted Luo Chen absolute control over his fleshly body. Even if he could not reach the rumored level of rebirth from a drop of blood, he could still rejoin severed limbs and regenerate flesh while his main torso remained.

He even felt that even severed parts of his body could move freely.

Under such circumstances, if someone wanted to kill him, they would need to sever all four limbs, cut off his head, and thoroughly destroy his main body.

Luo Chen did not know whether other desolate beasts were like this, but at least he was.

Moreover, unlike ordinary desolate beasts, he also cultivated the qi-refinement path.

Even if his body was destroyed, as long as his Golden Core remained, his soul would still have a temporary place to lodge.

After understanding all this, Luo Chen raised his right hand and slowly clenched his fist.

A feeling of great power arose spontaneously.

It was as if with this hand alone, he could crush mountains, break peaks, and battle the ocean.

"Power!"
