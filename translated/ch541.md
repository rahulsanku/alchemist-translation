# Chapter 541: Set Out! Xuanyan Sea Region!

On Invitation Moon Island,

the young man in a moon-white Daoist robe moved his hand seals like darting swallows, his mana surging like tides.

Under his full-hearted control, the medicinal materials gathering into the giant cauldron were innumerable, like stars and a hundred flowers.

With such an enormous workload, and without anyone assisting him, one could imagine the difficulty of controlling it all alone.

It was rumored that when those great alchemy sects carried out large-scale pill refinement like this, they often had to prepare for a long time.

Moreover, during refinement, they would have all kinds of formations, numerous low-level alchemists, and even other talents specializing in different skills assisting.

For example, when Azure Pill Valley refined Nascent Soul Pills, they had prepared five great third-tier alchemists in one breath, thirty experienced second-tier alchemists, and even an Azure Pill Combined Refinement Grand Formation[TERM?]. Only then, under the direction of Qing Danzi, that fourth-tier alchemist, had they refined the Nascent Soul Pills.

For Luo Chen to now attempt such a feat with his strength alone seemed like nothing but a wild fantasy in the eyes of the world.

Only Luo Chen himself knew that he possessed the corresponding foundation.

Whether it was the basic alchemy arts of every element all reaching Perfected Completion, or the True Flame Pill refinement method also reaching Perfected Completion, they were enough to support him to this step.

Especially since the Primordial Unity Cauldron, this supreme treasure of the alchemy dao, was his lifebound magic treasure, which he had painstakingly nurtured for many years and could control as he pleased.

Any change within it, he could capture at the first moment.

It was because of all these factors that he took this step.

Enlarging the alchemy cauldron was also forced by necessity.

For any large-scale pill refinement like this, those small cauldrons of one or two zhang were insufficient.

Only, with things like this, many matters were not very easy to conceal.

Of course!

There was also no need to conceal them!

In this place, he was king!

If someone glimpsed one move or two techniques, he did not care.

When it came to transmitting the dao and teaching arts, Luo Chen had always been fairly generous.

Whether in his humble days at Dahe Market or when he rose in Tianlan Immortal City, regarding what he had learned, aside from the most core parts, he was normally not very stingy and was willing to share the methods within.

In any case, if others learned them, they would also gain their own comprehension, then push out the old and bring forth the new.

He would then learn back from them, relying on the proficiency function of the attribute panel to cultivate it to the peak.

With this give and take, the one who benefited most was still him.

The refinement work on the island proceeded in an orderly manner under Luo Chen's control.

Increasingly scorching heat gradually radiated outward, forcing the medicine masters waiting nearby to retreat one after another.

Under this high temperature, even space seemed to become distorted.

Thousands upon tens of thousands of cultivators gazed from afar at the man in the sky, who moved with command and confidence, every move as smooth as flowing clouds and water. The meaning in their eyes also changed greatly.

It was no longer the simple fear and dread from before.

Instead, a few more traces of respect had appeared.

Those who possessed great skill, even if they were sinister and cunning as people or overbearing in conduct, would still draw three parts more respect from others because of their craft.

However,

some sharp-eyed people also discovered Luo Chen's constantly shifting expression through the seemingly distorted air.

Sometimes he was delighted and happy, sometimes he frowned in puzzlement. At times, he hesitated and slowed, at other times, he advanced with fierce speed. Later, every hand seal seemed extraordinarily solemn.

If not for some people thinking their eyes had blurred, they would even have suspected that Demon Lord Qingyang's face was sweating.

Naturally, there were people discussing these details.

But no matter what, as time passed, Luo Chen's pill refinement progress continued advancing slowly at an unquestionable speed.

One day, two days, three days...

The cultivators by the lakeside came and went, left and returned, scattered and gathered.

Aside from those truly interested in alchemy, the others naturally could not possibly remain to watch the whole time.

But with such a long pill refinement time, everyone could imagine how enormous the mana consumption was and how heavy the burden on the divine soul was.

It was precisely because of this that the Swallow Islands cultivators' respect toward Luo Chen grew increasingly deep.

Only at this moment, without experiencing battle, did they finally personally sense Luo Chen's strength!

It was a gentle display of foundation!

Naked, yet grand and dignified!

Finally, half a month later.

As a long howl came from the ten-li calm lake, blasting countless golden waves upward, everyone's gaze gathered on the same place.

In midair, the young man in white robes and with a red face held a green-skinned gourd[TERM?] in one hand, while his other hand plucked through the air. He shouted:

"The hundred pills are formed. The time is now."

"Out!"

The next moment, streaks of flowing light flew from the Primordial Unity Cauldron like meteors.

Each one was crimson throughout, like agate. As they reflected one another, they seemed like meteorites rolling back across the sky, flying toward the young man.

The man's finger pointed repeatedly, like an immortal plucking stars and taking the moon, placing streak after streak of meteor light into the green-skinned gourd.

One pill, two pills, three pills... at a glance, they seemed countless.

But cultivators' senses were extremely sharp. Since Luo Chen made no effort to conceal them, Foundation Establishment cultivators quickly counted the number.

A total of four hundred twenty-seven pills were formed!

Such a number shocked the entire crowd.

And every pill's grade was maintained at the high-grade level.

Rich pill fragrance filled heaven and earth.

Even the spirit plants on the island underwent slight changes under this dense pill fragrance.

Some water-attribute plants became listless and drooping.

Some fire-attribute plants stretched their branches and leaves, as if gulping down that fragrance.

Smelling those pill fragrances, the true Foundation Establishment cultivators by the lakeside looked at one another, their eyes full of disbelief!

More than four hundred third-tier high-grade pills!

Putting aside their effects, if calculated according to the normal market price in the Northern Sea cultivation world,

just third-tier low-grade pills alone cost at least three thousand spirit stones for a bottle of ten. Middle-grade doubled that.

As for high-grade...

Sorry, they had a price but no market!

This kind of pill generally appeared only in large-scale auctions, high-level black markets, or bartering exchanges between high-level cultivators.

If one really had to name a figure, a bottle would have to be ten or twenty thousand low-grade spirit stones no matter what!

If calculated this way, Demon Lord Qingyang's half month of pill refinement consumed one hundred portions of materials, costing three hundred thousand spirit stones. In the end, he formed over four hundred pills, all high-grade, with a sale price reaching around five hundred to eight hundred thousand.

After deducting costs, in other words, in a mere half month, Demon Lord Qingyang had earned roughly two hundred to five hundred thousand.

No, no!

He had no costs at all. Those medicinal materials had all been offered up for free by the various households.

In other words, it was pure profit!

After calculating this account clearly, everyone could not help swallowing, their eyes full of greed.

However, when Luo Chen's gentle gaze swept over them, everyone felt as if they had been drenched over the head by icy rain, and all greed vanished.

What joke was this? That was Demon Lord Qingyang!

Who would dare scheme against him?

Even so, they had finally seen the enormous profits of pills! They had seen the ability of alchemists to seize wealth!

There was nothing more to say. After returning, they definitely had to cultivate their own clan alchemists as much as possible.

Even if they could not reach Demon Lord Qingyang's level, what if they could reach half?

Would they not still earn pots full and bowls full?

The crowd stood on the shore, reluctant to leave for a long time.

On Invitation Moon Island, the nine-zhang giant cauldron had already shrunk.

And Demon Lord Qingyang's figure had long since vanished.

Threads of white mist breathed out from the island, gradually spreading across the ten-li calm lake and concealing the island at its heart.

...

Qingyang Hall.

Luo Chen stepped with somewhat floating footsteps and walked to the front of the hall.

Before he had even entered, laughter sounded from the soul banner leaning nearby.

"Luo Chen, sweating through your robes, were you?"

These words were not without reason.

Because he had to concentrate on refining pills, the qi-nurturing composure Luo Chen had tempered for a long time naturally could not be maintained wholeheartedly.

That was why he had "lost composure" in the eyes of many cultivators.

Luo Chen rolled his eyes and said full of confidence, "It was fine. Everything was within my calculations!"

Han Zhan smiled and did not expose him.

Luo Chen stopped, his eyes seeming to see through the soul banner and into Han Zhan's wisp of remnant soul hidden within.

He hesitated for a moment, but in the end did not speak. He stepped inside.

Because of that hesitation, Han Zhan also fell silent.

Only when Luo Chen entered the hall did he say softly, "Congratulations."

Luo Chen gave an mm, saying he was preparing to rest properly and not to let anyone disturb him. Han Zhan naturally remembered this.

This rest lasted three days and three nights.

After waking, Luo Chen rubbed his head, still feeling dizzy and swollen to the point of panic.

This was the aftereffect of hundred-pills simultaneous refinement.

Processing so many medicinal materials at once, while simultaneously controlling so many medicinal liquids to merge into pills, was an enormous challenge for any alchemist's divine soul.

Even though Luo Chen considered his current divine soul foundation no weaker than any mid Core Formation cultivator,

even a mid Core Formation cultivator would find it very difficult to complete this challenge!

The reason Luo Chen could succeed was that his terrifyingly rich alchemy experience had played an enormous role.

Inside the sleeping hall,

Luo Chen's face held a trace of regret.

If only the Tongyou Pill still worked, that would be good.

Unfortunately, there were no ifs.

After Core Formation, he no longer had any pills related to increasing his divine soul foundation to take.

The growth of his divine soul, aside from the tempering of years, the rise of realm, and the slight increase from the Fine Dust Primordial Art, had no other method.

This was also why Luo Chen had hesitated when looking at Han Zhan.

The Falling Cloud Sect was skilled in puppet arts and proficient in spirit-refining methods!

As far as he knew, the Falling Cloud Sect's Three Arts and Two Techniques[TERM?] were obtained by its founding ancestor from a secret realm. If practiced over the long term, the divine soul could grow stronger day by day.

It was precisely because of this that Falling Cloud Sect cultivators could control numerous puppets, puppets of higher realm than themselves, and achieve the feat of challenging across levels.

If he could learn the Three Arts and Two Techniques from Han Zhan, he would definitely be able to make up for this "shortcoming."

But very clearly!

How could a sect's inheritance be easily granted to another?

After hesitating, he did not speak.

That way, if the other party refused, both sides would not gain needless awkwardness.

After his regret, Luo Chen adjusted his mood, took out the green-skinned gourd from his storage ring, and calculated.

Four hundred twenty-seven high-grade True Flame Pills!

This number was very different from what he had imagined.

According to the success rates at every proficiency level, after reaching Perfected Completion, when refining high-grade pills, he could completely achieve a fifty-percent success rate.

With the assistance of his lifebound magic treasure, the success rate would certainly rise.

Even if not six or seven hundred pills, it should at least remain above five hundred.

But the final result was only four hundred-plus pills...

After a brief silence, Luo Chen reached a conclusion.

It should be because this was his first time carrying out hundred-pills simultaneous refinement, so he was not yet too familiar with it. After all, handling so many medicinal materials at the same time, being flustered was inevitable, and sweating through his robes was not impossible to understand.

If he did it a few more times later, once he was familiar with it, things would be fine.

Putting away the green-skinned gourd,

Luo Chen opened his mouth and spat out the Primordial Unity Cauldron, then carefully observed it.

In the end, he nodded in satisfaction.

As expected of one of Azure Pill Valley's three great alchemy cauldrons. It could use ordinary pill refinement to nourish its quality.

One round of hundred-pills simultaneous refinement had caused a large amount of pill qi to merge into the cauldron, and the fire-attribute power was incomparably flourishing.

Some ore materials that had not yet been refined in the past had also begun to thoroughly merge into the cauldron.

Half a month's work surpassed the ordinary slow grinding of half a year!

"If possible, if this continues, at most ten years, the Primordial Unity Cauldron may be nurtured to the utmost, shed all impurities, and reach the limit of a high-grade magic treasure."

"There is simply no such thing as a supreme-grade magic treasure. Otherwise, with the foundation of this magic treasure of mine, born high-grade and shedding all impurities, it could become supreme-grade!"

In delight, Luo Chen put away the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Continue refining pills?

Of course that was impossible.

His divine soul could not hold on, and his mana also needed time to recover.

Rest for half a month first.

Of course, this half month could not be wasted.

Before setting out, many things had to be prepared in advance, not merely cultivation pill reserves.

After washing up, Luo Chen first ate a full meal under Cheng Haixin's service. Then, while his body digested that enormous amount of blood food, he began reading books about the local customs of the Northern Sea.

Reading was a good habit he had maintained all these years.

Even after arriving in this unfamiliar land of the Northern Sea, when conditions allowed, he had not abandoned it.

Many things required practice to personally experience.

But there were also many things that could be understood in advance by reading books written by predecessors.

When he truly encountered them, he could also respond calmly.

So-called broad knowledge and rich experience were never limited to walking ten thousand li. Reading ten thousand scrolls was also a "shortcut."

After browsing through a Chronicle of Feileng City[TERM?], Luo Chen went to the side hall again.

"Senior, I have come to study formations again."

Inside the side hall thick with ghost qi, a three-inch little figure floated out.

Han Zhan looked at Luo Chen in surprise. "You have thoroughly mastered those first-tier formations?"

Luo Chen laughed freely and waved casually.

All kinds of basic materials immediately floated out from his storage ring. As he placed them according to specific trajectories and injected mana, streaks of light immediately lit up on the ground.

First-tier Fire-Gathering Formation[TERM?]!

It was not very complicated.

But being able to arrange it as Luo Chen did, without the slightest hint of smoke or fire, as smooth as flowing clouds and water, was truly somewhat shocking.

After Han Zhan's surprise, he looked deeply at Luo Chen, then beckoned.

"Come in!"

"Although I am not any top formation master, I can basically arrange first- through third-tier formations at will, and I know a little of some fourth-tier formations as well."

"Your foundation is very solid, and you have the Core Formation realm. Learning basic formations of each tier should not be difficult to enter."

Han Zhan, supreme elder of the Falling Cloud Sect.

Aside from his Sage realm, he mainly cultivated puppet arts and secondarily cultivated the formation dao.

Only by combining the two had he created that Yin-Yang Five Elements Sword Formation that forced Wolf Emperor Aoxiao into quite a sorry state!

Luo Chen's formation foundation originated from Min Longyu, and later Fu Jiusheng had taught him for a period.

Now that he had Han Zhan as a companion, it was impossible for him to let go of the formations the other party mastered.

The Falling Cloud Sect's Three Arts and Two Techniques were sect inheritance, so he could not obtain them.

But for these common formations, Han Zhan would not be stingy.

If he could master them one by one, they would play an enormous role for Luo Chen whether in cultivation or battle.

For an ordinary person, being able to push one path to the extreme already made them a genius.

If they secondarily cultivated other skills, even a genius like Han Zhan could only earn the evaluation of "knowing a little."

But Luo Chen was different!

He had the proficiency panel in hand. Now that his alchemy could advance no further, secondarily cultivating formations had great assurance of reaching profound depth.

Moreover, a long time ago, he had discovered

that the formation dao lay in copying.

So-called copying meant repeating again and again. This precisely matched the true meaning of the proficiency panel.

Luo Chen was very expectant!

Perhaps one day, he too would become a formation master of his generation.

...

Half a month later.

The white mist on the ten-li calm lake dispersed once again.

The nine-zhang giant cauldron once more stood on the island at the lake's heart.

This time, the cultivators who came to watch were fewer by more than half.

But among the crowd, many more young Qi Refinement cultivators had appeared.

Very clearly, the various clans planned to borrow the chance of watching Luo Chen refine pills to try to cultivate their own alchemists.

Toward this, Luo Chen did not drive them away.

He merely instructed Cheng Haixin and Xu Muxian, the nominal disciple he had taken in these years, to observe carefully before the refinement.

Afterward, the process was the same as before.

Only this time, Luo Chen was relatively much more composed, and the embarrassing situation of sweat appearing on his face like the first time did not occur.

The first time is unfamiliar, the second time familiar!

So-called hundred-pills simultaneous refinement, under the Perfected Completion proficiency level, was not that great a matter.

In this way, after half a month passed, the familiar pill-collecting scene appeared again.

Even though everyone had seen it once, the second time still shocked them. Quite a few people even revealed expressions of worship.

...

Half a month refining pills, half a month resting.

Time flowed bit by bit. After six months like this, the medicinal material stockpile the Swallow Islands had gathered for Luo Chen was finally consumed completely.

And Luo Chen's True Flame Pill reserves also reached an unprecedented three thousand five hundred pills!

As for why there were so many?

The reason was that, after the third time, he controlled the success rate above fifty percent!

In fact, the fourth time formed five hundred fifty pills.

The fifth time, five hundred ninety pills.

The sixth time, six hundred thirty pills.

Together with his previous stockpile, they totaled three thousand five hundred pills!

It was only natural that the later results were so gratifying.

Luo Chen had become familiar with the process of large-scale pill refinement, comprehended the essence within it, and had the Primordial Unity Cauldron's auxiliary effect of improving pill grade and pill quantity. Naturally, he obtained such abundant results.

At this point, the pill reserves were completely prepared.

Then next was going out, seeking that special place, refining Black Emperor Paste, and assisting body refinement cultivation.

...

On this day,

at Swallow Dock, seven cultivators respectfully sent him off.

Cheng Dou, who looked increasingly weathered, carefully instructed his clan elder Cheng Ji[TERM?], occasionally glancing toward Luo Chen with several traces of delight in his eyes.

Luo Chen was leaving, and before leaving, he had lent Invitation Moon Island to Cheng Dou so that he could attack the Core Formation stage.

This was something he had long yearned for.

After several years of cultivating the Qingyang Alchemy Canon, he was already close at hand to minor accomplishment.

With the aid of a third-tier spirit vein land, paired with his Foundation Establishment Perfected Completion realm, the Core Formation stage was within sight!

Cheng Haixin stood beside Luo Chen with some worry.

"Must you go to that Xuanyan Sea Region[TERM?]?"

Luo Chen glanced at her. The woman lowered her head and no longer tried to persuade him.

By her ear, there was only Luo Chen's faint instruction.

"Although Xu Muxian's alchemy has already reached the first tier, his foundation is still not very solid. The pill formula I gave him is rather complicated. Provide him with all the medicinal materials offered by the various clans, and let him practice more."

"In addition, your Cheng clan plans to marry a woman down to Xu Muxian and carry out a marriage alliance. I do not oppose this matter. But before Foundation Establishment, his primordial yang[TERM?] must not be broken."

"Finally, the Starfield Pill medicinal materials I asked you to gather must also be hurried along."

Cheng Haixin nodded, firmly remembering each matter in her heart.

She asked quietly again, "Should we also continue making inquiries about those three medicinal materials?"

"Yes. Continue asking. Once there is news, you must inform me. If you can take them, take them. If you cannot, wait for my return!"

"This concubine remembers!"

At this point, there was no more lingering.

Luo Chen glanced at the Cheng clan cultivators, at the young man Xu Muxian left by the dock, and at the Swallow Islands cultivators craning their heads and looking from afar, then smiled faintly.

He leaped onto the Cheng clan's demon-hunting ship.

Cheng Ji, the Cheng clan's experienced navigation master, shouted, "Set sail!"

The great ship slowly started. Beneath the water, five Blue-Ringed Fang Serpents circled along as escorts.

There was also a thick black jiaolong. After rolling over and splashing countless waves, it charged at the very front of the demon-hunting ship!

(End of chapter)
