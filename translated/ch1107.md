# Chapter 1107: One Body, Two Souls, Right Envoy of the Luo Cult

That Xu Muxian could form a Nascent Soul was quite unexpected to Luo Chen.

After all, back then he had casually accepted him as a nominal disciple only because he showed some talent in medicinal herbs. In terms of cultivation, Luo Chen actually hadn't thought much of him.

The other's Dao heart was not firm, his cultivation efforts were not considered diligent, and he had started a family at a young age.

If not for Luo Chen's restraint, he would likely have lost his primordial yang before even reaching Foundation Establishment.

Yet unexpectedly, the other had later obtained such a great fortune.

Perhaps it wasn't some coincidence of fate, but rather a change in the other's state of mind—willing to leave the comfortable Swallow Islands and venture alone into the Northern Sea.

As for Cheng Haixin reaching Nascent Soul formation, that was within Luo Chen's expectations.

After Cheng Haixin reached Foundation Establishment, she began cultivating the *Azure Yang Alchemy Canon* that Luo Chen had left behind. This so-called *Azure Yang Alchemy Canon* was actually something Luo Chen had improved from the top-grade Core Formation secret art, the *Fine Dust Primordial Art*.

Later, before Luo Chen formed his Nascent Soul and returned to the Eastern Wilderness, he came to the Swallow Islands once.

That visit was not merely to be intimate with Cheng Haixin; Luo Chen had also specially left behind a large amount of cultivation resources.

Cultivation pills for the Golden Core stage, auxiliary pills, cultivation insights, experiential methods for breaking through to the Nascent Soul realm, and even a Nascent Soul Pill!

In the following centuries, Luo Chen traveled to the Northern Sea twice in total.

On those two occasions, Luo Chen did not meet with her.

First, because his affairs were rushed; second, because he did not want his appearance to affect her cultivation.

Indeed, having been affected by Huiniang's failed breakthrough, Luo Chen knew in his heart that caring too much would lead to chaos. Matters of cultivation ultimately depended on oneself!

However, on both trips to the Northern Sea, Luo Chen would use his divine sense to probe Cheng Haixin's cultivation progress.

From Golden Core mid-stage to late stage, until now, when Luo Chen had timed his return perfectly, he happened to witness her breaking through to the Nascent Soul realm.

"Although she has never left the Swallow Islands her entire life, lacking experience and combat skills, this Dao heart that can guard a small island alone and endure lonely bitter cultivation is also rare in this world."

Luo Chen felt gratified, a look of satisfaction appearing on his face.

While shutting oneself away from the world was certainly not advisable, there were also ascetic cultivation methods in this world that could likewise temper one's mindset.

At the very least, he, Luo Chen, could not stay in the same place for centuries without developing any boredom.

However, although Luo Chen was satisfied, Xu Muxian beside him appeared somewhat worried.

"Master, although the momentum of Mistress's heavenly tribulation is not vast, the methods she uses to cross it are not powerful enough either!"

Luo Chen hummed in agreement.

The intensity of the heavenly tribulation varied from person to person.

The deeper one's foundation, the more powerful the tribulation they encountered—just like Luo Chen back then, who had faced a life-and-death crisis.

Although he had not encountered the Heart Demon Tribulation, after successfully forming his Nascent Soul, he had faced the lethal tribulation of Han Zhan attempting to seize his body.

Cheng Haixin was different. Her magical treasures were only of superior grade, not reaching true artifact rank, and her Dao techniques were not particularly exquisite.

At this moment, she was quite disheveled within the tribulation lightning.

Xu Muxian couldn't help but say, "Master, why don't you lend a hand?"

Luo Chen shook his head. "Don't you know that if an outsider interferes, the power of the tribulation will increase several times over?"

Xu Muxian's admiration for Luo Chen bordered on blind worship. "But with your current Ascendant-level abilities, even if the tribulation increased by several or even dozens of times, it should be nothing to you!"

"Haha."

Luo Chen laughed self-deprecatingly.

"If I were still at the Sage realm, even if I had to face the wrath of the heavenly tribulation and have it punish me, I would forcefully help cross the tribulation. In fact, I have done such a thing before, and successfully at that."

"But the problem is that I am now at the Ascendant realm."

"Once one enters this realm, one grasps the authority of laws, the spirit merges with heaven and earth, and one's own being is almost indistinguishable from this world. If I were to act, it would be me contending against the entire heaven and earth. The intensity of the heavenly tribulation would no longer increase by several or dozens of times, but rather..."

Luo Chen took a deep breath, and before his eyes seemed to conjure the scene of when Heavenly Origin Ancestor of the Nether Origin Sect had used a self-destructive forbidden technique to draw down the heavenly tribulation to kill Rosycloud.

What vast and majestic power that was!

Even someone as strong as Rosycloud had been reduced to a miserable state, trapped on Green Parasol Mountain for decades.

He himself did not currently have Rosycloud's capabilities and could not withstand such heavenly backlash.

In fact, if he truly did lend a hand, under the wrath of the heavenly tribulation, even just the residual waves would be far stronger than the tribulation lightning Cheng Haixin was now enduring.

That would truly be good intentions leading to disaster.

"Let's see her own fortune!"

"If she can endure it, she will gain five hundred more years of life and achieve the realm of a Sage. If she fails, with me here, I can at least preserve her life. Even if she has to cultivate again and break through once more, that is not impossible."

Luo Chen's tone in saying these last words was resolute, filled with great confidence.

Hai Xin and Huiniang were different. The former had already passed the Heart Demon Tribulation and only had to face external forces. Even if she failed, it would only be some physical injuries. Huiniang, on the other hand, had truly suffered damage to her primordial spirit, and her lifespan had reached its limit. At that time, Luo Chen had truly been powerless to turn the situation around, exhausting all his efforts only to extend her life by a year.

But now, for Luo Chen to preserve Cheng Haixin's life from under the heavenly tribulation was indeed not a difficult matter.

With Luo Chen's words, Xu Muxian finally felt relieved.

Compared to the strict master, Mistress Cheng Haixin had given him more care, and back when he was building his foundation, it was largely thanks to her guidance.

That was why Xu Muxian had traveled thousands of miles upon receiving the news to rush back and protect her, and was now the one most afraid of Cheng Haixin failing.

Luo Chen, on the other hand, appeared quite magnanimous. Not only was he paying attention to Cheng Haixin's tribulation crossing, but he was also diverting his attention to another battlefield.

Although Split Heaven Stream Lord was extraordinarily powerful, having the proud record of fighting three opponents of the same realm alone at the Primordial Beginning stage, and having earned great fame after advancing to the mid stage, he was still outmatched before the strength of Kongchan, who had advanced to Great Demon Emperor centuries ago.

Especially the two true artifacts, the Morning Bell and Evening Drum, which were quite mysterious. Amplified by the chanting Buddhist sounds, their terrifying sonic power affected the soul, causing Split Heaven Stream Lord to be unable to exert even ten or twenty percent of his combat ability.

From the very beginning, Split Heaven Stream Lord had fallen into a disadvantageous position.

Moreover, that space had been constrained by Luo Chen.

He could neither fight nor flee.

Suddenly!

Luo Chen's brow raised. "Interesting, interesting!"

Xu Muxian beside him did not understand.

But in that battlefield, a strange change suddenly occurred.

A patch of black mist spread out from the wounded Split Heaven Stream Lord.

"Old ghost, if you don't act now, when will you!"

With Split Heaven Stream Lord's loud shout, a black skull sitting on a white lotus emerged from the black mist.

The white lotus petals bloomed one by one, blocking the layers of sonic waves.

Then!

Boom!

That white lotus exploded!

It forcibly blasted an opening in the killing formation that Kongchan had set up.

The black skull did not hesitate at all, turning around and fleeing through the opened gap.

As for Split Heaven Stream Lord, who was within the range of the white lotus's self-destruction, far from being able to flee, he was caught off guard and his physical body was directly blasted into powder.

Only a Nascent Soul, clutching his lifebound magical treasure and carrying his storage bag, was dazedly sucked into the Morning Bell.

Before entering, he seemed to react and muttered a curse.

"Old ghost, you will die a miserable death."

After seizing the opportunity to collect Split Heaven Stream Lord's Nascent Soul, Kongchan's qi and blood surged, and his entire body's mana was in turmoil.

He looked at the self-destructed white lotus and the small black dot that had disappeared into space and reappeared a hundred li away a breath later, a look of helplessness on his face.

A Nascent Soul escaping through the void, and the other party seemed extremely skilled in escape techniques—he could not stop them.

He truly had not expected that Split Heaven Stream Lord's body had another person's Nascent Soul dwelling within it.

But the next moment, the helpless Kongchan was stunned.

In the distance, that small black dot stopped in midair, motionless.

On its small, pitch-black face, a vivid expression of fear appeared.

Kongchan turned his head and looked into the distance. The white-robed Daoist on Sea-Watching Cliff was still standing there, as if he had done nothing at all.

But this kind of subtlety, which revealed no trace, rendered even the strongest escape technique of a Nascent Soul cultivator powerless.

"So this is the means of an Ascendant great one? Truly possessing heaven-reaching and earth-shaking abilities!"

Kongchan swallowed his saliva, drove the Morning Bell, and also collected the black Nascent Soul with its darting eyes.

Then, he quickly flew back to Luo Chen's side.

"Master, I have fulfilled my mission."

"Mm."

Luo Chen nodded slightly, took the Morning Bell, swept it with his divine sense, and couldn't help but curl the corners of his mouth.

So it was this fellow!

Old Ghost Nanqi!

An existence he had met once at Molan Court. If he was not mistaken, this person was not a native cultivator of the Northern Sea, but a Sage from the Luo Cult of the Southern Frontier, whose real name was Nan Yiji.

His position in the sect was not low, holding the honor of Right Envoy, on par with the Left Envoy of the Luo Cult, Bei Puti.

Later, the other had stolen a heavy treasure, betrayed the Luo Cult, and come to this Northern Sea cultivation world to live freely.

Who knew what great misfortune had befallen him, ending up with his physical body destroyed and only his Nascent Soul dwelling on Split Heaven Stream Lord's body.

"Master, how should we deal with these two?"

"No rush."

Luo Chen waved his hand, signaling Kongchan to put away the two Nascent Souls.

What was the most urgent matter at present was clear to everyone.

After putting away the Morning Bell, Kongchan's gaze fell upon the tribulation-crossing site at Ten-Li Ping Lake. The tribulation was now at its end. With just one look, he couldn't help but let out an exclamation of surprise.

"Although the Mistress's strength is not very impressive, the methods she has prepared for crossing the tribulation are truly numerous!"

Under the gaze of the three, and under the adoration of countless Flying Swallow cultivators, facing the final wave of tribulation lightning, Cheng Haixin had already exhausted all her means.

A talisman transformed into a blue, shimmering light screen, enveloping her entire body.

Then, a silk handkerchief swirled, a short halberd struck the lightning, and she even raised a thick tortoise shell to block before her.

Only a shocking thunderclap suddenly exploded in the void!

Boom!

The thick tortoise shell collapsed and shattered.

The silk handkerchief was torn into countless pieces, and the blue light screen derived from the talisman also could not bear the burden, dissipating into streaks of light.

But under the triple protection, this final wave of tribulation lightning was ultimately endured.

Then, sweet dew fell from the heavens, and spiritual energy poured down.

A weak Nascent Soul flew into the air, greedily accepting the gifts of heaven and earth.

Under this eager drinking, the condition of that small Nascent Soul gradually improved.

After a moment, finally unable to bear the fatigue, it disappeared from the void and returned to its physical body.

Thus, the tribulation crossing ended.

The breakthrough was successful!

On the Swallow Islands, countless people who had witnessed this scene erupted in cheers of joy.

On Sea-Watching Cliff, Xu Muxian was relieved, and Kongchan also offered his congratulations.

"Congratulations, Master! The Mistress has successfully crossed the tribulation and has finally become a Sage!"

Luo Chen was also in an excellent mood. He casually took out two bottles of pills and rewarded them to the two.

Xu Muxian did not yet understand what these pills were, but Kongchan, just by taking one sniff, showed a look of ecstasy on his face.

It was the Great Soul Pill!

The most nourishing pill for enhancing the foundation of the soul.

For demon cultivators like them, it far surpassed all cultivation-type pills.

Indeed, choosing to follow Luo Chen was the wisest decision. Just this one bottle of pills was enough to save him a hundred years of bitter cultivation.

"You two may do as you please. I will go see Hai Xin."

Leaving these words, Luo Chen drifted away.

...

Ten-Li Ping Lake, Moon Inviting Island.

In the lake, blue sea snakes were swimming freely. The pressure of the heavenly tribulation had frightened them, but after the tribulation ended, the sweet dew falling from heaven was a great gift for them.

A white-robed Daoist suddenly landed, drawing the attention of the sea snakes. Some even bared their fierce blue fangs.

But after the Daoist glanced at them, these sea snakes inexplicably lowered their heads, showing docility.

"A few centuries have passed, and they have grown fat and strong. A Blue Fang Sea Snake King has even emerged."

Luo Chen chuckled lightly and strode into the palace.

A woman with a weak aura was sitting cross-legged on a cushion. She opened her eyes and looked at Luo Chen with surprise.

"Husband, you're back!"

She struggled to stand up, but was held down by an invisible force.

"Don't act rashly. Although you have successfully crossed the heavenly tribulation, your consumption is too great. If you don't seize the time to recuperate and stabilize, there is still a risk of your realm falling."

As Luo Chen spoke, he flicked his fingers repeatedly.

One healing spell after another was cast from his hand, landing on Cheng Haixin, who had opened her heart.

Furthermore, several pills were taken out by Luo Chen and fed to her.

Gradually, the broken body nurtured new life, and the weak aura gradually became strong and robust.

After a long while.

The most dangerous moment had finally passed.

Cheng Haixin finally came to her senses.

"Husband."

Just two simple words, yet they seemed to contain a thousand words.

Luo Chen stepped forward, embraced her in his arms, and affectionately kissed her forehead.

"Stabilize your realm well. I will wait for you to emerge from seclusion."

"Mm."

The woman softly responded, her face flushed with anticipation.

She had formed her Nascent Soul, her lifespan had greatly increased, and she would have plenty of time to accompany her beloved.

But she did not notice that Luo Chen had already become an Ascendant.

After exchanging heartfelt words in the hall for a while, Luo Chen specially left behind a high-level cultivation technique for her to switch to during her seclusion.

Then, he went to the side hall.

Kongchan had already been waiting for a moment.

The Morning Bell stood quietly within the hall.

"Bring them out!" Luo Chen said calmly.

Kongchan opened the Morning Bell, and two Nascent Souls bound by spiritual light were revealed before them.

As soon as they appeared, Split Heaven Stream Lord hurriedly kowtowed and bowed.

"Spare me, great one! It was this lowly one who failed to recognize Mount Tai and offended your Dao companion. Spare me!"

Luo Chen said nothing, merely releasing his divine sense and forcibly invading the other's soul.

For someone who dared to provoke those close to him, no amount of verbal begging for mercy would make him let them go.

The soul-searching technique was overbearing and unparalleled.

In an instant, that small Nascent Soul figure showed a dazed expression.

Clearly, Luo Chen had not cared at all about preserving the other's intelligence.

Old Ghost Nanqi beside him watched, his guts trembling with fear, shivering uncontrollably.

After a moment, Luo Chen crushed the small Nascent Soul figure with one hand.

The vast spiritual energy tried to surge out, but Luo Chen suppressed it tightly, kneaded it into a ball, and tossed it aside.

Such methods were awe-inspiring.

"So, this is the so-called evil technique of devouring Nascent Souls to increase cultivation!"

Luo Chen curled his lips, showing great disdain.

With his experience, he could naturally discern the foundation of Split Heaven Stream Lord's technique at a glance.

It originated from the Soul-Refining lineage of the Primordial Demon Sect, but followed the style of the Blood Sea lineage.

Split Heaven Stream Lord had even used it incorrectly!

The Blood Sea lineage had borrowed soul techniques from the Soul-Refining lineage, but not to increase their own souls, but to refine Blood God Children.

This Split Heaven Stream Lord had the fanciful idea of devouring others' Nascent Souls to help himself grow his cultivation and condense his primordial spirit early, without considering that the lingering thoughts of the cultivator's primordial spirit would corrupt the purity of his own soul.

"According to his memories, this technique was left by Blood Nightmare Moluo, but it was you who improved it." Luo Chen's gaze shifted, looking at Old Ghost Nanqi with a half-smile. "Right Envoy of the Luo Cult, Nan Yiji. Split Heaven kindly took you in, so why did you secretly harm him like this?"

Having his dark intentions spoken aloud and his origins revealed, Old Ghost Nanqi's mind was in chaos.

"Spare me, great one! I did not intend to harm him; it was his own greed..." As Old Ghost Nanqi explained, his mind raced. No matter how much he explained, the other party would probably not let him go. The matter of targeting Cheng Haixin could not be faked. But if he were to end up like Split Heaven, with his intelligence destroyed and his Nascent Soul crushed, he absolutely could not accept that. That would completely eliminate even the chance of maintaining his intelligence and reincarnation.

Suddenly.

Old Ghost Nanqi had an idea and tentatively said, "Since you know of this junior's origins, you must have been to the Southern Frontier or come into contact with members of the Luo Cult."

Luo Chen said indifferently, "So what if I have?"

Old Ghost Nanqi gritted his teeth and said, "In that case, are you not curious about what the heavy treasure was that this junior stole from the Luo Cult back then?"

"Oh?"

Luo Chen actually became a bit interested.

(End of this chapter)
