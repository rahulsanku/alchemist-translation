# Chapter 610: So-Called Soul Essence, Duan Li's Promise

Peng Island was rather large, and there was also a mountain upon it.

When ordinary immortal cultivators chose spirit lands, they prioritized mountainsides and peaks, because heaven and earth spiritual qi had clear and turbid portions, and when gathered in great quantities formed spirit veins.

At this time, the turbid qi sank downward while the clear qi floated upward.

Although both could be absorbed and used, immortal cultivators certainly preferred clear spiritual qi, which was why cave dwellings were often placed at higher terrain.

Luo Chen was different. Relying on the power of formations, he confined high-quality spiritual qi within Star-Dipper Hall, which left the tall mountain on Peng Island idle.

Calling it idle did not mean it was entirely unused.

Occasionally, Heavenjade would go there, transform into her fighting gull body, and fly freely for a while, so she had casually built a Lake-Viewing Pavilion on it.

At this moment, inside the Lake-Viewing Pavilion.

Three Daoists borrowed the clear breeze as they drank tea and discussed the Dao.

Viewed from afar, it truly had a trace of the immortal atmosphere spoken of in mortal tales.

Only, as one of them, Xiao Buhuan was still sunk in shock at this moment and had not quite recovered.

He looked out over the boundless great marsh, with fish playing, birds circling, and countless islands large and small dotted through it like stars.

Such a blessed land had actually been rented in its entirety by the fellow Daoist before him.

Before coming, he had only assumed the Myriad Immortals Assembly had loosened its restrictions and allowed Peng Island's spirit land to be rented separately. He had never imagined Daoist Azure Yang possessed such wealth that he had directly rented all of it in one breath.

As he was dazed, Daoist Azure Yang's warm voice came beside his ear.

"Fellow Daoist? Fellow Daoist?"

Xiao Buhuan shook his head and came back to himself. "What is it?"

Luo Chen smiled faintly. "Nothing. I merely wanted to understand the garrison mission at the Quyang defensive line."

"Did you not stay there for half a month?" Xiao Buhuan was puzzled.

Luo Chen replied, "What I wish to understand is not local customs or the state of the war, but the specific details of the garrison mission. For instance, personnel arrangements and the work that must be carried out each day."

"Oh."

Xiao Buhuan suddenly understood.

Afterward, he laid it all out for Luo Chen as if counting his own treasures.

The affairs there were not especially complicated.

They were Golden Core masters, after all.

When holding down a region, how could they personally handle every matter?

Quyang Island, as the central hub of the Quyang defensive line, had over a thousand cultivators stationed there year-round.

Of the cultivators there, around eighty percent were wandering cultivators from the Myriad Immortals Assembly, and they rotated defenses every year.

They were divided into several units, each led by a late Foundation Establishment great cultivator.

Formation squads, patrol squads, law enforcement squads, logistics squads, and so on.

These units were the main force handling all sorts of trivial affairs.

What a Golden Core garrison cultivator had to do was more a matter of combat assurance. With Golden Core cultivation, they ensured that if an unexpected situation arose, they could withstand the first wave of impact while waiting for reinforcements from the rear.

On the Quyang defensive line, there were three Golden Core cultivators carrying out garrison missions in total.

The mission durations for these three were also quite different. Xiao Buhuan's previous mission, for example, had been a long-term garrison mission, while the other two were short-term missions rotating once a year.

The reason Luo Chen had not seen the other two when he went to Quyang Island last time was that they had just happened to be transferred to the first defensive line, which was the Liuyang defensive line.

"Was it not a mission in the Quyang Sea Region? How could they be transferred to the Liuyang Sea Region as well?"

Luo Chen was puzzled. Could mission locations change too?

Xiao Buhuan said helplessly, "The rules within the Myriad Immortals Assembly have not fully taken shape yet, so sometimes missions are very flexible. Especially short-term missions like rotational garrisons. Golden Core cultivators are bricks, moved wherever they are needed."

At this point, he paused.

"Of course, it also had something to do with those two fellow Daoists being only four-star demon hunters and wanting to go to the front line to hunt demon beasts and raise their star rank."

So that was how it was.

Luo Chen nodded inwardly and memorized these details.

Xiao Buhuan suddenly reacted. "Daoist Azure Yang, why are you asking about this?"

Luo Chen smiled. "To prepare for my next mission."

Xiao Buhuan and Duan Li were first startled, then understood. The other man had accepted a short-term defensive-line garrison mission.

After that, the three chatted for a while longer, and Xiao Buhuan took his leave early, rising and departing.

He had just returned and had not yet rested, so he was somewhat mentally fatigued.

Especially since some matters were still waiting for him to handle back at his Xiushan cave dwelling.

Wandering cultivators who spent large sums renting spirit vein cave dwellings did not use them only for cultivation.

An environment rich in spiritual qi could be used for many things. For example, Luo Chen had the Zhang Jiadi family conduct aquaculture on his side.

Xiao Buhuan was the same. He had hired some wandering cultivators to plant large quantities of spirit plants on Xiushan while he was out executing his mission. Although he had learned about the corresponding profits over the years through letters, he still had to properly take stock of the true situation after returning.

After Xiao Buhuan left, the atmosphere inside Lake-Viewing Pavilion sank.

Luo Chen did not speak either. He merely drank spirit tea.

Duan Li looked at the steam rising from the cup on the stone table, his expression hesitant and uncertain.

Only after a long while did he speak with difficulty.

"Fellow Daoist, could you help me with something?"

The corners of Luo Chen's mouth lifted slightly. As expected.

Duan Li had something to ask of him. Otherwise, he would not have taken the risk earlier and acted righteously.

However, Luo Chen's impression of this person was rather good. If he could help, he would not be stingy.

"Speak freely. As long as it is within my power, I will do my utmost to assist."

Duan Li's expression relaxed slightly. Then he said solemnly, "During the battle in that assessment mission, the methods of the Primordial Demon remnant were vicious and sinister. One careless move, and I was struck by his attack. I thought that a minor injury could be recovered from with treatment, but I did not expect that battle to conceal deeper mysteries. The longer it dragged on, the more the injury clung like maggots on bone, impossible to remove."

"Over these years, I collected many healing methods and finally chose one."

"For this method to take effect, it requires a Golden Core expert whose divine soul foundation far exceeds mine to assist from the side."

"After thinking it over, within this Myriad Immortals Assembly, only you, Fellow Daoist, have this ability, and only you can make me trust you."

After saying this, Duan Li looked at Luo Chen expectantly.

If he remembered correctly, during that assessment mission, the other man had faced the soul-path methods of the Primordial Demon remnant without the slightest fear.

Yesterday, when Luo Chen faced Golden Core Stage Seven Huangfu Song, there had been an invisible contest between them. Outsiders had no way of knowing about it, but Duan Li had been very close and had also been affected by Huangfu Song's Soul-Shaking Imperial Voice. At that time, he had almost lost control of himself.

Yet Luo Chen had not been affected. Instead, he had used strange methods that greatly unsettled Huangfu Song.

From this, one could see that the man opposite him had attainments in the divine soul that absolutely far exceeded his own.

To resolve the injuries on his body, in the vast Myriad Immortals Assembly, Duan Li could only choose Luo Chen.

After listening, Luo Chen said without hesitation, "First tell me the method. I will look at the specific situation."

"Mm."

A jade slip was handed before him.

Luo Chen examined it carefully.

Only after a long while did he withdraw his divine sense.

"It is not difficult. According to the description, if preparations are sufficient and things go smoothly, it should only take half a day to resolve."

"Then..." Duan Li looked at Luo Chen nervously. To prevent the other man from having concerns, he added, "I have already made the related preparations. I only need you to visit Hidden Wind Cave."

Under the other man's nervous stare, Luo Chen thought for a moment and nodded slightly.

Duan Li finally let out a breath, rose to his feet, and cupped his hands in thanks.

"Many thanks, Fellow Daoist!"

...

That day.

Luo Chen followed Duan Li to Hidden Wind Cave.

This was his second time visiting the other man's cave dwelling.

When he had come before, there had not been much arranged here. Five years later, there still had not been much change. It was clear Duan Li had been troubled by his injury and had no mind to decorate his cave residence.

After arriving at an auxiliary cavern where there was almost no spiritual qi, Duan Li raised a hand and struck out several spiritual seals.

In an instant, lines of spiritual light appeared on the cavern floor.

One formation pattern after another revealed itself under the other man's control.

At the very center, a position had been left open, with a prayer mat quietly placed upon it.

Duan Li glanced at Luo Chen, then leapt down onto the prayer mat, his whole body surrounded by the formation.

"Daoist Azure Yang, I will trouble you next."

Luo Chen smiled faintly, holding a small white flag in his hand. "Fellow Daoist, be at ease."

The next moment, Duan Li closed his eyes, held one palm upright before his chest, and slapped the other palm onto the ground.

In an instant, the formation's light blazed brightly.

The white flag in Luo Chen's hand also began trembling violently.

At the same time, Duan Li, at the center of the formation, went from calm to trembling, and pain appeared on his face.

Watching this scene, Luo Chen murmured inwardly.

'Soul-Extracting Formation. Using such a method to forcibly remove the hidden danger attached to his body.'

'Fighting poison with poison indeed. What courage, Duan Li.'

One could imagine how great a threat such a method posed to a cultivator.

Duan Li taking such a risky move, even placing the authority to direct it in Luo Chen's hands, showed how resolute his will was.

But this was also treating a dead horse as though it were alive.

Under ordinary circumstances, no cultivator would be willing to truly trust a friend with whom he did not have especially deep ties. Even Luo Chen would not.

Duan Li truly had reached the end of the road.

When faced with another person's trust, Luo Chen rarely disappointed them.

Taking a deep breath, he released the white flag in his hand, sat cross-legged on the ground, and pinched spiritual seals.

An invisible pulling force erupted from the grand formation, like a rope tied respectively to Luo Chen and Duan Li's hands.

Luo Chen pulled hard.

Immediately, Duan Li, at the center of the formation, shuddered all over.

On his face, a wisp of black qi emerged like an earthworm and was instantly pulled out by Luo Chen.

The moment the black qi was extracted, it impatiently tried to return to Duan Li's body. But how could Luo Chen let it have its way?

The white flag floating in the air rolled, and the black qi was immediately taken into it.

The first step had succeeded.

This thought appeared in Luo Chen and Duan Li's minds at the same time.

As for what came next...

Luo Chen continued according to the method, constantly repeating the earlier operation.

Only, from the first instant extraction, it later became slower and slower.

Even half a day later.

"Ah..."

A low roar like that of a wild beast emerged from Duan Li's mouth.

Luo Chen somewhat laboriously mobilized his mana.

It was not that his divine soul foundation was insufficient, but that he had to strictly control his strength. Otherwise, what he extracted would not be those strands of black qi, but Duan Li's own soul.

Just as success was about to come, Luo Chen suddenly thought:

'This black qi is almost identical to the soul essence recorded in the classics. Using this method to attack enemies is indeed vicious beyond compare.'

'If I use the Dragon-Slaying Art at Great Completion, could it achieve the effect of this Soul-Extracting Formation?'

A thought flashed past.

The next moment, Luo Chen abruptly rose and shouted.

"If you do not come out now, when will you?"

Immediately, Duan Li trembled all over.

A thick stream of black qi as wide as a finger suddenly rushed out from the crown of his head.

The white flag in the air unfurled and took in all of this black qi. Looking at it now, how was it still a white flag? It was clearly a black flag.

The formation light gradually faded.

Duan Li seemed as if he had been fished out of water, drenched in sweat as he collapsed on the prayer mat.

Although his expression was twisted from pain, the corner of his mouth was slanted in a grin, clearly the extreme joy of being relieved of a heavy burden.

Luo Chen grabbed the black flag.

"Fellow Daoist, tidy yourself first. I will wait for you outside."

Duan Li blinked and tacitly accepted.

At this moment, he truly could not open his mouth.

After a long while.

Duan Li, having washed up, finally arrived inside the cave residence.

As soon as he entered, he saw Luo Chen examining the black flag.

"What is it?"

Luo Chen pointed at the black flag. "If I am not mistaken, this black qi should be the legendary soul essence."

All living beings had three souls and seven spirits.

Formless and immaterial, soundless and traceless.

Only after reaching a high enough realm, when the souls and spirits condensed almost into substance, could they manifest visibly.

So-called Ghost Generals and Ghost Kings looked as if their bodies were solid, but most of them were actually filled out by yin qi.

Cultivators were incomparably clever. From vast quantities of souls, they used secret arts to extract the essence, forming what was called soul essence.

Rumor held that this soul essence was a rare sacred medicine.

When consumed in coordination with a secret art, it could greatly increase a cultivator's divine soul foundation.

At the same time, if someone refined soul essence and transformed it into an attack method, its power would far exceed ordinary divine soul attack arts.

Duan Li froze, then understanding appeared on his face.

"No wonder I used so many methods and could not remove these things. If it truly is soul essence, then even after its master has died, it still possesses spirituality and will continuously attack my divine soul."

"Losing to this move was not unjust for me."

Luo Chen grew interested and directly asked, "Give this soul essence to me."

Duan Li hesitated somewhat, but thinking of the pain he had endured over these years, he finally nodded.

"Fellow Daoist has helped me greatly this time. It is only a little soul essence. Take it."

At the same time, he sighed with feeling. "The maggots on bone are gone. It truly feels as if a heavy burden has been lifted. My mind is clear and my spirit refreshed. I have not felt this easy in five full years."

Although his complexion was still pale, it was no longer the deathly pallor of before.

A trace of redness was enough to prove the result.

Facing Luo Chen, Duan Li bowed.

"This time, I truly troubled you, Fellow Daoist. I, Duan, owe you a favor."

Luo Chen avoided the bow. "It was a small effort. Why be so polite? When Huangfu Song provoked me before, did you not also draw your blade to help?"

Duan Li straightened and shook his head.

"It is not the same."

It truly was not the same. Favors had weight, light and heavy.

What Duan Li had done for Luo Chen had merely added flowers to brocade. What Luo Chen had done for Duan Li was sending charcoal in the snow.

"In the future, if Fellow Daoist has need, Duan Li is willing to act with all his strength for you once. I will absolutely not break this promise."

Looking at Duan Li's incomparably solemn expression, Luo Chen swallowed the polite words he had meant to say.

At a crucial moment, having a Golden Core cultivator's assistance would count as a major help.

Duan Li was not weak.

The fact that he had been able to forcibly break open the Black Domain while facing a Primordial Demon Sect remnant of the same realm showed as much.

One had to know, that had been a true inheritance disciple of a holy land, not a true disciple from some ordinary sect.

After exhorting the other man to rest and recover properly, Luo Chen left Hidden Wind Cave and returned to Peng Lake.

After returning.

He first summoned Zhang Jiadi, praised his calm performance in the face of a great enemy, and incidentally asked about the Golden Dragon Carp breeding situation.

Afterward, he met Heavenjade and instructed her on a few miscellaneous matters.

Finally, after meeting Han Zhan once, he brought Black King with him, steered a flying boat, and flew toward the Quyang Sea Region.

The teleportation formation in Dragon Abyss Immortal City cost a considerable number of spirit stones to use in ordinary times, apart from assessment missions and wartime summons. Luo Chen was not willing to spend so extravagantly.

In any case, the Quyang Sea Region was not far from the Fulong Mountain Range. If he traveled by sea ship, it might be much slower, but steering a flying boat would be considerably faster.

Controlling the normal consumption of mana, the flying boat headed north.

Wherever it passed, a white trace appeared in the sky.

...

Five days later.

In the Quyang Sea Region, a team of patrolling cultivators stopped in their tracks.

Looking toward the horizon, they saw a mass of spiritual light breaking through the air.

Only when it drew close did they see the shape of a flying boat.

Those who dared steer a flying boat across the sea in the Northern Sea were either making a short trip through a safe area, or were confident in their own great abilities.

Clearly, the Quyang Sea Region, which battled demon beasts year-round, was absolutely not a safe area.

Then the only possibility was the latter.

The patrol captain took the initiative to go forward.

"Senior, this is the Myriad Immortals Assembly's second defensive line. Please state your origin and purpose."

On the flying boat, the red-robed Daoist casually tossed out an object.

"Four-star demon hunter Daoist Azure Yang, here to carry out a garrison mission."

The patrol captain froze, looked at the identity token in his hand, and instantly understood everything.

An ingratiating smile appeared on his face.

"Senior, this way please. The other master arrived several days ago. You are the second. Shall I introduce you?"

Luo Chen raised a brow. Naturally, he knew about the one who had arrived first.

It was precisely the demon hunter replacing Xiao Buhuan, who was also carrying out a ten-year long-term mission. If there was no mistake, he was a wandering cultivator with the Dao title Lord Lushan.

What surprised Luo Chen was that the other person carrying out a short-term garrison mission like him had not arrived yet.

"Take me over first."

*(End of Chapter)*
