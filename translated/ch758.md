# Chapter 758: Golden-Winged Great Peng, the Sword Settles Wind and Waves

"We have roughly clarified the situation in the Western Desert. The demon race and desolate beasts have indeed joined hands. Led by the Ancient Red Sand Scorpion, they command an army of hundreds of millions and are forcefully counterattacking human territory."

"Have you determined which desolate beast has joined them?"

"Golden-Winged Great Peng! Once the guardian spirit beast of Hanging Temple's Relic Court."

"So it is him! Back then, when the chief seat of Prajna Hall converted it and brought it into Buddhism, it already drew no small amount of criticism. Later, this beast stole from within and carried away thirty-six eminent monks' relics, greatly damaging Hanging Temple's vitality. Now this beast has returned, yet it is attacking the human race in a demon-desolate alliance. I wonder what that Fellow Daoist of Hanging Temple thinks."

"The strange part is precisely here! A thousand years ago, after the thirty-six relics were stolen, Hanging Temple closed its gates for a thousand years. Logically, it should have recovered its vitality long ago, yet under this demon-race counterattack, it still ignores worldly affairs. It is truly strange."

"There is no need to bother with their closed-gate matters. Now is a perfect opportunity for us to reach into the Western Desert. When the Tianyuan army repels the demon race and conquers the Western Desert, the monks of Hanging Temple will have nothing to say. Let us discuss the Northern Sea situation!"

"I will speak. In the Northern Sea, the situation is the same as our earlier judgment. Great Power Li is also quite sensible and maintains tacit understanding with us. The only thing that exceeds bounds is that he is borrowing the opportunity of the human-demon war to attempt to swallow mountains and seas and refine a spirit treasure."

"No matter. Let him do it. At that time, however much he swallows, he will have to spit back out. So long as the Fallen Sea and Yemo Heaven remain, no great harm is done. What of the passage?"

"Reporting to Sect Master, the passage is about to be completely opened. Some natural barriers are also gradually being cleared by our sect's capable people. Along the route, we also received a group of Northern Sea Sages who came to Central Continent to seek refuge with Heaven-Earth Peak."

Mo Shouzhuo slowly nodded, relief appearing in his eyes.

Once the passage from Central Continent to the Northern Sea was opened, there would be no more obstruction when Central Continent's army marched on the Northern Sea. If they cooperated with Penglai Immortal Sect, they could recover the old lands of the Northern Sea human race overnight.

From then on, the Northern Sea would have only one Ascendant sacred land, the Tianyuan Dao Sect!

As for the so-called Yuan Demon Sect, no matter how deeply its remnants hid, they would be unable to restore the demon sect's glory.

Just as he was thinking this, his brows suddenly lifted.

"Someone has been eliminated."

Within the go-board space, the Tianyuan Sages froze and began discussing at once.

"So quickly?"

"Only eight days have passed, yes?"

"Eight days in the Mountains and Seas Realm is eight hundred days in the grotto-heaven. That is not short."

"It is still short. The limit for Adept juniors is one month, while Sage true disciples can remain for one year. Only eight days have passed, and someone has already been eliminated. I imagine they gained nothing."

Eight days in the Mountains and Seas Realm amounted to roughly eight hundred days inside the grotto-heaven.

Even counting fully, that was only a little over two years.

In such a short time, whether one could capture a fourth-tier illusion beast was already hard to say, let alone borrow illusion cores to cultivate and comprehend the true intent of laws.

Someone was curious and asked Mo Shouzhuo which family's true disciple it was.

Mo Shouzhuo smiled. "It is not a true disciple. It is only a Dao protector."

A Dao protector being eliminated first was also common.

However, in this case, without a Dao protector's support, even if the true disciple remained inside, their later steps would likely be difficult, with no great gains.

Everyone asked which unlucky fellow it was.

Mo Shouzhuo calculated with his fingers and already had the answer.

"It is Xiang Qi's Dao protector, Wrathful Vajra. At this moment, he has suffered heavy injuries, his golden body is shattered, and only one breath remains to drag out his life. He is currently receiving treatment from our sect members."

Once these words came out, everyone looked at one another.

Among those seated, an old man said "I will return shortly," then left the Rotten Axe-Handle Go Board space with a worried face.

Only after he left did everyone begin discussing.

"Wrathful Vajra, is that the chief seat of Golden Mountain Temple in the Western Desert?"

"It should be him. It is said he comprehended an incomplete Three Heads Six Arms method and achieved the Great Vajra physique in the first realm of body cultivation. He is a rare body-cultivation prodigy."

"That should not be. Mirage Dragon secret realm forms its own grotto-heaven. Inside it, a cultivator's Sage domain is greatly suppressed, ineffective not only against high-level illusion beasts, but also barely affecting human cultivators. Therefore, among the Dao protectors who enter each time, high-level body cultivators are especially suitable."

A high-level body cultivator's mighty strength was entirely contained within themselves and never relied on the might of heaven and earth.

Inside Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, everyone's Sage domain was not very useful, so the advantage of high-level body cultivators stood out.

This pattern had also been gradually discovered through every five-hundred-year grand competition.

But ever since the ancient human-demon war, without any great wars, the cultivation world's main theme had become pursuing longevity. The path of body cultivation that pursued fierce and mighty power had gradually declined, so high-level body cultivators were few and far between.

In this competition, among the thirteen Dao protectors who entered Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, even though the great clans had searched painstakingly, Wrathful Vajra was the only pure body cultivator.

There were three others who merely cultivated both law and body.

As the first of the Thirty-Six Aristocratic Clans, the Xiang family had deep strength and wide connections. The Wrathful Vajra they had invited was undoubtedly the outstanding one among those four Dao protectors.

It could be said that before entering Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, the Xiang family had already seized the first opportunity.

But no one expected that Wrathful Vajra would be the first eliminated.

"Could it be that Dao protector from the Shi family?"

"Qi Xiongcheng? Impossible. Although he made his name through body-cultivation attainments, he felt his hope of longevity was gone, so he switched to qi-refining methods. It was precisely because of that that he could reach the Sage stage at the 'advanced age' of four hundred seventy and just barely catch this grand competition. With his half-baked body-cultivation attainments, he would not be Wrathful Vajra's opponent."

"But what if Shi Ju acted together with him?"

Those who knew the inside story had no choice but to consider this possibility.

Unlike the other true disciples, Shi Ju was one of the few among the thirteen who could personally take action.

If he and Qi Xiongcheng joined hands to beat Wrathful Vajra to the verge of death, it was not impossible.

But very soon, this guess was overturned.

"My son is born noble. Why would he act against this man? Moreover, Xiang Qi would have to have the courage to provoke him."

It was the Shi family head!

A late-stage Sage great cultivator, one of Heaven-Earth Peak's nineteen elders!

He gave a cold laugh. "Rather than guessing that my son acted, you may as well consider whether other people joined hands first to drive that Wrathful brat out."

The many clan representatives who had not participated in this grotto-heaven competition nodded slightly in their hearts, acknowledging this explanation.

As the first of the Thirty-Six Aristocratic Clans, the Xiang family had acted ostentatiously in recent years, even more flamboyant than the Twelve Great Houses, and had offended no small number of clans.

If one said that the great aristocratic clans had secretly joined hands to kick Xiang Qi and his Dao protector out first, that possibility truly existed.

At this moment, the nine clan representatives, including Fu Chaosheng, kept exchanging glances, all carrying hints of doubt.

They did not have this plan!

Could it be that the juniors inside had tacit understanding and made this decision together?

Mo Shouzhuo said nothing. His fingertips rubbed several go pieces, and he already had a rough idea in his heart.

Before long, the Xiang family head hurriedly returned, his face extremely gloomy.

Before the others could curiously ask questions, Mo Shouzhuo coughed lightly.

"Continue the earlier intelligence summary. Let us discuss the Eastern Wilderness! Unlike the other places, the Eastern Wilderness is the most troublesome. Before we move, we must think clearly. Otherwise, one careless mistake may easily draw the calamity of sect destruction!"

Once these words came out, everyone's expressions grew solemn.

...

Time in the outside world passed day by day, like a trickling stream flowing softly.

Inside Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, however, years passed one after another, rushing onward like the water of a great river, never to return.

Twenty years later.

In a deep and secluded empty valley.

Green orchids were faintly fragrant, and sword sounds rang clear.

A man in white robes with a jade belt around his waist held a blood sword and slowly practiced all sorts of sword moves within the valley.

Between heaven and earth, there seemed to be spiritual qi flowing with the movement of the sword tip, but it was extremely thin.

Luo Chen focused on swinging the Yuantu Sword.

Over twenty years, he had done many things.

For example, after raising Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain to grandmaster proficiency, he had also practiced Flame Dragon Descends to the grandmaster level. But after reaching this point, improving the two spells became increasingly difficult. The main reason was that such spells consumed too much magic power, and practicing them too frequently would hinder his own cultivation.

For another example, the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion had had all its restrictions completely broken by him and was fully refined and controlled. After refining it, Luo Chen gained quite a harvest. Within several clearly divided spaces inside, he unexpectedly discovered several high-level spirit herbs hard to find in the world. Presumably, they had been harvested by those Penglai Immortal Sect disciples from Wood Heaven Plain in the Demon-Fall Land, but now Luo Chen benefited from them.

The gains from refining the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion did not stop there.

His attainment in formations, in the system's evaluation, had unknowingly reached fourth-tier formation master!

This improvement had nothing to do with talent. It was purely a qualitative change produced by accumulated quantity.

Finally, there was one more thing!

That was Luo Chen taking time to complete another division of his main soul, gaining a second divided soul.

No one knew about this change, including Fu Qinglan.

At the time, Luo Chen's long-suppressed realm had risen from the first level of Sage to the second level of Sage, causing his main soul to become especially full.

Luo Chen simply formed the thought of dividing his divine soul.

This time, the pain remained, but Luo Chen had long prepared. In particular, the Soul-Splitting Art's proficiency had reached the proficient level, making the cutting of his divine soul much faster and reducing how long the pain lasted.

As he had expected, the divine soul he divided was around the late Adept stage.

At this time, it was rather weak and not yet suited for battle, and it also had negative effects!

Luo Chen discovered that when three souls existed simultaneously in his sea of consciousness, even though they were the same consciousness, they still often gave rise to distracting thoughts.

This sign was not good!

If distracting thoughts frequently arose in the sea of consciousness, it could easily draw heart tribulations and demonic obstructions. If he was careless and suffered cultivation deviation, that would be very bad.

For this reason, he thought of many methods.

In the end, he set his sights on his self-created Fine Dust Primordial Art.

Although this secret art had been self-created after he achieved the Adept stage, its grade was extremely high, reaching the fourth tier! Even after Luo Chen entered the Sage stage, he often used it.

The occasions for using it, however, were no longer in places like condensing the Golden Core or tempering magic power, but in cutting away distracting thoughts.

The reason was that when Fine Dust Primordial Art had first been created, it had incorporated a core-formation secret art called Settling Wind and Waves.

Settling Wind and Waves had come from an old acquaintance, Miao Wen.

Tracing it back to its source, however, it was the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect's Wisdom Sword Art.

Therefore, Settling Wind and Waves also possessed wonderful uses similar to Wisdom Sword Art, severing thoughts and cutting demons.

At that time, Luo Chen had not studied Settling Wind and Waves deeply. He had merely taken the parts beneficial to core formation and integrated them into Fine Dust Primordial Art.

But now, he was preparing to excavate this art deeply.

First, to cut away the awkward situation of three souls standing side by side and distracting thoughts arising wildly.

Second, to borrow this to practice sword arts.

This could be called killing two birds with one stone!

Although the Yuantu Sword had had two layers of its seals refined by him and could be freely controlled, Luo Chen did not know profound sword-control arts and could only use the Yuantu Sword's sharp characteristics.

If he took it out to face enemies like this, regardless of victory or defeat, it would ultimately waste such an unparalleled sword.

Thus, if he could grasp some methods related to the sword path, when Luo Chen activated the Yuantu Sword later, its power might be even greater.

Along the way, among Luo Chen's enormous wealth, there were also no small number of classics related to the sword path.

For example, Feiyunzi's sword pellet refining method, and Han Zhan's sword array method.

But in the end, all of these had to borrow a special magic treasure like sword pellets.

The Yuantu Sword was a genuine flying sword, with characteristics different from sword pellets, so those methods were not suitable.

Although Settling Wind and Waves was low in grade, at least flying swords and sword pellets had some commonality.

All these reasons together were why Luo Chen deeply studied Settling Wind and Waves and began practicing it from inside to outside.

"If only I had a more brilliant sword-control method."

Luo Chen sighed as he swung his sword.

In the empty valley, a faint fragrance drifted over.

It was not orchid fragrance, but a woman's fragrance.

Fu Qinglan walked out from a stone door and found a blue stone on her own, smoothing her skirt and sitting down calmly.

She quietly watched Luo Chen practice his sword and did not disturb him.

Over these years, the two had unknowingly formed this kind of tacit understanding.

Perhaps because she had sat in bitter cultivation for too long, sitting on the blue stone was not very comfortable either. Her body tilted, and she leaned her hand against the mountain rock to support her chin, continuing to watch Luo Chen practice the sword.

In her heart, however, she felt some curiosity toward this man who was like a mystery.

At first, she had only regarded him as an ordinary rogue cultivator, but outside the Peach Garden, he had easily defeated Qi Xiongcheng, making her look at him anew.

Later, she discovered that he was proficient in spells, but in the battle with Wrathful Vajra, she discovered that he also had an extremely powerful body-cultivation realm.

If that were all, then fine. At least Luo Chen still had weaknesses.

That was that he did not have a suitable true artifact!

The close-fitting black armor was a decent true artifact, but it was limited to defense.

That gray small cauldron looked extraordinary, but it was also limited to suppression and confinement, showing neither mountains nor water in direct attack.

Even back when two great experts besieged him, Luo Chen had never activated any powerful magic treasure or true artifact.

Fu Qinglan had thought that because he was a rogue cultivator, possessing two decent true artifacts after advancing to the Sage stage was already fortunate.

But now, that blood sword he was swinging was clearly a top-class sharp weapon at a glance!

"With my family's support and the sect's cultivation, after forming my Nascent Soul, I only managed to nurture one Heaven-Shrouding Leaf by relying on myself. My other true artifact was collected for me by Grandfather, and is not very handy."

"He is merely a rogue cultivator, yet he actually has three true artifacts, covering offense, defense, and suppression. Did he once have some world-shaking opportunity, or did he painstakingly refine them himself?"

"Carrying such a sharp weapon, yet not using it in great battles. Is he truly that confident in himself?"

"A pity his sword moves are too shallow, far inferior to some senior brothers in the sect who are dedicated to the sword path."

"He has a treasure in hand, yet no matching sword moves... Eh?"

In her vision, the long sword in Luo Chen's hand suddenly trembled, and a sword ray about an inch long abruptly extended and withdrew from the sword tip, piercing the ground with a finger-thick hole so deep the bottom could not be seen.

It was sword radiance!

On the path of sword cultivation, there were sword qi, sword radiance, sword heart, and sword intent, corresponding respectively to Qi Refinement, Initiate, Adept, and Sage.

But this summarized cultivation system was generally used for orthodox sword cultivators, meaning sword cultivators who controlled sword pellets.

At this moment, under Fu Qinglan's eyes, the other party had shockingly used a flying sword to produce sword radiance!

Fu Qinglan's small mouth parted. "Could it be that he is also skilled in the sword path?"

Within the empty valley.

Luo Chen stared blankly at that sword radiance. It was absolutely not the sword light emitted by the Yuantu Sword, but sword radiance produced by his own magic power.

"Could I actually have talent in the sword path?"

Amid his confusion, Luo Chen's mind glanced toward the panel unseen by others.

The grand-completion entry for Fine Dust Primordial Art was shining brilliantly.

Understanding arose in his heart.

It was not that he had sword-path talent, but that under countless circulations of this art, he had long since thoroughly mastered the Settling Wind and Waves method.

Although he had not cultivated the sword path, he possessed a sword heart.

This was attainment in the third realm of the sword path. Now, using it downward to produce sword radiance was merely a matter of course.

But it was only an Initiate-stage method and did not greatly improve him. It could only serve as a supplement.

Luo Chen smiled faintly, put away the Yuantu Sword, and walked toward Fu Qinglan.

"Is your cultivation over?"

"Mm."

"Failed again?"

"Yes!"

The woman stood up, her expression somewhat embarrassed.

Luo Chen waved a hand indifferently. "Spiritual impulse is thin, and origin is hard to find. This cannot be blamed on you. In the end, there are too few illusion cores."

Fu Qinglan lowered her head somewhat bashfully. The illusion cores Luo Chen had found for her truly could not be called few.

Although after leaving that original place, the two had lived without a fixed location and their harvest of illusion cores had not been stable.

Over twenty years, however, there were still thirty or so.

Thirty illusion cores still had not let her find wood origin. Although Luo Chen had grown used to it, she herself was almost beginning to doubt her own aptitude.

Moreover, if this continued, who knew when she could let the other party escape this predicament.

Just as she doubted herself, eyes fixed silently on the grass breaking through the soil before the tips of her shoes, Luo Chen suddenly said:

"Your situation is not actually without a solution."

Hearing this suddenly, Fu Qinglan abruptly raised her head.

"If there is a large amount of spiritual impulse for you to cultivate continuously, it will surely provide enough time to support you until you find origin power!"

A large amount of spiritual impulse!

Could it be that...

But...

"Rest properly for two days. At that time, come with me. If this succeeds, perhaps you can achieve success in one stroke!"

After Luo Chen said this, he left directly.

Fu Qinglan remained in the empty valley, her expression shifting. She felt both the regret of letting out a breath and extraordinary curiosity.

The other party had indeed not given her that Heavenly Secret Pill. This was reasonable and not unexpected.

But she was extremely curious. What method did the other party have that could find a large amount of spiritual impulse?

Was he going to hunt several illusion beasts in one breath?

She did not doubt Luo Chen's ability, but she very much doubted whether there was still any place in Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven where large numbers of high-level illusion beasts existed at the same time.

I am also a destined one, destined by birth to suffer!
