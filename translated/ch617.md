# Chapter 617: The Master's Name Spreads, an Old Acquaintance Has Arrived

Although Dragon Abyss Immortal City was broken, its area was vast.

Within it was a mountain, and upon the mountain stood a Daoist temple. Rumor said Dragon Abyss Sage cultivated inside.

Beneath the mountain was a continuous stretch of newly built palace halls.

The Demon Hunter Office headquarters was located there.

However, with the date of the Dragon Abyss Auction approaching, the Demon Hunter Office was clearly no longer the main character.

The true main character had become Huo Fang, the deputy city lord of Dragon Abyss Immortal City.

This person's realm was profound. He was one of the few Golden Core Stage Nine great cultivators in the Myriad Immortals Assembly, standing shoulder to shoulder with the Demon Hunter Office's three eight-star demon hunters.

At this moment, Huo Fang sat at the head of the hall, listening as the people below reported the auction preparations bit by bit.

People entered the hall one after another.

When a young matron in a purple palace dress stepped into the hall, Huo Fang took the initiative to rise.

"Fellow Daoist Purple Dowager has arrived."

The Purple Dowager smiled with pursed lips. Her gorgeous beauty seemed to make even this simple palace hall shine with special radiance.

"The resources gathered by the Demon Hunter Office have already been delivered. They were all carefully selected by the Demon Hunter Office and will surely not disappoint City Lord Huo Fang."

Huo Fang nodded with satisfaction, then said, "How have you considered that matter? Although my Myriad Immortals Assembly has many capable and unusual people, only someone with your appearance, your realm, and your eloquence can serve that position."

"Hehe. It is only serving once as auctioneer. Deputy City Lord, you truly know how to praise people." The Purple Dowager covered her mouth and laughed, then openly accepted.

For an auction of this scale, the host auctioneer absolutely could not drag things down.

They had to either be a famous appraisal master or a high-level cultivator who was experienced and widely knowledgeable.

Huo Fang choosing the Purple Dowager was undoubtedly the most correct choice.

Since she had accepted this task, she naturally had to carry it out perfectly.

The Purple Dowager asked Huo Fang for the auction list on the spot. She needed to familiarize herself with the details of the corresponding auction items in order to organize the introduction lines for the auction, and also think in advance of phrasing that could raise prices and heat the atmosphere.

The order on the auction list currently only had a rough outline, and the Purple Dowager also needed to plan it beforehand. She could not let precious items appear too early, leaving no heavy treasure later and causing the atmosphere to cool, but she also could not let the front be too dull. There had to be some good things to raise participants' interest.

When her gaze fell on the name of one magic instrument, the Purple Dowager's expression paused.

"Nine Dragon True Fire Canopy?"

Huo Fang, busy nearby, heard this and casually replied, "An upper-grade magic instrument sold by a small general shop in the city. One steward happened to pass by, saw it, thought it somewhat interesting, and included it in this auction process. I remember that small shop was called Azure Yang Hall. On the surface, it is only a Foundation Establishment family's business, but in truth, it is a wealth-gathering method of a Golden Core artifact-forger. What was that person called again? Daoist Azure Yang Luo Hai..."

As he spoke, Huo Fang's hand stopped moving.

"Purple Dowager, does this person seem to have a grudge with you? Do you want me to decide and remove this item?"

It was a tiny matter, not even enough to count as lifting a finger, and he was happy to sell the Purple Dowager a favor.

Unexpectedly, the Purple Dowager shook her head.

"No need. It is only an upper-grade magic instrument. There is no need to make things difficult for him over such a matter. Besides..."

She said openly and gracefully, "Daoist Azure Yang cannot truly be said to have any grudge with me. It is merely some entanglement between a junior and him."

Seeing this, Huo Fang could not help nodding.

Although wandering cultivators were said to repay the slightest grudge, that was only when their own interests were involved.

More often, they upheld the idea that stepping back one step opened the sea and sky, and they lived harmoniously with each other. The conflicts of bloodline juniors would not extend too far as they did in great sects and great clans.

The scene of beating the young only to bring out the old rarely appeared in the world of wandering cultivators.

For wandering cultivators, especially high-level wandering cultivators, the concept of family affection was somewhat faint.

Just as they set this matter aside and prepared to continue their work, a hurried rush of footsteps came from outside the hall.

Before long, a Foundation Establishment steward walked in excitedly.

"Deputy City Lord, I have collected another treasure over here."

"What treasure has made you so excited?"

"A magic treasure, water-attribute."

"Oh. Bring it here for me to see."

Magic treasures were precious, but that depended on who was being asked.

Golden Core cultivators like them more or less had one or two magic treasures in hand. Especially for the lightning tribulation when breaking through to the Nascent Soul stage, every Golden Core cultivator would spare no cost after core formation to forge a natal magic treasure suited to themselves.

Ordinary magic treasures truly did not interest Huo Fang much.

However, when that blue armor plate landed in his hand, his expression changed.

Amid shock and uncertainty, he proactively injected mana. Immediately, a blue suit of armor, carrying gurgling flowing water patterns, appeared before his eyes.

"Hm? A pure defensive magic treasure?"

The Purple Dowager, seeing this from the side, revealed surprise.

"This is a good thing. Someone is actually willing to take it out and sell it?"

In the cultivation world, offensive magic treasures were common. As long as there were suitable materials and a decent artifact-forger, they could be refined.

Even unusual magic treasures with ingenious designs were only so-so.

Only defensive magic treasures circulated in very small numbers.

First, this had to do with the importance of defensive magic treasures in protecting the cultivator themselves.

Second, it was also related to the extreme difficulty of refining defensive magic treasures.

The latter was the greatest reason.

Destruction was always simpler than construction. By the same logic, defense was harder than attack.

In the same artifact-forging process, offensive magic instruments and magic treasures only needed to consider stacking killing power, allowing a weapon to possess the ability to harm an enemy.

Even if the weapon had all sorts of flaws and various aftereffects when activated, as long as it could injure someone, that was enough.

But defensive weapons were different.

After being forged, they had to ensure there were no obvious weaknesses, so that an enemy could not easily see a flaw in battle and break it in one blow.

Just thinking about it made clear how terrifying it would be if a defensive magic treasure a cultivator trusted were found to have a flaw at a critical moment, turning a place the cultivator originally did not need to worry about into a fatal gate.

Therefore, the forging of defensive magic treasures was extremely difficult.

From material selection to refining methods, and to the engraving of suitable formations, there were all sorts of strict requirements.

Because of these requirements, defensive magic treasures had an extremely high failure rate, and refining costs were also extremely high.

Even some famous artifact-forging masters, after forging a few representative defensive magic treasures, rarely took action to refine pure defensive magic treasures again.

They preferred refining so-called weapons that combined offense and defense.

But a clever person understood with a thought that combining offense and defense meant both offense and defense had weaknesses. How could that compare to a pure defensive magic treasure?

Yet these clever people had no other choice and usually could only accept the current state.

For all these reasons, although there were many magic treasures in the cultivation world, pure defensive magic treasures were few and far between.

Now, someone was actually willing to take out this sort of treasure for auction?

Even though its grade seemed only lower-grade, it was still quite prodigal.

Huo Fang naturally understood the reasoning here. He looked at that Foundation Establishment cultivator.

"Who submitted this? Were there any additional transaction conditions?"

The Foundation Establishment cultivator said without thinking, "This treasure was submitted by Zhang Jiadi of Azure Yang Hall. There are no additional transaction conditions."

Azure Yang Hall.

Huo Fang subconsciously looked at the Purple Dowager, and the two exchanged glances.

Earlier, they had just been discussing this shop and the master behind it.

Could this treasure be owned by Daoist Azure Yang?

"I remember now. Zhang Jiadi specifically mentioned that when this treasure is auctioned, we need to help publicize its creator's reputation."

"Who?"

"Artifact-forging master Daoist Azure Yang."

This time, the Purple Dowager's expression became somewhat interesting.

This treasure was not owned by Daoist Azure Yang, but personally refined by him.

Someone who could refine a pure defensive magic treasure deserved to be called an artifact-forging master without the slightest exaggeration. One had to know, even the Myriad Immortals Assembly's most famous artifact-forging master, the Moya Cave Master, had not personally refined this kind of magic treasure in many years.

Could this Daoist Azure Yang already compare to the Moya Cave Master?

If that were truly the case, she absolutely would have stopped Huangfu Song from provoking him at his door.

Such a master-level figure only had to release word that he was willing to tailor defensive magic treasures for people, and no one knew how many people would be willing to run errands for him.

How could the handful of people under Huangfu Song compare with Daoist Azure Yang's calling power?

Clearly, Daoist Azure Yang now wanted to use the Dragon Abyss Auction to spread his name. Perhaps he wanted to prevent similar incidents from happening again.

"Should I proactively visit him and explain the last matter?"

"Daoist Azure Yang clearly left room for compromise in how he handled things before. Obviously, he also did not wish to offend me too heavily."

"But going to his door like this would somewhat lose face."

Just as the Purple Dowager's expression shifted between clear and dark, rumbling sounds came from the side.

It was Huo Fang testing the power of the magic treasure named Dark Blue Mysterious Armor.

After a long while.

Huo Fang stopped his movements and revealed a satisfied expression.

"It is indeed the purest kind of water-type defensive magic treasure. Its restraint against fire is extremely obvious. In addition, it has another subtlety, divided into two layers, inner armor and qi armor, and can also resist physical attacks."

"In terms of power, when activated at full strength, it should be able to withstand the full-force strike of an early Golden Core cultivator without damage, and it also has sufficient effect against attacks from mid Golden Core cultivators. However, for great cultivators like us, it does not form an obstacle."

"Finally, this treasure's mana consumption is somewhat beyond my expectations. It even gives me the feeling that late Foundation Establishment cultivators could activate it."

After this assessment, Huo Fang could not help clicking his tongue in praise.

"That Daoist Azure Yang's artifact-forging art has something to it. I have not seen such a high-quality lower-grade defensive magic treasure in years."

"This treasure may enter the Dragon Abyss Auction. It is best placed in the opening stage to raise the interest of both Foundation Establishment and Golden Core cultivators."

"I also agree to Daoist Azure Yang's condition."

In a few crisp strokes, Deputy City Lord Huo Fang decided the matter.

This time, he did not ask the Purple Dowager's opinion again, as if he had forgotten that he had previously said the Purple Dowager had a grudge with Daoist Azure Yang.

Clearly, private friendship could not outweigh the major matter of the Dragon Abyss Immortal City auction.

The Purple Dowager watched this scene silently and did not speak in opposition.

But in her heart, she had clearly already made a decision.

She had to make a trip to Peng Lake, and not like last time, when she observed from outside. She had to personally go in.

...

As one of the few grand events in the Myriad Immortals Assembly, the Dragon Abyss Auction attracted countless cultivators.

Among them were wandering cultivators, and also sect and clan cultivators from near Divine Origin City.

The former came to purchase resources, while the latter mostly wanted to broaden their horizons and incidentally see whether there was anything that suited them.

This auction was held for two days in total.

It was neither long nor short, but enough to make Dragon Abyss Immortal City lively.

During the auction, the name of one artifact-forging master also rose from an initially small circle of fame and became famous in one stroke.

Daoist Azure Yang, master of Peng Lake, could refine lower-grade defensive magic treasures.

For a time, after the auction ended, many cultivators tried to visit Daoist Azure Yang at Peng Lake, but all were blocked outside by the formation.

Unable to visit, they did not become discouraged. Instead, they poured into Azure Yang Hall, Daoist Azure Yang's business in Dragon Abyss Immortal City.

Although there were no defensive magic treasures sold in the shop, disappointing many Golden Core cultivators into leaving.

The boutique magic instruments and large batch of talismans in the shop made the Qi Refinement and Foundation Establishment cultivators overjoyed.

Under the rush to buy, it even forced Wen Xiu, Zhang Jiadi's Dao companion who presided over this shop, to raise prices.

Even so, supply still could not meet demand.

Facing the high prices, wandering cultivators were naturally somewhat unable to keep up, but those cultivators from Divine Origin City had rather rich fortunes.

After sensing that the magic instruments and talismans were indeed good, they did not blink at all when taking out spirit stones.

This grand scene truly shook the street where Azure Yang Hall was located, even improving the business of nearby shops somewhat.

Naturally, the other resources the Zhang family placed in Azure Yang Hall for sale also earned them a basin full of profit.

Inside the small shop.

A group of cultivators looked at the empty magic instrument shelves and voiced dissatisfaction one after another.

Only when a female cultivator appeared.

Wen Xiu looked slightly nervous, but her tone was unhurried.

"Everyone, there is no need to be anxious. Master Daoist Azure Yang will occasionally place works in our shop for consignment. In the future, there will also be better weapons."

"As long as you have time, you can come to the shop and take a look. Perhaps you will encounter something."

"In addition, our shop's other goods are all guaranteed in quality. You absolutely will not suffer a loss or be cheated."

After this speech, the buying frenzy brought by the auction finally gradually settled.

However, it could be predicted that after its name spread, Azure Yang Hall's future business absolutely would not be poor.

Outside the shop.

Two cultivators watched this scene, one expression dark, the other taking pleasure in the misfortune.

"Moya Cave Master, I did not deceive you back then, did I?"

Facing Huangfu Song's sneer, the Moya Cave Master's expression was gloomy. He admitted that he had misjudged the situation.

Unexpectedly, Daoist Azure Yang truly could refine magic treasures.

But in front of Huangfu Song, he had to hold firm.

"It is only something obtained by chance, yet he dares show it off. I refuse to believe Daoist Azure Yang can refine defensive magic treasures again in the future."

Huangfu Song blinked. "What if he truly does refine one?"

The Moya Cave Master sneered. "Would he bear to place it in this small shop for sale? Artifact-forgers like us have high costs and great risks when refining defensive magic treasures. Unless someone else commissions it, provides the materials, and guarantees the loss caused by failure during refinement, we absolutely will not easily open the furnace. Unless this Daoist Azure Yang has gone mad, how could he be willing to place a defensive magic treasure he painstakingly forged in a mere small shop to sell?"

Huangfu Song was speechless for a moment, but inwardly he sneered.

This Moya Cave Master's mouth was as hard as a dead duck's. But between his lines, was he not admitting Daoist Azure Yang's artifact-forging art?

The Moya Cave Master glanced at Huangfu Song with disgust. "Do not think of sowing discord either. What my Moya Cave mainly manages are offensive magic instruments and magic treasures. Daoist Azure Yang has not yet provoked me, so I will not act against him on your behalf. Moreover, you have already changed to another place to manage spirit land. Why still covet Peng Lake's spirit land?"

Huangfu Song's thoughts were seen through, and his face flushed red.

He opened his mouth, but in the end, he only spat out one sentence.

"That is not what I meant. I am only thinking of Fellow Daoist."

"Enough. This old man accepts your intention, but recently, I truly am busy. Do not come disturb me again."

After saying this, the Moya Cave Master flicked his sleeves and left.

Only Huangfu Song remained in place, fists clenched, his face full of unwillingness.

If Luo Chen were here, he would probably shake his head again.

Who knew how many resources had been spent to force someone with this sort of temperament up to late Golden Core.

...

As the sky approached evening.

The Moya Cave Master returned to Moya Cave.

After returning home, puzzlement appeared on his face.

"It should not be."

"In our last exchange, Daoist Azure Yang clearly had not reached the level of refining magic treasures. Why, in only one year, did he truly refine one? And a pure defensive magic treasure at that."

His muttering received a response.

"Perhaps he truly is an artifact-forging genius?"

The Moya Cave Master turned and looked toward an enormous figure with a weary expression.

The reason he was enormous was that this person's body was incomparably fat, as if oil might drip from him.

Yet his face was thin and sharp, and his hooked nose stood firm, completely opposite his body shape.

If Luo Chen were here and saw this special physique, he would recognize him at a glance.

It was Ding Yi, the wandering cultivator who had traded soul-nourishing wood with him on Cold Light Island.

"Fellow Daoist Ding, did I not tell you to watch the furnace? That treasure concerns your life. Not the slightest mistake can be allowed."

Ding Yi forced himself to focus and waved a hand. "It is no matter. I can feel that it is about to succeed. Otherwise, you would not have put down the artifact-forging work and hurried out. Am I right?"

The Moya Cave Master was speechless. The other man was truly right.

The earlier refinement had depended on him.

But the later refinement had to depend on the magic treasure's master, Ding Yi.

Since the other party felt it was about to succeed, then nine times out of ten, it truly was about to succeed.

However, to prevent accidents, he still gave instructions.

"Living-refining the Zhu Turtle Beast and merging it into that Treasure Basin Fish, the early stage is not considered key. What matters is the later nurturing. It is absolutely not the work of a day or a night. According to my estimate, it will take at least ten years. During this, not a single mistake can occur. Fellow Daoist Ding, you still need to pay attention yourself."

Ding Yi nodded. This concerned his own major matter, so he naturally would not be careless.

At present, however, he was more curious about the Daoist Azure Yang the Moya Cave Master had mentioned earlier.

"That Daoist Azure Yang has a Dao title somewhat similar to a demonic path expert I know."

"Who?"

"Devil Lord Azure Yang."

"Uh... I will not hide it from Fellow Daoist. This person truly is that Devil Lord Azure Yang."

Ding Yi's small eyes narrowed, and his expression instantly became somewhat interesting.

"I did not expect him to also be in this Myriad Immortals Assembly. I should find an opportunity to visit him."

Regarding this, the Moya Cave Master waved a hand.

"Forget that thought. He has recently entered seclusion and sees no one. Even the Purple Dowager, one of our Demon Hunter Office's three eight-star demon hunters, personally visited and was refused at the door."

Ding Yi froze.

His bearing was that great?

That was somewhat different from the person he had seen on Cold Light Island.

But at that time, Devil Lord Azure Yang had concealed himself, and even his own abilities had only been revealed during the great battle on Cold Light Island.

Thinking of Devil Lord Azure Yang's formidable points, Ding Yi's eyes narrowed slightly.

"Moya is not weak, but what he truly excels at is artifact-forging. To go to that place, his abilities are still somewhat lacking."

"By comparison, although Devil Lord Azure Yang's realm is a little lower, his true combat power is publicly acknowledged as formidable."

"If he helps, perhaps my chance of success will be higher."

"Should I discuss it with him?"

For a time, the fat cultivator calculated constantly in his heart, especially as the rumored deeds related to Devil Lord Azure Yang continuously surfaced in his mind.

Finally, he seemed to make a decision.

*(End of Chapter)*
