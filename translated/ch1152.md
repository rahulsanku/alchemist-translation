# Chapter 1152: Refining Balefulness into Astral Qi, Unforgiving Heavenly Wind

Baleful qi!

Foul, vicious, murderous baleful qi!

*Sha* (balefulness) shares its root with *sha* (kill). Utterly malevolent, utterly evil—where it exists, no foreign thing can survive.

In places of extreme danger and evil, baleful qi would often be born.

Where corresponding baleful qi existed, aside from the thing that gave birth to it, no other foreign matter could remain.

In fact, for beings steeped in slaughter, their very flesh would naturally accumulate vast amounts of baleful qi over time.

How could Luo Chen be unfamiliar with such a special existence?

Back in the Mountains and Seas Realm, he had seen—even handled—a great many types of baleful qi.

Things like Yellow Spring Yin Balefulness, Earth Fire Balefulness, and so on.

Even when he first began body refinement, breaking through to the Blood River realm had relied on Earth Fire Balefulness to temper his physique.

But such foul things were something cultivators, who prized purity of qi and clarity of spirit, would always try to avoid as much as possible.

Luo Chen was no exception.

Back when his Cleansing Art had reached Great Perfection, giving birth to a method for removing foreign qi from within his body, he had actively purged the baleful qi that had stained him.

He never imagined there would come a day when he would actively seek out baleful qi.

"Quite a waste, really," Luo Chen muttered to himself.

If he hadn't insisted on removing the baleful qi from his body, after several hundred years, the quality and quantity of baleful qi he had accumulated would surely have been astonishing.

Of course, he couldn't say that either.

If he hadn't constantly purged baleful qi back then, he wouldn't have possessed such pure and overbearing dharma power.

Chu Zhaonan, who had been tasked with delivering the baleful qi, heard Luo Chen's muttering and showed a puzzled expression.

He merely lowered his head respectfully and asked, "Sir, may I ask if you are satisfied with the quality of this batch of baleful qi?"

Luo Chen casually flicked his hand, and that strand of baleful qi obediently returned to the large earthen jar.

Turning around, Luo Chen looked at Chu Zhaonan with curiosity. "In just half a month, you've managed to find this much baleful qi for me. It seems Ruins City isn't lacking in this substance?"

Chu Zhaonan nodded. "Indeed, it's not lacking. Not just Ruins City—any of the seventy-two cities produces baleful qi in abundance. If you're not satisfied with this batch of Red Flower Balefulness, sir, we can find you something better. It would just take a bit more time."

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow slightly. He had underestimated the resource abundance of the Great Cataclysm Realm.

But upon reflection, it made perfect sense.

The more dangerous and evil a place, the more easily baleful qi appeared.

This place was called the Great Cataclysm Realm, where natural disasters and earthly tribulations occurred at the drop of a hat. An abundance of baleful qi was only natural.

His gaze swept across the earthen jars in the courtyard. Luo Chen pondered for a moment, then spoke slowly. "This batch of Red Leaf Balefulness is of acceptable quality. It should be enough for a year or two of my cultivation. But if you could find me the Nine Tribulation Earth Balefulness recorded in the ancient texts, that would be even better."

"Nine Tribulation Earth Balefulness?" Chu Zhaonan's face showed difficulty.

"What's wrong?" Luo Chen asked curiously, speaking casually. "I saw in the ancient texts of the library that Nine Tribulation Earth Balefulness is considered the finest among baleful qi. It has appeared many times before, hasn't it?"

Chu Zhaonan sighed. "Nine Tribulation Earth Balefulness has indeed appeared many times, but always in very small quantities. The main issue is that this type of balefulness, even when a tiny bit is blended into a weapon, can greatly increase its power. It's now a controlled special resource under Carefree City. Acquiring even a strand or two for you, sir, would be extremely difficult."

"Carefree City, is it?"

Luo Chen hadn't expected this complication.

He thought for a moment, then took out an object.

Chu Zhaonan looked up and saw a token. He couldn't help but exclaim in surprise, "This is the legendary Carefree Token!"

"Yes," Luo Chen nodded. "This token was given to me by the Carefree Envoy when we parted. Take it and negotiate with Carefree City. Perhaps you can trade for some Nine Tribulation Earth Balefulness on my behalf."

Even as he said this, Chu Zhaonan still didn't dare to reach for the token.

The Carefree Token represented Carefree City's recognition of a Leakless Realm expert. Among the seventy-two cities, it could be seen as a mark of legitimacy.

In fact, this was precisely why Xiao Feng had been so wary of Luo Chen earlier. Even if Luo Chen killed Xiao Feng and took the city lord's seat himself, Carefree City wouldn't punish him.

Now that Luo Chen was offering the Carefree Token so readily, how could Chu Zhaonan not suspect it might be a test?

Perhaps sensing the other's hesitation, Luo Chen chuckled softly.

"Take it. If I wanted that seat, why would I need a token as leverage?"

Chu Zhaonan opened his mouth. From this man before him, he heard nothing but unparalleled confidence.

But upon reflection, the man truly didn't care about such things.

If Luo Chen wanted to, he could destroy all the top experts of Ruins City with a flip of his hand.

No!

Ten years ago, after all the Leakless Realm experts had gone to Carefree City, with Luo Chen's current strength, he could dominate even the seventy-two cities.

With that thought, Chu Zhaonan respectfully accepted the Carefree Token.

"Also, regarding the matter of divine wind—any leads?" Luo Chen suddenly asked.

Chu Zhaonan shook his head with a bitter smile. "Sir, the thing you described is too ethereal. We haven't found any news yet."

Luo Chen frowned, then waved his hand. "Hurry up and search. You may withdraw."

Chu Zhaonan immediately took his leave.

After he left, Luo Chen ordered a large earthen jar to be brought into his cultivation chamber.

He first sat in meditation, regulating his breathing until he was in optimal condition. Then he began.

With a flick of his finger, the jar's lid flew off, and strands of red baleful qi drifted out.

Red Leaf Balefulness!

North of Ruins City stood an ancient tree that perpetually bloomed with leaves like red maple.

Countless living creatures gathered there, then died mysteriously.

Over time, dense baleful qi had formed around that ancient red-leaf tree.

Later, experts from Ruins City discovered it and sealed off the area to humans, but they couldn't stop wild beasts and livestock from seeking death there.

This batch of Red Leaf Balefulness was what Xiao Feng had spent half a month sending people to retrieve.

Watching strand after strand of baleful qi actively erode his flesh, Luo Chen remained unfazed, merely examining it carefully.

"The grade is sufficient, but the quality is lacking."

This wasn't a failure on Xiao Feng's part. This world didn't have the advanced material refinement techniques of the Mountains and Seas Realm. The fact that they had managed to retrieve this high-grade Red Leaf Balefulness using special earthen jars was already no small feat.

Luo Chen had his own methods for extracting pure Red Leaf Balefulness.

Closing his eyes and mouth, he gently inhaled.

Instantly, the Red Leaf Balefulness nearby and within the jar transformed into two streams of air, drilling into his nostrils.

At the same time, the dormant qi-blood furnace within his body began to rotate slowly.

Amid this continuous rotation, his astral qi constantly compressed the Red Leaf Balefulness, expelling large amounts of impurities.

As Luo Chen's body trembled, those impurities were discharged through the opening and closing of his acupoints.

Luo Chen discovered that this process of refining baleful qi using the qi-blood furnace also served to train the opening and closing of his acupoints—an unexpected bonus.

After he had purified the Red Leaf Balefulness to its utmost, his internal astral qi wrapped around it in a simple, crude manner.

Within this wrapping, the pure white astral qi and the vivid red baleful qi gradually merged into one.

Time passed, bit by bit.

Several days later.

Inside the cultivation chamber, Luo Chen looked at the faintly red astral qi at his fingertip, a contemplative expression in his eyes.

In truth, the orthodox body refinement methods circulating in the Great Cataclysm Realm did not actively absorb external energy to blend into astral qi.

This would compromise the purity of one's own astral qi and make control much more difficult.

Wang Yuan's self-created *Divine Astral Combat Qi* method had actually borrowed and integrated a great deal of knowledge from the qi refinement cultivation system.

Techniques for material purification, methods for absorbing spiritual qi into the dantian, even the art of controlling objects with divine sense—all had been incorporated.

By using these highly developed cultivation techniques, he had circumvented the drawbacks of merging external energy.

This reflected both Wang Yuan's insight as a martial arts grandmaster of his generation and his unavoidable circumstances.

In the Mountains and Seas Realm, body refiners were in decline.

It wasn't just that the cultivation system was incomplete; the combat system was also severely lacking.

No matter how strong a body refiner was, when facing cultivators who used dharma treasures and true artifacts, they were often at a disadvantage.

That was why Wang Yuan had racked his brains to find ways to continuously strengthen his astral qi, bringing it to a level where it could clash head-on with dharma treasures and true artifacts.

In this process, Luo Chen had actually offered many suggestions.

But back then, he was ultimately an outsider looking in, unable to see clearly.

Moreover, he had only provided help in the early and late stages. When Wang Yuan was drafting the *Divine Astral Combat Qi*, Luo Chen hadn't been present—he was in the Northern Sea cultivation world.

So Luo Chen didn't fully understand why Wang Yuan insisted on refining divine wind and earth balefulness to strengthen his astral qi.

Now, cultivating it himself, he suddenly realized the necessity.

"Baleful qi is foul and turbid, fierce and violent. Once blended into astral qi, it becomes abundant in vigor but lacking in agility!"

"The Red Leaf Balefulness I'm blending now is actually a relatively light and ethereal type of yin balefulness."

"But the Red Leaf True Astral I've refined from it still isn't agile enough. In a close-quarters martial combat, insufficient speed is a major flaw."

"Blending in divine wind is absolutely necessary!"

At this thought, Luo Chen's expression darkened.

In the Great Cataclysm Realm, baleful qi was easy to find, but divine wind was rare.

Or rather, not rare—but difficult to capture and preserve.

In this world, martial artists focused solely on tempering themselves. Their use of external objects was unremarkable, because their own bodies were the best weapons.

Unlike cultivators, who relied on dharma treasures and weapons and thus put great effort into the art of forging materials.

As a result, even if divine wind existed in the Great Cataclysm Realm, martial artists found it very difficult to preserve it intact.

Luo Chen even found himself missing the Mountains and Seas Realm.

What was called divine wind there had another name: Immaculate Wind.

He had seen many before, such as Duckweed Nether Wind, Nine Nether Immaculate Wind.

For a time, he had even possessed a dharma treasure called the Nine Wind Plantain Fan, which stored nine types of Immaculate Wind.

Oh, and there was his conveniently acquired junior apprentice brother, Lingfengzi, who possessed a wind-attribute spirit root and was skilled at using Immaculate Divine Wind in battle.

If he were cultivating the *Divine Astral Combat Qi* there, it would surely be twice the result with half the effort!

"Alas, in the end, it's all because of resource scarcity."

Luo Chen shook his head, but he wasn't overly discouraged.

He would take it one step at a time for now.

He didn't rely much on this astral qi for combat anyway; his main fighting strength still came from his powerful physical body.

Moreover, if he could truly unlock the seal on his divine sense, he could then use divine sense to control the astral qi, avoiding the drawback of insufficient agility.

And to unlock the divine sense seal, the Leakless Realm was an indispensable condition!

For now, the priority was to continue cultivating!

Suppressing the restlessness in his heart, Luo Chen continued to polish his astral qi.

...

Since arriving at Ruins City, Luo Chen had indeed been quite conspicuous for a time.

Not just his stunning performance in the arena, but even the great battle within the city lord's mansion had become known to many.

But that conspicuousness lasted only that one year.

After that, Luo Chen truly kept to himself, rarely appearing in public.

He devoted himself entirely to hard training.

In the first year, he attempted the *Divine Astral Combat Qi* method and cultivated Red Leaf True Astral.

In the third year, Xiao Feng sent Chu Zhaonan to deliver a gourd filled with Nine Tribulation Earth Balefulness.

At the time, Luo Chen was somewhat shocked.

Because the method used to store the Nine Tribulation Earth Balefulness in that gourd was unmistakably immortal cultivation technique!

And the gourd originated from Carefree City!

In other words, Carefree City—renowned as the foremost of the seventy-two cities, the sacred land of martial arts—might still have cultivators!

Luo Chen was intensely curious about this but didn't delve too deeply.

Because the time wasn't right yet.

He simply settled down, expending great effort to refine the Nine Tribulation Earth Balefulness bit by bit, transforming his Red Leaf True Astral into higher-quality Nine Tribulation True Astral.

And so, ten years passed in the blink of an eye!

Such a long time, combined with his reclusive lifestyle, had nearly made all of Ruins City forget about Luo Chen.

If not for the fact that Jing Lan could still be seen occasionally at the Benevolent Aid Hall pharmacy, ordinary people would have suspected Luo Chen had already left Ruins City.

To certain people, Luo Chen's lifestyle was far too peculiar.

Inside Xiao Mansion.

Xiao Feng listened to his subordinate's report on Luo Chen's unremarkable decade of activity, his expression subtle.

"Doesn't go out, doesn't build influence, doesn't touch power or women—is he really trying to pursue martial longevity?"

Xiao Sanniang looked dazed. "Luo Chen's lifestyle is quite similar to your father's."

Xiao Feng frowned, somewhat displeased.

"How can the two be compared? Father was called a martial maniac, but he still went out from time to time and invited peers of the same level to exchange and fight. Luo Chen's closed-door cultivation is truly unwise."

Xiao Sanniang shook her head. "Perhaps it has to do with his past as a cultivator."

Rumor had it that cultivators valued tranquility and inaction, often undergoing bitter cultivation in their caves for hundreds or thousands of years at a time.

To them, a mere ten years was but a snap of the fingers.

Xiao Feng didn't want to discuss this anymore and changed the subject.

"Sanniang, you manage the mansion's accounts. Why does Iron Vajra say you've been skimping on his resource allotment?"

Xiao Sanniang showed a bitter smile. "It's not that I'm skimping—I simply can't keep up with the supply!"

"How could it be..." Xiao Feng's words died in his throat as the answer already came to him. "Is it because of Luo Chen?"

Xiao Sanniang nodded. "All the good meat in Ruins City has to be supplied first to Luo Chen. Even the Dragon Tooth Rice must be of the finest quality. At first it was fine, but after ten years, I've reached my limit. I can only cut back on the allotments of other guest elders to ensure Luo Chen's supply."

Xiao Feng found it hard to believe. "He's just one man. How can my entire Ruins City not be able to supply him, to the point of affecting others?"

Xiao Sanniang was speechless.

Or rather, she didn't know why Luo Chen, merely a Furnace Realm martial artist, required such vast resources.

The list of supplies sent from Luo's mansion every year had been increasing steadily.

Over time, she could no longer bear the burden.

Seeing this, Xiao Feng's expression turned uncertain.

Just then, footsteps came from the entrance.

Xiao Feng looked up and saw a middle-aged man in gray robes, one sleeve hanging empty, walking in.

He was momentarily stunned, then showed a delighted expression.

"Uncle Iron, your injury has healed?"

Iron Xiong smiled. "Not only has it healed, but I've also turned misfortune into fortune. I've cultivated a true Leakless Golden Body—though the price was somewhat steep."

As he spoke, the empty right sleeve swayed gently in the draft.

That was the price.

"A true Leakless Golden Body..." Xiao Feng's eyes grew brighter. He suddenly asked, "Uncle Iron, if you were to face Luo Chen again, what do you think your chances would be?"

Hearing this question, Iron Xiong, who had been in a fairly good mood, fell silent.

Seeing this, the thought that had barely taken root in Xiao Feng's mind instantly dissipated.

The psychological shadow Luo Chen had cast over them was simply too deep.

Moreover, no one knew if Luo Chen had used his full strength in that battle.

From start to finish, against anyone, it had only taken him one move.

"Forget it." Xiao Feng slumped into his chair, dispirited. "Fulfill Luo Chen's requests as best you can. I'll explain things to the other guest elders."

No sooner had he spoken than hurried footsteps came from outside again.

Chu Zhaonan, his face weathered, entered with a joyful expression. "Reporting to the City Lord—good news!"

...

Half a year later.

Luo Chen, having finished his cultivation session, had just emerged from seclusion when he received a message sent by the city lord's mansion.

"At Mocheng, there is a desolate mountain with many wind caves within. It may contain Unforgiving Heavenly Wind."

In an instant, Luo Chen knew he would have to move.
