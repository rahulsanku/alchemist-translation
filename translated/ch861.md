# Chapter 861: In the End, a Battle Must Be Fought!

Regarding the nine great demon commanders of the demon race, Luo Chen was already no stranger.

Their surnames and given names, their origins and backgrounds, and even their general methods of combat—all had been circulated among the human race.

Naturally, portraits of their appearances had also been specially made by people.

With just one glance, Luo Chen first recognized the woman standing beside Pingshan Lord.

Jinxiu Xian. Her true form was a golden pheasant. Her beauty was such that it could topple cities and kingdoms, and one look at her was unforgettable. Last time, it was she who led the horde all the way from Fenghua Domain to Tiandu Domain, slaying many Sages along the way.

Most importantly, she was Pingshan Lord's favored consort!

"This woman was able to break the Eight Trigrams Diagram Formation of the Tiandu Sect. She is no easy opponent!"

Luo Chen muttered inwardly, his gaze sweeping over the middle-aged man standing to Pingshan Lord's left.

His complexion was sallow, and he wore a sickly expression. But if anyone truly mistook him for an ailing tiger, they would be gravely mistaken.

Huang Man!

The bloodline born to Pingshan Lord before he attained the Dao. With unremarkable talent, he had walked step by step to the realm of a Great Demon Emperor.

His temperament was violent, his methods cruel and ruthless.

He had gathered countless powerful demons under his banner, establishing his own faction. He was also hailed as the one most likely to succeed Pingshan Lord's legacy in the future.

"Is it he who has kept Di Tu suppressed and unable to lift his head?"

Luo Chen's gaze shifted to the three figures standing further behind.

At that moment, a scrutinizing gaze happened to meet his own.

A green robe, a tall and lean figure, calm yet carrying a trace of sharp, unmatched momentum.

It was Ao Kuang, the Wolf Clan's Supreme Ruler!

Luo Chen forcibly averted his gaze, feigning disinterest.

But even as he observed the remaining two, he could still feel Ao Kuang's eyes appraising him.

"Is it because Han Zhan killed Ao Xiao, the junior he favored, and I used the Grand Five Elements Sword Formation that Han Zhan left behind, that he pays me such close attention?"

Luo Chen felt his scalp tingle.

After one great battle after another, he had a very clear understanding of his own strength.

With conventional means, he could easily crush early-stage Sages. In a full-force battle, he could even roam freely among mid-stage Sages.

If he were to face an ordinary Great Demon Emperor, with his dharma body fully unleashed, combined with the Yuantu Blood Sword, or even the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger, he would still have a chance to fight!

But this Ao Kuang was by no means an ordinary Great Demon Emperor!

He was the Wolf Clan's Supreme Ruler of the Eastern Wilderness. He had once contended with Pingshan Lord before the latter achieved the Ancient Demon realm, and had also fought to a draw with Azure Frost Demon Emperor.

In terms of skill, he could be considered one of the best under the Ascendant stage in the entire Mountains and Seas Realm.

Luo Chen did not want to provoke this demon!

But even if he didn't want to, the remaining two Great Demon Emperors, following Ao Kuang's gaze, continued to size him up.

The one with a fierce face was Bi Zheng.

The one whose hands were tucked into a wide, gaudy flowered robe was Bai Zu.

These two subtly stood by Ao Kuang's side, their positioning just like the three on Pingshan Lord's side.

Could it be that these two commander-level Great Demon Emperors were led by Ao Kuang?

"Are there also factions among the demons?"

Luo Chen's question arose, and the answer came when he met Tianduzi's concerned gaze.

That's right!

Even on the human side, there were two factions: the Nether Origin Sacred Land and the other sects of the Eastern Wilderness.

How could the demons, by nature unruly and unconstrained, be any different?

That Ao Kuang had dared to contend with Pingshan Lord for supremacy when he was young showed that he had always been his own faction.

Even as Pingshan Lord achieved the Ancient Demon realm, Ao Kuang could not have submitted to him so willingly.

There would certainly be no small number of powerful demons gathered around Ao Kuang.

In just a short moment, Luo Chen had analyzed some information—whether correct or not—based on their respective positions.

Of course, this information was of no use to the current situation.

Because the protagonists of today's negotiation were the Ancient Demon and the Ascendant!

"Fellow Daoist Heize, I have given my terms. Have you considered them?" the Ancient Demon Emperor Di Tian said resolutely.

Heize Ancestor shook his head. "The great war between humans and demons has lasted for a hundred and fifty years, and still no victor has emerged. Now, you want to end this war by returning only the three domains of Badao, Huoling, and Bikong? That is somewhat stingy."

"No victor?" the Ancient Demon Emperor Di Tian sneered. "In a short time, the human race has lost fifteen domains, and no fewer than ten more have suffered great calamities. How many sects have been destroyed? How many cultivators have perished? Why don't you ask them if there are any signs of victory or defeat?"

As he spoke, his gaze mercilessly swept over Tianduzi, Shenyuan, and Luo Chen.

Heize Ancestor did not ask. He did not even turn to look at Luo Chen and the others.

He merely said calmly, "It is a fact that the sects of the Eastern Wilderness have suffered calamity. But it is also a fact that our Nether Origin Sect has not yet exerted its full strength! If you want to fight, we will oblige!"

The Ancient Demon Emperor Di Tian's expression turned stern. "And do you know how much hidden strength the Million Mountains still possess?"

Heize Ancestor shook his head. "It seems you don't want to talk. Then let's continue fighting!"

With that, he turned to leave.

As he turned, Luo Chen and the others could see the indifference on Heize Ancestor's face.

He truly did not care about continuing the fight.

But could the Nether Origin Sect bear it? What about the other sects and forces?

The aged Tianduzi's lips moved, but under the majesty of an Ascendant, he could not utter a single word.

It was said that the Eastern Wilderness human race had two major factions, but in the end, the sky had only one sun!

Leidaozi and Lingtian City Lord silently followed behind Heize Ancestor, turning to leave.

Just then, a gentle voice came from behind.

"Senior, why be so hasty? Since we are willing to meet here today, there must be some sincerity."

Heize Ancestor's footsteps halted.

He looked at the gentle, stunningly beautiful woman and praised, "Jinxiu Xian, is it? I've heard that the establishment of Pingshan Sacred Land owes three parts of its merit to you. Truly, your reputation is well-deserved!"

His stopping showed that there was room for negotiation.

Jinxiu Xian curtsied gracefully. "Senior flatters me. I dare not take the credit. The Pingshan Sacred Land relies entirely on my lord's divine might!"

After speaking, she looked at the Ancient Demon Emperor Di Tian.

"My lord, do not act on impulse. Tu'er is still in their hands."

Di Tu!

The Ancient Demon Emperor Di Tian glanced at Luo Chen, who stood silently at the very back, and snorted coldly.

At this moment, Di Tu had instead become a stepping stone.

Taking a deep breath, the Ancient Demon Emperor Di Tian slowly said, "It is not that I am being stingy. It is that your human race, over the past three thousand years, has been encroaching and breaking its word, making it impossible for us to trust you. We will give you three domains, and we will keep the six easternmost domains. The six domains in between will serve as a buffer zone. If there are conflicts in the future, we will not have to resort to direct warfare."

Heize Ancestor had a different opinion. "The spiritual vein of Badao Domain has already collapsed. It is truly a land devoid of spirit. Those three domains are effectively only two. You must give us one more domain!"

It was only then that Luo Chen, who had been watching from the side, understood why the demons had so tacitly vacated those nine domains.

This negotiation was built upon the division of those nine domains.

If the demons agreed, then the human race's actual occupied area would be twenty-five domains.

The middle area—Jiuling, Fireworks Ancient Isle, Motian Cliff, and these territories—would be left empty as a buffer zone between the two races.

Would the demons agree?

The Ancient Demon Emperor Di Tian thought for a moment, then agreed.

"Taihu Domain, we can give it to you!"

Heize Ancestor was somewhat dissatisfied. "Taihu Domain's spiritual veins are scattered, and its underground water systems crisscross. It is not suitable for human habitation. It is hardly a good place. I want Jiuling Domain!"

Jiuling Domain was the territory once controlled by the Jiuling Sect, where Jiuling Yuanjun had resided.

It was a rather special great domain among the thirty-six domains of the Eastern Wilderness.

The terrain was high and steep, with countless spirit mountains. One peak even pierced into the Nine Heavens.

Its topography was quite similar to the Heaven-Earth Peak in the Central Continent.

It was precisely this unique terrain that made it relatively easier for the Jiuling Sect to gather resources from the Nine Heavens.

It was also by relying on the sect's many years of cultivation in Jiuling Domain that Jiuling Yuanjun had been able to cultivate the Great Nine Heavens River, which rivaled the power of a divine ability.

The Ancient Demon Emperor Di Tian instinctively wanted to refuse.

But Heize Ancestor laughed. "Surely the son of Pingshan Lord is not worth this one domain?"

As if in response, Luo Chen, standing behind him, opened a spirit beast bag in front of everyone.

A flash of golden light, and Di Tu rolled to the ground.

"You!"

Even though he had heard the rumors, seeing his own brother stuffed into a spirit beast bag with his own eyes still enraged Huang Man.

A burst of demonic aura erupted, pressing down towards Luo Chen.

But the momentum came quickly and vanished just as fast.

The Ancient Demon Emperor Di Tian merely flicked his sleeve, and Huang Man's aura dissipated without a trace.

In that fleeting moment, Luo Chen's expression did not change. His face remained as calm as a still lake.

Di Tian glanced at Luo Chen and said flatly, "This son of mine is useless. He failed in his task and only tarnished my reputation. Luo Chen of the Dan Sect, is it?"

Luo Chen bowed slightly, clasping his fist in salute to show respect.

"You are very good!"

Di Tian praised him, much like how Heize Ancestor had praised his favored consort earlier.

Then, the Ancient Demon Emperor Di Tian said directly, "Jiuling Domain can be given to you. Now, let us discuss the re-establishment of the ancient covenant!"

The negotiation had finally made progress.

A smile appeared on Heize Ancestor's face.

"However, before that, I must first see if my zombie servant is still alive," Di Tian said.

Heize Ancestor laughed heartily. "With your ability, Fellow Daoist, can you not judge whether the zombie servant lives or not? Zhang Che!"

"At your command!"

Behind him, Leidaozi responded. His palm flattened, and a cage covered in lightning and thunder appeared.

Inside the cage, a ball of white light trembled.

Seeing that white light, a flash of joy passed through the Ancient Demon Emperor Di Tian's eyes.

But when he saw the thunder cage that Leidaozi had conjured out of thin air, a look of wariness appeared on his face.

"The true meaning of the law! Your sect has truly produced an extraordinary genius!"

Heize Ancestor smiled slightly. "Zhang Che is merely inheriting the legacy of his predecessors. How could he compare to the divine bird Azure Luan of Green Parasol Mountain? Speaking of which, to instigate this war between our two races, you borrowed the names of Qinghe Xian and Rosycloud. Why have I not seen those two fellow Daoists here?"

The Ancient Demon Emperor Di Tian's expression darkened. "Did your sect not send only you here as well?"

"That is true." Heize Ancestor nodded, quite approvingly.

The Ancient Demon Emperor Di Tian spoke up. "I can fully represent the Eastern Wilderness demons. Even if those two are not here, let us directly negotiate the new covenant!"

As he spoke, a scroll with dim spiritual light appeared in his hand.

Heize Ancestor also took out a scroll.

This scroll was the one signed after the great racial war that had swept across the entire Mountains and Seas Realm in ancient times.

But as the predecessors either ascended or passed away, the binding power of this covenant had become very weak.

Coupled with the people below, on both sides, speculating on the intentions of their superiors and constantly breaking the covenant, by now it could no longer restrain the two races at all.

The focus of this negotiation was on re-signing the treaty!

As for the so-called division of spiritual lands, it was merely glossed over in a few words.

But those few words were as heavy as a mountain for Luo Chen and the others!

The number of cultivators currently entrenched in the five domains of Lingtian was indeed not large.

But the forces to which they belonged were very chaotic.

Many of these forces had been forced to leave their homelands over the past hundred-plus years.

Now, with a few words, the ownership of the spiritual lands had been divided, and the human race had suffered a great loss. Where were they supposed to go?

Could the Five Elements Divine Sect still return to the Five Elements Domain?

Jiuling Domain had been solemnly demanded back by Heize Ancestor. Would it still be handed over to the Jiuling Sect?

With such a small amount of territory, they could coexist for now. But when their strength gradually recovered in the future, what would they do?

Once the new ceasefire covenant was signed, its binding power would inevitably be very strong, and few would dare to defy it.

If they could not attack the demons, would internal strife begin among the human race?

Would established sects like the Tiandu Sect be impacted?

One question after another was placed before Tianduzi, Shenyuan Zhenren, and Luo Chen.

But they dared not say anything, dared not ask.

A venerable Sage was nothing more than a slightly stronger ant in the presence of an Ascendant!

Tianduzi glanced at Luo Chen and the other, a bitter smile on his face.

He might have been seeking resonance.

But Shenyuan was hidden under his black robe, revealing nothing.

Luo Chen's expression was calm, as if none of this concerned him.

Tianduzi opened his mouth, but his bitter smile turned into disappointment.

Shenyuan Zhenren's temperament had changed drastically after the battle at Green Parasol Mountain. In his early years, he cared most about the interests of his sect, but now he cared about nothing, focusing only on himself.

Luo Chen of the Dan Sect had already agreed to join the Nether Origin Sect, and the Luo Tian Sect under his command had very few people, so he didn't need to consider these things either.

In the end, he was the only one troubled.

Time passed, bit by bit.

The content of the scrolls was constantly being deleted and added in the hands of the two Ascendants. The new treaty was gradually being revised.

They only needed to imprint their primordial spirit marks on them, and the Eastern Wilderness might once again usher in a brief period of peace.

"The war of expansion must be preserved! Otherwise, given the rate of human reproduction, internal chaos will inevitably arise in a thousand or a hundred years. They must be given a way out."

"Does that mean we will still have to fight in the future?"

"Surely Pingshan Lord does not distrust the younger generation of demons? You have already established the Pingshan Sacred Land, and I hear you plan to establish a Demon Court. Surely you are not afraid of a few ripples in the future?"

"Establish a Demon Court? I didn't expect that even such a secret matter is completely clear to you, Fellow Daoist."

"If you want no one to know, you must not do it. I can make a slight concession. This war of expansion will not only be limited to newly established Sage-level sects, but also limited to once every five hundred years. How about that?"

The Ancient Demon Emperor Di Tian fell silent.

The so-called Pingshan Sacred Land was, in the end, just his Tiger Demon clan.

Establishing a Demon Court was his lifelong ambition.

After seeing the three-thousand-year foundation of the Nether Origin Sect, the idea of completely conquering the Eastern Wilderness had become a fantasy.

But after this war, the vast majority of forces in the Million Mountains had recognized him.

Even the usually unruly Ao Kuang was willing to be driven by him.

Taking advantage of this momentum to establish a Demon Court would be the perfect way to completely unify the Eastern Wilderness demons!

But if this war ended and there was no longer an external threat, would those unruly demons still listen to him?

The Ancient Demon Emperor Di Tian glanced at Jinxiu Xian. His favored consort nodded heavily.

They, too, needed the war of expansion!

"Once every five hundred years, that is acceptable! But..." The Ancient Demon Emperor Di Tian changed his tone. "Shouldn't you return my zombie servant and my worthless son? Otherwise, it will be difficult for me to convince the masses!"

Heize Ancestor smiled slightly. "That is only right and proper! As a sign of sincerity, I will return them first! Zhang Che! Luo Chen!"

"Here!"

"Here!"

Luo Chen and Leidaozi exchanged a glance and began their actions.

In front of the Ancient Demon Emperor Di Tian, Luo Chen moved his mind. A drop of soul blood seeped from his forehead and eventually returned to Di Tu's body.

Not only that, but he also formed hand seals and struck Di Tu with a palm.

"Release!"

The enslavement seal was released as commanded.

Freed, Di Tu's face lit up with joy, and he ran towards the Ancient Demon Emperor Di Tian.

"Father!"

"Useless thing! Stand to the back!"

Di Tian scolded him, his expression unpleasant.

Di Tu's whole body trembled. He passed by Jinxiu Xian and timidly stood behind Di Tian.

At the same time, the thunder cage in Leidaozi's hand slowly opened. The white light manifested into an ethereal human form, dazedly flying towards the Ancient Demon Emperor Di Tian.

Seeing this ghost and this demon regain their freedom, the Ancient Demon Emperor Di Tian finally breathed a sigh of relief.

"Fellow Daoist, please!"

"Please!"

Di Tian first imprinted his primordial spirit mark, holding the scroll as he waited for Heize.

The other did not waste words and imprinted his own primordial spirit mark at the same time.

The two exchanged glances and swapped scrolls.

As long as the marks were imprinted again, the scrolls would take effect. With the will of the Heavenly Dao added, as long as the two of them did not die, the Eastern Wilderness would once again usher in peace.

Everything seemed to be settled?

Luo Chen's gaze fell on the scroll that Heize was gripping tightly, and he quietly stepped back.

Just then, Heize shook his head.

"The thirty-six domains of the Eastern Wilderness were carved out by the heroes of our human race, sword and blade. If I were to give away half of them with just a few words, I fear that in a thousand or a hundred years, future generations would curse my name!"

"What do you mean? Do you intend to go back on your word?" The Ancient Demon Emperor Di Tian's expression was displeased.

Tianduzi, on the other hand, heard this and showed a look of joy.

But the next moment, a voice reached his ears, making him both shocked and angry.

"Flee quickly!"

It was Luo Chen's voice transmission!

Not only could Tianduzi hear this transmission, but the two Ascendants, whose divine souls were stronger than Luo Chen's, could also hear it.

Heize Ancestor smiled slightly. Holding the scroll in his hand, another scroll appeared behind him. That scroll was as deep as ink, with rippling water within, forming a marshland of its own.

"Make your move!"

He directly pointed a finger.

The Ancient Demon Emperor Di Tian did not even look at that finger. Without thinking, he struck back with a palm.

Di Tu, who had just raised his hand to make a small move, was caught off guard and was sent flying down Biyou Mountain.

Then, looking at the flying white zombie servant, his expression turned fierce.

"I knew you were up to no good!"

At the same time, a clear, ethereal sigh came from beyond the heavens.

"In the end, a battle must be fought?"

Heize Ancestor laughed heartily. "It's the same on both sides, Fellow Daoist Qinghe Xian. Haven't you been lying in ambush for a long time? Two senior brothers, lend me a hand and pull this demon tiger into the Netherworld Yellow Spring Diagram!"

As the words fell, golden light suddenly erupted from the foot of Biyou Mountain, and a phantom flickered out.

That white zombie servant also stirred up a violent gale, sweeping everything around.

Before this, those below the Ascendant stage had already scattered like birds and beasts.

Ao Kuang, in particular, was ahead of everyone else.

Because the finger Heize Ancestor had pointed earlier was aimed at Ao Kuang!

When Ascendants fight, the fish in the pond suffer.

No one dared to stay. They all used strength they had never mustered in their lives to flee outward.

But the vast origin qi of heaven and earth surged in from all directions, instantly smashing the towering Biyou Mountain into dust.

Heaven and earth were in turmoil. No one could escape!
