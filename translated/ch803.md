# Chapter 803: The Sleeping Ice Beauty

A lake-blue silk long dress, light gauze like clouds, cascaded to the floor.

As the woman knelt, it flowed like a babbling stream spreading outward.

Her slender waist was gently bound by a sash of the same color, giving the impression that it could be encircled with a single hand.

The former cold arrogance and indifference had vanished, replaced only by traces of humiliation.

Once upon a time, she had been the lofty Heartless Fairy, while he was merely a small Initiate cultivator struggling out of the wilderness.

In that Ice Blue Palace, when they first met, he hadn't even had the courage to lift his head and look at her.

But now, things had changed, times had shifted.

With their positions reversed, she was no longer a fairy devoid of love and attachment, but a junior Adept seeking a favor.

And he, on the other hand, was the unattainable Alchemy Saint, renowned even among the top powerhouses!

If this were ordinary times, given her personality, she would rather never interact with him again than come crawling to renew this connection.

Between her and Luo Chen, there had mostly been transactions, no grudges or grievances.

If one wanted to nitpick, it would be that she had once ordered the Luo Tian Sect cultivators to the Mount Accumulated Thunder battlefield, causing heavy losses to the sect.

Thinking of Luo Chen's former vengeful nature, how he had led his forces to slaughter into the Flame Alliance, this trip would most likely end in humiliation.

But there were some things she had to do, even if there was only a sliver of a chance!

So, she had gathered her courage and come!

The surroundings were eerily quiet, only the rustling sound of the woman's trembling body and the hem of her dress brushing against the ground could be heard.

At this moment, time seemed to freeze.

In that heavy and delicate atmosphere, suddenly an invisible force fell upon the woman.

Before the Heartless Fairy could react, Luo Chen's voice had already arrived.

"We were old acquaintances after all. Meeting again is not easy. Why put on such an act?"

Supported by his dharma power, the Heartless Fairy was uncontrollably lifted to her feet.

As the feeling of humiliation gradually faded, she couldn't help but be shocked by his strength.

So this was the power of a Sage? She had no room to resist at all.

She raised her head and met Luo Chen's smiling eyes.

Still as young as back then, but there was a touch of vicissitude in his gaze.

Not daring to look directly at him, the Heartless Fairy lowered her head slightly and parted her lips.

"Your Excellency is now the famous Alchemy Saint, busy with a thousand matters and with hardly any leisure. That you would deign to see me is already a grace. How could I be discourteous?"

These words were full of worldly wisdom.

But clearly, she was not skilled at saying such things.

Thus, her speech was halting and unclear in its emphasis. One might even think she was being sarcastic.

Luo Chen chuckled lightly. Knowing her personality well, he didn't nitpick.

He casually sat down on a nearby chair and asked:

"I heard from Xiao Liu that when the Great Snowy Mountains beast tide surged out back then, Icecastle suffered extremely heavy damage, nearly to the point of annihilation, with only a few survivors?"

The Heartless Fairy's body trembled, as if the scene from that year appeared before her eyes.

She nodded painfully.

"Icecastle's strength was never great to begin with. To help Senior Sister Canglong break through to Sage, we sacrificed most of our Adept combat power."

"During the expansion war, our sect intended to assist Superior Cloud Crane, who had emerged from the Joyous Union Sect, and invested a great deal of manpower and resources."

"When the beast tide poured out of the Great Snowy Mountains, Icecastle was destroyed in an instant."

"Even though I later gathered many surviving sect members, over the past hundred years, only about a dozen remain by my side."

After hearing her account, Luo Chen couldn't help but sigh.

Icecastle had once been a formidable power among the Adept-level great sects.

With nine Adepts, it dominated the northern region of the Jade Cauldron Domain.

It commanded tens of thousands of cultivators and had numerous vassals.

Even his Luo Tian Sect had once been a vassal of Icecastle.

But before the tide of fate, it was still fragile, reduced to the pitiful scale of only a dozen or so people.

To say the Heartless Fairy still held onto the name of Icecastle was more like saying they were just a group of scattered cultivators huddling together for warmth.

And this was the fate of a major power without a Sage to guard it.

Now, the Luo Tian Sect had him as its guardian, but when the Eastern Wilderness demon army arrived in the future, its forces would surely be a thousand times greater than the Great Snowy Mountains beast tide.

How much better would the Luo Tian Sect fare than Icecastle?

Luo Chen dared not dwell on it. The joy of reuniting with an old acquaintance had been mostly dampened by Icecastle's tragedy.

"Xiao Liu said you wanted to ask me to save someone?" Luo Chen took the initiative to bring up the purpose of her visit, causing the Heartless Fairy's heart to leap with joy.

She had been afraid he would beat around the bush and deliberately avoid the topic.

Taking a deep breath, the Heartless Fairy solemnly took out a jade box from her storage bag.

Several talismans were still tightly affixed to the box.

"I know that to ask other Sages for their help, one must pay a high price. So I made inquiries and exchanged my life's savings for a wisp of firmament qi. I hope it will satisfy you."

Luo Chen swept his divine sense over it and shook his head.

The item inside the jade box was indeed a wisp of firmament qi, but it was not one of the Three Firmament Qi he wanted.

However, this was to be expected. The Heartless Fairy was only an Adept; it was difficult for her to accurately inquire about the dealings between Sages.

Moreover, firmament qi was a celestial essence of the nine heavens, only collectible by Sages who could ascend into the azure void. Its value was extremely precious.

For her to go through so much effort to obtain a wisp was already no small feat.

Therefore, he was very curious about who she wanted to save.

"You may not know this, but my transactions with fellow Daoists are always based first on the difficulty of the matter, and then on the remuneration."

"Besides, I may not necessarily be able to save the person you want to save."

"So let's first understand the specific situation!"

"Who do you want to save?"

The earlier words made the Heartless Fairy's already fair complexion turn even paler.

Fortunately, Luo Chen finally asked about the situation. She no longer hesitated and waved her hand.

A miniature fortress appeared in her palm.

Luo Chen's gaze focused. It was Icecastle's inherited treasure—the Moonlight Ice Fortress!

But unlike the fleeting glance he had caught back then in the Great Snowy Mountains, the current Moonlight Ice Fortress had lost three of its nine layers, and the remaining six were covered in mottled scars.

There were cracks from water and fire impact, marks of metal and stone collision, and even traces of being struck by thunder and lightning.

A top-grade treasure had fallen to such a state.

Dharma power flowed, and the miniature fortress slowly expanded.

When it reached two zhang in size, a layer of its gate slowly opened.

The Heartless Fairy's expression was tense as she arduously output dharma power to control the fortress.

Her technique was not very skilled; clearly, she had not truly mastered this treasure, hence the difficulty.

But even so, something finally appeared within the gate.

An ice coffin, overflowing with cold air!

*Shua!*

Luo Chen stood up, his gaze falling on the figure within the ice coffin.

Lifelike, as gentle as ever.

Her delicate brows were slightly furrowed, as if she were enduring great pain.

This sleeping ice beauty was none other than the old acquaintance Luo Chen had often thought of with gratitude.

"Senior Sister... Canglong."

*Thump!*

The Moonlight Ice Fortress fell to the ground, but the ice coffin remained suspended in midair.

However, this time, it was Luo Chen's dharma power that held the ice coffin, preventing it from falling.

Seeing this, the Heartless Fairy breathed a sigh of relief. Only the sect master and the supreme elder of their sect had the control methods for the Moonlight Ice Fortress. But with Canglong asleep and the former sect master Ice Soul long fallen, and without knowing the methods, forcibly controlling it was a tremendous burden for her.

Seeing Luo Chen staring blankly at the ice coffin, the Heartless Fairy explained: "Back then, Senior Sister failed to break through to Sage, damaging her essence, energy, and spirit. Fortunately, Sage Han Zhan of the Falling Cloud Sect helped, using a secret art to gather her remnant soul, and our sect's treasure coffin preserved her physical body. That's how she has managed to linger on until now."

Luo Chen pursed his lips. This explanation was exactly the same as what Han Zhan had told him back then.

"Originally, if nothing unexpected happened, this treasure coffin, stored in Icecastle's forbidden grounds, could have sustained Senior Sister Canglong for several hundred years. It would only be when our sect produced a Sage that she might be rescued."

"But Icecastle was destroyed. The treasure coffin lost the nourishment of ten-thousand-year profound ice, and its ability to protect Senior Sister's physical body was greatly diminished."

"Based on my estimation, at most a few more decades, and the treasure coffin will fail. By then, when Senior Sister reappears under the open sky, she will likely perish in an instant."

Luo Chen's hand hovered over the ice coffin, and he already understood.

This ice coffin was not a treasure or a true artifact, but a piece of excellent ten-thousand-year profound ice, supplemented by formations, giving it the effect of preserving the physical body from decay and isolating it from external spiritual energy erosion.

Without the support of a cold environment, this coffin would not last long.

"Have you asked anyone else to treat her?" Luo Chen asked.

The Heartless Fairy shook her head. "No."

"Why?"

"I didn't dare!"

At Luo Chen's puzzled look, the Heartless Fairy said in a low voice: "In the current times, people's hearts are like demonic ghosts. There is no one I can trust. Moreover, Senior Sister's constitution is special. If she were to fall into the hands of scoundrels, the consequences would be unimaginable."

"Special constitution?" Luo Chen frowned.

The Heartless Fairy hesitated for a moment, then steeled herself and said: "I didn't know about this matter either. Later, when the Joyous Union Sect was destroyed by a powerful Demon Emperor, I happened to encounter an Adept from the Joyous Union Sect who had escaped. During our battle, I learned that back then, Superior Cloud Crane chose Icecastle as a partner to open up the Great Snowy Mountains precisely because he coveted Senior Sister's constitution. However, with Sage Han protecting her at the time, he couldn't make a move openly."

Luo Chen's frown deepened. There was so much hidden inside information?

Han Zhan had never mentioned this!

Looking at the woman in the ice coffin with her slightly furrowed brows, Luo Chen grew impatient.

"What constitution is it exactly?"

At this point, the Heartless Fairy seemed to have given up all pretense.

She no longer hesitated and said directly: "Senior Sister Canglong possesses a Seven-Orifice Exquisite Body. Besides her qi sea, she has another heart orifice that can store vast amounts of dharma power. Even after her failed Sage breakthrough, her cultivation level did not drop, and her dharma power was still not inferior to that of an ordinary Adept Great Perfection cultivator! So Sage Han solidified her soul, our sect's ice coffin protected her body, but they were not worried about her dharma power."

"And this Seven-Orifice Exquisite Body, besides making its owner naturally intelligent and possessing abundant dharma power, has another special feature: when engaging in dual cultivation with another, it can forcibly elevate that person's cultivation level!"

"What Superior Cloud Crane coveted was precisely this. He wanted to use the Great Snowy Mountains expansion war as an opportunity to legitimately annex Icecastle and then make a move on Senior Sister."

"Senior Sister never married. Even back then, when facing such an outstanding man as Sage Han, she remained chaste. She was also afraid of sinking into depravity and becoming someone else's cauldron."

Luo Chen listened with wide eyes and gaping mouth.

He had never imagined there was such a twisted inside story.

He had always felt that Senior Sister Canglong and Han Zhan had a special relationship. The former had helped him cultivate the *Soul-Splitting Art* ahead of time when he was building his foundation, and the latter, undeterred by the threat of the Whitebone Mystic Serpent, had helped Canglong forcibly cross her tribulation. Yet, they had never ended up together.

Han Zhan's explanation was that Icecastle had focused on cultivating Canglong and forbade her from marrying out, while he himself was too proud to force the fate.

But upon careful thought, Icecastle had never had such a rule.

The Heartless Fairy had once had a Dao partner.

And her disciple, Shi Lan, had even had a daughter with Luo Tian Sect's Xu Xiao Liu, hadn't she?

Now it seemed, it was Senior Sister Canglong who had rejected Han Zhan.

Of course, Luo Chen didn't want to delve too deeply into the truth of the matter.

He looked at the ice coffin, a thoughtful expression on his face.

"According to what you said, Senior Sister possesses a special constitution that can forcibly elevate someone's cultivation level."

"You were afraid her physical body would fall into the hands of scoundrels, which means even in her slumber, her constitution still..."

"Luo Chen!"

Before he could finish, the Heartless Fairy let out a sharp cry.

She even called him by his given name.

Luo Chen looked at her coldly.

The Heartless Fairy stiffened her neck and met his gaze, but gradually, she couldn't withstand the pressure. Beads of cold sweat dripped down her fair face.

She opened her mouth and said with difficulty: "I came to you because we have a connection, and Senior Sister was very good to you back then. Now that you have a reputation as an Alchemy Saint, just like the name of the Fire Spirit Lord back then, I trust you."

"If you are dissatisfied with the firmament qi as payment, if you still resent me for forcing the Luo Tian Sect onto the battlefield back then, I... I... I am willing to..."

As she spoke, the words almost couldn't leave her lips, and she nearly bit through her thin lips.

"Say no more. I will save her!"

Luo Chen waved his hand, and the Moonlight Ice Fortress that had fallen to the ground immediately flew up.

The miscellaneous residual divine sense imprints inside were instantly obliterated.

The Moonlight Ice Fortress landed in his hand, and in a moment, with his rich experience in artifact refinement, Luo Chen understood the control techniques of this treasure.

Like the Primordial Unity Cauldron, this treasure contained spatial storage capabilities, so it could accommodate Canglong's physical body.

The Heartless Fairy seemed somewhat dazed, both by Luo Chen's words and by her own interrupted speech.

She stared blankly at Luo Chen, as if waiting for him to finish his unfinished sentence.

However, Luo Chen merely smiled.

"Senior Sister Canglong was very good to me back then. Saving her is my duty. Why would I need any payment?"

"Let her stay by my side. I will find a way to cure her."

His words were sincere, his smile warm.

His deep eyes were now open and honest, without the slightest pretense.

The woman was caught off guard, but this answer seemed to be within expectations.

She took a deep breath and bowed deeply to Luo Chen.

"Gu Xiaorou thanks Your Excellency!"

...

The Heartless Fairy left.

But only temporarily.

After learning the current situation of Icecastle's remaining cultivators, Luo Chen had Xu Xiao Liu find Yang Pingdu and instructed Yang Pingdu to recruit the female disciples of Icecastle in Luo Chen's name, so as to conveniently look after them.

After she left, Luo Chen curled his lips.

The seemingly fragile Heartless Fairy today was actually still as formidable as ever!

Her words had all been using Luo Chen's "weakness" of valuing his reputation to provoke him.

One could imagine that if Luo Chen refused to save Canglong, or had any designs on Canglong's physical body, the Heartless Fairy would likely go all out to ruin his reputation.

That would not be a good thing for Luo Chen.

His current reputation could help him build a wide network of connections, to fight against that old thing from the Five Elements Divine Sect in the future. It could also make him increasingly important in the eyes of the Nether Origin Sect, so that even if they wanted to pursue the old matter of Green Parasol Mountain in the future, they would have to consider Luo Chen's alchemy skills.

Therefore, his reputation could not be ruined!

And!

Luo Chen's gaze fell on the ice coffin, to which he had just reapplied formations, and a look of reminiscence appeared in his eyes.

Senior Sister Canglong had indeed been very good to him back then.

She had let him use Icecastle's forbidden grounds to refine the Withering-Bloom True Flame, taught him the experience and insights of forming a core, and made him understand the strengths and weaknesses of his own aptitude.

It could be said that Luo Chen owed a great deal to Senior Sister Canglong for his current cultivation.

Most importantly, Senior Sister Canglong was one of the few people who had genuinely helped him without any major ulterior motives!

Throughout his journey, Luo Chen had encountered many "benefactors."

But whether it was Mi Shuhua, the Heartless Fairy, Han Zhan, Fu Chaosheng, or others, they all seemed friendly on the surface but harbored sinister intentions.

Either they coveted his alchemy skills, or they coveted his physical body for possession, or they made him lose fifty years of his lifespan.

Only Canglong had simply wanted to form a good connection with him.

His palm caressed the cold lid of the coffin, and a sincere smile appeared on Luo Chen's face.

"She will wake up!"

(End of this chapter)
