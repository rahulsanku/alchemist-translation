# Chapter 1049: Smashing Through Ninefold Gates, a Falling Star Plummets from the Clouds

Tianhai City!

Ten li from the city lord's mansion lay a quiet complex of palaces.

A black tower stood tall within it. Once, this place had been heavily guarded—no mosquito could fly in, not a drop of water could seep through. It was the second most important location in Tianhai City, second only to the city lord's mansion itself.

But today, most of the elite troops responsible for guarding this place had been redeployed.

From time to time, streaks of flying light flew over the tower, heading toward the city gates.

A gaze withdrew from overhead. Someone sighed. "I wonder if we can hold off those beasts this time?"

The companion beside him snorted coldly. "With Sage Wen here, with the Ninefold Tianhai Gates in place, who would dare to just barge in?"

Even so, the expressions of the few Initiate cultivators nearby were grim.

Clearly, the intensity of this sea beast attack far exceeded anything they had ever imagined.

The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect had enjoyed peace for a long time—fully three thousand years without encountering a large-scale beast assault. Even the Central Continent catastrophe decades ago had been a battle of a level that these Initiate cultivators could never face directly.

Thus, the audacious assault on Tianhai City by the sea beasts had thrown many low-ranking Dao Sect cultivators into disarray.

As time passed, the battle reports coming from the front lines tightened every cultivator's heart in Tianhai City.

Flying Eagle Isle had fallen; every cultivator stationed there had died in battle.

The Liubo Mountain defensive line had been breached; a great tide of sea beasts was charging toward the river mouth.

Even Shuozhou Island, which had been fortified with the most forces, was now locked in bitter combat. It was only a matter of time before it, too, was declared lost.

Under these grim tidings, the few Initiate cultivators guarding the black tower grew heavier of heart.

"What do we do if we can't hold?"

"Even if we can't hold, we have to hold. Do you dare defy the Dao Sect's orders and abandon this teleportation array?"

The group turned to look at the black tower and fell silent.

After the Central Continent catastrophe, the earth veins of the shattered continents had been severely damaged. The teleportation array framework that had once spanned Central Continent had suffered a devastating blow.

All arrays connecting to the overseas regions had failed entirely!

Even those connecting to the inland had lost most of their function.

The teleportation array in Tianhai City was one of the few relatively intact ones. Not only did it connect directly to the Tiannan Dao Palace, but it also maintained a faint link with the Dongyuan Continent across the sea.

In recent years, Dao Sect experts on the Dongyuan Continent had been trying to fully restore the Tianhai City teleportation array, hoping to use it as a foundation to radiate across the Tiannan Continent and rebuild the once-vast Central Continent teleportation framework.

How could such an important place be abandoned so easily?

If the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect later came to investigate, they—mere Initiate cultivators—could not bear even a fraction of their wrath.

"Abandoning it is out of the question. We live or die together. Otherwise, the punishment won't fall on us alone, but on the families behind us as well." The red-faced cultivator at the lead spoke with unwavering resolve.

Just then.

*Boom! Boom! Boom!*

Three deafening sounds echoed through the vast Tianhai City, as if heaven and earth were splitting apart.

The tower-guarding cultivators exchanged glances. A terrible guess rose in their minds.

"No way..."

The next moment, a shrill, piercing scream came from the front.

"The Tianhai Gates have been breached!"

The tower-guarding cultivators swallowed hard and quickly sent low-ranking cultivators to investigate.

Soon, they learned the general situation.

A dragon-shaped archaic beast, leading a million sea beasts, had smashed through three layers of the Ninefold Tianhai Gates in one charge!

Those were the Tianhai Gates!

Forged from deep-sea divine iron by the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's artifact-refining grandmasters, tempered by heavenly thunder and earthly fire, they were absolute-defensive true artifacts. Linked to the entire city and backed by the city-protecting grand formation—how could they possibly be breached?

And breached three layers at once!

No one dared to believe it, but the facts were undeniable. Worse, bad news kept coming: the fourth gate was tottering, about to fall as well.

At this, all the tower-guarding cultivators showed expressions of terror.

Once the Ninefold Tianhai Gates fell, Tianhai City—already defenseless at the river mouth—would be completely exposed to countless sea beasts.

"Maybe we should..."

"Don't even think about it. Even if we die here, we will not retreat." The red-faced cultivator at the lead barked, crushing any improper thoughts. Then, as if to reassure them, he added, "Sage Wen hasn't acted yet. Don't panic!"

The group's spirits lifted. Right! The city lord of Tianhai City, Sage Wen, was a centuries-old Sage. He had once cultivated at the Dao Sect's ancestral court. His strength was unfathomable.

Even compared to the famed true disciples of the Dao Sect, he was not inferior.

As long as he acted, he would surely stop that dragon-shaped archaic beast!

As if answering their thoughts, a figure flew out from the city lord's mansion ten li away.

It was Sage Wen!

The moment he appeared, his Sage domain spread outward, extending toward the Tianhai Gates.

But before he could get close, the wind and clouds shifted. Above Tianhai City, demonic aura billowed wildly.

Three figures hung high in the sky. They struck together, attacking Sage Wen.

"They're fast!"

Sage Wen's expression changed drastically as he deployed his defensive measures.

But caught off guard, how could he withstand the combined assault of three Demon Emperors?

*Boom!*

With a single blow, Sage Wen was smashed back into the city lord's mansion.

The vast mansion was reduced to dust in an instant.

Every cultivator in the city who witnessed this was dumbfounded.

In the void, the three figures gradually revealed their true forms.

One held a trident, exuding kingly bearing. Another was draped in aquatic grass ribbons, graceful and alluring. The last was hideously ugly, his gaping maw slightly open, making it impossible to look him in the face.

Three Demon Emperors!

Such a lineup was despair-inducing.

But Tianhai City, the foremost fortress against sea beasts, had its own foundations!

Within two breaths, a furious roar erupted from within the city.

"Beasts! How dare you invade the Dao Sect's territory!"

The next moment, brilliant light blazed around Tianhai City. Countless streams of light surged toward the ruined city lord's mansion.

A giant of azure blue rose from the ground, wielding a long sword, majestic and overbearing.

Facing this giant formed by the array's power, the three Demon Emperors shed their contempt.

"To withstand a blow from the three of us and not die—worthy of being a Dao Sect Sage!"

"This giant is entirely composed of spiritual energy, condensed without dispersing, with immense power. It likely possesses the strength of a grand cultivator. A bit troublesome!"

"No matter. Old Man Wen has drawn on the array's power, which will inevitably weaken the Tianhai Gates. Once the Ninefold Tianhai Gates are broken, this city can be destroyed with a flick of the finger!"

The last to speak was the man holding the trident.

The seductive woman beside him asked doubtfully, "Sebo, can that thing that can't even take human form really break the Ninefold Tianhai Gates?"

Sebo sneered. "Ignorant. What do you mean, 'can't take human form'? He simply walks the path of the archaic beast, and his intelligence far exceeds that of ordinary fourth-rank archaic beasts. Most importantly, he was sent by that lord from the Northern Sea to assist us. If you don't trust him, at least trust that lord!"

The other two stiffened, clearly aware of the existence Sebo referred to.

"Enough. Prepare to deal with Old Man Wen! As long as Tianhai City stands, this giant is undying. We just need to hold him off until the black dragon smashes through the Ninefold Gates!"

At Sebo's command, the two hesitated no longer.

The three struck together, attacking the giant.

But their attention was fixed on the distant Ninefold Tianhai Gates.

A rumbling sound echoed continuously. After the black dragon broke through three gates in a row, in just a few moments, the fourth and fifth gates also shattered.

Once the sixth gate fell, the million sea beasts nearby could use it to enter the outer city.

As for the remaining three gates, they offered little resistance.

In the rolling bloodwaters, the black dragon swam upstream. Its thick hide and tough flesh made it utterly disdainful of the low-ranking cultivators attacking it. It focused all its strength on charging the sixth gate.

Opening its gaping maw, it roared. A massive orb of light began to condense and take shape.

It spat it out!

"Break!"

The orb, brimming with surging power, shot forward. As it flew, it swelled and grew, its power becoming ever more terrifying.

Such a technique baffled many.

Even the black dragon itself didn't know how it had mastered this demon art. In its hazy memories, it only recalled someone once using a similar spell to teach it a lesson, beating it black and blue.

Later, it had gradually figured it out on its own, mastering this devastatingly powerful killing move.

And the effect did not disappoint.

*Boom!*

Another thunderous crash. The sixth Tianhai Gate was blasted open with a massive hole.

The black dragon's eyes lit up. Shaking its head and tail, it charged straight through the hole.

Now, the path lay open!

"Charge, my children!"

It cheered. Behind it, countless sea beasts surged along the river mouth into the outer district of Tianhai City.

"Now, for the last three gates!"

The black dragon panted heavily, its eyes gleaming with cruel, excited light.

At the same time.

The black tower in Tianhai City trembled for an instant.

The tower-guarding cultivators were startled and hurried inside.

There, they saw a graceful figure enveloped in gentle green light materializing within the teleportation array.

This sight surprised them.

The teleportation arrays on the Tiannan Continent were not particularly stable these days. Anyone daring to force a teleportation through them had absolute confidence in their own strength.

Who was this?

The green light faded, revealing the figure's exquisite face and aloof bearing.

She glanced at the few people around her, then stepped out of the tower.

Looking up, she furrowed her brows slightly.

Her divine sense swept out, instantly grasping the entire battle situation.

Three Demon Emperors were holding Sage Wen at bay, tying up the city-protecting grand formation.

Countless land-connecting beasts had charged into the outer district and were locked in combat with the local cultivators.

But the key to the city's fate lay with the Ninefold Tianhai Gates.

"A dragon?"

The green-clad woman snorted lightly. She shot into the air, transforming into a streak of light, heading straight for the Ninefold Gates.

Her appearance was abrupt, but she made no attempt to conceal herself.

The three Demon Emperors, still entangled with the giant, naturally spotted her. They understood her intent from her direction.

"Stop her!"

But the azure giant roared with laughter. "You think you're holding me back? In truth, I'm the one holding *you* back!"

Blue light flickered across the giant's body, transforming into dozens of vile flood dragons that surrounded the battlefield from all directions. The giant sword in its hand multiplied into thousands of sword shadows, striking at the three Demon Emperors.

"Fairy Qinglan, please lift the siege on the Ninefold Gates. In a moment, a few other fellow Daoists nearby will arrive, and together we will slay these three Demon Emperors."

The green-clad woman hummed in acknowledgment and continued forward.

Soon, the black dragon, which had been gathering strength to ram the seventh gate, came into view.

Without hesitation, she opened her mouth and spat out.

A single green leaf drifted out, instantly blotting out the sky.

The leaf fell lightly, shielding the Tianhai Gate behind it.

The black dragon charged with a ferocious grin, but after one collision, it was bounced back by the resilient green light.

It froze, looking up at the newcomer.

Why did she feel... familiar?

Fairy Qinglan frowned as well. She also felt a strange familiarity with this black dragon, as if she had seen it somewhere, heard of it somewhere.

But the current situation in Tianhai City left no room for further thought.

With one hand forming a seal and the other silently chanting, she brought her hands together and slowly pushed forward.

A massive palm print took shape in an instant.

Her jade wrist turned, and the palm pressed down!

**Great Heaven Creation Palm!**

The palm print rumbled down. Heaven and earth changed color.

Others might not feel it deeply, but the black dragon, bearing the brunt of the attack, widened its eyes.

It had a natural talent—it could sense the existence of heaven and earth origin qi.

At this moment, in its perception, this palm was clearly drawing on the momentum of heaven and earth, gathering vast amounts of origin qi.

What it had to resist was not a mere spell, but a piece of heaven itself.

"A divine ability!"

The black dragon roared, spitting out a massive orb of light.

Not only that, but at an insane speed, dense layers of earthy yellow scales emerged all over its body.

*Boom!*

With a single palm strike, the great palm annihilated the orb. The residual force did not diminish but pushed forward.

It crushed countless scales and blasted the black dragon a hundred li away, its flesh torn and bloodied.

Immediately after, a wreath of colorful flowers flew over, scattering in the air and surrounding the black dragon's massive body.

But the black dragon's huge form suddenly vanished, shrinking into a mere foot-long dragonlet, dodging the wreath.

"If I hadn't exhausted so much strength breaking through six gates, I wouldn't be so easily crushed!"

The black dragon cursed viciously. Then, ignoring the three Demon Emperors, it slipped away and dove into the sea.

It fled!

In the distance, Fairy Qinglan watched this scene but did not give chase.

Instead, she turned back to help Sage Wen.

Moreover, from the left and right sides of Tianhai City, several streaks of light were racing toward them at lightning speed.

Those were also Sages!

Seeing that the situation was lost, the three Demon Emperors could only abandon their plan to raid Tianhai City and forcibly retreat.

The battle did not last long.

After Fairy Qinglan joined forces with Sage Wen, in a short time, they blasted apart the ugly Demon Emperor's physical body. Even its Sage infant was crushed by the Tianhai City grand formation.

The seductive female demon fared little better. She took a heavy blow and used the momentum to flee.

Sebo was the fastest to escape. The moment the black dragon fled, he realized the situation was hopeless. Using one companion's death and the other's injury as cover, he escaped Tianhai City unscathed.

The situation was settled!

Though countless low-ranking beasts still remained in the outer district, once the array's power returned and the Tianhai Gates were restored, those low-ranking beasts would be trapped like turtles in a jar.

Fairy Qinglan looked at Sage Wen, whose face was pale and the corner of his mouth stained with blood. "Brother Wen, are you alright?"

Sage Wen gave a bitter smile. "I'm fine. I drew on the grand formation's power in advance. I just didn't expect three Demon Emperors to strike together. What about you?"

Fairy Qinglan smoothed her hair. "That black dragon should be an archaic beast. Its physical strength is immense. My palm strike couldn't kill it. And it's slippery—it mastered the technique of shrinking and expanding at will. Even my Wreath of Colors couldn't trap it..."

As she spoke, Fairy Qinglan's brows gradually furrowed.

A black dragon, an archaic beast, the ability to shrink and expand at will, and quite cunning.

This description... how similar it was to that certain creature that had once stayed at the Fu family for a year.

And that creature was the beast servant of another person.

At this thought, Fairy Qinglan's expression shifted slightly.

"Brother Wen, I'll leave this place to you!"

Sage Wen was taken aback. "A few other fellow Daoists have also arrived. Won't you meet them?"

Fairy Qinglan waved her hand. "No. I still have a few disciples outside. I need to check on them."

With that, her figure flickered and vanished from the spot. When she reappeared, she was already several li away.

In the sky, only green light flickered. After a dozen breaths, Fairy Qinglan's figure was no longer visible.

Sage Wen opened his mouth, then could only sigh. "This junior sister really values her disciples. She's even using the Wood Concealment Art, which consumes considerable mana, to rush over."

...

Liubo Mountain.

After the great battle, it was a scene of utter devastation.

Originally, there had been ten thousand stationed cultivators. Now, only a thousand remained.

Fewer than one in ten!

A figure emerged from the seawater, his aura extremely weak.

Lin Qingtong had been searching nearby. Seeing that figure, she was overjoyed.

"Cousin! You're still alive!"

"Cough, cough."

Zhou Fuyou coughed twice. His head felt as if it were splitting, and the meridians in his body seemed torn apart.

Though he still had plenty of mana, his condition was the worst it had ever been.

"What about the others?" he asked.

The smile on Lin Qingtong's face faded. Her voice was low. "Of the twenty senior brothers and sisters who came, only eight survived. Yuhao is dead. Zhou Yuqi's lower body was crushed. Only Yuwei and I were lucky enough to escape with minor injuries."

Hearing this answer, Zhou Fuyou opened his mouth, then could only sigh in dejection.

He might not care much about family bonds or sect camaraderie, but this squad was ultimately under his command.

And Zhou Yuhao had called him "boss" at every turn, only to fall here at Liubo Mountain.

Even with Zhou Fuyou's cold temperament, his heart felt heavy now.

"That black dragon..."

Just as Zhou Fuyou was cursing the black dragon's slaughter, puzzled by its restraint, mourning his fallen comrades, and tangled in a storm of emotions, a streak of green light suddenly shot down from the sky.

He looked up, and his spirits lifted.

"It's Master!"

The green light paused over Liubo Mountain for a moment. After sweeping its divine sense over Zhou Fuyou, it left only a command to return to the city before vanishing again into the distance.

Zhou Fuyou was puzzled. "Where is Master going?"

...

Fairy Qinglan, disregarding the drain on her mana, repeatedly used the Wood Concealment Art.

Unfortunately, among the Five-Phase Escape Arts, this one was relatively slow. Especially over this endless sea, its effect was even worse.

Chasing that black dragon was quite difficult.

Moreover, Fairy Qinglan could sense that the wood-origin aura she had left on the dragon was steadily fading.

Once it faded completely, she would never find it again.

"It must be Black King!"

"Since Black King has appeared, he must be nearby!"

"What does he want by attacking the Tiannan Continent like this?"

As she murmured to herself, Fairy Qinglan suddenly stopped.

She looked up at the sky instinctively.

Amid the thick clouds, a spark of fire suddenly fell from the heavens, like a meteor, crashing onto a small island a thousand li away.

The woman's heart clenched instantly.
