# Chapter 1025: Heavenly South Dao Palace, God-Burial Ghost Valley

Traveling through the Heavenly South was not a whim of Luo Chen's.

He had harbored vague thoughts on the matter ever since learning that the Dao Sect's territory had been split into three.

And most importantly, there might be something he needed on this continent!

Of course, it was also necessary to understand the composition of the forces on the Heavenly South Continent. This would be beneficial for his clone's future rise.

Since leaving White Egret Marsh, Luo Chen had been traveling from south to north at a moderate pace.

During this process, he refined the Scarlet Firmament Sword Qi while traveling. Little White, meanwhile, roamed outside, gathering relevant intelligence for Luo Chen.

It must be said, the depth of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's foundation was beyond ordinary imagination. Even after fighting on multiple fronts, suffering the great calamity of the Demon Phoenix, and now being split into three, the strength displayed on the Heavenly South Continent remained formidable.

At the top, two Great Houses held sway!

The Zhan clan naturally had the late-stage Sage Zhan Bingtao as their pillar. As for the Mo clan, descended from the sect leader Mo Shouzhuo, they had actually nurtured two more Sages in just over a decade!

Combined with their formidable clan leader Mo Shengxing, they had stabilized the situation.

The middle-tier forces consisted of Sage families formed from various Heavenly Origin aristocratic clans and former Star Gate guest elders.

This middle tier was not weak. At least, according to the data Little White had compiled, there were over a dozen Sages.

Adding the Sages from the two Great Houses, the entire Heavenly South Continent boasted around twenty Sage-level experts!

This number would have been insignificant during the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's peak.

But for the present, it was already quite substantial.

At the very least, as Luo Chen knew, after the great war between humans and demons in the Eastern Wilderness, the number of remaining Sages there was only a little over twenty.

One could say that even the weakest of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's three continents possessed high-end combat power comparable to the entire Eastern Wilderness.

Of course, with the batch of Nascent Soul Pills Luo Chen had released, and the various sects recuperating after the war, a steady stream of new Sages would inevitably emerge.

As for the lowest tier of humble clans, Luo Chen paid them little attention.

They might have the chance to produce a Sage, but with their limited foundations, the numbers would never be large.

Aside from assessing the overall strength of the Heavenly South, another major event caught Luo Chen's attention. The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect had established an organization called the **Dao Palace** on the Heavenly South Continent. This place would replace the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect in absorbing talented disciples from this land and providing resources to cultivate them.

"Setting up a branch to maintain the sect's reputation—this must be a necessary measure the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect was forced to take after the Central Continent collapsed!" Luo Chen thought upon learning this news.

The three continents were separated by vast oceans. For high-level cultivators, this was nothing, but for low-level cultivators, it was an insurmountable chasm.

To maintain basic sect cohesion, establishing branches to absorb talent was not only necessary but also a wise move for the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

Little White added from the side, "The Heavenly South Dao Palace is overseen by the great cultivator Zhan Bingtao as its master, commanding the overall situation. Below him are four main halls, with three true Dao Sect disciples and a former Star Gate guest elder serving as hall masters. These four hall masters are all formidable and can take disciples. The resources sent from the Dao Sect's ancestral court on the Eastern Origin Continent are also controlled by them."

Zhan Bingtao?

Luo Chen silently mulled over this somewhat familiar name.

During his journey to the Southern Frontier, he had met this man and had a brief interaction with him.

He had heard earlier that this man had miraculously survived the attack of the Divine Lord Youli before his ascension. Now, it seemed he had been entrusted with the important task of guarding an entire continent.

Had his injuries healed yet?

Luo Chen only gave the man a passing thought, not paying him much attention.

With his current strength, even if Zhan Bingtao were at his peak, Luo Chen could easily defeat him.

Instead, Luo Chen was more curious about the four hall masters.

If his clone wanted to enter the core ranks of the Dao Sect in the future, he would inevitably have to deal with them.

Thus, he asked a few more questions, but unexpectedly, he learned of two more acquaintances.

Little White recalled the intelligence she had gathered and listed the origins of the four hall masters one by one.

"The Violet Star Hall is led by Zhang Bairen, the patriarch of the Sage Zhang clan, who is at the sixth layer of the Sage realm. The Eternal Life Hall is led by Long Ting, the patriarch of the Sage Long clan, at around the fifth layer. The Myriad Stars Hall is led by Hui Yuanhong, a former guest elder from the Star Gate. He is a great cultivator and the highest in realm among the four hall masters. It is said that he had already filled the vacancy of the Star Gate's Nineteenth Elder left by the Alchemy Saint's ascension..."

"Hui Yuanhong, it's him!" Luo Chen muttered. This man had once tried to recruit him, but unfortunately, Luo Chen had left the Central Continent in a hurry and they had never crossed paths again.

Logically, when Rosycloud attacked the Central Continent, Heaven-Earth Peak was the core of the assault. As one of the nineteen elders, Hui Yuanhong had actually survived.

Not only that, but the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect had appointed him as one of the four hall masters of the Heavenly South Continent.

Second only to the palace master Zhan Bingtao—truly, surviving a great disaster brings good fortune.

Little White continued her introduction.

"Finally, the Houtu Hall is led by the Azure Blue Fairy, the matriarch of the Sage Fu clan. This woman's realm is not high, only at the fourth layer of the Sage realm. However, she has cultivated for the shortest time and was the top scorer in the Grotto-Heaven Grand Competition. She possesses both the true meaning of the law and the Ascendant inheritance of the great power Chunyuan. Therefore, among the four halls, she has the highest potential and is one of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's future Ascendant seeds. It is said that many families on the Heavenly South Continent want to send their trained geniuses to the Houtu Hall, just to build a connection with the Azure Blue Fairy in advance."

Hearing the familiar name, Luo Chen was momentarily dazed, followed by a complex expression.

It was that woman!

The granddaughter of Fu Chaosheng, the pride of the Fu clan, and a true disciple of the Dao Sect whom he had once protected.

Within the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, Luo Chen had been able to take a shortcut to comprehend the true meaning of Withering-Bloom, partly due to the advancement of the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame, but also somewhat related to observing her comprehension of the Wood Origin.

After winning the top spot in the Grotto-Heaven Grand Competition, Fu Qinglan had entered the Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak of the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range, becoming a disciple of the Ascendant great power Chunyuan. Her realm had advanced rapidly, and everything had gone smoothly.

She had a good relationship with him, and there had even been some ambiguous feelings between them.

He had heard that when the Nether Origin Sect was destroyed and the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect entered the Eastern Wilderness, she had even searched for him.

Under normal circumstances, since they were friends, Luo Chen might have visited her. But Luo Chen's current predicament was, in a way, thanks to Fu Qinglan.

If he hadn't entered the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, he wouldn't have had his fate-soul severed.

Nor would he have taken this path of opposing the Dao Sect.

The grudges and entanglements were hard to untangle, and Luo Chen didn't know how to face this woman for the time being.

"Forget it, I won't visit the Dao Palace."

Luo Chen shook his head and asked about another matter.

"Little White, any leads on that front?"

Little White perked up. "There aren't many clues, but I did manage to find a few locations. They are now listed as forbidden zones, off-limits to all, which makes them perfect for us to explore."

Luo Chen's spirits lifted as well. He waved his hand. "Then let's go!"

...

**God-Burial Valley!**

One of the current forbidden zones on the Heavenly South Continent.

Its danger level was even comparable to the Fallen Dragon Abyss, guarded by the Great House Zhan.

However, due to the current shortage of Dao Sect personnel and the remote location of God-Burial Valley, its treatment was far inferior to that of the Fallen Dragon Abyss. No one had been sent to guard it. They had merely hastily set up a sealing formation, erected some boundary markers, listed it as a forbidden zone, and left it at that.

But even without guards, no cultivator dared to rashly enter God-Burial Valley.

After all, legend had it that a ghost god was buried here!

On this day, a streak of black light came from the south, landing at the outskirts of God-Burial Valley.

The flying shuttle disappeared, and Luo Chen, with Little White, arrived at the stone tablet in front of the valley.

His gaze swept over it, and Luo Chen's eyes narrowed.

Besides the three characters **God-Burial Valley**, the stone tablet also bore a eulogy with countless names. Every name belonged to a renowned expert of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect from their lifetime.

But Luo Chen didn't care about them. He only cared about four words: **Nether Abyss Ghost God**!

"Just as I thought."

Luo Chen knew this trip wouldn't be in vain.

Back when Rosycloud attacked the Central Continent, she hadn't come alone.

She had not only driven four desolate beasts but also used the spirit treasure Myriad Beast Diagram to enslave nine fifth-rank ghost gods, comparable to Ascendant great powers, from the Nether Abyss!

It was precisely because of this luxurious lineup, combined with Rosycloud's own astonishing abilities, that she had been able to shatter the Central Continent in a single battle, despite the relentless attacks of countless Dao Sect experts.

As for the aftermath of that battle, Luo Chen had gradually learned the general details over the years.

Of the four desolate beasts, the weakest, the Netherworld Earth Dragon, had died on the spot. Qinghe Immortal had been severely wounded and taken away by Rosycloud. The Ghost Immortal Tower had been pursued by Dao Sect experts and eventually returned to the Yellow Spring Sea, its corpse lying in the Soul-Severing Cliff. As for the strongest, the Golden-Winged Great Peng, after the attack on the Central Continent ended, it had relied on its formidable strength to escape back to the Western Desert.

That was the fate of the four desolate beasts—some dead, some alive, which was considered fortunate.

As for the nine ghost gods, they were not so lucky.

Their birth had been achieved by the Nether Origin Sect's Ascendant great power, Heavenly Origin Ancestor, summoning them with his life, specifically to deal with Rosycloud.

The two had a long-standing grudge, and Rosycloud had only enslaved them as expendable materials.

Facing the incarnations of great powers descending from the Star Gate's Mystic Realm, these ghost gods had suffered tremendous damage.

All!

All of the ghost gods had been left behind on the land of the Central Continent.

After their complete fall, the remnant wills of these ghost gods, sweeping along the souls of countless Dao Sect cultivators who had died tragically, formed ghostly domains on the five pieces of the shattered Central Continent.

The collapsed Western Continent had two ghostly domains, swept away by the Yellow Spring Sea.

The Northern Continent had one, the Eastern Origin Continent had two, and it was said that the Heaven-Earth Peak ruins had as many as three.

As for the last ghostly domain, it was located on the Heavenly South Continent.

That was the **God-Burial Valley** before Luo Chen!

"Master, do we really have to go in?" Little White's eyes held a trace of fear.

Luo Chen asked back, "Scared?"

Little White didn't hide it. She said fearfully, "When I was collecting low-level ghosts in the Nether Abyss, I learned many legends about ghost gods from them. These beings are all ancient existences from thousands or even tens of thousands of years ago. If not for the Nether Origin Sect suppressing them, the entire Eastern Wilderness might have been shrouded by them."

Luo Chen shook his head with a smile. "But they are all dead now."

Little White bit her lip, quite worried. "For us ghost cultivators, there are many ways to preserve our lives. Even if our physical forms are shattered, we have means to reconstitute them. Especially for ghost gods like these, whose soul-wills are comparable to the primordial spirits of great powers, they can't be so easily wiped out. The fact that the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect only listed God-Burial Valley as a forbidden zone without directly dealing with it shows the danger within. Master, please think twice!"

This wasn't Little White's fault for being afraid.

For non-human beings like demon beasts, ghost cultivators, and even gu insects, the suppression of higher-level beings was an instinct rooted in bloodline or even the depths of their souls.

But Luo Chen laughed heartily, confident.

"Just follow behind me and protect yourself."

Seeing Luo Chen's determination, Bai Meiling couldn't help but ask.

"Master, why are you so insistent?"

"Have you forgotten? My Million Baleful Souls Banner is still lacking a main baleful soul!"

Bai Meiling was stunned. Her master actually wanted to use the remnant soul of a ghost god as the main soul?

And the next moment, Luo Chen had already stepped into God-Burial Valley without any hesitation. Bai Meiling had no choice but to hurry after him.

...

**Heavenly South Dao Palace!**

As the newly established branch of the Dao Sect on the Heavenly South Continent, this vast, majestic, and dignified palace complex gathered the most top-tier experts of this land.

A year after its completion, the palace master Zhan Bingtao personally convened a meeting.

The content of the meeting was simple: to pool the strength of all parties and vigorously cultivate a new generation of disciples.

This task fell to the Four Halls and the Seven Star Courts.

The Seven Star Courts were responsible for searching for good seedlings on the Heavenly South Continent and testing the talents of geniuses sent by various families. Then, they would be handed over to the hall masters of the Four Halls for guidance and teaching.

After confirming the meeting's main purpose, Zhan Bingtao began distributing the vast resources sent from the Dao Sect's ancestral court.

The four hall masters were all eagerly anticipating this generous resource package.

But before distributing them, Zhan Bingtao issued a warning.

"You must not take this lightly or neglect the task of teaching disciples. The Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range will hold a grand competition once every hundred years, as per tradition. The rankings in that competition will affect the next resource allocation."

"So, after you take these resources, I want to see results!"

The four hall masters and the seven elders of the Star Courts all acknowledged.

With that, the meeting could be concluded.

However, a member of the Zhan clan hurriedly burst into the meeting, reporting bad news.

"Palace Master, there is unusual activity in God-Burial Valley. For the past month, the sound of ghostly wailing has been unceasing. An elder from our clan has judged that the ghost god, which was suppressed and scattered, shows signs of revival!"

Upon hearing this news, Zhan Bingtao's expression changed drastically.

One of the four hall masters below immediately said, "A ghost god reviving is comparable to an Ascendant. This matter must be reported to Ancestor Liancheng!"

But Zhan Bingtao shook his head.

"No. The ancestor is currently occupied with other matters and cannot spare the effort to deal with this ghost god."

"Then what do we do?"

Zhan Bingtao pondered for a moment before giving his judgment.

"Back then, of the nine ghost gods, some were killed, some were scattered. How could they revive so easily? Perhaps it's a misjudgment by the people below, mistaking it for the ghost god's resurgence."

"Are we going to ignore it?"

Zhan Bingtao waved his hand. "Of course not. Let us observe first before making a decision. This time, you will all come with me to God-Burial Valley. If there are indeed signs of revival, we will suppress it again!"

This was the best solution without disturbing Ancestor Liancheng.

No one had any objections. They followed Zhan Bingtao as he left the Heavenly South Dao Palace, heading towards God-Burial Valley.

(End of this chapter)
