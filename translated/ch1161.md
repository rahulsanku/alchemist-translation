# Chapter 1161: Ghostmare King, Divine Thought Unsealed

Drip, drip, drip.

The cold wind blew as raindrops pattered down one after another.

In the dim sky, a curtain of rain formed, connecting heaven and earth.

As night fell, the rain grew heavier and heavier, almost as if the Heavenly River were pouring down in reverse!

Occasionally, silver serpents darted across the sky, inspiring awe.

Inside a sturdy fortress, a fire blazed brightly in the hearth.

One after another, Dream Stones of excellent quality were devoured by the flames, emitting wisps of green smoke.

Xiangfei huddled close to the firelight, yet still felt the cold.

Tonight's Ghost Rain was on a scale larger than any in previous years.

Even as a Leakless Realm martial artist, she couldn't help but light Dream Stones, just in case.

By contrast, Luo Chen stood exposed to the vast heavens and earth without any protection, which worried her greatly.

Everyone in the world knew that the Great Cataclysm Domain had three great natural disasters: the Heavenly Wind's harassment, the Ghost Rain's bone-chilling cold, and the Great Fog that annihilated everything.

Among them, the true danger of the Ghost Rain was never the cold, but the Ghostmares it brought that could drag one into depravity!

Even Leakless Realm martial artists, with their bodies sealed from all leakage and their powerful willpower, could manage to not fear ordinary Ghostmares.

But in this world, there existed Ghostmares powerful enough to threaten Martial Dao Human Immortals!

Now, deep within Red Leaf Valley, Luo Chen had already opened every single aperture in his body.

His surging blood qi rose like smoke into the sky, erupting like a volcano, so conspicuous beneath the night sky.

And he wasn't burning large quantities of Dream Stones to dispel Ghostmares. If he truly attracted a powerful Ghostmare, he would be in grave danger.

"Perhaps... his goal is precisely to attract powerful Ghostmares?"

Xiangfei speculated.

Even though she was ignorant of formations, she could still discern the various clues Luo Chen had arranged over the years.

Especially the large number of death row prisoners he had brought!

These death row prisoners, at least First Realm Body Refinement martial artists, couldn't control the leakage of their blood qi. Under the cover of Ghost Rain, if they sank into dreams, they would inevitably attract a great number of Ghostmares.

Luo Chen was probably using these people as "bait."

And he himself was the biggest lure, personally entering the game!

She hoped he would succeed!

Xiangfei inexplicably shuddered, vaguely sensing something in the world heading toward Red Leaf Valley.

Had the Ghostmares come?

...

The Ghostmares had indeed come!

And in extremely considerable numbers!

Even Xiangfei could vaguely sense them, which showed how vast their numbers were.

Inside Red Leaf Valley.

Luo Chen's eyes were wide open, his Crimson Eyes and Golden Pupils fixed firmly on that man-made azure lake.

Above every sleeping martial artist, Ghostmares crouched, ready at any moment to invade the dreams of these death row prisoners.

"Though the numbers are many, they are still far from enough."

"Especially, I lack the kind of fearsome Ghostmare that can threaten a Leakless Realm martial artist."

"My turn."

Luo Chen slowly closed his eyes.

At some point, the ground around him was already covered with Immortal Intoxicating Smoke, all of it lit.

The thick, hazy smoke, like clouds or dragons, enveloped Luo Chen.

As he inhaled, it all entered his body.

Luo Chen did not resist; he took great, deep breaths.

This Immortal Intoxicating Smoke was all top-grade goods from the Relic Immortal City, accumulated over seventy years!

Now, with it all lit, its potency could affect even a mighty Ascendant cultivator.

Luo Chen absorbed it freely, his entire being floating in bliss, unaware of his surroundings.

Layer upon layer of illusions arose, all of them beautiful thoughts suppressed deep in his heart—the stray thoughts he had cut away time and again with his Dao Heart Sword over six hundred years.

In the illusions, heroes bowed their heads, beauties were in his arms, and countless sects from the four seas and eight wastelands came to pay homage.

Even immortals descended, personally guiding him to ascend to the upper realm.

Even the Return to Void that had trapped him his entire life was merely a puff of smoke, laughed away in casual conversation.

Gradually, Luo Chen fell asleep.

His surging blood qi was no longer restrained in any way, flowing freely through his body.

His suppressed body naturally began to expand and grow, until it reached a thousand zhang in size.

Had he unconsciously activated a divine ability?

No!

Perhaps this was his true form!

The massive blood-red character "God" that had been there was now completely obscured by his enormous flesh.

If one were to look down from a high altitude at this moment, they would see a horrifying sight within Red Leaf Valley—as if the ancestor of ancient humanity had grown tired from running across the earth and simply lay down to rest.

Such a transformation naturally did not go unnoticed by the Ghostmares.

Almost instantly, all the Ghostmares turned their heads in unison, staring at Luo Chen.

Compared to those death row prisoners, Luo Chen, with his surging blood qi, was far more conspicuous.

His soul must surely be the most delicious food!

There was no need to call friends or communicate; a vast number of Ghostmares burrowed into Luo Chen's body.

Three or five formed a group, tens became hundreds.

Finally, thousands upon thousands, countless in number!

Having revealed his true form and making no defense, Luo Chen now seemed to be the sole existence in heaven and earth.

Centered on Red Leaf Valley, expanding outward for a hundred li, a thousand li, even ten thousand li.

Countless Ghostmares rushed toward Red Leaf Valley!

Wherever they passed, mortals suffered endless nightmares, and martial artists could not eat or sleep in peace.

Occasionally, a powerful martial artist would also be filled with terror.

Xiangfei, the closest to Red Leaf Valley, had already begun to sway, drowsy and on the verge of sleep.

In her daze, Xiangfei bit her soft tongue tip. The taste of blood jolted her awake, and she hurriedly threw more Dream Stones into the fire pit, lest she fall asleep and have her soul devoured by passing Ghostmares.

When green smoke once again rose from the fire pit, she couldn't help but breathe a sigh of relief.

"How much time has passed?"

Xiangfei's icy body trembled. She looked around but couldn't tell the time.

Perhaps it was already the third watch?

Suddenly!

Xiangfei's entire body went rigid; her very thoughts seemed to stop functioning.

She stared in terror toward Red Leaf Valley.

Beneath the curtain of the Heavenly River rain, sharp howls echoed. A shadowy figure spread its heaven-shrouding wings and tore through the air, finally disappearing slowly from Xiangfei's sight.

That thing!

Had entered the valley!

At this moment, inside the valley.

The wind and rain seemed to pause for an instant.

Then, a shadow descended upon the Illusion Dream Locust Tree.

Though it was merely a shadow, for some reason, when it landed, it splashed countless raindrops and red leaves.

The shadow stared at the sleeping giant on the ground, its smoke-formed eyes filled with uncontrollable greed.

"Hiss!"

With a sharp cry, the shadow charged straight into the giant's body.

A strange thing happened.

The moment that terrifying shadow entered the giant's body, the apertures that had been open on the giant's body instantly closed!

Not only that!

The character "God" formed by the ravines and Luo Chen's blood on the ground, which had been covered, also began to emit waves of light.

As if responding, the five great mountains, silent beneath the rainy night, also seemed to come alive, transforming into a dome that completely enveloped Red Leaf Valley!

Golden Body, Leakless—nothing in, nothing out!

Five Peaks Lock the Spirit—Ghostmares cannot escape!

Countless Ghostmares were completely trapped inside Luo Chen's body.

Including that powerful Ghostmare that could supposedly threaten a Martial Dao Human Immortal, it too could not leave.

For a moment, all the Ghostmares panicked.

They let out sharp cries in unison inside Luo Chen's body, fleeing in all directions, but could find no gap.

After attempt after attempt, they eventually converged into a torrent of nightmares, rushing in one direction.

That was the giant's head, the location of the human brain, the place of the cultivator's sea of consciousness!

This was the most fortified place, yet also the most vulnerable!

As long as they could devour the giant's soul, they could escape from this shell.

However!

When they reached the outside of the sea of consciousness, countless Ghostmares discovered that a golden net was spread there.

Attached to the net was illusory golden flame.

As the golden flame burned faintly, illusory phoenix feathers flickered in and out of existence.

By now, there was no way back!

The most powerful among the Ghostmares, like a king, let out a silent, sharp cry. Instantly, tens of thousands of Ghostmares rushed toward the golden fire net like moths to a flame.

Tearing, beating.

Gnawing, consuming.

In an instant, a sea of Ghostmares perished in the fire net.

But the effect was also visible.

The impenetrable golden fire net had become extremely dim, even showing some cracks.

The great Ghostmare King could no longer hold back. It flapped its wings and charged forward.

Its thick smoke formed claws like sharp blades, madly tearing at the golden net.

Time passed, unknown for how long.

When the smoke on the Ghostmare King had dissipated by eighty or ninety percent, the weakened creature finally opened a small gap and struggled into the giant's sea of consciousness.

This was a dead, silent ocean.

It seemed utterly lifeless.

But the moment the Ghostmare King entered, a thunderclap seemed to explode in the silence.

A soul, weakened to the extreme, slowly opened its eyes!

When those weak eyes opened, everything changed.

The sea of consciousness trembled; waves arose.

Golden ripples spread; brilliant light shone in all directions.

"Farewell, prison!"

Staring at the charging Ghostmare King, the soul giant slightly opened its mouth and uttered a single word.

"Zha!"

Whoosh...

The Ghostmare King, powerful enough to threaten a Martial Dao Human Immortal, vanished into smoke on the spot.

The soul giant seemed to have been imprisoned for too long, or perhaps had suffered severe damage earlier, and was now extremely weak.

After destroying the invading enemy, it did not hesitate. It released a strand of divine thought, plunging straight into the Purple Mansion within the body.

Several items were taken out.

Then, the divine thought returned to the sea of consciousness, re-entering a state of dormancy.

All of this happened in the blink of an eye.

But this world seemed to sense something.

Beneath the vast curtain of rain, thunderous roars sounded.

Soul-Shaking Thunderclap!

Boom!

Under the flash of lightning, nothing was found.

But the deafening thunderclap had already exploded across ten thousand li. Everyone who heard it, whether mortal or martial artist, was knocked unconscious.

Including Xiangfei, a Leakless Realm martial artist!

When she woke up, the sky was already growing light.

Xiangfei subconsciously looked ahead and saw that the five peaks had collapsed, and the red leaves were gone.

The woman was stunned.

Just then, a figure was walking out from the valley pass on foot.

He wore white robes, unstained by a single speck of dust.

As he walked, he was incredibly light, as if he might ascend and fly away at any moment.

It was Luo Chen!

Xiangfei leaped up and quickly came to Luo Chen's side.

"Senior, are you alright?"

Luo Chen stood outside the ruins of the collapsed mountain, a smile on his lips.

"Never have I felt as free and wonderful as I do at this moment!"

...

On a desolate, boundless Gobi desert.

A carriage trudged along.

The driver wore palace attire and was stunningly beautiful, a rare beauty in the world.

Yet beneath that peerless beauty was a majesty that no one could ignore.

It was clear that this woman must be someone of high status.

But at this moment, she was willingly driving this carriage, with no resentment on her face.

All of this was because the man in the carriage made her do such things with heartfelt submission.

Inside the carriage.

Luo Chen toyed with a gray, unremarkable small cauldron, a look of long-unseen joy on his face.

After decades of effort, gathering the right time, place, and people, he had finally broken the seal that Rosycloud had left outside his sea of consciousness, allowing him to mobilize his divine soul.

At the moment of breaking the seal, he had immediately used his divine thought to refine essence into qi, converting a portion of Blood-Refined Divine Astral into magical power, thereby opening his nearly petrified Purple Mansion.

After retrieving the several items he had planned for, he immediately resealed his Purple Mansion, withdrew his divine thought, and even made his main soul re-enter a state of dormancy.

This world placed too great a restriction on a cultivator's magical power and divine soul.

Releasing magical power outward would cause eighty or ninety percent of it to vanish instantly.

Leaving the body with divine thought would attract the Soul-Shaking Thunderclap.

Luo Chen dared not gamble.

But even though his main soul was now dormant, appearing the same as before, his personal feeling was completely different.

Long trapped in a cage, now returned to nature!

That was probably it!

Whether sitting, lying down, standing, or moving, he felt an indescribable lightness.

And most importantly, he, who had previously been empty-handed, now finally had a few handy items to accompany him.

The cauldron in his hand, his lifebound treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, was one of them!

Additionally, he wore a storage ring on his finger, containing some materials he had carried with him before his ascension.

For example, pills, talismans, formation tools, and so on.

When he had left earlier, he had taken out a Meridian-Repairing Pill from it, allowing Xiangfei to recover fully.

On the carriage table also lay an ancient sword and a jade box.

With these things, Luo Chen now had much greater confidence for his upcoming trip to Carefree City.

"Let me first try the Primordial Unity Cauldron!"

Luo Chen exhaled a breath of turbid qi, then swallowed the Primordial Unity Cauldron, which had been shrunk to its smallest size, into his abdomen.

As if responding!

The moment it entered, the Primordial Unity Cauldron actively floated above the blood qi furnace.

Strands of astral qi rose up, drilling into the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Forming patterns like blood vessels!

"Indeed!"

"When I broke through to the Furnace Realm back then, the trembling of the Primordial Unity Cauldron was not an illusion."

"If this is the case, perhaps my lifebound treasure really can give birth to a weapon spirit under the nourishment of the blood qi furnace, advancing to the spirit treasure rank!"

After verifying one conjecture, Luo Chen grew even more delighted.

He then picked up the ancient sword.

The sword was named Yuantu!

Although it was an immortal spirit treasure, the Yuantu Sword itself was sharp enough, and it could be barely wielded by infusing it with blood qi.

Thus, Luo Chen gained another handy sharp weapon.

Then, Luo Chen opened the jade box on the table.

Inside the jade box, a gu insect lay quietly dormant.

Its body was no larger than a child's thumb, with a mouth but no teeth. The whole thing seemed to be made of pure, dark crystal that had been stripped of all reflective ability.

But under the careful observation of his Crimson Eyes and Golden Pupils, it could be seen that this insect was not smooth like crystal. Instead, it was covered with countless extremely fine folds and grooves. Those folds and grooves, though seemingly small, gave one the impression of ancient ice layers crushed and overlapped upon each other.

The moment it appeared, the gu insect trembled slightly.

On the originally calm carriage, ripples seemed to spread out.

It was the Void Gu he had obtained by chance back then!

When he had ascended back then, Luo Chen had not brought many things, leaving most of them for the Luo Tian Sect.

Especially living creatures, he had not brought many, for fear of affecting the ascension.

Even demon beasts that recognized him as master, such as Black King and Kongchan, had their restrictions lifted by Luo Chen and were left in the Mountains and Seas Realm.

Only the Void Gu, because it was involved in the divine ability "Great Luo Tian's Sleeve" that he cultivated, had Luo Chen put it to sleep and stored it in his Purple Mansion along with the Yuantu Sword, the Burial Bone Umbrella, and the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

He had once worried that the Void Gu would be affected by the ascension.

But now that the ascension had failed, when he reopened his Purple Mansion, the Void Gu was still alive.

"A fourth-rank Void Gu... its grade is still a bit low. Fortunately, I now have food."

Luo Chen smiled slightly and pushed over the stone box that Fang Zhibai had used to contain the Immortal Origin Stone he had gifted.

This box was carved entirely from Void Sprout Crystal.

If Old Ghost Nanqi hadn't lied to him back then, Void Sprout Crystal was precisely the main material for the Void Gu's advancement.

Under Luo Chen's watchful eye, the gu insect seemed to discover something. Its entire body came alive, and it crawled unsteadily into the stone box.

From its toothless mouth, a special liquid was secreted, dripping drop by drop into the stone box.

"It really works!"

A flash of surprise passed through Luo Chen's eyes.

He then closed the stone box and tucked it into his sleeve.

After doing all this, Luo Chen felt as if a great weight had been lifted.

But occasionally, when his thoughts recalled the moment his divine thought had probed into his Purple Mansion, he couldn't help but feel a heavy heart.

Although, relying on his powerful foundation, his Purple Mansion had been preserved even after his primordial spirit had collapsed, the originally indestructible Purple Mansion barrier was now covered with dense cracks.

Moreover, he had also found the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame, barely clinging to life, inside!

Luo Chen was well aware that to escort him to this place of Return to Void, the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame, which had originally reached the fifth rank, had exhausted almost everything. Its condition was even worse than after the battle at Heaven-Earth Island.

This flame had saved him from danger many times. If he had the chance, he would definitely restore it to its peak state!

Of course, that might be something for much later.

For now, Luo Chen's focus still had to be on his martial arts cultivation.

Luo Chen took out two books and fell into deep thought.

*Divine Astral Combat Qi*

*Demon Phase Art*
