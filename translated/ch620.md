# Chapter 620: Cooperation and Marriage Alliance, Golden Core Stage Six, the Six Armors Complete, All Things Prepared

Inside the palace halls of Peng Island.

The guests took their seats, and conversation was cheerful.

The two Foundation Establishment juniors, led by Zhang Jiadi, went outside to play on their own.

Inside the hall, after some pleasantries, they gradually entered the main topic.

"Daoist Azure Yang, I, Huo Fang, represent Wuzhong Valley in inviting you to join our sect. Of course, if you are interested, the Tianqu Chen clan and Coral Sea Zhou clan may also be options for you," Huo Fang said solemnly.

The answer he received was a refusal without hesitation.

"My apologies. I am used to being loose and unrestrained, and cannot bear sect constraints. Fellow Daoist Huo, I appreciate your goodwill."

Huo Fang was not surprised by this answer. The Moya Cave Master had refused him the same way years ago, but he still wanted to make an effort.

"Will you not hear the conditions we offer you?"

Luo Chen smiled. "Better not. What if your conditions are too good, and I cannot help being tempted?"

Huo Fang was speechless. His Wuzhong Valley had indeed prepared a considerable price to draw in this artifact-forging master skilled in refining defensive magic treasures.

The most basic part was a third-tier mid-grade cave dwelling, plus an annual salary of five hundred thousand spirit stones.

Apart from this, there would be countless servants, and two to five concubines below the Golden Core stage could also be arranged for him.

If Daoist Azure Yang had other requests, they could also discuss further above the bottom line.

Of course, such conditions naturally corresponded to corresponding demands.

That was for Daoist Azure Yang to open an artifact-forging hall within Wuzhong Valley, teach Wuzhong Valley's low-level disciples artifact-forging arts, and tailor defensive magic treasures for Wuzhong Valley's Golden Core cultivators.

Now that Daoist Azure Yang did not even want to hear the conditions, Huo Fang truly had no way to discuss further.

But this was also the common style of wandering cultivators.

Huo Fang shook his head and sighed. "A pity. I cannot share the same sect with Fellow Daoist."

Luo Chen's expression was warm. "Though we are not in the same sect, we are still people of the same Dao. When Fellow Daoist has leisure, you may come visit my Peng Lake as much as you like."

Huo Fang chuckled and raised his backup plan.

"Then let us cooperate."

"What sort of cooperation?"

"For the magic instruments and magic treasures Fellow Daoist refines each year, our three Wuzhong Valley sides want seventy percent, at market price."

"City Lord Huo jests. The path of artifact-forging has unpredictable time costs, and failure is common. How could I possibly produce steadily every year? Moreover, long-term artifact-forging has a great effect on Luo's cultivation. I cannot focus only on petty profit and delay cultivation of the Great Dao."

Huo Fang frowned, his gaze landing on Luo Chen's face, which looked red-hued yet was in truth incomparably young.

In all their intelligence, there seemed to be no mention of Daoist Azure Yang's age.

They had only assumed that a master-level existence with such artifact-forging skill was surely an old-generation cultivator of three or four hundred years.

If it were such an existence with no hope of Nascent Soul, they would naturally covet enjoyment and profit.

Looking at him now, Huo Fang had misjudged the other man's heart that yearned for the Great Dao.

After thinking, Huo Fang loosened the conditions.

"Then let us not calculate by the year. How about a ten-year period?"

Luo Chen raised a brow. "That is much more relaxed. However, what benefit can I obtain? You must know, even if defensive magic treasures are not sold to you, there are plenty of people who want them. I will never lack spirit stones to earn."

"That is simple."

Huo Fang smiled. "You should be quite annoyed by the Demon Hunter Office's mandatory missions every five years, yes?"

Luo Chen's expression hesitated slightly, and his gaze fell on the Angler and Purple Dowager.

The two of them nodded faintly to him.

"Your meaning?" His gaze shifted, and Luo Chen looked again at Huo Fang.

Huo Fang nodded. "Although we three families cannot interfere with the Myriad Immortals Assembly's internal affairs, we still have some connections. As long as you are willing to cooperate with us, we will naturally operate on your behalf with the Demon Hunter Office and have them open convenient doors for you. Of course, if you do not execute missions, your demon hunter star rank will basically remain stuck at four stars."

"Fellow Daoist Moya, why not speak from experience?"

At Huo Fang's invitation, the Moya Cave Master coughed lightly.

"On this point, Daoist Azure Yang, you may be at ease. This old man has cooperated with them for nearly a hundred years. Apart from wartime summons, I have never once gone out to execute a mission. Even when beast tides arrive, we can use the identity of logistics personnel and avoid the front-line winds."

After listening to his explanation, Luo Chen fell into thought.

One had to say, this condition was truly tempting.

Putting aside the danger of going out on missions, just the most basic time cost was extremely annoying.

If the three Wuzhong Valley families could remove these troublesome matters for him, then he would have more time to focus on cultivation.

Clearly, they truly could do this.

The presence of the Angler and Purple Dowager explained everything.

It just so happened that, to refine the Demon Emperor shed husk, he had to refine a batch of defensive magic treasures next anyway. Trading them to these people was not impossible.

It was mutually beneficial. Why not do it?

Luo Chen did not hesitate for long and agreed on the spot.

However, he also raised a condition: in the short term, he would not accept custom magic treasure cooperation. Whatever he refined, the three Wuzhong Valley families could only buy that.

Huo Fang naturally would not refuse.

In the cultivation world, magic treasures were numerous, while defensive magic treasures were scarce. No matter what attribute or what style they had, obtaining them first was what mattered.

Even if one was not compatible with a particular person, the three Wuzhong Valley families had plenty of cultivators. They could always find someone suitable.

After discussing this matter, Huo Fang took advantage of the opened conversation and incidentally discussed some other cooperation.

"I see the black-gold dragon carp of Peng Lake are rather good in quality. When they mature, why not leave half for us? We can discuss the price in detail then."

"This spirit land, Daoist Azure Yang, is still not being used to its fullest. I have some suggestions. If you have enough manpower on your side, you could listen."

"Speaking of manpower, Daoist Azure Yang, you truly have too few usable people under you. That Zhang family has only a few core members. It is far from a true cultivation family. Why not let its family head spread branches and leaves, flourishing in population, so he can better assist you?"

"Today, I brought two juniors with excellent appearance and character from the Chen clan and Zhou clan. They met Zhang Jiadi and immediately felt like old friends. Perhaps they could form good matches. I simply do not know Fellow Daoist's opinion."

At the beginning, Luo Chen listened to some of the cooperation with a smile.

But toward the end, the flavor was somewhat off.

Marriage alliance?

Or inserting people?

Did they want to help him develop Peng Lake? Or were they coveting this spirit land?

Under Huo Fang's smiling gaze, Luo Chen calmly said, "Although the Zhang family is my affiliate, they are ultimately not my servants. As for the specifics, let things follow their natural course."

This meant he would not actively promote it, but also would not reject it.

Huo Fang laughed loudly. "Indeed. Matters of juniors should be left to them to follow their natural course."

At this point, the topic came to a close.

Next, Luo Chen chatted separately with the Purple Dowager and the Angler.

The former had come to explain Huangfu Song's provocation back then. Luo Chen smiled it off and said he had long since stopped keeping it in mind. After hearing this, the Purple Dowager naturally felt relieved, and even directly said that in the future she would organize a meal and have Huangfu Song personally serve Luo Chen tea and apologize.

As for whether this meal could happen?

Nine times out of ten, it would not, but at least the Purple Dowager's attitude had been shown.

On the Angler's side, there was not much discussion. Luo Chen only said that although his seclusion had been interrupted, it would restart in the short term.

In other words, the Angler would not be seeing him recently.

The other party was naturally greatly disappointed, but under Huo Fang, the Purple Dowager, and the others' gazes, he could not speak his true thoughts.

Of course, even if he said them, those two would only dismiss them.

Behind Huo Fang was Wuzhong Valley, while the Purple Dowager herself had the Nascent Soul legacy left by the Great Zhou dynasty. Each had grasped a Nascent Soul formation method.

What they had to consider was not a Nascent Soul formation method, but how to reach Golden Core Perfected Completion and survive heavenly tribulation.

Only a true wandering cultivator like the Angler had not even found the path to Nascent Soul formation.

After chatting idly with the two of them, it was the Moya Cave Master's turn.

The moment he came up, the other party impatiently asked about Luo Chen's artifact-forging method, and exactly how he achieved perfect compatibility between the formation and the magic treasure embryo.

Luo Chen naturally could not tell him the Primordial Unity Cauldron's core secret.

He merely dragged the topic left and right, talking for a while about the artifact-forging arts of Hundred Craft Mountain and Refining Edge, then bringing up an Eastern Wilderness artifact-refining method he had once learned by chance. During this process, he also occasionally had the Moya Cave Master express his own views.

The other party sought something from him, so he naturally spoke everything he knew.

Luo Chen seized the chance and learned some of the Moya Cave Master's minor artifact-forging methods.

At the end, he said seriously, "In truth, the formation remains the same formation. Whoever comes must follow the diagram and act according to the method. What truly matters is the magic treasure embryo itself. If you refine the embryo to perfection, it can naturally match the vast majority of formations."

A perfect magic treasure embryo?

The Moya Cave Master's brows furrowed into the character for river. Did this require excellent raw materials?

Thinking back to the Bright Yellow Boulder Armor he had handled, its materials did not seem especially good.

How had Daoist Azure Yang transformed ordinary raw materials from rotten wood into wonder and refined a perfect magic treasure embryo?

As he thought bitterly, the sky had already gradually darkened.

Huo Fang set down his teacup and leisurely rose.

"We have already troubled Fellow Daoist's seclusion today. We will no longer disturb you further and will leave now."

Luo Chen rose with him, his face carrying apology.

"My apologies. Time is indeed somewhat tight. Otherwise, I would certainly speak with fellow Daoists by candlelight through the night and discuss Great Dao cultivation freely."

Huo Fang laughed loudly and waved his hand.

"There is plenty of time ahead. No rush, no rush. After you emerge from seclusion, my Wuzhong Valley will always welcome your visit."

Luo Chen likewise smiled. "That Wuzhong Valley, said to be a place from which ten thousand demons never return, is indeed something I have long admired. If I have the opportunity in the future, I will certainly visit."

The two walked out side by side, and the others followed behind.

When they reached the lakeside of Peng Island, Luo Chen said farewell to them one by one.

When it was the Moya Cave Master's turn, the other man seemed to suddenly wake up.

"Daoist Azure Yang, are you fooling me?"

"What makes Fellow Daoist say that?"

"How could a perfect magic treasure embryo be easy to speak of? Such things are refined by us people and naturally contain flaws. They are not Dao artifact treasure embryos born at the opening of heaven and earth in immortal legends. How could they be perfect?"

Luo Chen blinked. "At least the direction I mentioned is not wrong, is it?"

Moya was speechless.

Luo Chen continued, "As for how to achieve it, that depends on each family's methods, and those are all secret transmissions. Just like your Thirteen Devil Cloud Hands, which has not leaked out even slightly over these years. Fellow Daoist surely does not think I am so selfless and generous that I would casually tell you, right?"

After the Moya Cave Master heard this, his face turned red, then white. He seemed somewhat embarrassed into anger, but he also knew Daoist Azure Yang's words were rough while the reasoning was sound.

He had been too rash.

After adjusting for a moment.

His face returned to calm. "Indeed, I asked too much."

Luo Chen smiled. "No matter. Although core secret arts cannot be shared, you and I each have different artifact-forging styles. If we exchange more in the future, we can also improve together."

The Moya Cave Master nodded, then suddenly said, "This old man is not the only one who wants to exchange with you."

"You mean the Angler?" Luo Chen did not think much of it.

However, the Moya Cave Master waved his hand.

"Not him, someone else. That person, you also know. I imagine that before long, he will have time to free himself and come to the island to visit."

"I also know him?" Luo Chen was puzzled. "Who?"

The Moya Cave Master smiled mysteriously and laughed it off.

"In the future, you will know."

After saying this, he flew onto the large boat in great strides under the gazes of Huo Fang and the others.

Luo Chen stood by the lake, unable to understand after thinking over and over.

In the end, he could only shake his head and smile.

This Moya Cave Master had been led around by him, and as he left, he had in turn sold Luo Chen a mystery.

He was rather interesting.

But what kind of person was Luo Chen? How could he scratch his heart and lungs over such a small matter? With a laugh, he let it pass.

In any case, when someone came to the door later, he would know who it was when he met them.

...

Luo Chen thought "later" meant in the short term.

But he had not expected that several years would pass without seeing the acquaintance the Moya Cave Master had mentioned.

Instead, within Peng Lake, earth-shaking changes occurred.

The first was that Zhang Jiadi, head of the Zhang family of Cailian Island, married a woman of the Coral Sea Zhou clan.

That day, Peng Lake's formation dispersed, and the wedding procession set out from Cailian Island and reached the Coral Sea Region. Along the way, flying boats were like clouds, colored ribbons fluttered, and celebration filled the sky.

When they returned, sea beasts followed, and many wandering cultivators offered congratulations.

At the wedding, Daoist Azure Yang, who had not appeared for many years, personally emerged and presided over this wedding for Zhang Jiadi as witness.

For this, he specially gifted the woman a jade ruyi magic treasure embryo, said to combine offense and defense, with all things going as wished.

The Zhou clan woman was named Zhou Shanshan.

After marrying into the Zhang family, in the second year, she bore Zhang Jiadi a son. Thus, Zhang Jiadi's bloodline finally had continuity.

Not only that, after Zhou Shanshan entered and presided over the Zhang family, she also brought new industries.

She planted a batch of Emerald Coral in Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake. This Emerald Coral was a rare freshwater coral. Its own grade was not high, and even after growing for a hundred years, it would only be first-tier.

But this thing could effectively improve water quality and automatically capture heaven and earth spiritual qi.

Under long-term planting, the spiritual qi environment of a body of water could be greatly improved.

Not only that, waters with Emerald Coral could also be used to raise other special aquatic products, and even spirit beasts from the sea could be transplanted over.

Because of this change, the Zhang family instantly gained several more industries.

Zhou Shanshan's status as mistress of the household also grew increasingly stable.

By comparison, Wen Xiu and her daughter, who had never had much presence to begin with, became increasingly marginalized.

Wen Xiu even stopped returning much to Cailian Island, spending most of her time guarding the small Azure Yang shop in Dragon Abyss City alone.

Regarding this, apart from saying to the puzzled Heavenjade, "Trivial changes reveal the human heart," Luo Chen basically gave no comment.

He watched all these changes indifferently and placed all his energy on magic treasure refinement.

In this way, five years passed in the blink of an eye.

Inside Star-Dipper Hall.

A white-robed Daoist sat cross-legged. A sound of thunder suddenly exploded inside his body.

His whole body trembled, and the joints within him crackled and shook.

Suddenly, a long white qi dragon emerged from his mouth.

The Daoist slowly opened his eyes. In that instant, it seemed as if white arcs of lightning flashed.

Within the brilliant divine light, Luo Chen's eyes held even more joy.

"Golden Core Stage Six. It came naturally when the conditions were right."

"The six sets of mysterious armor are all complete."

"Years of planning. Everything is ready. Next, it is time to formally refine the Demon Emperor shed husk."

Amid his murmuring, a simple and ancient turtle shell filled with the vicissitudes of time leisurely floated before him.

*(End of Chapter)*
