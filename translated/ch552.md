# Chapter 552: The Lone Island, the Puppet, the Final Battle

After tucking the enormous third-tier Ring-Headed Turtle corpse into his storage bag, Gu Shaoshang's gaze fell to the water below.

One look was enough to tell him something was wrong.

That spiritual qi fluctuation was not the signature of a Red Rock Crab self-destructing.

"Baleful qi?"

Even as the thought crossed his mind, his brow lifted — and he immediately withdrew, slipping back into the void.

Below the sea's surface.

Luo Chen was mildly startled. *A human cultivator?*

And an Adept Stage Seven at that — a genuine great cultivator.

In an instant his expression shifted. He dispelled the Blazing Fire Miasma and slipped silently into the deep sea.

The battle on the large island nearby was winding down, and the Red Rock Crab clan was already diverting soldiers to sweep the field. This was no place to linger.

Both men departed, and long after, a figure came flying to this stretch of sea.

He circled the area, frowning.

Nothing?

Neither a clansman nor a Ring-Headed Turtle demon king, anywhere to be found.

Had a clansman been killed, and the body carried off along with everything else?

After searching once more without result, the Red Rock Crab demon king could only return empty-handed. At least the big island had been taken — one step closer to Xuanyan.

...

In a cave on a desolate island.

Luo Chen, the moment he returned to his cavern dwelling, wasted no time pulling the freshly-killed third-tier Red Rock Crab from his storage ring.

He drove a magic tool with his mana, carefully dismantling the carapace from the body, then immediately immersed the pieces in a special medicinal solution for preliminary treatment.

Only then did Luo Chen let out a breath.

Third-tier demon crabs no longer needed to be refined alive.

But their materials still required prompt treatment to prevent death-qi contamination.

Too much death-qi absorbed, and any Black Emperor Paste refined from them would be poor in quality. All that trouble fishing in troubled waters, and he'd have nothing to show for it.

By his count, this was his third third-tier demon crab taken by stealth over recent months.

He had nearly given up on the idea entirely.

Then, unexpectedly, the demon crabs' offensive had changed.

From disciplined coordination at the start, it had devolved into hesitation and miscommunication — eventually dissolving into every clan for itself.

Add to that the Ring-Headed Turtles' increasingly fierce resistance.

The situation had grown more and more chaotic.

And chaos was exactly what Luo Chen needed.

With a third-tier Concealment Veil Formation inscribed on his person, Luo Chen could move and strike in near-complete silence as long as he avoided unleashing anything of overwhelming force.

The first two times, he had encountered a pair of third-tier demon crabs who had won their fights at great cost, both gravely wounded. After quietly deploying his life-bound treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, he had captured them with ease.

This time had been even simpler.

Thinking back on how it had unfolded, Luo Chen recalled the young-faced great cultivator he'd glimpsed.

"So someone else is playing the mantis-and-oriole game too?"

"Adept Stage Seven..."

A thought stirred in him. He sent out a transmission.

Some time later, a gull that appeared completely ordinary circled a few times in the sky and descended onto the nameless desolate island.

Following a specific route, she walked steadily forward and eventually entered Luo Chen's cavern.

"Master, Heavenjade has returned. What are your instructions?"

The young man with his back to her turned around, holding a painting — a lifelike portrait of a man's face.

Before he could speak, Heavenjade laid eyes on it and said at once:

"Gu Shaoshang!"

"As I thought."

Luo Chen gave a faint nod. A flame kindled at his fingertips, and the freshly-painted portrait crumbled to ash.

He had known it. This stretch of the Xuanyan Sea Region rarely saw human activity. An Adept Stage Seven cultivator appearing here without reason would make no sense.

But Gu Shaoshang? That was entirely unsurprising.

The man had arrived long ago and had even formed a working alliance with the royal demon crabs.

What was strange was that the Feiyun Ravine cultivators had supposedly withdrawn from the Xuanyan Sea Region — yet Gu Shaoshang had come back alone, playing the same oriole's game as Luo Chen himself.

"His target is obvious. He's come for third-tier Ring-Headed Turtles."

"Same as I came for third-tier demon crab kings."

"But if he already allied with the demon crabs earlier, why resort to this shameless skulking? Learning from my example?"

Muttering to himself, Luo Chen fell into thought.

Heavenjade stood respectfully to one side, not daring to interrupt.

After a long pause, Luo Chen surfaced from his reverie, a slow realization forming.

"He went away and came back — playing the oriole alone. He doesn't trust the demon crab side to honor their word, so he's using his own strength to act independently?"

He had to admit, his guess was not far from the truth.

It hadn't been difficult to deduce.

Heavenjade, veiled by Luo Chen's concealment techniques, had circled high overhead around the clock, watching the entire war's progression.

During that time, she had watched the Feiyun Ravine cultivators haul Ring-Headed Turtle corpses onto their demon-hunting ship multiple times.

That was clearly one of the agreed terms between the human-demon alliance.

He had simply assumed the Feiyun Ravine cultivators had earned their fill and made a quick exit.

Now it was apparent Gu Shaoshang's appetite ran considerably larger.

Of course, the man was also cautious — he'd sent his sect's weaker members away first, then returned alone to hunt more Ring-Headed Turtles.

The thought prompted a new question in Luo Chen's mind.

*Should I take advantage of his isolation and kill him? To repay the wrong done to Heavenjade?*

That wrong wasn't simply a matter of pride.

Because Heavenjade the Fighting Gull had been captured, three Demon Spider Crab demon kings had come to raid Heavenly Pivot Island, which had exposed Luo Chen's location.

Had that not happened, he wouldn't need to be taking these risks now, personally hunting demon crabs in the middle of a warzone.

After some deliberation, Luo Chen set the thought aside.

"Leave it."

"What matters most right now is quietly collecting more third-tier demon crabs to prepare Black Emperor Paste for the Archaic Tier Three late-period tempering."

"Stirring up extra trouble would only get in the way."

Luo Chen knew how to weigh priorities.

The current battlefield was chaotic and complex — precisely the conditions that let him fish in troubled waters and extract maximum gain.

Complicating things by alarming the five royal demon crab clans would only invite larger problems.

Moreover —

Taking on an Adept Stage Seven great cultivator would require him to use everything he had.

Whether he could win at all was one question. Causing too much noise was quite another.

And after all — the lord of Demon Snakes and Demon Gulls was the demon crabs' sworn enemy.

With his priorities sorted, Luo Chen looked toward Heavenjade.

"What is the current state of the crab-turtle war?"

Heavenjade answered without hesitation: "Fifty-four islands have fallen. Only eighteen remain in Ring-Headed Turtle hands. From what I have observed, the five royal demon crab clans are committed to winning this battle absolutely — they have deployed nearly all their elite strength. Taking the remaining eighteen islands is only a matter of days."

Only eighteen left?

Luo Chen turned the number over in his mind, expression thoughtful.

The Nine Palaces Eight Trigrams Formation used nine islands per formation-eye, dividing space into eight sectors forming the Eight Trigrams, with Xuanyan Island guarded at the center.

That meant the eighteen remaining islands were the final two formation-eyes.

Either they held, or they fell — and if they fell, a cascade would follow, deciding the war's outcome completely.

*Not much time left.*

"Keep watching the battlefield. The moment a major engagement breaks out, notify me immediately."

"Yes, Master!"

"What about Black King?"

"Using the formation map Master provided, Big Black has drawn very close to Xuanyan Island. But actually slipping inside — that may prove difficult."

Hearing this, Luo Chen nodded.

The Nine Palaces Eight Trigrams Formation looked imposing on the surface but was, at its core, rather crude.

Otherwise Gu Shaoshang wouldn't have found its weaknesses so easily and dismantled it island by island.

His own side had Han Zhan's guidance, and the Sealing Divinity Compass's method for tracing formations and tracking spirits. The grand formation's layout was far more transparent to him than to anyone else.

That was why, well in advance, he had sent Black King to use the cover of the heated war to quietly infiltrate the inner formation — prepared for whatever might come.

"Tell Black King: once he makes it onto Xuanyan Island, follow the formation map I gave him and go to the designated location. Plant the Sealing Divinity Compass there."

Heavenjade blinked in surprise. "Master, are you going to the island yourself?"

Luo Chen's mouth curved. "If it comes to that, I'd like to witness in person how this war comes to an end. Such a scene ought to be quite a spectacle."

To witness the rise and fall of an entire clan — that was worth something in a cultivator's lifetime.

Even if he came away with nothing, it would at least make for fine conversation with fellow daoist friends in years to come.

...

Time wore on.

Without Gu Shaoshang to direct them, the demon crabs' assaults grew chaotic.

But as each clan fought independently and no longer had to worry about backstabbing, the command confusion gradually worked itself out.

The cost was a thirty-percent increase in losses across all five royal clans.

Not just low-tier demon crabs — the high-level combat strength took far heavier casualties too.

By the tally of the great demon kings, the five clans had together lost nearly twenty-some third-tier demon kings over the course of this war.

And the Ring-Headed Turtle side's losses were heavier still.

But even so, the Ring-Headed Turtles' resistance never stopped — it grew only fiercer, yielding not an inch.

As did the five royal demon crab clans.

The battlefield kept advancing.

From the original seventy-two islands, to thirty-six, then eighteen.

Without anyone quite noticing, the campaign to break formations and drown islands had lasted nearly a full year.

And then the five great patriarchs stood together on Empty Serpent Island.

Jin Hun. Red Flame. Zhu Xiang. Nine Claw. Lüxiu.

Five figures, fully revealed in their true forms — massive bodies standing upon Empty Serpent Island.

Behind them: millions of demon crab soldiers.

Before them: a single enormous island spanning a thousand li in every direction.

Seen from above, it resembled a continent unto itself.

This was Xuanyan Island.

"Decades of preparation, five years of fierce battle — and we have finally arrived here. Everyone: victory or defeat, it all comes down to this last battle." Jin Hun exhaled the words in a voice that carried no weariness, only a chest full of surging ambition.

The four beside him wore complicated expressions.

Some had only joined this war late, arriving at its final stage as circumstance demanded.

Others had prepared for it long in advance, watching the Overlord Crab clan's progress from the shadows.

Whatever their paths here, all had paid enormous prices.

Lüxiu blinked her eyes slowly, her voice touched with sorrow. "To come this far, my Azure Emperor Crab clan has lost seven third-tier clansmen. The second and first-tier losses are beyond counting. Even now, the bodies of some third-tier clansmen have never been recovered."

Zhu Xiang said flatly: "Every sacrifice was worth it. So long as we take Xuanyan Island."

"All that moaning — I'd think we were a bunch of delicate human cultivators getting sentimental." Red Flame sneered.

Nine Claw had already run out of patience, baring the fangs at his maw.

"When do we move?"

Jin Hun raised his great claw high. "We've only one step left — no need to delay. Strike while the iron is hot. Today."

As he spoke, the great claw came crashing down.

"Attack!"

He rose into the air.

With him, the other four patriarchs.

Under ten thousand eyes, five colossal figures tore through the sky, driving straight toward the vast island.

The moment they launched forward, Xuanyan Island shuddered. A pillar of light erupted from the ground, vast spiritual qi boiling up from somewhere deep beneath the earth.

A terrifying suction force spread in every direction.

But sadly, that suction found no answer.

The Nine Palaces Eight Trigrams was broken. Its central palace stood alone, with nothing to respond to it — nothing more than an isolated island.

That pillar of light had barely begun to manifest its form when the five patriarchs struck as one, pouring in unmatched demon qi, and shattered it entirely.

As the light pillar collapsed, wave after wave of tremors rolled across Xuanyan Island.

Several mountains on the island crumbled.

The earth rolled and heaved as if a dragon were turning in its sleep beneath it.

Seawater poured in from every direction in a surge that bordered on a tidal wave.

Somehow, at some point, Ring-Headed Turtles had appeared across the island in vast numbers, watching with cold eyes — already ready for the battle to defend their home.

No words were wasted.

At the five patriarchs' single command, millions of demon crab soldiers began their landing on Xuanyan Island.

War cries shook heaven and earth.

Endless blood-rain fell, and reeking demon qi spread in all directions.

...

"Time for me to get on the island."

Within the demon crab army, one second-tier demon crab moved with slow, slightly awkward steps — conspicuously out of place.

Inside that carapace, within the hollowed abdomen, Luo Chen operated the demon crab puppet with something close to wonder.

A demon crab puppet, yes.

Han Zhan had made this simple puppet in his spare time at Luo Chen's request.

Its offensive and defensive capabilities were nothing special — what it offered was concealment.

Combined with the [Spirit Breath Absorption Art], which Luo Chen had brought to full mastery — a technique that allowed him to draw in others' auras and imitate their signatures — the puppet became nearly undetectable to a casual eye.

Together, the two methods meant that unless someone looked closely, no one would notice anything wrong with this particular crab.

As Luo Chen operated the puppet, a strange sensation washed over him.

"This is almost like piloting a mech from one of those science fiction novels I read in my last life."

Muttering to himself, the demon crab puppet spread its eight legs and fell in step with the army's advance.

But once on the island, rather than joining the battle, it found a direction and quietly split from the main force.

Moving forward, battle erupted all around, but Luo Chen skirted every one.

Then, abruptly, he paused.

His gaze fixed on a point ahead.

There, spiritual light flickered, and a figure slowly materialized from the void.

"It's him."

Gu Shaoshang had come too.

But compared to Luo Chen's silent approach, Gu Shaoshang's presence was already exposed.

In sparse demon beast territory it might have been manageable — but on this battlefield now swarming with demon beasts, a lone human cultivator's sudden appearance was impossible to miss.

The moment he appeared, Gu Shaoshang was forced to personally cut down two demon beasts.

Luo Chen glanced over, had no interest in getting involved, and steered the demon crab away from the battle's center.

"Hmph."

In the thick of the fighting, Gu Shaoshang leveled his fingers like a sword, driving a flying blade through the demon beasts around him.

He was not pleased with himself.

His concealment arts were mediocre, and he hadn't anticipated the final decisive battle would involve this many demon beasts.

One moment's carelessness, and here he was — exposed.

As two demon kings in the distance began flying toward him, he was forced to disengage and retreat toward the outer edge of Xuanyan Island.

"In this final battle, I may not even get to observe."

Frustrated, the corner of his eye caught a glimpse of a demon crab moving against the tide, heading the wrong way entirely.

An odd crab, refusing to follow the swarm's king?

Luo Chen had no idea he had briefly caught the other man's attention.

He only knew his own passage had gone smoothly.

Deliberately taking detours, he had avoided every skirmish, large and small.

At last, the demon crab puppet came to rest in a miasma-shrouded swamp.

Eight legs stopped.

The surrounding miasma parted as though blown by wind, opening a path.

A single-horned giant python poked its head out cautiously, staring at the crab with puzzlement.

Then a divine sense transmission reached it — and it snapped to attention at once.

"Welcome, Master!"

The demon crab made no reply, only stepped forward into the swamp.

Once inside, the carapace opened. Luo Chen stepped out.

He looked around and nodded in satisfaction.

"This place may be foul, but it connects to a deep-sea spring below and offers both approach and exit. A fine base. Black King — well done."

As he spoke, he tossed over a jade bottle.

Black King caught it like a precious treasure.

"Master, are we joining the battle?" he asked, cradling the bottle carefully.

Luo Chen's mouth curved slightly.

*Join the battle?*

He intended to, of course — but not rashly.

First things first: observe the battlefield.

Without answering Black King, Luo Chen found a dry patch in the swamp and settled into a cross-legged sit.

The Sealing Divinity Compass materialized before him.

He ran his life-bound cultivation method, absorbing the ambient spiritual qi of heaven and earth — and before it could convert to fire, immediately channeled it into a spell.

Soon, a great water mirror took shape, slowly clarifying.

Within it, battles played out across the island, vivid and alive.

But with demon qi and spiritual qi erupting everywhere, the mirror surface kept rippling, unstable.

"Even with a divine soul comparable to Adept Stage Seven, I can't hold the Water Mirror stable in a battle of this scale?"

He muttered to himself, then accepted reality.

He had another option.

"Senior Han, I'll have to trouble you for a moment."

From within the Myriad Souls Banner, Han Zhan appeared with a resigned look.

"You little..."

Even as he spoke, he raised one finger and pressed it lightly against the mirror's surface.

In an instant, the mirror became firm and clear as stone.

*(End of Chapter)*
