# Chapter 714: Power of Tribulation Thunder, Soul-Splitting Divine Art

Within the earth abyss.

A gray little cauldron flickered with threads of black electric arcs, quietly hovering in the air like that.

The surrounding spirit qi surged toward it, yet strangely vanished without a trace.

Suddenly!

A black electric arc shot out, striking a thick hole into the solid earth vein.

Looking at this scene, Luo Chen revealed surprise.

The recovered Primordial Unity Cauldron had, as expected, advanced to the true artifact level!

The many formations and restrictions throughout it had perfectly fused with the materials, no longer separated from one another, connecting with heaven and earth.

Its original powers had now become even more formidable. Even the sentient Withering-Bloom True Flame was somewhat afraid and kept its distance from the Primordial Unity Cauldron. Presumably, it feared the Primordial Unity Cauldron's special wondrous use of seizing Golden Cores and Nascent Souls.

But what surprised Luo Chen most was this new divine ability that could unleash black electric arcs!

He saw clearly that the nature of the black electric arcs was exactly the same as the formed thunder beast that had appeared in the final round when he crossed the Nascent Soul heavenly tribulation.

Perhaps its power was slightly weaker, but that depended only on the mana he injected and the heaven and earth spirit qi the Primordial Unity Cauldron could borrow.

If activated without regard for cost, this black electric arc's power might still rise!

"Tribulation thunder power?"

"A strange change..."

Although puzzled, Luo Chen was still extremely happy.

Ordinarily, he did not particularly feel the importance of the Primordial Unity Cauldron, but when he truly could not activate it, he felt inconvenienced everywhere.

Now that it was recovered and had new changes, no matter what, it was a good thing.

After murmuring once, Luo Chen withdrew the Primordial Unity Cauldron and let it be nurtured within his Purple Mansion.

Afterward, Luo Chen rubbed his brow, and a trace of fatigue could faintly be seen on his face.

Enduring the fatigue, Luo Chen's hand flickered, and a storage bag and a white belt woven with cloud patterns appeared.

Looking at these two storage treasures, Luo Chen fell silent.

The former was Han Zhan's relic, and could be considered one of the legacies he had left for Luo Chen.

It was covered in restrictions, and opening it in a short time would be rather difficult.

However, Luo Chen did not have much interest in this storage bag.

That was because Han Zhan's true storage treasure had already been taken by Demon Emperor Azure Frost back on Green Parasol Mountain. This storage bag was merely something Luo Chen had given him after coming to the Northern Sea.

According to Luo Chen's guess, there was probably nothing good inside. The only things that made him think of it were the Seven Baleful Swords and Myriad Souls Banner he had previously handed to Han Zhan.

As for that white belt, Luo Chen was rather interested.

Feiyunzi's relic!

During the battle in Bright Radiance Heaven, Feiyunzi abandoned his flesh body and escaped with his Nascent Soul.

At that time, the other man had only had time to take away his sword pellet and golden bell. The storage belt hanging on his flesh body had not been taken away in time.

This item, Luo Chen had not previously given to Han Zhan to help break the restrictions. Instead, he had specially kept it, waiting until after he formed his Nascent Soul to slowly open it.

One could imagine that a Sage Sage famous for many years, not a wandering cultivator but the master of a Sage upper sect power, Feiyun Ravine, would certainly possess no small wealth.

In the days to come, aside from bitter cultivation to consolidate his realm, he also had to spare time to open the restrictions on this belt.

Luo Chen left the two storage treasures outside his body, making it convenient to study them at any time.

Only then did he finally let out a breath.

Matters outside the body and matters inside the body had all been clarified.

Then lastly, only one matter remained.

Luo Chen's eyes slowly closed, and his consciousness returned to his sea of consciousness.

His main divine soul drifted weakly within the golden wave ocean. Although it was much, much stronger than when he had been at the Adept stage, it was absolutely not Luo Chen's peak state after his cultivation was complete.

In that soul-shaking battle with Han Zhan, although he had relied on the Great Completion Dragon-Slaying Art to erase Han Zhan, the other party's dying counterattack had still left Luo Chen's divine soul severely injured!

Faintly, one could still see fine cracks on the main divine soul.

They were not large, but they went from outside to inside, extremely deep.

If he wanted to repair such injuries, it would take at least a hundred years of work!

"Han Zhan..."

He had called him Senior for several decades, but they had only addressed one another as Fellow Daoist once, and that was when they turned against each other as enemies.

With a soft sigh, Luo Chen no longer tangled himself in the past.

Instead, he placed all his attention on the Han Zhan soul fragments he had absorbed.

The will within these fragments had already dispersed with their master's death, and they were undoubtedly relatively pure soul energy.

If he could absorb them harmlessly, perhaps they could supplement Luo Chen's currently weak divine soul.

But what drew his attention most were the memory fragments contained within.

Soul-Splitting Art!

The Falling Cloud Sect had three arts and two techniques.

They were respectively the Two Principles Crimson Cloud Art, Puppet True Scripture, Soul-Splitting Art, Locked Pearl Curtain, and Origin Core Art.

It was precisely by relying on these five systematic cultivation methods and secret arts that the Falling Cloud Sect could stand in the Jade Cauldron Domain of the Eastern Wilderness.

And even cultivate Han Zhan, the Sage Sage who changed the Jade Cauldron Domain's balance.

Among these five cultivation methods and secret arts, the Soul-Splitting Art was undoubtedly the most mysterious, strongest, and even the most core cultivation method!

In earlier years, Han Zhan had even been willing to teach Luo Chen the Origin Core Art, and even some small tricks for refining puppets, but once it involved the Soul-Splitting Art, he would keep his mouth shut.

He had once said that unless Luo Chen was willing to enter his sect and become a Falling Cloud Sect disciple, only then would there be a slight chance of being taught this cultivation method.

Such caution was naturally because of this cultivation method's wonders.

The Soul-Splitting Art had the effect of refining the spirit!

The nine great soul fragments Han Zhan had possessed at his peak had also been split off by relying on this cultivation method.

Luo Chen had coveted it for a long time, yet had not expected to obtain it through such means.

He carefully absorbed Han Zhan's memory fragments, setting aside those miscellaneous memories and carefully searching for content related to the Soul-Splitting Art.

Only after a long time did he collect seventy to eighty percent of it.

Inside the cave dwelling.

Luo Chen looked at the bamboo slip he had copied out by hand, studying it carefully. His face sometimes showed confusion, and sometimes a look of understanding.

The Soul-Splitting Art indeed had a spirit-refining effect.

But this effect was not that outstanding. It could not cause a fundamental transformation in a person's soul, nor could it allow one, at the Sage stage, to reach the great success stage of yin soul transforming into yang spirit.

Its main effect lay in changing the number of divine souls!

Through splitting the divine soul, then tempering and even strengthening the split divine souls bit by bit, they could finally recover to almost the same level as the main divine soul.

With this, at the same realm, a person cultivating this method could possess a divine soul foundation several times greater than their own body.

The exact multiple depended on how many soul fragments could be condensed.

Han Zhan was an unprecedented genius of the Falling Cloud Sect, especially talented in soul refinement, which was why he had been able to cultivate the Soul-Splitting Art to minor success while still "young," thereby bringing forth nine great soul fragments!

In other words, Han Zhan's divine soul foundation was absolutely nine times that of an ordinary Sage Stage One cultivator!

"No wonder Han Zhan feared neither Demon Emperor Aoxiao nor Cape Heaven Matron, and even crushed the latter. He could even, as an early-stage Sage, temporarily drive a late-stage Sage white tiger puppet."

"With this divine soul foundation, he was absolutely outstanding among early-stage cultivators!"

Luo Chen murmured to himself.

But afterward, distress appeared on his face.

The Soul-Splitting Art copied from those memory fragments was incomplete!

It only recorded how to cultivate soul fragments and deepen the connection between main soul and soul fragments, but lacked the two most basic points: splitting the main soul, and quickly strengthening the soul fragments.

The former was the beginning of everything, while the latter was the most important key for allowing a cultivator to quickly pass through the weak period after splitting the main soul.

Luo Chen was definitely going to cultivate this Soul-Splitting Art.

There was no doubt, and no hesitation needed!

He had enjoyed the benefits of having a sufficiently powerful divine soul foundation far too many times.

Whether alchemy, artifact forging, normal cultivation, or controlling magic treasures during battle, the stronger the divine soul, the better the effect.

And he had also experienced the disadvantages of an insufficiently powerful divine soul foundation in the Demon-Fall Land.

Jiaoren Emperor Liu Jun's slight charming voice had almost caused him to sink into it...

Thinking of the Demon-Fall Land, Luo Chen suddenly seemed to remember something.

In the Hundred Crow Ghost Forest that guarded Wood Heaven Plain, Han Zhan had once taught him an extremely practical soul art named the Mao-Opening Art!

This art was very difficult to cultivate.

But if one had Origin Core Art and Locked Pearl Curtain as a foundation, it was extremely simple.

Back then, Luo Chen had been pressed for time and used system Achievement Points to forcibly enter Beginner proficiency, so he had never deeply studied this spell.

But he clearly remembered the prerequisite process for using the Mao-Opening Art.

First, use the Locked Pearl Curtain secret art to split divine sense, then forcibly cut off a piece and arrange it on the second Origin Core.

With this, Luo Chen's body had the main divine soul and a tiny wisp of soul fragment on the second Origin Core.

Once he encountered an enemy using divine soul illusions to confuse him, even if his main divine soul was affected, he could use the soul fragment arranged in advance on the Origin Core Art to stimulate the main soul, making it suddenly enlightened and instantly awake!

Later, when he went deep into Wood Heaven Plain and in the pit of the Demon-Beast Heartlink Tree was confused by the demon-devil echo, he had relied on the Mao-Opening Art to break free from the influence of that echo on his own.

Thinking carefully now, the Mao-Opening Art combined with Locked Pearl Curtain was not precisely an exquisite method of splitting the divine soul?

Or perhaps these two were themselves also part of the Soul-Splitting Art.

Swallowing, Luo Chen carefully copied the two secret arts onto the handwritten manual.

Afterward, with a thought, he summoned the attribute panel.

[Incomplete cultivation method Soul-Splitting Art detected, seventh tier.]

[Complete it?]

In an instant, Luo Chen was stunned.

He was not surprised that the cultivation method was incomplete. After all, he had only completed the method of splitting the main soul according to his own conjecture. But what was with this grade?

If matched to realms, a seventh-tier cultivation method corresponded to an existence above Void Refinement. What realm was that?

Body Integration stage?

Great Vehicle stage?

The Falling Cloud Sect was only a mere Adept sect, and after Han Zhan's rise, it had barely reached the level of a Sage upper sect.

How could its core cultivation method be this high in grade...

"No!"

Luo Chen took a deep breath.

He thought of the old rumors in the Jade Cauldron Domain. The Falling Cloud Sect was not some branch established by abandoned disciples of a Holy Land or powerhouses from an upper sect, but a sect founded by a wandering cultivator who, by chance, obtained the inheritance of an ancient cultivator.

Including the three arts and two techniques, none were self-created. They had all been obtained by luck.

In other words, the Soul-Splitting Art was never created by the Falling Cloud Sect in the first place.

Especially since Han Zhan had once mentioned in conversation with him that he could only cultivate the Soul-Splitting Art to minor success because he lacked some key auxiliary treasure.

This also showed that the Soul-Splitting Art was not a Falling Cloud Sect method, but came from an even more powerful existence.

Thinking of this, Luo Chen made his decision.

Completing it was naturally impossible.

A seventh-tier cultivation method, even if he exhausted all the Achievement Points on him, could not be completed.

But even if it could not be completed, it was not impossible to cultivate.

Han Zhan had begun cultivating this method at the Adept stage, no, perhaps even at the Foundation Establishment stage, which showed that even an incomplete version of an incomplete version had certain merits.

Moreover, Luo Chen's current state was actually very suitable for cultivating this method.

Even after absorbing the soul power left by Han Zhan, the main soul in Luo Chen's body had still not fully healed, with many cracked places.

Those had been caused by Han Zhan using a soul art named Spirit-Seizing Needle.

It transformed one's own divine sense into invisible divine needles to attack an enemy's divine soul.

During ordinary combat, because of the obstruction of the flesh body and sea of consciousness barriers, it could only make someone instantly lose focus and become mentally clouded for only an instant.

Back then, because the two of their divine souls had clashed nakedly head-on, this Spirit-Seizing Needle soul art had displayed its full might, injuring Luo Chen so severely.

But now, the cracks caused by these injuries gave Luo Chen an innate condition very suitable for cultivating the Soul-Splitting Art.

"If I want to slowly repair these cracks, it will take too long."

"Better to seize the chance and split the main soul along the cracks, cultivating the Soul-Splitting Art."

"There may be temporary weakness, but if it can grow, the benefits will surely be immense!"

Luo Chen was not an indecisive person.

Even when he sometimes hesitated, that was before making a decision.

Once he decided to do something, he would not look ahead and behind, but would advance without hesitation!

During Qi Refinement, he had also wanted to befriend the Little Huan Mountain Duan family, but after the Duan ancestor acted to harm his interests, Luo Chen directly attacked the mountain and wiped out the clan.

At the Adept stage, because of the matter of going to the Demon-Fall Land, he had hesitated. But after truly deciding to go, he made every kind of plan and prepared everything fully. Even inside the Demon-Fall Land, with danger everywhere, he made decisions decisively and was never timid.

Now that he had a core cultivation method for strengthening his divine soul foundation, even if it was only incomplete, he could not possibly let this chance go.

After confirming that the slightly completed Soul-Splitting Art could be cultivated, Luo Chen rested for three days and directly began cultivation.

And the first step was splitting the main soul and forming a soul fragment!

Luo Chen carefully used this unorthodox method, combining Locked Pearl Curtain with the Mao-Opening Art, to cut the main soul bit by bit along the cracks on his main soul.

Rather than calling it cutting, it was more like tearing!

That kind of pain was something Luo Chen had never experienced in his life.

Even during all his past body cultivation, or during physical nirvana, he had never experienced pain of this degree.

It was like cutting flesh with a dull blade, and doing it with his own hand!

Even with Luo Chen's incomparably powerful and tenacious willpower, during the first attempt, he cried out in pain.

And because there was no soundproofing formation to restrict it, this sound spread out and fell into others' ears, extremely terrifying.

It was like the wail of some wild beast!

This kind of wailing lasted for more than half a year!

During this period, Heavenjade and Black King once tried to enter the earth abyss, and what answered them was only one word: "Scram!"

The violence and pain within that voice made one's whole body turn cold just from hearing it.

...

Time slowly passed.

Without realizing it, ten years had passed since Luo Chen entered seclusion to consolidate his realm.

Dragon Abyss Island, once a complete mess, had also recovered its vitality, and no scars could be seen anymore.

One could even say that the spirit qi on the island far surpassed what it had been before Luo Chen crossed tribulation.

Spirit qi density at the third-tier level was everywhere, and even several excellent feng shui treasure lands were filled with fourth-tier spirit qi concentration.

Countless strange flowers and unusual grasses rose from the ground. In only ten short years, Dragon Abyss Island had become a beautiful island with magnificent scenery.

Such a strange change was truly astonishing.

Because of this, even Black King abandoned the free and easy sea environment and came to the island to cultivate.

On this day.

Heavenjade stood beside a blue pool, curiously watching the middle-aged man Sang Jinghe keep working with hands and feet.

He tossed pieces of cut demon beast flesh into the pool.

From time to time, he poured in a few drops of green liquid.

These things combined together, turning the liquid in the pool a strange azure blue.

"Will this really work?" Heavenjade could not help asking.

As Sang Jinghe threw spirit stones inside, he said, "It should be feasible. This is only a conjecture I made after drawing reference from Sage's blood pool method. That Demon-Beast Heartlink Tree is truly strange. Whether all kinds of spirit soil or my Tumulus Mulberry Sect's ripening spirit liquid, none have any effect on it. So I changed my thinking..."

At this point, he turned and smiled. "Sage values me so much that he even rewarded me with Condensed Liquid Pills for condensing a Golden Core. No matter what, I must repay him. He no longer needs Purple Monkey Flowers, the Divine Iris was destroyed under the lightning tribulation, and Dragon Saliva Grass grows naturally without requiring much care. If I can research a cultivation method for the Demon-Beast Heartlink Tree, Sage will surely be very happy when he exits seclusion."

He certainly would be!

Heavenjade thought so.

Master placed great importance on that little tree, and even before becoming a Sage had taken it out several times for Sang Jinghe to study. If it could truly flower and bear fruit, he would certainly be greatly pleased.

Just as the two were speaking, they suddenly heard a dragon roar.

It was Black King!

"What happened to him?"

Heavenjade turned her head in confusion, looking toward a mountain stream that had formed only ten years ago.

In the next moment, she saw Black King sway his head and tail, stretching his posture, and soar out from within the stream.

He circled in the air, seeming to wait for something.

Heavenjade was extremely curious.

Until a vigorous aura spread out from deep within the earth abyss.

Sensing this aura, Heavenjade covered her mouth, her face full of disbelief.

This quickly?

In only ten years, Master had completely consolidated the Sage realm?

Amid her shock, that vigorous aura gradually grew vast. Wind moved with it, clouds gathered for it, and even the originally gentle sunlight seemed to converge.

In a dazzling golden radiance, spirit qi mist swirled, and a vast phantom stretched above Dragon Abyss Island.

The phantom gradually solidified, transforming into a fair, tender infant, happily playing above the white-robed Daoist's head.

Perhaps after playing enough, before long, it sank into the crown and vanished.

Seeing this, Black King, who had already landed on the ground, called out with joy filling his heart, "Congratulations, Master, your Nascent Soul is stable and your immortal path has succeeded!"

Sang Jinghe had long since knelt on the ground, shouting loudly, "Congratulations, Sage! Felicitations, Sage!"

Heavenjade gave a graceful bow, half-kneeling on the ground. "Congratulations, Master. Your immortal path has succeeded, and longevity is in sight!"

In the sky, Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes, feeling his realm incomparably solid.

With a casual wave, he seized a wisp of spirit qi from between heaven and earth.

With a thought, Nascent Soul power reflected through the Purple Mansion into heaven and earth, and in an instant an invisible pressure enveloped Dragon Abyss Island.

Because of this pressure, humans and demons all feared, grasses and trees all bowed, and even wind and clouds seemed to freeze in the air.

In this instant, Luo Chen's thoughts were clear. He raised his head and gave a long cry, spitting out all the accumulated frustration in one breath.

It was precisely:

At first I heard of refining qi and dining on golden fluid, then spoke of facing heaven and entering sunken ruin.

I seized the lotus platform to secure life's foundation, sun-soul to the east and moonlight to the west.

Sleeping in Dragon Abyss for several decades, drinking dry several thousand cups of flowing rosy clouds.

Within, I refined the medicine of longevity; having taken it, I return equal to heaven and earth.

*(End of Chapter)*
