# Chapter 813: Attack as Defense, Yuantu Sword Spirit

Because of the close distance, Luo Chen should have been the first to arrive at the City Lord's Mansion.

But the moment he stepped into that great hall, his hairs stood on end.

He looked up. Lingtian City Lord was still sitting cheerfully in the seat of honor, but beside him, on another chair, an old man was studying him with great interest.

"So this is the recently famous Pill Sage?"

With just one glance, Luo Chen recognized who this old man was.

He was none other than the formidable expert who had previously set up that great formation on Lingtian Pass which had dimmed the azure sky.

And given the earlier groundwork, his identity went without saying.

"This old man is Tianduzi. Greetings."

Luo Chen made a bowing salute. "Luo Tian Sect, Luo Chen!"

Then, at Lingtian City Lord's gesture, he casually found a chair and sat down.

No sooner had his bottom touched the seat than Tianduzi asked with great curiosity, "I've heard that Fellow Daoist Luo is extremely skilled in the dao of alchemy, reputed to be able to refine any pill, unrivaled in the Eastern Wilderness alchemy world?"

Luo Chen opened his mouth. When had the rumors become so outrageous?

Although he did intend to make a name for himself, he hadn't used such exaggerated advertising slogans!

No, wait—what he had said back then was [Refining a hundred pills, skilled in all]. It didn't seem much different from what the other party meant.

Before he could answer, Lingtian City Lord said with a smile, "Tianduzi, this is no mere rumor. In this city lord's opinion, in the vast Eastern Wilderness alchemy world, there is hardly anyone who can compare with Fellow Daoist Luo. Even some of my Nether Origin Sect's senior alchemists fall slightly short. Perhaps only when Alchemy Saint Chu Yan was alive could she have matched Fellow Daoist Luo."

Luo Chen swallowed his saliva. "Too kind, too kind!"

Tianduzi clicked his tongue in admiration. "Achieving so much so young! This old man has spent his entire life pushing the dao of formations to its peak, yet still cannot enter the dao through formations. Fellow Daoist Luo, so young, has already attained such mastery in the dao of alchemy. In the future, your achievements will likely be no less than those of Alchemy Saint Chu Yan."

Alchemy Saint Chu Yan had entered the dao through alchemy and advanced to Ascendant.

Moreover, under the gaze of countless onlookers, she had ascended to the upper realm.

Such news, even across vast oceans, was well known to top figures like Tianduzi in this realm.

Comparing Luo Chen to her showed just how highly Tianduzi regarded him.

Luo Chen felt a bit uncomfortable. He hadn't expected that after refining only a few batches of pills for others, his reputation would be inflated to such a degree.

Not wanting the other party to keep discussing him, Luo Chen quickly asked about the earlier anomaly.

Regarding this, Lingtian City Lord demurred, saying they should wait until everyone had gathered.

Luo Chen naturally didn't press further.

Soon, one Sage after another rushed from all directions of Lingtian Pass to gather at the City Lord's Mansion.

In his line of sight, familiar old friends, casual acquaintances, and even hostile gazers like Jin Ling and Motian Old Ghost all appeared one after another.

Luo Chen sat calmly, waiting for Lingtian City Lord to speak.

The only thing that surprised him was that, at some point, Lingfengzi had also returned safely to Lingtian Pass.

When he entered, he even nodded to Luo Chen in greeting.

Everyone was present!

Lingtian City Lord tapped the table and announced loudly, "Fellow Daoist Tianduzi—I'm sure none of you are unfamiliar with him!"

Everyone nodded in succession.

"From now on, the Black Tortoise Four Directions Formation, Lingtian Pass's city-guarding formation, will be personally taken over by Tianduzi."

"Not only that, but all formations within the city, as long as they are third-grade or above, will be handled by Tianduzi Sect disciples. All fellow daoists on fixed patrol duties should quickly send people to inspect the formations in their respective districts, report the degree of wear and tear and the complete status to the Tianduzi Sect disciples, and let them arrange repairs, reinforcements, and even coordinate modifications."

This was only to be expected, surprising no one.

Professional matters should be left to professionals; the worst thing was to pretend to know what one didn't.

As a famous formation grand sect in the Eastern Wilderness, from the supreme elder down to the entry-level disciples, everyone in Tianduzi Sect had deep knowledge of formations.

Entrusting them with Lingtian Pass's formations was the most suitable arrangement.

After Lingtian City Lord said a few words, he handed things over to Tianduzi.

The old man smiled slightly. "I'll only say two things!"

"First, once I fully take over the Black Tortoise Four Directions Formation, its power can be greatly enhanced, and the attack range of the Four Directions Mystic Swords will no longer be limited to within the city."

As soon as these words were spoken, everyone's spirits lifted.

Especially people like Qin Baixiang and Luo Chen.

They knew better than anyone the power of the Four Directions Mystic Swords.

An early-stage Sage would die upon contact.

A mid-stage expert would also find it hard to resist.

Only a grand cultivator might be able to withstand it for a while, but even then, at a considerable cost.

The only drawback was probably that the attack range was limited to within Lingtian Pass, allowing the demon emperors outside the pass to threaten the city's defenses from time to time.

If the Four Directions Mystic Swords could attack outside the city, even if activating them cost a great deal, at least the demon race wouldn't dare be so reckless.

"Second, the Zhou Tian Xing Dou Grand Formation that you fellow daoists helped me set up earlier has already taken effect. The azure sky above Lingtian Pass is completely under control. No more groups of demonic fiends will descend from the sky to launch a surprise attack on Lingtian Pass!"

The Zhou Tian Xing Dou Grand Formation!

One of the three most famous fifth-grade formations in Tianduzi Sect's heritage.

Unexpected that Tianduzi was willing to set it up at Lingtian Pass. Everyone expressed their gratitude.

Luo Chen also suddenly understood. The treasure-grade compass he had activated earlier was indeed part of the grand formation.

And this Zhou Tian Xing Dou Grand Formation, personally arranged by Tianduzi with the assistance of over a dozen Sages simultaneously, was naturally as miraculous as one could imagine.

Thus, the internal worry was resolved.

The Four Directions Mystic Swords, which could now attack outside, could also solve part of the external threat.

"Indeed, a powerful formation master can completely influence the direction of a war."

"Compared to him, my fourth-grade formation master level is far too inferior to Tianduzi's."

Luo Chen sighed in admiration.

He acknowledged the gap between himself and Tianduzi, but he didn't belittle himself either.

The other party specialized in the dao of formations; he himself had only dabbled slightly. Deficiency was only natural.

The atmosphere in the hall relaxed considerably after Tianduzi finished speaking about these two matters.

For the rest of the time, Lingtian City Lord left everyone to get to know each other better, facilitating coordinated operations later.

At the very least, they should recognize each other's faces!

What had started as a serious wartime meeting turned more into a normal gathering of Sages.

And in this environment, Luo Chen, unsurprisingly, became the center of attention once again.

One Sage after another took the initiative to approach and befriend him.

Even the Wan Jian Pavilion Pavilion Master, who had been standing with Lin Qingxuan and Motian Old Ghost, walked toward Luo Chen under their frowning gazes.

"Fellow Daoist Luo, did you receive the visiting card I sent you earlier?"

"Oh?" Luo Chen blinked, as if suddenly remembering, and said with a laugh, "I did see it, but I was busy with patrol duties earlier, and then the demon emperor attacked, so I put it aside for the time being."

Pavilion Master Li vaguely sensed Luo Chen's intention to decline, but he wasn't discouraged and continued to chat with Luo Chen.

...

Time passed little by little.

By evening, the Sages in the City Lord's Mansion gradually dispersed.

And in Luo Chen's cave dwelling, a private meeting of only five people was underway.

All attendees were old acquaintances.

Nine Spirits Primordial Lord, Lingfengzi, Elder Fu, and Idle Crane Sage.

All four had benefited from Luo Chen's pill refining, so they interacted frequently in their spare time.

After the meeting at the City Lord's Mansion ended today, Luo Chen had taken the initiative to invite them over for a gathering.

Looking at the four people deep in conversation, Luo Chen couldn't help feeling a sense of pride.

In just a few short years since returning to the Eastern Wilderness, he had built up a large network of connections, all of whom were Sage-level experts he could never have hoped to reach before.

Now, they all centered around him, gathering at his side, advancing and retreating together.

And outside, there were even more people trying to squeeze into this circle.

He had to admit, the earlier decision to make a name through the dao of alchemy had been absolutely correct!

"Fellow Daoist Luo, I must thank you again for the pills you sent last time, which helped me resolve the poison of the No-Light Beast," Idle Crane Sage suddenly said.

Luo Chen smiled slightly. "It was just a small favor. The pills I sent weren't a perfect match for the poison; you still had to regulate yourself skillfully."

Hearing this exchange, the other three became curious.

After Idle Crane Sage recounted the story, they couldn't help praising Luo Chen's noble character.

But they also gained a deeper appreciation of the dangers of fighting demon emperors.

"These demon emperor-level existences may not match our human Sages in technique, but each one has a trump-card innate demon art. If you're careless enough to fall for one, it can be very troublesome."

"Indeed. Back then, I was besieged by three demon emperors under Ox Ghost's command. I thought my mana was abundant, no less than a late-stage Sage grand cultivator, but I didn't expect one of them to have a bizarre technique that could absorb a cultivator's mana. If not for my abundant mana allowing me to fight my way out, I might have fallen there."

"If even Nine Spirits Primordial Lord, as formidable as you are, almost suffered a loss, what are we to do if we encounter such opponents?"

...

Unlike the united front and confident determination of the daytime City Lord's Mansion meeting, Luo Chen's private gathering was far less optimistic about the upcoming battle.

When it came to the various techniques of the demon emperors they had encountered, they were all deeply wary.

The demon emperor that Nine Spirits Primordial Lord mentioned, for instance, had never been recorded in any ancient texts. It was suspected to be a new variant that had appeared in the Million Mountains.

And that was just one subordinate under Ox Ghost's command.

The demon race had nine great commanders, each with formidable strength.

As far as everyone knew, there were Seven Rhinoceros, Yellow Barbarian, Ox Ghost, Hundred Legs, Arrogant Boast... and the infamous Azure Frost Demon Emperor.

No ordinary Sage wanted to encounter any of them.

Their only advantage was probably that they could defend the city and didn't have to leave Lingtian Pass, exposing themselves to these great demon emperors.

However, Lingfengzi's words cast a thick shadow over everyone's hearts.

"Don't be too optimistic. Perhaps soon, we'll have to leave the city to fight."

Idle Crane Sage was stunned. "That can't be, right? The demon race is currently powerful. It's difficult enough for us to hold our ground. How could we possibly take the initiative to attack?"

Lingfengzi sighed. "Do you know what my previous mission was?"

Amid everyone's confusion, he said quietly, "Lingtian City Lord gave me a complete map of the Eastern Wilderness human cultivation world, specifically marking the three domains of Overlord Blade, Azure Sky, and Fire Ridge. He ordered me to focus on investigating the deployment of demon race forces. If not to prepare for an active counterattack, I can't think of any other reason for him to do this."

Idle Crane Sage hesitated. "Know yourself and know your enemy, and you'll never be defeated?"

"The enemy is at the gates. Why still investigate?" Lingfengzi sneered. "After the army arrives, the enemy's strength is obvious at a glance. Moreover, the extra days he had me stay outside weren't just for this investigation. He also had me scout the marching routes of the other demon armies."

Idle Crane Sage opened his mouth but found nothing to say in response.

Luo Chen had experienced the first defensive battle of Lingtian Pass and had some insights.

After some thought, he said slowly, "If we allow the demon armies to converge, completing a four-sided encirclement with their massive numbers, cutting off support from the five Lingtian domains and even the sixteen rear domains, then Lingtian Pass will be an isolated city. At that point, tens of millions of demonic beasts will crash against Lingtian Pass simultaneously. Even if this is the Eastern Wilderness's first and greatest fortress, I'm afraid it won't hold."

Nine Spirits Primordial Lord nodded beside him. "When my Nine Spirits Sect was broken, it was also because we were overwhelmed by numbers. In fact, not just Nine Spirits Sect, but major powers like Taihu Palace, Overlord Blade Sect, and even Azure Sky Sect were all buried alive by the demon sea tactics."

Sometimes, when numbers reached a certain level, even the most exquisite techniques were just flashy tricks.

"So!" Luo Chen looked around at everyone and said solemnly, "As long as Lingtian City Lord isn't an inflexible man, he won't sit by and watch Lingtian Pass be surrounded. On the contrary, using attack as defense, achieving local victories before the nine great commanders converge, is the only way to make Lingtian Pass hold out longer. And those three domains are the most suitable strategic buffer zones!"

This remark drew nods from everyone.

Although there was a large plain outside Lingtian Pass, if it became the battlefield, it would almost cut off Lingtian Pass's supply routes.

But if they could capture the three frontline domains and make those places the battlefield, they would have much more room to maneuver.

At that point, whether to fight, retreat, or wait for reinforcements from the sixteen rear domains, they would have ample space.

Strategically, this was the best approach.

Lingtian City Lord was by no means an inflexible man, and his thinking was very likely along these lines.

But for those directly involved like Luo Chen and Lingfengzi, who would have to go out and fight, the danger was incalculable.

Elder Fu, who had been silent all along, couldn't help but let out a bitter laugh.

"No wonder Tianduzi specifically mentioned today that the attack range of the Four Directions Mystic Swords would no longer be limited to Lingtian Pass."

Everyone suddenly understood!

Tianduzi's words were nothing less than paving the way for a future counterattack, meant to dispel the doubts of ordinary Sages like them.

"So, when will that battle come?" someone asked quietly.

...

The small gathering in the cave dwelling ended in the third watch of the night.

Apart from deepening their relationships and discussing major matters, everyone also exchanged cultivation insights.

Nine Spirits Primordial Lord, abandoning his previous secretive attitude, generously shared a large amount of mid-stage Sage cultivation experience with everyone.

And during his occasional demonstrations, a hint of aura leaked out, as deep as an abyss, drawing everyone's attention.

"With the help of the Nine Separation Pill, he should have successfully divided into nine spirit infants and begun mastering the sect's secret great techniques. I wonder how strong Nine Spirits Primordial Lord is now?"

After everyone dispersed, Luo Chen casually pondered.

But he wasn't worried. Nine Spirits Primordial Lord was his ally; the stronger he was, the more Luo Chen benefited.

Moreover, his own progress was not to be outdone!

On the attribute panel, his cultivation was getting closer and closer to mid-stage Sage.

And once he broke through, with the transformation of his main soul, he might be able to split off a third divided soul!

At that time, he could also try to learn a defensive soul technique recorded in the *Soul-Splitting Art*.

When it came to offensive methods, Luo Chen was confident he was no weaker than any cultivator of the same rank. Even challenging those above his rank, he had some confidence.

But the battlefield situation was unpredictable and treacherous. Only defense was the true foundation of self-preservation!

Luo Chen took out a pill formula from his storage ring.

It was something the Wan Jian Pavilion Pavilion Master had given him when the meeting ended today. He wanted Luo Chen to refine this pill in large quantities and was willing to pay sufficient compensation.

And Luo Chen was quite familiar with what this pill formula produced.

It was none other than the advanced version of the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect's Penetrating Light Liquid—the Jade Emperor Clear Eye Liquid!

For sword cultivators, it was of great help.

"It must be a pill formula that Wan Jian Pavilion obtained from the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect."

Luo Chen glanced at it, showing little interest.

Refining this pill wasn't difficult for him; he could do it casually.

But at present, pill refining was too minor an improvement to his strength.

He'd be better off focusing his energy elsewhere.

Putting away the pill formula, Luo Chen once again took out the Yuantu Sword.

In the repeated refining of sword qi, even though his proficiency in the sword canon hadn't increased much and the sword qi he had nurtured wasn't much, he found that his control over the Yuantu Sword was growing stronger and stronger.

Vaguely, he seemed to be building an intimate connection with the Yuantu Sword Spirit sealed in the third layer.

"Thousand Skies sword qi is truly hard to cultivate quickly. It can only be accumulated over time."

"But if I can communicate with the sword spirit, I can fully master this killing sword, which is comparable to a spirit treasure. For me right now, that would undoubtedly be the greatest improvement!"

With this beautiful hope, Luo Chen closed his eyes, mobilized the trace of Scarlet Firmament Qi within him that had yet to be refined, and began to circulate it again.

Strands of sword qi infused with his mana flowed across the Yuantu Sword.

Between man and sword, in a daze, there was a strange resonance.

(Chapter End)
