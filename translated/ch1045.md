# Chapter 1045: Swift Core Formation, Eastern Sea Coast

Heavenly waves are easily spent; an inch of summer is hard to keep.

Time flows on, fleeting and swift.

Twenty years had passed since Zhou Fuyou joined the Southern Heaven Dao Palace and became a disciple of Azure Frost Fairy of the Houtu Hall.

In those twenty years, Zhou Fuyou had rarely left his dwelling and seldom interacted with others.

Nor did he need much interaction.

Though he had taken a master, Azure Frost did not frequently instruct the many cultivators of Houtu Hall. Her guidance was limited to formally accepted Adept disciples. These Adepts then taught and passed on their knowledge to the Initiate-level registered disciples beneath them.

In principle, Zhou Fuyou should have received similar instruction.

But with his ancestor Zhou Yunshen's half-year of intensive personal guidance, he had already thoroughly sorted out the entire cultivation process from Initiate to Adept. Thus, he had little need for the ordinary teachings of those eight senior apprentice-brothers and sisters.

Over these twenty years, from time to time, fellow Initiate true cultivators of Houtu Hall would come to visit.

Unlike the earlier provocations, these visits were mostly goodwill—attempts to form a connection and make friends.

But Zhou Fuyou refused almost all of them, accepting only a single gathering invitation passed along by Zhou Yuwei.

That one time was somewhat special!

The host of that gathering was his "cousin," a female cultivator from the Green Parasol Lin clan named Lin Qingtong.

Naming her after the clan's territory showed how much the Lin family valued and favored this female cultivator.

Nor did she disappoint their expectations. After twenty years of cultivation in the Jade Pivot Court, she successfully reached Initiation at thirty-two years of age and was accepted into Houtu Hall as one of Azure Frost's registered disciples.

After being assigned a cave dwelling, Lin Qingtong held a small Initiation celebration—that was the gathering.

This kind of "gathering-style celebration" was quite popular within the Dao Palace. It was an excellent venue for fellow disciples to build relationships and the best opportunity to expand one's social network.

Especially the latter—even those with no usual connections could attend under the pretext of offering congratulations.

And at that gathering, Lin Qingtong's most heavyweight guest was not the long-established Initiate true cultivators of Houtu Hall, nor her Seventh Senior Brother who had dropped by to see her, but the rarely seen Ninth Senior Brother, Zhou Fuyou!

At the time, Zhou Fuyou's appearance caught many off guard. No one expected that Lin Qingtong, who had only just joined Houtu Hall, could invite Zhou Fuyou.

Only later did some of the inside story gradually spread.

Zhou Fuyou's mother, Lin Jingshu, came from the Green Parasol Lin clan. She and Lin Qingtong's father were half-siblings—children of the same father but different mothers.

It sounded a bit convoluted, but in terms of kinship, the two were indeed cousins.

At that gathering, many people deliberately tried to befriend Zhou Fuyou. Unfortunately, almost none succeeded in becoming his friend.

Just as the rumors said, the supremely talented Zhou Fuyou had an eccentric personality and disliked interacting with others.

After the gathering ended, Zhou Fuyou rarely went out again, focusing entirely on seclusion in the Dawn-Dusk Palace.

Until the twenty-year mark drew near.

That year, Zhou Fuyou was about forty years old.

And his cultivation level?

*Boom!*

Among the western mountains of the Southern Heaven Dao Palace, a thunderclap rang out from a clear sky.

Then came a continuous, ear-splitting sound, as if someone's joints were cracking, or like peals of thunder rolling on.

In the sky, dark clouds spread, silver serpents flickering.

*Drip!*

At some point, a single raindrop fell.

Then, a downpour poured forth.

Before many could even react, vast amounts of heaven and earth spiritual energy converged toward the Dawn-Dusk Palace.

Wind and clouds gathered instantly; dragon and tiger roared in succession.

A tornado formed by spiritual energy pouring in spread outward in all directions.

Large quantities of nearby second-order spirit veins were attracted, releasing vast spiritual energy that joined that heaven-piercing tornado.

Such a scene—even the dullest cultivator realized what was happening.

"Core Formation!"

"Someone is about to condense a golden core?"

"But how can it be here? This area is mostly for Initiate true cultivators' cultivation. The highest spirit vein grade is only top-grade second-order—completely unsuitable for breaking through to the Adept stage!"

"That direction... could it be?"

"The Dawn-Dusk Palace!"

"Zhou Fuyou—he's actually about to form his core?"

Amid the exclamations, a voice came from Houtu Hall, perched atop the mountain peaks.

"Reckless!"

The voice carried a tone of reproach, yet also held some relief and joy.

Accompanying this voice was a beam of green light descending from the heavens.

After the green light fell from the sky, the earth rumbled!

The spirit veins stored deep in the earth's crust seemed to have their restraints undone, and a sea of spiritual energy burst forth.

This spiritual energy, like a living force, joined the spiritual energy tornado, pushing the person involved one step further.

Inside Houtu Hall.

Azure Frost Fairy, awakened from seclusion, watched this scene and couldn't help but breathe a sigh of relief.

Her hastily arrived senior disciple, Shi Miaoyin, felt the same. "Little Junior Brother is too reckless. He tried to force core formation without any notice at all. Fortunately, Master, you emerged from seclusion to help, or else all his efforts might have come to nothing!"

Azure Frost had initially thought the same, but after her divine sense enveloped a thousand li in all directions, her opinion changed.

She raised her head, gazing through the window at the dark clouds covering the sky, and murmured, "I'm afraid it's not recklessness!"

Shi Miaoyin was stunned, not understanding what her master meant.

Azure Frost didn't explain further. Instead, she frowned and continued scanning her surroundings with divine sense, the confusion on her face growing deeper.

The spiritual energy changes around them covered a full thousand li and were still expanding outward.

This was far too strange.

The spirit vein restriction she had undone only served to accelerate the process—it wasn't the main cause.

In fact, from her observation, even without her help, Zhou Fuyou probably wouldn't have failed. At most, it would have taken a bit longer.

"Sensing heaven and earth?"

"Impossible—he's only just condensed his golden core!"

"Could it be because of his Profound Nether Dao Body?"

"But even two hundred years ago, when Shi Ju, who possessed the Nine Yang Dao Body, formed his core, this situation never occurred!"

Azure Frost was puzzled, but that didn't stop her from making the best judgment.

She could no longer allow Zhou Fuyou to absorb external spiritual energy without restraint. Otherwise, the primary and secondary would be reversed, affecting the purity of his golden core.

With a thought, she formed a hand seal.

Instantly, a hazy green light slowly pushed out from her palm.

Shi Miaoyin watched this green light and felt her own mana become lively, her flesh and blood suffused with comfort.

Her eyes widened, and she couldn't help but exclaim, "This is Master's divine ability—the Great Heaven Creation Seal!"

Azure Frost seemed not to hear the exclamation. After pushing out the green light, she suddenly flipped her hand.

Instantly!

The violent changes in external spiritual energy were suppressed and began to recede.

The influence range of the spiritual energy tornado also stopped at the thousand-li boundary.

"That's enough!"

Seeing this, Azure Frost made a rough estimate and nodded in satisfaction.

Then she instructed, "Miaoyin, after Zhou Fuyou successfully forms his core, tell him to come to Houtu Hall."

"Yes, Master!"

...

"Enough!"

"Stop!"

Inside the Dawn-Dusk Palace, Zhou Fuyou sat cross-legged in the void. His robes rustled, and his black hair whipped like snakes.

His body was now like a bottomless pit. A sea of spiritual energy poured in and vanished without a trace.

Only in his qi sea did a massive golden core rise and fall, dazzlingly bright.

Core formation—he had already succeeded.

But the mysterious connection with the outside world still existed, preventing his body from stopping its absorption of external spiritual energy.

Zhou Fuyou now felt as if his body might burst.

Yet his qi sea, like a bottomless abyss, clearly told him he could continue!

"I can't go on!"

"I must stop!"

Zhou Fuyou controlled his golden core, using his derived mana to seal his meridians and acupoints, cutting off the outside world.

This act of "going against heaven" brought obvious backlash.

With a series of *puff puff puff* sounds, finger-sized holes burst open all over his body.

Just then, a hazy green light descended, enveloping his entire body.

The green light wrapped around him, bringing a feeling of warmth, and his injuries began to heal rapidly.

"Master has acted!"

Zhou Fuyou was startled, then immediately gathered his mind and began to deal with the situation inside his body.

Time passed bit by bit.

The dark clouds hanging over Houtu Hall thinned considerably, but a light drizzle still fell.

Everyone's attention was focused on the Dawn-Dusk Palace. Some even wanted to go and visit, but with the Houtu Hall Master in charge, no one could approach.

Three days and three nights passed like this.

Then, a white-clad figure stepped out of the Dawn-Dusk Palace.

Looking at the overcast sky, the white-robed Daoist first frowned, then smiled freely.

With that smile, for some reason, the drizzling rain gradually stopped, and the lingering dark clouds slowly dispersed.

The sky cleared, the azure expanse washed clean.

*Hoo...*

Exhaling a mouthful of turbid qi, the white-robed Daoist paid no attention to the increasingly numerous gazes fixed on him. He took a step and ascended to the mountain peak.

...

Zhou Fuyou arrived early. The gates of Houtu Hall were still closed.

Only his senior apprentice-sister waited there, specifically for him.

"Master wants to see me?"

"Yes."

"Good timing. I also want to thank Master for her help during my core formation."

Though Zhou Fuyou didn't know what would have happened if he had continued absorbing heaven and earth spiritual energy three days ago, he understood the principle that too much is as bad as too little.

Even having forcibly stopped, he felt his current situation was beyond normal.

"Before seeing Master, there's a letter here from your hometown."

"Oh?"

Zhou Fuyou took the envelope from his senior apprentice-sister and used the unique Zhou family restriction taught by his ancestor to undo the seal.

He scanned the contents.

Then his senior apprentice-sister saw an unprecedented brilliant smile appear on the young man's face—even purer and cleaner than the clear sky at that moment.

"What is it? Good news?" Shi Miaoyin asked curiously.

Zhou Fuyou couldn't suppress the grin spreading across his face. "My little sister has successfully reached Initiation."

Shi Miaoyin was taken aback, finding it somewhat amusing.

This little junior brother hadn't been this happy even when he himself succeeded in core formation, yet his little sister's Initiation success made him lose his composure like this.

Perhaps their sibling bond was truly deep!

Shi Miaoyin chose pleasant words. "Your sister is not old. This letter was sent three years ago, when you were still in seclusion. It seems her talent is quite good, to achieve Initiation so early."

Good talent?

Zhou Fuyou's lips curled slightly, but he didn't clarify.

He did feel a great weight lifted from his heart.

Calculating the time, the effect of that pill spirit root was about to expire. That his sister Zhou Qingyu could succeed in Initiation before then was extremely fortunate.

And now he himself had formed his golden core.

Zhou Fuyou's heart stirred. "Senior Apprentice-Sister, could my little sister come to the Southern Heaven Dao Palace as an auditor?"

"This..." Shi Miaoyin's face showed hesitation.

Zhou Fuyou pressed on. "Though my little sister has missed the foundation-building period of the Seven Stars Court, she can directly join Houtu Hall and receive guidance from you all and Master. As for coming with existing skills to seek instruction—you all did the same back then, didn't you?"

Shi Miaoyin thought about it. There were indeed such precedents.

But as the Dao Palace's system gradually perfected itself, such cases had become rare.

She considered. "This matter depends on Master's intention. But given how important you are in Master's eyes, as long as you ask, it shouldn't be a problem."

Zhou Fuyou's mood grew even brighter.

Even when other senior apprentice-brothers and sisters appeared later, he greeted each one with a smile.

This surprised the senior disciples, who were used to Zhou Fuyou's usual cold indifference, but they all sincerely congratulated him on his successful core formation.

After some pleasantries, they discussed the reason for their master's sudden summons.

"What does Master want to see us about today? It's not time for regular instruction yet!"

"Perhaps it's about Little Junior Brother's successful core formation?"

"In my opinion, it might also be related to the coastal demon beast disturbances."

As they chatted, the gates of Houtu Hall slowly opened.

Everyone straightened their robes and filed in.

Zhou Fuyou was last, his good mood turning somewhat anxious.

Would Master agree to his request?

As soon as he entered, Zhou Fuyou saw his master seated above—elegant as an orchid, noble as a lotus.

"Greetings, Master!"

Everyone bowed in unison.

"Mmm."

Azure Frost hummed softly, her beautiful eyes landing directly on Zhou Fuyou.

Her divine sense swept over him, and suddenly she let out a soft exclamation.

"Oh?"

The others were puzzled and also looked at Zhou Fuyou.

At this moment, Zhou Fuyou wasn't deliberately hiding his cultivation level, so his early Adept aura was very clear.

This wasn't surprising.

But under everyone's scrutiny, they also discovered something unexpected.

His mana fluctuation wasn't mixed and unstable like every newly promoted Adept's. Instead, it was as steady as an abyss, showing not the slightest restlessness.

This scene drew exclamations throughout the hall.

"Enough. Your little junior brother has extraordinary talent and successful cultivation—what's there to make such a fuss about!"

Azure Frost scolded, and everyone quieted down.

She still looked at Zhou Fuyou, then spoke slowly. "Too fast, Fuyou. Your core formation was too fast, leaving many things unprepared."

Zhou Fuyou said respectfully, "Master refers to the matter of the embryonic artifact?"

"Mmm." Azure Frost nodded slightly. "The Calming Sea Pearl you wish to refine is a rare treasure once crafted by a great sage of our sect. It has the power to suppress the heavens and calm the oceans! Naturally, the required materials are extremely numerous and precious. Even with my authority, I've only managed to apply for a portion so far."

Zhou Fuyou wasn't surprised. He had seen the artifact catalog and knew how precious and numerous the materials for refining the Calming Sea Pearl were.

With a thought, Zhou Fuyou took out several boxes and opened them one by one.

"Master, I have some things here that might serve as supplements for refining my lifebound artifact."

Everyone looked, then showed surprise again.

The most eye-catching was a glossy lotus seed, radiating powerful aura.

Then came a transparent, brilliant red rhomboid crystal, a fist-sized iron ingot, and a pile of fine, sparkling sand.

"Top-grade water lotus seed!"

"Top-grade fire spirit diamond!"

"This iron ingot—could it be Nine Yang Geng Metal? And this sand should be the legendary star sand!"

Azure Frost named each material one by one, showing her extensive knowledge.

But by the end, everyone could clearly hear the surprise in their master's voice.

In truth, they hadn't expected Zhou Fuyou to possess so many precious materials.

These were all top-grade fourth-order materials—things even Sage cultivators craved but couldn't obtain!

Zhou Fuyou said frankly, "My family's ancestor dotes on me greatly, so before I left, he gave me these things."

"This isn't just doting—it's emptying his entire storehouse!" Azure Frost sighed, then collected all the materials. "With these materials as supplements, refining the Calming Sea Pearl can now be put on the agenda. Rest assured, I will invite the best artifact smith from the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect to refine your lifebound artifact!"

"Thank you, Master!"

Zhou Fuyou bowed again.

After storing the materials, Azure Frost waved her hand, and a beam of light fell before Zhou Fuyou.

Zhou Fuyou looked closely and understood in his heart.

It was the continuation of his lifebound technique, the *Dew Cicada Art*. His master had actually found it for him.

"Keep it safe. Look at it later when you have time."

Before he could voice his thanks, Azure Frost cut off his thoughts and then straightened her expression.

"I called you all here today because of the coastal demon calamity!"

Everyone stood at attention, listening.

"Ever since the collapse of the Central Continent, the demon race of the Eastern Sea has grown restless, frequently appearing in the sea region."

"If it were only that, it would be tolerable. But they have grown bold enough to encroach upon the territory of our Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!"

"Moreover, some have discovered figures from the Northern Sea demon race among them!"

"In recent years, coastal demon beasts have appeared more and more frequently, and high-ranking demon kings have been stirring up trouble from time to time. Relying solely on the aristocratic clans and common families for suppression and defense has become insufficient. Therefore, it is time for our Southern Heaven Dao Palace to act."

"This time, I will send five of you, Adept disciples, to join with the other three halls, forming a force of twenty Adept-level experts and one hundred Initiate true cultivators. You will go to the Eastern Sea coast, subdue demons, eliminate evil, and clear the demonic atmosphere!"

These words explained everyone's doubts, and their faces lit up with excitement.

Clearly, this mission wasn't just about subduing demons. It was also meant to temper them.

Relying solely on bitter cultivation, resource accumulation, and master guidance cannot produce top-tier experts.

The tempering of blood and fire is the true cradle of the strong!

Thus, everyone had long been prepared.

Each person waited for their master to name them, hoping to be among the five.

But the first name their master called out exceeded everyone's expectations.

"Zhou Fuyou, are you willing to go on this expedition?"
