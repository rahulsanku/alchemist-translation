# Chapter 970: Divine Lord Ascends, Southern Frontier Concluded

The butterfly fluttered, its trajectory dreamlike.

As its wings trembled, trails of hazy radiance swirled in their wake, causing even the surrounding origin qi of heaven and earth to churn and surge.

At the sight of this object, Luo Chen's heart could not help but rise with a hint of delight, and he subconsciously desired to possess it.

But before a generation's Divine Lord, how could he dare to act rashly?

Suppressing the longing in his heart, Luo Chen swallowed his saliva and asked, "Is this a Fortune Gu?"

"Indeed."

"But according to legend, isn't the Fortune Gu a mighty divine gu that brings good luck and abundant opportunities to cultivators?"

"Have you forgotten what I said earlier?"

Luo Chen was startled, instinctively recalling what Netherspring Divine Lord had said before.

When fortune is too heavy, blessings turn into calamities.

This statement was not difficult to understand. It was like the examples of an innocent man being blamed for possessing a coveted treasure, or a child holding gold in a bustling market—when one's strength is too weak, overly precious things are actually harmful to oneself.

But could Netherspring Divine Lord and the Holy Land Life and Death Gate be considered weak?

Luo Chen's gaze swept across the omnipresent origin qi of heaven and earth, a thoughtful expression appearing on his face.

Netherspring Divine Lord manipulated the Fortune Gu while looking at Luo Chen, his eyes carrying approval, and then personally explained.

"The Life and Death Gate has three great divine gu: Fortune and Misfortune depend on each other, Life and Death coexist. Since ancient times, the Fortune Gu and the Misfortune Gu have always existed simultaneously. Once separated, they could brew uncontrollable great disasters."

"In those years, the senior brother who controlled the Misfortune Gu had important matters to attend to in the Mystic Realm, so he borrowed the Star Gate passage of the Tianyuan Dao Sect to leave the Mountains and Seas Realm. Before leaving, for the sake of my cultivation, he left the Misfortune Gu with me."

"Unexpectedly, the Misfortune Gu was lost outside."

Luo Chen frowned. "Was it taken away by Feng Jiang, the Initiate cultivator of the Nether Origin Sect in the Eastern Wilderness?"

"Correct!"

Luo Chen shook his head. "Although Feng Jiang was strong, he was ultimately only an Initiate cultivator. How could he take this gu from under your nose?"

"Haha." Netherspring Divine Lord laughed heartily, his tone relaxed as he said, "I let him take it."

"Eh?" Luo Chen was stunned.

Netherspring Divine Lord did not avoid the topic and spoke frankly. "My cultivation was stuck at the mid Ascendant stage at the time, and it was difficult to break through for a long time. The Fortune Gu was also restrained by the Misfortune Gu, making it hard for me to take that most important step. Therefore, I distributed the Misfortune Gu to my juniors under the guise of inheritance, and even turned a blind eye to Feng Jiang taking it away. In this way, the Fortune Gu had no suppression, and my cultivation began to advance by leaps and bounds."

So that was it!

Some doubts that had been pressing on Luo Chen's heart were now resolved.

Although Feng Jiang was strong enough to roam the five continents, how could he take a treasure from under an Ascendant cultivator's nose?

Netherspring Divine Lord chose to let Feng Jiang take this object away, rather than a disciple within the sect, probably because once the Misfortune Gu was separated from the Fortune Gu for too long, it would attract too many calamities and disasters.

In that case, Feng Jiang had become a mere tool.

As for how to recover this gu, for Netherspring Divine Lord, who had achieved great success in his cultivation, it was presumably not a difficult matter.

Or rather, he didn't even need to act personally—wasn't Luo Chen himself delivering it back to him?

While Luo Chen was pondering, the red butterfly circled the Primordial Unity Cauldron for a long time but still could not lure out the gray butterfly.

Netherspring Divine Lord subconsciously frowned.

While changing his techniques, he continued conversing with Luo Chen.

"But later developments also exceeded my control. The Fortune Gu not only helped me deepen my comprehension of law true intent and condense my Primordial Spirit Dharma Aspect, but also attracted a massive amount of origin qi of heaven and earth, thereby affecting the entire Southern Frontier cultivation world."

"With the massive influx of origin qi of heaven and earth, high-level cultivators could hardly maintain their original minds. With the slightest inattention, they would fall into qi deviation."

"With my ability, I could only shelter the cultivators of the Life and Death Gate, but was powerless to do anything for other sects."

"To prevent the situation from escalating, I had no choice but to close the mountain gate and forbid the Holy Land's cultivators from interacting with the mortal world."

As he spoke, he suddenly grunted.

"Come out!"

With a flick of his finger, the Primordial Unity Cauldron emitted a deep, low hum once more.

That gray butterfly finally broke free from its restraints and emerged from the cauldron.

Was this the Misfortune Gu?

Luo Chen looked up at the gray butterfly. In fact, this was also the first time he had seen the true form of this gu.

As soon as it came out, it entangled itself with the Fortune Gu.

The dense origin qi of heaven and earth around them also seemed to calm down.

This change caused Luo Chen's mind to fluctuate.

"The dao of gu is truly extraordinary!"

Seeing this scene, Netherspring Divine Lord also breathed a sigh of relief as if a heavy burden had been lifted.

"Although extraordinary, it is ultimately not the orthodox path of our cultivation. It was merely obtained by chance by the main sect of the Mystic Realm in those years, brought down when the Mountains and Seas were opened, and gradually developed and grew. It is precisely because of this that this dao currently has no method to break through to the Void Refining stage. That senior brother of mine had no choice but to go to the upper realm, attempting to borrow the power of the main sect to advance to the Void Refining stage."

As he spoke, he casually tossed it.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron spun and flew in front of Luo Chen.

"Your lifebound dharma artifact is extraordinary!"

Seeing the other party voluntarily return his lifebound dharma artifact, the great stone in Luo Chen's heart finally fell to the ground.

Of course, he did not show it on his face. He was even more curious about some ancient secrets revealed in the other party's words.

After Netherspring Divine Lord retrieved the Misfortune Gu, his mood seemed to become even better, and he did not begrudge introducing some secrets rarely known to the world.

Why did the Mountains and Seas Realm have the tradition of the War of Opening?

Because the process of humanity's growth itself was a large-scale War of Opening!

Ten thousand years ago, the five sects of the Mystic Realm, led by the Tianyuan Dao Sect, descended upon the Mountains and Seas Realm and launched a campaign of conquest and opening against this realm.

The transition from the ancient era to the upper ancient era was also that period.

After the five human sects established their dominion over the five continents of the Mountains and Seas, the history of the Mountains and Seas Realm declared the end of the bloody era of the human-demon war in the upper ancient era.

"The Star Gate of the Tianyuan Dao Sect is not just the name of an organization; it is even more a powerful spirit treasure, possessing the ability to traverse space and roam the universe. It is said to have been refined for the Tianyuan Great Sovereign by a powerful demon clan expert personally."

"Our five sects also relied on this treasure to cross the void and descend upon the Mountains and Seas Realm."

"But for some unknown reason, after the upper ancient era, the Star Gate passage became extremely unstable and difficult to use under normal circumstances. Only when a cultivator ascends, attracting the massive spiritual mechanism of this realm, can this object be activated to return to the Mystic Realm."

"In this way, the Tianyuan Dao Sect valued this treasure more and more, rarely lending it to the cultivators of the other four sects. The relationship between the five sects gradually became estranged, and even the connection with the main sect of the Mystic Realm was completely severed."

"Under these circumstances, the five Holy Lands stranded in the Mountains and Seas Realm became increasingly fierce in their competition for the interests of this realm. That the Tianyuan Dao Sect is now sending troops to three continents, intending to swallow the world, was something that could have been predicted long ago."

Involving ancient secrets, Luo Chen listened with surging excitement.

He had had similar speculations in his early years, and now they were finally confirmed.

And history was even more magnificent and grand than he had imagined.

Five sects from beyond the heavens, descending upon a realm.

Slaying demons, driving out the wild, opening up five continents.

"If you want to know more, you can take a look at this book in your spare time."

As he spoke, Netherspring Divine Lord took out a jade slip.

Luo Chen respectfully received it. Glancing at it, he saw—*Mystic Realm Secret Record*.

He said somewhat sheepishly, "Such a valuable item must be one of the inheritances of your sect. This junior feels unworthy to receive it!"

Even as he said this, his hand gripped the jade slip tightly.

Netherspring Divine Lord smiled. "It's nothing. What I gave you is merely a copy. Moreover, since you returned the Misfortune Gu when I was inconvenient to act personally, giving you some recompense is only fitting."

As he spoke, his tone paused slightly, and he suddenly took out another object.

"This object, I also bestow upon you!"

Luo Chen looked closely, and it was none other than that pair of yin-yang fish.

The Life and Death Coexisting Gu!

"This..."

At this moment, Luo Chen truly hesitated.

He spoke with a tone of refusal. "This gu is one of the three great inherited divine gu of your sect. This young one cannot accept it."

"There's nothing you can't accept. After all, it has not yet entered the fifth rank, only the fourth rank." Netherspring Divine Lord was very indifferent, his aversion to the Life and Death Coexisting Gu far greater than his fondness. "A fifth-rank Life and Death Coexisting Gu is a shortcut for cultivators like us, capable of both strengthening combat power and assisting cultivation. But shortcuts inevitably have their price. My senior brother, Yin Yangzi, had a promising youth, yet lived barely over a thousand years before dying early, precisely because of this burden. Leaving behind this fourth-rank Life and Death Coexisting Gu, there will inevitably be juniors within the sect who are tempted by it. Not to mention whether they can succeed in the future, the process of cultivating it from the fourth rank to the fifth rank alone would cause who knows how much bloodshed and turmoil."

Luo Chen still hesitated.

Netherspring Divine Lord smiled. "Don't worry. This gu is only at the fourth rank now and cannot contract with an Ascendant existence. Moreover, I have a secret art that can help you preserve your life when the contracted target dies. I imagine that with this gu, it may have miraculous effects at critical moments."

Hearing this, Luo Chen's eyes could not help but light up.

A secret art that could eliminate the negative effects of the Life and Death Coexisting Gu?

He could see that Netherspring Divine Lord was in a talkative mood, so he took the initiative to ask.

"Is there really such a secret art? Then why didn't Senior Yin Yangzi use it back then?"

"Because it didn't exist back then!" Netherspring Divine Lord smiled wryly.

Luo Chen was taken aback. Well, it was obvious that this secret art had only been researched in the past two thousand years.

"Senior Brother Yin Yangzi had researched such secret arts before setting up the Yin Yang Realm, but time was too short, and he could only give up, seeking instead the method of feigning death and reincarnation. Unfortunately, feigning death became true death, and what was reincarnated was merely an evil thought. The only gain was leaving behind a juvenile Nether Dragon, which, after the passage of time, has just stepped into the fifth rank."

"This secret art of mine was developed from the foundation of his earlier research."

"But don't rely on it too much. Its effective range is ultimately only within the fourth-rank Initiate stage. Once both parties' realms exceed this level, its effectiveness is greatly reduced."

"Of course, there's no need to worry about the situation after the Initiate stage. Once the contracted target and you both break through the Initiate stage, this fourth-rank Life and Death Coexisting Gu itself won't have much effect, at most some minor backlash."

After this explanation, Luo Chen finally accepted this gift.

In fact, he couldn't have forcefully refused anyway.

Who could forcefully refuse a gift from an Ascendant Great Power?

After sealing the Life and Death Coexisting Gu together with that secret art using restrictions, Luo Chen then curiously asked about that Nether Dragon that had just stepped into the fifth rank.

Netherspring Divine Lord's answer was—let it go!

Luo Chen was quite puzzled by this answer.

Although that Nether Dragon had only just entered the fifth rank, which was also consistent with his most basic judgment of its strength, it was still a fifth-rank desolate beast!

If properly tamed and nurtured, it would be enough to become the Life and Death Gate's guardian spirit beast.

How could he just let it go?

Regarding this, Netherspring Divine Lord gave a simple explanation.

"Once I leave, there will be no one in the sect who can tame this beast. Leaving it behind would instead bring disaster upon the sect."

"Leave?" Luo Chen was stunned.

Netherspring Divine Lord smiled. "Yes. My cultivation has long been accomplished. The reason I lingered was because I was suppressed by the ocean of origin qi attracted by the Fortune Gu. Now that fortune and misfortune depend on each other, this ocean of origin qi can no longer suppress me. At present, there is only the path of departure."

Luo Chen subconsciously looked up. "Is it returning to the Mystic Realm?"

But soon, he reacted.

To return to the Mystic Realm, one needed the assistance of the Star Gate.

But this was the Life and Death Gate in the Southern Frontier. Where was the Dao Sect's Star Gate?

Thus, departure had only two explanations.

One was sitting in transformation, the other was ascending.

Based on the other party's survival time, his lifespan had probably long exceeded the two-thousand-year limit of an Ascendant Great Power.

Luo Chen asked cautiously, "May I ask what realm you have reached, Senior?"

Netherspring Divine Lord asked in return, "Do you know why the world calls me Divine Lord?"

Luo Chen blurted out, "Could it be that you have already reached the realm of a Void Refining True Lord?"

"That is not the case. In this realm, aside from some native top-tier desolate beasts, no living being can break through to the True Lord realm. However!" Netherspring Divine Lord's tone shifted, and his dharma power rippled slowly like a tide. "If I succeed in ascending, the True Lord realm is merely a matter of course!"

Under the immense tide of dharma power, Luo Chen was uncontrollably pushed backward.

He looked with amazement at the blue-robed old man standing at the pinnacle of the sea of clouds.

A massive Primordial Spirit emerged from the top of his head.

The ocean of origin qi that had not yet dispersed transformed into myriad rays of light, rolling and surging, pouring into the Primordial Spirit.

Not only that, but under Luo Chen's Crimson Eye Golden Gaze, a massive amount of spiritual mechanism converged toward Netherspring Divine Lord's Primordial Spirit.

In just a few moments, the originally somewhat illusory Primordial Spirit became extremely solid.

Even its form and state were undergoing inexplicable changes, with hazy law true intent lingering outside, forming an illusory giant's image.

In the blink of an eye, it was a hundred zhang tall, like a giant overlooking heaven and earth.

Brilliant rays of light broke through the mist of the sea of clouds, shining straight down like heavenly light.

This change was even more spectacular than when Alchemy Saint Chu Yan ascended back then.

Connecting this with the other party's realm and previous words, a thought gradually formed in Luo Chen's heart.

"Dharma Aspect!"

The path of qi refinement was a joint transformation of dharma power and soul.

Especially the latter, which ran through all of a cultivator's realms!

Soul, spiritual sense, divine sense, Sage Infant, and then the Primordial Spirit transformation upon advancing to Ascendant.

What came after Ascendant?

According to worldly legend, to break through to the Void Refining stage, one had to condense a Dharma Aspect, replacing the physical body, to roam the ocean of origin qi, never again fearing the danger of being assimilated by heaven and earth.

As for how to condense a Dharma Aspect, Luo Chen did not know.

But from what he could see now, Netherspring Divine Lord had indeed already achieved this step.

"No wonder he said that as long as he succeeded in ascending, he could step into the Void Refining stage. He has already begun to condense a preliminary Dharma Aspect."

And below the pinnacle of the sea of clouds.

In the battlefield of the Life and Death Gate, which was like a Shura hell, countless cultivators sensed the heavenly and earthly changes.

Heavenly light broke through the mist, radiance illuminating the myriad realms.

An illusory giant's image stood atop the sea of clouds, overlooking the mountains and seas.

Everyone was stunned, not knowing what this scene meant.

It was the cultivators of the Tianyuan Dao Sect who reacted the fastest!

Because decades ago, they had seen this scene before.

"Ascension!"

"Someone is about to ascend!"

"Who would ascend at this time? Could it be the Life and Death Gate's ancestor, Netherspring Divine Lord?"

A figure suddenly appeared before the crowd. It was one of the five great ancestors of the Tianyuan Dao Sect, Ancestor Liancheng.

At this moment, he looked upward, his expression uncertain.

"It is an act of ascension, but why has Netherspring Divine Lord manifested an illusory phase? According to legend, wasn't he stagnant in his cultivation to extend his lifespan, unable even to move freely, only able to sit in meditation at the Life and Death Gate?"

With this thought, Ancestor Liancheng sensed an inexplicable danger.

Why did the other party choose to ascend neither early nor late, but precisely when the army was pressing down on the gate?

Ancestor Liancheng began to retreat.

But it seemed a little too late.

The illusory giant standing in the sea of clouds suddenly opened its eyes.

Those were eyes containing sun, moon, stars, and the vicissitudes of the seas.

Its gaze looked downward, and it casually pressed down with its hand.

Boom!

The ocean of origin qi that had still been lingering suddenly sank.

Terrifying origin qi instantly poured down into the Life and Death Gate.

At this moment, the origin qi seemed to possess intelligence, bypassing all the cultivators of the Life and Death Gate and striking the enemy.

Bang!

Bang!

Bang!

Sound after sound rang out continuously. With each sound, a puff of blood mist appeared, representing the disappearance of a life.

The cultivators of the Fierce Peak Stiff Alliance, the cultivators of the Tianyuan Dao Sect...

Ancestor Liancheng watched this scene, his eyes wide with rage.

"Netherspring! How dare you strike at my sect's cultivators! Do you not fear the Dao Sect's retaliation?"

In the sea of clouds, Luo Chen, dwelling in a corner, also suddenly came to his senses.

Yes!

With Netherspring Divine Lord's current peak state, about to ascend, there were few in the world who could match him. It would naturally be easy to annihilate all the invading enemies.

But once he successfully ascended, the Life and Death Gate would have no Ascendant Great Power.

The main force of the Fierce Peak Stiff Alliance was here, and destroying them was one thing.

But the Tianyuan Dao Sect still existed. Was Netherspring Divine Lord not afraid of the Dao Sect retaliating against the Life and Death Gate afterward?

Was he a cold-hearted person who only cared about the immediate situation?

Luo Chen did not think so!

From the fact that he had closed the mountain gate and sheltered his disciples for several hundred years, one could see his sincere intentions.

Why did he do this?

Netherspring Divine Lord did not answer Ancestor Liancheng. While pressing down the ocean of origin qi, he even pointed across the void.

Target, Liancheng!

Swish!

With one finger, the void exploded, and a black line stretching thousands of miles lay across heaven and earth.

Ancestor Liancheng's soul nearly fled in terror. Without hesitation, he dove into the void.

But in a moment, he was forced out, as if this sky was no longer under his control.

A thumb-sized bloody hole appeared in his chest and abdomen, and a large amount of dharma power uncontrollably dissipated.

With a mad expression, he shot into the distance at a speed he had never achieved in his life.

In the blink of an eye, he disappeared from everyone's sight.

Dead silence fell.

All that remained was a faint warning from Netherspring Divine Lord.

"Enter the Southern Frontier again, and you die!"

The illusory phase in the sea of clouds slowly withdrew its hand, as if it did not intend to strike again.

Within the Life and Death Gate, there were still some remaining enemies, such as Alliance Master Songyang, Zhan Bingtao, and the Burial Life Elder.

Their strength was far greater than ordinary cultivators, and they had managed to survive under Netherspring Divine Lord's blanket strike.

But even so, they had all lost their courage.

When the world's most top-tier Ascendant Great Power struck, killing a million cultivators with one blow and repelling Ancestor Liancheng with one finger, how could they dare to continue attacking?

One by one, they fled back the way they came without regard for anything.

The cultivators of the Life and Death Gate made a symbolic interception or two before stopping.

Everyone's eyes were fixed tightly on their own ancestor, as if afraid of losing something.

A sigh came leisurely.

"From now on, it's up to you."

Three streams of light fell from the sea of clouds—one red, one gray, and one cyan—landing respectively in the hands of three late-Initiate Grand Cultivators of the Life and Death Gate.

Netherspring Divine Lord withdrew his gaze and looked at Luo Chen in the distance.

"Young friend, I have retrieved the Misfortune Gu, but your small cauldron is miraculous. It still retains a trace of the Misfortune Gu's aura. A great calamity may come unexpectedly in the future. I have left behind a method. You can simply activate the residual Misfortune Gu aura, which may be able to withstand it for a while."

"Now, I will see you off on your way!"

A gentle stream of origin qi surged toward Luo Chen, pushing him into the distance.

Luo Chen's gaze was quite complex. His time of contact with this powerful being had been extremely brief.

Although brief, the goodwill the other party had shown was undisguised, and he had given much guidance.

Now that the other party was about to ascend, he had even specially seen him off. How could one not be moved?

He bowed respectfully.

"Thank you, Senior. If Luo Chen has the strength in the future, I will look after the Life and Death Gate a little."

Netherspring Divine Lord smiled slightly. "You have a kind heart."

"Congratulations to Senior on your ascension. May your immortal path flourish!"

Only these words from Luo Chen remained in the sea of clouds.

But among the mountains and fields below, there was a thunderous chorus of farewells.

"We respectfully see off the Ancestor on his ascension!"
