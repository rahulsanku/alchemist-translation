# Chapter 1090: Fourth Generation Sect Leader, Old Friend Forms Nascent Soul

"Luo Lingxi!"

Under Luo Chen's gaze, Li Yingzhang calmly spoke this name.

This name did not seem unexpected to Luo Chen. There was no great surprise, only a trace of doubt.

"Can he handle it?"

"Why not?" Li Yingzhang countered. "In terms of status, he is the child born of you, the First Generation Sect Leader and Supreme Elder, together. He is also my disciple, the Third Generation Sect Leader. There is no one more legitimate than him."

Luo Chen shook his head. "You know that's not what I mean."

He was referring to ability!

If the Luo Tian Sect were still that small-time Golden Core force, Luo Chen wouldn't mind letting his son practice his hand on this foundation that held little use for him.

But the current Luo Tian Sect already had over a dozen Nascent Soul-level combatants within its ranks alone, Golden Core cultivators emerged in an endless stream, and the number of core disciples under its command had long exceeded ten thousand.

Most importantly, the Luo Tian Sect now held a pivotal position throughout the entire Eastern Wilderness.

Once Luo Chen truly succeeded in becoming an Ascendant, the Luo Tian Sect's status would instantly transcend everything!

It would directly rival the position the Nether Origin Sect once held in the Eastern Wilderness!

Such a behemoth concerned the fate of millions upon millions of people, the rise and fall of an entire continent's cultivation world, and even the balance of the entire Mountains and Seas Realm.

If the chosen successor to the sect leader position lacked ability, who knew what chaos might ensue.

Li Yingzhang understood what Luo Chen meant. He pondered for a moment.

After a while, he firmly nodded.

"Luo Lingxi has been under my tutelage for over a hundred years. In these hundred-plus years, he hasn't just cultivated his realm to the Golden Core stage. Through constant exposure, he has also learned a great deal about managing the sect, and he frequently handles trivial matters for me."

"To be honest, in the twenty years since the Heaven-Earth War ended, I have gradually delegated authority, handing over important affairs to Luo Lingxi, including interactions with external Nascent Soul superior sects, and so on. He has handled them all quite well. At the very least, no one inside or outside the sect can find fault."

"Therefore, whether it's the righteousness of his status or the test of his ability, in my view, he is the most perfect candidate!"

Finally.

Li Yingzhang hesitated for a moment, then looked up at Luo Chen with a serious expression.

"Supreme Elder, there is an old saying: when recommending the worthy, do not avoid your own kin. Your understanding of Lingxi might still be stuck on that immature youth from a hundred years ago. But in reality, over this past century, he has grown far, far too much."

"This child was born under a halo, yet couldn't cultivate. He was once nearly led astray by his... mother's misguided influence. But precisely because of these things, it forged his tenacious character."

"In his subsequent cultivation and work, he has been more serious than anyone else. As his master, I have witnessed every bit of it."

"Especially with you as his role model, he has never slackened!"

Luo Chen listened quietly to these words, then smiled slightly.

"Since you say so, I naturally cannot refuse. Call him in!"

Li Yingzhang smiled as well and transmitted his voice outside.

Soon.

In the quiet hall, light footsteps could be heard.

Luo Lingxi walked in and respectfully bowed to Luo Chen.

"Your son greets Father."

He bent at the waist, head lowered, his posture respectful yet not lacking in composure.

An indistinct pressure, accompanied by a burning gaze, enveloped him, bringing immense pressure.

Unknowingly, cold sweat had soaked through his clothes from behind.

"Raise your head."

Luo Lingxi did as he was told, slowly lifting his head to look directly at the man above.

Their eyes met, like looking into a mirror.

The difference was that the man above, though seemingly youthful, exuded a majestic aura like a mountain. Under his direct gaze, it was almost impossible to stand.

This was Luo Chen, the Alchemy Sage who had roamed the Eastern Wilderness for centuries!

The one who had been renowned throughout the Mountains and Seas Realm, once the strongest below the Ascendant stage!

One of the strongest humans of the current era, who had slain an Ascendant in reverse on Heaven-Earth Island!

Facing his gaze, who could remain calm and unmoved?

In a daze, Luo Lingxi heard the question from the person above.

"Do you have the confidence to carry on the legacy and lead the Luo Tian Sect to create new brilliance?"

Luo Lingxi took a deep breath and answered heavily, "I do!"

"Good! Then so be it. You are the Fourth Generation Sect Leader of the Luo Tian Sect, bearing the responsibility for the sect's rise and fall. Do not disappoint the entire sect!"

Suppressing the excitement in his heart, Luo Lingxi declared loudly, "Lingxi will do his utmost to recreate Luo Tian's glory and will not disappoint Father, Master, or the sect!"

"Alright, go down now. Your father and I still have some things to discuss."

"Yes."

Luo Lingxi nodded. Before leaving the hall, he secretly cast a glance at Li Yingzhang, a look that seemed to say, "I didn't disappoint you, old man."

Above.

Luo Chen naturally caught this small gesture. For some reason, he felt a hint of bitterness.

"This boy is closer to you!"

Li Yingzhang was stunned, as if he had never imagined Luo Chen would display such human emotions.

But then, he let out a silent laugh.

"Perhaps it's because of a hundred years of daily companionship. There isn't much distance between him and me. On the other hand, Supreme Elder, you live in seclusion, immersed in bitter cultivation, and the things you do often shake heaven and earth. In his heart, you, his father, are already a lofty mountain—something to look up to, but not to get close to."

"Is that so..." Luo Chen leaned against the armrest of his chair, his expression inexplicably wistful.

...

A month later, the Luo Tian Sect held a grand ceremony for the succession of the sect leader.

Except for a few who were in seclusion breaking through realms, all the middle and high-ranking members of the sect were present.

Under the watchful eyes of the elders and the core strength, Luo Lingxi took over the position of Fourth Generation Sect Leader from Li Yingzhang.

Luo Chen naturally attended this ceremony in person.

Whether in his capacity as Supreme Elder or as a father, it was necessary for him to witness this moment.

For everyone else, this was a scene long anticipated and long expected.

From the moment Luo Lingxi was born, he was the only designated heir to the Luo Tian Sect in the hearts of countless people.

There was no second choice for the sect leader position!

Even if there were some twists and turns along the way, once Luo Lingxi truly began to cultivate, this answer was written in everyone's hearts.

Simply because he was Luo Chen's son!

As for whether he could shake off Luo Chen's halo in the future, that would depend on his own actions.

When this news spread to the various sects and factions of the Eastern Wilderness, this change of sect leader took on a special meaning for the outside world.

It was like a dividing line!

Separating the Luo Tian Sect's four hundred years of arduous development from its destined transcendent status in the future!

The older generation would eventually fade away.

Whether they fell, passed away, or retreated behind the scenes after breaking through realms, their influence would gradually diminish.

Accompanying this was the rise of a new generation of core strength in the Luo Tian Sect.

The stage of the future would also be theirs.

...

Above the Nether Abyss.

There was no longer any golden flame covering it. Most of the golden flames had been absorbed by Luo Chen during the celestial phenomenon he had triggered back then, used in his battle against Lian Cheng.

The dangerous spatial rifts, worn down by two hundred years of time, had also receded significantly.

From this perspective, what Azure Frost had written in her letter was indeed true.

Only a newly born world possessed sufficiently powerful world origin power, capable of self-restraining and even smoothing over spatial rifts that could threaten a world.

It was like the super-strong self-healing ability of a young person's body.

In contrast, an ancient, decaying world not only couldn't heal itself but might also see a leak become a flood. A single spatial rift would appear, then grow larger and larger, eventually devouring the entire world, returning it to the vast cosmos.

Luo Chen stood on the back of the Mysterious Turtle, his gaze distant as he looked at the spatial rifts that occasionally and slowly appeared in the void before gradually fading away, lost in thought.

Behind him was a scene of laughter and joy.

There weren't many people, but they were all old acquaintances and old friends—high-realm cultivators of the Luo Tian Sect.

People like Wang Yuan and Ling Fengzi.

They were all Nascent Soul-level existences. Except for Haoranzi, who knew his potential was exhausted and had no hope of breaking through to the Ascendant stage, and thus stayed at the Luo Tian Sect's mountain gate, most of the others still chose to return to the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land for secluded cultivation.

After all, the current Luo Tian Sect only occupied the former site of Shangyou City, with a mediocre spirit vein environment.

The Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land was different, possessing a deep and solid fourth-order spirit vein, enough for many Nascent Soul True Persons to cultivate.

Also on the turtle's back was another group of people.

Sima Huiniang, Li Yingzhang, Qu Lingjun and his wife, Qi Yi, Qin Yuanjiang, and others.

These people were once high-ranking members of the Luo Tian Sect, or held real power. But in recent years, as the Luo Tian Sect produced more and more new-generation experts, they had actively or passively transferred their power.

It was as if, with Li Yingzhang's abdication, they too had stepped down from the glorious stage.

This time they left the Blessed Land was also because they understood the importance of Luo Lingxi succeeding to the sect leader position, so they specially came to attend the succession ceremony.

After the ceremony ended, they unanimously chose to return to the Blessed Land together.

This unanimity was influenced by a certain person.

Not Luo Chen, but Min Longyu!

A few years ago, while Luo Chen was in seclusion healing his injuries, Min Longyu had forcibly attempted to break through to the Nascent Soul stage as his great limit approached. In the end, he failed and regrettably perished.

This incident, while causing many to feel sorrow for the fox mourning the hare, also served as a wake-up call.

They were not Nascent Soul True Persons, only Golden Core cultivators. Even with methods to extend their lifespans, their longevity was at most around five hundred years.

If they didn't break through to the Nascent Soul stage, they would eventually turn into a handful of yellow earth.

Therefore, after completely withdrawing from the Luo Tian Sect's core power structure, cultivation and breaking through realms became their top priority.

Especially since Luo Chen had personally appeared this time and explicitly stated that he would have some free time and would regularly hold lectures to guide everyone on the key points of breaking through to the Nascent Soul stage.

No one wanted to miss this opportunity.

Perhaps it would be their last chance in life!

This included Sima Huiniang.

Seeing her son Luo Lingxi truly take over the sect leader position, she felt relieved. But only then did she realize that, without knowing it, she had drifted further and further away from her husband. Instead, Gu Caiyi, who had once vied with her for affection, was now standing by Luo Chen's side.

What an ironic scene this was!

Once, Gu Caiyi's jealousy had made her disdainful. She had only thought about managing the Luo Tian Sect well and helping Luo Chen.

But somehow, Gu Caiyi no longer cared about that. She only focused on cultivation, seeking only to catch up with Luo Chen's footsteps, even just a little.

As for her, she had fallen to a lower level.

Was it because of the severe injury back then, her foundation nearly destroyed, that she had tried to hold onto Luo Chen, even going so far as to bear a child, attempting to bind him with blood ties?

But her injuries, her foundation, even her lost lifespan—Luo Chen had already helped her recover them all!

Why was she still lost and unable to find her way back?

In a daze, Sima Huiniang's expression gradually became resolute.

"If she can fight for it, so can I!"

...

Overall, this time, the group of Golden Core cultivators from the Luo Tian Sect preparing to form their Nascent Souls were quite confident.

In terms of resources, with Luo Chen's return, he had brought back a large quantity of medicinal herbs from Green Parasol Mountain and frequently refined Nascent Soul Pills. They were not lacking.

Fourth-order spirit veins were everywhere in the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land.

In terms of breakthrough experience and insights, the Luo Tian Sect not only had a vast collection of texts but also many existing Nascent Soul True Persons who selflessly shared their knowledge, making everything accessible.

Especially after Luo Chen returned, he frequently held lectures, breaking down the precautions for forming a Nascent Soul and explaining them in detail to everyone.

Moreover, for each lecture session, he would invite a Nascent Soul True Person to recount their own experience of forming a Nascent Soul, and then Luo Chen would analyze and explain it from his own perspective.

In just a few short years, the insights and understanding gained by this batch of late-stage Golden Core cultivators from the Luo Tian Sect were a hundred or even a thousand times greater than before.

From time to time, someone would have an epiphany during a lecture, perceive the opportunity to form a Nascent Soul, and voluntarily announce their seclusion to attempt a breakthrough.

When the youngest, Meng Qiner—Qu Lingjun's Dao partner, once the pride of heaven from Splendid Phoenix Palace—also felt adequately prepared and could form her Nascent Soul, Luo Chen's lectures finally stopped.

Outside the Alchemy Dust Hall.

Luo Chen stood with his hands behind his back, Wang Yuan accompanying him.

Wang Yuan was also a frequent guest at the lectures.

Although he wasn't a Nascent Soul True Person, he excelled in body refinement and had extremely unique experience in regulating qi and blood. This area was related to the key step of unifying the three treasures of essence, qi, and spirit, so Luo Chen often invited Wang Yuan to enlighten everyone.

Looking at Luo Chen's calm yet slightly worried profile, Wang Yuan said in a deep voice, "You're putting a lot of heart into this this time. Is it because of Min Longyu's matter?"

"Yes and no." Luo Chen sighed softly. "Perhaps this will be the most peaceful period for me within the sect. Once my injuries are fully healed, I will attempt to break through to the Ascendant stage. And after becoming an Ascendant, ascension will be imminent."

"So urgent?" Wang Yuan frowned, puzzled. "I've observed that the Ascendant Great Nengs of the five ancient holy lands also lingered in the mortal world for a long time."

Luo Chen shook his head. "Times have changed!"

In his eyes, the sky he gazed upon did indeed seem different from before.

Since the ancient times, in the last five hundred years of the Mountains and Seas Realm, Ascendant-level battles had been far too frequent.

The consequences were also clearly visible.

The Nether Abyss in the Eastern Wilderness had collapsed, and the spatial rifts had taken two hundred years to gradually heal. The Southern Frontier had once been overly saturated with spiritual energy, causing those with thin Purple Mansion barriers to frequently suffer qi deviations. The Western Desert was relatively fine, but the apocalyptic frozen scene in the Arctic Yemo Heaven still continued.

And the Central Continent was even more fragmented, with the land having sunk!

Although these post-war traumas were still within the world's capacity to bear.

But if this continued, could the Mountains and Seas Realm truly withstand it?

If he lingered in this realm for too long like the Great Nengs of the ancient holy lands, would he ultimately receive the world's gift, granted abundant spiritual opportunities to be lifted to the upper realm?

Luo Chen didn't dare to gamble.

Moreover, the "karma" he bore was not necessarily less than that of Ascendant cultivators.

The mountains, rivers, and seas he had destroyed were not few!

It was just that he hadn't yet become an Ascendant, so the Heavenly Dao hadn't taken notice.

"You go on ahead. I still have some trivial matters to attend to. I won't leave until my injuries are healed in the near future."

Wang Yuan looked at Luo Chen with a complex expression, then took his leave.

After he left, Luo Chen paced back into the Alchemy Dust Hall.

In recent years, besides holding lectures and healing his injuries, he hadn't been idle.

Once his injuries lightened, he could already do some normal things.

Such as alchemy.

He had refined quite a few Nascent Soul Pills, Great Soul Pills, Hongyuan Pills, and even the Initiation Pills he had previously promised to the various sects and factions of the Eastern Wilderness.

He hadn't done it all himself. During the process, Qu Lingjun and Qi Yi had assisted him.

Qu Lingjun's realm was still insufficient, barely at the fifth layer of Golden Core. Whether he would have the opportunity to sprint for the Nascent Soul stage in the future was still uncertain.

Cultivation with a four-spirit-root aptitude was simply too difficult.

As for Qi Yi, despite already having the eighth layer of Golden Core, he had no intention of forming a Nascent Soul.

After watching over the final batch of Initiation Pills being successfully formed, he smiled and said to Luo Chen, "I know my talent is insufficient, and my Dao heart is not exceptional. Forming a Nascent Soul is too dangerous for me. With over a decade of lifespan left, I plan to write books and leave behind a volume of alchemy texts for the sect. That alone is enough."

Luo Chen respected his opinion.

Even though, in his view, Qi Yi's current alchemy Dao attainment was barely at the fourth-order level, and the alchemy texts he would leave behind wouldn't be particularly profound, a person always had to leave something behind in life.

After closing the alchemy chamber, Luo Chen arrived at the artifact refinement hall.

In the blazing flames, a bone umbrella floated silently.

One of the three great spirit treasures of the Dao Sect—the Burial Bone Umbrella!

(The End of This Chapter)
