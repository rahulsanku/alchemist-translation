# Chapter 792: Divided Souls in Host Bodies, One Mind for Three Tasks, Black Ape Learns Talismans, Red Dog Refines Pills

"Junior Brother Qu, aren't you going to see your father?"

On the mountainside of Great Green Mountain, several figures stood together, gazing into the distance at the courtyard where the Sect Master handled daily affairs.

Two backs walked side by side into it.

Yuan Yijun looked at Qu Jiu with confusion. The other party stared into the distance, lips pressed tightly, facial muscles taut into sharp contours.

Qu Jiu spat out two cold words.

"No need!"

Yuan Yijun did not understand. He was his father after all; why be so estranged, as if they would never interact again?

As for the role of "father," she worshipped it from the bottom of her heart.

When she was young, her mother died early, and it was her father Yuan Dongsheng who raised her bit by bit, with utmost love.

Even though Yuan Dongsheng had passed away for many years, she still often missed her father's broad shoulders.

So, she did not understand why Junior Brother Qu's father had returned, yet he did not take the initiative to meet him.

Could it be because of that woman who came back with Qu Lingjun?

...

Drawing near, Qu Lingjun's steps paused slightly. He turned back and said to the woman, "Qin'er, wait for me a moment."

Meng Qin'er nodded, her eyes full of encouragement. "Relax. Since your master has taken the initiative to let you rejoin the sect, it means he has let bygones be bygones regarding the past."

Qu Lingjun smiled. That was what he thought too.

Just as he was about to enter, a tall figure walked out from the house.

Qu Lingjun's eyes lit up. "Yilong!"

Zeng Yilong was startled at first, then revealed a delighted smile, grabbing Qu Lingjun's arm.

"Senior Brother, you've finally returned!"

Qu Lingjun laughed heartily. "Yes, I'm back. If you ever lack any pills in the future, just come to the Pill Court and find me."

Within immortal sects, generational titles were often based on cultivation level, emphasizing strength as the deciding factor.

But there were occasional exceptions.

Take Qu Lingjun, for example. His cultivation was merely at the Initiate stage, inferior to Zeng Yilong's third realm of Body Refinement. However, because Qu Lingjun held a rather high status within the Luo Tian Sect as the sole personal disciple of Supreme Elder Luo Chen, even those with better aptitudes and faster cultivation progress still addressed him by his old title.

Hearing the words "Pill Court" now, Zeng Yilong's expression turned somewhat peculiar.

Qu Lingjun did not notice. He asked curiously, "Where are you off to?"

Zeng Yilong coughed dryly and pointed toward the mountainside.

"The Sect Master has given me a task: to receive a batch of talisman skins from the Supreme Elder of the Divine Talisman Sect. I'll also take the younger ones along to broaden their horizons."

"Our sect's younger generation has all come through life-and-death struggles; they've long since seen the world..."

Qu Lingjun was speaking cheerfully, but as he followed Zeng Yilong's pointing direction, he vaguely caught sight of a gloomy, stern face.

It resembled his own by six or seven parts.

The remaining three parts were the spitting image of the face in his memory.

"That's Xiao Jiu!"

His own son—even if they had not lived together day and night, he would never be unfamiliar.

"Yilong, please take care of Qu Jiu."

Zeng Yilong patted his sturdy chest. "No need for Senior Brother to remind me; this is my duty. I'll go first, and we'll talk when I return!"

"Yes, we'll talk when you return!"

Qu Lingjun smiled faintly. But as his gaze followed Zeng Yilong toward the mountainside, Qu Jiu merely cast him a cold glance, devoid of any warmth.

Qu Lingjun's smile gradually faded, yet he felt no anger at the other's coldness.

Qu Jiu was the son of him and Mi Li, his very name derived from "Qu Mi Cheng Jiu" (Qu Mi Becomes Wine).

But back then, he had abandoned his wife and child to join the Fenghua Palace, failing to fulfill his duty as a father. It was only natural that the other party would resent him.

"Husband, Sect Master Li is calling you."

A gentle female voice sounded behind him.

Qu Lingjun sighed, lifted his robe, and walked into the house.

"Lingjun, you're back..."

Li Yingzhang greeted him with a smile, showing no sign of distance.

Qu Lingjun also forced a smile back.

"It's time I returned. Being out there at this age is hardly peaceful. Coming back, I can still contribute some light and heat to the sect. I hear the sect is short on financial resources. My alchemy has improved considerably; I can lead the Pill Court to open up a source of income for our Luo Tian Sect."

Li Yingzhang waved his hand. "There's no rush for that."

He looked outside. "What's the situation with Fellow Daoist Meng?"

Mentioning Meng Qin'er, a tender expression appeared on Qu Lingjun's face.

"She was unwilling to divorce me. After pleading with the Great Palace Master, she came back to the Luo Tian Sect with me. When I pass away from old age in forty or fifty years, she will return to the Fenghua Palace. I ask for your approval on this matter."

Li Yingzhang suddenly understood and sighed at their deep conjugal love.

At the same time, he could not help but feel secretly pleased.

Meng Qin'er was considered quite talented among the younger generation of the Fenghua Palace, having reached the Adept stage at a young age. Now that she had come to the Luo Tian Sect with Qu Lingjun, even if only for forty or fifty years, it would be enough to bolster the Luo Tian Sect's prestige.

With Sima Wenjie crippled, the Luo Tian Sect's nine Adepts had been reduced to eight.

Meng Qin'er's arrival would fill that gap.

He naturally would not refuse and immediately agreed to Qu Lingjun's request.

Having his Dao companion stay relieved Qu Lingjun. He then asked curiously, "Where is my master?"

Li Yingzhang replied, "The Supreme Elder is preparing to refine the Nascent Soul Pill. He has been very busy lately and may not have time to see you."

Qu Lingjun's eyes lit up. "Is Master going to refine pills? I've heard that refining the Nascent Soul Pill is extremely complex. Back then, the Qingdan Valley mobilized the entire sect's strength and even specially invited my master to help, yet they still failed. I've returned just in time; I can go assist Master."

He spoke quickly and urgently, yet without losing confidence and pride!

His alchemy skills had now reached the point where he could refine third-grade pills, no less than some of the Qingdan Valley's elders back then.

He was confident that he had the ability to help his master.

However.

"That won't be necessary. Qi Yi has already gone to assist the Supreme Elder."

"I also..."

Qu Lingjun looked into Li Yingzhang's inscrutable eyes. The words about wanting to go together somehow caught in his throat.

He tested the waters. "Since Qi Yi has gone to help, the Pill Court should be short-handed. I can go manage the Pill Court for a while."

This time, he did not arrogantly treat the Pill Court as his own domain like he had when meeting Zeng Yilong earlier.

Because he vaguely sensed something was off.

Indeed!

Li Yingzhang coughed and shook his head. "You don't need to go to the Pill Court either. The sect has already arranged a cultivation cave for you. Go into seclusion and cultivate there."

Qu Lingjun was somewhat incredulous.

"Is this your decision, or my master's?"

Li Yingzhang did not answer. He merely handed over a storage pouch.

Given his current status, there was no need for him to beat around the bush.

His evasive non-answer meant the answer was...

Qu Lingjun's face gradually turned pale.

...

Baili Qingchuan was not large, but it possessed a rather solid third-grade spirit vein.

Relying on this decent spirit vein, it could nurture a hundred li of medicinal fields.

After the Luo Tian Sect arrived, they opened up five caves with third-grade spirit qi density for Adept-level cultivators to use in daily cultivation.

With eight or nine Adepts squeezing in and taking turns, they could barely manage.

After Luo Chen returned, he did not choose a third-grade cave.

He merely selected a scenic spot on Great Green Mountain as his temporary dwelling.

He was currently in a state of consolidating his cultivation, his body containing a large amount of unrefined mana—the origin qi that heaven and earth had poured into him when he comprehended the true meaning of Withering-Bloom.

Moreover, he still had a large stock of high-grade Hongyuan Pills he had refined earlier, so he was not short of qi replenishment for the time being.

At this moment, two figures were busy inside the cave.

"Xiao Qi, use the pill art I taught you to further refine the Purple Monkey Flowers. These Purple Monkey Flowers sent by the Divine Talisman Sect are too old; their medicinal power is not condensed enough."

"Understood!"

Qi Yi walked over to the shelf holding the Purple Monkey Flowers and took them out one by one.

After preparing, he suddenly asked, "Supreme Elder, why didn't you let Qu Lingjun come help you? I've heard his alchemy skills have reached a respectable level, no less than some third-grade alchemists."

Luo Chen, who was cutting the Five Elements Lotus Platform, did not look up as he countered, "What, do you think you're not up to the task?"

Qi Yi quickly shook his head. Being able to help Luo Chen refine pills and observe the process of making the Nascent Soul Pill was a dream come true for him.

How could he miss this opportunity due to a lack of confidence?

He hesitated for a moment, then said, "I was just thinking that one more person could share the burden for you, Elder. That way, the chances of successfully refining the Nascent Soul Pill would be greater."

"Don't worry. Refining the Nascent Soul Pill is no pressure for me. Don't overthink it; just focus on the preparations! This experience will benefit you greatly."

"Oh."

Qi Yi asked no more questions and concentrated on processing the Purple Monkey Flowers.

The small knife in Luo Chen's hand moved back and forth across the Five Elements Lotus Platform, cutting off pieces of the platform with its connected apertures.

In his heart, he had long had an answer to Qi Yi's question.

"Lingjun's aptitude is too poor. Over the years, he has expended too much energy refining pills for the Fenghua Palace, to the point that at his age, he still hasn't broken into the late Initiate stage."

"Now that he's back, I can't let him be distracted anymore."

"Choose a spirit land and cultivate bitterly for fifty years. Perhaps before his lifespan runs out, he might still have a chance to strive for the Adept stage. If he succeeds, his lifespan will double, and then infinite possibilities await."

"I hope he understands my intentions and doesn't let other things cloud his eyes."

Sighing inwardly, Luo Chen continued cutting the Five Elements Lotus Platform.

Elder Fu had given him two Five Elements Lotus Platforms, both without lotus seeds. With his alchemy skills, he could open the furnace and refine the Nascent Soul Pill twenty times.

But with his previous successful precedent, he could completely save one Five Elements Lotus Platform for his own use.

As for the wonders of this item, Han Zhan had explained them to him back in the Demon-Fall Land.

The Five Elements Lotus Platform was not actually meant for medicinal use.

The lotus seeds condensed the extreme essence of the five elements, making them too extreme and more suitable for artifact refinement.

The platform itself, however, was exceptionally translucent, capable of filtering impurities from heaven and earth's spiritual qi. It was often used by Sage-level cultivators as an auxiliary tool for daily meditation and cultivation.

Back in the Blazing Refinement Prison, Luo Chen had briefly borrowed an extreme-grade lotus platform for cultivation, which had greatly improved his cultivation.

Having experienced that effect, he had never forgotten it.

Although the two platforms Elder Fu had given him were not of particularly good quality, they were barely sufficient for use.

...

A few days later.

Luo Chen sat cross-legged on the platform he had kept for himself, resting with his eyes closed, preparing for the alchemy session.

But his mind was quite active.

"With all these miscellaneous matters now, though not troublesome, they are quite bothersome."

"Even if I can multitask, I cannot be in multiple places at once."

"But I have condensed two divided souls. If I can send them out to handle tasks, they might save me a lot of time and speed up progress on all fronts."

Thinking of this, Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes.

Divided souls cannot remain outside for long; they need a host body to reside in.

With Han Zhan's precedent, he could imitate it to some extent.

"I need two puppets!"

Puppets, he had!

In his storage ring, there was a White Tiger Puppet.

But that item was of too high a grade and contained a spirit soul within, which his current divided soul could not control.

After years of research, Luo Chen was about twenty to thirty percent confident he could subdue it. However, his own cultivation level was insufficient, and forcibly controlling it might lead to backlash—ultimately too risky.

"Let Yuan Jiang come here!"

With a thought, Luo Chen sent a divine sense transmission.

Soon, Qin Yuanjiang arrived at Luo Chen's cave.

When Luo Chen said he wanted two puppets, Qin Yuanjiang did not hesitate and took out a large pile of puppets from his storage pouch.

Looking at the dazzling array of puppets laid out in a row, Luo Chen was somewhat surprised.

"So many?"

Qin Yuanjiang smiled faintly. "All crude creations, prepared to be detonated directly when facing enemies."

Luo Chen understood. These puppets were indeed not fine works; not even one had a humanoid form.

In that case, there was no need to be polite.

He casually picked out a Red Dog puppet and a Black Ape puppet.

After he had chosen, Qin Yuanjiang put away the other puppets and looked at Luo Chen curiously.

"Elder, are you also proficient in the art of puppetry?"

"Not exactly proficient, just dabbling a bit. Moreover, my method of controlling puppets..."

As Luo Chen spoke, he closed his eyes directly in front of Qin Yuanjiang.

The next moment, two streaks of dim light flashed from the top of his head and directly submerged into the Red Dog and Black Ape.

Crack! Crack!

Crack! Crack!

The Red Dog opened its eyes; the Black Ape opened its eyes.

Qin Yuanjiang's heart trembled. This horrifying scene was clearly the divided soul technique recorded in the Luo Yun Sect's archives.

Given his former status, he had not been exposed to such methods.

But not long ago, the three volumes of scriptures Luo Chen had given him included the *Soul-Splitting Art*, allowing him to glimpse a corner of this technique.

On the lotus platform, Luo Chen's main body slowly opened its eyes.

"Yuan Jiang, do you understand my puppet art now?"

Qin Yuanjiang took a deep breath and slowly nodded.

Clearly, the Supreme Elder had obtained certain techniques from the Luo Yun Sect and cultivated them to a very profound level, allowing him to split two souls.

And his blatant display of this to him showed a great deal of trust.

"I was a close friend of your parents, so it is only natural that I look after you."

"Now that you have joined the Luo Tian Sect, do not worry about your future path. What the Luo Yun Sect could teach you, I can teach you as well."

"Go ahead and cultivate those puppet arts with peace of mind. Perhaps in the future, you can establish a lineage of puppetry within the Luo Tian Sect."

Luo Chen said this, his gaze full of earnest encouragement.

Luo Chen did not possess the complete Three Arts and Two Techniques of the Luo Yun Sect.

But he had the *Fine Dust Primordial Art* fused with the *Locked Pearl Curtain*, the *Aperture-Opening Art* passed down by Han Zhan, and the *Soul-Splitting Art*. These three techniques, when linked together, formed a path for cultivating the soul.

There might be some gaps, but if combined with Luo Chen's Netherpassage Pills and Great Soul Pills, they would essentially have no flaws.

If Qin Yuanjiang fully cultivated this set of methods and delved deeper into puppet creation and control, he might very well restore the might of the Luo Yun Sect's puppets.

Facing Luo Chen's earnest gaze, Qin Yuanjiang bowed respectfully.

"Yuan Jiang will not disappoint your expectations!"

Luo Chen smiled faintly and let him return.

After Qin Yuanjiang left, Luo Chen's gaze fell upon the two puppets.

They contained two of his divided souls. Even the weakest one had already reached the Sage level after consuming large quantities of Great Soul Pills.

And because they originated from the same main soul, they were connected in mind and spirit. Whatever they did, whatever they learned, Luo Chen's main body would be fully aware.

The only drawback was probably the lack of "energy"!

"After all, they are not true avatars. They have no Nascent Soul, no mana."

Luo Chen inexplicably thought of Shi Ju's lightning-attribute avatar he had glimpsed in the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven.

That one had a flesh-and-blood body and contained a second Nascent Soul within—a true avatar with great magical power and great ability!

It was a thousand times better than his hastily made puppet avatars.

Shaking his head, such things were not to be envied.

Luo Chen raised his hand and shot out two bursts of spiritual light. Dozens of high-grade spirit stones sank into the puppet bodies to serve as energy for movement.

Not only that, but he also opened his mouth and spat out a wisp of faint Withering-Bloom True Flame.

The Red Dog puppet simultaneously opened its mouth and absorbed it.

"Black Ape, go study the way of talismans. Master the theory and familiarize yourself with the drawing methods."

"Red Dog, follow me in refining pills!"

No further words were needed. With minds connected, the two puppets immediately set to work.
