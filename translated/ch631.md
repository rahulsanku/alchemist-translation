# Chapter 631: Withered Blood Vine, Withered Wood Ridge, Set Out!

"Daoist Azure Yang, are you certain?"

"Of course. Fellow Daoist Duan Li, could it be you are unwilling?"

Inside a side hall of the Demon Hunter Office, Luo Chen and Duan Li spoke quietly.

The latter's face showed hesitation.

"It is not that I am uncertain, but if I use that favor of mine on such a small matter, would that not be a waste?"

Luo Chen smiled faintly. "How is it a waste? This matter is extremely important to me. One more person means one more portion of strength. Especially that flower basket magic treasure of yours; it is the core of my plan. I cannot do without you."

"Since that is so, then Duan will follow your arrangement!"

Although Duan Li felt puzzled, he also let out a breath of relief.

As Daoist Azure Yang's reputation grew more and more flourishing, the favor he owed him became harder and harder to repay.

After all, what he had said back then was that he would exhaust all his strength to repay the life-saving grace. But if he were truly made to risk his life, how could he be willing?

Now, for the other party to choose to use up that favor on a small matter, he naturally had no objection.

"I have just returned from completing a mission on my side and still need to submit the mission proof, so I will take my leave first." Duan Li cupped his fist and left the side hall ahead of time.

After he left, Luo Chen remained in the hall, silently calculating the plan for the Purple Spirit Island trip.

There were three problems on Purple Spirit Island!

The first was the poison of the floral fragrance released by the Purple Monkey Flowers.

The second was the dozen or so third-tier Purple Qi Poison Monkeys entrenched there.

The third was that once battle erupted, it would spread across Purple Spirit Island and affect the Purple Monkey Flowers' growing environment.

For these three problems, Luo Chen had been solving them one by one during this period.

The improved fourth-tier Poison-Resisting Pills had already been refined, and the quantity was completely sufficient. With these, the so-called floral fragrance poison could be dealt with.

Then, he had invited the mid Golden Core Sang Jiugong and Lord Lushan, as well as Duan Li at Golden Core Stage Three. If he added himself and Black King, that would be five great Golden Core battle forces!

With such a lineup, under advance planning, it would be enough to deal with those Purple Qi Poison Monkeys with their scarce methods.

And the final problem was also the most important point.

After much thought, Luo Chen had also developed a method.

Using Duan Li's flower basket magic treasure as the core, he would arrange a formation to stabilize Purple Spirit Island's earth vein.

At the same time, he would supplement it with a set of Mountain-Suppressing Talismans Lord Lushan had researched before. In this way, he should be able to guarantee to the greatest extent that Purple Spirit Island's environment would not be destroyed by the aftermath of battle.

Of course, these plans were all built on the foundation of attacking Purple Spirit Island.

As for handling it after taking it down, Luo Chen still needed to perfect another thing or two.

Purple Spirit Island was deep in the Demon Sea and far from the human hinterland. He could not possibly watch over it there long-term, so he had to leave behind follow-up measures.

"Originally, the plan before was to leave Black King on Purple Spirit Island to watch the spirit medicines for me. But since this involves the Sunken Sea secret realm, I need helpers even more, so I have to seek another method."

"Formations may be one method."

"But what exactly Purple Spirit Island's situation is, I have not personally landed on the island yet, so it is not easy to prepare formations in advance."

"First, I should seek several sets of formations as backups, and prepare the corresponding formation-arranging tools as well."

Just as Luo Chen was thinking inwardly, footsteps came from outside the hall.

He originally thought it was Duan Li, finished reporting the mission and returning.

Yet before the person entered, an aged laugh came from outside the door.

"This old man had wanted to go to Peng Lake to seek Fellow Daoist, but unexpectedly our hearts are linked, and Fellow Daoist came first."

As the laughter entered, Angler, whom he had not seen for a long time, appeared before his eyes.

Luo Chen's brows furrowed slightly, then he smiled. "Angler, long time no see. I hear you have recently been busy handling matters for Blood Sage. How do you still have the mind to go to Peng Lake to find me?"

He wanted to change the subject.

He had not expected that it would exactly match Angler's intention.

"What a coincidence. This old man sought you precisely for the Sage's matter!"

This time, Luo Chen's expression instantly turned solemn.

Angler did not keep him in suspense and immediately spoke directly.

"I have already found the location of fifth-tier Withered Blood Vine. Now I am gathering manpower to retrieve the treasure for Blood Sage. Daoist Azure Yang, your divine abilities are vast. When the time comes, please lend me a hand."

Fifth-tier Withered Blood Vine!

Luo Chen's expression changed slightly. "Angler, could you be joking with me? I did not accept that Sage task."

Angler's smile faded. "What, are you unwilling?"

Luo Chen shook his head. "It is not that I am unwilling, but that I have other matters. Moreover, was it not Wuzhong Valley that vouched for me before, exempting me from Demon Hunter Office tasks?"

Angler snorted lightly. "That sort of thing cannot be placed openly on the table. When nothing is happening, naturally you people may roam freely. But now, this is a major matter the Sage specifically ordered handled. For us, it is a forced conscription."

At this point, his aged eyes narrowed slightly.

"Daoist Azure Yang, do not forget, you are a four-star demon hunter!"

Luo Chen fell silent.

Angler did not urge him, merely finding a chair beside him and sitting down.

After a long while, a faint voice sounded in the hall.

"Is this Blood Sage's order?"

"Yes. He has given me full authority to mobilize the Demon Hunter Office's strength. As long as I wish it, everyone must obey my command."

Eight-star demon hunters were already the actual power holders of the Demon Hunter Office, but they still had to obey the Three Great Wanderers who founded the Myriad Immortals Assembly.

Now that the Sage had delegated power, Angler could naturally act as he wished.

And among the task rewards Blood Wanderer previously released, there was knowledge related to Sage formation. To Angler, who had long sought a Sage formation method without success, this was temptation enough to risk everything.

His previously friendly relationship with Luo Chen was as brittle as silk paper before this task.

Luo Chen somewhat regretted coming to the Demon Hunter Office today.

But after another thought, even if he had not come, Angler would have personally come to his door.

Peng Lake was there.

He could flee as a monk, but not flee the temple.

Since the matter had already become fixed, resentment and regret were useless.

Luo Chen slowly voiced his concerns. "Fifth-tier Withered Blood Vine, possessing Sage power. How could we withstand it? Angler, you surely will not do something like throwing eggs against stone, right?"

Angler looked at Luo Chen in surprise. He had not expected the other party to consider these things in such a short time.

He smiled again.

"Rest assured. This old man will not seek his own death."

"For this task, apart from you and me, this old man has also mobilized two seven-star demon hunters, as well as one late Golden Core great cultivator."

Luo Chen's heart shook!

Seven-star demon hunters were without doubt all late Golden Core figures.

And that one great cultivator, even if not a seven-star demon hunter, had a genuine late Golden Core realm there.

Such a lineup could absolutely be considered the Myriad Immortals Assembly's high-end battle strength all emerging, apart from Sage cultivators.

What ability did he have that could make Angler regard him as equal in weight to those people?

"It is my attainment in body cultivation!"

Like inspiration arriving at the heart, Luo Chen thought of this point.

In the early years, Angler had privately exchanged body cultivation arts with him multiple times. Although the other party had not made great progress, his understanding of Luo Chen had increased day by day.

The body cultivation art comparable to great cultivator battle strength was precisely the reason Angler valued him so highly.

Add to that Devil Lord Azure Yang's fame outside, and how could Angler leave such a fine fighter unused?

"Daoist Azure Yang, you need not worry too much."

"Apart from the five of us, Blood Sage has also bestowed other methods, specially used to deal with the fifth-tier Withered Blood Vine."

"For you all, this trip is not dangerous, merely a long journey. After completing the task, you can even obtain merit points and raise your demon hunter star rank. Their seven-star demon hunter ranks are too high, so they cannot improve much, but you are only four-star now, enough to rise by a major level."

Angler spoke with confidence and ease, his words filled with certainty about the task's success.

Under his persuasion, the resistance in Luo Chen's heart gradually lessened quite a lot.

However, before that, he still had a question to ask.

"Where is the mission location? My own matter is also very urgent. It cannot delay me too long."

"What matter of yours is so urgent?" Angler casually asked.

Luo Chen did not answer.

Angler chuckled and took a jade slip from his chest.

Seeing Luo Chen reach out to take it, he paused and gripped the jade slip.

"Let you and I make a small oath. The circumstances of this trip must not be leaked."

Luo Chen frowned, but could only use divine sense as a guide together with Angler to make a small oath of secrecy.

This kind of oath did not have strong binding force.

But once it was leaked, the other side would sense it in their heart.

In this way, the consequences of leaking could be imagined.

After receiving the jade slip from Angler's hand, Luo Chen sank his divine sense into it and carefully observed.

"Starting from the Myriad Immortals Assembly, heading east... crossing seven mountains and five seas... until Withered Wood Ridge."

"Finished reading? Return it to me!"

Angler asked for the jade slip back, while saying, "I will notify you all of the specific departure time in three months. In these three months, prepare in advance and adjust your state. Although the task is said not to be dangerous, if accidents truly happen, you still have to rely on yourselves. This old man makes no guarantees."

"Also, the other three traveling companions, you have stayed secluded over these years, so you probably do not know them. They are... heh, I am so busy my head is muddled. How could you possibly not be familiar with Huangfu Song?"

Listening to Angler state the three people's names and origins, Luo Chen's expression was somewhat strange.

Angler only thought he felt awkward traveling with Huangfu Song and did not think much of it. After a few instructions, he hurriedly left.

Luo Chen left the Demon Hunter Office by himself.

Walking on the street, Luo Chen's strange expression gradually faded.

"It is actually on the way!"

If he had not seen wrongly, the map on that jade slip clearly pointed in the same direction as Purple Spirit Island, where he wanted to go.

Only, one was in front and the other behind.

Withered Wood Ridge was in front, Purple Spirit Island behind.

In an instant, inspiration flashed through Luo Chen's mind.

"That is it. Black King mentioned that on the route to Purple Spirit Island, he took quite a detour specifically to avoid an extremely dangerous mountain ridge. Looking at it now, that was clearly Withered Wood Ridge, where the fifth-tier Withered Blood Vine is entrenched."

After understanding this point, Luo Chen's heart immediately became lively.

He, an artifact-forging master, had basically secluded himself in Peng Lake over these years.

If he abruptly left, not only would it attract attention, it would also be hard to explain to Wuzhong Valley, the Zhou clan, and the Chen clan.

If someone attentive noticed and learned of the Purple Monkey Flower matter, perhaps some broad-knowledgeed person would guess Luo Chen's plan.

At that time, it would instead easily invite disaster.

But if he was forcefully sent out in the name of Blood Wanderer's task, naturally there would not be much dispute.

Even if someone was curious, they would only place their attention on the famous Blood Wanderer.

"Three months?"

"I do need to have Lord Lushan and the others leave one step earlier."

Luo Chen murmured inwardly.

...

After returning from the Demon Hunter Office, Luo Chen once again closed his doors and did not leave.

Only large batches of materials were collected from the Zhang family side, then delivered into the Pill-Artifact Hall through Heavenjade's hands.

Outside, there were indeed people constantly paying attention to Luo Chen's movements.

To Wuzhong Valley and the Zhou clan, they only thought Luo Chen was refining defensive magic treasures for them.

To Ding Yi and the Moya Cave Master of Moya Mountain, they thought Luo Chen was hurriedly refining a black turtle treasure to cross the Sunken Sea.

Angler also learned of Luo Chen's movements through subordinates. After hearing, he merely smiled. Luo Chen being so busy seemed to show he truly took the task to heart.

Three months passed in the blink of an eye.

On this day, Ding Yi took time out and personally came to visit Luo Chen.

Yet Heavenjade informed him that Luo Chen had already gone out.

"It was a forced task from the Demon Hunter Office. My master also had no choice."

Ding Yi was stunned. With an uncertain expression, he left only one sentence: "When your master returns, immediately invite him to Moya Mountain."

From the look of it, he was a little anxious.

Apart from Ding Yi, Zhou Shanshan also brought a young Zhou clan Golden Core cultivator to visit, and likewise found the doors closed.

The two were displeased.

But after they later returned to the Zhou clan and learned a little inside information, their resentment gradually dissipated.

Blood Wanderer's domineering nature had always been rumored within the Myriad Immortals Assembly.

For tasks he arranged, apart from Moon Wanderer, the rest of the Myriad Immortals Assembly's cultivators rarely dared disobey.

Even great forces with Sage cultivators such as Wuzhong Valley and the Zhou clan were like this.

Only because Blood Wanderer was a mid-stage Sage cultivator, and even had a glorious record of escaping from a late-stage Sage great cultivator.

As for the ancestors of Wuzhong Valley, the Coral Sea Zhou clan, and the Tianqu Chen clan, these three sides were merely early-stage Sage cultivators. How could they defy him?

No matter how many troublesome matters were behind him.

As for now, under Angler's introduction, Luo Chen met the two seven-star demon hunters traveling with them.

"Star Hook Envoy, skilled in star radiance arts, from the now-destroyed Heavenly Demon Star Sect."

Luo Chen was incomparably surprised, looking at the man of extraordinary bearing wearing a resplendent star robe.

"Could it be the Heavenly Demon Star Sect that, three hundred years ago, was known for killing countless demons and using demon cores as stars?"

Hearing someone remember his departed sect, a bitter smile appeared on Star Hook Envoy's face.

"Past glory, that is all. Now it is merely a handful of yellow earth, and even the mountain gate has been buried beneath the vast sea."

"My condolences."

Luo Chen sighed, and this condolence came from his heart.

The Northern Sea had many spirit veins, which also led to sects and clans emerging endlessly.

That Heavenly Demon Star Sect had been one of the famous ones among them.

This sect had an excellent relationship with the Primordial Demon Sect, and even its former sect territory had been near the Primordial Demon Sect's range. The disciples within possessed many methods targeting the demon race and took pride in the number of demons they killed.

But three hundred years ago, the Heavenly Demon Star Sect was broken by a powerful demon beast, with countless deaths and injuries and almost no disciples surviving.

At that time, outsiders only thought the Heavenly Demon Star Sect had provoked a great demon it should not have provoked.

Only later did they belatedly discover that perhaps it had only been the Northern Sea demon beasts eliminating the Primordial Demon Sect's wings in advance in order to counterattack it.

After Star Hook Envoy, the other person was a taciturn large man.

Luo Chen only knew the other party was named Dao Lan, a representative wandering cultivator in the Myriad Immortals Assembly, and that he rarely went out in ordinary times.

Or rather, it was not that he rarely went out, but that he had always been on the Liuyang defensive line, carrying out garrison missions.

According to outside rumors, Dao Lan was also the next existence most likely to advance to eight-star demon hunter.

This was a bitter cultivator among wandering cultivators!

And he used battle to temper himself as his cultivation method.

The dense killing aura made strangers unable to approach.

After meeting the two seven-star demon hunters traveling with them, Luo Chen could not help sighing inwardly.

For this task, Angler had truly spent great thought!

A former upper sect powerhouse, a peak expert of the first defensive line, plus himself, an existence skilled in body cultivation. Oh, there was still one person.

Luo Chen glanced at the room with tightly closed doors on the flying boat.

Huangfu Song was inside. He was rather arrogant, with no intention of coming out to meet them.

However, this arrogance seemed somewhat out of place when they were about to carry out a mission.

"Speaking of which, Purple Dowager once said she wanted him to come pour tea and apologize to me. Could this be the reason he does not want to come out?"

For some reason, Luo Chen laughed softly.

Angler looked at Luo Chen strangely. They were about to carry out a Nascent Soul Sage's task, and everyone else was extremely cautious, yet Daoist Azure Yang seemed rather composed.

Clapping his hands, he said loudly, "With the mission imminent, I will first carefully introduce the situation of Withered Wood Ridge to you all, lest an accident happen later."

As he said this, a strand of divine sense also extended from behind the tightly closed door.

The four Golden Core cultivators saw this and exchanged looks.

He was not a fool either!

*(End of Chapter)*
