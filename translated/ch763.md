# Chapter 763: That Touch of Green

Inside the belly of a nameless great mountain.

Fu Qinglan sat upright within, her eyes closed.

Although her eyes were closed, her divine sense was fully spread out, enveloping the nearby area.

Any living being that came close was instantly killed by her.

Before her, a round formation disk was quietly embedded in the rock layer.

Abruptly!

The formation disk trembled lightly, and one high-grade spirit stone after another rapidly turned to powder. Even that supreme-grade spirit stone began to dim.

Great amounts of spiritual qi were drawn out and surged into the formation disk.

Fu Qinglan suddenly opened her eyes.

Hum...

There was a light ringing, and the light distorted.

The next moment, a figure walked out from within.

"Luo..."

"Pfft!"

Before Luo Chen had even steadied himself, he spat out a spray of blood. His entire body cracked inch by inch, and from every exposed place blood shot out.

"How could this happen?" Fu Qinglan was greatly alarmed and hurried forward to support Luo Chen.

After he steadied himself, Luo Chen wiped the corner of his mouth.

He pushed away Fu Qinglan's hand, tapped one finger against his chest, and the cracked skin across the surface of his body immediately recovered as good as new. The surging blood also returned to his physique.

He then mustered his remaining magic power and cast a Cleansing Art, erasing all traces of the blood that had dripped around them.

In this way, it was as though nothing had ever happened.

Fu Qinglan swallowed and asked worriedly, "Brother Luo, are you all right?"

Luo Chen tugged at the corner of his mouth. "Aside from my magic power being a little lacking, there is nothing seriously wrong."

"But just now you..."

Luo Chen waved his hand, unwilling to explain.

Only when he put away the formation disk covered in cracks did he look at it a few more times.

He had never expected that those Sage cultivators would not pose much of a threat to him, while the escape route he had personally prepared would instead pit him once.

Yes, during the transposition, great numbers of restrictions had collapsed, and the terrifying spatial pressure had left even Luo Chen rather badly injured.

This was somewhat beyond expectation.

Luo Chen had ridden quite a few teleportation formations in the past, so his experience could be considered rich.

Other immortal cultivators with frail bodies might fear the pressure during teleportation, but he was unharmed by it.

But this time...

"In the end, I was not practiced enough in controlling this formation, and the formation disk was refined too hastily."

Luo Chen quickly found the reason for the backlash he had suffered. It lay with the formation disk.

The one thing worth rejoicing over was that the teleportation distance he had set earlier was around five thousand li, which was not too far.

Therefore, these injuries merely looked serious.

With his powerful recovery ability, once he drove source power, he could recover as good as new in an instant.

If it had been the kind of ultra-long-distance teleportation that moved tens of thousands or even millions of li at every turn, the consequences would have been difficult to estimate.

"The illusion cores are already in hand. Let's go!" Luo Chen said.

Although Fu Qinglan was somewhat curious, she still nodded on the surface.

In front of Luo Chen, she took out a talisman.

When magic power was injected into it, hazy earthen-yellow light immediately appeared, wrapping around her and Luo Chen. In the blink of an eye, they sank into the mountain stone and vanished.

Not long after they left.

An angry shout resounded outside the mountain.

"Get out here!"

A verdant bamboo staff transformed into a hundred zhang in length and struck the mountainside head-on.

Boom!

Smoke and dust rose at once. The Wang clan elder paid it no heed and charged straight in.

Another figure, however, spread his folding fan, and amid howling wild winds, all the smoke and dust was blown away.

Shi Ju stared gloomily at the shattered mountain range.

"He ran!"

"After using such a major method, his magic power should have been nearly gone, yet he could still run?"

Behind him, Gu Yuan brought Gu Chen drifting over gracefully.

As he flew, he laughed and said:

"Since the other party dared provoke us alone with a single sword, he certainly left himself a way out. From start to finish, Fu Qinglan, whom he protects, never appeared. She must have been used as support. Shi Ju, do not think of these rough wanderers of the jianghu too simply."

Shi Ju turned back and glared fiercely at him.

Then his expression abruptly shifted, and he sneered. "Right, you thought things through so carefully. Didn't you also suffer a setback beneath that man's sword?"

Gu Yuan's face turned somewhat unnatural, but very soon he shrugged indifferently.

"What setback? We harvested two illusion cores on this trip, so it was not in vain. Unlike certain people, who lost both the bride and the soldiers. Truly pitiful."

"You!"

Shi Ju snapped his folding fan shut and pointed it brightly at Gu Yuan, his face flushed.

The man in the golden robe stepped forward and blocked Gu Yuan.

Gu Yuan gave a light laugh and no longer provoked him. He called to Gu Chen, then drifted away.

Shi Ju gritted his teeth and stared at the other party's departing back, his hand gripping the fan handle as though he meant to snap it.

Just then, furious roars came from within the mountain.

He looked down and saw the Wang clan elder like a headless fly inside, flipping away one great boulder after another, yet still unable to find any trace of Luo Chen.

"Wang Yifu, he ran long ago. What are you still searching here for?"

The Wang clan elder raised his head, his face full of grief and anger.

"He caused my Young Master to be eliminated. Even if I must search the blue sky above and the yellow springs below, I will find him!"

Shi Ju scoffed and turned as if to leave.

But after flying a few steps, he suddenly turned back and threw out a jade slip.

"If you find him, notify me."

The Wang clan elder was startled, but quickly nodded.

"I will certainly notify you! This old man also hopes Fellow Daoist will inform me if you encounter Wilderness Wanderer!"

Shi Ju grunted and hurried back beneath Three-Rainbow Falls.

Qi Xiongcheng was holding a great gourd with a hole in its belly, standing there with a weary face.

Seeing Shi Ju, he gritted his teeth and said, "Young Master, I only caught back five illusion cores, and this gourd..."

"Useless trash!"

A fan struck over. Qi Xiongcheng's gaze struggled, but he did not dare dodge in the slightest.

Smack!

His physique at the third realm of body refinement was actually split open with a sinister bloody wound.

Shi Ju was too lazy to look at him again. He was only a household slave, and such a useless household slave at that.

If he had known earlier, he would not have brought him in. He had only become a burden instead.

His gloomy gaze swept across the surroundings, and for a long time he said nothing.

Only after a long while did he speak coldly.

"Leave the gourd behind! You and that Wang Yifu each take one direction and search for Luo Chen and Fu Qinglan. Once you find them, do not alert them. Notify me first."

"Also, if you encounter any other fellow disciples on the road, pass a message to me as well."

Qi Xiongcheng asked, "Are you going to join forces with the other true disciples to deal with Wilderness Wanderer?"

"Join forces?"

Shi Ju tugged at the corner of his mouth. "Wilderness Wanderer only caught us unprepared. Why would killing him require joining forces with everyone? What I need now is illusion cores. I imagine those fellow disciples of mine have accumulated quite a few, especially Gu Daosu, who ran away!"

When he spoke of Gu Daosu, cruelty flashed in his eyes.

The other party had lost his Dao protector, and unlike himself, could not act without restraint. He was the softest lamb, there to be taken as Shi Ju pleased!

...

On Heaven-Earth Peak.

Inside a great hall.

More than two months had already passed since the Alchemy Saint ascended and the grotto-heaven opened.

The various parties who had come to observe the ceremony had also departed one after another.

The internal meetings of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect had ended long ago as well.

At this moment, those still remaining here were basically the family representatives of various parties concerned with the results of the competition.

They gathered together, talking of the four corners of the world, discussing cultivation insights, and making all manner of guesses about the competition's outcome.

Among them, it was inevitable that some people would take out good things as stakes to bet on the result, using this to pass the time.

Originally, in everyone's hearts, aside from their own juniors, those they favored most were none other than the three scions of the Great Houses, as well as that younger member of the Xiang family.

But two months earlier, when Wrathful Vajra was eliminated, everything had changed.

The name Wilderness Wanderer Luo Chen once again surfaced by their ears.

Speaking of him, this person was not unfamiliar to the representatives of the great families.

The first time was because, as a rogue cultivator, he had exceptionally entered Heaven-Earth Peak and become an external affairs guest elder.

The second time was during the Alchemy Saint's ascension, when the Alchemy Saint gave him one of the five Heavenly Secret Pills!

One had to know that the destinations of those five Heavenly Secret Pills were either the three hidden cultivator experts on Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain, or someone from the Alchemy Saint's homeland.

Both of those destinations made sense.

But on what basis did Luo Chen receive one?

A gift to Heaven-Earth Peak's Star Gate?

What a joke. Why had the four late-stage Sage elders not received this gift?

If not for the grotto-heaven opening, and Luo Chen needing to enter immediately as a Dao protector, these people would have obtained that Heavenly Secret Pill from Luo Chen back then.

Leaving aside lowly methods like open struggle and secret robbery, as long as they offered sufficient compensation, with that wilderness rogue cultivator's limited experience, he would eventually be tempted.

Unfortunately, Luo Chen had entered the grotto-heaven.

Everyone had only been thinking that he should not use that Heavenly Secret Pill too quickly. They had not expected that the first news they received would be that he had fought one against two and defeated the two great experts Wrathful Vajra and Sage Heavenly Mountain.

This was the third time Luo Chen's name appeared before everyone.

And this time, it also meant that Xiang Qi, the Xiang family's prodigy who had seemed rather hopeful, was now in a precarious position on his path to comprehending the laws.

After all, without a Dao protector, whether hunting illusion beasts or cultivating alone would become difficult.

They no longer looked favorably upon Xiang Qi.

Instead, they added Fu Qinglan to their subjects of discussion.

Her foundation was thin and her aptitude slightly lacking, but her advantage lay in having formed her Sage recently, making her closer to heaven and earth. Moreover, with Luo Chen as such a powerful Dao protector, she could possess more illusion cores to supply her cultivation.

Just as they were discussing this.

On this day, a vortex appeared again inside the hall.

The Wang clan true disciple emerged from the vortex, his face blank.

Seeing that it was him, everyone was puzzled.

His Dao protector had not come out, so why had he come out first?

The vortex behind him began to slowly close.

Suddenly!

A trace of blood-colored sword light fled out from the final eye of the vortex.

"How bold!"

A great hand suddenly grabbed down, catching that sword light in its palm at the narrowest possible moment.

When the Wang clan true disciple saw this scene, his face instantly went pale. The Wang clan ancestor was both shocked and furious, and also relieved.

"Many thanks, Senior Brother, for protecting my child!"

Above them, the Gu Great House ancestor shook his head, then lowered his brows and looked into his hand.

"Such sharp sword qi. Such pure killing intent!"

His palm tightened, and the sword qi was immediately forcibly shaken apart.

Only then did he look toward the Wang clan true disciple.

"Were you eliminated by the master who cut out this sword?"

Only then did the Wang clan true disciple react. He hurriedly nodded, his face full of lingering fear.

The Gu Great House ancestor asked again, "Who is he?"

Before the Wang clan true disciple had time to answer, another vortex appeared in the hall.

Under everyone's shocked eyes, a weak and beautiful woman fell into the hall with a thud.

Her face was pale as golden paper, and her aura was incomparably withered.

Under everyone's perception, her entire realm was visibly falling at a speed the eye could see.

"It's Xiu'er!"

"She actually used that secret art!"

The Gu Great House ancestor's expression changed. He immediately shot out a medicinal pellet, supplemented it with the magic power of his body, and covered Madam Xu.

Finally, under his actions, Madam Xu's falling realm stopped, halting at the first level of the Sage stage.

"How could this be?"

Madam Xu slowly opened her eyes and said bitterly, "It was Wilderness Wanderer."

Wilderness Wanderer?

Everyone was startled.

The Wang clan true disciple, whose words had been interrupted earlier, also spoke in fury.

"It was Luo Chen!"

...

Madam Xu and the Wang clan true disciple were sent out to receive treatment.

But inside the hall, the competition taking place in Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven did not stop. Instead, it became even more heated.

Among everyone, Fu Chaosheng hid in a corner, afraid of attracting attention.

Especially when gazes swept over one after another, he was as silent as a cicada in winter, not daring to make a sound.

In his heart, on one hand, he complained about Luo Chen. How had Luo Chen offended three great families in such a short time?

On the other hand, he was also grateful that the other party had exerted such great effort and honored his promise so faithfully!

"I imagine he has already discovered the difference in the flow of time, yet he still exerted himself like this. This old man has wronged him."

"After he comes out, I must prepare a generous gift to express my apology."

While he thought this, time slowly passed.

Several days later.

A vortex appeared again in the hall!

The one eliminated was precisely that Xiang family prodigy.

This time, Fu Chaosheng was terrified, afraid he would hear Luo Chen's name again.

Someone went forward to ask about the situation and learned that Shi Ju had acted and eliminated Xiang Qi.

Only then did Fu Chaosheng let out a breath of relief.

But when he saw the Xiang family ancestor cast an angry gaze toward him, he could not help revealing a bitter smile.

Clearly, the Xiang family ancestor did not dare resent the Shi Great House, one of the Twelve Great Houses, and instead counted this blame upon his Fu family and Luo Chen.

After all, if Luo Chen had not eliminated Wrathful Vajra, Xiang Qi would not have been attacked by Shi Ju while in secluded cultivation.

"Fortunately, he joined Star Gate ahead of time, so he can also be considered part of the Dao Sect. Afterward, as long as he does not leave Heaven-Earth Peak, he will not suffer liquidation from the various families."

"But it will be difficult for my Fu family!"

"Who cares? As long as Qinglan accomplishes something, offending more people is still worth it. At worst, I will join Heaven-Earth Peak when the time comes and use this broken body to make a little more contribution for the sect. No matter what, the sect will have to shelter my Fu family somewhat."

...

With the elimination of two Dao protectors and two true Sage disciples, it was as though a signal had been lit.

In the following period, more and more people were eliminated.

Among them, the reason most of them were eliminated centered on Shi Ju, the chief culprit.

The other party seemed to have completely shed his reservations. He began making moves on a large scale, no longer hunting illusion beasts, but instead turning his target toward the various true disciples and robbing illusion cores from their hands.

There were even times when these eliminated true disciples had no illusion cores on them, yet he still attacked in force.

Unfortunately, this person had many magic treasures, and his magic power was tyrannical and deep. Slightly weaker Dao protectors were no match for him at all.

One against one, he held a great advantage.

One against two, he felt no fear either.

In this way, this person's sharpness was fully revealed. In barely a month, he stole Luo Chen's thunder.

Toward this, Fu Chaosheng cheered wildly in his heart.

"The more arrogant Shi Ju is, the better, as long as the ones eliminated are not Qinglan and Luo Chen!"

Although the other family representatives were furious and regretful, after their people had been eliminated, they still did not dare offend the Shi Great House again, and could only swallow that anger.

Within the tightly shut Star Gate on Heaven-Earth Peak.

Heavenly Origin Sect Master Mo Shouzhuo, who had remained and not left, was playing chess with Chess Saint Ke Lianshan.

Although the former's realm was high, in terms of attainments in the dao of chess, he was inferior to the latter.

Therefore, Ke Lianshan seemed quite at ease, and after placing a stone, he still had spare attention to chat.

"Shi Ju is too ostentatious. Such extremity is no good thing."

Mo Shouzhuo remained silent and continued holding a chess piece in thought.

Ke Lianshan asked, "Is it because that Nine Yang constitution of his is very suited to inheriting that fire-attribute divine ability within the sect that all of you indulge him like this?"

Mo Shouzhuo raised his head and glanced at him. He did not answer, but neither did he deny it.

Ke Lianshan wore an expression that said it was indeed so.

The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's seven great divine abilities were powerful indeed, but their cultivation thresholds were rather high.

Only Ascendant ancestors and Sage cultivators who had comprehended law power in advance could cultivate them.

But one fire-attribute divine ability among them was an exception!

It was not that the ability itself was an exception, but that Shi Ju was the exception. Because of his constitution, there was a very great chance he could learn that great divine ability without comprehending law power.

Once he refined it, he would inevitably possess terrifying combat strength!

At this time, with the world in chaos and the Dao Sect wishing to take the lead of the Mountains and Seas Realm, they did not lack people of longevity. What they lacked were precisely such people who were skilled in battle and fond of battle.

In the early years, rumors said that an Ascendant ancestor in the sect favored Shi Ju. Looking at it now, there was indeed a reason behind it.

Ke Lianshan shook his head. "Although you want to hone a good blade, I fear you may hone a double-edged sword. If he eliminates fellow disciples like this, and in this competition no one comprehends law power, then all the spiritual resonance brought by Fellow Daoist Chu's ascension will be far too wasted."

"It will not be wasted."

Mo Shouzhuo finally spoke.

"Among the people remaining, someone will comprehend it."

After speaking, he slowly placed a stone.

"Your turn."

Ke Lianshan looked at the place where the stone had landed and laughed despite himself.

"Sect Master, your intention is far too obvious. Are you afraid I cannot see it?"

Mo Shouzhuo stroked his beard, his face calm.

What if it was seen through? In the end, schemes and tricks in this world were minor paths. Once one possessed powerful strength, one should sweep forward with open and upright momentum.

In this game of chess, he might lose because his chess skill was insufficient.

But in the game of the Mountains and Seas Realm, he was certain to win! Because the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect was strong enough!

...

The far north of Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven.

Heavy snow filled the sky, and plants and trees had withered.

Among the peaks wrapped in silver, flying birds circled.

Abruptly.

A flying bird landed on a snowman. It pecked twice, then flew into the sky again.

After it left, snow fell away with soft rustles.

The snowman slowly opened his eyes.

Another twenty years had passed.

This time, Fu Qinglan's secluded cultivation had completely exceeded his expectations.

Fortunately, they had fled far enough at that time, and the place chosen for quiet cultivation was also an extremely remote outer region of the grotto-heaven.

Only because of this had they gained twenty years of peace.

"Counting the time, the spiritual resonance within the illusion cores should already have been exhausted. Why has she still not awakened?"

Doubt rose in Luo Chen's heart.

Suddenly, he lowered his head and looked at the ground.

Amid the accumulated snow, there was a touch of green that seized the soul.

*(End of Chapter)*
