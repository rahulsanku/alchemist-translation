# Chapter 1136: Moonstone Body, City of the Fallen Immortals

The concept of the "Heaven-Earth Furnace" was not something Luo Chen had conjured up on a whim.

It was a hypothesis proposed by Wang Yuan in the *Divine Astral Combat Qi* manual after reaching a high realm.

When Wang Yuan had looked back on his path of cultivation, he had discovered that every previous major realm still had flaws and omissions. Thus, he had conceived various perfect breakthroughs for each realm.

Take the third body-tempering realm, the Furnace Realm, for example!

Back then, Wang Yuan had sought more longevity. With no precedent to follow, he had groped his way forward and broken through.

Afterward, he discovered that his Furnace Realm had many shortcomings.

Therefore, Wang Yuan envisioned a more perfect breakthrough method, to be left for future disciples with outstanding talent.

He never expected that Luo Chen would be the one to test his ideas.

This perfect breakthrough required three extreme conditions!

An iron will capable of enduring any pain, an indestructible Great Vajra physique, and a vast sea of blood and qi energy.

With a powerful will, one suppressed the Dan Jin transformed from the vast blood and qi energy, then embraced the Dan into Gang, unleashing it in an instant to form the "Heaven-Earth Furnace." Throughout this process, the Great Vajra physique had to withstand that instantaneous eruption.

And Luo Chen indeed satisfied all three conditions.

In fact, his physical body was even stronger than the Great Vajra physique—a genuine fifth-rank Archaic beast's fleshly body.

It was more than sufficient to withstand the instantaneous eruption of all the blood and qi within him.

"This can also be considered a form of training for the internal power of the physical body!"

Luo Chen thought to himself during his leisure time.

In the past, his Archaic body-tempering method had focused on external refinement of the flesh.

Now that he had switched to orthodox body-tempering methods, it was the internal path of blood and qi.

One internal, one external—they perfectly compensated for the temporary lack of magical power and divine sense.

...

After the bandit attack last time, the merchant caravan became even more cautious, and their travel speed increased.

The frequency with which Steward Yan came to find Luo Chen had noticeably decreased.

Luo Chen was happy with this arrangement, devoting all his attention to his own cultivation.

Although outsiders might find cultivation a very boring affair, to Luo Chen it was extremely interesting.

After all, things he had once been unskilled at were now coming naturally, and the future was bright. How could he not be filled with fighting spirit?

As for Jing Lan, the farther they traveled from the small mountain village, the more taciturn she became. Occasionally, she would reveal some inexplicable emotions.

One day.

Luo Chen estimated they were nearing their destination, so he temporarily paused his cultivation.

And Jing Lan, after hesitating for many days, finally approached Luo Chen.

"You want to cultivate?"

Looking at the woman with determined eyes and a face full of longing, Luo Chen asked this.

Jing Lan nodded heavily, her eyes brimming with desire. "I can endure hardship. I want to cultivate too. I don't want to be a burden. Brother Luo, you are no ordinary mortal. You should be able to guide me!"

However, Luo Chen refused this request very decisively and bluntly.

"You are too old."

This caught Jing Lan off guard.

Before making this request, she had privately asked Steward Yan.

Although starting late at an older age would make cultivation more difficult, it wasn't impossible.

Given that Luo Chen was willing to take her away from the small mountain village, how could he refuse her with such a reason?

Yet Luo Chen had indeed used this reason to reject her.

This left Jing Lan stunned, unable to recover for a long time.

Luo Chen did not explain much. He was a man who repaid kindness. That was why he had brought Jing Lan out and intended to give her a lifetime of wealth and honor.

But cultivation?

Jing Lan truly could not.

With Luo Chen's experience and the long time he had spent with Jing Lan, he had long discovered that her body was vastly different from ordinary people.

Simply put, Jing Lan possessed a "Moonstone Body."

This constitution naturally had solidified meridians and blocked acupoints.

For the body-tempering path, which placed great importance on meridians and acupoints, the Moonstone Body was the most unsuitable.

Perhaps this was also one of the reasons Jing Lan had been abandoned as a child.

If Jing Lan were still young, before her meridians and acupoints had completely solidified and blocked, with the corresponding cultivation methods and supplemented by Luo Chen's alchemical skills, it might have been possible to set her on the path of body tempering.

But now, she was truly "too old."

Moreover, it would be difficult for Luo Chen to restore his peak alchemy skills in a short time.

Thus, Luo Chen refused very decisively, without even sparing her feelings.

Looking at the dejected woman, Luo Chen couldn't help but sigh inwardly.

"What a pity. She wasn't born in the Mountains and Seas Realm, but in this Great Destruction Realm without spiritual qi."

The Moonstone Body was a waste for body tempering, but for the qi-refining system, it was an extremely rare and excellent constitution.

Using divine sense to transport spiritual qi would be unimpeded. The solidified meridians and acupoints could also be slowly nourished and strengthened through the power of moonlight. She could even absorb the essence of moonlight like plants and spirits, mastering special spells and divine abilities.

The carriage fell into a wordless silence, with only the creaking of wheels and the neighing of horses coming from outside.

Until Steward Yan's slightly excited voice came from outside.

"Senior Luo, we've arrived!"

Jing Lan woke as if from a dream. She stood up and walked out, lifting the door curtain.

Then she gazed into the distance. With just one look, she was stunned.

It was as if a giant beast had rushed into her eyes.

A wall of pure white stretched for dozens of li, its beginning and end invisible, like a giant python biting its own tail.

And the wall top, a hundred zhang high, was impossible to see the summit of even when looking up.

On the wall top, banners fluttered, and figures could be seen in the distance.

At the city gate, pedestrians were as numerous as threads, carriages and horses like dragons, bustling with noise and excitement.

The setting sun's rays poured down, spilling onto the snow-white city wall, creating a dreamlike scene.

Compared to this, Green Mountain Town was utterly insignificant.

For a woman who had always lived in a mountain village, this formed an immense shock.

If she could live a good life in such a prosperous city, then this life wouldn't seem to have been lived in vain?

Behind her came a soft exclamation of praise.

"Not bad."

Luo Chen stood with his hands behind his back, his expression calm. There was no surprise in his eyes, only a hint of appreciation.

This caused Steward Yan, who had come early to inform him, to marvel inwardly.

The spectacle of the Fallen Immortal City ranked among the top three of the seventy-two cities. Anyone seeing it for the first time would be captivated, even a peak Furnace Realm powerhouse!

But this Senior Luo only gave a faint "not bad" evaluation.

Judging by this reaction alone, either he was accustomed to such sights and unshaken, or his mental fortitude was extremely strong, allowing him to remain unfazed even in the face of a collapsing mountain.

Or perhaps, it was both.

After all, Steward Yan had long guessed that the other party was a powerful cultivator from the outside world. It was said that those immortal cities were even more peculiar and magnificent. Compared to them, the Fallen Immortal City would appear somewhat ordinary.

And at this moment, Luo Chen had only one thought.

"In this land, what kind of enemy are such towering city walls meant to defend against?"

The merchant caravan temporarily stopped, beginning to organize their personnel and goods, preparing to enter the city.

In the distance, a fast horse galloped out from within the city.

When it drew near, the rider in black armor dismounted.

Steward Yan took the initiative to greet him. "General Chu!"

The black-armored knight called General Chu swept his gaze around, then landed on the black-robed man in the carriage.

"Are you Luo Chen?"

Luo Chen glanced at Steward Yan, then nodded slightly.

The black-robed man clasped his hands in a bow. "The Young City Lord has already prepared a banquet. Please!"
