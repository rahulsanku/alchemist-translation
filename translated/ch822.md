# Chapter 822: Frost Eagle Shuttle, Spreading Secret Arts (Attribute Inventory Included)

Fourth layer of the Nascent Soul stage, the middle phase!

Luo Chen looked at the attribute panel that had already unfolded before him, which was almost indistinguishable from his own perception.

**[Lifespan: 292/1500]**
**[Spirit Root: Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, Earth]**
**[Physique: Fire Spirit Body (Five-Spirit Dao Body, Hidden)]**
**[Realm: Nascent Soul Fourth Layer 1/100, Archaic Fourth Stage 45/100]**
**[Law: Withering-Bloom True Intent·Wood 12/100]**
**[Cultivation Methods: Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon, Proficient 110/200; Withering-Bloom Dao Scripture, Mastered 260/300; Myriad Beast Sutra, Grandmaster 510/1000; Soul-Splitting Art, Perfect 301/500; Myriad Path Convergence, Great Perfection]**
**[Spell Arts: Encompassing Fire Prison, Great Perfection; Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain, Grandmaster 630/1000; Flame Dragon Descends, Grandmaster 511/1000; Extreme Ice Seal, Beginner 10/100; Withering-Bloom Dao Finger (Divine Ability)]**
**[Body Arts: Seven Moves of the Devil Lord, Great Perfection; Wind, Moon, Thunder, Cloud—Treading the Immortal Path, Mastered 210/300]**
**[Soul Arts: Mirror Flower Water Moon, Great Perfection; Spirit-Intimidating Art, Great Perfection; Spirit-Bewildering Art, Great Perfection; Ghosts and Gods Give Way, Proficient 110/200; Aperture-Thatching Art, Grandmaster 502/1000]**
**[Achievement Points: 180]**

Looking at the dazzling array of entries that seemed to stretch endlessly before his eyes, Luo Chen couldn't help but sigh with emotion.

"Another twelve years have passed!"

At the Nascent Soul stage, as long as one didn't suffer any major injuries, one's lifespan was a full thousand years!

With such a long lifespan and the slow passage of time, it was almost impossible to feel time slipping away.

Only he, with his attribute panel, could see his lifespan at every moment, and thus had a clearer perception of how much time had passed.

Since leaving the Central Continent and returning to the Eastern Wilderness, twelve years had passed in the blink of an eye.

Many things had happened during that time. He had also made many new friends and forged new enemies.

These encounters were definitely not something one could gain by finding a quiet place and cultivating in seclusion.

But in the end, these were all external matters!

Luo Chen quickly scanned the attribute panel, gaining a complete understanding of his current state in the shortest possible time.

The progress in body refinement and qi cultivation needed no further mention.

The Law True Intent had changed slightly.

It had gone from the initial 1 to 12.

Luo Chen could roughly guess the reason for this change.

Although there had been no sudden enlightenment, after witnessing the rise and fall of the Luo Tian Sect and becoming accustomed to life and death on the battlefield, his understanding of this Law True Intent had naturally increased.

This was a good thing, but it also troubled Luo Chen somewhat.

He had no specific method for cultivating the Law True Intent. He had no idea how to actually cultivate it, and could only rely on luck.

Sometimes it wouldn't move for years, and other times it would suddenly surge.

He had no idea when this True Intent would reach completion.

"Surely I can't just endlessly use the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger, comprehending the True Intent through a divine ability?"

This thought inexplicably popped into Luo Chen's mind.

But he quickly cast it aside, because when he had privately practiced the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger, he had discovered a certain sense of dissonance between the Withering-Bloom True Intent and himself.

It seemed that this Law True Intent wasn't very suitable for him?

Therefore, he didn't dare to recklessly explore the corresponding methods, and could only take things one step at a time for now.

Then, his gaze swept over the cultivation methods column.

The proficiency level of the sword canon had increased slightly, but unfortunately, he had spent these five years focusing on cultivating his main cultivation method and hadn't continued to refine the Scarlet Firmament Sword Qi, so it had stagnated at the level of five years ago.

His self-created *Withering-Bloom Dao Scripture*, on the other hand, had improved considerably, showing that the five years of hard work hadn't been in vain.

"*Soul-Splitting Art* has reached the Perfect level!"

Luo Chen murmured to himself.

He could feel the completeness of his main soul. That feeling of vigorous overflow made him understand that this was another excellent opportunity to split off a soul.

Currently, he already had two split souls, both of which had been nurtured to the Nascent Soul level.

On a daily basis, these two split souls helped him a great deal.

Whether it was alchemy or talisman-making, practicing formations, or learning the art of puppetry, they saved him a lot of energy.

If he could have a third split soul, it would certainly allow Luo Chen to multitask and handle even more trivial matters.

But at this moment, Luo Chen hesitated.

The principle of soul refinement in the *Soul-Splitting Art* was very simple: compensate for quality with quantity.

For a cultivator's main soul to grow stronger, it could only rely on breakthroughs in each realm and the natural baptism of time.

Reaching one's own limit was already not easy.

If one wanted to break through the bottleneck of a realm and possess a soul foundation far exceeding one's own realm, it would be extremely difficult.

Even with the help of pills, it was ultimately not the correct path.

The *Soul-Splitting Art* could be considered a different approach, forging a unique path.

If vertical advancement was difficult, then develop horizontally.

Split the main soul, nurture the split souls to a level close to the main soul, and allow the main soul to relatively easily recover to its peak state.

At that point, merging the various split souls with the main soul would allow one to instantly transcend the limitations of the realm and possess a soul foundation far beyond one's own realm!

For example, Luo Chen's main soul was now roughly equivalent to that of a normal cultivator at the fifth layer of the Nascent Soul stage.

But once he merged his two split souls, he could contend with the soul strength of a cultivator at the sixth layer of the Nascent Soul stage!

Because the souls were of the same origin, the change wasn't simply a matter of one plus one equaling two.

Luo Chen had originally planned to do this.

Split off a third soul to help him learn more things while simultaneously strengthening his main soul. Additionally, he could use it to learn an extremely difficult defensive soul art recorded in the *Soul-Splitting Art*!

That was a defensive soul art called *Nether Prison Suppression*.

The difficulty of cultivating it was extremely high. Even Han Zhan hadn't been able to learn it back then.

Perhaps it was also because Han Zhan had been too focused on pursuing the number of split souls, coupled with the fact that he had formed his Nascent Soul too recently to have time to cultivate this art.

Its basic cultivation requirement was to have three split souls, respectively simulating the Heavenly, Earthly, and Human souls among the three souls and seven spirits of a living being.

However, in the surprise attack battle at the Saber Domain, Luo Chen's thoughts had been shaken.

"I've become quite famous now, and some of my powerful techniques have gradually been exposed to the public."

"If I encounter an enemy of a higher realm than myself, I'm not afraid of a normal fight, but I'm afraid they might attack me with soul arts."

"Splitting my main soul at this time would put me in a period of weakness. Moreover, the new split soul would need some time to nurture, and learning the *Nether Prison Suppression* art is extremely difficult, so it wouldn't be usable for me in the short term."

"In that case, I can only abandon long-term development and focus on short-term self-preservation."

As he murmured, Luo Chen reluctantly abandoned the plan to cultivate the *Nether Prison Suppression* soul art.

But this was only a temporary abandonment!

For the sake of the future, Luo Chen already had some plans in mind, which he would implement after leaving seclusion.

His gaze slowly descended, passing over the familiar entries for spell arts, body arts, and soul arts, until he saw the Achievement Points.

Seeing this, he couldn't help but click his tongue.

At his peak, he had accumulated over three hundred Achievement Points.

That was a huge invisible fortune!

Unfortunately, he had consumed a hundred points in the Demon-Fall Land to master the fifth-grade divine pill, *Law-Derivation Pill*.

Later, to cultivate the fifth-grade *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon*, he had consumed another hundred points.

Even though he had been constantly replenishing over the years, he only had one hundred and eighty Achievement Points now.

"Too few. It doesn't feel secure!"

"If I could replenish it to two hundred points, it would be much safer. Then I wouldn't have to worry about encountering a situation where I suddenly need Achievement Points but don't have enough."

Luo Chen sighed.

But it was just a sigh. Accumulating Achievement Points now could only be done by going with the flow. He wouldn't spend a lot of energy forcefully practicing low-level techniques that were useless to him.

Gaining achievements just for the sake of Achievement Points was meaningless!

Luo Chen also flipped through the hidden profession section behind the panel.

Compared to before, two new professions had appeared.

One was Talisman Maker, with a grade of third-tier.

The other was Puppeteer, with a grade of second-tier.

These were the results of Luo Chen's many years of research into the arts of talisman-making and puppetry.

For the Nascent Soul stage, they weren't considered great achievements.

Luo Chen just smiled dismissively when he saw them.

As for the original professions...

In the Alchemist section, several new high-grade pills had appeared.

Such as the Nine-Distance Pill, the Wind Aperture Pill, and so on.

The Formation Master section was the same as before, still at the fourth-tier.

The Physician section hadn't changed much.

But in the Artifact Forger section, Luo Chen, without realizing it, saw a floating message.

**[Heart-Inquiring Mirror Refining Blueprint detected. Would you like to master it?]**

Luo Chen was stunned. Had the split soul he left behind before going into seclusion actually researched the Heart-Inquiring Mirror refining blueprint?

With a thought from Luo Chen.

Instantly, two figures walked into the cultivation room from the cave dwelling.

They were his two split-soul puppets, Red Dog and Black Ape.

Two streams of light flew out, returning to Luo Chen's sea of consciousness like fledglings returning to the nest.

As soon as the main soul merged, while feeling immensely stronger, the memories that had been isolated over the years also instantly integrated into the main soul of the main body.

Luo Chen felt his brain swell. The research process related to the Heart-Inquiring Mirror unfolded before him like a movie scene.

After a long time, Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes.

"So that's the mystery of the Illusion Core."

After the Red Dog puppet had disassembled many Illusion Core magical artifacts, it had finally figured out how to use this thing.

The formation was secondary; the main thing was the need to use a sap from an ancient magical tree called "Return-True Liquid."

That ancient magical tree would produce a large number of illusions, causing people to get lost in them.

Only by taking its sap could one escape the illusions.

If this sap was refined and processed, one could obtain the Return-True Liquid.

And the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect used this material to controllably stimulate the illusion-inducing properties of the Illusion Core, thereby creating various weapons and magical artifacts with different functions.

Once the Red Dog puppet understood this, the remaining details in artifact forging couldn't stump it.

"In that way, as long as I forge a higher-grade Heart-Inquiring Mirror, it can not only help me temper my soul but also provide a solution to the Canglong problem."

Luo Chen's face immediately lit up with joy.

And the good news didn't stop there.

Before going into seclusion, he had left some tasks, and the Black Ape puppet had also completed them for him.

With a wave of his hand, a flying shuttle from the Black Ape puppet's storage bag fell into his hand.

The soul imprint of the Frost Eagle Demon Emperor on it had been completely erased.

Luo Chen first left his own soul imprint on it, then injected his spiritual power.

The flying shuttle began to slowly expand...

After it had grown to over a zhang in length, Luo Chen stopped injecting spiritual power.

Looking at this object, Luo Chen couldn't help but nod.

This treasure not only had the property of breaking through spells but was also exceptionally gifted in concealment and traversal.

That was why the Frost Eagle Demon Emperor had thought to bring this true artifact along when his Nascent Soul fled.

Moreover, another function of this treasure had also been researched by Luo Chen.

"Big! Big! Big!"

Luo Chen injected more spiritual power, and the flying shuttle became even larger.

One zhang, two zhang...

Unfortunately, the cultivation room was too small. After three zhang, Luo Chen had to stop his attempt to get a full view.

But in his estimation, this "Frost Eagle Flying Shuttle," when fully expanded, would be at least several hundred zhang in size, fully capable of being used as a large flying boat.

Not only was its flying speed fast, but its concealment effect was also quite impressive.

Divine sense below the Nascent Soul stage simply couldn't detect it.

Even for Nascent Soul cultivators, if they weren't on guard beforehand, it would probably be very difficult to detect.

Thinking about the concealment effect, Luo Chen thought of another item.

That was the corpse of the No-Light Beast!

This beast excelled at manifesting darkness, shrouding an area silently and invisibly. It was truly rare in the world.

Even Luo Chen, caught off guard, had fallen for it.

If the hide of the No-Light Beast were to be draped over the Frost Eagle Flying Shuttle, would the shuttle's concealment ability be greatly enhanced?

Many thoughts rose and fell in his mind.

For a moment, Luo Chen felt quite eager to act.

"It's time to leave seclusion."

Luo Chen put away the flying shuttle, preparing to leave seclusion.

But before that, he had another matter to attend to.

His ten fingers trembled, and spiritual power circulated.

One spell seal after another was struck onto his own body.

And his spiritual power fluctuations also began to slowly contract.

The realm aura displayed externally also slowly slid from the blazing fourth layer of the Nascent Soul stage down to the third layer, not much different from before.

This was Luo Chen's self-created *Withering-Bloom Divine Seal*!

Not only could it reduce spiritual power loss, helping to improve the realm, but it could also conceal one's true strength.

"Everyone is trapped by the war, and their realms are advancing slowly. If I advance too quickly, it will easily attract criticism."

"Moreover, suppressing my realm can also give a big surprise to those who want to target me at a critical moment!"

This last self-muttering carried a hint of coldness.

Luo Chen thought back to the battle at the Saber Domain, the fist that had broken through the formation and struck him.

If it hadn't been for his successful body refinement, he would have been severely injured by that unexpected blow.

Divine Fire Sage allowing a Demon Emperor to attack him through the formation from a distance was, in his opinion, definitely not a simple careless mistake!

---

A short man in a gray robe walked along the main street of Lingtian Pass.

He looked around, seemingly very curious about the bustling scene before him.

Casually, he pulled over a Qi Refinement stage cultivator. As the other person grew annoyed, he handed over a piece of mid-grade spirit stone.

The other person's face immediately lit up with a smile.

"Does Senior have something to ask?"

The gray-robed man pointed at the lively scene on the street and asked curiously, "I heard that Lingtian Pass was previously on high alert. Why is it so lively now? If I didn't know that the three great demon tribe commanders are still watching covetously, I would almost think it was still peacetime."

The Qi Refinement cultivator suddenly understood and immediately explained, "Senior doesn't know. After the Spirit-Severing Battle back then, our Lingtian Pass won a great victory, and human morale soared. Over the years, the sixteen domains in the rear have continuously sent people to Lingtian Pass, so naturally, it has become lively."

"The Spirit-Severing Battle?"

"That's the Saber Domain battle. Doesn't Senior know?"

The gray-robed man rubbed his chin, instantly understanding in his heart.

Sure enough, as the Qi Refinement cultivator explained, it was just as he had thought.

He had personally participated in that surprise attack battle, which had shattered the mountains and rivers of the Saber Domain, causing rivers to change course.

The earth veins had collapsed, and the earth qi had dispersed.

The numerous large spirit veins that had originally covered the entire domain had been shattered into pieces.

Coupled with the stench of demon blood polluting the battlefield, the originally spiritually rich Saber Domain had now become a place devoid of spirit energy.

Therefore, that battle was also called the "Spirit-Severing Battle"!

And the far-reaching influence of that battle was by no means something this Qi Refinement cultivator in front of him could fully explain.

"Actually, the recent liveliness isn't just because of the increase in people. It's also because, after the Nascent Soul elder of the Dongyang Sect arrived at Lingtian Pass, he fully promoted the first trading conference of Lingtian Pass. The intention is to allow cultivators within the city to exchange goods and resources, to enhance their strength as much as possible, so as to better cope with the upcoming war."

The Qi Refinement cultivator spoke eloquently, his words full of praise for that Nascent Soul elder of the Dongyang Sect.

The gray-robed man smiled and said, "It seems you've gained quite a bit from this trading conference?"

The man looked warily at the gray-robed man, but thinking that they were inside the city, even if the other party was a Foundation Establishment true cultivator, he couldn't do anything to him. He simply said frankly, "It's not a huge gain. I just sold some things I didn't need. I traded for some resources useful for my Foundation Establishment. I might have a chance at Foundation Establishment in the future."

Speaking of this, he couldn't help but show off, "Among them is a bottle of spiritual liquid. I heard it came from the Luo Tian Treasure Pavilion. It's very useful for my Foundation Establishment."

"Oh?"

A strange light flashed in the gray-robed man's eyes. "Can I take a look?"

The Qi Refinement cultivator hesitated for a moment, then took out a jade bottle.

The gray-robed man swept his divine sense over it and couldn't help but chuckle.

Diluted Imperial Nectar. It must have come from Qi Yi.

He let the other person take it back. The gray-robed man asked a few more questions, then thanked him and drifted away.

And after he left, the Qi Refinement cultivator stood there in a daze for a moment, a look of confusion appearing in his eyes.

He was usually quite cautious. How could he have so easily shown the Foundation Establishment resources he had worked so hard to buy to a stranger?

But looking at the piece of mid-grade spirit stone in his hand, his heart settled down.

"Maybe it's because that person looked harmless, so I let my guard down..."

---

The harmless-looking, short, gray-robed cultivator followed the directions given by the Qi Refinement cultivator earlier, turning left and right through Lingtian Pass until he entered a small alley.

He didn't go to the large trading conference in the city but instead came to a black market.

In the alley, the houses were crowded and densely packed, each with a window.

The gray-robed man paused for a moment, then directly entered a dimly lit room that emitted a strange, complex odor.

"Guest, what would you like to buy?"

The gray-robed man's gaze swept over the medicinal herbs on the shelves, a look of curiosity appearing on his face.

"With the current shortage of medicinal herb resources in Lingtian Pass, how do you have so many?"

A stooped old man walked out from the depths of the room and said casually, "If you have a bit of guts, when you go out on patrol, just sweep through the areas occupied by the demon tribes. You'll always find something."

"For example, this Blood-Stained Grass here. Because it was contaminated with the blood of a Demon King, it has some peculiar effects."

"Guess where it came from?"

The gray-robed man shook his head.

The old man smiled smugly, "It came from the Spirit-Severing Battlefield! This is a rare Blood-Stained Grass. Even the large shops in the city rarely see it."

The gray-robed man suddenly understood, then asked, "I imagine this grass must be very expensive to sell?"

"You have good eyesight, boy!" The old man praised him, then said, "If it weren't for that Nascent Soul elder of the Dongyang Sect relaxing the restrictions, this kind of good stuff could only be traded privately in the dark, and the price would only be higher. Now, you only need to take out three thousand spirit stones, and it's yours!"

"Three thousand!"

The gray-robed man frowned.

Hearing the shock in his voice, the old man said displeasedly, "You're a Foundation Establishment true cultivator. Surely you can't even take out a mere three thousand spirit stones?"

The gray-robed man gritted his teeth. "Three thousand is indeed a bit much. I have a cultivation method here. I wonder if I can use it to barter."

Barter?

The old man was a bit reluctant, but he still let the gray-robed man take it out and show him.

A tattered beast-hide booklet fell into the old man's hands.

With just one glance, the old man's eyes lit up.

"*Soul-Nabbing Essence-Plucking Art*, boy, where did this art come from?"

(The chapter ends)
