# Chapter 611: Lord Lushan, Dragon-Slaying Art

Upon first meeting Lord Lushan, Luo Chen was somewhat surprised.

He had thought someone with this Dao title would be a male cultivator, but unexpectedly, it was a clear and graceful-looking female cultivator.

Xiao Buhuan had not mentioned the other party's gender before, and Luo Chen had not thought in that direction.

This was because among wandering cultivators, male cultivators occupied seventy to eighty percent, while female cultivators were few and far between.

The reason for this was not complicated.

Although after grasping the mighty power of the immortal path there was little difference between men and women, most female cultivators yearned for stability. The cultivation world was full of shifting schemes and dangers, so many low-realm female cultivators chose to become dependents of powerful male cultivators.

Also, because female cultivators often had delicate and perceptive minds, they frequently performed well in production-related cultivation arts, and forces large and small were quite willing to accept unsupported female cultivators.

At the very least, they could be used to continue the family line.

When cultivators with spirit roots became Dao companions and bore children, the chance of those children possessing spirit roots was also much higher.

Luo Chen had never discriminated against female cultivators. One could even say that throughout his journey, he had known many powerful female cultivators with great abilities. There were some whom even he had to handle carefully.

And a female cultivator who could possess a Dao title among wandering cultivators naturally made Luo Chen respect her even more.

"Daoist Azure Yang?" In a palace atop a great mountain on Quyang Island, the clear-looking female cultivator examined Luo Chen with doubt on her face.

"That is me. Greetings, Lord Lushan."

Luo Chen smiled faintly and cupped his hands in salute.

After receiving confirmation, Lord Lushan also revealed a warm smile.

"I thought Fellow Daoist would take some more time to arrive. I did not expect you to come so quickly. It truly exceeds this humble Daoist's expectations."

Humble Daoist?

A strange light flashed through Luo Chen's eyes, and his gaze fell on the other party's hair. It was indeed arranged in an orthodox female Daoist crown style.

Simple and open, graceful and composed, giving those who saw it the feeling of bathing in a spring breeze.

Although cultivators in the cultivation world often addressed each other as fellow Daoists, in truth, "fellow Daoist" referred to those walking the same Great Dao, and few people called themselves humble Daoist.

Those who used such a self-address were nine times out of ten true followers of the Daoist path.

In the Mountains and Seas Realm, there were also the three teachings of Confucianism, Buddhism, and Daoism. This was especially so among mortals, but in the cultivation world, the divisions were not so carefully maintained.

He had not expected this Lord Lushan to actually be a female Daoist.

Though surprised inwardly, Luo Chen remained calm on the surface and began conversing with the other party.

Daoist practitioners generally had no major taboos, so Luo Chen did not need to deliberately avoid any subjects when speaking with her.

As such, their conversation proceeded quite smoothly.

Under Lord Lushan's introduction, Luo Chen's understanding of the wandering cultivator troops stationed on Quyang Island grew deeper and deeper.

The units Xiao Buhuan had described were only general terms. In reality, there were further subdivisions.

For example, the formation squad was further divided into defensive-line formation teams presiding over the defensive-line grand formation, central formation teams responsible for Quyang Island's central grand formation, and another team specifically responsible for maintaining the teleportation formation.

Another example was the logistics squad. As the name implied, it was the unit responsible for the logistical needs of the cultivators stationed on the defensive line.

In reality, the logistics unit had the most complicated composition.

It had to manage the distribution of living supplies and cultivation resources on the island, connect the first defensive line with Dragon Abyss Immortal City, and also record some cultivators' demon-hunting gains.

According to Lord Lushan, in a district of workshops below the mountain, there were even personnel specifically responsible for identifying, dismembering, and initially processing all kinds of demon beast corpses.

After this dense introduction, Lord Lushan also seemed somewhat tired.

Seeing Luo Chen frown.

She comforted him, "Daoist Azure Yang, do not be troubled. You only need a rough understanding of these matters. You do not need to worry about the specific affairs. You are carrying out a short-term garrison mission. Apart from occasionally accompanying patrol squads on inspection, you may arrange most of your time freely."

"Quyang Island also has a third-tier spirit vein. Just treat it as changing places to cultivate."

Luo Chen's brow relaxed. "In that case, it is quite easy. Many thanks to Lord Lushan for pointing the way."

Lord Lushan lightly tapped the table with a finger.

Before long, a Foundation Establishment true cultivator walked in from outside.

In front of Luo Chen, she introduced him. "This is Captain Wu of the logistics squad, responsible for managing cultivator cave dwellings. Next, he will take you to the cave dwelling arranged for you. If Daoist Azure Yang has any other needs, you may also tell Captain Wu directly. As long as Quyang Island can satisfy the conditions, it will serve you."

That Foundation Establishment true cultivator bowed to Luo Chen. "Junior Wu Ruofu greets Senior Daoist Azure Yang."

Luo Chen nodded slightly.

Then Lord Lushan took out a talisman from her storage bag and sent it before Luo Chen with mana.

"This is a voice-transmission talisman I personally and specially made. Send a divine sense message into it and burn it on the spot. Within ten thousand li, I can hear it instantly."

Receiving the white voice-transmission talisman, Luo Chen was somewhat surprised.

That far?

That fast?

The transmission efficiency of voice-transmission talismans had always been slow and often criticized in the cultivation world. They were mostly used for short-distance messages or for polite visits at someone's door.

Because of this, countless clever people had opened alternative paths and devised many voice-transmission arts.

For example, flying sword transmission, or the magic-instrument paper crane transmission method of Tianfan City, as well as the teleportation-formation transmission method used by top forces.

In addition, the voice-transmission jade slips commonly used between cultivators had a range of roughly one thousand li, which was already an excellent improved magic-instrument transmission method.

Unexpectedly, Lord Lushan had improved the voice-transmission talisman itself.

He could not help asking, "Did Fellow Daoist personally craft this talisman?"

Lord Lushan smiled and nodded.

"Can it only be used by those connected to you?" Luo Chen asked again.

Lord Lushan shook her head and smiled. "As long as they come in pairs, and the user's realm has reached Foundation Establishment, they may use this talisman with spiritual sense or divine sense."

At this point, the elegant woman blinked.

"Is Fellow Daoist interested in purchasing a few for contingencies?"

Ten thousand li was neither far nor near. At a Golden Core cultivator's normal flight speed, it was only the time it took to drink a cup of tea.

Even for a Foundation Establishment true cultivator flying at full speed, the time required was only about an hour.

For such a distance, a single round trip was enough to explain matters clearly, so there was no real need for some ten-thousand-li voice-transmission talisman.

But if one called it short, it was not short in truth.

If an urgent matter or life-and-death danger truly arose, forget an hour, or the time it took to drink tea or burn incense. Every breath was precious.

Luo Chen was somewhat tempted.

Seeing this, the female Daoist said with a trace of enthusiasm, "Although this talisman is as high as third-tier, it is not actually expensive. If you want them, one pair costs ten thousand lower-grade spirit stones."

Hearing this price, Luo Chen did not hesitate.

"Give me five pairs."

Two made one pair. Ten white talismans entered his hand, and Luo Chen also took out fifty thousand spirit stones.

After obtaining the ten-thousand-li voice-transmission talismans, Luo Chen could not help asking one more question.

"Might Fellow Daoist have voice-transmission talismans with even greater range?"

Lord Lushan gave Luo Chen a surprised look, then said, "I do have them, but those are custom-made and cannot transmit freely. Corresponding messages must be engraved in advance. Once one side activates it, the other side can receive the alert. Rather than calling it a voice-transmission talisman, it would be better to call it a token. As for price, the details would have to be discussed specifically."

This was a formidable talisman cultivator.

Luo Chen reached this conclusion.

Of course, he did not custom-order any ultra-long-distance voice-transmission talismans. For the moment, he had no need.

A few ten-thousand-li voice-transmission talismans were enough for contingencies.

Any farther, and even if some sudden event occurred, he would not be able to hold out long enough to rescue anyone.

Next, under Wu Ruofu's guidance, Luo Chen reached his temporary cave dwelling.

On the way, they flew over a stretch of low hills, with several rock caves on them and large numbers of houses made from wood and stone.

According to Wu Ruofu, those were the cultivation residences for Foundation Establishment cultivators and Qi Refinement cultivators respectively.

...

Inside a spacious stone cave in the belly of a mountain, Luo Chen nodded in satisfaction.

The environment was quiet and secluded, and the spiritual qi could be considered abundant. It truly had the quality of a third-tier low-grade spirit vein.

One could tell that someone had lived here before.

Luo Chen did not mind. In any case, it was only a temporary residence.

Facing the empty ground inside the cave, Luo Chen first took large pieces of jade from his storage ring and paved them over the floor. Then he opened his mouth and spat out the Primordial Unity Cauldron, which landed on those jade stones.

Around this area, Luo Chen walked in a circle, occasionally tossing down small formation flags of his own making.

With his current formation level, added to his artifact-forging attainment, he could already craft formation flags himself and no longer needed to buy them from outside.

In this way, a simple alchemy-and-forging chamber was complete.

It could be used for both alchemy and artifact-forging.

If Fei Ming of Ailao Mountain were here, he would probably marvel at it.

This kind of universal alchemy-and-forging chamber looked simple, but the formation understanding and material understanding it contained already surpassed far too many spirit architects.

Afterward, Luo Chen took out a large quantity of precious third-tier wood from his storage ring. All of it had been split in advance.

He stacked it neatly beneath the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Luo Chen pointed a finger, and a wisp of Withering-Bloom True Flame poured out.

In an instant, flames blazed, and the Primordial Unity Cauldron was slowly roasted.

"The Dark Blue Mysterious Armor I forged earlier only completed the initial smelting. It still needs constant adjustment. With so many materials invested into it, I cannot let the earlier effort go to waste."

As he smiled faintly, movement came from within the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

It was Black King.

Now, his body was growing larger and larger. Hidden inside the Primordial Unity Cauldron's space, even after shrinking his body, he felt increasingly cramped.

"Come out."

Light flashed inside the cauldron, and a streak of black light flew out.

It turned into a small snake and swam at Luo Chen's feet.

"Master, why not put me into the sea?" Black King sounded somewhat aggrieved. "It is uncomfortable here."

"In a couple of days," Luo Chen said calmly.

Black King did not understand.

Luo Chen specially explained to him, "The other Golden Core cultivator on garrison duty has not arrived yet. If I throw you into the sea now, and someone mistakes you for a wild demon beast and kills you, that would be troublesome."

Black King snorted. "They are worthy of killing me?"

After spending more than ten years digesting the Cliff Serpent corpse, Black King's realm had soared and already reached the peak of mid third-tier, only one step away from late third-tier.

He himself had awakened a bloodline devouring art, with vast and boundless power.

Added to the two mid-grade magic treasures Luo Chen had bestowed, which Black King refined day and night with demon power.

One could say that Black King's current combat power was no weaker than any mid Golden Core cultivator.

Relying on his thick hide and tough flesh, he even dared challenge late Golden Core great cultivators.

In the previous battle at Peng Lake, he had made a sudden move and suppressed the Night Elder on Huangfu Song's side until the man could barely fight back. From this, his current vicious might could be seen.

Seeing him act so wildly, Luo Chen looked over with a half-smile.

"You are quite arrogant."

Black King froze, then laughed. "That is not what I meant. This little one means that even when beating a dog, one must look at its master."

"Hmph."

Luo Chen lightly snorted and waved a hand.

"Go, go. Find somewhere to coil up. Do not disturb me."

After Black King left, Luo Chen sat cross-legged on the stone bed, and the thoughts in his mind gradually cleared.

He did not intend to waste this year.

Cultivation, naturally, would continue day and night. Even in a third-tier low-grade spirit land, with True Flame Pills and Black Emperor Ointment, he could still advance by leaps and bounds.

Apart from that, the work of artifact-forging could not be put down either.

Luo Chen had calculated that even if he could not refine a lower-grade magic treasure, as long as he could use the Six Armor Formation to separately refine one magic instrument, he would be confident in using that experience to refine the Demon Emperor shed husk.

After all, the defensive magic treasure refining method of the Penglai Immortal Sect valued not skill, but the material itself.

Originally, his plan had only been cultivation and artifact-forging.

But now, he had another idea.

With a flick of his hand, a small black flag fell into his grasp.

"Soul essence."

Rumor held that soul essence was soul-and-spirit essence refined from vast quantities of souls. If consumed together with secret arts, it could greatly increase a cultivator's divine soul foundation.

The reason it had this effect was that those soul-and-spirit essences no longer possessed the consciousness their owners had in life, making them the purest soul products.

This was the matter of borrowing the concept of supplementing form with form and applying it as supplementing the soul with souls.

It was the most open and overbearing method of the demonic path.

But clearly, Luo Chen could not do that now.

First, he did not have that demonic path secret art.

Second, the soul essence within this little black flag was no longer so pure.

It was stained with a great deal of Yan Zhixian's will.

One could even say that, after being attached for five years, it also contained a faint trace of Duan Li's divine soul aura.

Luo Chen absolutely did not dare swallow it.

But being unable to consume it did not mean it had no other uses.

"The Dragon-Slaying Art reached Great Perfection before and gained the effect of slaying the spirit, but I have never tested it."

"I originally intended to imitate the method I used to cultivate the Soul-Capturing Art, capturing some low-level demon beast souls and injecting them into my sea of consciousness, then cutting them down with the Dragon-Slaying Art. But I also feared something might go wrong and cause irreversible damage to my divine soul."

The divine soul was the most core part of a cultivator. Not even the slightest mishap could be allowed.

Someone as strong as Duan Li, after his divine soul was injured, had no choice but to take a risk and seek help from Luo Chen, with whom his friendship was not especially deep. This proved the point.

He had truly reached the end of the road.

But this soul essence was different.

Yan Zhixian was already dead, and the remaining will left behind was extremely little. Duan Li's divine soul aura was even less.

If Luo Chen borrowed this soul essence to test the Dragon-Slaying Art's effect, even if he failed, the impact would not be great.

And if he succeeded...

Sharp light appeared in Luo Chen's eyes.

"That means that even if someone forcibly breaks through the Rotten Axe-Handle Black Chess Pieces' divine soul defense in the future and invades my sea of consciousness, I will have a certain ability to protect myself."

Luo Chen rarely did useless work, especially regarding spell cultivation.

He already had a style that belonged solely to himself.

In his assessment, this method was feasible.

Therefore, after settling down, he could hardly wait.

After instructing Black King to watch the changes in the Primordial Unity Cauldron for him, and to add some third-tier wood if the flames were insufficient, Luo Chen's divine sense entered the black flag.

A mass of soul essence came invading as if it had seen food.

Without the slightest hesitation, Luo Chen withdrew his divine sense. At the same time, he swept up a portion of soul essence and brought it into his sea of consciousness.

The moment it entered.

Waves instantly rose in the golden sea of consciousness.

Deep within the sea of consciousness, a faintly discernible soul phantom used its divine soul body with difficulty to execute the Dragon-Slaying Art, intending to cut down the will that did not belong to this body.

...

Five days later.

Wu Ruofu stood respectfully outside Luo Chen's cave dwelling.

Before long, he saw the red-robed Daoist walk out from within.

He subconsciously lifted his head and looked. The instant he met Luo Chen's eyes, his heart shook.

What a sharp gaze that was.

It was like a blade, making anyone who saw it feel its edge.

"Is something the matter?"

A warm voice entered his ears, and Wu Ruofu instantly woke.

In another daze, it seemed as if nothing had happened. The red-robed Daoist's eyes remained gentle as water.

He swallowed. "Senior Daoist Azure Yang, Elder Sang has arrived. Lord Lushan invites you to come over for a while."

Elder Sang?

That should be Quyang Island's third Golden Core garrison cultivator.

Luo Chen nodded, summoned a cloud of magic, and flew toward Quyang's main hall.

Before long, the magic cloud stopped halfway up the mountain.

Before entering the hall, Luo Chen looked at the group of unfamiliar Foundation Establishment cultivators outside and frowned.

"These people do not seem to be wandering cultivators of our Myriad Immortals Assembly?"

*(End of Chapter)*
