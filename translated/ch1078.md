# Chapter 1078: Severing Heaven-Earth Passage, A True Lord Descends

The early autumn night was like ink, slowly spreading and blending.

Deep within the sea of clouds and mist, a young man with an immortal bearing and eyes like glazed crystal appeared.

He gazed across the void at the pair—Li Canghai and Luo Chen, one old, one young—his face utterly devoid of emotion.

"I was wondering who dared to trespass against my Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. So it's you two. Li Canghai! Luo Chen!"

The moment Li Canghai saw him, his brow furrowed slightly. "Liancheng, you're well, I trust?"

Even as he greeted the man, he sent a divine sense transmission to Luo Chen.

"Be careful. Liancheng's spirit is full and his qi is abundant. There's no sign of injury whatsoever."

Luo Chen's gaze flickered. He understood the implications of Li Canghai's words.

There were two possibilities for Liancheng being uninjured.

The first was the great battle at Broken Soul Cliff a hundred years ago, where the Five Elders of Heavenly Origin had faced Rosycloud. They had paid the terrible price of four deaths to severely wound her, leaving her immobile to this day. Liancheng, the sole survivor of those five, had also been gravely wounded. But in the past century, he had fully recovered.

The second possibility was that the sixth-rank grand formation, Heaven Dome Gathering Land, had been forcibly destroyed by Luo Chen's people. Logically, Liancheng, as the one controlling the formation, should have suffered backlash. Yet now, there was no sign of any such backlash on him. His aura was balanced and peaceful, with no trace of injury.

The first possibility was within Luo Chen's expectations. But the second was somewhat beyond Li Canghai's predictions.

For such a terrifying formation to be forcibly halted, and for the man to emerge completely unscathed?

Could it be that he wasn't the one controlling the formation?

"Naturally, I am well. But Li Canghai, for you to come uninvited today—is that not somewhat discourteous?" Liancheng's expression was calm. He remained seated cross-legged atop the broken Heaven-Earth Peak. The surrounding clouds and mist gathered, gradually forming a phantom dharma image that bore his exact features.

Seeing this, Li Canghai's expression shifted slightly. "A phantom image? You're only at early Ascendant stage. How could you possibly condense a phantom image? No, that's not a phantom image!"

He stared uncertainly at the giant formed from the sea of clouds, racking his brains to understand what kind of technique this was.

Luo Chen, however, found it somewhat familiar.

Had he seen something like this before?

At this point, Li Canghai naturally had no reason to hold back. Having determined that it wasn't a true phantom image, he simply assumed Liancheng was putting on a show.

He spoke loudly, "This old man has come today with modest demands. I only ask Fellow Daoist Liancheng to gift me one thing, to help me achieve complete success!"

As he said this, Luo Chen's expression grew somewhat more solemn.

Liancheng glanced at Luo Chen, then spoke. "What thing?"

Li Canghai laughed freely. "Very simple. This old man obtained a method for refining a spirit treasure from a certain ruin. I need to search famous mountains and great seas to refine it. In all the world today, when it comes to the greatest famous mountains, there are the Northern Sea's Primordial Magnet, the Western Desert's Green Screen, the Eastern Wilderness's Green Parasol Mountain, and in the Central Continent, Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain and Heaven-Earth Peak. The Primordial Magnet suppresses the five elements, so it won't do for me. Green Screen is sealed by eminent monks; I can't even see three-tenths of its true face. As for Green Parasol Mountain, naturally, this old man wouldn't dare to go knocking on that door."

A fierce look flashed across Liancheng's face. "So, you've set your sights on my Central Continent?"

Li Canghai said matter-of-factly, "Spirit mountains and precious lands belong to those with virtue. The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect of today is no longer the number one sacred land that once dominated the world. You have Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain, Tiannan Libei, and even Heaven-Earth Island—four fifth-rank spirit veins in total. That's far too extravagant. This old man doesn't ask for much. Just that one patch of land on Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain!"

When Li Canghai finally revealed his true purpose, Luo Chen, who had been walking alongside him, actually felt a sense of relief.

He had been genuinely afraid that the thing Li Canghai wanted was something Liancheng could actually give.

Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain—Liancheng would certainly never give it up.

As expected, upon hearing these words, Liancheng was so angry he laughed instead.

After laughing, his entire voice turned cold and stern.

"Fellow Daoist Li, do you not know that Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain is the ancestral court of my Dao Sect, the foundation of Heavenly Origin for ten thousand generations?"

"This old man knows. And I know you won't give it. So I've come to take it myself, sparing you the trouble of delivering it to my door."

"Relying on you? And that little fellow beside you?"

When he said "little fellow," Liancheng fixed a contemptuous stare on Luo Chen.

"You have quite the nerve. In all these countless years, you are the boldest I have seen—daring to offend a sacred land of Ascendants while still at the Sage realm."

"If I had no nerve, would I still be alive?" Luo Chen advanced steadily toward Heaven-Earth Peak, speaking slowly. "If you, Senior, are willing to return my life soul, then I, this sect master, can lead my people away."

The words "life soul" made Li Canghai instantly understand. No wonder Luo Chen was making such a big move, risking his life to deal with the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

Without a complete life soul, a cultivator simply could not break through to the Ascendant stage.

Even if they forcibly entered that realm, the incompleteness of their soul would cause their cultivation base to be unstable. They would inevitably fall back down.

And falling back down would be fortunate. If, due to a deficient primordial spirit, they lost themselves in the ocean of origin qi during daily cultivation, that would be a matter of life and death!

According to rumor, Luo Chen had once joined the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect and served as a guest elder for the Star Gate's external affairs.

Thinking of it this way, it must have been then that his life soul was taken!

At this thought, Li Canghai actually grew somewhat worried. What if Liancheng generously returned the life soul? Then his temporary teammate might change his mind, leaving him in a difficult position.

But Liancheng's reaction put him at ease.

On Heaven-Earth Peak, the illusory cloud-sea giant's features twisted into a contemptuous expression.

"Once you enter the Heavenly Origin sect, life and death are no longer your own. Alive, you are a disciple of my Dao Sect. Dead, you are a ghost of the Dao Sect!"

Luo Chen's expression remained unchanged. "I know. That's why I've come to take it myself. I hope you won't take offense, Senior!"

Liancheng grinned. "One of you wants to take my sect's things. The other wants to take back his own. But do you have the ability?"

Under the moonlight.

A speck of dust drifted lightly from Liancheng's palm.

Though the speck was tiny, it was like a mountain. The moment it appeared, ripples spread through the entire void.

The cloud-sea giant extended its enormous hands, gently cupping the speck, then brought its palms together.

The next moment!

The cloud-sea giant suddenly opened its hands.

A curtain of profound yellow light abruptly drew open.

With the mountain peak as its center, it spread wildly.

In an instant, the entire Heaven-Earth Peak was enveloped within the curtain of light.

"A formation!" Li Canghai blurted out, his eyes gleaming.

Then, he struck out with a palm.

A vast palm force, drawing upon the origin qi of heaven and earth, howled through the air and slammed heavily against the curtain of light.

But the reaction upon contact was eerily frightening.

Silent. No reaction at all.

That mighty palm strike vanished like a mud oxen entering the sea, disappearing without a trace.

"This..." Li Canghai was uncertain, seemingly finding it hard to accept.

Luo Chen frowned deeply, scanning Heaven-Earth Peak up and down, his gaze roaming over the profound yellow curtain. A sense of familiarity surged within him again.

This time, his memory gave him a good answer.

Luo Chen blurted out, "The Heaven-Earth Severance Formation!"

Back when he had attended the Alchemy Saint's Ascension Ceremony on Heaven-Earth Peak, he had sensed an omnipresent formation. Even his fourth-rank fleshly body had felt slightly pressured.

At that time, someone had told him that formation was the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's mountain-protecting grand formation, laid down over thousands of years—the Heaven-Earth Severance Formation.

But that formation had not stopped Rosycloud.

He hadn't expected that now, through Liancheng's hands, it would reappear before the two of them.

Hearing this, Li Canghai suddenly understood. "So it's this formation. I thought that after Heaven-Earth Peak was broken, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect could never reproduce it. I didn't expect you, Liancheng, to have such attainments in formation dao."

"If you want what you seek, break this formation first, then face me directly!"

After activating the formation, Liancheng paid them no further attention. He closed his eyes and sat cross-legged in meditation, just as before.

His strange behavior was somewhat baffling.

With a great enemy before him, he did not take the initiative to attack.

Especially since the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect was still under assault, he turned a blind eye to that as well.

If one thought he was afraid of the combined force of Li Canghai and Luo Chen, that was even more impossible. One had merely obtained a lucky chance and barely achieved Ascendant. The other was still at the Sage realm, nothing to fear.

Liancheng was the ancestor of the number one sacred land under heaven. He naturally possessed great pride. How could he be afraid?

If he was acting this way, there must be something more important than dealing with the two of them.

Luo Chen frowned, then looked at Li Canghai.

"Senior, do you recognize this formation?"

"Since you've named it, naturally I recognize it." Li Canghai smiled slightly. "Earlier, because of its different form of expression and different power, this old man didn't recognize it. Looking at it now, it is indeed a variant of the Heaven-Earth Severance Formation."

He no longer probed. Instead, his figure slowly rose, floating outside the profound yellow curtain.

In mythological legends, there was a term "Severing Earth and Heaven's Passage," meaning that heaven above and earth below, gods and humans, each had their own duties and did not interfere with each other.

But with the word order changed, in the hands of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, the power of this Heaven-Earth Severance Formation was completely different.

"According to Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's research, this formation is actually not complicated. It uses a fifth-rank spirit vein as its foundation, cuts off the origin qi of heaven and earth, and suppresses living beings. Those below the fifth rank cannot move. Those above the fifth rank cannot manipulate origin qi."

"So, this old man's strike vanished like a mud oxen entering the sea, with no reaction at all."

"This formation doesn't have much killing power, but it has an immediate effect against enemies at the Ascendant stage and above."

"If we break in, we will be stripped of our ability to manipulate the origin qi of heaven and earth, and can only fight with our own dharma power. But the formation's master is completely unaffected. Moreover, every move he makes will be empowered by the fifth-rank spirit vein, doubling its might."

"With this shift in balance, we cannot enter this formation."

After a bout of research, Li Canghai reached this conclusion.

Luo Chen quickly asked, "Can it be broken?"

"It can!" Li Canghai nodded. But before Luo Chen could relax, he continued, "But it takes time! The other party has been preparing this formation for a long time. Otherwise, it couldn't have been activated instantly. I'm afraid that the moment we set foot on the island, he could have activated it at any time."

Time...

Luo Chen looked gravely at Heaven-Earth Peak.

There, the cloud-sea giant was still holding up the curtain of light, maintaining the operation of the Heaven-Earth Severance Formation.

As for Liancheng himself, he was sitting cross-legged on the flattened mountain peak. Beneath his feet were countless densely packed lines, crisscrossing to an unknown endpoint. A wisp of blood-red color was slowly staining those lines.

If nothing unexpected happened, those lines were the foundation of another formation.

The God-Descending Formation!

This was the foundation of the number one sacred land under heaven!

At the same time, three top-tier grand formations were activated simultaneously.

The entire sect's power was used to activate the Heaven Dome Gathering Land Formation. Ten thousand years of accumulated foundation were used to activate the Heaven-Earth Severance Formation. And finally, Ancestor Liancheng was still maintaining the operation of the God-Descending Formation.

If he truly succeeded...

"Luo Chen, there's good news!" Li Canghai observed for a long while, then suddenly spoke.

"Speak."

Li Canghai said quickly, "This Heaven-Earth Severance Formation has been broken before. Moreover, Heaven-Earth Peak itself was broken in two, causing the formation's foundation to be unstable. Therefore, the formation before us is actually incomplete. Apart from its suppression of origin qi manipulation, all other powers are absent. So, I only need to target the earth qi here, and I can shorten most of the time needed to break this formation."

Luo Chen's eyes lit up. "Then I'll trouble you, Senior!"

Li Canghai chuckled. "No trouble. Once I break this formation, I'll also gain mastery of one more formation. I can set it up at my cultivation site in the future to better protect against external enemies."

Even as he spoke, he began to work.

Array flags flew from his hand, sinking into the ground below.

Hand seals flew from his hand, striking into the interior of the profound yellow curtain.

As time passed, the profound yellow curtain began to tremble slowly.

Seeing this, Luo Chen knew that Li Canghai's reputation as a great formation dao master was not false. In such a short time, he could already break a peerless grand formation that a sacred land of Ascendants relied upon.

But was that short time truly enough?

On Heaven-Earth Peak, Liancheng had opened his eyes at some point. He watched coldly, allowing Li Canghai to break the formation at will.

Luo Chen didn't understand. He didn't know why the other party was so steady, as stable as Mount Tai.

With anxiety in his heart, Luo Chen prepared for the great battle that would follow once the formation was broken.

To be safe, he even bit his fingertip and smeared the purest source blood over his eyes.

His complete Crimson Eye Golden Pupil was activated at full power!

At that moment, Luo Chen's mind shook.

Within his field of vision, wisps of lively spiritual energy were converging from four directions, continuously gathering toward a certain place beneath Heaven-Earth Peak.

That place was...

"The entrance to the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven!"

Luo Chen said coldly, a chill spreading over his body.

"Luo Chen, I've broken an opening in the formation. Give me a little more time... What did you say?" In the distance, Li Canghai seemed not to have reacted yet.

Luo Chen's body was already covered in layers of cyan-white flames.

"The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect isn't trying to reunite the Central Continent at all. That's just a convenient matter. What they want to do is to swallow up three continents—no, even the Western Continent that has already sunk into the sea is included. They want to take back all the scattered spiritual energy that was once dispersed throughout the Central Continent, to rebuild Heaven-Earth Peak and activate the God-Descending Formation!"

Li Canghai was somewhat incredulous. "Gathering spiritual energy is not something an Ascendant-stage cultivator can do. That's a technique that only a True Lord at the Refining Void stage can perform after condensing a phantom image. Liancheng absolutely cannot!"

"No, they can do it!" Luo Chen shook his head. The origin qi of the outside world was frantically gathering into his body under the manipulation of his divine sense. An extremely terrifying power was on the verge of erupting.

Putting aside whether gathering spiritual energy was truly something only a Refining Void True Lord could do, at the very least, Luo Chen knew that the Alchemy Saint Chu Yan had long ago researched a pill art called "Refining Heavenly Secrets," which could capture small amounts of spiritual energy and refine them into pills.

And within the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, there was a spirit treasure that could both suppress an entire continent's spiritual energy and gather it.

Luo Chen had personally seen the former Heavenly Origin Sect Master Mo Shouzhuo use that treasure to open the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven.

Its name—the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard!

Mo Shouzhuo had already fallen, but the master of this spirit treasure still lived. It was none other than the Central Continent's Chess Saint, Ke Lianshan.

If he had advanced to Ascendant stage and fully activated the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard, the effect would be even better than when Mo Shouzhuo had used it!

Ke Lianshan was nowhere to be seen now.

But the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven had opened a year ago, and a large number of Sage cultivators had entered. It couldn't possibly be just a simple grotto-heaven competition.

"He's breaking through to Ascendant stage inside, while simultaneously controlling the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard to absorb a massive amount of spiritual energy for Heaven-Earth Peak!"

As if to confirm Luo Chen's conjecture, a vortex appeared out of thin air at the entrance to the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven. A vast amount of heaven and earth origin qi poured into it.

And on Heaven-Earth Peak, Liancheng had already floated up from the mountain, his entire body merging into the giant formed by the clouds.

At this moment, the giant seemed to have gained a physical form.

The giant extended a massive hand, grabbing toward the nearby low but straight mountain ridge.

No!

That wasn't a mountain ridge!

That was the upper half of the former Heaven-Earth Peak. It had been broken off and fallen to the earth. A hundred years had passed, and it had formed a low mountain ridge.

The enormous mountain ridge was grasped in the giant's hand as if it weighed nothing. Then, it pressed it toward Heaven-Earth Peak.

"Reunite the Mountain!"

Liancheng's vast voice resounded through the void.

The moment Heaven-Earth Peak was reassembled, everything returned to how it had been. It was as majestic a mountain as Luo Chen had seen back then.

The next moment!

"Descend the God!"

A pillar of light shot straight up, heading for the starry cosmos.

Within the vast pillar of light, an extreme killing intent began to seep into this world.

All of this took a long time to describe, but it happened in the blink of an eye.

Li Canghai stared blankly at this scene. Feeling that extreme killing intent, he felt like an ant.

"Luo Chen, run!"

Without hesitation, Li Canghai turned and fled.

The God-Descending Formation had already been activated. The descent of a True Lord from the upper realm was now inevitable.

Staying here meant certain death!

But Luo Chen did not follow.

Li Canghai looked back. He saw Luo Chen, his entire body radiating cyan-white flames, charging through the opening that he had just broken in the Heaven-Earth Severance Formation.

A mayfly trying to topple a tree. A mantis trying to stop a chariot!

At this moment, similar thoughts arose in Li Canghai's mind.

He did not pause for even a moment. He directly fled Heaven-Earth Island, then activated the translocation formation he had left outside, disappearing into the distance.

As long as the green hills remain, there will be no shortage of firewood. He could not die here with Luo Chen.

Perhaps only two or three parts of admiration remained, as the respect he left for this young friend.

"Fellow Daoist Luo, take care!"

(End of chapter)
