# Chapter 536: Han Zhan Bows His Head, the Confidence to Range Across the Demon Sea

The gray great cauldron shimmered with hazy spiritual light.

Nine fire dragons emanating an aura that seemed ready to devour everything stared covetously at a certain space within the cauldron.

Not only that, Luo Chen, seated cross-legged on the ground, looked as if he were facing a great enemy. His mana was fully raised, azure flame covered his right hand, and he seemed capable of unleashing a heaven-shaking, earth-shattering strike at any moment.

Outside his sea of consciousness, a black chess piece radiated a dim black halo, tightly wrapping his sea of consciousness.

His eyes, fixed on the Primordial Unity Cauldron, blazed with spiritual light. Mirror Flower Water Moon rose and fell in succession, light and shadow flickering without cease.

No wind had risen, yet his sleeves already rustled sharply.

Such a scene truly answered the aged voice in the cauldron when it said "such a formation"!

One could imagine how terrifying it would be once Luo Chen suddenly erupted in attack.

Especially since his presumed enemy, Han Zhan, was inside the imprisoning space of his lifebound magic treasure.

Heavenly timing, earthly advantage, and human harmony were all in Luo Chen's hands alone. Even if Han Zhan had previously been an exalted Nascent Soul Sage, at this moment, he had no choice but to bow his head.

Hearing Han Zhan's bitter laugh, Luo Chen did not relax his vigilance.

Or rather, when dealing with any Nascent Soul Sage, one absolutely could not relax one's vigilance.

He calmly replied, "It is not that Luo is making a fuss over nothing. I truly have no choice but to act this way. I ask Senior Han to understand."

There was a moment of silence within the cauldron.

Afterward came a helpless voice.

"This old man can understand."

Yes. The situation the two of them were in now was already completely different from when the opening war had begun.

Yet Luo Chen did not possess absolute suppression in realm.

Under such circumstances, no matter how careful he was, it was understandable.

If it were Han Zhan himself, he would probably do the same.

One could even say that, toward the other party's caution, he felt a few extra traces of approval.

Han Zhan sighed emotionally, and his tone seemed to become more open.

"This old man now has only a wisp of remnant soul left. Even if I do not say I lack the strength to truss a chicken, my ability is truly limited. After escaping death, young friend did not kill me on the spot to eliminate future trouble. That already shows your true character."

"Then, how do you intend to deal with this old man now?"

How to deal with him?

This time, the one who fell silent became Luo Chen.

Toward Han Zhan, Luo Chen's feelings were very complicated.

This was a legendary figure who had often lingered by his ears ever since he transmigrated.

The most outstanding genius in the entire history of the Jade Cauldron Domain. His cultivation speed was astonishing, and his combat strength was exceptional, surpassing even Ren Pingsheng, the number-one Golden Core of the Sword Sect.

When he was only a little over two hundred years old, he had relied on his powerful strength to make Cheng Yan of the Falling Cloud Sect voluntarily abdicate in favor of the worthy, thereby ascending to become the sect's supreme elder!

After that, when he was a little over three hundred, he had quietly condensed a Nascent Soul, accomplishing that great undertaking of cultivators and stepping into the realm of Sages.

Originally, Luo Chen had no connection with him.

Yet because of one kind thought, saving his grandson Han Zheng, Luo Chen had entered Han Zhan's notice.

During that period, although the two had never met, because of this matter, the other party had shown Luo Chen many acts of goodwill.

Whether the battle at Incense Valley that annihilated the Yan Alliance, or Qu Xibai of the Falling Cloud Sect voluntarily opening Tianlan Immortal City's spirit vein restrictions when Luo Chen formed his core, both revealed the goodwill Han Zhan had expressed.

After the opening war erupted, the other party had also personally acted to save Luo Chen.

It was precisely because of these kinds of matters that, even though there had been give and take, and Luo Chen asked himself honestly and found that he owed the other party nothing, Han Zhan had still left an excellent impression on him.

Therefore, when Han Zhan had been at his last breath, Luo Chen had not taken advantage of the situation or struck someone when he was down.

But now?

If the other party were still a lofty Nascent Soul Sage, Luo Chen could still move like a fish in water and interact with him normally.

There was no other reason. He was used to it!

In this life, Luo Chen was simply far too familiar with dealing with those in higher positions.

If the other party had fallen into the dust while Luo Chen rose up, and their positions had reversed, there would not have been any major problem either.

After all, over these years, Luo Chen's art of controlling subordinates had also been tempered to considerable effect.

But now, the situation was precisely awkward.

Control him?

Luo Chen considered his methods absolutely not weak among Core Formation cultivators. Even if a late Core Formation great cultivator personally attacked, he had three parts confidence in protecting himself.

But before a Nascent Soul Sage, even if it was only a wisp of remnant soul, he did not dare get carried away.

If he suffered backlash, he would make himself a laughingstock.

Kill him?

Luo Chen could not pass that hurdle in his heart either.

Let him go?

Luo Chen also felt that would be a pity. He had lived inside the Primordial Unity Cauldron for nearly four years and had not even paid rent.

During these four years, Luo Chen had thought more than once about how to handle Han Zhan.

But no matter how many plans he had in mind, after a Sage cultivator awakened, all of them became pale and powerless.

The natural suppression of realm made it impossible for Luo Chen to easily voice those plans.

An indescribable silence filled the cultivation chamber.

The aged voice suddenly rang out.

"Luo Chen, you are hesitating."

"Heh."

"From the looks of it, you truly do have a few parts of the gentlemanly bearing spoken of in rumors. You do not do things like striking someone while he is down."

Luo Chen lifted his eyelids and calmly said, "Senior Han, why put a tall hat on me? If I were truly a gentleman, I would have released you openly and honorably long ago."

"Heh..." Han Zhan laughed softly. "A gentleman is judged by his deeds, not his thoughts. Whether a hypocrite or a true petty man, what is always judged is the final result. If the result is that I live and am free, then no matter what you did in the process, once it spreads, it will only become a fine tale of you and me supporting one another and escaping Cangwu Mountain. Young friend, what do you think?"

The final result!

Luo Chen's gaze focused, as if seeing through the thick gray cauldron barrier and into the little black wooden figure within that space.

"Senior wants to live, and to live freely?"

Han Zhan laughed self-mockingly. "Between life and death there is great terror. Especially for me, who survived by luck beneath that wisp of Nirvana Sacred Flame, I cherish my life all the more. I have made you laugh."

Luo Chen deeply agreed.

When he had struggled under Mi Shuhua, had he not also wanted to live freely?

For that, he had even secretly dug tunnels, ready to flee at any moment.

But precisely because he understood, he became even more cautious. A person who wanted to live freely was very frightening!

Seeing Luo Chen fall silent again,

Han Zhan seemed unwilling to keep beating around the bush and directly came to the point.

"Let us discuss terms!"

"What exactly must be done for you to let this old man out of the cauldron?"

The other party choosing to bare everything instead dispelled the hesitation in Luo Chen's heart.

His expression turned solemn, and he said frankly, "What Luo is worried about, Fellow Daoist must understand better than anyone."

"You fear this old man will secretly harm you?"

"Exactly. Senior's methods are mysterious and unfathomable, impossible to guard against. The Thunder Prison Divine Peng was deceived by you, allowing you to fake death and seek survival. I too once believed you lacked the strength to truss a chicken, yet beneath Nirvana Sacred Flame, you erupted with powerful strength. Although it seems you now have only a wisp of remnant soul, ancient legends say that the absolutely powerful, even with only a drop of blood left, still possess the might to stain the blue heavens with blood. How can one know whether a wisp of soul can erupt with the methods of a Sage?"

"You are very cautious. I can understand. But this time, you are indeed overthinking it."

"In all matters, calculations come first. There is no such thing as overthinking."

"How about establishing a contract and forming an agreement? Luo Chen, you previously founded the Luo Tian Alliance, so you should have extra talisman contracts. Or I also have a blood contract method. It requires only the blood essence of the two of us to take effect."

"Not enough. The binding force is far from enough."

"Then what do you want?"

"I want a Great Dao oath!" Luo Chen said with iron certainty.

Han Zhan seemed to have expected this long ago. He asked in reply, "Will you swear an equivalent Great Dao oath?"

Luo Chen denied it. "I will not. But your oath must include that you will never attack me for life, nor borrow a knife to kill me, and similar clauses."

"For life..." Han Zhan muttered, and finally smiled bitterly. "Too long. You know very well that we cultivators, the higher our realm, the closer we are to the Great Dao. The Great Dao oaths we swear take effect more easily. Those words 'for life' are a shackle. I fear that if this old man swears such an oath, I will be unable to advance for the rest of my life."

At this point, he paused, then said with a sigh,

"Luo Chen, do not look at how I call myself this old man with every breath. If you really calculate it, I am only a little over four hundred years old now. Among Sage cultivators, I can also be considered young and promising. That Ascendant realm is something I yearn for very much!"

Han Zhan had formed his Nascent Soul at three hundred. The Jade Cauldron internal war and opening war together had passed over a hundred years, so now he was a little over four hundred.

He was indeed very young!

Instead, Luo Chen caught another piece of information in the other party's words.

"Senior Han has only a wisp of remnant soul left now, yet you still long for the Ascendant realm. Could it be that you have a method to reconstruct a body, return the soul, and recultivate the Great Dao?"

Han Zhan froze. He had not expected Luo Chen's sense to be so sharp.

But with another holding the knife and him the fish on the chopping block, there was nothing much to hide.

"Others might be unable to, but I, who am proficient in the dao of puppets, do have corresponding methods."

At this point, he suddenly also realized something.

"You will not kill me, but you will not release me. From the looks of it, Luo Chen, you also have designs on me!"

"Let me think. What does this old man have that could make you covet it?"

Without waiting for Luo Chen to refute him,

he continued speaking to himself. "This old man's storage belt was long ago taken by the demon cultivators of Cangwu Mountain. I can be said to possess nothing."

"This Yin Pole Puppet is quite extraordinary, but it too was burned to ashes beneath Nirvana Sacred Flame and is not worth mentioning."

"Thinking about it this way, what you want is nothing more than the things in this old man's head."

"Cultivation arts, secret techniques, puppet-making arts, medicinal formulas, formation diagrams... or perhaps this method of reconstructing a body, returning the soul, and recultivating the Great Dao?"

"Luo Chen, do you think my judgment is correct?"

Luo Chen blinked. Although his thoughts had been guessed, he did not feel embarrassed or angry.

Discussing terms was precisely exposing each other's bottom lines step by step.

He said clearly, "Senior's four hundred years of knowledge and experience are themselves far more precious wealth than the things you just listed."

Han Zhan laughed freely. "Since that is so, why do you and I not each take a step back?"

"How do we step back?"

"I can swear a Great Dao oath, but the time limit must be controlled within a certain period. For example, until you condense a Nascent Soul and ascend to become a Sage of your generation. At that time, you will presumably have some ability to protect yourself and naturally will not fear me. What I can repay you with is to assist you as much as possible in forming your Nascent Soul, so long as it does not violate my own principles. Of course, during this process, you must help me collect the corresponding materials so that I may continue my Great Dao in the future."

After hearing these words, Luo Chen sank into a long period of thought.

It had to be said, this was the most ideal state!

In this life of his, although he had friends' support and a dao companion's assistance, he happened to lack guidance from a so-called "enlightened teacher."

If Han Zhan was willing to sincerely and wholeheartedly assist him, it would undoubtedly be of enormous help.

And what he himself would pay would not be too much.

Great Dao oaths had ordinary binding force on normal low-level cultivators, but toward high-level cultivators, they were very strong.

Especially since Han Zhan's current state could be called that of a naked child without even a garment to cover himself. Once he violated the oath, under the oath's backlash, he would die violently on the spot.

Suddenly, Luo Chen spoke with a clang.

"Deal!"

Once those two words left his mouth, even the heavy air seemed to loosen.

However, it was only seemingly.

After a long while, a laughing curse came from within the cauldron.

"Good boy. To think you are cautious to this extent."

Luo Chen tugged at the corner of his mouth. "I ask Senior to swear the oath first. This junior will immediately release Senior afterward."

"Fine!"

"Cultivator Han Zhan hereby swears that I will wholeheartedly assist Fellow Daoist Luo Chen... This oath is witnessed by the Great Dao! If I violate it, may the five thunders strike my crown, may my heart demons backlash, may I sink into Avici, and may I forever fall into reincarnation!"

Word by word, it was like establishing a contract and forming an agreement with another person.

Luo Chen listened carefully, ensuring there were no excessive loopholes of wording within.

Gradually... when the four words "witnessed by the Great Dao" were spoken, a faintly discernible connection was established between Luo Chen and Han Zhan.

For Luo Chen, it was like a clear breeze brushing his face, so faint he nearly did not sense it.

For Han Zhan, it was like golden chains crossing and a wooden cangue locking around his neck.

At this point, the oath was complete.

Luo Chen let out a long breath of relief.

The spiritual light in his eyes dispersed, and the azure flame in his hand withdrew.

He opened his mouth and inhaled. The nine fire dragons fled into his mouth.

Finally, with a wave of his great sleeve, the cauldron hummed, and a black light leaped out.

Before it could even say a polite word, that black light swayed and fell to the ground.

Crack, crack...

Luo Chen focused his gaze and saw that the black wood composing the little puppet figure had become like charcoal, splitting into pieces. Only a somewhat transparent illusory little figure looked bitterly toward Luo Chen.

"Young friend, now I must first trouble you to find me a place to reside."

Luo Chen was speechless.

After all this, this was the reason the other party, after recuperating in the cauldron for four years, had finally had no choice but to awaken!

Looking at the other party's condition, Luo Chen's heart suddenly stirred.

With a beckon, a long banner landed in his hand.

"Senior, please enter this banner!"

Seeing this long banner, Han Zhan could not help being both shocked and furious.

"We just finished negotiating terms, and now you want to refine me into a soul-banner devil? Luo Chen, you go too far!"

No wonder he was so furious.

There were differences when souls entered demonic banners.

The difference lay in living souls and dead souls!

Those who died wrongfully had resentment condense and linger after death, their divine souls remaining in the world. These were dead souls. If such existences were refined, they would become proper demonic path supreme treasures, impossible to guard against in battle, ever-changing, and extremely practical.

Living souls, however, were those cultivators who, after reaching success in realm, could still preserve their minds even if their bodies were destroyed, and could even borrow a body to recultivate.

If such an existence entered a demonic banner where death qi, ghost qi, resentment qi, and yin qi gathered, then even if it was a living soul, over time it would become a dead soul and would never again be able to return to the normal path of cultivation.

Han Zhan's current state was precisely that of a living soul who had achieved success in realm and unfortunately lost his body.

Toward his anger, Luo Chen chuckled.

"Senior, do not be angry. I have a method. You may listen to the details."

Under Han Zhan's half-believing, half-doubting gaze, Luo Chen recited word by word a cultivation art he had obtained in his early years.

Ghost God Secret Record.

Although it was a ghost cultivation method, it included both the paths of living souls and dead souls.

Not only that, it also contained Ghost God Valley's understanding of using formations to nourish and refine yin souls.

Back then, the reason Bai Meiling had been able to advance to a second-tier Ghost General in just a few short years was because she had cultivated the Ghost God Secret Record, and also had a Soul-Nourishing Banner assisting her.

Luo Chen did not have a Soul-Nourishing Banner, but he had a Soul-Refining Banner!

With Han Zhan's ability in the dao of spirit refinement, if he combined it with the Ghost God Secret Record and slightly modified it, would this banner not become an excellent dwelling place?

Nourishing and refining were originally two parts of one body!

After hearing Luo Chen's narration, Han Zhan revealed a hesitant expression.

"There are indeed a few feasible points, but this old man must study it first."

As he spoke, he leaped and drilled into that long banner.

Seeing this, the corners of Luo Chen's mouth could not stop rising.

This Myriad Souls Banner had now welcomed a remnant soul at the Sage level. If he completely replenished the low-level souls, not only could it restore the might of a Myriad Souls Banner, it could even contend with the rumored Hundred-Thousand Fierce Souls Banner.

Most importantly!

It did not require Luo Chen to divide his attention to control it.

In this way, during battle, Luo Chen was equivalent to gaining a powerful helper out of thin air.

With Han Zhan's ability, even if he could not display the Sage might of his full glory, he would absolutely surpass ordinary Core Formation cultivators.

"With this, I finally have a sliver of confidence to range across the Demon Sea!"

(End of chapter)
