# Chapter 1155: Heaven-Earth Reversed Wheels, Land of Return to Ruins

The sound of a dragon's roar resounded across the wilderness.

Rolling waves of sound stirred the Returning Wind Mountain Range, whipping up layers of storms.

At last, the long cry gradually faded, leaving only a single figure standing atop the peaks.

A white robe, moving without wind.

All around him, golden and black light flickered unceasingly, giving off a strange sensation—as if he were as heavy as a mountain, yet also as if he might ride the wind and depart at any moment.

Luo Chen quietly sensed the situation within his body, his expression as calm as ever, revealing neither joy nor anger.

Ten years to refine the Nine Tribulation Earthly Balefulness.

Another ten years to refine the Unforgivable Heavenly Wind.

At this moment, his body simultaneously possessed two types of top-grade astral qi, completely different in nature but comparable in power.

According to the description in the *Divine Astral Combat Qi* manual, he had not yet fused the two astral qi into one, so he could not be considered to have achieved great success.

But whether it was the Nine Tribulation True Astral or the Unforgivable Heavenly Astral, their power far exceeded the divine astral combat qi that Wang Yuan had cultivated back in the Furnace Realm. Even compared to his Leakless Realm, it was not necessarily weaker.

The reason the two great astral qi had not merged was precisely because each was of too high a quality and their natures were too extreme.

Based on Luo Chen's speculation, if he could solve this problem, advancing from the late Furnace Realm to the Leakless Realm would likely be a matter of course.

Indeed!

Ten years of bitter cultivation in the basin of the Returning Wind Mountain Range, and he had unknowingly broken through a minor realm.

Of course!

In the eyes of true experts, this realm still counted for little.

Just like the six figures who were now flying toward him at great speed, their gazes fixed tightly on him.

As soon as they approached, the six figures each took their positions, their auras spreading out to form an invisible net that locked Luo Chen firmly in place.

"Luo Chen!"

"A cultivator!"

"An Ascendant-level great power?"

"In just twenty years, to have cultivated to such a realm—if we give you another hundred years or so, your achievements would be unimaginable."

"What a pity..."

"If you lay down your arms and surrender now, offering up your primordial spirit, we can spare your life."

Facing the wolf-like gazes, a faint smile gradually curved at the corner of Luo Chen's mouth. He swept his eyes over the few people with an ambiguous expression.

"You've all waited long enough."

The West Ridge City Lord recalled the long cry that Luo Chen had just sent straight toward them, and his expression darkened.

"You discovered us long ago?"

Surrounded by six people, Luo Chen was not particularly flustered.

Instead, he laughed. "Who wouldn't notice being watched every single day?"

The Purple Gold City Lord seemed somewhat incredulous. "Within the Return to Ruins, a cultivator's divine sense can hardly extend outward. You haven't even reached the Leakless Realm—how could you detect our surveillance?"

Faced with this question, Luo Chen was too lazy to answer. Instead, he was very curious about another term the other party had used.

"Return to Ruins?"

But clearly, no one wanted to give him an explanation.

Especially the West Ridge City Lord, who was already impatient.

"Attack!"

The moment the words fell, the six experts moved together.

Five of them charged straight at Luo Chen.

The one who remained behind was a beautiful young woman. She held a bamboo flute and slowly brought it to her red lips.

Astral qi flowed, and the flute's tone was crisp.

The moment it entered his ears, Luo Chen's expression seemed to flicker, as if a patch of bamboo groves swaying in the wind appeared before his eyes.

This was actually a rare technique for attacking the soul!

Anyone who heard this sound would have their battle will weakened by at least three points.

Fortunately, the five companions were prepared and were not too affected. Instead, they seized this opportunity and unleashed their respective attacks, all of which struck Luo Chen simultaneously.

*Boom!*

A deafening sound erupted, momentarily drowning out the crisp flute tone.

Even the highest peak among the mountains turned to powder under this blow, sending up a towering plume of dust.

Having succeeded in their strike, everyone's faces lit up with joy.

"It worked!"

"After all, this is the Return to Ruins, which naturally suppresses cultivators. Even an Ascendant great power comparable to a human immortal is so vulnerable!"

However, this joy did not last long.

The next moment.

To everyone's horror, a giant hand that seemed to hold up the sky reached down from the void.

The experts all dodged, but one person was too slow to evade.

Or rather, from the very beginning, that claw-like giant hand had been aimed at that person.

Xiang Fei's expression changed drastically. Seeing that she could not avoid it, she raised her skirt sleeves and unleashed a series of attacks, but none of them could shake the giant claw descending from the clouds.

*Pfft!*

With a single claw, Xiang Fei was firmly caught.

The astral qi protective shield around her body shattered inch by inch under the terrifying force, and a mouthful of blood sprayed out.

The giant claw slowly withdrew, and Xiang Fei collapsed weakly into the ruins.

She looked up. The originally elegant and unrestrained man's white robe was already completely shattered. But the muscles exposed beneath were as unshakable as bedrock.

Xiang Fei's beautiful eyes widened, filled with disbelief.

A combined strike from five great experts had not even managed to break through his defense?

Just how great was the gap between them?

After capturing Xiang Fei, Luo Chen paid no attention to her thoughts. Several strands of astral qi had already forcibly broken through her Leakless Golden Body in that instant, forming a "blood qi restriction" within her.

Now, he only needed to deal with the remaining five.

"Having one person to answer my questions is enough. As for the rest of you..."

Luo Chen licked his lips, his gaze sweeping over the remaining five, who were like startled birds.

He had no interest in learning the names of these people—called Returners, but in truth, failures.

Their purpose now was only one.

"Perfect for testing my new move!"

Luo Chen raised both hands flat, and a golden and a black astral qi suddenly appeared.

Within his body, the massive blood qi furnace began to spin rapidly in a counterclockwise direction.

Guided by this, the two great astral qi also began to rotate wildly in opposite directions.

From the perspective of the West Ridge City Lord and the other four.

When Luo Chen did not act, it was fine. But once he did, it was a world-shaking move.

Fortunately, this move seemed to have a somewhat long prelude.

"We can't let him complete it."

"Break it together!"

The five experts were all strong practitioners who had been famous for many years. They had not yet lost their will to fight. Seeing Luo Chen gathering power, they each unleashed their ultimate techniques.

A great palm, pressing down through the void.

Astral qi taking form, a purple-gold dragon swaying its head and tail as it charged forward.

Someone wielded a long blade, slashing out a heaven-startling saber light.

Facing these attacks, Luo Chen still did not dodge or evade, letting these ultimate techniques and martial skills strike his body.

A fifth-grade Great Desolate fleshly body, comparable to a true spirit, was a full major realm above these Leakless Realm martial artists.

Let alone breaking through his defense—they could not even manage to move him a single step.

This was absolute disparity!

Just like an Initiate cultivator facing an Ascendant great power.

One after another, the attacks landed on Luo Chen's body like scratching an itch. He seemed not to notice, merely controlling the astral qi surging out from within.

The next moment!

He raised both hands.

*Hum...*

A peculiar sound rang out.

In an instant, two millstones appeared in the void—one forward, one reverse; one gold, one black.

Merely hanging in the air, they gave off a sense of power sufficient to grind everything to dust.

The five city lord-level experts were terrified. The battle courage that had united them just moments ago crumbled like mud and sand.

They each turned into arrows leaving the string, fleeing in five different directions.

However, how could Luo Chen let these petty villains who coveted him escape so easily?

With a low shout from him.

"Heaven-Earth Reversed Wheels, turn!"

As if responding, the gold and black millstones suspended in the void began to rotate with a thunderous rumble.

A tremendous suction force instantly enveloped a radius of a hundred li.

What vast blood qi, what abundant astral qi, was needed to achieve such a heaven-shading scale!

Caught within it, the five experts' bodies immediately became as if mired in a swamp, moving with extreme slowness.

And what terrified them most was that their most prized Leakless Golden Body showed signs of collapse under the reversal of this great mill.

The astral qi they could command as easily as their own limbs became a chaotic mess.

In an instant, each of them felt their souls flee in terror.

"Senior, spare my life!"

"I was merely incited by a petty person. Please let me go."

"Luo Chen, we have no past grievances or recent enmity. Why must you exterminate us completely?"

"Ah, hold on!"

"No..."

Accompanied by a miserable scream, a purple light was immediately sucked into the millstone. Then came a rain of blood, and a foul stench spread across the wilderness.

The Purple Gold City Lord had fallen!

"You tell me—should I spare them?"

Xiang Fei, staring blankly at this scene, heard a voice like a demon's whisper beside her ear.

Her lips trembling, she looked at the young man whose lips held a smile but whose eyes gleamed with cold light. For a moment, she did not know how to answer.

She knew that if she answered wrong, her fate would likely be the same as the others.

She did not want to die yet!

Swallowing her saliva, Xiang Fei hurriedly knelt on the ground.

"Senior, spare my life!"

"Spare your life? That means not sparing them, then."

Luo Chen gave a light laugh, then suddenly closed his hands together and began to rotate them slowly.

In the distance, the four who had already lost one supporter could no longer hold on against Luo Chen's increased astral qi output.

One by one, their figures turned into streaks of light, sucked into the massive millstone.

Under the continuous rotation, they let out screams of agony.

Then came another rain of blood.

The heavenly wind swept past, carrying away the thick smell of blood.

Luo Chen released his hands, his calm gaze falling on the woman kneeling before him.

"Alright, it's quiet now. Next, I ask and you answer. If I find you lying..."

"Senior, please ask. Xiang Fei will tell everything she knows without reservation!"

"Very good! Tell me, what is the Return to Ruins?"

Xiang Fei answered without hesitation. "The Return to Ruins is the Return to Ruins. Not only the Great Destruction Realm, but even the Corpse Remnant Immortal Domain and the Sea of Stars are merely parts of the Return to Ruins."

Hearing this answer, Luo Chen was momentarily stunned.

The seventy-two cities of the Great Destruction Realm were not the entirety of this world?

Where exactly were the so-called Corpse Remnant Immortal Domain and the Sea of Stars?

Clearly, the answers behind these questions might point directly to the truth of this world.

Luo Chen was silent for a long moment, then slowly said, "Get up. Let's talk as we walk."

Xiang Fei dared not delay. Even though her entire body ached from her bones, she forced herself to stand and followed behind Luo Chen as they walked out of the Returning Wind Mountain Range.

The previous battlefield was filled by violent heavenly winds.

Given the unique terrain of this place, it was likely that in a few years, new Unforgivable Heavenly Winds would accumulate here again.

...

On the wilderness.

Two figures walked one behind the other, and from time to time, the sound of questions and answers could be heard.

The man asked questions, concise and to the point, often striking at the core.

The woman answered, meticulously detailed, and when faced with questions she could not answer, she would give corresponding speculations as best she could.

"The Three Great Calamities do not only appear in the Great Destruction Realm. They are spread throughout every part of the Return to Ruins. Especially when the once-in-ten-thousand-years, epoch-turning Destruction Fog sweeps in, everything in the world returns to nothingness. Over time, this world came to be called the Return to Ruins."

"If that's the case, why are the seventy-two cities called the Great Destruction Realm?"

"It's only because at the core of Carefree City, there is a spatial vortex discovered by someone, called the Destruction Gate. That is the true origin of the name 'Great Destruction Realm.'"

"The Destruction Gate?"

"Yes. That vortex is called the Destruction Gate. Any being daring to venture deep into it will be ground to nothing."

When she mentioned the word "ground," the woman's voice trembled slightly, as if she recalled the move Luo Chen had used earlier.

Suppressing the fear in her heart, the woman poured out everything she knew as completely as possible.

"But the danger of the Destruction Gate is currently controllable, as long as one does not cross the Destruction Nine Rings."

"What are the Destruction Nine Rings?"

"That is a division made by the current strongest expert of the Great Destruction Realm, the Carefree City Lord, based on the outward expansion range of the spatial vortex. He divided it into nine relatively safe ring-shaped zones, which we call the Destruction Nine Rings. Within the nine-ring zone, it is filled with chaotic and mixed cosmic energy. Martial artists can use their blood qi furnaces to absorb energy and cultivate there. The deeper one can penetrate the nine rings, the higher their strength. It is said that in the inner three rings, only human immortal-level martial artists can stay for long. There is also a rumor that has circulated in Carefree City for a long time..."

At this point, a glimmer of hope appeared in Xiang Fei's eyes.

She spoke with excitement, "It is said that if someone can cross the nine rings and break through the Destruction Gate, they might be able to leave the Return to Ruins, leave this world destined for destruction!"

Luo Chen, walking ahead, stopped in his tracks.

He took a deep breath and asked, "Is this rumor true?"

Xiang Fei gave a bitter smile. "The reason it's a rumor is precisely that no one can verify its truth. But at least, martial artists at our Leakless Realm dare not attempt the Destruction Gate, which can grind everything to dust."

"Can human immortals do it?"

"Probably..."

"Mm?"

A nasal sound carrying questioning and dissatisfaction made Xiang Fei weigh her words carefully.

Too much subjective speculation would affect Luo Chen's judgment.

Xiang Fei hesitated and shook her head. "I'm afraid not either. I don't know about the old monsters in the inner three rings, but the Carefree City Lord is definitely a solid human immortal-level martial artist, possibly even a higher Destruction Realm existence. Even he remains stranded in the Return to Ruins, which shows that crossing the Destruction Gate is not an easy matter."

To prevent Luo Chen from getting angry, Xiang Fei hurriedly continued, "In fact, in many people's view, the people of this world simply cannot leave the Return to Ruins. That's why those top experts desperately seek various methods to leave. The method of breaking through the Destruction Gate is only one of them."

This answer caused the hope that had just risen in Luo Chen's heart to fade somewhat, and his mood rarely sank.

After a long while.

He changed the question.

"You've encountered high-level cultivators?"

The sentence was phrased as a question, but the tone was affirmative.

Xiang Fei nodded, then looked at Luo Chen with curiosity.

"How does Senior know?"

"Earlier, your flute sound technique for attacking the soul had similarities to the soul arts used by cultivators. The only difference is that one uses astral qi to drive it, forming special sound waves to shake the enemy's soul. The other uses divine sense to drive it directly."

Luo Chen said this.

With his attainments in the soul, he could naturally see through the essence of Xiang Fei's technique at a glance.

This was also the main reason he chose to spare Xiang Fei among the six—he wanted to learn about the situation of cultivators in Carefree City through her.

As for the intelligence on the "Return to Ruins," that was an unexpected bonus.

Xiang Fei nodded and said with emotion, "I have indeed encountered cultivators. It was thirty years ago when I entered Carefree City, by chance. I traded a batch of high-quality Mi Immortal Smoke with one of them for this modified sound wave technique. I intended it as my trump card, but I ended up showing off my skill before an expert."

It was indeed showing off one's skill before an expert.

Not to mention Xiang Fei's modified sound wave technique—even orthodox cultivators, few could shake Luo Chen's soul.

He simply had Xiang Fei hand over the corresponding method, then headed toward Mocheng City.

Xiang Fei could not help but ask proactively, "Does Senior not care why we attacked you?"

Luo Chen countered, "Didn't you say it earlier? You came for my primordial spirit."

At this point, Luo Chen was actually somewhat curious.

"Why do you believe that a cultivator's primordial spirit can be useful to you martial artists?"

"According to the cultivators in Carefree City, the primordial spirit is the combination of a cultivator's vast mana and soul. Even in the Return to Ruins, where a cultivator's mana is suppressed to the extreme, it is still the purest energy. For us martial artists, it must be a great tonic."

Luo Chen gave a derisive laugh. If the West Ridge City Lord and the others had known that his primordial spirit had long since collapsed and his mana was completely gone, they would surely cry out in injustice even in the underworld.

Xiang Fei's words continued.

"Moreover, I said earlier—to leave the Return to Ruins, many top existences have experimented with countless methods. One of them is devouring a cultivator's primordial spirit. Especially the legendary Great Vehicle stage cultivators who have cultivated a single law's true intent to great perfection. Their primordial spirits are almost equivalent to a complete law's true intent. If one could devour and absorb it, perhaps they could leave this world of chaotic Dao and broken laws. It is said that in history, someone actually did this. A group of supreme existences within the Return to Ruins joined forces to besiege a Great Heavenly Venerable who had mistakenly entered this realm!"

Suddenly, Luo Chen stopped in his tracks, his expression undergoing a drastic change.

A Void Refinement cultivator was called a True Lord, a Body Integration cultivator was called a Great Venerable.

And above the Body Integration stage, the Great Vehicle stage cultivator was honored as a Heavenly Venerable.

That was a powerful being who had comprehended a complete law's true intent and could wield the laws of heaven.

The people of the Return to Ruins had dared to besiege a Great Heavenly Venerable!

(End of this chapter)
