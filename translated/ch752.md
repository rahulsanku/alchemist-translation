# Chapter 752: The Origin of the Illusion Core

Fu Qinglan stared blankly at Luo Chen's resolute profile, completely unable to react.

This was the first time she had seen such a domineering Luo Chen.

She had also never expected that, just after entering the grotto-heaven, he would suddenly turn hostile here, of all places, with a friend who was on very good terms with her.

At first, she was somewhat angry.

How could he turn guest into host and violate her wishes?

But amid those sharp questions, she could not help asking herself in return.

'Should I yield?'

Luo Chen knew nothing of her current mental activity.

He only knew that since he had accepted another's trust, he had to be loyal to that task!

In the transaction with Fu Chaosheng itself, he had already gained a great advantage, and every one of those conditions had leaned in his favor.

At that time, Luo Chen had already thought that he could not handle this matter perfunctorily.

Afterward, Fu Chaosheng had given him pills, then Withered-Source Fire Marrow. Even though he had not managed to secure Luo Chen's official status as a formal Star Gate guest elder, he had already done everything to the utmost.

Since that was so, Luo Chen had to put all his strength into this act of Dao protection!

Within Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, fourth-tier illusion beasts had uncertain movements and hid extremely deeply. Every illusion core was extremely difficult to hunt.

Moreover, Luo Chen did not particularly want to waste too much time inside this grotto-heaven. The earlier he collected enough illusion cores and helped Fu Qinglan complete her cultivation, the earlier he could leave.

For that reason, it was even more impossible for him to give up this core that had been delivered right before him!

Luo Chen also did not care what Fu Qinglan thought of him. Even after they left, her grandfather, Fu Chaosheng, would probably support Luo Chen's actions.

Therefore, the style Luo Chen would use during the following journey through the grotto-heaven was already decided.

It would be different from his usual style of keeping well water and river water separate, of not troubling others when matters did not concern him. Instead, he would hunt illusion beasts and contend for illusion cores as much as possible, even if that meant clashing with others.

It would begin from this very moment!

Behind him, the scarlet chains transformed from the Primordial Unity Cauldron locked the illusion core and flew toward Luo Chen.

His right hand held a seal, already poised to strike.

His left hand also began to flex and extend its five fingers, forming one dazzling hand seal after another.

Vast magic power began surging out from within his body.

Opposite him!

Seeing this scene, especially Fu Qinglan's silent appearance, Lin Jingtang laughed from extreme anger.

"Good! Good! Good!"

"Since you want battle, then I, Lin, do not fear it!"

Before his voice had even fallen, he saw Luo Chen across from him cross both hands, merge two seals into one, and slowly push out with his right hand.

An azure palm print flew toward him with utter calm as Luo Chen pushed out his right hand.

"You dare show off such a petty art?"

Lin Jingtang sneered, swept his broad sleeve, and was about to scatter that handprint.

But an unexpected scene appeared.

The handprint remained steady and firm. Not only was it not dispersed, but as it flew swiftly toward him, it swelled and grew with mad speed.

Lin Jingtang frowned slightly, already forming a plan in his mind.

First, he would defend against this move, then launch a counterattack!

Without thinking, the great golden bell abruptly flew up and covered him beneath it.

The next moment, the azure palm print collided with the great golden bell.

Clang!

A heavy bell tone rang out.

The corner of Lin Jingtang's mouth lifted slightly. His counterattack would begin from this moment.

This bell was called the Fright Bell, fright and bewilderment.

Aside from its powerful defensive ability, the bell tone it released could cause enemies to fall under its effect without realizing it. Losing control of the mind was still a small matter; even the divine soul might possibly be shaken apart.

With this method alone, even though he was not a Tianyuan Dao Sect disciple, he was still famous within the Dao Sect.

"The family head has treated me well. How could I disappoint him? Just as Wilderness Wanderer Luo Chen said, inside Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, in all matters concerning Xiaofan, I cannot retreat!"

As thoughts flashed through his mind, he reached to draw the long sword on his back.

But!

"No!"

Lin Jingtang's expression changed drastically. He only felt that the azure great handprint blocked by the Fright Bell showed no sign of dispersing at all. Instead, its strength accelerated, becoming heavier and heavier.

His body began to fail under that massive force and was forced backward.

Through the hazy, boiling spiritual qi, he faintly saw that cold-faced middle-aged man. The other party seemed unaffected by the Fright Bell's tone?

Rumble!

The enormous azure handprint swept up the surrounding spiritual qi of heaven and earth and began wildly increasing its force.

It pressed the person covered by the golden bell shadow back again and again.

Ten zhang, a hundred zhang, a thousand zhang... ten li, a hundred li...

Everywhere it passed, wind tore apart clouds, the earth shook, and mountains, rivers, and streams were left in ruin.

Vast quantities of spiritual qi from heaven and earth poured continuously into that azure great handprint like young swallows returning to the nest, stirring up a spiritual qi storm in the void.

The one being attacked, however, was like a shooting star, pushed and hurled several hundred li away until he became a tiny black dot invisible to the naked eye.

"Its force has not weakened, and it keeps flowing without end... How can this be!"

Lin Jingtang's eyes nearly split with rage. He no longer had time to hold anything back.

Both palms struck out together, slamming against the inside of the golden bell.

One palm, two palms, three palms...

Countless afterimages gathered together, finally becoming one palm.

"Break!"

With a fierce shout, the Fright Bell abruptly trembled.

Clang!

A terrifying bell peal rang out instantly, and golden light spread outward like waves.

The azure great handprint pressing down on the golden bell dispersed inch by inch.

At last, the wind stilled and the clouds rested. The spiritual qi storm lost its core and scattered blankly across the earth.

A figure walked out from within it, his hair disheveled and his face pale.

He lowered his head and looked at the shrunken golden bell in his palm. On it were two completely different palm prints.

One inside, one outside.

The palm print outside was precisely the one Luo Chen had caused.

Under those circumstances, he had already realized that so long as he did not die and Luo Chen did not die, that palm would continuously accumulate the spiritual qi of heaven and earth and constantly grow stronger.

He could only drive all his magic power and unleash his strongest strike, shattering it before it reached its peak.

But the price was that he himself, positioned inside the golden bell, had suffered an enormous backlash.

If not for the golden bell being his life-bound true artifact and possessing the function of automatically protecting its master, his fleshly body and divine soul would probably both have been badly injured.

Suddenly.

He abruptly raised his head and roared:

"Wilderness Wanderer!"

Towering fury transformed into rumbling sound waves that surged toward the path he had come from.

However, a figure blocked him from the front.

"Xiaofan?"

Lin Bufan shook his head at him. "We have only just entered the grotto-heaven. There are still many opportunities. There is no need to act on impulse."

"But..."

"Wilderness Wanderer is very strong! Although this time he seized the first-move advantage and caught you unprepared, I can sense that his strength is like an abyssal sea, and his methods are not limited to that one palm. If you fight him to the death, even if you win, it will at most be a tragic victory. In that case, how will we still possess sufficient strength for the later struggle over illusion cores?"

Lin Bufan spoke slowly, choosing his words as much as possible to leave Lin Jingtang enough face.

In truth, while watching the battle earlier, he had sensed that Luo Chen was even more formidable.

Especially when Luo Chen activated that spell, there had been another suppressed power within his body.

Once that power broke its seal, Lin Jingtang would probably have no strength to resist at all and could at most rely on the true artifact Fright Bell to preserve his life.

Oh!

This still had to take into account whether the other party possessed a true artifact.

If he had a true artifact of the same tier, Lin Jingtang's outcome would probably...

Some of the violence on Lin Jingtang's face faded, but he was still deeply indignant.

"That illusion core was discovered by us first, and I spent enormous effort to force it out."

Lin Bufan shook his head. "It is only one illusion core. With my perception ability, finding the next fourth-tier illusion beast will not be difficult. With this experience, next time we only need to prepare in advance to collect the illusion core. As for that one, take it as the last gift I... give to Sister Qinglan."

The last gift?

Lin Jingtang raised an eyebrow.

Only, before leaving, he still looked hatefully in Luo Chen's direction.

It was obvious that he had taken the humiliation of this palm to heart.

...

Huff...

A fierce wind struck his face, and Luo Chen slowly withdrew his right hand, his face filled with satisfaction.

This was the first time since forming his Nascent Soul that he had used the Azure Yang Great Handprint at full strength, and the effect could only be called a pleasant surprise.

The Azure Yang Great Handprint had originated from the Great Handprint of Primordial Qi.

That Great Handprint of Primordial Qi had originally been a third-tier spell, with ordinary power that was not especially outstanding.

After he pursued the utmost and changed it to the fire attribute, its killing power had stood above ordinary third-tier spells.

Especially after cultivating it to grand completion, it had become enough to rival ordinary fourth-tier spells.

Originally, after reaching this stage, this art's potential had already been exhausted. For Luo Chen after advancing to the Sage realm, its assistance was also fairly ordinary.

However, after sensing the special nature of a Sage cultivator's control over the spiritual qi of heaven and earth, he had merged the Spirit-Enslaving Seal into it as well.

In this way, the Azure Yang Great Handprint had evolved once more.

When driven at full strength, if an enemy was careless and did not disperse it immediately, it would become stronger and stronger until the danger was like stacked eggs beneath the crushing weight of Mount Tai!

"A pity that Lin Jingtang retreated on his own. Otherwise, I still wanted to use him to test some other methods."

Luo Chen was rather regretful.

If Fu Qinglan beside him knew his thoughts, she would probably be stunned speechless.

Using a same-realm expert to test moves?

What sort of formidable ability did one need to possess such calm confidence!

Yet Luo Chen simply had that confidence.

All along, his opponents had often been older than him, higher in realm than him, and frequently crossed one major realm or several minor realms above him.

In this Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven grand competition, although there were many enemies, Luo Chen was not worried at all.

Because these Dao protectors were all limited by the rule that they could not have advanced to Sage more than a hundred years ago.

In other words, every Dao protector's realm was similar to Luo Chen's.

They were either first-level Sages or, at most, second-level Sages. Between them, there was almost no gap in realm.

And in battle against those of the same level, Luo Chen feared nothing!

If not for taking Fu Qinglan's face into consideration, he would have chased after Lin Jingtang just now and used other methods, with a ninety-percent certainty of severely injuring him!

With the Primordial Unity Cauldron assisting him, the other party's Nascent Soul would not even be able to run.

"Knowing difficulty and retreating. At least you know your place!"

Luo Chen snorted softly, then glanced to the side.

"Let us go!"

Fu Qinglan stared at him blankly, for a moment not even reacting to what he had said.

Only when she saw Luo Chen's brows knit did she hurriedly ask, "What?"

Luo Chen's frown deepened.

"Go. The commotion here was too great and will draw others over. We should not linger."

"Oh, oh."

Fu Qinglan hurriedly followed Luo Chen's steps. Only, as she looked at his back, the shock in her heart still could not calm for a long time.

One move!

With just one move, he had blasted the famous Lin Jingtang several hundred li away!

That sort of commotion shocked her far more than when he had quietly defeated Qi Xiongcheng back then.

She could not help asking herself: would she also be able to grasp such power later?

Were those early-stage Sage senior brothers within the sect this formidable?

Searching through her memories, it seemed that only a few could just barely compare with Luo Chen's bearing from that moment.

...

Mountains lay hidden one behind another, and ancient trees towered into the sky.

As sword light swept forth, a simple cave abode took shape.

Luo Chen stopped the motion of his hand in satisfaction.

"Here will do. Inside Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, one cannot see any place with especially condensed spirit veins. At least the scenery here is fairly lovely."

Fu Qinglan nodded.

More than lovely. Looking down from the rock jutting out from the mountainside, vast swaths of ancient trees formed a boundless primeval forest.

In the distance, mountains of different styles rose from the earth, thrusting into the firmament like sharp swords.

There were also stars gleaming one by one in the night sky.

Starlight spilled down, illuminating this place until it seemed dreamlike and illusory.

Only...

She turned back and glanced. The newly opened cave abode was not large, barely enough to accommodate one person in cultivation.

Luo Chen's words happened to explain her doubt.

"You may freely refine the illusion core and cultivate inside. I will guard you from outside."

Immediately, Luo Chen changed the subject.

"First go regulate your breath for a while. I will study that illusion core and give it to you tomorrow morning."

"Mm. I will trouble Dao Brother, then."

Fu Qinglan walked inside.

After she left, Luo Chen's mind stirred, and a pale, round pearl appeared in his hand.

Unlike the previous third-tier illusion core that had resembled a jujube pit, this fourth-tier illusion core had no rough patterns on its surface and appeared incomparably smooth.

But the condensed cloud qi was enough to show this core's uniqueness.

"This cloud qi is Mirage Dragon power?"

Luo Chen examined it for a while and found that he had no idea where to begin.

He knew that the Tianyuan Dao Sect's Sage true disciples who entered the grotto-heaven this time had unique methods for refining Mirage Dragon power, and they would not pass them to Dao protectors like them.

But could one comprehend the true intent of laws and open the path to Ascendant merely by relying on this cloud qi?

Luo Chen was full of puzzlement.

He released his divine sense and began eroding the illusion core, but under the obstruction of that condensed Mirage Dragon power, he was completely unable to penetrate very deeply.

Luo Chen frowned, then suddenly had a thought.

Spiritual light shone brilliantly in both his eyes. Not only that, source power poured into his eye sockets, and crimson light flickered.

From surface to interior, he vaguely perceived certain lively things.

"Hm?"

Amid Luo Chen's surprise, another white pill appeared in his hand.

It was precisely the Heavenly Secret Pill that the Alchemy Saint had given him before ascending.

The instant the Heavenly Secret Pill appeared, the sealed fourth-tier illusion core in his hand suddenly grew restless, as if it were about to pounce toward the Heavenly Secret Pill.

Luo Chen pressed his lips together.

"I did not see wrongly after all. Inside this illusion core wrapped in large amounts of Mirage Dragon power, there is a trace of spiritual impulse! It comes from the same source as the spiritual impulse that served as the main material for the Heavenly Secret Pill refined by the Alchemy Saint!"

"Could it be that those spiritual impulses intercepted by the Tianyuan Sect Master at the time were thrown into Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven?"

"Fu Qinglan and the others, these true disciples of the Dao Sect, are not absorbing Mirage Dragon power at all. They want to absorb these spiritual impulses and use them to comprehend the true intent of laws!"

"Spiritual impulse... world consciousness... true intent of laws..."

His gaze moved constantly between the white illusion core and the white Heavenly Secret Pill, and Luo Chen's eyes kept turning as certain plans faintly formed in his mind.

*(End of Chapter)*
