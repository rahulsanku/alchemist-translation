# Chapter 1082: Battle in the Grotto-Heaven, Wilderness Wanderer Reappears

Within the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven.

Thirty-six Sage-level cultivators were scattered about, each controlling a portion of the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard.

Though they appeared scattered, all their power was eerily fused together at this moment.

This eeriness lay in the aged figure seated high on the central platform.

Chess Saint Ke Lianshan!

Using a formation as a guide, he drew upon the power of all present, channeling it into himself, manipulating the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard to absorb the spiritual opportunities from the four continents that had split off from Central Continent. This included the Western Continent, which had long ago been swallowed and sunk into the ocean by the Yellow Spring Sea. Even now, at the underwater ruins, vast amounts of spiritual opportunity were spreading outward, then being absorbed.

As the immense spiritual opportunity converged, the wilderness transformed—wind and clouds shifted, spiritual energy surged like tides.

This spectacle was nearly comparable to the ascension of Alchemy Saint Chu Yan back in the day.

Within the grotto-heaven, they were not the only ones present. There were also a group of core Adept disciples who had been arranged to undergo bitter cultivation here.

With the day of departure approaching, these core Adept disciples were now gathering closer, observing the grand undertaking of their sect's elders from up close.

Among them were those from learned families with broad knowledge, who vaguely guessed what was happening after witnessing this scene.

Especially Ke Lianshan at the very center!

His aura was continuously rising, an immense soul pressure spreading outward, attempting to connect with heaven and earth.

"Senior Uncle Ke is using this opportunity to break through to the Ascendant realm!"

With just one glance, someone had discerned the truth.

Then, before they could even express their astonishment, they were drawn to the young man slowly walking along the edge of the wilderness.

"Is that Senior Brother Zhou Fuyou?"

"Eh, his realm..."

Someone noticed the fluctuations of magical power Zhou Fuyou was currently revealing, and each was shaken.

A hundred years in the grotto-heaven—everyone's strength had improved, but to form a Nascent Soul ahead of time like Zhou Fuyou was exceedingly rare.

As the top ten of this sixty-year grand competition, the other disciples were not weak. They were already the favored children of heaven in this generation of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

In time, they would surely become the outstanding figures of the younger generation, shouldering the great responsibility of revitalizing the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

But compared to the prodigiously gifted Zhou Fuyou, they still appeared inferior.

Gu Muyu stroked the ring on her finger, within which was a portion of ten-thousand-year spiritual herbs she had picked in advance. Her journey here could already be considered complete. Once she left, she would surely receive rewards from her family.

It was just a pity she hadn't had much free time for cultivation; otherwise, she might have formed her Nascent Soul early like Zhou Fuyou.

Her gaze falling on Zhou Fuyou, Gu Muyu's eyes held a hint of envy, along with some confusion.

"What is he doing?"

Zhou Fuyou's steps suddenly stopped at a certain place.

This was the farthest point from the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard, yet it was the spot from which one could observe the entire wilderness most completely.

Gazing ahead, Zhou Fuyou's eyes revealed a thoughtful expression.

"Using the power of a spirit treasure, gathering the cultivation of thirty-six Sages into one body, allowing Ke Lianshan to briefly possess power comparable to an Ascendant. Thus, he can manipulate the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard to absorb the spiritual opportunity from the four continents of Central Continent."

"Similarly, in reverse, such vast spiritual opportunity not only supplies the God-Descending Formation outside but also helps Ke Lianshan climb further toward the Ascendant realm!"

"Not only that—the thirty-six Sages within can also use this to sense Ke Lianshan's insights into breaking through to Ascendant, which will be of immense benefit to their futures."

"No wonder that Senior Uncle Li once said that when the grotto-heaven opened again, there would be another opportunity. So that's it!"

For a cultivator to break through to the Ascendant realm, they must rely on a fifth-rank spiritual vein.

But the fifth-rank spiritual vein was not the key point. The key point was the vast amount of spiritual opportunity naturally accumulated by the fifth-rank spiritual vein. By relying on this spiritual opportunity, a Sage could tightly connect their soul with heaven and earth, roam the ocean of origin qi, and achieve a primordial spirit during that period. At the same time, after the spiritual opportunity was consumed, the Sage would naturally detach from that state, avoiding prolonged close connection that could lead to being assimilated by heaven and earth.

The opportunity Senior Uncle Li spoke of was actually the vast spiritual opportunity drawn in by the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard.

If he were at the Sage stage at that time, by quietly comprehending, he might be able to gain insight into a law true intent unique to himself.

"But unfortunately, it's no longer needed."

Zhou Fuyou sighed softly and looked up.

In the sky, a massive vortex had appeared.

It was a gateway, a gateway to the outside world.

At this moment, Ke Lianshan was connecting with the outside world through this gateway, and also channeling vast amounts of spiritual opportunity into the God-Descending Formation through it.

To stop the God-Descending Formation from operating, one only needed to interrupt Ke Lianshan's breakthrough.

Everyone present belonged to the same sect, so naturally, they would not do something that would pain their own and delight their enemies—including Zhou Fuyou from before.

But now...

Zhou Fuyou took a slight breath, curled his fingers slightly, and following the understanding deep in his memory, strands of cyan gas gathered at his fingertips.

As they gathered, Zhou Fuyou's face turned slightly pale, as if this body could not bear this technique.

But this technique was ultimately taking shape.

When no one was aware, the surrounding vegetation turned grayish-white, the rocks and soil on the ground crumbled into sand, and even the distant mountains began to loosen.

Their vitality was being drained.

No one noticed this. All the Sages were immersed in the process of sensing Ke Lianshan's breakthrough to Ascendant.

Even Gu Muyu, who was watching Zhou Fuyou's movements, did not understand what he was doing.

Only one person!

Only Chess Saint Ke Lianshan, who was at this moment closely connected with heaven and earth, sensed something unusual.

His eyelids twitched, and his eyes opened. Subconsciously, he looked far away at that chess piece beyond the board—that effeminate young man.

The other seemed to sense this and smiled slightly at him.

Then he pointed out a finger.

In the sky, wind and clouds changed abruptly!

The origin qi of the grotto-heaven surged. Within the thick clouds, a massive finger slowly extended. Amid the exclamations of nine core Adept disciples, it pointed through the air toward Ke Lianshan.

He was unmoved, powerless, unable to resist. He could only watch helplessly as this finger descended.

Vaguely, Ke Lianshan seemed to hear two words.

"Withering... Bloom!"

The finger descended. Ke Lianshan's pupils widened. He only felt the continuously rising aura on his body recede like a tide.

A withering intent, turning from green to white, coiled around him, making him appear increasingly aged.

If it were only this, it would be bearable.

Ke Lianshan raised his head with difficulty, looking at the origin qi vortex above that connected to the outside. It was now trembling violently, unable to channel vast amounts of spiritual opportunity outward.

The God-Descending Formation was interrupted because of this!

It didn't matter that he had failed to reach Ascendant. But the failure of the God-Descending Formation affected the descent of the True Lord from the Mystic Realm—the plan of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect to take revenge and rise again.

All of this was thanks to that effeminate young man!

"Pfft!"

A mouthful of blood was spat out. The wilderness trembled, and all the Sages were jolted awake.

They were lost and dazed, as if waking from a dream.

Then, following Ke Lianshan's gaze, they all looked toward the young man in the distance.

Around him, nine figures had realized something and simultaneously pounced toward the young man.

Then!

Before the nine figures could even get close, they crashed to the ground, pressed down by an overwhelmingly powerful Sage domain.

There was no trace of pity in the man's eyes. With a thought, the immense pressure instantly detonated.

Bang! Bang! Bang!

Blossoms of blood bloomed, forming the most sorrowfully beautiful scene.

The thirty-six Sages were all glaring with fury!

"Go kill him!"

Ke Lianshan said in a low voice, his tone filled with endless anger and fear.

No one hesitated. The Sages moved like thunderbolts, swift and fierce.

The young man did not retreat but advanced. His whole body shook, and streams of black qi shot into the sky, forming a vast, smoke-like canopy that covered everyone.

Qi Sea Floating Heaven, Myriad Manifestations Dense and Majestic!

A graceful figure ignored the battle group over there, flew to the high platform, and half-knelt beside Ke Lianshan, looking at him with concern as he swayed unsteadily.

"Senior Brother, how are you?"

Ke Lianshan had aged to the extreme. The wrinkles on his face were like the bark of an ancient tree, mottled and uneven.

He struggled to sit upright. "Junior Sister Mingyan, this old man has failed to break through to Ascendant. This remnant body will soon decay."

Mingyan covered her mouth, her beautiful face losing color. She could hardly believe it.

Earlier, she had been closely connected with Ke Lianshan through the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard along with the others. Naturally, she could feel how smoothly this sect's Chess Saint senior brother was breaking through to the Ascendant realm.

Senior Brother Ke's foundation was so profound that he easily passed every obstacle he might encounter when breaking through to the Ascendant realm.

Even the legendary heart demon invasion was suppressed with a backhand.

But who could have imagined that suddenly, he would end up like this?

Ke Lianshan's body trembled as he looked toward the vortex leading to the outside world. Despair mingled with a trace of hope.

Then, that hope grew larger and larger, and the despair in his eyes became brighter and brighter.

"No, it hasn't failed yet!"

"The True Lord wants to forcibly enter this realm. He is still persisting!"

"Although this old man has no hope of breaking through to Ascendant, this remnant body still remains. I must burn it for a while, to do one last bit of effort for the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!"

Muttering to himself, Ke Lianshan forcibly steadied his mind.

"Mingyan, you are the one with the highest realm and the strongest strength among those present. Moreover, you have comprehended a law true intent. You must kill Zhou Fuyou. Do not let him interfere with me again."

Before he finished speaking, as if experiencing a final burst of vitality, the aura on his body began to surge again.

"Senior Brother, what are you going to do?" Mingyan asked, her face full of shock.

"For the True Lord to descend, there must always be a beacon as a guide. This old man will burn his own magical power, channel all the spiritual opportunity here into the God-Descending Formation, and open a passage for him!" Ke Lianshan's expression was resolute. He pointed a finger at his chest and abdomen.

Mingyan's expression was terrified. She seemed to hear the sound of something breaking.

It was the Nascent Soul!

It was Senior Brother Ke Lianshan's Nascent Soul, cultivated for a thousand years—the foundation of the Great Dao that had nearly achieved the Ascendant realm!

Senior Brother had completely given up on himself, burning himself, just to complete what had not yet been accomplished.

"Go. Do not let Zhou Fuyou survive!"

Mingyan hesitated no longer. She bit her lip tightly, stood up, bowed deeply to Ke Lianshan, then turned around, her face cold as she stared at the young man in the distance.

"Traitor!"

...

"Fuyou, do you know what you're doing?"

"Attacking sect elders, slaughtering fellow disciples—have you gone mad?"

Senior Uncle Li's mind was in turmoil. He could hardly believe what had just happened.

He had a good relationship with Fu Qinglan and often visited.

Naturally, he extended that goodwill to Zhou Fuyou.

Especially since Zhou Fuyou had performed excellently in the sixty-year grand competition, with talent and aptitude unprecedented—even Shi Ju seemed inferior by comparison.

It could be said that Zhou Fuyou was not only a junior he favored but also the hope for the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's revival in the future.

Thus, in daily life, he often gave Zhou Fuyou guidance, and when entering the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, he frequently gave instructions.

But everything that had just happened made him almost think he was in a dream.

Until!

"Watch out!"

A warning cry came from a fellow disciple behind him.

A fierce wind rushed toward him from ahead.

He instinctively released his defensive magic treasure.

But the black qi washed over, heavy as a thousand jun, like a battering ram, slamming heavily against the defensive treasure.

Dong!

The treasure shattered instantly.

Not only that, the black qi spread and enveloped him. Its terrifying corrosive power dissolved his protective qi shield, his dharma robe, and even his dharma body into nothingness.

Senior Uncle Li's gaze was vacant as he watched Zhou Fuyou brush past him.

He murmured, "Why?"

Zhou Fuyou swept his sleeve wide, and the black canopy swallowed his remnant body.

He gave no answer, only continuing to charge forward. Facing the Sages coming at him one after another, and the powerful attacks, Zhou Fuyou's expression was cold.

Among this batch of Sages, aside from a few, most were obscure and unknown.

But being obscure did not mean they were useless.

On the contrary!

These people were the backbone of the various great families within the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!

Their realms were neither high nor low, their ages neither old nor young. In their respective families, they were all those who cultivated diligently in seclusion.

Most importantly, they were native-born sect disciples, possessing extreme loyalty to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!

Therefore, in this "True Lord Descends" plan, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect had selected these most loyal members.

This was both to ensure the smooth progress of the plan and to give them some experiential insights through Ke Lianshan's ascension to Ascendant.

In time, after these thirty-six Sages left, their realms would surely advance by leaps and bounds, filling the void left in the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect after the great calamity in Central Continent back then.

It was just a pity that they would all be buried here today.

Zhou Fuyou let out a low shout. Qi Sea Floating Heaven enveloped all the attacks.

Facing one Sage, he curled his finger and pointed.

Within the black canopy, lightning flickered, and a thunderbolt suddenly fell.

Crack!

That figure trembled all over. The moment he was paralyzed, a large amount of black qi swept over, enveloping him. Screams immediately rang out across the wilderness.

All the Dao Sect Sages halted in their tracks.

After overwhelming anger came fear of Zhou Fuyou's methods.

How could this technique of the Zhou family be so powerful and so sinister?

"Don't be afraid!"

One of the more experienced and steady members stepped forward in the crisis to stabilize morale.

"He is only one person, and he just broke through to the Sage stage not long ago. His magical power must be thin. If we join forces, we can surely kill him!"

The Sages' hearts were greatly settled. They first activated their defensive protections, then launched attacks at Zhou Fuyou one after another.

Hearing these words, Zhou Fuyou sneered at the corner of his mouth.

Thin magical power?

They had no idea what they were facing!

Zhou Fuyou slapped his palm on his Purple Mansion position, twisted it, and turned.

"Open!"

As if some shackle had been unlocked, a torrent of power poured out.

Terrifying black qi, clawing and writhing, spread wildly in all directions.

Qi Sea Floating Heaven had originally only covered a few li. At this moment, it expanded to ten li, a hundred li, even a thousand li.

The entire vast wilderness, except for the high platform where Ke Lianshan was, was completely enveloped.

A black river, as if from hell, surged forth.

Zhou Fuyou stood on the Nether River, the black canopy above his head, coldly looking at everyone, saying nothing.

But his hands never stopped moving.

With casual gestures, turbid waves surged, qi currents swept. He was actually fighting everyone at once, single-handedly.

Such arrogant behavior would normally be ridiculed.

However, after that black river appeared, no one would have that thought anymore.

They only had one thought.

"Zhou Fuyou has only just entered the Sage stage. How can he have such abundant magical power, enough to rival even an ordinary late-stage Sage?"

Zhou Fuyou did not answer this question.

Between them, there was only the most brutal and direct combat.

The war outside changed with every moment, but the battle within the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, due to the difference in time flow, was prolonged and drawn out.

When Zhou Fuyou unsealed the Nether Water Spring Eye and used it to enhance his magical power, his magical power could no longer be measured by the standards of an early-stage Sage.

That was a spring eye that had once given birth to a Nether Abyss and had sheltered the Nether Origin Sect for several thousand years.

Its internal power was vast as an ocean!

Especially the water origin within it, which complemented Zhou Fuyou's body perfectly, allowing him to master it completely.

Coupled with the fact that the soul master now occupying this body possessed an extremely rich store of combat experience in his memory, once the battle began, it became a one-sided situation.

Zhou Fuyou roamed the battlefield. Though he was outnumbered, one against thirty-five.

But with the blessing of Qi Sea Floating Heaven, a technique comparable to a divine ability, the battlefield was divided into piece after piece. In reality, it was thirty-five one-on-one battles.

Senior Uncle Li's fall was like this.

The deaths of the Dao Sect Sages that followed were also like this.

As Zhou Fuyou fought with leisurely ease, he still had the energy to sigh with emotion. Before his old friend Zhou Yunshen's great limit had arrived, he had actually been able to create such a highly scalable technique.

The threshold for cultivation was extremely low. An Initiate-stage cultivator could already master it initially.

And its scalability was extremely high. Depending on the cultivator's characteristics, various treasures and materials could be continuously integrated into it, enhancing the technique's power.

Especially when Zhou Fuyou advanced to the Sage stage, he refined the power of tribulation lightning and integrated it in.

At that moment, Qi Sea Floating Heaven finally had the rudiments of a divine ability!

Enemies were broken one by one.

Lives continuously dissipated within the Nether.

Until!

"Break!"

A sharp cry suddenly rang out.

A sword light broke through the darkness, tearing apart the canopy.

Mingyan was fortunate. Because she was concerned about Ke Lianshan's actions, she had lagged behind the others for a while and did not enter the battlefield Zhou Fuyou had demarcated.

But she was also unfortunate.

After expending a great deal of magical power to unleash the divine ability sword strike bestowed by the Dao Sect after the grotto-heaven grand competition ended, what she personally faced was a battlefield full of devastation, wailing everywhere, and the terrifying sight of only one person standing.

Zhou Fuyou looked up coldly at the woman in the distance.

"Mingyan, the pride of the master-disciple lineage, the greatest beneficiary after the Dao Sect's reforms, and also the most likely candidate to succeed the sect master position aside from Ke Lianshan."

Mingyan was startled, meeting those eyes as cold as a frozen pool.

"You are not Zhou Fuyou?"

Then, her jade-like face turned cold.

"No matter who you are, you must die here today!"

The sword light rose again, and origin qi stirred.

The sword strike that had broken through Qi Sea Floating Heaven earlier appeared once more in the world!

A sharp, unparalleled sword light pierced through the wilderness, slashing straight down at Zhou Fuyou.

A terrifying sword intent shook the universe, and even the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard beneath their feet seemed to tremble.

Clang!

The sword fell, the earth split open, and distant mountain peaks were cleaved in two.

However!

A figure leisurely stepped out from within, only a wisp of robe hem fluttering down.

"Pity. Missed by three inches."

Zhou Fuyou's form suddenly moved. Before the woman could react, he took a step.

When he reappeared, he was beside her.

He swept his sleeve!

The woman was sent flying dozens of feet like a torn cloth sack. A large amount of black qi eagerly burrowed through her seven orifices into her five organs and six viscera.

Mingyan struggled to her feet, supporting herself with her sword. While suppressing the corrosive black qi on her body, she stared in terror at Zhou Fuyou's back.

That sword strike could not have missed!

Her divine sense had completely locked onto Zhou Fuyou.

But that sword strike had indeed missed, by three inches, only cutting off a wisp of fluttering robe hem.

This scene was unbelievable, yet it left one dazed and unable to control oneself.

It seemed that deep in memory, there had also been such a scene.

When was it?

In a daze, Mingyan seemed to return to the time of the grotto-heaven grand competition back then.

There was once a person who had used a similar method to evade the killing strike of the Gu family's Wuwang Treasure Mirror.

It was the Wilderness Wanderer!

"Luo Chen, it's you!!!"

An angry voice sounded from behind.

Zhou Fuyou—no, after the return of the remnant soul, it was Luo Chen who had taken control of this body.

He did not look back as he strode forward, his gaze only on the origin qi vortex that had stabilized again.

Though he did not have his Crimson-Eyed Golden Pupils on him, Luo Chen could still sense vast amounts of spiritual opportunity being channeled from that vortex into the God-Descending Formation outside.

And all of this was barely achieved by Ke Lianshan, who had scattered his own cultivation and damaged a thousand years of Dao conduct.

"Chess Saint, why go this far?"

Ke Lianshan's dead gaze projected over, meeting Luo Chen's from afar.

"You can no longer stop this old man."

As he spoke, beams of light shot up from the wilderness, completely enveloping Ke Lianshan's position.

He had activated the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard!

Protected by a complete spirit treasure, with Luo Chen currently controlling Zhou Fuyou's body, he indeed could not stop him.

Perhaps if his true body were here, he could!

But Luo Chen was not just that!

His expression gradually turned cold. His entire person flew toward that vortex, and the aura on his body began to rise uncontrollably.

A destructive aura gradually enveloped the entire Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven.

(End of this chapter)
