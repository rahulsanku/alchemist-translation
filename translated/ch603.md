# Chapter 603: Five Years in the Blink of an Eye — Artifact-Forging Master Qingyang

Time slips away; the years flow past.

Tides rise and fall with ease; not a breath of summer can be held.

In a blink — five years had gone by.

For the cultivation world of the Northern Sea as a whole, five years was barely a heartbeat — the ocean unchanged, the fields unshifted. The so-called great war between the righteous and demonic factions continued as it always had, burning steadily in the background.

But for lower-level cultivators, five years meant that a great many things had changed.

A minor breakthrough in realm. A turning point in the direction of one's life. Even the arrival of a new life into the world.

In Longyuan Immortal City.

A man with eyes that had seen much sat across from a merchant and talked shop.

"Why has the price on this batch of copper essence changed? It's up a full twenty percent from before!"

"So you haven't heard, Fellow Daoist Zhang — Shouyang Mountain has fallen."

"Isn't that the Shouyang Mountain? The one with the highest confirmed copper deposits in the entire Northern Sea?"

"The very same. It was under the Cang Hai Alliance's control — every year, enormous quantities of raw copper were steadily extracted and refined into copper essence. But with Moluo Stream launching a large-scale invasion and Penglai Immortal Sect pressing from the rear, the heavy garrison at Shouyang couldn't hold. Now that there's no stable supply of copper essence, prices have naturally shot up."

The merchant gave a wry smile. "And it's not just copper essence. Iron essence, lead-wood, even cinnabar for talisman-making — all the basic materials have gone up with the war."

Watching the shifting expression on Zhang Jiadi's face, the merchant changed tack.

"Your Qingyang Hall isn't a large operation — why do you need copper essence in such quantities?"

Zhang Jiadi frowned slightly. "That's none of your concern."

After a long moment's deliberation, he made up his mind.

"Supply me three thousand jin of copper essence at your quoted price. Quality must be good — I'll not accept a single inferior piece."

Three thousand jin.

A sizable order. Even given the modest price of raw copper essence, the profit at that volume was far from small.

The merchant agreed cheerfully on the spot.

"Oh — one more thing. Before I came in, I noticed a white horse at your shop entrance. Throw that in as a complimentary gift, would you?"

The merchant was taken aback. "That's a Tier-Two puppet with wave-treading and sky-crossing capabilities. Worth no less than five thousand spirit stones — calling it a gift seems rather —"

But looking at the years of combat-forged presence radiating from Zhang Jiadi, the merchant thought it over.

"Very well. You and your Qingyang Hall have been reliable partners for years. One puppet is nothing."

"That said, Fellow Daoist Zhang — would you consider my earlier proposal once more?"

Zhang Jiadi waved it off without hesitation.

"That matter is closed. Qingyang Hall's current production capacity is limited. Premium-quality magic instruments go through our own shop only — I can't supply them in bulk for resale."

"Have the copper essence delivered to Wind-Wave Pavilion on the shore of Peng Lake at the usual time. I'll have someone there to receive it. As for the little white horse — I'll take it now."

With that, Zhang Jiadi passed through the inner courtyard, walked to the front, and retrieved the white horse.

Walking down the street, his heart was full of thought.

If a man's life could be said to have a handful of encounters that truly changed its course, Zhang Jiadi, tallying them up, could count at most three.

The first: encountering immortal arts, taking up true cultivation, stepping onto the cultivator's path — lifting him out of the vast anonymity of mortal life to join the ranks of those who practiced the Way.

The second: rescuing a young woman in distress and finding in Wen Xiu a life's companion. This fallen daughter of a prominent family — with her breadth of vision and grasp of the world — had pulled him out of the narrow confines of the small island he'd grown up on, brought him to the Myriad Immortals Assembly, broadened his mind, and even helped his cultivation reach late Foundation Establishment stage.

The third: making the acquaintance of Senior Immortal Qingyang.

From the moment he met that man, the small, predictable life he had always been able to see the end of suddenly filled again with possibility.

Not only had his little daughter been safely born — they had established a cultivation family on Cailian Island within Peng Lake. And beyond that, trading on the Senior Immortal's name, he had come to own a shop in Longyuan Immortal City.

This shop, named for the Senior Immortal, was the product of years of accumulated connections.

Its merchandise was mostly resale goods, earning thin margins — never intended to be anything more than a modest supplement to the family income.

But three years ago, Senior Immortal Qingyang had suddenly produced a magic instrument and handed it over for sale.

The instrument was not high-grade — only lower-quality — but its material construction was exceptionally fine. Even among lower-grade instruments, it was a standout.

And that had only been the beginning.

Over the three years that followed, the number of instruments the Senior Immortal entrusted to his care grew steadily. The grades rose. The volumes increased.

Most recently, the Senior Immortal had bestowed upon him a canopy-type magic instrument. Listed as upper-grade, but its defensive capacity was extraordinary — approaching the level of a premium instrument.

The moment it was put out, it became the crown jewel of Qingyang Hall — drawing no small amount of attention. Had it not been for a city administrator putting in a word to have it consigned for the next Longyuan Auction, Zhang Jiadi would have sold it at a high price long ago.

In short: the once-unremarkable Qingyang Hall, now furnished with a succession of outstanding instruments from the Senior Immortal's hand, had a genuine flagship product to offer.

Within vast Longyuan Immortal City, it had slowly built a solid footing. And with it, the shop's other business had grown increasingly brisk as well.

*Revenue keeps climbing. Spirit stones keep flowing in. And with Senior Immortal Qingyang's occasional guidance — I, Zhang Jiadi, just might have a chance at Golden Core one day.*

Full of thought, Zhang Jiadi returned to the small shop in the south of the city.

Before he even stepped through the door, he could see the steady flow of visitors coming and going. One glance, and he broke into a grin.

"The proprietor's back!"

"Fellow Daoist Zhang, looking well!"

"Heard you voluntarily gave up your three-star demon hunter rank? That took some nerve!"

His last assignment had seemed simple but concealed great danger. It was through that mission that he had risen from his longtime two-star rank to three stars.

To willingly relinquish that title seemed, to outsiders, like throwing away something valuable.

But Zhang Jiadi had no regrets.

As long as a demon hunter rank was attached to his name, a great many things were no longer his own to decide — he would be subject to the Assembly's call at any time. And surrendering his three-star rank had also bought him the lease rights to this small shop. The trade was fair.

He greeted the Qi Refinement and Foundation Establishment cultivators, then went upstairs into Qingyang Hall.

Wen Xiu, who had been doing accounts, looked up instinctively.

"Did you get the copper essence Senior Immortal needs?"

"Got it." Zhang Jiadi answered, though his expression soured. "The price went up again. I'm not sure how to explain that to Immortal Tian Xuan."

Wen Xiu — who had rested fully from the birth of their child — had returned to her former self.

Contrary to the common story that bearing a child would set back a cultivator's realm, in her case the stability of their life here — and the quality spirit vein to cultivate in — had carried her from Foundation Establishment Stage Three to Stage Four in one stride, placing her squarely in the middle Foundation Establishment stage.

Years of managing Qingyang Hall had only deepened the composed bearing that had always been natural to her.

She set down her brush, unhurried. "Don't worry about that. Immortal Tian Xuan looks impenetrable to outsiders, but she's genuinely protective of her own. Last time someone tried to raid our caravan, she personally stepped in to resolve it. A small matter of pricing — if you explain the situation honestly, she won't take issue with you."

"Let's hope so."

Zhang Jiadi shrugged.

In front of his wife, and before Immortal Tian Xuan, even he — already at late Foundation Establishment — never quite felt at ease.

Especially with the latter: an overwhelming difference in cultivation, and some indefinable quality about her, made him instinctively reluctant to draw too close.

He pointed downstairs. "Lot of customers today."

Wen Xiu smoothed her hair, stepped to the window, and let a smile settle on her face.

"Most of them are here to see the magic instrument crafted by Senior Immortal Qingyang."

Zhang Jiadi was puzzled. "But that Nine Dragon True Fire Canopy — wasn't it already claimed by the city administration long ago?"

Wen Xiu shook her head. "Even if they can't buy it, people still like to come and look. Besides, the other instruments in the shop are all of excellent quality — hard to come by for the average wandering cultivator."

She paused, a note of wonder in her voice. "To think — Senior Immortal Qingyang is also a master artifact-forger."

Zhang Jiadi was just as amazed. "Indeed. Not only are his instruments of outstanding quality, his output is extraordinarily high — thirty-plus instruments in three years. Among the independent craftsmen I've encountered, only the Moya Cave Master could arguably edge ahead of him."

The Moya Cave Master — the foremost artifact-forger in the Myriad Immortals Assembly.

Not only did the man produce many fine-quality instruments, he had also forged several lower-grade law devices, and even took commissions crafting custom natal law devices.

By any rough outside estimate, the man's annual earnings were enough to make other cultivators green with envy.

"Anyway — now that I've settled things here, I'd better get back to Peng Lake and report in." Zhang Jiadi pulled himself together, casting a concerned glance at Wen Xiu. "Madam, take care of yourself — don't work yourself too hard."

Wen Xiu laughed and waved him off. "It's a little paperwork — I'm a Foundation Establishment cultivator, I won't wear out from this. You go back. Qingqing has been getting wilder lately — make sure she doesn't go causing trouble over at Peng Island."

Qingqing was their daughter.

Four and a half years old now.

Her formal name was Zhang Jingqing — the meaning of which needed no explanation.

They had originally intended to ask Senior Immortal Qingyang to bestow the name, but the Senior Immortal had been in seclusion during the first two years of their time at Peng Lake, only emerging three years later.

So the couple had gone ahead and named the girl themselves.

Simple — but no less expressive of their respect for the Senior Immortal.

"Hehe — I brought her a little gift. She'll be thrilled."

Zhang Jiadi smiled, his expression full of indulgent affection.

After bidding farewell to his wife, he departed in a hurry, flying toward the Fulong Mountain Range and Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake.

Senior Immortal Qingyang's intensive artifact-forging consumed enormous quantities of raw materials. The Zhang family owed him a great debt — running these errands was the least they could do.

When Zhang Jiadi arrived at Wind-Wave Pavilion on the shore of Peng Lake, a Qi Refinement cultivator was already coming forward to meet him.

"Welcome back, family head."

"Mm."

Zhang Jiadi nodded. This cultivator was one of the wandering cultivators he had recruited as a family retainer over the years.

No salary needed — the offer of a spirit vein cultivation residence was more than enough for wandering cultivators who craved one.

His gaze fell on the enormous object resting outside the pavilion and a look of surprise crossed his face.

"Seems we have a guest."

An ornate cloud-carriage, drawn by exotic beasts.

The conveyance had stopped voluntarily outside Peng Lake — a gesture of respect for the lake's master.

The guard cultivator explained at once: "It's the master from Moya Cave."

"The Moya Cave Master?"

Zhang Jiadi was startled, then murmured, "We were just talking about this forging master, and now he's come calling in person. Senior Immortal Qingyang has truly made a name for himself — even here in the Myriad Immortals Assembly, he's established a place."

...

On the central island of Peng Lake, an elder and a younger man sat across from each other.

The smiles that surfaced on both faces made it clear the conversation was going well.

"I went to see that Nine Dragon Canopy of yours in person — truly remarkable. The fineness of the crafting was something I'd never seen before. If Longyuan Immortal City hadn't already claimed it, I'd have bought it myself just to study."

"Cave Master flatters me — it's only an upper-grade magic instrument. Once the Longyuan Auction opens, you can bid freely. A few spirit stones are surely nothing to you."

"The spirit stones for one instrument are no obstacle. But given your production rate, Fellow Daoist Qingyang, the share of the instrument trade you're influencing across the Assembly — that involves considerably more."

"Cave Master worries too much. The Assembly is vast — Foundation Establishment cultivators are as many as fish in a river. We can each build our own livelihood without treading on each other's territory. Isn't that the better outcome?"

"You do have a point there. No one shop can swallow a prize that large. But I'd like to know — do you plan to enter the law device trade in the future?"

"Ha — my crafting skills are far from refined enough. I haven't managed to produce a single lower-grade law device, let alone anything higher. Cave Master is overthinking things."

...

"Master, this Moya Cave Master did not come with good intentions."

By the shore of Peng Island, Tian Xuan's expression was unfavorable.

Luo Chen waved a hand, eyes narrowed as he watched the direction the Moya Cave Master had departed.

"It's nothing. He was likely manipulated by someone. The instrument trade in an assembly this size involves far more than my shop. His visit today was simply to test the waters."

"I played to his assumptions by appearing less capable than I am. To him, I'm a craftsman without an established style — nowhere near the level of a true master. Short-term, I pose no real threat to his position in his view."

"Besides — artifact-forging is only the tip of the iceberg of my capabilities. No one can restrict my main sources of income."

Tian Xuan understood.

The longer she had been in human form, the better she had become at thinking from a cultivator's perspective. She could follow Luo Chen's reasoning in ways that once would have confused her.

Thinking it through, she could see exactly why the Moya Cave Master had left with such a pleased expression.

The master had deliberately appeared weak.

Though — the courtesy shown to the Cave Master also had something to do with the reputation "Demon Lord Qingyang" had built ahead of him. Otherwise, a Golden Core Stage Seven cultivator like the Moya Cave Master would not have been so deferential to a mid-stage Golden Core practitioner.

"Have your injuries healed completely?" Luo Chen asked suddenly.

Tian Xuan looked at him with gratitude and admiration. "All healed, thanks to Master's help. And the Huan Yu Secret Canon — I've already reached the stage of entering the hall and mounting the stairs. My cultivation speed now is very fast."

Luo Chen gave a slight nod. Good. The effort had been worth it.

Tian Xuan was born with a wind spirit root. The Huan Yu Secret Canon had been crafted specifically for her — rapid advancement was only to be expected.

His brow lifted suddenly.

"Zhang Jiadi is back — he'll be looking for you. Go ahead."

Tian Xuan was startled. She extended her divine sense, but for a long moment could not locate the figure at all.

A cold shock ran through her. Had Master's spiritual sense grown even deeper? She had searched actively and failed — and yet Master had perceived it in an instant.

A divine sense with that kind of range — was that truly something a mid-stage Golden Core cultivator could possess?

Having dispatched Tian Xuan, Luo Chen lingered for a moment, then returned to an annex hall on the island.

The enormous Primordial Unity Cauldron stood at the center of the hall.

Below it, flames burned fierce and relentless, as if inexhaustible.

Luo Chen stood beneath the cauldron, dwarfed by it like an ant at the foot of a boulder.

With a sweep of his hand, several streams of light shot out from the cauldron's interior.

As the radiance faded and their forms became clear — blades, spears, swords, shields, every variety — flying swords most numerous among them.

Every single one was of premium-grade magic instrument quality.

Had the Moya Cave Master witnessed this scene, he would not have left wearing a smile.

*Premium-grade instruments.*

This meant that Luo Chen — the "Demon Lord Qingyang" whose reputation rested on combat prowess — had already ventured into law device territory. He was not an ordinary craftsman limited to upper-grade instruments.

But looking at this batch, Luo Chen shook his head in dissatisfaction.

"Time has still been too short. Even with the system's skill-level boosts, a mere three years was not enough for me to successfully forge even a single lower-grade law device."

"Thinking about it — when the Qingdan Valley people claimed forging the Primordial Unity Cauldron was simple, they were likely deceiving me."

His murmuring faded into silence.

The reason he had been mass-producing instruments was not to earn spirit stones. If earning spirit stones was the goal, his familiar alchemy trade would have filled his coffers far more easily.

The real reason for the intensive crafting was to build his artifact-forging skills in preparation for the day he would forge the Xuanyan Armor — the relic shed by a demon sovereign.

The instruments flowing into Qingyang Hall were nothing more than byproducts he turned to good use to recover costs.

The title of "Artifact-Forging Master Qingyang" was purely an unintended gain.

After a long pause, Luo Chen shook his head and gave a quiet, self-deprecating smile.

*I suppose I was greedy. Expecting to erase a hundred years of a master craftsman's work in a mere three years — that was rushing things.*

He drew a long breath, waved a hand to extinguish the azure flame in the hall, and recalled the Primordial Unity Cauldron into himself.

He gently closed his eyes and let the landscape of his qi sea come into view.

Within a surging sea of fire, an enormous bronze basin gathered countless crimson currents of mana. Above it, the Primordial Unity Cauldron hung at the center, a pair of wings and a small hammer hovering in attendance.

Beyond these, a scarlet golden core blazed like a sun, hanging high in the qi sea's sky — ceaselessly inhaling and exhaling the light of the great day.

At the golden core's edge, something moved in the shadows.

Looking closely — not shadows at all. A flawless white orb, circling the scarlet core like a moon orbiting a sun, slow and steady. The mana within it was equally pure — and in fact its momentum was even more vigorous and vital.

Gazing inward, mana rippled outward like rings on water.

The immense spiritual pressure of Golden Core Stage Five filled the entire annex hall.

Luo Chen opened his eyes slowly, satisfaction in his expression.

He thought the word: *Panel.*

At once a curtain of light poured down like a waterfall.

*(End of Chapter)*
