# Chapter 1033: The Dao Palace Arrives, Luo Chen’s Name

At the Spirit Testing Grand Ceremony site.

One by one, spirited young boys and girls filed in from outside, scattering in twos and threes across the square.

But if one looked down from above, it was easy to see that even among peers of the same age, these youths had formed their own little circles.

The liveliest was naturally Zhou Yuhao's circle, while the most prominent was a group of only three people.

Elsewhere, not far away, Zhou Yuqi was also surrounded by a large crowd of fawning youths.

Perhaps this seemed incongruous.

After all, as long as one tested positive for a spirit root, they could embark on the path of immortality. The future would depend on personal opportunity and effort—there was no need to curry favor with peers.

So-called status and fame were utterly useless in the face of absolute strength.

But in reality, absolute strength came from cultivation, and cultivation depended on resources.

These resources weren't just spirit stones and pills, spirit veins and cave dwellings, but also guidance from elders, instruction in techniques, and so on.

The reason those three circles were so sought after was that they shared a common trait: behind each of them stood a relative at the Adept realm.

Moreover, their families were not lacking in Initiate cultivators.

Zhou Yuhao's grandfather was Zhou Xiaocheng. Behind the circle of three stood the Zhou family head. And Zhou Yuqi was so popular because his father had recently broken through to the Adept realm.

As long as one curried favor with them, after testing positive for a spirit root, one's future cultivation could proceed much more smoothly.

Of course, even wanting to be a lackey wasn't something just anyone could do.

At the very least, the dozens of children born to mortals sent over from Coral Town couldn't even squeeze into those circles. They could only huddle enviously in the corners, watching those people.

Suddenly!

A stir ran through the venue. Two of the three circles began to move.

They were the three from the family head's lineage, and the seven or eight cultivators led by Zhou Yuhao. All of them surged toward the entrance to welcome a young man and a young woman.

A group of ordinary youths from Coral Town was bewildered.

"Who are those two?"

"Why do the descendants of the family head and the elders have to flatter them?"

"Tsk, could that guy be the legendary prodigy of our Zhou family?"

"You mean that genius Zhou Fuyou, who was exceptionally intelligent since childhood, well-versed in martial classics and Daoist scriptures, and once personally adopted by the family head?"

Leaving aside the surprise and curiosity of the Coral Town youths, from the moment the Zhou Fuyou siblings entered, they were surrounded by a warm crowd of peers.

"Boss, you're finally here!" Zhou Yuhao waved enthusiastically, greeting him.

Zhou Fuyou glanced at him, said nothing, but nodded.

Zhou Yuhao chuckled, not minding at all. Instead, he greeted the girl beside Zhou Fuyou.

"Sister Mao Mao, you look so beautiful today! Don't you all agree? Isn't she beautiful?"

The group of youths following behind shouted in unison: "Beautiful!"

Zhou Qingyu's brows curved into a smile. "Little Mouse, your mouth is so sweet."

Their harmonious relationship made it impossible to tell that six years ago, they had exchanged harsh words and come to blows.

But that was reality.

After that incident, Zhou Yuhao had deliberately cultivated good relations with the Zhou Fuyou siblings.

Although Zhou Fuyou had always been cold and aloof, his sister Zhou Qingyu had quickly become friends with them.

Zhou Fuyou didn't refuse. He understood clearly that this was his grandfather's intention—they were still friends, still brothers!

Besides, his sister had always lacked friends. Her childhood had been too lonely.

Having this group of people to play with her would keep her from becoming too withdrawn.

After a few simple exchanges, Zhou Qingyu asked, "Little Mouse, are you confident today?"

Zhou Yuhao lowered his voice. "I'm definitely confident. Father tested me privately—I have a Water-Wood dual spirit root. It's not a heavenly spirit root, but it's the best combination among dual spirit roots!"

Zhou Qingyu blinked, a hint of envy clearly visible in her eyes.

She asked curiously, "Why is the Water-Wood dual spirit root the best among all dual spirit roots?"

Because the Five Elements generate each other—Water gives birth to Wood. Someone with this dual spirit root who primarily cultivates Wood-type techniques will surely achieve twice the results with half the effort.

This thought flashed through Zhou Fuyou's mind.

Then, ignoring Zhou Yuhao's showing off, he looked toward the three from his eldest uncle's family.

Those three greeted Zhou Fuyou one by one.

"Brother Fuyou!"

Zhou Fuyou forced a smile. Though it was ugly, at least it was a response.

Then Zhou Fuyou swept his gaze across the entire venue, taking in all the youths participating in this Spirit Testing Grand Ceremony.

About a hundred people in total!

According to clan records, this was the largest batch in recent years.

And among this batch, there would likely be no shortage of youths who could test positive for spirit roots.

Once the Zhou family recovered its strength, the numbers might be even greater.

Zhou Fuyou's gaze stopped on one person.

It was his childhood playmate, Zhou Yuqi.

Surrounded by a crowd, Zhou Yuqi looked back at him with a complicated expression.

Ever since the family head had rejected Seventh Uncle's family's adoption request, Zhou Fuyou's relationship with Seventh Uncle's family had grown distant. He had never visited them.

Zhou Yuqi seemed to have received some instruction as well—he rarely interacted with him anymore.

His only friend was gone too!

Zhou Fuyou felt a pang of loss in his heart, but he didn't regret it.

Regarding the matter of the tonic from back then, he would definitely settle accounts with Seventh Uncle in the future. Having too close a relationship would only hinder his plans.

"Silence!"

Suddenly, a voice rang out.

Then, one by one, Qi Refinement cultivators walked in from all directions, began to organize the order, and made the youths line up one by one.

Furthermore, five Initiate cultivators ascended the high platform and took their seats at the corners.

"Is it about to begin?" Seeing this, Zhou Fuyou murmured.

Without him noticing, his sister had come to his side, her expression both nervous and expectant.

"Brother."

Zhou Fuyou looked at her, his eyes full of encouragement.

Everything was ready, but the spirit root testing had yet to commence.

Amid the unspeakable anticipation of the youths, streaks of light flew in from the center of the clan.

Leading them were three people.

The family head, Zhou Junxiao; the elder, Zhou Xiaocheng; and the newly promoted elder, Zhou Haiye!

Three Adept realm cultivators had appeared. Even without displaying any magical power fluctuations, their invisible pressure silenced the entire venue.

The others were a group of Initiate true cultivators from the clan, most of them parents of the youths who would be participating in this spirit root test.

The family head, Zhou Junxiao, looked at the hundred youths below, a satisfied expression in his eyes. Then he asked in a low voice, "The guests haven't arrived yet?"

Zhou Xiaocheng was about to say something when Zhou Junxiao suddenly lifted his head.

"They're here!"

Outside the clan, three flying boats—one grand, one ornate, one luxurious—all flew toward this place.

Before they even got close, several people flew out from them one after another.

Zhou Junxiao smiled slightly. "Brother Lin, Brother Yin, you've come!"

Then, he looked hesitantly at another beautiful, mature woman.

"And this is?"

The Yin family head respectfully introduced her. "Mo Wanting, the head of the Jade Balance Court of the Seven Star Courts of the Dao Palace."

Zhou Junxiao's expression shifted slightly. He bowed respectfully. "I am Zhou Junxiao, current family head of the Zhou clan. Greetings to the Jade Balance Court Head."

Mo Wanting smiled faintly. "Fellow Daoist Zhou need not stand on ceremony. I was merely passing through White Egret Marsh and heard that the Zhou family was holding a Spirit Testing Grand Ceremony, so I came to take a look. You know the duties of our Seven Star Courts. I came uninvited, and I hope you'll forgive me."

How could Zhou Junxiao not forgive?

In the current Tiannan Continent, families stood in abundance, but there was only one true leader—the Tiannan Dao Palace!

Within the Dao Palace, whether it was the Observatory Master or the four Hall Masters, they were all too distant. But the Seven Star Courts were very close indeed.

Because the duty of the Seven Star Courts was to search for talented geniuses and deliver talents to the four halls of the Dao Palace.

Countless families hoped that people from the Seven Star Courts would visit their doors.

Moreover, according to rumor, all seven courts of the Seven Star Courts were controlled by the Mo family.

The Mo family—one of the former Twelve Great Houses, and the family of the previous sect master, Mo Shouzhuo!

They had given up the four halls and taken only this Seven Star Courts institution, thereby gaining immense influence over the various families of Tiannan.

Zhou Junxiao's heart was now both nervous and excited.

If today, Jade Balance Court Head Mo Wanting could take a few clan members away from the Zhou family, then his Zhou family could truly be considered as having integrated into the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!

"Someone, arrange seats for the honored guests!"

After giving the order, Zhou Junxiao called Zhou Xiaocheng aside.

"I'll give you the time it takes to burn one incense stick. Quickly contact the parents of the youths and ask if they've had their children's spirit roots tested in advance. If there are any outstanding ones, make a list. During the upcoming test, intersperse them among the children of mortals. The better the talent, the later they should be placed."

Zhou Xiaocheng glanced in Mo Wanting's direction and immediately understood the family head's intention.

If there truly was someone astonishingly talented, and they appeared too early, arousing Mo Wanting's interest, then those who came later—even if their talents were not bad—would easily be overshadowed if they were even slightly inferior.

By presenting them gradually, it would increase the chances of clan youths being selected into the Tiannan Dao Palace.

"Understood. I'll do it right away!"

Just before leaving, he suddenly remembered something.

"Family head, what is Zhou Fuyou's spirit root aptitude like?"

Zhou Junxiao was stunned.

He really didn't know!

It wasn't that he didn't want to test Zhou Fuyou in advance, but the ancestor had once instructed him not to interfere too much in matters related to Zhou Fuyou.

Moreover, that child was particularly independent, so no such work had been done.

Now that Zhou Xiaocheng asked, he gritted his teeth and said, "Arrange him for last! His sister as well, just one spot before him."

...

"Why hasn't it started yet?"

As time trickled away, the youths could barely contain themselves.

Surrounded by so many high-level cultivators, even their gazes brought immense pressure.

Coupled with the fact that having a spirit root or not would determine their entire future, they were restless and uneasy.

Some with weaker wills were already drenched in sweat under the sun's rays.

Zhou Qingyu was the same, but with her brother beside her, her heart was somewhat calmer.

"Brother, what are you looking at?"

"Nothing." Zhou Fuyou withdrew his gaze from the high platform. His expression was calm, but there was a meaningful look in his eyes.

That bearded, dignified Lin family head—according to blood relations and the clan genealogy—should technically be considered his maternal grandfather!

But his mother had never been highly regarded in the Qingtong Lin family. This grandfather probably didn't even remember that he had two grandchildren in the Zhou family.

Suddenly, his sister's delighted cry came from beside him.

"It's starting!"

The long-prepared Spirit Testing Grand Ceremony, at high noon, had finally begun.

On the high platform.

The five Initiate true cultivators sat at the corners, presiding over a massive formation.

The newly promoted Adept, Zhou Haiye, floated down from the spectator seats and landed on the high platform, holding a compass.

He looked down at the crowd below and called out.

"Zhou Li!"

"Present!"

A sturdy youth responded, stepping nervously onto the high platform and entering the very center.

The five Initiate true cultivators exchanged glances and each formed a hand seal.

In an instant, five colors swirled on the high platform, and spiritual energy steamed and billowed.

Zhou Haiye said gently, "Relax your mind, don't resist. Focus on feeling—feel if there is anything that can resonate with you."

The youth named Zhou Li nodded, closed his eyes, and concentrated with all his might.

Meanwhile, the compass in Zhou Haiye's hand began to tremble slightly.

After about ten or so breaths, three beams of light suddenly surged toward Zhou Li's body.

"Metal, Wood, Fire—triple spirit root aptitude!"

Hearing this voice, the youth woke as if from a dream.

Zhou Haiye smiled faintly. "Triple spirit root—you can embark on the immortal path. Go stand at the back!"

Upon hearing this, Zhou Li's face lit up with ecstasy.

He was merely the child of a mortal from Coral Town, not even entered into the Zhou family genealogy. Now that he had tested positive for a spirit root, it was a meteoric rise!

From now on, he could cultivate, he could bring his parents to the clan to enjoy a good life, and he even had the chance to be recorded in the Zhou family genealogy.

How could he not be overjoyed?

Above, in the observation seats.

The Yin family head congratulated Zhou Junxiao. "Congratulations, Brother Zhou! What a great start! This youth is simply dressed, and his name doesn't carry a generation character—he must be a mortal child. For such a one to produce a triple spirit root, it seems this generation of Zhou clan disciples is blessed with profound fortune!"

Zhou Junxiao smiled modestly, his gaze unconsciously drifting toward that beautiful, mature woman.

Mo Wanting was still lounging lazily in her seat, as if nothing interested her.

"A triple spirit root isn't even enough to meet the Dao Palace's standards?"

Zhou Junxiao thought to himself, and his anticipation for the subsequent tests grew even more anxious. His mood was no different from the youths below.

The Spirit Testing Grand Ceremony continued methodically.

With the newly promoted Adept Zhou Haiye presiding, the pace was very fast.

One by one, youths went up to the platform and came down.

Those who tested positive were naturally overjoyed, pretending to be composed as they went to stand behind the high platform.

Those without spirit roots, however, couldn't maintain their composure in front of the elders and returned to the square in despair.

As for the test results?

They weren't very satisfactory.

At most, one or two out of every ten would test positive.

Among those eliminated were not a few descendants of cultivators who had grown up within the clan.

Such a harsh reality made those who remained increasingly anxious.

"Zhou Yusheng, Metal-Fire dual spirit root!"

The first dual spirit root had appeared!

The youths in the venue all looked at that guy with envy, wishing they could take his place.

His parents, in the observation seats, were also extremely excited.

The family head, Zhou Junxiao, revealed a satisfied smile.

However, upon seeing that woman merely shift her hand to support her chin, that satisfaction gradually dissipated.

A Metal-Fire dual spirit root wasn't even enough to meet the Dao Palace's standards for accepting disciples?

Then what was required?

Did it have to be a heavenly spirit root?

While Zhou Junxiao was deep in thought, the spirit root testing continued below.

"Zhou Ping'an, Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, Earth—all five spirit roots present."

The youth named Zhou Ping'an was a simple, honest boy. Upon hearing that he had tested positive for a spirit root, a genuine smile appeared on his face.

He instinctively walked toward the back.

However, Zhou Haiye stopped him.

"Five spirit roots are equivalent to waste spirit roots. You cannot cultivate. Step down!"

Zhou Ping'an was dumbfounded. His smile hadn't even had time to fade as he stood awkwardly on the high platform.

He asked, almost in tears, "But doesn't having a spirit root mean you can cultivate?"

Zhou Haiye shook his head calmly. "Five spirit roots won't work."

"But, I... I..."

Just as the boy's tears began to fall, a charming voice suddenly came from the observation seats.

"Our Jade Balance Court will take him."

The entire venue fell silent.

Zhou Junxiao was caught off guard.

They didn't want a dual spirit root, but they wanted a five spirit root that was barely different from a waste spirit root?

What was this?

He asked instinctively, "Court Head Mo, does the Dao Palace also accept waste spirit root disciples?"

His voice was a bit loud, and even the youths below could faintly hear it.

Mo Wanting laughed charmingly. "Has Family Head Zhou never heard of a certain person's name?"

Zhou Junxiao was bewildered.

"He was once a guest elder of the Star Gate, one of the Four Great Wanderers of the Northern Sea. Legends of him spread through the Southern Frontier. In the Western Desert, he was crowned with the name Moluo. In the Eastern Wilderness, he was revered as a great grandmaster. And across the Mountains and Seas Realm, he was hailed as invincible beneath the Ascendant realm!" Mo Wanting stared at Zhou Junxiao with interest. "Family Head Zhou, that person has quite a connection with your Zhou family. Haven't you remembered yet?"

Zhou Junxiao's expression changed dramatically. "You mean... the Wilderness Wanderer, Luo Chen?"

Mo Wanting's smile faded, and she said seriously, "Exactly. It is him. According to legend, Luo Chen began his cultivation journey with a five spirit root. Since his debut, he has been almost undefeated. This shows that five spirit roots can also achieve great things. This time, the Tiannan Dao Palace is searching far and wide for talent, and one of the criteria is to accept disciples with five spirit root aptitudes, as an experiment."

Zhou Junxiao opened his mouth, wanting to say something, but stopped.

Finally, he shook his head.

He never expected that the reputation of a friend of the Zhou family could also benefit the younger generation of the Zhou clan.

That child named Zhou Ping'an had truly struck it lucky.

"Continue the testing!"

At his command, the interrupted Spirit Testing Grand Ceremony resumed.

And the youths, who had been extremely anxious before, inexplicably gained a glimmer of hope.

If you had a spirit root, you could cultivate. Even if you tested positive for the so-called waste spirit root—the five spirit root—you still had a chance to join the Tiannan Dao Palace. This was equivalent to having an extra path.

At the back of the crowd, the Zhou Fuyou siblings stood quietly.

Zhou Qingyu heard a faint muttering beside her.

"Luo Chen..."

She looked up and saw a dazed expression on her brother's face.

"Brother, what's wrong?"

Zhou Fuyou snapped back to reality as if waking from a dream. "It's nothing."

Even as he said it was nothing, his hand unconsciously rubbed his brow.

Why did he feel a sense of familiarity?

Why?
