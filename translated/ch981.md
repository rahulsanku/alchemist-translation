# Chapter 981: Throat-Cutting Friendship, Speaking from the Heart

On the desolate, boundless desert, mountain ridges rose and fell.

Under the night sky, those winding, crisscrossing ridges looked like lines encircling a circle.

As dawn was about to break, gentle sunlight slowly spilled down, revealing the scene within.

The mountain ridges stretched for a hundred li, truly forming a deep valley.

From the outside, almost nothing seemed amiss.

But only when one reached above and looked down could one see the terrifyingly massive demon beast army entrenched within.

At this moment, when they should have been resting and sleeping, the myriad demons suddenly awoke in alarm.

Because from the void came bursts of explosive sounds, growing closer and closer to them.

The leading great demon, clad in silver-threaded black robes, with a long face and an eagle-like nose, reacted extremely quickly.

The moment the first explosive sound rang out, he was instantly jolted awake.

His expression shifted between shock and anger. "Could it be that someone has discovered our plan to gather troops in this Sand-Trapping Valley and ambush Five Platforms Temple?"

He shot into the air, spreading his divine sense outward, and then his face turned deathly pale in an instant.

Because another, even more powerful divine sense, like a boundless ocean, was sweeping toward him, containing a murderous intent that chilled one to the bone.

"An Ascendant-level powerhouse?"

The black-robed great demon shuddered all over and, without hesitation, turned and fled.

Inside Sand-Trapping Valley, a hundred thousand demon beasts watched this scene in confusion, not understanding why Lord Refining Silver Roc had suddenly departed.

Their gazes instinctively turned in the opposite direction.

Before long, a somewhat disheveled figure appeared above Sand-Trapping Valley.

As their eyes met, the demons suddenly understood.

A human?

The next moment!

*Whoosh!*

A sharp, explosive sound suddenly rang out. The bare-chested man casually raised his hand, and the blood-colored longsword in his grip shot forth.

The blood-colored sword tore through the air, transforming into a streak of rainbow light, instantly claiming countless lives.

"Roar!"

"Wail!"

In an instant, chaos erupted in the valley.

Waves of demon beasts tried to charge into the sky, escaping this prison.

The bare-chested man had only one arm. After releasing his sword, he seemed to finally have a free hand.

He formed a single-handed seal and pointed at the sky.

On the desert, which should have been cloudless, at this moment when day and night were about to separate, a blurry white cloud suddenly gathered.

The demons were baffled.

Then they watched as, within that boundless white cloud, a giant eye suddenly opened.

Blood-red all over, with a golden pupil gleaming.

It was the Crimson Eye, Golden Pupil!

The cloud eye opened, emitting a hazy, dazzling light. Wherever it shone, the demons that had been frantically scattering and fleeing moments ago suddenly fell silent.

But they were silent; the blood-colored sword was not.

A wheel of sword light continuously expanded, growing larger and larger, reaching ten li, twenty li, a hundred li...

Wherever it passed, clouds of blood mist sprayed into the air.

And within it, the blood-colored sword frantically devoured the endless blood mist, becoming increasingly sinister, its entire blade as if made of glass.

This process, though it sounds long, actually happened within only a few breaths.

After doing all this, the bare-chested man's aura had already withered to its limit.

All over his body, even the fluctuations of his mana were faint, as if barely present.

A pair of eyes that should have been crystalline involuntarily shed two lines of bloody tears.

He slowly turned his head, looking back at the path he had come from.

"Azure Frost, can you take this sword strike?"

"Screech!"

A piercing cry tore through the clouds and mist.

A divine cyan bird crossed space to arrive. Just as it was about to strike, it saw Luo Chen's ghastly appearance and the scene behind him, like a Shura hell, and its heart trembled.

Yet the hatred and killing intent flooding its mind could not be suppressed no matter what.

"Even if I have to destroy all my cultivation, I will kill you!"

In a shrill cry, a hazy cyan light poured out from all over her body.

A pitch-black crack, shaped like a crescent moon and a full hundred zhang long, slowly emerged before her.

It was that same move from before!

**Subspace Severance!**

After unleashing this move, Azure Frost seemed unable to hold on any longer. Her massive true body shrank rapidly, growing smaller and smaller, returning to her human form.

The Subspace Severance slowly advanced, devouring everything in its path.

The speed appeared agonizingly slow, as if one could easily sidestep it. But Luo Chen, who had suffered a great loss from this move before and lost an arm, knew well its profound mysteries.

Unless one's escape speed surpassed Azure Frost's and one was proficient in the dao of space, no matter how one dodged, one could not evade this strike.

But he had never intended to flee again!

His single arm raised, fingers pointing out.

"Go!"

*Whoosh!*

A sword light shot up from the deep valley behind him.

Endless blood qi transformed into a blood-colored rainbow, trailing behind the Yuantu Sword, forming a long tail, hurtling straight toward the hundred-zhang black line.

**One Hundred Thousand Demon Blood Slays the Cyan Luan!**

Closer!

Closer!

The two collided in an instant.

No earth-shattering sound was made, nor was there much dazzling, colorful brilliance.

There was only silent, soundless annihilation.

The black crack voraciously devoured the endless sword qi.

The blood-colored sword furiously assaulted that bizarre divine ability.

Luo Chen pressed his lips together, his expression like eternal, unmeltable ice. His vast soul power strained to control this sword strike.

This sword strike was one he had executed using his Crimson Eye, Golden Pupil to perform **Mirror Flower, Water Moon**, then amplified it with the **Cloud-Chess Water Mirror** spell to briefly control the hundred thousand demon beasts, before driving the Yuantu Sword to massacre them and gather their blood qi.

And this move was a killing technique from the fifth-rank sword canon, *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon*, called **Thousand Skies Converging Flow**.

It was a sword dao killing move that was not a divine ability, yet surpassed one, requiring a massive consumption of Nine Skies Qi.

Luo Chen's Nine Skies Qi had long been exhausted, so he had to use the massive blood qi of the demon beasts as a substitute.

Consequently, controlling it with his soul power was extremely difficult. His already heavily injured body was on the verge of collapse.

But he could not fall!

Could not fall here!

Huiniang, Caiyi, his son, friends, disciples, old acquaintances... and that long life which was just one step away!

"Explode for me!"

Like the roar of a wounded beast, it rang out in an instant.

Luo Chen's sword finger suddenly turned, his sword technique abruptly changing.

He directly detonated the subsequent changes of this move, using the power of a hundred thousand demon beasts to strike that Subspace Severance divine ability.

The previously deadlocked situation was instantly detonated.

*Boom!*

The hundred-zhang pitch-black crack dissipated in an instant.

A terrifying shockwave, centered on Luo Chen and Azure Frost, surged in all directions.

The white clouds scattered, the earth sank.

Luo Chen's figure fell powerlessly into the vast, sinking sand and mud of Sand-Trapping Valley.

A figure staggered over, covered in fine wounds.

Suddenly, a flash of blood light shot through the air, stabbing straight at her lower abdomen.

This sword strike came abruptly.

The woman leaped sideways.

*Pfft!*

Her right foot was severed cleanly in the middle.

"Hmph..."

The woman grunted, turned, and grabbed, actually catching the sword firmly in her hand.

Ignoring the blood dripping from her hand, her whole body trembled.

After their ultimate clash, both she and Luo Chen were nearly at the end of their rope.

She had never expected that the sword itself had a spirit and could still ambush her.

"Your master isn't dead yet!"

She flicked her finger, and the blood sword, which was on the verge of falling into weakness, immediately quieted down.

Azure Frost, ignoring her surroundings, leaped down, plunging into the deep valley where boundless sand and mud were cascading downward.

...

A thousand li away.

Refining Silver Roc stared pale-faced in the direction he had come from.

"So strong!"

In his muttering, his pale face gradually became hesitant.

"Could it be... not an Ascendant-level powerhouse?"

He recalled the moment of his divine sense collision with that unfamiliar cultivator.

"It wasn't as pure and overbearing as Big Brother's consciousness, nor as ever-changing as Ancient Red Sand Scorpion's divine sense. It seemed to have the same soul essence as an ordinary Sage, just more abundant."

With this thought, he grew even more hesitant.

Deserting in battle, abandoning a hundred thousand troops—when Big Brother returned, he would definitely be in deep trouble.

And clearly, that person had arrived hastily and abruptly, not as if prepared, but rather as if he had shifted over while in the middle of a fight.

"It must be a life-and-death battle between two late-stage Sage cultivators!"

"With such ultimate moves, both sides are bound to be severely wounded. If I can profit from the chaos..."

Refining Silver Roc licked his lips, the hesitation in his eyes gradually fading, replaced by thick greed.

Not to mention the legacies of two severely wounded great cultivators, their very Sages would be a great tonic for him!

If he could obtain them...

Refining Silver Roc took a deep breath, slapped a few talismans on himself, and then quietly advanced toward Sand-Trapping Valley.

...

Sand-Trapping Valley collapsed, massive amounts of quicksand flooding the entire valley like tidal waves.

Two figures struggled within, unable to escape.

But they didn't want to escape either!

Azure Frost followed the mutual qi sensation, rapidly pursuing.

Suddenly!

From the vast sand and mud, a fist shot out.

Catching her off guard, it punched a concave hole in her body, which was as hard as a true artifact.

Wincing in pain, she reached back and grabbed.

That hand tried to pull back, but as her fingers locked around it, it could no longer retreat.

"Found you!"

Her other hand followed like a snake.

*Bang!*

A foot kicked up from below, colliding heavily with her left hand.

As they separated, that leg burst with blood, while Azure Frost's left hand hung limply.

Sand was everywhere, but it could no longer hide their figures, nor those two pairs of eyes filled with blazing murderous intent.

"Die!"

"Die!"

*Boom!*

*Bang!*

*Thud!*

The two figures sank deeper and deeper, ignoring the scouring sand around them, ignoring their broken bodies. Even with only one hand and one foot left, they still attacked each other's vital points.

But the one who was once the number one under Ascendant, the famed Alchemy Saint of the Eastern Wilderness, had now lost all grace and bearing.

The means of their attacks were no longer the endless stream of divine abilities, spells, treasures, and true artifacts, but had become as savage and primitive as those of the mundane world.

Fists, palms, legs, kicks, headbutts, elbow strikes.

When pushed to the limit, Azure Frost even used her silver teeth to bite a chunk of flesh from Luo Chen's shoulder.

Of course, given the strength of Luo Chen's physical body, the price for that bite of flesh was naturally one of Azure Frost's teeth being knocked loose.

No one knew how long passed.

The speed of the sand and mud pouring down slowed.

Deep underground, a huge cavern had long been filled with countless coarse yet fine grains of sand, forming a large sand dune.

Suddenly!

*Bang!*

Two figures burst out of the ground, instantly entangling again.

Each struck out with a palm, then tumbled backward like rolling gourds.

Hitting the wall, they rebounded and clashed again.

In the narrow space above the sand dune, leg shadows crisscrossed.

Just as they were about to separate again, Azure Frost suddenly swung her long leg like a whip, coiling it around Luo Chen's thigh.

As Luo Chen was stunned, she bit down, directly latching onto his neck.

"This madwoman!"

Unable to break free, Luo Chen stabbed back with his hand, forcibly piercing her incredibly hard flesh, plunging his hand deep inside and grasping her heart.

But he did not crush that heart!

Because his own throat was also clamped in her teeth.

"You bite—even if you bite through my neck, I can still crush your heart!"

At this moment, the two were intertwined, one with a "throat-cutting" kiss, the other with a hand through the chest.

From a distance, it looked quite tender and ambiguous.

But the danger between them was known only to them.

Without source power to support it, if the neck was bitten through, it couldn't be restored in a short time. This normally insignificant wound would become a fatal one.

The same was true for her. Once the heart was crushed, even a divine beast's body would not fare well.

"I'll count to three. We each let go, and then we fight to the death!"

Without waiting for Azure Frost's response, Luo Chen slowly loosened his grip on the beating heart.

"One!"

"Two!"

"Three..."

"You crazy woman!"

Luo Chen tightened his grip again, clutching the lively, beating heart.

Because the pain at his neck came again.

She didn't want a truce!

He tried to take a deep breath, but couldn't. His gaze flicked upward for a moment.

Then, suppressing his killing intent, he said in a low voice, "If we keep fighting like this, neither of us will end well. I... don't want to die!"

Without waiting for Azure Frost's reply, he spoke with difficulty, "I know you're not afraid of death, but can you die? If you die, who will go to Severed Soul Cliff to save your sister?"

"With Rosycloud's abilities, do you truly believe she is already dead?"

"Fine, even if she is dead, I have cultivated the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture* and know the method of phoenix nirvana. Although I cannot use this method, your sister must know that technique. Is there still a chance she could be reborn through nirvana?"

"You have to live, to have the chance to find Severed Soul Cliff and save your sister!"

The sharp teeth at his neck seemed to loosen slightly.

Luo Chen breathed a slight sigh of relief and no longer gripped her heart so tightly.

"Speaking from the heart... ahem, in any case, you and I actually have no deep, irreconcilable enmity. What I did before was forced by the Tianyuan Dao Sect. You saw it yourself—I originally wanted to leave Severed Soul Cliff and not interfere in your conflict."

"In short, let go now, and then leave. If there's a chance later, I can even help you find Rosycloud. With my Nirvana Sacred Flame, combined with you, finding the ever-wandering Severed Soul Cliff won't be difficult."

"I'll count to three again. We each let go, alright?"

"One!"

"I don't trust you!"

"Two... Huh?" Luo Chen said in surprise, looking down at the pale face so close, a face that even the sand covering it could not hide.

He thought for a moment, then suddenly said, "I have something called the Life-and-Death Gu."

"Your gu insects, I trust them even less!"

Luo Chen didn't know how she was producing sound. It wasn't by opening her mouth, nor by ventriloquism, nor by divine sense transmission. But as her body trembled, a clear voice indeed came out.

Luo Chen thought again. "How about we each place a restriction on the other?"

"You managed to hide under Ancestor Chunyuan's nose for so long. Your skill in formations and restrictions must be extremely high. I still don't trust you."

Luo Chen gritted his teeth. "A Great Dao oath!"

This time, Azure Frost was silent.

Silence meant she was somewhat tempted.

Luo Chen really didn't want to die in this sunless, lightless deep pit.

He had been through lows, had his moments of glory, and had once been illustrious.

He didn't want to die here, becoming a chance encounter for future generations of juniors.

He gave his final condition.

"Each of us plants a Life-and-Death Gu in the other, then places a restriction threatening a vital point to prevent any last-minute betrayal, and finally swears a Great Dao oath not to attack each other before reaching Ascendant. With these three layers of restraint, we turn from enemies to friends. What do you say?"

As he spoke, he completely released her heart.

A surge of breath rose from his nostrils—the sign that she had opened her mouth to bite down again.

Luo Chen growled.

"Think of your sister!"

Azure Frost, whose mind had been clouded by rage, finally regained clarity at this roar.

She slowly released her bite, her sharp teeth like swords pulling out of Luo Chen's neck bit by bit.

Finally, she raised her head, sand falling in a shower, and stared at Luo Chen with a complex gaze.

"Let's begin!"

Luo Chen breathed a sigh of relief and glanced upward.

"So you noticed that guy's arrival too."

(End of chapter)
