# Chapter 1116: Mahakala, Luo Chen Burns Incense

"Transformation Spirit Crystal—such a legendary relic from ancient times!"

Inside the flying shuttle, Luo Chen held a fist-sized crystal in his hand, still able to feel a trace of the warmth it had carried earlier.

This was the very essence of that enormous celestial star, which had traveled countless light-years before finally landing in the Mountains and Seas Realm. After its long journey, it had suffered tremendous loss, and after taking a full-force Thousand Skies Convergence from Luo Chen head-on, this was all that remained.

Only after identifying the origin of this object did Luo Chen understand why that meteor had been able to withstand a Thousand Skies Convergence.

Transformation Spirit Crystal!

It was an extremely special material.

It could absorb, digest, and even reverse and expel the spiritual power converted from the origin qi of heaven and earth.

According to legend, in ancient times, a Heavenly Origin Dao Sect Ascendant had excavated a Transformation Spirit Crystal from the Mountains and Seas Realm. Using it as a foundation, he refined a dharma treasure called the [Yin-Yang Reversal Mirror]. With this treasure, he dominated the battlefield, slaying enemies in the Great Desolate, defeating other holy land Ascendants, and finally ascending gracefully.

The Yin-Yang Reversal Mirror was said to be capable of completely absorbing an enemy's killing moves and even perfectly rebounding them—truly an inconceivable top-tier spirit treasure.

But in Luo Chen's view, using a Transformation Spirit Crystal as the main material for an offensive dharma treasure was rather wasteful.

This object could absorb, digest, and even rebound, but the force of the rebound was limited. If it encountered an attack surpassing the user's own strength, not only would it fail to rebound perfectly, but because it couldn't fully digest the attack, the treasure's master would end up bearing the full brunt of the assault.

That was far too risky!

On the contrary, if this material were refined into a pure defensive treasure, the effect of absorbing and digesting spiritual power could be maximized.

Attacks from those weaker than or equal to oneself would be perfectly defended against.

Even if facing an enemy far stronger than oneself, the special properties of the Transformation Spirit Crystal could still dissolve most of the attack's power!

It was the most coveted refining material for all defensive treasures.

"The Bone-Burial Umbrella has been in my possession for a long time. Despite adding large amounts of materials to refine it, I still haven't fully restored it. Now that I have the Transformation Spirit Crystal, perhaps I have a chance to completely repair it."

Luo Chen made some calculations and came to a decision.

He didn't have many defensive treasures. The Primordial Unity Cauldron served both offense and defense, so it was out of consideration. That left only the Bone-Burial Umbrella and the Mystic Dust Armor.

The latter had considerable potential, but it had only been refined for four hundred years. Elevating it to a spirit treasure couldn't be achieved by simply fusing one or two materials.

The Bone-Burial Umbrella itself was already at the spirit treasure level. After incorporating the Transformation Spirit Crystal, it might be restored to its original state.

At that point, with the Yuantu Sword for offense, the Bone-Burial Umbrella for defense, the Primordial Unity Cauldron for both offense and defense plus suppression and control, and his full repertoire of techniques and divine abilities, Luo Chen would have no weaknesses in combat at the Ascendant stage!

With this thought, Luo Chen hesitated no longer.

His fingertip trembled, and a wisp of blue-white flame rose gently.

It was the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire!

As soon as it appeared, the flame burned on Luo Chen's fingertip with considerable intelligence, even beginning to lick at him with its fiery tongues.

But the burning was very mild, more like an affectionate nudge or touch.

Seeing this, Luo Chen couldn't help but show a satisfied expression.

After devouring the vast life force from the bodies of four fifth-rank Great Desolate beings, the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire had finally recovered, and its intelligence had advanced even further.

When it came to alchemy and artifact refining, Luo Chen preferred the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire, which had accompanied him for centuries, over the Nirvana Sacred Flame. No particular reason—just habit.

"It's a pity about that Shark Emperor True Flame. It was lost in that great battle between heaven and earth."

Looking at the affectionate behavior of the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire, Luo Chen felt some regret for the blue flame he had obtained from Shark Emperor Liujun.

That was a high-grade flame passed down through the sharkfolk race for thousands of years, of the water-cold attribute. Though it had impurities, its foundation was considerable.

After removing the impurities, if he had nurtured it well for a few hundred years, it could have advanced to the fifth rank like the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire.

But to kill the Dao Sect Ascendant, Ancestor Liancheng, Luo Chen had merged the Shark Emperor True Flame, the Nirvana Sacred Flame, and the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire into one, using the Three-Flame Fan to unleash a terrifying strike.

While the Nirvana and Withering-Bloom flames could preserve their seeds through their own abundant foundations, the Shark Emperor True Flame, which hadn't reached the fifth rank, wasn't so fortunate.

After that battle, its flame seed was completely extinguished.

If it were to reappear in the Mountains and Seas Realm in the future, it would probably have to rely on that oil lamp in Xiaobai's hands, supplemented by sharkfolk oil, nurtured bit by bit.

The Withering-Bloom Divine Fire shared a spiritual connection with Luo Chen. At this moment, it seemed to sense his emotions. Coupled with the time the Blue Fish Fire Spirit had spent living in his Purple Mansion, it now felt a share of sympathy and sorrow, sending warm flames to brush over Luo Chen.

"Alright, let's start refining this Transformation Spirit Crystal. You've eaten so much lately—don't you dare slack off!"

Luo Chen laughed and scolded, then flicked his finger. The Withering-Bloom Divine Fire completely enveloped the Transformation Spirit Crystal.

...

"Husband, we've arrived at the Hanging Temple!"

A gentle voice came from outside the door.

Inside, a bone umbrella floated in the air, slowly rotating, with a faint red-clad woman's shadow flickering in and out of view.

Below it sat Luo Chen, cross-legged.

Hearing the voice outside, Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes and glanced at the bone umbrella first.

"Not bad. The restoration progress far exceeds my expectations. This Transformation Spirit Crystal truly lives up to its reputation as an ancient treasure material. This trip has brought me an unexpected opportunity."

Obtaining the Transformation Spirit Crystal could indeed be considered a small stroke of fortune.

While exploring the Loulan ancient city for treasures, a meteorite had suddenly fallen from the sky, bringing him this rare treasure. How could that not be considered an opportunity?

But Luo Chen wasn't secretly pleased about it.

All of this was built on the foundation of his invincible Ascendant-stage strength in the current era.

Without this level of power, an ordinary Sage would have been severely injured or killed by that terrifying meteor's impact. How could they talk about opportunities and fortune?

In truth, this principle had run through Luo Chen's entire cultivation career.

Throughout his journey, he had encountered countless opportunities and fortunes, but most of them he could only watch from afar.

Like the Golden Core ruins back in Dahe Market, or the White Night Cave Mansion in the Accumulated Thunder Nine Mountains—hadn't they all been placed before him?

But in those days, his cultivation was low and his strength weak. Even if they were delivered to his doorstep, he could only stare helplessly.

In the end, opportunities and fortune in the immortal cultivation world belonged to those with virtue.

And that "virtue" meant qualification, meant ability.

He, Luo Chen, was now a great Ascendant. Of course, he was more qualified than anyone to collect all kinds of opportunities and fortune!

With a casual wave of his hand—

The red-clad woman glanced at Luo Chen, then obediently slipped into the umbrella.

Luo Chen put away the bone umbrella and left the quiet chamber.

Outside, Gu Caiyi had been waiting for some time.

"Husband."

"Mmm." Luo Chen looked around while releasing his divine sense. "Where are the others?"

Gu Caiyi's expression was somewhat strange. "They're outside, confronting the Hanging Temple monks."

Luo Chen nodded. His divine sense had already taken in the entire situation.

At this moment.

Because the Frost Eagle Flying Shuttle had been hovering above the Hanging Temple for some time, it had drawn the attention of the experts within.

Especially after sensing the soaring demonic aura inside, the Hanging Temple was on high alert.

Amidst the mountain ranges, countless temples and pagodas stood, with incense burning brightly.

Truly worthy of being the number one Buddhist sect in the Western Desert!

But the current Hanging Temple was not the original one.

The real Hanging Temple had been sealed away by Great Dharma Master Xuan Du using the spirit treasure Glazed Pure Land.

These temples at the foot of the mountain had been built by the group of experts who had attacked the Glazed Pure Land back then.

Though they were a remnant lineage, their strength was still considerable.

More than twenty auras comparable to the Sage stage had locked onto the Frost Eagle Flying Shuttle, ready to fight at the slightest provocation.

At the head were the abbots of the Dharma Hall and the Precept Hall.

Behind them, the abbot of the Arhat Hall was on high alert, having mobilized all the temple's experts.

Kongchan and the others were naturally unconcerned, but Qi Ying was filled with confusion.

Why would Senior Luo Chen come to provoke them for no reason?

Could it be that he still harbored resentment over the incident when the Hanging Temple's thirty-six eminent monks had besieged him?

The details of that battle in the Glazed Pure Land had long spread throughout the world.

Luo Chen's true rise to fame could be said to have begun in that battle.

The demoness and the Moluo joining forces to break the Arhat Grand Formation, which could rival an Ascendant, was still praised to this day.

Just as the standoff was underway, two people walked out of the flying shuttle.

But all eyes fell only on the white-robed man.

"It's you!"

"Luo Chen!"

"Formless Moluo!"

Exclamations came from the mouths of the monks one after another.

Heavenjade snorted coldly. "How dare you! Do you know to address my master with respect?"

"Heavenjade, stand down." Luo Chen smiled slightly, walking to the front of the group. His gaze swept over the crowd, finally landing on the two leading monks. "Two abbots, it has been a while."

The Precept Hall abbot's face was grim. "Luo Chen, don't think that just because you killed a Dao Sect Ascendant and possess invincible power in this age, you can insult the Hanging Temple. If you come to provoke us today, even if we have to sacrifice our lives and dao, we won't let you off easy."

Luo Chen was not angered. He smiled and said, "Fellow Daoist, you misunderstand. If I wanted to provoke you, would I come to your door like this?"

As he spoke, a trace of his aura leaked out.

The Dharma Hall abbot, who had the highest cultivation, changed color instantly. "Ascendant stage!"

At these words, all the Hanging Temple experts were shaken.

Back when Luo Chen had not yet become an Ascendant, he could fight the thirty-six Arhat Grand Formation to a standstill.

With the demoness Azure Frost joining him, they had even dismantled the formation and slain more than ten of his fellow disciples with his sword.

Now that Luo Chen had become an Ascendant, the entire Hanging Temple couldn't even gather enough experts to form the Arhat Grand Formation.

With this shift in power, let alone a fight to the death—they would be lucky to even tear a corner of his robe.

With this thought, the Dharma Hall abbot stopped the Precept Hall abbot, forced a smile, and said, "May I ask what business the senior has here?"

Luo Chen's gaze was distant as he looked up into the sky.

In that endless high sky, where a fierce, clear wind blew.

The Emerald Screen Wind Barrier was up there!

And the real Hanging Temple was sealed within it.

Not only that, but the fallen Buddha who had once made Great Dharma Master Xuan Du so wary—Mahakala—was also sealed inside the Glazed Pure Land.

"This trip, I am merely fulfilling an old agreement with Fellow Daoist Xuan Du, to come and burn a stick of incense."

"Just to burn incense?" The monks were all stunned.

Without another word, Luo Chen had already floated up into the sky.

No one stopped him—not because they didn't want to, but because they didn't dare.

They could only watch helplessly as Luo Chen entered the fierce wind.

...

His protective qi shield was as thick as a mountain.

Countless gusts of astral wind beat against it, producing an ear-piercing scraping sound.

"So this is the protective heavenly barrier of the Buddhist holy land?"

"Standing within it, an ordinary Sage cultivator wouldn't last long before being torn apart by this Emerald Screen Wind Barrier."

Luo Chen did not dare to be careless. He guarded his mind and revealed a suit of black armor on his body.

Step by step, he advanced deeper, finally stopping before an invisible boundary.

A familiar feeling arose spontaneously.

This invisible space must be the Glazed Pure Land!

"Xuan Du dissolved his entire Arhat cultivation, combined with the spirit treasure Glazed Pure Land, to forcibly seal the Hanging Temple!"

Luo Chen could sense within that spirit treasure the thick aura of his old friend.

Not only that, but there was also a suppressed thought to the extreme, wanting nothing more than to break through the seal at every moment.

Unfortunately, it couldn't get out.

But Luo Chen's divine sense could enter.

The moment the two made contact, boundless Buddhist chanting and sutra recitation rang out in Luo Chen's sea of consciousness.

"Hmph!"

Luo Chen snorted lightly, and the Soul-Imprisoning Art burst forth.

In an instant, the chanting ceased, and the sutra sounds became like wind.

Not only that, but Luo Chen's divine sense gathered into one, like a sharp sword, slashing across the void.

The divine senses of two Ascendant beings collided in the purest and most direct way.

Boom!

A silent roar echoed in each other's hearts.

"Ugh..."

A muffled groan sounded within the Glazed Pure Land, followed by a furious roar.

"Who are you!!!"

"I am Luo Chen. Mahakala, just follow Xuan Du's wish and stay in there peacefully!"

Luo Chen replied with a smile, then withdrew his divine sense.

He, Luo Chen, was now least afraid of soul battles.

From the very beginning of Qi Refinement, his soul foundation had far exceeded that of his peers. After cultivating the Soul-Splitting Art and tempering it with large amounts of Great Soul Pills and soul stones, the strength of his soul foundation had surpassed what ordinary people could imagine.

Now that he had broken through to the Ascendant stage, his soul had doubled. Under the refinement of unity, although he was only at the early Ascendant stage, he was probably not far from the late Ascendant stage.

Having achieved this fortune today, Luo Chen would never forget Xuan Du's gift back then.

Without his gift of the *Moonlight Heart-Cleansing Mantra*, which helped him condense his primordial spirit in advance despite his missing life soul, he would never have had a chance against the Ascendant Liancheng later.

Even though Xuan Du had already acted as his Dao protector back then, Luo Chen still cherished that debt of gratitude.

Coming here today, this stick of incense—he was determined to burn it!

"The Glazed Pure Land alone won't seal Mahakala for long. Let me, Luo Chen, lend a hand to Fellow Daoist!"

Luo Chen had already made up his mind.

He took out a large amount of materials from his storage ring and struck them one by one into the periphery of the Glazed Pure Land.

He also took out a carefully refined formation plate and drove it into the Pure Land.

Then, Luo Chen frequently struck out various restrictions.

Mountain, water, wind, cloud, darkness, light, void—all kinds of restrictions emerged in endless succession.

But the main one was the wind restriction!

As he worked, the being within the Glazed Pure Land had already sensed something was wrong.

"What are you doing?"

Luo Chen did not answer, only continuing to set up the formation.

Time passed bit by bit. Unbeknownst to them, half a month had passed since Luo Chen had come to the mountain.

And the terrifying astral wind above the mountain peaks was gradually dissipating.

This scene puzzled everyone.

But Kongchan vaguely saw what was happening.

"He's setting up a formation, and that formation looks familiar."

Kongchan frowned, thinking continuously, until he finally found the source of that familiarity.

"It's the Arctic Demon-Sealing Formation!"

"No!"

"It seems the master has made some changes to it?"

As he expected, what Luo Chen was setting up now was exactly the Demon-Sealing Formation that Li Canghai had arranged outside the Arctic Yemo Heaven back then.

Because Li Canghai had personally given him a token, and Luo Chen had spent a long time observing and even breaking into the formation, he had gained a very high understanding of that Demon-Sealing Formation.

Now, he was recreating the old formation, abandoning the concept of drawing water and earth qi, and instead combining it with the endless astral wind here. Li Canghai's legacy formation reappeared on the Buddhist soil of the Western Desert!

"Gather!"

With a single explosive shout!

The Emerald Screen Wind Barrier that had shrouded the Hanging Temple for countless years all surged into a vortex.

This process was not instantaneous but continuous. As long as the formation's power remained, the Emerald Screen Wind Barrier born in this space would be drawn into the Glazed Pure Land by the formation.

Inside, it would continuously grind against Mahakala.

Perhaps it couldn't truly extinguish this Arhat, but at least it could disrupt the process of him gathering power to break the Glazed Pure Land seal.

With this, Luo Chen had made up for that stick of incense.

"This should also be what you wanted, Fellow Daoist Xuan Du!"

Looking at that Pure Land, Luo Chen spoke thus.

As if sensing something, as the clouds and smoke dissipated with the wind, a faint shadow appeared, performing a Buddhist greeting toward Luo Chen.

(End of chapter)
