# Chapter 891: Father and Son, Three Cultivators Arrive

Under the moonlight.

Two figures walked hand in hand, strolling across the white jade plaza.

In the quiet atmosphere, there was a woman's soft sobbing, a man's comforting words. Later, no one knew what the man said, but the woman inexplicably laughed, and the atmosphere became less heavy and instead grew warm.

Inside the [Mystic Dust Hall], Yao Mingyue, who had only recently emerged from seclusion, timely withdrew the foot she was about to step out.

But the young boy quietly poked his head out, curiously watching the scene outside.

Suddenly.

Sima Huiniang, nestled in the man's arms, looked this way and waved at the boy.

"Lingxi, come here quickly!"

Luo Lingxi bit his lip, timid and hesitant.

Behind him, Yao Mingyue gave him a push.

"Go on, that's your father."

Luo Lingxi stumbled forward a step, casting a pleading look at Yao Mingyue, who only smiled encouragingly.

Biting his lip, he walked toward Sima Huiniang, looking back every few steps.

Yao Mingyue kept smiling to encourage him, which gave him a little more courage.

Over the years, his mother would sometimes get angry, but Mingyue-jiejie had never lost her temper with him. He trusted Yao Mingyue.

But was that man really his father?

As he drew closer, a feeling of closeness welled up from within, as if it came from the marrow of his bones and blood, and even the boy's steps quickened slightly.

Only when he timidly reached the man's side did Luo Lingxi dare to truly look up at his "father."

So young?

Even younger than Uncle Qin Yuanjiang and Uncle Baleful Dragonzi!

He instinctively grabbed Sima Huiniang's hand.

"Mother..."

Sima Huiniang said joyfully, "Husband, this is our child. Look, he looks just like you! The mouth, the nose, and these eyes!"

Luo Chen looked down at the small boy. He was somewhat thin, and his spirit and energy were not very abundant.

As for what Huiniang said about the mouth and nose, they actually didn't resemble him much.

His own mouth was thick and full, while the boy's mouth was slightly thin, even a bit sharp.

His own nose was high and straight, while the boy's nose, though also prominent, had a slight downward curve at the tip, like an aquiline nose.

Only those eyes!

They were truly identical to his!

Black pupils, deep and profound; whites of the eyes clear and distinct; large and spirited, as if they could see through a person's heart and mind.

With just these eyes, even if the two stood together, one would instinctively overlook the other features and think they were seventy or eighty percent similar.

Was this his child?

Luo Chen smiled slightly and reached out his hand.

Luo Lingxi timidly extended his hand, and then a great force came, lifting him up and holding him.

"Come, let's eat!"

Luo Chen, holding the child in one arm and Sima Huiniang's hand in the other, walked toward the Mystic Dust Hall.

Yao Mingyue stood guard at the entrance, bowing gracefully. "Disciple welcomes the Supreme Elder's return!"

Luo Chen glanced at her, his brow slightly furrowed.

Sima Huiniang seemed to realize something and immediately pleaded for her, "It's not that Mingyue disobeyed your orders. It's just that I've been a bit lonely these past years, so I specially called her back from Little Xuan Mountain to keep me company. And Mingyue has been helping take care of Lingxi all these years. She's helped me a lot."

Luo Chen's brow gradually relaxed, and he gave Yao Mingyue a slight nod.

"Join us for dinner!"

This signified that he would overlook her disobedience of his order to reflect in seclusion.

Yao Mingyue was relieved, a look of surprise appearing on her face.

That evening's meal was not the usual spiritual tea and fruits, but rather had the smoky flavor of mortal life.

Strong liquor and hot dishes, fragrant and steaming.

Luo Lingxi, who had been playing all day, ate heartily. No one knew how that thin body managed to swallow so much food without getting any fatter.

Yao Mingyue sat beside him, sometimes adding food to his bowl, sometimes wiping his mouth.

On Luo Chen's side, he drank wine while chatting with Sima Huiniang about various matters.

There were his fabricated stories of recuperating from injuries in seclusion outside, the development of the Luo Tian Sect over the years, and Sima Huiniang's personal cultivation progress.

He listened to a lot, but wasn't particularly interested in any of it.

Only regarding Sima Huiniang's slow cultivation speed did he express an opinion.

"Now that you no longer have the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu affliction, you're still young, and you have hope of reaching the Sage realm. Why aren't you cultivating diligently?"

"When I swept my divine sense over the area earlier, Caiyi's cultivation progress is almost catching up to yours."

Gu Caiyi's aptitude wasn't outstanding.

But she had the advantage of being Luo Chen's Dao companion, enjoying abundant resources.

Especially now that she had a third-grade upper spiritual land cave dwelling. In just a few short years, her realm had already crossed the early Adept stage and reached Adept Four.

It was only a step away from Sima Huiniang's Adept Six, which had stagnated for over a hundred years.

By now, Luo Chen understood that before the Sage realm, aptitude was merely a threshold for cultivation. The supply of resources was the most important thing.

As long as there were enough resources, even a pig or a blade of grass could achieve something.

Let alone a Adept cultivator who wasn't bad to begin with.

Sima Huiniang looked lovingly at Luo Lingxi and smiled, "Husband, your cultivation is accomplished, and the sect has great talents like Li Yingzhang to help manage things. They don't need me for much. If I really go into secluded cultivation, who will take care of Lingxi?"

Luo Chen frowned and instinctively looked at Luo Lingxi.

He was about to say something when his gaze was drawn to the small white jade horn hanging around the boy's neck.

"Oh?"

...

The night was deep.

Luo Lingxi had already fallen into a deep sleep.

From beginning to end, he hadn't called out the word "Father."

Luo Chen didn't mind, thinking he was just shy. His entire attention was focused on the small white jade horn he had taken from the boy's neck.

It felt warm and smooth in his hand, its color pure white, but at the tip, a faint seven-colored halo flickered in and out of sight.

Luo Chen played with the small horn, inexplicably thinking of someone.

"Seven Rhinoceros!"

Sima Huiniang draped an outer robe over herself, walked to his side, and said softly, "This was a gift that the Lingtian City Lord sent when he came to inquire about news after you disappeared back then."

"I had the sect's artifact refiners examine it. This object hasn't undergone complex refinement, only having a size-manipulation formation engraved on it."

"Moreover, it emits an auspicious aura, with the effect of suppressing demons and warding off evil, so I've always had Lingxi wear it."

Luo Chen gripped the warm white jade rhinoceros horn.

This was the horn from the head of Seven Rhinoceros, the Great Demon Emperor of the Eastern Wilderness. Even after transforming, he had deliberately kept this characteristic of his original form.

His original body was a seven-colored rhinoceros, truly capable of suppressing demons and warding off evil.

However, back in the Overlord Blade Domain battle, the Lingtian City Lord had personally cut it off. It had once been a glorious deed of the Lingtian City Lord, who rarely made moves.

If this horn were refined into a magic treasure, it would be most effective against yin and evil ghostly things.

He never expected that the other party would give it to his son.

What a generous gift!

Sima Huiniang asked worriedly, "Husband, do you see any hidden problems?"

Problems?

What problems could there be!

Luo Chen's artifact refinement skills were superb. He might not be a grandmaster, but he was at the master level. A simple inspection showed that it only had a size-manipulation formation.

"Let our son wear it. With this rhinoceros horn's protection, the gu insect won't dare to act recklessly."

Sima Huiniang took the white rhinoceros horn and carefully placed it around the sleeping Luo Lingxi's neck.

Sitting by the bed, she lovingly tucked the corner of the blanket in for him.

Motian Cliff was too high, and the mountain was cold. For cultivators living there, it might be fine, but for the young Luo Lingxi, it was somewhat chilly.

Even though there was a formation isolating the cold covering the hall, the boy still curled up his body.

Seeing this, the woman couldn't help but let tears fall.

Turning her head to look at the man, she trembled her lips and said, "Husband, can our child really not cultivate? I asked Lingfengzi to test him in advance. Lingxi has very outstanding water and fire spirit roots. In the future, whether he cultivates water or fire techniques, he will definitely have a promising future."

Luo Chen also looked at the boy who had inherited his eyes.

Although his features weren't very similar, his spirit root attributes had inherited half from each parent. Especially his fire spirit root, which was almost comparable to the fire spirit root nurtured by Luo Chen's Fire Spirit Body.

But...

Luo Chen said calmly, even somewhat coldly, "He cannot cultivate. The Devil Heart Gu is rooted in his body, even fused with his soul. Because of this white rhinoceros horn, he has been free of illness and disaster for the past ten years. But if he once sets foot on the path of cultivation, the white rhinoceros horn will no longer be able to protect him. That is an internal strengthening. The stronger he becomes, the stronger the Devil Heart Gu will grow. In the end, all his cultivation will be devoured by it."

Seeing Sima Huiniang's unwilling expression, Luo Chen shook his head.

"You don't want Lingxi to suffer the same pain you did back then, do you?"

The woman lowered her head and no longer insisted, but tears still couldn't stop flowing.

Luo Chen pulled her head into his embrace, gently stroking her.

"It's fine. Even if he only has a hundred years of life, he can still live a wonderful life."

"Over by Taihu Domain, haven't a group of mortals been relocated there to thrive and multiply? Our Luo Tian Sect must have arrangements there too. When Lingxi is a little older, we'll send him over."

"Whether he becomes a wealthy merchant or an emperor or general, he won't live a life of mediocrity."

The woman remained silent, but in her heart, she did not want to accept this outcome.

She had to find a way to resolve the Devil Heart Gu!

...

Luo Chen's return was quiet and unnoticed.

But as the Luo Tian Sect cultivators began to stir, the news gradually spread.

Adept Elder Gu Caiyi frequently appeared at Motian Cliff, even moving in directly.

Lingfengzi and Wang Yuan also visited Motian Cliff many times.

Especially Sect Master Li Yingzhang, who climbed Motian Cliff seven times. Each time he returned, there were some changes in the Luo Tian Sect.

Those changes were not something Sima Huiniang on Motian Cliff could decide.

Only with the Supreme Elder's permission could he act so boldly.

But the last time, Li Yingzhang's expression was quite unwilling.

Inside the Mystic Dust Hall, Luo Chen stood with his hands behind his back, quietly watching Li Yingzhang descend the mountain.

Gu Caiyi stood beside him and asked curiously, "Husband, why don't you agree to the Luo Tian Sect's large-scale recruitment of disciples?"

Luo Chen asked calmly, "Do you also agree with Li Yingzhang's suggestion?"

Gu Caiyi hesitated for a moment, but still expressed her opinion.

"Our Luo Tian Sect is honored as a Sage Supreme Sect. Within the sect, we have you, Elder Lingfengzi. If we count Wang Yuan, Black King, and Bai Meiling, we can be said to have five Sage-level combat forces. Under our command, we control the vast Luo Tian Domain."

"In terms of mid-level strength, we're not lacking either. Baleful Dragonzi, Qin Yuanjiang, Yang Pingdu, and others are all outstanding among Adept cultivators."

"The only area where we're short-handed is in low-level cultivators."

"If we could open the mountain gates wide and recruit disciples broadly, even if we don't expect thousands of disciples, it would at least greatly help us control the Luo Tian Domain."

"As things stand now, it's truly a bit shabby."

Luo Chen nodded. That was reasonable.

Seeing that he agreed, Gu Caiyi became even more puzzled.

If so, why didn't he agree?

Given her doubts, Li Yingzhang's dissatisfaction, and the voices from all levels of the sect, Luo Chen didn't explain much.

There was one piece of intelligence that many people didn't know.

That was that Rosycloud had awakened!

Although the Ancient Demon Emperor Tianyun had fallen and Qinghe Immortal had been captured, greatly weakening the Eastern Wilderness Demon Clan's strength, Rosycloud's power was beyond imagination.

If she had the intention to unify the Eastern Wilderness Demon Clan, there would be absolutely no resistance now.

If she had the intention to encroach upon the Eastern Wilderness Human Cultivation World, then another bloody storm would surely come.

If that day truly arrived, the Luo Tian Sect, which had just recruited a large number of new people, would not increase its strength but would instead gain burdens.

On the contrary, by not expanding the number of people for now, and instead using the rapid development momentum of the Luo Tian Domain to draw resources from all sides to support the few cultivators of the Luo Tian Sect, their individual strength could become stronger.

Once a great war restarted, the Luo Tian Sect, with fewer people but extremely strong individuals, could come and go like the wind, moving freely.

There would be no need to be forced into the quagmire of war like before.

At worst, they could find a secluded place, take two or three hundred people, and flee far away in one go!

He said calmly, "Wait a little longer!"

"Until when?" Gu Caiyi asked.

"Until the Nether Origin Sect makes external moves, until the Demon Clan's claws retract, until I have the confidence to influence such a war."

Leaving these words, Luo Chen entered the Alchemy-Artifact Hall.

A flying shuttle was quietly floating inside.

It was the Frost Eagle Flying Shuttle.

But unlike before, it was now covered with a dark beast hide.

The hide of the Lightless Demon Emperor!

It had been deliberately kept by Luo Chen back then. Taking advantage of the time now, Luo Chen planned to incorporate it into the Frost Eagle Flying Shuttle.

If successful, this extremely fast flying shuttle would achieve great mastery in stealth and concealment.

By then, even a Sage would find it difficult to detect its movements.

This was an idea Luo Chen had conceived after the Medicine King Domain battle.

When he traveled, his movements were too conspicuous and not secretive enough. If someone wanted to ambush him, they could make arrangements based on the general situation.

But with the Frost Eagle Flying Shuttle's concealment, those petty villains wouldn't be able to accurately track his whereabouts.

He needed such a mount!

The refinement process wasn't difficult, but it was quite time-consuming.

So after returning, Luo Chen had been staying in the Mystic Dust Hall on Motian Cliff.

He didn't need to personally come out to reassure people. The Luo Tian Sect only had two or three hundred people. Once the news spread, they would naturally settle down.

Sima Huiniang, on the other hand, had taken the child down the mountain.

Perhaps she had taken Luo Chen's words to heart. She began to consciously let Luo Lingxi see the outside world, so that when the time came to send the child into the mortal world, he wouldn't resist.

But unexpectedly, after only half a month, Sima Huiniang hurriedly returned with Luo Lingxi.

Learning from Gu Caiyi that Luo Chen was in seclusion refining a magic treasure, she didn't want to force an interruption and just waited.

Until another half month later, when Luo Chen walked out of the Alchemy-Artifact Hall with a tired expression, she greeted him.

"Husband, I've found a way to resolve the Devil Heart Gu!"

Luo Chen was stunned.

That Devil Heart Gu had troubled him for decades. Later, it was only under the suggestion of Sage Heavenly Mountain's letter that he found a makeshift solution.

Huiniang had just gone out once and already had results?

He was about to ask, when suddenly his brow twitched.

"They're here!"

Luo Chen raised his hand to interrupt Sima Huiniang's words, walked out of the Mystic Dust Hall, and looked up at the sky.

Three figures slowly descended in front of the hall.

It had been a month since his return, and the Nether Origin Sect had been slow to make a move. He had thought they had forgotten about him, this Alchemy Grandmaster.

But it turned out they hadn't forgotten. They had simply sent three top-tier experts at once!

"Thunder Daozi!"

"Lingtian City Lord!"

Luo Chen slowly uttered the names of two acquaintances, and finally his gaze fell on the stranger who felt somewhat familiar.

"And this fellow Daoist is?"

Before Lingtian City Lord could introduce him, the young man took the initiative to make a bow to Luo Chen.

"Nether Origin Chongming. Fellow Daoist, have you forgotten the Alchemy Saint's Ascension Ceremony?"

As he spoke, Chongming's body rippled with a strange aura. Water-attributed spiritual energy immediately gathered around him, and even the remaining attributes of heaven and earth spiritual energy were not repelled, but instead surrounded the periphery.

A crude method of manipulating the origin qi of heaven and earth!

Law True Intent!

Luo Chen remembered. This was the Sage who had represented the Nether Origin Holy Land at the Alchemy Saint's Ascension Ceremony back then, when they went to the Central Continent.

Three Sages, including two Grand Cultivators, and two who had comprehended Law True Intent in advance.

Such a lineup to welcome him, Luo Chen—truly an honor!

Smiling slightly, Luo Chen raised his hand in an inviting gesture.

"Please, come inside!"
