# Chapter 1004: Water Origin, Nether Water Giant

On the white jade plaza.

The members of Luo Tian Sect watched the Heavenly Nether Palace with tense hearts.

The great doors were tightly shut, yet the commotion within grew louder and louder, a clear indication of the fierce battle raging inside.

"Does the Supreme Elder truly not need our help?"

Ye Suifeng, who hailed from Misty Peak, asked with concern.

Jueqing the Heartless hesitated before answering, "It shouldn't be necessary, right?"

Their cultivation was merely at the Adept stage. In the eyes of Adept-stage cultivators, all Sages were unattainable existences. Thus, it was difficult for them to discern just how vast the gap between one Sage and another truly was.

The only thing clearly visible was the numerical disparity between the two sides in combat.

Excluding the five Ghost Kings that Luo Chen had already collected into his Soul-Refining Banner outside the palace, the current battle situation inside was one against twenty-five!

No!

If you added the Nether Ghost King who had appeared later, it was one against twenty-six.

Moreover, given Luo Chen's clearly stated attitude, there was no room for division or cooperation here. It was a straightforward one-against-many battle.

Could this really be accomplished?

Several late-stage Adept cultivators, amidst the unbearable wait, unconsciously turned their hopeful gazes toward the few Sages present.

Lingfengzi comforted his own disciple. "Since the Supreme Elder dares to act this way, he must have his reasons."

Bai Meiling floated beside Gu Caiyi and whispered, "Your master's current capabilities are far beyond what you can imagine, Caiyi. Rest assured!"

Canglong said nothing, merely nodding briefly to Jueqing the Heartless to signal her to relax.

As for Min Longyu, under Deng Taiyue's assistance, he had begun setting up formations at the only entrance to the plaza.

This way, even if any ghost cultivators managed to escape from inside, they couldn't bypass them and flee directly.

Time passed, minute by minute. The battle within the Heavenly Nether Palace grew increasingly fierce.

What manifested externally was the occasional leakage of overbearing dharma power and surging ghostly qi.

And as this comparison continued, with the familiar dharma power aura growing ever stronger and the ghostly qi gradually dissipating, it signified that the battle was approaching its end.

Whoosh...

The ghostly qi suddenly withdrew entirely, leaving only that one aura emanating from the Heavenly Nether Palace.

Everyone held their breath in anticipation.

"Did my husband win?"

"Has Senior Brother Luo Chen overpowered all the ghost cultivators?"

"I can sense the Soul-Refining Banner. It seems the master has collected all the Ghost Kings inside!"

"Is that really true?"

Faced with everyone's gazes, Bai Meiling nodded emphatically.

Beside her, Deng Taiyue also let out a long breath and praised, "Truly worthy of Brother Luo! Even if the Demon Emperor Azure Frost were here in person, I fear such a feat would be impossible to achieve!"

With these two offering their final verdict, everyone finally set their anxious hearts at ease.

But the next moment!

Deng Taiyue suddenly looked up in alarm, staring at the top of the Heavenly Nether Palace.

A jet-black water column had instantly pierced through the palace's dome.

This was the traveling palace of a Ascendant-level powerhouse! Even the chaotic battle between more than twenty Sage-level existences earlier had failed to breach the Heavenly Nether Palace!

And now, it had been forcibly pierced by a single water column?

What was most terrifying was that the residual force of the black water column was not yet exhausted. It shot straight into the sky, instantly breaking through the remaining restrictions on Mount Yanfu.

In that instant, an endless torrent of Nether Water poured down from the Eastern Nether Abyss outside.

This scene was like the end of the world!

If that were all, it might still be bearable. But Deng Taiyue sensed a complex, unknown energy—far more powerful than spiritual qi—pouring down from above the Eastern Nether Abyss.

"It's the origin qi of heaven and earth!"

Deng Taiyue's expression changed drastically.

He had once entered a state of enlightenment regarding the true meaning of laws and had briefly sensed the existence of the world's origin power.

Although his enlightenment had failed and he had not comprehended the true meaning of any law, he would never mistake that complex and powerful energy.

Had Luo Chen summoned the origin qi of heaven and earth?

He knew that Luo Chen had grasped the true meaning of laws back then and could draw upon the origin qi of heaven and earth for his own use.

But since victory and defeat had already been decided, why was he still making such a move?

Could it be that there were still enemies alive?

At this thought, he couldn't help but shout, "Everyone, be on guard!"

...

Inside the Heavenly Nether Palace.

Luo Chen stared fixedly at the being that had crawled out from the dark pool.

A pitch-black humanoid figure, its entire body shimmering with rippling light, as if transparent.

A face was constantly twisting and contorting within it.

"It's the Nether Ghost King!"

"Has he already refined the Nether Water Spring Eye?"

Countless thoughts flashed through Luo Chen's mind, but he quickly made a judgment.

"His refinement has failed. He's even beginning to be assimilated!"

A heavenly treasure like the Nether Water Spring Eye was not something that could be easily refined.

Even the Nether Origin Sect, at its peak, had only managed to confine it with formations and Mount Yanfu, occasionally borrowing a fraction of its power.

A mere late-fourth-stage Nether Ghost King could never refine it quickly.

This was also why Luo Chen had not been anxious earlier.

But what he hadn't expected was that although the Nether Ghost King's refinement wasn't fast, his failure had come far sooner than imagined.

Now, having been assimilated by the Nether Water Spring Eye, it was instead controlling his body.

"This is fine too. It saves me the trouble of going deep into the dark pool to retrieve the spring eye."

A brilliant light flashed in Luo Chen's eyes as he began forming powerful techniques once more!

Azure Yang Great Handprint!

Boom!

The handprint was unleashed instantly, three strikes in succession, heavily slamming into the Nether Ghost King.

The Nether Ghost King seemed to retain some remnant will, instinctively raising a water curtain to block.

But the three overlapping seals shattered it in an instant.

Seizing the advantage, Luo Chen pressed his attack. The Fire Phoenix and Flame Dragon intertwined, roaring forth.

Screech!

Roar!

Amidst the shrieks and bellows, the Nether Ghost King was severely wounded in an instant, his body beginning to disintegrate continuously.

A thing resembling an eyeball, or perhaps a luminous pearl, appeared on his chest.

"That's the Nether Water Spring Eye!"

Luo Chen's eyes lit up. He reached out with his Cloud-Seizing Divine Claw, grasping through the air.

As if realizing that once the Nether Water Spring Eye was stripped away, he would die, the Nether Ghost King let out an enraged roar.

Boundless Nether Water poured into his entire body, drawing in the origin qi of heaven and earth.

"ROAR!"

A giant hand expanded wildly, not only swatting away the Cloud-Seizing Divine Claw but also slapping directly toward Luo Chen.

Luo Chen tried to dodge, but in that instant, he felt a trace of restraint.

In that single moment, the giant hand struck.

Boom!

Caught off guard, Luo Chen was sent flying, crashing directly into the great doors.

Under the immense force of the impact, the hastily closed doors were instantly smashed through.

The next moment, Deng Taiyue and the others saw a figure fly out of the Heavenly Nether Palace like an arrow loosed from a bowstring, slamming straight into the white jade plaza.

The figure skidded backward, crouching low, one hand deeply embedded in the plaza floor beneath him.

In this manner, he flew backward for several li, carving a deep trench, before the residual momentum gradually faded.

Everyone looked up and couldn't help but cry out in alarm.

"Brother Luo!"

"Supreme Elder!"

"Husband!"

Different voices, all expressing the same concern.

But at this moment, Luo Chen paid them no heed.

Origin power circulated, and the wounds on his body healed in an instant.

The Pingshan Crown trembled, and the shattered protective light curtain descended once more.

He raised his head, staring fixedly at the location of the Heavenly Nether Palace.

Everyone held their breath, looking in that direction as well.

"Slap!"

A massive hand landed on the previously shattered doorway.

Then, another hand gripped the opposite side.

With a heave, the great doors of the Heavenly Nether Palace were torn open.

At the same time, a rumbling voice echoed from within the palace.

The next moment, everyone's jaws dropped in disbelief as they witnessed a staggering sight.

A giant, its entire body composed of Nether Water, forcibly tore open the dome of the Heavenly Nether Palace and slowly stood up.

Its feet planted on the ground, its head touching the sky.

Towering a thousand zhang high, it looked down upon the world.

Endless Nether Water swirled around it like ribbons, and a vast, ocean-like aura slowly spread out.

Before it, Luo Chen was as insignificant as a grain of sand.

But Luo Chen's spine was ramrod straight. His previously gloomy expression had instead become much calmer.

His eyes fixed on this Nether Water Giant, a thoughtful look appearing on his face.

Using Nether Water as a body, commanding the origin qi of heaven and earth—it was very much like a sentient being.

Similar to those formed from source-less fire.

But the question was, was this giant primarily controlled by the Nether Ghost King's soul, or was it under the command of the Nether Water Spring Eye?

Judging by its displayed strength, it was nearly touching the threshold of the Ascendant stage. Yet, the feeling it gave him was one of external strength but internal weakness.

The sounds of concern from behind reached him. Luo Chen waved his hand without turning his head.

"You all go down the mountain first!"

"Are you sure you don't need our help?"

Luo Chen shook his head. "You can't intervene in a battle of this level. Joining in will only distract me. Don't worry. If things become untenable, I will take the initiative to retreat. Brother Deng, take them away!"

Having said this, Luo Chen stopped suppressing himself.

He pointed a finger at his chest, and vast origin power instantly circulated throughout his body.

The next moment, a thousand-zhang giant also rose up from the ground.

Not only that, but it also manifested the majestic aspect of three heads and six arms.

The three-headed giant and the Nether Water Giant locked eyes. Without restraint, they took great strides and charged toward each other.

The earth-shaking sound of their running drowned out almost everything else.

Deng Taiyue watched this scene with a horrified expression and shouted, "Go down the mountain first!"

Lingfengzi didn't hesitate. He released the Nether-Crossing Ship and called for everyone to board.

This way, they could avoid being eroded by the Nether Water.

The group quickly retreated down the mountain, but their gazes remained fixed on the summit of Mount Yanfu.

There, the two giants had already clashed. Their moves were simple and direct, yet the destructive power they unleashed was rarely seen in the world.

Someone couldn't help but groan, "Is this truly something human strength can achieve?"

No one paid attention to this muttering. Everyone's focus was on the pure physical brawl above.

From the very first exchange, Luo Chen gained the upper hand!

Their hands locked together as they wrestled for leverage. Luo Chen immediately gained the advantage, pushing his opponent back step by step.

His other four hands reached out from various angles, forming fists, palms, claws, and fingers.

With overwhelming strength, he began to dismantle the Nether Water Giant's body.

"It's just a paper tiger!"

Thoughts flashed through Luo Chen's mind as another punch shattered the water shield enveloping the Nether Ghost King.

But soon, he noticed something was wrong.

As vast amounts of Nether Water poured into Mount Yanfu, his physical body began to suffer corruption, while his opponent's Nether Water body was continuously replenished and restored.

With this shift in momentum, the slight advantage he had gained was instantly neutralized.

Even the physical wrestling, which he had dominated, began to reverse.

It felt as if he wasn't just facing a single water giant, but an entire abyss, a vast ocean.

"Can the Nether Water Spring Eye truly control the entire Eastern Nether Abyss?"

A fierce glint flashed in Luo Chen's eyes. All six arms struck out simultaneously.

Boom!

The Nether Ghost King, who had been about to resist, was directly knocked to the ground.

Even if mountains stood in his way, even if oceans lay before him, he, Luo Chen, could overturn mountains and drain seas!

A giant foot stomped down.

The Nether Ghost King crossed his arms before his chest, blocking the stomp, and even had the strength left to push Luo Chen away.

His power was still growing!

Luo Chen's expression remained unchanged. His six hands each formed seals.

Fire serpents danced wildly, a blazing sun rose high, and the Flame Dragon and Fire Phoenix emerged once more, all charging together at the forcibly rising Nether Ghost King.

His opponent's eyes were wild as he summoned a water mirror.

All the spell techniques that fell into it vanished without a trace, like mud oxen entering the sea.

Not only that, but streams of water ribbons transformed into jiaolongs, roaring as they bit toward Luo Chen.

Luo Chen allowed these jiaolongs to bite into his arms and legs, his eyes surveying the surroundings.

"The Nether Water Spring Eye seems to contain the power of water laws. Within the Eastern Nether Abyss, it's its home ground. My fire origin is heavily suppressed, and even my usually invincible high-level fire spells are less than half their former power."

A wisp of cyan fire robe spread from his chest, covering his entire body.

In an instant, the Nether Water jiaolongs biting into him were incinerated.

With the immediate threat gone, Luo Chen stepped back, pondering a way to break this deadlock.

Water inherently restrains fire. The solution was either to overpower it by a full level or to seek another path of mutual restraint through the Five Phases.

Earth techniques?

He knew some, but ordinary earth spells couldn't threaten this opponent.

If mutual restraint wouldn't work, then he would take the path of mutual generation!

With this thought, the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire blazed even higher.

"I can't let it keep growing without limit!"

Luo Chen had already made up his mind. His other five hands casually scattered something.

Swish, swish, swish, swish, swish!

Five streams of light scattered in all directions. In an instant, sword qi shot into the sky, crisscrossing and slicing through the giant's body.

The Grand Five Elements Sword Formation, its strength lay in cyclical suppression.

This sharp, unparalleled cutting force originated from the sword pills themselves, from the top-grade Nine Yang Geng Metal.

No Sage could withstand this power.

The Nether Ghost King was no exception!

The moment the Grand Five Elements Sword Formation appeared, wounds began to appear on his body continuously.

But it was too slow.

As more Nether Water poured down from the Eastern Nether Abyss, the speed of his body's recovery completely outstripped the speed at which the sword formation was cutting and destroying him.

If this stalemate continued, the outcome was obvious.

But Luo Chen no longer wanted to drag it out!

His remaining hand began forming seals and chanting incantations, seemingly slow yet actually fast.

After about five breaths, Luo Chen's movements suddenly froze. He pointed his finger like a sword, directing it toward the Nether Ghost King.

"Withering-Bloom Dao Finger!"

The divine ability appeared once more, instantly unleashing boundless power.

This was a high-level true meaning of laws within the wood element. It was not suppressed by water; on the contrary, due to the mutual generation of the Five Phases, it became even more powerful.

Coupled with the vast amount of deathly stillness qi left over from the previous battle, this finger surpassed all its previous uses!

The Nether Ghost King's massive body had no time to dodge this strike. It was immediately grievously wounded.

Its body stiffened. The wounds originally cut by the Grand Five Elements Sword Formation began to spread wildly at a visible rate.

Even with a sea of Nether Water pouring in, it could not recover quickly.

Not only that!

In the distance, a blood sword materialized before Luo Chen.

He reached out and grasped it. The Yuantu Sword expanded to a thousand zhang.

"Slash!"

The giant sword fell, splitting heaven and earth.

The Nether Water Giant uttered its first words since the battle began.

"No!"

But it was all too late.

The colossal giant, under the enhancement of multiple techniques, was instantly split in two.

Luo Chen opened his mouth and spat out a golden light that shot through the air.

It was the Soul-Weaving Needle he had obtained from the Hundred-Legged Demon Emperor.

The golden needle pierced through the Nether Ghost King's body, stabbing deeply into his soul.

Before he died, Luo Chen clearly saw a flash of clarity in his eyes.

A great Ghost King, attempting to forcibly refine the Nether Water Spring Eye with a ghostly body condensed from Nether Water True Essence, had instead ended up being assimilated.

Whether to call it overreaching or pitiable was hard to say.

But Luo Chen had no time for such thoughts, because a water droplet was spinning and rising from the ground.

Like an eyeball, like a luminous pearl—it was the Nether Water Spring Eye!

As long as he mastered it, he would have the opportunity to control the Eastern Nether Abyss!

Luo Chen's eyes lit up. His giant hand grabbed at it!

But unexpectedly, the moment the spring eye entered his hand, a strange power directly pierced through his Withering-Bloom Fire Robe, blasting a large hole in his palm.

The spring eye spun in the air, then tried to fly away.

With such a treasure before him, how could Luo Chen let it go?

"Stay right here!"

Clang!

The Primordial Unity Cauldron soared into the air.

His six arms struck it simultaneously, producing a deafening sound.

One by one, chains extended from the void. Their number went from one to nine, then instantly exceeded the limit—ten, a hundred, a thousand, countless.

In that instant, Luo Chen's lifebound treasure sealed off this region of space. The immense suppressive force slowed the Nether Water Spring Eye's movements.

It seemed to possess sentience. Unwilling to submit, its body trembled with dark light.

Vast Nether Water surged forward, attempting to drown the treasure's master.

Luo Chen was fearless. He opened his mouth and spat.

Golden Nirvana Sacred Flame emerged, mutually canceling out with the vast Nether Water, continuously dissolving it.

The contest of strength began once more!

This time, Luo Chen was truly contending against this entire Eastern Nether Abyss.

(Chapter End)
