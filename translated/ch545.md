# Chapter 545: The Seven Feiyun Cultivators, Han Zhan Acts

"Seventh Elder, are we truly going to let that thief bird go?"

In the deep seabed, three demon crabs raised their heads toward the sky, their gazes seeming to pierce through endless seawater and see the scene outside.

These three demons were extremely ugly.

Compound eyes covered their bodies, threads of white markings coiled around their carapaces, and the crab pincers lying on the ground were covered in sharp, hideous fuzz.

It was precisely the standard appearance of Demon Spider Crabs.

The three demons on the seabed were not large, but the powerful auras they emitted were truly frightening.

They were clearly third-tier demon king existences!

And the fact that they deliberately shrank their bodies was not without reason.

This trip was aimed specifically at the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull!

Over these five years, in the Xuanyan Sea Region, second-tier demon crabs from the various crab races that ran rampant there had repeatedly gone missing.

Later, it was investigated and found that an evil foreign jiaolong was feeding by hunting demon crabs.

For this, the Azure Emperor Crab race had acted several times, and even the Overlord Crab race had once, under the lead of First Elder Golden Claw[TERM?], tried to capture and kill the evil jiaolong.

It was precisely that time that revealed the evil jiaolong was not isolated and helpless.

It had actually joined hands with the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull in a strange way, forming an offensive and defensive alliance.

One had to know, the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull was a natural enemy of the Black-Scale Jiaolong!

From then on, capturing and killing the Black-Scale Jiaolong became increasingly difficult, and the other party clearly grew even more cunning.

At the same time, their actions became more and more rampant. Not only the Black-Scale Jiaolong, even the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull joined the reckless hunting of demon crabs.

The two of them came and went unpredictably in the Xuanyan Sea Region, running amok without restraint. One jiaolong and one gull, present across sea, land, and sky, truly made it impossible for crabs to guard against them.

If such unbridled violence had occurred in past years, they would long ago have been encircled and killed by the five great royal races working together.

Yet strangely, the strongest Overlord Crab race, after acting only once, no longer paid attention to Black Scale and the others.

And the Azure Emperor Crab race, which had suffered the greatest losses, inexplicably sealed off its clan land and reduced the activity range of its members.

As for the other three races, the Red Rock Crabs lived in deep-sea volcanic trenches and rarely moved in the shallows.

The Nine-Clawed Poison King Crabs ignored this completely, because from beginning to end, Black Scale and the others had not made any moves against Poison King Crabs.

Instead, it was their Demon Spider Crab race, infamous as they were, that had been targeted by that evil jiaolong and thief bird!

Not only second-tier clansmen, they did not even spare the first-tier ones!

Such violent acts, beneath clear heaven's justice, could not be endured by anyone.

Therefore, the three great demon kings of the Demon Spider Crab race had jointly set out, laying an ambush in order to kill the evil jiaolong and thief bird!

However, the affairs of the world were impermanent, and plans could never keep up with changes.

After they used one clansman as bait to lure the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull into taking the bait, an accident appeared.

Before they had time to act,

a group of human cultivators suddenly arrived in this sea region.

The instant this group of human cultivators saw the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull, they directly acted and began attacking it.

At the seabed, the leading Demon Spider Crab's expression was full of alarm and uncertainty.

Looking at the battle on the sea surface, he blinked and said, "This Fighting Gull cannot escape."

The two demons beside him did not understand his meaning.

"That is only an early Core Formation human cultivator. Perhaps he can entangle it for a while, but killing the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull is probably beyond his ability."

Seventh Elder shook his head. "No. Your realms are weaker, so you cannot sense the true situation. They are not trying to kill this Fighting Gull. They want to capture it alive."

Capture it alive!

Once these words came out, the two companions looked at one another.

An early Core Formation cultivator wanted to capture alive a Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull with strong combat strength and extremely fast flight speed? Was that not wishful thinking?

Seeing this, Seventh Elder quietly explained, "That great ship is shrouded by a powerful formation, and there is an even stronger existence inside. Since the other party is not acting now, clearly he holds victory in his grasp. Forget this Fighting Gull. Even if the three of us acted, I fear we would not gain the slightest benefit."

The other two demons were dumbfounded.

The two of them might be lacking, but Seventh Elder was a genuine mid third-tier existence.

With the three of them joining hands, and with the talents of the Demon Spider Crab race, even against human cultivators famed for many methods and endless schemes, as long as they were not late Core Formation, they were enough to fight.

Could it be...

The two demons looked at one another, faintly forming a guess.

The Demon Spider Crab race was already famed for sharp perception, and Seventh Elder had once had a fortuitous encounter in his early years, making his perception even stronger.

It was very possible that Seventh Elder had sensed an existence on the great ship that even made him afraid.

"Then what do we do now?"

Seventh Elder was also somewhat hesitant. After pondering for a moment, he said, "Let us keep watching for now. If that Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull truly escapes, it will not be too late for us to chase and kill it. If it is captured, its end will not be good either. It will either be killed for materials, or reduced to a mount under a cultivator's crotch. In that way, it can also be considered us borrowing a knife to kill, avenging our low-tier Demon Spider Crab clansmen. Moreover, once this Fighting Gull is gone, the other evil jiaolong will have no one to look after it, and killing it will be as easy as flipping a hand."

His words came slowly, clear and orderly.

If one did not know better, one might even think he was a human cultivator famed for intelligence.

After hearing him, the other two demons accepted Seventh Elder's plan with sincere admiration.

Putting aside everything else, within their Demon Spider Crab race, Seventh Elder was famed for schemes and had often provided many plans for the race's development.

This time, if not for human cultivators interfering, they could have steadily killed the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull.

Listening to him would definitely not be wrong!

...

On the sea surface.

A colossal great ship stretched across the sea.

Its sails were half-raised, and figures moved indistinctly upon it.

Even with a formation concealing it, several figures still revealed astonishing auras, making people fear to look.

Among the crowd, six people stood at the head.

And among those six, a man and woman in the center seemed faintly to be the core.

Under this arrangement of stars surrounding the moon, the young female cultivator looked at the battle between one person and one bird in the sky, a trace of worry faintly appearing on her delicate face.

"Elder Brother, can Sixth Senior Brother truly capture this Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull?"

The richly dressed man standing beside her laughed softly. "Although Sixth Junior Brother has decent talent, his years of cultivation are short after all. With his ability, entangling this beast is already his limit. As for capturing it alive, heh..."

The unspoken words were understood by everyone.

After hearing this, the young female cultivator said anxiously, "Since that is so, why not have Sixth Senior Brother stop, lest he injure himself?"

The richly dressed man looked tenderly at his little sister. She was simply too kind-hearted.

"Sixth Junior Brother wishes to use battle to temper the method he recently cultivated. Naturally, I must give him this chance. Moreover..."

At this point, he rubbed the female cultivator's head.

"Little Sister, after you formed your core, I was busy helping Father with matters and still have not given you a congratulatory gift."

"This Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull has an excellent appearance, and its bloodline is presumably also very pure. As a Core Formation gift, whether Little Sister uses it as a travel honor guard or a dao-protecting spirit beast[TERM?], it will be a beautiful thing."

The young female cultivator froze. Elder Brother actually wanted to capture that demon beast alive as a gift for her?

For a time, warmth could not help filling her heart.

The four Core Formation cultivators beside them also smiled and spoke.

"Junior Sister Xiaolian[TERM?], don't worry about Sixth Junior Brother. With us here, nothing will go wrong."

"Exactly. Even if you do not trust Sixth Junior Brother or us, you should at least trust Eldest Senior Brother!"

"In the vast Demon Sea, who does not know that our Eldest Senior Brother Gu Shaoshang[TERM?] has outstanding talent and is one of the heaven's chosen most likely to advance to the Sage stage? Forget an early third-tier Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull. Even if a late third-tier great demon king stood before him, Eldest Senior Brother could capture it alive!"

"Eldest Senior Brother, will you act?"

After this round of flattering words, Gu Xiaolian's worried mood was indeed soothed.

Gu Xiaolian also could not help looking toward her elder brother. "Elder Brother, are you going to act?"

In recent years, Gu Shaoshang's dao attainment had advanced, and after reaching Core Formation Stage Seven at two hundred years old, he had rarely acted.

Thinking of the series of battle achievements he had made after Core Formation during the hundred years of upheaval following the Primordial Demon Sect's destruction, everyone was very curious whether his methods had become even more powerful after advancing to late Core Formation.

Facing the worshipful gazes of the group looking at him, the corners of Gu Shaoshang's mouth rose slightly.

His purple brocade robes drifted and swayed.

He slowly lifted his hands.

"The strength of this Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull has already been proven."

"The purpose of this trip is still to collect large numbers of Ring-Headed Turtle shells for Father. For this, all of us Seven Feiyun Cultivators[TERM?] have moved out. We cannot delay here too long."

"Since that is so, let us close the net!"

The moment his words fell,

his two hands suddenly clenched.

At the same time, between heaven and earth, thread after thread abruptly appeared.

Their range was vast, encompassing the hundred li of sky and earth around them. Even as mana extended, it still seemed to have energy left over!

Seeing those threads, the hundreds of cultivators on the enormous demon-hunting ship bearing the Feiyun Ravine[TERM?] emblem all revealed cheers.

It was Heavenly Silk Cloud Threads[TERM?]!

One of the early famous magic treasures of Feiyun Ravine's ancestor Feiyunzi[TERM?].

Its grade was high-grade!

After he advanced to the Sage Great Dao, he bestowed this treasure upon his own son, Gu Shaoshang.

Gu Shaoshang had also not let this treasure's prestige fall.

Along the way, this magic treasure had shone brilliantly in many battles.

Gu Xiaolian looked at this scene, and worship could not help appearing in her eyes.

"No wonder Elder Brother was unhurried before. So he had long since used the Heavenly Silk Cloud Threads to lay a heaven-encompassing, earth-netting snare."

"This battle principle truly coincides with Father's occasional teaching that in all matters, calculations come first."

"Now that I have also formed my core, I cannot rely on Father and Elder Brother's protection in everything from now on. This time hunting Ring-Headed Turtles, I must properly temper my combat methods and make a name for myself!"

While she secretly swore in her heart, Gu Shaoshang surged with majestic mana, controlling the boundless Heavenly Silk Cloud Threads with both hands as they closed in.

When they passed by a middle-aged man, they revealed an opening, allowing him to remain outside the threads.

Only the great bird in the center, over ten zhang wide, was wrapped within.

"Li!"

A sharp bird cry resounded through the clouds. Razor-sharp wind blades erupted from the great bird, trying to cut through the net of threads.

But wherever they passed, the Heavenly Silk Cloud Threads were incomparably tough.

Seeing this, the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull's two wings trembled wildly. Wind vortexes formed beneath its wings one after another, finally transforming into a tornado piercing the heavens.

Gu Shaoshang snorted coldly. "A turtle in a jar, resisting stubbornly at the end!"

Immediately, the Heavenly Silk Cloud Threads erupted with layers of spiritual light, actually cutting the heaven-piercing tornado into scattered pieces. The encirclement's momentum was unstoppable.

Rumble!

On the huge deck,

a handsome azure bird, its whole body covered in blood, lay dispiritedly upon it.

Cheers immediately rang out.

Voices calling Eldest Senior Brother mighty rang without end.

Gu Xiaolian's eyes were also full of worship.

Only when she looked at the miserable Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull did she reveal a look of pity.

"Elder Brother, since she has already been captured, why not loosen the net first? Her beautiful feathers have all been cut."

Gu Shaoshang shook his head and smiled. "My little sister is too kind-hearted. That is not good for walking in the cultivation world."

Although he said this, he had already put away the Heavenly Silk Cloud Threads.

The Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull on the deck tried to struggle, but a talisman drifted onto its forehead, forcibly preventing it from circulating even half a trace of demon power.

After finishing this, Gu Shaoshang casually said, "If you want to subdue this demon as a mount, forcing it will only make it a fighter. If you want it to be of one mind with you, you must tame it personally. Little Sister, I will first hand this Demon-Suppressing Talisman[TERM?] to you for safekeeping."

Gu Xiaolian excitedly accepted another yellow talisman, exactly identical to the one on the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull's forehead.

It was also at this moment!

Gu Shaoshang's expression changed instantly. He abruptly struck a palm toward a black cloud in the sky.

"Who is there, hiding head and showing tail, sneaking around like a ghost?"

"Get down here!"

Amid his fierce shout, there was no response from the sky.

There was only ghost qi spiraling through the air, black clouds pressing down on the sea!

Over a thousand Ghost Generals surged out.

Two giants with illusory bodies a hundred zhang tall stepped out from the black clouds, their huge feet treading toward the palm Gu Shaoshang had struck.

When the two made contact, rumbling shockwaves erupted.

"Myriad Souls Banner!"

"It is a remnant evil of the Primordial Demon Sect!"

"Fellow disciples, beware sneak attacks!"

In an instant, Gu Shaoshang recognized the newcomer's methods and issued warnings one after another.

But those black cloud methods were too fast. At the moment they erupted, they had already wrapped the demon-hunting ship layer upon layer.

Beneath the black mist filling the sky, ghostly shadows were everywhere.

The seven Core Formation cultivators of Feiyun Ravine all acted, slaying one second-tier Ghost General after another.

Gu Shaoshang displayed even greater divine might, repeatedly shattering the two Ghost King-level main souls within the Myriad Souls Banner.

High in the nine heavens, Han Zhan looked at this scene, approval appearing in his eyes.

"Worthy of being a late Core Formation great cultivator who made a name in the Demon Sea. He does indeed have some methods."

"If we fought to the death, with my current methods, perhaps it would be fifty-fifty."

"Unfortunately, this old man's purpose is not killing."

As he muttered, he issued an order.

The next moment,

in the deep sea, an enormous shadow suddenly swam over.

Facing the huge ship above, it opened its great mouth.

A terrifying devouring force erupted with a boom.

This method came so abruptly that it was impossible to guard against.

Large sheets of deck were swept into the sea with a crash, and there was even a trend of dragging the ship into the deep sea.

But its target was very clearly not the great ship.

It was the demon bird on the deck.

Thump!

The demon bird fell into the sea and was swept by that shadow into the seabed.

Just as it escaped, the majestic black mist enveloping the Feiyun Ravine demon-hunting ship also immediately dispersed completely.

Under clear sky and clean air, the great sea was boundlessly blue, as if nothing had happened at all.

Gu Shaoshang descended onto the great ship, his face gloomy, divine sense spreading in all directions.

"How bold!"

This time, he had truly drawn water with a bamboo basket and ended with nothing.

...

On Heavenly Pivot Island.

One jiaolong and one gull landed in sorry straits.

A soul banner drifted into the depths of the island.

Before the Black-Scale Jiaolong had time to mock Heavenly Pivot, it saw a figure appear before them like a ghost.

It raised its head and looked. White robes drifted, expression indifferent.

A terrifying aura rose and fell without cease, as if not yet completely stabilized.

It was precisely his master, Luo Chen.

"Master, I..."

Before he could finish speaking, Luo Chen lifted a hand and interrupted them.

Facing the shallow sea ahead, filled with hidden reefs, Luo Chen coldly spoke.

"Three Fellow Daoists, coming uninvited is not the way to be a guest!"

Splash...

The seawater parted, and three figures entered his eyes!

Compound eyes, spider silk, crab pincers, white markings.

They were precisely Demon Spider Crabs!

(End of chapter)
