# Chapter 996: Number One in the Eastern Wilderness, Son of the Ancient Demon

With the retreat of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect and the conclusion of the human-demon war, the Eastern Wilderness, which had been turbulent for two hundred years, finally welcomed a rare period of peace.

Although no one knew when the demon race from the Million Great Mountains would strike again, cultivators throughout the Eastern Wilderness all seized the time to recuperate and rebuild.

The Nine Spirits Sect was no exception.

Ever since they had taken root in the Joyous Union Domain, they had been cultivating tirelessly, improving their own strength while doing their utmost to restore the sect's vitality.

Rebuilding the mountain gate, sorting out spirit veins, opening up spirit fields, mining mineral deposits...

Under the command of the sect's knowledgeable members, everything was proceeding in an orderly fashion.

Compared to other Sage Sects, the Nine Spirits Sect's recovery progress was much faster.

There were many reasons for this, but the most important one was something the entire sect agreed upon.

Because they had a vastly powerful ancestor—Nine Spirits Primordial Lord!

In his youth, Nine Spirits Ancestor had not been particularly famous. He had taken over the sect's leadership steadily and quietly, only revealing his true strength during the first human-demon war.

At the mid-Sage stage, his boundless magical power was no less than that of an ordinary Grand Cultivator.

He had demonstrated his capabilities in several major battles.

And when the second human-demon war arrived, while the overall strength of high-level cultivators in the Eastern Wilderness had declined by a level, Nine Spirits Ancestor had risen abruptly, becoming a pillar of the Eastern Wilderness.

Especially!

Some high-level members of the sect knew, more or less, a piece of news.

"Nine Spirits Ancestor has successfully entered the late-Sage stage!"

This news was like a calming pill, a sea-suppressing divine needle, settling their hearts.

In the current era, ordinary Sages could barely protect themselves, let alone shelter a sect.

Only Grand Cultivators had the spare strength to safeguard a sect's rise and fall.

Look at the Divine Talisman Sect—the day their ancestor passed away in meditation, the entire sect was scattered by wind and rain, falling into complete decline.

But their Nine Spirits Ancestor had now become one of the three great Grand Cultivators of the Eastern Wilderness!

Even if the demon race were to strike again, as long as no Ascendant ancient demon was involved, Nine Spirits Ancestor would be enough to determine the outcome of a battle.

Because of this, the Nine Spirits Sect had let loose, massively increasing production to restore the sect's vitality as much as possible.

One day.

Low-level cultivators opening up spirit fields at the foot of Nine Origin Peak suddenly felt the earth shake and mountains tremble, unable to stand steady.

They looked up in shock towards the peak.

They saw the clouds and mist that surrounded Nine Origin Peak all year round, never dispersing, now being stirred as if by an invisible giant hand, spreading outward from within.

The mysterious Nine Origin Peak revealed its truest appearance.

"Is the ancestor fighting?"

"An enemy!"

"An enemy has invaded!"

For a moment, the disciples cried out loudly.

Elsewhere in the mountain gate, some high-level cultivators also sensed the anomaly.

One by one, Initiate and Adept cultivators grabbed their magical treasures and rushed towards Nine Origin Peak.

Even a newly advanced Sage, who had been in seclusion consolidating his realm, suddenly awakened and unhesitatingly charged towards Nine Origin Peak.

The ancestor was the sect's most important treasure; the rise and fall of the Nine Spirits Sect all rested on him alone. He must not be harmed!

No matter who was bold enough to penetrate deep into the sect to assassinate the ancestor, they must not be allowed to succeed!

For a moment, thousands of fleeing lights surged forward with fervent emotion.

But when they reached the foot of the mountain, they only saw the ancestor standing at the peak, facing the wind with a composed posture, showing no sign of having been in battle.

"Elder Brother!"

"Ancestor!"

"Master, are you alright?"

"Where is the enemy?"

Amidst the clamor, Nine Spirits Primordial Lord waved his hand.

"All of you, withdraw!"

"Changxi, stay!"

The disciples did not understand, but they obeyed the ancestor's command and slowly dispersed.

Only the Nine Spirits Sect's newly advanced female Sage obediently remained.

She hesitated, then spread her divine sense to perceive the surroundings.

But after a preliminary scan, her face showed shock.

On Nine Origin Peak, there lingered another powerful and domineering magical power, one that was in no way inferior to her senior brother Nine Spirits!

She couldn't help but speak: "Senior Brother, who was that just now?"

Nine Spirits Primordial Lord smiled faintly. "Just an old acquaintance. No need to be alarmed."

Changxi murmured the words "old acquaintance," her face showing a mix of belief and doubt.

In the Eastern Wilderness today, which cultivator could have such strength?

Her senior brother was now one of only three Grand Cultivators left in the Eastern Wilderness. The only ones who could compare with him were the Great Elder of the Eastern Sun Sect and Tiangouzi of the Heavenly Capital Sect.

She racked her brains for a long time but couldn't find anyone who fit such a realm.

In fact, in her view, even if someone had the same cultivation level as her senior brother, in terms of magical power attributes, few could clash head-on with his overbearing Great Nine Heavens magical power.

Overbearing?

At this thought, a name popped into Changxi's mind.

That one was also famous for his overbearing magical power!

Seeing her look of dawning understanding, Nine Spirits Primordial Lord chuckled lightly.

"It seems you've guessed."

Changxi whispered, "That one... has returned?"

"Mm."

Nine Spirits Primordial Lord nodded but did not say more.

He turned his wrist, and a thin booklet appeared before him.

"Take it. That old acquaintance came in a hurry and was somewhat hard up. To congratulate you on achieving the Sage stage, he could only offer a cultivation insight. You are currently in the stage of consolidating your realm. With this book's help, you may save several years of bitter cultivation."

Changxi's eyes lit up as she took the nameless book.

Suppressing the excitement in her heart, she asked casually, "Senior Brother, what is that one's current strength? The rumors say he defeated three great demon emperors, killed the Five Elements Divine Origin, and even fought to a standstill with the number one under the Ascendant stage. But he's not even a Grand Cultivator. Are the rumors wrong?"

"Wrong?"

Nine Spirits Primordial Lord laughed, a hint of admiration in his smile.

He had always prided himself on having the greatest magical power at the same cultivation level. At the mid-Sage stage, he had dared to rely on his profound magical power to clash head-on with a great demon emperor.

But only today did he realize how arrogant he had been.

"That one... I'm afraid he is now the number one in the Eastern Wilderness!"

Changxi's bright eyes suddenly widened.

...

High in the sky, a flying boat drifted unhurriedly.

It was not a powerful magical treasure, let alone a true artifact like the Frost Eagle Shuttle that excelled in flight. Its current speed was only possible because of the pilot's profound magical power.

But Luo Chen was not in a hurry.

Since he had already returned to the Eastern Wilderness, returning to the Luo Tian Sect a day earlier or later was only a matter of time.

At this moment, he sat quietly at the bow of the flying boat, holding a volume of alchemy scripture and studying it carefully.

Behind him were two increasingly awed gazes.

Sensing their hesitation to speak, Luo Chen put down the alchemy scripture and said without turning his head, "Is there something you want to ask?"

One of them, mature and steady, thought about it and did not speak.

The other, a petite woman, could not hold back and blurted out, "Hall Master Luo, what cultivation level are you at now?"

The question was too direct. The elderly man beside her pulled at the petite woman. "Fellow Daoist Yu, you overstep."

But to their surprise, the robed cultivator in front only smiled lightly.

"Mid-Sage stage. Still a step away from the late stage."

"Just that?" The petite woman clearly did not believe it. She was now a Sage herself and knew well the gap between each stage. Earlier, Luo Chen had overpowered Nine Spirits Primordial Lord, even briefly forcing open the mountain-protecting grand formation, demonstrating astonishing ability. Such means, and he was only at the mid-Sage stage?

Luo Chen did not explain. His life's experiences could not be summed up in a few words.

Moreover, between people at the same cultivation level, the gap in strength could sometimes be greater than the gap between a person and a dog.

Azure Frost had once been able to instantly defeat early and mid-Sage cultivators. He could do the same now.

In fact, relying on his vast soul foundation and casting illusions, even late-Sage cultivators were sometimes no match for him in a single encounter.

His earlier contest with Nine Spirits Primordial Lord had only been a clash on the level of magical power.

Even so, he had overpowered Nine Spirits Primordial Lord, who had always prided himself on his abundant magical power. This showed how deep and vast Luo Chen's strength was.

Luo Chen was in a good mood and spoke again: "Haoranzi, do you have anything you want to ask?"

Clearly, under his current good mood, if the other party had questions, he would be willing to answer a few.

But the white-haired old man pondered for a moment and finally shook his head with a long sigh.

"Trapped in Green Parasol Mountain for two hundred years, the world outside has long since turned upside down. My doubts are too many to be resolved by just one or two questions. I can only say that the greatest fortune of this old man's life was meeting you, Hall Master Luo!"

Luo Chen smiled slightly upon hearing this.

Back then, Haoranzi had been one of the few senior Adept cultivators who treated him well.

Although there was no particular guidance, their resource transactions and daily interactions had been fair, and he had given Luo Chen enough respect.

With such a connection, Luo Chen naturally could not bear to see him trapped in prison forever.

Yes, sitting on Luo Chen's flying boat at this moment were none other than the survivors of the Great Snowy Mountains battle—Haoranzi and Jadewater Dragon.

One was a seasoned Adept, the other a gifted water-element heavenly spirit root cultivator.

Although they had managed to survive in Green Parasol Mountain, they had spent the darkest two hundred years there.

Even after being helped by the Green Parasol Mountain demon cultivators to advance to the Sage stage, their fate was even worse—they were used as "medicine men" day in and day out.

Their Sage essence blood was extracted to refine Transformation Pills.

If Luo Chen had not used his authority as the Truth Crossing Hall Master to bring them out of the prison before leaving, they would have grown old and died in Green Parasol Mountain.

Now, having just escaped their cage and regained their freedom, the more excited they were, the more grateful they were to Luo Chen.

Addressing him as "Hall Master Luo" showed their respect.

"Although you were cultivators of the Truth Crossing Hall, now that you have left Green Parasol Mountain, you can no longer use that identity to walk the cultivation world."

"Have you considered my earlier proposal?"

The old man and the young woman exchanged a glance, then answered in unison without hesitation: "I am willing to join the Luo Tian Sect!"

"Good!"

Luo Chen clapped his hands in approval.

"In that case, don't call me Hall Master anymore. Just call me Senior Brother."

"Greetings, Senior Brother!"

"Greetings, Senior Brother!"

Two voices rang out in succession, accompanied by Luo Chen's two or three laughs. The flying boat gradually quieted down.

Only the sound of the alchemy scripture being reopened, with sparse pages turning, remained.

...

The flying boat traveled westward, crossing thousands of mountains and rivers, traversing ten thousand miles of layered clouds.

Luo Chen sat cross-legged at the bow, his eyes fixed on the alchemy scripture in his hands, but his divine sense occasionally scattered downward.

The Divine Talisman Sect, just as Talisman Elder had predicted, had fallen from the ranks of Sage Sects after his passing. Due to the second human-demon war, it had been nearly destroyed.

The mountain gate showed no sign of glory.

Perhaps a few disciples still huddled together for warmth, but ultimately, they were not enough to make a difference.

Luo Chen did not go down. To revive a Sage Sect, all it took was another Sage. It was that simple.

The Five Elements Domain, once home to the Five Elements Divine Sect, revealed no powerful cultivators after Luo Chen's divine sense swept over it.

Instead, many cultivators had settled on various peaks.

This once-famous great domain, after his battle with Divine Origin Sage back then, had its main fourth-order spirit vein shattered into countless second and third-order spirit veins. It had now become a holy land for scattered cultivators.

Similar situations were not uncommon in the Eastern Wilderness today.

Just like the next domains he passed through—the Misty Domain, the Fireworks Ancient Domain, and even the Luo Tian Domain once occupied by the Luo Tian Sect—there were few top-tier experts to be found.

Seeing this, Luo Chen could only sigh. The two human-demon wars had been a devastating blow to the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world.

Even if they had won, it was only a pyrrhic victory.

Of course, for small sects, minor powers, and some surviving scattered cultivators, this was instead a tremendous opportunity.

As long as they seized the chance in the next few hundred years, they might achieve great things and establish their own sects.

Some fall, so others may rise!

This sigh paused temporarily when he crossed the Calm Sea and reached the former Azure Sky Domain.

On the flying boat, Haoranzi and Jadewater Dragon stood behind Luo Chen, looking down with some fear.

A world shrouded in countless sharp golden wind astral winds!

They had a feeling that even as Sages, if they entered it, they would inevitably be reduced to ashes.

"In the heart of the human territory, there is such a dangerous and deadly place?"

"Is this man-made, or created by nature?"

Luo Chen did not answer their questions. Instead, he opened a pair of crimson-gold eyes and stared fixedly at the Azure Sky Domain.

From bottom to top, spanning thousands of miles, he seemed to look into the depths of the Azure Sky.

"That fellow is still sleeping inside, and his aura is growing stronger. He has already reached the great demon emperor realm!"

If outsiders knew that within this Azure Sky Domain, where life had been wiped out, there was still a great demon emperor, it would be shocking.

But Luo Chen was not surprised.

As early as his first return, he had sensed that after the ancient demon Emperor Heaven fell, he had used a secret technique to preserve his bloodline descendant, Emperor Slaughter.

Moreover, Emperor Slaughter was still sleeping, slowly absorbing the gold-attribute origin power left behind by his father.

If he absorbed it all, even if he could not reach the ancient demon realm, he would at least be a peak great demon emperor!

"Should I re-enslave Emperor Slaughter before he awakens?"

Luo Chen was somewhat tempted.

He had not cared about Emperor Slaughter before. Although he had the cultivation level, he lacked matching combat power.

But if he became a peak great demon emperor, or even a future ancient demon, he would be a very useful asset.

If he wanted to retrieve his main soul in the future, he would inevitably clash with the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. Such an asset must be kept in hand.

With a thought, Luo Chen leaped off the flying boat.

"Wait for me here. I'll be back soon."

Before Haoranzi and Jadewater Dragon could react, they watched in shock as Luo Chen's figure disappeared into the endless golden wind astral winds.

The two looked at each other, their awe for Luo Chen's unfathomable strength growing even deeper.

...

Deep within the Azure Sky Domain.

A figure walked alone.

Once, he could only penetrate a hundred li. Now, he had already crossed a thousand li.

The surrounding golden wind was biting, the astral wind scraping bones.

But with Luo Chen's magical power, which contained the origin of fire, he could no longer be stopped.

Even if occasional golden wind broke through his magical power and harassed him, it could only inflict minor injuries on his powerful physical body.

Touching the wound on his cheek, Luo Chen sighed softly.

"A dead tiger still leaves its might! One can imagine how unwilling the ancient demon Emperor Heaven was when he died under the siege of the three Nether Origin Sect ancestors."

Shaking his head, Luo Chen took a step forward and finally arrived before the golden tiger, which lay like a mountain.

As expected!

It was Luo Chen's former spirit pet—Emperor Slaughter!

He had to re-imprint the enslavement seal before it awakened.

Luo Chen pursed his lips and skillfully formed the Spirit-Enslaving Seal.

A red light, containing Luo Chen's essence blood, slowly descended towards Emperor Slaughter's head.

But just as it was about to sink in, a green light filled with vigorous vitality suddenly appeared and scattered Luo Chen's Spirit-Enslaving Seal.

Luo Chen was both angry and shocked.

"Who got to Emperor Slaughter before me and placed a slave seal on it?"
