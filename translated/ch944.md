# Chapter 944: Born Noble – Name Known Before Arrival

Inside the cabin.

Familiar posture, familiar atmosphere. Luo Chen sat quietly in cultivation.

Since he was not in a spirit-vein location, the external spiritual energy was not particularly dense, so his nascent soul was not as lively as before.

After a while.

Luo Chen let out a long breath, ending a session of cultivation.

The increase in his internal mana was negligible, but he paid it no mind.

Instead, with a thought, he focused his attention on his nascent soul.

Upon the fair, tender little nascent soul figure, a black halo rippled outward like gentle waves. As his mind sank into it, the subtle changes in his cultivation process that he had previously overlooked due to excessive focus surfaced one by one.

The rhythm and cadence of his breathing and exhalation. The drawing in and refinement of external spiritual energy. The reactions of his extraordinary meridians, apertures, and Purple Mansion as mana flowed through them…

Bit by bit, even the faintest changes within his Purple Mansion were revealed with perfect clarity!

This was the first time Luo Chen had witnessed his own cultivation process with his own eyes.

That feeling… how to describe it? Four words summed it up—**flaw-ridden!**

Luo Chen was stunned for a moment, then murmured, "I always believed I had achieved considerable mastery in cultivating the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture*. But why does it look now like something is off everywhere, with negligence at every turn?"

Yes, Luo Chen had never before doubted his proficiency in his natal cultivation art.

This was reflected in the rapid advancement of his cultivation realm.

But now, looking at it, he was far from reaching perfection. There were still many shortcomings.

Suddenly, Luo Chen realized something.

People tend to subconsciously beautify their own actions, or they are like one standing amidst a mountain, unable to see its true face, blinded by a single leaf.

But as the saying goes, the onlooker sees the game best!

A chess player who believes every move is excellent will, in the eyes of an observer, inevitably make some muddle-headed, mediocre plays.

Just like the top-tier gaming gods of old, in the eyes of keyboard warriors, they would still make idiotic moves.

Only by stepping away from the game itself, by breaking free from one's own perspective, can one perceive the shortcomings.

"Fortunately, they are all minor issues. A little adjustment and correction will suffice."

Luo Chen smiled faintly and once again entered a cultivation state.

His goal was not to increase his power, but to correct some of the bad habits in his previous cultivation.

Adjusting the rhythm of his breathing and exhalation, controlling the flow path of mana within his body…

Slowly, Luo Chen's proficiency in the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture* began to skyrocket at a visible rate.

One must understand that the current *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture* was no longer the mere Adept chapter from before.

It was a relatively complete full text—a peerless cultivation art sufficient for the Phoenix Clan to cultivate all the way to the Great Vehicle stage!

Even when cultivated by a human body, its upper limit was extremely high.

Under such circumstances, Luo Chen could still rapidly increase his proficiency in the art, proving that the changes he made to himself were indeed effective.

That Illuminating Spirit Gu truly lived up to its reputation!

Perhaps it was not like other gu insects or pills that could instantly boost a cultivator's strength, but in assisting cultivation, it produced immediate results.

Especially since Luo Chen could use his proficiency panel to verify whether the changes he made were beneficial or harmful—this created a synergistic effect where one plus one was greater than two.

"If I had a few more types of gu insects like this, perhaps that distant, unreachable late-Sage realm would be within arm's reach."

Luo Chen thought this during a break in cultivation.

Then he quickly returned to the state of making minor adjustments to his own cultivation.

---

Half a year later.

The flying boat gradually came to a halt in a stretch of clear blue sky.

Minglei Cave Master gazed ahead and let out a relieved sigh.

Since the Shadow Image Sect incident, there had been no more mishaps along the way.

Now, he only needed to introduce Luo Chen into that circle, and his promise would be fulfilled.

"Have we arrived?"

A voice came from within the cabin.

Minglei Cave Master instinctively replied, "We have arrived at Nine Haze Immortal City."

As he spoke, he turned back and saw Luo Chen walking out of the cabin.

Cloud boots treading on waves, dark armor with a jade belt.

A golden crown with feathered plumes, eyes bright as stars.

Minglei Cave Master was momentarily stunned.

Luo Chen had always dressed like this before, giving off an aura of overbearing dominance, looking down on all, as if he scorned everyone he saw.

But back then, there was still a hint of stiffness and artificiality.

Yet, somehow, in just a few short months, an imperceptible change seemed to have occurred in the other party.

Still that same overbearing dominance, still that same contempt for all beings.

But behind this dominance, the former stiffness and artificiality were gone, replaced by something more natural.

It was as if he was born to be exalted, supremely noble beyond measure, viewing all beings as nothing.

How to put it?

A few words flashed through Minglei Cave Master's mind—**"Born noble!"**

Seeing Minglei Cave Master staring at him in a daze, Luo Chen frowned. "What is it?"

Minglei Cave Master quickly shook his head and introduced to Luo Chen, "Nine Haze Immortal City has arrived. The entrance to Mi Realm Mountain is inside. Let us first settle down in Nine Haze Immortal City!"

Luo Chen's brow remained slightly furrowed.

He noticed that the other's voice had unconsciously lowered, as if in awe of him.

"Could there be something wrong with my cultivation again?"

Luo Chen muttered inwardly, but did not show it. He followed Minglei Cave Master toward the renowned Nine Haze Immortal City.

What he did not know was that even slight changes in his cultivation art, after daily practice, would affect his entire temperament, both internally and externally.

Just like back then, when taking True Flame Pills to cultivate the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture* had caused changes in his appearance, making his face extremely red.

And now, after continuously adjusting various details he had previously ignored, he had indeed cultivated a certain so-called "noble aura."

The cultivation art itself did not possess such a thing as noble aura, but the divine bird Phoenix did!

This set of arts, tailor-made for the Phoenix Clan, inherently carried that sacred nobility.

Luo Chen had always valued practicality and never paid attention to this before. But now, as he perfected the art, he gradually cultivated—no, he should say he had nurtured—a unique "noble aura" of his own.

---

Nine Haze Immortal City!

It had a great reputation in the Southern Frontier, but cultivators from the other four continents of the Mountains and Seas Realm knew little about it.

This was because the city's foundation was not deep; it had only been established in the last few hundred years.

"This city is the gateway to Mi Realm Mountain. It was built at great cost outside the entrance after Songyang Sage found the way into Mi Realm Mountain back in the day."

"After the Fierce Mountain Stiff Alliance emerged as a new organization, Nine Haze Immortal City became increasingly lively."

"As for the name 'Nine Haze,' Brother Qi will understand at a glance!"

Putting away the flying boat, the two transformed into beams of light and sped forward.

Soon, the outline of a towering, majestic city came into Luo Chen's view from afar.

With just one glance, Luo Chen memorized the appearance of this immortal city.

Standing above the nine heavens, hidden within the haze, appearing and disappearing, ethereal and otherworldly.

Perhaps it was not as fierce and imposing as Lingtian Pass, nor as deeply founded as Shangyou Immortal City.

But that transcendent, unworldly quality, like a sacred immortal paradise, was unforgettable to anyone who saw it.

"What a fine immortal city!" Luo Chen said.

Minglei Cave Master laughed. "I felt the same way when I first saw Nine Haze Immortal City. In all the vast Southern Frontier, there are few that can compare to it. It is also hard for the world to imagine that such an ethereal immortal city was built by Songyang Sage."

Songyang Sage!

A grand cultivator who had been famous in the Southern Frontier cultivation world for many years.

He had personally established the Fierce Mountain Stiff Alliance.

Just hearing the name "Fierce Mountain Stiff Alliance" gave a glimpse of that grand cultivator's style of conduct.

Under the construction of an immortal city overseen by such a powerful figure, the style should have been more like Lingtian Pass.

The current Nine Haze Immortal City indeed did not match the organization he had founded.

Of course, dwelling on this now was meaningless. The two pierced through clouds and mist, making no attempt to conceal their presence, and sped toward Nine Haze Immortal City.

Their massive beams of light exuded a formidable aura.

Because Nine Haze Immortal City was situated at an extremely high altitude, low-level cultivators had to fly up from below to enter the city.

Thus, outside the immortal city, countless streaks of light crisscrossed like a palace in the sky.

Two powerful beams of light flew overhead, instantly drawing the attention of many.

"They are Sage-level cultivators!"

"Another two old monsters at the Sage realm have arrived!"

"Is the Fierce Mountain Stiff Alliance really planning something big, to attack the sacred land?"

"With such a widespread invitation to the top experts of the Southern Frontier, forming alliances and building connections, if they don't have some agenda, I certainly wouldn't believe it."

"I hope my clan's ancestor doesn't get involved. That sacred land is not something we dare to provoke!"

"Hah, the Life and Death Gate is like a turtle hiding in its shell, not emerging for so long. Even the tributes they used to receive have been cut off without any reprimand. It's likely they have internal problems. What's there to fear!"

---

Swish!

Swish!

Two beams of light landed before the towering city gates.

After landing, Luo Chen and Minglei exchanged glances, both showing surprise on their faces.

With their divine sense, they could naturally overhear the whispered discussions of the low-level cultivators.

Only now did the two realize what major events were about to unfold at their destination.

"Is it true?" Luo Chen asked.

Minglei Cave Master looked bewildered. "The Wind Thunder Gorge is too remote. I know nothing of these changes in the Southern Frontier situation!"

Luo Chen took a deep breath, stopped discussing it, and strode toward the city.

But it felt as if he was not entering an immortal city, but a massive vortex.

Just like back then at Lingtian Pass!

Yet Luo Chen did not retreat. Whether it was the persona he was deliberately maintaining or his current formidable strength, both were enough to allow him to step into any vortex without fear.

Seeing his unwavering determination, Minglei Cave Master hesitated for a moment, then chose to follow.

Once inside the city, Minglei Cave Master instinctively spread his divine sense.

In just a moment, he gasped and quickly withdrew his divine sense.

"Hiss!"

"There are so many of our peers in this city?"

As he spoke, he involuntarily looked at Luo Chen.

But he found that the other party merely raised an eyebrow, without any sign of losing composure.

"Could it be that he didn't sense those Sage-level cultivators?"

Body cultivators were strong in physique but weak in spirit.

This could explain Luo Chen's calmness.

However, Luo Chen's next words dispelled his speculation.

"Seven!"

"There are seven Sage-level cultivators in the city. Most are at the early Sage stage, with only two at the mid-Sage stage."

After speaking, Luo Chen stood still, not moving.

Minglei Cave Master was stunned. Not only had the other party detected them, but he had also accurately stated the number of Sage cultivators. Could it be that his spirit was even stronger than his own?

How was that possible!

Just as he was marveling, amidst the crowded street ahead, a figure hurried over. Without any hesitation, he recognized Luo Chen and Minglei at a glance.

Whoosh…

Like a gust of wind, the young man arrived before Luo Chen and Minglei Cave Master, bowing respectfully.

"Junior Hu Jin, under orders from my master, Zhenguan Daoist, to welcome two esteemed seniors."

The name Zhenguan Daoist immediately rang a bell for Minglei Cave Master.

He transmitted to Luo Chen, "He is the lord of Nine Haze Immortal City, also the host of that exchange meeting back then. His cultivation is at the mid-Sage stage, one of the two you sensed just now."

After speaking, he nodded to Hu Jin, who was only at the Adept stage. "Young friend, I am Minglei. I have met your esteemed master once before."

Hu Jin smiled. "Cave Master, my master has mentioned you before. He called you a rare genius of the Southern Frontier, advancing to the Sage realm as a rogue cultivator. He often uses your experience to discipline us useless juniors."

Minglei Cave Master's eyes lit up. "Oh, is that so? Then I am honored to be used by Fellow Daoist Zheng as a positive example to encourage his disciples."

After exchanging a few pleasantries, Hu Jin looked at Luo Chen.

"And this senior is…"

"Lead the way!"

Luo Chen said indifferently, not intending to announce himself before a junior.

This made Hu Jin's expression stiffen. He understood that the status of being a Sage's disciple did not carry much weight before this person.

This one was probably not easy to deal with!

But he did not feel any resentment. It was as if the other's demeanor was natural, not deliberate.

That innate nobility was simply meant to be!

"Two seniors, please follow me!"

---

Meanwhile, inside a palace.

A white-bearded old Daoist holding a whisk stood at the door to welcome guests.

Those familiar with him were puzzled, not understanding why he was acting this way.

They knew that the white-bearded old Daoist was the master of a faction, and even after joining the Fierce Mountain Stiff Alliance, his status was quite exalted.

That even the guardianship of Nine Haze Immortal City, the gateway to Mi Realm Mountain, had been entrusted to him showed that both his reputation and strength had been recognized by the few grand cultivators of the Fierce Mountain Stiff Alliance.

Especially another Sage-level expert who was visiting inside the palace frowned.

When he had come earlier, Zhenguan had not shown such warm hospitality!

He wanted to see who it was!

A moment later, three figures appeared before the palace.

Zhenguan Daoist, from a distance, flicked his whisk and laughed heartily. "Could it be the Overbearing Cultivator Qi Tian himself?"

Minglei Cave Master was taken aback. Qi Tian had not announced himself, so how did the other party know his name?

Luo Chen grinned, not being polite at all. "It is I!"

Zhenguan clasped his hands in a salute. "Please, come in!"

Luo Chen returned a simple clasped-hand salute and strode into the hall without hesitation.

Minglei Cave Master opened his mouth, but the situation had completely exceeded his expectations.

It seemed he had not even had the chance to make an introduction yet!

Hu Jin, who had led the way, was also caught off guard. But after a moment, he seemed to remember something.

Looking at Luo Chen's retreating back, he could not help but show a trace of reverence.

Inside the hall, the Sage-level expert who had arrived earlier immediately furrowed his brow.

"Overbearing Cultivator Qi Tian… could it be that one?"
