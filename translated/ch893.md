# Chapter 893: Dan Saint’s Legacy, Piercing the Void

“For this journey, we will first go to Lingtian Pass, then take the teleportation formation to Shangyou City. Entering the Nether Origin from Shangyou will be the fastest route.”

Inside the hall, the City Lord of Lingtian was explaining the specific route for this trip to Luo Chen.

Lingtian City Lord was explaining the specific route for this trip to Luo Chen.

Shangyou Immortal City—Luo Chen knew of this place.

Before Lingtian Pass existed, this was considered the foremost majestic city of the Eastern Wilderness.

It had existed since ancient times and had endured many great battles. Built, destroyed, and rebuilt, as the Nether Origin Sect gradually established a firm foothold, Shangyou City became one of the sect’s iconic gateways.

In the subsequent three thousand years, it had never again suffered the flames of war, developing into a very prosperous place.

Many of the top great sects of the Eastern Wilderness had set up “business offices” there, both for conducting trade and for convenient access to the latest information from the Ascendant Sacred Land.

For the Nether Origin Sect, the significance of Shangyou City was virtually no different from the relationship between Taohai City and the Primordial Demon Sect.

It was both the sect’s gateway and an important arrangement for external communication and exchange.

Under normal development, the Luo Tian Sect would later establish an office in Shangyou City as well. Moreover, due to Luo Chen’s connections, they might even receive preferential treatment.

“Is it that urgent?” Luo Chen asked. He smiled, then added, “I was thinking of traveling along the way, taking in the scenic splendors of the other immortal sects of the Eastern Wilderness. For over a hundred years now, I’ve been fighting constantly, truly neglecting too many beautiful sights.”

“It is a bit rushed, but my sect is eager for talent. We have been looking forward to the arrival of the Pill Sect with great anticipation.”

As he said this, Lingtian City Lord winked.

Luo Chen smiled knowingly and said modestly, “The Nether Origin Sect is a noble Ascendant Sacred Land. Talents in the Dao of alchemy must be as numerous as the carp crossing the river. How could I possibly be so important?”

“Fellow Daoist, you are being too modest.”

Lingtian City Lord did not agree with Luo Chen’s excessive self-deprecation, but he did not refute certain parts of his words.

“In terms of the Dao of alchemy, the Nether Origin Sect does indeed have a few who have entered the hall. You will need to pay some attention to this matter when the time comes, Fellow Daoist.”

“Oh? What do you mean by that?” Luo Chen became interested.

Lingtian City Lord did not keep any secrets. Apart from Luo Chen’s own friends, he was the one who most deeply understood Luo Chen’s alchemy skills. Now that Luo Chen was about to arrive at the Sacred Land, as long as no mistakes occurred, he would surely rise swiftly in the future.

If he could build a good relationship with him, it would be an important network of human resources, not only for himself personally but also for the Ye family behind him.

Moreover, their relationship was not bad to begin with.

Therefore, Lingtian City Lord immediately laid out the situation regarding the Dao of alchemy within the Nether Origin Sect.

“Although Junior Sister Chu Yan has gone far away to the Central Continent for personal reasons, she still left a very deep foundation in alchemy within the sect.”

“Currently, our Nether Origin Sect has five alchemy grandmasters!”

“They are Junior Brother Lu Chun, Junior Sister Chu Yu, Master Xuanyan, Chen Mozi, and Xinyi Sheng.”

The moment he heard these five names, Luo Chen’s gaze sharpened.

Some of them sounded quite familiar to him.

Seeing his reaction, Lingtian City Lord couldn’t help but smile.

“It seems Fellow Daoist is also well aware of the weight behind these names!”

Luo Chen did not deny it. He said seriously, “I may not have heard of the others, but I do know the great name of Master Xuanyan. It is said that the Medicine King of the Medicine King Sect was once his medicine boy before he rose to prominence. By merely dabbling in a portion of Master Xuanyan’s alchemy techniques, he was able to form his own school and even establish the Medicine King Sect independently.”

“That is correct.” Lingtian City Lord nodded, but then said with some regret, “Unfortunately, he is old, very old. Even though he himself is well-versed in longevity techniques, and my sect provides him with longevity-enhancing spiritual medicines, his qi and blood are now extremely weak and declining. He rarely refines pills personally anymore. He devotes himself entirely to studying theory in seclusion, intending to write another book before his passing.”

Cultivators are not body refiners. Those who refine qi and preserve the spirit are inferior to those who temper the body in terms of longevity.

Therefore, as one ages, the qi and blood of the physical body inevitably decline.

Even a Sage cannot avoid this.

In fact, it is not just cultivators; even orthodox body refiners face this problem.

Only after reaching the fourth realm of body refinement and cultivating the Leakless Golden Body can one ensure that their qi and blood remain abundant and undiminished, lasting for a long time, only to be suddenly released just before their passing.

Even demon beasts cannot escape this rule!

Otherwise, why would they seek to transform into human form?

The massive bodies of demon beasts constantly leak qi and blood. Only the smaller human form can relatively well seal in one’s essence energy.

After sighing with emotion, Lingtian City Lord returned to the main topic.

“These five people I mentioned each have their own achievements in the Dao of alchemy, in no way inferior to famous alchemy grandmasters of the Eastern Wilderness like the Medicine King or Pill Master.”

“Among the five, Junior Brother Lu Chun and Junior Sister Chu Yu are both from our Nether Origin Sect. Junior Sister Chu Yu, in particular, comes from the Chu family, the same family as Junior Sister Chu Yan.”

“The other three were invited into my sect over the past thousand years from the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world.”

Luo Chen pointed at himself. “Just like me?”

“Correct!” Lingtian City Lord nodded and casually stated the respective origins of the three.

Master Xuanyan had inherited a sect’s legacy in his early years, but after his sect was destroyed, he did not revive it. Instead, he studied the Dao of alchemy alone.

During this process, many powerful cultivators who admired his alchemy skills became his disciples, tending his fires, refining pills, and selecting medicinal ingredients.

The Medicine King of the Medicine King Sect was one such example.

Chen Mozi was different. He was a great talent from the Dongyang Sect. Because the two sects had a good relationship and frequently interacted, he gradually settled down in the Nether Origin Sect.

Xinyi Sheng came from the ancient sect Meihua Sect. He was relatively younger than the previous two. During the Adept stage, he had already displayed extraordinary alchemy talent and became a renowned alchemy master.

Admiring the style of the Dan Saint of that time, he left the Meihua Sect and joined the Nether Origin Sect, hoping to refine his alchemy skills under the Dan Saint’s guidance.

Unexpectedly, before he could even meet the Dan Saint after joining, the Dan Saint had already left the Nether Origin Sect and gone to the Central Continent.

After understanding all this in detail, Luo Chen couldn’t help but be inwardly astonished.

This was the foundation of a Sacred Land!

Alchemy grandmasters, rarely seen in the outside world, numbered as many as five in the Nether Origin Sect.

Even, from what he understood, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect did not have this many alchemy grandmasters.

Thus, Luo Chen had to once again reassess the true strength of the Nether Origin Sect.

With five alchemy grandmasters, was the Nether Origin Sect’s true strength merely what was shown on the surface?

The Ascendant cultivators need not be mentioned; ordinary alchemists were of little help to them.

But below the Ascendant stage, a large number of cultivators could be cultivated with the help of miraculous elixirs refined by alchemy grandmasters.

As for the hidden dangers of pill toxins from consuming elixirs?

Take the *Clear Source Marvelous Pill Method* of the Azure Pill Valley, for example. Based on Luo Chen’s current knowledge, it was not so exquisitely unparalleled.

As long as one was willing to invest effort, a skilled alchemy grandmaster could also research it.

Of course, no matter how thoroughly pill toxins were cleansed, excessively consuming elixirs to advance one’s realm would still create obstacles during breakthroughs.

But at least with the aid of corresponding secret arts, one could quickly increase their cultivation speed.

So, with the assistance of five alchemy grandmasters, how many Sages and how many Adepts did the Nether Origin Sect actually have?

Luo Chen did not know. What he was more concerned about now was the reason Lingtian City Lord had taken the initiative to bring up these five people.

Under Luo Chen’s expectant gaze, Lingtian City Lord did not keep him in suspense.

“After Junior Sister Chu Yan left, the Dan Saint Hall was closed. To enter, one must pass through layers of trials—trials related to the Dao of alchemy!”

“You must understand, Junior Sister was someone who could transcend levels to refine fifth-grade divine pills and earned the title of Dan Saint. The things she set up trials to protect are bound to be extremely high-quality alchemy legacies.”

“Over the years, aside from Master Xuanyan, who went in and stayed for half a year, gaining some insights, the other grandmasters have all been helpless!”

“The complete alchemy legacy remains unclaimed.”

Luo Chen said thoughtfully, “Previously, Patriarch Black Marsh promised that after I joined the Nether Origin Sect, I could take charge of the Dan Saint Hall. But now, City Lord, you say the Dan Saint Hall is closed. Does that mean if I also cannot obtain the Dan Saint’s legacy, the so-called ‘taking charge of the Dan Saint Hall’ becomes an empty promise?”

Lingtian City Lord laughed heartily. “Correct. As you think, since you cannot enter, naturally there is no such thing as taking charge.”

Luo Chen said helplessly, “In the end, it still depends on my ability!”

“You are entering the Nether Origin Sect precisely because of your great alchemy skills. Naturally, to strive for status, you must also rely on your alchemy.” Lingtian City Lord did not avoid the issue and frankly admitted this matter.

He continued, “The Dan Saint’s legacy has been left vacant for a long time, which is a concern for many within the sect. Last time, my sect set aside past grievances and sent Junior Brother Chongming to the Central Continent to observe the ceremony, hoping to get some guidance from Junior Sister Chu Yan. But she still did not relent. She merely fobbed Junior Brother Chongming off with a Heavenly Secret Pill.”

“Master Xuanyan was originally the most promising candidate. But after gaining some insights in the Dan Saint Hall, he admitted that he was too old to make further progress and could not retrieve the complete legacy. Moreover, a fragmented legacy could easily mislead his own alchemy philosophy. Therefore, he has decided not to attempt entering the Dan Saint Hall again. He will merely borrow from Junior Sister Chu Yan’s alchemy philosophy to perfect his own alchemy legacy.”

“For our Nether Origin Sect, the Dan Saint’s legacy can be given to anyone, as long as they are a member of the Nether Origin Sect!”

“But for you alchemy grandmasters, it is especially important.”

“It is not just a legacy; it also represents your status within the Nether Origin Sect and the proportion of resources you can allocate.”

“Pill Sect, you wouldn’t want to enter the Nether Origin Sect only to be subordinate to others, would you?”

Luo Chen smiled.

“We are all alchemy grandmasters. I alone bear the title of Pill Sect. Do you think I would admit to being inferior to others?”

“Well said, Pill Sect!” Lingtian City Lord clapped his hands in admiration, greatly appreciating Luo Chen’s confidence.

However!

He was also a little puzzled. Anyone who heard about the Dan Saint’s legacy would be extremely excited.

Yet from beginning to end, Luo Chen had not been excited. He had only shown a bit of curiosity.

Was it self-confidence to the point of arrogance?

Or did he not understand the importance of the Dan Saint’s legacy?

…

After coming out of the hall, Luo Chen’s expression was calm.

He now understood why the three alchemy grandmasters of the Nether Origin Sect had been so concerned about when he would arrive at the Sacred Land.

So this was what it was all about!

The Dan Saint’s legacy was not only about progress in the Dao of alchemy but also about one’s status and resource allocation within the Nether Origin Sect.

People like Lu Chun and Chu Yu did not have such worries.

They were originally from the Nether Origin Sect. With elders or families backing them, whether they obtained the Dan Saint’s legacy or not would not affect their current situation.

“Ah, people, wherever they go, there is competition.”

“Contending with heaven, contending with earth, contending with others—it is truly an endless joy.”

Luo Chen smiled and returned to his residence, as if he did not care about this matter at all.

In truth, he really didn’t care much.

For someone who possessed the Devil Lord’s legacy, his alchemy skills integrated the three schools of demon, dao, and devil. Although it seemed to lack a distinctive style, it actually merged the strengths of a hundred schools.

No matter how exquisite the Dan Saint’s legacy was, how could it compare to the Devil Lord’s legacy?

“Pill Sect!”

“Pill Sect!”

The two maidservants guarding the door bowed respectfully.

Luo Chen nodded. “You may withdraw.”

“This servant takes her leave.”

After the two maidservants left, Luo Chen pushed open the door and entered.

With just one glance, he saw a young boy lying on the window, staring curiously at the outside.

Sunlight fell on his profile, making it appear golden.

“Lingxi, what are you looking at?”

The boy pointed at the line where water met sky in the distance.

“The sea!”

Luo Chen walked to the window and said with a smile, “That’s not the real sea. It’s just a slightly larger lake.”

Luo Lingxi asked curiously, “But I heard the maidservant sister say earlier that the place below is called Pingshan Sea.”

Luo Chen’s tall body blocked the direct sunlight. He shook his head slightly.

“The real sea is boundless, a vast expanse of blue. Oh no, there are also seas that are not blue—black, red, yellow—all kinds exist.”

A strong curiosity lit up Luo Lingxi’s eyes.

“A black sea, a red sea, a yellow sea… do they really exist in this world?”

“They do! In the *Northern Sea Travelogue* that I personally wrote, which is kept in the Luo Tian Sect’s Scripture Pavilion, they are recorded. The black one is called the Fallen Sea, the red one is the Coral Sea, the yellow one…” Luo Chen paused subconsciously. The mere thought of that yellow in his memory brought a sense of palpitation to his soul. It was better not to mention it.

He continued, “In that boundless ocean, there are jiaolongs emerging, giant whales stirring up waves, and swarms of shrimp soldiers, crab generals, and schools of fish. At the bottom of the sea, there are deep trenches and undulating mountains, a world of their own. Its splendor is not something that this newly formed Pingshan Sea below can compare to.”

Luo Lingxi’s eyes sparkled as he listened. He murmured, “I really want to go see it someday!”

Luo Chen rubbed the thin boy’s head. “There will be a chance. If this trip goes smoothly, you will have the opportunity to see that boundless ocean full of vitality with your own eyes.”

The Northern Sea in Luo Chen’s experience was filled with slaughter and bloodshed, cold and cruel.

But it was also in such an environment that he had gained a hundred years of freedom and broken through to the Sage realm. Calling it full of vitality was not an exaggeration.

When telling a child, one naturally speaks of the good aspects.

“Stop looking. If you keep at it, your eyes will hurt. Get some rest!”

Luo Lingxi obediently jumped down from the chair. In front of Luo Chen, he was always well-behaved, lacking the lively wildness he showed in front of Sima Huiniang.

Standing before Luo Chen, Luo Lingxi opened his eyes wide, as if waiting for something.

Luo Chen smiled, took out a jade bottle, and dripped two drops of a transparent liquid into the boy’s eyes.

“Alright, go rest!”

Luo Lingxi rubbed his eyes, showing signs of fatigue, and went to bed to sleep on his own.

Luo Chen remained standing by the window. He also took out a jade bottle and dripped two drops into his own eyes.

However, unlike the transparent liquid that had been diluted countless times earlier, these two drops were a brilliant golden liquid.

His tightly closed eyes slowly opened. Luo Chen stared intently at the void, as if trying to see something.

But unfortunately, he found nothing.

Luo Chen sighed and put away the bottle containing the Jade Emperor Clear Vision Liquid.

“Slow and steady wins the race.”

“To pierce the void with the naked eye is not something that can be achieved overnight.”

“But I believe that as long as I persist unwaveringly, I will one day be able to see through that woman.”

In his mind, a figure in green robes flashed by.

That was the greatest setback he had suffered in a battle against a peer since becoming a Sage.

Even though she had struck when he was at his weakest after a great battle, Luo Chen did not think he would have had much chance of winning even at his peak.

But Luo Chen was not discouraged.

The first time they met, he was captured without any sensation.

The second time they met, with the combined strength of many, relying on the sharpness of Yuantu, he managed to wound her, drawing a single drop of blood.

The third time, he burned one of her hands, and then they ceased hostilities.

And the next time?

Perhaps after entering the Nether Origin Sect, there would be no next time. But Luo Chen wanted there to be one.

A fair and just duel!

Luo Chen looked forward to that day. His eyes, gazing at the mountains and seas, shone brightly, as if crossing space to capture that spirit leaping through the void.
