# Chapter 755: The Secret of Origin, Two Sages Attack

Rain hung like a curtain, concealing the lush green outside.

"Have you used it up again?"

Luo Chen asked this with his eyes closed.

He did not wait for the other party to answer before adding another sentence.

"You must wait two days. At that time, I will go out and hunt illusion beasts again."

At this moment, he was at the stage of undoing a key restriction within the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion.

As the inheritance true artifact of the Penglai Immortal Sect, the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion had incomparably complex restrictions set up inside it, thereby constructing formation after formation to divide the inner space and ensure that the medicinal herbs within would not affect one another.

These formations were rather obscure. Even Han Zhan back then had only undone a very small portion, giving Luo Chen temporary control rights.

During those years on Longyuan Island, Luo Chen had tried to undo all of them, but had still fallen short by quite a lot.

Now that he had a large amount of free time, Luo Chen had formed the thought of thoroughly unsealing it and refining it for his own use.

After half a year passed, Luo Chen had again overcome the general outline of a fourth-tier formation and had more or less figured out its complex restriction composition. Given another two or three days, he could completely undo it.

At that time, he would probably grasp eighty percent of this true artifact's control authority.

Not only that, he could also grasp that fourth-tier spatial formation related to spatial restrictions.

It had to be said that undoing the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion's formation restrictions was something that benefited Luo Chen endlessly!

This true artifact was a multi-attribute magic treasure. The formations and restrictions it involved were also of all kinds, and among them, the most outstanding and most numerous were spatial restrictions and the spatial formations composed from large numbers of spatial restrictions!

By Luo Chen's own estimate, if he could understand all of them, he might even grasp the means to construct teleportation formations.

At the very least, right now, he had already grasped one Small Transposition Formation!

While he was immersed in unsealing restrictions and breaking formations, a soft "Mm" came by his ear, accompanied by another sentence.

"This time, may I follow along?"

Luo Chen opened his eyes and looked at the woman standing prettily at the cave entrance. After three consecutive rounds of deep bitter cultivation, the other party's figure had grown somewhat thinner, and even her cheeks were a little gaunt.

At this moment, she pressed her lips together and looked at Luo Chen timidly.

"If you want to follow, then follow."

Luo Chen said this calmly, then closed his eyes again and focused on refining the small octagonal pavilion before him.

The one who had calculated against him was not Fu Qinglan, but Fu Chaosheng.

The long passage of time had already calmed Luo Chen, and he would not vent anger on others for no reason, even if this matter had arisen because of the woman before him.

...

Three days later.

Luo Chen set out again and arrived at the plain where he usually arranged the formation and lured illusion beasts.

Fu Qinglan followed behind him, personally watching him set down the Heavenly Secret Pill bait and arrange the formation.

For some reason, she discovered that the other party's formation-setting methods were several degrees more practiced than before.

Especially the Small Transposition Formation beneath the Heavenly Secret Pill, which could not be discovered without careful attention.

After finishing all preparations, Luo Chen again sat down cross-legged and closed his eyes to rest his spirit.

Of course, he was not actually so idle. In truth, his mind was constantly reviewing the contents of the Withering-Bloom Dao Scripture.

In his view, this self-created cultivation art still had great room for improvement, especially after cultivating it again and again. He felt it could absolutely be improved further.

Excellent cultivation arts had always been like this.

After being created, they required constant additions and revisions until reaching the limit.

Luo Chen did not know where the Withering-Bloom Dao Scripture's limit lay. For now, he could only take things one step at a time and do his utmost to perfect it.

Fu Qinglan stayed beside him, staring at him in a daze.

Unlike last time, when the two of them could borrow the clear wind, shell beans together, drink wine, and talk and laugh freely.

This time, they were as unfamiliar as strangers on the road.

She wished to find a topic, but the other party's appearance of keeping strangers away made it difficult for her to take the initiative.

The stiff atmosphere made her feel as if she were standing on a mountain of blades.

The wait for an illusion beast to come to them grew longer and longer.

One day, two days...

Before they knew it, more than half a month had passed.

At last, an illusion beast came.

It was a large white ox.

With twin spiral horns on its head, it ran over from among the mountains and ridges and rammed straight into the formation.

Then Luo Chen acted.

The familiar fire phoenix reappeared, heavily injuring and scattering it.

Thick cloud and mist gathered and dispersed across the plain, but this time Luo Chen did not directly use the Primordial Unity Cauldron to seal and collect it. Instead, he cast spell after spell.

Fire phoenix, flame dragon, Azure Yang Great Handprint... he even tried casting the Encompassing Fire Prison, which had little effect on illusion beasts, twice.

Under his constant bombardment, the white ox gathered then scattered, scattered then gathered, roaring without end.

Watching this scene, Fu Qinglan clenched her hands.

She did not know Luo Chen was borrowing this living target of an illusion beast to raise his spell proficiency. What she saw was only the other party slaughtering a fourth-tier illusion beast that could not withstand a blow again and again, meaninglessly.

Those spells were so gorgeous and dazzling, with vast power.

But each release always consumed magic power.

How was such meaningless action any different from venting anger?

"In the end, he is still angry with me!"

Amid her inner torment, at some unknown time, Luo Chen had already dealt with this white ox illusion beast.

He stood quietly on the plain, looking at the surrounding mountain peaks and cliffs that had been smashed apart, his brows slightly furrowed.

He was thinking about something in his heart, but before long, he flew up lightly and prepared to return.

After flying a few hundred meters, he suddenly turned back.

"Are you staying here?"

Fu Qinglan, who was standing dazed on the mountain peak, woke as if from a dream, gave an "oh," and hurriedly followed.

Seeing her absent-minded appearance, Luo Chen frowned.

"This is the fourth illusion core. Are you confident you can touch upon some clue?"

The woman's small mouth parted, then she lowered her head in grievance.

Luo Chen shook his head and sighed.

"If you do not mind, tell me about the confusions you have during cultivation. One person has short plans, two have long plans. Perhaps by discussing it, I can give you some suggestions. If you can comprehend the true intent of laws earlier, I can also leave Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven earlier."

"Of course, if the Tianyuan Dao Sect has rules, you may say nothing."

Fu Qinglan hesitated somewhat.

But after considering it, she still slowly opened her mouth.

"I cannot find origin power."

...

It was still that mountainside.

Luo Chen still guarded outside, but this time he did not go about his own affairs. Instead, he spread his divine sense and carefully perceived something.

Fu Qinglan had not told him the specific cultivation art related to comprehending the true intent of laws. After all, that was a core inheritance of the Dao Sect.

But she had given him a general explanation of the method.

Heaven and earth have spirit. Origin qi has no soul.

If a cultivator could find the origin power corresponding to a certain law, they could use it to bestow consciousness upon the origin qi of heaven and earth, borrowing the might of heaven and earth to control origin qi.

This was the secret of why Ascendant great powers could control the origin qi of heaven and earth.

As for how to search, Fu Qinglan had mentioned a general method.

Using the Nascent Soul to bear the divine soul, merge into heaven and earth, and search for the most suitable origin power.

To put it vividly, the Nascent Soul was a boat, and the divine soul was a person. The person sat in the boat and fished up treasures from the boundless ocean of heaven and earth's origin qi.

Spiritual impulse was the line that led the person to the corresponding origin power!

When Fu Qinglan previously released the true body of her Nascent Soul and swallowed and exhaled spiritual qi in great mouthfuls, she had not been cultivating at all. Instead, during that process, she was searching for the corresponding origin power.

And this so-called origin power, according to the Tianyuan Dao Sect's records, was shockingly the most basic and easiest-to-comprehend form of law true intent!

"Fu Qinglan primarily cultivates the wood attribute. What she needs to search for is wood origin, so her Nascent Soul swallows and exhales wood-attribute spiritual qi, bringing her divine soul into the omnipresent sea of origin qi, then bit by bit searches for wood origin."

"This kind of search originally has no signs to follow and depends on accumulated luck over long years. But with spiritual impulse as guidance, it gains a certain feasibility."

"Only, is the tiny spiritual impulse of one illusion core enough?"

Luo Chen paid attention to the other party's cultivation actions for several days in a row.

Gradually, he felt something in his heart.

If he integrated this method into the Withering-Bloom Dao Scripture, would it complete the missing piece leading toward the Ascendant realm?

And how exactly should this method be carried out?

Luo Chen wanted to experiment.

But at present, the sample he had observed was too small, so he did not dare take the risk personally.

Moreover, he also did not have spiritual impulse to serve as that fishing line to angle and haul up the origin power hidden in the sea of origin qi.

"I will use Fu Qinglan as the sample and observe carefully. Perhaps I can summarize that method."

Luo Chen thought this.

...

Half a year later, Luo Chen went out again.

Fu Qinglan obediently followed behind.

After the previous exchange on cultivation, their distant relationship had been repaired a little.

Luo Chen was a person who held grudges, but holding grudges depended on the target. The one who had truly made him suffer a loss was Fu Chaosheng. Toward Fu Qinglan, it was merely hating the house and therefore the crow.

The other party was even willing to bring out cultivation secrets and discuss them with him, so naturally he would not deliberately show her a cold face.

At the place where the three mountains encircled the plain, the formation had already been activated for several days.

This time, nearly a month passed.

Luo Chen finally could not hold back and fully activated the formation, causing the Heavenly Secret Pill's medicinal fragrance to spread out many times farther.

Originally, the area it covered was no more than a thousand li.

This time, however, it spread across several thousand li.

After doing this, he did not have to wait long before movement sounded among the clustered mountains.

Fu Qinglan cried out in surprise from the side, "They are here, and there are two fourth-tier illusion beasts!"

A giant python and a flying eagle.

One above and one below, they rushed toward the plain beneath Luo Chen's feet as if racing.

Luo Chen lifted his eyelids, his expression somewhat strange.

"They have come, but it is not only them."

Fu Qinglan was puzzled, but Luo Chen had already moved.

The instant the two illusion beasts entered the plain, they lost their perception of the Heavenly Secret Pill and began growing furious.

Before they could even search for where the Heavenly Secret Pill was, a figure dropped down like thunder.

Thunder Kick!

With just one kick, Luo Chen scattered the giant python illusion beast.

He crashed to the ground, both feet sinking into the soil.

Afterward, he raised his head, formed seals, and cast a spell. A fire phoenix shot into the sky trailing a long meteor tail, pouncing toward the flying eagle.

Not only that, the Cloud-Seizing Divine Claw descended from the sky, surrounding the flying eagle from above and below.

As the two crossed, the flying eagle suffered a heavy blow and immediately collapsed.

Large amounts of cloud and mist spread through this stretch of heaven and earth.

Faintly, two figures were about to gather.

But as the formation Luo Chen had arranged long ago activated, the cloud and mist that filled the sky surged like a tide into a small cauldron.

They had no power to resist at all!

Fu Qinglan watched this entire scene personally and could not help sighing at the depth of Luo Chen's strength.

Although illusion beasts were weak, their strength could still compare to early-stage Sage cultivators who had no particular methods.

Yet under Luo Chen's hand, they often collapsed at the first touch.

And because of the formation and that small-cauldron true artifact, even their strongest and most proficient life-saving escape method could not be used.

They were simply no different from lambs awaiting slaughter.

"Two illusion cores at once. The harvest is quite rich."

"And this time, he did not use these illusion beasts to vent anger. It seems his opinion of me has lessened quite a bit."

Just as Fu Qinglan thought this, the large amount of cloud qi on the plain thoroughly dispersed, and Luo Chen's figure appeared.

One hand was behind his back, while magic power coiled around the other as he held and toyed with two illusion cores.

His eyes, however, looked far into the high sky.

"Since the two fellow Daoists have come, why hide your heads and tails? Could it be you want to be the oriole behind the mantis stalking the cicada?"

Fu Qinglan was stunned and subconsciously looked in the direction Luo Chen was watching.

On the path from which the illusion beasts had come, one person stood to the left and one to the right.

On the left was a withered figure entirely shrouded in a black robe, his true appearance unseen. The person on the right had a gleaming bald head, wore a string of large prayer beads around his neck, and had eyes bright and piercing enough that one dared not meet them.

With just one glance, the woman lost color from her face and blurted out:

"Sage Heavenly Mountain!"

"Wrathful Vajra!"

*(End of Chapter)*
