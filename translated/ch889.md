# Chapter 889: Nirvana Sacred Flame, Prison-Suppressing Art Complete

"Success!"

Inside the Truth Crossing Cave Mansion, Luo Chen opened his eyes with a delighted expression.

At this moment, his mana was circulating along a completely different route than before.

From the soles of his feet to the crown of his head, and even outside his sea of consciousness, strands of mana were nourishing every part.

Especially the latter. Others might not know what this signified, but Luo Chen understood it very clearly.

This meant that the complete *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture* possessed an effect that the vast majority of techniques in the world lacked—the ability to temper the soul!

For Luo Chen, this was an unexpected joy.

He had cultivated the *Soul-Splitting Art* and understood the principle of expanding the soul's foundation: splitting the main soul, allowing both the main soul and the divided souls to grow simultaneously, achieving twice the result with half the effort.

But the awkward part was that Han Zhan's soul fragment memories had been incomplete, missing one of the most crucial parts—the method for enhancing divided souls.

For this reason, Luo Chen had painstakingly collected soul essences and refined Great Soul Pills, using external forces to compensate for this deficiency.

But now, the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture* used mana to nourish the soul through the most orthodox path of "refining qi to transform spirit," leaving Luo Chen with no more flaws!

From now on, the divided souls he split off would no longer have their growth speed lag far behind the main soul.

And all the credit for this...

Luo Chen took a deep breath, and with a certain inexplicable anticipation, extended his divine sense into his Purple Mansion.

The infant-like Nascent Soul suddenly opened its eyes.

With a childlike curiosity, it walked to a corner of the Purple Mansion.

There, a green-and-white fire tree stretched its branches.

However, compared to before, the fire tree had become much more restrained.

Because, atop its crown, a small golden bird was sleeping soundly with its wings curled up.

Seeing Luo Chen approach, the green-and-white fire tree shook its branches, as if feeling very wronged.

But Luo Chen paid it no attention. His gaze fell upon the small golden bird.

Mysterious and magnificent, possessing breathtaking beauty and majesty.

Its feathers were soft, as if meticulously woven from rosy clouds.

The crown feathers atop its head were as dazzling as the blazing sun, so noble and extraordinary.

With just one glance, Luo Chen recognized what this form represented.

A phoenix bird!

The divine phoenix from immortal and mythical legends!

But at this moment, the phoenix's golden light was dim, its figure illusory, as if it would be scattered by the wind at any moment.

Luo Chen cautiously extended his chubby little hand. The golden flames that should have been blazing were warm to him, not burning at all.

As mana was infused, the small golden bird seemed to be replenished. In a daze, it changed its sleeping position and continued its slumber.

The green-and-white little tree beneath it felt very aggrieved. It felt that portion of mana should have been its!

Luo Chen withdrew his hand, a satisfied smile spreading across his face.

Nirvana Sacred Flame!

Even he hadn't expected such an unexpected gain.

Earlier, cautious Luo Chen hadn't dared to let his main soul undergo nirvana. He had chosen to let the relatively strongest divided soul go through that process.

During the arduous nirvana process, the divided soul gradually couldn't bear it, getting closer and closer to failure.

Luo Chen had almost accepted that reality.

But unexpectedly, when he activated the nirvana method within the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture*, a golden speck was drawn out from within the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire.

The moment that golden speck merged with Luo Chen's mana, it transformed into golden flames.

Although the flames were extremely weak, far inferior to the full-strength Withering-Bloom Divine Fire, their rank was far beyond what the current Withering-Bloom Divine Fire could compare to.

These golden flames rushed into his sea of consciousness, enveloping Luo Chen's second divided soul.

Although it ultimately failed to complete the soul's nirvana, it preserved that divided soul.

Moreover, as the golden flames coursed through Luo Chen's entire body, the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture*, which had previously been impossible to master, finally opened up. Mana flowed through his limbs and bones, circulating with a familiar smoothness.

At that moment, Luo Chen knew he had entered the gateway of the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture*.

The conversion to his new lifebound technique was also declared successful!

When everything ended, Luo Chen belatedly realized what had happened.

That golden flame was the Nirvana Sacred Flame!

Its origin lay in the Demon-Fall Land—the Heaven-Refining Cauldron.

A thousand years ago, the great battle between Devil Lord Heaven-Refining and Rosycloud had far-reaching consequences.

Devil Lord Heaven-Refining had fallen, and Rosycloud had vanished without a trace.

But the Nine-Refined Beacon Fire and Nirvana Sacred Flame they left behind had been ceaselessly fighting within the Heaven-Refining Cauldron. Even after their fire spirit consciousness had been worn away, their instincts still continued to battle.

Until Luo Chen arrived, attempting to make the Heaven-Refining Cauldron recognize him as its master.

At that time, the Nirvana Sacred Flame had already lost its consciousness, but its instinct first turned to Luo Chen, because he was cultivating the Adept chapter of the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture*.

Inside the cauldron, the Nine-Refined Beacon Fire had the home-field advantage, grinding away its consciousness.

Luo Chen's body became its home ground.

The Nine-Refined Beacon Fire relentlessly pursued him inside, causing Luo Chen's body to collapse, forcing him to undergo physical nirvana.

In the end, Luo Chen succeeded. The Nine-Refined Beacon Fire disappeared, and the Nirvana Sacred Flame was also nearly dispersed, leaving only a spark that was swallowed by the then-gluttonous Withering-Bloom True Flame.

But at that time, the Withering-Bloom True Flame's grade was too low to completely devour and merge with it.

So, for all these years, the spark of the Nirvana Sacred Flame had remained stuck inside the Withering-Bloom Fire Tree.

That golden speck had been there all along!

Now, as Luo Chen cultivated the complete *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture*, the spark reignited. Not only did it recreate the form of the Nirvana Sacred Flame, but it also helped Luo Chen cross the final threshold of entry.

"What grade does it belong to?"

Luo Chen was very curious.

There was no answer. Generally speaking, once any sentient being in the world reached the fifth grade, they were no different from ordinary people, and classifying them by grade was inappropriate.

But Luo Chen knew that this Nirvana Sacred Flame, which had fought the Nine-Refined Beacon Fire for a thousand years, was definitely of a very high rank!

It was just that, at present, it was still too weak.

"Don't bully it."

Luo Chen looked at the aggrieved Withering-Bloom Fire Tree and instructed carefully.

"I know you're good at absorbing vitality, and I haven't been stingy in nurturing you over the years. Next, share a portion with it, to help it through this most fragile infancy."

The branches of the Withering-Bloom Fire Tree drooped, looking listless.

It didn't like its master's favoritism.

But it couldn't resist.

Moreover, it could feel that the little bird sleeping on it seemed to have a blood connection with it. How could it possibly ignore it?

After settling this matter, Luo Chen didn't withdraw his mind.

The infant Nascent Soul sat cross-legged beneath the fire tree, and armor gradually materialized on its body.

But the armor was tattered, almost unable to cover its body.

It was the collapsed Mystic Dust Armor!

Luo Chen felt quite pained.

He had spent so much effort refining this true artifact armor in the Northern Sea. It had rendered meritorious service in many battles, even if not always decisive. Now it had become like this.

"Huang Man is dead, but Bi Zheng, Niu Gui—just you wait!"

The infant Nascent Soul uttered fierce threats, then expelled the Mystic Dust Armor from its body.

At this point, the Mystic Dust Armor couldn't be restored simply by nourishing it with mana. It required a large amount of supplementary materials.

Then, the infant Nascent Soul hugged a small gray cauldron to its chest.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron was in much better condition.

Although it had been damaged, the cracks were healing remarkably fast!

If it received ample mana nourishment, it would likely recover completely in no time.

Only then did Luo Chen's mind slowly withdraw from his Purple Mansion.

Holding the black armor piece in his hand, a rare look of hesitation appeared on Luo Chen's face.

Should he go back?

Now that the technique had entered the gateway and the deviations in his cultivation path had been corrected, it was time to return.

But as soon as this thought arose, Luo Chen dismissed it.

Going back wouldn't mean returning to the Luo Tian Sect. He would likely have to report to the Nether Origin Sect first.

At present, his protective armor was broken, and his lifebound treasure wasn't suitable for use. If he encountered any unexpected trouble, he would have to fight for his life again.

Moreover, he had already sent Black King to notify the Luo Tian Sect.

So, this was actually a rare period of freedom before he had to go to the Nether Origin Sect.

"First, repair the Mystic Dust Armor, then familiarize myself with the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture*, striving to reach the proficient level!"

With his mind made up, Luo Chen hesitated no longer.

He took out a large amount of mineral materials from his storage ring.

After so many years of cultivation, he had accumulated a vast fortune.

Part of it had been consumed in cultivation, part had been left for the Luo Tian Sect, and the rest consisted of many "illiquid assets."

These mineral materials, mostly from the Thousand-Ren Mountains in the Demon-Fall Land, were his treasures.

Their value was incalculable!

If Luo Chen ever died in battle and someone obtained his storage ring, they would surely strike it rich!

Repairing the Mystic Dust Armor wasn't difficult for Luo Chen.

After all, when he had originally refined this treasure, to prevent failure, he had deduced every major and minor detail to perfection.

Even the six types of armor arrays, he had practiced individually to a very high level of proficiency.

Now, it was just a matter of patching it up. As long as he had the materials, it was nothing.

In less than three months, Luo Chen had roughly repaired the Mystic Dust Armor.

It still looked the same as before. The only drawback was that it was too weak and required mana nourishment and enhanced mental refinement.

Luo Chen didn't mind the trouble.

He had plenty of mana.

As for mental refinement, he could have his second divided soul do it for him.

Next!

Luo Chen exhaled lightly. First, he had the Nascent Soul put on the armor for close-contact mana nourishment. Then, in his sea of consciousness, his massive main soul merged with the divided soul that had failed its nirvana.

As the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture* circulated, his mana began to ebb and flow.

The abundant spiritual energy in the cave mansion surged toward him like rolling waves. When he opened his mouth to breathe, it was like a giant whale drinking water.

And his aura began to rise steadily.

...

In cultivation, there are no days or months. A hundred years pass in a flash, a thousand years in a blink.

In the blink of an eye, seven years had passed!

This was the tenth year since Luo Chen had returned to Green Parasol Mountain. Having stayed indoors the entire time, he had almost been forgotten.

Until a powerful aura broke through the array's concealment, scattering the clouds in the sky, making people realize that there was such a special existence within the Truth Crossing Hall.

"He broke through?"

Pure Crane was shocked. She had previously thought Luo Chen's cultivation speed was already fast enough, that in just over two hundred years, he had caught up to her nearly thousand years of bitter cultivation.

But now, she realized that wasn't Luo Chen's limit at all.

If such an existence could become a Dao companion in cultivation, he would be the absolute best potential stock.

If Luo Chen could maintain this cultivation speed, even the Ascendant realm might not be impossible.

A Dao companion of an Ascendant great power... Pure Crane inexplicably thought of that Embroidery Immortal.

That person wasn't particularly outstanding, but by attaching herself to the Ancient Demon Emperor Tian, she had reached the Great Demon Emperor realm and even attained a high status.

"I can't let such potential stock slip away!"

Pure Crane licked her red lips and hurried toward Luo Chen's cave mansion.

But the cave mansion door remained tightly shut.

Instead, outside the cave mansion, the Thunder Prison Roc was walking out with a dejected expression.

Seeing Pure Crane, he didn't even greet her.

The Thunder Prison Roc had already discovered that the vast mana Luo Chen had revealed after his recent breakthrough had surpassed his own.

Although he had now recovered more than half of his cultivation, he was still far from his peak.

But even if he truly recovered to his peak, could he settle scores with Luo Chen?

"Forget it!"

"The one who fought me back then was ultimately Nine Spirit Primogenitor. Why should I vent my anger on Luo Chen?"

Heh!

With that thought, the sense of frustration instantly dissipated.

Pure Crane curled her lips at the Thunder Prison Roc's retreating back. She liked the refined elegance of Cloud Crane Sage and the handsome appearance and boundless future of Luo Chen. A rough man like Thunder Prison didn't interest her at all.

Of course, if she had personally seen Luo Chen transform into a giant several hundred zhang tall, she might not think that way.

Pure Crane's request for an audience went unanswered.

At this moment, Luo Chen had no time to attend to her.

Because, taking advantage of the breakthrough opportunity, he was splitting his main soul.

He was now at the fifth layer of the Nascent Soul stage!

His already profound mana had surged again, not inferior to true Great Cultivators, and even slightly superior to those at the seventh layer of Nascent Soul.

As for his soul, needless to say, with the fusion of three souls, he was likely on par with the Nether Spring of before.

But Luo Chen was still unsatisfied.

At the fourth layer of Nascent Soul, he could have actually split off a third divided soul. However, considering that the human-demon war could break out at any time, he had delayed it.

Now, there was no such need.

Not because the future was worry-free, but because after cultivating the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture*, he could quickly recover from soul weakness, thus maintaining stable combat power.

Most importantly, it was time to master the *Prison-Suppressing* soul art.

Inside the cave mansion, Luo Chen's face was contorted, his muscles twisted, and his teeth were grinding audibly.

Even after experiencing this situation twice, the pain of splitting the main soul was still hard to get used to.

But this time, he controlled himself and didn't cry out in pain.

This was progress!

It felt like a long time had passed, but in reality, it was only a few dozen breaths.

Suddenly, Luo Chen's entire body relaxed.

The main soul trembled, and a mass of golden light slowly descended into the sea of consciousness.

The first divided soul and the second divided soul both looked at the golden light. It tumbled once in the golden-wave-rippling sea of consciousness, slowly condensed into a phantom, and finally took Luo Chen's form.

The third soul occupied another position, guarding Luo Chen's main soul in the center along with the other two divided souls.

Luo Chen, looking somewhat weakened, gazed at the third soul.

"Begin!"

The third soul showed no discomfort, as if it had long known what to do. It immediately began forming hand seals.

The other two powerful divided souls also formed the same hand seals.

Instantly, the golden waves in the sea of consciousness rippled, and scattered spiritual power surged toward the three souls, causing them to rise continuously.

When their forms reached the same size as Luo Chen's main soul, they stopped this process.

Then, each assumed the Embracing Origin Seal and gently exhaled.

Whoosh...

Golden light sprayed out, forming a spherical light barrier that enveloped Luo Chen and his three divided souls simultaneously.

The *Prison-Suppressing* art was complete!

Only then did Luo Chen finally relax.

With a thought, the three divided souls dispersed the spiritual power they had absorbed and returned to their original state.

He felt the defensive strength of the *Prison-Suppressing* soul art and was extremely satisfied.

The records in the *Soul-Splitting Art* were correct. This defensive soul art had no grade distinction; it only depended on the number and strength of the divided souls.

The more divided souls and the higher their realm, the stronger the defensive power of the *Prison-Suppressing* soul art.

At this point, unless there was a soul attack exceeding three times the strength of Luo Chen's main soul, it couldn't harm his main soul at all!

Among the current generation, aside from Ascendant great powers, who could possess such soul foundation?

Very few, if any.

And even if there were, Luo Chen wouldn't stop his forward progress. His divided souls would only increase, and the *Prison-Suppressing* soul art would only grow stronger.

"Han Zhan was truly a pity back then!"

"If his body had still existed, with a sea of consciousness to rely on, and using this art with nine divided souls, even with my Dragon-Slaying Art, it would have been difficult to break his defense."

Not forgetting the well-digger when drinking water, Luo Chen murmured a few words about that old acquaintance whose impression was growing increasingly blurry.

Then, he settled down to recuperate.

...

A few days later, at the entrance of the Profound Mystery Hall.

Luo Chen, dressed in white robes, stood silently for a long time before finally turning and leaving.

Rosycloud wasn't there!

The "moon-viewing maid" inside the hall naturally wouldn't and didn't dare to open the door for him.

Luo Chen didn't insist. He had only come to make a superficial gesture, to thank this "senior" who had conducted a fair transaction.

He also didn't visit Azure Frost or Nether Spring. Their relationship wasn't that good.

He only sought out Huamei.

He handed over the new Transformation Pill formula he had obtained from Devil Lord Heaven-Refining.

"This formula doesn't require the Nascent Soul blood essence of a Nascent Soul cultivator, and its scope of application isn't limited to below the fourth grade. Even fourth-grade demon beasts can use it."

Huamei was overjoyed and even wanted to pull Luo Chen aside to ask more questions about alchemy.

But Luo Chen's mind was made up to leave.

He didn't have the right to take away his old friend Haoranzi. The most he could do was this much.

At least, from now on, when the Green Parasol Mountain refined Transformation Pills, they wouldn't come to draw his blood anymore.

Even as a prisoner, his days would be a little better.

Luo Chen transformed into a beam of red light and disappeared into the bird-circled Green Parasol Mountain Range.

When he reached a thousand li outside the mountain, his figure paused.

A black light burst out of the ground and rushed toward him, finally disappearing into his arm.

With a flash of red light, he was already at the horizon.
