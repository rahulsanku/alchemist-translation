# Chapter 866: The Nether Origin Sect's Warning, Qinghe Immortal's Origins

"Since you have returned, Fellow Daoist, why not come into the residence for a chat?"

It was the voice of Lingtian City Lord.

Luo Chen's expression shifted slightly as he subconsciously looked around.

At this moment, his mana was restrained, his aura nearly nonexistent, and even his features had changed. Even an acquaintance who met him face-to-face would have trouble recognizing him at first glance.

How did Lingtian City Lord know he was back?

A formation?

No. The grand formation of Skysoaring Pass had been severely damaged through successive battles—how could it possibly have such detection capabilities!

His gaze fell on the oblivious Lingfengzi. Could it be that *he* had been discovered?

Luo Chen shook his head inwardly.

No!

Lingfengzi's ability to conceal his aura surpassed even Luo Chen's. Otherwise, in earlier years, he wouldn't have been sent to gather intelligence and walk the edge of danger.

Suddenly, Luo Chen reached up and touched his brow.

There, a water-drop pattern lay. Even though Luo Chen had deliberately concealed it, it still remained.

"What remarkable methods!"

Luo Chen snorted coldly.

Hearing the sudden snort, Lingfengzi looked at Luo Chen in confusion. "What's wrong?"

Luo Chen's face darkened. He waved his hand. "Nothing. You go back to Splendid Phoenix Palace first. I need to enter the city."

"Oh."

Lingfengzi nodded. Though he was somewhat puzzled that Luo Chen had been hesitating moments ago and now suddenly made a decision, since he knew Luo Chen was still alive, his mission was complete.

After Lingfengzi left, Luo Chen wiped his face. His muscles shifted, and his features instantly returned to normal.

His gloomy expression also adjusted immediately, and he could even force a faint smile.

No longer concealing himself, he entered Lingtian Pass openly and boldly.

Many powerful figures in the city recognized Luo Chen's face. As he appeared, gazes followed him wherever he went.

Their meanings were hard to discern, but most were filled with envy.

As for what exactly they envied, everyone understood in their hearts.

Luo Chen ignored all these stares and flew straight to the City Lord's Mansion.

After landing, the low-ranking Nether Origin Sect cultivators stationed there immediately came forward to greet him.

The leader was Ye Hong. Luo Chen had met him a few times and knew he was a direct descendant of the Lingtian City Lord.

Seeing Luo Chen, Ye Hong did not hesitate to speak: "Ye Hong pays respects to Uncle-Master Luo. My grandfather has been waiting for some time. This way, please!"

He had changed his address quite quickly!

His demeanor was respectful, his posture humble—no longer bearing the arrogance of a holy land disciple.

Well, wasn't Luo Chen now considered a part of the Nether Origin Holy Land?

Luo Chen smiled and nodded in acknowledgment. Under the other's guidance, he stepped deeper into the City Lord's Mansion.

They did not go to the usual meeting hall but arrived at a secluded courtyard protected by layers of formations.

"My grandfather is inside." Ye Hong said respectfully, then stepped aside.

Luo Chen nodded slightly and entered without hesitation.

A pavilion with a stone table. A chessboard sat quietly.

Black and white pieces formed a deadlocked endgame with no clear direction.

Ling Tian City Lord sat beside it, holding a piece but not placing it.

When he saw Luo Chen, his somewhat pale face broke into a smile.

"I knew that with Junior Brother's abilities, you would return safely."

Luo Chen stepped into the pavilion and said with a half-smile, "I'm afraid it's not confidence in me, but confidence in Black Marsh Ancestor's protective divine ability!"

On his brow, the water-drop pattern was vivid and lifelike.

Ling Tian City Lord did not hide it. He said frankly, "Indeed. The Ancestor went to great lengths to place our sect's great divine ability, Heavenborn Water, on you, fearing you might meet with mishap. With this divine ability protecting you, few below the Ascendant stage can kill you."

Such candor?

Was this also an admission that he could sense Luo Chen because of that divine ability?

Luo Chen subconsciously touched the water-drop mark on his brow.

So that was the name of this divine ability—Heavenborn Water!

Luo Chen exhaled a faint breath of turbid qi and walked to the chessboard.

"City Lord..."

"Since you have agreed to join Nether Origin, let us address each other as fellow disciples!"

"Elder Brother Ye!"

Luo Chen did not insist and changed his address.

His gaze fell on the chess endgame. He asked with concern, "I heard you were seriously injured. Shouldn't you be resting and recuperating? Why are you here pondering a chess endgame, draining your mental energy?"

"The injury is indeed severe, but it won't heal in a short time." Ling Tian City Lord did not avoid the topic. As he spoke, he couldn't help but cough lightly. "Compared to that, meeting Junior Brother in person is more important."

"Cough, cough..."

After a few coughs, Ling Tian City Lord pointed at the chessboard.

"Junior Brother Luo has been to the Central Continent, even entered Heaven-Earth Peak, and became a guest elder of Star Gate. I imagine you are well-versed in the art of chess. Can you see the direction of this endgame?"

Luo Chen swept his gaze over the endgame and said calmly, "As for the way of Go, I am merely a beginner, far from proficient. The direction of this endgame is like looking at flowers through fog to me. Elder Brother Ye, if you have something to say, please speak directly."

Ling Tian City Lord was somewhat regretful that the other was not a fellow enthusiast. He could only point at the chessboard and say:

"This endgame is a classic passed down from the Central Continent. The black pieces have already taken shape, forming a great dragon, and have created a multi-kill cycle at a critical point. Ordinary players find it difficult to break."

"It is like the earlier situation in the Eastern Wilderness. The demon race came fiercely, and the human race could not withstand them. Even setting up the Heaven-Soaring Pass only dragged the enemy into a quagmire of war."

"But this endgame was long ago solved by that Chess Saint of the Central Continent. The method used was to select points to strike the surface, using local influence to affect the whole board—both breaking the cycle and slaughtering the great dragon."

"The method our Nether Origin Sect used to resolve the Eastern Wilderness crisis was similar, only more violent."

Indeed, it was very violent—a direct decapitation strike!

And the effect was excellent.

From then on, the situation in the Eastern Wilderness had completely tilted in favor of the human race.

But as Ling Tian City Lord placed pieces to demonstrate the endgame's progression, Luo Chen's breath caught.

One by one, black pieces were removed. At the same time, white pieces also perished together. What remained in the end was only a relatively intact portion of white pieces.

Ling Tian City Lord raised his head and looked sincerely at Luo Chen.

"Junior Brother, the situation is now clear and simple. You should be able to understand it!"

Luo Chen opened his mouth, then finally nodded lightly.

Now, even a beginner could see it at a glance.

Among the various sects of the Eastern Wilderness, some were crippled, some destroyed. Even East Sun Sect, which had an excellent relationship with Nether Origin Sect, had its Sage severely wounded.

In this situation, only Nether Origin Sect stood firm!

By presenting this situation in the form of a chess endgame, he was essentially warning Luo Chen.

Do not be of two minds!

Since he had agreed to join Nether Origin Sect, he could not waver between two sides. Otherwise, his fate would be like those white pieces that perished together with the enemy—ending in obscurity.

Ling Tian City Lord observed Luo Chen's expression. This task had been assigned by Imperial Darkness Ancestor.

Black Marsh Ancestor had used force to pressure him, which might breed resentment in the other's heart.

His relationship with Luo Chen was still decent, so now he was using reason to convince him, hoping to dispel some of his grievances.

"I know you still have some friends and old acquaintances to arrange for, so I can give you some time."

"Now the situation is clear. The demon race of the Eastern Wilderness has lost its leader and is destined to be a heap of loose sand. Coupled with the removal of treaty restrictions, our human race can act freely."

"Next, I will announce in the name of the Nether Origin Holy Land: restart the war of expansion!"

"Anyone with the ambition to establish a sect may receive a holy land decree to reclaim lost territory, or even expand territory and charge into the Million Mountains."

"The Luo Tian Sect does not have many cultivators, and you will later need to cultivate at the Nether Origin Holy Land. Therefore, I do not recommend that the Luo Tian Sect choose vast territories."

"Pick one from among those several lost human domains!"

"After everything is arranged, I will return to the Nether Origin Sect with you. At that time, I will no longer be the Lingtian City Lord, but you will become the master of the Elixir Saint Hall—your status only below the three great ancestors, above billions of people!"

Luo Chen listened quietly, his lips pressed together, not saying a word.

The holy land... truly had a connection with him!

In the Northern Sea, he had once been mistaken for a remnant of the Primordial Demon Sect.

In the Central Continent, he had been recruited by the Tianyuan Dao Sect. But at that time, the Dao Sect's attitude toward him was indifferent—they accepted him but did not care. Even after he dominated the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, the Tianyuan Dao Sect still looked down on him from on high, letting him come and go as he pleased.

In comparison, the holy land of the Eastern Wilderness was... hard to describe.

They valued him, but they did not respect him!

The water-drop mark on his brow was like a collar around a dog's neck.

They fed him well and treated him well, but the collar constantly reminded Luo Chen that he could not resist.

Had he overestimated the magnanimity of Ascendant-level powerhouses?

At the same time, he had underestimated his importance to the Nether Origin Sect after the Elixir Saint's departure!

"Cough, cough. Junior Brother Luo, let's stop here for today!" Ling Tian City Lord coughed a few more times, and even his mana fluctuations leaked out uncontrollably.

Luo Chen nodded. "Elder Brother Ye, take care. Once I have arranged for my disciples and old acquaintances, I will return to the Nether Origin Holy Land with you."

"Good. If you have any problems, come directly to me."

Ling Tian City Lord smiled faintly. At least on the surface, Luo Chen was not very resistant to the Nether Origin Sect. His task was completed satisfactorily.

And why should the other have any resentment?

How many great cultivators—like Divine Origin, Joyous Union, and others—wished they could enter the Nether Origin Holy Land for secluded cultivation but could not!

Imperial Darkness Ancestor was truly overthinking things.

Watching Luo Chen's retreating figure, Lingtian City Lord lowered his head to look at the clear chessboard, calculating the course of treatment for his injuries.

Suddenly.

"Elder Brother Ye, what happened to Qinghe Immortal in the end?"

Ling Tian City Lord was momentarily stunned. He raised his head to meet Luo Chen's curious eyes.

The other truly dared to ask!

...

Qinghe Immortal!

His name was Qinghe, and his original form was also a green grain seedling!

This piece of intelligence left Luo Chen stunned for a time.

He was no stranger to green grain spirit-beasts.

Back when he was still at Dahe Market, there was a Green Grain Mountain in the nearby Ancient Plains Mountain Range, covered in first-rank green grain spirit-beasts. Like wild grass, they grew fragilely yet tenaciously.

This kind of creature had no killing power and little use.

Their only purpose, later discovered by cultivators, was to be used as fuel!

After being processed with spells, once ignited, they would release immense firepower, most suitable for alchemy and artifact forging.

Many low-rank cultivators, when hunting in the mountains, would casually harvest some mature first-rank green grain spirit-beasts and sell them to the Lianyun Merchant Alliance to supplement their livelihood.

Luo Chen himself had once used cheap green grain spirits to replace more expensive spiritual wood for refining pills.

Strictly speaking, this kind of thing was not considered a demon beast.

It was only because they had ranks and, when burned, would emit mournful cries as if they had intelligence, that people gave them the name "green grain spirit-beasts."

Based on Luo Chen's current knowledge, he would classify them as a type of plant and wood spirit-creature.

Luo Chen had never imagined that Qinghe Immortal, one of the three great ancient demons of the Eastern Wilderness, had originated from a green grain spirit-beast.

After all, no one could imagine that the most ordinary and weakest existence could give birth to intelligence and cultivate all the way to the fifth rank!

But in fact, the name itself had already declared everything!

Truly, a leaf had blocked the view of the mountain!

However, although this matter was astonishing, Qinghe Immortal's ceiling was ultimately limited by his humble origins.

His foundation was shallow, and he had no killing ability.

His time of enlightenment was too short. Even if he achieved the fifth rank, his strength was completely inferior to normal Ascendant powerhouses.

When facing the Nether Origin Sect's ambush—which even included the thunder-attribute law true intent that naturally restrained plant and wood spirits—his fate was already sealed.

Qinghe Immortal had struggled for a time, inflicting heavy casualties on the Nether Origin Sect's thirty-six Sages. But once Imperial Darkness Ancestor, one of the three great ancestors, freed his hands from the Netherworld Yellow Spring Diagram battlefield, he could no longer resist.

"White Tiger has fallen, Qinghe is defeated. Two of the three great ancient demons of the Eastern Wilderness are gone."

"Now, Qinghe Immortal has been escorted back to the Nether Origin Holy Land by the three great ancestors. He will become one of the foundations of our sect's rise!"

"Junior Brother, cultivate diligently. In the future, you may even be able to use Qinghe Immortal's original form to refine pills and make medicine."

The last few words of Lingtian City Lord echoed in Luo Chen's mind as he left Lingtian Pass. He could not help but sigh.

A fifth-rank spirit-creature, enough to rival a Taisui, had now become a prisoner, even treated as a resource.

In comparison, his own situation seemed much, much better?

Of course, this kind of self-consolation was merely a spiritual victory. Luo Chen thought about it for a moment, then cast the thought aside.

Now, he had to do something real.

...

After Lingfengzi brought back news of Luo Chen, the restless Luo Tian Sect quieted down.

That night, Luo Chen returned to Splendid Phoenix Palace without concealment.

Wang Yuan, Sima Huiniang, Gu Caiyi, and others had been waiting for a long time.

Amid a sea of joyful gazes, Luo Chen took out the Soul-Nourishing Banner in front of everyone.

"Look and see who this is." Luo Chen smiled.

Whoosh...

A nether wind blew. From within the banner, a great python swam out.

Everyone was startled.

Especially Gu Caiyi. The natal spirit treasure, Nether Python Whip, which she was constantly refining in her qi sea, trembled for a moment.

Bai Meiling excitedly looked at everyone. "It's me!"

Everyone was stunned. Gu Caiyi hesitated. "Xiao Ling?"

Bai Meiling nodded repeatedly and even swam to Gu Caiyi's side, rubbing against her body affectionately.

At first, their relationship had been ordinary. But after the vicissitudes of life, they had grown closer.

The others still found it hard to believe.

Bai Meiling, long thought to have fallen, was still alive. Moreover, the powerful aura emanating from her body declared that she had reached a realm comparable to a Sage.

Sima Huiniang couldn't help but ask, "Xiao Ling, where have you been all these years? And how did you come back with my husband?"

Bai Meiling hesitated for a moment, then looked at Luo Chen.

Luo Chen nodded. He trusted that she knew what to say and what not to say.

Next came the lively scene of old acquaintances catching up.

Luo Chen sat on a chair, drinking the Five Temple Immortal Brew. His heart was warm, and a smile was on his face.

Wasn't this exactly what he had wanted to see after all his hard work?

As long as he settled everyone next, he could go to the Nether Origin Sect without any worries.

In that holy land, he would cultivate with all his heart, striving to reach the Ascendant stage!

At that time, all problems would be solved!

Amid the laughter and joy, a guest arrived.

Or perhaps, the host had come.

When Splendid Phoenix Fairy arrived at Green Duckweed Mountain, what she saw was this harmonious scene.

"Brother Luo has returned safely. Little Sister can finally rest easy."

Luo Chen smiled. "I made you worry."

Splendid Phoenix Fairy's gaze swept over the crowd, pausing especially on Bai Meiling for a long time. Then she chatted casually with Luo Chen.

After talking for a while, not wanting to disturb their reunion with old friends, she proposed to take her leave.

Luo Chen personally saw her off.

At their parting, Luo Chen mentioned the matter of expansion.

Clearly, Splendid Phoenix Fairy visibly relaxed.

"If the Luo Tian Sect launches a war of expansion, my Splendid Phoenix Palace will definitely support you with all our strength!"

After giving this promise, Splendid Phoenix Fairy left happily.

She was naturally delighted.

Now, although the Luo Tian Sect still had few disciples, its high-end combat power had completely surpassed that of Splendid Phoenix Palace.

Luo Chen, as their supreme elder, need not be mentioned.

There was also Lingfengzi, who had recently defected—a Sage.

And now, Bai Meiling had inexplicably appeared as a Ghost Emperor!

They also had two fourth-rank spirit pets...

Green Duckweed Mountain could no longer contain the Luo Tian Sect.

She had been afraid that inviting gods would be easier than sending them away!

Now that they were voluntarily proposing to leave Splendid Phoenix Palace, she naturally wanted to support them with all her might.

Luo Chen did not refuse. The Luo Tian Sect still had many preparations to make in finding a suitable spiritual land to establish their mountain gate. Splendid Phoenix Palace's willingness to support was naturally for the best.

...

The ancient demons had fallen. The demon race of the Eastern Wilderness had lost its leader.

In this situation, the seriously injured and not yet recovered Ling Tian City Lord personally appeared, announcing the arrival of a full-scale counterattack.

Not only that, he also made promises: all major Sage-level sects could receive decrees from the Nether Origin Sect to launch wars of expansion.

Whether they were old-established sects or new powers that had risen over the past century, all could participate.

For a time, countless cultivators in the Eastern Wilderness were stirred.

New forces rubbed their hands together, eager to open mountains and establish sects.

Old forces were excited and restless, trying to return to their former lands.

Those ancient sects located in the deep rear, which had been relatively less affected, were also scheming on how to get a share of the post-war spoils.

It was at this time that the Luo Tian Sect, residing within Splendid Phoenix Palace, spread word that they were recruiting vassals to join the war of expansion.

As soon as the golden signboard of "Elixir Sect" was raised, countless people came to pledge their allegiance!
