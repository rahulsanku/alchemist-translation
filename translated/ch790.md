# Chapter 790: Nether Origin’s Recruitment, Return to the Sect, Seeing the Pill Formula Again, Azure Yang Alchemy King

The idle crane sage had left.

He had left with the Transformation Pill!

Before departing, he left behind two sentences.

“Luo Tian and White Crane, advance and retreat together.”

“Within one year, deliver fifty spirit cranes!”

In the face of the Transformation Pill, words like “the precious fowls of the White Crane Sect cannot be traded externally” were merely words spoken for the sake of formality.

Luo Chen was extremely satisfied with this outcome.

On his journey to the Demon-Fall Land, he had refined five Transformation Pills within the Pill Realm.

Now, by bringing out just one, he had gained the friendship of two Sage-level cultivators, forged an alliance between Luo Tian Sect and another Sage-level Upper Sect, and even opened a channel for trading spirit cranes.

Such a triple gain was truly a delight!

However, Luo Chen did not bask in his complacency for long. There were still others waiting for him.

And not just one person!

……

“First in Jade Cauldron Foundation Establishment, the Northern Sea Wilderness Wanderer, a Grandmaster of the Alchemy Dao… Fellow Daoist Luo, the surprises you bring me are truly endless!”

Seated on a chair, the City Lord of Lingtian toyed with the high-grade Hongyuan Pill, his expression complex.

Some things were not so deeply felt when viewed merely on paper.

But upon actually witnessing the terrifying medicinal power within the high-grade Hongyuan Pill, the feeling of being stunned made him understand what an “Alchemy Grandmaster” truly was!

Such pills were extremely rare even within the Nether Origin Sect.

Yet the man before him could casually produce one, demonstrating the depth of his alchemy skill.

Faced with the City Lord of Lingtian directly exposing his background as the Northern Sea Wilderness Wanderer, Luo Chen, who had just returned, merely raised an eyebrow.

“In the end, it is all just Luo Chen of Luo Tian!”

The City Lord of Lingtian slowly raised his head. “Is it not that you are a Guest Elder of the Star Gate?”

Luo Chen chuckled lightly. “There is no great difference.”

“No, the difference is vast!” The City Lord of Lingtian straightened his posture and put away the Hongyuan Pill. “Luo Tian is in the Eastern Wilderness; the Star Gate is in the Central Continent!”

Luo Chen frowned and fell silent.

Ever since he stepped into the Eastern Wilderness and actively revealed his identity as a Star Gate Guest Elder, certain matters had already been placed on the table.

The identity of a Star Gate Guest Elder had become a protective talisman, allowing him to act with convenience.

But at the same time, given the precedent of Star Gate Guest Elders bringing their clansmen and disciples to the Central Continent, Luo Chen’s intentions had also been exposed.

Clearly, after the incident of the Alchemy Saint’s departure, the Nether Origin Sect was very sensitive to such matters.

The City Lord of Lingtian’s words at this moment had almost made the Nether Origin Sect’s attitude clear.

Luo Chen pondered for a moment. “Is it not possible to take them away?”

“You should be clear that they are not important. Even Wang Yuan can only enter my personal estimation.” The City Lord of Lingtian said.

Luo Chen nodded. “What is important is me!”

“You are very clever. Truly someone who could break through to Sage on your own.” The City Lord of Lingtian praised, then changed the subject. “Why not consider joining my Nether Origin Sect? What the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect can give you, we can give you as well.”

Luo Chen was curious. “Is this your intention, or the intention of your sect’s great power?”

The City Lord of Lingtian was speechless.

Regarding the disposition of Luo Chen, the sect had indeed not yet given a clear answer.

It was only because he had witnessed Luo Chen’s bearing, demeanor, and alchemy skill that he could not help but feel a sense of cherishing talent, and thus took the initiative to recruit him.

Heaving a sigh, the City Lord of Lingtian said helplessly, “If you were to choose for yourself, which side would you pick?”

Luo Chen smiled but did not answer. To be valued by a Holy Land was already an immense fortune; how could there be a chance to choose?

If he truly expressed his stance, it would be easy to offend people.

Even if this City Lord of Lingtian seemed like an easy person to talk to.

Seeing Luo Chen silent, the City Lord of Lingtian thought for a moment and changed the question.

“Now, the myriad beasts of the Eastern Wilderness’s million mountains have all emerged, and the Northern Sea Demon Alliance is running rampant. Tell me, will my Nether Origin Sect suppress the demon chaos first, or will the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect conquer the Northern Sea first?”

Luo Chen hesitated for a moment at this question, but ultimately gave his answer.

“I believe it will be the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!”

“You have that much confidence in them? My Nether Origin is fighting on home ground, possessing the advantage of location and popular support.”

“The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect occupies the fertile lands of the Central Continent, has nurtured elite forces for three thousand years, and harbors the ambition to sweep across the world, encompass the universe, and swallow the Mountains and Seas. Their campaign against the Northern Sea has been launched with full force, committing the strength of an entire province to the attack. Meanwhile, the Nether Origin Holy Land remains still, merely using the various sects of the Eastern Wilderness to trade land for beasts, carrying firewood to put out a fire. Although my insight is shallow and my vision short, I can still see the difference in resolve between the two sides when facing the demon calamity. Both are Holy Lands of a province; I do not doubt the Nether Origin Sect’s foundation in terms of strength. But the greater one’s resolve for a matter, the greater the strength one will expend. In this war, Heavenly Origin will be the first!”

“Haha, you have that much confidence in the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. However, you underestimate my Nether Origin Sect!”

The City Lord of Lingtian laughed heartily, not agreeing with Luo Chen’s statement.

As for the reason, he did not elaborate to Luo Chen.

Instead, he steered the conversation to another matter.

“The method for producing spirit stones has been recorded on the jade slip I gave you. However, before learning this method, you must swear an oath not to pass it on.”

“This is the rule for every Adept-level sect to become a Sage-level Upper Sect!”

“Ordinary spirit stone raw ore contains a vast amount of spiritual energy. Common methods cannot precisely cut it, nor can it be easily mined on a large scale. If the method for producing spirit stones were to become widespread, it would be difficult to prevent rogue cultivators from committing the foolish act of killing the goose that lays the golden eggs, severing the veins and digging out the source.”

“This method is a symbol of an Upper Sect. Only Sage-level Upper Sects can stably mine and produce spirit stones, controlling the economic lifeline of a region.”

“At the same time, attached to the jade slip is a method for nurturing and restoring spiritual veins under the condition of restrained mining of spirit stone raw ore.”

“Of course, the current Luo Tian Sect does not have its own mountain gate headquarters, let alone a fourth-grade spiritual vein. This method is somewhat useless. But if you have the opportunity to establish a mountain gate later, you can use this to quickly acquire a sum of spirit stones as cultivation resources for your Sage realm.”

At the end, the City Lord of Lingtian seemed somewhat unsatisfied and brought up the matter they had discussed earlier.

“Do not think too highly of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. Even if you really bring these few hundred people of Luo Tian Sect over, they will eventually be broken up, separated, and integrated into the various great families and clans. By then, you will be powerless, and their fate may not be better than it is now.”

After saying this, the City Lord of Lingtian did not stay any longer and left directly.

Luo Chen was left alone in the corridor, pursing his lips, his brows slightly furrowed.

After the other party’s reminder, he realized that his previous thoughts had indeed been somewhat off.

He had only seen the benefits of the Central Continent, but had overlooked the fact that the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect’s conditions for the dependents brought by Star Gate Guest Elders were actually extremely strict.

If they were family disciples, they could only join the Dao Sect after five hundred years.

If they were sect apprentices, they would have to be separated, either establishing their own families to spread their branches, or integrating into the various great families and clans.

Especially now that the great war had begun, if he really brought this group of people over, their future fate would definitely be worse than it is now.

At the very least, this group of Luo Tian Sect could still huddle together for warmth at present.

In fact, this could not be said to be a lack of thorough consideration on Luo Chen’s part.

After all, he had not expected Sima Huiniang and the others to be so persistent, managing to preserve for him a force that was originally merely at the Adept level.

If it were only a few people, or a dozen or twenty, he, Luo Chen, could just take them away.

Now that there were too many people, they had instead become a burden.

Suddenly, Luo Chen’s brows relaxed, and a slight smile curled at the corner of his mouth.

“How can it be called a burden? These are all people who are loyal to me, who respect me, who love me!”

Stepping under the moonlight, Luo Chen walked towards the next guest waiting for him.

……

Calling them guests was not quite accurate.

The other party was actually the host!

Under the moonlight, in a pavilion halfway up the mountain, a beautiful woman leaned against the wind, overlooking the vast earth.

“Great Palace Master, sorry to have kept you waiting.”

Fenghua Fairy did not turn around. Instead, she pointed at the scenery below, faintly visible under the silver-white moonlight, and sighed softly.

“This land has been managed by my Fenghua Palace for two thousand years. Sage Luo, if it were you, would you be willing to give it up to others?”

“I naturally would not be willing to give it up.”

Luo Chen walked to her side, the scent of the woman’s ripeness wafting between his nostrils.

“Similarly, I will not set my sights on this land.”

Fenghua Fairy was startled. She had not expected Luo Chen to be so direct, seeing through her thoughts.

As a result, some of the words she had prepared in her heart could not be continued.

The woman could only stiffly say, “Although the great mountains and rivers are not easily given to others, this hundred li of green river can be lent to you, Sage, for temporary residence. That is no trouble.”

Luo Chen nodded. “Thank you for your kindness, Great Palace Master. I do indeed need such a place to stay now. However, the produce of this hundred li will still be the wealth of Fenghua Palace. A gentleman does not take what others cherish.”

Fenghua Fairy was stunned. Although the hundred li of green river was not large, the medicinal herbs produced here were among the best in the entire Fenghua Domain.

He, Luo Chen, did not even want this?

Luo Chen suddenly asked, “I heard that your sect has obtained the Netherpassage Pill formula and the stable refining method for high-grade Jade Dew Pills?”

Fenghua Fairy did not deny it, but generously admitted the matter.

“It came from Qu Lingjun. Although it was taking advantage of his vulnerability, my Fenghua Palace also paid a considerable price for it. If you, Fellow Daoist, want those back…”

“No need.”

Luo Chen shook his head, directly interrupting her words.

Fenghua Fairy frowned, utterly confused.

The man before her had no interest in the Fenghua Domain, no interest in the produce of the hundred li of green river, and did not even want the pill formulas he had previously researched. What did he want?

Suddenly.

“Lingjun has served Fenghua Palace for a hundred years. That should be enough. It is time for him to return.”

Fenghua Fairy’s red lips parted slightly, her expression one of astonishment. “Just for this?”

“Yes.” Luo Chen said calmly. “He is my disciple. Even if his aptitude is poor and the Great Dao is hopeless, he should not be left to wander outside.”

Fenghua Fairy fell silent.

After a long while, she nodded slightly, granting Luo Chen this face.

Not only because Luo Chen had given up a lot, but also because Qu Lingjun was already over two hundred years old, with only a few decades left before his lifespan ran out. Given his current realm, breaking through to the Adept realm was extremely difficult.

His value for use was almost gone.

Letting him return to Luo Tian Sect was also a way to do him a favor.

Luo Chen placed his hand on the pillar of the pavilion, exerting a little force. “Great Palace Master, if the Motian Cliff dares to reach out again in the future, just beat them back. If the Five Elements Divine Sect helps them, feel free to call me along.”

This was repaying a favor?

Fenghua Fairy smiled radiantly. “Are you not afraid of the old monster of Divine Origin?”

The Supreme Elder of the Divine Sect—Sage Divine Origin!

A late-stage Sage Grand Cultivator!

Luo Chen’s eyes narrowed slightly. He wanted to see that old monster.

Ever since returning to the Eastern Wilderness, he had been pressing the Five Elements Divine Sect, his purpose being to force out Sage Divine Origin and see what his current condition was.

According to rumors, in that battle at Green Parasol Mountain, the Nether Origin great power was blocked outside the mountain. Of the three late-stage Sages, two died and one was injured.

The old monster Divine Origin was the only Grand Cultivator to survive, and he was severely injured.

Even when the Five Elements Divine Sect was attacked by the demon race, he did not personally intervene.

This man had always been watching him covetously, and his acceptance of Luo Tian Sect was ill-intentioned. He, Luo Chen, would inevitably cross paths with this man sooner or later.

If he could force him out and learn the old monster’s condition in advance, many things would become much more convenient.

……

The last person Luo Chen saw was, unsurprisingly, the ancestor of the Divine Talisman Sect, who had been waiting for a long time.

Many years ago, this man had a rather resounding Daoist title.

But as the years passed, people gradually stopped using that title and respectfully called him “Elder Fu” instead.

The other party had waited until late at night to see Luo Chen, which showed how urgent his mood was.

And his purpose went without saying.

The Nascent Soul Pill!

Faced with the old man’s eager gaze, Luo Chen slowly nodded.

“If you trust me, you can first let me take a look at the pill formula.”

“Fellow Daoist can truly refine realm-breaking pills?”

Just as realm-breaking pills were far more precious than ordinary cultivation pills, their refining difficulty was also greater than ordinary pills.

After all, to contain the terrifying medicinal power needed to break through a major realm within a single pill required extremely high alchemy skill from the alchemist.

In the vast Eastern Wilderness, there were countless alchemists, but very few could refine realm-breaking pills.

Elder Fu, his face full of excitement, took out a newly inscribed pill formula from his storage ring.

Luo Chen took it, and with just a single glance, his heart stirred.

It was exactly the same!

He asked casually, as if unintentionally:

“Where did this formula come from? I find it somewhat familiar. I suppose it is not your sect’s inheritance!”

Elder Fu’s excited expression gradually faded, becoming somewhat awkward. But in the end, under Luo Chen’s gaze, he revealed the truth.

“Obtained from the Medicine King Sect.”

“Oh?”

Luo Chen was somewhat surprised. It was different from what he had imagined. He had thought it came from the Azure Pill Valley.

After all, this pill formula was the same one he had used to refine the Nascent Soul Pill back then.

Exactly the same!

There were only three parties that possessed this pill formula: Tianfan City, Han Zhan, and the Azure Pill Valley.

Han Zhan had gone with him to the Northern Sea. Tianfan City had two other Sages who survived before its fall. That left the Azure Pill Valley as the weakest.

If the Divine Talisman Sect wanted to seize this pill formula, ten times out of ten they would have started with the Azure Pill Valley.

He had not expected that this pill formula did not come from the Azure Pill Valley, but from another Sage-level Upper Sect that was dripping with wealth!

After uttering the name of the Medicine King Sect, Elder Fu seemed to have shed some psychological burden and began to recount the past events in detail.

“Before the Jade Cauldron Domain was breached, the Azure Pill Valley was the first to be destroyed.”

“But it was not destroyed by demon beasts. Instead, a disciple named He Yun colluded with the Medicine King Sect from within, annexing the Azure Pill Valley. All their alchemy treasures were plundered, including this ancient Nascent Soul Pill formula.”

“However, the plunderer was eventually plundered. The Medicine King Sect possessed terrifying wealth, but lacked absolute martial strength to protect it. When the chaos came, many strong men fished in troubled waters, causing the Medicine King Sect to suffer a great loss of vitality. Later, it declined to the point where the sect could not even be preserved.”

“I was one of those who fished in troubled waters.”

“Originally, I only wanted to snatch the Medicine King Sect’s Nascent Soul Pill to help my sect’s juniors condense their Nascent Soul. I never expected that Lin Qingxuan, that junior, would get it by luck. What I obtained was only a part of the pill formulas treasured by the Medicine King Sect, including this ancient Nascent Soul Pill formula.”

Elder Fu spoke in a muddled and general manner.

But within those few simple sentences, the complexity and twists and turns of the story contained were enough to make one imagine a thousand possibilities.

Luo Chen understood how Lin Qingxuan had managed to form his Nascent Soul.

He also remembered who that Azure Pill Valley disciple named “He Yun” was. He was a member of the Yu Xuan He family, with a Daoist title called Xuan Yunzi.

He had also noticed signs that this person had been possessed by someone else back then.

He had not expected that it was actually a spy planted by the Medicine King Sect within the Azure Pill Valley.

The past was too complicated. Even the Medicine King Sect, which he had once looked up to, was gone. Luo Chen had no intention of delving deeper.

Holding the jade slip, he said frankly, “I can refine this pill. You provide the materials!”

Elder Fu let out a long sigh of relief!

Sure enough, his willingness to expose even his “shameful deeds” to the other party had finally earned him some trust and goodwill.

He nodded without hesitation. “My Divine Talisman Sect can be considered to have some assets. The required materials have long been prepared. You, Fellow Daoist, just need to speak.”

Luo Chen smiled slightly. “I do not doubt the wealth of the Divine Talisman Sect. But have you, Elder, prepared my remuneration for refining the pill?”

“I have three strands of Crimson Cloud Qi!”

“Not enough.”

“Seven types of pill formulas from the Medicine King Sect.”

“Still not enough. The difficulty of refining this pill, Elder Fu should know in your heart. Otherwise, it would not have fallen to me, Luo.”

“Then what else do you want? To make up for it with ten million spirit stones?”

“You jest. How could mere spirit stones bear the price of me personally taking action? I have heard that your sect excels in talisman-making, as well as matters related to talisman-making…”

“You want high-grade talismans? That is not difficult. I can give you ten third-grade talismans and one fourth-grade talisman!”

“No, I want talisman skins, a large quantity of talisman skins! If there are excellent methods for controlling talismans, that would naturally be even better.”

Elder Fu stared at Luo Chen in astonishment.

He did not want finished talismans, but the most basic talisman skins?

Had he heard correctly!

……

The Divine Talisman Sect’s methods for controlling talismans were one of their core inheritances, so naturally they could not be given to Luo Chen.

But mere talisman skins were not a problem.

Especially since the Divine Talisman Sect had mastered the method of making talisman skins from beast hides. In the current great war between humans and demons, the raw materials were inexhaustible. Elder Fu, at the cost of an extra fifty percent, finally negotiated the remuneration for inviting Luo Chen to refine the pill.

Three strands of Crimson Cloud Qi, seven types of pill formulas from the Medicine King Sect, and a total of ten thousand talisman skins from first-grade to third-grade.

In addition, Elder Fu also did him a favor, promising that if Luo Chen wanted fourth-grade talisman skins in the future, his Divine Talisman Sect would also open the door wide for him.

Thus, this cooperation was reached.

But what Luo Chen had gained was far more than this!

In the dead of night, his divine sense slowly withdrew from his storage pouch.

Inside were the raw materials for refining the Nascent Soul Pill, all kinds, enough for twenty portions!

Even the precious Five Elements Lotus Platform had two!

“The Divine Talisman Sect is truly wealthy!”

Luo Chen sighed with emotion.

With his alchemy skill, refining two or three Nascent Soul Pills from so many materials was more than enough.

The surplus materials would naturally belong to him.

This was an unspoken rule among alchemists, and few would question it.

As long as one had the skill to refine the pills the employer needed, the saved materials would be considered additional remuneration.

If one did not have the skill?

Like the Azure Pill Valley back then, they had gathered the entire sect’s strength but had not managed to refine a single Nascent Soul Pill, wasting manpower and resources.

“Fortunately, there is an extra Five Elements Lotus Platform. Although its quality is far inferior to the one I obtained back then, it can still be used as an auxiliary tool for cultivation.”

Luo Chen smiled, a look of relief on his face.

The overall direction of this banquet had been within his control.

It could be imagined that his reputation for excelling at alchemy would be spread by these people.

Then, what he needed to do next was very simple.

Refine pills to build his reputation!

Sharpen his skills!

Study the talisman dao, and incidentally teach his disciples.

It sounded a bit tedious, but taking it step by step would not feel so hard.

A faint fragrance wafted to his nostrils.

“Husband, it is time to rest.”

Luo Chen turned around. Amidst the dim lights, Huiniang was waiting for him with a lantern, looking lovely.

He did not linger. He strode towards the woman.

Taking the red firefly lantern with one hand, and wrapping the other around her slender waist, he rubbed her delicate skin through the light gauze.

“What about Caiyi? Is she still that shy?”

“She is preparing the warm pool. She said she wants you to soak well and relieve your fatigue.”

“Good, then let us hurry. The night is deep and the dew is heavy. We must not let the water get cold.”

With the means of cultivators, how could the water easily get cold?

A flush of red appeared on the woman’s face. She lowered her brows and smiled, but did not refuse.
