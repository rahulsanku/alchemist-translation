# Chapter 1162: Dual Cultivation of Body and Qi, the Mist of Annihilation

The path of martial arts—each realm, a mountain pass.

The first two realms were manageable, focused primarily on laying a foundation. The true qualitative transformation came with the third realm, the Furnace Realm.

Upon reaching this realm, a martial artist could rely on the condensed qi-blood furnace to directly absorb and convert external world energy.

Only then did they gain the explosively increased lifespan and the foundation to climb upward.

As for the fourth realm of body refinement, the Leakless Realm?

Based on Luo Chen's extensive reading of numerous texts and his personal experience, the fourth martial realm, which clearly corresponded to a Sage's Nascent Soul stage, seemed more like a transitional phase!

Yes, it sounded somewhat unbelievable.

But this was precisely how Luo Chen saw it.

After a cultivator reached the Sage stage, their Nascent Soul could simply resonate with heaven and earth. Those with exceptional talent could even glimpse a corner of the laws' true meaning.

Therefore, Sage-stage cultivators underwent earth-shattering qualitative changes in both cultivation and combat.

However, the changes for Leakless Realm martial artists in this regard were not very significant. They mainly continued the habits of the Furnace Realm.

Perhaps the only change lay in the word "Leakless" itself.

After cultivating the Leakless Golden Body, a martial artist became an existence akin to a *Pixiu* or a *Taotie*—a mythical beast that only consumes, never excretes; only takes in, never lets out!

Vast amounts of external energy entered the body, were refined and absorbed by the qi-blood furnace, and transformed into the martial artist's own foundation.

Furthermore, because all the body's acupoints were sealed, any possibility of energy leakage was blocked.

Thus, a Leakless Realm martial artist embarked on a cultivation trajectory that only went upward, never downward.

Once decline set in, it was like an avalanche or the sky collapsing—irreversible and beyond recovery.

This was the case for a failed returnee like Xiangfei.

If Luo Chen hadn't first used himself as bait to lure out the chaotic energy that damaged her acupoints, and then used the top-grade Meridian-Repairing Pills he had brought even during his ascension to mend her meridians, Xiangfei's end would have been near!

"If one keeps going upward, either they burst from within, or the world they inhabit can no longer sustain or support them. Then there is only one final outcome."

Luo Chen thought silently, and the end result for a fifth-realm martial artist had already appeared in his mind.

See the Divine, Become Invincible, Shatter the Void!

Use strength to prove the Dao, and depart for a new world that can bear one's existence!

"I'm thinking too far ahead. Let's first consider the cultivation technique for the next step!"

Luo Chen's cultivation technique for the first three realms was the *Divine Astral Fighting Qi* authored by Wang Yuan.

This technique originated from the Mountains and Seas Realm, where the path of body refinement had declined.

Even with Luo Chen's help and the support of the Luo Tian Sect, the limit Wang Yuan could reach was merely the fourth realm of body refinement.

As for how to cultivate to the fifth realm, Wang Yuan had made a large number of hypothetical guesses but had not put them into practice.

"Perhaps the time was too short. It's been so long since I ascended, I wonder if he has finished deducing the subsequent conjectures?"

"Fortunately, I also have another set of higher-grade body refinement techniques in my possession."

"*Demon Aspect Art*, a demon race's body refinement technique?"

Luo Chen stroked the *Demon Aspect Art* in his hand, his expression somewhat hesitant.

Calling it a "book" was inaccurate; it was actually a demon-man statue with two horns on its head.

Its veins were crystalline, its face ferocious.

Black qi swirled around it, engraved with a technique written in demon script.

This technique had come into his possession by chance.

It originated from the ruins of the Primordial Demon Sect.

The script had already been deciphered by Layman Fengling, and Luo Chen naturally recognized it after learning it.

This was a body refinement technique of unknown grade but with immense power.

And miraculously, cultivators could also practice it concurrently!

The reason was that its foundation lay in tempering qi and blood. When facing an enemy, it could convert qi and blood, condensing a demon aspect outside the body. This demon aspect possessed vast power. As long as the cultivator's qi and blood power was not exhausted, the demon aspect would be immortal and indestructible—truly wondrous and mysterious.

This point was very important.

Body refinement and qi cultivation, when taken to their highest levels, were two opposing paths.

A martial artist sought a golden body without leaks, not allowing any energy to escape, while a cultivator needed to open all the body's acupoints to draw in the power of heaven and earth. This clearly showed the vast difference between them.

The world was not without those who cultivated both body and qi, but they would often try their best to avoid this contradiction, seeking a cultivation method that focused on one path.

Take Luo Chen, for example!

At his peak state in the Mountains and Seas Realm, he possessed both the Ascendant stage and the fifth-rank Archaic stage.

But in reality, his main focus was on the qi cultivation stage. The fifth-rank Archaic body refinement stage only tempered his physical body, without delving deeply into the path of qi and blood.

This method of concurrent cultivation came at a great cost!

The difficulties involved were beyond the imagination of ordinary people.

Even Luo Chen had to invest a massive amount of resources to forcibly cultivate an Archaic body that could withstand tribulation without being damaged.

And this *Demon Aspect Art* before him mentioned the point that "as long as the cultivator's qi and blood power is not exhausted, the demon aspect will be immortal and indestructible." This showed that this technique had already bypassed that contradiction.

It indicated that this technique could achieve dual cultivation of body and qi.

"Is it a demon aspect cultivated to a high level?"

The *Demon Aspect Art* recorded that when a martial artist or cultivator reached a certain realm by practicing this technique, they could condense a demon aspect from their main body. At this point, the demon aspect could concurrently cultivate another system on behalf of the main body, without affecting it.

"Sounds somewhat similar to the method of an external incarnation?"

Thinking of "external incarnation," Luo Chen fell into deep thought.

His gaze occasionally swept across the desolate scenery of the Gobi Desert, and he gradually came to a decision.

Being in the Guixu, martial arts were the mainstream and the only path for the present.

Whether he could continue his immortal destiny in the future was still unknown.

Therefore, he must stand firm in the present and consider the future later.

The *Demon Aspect Art* was the seed for continuing his immortal destiny in the future.

"But switching my main cultivation technique now is a bit rushed. Who knows if this demon race body refinement method has hidden dangers, and it might not necessarily be compatible with my physique."

"For the possibility of dual cultivation of body and qi in the future, I still need to make certain modifications based on my own situation."

"Perhaps, I should follow Wang Yuan's precedent next."

After a long while, Luo Chen emerged from his contemplation, revealing a knowing smile.

He was not unfamiliar with what he needed to do next.

Based on the existing techniques and his accumulated knowledge and experience, he would recreate a body refinement technique suitable for himself.

This was something Wang Yuan had always been doing, and Luo Chen himself had done it before.

But this time, Luo Chen was filled with fighting spirit!

...

On the Gobi Desert, Xiangfei drove the carriage, slowly advancing towards Wuyou City.

Her mood was excellent at the moment.

After having her chronic ailment completely removed, she not only avoided death but, most importantly, continued her path of martial arts cultivation.

Compared to her mood when she had dejectedly returned from Wuyou City, it was like heaven and earth.

At this point, she didn't know whether to resent Xiao Feng's instigation or be grateful for that one act of audacity.

But one question still lingered in her mind.

"Returning to Wuyou City, should I challenge the Nine Rings of Annihilation again?"

A hint of hesitation occasionally flickered across the woman's face.

There were many routes from Yixian City to Wuyou City, and the fastest and safest one was not the one they were currently on.

But at the start of the journey, Luo Chen had specifically chosen this "more dangerous" route.

As for the reason, he hadn't said.

But Xiangfei could vaguely guess one or two.

Perhaps it was because she had spent a long time with Luo Chen. For this deeply calculating senior who never showed his emotions, she could gradually grasp his thoughts and style of conduct.

His style of conduct was always steady, but at critical moments, he would never hesitate to take a risk.

This journey was likely the same!

However, three months had passed, and with only a short half of the journey left to Wuyou City, Luo Chen's expectations might be dashed.

Suddenly!

Xiangfei's expression froze.

A tall figure had already emerged from the carriage, standing beside her, gazing into the distance.

"Senior, what's wrong?"

"Is that the Mist of Annihilation?"

Luo Chen's characteristic steady tone sounded, seemingly asking, but actually confirming.

Xiangfei was stunned for a moment, then followed Luo Chen's gaze to the boundless Gobi Desert hundreds of miles away.

At some point, the horizon had turned pitch black.

A vast amount of mist was rolling in, covering everything.

The black sky was like a ferocious beast opening its giant maw, wanting to devour everything in this world.

Xiangfei's whole body trembled, and she quickly said, "Yes, that kind of black mist is the Mist of Annihilation, one of the three great heavenly calamities!"

Looking at the mist in the distance, a thoughtful expression appeared in Luo Chen's eyes.

The Mist of Annihilation!

Legend said it appeared once every ten thousand years, annihilating all living beings.

Even when the cycle of an era turned, it would enter the Great Annihilation Era. Not to mention living beings, even the heavens, the earth, and the sun, moon, and stars would be annihilated along with it.

Of course, that level of Mist of Annihilation had not appeared for a very long time.

In the past thousand years, even when it did appear, it was mostly small-scale Mist of Annihilation, only posing a certain danger to humans and livestock.

Moreover, the locations where it appeared were mostly in the desolate borderlands near the seventy-two cities.

This travel route was chosen by Luo Chen. He wanted to try his luck before entering Wuyou City, to see if he could witness the so-called "Mist of Annihilation" and whether it was as terrifying as the legends said.

"You continue forward, don't stop. I'll go and come back!"

As he spoke, Luo Chen jumped off the carriage, heading in the opposite direction, charging towards that vast black misty sky.

"Senior!"

Xiangfei couldn't stop him in time, and could only watch as Luo Chen's figure became increasingly blurry.

Without time to marvel at Luo Chen's escape speed, Xiangfei drove the carriage, making it go even faster.

A thousand miles, reached in an instant.

Soon after, Luo Chen stood atop a tall stone pillar, looking with great curiosity at the rolling black mist beneath his feet.

According to the records, this kind of small-scale Mist of Annihilation was actually not dangerous.

The strength of any major city could defend against it.

The real danger was the "Mist Beasts" mixed within, also known as "Chaos Beasts."

Those things killed people on sight and devoured livestock when encountered. That was why tall city walls like those of Yixian City and Mofeng City were needed for defense.

Since he had encountered it this time, and Luo Chen was confident in his own strength, he naturally wanted to see it for himself.

He took a deep breath, sealed all the acupoints in his body, and drew the Yuantu Sword from his waist, standing tall and solemn.

Finally!

The black mist washed over him!

At that moment, Luo Chen's whole body stiffened. He felt his blood turn cold, his thoughts slow, and even the dormant soul in his sea of consciousness felt a sense of shock, almost waking up.

Luo Chen snorted lightly. The vast qi and blood within his body began to circulate, and the Blood-Refined Divine Astral burst forth, forming an astral protective shield outside his body.

"This feeling is too terrifying. Is it just me, or do others feel this way too?"

He didn't understand.

Apart from the strange color of the mist, there was nothing different about it in other aspects, yet it gave him an extremely dangerous feeling.

And deep within the mist, something was coming.

He activated his Crimson-Gold Pupils, but the turbid mist actually blocked his vision.

At this moment, Luo Chen was even more surprised.

He had been cultivating these eyes since the Qi Refinement stage. Through countless enhancements and transformations over the years, they now had the effects of seeing through illusions, breaking illusions, and even seeing through the void!

Let alone ordinary mist, even the divine abilities cast by Ascendant-level great powers might not be able to block or confuse his eyes.

But now, he couldn't even penetrate this thin layer of mist?

"Truly bizarre!"

Luo Chen held his breath, sword in hand, facing the enemies emerging one by one from the mist.

Chaos Beasts!

Through their heart-to-heart connection, the Yuantu Sword let out a clear, resonant sword cry.

...

Swish!

A streak of blood light tore across the sky.

It finally landed on a carriage traveling at extreme speed.

Xiangfei stood up in surprise to welcome him, "Senior, you've finally returned."

"Mm."

Luo Chen nodded, his expression somewhat gloomy.

He looked down at the long sword in his hand. The Yuantu Sword, which had broken through to the spirit treasure level, should have been chillingly sharp, but now it appeared somewhat dim.

Those strange Chaos Beasts, in his opinion, were not very strong.

With his strength, wielding the Yuantu Sword, he could kill them one by one with a single strike.

But after a short period of slaughter, the sword spirit of the Yuantu Sword sent him a warning. It seemed that if he continued fighting, the sword spirit of this great baleful sword would also suffer severe damage.

"No wonder it's the Mist of Annihilation, the foremost of the three great heavenly calamities!"

Luo Chen spat out a mouthful of turbid qi and sheathed his sword.

Xiangfei couldn't help but sigh with emotion, "Senior is truly bold and skilled. In the past, when major cities encountered this scale of Mist of Annihilation, they would only defend within their walls, slowly fending off the Chaos Beasts' attacks, barely holding on until the mist dissipated. For you to charge in alone and fight your way out—such strength is truly rare in this world."

If it were before, Luo Chen would have been happy to hear such flattering words.

But now, he was not in the mood.

He couldn't see through the true nature of that mist, couldn't penetrate its essence.

This was hard for Luo Chen to accept.

If he could release his divine sense, would he be able to understand its essence?

He had no answer to this question, but his subconscious told him that it would probably end inconclusively.

He had simply accepted a reality.

If the once-in-ten-thousand-year Mist of Annihilation struck, all living beings would be buried within it, even Leakless Realm martial artists!

And if the legendary cycle of the era turned, the Great Annihilation Era arrived, this very world would likely perish as well.

"No wonder even existences as powerful as the Void Refining True Lord Xiao Lan and the Lord of Wuyou City, the foremost of the seventy-two cities, are thinking about how to escape the Guixu."

"They must have personally witnessed the power of the Mist of Annihilation and seen the danger within it."

"This time, when I go to Wuyou City, I must find out the exact time of the next Great Mist of Annihilation!"

A sense of crisis brought on by the passage of time settled over Luo Chen's heart.
