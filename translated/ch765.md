# Chapter 765: The Might of Origin, One Against Three

Seven years ago.

After the Heavenly Secret Pill personally refined by the Alchemy Saint before his departure was refined by the Withering-Bloom True Flame, surging spiritual resonance immediately poured out.

The abundance of that spiritual resonance far exceeded Luo Chen's imagination!

If the spiritual resonance inside an ordinary fourth-tier illusion core could be described as strands and wisps, then the amount of spiritual resonance within the Heavenly Secret Pill could be called a "current"!

It was formed by blending dozens or even a hundred strands of spiritual resonance together, all of it completely confined within the pill, and could be called vast!

It was precisely after seeing that surging spiritual resonance that Luo Chen understood why people like Xiang Qi and Shi Ju coveted the Heavenly Secret Pill so deeply.

In a hundred years inside Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, the limit of what one could obtain from hunting illusion beasts would probably be about this much.

After Luo Chen saw that current of spiritual resonance, he did indeed feel some regret.

But what was done was done, and he could only let it be.

To guard against the unexpected, aside from his first divided soul, he placed all his attention on watching the transformation of the Withering-Bloom True Flame.

If anything truly went wrong, at least he would still have time to remedy it.

However, this time, everything went smoothly, and no further accidents occurred.

With this vast current of spiritual resonance supplementing it, the spent Withering-Bloom True Flame once again radiated power and began climbing upward.

At the core of the true flame, that streak of Nirvana Sacred Flame remained indestructible and could not be refined, and it even continued influencing the transformation of the Withering-Bloom True Flame.

But that spiritual resonance did not merely provide origin qi!

At that moment, the Withering-Bloom True Flame resonated with this heaven and earth!

Borrowing that current of spiritual resonance, the fire spirit fled deep into this world's sea of origin qi, continuously diving downward and seeking the origin power that answered it.

And because it was a natural strange fire, born of heaven and earth, this process proceeded exceptionally smoothly.

Therefore, it stirred great amounts of origin qi of heaven and earth to pour in. Through Luo Chen's fleshly body, that qi continuously entered the Withering-Bloom True Flame inside his Purple Mansion.

And within the Purple Mansion, the Withering-Bloom True Flame also began its final transformation.

Benefiting from the great influx of origin qi, it forcefully suppressed the influence of the Nirvana Sacred Flame and began to grow madly.

If things continued like this, Luo Chen only needed to wait until success was complete.

But what was wondrous was that as he carefully watched the Withering-Bloom transformation, he gradually gained some insights.

All things in the world, from the moment they are born, live toward death.

Yet if life has spirit, no one wishes for death, and so all things flourish, seeking longevity and immortality.

It was like this fire. Beneath its roaring blaze, it could not escape the ending of turning into ash. Yet within the ashes, the power of new life was also contained, and so dead embers could flare again.

It was like that wood. Its emerald shade covered the sky like a canopy, but even as the four seasons changed and sun and moon revolved, it would ultimately become withered wood. Yet its roots buried deep underground would still accumulate strength, awaiting the day when it could return to flourishing.

Such was the cycle of heavenly principle, withering and blooming in return. This was the Dao of Withering-Bloom.

Luo Chen did not know whether his insight was right or wrong. The myriad arts of the world were illusory and unreal, subject to decay and impermanence. Merely through the transformation of his origin true flame, perhaps what he observed was only the surface.

But worldly affairs were always like this: what was lost in the east was gained from the mulberry.

The majestic origin qi of heaven and earth poured through Luo Chen's body into the Withering-Bloom True Flame. Luo Chen also formed a connection with this heaven and earth, and even as the master of the Withering-Bloom True Flame, he faintly sensed the place the fire spirit had gone.

He also wanted to go there!

The instant this thought was born, majestic azure fire enveloped Luo Chen's Sage, and his divine sense entered the azure clouds directly!

Following a boundless, unknowable direction, he stepped into the sea of origin qi.

It was a strange place. With Luo Chen's knowledge, he could not describe it in words. He only felt five colors bewildering and dazzling, and if he was even slightly careless, he would sink into it.

It was also like a mother's womb, like a wandering son returning home. In that closeness, the thought of never leaving was born.

Vast and dazed, he did not know where he had come from, nor did he have anywhere to go.

Just then, a lively voice rang out.

"Master, I am here!"

...

Today, seven years later.

Lin Bufan crouched in a mountain stream, scooped up a handful of clear snowmelt, and looked bewildered.

"Why has the snow melted?"

Lin Jingtang frowned. "Why observe these things? Shi Ju and the others have gone over. We should go see what is happening too!"

Lin Bufan casually shook the water from his hand and followed behind the other man, heading north.

Only, when they passed a small mountain covered in blooming mountain flowers, puzzlement rose in his heart again.

"Why has the Child-Seeking Flower bloomed?"

"The Child-Seeking Flower blooming is one thing, but the Mother-Lodging Flower so close beside it has clearly withered."

No one answered his doubts.

What came instead was an explosive shout from the sky.

"Luo Chen, get out here!"

The one speaking was Shi Ju, but the one who acted was Qi Xiongcheng.

A giant hand that seemed to prop up the sky slowly appeared and slowly descended.

As this palm struck out, the mountains seemed unable to bear its weight. Large swaths of snowy mountains began collapsing, and curtains of light rose and fell in succession, only to shatter inch by inch.

Crack! Crack! Crack!

The many concealment formations and defensive formations Luo Chen had arranged in advance were all destroyed beneath this palm when no one presided over them.

When all the formations vanished, Luo Chen's figure appeared atop a tall mountain.

Below the mountain, avalanches rolled, gathering into a white sea.

Among the mountains, wild winds blew, making Luo Chen's white robes snap loudly and his long hair dance madly behind his head.

Yet he still did not open his eyes. He was unmoved by everything in the outside world and continued sitting there quietly.

Seeing this scene, Shi Ju revealed a pleased smile.

He had found him after all!

But Luo Chen's unmoving appearance also made wariness rise in his heart.

"Go. Kill him!"

At Shi Ju's command, Qi Xiongcheng acted without hesitation. A black longsword appeared in his intact left hand, and he charged toward the snowy mountain.

As for Wang Yifu, who had followed them all the way here, there was even less need to speak. His eyes were full of hatred, and he rushed forward holding his verdant bamboo staff. Halfway there, he struck out staff shadows one after another.

Shi Ju's folding fan was half-open and half-closed in his hand, strands of golden flame spreading from it, ready to add a strike at any moment.

In the high sky, the white eagle cried without end, staring fixedly at Luo Chen.

It still remembered that this person had sucked away a great amount of spiritual resonance, and had even heavily wounded its body with one sword.

Farther back, Gu Yuan's side and Lin Bufan's side quietly watched this scene, as though only observing from the wall.

But judging from the eager movements of their respective Dao protectors, they clearly would not stand aside.

"This time, he is in the open. Without the chance to accumulate that sword in the dark, can he withstand the attacks of Shi Ju and the other two?"

"He is in sudden enlightenment and no longer has the strength to retaliate!"

This exchange came from Gu Yuan and Lin Bufan.

At the mountain summit.

Luo Chen's eyelids moved slightly, as though he were about to awaken, but in the end, he did not wake.

The divided soul left behind gave a faint sigh and had already summoned the Yuantu sword.

"Unfortunately, the Sage is carrying the main soul and cannot move, and the magic power I can mobilize is pitifully little. I cannot buy much time."

"I will do all I can. At worst, I will shatter this Sage-level divided soul!"

Just as Luo Chen's divided soul prepared to stake everything on shattering the soul, a figure appeared at some unknown moment and blocked in front of him.

It was her!

Her appearance was peerlessly beautiful, her figure curving gracefully. On her face, which had grown much thinner, there was a trace of complicated emotion.

"This time, it is my turn to protect you."

Fu Qinglan murmured, then lightly parted her lovely mouth. A tender emerald leaf slowly flew out from her cherry lips.

As soon as the green leaf emerged, great amounts of magic power surged into it.

In an instant, the green leaf expanded wildly, like a heavenly curtain, blocking in front of everyone.

Saber light and staff shadows fell at the same time.

No sound rang out, and the green leaf remained as tough as before.

Fu Qinglan's brows, however, furrowed faintly.

Suppressing the discomfort in her heart, she moved with lotus steps and blocked everyone's line of sight toward Luo Chen.

"Everyone, why not give Qinglan some face and each depart?"

Wang Yifu was the first to refuse!

The bamboo staff in his hand gathered force, about to attack again.

Shi Ju flew in front of him, eyes narrowing as he looked at Fu Qinglan.

She was no longer at the realm of one who had just entered the Sage stage, but was a genuine first-level Sage, and her aura was full, brimming with vitality.

Especially because she had just easily blocked the methods of two great Sage cultivators...

"You succeeded?"

Facing Shi Ju's aggressive gaze, Fu Qinglan turned back and glanced at the white-clothed man seated cross-legged on the mountain summit.

"Yes, I succeeded."

As soon as these words came out, everyone changed color at once.

Lin Bufan wore a bitter smile. He had not expected that among him, Yu Xu, and Fu Qinglan, the first to comprehend the true meaning of the laws would not be himself, whose spiritual perception was most acute, nor Yu Xu, whose family power was deep, but Fu Qinglan, who had forcefully broken through to the Sage stage early in order to participate in the competition, and whose foundation was not especially stable.

Lin Jingtang clenched his fists. The gap between them was absolutely not between Xiaofan and Fu Qinglan, but merely the gap between their Dao protectors.

He had handled matters poorly!

Gu Yuan and Gu Chen exchanged a look, then slowly nodded.

"The Fu family will flourish again because of this woman. If she can achieve the Ascendant realm in the future, perhaps Heavenly Origin will gain another Great House! After returning, we should draw the Fu family in."

Shi Ju, who faced Fu Qinglan directly, was silent at this moment.

But very soon, he laughed.

"So what if you comprehended the true meaning of the laws? That is only the most basic origin power. At most, it strengthens your magic power by a few degrees."

"In the end, you are still only a first-level Sage!"

"You cannot protect him!"

As he spoke, the folding fan in his hand fully opened.

His mouth also rapidly transmitted to Qi and Wang.

"She has been a Sage for too short a time and has not mastered many Sage methods at all. In addition, her magic power is thin. She cannot block you for long. Attack with full force!"

Qi and Wang, from their initial shock, also began to calm down.

For a Sage cultivator to comprehend the true meaning of the laws was formidable indeed.

But when facing enemies in battle, it ultimately depended on each person's methods and corresponding realm.

Fu Qinglan's methods were unrefined, and her realm was not high. Even if wood origin could amplify her magic power, she could not endure the consumption.

Attack with full force!

The next moment, the two acted together.

Black saber light transformed into a hundred zhang, and the verdant bamboo staff filled the sky with bamboo shadows. Together they struck toward Fu Qinglan.

The green leaf appeared again.

On it, the leaf veins were fine, like the blood vessels and meridians of a person. As it unfurled, it drew great amounts of spiritual qi of heaven and earth to gather.

This magic treasure had been refined by the Fu family from a ten-thousand-year Fusang leaf, then when Fu Qinglan broke through to the Sage stage, it drew the power of heavenly lightning to wash over it, thus achieving the rank of true artifact.

She named it the Heaven-Shrouding Leaf, meaning one leaf could shroud the heavens.

It was also the method she was most skilled with after achieving the Sage stage.

Once the Heaven-Shrouding Leaf emerged, just like before, it blocked the two men's full-powered attacks.

But in the next moment, a current of blazing flame swept upward!

Shi Ju broke through the leaf and emerged, his folding fan held horizontally, his face savage.

"I said, you cannot protect him!"

Fu Qinglan bit her silver teeth and recalled the Heaven-Shrouding Leaf.

But unlike before, when it blocked in front, this time her wrist shook, and the leaf covered downward through the air, completely wrapping the mountain summit where Luo Chen sat.

Swish!

A longsword like autumn water fell into her slender jade hand.

"He protected me for fifty years. I naturally should protect him for a time!"

Looking at that sword, Shi Ju raised a brow.

"You want to draw a sword against me?"

"Senior Sister Qinglan, have you thought through the consequences of opposing me?"

"You have merely comprehended the true meaning of the laws. You have not yet achieved primordial spirit, much less become an Ascendant great power. The Fu family is also only a family at the bottom of the Thirty-Six Aristocratic Clans. And my Shi Great House is one of the Twelve Great Houses!"

Listening to these words, Fu Qinglan's face showed conflict.

The corner of Shi Ju's mouth lifted slightly. "Since you have already comprehended the true meaning of the laws, this competition's first place should belong to you. Continuing to remain inside the grotto-heaven has no meaning. Why not go out first? Then the remaining matters will have nothing to do with you."

Fu Qinglan's eyes trembled continuously, and even her body subconsciously took a step backward.

It was precisely this step that let the corner of her eye see two figures approaching within ten li of the mountain peak where Luo Chen sat.

Without thinking, she drew her sword and cut out two streaks of sword light.

"Bitch, refusing a toast only to drink a forfeit!"

Shi Ju cursed under his breath, and great amounts of golden flame appeared from within his body.

Whoosh!

His body began to disperse inch by inch inside the flames, specks of golden radiance spreading.

When the firelight brightened again.

The next moment, he had already appeared within ten zhang of Fu Qinglan.

With a wave of his folding fan, flame currents swept out.

Fu Qinglan poured sword light from her longsword, showing no weakness as she blocked the other party's attacks.

She said nothing more and gave her answer through action.

It had to be said, though she looked delicate and frail, the sword light within each move and stance was incomparably dazzling. Her magic power, which should have lacked endurance, for some unknown reason flowed endlessly, without any sign of decline.

In the distance, Gu Yuan saw this scene and nodded slightly.

"It seems she has comprehended some methods of using origin power."

According to the clan's records on origin power, although this true meaning of the laws was the most basic, it was also wondrous and extraordinary.

Not only could it increase the might of magic power, but during battle, it could rapidly transform the spiritual qi of heaven and earth into one's own magic power, making magic power flow without end.

If one was in a place rich with spiritual qi of the corresponding attribute, then the transformation speed would be even faster, almost inexhaustible!

If one reached the Ascendant realm, then even without favorable terrain, one could rely on origin power to forcibly transform the natural environment and gain the advantage of a home ground.

Fu Qinglan was not stupid.

She merely lacked experience in life-and-death battles with others.

At this moment, under full-power battle, her combat ability was growing madly!

Fu Qinglan herself also clearly discovered these signs. From her originally conservative defensive posture, she gradually became proactive. She even fought Shi Ju while urging her magic power to maintain the Heaven-Shrouding Leaf's state and resist the other two men's attacks against Luo Chen.

Abruptly!

"Deal with this bitch first!"

Seeing that he could not take down Fu Qinglan even after so long, while Qi and Wang were also helpless against the Heaven-Shrouding Leaf, Shi Ju grew anxious.

At his call, those two immediately flew over and joined hands to attack Fu Qinglan.

One against one, Fu Qinglan still had room to spare.

But one against three, especially when Qi Xiongcheng and Wang Yifu both had rich battle experience, she clearly began showing signs of being unable to hold on.

The magic power converted from thin air also became intermittent.

After only a few dozen exchanges, injuries appeared on her body.

In the distance.

Lin Bufan watched this scene. He was naturally glad that Fu Qinglan's strength had greatly advanced and that she could fight one against three. Yet because her time since attaining the Dao was too short, and her methods unrefined, she retreated step by step under the encirclement of three people, and worry appeared on his face.

Lin Jingtang seemed to know what he was thinking and said in a low voice, "You said that illusion core was the final gift for her. From the moment Wilderness Wanderer seized love with a horizontal blade, Fu Qinglan was no longer your friend."

Lin Bufan opened his mouth, then ultimately sighed in dejection.

Seeing him like this, Lin Jingtang licked his lips, stretched out his hand, and with a shake, the Fright Bell appeared in his hand.

He did not hesitate and flew straight toward the battlefield.

"Jingtang, don't touch her!"

Lin Jingtang looked back at Lin Bufan. Their eyes met, and the meaning between them went without saying.

He nodded, raised his escape light, and flew toward the mountain peak where Luo Chen sat, supporting the great bell in his hand.

At this moment, Fu Qinglan had no attention to spare.

Holding the great bell, Lin Jingtang lowered his head and looked toward that emerald mulberry leaf.

"I remember that palm strike perfectly clearly!"

He slapped out with one hand, and the great bell crashed down!

The Heaven-Shrouding Leaf erupted with green light, firmly blocking it outside.

Lin Jingtang gave a low shout and struck the great bell three more times in succession.

Clang! Clang! Clang!

"No!"

In the distance, Fu Qinglan flew backward, spitting blood, her face full of despair.

On the mountain, the green leaf hurriedly flashed several times, then suddenly shrank, turning into a mulberry leaf that flew toward Fu Qinglan.

And Luo Chen once again appeared in the world.

Lin Jingtang was delighted and urged the great bell to descend.

At this moment, however, a smear of bloodlight was reflected in his pupils.

Hiss!

The sword light, condensed to the extreme, gradually scattered, turning into a blood sword that circled above Luo Chen's head, whining.

Lin Jingtang lowered his head and looked at his own chest. There was a huge hole there.

The high-grade defensive robe, beneath that incomparably sudden sword, had not produced any defensive effect at all.

Where the blood sword passed, the Purple Mansion was broken.

His Sage true body appeared with a great hole in it, and large amounts of pure magic power poured uncontrollably toward the outside world.

"How could this be?"

He opened his mouth.

By his ear, apart from Lin Bufan's anxious voice urging him to activate the illusion chess piece, there was also Luo Chen's cold laugh.

"A defeated foe seeking his own death!"

*(End of Chapter)*
