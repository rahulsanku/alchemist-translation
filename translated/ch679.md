# Chapter 679: Canglan Water Monster, Fallen Abyss Dragon Sect

Tai Sui was in a poor mood.

He had originally thought that the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion would force the hidden person beside Luo Chen to reveal himself.

That hidden person had become a thorn in his heart.

He did not know who the person was, where the person hid, or what relationship they had with Luo Chen.

But the old things in Hundred Crow Ghost Forest had sensed three helpers.

Tai Sui believed in their perception more than he believed in his own eyes.

After all, true artifacts were not easy to suppress.

Even if a Golden Core cultivator possessed one, releasing it and storing it were two entirely different matters. Without a Sage's domain to act as a fulcrum, most Golden Core cultivators could only watch helplessly as such a treasure resisted them.

Yet Luo Chen had once again exceeded his expectations.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron was not a true artifact.

But it was too broad.

Five elements, thunder, ice, and wind.

It was the kind of treasure that did not appear outstanding in a single attribute, but once faced with a complicated situation, its breadth became unexpectedly troublesome.

If the Octagonal Pavilion had possessed only the five elements, Tai Sui would not have been surprised.

But even its ice, wind, and thunder qi had been matched.

Together with the All-Encompassing Fire Prison isolating heaven and earth qi, it had actually suppressed the Octagonal Pavilion by force.

Tai Sui looked at Luo Chen's back and felt increasingly that this person could not be judged by normal standards.

Luo Chen himself was also pondering the matter.

After the Octagonal Pavilion was sealed, he finally gained a clearer understanding of true artifacts.

To a Sage, a true artifact was like a lever.

The Sage's domain was the fulcrum.

The true artifact was the long arm.

With both together, one could move heaven and earth qi and display power far beyond ordinary magic treasures.

But if the fulcrum was missing, the true artifact was awkward.

It still had immense material and spiritual rank, but many of its wonders could not be fully drawn out.

That was why Penglai had loosened many of the Octagonal Pavilion's restrictions so that Golden Core disciples could use it to move spirit gardens.

Those loosened restrictions gave Luo Chen a chance.

If the pavilion had been controlled by a true Sage, the Primordial Unity Cauldron would not have been able to suppress it so smoothly.

Even now, the suppression was not true refinement.

Luo Chen could feel the pavilion constantly resisting inside the layered restrictions.

Sometimes a wisp of wood qi would try to seep out.

Sometimes the thunder eave would tremble, attempting to break one of the cauldron's chains.

He had to check it several times a day.

Only after confirming that the All-Encompassing Fire Prison could completely cut off its connection to the outside world did he dare begin shrinking it.

Inside Luo Chen's sea of consciousness, Han Zhan asked, "You have decided to leave Myriad Immortals Assembly?"

"Yes."

Luo Chen answered without hesitation.

Han Zhan sighed softly. "Because of Moon Wanderer?"

"That is only one reason. I will not hand the Octagonal Pavilion to her. Since I will not hand it over, I have already offended her."

"And the deeper reason?"

Luo Chen was silent for a while.

When he left Peng Lake this time, he had taken almost everything that truly belonged to him.

That had not been an impulsive decision.

The cave residence had been emptied.

Important pills, spirit stones, source blood, magic treasures, and notes had all been carried away.

Even some things that seemed temporarily useless had been stored in the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

At the time, Luo Chen had not said it aloud, but he had already been preparing to sever a path of retreat.

The thought of leaving Myriad Immortals Assembly had first appeared when he was at Three Pillar Gorge.

At that time, he had personally seen Liujun flee from Arctic Yemo Heaven.

If one Sage demon could escape, then others could as well.

The Fallen Sea defense line guarded by Myriad Immortals Assembly would sooner or later become the first place to bear the pressure.

And if an ancient demon Ascendant truly emerged one day, then the so-called defense line might become a grave for countless cultivators.

Luo Chen did not want to place his fate entirely on that line.

He said slowly, "I do not know where I will go yet. But remaining there is no longer suitable."

Han Zhan did not persuade him.

Half a month passed.

During that time, Luo Chen focused entirely on sealing the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion.

Layer after layer of restrictions were laid over it.

The All-Encompassing Fire Prison formed the outer shell, the Primordial Unity Cauldron provided suppression, and Luo Chen's spiritual sense carefully marked every loosened gap in the pavilion's original restrictions.

He did not understand Penglai's inheritance restrictions.

But he understood spirit-planting formations, and he understood how magic treasures drew spiritual qi.

The Octagonal Pavilion's greatest feature was that each eave corresponded to an attribute.

If he blocked those eight eaves one by one, the pavilion would lose the channels it used to breathe.

That was a rough method.

It was also the only method available to him for now.

He could not truly refine it yet.

He could not inspect everything inside either.

But at least he could shrink it enough to carry and prevent it from drawing outside qi.

While Luo Chen worked, Tai Sui and Moya Cave Master did not remain idle.

Wood Heaven Plain was full of spirit herbs.

Even if the strongest gardens were not touched, the outer regions still contained many good things.

When the group regrouped, Moya Cave Master's face was full of joy.

He had obtained two Sage-formation spirit herbs.

For a late Golden Core cultivator, that alone was already a massive harvest.

Even so, his eyes still drifted toward the sealed Octagonal Pavilion.

"Fellow Daoist Luo, that Divine Iris..."

Luo Chen glanced at him. "The pavilion is only sealed, not refined. I cannot see clearly what is inside, let alone take anything out."

Moya Cave Master laughed dryly. "I only asked casually."

He did not dare press further.

In truth, he understood very well that even if Luo Chen could take it out, there was no reason to give it to him now.

The Divine Iris was useful for forming the Sage.

He needed it.

Luo Chen might not need it immediately, but who could say he would not need it in the future?

Among cultivators, a treasure that entered another person's storage was no longer something one could claim by looking at it with eager eyes.

Tai Sui had also gained much.

After entering Wood Heaven Plain, his state had clearly improved. His aura was more stable, and even the injuries he had carried before seemed to have healed by more than half. He had also helped Moya Cave Master remove some old wounds along the way, which made the latter's attitude toward him far warmer.

Once everything was ready, the three finally left Wood Heaven Plain.

Behind them, the vast spirit herb garden gradually disappeared beneath layers of green mist.

Moya Cave Master looked back several times, clearly reluctant.

Tai Sui, on the other hand, did not look back.

The closer he came to Falling Demon Abyss, the quieter he became.

Their next destination was Yue Canglan.

According to Tai Sui, Yue Canglan was a vast water region inside Five Elements Heaven. It was not a true sea, but its waters were deep and wide, with countless currents and hidden monsters.

However, the moment they approached it, all three stopped.

A terrifying demonic pressure rolled over from the water region ahead.

Tai Sui's face changed. "Yue Canglan was not like this before."

Luo Chen inhaled lightly.

His expression became grave. "Demonic qi."

It was not the thin, scattered demonic qi left behind by ordinary demon beasts.

It was thick and heavy, mixed into the water vapor and mud.

Every gust of damp wind carried a faint bestial pressure.

Five Elements Heaven should not have demon beasts.

This place had been arranged by Demon Lord Heaven-Refining. Its native beings were mostly spirits, strange plants, and elemental creatures. Even if water monsters existed in Yue Canglan, they were not true demon beasts.

Tai Sui said uncertainly, "There should have been Canglan Water Monsters [TERM?] here. The strongest one was fifth-tier, but they were water spirits, not demons."

Those water monsters had been troublesome, but they were born from the water qi of this heaven.

They had no reason to produce such a dense demon aura.

Moya Cave Master swallowed. "Then do we still cross?"

"We cross quietly," Luo Chen said.

With a thought, he released Black King.

The black dog landed on the damp ground, sniffed, and immediately looked toward the waterlogged mud ahead.

Luo Chen followed its gaze.

The next moment, a wave washed across the mud, stripping away the surface layer.

Beneath it, dense worm-like creatures were revealed.

They were half a zhang long, thick as a person's arm, and covered in slick mucus. Their bodies wriggled silently through the mud, leaving behind sticky channels.

Moya Cave Master's scalp tingled. "Bend eels?"

"dragon earthworms," Luo Chen said.

He recognized them.

They were not high-tier demon beasts. Most were only first-tier. But the individuals here were much larger than ordinary records described.

Dragon Earthworms could secrete mucus and spit mud, gradually altering soil and water environments. A large group of them could turn firm earth into marsh.

Looking out across Yue Canglan, Luo Chen saw that many of the former waterways had become muddy swamps.

Reeds that should have grown in clear water were half-submerged in silt.

Some river channels had been blocked entirely.

The surface seemed calm, but beneath it, countless bodies were moving, grinding, swallowing, and spitting mud day after day.

Han Zhan's voice sounded in his mind. "Someone is doing what you did in Blazing Refinement Prison."

Luo Chen's heart moved.

In Blazing Refinement Prison, he had used the Five Elements Lotus Platform and the fire qi environment to sense the fire law.

Here, someone was transforming Yue Canglan into an environment that suited himself, then using that environment to sense water law.

Only a Demon Emperor would have the ability and patience to do this on such a scale.

The comparison made Luo Chen's expression even more solemn.

He had relied on the Five Elements Lotus Platform, the Blazing Refinement Prison environment, and his own fire path accumulation.

Fallen Abyss Dragon Sect had brought an entire race with him and was remodeling the land itself.

Different methods, same purpose.

Both were attempts to borrow a special environment to touch law.

On the seventh day after entering Yue Canglan, their path was blocked.

A third-tier Dragon Earthworm rose from the swamp.

Its body was several zhang long, and two short horn-like protrusions grew from its head. Its voice was low and muffled.

"Lord Dragon Sect is sensing heaven and earth. Outsiders must not disturb him."

As it spoke, countless auras stirred beneath the swamp.

Dozens of third-tier Dragon Earthworms.

Thousands of lower-tier ones.

The swamp surface bulged everywhere.

Some Dragon Earthworms did not show their full bodies, only revealing horned heads or slick backs beneath the mud.

The air immediately became stifling.

If a fight broke out here, they would not be facing one third-tier demon beast.

They would be facing a whole nest, and behind that nest stood a Demon Emperor who might open his eyes at any moment.

Moya Cave Master immediately cupped his hands. "We did not know this place was occupied by Senior Dragon Sect. We will take another route."

Luo Chen did not object.

Tai Sui's eyes flickered, but he also remained silent.

After retreating, Luo Chen said, "Fallen Abyss Dragon Sect [TERM?]."

Moya Cave Master was startled. "You know him?"

"I have heard of him. Lord of the Dragon Earthworms. A Demon Emperor who slaughtered many human cultivators in the demon sea."

The name Fallen Abyss Dragon Sect had circulated among demon hunters for many years.

He was not famous for lone combat like some Demon Emperors.

He was famous because wherever he appeared, the terrain changed, routes collapsed, and cultivator strongholds were swallowed by mud and flood.

In a defensive war, such a demon was even more troublesome than a beast that only knew how to charge.

Tai Sui glanced back toward the swamp.

Luo Chen said coldly, "Do not cause complications."

Tai Sui gave a dry laugh. "I had no such intention."

Whether or not that was true, he behaved afterward.

The group changed direction and moved quickly through the edge of Yue Canglan, avoiding the center of the transformed swamp as much as possible.

The demonic qi there was too heavy.

Even with Luo Chen's current strength, he did not want to collide with a Demon Emperor who was in the middle of sensing heaven and earth.

For the next several days, they traveled almost without stopping.

Whenever Black King sensed large movements beneath the mud, Luo Chen would change direction in advance.

Moya Cave Master suppressed even his usual urge to collect materials.

Tai Sui seemed to want to say something several times, but every time Luo Chen glanced at him, he swallowed the words back down.

Behind them, the mud slowly closed again.

Countless Dragon Earthworms returned beneath the surface, continuing their silent work.

Yue Canglan, once a water region of Five Elements Heaven, was gradually becoming something else.
