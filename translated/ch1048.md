# Chapter 1048: Black King Breaks the Mountain, Creation Divine Palm

In the azure void.

A black-robed Daoist sat cross-legged. Before him stood a silent black earthen jar, quietly upright.

Man and jar remained still for a long time, as if waiting for something.

Suddenly!

"Here!"

Luo Chen's eyes blazed with sharp light as he looked into the distance.

A swarm of multicolored flying insects, wings beating, came screeching from the east in this direction.

These were *xiao* insects, also known in the cultivation world as Rosy Cloud Beasts.

But Luo Chen's target at this moment was not them—it was their food!

Ahead of the flying insects, a wisp of purple qi shuttled through space, roaming freely within the azure void.

Just as it entered within ten *li* of him, he pressed one hand onto the black earthen jar.

"Come in!"

The jar hummed and trembled. Instantly, a powerful suction force generated, drawing that wisp of purple cloud qi inside in the blink of an eye.

With their target gone, the swarm of insects flew into a rage, fixing their attention directly on Luo Chen.

These things were not to be trifled with.

They were the true overlords of the azure void. Though individually not particularly formidable and lacking intelligence, when gathered together, even a Sage would not dare to lightly challenge their edge.

Once surrounded on all sides, no matter what defensive measures one employed, everything would be gnawed to nothing.

Watching as he was about to be encircled, Luo Chen showed no panic on his face. He merely smiled faintly.

The next moment!

*Boom!*

The void exploded with a sharp sound, and dazzling flames flickered.

By the time the sparks dissipated, Luo Chen's figure had vanished from the spot.

The swarm of insects hovered blankly in place. Their food was gone, and their enemy had disappeared as well.

Several dozen *li* away, flames bloomed, sparks flickered, and gradually revealed a tall, straight figure—like a sharp sword that could pierce the heavens.

It was Luo Chen.

"Combining Voidburst Step with the Fire Concealment Art, the instantaneous burst distance can be this far! I wonder how my Voidburst Fire Escape compares to Azure Frost's Void Escape Divine Art?"

Luo Chen murmured to himself, lightly laughing as he halted.

Ever since forming the life-and-death gu with Azure Frost, although he still couldn't rely on the fifth-order gu to siphon her cultivation, Luo Chen had indeed developed a post-natal innate affinity for space.

Thus, during the twenty years of pursuing purple cloud qi, he had tried fusing his self-created Voidburst Step with the Fire Concealment Art.

One was an extreme burst of physical power, the other a skilled application of fire origin. Combined with his sensitivity to space, Luo Chen could now instantly teleport dozens of *li*!

This had also become his perfect means of escaping encirclement by the swarm of insects.

After a moment of reflection, Luo Chen looked at the jar in his hand.

He had captured another wisp of Purple Cloud Qi!

He was one step closer to advancing the *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon* from the master level to great perfection.

"But there's still a long way to go!"

Don't be fooled by it being just the final level—it required the sum total of all previous proficiency.

What limited Luo Chen's refinement of the Crimson Cloud Sword Qi now was no longer time, but the quantity of cloud qi.

The last five hundred wisps!

Relying on aimless searching through the azure void on his own, who knew how much time it would take to gather so much cloud qi.

If the opportunity to reclaim his fate soul arrived before the *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon* could reach great perfection, then Luo Chen would have no choice but to give up.

After all, at the master level, the Crimson Cloud Sword Qi was already quite sufficient.

At least from Luo Chen's experiments, the killing power of this technique now far surpassed the great-perfection fourth-order Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain, and was even not inferior to the fully deployed Great Five Elements Sword Formation.

And compared to the cumbersome setup of the Great Five Elements Sword Formation, the Crimson Cloud Sword Qi could be unleashed at will—far, far faster.

With this against an Ascendant great power, it should be usable as a conventional combat method... right?

Luo Chen wasn't certain, because he had never truly fought an Ascendant great power.

Which methods could contend with an Ascendant, which could threaten their life—all of it was still just Luo Chen's estimation.

He didn't yet know exactly what level his strength had reached.

Ordinary Sages were no match for him in a single exchange. Even long-famous late-stage Great Cultivators, he could destroy with a flip of his hand.

But Ascendant great powers—those were beings beyond reckoning.

All he could do now was improve himself by any means necessary!

"Let's find another place to continue capturing cloud qi!"

Just as Luo Chen was about to move, his heart stirred.

A jade token appeared in his hand.

Luo Chen frowned. Why was Little Bai transmitting a message at this time?

He unfolded the jade token, and the first message he received was: "Master, Black King has appeared!"

Following that, to avoid wasting a second transmission opportunity, Little Bai had deliberately added much information about his clone, Zhou Fuyou.

Things like joining the Tiannan Dao Palace, becoming a disciple of Fu Qinglan of the Houtu Hall, and advancing to the Adept stage at forty years old with peerless talent.

But Luo Chen paid little attention to any of that.

His focus remained on the first message.

"That fool is still alive after all!"

Luo Chen chuckled lightly, paused his cloud-qi capturing efforts, shifted direction, and turned into a streak of light flying toward the Tiannan Continent.

...

Liubo Mountain.

Three days had passed since Zhou Fuyou arrived.

Unlike the occasional battles he had imagined, from the very second day, he was busy with his feet barely touching the ground.

Starting on the second day, a considerable number of sea demons had landed from the sea, attacking Liubo Mountain.

The sheer quantity had dealt a massive blow to Liubo Mountain from the very first moment.

As a mobile unit, Zhou Fuyou led twenty Initiate true cultivators, divided into five squads, frequently appearing on the battlefield to help the stationed cultivators reduce the pressure from the beast tide.

As for himself, relying on his abundant mana, he roamed freely across the eight-hundred-*li* Liubo Mountain.

Wherever he passed, low-level demon beasts died on contact.

Even third-order demon kings!

On the main battlefield, the mountain-protecting grand formation began to break. The support squad led by Zhou Yuwei was also delayed.

Then, everyone watched as demonic waves churned in the sea, and three monsters, each forty to fifty *zhang* in size, charged violently ashore on seven or eight legs.

Such size, such demonic aura—it shocked everyone.

"Third-order demon kings, three of them!"

"They're Overlord Crabs!"

"We can't hold! Run!"

Just as panic spread, the sky suddenly darkened.

Streams of black qi surged forth, covering the front battlefield. All the demon beasts charging forward, including the three Overlord Crabs, were enveloped.

This scene stunned everyone.

They couldn't see what was happening inside the battlefield, only hearing one agonized scream after another.

Time passed slowly.

When the screams gradually faded, a figure walked out from within. Behind him lay a swamp of blood and a forest of white bones.

Sharp-eyed Lin Qingtong recognized the newcomer at first glance.

"Cousin!"

The others also cheered.

"Senior Zhou is here! We're saved!"

"All the demon beasts are dead."

"The main front is held!"

Zhou Fuyou, his white robes spotless, not a trace of blood on him, drifted down before everyone.

He said solemnly, "Replenish the formation, reorganize the defensive line. Don't let the demon beasts charge up. If you encounter a large beast tide, send a signal for reinforcements!"

Everyone acknowledged in unison.

Zhou Fuyou walked over to the four-person squad, looked at the panting Zhou Yuwei and the others, and calmly asked, "Everyone alright?"

Zhou Yuwei shook her head. She had merely overexerted her spiritual power output, and her meridians couldn't bear it for a moment. She just needed to rest.

The only injured member of the four-person squad was Zhou Yuqi.

With the poorest aptitude and the shortest time since foundation establishment, even his superior-grade magic tool had been destroyed in the battle. The backlash from the spiritual connection had severely injured him.

Looking at Zhou Fuyou, who had suppressed the demon tide with one hand and destroyed three Overlord Crabs with the other, his expression was complex.

The childhood friend of yesteryear had already left him far behind. The gap was like heaven and earth.

Lin Qingtong, on the other hand, looked at Zhou Fuyou with stars in her eyes, full of admiration.

"Cousin, you're amazing!"

"Amazing?" Zhou Fuyou shook his head. "They're just a group of low-level sea demons, still following instinct. Once they land, their strength is greatly reduced. Don't fight them head-on. Use guerrilla tactics, and you can easily win."

Is that so?

Lin Qingtong and the other three were lost in thought.

Zhou Fuyou's words hadn't been soundproofed, and nearby cultivators could also hear them.

Those who had been stationed here long-term naturally knew these lessons, but putting them into actual practice on a chaotic battlefield was not so simple.

And you, Zhou Fuyou, hadn't used much guerrilla tactics just now—you simply crushed them head-on.

Laughter rang out from afar, and a figure flew over.

"Daoist Zhou, you're too modest. It's one thing for low-level demon beasts to follow instinct, but those three Overlord Crabs were mid-third-order demon kings, and they couldn't withstand a single blow from you. Tsk tsk, with such ability, even among the Dao Palace's peers, you could be called outstanding!"

Zhou Fuyou looked at the newcomer. "So it's Daoist Ji."

Ji Du Shangren swept his gaze across the battlefield and unconsciously breathed a sigh of relief.

"Fortunately, you arrived first and stabilized the line, preventing this batch of demon beasts from charging up Liubo Mountain. Leave the rest to me, Daoist Zhou. Continue supporting other places in need."

Zhou Fuyou nodded, turned, and led the four-person squad away.

Today's beast tide was too fierce, exceeding all estimates.

And this was Liubo Mountain, a relatively remote and safer location. If it were the places where his second senior brother and the others were stationed, how dangerous would it be?

His master had probably instructed his second senior brother to look after him, which was why he had been assigned the Liubo Mountain mission.

But if the demon beasts attacking the defensive line were only at this level...

Zhou Fuyou twitched the corner of his mouth, showing no fear on his face.

With his abundant mana and relying on the spell *Qi Sea Floating Heaven*, he was a great killer on this kind of battlefield. There was no need to worry about his life.

Just as Zhou Fuyou was calculating inwardly, his returning steps suddenly froze.

"Cousin, what's wrong?"

Lin Qingtong greatly admired and worshipped her cousin, so she had been watching him closely. Seeing him suddenly stop, she didn't understand what was happening.

Zhou Fuyou stiffly turned around, gazing into the distance from the foot of the mountain.

He couldn't see clearly, only vaguely making out a wall-like wave pushing forward from a hundred *li* away.

He swallowed his saliva and shrieked, "Run!"

Saying this, ignoring everyone else, he shot up into the sky, reaching the summit of Liubo Mountain.

Looking out, the wave-formed wall was a thousand *zhang* high, rolling forward, destroying everything in its path.

Small islands several *li* in radius sank and vanished. Millennia-old reefs crumbled to dust. Even the low-level sea demons blocking its path were swept in, turning into bloody waves.

Too fast! A hundred *li* distance, almost there in an instant.

Too late to escape!

Zhou Fuyou watched helplessly as the main defensive line, just reorganized, collapsed in an instant.

Ji Du Shangren turned into a streak of light, shooting into the sky.

But his divine sense was not as sharp as Zhou Fuyou's. By the time he detected it, it was too slow. Even his escape was a beat too late.

That streak of light was helplessly swept into the thousand-*zhang* giant wave wall.

Zhou Fuyou's eyes nearly split.

"I can't die!"

*Boom!*

Qi Sea Floating Heaven appeared again. Black qi currents covered the summit of Liubo Mountain, and gurgling dark water flowed out, wrapping Zhou Fuyou tightly.

Instead of retreating, he charged forward, rushing toward the giant wave wall.

*Boom!*

But with just one impact, Zhou Fuyou was sent flying back, crashing onto the summit of Liubo Mountain.

Qi Sea Floating Heaven dissipated instantly.

And the giant wave wall still surged forward.

Just then...

*Hum...*

A green light emerged from Zhou Fuyou's body, forming a palm.

The palm was enormous, but its five fingers were slender, giving a graceful impression.

This palm slowly pressed forward, imprinting itself on the giant wave wall.

The thousand-*zhang* wave wall eerily halted.

Then, under everyone's despairing gaze... *Crash!*

The giant wave wall collapsed and shattered!

The sky rained a torrential downpour. The salty seawater receded back into the vast ocean.

Zhou Fuyou stared blankly at this scene, murmuring, "Master."

Clearly, that green-light palm was a technique of his master, Fairy Qinglan.

When had she left it on him?

At the time of his core formation?

Just as Zhou Fuyou whispered, as everyone rejoiced in their survival—

A terrifying black shadow, bearing a huge palm print on its body, roared up from the ocean, its tail lashing.

*Boom!*

Eight hundred *li* of Liubo Mountain collapsed more than half in an instant.

Countless shattered stones shot through the air, piercing through cultivator after cultivator, splattering blossoms of blood.

The terrifying black shadow seemed furious—furious that someone had blocked its advance, furious that someone had used divine powers to wound it.

Snorting hot air, it approached Zhou Fuyou, who had just released that green-light palm. Was this ant-like creature the one?

Deer antlers, camel head, rabbit eyes, snake neck.

Prominent horns, beard and mane like smoke.

This was a dragon!

A true dragon!

Facing the cruel gaze of the black dragon, Zhou Fuyou's mind trembled violently, almost losing control.

The so-called Dao Palace true disciple, the once-in-a-millennium genius—all of it shattered in this moment.

A genius that hasn't grown up is forever just a genius, not a strong one!

This dragon could destroy everything!

Suddenly, a figure emerged in his fear.

"I can't die!"

"I can't die here!"

"Little sister is still waiting for me to return!"

In a silent roar, Zhou Fuyou's eyes bulged wide, unyieldingly meeting the black dragon's cruel gaze.

Illusion Art: Mirror Flower Water Moon!

This was Zhou Fuyou's final struggle. He didn't seek to kill the opponent, only to buy a chance to escape.

Whether it would succeed depended on the strength of the opponent's soul foundation.

Exerting all his strength to cast this art, Zhou Fuyou's face instantly turned pale, blood flowing from his seven orifices.

After casting it in an instant, without a moment's hesitation, he plummeted from the high altitude, plunging into the sea.

The Water Concealment Art unfolded, and he vanished into the vast ocean.

On the summit of Liubo Mountain, the dragon's head lowered, a hint of daze in its eyes.

For an illusion art of this level, to it—which had absorbed the remnant soul of the Stone Dragon—it was utterly useless.

But for some reason, at that moment, it truly was dazed.

From its buried memories, the figure of a Daoist surfaced, one who had performed this illusion art countless times by its side.

"Master..."

The black dragon murmured, its gaze following the direction of the sea.

If it wished, it—also proficient in water concealment—could definitely catch that little fellow.

But the black dragon stopped.

*Pfft! Pfft!*

Two streams of air shot from its nostrils. The black dragon slowly retreated from Liubo Mountain, swimming along the nearby sea area toward Zhenhai City.

Behind it were millions of low-level demon beasts.

As long as it broke through the Ninefold Zhenhai Gates, these demon beasts could charge into Zhenhai City and slaughter their way onto the Tiannan Continent!

On Liubo Mountain.

The surviving thousand cultivators stared blankly at this scene, not understanding what had happened.

The only thing they knew was that they had kept their lives.

Suddenly, a piercing scream rang out.

"Cousin!!!"

...

Tiannan Dao Palace, Houtu Hall.

Within closed-door cultivation, Fairy Qinglan's brow flashed with green light. She abruptly opened her eyes.

"So quickly, my Great Heaven Creation Palm was passively triggered?"

"Fuyou and the others encountered an irresistible danger!"

Exhaling a mouthful of turbid qi, Fairy Qinglan rose to her full height, transforming into a green light that flew into the Heavenly Origin Observatory.

"Senior Brother Zhan, I request permission to use the teleportation formation!"

(End of chapter)
