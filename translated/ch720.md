# Chapter 720: Langya's Mei Yiqi, Wilderness Wanderer Luo Chen

On the Sixfold Imperial Flying Cloud Carriage.

Beneath the jade-green curtain, Luo Chen held a jade slip in his hand and studied it carefully.

At times he pondered, at times he frowned, and surprise kept surfacing on his face.

Only after a long while did he put down the jade slip, clicking his tongue in wonder.

"To think such a formation exists in this world. On the surface, it seems no more than linking terrain, but in truth it relies on the principle of earth qi communicating between veins, taking one island as the center and continuously covering and connecting the surrounding spirit veins. And in this process, every island drawn into the formation can be treated as a center point and spread outward... In this way, with accumulation over long years, even the smallest, most scattered spirit-vein islands can lean on one another and strengthen one another."

"In its concept, defense is secondary. What truly astonishes me is its ability to link earth veins and nourish spiritual qi."

"As expected of a formation personally passed down by an Ascendant great power of the formation dao!"

Indeed, the jade slip in Luo Chen's hand was none other than the most famous formation of the Northern Sea cultivation world over the past two hundred years, the island-linking formation!

After the Xu clan's third elder joined the Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance, an expert of the Cang Hai Alliance bestowed this formation upon him.

The corresponding formation disks, formation flags, and all the implements required to lay the array had naturally been taken away by the Xu clan's third elder.

But mindful of the friendship between the Xu and Cheng clans, that third elder had still left the formation diagram to Cheng Haixin.

The Swallow Islands did not lack spirit veins. Of their thirty-some islands, ninety percent could support cultivation.

Yet because the spirit veins were scattered, and the islands were split apart like stars across a chessboard, the whole region had never amounted to much.

If this formation could be used to link Swallow Islands, then in less than a hundred years, the islands might be fully connected, thereby gaining deeper earth veins and water veins, becoming a true cultivation land suitable for Adept cultivators.

With such foundation, the defensive formations built upon it would naturally become even more powerful.

So long as they did not encounter enemies at the level of a Demon Emperor or a Great Demon King, they would be more than able to protect themselves.

Now that Luo Chen held the honored status of a Sage, Cheng Haixin had nothing weighty enough to give him when he left this time, so she copied the method of the island-linking formation and gave him a duplicate.

From Luo Chen's perspective and experience, so long as this formation was used properly, small islands could be strengthened, scattered lands could ally and link together, and if it were used on a third-tier spirit vein with decent foundation to begin with, there was even a chance that, after long-term nurturing, it could advance into a fourth-tier spirit vein!

That great power Li Canghai truly had magnificent breadth of mind, to be willing to grant the Northern Sea cultivation world, without stint, a formation that could serve as the core inheritance of an entire sect!

Yes. In Luo Chen's view, Li Canghai had not merely bestowed this upon the Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance under his command.

So long as powerful beings had the intention, they could surely obtain this formation from the disunited Cang Hai Alliance one way or another, then pass it down to the forces under them.

At the very least, Moluo Stream had surely entertained the thought. Perhaps they had done so long ago.

"For me, this is a good thing!"

"In my early years, I already obtained the mountain-moving method of the Five Elements Divine Sect, and Black King possesses the talent to automatically draw in wandering spiritual qi from heaven and earth. If this formation is added as well, then cultivating a fifth-tier spirit vein in the future will become much easier."

"Mm. If I can also obtain Green Pill Valley's set of Five Peaks Valley-Canopy Art, that would make matters even more foolproof!"

While Luo Chen was muttering inwardly, his brow suddenly lifted.

"Heavenjade, slow down."

"Yes, Master!"

Although Heavenjade did not understand Luo Chen's intention, she still controlled the Sixfold Imperial Flying Cloud Carriage and slowed its speed.

Inwardly, however, she was still wondering. There should still be several days before they reached Coldlight Island, so why had he stopped now?

At precisely this moment, an enormous flying ship came rumbling from north to south out of the clouds.

Upon it were pavilions and towers, artificial hills and flowing water, exquisite beyond compare.

Banners snapped in the wind, figures moved everywhere, and an aura of killing and conquest spread across the sky and fields.

By comparison, though the Sixfold Imperial Flying Cloud Carriage was the mount of Hundred Crafts Mountain's sect master, splendid and extraordinary, it was still slightly inferior in scale.

With a single glance, Heavenjade realized what sort of existence deserved to possess such a traveling conveyance.

A Sage!

At that moment, a mellow male voice came from the enormous flying ship.

"This poor Daoist is Mei Yiqi of Langya Mountain. May I ask which exalted immortal sect expert is ahead?"

By stating his origin, his meaning was self-evident.

Luo Chen smiled faintly. After a brief thought, he said in a clear voice, "I am by nature an idle wanderer, so what immortal sect cultivator could I be? I have long heard Sage Langya's great name. I am Wilderness Wanderer. Greetings from here."

On the Langxuan Cloud Ferry, Sage Langya, dressed as a Daoist, subconsciously furrowed his brows.

A rogue cultivator?

And at the Sage realm?

Such existences were few and far between in this world.

He had heard of Mad Wanderer, Blood Wanderer, and Moon Wanderer, but he had never heard of a Wilderness Wanderer!

The disciple attending beside him said cautiously, "Master, be careful. This may be an ambush set by Moluo Stream!"

Sage Langya shook his head. "This journey is undertaken under the principle of ceasing war. How could there be talk of an ambush? Moreover, this person's divine sense is rather strong. He slowed before us and waited politely. Among Moluo Stream, as far as I know, only a few possess such bearing, and none match him."

The disciple understood, then hesitated. "Then we..."

"No matter. Though this person's divine sense is strong, the fluctuations of his magic power are fierce and blazing. I imagine he only broke through to the Sage stage recently. Your master flatters himself that he has a few methods, so I do not fear him." Sage Langya stroked his beard and smiled. "Invite him aboard for a talk!"

...

"Master, are these actually cultivators of Langya Mountain?"

"Did you not see the blue-flower emblem [TERM?] on that flying ship? Back when we rode Langya Mountain's merchant ship to Giant Whale Harbor, that ship had this emblem on it."

Beneath Langya Mountain, a hundred flowers bloomed year-round, and it was especially famous for a kind of pale-colored yet extraordinarily fragrant blue flower [TERM?].

Thus, this blue flower was the emblem of Langya Mountain's faction.

Heavenjade came to a sudden understanding. Back then, she had been heavily injured and had no leisure to pay attention to such things while they traveled.

Now, thinking back carefully, she did have some impression of it.

Just then, the elder's mellow voice came again from the flying ship.

"Wilderness Wanderer, seeing you here, could it be that you also intend to go to Molan Court to attend the righteous-demonic War-Ceasing Assembly?"

Luo Chen's brow creased.

The righteous-demonic War-Ceasing Assembly. What was that?

And the three words Molan Court made his heart give a fierce leap.

If he remembered correctly, Blood Nightmare Moluo's lair in Moluo Stream was Molan Court!

On the flying ship, Sage Langya continued, "If that is so, why not come aboard for a conversation? This poor Daoist would also like to befriend one more new Fellow Daoist."

Luo Chen took a deep breath and walked out from the carriage.

Behind him, Heavenjade and Sang Jinghe looked over with worried gazes, but he smiled and waved a hand.

"It is fine!"

As the words fell, he drifted lightly onto the ship, without the slightest hint of worldly smoke or fire.

There were no clouds beneath his feet, nor any clear wind holding him up.

This one move alone caused the hundred-some Langya disciples on the Langxuan Cloud Ferry to turn solemn inwardly.

This was indeed a Sage's method!

In utter silence, he controlled the spiritual qi of heaven and earth to aid his flight.

Or perhaps this was already almost an instinctive motion!

The moment he boarded, Luo Chen looked left and right, then nodded slightly.

Langya Mountain seemed to be managed rather well. Of the hundred or so people here, all were Initiate true cultivators, and there were even several Adept auras faintly discernible inside the flying ship.

And this was only the configuration for a journey outside.

One could imagine that Langya Mountain at full strength was absolutely no weaker than any normal Sage-level upper sect.

"Fellow Daoist, this way, please!"

Luo Chen smiled faintly and strode into the Daoist-temple-like palace at the very center of the flying ship.

Once inside, he saw a Daoist with white hair, a long beard, and a ruddy face waiting with cupped hands.

"This poor Daoist is Mei Yiqi. Greetings, Fellow Daoist."

Luo Chen smiled faintly and likewise returned the Daoist salute. "This humble one is Luo Chen. Greetings, Fellow Daoist."

After exchanging names, Luo Chen finally took his seat.

Amid the curling fragrance of tea, Sage Langya examined Luo Chen carefully. Only after a long while did he shake his head and sigh. "Wilderness Wanderer looks so young. This poor Daoist cannot see the slightest weathering of time upon you, yet your realm is nearly equal to mine. Tsk. The cultivation world truly hides dragons and crouching tigers!"

Luo Chen chuckled. "I have merely cultivated quietly in the wilds, spared the battering winds and waves of the cultivation world, so I appear somewhat young. Sage, you overpraise me!"

In those few brief words, probes were hidden.

Luo Chen did give the other party an answer, but it did not answer the real question.

He changed the subject and went straight to the point.

"This wanderer has spent these years bitterly cultivating in the Demon Sea, long absent from the mortal dust. May I ask what this righteous-demonic War-Ceasing Assembly is?"

Sage Langya smiled. "Ceasing war means stopping the fighting. The hundred-year righteous-demonic war has failed to decide a victor, and now the Northern Sea Demon Alliance is launching a large-scale assault against our human territories. This righteous-demonic struggle ought to be declared over."

"The demon calamity is actually so severe?" Luo Chen frowned.

Sage Langya was still smiling, but this time there was bitterness in it.

"Fellow Daoist has been free and unfettered in the Demon Sea. How could you know that the demon beasts have grown so powerful they are already difficult to contain? Thirty years ago, the desolate beast Ghost Immortal Tower broke Great Power Li's demon-sealing array [TERM?] and charged into Arctic Yemo Heaven. Borrowing the lingering force of the Fallen Sea's abnormal phenomenon, it forcibly tore a gap through the Billion-Soul Demon-Locking Grand Array laid down before death by the soul-refining ancestor of the Primordial Demon Sect. A thousand-li dike collapses through an ant hole. Because of that one gap, the countless powerful demon beasts sealed in Yemo Heaven gained complete freedom."

"I hear that Great Sage Beili now holds Yemo Heaven, while Sea Emperor Whale has emerged from seclusion and stands in confrontation with our human great power Li Canghai, neither side gaining the upper hand."

"Logically, this should be a balanced situation, yet wherever that desolate beast Ghost Immortal Tower passes, no one can withstand even a single exchange!"

"The Myriad Immortals Assembly has been broken, Divine Origin City is besieged, and the Penglai Immortal Sect has fled. Our Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance is attacked from front and rear. This righteous-demonic war truly cannot continue."

One piece of news after another burst into Luo Chen's ears like exploding beans, and his calm expression could no longer remain indifferent.

He had never expected the situation to have deteriorated to this degree.

Even if Arctic Yemo Heaven had sealed away many demon beasts, it had still experienced the demon war; with the Primordial Demon Sect's foundation, its dying counterattack should have taken away most of the elites no matter what.

Unexpectedly, the demon coalition still retained such powerful battle strength.

And that desolate beast Ghost Immortal Tower, which he had glimpsed in haste back then, was truly that formidable? Even human Sage experts could not resist it?

The news that the Myriad Immortals Assembly had been broken was startling at first hearing, but it was still within reason.

But Divine Origin City being besieged was somewhat unbelievable.

One had to know that the power behind Divine Origin City was the Tianyuan Dao Sect of Central Continent!

Luo Chen took a deep breath and asked about another matter.

"Moluo Stream is willing to stop the war?"

Sage Langya poured Luo Chen a cup of tea, then said, "Why would they not be willing? Their ally, the Penglai Immortal Sect, has fled, and their own strength was inferior to our Cang Hai Alliance to begin with. Stopping the war is the wise choice."

Luo Chen was puzzled. "But I remember that Blood Nightmare Moluo of Moluo Stream is someone who fears the world will not fall into chaos. He is actually willing to join hands with you to resist the demon race? He even yielded his cultivation land as the venue for the War-Ceasing Assembly?"

"Heh, you mean Blood Nightmare Moluo!"

At this point, Sage Langya looked at Luo Chen with a smile that was not quite a smile.

"It seems Fellow Daoist has not been cultivating in the Demon Sea for very long!"

Luo Chen smiled faintly and did not argue.

Blood Nightmare Moluo's nature had only later become known to the world.

The ambush and killing of Mu Lan Gaoxiong, a high-ranking member of the Cang Hai Alliance, which completely set off the righteous-demonic war, had occurred eighty or ninety years ago.

Since Luo Chen knew the other party's style of conduct, that meant he had still been in the Northern Sea cultivation world at that time.

Sage Langya had tested out a bit of Luo Chen's true situation and was rather satisfied.

Then his tone sank.

"After Blood Nightmare Moluo emerged from the Fallen Sea, Blood Wanderer pursued and killed him the whole way, making him no better than a stray dog. He could not hide fast enough, so how would he dare return to Moluo Stream?"

"In particular, the matter of him refining all the Primordial Demon Sect disciples and followers he had recruited in earlier years into Blood God Children was discovered."

"With such demonic conduct, the Northern Sea cultivation world has long had no place left for him!"

Luo Chen was stunned.

The man had originally been a member of a demonic sect, so what was surprising about his conduct belonging to the demonic path?

But immediately after, he suddenly realized what was wrong.

Refining Blood God Children was already contrary to human nature.

Yet he had used the remnants of the Primordial Demon Sect whom he had recruited in earlier years to refine Blood God Children.

One had to know that when Blood Nightmare Moluo first established Moluo Stream, the banner he raised was taking in survivors of the Primordial Demon Sect and restoring the Primordial Demon Sect's glory.

This act of his was no different from destroying his own credibility!

Credibility, reputation, righteousness: Luo Chen knew very well how much power these things possessed.

In earlier years, the Daoist title Fire Spirit Lord [TERM?] alone had made many strangers believe in him directly. This was the benefit reputation brought.

Moluo Stream's founding principle had also been to restore the Primordial Demon Sect's glory.

Whether its founder had been that late-stage Sage great cultivator behind the scenes or Blood Nightmare Moluo, this elder of the demonic sect, in short, they had torn down their own banner and no longer possessed any name of righteousness.

Therefore, when Sage Langya said there was no place left for Blood Nightmare Moluo in the Northern Sea, he truly was not wrong.

Moluo Stream would be the first to refuse to spare Blood Nightmare Moluo!

As for the Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance, that went without saying.

As for the Myriad Immortals Assembly, once one of the three great overlords, it had already scattered to wind and cloud.

If Blood Wanderer's relentless pursuit were added as well...

Luo Chen shook his head and sighed. "No wonder Molan Court, the cultivation land of that demonic giant, has become the place where the righteous and demonic sides stop the war and negotiate peace."

"The affairs of the world are impermanent. Who could have known before that matters would become like this? However..."

Sage Langya first sighed, then revealed a smile.

"However, this has given us an opportunity."

"An opportunity?" Luo Chen was curious.

Sage Langya nodded. "Yes! In the past, the righteous and demonic sides fought fiercely, communication between the two was severed, and trade in all kinds of cultivation resources was also suspended. For example, one material required for a secret art of this poor Daoist's lineage is produced only by those under Split Heaven Stream Lord of Moluo Stream. Now that the righteous-demonic war has ceased, I can also purchase that material again and continue cultivating the secret art that had previously stalled. This righteous-demonic War-Ceasing Assembly is not only a negotiation to stop the war, but also an excellent chance for us Sage cultivators to exchange with one another."

Luo Chen came to a sudden understanding. So that was why!

In the past, Langya Mountain had not been part of the Cang Hai Alliance, but after the righteous-demonic war began, they were forced to join.

Since they sided with the Cang Hai Alliance, Moluo Stream naturally would no longer trade relevant resources with them.

Now, this was indeed an opportunity.

Thinking of this, Luo Chen abruptly asked, "I hear that the Primordial Demon Sect once circulated a method for producing soul essence, and most of the forces that mastered this method are now within Moluo Stream. I wonder, are they still continuing to produce soul essence?"

The moment these words emerged, the atmosphere in the hall abruptly congealed.

Sage Langya pinched his grizzled beard, eyes narrowing as he looked at Luo Chen.

"Soul essence [TERM?]?"

"Wilderness Wanderer, could you also be a remnant of the Primordial Demon Sect?"

*(End of Chapter)*
