# Chapter 720: A Night on Calm Lake, Haixin as Ever

The geographic environment of the Swallow Islands was extremely remote!

Luo Chen had known this trait from the beginning, but after circling halfway through the Northern Sea cultivation world, his impression of it had only deepened.

Nearby, there were basically no large sea regions suitable for cultivation, and even slightly powerful cultivators were few and far between.

Outsiders were not often seen either.

If not for relying on Luo Chen's name back then to establish a merchant caravan that regularly traveled to other flourishing places, perhaps even communication with the outside world would have been extremely closed off.

The reason Yan Nantian [TERM?] had been able to cultivate in seclusion in the Swallow Islands and avoid Moluo Stream's pursuit back then was also because he benefited from this aspect.

The reason this situation formed, apart from sparse spirit veins, was also connected to the vast ocean behind them that was shrouded in dense mist all year round.

Its name was the Southern Ocean, and it bordered both the Southern Border and the Eastern Wilderness.

No one knew whether those thick mists were the gu-poison mists [TERM?] of the Southern Border or the nether qi ghost mists [TERM?] of the Eastern Wilderness's Abyssal Deep [TERM?]. In any case, few dared cross the Southern Ocean.

On this day.

On the sea horizon where the Southern Ocean and Northern Sea met, a figure slowly appeared.

White robe, red sash, long hair tied behind him, and a pair of black eyes like cold pools and deep abysses, calm yet containing endless heat.

This person was precisely Luo Chen.

He looked at the faintly floating black mist, as if he stood on the boundary between life and death.

Taking a deep breath, Luo Chen waved a hand to disperse the black mist and stepped inside.

The sea wind blew, and black mist from every direction gathered over, gradually filling the empty space.

...

In less than the time it took an incense stick to burn, something seemed to boil within the Southern Ocean.

Afterward, a figure wreathed in firelight flew out from within.

Luo Chen turned back to look at this ocean shrouded year-round in thin mist and thick cloud, his face neither pleased nor sorrowful.

Closing his eyes, he looked inward at himself. Some tiny insect eggs invisible to the naked eye were lying dormant in his meridians and blood vessels.

"Are these gu eggs [TERM?]?"

With a thought from Luo Chen, the Withering-Bloom True Flame burned through his entire body.

In an instant, those insect eggs were silently burned to ash, transforming into pure energy that fed back into Luo Chen's fleshly body.

"Rumor says Southern Border cultivators are skilled at using gu insects [TERM?], and most of their gu insects [TERM?] come from the mysterious Southern Ocean. Seeing it now, its reputation is deserved!" Luo Chen muttered to himself.

In that short span just now, as he flew inside and breathed normally, he had inhaled some unknown insect eggs into his body.

One could see how dangerous it was!

And this was only the border of the Southern Ocean.

If he went deeper, there would probably be even more powerful insect eggs, perhaps gu insects [TERM?] of ranked grade.

Luo Chen was proficient in breath-sealing methods and believed that with his current realm, he could seal his breath for several months in one go and create an internal cycle. Even if he opened his breathing, he had true flame protecting him. Yet even so, he could not defend against the omnipresent invasion of gu insects [TERM?].

He did not know what kind of fortune of heaven and earth had nurtured such a strange place within the Mountain and Sea Realm.

Luo Chen shook his head and turned to fly toward the Swallow Islands.

The plan to land in the Southern Border through the Southern Ocean died before birth.

Luo Chen was not disappointed. It had only been an attempt to begin with.

The Southern Ocean was strange and treacherous; everyone knew this.

With the Northern Sea's abundant resources, its trade spread throughout the five continents under heaven, yet even it could not directly reach the Southern Ocean and instead had to pass through Central Continent as a transit station.

Since there was already a ready-made route, smuggling across the Southern Ocean was truly unnecessary.

Thinking of this, Luo Chen grew increasingly curious about the structure of this world.

With his current Nascent Soul realm, where he could briefly merge spirit with heaven and earth, he had already seen the true face of the Mountain and Sea Realm.

This world was not the imagined round heaven and square earth!

It was more like the "planet" Luo Chen knew!

But cultivators had conquered and developed it for only ten thousand years. This world was still very primitive.

Beyond the Eastern Wilderness, Southern Border, Western Desert, Northern Sea, and even Central Continent, there were still many places that had yet to be explored.

For example, the Abyssal Deep [TERM?] surrounding the Eastern Wilderness sacred land, the Abyssal Deep Sect [TERM?], and the boundless Hundred Thousand Mountains [TERM?].

Or the Northern Sea's endless Demon Sea.

The Southern Ocean on which the Southern Border relied remained incomparably mysterious to this day.

The back side of the Mountain and Sea Realm was roughly this kind of structure: the Abyssal Deep [TERM?], the Gu Ocean [TERM?], the Demon Sea, and the barbaric mountain ranges [TERM?], filled with endless ghost mist and yin qi [TERM?]. Perhaps there might even be surviving ancient beasts roaming inside!

The so-called ancient, archaic, and similar era-counting methods commonly used by the human race were likely only the snap of a finger compared to the history of this world!

"The Nascent Soul realm is still somewhat insufficient. If I can reach the Ascendant realm, perhaps I can explore this world completely."

As Luo Chen thought this, his feet had already landed on a high cliff.

Sea-Watching Cliff [TERM?]!

When he arrived, someone was already waiting there.

Looking at the tall young matron full of mature charm, waist truly waist and hips truly hips, with a pair of long legs still eye-catching even as she bowed respectfully, Luo Chen swept his eyes over her and revealed a smile.

"Haixin, you have improved quite a bit over these years!"

Only after Luo Chen spoke did the cautious Cheng Haixin relax and raise her graceful head.

With one glance, her brows and eyes froze.

The Perfected Being's face was just as it had been back then, yet his complexion had grown even fairer.

Not only that, as he stood there with hands clasped behind his back, even if he did nothing, he seemed like a natural-born flame, scorching the eyes so that one did not dare look straight at him.

She drew a breath and said softly, "Perfected Being overpraises me. Haixin's aptitude is dull, and after a hundred years I still have not formed my core. I have disappointed Perfected Being."

A hundred years had passed. Luo Chen had already formed his Nascent Soul, while Cheng Haixin was only at the ninth level of Foundation Establishment, not even at Great Completion. By comparison, she was indeed overshadowed.

But if judged by age, she was only around one hundred and forty, and possessing this realm already counted as a genius in the cultivation world!

It could be seen that when Luo Chen left back then, the pill resources he left behind and the abundant spirit vein of Invitation Moon Island had greatly benefited this woman.

Luo Chen smiled and asked about the situation in the Swallow Islands.

At present, among the islands, the Cheng Clan stood alone as the dominant family, occupying seven islands exclusively. The number of Foundation Establishment cultivators in the clan had even surpassed the count of two hands, and they truly counted as the number one Foundation Establishment clan of Swallow Islands!

However, in this hundred years, it was not as if no Golden Core experts had emerged in the Swallow Islands.

For example, the Xu Clan [TERM?], which had good relations with the Cheng Clan, had a third elder who had fortunately advanced to the Golden Core stage after comprehending the Azure Yang Pill Classic [TERM?] Luo Chen had left on the stone stele on Invitation Moon Island.

"And where is he?"

"He left."

"Hm?"

Cheng Haixin explained the reasons in detail.

It turned out that after the Cheng Clan's third elder formed his core, he deeply felt that the Swallow Islands were poor in resources, and that relying only on resources exchanged by merchant caravans was difficult to support a Golden Core cultivator's cultivation.

Especially since the only third-tier spirit vein nominally belonged to Cheng Haixin.

And this spirit vein even bore the mark of Azure Yang Demon Lord!

Remembering the friendship between the Xu and Cheng clans, and also intimidated by Luo Chen's demonic might, the third elder could only go out to search for a cultivation environment more suitable for himself.

At that time, the righteous-demonic war was intensifying. The Xu Clan's third elder seized the opportunity to make a name for himself and indeed gained quite a bit.

In this process, he was also noticed by experts of the Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance [TERM?], who brought him into a power under the Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance [TERM?] and even gave him a small island with a third-tier spirit vein, allowing his clan to recuperate and build strength.

"He is rather sensible," Luo Chen chuckled. Then, as if thinking of something, he suddenly asked, "What about Xu Muxian [TERM?]?"

This name had not come from Luo Chen's mouth in a very long time.

But he would not forget it.

No matter what, this was his nominal in-name disciple.

On the path of alchemy, he had quite some talent.

Faced with Luo Chen's question, Cheng Haixin hesitated a moment, but still answered, "He left as well."

Luo Chen's brows furrowed. "Did he leave together with the Xu Clan?"

The reason he frowned was because he remembered that Xu Muxian [TERM?] did not have a very good relationship with the Xu Clan itself. Later, because of his marriage alliance with the Cheng Clan, he had inclined even more toward the Cheng side.

To this, Cheng Haixin shook her head.

"No. Muxian left very early. After his wife passed away, and after he took the Foundation Establishment Pill you specially left for him and successfully established his foundation, he left the Swallow Islands without hesitation to venture into the outside world. This happened before the Xu Clan's third elder formed his core."

Hearing this answer, Luo Chen fell into silence.

He vaguely remembered that the in-name disciple had once been lost, and Luo Chen had even scolded him for aiming too high by thinking of core formation before even establishing foundation.

Their final conversation had been Luo Chen encouraging him, saying that even if the Great Dao was hard to seek, there were countless sights in the world, and one should always go out to see them.

Unexpectedly, the other party had not only successfully established foundation, but had truly jumped out of the well-like place of the Swallow Islands to see the world's scenery.

'Did I count as a good example?'

Luo Chen sighed and asked, "Did he leave behind any children or descendants?"

Cheng Haixin shook her head and said no.

Since that was the case, Luo Chen asked no more and raised another matter instead.

"I am only passing through the Swallow Islands and will not stay long. Tomorrow morning, I will leave here. This time, I will give you another choice. Do you want to leave with me?"

Cheng Haixin froze, then an incomparably beautiful smile bloomed across her face.

Back then, Luo Chen had also given her such a choice, and now it had come again.

And unlike back then, this time Luo Chen's words carried even stronger confidence.

If she agreed, then surely the journey afterward would be smooth, and she would no longer experience as many storms as Luo Chen had in those years.

This was the powerful sense of safety brought by a Nascent Soul Perfected Being.

But this time, her answer remained the same as ever.

"That Perfected Being still remembers me makes this humble woman very happy, but..."

Luo Chen frowned. "Back then, you were held back by your clan, and I could understand that. But now, the Cheng Clan is already the foremost clan here, with more than a dozen Foundation Establishment cultivators. They do not lack you at all. Why cling to them?"

Cheng Haixin smoothed a fallen strand of hair and smiled gently. "It has nothing to do with the clan. It is only that this humble woman is used to it."

"Used to it?"

"Used to the sea and mountains here, used to the quiet on Invitation Moon Island. I have also heard some rumors about you, Perfected Being, from the Swallow merchant caravans. Whenever they speak of them, they are always so dangerous, so magnificent, that one's blood heats. But Haixin has known little turbulence since childhood, rarely fights with others, and does not understand the ways of combat. Perfected Being, you are someone who will accomplish great things, and future battles will certainly be unavoidable. If I stayed by your side, perhaps I could receive your protection, but I could not assist you wholeheartedly like High Person Heavenjade or Lord Black King. In the end, I would only be a burden."

This time, Luo Chen did not interrupt the woman, only listened quietly.

He knew that what she said was correct.

On this trip to the Northern Sea, there was both righteous-demonic conflict and the Demon Alliance invasion. Even if he returned to the Eastern Wilderness, schemes and deceit would not be lacking.

With Cheng Haixin by his side, she would indeed be a burden.

Even though he had a certain confidence that he could protect her, he did not have complete certainty that he could care for her in every respect.

From the beginning, those who surrounded him had all been "useful people"!

"This humble woman has thought it through clearly. War and fire, authority and power, none are what I want. Only quiet cultivation and comprehending Dao and law are the lifelong refuge of my heart."

"That Perfected Being remembers me is already extremely good."

"In the future, if Perfected Being is idle, has free time, or is inj... tired, then come back to Invitation Moon Island and sit for a while."

"This humble woman will warm wine and wait!"

Luo Chen looked seriously at the woman. Time had left traces on her face.

Even though her appearance was still beautiful and alluring, she was no longer the delicate young girl she had been when they first met.

But her bearing had grown increasingly mature, and her thoughts were no longer the passivity of someone ordered by her clan's elder brother.

He did not try to persuade her. He merely reached out, wrapped an arm around her waist, and drew her into his embrace.

"The wind is getting strong. Let us return to the island!"

Cheng Haixin's brows and eyes curved, and the corner of her mouth lifted slightly.

She still remembered that back then, the phrase "On Sea-Watching Cliff, the night is cold and the wind is strong" had been spoken by her.

...

At midnight, Luo Chen, wearing soft silk inner clothes, stroked the woman's smooth jade back while his divine sense swept over her whole body.

Suddenly!

His fingertip lightly tapped, and strands of gentle magic power entered her body.

Thread after thread of foul qi [TERM?] and impurities were expelled from the apertures on her skin. With a sweep of his sleeve, they all fell to the floor, while her skin remained smooth as ever.

With a soft moan, Cheng Haixin woke from her sleep.

"Husband?"

"Not calling me Perfected Being now?"

Cheng Haixin's face flushed with shyness. Ever since the night of their farewell back then, she had regarded Luo Chen as her husband. Earlier, it had only been because of the difference in realm that she had not dared climb too high.

Now, after blossoms bloomed a second time, it naturally could not be compared to before.

Luo Chen smiled. "Just now, while you were asleep, I unblocked your meridians and removed some impurities from your physique. But I cannot rashly touch the small amount of pill toxin on the barrier of your qi sea. So if you wish to form your core, you still have to bitterly cultivate that alchemy art in the Fine Dust Primordial Art that removes pill toxin."

"Also, keep these few bottles of pills well. They will be of great use when you form your core, especially this bottle of mild Condensing Liquid Pills [TERM?]!"

"And this water-attribute cultivation art can connect seamlessly with your later cultivation. Even cultivating all the way to the Nascent Soul stage, it will be enough."

"As for this high-grade water-attribute flying sword, with your current ability you cannot sacrificially refine it, so I will seal it properly for you. After you form your core..."

While Luo Chen was speaking at length, a tender finger covered his mouth.

"Husband, tonight, can we not speak of the Great Dao, and speak only of wind and moon?"

Luo Chen paused, then laughed freely.

It was he who had become attached to appearances. For decades without rest, he had only cared about advancing bravely and fiercely.

"True. Only wind and moon."

That night, the wind on the island was urgent, the moon unclear, and rain fell, raising ripples across the calm lake of ten li.

...

Early the next morning, Luo Chen quietly departed with Heavenjade driving the carriage.

Across the entire Swallow Islands, countless cultivators never knew that Azure Yang Demon Lord, who had once overawed the islands, had returned.

Cheng Haixin did not see him off. She simply stood before that stone stele, staring blankly.

After a long while, she paced back into the hall.

The palace doors closed heavily, and this closure would last for several decades.

...

"Master, where are we going now?"

Heavenjade asked.

The tenderness in Luo Chen's eyes gradually faded, leaving behind a trace of fierceness.

"First, to Coldlight Island [TERM?]!"

Heavenjade froze, then suddenly understood.

Some people and some matters, no one mentioned them, but that did not mean no one remembered.

The Sixfold Imperial Flying Cloud Carriage [TERM?] abruptly shook, its speed swift as it streaked across the sky like flowing light!
