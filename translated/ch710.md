# Chapter 710: Discussing Dao on Rosy Hill, Nascent Soul Pills Completed

One year later.

On the western side of Dragon Abyss Island [TERM?], there was a stretch of lush green.

When a breeze blew past, one nimble bird after another seemed to dance in the air.

Looking closely, where were there any birds? They were clearly peculiar flowers and grasses connected to vines. Because their fluttering bodies resembled the cut-open tails of divine birds, kites, and swallows, they gave people the illusion of flying birds.

These were Divine Iris!

A white figure moved through the flowers, circulating qi and forming seals, using alchemy arts to pick flowers into a basket.

His movements were very slow and very gentle, for fear of harming the Divine Iris in the slightest.

One, two, three, four... twelve fourth-tier Divine Iris in total!

The rest were basically too immature, their years insufficient, only around second or third tier.

Sang Jinghe [TERM?] stood outside the spirit field, watching the white-robed Daoist slowly walk out along the field path.

When he came near, Sang Jinghe [TERM?] respectfully said, "Congratulations, Senior, on the spirit medicine harvest!"

Looking at those spirit-light-bright medicinal grasses inside the bamboo basket woven from special vines, Luo Chen could not help smiling as well.

He praised Sang Jinghe [TERM?], saying, "You have worked hard. I remember that when this patch of Divine Iris was transplanted, there were only eight at the fourth-tier level. In your hands, after a short nine years, four more have appeared. You have truly helped me greatly."

Sang Jinghe [TERM?] was flattered, but still humbly said, "This junior does not dare claim credit. This is because Dragon Abyss Island's [TERM?] spirit soil is fertile and extremely suitable for medicinal materials to grow..."

The two walked along the field path without hurry, discussing the situation on Dragon Abyss Island [TERM?].

Speaking of which, ever since taking control of this island, Luo Chen had focused on entering the earth abyss to cultivate and had never learned the details of this island in depth.

Ordinarily, most of it was left to Heavenjade and Sang Jinghe [TERM?] to manage.

Now, under Sang Jinghe's [TERM?] explanation, he learned what kind of treasure land this was.

Natural spirit qi accumulated in the depths of the earth abyss, and when it leaked outward, it first passed through the soil.

There were also countless Dragon Earthworms [TERM?], constantly loosening the soil and secreting special mucus over thousands of years.

Over time, most of the soil on this island possessed rather decent spirit qi.

There were seven kinds of fourth-tier spirit soil alone!

As for third-tier ones, there were about twenty-one.

Below third tier, they were countless, spread across every corner of Dragon Abyss Island [TERM?].

Because each type of spirit soil had different properties, many strange spirit medicines, flowers, and grasses had grown upon these soils.

Most flowers and grasses with decent spirit qi had been eaten raw by the Dragon Earthworms [TERM?] here.

For example, when Luo Chen had first entered the earth abyss, the three third-tier Dragon Earthworms [TERM?] he encountered had forcibly broken through after the Fallen Abyss Dragon Sect [TERM?] left by eating several high-tier spirit medicines.

But there were also some spirit medicines Dragon Earthworms [TERM?] naturally disliked that had luckily survived, and their grades were not low.

These spirit medicines were currently all growing vigorously under Sang Jinghe's [TERM?] care.

As one name after another was reported, Luo Chen gradually gained a sense of them in his heart.

Especially when matched against the pill books and formulas he had been reading during this time, he slowly outlined a medicinal material combination that could assist in condensing a Golden Core.

At the end.

Sang Jinghe [TERM?] sighed with both joy and regret:

"This island truly is a rare blessed land in the world!"

"Even if it is not very suitable for cultivators to cultivate, if treated specifically as a spirit medicine garden, Dragon Abyss Island [TERM?] would absolutely make countless Sage upper sects fight until their heads split."

"It is just a pity that these Dragon Earthworms [TERM?] do not understand spirit plant cultivation methods. They guarded a treasure land for thousands of years but did not know how to use it properly."

Luo Chen listened with a smile, and in his heart, his thoughts were roughly the same.

Back when the Luo Tian Sect was on Danxia Mountain [TERM?], at first it mainly operated a pill and medicinal material business.

To plant medicinal materials, they had also spent great effort on spirit soil and spirit fields.

At one point, they had imported green-scaled fish [TERM?] from the Li family of Qinhua River [TERM?], using this fish's waste to cultivate spirit soil.

Even so, they could only cultivate relatively ordinary first-tier spirit soil.

How could that compare to Dragon Abyss Island [TERM?], where spirit soil was everywhere and could be called a blessed land for spirit planters?

Luo Chen could not stay on Dragon Abyss Island [TERM?] forever. He would certainly leave in the future, and when that time came, this spirit soil could not be wasted. If he could completely refine the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion [TERM?], perhaps he could take away most of the spirit soil.

As they spoke, the two had already left the Divine Iris medicinal field.

"Continue taking good care of the remaining Divine Iris. Do not be negligent," Luo Chen instructed.

Sang Jinghe [TERM?] bowed and cupped his fists. "Senior, rest assured. This junior understands."

Luo Chen smiled. "In addition, take the seven medicinal materials you mentioned earlier, Golden Cotton Bramble Flower [TERM?], First Dusk Fruit [TERM?]... pick ten mature portions and deliver them outside the emerald pool."

He did not mention what he needed these spirit medicines for, and Sang Jinghe [TERM?] did not ask more.

Only when they parted did Luo Chen give one extra instruction.

"Immersing yourself in spirit plant cultivation is good, but do not neglect your own cultivation."

Sang Jinghe [TERM?] froze.

He could be considered the successor Sang Jiugong had spent great effort cultivating, so his cultivation naturally was not poor. He had already reached Initiate Stage Nine.

Otherwise, he would not have gone alone to Purple Spirit Island [TERM?].

But in his view, his value to Devil Lord Azure Yang was helping him cultivate medicinal materials. The so-called Initiate Stage Nine realm was not worth mentioning.

He had not expected the other party to actually remind him not to walk a crooked path.

For a moment, Sang Jinghe's [TERM?] heart was somewhat moved.

...

Important figures were usually used to being strict, and occasional concern always caught people off guard, giving rise to gratitude.

Luo Chen did not care what the other man thought. Carrying the bamboo basket, he rode a clear breeze and flew toward the emerald pool.

When he passed Mount Rosy Hill [TERM?], he stopped.

Looking from afar, countless rosy clouds fell into that mountain peak, and thus it was named Mount Rosy Hill [TERM?].

On the mountain, someone invited him.

Luo Chen smiled faintly and turned toward Mount Rosy Hill [TERM?].

As soon as he reached the summit and looked at the distant scenery, Luo Chen's eyes could not help brightening, his mind broad and spirit refreshed.

It was precisely high sky and vast sea, ten thousand qing of emerald waves, rosy light woven like brocade, and weary birds like clouds!

Han Zhan greeted Luo Chen and had him sit opposite him.

Blue stone served as the table, fine wine as companion.

With a slight tilt of his head, a mouthful of clear wine entered his throat, as if he had also drunk down ten thousand rays of rosy light.

"Tsk..."

"Senior truly has fine leisure. Drinking wine and admiring scenery every day, how carefree. Unlike me, sitting alone in a cold, hidden abyss, not knowing sun or moon."

Luo Chen let out a long breath. It had been a long time since he had been this relaxed.

Han Zhan was unconcerned by his sigh.

"This old man is currently adapting to the body and cannot over-cultivate. Otherwise, do you think I would not want to cultivate bitterly?"

"As for you..."

Han Zhan glanced at the bamboo basket placed beside the blue stone.

"In the first half of the year, you picked all the mature fourth-tier Purple Monkey Flowers, and today you personally harvested Divine Iris. It seems refining Nascent Soul Pills has already been placed on the schedule, no?"

Luo Chen smiled faintly. "Senior's insight is keen as a torch!"

Afterward, he paused, then slowly said, "Does Senior still remember the matter you promised me back then?"

"What matter?" Han Zhan asked casually.

Luo Chen was not annoyed by the other man's forgetting. He said seriously, "Back then, Senior mentioned that after I refined Nascent Soul Pills, you could tell me your insights from taking a Nascent Soul Pill."

"Oh, that!" Han Zhan was surprised for a moment, then looked at Luo Chen in astonishment. "From the look of it, you have quite a lot of confidence in refining Nascent Soul Pills, since you have come to ask ahead of time."

How could Luo Chen not have confidence!

With his current alchemy attainments, low-tier pills came easily to hand. Fourth-tier ones only required a little more effort.

Even if this ancient Nascent Soul Pill formula was rather complicated, at worst he could invest ten Achievement Points and make complete preparations.

Seeing Luo Chen's resolve, Han Zhan did not refuse. Taking advantage of the wine, he simply recounted one by one the insights he had gained when taking a Nascent Soul Pill in the past.

"That Nascent Soul Pill of mine was obtained from the ruins of an ancient cultivator. It was very old, and much of its medicinal power had been lost. When I took it, it nearly caused me to fail at the last step. Fortunately, this old man's foundation was deep, and supplemented by other Nascent Soul formation resources bestowed by Sky Sail City [TERM?], I luckily succeeded in one attempt. I remember that when the Nascent Soul Pill entered my throat, it was incomparably spicy. When I refined it, the medicinal power was still incomparably condensed. It did not enter apertures, nor did it stagnate in the meridians, but rushed all at once into the qi sea. After entering the qi sea, it did not scatter either, but completely wrapped around the Golden Core and began continuously seeping in..."

...

Luo Chen listened with great seriousness, not missing even the smallest point.

Especially concerning certain taboos, he repeatedly sought confirmation from Han Zhan.

After the Nascent Soul Pill was refined, its medicinal power absolutely could not be forcibly divided.

When it invaded and dyed the Golden Core, one had to borrow this power to achieve the unity of essence, qi, and spirit, forming the sign of three flowers gathering at the crown [TERM?]!

At the end.

Han Zhan drank a mouthful of wine and moistened his throat.

Putting down the wine cup, he looked at Luo Chen with the same seriousness.

"In truth, in this old man's view, your foundation is so mighty that among Adepts of this age, few can compare. In particular, when I observe your constitution, it is invasive like fire, grand and upright, as if you were born as a true flame!"

"Although I do not know what changes your body has undergone."

"Under such circumstances, perhaps you do not need to borrow that Nascent Soul Pill at all and still have a seventy to eighty percent chance of success. There is no need to seek external objects, is there?"

A seventy to eighty percent chance of forming the Nascent Soul was truly terrifying.

Few people in the world could build their Adept-stage foundation to such a degree.

Even heavenly spirit root cultivators, said to have no bottlenecks in breaking through realms below Ascendant, actually only had a fifty-fifty chance of success or failure when condensing the Nascent Soul.

Luo Chen naturally knew the condition of his own body.

After soaking in the Spirit-Molding Sacred Spring [TERM?], he had possessed a Five-Spirit Dao Body [TERM?] comparable to five-element heavenly spirit roots!

Even though this Dao body had been reshaped because of physical nirvana, his new Fire Spirit Body was still an excellent constitution rarely seen in the world.

With this constitution and his foundation, the chance of successfully forming a Nascent Soul had greatly increased.

But!

"If there is absolute certainty, why be stingy with one pill? If I fail at the last step because of that twenty to thirty percent chance, how could I face the countless bones of those who died by my hand along this road?"

Luo Chen spoke indifferently.

Han Zhan nodded, unable to refute him.

After this conversation, the sun had already set westward, and the moon had risen to the middle branches.

The rosy clouds were gone. Only clear wind and bright moon remained as companions.

Han Zhan filled another cup of wine for Luo Chen, and at the same time raised his own cup, his face filled with emotion.

"Seventy years, has it been?"

"Since you and I drifted to the Northern Sea, more than a sixty-year cycle has passed."

"At that time, you had just entered the Adept realm, and I was a wisp of remnant soul. Now, your Nascent Soul is imminent, my flesh body has been reshaped, and both you and I have hope on the great road."

"Looking back now, the past truly is like smoke!"

"Come, a toast to these seventy years of wind and moon!"

Luo Chen raised his wine cup and clinked it with his.

He could not experience that feeling of vicissitude, only because he was not yet two hundred years old, and on the path of cultivation was still at the time of advancing bravely. Where did he have the mood to speak of wind and moon and lament the passage of time?

But his heart was also heavy.

Seventy years had passed, and he did not know whether old friends and former companions were well or ill.

Just then, the sound of startled waves striking the shore rang out.

Under the moonlight, a vigorous figure leapt out from the sea, soaring for an instant before falling back into the vast ocean.

Luo Chen's gaze focused.

It was Black King!

After walking once along the edge of death, he had gained new life.

Now his aura was growing increasingly majestic, faintly possessing the bearing of a great demon battling the vast ocean!

"You must work hard, or this spirit pet of yours may walk ahead of you."

Beside his ear came Han Zhan's soft laugh.

Luo Chen pursed his lips and nodded slightly.

...

Black King's progress was unexpected, yet also reasonable.

Luo Chen had cultivated him for many years, and his foundation had already been quite good.

Before and after this great life-and-death tribulation, he first soaked in the Spirit-Molding Sacred Spring [TERM?], then absorbed the blood of several million Dragon Earthworms [TERM?]. In particular, within it was a drop of Luo Chen's incomparably precious tongue-tip essence blood, which contained Tai Sui vitality and Luo Chen's origin power.

After he devoured the fifth-tier Stone Dragon remnant soul, he not only returned from death, but also filled in his final weakness.

Over this past year, hidden deep in the sea, he was clearly undergoing some kind of transformation!

But Luo Chen had no time to pay attention.

The matters in his hands were far more urgent.

Apart from his daily mana tempering, advancing his cultivation, and moving step by step toward Adept Perfected Completion, Luo Chen placed all his energy on alchemy.

Not only Nascent Soul Pills, but also Condensed Liquid Pills [TERM?]!

This pill originated from one of Bright Radiance Heaven's [TERM?] thirty-six pill formulas and was specially used for Initiate cultivators to advance to Golden Core.

Clearly, the three series of pill formulas left by Devil Lord Heaven-Refining for humans, demons, and devils did not pursue extremes, but universality.

Luo Chen had not refined pills for some years.

After he personally processed those main materials for the Nascent Soul Pill, they still needed a little time to settle.

During this interval, in order to get used to the three newly refined alchemy tools, Luo Chen began practicing.

The pills of the past had no meaning for him no matter how many he refined.

So he simply set his sights on the pill formulas left by Devil Lord Heaven-Refining.

The Condensed Liquid Pill [TERM?] was undoubtedly a very good choice.

The medicinal materials he had specially asked Sang Jinghe [TERM?] to collect earlier were precisely the main materials required for the Condensed Liquid Pill [TERM?].

The other missing parts could be supplemented by his own stores.

After trial after trial, Luo Chen not only grew increasingly familiar with the new alchemy tools, but also began harvesting one Condensed Liquid Pill [TERM?] after another.

On this day.

In the earth abyss, Luo Chen stood outside the thatched hut, looking at the ten azure pills in his hand, and nodded in satisfaction.

This was the third successful batch of Condensed Liquid Pills [TERM?].

Adding the earlier ones, there were thirty in total!

If placed in the cultivation world, they could allow thirty Initiate Perfected Completion cultivators to attempt Core Formation.

Moreover, the medicinal power of the Condensed Liquid Pill [TERM?] was rather moderate and balanced, enough to raise the success rate of Core Formation by twenty to thirty percent. If combined with a decent Core Formation method, the success rate should be around fifty percent!

If he were still within the Luo Tian Sect, this batch of Condensed Liquid Pills [TERM?] would have enormous use.

Unfortunately, for now, they could only be shelved.

Luo Chen placed all of them into a green-skinned gourd. He planned to keep them for the future. Once he returned to the Eastern Wilderness, this batch of pills would be enough to revive a sect.

Yes, an excellent alchemist was just this formidable!

The power of one person was enough to revive a sect!

Afterward, he sent out a voice transmission.

"Give this item to Sang Jinghe [TERM?]."

"Consider it a reward for his diligent work for me over these years."

One supplementary material after another, prepared long ago, was taken out by him for final processing.

The Divine Iris was dried to preserve its medicinal power, and the Purple Monkey Flower was soaked in spirit liquid to reduce its toxicity.

Finally, there was the Five Elements Lotus Platform!

If the Primordial Unity Cauldron could still be activated, relying on its might to suppress the five elements, this lotus platform certainly would not dare act up.

And the quality was so good!

The pill quality was insufficient?

One could supplement with other spirit medicines to achieve higher-grade effect.

Or return them to the furnace and refine again, making the good even better.

And in Luo Chen's memories, there was also one method that had a chance of improving pill quality!

Even for completed pills!

Thinking of that method, Luo Chen's heart began stirring restlessly for a time.
