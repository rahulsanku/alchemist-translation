# Chapter 786: Nascent Soul Grand Ceremony, Second-Tier Talisman Master

"Father, below is Lie Feng Valley!"

On the flying boat, Xu Xiao Liu poked his head out to look at the place below, where black smoke billowed and fierce winds howled. He couldn't help but curl his lips.

After all, it was a Golden Core great sect—how could their living environment be so harsh?

But he could understand. Fenghua Domain was ruled by Fenghua Palace, where women held higher status. This carried over to the entire domain, making the circumstances of male and female cultivators vastly different.

For a male-dominated force like Lie Feng Valley, being assigned such a poor spiritual vein location was only fitting.

Thinking about it this way, Fenghua Domain had been quite generous back then to cede Bai Li Qingchuan to the Luo Tian Sect as their settlement!

"Father, should we go down and announce ourselves?"

The young girl beside him asked eagerly, looking like she could barely contain herself.

Xu Xiao Liu stroked his beard and smiled. "No need for you to go. I'll handle it!"

Showing off in front of others was something he loved most. Why else would Luo Chen have entrusted him with this task?

It had been over a hundred years since he last experienced this!

The flying boat descended slowly. In the distance, two cultivators flew over.

"Who goes there?"

Xu Xiao Liu clasped his hands behind his back and said with a chuckle, "An envoy from the Luo Tian Superior Sect has arrived. Why don't you take me to see your Valley Master?"

The two cultivators exchanged glances.

They knew of the Luo Tian Sect.

They also knew what "Superior Sect" meant.

But the Luo Tian *Superior* Sect?

A moment later.

In a magnificent hall, Xu Xiao Liu faced a burly man whose aura erupted like a volcanic explosion. Though his body trembled slightly, his expression showed no fear.

"Could it be that you don't know? Our Luo Tian Sect's Supreme Elder has returned from overseas and has already reached the Primordial Sage realm!"

"This time, our sect is holding a grand ceremony, inviting only Nascent Soul seniors. It's only because the Golden Core great sects of Fenghua Domain are temporarily our neighbors that we've given you a seat at the table."

"On the third day of next month, don't forget, Valley Master!"

As he spoke, Xu Xiao Liu tossed out an invitation.

The burly man was initially skeptical, but the moment he received the invitation, his expression changed slightly.

The characters on it were bold and vigorous, like dragons and snakes dancing. At first glance, they seemed to be array-pattern restrictions!

Moreover, strands of mana clung to it. When he opened the invitation, that wisp of mana seemed to remember him and sank into the paper.

Thus, even if someone else took the invitation, they couldn't impersonate him.

The burly man opened his mouth. To incorporate array-pattern restrictions into an invitation, with mana flowing like drawing a talisman—such methods were no ordinary feat!

Could it be that the Luo Tian Sect, that pack of homeless dogs, had truly welcomed back a Nascent Soul Sage?

He swallowed his saliva, retracted his fierce aura, and forced a smile.

"Fellow Daoist Xu... Xu, the journey was long. You must be tired. Why not rest here in my Lie Feng Valley for a while?"

Arrogant first, respectful later!

Xu Xiao Liu smiled. "No need. I still have to notify the other Golden Core sects of Fenghua Domain. I won't stay. The third day of next month..."

"Remember! Remember!"

The Lie Feng Valley Master personally saw Xu Xiao Liu out.

After he left, the Valley Master couldn't help but smile bitterly.

Fenghua Domain originally only had Fenghua Palace as a Nascent Soul superior sect. But after a hundred years of beast tide devastation, more and more forces had retreated to Fenghua Domain.

Earlier, the Five Elements Divine Sect and Motian Cliff had temporarily settled in Fenghua Domain.

Those two sects were restless, constantly testing the waters by expanding their territory and recruiting vassals.

Now there was another Luo Tian Sect?

"I wonder what Fenghua Palace thinks about all this. We small sects really can't read the wind direction!"

...

"Sister, has the Luo Tian Sect really brought back a Nascent Soul Sage?"

"Yes."

Fenghua Fairy narrowed her eyes, thinking of her two encounters with that Luo Chen. She exhaled softly.

"That man's cultivation is profound, not inferior to mine!"

Upon hearing this, the Second Palace Master of Fenghua Palace changed expression slightly.

Her senior sister was a mid-stage Nascent Soul cultivator, a formidable figure even in the vast Eastern Wilderness. That Luo Tian Sect's Supreme Elder had achieved Nascent Soul in just a hundred years, which was already remarkable. But to rival her senior sister?

Seeing her senior sister's expression, however, it didn't seem like she was lying.

As the Second Palace Master wavered between doubt and belief, she suddenly thought of something.

"The Netherpassage Pill formula we obtained from Qu Lingjun is said to be Dan Chenzi's. He wouldn't..."

"A mere second-tier pill formula. Given that man's temperament, he shouldn't make trouble over it." Fenghua Fairy shook her head, then said with a worried expression, "What I'm concerned about is his dissatisfaction!"

Dissatisfaction?

The Second Palace Master immediately understood her senior sister's meaning.

Although Bai Li Qingchuan was good, it was simply too small.

In the past, Nascent Soul superior sects would control an entire domain, tens of thousands of li of territory!

Even a weak sect like the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect was the nominal master of Jade Cauldron Domain, openly controlling thousands of li of territory.

Times had changed. While one couldn't demand an entire domain as a Sage's dojo, at least a fourth-tier spiritual vein was necessary for cultivation!

With Luo Chen's rapid advancement in realm, he would certainly be more desperate for such things.

What if he set his sights on Fenghua Palace?

"If it were just one person, you and I could join forces and not fear him. But the Five Elements Divine Sect and Motian Cliff have had the same intentions before."

Facing her junior sister's worries, Fenghua Fairy waved her hand.

"I'm not worried about that. Luo Chen can't possibly join forces with those two. But the fact remains that Fenghua Domain has no other fourth-tier spiritual veins."

The Second Palace Master said worriedly, "Also, we have to guard against him seducing our vassals and encroaching on Fenghua Domain's territory!"

Turning guest into host, occupying another's nest—such things were never in short supply.

When the Second Palace Master said this, there was hidden resentment in her words.

But that resentment wasn't directed at Luo Chen. It was directed at the holy land above them!

It was the holy land, the Nether Origin Sect, that had stopped these refugees of the cultivation world in the Five Domains.

And they had stayed for years. Over time, those parasites naturally developed some improper thoughts.

She was now hoping for a fight!

Hurry up and start fighting with the demon beasts, send all these people to the battlefield, and stop them from defiling their own garden.

A voice sounded outside the hall.

"Palace Master, someone has delivered an invitation!"

"Who?"

"The Luo Tian Sect!"

The Great Palace Master, Fenghua Fairy, frowned. She reached out, and the next moment, an invitation fell into her hand.

Her divine sense swept over it only once before she read aloud:

"'A belated Nascent Soul Grand Ceremony, widely inviting fellow Daoists to gather at Bai Li Qingchuan.'"

"What does this Luo Chen mean?"

...

Unlike what many thought, Luo Chen indeed didn't need to cultivate much recently.

In that short year or so in Central Continent, his realm had soared twice in succession.

From the first layer of Nascent Soul straight to the third layer, he had even nearly charged into mid-stage Nascent Soul.

Although his foundation for the second layer was fairly solid—having spent decades honing it in the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven—the suddenly inflated third layer was far too unstable.

Outsiders saw Luo Chen's sharp edge and overwhelming aura, partly due to deliberate display, but also because he couldn't control his surging mana.

Thus, in the short term, Luo Chen wasn't pushing for cultivation.

Instead, he turned his attention to other matters.

Taking pills to nourish his soul was one aspect. The second split soul, after years of nurturing, was about to break through Nascent Soul standards.

Practicing the sword canon was another. The *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon*, if he could achieve minor success, would be a major supplement to his combat methods. In particular, it would make it easier to wield the Yuantu Sword. So Luo Chen had been focusing on practicing the sword canon throughout the journey.

Of the seven strands of Crimson Firmament Qi, he had refined two so far. The progress was acceptable.

But although this thing was powerful, it was a consumable!

Relying solely on Luo Chen to ascend the azure sky and collect it himself was too time-consuming.

He still had to think about how to gather more Crimson Firmament Qi.

Now that he had returned to the Luo Tian Sect and seen the sect's situation, Luo Chen had one more matter to attend to.

On Little Stone Flat.

Luo Chen sat upright at a stone table, holding a golden lion brush of artifact grade. He was concentrating on drawing intricate patterns on a piece of specially made white paper.

He was so focused that nothing in the outside world could disturb him.

He didn't even notice Yao Mingyue helping the awakened Sima Huiniang out of the cave dwelling.

After about half an incense stick's time, Luo Chen's hovering hand finally stopped.

He let out a long breath and carefully picked up the paper, whose ink was still wet.

Intricate patterns, strange characters, and occasionally obscure mana flowing through it.

"Husband, are you drawing a talisman?"

Luo Chen turned around and saw the two women curiously staring at the talisman paper in his hand.

"Huiniang, how do you feel?"

Sima Huiniang smiled. "I feel fine, but my realm has dropped somewhat for some reason."

A drop in realm was normal.

Luo Chen had absorbed a lot of her mana and sealed her Golden Core, limiting her to at most mid-stage Golden Core strength.

This was to prevent excessive mana consumption that would cause the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu to devour Huiniang's mind in return.

He took Sima Huiniang from Yao Mingyue's hands and sat her on his lap.

"With me here, whether your realm is a little higher or lower doesn't matter much."

"Mm."

Sima Huiniang clearly didn't care much about such things anymore. Instead, she looked curiously at the paper on the table.

"What talisman is this?"

"Blazing Sun Talisman!"

Luo Chen smiled slightly and casually activated the talisman paper.

Then they saw the paper float upward, ignite spontaneously, and finally...

*Boom!*

A blazing sun suddenly appeared, radiating immense explosive and destructive power.

But all of this was restrained by Luo Chen, not spreading everywhere.

It was as if a firework had simply bloomed in the sky.

Sima Huiniang's eyes lit up. "It really is a second-tier Blazing Sun Talisman!"

"Evaluate the power?" Luo Chen asked casually.

Huiniang smiled. "It's about the full-force strike of a late-stage Foundation Establishment cultivator. It has some of the flair from when you used this technique to kill powerful enemies back in the day."

Saying this, she paused and looked puzzled.

"Such talismans should be useless to you now, right?"

Luo Chen chuckled and nodded.

"Useless to me, but greatly useful to the disciples under me."

Sima Huiniang was sharp-witted and immediately understood.

"You want to mass-produce low-grade talismans and give them to the disciples to enhance their strength?"

"Mm, but not just low-grade talismans. I'll draw third-tier and fourth-tier ones too."

"Can you draw high-grade talismans?"

"It shouldn't be difficult. Give me some time, along with the corresponding materials."

As he said this, Luo Chen pointed at the leftover materials on the table.

With first-tier cinnabar, second-tier talisman paper, and an artifact-grade talisman brush, he could only produce second-tier top-grade talismans at most.

Sima Huiniang understood and looked at Yao Mingyue.

"Mingyue, does the sect have any relevant stockpiles?"

Yao Mingyue had recently stepped down as Sect Master but still remembered the Luo Tian Sect's assets, big and small.

Without hesitation, she replied, "There's one third-tier talisman brush, made from the neck soft fur of a Green Mane Lion. Excellent quality. But we don't have corresponding talisman ink or cinnabar. Those are easy to buy, though. As for talisman paper, it's been in high demand in recent years. Our sect doesn't have much stock, and the quality is low. However, the method of making talisman paper from beast hides has become increasingly popular. We could try buying beast-hide talisman paper from the Divine Talisman Sect. They sell finished talisman paper!"

After answering, she looked at Luo Chen with sparkling, amazed eyes.

The uncle-master actually had exquisite talisman-making skills?

This was quite unexpected.

Luo Chen just smiled and didn't explain.

Exquisite talisman-making skills?

He didn't have them yet!

But it wouldn't take long to acquire them.

He had never practiced talisman-making, but he had been exposed to a lot of it!

When he was young, his friend Old Daoist Chen, who set up a street stall, was a talisman master. Later, a family he personally destroyed was also primarily in the talisman business. He had even harvested many basic talisman-making texts from them.

During his years drifting on the Northern Sea, he had gotten to know talisman masters like Lord Lushan and received the *Lushan Talisman Notes* as a gift.

One could say he had a thorough understanding of talisman-making knowledge, taboos, and essentials.

The reason he hadn't personally made talismans earlier was simply that he thought it wasted mana and time, and the returns were far inferior to alchemy.

But times had changed!

Now, Luo Chen had a different perspective.

He didn't have many subordinates. With his wealth, he could give each of them a magic treasure or artifact.

But such sharp tools often required a lot of time to refine. Moreover, due to individual differences, not everyone could simultaneously control multiple magic treasures or artifacts.

Sometimes, greed was not good!

In comparison, a talisman inscribed with one of Luo Chen's powerful spells was much more convenient.

Of course, this overly protective attitude toward his disciples wasn't the main reason.

Luo Chen's primary purpose in starting talisman-making was to use it to polish his mana and accelerate the stabilization of his realm!

There was no technique that required more precise control of mana output than talisman-making!

As long as he could control every bit of mana, it meant his realm was completely stable, and he could use all his techniques without fear of backlash.

And talisman-making?

To be honest, it really wasn't difficult for Luo Chen.

He just hadn't wanted to before. It wasn't that he couldn't.

He had a deep foundation in talisman knowledge, various spells honed to perfection as his base, and through practicing formations, he had also learned good calligraphy and painting skills!

On top of that, his mana was abundant and his soul powerful.

In short, all conditions were fully met.

The only thing to consider was the so-called "talent" that was often talked about.

But this was the thing Luo Chen least needed to worry about.

He had the attribute panel!

As long as he got started, it was just a matter of repeatedly grinding proficiency, raising his skill level, and then developing like a snowball.

In just half a month since returning to the Luo Tian Sect, Luo Chen's talisman-making skill, practiced in his spare time, had already reached the level of a second-tier talisman master!

Moreover, if not for material limitations, a third-tier talisman master might not have been his limit either.

With matching materials, he could try to draw higher-grade and more varied talismans.

Even!

Luo Chen thought even further ahead!

"The proficiency of fourth-tier spells is very hard to increase. Each use requires full effort. But the power of fourth-tier spells is too grand; there's no spacious enough place for me to practice."

"If I can inscribe a complete fourth-tier spell into a talisman, then each drawing would equal one full-powered use."

"In this way, after several hundred successful attempts, I won't just have a batch of extremely powerful talismans—I'll also be able to push a particular spell to its peak!"

Thinking of this future prospect, even the usually calm Luo Chen felt a fire in his heart.

The woman on his lap seemed to sense the surging emotions within the man. She reached out and gently rubbed his brow.

"Talisman-making is good, but it's too taxing on the spirit. Husband must take care of his body."

"Mm."

Luo Chen nodded.

For low-level cultivators, the limitation in talisman-making was mana. But for high-level cultivators, besides mana, there was also the constraint of the soul.

If one greedily pursued the power and value of talismans and made them in large quantities, it could easily affect one's own cultivation.

Luo Chen naturally wouldn't do something that sacrificed the essentials for the trivial.

He picked up Sima Huiniang in one motion.

"Husband?"

"It's time for you to take the Hundred-Headed Asparagus. At the Nascent Soul Grand Ceremony, you and Caiyi must both appear, accompanying me."

The woman was startled, then smiled radiantly.

She was also looking forward to that day.

And for high-level cultivators like them, who measured time in years for seclusion, that day was merely a snap of the fingers away.

...

Mountains and Seas Calendar, Year 3611, Ninth Month, Third Day.

White dew fell at night, and the autumn wind was biting.

At Bai Li Qingchuan, the mountain gates were wide open.

Beams of light converged from all directions, straight into Daqing Mountain.

(Chapter End)
