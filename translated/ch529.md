# Chapter 529: Devil Lord Azure Yang, I Want It All!

---

The information in the jade slip was not complicated.

It described in detail the intelligence on Senior Nantian, the man occupying the Swallow Islands.

His real name was Yan Nantian. He came from a cultivation clan on a large third-tier island in the early Demon Domain called Laboring Swallow Island.

Later, Laboring Swallow Island suffered a great calamity. The Yan clan was almost completely wiped out, and only Yan Nantian's branch luckily survived and fled.

Even so, great enemies continued hunting Yan Nantian's line.

During the pursuit, that branch dwindled, gradually leaving only Yan Nantian.

However, this man also possessed fortune, or perhaps he simply had a ruthless nature.

Not only was he not killed, he instead grew stronger and stronger, thoroughly integrating his clan's cultivation method while also cultivating secret arts of the Primordial Demon Sect's soul-refining lineage that had circulated outside.

After forcefully counter-killing several groups of pursuers, he temporarily suppressed his enemies' pursuit.

But because of that, he was also injured.

To prevent his enemies from taking advantage of his weakness, he deliberately left the flourishing regions of the Demon Domain and quietly fled to the remote Swallow Islands, occupying land and proclaiming himself king.

As for the methods he excelled in, they were one sword, one spike, and one soul banner.

The sword was his life-bound treasure refined in his early years, a water-element flying sword.

The spike was called the Water-Parting Spike, an unusual treasure possessing a method of parting water and transforming into a dragon.

That soul banner was the iconic Soul-Refining Banner of the Primordial Demon Sect's soul-refining lineage. However, unlike ordinary cultivators' soul banners, refined with large quantities of sea demon souls, his Soul-Refining Banner had been fused with a great many cultivators' souls.

Within were eight great split souls, all late-stage Initiate great cultivators.

The sole main soul was shockingly the soul of an Adept cultivator.

It was precisely because of this that Yan Nantian's strength grew stronger the more he fought, and his courage rose with every battle.

As for his own realm, he was impressively at Adept Stage Three, only one step from middle-stage Adept.

After reading this intelligence, Luo Chen could not help clicking his tongue in wonder.

Clearly, this was not something Cheng Dou had hastily collected. It must have taken a great deal of time and energy, gathered bit by bit.

In other words, this intelligence had not even been specially prepared for Luo Chen from the beginning.

"Perhaps even if I had not come, he would have thought of a way to invite someone to get rid of Yan Nantian, this mountain pressing over his head."

"External reinforcements... the enemies who hunted the Yan clan of Laboring Swallow Island back then?"

Luo Chen's eyes narrowed slightly.

He also roughly understood why Cheng Dou believed he possessed the strength to deal with Yan Nantian.

Calling himself Senior Azure Yang, controlling source true fire suspected to be Azure Yang Demon Flame, showing particular concern for intelligence on the Primordial Demon Sect: there was a ninety-nine percent chance he was a Primordial Demon Sect cultivator.

With the strength of a demon-path Holy Land cultivator, dealing with a mere remnant who had fled from a clan should be practically certain.

"But is he not afraid his judgment is wrong? If I fail, won't he bring destruction down on the Cheng clan?"

As the question arose in his heart, Luo Chen quickly answered himself.

Most likely, Cheng Dou had already been forced into desperate straits.

The man was stuck just before Perfected Completion, unable to advance an inch. The pressure from his realm had nearly driven him mad.

For this, he did not hesitate to gamble with his clan.

When he first met Luo Chen, he had even sent his own younger sister forward to win Luo Chen over.

Looking at it this way, he did not lack the courage to risk everything in one desperate throw.

"It is somewhat similar to when I, at late-stage Initiate, dealt with the Yan Alliance."

Luo Chen smiled faintly and put away the jade slip.

Seeing the smile on his face, Cheng Haixin, who had been quietly observing him all this time, felt her spirits lift.

Was this confidence?

A calm voice entered her ears, carrying Luo Chen's casual attitude.

"I understand the situation now. I have only one question, and I need your Cheng clan to answer it."

Joy appeared on the woman's face. "Senior, please ask."

"Very simple. It is not impossible for me to act against Yan Nantian. The question is: what compensation will you use to invite me?"

Cheng Haixin subconsciously said, "The Cheng clan has some savings. We can still take out several hundred thousand spirit stones."

Luo Chen shook his head. When he had been wealthiest in the past, he had possessed over ten million spirit stones.

Even if his purse was currently tight, he did not lack a mere few hundred thousand.

"Treasures..."

"Cultivation resources..."

"Cultivation methods..."

One condition after another, all of which Cheng Haixin considered quite valuable, was proposed. Each one was met with Luo Chen shaking his head in refusal.

By the end, a thin sheen of sweat even appeared on the woman's face.

"Adding this concubine as well... is not impossible?"

Luo Chen looked at her with a smile that was not quite a smile. "From the first day we met, were you not already mine?"

Cheng Haixin bit her lip with pearly teeth, and a sudden feeling of powerlessness rose in her heart.

"Enough. Go back and discuss it properly with your elder brother. Once you have thought clearly, come give me your reply."

Luo Chen waved his hand. A cool breeze lifted the woman and sent her outside the cave residence.

Rumble...

The two stone doors slowly closed, leaving only the woman's dazed expression behind.

...

On Black Swan Island, mortals came and went in lively streams.

Because of a sea disaster, the Cheng clan's demon-hunting ship had lost a large batch of skilled mortal crew members. Recently, it had reached a new recruitment period.

Even though work at sea was extremely dangerous, the treatment offered by the Cheng clan was high.

Thus, this recruitment truly attracted no small number of formidable mortals from other islands.

The recruitment site was placed outside the Little Mirror Lake manor.

From far away, one could see the crowd lined up like a long dragon.

At a glance, everyone participating in the recruitment possessed powerful qi and blood.

Judged by the realms of the secular martial world, even the weakest of these people possessed second-rate strength, while the powerful ones were even comparable to master-level existences.

Suddenly, waves of commotion rose from the end of the crowd, causing those in front to look back despite themselves.

Within their sight, a tall, beautiful woman slowly walked over.

She seemed lost in thought and paid no attention to outside gazes.

The occasional furrow of her brow added a few shades of fragile sorrow to her beauty.

Only after she completely vanished into the depths of the manor did low discussion rise through the long line.

"That was Fairy Haixin, wasn't it?"

"It should be. With such a tall and outstanding figure, and such a lovable beauty, she ranks among the very best female immortals of the thirty-six Swallow Islands."

"Brother, judging only by her face is too shallow. Isn't what Fairy Haixin is most praised for her terrifying aptitude, successfully reaching Initiate at thirty-six?"

"Indeed. Ever since the hundred-year calamity, the Swallow Islands have been turbulent. They only stabilized thirty years ago. In these thirty years, those able to successfully reach Initiate have been few and far between. Fairy Haixin rose against the trend and helped forge the Cheng clan's prestige of seven Initiates in one household. It truly inspires admiration from people like us."

"Given time, Fairy Haixin may not be unable to climb the peak of Adept. Everyone, chat if you must, but be careful with your words."

"Right, right. Do not discuss immortal matters."

The discussion behind her did not matter in the slightest to Cheng Haixin, who in outsiders' eyes was a proud fairy.

At this moment, her mind was entirely occupied by Luo Chen's attitude.

He wanted nothing?

Then what did he want?

Before long, she arrived outside Cheng Dou's door.

As if sensing someone had arrived, the door opened by itself.

Cheng Dou saw Cheng Haixin and showed slight displeasure. Why had she come back again?

Cheng Haixin naturally knew what her elder brother was thinking, but she said nothing of it. Instead, she recounted the earlier situation in full.

Joy first appeared on Cheng Dou's face, but he quickly sank into thought.

What did Senior Azure Yang want?

No. Or rather, what could the Cheng clan give?

Thoughts turned constantly in his mind. A moment later, Cheng Dou smiled bitterly.

"We are merely a trifling Initiate clan. What do we have that an Adept Senior would take interest in?"

"Nothing!"

Cheng Haixin froze. "Ah?"

Cheng Dou shook his head. "To say we have nothing is true, but in reality, it means we must give everything. In other words, if we want to invite Senior Azure Yang to act, then my Cheng clan must submit to him without reservation and be driven by him. If he wants people, we give people. If he wants things, we give things. That is all."

Cheng Haixin's expression changed instantly. "Such conduct... isn't that the style of a devil? I thought that because he did not feign lust and observed principles, he had the bearing of a gentleman."

Cheng Dou sighed. "He is a demon-path man to begin with. Even if he is a gentleman, he is only a demon lord."

Devil Lord Azure Yang?

Cheng Haixin said weakly, "Then we..."

"Give!" Cheng Dou seemed to have prepared himself long ago. He gritted his teeth and said hatefully, "Whatever he wants, we give. Even if my Cheng clan becomes someone's dog, the master still has to give the dog bones to eat. With his style of conduct, he should allow me to charge into Adept in the future."

Cheng Haixin worried, "Elder Brother, staking everything on an unfamiliar Adept cultivator... is it too risky?"

"I have no other choice!" Cheng Dou let out a long sigh. "Besides, now that matters have reached this point, the arrow is already on the string and must be released. It is no longer up to you or me."

The wolf had already entered the house.

When one's realm was inferior, one had no control over anything.

Beside Little Mirror Lake, the siblings faced each other in silence.

...

On Sea-Watching Cliff.

Luo Chen held a bamboo slip and read it at an unhurried pace.

After recultivating and planning his combat system, his current methods were already no weaker than they had been during the expansion war.

Moreover, his realm had risen to Adept Stage Three, making all his methods even more formidable.

With strength came confidence. And with confidence, he had enough mental energy to study other things.

The Starfield Pill was no longer sufficient to support his cultivation going forward.

Its medicinal properties not matching fire cultivation was one reason.

Another reason was that the medicinal ingredients required were too complex and too numerous.

With the Northern Sea's abundant resources, if he had enough manpower, they truly could gather everything for him. But processing them would not be so easy.

Back in the Jade Cauldron Domain, it had been because his Luo Tian Sect had a batch of physicians and alchemy apprentices raised from youth. From the moment they entered the profession, they processed all manner of medicinal materials for him, saving Luo Chen tedious labor.

Now, that would not work.

Moreover, Luo Chen did not trust others.

The pills he refined were for himself to eat.

If there were problems with the raw materials, he could inspect them.

But if processed materials still had problems, that trouble would be far more than minor.

Thus, no matter the reason, the Starfield Pill no longer met his requirements.

For this, Luo Chen had another solution.

Change to another pill, one more compatible with his attributes and with relatively fewer main and supplementary ingredients.

That was the pill formula in Luo Chen's hand: the True Flame Pill.

It came from the Yan Alliance, a third-tier fire-element pill.

In the Jade Cauldron Domain's four-hundred-year history, this pill had never been famous. Even within the Yan Alliance, those who could refine it and were willing to take it were few.

The reason was that this pill's medicinal power was tyrannical and came with endless hidden aftereffects.

After taking a True Flame Pill, a force of fire balefulness would remain in the body.

This fire balefulness within the medicinal nature was shapeless and traceless, difficult for mana to dissolve. With long-term use, it would cause irreversible damage to the qi sea.

Moreover, this fire balefulness was part of the medicinal nature itself and did not count as pill toxin. Ordinary detoxification methods also found it difficult to dissolve.

This was why the True Flame Pill did not circulate widely.

However, under Luo Chen's research, he had ways to overcome all these shortcomings himself.

Its tyrannical medicinal nature mainly manifested as an impact on the physique. With his physique at early Archaic, he could fully withstand such impact.

As for the so-called fire balefulness medicinal nature, he also had two preparations.

One was the Fine Dust Primordial Art, which had incorporated the Clear Origin Wondrous Pill Method. The other was the fourth-tier Dragon-Slaying Art.

With both approaches together, he refused to believe he could not resolve the lingering harm of a mere third-tier pill.

Once he made up his mind, what he had to do next was simple.

Study the pill formula and become proficient with it as quickly as possible.

Find suitable manpower to gather all kinds of materials for him, allowing him to refine large quantities of True Flame Pills.

The latter was also one reason he lingered in the Swallow Islands for now.

This place was remote, and there were not many Adept experts across the archipelago. It was very suitable for developing a force.

After studying the True Flame Pill formula a while longer, Luo Chen put away the bamboo slip.

He took a handful of Spirit-Feeding Pills from his storage ring and casually tossed them into the sea below Sea-Watching Cliff.

Watching the Blue-Ringed Fang Serpents swim back from every direction to fight over the pills, Luo Chen considered the Cheng clan's reaction.

It was not that he wanted nothing.

He wanted everything.

With Cheng Dou's clever mind, he should be able to comprehend that.

Having reached the end of the road, or rather, having no other choice, he would inevitably agree.

And cultivation clans were, in truth, the easiest forces to control and the fastest to take shape.

They had many clansmen, were bound by blood ties, and had fixed territory. Unless the clan was broken and destroyed, they would not drift everywhere.

On top of that, Luo Chen had no blood relation to them, no master-disciple relation, and no friendship or kinship. Their connection was not deep. If something truly happened, he could simply leave.

Such a force would not restrain him. It was precisely the best kind.

"They should be making their decision soon."

Luo Chen smiled faintly, clapped his hands, and waved for the full Blue-Ringed Fang Serpents to leave and once again form a protective formation around Sea-Watching Cliff.

He allowed the sea wind to strike his face and lift his black hair.

"And I should also consider how to deal with Yan Nantian."

Turning, Luo Chen cast his deep gaze a thousand li away, toward the lake-heart island at the center of the archipelago.

Invitation Moon Island.

Swallows circled it, and at the heart of the lake, it invited the moon.

Vast black fog shrouded a radius of ten li, forming an absolute forbidden zone in the Swallow Islands.

Whether mortal or cultivator, all were prohibited from setting foot within.

With Luo Chen's experience, he could naturally tell that the vast black fog was ghostly qi on an enormous scale.

Within it, low roars and howls of Ghost King-level existences could even be heard from time to time.

To enter Invitation Moon Island, he first had to break this Ghost Mist Grand Formation, arranged with a Soul-Refining Banner as the formation eye.

Luo Chen weighed his own methods. In the end, his gaze landed on a mass within the Primordial Unity Cauldron that was thick and viscous like blood and fire.

"Ever since forming my Core, I have spent countless manpower and resources of the Luo Tian Sect, as well as my own energy, nurturing Blazing Fire Miasma. It should finally have a place to be used."

*(End of Chapter)*
